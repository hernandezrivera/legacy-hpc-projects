Project Summary

HNO 2024 indicates that both Aleppo and Idleb are facing significant WASH challenges. The HNO highlights that a total of 2.96 million people in Aleppo and 2.57M people in Idleb need WASH assistant. The access to WASH services in these regions has been compromised due to the prolonged deterioration of existing systems, primarily caused by years of conflict and further exacerbated by the earthquakes in February 2023. A notable concern in the area is the persistent risk of a cholera outbreak. According to the cholera situation report (October 2023), 40.1% of suspected cases (75,959 cases) are in Idleb, and 33.4% (63,255 cases) are in Aleppo.
NGO X conducted a Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment (MSNA) in November 2023. The results emphasize the critical link between access to safe water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene practices and their profound impact on nutritional outcomes, especially in the context of child health. In terms of Solid waste management, NGO X 's MSNA findings align with REACH's Humanitarian Situation Overview (November 2023), which reports that 25% of assessed communities in Northwest Syria consider waste removal services a critical WASH priority. The lack of sanitation is strongly correlated with acute malnutrition and stunting, particularly when water is contaminated with human feces. According to REACH's estimates, less than 50% of water and sanitation systems are operational at the community level as of November 2023, emphasizing the urgent need for comprehensive and targeted interventions in the region. NGO X has had extensive expertise in delivering water supply programs in NWS since 2014. The primary objective is to ensure access to reliable, safe, and clean drinking water for vulnerable conflict-affected communities and IDP sites, encompassing both host and displaced populations in high-priority northern sub-districts of the Idleb Governorate and North Aleppo.
Under water supply sector will involve providing lifesaving safe water to approx. 675,278 vulnerable individuals in Idleb and North Aleppo, this will be implemented via supporting 42 Water Stations (WSs). The support extends to operational costs associated with water infrastructure activities, solarization, and training for WSs staff. Adhering to WASH cluster standards, between 25 - 40 liters/person/day (LPD) will be piped directly to households (HHs) to ensure equitable access. In the sanitation sub-sector, life-saving activities in IDP sites involve desludging activities, and the rehabilitation of sewage infrastructure in six locations in Northwest Syria, aims to mitigate the impact of open sewage systems and ensure the sustainability of sanitation services. Additionally, NGO X will construct wastewater treatment plants in two locations to reduce the negative environmental impact of sewage water.
Within the Environmental Health sub-sector, NGO X will continue supporting waste collection in IDP sites and, at the community level, proposes the rehabilitation of four dumpsites in NWS to enhance the technical and environmental conditions of these sites. In the Hygiene Promotion sub-sector, NGO X acknowledges the urgent need for comprehensive hygiene promotion (HP) initiatives to mitigate the spread of waterborne diseases. HP sessions will be conducted at the community level and IDP sites by Community Mobilization and Hygiene teams, ensuring alignment with WASH cluster recommendations and coordination with other relevant sectors such as Nutrition.
Regarding protection risks, the primary gender concern within the WASH context is ensuring the safe and accessible acquisition of water. The provision of piped water at the household level is pivotal in enabling all segments of society, particularly women and children, to access water within the safety of their homes. Moreover, interventions related to infrastructure will contribute to service enhancements, fostering increased access to the water network at the household level.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

NGO X operates on the premise that GBV is prevalent in all humanitarian contexts and takes robust measures to identify and mitigate this risk. The WASH operations of NGO X are increasingly aligned with IASC guidelines for preventing GBV in humanitarian settings. To enhance the risk assessment related to Safe, Accountable & Inclusive, NGO X improved its SAIP risk assessment process, aligning it with the Whole of Syria (WoS) WASH sector Protection Risk Analysis matrix. This alignment will be considered throughout the implementation of the proposed activities.Risk: Water distribution not safe to access or not accessible, especially for women, girls (exposure to SEA & GBV risk) and children. Mitigations:- Providing water at the HH level will reduce protection risks when collecting and transporting water.- Direct supply will improve accessibility and benefit persons who cannot collect or transport the water themselves, like older persons and persons with disabilities, and women, etc.- Provide clear information to affected population explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services/favors to anyone in exchange of receiving assistance. - The Outreach & Protection Team will provide rising awareness and Information on the CFM and other protection related topics.- Visibility products on PSEA and child safeguarding will be available for BNFs.Risk:Children at risk of being engaged in child labor by contractors in WASH activities. e.g. child labor.Mitigations:- Include clauses against Child Labor in the WU’s service agreements, MoUs that state clearly, they cannot employ children “all Water Unit staff must be more than 18 years old”.- Raise awareness on child labor for staff, WU’s staff and beneficiaries in communities.- Implement an internal referral mechanism to ensure the Identification and referral for child labor cases.- Assigned a protection focal point from program staff to ensure smoothing safe identification and referral process for children at risk.- Launce GOAL Guidelines for engaging with and distributing assistant to CHH to inform staff and other stakeholders on related minimum standards.Risk: Potential exposure to diffident hazards during the project's implementation.Mitigations:- Necessary safety equipment and personal equipment will be provided at the water station.- During any water network repairs activities, GOAL ensured that there are the necessary signs to protect the community from any risk, especially the children.- Beneficiaries will be informed of the different project activities that involve construction safety threats to ensure that children are prevented from accessing these areas during work.- Water quality was ensured by regular monitoring of the water quality at HH level, public places and water source level.- Al proposed locations under this intervention will be selected based on the high severity scale of WASH.
