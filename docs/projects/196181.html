Project Summary

Takaful has designed this program aims to support the food security of the most vulnerable IDPs and part of the host community in Idleb and Aleppo governorates,
Main interventions:
First: providing most vulnerable IDPs and HCs standard food baskets on monthly basis, targeting 12,000 HHs 66,000 individuals (11880 men 14520 women – 17820 boys -21780 girls ) in Idleb and Aleppo governorate, The distribution will be conducted at specified food distribution points (FDPs), safe FDPs will be chosen, away from the military centres with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them. For beneficiaries who have difficulties to reach to distribution points such as families headed by child or Disabled persons, the baskets will be transferred to their location using light vehicle. The HHS who live in camps will receive baked bread in. Second: providing newly IDPs with Ready to Eat rations (RTE) which enable them to provide their families with required food for the first week of the displacement. This intervention targets 3000 HHs 15,000 individuals (2700 men - 3300 women - 4050 boys 4950 girls ) The identification of these IDPs is the responsibility of the Takaful MEAL team, who track the movements of the IDPs, collect their data, and verify these data. Third: Cooked Meals: In this intervention, Takaful Al-Sham will provide 5,000 cooked meals to 5,000 individuals (1296 men, 1466 women and 1653 girls 1584 boys), The new IDPs from the areas that have been displaced by the recent military operations will be targeted. The meal will contain the calories agreed to in the standards set by the Cluster. Takaful will coordinate with the food security cluster and with other organization who works in the same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication. The project will ensure equal access for both genders with the priority for women, children headed household, disabled person, sick, PLW and elderly in addition the project staff will include female and male members trained on the protection principles and gender equality
Both in-kind and CVA modalities will be used to distribute food support, the CVA will be used in the areas that have a function market such as Azaz, Salqin, and Idleb, while the in-kind modality will be used for the areas that lack function market, especially inside camps and frontline areas.
We already secured 5 M $ though the BHA fund.

3 - Needs

The food security situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated since last year. In 2023, an estimated 15 million people will need some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68% of the total population. At least 12.1 million people are estimated to be food insecure, including all 2 million people living in camps who are deemed to be 100 percent food insecure, and 2.9 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity, an alarming increase of people at risk who could shift to become food insecure during 2023 if the crisis continues at same worsening levels [HNO, 2023].The Food Security and Agriculture sector was able to reach on average 6.5 million people with regular food assistance and another 2.4 million people with bread and flour distribution in 2022. At least 1,462,056 beneficiaries have been reached with livelihood interventions during 2022. Despite these efforts, 12.1 million people remain in need of food assistance, which highlights the severity and acuteness of the need across Syria. [HNO, 2023].. 4 - Implementation of activities

In-kinf food assistance for the assessed food insecure people: Takaful will either receive baskets from the donor, or purchase them from the markets according to Takaful policies. The distribution will be conducted in specified food distribution points (FDPs), safe FDPs will be chosen, away from the military centers with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them. For beneficiaries Who have difficulties to reach distribution points such as families headed by a child or Disabled persons, the baskets will be transferred to their location.Food voucher modality: The value of each month $78 enables the beneficiaries to obtain approx. 78 KG of food assistance which covers the around 2110 Kcal per person per day for the family as the items selected are in accordance with the cluster guidelines. Takaful has a long term agreement with a service provider to provide the e-voucher system and its devices, on the other hand the sales points will be selected from the local market according to specific criteria including; reputation, geographic locations, accessibility, and the commodities’ quality and prices. The vouchers will be distributed to households after verifying their IDsSupply flour or bread directly to households or to bakeries: the activity will be as follow: Takaful Al-Sham will agree with the bakeries through tenders in order to provide them with flour to bake it and deliver it to the beneficiaries as bread bundles

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk1:The vulnerability of children and women in conflict circumstances highly increases and places them at higher risk of being exposed to exploitation.MS: 1-Mandatory PSEA training will be conducted for all project staff before doing any task.2-Staff will sign the code of conduct and safeguarding policy. 3-PSEA FPs will provide awareness sessions to the beneficiaries about PSEA.4-The team will publish clear and accessible complaint mechanisms in all distribution points.Risk 2: There are not enough humanitarian staff (both male and female) at the distribution site to monitor the distribution process.MS: 1-Deploy gender-balanced teams, considering gender and the presence of female employees with female beneficiaries.2-Ensure field-level PSEA Focal Points in distribution teams and/or that staff involved in distributions know how to report or refer any PSEA allegations received.3-Consider wearing clearly marked organization logos on shirts/jackets/caps for ease of identity by beneficiaries.Risk 3: when challenges and issues arose during distribution, they are not handled well, and there is no female employee if the beneficiary was a female.MS: 1-Ensure the existence of PSEA focal points at all distribution points.2-Sensitize affected communities on PSEA and who/where to report.3-accessible complaint mechanisms in all distribution points.Risk4: Female beneficiaries are sexually abused by sellers.MS: awareness sessions about PSEA to beneficiaries and sellers.
