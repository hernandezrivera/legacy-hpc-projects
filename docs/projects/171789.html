Project Summary

The continuation of protracted crises in Somalia, now compounded by the effects of Covid-19, Floods, and droughts has resulted in massive challenges pertaining to Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene. Approximately, 4.6 million Somalis are still in need due to insufficient access to portable water and inadequate sanitation services of the affected population in many districts, as reported by the HNO of OCHA.
It was recent, in the mid of 2020 riverine floods occurred in lower Shabelle districts where a huge number of agro-pastoral households were displaced and fled to surrounding districts and villages in Afgoi, Marko, and Mogadishu as indicated by OCHA in August Hagaa, Also the flooding struck after rivers broke their banks in Marka district, South West State, on 01 August 2020. According to the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (UN OCHA), flooding has damaged homes and crops. Around 6,000 people have been displaced and moved to nearby internally displaced people (IDPs) sites. UN OCHA added that 27,000 IDP families in eight settlements at risk of flooding as their shelters are located in low lying areas.
Thus negative affecting to the community-owned WASH services and the dignity of highly vulnerable and displaced communities, the displaced pastoralists and host communities have now faced critical WASH services in terms of Latrines, clean and safe water, appropriate hygiene promotions services in their hosting IDP settlements in Mogadishu, Afgoi and Marka. Besides that Persons with disabilities face additional challenges and barriers. Barriers include environmental barriers, which inhibitive access to low services of WASH facilities, as well as social barriers, leading to discrimination within communities and further aggravating the risk of exclusion to WASH services. Physical barriers are often due to the design and distance to water and sanitation facilities, which makes it difficult for persons with disabilities to have equal access to WASH services. The lack of consultation and engagement with persons with disabilities in WASH designs limits the opportunity for an inclusive response and contributes further to the marginalization of this vulnerable group.
Overall the above-mentioned WASH problems and needs, Through, this project, HINNA will target the floods, drought, and conflict-affected population and returnees in L/Shabelle and Benadir regions by providing Emergency Responses especially in the areas of in terms of WASH through the provision of adequate safe and clean water, construction of gender-sensitive latrines, installation of water taps and construction of water kiosks, provision of culturally appropriate hygiene education, promotion of household water treatment through provision and distribution of water purification tablets prioritizing the most vulnerable households targeting girls, boys, women, and boys. PWDs will have active participation in all the project phases from the project design, implementation, and monitoring stages.
Overall of the project objective is to improve the living conditions of the host communities including women, girls, boys, elderly and disabled persons through clean and safe water, sanitation, and appropriate hygiene promotion.
Provide safe emergency and sustained water, appropriate sanitation through latrines construction, culturally appropriate hygiene education, and distribution of water purification tablets prioritizing the most vulnerable persons affected by the droughts, conflicts, and floods affected vulnerable women, girls, boys, and men included elder persons in Lower Shabelle and Benadir regions of Somalia. as well as local walls chlorination using local communities and the formation of the water management committee for the control and awareness of the water quality and water in use during and after the project period.

Project Activities

During the project period of 12 months, HINNA will implement meaning full emergency-related activities including, clean and safe water provision through the constructed of water kiosks and by contracting local water for 12,000 persons or 2000 HHs in all the three areas of the project and of which 4 water kiosks for 566 HHs (3396 persons) will be in Daynile and 3 water kiosks for 516 HHs (3096 persons) will be in Kahda districts of Benadir region, while 3 water kiosks for 480 HHs (2880 persons) will be in Afgoi and 2 water kiosks for 438 HHs (2628 persons) will be in Marka districts of Lower Shabelle regions of Somalia. And the construction of 560 gender-sensitive latrines of which (200 latrines will be constructed in Daynile, 110 latrines will be in Kahda district, 100 latrines will be in Marka and 150 latrines will be in Afgoi and as well as, hygiene promotion and distribution of water purification tablets at household level of (1320 HHs in Benadir and Lower Shabelle regions of Somalia). HINNA will provide the most vulnerable households with water storage containers to store drinking water with the hygiene kits distribution. Based on the water quality monitoring results, HINNA will provide remedial measures through awareness-raising on safe water handling by regularly conducting sanitary surveys to suspected water sources to identify risk factors, treatment options like chlorination will be done during the surveys of the wells and boreholes and HINNA will promote household water treatment in areas where water treatment is not feasible at the water source. In addition, HINNA will also form water management committees for the proposed installation and construction of water kiosks and build their capacity to manage the water sources to ensure the sustainability of the proposed interventions. This will be done in close collaboration with the local authorities and water ministries in the respective areas. A total of 2833 HHs or in other terms 17,000 individuals of which 2550 are women, 2550 men, 5610 girls, 5610 boys, and 680 elderly persons) are expected to benefit from these project activities. Overall the project activities, each task's beneficiaries will include 15% of disabled persons, for instance, the PWDs project participates from the project assessment, designing, planning and implementation and monitoring of all the project phases, like all of them will have disabled persons of 15% as mentioned in the project summary there will be 15% of disabled persons from the total project beneficiaries of 17,000 persons that is (15% times 17,000 equal to 2,550 of disabled persons from all the project areas).During the designing, implementation stages, and monitoring cycles of the project, HINNA will give consideration that women, girls, boys, and men will be participated and consulted equally as well as the disabled persons will elso strongly have active participation and consultation.
