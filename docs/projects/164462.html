Project Summary

Silk Road seeks through this project to meet the needs of people through the provision of housing rehabilitation services for hosts, returnees & IDPs people for the affected beneficiaries whether they were tenants owners depending on the actual needs of the targeted people which will be identified and verified by our field team where Silk Road will provide i0-formation and data of existing vulnerabilities related to Shelter, negative coping strategies and damage level as well.
The project will be advertised and explained within the selected communities through contacting the local authorities, and local representatives to inform about our shelter programme taking into the consideration assessing regarding local councils and authorities affiliations prior to engaging to prevent interference from authorities and or armed groups. Then we will form a follow-up committee includes three representatives from the Local Council in addition to three representatives for the Silk Road organization (MOU will be signed ). The criteria ( Vulnerability and technical ) will be advertised and explained within the community using social media and announcements in the public places and in the offices of local councils and Silk Road program team will receive applications from the population in cooperation and coordination with local councils. Following to finalizing the registration process which may include Initial lists from the local council if any Silk road will create a preliminary beneficiary list then, we will start verification of the preliminary list through a comprehensive assessment to identify and select the final beneficiaries based on the actual and real needs (Selection criteria will match what mentioned in Shelter Repair and Rehabilitation Guidelines 2018 ) to ensure the selection criteria, and enable the engineers to identify basic and essential needs of each shelter in the rehabilitation process then we will produce beneficiaries list of 1000 eligible HHs according to technical criteria where the damage is graded as moderate then we will develop the BoQs and the scope of works as well for each house (Shelter technical assessment will be undertaken to determine the most vulnerable households that meet the Programme criteria, including technical shelter needs). Also, Silk Road will collect detailed information about each beneficiary and the building (Property Information). Also, we will conduct a Pre and post-assessments survey aimed at benchmarking all Shelter indicators for this project. Before starting of the rehabilitate works, Silk Road will sign an agreement (Shelter repair agreement) with beneficiary/owner also both parties (Silk Road and the beneficiary) will agree on the identified BoQ of the house and sign it as well.
After signing the agreement, Silk Road will identify the contractors and the construction service providers. Silk Road will identify contractors (female – if any) in each area or surrounding knowing that this will be through fixed prices and fair tender. However, Silk will announce this tender after finalizing all BoQs. After the signing, the contract with the contractor to implement the activities of the project, the contractor/s will start the rehabilitation works immediately, also a periodic meetings between the contractor and the engineers in the field will be conducted almost daily, in addition to bi-monthly meetings between the contractor and the project coordinator in order to follow up the progress and develop a phased implementation plan in coordination with the engineers in the field. After completion of the work, Silk Road will Issue the certificate of completion for each completed house and then pay the contractor.
At the end of the project, a questionnaire (Satisfaction survey) will be conducted by using the KOBO tool with 25 % of beneficiaries to identify their opinion about the services and to determine their condition after providing services and their satisfaction.

4 - Needs

According to the need assessment conducted by Silkn Road in the assessed areas, Shelter situation continues to worsen, in terms of the available shelter capacity, the adequacy of shelter conditions, the ability of people to repair or rehabilitate their own damaged house, and in particular the affordability of rent. There is significant overcrowding in assessed areas including the northern Idleb and north-western Aleppo. Shelter capacity in Idleb has been exhausted with no additional capacity to absorb further arrivals, while access to northwestern Aleppo is partially restricted. The people in need include different population groups, such as newly IDPs, returnees, and host communities. there are increased needs related to the repair and rehabilitation of damaged shelters and infrastructure as people seek more durable solutions. The most important thing which prevents people from repairing their houses was the lack of materials in the areas, lack of financial means and fear of retorting the damage to their houses. The most common issue facing by population in the shelter was the lack of scarcity of insulators cold and lack of scarcity of heating;The average cost of repairing a house or apartment in USD for damage over light 10 to 20 per cent of the size of the house is 410 $.The average cost of repairing a house or apartment in USD for damage that is below average 20 to 30 per cent of the size of the house is 620 $.It is likely that these needs will further increase with those long-term IDPs in the assessed areas, many of whom continue to face a range of barriers preventing return where a significant proportion of the population living in rented accommodation unable to afford rent. This issue disproportionately affects IDPs, who are renting. In addition, host communities face significant pressures in terms of shelter capacity and adequacy, as well as services a lot of population require NFI assistance.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Scenario 1: Discrimination against beneficiaries Threats1 -Bribes and Payments2- Diversion of humanitarian aid3- Some employees or stakeholders are involved in some suspicious business such as bribes in return for service deliveryMitigation measures:1- Staff training and use non-discrimination policies/code of conduct and involve Local authoritative bodies.2- Establish a strong feedback mechanism3-Train staff on the code of conduct4- Direct follow up by the administration in Turkey and Syria.5- Ensure distribution is well-organized.6- Efficiently deliver on the obligation of ‘Accountability to (Affected) Populations’, by keeping the communities concerned regularly informed of the project and its implementationScenario 2: Risk of sexual exploitation and abuse by stakeholders Threats1- Rape or sexual assault.2- Sexual exploitation versus service delivery." Mitigation measures :1-Coordinate with community representative during the implementation of the project.2- Direct follow up by the administration in Turkey and Syria.3- Regular inception visits by other employees from other offices.4- Code of conduct in all staff contracts.5- Establish a confidential complaint mechanism.Scenario 3: Risk of exclusion from shelter assistance due to lack of civil status document/ID.Mitigation measures:1- Work closely with communities, community leaders and authorities to identify an alternative way of identification. Scenario 4: Physical safety of beneficiaries/communities are at risk due to the structural integrity of the building and/or the presence of ERWs in or in close proximity to potential work sites. Mitigation measures:1- Structural assessment of buildings by qualified personnel.2- Repair/rehabilitate the buildings only if the building is found structurally safe.3- Orient contractors/workers on risks associated with the presence of ERW.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Scenario 1: Security incidents affect the project activities (beneficiaries, staff, Vendors): Silk Road field team has been trained security conflict dynamics analysis and they have the full rights to have the decision on the ground. A security officer is in place to follow up with the team with all relevant support in terms of building the field team capacity, following up their issues and analyzing the field situation in a regular and incident basis Scenario 2: Inflation, especially the cost of construction materials, fuel, transportation and etc, may result in higher than planned prices. If prices spike too high, then Silk Road may not have the budget to complete the project or /and Market system disruption.Silk Road will monitor inflation and prices and include this risk in itscontracts with service providers. If inflation rises, they will work with the donor to re-align the budget and program accordingly. - We will include the ability to ensure procurement as a criterion in the contractor's bid analysis. We will Consider using fixed prices and placing this risk in the contractor's contracts. We will Conduct a strong and constant market monitoring before starting any local procurement to have early warning of potential lack of supplies, and also map out alternative supply.Scenario 3: Inter or intra-sub district tension due to service delivery locations causing perceptions of discrimination or favouritism.1-Threats2- Seek to prevent the provision of services by some stakeholdersMeasures to mitigate:1- The locations where the project will be implemented will be chosen according to transparent and clear selection criteria.2- Targeting the most vulnerable and selection criteria clearly communicated to the community3- Coordination with local authorities and local water institution. 4- Ensuring adequate service mapping, needs assessment.
