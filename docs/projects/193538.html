Project Summary

The Occupied Palestinian territories (oPt) faces various long-standing challenges under occupation and blockade by Israel. Palestinians in the Gaza strip is forced to live harsh life in the besieged area. People are troubled by compounding problems of worsened economic condition, high unemployment rate, price increase, damaged infrastructure, limited water and electricity, and so on. This socioeconomic deterioration was accelerated by COVID-19 pandemic and air raid in 2021 and 2022.
This context also affects the nutrition condition and development of children under 5 years old, pregnant women and nursing mothers. Food insecure due to the above mentioned situation and lacking opportunity to obtain appropriate knowledge about child nutrition and development prohibit children from enjoying their rights to health which is one of a set of internationally agreed human rights standards.
The main goal of this project is “Health status and growth and development related to mainly malnutrition and development of children under 5 years old in target area will be maintained and improved by prevention and proper treatment.”
This project will be implemented by Japan International Volunteer Center (JVC) in partnership with Ard El Insan (AEI). We targeting the children under 5 years old, their family, pregnant women and nursing mother living in vulnerable area.
JVC and AEI will provide health check-up to 12,000 children under 5 years old and follow-up or referral to the children detected as malnutrition, anemia, rickets, developmental delay or disorder and/or having other problem. Concurrently with provision of micro nutrient supplement and distribution of food package, we will provide several lectures, sessions and counseling to the family who has children under 5 years old, pregnant women and nursing mother about basic child-raring. In terms of sustainable child well-being support within the community, we will train the Community Health Activators (CHAs) as advisor of child-raring and organize mother to mother support group. Also JVC provides capacity building training to AEI.

1 - Needs

Over the past decades, the nutrition condition of children under five years old in Gaza have steadily deteriorated. According to World Bank, 64.4% of Gaza people are in food insecure driven by protracted economic stagnation and restricted trade and access to resources, coupled with high unemployment and poverty rates due to longstanding Israeli blockade, repeated heavy air raid.Heightened food insecurity contributes to an insufficient intake of micronutrients, and poor households especially have limited access to micronutrient-rich foods, such as red meat, fresh vegetables, fruits and milk. Other factors, such as limited access to health services prevents many initiatives that have been taken to address micronutrient deficiencies from having much effect. The resulting, persistently high prevalence of micronutrient deficiency is likely to have grave consequences for human health and economic development if no action is taken.Chronic malnutrition in children affects the growth and brain development which may lead to developmental delay or disability, and weakens their immune system. Children born to anemic mothers are at high risk of premature birth and low birth weight. Even if they were born healthy, child will not be able to obtain sufficient nutrition from malnourished mother’s breast milk and it creates a vicious cycle between mother and child.Anemia is also a severe public health problem. Over half of pregnant women and over half of children aged 6–23 months were anemic, according to Ministry of Health (MOH) and national nutrition surveys conducted in Gaza in 2018 and 2020. High proportions of the Palestinian population are also deficient in vitamins A, D, and E, which play key roles in vision, bone health, and immune function. According to Health Annual Report(HAR) and National Nutrition Surveillance System (NNSS), 55% of children 6-23 months and 57% of pregnant women are suffered from anemia. This situation is caused not only by economic problems, but also by the lack of opportunities for care-givers to obtain proper knowledge about child-rearing. According to the interview conducted by AEI and JVC in 2021, it was found that there are some women in poor contact with their original family after the marriage and they cannot get a lot of support from their family in law and struggling to raise their child. In Gaza, where access to medical services is very limited, the prevention and improvement of the nutritional status of pregnant women and children in the early stage is essential. Investing in the malnutrition and early child development is a very effective intervention that benefits the child, family and community’s well-being.

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Health check-up and early intervention for nutrition and developmental status of children under 5 years old. (1-1) Health check-up and follow-up at home visits and CBO base will be provided to 12,000children under 5 years old to find the nutritional and/or ECD problem so that the children can get appropriate treatment in early stage.(1-2) Referral of children in need of treatment to AEI clinic or other specialized institution (transportation expenses assistance will be provided to the HH who cannot afford it).(1-3) Distribution of food packages to poorer 200 HH who have children with poor nutritional status. The package will contain Olive oil, Dates syrup, Tahine (sesame paste), Lentil, Semolina flour, Rice, Green bean, Oats and rice porridge which are familiar to mothers and available in local market. The explanation of how it is rich in nutrition and how to use at the time of distribution will be given at the time of distribution.(1-4) (JVC responsibility) Provision of multivitamin and minerals supplements to 3,000 referred cases to AEI clinic (Multivitamin, Iron drops, high nutrient therapeutic powder, Folic acid and anti-protozoal ). And Provision of Vitamin A&D drops for 6,000 children from 2-24 months old assessed as a mild malnourished (quantities are estimated based on AEI database and are subject to change upon actual admission of cases). Activity 2: Training of CHAs to serve as childcare advisors in the community (JVC responsibility)(2-1) Recruiting volunteers from the project area and providing lecture and on-the-job training (40 volunteers in total, lecture, on-the-job training). Topics will be young child health, nutrition (including breast feeding, IYCF, nutrition for nursing mother and pregnant women), development, positive parenting, PFA First Aid) and toy making. (2-2) Pre/Post test will be conducted at each lecture to follow-up each CHA’s knowledge acquisition level. Activity 3: Individual counseling and lecture for mothers, pregnant women and nursing mothers. (3-1) Providing lectures to total of 3,000 caregivers of mal nourished children and community parents. Topics will be young child health, nutrition (including breast feeding, IYCF, nutrition for nursing mother and pregnant women), development, positive parenting and toy making. The nutrition lecture will be provided with food tasting sessions so that mothers can learn proper seasoning, softness and nutritional balance. (3-2) Providing individual child raise counseling to the mothers during their child's check-up thorough interviewing mothers about their child’s condition at home and giving advice according to answer and inquiry from them. (3-3) Distribution of the booklet regarding basic nutrition and development of the children under 5 years old. Activity 4: Mother to mother support groups(4-1) Providing the training of leadership and basic child raising knowledge to mother leaders in the community.(4-2) Monitoring mother to mother support groups and providing support as needed.Activity 5: Technical and management capacity building for AEI (5-1) Providing training of review and developing technical staff and community health workers job aids.(5-2) Reviewing guidelines, manuals, policies and procedures to meet the national and global standards.

5.2 - Please briefly explain why the specific modality/ies for this response were chosen.

JVC’s in-kind food assistance aims to fill the gap for the services provided by AEI regarding the full assessment and follow up the malnourished children and provide them with the needed medication and multivitamins beside the awareness session to the community families besides the malnourished children’s caregivers, the In-kind assistance involves the distribution of a selection of foods chosen to prevent malnutrition and meet the energy needs of the mal nourished children served. Food can be provided to everyone needed in a geographic area or a camp or provided to specific individuals or groups considered particularly vulnerable (targeted distribution).It will be short term and has a phase-out strategy that allows it to be discontinued as soon as the malnourished children recovered and be in a good wellbeing health.
