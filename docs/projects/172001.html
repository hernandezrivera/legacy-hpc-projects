Project Summary

This project is the WASH component of a multisectoral response of ASB in the 5-km zone along the ‘contact line’ in the GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts. Overall, the project “Hygiene, Nutrition and Winterization as Cash as well as Psychosocial Support for Conflict-Affected People in need along the Contact Line, Eastern Ukraine” (14 June 2019-13 June 2021) aims to improve food security, hygiene conditions, preparedness to winter and psychosocial well-being of conflict-affected populations in the 5-km zone along the 'contact line' in Eastern Ukraine (GCA of Luhanska and Donetska oblasts). Whereas selection criteria are similar for some project components (e.g. food, hygiene and winterization), some vulnerable people benefit from several project activities. Such approach strengthens synergies between different components of this multisectoral project.
Whereas the project’s food, hygiene and winterization components are implemented by ASB Country Office Ukraine, the psychosocial support (PSS) is provided by ASB’s local partner, Samaritan Union of Ukraine (SSU).
Within the framework of the WASH component of the project, vulnerable beneficiaries receive cash and/or in-kind hygiene assistance. The vulnerable households in the 5-km zone near the ‘contact line’ will be provided with cash for hygiene to cover their basic hygiene needs (soap, washing powder, toothpaste, etc.). Moreover, people in need of adult diapers will be provided with hygiene kits for persons with special needs and, depending on a need, small family hygiene kits/PPEs. These project activities contribute to the WASH Objective 2: “Provision of critical hygiene-related supplies and information for the prevention of infectious diseases”.
The project is currently funded by the Federal Foreign Office of the Federal Republic of Germany.

1 - Needs

The conflict in Eastern Ukraine continues to have a negative impact on the socioeconomic situation in Ukraine, particularly in the areas adjacent to the ‘contact line’. According to ACCESS/NRC Rapid Market Assessment (April 2020) conducted in the GCA along the ‘contact line’, 25% of people reported not full availability of hygiene items in their settlements, compared to 7% in February 2020. The study also highlighted a 7% increase in hygiene prices between February and April 2020. Moreover, 45% of respondents noted that they needed to travel to another location to buy hygiene items (e.g. washing powder). As a result, essential hygiene needs may remain uncovered, which poses a serious risk of COVID-19 disease, especially in conditions of reduced water supply in the conflict area.As highlighted by WASH Cluster Needs Assessment Study 2019, vulnerable groups such as older people and persons with disabilities are particularly in need of WASH assistance. According to the needs assessment of HelpAge International (HAI) conducted in rayons near the ‘contact line’ in the GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts (August 2020), 99,46% of older women and men rely on pension as their main source of income. The needs assessment by HAI also highlights that 92,38% of older women and men residing in rayons near the ‘contact line’ in the GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts require basic hygiene items, whereas 21,20% of respondents noted having a need in urological pads. In terms of protection from COVID-19, 71,31% of respondents indicated the need in antiseptics, medical masks and latex gloves to protect themselves against the coronavirus disease (“Humanitarian needs of older women and men in Government Controlled Areas of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts, Ukraine”, HAI, August 2020).According to the WASH Cluster, over 3 million people in the Eastern conflict area require WASH-related humanitarian interventions. The residents of areas along the ‘contact line’ are in a particular need of assistance. As demonstrated by the WASH Cluster Needs Assessment Study 2019, 22% of households in the 20-km zone are in the poorest category, compared to 12% in GCA as a whole. In turn, the demographic profile in the 20-km zone is characterized by a higher number of older people (42% of the population and 67% of all HHs) when compared to the rest of GCA.Based on the data from the ASB’s ongoing project “Hygiene, Nutrition and Winterization as Cash as well as Psychosocial Support for Conflict-Affected People in need along the Contact Line, Eastern Ukraine” (2019-2021), beneficiaries supported by ASB between 1 January and 30 September 2020 demonstrate significant vulnerabilities in terms of age, gender and disability. Thus, among the recipients of cash for hygiene, 56% are older people, 66% are women, 14% of the caseload are boys and girls, 23% are persons with disabilities, and 8% are IDPs. As for the persons with special needs who receive additional hygiene support (including adult diapers) from ASB, 89% are older people, 77% are women, 90% are persons with disabilities and/or home-bound, and 6% are IDPs. Most people who need adult diapers (86%) are bed-ridden. Since only 30% of the caseload have an officially registered disability, they cannot claim disability-related social payments from the state and thus are in a great need of hygiene assistance. Overall, older people and persons with comorbidities have a higher risk of COVID-19 infection. In order to prevent/minimize health risks, people need clean water, adequate sanitation, hygiene items and hygiene promotion. Therefore, the lack of funds to purchase basic hygiene items and/or unavailability of these items in their localities undermine the ability of vulnerable people to follow good hygiene practices amidst the COVID-19 pandemic. So, both the primary and secondary data indicate a high need in provision of hygiene assistance in the areas close to the ‘contact line’, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

Purpose: To meet hygiene needs of vulnerable conflict-affected populations in Eastern Ukraine.Output: vulnerable beneficiaries along the 'contact line' received hygiene assistance.Outcome: target beneficiaries improve their hygiene situation.Activity 1. Beneficiary identification Given the multisectoral nature of the project (food, hygiene, winterization), the beneficiary selection methodology is based on a combination of food security indicators and general vulnerabilities. Since vulnerable households often prioritize their food needs over other expenses, the beneficiaries identified for cash for food will automatically receive cash for hygiene. Simultaneously, specific hygiene needs of persons with special needs (e.g. adult diapers) will be taken into consideration.The beneficiary assessment will be undertaken in coordination with WASH Cluster, local authorities and humanitarian agencies operating in the area. ASB will ensure that beneficiaries meet vulnerability criteria identified by HRP 2021.Activity 2. Distribution of cash to cover basic hygiene needs There will be 2 distribution cycles of cash assistance in 2020. In each caseload, 3500 beneficiaries will receive cash assistance to cover their food and hygiene needs:• Caseload 1: January-June 2021• Caseload 2: July-December 2021Cash transfers to 3500 beneficiaries will take place once in 2-3 months, covering food and hygiene needs for the respective period. ASB follows current WASH Cluster’s recommendation (UAH 300/person/month). However, due to the exchange rate fluctuations, ASB may reduce number of beneficiaries and/or revise cash value. For the population in the 5-km zone near the ‘contact line’ (GCA), the most preferred modes of delivery for cash assistance are bank account (48%) and post office (32%) (REACH Humanitarian Trend Analysis 2018). Thus, the modes of cash disbursement used by ASB (Oshchadbank and Ukrposhta) are in line with the preferences of the affected populations. Due to the home delivery of cash by Ukrposhta, older people and persons with disabilities would not need to travel to bank to withdraw the cash. Activity 3. Distribution of additional hygiene support to persons with special needsDuring 2021, ASB plans to provide in-kind hygiene assistance (adult diapers and accompanying materials) to 1000 persons with special needs (617 beneficiaries during the first half of 2021, with the increase of target to 1000 beneficiaries during the second half of 2021). During the PDM exercise conducted by ASB in summer 2020, 12,7% of PDM respondents asked for additional hygiene items, such as soap, shampoo, washing powder. Given the COVID-19 risks and the fact that 86% beneficiaries of in-kind assistance are bed-ridden, ASB plans to provide them with small hygiene/COVID-19 kits when delivering adult diapers and accompanying materials. So, during July-December 2020, based on their needs and preferences, up to 500 households may be supported with basic hygiene kits and/or PPEs. The composition of hygiene kit for persons with special needs is in line with the recommendation of the WASH Cluster (e.g. adult diapers, wet towel wipes, gloves, absorbent pads, powder). The PDM findings demonstrate high satisfaction of beneficiaries with the contents and the quality of the kit. Also, as the monitoring study has shown, 98,3% of beneficiaries reported using all hygiene items in the received kit to cover their special needs. The composition of a small family hygiene kit/COVID-19 kit will also follow recommendations of the WASH Cluster.Activity 4. Hygiene promotion or COVID-19 awareness ASB will raise awareness of project beneficiaries on the risks of COVID-19 disease through SMS-informing, leaflets, radio information campaign and other means of communication. The target for this component is 4000.

3 - Indicators & targets

Indicators & targets / Индикаторы & Цели/ Индикатори & Цілі(4,000 characters max)The main indicators and targets are the following:• 85% of beneficiaries are satisfied with the aid provided;• 85% of beneficiaries report that the assistance was easily accessible;• 70% of beneficiaries report that they are aware of the complaint mechanisms.• 7000 people benefiting from cash or vouchers for hygiene supplies (3500 individuals in each caseload)• 617 people benefiting from the provision of hygiene items (basic and additional hygiene support)• 4000 people reached with hygiene promotion or COVID-19 messages• 60% of program beneficiaries are female;• 35% of beneficiaries are older people;• 15% of beneficiaries are persons with disabilities and/or homebound.A hotline feedback mechanism is available to ask questions, request assistance, provide feedback and lodge complaints. All complaints are recorded in the Complaints Registration Database for follow-up. At the end of each distribution cycle, ASB will conduct the PDM. Households who were the most vulnerable during the initial stage of beneficiary selection, will be re-assessed and, if needed, included into the next caseload. This may eventually reduce the overall number of unique beneficiaries. All assessments, including PDM, are designed to allow age, gender and disability disaggregation.Given the demographic and vulnerability profile of the population in the project area, the main target group of the project will be older people, who currently represent 56% of the ASB caseload of beneficiaries supported with cash for food and hygiene in 2020. Given a longer life expectancy of women in Ukraine, approximately 74% of current ASB beneficiaries of cash for food and hygiene assistance in the 60+ group are female. Overall, Activity 2 is tailored to the needs of older women, who constitute 41% of the current caseload. As identified by REACH Humanitarian Trend Analysis (July-August 2020), in the 5-km zone along the ‘contact line’ (GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts), the need for adult diapers is higher among the HHs headed by older women (7%) than among general population (5%). Moreover, 77% of ASB beneficiaries who receive diapers are women. Therefore, women will particularly benefit from the distribution of hygiene kits for persons with special needs.
