Project Summary

This project is specifically meant to address WASH Cluster Specific Objectives. CO1: WASH integrated intervention starting at OTP/SC targeting the couple “mother/care-taker – severely malnourished child” until HH level aiming at breaking WASH-related diseases transmission cycle, CO2: Reducing prevalence of WASH-related diseases and risk of outbreak by transmission contexts upon epidemiological investigation, CO3: Access to basic services in emergencies, CO4: WASH specific activities for GBV mitigation, CO5: Provide permanent access to water and sanitation services for IDPs, returnees, hosts and chronically affected by floods and (pre-)famine conditions and CO6: Improve access to safe water and promote safe hygiene practices in communities showing high prevalence of GAM and high WASH vulnerabilities.
Provide timely/sustainable, equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for most affected vulnerable women and children. Integrate WASH in nutrition response through famine
prevention minimum package; provision of timely sustainable and equitable
lifesaving access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs, host communities and
returnees. The proposed project will target most vulnerable women and children who are IDP, host communities and returnees, school going children, women, vulnerable groups including HIV victims and youth in Manikakara Payam, Ibba centre Payam, Madebe Payam and Nabanga Payam. in Ibba County. Training of trainers will be organized for the staff who are frontline runners to build their capacity in WASH. In collaboration with other WASH partners on the ground, the project will focus to address water, sanitation and hygiene needs of displaced populations and host communities and provision of GBV intervention in Ibba County.
Integrated WASH and GBV-TARGET Association will prioritize WASH GBV in communities
and schools to improve access to safe water for conflict affected IDPs and host
communities in Ibba County through rehabilitation of boreholes/hand pumps, MHM kits
to women and school girls and rehabilitation/construction of latrines in schools/health
centers, borehole drilling, Motorization and spring protection.
TARGET will establish Water User Committees at all borehole/hand pump
rehabilitated that will be trained to strengthen their capacity to provide maintenance of the
water point for sustainability. The rehabilitation will be conducted through
existing local hand pump mechanics. TAEGET will request some essential tools
from core pipelines, local existing spare parts will also be used, however, the project will supply those spare parts missing considering the variability on
the ground. The projects will also focus to achieving hygiene behavior changes at
household level and in schools through hygiene and sanitation promotion with much
emphasis on behavior changes communication (BCC).
TARGET Association will supply and install hand washing facility/tanks in schools to
mitigate GBV at schools, promote sanitary practices and hygiene behavior changes in
schools. Dignity kits will be distributed to the affected Mothers, school girls as menstrual management tool and mitigate GBV in community.
Integrated WASH and Nutrition: The food insecurity in Ibba still remains crucial. TARGET
Association will prioritize that SAM children and their families have access to safe
drinking water and sanitation to improve clean hand washing at critical times
and safe water storage at households and reduce spread of diseases or chance of
contracting diarrhea disease in children which results to malnutrition. TARGET
Association will target training mothers of SAM/MAM patients on correct uses of water
treatment. TARGET will distribute washing soap, bucket, water
purifier and water filter to each SAM/MAM house hold. At the health facilities, TARGET
Association will supply and install, hand washing facility (tank), Waste Bin.
Finally, TARGET will ensure all the protective and preventive measures are in
place.

1 - Needs

Ibba County is located in Western Equatoria state, it is located between Yambio and Maridi Counties, bordered by lakes state to the north, and DRC to the South. It had a population of 42,370 People (2008 Sudan Census), but current population projection by County government is put at 57,635 people. The County is predominantly inhabited by the Zande tribes, who are entirely crop farmers. The humanitarian situation emerged from the July 2016 political conflict between the government and the SPLA in Opposition (IO) in Juba. In Ibba, the effect of this conflict on the communities was in July 2017 where the armed groups attacked civilians. Prior to the 2017 political conflict in Ibba, there was a conflict between farmers and cattle keepers in 2015 caused by the destruction of agricultural farms of the farming community by cattle which are primarily owned by nomadic cattle keepers. This conflict started in Maridi Town and spread to Ibba County. It’s worth nothing that the same communities were earlier in 2006 affected by the Lord Resistance Army (LRA) conflict. The 2019 IRNA indicates series of conflicts led to multiple displacements, killings of civilians and separation of children from their parents in Namarabia IDP site. It also resulted to the looting of Schools, health facilities and Mobile antennas. In Mazengi which hosts 285 IDPs and 100 returnees, there are no Boreholes schools, Health facilities, or even a church in some part of Ibba County e.q Maruko, Bamani Wowo and Mazengi in which situation has led to increased child marriage Gender Base Violent (GBV) in the community (Maridi State IRNA Report, May 2019).The conflict has let to cutting off basic services such as schools, safe water supplies and health services. Women and girls were subjected to high risk of GBV including physical assaults, rapes, forced early marriage and various forms of GBV. Since IDPs are integrated in the host communities, it amounts pressure on limited available water sources and other social services such as WASH, health and schools. Maridi State Inter-Cluster Assessment conducted in May 2019 shows that Communities and IDPs are drinking water from seasonal streams which is risk to women and girls and not safe for the health. Provision of safe drinking water & improved health services was prioritized by the community as most communities’ drink from unsafe water sources like streams, seasonal rivers that dry up in the dry seasons.Ibba County has huge humanitarian delivery gaps since some areas such as Tanamuko, Mazengi, Nabanga, Mazengi and Moruko Payam are inaccessible during rainy season. Meanwhile, WASH services in many parts of Ibba county are limited and are not protected, few hand pumps need rehabilitation, Some open water source where there is high population need spring protection, most community practice open deification and there is limited access by women to WASH supplies for household and personal hygiene. These includes water containers, soap, sanitary pads, etc. The most affected payams that TARGET Association would want to intervene are Nabanga payam, Madebe Payam, Manikakara Payam and Ibba center Payam. This WASH saving response will be implemented in coordination with the Directorate of Water and Sanitation and other WASH partners AMREF. Moreover, according to the South Sudan HNO 2021, an estimated about 5.6 million people in South Sudan which Ibba is included will be negatively impacted by lack of or inadequate access to clean water and improved sanitation and hygiene practices in 2021. TARGET Association has been working in Western Equatoria since 2015 (Yambio and Mundri) implementing WASH and as community based promoter of girls’ child education. the girls’ children supported from Western Equatoria hail from different background. Thanks to these previous experiences, TARGET Association has identified few major challenges which communities in western Equatoria and Ibba in particular needs.

2 - Project activities

With the current Covid-19 pandemic, TARGET Association will make sure it incorporatesall the protective and preventive measures in to the project. All the guidelines provided bythe ministry of Health and the Cluster will be taken in to consideration when designing theproject especially during campaign. Training of trainers will be organized for the staff who are frontline service providers to build their capacity in WASH. Water supply, Sanitation, Hygiene promotion activitiesinvolving trainings and awareness campaigns in the community• Water supply includingborehole rehabilitation/repair will include water quality analysis, constructions of VIP latrines in health/school facilities to mitigate OD, hand pump mechanicstraining and Water Sources management committees for the rehabilitated/repaired watersources. Hygiene sessions for pupils, menstrual hygiene management sessions for schoolgoing females above 12 years old, provision of dignity kits to schools’ girls ofmenstruating age. • Community WASH will target host communities and includecommunity targeted hygiene promotion activities, household water treatment sessions forvulnerable groups and media campaign. Much of the hygiene promotion activities willhave more focus on hygiene education than promotional activities with many of the targetbeneficiaries sensitized on the importance of good hygiene practices, such as handwashing and prevention of diarrhea and Covid-19 pandemic. • Hygiene promotionactivities will continue with more focus on promotional and demonstration activities likelatrine use and maintenance, safe water chain, and households’ visits. The program willsupport activities that will ensure its sustainability such as intensifying informationcampaigns, education and promotional activities. TARGET Association will usecommunity-based approach to implement these activities, continued collaboration withhost community and schools using life skill education and strengthening committees andhygiene promoters to support follow-up activities. To ensure that the intervention issuitable to the current situation in the areas of concern, water sources will be assessedand included in the project on a rolling basis, in close collaboration with DWSS and otherpartners on the ground. Water point’s repairs, rehabilitation, borehole motorization and spring protection will be outsourced to the outside supplier, which will be closely monitored and supervised by TARGET Association WASH Technician and DWSS staff. Formation and training of Water User Committees will provide the sustainability and strengthening of the Operation & Maintenance system of water sources, as a community-led approach to increase resilience to adequately deal with emergencies and with the aim to create a strategy for reduction of borehole spare parts subsidy, taking into special consideration the active participation of women in the decision making processes. A Water User Committee (WUC) will be formed in all locations where TARGET Association will take care of water sources to ensure maintenance and sustainability of the water sources. TARGET Association will also organize three trainingsfor hand pump mechanics in the three payams of operation. The workshops will concernhand pump repair and maintenance and go more in-depth on the technical issues.Selection and training of SAM/MAM on correct uses of water treatment and minimumhygiene practices, Streamlining training in hygiene promotion messaging-prioritizingnutrition teams at Health centers and OTP/TSFP/SC on WASH aspects, Training ofcommunity based hygiene promoters and nutrition workers/Volunteers from targetedvillages on WASH and Nutrition, Distribution of Hygiene kits and WASH NFIs (buckets,soaps, filter, water purifier)-for 500 most vulnerable community members/IDPs andreturnees/SAM/ MAM children.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

Populations with specific vulnerabilities will be specially considered andtargeted with WASH/NUT assistance that can meet their specific needs, and will be servedusing methodologies that ensure their access to safe water/NFIs materials and preventharassment or theft of property provided. For example, provision of assistance to PIN will be focused on most vulnerable (Girls, Female Headed Household, Pregnant and Lactating Women, Unaccompanied Children, Chronically Ill/ Disabled, Unaccompanied Elderly, and person without communal link). The specific needs of girls, boys, women and men will be takeninto consideration throughout the project cycle. All forms of PSEA contributingfactors related to locations selection of water points and distribution of assistance will be reviewed before any action in advance to mitigate PSEA related risks. TARGET’s protection against sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) policy will be used to access the level of vulnerability of beneficiaries to PSEA. The PSEA reporting desk will be well equipped at both field level and head office to ensure timely reporting and actions are taken.
