Project Summary

SAADO integrated Wash project intervention is meant to improve wellbeing of people affected by disaster (Flood, drought and conflict) leading to displacement or chronically exposed to lack of basic water and sanitation services in the targeted counties resulting in public health consequences and high GBV. This will be achieved through four cluster objectives:
• Contribute to reducing WASH- related morbidity and mortality in the targeted priority counties through WASH integrated programming with Nutrition
• Provide access to basic WASH services in emergencies and/or WSC 4 and 5 counties.
• WASH specific activities for GBV mitigation
The WASH intervention will target most Vulnerable Women, Girls, boys, Men and people living with Disability (PWD) of the Returnees, IDPS and the host communities affected by shocks including Flood and conflict in the hard-to-reach areas of Pigi, Fangak and RENK counties. The invention will reduce transmission of faecal- oral disease and exposure to disease caused by Unsafe water thus reduced morbidity and mortality related with WASH, reduction of prevalence of malnutrition among the children since greater percentage of malnutrition is cause by poor WASH and lessen the burden on women and children by reducing the distance to water collection points which usually increase GBV cases
The beneficiaries for this project will be 11,333: 3182 women, 2711 Girls, 2938 Men and 2502 Boys respectively. The project is estimated to cost USD 500,000 for the period of one year (12 month) from January 2025 to December 2025. SAADO has a well-established presence in the proposed targeted areas with qualified local staff to ensure timely delivery of services.
The project main activities will include; rehab. of the existing borehole and water yard in the communities facing high malnutrition and High WASH vulnerability; distribution of water cleaning tablet: Aquatab and PUR, provide MHM kits to adolescent girls in schools, provide WASH kits to children admitted for SAM/MAM treatment in OTP/SC, Provision of key hygiene promotion messages/sessions to communities include in health facilities, OTPs/SC and facilitate gender risks analysis, through focus groups discussion with women and girls; Establishment of Hgiene/ Water committee, Construction of gender appropriate and secured sanitation facilities in Institutions. The targeted groups/communities will be women, Girls, Boys, Men and person living with disability (PLWD) of IDPs, Returnees and Most Vulnerable host communities living in Fangak, Pigi and RENK counties.
Protection will be mainstream in the project; safety audits assessment will be done by identifying GBV concerns and implement mitigation measures. On inclusion of disability, barrier analysis and implementation of action plan will be key in this project. SAADO has strong presence and well-establish office and team with fully capacity to respond in emergency situation. SAADO will maintain a good relationship with other clusters members; Nut, Health, Edu,and FSL to enable it to provide integrated quality services to the vulnerable communities through synergies. We will increase our participation in WASH Coordination Meeting in varies level to share and learn from other partners.
For, accountability and quality assurance (AQA/AAP), the project will ensure inclusivity of all the gender, Age, women headed household, Ethnic minorities and person with disability. The project will have two-way communication channels that will facilitate feedback/complaints and provide redress for complaints, are committed to ensure affected population participate in decisions. For sustainability of the project, Committees will be formed to maintain WASH facilities. By implementing this Emergency WASH intervention, we envision creating resilient communities with improved access to safe water, enhanced sanitation facilities, and the adoption of hygienic practices

1. Needs

According to USAID Afia WASH/DT global report it states that despite significant donor investment in the south Sudanese WASH sector, only 41% of SS have access to basic drinking water and 11% to basic sanitation, and 63% of the population still practices open defecation. Greater % of this population are from the hard-to-reach counties that are identified in the WASH cluster strategy including Pigi, Fangak and RENK where SAADO has predominant presence. The conditions are even more dire in targeted areas, with greater than 80% of households lacking latrines, and practice open defecation and also less than 36% of the population having access to clean water. Most Vulnerable girls, Women, Boys and Men including disable persons in targeted areas are at heighten risks of shock and stress. According to UNOCHA SS, Flood Snapshot report (As of 15 November 2024), In SS, about 1.4M people remain affected by Flood across 44 counties. More than 379,000 people are flood displaced. Recent assessments in RENK identified over 32,000 flood affected people. The UNOCHA report state that Pigi 41k people are affected by flooding and 154k in Fangak, all being displace to higher ground. The population displaced is facing lack of safe drinking water and outbreak of diseases due to contamination of water sources. In RENK, humanitarian partners have reported a substantial rise in cholera cases due to inflows from Sudan, Limited access to clean water and sanitation and ongoing flooding. In Pigi and Fangak partner are on higher alert of cholera since the counties also received returnees fleeing from Sudan crisis. According to UNHCR and IOM dashboard 877 240 individual, have crossed into South Sudan from Sudan, putting more pressure to existing limited WASH facilities in these targeted counties. The targeted counties are among the counties project to experiencing food security; phase 4. According to recent IPC report, the nutrition situation has also deteriorated, with an estimated 2.1 million children aged 6-59 months suffering or expected to suffer elevated levels of acute malnutrition between July 2024 and June 2025, including 650,000 cases of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM). With all this shock and stresses, Girls and women face GBV whilst trying to access scarce water sources, and their dignity is compromised by poor sanitation and hygiene practices in communities and HHs. Limited access to clean water and sanitation services contributes to the high prevalence of Malnutrition, Water Borne disease, Poor Menstrual and Hygiene management, risks of GBV at water port absenteeism from school, and school dropout. Women and girls have specific Wash risks and needs, for example, they may face increased risk of GBV when collecting water and using communal latrine; in 2020 more than 20% of people using water facilities reported safety concerns, including violence and discrimination, particularly in IDP sites and other crowded settlement according to UNICEF. A stronger framework to support women’s participation in the WASH sector is needed. Persons with disabilities may face barriers due to inaccessible hand washing stations, inaccessible latrines and bathrooms, and long distances to water point.90% of returnees and 89.7 % of IDPs live in settlement without segregated latrines, particularly in rural areas of Upper Nile-RENK and Jonglei states- Pigi and Fangak counties. With all above challenges and to prevent the outbreak of disease to the vulnerable IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in targeted areas, there is an urgent need for safe water, Sanitation and hygiene services among the population living in this hard-to-reach counties thus averting the potential public health risk to communities. These counties often suffer from, poor access and infrastructure which is hampering humanitarian timely respond and hence increasing suffering of women and other vulnerable groups including PLWD

2. Project activities

To ensure the project deliver the key needed services to the needy beneficiaries, SAADO will implement activities that are aligned with WASH cluster, to Increase access to Quality WASH services to the returnee’s, IDPs and Most Vulnerable Host communities in Fangak, Pigi and RENK counties. This will include.• Provide safe drinking water to (targeted) affected people as per SPHERE guidelines • Rehabilitation of existing borehole in the targeted counties more focusing on the broken borehole near institution (Health facilities, OTP/SC, Schools and Market) and factoring flood resilient design in flood areas• Upgrading of 3 existing boreholes to water yard in Urban centers of the targeted counties to increase access to clean water • Provision and distribution of water purification tablets Aqua tabs (1-67mg tab/10ltrs) or PUR sachets(4mg-1/10ltr) in the communities, School, OTP/SC, and Health facilities • Training of water management committees on management of water points • Construction of segregated emergency temporary latrine (Segregated by sex) as per sphere standards (afterward decommissioning) in Returnees and IDPs camps and under served institution in the targeted county and factoring flood resilient design in flood areas• Construction of semi-permanent segregated latrine in the Vulnerable targeted communities considering disability inclusion• Construction of temporary bathing places, with drainages, segregated by sex as per SPHERE standards (1 for 100 people in temporary settlements only) • Installation of handwashing facilities in the returnees and IDPs camp, Health facilities, OTP/SC and Schools in the targeted counties• Conduct hygiene promotion awareness and campaign for adoption of safe and healthy hygiene practices (focus on developing and dissemination of key massages: Use of clean drinking water, safe disposal of excreta, hand washing with soap at critical times) to the targeted communities including Health Facilities, OTPs/ SC, Market place, Schools, Public Meeting, Church and other gathering • ToT of hygiene promoters on delivery of key hygiene messages in the targeted counties who will train others in the county• Provision of WASH kits to household with demonstration: Kit contents: 2-1.5 mtr female hygiene cloth, 6-bars of 100–125-gram body soap; 2-10 ltr Jerry can & 1-20 ltr size bucket with lid /household targeting Malnourish children in the OTP/SC through support of Pipeline partner, • Distribution of MHM kit to women and adolescent schoolgirl of reproductive age • Provide WASH Cash assistance to the most vulnerable in the targeted counties
