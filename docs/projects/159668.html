Project Summary

To ensure improved equitable access to basic primary and life saving health care and emergency obstetric, neonatal health care services to 3000 boys, 3000 girls, 5,000 women, and 3,500 men living in Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi districts.
Improved access to basic primary and life saving health care and emergency obstetric, neonatal health care services to 3000 boys, 3000 girls, 5,000 women, and 3,500 men living in Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi districts.
Improved emergency outbreak preparedness and response through training of 12 male and 15 female for diagnosing, case management and early warning alert in target areas of Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi districts.
Project targets the host communities, and IDP's in Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi districts of Hiran region.
Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi general hospitals are underfunded and its populations are having high need of primary health care projects.
EPI will be part of project and UNICEF will be the source of vaccines.

Project Activities

Support and scale up operations and maintenance of primary health care services in the target of 3000 boys, 3000 girls, 5,000 women, and 3,500 men through provision of OPD services, trauma management, health education and management of common illness diseases.• Provision of safe motherhood primary health care services in antenatal, delivery, postnatal and gynecological care to a total of 5000 women in Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi districts of Hiraan region.• Providing adequate essential medical supplies and equipment as needed to provide quality primary health care services 3000 boys, 3000 girls, 5,000 women, and 3,500 men• Regular training of 12 male and 15 female for HACDESA health staff on integrated IMCI and communicable disease management. • Regular training 32 female and 27 male CHWs and community volunteers to provide prevention and Integrated community case management (CCM) for communicable diseases and diarrhea management. • Establish good referral mechanism between CHWs and health centers for complicated cases in early time for early intervention. case management through health centers and outreach health services 3000 boys, 3000 girls, 5,000 women, and 3,500 men living in our target areas.• Continues disease surveillance and early reporting of outbreaks Beledweyne and Jalalaqsi districts of Hiraan region. • Outbreak investigation and coordination with other stake holders to ensure appropriate and timely response
