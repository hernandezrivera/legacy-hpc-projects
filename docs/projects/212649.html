Project Summary

Mercy Without Limits based its proposed activities on the 2023 HNO report for Syria, which highlighted the significant impact of Syria’s crisis continues to hinder people’s ability to meet their immediate food needs and livelihoods, in a context marked by very complex drivers and contributing factors. This includes economic worsening and weakened local currency, soaring food and non-food prices, as well as drought like conditions and severe agro-climatic fluctuations, water scarcity, and limited energy supply. In addition, food and agriculture commodities prices increase, inflation, lack of purchasing power due to limited livelihood and lack of income sources, population movements that shows no sign of abating, and the Ukraine crisis have had an important economic impact on markets and commodities cost.
The intervention targets villages in the northern Rural Aleppo and Idleb governorates.
For that, the proposed interventions aim to improve food security and livelihoods for IDPs and vulnerable households and to address the livelihood gaps by enhancing the resilience and livelihood opportunities of the most vulnerable farmers and livestock breeders, thereby revitalizing the agricultural sector in selected locations.
The program intends to achieve the following interventions:
• Enhance resilience and income generation for 700 livestock breeders (including 300 cow breeders, 200 sheep breeders, and 200 women) in the target location by providing support with livestock inputs. This will be along 3 outputs:
- 300 Cow breeders (having 300 cows) received compound cow fodder sufficient for 4 months.
-200 vulnerable sheep or goats’ breeders having (2,000 Sheep or Goats) received compound sheep fodder sufficient for 4 months.
-200 female-headed households of most vulnerable families received 3 sheep animals (totally equals to 600 sheep) with its fodder and veterinary care for 4 months.
- Four mobile veterinary clinics were established to serve 2900 animals (300 cows, and 2600 sheep or goats) owned by the target beneficiaries.
• Build technical skills for 200 male and female cow and sheep breeders through a Train-the-Trainer (ToT) program focused on best livestock practices. MWL will select ToT trainees and divide them into eight groups of 25 trainees each. They will undergo training and subsequently receive supervision and support in sharing their knowledge within the target communities.
• Enhance resilience and income generation for 700 male and female summer vegetable farmers. This will be implemented through the following 2 outputs:
-700 vulnerable summer vegetables farmers received agriculture inputs (summer vegetables seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, fuel for supplementary irrigation) for farming 4 dunums of summer vegetables. MWL has selected the best and valuable kinds of summer vegetables of tomato, cucumber, eggplant, and pepper. These vegetables are preferred by ‎farmers, being profitable when cultivating it in wide areas, especially tomato.
- 200 of the target summer vegetables farmers received ToT training on the best Agricultural Practices for summer vegetables farming.
As for the food security:
Output: 20,800 severely food-insecure households (104,000 individuals) of unconditional regular food assistance of Food Baskets and Value-based E-vouchers in Mare', Sharan, Afrin, Bulbul, A'zaz sub-districts met their food needs over eight months and reduced negative coping strategies such as selling assets or using risky behaviours in times of sudden-onset disasters of Earthquake and Shelling.
* Activities:
• Use Value-based E-vouchers to provide regular food assistance under Emergency Response over eight months to 2500 households (12500 individuals) in Mare', Sharan, Afrin, Bulbul, A'zaz sub-districts.
• Provide monthly in-kind food assistance baskets under Emergency Response over eight months to 18,300 households (91,500 individuals) in Mare', Sharan, Afrin, Bulbul, A'zaz sub-districts.
