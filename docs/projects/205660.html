Project Summary

CEDS intends to provide timely life-saving integrated WASH interventions that aimed at increasing access to improved WASH services to target population of 30,303 (Men 7,738, Women 7,273, Boys 7,930 and Girls 7,362) of 1,015,101 (Host: 718,740, IDPs: 122,093 and Returnees: 174,268) affected vulnerable population in seven (7) priority 1 & 2 counties of three (3) states, namely Northern Bahr el Ghazal, Lakes and Warrap. The priority 1 counties include Aweil East and Cueibet, respectively in Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Lakes States. Whereas priority 2 counties include Twic in Warrap State; Awerial and Yirol West in Lakes State; and Aweil South and Aweil North in Northern Bahr el Ghazal State.

1. Needs

Based on the 2024 HNO 5.5 million most vulnerable people (IDPs: 1,135,604, Returnees: 695,914 and Host communities: 3,754,933) are in critical need while 2.6 million (IDPs: 1,135,604, Returnees: 695,914 and Host community: 812,172 will be targeted for interventions, out of which 13 intersectoral priority 1 counties with a total target of 743,302 individuals that include Cueibet and Aweil East in Lakes and Northern Bahr el Ghazal States respectively and 18 intersectoral priority 2 counties for WASH response with a total target of 605,635 individuals that include Awerial and Yirol West in Lakes State; Aweil South and Aweil North in Northern Bahr el Ghazal State and Twic in Warrap State. Data from states line ministries of Water, Cooperative and Rural Development, Housing, Lands & Public Utilities and Physical Infrastructure of Northern Bahr El Ghazal, Warrap and and Lakes respectively indicate that 901 (37%) out of 2,425 total existing hand pumps and 64 (47%) out of 137 total existing solarized/motorized water systems are not functioning and need repair/rehabilitation; Warrap State: 1,475 (48%) out of 3,047 total existing hand pumps are not functioning with 285 (19%) confirmed to have dried up and beyond repair, and 45 (61%) out of 74 total existing solarized medium sized water supply systems are not working and need repair; and in Lakes State: 414 (17%) out of 2,486 total existing hand pumps and 27 (31%) out of 86 total solarized water systems are not functioning. With this project, CEDS intends to provide timely life-saving integrated WASH interventions to a target population of 30,303 (Men 7,738, Women 7,273, Boys 7,930 and Girls 7,362) through (1) provision of standard kits paired with hygiene promotion to registered/screened SAM children/caretakers in nutrition sites/facilities, (2) Construction/rehabilitation of gender segregated latrines in nutrition facilities; (3) Construction/rehabilitation of boreholes in nutrition facilities (4) Conduct sanitation and hygiene promotion campaigns messaging through household-to-household and public mass awareness sessions on hygiene promotion and distribution of soap; (5) Conduct Safety audits to select WASH infrastructure for construction/rehabilitation through focus group discussions; (6) Construction /rehabilitation of water points with a flood-resilient design; (7) Rehabilitation/construction of latrine stances with a flood-resilient design with a disability inclusion; and (8) Designing of disability inclusive sanitation facilities at nutrition sites/facilities, thus reducing morbidity and mortality in Lakes, Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States.
