Project Summary

To provide quality and accessible SR services to the affected population, with focus on main causes of mortality, particularly SRH, UNFPA will: 1. Increase accessibility to comprehensive SRH services to women and girls, including safe delivery, antenatal care, postnatal care, family planning, HIV/STI prevention and links to treatment, newborn care, clinical management of rape survivors, and post-abortion care;
2. Strengthening of the referral system to manage obstetric emergencies; support govenment partners to provide CemONC services in underserved, cyclone-affected areas; 3. Provision of life-saving Reproductive Health Kits, medicines and supplies, including tents, for temporary services;
4. Deployment of SRH Specialist, Nurse Midwives and Community Health / Youth Volunteers for awareness raising on health issues such as family planning, HIV/STI prevention, and danger signs during pregnancy;
5. Strengthen access to Adolescente Sexual and Reproductive Health information, services and rights; 6. Provide access to information and SRH and GBV services for women and girls living with disabilities and other vulnerable persons 7. Identify and integrate trained health providers to implement Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP),
