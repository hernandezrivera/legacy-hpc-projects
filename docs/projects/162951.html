Project Summary

In Sudan, more than 16 years of internal conflict have decimated the infrastructure and workforce. The population is mainly rural and widely dispersed with more than 65% living below the poverty line. Sudan is among the poorest and least developed countries in the world, ranking 161 on the 2016 Human Development Index, (out of 187 listed countries.
Last year, a youth revolution changed the system of government in the country, which caused economic impacts represented by the high cost of services specially food and water. Most families, especially those who live in IDP camps, have had their lives severely affected.
Some of the gains witnessed in the WASH sector were eroded by the economic situation. At the peak of fuel scarcity, the cost of transport rose with some affected people reporting increases in the cost of a water barrel ranging up to 200 percent, forcing people to reduce their intake or to resort to unsafe water sources.
The purpose of this project is to respond for the Cholera and Acute watery diarrhea AWD, as a resolute of last year Cholera out brick in several Sudanese States, by Providing Basic WASH, Sanitation and Hygiene service delivery (including sustainable skills to the community in clean water management), based on needs assessments in several locations targeted IDP camps in five localities (Kass , Nyala, Nyala North, El Salam and Mershing) in South Darfur State, to reduce Morbidity and Mortality among the venerable IDPs and host community.
The project aim to improve general environment, public health and easy access to clean and good manage the water resource in the targeted area, by addressing Participatory Hygiene and Sanitation Transformation (PHAST) Approach, to improve skills of the community to fighting diseases.
The project targeting children, women headed household, widow, Girls, elders, disables and youth through provision of Water service, Sanitation and Hygiene Promotion activities to support direct beneficiaries to get their basic WASH, Sanitation, and Hygiene promotion activities. Specially the children at the schools.
Within this project, (Al Shrooq Organization), will work to achieve clean environment service, to the targeted beneficiaries by increase awareness among them and provide services. Such (solid waste management campaigns and clean up exercises in targeted areas, conduct vector control campaigns in collaboration with State Ministry of Health (SMoH), Construction of six garbage pit collection points, two in Kass, two in Otash IDPs camp and other two around returnees village.
The project will target 3,515 HHs in Sakali & Elseraif IDPs camps in Nyala locality, also we will targete Bulble Abou Jazo and three other villages in Alsalam Locality. The target beneficiaries will be organized in committees and will be involved in all stages of the project implementation cycle; assessing needs, planning, implementation, monitoring and follow-up the activities, assessing impact (for improving ownership & reducing tensions).
1- Formation and training of (six WUCs) 66 person, in all targeted areas in water management.
2- Provide training of 200 individual beneficiaries (women, Gils, Youth and men) in maintenance of hand pumps. 30% will be Women and Girls.
3- Training of 33 person on operate and maintenance mini water yard (solar system).
4- Conduct 24 solid waste management campaigns and clean.
5- Conduct 24 general clean up camping in all targeted area ( Kass , Otash IDPs and Mershing village)
6- Tainting of 75 Hygiene promoters to conduct general hygiene promotion campaign (home visits, inspections etc.)
7- Provided cleaning tool, equipment and disposal transportation. For solid waste management.
8- Conduct gender sensitive training to the target population to ensure privacy of the gender, to get clean water to avoid (GBV) in WASH component.
9- Planting 1000, fruit tree in the target population.
