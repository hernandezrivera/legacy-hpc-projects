1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The UNICEF programme is committed to addressing the needs of conflict-affected children and their families, with particular attention to adolescent boys and girls. The main pillars of intervention will address child protection risks, including sexual violence, the recruitment and use of children in armed groups or armed forces, unaccompanied and separated minors, mental health and psychosocial needs, and environmental hazards. UNICEF will adopt a strategic approach that includes strengthening community-based mechanisms, enhancing systems of referral and case management, and providing a variety of focused group activities. Where possible, UNICEF will collaborate with local actors and civil society, emphasizing child participation and ensuring all actions are in the best interests of the child. Child protection monitoring will be strongly emphasized to enable evidence-based programming. Prevention efforts will involve working with children, communities, and security forces. Response activities will aim to increase both access to and the quality of services. These services will adhere to global standards, such as the Child Protection Minimum Standards (CPMS) and GBV Guidelines ensuring a right-based and survivor-centered approach. As the lead agency for the Child Protection Area of Responsibility (CP AoR), UNICEF will prioritize integration with health and education sectors and strengthen collaboration with the GBV AoR to address the needs of child and adolescent survivors holistically. By fostering sectoral integration and prioritizing quality, UNICEF aims to build a protective environment where all children can thrive despite their challenges. 5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

This project will be based on a needs assessment that looks at the protection risks of boys and girls in the context of the northern provinces. The assessment will look at barriers to services for both boys and girls to ensure access to services and that service provision is tailored to the needs of the individual accessing the service. The programme will target under 18s with a particular focus on the needs of adolescent boys and girls. The programme will run safety audits that target girls and boys separately to fully understand the needs of this population, this information will also be triangulated with caregivers. Adolescent girls and boys will be consulted separately in the design of the programme and the timing of activities to influence the design of the programme. The programme will also look at the needs of child mothers, and caregivers with a focus on mothers but also including fathers. All data will be disaggregated by age, gender, and diversity.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

UNICEF uses child rights framework in the planning and design of programmes. This approach places children at the center of a response and is based on principles relating to the best interest of the child. This involves a process of designing programmes based on assessment of needs, child participation, and triangulation with communities and caregivers. MHPSS programmes are designed in consultation with the persons targeted for the programming including time, location, and consultation on activities. The programmes have feedback sessions with the participants at the end of each session to ensure amendments can be made throughout the cycle of programming. Child protection monitoring is completed in consultation with communities and action plans are developed with communities to implement protection strategies, these are reviewed with the community on a monthly basis through community groups that are accessible to men, women, youth, and children with disability. All UNICEF partners have access to training on how to work with persons with disabilities and in the use of inclusive approaches. Programmes will be tailored for age and gender and inclusive of abilities considering the use of language, and literacy levels and will be adjusted accordingly. The program will target at-risk groups through community outreach and by engaging youth, and community leaders who are from different backgrounds and genders.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNICEF will ensure to integration of the training to implementing partners on disability inclusion and also to link referrals for assistance for specialized support. Structures, facilities and recreation kits, and other forms of kit are designed with consultation of disability agencies to ensure the design is accessible for all and all can-use items.Data is also disaggregated by age, gender, and disability. Awareness raising is implemented at the level of the community to ensure that communities are aware of disability inclusion and support members of their community in participating in activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The programme will use an approach of resilience building of communities to ensure that communities can build on their own resources and strengths to limit the need for outside materials and support. The design of the multipurpose centers will be based on local natural materials. The program aims to empower communities to participate in the building of the structures. Implementing staff will be recruited locally to provide sustainable approaches to knowledge development and resilience. Where possible, kits will be locally procured to put back into community business and use materials that are familiar and used by communities.Emergency preparedness measures will be supported at the district and provincial levels to support local response capacity and regional responses to reduce the need and use of flights in case of further crises or natural disasters. The program is also looking at the set up of satellite offices to reduce travel by car or flight.
