Project Summary

The major humanitarian needs in South Darfur State result from different underlying root causes and drivers including instability and multiple conflicts, recurrent natural disasters including droughts and floods as well as disease outbreaks. This project aims to work with communities to build their capacities not only identify cases but to support to fight the practices that continued to burden women. Through women centers, which women and girls have a safe space to conduct their activities and through the project we can collect information about the communities with regards GBV, women and girls continue to suffer suspiciously from GBV due to violations of basic rights, insecurity, low economic status, rejection of livelihood opportunities, lack of community awareness on women's rights and GBV as well as due to negative cultural and societal norms. Rape, physical violence, FGM, early/ forced marriages, denial of resources opportunities, and verbal abuse are some of the common violations perpetrated against women and girls. The 2023 HNO indicated that there are 3.1million People in need of GBV services, out of which the GBV Sub Sector is targeting 1.3 million.
The project will contribute to the cluster objective by reinforcing social services through access to these services for women and children in Nyala shamal, Geraida and Bilel localities and strengthening the community capacity to protect its members. The economic crisis and persistent insecurities have placed many girls out of schools' safe environment. Displacement as result of conflict has also hindered girls from accessing education services. At current, in three localities there is no protection services available despite of the needs. The project will set up friendly and safe spaces where women, girls, and adolescents can participate in various learning and recreational activities and receive protection services (including through referral pathways). ECORD will work closely with communities through leaders and CBPNs to ensure the services are accessible especially for women and girls (including adolescents). Establishing or strengthening the capacity of CBPNs will facilitate the awareness creation and also capacity development for community to protect their own members.
Elgossa Organization will contribute to the three GBV Sub -Sector objectives:
1- Strengthen the provision of life- saving specialized quality GBV services, for survivors of GBV and vulnerable women and girls. (With a focus on women and girls with disabilities and adolescent girls (Linked to HRP Strategic Objective 1), through the provision of different services to vulnerable women & girls including GBV survivors (Case management, PSS, referrals), assessments, community awareness-raising, and programming, provision of dignity kits, and capacity building of GBV front line actors.
2-Enhance strategies for community resilience through the empowerment of women and girls and strengthened community-based interventions (Linked to HRP Strategic Objective 2), by supporting women centers, community protection networks, and training of women & girls on vocational skills,
3- Promote GBV risk mitigation into all aspects of the humanitarian response (Linked to HRP Strategic Objective 3), by training on GBV for Non-GBV actors.

1 - Needs

GBV remains a grave concern in Sudan. The MSNA shows that more than half of the respondents are not aware of support services for women and almost 80 per cent have heard about cases of violence against women or girls. This is also confirmed in the findings from “The Voices from Sudan” report. 19 per cent of respondents of this study perceived domestic violence and sexual violence as the most common GBV incident happening in their community. Negative social norms and the collapse of traditional community support structures has increased the vulnerability of affected people, and particularly persons with specific needs, including persons living with disability. According to the first nation-wide, qualitative GBV assessment, “Voices from Sudan 2021”, physical and sexual violence are prevalent both inside and outside the home, and sexual violence is more pervasive in rural and conflict-affected communities including refugee camps. Intimate Partner Violence is not considered a crime, and archaic laws such as the Personal Status Law (1991) mean that incidents that occur in the home are viewed as private issues to be resolved within the confines of the family, thus women tend not to seek legal redress. Under the proposed interventions herein, Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) is seeking funding to increase coverage and access to primary services for vulnerable communities within Geraida locality, while enhancing their capacities for sustained resilience, involvement and community participation. In Gereida and Belil (Dereige – Alserif IDPs Camp), on 1 September an Inter-agency assessment, team comprising OCHA, HAC, IOM, UNHCR, Al Busma, Sada Alaon, SAO, Elgossa, Trust and WES deployed a team on 4 September to assess the situation and determine the needs, for Al serif and Dereige and the assessment identified the needs of the GBV concerns, which the project will address, also on 4 September an inter-agency assessment mission has been conducted to address the needs of Alsalam IDPs, and There are issues and protection concern but under reporting with regards to protection outside Al Salam IDP camp, issues of insecurity have been reported that women and boys facing GBV incidences specially when they are going and back between or at the farming areas and where IDPs women goes for firewood and grass collection. And due to all those issues Elgossa Organization will address the solutions of those problems directly through this project.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The activities and implementation design considered the impact on the environment with a mitigation and enhancement process, the planned women centers – if supported - will be rehabilitated using to the extent possible the no environmentally harmful materials. The promotion of fuel-efficient stoves - if supported - will contribute to the improved livelihood of vulnerable households while reducing environmental impact as fewer trees are cut for cooking fuel. In addition, during the dignity kits distribution, women and girls will be sensitized to environmentally friendly ways of disposing of the used sanitary wear. Through the women and girls’ empowerment program, the GBV AoR partners will explore the production of reusable sanitary pads as a replacement for disposable ones.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this project, Elgossa Organization is focusing on promoting effective participation of targeted communities across gender, age, and vulnerability throughout the project cycle to ensure GBV survivors and those at risk are not exposed to additional harm. In addition, the project will take into the account mainstreaming of meaningful access, safety, and dignity when implementing activities. Project will ensure inclusion of most vulnerable groups within the target communities in close coordination and collaboration with key stakeholders, this project will follow the reporting protocol and use reporting templates that Donor will provide off and on-line. A comprehensive Performance Monitoring Plan (PMP) will be developed by the M&E Manager in the first month of the project. Data collection tools including forms and templates will be developed by the M&E Manager who will similarly train project staff on how to use these tools, the project will put in consideration the coordination with the GBV Sub-cluster, and government bodies in line to make the protection monitoring consolidated and put in a locality level protection monitoring report to be shared with protection sector at the state and national level.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Accountability to the affected population is core in the Elgossa Organization response and will be strengthened through continuous consultation with the affected population on the response modalities as well as their satisfaction with services provided. In addition, Elgossa Organization is an active member of the national PSEA technical and coordination team and leads the survivor support task team and together is working with the team members to ensure that GBV referral mechanisms are accessible to survivors of SEA. Elgossa Organization will equip implementing partners and targeted communities with information regarding the SEA, and how to report and access services including health, legal and psychosocial support services. Elgossa Organization ’s partners and beneficiaries play an active role in the decision-making process in regards to the planning, implementing, and evaluating of activities.This is done by conducting focus group discussions with women and girls in women centers. The project will support the development of self-protection capacities and aim at improving beneficiaries’ daily lives through improved formal and informal protection mechanisms. This is considered the most effective way of tackling gender inequality as the root cause of GBV.
