Project Summary

Over the last years, Somalia people has been struggling recurrent conflicts (clan based and armed conflicts), climate changes including droughts and flooding, continues evictions due to lack of informal camps owned by individuals, Somali HNO 2022 reports that 7.7 million are in need of humanitarian needs out of 2.2 million are internal displaced persons (IDPs) and 5.5 million are non internal displaced persons (IDPs).
HNO 2022 indicates that an estimated 6.4 million people, 40 per cent of the Somali population need emergency life-saving WASH assistance of which 3.5 million will need safe drinking water, 5.1 million sanitation services, and 6.4 million hygiene supplies, 16% of these are females and men, children for 64% and the elderly for 4%.
The increasing humanitarian WASH needs were driven by seasonal floods, severe drought conditions acute water diarrhea/cholera outbreaks and protracted crises, exacerbated by COVID-19 outbreak.
Majority of the households across Somalia especially women and girls to walk long distances and/or fetch Water, this exposes to protection risks including GBV while collecting water from too distant water sources or using unimproved overcrowded sanitation facilities which lack safety and protection features limiting women and girls' access at night.
Thus negative affecting to the community-owned WASH services and the dignity of highly vulnerable and displaced communities, the displaced pastoralists and host communities have now faced critical WASH services in terms of Latrines, clean and safe water, appropriate hygiene promotions services in their hosting IDP settlements in Banadir, Afgoi and Marka, Baidoa and Beletweine districts of which our project will be targeting, besides that Persons with disabilities face additional challenges and barriers. Barriers include environmental barriers, which inhibitive access to low services of WASH facilities, and social barriers, leading to discrimination within communities and further aggravating the risk of exclusion to WASH services. Physical barriers are often due to the design and distance to water and sanitation facilities, which makes it difficult for persons with disabilities to have equal access to WASH services. The lack of consultation and engagement with persons with disabilities in WASH designs limits the opportunity for an inclusive response and contributes further to the marginalization of this vulnerable group.
This project’s objective HINNA will improve the living conditions of the IDPs & host communities including women, girls, boys, elderly and disabled persons through clean and safe water, sanitation, and appropriate hygiene promotion.
HINNA of this objective was intended to cover the Overall above-mentioned WASH problems and needs, Through, this project, HINNA will target to floods, drought, and conflict-affected population and host communities in Bay, L/Shabelle Hiran & Banadir regions by providing Emergency Responses especially in the areas of WASH to 12000 persons (2000HHs) including PWDs for community inclusion and participation, construction of gender-sensitive latrines with lightening at night and lockable from inside the latrines for the protection of GBP risks, installation of water taps and construction of water kiosks for violence protection, provision of culturally appropriate hygiene education and distribution of hygiene kits, promotion of household water treatment through provision and distribution of water purification tablets prioritizing the most vulnerable households targeting girls, boys, women, and men. PWDs will have active participation in all the project phases from the project design, implementation, and monitoring stages, the project tasks include walls chlorination using local communities and the formation of the water management committee for the control and awareness of the water quality and water in use during and after the project period.

1. - Projects activities

During the project period of 12 months, HINNA will implement meaning full emergency-related activities including, clean and safe water provision through the constructed of water kiosks and by contracting local water wall distributors/suppliers in all the SIX (6) targeting districts of the project and of which 4 water kiosks will be in Daynile and 2 water kiosks will be in Kahda districts of Benadir region, while 2 water kiosks will be in Afgoi and 2 water kiosks will be in Marka districts of Lower Shabelle regions of Somalia and 1 water kiosk will be in Baidoa of bay region and lastly 1 water kiosk will be in Beletweine district of Hiran region. And the construction of 560 gender-sensitive latrines of which (150 latrines will be constructed in Daynile, 100 latrines will be in Kahda district, 70 latrines will be in Marka, 80 latrines will be in Afgoi and 100 latrines will be in Baidoa and 60 latrines in Baletweine districts and as well as, hygiene promotion and distribution of water purification tablets at household levels in Bay, L/shabelle, Banadir and Hiran regions of Somalia). HINNA will provide the most vulnerable households with water storage containers to store drinking water with the hygiene kits distribution. Based on the water quality monitoring results, HINNA will provide remedial measures through awareness-raising on safe water handling by regularly conducting sanitary surveys to suspected water sources to identify risk factors, treatment options like chlorination will be done during the surveys of the wells and boreholes and HINNA will promote household water treatment in areas where water treatment is not feasible at the water source. In addition, HINNA will also form water management committees for the proposed installation and construction of water kiosks and build their capacity to manage the water sources to ensure the sustainability of the proposed interventions. This will be done in close collaboration with the local authorities and water ministries in the respective areas. A total of 2,000 HHs or in other terms 12,000 persons of which 1920 are women, 1920 are men, 7680 girls, 7680 are boys, and are 480 elderly persons) are expected to benefit from these project activities. Overall of the project activities, each task's beneficiaries will include 15% of disabled persons, for instance, the PWDs project participates from the project assessment, designing, planning and implementation and monitoring of all the project phases, like all of them will have disabled persons of 15% as mentioned in the project summary there will be 15% of disabled persons from the total project beneficiaries of 12000 persons, that is (15% times 12000 equals to 1800 of disabled persons from all the project areas). During the designing, implementation stages, and monitoring cycles of the project, HINNA will give consideration that women, girls, boys, and men will be participated and consulted equally as well as the disabled persons will also strongly have active participation and consultation.
