Project Summary

The project aims at providing comprehensive protection services to the vulnerable people in Damascus and 13 areas in Rural Damascus, project activities are tailored to support vulnerable groups (men, women, boys, and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees, and host community) taking into consideration the inclusion of people with disabilities and variations in needs among all groups.
The project will provide services related to GBV prevention, CP, general protection, and RE. People of high protection risks (IPDs, Returnees, margainalized women,..) will be provided with NFIs to meet their basic protection needs under NFIs sector fulfilling specific criteria (No. of family member, one or more member with disability, women HHs, elderly,..). women headed HHs who are at risk of GBV will also be supported with multi-purpose cash to protect women from following negative coping mechanism. multi-cash will be distributed to GBV survivors, marginalized women (for being widows, IDPs, Returnees, or with disability) this those women will be identified and selected through implementation of protection and GBV activities in the same locations.
Activities in Damascus, Duma Harsta, Az-Zabadani, and Darayya will be as an expansion to already existing activities where the need is very huge. Newly established centers in other locations in RD aim at expanding services to new geographic areas and new populations, noting that Altamayoz has well trained staff and access to RD
Activities in the existing centers will provide an added value to the services that are being implemented due to the high need for protection services especially with the COVID-19 pandemic and the harsh living conditions, duplication will be avoided through coordination with other actors. AlTamayoz conducted field visits through volunteers who confirmed the needs expressed by people (visits were conducted to 15 locations, out of which 13 were selected where the returnees movement is high and a high percentage of IDPs exists, and where protection issues such as sexual harassment, child marriage and child labour were highly reported.
Activities under this project will interlink with other activities implemented under education especially those of MR education and PSS activities, on the other hand, students with special needs can be referred for case management and further referrals under this project when needed. Multi-purpose cash will support families and reduce the risk of children drop out of school
Activities implementation will consider COVID-19 situation and will maintain social distancing and assure precaution measures

3 - Needs

As per draft HNO 2023, It is estimated that 15.1 million people will need protection assistance in 2023. GBV continues to increase and become normalized in the country, affecting women and girls’ coping strategies and depleting their resilience. Children and adolescent boys and girls face violence, abuse and exploitation in many life situations. The presence of explosive ordnance compounds the humanitarian and protection crisis, causing civilian casualties, exacerbating the vulnerabilities of people at risk, and hindering access to humanitarian support, services and livelihood.Households with members with disabilities are likely to have insufficient income to meet their basic needs.Persons with physical/mental impairment and injuries, including direct victims of explosive ordnance incidents and their families, are at greater risk of developing additional acute needs due to the lack of sufficient and continuous specific care.GBVrisks together with widows and divorcees, older women and female IDPs continue to be the group that faces most of the risks.These risks are compounded for women who are the heads of households, and thus responsible to meet the financial/material needs of their families.During AlTmayoz's work in Rural Damascus, it was observed that child marriage reached 55% of girls 14-17 years, 40% of children aged from 12-18 are engaged in child labor, about 65% of women are exposed to GBV.this assessment used to plan for activities in the targeted locations

4 - Implementation of activities

Activities in Damascus will be implemented in AlMidan in Southern Damascus where a density of IDPs and overburdened host community exist.This project will support the already exist community center in Almidan in order to expand its activities due to the increased number of beneficiaries seeking for its services.In Rural Damascus,the proposed project aims at establishing community centers (including women and girls safe spaces) in 10 locations (Arbin Babella,Kafr Batna,Madaya,Syrghaya,Qudsiya Maliha,Nashabiyeh,and Ghizlaniyeh) where severity scale is 3,4 and 5, and to expand services in 4existing centers in(Harasta Duma,Daraya and Zabadani).Centers in all locations will act as a space where affected people especially women and girls can express and exchange concerns and receive appropriate services, and where children can interact with peers and where special needs of children are met.Activities will be implemented in coordination with other humanitarian actors in order to provide a complementary service and avoid duplication.The proposed project mainstreams protection through considering the principles of Do No Harm. GBV issues will be considered through conducting awareness-raising sessions to mainstream GBV prevention.All staff members have to sign PSEA code of conduct. AlTamayoz has an organized database for M&E. NFIs will be procured as per procurement policy accredited by UN agencies. NFIs are planned to be distributed to most vulnerable as per clear selection criteria
