Project Summary

Prantic Unnayan Society (PRANTIC) is targeting a total of 4,623 vulnerable households (HHs) including 1,752 Rohingya Refuge HHs to make self-reliant through different livelihood and climate-smart activities in Jaliapalong union and camp-4, under Ukhiya Upazila, District Cox’s Bazar through the proposed plan of JRP 2022. It is targeting 8,760 Rohingya population living below SMEB (Survival Minimum Expenditure Basket) covering 45% of the total 19,327 SMEB living in 7 blocks of Camp-4 (Ref: JMNSA2021) and also covering 27% of People in Need (PIN) (32,312, Ref: FSS_JRP22_Population Numbers) in the same geographic area. Similarly, the plan is targeting 2,871 HHs of 9 wards living in Jaliapalong Union under same Upazila and same district covering 30% of the total population in need in host community (ref: FSS_JRP22_Population Numbers). The variation in targeting Rohingya and host community population is due to the dissimilarity in needs and feasibility of food security activity in the both communities. The project integrates various livelihood activities to be implemented by block and ward based associations comprising both men and women. There are main 11 activities planned to be implemented. Out of these 5 activities are in Rohingya community and 6 will be implemented in the host community. The activities for Rohingya communities are 1) Provide necessary training and input supports to conduct on-farm activities like production of Vermi-Compost, promote Roof Gardening, Seed Bank, Bio-pesticide production, and reforestation 2) Provide necessary training and input support to conduct Off-Farm activities like Tailoring, Handicrafts, Block-printing and Mask Production 3) Organize the target people under an organizational portfolio 4) Create social cohesion & market linkage 5) Disaster Preparedness. For the host community the activities are 6) Provide necessary Training and input supports to conduct on-farm activities like rearing milking Cow, Duck, Chicken, Pigeon, Horticulture, vegetable gardening, Seed bank, bio-pesticide and Pit compost 7) Provide necessary training and input support to conduct Off-Farm activities like Tailoring, Handicrafts, & Block-printing 8) Provide Conditional Cash Support to conduct Off-Farm business 9) Organize host community beneficiaries under an organizational portfolio.10) Create social Cohesion & market linkage and 11) Disaster Preparedness. The project emphasized organic farming to promote climate smart agricultural activities in the both communities. Capacity building of the target people has also been prioritized to ensure sustainable self-reliance. The off-farm activities are considered mainly to develop vocational skills which they would be able to utilize with social cohesion and community approach under the leadership of the association. The project will create market linkages to promote gender inclusive opportunities among the both communities. The project is environment friendly and build capacity of the beneficiaries to increase rapid climate change resilience and preparedness to face possible disaster. The project will promote self-reliance of the beneficiaries and contribute them to reduce relief dependency. There are some specific focuses which include a) design gender transformative activity and address barriers to social cohesion b) encourage leadership and entrepreneurship within the community. c) Create sense of self-identity and self-esteem within the community d) engage the community members in self-reliant activity through providing training, technical and inputs support e) social cohesion by- inviting local community traders for marketing, creating mutually linked activities between the host and the Rohingya community, organising exposure visits, monthly coordination meeting, annual meeting and so on. The project is inclusive to adolescent, youth, young and adult men and women of both the communities.

1 - Identified Needs

As of September 2021, almost 1 Million of Rohingya refugee has been crossed the Bangladeshi boarder from Myanmar and get shelter in 32 heavily congested camps in Ukhiya and Teknaf sub-districts of Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh (UNHCR, Population factsheet, September 2021) and this displacement of Rohingya Refugee is increasing intense pressure and competition in the host communities of Bangladesh for local resources like food, labour market As the crisis is now into the fifth year, the refugee population in camps remains highly depend on humanitarian assistance and almost all households have unmet multi-sectorial needs because of limited access to self-reliance activities and also impacts of COVID-19. J-MSNA (Joint Multi Sectoral Need Assessment), 2021 reveal that 72% of households require food security/livelihood as one of the top priority needs out of 3 in 2022. The Refugee Influx Emergency Vulnerability Assessment (REVA 4, WFP April 2021) reveals that overall vulnerability has been increased since 2019 to 2020 across all populations in the both Rohingya and Host community, and 24% of Rohingya and 30% of host community population have moved into higher vulnerability. The project thus covers 27% of the total target population in camp-4, living in 7 Blocks (A-G). The project is also targeting 30% of the total population of host community living in 9 wards of Jaliapalong Union of Ukhiya under Cox’s Bazar district. The project is very much inclusive to age, gender, and disability by targeting 1,752 HHs in Rohingya Community and 2,871 HHs in host community comprising 20% of age below 18 years, 60% of age 18-59 years, 10% of above 59 years, 6% disability and 4% from unknown sex and age in camp-4 and Jaliapalong Union with more than 50% female participation in all age groups.

2 - Activities or outputs

The Project plans for a total of 11 Activities. The first 5 activities (1-5) would be implemented in Rohingya Community (RC) and the other 6 activities (6-11) will be implemented in the host community. The activities for Rohingya communities are: 1. Provide necessary training and input supports to conduct on-farm activities like Vermi-Compost, Roof Gardening, Seed Bank, Bio-pesticide, and reforestation to 2,426 HHs, 2. Provide necessary training and input Support to conduct Off-Farm activities like Tailoring, Handicrafts, Block-printing and Mask Production to 326 HHs, 3. Organize Livelihood entrepreneurs (Adolescents, youths and adults in RC) under an organizational portfolio, 4. Create Cohesion & market linkage, 5. Disaster Preparedness for Rohingya Community.The activities for Host Community (HC) are 6. Provide necessary Training and input supports to conduct on-farm activities like rearing Milking cow, Duck, Chicken, Pigeon, Horticulture, vegetable gardening, Seed bank, bio-pesticide and Pit compost to 2709 HHs 7. Provide necessary training and input Support to conduct Off-Farm activities like Tailoring, Handicrafts, & Block-printing to 135 HHs, 8. Provide Conditional Cash Support to conduct Off-Farm business to 27 HHs, 9. Organize Livelihood entrepreneurs under an organizational portfolio, 10. Create Cohesion & market linkage for host community entrepreneurs, 11. Disaster preparedness for host community entrepreneurs.
