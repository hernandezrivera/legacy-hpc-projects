Project Summary

In November 2020, the outbreak of the Tigray conflict resulted in a dire shortage of vital supplies, leading to a dramatic increase in humanitarian needs in an intense conflict setting. Preliminary humanitarian assessments and consultations with the Government of Ethiopia and relevant humanitarian stakeholders highlighted the limits on accessibility for logistical services. Despite vigorous efforts to overcome these operational constraints, the challenges faced with in-land transport persist and have hindered the delivery of much-needed humanitarian assistance. As of 18 March 2022, no humanitarian convoys have entered Tigray since 14 December 2021 due to significant access constraints along the Semera-Mekelle humanitarian corridor, the last remaining inland corridor to Tigray. While discussions and negotiations for the resumption of convoy movement through the Semera-Mekelle corridor are ongoing, the humanitarian community requires an immediate solution for the delivery of lifesaving cargo given the dire humanitarian situation.

In response to these ongoing challenges and in the face of uncertainties with regards to when the inland access constraints will be eased, the United Nations Humanitarian Air Service (UNHAS) and the Logistics Cluster have been supporting the humanitarian community in facilitating airlifts of life-saving prioritized cargo from Addis Ababa to Tigray. Since January 2022, the Logistics Cluster has facilitated airlifts of approximately 200 metric tons of lifesaving prioritized medical and nutrition cargo on behalf of twelve partners.

As part of this effort, both UNHAS and the Logistics Cluster would like to propose a temporary increase in air transport of life-saving and critical humanitarian cargos for a limited number of rotations from Addis Ababa to Tigray. This will ensure that life-saving cargos reach Tigray as an exceptional measure in an extraordinary circumstance marked by the lack of access into the region. At the time of writing, UNHAS remains one of the only ways to access some remote locations safely in the country, operating in isolated areas where humanitarian assistance is critically needed.

1. - Projects activities

The Logistics Cluster will work closely with UNHAS to deliver the following activites: UNHAS: - Continue using the Boeing 737 and Q400 aircrafts with a 13 MT capacity and 5 MT capacity for rotations from Addis Ababa to Mekelle, Tigray.- Chartering an aircraft with a potential 15 MT capacity per rotation from Addis Ababa to Mekelle, Tigray- Exploring airlift of cargo from Addis Ababa to Shire. - Transportation of humanitarian cargo from Addis Ababa to Mekelle. Logistics Cluster:- Collection of prioritized cargo lists of life-saving humanitarian cargos, in close collaboration with the identified clusters and the oversight of the Inter Cluster Coordination Group and OCHA- Transporting cargo from Addis Ababa to Mekele on behalf of the humanitarian community, free of charge to the user organization.- Transportation of fuel from Addis Ababa to Mekelle- Providing guidance in the clearance processes of cargo for airlift. - Loading and off-loading of cargos at the airports
