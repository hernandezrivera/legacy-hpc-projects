Project Summary

The project is designed to be carried out in partnership with Public Aid Society (PAS), aiming at ensuring availability of acceptable and quality essential healthcare services to vulnerable communities in Gaza, through timely delivery of safe surgical services.
Project includes:
- Conducting surgeries (Minor, moderate and Major) for 1,020 Male, Female, Children patients in the specialties of General surgery, ENT, Urology, Pediatric and Orthopedic.
- Procurement of medical supplies and disposables of surgeries, medications, PPEs for the staff and Hygiene and infection control materials.
Targeted patients will be selected from updated waiting lists of MoH hospitals in Gaza City.
Coordination will take place with all related parties and other institutions providing similar services to avoid duplication.
The proposed intervention is expected to last for the duration of the project (9 months) and to benefit a total of 1,020 direct beneficiaries. Moreover, its impact will last beyond the project's period as it will ensure avoiding deterioration in health status of people targeted in a safe manner and will minimize the load on MoH hospitals.

1 - Needs

In March 2020, WHO officially declared COVID-19 a pandemic, across 110 countries which have a significant effect on whole the world. Gaza Strip where a harsh economic situation, extreme poverty and bad health conditions, exacerbated by years of blockade and siege is considered one of the most areas affected negatively. In August 2020, Gaza was placed on high alert after it reported its first cases of the coronavirus outside of the quarantine facilities. A lockdown took hold in Gaza strip. Strict measures have been taken by the Ministry of Health (MoH) at the primary health care level and the secondary care level as resources are being diverted to respond to the growing COVID-19 related needs. In the Gaza Strip, according to health authorities, only seventeen primary healthcare centers are functioning out of the 52 and two health centers have been converted to quarantine areas. Four hundred doctors, nurses and administrative staff were mobilized to support the quarantine centers. Meanwhile, breast cancer screening has stopped and limited only to diagnostic services. Routine NCD patient care has also been postponed, in addition to early child growth and development services, oral and dental health services and physiotherapy. Meanwhile, in August 2020, all hospitals in Gaza have postponed elective surgeries and outpatient services. More than 4,000 elective surgeries have been postponed due to preparedness measures for possible management of COVID-19 cases. Additionally, about 1,300 medical cases cannot be referred to hospitals outside Gaza due to crossing closure. (Health Cluster report, 2020). According to the HNO 2021, Patients who need Access to specialized services within Gaza are considered as a priority and stated that patients in need of elective surgeries and in the waiting list are the most vulnerable group in need of health intervention. Despite implementing such interventions by other international & local institutions, the need still exists & the number of patients increases steadily especially in light of the fact that MoH allocated a large part of its facilities to deal with emergency & COVID-19 cases. After communication and coordination with MoH and other service providers, it is found that QRCS’s proposed intervention will complement the role played by UHWC, Al Ahli Arab Hospital and other service providers who cover patients in other geographic locations (e.g. Northern and Southern governorates) and other specialties (e.g. Eye surgeries, Gynecology and Obstetrics, Minimal Invasive Surgeries …etc.). It is worth mentioning that the project responds to the Health Cluster’s Objective no.2: “Ensure the availability, accessibility, acceptability and quality of essential lifesaving health services to vulnerable communities in Gaza and West Bank, including through health” and also QRCS slogan "Saving Lives and Preserving Dignity".

2 - Activities

Project's activities to include: 1. Conducting 1,020 elective surgeries for patients (women, men, girls, boys, elderly, PwDs) including: a. 300 E.N.T surgeries (50 Minor, 100 Moderate and 150 Major); b. 100 Orthopedic and tendon repair surgeries (50 Minor and 50 Moderate); c. 270 General Surgery (50 Minor, 70 Moderate, 150 Major); d. 200 Urology surgeries (50 Minor, 50 Moderate and 100 Major); e. 150 Pediatric surgeries (100 Minor, and 50 Major). 2. Procurement of medical supplies for surgeries, disposables, medications and PPEs for the staff and Hygiene and infection control materials.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

Planned intervention is designed in a manner that guarantees dignity and respect of patients regardless of age, sex, religion, ethnicity or political affiliation. Services are to be provided through specialized medical teams whom safety will be guaranteed through the provision of all necessary PPE and Hygiene kits and infection control materials thus will help maintain patients’ good health status with minimum risk of getting COVID-19 transmitted to them. The project is going to provide services to 1191 patients (265 Men, 255 Women, 250 Boys, 250 Girls) who will receive equitable services without partiality or discrimination taking into account each patient individual characteristics and special needs, especially individuals with disabilities, children and women as the most vulnerable in the society and who need special attention and protection depending on Human dignity, confidentiality and non-discrimination. Consent form will be used giving the patients the rights to decide to get or reject receiving the services.Patient consultations, documentations and records will be saved to be private and confidential. To ensure Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, Project related contracts will include standard clause on PSEA in accordance with IASC principles and guidance and also Project’s staff will directly and in directly engage in the HCT oPt PSEA network.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

Project is designed to be implemented at a well trusted hospital located in an accessible and safe location (Center of Gaza City) targeting in need vulnerable individuals and communities without partiality or discrimination. Moreover, the project's design takes into consideration the principles of protection stipulated in the humanitarian charter including the right to live with dignity, the right to receive assistance and the right to protection which align with QRCS slogan (Saving Lives and Preserving Dignity) and QRCS fundamental principles (Independence, Volunteering Service, Neutrality, Unity, Non-Bias, Universality and Humanity).
