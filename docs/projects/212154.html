Project Summary

In 2024 and 2025, based on lessons learned from years of protection programming, COOPI plans to address protection concerns identified during implementation and needs assessments with affected communities. This initiative aligns with all strategic objectives outlined by the protection sector for the 2023/2024 strategy. The project strategy is firmly grounded in the findings from a comprehensive multi-sectoral needs assessment conducted by COOPI in September and December 2023, highlighting the urgent need for child protection, Gender-Based Violence (GBV), and psychosocial support programs in targeted areas. The 2024 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) summary underscores significant protection needs, with alarming stress levels reported by 60% of women, 61.5% of men, 34% of girls, and 31% of boys. With approximately 8.5 million Syrians in need of GBV assistance, notably 93% being women and girls, the strategy also acknowledges challenges from economic crises, conflict, earthquakes, and limited access to essential goods and services. Concerns such as intimate partner violence, domestic violence, sexual violence, are particularly alarming. The prevalence of forced marriages, especially among divorced and widowed women and girls, underscores their vulnerability and barriers in accessing basic rights. Moreover, the persistent threat of explosive ordnance contamination poses additional risks to lives, often leading to accidents during everyday activities.
To ensure equal access for boys and girls to quality child protection interventions, COOPI supports community-based child protection responses in targeted locations. This includes providing MHPSS services for girls, boys, and caregivers, parenting programs, awareness-raising, direct case management, and establishing committees for community participation in preventing and responding to child protection issues. Furthermore, the project plans to build on successes providing speech, physical, and mental health therapy to vulnerable individuals. This impactful service, combined with the provision of medical in-kind assistance, alters the lives of the most vulnerable people living with disability and/or chronic injury. Mental therapy complements the case management services.
The project also emphasizes promoting and implementing community-led initiatives in each targeted locality as an efficient tool for preventing and responding to protection risks. Building upon identified needs, COOPI plans to deliver comprehensive GBV services tailored to specific needs of affected populations. Prevention initiatives will be supported through targeted awareness campaigns on GBV-related topics, alongside the establishment of women community committees to foster dialogue and support systems. In direct response to GBV, COOPI will offer specialized case management services for survivors, ensuring they receive necessary care and support. These interventions will be complemented by the distribution of dignity kits to women and girls, providing vital resources to address basic needs and enhance resilience. The Mine Action (MA) activities proposed in the project will be centered on prevention, particularly through explosive ordnance risk education initiatives. These efforts aim to equip communities with knowledge and skills needed to identify and mitigate dangers posed by explosive remnants of war.
An integral focus for COOPI is the operation of Community Centers, centralizing services in hard-to-reach areas. Static centers, complemented by mobile outreach, ensure assistance reaches the most vulnerable populations in rural, underserved areas. COOPI aims to strengthen its area-based approach, reinforcing internal and external referral mechanisms across all sectors. Protection mainstreaming is ensured, with particular attention to safe distribution modalities, a gender, age, and diversity approach. Additionally, Prevention and Eradication of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) is a cross-sectoral priority.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

COOPI's planned activities presented for the Humanitarian Response Plan for 2024 and 2025, aim to address pressing protection concerns identified within affected communities. The intervention targets the most critical protection issues in Syria, spanning all Areas of Responsibility, including General Protection, Child Protection, Mine Action, and Gender-Based Violence. The description of needs and COOPI's proposed responses are outlined below. Identified Needs and COOPI's Response:Child Protection:• Child Labour Concerns: In the six months prior to the assessment, 100% of respondents highlighted child labour as a concern hindering regular school attendance. COOPI's actions include:o Community-based interventions targeting child labour.o Mental health and psychosocial support for affected children.o Parenting programs and awareness-raising to prevent early marriages.o Child Protection Case Management for individual support.o Support for existing committees to address child protection issues.Vulnerable Populations:• Psychosocial Support: Recognizing the need for psychosocial support, COOPI will:o Provide ongoing mental health services.o Establish community-led initiatives for well-being promotion.• Urgent Medical Situations: COOPI will address urgent medical needs by:o Providing medical in-kind assistance and therapy.o Strengthening referral mechanisms for medical care.• Households Led by Women: Special focus on women-led households:o Capacity building for women-led committees addressing protection risks.Specific Vulnerabilities:• Older People with Chronic Illnesses and People with Disabilities (PWDs):o Targeted support for mental health and chronic illness management.o Provision of rehabilitation and medical assistive devices.• Women and Survivors of Gender-Based Violence (GBV):o Specialized interventions including direct Case Management.o Physical and mental health therapy for survivors.o Inclusion initiatives to tackle integration challenges.• Girls at Risk of Early Marriages:o Prevention strategies such as awareness campaigns and dialogues.o Support for girls' access to education and opportunities.Innovative Approaches:• Sports for Development: Using sports for positive change and inclusion.• Cash for Protection: Responsive action to mitigate protection risks.• Hybrid Outreach: Mobile teams reaching rural and underserved populations. COOPI's comprehensive approach aims to create a protective environment, addressing diverse and deeply entrenched social and protection challenges within communities. Prioritizing community participation, specialized interventions, and innovative solutions, COOPI strives to safeguard the rights and well-being of vulnerable individuals, particularly children, women, survivors of violence, and persons with disabilities in 2024 and 2025.
