Project Summary

In the context of the Strategic Partnership - Responding to GBV in Acute Emergency and Protracted Humanitarian Crises(2019-2021)-theIRC wishes to further invest in these existing pillars, as well as amplifying the generation and sharing of learning, both internally and externally, from across the IRC’s WPE global response in over 30 acute, protracted and recovery phase humanitarian crisis across the world. The proposed pillars are:
1. GBV Response, Preparedness and Recovery: This pillar aims to “Strengthen preparedness, prevention and response to GBV and protect women and girls in emergencies and protracted humanitarian crises”.
2. GBV Emergency Response: This pillar aims to ensure “Women and girls, including survivors of GBV, have access to life-saving services and face reduced risks from violence”.
3. GBV Policy and Advocacy: This pillar aims to ensure “The needs of womena nd girls in acute and protracted emergencies are given increased prominence and priority across global humanitarian frameworks and policies”.
4. GBV Learning: This pillar aims to “Consolidate and share learning from across the Strategic Partnership and WPE programming to promote adaptive, inclusive GBV programming which is accountable to women and girls in acute and protracted emergencies”.
5.Management,Coordination and Quality Assurance.

Besoins / Needs

Cameroon is categorised under category 2 based on its national-level INFORM risk index rating. However, the Far North region targeted by the proposed project has an INFORM risk index rating of 7.3, putting it on par with countries categorised under category 1. The targeted geographical areas –Mora and Amchidé, two sites within the Mayo Sava district –were selected on the basis of being among the most conflict-affected areas of the Far North. Specifically, the IRC will target vulnerable women and girls for all activities. For community education and awareness raising activities the IRC will seek to identify influential members within target communities who are well placed to change social norms that stigmatize survivors. Women and girls will be reached through: GBV Case Management services 600 (200/year); life skill and psychosocial support activities: 2,700 (900/year); Girl Shine: 150 (30/year 1, 60 for years 2 and 3); Vocational Training: 600 (200/year); Distribution of risk reduction support material (dignity kits): 1,500 (500/year); and the wider community will be reached through GBV community education/awareness: 4,500 (1500/year). This project "strategic partnership" (SP) is informed by the IRC 2020 global strategy that seeks to achieve evidence driven, gender transformative outcomes across health, safety, power, education and economic wellbeing outcomes. This pillar will contribute to health, safety, power and economic outcomes areas guided by the WPE Theory of Change (ToC)and evidence based, field tested program approaches. The IRC continues to invest in high quality, practitioner informed research on women and girls protection and empowerment in humanitarian settings. As research projects are completed, learning will be shared with WPE teams within the Strategic Partnership through the annual learning forum. Women and girls protection and empowerment programming aims to ensure “Women and girls pursue their potential, free from violence and inequality” across all outcomes and sectors.Women and girls have inherent strength and power that they use to transform the world around them in spite of being born with unequal life chances and the constant threat of gender-based violence. WPE programming is informed by evidence that VAWG is systemic and is used to maintain the subordinate status of women and girls; VAWG is a primary obstacle to women and girls pursuing their potential as equals; and ending violence requires expanding women and girls’ space to transform themselves and their world. The proposed interventions will therefore contribute towards addressing the systemic contributions to VAWG and expanding the space for women and girls to transform themselves while responding to the cases of GBV. The overall objective for the GBV recovery and response pillar will be to strengthen preparedness, prevention and response to GBV and protect women and girls in emergencies and protracted humanitarian crises.
