Project Summary

Purpose:
This project aims to empower women and girls affected by crisis in the North West region of Cameroon through a holistic approach that prioritizes life-saving interventions. By providing comprehensive support in the areas of GBV prevention, mental health and psychosocial support, vocational training, and economic empowerment, the project aims to equip women and girls with the tools and resources they need to heal, build resilience, and rebuild their lives.
Target:
500 women and girls affected by crisis in the North West of Cameroon, with a specific focus on GBV survivors.
Key Activities:
GBV Case Management:
Provide access to quality MHPSS services, including individual and group counseling, trauma-informed care, and referrals to other essential services.
Offer legal aid and support to survivors seeking justice.
Support survivors in accessing medical care and sexual and reproductive health services.
Safety Planning:
Support survivors in developing safety plans to protect themselves from further harm.
Empowering and Building Resilience:
Community-Level GBV Prevention:
Conduct sensitization campaigns and awareness-raising activities to address harmful social norms and promote gender equality.
Engage community leaders and influencers in GBV prevention efforts.
Support the formation of women's groups and networks for peer support and empowerment.
Mental Health and Psychosocial Support:
Provide trauma-informed individual and group counseling to help survivors cope with the emotional impact of violence.
Offer social and emotional learning programs for children affected by the crisis.
Train community members to provide basic mental health support.
Livelihoods and Economic Empowerment:
Vocational Training:
Provide market-driven vocational training in sectors with high demand, such as renewable energy, IT, agro-pastoral agriculture, and fashion design.
Offer literacy and numeracy skills training to enhance employability.
Assist trainees in accessing job placements or starting their own businesses.
Economic Support:
Provide financial assistance to help women and girls start or expand their businesses.
Offer mentorship and business development support.
Facilitate access to markets and resources.
Expected Outcomes:
Life-Saving Interventions:
Increased access to quality MHPSS services for GBV survivors.
Increased awareness of GBV and improved access to justice for survivors.
Reduced risk of further victimization for survivors and their families.
Empowering and Building Resilience:
Reduced stigma and discrimination against GBV survivors.
Improved mental health and well-being of women and girls affected by crisis.
Increased social support and networks for women and girls.
Livelihoods and Economic Empowerment:
Increased employment rate for women and girls who complete vocational training.
Increased income and financial security for women and girls.
Increased participation of women in the local economy.
Indicators of Success:
Number of women and girls accessing GBV case management services.
Number of women and girls participating in mental health and psychosocial support programs.
Number of women and girls completing vocational training and securing employment.
Number of women and girls receiving economic support and establishing businesses.
Increase in income and financial security for women and girls.
Sustainability:
The project will adopt a sustainable approach by:
Building the capacity of local organizations to provide essential services to women and girls.
Partnering with community leaders and influencers to ensure long-term commitment to GBV prevention efforts.
Empowering women and girls to become advocates for their own rights and well-being.
Building resilience within communities to withstand future crises.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The ongoing crisis in the North-West region of Cameroon has created a complex humanitarian situation with a multitude of assessed needs. The most pressing needs fall into the following categories:Physical security: The conflict has led to widespread violence and insecurity, with civilians often caught in the crossfire. There is a need for protection from armed violence, including sexual and gender-based violence (GBV). Human rights violations: Human rights abuses are widespread, including arbitrary arrests, torture, and extrajudicial killings. There is a need for accountability for these abuses and the protection of human rights defenders. GBV prevention and response: GBV is a major concern in the region, with women and girls particularly vulnerable. There is a need for prevention programs, as well as access to essential services for survivors, including medical care, psychosocial support, and legal assistance.
