Project Summary

Violence holds back social and economic development in Nigeria reducing investments and jobs, destroying individuals’ means of livelihoods and public properties, preventing children from going to school and reducing access to health care. Since 2014, the security situation in the Northeast (NE) has deteriorated drastically, with the intensification of the insurgency by Armed Opposition Groups. The resulting humanitarian crisis has involved significant loss of lives, livelihoods, productive assets and infrastructures, and thousands of people killed while others displaced from their homes and farming communities to urban areas to avoid being killed, therefore hindering agricultural activities which further limits livelihoods activities.
In northern Nigeria, more than eight million people are in need of food security and livelihood intervention including protection. Nkafamiya Rescue Mission partnership (NRM) will implement a livelihood and economic inclusion (LEI) project in Adamawa state. The project will ensure Five Thousand (5000) vulnerable conflict affected households with 70 percent of the targets being women and youths to benefits from the following outcomes:
Outcome 1: Improve agricultural production for smallholder farmers by use of modern farming methods and strengthened links to market actors for improved household income and food consumption through;
Outcome 2: Establish and sustained Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) for vulnerable households in Adamawa State for a better means of livelihood and economic independence
Outcome 3: Engage Target beneficiaries in the agriculture, livestock and fishery-oriented livelihood opportunities.

2 - Activities

• Baseline and household vulnerability assessment• Engagement with community stakeholders to introduce the project and seek their support• Mapping of Market actors and relevant Government Ministries and Agencies• Registration of project participants• Strengthen agriculture and livestock training guidelines/curricula with the Ministry of Agriculture and train the Agricultural Development Project (ADP) staff on skills gaps • Provide complementary training for farmers on Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) in collaboration with Ministry of Agriculture and ADP• Distribute cash to farmers for purchase of farm inputs • Facilitate direct links between farmers, farm inputs suppliers and off takers • Link farmers to modern farming service providers such as Hello Tractors to access mechanization services • Market analysis to determine types of viable businesses putting into consideration the locations context• Registration of project participants• Training of trainers (ToT) to Nkafamiya Rescue Mission staff and relevant Government Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) by a certified consultant on Entrepreneurship, Employability, financial literacy and life-skills to facilitate/step down the training to IDPs and Returnees.• Development of Business Plan for 5000 IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities.• Sign MOU with qualified financial institution for cash disbursement to participants• Account opening for participants• Disbursement of cash to vulnerable households for establishment of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise to start Income Generating Activity (IGAs.• Training/group formation of the participants on Village savings and loans Association (VSLA)• Organize MSME clinic to link IDPs and returnees (women and Youths) who are into micro enterprise with relevant Government regulatory bodies such as CAC, NAFDAC, Departments of Cooperatives, SON, etc to access firsthand information on their activities and how to access help to formalize and upgrade their businesses.• Beneficiaries Routine business monitoring, mentoring and coaching, monitoring of VSLAs meeting for 40 to 52 weeks• Distribution of shares by the VSLAs groups to members after 52 weeks• Business cash top up to beneficiaries for IGAs expansion with the help of financial service providers.• Advocacy and Awareness on Agriculture, livestock and livelihood activities at community level for acceptance and participation.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Staff of Nkafamiya Rescue Mission together with relevant Government MDAs using the experiential learning methodology will conduct the capacity building training on life skills and entrepreneurship to the participants for sustainability purpose. The Cash assistance for the establishment of Micro enterprise and purchase of farm inputs for the smallholder farmers will be distributed to the vulnerable households, with the support of qualified financial institutions through the participants’ accounts. For locations where there are no/nonfunctional Banks, the financial institution will send their agents to disburse physical cash to the participants with thorough supervision from staff of Nkafamiya Rescue Mission. This model is used over many years and in a range of contexts, including in North East Nigeria. Nkafamiya Rescue Mission commitment to collaborating with government Ministries, Departments and Agencies for sustainability is still an existing implementation strategy.
