Project Summary

The project aims to address critical and urgent life-saving needs for winterization and protection of the most vulnerable internally displaced persons several camps of Harim and Salqin in Idleb governorate during winter 2023.
The project will target 3000 IDP families with a total of 15,000 individuals disaggregated roughly as(3,300 men, 3,600 women, 4,000 boys, 4,100 girls) in order to improve their ability to face the burdens of winter.
The project includes the distribution of the following in-kind items in line with SNFI activity "Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs including heating fuel":
1. Stove kits.
2. Eco-friendly biofuel through 3 rounds during December, January, and February months.
Each family will receive 250 KG per round of olive-pit biomass fuel for heating, they will also receive heating and cooking stoves of good quality and applicable to the heating fuel type that will be distributed.
The project team will prepare beneficiaries' lists according to specific criteria for vulnerability, including giving priority to families of orphans, families headed by women, families with disabled persons, elderly, patients, and a big number of children, and families with breadwinner who has difficulties in securing income.
IAC non-food item response will be based on the SNFI cluster guideline for the distribution of stoves and heating fuel in IDP sites.
Thus, the most vulnerable groups among the targeted sites will be strengthened and supported to face the difficult living conditions that increase in the winter season, by ensuring warmth.

3 - Needs

According to HNO 2023, as the security environment remaining somewhat volatile, deterioration in economic conditions, compounded by a depreciating Syrian pound, has led to further intensification of the severity of need for NFI support, particularly during harsh winter seasons when access to basic NFIs is considered lifesaving.As such, the overall NFI PiN has increased by 5 per cent to 5.7 million (from 4.90 million in 2022).In northwest and northeast Syria, 2.05 million IDPs reside in last resort sites, a marginal increase from 2.02 million in the previous year.Such locations lack crucial infrastructure and rely on emergency shelter solutions and continued humanitarian support.IAC has conducted a rapid needs assessment in many camps in several locations as Harim (severity 3), Salqin (severity 4) according scale of WOS_SNFI_PIN&Severity. According to IAC team we found: - Harim:17 camps 14935 IDPs (2820 HHs).- Salqin :9 camps 6474 IDPs (1216 HHs).Although most of the displaced received some NFI assistance, but about 50% were not fully satisfied with it, mainly due to insufficient quantity or quality of it, and receiving it once or a few times in irregular periods, 60% do not have the ability to secure their needs on a permanent basis and sufficient, especially safe heating materials in winter.So IAC is planning to meet these needs.

4 - Implementation of activities

The project activities are:- Beneficiaries registration: the project team will prepare lists of beneficiaries who will be selected according to specific criteria for vulnerability, including giving priority to families of orphans; families headed by women; families with disabled persons, elderly, patients, and a big number of children; and families with breadwinner who has difficulties in securing income.- Contracting for the purchase of stoves and Olive-pit fuel.- Distribution process: specific distribution points will be selected in coordination with the local authorities of the targeted areas and the administrations of the targeted camps, and the beneficiaries will be informed of the details of the distribution process 3-7 days before the event, and during the distribution, the field team will verify the beneficiaries’ data previously registered within the beneficiaries’ tables before handing them the assistance, and female staff will be assigned to be present during the distribution process to the women to ensure access and protection. In addition, beneficiaries will sign checklists confirming their receipt of the assistance.- Monitoring and Evaluation throughout all implementation steps including Price Monitoring and Market Assessment.Field MEAL team will also conduct the PDM after each round of distribution to measure the beneficiaries' satisfaction regarding the the quality of the distributed items, the field team behaviors, and the timing of the response.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

To prevent any PSEA issue from occurring during the implementation period of the project activities, especially the activities in which the project staff are in direct contact with the beneficiaries, such as the distribution team. All field activities have been reviewed and assessed for risk factors for PSEA with the use of the PSEA Network Field and Organizational Mitigation and Prevention Checklists and the following prevention and mitigation measures will be taken: 1- IAC will make sure that All staff, and volunteers understand and sign the Code of Conduct. 2- Field staff with direct contact with beneficiaries will receive PSEA training 3- IAC will review internal reporting mechanisms and ensure all staff know about reporting mechanisms 4- female staff will always be present at the distribution site and involved in the distribution process 5-Benefeciaries will be given clear information on timing and content of distribution 6- Beneficiaries will be given information that aid is free7- PSEA messaging and awareness raising will be done though pasting PSEA posters and placing PSEA banners at the distribution site or other service place.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:- Access to specific Sub-districts remains open.- The items arrived to our warehouses on time.- No drastic changes on the Bab-Alhawa border regulations. Risks:Risk 1: escalation the airstrikes in targeted areas. Mitigation 1: Conducting the distribution via many micro distribution points meaning don't have any high number of collected people in the same place in addition to cancel the day of distribution when mentioning any fighter jet hovering in the sky. Risk 2: Turkish Government decided to close the Bab Alhawa border for urgent issue. Mitigation 2: 2.1. put this issue on the contract with the supplier and put a condition that after signing the contract if Bab-Alhawa closed for any issue, the supplier will provide the same quantities of agreed items in Side Syria with the same specification from local resources. 2.2. Immediate contracting with multi suppliers inside Syria to provide the same items with the same quantities. Risk 3: Delay from the supplier on delivering the items to warehouse.Mitigation 3: Put a high penalty for the delay in delivering on the supplier in the signed contract, so the supplier will be more focus on his commitment with the delivery date.
