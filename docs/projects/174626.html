Project Summary

The action aims to : (GO) "Strengthen national capacities for humanitarian mine action to reduce the threats posed by explosive ordnance in order to guarantee a safer environment for the populations of the regions of central Mali: Mopti and Segou". To achieve this, the action plans to : (SO1) "Provide individual assistance to explosive ordnance survivors by setting up a referral system", and : (SO2) "Support local communities and institutions in the prevention of risks related to explosive ordnance through awareness-raising and capacity building".
This proposed Action aims to reinforce the capacities of each partners. DRC/DDG will be implementing all activities directly in Koro and Douentza where DRC/DDG is implementing protection response through ECHO and SDC funding. IMADEL will be implementing activities directly in all other supported circles: Niono, Macina, Bankass, Bandiagara, Tenenkou, Youwarou, Mopti, Djenné. To ensure quality of activities and build IMADEL’s capacities DRC/DDG will provide technical support to IMADEL through trainings of trainers and will conduct conjoint visits in all other circles where DRC/DDG has access.
