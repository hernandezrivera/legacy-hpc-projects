Project Summary

Support the Resilience of Syrian Women and youth in Aleppo by Providing small grants:
The project design is based on the following:
Reviving the local market, and enhancing the livelihoods of IDPs and Host Communities (65% IDPs, 35 % host communities, 70% women and 30 young men) in Aleppo governorate (Jarablus District) as following:
1- (150 HHs) in (Jarablus, Tal Elamara, Zoghra, Lower Um Rotha, Jame l, Upper Um Rotha, Lower Jarablus, Marma Elhajar) will receive Training on (local market analysis, economic feasibility, budget, project management, marketing).
2- ( 50 HHs) in Ghandorah will receive Training on (local market analysis, economic feasibility, budget, project management, marketing).
3- (100 HHs from 150 HH) in (Jarablus, Tal Elamara, Zoghra, Lower Um Rotha, Jame l, Upper Um Rotha, Lower Jarablus, Marma Elhajar) will receive samll grants ( 80 new small projects and 20 small Stumbling projects).
4- (30 HHs from 50 HH) in Ghandorah will receive samll grants ( 20 new small projects and 10 small Stumbling projects).
WSA needs assessments have identified significant need for both IDPs and host families who have lost livelihoods due to ongoing conflict or displacement, and as a result have reduced purchasing power and incomes that hinder the economic stability.
It is critical to support these households to begin to restore lost livelihoods.
Key interventions will include:
1- Training 200 beneficiaries on (local market analysis, economic feasibility, budget, project management, marketing).
- 200 Beneficiaries in region will take the lead of conducting Market Assessment to determine what are the elements, gaps and the opportunities for livelihoods, and interview employers through employers' survey, so the beneficiaries themselves study and analyze local markets in order to determine what is suitable to them and to acquire sense of ownership of these projects)
3- Funding acceptable projects as following:
- Funding 100 new small projects in each region for Average value $ 2000.
- Funding 30 small Stumbling projects in each region for Average value $ 1000.
WSA will select the list of suppliers in the local market, and then contracted with them to secure project requirements according to the appropriate price and quality, and then hand them over to the beneficiaries.
WSA will identify, verify and profile target beneficiaries through community leader, Volunteer and local council (where the long list will be prepared). Then the short list will be identified by the criteria of selecting beneficiaries, considering vulnerable people, including; IDPs, hosting families with vulnerable women , and female headed HHs as priority.
Throughout the duration of the project a market monitoring system will be put in place in all targeted areas.
To ensure that the project does not disrupt or affect the markets systems and balance.
1. 150 HHs in Jarablus and surrounding villages (410 female and 340 male), approximately ( 130 men, 202women, 208 girls, and 200 boys) (the women and young and their children).
2. 50 HHs in Ghandorah (136 female and 114 male), approximately (54 men, 71 women, 65 girls, and 60 boys). (the women and men and their children).
WSA monitors will collect information for the market monitoring during spot checks monitoring visits at the local shops involved in the project. Traders will be warned ahead of the project start that spot checks can be conducted by WSA so as to discourage any forms of corruption.
WSA will also establish a complaint mechanism (hotline), and a participatory monitoring system through working with local authorities, beneficiary representatives. in Jarablus and Ghandorah, All these measures to mitigate the risks of fraud and to assure the gender sensitivity.
WSA is going to invest the coordin. approvals with Turkish Government through the ongoing work in SALAM center 5 in Jarablus (Safe Space for Women and girls). (providing Women and girls Protection + Micro Credits for women and youth).

4 - Needs

The crisis in Syria continues to strain the sociology-economic foundations of Syrian people and the opportunities and abilities for the affected population to find decent sources of income are highly constrained . Out of around 11 million potential members of the workforce (between 18 and 65 years of age), more than half (3.4 million males and 3.1 million females) are unable to find sustained, reliable and decent work. The assessment also indicates that 55 per cent of total males and 75 per cent of total females report less income from all sources than they need to maintain a moderate living standard. In addition to a lack of working capital to re-establish the household economy, there is a severe lack of access to training for employable skills especially females. The economic hardship and scarcity of opportunities to find decent livelihoods may lead populations particularly the youth to resort to harmful (extremist groups,crime, and trafficking) HNO 2020 WSA has gathered information in SALAM5 center in Jarablus through the focused group discussions with women, girls and the young people in addition to the local councils. • The results showed that the economic situation for the women is miserable especially for those who lost their husband and they had to work very hard jobs in order to feed their hungry children, or to wait for the food basket aid. . • the general feeling of the young people is they were forming a new pressure on their families who already suffering from the general bad financial situation, which led the young men to join the armed groups for the sake of money and push the girls into leaving the schools and getting married in an early age.• On the other hand, Continued displacement movement and the arrival of new IDPs from Hamah, South Idlib and rural Aleppo, had very bad impacts on both the IDPs and the host community, and these impacts have affected the economic situation, the movement of the cash a lot with lack of job opportunities

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk1:Distinguishing between residents and displaced citizens Level: Average /Medial Mitigation Measure :Full survey of the percentage of the displaced people in the region and implementing external activities for them and inviting them to the center. Risk2:Distinguishing between residents of the city and the residents of the neighborhood areas Level:High Mitigation Measure: Increase the external activities in the neighborhood villages more and more.Risk3:The inability to meet the needs of all women in the region that we work in. In terms of lack of GBV centers(it is only WSA and Ihsan providing GBV activities) Level :Average/Medial Mitigation Measure:Prioritizing the women according to their needs and provide them the services they need. Risk4:Fear of the inability to keep the conﬁdential and secret documents safe due to the emergency security incidents. Risk Level: low. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

A- IEDs/VBIEDs/PBIEDs/ Can be direct or indirect. It is one of the major threats in Syria. Solution/ Mitigation measures: 1- Choose centers far from military locations 2- Avoiding the crowding of beneﬁciaries during activates. 3- Commitment to all humanitarian principles, especially neutrality and impartiality to avoid Targeting. 4- Continuing coordination with the Security and Safety department during staff movement B- The intervention of Armed groups in humanitarian action/ Its common risk, to obstruct humanitarian action (blackmail) Solution.Mitigation measures: 1- The organization's obligation not to provide any information about the donor to any military faction interventions and other related information. 2- WSA policy is clear, do not to make a concession. 3- Coordination with civilian local authorities and Turkish government. C- Fluctuation of Dollar rate and issues of money transfer, Mitigation: 1- WSA is using OWNDA &QUICK BOOKS tools of cash monitoring and follow-up. 2- Sending money to Syria by PTT (Turkish government postal and telegraph organization)D- Lost of Access: The areas of project are located under the control and administration of Turkish Army Forces , and WSA has full access and full approval from the related directorates , in case of change of the controlling authorities , the alternative plans will depend on the phase of the project reached , and the action plan implemented and achieved , all beneficiaries (the women farmers) will be trained and oriented on all S&S procedures and mitigation measures (how to choose the location of land , how to set the routs of transportation and materials distribution , how to act in cases of bombs or shelling or any military action , Humanitarian Risk education sessions (tailored for women farmers will be conducted).
