Project Summary

the project is emergency response .The planned activities should enable internally displaced persons in informal camps in the Idlib region to survive in dignity and live saving . By providing drinking water, desludging , solid waste removal, hygiene promotion and hygiene kits . The construction of gender-sensitive and safe latrines should ensure better living conditions. Solar-powered lighting to latrines road should offer more protection to the target group, especially women, the elderly, people with disabilities and children.

4 - Needs

Due to the war, a large part of the water and Sanitation infrastructure has been destroyed – up to 30% of the water infrastructure is gone functioning. Figures from UN OCHA (March 2021) indicate that approximately 2 million people in north-west Syria depend on water supply from road tankers are. At the same time, the (high) price for this community service was identified by 87% as the greatest obstacle to an adequate water supply (HNO 2021). In addition, there are sanitation and hygiene problems due to inadequate and irregular waste disposal, the lack of latrines in the informal settlements and a lack of awareness and knowledge of the inadequate hygienic conditions and the resulting consequences.According to 2021 HNO, 45% of households in IDP areas reported not having access to one or more hygiene items, including sanitary napkins (10%).There is little awareness of the dangers of COVID-19 and limited understanding of the routes of infection, contributing to the exponential spread of the virus. Neither the desolate health system nor the inadequate sanitation infrastructure offer the population in need adequate protection. To prevent the dramatic consequences of mass illnesses from the virus, all suitable effective measures are of great importance

Depending on the particular needs of the families, the hygiene sets are adapted.Activity 4:Garbage collection (twice a week), 1,200 truckloads totalThe contractors collect the waste from the informal settlements and take it to the nearest landfill.Activity 5:Construction and maintenance of 145 latrinesModality: The construction of gender-sensitive, inclusive and safe toilets will be carried out by local construction companies. The material for the latrines is bought locally. The need is coordinated with the management of the informal settlements, including the target group.The construction work will be overseen by our technical and M&E team in Syria.Activity 6:Emptying septic tanks in the informal settlement for 5,500 IDPsPeriod: regular emptying throughout
