Project Summary

The proposed HRP 2020 plan will be increased access to emergency food security and livelihood assistance to the IDPs and vulnerable host/returnees at Wau, Jur River in Western Barh El Ghazal, Awerial, Yirol East and Yirol West in Lakes, Bor South, Twic East, Pibor and Pochalla in Jonglei, and Magwi county in Eastern Equatoria States.
This plan will target 3,200 HHs (16,000 beneficiaries) affected by flooding, poor harvest, drought, food insecurity and also described in the IPC 4 & 5 according to IPC July 2019 report. The beneficiaries will be disaggregated based on the following states:
o Western Barh El Ghazal will have 4,000 beneficiaries in Jur River and Wau
o Jonglei State will have 8,000 beneficiaries in Bor South, Twic East, Pibor and Pochalla
o Lakes State have 2000 beneficiaries in Awerial, Yirol East, Yirol West
o Eastern Equatoria State will have 2000 beneficiaries in Magwi & Kapoeta East
LiveWell is a leads development partner in Boma State and lead emergency health/nutrition partner in Yirol East and West through the SSHF support and the World Bank. The organization has a long experienced in health, WASH and Nutrition in mentioned counties. With the approval of this response plan, LiveWell will carry out the following activities;
o Conduct General Food Distribution for 2,000 IDPs and vulnerable host communities in Awerial especially Mingkaman IDPs settlements.
o Cash and vouchers-based transfers with food security/nutrition objectives in Yirol East, Yirol West, Wau and Jur River to support 10,000 beneficiaries
o Distributes Fishing nets, seeds, agricultural tools in Bor South, Twic East, Magwi, Pibor to support 1,000 beneficiaries
o Conditional cash transfers (for social skills and safety nets) relating to assets investment, capacity building, communal work groups, and planning communities etc in Magwi and Yirol East, Yirol West for 5,000 beneficiaries
o Conditional food assistance through Food for Assets (FFA) for Bor South and Twic East to support 3,000 beneficiaries
o Support value change additions through fish and meat preservation and marketing in Pibor, Pochalla, Twic East and Bor South to support 1,000 beneficiaries
o Training of 500 local beneficiaries on improved agronomic practices
o Conduct mass vaccination campaigns targeting 10,000 livestock (cows, goats, sheep)

1 - Needs

• Access to food security and livelihoods is a major challenge in several parts of Western Bahr el Ghazal, Eastern Equatoria, Jonglei & Lakes States and it has caused food and livelihood insecurities to worsen over the second quarter of 2019 leading to increased suffering, morbidities and mortalities. This is largely attributed to ongoing conflict and impassibility of road network by humanitarian actors (REACH, April-June 2019), hence increased morbidity and mortality in USR children due to excess malnutrition. • Inadequate provision of essential healthcare services due to inadequate funding led to inadequate access to improved services such as RH and MPSS services. Development partners like World Bank support 27% of the existing health facilities in Jonglei State, 63% of Health Facilities are non-functional due to lack of funding (IRNA report, 2016/2017), resulting into increased morbidity/mortality of common diseases. • IPC report (July, 2019), 10,000 people in Yirol East and Yirol West in Lakes State are likely in Catastrophe (IPC Phase 5), Jonglei State populations estimated to be food insecure and 73% of its population facing crisis (IPC 3). All the proposed counties in Lakes are classified in Emergency phase (IPC 4); Awerial, Cueibet, Rumbek North, Yirol East, Yirol West etc. This is caused by a lean season, delayed rainfall, persistence inflation, displacement, assets depletion and destruction of livelihood as a result of ongoing conflicts in WES, CES, EES, Jonglei, WBS, and Lakes. • Of the 22 SMART surveys conducted between September 2018 and January 2019, eight of them showed GAM rates above the 15% WHO emergency threshold. However, five counties in Jonglei (Ayod, Pibor, Pochalla, and Twic East), Kapoeta East in Eastern Equatoria and Jur River in Western Bahr-al-Ghazal State show Critical levels of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM 15.0%-29.9%) (IPC Reports 2019). • LiveWell’s PHSA assessment (March, 2019) reported that inadequate Nutrition services, GAM rate of 19.9%, EPI coverage, inadequate provision of health, nutrition and WASH services are major causes of measles outbreaks in greater Pibor (IDSR, Week 8 of 2019), therefore, HC supported IOM/LiveWell, and MEDAIR/LiveWell to conduct measles vaccination campaign in 5 payams in March and September, 2019. Inadequate provision of primary health care and mental health services to be specific (9%) as a result of inaccessibility, insecurity, attack on health etc. Flooding Assessment in Pibor (Oct, 2019), 13,000 host populations are displaced as a result of flooding, and health services are severely disrupted. • Increased risks of Ebola Virus Disease (EVD) in borders of Yei & Magwi, and measles outbreaks since the beginning of the year have been reported in 15 counties including Pibor, Yirol East, Jur River and including Juba and Wau. 308 suspected measles cases in Jur River have been reported of which 4 tested positive for measles. It was recommended that WHO and humanitarian partners should carry out a reactive campaign in the areas (WHO EVD bulletin & IDSR report of weeks 36 & 37, September, 2019). • Low EPI coverage in Pibor, Jur River, Yirol East, Yirol West (EWARS Week 34, 2019), coupled with flooding in major counties in Jonglei State is anticipated to likely precipitate the outbreaks, malaria outbreaks, acute watery diarrhea (AWDs) and resultant severe acute malnutrition (flooding effect on health report in Pibor, Oct 2019) in under 5 year children and other vulnerable group like disabled, and pregnant women.
