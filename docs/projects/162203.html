Project Summary

"The overall objective of the proposed project is to enhance the food security and self-reliance of the Rohingya refugees, while strengthening, at the same time, the livelihood bases and resilience of impoverished host families that bear an excessive burden from the massive refugee influx. HELVEAS will implement project in Rohingya and Host Communities. Women will be get priority in the project activities and minimum 50% beneficiaries will be women participant. HELVETAS will do need assessment prior to finalized beneficiaries, where selection of beneficiaries will be based on HELVETAS beneficiaries selection criteria such as, women headed HHs, influx affected HHs, HHs with more children and family members in the HHs, elderly and ill people within the HHs and Person leaving with disability will be getting the preference.
HELVETAS plans to achieve this through three inter-linked project components.
- Component A: Provide support to 12000 refugee HHs (women and men) to produce their own vegetables nearby/beside their home.
- Component B: Fresh food voucher distribution to 1838 refugee HHs (elderly, women and PLW and PLD) for improving their dietary diversify.
- Component C: Enhancing income improvement of 8056 farmer HHs at the host community through farming input supply support for productivity gains, production increases, asset improvements and market system development.
Based on the above assumptions, the cumulative total for the three components comes to approximately 21,894 HHs (corresponding to around 109,470 individuals).
"

Objectives

"1 Ensure and sustain timely provision of life-saving food assistance for Rohingya refugees(Women, Ma, Youth and Adolescents).2. Promote portable skills development opportunities for Rohingya and enhance the livelihoodsand resilience of host communities. (Women, Man, Youth and Adolescents).

Needs

A continuing very high degree of overall economic vulnerability within the Rohingya refugee population resulting in equally high levels of dependence on humanitarian aid to cover even the most basic needs, but still there is gap to have proper nutrition divers and balance diet, access to complementary food support at household level. Due to influx, host community suffering with difficulties such as limited access to their own land, hike in prices, crisis in the labor market which is has a strong correlation between access to income-generating activities and improvements in food security.

Activities or outputs

"1. Rohingya Refugee HHs selected to benefit from project component A are enabled to successfully grow own vegetables.2. Particularly vulnerable refugee HHs selected to benefit form project component B received complementary fresh food commodities through a voucher scheme3. Host community farming HHs selected to directly benefit from project component C manage to significantly increased their HHs income and having increased their vegetable consumption."

Key project/performance indicators

% of project beneficiaries improve their Household Dietary Diversity Scores as a result of the assistance % of HHs consumption of vegetables through their own vertical and/or roof top gardening. % of HHs increase the amount of fresh food/vegetables.% of households improve their HHs economy as income from commercial agriculture practice.
