Project Summary

The project will be implemented by CAFOD and its Caritas partners (listed below) in the rural areas of Binga, Gokwe North, Gokwe South, Mudzi and Uzumba-Maramba-Pfungwe. The project will focus on establishment/rehabilitation of water points, strengthening participatory community hygiene (with the focus on enhancing active women participation both in leadership and decision-making in the management and use community WASH assets and behaviour change). The project seeks to achieve the following objectives:
1. To improve and restore access to safe and adequate water (people getting at least 7.5 to 15 litres each of clean water per day) in the rural areas of the 5 targeted districts.
2. To promote safe hygiene and sanitation practices in the rural areas of the 5 targeted districts by: a) improving and reinforcing knowledge on live saving hygiene and sanitation and b) improving access to hygiene enabling NFIs.

Needs

Due to the predicted El Nino induced drought (which will negatively affect the provision of safe water for basic needs), the prevailing cholera outbreak and the historical low or non-participation of women in the management and use of WASH resources, there are serious needs for;1)Restoring and improving the availability of water for basic needs in the rural areas of the targeted districts. 2) Improving awareness and knowledge on safe hygiene and sanitation practices 3) Increasing access to and use of WASH NFIs to prevent occurrences water-related diseases. 4) Improve gender sensitivity, particularly active participation of women in the management and use of water resources, like being actively involved in Water point committees.

Activities

Installation of 4 new solar piped water schemes, Rehabilitation of 2 solar piped water scheme, Extension of 2 solar piped water schemes to reach respective clinics and more villages, Rehabilitation of 60 Boreholes, gender training to community leaderships, Composition and capacity strengthening of water resources management committees, Participatory health, hygiene and Sanitation education (training of local stakeholders, school health masters and VHWs) and awareness raising( IEC material development & distribution and dissemination and broadcasting of life saving hygiene and sanitation messages), Distribution of NFIs to 3000 Households, water quality testing, household water treatment, Monitoring diarrhoeal diseases
