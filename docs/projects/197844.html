Project Summary

Through the discussion with local actor, they have reported that there is a lack of cleaning services in the city due to the lack of resources that are needed in terms of tools and labor. City cleaning schema could absorb good number of labor and will result in having healthy environment.
Throughout this project, CFW beneficiaries will clean the city streets and public spaces like public garden, schools, mosques, …. Etc. public spaces will be chosen in consultation with local actors.
This schema will be implemented in close coordination with local council. iMDAT main role will be creating the work opportunity, selecting the beneficiary and provide input support such as renting tractors to move the garbage from city to land fill, and providing light tools that includes, brooms, safety shoes and caps, and wheel borrow. The tools will either be used for future CFW project or will be donated to the community.

3 - Needs

Through the discussion with local actor, they have reported that there is a lack of cleaning services in the city due to the lack of resources that are needed in terms of tools and labor. City cleaning schema could absorb good number of labor and will result in having healthy environment. Throughout this project, CFW beneficiaries will clean the city streets and public spaces like public garden, schools, mosques, …. Etc. public spaces will be chosen in consultation with local actors. This schema will be implemented in close coordination with local council. iMDAT main role will be creating the work opportunity, selecting the beneficiary and provide input support such as renting tractors to move the garbage from city to land fill, and providing light tools that includes, brooms, safety shoes and caps, and wheel borrow. The tools will either be used for future CFW project or will be donated to the community. these CFW opportunities, will generate sources of income for the most vulnerable groups, 60 HHs will be selected to receive financial assistance for three months, priority will be given to IDPs, families include, Elderly, PWD ..etcThe project will also facilitate easy and safe movement of women, children, elderly and PWD within the city It reduces the possibility of infection with diseases.

4 - Implementation of activities

1.1.1 Advertisement; iMDAT team will announce the agreed project type at the targeted communities to ensure that all community’s members _from different sects_ are aware of iMDAT intervention.1.1.2 Beneficiary Selection; iMDAT team will open the application process to allow all eligible community members to apply for the CFW opportunity with the aim to avoid any duplication and to not cause any social tension. The applicants will be selected based on the aforementioned selection criteria, new indicators might be added to the selection criteria.1.1.3 Conducting Base line assessment; iMDAT will conduct a base line assessment for the selected beneficiaries to evaluate their ability to meet their basic needs.1.1.4 Conducting orientation/ inclusion workshops for the participants; iMDAT field team will provide 3 days’ orientation/ inclusion workshop to the selected CFW participants to ensure that all participants are aware of this intervention as a community-based intervention and this will create a safe space for all participants to know each other.1.1.5 Conduct the related activities; iMDAT will conduct the activities of the selected schemas. In city cleaning, iMDAT will ensure the public spaces are selected in close coordination with local actors.1.1.6 Paying CFW Beneficiaries; CfW participants will be paid in accordance with the followed and agreed practice by the Cash working group for a SMEB amount on monthly basis.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The deterioration of the security situation / High Likelihood- IMDAT has developed a security guideline and plan that covers the evacuation of the sites in case of emergency. - iMDAT to provide training for the boths staff and beneficiaries before starting the activity, including evacuation training in case of danger, and first aid training.· iMDAT to continuously monitor the security situation and coordinate with the local authorities.· iMDAT to suspend all field work in case the security situation deteriorates in a way that may pose a danger to the employees or beneficiaries of the project.· iMDAT to coordinate intensively with partners in case the security situation changes.· In case of security risk cases, participants will be asked to stay at home and he/she will be eligible for the wages during the stopped period. In accordance with the mitigation and escalation plan that has been developed throughout the previous project, the red light alert will be activated once the first scenario came true. In this case the following emergency activities will be kicked. Below are the main designed activities. 1. Move from Green to Orange zone according to our emergency response2. Halt all field activities during the fight period 3. Provide an advance salary, covering three months, to all field staff.
