Project Summary

The objective of this project is to promote awareness and the facility of access WASH both for the Rohingya and host community. This project will be establish water points for the vulnerable people to get save drinking water especially targeting the PLW and children to reduce the water born diseases to outbreak. On the other hand, this will promote community awareness for hygiene improvement into the community

Objectives

1. To ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities to sufficient and safe water for drinking and domestic needs.2. To ensure all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times.3. To ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through access and exposure to innovative hygiene promotion (including hygiene items) for all refugees living in camps and affected host communities.

Needs

The project has targeted both in camps and the host community. WASH infrastructure specially latrines/bathing cubicles/water points operations and maintenance, fecal sludge management in underserved areas, and human resources for hygiene promotion activities remain critical in target locations where LoCOS is proposing the said project. There is a signiﬁcant need to clean the latrines, both existing and new, through the FSM systems and also through solid waste management processes. Given the sheer proximity of WASH facilities to shelters, more efforts need to be put toward constructing water points which can provide safe water, particularly for consumption through more sustainable water supply systems. Protection meassures need to be considered when constructing WASH facilities including using designs that can serve PwDs and women. Finally, the need for hygiene promotion remain dire given the already dreadful conditions in the congested camps. Need for communities to take ownership is necessary, so WASH committees need to be formed and robust hygiene awareness campaigns need to be carried out throughout the target camps. It has been noted and asessments have proved that women are more akin to be active in maintaining their facilities and provide more active feedback on their needs with regard to WASH facilities and access, which then will need to be taken into consideration when tailoring and implementing the propose dproject activities. For the hos community the existinng livelihood condition is under challange due to massive influx and the potection and security getiing vulnerable day by day. this refugee people hase created extra pressure on existing local resource and source of water are getting pulluted and contaminnated.
