Project Summary

the overall aim of the project is to support the most vulnerable and conflict affected population in Darfur, Kordofan, Eljazeera, Red Sea, Khartoum and Gadaref States. the key objective of the project is to 1) Reduce acute food insecurity and save lives of extreme vulnerable populations affected by conflict and natural disasters, by providing emergency agriculture assistance to the underserved populations and 2) Increase access to food security and livelihood diversification to the newly displaced and most extremely vulnerable conflict affected populations. the project will focus on provision of agricultural inputs and seeds for (women, men, boys and girls), conducting extension trainings and vaccinations to the animals/livestock. the project also will ensure provision of emergency livelihood start up kits and agricultural inputs and tools for most vulnerable affected HHs. awareness campaigns will be conducted for the most vulnerable population both female and male on environment protection, gender involvement and equality. in addition, 40 % of this program will be cash through distribution of vouchers, cash for work and cash for business/start-up kits. the project will be implemented in close coordination with the relevant actors as well as ensuring that gender sensitive programming and investment including protection are adhered to during the implementation. UPO focuses on maintaining an inclusive, sustainable and accepting implementation environment that results in community collaboration and ownership .

1 - Needs

The ongoing WAR between SAF and RSF in addition to the intensified tribal armed conflict resulted in over one million new displacement with trends indicating continued displacement as armed violence continues to breakout in Sudan. Market inflation, economic challenges and supply chain crash with almost 99 % of the production industry out of service , infrastructure burned down , delivery pathways cut ,all of this among other challenges has significant impact on the Sudanese people, increasing their vulnerability to risks. In 2023 HNO, estimated over 19 million will encounter acute food and livelihood insecurity in Sudan based on review and analysis of various food securities information reports IPC 2023. Lack of adequate income and minimum livelihoods diversity, lack of agricultural extension activities, lack of improved seeds, insecticides, pesticides chemicals, lack of mobility tools for extensionists, lack of the fruit's seedlings propagation nurseries, and lack of improved vegetable and fruits demonstration plots are the common challenges. majority of communities have the common constraints such as lack of sufficient on farm seed production, poor agricultural production methods, lack of skilled human resource capacity at the state, locality and community levels, and lack of skills particularly among the youth. hundreds of thousands of displaced and conflict affected individuals including women, men, boys and girls were identified to be in urgent needs for food security and livelihood coping mechanisms. many IDPs, returnees and conflict affected population were assessed to be in need of agricultural inputs, extension services, training on clay stoves and other substantial livelihood opportunities to help them possess new income opportunity. UPO has identified limited opportunities for households to generate income and produce food, with agricultural inputs and livelihood activities. In the recent UPO assessment, community interviews more than 60% of households in Darfur indicated they do not have sufficient food. The majority of respondents cited a lack of seed, and 78% indicated they have not been able to store their own seed. Community members identified insufficient land, due nomadic occupation, requiring improved methods to maximize output and diversity their livelihoods. Animal health is also poor, particularly amongst IDPs and host households, with only 5.6% indicating good animal health. UPO FSL intervention will focus protecting animals' health, provide quality declared drought tolerant and early maturing seed to enable their own production and ensure self-reliance.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Provision of agricultural inputs and seeds for 80,000 conflict affected farmers HHs (women, men, boys and girls) in camps, gathering and underserved areas to help them meet their food needs from own production and improve livelihood condition. 2 conducting extension trainings for 1,000 most vulnerable farmers (600 female& 400 male) on modern agricultural techniques. 3 Provision of vaccinations to 250,000 head of animals owned by 6,000 (2000 females, 4000 males) most vulnerable HHs dependent on livestock. 4 – Provision of emergency livelihood start up kits for 600 for most vulnerable women headed HHs in camps . 5 Provision of agricultural inputs and tools for 6,000 most vulnerable affected HHs (3500 female, 2500men) to enable them have new income opportunity and cope with the current situations. 6 Conducting awareness for 1000 most vulnerable populations both female and male on environment protection, gender involvement and equality. in addition 40 % of this program will be cash through distribution of vouchers, cash for work and cash for business/start-up kits. UPO has 3 years history of cash-based intervention in Darfur and eastern Sudan. business start-up capitals , Agricultural inputs will be partially implemented through cash transfer: UPO will establish groups/association , facilitate bank account opening for the group enable easy transfer of the cash. the intervention will be one-time off based on the activities designed
