Project Summary

Having witnessed the damages that the explosion caused in Beirut, and based on the rapid needs assessment that IOCC conducted, the organization is suggesting to provide full rehabilitation works to residential apartments as well as to primary health care centers and schools in the areas that were most affected by the explosion.
The aim of this intervention is the restore people's home, protection and dignity by rehabilitating their shelters. It will also reduce the percentage of internal displacement and minimize the costs of rehab on the affected families especially during the economic crisis that Lebanon is passing through.
On the other hand, facilities and institutions that were accessed by vulnerable population such as Primary Health Care Centers (PHCs) and Public Schools were also heavily damaged and currently put out of service. IOCC is proposing a full rehabilitation for these institutions so that they can soon enough operate again and receive people who need support.
This intervention will target 150 residential apartment and 10 institutions

1 - Needs

The overall goal of the rapid needs assessment conducted by IOCC is to determine the pressing needs of the families affected by the explosion in the target areas. The collection and analysis of the quantitative and qualitative assessment data will ensure that IOCC’s emergency response will be adequately designed and implemented in accordance with the determined needs. The assessment also helped to guarantee community engagement and acceptance of the planned intervention. The overall observations from the rapid assessment show that families are living in modest households with minimal belongings and are dependent on assistance. In most cases, their homes were old and in poor condition even prior to the explosion. The families were unable to afford the cost of repairing their homes, and were limited to cleaning up debris, which they could do themselves.On the days of the assessment, the families were welcoming despite the stress they have experienced. Some families’ pride was holding them back from requesting help. They were asking for less than needed and sometimes asking to shift any support to more vulnerable neighbors. All of the assessed households sustained certain level of damage. The severity of the damages varied between neighborhoods. Around 40 buildings had to be referred to IOCC engineers for structural assessments due to the level of damage. One building that was previously evacuated was in danger of collapsing. Pursuant to the guidelines issued by the Lebanese government, IOCC reported the building to the authorities for action.Based on the quantitative results of the needs assessment and the observations of the enumerators, the rehabilitation of the houses is one of the major first-stage needs of the intervention, especially in severely damaged areas. Poor families were not able to relocate and had to stay in unsafe houses. Others have been displaced, relocated to their relatives or collective shelters in other areas. Elderly people were found to be the most vulnerable groups within the affected areas. They are facing challenges in securing their medication for chronic diseases and ailment. Their movement is hindered by neighborhood damage, with debris still blocking walkways. They also reported reduced access to hospitals because of limited capacity. Psychological trauma is considered to be the main protection issue that has been reported as a result of the explosion. A total of 275 households from Geitawi, Badawi, Karm El Zeitoun, and Hekmeh communities of Achrafieh participated in the needs assessment. Approximate number of people leaving in the assess households is 680.Based on the guidelines provided by the local authorities, and IOCC’s goal to address the needs of the most vulnerable population, 55% of the assessed households were located in Karm El Zeitoun community, which is very poor and was severely affected by the explosion. The vast majority of the surveyed households are Lebanese. Only 9 percent were Syrians and 3 percent other nationalities.According to the survey results, 35% were scored as severely damaged, 57% as medium and 8% were considered as having minor damages.The highest need consisted of windows replacement (70%), followed by door replacement (45%) and building rehabilitation (24%) in addition to other minor rehabilitation items.

2 - Activities

As mentioned earlier about the huge cost that families will need to pay to rehabilitate their houses, IOCC proposes to contribute in rehabilitation works, mainly the works that ensure the protection of people. This might include electricity works, as well as some civil works that will protect the houses and the inhabitants from stealing and other potential hazards. In addition, WASH rehabilitation will be taken into consideration in the BoQs of the houses to be repaired.In addition to damaged households, it is worth considering rehabilitation works for public institutions that the most deprived used to access before the explosion; such as primary health care centers PHCs and public schools. * Rehabilitation of Households: IOCC will ensure to identify in collaboration with the Shelter Sector and the Lebanese Authorities the areas where the intervention would take place. The intervention will consist of:-SELECTION OF AREAS/STREETS AS PER THE EMERGENCY RESPONSE LEAD CRITERIA-Conduct Household visits-Drafting detailed Bills of Quantities BoQs for each specific household-Draft a combined and cumulative BoQ per zone-Hand over every zone to a contractor in order to move forward quicker with workFOR THE HOUSEHOLDS WHOSE DAMAGE LEVEL SCORES 1 AS PER THE SHELTER SECTOR, IOCC WILL CONDUCT MINOR REPAIRS SUCH AS FIXING LOCKS MAINLY TO ENSURE PROTECTION AND SAFETY OF INHABITANTS, REPAIRING INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL DOORS WHEN APPLICABLE AS WELL AS BROKEN WINDOWS...AS FOR MORE DAMAGED HOUSES, MAINLY THOSE THAT ARE CLASSED AS MODERATELY DAMAGED, IOCC WILL ENSURE FULL HOUSEHOLD REHABILITATION INCLUDING IMPROVED ACCESS TO WATER AND SANITATION INFRASTRUCTURE WHEN NO LONGER ACCESSIBLEMOREOVER, STRUCTURAL DAMAGES WITHIN THE IDENTIFIED AREA COULD BE ALSO REPAIRED IF FOUND IN ANY SEVERELY DAMAGED HOUSEHOLDIOCC AIMS TO REACH 400 HOUSEHOLDS WITHIN THIS ACTIVITY MONITORING:IOCC engineers will ensure that material to be used throughout this rehabilitation is adequate and according to standards, proper removal and disposal of asbestos (if any) and will keep on monitoring daily the works of the contractors in order to provide comments when available and ensure that works do not take long to be completed. PROCUREMENT:IOCC WILL DRAFT A TENDER BOOK TO BE SOLICITED IN LOCAL NEWSPAPERS AS WELL AS ON DALEEL MADANI. THE CONDITIONS WILL ENSURE THAT ONLY CONTRACTORS WHO ALREADY HAVE A SIMILAR EXPERIENCE IN REHABILITATION/CONSTRUCTION WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO SUBMIT OFFERS.EACH AREA WILL BE SPLIT INTO ZONES AND EACH ZONE WILL BE HANDED OVER TO A DIFFERENCE CONTRACTOR IN ORDER TO ENSURE SMOOTH IMPLEMENTATION THAT WILL NOT TAKE LONG.
