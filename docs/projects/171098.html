Project Summary

The proposed project aims at providing integrated Nutrition treatment and prevention services for drought affected under five boys, girls and caregivers in Daynile, Dharkenley of Benadir region and Afgoye district of Lower Shabelle region. It will contribute to reduction of nutrition related morbidity and mortality rates. SOYDA intervention will target pregnant and lactating mothers together with children aged below 5 years among the host communities, IDPs and returnees. Active case finding for malnourished cases will be done through mass screening of children aged below 5 years and pregnant and lactating mothers for rapid identification of malnourished cases. Moderate acutely malnourished children and pregnant and lactating mothers will be referred for enrolment in the SFP programs run by other actors in Benadir and Lower Shabelle Region The project will support a network of 6 fixed Outpatient Therapeutic Programs (OTP and 12 mobile sites fully equipped with OTP commodities including Ready to Use Therapeutic Foods (RUTF), essential medicines for treatment of communicable diseases, vaccines, trained OTP nurses, vaccinators and hygiene promoters. The trained OTP nurses will treat severe acutely malnourished children in the static and mobile OTPs according to IMAM protocol. Mass screening will be done by trained health workers deployed in the 8 mobile clinics and trained 30 CHWs each assigned at least one village in the areas covered by the project. Immunization status of all children screened will be checked by both static and mobile OTP staff and children who are eligible for vaccination and do not have updated immunization status will be immunized by the vaccinators and assistant vaccinators in the mobile OTPs. Severe acutely malnourished children with medical complications will be referred by the mobile clinics and the CHWs to the stabilization Centre in Afgooye and Benadir hospital for nutrition rehabilitation. Hygiene promoters will during the activities of the mobile clinics disseminate hygiene promotion messages to the communities to prevent outbreak and spread of communicable diseases like AWD/cholera and measles. During the same time, mothers will be counseled individually and in groups by trained health workers and Community Health Workers (CHWs) on appropriate maternal infant and young child feeding as part of efforts to prevent malnutrition. The project will also provide micronutrient support by distributing multiple micronutrients (MNPs) and Vitamin A supplements through the static and mobile clinics. Staff dedicated to the static and mobile OTPs will be subjected to proper trainings on infant and young child feeding, integrated management of acute malnutrition and nutrition, health and hygiene promotion. Efforts will also be made to link households with children with Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) conditions and suspected cholera cases to health, WASH interventions and food security/ livelihood programs. There will be regular coordination meetings at the field level between WASH, Health and Nutrition clusters to foster synergy, avoid duplication of efforts/ resources and share information on response efforts. SOYDA will keep the nutrition cluster and report monthly ONA reports. Ministry of Health and UNOCHA regularly informed on progress of activities through sharing of progress reports and also share any challenges encountered for rapid assistance.

Project Activities

1. Screen and admit 1700 boys, 1700 girls and 1200 PLWs of moderately malnourished within the targeted through 6 fixed and 12 mobile Outreaches of integrated TSFP/OTP in Daynile, Dharkenley districts of Benadir region and Afgooye district of Lower Shabelle region.2. Screen and admit 300 boys, 300 girls for severely malnourished without medical complications within the targeted through 6 fixed and 10 mobile Outreaches of integrated TSFP/OTP in Daynile, Dharkenley districts of Benadir region and Afgooye district of Lower Shabelle region 3. Provide micro nutrient supplementation, Vitamin A and Iron folic supplementation to 1200 acutely malnourished children (boys (1700) and girls (1700). 4. Provide multiple micronutrients supplementation to 1200 pregnant and lactating women to reduce micronutrient deficiency among Pregnant and lactating women in our target areas 5. Provide referral support for critically ill severely malnourished children with medical complications to the stabilization center through community case management and outreaches 6. Conduct community and outreach-based IYCF –E promotion in target areas. IYCF-E counselor will organize topics on IYCF-E, maternal nutrition at facility level targeting mothers of children 6-59 months served at the nutrition sites, the staff will also organize 36 sessions on IYCF-E at community level targeting to reach a total of 450 men and 800 women caregivers /parents hence improving the skills of the target beneficiaries7. Form and support 4 mother to mother support groups consisting of mothers attending OTP, SC TSFP, antenatal and postnatal care sessions at integrated nutrition and health facility. Each mother to mother support group will consist of 10 mothers. The mothers support group activity is aimed at improving attendance of antenatal and postnatal clinics as well as promoting behavior change and improved care practices among pregnant and lactating women 8. Conduct IYCF Counseling to both individual and group counseling for mothers/caregivers visiting the nutrition sites in Marka district targeting pregnant and lactating women through daily nutrition counselling sessions9. Conduct structured promotion sessions targeting 4600 caregivers of under five children to raise awareness on prevention and referrals of community case managements, AWDs, cholera, malaria and other communicable diseases (includes provision of IEC materials, group sessions, counselling, home visits etc). Also, on promoting hygiene and sanitation practices (included also in NHHP activity above to ensure a multisectoral approach). 10. Conduct one session of Integrated Management of Acute Malnutrition (IMAM) training on effective acute Malnutrition management for 16 Male and 23 female staff.11. Conduct one session training for Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) for 10 Male and 15 female staff and, 6 Male and 8 Female community volunteers.12. Conduct three infants young child feeding promotion sessions to 200 caregivers for effective child nutrition practices among the affected communities in the target location.

Mitigation Measures

The project beneficiary may face different level of risk based on the contentious displacement in most target areas such risk include, cultural and religious barriers that might led discouragement from using health services such as MNPs, immunization,vtamin A supplementation among other nutrition service that is necessary for immediate treatment of severely and moderately malnourished children, confidential or privacy problem, poor nutrition outreach and facility based design that would not provide the beneficiaries the required attention and well as the confidence for caregivers (male and female) separation coupled with lack of information documentation among the most vulnerable population specific needs in the target location. SOYDA organization shall however intends to mitigate such risk through improving the community information sharing on the nutrition needs and services as well as improving the design, confidentiality/privacy and coordination mechanism in working with all actors as well improving the distance and time needed for immediate access to basic nutrition service packages(BNSP) by creating mobile teams that will provide services closer to these disable and marginalized communities in both rural and urban IDPs set up in Mogadishu and Afgooye. SOYDA will also ensure to provide effective community mobilization and sensitization to avoid any child to missed most important vaccination/immunization ,it shall also improve community engagement to review the challenges and barriers of nutrition services on both local leaders religious leaders, women group and youth for better health services uptake. In implementing the nutrition service provision in highly risk and unpredictable areas there might bedifferent factors that might led the risk of exclusion based on age and gender in circumstance such exclusion may include sexual exploitation among the female health/Nutrition employee, exclusion on job opportunities duty discrimination against minorities, physically challenge person, poor representation among the affected people, poor feedback mechanism as well as lack of code of conduct, monitoring and reporting channel, such risk factors might hinder the actual program implementation purpose thus led it to ineffectiveness in reaching the affected people. To mitigate this SOYDA organization intends to established clear feedback mechanism for complaints during such instance on sexual exploitation as well as poor nutrition staff conduct that calls for unprofessional behaviors in doing so, SOYDA will provide the project beneficiaries with hotline/call center that shall be responsible by a compliant officers who is going to directly report to executive director for immediate action this will further ensure the accountability to the affected population, it will also create reporting and monitoring system that shall ensure properly documented project information, challenge, lesson learnt as well as ensure all nutrition staff signed code of conduct. Due to the porous security situation in most part of the country Somalia, there might be many conflict related risk towards the beneficiary such are military occupation of the health and nutrition facilities, untargeted stray bullet to beneficiaries, cross fire violence that might erupt between the militia group and the government forces as well as sexual abuse among the vulnerable girls and women in the IDPs set up hence this might directly inflict more harm to the beneficiaries in the target location To mitigate this, SOYDA shall ensure the risk of beneficiaries are minimized during the displacement by putting health and nutrition facilities far from military bases to enable avoid any stray bullet and cross fire that could have otherwise danger the already displaced beneficiaries, it shall also ensure to provide psychosocial support to its project beneficiaries to cater for the need of the sexual gender based violence (SGBV) as well as establish coherence reporting and cordinat
