Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the effects of the droughts, conflicts and floods on vulnerable groups of women, children and elderly persons through provision of child protection and comprehensive services to Gender Based Violence survivors.
The services to be provided to GBV survivors include Medical care, Psycho-social and legal support services. The project envisages that conditions and the well-being of Gender Based violence survivors will be improved through psycho-social support services and survivor centered approach to enhance quick recovery and establishment of enduring community support groups for seamless community reintegration. Aid Vision will directly implement the project in Balcad (Middle Shabelle Region), Shangani and Garasbaley of Banadir Region to contribute towards protection, safety and promotion of the dignity of conflict, droughts and flood-affected women, men, girls and boys, in the target districts, it is anticipated that a high number of newly displaced people and survivors of protracted droughts and conflicts in the region will immensely benefit from the project
The project aims at protecting vulnerable people in areas affected by drought and/or conflict from further risks of exclusion, exploitation and violence through the deployment of specialized mobile teams composed of Case Managers, Health Workers and Legal Officer, for increased identification of those in need and enhance availability and access to quality protection service delivery.
The project will give specific focus on; increasing knowledge and awareness of vital and critical protection needs, mitigation and response to impacts of protection risks associated with droughts and conflicts on vulnerable groups such as women, children and those with no or weak social support networks. The key target beneficiaries of the project are: women, men, girls and boys, IDPs -including newly displaced people and/or newly accessible people, returnees, and receiving community members. The guiding strategy will be twofold: dissemination of relevant information; and direct support of identified cases through comprehensive case management (including internal/external referral) and distribution of emergency kits. Target beneficiaries will include: 400 men, 1200 women, boys 920 and 1480 girls will be directly be benefiting from the services while other 14,000 indirect beneficiaries will be expected to benefit.

Project Activities

Output 1: Timely and quality support to GBV survivors in Jowhar through expansion of Community based GBV services, establishment of referral pathways, provision of medical/ clinical services, psycho-social support, material, legal assistance and awareness creation.Activity 1.1.1: Provision of dignity kits to displaced women and girls who are affected by conflict in Balcad district middle Shabelle region, Shangani and Garasbaley (Khada)Districts of Banadir.The kits will contribute to psychosocial and emotional wellbeing of recipient women and girls, particularly the component of sanitary towels which will improve their mobility and confidence. Other clothing items such as dirac (Somali dress), scarfs, and petticoats also play important role building confidence and improving girls and women mobility and reducing protection risks. Activity 1.1.2 Provision of qualified, quality counselling services to GBV survivors. The survivors will have requisite information on available services and ways to access. The counselor is trained on survivor centered approach which will guide service provision to allow survivors to access the information they need to make decisions and will be based on consent at each stage in the process. Services are provided within the one stop center’s by counselor who also recommend referrals when survivors need specialized services that are not available at the centers. It is expected all the 700 survivors to receive one on one psycho-social counselling sessionActivity 1.1.3 Trained nurse will be deployed at the GBV centers/desks to be available for GBV survivors to offer CMR services. The treatment will be offered by the nurses at the GBV Centers to survivors in Balcad and Shangani Districts. It is expected 400 Survivors to be treated on sexual and other gender-based violence incidents Activity 1.1.4 Solar Lamps will be provided to women and girls faced with protection risks. This will enable light up their environment- houses to reduce protection risks such as abductions, rape and other risks associated with dark environments. Due high risks posed by congestion compounded by poor shelter materials and poor lighting in the camps and urban integrated IDPs, girls and women are vulnerable to protection risks like rape, militia attack, and abductions at night. These necessitates to light up IDPs in Balcad and squalor shanties in Shangani District of Banadir Region. The solar lamp would Contribute to the protection of women and girls, by lighting their environment so that women and girls do not have to live in fear of darkness and attendant psychological trauma.Activity 1.1.5 A legal aid officer will be made available and responsible to attend to cases and incidents of GBV. This will include but not limited to: neglect, rape, sexual and physical assault, domestic violence and denial of resources that need to be addressed. The lawyer will make both formal and informal legal representation to avail justice to the survivors. It is expected 100 GBV Survivors will find justice. The formal representation involves in government court representation while the informal representation involves in consultative traditional mechanisms of resolving issues using elders. The lawyer will at all time represent and upheld the interest of the survivors.Activity 1.1.6: Train 100 women composed of GBV survivors, women leaders and 50 men (including youth) as change agents on GBV. This will create a pool of advocates on GBV risks in the targeted districts and enhance behavioral change in communities. The change agents will be the first referral responders in the communities and will create awareness, break silence on GBV demystify reporting of GBV. This will have the long-term impact of increased service/justice seeking behavior for survivors and enhance capacities of communities to deter GBV incidents. Mitigation Measures

Members of marginalized community’s/ minority groups who are not often included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance will be deliberately targeted to partake in decisions. Clear stakeholder analysis will be undertaken to ensure inclusivity at decision making levels of the project. Women, girls and boys are often the ones left out in consultations and decision-making processes because of their age due cultural barriers. The project will ensure community decision makers will be representatives of all, young and old, men and women, boys and girls to ensure the specific needs of each are adequately listened to and addressed.The project will address the risk of exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance. This will be done through: staff and vendors signing code of conduct that expressly prohibits the above violations and makes consequences of the same clear. Exposure of displaced women and girls to sexual violence, caused by living in highly insecure settlements, distance to and location of humanitarian assistance and services will be addressed through scheduling of distributions, meetings and consultative forums in such a way that they do not pose risks to beneficiaries, particularly girls and women. The distances covered by girls and women to seek services will also be compliant to Sphere Minimum Standards and other best protection best practices. Synergies and collaborations will be developed with other partners in the field shelter and camp coordination to ensure there is complementarity in addressing needs of IDPs in holistic manner. Aid Vision runs health and nutrition projects in Garasbaley of Kahda district and Shangani District. This will complement the proposed protection project.
