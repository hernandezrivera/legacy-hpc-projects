Project Summary

Implement mass media communication (radio) on family feeding, reduction of food waste, and dietary recommendations for the nutritionally vulnerable, tailoring the messages and communications channels to the urban, peri-urban and rural contexts. This include the promotion of recommended maternal, infant, and young child nutrition practices (MIYCN) adapte to the context of COVID-19
