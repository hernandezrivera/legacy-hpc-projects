Project Summary

According to the latest Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC Analysis for May 2019), the key findings shows that the population in Western Equatoria State is 790,729.Over 300,000 of the total population in are under Phase 2 (Stressed), 220,000 are in Phase 3(Crisis) and 55,000 people are in Phase 4(Emergency).
Narrowing these findings to Mundri East, Mundri West and Mvolo counties, the food security and acute malnutrition levels attributed to low food production, reduced livestock rearing due to diseases, poor access to health and nutrition services, high morbidity, extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene is very high. The three counties are categorized to be in Phase 3 (Crisis).
The three counties had had an estimated population of 198,778 people by mid of May 2019 out of which 97,000 people were categorized in IPC Phase 3-Crisis and 37,000 people are in IPC Phase 4- Emergency.
The key drivers to food insecurity in the three counties include:
• Protracted Crisis (displacements, disrupted livelihoods, disrupted markets, restricted physical movement in some locations)
• Low crop and livestock production due to late seeds and tools distribution, Fall Army Worm infestation on crops and continuous outbreaks of common livestock diseases
• Economic Crisis(inflation, high food prices and low purchasing power among the vulnerable communities)
• Inter-communal conflict and livelihood raiding especially in Mvolo County (insecurity, random cattle rustling, displacement and asset loss)
• Climatic shocks ( intervals of prolonged dry spells and rainy seasons affecting movement of people and goods)
• Increased number of Returnees leading to depletion of the available food reserves

1 - Needs

The proposed project will be of great impact to the vulnerable population because we will integrate the activities with nutrition and veterinary services that are already being implemented by the organization in the three counties. Improved agricultural production and empowering the Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) activities will help in improving the food security status. Asset creation activities such as clearing and leveling of feeder roads linking the people to the markets, schools, local administration offices and health facilities will improve access to nutrition, health and other Government services. Insecurity cases are expected to go down, easy movement of people and their farm produce will enhanced activated markets hence improved economic situation. The soft component of the project that involves identifying and training of the beneficiaries on good agricultural practices will lead to increased food production, household food security and nutrition status.
