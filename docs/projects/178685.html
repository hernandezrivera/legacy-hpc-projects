Project Summary

This project will greatly contribute to the cluster/sector objectives 2 because it will support the self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives.
This project is going to close some of the gap the PiN. It's FSL project that will help support 7000 direct host and IDPs with agriculture, cash for work related to agriculture, as well More than 100,000 Indirect bene ciaries of the rehabilitation of bakeries warehouse The project will mainly support the value chain of 1000 wheat farmers (supporting 1- hectare pre-planted wheat per farmer) as follows:
-distribution of agricultural inputs (The wheat seeds)
-Cost sharing of fuel for irrigation

-The value chain includes training activities like TOT of key technical personnel from the Local Council & Ministry of Agriculture for the extension units, which will provide long term sustainability after project closure.
