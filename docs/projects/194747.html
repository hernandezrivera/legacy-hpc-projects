Project Summary

Through out North Darfur state, the overall humanitarian and environment is extremely worrying. As the escalation in violence between the Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) spreads throughout Sudan, civilians in Darfur continue to suffer, 20 years after the Darfur conflict began. The start of Sudan conflict on 15 April 2023 has destabilized the entire Darfur region with opposing groups along ethnic and tribal lines further exacerbates conflict dynamics, posing significant access/security challenges for the humanitarian community to resume operation at scale to meet the life-saving humanitarian need.
Through the proposed project, Sahari will target Refuges and host communities in North Darfur state by improving access to basic curative and preventive primary health care, and increasing access to nutrition assistance for affected children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women (PLW). Integrating Health and Nutrition activities into the project will contribute to saving lives by reducing wasting and stunting levels caused by Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM). It will also improve access to quality healthcare.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project will provide health and nutrition services to girls, boys, women and men in the targeted localities/states. Access to treatment will be universal, recognizing the community/household power dynamics that inform the choices of community members. Mothers will be targeted as the primary caregivers, and fathers as the decision-makers of the households. The IP will ensure equal opportunities/access to the health and nutrition services for girls and boys. The programme will place particular emphasis on care for malnourished, pregnant and lactating women. Training opportunities will allow adequate participation of either gender. Assessments & monitoring data will be segregated into women, men, girls and boys.Equity This project is targeting some of the neediest locations in line with the Humanitarian Needs Overview ( 2022). The nutrition cluster intersectoral prioritization score will be used in identifying localities with the greatest need. IPs will ensure that facilities and services are accessible to all groups, including people with disabilities and conflict-affected communities. Mobile and Outreach service delivery approaches will be used to expand and reach underserved communities.
