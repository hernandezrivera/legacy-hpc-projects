Project Summary

General objective:
Contribute to a comprehensive Protection system of vulnerable children in Mulang and Ntarinkon communities
Outcomes:
1. Mitigate the impacts of conflict related violations on the mental health and
psychosocial wellbeing for children
2. Strengthen the resilience and positive coping mechanisms of children in Mulang and Ntarinkon communities
The project therefore seeks to develop initiatives that aim at addressing child protection concerns, prevent violations or enhance accountability for child abuse by all duty bearers (Responsive and Remedial)

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Bamenda II subdivision is continuously being affected by critical incidents stemming from the political crisis including the use of Force (Live Ammunition, physical assaults, violence against schools & child abuse). This violence and inhumane actions have a significant impact on MHPSS well being especially for children. Up to this moment, Bamenda II subdivision is observing a significant amount of violent incidents, that have been reflected in the number of people in need of MHPSS support. So the project seeks -To raise awareness on children protection mechanisms.-To sensitize, educate and create and enabling environment for children and victims of critical events- To set up case management, psycho social support and mental health programmes aimed at supporting vulnerable children and familiesThese will be done through the creation of 2 children safe spaces at Ntarinkon amd Mulang communities.So the activities will include - The establishment of birth certificates, Training on PSEA, assist victims of GBV and organize seminars on Mitigation of GBV.
