1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

World Vision will aim to reach 33,497 affected people in Metuge District, in Cabo Delgado. The intervention will address the sustainable livelihoods needs. The selection criteria will target female-headed households, elderly persons (>65 years), child headed households, disabled headed households and the chronically ill. The goal of the project is to reduce vulnerability to shocks by providing livelihoods needs Cabo Delgado Province.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Identifying potential risks and avoiding harm is a fundamental aspect of responsible project design and implementation. The following framework describes how a proposed project can achieve this goal effectively1. Stakeholder Engagement: Engage diverse stakeholders, including marginalized and vulnerable groups, to understand context-specific risks. This ensures that perspectives often overlooked are incorporated.2. Inclusive Design: Ensure project interventions respect cultural, social, and environmental sensitivities. 3. Safeguarding Policies: Adopt safeguarding measures to protect individuals and communities from exploitation, abuse, and harm.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Encouraging meaningful access to assistance while ensuring the participation and empowerment of women and elderly individuals requires intentional strategies to address their unique needs and barriers. Here's how a proposed project can achieve this:1. Removing Barriers to AccessNeeds Assessment: Conduct targeted assessments to understand the specific challenges women and elderly persons face in accessing assistance, such as mobility, language, or technology.2. Encouraging ParticipationInclusive Representation: Actively involve women and elderly persons in planning and decision-making bodies, ensuring their voices shape project activities.Consultative Forums: Use focus groups or community meetings designed to be welcoming and accessible for these groups to collect their input.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

WV uses the rights-based social model of disability inclusion which recognizes and partners with women and girls, men and boys, elderly persons and persons with disabilities and addresses barriers. Building on World Vision’s Guidelines for Inclusion of Persons with a disability (PWD), and individuals from minority groups all face distinct risks in crisis settings and have unique needs. WV response teams will proactively seek to understand the specific experiences of these groups, and will ensure that the assistance and services are accessible to the most vulnerable. This includes ensuring a gender perspective, disability awareness, and proactive identification and support for individuals with specific needs

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

WV is aware of environmental considerations and will act appropriately to implement activities that are not harmful to the environment. These efforts will include working together with other partners to ensure proper waste disposal. The projects will also support the community to promote appropriate environmental hygiene and sanitation practices.
