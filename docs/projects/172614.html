Project Summary

The main objective is to provide immediate relief to vulnerable households in Chimanimani,Chipipinge,Beitbridge ,Nyanga districts.This will target vulnerable households affected by COVID19,severe food insecurity and economic crises.The response will be through multipurpose cash transfers, livelihoods and resilience promotion,. In this regard, all the food security gaps in these targeted districts need to be addressed immediately before the communities plunge into severe food insecurity. The total target will be 21,322 beneficiaries and the people target is attached on the separate excel.

1 - Activities

1. Multi-purpose Cash Transfers: The IRC will provide monthly food support through cash-based transfers to 21,322 acutely food insecure population in rural areas, through unconditional cash transfers to vulnerable households amounting to USD13 per individual for 8 months of the lean season and beyond to improve access to food for the target group. This will cover mainly basic food needs and the targeted districts are Chipinge, Chimanimani, Nyanga, Beitbridge and Chiredzi Districts. 2. Resilience and Livelihoods: The IRC intends to enhance community resilience through a number of activities targeting vulnerable communities in Chimanimani, Chiredzi, Chipinge, Nyanga abd Beitbridge. Amongst the cash transfer beneficiaries some (1,706 HHs) will be selected for resilience programs to boost food security. These will benefit from the following activities: • Provide drought-tolerant seeds for cereals eg rapoko, millet, sorghum and this will be done before the onset of rains October 2021 to December 2021. Total tonnage can be 26. Considering some have no arable ;land and the activity will target lead farmers and demonstration plots.• Providing seed packs for Kitchen gardens at household level, so that they access veggies etc.• Provide training, extension and advisory services to extension officers and farmers focusing on good agriculture practices with emphasis on climate-smart agriculture• Rehabilitate dip tanks and provide dipping chemicals, dip tanks are key community assets that can be rehabilitated through cash for work activities so that the community members who volunteer to work will receive an incentive. Dip tank chemicals will be ideal for free giving to community at initial stages , then after they can have a committee to fundraise for treatment of cattle.3. Protection and Gender Mainstreaming -protection and gender will be mainstreamed in the project to ensure safe and dignified access to food security

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Communities were consulted through a CVA survey which was done in April 2020. The study specifically aimed to provide an overview of the use of CVA in Zimbabwe; determine the appropriateness of CVA; and Support the choice of modality (cash/voucher), delivery mechanism, currency and financial serviceprovider (FSP). PDM surveys and Endline surveys also informed the designing of the project. In these surveys communities were consulted through face to face interviews using a questionnaire and also through focus group discussionsA community participatory approach was used during the registration process. The community did a self ranking exercise were they identified and selected the most vulnerable people in their villages.Feedback from communities is constantly collected through interactions with the beneficiaries, through PDM surveys, via help desk committees, through local leadership and also via the CRM hotline.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Collaboration and consultations with AGRITEX and Environmental Management Agency will be done before and during basic fencing of community gardens. EMA and AGRITEX will advise if a garden is at an appropriate place and will not cause siltation.Farmers who will receive smart inputs will receive training from AGRITEX where they will be taught on other ways of making soil fertile and manage pest and weeds so that they do not resort to using chemicals which can be a threat to the environment.
