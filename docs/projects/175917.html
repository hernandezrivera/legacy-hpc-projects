Project Summary

The project aims at providing meaningful access to safe water, dignified sanitation services and access to proper hygiene means and pratices to Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) hosted in communities, transit / temporary camps and resettlement / expansion areas.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Water, sanitation and hygiene activities will be implemented for the benefit of both Iternally Displaced Persons and resident people in hosting communities. Activities will be tailored according to the group of beneficiaries (e.g. resident people, Internally displaced, vulnerable groups and persons with disabilities) and to the type of hosting site (i.e. community, accommodation centre / transit camp or resettlement site).Meanwhile in the accommodation centres / transit camps the activities will have a short-term view (distributions, rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points, emergency latrines and bathing units, personal hygiene messages) and finalized to a rapid response to the emergency, the activities in the resettlement sites and hosting communities will have a more mid and long-term view (new water sources and piped networks, promotion activities for the self construction of latrines at household level through CLTS, WaSH related messages) and will stimulate the stabilization of the newcomers in the new area and within the hosting community.Specific activities will target specific groups of people, e.g. latrines and bathing units specifically designed and realized for people with disabilities, dignity kit distribuited to women and adolescent girls, hygiene kits for IDPs families.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

All the activities will be implemented in the proposed districts of Ancuabe, Chiure, Memba, Erati and Nacaroa.Water activities are:- Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level (all the sites)- Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points, including support for water committees (host communities, accomodation centers, and resettlement areas).- Construction of new water sources (host communities and resettlement areas).- Expansion of piped networks (hosting communities, resettlement sites, and hosting communities).To promote a proper sanitation both for IDPs and residents, the project will include:- Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities (accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites, as first phase)- Promotion activities for self-Construction of household latrines and garbage pits, including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households (resettlement sites and hosting communities).To guarantee access to proper personal hygiene, the responses are:- Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media (all sites)- Procurement and Distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, with focus on most vulnerable families / households (all sites)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Through a participative approach to needs assessment and project’s activity definition, specific situations of potential risks are identified and targeted. For a better understanding of the social context, both local authorities and all the different vulnerable groups are involved.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project will reinforce the already existing community bonds among the residents and IDPs, supporting their self-solidarity through the implementation of different activities for each group.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.The project will support this group of vulnerable people by realizing a specifically designed latrine. Adapted hand washing facilities will be tested and implemented.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Fostering the self construction of latrines and realizing special and emergency latrines has a positive impact on the human environment. New water sources are opened only after the results of: i) a hydro-geological survey aimed at guaranteeing enough presence of water in the aquifer; 2) a sanitary survey aimed at guaranteeing the absence of local sources of contamination of the soil in the surrounding area.To prevent over-exploitation of the acquifer, a daily use plan of the new water source will be prepared with the water committee taking into consideration the availability of water in the acquifer.
