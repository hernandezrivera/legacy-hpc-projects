Project Summary

The project is aimed to address the needs of the IDP population within sites/site-like settings, relocation sites and host community with a comprehensive CCCM approach. The intervention will respond to three layers of needs: basic life-saving assistance in the priorities areas identified by the Cluster; community engagement and protection mainstreaming approach.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at targeting the displaced and host vulnerable population within Montepuez and Quissanga districts with a multi-purpose response including three main areas of intervention:i) Protection, community participation and engagement; ii) First relief services provision;iii) site structural organization, planning and development. First and foremost, the intervention will ensure the participation of all groups of the population in the decision making and the site/camp governance structure so that the basic rights of the displaced population are observed. This objective will be achieved through establishing diversified, transparent and community based Complaint and Feedback Mechanism, (CFM) and with the creation of the integrated site Management committee, respecting the diversity of groups residing in the area and ensuring inclusion of the mostvulnerable groups. The estimated number of the people in need that will be targeted are: in Montepuez 27.538 individuals in which 14,786 are women and 12,752 are men. In Quissanga, the project will target 4,782 individuals: 2,216 are men and 2,566 are women.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Montepuez & Quissanga - Capacity Building to the Site Management Committees, host Community leaders, local authorities and local development partners. Site coordination and planning meetings, development training sessions and maintenance related activities, limited to "permanent" relocation sites.Community Engagement/AAP: meaningful participation, leadership structures, CFMs in all sites, through static presence on site and regular meetings;Information Management: site level indicators, dis-aggregated data for service providers programmatic prioritization: at least 1 headcount per year, plus monthly update on site profiles, biweekly updates for service providers meeting. Surveys: intentions, community level information gathering to inform trends/returns/movements: 2 multi sector survey per site and FGD with communities to discuss results and way forward. Mobile/remote management for hard to reach or implementation in districts of return where populations are not yet able to fully return: planned for Quissanga district.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential harms and risks may arise in the general access to re-allocation procedures, access to services provision, with special concerns towards vulnerable categories, such as un-accompanied minors, female headed households, elderly, people with disabilities, victims of abuse. AVSI will ensure participation of all groups within the decision making process and governance designing of the re-allocation/re-settelement area. Also, through constant monitoring – both through the multiple CFM and routinary HHs assessments – and sensitizstion campaigning on protection standards, PSEAH, GBV prevention the community will be capacitated and well informed on rights and prerogatives, as well as on referral and complaints systems. To avoid the major protection risks of resistance, exploitment and/or conflict between IDP and host community consultation meeting will be carried out in order to create a community engagement comprehensive mechanism.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project is mainly based on the strong participation of the beneficiaries along the intervention process. Before the beginning of the project interventions, focus group activities will be planned to understand what are the beneficiaries expectation concerning project impact. The meetings will be organized and held in coordination with the community leaders and the existing government structure (provincial, district and administrative levels) at community level. Firstly, community’s opinion on how toadapt the general aspects of the interventions and coordination according to local culture, customs, beliefs and capacity, will be taken into account and incorporated into the work methodology. Secondly, it will be created a specific hotline for feedback and complaints related to the project and it will be promoted the Linha Verde. It is worthy to note that AVSI has adopted an internal policy on accountability to Affected People on the March 23th, 2020 based on the IASC Commitments on accountability to Affected Populations (CAAP).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. This emphacises into prioritizing the above mentioned target population as to the assistance for Shelter, NFI and Protection, especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support. Moreover, it is worthy to note that very often people with disabilities are discriminated within their own communities. This can be a compounding factor when people with disabilities arrived in a host community, as in the case of IDPs. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy efficiency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal. In addition of that, it will be prioritazed the use of local materialsin order to adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. Moreover, with a strong community engagement and participation; on rehabilitation activities, specific attention regarding the site selection and sourcing of materials, favouring the use of local resources. Finally AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
