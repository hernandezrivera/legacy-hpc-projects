Project Summary

The project aims to expand protection interventions and Gender Based Violence (GBV) services for 4200 beneficiaries in Child Protection (1700 boys, 1700 girls, 400 women and 400 man), for 2700 beneficiaries in GBV (2700 women and girls), and for 2300 beneficiaries in General Protection (545 boys, 545 girls, 605 women and 605 man) in the areas of Yabroud, Jaramana, and Babella. While Aram Society operates two centers (in Yabroud and Jaramana), where protection services, PSS and self-defense activities are provided. Through assessments and interviews with community leaders, the need to extend these interventions to include CM and GBV CM services, and to Babella and rural Yabroud has been identified, and planned to be targeted by mobile teams, to implement PSS, GBV, self-define, and case management activities
The centers will be rehabilitated to be accessible safe spaces for women, children and PWDs, opened and have running activities from Sunday to Thursday, and the mobile team will implement activities for 4 days, the 5th to prepare plans and reports (4 days mobile team's activities in Babella, and for Yabroud mobile team two days for Rima and two days for Ras Alain), every mobile team will contain CP, GP and GBV animators with CP and GBV case managers
Additionally, ARAM Society conducted field visits, and this project is a response for part of the needs in the targeted areas, according to ARAM assessments and interviews, HNO, and the protection severity scale, these areas need to be targeted in Protection and GBV, while these locations were affected by the years of war in Syria, also there is a need to conduct capacity building for the project staff about protection and GBV mainstreaming and PSEA. These trainings will include an explanation of all violations and the consequences that result from them, in addition to reporting mechanisms in the event of suspicion that one of these violations has occurred
To ensure that the project is accountable and responsive to the needs and expectations of the affected population, the following AAP, feedback, and complaints mechanisms will be established:
AAP mechanisms: the project will establish an AAP mechanism to ensure that the voices of the affected population are heard and taken into account in the project design, implementation, and evaluation processes
Feedback and Complaints mechanisms: The project will establish feedback and complaints mechanisms to receive, document, and respond to feedback, complaints, and suggestions from the affected population. The feedback and complaints mechanisms will be accessible, transparent, and confidential
Response mechanisms: The project will establish response mechanisms to address the feedback, complaints, and suggestions received from the affected population. The response mechanisms will ensure that the feedback, complaints, and suggestions are analyzed, documented, and acted upon
Monitoring and Evaluation mechanisms: The project will establish monitoring and evaluation mechanisms to track the performance of the feedback and complaints mechanisms. The monitoring and evaluation mechanisms will ensure that the feedback and complaints mechanisms are effective

ARAM Society already work in Yabroud and Jaramana centers in GP, CP, and GBV activities, and already fill the 4Ws for the Protection sector, also sent requested to GBV and Child Protection sectors to be involved and to be able to send reports about the implemented activities, all the implemented during the two previous years were prevention activities, while ARAM Society focused on self-define activities for children and women to impower them to face any situation they may me involved in, also PSS activities and awareness raising sessionsBut the current plan now is to target new areas (Rima, Ras Alain, and Babella) through mobile teams, and expand services to include case management, because of its importance in supporting these most vulnerable groups, and supporting and helping them to overcome past memories and trauma.And this project also aims to retrain staff and volunteers about protection and GBV mainstreaming, and PSEA strategy, and to aware beneficiaries about the violations they may notice, and about the way to report it to protect themselves and protect their children from any risk in ARAM Society intervention locations, by customizing 10 mins from al activities to remind beneficiaries of the complains methods(boxes, phone numbers, email, Facebook page), also there will be posters with complain methods, and information about Protection and risks hung throughout the centersThe project will also address risks(like exposure to Exposure to physical security/violence including GBV, abuse and exploitation, exclusion /physical access, discrimination, selection criteria not understood), assumptions, and mitigation measures (by consult different segments of affected population on NEEDS, type of assistance, modalities of interventions most suitable for them, consult women and girls on possible risks associated with the assistance delivered, assess possible negative consequences/ impact of programs, reflect on modality in which assistance is provided, reflect on the inclusivity of the target and analyze how each target group, consider adequate resources to ensure quality delivery consider feedback mechanisms and remedial action, consider adequate resources to monitor and evaluate the impact, consider adding qualitative indicators to monitor the measures implemented to mitigate the risk of GBV) to ensure the safety and well-being of all individuals involved.
