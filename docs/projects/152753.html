Project Summary

In the 12 months of 2019 CIDO plans to provide life-saving emergency S/NFI in a safety and dignity to vulnerable populations ( Men women, boys and Girls ), focusing on internally displaced persons (IDPs) and returnees at HH level .The IDPS targeted are those displaced by the protracted political conflict in South Sudan as well as secondary displacements. The project will target female headed households, unaccompanied elderly, people with no links to the community, separated children, women at risk (pregnant and lactating mothers), child-headed HH, people living with disability and returnees assessed and verified to be in need The counties targeted include Duk, Akobo and Ayod counties of Jonglei State. CIDO intends to implement both static ( specifically in Duk county ) and mobile response activities ( Specifically in Akobo and Ayod) with an overall objective of saving lives which would otherwise been lost as a result of protection risks ,exposure to diseases and harsh weather linked to absence of or poor shelter conditions . Through coordination with other partners and multi sector integration of protection component, CIDO will support positive coping mechanisms among beneficiaries and advocate for durable solutions to S/NFI needs. CIDO will prioratirize CBI in Akobo in lieu of the fact that a high influx of returnees are expected to arrive from the border of Ethiopia and the presence of cross country trade. This will also be determined by the market research. The NFI cluster coordination team both at national and state level will be engaged to support the interventions right from assessments, verification, distribution and PDM. The complaint desk at distribution points and the overall feedback and complaint mechanisms to be established will play a key role in providing critical information for CIDO’s M&E team. The project is estimated to cost $163,500

Needs

Thousands of displaced people have been affected in the political conflict of South Sudan . Hundreds are trapped in hide outs without access to humanitarian assistance while others are refugees in neighbouring countries. Critical social amenities like schools and hospitals were destroyed and burnt down in the conflict. Jonglei State experienced intense fighting with an estimated 150,000 lives lost , houses and property burnt to ashes. In Akobo, Duk and Ayod counties un verified number of men and women together with their children fled to Ethiopia (OCHA Humanitarian Bulletin, April 2018) furthermore host communities in Ayod were forced to leave their homes to protect themselves (IRNA February 2018) an estimated 12000 IDPS are living in Akobo. Duk County has experienced multiple displacement with high demand for essential humanitarian assistance that include NFI/ES support amidst access challenges. CIDO has witnessed a serious need for NFI/ES in its FSL operation in this area. IDPs, new returnees and vulnerable host communities most explicitly women and children are exposed to rain, sun, wind, cold weather and other life-threatening harsh environmental conditions in their current location due to loss of properties. Jonglei being one of the malaria prone zone in addition other natural hazards. The Collapse of basic human service like health, education and functional markets due to hyperinflation is an indication that the projected returnees may not afford to buy new NFI items however this project is guided by the market research and recommendations of a report of CSRF (Conflict Sensitivity Resource Facility) conducted in February March 2018 which projects that CBI are feasible .CIDO places itself to respond positively to NFI/ES through this proposed project in coordination with the cluster and other partners to show direct accountability to the vulnerable IDPs and host communities of Duk county. CIDO has the capacity to respond to these highlighted needs.CIDO has the experience of working with communities in Duk, Akobo and Ayod to scale up Nutrition under the SUN Movement thus the organization is best place to respond in these locations. In the past 12 months, CIDO’s team has understood the cluster methodology and approaches having participated in the cluster meetings, capacity building trainings, assessments in 2018. CIDO is an active member of S/NFI cluster in Jonglei state, Central Equatoria State and at national level. The organization also sits at MHPSS technical working group.CIDO has competent staff and experience in distribution of Multi-sector survival kits. Dignity kits, solar lamps and kangas in Greater Equatoria. As an active protection partner CIDO will bring to the cluster a wealth of experience in mainstreaming GBV, HLP and conflict sensitive approaches.CIDO gained significant experience in delivering CBI interventions in its GBV programming in Yei County. The project provided conditional cash transfers for 400 women and girls who having been trained managed to make and use fuel efficient energy stoves as a GBV mitigation strategy. To build on the CBI experience CIDO will work with INTERSOS and Cash working Group. CIDO intends to work with other cluster partners in joint missions to deliver quality programming and continue learning from Monitoring Evaluation and Learning (MEAL) activities.

Activities

1. CIDO will adopt an integrated approach and collaborate with FSL, WASH, Protection, Nutrition and Education partners to rebuild lives through coordinated, integrated and targeted shelter and NFIs solutions by coordinating joint assessments and analysis and response plans. 2. CIDO will participate in at least six coordinated needs assessments for in-kind response focusing on S/NFI, WASH, FSL, Protection, Education, Nutrition and at least six coordinated need assessment and analysis focusing more on the protection risk, gender issues and people with disabilities. 3. CIDO will implement CBI interventions based on functional and accessible markets through conducting rapid market assessments, needs analysis and provision of conditional or unconditional cash to beneficiaries .The analysis will help to generate the risk mitigation matrix to guide the modality for CBI. The team will be keen to involve stakeholders (local authority and partners) in conducting participatory community sensitization and awareness on Housing, Land and Property (HLP) through community meetings . CIDO will coordinate with cluster partners on ground to conduct needs analysis for In-kind response and support mobile response team in the whole response. 4. Conduct beneficiaries' identification, varication and registration based on the most critical needs (( gender , age and vulnerability). The needs assessment and analysis will guide identification of the most vulnerable displaced population with critical needs. The NFI team will collaborate with both male and female community leaders of all categories of targeted beneficiaries to identify and register the most vulnerable individuals. 5. Conduct Emergency shelter materials/ or loose NFIs distribution. CIDO will ensure the distribution is coordinated and conducted in line with procedures of core pipeline. NFIs will be provided by the core pipeline 6. Facilitate voucher distribution of unconditional but restricted commodity vouchers for locally available shelter materials. CIDO will provide unconditional but restricted commodity vouchers for locally available shelter materials in order to leverage resilience and coping mechanism of displaced population. The distribution committee will be established comprising of equal ratio of men and women to support effective complain/feedback mechanism and to support effective messaging on use of NFIs. 7. Conduct awareness on Housing Land and Property (HLP) – In coordination with protection partners, CIDO will conduct awareness on HLP and conduct referrals of HLP cases to protection partners using the existing referral pathway. 8. Conduct PDM- CIDO will conduct PDMs using the cluster tools to ensure fast, life-saving response to the right people and enhance ability to use S/NFIs for supporting results-based health, protection, livelihood, and hygiene needs

Centrality of Protection in humanitarian action
Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO being an active Protection Cluster partner has conducted a protection Mapping and risk analysis exercise of the targeted beneficiaries. Both exercises provided essential information on groups and individuals who are at risk, the reasons why they are at risk and how, while taking into account the specific vulnerabilities that underlie these risks. The project therefore take into consideration specific and unique needs for men, women, girls and boys, and groups such as internally displaced persons, older persons, persons with disabilities, and persons belonging to sexual and other minorities and most importantly how to mitigate the identified risks.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In reference to the risk analysis exercise, it is noted that some beneficiaries could be falling under the elderly, Physically handicapped or under Persons Special needs (PSNs) categories. Under such circumstances, the project activities are designed to give priority to safety and dignity of all men, women boys and girls and prevent negative coping strategies and mitigate risks including issues of discrimination in regards to accessing services. Through regular coordination meetings, partners on ground will be encouraged to train the frontline workers on disability mainstreaming and also report and share protection concerns for persons with specific needs.
