Project Summary

WFP’s programme focuses on preventing malnutrition amongst the most vulnerable populations through strengthening supplementary feeding with focus on the first 1000days of life. The programme targets all children aged 6-23 months MAM children 24-59 months
in the most food insecure departments presenting highest GAM rates in the North,
Adamawa and East region, The programme activities include: Screening for malnutrition
and referral of severe cases for treatment, Social and behavioral change communication
in favor of appropriate infant and young child feeding practices and other Family
practices, distribution of specialized nutritious food to children aged 6-23months and pregnant and lactating women an girls in food insecure household in the NWSW regions afected by the Crisis. key project activities include distribution os specialised nutritious food, screening and referal of cases of acute malnutrition to relevant treatment programmes , social behaviour change comunication on infant and young child feeding practices.WFP ensures that its programmes take into account the needs of men, women, girls and boys, as well as people with special needs. Distribution points are located in locations that do not pose a particular risk to women's access. Practical procedures are taken to reduce queues at distribution points, and to give priority to pregnant women, people with disabilities and the elderly. WFP’s programmes are designed and implemented with due consideration of population protection concerns or barriers for people with specific needs for safe and dignified access to Nutrition services.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The consequences of the various humanitarian crises in Cameroon are aggravatingfactors for the deterioration of the already fragile nutritional status of affectedpopulations. Indeed, the prevalence of stunting in children under 5 is above the WHOserious threshold of 30% in all regions affected by humanitarian crises and rates of acute malnutrition do not show significant changes for several years and remain on the poor thresholds. Children under the age of 5 with acute malnutrition are more likely to die thantheir healthy peers. Pregnant and lactating women are also particularly affected because they have limited access to adequate quality and quantity of food in a context of limited access to basic social services. In a context of massive displacement of people (internally displaced persons orrefugees), women and young children are particularly exposed to poor living conditionsand the accumulation of trauma that can affect practices feeding infants and young children, in particular the ability of mothers to ensure optimal breastfeeding, further weakening the nutritional status of the already most vulnerable populations. The level ofacute malnutrition in the North West and southwest regions are not well known asaccess constraints have hampered the realization of a formal nutrition survey. Nutritionindicators has been integrated into emergency food security assessment (EFSA) conducted in October 2019 and revealed a proxy Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) rates of5.1% and 7.4% for the NW and SW regions respectively. The nutrition situation is likely todeteriorate in the coming months since acute malnutrition in children aged 6-59 months isa direct outcome indicator of recent changes in the nutritional status which could be attributed to changes in food security, health, and WASH situations among others considering access challenges and the very low coverage of emergency nutrition response activities in 2020. WFP’s programmefocuses on preventing malnutrition amongst the most vulnerable populations through strengthening supplementary feeding with focus on the first 1000 days of life. Theprogramme targets all children aged 6-23 months and pregnant and lactating women and girls in a food-insecure household. with a package of activities including, distribution of specialised nutritious food, screening and referral of malnourished cases to relevant programmes for treatment and social behaviour change communication in favour of appropriate IYCF practices in emegency. WFP in 2020 intends to target about 40,000beneficiaries in the NWSW regions
