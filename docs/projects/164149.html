Start Date

Project Summary

The project aims to access to formal and informal education safely for girls and boys at school age affected by crises (5-17 years)
The project is establishing tents in camps that have received new numbers of displaced people in the recent period
in the north of Syria and Preparated it to receive children (3-17 years, girls/boys) In the following areas :
(Kafar Naha/ the finished part from the project in 2019 / - Toum - Daret Azza / deir samaan, bsratun - Afrin)
And support this educational process as required, and Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns and present the curriculum through an intensive curriculum so that the student can join in his appropriate capacity in the nearest formal school After the student has completed the intensive curriculum within 3 month
Note: tents' numbers were specified according to statics that made by our team in the mentioned areas.
4 tents will be established in Deir Samaan, two for primary, two for secondary (male-female), comprising 120 students at one stage within 3 months.
4 tents will be established in Afrin, two for primary, two for secondary (male-female), comprising 120 students at one stage within 3 months.
3 tents will be established in the Toum Camp, one for primary,two for secondary (male-female), 90 students at one stage within 3 months.
3 tents will be established in Bastroon camp, one for primary, two for secondary (male-female), 90 students at one stage within 3 months.
7 classes have been rehabilitated in five schools in Kafr Naha, and it includes 210 students for one stage in 2019.
66 teachers (male and female) will be appointed to secondary school, 15 (male-female) teachers for the primary stage.
9 (male-female) mentors.
7 (male-female) administrators.
The first phase of this project was finished in Kafr Naha, Khan Al-Asal, through 7 classrooms in five schools, and the second stage is the Toum camp, which was established by our organization on 01/09/2019 and still in progress.
Daret Azza / dier samaan, Basratun - Afrin will be in later stages With the start of the second semester
Based on the reports of HNO-2020 and the questionnaire of the data collection team of our organization that was conducted between 01/28/2020 until 06/02/2020, where it was found necessary for rapid intervention in the educational process and its importance
(Note that the numbers are changing on a daily basis and with significant differences due to the military actions, the continuous displacement and the continuous movement of the IDPs)
In the Toum camp:
Work has stopped after a short period of its start due to the last bombing, and we will return to work when the area calms down again.
Note: 22.25% of the project has been completed in 2019.
The project was partially secured by our organization

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Violence against education facilities and education providers:All attacks will be reported to the Attacks on Education Reporting Mechanism.Learning facilities have safety protocols in placeResources include walkie-talkies, basic firefighting equipment, etc. Caregivers know the learning facility’s safety protocols and what will happen in case of a security event.Preparedness training such as self-care, first aid, etc drills will be regularly practiced. Students, teachers will be provided with psychosocial support services. For needs that are beyond what should be reasonably expected from learning services, there will be a referral system to specialized protection services.- Physical punishment or bullying in schools: Learning facilities have an enforced code of conduct and policy on bullying. Students and their caregivers will be sensitized on the prevention, and issues related to, physical punishment and bullying and the systems in place to present and respond to it. Students, their caregivers, teachers, and school personnel will be encouraged to report issues and have safe access to a trusted person they can report instances of physical punishment or bullying. Those services have the capacity, ability, and resources to address basic needs.Sexual Exploitation and Abuse:Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies was in place, practiced and monitored.The targeted communities and learning facilities will be aware of the organization’s policies and procedures.Organizations will ensure a survivor-centered approach that ensures the safety and confidentiality of the affected person. The survivor has a say in the processes.
