Project Summary

The project aims at ensuring access to life-saving emergency referral services for the communities living in the city of Beira, especially for the most vulnerable categories, namely pregnant women and children under five, covering a total population of 592,090 inhabitants. Moreover it aims at strengthening the MNCH services offered at the HCB, by supporting some rehabilitations of the department of Maternal and New-born care. The main activities are the following: 1) supporting the newly established Emergency Medical Service organized with 5 ambulances located in 5 key health centers of the city of Beira (those more populated). Such a service aims at referring all paediatric and obstretric emergencies coming from all health centers of the District City of Beira (15) and some of the neighboring districts to the Central Hospital of Beira in order save preventable deaths due to obstetrics complications and/or paediatric emergencies. A parallel but necessary intervention to ensure sustainability to the intervention as well as quality care offered to the patients, is the provision of support to the Beira Central Hospital (Tertiary Hospital and referral CEmONC for the entire Province of Sofala and Central Region of Mozambique) in procurement of equipment and in some minor repairs for the rehabilitation of the Maternal and Child health department of the Central Hospital of Beira, since the old one has been highly damaged by the Cyclone. This building would include a labor ward, operating theatre to perform caesarean sections and a newborn care department.
