Project Summary

The proposed activities will improve equitable access to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services through systemic identification, referral, and treatment of acute malnourished case boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women. It will also provide holistic life-saving and life sustaining nutrition intervention DEH intends to continue its current nutrition intervention in Galgadud and Mudug region through activating the static side and mobile outpatient therapeutic program sites hence provide active community case finding, screening, admission and treatment of severe acute malnutrition cases boys and girl <5 children in the targeted internally displaced person.DEH shall enhance referral services to the effective and most complicated cases to the nearest stabilization centre to enable provide adequate attention to the severely malnourished boys, girls among the internally displaced person. DEH shall also provide routine immunization, deworming and vitamin A supplementation services to <5 children boys and girls as well as multiple micronutrient to the target beneficiaries.DEH shall provide integrated management of acute malnutrition and basic service for nutrition packages training packages to its technical nutrition staff as well as community health workers to enable them deliver effective management of the IMAM programs in the target Internally displaced person in Dhusamareeb.DEH shall be able to provide integrated management of acute malnutrition and basic service for nutrition packages training package training packages to its technical nutrition staff as well as community health workers to enable them deliver effective management of the integrated management acute malnutrition programs in the target internally displaced person and host communities.The project will work closely with food security,WASH,health and protection cluster to ensure improved the life of the vulnerable population both Internally displaced person s,returnees and drought/floods affected vulnerable girls, boys and PLWs in the target area

Needs

Somalia is one of the top ten countries with the highest prevalence of malnutrition in the world and the third highest in the eastern and southern Africa region, at 17.4 percent Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) amongst children under five years, with 3.2 percent being severely malnourished. The projected number of children who are, or who will be acutely malnourished, has increased since the beginning of the year, nearly 954,007 children are expected to be acutely malnourished, including 173,594 who are expected to be severely malnourished during the next one year period (Sep 2018-Sep 2019). Severely malnourished children are nine times more likely to die of killer diseases such as acute watery diarrhoea (AWD)/cholera and measles. Predictably the drought impact combined with the ongoing conflict is a manifestation of the rising malnutrition outcomes. According to Somalia severity ranking Galgadud region have displacement population of 38,044 people with 4,106 AWD/cholera cases (72%) and high measles cases of 475 (8%) The severity was also evidence within the various district of Haradheere, Ceelbur, Hobyo and Adado where Ceelbur have a total of 15,152 <5 children and 7,061 pregnant and lactating women with SAM and GAM of 4.6 and 20.0% respectively as well as emergency caseload of 3,375 SAM and 14,688 GAM across all the target district of Haradheere, Hobyo and Adado where (Haradheere GAM=9.8%, SAM 2.1%, Hobyo SAM 2.0 and GAM 14.9%,Adado SAM 4.1% and GAM 22.4%) as indicated by Gu 2018 nutrition assessment per district prevalence hence high needs of urgent life-saving nutrition services.DEH relief therefore, intend to scale up its current nutrition intervention to the vulnerable house through provision of improved therapeutic treatment, micronutrient supplementation as well nutrition, health and hygiene promotion and vitamin A supplementation to enable save life of the vulnerable boys, girls and women in the target district.

Activities

1. Screen, admit and treat under five girls and boys and pregnant and lactating women suspected of malnutrition; treat common illness among 3000 boys and 3000 girls with SAM in the established OTPs, and refer those with complications to the nearest SC.2. Provide Albendazole for deworming of 3000 girls and 3000 boys and 2000 PLW who meet the deworming criteria.3. Provide micro nutrition’s (Vitamin A, zinc/ORs, and MMN) to 3000 girls and 3000 boys and 2000 PLWs in the OTP program who are eligible for enrolment. 5. Conduct 24 outreach drama session to disseminate key messages on nutrition, health and hygiene; 6. Train 30 CHWs, care givers & grandmothers, volunteers and field staff (attached to OTPs) on BSNP, IMAM, , active case finding, referral and health and nutrition education at community level7. Conduct group nutrition counselling/ health and nutrition education at DEH OTP centers in Ceelbur and Haradheere district

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The project areas have high number of mixed pastoral and IDPs as well as scattered rural areas in Ceelbur and Haradhere as well as coastal deh with different livelihood status, due the diverse in the nature of the target beneficiaries the rural communities displaced might not welcome positively to the IDPs and conflict between the previous establish IDPs managers and newly displaced might conflict hence creating community tension, DEH will ensure to provide better coordination and information sharing when such eventuality hits the target community, also on the employment issues there might be discrimination or disparities between the women and men and to address this DEH will ensure to competitively sources the local community with expertise for employment considering women in equal measures this will then reduce the risk of gender disparities and minimized community expectation.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Lack of desegregated data impedes well planned interventions based on needs. HIDIG intends to overcome this by having a need based assessment and disaggregated data so that the special needs of youth out of school and those with special gender needs are taken care of. Location, distance and access routes to the OTP sites and services might be a challenge of preventing factors limiting the access of the OTP sites DEH will enable its beneficiaries have accessible to all of the boys and girls, including the most vulnerable IDPs children. To support the inclusion of women DEH will support training of female members through employment and capacity enhancement. To ensure equity, OTP mobile/outreach teams will be used to reach rural communities as well as the IDPs and host communities, limited information and communication might hinder the understanding between DEH teams in the field and community leaders, however, to mitigate any such eventuality DEH will create feedback mechanism and reasonable compliance officer within its office as well as hotline mobile services to ensure all beneficiary complains are addressed timely

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Project staff will be trained on the basic human right issues and approaches to enable them understand the principle of equity and ensure those fleeing communities were well attended, the project will consult the communities where treatment centers will be place in avoidance of near military settlement as well as to avoid the risk associate with landmines and explosive targets.DEH will ensure all the structure within the OTP sites are user friendly to disable and marginalized and minority children boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women.
