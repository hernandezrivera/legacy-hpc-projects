Project Summary

This emergency response was triggered by shortage of sufficient quantities of safe water for human and animal consumption coupled with poor hygiene and sanitation situation, death of livestock, which is the main source of livelihood and nutrition for pastoralist communities. Specifically drying of water sources, dropping of water supply below 5 lit/person/per day, increased distance to fetch water by over 10km and lack or limited capacity of local government and other partners to respond. In addition, critical shortage of pasture and water for animals and deteriorating livestock body condition, high numbers of animal deaths and expected loss of livestock herds, risk of outbreak of animal disease, and shortage of veterinary drugs and capacity to respond to livestock health issues and the subsequent regional and local government appeal triggered the response.
According to the national displacement report, the IDP numbers in Oromia region is almost 91,337 households comprising 506,133 IDPs were identified in 440 different IDP sites within the region and among the total IDP community there are many person with specific groups with significant in number such as pregnant women and girls, Breastfeeding mothers, persons with mental or physical disabilities, Persons with chronic diseases, Single-female headed households, Single-male headed households, Vulnerable older persons who need support but do not have care givers and Child-headed households are the major categories. In addition to that Across 404 villages (34%), it was reported that services to resolve disputes over housing, land and property exist. However, there were no protection services available in 398 villages (34%). The number of IDP community in Borena zone is nearly 21,180 households and a total of 132,676 individuals sheltered in 25 IDP sites.
HelpAge has been operational in Borena and currently it is implementing an integrated humanitarian response project in seven woredas of Borena and Bale zones through inclusive humanitarian action where most vulnerable groups are supported to access essential services. The project also addresses the protection needs of vulnerable households through evidence-based programing by conducting regular protection monitoring to identify protection risks.
The project will target 79,045 (40,069 Female) beneficiaries living in the four woredas of Borena zone. Mainly households with Older Persons/PwDs, very poor older women & men-headed households, and those without productive resources and school aged children will be targeted. The selection of the specific households will be done in collaboration with local government structures and the Kebele level targeting committee (to be set up at the beginning of the project). The beneficiaries will be verified through community meetings, which includes measures to ensure accountability to affected people. Protection will be mainstreamed into the project and interventions will be aligned to the Humanitarian inclusion standards and age, gender and disability to ensure inclusive programming. HAI standard procedures will be put in place to mitigate safeguarding risks during implementation.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1: Provide water trucking service for two months to communities severely affected by the drought reaching a total of 5,330 households.Activity 2: Provide water trucking to 16 schools and health facilities in the targeted woredas to complement the school feeding program, facilitate lifesaving and emergency health service provision for 3,645 (1,531 female) students.Activity 3: Installation of Plastic water reservoirs with water harvesting technology to allow for sustainable access to safe drinking water in 12 schools and one water scheme (Higo site) beyond the drought period. Activity 4: Sanitation and Hygiene promotion at community level and schools through community mobilisation using community volunteers and conducting public awareness raising sessions benefiting a total of 8,012 (4,046 female) individuals. Activity 5: Water scheme rehabilitation and maintenance (3 motorized schemes) in Dubuluk and Dire woreda to reach 15,020 individuals (7,585 female). Activity 6: Rehabilitation of 10 Shallow wells in Miyo and Guchi woreda benefitting a total of 3,457 individuals (1,765 female)Activity 7: Provision of WASH NFIs including dignity kits for most affected and at-risk 600 households reaching 3,000 individuals including women and girls.Activity 8: Provide capacity building / refresher training for 13 WASHCOs on the water scheme management and maintenance in the four woredas.Activity 9: Provide maintenance equipment and tools to the 4 woreda water offices.Activity 2.1: Conditional cash support (for food and animal feeding) to 2,400 most vulnerable community groupsActivity 2.2: Post distribution monitoring for project deliverables
