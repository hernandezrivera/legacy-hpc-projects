Start Date

01/01/2025

End Date

31/12/2025

Project Summary

• Improve access of host communities and internal displaced people to quality HIV, TB, hepatitis prevention, care and treatment services including other chronic diseases treatment,
• Strengthen health workers and Community Health Workers (CHW) to facilitate the provision of quality and adequate prevention, care and treatment services for HIV, TB, Hepatitis, and other chronic diseases (Diabetic, HBP)
• Reduce the number of loss to follow up among Host Community and IDP's under ARV and TB treatment
• Strengthen monitoring and evaluation systems to improve the quality and effectiveness of HIV and TB prevention and treatment programs
