Project Summary

The 2018/19 rainfall season was among the worst on record in parts of the country characterized by significantly below average rainfall with drought conditions across much of the country. Compared to the El Nino 2015/16 rainfall season, this season had more widespread rainfall deficits, especially in the north and west parts of the country. The effects of the El Nino induced drought are seen in water sources depleting and groundwater levels going down.
According to the ZimVAC RLA 2019, the economy performed poorly, characterised by:
• Lack of decent and secure employment.
• Liquidity challenges for both local and foreign currency
• The use of alternative modes of payment
• Fast changing prices of basic commodities resulting in depressed purchasing power of the vulnerable populations in the country which was exacerbated by stagnant salaries and wages. Payment for services has significantly declined thereby reducing services such as water supply to communities. After the breakdown of water points, communities do not prioritise repair as priorities are around food security.
Nationally 37% of households utilized basic water services as their alternative source of drinking water. The proportion of households utilizing surface water increased from 8% to 17% when households adopt an alternative source of drinking water, thereby increasing the population at risk of waterborne diseases. The proportion of households using unimproved water is highest in Gokwe North (64%). Other districts with relatively high usage of surface water included Gwanda (30%). Gokwe North had the highest proportion of households (45%) travelling for more than 1 kilometre to access water points, Gwanda 41% and Mbire 24% .
Open defecation was practiced by 33% of households nationally, with Gokwe North 62.9%, Tsholotsho 61.85%, Gwanda 30.04%, Mbire 20.88%, Mt Darwin 17.55%1.
It is therefore important to respond by improving access to water for both people and livestock. It is against this background that this project seeks to reduce deaths and incidences of water related diseases through the drilling, repair and rehabilitation of existing water points and hygiene promotion in Gwanda, Tsholotsho, Gokwe North, Mt Darwin and Mbire districts.

1 - Activities
