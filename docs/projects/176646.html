Project Summary

The project aims (1) to ensure that refugees and asylum-seeers access life-saving assistance; (2) to provide refugees and asylum-seekers life-sustaining support, such as self-reliance and livelihood; (3) to assist them with individual documentation and legal support; and (4) to protect them from violence and exploitation.
Target beneficiaries include: 27,000 refugees and asylum seekers, as well as members of their host community, government stakeholders and civil society partners.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

UNHCR will undertake the following activities: 1) Community outreach of refugees and asylum-seekers outside Maratane camp ensure that they are registered and there are two-way communications between them and UNHCR/ its partners to monitor protection risks and proide support2) Provide support in documentation and access to legal assistance. 3) Protection monitoring in Maratane Refugee camp: the aim of identifying protection gaps and incidents, conducting advocacy with Government for refugee inclusion in national services 4) Protection services for persons with specific needs: UNHCR will undertake case management of persons with specific needs, including GBV survivors and children at risk, and will provide specialized services such as psychosocial support, legal assistance (specially for legal documentation and birth registration)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The protection risks are identified through UNHCR strong protection presence in the field in Nampula (covering Nyassa as well) and Cabo Delgado. Protection teams conduct regular monitoring in the field and talk directly to people of concern who report their needs and the risk they face. Appropriate response to these risks is discussed by the target beneficiaries as in coordination with relevant state stakeholders. That way the response is based on the needs with due consideration to the context. that way, host community beneficiaries are also covered when required to reduce the risk of tensions if interventions are only targeting refugees and asylum seekers where the same needs are also been identified in the host communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project works in close collaboration with community representatives and reaches out to the larger community through focus group discussions and individual meetings.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Through protection assessment visits, including home visits, as well as access to feedback/complaint mechanisms, the most vulnerable individuals are identified and given the opportunity to forward their needs and to give a feedback on the quality of assistance received from the project. This is particularly the case during post-distribution monitoring.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project does not have a big environmental impact. For shelter assistance in particular, access to shelter material will promote local material, with due consideration on preventing deforestation.
