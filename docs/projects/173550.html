Project Summary

Chameg`s intervention is envisioned to improve GBV, shelter and NFIs response through increased access to temporary shelter, NFIs , GBV services and provision of immediate and comprehensive lifesaving services to 3000 women and men survivors with ages ranging from 10 to 35 years including persons with disability. The project will be focused in the Manyu division of Cameroon covering all the four sub division including Mamfe center, upper Bayang, Eyumojock, and Akwaya Sub –Divisions. This project will ensure accountability toward the affected population and Prevention against Sexual Expliotation and Abuse (PSEA) are understood. As such, feedback and complaints mechanisms will be put in place and used by all (including sensitive complaints). It will also ensure that affected people understand the behavior they should expect from aid actors and be informed on the PSEA survivor assistance package
1. Psychosocial support services (PSS) including psychosocial counseling, psychosocial first aid (PFA), social networking and access to information on available GBV services, through women and girls friendly spaces/safe spaces, and establishment and strengthening of GBV referral pathways (to enable access to comprehensive GBV services including legal, medical and livelihood services) and provision of emergency shelters, NFIs to most vulnerable and displaced female headed and people living with disability special need in the target communities, including IDP,returnees and Host communities in the target locations.
2. Chameg will also strengthen GBV risk reduction through strengthening and engaging community protection groups,
distribution of dignity kits, whistles and solar lanterns to vulnerable women, girls.
3.Provide capacity building and trainings to community volunteers/workers and GBV service providers to enhance service provision
and risk reduction for women and girls, and conduct safety audits for GBV risk assessments to inform the project implementation and decisions, among other interventions. The proposed project will therefore increase accessibility, quality and
Coordination of GBV, shelter, and NFIs.
4. Chameg will work with various stakeholders to ensure women and girls are protected from GBV and receive support when they experience harm. Some of the stakeholders include line ministries at state government level, the local administration, the police, and women leadership at community level, community and religious leaders and will establish protection networks for women and girls. Community action plans and protection mechanisms will be developed and implemented to reduce protection risks for women
and girls including those in IDP settings.
5. The proposed intervention will additionally address Shelter and NFIs through the immediate material needs of newly displaced populations in Eyumojock, mamfe Central and Upper Bayang including, the most vulnerable by conducting timely protection and shelter assessments and distributing the essential emergency kits (emergency shelter/NFI) as well as, referrals of protection and other needs and some limited direct provision of protection services to affected communities, particularly persons with specific needs.
6. The project will assist in capacity building local authorities, IDP committees etc. on IDP rights/obligations. In addition, the project will also aim to mainstream protection across sectors through advice on effective implementation of protection-sensitive and rights - based approach into the relief assistance for other humanitarian actors/ clusters – PWG/All sectors, training and sensitizing duty bearers and other key stakeholder on protection concerns including GBV, and freedom and safety of movement.

Awareness activities will be intensified during the marking of international days like the international women’s day, sexual awareness month, HIV/AIDS day, disability day in relation to GBV and the 16 days of activism. 4) Provision of risk mitigation items including Mobility Aid Kits for disabilities, Solar light, whistles and solar lamps installation: Chameg will procure disability mobility kits to respond to the need of people living with special need, disability is estimated 10% of general population in Manyu division due to violence prone and longtime conflicts. 5) Provision of the emergency temporary shelters and non-food items to newly evicted, displaced IDPs and vulnerable host poor households: The Proposed intervention will distribute the essential NFIs and emergency shelter kits to People in need (PIN) captured under humanitarian need analysis in Manyu carried out by Chameg. 7) Capacity building to duty bearers and others:Chameg will support and train key duty bearers, key stake-holders, IDP committees, leaders and other clusters on GBV basic principles, IDP rights, and obligations . 8)Strengthen the protection mainstreaming within the humanitarian actors in Manyu: chameg will conduct a series of protection mainstreaming trainings for , Local NGOs and CBOs staff from their various sectors. The primary objective of this activity is to ensure that all humanitarian interventions are provided in a safe, dignified and in accountable way. 9) Identify and train GBV community activists: In line with the recommendations of the safety audits and protection assessments, Chameg will identify and train GBV activists at IDP site level. 10) Create and train women vigilant groups: Chameg will identify and train women activist groups in Manyu.
