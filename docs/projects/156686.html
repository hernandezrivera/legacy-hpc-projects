Project Summary

Drawn from a countrywide household assessment, an estimated 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity. From the assessed households, an estimated 40 percent of Syrians spend more than 65 percent of their expenditure on food, 57.2 percent of food insecure households reported to have outstanding debt and 65 percent of food insecure people are adopting crisis or emergency negative coping strategies. Eight years into the crisis, the main drivers to food insecurity are prolonged displacements, loss and lack of livelihoods and reduced production capacity which in turn resulted in limited financial access to food, high prices and inflation contributing to reduced purchasing power and continuous depletion of livelihoods assets of the most vulnerable populations. Almost 40 percent of the surveyed households reported to be an IDP or returnee of whom 74.5 percent reported to be displaced for more than 12 months. With an 83 - 89 percent people living below poverty lines , and with cumulative GDP loss of $226 billion between 2011 – 2016 out of which $16 billion in losses and damages attributable to agriculture sector alone, the relationship between poverty and food security is becoming inseparable. A monthly food ration with staple items costs at least 80 percent of an unskilled laborer’s monthly salary and 50-80 percent of a public service employee’s monthly salary thus demonstrating the existence of “working poor” in Syria.
The humanitarian needs in Syria are expected to remain high in 2019 and multiple actions are needed to ensure that all four pillars of food security related to access, availability, utilization and stability of food are adequately addressed through lifesaving, life sustaining, appropriate resilience, and early recovery interventions.
TdH-IT plans of delivering 40,000 Food parcels (based on IFRC standard) to HHs in need all over Syria through our Implementing Partner SARC, that has already identified that there is a gap in needs coverage especially in North and North east of Syria.
In addition, to a pilot project with SARC as a start up to conduct Food voucher distributions in Hama governorate (1,600 FVs in two locations, recurrence 5 months).
TdH-Italy has pre-assessment for Aleppo , Homs, and Hama since we had different kind of interventions with need assessment for PWDs, and we have a very strong referral system to our projects in the same areas such as the physiotherapy centers in Aleppo to support disabilities with PSS and medication.

Needs

Emergency Response aims to reach crisis-affected people to cover a minimum of two to six weeks of their immediate food needs through short-term support such as food parcels and food voucher modality. This assistance modality can be extended up to 3 months only if regular food assistance cannot be provided due to unavoidable circumstances. This response will provide 2,100 kcal per person/per day as per Sphere standards. The estimated target for this activity is a projected caseload of 200,000 via Food Parcels and 8,000 via food voucher of newly crisis-affected people as well IDP returnees (out if almost 1,672,682 Food insecure people in selected 3 governorates – based on HNO data 2019) and is included in this response plan to ensure adequate preparedness. The project is intended to be implemented in 3 different governorates Homs, Hama and Aleppo, as in Homs, and Aleppo TdH along with IP SARC by distributing 40,000 Food Parcels for PiN, Procurement will be a local tender based on the experience of TdH with Local partners and the inability to procure through IFRC for importation sanctions. Another modality will be submitted for the distribution in Hama, as TdH has gained relevant experience in distribution of food vouchers during the past two years with a different IP, however; and after discussions with SARC, the plan will be to introduce this modality and locations if applicable during the year 2019. 1,600 Food Vouchers will be distributed to PiN based on vulnerability criteria pre-defined with SARC. After conducting market assessments, and selecting 1600 HHs, whom will receive food assistance with a pre-defined list of items for a duration of 8 months based on sector activities.If modality is accepted TdH will conduct capacity building for local partner on how to ensure transparency, and explain the modality and project objectives in detail.
