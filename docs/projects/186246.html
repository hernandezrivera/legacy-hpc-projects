Project Summary

Snail farming project seeks to mitigate the impact of the armed conflict in the North West and South West on women and girls through training and installation of small holder snail farms for livelihood. It shall build a snail multiplication facility which will serve as a seed bank and hands-on training farm for the potential snail farmers.
Direct beneficiaries shall be IDP women and other vulnerable members of host communities including some unaccompanied non-school going IDPs. Beneficiaries shall acquire hands-on skills in snail farming, including pen construction, snail husbandry, processing, storage and market penetration strategies. The project will also provide start-up support for the creation of individual and/or group farms and ‘seed’ snails to populate the farms.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The increasing number of IDPs caused by the crisis in the North West region of Cameroon is exerting enormous pressure on the host communities. This is fast weakening the community support chain of host communities in Bamenda. As a result, most IDP especially unaccompanied children, victims of sexual exploitation girls are forced to fend for themselves. Some have been abused by paedophiles and sex traffickers while other embrace survival sex work (prostitution). Our initiative seeks to reduce the vulnerability of these sexually exploited women to further abuse through a sustainable livelihood initiative of snail farming. It shall contribute in solving the challenges faced by victims of the crisis while providing them with psycho-social support. Snails are an outstanding source of proteins and a delicacy cherished by a majority of urban duellers in Cameroon and beyond. Through the provision of vitals skills for snail domestication and marketing, the project shall empower the beneficiaries both economically and nutrients wise.The main need here is that of livelihood for vulnerable women and girls
