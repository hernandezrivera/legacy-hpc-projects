Project Summary

The proposed project is anticipated to address the needs of up to 11,520 beneficiaries (up to 50% female) constituting 9,300 children (anticipating to reach 50% to be girls) and 2220 caregivers identified in Puntland from the 1.6 million girls and boys and their families identified by CP AoR by focusing on improving their access to quality CP service in gender sensitive manner for most at risk boys and girls, providing tailored case management for each individual child, supporting safe alternative of family-based care for unaccompanied and separated children as well as Family Tracing and Reunification.
PMWDO will also support to caregivers and families (with MHPSS, positive parenting and other kind of support) for children.
Additionally PMWDO coordinate with CP AoR on safe strategies for preventing and responding to grave violations of children’s rights through advocacy and referrals for services such as medical and rehabilitation for injured children.
The project also anticipate working with up to 10,920 beneficiaries consisting with vulnerable women and girls (the project anticipate to reach up to 1,000 PLWDS and minority clans reflecting up to 10% of the target beneficiaries ) to access gender, age and culturally sensitive sex-disaggregated data and information on GBV coordination, mitigation, prevention and response by providing and facilitating access to safe, confidential, timely and quality coordinated GBV/FGM services through integrated community referrals network.
The project focus will be in Pundland focusing in Bossaso where the are 62,018 people in need , Qardho with 18,024 PIN , Garowe with 42,277 PIN, Galkayo with 125,447 PIN, Galdogob 22,863 PIN and Burtinle with 2,368 people in need.
