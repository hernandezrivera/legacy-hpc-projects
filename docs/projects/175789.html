Project Summary

The outbreak of conflict in South Sudan in 2013 and the renewed violence in 2016 resulted in Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) in the Republic of South Sudan. Several other South Sudanese took refuge in the neighboring countries, the Democratic Republic of Congo (DR Congo), Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia, and Sudan. Some IDPs end up in protection of civilian sites/camps (POC) under United Nations protection while others like those in Lakes State immersed within the host community.
South Sudanese IDPs & refugees gave several reasons for displacements and why they fled their beautiful country. The armed conflict is one of the main reasons, while others including; economic hardship, vandalization of public and individual homes and assets, looting of individual properties, and human rights violations and abuses is another key reason as several reports indicate that human rights violations and abuses against people fleeing the conflict were perpetrated by government SPLA-IG soldiers and armed groups. Some of the violations and abuses include; torture, enforced disappearance, arbitrary detention, prejudicial killing, and abduction.
The IDPs and the host communities faced and are facing similar hardships, violations, or abuses mentioned above mostly on their way to safe protected places or as they search for a living.
AMA intends to intervene and respond to the above-mentioned violations, and abuses, and hardships in Lakes State Rumbek Center and specially Amongpiny which is a hard-to-reach area. The primary target group in this project includes; persons who suffered displacement or whose rights were violated.
The purpose of the project is to develop mechanisms to respond to violations and abuses, general protection concerns. it hope to provide Legal aid to the people and their properties in the mentioned project locations. The project aims to form and enhance the capacity of community focal point persons (CFPs) and Local government Authorities, ABC Court and paralegal (LGA) to be able to respond to general protection concerns.
Key concerns include growing displacements of populations in Yirol West, Rumbek North, Rumbek Center, CueIbet and more recently Awerial. This recent conflict affected displacements worsens existing displacements due to flooding and violence which had displaced over 20,000 people by October 2020. Sexual violence and other forms of GBV against women and girls are becoming more widespread in Lakes state due to continued conflict and population movement. Since mid 2020 through AMA’s existing programming, and in line with Protection Cluster findings, AMA has noted increasing and targeted attacks against women and girls with growing levels of sexual violence perpetrated. These are linked to the escalating insecurity, militarization of political leadership, mobilization of armed groups and instability caused by violent.
Community are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information. The recent and ongoing insecurity disproportionately affects community . Based on programming experience in Yirol and Rumbek, the majority of adolescent girls are out of school, experience growing levels of sexual violence, abductions, sexual harassment, early and forced marriage, sexual exploitation and abuses. the restrictions placed on their movement by their families and insecurity . In AMA’s GBV intervention in Lakes, at least 30% of beneficiaries targeted through PSS activities will be affected community. According to the Lakes November update, conflict affected communities continue to have cattle confiscated or looted by state security actors. This depletion of assets is significant for counties falling under category 4 of the IPC . The cattle camp youth in Cueibet, Rumbek East, Rumbek Center, Wulu and Rumbek North staged inter and intra-clan conflicts.

1 - Needs

Lake state has been classified by the south Sudan peace commission as the most violent state in south Sudan(south Sudan peace building network report analysis 2020). In her incident reporting, AMA had 1200 incidences half of them revenge killings. Based on AMAs outcome harvesting report (Yirol and Amongpiny 2020, rape and forced marriage accounted for over 36% of the incident reporting in both 2019 and 2020. There has been killings related to revenge in proposed project area which date as old as 2005. The relatives of someone killed by a clan always looks for a high profile person to kill for revenge. Road ambushes between Rumbek center and Amongpiny caused a temporarily closure of the road in June and July 2020. Other conflict issues are inter and intra communal conflicts which has involved the pakam community of Rumbek north who has settled in Yirol East Pagarau and Jier and mamer as IDPs until February2019 when they created insecurity raping women and killings the host and raiding cattle from the host., they left and settled somewhere in thiang Ruel between Paoch and Amongpiny. This issues created insecurity for Rumbek center, Rumbek east and the greater Yirol. The delayed implementation of the government leaving vacuum in the counties and the state allowed lawlessness and the revenge killings incidences increased in the period of the vacuum. This project aspires to resolve conflict better, protect most vulnerable ,members for the communities in Rumbek center including women and girls, IDPs, persons with disabilities, elderly and children's by building the local capacities for peace and mounting psychosocial support network. Other interventions will include trauma healing and legal aid trauma and injustices. It cover also interventions like the awareness raising around mine risk, lobby and advocacy with traditional leader, spear-master, traditional leaders on human rights and harmful gender and social norms.Responses to address the recent conflict, displacement and violence in Lakes are current priorities within the Protection Cluster in South Sudan. The interventions shall focus at the working on strengthening the capacity of CSOs I.E Traditional leaders, cattle camp youths, Faith base, Women Associations, ABC courts. Engage them in conflict mitigation, peace building to response to revenge killing, life saving skills to respond to risks of GBV/SGBV, Behaviors change in adolescents, in school and out of school. Lobby and advocacy with local government authorities, traditional leaders/spear masters, youth leaders, women leaders and church leaders, AMA protection responses that are already in place in Rumbek Center and Rumbek East. GBV or Protection services and health facilities are not equipped to offer clinical care for sexual assault survivors at present. AMA will utilize internal emergency response capacity to respond to this emerging crisis and will set up legal clinics and response team to provide GBV case management, information, protection monitoring. A key focus of this intervention will include strengthening and monitoring GBV coordination and referral systems with other local and international actors. AMA shall analyze the findings and share protection monitoring reports with clusters and other humanitarian actors to inform advocacy and recommend programming adjustments. In addition, the protection team will provide protection mainstreaming training to CSOs I.E Traditional leaders, cattle camp youths, Faith base organization, Women Associations, ABC courts and Community Focal Persons (CFPs).

2 - Project activities

AMA will carry out the following activities to address the needs identified above under this project. 1. Monitoring the movement of IDPs, documenting and reporting violations and abuses of human rights in Rumbek Center (Amongpiny) County. With the help of the county local authorities, AMA will identify community based focal point persons (CFPs), train the CFPs on basic human rights laws and form the CFPs in pairs to make the CFPs monitor, document and report human rights violations and abuses to legal aid officers to intervene.2. Providing general protection in coordination with protection clusters. AMA will identify partners' organization that are members of general protection cluster and will coordinate in providing necessary protection to people in need. 3. Establishment of two legal aid clinics in the Rumbek Center (Amongpiny) and employ two Legal aid officers that will be base in the project locations with the aim of providing legal aid (pro Bono) to victims of grave human rights violations, abuses and violations of rights and properties. 4. Advocacy for the respect and general protection of the rights of people in the mentioned project locations. AMA will advocate for the respect, general protection and promotion of human rights at the local level of government. AMA will advocate for the enactment of laws/polices that shall provide general protection to people at the local level and at the national level. 5. Carrying out awareness raising (9 Radio talk shows) on general protection, human rights and legal service that will be provided at the legal aid Clinics. AMA will use the available radio stations to educate the public on general protection, human rights and legal services that will be provided at the legal Aid Clinics. 6. Capacity building of local authorities on human right and rule of law. AMA will train at least 60 members of LGA in the project locations. The LGA will be supported to enact and implement laws/policies that will support general protection, respect and promote human rights.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

AMA is a faith base organization with strict code of conduct. All employees of AMA are obliged to the code of conduct. This project will take in to consideration that nobody should be sexually exploited or abused. AMA will apply the international protection principles at all stage of implementation of this project as required of all humanitarian organizations. The main protection principles that shall be applied all through the life span of the project includes; 1- Avoiding exposing people to further harm as a result of AMA's actions. Cases documented from victims of all forms of violence or abuses will be keep confidentially and encrypted. 2. Ensuring people’s (beneficiaries) access to impartial assistance – in proportion to need and without discrimination. AMA will reach with the services to the communities and make public aware and educate through radio talk shows that will be conducted during implementation phase of this project. 3. Protecting people from physical and psychological harm arising from Violence and coercion. AMA will refer victims that need counselling and psycho-social supports to partners with expertise. 4. Assisting people to claim their rights, access available remedies and recover from the effects of violations/abuses. This shall be done through the provision of legal aid at the legal aid clinics that shall be established in the project locations.
