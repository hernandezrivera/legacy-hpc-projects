Project Summary

This project aims to address the current gaps and emergency shelter/NFI needs identified by the Emergency Tracking Tool (ETT) of IOM-DTM Unit, and reinforce the recovery mechanism in-country through a Disaster Risk Reduction approach, by:
a) Reinforcing the capacity of national and local authorities on DRR response
b) Creation of an early warning system and strengthening the rapid assessment capacity following natural disasters, including monitoring of IDPs
c) Building a recovery response to natural disaster through the distribution of livelihood assistance
d) Implementing Quick Impact Projects (QIPs) through cash-based intervention and social cohesion activities
e) Providing emergency shelters and NFI response to populations affected by natural disasters, including cash-based intervention to support during construction process
f) Providing transitional shelters and shelter repair kits to the most vulnerable households affected by natural disasters, including cash-based intervention to support the construction process
g) Cash-based intervention for the construction of improved kitchens
h) Reinforce Emergency Communication with Community mechanism in country, specifically the Hotline -109
In addition, this project will reinforce the IOM’s Emergency Event Tracking system (EET) for rapid assessment following natural disasters and other displacement events, including the development of an emergency response team dedicated to assessing life-saving shelter needs of returnees.
The project, and, specifically, the Emergency Event Tracking (EET) will covering the 18 provinces of Burundi, namely Makamba, Rumonge, Bubanza, Cibitoke, Kayanza, Ngozi, Ruyigi, Rutana, Gitega, Bururi, Mwaro, Murumvya, Karusi, Cankuzo, Muyinga, Kayanza, Bujumbura Mairie, Bujumbura Rural.
