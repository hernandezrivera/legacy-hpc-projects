Project Summary

Frontier Organization for Reforms & Transformation (FORT) proposes rehabilitation/ construction of six (6) drinking water supply infrastructure (DWSI) Sarwakai, South Waziristan Tribal District (SWTD). Eighty (80) hygiene education sessions will be carried out with men, women and children being beneficiaries of water supply infrastructure. Additionally, FORT will conduct two Operation and Maintenance (O&M) training on minor repair of water infrastructure. This project will benefit a total of 6624 individuals including 3444 women and 4193 men, girls 1308 and boys 1174.

Needs

A rapid need assessment was carried out by FORT from 1st to 3rd Jan 2019 in Tehsil Sarwakai in consultation with DA to identify the most immediate needs of the returnees. The above two tehsils were allocated on the basis of return and access in the harsh winter as majority of the returnees migrate from the rest of the tehsils and due to heavy snowfall work is not possible. FORT conducted WASH focused need assessment upon government recommendation, consultation with communities, stakeholders and WASH Cluster/UNOCHA prioritized sector for intervention. Data collected by FORT team members was analyzed and it was observed that although the targeted tehsils has had interventions and many villages have been assisted but man of the villages still require support with access to clean drinking water. The targeted villages of Tehsil Sararogha & Sarwakai lack proper infrastructure mechanism in terms of access to clean water as militancy, lack of maintenance and at present limitations on part of Government to cater to immediate needs has led to significant challenges for community to have access to clean water. People mostly rely on water sources that are placed or located in nearby villages or within villages. Majority of the returnees travel a certain distance depending upon proximity to nearby water sources to collect water. Returnees are faced with the challenge of access to clean drinking water many of the returnees are utilizing water for drinking and other sources from unsafe drinking sources. It was observed that returnees use water via open springs/ponds or through unprotected springs and few wells as well. Girls help considerably in household responsibilities including collection of water that often lead to sickness of young and school going girls thereby, leading to low school attendance and ultimately drop out from schools. The assessment revealed that in order to collect water, returnees with majority as females travel to collect water ranging from less than a minute to more than one hour in certain cases. Returnees satisfaction ratio of the quality of water being utilized and consumed was mostly observed as not satisfactory. Community knowledge and sense of awareness was limited regarding water purification method or in certain cases people gave little importance to water treatment. It was observed from the field assessment that people often wash hands at critical moments but few families and individuals use soap with waster for numerous reasons ranging from lack of knowledge to limited resources to buy soap. Hygiene awareness is not common among returnees especially among children while the area as per number of health incidents shows that waste related diseases are common such as diarrhea. Based on assessment carried out and as per consultation with Government counterpart, immediate relief to returnees can be provided by the rehabilitation of six PHED identified gravity infrastructure as this will lead to maximum beneficiary outreach and long-term sustainability of the infrastructures. The six villages comprise of large number of families and FORT will carry out awareness campaign in selected areas for better hygiene knowledge and practices to be adopted by community.

Activities

Activity 1. FORT will provide access to clean drinking water in the areas of return of Tehsil Sarwakai South Waziristan Tribal District to most Vulnerable families with the following activities:1. Endorsement of villages from FDMA and PA2. Field detailed assessment of villages3. Village profiling and documentation (Pre-KAP Survey)4. Cross analysis of FORT and government assessment data (PHED)5. Assessment of government institutions in the area (school and health centers)6. Formation of 24 Community Organizations (CO) to look over WASH activities in their respective areas with 8-12 persons with age group 24-60 or more7. 50% female participation and involvement to be ensured during CO formation 8. Selection of 6 sites for Water Gravity Schemes for rehabilitation and its endorsement (resolution) by WASH committees with maximum beneficiary outreach including most vulnerable 9. Preparation of BOQs and their approval from WASH Cluster and UNOCHA10. Procurement and Tendering11. Rehabilitation of 3 gravity schemes catering to the needs of registered and unregistered returnees.12. Construction and installation of water bore and motor in GGPS Pirmal Kot and GGPS Kamal Kot Sarwakia respectively. Rehabilitation of water storage tank in both schools.13. Supervision and maintenance to be carried out by WASH committees14. Water quality testing to be carried out for the 6 water gravity schemes15. Operation and Maintenance Training(O & M). Training will be conducted on the operation and maintenance of water supply schemes. Water Management Committee (WMC)/WASH Committees members will be briefed and trained on the maintenance of the schemes.16. 100 Hygiene kits to be distributed with 50% females headed registered and unregistered households. Each kit to consist of Towel Cotton, Soap Bar, Washing Soap, Nail Cutter Medium Size, Comb Nylon,Lice Comb Medium, Tooth Paste, Tooth Brush with cap for adults, Tooth Brush with cap for children’s, Cotton Flannel clothe, Mug with handle, Lota, medium size, and a Packing Bag.17. Post KAP SurveyActivity 2. In order to ensure maximum community participation and to improve local economy construction of 2 latrines in heath centers and 4 in schools will carried out through cash for work by involving local community and ensuring its sustainability and ownership through WASH committees. Similarly, latrine kits will be distributed through WASH committees for the latrines construction. The locations for latrine construction will be based upon the endorsement from FDMA and PA and for community level it will depend upon the recommendations and resolution approved by WASH committees. Pour Flush latrines will be constructed in the areas of return in accordance with the SPHERE standards. BOQs and the design are based on the UNICEF standard model. The latrines will be constructed by the local community through the active involvement and supervision of FORT staff along with WASH committees. The community will be engaged in the latrine construction through cash-for-work initiatives. Cash will be disbursed after the successful implementation and step wise completion of the latrines. Latrine kits will be distributed through WASH committees, a total of 06 sanitation kits will be distributed that will help in the construction of latrines. FORT engineer and social mobilizers will supervise the work and WASH committee will also be involved during the whole process.
