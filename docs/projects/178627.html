Project Summary

With the staff of the Syrian Society for Social Development present in the areas of returning population in the governorates of Rural Damascus, Hama & Idleb, & following the needs assessments conducted in November 2020, the Syrian society for Social Development aims to rehabilitate 100 Damaged houses in Rural Damascus, and Doors & Windows for 400 apartments in Hama & Idleb for beneficiaries of returnees & host community.

4 - Needs

Following the figures of beneficiaries listed in the "SNFI_PIN_Severity_HNO_2021" documents (1398 in Az-Zabdani-Rural Damascus, 840 in Muhardah, 2408 in Suran, Rural Hama), & aiming to provide the necessary services for returnees & host community in the selected areas, as well as the request from the authorities & responsible municipalities, a needs assessment was conducted in November 2020, by the staff of the Syrian Society for Social Development implementing other projects in the selected areas, over 170 Damaged Houses in Az-Zabadani with various levels of damage have been assessed & are in need for rehabilitation works. as well as 500 apartments in various areas in Hama, Idleb & surrounding. The project focuses on 100 houses of medium & light damages in Rural Damascus, & 400 apartments in need of light intervention (Doors&Windows) in Hama & Idleb

5 - Implementation of activities

Following the findings of the needs assessment, & taking in consideration the similar work taking place by SSSD in the areas, focusing on houses with medium & light damage in Rural Damascus, & apartments with lighter damages in Hama & Idleb, the Syrian Society for Social Development shall start with applying for official approvals for the rehabilitation of 100 Damaged Houses in Rural Damascus, Installation of Doors & Windows for 400 apartments in Rural Hama & Idleb. Procurement of material shall be done through a tender to be announced in the local market. & once the ﬁnancial fund is secured, implementation of the activities (Support to sustainably repair/rehabilitate housing and related community/public infrastructure and facilities to owners/tenants/returnees/host families/Rehabilitation of damaged houses shall be realized using local contractors, with supervision by the staff of the Syrian Society for Social Development from the main office in Damascus & the branch office in Hama. Monitoring and Evaluation shall be conducted by SSSD's engineering team following the technical speciﬁcations presented in the tender and the sector's guidelines. The handover of the project shall be done to the Beneficiaries & municipalities in the selected areas.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of Physical safety of beneficiaries due to structural integrity of the building and/or presence of ERWs in close proximity to potential work sites. Mitigation: 1. Structural assessment by qualified personnel. 2. Repair/rehabilitate only structurally safe building. 3. Work closely with local authorities involved in ERW clearance to ensure that area is free from ERW. 4. Orient contractors/workers on risks of ERW and ask them to immediately report suspicious object & suspend work until object is removed safely.Risk: Increased community tension due to different assistance packages, unclear beneficiary selection criteria leading to feeling of discrimination. /Selection criteria influence by local authorities may contribute to nepotism, tension in the community and risk of most vulnerable being left out.Mitigation: 1. Standardize assistance package with guidelines followed by all sector partners. 2. Ensure beneficiary selection criteria is uniform/agreed among sector members and with the government and is clearly communicated widely within a community and amongst different groups. 3. Engage with community leaders, to raise awareness in the community. 4. Establish complaint mechanism at community level. 5. Training to humanitarian staffs.Risk: Lack of documents to prove property/occupancy rights incl civil documentation.Mitigation: 1. Work together with MoLAE to ensure that alternative documents to ownership are respected by all governorates. 2. Share the list of alternative documents to prove ownership with all partners. 3. Work together with local authorities, partners and communities to establish a mechanism to deal with such issues at local level, & referral for legal assistance.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Damaged Houses may cause potential exposure to GBV risks to women & girls as well as affecting children, elderly & people with disabilities. Mitigation measures: 1.Ensuring agreed/SPHERE standards & "Do no harm" principle with training & requiring all staff, contractors to sign the Code of Conduct referring to the protection of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), and Gender Based Violence (GBV) 2.Consulting the various categories of affected population in the assessment, design & implementation phases 3.Establishing complaint & feedback mechanism +close monitoring during implementation

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk/Assumption: Potential difficulties in having secure access to the selected areas of the projectMitigating Measure: Implementing the works mainly by recruiting local hand labor from inhabitants of the areas of the projectRisk/Assumption: Delay in getting the necessary official approvals of beneficiariesMitigating Measure: Applying in advance for approvals, & having alternative lists of beneficiariesRisk/Assumption: Changes in the foreign currency exchange rate, potentially leading to changes in the project's cost, as well as potential delays in procurement/ scarcity or lack of materials in the local marketMitigating Measure: Supply of needed materials in advance of the beginning of implementation.Risk/Assumption: COVID-19 New waves and lockdown/curfew, & potential delays in implementationMitigating Measure: Coordination with concerned stakeholders to anticipate such measures, and Ensuring the staff's protection during the site's visits & implementation of the works by applying preventive measures & use of masks by all Staff, partners, contractors & employees to reduce spreading of the infection
