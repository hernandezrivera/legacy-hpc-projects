Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to improve lifesaving by immediate access to food and voucher relief for the most vulnerable populations in crisis and emergency situations through the provision of unconditional food transfer during the critical months. Also, the project is aimed to strengthen community resilience through the restoration of livelihood assets of pastoralists and agro-pastoralists to resist future shocks and prevent further deterioration. the project is intended to target 28,170 pastoralists and agro pastoralists living in Sanaag, Sool and Awdal regions in Somaliland.

Project Activities

1. Provision of food relief for 500 vulnerable households through voucher value of USD 105 for 5 months. Food items will consist of 50 kgs of rice, 25 kgs of flour, 5 kgs of cooking oil, 12.5 kgs of sugar, 2 kgs of dates, 1 tin of milk powder. 500 households (3500 beneficiaries) of vulnerable people in Sanaag, Awdal, and Sool regions. 2. Provision of voucher relief worth 160 USD per month for 2 months to 500 women headed HH’s in crisis targeting most vulnerable households. 3. Generating short-term job opportunities for 300 (190 women-headed households and 110 men headed households). Before the rainy season, 35,000 linear meters of soil bunds will be constructed in cultivable agriculture areas aiming at harvest rainwater in rain-fed agriculture areas.4. Distribution of emergency agricultural inputs packages containing land preparation support through the provision of tractor hours, seeds (sorghum, maize and cowpeas seeds) to 350 HH’s in Sanaag, Awdal and Sool regions. 5. Conduct 6 training workshops for better agronomy practices for 50 lead farmers in selected target areas. 55% of trainees will become female farmers. Trainees will be selected from target agro-pastoral areas in Sanaag and Awdal regions. 6. Train 6 community health workers and provide veterinary drug kits and treat 2,500 sick animals owned by 330 HH,s in pastoral areas of Sanaag, Sool and Awdal regions
