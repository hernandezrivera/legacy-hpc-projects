Project Summary

This projects seeks to strengthen COVID-19 response and access to essential health services following the disruptions due to COVID-19 induced restrictions. To date (22.11.20) Zimbabwe has recorded 9220 cases of COVID-19 and currently recording a rising moving 7 day average of 56 new cases per day, which went down to 12 cases at some point. It remains critical to promote prevention measures and strengthen case management, IPC, considering the new cases are mainly local cases; increasing risk due to relaxed measures low risk profile among the genral public, and the upward trend of cases in regions like America, Europe as they experience second wave. The anticipated availability of a vaccine in 2021 is giving hope towards ending this acute phase of the outbreak and need to continue with the other prevention measures. On the other hand, COVID-19 response and restrictions negatively impacted access to essential health services that include. According to UN OCHA, for maternal health services, at least 4th Antenatal visit declined by 45 per cent in 2020 (April -July) compared to same period in 2019. On immunization services, the proportion of districts reporting at least 80 per cent routine immunization coverage with Penta3 declined from 90 per cent in December 2019 to 59 per cent by end June 2020. The project will give special consideration to vulnerable groups that include: elderly, patients with chronic conditions, pregnant and lactating women, children, people living with disabilities, people living with HIV, affected populations.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Affected populations were engaged in coming up with priority interventions and they will continue to be engaged during implementation and monitoring. Community dialogues and other feedback mechanisms (like suggestion boxes) will be implemented to give affected populations an opportunity to air their views, provide feedback and meaningfully contribute to the project management. In addition, the affected people will also be consulted on the best feedback mechanisms they are comfortable with. Special arrangements will be put in place to facilitate full participation for people living with disabilities depending on the specific type of disability.
