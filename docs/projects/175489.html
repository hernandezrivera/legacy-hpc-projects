Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNICEF will work with government and civil society partners to strengthen a social welfare safety net for the most vulnerable children and families in the IDP resettlement areas. This involves identifying and training caseworkers who will work with IDPs communities to identify vulnerable children, assess needs, and provide or refer to appropriate social, protection and judicial services, including services to respond to violence, neglect and abuse, birth registration and identity documents issuance. To complement these activities, IDP communities will be supported to create safer communities for children by training key community actors on child rights, protection and risk mitigation, the identification of vulnerable and at risk children, and referral mechanisms to access child protection services. Inclusive child friendly spaces will be established in resettlement sites to provide children with an opportunity to interact under guidance with peers and experience some degree of normalcy. Partners will have the opportunity to observe children and identify those in need of more support, particularly psychological assistance. Finally, a child protection system is incomplete without a response from justice actors. The Legal Aid Institute will be supported to provid free legal services to survivors of violence, abuse, and neglect, including GBV and SEA.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Hire and train posto-based Child Protection Case Workers to identify and refer child protection cases. • Support the organization of mobile birth registration activities to the displaced persons due to armed conflict. • Provide training and ongoing support to government partners in affected communities and districts to respond to violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect, including GBV and SEA.• Support the Legal Aid Institute (IPAJ) to conduct legal outreach in affected districts reaching at-risk persons and providing free legal services to survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect, including GBV and SEA. Capacity building on SEA to Linha Verde, LFC & Police (to strenghten safe reporting & referrals. Community mobilization on PSEA)• Provide age-appropriate PSS to children through CFS, and train CFS facilitators and teachers in PSS methodology and approaches.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNICEF takes careful consideration to ensure non-discrimination during the implementation of activities. All child protection safeguarding measures will be fully ensured (confidentiality, equitable access, and non-discrimination). The protection and well-being of children is a priority for UNICEF and all implementing partners contracted to carry out specialized activities will sign UNICEF PSEA contracts which detail child protection safeguards as well. Complaint mechanism will be established for cases of PSEA and disseminated in all communities targeted) In addition, UNICEF will work together with community leaders to ensure that those receiving case management support are considered the most vulnerable by the community, hence avoid perceptions of inequality and discrimination. UNICEF also aims to provide inclusive services in order not to exclude this population group.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project is designed in consultation with the affected population through focus group discussion and individual interviews conducted through a telephone and in some cases by community works. The communities will be an integral part of all the implementation processes. The affected population will be included in the process of identification of beneficiaries, throughout the deliverable of humanitarian assistance, and in monitoring. At least two complaint and feedback mechanisms will be put in place to allow the beneficiaries to share their concern. These are 1) community feedback box and 2) two hotlines to which communities can report or share their concerns (Linha Verde) and Child Help Line.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNICEF, in partnership with AIFO, is starting training and mentorship of mainstream child protection partners on disability-inclusive programming, including identification of children, and provision of inclusive PSS and case management services, aiming to ensure that 5-10% of children reached are children with disabilities. Partners will identify children in the communities by themselves as well as receive referrals through AIFO's community volunteers and as well as FAMOD and local DPOs. In addition, CP partners will be provided with a dedicated budget to cover the additional costs of supporting children with disabilities (e.g. assistive device, clinical assessment or transportation to PSS service).

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Child protection assessments incorporated environmental considerations, and various child protection assessments are planned for all new resettlement sites to be constituted in 2021.
