Project Summary

Almost four million people are currently living in (NSAG) areas of Idleb, western Aleppo, northern Hama and Eastern Latakia, where the security situation remains volatile and unstable. The ongoing conflict has had a negative impact on civilian populations, resulted in mass displacements and the destruction of civilian infrastructure, including medical facilities and settlements hosting displaced populations.
With increasing violence and continued displacement to the north of Syria and many factors, amputations are increasing against the acute shortage of artificial limbs, existing rehabilitation services do not come close to coping with this growing demand. There is a great, unmet need for orthopedic devices (prostheses and orthoses) in low-income people. Only about 5% of those who need prosthetics and orthotics (P&O) services have access to them.
It is reported that about 30% of trauma injuries lead to permanent disability and that the rest of trauma survivors suffer from temporary physical disabilities. This is a matter of concern because the intensive fighting in eastern Syria during the second half of 2019 has increased the number of trauma cases.
For this reason, Balsam has established a prosthetic and orthotic center in Termanin, Idleb since 2017. This center provides amputees with a variety of services, the most important of which are: manufacturing of lower limbs, manufacturing plastic splints, medical shoes for the disabled, limb maintenance, special training exercises after installation, physiotherapy (physical), psychosocial support.

Beneficiaries:
The beneficiaries of this project are persons with movement disabilities resulting from war injuries or other causes, who need orthopedic services and prosthetics. Beneficiaries will be selected through a clear community mobilization mechanism. Families are identified through the mechanism of registering the weak points, where priority is given to emergency cases, in addition to those with disabilities other orthopedic and rheumatoid patients (NCD) will be served through medical consultation by the project’s orthopedic doctor.
All the direct beneficiaries are vulnerable as they are part of the PEOPLE WITH CHRONIC ILLNESS AND INJURIES.

Coordination with partners:
The project include psychosocial support for amputees, it is provided by a psychologist, for more elaborated cases or cases that may need a medical procedures, we will refer the patient to a 15 km away clinic managed by SAMS (Tranquil Soul mental health center).
The project provide physiotherapy sessions for amputees, but for more elaborated cases like spine injuries, we will refer the patient to a specialized center of physiotherapy that use more advanced supplies and specialized physiotherapists in the same location (Termanin) Termanin Rehabilitation Center and Splints managed by SAMS
Monitoring and Evaluation
The M&E team will be monitoring the implementation activities through registries, like number of consultations, prosthesis manufactured, repaired …, physiotherapy sessions, PSS sessions ….
After completion, the team will be sent to visit selected samples of beneficiaries and conduct special questionnaires based on indicators, to be sure of the number and type and quality of services recorded.

4 - Needs

Recent data suggests that the current prevalence rate of persons with disabilities aged 12 and above in Syria is 27 percent, which is nearly double the global estimate (United Nations Humanitarian Needs Assessment Programme HNAP 2019. Disability: Prevalence and Impact .According to the need assessment done by our organization BALSAM, out of 437 people in need, need prosthesis implimentation, need prosthesis reperation, need splint implimentation, medical showes modification, 60% of them are IDP's and 40% are local, 17% female and 83% male, 14% children and 86% adults.According to a field study conducted by Balsam on a group of the injured in Idleb region, about 63% of the population surveyed are unemployed and about 66% of them do not have a steady source of income and depend on providing their living needs on other members of the family (especially women) and the support of civil society organizations, 95% of them said that the medical and vocational rehabilitation programs were insufficient, and the first need for the injured was to provide job opportunities according to the opinion of 67% of the target group.

5 - Implementation of activities

Our activities are mainly related to the cluster activities 1.1,"Provide essential primary and secondary health care services, including trauma care, EmONC and referral".Patients submitted their basic information on an online form shared and published on our social media sites, are contacted for a preliminary appointment with the orthopedic specialist for medical examination and to determine the possibility of installing the limb. Specific dates will be given to those whom the Center will install the prosthesis to make measurements, start the rehabilitation process, and give the installation date.Sessions of training and physiotherapy for people who have prosthetics installed or maintained by the center or those who come to the center for physical therapy only.Sessions of PSS conducted by a specialized psychologist for amputees are conducted.Also our activities are related to the 3.2 Increase access to health services by establishing functional health facilities and mobile medical units and supporting referral, We will conduct at first a repair and maintenance activities for the center to receive more patients.as mentioned in the summary, in case the patient needs other physiotherapy or PSS than provided by our center, a referral to other specialized centers is planned.The orthopedic doctor will examine also patients (excluding mental health, trauma consultations, and physical rehabilitation), but having orthopedic or rheumatoid illness, those can come immediately to the center without the need to register online.After the completion of the prosthesis installation and physiotherapy sessions for the beneficiaries, specialists and orthopedic at the center will prepare a final report for each beneficiary explaining the physical health situation. the administrative staff will transfer the beneficiaries files to the second stage, vocational training, related to Lively hood cluster.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

R1: Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers.The center is based in a relatively safe place (Termanin close to the Euphrates shield operation)M1:- Applying the designated de-confliction mechanism for health facilities. - Continued reporting on attacks against healthcare through (SSA), (MRM), as well as Health Cluster Flash Updates. - Providing preparedness trainings for the facilities and staff-care.R2: Gender Based Violence (GBV)M2:-Training health provider on identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals.- Presence of female staff in the center (Nurse and Physiotherapist Technician), women-only hours for sessions.- Ensuring confidentiality of all patients.R3: Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA).M3:-A PSEA policy describes standard of behaviour for organization’s staff and representatives, specifically forbidding sexual exploitation and abuse, and inclusive to accountability measures signed by every employee and fully explained through meetings.- This is the 3rd year of the center and the majority of its staff are well known and continuously evaluated.- A complain box has been set which is checked up regularly, a direct phone line (with whatsApp activated) to the administration based in Turkey and E-mail (info@balsam.org.tr) is clearly provided. R4: Safe Closure of Facilities and Protecting DataM4: Electronic data and information are properly protected and backed up in a third location, according to technology best practices. R5: Community Engagement and Participation.M5:- the community voice and patient feedback are included in the Monitoring mechanisms.- Regular follow-up and reporting on the results of accountability mechanisms in place. R6: Provision of Healthcare for Vulnerable Groups and Equitable Access to Health Services.M6: data will be disaggregated by sex, age and IDP, beneficiaries are equally distributed between male 372, female 353, girls 134 and boys 120.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

R1: The military and political conflict may extend to the region.M1: In case of a military escalation we have a plan to relocate the centre to Afrin (40 km north) or Ar Ra'i (100 km north-east where the local council has proposed our centre to be located to if possible because it is a relatively central in Euphrates shield zone and nearer to Turkey cross border thus may be safer for long time), these places were chosen upon the criteria that beneficiaries among IDP's and locals living in Idleb will also be displaced to either Afrin or most probably Euphrates shield zone due to its safety.
