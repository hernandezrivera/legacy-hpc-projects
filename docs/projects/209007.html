Project Summary

PUI seeks to improve the emergency response to populations most affected by the conflict, IDPs and host communities, in Eastern Sudan, providing a multi-sectoral and integrated intervention in Health, Nutrition and PSS in Al Jazirah and Gedaref States. The specific objectives are to i) improve immediate access to free, quality emergency health care to IDPs and host communities, providing support to 12 Health Facilities and 3 Mobile Health teams, 9 HFs and 2 MHTs across Al Jazirah state (in Al Hasahisa locality, Janub Al Jazirah and East (sharg) Al Jazirah) and 3 HFs and 1 MHT across Gedaref state (in Madinat al Gedaref and Wast Algadarif). ii) To contribute to a reduction in mortality and morbidity by increasing access, coverage, use and quality of Integrated Management of Acute Malnutrition; iii) increase individuals and community levels of well-being based on psychosocial support.
Health:
-support 12 health facilities, with provision of essential pharmaceutical commodities following standard quality process for purchase, storage, distribution and management, and nonmedical equipment and supplies to HFs, + necessary rehabilitation. PUI will continue to coordinate with UNFPA and WHO for SRH and IEH kits.
-provide free primary healthcare service: medical consultations, through mobile and fixed HFs, including general adult and Child Medical Consultations, IMNCI and immunization as per the EPI; Sexual and Reproductive Health Services (SRH), with deliveries assisted by skilled birth attendants, ANC and PNC, family planning. SRH services being one major entry point for GBV survivors identification, PUI will ensure, upon consent, safe referral to facilities identified by the Protection Cluster and GBV Sub-Working Group
-provide health staff training and support of Community Health Workers (CHWs).
In nutrition:
-screening, treatment and referral of children with malnutrition, will be carried out in targeted HFs and MHTs. PUI will ensure the availability of essential commodities and will support technically the front line workers to ensure compliance with national protocols. Based on nutrition cluster recommendations and considering the high risk of supply shortages (RUTF in particular), simplified protocol for acute malnutrition management will be applied. Training of healthcare staff in the prevention and management of acute malnutrition
-a community volunteers network will ensure screening, referral and search for defaulters as well as raising awareness at both community level and HFs on nutrition issues.
-following the Nutrition cluster recommendation, family MUAC will be introduced in targeted locations where access and resources are available.
PSS:
-Creation of a service mapping and strengthening the referral systems
-Capacity building for the health team on basic PSS, including Psychological First Aid
-Provision of psychosocial support group sessions to individuals in distress
Given the recent events which affected Al Jazirah state and its capital Madani, part of these activities could be relocated to other areas, such as River Nile, Kassala, Sennar, White Nile or Khartoum, and the response re-adapted to the context evolutions.
The project targets to provide health assistance to 68,800 beneficiaries, nutrition services to 13,763 beneficiaries and PSS assistance to 2,880 beneficiaries.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The protection of populations affected by crisis and conflicts is a core principle of PUI interventions and programs. PUI follows safe and dignified programming, to ensure protection is taken into account in every step of its activities. - Prioritize Safety and Dignity and Avoid Causing Harm:The objective of PUI is to ensure the safety and dignity of the most vulnerable people, complying with the "do no harm" principle and making sure individuals who benefit from PUI interventions are not exposed to, and do not face further harm. PUI will consult the different social groups (women, men, children, the elderly, people with disabilities, etc) during the implementation of its activities to avoid unintended negative effects. PUI will try to ensure as much as possible a gender balance in its teams to enable them to offer the most appropriate and less potentially harmful approach to individuals. Complaint mechanisms will be in place to report cases of exploitation and/or abuse that may occur, following PUI policies on safeguarding. Lastly, PUI will follow a trauma informed approach and a survivor centered approach which will be mainstreamed across sectors to ensure the do no harm principle and avoid potential re-traumatization. - Meaningful Access: Many barriers, among which religious, cultural and financial ones, the volatile security context, prevent individuals to be informed of and have access to MHPSS services. Through its activities under the Protection Sector, PUI will ensure that individuals have information on how to access the available MHPSS services, giving particular attention to the most vulnerable individuals. Following this approach, PUI will train the members of the mobile health team on PFA and will organize sessions to reduce the risk of stigmatization of mental health. - Accountability:PUI will be accountable towards the population affected by the crisis, considering their needs, concerns and preferences. PUI will do this by providing individuals with accessible information and putting in place effective complaints and feedback mechanisms. - Participation and empowerment: PUI relies on its network and on the community to design its projects. Under the Protection Sector, PUI will offer group sessions on awareness raising on stress and stress management, equipping individuals with tools and coping strategies they can use and spread within the community, following a community based psychosocial support approach and aiming at reinforcing community links and community resilience.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

PUI will ensure that the field staff share with the community PUI’s commitment toaccountability to the affected population on issues such as staff recruitment andtraining, code of conduct, information sharing and transparency. All PUI staff receivethe organization Code of conduct and all PUI ethical policies, including PSEA. PUI hasa zero tolerance towards all forms of abuse, exploitation, mistreatment and moral orsexual harrassement. All persons entering in contact with Première UrgenceInternationale collaborators, members, partners, suppliers, associates, andbeneficiaries in relation with its activities must be protected to the greatest extentpossible against deliberate or involuntary actions and failures which would exposethem to risks of abuse, harassment or sexual exploitation and any other prejudice. PUIwill ensure cases can be reported. All representatives and collaborators of PremièreUrgence Internationale undertake in all circumstances to respect the provisions,norms, and commitments to report any concerns, suspicions, or allegations of PSEAand any violations of this Policy in accordance with the wisthleblower mechanism andrecommended timelines, as detailed in PUI Ethical Framework. The signature of thecode of conduct includes a commitment to respect all policies and to maintain highethical standards. PUI will set up a CFRM adapted to the local context to collect theviews and complaints of a wide range of stakeholders (affected populations,communities, local civil society actors, international humanitarian actors, localauthorities or national governments and donors) and take appropriate action. It will bedesigned in line with the protection principle of Do No Harm, in consultation withpartners and affected communities, taking also into account available internalresources (financial and human) and with the objective of gradual scaling up. PUI thusaims at guaranteeing the availability of appropriate Feedback/Complaints channels,while ensuring effective complaints handling, in order to avoid creating frustration ormistrust with regard to the proposed mechanisms. Depending on the context, thismechanism consist of i) Community Mobilizers, who would be important messengersconveying feedbacks and complaints from the population, themselves being part ofthe community ii) Complaint boxes, especially in health facilities ii) Regularconsultations with community focal points / community committees iv) Focus groupdiscussions led by PUI teams. Whatever the mechanism(s) chosen, PUI will inform thepopulation of the existence and functioning of the complaint and feedbackmechanism. This information will be disseminated through several channels(i.e.through community relays, during surveys etc.) in order to ensure that the targetedcommunity can make use of it as soon as the first activities are launched and Informthe population in the intervention area of the anti-fraud, anti-corruption and PSEAprinciples.
