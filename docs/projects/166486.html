Project Summary

This project is focused on providing an integrated emergency WASH intervention in Rann town and the 3 IDP Camps there. The intervention is in response to the widespread displacement of the conflicted affected population from the hinterlands which enjoys relative peace leading to the overcrowding of camps and over utilization of basic amenities, WASH facilities and services inclusive. The project will address deterioration in positive hygiene practices as a result of limited supplies and sustained sensitization, insufficient safe access to water due to over-utilization or lack of proper O&M support, insufficient access to safe bathrooms and latrines fitted with hand-washing facility and lack of proper solid waste management in the 3 IDP Camps and the larger town of Rann
In order to reestablish improved and safe access to general WASH services to the conflict affected population in the camps and the Town the following Key WASH intervention has been designed for this project with the objective of lifesaving lives and ensuring dignity and protection of the IDPs.
1. promoting good hygiene practices:
Lifesaving hygiene practices capable of maintaining and improving the health status of the New Arrivals will be introduced/reintroduced to the camps. Community leaders, heads of households, women groups, girls of reproductive age and children will be sensitized on hygiene practices which include hand washing practices and menstrual hygiene management among other things.
- Conducting Hygiene Sessions by the trained Hygiene promoters twice every month throughout the project duration- 12 rounds of Hygiene promotion sessions by the trained Hygiene promoters in favor of the IDP Communities and the larger town
2. Provision of adequate water for domestic use
In line with sphere standards, the project will provide water for domestic use to IDPs in the overcrowded camps. It will explore the following approach:
- Rehabilitation of boreholes, - Repair of hand and motorized pumps, - Provisioning of new water points with manual pumps
3. Providing sanitation facilities; (30 latrines and 20 bathrooms )
In line with the Sphere standard, gender dis-aggregated bathrooms and latrines (fitted with hand washing features) supported with structured supplies for the duration of 6 months will be provided. Provisions for PLWDs and people with limited movement will be provided in the construction of the facilities. It include the rehabilitation of bathrooms and latrines, latrines with hand-washing features will be constructed (a blocks will have 5 compartments and segregated based on gender), Bathrooms will be constructed, Bathing and latrines supplies for 6 months will be provided (soaps, disinfectants and Engagement of O&M Officers in selected camps)
4. Provision of context-specific hygiene dignity kits to women and Adolescent girls
With the aid of a survey of the target population, a context-specific hygiene dignity kits will be procured and distributed the women and adolescent girls. The kids will include sanitary pads and other identified needs of the women and girls. After the distribution and before the end of the project end line assessment will be conducted again to evaluation the impact of the intervention. The distribution will be preceded by an elaboration menstruation management education covering reproductive health, disposal of used sanitary pads and practices that promote protection of women and girls
Sustainability of the project is hinged on two principles – 1) building the capacity of the community (selected WASHCOM members and selected Gwoza LGA Works, Sanitation and Environment staff) by training and involving them in the implementation of the project. 2) By providing post implementation oversight and maintenance of WASH facilities by monitoring and following-up with concerned departments in Kala LGA. COVID-19 response interventions in form of sensitization, hand sanitizers and face masks would be provided to the beneficiaries

2 - Activities

According to the 2019 Humanitarian Needs Overview, 3.6 million people need support to meet their basic water, sanitation and hygiene needs across the BAY states. The main drivers of vulnerabilities related to WASH are closely linked to the conflict, including recurrent, unpredictable and continuous population displacements. Historically low levels of access to WASH services as a result of chronic underdevelopment have also contributed to the current situation. Ongoing and protracted displacement puts an additional stress on existing and already limited WASH services and facilities, and with congested populations in displacement sites, waterborne diseases spread rapidly. Cholera outbreaks have struck each year: in 2018, the cholera outbreak has affected 18 LGAs with a total of 4,250 cases104 and 1.5 million people105, including IDPs and host communities, are considered at risk of cholera in the affected LGAs. The protection and well-being of 2.7 million children and adolescents (1.5 million girls and 1.2 million boys) in the north-east remains seriously at risk mainly due to the complex and protracted conflict, with higher vulnerabilities anticipated to exist in inaccessible areas. The resilience of communities for protection of children has been weakened resulting in negative coping mechanisms. Family separation (affecting 52 per cent girls), abduction and recruitment by armed groups (affecting 57 per cent girls), among other grave child rights violations and sexual violence have directly affected at least 49,500 girls and boys (51 per cent girls). . In preparation for the 2019 2nd reserve allocation and in line with KABHUDA policy on Needs Assessments, KABHUDA conducted a WASH needs assessment in Rann with the objective of identifying and responding to humanitarian needs critical to the protection and dignity of conflict affected populations in there. Specifically KABHUDA carried out a WASH needs assessment exercise in Rann town between 7th and 11th October 2019. See attached needs assessment report attached under the Documents tab of this portal. In line with the 2019 HNO and the 90 days response plan, complemented by the KABHUDA Needs Assessment, KABHUDA has identified the following critical needs covering WASH and Child Protection namely: 1. Need to maintain existing water supply facilities to function optimally through the year (over 74% of engaged households travel or wait for over 30 minutes - Reach February,2019 Report). 2. Ensure that the water supply is safe from sources to usage while passing through the supply chain. 3. Ensure that existing house hold/community latrines are maintained to provide dignity to users by involving beneficiaries to participate in manner that is acceptable to the people (31% of HHs do not have safe access to latrines) 4. Promote good hygiene practices to mitigate against open defecation, promote handwashing, food hygiene and environmental hygiene. 5. Support the WASH sectors by providing study on risky hygiene practices in Rann , identify critical gaps in WASH service delivery. 6. Provision of hygiene dignity kits to women and girls. COVID-19 response by way of sensitization, hand sanitizers and face masks would be provided
