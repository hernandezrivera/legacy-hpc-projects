Project Summary

it is estimated that over 600,000 youth are out of school in the two English regions. An assessment conducted by LUKMEF in September 2019 in Fako and Mezam divisions revealed that about 36.25% of youth above the ages of 15 now show no interest in returning to formal education even if schools were to resume. Within these two egions, women and girls in and out of school are affected differently by the current crisis. women and however the most affected. Boys are largely targeted by the military and hence increases their vulnerability.
mindful of this and of the risk of these children engaging in to deviant activities such as prostitution, drugs and gang life, mindful of the huge reconstruction needs in the two regions, our current project is designed to provide these youth with a second chance in life through the establishment of vocational training and job insertion programs. The over 2000 youth to be trained in different domains such as woodwork, sawing, iron work, basic electricity, building and construction and electronics etc will eventually be systematically absorbed into a youth volunteer services to help communities rebuild as soon as the crisis ends. while the crisis continue, these trained youth will be able to provide basic services to new IDP settlements, returnees wishing to rebuild their homes as well as to host communities.
1. within 12 months, 2000 formal students and now school-drop-out will be enrolled into different vocational training facilities including a dedicated facility based in Buea to acquire fast and self employable skills.
2. These youths will be supported to produce prefabricated home constructions materials such as windows, doors, beds etc that will be donated and/or used to construct low cost houses in the over 300 burnt villages in the two regions.
This project will therefore not only give youths a second chance but will directly create jobs, support community recovery and bring solace to the families whose homes where burnt.

Besoins / Needs

over 600,000 children of school going age are out of school in the two English region. formal education has been attacked in most parts of the two regions . most children aged 15-17 interviewed during a rapid access to education assessment by LUKMEF in September 2019 reveal that 36.25% of them say they will not go back to school even if schools were to start normally. such therefore prompted us to consider targeting this group with vocational training geared towards meeting the needs of the communities such as reconstruction of the bunt communities and homes. To this effect, LUKMEF will1. train 2000 youths ( 15-17 years) who are no longer willing to go back to formal education an alternative hands-on vocational skilled tailored for use in communities for reconstruction2. use the trained youths to establish a youth volunteering programs for low cost housing and community recovery in the two regions3. The trained youths will Provide prefabricated rapid basic furniture ( doors, windows, beds etc) to returning IDPs engaged in some form of reconstruction.
