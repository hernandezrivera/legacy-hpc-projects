Project Summary

AFAQ Organization aims to contribute to the provision of access to lifesaving and life-sustaining humanitarian health services for 520979 people in need and conflict-affected populations in northern Syria in 2024. The proposed intervention will prioritize internally displaced people and other vulnerable populations especially (women, children, and people with disabilities) in the targeted areas. The intervention will be implemented through:
1. Continue supporting a comprehensive package of health services including Primary Health Care, and Mental Health in 4 of the existing primary health care facilities and one mobile clinic in northwest Syria.
• AFAQ Dier Samaan PHC!B0200221-Aleppo/Jebel Saman/Daret Azza/Dier Samaan
• AFAQ Eastern,Marj Akhdar !G0704030-Idleb/Jisr-Ash-Shugur/Jisr-AshShugur/Eastern,Marj Akhdar
• AFAQ Jdidet Eljisr PHC!G0704044-Idleb/Jisr-Ash-Shugur/Janudiyeh/Jdidet Eljisr
• AFAQ Mobile Clinic!G0700153-Idleb/Harim/Dana/Dana
• Alsahhara PHC!B0200052-Aleppo/Jebel Saman/Atareb/Alsahhara
The selected facilities were prioritized in terms of importance, caseload, location, availability of services and IDPs concentrations.
On average the expected beneficiaries for all proposed PHC facilities is 108000 for the whole year (10800 Men, 43200 women, 27000 girls, 27000 boys).
2. The provision of Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (EmONC) services and management of SRH-related diseases in addition to pediatric health care through supporting the specialized hospital for women and children which provides maternal and child health services to most vulnerable people, Salqin Hospital for child and Maternity! G0703109 Idleb/Harim/Salqin/Salqin.
On average the expected beneficiaries for the proposed hospital are 48,000 for the whole year (20,000 women, 14000 girls, 14000 boys).
3. Continue Supporting the dialysis center in ALHIDAYA Specialist Hospital! G0703077 Idleb/Harim/Dana/Qah with running costs and dialysis kits. In addition to conducting capacity building and training for the medical and nonmedical staff. 179 ESRD patients registered at this center, 23 dialysis machines, and 24/24 services.
4. Providing physiotherapy services, which include educating the beneficiaries and their caregivers about the beneficiaries’ physical disabilities and impairments, providing assistive devices and psychosocial support services.
On average the expected beneficiaries are 4,800 for the whole year (2000 Men, 2000 women 400 girls, 400 boys).
5. Enhance Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) to respond to the outbreaks through several modalities (100 CHWs, FGDs, IECs, social media, engaging the influencers and stakeholders, capacity building, etc) targeting 360000 beneficiaries (100000 men, 100000 women, 160000 children).
AFAQ will coordinate with local stakeholders, including health directorates, local councils, and NGOs, to ensure smooth operations and prevent duplication of efforts. Additionally, AFAQ will collaborate with international organizations such as WHO and UNFPA to procure necessary medical kits. These partnerships aim to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of our humanitarian health interventions in northern Syria.
By achieving the above components, AFAQ will ensure the continuation of essential and life-saving health interventions in addition to maintaining essential health services to contribute to decreasing mortality and morbidity in NWS.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

In line with AFAQ's strategy, our responsibilities include direct implementation of this project in target locations, coordinating closely with health authorities in Northwest Syria. We will leverage their extensive network to facilitate access. Local communities will actively participate in our capacity development efforts through appropriate training initiatives.AFAQ will ensure adherence to quality and accountability standards, providing necessary training to staff and fulfilling contractual obligations. Additionally, we will manage all program and financial reporting to the donor. Our coordination efforts will extend to relevant clusters and coordination bodies in Turkey.This program will progress through the following phases:Procurement of essential equipment, devices, drugs, consumables, and conducting the required training.Operate the health facilities and launch of outpatient services, comprehensive reproductive health services, chronic disease treatment, dialysis services, physiotherapy, emergency obstetric and newborn services, RCCE and community health services and primary health care services in alignment with health cluster and Technical Working Group (TWG) guidelines, particularly focusing on Essential Health Service Packages (EHSP), Non-Communicable Diseases (NCD), and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS).Regular reporting, data analysis, monitoring, and evaluation. This will involve the development of the Indicators Performance Tracking Table (IPTT), Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) work plan, and a disaggregated database. The reporting will be according to guidelines agreed by the technical group of the health cluster (4Ws, HeRAMS), and to dHIS 2 as well.
