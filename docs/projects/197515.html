3 - Needs

Syria remains a complex humanitarian and protection emergency characterized by more than 10 years of ongoing hostilities and its long-term consequences including widespread destruction of civilian infrastructure, economic collapse, explosive ordnance contamination and the largest number of internally displaced persons in the world.Recently, accelerating economic decline and COVID-19 have become additional major drivers of needs, exacerbating vulnerabilities even further.Access to basic services, including water, electricity, and healthcare is becoming increasingly limited, and the resilience of the population has reduced.The World Health Organization report on Syria 2022 indicates that of the 4.4 million people currently living in northwest Syria, 3.1 million people need health assistance.Critical challenges are associated with increased insecurity, displacement and high levels of poverty.Health needs are considered acute and are among the highest priority needs, especially among households headed by women (36 percent) and vulnerable households (44 percent). The healthcare infrastructure has been damaged and its rehabilitation requires huge resources Through our project, we seek to providing reproductive health services (natural childbirth for the first time) and hygiene services to the most vulnerable/crisis affected/displaced communities including IDPs in order to reach a cleaner and healthier community.

4 - Implementation of activities

During the project we will provide: • A reproductive health centre that provides, primary health care and fixed and mobile awareness services, including basic clinical care to raise the level of physical and reproductive health.• Essential medicines and supplies by the reproductive health center (personal protective equipment, diagnosis, case management) to improve family health.• Sexual and reproductive health services including SGBV.• Mental health/psychosocial and legal (consultation) services.• Establish referral pathways (for GBV, mental health and PSS services).• Community engagement and outreach. - With the continued implementation of the necessary interventions, these interventions (activities and operations) are monitored and evaluated by a specialized team that is the periodic reports through which it measures the indicators of directing in terms of quantity and quantity.- Reports and results are shared with the committee using the monthly report form, in addition to other models related to monitoring and evaluation approved by the committee.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

We have a code of conduct as well as a protection policy that all employees and volunteers of the association adhere to.The association’s management and work teams in charge of implementing the project’s activities are keen to exercise caution and caution in dealing with women and girls, especially young ages, in a way that guarantees their dignity and rights, using positive language while taking care not to discriminate on the basis of gender and social classes, respecting privacy and promoting a safe space for every young woman and girl. In addition to respecting the evolving capabilities of persons with disabilities and their right to preserve their identities. Al Birr Association takes all allegations of harassment, exploitation, and discrimination seriously, and does not tolerate perpetrators of harassment and exploitation whatsoever. It has a complaints office that allows responses and complaints. In our project, we will provide awareness on how to file a complaint in the event of harassment or exploitation.
