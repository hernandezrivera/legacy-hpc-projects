Project Summary

HLBY21-PRO-173395-1
The IRC’s proposed project, Community Outreach Mobilization Empowerment (COME- MT), aims to provide community-based protection response to vulnerable people with specific protection needs and to strengthen the protection environment for these people with information dissemination and awareness raising with broader communities. The IRC will add an individual protection assistant (in-kind) component to complement the immediate material needs of the most vulnerable to support their socio-economic well-being and to prevent them from adapting a harmful mechanism to cope with their specific needs. COME will target the people not based on the status but on their needs and protection risks. The specific prioritization and the focus will be given on the people in need of mental health and psycho-social support, missing documentation, people with disabilities, and elderly among the communities targeted with this action.
The IRC has successfully utilized the community volunteer system for implementing the community-based protection interventions in Libya to address the protection and health needs of the targeted communities (IDPs, returnees, non-displaced, migrants and refugees) since 2018. For the proposed action, IRC will leverage its community outreach volunteers in Tripoli and Misrata (including, medina, Bani Walid and Tawerga) to identify the people in need of protection assistance, information and access to the required services. The COVs will be trained on psychological first aid, identifying the MHPSS and protection needs, and administering the safe service referrals to the specialized services. The IRC will ensure that the COVs are selected among their respective communities and representative for different age and gender groups.
To strengthen the protection environment and for its sustainability, the IRC will provide capacity building trainings on Protection and Humanitarian Principles, Protection Mainstreaming, and International Legal Framework and Refugee Protection for the local humanitarian and civil society organizations, local authorities (Ministry of Health, Ministry of Social Affairs, and relevant Municipality staff) (75), and community committee leaders and members (80).
The IRC has supported the establishment of five migrant and refugee community committees in Tripoli and will target to add five more with this action. The IRC aims improving the function and participation of the committees in responding the needs of their respective communities by supporting them with guidance to create and implement their community based protection action plans and with small funds for the identified urgent action plans (in-kind support). (10)
The action will be complementing the IRC’s ongoing protection, health and governance programs in the target areas, which will help for the timely and safe referrals between health, mental health, and protection (IPA, GBV, and CP) support services provided by the IRC. This will also help the IRC to leverage the exiting CBP capacity and the already established relations with and the ongoing support to the local CSOs and stakeholders.
