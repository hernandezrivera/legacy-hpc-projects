Objectives

Engage and ensure Rohingya and host community to reduce food insecurity, creating self-reliance opportunities, support social cohesion through enhancement and restoration of natural resources and prepared against climate shocks.

Needs

Today, more than 930,000 people are trying to support themselves in the deplorable conditions of these overcrowded makeshift camps in the hills of Cox’s Bazar. The needs of these victims (Rohingya and Host community): difficult access to food and water, makeshift shelters unsuited to the needs of families, inadequate sanitation and sanitary structures, no access to medical or psychological care, just to name a few. Without swift action towards to concrete resolution, the situation of Rohingya refugees and host community in Teknaf and Ukhiya is likely to worsen. There are huge needs for support to both communities Rohingya and Host. Save the Children will work to support Rohingya and Host communities in Teknaf and Ukniya, through food security and Livelihood interventions. There are gaps such as nutrition food, cloths, work for youth, engagement of women and elderly people in work, Host community have lost their productive assets and agriculture land and competition in labour market due to influx. Save the Children will implement diverse comprehensive food security and livelihood program to mitigate or fill some of those gaps at Rohingya and Host community through Agriculture and Nutrition garden, Skills Development and income generating activities (IGA) for the community for their betterment. Under the above analysis of problems and need of Rohingya and host community; 1. Rohingya people need cash for food diversity and other livelihood necessities.2. Farmer's need to increase agriculture production in usable field.3. By using the fallow land of home state community women can have improved food intake. 4. There is need to support to extension of the existing business.5. Need to start alternative income generating activities to sustainably increase income.6. Host community need to create new employments opportunity for young people through vocational training.

Activities or outputs

Tree and Grass Plantation, Agriculture and Fishing support, IGAs Support and Skills Development Program and Capacity building for all the beneficiaries on awareness on CP, DRR and Hygiene promotion.
