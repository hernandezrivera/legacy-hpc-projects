Project Summary

The aim of the programme is to provide legal aid services as to enhance displacement-affected individuals access to their rights and services in relation to their legal identity and civil documentation, HLP rights, including eviction prevention and response, as well as employment laws and procedures. The project seeks to identify, mitigate and respond to protection risks and rights violations through Protection Monitoring. Jointly with the community, NRC will analyse protection risks and develop community safety action plans. There will be provision of life saving information to affected populations on access to essential lifesaving services.
The project will strengthen already existing community-based protection mechanisms and establish new ones, to ensure a sustainable and in-depth engagement with the communities

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

n the implementation of our legal aid program in Sudan, we are committed to mainstreaming key protection principles to ensure the effectiveness and ethical integrity of our services. Firstly, the "do no harm" principle is at the forefront, guiding us to carefully assess and mitigate any potential negative impacts of our intervention on displacement-affected individuals. This involves continuous monitoring and adapting our strategies to avoid exacerbating vulnerabilities. Secondly, "meaningful access" is ensured by designing our services to be easily reachable and understandable to all, particularly focusing on marginalized and hard-to-reach groups. This includes providing information in local languages and ensuring physical accessibility. Thirdly, "participation and empowerment" are central to our approach. We actively involve the affected communities in the decision-making processes, respecting their agency and empowering them to assert their rights and make informed choices about their legal identity, civil documentation, and housing, land, and property (HLP) rights. This participatory approach not only enhances the relevance and effectiveness of our services but also fosters a sense of ownership and resilience among the beneficiaries.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

Accountability to Affected Populations is a key pillar of our program. We establish clear mechanisms for feedback and complaints, ensuring that our beneficiaries can hold us accountable and that we are responsive to their needs and concerns, in line with CFM and AAP mechanisms set up by NRC Sudan. By integrating these principles, our program aims to deliver ethical, effective, and empowering legal aid services to those affected by displacement in Sudan.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Collaboration with Other Agencies: We will actively collaborate with other agencies and stakeholders involved in humanitarian and legal aid efforts. This collaboration will include sharing best practices, participating in joint training sessions, and coordinating our efforts to ensure a unified approach to accountability and feedback mechanisms. Establishing Clear Protocols for SEA Complaints: We will develop and implement clear, survivor-centered protocols for handling complaints of sexual exploitation and abuse. These protocols will be in line with international standards and guidelines, ensuring that they are sensitive, confidential, and prioritize the safety and dignity of survivors. Training and Capacity Building: Our staff and volunteers will undergo regular training on SEA prevention, response, and the ethical handling of complaints. This training will ensure that everyone in our agency understands their role in preventing SEA and is equipped to respond appropriately to complaints. Community Engagement and Awareness: We will engage with the communities we serve to raise awareness about their rights, the risks of SEA, and the availability of complaint mechanisms. This engagement will be culturally sensitive and will utilize appropriate communication channels to reach diverse groups. Accessible and Safe Complaint Mechanisms: We will establish accessible, confidential, and safe channels for individuals to report complaints, including SEA. These mechanisms will be widely publicized within the community and designed to be approachable and user-friendly. Feedback Loops and Transparency: We will create feedback loops that allow affected individuals to provide input on our services and report grievances. We will ensure transparency in how complaints are handled and provide regular updates to the community on the actions taken in response to their feedback. Inter-Agency Reporting and Coordination: We will participate in inter-agency reporting systems and coordination mechanisms for SEA complaints. This will ensure that complaints are handled consistently across agencies and that there is a collective effort to address SEA in our area of operation. Monitoring and Evaluation: Regular monitoring and evaluation of our accountability and feedback mechanisms will be conducted to assess their effectiveness and make necessary improvements. This will include seeking feedback from the community on how these mechanisms can be enhanced
