Project Summary

In a context of climate change, security degradation and economic and health crisis (particularly related to Covid-19), the food and nutrition situation in the circles of Niono (Ségou), Ansongo and Bourem (Gao) is characterized by great insecurity and major population displacements are expected.
The project will contribute to maintaining a social protection floor linked to the seasonality of vulnerability via electronic MT (R1). The action will also provide emergency assistance to IDPs (R2), a complete package in NFI, food and WASH will be provided to IDPs and other people in Ansongo, Bourem (Gao) and Niono (Ségou) and (R3) relates to nutrition.

1 - Contexte

The project takes place in the context of the protracted and complex Sahelian crisis characterized by factors both structural (climatic shocks, extreme poverty, weak governance, rapid demographic growth, natural risks, seasonal food and nutritional shortages) and conjunctural (economic and health crisis linked to the Covid-19, security degradation). In this context, humanitarian access is very difficult.According to the Cadre Harmonisé of November 2020, the number of people in phases 3-5 in these areas will almost double in lean 2021 (compared to 2020) to reach 102,513 people. From a nutritional point of view, all these circles are characterized as in phase 3 (phase 3 ++ for Ansongo) according to the latest projections from the Nutrition cluster.ACF has been operating for many years in Gao, in particular with the support of USAID and GIZ, and more recently in Segou as part of its RRM activities. It has strong and long-standing expertise in the project sectors and humanitarian coordination in this context. Consortium leader, ACF is also colead of the nutrition cluster.ACF will target the most vulnerable populations in the project area. Nutrition activities will target children suffering from severe acute malnutrition, FEFA as well as staff from health districts and more generally women of childbearing age and children under 5 years old. Community WASH activities targeting men, women, boys and girls will focus on preventing malnutrition. Community WASH committees with the participation of men and women and school hygiene clubs with the participation of boys and girls will be set up and run. The participation of women and girls will be strongly encouraged. Special attention will also be paid to the elderly and those with disabilities. Awareness-raising activities will focus on protection (awareness of women's rights and empowerment as well as gender-based violence, IGAs, project accountability, ...), a complaints and feedback management mechanism will also be put in place.
