Project Summary

PBA will target schools at El Roserise and Giesan Localities namely: Jiwaria for girls with total of (690) students at El Roserise Admin Unit, Elimam Elmahdi Mixed School with total of (235 students) at Bados Admin Unit, El Roserise Locality and Anas Ibn Malik Mixed School with total of (163) and Tajamoa Bakori Mixed School with total of (590 IDPs students at Giesan Locality.
The initiative will target also the teachers, PTAs members and the community at large with specific activities mainly:
- Hard component: classrooms and school’ fences construction and rehabilitation, CFS construction (one at each Locality), Latrines and hand- washing points construction and provision of clean drinking water supply, pave the schools entrances, provision of seating and educational materials for students and teacher at all targeted schools,
- Soft component: training workshops for teachers on EiE and core subjects, PSS training sessions and school management for PTAs and teachers, health promotion sessions, awareness raising campaigns and environmental health activities at schools and at community level, Protection activities (GBV awareness sessions and distribution of dignity kits for the girls at grade 6 and 7th), IC materials designing and distribution (posters, pamphlets, Radio and TV messages, mobile video and cinema shows etc…),

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Blue Nile State has received recently an above average heavy rainy season resulting into flooding and related diseases including a cholera outbreak affecting 5 out of 7 localities which affected the whole State system including the education sector. According to the FMoE assessment (92) basic schools at Blue Nile State have been affected by the heavy rains and flash floods (Emergency Committee statistics survey on September 2019). OCHA, WHO, UNICEF and the Government led joint efforts to assess and find out the humanitarian needs caused by flash floods and the Cholera outbreak. At the State (364,200) from the total population have been identified as affected,

2 - Activities / Outputs

- Rehabilitation and construction of damaged and destroyed classrooms and latrines at the targeted schools is the most important intervention right now. - Provision of the EiE materials for students and teachers is also a priority. Training for teachers on EiE, PSS, Core subjects and for the PTAs is essential especially now. - Environmental health and hygiene promotion campaigns at schools and the community is very important as well. - Provide rapid response and dignity hygienic kits to meet protection needs among affected students and family visits by social workers to the families to raise their awareness on GBV and other harms around the targeted areas.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The constructions will avoid using or damaged natural resources materials such as wood from trees, PBA will try to make use of the surplus water from the water points to irrigate new seedlings and spread the culture of reserving natural resources and environment conservation among young children through the CC activities, public campaigns and national events.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project will provide rapid response and dignity hygienic kits to meet protection needs among vulnerable students specially girls, and also will arrange home visits by social workers to the families to raise their awareness on GBV and other harms around the targeted areas. PBA also will adopt the PSEA JfA on our future projects including this project and work with communities on SEA and how they can report cases and establishing local mechanisms.
