Project Summary

South Sudan is struggling to cope with different shocks that have affected the WASH infrastructure in the country. This has left a lot of people in the country struggling to meet the basic WASH needs. Integrated WASH service delivery is essential in alleviating the immerse WASH needs of the vulnerable and affected populations in South Sudan that have been affected by conflict and shocks associated with flooding as a result of climate change.
Rubkona county is having Immerse WASH needs and are in need of urgent which needs integrated WASH services to alleviate the suffering of the vulnerable populations. Rubkona County is bordering Sudan and in the recent days received influx of returnees
The county is in IPC phase 4 and therefore require urgent WASH intervention to ensure that the vulnerable and affected populations have access to safe drinking water, sanitation facilities, protection mainstreaming is observed and malnourished children in the counties have access to clean water for consumption.
Women Agency for resilience and Transformation (WART) proposes to cater for WASH needs of the vulnerable population in the three counties by integrating WASH in nutrition by provision of clean and safe water in the nutrition centers and supplying WASH NFIs and WASH kits to Couples with Malnourished children(Caretaker/child). This is because it has been proven that WASH needs aggravate or contribute to malnutrition.
Hygiene promotion is key in supporting behavior change among the communities to avoid hazardous behavior like open defecation that lead to contamination of water points thus increasing the risk of water infections. WART will conduct hygiene promotion in the target counties with focus on open defecation free bomas and proper utilization of sanitation facilities.
WART will also ensure access to sanitation facilities by rehabilitating temporally sanitation facilities to the vulnerable and affected populations in the target counties. Temporary water solutions will be offered by light rehabilitation of the water points and formation of water management committees.
WART will offer integrated WASH and protection services by conducting safe audits during rehabilitation of the water and sanitation facilities as well as distribution of MHM kits and ensure that the sanitation facilities are safe for women and girls by having locks that will ensure their safety during use of the sanitation facilities.
Training health workers and nutrition workers on minimum WASH package activities is key in supporting integration of services, WART will train the health workers and nutrition workers on this key topic to ensure that they are able to implement WASH activities in the health facilities and the nutrition centers. The organization will as well distribute hygiene kits to the children admitted in stabilization centers and nutrition facilities to ensure proper and hygienic practices that will hence improve their recovery.
Project will target 11111 vulnerable and affected populations in the three counties with of which 2500 will be children under five with malnutrition, 5511 women and 4100 men. 1029 will be persons with disabilities and the rehabilitation of water points and the sanitation facilities will also be disability inclusive to ensure that access to all in the project implementation.

1. Needs

South Sudan has been having high WASH needs. This can be attributed to years of political unrest and conflict among communities that have destroyed WASH infrastructure in the community. WASH needs continue to increase over the years as signing of peace agreement in 2018 and the formation of transitional government of national unity have seen influx of returnees in the country. The increased population has strained the available WASH facilities.The country was devastated by ravaging floods caused by abnormally high rainfall that has also contributed immensely in destruction of WASH facilities contamination of water and thus exposure to Water related infections as reported by health partners. (South Sudan Flood Snapshot issue 3&4 2022) The country is experiencing severe food insecurity with all the three target counties being in IPC 4 an emergency situation (South Sudan IPC Report Oct 2022) and high GAM rates of above WHO emergency threshold of 15% (Nutrition Cluster GAM rates Jan 2022). This places the vulnerable children who are malnourished to dangers of high morbidity and mortality from WASH related infections like cholera and Acute Watery Diarrhea.The conflict in Sudan has led to displacements as people are running away to the conflict and the country has reported over 340000 people who have crossed to South Sudan. Rubkona county is at the boarder and are receiving a huge number of returnees and this is greatly draining the available WASH facilities with the returnees facing acute water shortages and lack of essential WASH facilities.Women and school girls are exposed to protection threats as they walk long distances in search of water and as they look for sanitation facilities. They also lack essential menstrual hygiene management kits that are very important for safety and dignity of women and girls. This has made school going children miss school during menses therefore contributing to the low literate levels in the country.Open defecation is a major issue in South Sudan and has led to contamination of water points. Over 69 % of the communities in the country, Rubkona county populations are practicing open defecation and this has led to increased numbers of persons affected by water borne infections.WART will therefore implement integrated WASH activities to ensure that the vulnerable and affected populations are able to get enough water for drinking and domestic use as per the sphere standards, sanitation facilities and safe menstrual hygiene management for women and girls.

2. Project activities

1. Integrate WASH and Nutrition In the three counties which are having High GAM rates and highly food insecure by doing the following activities:• Rehabilitation of water points in the Nutrition centers• Hygiene promotion for caretakers of children admitted with SAM and MAM in Nutrition facilities• Distribution of WASH NFIs and Hygiene kits to couples(Caregiver/child)admitted with SAM and MAM in nutrition centers2. Integrate WASH in health facilities in the three counties and building resilience to flood affected populations by doing the following activities• Rehabilitation of semi-permanent water points after safety audits in health facilities and in the community affected by floods.• Rehabilitation of sanitation facilities in health facilities and communities affected by floods• Hygiene promotion in the communities• Training health workers on minimum WASH activity package3. Mitigation of gender based violence through WASH interventions by doing the following activities• Safety audits before rehabilitation of latrines and water points.• Distribution of MHM kits to vulnerable and affected women and girls.
