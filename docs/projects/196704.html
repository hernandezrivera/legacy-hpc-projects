Project Summary

Bonyan project is intending to expand its provision of integrated community-based and individually targeted protection interventions across NWS. The Project aims to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs providing protection prevention and response services to most vulnerable due to a combination of physical, social, environmental, and political factors: age,gender,diversity,specific needs,displacement status, Health Conditions and Disabilities including IDPs, HCs and returnees. Bonyan will establish 6 community protection centers in Albab, Azaz, Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim and Sarmada, expand protection services through 14 mobile teams in (Albab, Azaz, Deir Samaan, Idleb, Harim, Sarmada, Tilaada, Qah, Maaret Tamsrin and Kelly). Bonyan is an active member of the protection cluster and regularly sends all reports like 4Ws for the cluster.
Child Protection Targeting 21430 individuals (800 CWD) through CM services for 600 B/G, ECF for 300 children, 500 Referral. Bonyan will focus on children maybe be exposed to: (Psychosocial distress Neglect Threat/danger to the child's life, Violence Abuse in all its forms ChildHeaded families Risks related to Children with disabilities, Child exploitation and employment Children without parental/family care). Structure PSS activities inside Centers, Sime Structure PSS through outreach teams, awareness-raising and parenting sessions. Bonyan will establish and train 7 CPNs in the Idleb Harim, and Jabal Saman, who will inform community members about CP services. This project will target adolescents and youth through life skill sessions, awareness, and (Initiatives) preparation and implementation.
General Protection Targeting 20200 (M5000, W6075, B4125, G5000) including PWD. Bonyan during this project will Establish Protection Help Desks to share and publish the information about the service in each location, teams will conduct referrals, legal awareness about the importance of civil documentation with Key messaging on PSEA. IPA services will be delivered to the vulnerable people Protection monitoring will continue in this project.
Mine Action Targeting 11100 (M2100, W2400, B3600, G3000). The mobile team will conduct RE sessions and distribute mine RE materials whilst also delivering RE sessions in schools, public facilities, or in private halls. RE sessions will target male and female, community members who are considered at high risk of explosive hazards. The session contains a lot of information using MASC materials. Train ERW community focal points to raise their capacity in delivering training and awareness on risk education. The training contains a lot of information about ERWs and the best ways to report any remnants, each FP will provide MASC brochures.
Gender Based Violence Targeting 6500 (M1450, W2400, B1200, G1450). Bonyan teams will conduct activities aiming to identify and response to the most common types of GBV that (W&G) experience like forced and early marriage, economic exploitation by intimate partner, and family members, community, denial of work and education, and physical violence ( awarenessraising using GBV SC toolkit and referral aim to increasing the aware of affected W&G in addition to increase a positive attitude towards gender-based violence among men and boys. Empowerment activities for women and girls .M & F GBV actors will be trained on GBV topics Bonyan will coordinate with GBV SC to receive the required trainings. M&E Toolkit and SOPs will be the resource of the MEAL team to monitor and evaluate GBV activities. Bonyan will arrange for access to assistance and services without any kind of discrimination and enable equal and impartial access for vulnerable groups or have difficulty accessing assistance and services like PWDs, and elderly people.

3 - Needs

On HNO2023 the absence of longer-term solutions to displacement, ongoing violations of IHL and human rights law, extensive destruction of infrastructure, and inadequate access to services and to the population in need are likely to continue impacting the protection landscape.The price inflation of basic commodities, shortages of food and fuel and depreciation of local currency are expected to worsen, driving increased vulnerability, and leading to heavy reliance on humanitarian assistance and resorting to harmful coping mechanisms.Potential escalation in conflict activity is expected to contribute to increased displacement where 278,200 IDPs already reside in over 260 sites.Related to GBV a recent study from "Voices from Syria 2022" 1,225 girls and women in NW Syria showed that: “When a family is subjected to different types of pressures during military escalation, women suffer a greater and more diverse number of abusive and violent acts by their husbands compared to that during times of peace”. the continuation of armed conflict significantly restricts W&G freedom of movement and, protection services.Child rights violations (child recruitment, detention, neglect, exploitation, sexual violence, abduction, attacks on schools and hospitals, and denial of humanitarian access) will likely increase in 2023.HNO2023 area Severity Scale:In Idleb sub-district:4/MaretTamsrensub-district:5/Harim sub-district:5/Dana sub-district:5 /Jebel Saman:4 / Al Bab :4 / Azaz:4.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy
