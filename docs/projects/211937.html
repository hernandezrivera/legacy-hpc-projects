Project Summary

The continuation of the deteriorating conditions over the past years has tremendously increased the number of socially, psychologically and physically affected persons along with their multiple diverse needs. This project is a leveraging of Al Nada Development NGO's vast experience in the humanitarian fields, especially after partnership with UNHCR and UNICEF via its community centers in Damascus, Rural Damascus providing wide range of services (PSS, HRBP, GBV, CP, CLI & YLI, SSBP and education). This project aims to provide all kinds of support and services which contribute to rehabilitation and development of the affected people in Sbeina & Beitsawa by offering psycho-social support (recreational activities to shape up psychological well-being and mental health, counselling, raising awareness sessions, workshops, referrals and home-based rehabilitation services includes parental skills) Also Child protection: all children have the right to live in safe and violence-free, abuse-free surroundings consolidating their skills to develop and progress. Children and adolescents in Sbeinh, Beitsawa are unable to attain minimum proficiency levels in reading, writing and fundamentals of learning. Besides the number of drop outs that is determinately increasing. Wide spread of poverty, economic fragility and the extremely deteriorated livelihood conditions forced children and adolescents to drop out of schools and get involved in labor to support their families. Activities will focus on Children/Children with disabilities and Caregivers including raising awareness sessions, case management for children and adolescents prone to explosive ordnance risk, abuse, violence and harassments. Group activities enhancing children’s social and educational inclusion. Education activities will focus on non-formal settings to enhance numeracy, proficiency in reading and writing. Besides, workshops to revive life skills, remedial classes in both English and Arabic, learning via playing. In addition to explosive ordnance risk education. The project tackles diversity of people of concerns including explosive ordnance victims either direct or non-direct and disabilities (children, adolescents, women, men) from local community, IDPs and returnees. GBV services will be provided including raising awareness sessions, case management, referral to specialized services and dignity kits. Concerning M& E plan, all activities will be reported and monitored on weekly, quarterly and yearly basis plus regular 5Ws, PRMD, PFR, and Activity Info. 10% of children with disabilities will be targeted in education.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

According to HNO ,92% of individuals older than 59 have disabilities. Children with disabilities are more likely not to attend or drop out of school. 22% and 47% of children with disabilities aged 6-11 and 15-17 are not in education. Both Beitsawa, Sbeina are in urgent need for several basic needs and have no community center yet. Shortage of schools, poor livelihood conditions, no access to basic services such as electricity, education, water, health services, GBV risks, and children labor are major problems in both areas. Persons with physical/mental impairment and injuries, including direct victims of EO incidents and their families, face urgent needs. Children Labor in Beitsawa is 70% while 40% in Sbeina. School dropout exposes children to protection threats such as child labor mainly for boys and early marriage mainly for girl. Negative coping mechanisms are on the rise such as child labor including its worst forms to generate income, in Beitsawa most children are working on melting metallic collections to extract copper but faced death .Similar to other community center of Al Nada, protection activities will be arranged: case management, PSS, CP, GBV, legal aid via (collaborating with SARC), mine risk-education, social and recreational activities, health awareness and counselling consultation. The project will target beneficiaries of diverse social, educational, ethnic and religious backgrounds.Risks Management: Children Labor. Ignorance. Children with disabilities facing bullying. Children safety. Mitigation Measures raising awareness sessions for caregivers. Case management. Individual or group counselling, activities/workshops. Remedial classes. location of the facility is away from threats. child-friendly spaces, surrounding pose no safety risks, also establishing separate, secure, hygienic private washroom facilities. Trained staff members. Paying particular attention to children with disabilities. Infrastructure and services can be accessed by persons with disabilities, make access paths for wheelchair access. Other risks Bad electricity supply, lack of enough lighting. Mitigation: installing lights or distributing personal flashlights. Risk: Women could be prone to gender-bias due to cultural norms, women are more prone to violence/harassment being shy/afraid to report their accidents. Mitigation: Assuring women beneficiaries that confidentiality, privacy is respected in all forms of consultation, counseling and personal information. Well-separated rooms. Risk: Victims of explosive ordnance especially girls and women are more likely to face discrimination, and isolation due to their disabilities. Prosthetic referrals could face risk of distance and accommodation. Al Nada will apply PSEA policy consist of a Code of Conduct, details of reporting any misconduct (direct reporting, complaints mailbox, WhatsApp, and e-mail). Zero Tolerance policy is declared against any type of SEA.
