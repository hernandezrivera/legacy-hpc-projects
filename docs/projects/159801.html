Project Summary

The project aims to mitigation the farmer struggles and suffering resulting from the
Israel's occupation and settlers attacks in area C in West of Salfit by targeting farmers
who highly vulnerable and lack access to basic services, The specific objective is to increase the well being and income of women and men engaged in small-scale agriculture in vulnerable communities through improved agricultural and access to land and market. The project support 1647 individual beneficiaries (840 M, 807 F) of the communities and families to build their capacity to absorb shocks, including access to land through facilitate these essential services through reclamation 150 dunums of agricultural land, construct 6 kilo of agricultural roads and rehabilitate 7 water cisterns in Kafr al Dik, Bruqin and Deir Ballut to ensure threats to food security, income and life in general are minimized. More precisely, this will contribute to:
1- Support farmers’ resilience in the targeted locations to be able to withstand settlers
attacks and the occupation practices on their land's productivity.
2- Improve farmers' livelihood sources in the targeted locations.
3- Prevent farms and trees from wild pigs which mainly brought up by settlers. which become an extra risk for farmers mainly women when attending their lands
4- Direct access to food by unemployed through working in agricultural land.
The entire area are severely suffers from occupation threat and settlers attack like land confiscations, so the entire targeted communities will have access to better and more water, better access to their land through the rehabilitated roads, which will serve as well as alternative and saver roads whenever the main roads are closed by the occupation and its settlers.

1 - Needs

To understand the causes behind deteriorating livelihood conditions in Salfit, various economic, demographic, agricultural, nutritional, health, environmental and food security issues must be considered. So the farmers suffer of strangling and restrictions on movement by separation wall, bypass roads that are not allowed to use, limited access to water, and climatic change (less rain water and high temperature) and settler attacks, so the suggested interventions like agricultural road, land rehabilitation, and rehabilitation of cisterns wells, will facilitate/ease farmers’ movement, and work on their adherence to land and plant them. Also they will use the harvested water in supplementary irrigation during hot summers, this will support farmers livelihoods and resilience to stand in the face of land confiscation of and therefore their survival in the profession of agriculture, which keep their livelihood source secure, profitable and sustainable. The needs identified for this project emerged during AAA implementation of the Previous project activities and according to the needs identified in this project to continuous needs in these targeted areas, (after consultation with all parties the Directorate of Agriculture and Municipalities ). so the idea falls in cluster objective (SO3). To support the resilience and the productive capacity of farmers, particularly small-scale ones are restored / enhanced and livelihoods protected in Bruqin, Deir Ballut and Kaf al Dik in area C West Salfit. AAA did a comprehensive needs assessment in Salfait area early 2018, This helped AAA to confirm the farmers’ challenges and needs, like farmers are facing various levels of drops in Agricultural crops productivity, and access restrictions to their lands. The main reasons are Israeli illegal settler's violence against farmers and their farms, confiscation notices against the possession of their land, release of wild pigs (which become an extra risk for farmers mainly women when attending their lands), limited services like an extension, damaged roads, and continues flash floods. The assessment reported sharp increase in the unemployment rate in those villages mainly among farmers and especially young people and women It is worth to mention that 9 Israeli settlements are exist in the three targeted communities, The project is targeting a total of 1647 individual beneficiaries (dis-aggregated by gender) beneficiaries will benefit from increased access to their land to ensure inclusion of women, and other groups as well as involve men, girls and girls at schools, youth, and elderly. These are the main issues that farmers are currently facing farmers from obtaining the optimal use of their main source of income that contributes significantly to their families' food security. AAA offers to work on the most badly eroded 6 km of agricultural roads, rehabilitate 150 dunum and 7 rehabilitation of cisterns for the most vulnerable farmers. This will enhance farmers' access to their agriculture lands, stability, better access to livelihoods source. AAA will implement this project in close coordination with the MoA and other actors in the area to avoid any duplication and overlapping. The assessment revealed that women are more vulnerable to the limited access to land and low income, it is worth to note that over than 65% of the workload in agriculture is made by women Discerption of targeted villages needs and situations: land confiscation from the Israelis occupation, colonies and bypass Israelis roads built on the land of these villages etc. The intervention objectives of the road rehabilitation, wells, and land rehabilitation is to facilitate the access of farmers to their lands, and facilitate the transportation of the inputs and outputs to and from the land without any extra cost or effort, better access to water and other inputs, and this more sustainable income. It will reinforce farmers' resilience on their lands.

2 - Activities

Prior to the project implementation, AAA will strictly coordinate the final definition and selection of localities and beneficiaries with line ministries offices, and with any other organization working in the same area and sector of activities. AAA will be targeting farmers in all the communities mentioned with main activities including (rehabilitation of agricultural roads, rehabilitation of agricultural lands and cistern rehabilitation). Through agricultural roads, farmers' mainly women access to their agricultural land will be facilitated, with less time and more cost effective. Farmers will perform farming practices more effectively and easily and secure, this will increase productivity and quality of the produce and easy access to marketing of high quality of agricultural products. Furthermore, trees and human including women and children will be safer from wild animals’ attacks. AAA in coordination with local CBOs, related ministries, and village councils, will formulate a steering committee in each community, which will create clear bene ciary selection criteria with clear marks that will 'help in the selection of the poorest farmers. The criteria should include technical standards, household status, and income, 15% extra marks will be given to each household headed by women, number of family members, disability members in the family, chronic diseases in the family, having a university students in the family, the benefited land space, the type of crop, where we will target crops with higher viable economically, and land location if its near settlements and bypass roads, military camps... etc. AAA. will discuss the applications with the participation of the selected committee to each location with the implementation of field visits to verify the information written in the applications specifcally (the technical standards, the economic situation) AAA will make sure to enhance women role through participation in the local committees and will give priority to women-headed households and youth. Rehabilitation roads will facilitate farmers mainly women access to cultivated lands, in addition, to transfer the product or production materials to the cultivated land. The agricultural road will ease the access to tools and machines to the agriculture land, which decreases women workload, noting that 65% of the agricultural work is on the shoulders of women (Oxfam’s olive value chain report). AAA will make sure to women representative on the selection committee. Activity : Rehabilitating or restoring essential productive assets. 1- Rehabilitation150 dunum . - Rehabilitate 80 Dunum in Kafr ad Dik, Rehabilitate 70 Dunum in Bruqin, owned by over 230 individual beneficiaries in total The activity will provide 107 workers with over 1933 working days, ( 65 individual beneficiaries) . 2- Rehabilitation of 7 cistern. that serve around 49 dunums of agricultural lands , owned by over 32 individual beneficiaries in total, The activity will provide 14 workers with over 266 working days, ( 492 individual beneficiaries) . - Rehabilitate 5 cistern in Kafr ad Dik, 2 cistern in Bruqin. 3- Rehabilitation of 6 km of agricultural roads that serve around 1680 dunums of agricultural lands owned by over 828 individual beneficiaries in total. Rehabilitate 2 Km of Agriculture roads in Kafr el Dik, Rehabilitate 1 Km of Agriculture roads in Bruqin, Rehabilitate 3 Km of Agriculture roads in Deir Ballut. Additional Targets : The expected objectively verifiable indicators and target of the project are: 1. A total of 1647 number of individual beneficiaries (dis-aggregated by gender) beneficiaries will benefit from increased access to food. 2. Increase the outcome of confrontations with settlers.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

AAA will follow protection mainstreaming guides disseminated by the FSS Cluster: - All activities aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individual in the good spirit of the relevant bodies of human rights law, international humanitarian law and local laws. - Key Protection principles AAA will ensure will include: Do no harm: by avoiding exposing people to further harm as a result of your actions, and ensure that: * The environment and way in which assistance is provided do not expose people and environment to further human rights abuses or violations; * Take all reasonable steps to ensure that the affected population are not subject to violent attack, or forced or induced into undertaking actions that may cause them harm or violate their rights; * Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. * Assistance and protection measures do not undermine local capacities for self-protection. Support the efforts of the affected population and local community to find security and restore dignity. * None-discrimination: ensure equitable and impartial access to assistance, without discrimination on any grounds: * Challenge any attempts to exclude parts of the target communities. * Promote respect for human rights, and assist and support affected people to claim their rights and access remedies from relevant authorities; to obtain information on their entitlements and secure the documentation needed to demonstrate their entitlements, and to recover by providing psychosocial and community support. * AAA will Ensure consultation with the target population at all stages, and the participation of all in the design and targeting of interventions, in particular vulnerable and marginalized groups. * Environment-building - AAA project will contribute to creating an environment conducive to respect for human rights and the rule of law, including the reduction of exposure or vulnerability to protection risks.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

AAA conducted the needs assessment in a very collaborative way, with the targeted municipalities, women club, cooperative, and community leaders. The needs assessment results then validated by an open workshop in the community which includes affected people, farmers, women, youth and elderly and owners. Moreover, AAA agreed and set up the local committee which consists of the municipalities, women club, cooperative and community leaders. The committee will govern the implementation of the project through out the project life. AAA is mandated to and work with rural communities and Farmers across the WB, AAA team will ensure the implementation of its feedback complaint mechanism utilizing the following actions: -Regular daily site visits by AAA field supervisors with on-site response to concerns from people in (Kafr al Dik, Bruqin and Deir ballut). - Facilitation of information sessions between people and the 3 municipalities where all people's concerns are publicly heard and adjustments made accordingly to any activity. this implies both men and women are participating. - The communities will have access to a complaint box at the municipalities, also can reach up to the AAA office and team through phone calls and direct visits. - Updated AAA feedback/complaint database will be shared accordingly with Donor reporting. - Legal awareness sessions with people to be aware of security assumptions and legal status of the area. By including different stakeholders in the community and other actors from outside the community, including farmers and owners, AAA believes they have taken concrete steps in considering Do No Harm policy. AAA will keep an eye on any new issues in the area to avoid any negative impact as a result of the project activities.
