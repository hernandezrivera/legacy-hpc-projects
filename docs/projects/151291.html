Project Summary

The intent of the project is to fill the existing critical gaps in protection targeting 14,000 beneficiaries in Afgoye corridor particularly Kahda district. The purpose of the intervention is to reduce the incidents of SGBV and providing child protection services in the community living in Kahda. The project is intended to respond, have positive impact in reducing SGBV and improving the life of children and help the community to have behaviour change towards GBV.
Most of the peeople who are violated particularly in the IDP camps are women, they have limited information and their rights are violated to the extreme therefore the project will enlighten the GBV survivors on our centres to seek protection services.
The other vital purpose of the project is to respond to GBV incidents by providing quality survivors centred services. The protection activities include counselling, medical support, referral, awareness and sensitization and legal services to overcome trauma and depression. The child protection services will include establishment of Child friendly spaces, identification, documentation and reunification of children with their families.
Material supplies for GBV survivors and children will be provided. This include dignity kits, solar lamps, child playing materials and children clothing.

Needs

According to UNHCR bulletin, there is widespread of sexual violence (SGBV), child recruitment, limitations in the freedom of movement, forced evictions and displacements remains a perceive feature of conflict in Somalia.According to HCT an estimated 3.6M people are in need of protection services. According to SSWC protection assessment report in 2018 showed 76% of reported GBV incidents happened in the IDP camps which largely affected the women headed households. There is high need for protection services in South Central Somalia, particularly in Afgoye corridor where there was high displacement of internally persons due to modernization of the city. Most of the camps have moved towards Sinka Dhere along the Afgoye corridor. Apart from displacement there were evictions of the most vulnerable persons from their camps in a multi agency assessment reported over 50,000 persons were evicted from the IDP camps in Mogadishu. Of particular concern is the situation of conflict-affected populations, including those in hard-to-reach areas and/or displaced and marginalized populations. These groups face an elevated risk of violations due to exploitative and abusive structures faced by many – especially protracted – displaced communities. These violations include pervasive gender-based violence (GBV), child rights violations – child recruitment and early marriage for example-, and forced evictions which have seen a concerning increase in the first five months of 2018, affecting more than 171,000.

Activities

The project will address the needs of the community through:1. Provision of Psycho social- Survivors of GBV require appropriate support to deal with the psychological and mental health consequences of an attack – which may include depression, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), anxiety, and thoughts of suicide or self-harm – and the potential social consequences, including stigma, discrimination and rejection. Psychosocial support, as part of a holistic and community-based intervention, can provide crucial assistance to survivors, as well as their families, social networks and communities, and help to prevent violence in the future.2. Provision of medical support services- Survivors of GBV undergo different GBV types medical support is paramount particularly to survivors of rape who who require history and physical examination to be done, this can be done by trained nurses and provide medical treatment for the survivor3. Develop community structures in the IDP camps- Its important to build the structures in the community by training them on the prevention and response of GBV so that they can be agents or ambassadors of change. SSWC will train selected community members on community based protection as part of establishment of community structures. Training of community support groups will advocate for protection concerns within their communities.4. Legal supprt- This will offer justice to the survivor of GBV, the perpetrator will be brought to book for violating the rights of the abused victim.5.Distribution of material support- GBV survivors and children will be supported with materials like dignity kits, clothing, playing materials. The dignity kits incentifies the survivors.6. Awareness raising and capacity building sessions on training on production of fuel efficient stoves and services provided including sensitisation on harmful practices.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

There is the high risk of displacement and evictions of the most vulnerable persons in the IDP camps. SSWC will encourage the government to lease land for more than 10 years or buy permanent land for them.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Women might face risk of exclusion this is because of the norms and the culture of the Somali people. Women are not given equal opportunities like their male counterparts, to mitigate this SSWC will develop a strong criteria for selecting the beneficiaries and sensitizing women on how to echo their concerns. Separated and unaccompanied children might also face the risk of exclusion since they are not given time to address themselves openly. The project will target children between the ages of 3-17 years so as they can be protected from violence and exploitation.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The intervention will address mitigation measures to overcome armed conflict and violence by advocating for peace and shunning conflicts. Peace building forums will be conducted in the camps during sensitization meetings and awareness campaigns.
