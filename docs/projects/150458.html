Project Summary

The project aims at supporting farmers and improving their economic situation through rehabilitation of their agricultural facilities, the project activities will be implemented during a period of two years and they are described as follow:
In the first year of project the implemented activities will be : The rehabilitation of 18 poultry farms and 2 agricultural wells , after the rehabilitation 20 solar units will be installed for the rehabilitated agricultural facilities , out of them 2 units for the operating of the agricultural wells and 18 for ventilation and lighting of poultry farm, Moreover , through the implementation of the project activities we will rehabilitate 100 donums of destroyed agricultural land and 25 water ponds to store and harvest the water for the use of irrigation, in the other hand , 1200 working days will be created during the implementation of the activities which will lead to employ 48 unemployed workers for the duration of 25 days each, out of these workers 12 will be skilled and 36 unskilled .
The activities and the outputs of the project can be summarized as follow :
Activity 1: Rehabilitation of poultry farms and installation of solar units for generating electricity to light and to ventilate the rehabilitated farms .
Output 1: 18 poultry farms are rehabilitated , lighted and ventilated.
Activity 2: Rehabilitation of agricultural wells and installation of solar units for operating the rehabilitated agricultural wells
Output 2: 2 agricultural wells are rehabilitated and operated through use of solar energy.
Activity 3: Rehabilitation and cultivation of destroyed open field agricultural land
Output 3: 100 donums of destroyed open field a agricultural land are rehabilitated and cultivated
Activity 4: Rehabilitation of ponds for water harvesting
Output 4: 25 Water ponds are rehabilitated

Activity 5: employing of unemployed workers
Output 5 : 48 worker are employed and 1200 working days are created , each worker 25 working days
Multi year intervention:
The project is planned to be implemented for a period of two years to achieve complementary activities aiming to enhance the productive capacities of farmers at all levels. The proposed interventions for the period of 2 years are :
1. Rehabilitation and operating of 2 agricultural wells through using the solar energy
2. Rehabilitation , lighting and ventilation of 43 poultry farms through using the solar energy
3. Rehabilitation and cultivation of 200 donums of destroyed open field agricultural land
4. Rehabilitation of 50 ponds for water harvesting
5. Employing of 96 unemployed workers

following with phase two which is the 2nd years we will complement the work of the 1st year

Needs

The 2 million people living in the Gaza Strip are witnessing a continuous decline in their living conditions. Their food insecurity is directly linked to households’ inability to purchase food available on the market. Food security situation in Gaza Strip continues to be characterized by high levels of food insecurity, with ranges of 40-47% HHs being food insecure for the last five years. This condition is also driven by the increased rates of unemployment, which exceeded50% in the second quarter of 2018, for the first ever. This results in increased poverty levels, which is one of the main determinant of food insecurity. It also increases the level of aid dependency, especially on food assistance.The situation has been worsened by the funding crisis for agencies funded by USA funds, especially UNRWA, considering that approximately 66% of the population in Gaza Strip are refugees. The funding crisis is clearly represented also by the FSS HRP funding appeal shortfall, with only 24% of the overall FSS budget being funded as of mid-year 2018.The continuous salary crisis for civil servants adds new layers of the population in need for immediate assistance, including food assistance. If the situation continues, it will lead to further increase in poverty rates, where 53% of individuals in Gaza Strip are reported living below the poverty level based on their consumption pattern, as reported by PCBS for 2017.Moreover, the long lasting energy crisis has put an additional burden on farmers who already suffer from an increase in agricultural input costs accompanied by low purchasing power by households, and compounded with a decrease in vegetable and poultry meat market prices, compared to the same period last year. This situation further reduces the profitability and sustainability of agricultural activities for many farmers, driving them to continuous losses, putting their agricultural practices at risk. Hence, the already poor and limited investments in agriculture are generally highly expected to decrease, leaving vulnerable farmers with no choice but abandoning their activities.All this has put more pressure on households capacities to address their basic needs, as their income, and therefore their purchasing power, further decreased, potentially forcing them to resort to negative copying strategies. The proposed activities have been determined based on the results of the focus groups discussion which were held by UAWC team during August /2018, September / 2018 and after the analysis of 600 questionnaires that were filled by the farmers, through UAWC ’s team and UAWC local committees covering over all GS; all the results showed that there is a high need to rehabilitate damaged agricultural land and other agricultural facilities such as water ponds , poultry farms and agricultural wells and to find solution to overcome the problem of electricity cut that affected their production because they cannot irrigate their crops in regulated time. . During the needs assessment the women in UAWC agricultural local committees (The percentage of women in agricultural committees is more than 30%) played significant role in the identifying the needs especially for women who are working in the agricultural activities and marginalized by male farmers. Therefore, the women will have priority during the selection of beneficiaries and their opportunity in selection will be equal to the opportunity of men without gender discrimination. Based on UAWC team and the agricultural local committees needs assessment the project will target the more vulnerable farmers groups in 9 communities across the Gaza strip, in the other hand the intervention priorities of FSS which were recommended by MOA are taken in consideration during the design of the project activities

Employing of 96 unemployed workers The project activities will be followed up monitored by the project staff that will be recruited by UAWC, they will be responsible for the selection of the beneficiaries and implementing all project activities. Furthermore the UAWC operation manager will be responsible for monitoring the implementation of the work-plan, will observe and document any obstacles that might delay the implementation of some activities, and will find alternatives and solutions. UAWC’s operation manager will coordinate with FSS coordinator, with whom they will establish daily contact for issues related to the implementation of the project, both will visit field locations at least once a month to ensure that the project is following the implementation plan, to measure the achievement of results and indicators and to discuss the budget follow up and expenditures.UAWC will submit monthly data reports and budget follow-ups to FSS coordinator as well as full interim and final narrative and financial reports. Adding to above mentioned, following activities will be done • Additional feedback and complaints mechanism will be put in place to ensure that communities provide regular feedback about the pertinence and the quality of the services.• Project reports, evaluation result and database of beneficiaries will be shared with FSSBeneficiaries selection criteria:The selection of beneficiaries, which is based on combined criteria , will be coordinated with the MoA, and will consider the following: 1) HHs who own Destroyed agricultural land and located in the project targeted area.2) HHs have big family member( an average of 5 Members) and the agriculture activity is their only source of income.3) priority will be given to women headed HHs.4) priority will be given to farmers’ HHs affected by the 2014 war ( wells and poultry farms) 5) Small farmers who's land ownership not exceed 5 donum 6) Small Scale poultry farmers and their farms are full in use and not neglect . 7) farmers who are ready to facilitate the work of the project teams and their land located in a safe and accessible area 8) Priority will be given for Families with disable member 9) worker who are unemployed and not receiving any employment opportunities in the last 6 months
