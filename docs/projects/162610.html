1 - Needs

As a result of relative improvement in security situation in south Darfur and reduction of food assistance and other humanitarian aid in the return areas, large number of families from farmer and agro-pastoralist has returned to their original locations to practice their farming activities many difficulties are faced returnees in Girada, kasss , beilil and Nitaga localities in starting their farming livelihood activities, lack of agriculture input, poor agriculture production especially in the last rainy season as result poor rain, lack of awareness in agriculture techniques. Agro-pastoralist in the target area where water for livestock and human use is problematic and source of conflict, there is dire need for water for their livestock they share hand pumps that is for human use with poor water yield, need for veterinary services through vaccination and capacity building of pastoralist and water for livestock in Nitaga locality. This project aims at provision of agriculture inputs for new returnees’ households in Girada, kasss , beilil and Nitaga localities

2 - Activities / Outputs

 Provision of rainy season crop seeds and vegetable seeds and tools for 3,400 households from IDPs , returnees and host communities as well as conduction of agriculture extension training.  Support Vaccination and treatment of 6000 heads of animal in south Darfur (Girada, kasss , beilil and Nitaga localities ),and the animals belong to 1000 households in four localities. Rehabilitations of tow diagnostic labs in Girada, and kass) . Training of 200 CAHWs from Girada, kasss , beilil and Nitaga localities on cost recovery  Training of of 200 pastoralists and agro pastoralist on animal feed preservation 65 participants from Girada ,70 participant from beilil and 65 participants from Kass  agriculture extension training in vegetable gardening for women group participants from Three localities  Rehabilitations the water point by Samaritan Aid Organization to meet the needs of the local livestock’s of each locality in dry season by 30% from total budget in four targeted Areas of project. Among the 17000 individuals each locality the total number of individuals shall be 4250 for each locality among the 4 localities.

B: Medium environmental impact with NO mitigation

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

S.O.A together with the responsible ministries have agreed to dispose all the syringes and vaccination bottles in an organized manner by burying them in a pothole so to increase more fertility to the soil that may perhaps be used for agricultureInstead of burning the used materials of vaccination in a rubbish pit shall cause destruction of the ozone layer thus affected the environment negatively. The animals that have not survived when vaccination has been conducted are to be buried to contribute to soil fertilization. in seed distribution the tins and polythene bag containing the seeds are also disposed through burying to avoid water contamination and pollution of the environment. some seeds are chemiclised thus affected the germination of the plants so S.A.O monitoring and evaluation team will ensure that the seedlings are none chemicalised and not expired
