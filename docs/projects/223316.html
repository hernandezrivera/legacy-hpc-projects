Project Summary

The proposed project 'Enhancing Household Economy for Vulnerable Communities' will employ a multi-faceted approach to enhance household economies, aims to support vulnerable women in communities to attain economic resilience and sustainable household cashflow, through VSLA, entrepreneurship support and strengthening income generating activities. The project will work with returnees and women in host communities, targeting 5607 women within communities of Mubi North (485 returnees, 485 host community in VSLA and 341 returnees, 341 host community in income generating activities), Mubi South (512 returnees, 512 host community in VSLA and 260 returnees, 267 host community in income generating activity), Michika (650 returnees, 651 host community in VSLA, and 145 returnees, 145 host community in income generating activity), Madagali (210 returnees, 211 host community in VSLA and 196 returnees, 196 host community in income generating activities) in northern Adamawa State.
The project will identify women within clustered communities, establish and strengthen savings group, conduct micro enterprise development training, monitor groups and provide grant to existing savings group members and identified women engaged in income generating activities, towards sustainable business growth. The project will further conduct community and household social cohesion session for project participants and their spouses, this to ensure meaningful participation of women in household decision making and access to economic opportunities, towards achieving the project’s goal.
The project will employ a participatory approach, engaging household members and community leaders and relevant stakeholders in the design, implementation, and monitoring of project activities. The project will also establish a monitoring and evaluation system to track progress and outcomes.
It is expected that the project will yield increased household income by at least 60% among project participants, enhanced household financial management and savings practices, with at least 70% of households saving regularly and increased entrepreneurship and business skills among household members, with at least 70% of households engaging in income-generating activities.
The project will be implemented in three phases:
1. (Months 1-2): Project inception, baseline survey, identification of project participants and establishment of VSLAs.
2. (Months 3-5): micro-enterprise and entrepreneurship development training, and disbursement of grants to project participants
3. (Months 6-9): supervisory support and mentorship, consolidation of project gains, monitoring and evaluation, end line survey and complete deployment of sustainability mechanisms.

Nigeria Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2025

Plan fields

1 - Needs

The project through its activities will address the identified needs of poor savings and investment culture, inadequate household income, limited access to financial services, poor financial management and micro enterprise development gap among community members engaged in income generating activities. The project through social cohesion mainstreaming will address the gap of exclusion among returnees and host community members in communities of implementation.

2 - Activities

The proposed project activities include community entry and stakeholder engagement, establishment of VSLAs, training on financial literacy and micro enterprise development, disbursement of grants to project participants, establishment of mentorship programs, monitoring and support, midline survey and endline survey, these activities aim to ensure project expected outcomes are met and community-led implementation is adapted for sustainability and accountability. The project will establish feedback mechanism in communities for informed decision-making and adjustments.Safety audit and risk assessment will be conducted continuously through implementation cycle to mitigate risks and ensure project results are achieved.
