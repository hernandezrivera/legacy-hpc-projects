Project Summary

The world is experiencing Covid-19 pandemic and Zimbabwe has not been spared either of this scourge. Globally, as of 20 May 2020, more than 4 918 938 people, had tested positive for the disease, with a huge figure of 323 723 sadly succumbing to the disease (Johns Hopkins University, Dashboard).To date Zimbabwe has recorded 46 cases of Covid-19 with 4 deaths (MoHCC COVID 19 Update of 19 May 2020). The President of Zimbabwe declared COVID-19 a national disaster and Zimbabwe is currently under lock down level 2 now, in an effort to contain and reduce the risks of spreading this disease. While the figures for COVID 19 in Zimbabwe seem low, this can be attributed to low screening and testing due to resource constrains. The disease has a potential to affect our total population of 13 million people across the country. Zimbabwe continues to receive returnees from outside the country, the majority of these, coming from hot-spot countries. This means the risk of spread of the diseases is still very high. The project seeks to reduce the risk of spread of Covid 19 to the high risk population targeting high traffic sites like markets, bus termini, public offices, any organised gatherings and water points through installation of 350 hand washing stations, distribution of 6350 hygiene kits, hygiene education on Covid 19 prevention to 148 500 people and support improvement of WASH infrastructure at 21 Health centres.

Plan fields

1 - Activities

1. Hand Washing Stations. These will be installed at various high traffic sites like markets, bust stations, public offices like Registry and District administration centres and Police stations. Volunteers will be recruited preferably from the Community Health Clubs or Village Health Worker/Community Workers. to ensure water is available in the buckets and waste water is properly and safely disposed. 2. Hygiene education will be provided though community campaigns using loud hailers or through small group discussions in the community. areas to be target are also high traffic areas like boreholes, market places, including community committees like water point committees3. Hygiene kits which include buckets, soap and water treatment tablets will be distributed to most vulnerable Households like the very poor, the disabled, the elderly, the sick and child headed family. These kits will assist in promoting hand washing and use of safe water for drinking.4. Health facilities will also be supported to improve the WASH services like repair of boreholes, construction of incinerators or repair other WASH infrastructures and procurement of PPE
