Project Summary

The project will target adolescent girls between the ages of 10-19 residing in Manicaland Province; in Buhera, Makoni and Nyanga districts, Harare Province, Matabeleland North province; Binga district . The selection of these areas was based on two criteria; the fact that they were identified as vulnerable and in need of humanitarian assistance as indicated in the Zimbabwe Vulnerability Assessment report of 2019 and also in order to build on the already existing ActionAid interventions. The target population will be 46992; 36992 women and girls and 10000 will be men and boys including traditional leaders.
Adolescent girls face heightened protection risks in emergencies, including Sexual Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR), sexual and gender-based violence as well as forced marriages and harmful practices. The project will address threats to the protection of adolescent girls in emergencies. This includes problems that existed before the emergency which are aggravated by the crisis including domestic violence and early child marriages. The project will also address direct threats such as demand of sex for food, or indirect threats like women being put in unsafe positions when they walk long distances for distribution.
Service Provision and Mitigation
ActionAid will establish / strengthen safe spaces in all the districts of operation. The project interventions will include; provision of safe spaces for women and girls following the core principles, on provision of psycho-social support, supporting survivors of violence to access justice and health services. AAZ will also ensure that there is adequate lighting through provision of solar lamps. In addition, dignity kits will be provided to all women.
Capacity Building
Women and girls will be trained on GBV and referral pathways. Community leaders will also be trained on GBV, case management and safe guarding. Committees will also be trained on GBV, referral pathways, case management and Sexual and Reproductive Health Rights (SRHR). Campaigns will be held and livelihoods challenges which increase vulnerability of women will be addressed.
