Project Summary

The project seeks to provide 360 beneficiaries (Females/ Males) yearly with the most popular professional and industrial skills required in the labor market. That will enable them to find job opportunities or start their own business and provide them and their families with a source of income in light of the difficult conditions that Syrians live in this region.
The project also seeks to supply the industrial sector with appropriate competencies to compensate for the increasing shortage of industrialists and professionals due to migration.
The project will also create 20 job opportunities (trainers) for qualified engineers and engineer assistants from the town of Atmah, which will help them improve their families’ economic situation.
We have a pre-equipped training center with the best equipment and tools. It includes two training rooms and four mechanical and electronic laboratories. The center is located in the town of Atmah, which includes the largest gathering of camps in Syria.
Due to the big number of people with disability, the center and the facilities are designed to address barriers to participation for people with disabilities; in addition to that, the people with disabilities will be consulted during the implementation of the project to facilitate the participation of people with disabilities.
The trainings are divided into three programs; each program contains a set of courses and
specializations according to the following:
Electricity and Electron Program:
1- Courses in Electron and Alternative Energy (125 hours).
2- Courses in Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (125 hours).
3- Courses in Household Electrical Wiring (60 hours).
Electricity and Mechanics Program:
1- Courses in Automatic Control (125 hours).
2- Courses in Pneumatic and Hydraulic Control (125 hours).
3- Courses in Automotive Electricity and Mechanics (125 hours).
4- Courses in Winding and Maintenance of Electric Motors (125 hours).
Computer and Mobile Program:
1- Courses in Mobile Maintenance (125 hours).
2- Courses in Computer Maintenance (125 hours).
3- Courses in Web Programming (125 hours).
The duration of each specialized program is four months, including the advertisement, registration, and exams process.
The training is launched in three stages, and each stage includes six different courses (20 trainees in each course) so that the total number of beneficiaries in each stage is 120 trainees within each phase, which lasts for four months.
At the end of the training, the successful trainee will receive a bag of equipment for the profession in which he has been trained, at the cost of 50 dollars, to encourage the trainee and facilitate his involvement in the labor market. The bag of equipment includes each profession's main tools as it was recommended by trainers such as ( Electric Soldering Iron tool, Tin Wires, TEST PENCIL, Duct Tape, pliers, Pliers Wire Cutter, CUTTER KNIFE, Screwdriver set, Plastic Toolbox).
A trainee who has completed a full training program will receive $75 at the end of the program as transportation compensation ($25 for each month).
The training and job opportunities that the project will provide to 380 beneficiaries will reduce the possibilities of migration or engaging in violence for these young people, overcome the economic obstacles that face them, and thus enhance society's resilience and stability.

4 - Needs

Atmeh did not have adequate basic educational facilities and vocational facilities. it lost thousands of young competencies.Afaq conducted a study in Atmeh and its camps to identify training needs and develop the project. The study included 3 main tools:1. Group discussions. 2. Interviews. 3. Field visits.The team gave a presentation about the Afaq Institute in Atmeh, asking for more ideas to improve it.Key findings- There are very few vocational training centers in and around Atma offering specialized technical training- Providing shorter intensive specialized pieces of training- Making use of the available equipment to focus on specialized pieces of training- Providing advanced computer training- There are many qualified trainers in the mechanical, electrical and electronic fields but many of them work in other jobs and sometimes below their level of education due to the lack of relevant training centers or appropriate opportunitiesProject justification- Reducing unemployment- High rates of population growth in the region, population inflation and IDPs in large numbers- Low levels of education and the absence of an industrial project- The available resources are wasted considering the inability to invest the human resources of the youth- Youth joining the military factions which reflects on civil society- The deviation of youth to crimes and thefts to meet their needs in absence of available alternatives
