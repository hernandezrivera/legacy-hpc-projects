Project Summary

Due to the current global pandemic of COVID-19 Like many other countries globally the health system in Pakistan is overwhelmed due to required response to COVID-19. Therefore, there is an urgent need to support and compliment government efforts for coping with the current pandemic alongside with continuity of essential health services including MNCH, RH, EPI and Non-Communicable diseases. Project aims to reduce avoidable morbidity and mortality as a direct or indirect consequence of COVID-19 outbreak.
The proposed project is designed according to the health needs of local population of district Shangla and health cluster strategic objectives, to avoid preventable morbidity and mortality among the affected population by ensuring access of locals and other vulnerable groups to essential life-saving Health Care services, infection prevention and control (IPC), alongside with continuity of essential health services including MNCH, RH, EPI and Non-Communicable diseases, provision of life-saving medicines and other medical supplies. The propose project will support communities through IPC, Primary Health care and MNCH and other essentials health services in selected villages in the catchments of health facilities (THQs, RHCs, BHUs and CDs) in the tehsil of Besham and Alpurai.

Major interventions/activities in the proposed Project are:
• Revitalization and operationalization of health systems in target areas through provision of facility based and mobile health services, necessary equipment and medicines.
• Continuation and strengthening of Primary Health Care services in selected villages of vulnerable population in areas of Tehsils Besham and Alpurai of district Shangla.
• Establish communicable disease surveillance and response mechanism to mitigate morbidity and mortality among affected population through infection prevention control.
• Provide life-saving critical MNCH and reproductive health services through treatment of children under (boys and girls), PLW and referral mechanism will be established for high risk cases.
• Provide mental health and psycho-social support to the affected vulnerable groups like women, children, chronically ill and disabled.
• Provide essential life-saving medicines and supplies necessary for effective service delivery to affected population
• Conduct Health and Hygiene sessions to raise Health and Hygiene awareness among the targeted Population to counter COVID-19 in communities.
• Provision PPEs to health staff and communities include hygiene kits

1- Project Activities

Activity 1.1.1 Training and Capacity Building of government and private health facilities staff (doctors, paramedical, vaccinator, and disease surveillance officer etc) to continue providing essential health services including provision of IPC, MNCH, immunization, antenatal, postnatal care and safe delivery services. Activity 1.1.2 Support outreach activities through provision of medicines, medical supplies, maternal life-saving supplies, modern contraceptives, and availability of basic maternal health equipment. Activity 1.1.3 Provision of appropriate Personal Protective Equipment for government, community and Public Health Facilities including Doctors, paramedics, LHW, CMW, FWW, FWAs, LHV, Dispensers, WMO/MOs and vaccinators. Activity 1.1.4 Ensure continuity and effectively operationalized mobile units’ services in district Shangla KP to strengthen CEmOC referral and onsite SRH, including EmOC and maternal health care services. Activity 1.1.5 Engagement of community health workers to raise awareness for reduction of virus transmission and panic, improve care seeking for fever and respiratory symptoms, MNCH and immunization services and referralActivity 1.1.6 Strengthening of Community and Facility based MNCH & Immunization services and establishment of telemedicine for ANC, PNC, newborn and childcare.Activity 1.1.7 Strengthening of Sick newborn care services in the targeted district of Shangla.Activity 1.1.8 Implementation and monitoring of guidelines and protocols for health care providers and patients elaborating social distancing during patient-patient and patients–provider interaction, service provision, hygiene, infection prevention and respiratory etiquette etc. Activity 1.1.9 Disinfection and Infection Prevention at facility, equipment and furniture, Health House of LHW, Delivery room of CMW, FWC including waiting area, BHU and RHC, RHS/FHC. Activity 1.1.10 Support to reproductive health facilities to ensure pregnant women have access to quality and timely emergency obstetric care and psychosocial support.Activity 1.1.11 Engagement of District Support Groups, Local Support Organizations (LSOs) and Community Based Organizations (CBOS) for community engagement and active participation. Activity 1.1.12 Reactivate Health committees at village level formed by Lady Health Workers for supporting LHWs to increase awareness and acceptance for RH services in the community. Activity 1.1.13 Provide support to dedicated reproductive health facilities and hospitals to ensure pregnant women with suspected, probable, or confirmed COVID-19, have access to woman-cantered, respectful skilled care, including obstetric and neonatal care, as well as mental health and psychosocial support with readiness to care and outreach for maternal and neonatal complications.
