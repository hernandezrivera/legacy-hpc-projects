Project Summary

The project aims to improve the life conditions of families displaced and host communities affected by the conflits in Cabo Delgado Province.
The intervention foresees an initial assessment on wash needs for the most needy displaced families and families hosting IDPs fleeing from conflits areas.
Higiene & sanitation training and sensitization will be privileged for IDPs in accommodation sites where needs are identified, in close partnership with local authorities and reference clusters.
For individual action, such as distribution of kits, the beneficiaires lists created by the government must be verified by AVSI WASH and Protection staff in order to guarantee trasparence and objectivity in the selection process, avoid duplications and overlapping during the distributions.
Selection will be made according to vulnerability criteria e nont by status, in order to guartantee the do no harm principle and do no create conflits amongs targeted communities.
AVSI will target 59.266 people, IDPs and host community among the districts of Balama (4.000), Cidade de Pemba (30.000) and Metuge (25,000).
In all interventions, AVSI will coordinate with the WASH Cluster at national and sub-national level, as well as with Municipality of Maputo and Pemba, and Ministério de Obras Públicas, Habitação e Recursos Hidricos and District Services of Planeamento e Infra-Estrutura and Direcção Provincial das Obras Públicas to ensure smooth coordination, facilitate prioritization and avoid overlapping of activities, and through national partner CSOs for implementation, where established partnerships already exist. AVSI will implement the activities under this project following the Mozambican WASH Cluster response strategy and standards for emergency interventions and will regularly report results to the WASH Cluster.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, targeting for individual assistence will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. Protection monitoring will be garuatee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and receive refresher trainings to stress the importance of the opinions and views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. This project will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities and will aim to upgrade living-conditions within a settlement and when possible through the construction of new shelters. AVSI will conduct the assessment in KOBO according to the agreed criteria, which will be reviewed and verified by the local authorities and the representatives of the communities. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account. Example of good practices: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/c891c32a-7c60-4aa9-bf6d-a8b48a84730a Activities of MEAL and surveys will be utilized not only for informing program strategies, but also to devise possible and feasible way forward with local communities as per cases described below: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/cded105d-be61-4480-b95c-117ea9fb4013

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. In WASH intervention AVSI prioritise disability inclusion as essential to: - achieve the basic human right to water and sanitation; - reach the most vulnerable - set the tone for inclusion in other community-based projects - achieve Sustainable Development Goal 6: Access to water and sanitation for all. AVSI focus on assuring disability inclusion in the proposed WASH intervention form the identification and selection process up to the adaption of the WASH facilities for persons with disabilities (latrines, handwshing points).; up to sensitization messages who will always focused on inclusitivity.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy effciency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal. In addition of that, it will be prioritazed the use of local materials for construction of Wash facilities in order to adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. Local procurement of materials will be a positive contribution to local economies. Moreover, with a strong community engagement and participation; on rehabilitation activities, specific attention regarding the site selection and sourcing of materials, favouring the use of local resources. Kits will be distributed in no plastic bag and in general use of plastic will be limited in the implementation of the activities as well as in the operation. For this, AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
