2. Project activities

.1 Conduct hydro-geological survey, drill and equip two borehole to serve 5620 persons in Abyei Town and also construct a 12000 cubic meters water pan, to serve 5460 persons in matukbet , which has low ground water potential. Hydro-geology survey will be guided by our expert Gedo potential ground water working team and recommendations. The water pan will be mechanically constructed as recommended by the WASH cluster to ensure large volume, minimal evaporation and have a well fitted with an Afridev hand pump that is easy for women and children to use. These facilities will increase water, reduce congestion and queuing time, particularly for women and girls. Selection of water point will be done in consultation with men, women and girls to ensure it does not fuel conflict and that no protection threats exists. Sanitary survey will be conducted to identify public health risks and well apron raised above runoff for DRR considerations. 1.2 Capacity building of 10 committee members, who forms 2 WASH committees to be involved in operations, maintenance and management of all the water facilities that will be constructed. Each committee will consist of at least five persons, and will be reflective of women and men, where at least two members will be women. A Do No Harm approach will be used to ensure committees are selected with the participation of representatives of community members, particularly women that traditionally have a big role in ensuring water is available at household level. The Rights for All team also consider geographical distribution and clan dimensions while constituting the committees to minimize conflict in water areas in most location following previous case study of the past.2. 1 Construct 40 latrines fitted with hand washing facilities for most vulnerable community and institutions. The latrines will have adequate privacy and complete with inside locks to ensure protection of women and girls. Women, men, girls and boys will be consulted on the design and siting of latrines. 10% of latrines will be reserved to consider elderly and disabled populations needs. The location of latrines will be discussed with women and girls. To ensure safety of women and girls using latrines, we will also distribute solar lamps to 300 beneficiaries and poorer households to light their latrines at night.2.2 Conduct solid waste management activities to all the target 11,111 people (2886 women, 2515 men, 2951 girls and 2724 boys). Community discussions in consultation with Local Administration the administrative area to formulate a mechanism for solid waste management in the settlements of urban villages. A 30 standard sanitation kit will be procured and will be issued Abeyei women's groups for use in of Abeyei waste and sanitations management policy.2.3 Conduct CLTS triggering process in host communities to create demand for good sanitation and minimize open defecation communities and returnees locations. Communities will be mobilized and made aware of the health risks associated with open defecation. The activity will be done two settlement Madol village and Abyeijokyom villages and we will continue monitoring to see the increase in number of new latrines constructed which attributable to the CLTS process. Separate meetings will be conducted for women and men to enhance participation and behavior change towards good sanitation practices. 11,111 people targeted (2775 women, 2775 men, 2775 girls and 2775 boys) people are expected to be reached through CLTS process.Activity 3.1 Improved safe hygiene practices among targeted community that lead to reduced Acute Watery Diarrhea incidences through enhanced hygiene promotion, training and mobilizing communities to adopt practices that minimizes on diarrhea transmission. Communities will also be empowered to monitor and put in place AWD preparedness and response activities during risk periods .Activity 3.3 Procure and distribute more than 300 hygiene kits as recommended by the WASH
