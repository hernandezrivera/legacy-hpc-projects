Project Summary

UNICEF’s WASH humanitarian response will focus on the provision of and sustaining access to lifesaving gender responsive basic level water supply, adequate & equitable sanitation facilities and promoting personal and environmental hygiene for 5 million most vulnerable people affected by the massive wide scale armed conflicts, WASH related epidemics with focus on cholera response, and natural disasters, mainly floods and drought. The project geographic focus will be Khartoum, North, South, East, West and Central Darfur, South, West and North Kordofan, Blue Nile, White Nile, Sennar, Geziara, Kassala, Gedarif, Red Sea, River Nile and Northern States.
UNICEF’s WASH humanitarian response will focus on:
• Provision of and sustaining access to lifesaving basic level water supply for 4.5 million affected population.
• Provision of and sustaining access to adequate emergency sanitation facilities for 270,000 affected population.
• Promoting personal and environmental hygiene for 4.5 million affected population.
• Distribution of critical hygiene promotion supplies for 450,000 affected population
• Providing 75 schools and 75 health/nutrition institutions with adequate basic WASH facilities
• Building communities' resilience to WASH related recurrent shocks with emphasis on community empowerment of women, girls, young people and people with disability.
The following indicators will be used to monitor the progress:
• # people accessing a sufficient quantity of safe water for drinking and domestic need (target: 4.5 million)
• # people in humanitarian situations accessing appropriate sanitation facilities and living in environments free of open defecation (target: 270,000).
• # people reached with hand-washing behavior-change programmes (target: 4.5 million).
• # people reached with critical WASH supplies (target: 450,000).
• # health centers with access to basic WASH service (target 75).
• # schools with access to basic WASH service (target: 75)
Main Targeted Interventions:
Deliver coordinated/integrated gender-responsive lifesaving domestic WASH services for crises and epidemics affected and rural unserved population as follows:
Basic Water Supply:
-Water disinfection, operation and maintenance and water trucking support
-Rehabilitation and constructing of gender responsive basic water facilities
-Promotion of WASH services sustainability through Community management with focus on women/youth engagement.
Basic Sanitation and Hygiene:
-Construction/rehabilitation of gender responsive (separated, locked and nearby) emergency latrines.
-Desludging IDPs settlement/gathering sites communal and shared latrines.
-Conducting effective hygiene promotion interventions with focus on handwashing with soap and cholera prevention
-Distribution of hygiene promotion supplies, mainly soap, jerricans, hygiene kits and MHM supplies.
-Supporting solid waste management interventions.
WASH In Institutions:
-Construction/rehabilitation of gender responsive and disabled friendly WASH basic services for schools, Health/nutrition facilities and child friendly spaces.
-Conducting hygiene promotion interventions with focus on handwashing with soap, cholera prevention and MHM.
WASH Enabling Environment:
-Maintain and support active sectoral/intersectoral humanitarian coordination forums national and state levels
-Strengthening WASH IMS/monitoring systems in terms of HR capacity and input-process-output stages.
-Invest on WASH sector partners capacity building to overcome the staff high turn-over rates.
