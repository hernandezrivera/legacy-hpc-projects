Project Summary

(Women and Girls’ Safe Space) is focused on the location of Ar-Raqqa& Derbasyea.
Al-Derbasyea is a Syrian town on the Syrian-Turkish border opposite the Turkish town of
Senyurt. Administratively it is part of the Al-Hasakah Governorate. It is the administrative
center of a nahiyah consisting of 113 localities. The majority of the inhabitants of the
town are Kurds with a large Arab and a smaller Assyrian minority. It is connected by road
to Tell Beydar in the south. On 22 July 2012, YPG forces took control over the town, after
the security and political units withdrew from it, following an ultimatum issued by the
Kurds. The town has been under Kurdish control since then until now.
Its population is 22,284, of which 1,700 are IDPs. This region has recently witnessed
heavy fighting, culminating in the retreat of ISIS on 10 June 2015. The area still has gaps
in the needs of women and girls today.
The change of gender roles for Syrian women is challenging for all members of the Syrian
household. In a recent research study by CARE entitled: WOMEN, WORK & WAR, it was
illustrated that, “both women and men explained that it was challenging for everyone to
adapt to women’s new roles and responsibilities.”
When women were supported in their new roles, they felt empowered and proud. However,
failing to cope with the gender roles changes can have serious protection risks that
include different forms of risks like sexual exploitation, sexual harassment.
This project contributes to the enhancement of high quality protection services and
measures, and works on placing the suitable and needed services in place to prevent and
reduce risks of protection. Through this project, the activities are set to make up for the
gap in terms of psychological and social support in order to enhance strategies to
empower women.
A large segment of women and girls who currently reside in Derbasyea have been
displaced from Al Raqqa. White Hats will also work on reaching out to community leaders
and representatives to coordinate to target priority to the most acute needs.
In general, project activities will provide psychological support to women and girls who have been subjected to sexual violence, and to members of vulnerable and traumatized groups – the most affected by gender-based violence and sexual violence. They also empower women by providing them with the knowledge and skills that enable them to know their rights identified by the international agreements and conventions.
Geographic Areas: White Hats will open a new center and mobile team in Tabqqa city and the Tabqqa countryside, and we will undertake these activities in White Hats centers in Suluk, Till Abead and Ain Essa Center & Camp Ain Essa, Therefore, this project will cover the 4 cities mentioned above located in Raqqa province.

Implementation of activities

Activity 1 – Awareness raising activities via advocacy campaignsAdvocacy campaigns and provision of psycho-social, social support, as well as providing consultations through the following: (a) Raising awareness about the rights of women and female children/youth through a mobile team that provides social support in all selected cities. (b) Producing videos that contribute to the dissemination of the concept of women's rights and highlight the causes of gender-based violence and its consequences within society. (c) Delivering protection awareness sessions designed for the females and focused on domestic violence, rape, early marriage and discrimination. Activity 2 – GBV Capacity building eight training activities will be conducted across project locations. These training include standards for the prevention of gender-based violence, best practices for documenting cases of GBV The training is delivered by a team consists of a psycho-social and social support trainer, and a training facilitator from White Hats teams.Indicator 1.1.1 # of humanitarian actors trained on PSS Activity 1.1.1 Provide PSS trainingfor PSS workers in Derbasyea center for 2 days from 9:00 am until 5:00 pm on first weekin September Indicator 1.1.2 Women and girls engaging in structured and sustainablewomen and girls’ protection programs, including PSS Activity 1.1.2 Provide PSS sessions(group and individual) to vulnerable girl and women in Derbasyea Indicator 1.2.1 # ofbeneficiaries reached with women and girls’ empowerment via trainings and protectionprevention activities via PSS sessions Activity 1.2.1 Enhance strategies to empowerwomen and girls and prevent GBV, with a particular focus on adolescent girls
