Project Summary

The project seeks to strengthen the capacity of targeted communities to reduce the risks of child trafficking in Manicaland, Matebeleland North, Matebeland South, Masvingo, Mashonaland Central and Mashonaland East. Due to the vulnerabilities and exposure children are subjected to in the context of crisis/emergencies, there is a risk of children falling victims to both internal and external trafficking.The targeted districts have been affected by different crisis, including cyclone Idai and prolonged drought. In Manicaland province, there are reported cases of school dropouts in most schools and these out of school children are at a high risk of being lured by traffickers to join the great trek across borders to South Africa. Since 2018 reported cases of school dropouts in Manicaland Province stood at 9,559 out of 689,225 students. Government authorithies constantly mention that Manicaland has recorded the highest school dropout rate due to adolescent delinquency, debts, exclusion, illness, child marriages and pregnancies among others. Among the districts that recorded the highest drop-outs in Manicaland are Chipinge, which recorded the highest, followed by Mutare, Buhera and Chimanimani. Since Chimanimani and Chipinge districts were the most affected by Cyclone Idai there could already be a spike in figures. Since the two districts are among the highest migrant sending areas in Zimbabwe, the impact could translate into forced migration, especially to South Africa. Some girls, owing to poverty and lack of opportunities, will resort to early marriages and end up in exploitative situations, particularly in bordering towns like Kariba, Binga, Beitbridge, Mbire and Mudzi. According to UNICEF (2017), 32% of girls in Zimbabwe are married before the age of 18 and 4% are married before their 15th birthday. The key drivers of child marriages have been noted to include poverty, low level of education, religion and traditional customs among others. It's commonly known hat crisis situations worsens the plight of children particularly the girl child. The project will focus on strengthening the existing child protection structures through capacity building workshops on trafficking in children related issues, awareness raising and direct assistance to children victims of trafficking.
