The project will provide life-saving interventions for the vulnerable IDP, returnee, and resident households in South Darfur state to prevent further losses of their productive assets and enhance their capacity to access, through crop and livestock-based livelihood interventions, sufficient nutritious food and to diversify their food basket and improve their income. Overall, the project will target 20,600 food insecure 10,400 HH in Bull bull,Katella , 10,200HH locality IDP , returnees and host community. The project aims to restore and improve the productive capacity of the targeted farmers to reduce the severity of the hunger gap and enable them to become self-reliant down into the harvest period. This will eventually support in halting the deterioration of the food and nutrition situation and preventing relapse of Malnutrition. to children and pregnant and lactating women,. AOSCD intended beneficiaries of the project.
