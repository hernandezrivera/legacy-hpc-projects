Project Summary

The proposed intervention will be carried out in partnership with National Society for Rehabilitation (NSR) through running 4 mobile teams, and covering four geographical locations: (Gaza, Der El Balah, Khan Younes and Rafah). The proposed intervention targets persons with disabilities (PwDs) as the main beneficiary group which has been identified as a vulnerable group in need.
The intervention includes the following activities:
- Provision of hygiene kits for 1500 vulnerable PwDs (400 Men, 250 Women, 500 Boys and 350 Girls) and their families (about 7500 in total).
- Provision of home-based rehabilitation services including physiotherapy for 500 PwDs (133 Men, 84 Women, 167 Boys and 116 Girls).
- Distribution of educational materials about health safety measures, map of services and home care sessions for 1500 persons.
- Provision of 500 different assistive devices for people with physical disability (133 Men, 84 Women, 167 Boys and 116 Girls).
- Organizing individual counseling services for 500 PwDs through hotline.
- Mobile team members will be provided with PPE and Hygiene kits to ensure their and the beneficiaries’ safety.
The proposed intervention is expected to last for a duration of 10 months and to benefit a total of 7500 direct beneficiaries.

1 - Needs

People with disabilities in Gaza experience multiple institutional, attitudinal, environmental, andinformational barriers in exercising their rights. The protracted conflict and resulting damage toinfrastructure and the economy, severe pressure on services, coupled with negative cultural attitudestowards disability in the Occupied Palestinian Territories (OPT), mean people with disabilities face multiple and severe barriers to access to public services, employment and social inclusion (UNICEF, 2017). The increased barriers (i.e. recently the emergency due to spread of COVID-19) in accessing services has reportedly led to disabled people’s medical conditions worsening in recent years. As is the case world-wide, the COVID-19 emergency has been predominant in the Palestinian Territories in recent months, starting on March 5th in the West Bank with the discovery of first cases, albeit hitting Gaza Strip a bit later, particularly during the last week of August with the discovery of first cases from the community. Prior to August, all COVID-19 cases registered in Gaza Strip had been limited to people coming back to Gaza through the Erez checkpoint or the Rafah crossing. As of October10th, there has been 3,897confirmed cases, 2,303 recovered and 24 deaths. This emergency has added a huge burden to an already deteriorating health care system. Due to the imposed lockdown, thousands of people have lost their jobs and are becoming newly poor. Health and other facilities have closed down, thus making it difficult for people to access essential care and service. Persons with disabilities remain one of the main groups that suffer poor access to services. Needs assessment for this project comes within needs assessment carried out for related sectors as prepared by applying organizations. The Health Cluster Humanitarian Needs Overview 2021 for Gaza has identified persons with disabilities as a vulnerable group of people in need stating that Persons with disabilities (PWDs) are in need of health care intervention. This includes cases with potential deprivation from medical services due to repeated emergency situation. It has identified their specific needs as: Multidisciplinary rehabilitation programs, data keeping system interconnected with all the partners and assistive devices.
