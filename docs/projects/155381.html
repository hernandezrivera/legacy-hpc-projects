Project Summary

The main aim of this intervention is to reduce or counter the negative effects of drought during the year 2019. The project will target Institutions (Schools and Health facilities) and will ensure that at least 103,554 children (boys and girls), men & women are reached with access to sufficient and quality water; sanitation, health and hygiene messages and NFIs. This will be achieved through the drilling of new water points, rehabilitating water points, Installation of solar water reticulation systems, construction of headworks, Distribution of NFI kits to Schools and Health facilities, Distribution of NFI kits to Vulnerable people in the community, Strengthening and training of Environmental Health Technicians (EHTs), Village Health Workers (VHWs), Community Health Workers (CHWs), Care givers, School Health Clubs (SHCs) and School Health Masters (SHMs) on Hygiene promotion, Dissemination of critical hygiene messages and Improve capacity for Operations and Maintenance in 5 Districts (Chipinge, Binga, Gokwe South , Gokwe North and Uzumba Maramba Pfungwe). Nutrition gardens will be set up around these solar powered water reticulation systems and that will enable schools and Health facilities to provide relish for their supplementary feeding programs. These sustainable supplementary feeding programs will ensure continued high enrollment of pupils every year and thus improve the level of education in the community as a whole. Because targeted schools and health facilities are in drought affected districts, the project will work closely with the Civil Protection Unit structures at district level to ensure resilience construction and rehabilitation works. To ensure sustainability of the infrastructure provided, the project will also embark on capacity building training's for builders, pump minders and water point committees.

Needs

1. To restore and improve the availability of sufficient and quality water in areas that have been greatly affected by the drought. 2. To strengthen the link between WASH, health, livelihoods and resilience by harnessing water from high yielding perennial water. 3. To distribute hygiene enabling NFIs and hygiene education pertaining to safe and hygienic child care and feeding practices to prevent occurrence of diarrhoeal & other water related diseases. 4. Provide access to critical WASH related Non-food items (NFIs), with a focus on the most vulnerable families in the targeted areas
