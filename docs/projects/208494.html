Project Summary

The lingering crisis in the BAY States, coupled with destruction of water and sanitation facilities, vandalization and theft , climate change, have increased the gaps existing already and have increased the vulnerability of the people in the region. These challenges and population increase have also increased thereby throwing people deeper into vulnerability.Inadequate supply of safe and clean water, poor sanitation and hygiene condition of the populace have further compounded their problems increasing chances of disease outbreak including acute watery diseases like cholera. If these are not urgently address will lead to death especially of children under 5.
Caritas Nigeria will provide provide safe and clean water , improved sanitation and deliver comprehensive Hygiene promotion to the proposed locations i the BAY States. This is based on needs identified by our project team over the past 10 years in the BAY States from our needs assessment.
Caritas Nigeria have identified wide gaps in these thematic areas which are corroborated by reports from the sectors' dash boards.
Our provision of water is Eco-friendly as we will be upgrading/converting high yielding hand pumps to solar powered boreholes with full reticulations. These facilities will be designed with rams and rails at water fetching points for inclusivity of people with disabilities. The reticulation of water close to the beneficiaries is meant to reduce vulnerability of girls and women who are often exposed in their quest to search for water. We will also providing dignified sanitation facilities by construction and rehabilitation of VIP latrines and regular de-sludging for safe use by the public. Young girls and women within reproductive ages will be trained on production of dignity kits (re-usable sanitary kits) and will be given starter packs. Others include regular hygiene promotion in the IDP camps and Host communities, so that the chain of diseases transmission will be broken.

1 - Needs

The needs of the affected people in the project location include lack of adequate water supply, sanition and hygiene promotion that has affected the quality of life in their settlements.

2 - Activities

1. Supply of safe and clean water: 87,000 individuals. Caritas Nigeria will upgrade high yielding hand pumps to solar boreholes and rehabilitate broken ones to restore full functionalities to meet water needs. we will convert 10 hand pumps and rehabilitate 4 broken solar boreholes and 14 broken down hand pumps and be equipped with toolkit. 2. Sanitation and hygiene: 100,000 individuals: VIP latrines will be constructed and or rehabilitated and de-sludge regularly in the communities. WASH NFIs will be distributed such as hygiene kits and Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM). Young school girls and women of reproductive ages will be trained on how to produce re-usable sanitary kits and will be given the starter kits. This will be followed by regular hygiene promotion its 5 domains.This include host communities, IDP camps and in schools.

Caritas Nigeria will set help desk in all project location for complaint and feedback. Trained community mobilizers will be stationed in each location to collect, document and respond to them and also escalate where necessary.The data collected will be used with utmost confidentiality. The ones that can addressed at community level by our desk officers based in the communities will do so immediately while the ones that need to be escalated will be done so quickly and feedback will be provided to the affected persons.
