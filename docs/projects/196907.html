Project Summary

This project aims to reduce the suffering and the burden of vector borne disease in Internal Displaced Population and host communities of Cabo Delgado.
Hygiene promotion campaigns and vector control activities will be conducted to prevent malaria cases as well as diarrheal diseases in the communities targeted.
The reinforcement of primary health care will be conducted through vector borne diseases training and Integrated Management of Childhood Illness to health technicians.
The support of the APE (front line community health workers) will be given through refresher trainings on management of malaria, diarrheal and respiratory infections as well as Integrated Management of Childhood Illness. Supervisions and support of the supply chain will be added.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project aims to reduce the mortality and the suffering of the Internal Displaced Population (IDP) and host communities affected by the conflict in Cabo Delgado due to Vector Borne Diseases such as malaria and diarrheal diseases. IDPs and host communities are severely affected by vector borne diseases due to the current conditions of overpopulated households, undernutrition, compromised hygiene and sanitation infrastructure and practices. An integrated and comprehensive project to tackle vector borne diseases from the different areas of vector control, prevention, case management and reporting is needed to reduce the burden of these diseases which are the main public health problems currently happening in Cabo Delgado.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities that would be included in this project are: 1. Hygiene promotion and larval source management campaigns across 6 districts (Pemba, Metuge, Ancuabe, Chiure, Montepuez and Mueda). This activity would include BCC campaign through door-to-door visits and community gatherings. Messages on water treatment and storage, diarrheal diseases and cholera prevention, prevention of malaria and other vector borne diseases, elimination of vector breeding sites (Anopheles, Aedes, Culex), solid waste management, promotion of handwashing. Construction of handwashing stations.2. Training of Agentes Polivalentes Elementares (APEs) and APE Supervisors (health workers) on Vector Borne Diseases and Integrated Management of Childhood Illness across the districts of Pemba, Metuge, Mecufi, Ancuabe, Chiure, Namuno, Balama, Montepuez, Mueda, Meluco, Nangade, Ibo and Palma.Training of health professionals of health centers on diagnosis, treatment, management of cases, referral and reporting of cases of malaria and other vector borne diseases such as dengue, chikungunya, diarrheal diseases and of Integrated Management of Childhood Illness. 3. Support of APEs on the management of malaria cases, pneumonia and diarrheal diseases in 13 districts of Cabo Delgado: Pemba, Metuge, Mecufi, Ancuabe, Chiure, Namuno, Balama, Montepuez, Mueda, Meluco, Nangade, Ibo and Palma.Supporting the supervision and target refresher trainings on malaria management and increase the epidemic alert system of cholera and other vector borne diseases.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

An initial risk assessment will be conducted in order to identify the potential risk that the programme may encounter or cause. Follow-up assessments and meetings with representative beneficiaries will occur throughout the programme to understand if life and condition has changed in the population and if any kind of harm is being caused. MENTOR coordinators will be trained to monitor these risks and identify potential harms. The findings of the risk assessment will be used to design specific action plans to mitigate the risks identified. All MENTOR staff will be trained on PSEA and they will be committed to respect the Code of Conduct. Specifically, for the different activities the practical points that we will implement are:- Implementation of a green line available to denounce any kind of harm. - Implementation of suggestion boxes across the sites of implementation. - Ensuring that handwashing stations are being set up in safe spaces and do not cause harm to population.- Including programme beneficiaries in the planning and implementation phase.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

MENTOR will include women and elderly people within the disaggregation of the data collection indicators. This will ensure that specific groups are reached. MENTOR will include in the trainings of staff and short-term activists PSEA and points about elderly population and people living with disabilities. When implementing the activities, the activists and MENTOR staff will need to map and target people that may encounter difficulties in accessing assistance. Supervision of the activities will also focus on this matter. In all activities proposed, MENTOR will look for gender parity across all human resources. MENTOR will also work together with representatives of different groups of the communities including women groups and community leaders. These meetings will serve to communicate and collect feedback from the activities at the planning phase as well as in the evaluation phase in line with accountability of affected population.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People living with disabilities may have social and physical barriers to access assistance. In the different activities of these project, the community activists, APEs and health technicians, will be trained to map individuals with difficult access to assistance and will be encouraged to give attention to this. Supervisors will monitor the activities in order to include mapping of people living with disabilities. Due to the nature of the work of hygiene promotion, activists will go house-to-house to conduct the activity. People living with disabilities will be prior identified by community leaders and social mobilisers and with the right consent, the teams will visit their house and overcome any possible barrier to access to assistance.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

MENTOR choose as possible, to use reusable materials and materials with no or low impact on the environment damage.
