Needs

Over 20 villages have an approximate population of 12845 with pastoralism as their main livelihood. Approximately 10 villages in Diinsoor and Qansah Dhere remain uncovered by nutrition services since 2017. Its important to note that the nutrition situation has sustained above emergency threshold since 2013 and very critical in the last 4 seasons. (FSNAU,2018). Access to health services is low, including life saving emergency medical assistance. Public health services are entirely delivered by external actors with limited government oversight. There is only one Health facility in the area, nearDiinsoor and Qansah.Dhere town with access to this facility remaining a challenge due to distance (OCHA, 2018; Nutrition cluster, 2018). Coverage for immunization is way below 80% recommended by Sphere. Integrated food security analysis shows that the food security situation in these areas is critical. This state remains fragile considering the prevailing drought and death of animals. It is anticipated that the situation will deteriorates further if the Gu rains fail. Lack of nutrition humanitarian activities in the area since 2013, is likely to contribute to deterioration considering otherfactors. SDIO therefore intends to intervene with a project to improve the nutritional status of women and children (Boys and girls) in the area, with a multi sectarian approach and addressing most aspects of both immediate and underlying causes of malnutrition. Interventions will focus identification and treatment of malnutrition and resilience activities like Promotion on optimal IYCF practices, micro nutrient supplementation and health and hygiene education. Active case finding, through CHWs, regular and mass screening, will be employed to improve identification of cases.The organization intends to greatly improve community participation through recruitment and involvement of the community in implementation activities.
