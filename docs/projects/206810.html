1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project’s overall objective is to contribute to the provision of integrated services to address Gender Based Violence (GVB) and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) needs of the most vulnerable people, both Internally Displaced People (IDPs) and host communities (HC) living in areas affected by the humanitarian crisis in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique. Activities will be implemented in the districts of Metuge (sites of Naminawe, Ntocota and Ngalane), Chiure (Maningane, Megaruma, Meculane and Marrupa), Montepuez (Massingiri, Nacaca and Ntele) and Pemba city (neighborhoods of Cariaco and Josina Machel). Other sites within the same districts might be considered based on arising needs, specific cases (eg. high risk cases), security, proximity of GBV and MHPSS related services and logistics capacity. Outcome: Referral pathway for GBV survivors strengthened and case management improved Output: 1. Strengthening quality life-saving services to address inclusive protection immediate risks and needs (including GBV) of the most vulnerable populations in priority areas. 2. Prevent protection risks (including GBV) of most affected and vulnerable populations in priority areas through enhanced preparedness and resilience capacity. 3. Strengthen the protection environment and enhance the protection of affected populations – including related to GBV, and through collective and intersectoral action. 4. Improving access to community-based mental health care and psychosocial support (MHPSS) of most affected and vulnerable populations through integrated, community based MHPSS interventions.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

CUAMM designs its projects in close cooperation with local authorities, in order to ensure intervention endorsement and local actors’ active involvement. Project activities will be implemented through participatory approaches, ensuring community members and beneficiaries are actively involved in the development and implementation of project activities in order to ensure that their needs are effectively met. Prior to the implementation of any activities and regularly throughout the project, information meetings at community level and with government services will be promoted with the aim of ensuring support and accountability. CUAMM will promote the setting up of effective project community feedback and complaint mechanisms in coordination with the Protection Cluster to enable beneficiaries and community members to provide feedback in relation to CUAMM activities in their communities, in a manner that is safe, non-threatening and accessible. Community feedback and complaints might be collected at service delivery points through focus group discussions with beneficiaries and community members. Moreover, CUAMM will continue promoting the access to ‘Linha Verde’, as an effective inter-agency complaint, feedback and information hotline, including for sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA).

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project's activities encourage meaningful access to assistance thanks to the implementation of tailored integrated measures strenghtening both the demand side and the supply one. On the demand side CUAMM will identify the difficulties, challenges and barriers encountered by the different groups of population in accessing assistance. Togheter with the beneficiaries and the local authorities, common, accepted and feasible meaures will be identified and put in place. Moreover, community awareness activities will be implemented in collaboration with local CSOs and activists, to develop a better attitude of community members and leaders towards gender and disability and comprehensive knowledge of women and girls about available services at the nearest functional services. On the supply side, CUAMM, in collaboration with the local authorities and the cluster, will contribute to facilitate the referral and follow up system for GBV victims from communities to dedicated services. In collaboration with local CSOs and activists, CUAMM will produce accessible IEC materials and participate to inclusive awareness campaigns promoting the active participation of disadvantaged groups. i.e. women, girls, people with disabilities and elderly persons. Finally, considering the inequalities affecting women and elderly persons in participating to the activities, CUAMM will implement specific measures to facilitate their active involvment and engagement in the intervention.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

CUAMM analyses the barriers, constraints and difficulties faced by the different groups of population, impeding the equal access to humanitarian services and assistance. A particular attention will be paid in identifying the barriers encountered by PwDs. A protection map will be defined through the feedback collected at community level and referring to other partners/institution assessment so that identifying vulnerable groups and situations that might endanger the goals of the interventions and maintaining a focused approach, avoiding wasting resources and addressing the priority sites and issues. In the implementation of the activities, CUAMM will adopt and respect a human rights based and people centered approach, in order to meet the specific needs of PwDs: women, men, girls, boys and elderly.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed programme will limit its impact on the environment by ensuring appropriate sensitization on healthcare workers as well as community health workers on safe disposal of consumables, especially PPEs. In addition, the project will focus on sensitizing host and displaced communities on environmental and personal hygiene, sanitation, disease outbreaks prevention/control at the community level during BM. During the project implementation, CUAMM will encourage: - integration of activities, implying movements by vehicles, to limit the number of cars and motorbikes going to the same destination and, consequently, the consumption of fuel and petrol; - careful monitoring of fuel storage and management, to avoid leakage and waste and hazard for the surrounding environment; - discussion with local health authorities on how safely handling the products resulting from the drugs store dejunking regular exercise to avoid all wastage to be left in the field, contaminating water sources or cattle feeding spaces.
