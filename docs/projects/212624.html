Project Summary

The main needs in Aleppo remain, even a year on from the earthquake - With 6.8M Syrians have shelter needs and NFI needs have increased to 6.2M (HNO 2024 summary). Hyper-inflation, natural disasters and decreasing purchasing power means that many families are still living in tents, flimsy shelters, and partially destroyed buildings unable to afford to fix them or find something better. Medair needs assessments have shown that people are more likely to spend money on food to feed their families.
Technical assessments will define required minor rehabilitation for each site. Rehabilitation focuses on basic house repairs, protection, security, and WASH needs. Each apartment will be rehabilitated according to Sphere standards. Additional work will be completed to accommodate special needs of elderly or PLWMI. Based on technical assessments, rehab may include walls, doors, windows, roofs, electrical wiring and chimneys for winter fireplaces. This will be done using a vulnerability assessment criteria framework. The project will incorporate HLP components as per sector guidelines. For each apartment, Medair customizes interventions per beneficiary needs, tailoring up to 12% of 100 targeted households for members with PLWMI and the elderly. Medair will distribute winterization NFIs to IDPs and the severely affected host populations by targeting HHs in locations impacted by harsh weather conditions and in locations where Medair is already working in critical sectors.
Protection Risk Analysis:
Common mitigations: Engagement with communities and local authorities for planning, inclusive communication/awareness raising and reducing risk; Establish community complaints mechanism Risk: Increased community tension due to beneficiary identification process; variance in resource or information access; and/or differing aid packages Mitigation: Standardize package with guidelines for all partners; Ensure selection criteria is uniform among sector members and the government; Train staff Monitoring: Engagement records; feedback/complaints log Risk: Exclusion due to lack of civil status document/ID OR barriers to occupancy due to lack of documentation of property/occupancy rights Mitigation: Identify alternative identification; work with cluster, MOLAE to ensure alternative documents are accepted; design, support local resolution mechanisms to restore rights; work with protection partners for referrals to legal assistance. Monitoring: Case tracking for beneficiaries with alternative documentation; referral records.
PSEA:
Risk: Exploitation by Medair staff, distribution actors. Mitigation: Ensure partners/vendors read Code of Conduct; job descriptions will include PSEA responsibilities; communicate code of conduct to affected population; clear communication with communities to prevent PSEA and make feedback mechanisms known; principled response to any disclosed PSEA, abuse, or violence. Feedback and reporting logs and actions taken on reports of abuse.
Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures:
RISK: Delays in planned procurement and/or importation. MITIGATION: A procurement plan will be completed at start of project cycle to ensure sufficient lead-time for normal operations. Procurement is planned to maximise use of reliable in-country suppliers, transfer of responsibility for import issues to suppliers as they are more able to manage the process. RISK: Fluctuations in exchange rates, differences between black market and CBS rates and inflation impact suppliers, in turn affecting procurement and budget management. MITIGATION: Monitor exchange rates and inflation, monitor % of funds that need to be in SYP, use rapid procurement processes. Monthly budget and inflation monitoring as well as use of forecasting spending. Appropriate clauses in supplier contracts, for mutual balance, but with clarity that Medair follows Central Bank of Syria exchange rates only and on terms of potential compensations for black market rate inflation.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Medair will provide shelter kits to 165 vulnerable HHs to improve climatic protection and privacy while providing more digniﬁed living conditions, benefiting 825 people in Khan Tuman, Aleppo Governorate. The guidelines of the Shelter Sector will be followed, which could include items that support household safety/security (doors, door frames, locking mechanisms), protection from the elements (window frames, glass, plywood, plastic sheeting), and access to basic services (water/sanitation related plumbing, piping, fixtures, and fittings). All proposed shelter activities will take place in either Aleppo and Deir-ez-Zor Governorates where Medair is also conducting multi-sector humanitarian interventions. The most vulnerable HHs will be prioritized. Brief summary:- 20,500 people benefitting from winter NFI distribution (8,000 Khasham & 12,500 Al-Mayadin, Deir-ez-Zor) - 825 people benefiting from emergency shelter kits (Khan Tuman, Aleppo) - 2,025 people benefiting from minor repairs to damaged housing (Aleppo city, Aleppo) - 35 people with specific needs benefiting from minor repairs to damaged housing (Aleppo city, Aleppo) - 500 people benefiting from rehabilitation of damaged housing (Deir-ez-Zor city, Deir-ez-Zor) - 15 people with specific needs benefiting from rehabilitation of damaged housing (Deir-ez-Zor city, Deir-ez-Zor) - 1400 people benefiting from the rehabilitation of common areas (800 Deir-ez-Zor city, Deir-ez-Zor & 600 Aleppo city, Aleppo) At this stage in our programming, Medair is not planning to use CVA for this Shelter/NFI. Medair have started using CVA in health within the past year and also conducting MPCA distributions and plan on becoming confident and good at this before moving into other sectors. Plus, in some locations where Medair operates, Deir-ez-Zor, for example, the state of the markets means that at this time CVA is not as viable as kits. Winter NFI kit items: Adult: Female Sweater x1, Female Underwear x1, Female Thermal underwear x2, Male Sweater x1, Male Thermal underwear x2 Women Winter Jacket x1, Men Winter Jacket x1 Infant 0-2yrs: Overall x1, Baby hat x1 Children 3-5yrs: Sweater x1, Socks x1, Hat x1 Children 6-9yrs: Sweater x1, Socks x1, Hat x1 Children 10-13yrs: Sweater x1, Socks x1, Hat x1 Thermal blanket x3 Rubber boots: For adult x2, Children 6 - 9 yrs x1, Children 10 - 13 yrs x1
