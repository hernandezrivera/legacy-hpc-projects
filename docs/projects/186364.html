Project Summary

Please provide a short summary of the intervention, describing its purpose and target (4000 words)/ Veuillez fournir un bref résumé de l'intervention, décrivant son objectif et sa cible
The INGOs forum in Cameroon (CHINGO) was established in June 2019 with the objective to enhance collaboration and coordination between INGOs and between INGOs and external stakeholders (line ministries, UN agencies, donors, civil society organizations) engaged in the humanitarian response to populations affected by the three on-going crises in Cameroon.
Under the strategic guidance of its Steering Committee and the leadership of its coordinator, the INGO forum aims at enhancing information-sharing between INGOs, notably to support coordination of operations and build common positions, improving representation of INGOs and joint advocacy and contributing to the overall humanitarian coordination architecture. The project includes various services to INGOs, including trainings for INGOs and partners’ staff, sharing of experience and lessons learnt, and facilitation and support on issues of common interest. CHINGO also engages with external stakeholders and provides a collective voice for INGOs as well as an entry point for requests to INGOs. Advocacy and communication activities aim to support better understanding of the assistance provided by INGOs in Cameroon, identify and propose solutions to challenges faced by INGOs in humanitarian operations and increase the visibility of the crises in Cameroon with the overall objective to better respond to humanitarian needs of populations affected by displacement.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Several impediments undermine or limit INGO action in Cameroon: unique access challenges resulting from a combination of insecurity caused by different armed groups and physical access constraints, administrative hurdles at central and field levels, limited visibility of Cameroon crises and insufficient funding, challenging coordination with three different coordination architectures in the country and limited human resources available within INGOs to allocate sufficient time to coordination issues. The INGO forum aims at collectively tackling these challenges. CHINGO’s work is critical for INGOs as it supports a coordinated and efficient INGO response to humanitarian needs as well as consistent representation and joint messages to be shared through the overall coordination architecture. CHINGO also contributes to enhance capacities of INGOs and their partners, to ensure that humanitarian principles are respected in the delivery of assistance and humanitarian space is being preserved in this challenging context. Legally and administratively hosted by the Norwegian Refugee Council, CHINGO provides some core services to its 20 members and 3 observers, in terms of information-sharing (effective and transparent sharing of information relevant to its members, coordination between INGOs (sharing of lessons learnt, best practices, identifying common interests and priorities) and with external stakeholders, representation of the interest of its members in external coordination mechanisms with key stakeholders including donors, UN, government, Cameroonian Civil Society Organizations to ensure INGO participation in critical decision-making platforms, evidence-based joint advocacy and partnerships and capacity development. This project is expected to contribute to achieving the following outcomes:• CHINGO members collectively advocate on key issues of common interest, more effectively engage in response coordination and leadership, and help ensure humanitarian operational space is maintained, to address the needs of the most vulnerable people affected by the various crises in Cameroon.• CHINGO gains the acceptance of donors, authorities, civil society and the people of Cameroon, to improve their access to humanitarian assistance, programme continuity and unimpeded service delivery. To achieve these outcomes, the project will implement the following activities: Organize CHINGO internal meetings: Country Directors monthly plenary meetings to define common positions on key topics, Steering Committee quarterly meetings to provide the strategic direction of CHINGO, Monthly Working Groups meetings (on advocacy, administrative/human resources, quality programming, advocacy), INGOs coordination at regional/field level.  Participate actively to the meetings of the Humanitarian Country Team, the Inter-sector, COVID-19 Task Force and any additional external meetings relevant to the mandate of CHINGO. Systematize the distribution of all information collected, including all meeting minutes and documents from all stakeholders Hold periodic meetings with representatives of national authorities to ensure mutual understanding of the issues of humanitarian assistance Organize training sessions on humanitarian principles and humanitarian access negotiations for CHINGO members and their partners. Develop factsheets and position papers on humanitarian context of Cameroon and situation/perspective of INGOs Hold advocacy initiatives (roundtables, press statements, position papers) and develop communication tools about the actions of CHINGO members Organize roundtable meetings with target stakeholders (ministries, donors, diplomatic community)
