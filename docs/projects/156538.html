Project Summary

The facility is currently being operated by HIHFAD and will continue to provide people in need of healthcare assistance within its scope area with primary, secondary and tertiary healthcare services. The facility is planned to keep operating 24/7 for the whole year of 2019. The services planned to be provided through the facility entail:
• Outpatient general, orthopedics, urological and ENT related surgical counselling.
• General, orthopedics, urological and ENT related surgeries.
• Outpatient cardiology and pulmonary consultations.
• Inpatient cardiology and pulmonary hospitalization services.
• Intensive care units for surgery and cardiology related cases.
• Dialysis unit.
• Basic trauma care.
• Laboratory services.
• Provision of essential medicines.
• Imaging services (Plane X-ray, CT scan and ultrasound imaging)
• Ambulance referral services.

Needs

Kafr Nobol is a sub district located far most south to Idleb governorate and host some 115,370 population according to the latest data communicated by the health cluster/ Gaziantep hub. According to the HNO data for Syria 2018, the sub district suffers a severity of 4/6 in terms of healthcare and suffers profound shortages not only in terms of primary but also the secondary and tertiary healthcare. Currently, HIHFAD hospital in Kafr Nobol city is currently the only facility in that area that address the people in need of specialized primary, secondary and tertiary healthcare services related to surgical conditions as well as those related to specialized internal medicine including the management of renal failure, cardiac and pulmonary conditions. Given that HIHFAD will endeavor to continue operating the hospital in order to cope with the relevant healthcare needs of the population not only within Kafr Nobol sub district but also those in its adjacent ones.

Implementation of activities

• Provide essential primary and secondary health care services, including trauma care, EmONC and referral to specialist services: the healthcare services will be provided on daily basis and round the hour. The primary healthcare services will be provided in line with the essential primary healthcare package adopted by the health cluster- Gaziantep hub. In addition, the service package entails the provision of specialized healthcare services that are related to the surgical and internal medicine including and not limited to general surgery, orthopaedics, ENT, cardiology and pulmonology. As for the secondary healthcare services, they entail the inpatient hospitalization services for the surgical and internal medicine related cases that are in need. The means of tertiary healthcare; like mechanical ventilators and dialysis machines; are made available for the purpose of providing compressive healthcare services for the patients in need. In addition, the facility is equipped with an ambulance for transporting the referral cases in need of further medical attention to the appropriate facility.• Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies: HIHFAD will ensure that the facility consistently has adequate quantities of the essential medicines and medical supplies that are required for the implementation of the planned activities, where that will happen through the funding channels HIHFAD has established. Also, the provision of such items will be promoted through the coordination with WHO and since they provide those items through their supply lines.• Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services: HIHFAD consistently assesses the gaps in terms of medical technical capacities of the staff and conduct the required trainings accordingly.• Increase access to health services by establishing functional health facilities and mobile medical units and supporting referral: HIHFAD will provide the facility with a well-equipped ambulance to ensure timely transportation of any referral case that require further medical intervention to the appropriate nearby or remote facility. The ambulance will be operating 24/7 for the whole period of the project and will support the referral network in the scope area of the facility as needed.

Protection Risk Analysis

The list of the key protection risks includes and not limited to the followings:(Abbreviations: LCs: Local councils/ HDs: Health directorates/ L: Low/ M: Medium/H: High)1. Beneficiaries are affected by conflict related incidences while seeking healthcare at the Facility.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Facility are based in areas included in the de-escalation zones agreement at adequate distances from gatherings of armed groups. Coordinate with the local authorities (Civilian LCs and HDs) to support the exclusion of the health facility from any possible clashes. Technical support by HIHFAD safety and security team. Training of staff on contingency plans. Ensure about the establishment of emergency exits within the facility.2. Patients are subject to harm due to low-quality healthcare services.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: HIHFAD follows strict HR recruitment procedures in terms of the qualifications and expertise required for medical personal. Also, HIHFAD consistently evaluate any gap of the technical capacities for its medical staff. Feedback mechanisms to be made available for the beneficiaries to ensure their participation in identifying any gap in the staff technical capacities that need to be addressed. In the same context, HIHFAD consistently seeks to ensure the standardization of the services provided in terms of medical equipment, medical supplies and pharmaceuticals.3. While at Facility, Beneficiaries are abused, harassed, undergo compromised privacy or dignity.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Child safeguarding training provided for all staff. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. Ensure individuals of vulnerable groups (children, women, elderly and people with disabilities) are accompanied while at the Facility. Ensure adequate female staff representation. Application of privacy screens and separation of medical examination theatres. Establishment of breast-feeding corners. Separation of the bathrooms by sex. Label tags to be demonstrated within the Facility. Establishment of adequate waiting areas and ensure adequate lighting of the facility is in place.4. Confidentiality of the information provided by the Beneficiaries is compromised.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: staff of the Facility adapt HIHFAD code of conduct. Tags about confidentiality of patient records and information to be demonstrated within the Facility. Maintaining patient information through well secured and strictly monitored modalities inaccessible to public. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. 5. Communities are affected by medical wastes produced at the Facility.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Securing the medical wastes prior to disposal. Application of medical waste management pathways for the purpose of proper disposal of them.6. Staff/ patients and their companions acquire nosocomial infections while at the facility.• Likelihood: M/ Impact: M to H• Mitigation: programmed use of disinfection measures. Programmed microbiological screening of the Facility. Establishment of clean and well-ventilated waiting areas. Proper handling of medical wastes. Use of personal protective equipment when indicated. Children presented at the Facility to receive EPI vaccines are offered the service within the outpatient department to ensure minimum contact with hospitalized patients.
