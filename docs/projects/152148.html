Project Summary

LIVESTOCK DISTRIBUTION (RE-STOCKING) TO CRISIS AFFECTED POPULATION IN SOUTHERN ADAMAWA STATE
The recent crises that erupted in the southern part of Adamawa State as a result of farmer-herder & communal crises and perennial flooding had affected the major means of livelihood of the population of the area. Most of the livestock in the area which is one of the means of livelihood was either killed, washed away, missed and or stolen. The major local government councils that were affected include among others: Demsa, Guyuk, Lamurde, Mayo Belwa, Numan and Shelleng local governments.
The project will target women and youth headed households for the goat and bull support respectively. Each of the Women Headed household will receive one male and three females’ goats for breeding while the youth headed household will receive one bull for fattening. The project will target 200 female headed household in each of the 6 selected local governments total 1,200 household. While 100 Youth Headed households totally 600 will be target in the same locations.
Generally, women in the affected areas are used to breeding of small ruminants and poultry and sometimes farm vegetable gardens at their back yard, while men and youth managed cereal production for the rainy season and also keep large ruminants. Taking away these from them will make their lives miserable. Therefore, there is a need to continue and strengthen the support to sustainable the means of livelihoods of the most vulnerable population of the affected local governments. Supporting the affected households with an adequate package (goat, bulls, feeds and drugs) will decrease the food assistance dependency and restore the dignity of the beneficiaries. It will also complement the food assistance in a more sustainable and resilient manner and link pure life savings activities to recovery and development programme. It also provides additional sources of nutritious food (milk & meat) and is a good source of revenues for women, which contributes to improve the nutrition status of the beneficiaries’ households.
The project will also address the issues of protection mainstreaming, GBV, PSEA and AAP.

Needs

A serious food crisis has unfolded in the southern part of Adamawa State because of the multiple of crises currently going-on in the area. A recent, Inter-Agency Rapid Needs Assessment (IRNA) conducted in August, 2018 had revealed that crises such as farmer-herder, communal crisis and more recent the perennial flood had caused a massive displacement of people, destruction of agricultural activity, lack of access to farmland and loss of livelihoods. It has destroyed considerable numbers of livestock which is one of the means of livelihood and source of nutrition. This proposed project will support female and youth headed households in the selected LGAs and community to restore their lost animals. The support of livestock, feeds and drugs by CSCED will improve their food security while enhancing their nutrition, health and well-being status especially for children under 5 pregnant and nursing mothers. It will also restore the dignity of the affected population by protection mainstreaming, addressing GBV issues and putting into consideration AAP and PSEA in all the activities.This proposed project by CSCED will directly contribute to the HRP, 2019-21 FSS objective 2 – To strengthen resilience of crisis affected people by re-establishing, improving and diversifying key agriculture livelihood (including crop production, livestock, fisheries, forestry and natural resource management) (contributing to HRP Objective 2 and 3)

Activities

Project Activities:- Project planning meeting- Advocacy, Community mobilization and sensitization with the stakeholders on the project and get their buy-in. The purpose of the project, entitlements, gender-based violence, PSEA and a complaint mechanism will be addressed.- Formation of cooperate societies and strengthening existing societies- Beneficiaries Criteria Setting, Identification.- Beneficiaries Selection and Registration- Selection of Stock Distribution Committee- Capacity building and knowledge transfer (Training of Farmers Representative and Veterinary workers)- Compilation of Beneficiaries list and Submission to donor- Identification and renting of temporary warehouse for storage of inputs- Transport of Inputs to location- Pre-distribution meeting and logistic with communities.- Distribution of Livestock (goats, bulls, feeds and drugs) for restocking- Protection mainstreaming.- Gender consideration and mainstreaming, AAP, PSEA- Monitoring and evaluation: the activities of the project will monitor the processes from planning to conclusion. Especially attention will be given to the selection of the beneficiaries, procurement of the inputs (size, breed, health etc), distribution (ensure the beneficiaries receive the planned quantity and quality) etc.- Project documentation and Reporting to donor(s).
