Project Summary

• awareness on preventing transmission of COVID-19, for communities
• training for activist in 6 health centers of project area
• support and training in Covid19 the health committees in the communities in the project area
• Covid19 Communication campaigns on Community Radio Stations in Buzi and Chivababa districts; local lenguaje and portuguese .
• production and distribution of IEC materials Health centers, communities, schools and markets in project area
• training for community leaders in covid19 health/protection measures and social behaviour.
• distribution of soap and masks (local fabric) among the population and trining in it correct use
• support the local production of masks in local fabric, improving quality tested and aproved by WHO recommendations
• production and distribution of guidelines at individual, community and leadership levels to guide the response.
