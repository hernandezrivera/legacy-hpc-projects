Project Summary

The project aims at supporting households affected by the war through rehabilitating partially damaged houses and distributing NFI kits to displaced and returnees in Afrin and Azaz in Aleppo.
In total, 1200 HHs will benefit from the activities of this project (3150 females –2980 males) - (Men:1300, Women:1550Boys:1680, Girls:1600), Total 6130 (yearly) 2022 -2023
- The rehabilitation will target 400 houses that are partially damaged houses that people are living in or have returned to (yearly)
- NFI kits will be distributed to 800 HHs that have been displaced from different areas into Afrin and Azaz.(yearly)2022 -2023
So the Main activities will be as following:
Houses rehabilitation: 800 houses (2022 -2023), BRD will rehabilitate, minor rehabilitation for damaged houses.
NFI distribution: 1600 HHs (in 2022 -2023), which the kit content (emergency kits (Plastic Sheeting, Jerry Can, Solar Lamps, Mattresses, Blankets, Hasira in summer and Carpet in winter as per cluster technical guidance)
BRD will conduct segregated awareness sessions while distribution to keep the IDPs aware about the social distance while distribution, risk of COVID-19 and prevention measures as well as BRD will distribute soaps to ensure the IDPs prevention measure against COVID-19 in addition to provide the masks, gloves and sanitizers during the distribution process for the beneficiaries

4 - Needs

2021 witnessed several forced displacements from south of Idleb to Aleppo, CCCM Cluster tracked 520K displacements up to Jan2021. According to HNO 2022, 4.7 million people need shelter support in Syria due to inadequate conditions in existing shelters, the number of people in need of NFI assistance in Idleb has increased by over 850,000. The need for durable shelter solutions is increasing as most people have been displaced for more than a year in Idleb and Aleppo and even the host communities whose houses have been damaged are trying to return and repair their houses if possible. Azaz and Afrin have a damaged buildings, general infrastructure, as well as private houses. In 2021, these two districts hosted a lot of IDPs coming from Idleb. In addition to the returning movement to Afrin There are alot of houses are damaged and do not provide the basic living conditions Some do not have doors or windows for the rooms or even for the house which may result in some protection issues and leave residents vulnerable to theft and exposed to harsh weather conditions. Given the poor economic conditions and lack or non-existence of livelihood opportunities for majority of the youths who are able and willing to work, they cannot rehabilitate their houses and live in them. Some of these people continue to live in the damaged houses as they have no other alternatives On the other hand, a significant number of IDPs were able or would like to return to their communities of origin.

5 - Implementation of activities

This project will compromise Rehabilitation of 400 partially damaged houses and distributing 800 core NFI kits in addition to Rehabilitation of damaged houses BRD will receive applications from bene ciaries who are in need of house rehabilitation, the application will include the ID of the applicants in addition to property ownership documents that will be veri ed by the community. In case of rent, there must be a contract for at least six months from the completion of the repair Two members of the staff (one male and one female) who are trained on HLP will be focal points responsible for any HLP issues confronted during implementation of the project. A technical needs assessment of every house will be conducted before starting the implementation of the rehabilitation works. The house should be impartially damaged, and the cost of all rehabilitation works will not exceed $500 for each house The selection of bene ciaries for the Shelter rehabilitation activities will be based on the following criteria: -HHs who have returned to their damaged houses. - Female headed households (both from host communities and IDPs) -HHs with chronically ill members or with disabilities (both from host communities and IDPs) - People from HC who still live in their damaged houses After identifying the needed works, BRD will announce a tender to contract with a competent local contractor to provide some job opportunities for the youths in the targeted area.
