Project Summary

Faced with armed violence in Cabo Delgado that caused a humanitarian crisis, the project aims to minimize the psychological trauma of children, adolescents and young people from 5 to 18 years of age of both sexes living in humanitarian emergency situations through psychosocial support sessions using therapeutic play therapy.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Contribute to overcoming the emotional trauma of 350 children, teenagers and young people from 5 to 18 years of age of both sexes who live in a humanitarian emergency resulting from the conflict and insecurity caused by the actions of terrorism in the north of the province of Cabo Delgado and who are housed in the resettlement centers of the administrative post of Catapua Chiure district.The project responds directly to indicators 1 and 2 of the ToRs of the Education Cluster Project referring to the assistance of children aged 5 to 18 years psychologically and socially affected by violence, so that they can participate in recreational and therapeutic activities that allow them to express their feelings. and anguish for psychological well-being.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The armed violence in Cabo Delgado has provoked a humanitarian crisis. The number of internally displaced people continues to grow, around 800,000 people are displaced of which more than 120,000 are school children, 1,486 teachers forced to leave behind all their experiences causing people to erupt and affecting their minds. These people need to overcome the psychological and emotional trauma through play therapy actions.The REMA project aims to bring solutions to 350 IDPs, providing them with a different way of life and removing the anguish of direct and indirect beneficiaries.Strategically, REMA will work with the target group divided by age groups: 75 children from 5 to 9 years old; 100 pre-teens aged 10 to 14 years; 150 teenagers and young people from 15 to 18 years of age, and 25 teachers who will be trained in play therapy to be replicated in schools, totaling 350 direct beneficiaries and about 2,284 indirect beneficiaries, 65% of whom will be female.This strategy will allow you to determine the type of creative material and release of emotions, and the beneficiaries will learn coexistence rules, they will learn to compete, to be focused on the objective, and all this will occur in a spontaneous and fun way.  Socialization of the REMA project with the provincial level government authorities (DPE) including the Education Cluster, and at the local level (district and Administrative Post, and structures within the resettlement centers);  Target group mapping including organizations that implement similar actions in the field to harmonize interventions in the resettlement centers in Katapua, Marrupa and Meculane;  Identification of interest groups and mapping of therapeutic needs in the resettlement centers in Katapua, Marrupa and Meculane; Carrying out 3 playful sessions for the target group from 5 to 14 years old using toys including attribution of prizes in the resettlement centers in Katapua, Marrupa and Meculane;  Risk assessment for the target group, young girls and boys and adolescents from 14 to 18 years of age and mapping of therapeutic needs in the resettlement centers in Katapua, Marrupa and Meculane;  Realization of 3 playful sessions for the target group from 15 to 18 years old through drawing and painting, puppet theater, women's and men's soccer games to bring dynamics and awaken emotions.  Community Actors Puppet Training  Conducting 3 legal literacy sessions for teenage girls and young people aged 14 to 17;  Training for teachers in terms of play therapy so that the teacher is connected with the world of playfulness, toys and games.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Project implementation relies on an information flow from the base to the provincial level.At the grassroots level, permanent interaction with the structures set up in the resettlement centers will be privileged, up to the administrative, district and provincial level, with project performance reports that will be shared on a quarterly basis. With the structures of the centers, agreements will be signed to implement activities with a commitment to produce immediate changes.To this end, teachers will be trained who will serve as focal points for each centre. At the provincial and district level, joint visits will be organized between the project and the government as a way of guaranteeing joint assistance to the beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project identified the exposure of young girls and adolescents as having the greatest associated risk. This layer can be more affected and traumatized due to separation with their families during the displacement. Another group is the boys for the same cases, but also the separation with friends during the flight and the loss of their belongings, toys, school material and equipment and equipment, are in absolute despair. It is an easy-to-manipulate group to engage in wild behavior and embrace the insurgency. The REMA project will carefully look to greater attention to the need for play therapy centered on orphaned children, as well as on their parents and foster families.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Before starting with the concrete activities, the project will map young children and teenagers living with disabilities, aged between 5 and 18 years old, disaggregated by sex, with a view to a privileged treatment and assistance in play therapy centered on people with associated risk. . Naturally the person with a disability is vulnerable and may be suffering twice as much vulnerability due to insurgency actions.The project therapist will carefully select the appropriate material for each type of disability, and the activities with the greatest impact on the psychological rehabilitation of people from that social layer.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project did not carry out an environmental study, but considers a cross-cutting issue during the implementation of its activities. Naturally, it will be essential in your approach to touch on a healthy environment with the hygiene and sanitation of the environment observed to ensure the well-being and health of communities.
