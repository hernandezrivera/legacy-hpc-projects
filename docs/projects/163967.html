31/12/2020

Project Summary

The project will provide life-saving humanitarian food assistance to 1250 HHs (1000 IDPs/250Host), through a restricted voucher modality (E-voucher) with the aim of saving lives and ensuring continued and regular access to food by a monthly 60$ which will last for 8 months with the total of 480$ for each HH.
In coordination with GBV protection sub-cluster, GAN will allocate 1% of the project beneficiaries for the survivors, also GAN will target the beneficiaries of the livelihood program to alleviate the burden and get more benefit from the programs in coordination with the FSL and ER clusters.
And will provide livestock support to 439 HH (70% IDPs + 30% HC) in order to avoid any internal conflict and conflict between IDPs and host communities.
Project consists 2 phases:
1- Providing 439 HHs in the targeted communities with:
1.1- Animal feed (compound fodder) for 3 months.
1.2- Veterinary services for livestock (sheep and goats).
to protect livestock as key livelihood assets against livestock diseases
1.3- Awareness creation training sessions for the breeding.
2- Providing vaccinations for all animal heads (sheep and goats) in the targeted communities.
GAN will provide vaccinations through organized vaccination campaigns estimated for 20,000 animal heads (sheep, goats)
This project focuses on targeted HHs with Negative Livelihoods Coping Strategy, the project approach aims to Support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives.

4 - Needs

7.9M people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. particularly in the Idleb de-escalation area, continues to deteriorate as a result of the hostilities that escalated in late 2019.A monthly food ration with staple items costs at least 90% of an unskilled laborer’s average monthly salary and between 50-80% of average monthly salary.4M people live in NWS, of which 2.8 are estimated to be in need. Women and children are estimated to make up for 76% of this population.The main needs of the most recently displaced individuals, as reported by the CCCM, are NFI, shelter, food and cash assistance.Since 1Dec-2019, almost 600,000 people fled from their homes, mainly from southern Idleb governorate, moving further north away from the hostilities, 80% of whom are women and children. Many fled their homes within the space of hours without any belongings.After the initial displacement waves, lifesaving livelihood initiatives are also needed to support the affected communities and households in increasing agricultural production, sustain small scale food production, protection of productive assets, and restoring or creating income-generating activities to prevent negative or irreversible coping mechanisms.Provision of animal feed to limit sales of livestock by displaced and impoverished HHs. Due to transport cost, feed distributions are expensive and hence extremely limited in scope. Voucher schemes for provision of animal feed involving local suppliers are cost-efficient and much more sustainable. To enable improved planning and targeting of activities, there is a need to obtain updated estimates of livestock numbers owned by IDPs.In NWS, vaccination of 2.4M smallholder chickens against Newcastle Disease and of smallholder almost 18,000 dairy cattle against Lumpy Skin Disease has prevented a resurgence of these two devastating livestock epidemics in 2019.HNO2020Situation Report OCHA NO.6/2020

5 - Implementation of activities

1-Monthly food assistance for the assessed food insecure people: The project will provide life-saving humanitarian food assistance to 1250 HHs (1000 IDPs/250 Host) HHs. The project will last for 10 months through a restricted voucher modality (e-voucher) with the aim of saving lives and ensuring continued and regular access to food by a monthly 60$ which will last for 8 months with the total of 480$ for each HH.We will use beneficiary selection criteria that prioritizes vulnerable HHs, including female or youth-headed households, those with elderly or disabled family members, and households with more than six persons They will be able to use the e-voucher card immediately after the orientation sessions. the beneficiary will be able to buy with 60$ his/her needs among 40 specified food items with restricted amounts that are allowed in the e-voucher card to fulfill the calorie standard set by the WFP. GAN chose to implement through e-voucher system based on many reasons, such as saving the dignity of beneficiaries, as to prevent of food basket selling which is frequently happened and GAN implemented and used this modality in 2019 and the beneficiary satisfaction rate was 98%2-Providing 439 HHs with:2.1-Animal feed (compound fodder) for 3 months. After beneficiary selection based on HH who have =< 10 goats/sheep and whose livelihoods entirely depends on livestock based on vulnerability criteria, 439 beneficiaries (70% IDPs + 30% HC), GAN will provide 33 kg of fodder for each animal head per month for 3 months to reduce risk of malnutrition to ensure regular feeding.2.2-Veterinary services for livestock (sheep and goats)for 6 months. 2.3-Awareness creation training sessions for the breeding. GAN will train the beneficiaries on how to breed livestock and veterinary care to create awareness on prevention and control of zoonoses 3-Providing vaccinations for all animal heads (sheep and goats) through organized vaccination campaigns estimated for 20,000 animal heads

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: -Security situation does not deteriorate. -Adequate presence of vendors to support the voucher program. -Stable food prices in the targeted locations. -Project is accepted in the targeted locations Risks: - Target locations are inaccessible for our staff due to security incidents; if this happens, we will monitor the security situation on a day to day. Should the situation not improve, we will work to revise our action plan. - Communities and key stakeholders reject our project/plan; if this were to occur, begin the process of selecting new target locations. - Local markets cannot provide supplies/services/labor/goods; we will continually assess the market, and if an incident occurs that causes a sudden shift in the market (non-availability, increased cost), consider changing the locations to where markets and vendor with sufficient capacity are available. - Some local authorities may have restrictions on using e-vouchers. GAN has already activities in the targeted locations with no objections from the local authorities to use this system. If this were to occur, as a mitigation measure, we will use paper-based voucher system that can replace the e-voucher system or change the location based on the situation.
