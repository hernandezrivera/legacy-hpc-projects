Project Summary

The project will support the livelihoods of the most affected population by providing food security in the most in need areas in Albab district. the intervention will be through the distribution of wheat seeds and sheep to the most affected and needy farmers to ensure their food security and support their livelihoods in the long term. The project will also support the local market by purchasing all project inputs from the target areas.
Selection priorities:
1. most affected farmers from host communities and have more than 10 dunums. As we cannot target IDPs because of their lack of adequate land ownership
2. Agriculture is the main source of income for beneficiary households
3. Farmers directly or indirectly affected by the ongoing crisis
4 - family affected and headed by a woman
the project will enable the most affected population to resist poverty and gain resilience to fight the severe economic conditions, overcome challenges caused by conflict, to become independent from humanitarian aid and move towards self-reliance.
this project will provide an opportunity to female-headed households and engaging them to create sustainable income and lives in dignity.
the agricultural kits will include as follows:
-150 kg of wheat seeds
-500 kg of fertilizer
- pesticides
the livestock kit:
- 2 sheeps
- 600 kg of fodder
and dairy production tools

4 - Needs

Vulnerable populations such as people with disabilities, single-headed households, and the elderly often face particular challenges physically accessing food, for reasons ranging from a lack of mobility to threats to their physical safety. In many communities, an influx of internally displaced persons (IDPs) and returnees contribute to socio-economic pressures that also create vulnerabilities for host communities.Food insecurity in Syria is also driven by a lack of financial access, as high food prices, low purchasing power, and the collapse of income-earning opportunities have hampered most Syrians’ ability to acquire food. These challenges are reflected in a heavy reliance on negative coping strategies, a lack of dietary diversity, and significant gaps in food consumption. Among conflict-affected households, inflation, market disruptions, and the extreme loss or depletion of productive assets all contribute to the economic challenges to either purchasing or producing food. The agricultural sector, which normally serves as a major engine of the economy and a source of cheaper and more abundant food. As a basic reference, on 10 Dec 2019, Maram field team conducted a field visit to AlBab countryside to verify from the needs and take a close look about livestock and agriculture situation where Maram field staff visited (Borj, Hadath, Hazwan, Maqri, Noman, Olan, Qdeiran, Qemmet Daghelbash, Saflaniyeh, Sheikh Elwan, Sheikh Jarrah, Sosyan, Su Sinbat, Tal Jerji, Waqqah, Borj, Noman and Waqqah) More than 3500 HHs in need of food security and they practicing coping strategies.Maram’s program will target vulnerable populations affected by conflict and displacement living in the most sub-standard living-conditions in Al-Bab district through wheat and sheep distribution. As defined by the FSL cluster, the most vulnerable populations include female-headed household the project will target female3 headed households with sheep distribution.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Girls, boys, women, and men's physical security is put at risk as a result of the violence associated with civilian populations/spaces being targeted by armed parties to the conflict; a lack of consideration for the safety and security to access/receive services; lack of consideration of contextual information (e.g. aerial bombardment) and targeting of civilian infrastructure in route to, during and directly after service provision. -Likelihood: High - Impact: High - Mitigation: Consider conflict patterns and contextualanalysis as part of decisions for establishing a fixed site or mobile service provision. -Ensure safety audit is conducted to ensure safe access (route and location) by boys, girls,women and men, elderly and populations with decreased mobility (due to physical,visual/hearing deficits, psychological issues). - Engage with local authorities on safetyand security in the community.
