Project Summary

In 2024, 5 million people urgently requiring humanitarian assistance—about the same needs for the past year in severity from the previous year. However, deterioration is widespread across 34 Local Government Areas (LGAs), reaching a critical situation (phase 4). Particularly vulnerable populations, including women and girls (52%) and children (60%), face formidable challenges such as distant water fetching, lack of knowledge to stay safe resulting to heightening exposure to protection issues and susceptibility to WASH-related diseases and malnutrition.
Addressing the critical WASH gap requires a robust hygiene promotion initiative, recognized as a cornerstone for preventing diseases, enhancing community well-being, and ensuring sustainable practices. Hygiene promotion is intrinsically linked to the urgent need for Information, Education, and Communication (IEC) materials. These materials play a pivotal role in disseminating culturally appropriate hygiene messages, fostering social behavior change, and empowering communities to adopt and sustain WASH best practices. In the past, about 80% of WASH Partners have indicated that they are not able to provide IEC Material to the commnities they respond, some because of funding, but most because of the technical capacity to design and produce.
To address this imperative need, our project focuses on the design, production, printing, and translation of IEC materials into local languages. Hausa, Kanuri, Fulfulde, Shuwa Arab, Marghi, Mandra, Babur Bura, Chibok, and Kibaku have been identified as key languages for translation, ensuring accessibility and cultural relevance.
Also, these IEC materials will be strategically deployed in three key areas to support WASH sector partners:
Global Days IEC Materials: Tailored materials for global WASH celebrations, including World Water Day, Global Handwashing Day, World Toilet Day, and Menstrual Hygiene Management Day. These materials will raise awareness and engage communities.
Cholera Prevention and Response Materials: Customized materials addressing cholera prevention and response strategies. These will be deployed in priority LGAs identified as particularly vulnerable to cholera outbreaks.
Hygiene Promotion IEC Materials: Culturally sensitive materials promoting hygiene practices in priority LGAs, contributing to behavior change and fostering sustainable WASH habits.

1 - Needs

WASH needs remain unmet in the BAY States and have spreading rapidly across Local Government Areas in 2023 amounting to 5 million in need of WASH services in 2024. This project is dedicated to addressing the hygiene promotion needs of vulnerable populations. GOALPrime as the common pipeline partner for the WASH sector for IEC materials, we are committing to target 80% of this 2.4 million population, which amounts to approximately 1.92 million individuals to be reached with messaging via IEC Materials. The humanitarian situation in the BAY States is particularly severe, with 34 LGAs in a phase 4 crisis. In our efforts, we focus on the needs of women and girls, who make up 52% of those in need and often have the responsibility of fetching water from distant sources. Additionally, children, who represent 60% of the population in need, are at heightened risk of WASH-related diseases and malnutrition, underscoring the need for this project. GOALPrime will fill the crucial gap in hygiene promotion, which is critial for preventing disease and enhancing the well-being of communities. The cornerstone of our project is the development, production, printing, and translation of essential IEC materials into key local languages, such as Hausa, Kanuri, and Fulfulde. This approach ensures that our materials are culturally sensitive and accessible to the diverse communities we serve. These materials will be distributed strategically, focusing on Global Days IEC Materials for WASH celebrations, Cholera Prevention and Response Materials in areas prone to cholera outbreaks, and General Hygiene Promotion in priority LGAs. A key part of our strategy is to mainstream protection and mitigate gender-based violence (GBV) in our activities. GOALPrime will ensure that our materials and messages are inclusive and resonate with the diverse communities we aim to reach. By targeting 80% of the sector's goal, we expect a substantial shift in hygiene practices and a decrease in the incidence of WASH-related diseases. This project is not just about addressing immediate needs; it's about laying the foundation for long-term behavioral change, fostering resilience, and promoting self-sufficiency in hygiene practices within these communities.

2 - Activities

This project focuses on the critical dissemination of IEC Materials, aimed at improving hygiene practices and preventing diseases such as cholera. We have chosen this approach based on the urgent need for effective hygiene promotion in the face of ongoing health challenges resulting from lack of information on basic Water, sanitation and Hygiene behaviors in the BAY states, The intervention is structured around three main distribution rounds, each targeting a specific aspect of hygiene promotion. i. Global Days Distribution - aligning with global WASH celebrations, these materials aim to raise awareness and engage communities in special observances like World Water Day and Global Handwashing Day. ii. Cholera Response Distribution - given the recurrent cholera outbreaks, this round focuses on delivering targeted materials for cholera prevention and response strategies in high-risk areas. iii. General Hygiene Promotion Distribution - This round is broader, encompassing a range of hygiene practices including hand hygiene and printing of IEC Materials already designed by the WASH sector like the flip chart. The sector costing for IEC Material is $0.158 per individual. Based on our target of reaching 1,920,000 individuals out of the 2.4million targeted by the WASH sector, across three distribution rounds, the total program cost for this intervention amounts to $910,080. This cost is a calculated investment in the health and well-being of a significant portion of the population. 1. Selection of Topics for Hygiene Promotion through Assessment:GOALPrime will conduct an assessment to identify key topics for hygiene promotion. This this will involves gathering data on local WASH-related challenges, health trends, and community practices. The assessment will help pinpoint specific areas of focus such as disease prevention, safe water practices, sanitation, and personal hygiene. 2. Development of IEC Materials: Based on the assessment findings, GOALPrime will develop a range of IEC materials. These materials will be designed to address the identified topics and will be tailored to the context friendly in the target communities. 3. Technical Review Workshop: Organize a technical review workshop involving experts in WASH sector including the government . This workshop aims to critically anD technically evaluate the developed IEC materials for accuracy, visual appealing, relevance, and effectiveness. Feedback from this workshop will guide the refinement of the materials. 4. Designing of IEC Materials: Incorporate feedback from the technical review to finalize the design of the IEC materials. Ensure that the design is engaging, culturally appropriate, and easily understandable to the target audience. 5. Testing of Materials in the Field: Pilot the IEC materials in select communities to test their effectiveness. Gather feedback from community members and local stakeholders to evaluate the materials' impact on understanding and behavior change. 6. Printing of the Materials: Once the materials are finalized and tested, proceed with the high-quality printing of the IEC materials to reach the targeted population. 7. Distribution of Materials to WASH Partners: Implement a distribution strategy to disseminate the IEC materials among WASH partners. Ensure that the materials are distributed effectively and reach the intended recipients, including IDP camps, host comunities, health facilities, and schools. 8. Post-Distribution Monitoring: Conduct post-distribution monitoring to understand the satisfaction and comprehension of the material among the target audience. This involves collecting feedback, conducting surveys, and organizing focus group discussions to assess the effectiveness of the materials in conveying the intended messages and prompting behavior change.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

To ensure protection mainstreaming in our hygiene promotion project, GOALPrime will integrate protection principles at every stage of the project, with a strong focus on safeguarding beneficiaries and stakeholders. This commitment is central to our approach, emphasizing the prevention of gender-based violence (GBV), ensuring Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), and upholding the dignity and well-being of all participants. Key Modalities for Protection Mainstreaming:Assessment for Free Distribution and Awareness: Our initial assessments will not only identify needs but also ensure that communities understand that all humanitarian assistance, including our IEC materials, is provided free of charge. This approach helps prevent any misconceptions that could lead to exploitation. Emphasis on Equitable Access and Non-Discrimination: We will ensure that access to our services and materials is equitable, without any biases or obstacles, particularly focusing on the needs of vulnerable groups, including persons with disabilities and survivors of GBV. GBV Risk Mitigation Measures: In line with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards, our project will implement GBV risk mitigation measures to minimize potential harm to beneficiaries, ensuring that our activities do not inadvertently increase vulnerability. Mandatory PSEA Training and Code of Conduct: All individuals involved in the project, including staff, volunteers, and contractors, will receive mandatory training on PSEA and will be required to sign a Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. This ensures a common understanding and commitment to upholding these critical standards. Confidential Reporting Protocols for PSEA: We will establish clear and confidential reporting protocols for PSEA incidents. This includes promoting anonymous reporting mechanisms to protect the identity of reporters and encourage openness in reporting any concerns. Community-Based Complaints Mechanisms (CBCMs): In line with recommendations from the IASC Task Force on PSEA, we will cooperate with other agencies to create and maintain CBCMs. These mechanisms will be crucial in preventing and responding to SEA, offering accessible and confidential avenues for complaints. Awareness Campaigns on Unconditional Assistance: Through our awareness campaigns, we will reinforce the message that humanitarian assistance is unconditional and must never be exchanged for sexual favors. This will be a key message in our IEC materials and community engagement activities. Support for Victims of SEA: In cases where SEA occurs, we will ensure that victims have access to appropriate complaint mechanisms and support services. This includes working closely with local organizations and authorities to provide the necessary care and assistance. Environmental and Climate Change Sensitization: Recognizing the intersection of environmental sustainability and protection, our project will incorporate climate change integration and routine sensitization into our activities. This approach aims to foster community awareness and resilience, reducing environmental degradation and promoting sustainable practices.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime will collaborate with the government and WASH partners to develop culturally appropriate and relevant Information, Education, and Communication (IEC) materials. Once these materials are developed, they will be distributed to various WASH sector partners, who will then pass them on to beneficiaries in their respective areas. Following this distribution, GOALPrime will conduct follow-up visits to beneficiary communities. These visits are crucial for directly engaging with beneficiaries to confirm whether they have received the materials and understand them. This direct interaction allows GOALPrime to gather feedback on the clarity, relevance, and effectiveness of the IEC materials. The feedback collected from beneficiaries is vital for making necessary improvements to the materials. It ensures that the materials effectively communicate key hygiene messages and are aligned with the needs and understanding of the community. GOALPrime will also schedule periodic monitoring visits to the project locations. These visits will serve the dual purpose of overseeing the progress of material distribution and usage, and gathering informal feedback to observe the impact of the materials on the community. Simple and accessible feedback mechanisms, such as suggestion boxes or direct interviews during monitoring visits, will be utilized to enable beneficiaries to share their experiences and insights easily. The feedback received will be regularly analyzed to understand beneficiaries' perceptions and suggestions. This ongoing analysis is key to adapting and refining the IEC materials, making them as effective and user-friendly as possible.
