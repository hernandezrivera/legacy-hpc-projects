Project Summary

PIN plans to respond to WASH needs in Northern Syria by delivering humanitarian WASH supplies, services and improve hygienic behavior and practices of most the vulnerable people. PIN will focus on both distributions of essential hygiene kits and hygiene promotion and promoting nature-based and environmental solutions under WASH like wastewater treatment plants and improved access to humanitarian lifesaving/emergency WASH services. Interventions will primarily target newly displaced/returned populations in informal settlements, camps, and host communities, based upon coordination with relevant partners to maximize response coverage and minimize gaps. Technical design and proposed level of services provided through these interventions will follow standards such as SPHERE and those established by the WG and will consider varying needs among different groups in the community, such as women, children, the elderly, and people with disabilities. Capacity building for local councils and water departments and operators will be provided by PIN staff through field coaching sessions focusing on operational aspects and management of water systems and supporting water departments with maintenance and spare parts for water stations and rehabilitating networks. PIN is planning to implement WASH activities in schools, taking into consideration locations with WASH infrastructure damaged by war and earthquake, locations with high IDP populations, locations with non-functioning WASH infrastructure indirectly affected by war, locations with unstable electricity supply, as well as locations with lack of maintenance and lack of skilled staff for maintenance.
PIN is aware that needs are different amongst gender and age groups and other vulnerable groups such as people with disabilities. PIN aims to provide the most needed assistance to such groups through securing a wide spectrum of different activities, establishing beneficiary selection criteria to prioritize the inclusion of those most vulnerable, and adapting its assistance delivery to promote access for the most vulnerable people. PIN’s programs are coordinated with local authorities at various levels from cross-border, to hub, to district and village levels. PIN engages in regular dialogue with authorities to ensure transparency, accountability, and protection mainstreaming. This includes consultations and briefings on needs, humanitarian principles, PIN procedures, and beneficiary selection at projects' planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation stages. Solar power plants will reduce the operational support cost for centralized water supply systems off-camps, connecting water/sanitation networks in camps to nearby systems will reduce the cost currently spent on the water trucking and septic tank desludging. In addition to cost recovery activities the establishment of a Tarifa system will reduce the operational support needed even when the solar power plants are not sufficient. In 2023, PIN will continue to us use solar panels in Menbij reservoir to generate electricity.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

PIN plans to implement activities such as distributing essential WASH NFIs and hygiene promotion kits. PIN will also undertake hygiene awareness activities tailored to the context. The targeting criteria include vulnerable communities, schools. PIN will provide training/mentoring to institutions and assist in maintenance and technical/management support to promote the sustainability of services. PIN will also install solar power systems for water stations, water stations connections to electricity grid, water networks rehabilitation, ensuring long-term sustainable and environmentally friendly solutions, extension of sanitation networks to minimize flood risks, water stations operational support, and establish a sustainable fee collection system. In addition to nature-based solutions, PIN will establish a wastewater treatment plant to protect the groundwater and improve public health. The treated water will also be available as a safe, low-cost, and sustainable water source for irrigation. After completion, PIN and respective LC will conduct follow-up monitoring visits to ensure fulfillment of operations and maintenance duties. PIN actively responds to the cholera outbreak by ensuring correct chlorination and treatment of water, cholera kit distribution, water quality testing etc.
