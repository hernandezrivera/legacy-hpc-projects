Project Summary

The current outbreak of COVID-19 has heightening the risk to vulnerable populations, internally displaced people and people on the move. Local populations have been reported to be hosting two or more families in their compound and house. This has placed a strain on the ability of host families to provide adequate covered living space. Overcrowding inside the house will limit the household's ability to adequately practice safe distancing within the household and therefore the rapid proliferation of disease between household members.

Through this funding, CARE proposes separated and isolated living space to allow families living in overcrowded conditions space to mitigate risks related to the spread of COVID19 as well as a GBV risk mitigation. The following activities are included; distribution of shelter kit to build separation or isolation (USD 120/ kit), NFI kit (USD 70/ kit) and kitchen kit for separate cooking space (USD 25/ kit); and awareness raising on use of shelter kits and safe hygiene practices through radio and social media. CARE will work in Beira and Pemba to reach maximum numbers in urban setting and leverage it's operational presence in both areas. CARE has successfully implemented shelter projects in Sofala and Cabo Delgado through OFDA and DFID funds.
