1 - Needs

Children are especially a vulnerable group facing several protection risks including malnutrition. Before the explosion, more than half a million children in Beirut were at risk of malnutrition; after the explosion, the numbers are expected to increase significantly. The well-being of children is also affected by the distress and post-traumatic stress caused by the experience of the explosion. On the other hand, displacement puts them at risk of violence, abuse and exploitation. Some are even at greater risk of being separated from their parents. Amel will ensure coordination with other projects in which the organization is an implementing partner for the Beirut Blast response, in partnership with UNICEF and Plan International.This will ensure that there is no duplication of activities. Amel, through its regular participation to the BML CP WG will also ensure that it fills in gaps and do not overlap with existing NGO’s and services

2 - Activities

Community Based Psycho-Social Support (mobile and in rented appartments): This component / the outreach, will be undertaken both by mobile roving teams (social worker, facilitator, animator) on site and in fixed premises (community center(s), rented appartments, etc.) to ensure that the CBPSS is conducted in safe spaces. Amel will indeed focus on prevention but as well on structured activities in order to ensure that parents and children are engaged.
