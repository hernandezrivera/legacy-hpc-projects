Project Summary

The proposed project consists of 1) support to building repairs for vulnerable affected households and 2) safe rubble and debris removal/disposal from damaged and destroyed houses. These components contribute to the shelter sector log frame Outcome 2 (Contribute to longer-term repair or rehabilitation and occupancy of damaged buildings) and Outcome 3 (Support multi-sectoral approaches to rehabilitate affected areas) respectively.
For the first component, rehabilitation and repairs of housing units/homes affected by the Beirut port blast in the vulnerable affected areas will be undertaken in coordination with UN-Habitat and other shelter partners. This component will contribute to the shelter sector Objective 2. The detailed activities include:
- Detailed technical assessments of damaged buildings in coordination with partners of the Shelter Sector and relevant authorities in the targeted area
- Rehabilitation and repair of damaged houses for affected vulnerable households

The second component will include environmentally sound safe removal and disposal of rubble and debris from damaged houses within the city of Beirut. The project will also closely coordinate with UN-Habitat on the ground-level. This component will contribute to the shelter sector Outcome 3. The detailed activities include:
- Collection of debris and rubble (bricks, glass, concrete, asbestos, steel) from around the damaged and destroyed houses in the affected areas and transportation to “temporary” sites assigned by the government/municipality
- Technical assessment of rubble and debris from destroyed houses for safe and environmentally sound removal and disposal
Both activities will be built on the UNDP's years of experience and presence on the ground in the relevant fields as well as the extensive network with national partners. The project is not funded yet as of August 23.

1 - Needs

The explosion of the port of Beirut on August 4, 2020, has had a massively destructive effect on the country’s capital and main economic engine. A death toll of around 250 persons from different nationalities, more than 6,000 persons injured around 2000 buildings were destroyed, and between 250,000-300,000 individuals are homeless due to destroyed homes in Achrafieh, Gemayzeh, Mar Mikhael, Beirut Central District, Qarantine, Bourj Hammoud, making urgent support building repairs especially for vulnerable affected households in the affected area indispensable. Also, the blast generated layers of debris (bricks, glass, concrete, asbestos, steel) from houses, structures, and public facilities filling up the streets. Disposal of rubble and debris in a safe and environmentally sound manner is key. For this sake, UNDP is currently undertaking the rapid assessment of the rubble and debris at the surrounding area of the Port of Beirut in close coordination with the Municipality of Beirut, the Ministry of Environment, EU delegation to Lebanon, and other national and international actors. This survey is being conducted by the volunteer group of engineers on the ground to quantify the assess and quantify the volume and type of debris and rubble in residential areas outside the port from the site survey. The environmental assessment inside of the port is currently undertaken by the UNDAC/UNEP mission due to the access restriction of the port. Based on these analyses, the UNDP will develop an action plan for safe debris and rubble clearance and disposal.

2 - Activities

Activity 1: support for building repairs for vulnerable affected households 1) Carry out detailed technical assessments of damaged homes/buildings in coordination with partners of the Shelter Sector and relevant authorities 2) Support affected households through rehabilitation and repair works in 400 damaged houses as per shelter sector standards. Activity 2: 1) Collection of debris and rubbles from destroyed houses in the affected area and transportation to the temporary site. 2) Technical assessment of rubble and debris from destroyed houses for safe and environmentally sound removal and disposal
