Project Summary

The project will focus on Improving Access and Quality of Basic Education to achieve increased access to quality primary education for improved learning outcomes for some of the most marginalized children boys and girls in Duk, Ayod and Uror Counties in Jonglei State .The project will respond positively to support improving access and quality of social services in the three counties mentioned above as Duk , Uror and Ayod .The project is in line with the National General Education Policy Framework 2023-2026 which places strong emphasis on basic , Secondary education and teacher training.
The project will focus on capacity strengthening of stakeholders in School management in Duk, Uror and Ayod to upgrade the learning boys and girls affected by conflict and flood emergencies
The key outcome will be to improve access to quality education for 6645 Children boys and girls in Duk, Ayod and Uror Counties. Enhance the capacity of 100 teachers to effectively teach and support school children using Child centered teaching methods and improved Classroom Management
The project Objectives and its goal will be to contribute to equitable access to quality basic education in Duk, Uror and Ayod Counties.
Enhance access to primary education through improved school structures and teacher training for development of education at the Community level.
Enhance the capacity of volunteer teachers to effectively teach and support Children learn through teacher training being one of the essential components aimed at achieving the goal.
Capacity development of the County education department, School Management Committees, Parents and Teachers Association on prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse
Enhance inclusive education involving the Children with disabilities to address the underlying drivers of fragility and build resilience. In addition to that, the project will ensure that girls have equal access to education while boys and men become advocates for, not obstacles to, equitable education for all.”
The project will support 30 Schools in which each County will get 10 Schools respectively whereas the teacher training will support100 teachers coming from the three Counties.
Improve teacher capacity and support, increase the number of teachers in
training, increase English language training for teachers, and strengthen
capacity of central and state officials, head teachers, inspectors, supervisors,
as well as TTI and CEC tutors. The project will ensure that children have a conducive environment at home and in their community, for them to develop to their fullest potential – so awareness-raising and social behavioral change at all levels as key factor in the project. The main goal for this project placed in Duk, Uror and Ayod is to become more responsive to the impacts of crisis, whilst ensuring resilience of teachers and learners, through increased provision of mental health and psychosocial support. In addition, that, the project will ensure girls have equal access to education while boys and men become advocates for, not being obstacles to, equitable education for all.” The project shall Integrate child protection and education to create a mutually-reinforcing cycle that can reduce children’s vulnerability in emergencies. Offering quality education shall increase children and families’ resilience in adversity, empower children, and promotes a protective environment. An environment free from unchecked child abuse, neglect, violence or exploitation fosters a quality education. This advocacy brief posits that integrating child protection and education programmes, policies, and minimum standards will maximize available resources to better address the multifaceted challenges and risks children face in humanitarian settings. Emergency teaching and learning materials will be distributed and prepositioned in Duk, Ayod and Uror on timely basis

1. Needs

The outbreak of fighting in Sudan on 15 April 2023 resulted in an influx of people fleeing the country, with more than 111,000 individuals, including 93 per cent of South Sudanese nationals, crossing border entry points along the Sudan-South Sudan border, as of 12 June 20233. Furthermore, the people in Sudan and those fleeing to neighboring countries like South Sudan are facing a protection crisis, with women, Children and vulnerable men and boys experiencing the worst consequences. Violations and needs such as family separation, alerts of trafficking, conflict-related sexual violence (CRSV) and other forms of gender-based violence (GBV) and lack of education for Children have put significant strains on existing weak protection and education structures with limited services available. The national flood preparedness and response was developed based on a worst-case scenario planning focusing on mitigation, preparedness, and response efforts in the most affected states: Unity, Jonglei, Upper Nile, Warrap, Lakes, and Central Equatoria About 3.3 million people were affected by the flood in the Country. Following the resumption of classes on April 3, 2024, after the heatwave, that closed schools for two-weeks, UNICEF and collaborating partners persistently delivered educational services to populations impacted by emergencies. Many schools nationwide including those in Duk Ayod and Uror encountered the predicament of limited learning spaces, compelling certain classes to operate outdoors including under trees.According to the Education Cluster in South Sudan (2025), 1.6 million Children are in need humanitarian assistance (CiN) may now fall behind without provision of equitable access to safe and protective education services. Ayod, Uror and Duk Counties of Jonglei State account for 14.8 % for education services. In Jongle state Duk , Uror and Ayod Counties education services have been severely affected by the conflict, floods, and the protracted intercommunal violence. Many primary and secondary schools were destroyed, and some schools remain closed or operate in the open air or under trees. In May 2024 an Inter-agency Rapid Needs Assessment Team in Ayod County was conducted 5 Payams comprising of 89 villages cam out with result that, the areas were mostly affected by heavy floods with about 31,400 people reportedly displaced from their villages in Padek, Bajack, Guadit, Gorwai, Buot, Mhaar Weriath Nyanepal Kharmun, Jiech and others. Few schools reopened following resumption of learning on 3rd May 2024 and there was a sharp drop of over 70% in school enrolment due to flood waters engulfing school facilities. In Duk County, and interagency Assessment was conducted 3 surveys in August 2024 on EGRA & EGMA, Child Marriage & Child Labor and Social & Emotional Learning (SEL), targeting 13 schools in Poktap. The survey or assessment provided a definitive statement about the low levels of student achievement in basic literacy and numeracy skills and the identification recommended for reinforcement. The key finding of the survey on child marriage and child labor was that not enough has been done to counter the challenges of access to education by girls. The assessment team recommended a course of action for creating alternatives for children other than child marriage and child labor anchored in the value of the child and benefits of education that can lessen the economic burden of adolescents on the family and enable young people to establish an independent life. The findings of the survey on SEL have established that there is need to develop and accept a set of standards or guidelines describing the social and emotional competencies students should know and be able to demonstrate at different grades. So as to acquire lifesaving and essential skills such as managing negative emotions, being calm and focused, following directions, and navigating relationships with peers and adults is required .

2. Project activities

Sectoral Objective 1: Improve equitable access to safe and protective education services for 7, 339 emergencies affected IDPs, returnees, spontaneous refugees, and host community children including children with disabilities and youth (girls and boys). 1. Enroll 7, 339 (3, 302girls) children in 25 Pre-primary, Primary and secondary schools/learning centers. 2. Establish 30 temporary learning spaces and classrooms 3. Establish 30 gender segregated latrines, water points 4. Distribute or provide sanitation and hygiene materials (hand washing soap, sanitizers, Dignity kits 2400 adolescent girls etc.) and PPE for students and teachers Sectoral Objective 2: Improve the quality of gender and disability-inclusive education services and teachers' capacity to protect the wellbeing of all children and prevent the negative impact of the emergencies on learning environments. 1. Provide emergency-affected children 250 quality education recreational materials 2. Provide teaching and learning materials for ECD (10 kits), primary (250 kits), and secondary school education (10 kits) 3. Train 150 teachers on EiE pedagogical skills and child-centred methodologies to improve teaching and learning process for emergency-affected children Sectoral Objective 3. Strengthen/enhance the education system’s response capacity of the school community and national/local education actors to mitigate the impact of existing emergencies on learners, teachers, and communities. 1. Train 300 SMCs/PTAs and education authorities on school management, governance, psychosocial support (PSS) and Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR)/ climate change 2. Support prevention and mitigation of communicable diseases through training school communities by providing appropriate Information and Education and Communication (IEC) materials (i.e. posters, leaflets) 3. Support conducting 15 back to learning campaigns .Conduct 4 coordination mechanisms for County education departments by making sure that they are in support of stakeholders working to access continuity of quality education Train 150 staff , PTA and SMC on prevention of Sexual exploitation and Abuse policy in line with education ethics and and standards WAO shall collaborate with other partners providing health services in those Counties to prevent the communicable disease like cholera, Red eyes , enhancing MHM services to adolescent girls to improve access to quality sexual and reproductive health ,education , child protection and others .Cash for Asset creation will be conducted during the renovation of learning spaces and institutions that have been affected by conflict and floods Establishment of 12 School gardens in the 3 Counties to grow vegetables to improve malnutrition disorders among the Children
