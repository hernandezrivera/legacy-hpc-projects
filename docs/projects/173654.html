Project Summary

Balochistan rural support program BRSP which is the largest civil society organization in Balochistan province having roots in the community through more than 22000 community institutions. It proposes to strengthen 100 health centre facilities through WASH services and raise the awareness level of general public. In this regard, BRSP will focus to strengthen “preparedness” through water, sanitation and hygiene WASH with proper hygiene awareness and practices, interventions that will contribute to Infection prevention and control (IPC) measures. these health WASH services and awareness raising interventions will be carried out in 100 government health facilities centers and awareness raising campaigns in general public areas in 10 districts of Balochistan.
Overall objective: To strengthen “preparedness” in 100-health center facilities through water, sanitation and hygiene WASH with proper hygiene awareness and practices, interventions that will contribute to Infection prevention and control (IPC) measures
Following are the major activities will be carried out in the project
1. 700 front line health workers in 100 health facilities trained
2. 200 Sanitary workers in 100 health facilities trained
3. 100 health care facilities cleaned and disinfected for six months
4. 400 CRPs one day orientation on awareness raising regarding COVID-19
5. 900 Personal Protection Equipment (PPEs) kits provided to HCP for six months.
6. 3000 Safety Kits provided to sanitary workers, CRPs and Staff
7. 108000 masks for community member during sessions
8. WASH services to 100 health care facilities provided
9. 400,000 Approved IEC material for Preventions of COVID-19 is reproduced and dissiminated.
10. Specific Messages on COVID 2019 through FM Radio are broadcasted for six months
11. Religious leaders (Imam masjid) of 100 mosque announcements are Engaged in announcements for three months
12. 7200 awareness raising sessions conducted with Community.
13. 3000 hygiene kits (soaps, sanitizers, detergent, mops etc) provided to HCF , CRPs, Immam masjid
