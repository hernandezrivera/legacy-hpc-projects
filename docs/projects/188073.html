Project Summary

Under the WASH sector, Violet (VO) is planning to implement a project to address the WASH needs of both IDPs & Host Communities who are living in both settings (in-camp & off-camp) through the provision of comprehensive WASH services as follows:
1. Provision of 35 liters of drinking & chlorinated water for BNFs inhabiting IDPs camps. The water quality will be insured by conducting the water tests by VO's monitors. Moreover, VO will coordinate with ACU to ensure the quality of the water sources and ensure the FCR tests are conducted on a water delivery bases to ensure the safety of the water for drinking
2. Rehabilitation of boreholes, water stations, & water networks in both camps and out camps.
3. Support the operation of the water system by the provision of fuel for water systems operation, Establishing Tariff system & re-activate the cost recovery mechanism to ensure an effective response
and training to the operators on the.
4. Equipping water systems with PV Solar energy systems to minimize the water system operating costs & to ensure the sustainability & long-term impact of the project.
5. Rehabilitation and construction of gender-disaggregated latrines and WASH facilities in camps to enhance the access of the affected groups taking into consideration the protection and privacy of women and children.
5. Rehabilitation of the sewage systems in both settings (in camps and out of the camps.
6. Solid waste management & septic tanks desludging septic tanks in IDPs camps
7. Distribution of Hygiene kits to the IDPs inhabiting the camps to improve the BNFs access to most essential hygiene supplies.
8. Disseminate awareness messages related to COVID-19 (RCCE), the importance of preventive measures, & hygiene promotion.
9. Distribution of hygiene kits for the most vulnerable groups inside and off camps
During Project Implementation, VO will ensure COVID-19 preventive measures are applied to avoid exposing the communities to harm. Additionally, Violet will uphold accountability to affected persons through awareness messaging on complaints, feedback, and PSEA mechanisms.
out of the requested amount (1,285,397 USD) for the implementation of WASH activities in 2022, 450,000 USD is already secured through partnering with QC and SCHF.

4 - Needs

According to the HNO report, the shortage of electricity remains the root cause for water supply systems underperformance and is mostly related to the significant shortages of fuel for power plants and water supply systems backup generators. WASH infrastructure requires significant support and substantial investments. The efficiency of water systems has sharply decreased for the first time since 2016, with nearly 2 M fewer people using water networks as the only HH water supply modality, in comparison to mid-2020. This means up to 47% of the population is relying on unsafe alternatives to piped water, to meet or complement their water needs. at least 70% of sewage is discharged untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems are not functional. In NWS, wastewater from huge camp clusters is openly discharged, forming sewage rivers Over 30% of garbage is inappropriately disposed or collected with low frequency, while the dumpsites often pose serious health, environmental, and protection risks WASH systems have suffered from damage owing to hostilities, strain from years of functioning at high capacity due to the growing demand, limited or no maintenance, the continuous drain of technical staff, and poor water resource management. Imposed coercive measures present a huge burden on ease of access to WASH consumables and equipment, with negative implications on the WASH response. Since 2020, WASH response has become more needed to prevent the spread of COVID-19.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be started with mobilization for the resources along with signing the required MoU's between Violet and Local Authorities such as Local Councils and Camps Manager which shows the responsibilities for all involved parties. The services will cover the life-saving WASH services in IDP's camps to deliver a comprehensive WASH Package for the targeted IDP's along with a) implementing a sustainable solution to access the drinking water and b) also proper disposal for the wastewater along with rainwater drainage to avoid the flood. In addition, deliver hygiene supplies to be distributed for the targeted beneficiaries. c) Moreover, implementing SWM-related activities to enhance public health and environmental hygiene. d) Further, rehabilitation for water and sanitation systems inside the targeted communities in order to ensure access for safe drinking water and proper disposal for wastewater along with rainwater. e) SWM and Cost Recovery Mechanism will be implemented too in the targeted communities in order to ensure sustainable access for the beneficiaries. Ensuring the connectivity of the other facilities inside the targeted communities to the WASH services will be considered in the project implementation. Moreover, the activities will be implemented taking into consideration the WASH Cluster Guidelines and recommendations in order to ensure the impacts and appropriateness of the intervention.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Violet has a protection department and is a member of the protection clusters and sub-clusters.Violet will establish a WASH Committee in IDP's sites as the main activity in order to increase community engagement and ownership, they will be selected from volunteers from men and women according to elections in the camps. Violet takes into consideration within the distribution approaches, all protection risks, as well as mitigation measures to PSEA and child, save guarding. Divide the distribution points into many areas and parts to avoid any crowdedness and keep the social distance between the beneficiaries. Moreover, separate the points by gender and hire female distribution workers to respect the dignity and privacy of the beneficiaries. Violet adapts the complaints and feedback mechanisms according to the implementation of modality. Violet will conduct an accountability survey about the services provided with them, where it is not only be focused on the services but also PSEA related and the ways they see fit to communicate with them. Violet's PSEA and safeguarding focal point will receive the complaints via the PSEA email or WhatsApp. These will be immediately reported to the protection coordinator and Violet’s CEO will nominate an investigation committee. The committee will conduct a risk assessment mitigation measures and investigation plan.
