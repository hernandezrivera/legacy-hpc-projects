Project Summary

Although last years Somalia hosted severe droughts that resulted in wide displacement across the country, the very recent Gu rains presented a hope for the affected communities though pastoral communities haven’t yet well recovered. Since Somalia doesn’t utilize rain water by harvesting means including durable dams, efficient subtraction of underground water, yet water scarcity persists in large parts of the country. Deteriorating Sanitation conditions of displaced people signals the importance of a collective efforts to end open defecation and maintain existing latrines through comprehensive hygiene and sanitation promotion. The ongoing conflicts continue to reduce the resilience of communities, trigger displacement and impede civilians’ access to basic services and humanitarians’ access to those in need. Resilience of pastoral communes is getting weaker as well as agricultural communities due to lack of comprehensive support that enables them to move forward. Communities used to have permanent water sources in riverine areas started to appeal for water as water table gets down and hence causing water sources to dry and thereby engage in unhygienic way of water collection that increase the likelihood of AWD outbreaks as happened during the last years in major and minor cities along Shabelle river.
However, Zamzam Foundation has embarked on a strategy that contributes to the wellbeing of affected communities who are in need especially the most vulnerable people, IDPs and people at risk of AWD/ cholera, acute malnourished children, people living in affected areas and children attending school through provision of sufficient and clean water and provision of healthy sanitation facilities and by Engaging communities to build their own latrines through the CLTS approach, and raising and promotion of communities' awareness to hazards of misuse of the sanitation facilities and negligence of good hygiene practices to deter and prevent diseases through targeted and comprehensive hygiene promotion activities. However, Project activities will be implemented in close coordination with other clusters (shelter, nutrition, health and education) to respond to life-saving emergencies.

Activities

1. Provision of durable clean water in highly needed communities by drilling 3 Strategic boreholes in highly water stressed areas in Somaliland, Puntland and South Central Somalia.2. Construction of 21 hand pump wells for affected communities in low water table areas in Somalia.3. Construction of 10 modified wells powered with solar energy complete with well infrastructure in lower water table areas in Somalia.4. Rehabilitation of strategic boreholes in Hiiraan, Middle Shabelle, Lower shabebelle, Galgadud and Bay Bakool regions Central Somalia to minimize risks, that might jeopardize protection of women and children, associated with emergency breaks for the boreholes’ accessories eg. generators, pumps, pipes etc.5. Improvement of sanitation and hygiene status of displaced and stressed communities in Somalia by provision of sanitation facilities (100 VIP latrines) through CLTS approach and by promotion hygiene of affected and host communities in emergency and post emergency times and provision of hygiene sanitation kits 100 HH in Somalia.6. Hygiene promotion activities at schools and health centers in Somalia using modern and friendly approaches.7. Chlorination and disinfection of communal water sources, health centers in emergence context.
