Project Summary

The proposed project aims to rehabilitate markets & improve access to basic services, thereby enhancing livelihood opportunities for vulnerable populations in Aleppo & RDam. Through collaborative assessments with beneficiaries (BEN), local committees, City Councils, municipalities, & Mukhtars, critical needs have been identified, leading to the development of a comprehensive response plan.
Aligned with ERL Sector Humanitarian Needs Overview 2024, Aleppo-Jebel Saman PiN 928,640, RDam-Maliha PiN 32,000, & RDam-Nashabiyeh PiN 25,000, the project seeks to meet the dire needs of returnees & communities. The main goal is to improve ERL conditions for the most vulnerable, fostering equal opportunities amidst socio-economic challenges.
Our activities include coordination with MOLAE & Govs, procurement procedures, contract management, site supervision, monitoring & evaluation, field visits, beneficiary feedback mechanisms, & reporting to stakeholders & donors.
Efforts are directed towards area-based complementary responses to address communities’ dire needs. Rescate's ongoing projects in RDam & Aleppo including RDHs & EQ response, necessitate complementarity with income-generating livelihoods to tackle socio-economic challenges effectively. This includes maintenance of SHF-funded assets in Ramouseh established in 2018, which will also encompass vocational training for 150 individuals, with a focus on 30% female participation.
Key project activities
Act 1.01 Provision & installation of solar systems for public facilities in 3 schools & 51 street lights in RDam.
Act 1.13 Debris removal in Aleppo & RDam.
Act 2.12 Rehabilitation of local markets, creating 25-generating livelihoods in RDam in 4 NBHD including shutters, facade, electrical solar system & WASH. with startup in-kind grants. Jobs cover hairdressing, tailors, dairy products, electrical maintenance & food snacks.
Act 1.16 Support for the establishment/strengthening of sustainable waste management systems in Aleppo & RDam. Reviving Ramouseh plant with Assets maintenance & VT
Act 2.05 Provision of Technical business & vocational training for 150 individuals on 14 different professions
Act 2.09 Creation of short-term jobs for 150 individuals, providing six months of employment. Under this component, in-the-job training, short term Livelihoods, then workers will have potential to sustainable jobs & increase resilience. The plant contributes to environment & communities will also benefit from the bricks productions at subsidiced prices.
Safeguarding measures: Safety, dignity & "Do No Harm" principles are paramount throughout the project. Special attention is given to vulnerable groups, including PWDs & those with chronic diseases, ensuring equitable benefits & addressing their specific needs. Proactive measures are taken to prevent discrimination & mitigate health risks associated with construction works.
Risk Mitigation Operational risks such as miscommunication, currency devaluation, security issues, & fuel shortages are acknowledged & addressed through clear communication channels, contingency planning, adherence to safety protocols, & strong coordination with partners.
In conclusion, the project aims to complement existing initiatives & provide a comprehensive response to the multifaceted challenges faced by vulnerable populations in the targeted areas. Financial contingencies & a risk mitigation plan are in place to ensure smooth execution despite potential challenges.
