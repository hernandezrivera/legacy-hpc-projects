Project Summary

The project will support women and youth led volunteer teams and grassroots organizations to conduct structured dialogue processes to support peaceful coexistence in communities across Maarat Masrin subdistrict. The dialogue processes will be followed up by design and implementation of community action plans.
Additionally the project will support civic awareness through a series of civic education programs aired on mass media, as well delivery of a civic education curriculum for children and adolescents.
