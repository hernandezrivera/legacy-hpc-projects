1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the life conditions and access to food of 47.102 persons members of IDPs families and vulnerable host community members in Balama, Montepuez, Namuno, Ancuabe, Mecufi, Metuge, Pemba and Quissanga districts renforcing and giving continuity to the ongoing AVSI multisector interventions on CCCM, Protection, Wash, Food Security, Education. The program foresees Food-for-asset (FFA) to build infrastructure for agriculture and accommodation/resettlement sites targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities; reinforcing resilience of the communities throught Training on sustainable agriculture techniques targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

O.S.2. 1.1 Food-for-asset (FFA) to build infrastructure for agriculture and accommodation/resettlement sites targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities This activity entails the creation of community seedling nurseries for horticultural and fruit produce, irrigation canals from main water sources and set-up of plots suitable for irrigation, planting of trees (windbreaker, repellent, and fruit varieties) in resettlement camps, and general maintenance works in the resettlement sites whereas needed. 1.2 Training sessions on sustainable agriculture techniques targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities Training sessions will be based mainly on conservation agriculture techniques, and some agroforestry techniques aimed at ensuring that the soils cultivated do not undergo depletion of nutrients and erosion due to practices based on monoculture, use of chemicals, and heavy reains 1.3 Seeds and agriculture tools distributions targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities. Distribution of seeds and tools will privilege the IDPs community and will be mainly based on crops suitable for the rainy season considering the high prevalence of rain-fed plots allocated to IDPs.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential harms and risks may arise, for instance, either when the lists of bene􀂦ciariesselected for the activity of distributions (1.1 and 1.4) are not double-checked and thenthe products ends up in the hands of wrong bene􀂦ciaries; or when the intendedbene􀂦ciaries sell the products received from the distributions or give them to a thirdparty/person in exchange for personal favors not related to improving the foodsecurity of the target population. To avoid this, the list of bene􀂦ciaries will be veri􀂦edby AVSI staff before the distributions to ensure that the bene􀂦ciaries meets thevulnerability criteria. Moreover, the distributions will be monitored through postdistributionmonitoring activities through questuonnaires elaborated with kobtool. Theaccess to assistance that does not depend directly from the implementing partner isalso promoted at the SDAE and DPASA level. By coordinating closely with the localauthority, AVSI will advocate for increasing the assistance to people in need wheneverdeemed necessary.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project itself is based on the strong participation of the bene􀂦ciaries for theactivities implementation. Before the beginning of the project, focus group activitieswill be planned to understand which are the bene􀂦ciaries expectations concerningproject impact. These meetings will be organized and held in close coordination withlocal leaders and the existing structure at the community level. Firstly, community’sopinion on how to adapt the general aspects of activities implementation andcoordination according to local culture, customs, beliefs and capacity, will be takeninto account and incorporated into the work methodology. Secondly, it will be created aspeci􀂦c project hotline for feedback and complaints related to the project and it will bepromoted the Linha Verde. It is worthy to note that AVSI has adopted an internal policyon accountability to Affected People on the March 23th, 2020 based on the IASCCommitments on Accountability to Affected Populations (CAAP).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Distirbutions of seeds, tools and cash-based transfer (1.1 and 1.4) will be insured forthe swath of the population having disabilities. During the identi􀂦cation of thebene􀂦ciaries in close coordination with local SDAEs, Acção Social (INAM), andreference cluster partners, special attention will be given to the most vulnerable,including persons with the disabilities. Accordingly, the post-distribution monitoringform will be elaborated so that to swiftly track individuals with disabilities andtherefore check regularly the assistance provided to them. Moreover, it is worthy tonote that very often people with disabilities are discriminated within their owncommunities. This can be a compounding factor when people with disabilities arrivedin a host community, as in the case of IDPs. AVSI Foundation will ensure theimplementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that thesepeople are supported by the community and given special attention when coordinatingand planning activities with local authorities and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI will follow the policies and rules contained in the AVSI Environmental Policy.Environmental considerations include the use of locally sourced materials. Agriculturetrainings will link environmental health with agricultural productivity, stressing theimportance of water conservation and the effects of excessive charcoal production.The environmental sustainability and appropriateness of all agriculture activities,including seed varieties and land and water usage, will be ensured through regularconsultation with FAO and the relevant country and state authorities
