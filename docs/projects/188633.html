Project Summary

The project aims to improve the food security situation and increase the self-reliance for the women headed families by starting their own projects and gaining their incomes, in addition, to strengthen local production capacity and support the local markets by availability and diversity of processed food, therefore, the community will be stable against external conflicts.
This project will support 60 women-headed families with vocational training, Tools, and equipment, in order to start their own projects and secure incomes to cover their families’ needs.
Each family will receive:
-Tools and equipment of up to 1500$ maximum depending on production requirements for creating a small profitable project. based on the available items at the home to the items include (Manual milk separating machine, Refrigerator, Burner boiler for milk, Milk agitator, Stove gas, Gaz bottle, Plastic barrel, Steel wheelbarrow, pair of Boot, Fire extinguisher, First aid kit, Rennet, cloth gauze for filtration, medical hair caps, jars- plastic bags -plastic pails, face masks, gloves, Apron, Strainers with different sizes, Thermometer, Foldable table with umbrella, Plastic bag welding machine).
-Preparing a workspace to be paved with ceramics and sanitation flitting, not exceed 6 M/2
-Three types of training: Food Processing (dairy production), Profit project management, and Marketing.
The beneficiaries will prepare the dairy products using the provided items sell them in the local markets and gain income to secure the family’s needs and cover the cost of the next month.
The Beneficiaries can add their dividends to their capital in the next month to buy more equipment and raw materials and produce more products, maximizing output.
Deirna team will hang awareness posters against COVID-19 in streets and gathering places and stick awareness stickers on the kits that will be distributed to the beneficiaries.
The Beneficiaries will be selected based on the following criteria:
-Female-headed households (widows and divorced only).
-Deirna will apply comprehensive criteria for the selection of project beneficiaries such as owning at least 100 heads of sheep, goats, or 2or 3 cows selling up to 80-90% of dairy products to local markets.
-The family should have a female over 18 years old who is able to work in food processing/dairy production.
-The family should not have participated in a similar activity with another NGO.
-The family should be in the targeted areas which are negatively conflict-affected.
-The family should accept all the grant conditions.
-Commitment to attending all the trainings that will be provided.
-Commitment to the project's aims and do not sell the assets that will be provided.
Priority will be given to applicants who meet the following criteria:
-They have the number of cattle to at least 2 cows or a constant source of milk production on a daily basis.
-Allocate a space of 6 M/2 of the house to be prepared for work
-The family is one of the most vulnerable households in the community.
-Good previous experience in food processing/dairy production.
-The family did not have another source of income.
-The displacement families.
-The Family includes breastfeeding or pregnant women.
-Large families with 6 members or more.
-Families with over than 5 children.
-Families caring for orphans or unaccompanied children.
-The presence of people with disabilities or elderly in the family.

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2021, the food security situation in Syria has steadily worsened over the past three years, 56 percent increase in the number of food-insecure people from 7.9 million in 2019 to 12.4 million by the end of 2020 is unprecedented. Two out of three Syrians are in need of some form of food and agricultural assistance, with an additional 1.8 million at risk of falling into food insecurity. The number of severely food insecure people has more than doubled, from 0.6 million in 2019 to 1.27 million people in 2021 (six percent of the population). HNO 2021 also mentioned that the economy has experienced irreparable harm since the crisis began, with the gross domestic product having declined by 60 percent and the government increasingly unable to raise sufficient revenue to subsidize essential commodities such as fuel and bread on which the most vulnerable families The Syrian pound is in virtual freefall having lost 78 %of its value since October 2019, while price increases for staple goods are at an all-time high. More than 90% of the population lives below the poverty line. 60-65% are estimated to live in extreme poverty, up from 50-60% in 2019. Through our assessment of the markets, we found that they are in dire need of these products because of the high prices of fuel. There is no longer the possibility of import, and this is what caused their disappearance product from the market.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The risks of GBV can happen because of overcrowding in distribution points or may happen after distributing while returning home, which may lead to harm to some women and may cause domestic violence. - Likelihood: M. - Impact: H. -Mitigating: -We will not distribute at a late time of the day. -We will not choose distribution points far from the residential complex, also we will distribute them in each village separately. -During distribution, we will divide the location into two sides, one side for women and the other for men. -Ensure that complaint mechanisms for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse/GBV are accessible (WhatsApp, email, etc.) and that focal points for receiving complaints understand how to discharge their duties. **The risks of exploitation of beneficiaries by the field workers. -Likelihood: w. - Impact: H. -Mitigating: 1- We will choose the field team carefully, who have high behavioral qualities. 2- 4- Ensure that the complaint mechanisms for reporting SEA/GBV are accessible (WhatsApp, email,..) and that focal points for receiving complaints understand how to discharge their duties. 3-In case any exploitation happens, Deirna will take appropriate action to protect persons from retaliation where allegations of SEA/GBV are reported involving our personnel (suspending staff till the investigation is conducted, referring the victim to PSS, and even witness protection program if needed) and investigate allegations in a timely and professional manner.
