Project Summary

Global Aid hand is one of well-known humanitarian organizations working in Abyei PCA,West Kodofan and South Darfur , reaching more than 50000 people annually through disaster response, early recovery programmes and youth initiatives, targeting IDPs and host communities, during conducting the interventions in other sectors we have realized the huge gap in health and nutrition specifically, because of the continual increasing of population numbers, this project aims to increase the level of awareness in all humanitarian aspects because it’s the base of interventions, then going to focus on training of more midwives because they are the main change agents among these communities, also we would continue to support the health facilities with the medicine, supplies and strengthen the existing referral and EWAR systems for any outbreak and complicated cases for further treatments in nearby towns with superior quality services with special focus on women, girls and children under 5.
The project will also deliver complete CMAM package and nutrition services interventions for the treatment of malnutrition among children U5 and PLWs through the provision of the needed supplies and nutrients, building the capacity of the medical staff in the target nutrition centers and inclusion of community participation in outreach activities to ensure project sustainability. The project also intends to raise the awareness of the mothers and/or care givers in the target communities and create a positive change in their feeding practices to decrease the high rates of GAM among the vulnerable PLWs and children U5.
This project intends to provide these services in an integrated manner to maximize the positive impact through training of community volunteers on CMAM, IYCF and health interventions, raising community awareness on different health and nutrition issues/topics and providing of health and nutrition services within the same HFs

1 - Needs

The situation in Abyei is vulnerable; the political situation has had an impact on the context, which reflects in all humanitarian sectors. The gap is increased everyday especially in Health and Nutrition. The main reason for these gaps is the relocation of the medical personnel due to the economic situation, besides the recent outbreak of diseases such as watery diarrhea and respiratory infections due to the lack of prevention measures; even the absence of basic drugs has worsened the situation. Abyei has to be on regular screening and assessment due to its location at the border.In West Kordofan and South Darfur GAH has been conducting a protection and Wash programs with the support from different partners. The challenge still remains due to gaps in other sectors on provision of services and low capacities of staff in health facilities as well as the extremely low numbers of prepared HFs clearly indicated in the assessment made by GAH.Also according to the 2020 HNO, Abyei is in a dire need for nutritional intervention due to the high prevalence of GAM among PLWs and children U5. the high risk of floods and droughts in addition to the high risk of tribal conflicts may consequently cause disturbances in the nutritional wellbeing of Abyei residents. the fact that Abyei is considered a disputed area and the absence of interventions in the area, hinders the availability of nutrition services and treatment for malnutrition cases. This necessitates the importance of having nutrition interventions there. In the state of South Darfur, in East Jabal Marra locality, the total number of people in need of nutritional services is 19,565, this includes a prominent number of IDPs and Returnees whose status makes them more vulnerable to malnutrition thus more prone to AWDs, there is also a high risk of armed conflict. The GAM rate for children U5 is very high at a rate of 37.15% and there is a significantly high rate of SAM 17%. In Sonta locality, the HNO indicates a high risk of armed conflict, the GAM rate for PLW is 7.24%, while the GAM rate for children U-5 is considerably high at 19.14%, and the high risk of armed conflicts ensuing may further affect negatively these rates. In the state of West Kordofan, Nuhod locality, is identified as a flood-prone area which consequently lead to an increase in the risk of AWDs, and with the high risk of tribal conflicts gives it an overall rank of 5 regarding severity, the GAM rate for PLW and children U5 is high clearly indicating a need for nutritional interventions. Aldebab locality in accordance with the HNO the GAM rate for children U5 is high (22.58) indicating the importance of nutrition interventions and is ranked 4 on the nutrition severity scale.Keilak locality has a total of 6,281 people in need of nutritional services, which need to be addressed due to fact the Keilak has a high risk of AWD outbreak according to 2020 HNO/HRP, that could increase the rate of GAM, further worsening the health and nutrition situation.

2 - Activities / Outputs

The health interventions in Abyei, West Kordofan (WK)and South Darfor (SD) would be strengthened by GAH and the other partners in 2020. Currently we supporting the health facilities of Abyei, and would propose activities for health in WK and SD project since we are conducting protection interventions there.GAH would provide timely life-saving health services to people affected during and after the crisis besides to the basic package of health services that include the infection prevention, strengthening the expanded program of immunization EPI and raise the awareness of all health related issues Also would ensure the availability of essential medicines, supporting reproductive, mental and child health, the treatment of communicable ( Acute watery diseases) and non- communicable diseases, ensuring the availability of essential medicines and psychosocial support. The nutrition interventions will include the rehabilitation/equipping of OTP/SFP centers, referral and treatment for SAM children (boys and girls) U5 with or without complications in SCs/OTPs respectively. The activities will also include the referral and treatment of MAM children U5 and PLWs in the respective SFP centers. The project will also conduct community outreach activities for active case finding and follow up of detected cases, in addition to conducting screening for boys and girls U5 and PLWs in the target communities, and provision of micronutrient supplementation for prevention of malnutrition occurrence. The project will also conduct IYCF programs for mothers and/or caregivers, conduct awareness sessions for the target communities on various nutrition topics. Additionally, the project will train community volunteers (men and women) on CMAM and IYCF and will build the capacity of the medical staff (men and women) in the target OTPs, SFP centers and SCs through conducting trainings and/or refresher trainings on CMAM and IYCF

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

During this project’s design, the environmental effects and climate changes in the target states and locations, as provided in 2020 HRP/HNO, have been given pronounced considerations. The high risk of droughts in some of the target locations may cause additional disruption in the normal feeding practices of the residents of these areas, which may increase the GAM rates in the long run if no appropriate preventive and curative measures are put in place. Some of these areas also have a high risk of flooding and have suffered from it in the previous years of 2017, 2018. The floods also have a negative impact on the agriculture and livestock which may lead to disturbance in the positive nutritional behavior. The project aims to mitigate these negative effects on the nutritional health of the vulnerable communities through provision of micronutrient supplementation for prevention and treatment of malnourished cases. Additionally, floods may cause AWDs outbreaks that necessitate the presence of efficient and responsive health plan, as proposed in the activities. To ensure that the project will have no negative effects on the environment the design includes establishment of a medical waste management system, to guarantee safe disposal of the waste from the target HFs and nutritional centers. And for the rehabilitation, where fired bricks or local materials that use wood from trees and may cause an increase in deforestation rates, awareness sessions on importance of environment preservation and encouragement of tree planting and the impact of deforestation on the environment. Green fencing of the HFs and Nutrition centers will also be considered.To encourage the use of clean energy, the project considers using solar panels as sustainable sources of power.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

During the project design, segregation data influenced the choice of interventions and how they are implemented. The project ensures that community-based interventions are in place, and encourages community involvement, while ensuring gender sensitivity in this involvement. The project is also designed to include topics of protection that women face in the target communities such as FGM and early marriage and child protection topics in the awareness sessions that address these topics for the community, especially in any social events explicit to women, and conduct specific trainings for the community volunteers and medical staff on these serious topics. A special consideration is also given to create a separated and safe environment for women, young girls and children in the targeted health facilities while receiving their medical and nutritional services. In addition, the project design is created to allow people with disabilities easy use of the health facilities and nutrition centers, through construction of PwD-friendly outlets, and ensure Pwd inclusion in services provided.

7 - Accountability to Affected People and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse
7.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback and use it to improve your activities? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with community leaders with aid workers

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Before the beginning of the implementation, GAH will train all appointed staff on PSEA, and make sure all staff have signed the PSEA, code of conduct and child protection. The vendors associated with GAH will also sign a vendor code of conduct. GAH will also conduct awareness sessions for the community in PSEA. During the implementation of this project, interviews with beneficiaries on the quality of the services provided will be conducted, in addition to provision of complaint boxes in each of the targeted health facilities and nutrition centers, and informing the beneficiaries of the importance of reporting and how it will not have any consequences on them or on the services provided for them. The beneficiaries will also be informed on that these services are given to them without anything in return, and if any violations occur from our staff, they should be reported via a hotline number given in each HF and nutrition center. All the information and violations, should they occur, will be reported to the respective donors and agencies, after being rectified and immediate actions taken by GAH
