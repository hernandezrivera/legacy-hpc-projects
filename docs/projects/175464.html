1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project seeks to reduce the educational impacts on vulnerable and marginalized children effected by the cyclone, insecurity and displacement, and COVID-19 through the provision of distance learning materials, safe temporary learning spaces and access to PSS and PFA. Target populations will be vulnerable children displaced by insecurity, with a focus on girls and children with disabilities and communities of host families who continue to recover from cyclone Kenneth. Working in the province of Cabo Delgado 8176 primary school aged children currently residing in IDP centres will receive learning opportunities through distance learning packs and small classes in constructed temporary learning spaces, with teachers identified from community networks to deliver the lessons. In addition to this 30 trained faciliaitors will deliver PSS sessions, within target communitities identifying children who are in need of PFA and provision of available referral services if required. 40 teachers attached to the host communities will receive teaching materials and training workshops on PSS, CPiE, GBV, PSEA and DRR to strengthen reporting and referral mechanisms and ensure schools are safer places for students. Students and teachers will receive orientation sessions on COVID-19 prevention.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Cabo Delgado: Montepuez and Chiure• Provision of distance learning packs for 8176 IDP children in primary school grades 1-7. This will include 5000 girls and 300 children with disabilities to be used at home. Utilising already developed material by MINEDH, this will be tailored to be gender and disability sensitive and there will be coordination with provincial and district departments before implementation. • Provison of classes for grades 1-7 in temporary learning spaces within IDP centres for 2000 children. Sessions will run for 2/3 hours per day, ensuring adherence to COVID-19 restrictions. • Construction of 15 temporary learning spaces within IDP centres.• Training of 80 identified teachers in teaching methodologies, play based learning, inclusive education and EiE and CPiE. • Training of 30 facilitators on PSS and PFA and COVID-19 prevention measures to deliver PSS sessions in target communities with initial mapping of available services and referral pathway • Provision of learning and recreation materials for 2000 cyclone affected children, targeting 1500 girls and 100 children with disabilities, supporting reengagement with learning and enrolment in schools once opened. • Training of 40 teachers and 20 school council members on PSS, CPiE, GBV, PSEA, DRR and COVID-19, ensuring strengthened protection mechanisms and support for school councils to develop a basic school emergency plan.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

All staff working on this project, from both partners, are required to have received training on safeguarding and child protection and adhere to organisation policy prior to conducting any field visits or contact with beneficiaries. In turn beneficiaries will have access to a complaints and feedback mechanism, with promotion of the ‘linha verde’ line. Street Child has extensive experience of delivering child protection in emergency projects, working in protracted crises through operations in DRC, Afghanistan, Cameroon and North East Nigeria. Facilitators of PSS and PFA will be trained to a high standard, and align with the do no harm principle. Available services and referral mechanisms will be identified prior to the project starting. The project components and activities will be delivered through coordination with community leaders and engagement with community ensuring transparency and accountability, with a partner focal point identified for complaints and feedback.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

In May 2020, Street Child conducted a rapid needs assessments, including 4 districts of Cabo Delgado, Metuge, Montepuez, Ancuabe and Mecufi, incorporating host communities. The results showed that 35% of respondents reported that children did not have access to learning due to school closures and the restrictions put in place; in Metuge this figure increased to 48%. Of those who were accessing education 36% reported this was through textbook and workbook instruction, while only 4% reported access to radio instruction. Initial community engagement with target areas will take place to introduce the projects. Community leaders and key community members will also be consulted to finalise the vulnerability criteria for selection of beneficiaries for the project. Each community will also identify a preferred complaints and reporting mechanism that will be located in each project area, either through face to face reporting or a complaints box that will be checked on a weekly basis. Coordination with district education department will also take place for identification of teachers and in consultation for teacher training content

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

This project will target a minumum of 350 children with disability, to access distance learning materials and safe spaces. These materials will be based on the available resources produced by MINEDH and adapted to the needs of children with disabilities. There is provision of access to temporary learning spaces with trained teachers on inclusive education, which in turn will promote and support integration into formal schooling. Identification of both children with disabilities and the specific disability will be determined through a baseline community assessment and in consultation with district departments and community members. This assessment will be conducted using the Washington group tool.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Disaster risk reduction will be incorporated into the teacher training for cyclone affected communities and will require the participation of the school council members. They will be supported to develop a basic school emergency plan in order to respond to disasters, identify risks and dangers and prevent and mitigate their impact, promoting a sustainable culture of prevention and resilience. In addition there will be promotion of community advocacy on DRR through school council engagement.
