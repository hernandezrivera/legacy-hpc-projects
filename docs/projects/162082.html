Project Summary

This project comprises 3 components, in alignment with the Shelter/NFI sector's objectives: Transitional Shelter Assistance (first phase), Transitional Shelter Assistance (second phase), and Emergency Shelter Assistance in Camps 8E and 8W. It follows from World Concern's Transitional Shelter Assistance project in 2019, and its role as focal point for shelter emergency response in Camp 8E. The project plans to assist 4,000 households over 12 months. The activity is supported by IOM through pipeline materials for all shelter materials.

Needs

Rohingya continue to live in congested and poor shelter conditions in Kutupalong Mega Camp. Camp 8W TSA coverage remains low with significant unmet needs and existing shelters in poor condition. World Concern has previously worked in Camps 8E and 8W in 2019, so it would be an ideal location for TSA 2, as community relationships are already established. Existing shelter materials provided to the displaced population and improvements in camp infrastructure remain insufficient for protection against adverse weather events. Recent monsoon season had weakened the shelters. Untreated bamboo has deteriorated due to beetle infestation. Female key informants emphasized the discomfort in existing shelters due to heat and humidity, resulting from lack of ventilation and materials used. Families’ main concerns regarding their current shelters were related to durability of shelters, volume and quality of materials, heat and lack of ventilation and privacy.

Activities or outputs

Support for construction of 2,000 TSA1 in Camp 8W, 750 TSA2 in Camp 8W & 1,250 TSA2 in Camp 8E, plus catchment Focal point for Moderate & Major event only: Full-time emergency response capacity to respond with ESK –all with IOM pipeline materials.- Support for Community engagement: Community mobilizers to mobilize & communicate with HHs.- Registration: volunteers will help the enumerators identify each house & each head of HH on beneficiary lists. Assisted HHs registered for transitional shelter participation, training, distribution of materials, & construction supervisions- Distribution: volunteers will ensure that everyone in their sub-block attended the distribution, was served & distribution ran smoothly. - Construction: volunteers will visit each family to make sure they were motivated to build. - Monitoring: Of all HHs to ensure construction & quality including EVI HHs Social cohesion: Host community carpenters & volunteers will be engaged- Improving social cohesion through working together with Rohingya, building social bonds & income for unskilled & semi-skilled host community workers (helping mitigate economic pressures within the host community that contribute to tension)- Training of selected community members, involving women, men, elderly, child-headed HHs. Technical training of carpenters & key HH members (as selected by each HH) on TSA & disaster risk reduction, separately for Women, Men, child-headed HHs. Child-headed HHs & elderly persons with limited mobility will likely be selected as EVI HHs & provided full assistance to construct. HHs may not qualify as EVI but experience barriers to access –community mobilizers will identify & respond to these barriers- eg separate trainings, direct assistance & case-by-case consideration (adjusting training, inclusion as EVI, intensive site supervision etc)- Information dissemination, feedback, consultations: Various formats – through gender/age disaggregated FGD & KII, HH visits & construction supervision, meetings with traditional (mahjee) & GoB leadership (CiC, SMS), feedback boxes, trainings & meetings. Efforts will be made to recruit both male & female staff, to assist access to supported HHs in a gender-appropriate way. These inputs will inform project activities, which will be adjusted according to needs of different population groups (men, women, boys, girls, elderly men & women)- IOM materials pipeline: Will be used for all materials, inclusive of TSA1, TSA2 & Emergency Shelter Kits, including treated bamboo.- Voucher system for materials selection & distribution: Providing more choice & enabling supported HHs to decide which items they need most, a voucher system will be used. A ‘menu’ of options will be provided to each HH – allocation of points can be used on various items. Suggested package options will be provided for guidance- Construction & supervision: Including complete direct construction of EVI HHs by carpenters, supervision for non-EVI HHs- Post Distribution Monitoring survey after construction is complete- Assessment, training & provision of Emergency Shelter kit: in case of damage/destroyed shelter- Site improvements: Community mobilizers will review the shelter sites against a checklist, (topography, size, drainage, location etc) and recommend/advise/assist on site improvements
