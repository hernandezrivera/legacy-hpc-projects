Project Summary

In the North West and South West is now entering its fifth year of crisis that has led to massive population displacements and an increased vulnerability of people. An additional 2.3 million people are estimated to be in need of humanitarian assistance due to the impact of COVID-19, bring the total number of people in need from 3.9 million prior to the COVID-19 outbreak to 6.2 million.
Women and children are the most affected . Violence against women and girls is a preoccupying reality in Cameroon. Rates and severity of domestic violence, including sexual violence has increased tremendously. .
GBV Siterep reports indicates that 43 women and girls and 4 males raped cases are reported every six months The Gender Based Violence Sub-Sector Working Group (GBVSWG) 2018 report briefings have indicated a focus towards the physical and sexual abuse of women. This is attributed to the tendency that such women are subjected to less or no means of assessing service centers to report such acts.
The above statistics and others obtained through various means are just a part of the gruesome effects of the crisis on women and girls especially gender based violence. Hence, there is an inevitable need to look into and take positive action to prevent, mitigate and reduce the effects of this crisis in this domain.
This project intends to improve on Economic and mental wellbeing of 400 GBV survivors in Bui ad Donga Mantung before the end of 2021.

1 - Besoins / Needs

GBV is not only a fundamental violation of human rights, but leaves deep scars on societies that it affects, both in terms of psychosocial trauma, community cohesion, stigmatization of survivors, and development outcomes. The threat of Gender Based Violence (GBV) amplified by the conflict in the North West and South West Region of Cameroon, and even aggravated by the occurrence of the COVID-19 pandemic has underscored the findings by numerous scholars throughout the world. Occurrence of GBV is exacerbated when emergency hits. This project, includes prevention, mitigation and response in terms of case management, by comprehensive implementation of Psychosocial support, Creation of safe spaces for GBV victims in Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions, Reinforce the capacity of Community based focal points to provide psychosocial first aid to GBV survivors in these areas, Provide livelihood support to GBV survivors, not leaving out COVID -19 preventive measures. Hence the project will;Increase access to a comprehensive and well-coordinated GBV response services including livelihood support for survivors. ACTIVITIES1. Sensitize the population of Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions GBV on Building the capacity of 5 Community based focal points to sensitize their communities on GBV, Track GBV cases and Direct to safe spaces in Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions• Recruit and train 5 focal points for the 5 targeted Sub Divisions • Sensitization on GBV causes, effect , prevention and how GBV survivors can access assistance.• Track and refer GBV cases 2. Engage 200 men and boy in the fight against GBV in Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions by the end of 2021• Organizing focus group discussion with men and boys on GVB were in they are giving room to bring in their own ideas on how they can help reduce GBV by the end of 2021• Sensitization talks to men in different men groups on the importance of them taking up assisting roles at home.3. Provide 5well protected safe spaces to benefit atleast 400 GBV survivors in Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions for 12 months • Identify a secured accessible safe spaces for GBV Survivors in each Sub divisions • Equip the safe spaces with beds, tables, chairs , Television set, Musical set, etc• Training on varying life skills (Pedicure/ Manicure, Cake making and other pastries, beads making, reusable pads, face mask )• Support Survivors with startup materials • Carryout counseling sessions with the GBV survivorsAt the end of the project we hope to realize the following results;- 50,000 people sensitized on causes, effect , prevention and how GBV survivors can access assistance.- 200 men and boys become activist of fight against GBV in Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions - 400 GBV survivors rehabilitated in five safe spaces for a period of one year 2
