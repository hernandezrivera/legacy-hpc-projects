Project Summary

The Project is expected to strengthen the protection environment for internally displaced persons due to armed conflicts, droughts, climate shocks in targeted communities in Somalia through strengthening the protection and camp coordination mechanisms. The interventions of this proposed Project will culminate in a significant improvement in the lives of the vulnerable displaced communities as well as the affected communities. UNHCR will leverage its presence in IDPs sites and through its community links at the site level will contribute to the CCCM reports through the provision of available site profiling information to the CCCM sector

Project Activities

The proposed action will focus on maintaining the capacity to conduct immediate protection and assessments in situations of humanitarian crisis and inform Protection actors and UNHCR of the emergency protection needs. The identified gaps and needs will be shared with relevant sectors for immediate response. Through the assessments, the target communities get empowered to report their emerging needs. Community leaders from the targeted communities will be reached out to report on various protection concerns through the different mechanisms. UNHCR will establish dynamic and mobile team to regularly monitor the situation of IDPs and vulnerable host communities. The project will also assist the local authorities, community leaders in age-gender desegregated enlistment of the displaced population taking into account the vulnerabilities of women and children as well as persons with limited mobility due to disability or age. To respond to all these eventualities faced by the most vulnerable persons living in the camps, UNHCR extends its protection Programme in partnership with its implementing partners towards enhancing camp management, camp coordination, and promoting the dignity of conflict-affected people. UNHCR focuses on creating governance structures and mechanisms to ensure “meaningful access to the services in IDPs settlements. Communication channels will be enhanced to foster information sharing in the camps with other service providers in the targeted areas. The project will develop service mapping mechanisms to address COVID risk and promote response, the population movements, trends of new arrivals, exiting individuals, and returnees in the IDP camps. This will be enhanced by engaging focal points in the camps. They will conduct service mapping and record all the essential needs/gaps in the camps. UNHCR is seeking a partner to support in implementing these programmes. The selected partners should demonstrate capacity to work in a security and politically sensitive environment. The following activities will be implemented throughout Somalia: • Implement community-based activities to promote community cohesion andrecovery.• Support communities in IDP sites towards self-reliance in locations where prospects of obtaining land rights are achievable• Mapping of existing community structures• Identify and support governance structures, encouraging participation of all different segments of the displaced population and, as relevant, from the host communities•Support for minimizing environmental impact provided•Information management systems, incl. needs assessment and monitoring, are integrated across camps•Site selection, site planning, and site monitoring/implementation conducted against UNHCR and/or SPHERE standards•CCCM coordination mechanisms are established/reinforced at appropriate levels•Site level information is available and shared with stakeholders •Management of sites and settlements is improved•Improve community participation, living conditions, and safe access to services and assistance in selected sites •Involvement and participation of the affected community is ensured/ increased •Affected populations living in sites and settlements have the information they need to access services Conduct COVID-19 awareness sessions for IDP populations to emphasis physical distancing procedures and local authority policy minimizing the number of individuals while targeting community members, religious leaders, camp management committee members (CMCs) and not leaving behind persons with disabilities)Service mapping and site monitoring at the site-level, delivering information related to COVID-19 Distribution of hygiene kits to reduce the risk of COVID

Mitigation Measures

Ensure prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse is mainstreamed across the whole project cycles and that partners and community leadership structures are also aware of their obligation in this regard. Communities will be sensitized on the complaint mechanism to ensure high standards of accountability are maintained and to meet the policy of zero tolerance to PSEA. With regard to feedback and response, UNHCR will ensure that all formal and informal communications from IDPs and Host Community, both positive and negative, inform protection, assistance and solutions programming, and that corrective action is taken as appropriate. To achieve this, UNHCR will establish and maintain effective feedback systems (including comments, suggestions, and complaints), using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non-sensitive feedback. UNHCR has effective complain response mechanism and the key objectives include: Acknowledgement of community concern and protection of the beneficiaries’ rights and willingness; centralizing services to beneficiaries’ feedback and priority, Preventing and mitigating risks before they escalate; inclusive decision making and close interaction with the community and Promote total accountability of service delivery. The complaint mechanism contributes towards creating a safer environment for the most vulnerable members of a community as well as increasing their voice during the implementations of the project. ideally standard set of data is stored in UNHCR database, whereby only authorized users can access the database. Any confidential or sensitive information1 is restricted to authorized protection and/ or community services staff. Staff of UNHCR partners including FSPs only access basic data from the database to facilitate payments and assistance provision.

UNHCR will continue to support the Durable Solutions Initiative (DSI), aiming at strengthening ability of local government authorities to take over service delivery, plan across sectors, establish service referral mechanisms and reinforce formal structures in settlements. In this sense UNHCR will continue to support engagement with development and resilience actors by considering existing community-based planning processes and local government plans, as adequate. Durable solutions for IDPs were incorporated in the Somalia National Development Plan 2020-2024 (NDP-09) and draft National Durable Solutions Strategy. . These requires engagement of both development and humanitarian partners to address the causes of displacement and build community resilience to avoid further displacements and provide opportunity to displaced population to find a feasible durable solution however, with a limited convening authority for Durable solutions by a government and limited understanding of rights by local authorities The project will draw on the skills and resources that already exist in a displacement situation, particularly among persons of concern. This ensures that people have a say in matters related to their lives, while instilling in them a sense of dignity and building their capacities. Moreover, it represents a more efficient and sustainable use of the limited resources available and facilitates sustainability
