Project Summary

Almassar will work in consultation and coordination with MOE and will respond to any urgent and critical emergency for children affected by the conflict in Blue Nile state. Almassar will target vulnerable, undeserved children, teachers, PTAs and parents.
The activities of this project will include construction /Rehabilitation of 16 classrooms and 4 teachers offices in 4 schools using environmentally friendly soil-stabilized blocks (SSB), Construction /rehabilitation of 4 schools latrines each with 4 doors, provision of Hand washing facilities with water tanks for 4 schools and provision of plastic water tanks for drinking for the 4 schools. Almassar will provide Education Materials Supplies ,provision of 101 seating’s benches, targeting 5028 students in AlDamazin, Alrosieries ,ALtadaman and Alkurmuk localities (half of them female ), conducts training for 400 Teachers,200 PTAs(45% female) including psychosocial support and other life skills (health and hygiene Education,Disaster Risk Reduction, child protection ) for 5 days.
. Almassar aims to enhance the capacity of communities, the Parent-Teacher Association (PTAs
Almassar will closely monitor the ongoing activities through systematic reporting, observation, and documentation of lessons learned and success stories of change, based on the project plan and implemented interventions. Regular joint field visits with local education authorities will be conducted for monitoring, information collection and to assess the achieved results of agreed project objectives. MOE will be consulted and involved in monitoring processes. Monthly staff review meetings and activities tracking joined visits to the field will be made. Narrative and financial reports will be prepared and shared with Donors and MOE with all required information and documentation (monthly, quarterly,annually and final report).

1 - Needs

This project aims to ensure that school age children affected by emergencies (including conflict, natural hazards), particularly children with disabilities, continue to get access to safe and quality formal and non-formal education with targeted interventions. The 2020 HNO has identified 1,461,032 people in need of access to educationthe statistics show that around 80 per cent are children 4-17 years, and 30 per cent are adults including, youth, education personnel and community members, however Children in emergency affected communities are adding pressure to existing education facilities which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources, Moreover, the economic crisis impacted people’s lives and their purchasing power, thus, many more households cannot afford schooling costs. As a result, children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor
