Project Summary

Approximately 36% of the displaced population in West Darfur reported that they cannot access or affordable drinking water. Women and girls who mostly fetch water for households from far distances water sources are more susceptible to GBV. Lack of gender-segregated latrines, undignified, unlit, and insecure sanitation facilities pose an additional risk of GBV for girls and women. Menstrual hygiene management is often not prioritized, negatively impacting girls' attendance and enrollment in schools. Diarrhea kills one in ten children. A considerable number of children suffer from acute malnutrition, 50 per cent of which is associated with repeated diarrhea or worm infections related to poor WASH conditions. WASH needs for vulnerable populations are expected to increase due to un predictable conflicts, natural emergencies including dry spell, floods attributed to climate change. This project aims to provide clean water, sanitation services & hygiene services. The project targets IDPs & vulnerable host communities and IDPs in El Geneina (Assalam camp), Kereinik (Omdiwain&Aboja camps) & Foro Baranga Localities. The project targets a total of 48,440 people in the three target localities. Moreover, the project aims to provide protection to women & girls in addition to promote peace & co-existence between the communities who are going to share the provided WASH services and the project will contribute to mitigation of climate change problems.

1 - Needs

The general Analysis of Humanitarian Needs showed that round 74% of the population access basic water services from improved water sources and with a collection time less than 30 minutes. However, MSNA 2022 results indicate that 77% of the population have access to improved water services from improved water sources without factoring the collection time. About 28% of the water sources do not have sufficient water, and 25% of the water sources are not functioning. The increase (5 times especially the time those water yards were constructed, and community contribution were determine) in fuel prices means that fuel reliant water yards cannot be maintained as it is beyond the affordability of the affected people. Sanitation coverage in Sudan continues to be amongst the worst in the MENA region. Over 30 percent of the population have access to basic sanitation with prevalent open defecation practices at 33.3%. Displaced people, returnees, vulnerable residents and refugees are all affected by WASH constraints. The project targets West Darfur conflict effected population where the estimated number of people displaced from Kereneik following armed clashes between Arab nomads and the Massalit tribes between 22-25 April is estimated at around 125,000 people, according to inter-agency assessments carried out in April and May 2022. The people in rural areas have lost access to health and other basic services. Due to the insecurity, people’s movement remains limited in certain areas. Though the gap in WASH services is very huge. The assessed areas have no access to improved water sources. There is very poor sanitation in the areas of displacement, where it was reported that up to 97 per cent of the IDPs do not have access to sanitation facilities, posing a risk of water contamination. Water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) needs to be addressed as lifesaving act. 2 - Activities/Outputs

The project proposed (1) provision of water activities that will include; digging & installation of four hand-pumps, construction of two water yards with solar energy & accessories, rehabilitation of six hand-pumps, provision of water supply by water trucking (10KLTRS) =30 days x6 months; 1 truck @5000/month which covers 10 locations at an average =500 USD/location/month, conduction of water quality monitoring (10 hand pumps & 4 water yards for 8400 people and formation /activation & training of six water management committees. (2) provision of sanitation services that include; construction of eight communal latrines (6 pits -2 for people with disabilities), construction of 4 institutional (Health/nutrition/schools) centers latrines (4 stances per latrine) @30 person/latrine), (3) provision of hygiene & environmental health service that will include the manufacture & Provision of 10 sets of cleaning tools, PPE, disinfection and operational support for environmental cleaning at health/nutrition care facilities (6 labour plus cleaning kit includes PPEs), manufacturing & distribution of 24 hand washing facilities in communal set ups (communal toilets in camps, schools, markets, public places and health /nutrition facilities)., distribute HHs hygiene dignity kits for 4000 HHs (multi-purpose soap, Ibrigs, covered packets etc.), distribute menstrual hygiene materials (re-usable, 6 pads/ pkd for 12,000 women & girls), conduct 20 hygiene campaigns in communal set ups (Include Nutrition and Health facilities, schools, or other communal setups, conduct 4 vector control (equipment, chemicals, labour) and facilitate the formation & training of four hygiene & environmental health committees.Al Massar NGO applies participatory approach that involves target population and all stakeholders in the project design, planning, implementing and monitoring. The good practices & lessons will be shared with all actors & donor. The project will apply transparency & accountability, where a feedback mechanism is going to be adopted and complaint boxes are going to be located easily to reach & to access. Quarter project review is also going to be adopted to allow the early addressing of any deviations.
