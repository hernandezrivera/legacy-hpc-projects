Needs

Longstanding grievances in the Anglophone community in the Northwest (NW) and Southwest (SW) regions due to marginalization by the francophone-dominated government escalated into widespread protests and strikes in late 2016. Conflict escalated in the second quarter of 2018, with 239 reported fatalities in Northwest and Southwest regions as of July 2018.According to the data collected from the Ministry of Public Health, in the NW region, 14 health facilities are completely damaged in seven health districts, including two district hospitals. Twenty-one health facilities are closed and 126 health workers have deserted health facilities. In the SW region, a health district was completely deserted by health staff, two health facilities destroyed and 35 others damaged. The disruption of health services has also impacted on regional disease surveillance capacities.Weekly epidemiological bulletins show a gradual decrease in surveillance data completeness in both the NW and SW regions and have now reached critical low levels. According to the South West Regional delegate of public Health on the socio-political impact of the crisis in the SW revealed that out of the 18 health districts in the region, only 01 health district (Limbé Health district) where the security risk is low. Priority needs identified are:- Set up of mobile clinics- lack of the Health Personnel for patients and for psychological and mental trauma patients ;- lack of trauma kits for rapid care of severe injuries;- lack of medicines and other inputs for the response to potential outbreak’s diseases;- Low vaccination coverage rate;- Low level of community activities for the awareness about health risks and prevention of potential outbreaks;- Insufficient access of women, girls and children to health cares.
