Project Summary

The ongoing conflict has also further affected the health system with only 30 per cent of health facilities remaining functional in Borno State, where malaria is endemic and cholera and other diseases affect the population regularly and are often life-threatening
The last Nutrition surveillance was conducted between the month of May and June 2018 has highlighted a downwards deterioration in GAM rates between round 4 to round 5, from 6.0 (4.0- 8.9 95% CI) to 8.8 (6.4-13.8 95% CI) in East Borno (Bama, Dikwa, Gwoza, Kala/Balge and Dikwa LGAs) and from 6.9 (4.8-10.0 95% CI) to 10.7 (7.4-15.2 95% CI) in Northern Borno (Mobbar, Kukawa and Nganzai LGAs). This may be attributed to the high influx of new arrivals in the last few months and the high malnutrition rates among newly arrived children as they have been deprived of humanitarian assistance for several years
Recent SMART nutrition surveys conducted by both SCI (in Kaga, Jere, Konduga and Magumeri LGAs) and UNICEF (in Bama LGA) in August 2018 indicated poor prevailing nutrition situation with GAM rates estimated at 15.7% (12.2-20.0 95% CI) and 7.8 % (37.5% per cent in reception sites among newly arrived children). The observed high level of GAM among newly arrived children from hard to reach areas has also been seen through the periodic MUAC assessment conducted systematically by the nutrition sector partner.
The Nutrition sector projection for 2019 has identified about 2.248M people in need of some sort of nutrition assistance across Borno and Yobe state and the majority of them are IDPs, Returnees and communities living in hard to reach areas.

Needs

Recent SMART nutrition surveys conducted by both SCI (in Kaga, Jere, Konduga and Magumeri LGAs) and UNICEF (in Bama LGA) in August 2018 indicated poor prevailing nutrition situation with GAM rates estimated at 15.7% (12.2-20.0 95% CI) and 7.8 % (37.5% per cent in reception sites among newly arrived children). The observed high level of GAM among newly arrived children from hard to reach areas has also been seen through the periodic MUAC assessment conducted systematically by the nutrition sector partner. Action against hunger is providing Community based and inpatient Nutrition and health services for SAM, CMAM, IYCF treatment as well contribution to emergency humanitarian efforts to build nutrition security.

Activities

To achieve the proposed project objective below activities will be carried out:- Conduct MUAC screening and referral of SAM cases- Treatment of SAM with or without medical complications- Support training of key health and nutrition providers in CMAM including both inpatient and outpatient care;- Support joint nutrition situation monitoring and analysis;- Implement Nutrition SMART survey in collaboration with the SMoH nutrition focal points and the Nutrition sector coordination team;- Conduct CMAM program coverage assessment in the targeted areas- Support Nutrition sector plan and focus through the active participation in the sector coordination meeting- Implement IYCF interventions through the use of Mother support groups and Mother Care Groups approaches- Support the implementation of the micronutrient supplementation
