Project Summary

The League was founded to coordinate the multiple CSOs present on the Syrian territory. It intends to undertake a leadership role in the defense of the aspirations of the Syrian civil society by rebuilding and developing its capacities. The League aims to become a balanced and harmonized representation of the Syrian humanitarian workers and civil society and support it through crowdfunding and advocacy for its main issues. Moreover, the League seeks to establish a network of Syrian humanitarian workers and organizations dealing with Syrian affairs in order to support nascent initiatives and organizations by backing and developing their projects, pairing them with international partners, and exchanging expertise between operators.
in the last 2 years, the League was the main partners for many coordination mechanisms, Also the League coordinated more than 5 humanitarian elections for Humanitarian platforms like (HLG-SSG-ISG-ICCG) between all Syrian NGOs, and conducted 4 elections for HF Advisory Boards in cooperating with HF and OCHA team, moreover, the league conducted more than 15 technical workshops and coordinated 30 meetings with the humanitarian leaders with OCHA, NGO forum, and other stakeholders.
In the first phase of the project, the League will conduct a needs assessment of the networks within and outside the League to identify the greatest needs for strengthening and enabling network abilities.
Based on the results of the needs assessment, the League will provide specialized technical training to the networks to increase their capacities in coordination, advocacy, and civil society affairs.
In the second phase, the League will coordinate monthly periodic meetings of all Syrian institutions and NGOs, to explain the latest developments of the field and humanitarian operations in Syria and the latest developments of the geographical maps and the need for humanitarian intervention, to guide organizations to the areas most in need.
Also during the project, the League will hold 4 local conferences to raise awareness among civil society to reach effective participation in international conferences like (Geneva - Brussels ...).
The League will also coordinate the election operations of the humanitarian platforms and the Humanitarian Fund (OCHA - HF) Advisory Board Committee and will follow up the work of the candidates after they elected to represent the Syrians in these platforms through conducting regular meetings between representatives and Syrian NGOs and sharing information and the minutes of meetings.
In addition, the project will include a technical support platform (like a database) for the Syrian Network League (SNL), where the League will launch its official website to be a comprehensive and powerful electronic platform for all Syrian civil society networks and organizations.to enhance the mechanisms of sharing information among Syrian institutions

4 - Needs

The Lack of an umbrella or coordination body that brings Syrian institutions and networks to represent them locally and globally, and the weaknesses in coordination mechanisms, advocacy, exchange of information and exchange of experiences between Syrian organizations and networks, and the large number of Syrian organizations, which leads to the difficulty of reaching among them internally or communicate with them by the UN-OCHA And global coordinating platforms.

5 - Implementation of activities

1- In the first 2 months will conduct an OCA ( Organizational capacity Assessment ) in cooperating with UNDP and OCHA for all Syrian Networks and Organizations to know what kind of trainings and intervention will be, like these trainings :- Organize 5-days training in Governance - Organize 5-days training in News editing- Organize 6-days training in Advocacy - Identify 4 sectors of focus (health, education, human rights, and civil society work) to analyze the performance and evolution of the sector and the areas for improvement; each sector will be researched by groups of 15 people to settle polices and working methods2- Conduct monthly periodic meetings of all Syrian institutions and NGOs, to explain the latest developments of the field and humanitarian operations in Syria and the latest developments of the geographical maps and the need for humanitarian intervention.3 - Organize and hold 4 local conferences and workshops to raise awareness among civil society to reach effective participation in international conferences like (Geneva - Brussels ...).4- Organize and coordinate 4 elections for Humanitarian platforms and HF Advisory Board5- Establish the database for the Syrian Networks and NGOs including the website for the League.
