Project Summary

This project aims in a duration of nine month at providing emergency energy to one pumping station feeding three localities in El Minieh Dinnieh. Beshtayel, Mrah El Sraj, and Bet Zod are three localities with 360 households and three public schools for 900 students. The localities with their schools were being provided water from one borehole and one pumping station that are managed by the North Lebanon Water Establishment (NLWE). With the fuel price increase, the NLWE has stopped the water pumping and hence the water service provision for these localities since the beginning of June 2022. Consequently, LebRelief wishes to install a solar system on an available area owned by the NLWE to feed the pumping station and provide water to 1600 residents subscribed with the water establishment and three public schools.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

LebRelief has a zero-tolerance policy towards all kinds of abuse. Employees, consultants, volunteers, community mobilizers and other related personnel sign the Code of Conduct and Ethics, which includes a specified contract clause on Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. Policies are explained during the introduction sessions to the employees and are repeated every year. Lebrelief's Protection Officers holds info sessions for all participating staff, for example workers in construction programmes, to explain all kinds of abuse, gender discrimination and hotline numbers are distribute. Lebrelief's training agenda includes and ensures all frontline staff can identify and act wisely on such incidents. It informs them where and how they can contact their team leaders and referrer it to one of our protection officers and referred to Lebrelief case workers if they can not interfere it is referred to another organization. The referral services earned by all our frontlines.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

LebRelief insures that stakeholders have access to the different channels that allow them to report issues of conduct, fraud and/or safeguarding to LebRelief’s works and complaints about breaches in policy and stakeholder dissatisfaction. These channels include the website, email, and hotline (in case the stakeholder preferred to stay anonymous) and in person. These channels are distributed via a hotline card. The social worker will make sure to distribute the hotline card during all 20 sessions conducted to the 300 right holders and during all meetings with the stakeholders. All project staff are trained to receive community feedback. At the start of the project activities, the staff will be reminded and updated on the Complaints Response Mechanism to tackle complaint response and ensure the right holders have the opportunity to raise and receive feedback on a complaint. These complaints are received via a dedicated phone line, and also in person by field staff.
