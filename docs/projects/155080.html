Objectives

1. Ensure effective, sufficient and equitable provision of life saving water and sanitation services for targeted men, women, boys and girls 2. Ensure that all targeted women, men, girls and boys have the means and are encouraged to adopt individual and collective measures increasing health seeking behaviors to mitigate public health risks for the well-being of the affected population 3. Ensure that all WASH assistance promotes protection, safety and dignity of targeted men, women, boys and girls with focus on sustainable resilience-building approaches

Needs

Bangladesh has welcomed over 921,000 forcibly-displaced Myanmar nationals (FDMN). Following the influx of the Rohingya refugees and their settlement in currently populated areas, the host communities has been affected in different dimensions; from the access to the basic services; use of the land in cultivation; and livelihood in general. In regards to WASH the water sources used by host communities to obtain drinking water have shrunk as the land is used by the refugees and converted to settlement, this also applies to safety of sanitation infrastructures. The existing lack of, and negative effect on, basic services within the host community contributes to tension between the host and refugee’s communities, something that has already been observed in the surrounded host communities.In Water Supply, Although there are huge numbers of tube well but many of them remains shallow wells which can be easily contaminated. While the access to water points varies, the average daily reported water consumption is low, estimated at less than 10 l/p/d. One of the main factors for this figure is the distance to the water point, others include limited access to water collection and storage containers, waiting times and queues, and the many steep slopes which people must climb up while carrying their load of water containers.WASH sector with the DPHE are condcuting water modelling to see the feasibility of exapanding the water network also the results are not yet came out but BRAC will remain seeking use of rain water harvesting to support the demand in both host community but also the refugees.In Sanaition and Hygiene promotion: the replacement of the emergency latrines and upgrading the semi permeamnat latrines are clear needs for the camp population to secure the good access to the sanotaion servuces and maitain the protection of the ground water form any possible contaibination.Hygiene promotion and general awareness remained a challenge bridge the hard components ( water supply and latrines) usage and practice, to do so, BRAC will work with the sector partners to implement innovative hygiene and awareness program where different community group will be engaged maintaining the feedback mechanism within this process.

Activities or outputs

• Around 10% of the sector target will be supplied through water trucking and up to 25% in peak dry season (especially March-Apr) when some of the surface water sources and shallow tube-wells dries up. • Construction and equipment of deep tube-wells with lift pump like deep TARA or IMK2/3s• Decommissioning of some water points based on a risks analysis• Emergency phase bulk water treatment- batch treatment with coagulation/flocculation and chlorination when surface water is the water source• Operation and maintenance – Minor and major repairs of hand-pumps and tube wells / Water Point Attendant trainings and equipment’s including for bucket chlorination if needed• Promotion and provision of household water treatments in all settlements with the distribution of coagulation or disinfection products – AQUATABS will be the preferred options, excepted if identified iron level undermine the effectiveness of the products as well as Analysis of taste preferences and acceptability of centrally chlorinated systems to inform dosing parameters• Care and maintenance of all communal latrines• Decommissioning of latrines based on risks analysis• Provision of specific items for specific needs (E.g: Potties for children, bedpans for elder people…)• Training and support to outreach workers to mobilize different sections of the targeted population and encourage positive health and hygiene behaviors through participatory methodologies (support includes incentives/ training equipment’s/visibility tools and IEC materials to implement activities)• Supporting outreach activities to maintain or increase health-seeking behaviors at household level and community level. This includes working with all different segments of the population• Establishment of a monitoring system to monitor the performance of partners against the 5-minimum commitments• Access to preferred products, information and supportive facilities for menstrual hygiene management for women and girls• Construction and maintenance of communal/HH/shared bathing facilities • Development of a comprehensive mathematical water resource modelling that includes ground water vulnerability and alternatives to ground water such as rain and surface water feasibility. Surveys and feasibility assessments of surface and rainwater sources to facilitate sustainable water supply and reduce groundwater depletion• Provision of appropriate water storage and transportation containers at household level through various distribution modalities such as cash, vouchers and in-kind• Engagement of affected communities in planning, design, implementation and O&M of water infrastructure• "Installation of solid waste collection bins on at least every hillock• Construction, operation and maintenance of intermediary disposal sites"• Host Communities - Implementation of a modified CLTS strategy • Capacity building activities for the WASH sector partners to be able to implement efficient hygiene promotion programs. This includes development of guidelines, Trainings of Trainers…• Engaging communities in the care and basic maintenance of WASH facilities • Communication campaigns based on priority public health risks and in collaboration with Communicating with Communities (CWC) working group, including radio, shows, events etc. • Maintaining a common platform to collate WASH related information on behavior and preferences to inform WASH partners programing, including systems to collect qualitative data (feedback mechanisms, FDGs…)• Technical oversight of hygiene promotion and community mobilization intervention to scale up intervention and consolidate lessons learnt to improve existing policies and influence on decision makers • Training of communities to monitor, report, and provide feedback on WASH disease incidence and risk behaviour• Training of outreach workers to mobilise different sections of the targeted population and encourage health seeking behaviour (Facilitation/ communications skills /
