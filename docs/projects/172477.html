Project Summary

The project targets 100 P3 schools in Harare (Chitungwiza and Harare Central districts), Rushinga and Gwanda reaching 8,500 ECD learners. It includes supporting schools with distribution and facilitation of ECD kits; Early Childhood Stimulation training for caregivers; emergency response planning; and nutrition, hygiene and COVID-19 infection, prevention and control in support of school feeding.
Due to the closure of schools from March 2020, ECD learners, particularly in the rural community, have been out of schools for over 8 months. This has implications on the rate of children who will enrol back in school, as it has been noted that vulnerable children are less likely to return to school the longer they are out of school. To support schools to stimulate enrolment the project will support schools with ECD kits as schools have little to no access to toys and other learning materials to promote early stimulation and development. Further, due to the amount of time that children have spent out of school it is important for early learners to receive education both at school and at home to ensure preparedness for Grade 1, thus the need for Early Childhood Stimulation for ECD A caregivers. In addition, schools will be supported to develop emergency response plans and will review School Development Plans to ensure that these are resourced. To support school feeding, caregivers will be mobilized and trained in nutrition, hygiene and COVID-19 infection, prevention and control (IPC) guidelines to ensure safe food preparation. Food for the school feeding program will come from the Department of Social Development supplemented by the community and school based nutrition gardens in the form of relish. This activity will be aligned to the Alternative School Feeding Working Group.

1 - Activities

[i] Baseline and mid terms assessments – these will be conducted at the beginning of the project and midway through project implementation to ensure that the project addresses relevant issues and flashes out emerging needs in the midst of implementation. These assessments will be done by the partner involving all relevant stakeholders including the Ministry of Education at all levels, schools, community members and children. [ii] Distribution and facilitating and use of 100 ECD kits in emergencies – With the reopening of schools for ECD learners in November 2020 there us an opportunity for the proposed project to support p3 schools with ECD kits utilizing ECD class rooms as spaces for use of ECD kits to stimulate children’s development, in addition to encouraging early learning. Teachers will be oriented on the correct administration of the kits and sensitised on standard operating procedures on how to use the kits whilst observing COVID-19 infection, prevention and control (IPC) protocol. [iii] Training of 3,000 ECD A caregivers in Early Childhood Stimulation (ECS) - these will be trained by trained community volunteers who will serve as facilitators of 11 ECS sessions on various issues including early childhood development milestones, how to utilise locally available resources to make developmentally stimulating toys and games, communication and amongst other ECS session topics. Caregivers will be trained in small clusters until their graduation. [iv] Support the development of emergency response plans and review of School Development Plans (SDPs) for 100 schools – the project will support schools to develop emergency response plans at school level and ensure that ECD is included in any emergency drills and other response systems. In addition, the project will support the review of SDPs to ensure that these emergency plans are resourced for their effective implementation. [v] Nutrition and hygiene training for 500 caregivers volunteering in school feeding – this will include mobilisation and capacity building of caregivers involved in school feeding in nutrition, hygiene and COVID-19 IPC guidelines. These will be trained in conjunction with local health personnel who will monitor adherence to standards set during trainings post training, for sustainability.

2 - Indicators

The project will feed into the following cluster specific objectives: To reduce by 60% the proportion of children not attending school during humanitarian emergencies in priority districts. To reduce by 60% the proportion of children not receiving school feeding during humanitarian emergencies in priority districts. The project specific indicators are:# and % of early learners enrolled back into school; and# and % of early learners receiving school feeding School registers completed daily by school teachers will be used to track the enrolment of children on a monthly basis, whilst the percentage of children enrolled back into school will be determined through comparison to enrolment pre-COVID-19 pandemic. The organisation will also make use of an online data collection platform to keep track of enrolment given the high volume of data to be collected. In addition, monitoring of the districts in conjunction with the District Schools Inspectors will determine the schools supported by school feeding and verify children who have been reached with school feeding as a result. Project results will be shared with the Ministry, the Education Cluster and other relevant stakeholders. In addition, feedback will be provided to schools, community caregivers and local leadership through feedback platforms such as SDC and community meetings.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

The project was designed based on evidence from consultations with District Schools Inspectors, School Development Committee members, local leadership and community caregivers particularly on issues such as COVID-19 preparedness when it comes to school feeding and the school's preparedness upon the opening of schools after the national lockdown due to COVID-19. Concerns were also raised around the expectation of low enrolment of children upon schools opening and the need for continued community support in catch up strategies to learning. Based on this feedback the organisation designed a project set up to address these emerging issues. The project itself will continue to incorporate feedback from communities on project progress and impact through existent feedback structures such as SDC and community meetings, as well as utilising district monitoring visits to discuss key issues as they emerge. Other feedback mechanisms used for local communities will include anonymous reporting mechanisms through established focal persons for the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, suggestion boxes set up in communities and wide sharing of the organisation's WhatsApp platform which is directly accessible to senior management and the director.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will limit the use of paper based work making use of electronic options to collecting data where resources permit. Further, trainings and other community gatherings will be done taking into consideration environmental laws on material disposal. In addition, a clustered approach to project activities will be done for he efficient use of fuels and other environmental resources.
