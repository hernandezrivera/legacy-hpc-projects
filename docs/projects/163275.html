Project Summary

The NW/SW Regions of Cameroon (Southern Cameroons) are in armed conflict with the Cameroons central government since November 2016. The on-going conflict has left hundreds of thousands homeless and or displaced. The most vulnerable of whom are the children who are exposed to displacement, loss of childhood, torture and exposure to violence, which are all contributing factors leading to poor mental health and . Schools have been shot down for three years for 80% of the region.
MWDA believes that comprehensive education ie creating a learning environment to teach the basic languages, numeracy, science and vocational skills is capable of healing lives amd creating a bright future.
The over all activities of the project is organising education opportunities through creation of learning centers in areas with no formal schools for IDPs, returnees and hosts community children 4- 17years. These programs content will include literacy and vocational skills. The project directly supports 2000 beneficiaries IDPs, returnees, and host community children including adolescent girls, and youths from 3 Divisions Lebialem , Fako, Meme ,South Cameroons with a future of replicating.
MWDA is soliciting funding of 31,500,000 million francs to carry out this life saving project.

Besoins / Needs

Education is a basic human right. This is underscored in human rights treaties and international agreements; most notably, the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child, which provides the foundation for UNICEF work in education as in other sectors.The NW/SW Regions of Cameroon (Southern Cameroons) armed conflict has led to the displacement of over 600000 people and to the closure of over 80% of formal schools, resulting in cumulative deficits that are detrimental to education outcomes and the well-being of children. Mwda Surveys shows that for 3 years- Over 20 000 children of age 4- 9 lost 3years of school hours and cannot read and write. - Children of age 4-9 cannot speak, greet or communicate in English. - Children of 10-17 years have lost their basic knowledge of language ,science and numeracy that they had been introduced to three years ago. -This has resulted in compromised futures as instead of looking forward to the next educational level they are being recruited into armed groups. There is a vicious cycle between education, inequality and violent conflict.. Violent conflict is in turn associated with loss of schooling and with education inequality including gender based violence. With this project MWDA is : (1) To provide equitable access to learning opportunities; (2) improved learning and Vocational skills for children 4-17 years; and (3) Remedial teaching through a non-formal education and alternative delivery models in the highly affected villages of Lebialem , Fako and Meme.
