Project Summary

The project focus on the promotion of child right. The project will work with children from disadvantaged background and forgotten communities. The project takes into consideration the best interest of the child such as: - Protection : Psychosocial assistance
- Learning : Approach breakfast club ,Approach comic strip
The aim of the project will be to set up a new monitoring system for children who are reintegrated socially and academically. This project will use the 'Breakfast Club' approach on 4 points:
- Reinforcement of school capacities to allow vulnerable children who do not have access to educational support for their families to benefit from assistance in the lessons learned
-Active participation: Provide a framework for expression and exchange for the child. every child has the right to come up with ideas and solutions that can be taken into account to meet these urgent and important needs.
-Food: Each child has the right to healthy and quality food to maintain his assiduity in learning.
- Psychosocial assistance:
1. Social and family reintegration of children: the therapy will consist of reintegrating the children benefiting from the project at the social and family level.
Psychosocial support for children: it will be a consist on assisting each child for a complete psychosocial reintegration.
2. Family and systemic support: aware that every individual does not exist alone, with therapy, it will be a matter of replacing the child and the young person in an ecosystem, in his family, in his system. In addition, the therapy will focus on the construction of the reality of the family system, this through the therapeutic communication of children.
The purpose of this system is to arouse curiosity, heighten the desire to learn and help them to adapt to their new environment since many are used to live in street life. It is our responsibility to help these children love school from an early age. The project will demonstrate a positive impact in reducing cases of school drop-out.
It is in this same logic that the activities will support the comic strip approach, which motivates children to stay on the school bench despite the vagaries of life. We believe that this comic book is a learning and awareness tool on child protection.
