4 - Needs

The devaluation of the SYP intensifies the existing precarious economic situation and is worsened by the impacts of COVID-19 on communities and markets. Between December 2019 and early March 2020, some 960,000 people were displaced in northwest Syria. Sanitation & Hygiene: According to NA reports access to sanitation facilities and appropriate levels of hygiene (i.e. poorly smelling latrines, lack of cleaners for latrines, rubbish disposal on the street) appears to be where the largest gap exists, most notably around sanitation systems in addition to access to latrines which are designed for people with disabilities (PwD). Hygiene kits have been provided previously but households are now in need of replenishments. Therefore, in addition to increased access to safe water, IHR will address a number of sanitation gaps which emerged from the needs assessment.IHR MEAL staff conducted need assessment in 11 camps,3 of them receiving wash services from IHR until now, by Interviews with 3Key Informant from Camps' management, survey a random sample of IDPs,10 from each camp cluster. The statistic results indicated that the population in camps was were 20,148 individuals 3,358 families. Respondents confirmed that during the past 30 the main source of water days was from the water trucks.55% of respondents were unable to distinguish between water of use and drinking in terms of conservation. Some practices were undertaken to ensure that drinking water was separated from water for hygiene use. IHR will target (12567) IDP's (2,460 Men, 2,720 Women, 3,755 Boys, 4,015 Girls) living in (11) settlements and camps and 200 p isolation center.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

This program is designed to contribute significantly to gender equality for several reasons. First and foremost according the last CCCM cluster report , the women and children are forming 79 % of residents in the camps , In regards toIHR field staff,will be from both men and women to facilitate the contact and cordinatiopn with woen and girlsIn terms this project IHR The equality and impartiality of access to the assistance will be ensured through the vulnerability assessment with priority given to the most vulnerable groups including, women, Girls,Covid 19 patients ,Person in IC and Home isolation , elderly people, GBV survivors, people with disabilities and people with special needs requiring urgent assistance. IHR believes that the success and sustain any project is depending on the stakeholder’s engagement from the design phase until handover the project so We take high consideration for the engagement of affected persons and local Authorities during designing this project. IHR adopted a two-way communication flow with affected communities (HHs, IDP, LC, Key person, Camps managers, Women Headed household ) through the most appropriate channels in both camps and IC(Including the persons in Home quarantine) as well as in the communities. This includes access information on available aid and other wash services and needs IHR will apply the protection mainstreaming mechanism, referral pathway, health pathway furthermore capacity building of all new and old staff about PSEA,Safguarding ,And working under covid conditions With better access to Protection, life saving Wash services for the entire population, women and girls will not be forced to travel long distances to access water and other wash service
