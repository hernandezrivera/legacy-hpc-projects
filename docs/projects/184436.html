Project Summary

Somalia remains a challenging context revealing quite protection challenges, the ongoing multi- layered conflicts and insecurity continue to heavily influence the situation throughout Somalia, impacting on access to affected populations and causing displacement. The situation is complex with wide variety of causes of displacement, armed groups and protection violations.
Somalia exists in a state of legal pluralism where customary law (Xeer), religious law (Sharia) and secular law operate. Among the three, Xeer is the dominant system to govern societal relations and serious crimes and there is weak enforcement of secular law, and most areas in south central, formal courts and government structures exist only in urban centres. The divisions between social groups, coupled with competition over scarce resources, fuel conflict as well as systemic discrimination and marginalization of groups across the country. Exclusion from receiving humanitarian assistance due to status, abilities or minority clan affiliation is a protection concern.
According to the JMCNA data, about 62 per cent out of the total population of IDPs and non-IDPs reported main safety and security concerns from different harms, physical threats, and discrimination in the area they are living, of which 35 per cent are suffering from physical harassment or violence, verbal harassment (8 per cent), sexual harassment or violence (7 per cent), being exploited (5 per cent), being recruited by armed groups (4 per cent) and being forcibly married at a young age (4 percent).
The recently released 2022 HNO identifies 4M people need of protection and for specifically for the general protection, 2.7 million are need, 2.3 M CP, 2.3M GBV and 2.7M for HLP. From January to August of 2021, PRMN reported conflict as the main driver of displacement, with over 413,000 displacements countrywide. Conflict and insecurity prevail in certain parts of the country, particularly in south and central Somalia, and results in particular protection challenges including physical attacks and threat to life, arbitrary arrest and detention, kidnapping, sexual assault, child recruitment, amongst others.
CESDO through this application is aiming to respond to the foresaid and identified need to address the acute protection needs of individuals facing life-threatening risks of abuse, violence, exploitation, injury, and severe distress by the end of 2022, while providing wide range of activities that are aimed at ensuring respect for the rights of all individuals, regardless of their age, gender or social, ethnic, national, religious or other background and in accordance with needs identified.
Some of the activities proposed under this HRP are:
General Protection: Protection Monitoring, Specialized Mental Health and Psychosocial Support services (MHPSS), strengthening the referral pathways, establishing of protection committees and training them on monitoring, reporting and response.
Child Protection: MHPSS, Individual case management services that meets children’s special needs, responses to children associated with armed forces and armed groups and integrated with their families, Identification, Tracing, and reunification of unaccompanied and separated children with their families, Training of CP actors and CBCPMs on mitigation, prevention and response to children, CFS, strengthening of the referral pathways and contributing to the CPIMS+ data provision.
Gender Based Violence (GBV): Provision of legal aid and access to justice for survivors of GBV, Cash assistance including at risk groups, Training of GBV actors on GBV prevention and response, Clinical management of rape, provision of recreational and livelihoods skills for GBV survivors and those at risk, PSS through community centres, Safe centres and One stop centres.
Housing, Lan and Property (HLP): eviction monitoring, reporting, response and advocacy, information, counselling and legal assistance services for HLP cases.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Given insecurity and the logistical challenges in Somalia, Cash-based assistance allows affected people to choose and buy their own needed response from local shops across the country, helping to sustain the local economy with ease in this critical time.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

Markets remains unstable and un-predictable in Somalia, particularly the south and central regions which always face recurrent conflicts, geo-political upheaval and severe drought and flooding. although there has been improvement in past five years in most of the regions, the situation can change any time. Under these circumstances it always important to conduct rapid assessments on the market not only the functionality but availability of the protection response especially the materials and in kind items to be procured. To minimize risks associated with market, CESDO is proposing multiple approaches (cash/in-kind) and opting to adapt which ever works. Rapid market assessment will be conducted prior any implementation to inform approaches to adapt.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

CESDO has strong capacity including human resources and systems to reduce risksanticipated. The management and the board have taken series steps to uphold theorganizational accountability, to that effect, comprehensive community accountabilityoutlining the community accountability and levels of engagement has been developed. This framework gives more role to the community particularly when it comes to project cycle management, the framework is community driven and community centered approach. It gives clear steps on how staff should engage communities in different levels including who, when and what. CESDO will use that framework for this project like any other project while maintaining the communities are involved in all the processes of the project. CESDO will hold familiarization meeting for the project stakeholders when this HRP is funded to ensure different people have their views about the project implementation, this will be followed by gathering meeting to provide comprehensive understanding to the communities of the project contents including the project coverage, activities, objectives, and role of different stakeholders. Ad hoc and specific meetings will be held with those with specific needs including persons affiliated with minority clans, women, girls and disable people to ensure their views are incorporated throughout the project cycle. CESCO will ensure the targeting of the project is done inclusively in consultation with respective stakeholders, set criteria’s will be developed to inform the different beneficiaries, selection protocols in line with the vulnerability assessment will be followed to avoid misuse and wrong targeting. Independent verifications will be carried out by CESDO Monitoring and Evaluation staffs. Proper community feedback and response mechanism accessible to everyone will be installed in all project locations to ensure everyone has the access to raise his/her voice when and where needed. The mechanism will be free and accessible to everyone and it will be mobilized to all community members regardless of their groups. This system will enable beneficiaries to have a direct access to a voice in the decision-making process confidentially. Regular monitoring will be carried out by CESDO management to review the accountability practices at field level as this will help management to understand the loopholes without relying on anyone. Furthermore, posters containing project information’s, CFM contact and executive directors' contactdetails will be installed in the different IDPs. Gender and Protection mainstreaming sessions will be incorporated in all implementations, at the same time CESDO will ensure that all infrastructures and other deliverables are designed in way that addresses the specific needs of persons living with disabilities, persons with minority clan affiliations, marginalized groups, girls, boys, women and people with specific needs.CESDO will intentionally target settlements inhabited by persons affiliated with minority clans, persons with disabilities, elderly persons and other vulnerable groups in its response while ensuring that they are consulted in every stages of the project and their feedbacks in cooperated into the project.During the consultation process, CESDO will ensure to organize sessions for persons affiliated with minority clans only to allow them to freely share their feedbacks and concerns.The CFM will be available for all persons and their feedbacks and responses will be solely guided by the need of the person.
