Project Summary

This project is going to improve the access of 23,000 children in 42 schools located in Idleb , Aleppo and Raqqa governorates to quality formal and non-formal education services by providing a package of education services that target the gaps and needs of the most vulnerable groups of children in 8 sub-districts in the camps and off camps.
This project is going to improve the access of 23,000 children(11730 boys / 11270 girls) to quality inclusive education and learning opportunities in safe and protective environment in 42 schools including 20480 children(10445 boys & 10035 girls)in formal education and 2520 children(1285 boys & 1234 girls)in non-formal education(1000 children in catch-up & 1520 in remedial) including 300 children with disabilities (143 boys & 157 girls) with disabilities through establishing 90 new classrooms in tents in the camps , conducting light repair to 150 classrooms, conducting light repair to wash facilities , providing 30 schools with school furniture , conducting 42 back to Learning campaigns in 42 different locations targeting 4200 beneficiaries(2100 children and 2100 caregivers and parents) , providing heating fuel and heaters to 42 schools , procuring and distributing 23,000 school bags and education kits to 23,000 children in 42 schools ,providing 42 schools with operational cost(drinkable water - cleaning tools & materials - buffet materials- stationery), providing transportation for 300 CWD in 42 schools, activating referral pathways from the schools to the Protection & Health specialized service centers , providing the 42 schools with safety equipment, paying the incentives to 1040 teachers(613 males & 427 females) and supporting staff working in the 42 schools in formal and non-formal education , providing light professional development package including training on (Child Protection Principles –Integration of Children with disabilities in Education Response - Modern Teaching Methodologies– Education in Emergencies ) , providing the 42 schools with recreational materials , printing 23,000 copies of formal education textbooks and 2520 non-formal textbooks , providing teaching materials kits to 42 schools , providing 23,000 children with school-based psychological in formal and non-formal , support conducting safety training to the children and teachers , conducting ASER , pre and EGRA as well as EGMA.
The catch-up classes will target the children aged 7-18 (any child who dropped out of school for one academic year starting from grade 2)
The remedial classes will target the children aged 6-18 (any child currently enrolled in formal schooling and at risk of dropping out)

4 - Needs

Basing on the HNO 2020, there is a total of 7-million people in need of education support, 86% of these people in need have acute and immediate need of humanitarian education assistance (severity of 4-5-6). Basing on JENA that conducted by SCI, ACU and 13 of Education Cluster members, the number of children between 6 – 18 years in NW of Syria within the assessed areas reached 1,712,468 children; and according to JENA 66% (1,130,229 children) are attending schools and 34% (582,239 children) are out of school. Basing on the rapid need assessment that SCA conducted in 8 sub-districts in Idleb, Aleppo and Raqqa Govern-orates in December 2019, there are 245 functioning schools, 42 schools out of them need support:First : The needs of children(6-17)years: 1- There are 23,000 children (11730 boys & 11270 girls) enrolled in formal education in 42 schools 2-All the children in the 42 schools need support with school bags and stationery kits 3- 88% of children in 42 schools need textbooks in formal education 4- 300 CWD need transportation to reach the schoolsThe needs of teachers: 1- There are 1040 teachers working without incentives in the 42 schools 2- 80% of teachers in the 42 schools need capacity building trainingThe needs of schools: 1- The 42 schools need support with teaching materials2- The 42 schools need recreational materials3- The 42 schools need support with safety equipment4- The 42 schools need support with running cost (drinking water - cleaning tools & materials - buffet materials, stationery for education personnel)5- The 42 schools need support with heating fuel and heaters during winterTh needs of rehabilitation1- There is a need to establish 90 new classrooms in tents in Dana sub-district 2- 150 classrooms need light rehabilitation in the 42 schools 3- The wash facilities in 30 schools need light rehabilitation

5 - Implementation of activities

Act 1.1: Provide formal education services to 23,000 children(11730 boys / 11270 girls ) in the host community and displaced children in the camps for 9 months from 1- 1 to 31-5 and from 1-9 to 31-12-2020 including the children with disabilities using (SIG) curriculum Act 1.2: Provide non-formal education to 2520 children (1000 children in catch-up & 1520 in remedial) using UMMAH curriculum Act 1.3: Establishing 90 new classrooms in Dana sub-district (in the camps) Act 1.4: conducting light repair to 150 classrooms taking into consideration the needs of CWD.Act 1.5: conducting light repair to wash facilities taking into consideration the needs of CWD and having separate entrances for girls and boys in line with Sphere StandardsAct 1.6: providing 30 schools with school furnitureAct 1.7: conducting 42 BTL targeting (2100 children and 2100 caregivers and parents) to raise the awareness of parents towards the importance of education and reach out to the OOSCAct 1.8: providing 140,200 liters of heating fuel and 400 heaters to 42 schoolsAct 1.9: Distributing 23,000 school bags and stationery kitsAct 1.10: providing 42 schools with operational cost for 12 months Act 1.11: providing transportation for 300 CWD Act 1.12: providing the 42 schools with safety equipmentAct 1.13: paying the incentives to 1040 teachers in 42 schools for 9 months in formal and 3 months in non-formal education. Act 1.14: providing light professional development to 1040 teachers in formal and non-formal education Act 1.15: providing 42 schools with recreational materials Act 1.16: printing 20480 copies of formal and 2520 non-formal textbooks Act 1.17: providing teaching materials kits to 42 schools Act 1.18: providing 23,000 children with PSSAct 1.19: conducting safety training to 1050 teachers and 23,000 childrenAct 1.20: conducting ASER, EGRA as well as EGMAThe MEAL team will monitor the implementation of the activities and will conduct PDM after each distribution

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Assumption: The PSEA policies are in place and organization’s staff is trained how to report Protection Risk: Abusing or exploiting the beneficiaries by the service providers Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating Measures: 1- SCA will make sure that PSEA policies are in place, practiced and monitored2- SCA will ensure that targeted communities and staff have access to safe and effective reporting systems including when they will get response Assumption: All the teachers in the schools are fully aware towards the impact of physical punishment and bullying on the childrenProtection Risk: Teachers and other supporting staff participate in physical punishment or bullying of children Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating Measures: 1- All the teachers and supporting staff in the schools will sign on a code of conduct on physical punishment and bullying 2- All the teachers and supporting staff in the schools will receive training on the prevention and reporting issues related to physical punishment and bullying Assumption: There is Child Safeguarding policy in the schoolsProtection Risk: Children are over-exposed to the risk of, or experience, harm by teachers, school personnel or organization staffLikelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating Measures: 1- There will be Child Safeguarding policy signed by all the teachers and supporting staff in the schools and monitored on annual bases2- SCA will provide sufficient safe and clean bathroom facilities that segregated basing on sex and age Assumption: The schools are structurally safe and provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, etc.)Protection Risk: The schools don’t provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, etc.)Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating Measures: SCA will do rehabilitation to the wash facilities and classrooms including Painting, blacksmithing, Electricity, building works, building works for CWD)
