Project Summary

Improve access and scale-up responsiveness to essential healthcare needs of the vulnerable populations by focusing on the major causes of morbidity and mortality through the existing 8 Health Facilities in Pochalla (7 PHCUs, and 1 Upgraded PHCC+ in Pochalla (Pochalla, Alari, Ajwongbai, Otali, Ajwara, Ojangbai,Obudi and Okadi), Uror (Yuai, Pathai, Payai, Wei-kol, Patuet and Thiam) Koch ( Koch PHCC , Bow PHCC and Bieh PHCU) Pibor (Verteth, Thanajon, ) Mayom (Kueryei, Mankien, Mayom town and Bieh) .To Prevent, detect and respond to epidemic prone disease outbreaks and promote WASH in health facilities in conflict affected and vulnerable populations.

Needs

Due to the worsening situation in former greater regions despite recent Khartoum Peace Agreement many people are facing humanitarian crisis in the regions and it is expected that the total number of people within the three regions will greatly facing serious humanitarian crisis the end of 2018 if there are no health services being provided and if peace is not achieved as well as the current roaring climatic condition that is affecting the host communities and the IDPs. The majority affected by this man-made crisis are women and children under 15 years. The ongoing humanitarian catastrophe is putting high pressures on the host communities and stretching the resources of the local health system.CRADA is target the following Counties for our humanitarians assistants for 2019 if we secure funding from our donors, Pochalla (7 PHCUs, and 1 Upgraded PHCC+ in Pochalla (Pochalla, Alari, Ajwongbai, Otali, Ajwara, Ojangbai,Obudi and Okadi), Uror (Yuai, Pathai, Payai, Wei-kol, Patuet and Thiam) Koch ( Gany PHCU , Mer mer PHCU and Bieh mobile clinic) Pibor (Verteth, Thanajon,) Mayom (Kueryei, Mankien, Mayom town and Bieh). CRADA has recently signed an MOU with the states government to support the health facilities who not operational to provide emergency health intervention as communities are moving back to their respective places because of the recent signed peace. As agency lead in Pochalla County, HIV/AIDS become endemic because the county is bordering with Ethiopia and over 150 HIV/AIDS patients have been diagnosed and refer them to Pinydu for treatment, a distant of three to four days walk, in search of health services. The population of Pochalla is estimated to be 66,323 according to the 2008 South Sudan household census. There are about 2491 refugees (RRC) in Alari payam from Ethiopia and 1500 IDPs. Over 63,616 people are in need of lifesaving medical services in Pochalla. TB clinic and VCT have been closed because of funding. The seven PHCUs are located in remote areas of 4-8 hours walk from each other, making it difficult for the population in Pochalla to have access to the services. With unavailability of health services in Pochalla, prolonged food deficiency, poor access to safe drinking water, and improper sanitation, the children and women become more vulnerable and at high risk of contracting diseases outbreaks including the diarrheal diseases and vaccines preventable ones. In Uror County there are over 100,000 people displaced by the ongoing fighting between the government and the SPLA IO. Some health facilities in Uror County not operational, no drugs stocking, and other medical supplies, poor infrastructure without staffing. The PHCC in Yuai and the rest of the PHC in various locations have not been functioning since the eruption of the crisis with only SMOH supporting 1PHCU in Pulchuol without drugs and staff, making it difficult to reach other areas. it’s expected the situation may get worse and more displacement due to unavailability of health services and insecurity during the dry season. CRADA will support 30,000 host and IDP community through provision of emergency lifesaving health services in Targeted areas.
