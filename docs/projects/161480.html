Project Summary

Rural Agricultural organization for Relief and Rehabilitation in south Sudan ( RAOSS) P) is a national Non -governmental Organization established in 2018 and operating in Yirol West County, Lake State funded by UNFAO for over year . The organization have strong field presence in Yirol West and is based in Yirol Town and in Yambio.
The key objective of the project is to increase vulnerable household access to food through support to increased food production and to reduce the level of vulnerability amongst malnourished children through linkage of nutrition related activities to Food security activities which is contributing to FSL cluster objective 2 and it is in line with HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) .
The geographical scope of the project is in Yirol East , Yirol West and Yambio Counties . The project will target 120,000 beneficiaries mainly IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host population in these areas.. These beneficiaries will be supported through direct input distribution of the inputs, resilience light training, vaccination and treatment.
The project will support gender balance and has a strong protection consideration.
The project will main supplies from FAO pipeline and it will cover a period of one year

1 - Needs

The current conflict which started in 2013 have created very complex Humanitarian situation in the Country , with the Humanitarian situation deteoriating throughout the Country. In the most recent IPC report In August 2019, an estimated 6.35 million people (54% of the population) are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, among whom an estimated 1.7 million people are facing Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity and 10,000 people are in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5). Compared to the same period in 2018, there is a slight reduction in the proportion of people facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity by an estimated 5%. However, high levels of acute food insecurity still persist in the country. The main drivers of this conflict and insecurity mainly by cattle raiding, displacement , economic decline, flooding , fall army worm in these areas. In Lakes State , the high food insecurity is linked to cattle raiding which have caused huge displacement this year that have resulted to low production; poor access to food due to restricted movement to productive farmland, limited access to natural food sources, Seasonally poor feeder road conditions causing low market supply and irregular salary payment to the civil servants and outbreak of fall army worm which extensively destroyed most of the crops.In Awerial , movement toward Awerial County and Mingkaman informal settlement sitecontinued in June, driven by access to humanitarian services, perceptions of safety, and presence of relatives. Furthermore,conflict at the border of Yirol West and Rumbek East counties in late May resulted in displacement toward Mapourdit, Tali, Yirol Town, andMingkaman informal settlement site.; Only 30% of assessed settlements in eastern Lakes reported having adequate food access and more than half of the assessed, settlements reported eating only one meal a day. In June, poor harvests in 2018 caused by irregular rainfall and insecurity remained the primary reason for inadequate access to food. The IPC report further indicates that by 2020 , The nutrition situation is further expected to deteoriate generally in the Country of which more than 5.50 million people are expected to be in crisis (IPC Phase3) or worse from January to April 2020

2 - Activities

The key activities are: community sensitization and mobilization, formation of Accountability to affected committee, distribution of main agricultural crops seeds, vegetable seeds, Tools and fishing, distribution of dry season vegetable seeds, tools, fishing kits Training of farmers in resilience light intervention, vaccination of livestock, Treatment of Livestock.
