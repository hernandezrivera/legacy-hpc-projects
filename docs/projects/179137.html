Project Summary

This project is an urgent humanitarian nutrition intervention by filing crucial nutrition gap in areas populated with IDPs and vulnerable group in Harem, Qurqaneea, and, Idleb sub- districts.
This project will support three RRTs in targeted area as follow:
One in Qurqaneea sub-district
One in Harem sub-district
One in Idleb city
These seven RRTs will serve PLW 18000, 11,000 boys, girls 10000 so the total beneficiaries will be 39000.
RRTs will Screen of children, pregnant and lactating women for malnutrition signs and PSS cases. Also, they will refer boys, girls, pregnant and lactating women to the fixed primary healthcare centers if there is a malnutrition case and PSS center if needed. Along with disseminating IYCF-E messages among mothers of children under five (group sessions). And finally, they will Facilitate IYCF-E campaigns integrated to the Child Health Days, EPI campaigns.

4 - Needs

By referring to OCHA’s 2021 Needs and Response Summary, chronic malnutrition is anticipated to increase, with nearly 0.5 Million children losing their learning and developmental potentials every year in Syria. Prevalence of maternal anemia and maternal malnutrition is expected to continue rising due to deteriorating socio-economic conditions. During 2021 it is expected that acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months in hard-to-reach areas and IDP camps will continue spiking, endangering the survival and wellbeing of nearly 90,000 children in Syria. Without appropriate care as high as 50 percent of these children might lose their lives because of acute malnutrition related complication. Poor dietary diversity among children as well as mothers in Syria will impact their capability to optimally breast feed their children and hence the already low levels of poor infant feeding and caring practices will continue to be evident. The same report mentioned in Immediate Response Priorities that scaling up the prevention and the treatment of acute malnutrition by increasing community management of acute malnutrition (CMAM), promotion of optimal infant and young child feeding and caring practices (IYCF) and age-appropriate micronutrients supplementation, including ensuring healthy and sustainable supply chain of life-saving nutrition supplies.According to UNICEF’s Whole of Syria Humanitarian Situation Report January 2021, chronic malnutrition continues to be a major public health problem in NW Syria where December 2020 nutrition surveillance data reports an increase in chronic malnutrition among children 6-59 months, with an estimated overall chronic malnutrition prevalence of 34%. 5 - Implementation of activities

Project’s activities will last for 12 months in Harem, Qurqaneea, and Idleb subdistricts. These activities will target PLW and children under five years including IDPS and host communities. Serving 18000 PLWs, 11,000 boys, girls 20,000 Therefore, the total beneficiaries will be 39000. Those three RRTs will Screen of children, pregnant and lactating women for malnutrition signs and PSS cases. Also, they will refer boys, girls, pregnant and lactating women to the fixed primary healthcare centers if there is a malnutrition case and PSS center if needed. Along with disseminating IYCF-E messages among mothers of children under five (group sessions). And finally, they will Facilitate IYCF-E campaigns integrated to the Child Health Days, EPI campaigns. Monthly MEAL reports will be issued by SIMRO MEAL department after he will analyze the collected data from the field. MEAL field staff will conduct weekly surveys and interviews with targeted women and other PLW. The key planned M&E activities for this project includes: § Baseline study, which provides the benchmark for progress measurement. (Before project start date) § Project (results, activity, financial) monitoring (monthly) § Beneficiary monitoring (biweekly through field visits) § Feedback and response mechanism updates (biweekly) § Regular operation updates (weekly) § Final evaluation (after project closure)

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

SIMRO has taken into consideration all the appropriate protection analysis. As such, all the RRTs team will be female to ensure full engagement with Syrian culture. On the other hand, SIMRO as usual will be conducting field monitor visits to ensure full satisfaction for the beneficiaries. finally, SIMRO has always been fully aware of all activities and procedures in regard to PSEA and GBV making sure to raise the awareness for their staff. This project will enhance and promote the equal access for this humanitarian intervention by ensuring that all the targeted boys, girls, women, and men will be able to receive the agreed primary health care services and nutrition intervention in line with humanitarian principles and guidelines. This will be fulfilled throughout conducting biweekly field monitoring visits making sure to receive regularly feedback and remarks concerning beneficiary’s satisfaction. On the other hand, this project will give extra attention ensuring the dignity and safety as per high standard and promoting the PSEA in the all the project's phase and level. Finally, All RRT staff who will do the field visits will be female to meet the Syrian context and raise the access level for all targeted PLW, boys and girls.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: if the hostilities either carry on or get closer to our working areas, we instantly coordinate with cluster and partners to relocate our project filling other crucial gap.Mitigation: we will coordinate with local health partner on the ground to relocate this health facility to more safe and secure place in areas withIDPs waves. Risk: operational risks comes in second as, there are many instances where the fuel prices increase insanely affecting the projectimplementation.Mitigation: As for our response to mitigate these huge obstacles, fuel will be procured from the very beginning at a fix price then it shall besafely stored in our warehouses. Risk: disbursements might be delayed due to uncontrollable conditions which may hugely affect the entire chain of the activitiesMitigation: Therefore, we have sought from the very beginning to secure alternative payments from SIMRO side to cover the potential shortages in the financing. Risk: use of cashMitigation: We developed out policies to reduce the use of cash to the lowest level and depend as much as possible on the PTT office by adding this term to all request for qutations and tenders.
