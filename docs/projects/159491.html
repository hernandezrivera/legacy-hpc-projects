Project Summary

WATER: Provision of emergency safe water to 1000 IDPs through voucher; access of lifesaving water through rehabilitation of 2 shallow wells equipped with solar power complete with water tanks and water kiosks, upgrading 1 boreholes through hybrid system complete with construction of 2 elevated water tanks, 3 water kiosks supplied water through piping system; capacity building training to 4 WASH committees and 4 well operators on operation and maintenance. As cross cutting, NWO will conduct DRR and conflict resolution mechanisms workshop to 100 community members.
SANITATION: To improve the sanitation of the IDP camps and institutions, NWO will construct 2 twin block institutional latrines in 2 school and 100 in the IDP camps.
HYGIENE: NWO will train 20 CHPs to carry out comprehensive hygiene promotion campaigns to educate the community on better hygiene promotion practices including hand washing with soap practices through visiting house-to-house, schools, feeding/nutrition centers and health centers; in this project, NWO will provide 804 HP kits to 804 households and IDPs who are at risk of AWD/cholera and malnutrition.
The proposed response took into consideration the impact of the disaster on women, children and people with disabilities in terms of access to WASH services; it proposed specific actions to prevent risk of gender-based violence and hence will contribute to greater gender equality. NWO top management will monitor the quality and quantity of the WASH intervention during the project implementation and its completion.

Mitigation Measures

NWO will hold at the inception of the project, community mobilization, and sensitization meetings with all stakeholders including the local authorities to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholders. Women will be part of the participants of the mobilization workshop. NWO will hold special meeting with women, girls, boys, people with disabilities and minority groups to know their needs. NWO will involve the target community through its leaders and engendered committees in the first stage of planning of the project to ensure community participation. The organization will establish engendered committees (women participation will be strictly adhered) that will have together working relations with the project team and beneficiary communitiesNWO will Ensure the location and access routes to WASH facilities and distribution points are safe. NOW will:- Locate water sources in visible, central locations and not more than 500 metres from settlement;- Install lights near communal facilities. If lighting is not possible, NWO will consider coordinating with NFI partners for the provision of solar lamps / torches for each household.- Avoid placing facilities or distribution points near military installations or threats such as attacks stray bullets, crossfire or violence. Weapon bearers should not be present inside distribution points. - Introduce accompaniment arrangements to reduce threats to the safety and dignity of women and girls e.g. fetching water in groups.- Identify central locations for distribution points - beneficiaries should not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points. - Distributions will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries enough time to travel home.- Latrines will be separate between female and male and will be lockable inside and outside to keep the privacy of women and girls.NWO will hold at the inception of the project, community mobilization, and sensitization meetings with all stakeholders including the local authorities to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholder including M&E roles will be discussed and agreed. NWO will develop elaborate beneficiary complain and feedback tool, project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be given 1-day workshop on how to use the complaint/feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. The feed complain mechanisms is accessible to everyone including women, children, people with disabilities and minorities

Please provide further explanation

NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work the Government in order to build a resilient and responsive system infrastructure. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will focus both on the everyday routine challenges that front line staff face on a regular basis as well as focus on sudden shocks such as disease outbreaks. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work to build the leadership capacity of district and regional state representatives and in this regard will target Government key managers, field staff, community members as well as Community Resource Persons so as to empower them to solve problems at their level and build relationships and social networks. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will focus on strengthening existing social networks to provide access to multiple resources available in the community and forms of value such as community knowledge and reputation that can build and restore resilience to the existing systems. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will use the model of everyday resilience building in the systems of the Government through a combination of absorptive, adaptive and transformative strategies that authorities, staff and community members can use to adopt the system in responding to strains and shocks. This will help in producing a strengthened and more resourceful resilient system infrastructure. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will train the Local Authorities, field staffs, resource persons and community members on how to deal with sudden shocks such as outbreak of diseases. All the different strata of the community system will be taught how to manage such disease outbreaks at different levels in order to build a resilient and responsive infrastructure into every level. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will use the funding from donors as well as its own funds to ensure adequate resources and strong government system infrastructure is built to contribute to generating a resilient and responsive system in Galgaduud, Lower Shabelle and Bay regions.
