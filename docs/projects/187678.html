Project Summary

Northeast Syria has experienced some of the conflicts worst which damaged all infrastructures in Syria in additional to stop all submitted services to population. The massive urban destruction has affected Electricity on a large scale .Electricity is one of the most important basic services lost in many Syrian cities because of the devastating war that took place in these cities.ِAlso all linked Services with electricity has ceased on a large-scale. Families have lost security and access to markets, financial services, Critical electrical infrastructure such as lighten streets , maintenance plans and providing houses with electricity, also electrical grids became old and a lot of it are in desperate need of repairs.
After people returned to their houses, electrical services were in high demand, families face major obstacles in getting their requirements and movement at night due to damaged electricity, roads, insecurity and moving in cities and villages. In 2013, The Syrian government lost control on Raqqa, all services stopped but life still continue. All villages expanded after 2013, there are many houses in these villages have been built, but they are still without water and electricity because war has stopped all expanding projects of Electricity and WASH. People followed negative adaptation strategies to meet their needs like un legal and Random connecting to the grids and using unsuitable cables. This mater caused unbalancing in loads on electrical lines, many losses in voltages and overloads on electrical transformers in additional to many breakdowns, electrical network faults,many accidents and death and conflicts between people.
Many people forced to use generator,this matter depleted thier income because of the high cost of fuel and maintenance .In spite of
Due to the importance of social cohesion and electricity in the life of people, Nawat is going to target 25 village in this project where donors to this sector have provided a very limited assistance. Based on a selection criterion that have been developed by Nawat for 25 villages that will support security, electrical services and improve social cohesion. Also expanding the arrival to electrical services to enhance and encourage commercial activities in the targeted regions
Through two years Nawat will install new electrical transformers and expand grids to the new houses, repair electrical grids streetlights, replaced old transformers to others with bigger capacity in the targeted villages to improve electrical services and support Critical electrical infrastructure.
As a first step, providing new houses after 2013 with electricity is considered one of the most important services that the villages need. The basic materials will include:
•Cables.
•Columns
•lamps.
•Electrical transformers.
And Other required items (as will be outlined in the budget).

Project Objective:
The goal: Expanding the arrival to electrical services.
1-Providing families affected by crises in Raqqa governorate with basic services (Electricity) and supporting security and social cohesion.
2-Provide the new houses near electrical grid with electricity.
3- Restore transformers capacity to meet people needs

4 - Needs

Electricity is very important for people and many projects and activities are linked strongly with it in the region. Houses still need to electricity. The expanding process has been stopped since 2013, after that a very limited assistances have been submitted in this field...For providing the new built houses with electricity we need to develop and expand electrical grids. Nawat organization met with many people, many contractors and many local representatives authorities and local authorities, they explained the requirements for provide houses with electricity like transformers, cables, towers, columns, lampposts electrical insulators and accessories. These needs are required continuously due to continuous increasing in population number. They mentioned that about 20,000 houses in the rural are not provided with electricity.In this project, Nawat plans to targeted 2500 houses in 25 villages by cooperation with donors to provide them with electricity. To do that we need to install transformers with control panels, aluminum and copper cables, columns and towerswe think this project will improve all sides of people life
