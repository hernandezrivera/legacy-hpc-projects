Project Summary

In the proposed plan RRP will be engaging in active case findings of cases of malnutrition through regular screening of beneficiaries. An added intervention of building the capacity of mother though mother led MUAC training will done to enable mothers easily identify at an early stage the cases within their HHs. RRP will provide treatment of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) through OTP (Outpatient Therapeutic) services in line with the Basic Nutrition Service Package (BNSP) at up to 3 fixed and 9 outreaches in Buloburte Jalalaqsi and Mahaas district. RRP will train and support nutrition workers to identify, provide lifesaving treatment and follow up on cases of malnutrition. RRP will also integrate preventative services including providing Infant Youth and Child Feeding (IYCF) counseling, micronutrients supplementation at MCHs and routine immunization. Community level social mobilization will be done to promote healthy nutritional practices, including campaigns for micro nutrient supplementation, deworming, and improved feeding practices. RRP will actively participate in both national and Hiran, Beletweyne regional cluster meetings. Participation of the community groups such as women, youth, and local leaders will be prioritized so as to enhance and get more feedback as an aspect of accountability to the affected population. RRP will do monthly, quarterly , midyear and end year reporting. RRP will work towards reduction of the high prevalence of SAM and MAM in the two districts. hence reducing mortality and morbidity rates related to malnutrition and improve, provide and integrated, nutrition related resilience interventions life saving and quality nutrition services to acutely malnourished children and PLWs through community based nutritional service.
Increase access and utilization of BNSP, MCH services to acutely malnourished boys girls U5, and PLW and integrate WASH services.
Capacity building to the community health workers, volunteers, care givers and field staff and strengthen the existing community based nutrition service structures to provide comprehensive basic nutritional service packages (BNSP) to malnourished children U5 and PLW in the three districts. (Mahas and Bula Burte).
RRP is currently having and integrated project in Jalalaqsi of both health nutrition and WASH, to be able to have continuity of the intervention, we will ensure the the response plan captures the needs already identified in the area during the implementation. The FAO FSC project that is ongoing in Bulaburte has a component of sensitizing the community on nutritional best practices through the use of nutrition champions.

The target population is at risk of displacement that may pose human rights violation. The Protection Risk Analysis highlights the extremely difficult situation in IDP settlements and collective centres across the country, in light of growing displacement, due to drought and conflict and continuing protracted and secondary/multiple displacement situations (including the particular challenges facing refugee returnees who get displaced again). The IDPs in these displacement locations frequently live in undignified and hazardous circumstances, where they face multiple protection risks/threats such as, unlawful evictions, overcrowded and unsanitary environments with limited access to basic services, exposure to explosive hazards, increased risk of Gender Based Violence (GBV), negative coping mechanisms such as child marriage and child labor, and tension with the host community. Children and young people (mainly male) in displaced situation, particularly those from Al-Shabaab controlled areas face risks of arbitrary arrest once out of those controlled areas, and therefore live in fear and sometimes isolation. Lastly, IDPs continue to struggle to end their displacement and pursue voluntary, informed, safe, and dignified durable solutions based on their individual and household needs.
