Project Summary

Several factors have adversely impacted access to WASH services in Syria, including the February 2023 earthquake, conflict hostilities, economic decline, power shortages, and the impact of climate change. As a result of the conflict and earthquake, damage to WASH systems has restricted the population’s access to water and sanitation services in affected communities. The five out of 14 Governorates with unacceptably high stunting rates exceeding 20 per cent (Aleppo, Idleb, Deir-ez-Zor, Al-Hasakeh, and Ar-Raqqa)21 also presented a high WASH severity rate and have been heavily affected by both water scarcity and AWD/cholera outbreak. "HNO 2024".
The project supports IDPs and host communities in Idleb and Rural Aleppo with WASH services, both emergency services for IDPs in camps and out of camps. People in 38 camps will receive drinking water through water trucking or water pumping where possible, water storage tanks, constructing septic tanks, latrine blocks, desludging, garbage bins and garbage collection in addition to conducting hygiene promotion sessions. Hygiene kits and diapers for the elderly and PWD will be distributed three times.
In communities out of camp, both IDPs and host communities will benefit from rehabilitation and operation of water pumping stations, operation of station on solar power where never possible, and the rehabilitation of sewage network. This project will contribute to the improvement of the Cholera preparedness and prevention capacity of IDPs and host community residents, through ensuring the continuity of the WASH services and provide hygiene and protective materials as per the recommendation of the WASH Cluster about cholera to limit the transmission of Cholera outbreak in NWS.
Risks:
Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of contractors: HIHFAD inform all contractors and included a paragraph in the contract to forbid any supplier from contracting children in daily labor. Movements of garbage trucks and water trucks incongested camp: HIHFAD discuss with beneficiaries and camps managers the best routes leading to the water storage tanks, latrines and garbage bins in order to avoid any potential risk. Health risk associated with garbage removal: HIHFAD ensures that contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment like gloves and face mask during work. Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion. Water distribution not safe to access or not accessible, especially for women, girls (exposure to GBV risk) and children elderly and people with disabilities, inequality in distribution. Mitigation: Consult separately girls, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases; Make sure that distance of water collection points remains at minimum distance.
SEA Risk:
Timing and process of distribution may put women and girls at higher risk of GBV. Mitigation: HIHFAD will promote the establishment of adequate management plan; engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback from different groups on package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among partner staff from different groups on package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among partner staff.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The intervention will be based on the technical needs assessments by HIHFAD, the targeted camps will receive the following services (water trucking, water storage tanks, latrine blocks and septic tanks desludging of septic tanks, garbage bins, hygiene promotion and hygiene kits. Moreover, garbage bins will be distributed in all targeted camps, and they will be discharged three times per week (for every 10 HHs 1 garbage bin of half a barrel). HIHFAD field team will be responsible of implementing the activities of the projects. Latrines will be purchased from Syrai according to the design which includes three latrines+1 for people with disabilities and are gender segregated. Al Manhal water station in Abin and Naza stations will be rehabilitated and equipped with solar systems. The project will take into consideration factors that hinder PWD from benefiting and participating in the project, by ensuring representatives of PWDs in water committees in the camps, and tailoring the activities to reach them as the location of PWD latrines, and tent to tent distribution of kits for them. Rehabilitation and extension of the sewage network and water network will be conducted in Al Bab, Maaret Tamsrin and Azaz.
