Project Summary

According to the Somalia HNO , 2021-2022, Somalia is among the countries with the highest rankings in infant and child mortality rates of, respectively, 76.6 and 121.5 per 1,000 live births.337 The maternal mortality rate (692 deaths per 100,000 live births) and the fertility rate (average of 6.9 children per woman) are also among the highest in the world, 79 per cent of all births are home delivered without skilled assistance.338 Overall, 99 per cent of Somali women have undergone female genital mutilation, 64 per cent. Overall, only 11 per cent of children aged 12-23 months are fully vaccinated, around 19 per cent of children in urban areas have received all basic vaccinations. Average lifespan is 51.5 years as confirmed by UNDP Somalia. Pregnant and Lactating Women and Girls along with the under-5 children with disabilities experience stigmatization, depression and discrimination without any social, political and economic intervention.
This project aims at
• providing quality essential lifesaving and life-sustaining health with focus on women, children, and other vulnerable groups and strengthens the local capacity to deliver evidence-based and cost effective services based on the EPHS and Primary Health Care Approach.
• measles case management, immunization campaign, emergency preparedness, and response capacities, awareness and mobilization campaigns including COVID-19 messaging , distribution of masks, soaps and sanitizer to prevent the spread of the virus.
• . Ensure 4,000 Pregnant and Lactating Women and Girls with 6, 000 facing Disabilities undergo counseling, prenatal and post-natal counseling, psycho-social guidance and reintegration for adaptability into motherhood; basic communication and IYCF . Most Pregnant and Lactating women and Girls facing disabilities such as deafness and blindness experience rejection, depression, rape and stigma just as children.
• Ensure the under-five 8, 000 ( 4,500 Girls and 3500 Boys) access primary health-care, IYCF, counseling and protection especially 1, 000 Girls and 500 Boys with Disabilities.
• Purchase and distribution of sanitary kits to PLWGs.
• support 7 fixed health facilities and 10 mobile teams
• Ensure access to a package of Emergency Primary Health Care (EPHC) including 8,000 children under 1 year of age and essential health services to PLWGs).
• Selection and training of community Health Workers to strengthen the outreach components
• Enhancing & providing quality emergency obstetric care (EmOC) to reduce the risk of maternal and newborn mortality and morbidity e.g ambulating the Pregnant and Lactating Women and Girls.
• Clinical rape management and referrals
• Treatment of AWDs.
