Project Summary

Zimbabwe has been hit by droughts for several years and it is so unfortunate that in such disasters, children are the most casualties as they are neglected or deprived of parental care by their primary caregivers. The recent droughts have shown that, primary caregivers tend to migrate locally or even outside the country looking for different livelihoods to feed the families. In the meantime, children are left without proper caregiving. Due to the parenting gap, children tend to also migrate to hot places like growth points or even towns. This family separation as a result of drought has huge impact on the welfare and wellbeing of the children. Drawing from experiences of previous droughts, children especially girls are violated in different ways like sexual exploitation as they will be trying to engage in various livelihoods to cushion themselves from the effects of drought. Such situations are more detrimental if children are left to care for themselves as child headed families. This project seeks to strengthen and solidify the family system in 15 selected districts such that children receive proper parental care and at the same time minimising family separation.
In a bid to achieve the above mentioned objective, various activities will be implemented and some of the activities include: trainings to community child protection structures on child protection issues in emergencies as a way of capacitating them to handle child protection issues. Groups of such people to be capacitated include; community child-care workers, school teachers, foster parents, traditional leaders and children themselves among other structures. In a bid to strengthen the family system, separated children are going to be identified and reunified to their relatives in their usual places of residence. To avoid further family separation, follow ups or home visits are going to be conducted to the reunified children to ascertain the coping and adaptation levels of children to community life as well as identifying emerging needs. Children whose relatives will be untraceable or unwilling to take care of them, shall be put under foster care whilst permanent solutions are being sort for. A total of 76 000 children are going to benefit from this project. These children include those who have already separated from their families and those at risk of family separation.
After the successful implementation of this project, child protection systems at both the village and district levels will be strengthened and in a position to use the acquired knowledge to prevent family separation. There is going to be reduced child protection violation as communities will be conscious of the various needs of the children and in a position to fulfil such needs. Foster parents will be equipped with parenting skills for children in emergencies. To ensure that the project is implemented as planned, the project shall be subjected to close monitoring and evaluation by the Organisation and donors.
In implementing this project, the organisation is going to work closely with Government Departments like Social welfare, Ministry of Primary and Secondary education, among others. Other Non Governmental Organisations are also going to be collaborated with in a bid to offer a holistic package of services to the children. The involvement of Government players will promote the sustainability of the project as the Government will keep on offering services to the affected families beyond the scope and time frame of this project. Child participation is also going to be promoted at all levels

Needs

Zimbabwe suffered immensely due to the drought of 2016 to 2017 period. There were a lot of child protection violations experienced by children left with no proper parental care as parents moved away in pursuant of different livelihoods in other districts and sometimes out of the country. Many families were left as child headed and some children moved to hot spot places like growth points engaging in various livelihoods many of which were detrimental to their wellbeing. Some children moved to towns where they started to live and work on the streets. Due to such vulnerabilities caused by family separation, children were exploited in different ways thus the need for this project to avoid the repetition of such child protection violations. The vulnerability of the children was worsened by the weak child protection structures that failed to maintain and preserve the family system. Children ended up being exposed to otherwise avoidable child protection violation practices. This scenario is likely to happen in the year 2019 thus this project seeks to offer family strengthening services to the 15 selected districts. More so, the Organization seeks to strengthen the child protection systems at ward and district levels to respond swiftly to any child protection concerns associated with the projected drought. To reduce chances of child rights violation during the drought period, children themselves will also be equipped with child protection information which enables them to protect and safeguard themselves.

Activities

In order to achieve the objectives of the project, the following activities are going to be implemented. The activities shall be done together with Department of Social and other child protection stakeholders in the areas of operation. Identification of children in households where caregivers have gone away in search of food and other livelihoods. Due to drought, parents are forced to move out of their usual places of residence thus their children are left exposed to many child protection violations. The children will be linked to community child protection structures such as Community Child-Care Workers as well as other Local Organizations that offer different child protection services. Identification, Tracing, and reunification of children who have moved from their usual places of residence in search of food and livelihoods within and outside Zimbabwe. Families that receive such children will also be assisted with relief items for them to meet the basic needs to avoid further separation. To ensure that reunified children are adapting and coping well in their various communities, follow ups will be made offering psycho-social support and also identifying emerging needs. This helps in strengthening the family system and keeping the children in family care. Facilitate placement of children in foster care. Some of the children's relatives will be unavailable or unwilling to take care of the children thus some children will be put under the care of foster parents. These families wil aslo be assisted with relief items to meet basic needs. To ensure quality care for the children placed under foster care, these foster parents will also be trained on issues of child protection in emergencies. Child protection in emergencies training to teachers, community leadership, Community Child-Care Workers, water point committees and children themselves. These are community structures that make sure children's rights are safeguarged. By having these structures well knowledgiable on child protection issues, chances of familiy seperation are minimised as children at risk of family seperation are quickly identified and assisted to avert the possible seperation.
