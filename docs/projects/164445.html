Project Summary

Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation has a good experience in Humanitarian Action
especially bakeries. The foundation owns 4 bakeries and 7 flour mills offering bread
during the year of 2016 for 1.8 million citizens.
The project aims to recycling the revenue for four months.
Via this project, the bread will be offered to targeted families to reduce burden of living,
improve their life condition, and support their resilience. The beneficiaries will be chosen
as per most vulnerable families with the help of hosting camp in the region. Through
these procedures, the foundation ensures most benefit for vulnerable families as that will
be measured by consult those people about the feasibility of the project and its outputs.
This project is designed to respond to the needs of the new influx of IDPs from southern Idleb, fleeing bombardment and the escalation of hostilities since November 2019. This project will provide cooked meals, RTERs and food parcels. As per the FSL Cluster recommendations, the cooked meals are going to be implemented for the first 3 days of the emergency distributions, which will be followed by RTERs for a period of 4 weeks, and finally, we will distribute food parcels for 3 months to the same IDP households.The total number of beneficiaries targeted are 5,500 individuals (1,100 households) i. This project will also help in decreasing severe food insecurities and that may result in malnutrition and other health related problems. The food parcels contain the needed essential protein, carbohydrates and other minerals which will provide the needed kcals for the IDPs during these next few months. The modality of the project will be in-kind distributions of food parcels and RTERs.

4 - Needs

According to the HNO’s initial report in 2019, seven years after the commencement of thecrisis, offering the needs of Syrian people continues to be a challenge all over Syria.Nearly 12.7m people needs Humanitarian aids in Syria. Among those people, 4.5mpeople are in dire need due to the similarity of the weak points resulting fromdisplacement process, confronting war and lack of having goods and essential services.Those who suffer from absence of food security, and those who are at the risk of foodsecurity absence constitute 54% of Syrian citizens. This situation is due to recur ofdisplacement movement within the region, low level of protection and declining ofpurchasing power of the families in the north of Syria which led to decrease of food at thefamily level and the growing need of providing food supplies as a part of protection andpreservation of life. Actually, purchasing food and depending on food aids are mostcommon sources for food availability and access to it. Taking into consideration rising offood prices especially basic food stuff such as bread which makes the families arecompelled to spend most of their money on food and to forgo other vital expenses. Inaddition, as per HNO in 2019, more than 90% of Syrian families spend 50% of theirincome to secure food; therefore, it was necessary to offer food aids as the mostessential need for Syrian people as per HNO’s report.
