Project Summary

The ongoing crisis in the North-West and South-West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon has displaced over 74,607 households, severely impacting children's right to education since 2016. More than 95.7% of internally displaced persons (IDPs) fled due to armed conflict (MSNA 2024). This region is plagued by violence and insecurity, resulting in widespread school closures and targeted attacks on educational institutions. Consequently, many families are forced to relocate, putting children at greater risk of human rights violations, including family separation, recruitment by armed groups, and gender-based violence.
The educational landscape in these regions is dire, with 11,937(16 %) of the population in need of education (MSNA 2024). Many of these children are currently out of school due to the crisis, with many schools either closed or operating under severe constraints. Children in the Northwest and Southwest regions are missing an average of three-and-a-half years of schooling, leading to significant learning losses (Global Education Monitoring Report, 2022). The impact on girls' education is particularly severe, with a reported 45% increase in teenage pregnancies during the conflict, further hindering their educational opportunities (UNICEF, 2023).
In response to this pressing situation, the initiative "Building Brighter Tomorrows: Enhancing Educational Access and Quality through Targeted Education for Vulnerable Learners in Crisis-Affected Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon" aims to improve educational opportunities for children and adolescents in the conflict-affected divisions of Boyo, Bui, Donga-Mantung, Mechum, Ngo-Ketunjia, Fako, Kupe-Manenguba, Lebialem, Manyu, and Meme.
Core Strategies of the Intervention
This intervention employs three core strategies:
Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL): This methodology assesses students' learning levels, organizes them into appropriate groups, and provides culturally relevant, engaging learning materials. Research indicates that TaRL can significantly enhance learning outcomes by filling knowledge gaps and ensuring relevant, engaging instruction (Banerjee et al., 2017), thereby helping out-of-school children to accelerate their education.
STEM Education: The integration of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) education aims to foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills. By incorporating hands-on activities, the program enhances student engagement and prepares them for future opportunities in STEM fields, driving innovation and economic development, particularly in crisis-affected areas.
Radio Education Program: This vital initiative delivers educational content to children in remote areas where traditional schooling is not feasible. It fosters community engagement by informing parents and caregivers about the importance of education and supplementing the formal education system with additional resources. The use of radio has proven effective in reaching marginalized populations, ensuring continuity of education during crises.
Target Population
The intervention specifically targets:
Vulnerable Children and Adolescents: Particularly those aged 3-15 years affected by the ongoing conflict in the Northwest and Southwest regions.
Displaced Learners: Children forced to flee their homes due to violence, resulting in interruptions to their education.
Adolescent Girls: Who encounter additional barriers, including early marriage and gender-based violence, exacerbated by the crisis.
Building Brighter Tomorrows" aims to transform education in crisis-affected regions of Cameroon through targeted strategies tailored to vulnerable learners. By employing the Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) methodology, integrating STEM education, and utilizing a Radio Education Program, the initiative seeks to empower children and adolescents with the skills and support needed to navigate their challenges and create a brighter future.

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project "Building Brighter Tomorrows: Enhancing Educational Access and Quality through Targeted Education for Vulnerable Learners in Crisis-Affected Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon" stands as a vital response to the educational crisis exacerbated by ongoing violence and instability. This initiative is committed to addressing the urgent needs of children and adolescents in severely affected areas, including Boyo, Bui, Donga-Mantung, Mechum, and Ngo-Ketunjia in the Northwest, as well as Fako, Kupe-Manenguba, Lebialem, Manyu, and Meme in the Southwest. Below are the key assessed needs that the project aims to address through its activities: The ongoing crisis in the North-West and South-West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon has resulted in the displacement of over 74,607 households, causing widespread school closures, the educational landscape in these regions is dire, with 11,937(16 %) of the population in need of education (MSNA 2024). Traditional schooling is often unsafe, highlighting the need for alternative solutions. Many children miss an average of three-and-a-half years of schooling, resulting in knowledge gaps. Displaced children face increased risks of human rights violations and gender-based violence. Adolescent girls face challenges like early marriage and increased teenage pregnancies, necessitating education to empower them. Targeted strategies are essential for helping these learners catch up. Trauma has reduced children's motivation, requiring culturally relevant and engaging educational materials. The Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) approach will customize learning experiences. Integrating STEM education fosters critical thinking and problem-solving. The Radio Education Program provides accessible content in areas where traditional schooling is impractical. Community support is crucial for a supportive educational environment and raising awareness about education's importance.To effectively address these critical needs, the project will implement a series of impactful activities:1. Establish 30 Neighborhood Learning Corners (NLCs) in secure locations to provide accessible educational spaces for children affected by the crisis, ensuring they have a safe refuge for learning.2. Implement the Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) approach to assess and customize instruction according to individual learning needs of 3000 vulnerable children, while providing learning kits and resources to displaced learners for continuous educational support.3. Develop and implement gender-sensitive programs to promote girls’ education through mentorship and community awareness campaigns, alongside conducting sensitization initiatives on Gender-Based Violence in 100 schools to foster safe learning environments.4. Distribute essential learning materials to 3,000 crisis-affected learners (1,000 boys and 2,000 girls) and equip 20 local schools with resources, while integrating engaging and culturally relevant materials within the TaRL framework to motivate children. 5. Launch a Radio Education Program to provide educational content to remote learners, organize recreational activities through Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS) for 3,000 children and adolescents, and train 100 community facilitators on TaRL, STEM education, and the Radio Education Program to strengthen local educational initiatives. 6. Raise Awareness and sensitize 3000 community members against GBV/Child Abuse in North west and South west region of Cameroon.The "Building Brighter Tomorrows" initiative is a beacon of hope for vulnerable learners in Cameroon’s crisis-affected regions. By addressing critical educational needs through innovative and targeted strategies, this project not only empowers children but also nurtures a supportive educational ecosystem. Through its comprehensive approach, the initiative aims to ensure that children in the Northwest and Southwest can overcome their challenges and build a brighter future filled with opportunities.
