Project Summary

The Syrian society, especially in Aleppo, was affected by the crisis in various aspects, especially the disruption of economic activities because of the war. The professional and industrial establishments that produced foodstuffs in Aleppo were among the establishments that were affected by the loss of machines and equipment in which they were in possession of the capital that was making these establishments work.
Supporting the reactivation of these facilities after the stability of the security situation in Aleppo is a top priority for three reasons:
1 - These establishments are employing a lot of labor, which will make re-activation leads to the provision of livelihood for a large number of unemployed.
2 - The workers required by these establishments do not require the achievement of scientific or professional experience because most of these works are manual and do not require knowledge and science, which gives the opportunity to a very marginalized category, which are uneducated women and not experts in any previous profession and who do not find opportunities to work in many professions.
3 - The products of these facilities are vital products for the Syrian society and Aleppo society in particular and interfere in the daily food.
The project aims to:
1- The mentioned establishments to enhance the livelihoods of their owners, revive their families and enhance the economy through their products.
2. Providing permanent employment opportunities for the employees of the establishment
3 - vocational training in professions required by the market so that trainees can be chosen by the owners of establishments to continue with them and the rest can find good opportunities in other establishments

Needs

The population of Aleppo suffers from the deterioration of their living conditions due to unemployment, which covered a large segment of society as a result of the current crisis. On the other hand, many women become the main dependents of their families after the absence of their husbands due to the violence they are experiencing or because their husbands lose their work without the ability to work because they do not have any skill for a job required in the labor market, especially those with low employment opportunities because of not completing their education. The need to empower women and girls to obtain employment as a source of livelihood for themselves and their families has been highlighted by supporting projects that work in the field of food supply without requiring them to have significant experience in the field of work. The work of the project is based on the training of female workers by employers on the principles of processing products.The project provides support to establishments that manufacture food materials and supplies through:- Completion of established assets of equipment, machines and tools to become complete production lines.- Supporting facilities with raw materials to enable them to start production.- Cost coverage Training of trainees targeted by the project.

Implementation of activities

First: Stages and mechanisms of implementation Phase I (Preparation) Activities:• finding workshops and small existing factories, need equipment and trained staff to do this work and need raw materials to reactivate this workshop. • Selection and hiring of the administrative and technical staff required to manage the project• Evaluating of selected workshops according to the mentioned criteria.• Purchasing necessary equipment (machinery, raw materials), installing and adjusting them to be ready to start working.• Attracting people who need work and training them to be effective worker in these workshops.  Phase II (production and selling): Manufacturing:• manufacturing materials of food using machines provided to factories for some kinds of making hoard of food, which reduces the efforts of female workers and ensures a greater amount of production at the same time• The cost of required raw materials for the project is calculated for a period of one month only, until the sales are launched, consequently after the end of this month the project will be Self-financing • Paying financial compensation to candidates to attend the training, after that, giving salaries to whom adopted and started working in the workshop or factory.• The project center operates daily except for weekly and official holidays• Daily working hours / 6 / hours from 9.00 am to 3.00 pmSecond: Partners' roles:The role of the implementing partner (AL-TA’ALOUF charity association):1- Managing of the project as a whole2- Supplying equipment for selected workshops.3- Training the project staff and beneficiaries on hoard of food manufacturing.4- Supporting the project for 6 months (month to buy - and 5 months to follow up)5- Releasing weekly reports about activities of the project which clear outputs through indicators and periodic financial and non-financial reports according to the templates approved by the funded partner. 6- Providing the funded partner with the mentioned reports

Protection Risk Analysis

Exploitation of the beneficiaries by the employers by making them work a number of hours above the agreed Mitigation measuresThrough the presence of a complaint box and a hotline to report any case of abuse or violation. And close supervision of the project supervisors on the training mechanism

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Lack of the necessary raw materials at any time and It will be purchased from areas adjacent to the target area Instability of the dollar exchange rate against the local currency which results increasing the prices of materials and equipmentTherefore, the quantities of materials will be treated to match the project funding time The scarcity of energy sources which makes provision services costlier which forced the use of electricity generators and water tanks in order to provide required energy. The lack of sufficiently professional staffs because of the travelling of many people outside the country so that we will train staff as well as beneficiaries
