Project Summary

One centre with three mobile teams in Harim, Al dana will focus on reducing risks and strengthening protective factors for children to ensure their physical, social and emotional development that will contribute to the cluster priority of protecting children and mitigating the effects of violence and displacement. The project's interventions will complement the education project in Aqrabat, Atma, Bab El Hawa, Babisqa, Burdaqly, Hezreh - Hezri, Qah, and Selwa
The proposed interventions are aimed to reduce/mitigate the impact of threats to children’s physical and psychosocial security in given the seriousness of the protection threats experienced by children affected by conflict and displacement, the child protection interventions proposed are one of the frontline services that are critical to the physical and psychosocial survival of conflict-affected and displaced children. This project will contribute to addressing child protection issues and concerns, especially since Maram has schools within the region that will contribute greatly to integrating child protection services with education
This project will promote boys and girls participation in the CFSs and provide them with opportunities to safely talk about CP concerns. MF will also enhance the ability of CP staff to detect and support specific vulnerabilities amongst girls and boys through comprehensive case management and referral. Children with complicated mental health problems will be referred to other service providers. Children involved or at risk of hazardous labour and in the same line, the center will provide parenting programs to the parents and caregivers. This issue will be approached by MF case managers to comprehensively be managed by working with children and their caregivers to promote minimal standards and involve children in MF CFSs and within schools that MF is managing. The activities will aim at increasing boys’ and girls' knowledge and awareness of possible threats, allowing them to better protect themselves. MF will also be involved in raising awareness on the prevention of recruitment of children by armed groups, children involved in hazardous labour and prevention of family separation and also referrals of GBV/SGBV cases.
The main target will be children, parents, caregivers and community stakeholders such as religious leaders, local council members, teachers, medical point’s directors, etc. and through the mobile teams we aim to reach the entire surrounding communities by interactive activities and awareness raising sessions. MF will train caseworkers to identify and respond to the needs of such children. MF will use the CP sub-cluster SOPs and referral pathway to provide the needed services through Case management. In the child-friendly space (CFS), dedicated time and space will be created for adolescent girls where they can be together and where they do not have to compete with boys. MF will ensure that a female facilitator is available to work with the girls, who can offer confidentiality, warmth, and advice. MF will seek also to link CFS to other services such as education, health, and social services to enhance the referral of children in need of special assistance. MF seeks to build capacities of child protection staff and community stakeholders to provide protection services for children according to CPMS. These Interventions will aim to address vulnerabilities related to child labour, child recruitment, child marriage, family separation and case management.

4 - Needs

The escalation of fighting in the northwest since December has pushed over 900,000 people including over half a million children away from their homes and into danger. A staggering mass movement of people in such a short time According to northern Syria CP situation monitoring dashboard in Al dana sub district a 1.1 % of KIs reported children in need of focused non-specialized services,1.2 % of KIs reported children in need of specialized services 1.3 % of KIs reported caregivers in need of specialized services, 2.1 % of KIs reported children engaged in type of work that prevents them from going to school,2.2 % of KIs reported children living in child-headed households (CHH) engaged in child labour, 2.3 % of KIs reported children living in households with persons with specific needs (PWSN) engaged in child labour, 3.1 % of KIs reported children being separated from their usual caregivers, 3.4 % of KIs reported newly established institutions for children without their usual caregivers. The displacement has exacerbated family stresses, disrupted normal patterns of living and crumbled social support systems exposing boys and girls to protection threats and psychosocial distress. These children will be forced to process their conflict experience alone and face pervasive violence. Grave child rights violations remain a significant concern, including in areas where hostilities have declined, with boys & girls at risk of killing and injury, recruitment and use in hostilities, torture, detention, abduction, sexual violence, attacks on schools and hospitals and denial of humanitarian access by parties to the conflict. The crisis has deepened poverty, fueled harmful coping mechanisms and increased the risk of child protection concerns such as child labour and child marriage, child rights, including the right of education.

5 - Implementation of activities

We will start with establishing child-safe space and hiring the staff taking into consideration the gender balance, there will be male and female facilitators and 3 mobile teams who take into account the context and gender differences between childrenAlso, we will conduct awareness sessions that will focus on mitigating the risks of child labour and encouraging them to return to school, the centre will also target caregivers through awareness sessions parenting skills, providing structured psychosocial support for groups of children, Psycho-drama activities, Grandma Home telling stories and Recreational activities. This project will promote girls and boys to participate in the CFS and provide them with opportunities to safely talk about CP concerns. MF will also enhance the ability of CP staff to detect and support specific vulnerabilities amongst girls and boys through comprehensive case management and referrals, including those recruited by armed groups, child labour, children with disabilities and children without the appropriate care. It will provide training on core CP and some related topics to the local community male and female and form social networks in local authorities to respond to any child protection concerns. MF is keen to grant a quality assurance in accordance to the CPMS while implementing this project through continuous coordination with the M&E department who have personnel specialized with CP, also a feedback system with complaints mechanism will be activated. Through this project, we aim to strengthen human resources capacity to respond to child protection concerns in Syria by improving the social coherence on this behalf.
