Project Summary

The project aims to secure the safety and dignity of the vulnerable internally displaced households in 10 informal camps by maintaining the available and essential services, providing light rehabilitation for the infrastructure, and deploying firefighting points in the site.
The intervention seeks as well to build an accountability-based community in sites through establishing well trained having-capacity site committees, which are responsible for transmitting the gained knowledge to the IDPs and reflect the governance environment in these sites. The site committees will be accountable for the provided services, reporting the gaps and needs and coordinating with the respective organization, that provides services. The intervention will focus on the care and maintenance of infrastructure and facilities to ensure safe and dignified living conditions and to prevent and mitigate the impact of incidents in IDP sites.
Therefore, this project seeks to provide support to IDP sites in Idlib and Aleppo to build the resilience of the communities and promote a safe environment and dignity of life-saving services at camps located in NWS.
By pursuing the referred main objective, the project aims to achieve the following outcomes:
1. Improving the management quality in and accountability of IDP sites
2. Provision of streamlined humanitarian life-saving, multi-sectoral assistance in IDPs sites
For this purpose, a set of comprehensive and interlinked activities will be conducted in coordination with CCCM, WASH, Shelter and Education Clusters. In particular, the project's activities in 10 camps include:
• Establishment Camp management committees as well as Site Management committees, that are going to engage the IDPs and build the governance and accountability mechanisms of the site.
• Building the Communities’ capacity by establishing a rapidly deployed team, which will conduct diverse training and capacity-building sessions for the established committees.
• Enhancing the Engagement and accountability of Community members through FGDs, establishing sub-communities and feedback mechanisms.
• facilitation IDP sites with Fire Safety Points and Fire response items
• Provision of camp care and camp maintenance through conducting regular repair activities to infrastructure and public facilities.
- Coordination of multisectoral services provision.
- Establish inclusive sectroal camp committees with the balanced representation of women, older people and people with disabilities.
- Expand CCCM presence to remote sites through CCCM mobile response mechanism .
- Engage IDPs on sustainable solutions to their displacement and advocacy on alternatives; including livelihoods, HLP access etc.
- Incident Reporting .
- Discussing exit /phaseout strategy: mapping relevant stakeholders, capacity building and handover of site management functions.
Furthermore, the project foresees the identification of vulnerable HHs with a specific focus on severe vulnerability, including female-headed households and PwDs. Moreover, the project is specifically designed to foster social cohesion among IDPs themselves through the establishment of Community Committees for advising the design and implementation of the project activities, fostering local ownership, being accountable, and ensuring the sustainability of the achievements of the project upon its completion.
The project foresees the participation of all relevant stakeholders at all stages of the operations management cycle.
During camp management committees' trainings, TAS will focus on the importance of preserving the environment, where these committees will spread knowledge and raise the camp residents' awareness of the best ways to help preserve the environment and the most key factors that lead to damage to it.
