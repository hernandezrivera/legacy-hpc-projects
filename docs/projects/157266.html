Project Summary

A decade and half of hostile conflict, insecurity and droughts led to massive displacement of civilians in Jebel Marra areas such as; Tur, Nertiti and the surrounding areas and have placed women and girls in extreme vulnerability and at increased risk of gender-based violence (GBV). Over the years Tur and Nertiti were two of the most affected, with very high levels of displacement and food insecurity. Even though the humanitarian communities have been responding, continuous external shocks limit the population’s access to basic services and the levels of vulnerability constantly fluctuate. As a result women and children are forced to adopt risky and sometimes harmful coping strategies, including collection of firewood (where they’re faced with multiple risks) as well as forced marriages for under-aged girls.
Secondly, pressure for survival intensifies the potential for intimate partner violence leading to high level of domestic violence that are mostly gender based. PCS has noted high prevalence of GBV and lack of capacity to prevent and respond to it during its recent assessment.
Thirdly, Despite the fact that there are cases of rape, physical / domestic violence and child molestation in Nertiti IDPs camps and Tur and their surrounding areas such as; Guildu, Gurney, Saaga Naga, Saaga Dir, Guildu, Male, Dabanga and Dumu, there is no aid agency actively providing GBV services such as; post rape kits, treatment and counselling of rape survivors as well as case management
There are currently no foster families trained and no safe shelters or material support for SGBV survivors.
PCS has also identified a wide gap in knowledge and practice resulting in authorities and community leaders having limited to none practical involvement on GBV prevention and response.
The Proposed Intervention
This intervention aims to provide an appropriate multidimensional response to reduce the incidence of GBV, mitigate its impact through innovative approaches, interactive and proactive strategies and address sector priorities as well as curtail domestic and community level violence and reduce women and children’s vulnerability.
Description of the proposed intervention
PCS is proposing to implement this integrated protection project across 8 communities and 3 IDPs camps in west Jebel Marra locality in Central Darfur. The 8 communities include; Nertiti, Tur, Guildu, Saaga Dir, Saaga Naaga, Debanga, Male and Dumu and 3 IDPs camps are; Khor Ramla IDPs camp and Northern and Southern IDPs camps.
The intervention will focus on prevention and response to gender-based violence, with particular emphasis on building the capacity of the target communities to understand the different vulnerabilities and risks factors faced by men, women, boys and girls and take practical measures to mitigate, prevent and respond to violence against women and children. The GBV prevention and response activities proposed are specifically tailored for women, men, boys and girls. Special needs will be taken into consideration throughout all stages of the intervention. Collection of data (always disaggregated by sex and age) will be conducted by gender balanced teams. Capacity building components will guarantee equal participation of men, women, girls and boys. Taking into consideration their specific needs and risks, the intervention will give special attention to girls and women.
The proposed activities will result in securing the protection of women, girls and children who are SGBV survivors and increase their access to life-saving services.

Activities

1. Provision of GBV services including case management, safe shelter, basic emotional support and health referral to the main health facilities in Nertiti town 2. Provision of life saving material assistance (dignity kits, shelter kits and transport cost) to GBV survivors based on individual assessments by GBV case workers. 3. Identification and training of foster families and provision of material assistance and emergency safe shelters to survivors by already identified foster families. Foster families will be asked to be on standby in case an emergency safe shelter is needed to secure the protection of women and girls GBV survivors. 4. Provision of tailored NFI/Shelter items to the survivors’ host to assure hospitality in a dignified manner. 5. Provision of life saving emergency food (flour, sugar and oil) for GBV survivors (based on the IASC guidelines on MH and Psychosocial support in emergency settings (Geneva 2007 – first layer of the intervention pyramid called basic services and security) 6. Capacity building on Clinical Management of Rape Survivors (CMR) for 10 health staff from Nertiti Hospital (formal training and mentoring sessions). Among the participants GBV focal points will be identified and supported to provide case management and referrals Creation of awareness campaigns to communities, authorities and humanitarian actors to strengthen the engagement of communities, service providers and authorities and support GBV mainstreaming with cluster partners in other humanitarian priority areas. 8. Creation of GBV working group / community in all target areas to discuss GBV issues in a safe and confidential manner in communities and at the GBV working group. 9. Conduct 66 GBV field coordination meetings with community leaders and local authorities to link them into the GBV referral system.
