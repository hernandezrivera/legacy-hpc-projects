Project Summary

Samaritan's Purse (SP) has reflected upon lessons learned from a current three-year water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) program nearing completion in Lare woreda, Gambella region, to develop a strategy to increase access to potable water and sustainable WASH facilities. Samaritan's Purse will seek to achieve this through the construction, rehabilitation and installation of hand and solar water pumps, water points, water pipe networks, reservoirs and communal latrines; the training of water mechanics and water user committees; the implementation of mass health, hygiene and sanitation campaigns and awareness trainings; and training people in biblical leadership and discipleship through local churches across the target areas. • The project will reach 47,147 individuals (23,102 male and 24,044 female) across the Lare and Abobo woredas of Gambella region. All beneficiaries will be impacted by improved, clean water schemes; the same beneficiaries will receive supplemental sanitation and hygiene trainings and resources.
