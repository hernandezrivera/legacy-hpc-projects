Project Summary

The project will support 18,000 direct beneficiaries (52% female; and 48% male – as per the Shelter Cluster Caseload disaggregation) in the IDP settlements in Banadir and Bay regions (9,000 beneficiaries in Banadir and 9,000 in Bay region), who have been affected by significant increase in forced evictions in urban centers, ongoing conflict and insecurity, and the continued lack of livelihood opportunities in the region. These beneficiaries will be supported with access to adequate and dignified shelter solutions, provision of basic NFI kits, and provision of cluster standardized Emergency Shelter Kits (ESKs) & Basic NFI (BNFI) kits.
The Emergency Shelter and Non-food Items (ES/NFI) response will be consolidated by establishing a team that will provide assistance on a regular basis to the displaced populations in the proposed locations.
Human Appeal will collaborate the Ministry of Humanitarian & Disaster Management whereby the ministry will provide 1,000 tents to install for the people in need in Baidoa and Mogadishu. HA will therefore mobilize complementary funds for this project to ensure that 1,000 households have improved access to temporary shelter.
The Land certificates will be obtained from relevant local municipalities before the implementation of the project is initiated. Beneficiary choice is of primary importance and Human Appeal will present them a first choice of prototype shelter, which they will be able to evaluate and prioritize.

Project Activities

The project will support 18,000 of direct beneficiaries through the following activities:1. Installation of 1,000 tents for the affected population in IDP settlements in Mogadishu and Baidoa2. Support 18,000 beneficiaries by providing 3,000 with basic NFI kits (including kitchen set, jerry can, solar light, ropes, and plastic sheets).3. Distribution of 1,000 Emergency Shelter Kits (ESOKs); supporting 6,000 beneficiaries of which 25% are men, 47% women, 21% children and 1% are disadvantaged groups (elderly, disabled etc.)4. Human Appeal will conduct a Post Distribution/construction Monitoring (PDM/PCM) to ensure accountability, appropriateness of materials and effectiveness of distribution. Data will be disaggregated by gender, age and location to evaluate whether specific needs of women, girls, boys and men in these target locations are met. The results will be shared with the S/NFI cluster and donor for evaluation.

Mitigation Measures

1b. Protection of civilians: People displace to a certain city/camp for the purpose of having better access to livelihood opportunities. Given that fact, the impact of armed conflict between government in collaboration with AMISOM against Alshabab will be mitigated through proper coordination with S/NFI Cluster, engagement of relevant local authorities and advocacy at national and international level. 4a. Protection mainstreaming: The project will ensure protection is mainstreamed in all shelter interventions. To proactively avoid possibilities of forced evictions, the project will timely engage with relevant stakeholders’ i.e. local authorities, municipality, community/camp leaders etc. to ensure that legally compliant and effective land title deeds are provided to beneficiaries. Furthermore, Human Appeal will ensure that land tenure to be long enough through legal agreement with the landlord in the witness of the government/local authority, community leaders and relevant stakeholders. This is the purpose of having Land certificates from relevant local municipalities before the implementation of the project is initiated to ensure that the infrastructure will last long and also people are not evicted. 4b. Human Appeal will ensure the four key Protection Mainstreaming principles are met, those specifically of safety and dignity in implementation, meaningful access to services, participation and empowerment of affected communities to the services provided, and accountability. Human Appeal will ensure that safety and dignity will be assured for beneficiaries by maintaining confidentiality from first contact through assessments through referrals and provision of assistance and distributions. Safety and dignity will also be established in that all sites are free of hazards, are in safe locations, and are clearly humanitarian spaces. Human Appeal will ensure meaningful access to services is met by establishing distribution locations that are accessible to persons with disabilities, in close proximity to where beneficiaries reside.

Please provide further explanation

Human Appeal will ensure that the planned construction of the shelters will be durable to enable the beneficiary maintain their normal life. The project will help the beneficiaries have access to durable emergency shelter where they will not suffer from the hotness of the sun and raining/cold as well. Similarly, the project will contribute to the increase of the number of people protected from the weather by provision of emergency shelter and NFIs. This will extend the response acquired by the beneficiaries. Also, the project will contribute to the resilience of the affected population in the target areas by providing durable shelter to protect them from the cold. The project, however, ensures that Human Appeal exercises the Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) principle by deploying beneficiary feedback mechanism where people will have access to hotline number and suggestions boxes to ensure that corrective action can be taken.
