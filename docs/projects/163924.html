Project Summary

AL Resala Foundation (RF), aim through the WASH services in 3 camps North west of Idleb in sub district of (Dana, Salqin, and Harim,).
From 1st December 2019 to 1st January 2020, almost 300,000 people fled from their homes, mainly from southern Idleb governorate, moving further north away from the hostilities. Ma’arrat An Nu’man and its countryside are reportedly depopulated, while thousands of people from Saraqab and its eastern countryside fled in anticipation of hostilities extending to their area.
The project will continue provide the WASH services package in the camps were located in Idleb - Harim(Al Manarah, Alwadi ,and Jannat Alqra) . in terms of construction and rehabilitation
The project taken into account intervention in case of flooding for the selected camps, bathroom/toilet units rise from the ground 20cm in addition to the tendency of the roof in a calculated manner to flow the rain water to manholes. concrete foundation builds for each storage water tanks for drinking and rise from the ground. Construction of septic tanks will be in the low ground of the camp to avoid mix the water from or flood or rain. It has been verified that the selected camps are not located in the valley of flood channel. The construction for the WASH infrastructure in the camps has been designed for future construction for influx of additional IDPs. The project aims to target 2,100 families.
- Construction, and rehabilitation of Bathrooms / toilets units,
- Construction, and rehabilitation reinforced concrete septic tanks,
- Construction, and rehabilitation of sewer network system and this will take two months,
- WASH services for the camps Providing daily water trucking 287 cubic meter, water treatment services. Solid waste management, and this service will run for 5 months. Dislodging of the sewage from septic tanks, Running and cleaning Bathroom and toilets units, the services run for 3 months.
- WASH installation installing Communal water storage tanks for drinking, solar power for lighting units, and galvanized water tanks for washing Bathrooms and toilets units.
- Distribution of 2,100 Hygiene kits in the second month of the project implementation, in addition to post distribution for the hygiene kits. Distribution 8,400 Jerry cans for each family two Jerry cans per duration, during the project implementation will be two distribution of Jerry cans.
- Focus group discussion (FGD) eight
- Monitoring and Evaluation by RF staff
- Consultant for training hygiene Promotion campaigns for one month consist of men and women team
Topics are
- Drinking Water Quality, Effective water management, Measurement & Treatment water
- Communication skills between trainees and IDPs.
- Hygiene Awareness and Environmental Sanitation, Importance effective hand washing in the critical times and Safe use of latrines and blocking the route of germs.
- Water & sanitation Related Diseases.
- Monitoring tools explanation.
- The project starts in first of April 2020 running for six months’ period, with a clear exit strategy
This project aims to support the coordination with the WASH actors and local councils and camp managers through monthly and weekly meetings to review the WASH needs and to develop an integrated WASH response. However, monthly WASH cluster meetings will be attended by RF

4 - Needs

Since the 1st of December, more than 349 have been displaced mainly from the southern Idleb governorate. The displaced households were basically from Ma’arrat An Nu’man and Saraqeb Sub Distrıcts moving further north away from the hostilities to Atreb, Idleb, and Maaret Tamisrin Sub Districts. This is raising the total displaced people in northwest Syria to 2.44 M. CCCM updates.Most of IDPs in the assessed locations are coming mainly from Ma’arrat An Nu’man (75%) with some from Saraqab (5%), Kafr Nobol (4%), Heish (4%) and other locations such as Ariha and Khan Shaykun. 98% of the IDPs have displaced from less than a month, so during the last trigger which started from the 18th of December 2019. While 20% have arrived within only 4_7 days, 41% within one to two weeks, and 37% within 2_4 weeks. Only 2% displaced from more than a month. Among the assessed locations, 32% of the vulnerable individuals are lactating women or pregnant, 13% persons with chronic diseases, 9% single person head of household, 6% are war wounded, and 5% are persons with disability.52% of the people are facing a shortage in WASH services, the primary source of water drinking as HHs mentioned is mainly water trucking (96%) and the secondary source is piped water to dwelling (4%). On the other hand, the confirmed that (91%) of the water source is through water trucks and (9%) are piped water. 57% mentioned that women can’t access clean and safe water, the most challenges of accessing for both women and men are (62%)The most hygiene items needed according to the interviewed HHs, are mainly detergents for dishes, soap for washing and laundry, shampoo, garbage packages, towels, sanitary napkins and other as in the figures on the side

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: health and safety of the community during implementation or rehabilitation of the water networks. Mitigation: ensure through regularly monitoring of the rehabilitation site; awareness raising meetings with the families regarding the ongoing work; well planned interventions (phased intervention) and adherence to strong guidelines for safety and protection. Risk: theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by groups and used in conflict activities.Mitigation: MoUs with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities; promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

principles of neutrality and ensure equal access to all beneficiaries without discrimination. Participation of the community leadership and the stakeholder in the need assessment and survey, beneficiaries contribute to different stages of the project since that was an idea of project interventionThese mechanisms include the creation of feedback and complaint mechanisms for projectRisks linked with activity 1: Inequality in distribution: this risk is mitigated by the geographical dispatch of water tanks and the coordination with other agencies involved in WT. a close management and monitoring system is already in place (complaint mechanism). The proposed WT is proposed along with an exit strategy towards the restoration of water supply services granting communities with an equal access to water. Risks linked with activity 2: Unequal distribution of services: this risk is mitigated by the selection for rehabilitation, jointly with key local stakeholders, of pre-existing water systems which used to guarantee an equal access to the resource. The design of the planned works takes into account the demand for an equal coverage of the targeted locations (connection to networks possible, gravity system). inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors & Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials: this risk is very low (formerly besieged locations now controlled by the GoS and far from armed groups).The transfer of materials and equipment will not imply crossing risky areas. Given the critical situation with regards to water and the demand of communities and authorities alike for interventions, the risk of looting of equipment is considered as low; it will be lessened by the coordination with Water Authorities and by a final formal handover.
