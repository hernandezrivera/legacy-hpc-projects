Project Summary

MWL proposed project seeks to provide protective education environment opportunities to vulnerable children (boys and girls) n four sub-districts: (Dana, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin). that selected as most priority to ensure expanding ongoing education services by availing inclusive protective learning spaces to ensure that no children absent or drop out of schools' due to lack of learning spaces, learning and teaching materials, economic capacity of the Education Directorate and the parents to cover all the schools & Student’s needs.
MWL proposed project aims to contribute in supporting crisis-affected-students to get access to Education (formal and non-formal) education and raising the quality of education in-line with EiE standards and inclusion Children with Disability through rehabilitation and equip schools, provision transportation and provision PSS, awareness raising and inclusion activities.
That will be achieved through;
1)Rehabilitation for classrooms in targeted schools to raise their capacity to absorb more IDP students
2) providing Schools' furniture / Students' seats to enhance the capacity of schools;
3) Training the targeted teachers and administrative staff on CP core, PSS, classroom management, modern teaching methods;
4) Provision stationary, teaching & learning materials, textbooks and Student's kit;
5) Using the interim government curricula in formal education and curriculum B for non-formal education in the two semesters during the school year, and Providing literacy and numeracy skills, remedial & catch-up classes for non-formal program
6) Conducting ASER to assess learning;
7) Establishing 2nd shift to providing formal / non-formal education in the school to raise its capacity to absorb new IDPs Boys and Girls;
8) Establishing CFSs inside schools in line with INEEE & CPMS that will provide structured PSS, sensitization that target students, caregiver, parents, community key members and mobile teams will conduct BTLs, awareness raising sessions on ERW (Explosive Remnant War)
9) providing incentives for teachers and administrative staff
10) Conduct winterization campaign through distributing of Heating fuel and heaters the through Voucher Scheme to ensure the safety and security for the schools, prevent the fraud & corruption in procurement process
11) Establishing PTAs to follow up with student’s education improvement and issues;
The M&E Coordinator will be responsible to oversight the complaint mechanism where beneficiaries will be able to provide feedback or complaint to ensure satisfied of beneficiaries
Evaluation of the project will be conducted by the M&E staff throughout all the project duration that will started by conducting “base-line survey”, monitoring and compares the progress development and conduct an “end-line survey” to measure the achievements of intervention outputs.
A lesson learning event will be conducted at the end of the project life cycle to draw some lessons and best practices from the project.

4 - Needs

The proposed project was built based on the needs of affected persons in the northwest of Syria., MWL has targeted the locations with the most needs & highest severity based on the ASO1. Based on the HNO report 2019, in the Education Cluster, on level of sub-district. The severity scale in 4 targeted location is very high. Based on the HNO report 2019. Those 4 sub-districts have been visited to conduct the need assessment in terms of vulnerabilities of children and their families, technical needs, security situation, complementary and GAM considerations. MWL team conducted needs assessment using KII & Filling Questionnaire focusing on Educational and Protection needs. The assessment has been conducted in coordination with Education Directorates, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. The assessment gathered Sex and Age Dis-aggregated Data of HC & IDPS, PWD, Out of School/B&G. According to the information that we received, in targeted sub-district schools absent due to harsh cold weather last year, with inability to provide fuel for heating for schools, schools’ administration forced to return back the children to their homes daily due to Severe cold, that’s leads the Education directorate concern from additional dropping out of schools.High level of psycho social distress among children (B&G) and youth (M&F) due to traumatic experiences, and violence were the key findings. Some deaths due to ERW-related causes were also reported. As per discussions with Education directorates that shown the weakness of education services in the selected area" The education system is overburdened and overstretched to the extent that schools, overcrowded and under-resourced. The key impediments for children accessing to education are including safety and security along roads to and from schools and alternative learning spaces; Lack of teaching and learning materials; poor quality teaching; damage of education infrastructure.

5 - Implementation of activities

Based on a broad and deep understanding of needs and barriers to access to quality education for children in the target areas, MWL designed the project activities. The project will implement the following activities:1- Educational activities:• MWL will provide schools with formal and non- formal education activities (ALP and remedial), this will contribute in support the efforts to return the children into formal education,• Also, teachers in the target schools will be provided with incentives.• Teachers will be trained on interactive non-formal education programs.• MWL will Conduct Back to Learning " BTLs" campaigns• Project will work on rehabilitation the school’s infrastructure, equip Wash facilities and special needs bathrooms, with gender-sensitive approach2- PSS services3- Protection activities: identify and refer children with CP issues to appropriate interventions, as well as contributing to protect the students and teachers from risks of fire by providing schools with fire extinguishers and putting wall stickers "with graphics that suit the ages, sex, and consciousness of children" in the schools for fire-safety and instruction manuals for using the extinguishers, in addition to providing schools with first aid kits,- Project will enhance the resilience, awareness and capacity of children and caregivers and community members to prevent and respond to the violations against children and their rights.Two levels of reporting will be conducted throughout the implementation of the project. Programmatic reporting provided from the team leader on the field directly to the Program Coordinator & Project Manager.2. Monitoring reports will be provided from the M&E field officer on the field, directly to the M&E Coordinator based on the data that M&E team collected in the field and the quality of implementationProject monitoring and evaluation systems will be predicated on the project log-frame and M&E plan focusing specifically on indicator measurement
