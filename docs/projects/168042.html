Project Summary

To respond to the impacts of COVID 19, the Zimbabwe government, through the National Preparedness and Response Plan (NPRP, 2019) has established prevention and containment strategies and procedures. An important area of focus is containment measures at Points of Entry (P0E), identified in the NPRP as airports and land crossing of international entry and exit of travellers, cargo and conveyances. To slow down the international spread of COVID-19, the NRP points to the need for staff and infrastructure at POE to be prepared to detect and manage ill people and to refer them to public health services safely. Women and men's experiences of point of entry procedures and requirements are different, as are the risks. For example, pregnant and lactating women, risks of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), as well as sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) and issues such as maintenance of menstrual hygiene. Activities at POE should also support surveillance and risk communication activities that integrate a gender dimension. This will include institutions and organisations with a gender mandate and national focus, in particular the Zimbabwe Gender Commission. It is thus essential that standard operating procedures and staff at points of entry mainstream gender and protection, and have the capacity to respond to gender and protection issues respectively.
Part of the measures to contain the spread of COVID 19 includes establishing quarantine facilities. Isolating people is an important factor in containing the virus, but this can also expose quarantined people, in particular women and girls, to gendered risks including SGBV and SEA. There are three dimensions that authorities must pay attention to, namely integrating gender and protection into quarantine centres design and management, prevention SGBV and SEA from taking place within the centres, and provision of an effective and prompt response to gender and protection issues in the centres. It is critical that authorities managing quarantine build their capacities to respond to gender and protection issues, whilst people, in particular women and girls, are effectively protected.

1 - Activities

The current COVID 19 National Preparedness and Response Plan pillar focusing on points of entry (POE) and isolation of people as a prevention and containment measure, does not adequately address gender and protection issues. It is thus important that gender and protection are mainstreamed in all strategies and standard operating procedures. This implies supporting the targeted authorities to raise awareness and providing them with technical backstopping to integrate gender and protection in their guiding documents. This shall extend to personnel managing quarantine centres, and ensuring support and protection of women and girls from SGBV and SEA. Access to risk related information by persons, especially women and girls, coming through points of entry, is key, and organisational partners and institutions' communication strategies related to COVID 19, and authorities at POE, shall be supported to ensure effective implementation of these strategies. In particular the Zimbabwe Gender Commission, which has a mandate to monitor, from a gender perspective, the implementation of the COVID 19 National Preparedness and Response Plan, shall be supported to ensure its risk communications are widely disseminated and reach intended beneficiaries, including at POEs and quarantine centres. The main objective of this project is the strengthened resilience of the most vulnerable women and girls, to mitigate their risk of exposure to protection risks during COVID-19 IPC and response. In this regard specific objectives shall focus on:-1. Integrating gender and protection into all POE and quarantine centres guiding documents, including standard operating procedures. Technical backstopping shall be provided to the relevant authorities.2. Building the capacity of POE officials, including protection officers and front line responders in quarantine centres to identify and address gender and protection issues 3. Provide targeted risk communication information integrating gender and protection issue at POE and in quarantine centres, with a special focus on women and girls 4. Enhancing protection of women and girls in quarantine centres through ensuring targeted support is provided by the relevant authorities (women officers, gender responsive infrastructure)4. Support the Zimbabwe Gender Commission in its monitoring role of the implementation of the COVID 19 National Preparedness and Response Plan, through production of knowledge products integrating gender and protection
