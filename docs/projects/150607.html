Project Summary

DCA with Ahli Arab Hospital (AAH) will support trauma and non-trauma patients by providing much needed surgeries for them. Regarding non-trauma patients AAH will target 800 cases that are on the waiting list of the Ministry of Health (MoH) and in need of elective surgeries either through Minimal Invasive Surgeries or by open abdomen. Selection of the beneficiaries will be based on the vulnerability of the patients and AAH capacity in cooperation with MOH. Regarding trauma patients, 200 cases will undergo 2-3 surgeries along the project. Therefore, the trauma management procedures will be 400 surgical procedures. Moreover, 200 injured patients will have, follow up services in the OPDs and physical therapy department for a year. The cases will be either orthopedic or general surgery related to the recent escalation in Gaza. Criteria of vulnerability will be defined in a participatory manner with MoH, cluster level and partners.
Furthermore, DCA will build the capacity of 30 of AAH technical team through Augusta Victoria in IPC. Augusta Victoria who is DCA’s partner will conduct 3 visits throughout the project cycle and provide assistance to AAH in IPC and other needs.

Needs

Overall, an estimated 2.5 million people needs humanitarian assistance in the occupied Palestine, including 1.2 million refugees mostly in the Gaza Strip. Gazans suffer from protracted crises in all life aspects. For example, according to the world bank September 2018, “Gaza is collapsing economically” as well unemployment rate in Gaza Strip is 49.1% according to the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics. While the blockade is a major component in the collapse of the Gaza Health Care System, the 30th March escalation has overburdened the health care system in either responding appropriately to trauma management of cases which is beyond Government capacity or by meeting non-trauma case’s needs.Regarding trauma cases, around 20,000 were injured while around 10,000 are in need for hospitalization and trauma management as the average cost of each case exceeds 1,000 USD as each one would need 2 surgical procedures at least, devices such as external fixator or lizarov, dressing and nursing care, follow up in OPDs and rehabilitation. This exhausts the Government Health Care System, which makes it essential to increase the capacity of the health NGOs especially surgical hospitals. Regarding non-trauma, there are 8,000 patients who have limited access to emergency healthcare services due to the increased burden caused by the trauma casualties. Those, non-trauma patients are being affected by the ongoing violence, not because they are being directly injured in large numbers, but because their treatment and healthcare is being interrupted for other urgent conditions.These challenges leave the health system broadly unable to cope with the high casualty rates from the demonstrations without additional support. Therefore, AAH through DCA will support those cases, conduct surgeries for them, and follow up the cases until rehabilitation phase for free of charge, Based on coordination and communication with MOH. As AAH is a surgical hospital with Minimal Invasive Surgeries (MIS) capacity in General laparoscopy, Gynecology and Arthroscopy. AAH has a very good capacity in surgical field and post op management. Moreover, AAH Operations Room has been empowered and equipped with Minimal Invasive Surgeries (MIS) equipment for general, gynecology and arthroscopy as well as E.N.T. In addition, DCA/ NCA wants to maintain and develop the support to AAH through Augusta Victoria Hospital (AVH) in IPC work at AAH. AVH will contribute through its technical teams to addressing standards in infection prevention appropriate use of antibiotics and appropriate laboratory testing standards.

Activities

Outcome 1: - Injured trauma people received a prompt and comprehensive lifesaving trauma management services.According to the recent escalation and conflict, there are lots of trauma cases who need prompt and comprehensive trauma management, which is beyond the government capacity. Therefore, AAH through DCA will support those cases, conduct surgeries for them, and follow up the cases until rehabilitation phase. AAH has a great capacity in surgical field and post op management as AAH has recently developed its capacity in Infection Prevention Control (IPC) work by Augusta Victoria. Moreover, AAH guest surgical international missions that support patients in follow up surgeries such as reconstructions surgeries as skin grafts flaps …etc. In addition, AAH Operations Room has been equipped with Minimal Invasive Surgeries (MIS) equipment for general, gynecology and arthroscopy as well as E.N.T. AAH will conduct 400 surgical procedures for trauma cases free of charge as follows:1.1.1 Conduct 400 surgical procedures for 200 trauma cases as each patient might need 2-3 surgeries.1.1.2 Provide 200 cases receive follow up in OPDs (outpatient clinics) including (80 cases) receive physical therapy rehabilitation. Outcome 2: - Patients who needed elective (non-trauma) surgeries receive a comprehensive surgical and health management.Based on coordination and communication with WHO, there are 8,000 patients needed elective (non-trauma) cases at MOH waiting list. As AAH is a surgical hospital with Minimal Invasive Surgeries (MIS) capacity in General laparoscopy, Gynecology and Arthroscopy, AAH is able to conduct non-trauma surgeries for patients with much less complication. Therefore, AAH wants to target 800 patients along the project life cycle including men, women, girls and boys. AAH has the capacity to conduct general surgeries, gynecology and orthopedic using MIS equipment. Recently through USAID/IMC fund for the last 14th month, AAH has increased its OR capacity and been equipped with new laparoscope and arthroscopy as well, developed guidelines in MIS & IPC. In addition, AAH has highly qualified consultant surgeons in Orthopedic, Gynecology, E.N.T and general surgeries. Furthermore, AAH receives national & international medical missions and has cooperation and coordination with AVH. 1.2.1 Conduct 800 surgeries for elective/ non-trauma cases including women, men, boys and girls.1.2.2 Provide postoperative & follow up for the cases either inpatient as pot operative in the departments or outpatient clinics (OPDs) including dressing & nursing care, medical follow up until Physical therapy stage and full recovery.Outcome 3: - AAH health system strengthening, preparedness and quality assurance for the program implementation provided.DCA/NCA will AAH develop its support through Augusta Victoria Hospital (AVH) in IPC work at AAH. Moreover, AVH will contribute through its technical teams to addressing standards in infection prevention appropriate use of antibiotics and appropriate laboratory testing standards. This need was identified through the AVH missions attending to the injured in Gaza where the main threat to their survival was wound infections. AVH team can also contribute through this support to the patients’ safety in the area of surgical safety addressed under outcome one and two. As well; DCA/NCA will monitor project activities and ensure compliance to donor. In addition, DCA/NCA will jointly participate in the health cluster and update on the activities to ensure that project activities are being announced and information reached patients. 3.1.1 Commission three technical support missions for AAH to build on the IPC work through Augusta Victoria Hospital (3 missions of 3 experts for 3 days).3.1.2 Conduct monitoring visits, procurement support, financial transfers, management support and reporting the progress of the project.3.1.3 Provide regular update to the health cluster about the progress and challenges.
