Project Summary

In the emergency context of Burkina Faso, telecommunications assets are being targeted through security incidents, which limits access to connectivity to the humanitarian responders. Under the leadership of the World Food Programme, the Information Technology Common Service (ITCS) sector aims to provide reliable and efficient communications services, including connectivity via VSAT, green energy solutions, and technical support for the wider humanitarian community in the five UN hubs and various hard-to-reach areas.
Furthermore, this project will ensure that the humanitarian community can access vital information, and provide timely information sharing, and reporting to carry out their life-saving activities. This will also improve communication and facilitate connections with the broader community and will help the community to stay connected with family and friends and participate in online meetings, webinars, etc.
