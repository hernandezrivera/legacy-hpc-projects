Project Summary


The project aims to increase access to basic curative and preventive health care services for IDPs and conflict affected populations in targeted areas in Al maara area in Idlib governorate .
activities focus on meeting health cluster’s response strategy and EHSP through the provision of primary health care that include reproductive , maternal , Antenatal & postnatal care , and child Health .
general practitioners provide treatment of non communicable diseases & communicable diseases. The project provides dental ,physiotherapy, nutrition services as well as supportive services with simple laboratory performing CBC, urine analysis, blood group and stool analysis.
100% of all provided services including drugs and lab are given free of cost.
The project is part of referral system , 1 ambulance provide referral services to specialized centers and hospitals . The project is sentinel site to EWARN .
mobile medical clinics/units provide primary health care services to displaced and vulnerable who identified in the needs assessment as having difficulty accessing health services .
The project will serve a target of about 50,000

Needs

• Provision of health services continues to be disrupted by the ongoing conflict. Attacks on health have rendered 46 per cent of hospitals and primary health facilities in Syria as either partially functional or not functional , while in some areas humanitarian partners have been unable to secure sustained and predictable access to populations in need.• Displaced populations and returnees, particularly those in northeast and northwest Syria, are vulnerable to outbreaks of infectious diseases due to unsanitary living conditions, overstretched health services, and low coverage of routine immunization.• Sustaining basic essential health services remains a key need across the country. Thirty-seven per cent of the affected population requires routine reproductive, maternal, neonatal and child health (RMNCH) services; an estimated 41 per cent of Syria’s adult population requires treatment for one or more non-communicable diseases (NCDs) ; and at least 15 per cent of the population requires mental health and psychosocial services (MHPSS) , but the infrastructure and referral system that remains functional after seven years of conflict is inadequate to cope with existing needs. According to HNO 2019 PIN (160,129 ) in Maaret Annuman and Health severity is 3

Implementation of activities

1- provide essential primary health services to cover the health care needs of vulnerable populations, including Reproductive, and Child Health ,dental , internal as well as continuity of treatment for people who live with non-communicable disease .2- provide physical rehabilitation services and assistive devices.3- provide medicines . 4- provide health education and public health awareness .5-provide PHC services through medical mobile clinic6- support epidemiological surveillance and support the response to diseases outbreaks through early warning systems. 7-Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services .8-provide laboratory services 9-provide nutrition services10- support referral system through providing referral services.

Protection Risk Analysis

risks risk of sexual exploitation 1- ( presence of female cadres to increase the access of women to health services ) 2- provide community members information about existing code of conduct for health personnel ,as well as to where to report sexual exploitation and abuse committed by health personnel ) 3-provide training for staff on the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse . 4- Establishing effective and comprehensive mechanisms to ensure awareness raising on PSEA amongst personnel, with continuous training on the topic. 5- Effective recruitment and performance management of all humanitarian staff. 6-Provide information to communities and people affected by crisis about the organization, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave, the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver. risk of travelling for older person and person with disability 1- (provide free transportation through referral system ) - risk of GBV:- access to the reproductive health services of the Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) as part of the essential health package.- keep records of patients in reproductive health clinic confidential .- provide awareness sessions on GBV.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

risks : - The lack of information about available basic and specialized care and services location. ( work together with local and national authorities to inform the IDPs, host communities about the availability and location of services)- Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers: ( Applying the designated de-confliction mechanism for health facilities) ( Continued reporting on attacks against healthcare)-The continuous displacement and uprooting of vulnerable persons, weakening their health status( introduce mobile clinics to provide health services to affected people)- The general insecurity impeding access to health services. - The shortages of drugs and medical supplies due to increased demand. ( finding different sources who can provide medicine)
