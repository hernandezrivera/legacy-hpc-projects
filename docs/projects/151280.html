Project Summary

The proposed project will targeted both drought and conflict displaced population in Hiran region of Somalia . The primary objectives of the project will be to improve a) protection of conflict affected children by preventing violence; abuse; exploitation and neglect through preventative and responsive child protection interventions and B) Strengthen prevention and response interventions against sexual and gender based violence against most vulnerable groups specially displaced women and girls.
The targeted IDP communities in Hiran region faced numerous protection threats and risk on regularly bases specially boys and girls; and women. Violence against the children are too common specially physical abuse ; neglect and exploitation as well as increased incident of sexual violence against women and girls. This project will have two prong approaches towards addressing the protection concerns and risks ; firstly by undertaking preventative approaches where appropriate community based structures will be established; trained and will subsequently undertake awareness actions within their communities and as well as empowering children and women with life-skills interventions which can significantly increase their protection; secondly responsive interventions will be initiated to response in the addressing immediate the protection incidents through case management and structured psychosocial support for children and parents.
The following interventions will be carried out through this project:
1. Case Management : Children with protection concerns/issues and sexual violence against girls cases will be provided direct support through holistic case management interventions . ( Target 600 caseload i.e 200 per region)
2. Community Based child Protection mechanism : Child welfare committees ( CWCs) will be formed , trained and mentored to undertake community based protection activities /tasks ; a total 3 CWC each consisting of 20 members will be formed and trained. ( CWC member 60 )
4. Psychosocial Support Interventions: Newly displaced children with high level psychological distress will be targeted through structured mobile or static CFS interventions. Improving PSS wellbeing is an important first line emergency response for the protection of children during acute humanitarian emergencies. 3 CFS will be established in Hiran ; total 3 CFS @ targeting 250 children = total Children 750)
5. GBV Preventions Awareness campaigns: A series of Awareness and outreach activities will be undertaken in-order to significantly reduce the prevalence of the GBV incidents. ( Target- 1200 Adults, 400 Children will be reached directly with GBV awareness activities)
6. Life-skills trainings for GBV survivors or girls at risk of SGBV; a total of 250 girls will be targeted for a series of structured life-skills trainings and this will provide remedial recovery of the girls .
7. Provision of dignity kits for adolescent girls as well as providing them orientation on the use of sanitary pad so that the risks related to re-infections is prevented , A total of 250 girls will be targeted.

Needs

Conflict displaced IDPs in Hiran region faced numerous combined protection problems; the impact of the armed conflict and forced displacement has had huge psychological distress on the population; the safety in the IDP camps is also poor; Many of the IDP Household member have adapted negative coping mechanism specially sending children to work in very hazardous work; Child labour is endemic in all assessed IDP locations in Beletwein( All IDP sites including floods affected IDPs sites). Child impedes access to education for children and further expose children to abuse ; violence and exploitation. Many of the parents in the assessed IDP camps don’t comprehend the negative impact of child labour; through this proposed project concerted effort will be made through parents awareness and community based structures in create a long lasting awareness on the negative impact of the child labour . FGDs with boys and girls in 6 assessed IDP sites mentioned increased parents beating and this is linked with parental distress; Positive parenting session will included in the awareness sessions so that this physical abuse is reduced and addressed. According to JMCNA ( August 2018); Sexual and gender based violence is still a common threat to IDP women and girls; the heightened risk of SGBV is too common in all the IDP sites in southern and central Somalia . HRDO will address the SGBV through case management and prevention awareness interventions. Women and Adolescent girls from then minority groups have been particularly vulnerable to SGBV incidents due their reduce community based protection and clan protection in particular . Women and Adolescent girls will be trained on relevant life skills for their empowerment and as well provided with strong structured awareness interventions.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

IDPs in the targeted locations are discriminated from accessing public services and are sometime denied access to security due to the displacement . Clan-based discrimination is a common incidents in all the targeted camps specially IDPs household from minority groups as well as female headed households are particularly affected by the discrimination. Gatekeeping from the landowners and their proxies exploited IDP due to their vulnerability and sometimes subject them to absolute deprivation from aid service or even public services. Sexual exploitation is relation to access and document for humanitarian aid has been identified as common. Sexual violence against minority women and girls groups is extremely common and most often unreported due to lack of clan protection. Preventions/Mitigation Measures: We will conducted awareness on SGBV and discrimination issues through structured engagement with relevant stakeholders including religious leaders and business people . We will make concreted efforts to bring local authorities and traditional communities leaders to raise their voices on the clan discrimination and will call for the protection of IDP minority population. IDPs will be empowered to raise their voices on matters that affect them and will be asked to prevention clan based discrimination and calls for minority protection will be raised through out the project period. The Community based Child Protection structure will play a key role in ensuring that all protection issues are identified and reported through appriopriate channels

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Due to existing community norms ; girls and women are often subjected to exclusion due to male dominancy in the community . girls and women needs are often overlooked and are not included ion community decision making processes . Children and elder people aren't also consulted in decision making within their communities and their actual needs are also not considered .girls and women are sometimes faced the risk of sexual exploitation due their vulnerability and lack of protection. Mitigation measures: all ages and genders will be included in the project activities ; awareness will be made on ending gender and age based discrimination . We will ensure to have a balance male and female representation as well as age groups in all relevant activities.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Displaced population in the targeted locations face the threat of forced eviction or live in IDP sites where there is exploitation is too common due to lack of secured land tenure for the IDPs.Land owners or their proxies often taken some of the humanitarian support from the IDPs as part of land rental cost. Armed men from both state and non-state actors have multiple of times sexually assaults women and girls within the IDP sites or outside of the camps thereby create a sense of insecurity and fear for IDP populations. Sexual violence against minority women and girls groups is extremely common and most often unreported due to lack of clan protection.
