Project Summary

Through the project WFP will be providing unconditional food support to 73,000 refugees in the Far North region. In line with WFP Cameroon Country Strategic Plan (CSP 2018-2020 extended to 2021), life-saving assistance will be provided LVPs in Far-north. The transfer modality of food assistance to the beneficiaries will be cash-based transfers through unconditional monthly food distributions to support Local vulnerable population ’ households’ access to adequate and nutritious food, therefore prevent them from adopting negative coping practices. The full transfer value distributions will provide 2,100 kcal energy per day per person for the beneficiaries who entirely rely on humanitarian food assistance The daily food rations will comprise 350g cereals, 100g pulses, 35g vegetable, and 5g salt

1 - Besoins / Needs

For the past 9 years, the Far North Region has been experiencing increasing humanitarian crises mostly linked to the Lake Chad Basin crisis caused by Boko Haram armed groups. People affected by the crisis are struggling to survive in strenuous conditions in villages or small towns, putting enormous pressure on already scarce community resources and the local economy. The Logone-et-Chari, Mayo Tsanaga and Mayo Sava are the most affected divisions with more than 400,000 persons in situation of displacement. Whilst the majority of this mobile population is IDPs (63%), the other part is composed of returnees (23%) and out of camp Refugees (11%). The displacement has increased in the third quarter of 2019, while the returns slowed down in the same period. The arrival of IDPs in food-insecure areas has severely affected host communities. Boko Haram attacks have disrupted the economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in the Far North region, causing a depletion of livelihoods and household assets. Therefore, the existing chronic vulnerability and structural food insecurity have been aggravated. The region is predominantly arid and regularly hit by drought and seasonal floods. The most recent floods in October 2019 left about 40,000 people displaced and above 500,000 hectares of cultivated farmland destroyed. The October 2019 Cadre Harmonisé highlights that 1.3 million people in the Far North are food insecure. Out of this number, 214,324 people are severely food insecure, which means that they have limited or no access to sufficient, nutritious food required to live a healthy life. The Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) reports that vulnerable families in the conflict-affected areas of the Far North Region are experiencing ‘Stress’ level of acute food insecurity (IPC 2) due to flood and pest-related crop damage, and also attacks by Boko Haram.WFP and partner will support the setting and training of beneficiaries’ committees, who will be involved in preparation and in every food distribution and monitoring. WFP and partners will ensure that women, people with disabilities and young people have the same level of representation with men in the committees, and WFP and partners will ensure their opinion is collected at every meeting. WFP’s and partners will use the complaint and feedback mechanism, by informing beneficiaries of the existence of a green and free line through which they can submit they complaints. This information and sensitization will be done in English and in local languages, to ensure a massive understanding by refugees, and will start before the beginning of project cycle, and will go throughout the project implementation, before every monthly distribution in all distribution sites. The green number will be put at all distribution sites, and female and male personnel will be based in camps to help collecting complaints from beneficiaries. Trainings will be carried out on the use of food regularly, and sensitizations conducted on protection, fight of gender-based violence. Post distribution Monitoring will be carried out every quarter, to ensure beneficiaries are receiving and using safely nutritious food to meet their food needs, stop using coping strategies and become food secured.
