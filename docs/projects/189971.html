1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project aims at reducing the incidence rates for the most frequent water borne diseases through improvement of WASH facilities and hygiene practices especially in and around densely populated areas (IDP sites, villages hosting a high number of IDP HH and densely populated villages/areas).This multidistrict project is planned for 12 months and will support a total of 67,700 HH. The project will focus on limited geographic areas, rather than covering large areas with isolated activities, in order to achieve 100% open defecation free communities and effectively beat Cholera and AWD in those areas.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) Construction of Semi-temporary Latrines in temporary settingsCommunal latrines on a ratio of min 50 persons/1 latrine will be built in order to bridge sanitation gaps until HH-sanitation is established.2) CLTS latrine construction mobilizationHH will receive dome slabs and technical support for the self-construction of their HH-latrines, as well as HP-sessions on the importance of latrine use.3) Creation of waste management sites and teams for sites and HH waste systems for community settingsIn order to improve general hygiene conditions, HH will receive necessary inputs and training on HH based waste management (such as collection, recylcine, disposal). In sites waste management sites will be built, public, as well as HH based bins will be installed, and waste management teams will be equipped and trained. 4) Construction and Rehabilitation of Water PointsNew boreholes will be drilled, and broken waterpoints rehabilitated. Both activities will be done in a participatory manner witht the regardin water committee, which will receive training and a basic stock of maintenance materials for long term sustainability. 5) Logistic and Technical support for FIPAG for concrete water provisioning worksProcurements and technical follow up will be provided for FIPAG in order to increase their capacitiy to fix and establish water networks in a more timely manner. 6) Distribution of Hygiene and Dignity Kits according to WASH cluster standards7) Hygiene Promotion through community HPs as well as through locally developed theater and child friendly playful activities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

JIA projects are planned under strong involvement of the community on the planning process. Therefore, JIA consults different age and gender groups on their issues, needs and access to goods, service and assistance through FGDs.Additonally, in depth context and risk analysis are beign conducted befor activities initiate. Through this improved knowledge on the context and by active participation inflicting harm is being avoided and meaningful access can be guaranteed. Strong women participation, as well as participation and barrier free access for PWD will be guaranteed in all community activities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities during the project planning phase, as well as throughout the entire project cicle. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through FGD and regular community meetings and committees. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responed to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster. Additionally, JIA will implement their own feedback mechanisms including complaint boxes, community discussions and complaint desks.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Effective disability inclusion will be assured through identification of persons with disabilities including different disability status in an exhaustive vulnerability assessment and using the fundamental tools (Washington Group Questions). Persons with disabilities will be prioritized as especially vulnerable group.All project elements will adehere to the standards for physical accessibility. Furthermore, FGD wil we bonducted with PWD or with their families in order to understand specific needs and barriers in accessing WASH infrastructures and services

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project aims too reduce the environmental footprint wherever possible. As far as quality permits, NFIs are locally purchased in order to reduce transport and the related emissions and plastic is avoided wherever possible.
