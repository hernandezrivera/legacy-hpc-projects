Project Summary

to meet the immediate needs (primarily food) of children and their caregivers, especially children under five years, in locations of Kenya that are experiencing the worst food and nutrition insecurity resulting from the ongoing drought. As well as meeting immediate needs, the intervention aims to provide a holistic integrated system of support for children and caregivers through complementary measures.
