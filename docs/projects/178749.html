4 - Needs

According to HNO reports:subsidized bread prices have continued to increase throughout 2020. In government-controlled areas and NES, a bundle of bread has doubled in price (now at 100 SYP/ bundle) while both size and quality of bread have decreased.Commercial bread prices have catapulted, with an increase of more than 300 per cent since November last year. The increase in the price of bread has been caused by the Syrian currency depreciation as well as the wheat shortage in the country. In NWS, prices of bread have also tripled for a bundle of bread and only 58 per cent of the population’s minimum bread needs were met in 2020.After the recent displacements, Abrar conductedthe following survey through focused dialogue sessions, personal interviews and questionnaires, and retrieved data from the departments of the camps and through the Kobo and obtained the following data: The arrangement will be as follows: Camp Name- Number of Families-Number of People-Males (2-6)-Female(2-6)-Male (7-17) - Female (7- 17) - Male (18-59) - Female (18-59)-Males over 59 Dabeq 102 536 50 46 82 77 120 118 12 2 Toum 124 527 49 44 88 82 115 109 12 4 al bab City 300 1583 146 135 248 237 358 346 36 7In addition to 184 persons with disabilities in all regions The first concern was obtaining daily food due to poverty and the inability to make money in the event of displacement

5 - Implementation of activities

- Supply flour to bakeries After baking it, it is distributed to the beneficiaries through the distribution points We will announce tenders for the supply of flour on a daily basis According to the production capacity and the place, reputation and approval of the conditions specified by our organization, Thus, four furnaces will be selected for contract and a backup furnace will be chosen for any emergency We will supply flour daily to bakeries and receive the baked quantity packed in a bag weighing 1 kg. The distribution team will receive the quantities from the ovens and distribute them through approved distribution centers within the mentioned regions,Where distribution centers will be identified commensurate with the spread of beneficiaries and ease of access.In addition to delivering bread for the handicapped and the elderly to their places of residence where the distribution centers have prepared cards that determine the beneficiary and the quantity so that one kilogram will be distributed to each family.The arrival of the flour to the bakeries will be monitored through the receipt and delivery, after which the quality of the bread will be monitored by a dedicated employee to monitor each period, after which the quantities distributed at the distribution points will be verified through the arrival of the receipt and the beneficiary is given the stamps the card distributed to him as evidence of his receipt of his bread allocations.The number of beneficiaries is 2646
