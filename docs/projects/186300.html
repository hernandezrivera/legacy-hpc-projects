Project Summary

The project will take place in 2 Subdivisions of Mezam Division( Bamenda II and III) and 1 Subdivision in Bui Division (Kumbo) of the North West Region of Cameroon for one year. The socio-political crisis rocking the North and South West Regions of Cameroon has displaced many people and made life very difficult for host communities. Young girls are exposed to several negative and dangerous factors such as unemployment, school drop out, poverty, gender based violence, sexually transmitted diseases and death. Poverty is the order of the day making it difficult for these girls to empower themselves. They have lost loved ones, properties and people that could assist them. The project aims at training 100 vulnerable adolescent girls (15 to 17years), and younger women(18 to 24years) on skill development and provide start up kits to create a better life for themselves and their families. OWED has as one of its objectives to mobilize and encourage girls to undertake activities and projects that will help them generate income for sustainable development through handicraft and vocational training.
The impact of this project is contributing towards gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls affected by the crisis. This empowerment will give them confidence and ability to obtain a job and generate income for the entire family
