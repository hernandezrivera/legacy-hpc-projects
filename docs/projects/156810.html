Project Summary

The project aims at supporting vulnerable children in East Ghouta who suffered from hard conditions during the crisis, it will focus on children protection issues through 3 static centers in Rural Damascus (Saqbq, Hamoria and Ein Tarma) under the sub district Kafar batna and will also cover children coming from Jesreen and Beit Sawa
Activities will be in line with sector objectives No. 5 and will include:
- Establishing 3 child friendly spaces in (Hamoria, Saqba, Eintarma) to be as a safe environment for children to interact with beers and to be safe places where specific needs of children are met.
- Conducting recreational activities, awareness raising sessions, providing structured psychosocial support and case Management for children. Those activities will target 9500 people.
- Activities will also include establishing 3 community based child protection structures and capacity building for staff and humanitarian actors in the field of child protection.

Needs

Complex and interlinked protection needs continue throughout Syria, driven by a range of situations including active hostilities, new and protracted displacement and dynamics linked to return increasing the stress on already overburdened communities. According to needs assessment initiatives, the occurrence of multiple protection issues in the same community is not uncommon . This environment continues to require a sustained presence of protection actors, capable of interacting with communities, to intervene and provide specialized services to respond to the most urgent needs, to mitigate risks and to contribute and build a favorable environment for the entire population, with particular attention to groups with specific needs and/or who are at risk. In several areas, the protracted effects of the crisis have disrupted social services and weakened institutions; have put strain on family coping mechanisms and community safety nets; and have left a legacy of psychological distress. The socio-economic impact of the crisis remains a main cause for families to continue to be highly dependent on humanitarian assistance and to resort to harmful coping strategies, which affects women, adolescent girls and children in particular. Such conditions affect multiple groups within communities, including the displaced, returnees, hosting communities and Palestine refugees. In addition, the strain and poverty generated by the protracted crisis may also cause marginalization and the neglect of groups with specific needs such as the elderly and persons with disabilities. Girls and boys of all ages continue to endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights on a daily basis. Continuous displacement, exposure to violence, deepening poverty and the persistent lack of access to services and even the most basic necessities, including in newly accessible areas, are taking a huge toll on children. Grave child rights violations remain a critical concern. This toxic environment leaves many girls and boys deprived of their psychosocial needs and experiencing profound distress. Girls and boys, including adolescents and children with disabilities, are also at risk of other forms of violence, abuse, neglect and exploitation in their homes, schools, workplaces and communities. According to Al Tamayoz presence in East Ghota from June 2018 till January 2019 , many needs have been observed regarding protection issues: The most important child protection risks observed are child labor, dropping out of school, disability, and child marriage

Implementation of activities

The project aims at supporting vulnerable children in East Ghouta who suffered from hard conditions during the crisis, it will focus on children protection issues through 3 static centers in Rural Damascus (Saqbq, Hamoria and Ein Tarma) under the sub district Kafar batna and will also cover children coming from Jesreen and Beit SawaActivities will be in line with sector objective No. 5 and will include:• Establishing 3 child friendly spaces in (Hamoria, Saqba, Eintarma) to be a safe environment for children to interact with beers and to be a safe place where specific needs of children are met and will be as a tool to identify children with specific protection concerns and provide them with needed psychosocial support and refer them to services relevant to their needs. Al Tamayoz have the approval to work in 5 locations in East Ghota including Saqba, Hamouria, Eintarma, and the catchment area of activities implementation will expand beyond these 3 areas• Conduct recreational activities for children, those activities will help better observation of children with specific protection needs to be provided by psychosocial support, and ensure a referral pathway for more specialized psychotherapy• Conduct awareness raising sessions and campaigns to spread knowledge about child rights and some protection concerns within the community, it will also target parents to support them in meeting the specific needs of their children through the parenting activities.• providing structured psychosocial support and case Management for boys and girls children.• Establishing community based child protection structures (Children Clubs in centers and/or schools) and work in parallel with key community members in establishing Child Welfare Committees in different locations, the target is to establish 3 children clubs one in each center• Training will be provided to Al Tamayoz staff on child protection concerns, staff will be selected according to qualifications and interest

Risk of inter or intra-community tension due to service delivery locations or types of services offered if one community is served and a neighbouring community not served.Mitigating measures: Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed. 2. Self-care strategies as part of programmes and organizational HR policies. 2 training practitioners and service providers on doing no harm

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: lack of electricity and water in the area that might affect the implementation efficiency Likelihood: high. impact: mediumMitigating measure: support the center with generator or led lamb, support the center with tank for waterRisk of staff refusal to work in far areas from their homes Likelihood: high. impact: highMitigating measure: support transportation for staff (rent a bus to take the staff from Damascus and return them back according to agreed-upon working hours) Displacement of children and staff during project implementation, this will affect the mobility of beneficiaries/staff and results in low impact of the project. Mitigating measures: train staff on mobile approach and follow up with displaced children through mobile volunteers and the possibility of accompanying children to participate in the designed activities Al Tamayoz has a very good experience in supporting children especially orphans and has a well-qualified trained staff and experience in working in East Ghota content.The project implementation will be monitored through periodical assessment to make sure that designed results and project outcome are on track, assessment are made during activities implementation, Monitoring activities will include : Establish consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place, Monitor the adaptation and development of messages and monitoring the availability of gender analysis report, Perform pre and post tests for trainings, pre and pro assessments for the awareness raising sessions to make sure that sessions achieved the desired impact.Evaluation will be through collecting and analyzing of information that will be delivered to the project manager for the timely action and for any needed modification. As part of evaluation, a complain box will be available in the center and will be opened on daily basis and supervised by the M&E officer who in turn will direct the complains to the general manager for if any required modification.
