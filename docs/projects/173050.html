Project Summary

The project is targeting (1560( 764 boys and 796 girls) child of South Sudan Refugee(SSR), disgorged as (385Girl /3665Boy), including Children with Disability(PWD), in West Kordofan state in two localities(Keilak,/ Al Meriam), also the project will extend to South Kordofan state targeting (300), Child from SSR,( 160Girl/140Boy) in one locality of Leri. To serve access to basic services, lifesaving, quality education the project planned to start in January 2021 &end on 31/12/2021, entitle (Improving the effectiveness of life-saving and quality education for South Sudan Refugees in West Kordofan / South Kordofan. Furthermore, the project is focusing on the Education sector, the project provides the SSR, children with Contractions of new classes, also rehabilitation for old classes, provision and rehabilitation of latrines with especial consideration to the gender sensitive and (PWD), design. Finally, the project will provide the children with Educational material kits. Equip the Teacher's training on the new Methodology according to MoE standard and with a consultation with UNICEF sector lead, and COVID 19 Prevention, PTAs will give special training on School management and issues related to COVID 19 Prevention.

1 - Needs

There is a significant humanitarian need in West Kordofan, WK, South Kordofan SK, are one of the Sudanese bordering states to South Sudan, due to conflict which erupted in South Sudan 2016 which force many SS to flee the country especially in the Region of Bahr El Gahzal, Majority of these emigrants are women girls boys Disables and Elders, and due to lack of resources their children have no access to schools, No money for enrollment feeding, there the children especially Girls are exposed to exploitation and sexual abuse, and child labor for boys. Moreover, boys find themselves outlaws. The targeted localities (Elmeiram and Kaliakl/Leri) are high priority gap areas aligned with the 2020 RCF . for education and live-savings services as well AORD have an ongoing project during 2019-2020 in West Kordofan

2 - Activities/Outputs

Education activities:- AORD will be constructed 6 classrooms in a primary school in West Kordofan (Keilak, Al Meriam) and South Kordofan (Leri)(2 per locality). - AORD will rehabilitate 6 classrooms in a primary school in West Kordofan (Keilak, Al Meriam) and South Kordofan (Leri)(2 per locality). -AORD will rehabilitate 3 latrines (gender-segregated) in a primary school in West Kordofan (Keilak, Al Meriam) and South Kordofan (Leri). -AORD will construct 3 latrines in a primary school in West Kordofan (Keilak, Al Meriam) and South Kordofan (Leri). - AORD will build the capacity of 150 teachers/school staff on PSS / resilience building in West Kordofan (Keilak, Al Meriam) and South Kordofan (Leri). - AORD will build the capacity of 150 PTA members on positive discipline in West Kordofan (Keilak, Al Meriam) and South Kordofan (Leri).
