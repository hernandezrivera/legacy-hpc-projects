Project Summary

"This project aims to strengthen the health sector by making completion in the medical services for patients in the targeted areas especially in the countryside of Aleppo through:
* Increase access to life saving and life sustaining health services for those most vulnerable and in need.
* Strength the health sector in Aleppo and its countryside by providing primary and secondary health care services.
* Increasing the access of health services to hard-to-reach areas through mobile clinic, in coordination with DoH & Health Sector.
* Reduce excess morbidity and mortalities associated with the earthquake and their consequences & rapidly restore health services.
* Ensure minimum /basic integrated package of response at primary health care level"

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The medical services that will be provided are providing health care services (in Aleppo & its countryside) by three medical teams ( contain two mobile unites that have been pre-equipped with medical devices such as: ECG, Echograghy, portable X-Ray device)Each team include Three medical specialties: internal clinic, gynecological clinic, pediatric clinic.In addition to having a highly qualified medical staff to deal with patients, and have previous experience working within mobile medical teams. The medical services that will be provided are:1) Primary health care services:• Providing patients with drugs and medical analysis.• Awareness sessions and psychological support for the beneficiaries (such as: Corona virus).• Investigations: ECHO, ECG, X-Ray. 2) Secondary health care services, such as:* Referral of C.T Scan* Referral of M.R.I.* Referral of cesarean deliveries* Referral of normal deliveries* Referral of general surgeries* Referral of orthopedic surgeries* Referral of gynecological surgeries * Referral of ophthalmology surgeries (Cataract,…)* Referral of incubator services for infants * Referral of urinary surgeries Through the system of referrals from the countryside to the city to implement the required action.3- haemodialysis sessions :The charity has two dialysis devices with a desalination unit, and they are ready to work in Al-Hikma Hospital. The two devices will be activated by providing dialysis sessions at a rate of four sessions per day within 8 working hours under the supervision of a specialized staff who has experience in working with high quality.4- Providing MHPSS through:- Providing general awareness sessions.- Providing PFA.- Providing group counseling.- Providing individual counseling.- Providing GBV.- Providing health education.
