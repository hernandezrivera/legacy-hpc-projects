Project Summary

In North West of Syria (NWS), deteriorating economic conditions remain a challenge for vulnerable communities and households in accessing safe and equitable WASH services, notably for those that have to purchase services like water from private water trucking vendors, and in general, 60% to 70% of the population in communities and camps relying on water trucking and often unsafe water supply modalities to meet their water needs. In many parts of Syria, the WASH infrastructure and service provision require significant repair and operational support, with the continued transmission and outbreak of COVID-19, in addition to the depreciation of currency erodes households’ purchasing power and subsequently household spending capacity on WASH services, supplies and consumables.
The proposed action will provide life-saving assistance to increase and sustain access to critical WASH supplies and services in Idleb and Aleppo governorates, and will place specific attention on the needs of 122,080 BNFS (75,572 IDPs, and 46,508 Host)-(64%female-36%male)-(62% under18 - 38% above18) in:
Aleppo: Atareb, and Al Bab.
Idleb: Maaret Tamsrin, Harim, and Dana.
ATAA focusing on delivering sustainable and life-saving WASH assistance. Also, they will benefit from the COVID19 response, which will mitigate the pandemic of virus spread inside communities and IDPs camps through:
Provide immediate access to adequate and clean water (minimum 35 to 40L/Person/Day) by water trucking for three months, and establish a sustainable drinking water system and connecting it to the main water stations, and support the operation cost for the water stations, the water system will be connected to the chlorination unit. In addition, ATAA will establish water units in the targeted locations and provide them with O&M training, and work closely with the local councils to establish the tariff system for sustainability of the water system.
By operating the water stations, the drinkable water will reach all BNFs in Host in communities and camps, and the operation cost will be decreased by providing PV solar system based on the required operating hours in each location.
The providing of drinkable water through the water network will provide good conditions to operate the sanitation system in the targeted locations, which will make the need to rehabilitation all damaged lines is critical and required, ATAA will rehabilitate the sanitation systems. In the camps, ATAA will connect the camps to the main and close sanitation system.
This project will ensure protecting project beneficiaries from the spread of diseases by providing waste bins 100L capacity (1 bin for 10 HHs), and renting garbage trucks to collect garbage daily, as well as improve hygiene behaviors to increase the health practices and distribute hygiene items by CVA modalities, in addition, conduct hygiene promotion sessions about COVID-19 pandemic and hygiene awareness messages.
Providing rehabilitation for the communal latrines and construction of proper PWDs latrines in close consultation with PWDs and providing lighting inside and out to ensure all latrines are usable and maintain a healthy environment.
During the implementation process, if the targeted locations receive more IDPs, ATAA will target the newly IDPs with all project activities to decrease the disturbances that emerged between host and IDPs.
Conduct FGDs with targeted BNFs (especially with PWDs), and Local councils to inform them about the goal, outcomes, and, activities, as well as brochures and banners will be used to share information with the locations.
Monitoring activities: rehabilitate activities, confirm the distribution of hygiene items by CVA modalities through onsite monitoring, monitoring report, and confirm beneficiary verification for their access to cleaned and chlorinated water, feedback, and satisfaction.
ATAA will set a community-based mechanism to make sure that community members can submit their complaints and feedback.

4 - Needs

Idleb and Aleppo Governorates, with three sub-districts– Dana and Maaret Tamsrin (Idleb Governorate) and Azaz (Aleppo) – alone hosting two-thirds of all IDPs in last resort sites in the entire country. Less than ten per cent of all IDPs in camps/sites in Idleb and Aleppo live in planned, relatively better-serviced camps; the vast majority reside in informal settlements, where shelter and WASH conditions as well as access to basic services, including health and protection, are inadequate or absent.(HNO 2021). Based on WASH CLUSTER information, more than 90% of Syrians had access to improved drinking water, but currently 12 million people need WASH services, in addition to the massive direct and indirect effects of the conflict on the WASH sector, the climate had a significant impact as well, where the national news published pictures in a report called climate change leaves reservoir in Syria dry for the first time, shows dry Duwaysat dam outside the town of Al Diriyah in Syria's northern Idleb governorate. Since WASH one of the basic elements of life and immediate priorities for human survival and dignity in emergencies, humanitarian actors scrambled to provide assistance related to WASH sector, however the scope of long and medium-term interventions has been limited and the majority of focus has been on providing immediate assistance, till this day, WASH services remain the most important need to secure people's lives and stabilize the people status in their areas

5 - Implementation of activities

ATAA will conduct an infrastructure assessment for the water network and prepare estimated costs and quantities.ATAA will rehabilitate and install 14000M(110-75mm) polyethylene pipes to rehabilitate the old network and as a new network, and connect it with the existing main pipe, and connect the camps with the communities water network to provide drinkable water daily.ATAA will rehabilitate and install sewage systems network, the process of drilling and filling, and installing 7500M(1.5 length - diameter 0.3-0.4 M) diameter of new and sub-network, and provide rehabilitation for the communal latrines and construction of proper PWDs latrines.ATAA will cover the sewage pits with reinforced concrete and provide an iron top cover to the sewage pits, provide and install asphalt for the top covers. (This network will be in the communities and will connect the camps with the communities sewage network to cancel the septic pits in the camps and prevent the spread of diseases).Reducing operating costs, by providing PV solar systems to the water stations, as well as providing monthly running costs for first 2 months to cover the operation costs. ATAA will conduct hygiene awareness sessions and distribution 22000 Hygiene kits by CVA modalities. Conducting the PSEA/GBV awareness-raising sessions to the beneficiaries.ATAA will distribute waste bins (1 bin 100L for 10 HHs) and collect waste daily.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ATAA PSEA policy was developed in December 2021 and shared with donors, and set a dedicated PSEA committee including (PSEA focal point, HR focal point, MEAL focal point, Grants and partnership focal point).ATAA adopted a core concept in all intervention to make PSEA and CSG as mandatory in all projects and activities, in this regard, all ATAA staff, volunteers and contractors will got orientation sessions about PSEA and CSG policies and sign both PSEA and CSG code of conducts.All project locations will include banners and complaint boxes with delivering messages from accountability teams regarding the complaints and feedback channels in addition to ATAA Feedback and Complaints system regarding reporting and response of any PSEA case starting from receiving the report, to investigation process, and provide aid and support to the survivor till closure of the case after achieving the objectives of investigation.ATAA is PSEA network member participate in all regular network meetings, in addition to submit monthly reports to the network, in addition to conduct training for all staff about PSEA and target humanitarian workers in the intervention location to support the role of PSEA network.Gender balance will be a core point in the project within all teams (programs, media, MEAL) and locations will be accessed by women and girls safely.
