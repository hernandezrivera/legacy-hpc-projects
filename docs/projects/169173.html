1 - Needs

This disaster has had serious consequences for families on several levels, affecting their shelter, health, hygiene, protection, livelihoods and food security. Health is also one of the main sectors affected by the disaster, with several medical centers damaged, overcrowded hospitals (cases of COVID-19 and the injured) and large stocks of medicines and supplies completely destroyed.The simultaneous risks of the spread of COVID-19 remain high. After the explosion, Lebanon has reported its highest confirmed cases per day since the start of the pandemic, with 355 cases reported on August 6th. The capacity of hospitals in Beirut, as well as overcrowding and a lack of social distancing in the aftermath of the disaster are a main concern

2 - Activities

Distributing hygiene kits to affected families. Two types of hygiene kits will be distributed, depending on families’ needs, including a basic hygiene kit and a household hygiene kit. . The objective is to provide a box including main hygiene items and supplies needed to protect families from COVID-19 (soap bar, shampoo, laundry washing powder, toothpaste, Alcohol antiseptic 70% ethanol, sanitary pads, cotton towel, cleaning cloths, reusable face mask...). The kit should suffice for a duration of 1 month for a family of 5 including two females. Basic Hygiene Kit will be distributed to young girls & women and each hygiene kit should be enough for 1 month and is for personal use (1 kit per person). To be delivered in large zipper bags or equivalent and include (soap bar, cloth, alcohol spray 70% alcohol, face mask, sanitary pads (2 boxes)). These items will be distributed following a door to door policy directly to beneficiaries due to COVID-19 pandemic and in respect to social distancing guidelines and to avoid large gatherings.
