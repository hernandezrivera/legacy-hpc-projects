Project Summary

The food security interventions will be focus on better livelihood both for the host community and the Rohingya people. Skill development training will be organised for the host community youth so they will be able to develop their own entrepreneourship on the other. hand the youth in the refugee camp will be facilitated to promote homestade garden at their households thus the nutrition demand could be minimise. Beside that the common free place at the rohingya camp could be possible to bring under agriculture production this collective effort.
