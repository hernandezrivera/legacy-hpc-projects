Project Summary

Islamic Relief will continue to strengthen its existing site coordination and management in Baidoa - SWS region to provide life-saving services to flood and drought affected IDPs. In line with the CCCM Cluster Strategic Objectives for 2020 - 2021, IR will seek to strengthen camp coordination and camp management structures to coordinate the delivery and monitoring of humanitarian services to improve living conditions of IDPs living in Bay region. In order to reach this objective, IR will contribute its expertise and contextual knowledge to the cluster system. Furthermore, IR will work with the communities to guarantee that governance structures are in place and accountable to population needs, while ensuring that both sites are organized, maintaining basic infrastructure and guarantying site preparation, site demarcation, and allocation of plots to new arrivals or relocated IDPs.Considering, the current and existing conditions and CCCM coordination, IR will support IDPs towards attaining durable solutions disseminating information on the peace agreement and facilitating that their views and concerns are taking into consideration in the planning process.Variations in gender, ethnic origin, physical ability and age that affect vulnerability and coping strategies will be identified and taken into account for planning to prevent their situation from deteriorating even further, additionally, contingency plans are developed and put in place to ensure that Islamic Relief can quickly adapt to and respond to changing humanitarian needs on the ground.
In line with the 2020 -2021 CCCM Cluster Strategy- execution of capacity building with staff and local authorities where necessary in coordination with other CCCM partners in Bay/Bakool and the CCCM Cluster.This project proposal outlines project activities within the framework of a 12 month project.
The proposed rapid response project aims to provide immediate lifesaving humanitarian assistance to 15,000 households (or 90,000 individual beneficiaries) through CCCM support .IRS will conduct Capacity building training to CMC ,provide DRR toolkits and ,Establishing/Rehabilitating/Enhancing drainage systems in IDP sites through incentivized workdays carried out by site-level site maintenance committees(SMC)
