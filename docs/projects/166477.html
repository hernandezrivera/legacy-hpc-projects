Project Summary

According to the Federal Ministry of Health the first case of corona virus disease (COVID-19) was confirmed in Lagos State, Nigeria. The case, which was confirmed on the 27th of February 2020, is the first case to be reported in Nigeria since the beginning of the outbreak in China. The Virology Laboratory of the Lagos University Teaching Hospital, part of the Laboratory Network of the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control (NCDC) confirmed the case after lab test.
To date the Corona virus has spread from Lagos to FCT and some states in the southern and northern region with about 11death.
As at 11pm on 14th April, 2020- 373 confirmed cases of corona virus has been confirmed across 19 states plus Federal Capital Territory (FCT).
The official data from NCDC on 14th April is as follows; Lagos- 214 FCT- 58 Osun- 20 Edo- 15 Oyo- 11 Ogun- 9 Bauchi- 6 Kaduna- 6 Akwa Ibom- 6 Katsina-5 Kwara- 4 Kano- 4 Ondo- 3 Delta- 3 Enugu- 2 Ekiti- 2 Rivers-2 Benue- 1 Niger- 1 Anambra- 1.
In Nigeria the increase and spread of COVID-19 is very threatening considering the poor health systems, some aged long cultural/traditional practices couple with some religious doctrines and believes.
As a country, the Federal and State governments have taken some steps aimed at containing this deadly pandemic. In Borno state considering the already fragile nature of the state associated with the decade of violence conflict and massive displacement caused by the Armed Opposition Group (AOG), which splintered into two distinct groups in August 2016 as Jama’atu Ahlis Sunnah Lida’awati wal Jihad (JAS) – Boko- Haram and Islamic State West Africa (ISWAP).
There is a lot of apprehension around the magnitude of impact COVID-19 will have in the three (3) BAY states of the Northeast considering their degree of vulnerability especially women, children under 5, the elderly (aged) and person living with disability.
Understand the impact of COVID-19 should Borno state record any proven case of the Corona virus. As part of the contingency plan- the Borno state government have begun enforcing restrictions on inter-state travels except for food, drugs, petroleum products and humanitarian aids only beginning from 14th April,2020. In addition, the state has designated two isolation centers for any suspected case of virus, although the state does not currently have a testing centers no restriction/enforcement on public gathering especially religious and other social gatherings.
The current situation in Borno and project location is worrisome as the targeted population lack of soaps, hand washing facilities, water storage containers, low knowledge of improved hygiene practices, no income to buy face masks, couple with loads of fake news flying around and myth that the virus is not real.
In view of the above needs and gaps, there is urgent need to mobilise resources and increase the knowledge level of targeted population and provide soaps and hand washing facilities, while increasing water supply for frequent hand washing practice and other hygiene practices.
According to WHO guideline for the prevention of the spread of corona virus is to protect oneself and others from infection by washing your hands with soaps and rung water or using an alcohol-based hand-sanitizers by rubbing it frequently and not touching your face as well as using facemasks and hand groves. Already, the population in GREENCODE project locations do not have access to income and sustainable livelihood to afford them some of these kits, so there is an urgent need for the raise support and assistance to help them with some hygiene kits and increase awareness on WHO’s recommended strategies using local languages.
Goals and Objectives: The project aims to contribute to reducing the vulnerability of conflict affected population

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Initial Community and Stakeholders visits (Community/Stakeholders mobilisation). Activity 2: Identification and Selection of target beneficiaries for the Hygiene kits Activity 3: Procurement of Hygiene Kits (Based on WASH standard with some special COVID-19 prevention kits) Activity 4: Branding of Special COVID-19 Hygiene Kits Activity 5: Distribution of Special COVID-19 Hygiene Kits Activity 6: Production and airing of Targeted and Inclusive COVID-19 prevention messages -IEC materials. Activity 7: Recruit and training special Community Hygiene and COVID-19 Prevention Message Promoters. Activity 8: Conducting Targeted Hygiene and COVID-19 prevention promotion Activity 9: Identification, selection and registration of most vulnerable (Pregnant, nursing mothers, elderly caregivers and persons with disability) with conditional cash transfer for Food items. Activity 10: Construction and Placement of some Handwashing stands in project communities. Routine Monitoring and Data collectionFinal Project Documentation and Reporting
