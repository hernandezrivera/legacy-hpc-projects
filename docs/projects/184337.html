Project Summary

2021 Post Gu FSNAU nutrition analysis shows that at the national level the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) has still remained at a serious level of 11.1%. Based on this analysis and the latest population figures, the cluster estimates that in 2022 around 1.2 million children, under the age of five years, will be at risk of acute malnutrition and will therefore need emergency nutrition services urgently. Out of this a total of 295,515 will be severely acute malnourished children (SAM) and around 986,000 children will be moderately acute malnourished (MAM) children. In addition to this, around 245,909 Pregnant & Lactating Women (PLWs) will be at risk of acute malnutrition. In this regard, it is estimated that around 629,567 children between 6 to 23 months and 283,305 PLWs will at a greater risk as well. Hence, the total people in need (both for curative and preventive services) for nutrition in 2022 will be 2.37 million including IDPs and Non-IDPs. Therefore, as per HNO 2022, immediate lifesaving humanitarian nutrition services covering both preventive and curative aspects are required for approximately 1.2 million children under the age of five years who will likely face acute malnutrition in 2022. Nearly seven in ten Somalis live on less than USD 1.90 per day, the sixth-highest poverty rate in sub-Saharan Africa. Poverty is both widespread and deep, particularly for households in rural areas and internally displaced person (IDP) settlements. The number of food-insecure people experiencing long-term stress is trending upwards, indicating that chronic food insecurity persists, and requiring sustainable solutions. FSNAU project that in 2022, over 1.2 million under 5, children will suffer from acute malnutrition in 2022 according to FSNAU. Contributing factors include high morbidity, low immunization rates, low vitamin-A supplementation, poor care practices and food insecurity.
PAH intends to provide equitable access to quality nutrition services to floods, conflict and drought affected boys and girls (06-59) months and PLWs in Balcad district in Middle Shabelle region of Somalia. This shows that the moderate malnutrition rate is high in Balcad district in Middle Shabelle region where CGAM was found to be at 11.9% while CSAM stands at 2.2% indicating serious IPC3 status and risk of falling into IPC4.PAH intends to provide nutrition services to 2000 severely malnourished under 5 children, 3200 moderately malnourished under 5 children and 1200 PLWs with acute malnutrition in Balcad district in 3 nutrition sites namely Balcad town, Kurshaale and Calalyaale from 1st January 2022 to 31st Dec 2022.
The target areas in Middle region are characterized by constant conflict, disease outbreak such as AWD, high influx of IDPs displaced by floods, conflict and violence. Balcad district is immediate entrance to the city , Mogadishu, from other parts Hirshabelle state and as such experiences constant influx of internally displaced people running from hunger, conflict and recurrent flooding in other parts of the country. These areas also experience recurring flooding as well as constant conflict either tribal or between Somali national army and Al-Shabaab militants. Balcad district suffered heavily from the recent locusts invasion as well as covid19 pandemic and also recent floods in Middle Shabelle region in May to August 2021 has destroyed over 85000 ha of farmlands which were the main source of income for these communities. The above factors have made many people especially under 5 children and women to face life-threatening hunger and if not immediate humanitarian assistance was not provided then these people face risk of falling into IPC3 &4

1. - Projects activities

Provide high quality basic Nutrition Services (TSFP & MCHN as well as severe malnutrition treatment) at 3 fixed sites in Balcad district, each site having one outreach program. The 3 fixed sites will be located, one in Balcad town, one in Kurshaale and the third one in Calalyaale village. Each site will have one outreach program. The 3 sites target to reach 2000 under 5 children with severe acute malnutrition, 3200 under 5 children with moderate acute malnutrition as well as 1200 PLWs with acute malnutrition•Screening and treatment of 5200 under 5 boys & girls in Balcad district in Middle Shabelle region for 12 months treated admitted for MAM and SAM treatment•Screening of Pregnant and Lactating Mothers; 4.6 - Mitigation Measures

There will be an informed project off workshop conducted for all members of the target communities including women, youth, elderly people, people living with disability as well as minority groups/clans to educate them on project entitlement, scope and threshold. This will ensure that minority groups are not left out. Also since the project is a nutrition project, idnetification of project beneficiaries both under 5 children and PLWs will be based on standard scientific anthropometric measurements such as MUAC, weight for height foe Z-score calculation as well as other malnutrition identification measures. This will prevent discriminatory selection of beneficiaries. The project sites will be located close to the target communities in coordination with the local authority to prevent sexually based violence and for closer accessibility purposes. The sites for the project will be established on public land donated by local authority in the target locations to prevent land ownership dispute.
