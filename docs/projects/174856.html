Project Summary

The Proposed GBV prevention and mitigation project will aim to sustain and scale up GBV, Covid-19 prevention and mitigation interventions as well as build resilience through promoting social and behavioral change to address the root causes of GBV and Covid-19. The project will seek to improve safe access to quality survivor-centered GBV services for survivors of GBV in accordance with Covid-19 guidelines in two (2) local government areas (Monguno and MMC) in Borno state.
This project will use a consultative, phased-in approach to allow SAHEI to work with individuals, groups and institutions over time using different strategies to inspire, encourage and support them in making positive changes in their lives which will eventually influence a change in social norms. For example SAHEI will use the “Engaging Men through Accountable Practices” (EMAP) framework to engage men and adolescent boys during the first six months of the project implementation to address the root causes and contributing factors of GBV, while preparing to roll-out EMAP approach in the next 12 months in communities that meet EMAP criteria.
SAHEI will also conduct advocacy activities to spread information on GBV and Covid-19. IEC materials will be produced and used to educate individuals and incite critical thinking around the consequences of GBV within the family and community, negative behaviors contributing to GBV and how to mitigate the spread of Covid-19 will be stress.
The activities outlined below are organized under two major areas of intervention: Community prevention, and risk mitigation for women and girls at risk of GBV, and empowerment services for women and girls with awareness raising on Covid-19 spread mitigation.
Project activities will reach an estimated 15,411 persons, but the exact number cannot yet be targeted due to the very fluid situation.
