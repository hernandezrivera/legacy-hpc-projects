Project Summary

Most health centers in northern Syria provide similar services, but there are some specialized quality services that people desperately need and that cannot be dispensed and that are provided in primary care centers such as / dialysis - tuberculosis treatment - advanced diagnosis - blood bank - thalassemia treatment /
The number of centers is six, but because there are two centers that provide the services of Thalassemia and Blood Bank, the number of services becomes five.
The project aims to support health care services in five specialized health care centers in northern Syria which provide the following services:
1- TB center services in Idleb city
2- Blood bank and thalassemia services in Idleb city and Kafrnobol.
3- Renal dialysis services in Idleb city.
4- Advanced diagnostic services in Idleb.
5- Physiotherapy and prosthetic services in Sarmada.
What distinguishes this project is the following:
a - It provides life-saving services and prevention of serious complications b- It provides quality and uncommon services as is the case with health centers there c- It is integrated in terms of diversity and there is no repetition of it within the project to avoid duplication
d- It is integrated within the single center, and there is no shortage of the single service delivery chain e- It is geographically dispersed in the north of Syria, achieving a fair distribution of healthcare f- It is considered a referral center for many primary and even secondary care centers that need these specialized services
These centers will provide services for 72,000 beneficiaries per year, most of whom will be affected, who have only these centers to save them from complications of their diseases and injuries.

4 - Needs

5.6 million people are in acute need due to a convergence of vulnerabilities resulting from displacement, exposure to hostilities, and limited access to basic goods and services.The destruction of life-sustaining civilian infrastructure and services such as water,sanitation and electricity systems,as well as attacks affecting hospitals, schools,housing land and property have continued to undermine support structures in urban and rural areas,ultimately endangering civilian lives and hampering the return of the displaced populations after the cessation of hostilities.The Health Resources Availability Mapping System(HeRAMS) implemented by the Health sector in Syria,indicated that of the public health facilities across all 14 governorates and 105 health districts,more than half of public hospitals and health centres have either closed or are only partially functional.11.3 Million people in need for healthcare services in Syria,high percentage of them are in the NW of Syria,(5.5 M Male, 5.8 M Female–1.3 M Children 0-4 years,3.4 M Children 5-17 years,6.1 M Adults18-59 years,and 0.5 M Elderly>59 years).(HNO 2018).This situation had led to the destruction of all specialized hospitals capable of conducting advanced healthcare services in NW of Syria,and forced the people to be referred to Turkey for second and advanced health care services.The latest updates had led to huge number of IDPs to be focused in the NW of Syria, with this huge numbers of people in need it’s clear that the area need more specialized hospitals capable of providing advanced healthcare services.Based on the number of beneficiaries in the past year,it becomes clear to us the severe need for these centers, which is shown in the following table:Tuberculosis Center:3060. Diagnoses: 29280. IBN Sina Dialysis:2112. Blood Bank :2831. Thalassemia: 1760. Rehabilitation unit in Sarmada: 1200.

5 - Implementation of activities

These centres will receive all patients without any racial, gender or religious discrimination, and since these centers provide various services, they will complement each other.1- The TB Center will install, diagnose and treat TB cases2- The Thalassemia Center and Blood Bank will also diagnose blood diseases and provide blood to patients in need.3- While the advanced diagnostic centre will perform diagnostic procedures such as ST-Scan to referred cases from hospitals.4- The Physiotherapy Center will also conduct physical therapy sessions for those referred to it.5- While the Renal dialysis centre performs haemodialysis sessions.6- The bladder rehabilitation centre will perform its surgeries after the necessary investigations.The centres will submit all their monthly reports on the number of beneficiaries with their classification according to age groups and by gender and the beneficiary's residence to the approved systems such 4Ws, HeRAMS and DHIS2.Before starting the project implementation,M&E plan should be prepared to guide us to how we can monitor the project implementation.MEAL team will make a regular collecting the required data that help in •Making enlightened decisions based on the collected data.•Monitoring and evaluation of the quality of the service provision through analyzing the collected dataBased on the Log-Frame,the project will be monitored and evaluated through three different channels,and on three different dimensions: -The operational dimension, for which the project officer is responsible, -The technical dimension, for which the technical team representatives in the field in collaboration with technical programs are responsible.-The objectives dimension, for which the MEAL team is responsible.Monitoring tools:•(PITTs), issued on a monthly basis.•Satisfaction survey among beneficiaries and stakeholders will be analyzed and reported to stakeholders. •Project Evaluation Report,by the end of the project-term.

Likelihood: low. Impact: High. Mitigating Measures:1. Providing a simple and clear compliant mechanisms2. All staff commits to sign a code of conduct3.
