Project Summary

Mercy-USA in this project will provide the most vulnerable host communities and IDPs in Afrin district in Aleppo governorate with garden seed kits for agriculture, tool kit, water trucking, and training, in order to support their livelihood source for them. Targeted beneficiaries 150 HH can be broken down as following (Men 138, Women 167, Boys 388,
and Girls 337).

4 - Needs

Humanitarian needs in Syria are expected to remain high in 2020 and multiple actions are needed to ensure that all four pillars of food security related to access, availability, utilization and stability of food are adequately addressed through life-saving, life-sustaining, appropriate resilience, and early recovery interventions. Emergency response at the early onset of any sudden population movement as well as targeted food assistance and lifesaving emergency agricultural assistance to meet the nutritional and kilocalorie deficits of the most vulnerable populations as per food security indicators is crucial across Syria. The agriculture sector accounts for up to 30 per cent of Syria’s GDP, and it is also producing food for more than half of the population. Therefore, saving, restoring and protecting agricultural and related livelihoods and value chains will be critical to increase the self-reliance and resilience of Syrians. (HNO 2020)Afrin area is considered from the richest lands in Northwest Syria, and given the huge displacement from other areas, makes it from the most in need to be supported by agriculture in the small scale such as micro gardens to provide kind of livelihood to those families.

5 - Implementation of activities

Target is 150 HH (1030 individuals). Mercy-USA will conduct training sessions for host community and IDPs in Afrin district to give them knowledge about this project and goal and outcomes behind it, moreover, all beneficiaries will be given: a. Printed instructions, and gardening guide. b.Community based technical training sessions (including pest and disease management, organic composting, food preservation). then MOU will be signed with beneficiaries including all agreement details. To this end, Mercy-USA will distribute agriculture tool kits: a. Garden rake b. Garden hoe c. Planting hoe for transplanting d. Watering can e. Hosepipe f. Hand compression sprayer 10 L g. Plastic sheeting/films h. Sticky board traps (pest control) As well as spring seeds in line with FSL recommendations include: a. Tomato b. Cucumber c. Eggplant d. Zucchini e. Parsley f. Beans g. Cowpea h. Onion i. Garlic j. Cabbage k. Chilli (hot pepper) l. Green/ Red pepper m. Okra n. Lettuce o. Radish Furthermore, Mercy-USA will provide. weekly water trucking. Mercy-USA will follow up implementation on regular basis by technical staff for resolving any problem might befaced, and with MEAL staff for following up activities delivery within the proposed timeline. Mercy-USA will use lessons learned from this project to be applied for other locations. Mercy-USA will engage the most vulnerable households including Men, women, youth, adolescents, boys and girls in order to maximize the benefit as much as possible.
