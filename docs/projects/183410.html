Project Summary

The Treat-me campaign is CCCL’s major campaign for the year 2021. Through this campaign, we are asking donors and supporters from all over the globe, to help the center’s mission in this critical time where all medications are scarce and the devaluation of the Lebanese Lira; and this is through donating via this campaign to help the center save kids’ lives
Today, we need to insure the cost of medication so that we can import them from abroad.
These medications are critical to every child .
At CCCL we have a survival rate of 98% It is unacceptable to lower this survivorship percentage because medications are not secured. It is unacceptable because this percentage is not a number. This percentage is kids with names, parents, siblings and friends at school. It is unacceptable because your support on medications will not stop.
