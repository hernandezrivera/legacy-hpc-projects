Project Summary

Evidently, the harsh weather conditions during the Deyr 2020 season between the month of Oct to Dec 2020 accompanied by shortage of rainfall (GU rains) between the month of April and June 2021 has contributed to worsening drought conditions in Gebo region including Luuq district.
According to latest Famine Early Warning System (FEWS NET) 2021 report, if the Deyr rains continue to perform poorly through the remainder of the season in Nov and Dec, further worsening of food security and nutrition outcomes would be likely, due to widespread crop failure resulting in deficits of high percent, a significant decrease in livestock price , and significant population displacement that already occurring in Gedo /Bay and bakool and Luuq been destination for IDPs.
The Project will be implemented in the Luuq District, in areas where communities have been affected by erratic climatic, consecutive drought and most of the time survive on coping mechanism and most of the are farmers and ago-patrol livelihood zone.
.The livelihood and food security of its inhabitants was greatly been affected, making the people to be in need of urgent humanitarian assistance and sources of income.
The project’s objectives are: (1) To help 6,600 HH, including 33,00 hosting community members and 33,00 displaced persons have increased production at community and household level through better agricultural and pastoral productivity.
(2) To help 6,600 HH, including IDPs have increased income and diversified livelihood
(3) Some of the beneficiaries will have benefit from improved household and community access to efficient markets, social behavior change and communication, and alternative energy value
The project purpose is to support the host communities and displaced communities to enhance their production capacity for improved livelihood and food security through provision of agricultural input and fodder seed distribution .the programs support in climate smart crops and livestock production, link them to markets, create conductive environment to economic and social collaboration of host and IDP communities and ensuring self-reliance and minimizing over dependency on unsustainable coping mechanisms. Capacity building interventions adapted to all level (communities, local leadership, youth local government and other relevant entities) are made integral to resilience building and sustainable livelihoods intervention will be able to access irrigation systems, conservation agricultural services, tools, training and services. With support, the project will provide assistance to 1800 direct beneficiaries in luuq district of GEDO Region.
The innovative approaches to building resilience for IDP and host communities in luuq district of gedo region the project aims to provide livelihood options for both IDP and host communities in luuq district of Gedo region to overcome climatic changes, and poor rain lead livelihood.
Advocacy, awareness creation on protection and rights of the IDPs, as well as women and children within the host communities will be done.
The project will be implemented by Ebon Development and Relief Organization (EDRO) and a member of Food Security Cluster in operating in Luuq District. EDRO is currently Implementing nutrition resilience program supported by UNICEF/WFP.
The project is inline with the HRP/FSC objective 1. Reduce loss of life by providing ways in accessing a sustainable livelihood.
2. EDRO program will protect live hood affected by shocks through proving production techniques and inputs.
3. The protect will assist 50% host and 50% IDPs thus providing re-settlement for the IDPS and also reduce displacement for the host population.
EDRO will target using the following croteria-
1. House hold with malnourished children.
2. Female headed household.
3. Poor house hold
4. Hoiuse hold with no assets
5. House hold who lost farming and livestock.
6. Household from Minority clan.
7. Household who does not have income.
