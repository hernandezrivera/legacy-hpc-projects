Project Summary

this intervention aims to support primary schools in the targeted locations to develop and implement SIP and to prepare for the safe return of children after the school closure do to the covid 19.
also build the capacity of the teachers and school staff in the filed of EiE and help schools develop and implement preparedness plans and safety protocols. engaging the community in the process.
This project will benefit 7,000 child directly and over 50,000 indirectly.

1 - Needs

Education needs are rising rapidly across the different states of Sudan specially with the possibility of reaching previously conflict affected states like south and west kordofan and the blue Nile state.There are 1.5 million people with education needs in Sudan, 60% are boys and girls (0-17) years old and 15% are women (HNO 2020).All children equally deserve access to education and good quality education, but access to education of poor quality is equivalent to access to no education at all.A total of 250 million children worldwide cannot read or write. This represents one-third of all the children in the world.130 million of the 250 million who can neither read nor write have attended school for at least four years. (GEM report 2013/14/15)Good quality education is influenced by what goes on in the class room and beyond that is why it is important to have a solid school Improvement Plan (SIP) to get effective academic outcomes and achievements as well as in developing the social and cultural wellbeing of the children and adults within the community and the school.The recent world wide pandemic of the novel corona virus has led to 130 countries to declare complete lock down which affected the education of millions of students. in Sudan schools are still closed with the possibility of resuming education with the start of 2021. the safety of our children in schools and the staff is a high priority that is why it is important to prepare the school staff/ parents/ community with the right knowledge about safety and how to be prepared to deal with different kinds of emergencies while commencing education.Also the schools must be equipped with the right tools and facilities that meet the minimum safety standards to accommodate the needs of the students/teachers and staff with special attention to vulnerable groups like children with disabilities (CWD).Times of emergency call for special measures and procedures, that is why building the capacity of teachers on the differet aspects relating to the emergency situations/ building resistance and providing PSS to children and parents is very important and will improve the education experience for them allowing them to develop and prosper.

2 - Activities/Outputs

To address the needs mentioned above GOFCW will implement the following activities in seven states:-- conduct teacher training on EiE to 700 Primary school Teachers.- Provide Primary school Teacher kits to 140 schools.- Support 28 Primary schools to develop and implement SIP- Support 28 PTAs participating in the development and implementation of primary school SIP.- Establish 140 temporary hand washing stations in targeted localities.- construct 26 permanent/ semi permanent hand washing units in targeted localities. - Distribute hard copies of COVID-19 protocols to 140 schools.- Provide 131 schools with WASH minimum package.- Train 700 school staff/ PTAs on COVID-19 protocol.- Train 697 teachers on COVID-19 teachers preparedness package.- Provide 7,000 children with no medical masks.- provide 700 teachers/ school staff with non medical masks.- Distribute COVID-19 grant to 140 schools.- Train 700 teachers/school staff on PSS/ resilience building.- train 700 parents on positive parenting. - train 700 teachers/ school management members on GBV prevention.- Establish 140 schools with child protection referral pathways.
