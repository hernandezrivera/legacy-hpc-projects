Project Summary

•Training of students nutrition education & covid19 in schools
•nutritional education and how to acquire vit C source based on local resources,
•Culinary demonstrations in heatlh centers during CCR and CPN consultations,
•IEC distribution to risk groups in health centers and communities .(covid19 & Food education in consumption of local vitamin C rich foods). To encourage and educate on maternal and child health in the promotion of SMA up to 6 months of age and to demystify the transmission of VOCID-19 during breastfeeding and care during breastfeeding (artificial and breast-feeding), specifically hygiene and individual protection, according to WHO and MISAU protocols.
•Distribution of supplements to patients at risk of malnutrition, malnourished and at risk of covid-19) with difficult access to health facilities. Replication of IEC material, with national and WHO standards for nutrition and Covid-19 related health unit areas.
•Training of the target group in the hygiene standards already established and ensuring buckets of water in the most crowded places
