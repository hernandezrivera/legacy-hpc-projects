Project Summary

The project is an Emergency Food security support intervention with promotion of resilience light activities. The implementation period is one year running from January 2019 to December, 2019 . The key objective of the project The key objective is to increase people’s access to food and availability of food through support to food production. the project is line with HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2 and FSL cluster objective 4
The main programmatic approach is through direct distribution of inputs, input trade fairs , training and working with accountability to affected committee. The activities are in line with the indicative activities under the Food security cluster The activities falls within the seasonal calendar and within the livelihood zones of warrap state
The targeted households are mainly vulnerable households in host and IDP areas50,000 vulnerable households will be supported . The main target location is in Gogrial West and Twic Counties in warrapstate which falls mainly in Phase 3 and 4 of the food security classification phase. Centre to the project implementation is the consideration given to address protection concern and to involve and be accountable to the affected community. The needs of Women, boys and children are focused. these needs will be established in close collaboration with the protection cluster. Another sphere of integration is with the nutrition cluster wherein the caretaker or mothers of malnourished children will be trained and supported with high nutritious vegetable seeds for production. The project will also participate in joint assessment missions and response with both the nutrition and protection cluster.

Needs

The political conflict which started in Dec. 2013 have had a devastating effect on the country. Whilst it is evidence by widespread displacement, killing and looting in the country, the Humanitarian situation continues to be worsen . The most recent IPC report in september, 2018 indicates that morethan half the population is facing hunger and starvation with an estimated 6.1 million people (59% of the total population2) faced Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity3 at the peak of the lean season (July – August), of whom 47,000 were in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5) and 1.7 million were in Emergency (IPC Phase 4). In warrap State, the Food insecurity situation continue to be very much critical in Gogrial West and TwicCounty . In Gogrial west, as per FSNMS Round 23 the key shocks are Economic hardship compounded by continued inflation, Prolonged dry spell, Fall Army Worm, Crop Pest and diseases, Flooding in Akon North and South,livestock outbreak ((Anthrax, CBPP, BQ, HS, PPR and Newcastle) and Human sicknesses . most vulnerable HH are expected to be 39% households reported decreased income over the past year, 11% HHs reported shortage of seeds,52% HHs have their crops affected by dry spell, 59% of HHs reported decrease in livestock due to disease outbreak, 28% HHs who have lost maize crops because of fall army worm outbreak, 32% severe, 48% moderate and 13% slight86% HHs reported member sick of which 70% of malaria + poor HH diet + poor WASH. Food consumption score for the county is in phase 4 and GAM prevalence of phase 4 in which GAM: 26.6%, SAM: 5.4%, Mortality: CDR: 0.37% U5DR: 0.24%In TwicCounty, which is also affected by Economic hardship compounded by continued inflation, with 32% households who reported decreased income over the past year, 11% HHs reported shortage of seeds, irregular rains and prolonged dry spell reported by 44% households. Effect of Fall Army worm reported by 14% HHs with destructions on cereals crop (maize, sorghum) 27% severe, 53% moderate and 20% slight, heavy weed infestation reported 26% HHs and late flooding caused some crop loses. Large/minor decrease in livestock due to disease outbreak reported by 56% of HHs. The over 40,000 IDPs from Abyei in 2011 and from Unity State are integrated into the host community increasing agriculture land requirement. 26% HHs with Poor FCS (<21)-Phase 4 with observed evidence of the emergency level of livelihood coping, HHS 1% HHs - Extreme (6) and 1% - Severe (4-5), worse compared to FSNMS R21 (0%), high food prices + significant 30% HHs in emergency coping 30% & 42% HHs with very high food expenditure share – livelihood change; plus, high GAM WHZ: 19.1%, (IPC 4). The above result from the FSNMS is pointing to a deteoriating Food insecurity situation. 50,000 people are expectedto face high food insecurity. Due to the conflict between the Apuk and Agok communities, another worrying protection trend affecting peoples’ access to food is the abduction of women and children and the frequent fighting between these two clans. Because of the conflict, women are the most productive household members in farming participated less in farming activities this contributing to food insecurity. Youths mainly boys who would have also been involved in farming are been conscripted to join the fighting forces As a result of the above problems, there is need to increase people access to food through support to vulnerable households to engage in production, treatment of livestock affected by disease outbreak
