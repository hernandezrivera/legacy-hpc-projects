Project Summary

This proposal is aimed at reducing girls and young women's vulnerability to protection and GBV risk through GBV prevention and response services such as GBV risks mitigation at the community level, and improve resilience and self - empowerment of GBV survivors through psychosocial activities in the Far North region of Cameroun. Response is targeted to 21000 women, girls and men in different communities.

Besoins / Needs

Since the beginning of the Boko Haram crisis within the four Lake Chad Basin reparian countries, millions of people including men,women,boys and girls have been uprooted from their homes and communities and forced into displacemet within the Lake Chad region. known as one of the most important humanitarian crisis in the world, females particularly ypung girls are highly subjected to all kinds of human rights' violation; that include Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, physical assault, rape, forced marriages, emotional abuses, denial of resources, etc. In fact, this is exacerbated by the lack of resources by women and girls to overcome their situation. GBV, as a human rights' violation, is actually one of the most important protection concern for girls and young women in the Far North region of Cameroon. Despite the prevention and response mechanisms set up by humanitarian actors and line ministries, the situation of girls and women remains alarming. According to the UNOCHA, "for the first half of 2019 data collected in the most affected divisions of the Far North region in Cameroon, namely: the Logon and Chari, Mayo Sava and Mayo Tsanaga divisions; a total of 1016 cases of GBV were reported, including 37% of cases of lack of resources and opportunity, 11% are sexual violence. 84% of GBV cases are inflicted by the intimate partner, there are also cases of sexual exploitation and sexual abuse of children, and cases of harmful traditional practice.(HRP, 2019). With regard to travel status at the time of reporting, it was noted that more than half (54%) are the most vulnerable populations among which 27% of IDPs and almost a quarter 23% of refugees. As for care, 87% of cases received psychosocial assistance, 65% of cases of rape taken care of, including 6% within 72 hours after the declaration, there is a growth in the use of security and legal assistance compared to the 2018 data". From this point, there is a need to increase prevention among the target population, through risk mitigation activities, and to improve the quality of response, in order to provide constant, adequate and holistic support to all GBV survivors beeing identified since the beginning of the crisis, so as to support them, and lead them to complete resilience.
