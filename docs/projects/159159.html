Project Summary

Nutrition situation in Somalia has been very fragile with the rates varying from emergency to critical. Lower Juba is one of the areas in Somalia with chronic high levels of acute malnutrition beyond the emergency threshold. It is also a district where the IDPs report high prevalence acute malnutrition very few women with access to Maternal and child care services including nutritional services. Prevalence of acute malnutrition and health needs affecting more than 1.6 million people. Therefore, WRRS in line with the national response plan will work in the proposed areas to ensure that in integration with other sectors prevalence of global acute malnutrition of 925,000 children under 5 and 270,000 PLW is below 13% within our target districts. This will target IDPs and non-displaced people so as to decreased it by 5% in our targeted districts. The focus will as well be to increase coverage of immunization among other factors to improve measles vaccinations and prevention and case management of AWD/cholera.
Capacity building of staff will as well be done so as to improve on the quality of service delivery. The static nutrition sites will be strengthened by adding WASH and health Services, provision of nutrition supplies and the improvement of staff capacity. WRRS will therefore work to improve the access and utilization of Emergency integrated Nutrition services for women and children living in the most affected areas in Afmadow, and baidoa IDPs using both static and mobile sites. Far to reach villages will be covered by the mobile teams with the support of community mobilizers and CHWs as well s the volunteers. NHHP will be employed in the various regions to promote good health and hygiene practices to the affected communities, IYCF promotion and support for infant and young child feeding including mother lead MUAC way of identifying cases of malnutrition will be done.
WRRS will adhere to active case findings of the SAM and MAM cases through screening of beneficiaries with a subsequent admission for treatment and management of the cases. SAM cases will have hygiene kits provided and in linkage with the existing WFP project we will enroll them to the scope card registration.
The targeted group Interventions will target children (Boys and girls) 6 59 months and pregnant lactating women, who are acutely malnourished and at risk of either contracting diseases or dying due to malnutrition.
The project staff (70 female and 41 men) will primarily target 20829 children 6-59 months (11056 boys and 9773 girls) with screening , Treatment of 1625 SAM cases, (926 boys and 699 girls) and provision Basic nutrition service package interventions like Health promotion, Deworming for children under 5 and 8000 PLW, immunisation and vitamin A supplementation. Staff will also target to pregnant and lactating women (PLW) with IYCF education and support including IYCF E among IDP, micro nutrient supplementation and hygiene promotion with distribution of hygiene kits where feasible.
As indirect beneficiaries, the project is also expected to reach 1000 grandmothers, 1250 willing fathers and 560 child caregivers who are likely significant others for most women. 5414 Women of child bearing age who are not caregivers of children with SAM will also be targeted by primary prevention interventions.
These will be reached through female CBW, who will be living and working in the community.
100 Community leaders and elders will benefit and also contribute to the project through community conversations and involvement in community health board.
The entire community will also benefit from campaigns, Mother led MUAC screening and mass screening exercises.
WRRS will ensure that the coordination with other actors within the area of interventions is uphold, MOH, UNICEF and WFP from the targeted states will be involved and the clusters, which include, health Nutrition and WASH cluster among other clusters where relevant at that point in time.

Mitigation Measures

The project intends to involve the community both male and female, boys and girl of all ages in all stages of the program cycle, so that they understand the rationale behind decisions made.The project intends to use already existing community governance structures and use them as health committees. Health committees will be involved in recruitment of staff, community mobilization and monitoring of the project.Their presence will represent the interest and role of the community in successful completion of the project. The project will also have a feedback mechanism , from which complaints and comments from the community will be collected, and fed back to the same. This will be done in a highly confidential manner with very few staff in the management allowed to interact with this process. Complaints will be addressed immediately while compliments will also be shared with staff. Protection will be key in the project. We will ensure that girls and women that face abuse discovered in our centers get help. Our project will also treat all beneficiaries equally and protect the dignity of our beneficiaries.We will ensure that our project involves most community groups so as to demystify operations and avoid rumors of the projects' activities. We will also protect our staff by collecting security updates regularly and sharing with them in time so that they do not go to areas where their lives will be endangered.

Please provide further explanation

The project for sustainability and durable solution will focus on mitigating the effects of the ongoing catastrophes in an integrated manner. The project intends to integrate treatment of malnutrition by treating Moderate acute malnutrition,Un complicated severe acute malnutrition, and complicated severe acute malnutrition all which have reported increased prevalence in the last few months in most areas of Somalia. These will integrate other health and WASH related integration like hygiene promotion, and education treatment of common illnesses and expanded program for immunization (EPI). WRRS runs an maternal child and health clinic (MCH) / out patient department (OPD) linked together. These interventions are life saving interventions because they focus on those at risk of death due to any additional shocks. The project will also target children under five , pregnant and lactating women as the most vulnerable and should be prioritized in emergency settings.At the end of the project, a strong cadre of community-based structure will be built for service delivery. Community based participatory approach will be employed through strengthening community capacity by capacity building own staff, MoH staff, establishing and training CBWs and CHWs. WRRS shall gradually designate CBWs, CHWs and mothers/caregivers to continue the delivery of basic services e.g. screening, including preventive, protective and pro-motive actions, and their roles and responsibilities will be transformed, from direct implementation to technical support, through continuous support on knowledge transfer and capacity development activities, monitoring and coordination within the existing structures.
