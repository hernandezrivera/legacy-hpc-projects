Needs

In Donbass, for the fifth year, the armed conflict has not stopped. Due to the numerous shelling of settlements and the mining of large areas, civilians continue to die, including children and young people. According to open sources, the number of victims of the conflict among the civilian population exceeded 2,800 people, among them 138 children (as of March 18, 2017), and the number of injured civilians is about 7,000 to 9,000. To reduce the number of fatal cases and reduce injuries among pupils and students, as well as to improve the psychophysical health of the younger generation in the Donetsk region, a complex of measures is needed to train the younger generation to provide first medical and psychological assistance, including mine safety.At the same time, in the Donetsk region, the socio-economic situation of young people remains tense due to employment problems. In this regard, a hundreds of young people left Donbass for decent work, allowing them to satisfy their material and other needs. Among the young people (under 35) who now live in the Donetsk region, there are more than 15 thousand unemployed people. To reduce the number of internally displaced persons and migrants from the Donbass region, as well as to stabilize the socio-economic situation, it is necessary to create a permanent platform for retraining and teaching the basics of enterprises of young people. Apart from this, in order to reduce youth unemployment, it is proposed to organize courses on remote search of work (including for people with disabilities) at the base of the Donbass Development Center, as well as to hold a number of youth meetings with representatives of industrial enterprises in the region dedicated to employment and advanced training. The project provides for the creation of flexible tools for wide application, for example, people with special needs and residents of remote locations will be able to receive all the competencies and services presented in the project through remote components. In addition, taking into account the individual needs of beneficiaries, the structure and breadth of representation of individual activities will be adjusted throughout the entire project implementation.

Activities & outcomes

Within the project it is planned:Activity 1: To develop methodological recommendations in the form of a brochure and digitized materials on the provision of pre-medical and primary psychological assistance, as well as the formation of competences in civil defense issues, including mine safety among residents living in the zone of armed conflict. Outcome 1: It is planned to create and distribute among the residents of the region at least 2500 corresponding brochures. Activity 2. Distribute the indicated methodical recommendations among the inhabitants of the Donetsk region, including by means of remote access to this document. Outcome 2: Methodical recommendations will be posted on the website of the organization, as well as on social networks with coverage of at least 200 thousand views. Activity 3. To organize courses on the provision of pre-medical and primary psychological assistance, as well as the formation of competence in matters of mine security among young pupils and students of the region. Outcome 3: Courses on the provision of pre-medical and primary psychological assistance, as well as on the formation of competence in mine safety issues will include 3 workshops and 5 trainings. 4. To organize courses for youth on self-employment and remote work in the Internet (including for people with disabilities), as well as to hold a series of meetings of young people with representatives of industrial enterprises in the region, devoted to issues of employment and advanced training. Outcome 4: Courses will include 2 seminars and 5 practical classes. In addition, at least 10 meetings of young people with representatives of industrial enterprises of the region will be held. It should be noted that the ratio of the number of women and men, as well as children and adults who will receive appropriate assistance under this project, was adopted based on the analysis of our practical activities.
