Project Summary

In order to address the urgent humanitarian needs of the most vulnerable internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees and host community members affected by the conflict in Borno and Yobe States, Northeast Nigeria, Action Against Hunger is proposing a cash-based transfer program. The aim is to prevent and mitigate the shocks of the lean season on food-insecure households and build resilience as well as economic recovery. The lean season months in Borno and Yobe include May to October (with peaks in July, August & September) Hence, the proposed intervention will prevent deterioration and contribute to the improvement of the food and nutrition status for the most vulnerable people in the highly food insecure areas of Borno and Yobe. It will also start them on a journey towards income resilience through conflict-sensitive and context-specific economic empowerment initiatives, while at the same time improving the consumption of nutritious food and biomass production in the community through community agroforestry using tree seedlings raised under cash for work intervention. The proposed intervention places gender, protection, COVID-19 prevention, conflict sensitivity and environmental consideration at the forefront in the programming.

1 - Needs

As the conflict in North East (NE) Nigeria enters into its 12th year, millions of people have been displaced, agricultural outputs and other livelihoods have been destroyed, basic services have been cut off and security challenges have arisen. Nigeria is facing a protracted crisis. In the last quarter of 2021, the national unemployment rate reached 40%. Coupled with the economic effects of the COVID-19 crisis, the national poverty rate is forecasted to jump from 40.1% in 2019 to 45.2% in 2022. According to the World Bank (2021), the COVID-19 crisis alone is expected to drive an additional 10.9 million Nigerians into poverty in 2022.Nigeria's Northern East region has been battling a multidimensional crisis for over a decade that has left millions displaced and thousands of people dead. The population is experiencing a severe food crisis due to limited engagement in agricultural activities, a decline in humanitarian access, and recurring population movements. Much higher-than-average pricing for staple foods (compared to the same periods of previous years) and diminishing household purchasing power have further decreased the population’s access to food. Furthermore, this crisis is exacerbating pre-existing interrelated factors of food insecurity such as the high prevalence of water-borne and preventable childhood diseases (diarrhoea, acute respiratory infections, and malaria), sub-optimal hygiene conditions, inflation, and seasonal vulnerabilities. The Ukraine war is now worsening the situation having negative effects on access to wheat and fertilizer as well as higher fuel prices (International Food Policy Research Institute IFPRI, June 2022). It's this combination of factors i.e., effects of the North-East conflict, the continuation of COVID19 effects, onset of the lean season, high fuel prices, inflation, and the effects of the Ukraine crisis that have led to the current food and nutrition emergency that requires urgent intervention.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

To ensure safe and accountable programming ACF shall mitigate the potential of conflict between host community members and IDPs or project beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries in the location of intervention through strong community engagements and consultations and project monitoring. The project shall target vulnerable host community members and IDPs as part of measures to ensure that no tension is created between host population and IDPs. Community stakeholders shall have representatives from host community and IDPs. ACF shall mitigate risks associated with cash and livelihoods by selection and siting of distribution points in close proximity with beneficiaries' homesteads or camps. Distribution shall be done only during the daytime and on market days. Beneficiaries will be encouraged to travel in groups to serve as a deterrence measure to any form of risk when alone. Security assessment of distribution points and location of intervention shall be conducted prior to commencement of the intervention. ACF will undertake constant monitoring of the context and review. The proposed intervention will seek to address household decision-making and resource management through the engagement of men and women in the intervention. The intervention will consider active participation of men and women in project meetings, monitoring, selection, trainings, project activity review and feedback.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Action Against Hunger (ACF), ensures that participation, feedback and complaints mechanism are integrated in all strategies including Food Security and Livelihood sector, plans and programs. It also provides a range of rapid and accessible channels of communication with affected populations and use them to inform affected populations about procedures and processes that affect them so that they are able to make informed decisions and choices. This is in line with IASC principles and commitment. ACF considers sexual exploitation and abuse by humanitarian workers as an act of gross misconduct and therefore perpetrators will face termination with immediate effect. Irrespective of any form of consent, if the sexual activity with children (anyone under the age of 18 years despite the customary or legal definition in the country) by humanitarian workers is prohibited regardless of explanation. Moreover, sexual exploitation as a mode of exchange for benefits are completely prohibited not only with beneficiaries but also within humanitarian workers. Apart from this, any form of exchange whether it is monetary or physical for providing humanitarian assistance to the beneficiaries including sexual or other degradable forms of favors are strictly prohibited. The workers, vendors or community volunteers who were caught in this act will be immediately suspended from the program/ mission and if proven guilty after investigation, they will be terminated. ACF will also ensure to create and maintain an environment that will prevent such sexual exploitations and abuse and promote the implementation of AAH code of conduct. Various departments of ACF Nigeria will be involved in sensitizing and capacity building of staff on a routine basis.
