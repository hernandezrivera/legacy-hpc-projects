Project Summary

This project that Sudan Evangelical Mission (SEM) proposes will focus on the gender based violence sub cluster priority activities that target boys, girls, women and men who are survivors, or are at risk of gender based violence through tailoring our interventions to respond, prevent and mitigate risks of gender based violence in Ibba County that will reach 6,804 beneficiaries.
Our activities are in line with the protection cluster objectives that aim at provision of critical protection-related assistance and specialized services through an integrated approach to address the priority needs of vulnerable women, men, girls and boys as well as prevention and mitigation of protection risks through enhanced preparedness.
We shall achieve the objectives by responding to the needs of gender based survivors by various activities; among them being provision of timely, comprehensive case management process that will address their needs from the point they were identified until when their cases shall be closed. Provision of case management will be strengthened by the training that we shall deliver to frontline service providers.
By establishing women and girls friendly spaces, we shall provide women and girls a safe entry point for services and a place where they can access information. They shall be able to interact with each other exchange information and rebuild their social networks. The friendly spaces will not only help the women and girls to promote connectedness with a wider audience and build their social assets, but also a point where they shall receive psychosocial support. We shall promote their psychosocial wellbeing, providing appropriate care, protection and social integration to them. We have considered the social aspects as they are often shaped by the interaction with traditions, culture, values, family and community around them.
By carrying out protection awareness raising and prevention messaging, we shall pass key messages that will focus on awareness of gender based violence, prevention/ harm reduction. We shall pass messages that are short, memorable and communicated in a local language that shall be understood by the beneficiaries in Ibba County.
For community based protection preparedness and protection risk mitigation, we shall enhance the capacity of youth and women to carry out risk reduction action plans that will increase the safety of women, men, boys and girls through mitigating risks, prevention measures and responding to incidents of GBV. We shall also carry out safety audits, and support other cluster partners to conduct safety audits that will inform responses and mitigation of GBV. SEM will contribute to other initiatives aimed at development of GBV standard operation procedures (SOP`S) which guides service provision to survivors of GBV.

1 - Needs

Sudan Evangelical Mission (SEM) joined other partners in planning and conducting an IRNA that was carried out in Maridi and Ibba Counties from April 24th to May 7th 2019, which revealed that gender based violence against women like wife beating, harassment, and other forms of abuses is reported as common problem facing the IDPs, and returnees at the sites visited especially in Nabanga and Maruko payams of Ibba County. Generally, such incidents are not reported due to the stigma attached to it and the victims are struggling with the trauma from within the community without any psychological support. Most of such cases are reported to local chiefs who have limited powers to deal with the perpetrators and there is little knowledge among the community members on referral pathway and psychosocial supports for survivors of GBV that are available within their area. Women mentioned during the assessment said that they do not feel safe and their freedom of movement is restricted as a result of the risk of sexual assault yet they have very limited access to available support.One of the priority need identified by the IRNA was to collectively fight GBV, this project brings together community members composed of girls, boys, men and women and groups affiliated to youth and women to take part in identification, development of realistic solutions and implementing action plans that intend to prevent incidences of GBV and provision of support to survivors. Community members will be encouraged to identify social structures and other resources within their locality that they can utilize to promote a protective environment . We will also build on risk mitigation efforts by other partners and support their activities that aim at combating GBV through conducting safety audits and assessments, and strengthening the referral pathway in Ibba County which shall be crucial in responding to the needs of GBV survivors.It is therefore, absolutely critical for women and girls to be accorded extra protection through the establishment of a functional safe spaces in Ibba County to enhance GBV prevention, care and access to information to promote development and well-being of individuals, households, and communities. We shall supplement other interventions being carried out in Ibba County that address GBV and assist survivors through distributing dignity kits, clinical management of rape, and maternal healthcare. This project has put additional interventions in place that are holistic and practical that aim at prevention, risk mitigation and response to gender based violence.SEM will aim at strengthening GBV prevention and response programs in Ibba County through the provision of case management services, establishing women and girl friendly spaces, referral services, community awareness raising on protection, training frontline service providers and community based protection activities that mitigate protection risks. We shall also reach out to people in locations highlighted by the IRNA to be hard to reach during rainy season like Tanamuko, Moruko and Nabanga where we will provide services to them through placement of social workers who are based in those locations.

6.6 - Will beneficiaries' rights be legally recognised?

All the beneficiaries will have their inherent fundamental rights recognized and promoted, while treating them in a humane way. We shall recognize the basic rights of all beneficiaries, their freedoms of expression to enable them participate fully in this project. We shall encourage the participation of all targeted beneficiaries and disseminate information on available services to them without discriminating them on basis of their cultural, economic, social status or gender.

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

This project will target internally displaced persons and the host community residing in Ibba County. We shall support and promote the resilience by enabling them actively participate in the various priority activities that we shall deliver to respond, prevent and mitigate gender based violence.

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

This project has an activity which shall refer survivors of gender based violence to various service providers who shall provide assistance to them. Among the frontline service providers, we shall government systems involved e.g. healthcare workers, gender child and social welfare department who shall be part of the referral pathway in place and to be used for case management. Other community systems in place, e.g. local authorities, representatives of youths, women shall be involved in community mobilization for promoting their involvement in protection and gender based violence prevention.

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

This project is aligned to the standard operating procedures (SOP) for prevention, protection and response to gender based violence (GBV ) of South Sudan which aim at helping stakeholders like SEM, other agencies and government institutions in their attempt to prevent, protect and respond to GBV. The standard operating procedures sets clear systems, roles and responsibilities for all institutions involved in the prevention, protection and response to GBV in South Sudan. This has given relevance, quality and lay basis for interventions that will be tailored to adequately address the needs of the survivors of gender based violence that we shall support by this project.

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

The protection environment in Ibba County is sufficiently stable at this moment. This project will contribute to creating a positive attitude towards internally displaced persons/ returnees with the host community where there shall be peaceful coexistence. We shall also ensure that rights on women, children, persons with disabilities and all persons have been recognized and upheld. Community awareness raising sessions that we shall carry out will be used to disseminate information on fundamental human rights. We shall promote the contribution of internally displaced persons to the development of the community alongside the host community in Ibba county.

6.4 - Does the project include sustainability/handover?

By promoting the participation of women in activities like basketry, bead making and embroidery, they shall be able to utilize the skills beyond the life of the project and generate income that will sustain them and their families.Raising community awareness on referrals and the existing referral pathway will enable them to continue benefitting from referral after the end of the project from the service points in the community.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

Lack of access to/awareness of referral services for survivors of protection incidents (GBV, child protection, for example)-We shall put in place a robust communication mechanism that allows the timely dissemination of information to all agencies and community members.We shall pass all information on referral services in local languages commonly spoken in Ibba County to ensure majority and minority groups in the community are able to get the information through using Zande language, Watu and Baka languages to break possible language barriers that would hinder access to information. Members of marginalized community’s/minority groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance:-We shall ensure inclusion of marginalized and minority groups as we deliver services through this project.We shall reach out the youth, children, people with disabilities, older people , minority ethnic groups with the aim of enabling them to give their input and opinions regarding this project, deliver meaningful participation and consultation to ensure that their views are reflected in all aspects of the response including assessment, delivery, monitoring and evaluation. Beneficiaries do not access/use complaint and feedback mechanisms:-As part of our field activity monitoring practices in SEM, we have included collection of feedback from our beneficiaries in part of our decision making processes. This ensures that collection and analysis of the feedback will not just be random, but as part of our important practices that will not be left out in this project. This will be practiced in all bomas and villages in Ibba where we shall be present and all beneficiaries shall be able to access and give their complaints and feedback though the laid out channels.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

In collection of beneficiary feedback, we shall use a variety of different mechanisms and sensitize target beneficiaries to the purpose of giving feedback to build confidence and overcome fears about giving feedback e.g. verbal face to face mechanisms to the protection officers, through community leaders, suggestion boxes or by telephone to our designated complaints number. We have developed a form that is available in the application to gather client satisfaction feedback. We shall employ the electronic method in Ibba to protection officers working in the county to gather beneficiary feedback. However, we shall consult our beneficiaries on the preferred methods that they would want to use in giving feedback. The collected feedback shall be shared across the entire project team, including the project management team that shall trigger adaptation of project to the needs of the target group and context.The vulnerable persons shall be given meaningful participation in all phases of this project. Our social workers and protection officers will ensure that they are given equal and non discriminatory access to the protection services that we shall offer and link them with other service providers through referral services. We shall ensure that communication methods used in this project are diverse, to cater for persons who are hard of hearing/ deaf persons, persons with blindness, and persons who are illiterate. When communication project information or gathering feedback and complaints, a mixture of written, oral, diverse local languages in Ibba (e.g. Zande), use of sign language interpreters and personal assistants will be provided for. This will help to eliminate communication barriers that can block gathering feedback.Our staff profile will also include women and female youths to ensure that there is no barrier in feedback provision. Specific groups e.g. girls would feel comfortable to give feedback to girls than to elderly women/ to men.
