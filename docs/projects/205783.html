Project Summary

This project will provide support to the extremely vulnerable, conflict and flood-affected Women, girls, boys and men, targeting IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in Ayod, Canal/Pigi, Longochuk and Maiwut Counties in Jonglei and Upper Nile States. The project will target 5,100 beneficiaries disaggregated as: 2,000 women, 1,300 girls, 900 boys and 900 men reached with the total budget of $255,000. 15% of persons with disabilities a total of 765 (PwDs) will also benefit through GBV awareness raising, provision of psychosocial support (PSS), door to door meetings, individual counseling and through other mobile emergency services. Universal Network for Child Defence Rights (UNCDR) has been operating in Ayod and Canal/Pigi counties of Jonglei state and in Longochuk and Maiwut counties of Upper Nile state by providing Protection and GBV services such as monitoring and data collection/assessment relating to GBV/Protection risks, returnees needs monitoring, advocacy on gender equality and inclusion through local engagement, awareness raising on protection/GBV concerns, youth and women empowerment activities and WGFS programming and referrals. UNCDR will continue to cement on the existing support in-line with Cluster strategic objective and HRP 2023-2024. UNCDR will further expand its areas of coverage to Fangak, Duk, Nasir and Ulang counties to increase on the population caseload reached while mitigating GBV risks in the targeting locations.
In order to ensure durable solution is achieved towards combating GBV, Gender and Disability Inclusion gaps in the community, Universal Network for Child Defence Rights (UNCDR) will work in collaboration with the existing local stakeholders to mainstream local solutions while uplifting centrality of protection to ensure safety and dignity of the affected persons. UNCDR will work in collaboration with WASH to support women and adolescent girls of the reproductive age with the menstrual hygiene kits (MHKs) or dignity kits, Education partners or cluster to raise awareness on gender consideration toward equal opportunity to education between girls and boys and health partners to support the survivors of rapes while providing specialized GBV services such as PSS, case management and counseling and referrals for MHPSS or CMR. UNCDR will also work with local structures to strengthen local policies that advocate for Gender equality, Disability inclusion and mitigate GBV in the community. UNCDR will strengthen institutional policies, standards and practices that drive action and accountability to GBV and equality. Universal Network for Child Defence Rights (UNCDR) will continue to identify and explore existing opportunities within the cultures to mitigate GBV and enhance the enablers that bridge gaps of gender and disability inclusion.
Universal Network for Child Defence Rights (UNCDR) will establish safe space programming such as women and girls’ friendly spaces to carryout recreational activities, counseling, PSS, case management and facilitate referrals that link beneficiaries to the available services. Local leaders and community stakeholders’ groups will be engaged and trained to become the champion of GBV, PwDs and gender inclusion in the community. Other component to mainstream include training of other partners on AAP, PSEA and Do No Harm Principles and protection/GBV mainstreaming approaches in Ayod, Canal/Pigi, Longochuk and Maiwut counties.

1. Needs

According to Humanitarian Needs Overviews (HNO) 2023, women and girls are differently affected, and their vulnerability is exacerbated by various forms of GBV, including rape, physical assault, forced marriage and SEA. Similarly, men and boys are also exposed to killings, abductions and forced recruitment, especially in conflict settings. As estimated 2.8 million people faces GBV risks in 2023 – a 9 percent increase from 2022 estimates. They will need GBV prevention, risk mitigation and scale-up of specialized GBV response services in 2024. GBV is reported to be high in some areas including in the counties targeted by this project. The situation is exacerbated by flooding and conflict leaving women and girls more vulnerable. Women feel unsafe to carry-out their routine duties such as collection of firewood and charcoals, access to water points, latrines and to the distribution sites including access to market places has been restricted. While justice and human rights are suppressed in the country and in the targeted areas in particular, many violations are under reported. The vulnerable women and girls, children, older persons and persons with disabilities are more expose to risks of exploitation with no access to justice and rule of law. Continuation of sub-national conflict is likely to disrupt the livelihoods and increase humanitarian needs leaving women to pressing economic situation causing physical and mental health deterioration. Early this year according to Inter-Agency Assessment Reports 27/10/2023 led by CMD in Ayod County, the Northern Unity have been affected by consecutive events of conflicts combined with flooding causing mass displacement. UNCDR field reports on GBV and Protection intervention further highlighted a concerning situation as well. The two states in the targeted counties in particular continued to experience cattle raiding and ambushes restricting humanitarian access and intercommunal fighting exposing women and girls to high level of rapes, sexual assaults and domestic violence. The negative cultural practices biased against women and girls, poverty and constraint to access to services pushes women and girls to become more vulnerable. Lack of legal and judicial system limit provision for legal redress in response to gender based violations. The medical, mental health and psychosocial support services have diminished as the country rages with no support mechanisms. Through this life-saving project, UNCDR will ensure that provision of GBV prevention and risks mitigation measures, to reduce the suffering of GBV survivors and to strengthen vulnerability assessment and risk analysis to identify risks to various forms of gender-based violence by using data analysis. UNCDR will be using two approaches; 1. Both static and mobile service delivery (Integrated mobile protection services) and 2. Primary GBV prevention intervention, such as Engaging Men and Boys through Accountable Practice and Community Care Programme, will address harmful social norms and systemic gender inequality. Community engagement and awareness raising activities will focus on the risk factors for GBV, available response services and timely seeking of services and reporting of GBV incidents within 72 hours. UNCDR will work closely with women, girls, boys, men, leaders, clusters, existing partners, local actors, persons with disabilities and cluster to combat GBV and gender barriers and create safe environment for vulnerable persons among the IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in Ayod, Canal/Pigi, Longochuk and Maiwut Counties.

2. Project activities

Activity 1: Provide PSS focusing on healing, empowerment and recovery. Activity 2: Provision of case management services including coordinated care and support Activity 3: Establish and strengthening GBV referral systems and linking GBV survivors to available response services and support. Activity 4: Implement Women and Girls Friendly Space (WGFS) programming to provide services, information and activities that promote healing, well-being and empowerment Activity 5: Provision of legal services that protect the rights of GBV survivors and promote their access to justice Activity 6: Conduct dignity kit distribution and programming targeting vulnerable women and girls as part of GBV risk mitigation. Activity 7: Conduct community awareness raising activities on GBV risk factors, mitigation measures and on how survivors can access support and services in line with the “do no harm” principle Activity 8: Implement primary GBV prevention interventions to addresses harmful practices, systemic gender inequality and power imbalances Activity 9: Provide capacity building trainings on various topics of GBV to frontline service providers Activity 10: Enhance GBV preparedness, contingency planning and resilience building to prevent and respond to GBV Activity 11: Conduct Mobile GBV services to respond to the community. The project will collaborate with community social workers and GBV Officers to conduct 2 mobile GBV sessions per month to provide information and services to girls, boys, adolescents, women, and men including people with disability to most identified GBV risk areas during mapping. This mobile services will be integrated into other UNCDR project activities, specifically during nutrition and FSL sessions, health, sanitation and hygiene promotion meetings.
