Project Summary

UNICEF will focus its interventions in the hard-hit subdivisions from the divisions affected by floods which are Kai-Kai in Mayo-Danay and Logone-Birni in Logone-et-Chari. It is expected that these interventions benefit to a total number of 14,157 children (5,748 girls) from 48 schools (44 primary & 04 secondary). UNICEF’s interventions shall (i) support the effective resumption of learning activities for all targeted children and (ii) improve the readiness of children enrolled in last year of primary for the cycle-end examinations. To ensure the sustainability, these interventions shall be addressed to affected schools for them to benefit to several generations of pupils.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In 2022, 151 schools have been affected by floods in Logone-&-Chari & Mayo-Danay divisions, depriving approximately 54,511 pupils (32,231 girls) from their right to Education. Only 09% of the total people in need, 3,418 children (1,294 girls) from 11 primary schools in Logone-&-Chari could effectively resume learning activities with the support from on sector actor, which is UNICEF. UNICEF will focus its interventions in the hard-hit subdivisions from the divisions affected by floods which are Kai-Kai in Mayo-Danay and Logone-Birni in Logone-et-Chari. It is expected that these interventions benefit to a total number of 14,157 children (5,748 girls) from 48 schools (44 primary & 04 secondary). UNICEF’s interventions shall (i) support the effective resumption of learning activities for all targeted children and (ii) improve the readiness of children enrolled in last year of primary for the cycle-end examinations. To ensure the sustainability, these interventions shall be addressed to affected schools for them to benefit to several generations of pupils.
