Project Summary

The proposed project interventions will broadly compliment to the efforts of other agencies in improving the overall vulnerability conditions of the peoples in Rohingya camps and host communities living close to the camps. EKATA will provide 252 new double story shelters in camp 21 and 40 new one story shelters to HC. In targeting the beneficiaries for the proposed interventions, priority will be given to the families who are the most vulnerable in terms of abilities and accessibility to the basic facilities relating to the planned deliverables of this project
This project will specifically consider some criteria’s to ensure inclusion of the most vulnerable and the special needy groups available within the targeted project areas as follows:
 New arrival Rohingya people and Families living in unsafe conditions and having no/least access to the basic services
 Families in host communities having no/least access to the basic services
 Families living in the areas at the risk of natural disasters and experiencing impacts during/after monsoon, cyclone, tornado, landslide etc.
 Women/widows/PLW headed families.
 Families with PWDs, old aged peoples with no earning member.
 Families with adolescent girl.
In the host communities, EKATA will provide new shelters. EKATA will engage the host communities in all levels of the decision-making of the activities, to promote ownership and increase the empowerment of vulnerable and marginalised individuals. Sector guidance for HC assistance will be followed and EKATA will actively participate in the HC TWiG meetings.
EKATA will undertake all the activities using a team that will be trained in gender and disability inclusion and PSEA. Gender equality will be mainstreamed throughout the project and each of the activities will include sensitization activities on abandonment of gender-based violence (GBV) and the promotion of women and girls’ rights. EKATA will also ensure that there is an independent complaints mechanism available for all activities.
Purpose-1: Provide safe and dignified living conditions to reduce suffering and increase resilience

1 - Identified Needs

EKATA, being an agency responding to the crisis, has carried out a comprehensive Need Assessment (NA) covering both Rohingya and host communities during August-September 2019 aiming to establish an evidence based baseline, multi-sector information as well as to chart needs, status of accessibility/ availability of basic services relating to livelihood, shelter, education, WaSH and protection. This provide the basis for designing/panning interventions for both Rohingya refugees and the host communities in the targeted geo locations. To ensure targeting the most vulnerable populations in camp location and host community MSNA data findings were also considered. The MSNA data findings of camp 21 shows that 18% HH have problem with shelter, leaking roof of 73% HH, leaking walls of 41% HH, rotten/ broken bamboo of 43% HHs. In addition data findings also shows that 44% HH did not improved shelter in last 6 months, replace roof 43% HH, replace walls 28% HH and replace bamboo only 27% HH. The data indicate that target population needs of more durable shelter. As per decision of MoDR, RRRC office no single shelter will be built and all new shelter will be double story shelter. No more land will be allowed from govt. and all existing Rohingya population should be accommodating within existing camp boundaries. Considering the situation RRRC decision is to build double story shelter in all camps. Accordingly EKATA collected working design and site plan in coordination with shelter sector and UNHCR team. EKATA will include the most vulnerable HHs both in camp and host community through community consultation in coordination with site management agency and local government bodies. EKATA will involve women, older people, youth in the targeted small scale construction works during 2 storey shelters construction: bamboo mat preparation, cleaning, installation.
