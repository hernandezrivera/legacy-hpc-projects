Project Summary

The proposed project’s overarching aim is to contribute to improving the living conditions for refugees in camps/settlements through the provision of safe, adequate and appropriate shelter and related NFI according to the assessed needs . To achieve this, the project proposes to continue and expand shelter initiatives in camp 23 Shamlapur, in Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, launched by PIN in partnership with local organization PULSE in 2018. This approach is in line with the strategy adopted by the shelter coordination structures, who are beginning to move into more durable solutions in recognition of the protracted nature of the refugee crisis, and the need for improved shelter and camp settlement safety. (Complementary activity to the IOM assistance)

Needs

The proposed project is in line with the strategy adopted by the shelter coordination structures, taking into account the needs and beginning to move into more durable solutions in recognition of the protracted nature of the refugee crisis, and the need for improved shelter and camp settlement safety. As per the shelter sector needs analysis, camps and sites remain congested seriously impacting physical and psychological well-being, especially of children, women, and persons with disabilities, elderly. According to the recent NPM data, neither the average area per person nor the covered living space per person meet the sphere standards with significantly lowered standards as per the square meters in reality available per person Also, the lack of adequate lighting in shelters and public spaces was reported as a top safety concern in the camp areas. Considering the volume of shelter needs in the targeted areas of implementation, this proposal aims to support persons of concern with transitional shelter solutions (Phase 3), and key NFI items in targeted areas.

Activities or outputs

Project and activities are designed to complement IOM funded activities in the area of Shamlapur1. Preparatory activities1.1 Needs assessment, community risk mapping, and selection of beneficiaries: Based on the experience from PIN community-based protection activities, selected community members will be trained and involved in community risk mapping to enable them to identify risks, including risks related to disasters and protection concerns. 1.2 Shelter construction trainings: Selected Households will be trained by experienced staff in durable construction techniques to maintain and/or upgrade shelters and site infrastructure, allowing communities to be more resilient in the future. Trainings will be conducted by experienced PIN/PULSE staff.2. Transitional shelters2.1 Designs of transitional shelter models: Consortium partners will work with RRRC-approved transitional shelter designs to be applied and adjusted to the site specificity of the HH’s shelter. 2.2 Procurement of material: Materials will be procured (if not provided by IOM) where possible locally or regionally through PIN procurement procedures. PIN will ensure all logistic arrangements for the safe storing and distribution (warehousing in a PIN/PULSE warehouse, trucking).2.3 Distribution of transitional shelter kits: All material will be distributed directly to pre-selected beneficiaries based on assessments conducted by PULSE field staff. 2.4 Implementation of activities: PULSE will provide the labour force for all shelter repairs by assembling local skilled and unskilled community volunteers selected through camp management bodies. Volunteers will be trained adequately by PIN and PULSE technical staff. This will give them the capacities to support other community members in constructing transitional and mid-term shelters through cash for work mechanisms. 2.5 Post distribution monitoring: post distribution monitoring will be conducted to monitor the use of the provided assistance and any possible challenges. 3. NFIs & improved lighteningBased on needs assessment and community risk mapping, identified NFI needs (including the lightening system) will be identified 3.1 Procurement of materials (solar lights, other identified NFI) – as per activity 2.2 or cash/voucher transfers, in case cash modality is approved3.2 Distribution of solar lights – as per activity 2.33.3 Installation of solar lights: beneficiaries supported, if needed, by the volunteers – as per activity 2.43.4 Post distribution monitoring – as per activity 2.5
