Project Summary

In this project, NGO X is submitting an integrated approach to Shelter and Winter cash to address the multiple vulnerabilities that households are facing.
Shelter activities target beneficiaries at a HH level creates links between multi-donor funded emergency response, winterization and shelter programming, to complement food assistance programming. Households are targeted with CASH for Shelter units in IDP sites repairs and rehabilitation, NGO X will rehabilitate shelter units in IDP sites using self-help modality providing CASH grants to HHs to carry out the required work themselves. The shelter rehabilitation activity will target 760 HHs. Moreover, NGO X will target two IDP site (340 HHs) with the construction of dignified shelter units, and carryout site planning and infrastructure rehabilitation and upgrades in addition to providing basic WASH services. The HH level interventions are done in tandem with distribution of MPCA and winterization cash.
On the settlement level, the program will focus on flood prone IDP sites, The intervention will aim to providing preventive measures to alleviate the effects of flooding and prepare for winter to ensure uninterrupted access to assistance and services. NGOx will carry out IDP site improvement activities including roads gravelling, construction and rehabilitation of rainwater drainage systems, tents levelling, and installation of solar lights targeting 6,648 HHs.
NGO X will enable Highly vulnerable IDPs and resident households to meet their basic winter needs through coordinated and timely winter cash. The priority locations for the winterization response are typically selected according to the following criteria: mountainous areas, areas with snow coverage and areas with historic cold weather patterns. A fuel market assessment with fuel vendors and Winter Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment (MSNA) with affected people will then be conducted to ascertain the availability of fuel, market access and functionality. NGO X will aim to complete winter cash response by the end of November each year. Cash will be the main and the default response mechanism as long as minimum preconditions for the use of cash exist. The cash transfer value per HH is $ 150 which is informed by SNFI cluster winterization guidance. NGO X plans to utilize an e-card platform to deliver cash where feasible to reach a selected number of particularly vulnerable beneficiaries. Under this activity, priority will begiven to child headed households, single/widowed women, older persons and people with disabilities. For the Winter cash component, a total of 10,000 households/ 50,000 Individuals living in particularly cold and mountainous areas, including camps and informal settlements will be targeted under this project.
In total the unique target for 2023 is 10,000 HHs and 50,000 individuals

3 - Needs

According to the Whole of Syria (WoS) SNFI Dashboard (MSNA 2022/HNO 2023) 53% of respondents in NWS mentioned Shelter as one of their top three humanitarian needs; 42% mentioned rehabilitation of infrastructure. 95% of respondents reported shelter inadequacies related to electricity/lighting; 82% mentioned lack of insulation and 37% reported poor facilities for persons with specific needs. Poor shelter conditions place already vulnerable households at a higher risk, impacting on health, safety and security, protection and wellbeing. Respondents of the October 2022 HSOS indicated that the cost of shelter materials and tools for repair (by 98%) or to hire required professional services (by 74%) are too high to be covered by the little or inexistent financial capacity of households in NWS. In NWS, 26% of HHs reported roads as a key missing infrastructure. NGO X Fuel Market and Winter MSNA in Sept 2022 found that fuel for heating/cooking was the top priority need for 77% of interviewees during the winter season. Although the fuel market was functional, the assessed populations didn't have the financial capacity to access it. 99% of interviewees preferred cash as a response modality.

4 - Implementation of activities

NGO X will install dignified Shelter units alongside with the necessary infrastructure to ensure dignified living conditions. Moreover, shelter units’ upgrades using cash modality and contracted service will be carried out.Tented settlements and IDP camps will be targeted with site and access improvements including rehabilitation of roads to ensure uninterrupted access within camps during winter months, installation of solar lighting, including around WASH facilities, installation of sewage system and rainwater drainage improvements. These activities will reduce protection risks, especially for women and children, and improve access for persons with reduced mobility (e.g., older persons and persons with disabilities). All planned construction and infrastructure activities will be monitored by NGO X technical team, pre and post implementation safety audits, and satisfaction surveys are planned as well by NGO X quality department. For winter cash, NGO X will:• Conduct Fuel Market assessment and Winter MSNA of households.• Identify the initial locations most vulnerable to cold weather. Then will carry out 100% HHs selection and verification process. • Provide winter cash to vulnerable households. The cash value is budgeted at $150 in line with S/NFI cluster winterization guidance. • Conduct Distribution Monitoring (DM) during the distributions and Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) surveys following distributions.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of physical harm at CASH distribution points (disproportionately affecting women)Mitigation: Select safe and accessible distribution points in consultation with the beneficiaries; Organise separate queues for men and women; Provide a ‘home delivery’ option for at-risk groups and persons with mobility difficulties. Monitoring: Regular on-site monitoring and post-distribution monitoring. Ensure there are female staff present at distributions. Risk of tension between host communities, IDPs and returneesMitigation: Ensure the response is designed based on needs assessments and in collaboration with affected communities and local stakeholders. Promote the active participation of women and other at-risk groups in all assessments; Raise awareness on selection criteria in collaboration with local authorities; Diversify CFM and ensure accessibility, in particular for vulnerable groups. Monitoring: Regular discussions with local authorities and monitoring of inter-community tensions. Risk of eviction from dignified shelter due to lack of approval from property owner and/or exposing beneficiaries at risk due to lack of HLP documentation for the shelter rehabilitatedMitigation: Carry out HLP due diligence as part of the needs assessment and ensure that consensus have been obtained before proceeding with constructions; Ensure signed housing agreement with local authorities include a HLP rights clause. Monitoring: HLP Due Diligence based on guidance prepared by Clusters.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Risk of abuse of authority by distribution actors (GBV, discrimination, monetary coercion), disproportionately exposing women to risks of sexual exploitation and abuseMitigation: • Ensure all involved actors (staff, contractors) are informed about and sign the NGOX’s Safeguarding Policies and Code of Conduct; • Ensure shelter staff working with affected populations and in direct contact with beneficiaries have received training in prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA); • Inform about NGOX’s CFM and ensure this information is accessible to all affected people; • Provide clear information to affected population explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services/favours to anyone in exchange for receiving shelter or assistance. Ensure that this information is communicated to women and girls; • Ensure risks of SEA are considered and mitigated when selecting sites, planning and conducting distributions; • Ensure at-risk groups (women, children, persons with disabilities) are prioritized during distributions and that separate queues are organised; • Train NGOX’s investigation department on safeguarding to handle complaints in an ethical, safe and confidential manner. Monitoring: Ensure various groups (including women, older persons, persons with disabilities) participate in distribution and post-distribution monitoring.
