Project Summary

The proposed project adopts a comprehensive approach to address critical humanitarian challenges in NW Syria, focusing on protection, psychosocial well-being, and resilience-building initiatives. Aligned with the principled response to achieve Protection Objective 3, the project aims to establish 3 community centers equipped with integrated protection and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) services. These centers will extend outreach through mobile teams to reach vulnerable populations in informal settings, addressing gaps in assistance, including violations related to housing, land, and property (HLP) and ethnically driven abuses.
Specific objectives include improving access to quality protection and MHPSS services, directly addressing challenges such as restricted resource access and protection risks. The project's holistic approach encompasses legal advice/counseling and cash for protection interventions.
Additionally, structured awareness sessions, community-based educational campaigns, and information provision aim to enhance understanding of protection risks and mental health issues. Youth-driven initiatives focusing on social cohesion promote unity within diverse communities.
The project also strengthens the capacity of humanitarian actors, engages with local organizations, and fosters community-based protection committees to build technical capacity and advocate for inclusion.
In summary, the proposed project's comprehensive interventions aim to effectively respond to critical humanitarian needs in NWS. Through tailored approaches, it seeks to provide essential services, empower communities, and promote long-term resilience in the region. Additionally, it places emphasis on peace-building interventions, coexistence initiatives, and transitional justice measures to foster sustainable peace and reconciliation.
