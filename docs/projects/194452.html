Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to strengthen prevention, response services for Children and GBV programming in Mundri, Terekeka and Juba Counties. This humanitarian response plan is an integrated child protection and GBV project. The plan targets four counties Mundri West and Mundri East counties in Western Equatoria state and Juba and Terekeka counties in Central Equatoria state. This is an Integrated Project which includes the outcomes for Child Protection and Gender Based Violence Programming. Based on the competency and experience of Active Youth Agency (AYA) in both Child Protection and GBV interventions, the organization aims to reach approximately 16500 beneficiaries. Directly under Child Protection, the calculated number of beneficiaries amounts to 8250 while the 8250 beneficiaries will be reached through GBV interventions. One percent (1%) of beneficiaries will be persons living with disability. 330 individuals (165 male/ 165 female). These people will be targeted and specifically looked for during the program implementation.
Major activities includes Case Management for both GBV survivors and Child Protection, awareness raising in communities on topical issues, conducting prevention interventions (EMAP), women and girls friendly space programming, both community based structural child friendly spaces that will enable healing process for survivors of Gender Based Violence and Child Abuse. AYA piloted Caring for Child Survivors Initiative (CASI) program in Juba and therefore, the organization will use the knowledge to create the synergy in the activities. Hence all the groups will benefit. This project builds on gains of the services AYA is providing through women and girls friendly space in Juba. As well the prevention programming the organization is exposed to in last few years presents corporative advantage since there will be no new invention.
Currently AYA participates in different coordination forum and above all the organization is leading Consortium of Resilient Innovative and Sustainable- South Sudan (RISE-SSD). This consortium has started gaining momentum in recent months. The consortium aims at bring Localization from International Discourse to Local Action. This therefore, explains the potential the organization is placed to advance the GBV and Child Protection agenda. AYA have also piloted innovative Self-Help Groups in Greater Mundri which comprises of 90% female. This initiative has proved to be an excellent women empowerment strategy. Therefore, via innovation, new ideas always comes where practical solutions to problem facing women, girls, boys and men will practically be identified, developed, tested and piloted. AYA is currently implementing Peace Building Projects and Food Security and Livelihoods projects in Greater Mundri Counties. These initiatives or projects are the true meaning of promoting triple nexus.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

All the feedback will be given high priority. Especially from persons with disability that report distance concerns, women that report risks and protection concerns shall be addressed immediately. Safety plans will be discussed with the community leaders and the beneficiaries themselves to mitigate the challenges that arises as result of intervention in the community. Especially for women and persons with disability.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

There will be suggestion boxes and explained to the community on how to put in feedback and complaints. AYA already have contact details of the dedicated staffs to receive the complaints and feedback. These contacts shall be disseminated to the community through the awareness sessions and therefore, all the complaints that come shall be directed to the contacts available and this will make the communities feel comfortable to share their issues.AYA top management will analyze all the complaints and provide answers or feedback. The top management is responsible to go and give the feedback to the aggrieved community or beneficiaries.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

AYA has PSEA policy in place. The organization during recruitment asks the new staffs to sign the policy and Code of Conduct that prohibits the organizational staff and the other personnel including vendors from exploiting the vulnerability of the beneficiaries. AYA shall conduct continuous awareness raising to the beneficiaries on how to report issues related to its staff that might engage in sexual exploitation and abuse. There are already contact details of the Focal Points to whom the cases of sexual abuse and exploitation shall be reported. This will enable the community specifically the beneficiaries to report the abuses and it will dealt with using the appropriate channels.AYA as strong GBV partner shall also report any case that comes from its partners using the contacts of the Focal Points including the UN who is the lead.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

All the feedback regarding the quality of assistance, location, timing and waiting period that poses protection concerns will be documented and appropriate actions will be taken to resolve the matter. The organization works always to mitigate the protection concerns that will arise as result of its work. AYA uses do no harm principle to deliver assistance to the beneficiaries.

8.11. Mitigation Measures.

- Violence during registration, AYA shall train its staffs on key protection mainstreaming techniques including DO NO Harm principles that will not expose beneficiaries to risks of violence during registration.- PSEA- AYA has PSEA policy and orients the team who enter the organization. Everyone signs the commitment whether staff or any other personnel working for AYA including contractors.- Dissemination of awareness on potential of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse to the community and giving out the contact information for the Focal Points to be reached in case of any suspicion.- AYA shall always ensure that awareness on referral services is done to the beneficiaries this includes the contact information of Case Workers on the referral pathways for GBV or Child Protection.- The project will keep special emphasis on marginalized groups of people. Therefore, special invitation will be done to ensure the groups are not omitted from the activities of the project. Therefore all will receive the same services.- The complaint mechanism including suggestion boxes will be placed in open and continuous awareness to the beneficiaries about the need to launch complaints shall be conducted by the team. Proper contacts of the management shall be put in place for easy access by the beneficiaries. The management will respond on the complaints launched.- Training of Case Workers, management and the data shall only be managed by Focal Point of GBVIMS or CPIMS. All the data collected shall be confidential and can only is disseminated to intended recipients. - To mitigate the interference by the stakeholders shall be done by proper selection criteria, monitoring the selection process and gathering feedback from beneficiaries on previous work done in the community. - Clear dissemination of information on risk or hazardous areas. Clear advocacy on land use will be disseminated to the beneficiaries and appropriate referral to secure services.
