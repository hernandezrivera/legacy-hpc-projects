Project Summary

This project with project code of (HSDN24-PRO-209353-1) aims to provide multi sub-sector protection service including Child protection , GBV and general protection for 68,372 individuals among affected population Gaziera, Sennar, Gadaref, and Central Darfur states,
The intervention is mainly designed to meet immediate lifesaving and emergency needs of the vulnerable people mainly women, men , girls, and boys with special focus of persons with specific needs among targeted communities children, including disable children, elderly , ethnic minorities.
This project will carry out extensive consultations with local community and line ministries as an entry point for smooth project implementation. HOPE embraces community based protection approach by building capacity of 27 community based protection networks of 405 members, in Zaligi, Wadi Salih of CD , Senga, Dander, Alsouki, and Sennar, Madani, Hesahisa, Sharuq –El-Gaziera and UmElqura localities of El-Gazeira stat and Madainat ElGadaref on Protection to take major roles in project activities implementation . The participants will be equipped with necessary skills and knowledge to implement community driven solutions to their local communities. 500 vulnerable separated boys and girls will be identified and registered for FTR services while other 4,000 children will provided with PSS , on the other hands the project will support PSN through direct services and referral to services providers to increase their well-being , awareness raising session and public campaigns in Protection and GVB will be organized in the project targeted locations reached by 26,660 community members . Two community multi perpose centers will be constructed along with establishment of 4 help desks for effective identification of PSN to be access for individuals protection assistance (IPA) by providing cash grants to prevent , mitigate and response to protection concerns.
The project will also construct 3 women centers in Senga, Hesahisa and Gadaref and rehabilitate another 3 in Madani, Zaligi, and Sennar. About 8,500 women and girls will be reached through distribution of dignity kits in project targeted locations.
HOPE will consider accountability to affected people through full community participation, strengthen the current community mechanism/ CBPNs as well as do follow up for all benefit targeted separately to assess the project, identify challenges and provide feedback/ recommendations which will be used to inform strategy and for future plans improvements

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

o ensure protection mainstreaming, HOPE will use the below actions: • Conduct regular consultations with particularly high-risk groups of the affected population such as women, girls, and children with disabilities. • Support community engagement and interaction. • Establishment of complaints and feedback mechanisms. • Foster community-based Disaster Risk Reduction plans/strategies; to support activities that contribute to child protection and gender-based violence (GBV) prevention including psychosocial support, and referral pathways; • Teachers, learners, and parents, will be oriented on topics such as protection, human rights, sexual and gender-based violence, and non-discrimination approaches to access humanitarian aid. • Ensure that all multi-purpose center construction is in line with accessibility standards for persons with a disability, cash-based assistance will be provided to children with disabilities to access tools and materials to enhance their learning. • Pay attention to the choice of locations and targeting of beneficiaries to avoid perceptions that assistance is delivered in a discriminatory or non-equitable manner. • Conducting community safety audits and mapping of exiting services and ensuring that the results of these two exercises inform programming and advocacy. • Maintain transparent, inclusive, and adequate communication with communities on available services; and by consulting communities and duty bearers in the planning, selection of services, feedback after service provision. • Guided on ensuring prioritization of services to groups considered particularly vulnerable, such as young children, children with disability adolescent boys and girls, children without adult care, the elderly, and children and adults with specific needs ensuring that programming is tailored to respond to the needs of adolescent boys and girls. • Involve community actors (especially CBPN, and parent) in the project planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation to promote participation and empowerment. • Involving and consulting CBPN in planning and conducting a monthly meeting at the community level to avoid causing harm and prioritize safety and dignity.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

To guarantee accountability and full participation of the affected population including children, the community will be consulted for culturally acceptable, safe, and accessible mechanisms in terms of diversity. This consultation will be done in the form of rapid needs assessment, community stakeholder meetings, and service mapping. Moreover, HOPE will establish various community project management committees to support the intervention. These committees will facilitate the identification and registration of beneficiaries and receive and address complaints, including the supervising provision and distribution of relief including hygiene kits. Water Management Committees will also encourage community participation and ensure ownership and sustainability of WASH installations. Increased knowledge and awareness of the project and details of key contact persons will be provided on the strategically affixed manner in the target project locations. Moreover, to further ensure inclusive participation of all crisis-affected people HOPE will designate complaint and feedback focal points – from both males and one females, in all locations to respond to traditional barriers in communication between women and men, limited access for persons with disabilities, and persons with chronic illness, and low literacy among the population in intervention. Also the Hygiene Promoters, Water user committees, and community based protection networks members will be trained on complaint handling and reporting to make sure that all voices are heard and any concerns or complaint are well considered. The feedback collected will be used to improve program quality as well as inform strategy and future‘s plan improvements in gaps that shall be identified. The feedback is intended for HOPE to respond to sensitive complaints and establish a safe and confidential strategy for safeguarding communities from any forms of exploitation and abuse
