Project Summary

The project aims to provide life-saving WASH assistances to 100,000 IDPs, migrants, and refugees in the in-need and hosting communities including other locations where humanitarian WASH assistance is needed (such as Al Jabal Al Gharbi, Sabha, Tripoli, Ubari). The project will preposition essential WASH supplies that is sufficient to respond to some 20,000 people in case of emerging humanitarian WASH needs across the country in a timely manner. The project activities include: a) provision of safe water supplies through water trucking, minor rehabilitation of water networks, and installation of water storage tanks. b) provision of safe sanitation facilities through installation of emergency latrines, emptying septic tanks, fumigation of potential areas of contamination, decommissioning of emergency latrines c) Prepositioning and distribution of hygiene items.
The Rapid Response Mechanism (RRM) is designed to deliver immediate, life-saving supplies to affected families, most notably food (immediate Response Rations), drinking water and hygiene kits, dignity kits with a focus on women and girls of reproductive age, NFIs, mother/baby kits, basic lifesaving maternal health care kits and vaccines. These services will be provided to persons on the move or displaced due to the armed conflict, hard-to-reach areas, caught at checkpoints or stranded between front-lines within 72 hours of receiving DTM reports, or DTM verification of the needs highlighted by government or non-government partners in letters of assistance submitted to the consortium. The overall goal of the Libya Integrated Rapid Response Mechanism is to save lives and mitigate negative impact of acute crisis on lives and dignity of affected groups with emphasis on vulnerable groups especially women and children. It is expected that RRM will be able to reach affected population with hygiene kits along with other necessary critical supplies at the early onset of a crisis. The intervention will be a geographical focus based on a vulnerability in given locations of displacement and will precede or complement the sectoral response, but delivery will remain affected by insecurity, accessibility, logistics…etc.

Libya Humanitarian Response Plan 2020

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Recent multi-sectorial assessment conducted by UNICEF, REACH and other agencies highlighted the dire humanitarian needs of the displaced population due to the recent conflict in some parts of the country such as Murzouq, Sebha, and the southern neighborhoods of Tripoli. The main WASH needs of newly conflict affected population include life saving WASH assistances in collective centers and the areas of return and essential hygiene items for the most vulnerable IDPs and returnees families.

2 - Activities

- Water trucking where there is no access to safe water source - Minor rehabilitation of water systems - Installation of storage water tanks - Installation of emergency latrines that are separated by sex - Emptying septic tanks/ decommissioning of emergency latrines - Fumigation - Hygiene awareness raising and distribution of basic hygiene items with male and female hygiene promoters/volunteers
