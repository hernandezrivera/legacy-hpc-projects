Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to give dignity to the life of the people who are the most vulnerable people in Northwest Syria thru giving them short-term work opportunities, vocational and skills training, basic housing repair, rehabilitation of social infrastructure, and other early recovery and livelihood activities thru out 2023.
Persons with disabilities will be prioritized in activities.
As stated in HNO 2023, ERL activities and Livelihoods activities are priority needs due to high inflation and price destabilization. In addition, ERL activities are very important in this economic instability environment.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project aims to restore dignity and resilience among the most vulnerable populations in Northwest Syria by providing short-term work opportunities, vocational training, skills development, basic housing repair, social infrastructure rehabilitation, and other early recovery and livelihood activities throughout 2024. Special emphasis will be placed on prioritizing persons with disabilities in all project activities. Overview:The project addresses priority needs identified in the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2024, focusing on Early Recovery and Livelihood (ERL) activities to mitigate the impact of high inflation and price destabilization. By providing opportunities for employment, skills enhancement, and community infrastructure rehabilitation, the project seeks to empower vulnerable individuals and communities to rebuild their lives and livelihoods. Key Components: Short-Term Work Opportunities: Creation of short-term employment opportunities for vulnerable individuals, providing income generation and economic stability. Vocational and Skills Training: Provision of vocational training programs to equip beneficiaries with marketable skills and enhance their employability in various sectors. Basic Housing Repair: Support for the repair and rehabilitation of basic housing infrastructure, ensuring safe and dignified living conditions for vulnerable households. Social Infrastructure Rehabilitation: Rehabilitation of essential community facilities such as schools, healthcare centers, and water supply systems to improve access to vital services and enhance community resilience. Priority for Persons with Disabilities: Special consideration and prioritization of persons with disabilities in all project activities, ensuring their inclusion and participation in the recovery process. Expected Outcomes: Economic Empowerment: Beneficiaries will gain access to income-generating opportunities and skill development programs, enhancing their economic resilience and livelihood prospects. Improved Living Conditions: Through basic housing repair and social infrastructure rehabilitation, vulnerable households and communities will experience improved living standards and access to essential services. Enhanced Community Resilience: The project contributes to strengthening community resilience by restoring social infrastructure and fostering community cohesion and self-reliance. Inclusive Development: By prioritizing persons with disabilities, the project promotes inclusivity and ensures that all members of the community have equal access to opportunities for recovery and livelihood improvement.
