Project Summary

The purpose of this project, funded through DCA's Innovation Fund, is to test the ideas for youth empowerment and development in conflict zones through the activation of the "CLAC process" (CLAC: "Club d'Action Citoyenne", or youth citizen clubs). In partnership with youth organisation AJCAD, DCA Mali aims to support the establishment of 10 CLACs and ensure that 50 youth develop action plans for active citizenship and resilience. Youth are trained, through the CLACs on citizenship, conflict prevention and management and entrepreneurship. The project's intervention zone is the Douentza circle in the Mopti Region.
