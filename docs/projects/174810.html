Project Summary

The project will support vulnerable food insecure people (moderate food insecure) through seasonal in-kind food assistance in targeted areas of the Far North regions. The targeting will be based on Cadre Harmonise analysis. A four months food assistance will be provided to the beneficiaries during the lean season June to September. Through the in-kind food assistance, the vulnerable households will have access to adequate and nutritious food, thus preventing them from adopting negative coping practices. The 50% ration of the in-kind food will provide 1,050 kcal energy per day per person. The food basket and the daily rations will comprise 175g cereals, 50g pulses; 17.5g vegetable, and 2.5g salt.
The assistance aims at contributing to ensure food security of the households during the period of hardship and “Leave No one Behind” is key throughout the project. In line with WFP protection strategy; special attention will be paid to households that include people with specific needs and an adequate support will be provided to these people while taking while into consideration the situation of the head of the household (whether male or female or child). The presence of pregnant woman, elderly person, disabled person etc. in the household also has an impact on food security.
Regular monitoring activities will be conducted during food distribution and monthly data collected against performance indicators for reporting. The gender distribution of roles between men, women, girls and boys in decision-making on food consumption in the household will constitute a key focus. Targeted vulnerable local populations will be closely involved in various stages including beneficiaries targeting process, food distribution also through complaints and feedback mechanisms they could freely express their views and concerns on the project implementation.
Within the framework of Accountability to Affected Populations, beneficiaries’ is ensured through an operational complaints and feedback mechanism established. Complaints will be treated acted upon respectfully and considered during project implementation. In collaboration with local administrative and traditional authorities as well as cooperating partners, joint assessments will be conducted to identify the most vulnerable people among the local populations for consideration in the projects based on vulnerability criteria. These criteria consider household size, presence of children under-5, elderly and handicapped, women-headed households, and household’s assets. To enhance resilience sensitization sessions will organized for women and men with emphasis on equal participation in decision-making on matters pertaining to household’s food security as well as embarking on income generating activities. WFP will ensure that beneficiaries, women, men, girls and boys are involved and consulted at different stages of the project through focus group discussions, gender and protection evaluation field missions, post distribution monitoring exercises and others. Through CFM, beneficiaries can voice out their opinions on various aspects including food preferences and transfer modalities and targeting criteria.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The Lake Chad crisis has continued to affect the Far North Region mainly Mayo Sava, Logone and Chari and Mayo Tsanaga Divisions. Despite Government efforts to ensure security in these regions sporadic attacks are perpetrated in these divisions by the Boko Haram groups. In addition to the chronic vulnerability of these division, the presence of Refugees and IDPs affected some of them living with host families in various safer locations in villages or towns, is putting pressure on the scarce community resources and other economic assets in these areas. Boko Haram attacks have disrupted the economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in the Far North region, causing a depletion of livelihoods and household assets. Therefore, the existing chronic vulnerability and structural food insecurity have been aggravated. The region is predominantly arid and regularly hit by drought and seasonal floods. The most recent floods in October 2020 left about 10,000 hectares of cultivated farmland destroyed. The October 2019 Cadre Harmonisé highlighted that 1.3 million people in the Far North are food insecure. Out of this number, 232,934 people are severely food insecure, which means that they have limited or no access to enough, nutritious food required to live a healthy life. The Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) reports that vulnerable families in the conflict-affected areas of the Far North Region are experiencing ‘Stress’ level of acute food insecurity due to flood and pest-related crop damage, and attacks by Boko Haram. As food insecurity affects differently men, women, girls and boys of various ages, Food assistance will prioritize the most affected local vulnerable populations. In addition, the limited access to land, resources, income-generating activities; credit, seeds have an impact on the economic situation of the household and on food security. The local vulnerable people are in a dire need of food support while there are busy with farming activities. The assistance will support households’ access to adequate and nutritious food, therefore prevent them from adopting negative coping strategies during the lean season.
