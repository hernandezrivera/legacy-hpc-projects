Project Summary

The region has witnessed the scourge of problems and painful events that it has witnessed over the course of nearly fourteen years, leading to a decline in the standard of living of the people as a result of the rise in the prices of materials, in addition to the drought that the region has been exposed to during the past three years and its severe impact on agriculture. This has led to the inability of many families to sustain themselves. Securing its basic needs, especially since the area is agricultural and pastoral par excellence, extending from the city of Qamishli to the town of Al-Yarubiyah, Ma’bada, Al-Jawadiyah, and the countryside of the city of Al-Qahtaniyah. Its countryside is agricultural, with agricultural lands in addition to the presence of domestic spaces suitable for agriculture. The low standard of living has put many people below the poverty line, and the high prices of goods, foodstuffs, etc. ... Among the expenses of daily life, these conditions were reflected in the standard of living of the people, most of whom work in agriculture and all of whom suffer from food insecurity, which drew attention to finding solutions to save many families from the hunger and thirst that spread in the countryside as a result of the bombing that affected the power station and its out of service. Accompanied by the rise in fuel prices, if found on the black market, they are not permanently available in the market. This reality calls for taking advantage of alternative energy sources. There are many farmers who have surface wells that provide a quantity of water and they have limited areas that can be invested, but they do not have the ability to invest and work. It is possible to support them to invest these areas in order to earn from them and secure an income for the people by providing the necessary raw materials for agriculture and providing alternative energy means. The problem of electrical stations being out of service may last an unknown period of time. The solution is embodied in providing alternative energy to farmers who have the capabilities of agricultural work and are in need. To help them secure alternative energy and materials needed for agriculture from the following crops: summer crops / vegetables, legumes... etc. / and winter crops / wheat, barley, lentils, cumin..... etc.
Supporting the target with solar panels to obtain the necessary electricity, while providing a diver with a cable, rope, and hose to obtain the water with which to irrigate his crops. Providing the required winter seeds for planting 40 dunums and summer seeds, such as vegetable seeds for planting 10 dunums, and providing the required fertilizers and pesticides in addition to agricultural wages

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The production of these projects will generate income for 100 targeted poor families consisting of men, women and children, by transporting the products to the markets and selling them at the lowest prices, as they are free from the expenses of agriculture, transportation and raw materials. Material. With these cheap prices, another estimated 5,000 families (women, children, and men) will benefit, as they will buy these items (vegetables, legumes, and grains) at prices commensurate with their income. There will also be more than 100 families (women and men) benefiting from the project through labor that will work on cleaning and harvesting the fields. Livestock owners will also benefit from the project by providing fodder for their livestock. The project targets 75 agricultural projects. Each project covers 40 dunums, which is equivalent to 3,000 dunums of the total targeted area for winter crops such as wheat, barley, and legumes, and summer crops such as vegetables, cotton, and others.The project targets 75 agricultural artesian wells with 5-horsepower pumps, a cable, a high-pressure hose and a rope, and a solar energy system of the Malaysian Ginkgo type with a capacity of 540 watts, with a number of panels of 15 panels and an inverter of 5.5 kilograms, the type of wings with a tableau and cable system. 10 mm and an iron base weighing approximately 180 kg, at a cost estimated at 3,200 US dollars for one project.- 25 houses and farms with areas ranging from 3 to 6 dunums will be targeted using a 3-horsepower submersible and an energy system with the same previous specifications, with a number of panels/7 panels, and a total cost for each well equal to 2,000 US dollars. Note that the production requirements of seeds, fertilizers and agriculture are estimated at $800 for each agricultural project and $280 for each home garden. Families headed by widows, families without a breadwinner, young children in need of income, families headed by elderly people who are unable to work to secure their income, and families will be targeted. People with disabilities and families affected by cancer. The work will be joint and based on consultation with the project owners on the ground. Discussing difficulties and challenges and providing protection for those targeted to achieve a decent and safe life
