Project Summary


Since the start of the ongoing crisis here in Bamenda, there has been a steady raise in the number of displaced families, school drop-outs and crimes in this part of the country.
In an attempt to reduce the number of internally displaced adolescent boys (15 – 24years) who have dropped out of school and are engaging themselves to crime, associating with armed groups, drug abuse, child labour and other social ills, the Carpentry and Joinery Association (CJA) intends to engage 200 of these boys into a nine (9) month full time woodwork training program.
According to the reports from the focused group discussions carried out in five communities (Mulang, Ngomgham 2 , Ngomgham 3, Musang and Ntarinkon) organized by the traditional authorities and supported/facilitated by CJA, 55 out of the 132 boys (15 – 24years) in attendance indicated their interest in learning vocational trades.
Out of these 55 boys 27 of them expressed their interest to do carpentry and modern furniture. However, they complain of the lack of means (training fees, basic tools and sustenance during the process and start-up assistance)
It is therefore in the light of the above that Carpentry and Joinery Association through the project seeks to engage in the training of 25 adolescent boys into a nine months intensive training course to acquire skills in woodwork and to provide them with basic tools and start-up capital at the end of the training to enable them to become self-employed/productive to generate income for themselves and families.

Objective 1: Improve the lives of 200 adolescent boys \through training in the domain of wood works Activities:- CJA will train 200 adolescent boys to acquire wood work skills in the production of house and office furniture. It is worthy to mention that some of these children (apprentices ) will be spread or also placed in workshops of other ex-trainees now operating in different areas around the town. So they will be placed according to the proximity distances from their places of residence. Objective 2: To sustain and keep the apprentices in the workshop up to the end of the course.Activities: a) provision of daily meals to the apprentice during trainingsActivities: b) Provision of training materials (wood and finishing products) alongside basic hand tools (measuring tape, saw, hammer, square and chisels) during the training course.Objective 3: To establish ex-trainees upon graduation.Activities: a) Provision of start- up capital for establishment upon graduation.Activities: b) Follow- up of ex-trainees to advice and ensure that they continue in the trade with and provision for upgrading courses.
