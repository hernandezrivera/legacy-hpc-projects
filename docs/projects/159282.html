Project Summary

The DRC Humanitarian response Plan will target over 40000HHs (24000vulnerable beneficiaries) among the need 3.6 million people that internally displaced and host communities that requires assistance in the greater Somalia/land. The objective is to ensure the essential self-sustenance capacities of the target beneficiaries including IDPs and non-IDPs is improved, by providing access to basic services, food and livelihoods support. This will ensure that the needs of this group will not improve but also reduce their negative coping mechanism
The DRC 4000HHs (24000 beneficiaries) will receive both livelihoods support, conditional and unconditional cash transfers. The injection of cash is meant to increase the purchasing power for target households with limited access to food commodities that are available in the local markets. The provision of cash will be a dignified means of supporting beneficiaries as they allow them to make their own choices in terms of the types and quantities of food to purchase. Under cash for work activities component, community members will be engaged to rehabilitate productive.

Project Activities

Provision of unconditional food vouchers to vulnerable households in urban areas in emergency and crisis in order to increase access to essential food and non-food items through supply of selected quantities of dry food items from selected vendors on a monthly basis. The project will target the most vulnerable persons including boys, girls, men and women. At least(4000HH) 50% of the targeted households will be female headed and will be given at least 100% of MEB. Specific food that is culturally and religiously appropriate to the beneficiaries may be provided on a monthly basis Provision of farming inputs (spade, wheelbarrow, panga, shovels,seeds ,fertilizers) to support irrigation and increase agricultural production for small scale subsistence farmers from asset poor HHs and minority people who lack the necessary collateral to access credit.. This activity will target(10000Ben) 40% female-headed households to reduce negative survival mechanisms including livelihoods that increase their risk of violence and exploitation. Provision of Cash for Work activities to generate sufficient income for households in Emergency and Crisis to access at least 75% of the cost of the MEB while maintaining and rehabilitating communal assets(10000ben). DRC will target 60% female participants. Gender roles and cultural context will be a key consideration, so that women can undertake activities that are consistent with Somali cultural tradition. Provision of unconditional cash transfers to vulnerable households and those at particular risk of exclusion from accessing livelihoods to increase their access to food, and strengthen purchasing power for essential household items and services. These activities will target at least 50% female beneficiari

Mitigation Measures

DRC will work closely with other stakeholders and will ensure the vulnerable, minorities needy women; girls, men and boys are given priorities. Consultations will be held with community leaders, women groups in the focus group discussions (FGD) with women, girls, boys and men to explain the project to them and hear their views about it in relation to issues such as selection criteria and activities appropriate for all groups. Such consultations are expected to give traditionally marginalized and displaced groups, women and girls a sense of ownership and empowerment. The Do No Harm policy will applied to target affected population. All interventions will be implemented so as not to exacerbate existing gender imbalances.DRC will work with sister partner DDG on mine risk Education and UXO so that communities report suspected UXO.DRC will also mobilise the communities on their entitlements, right to receive all the money and why they should not pay tax.DRC will also apply AAP to ensure accountability.DRC is working in the area for long time and has skilled local staff with good knowledge of the area of implementation, DRC have good relation and reputation with the community which increases acceptance.. Dialogue with local authorities and information sharing on project activities will be privileged. DRC safety coordinator ensure that security and context assessments before the start of the project and advises the management accordingly. DRC will also engage with conflict-affected communities and parties (national and international) in order to minimize disproportionate and indiscriminate targeting of civilians and civilian assets vital for survival, including through enhanced delivery of assistance and support to community based self-protection mechanisms. DRC has opened up transparency website for stakeholders and beneficiaries to access and report freely(www.drc.ngo/about -drc/open-drc)

Please provide further explanation

DRC will ensure this Project contributes to durable/ resilience of vulnerable populations against shocks as part of FSC objectives. This will include protecting and conserving natural resources on which lives and livelihoods depend, such as water, soil, land and forest resources. The activities will be directed at repairing and effectively managing small-scale productive infrastructure to mitigate shocks (e.g. river de-silting/rainfed and embankment repair to prevent seasonal floods) using a combination of machinery and human works as appropriate. As well, life skills training activities will be prioritized to increase household income for the vulnerable and food insecure households. Community-based preparedness, surveillance and early warning systems/networks will contribute to prevent damage or losses to crops, livestock and coastal fisheries.AGD will be used during registration to ensure Minorities, Disabilities are targeted. This project will be linked to other DRC long-term project to ensure sustainability
