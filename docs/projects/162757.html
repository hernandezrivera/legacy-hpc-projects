Project Summary

Elementary schools in the area affected by the conflict lack basic facilities such as blackboards, and many children whose partents have died or separated from their families due to conflict cannot bring notebooks for economic reasons. We aim to improve the educational environment by supporting blackboards and school supplies.

1 - Needs

In June 2011, a large-scale conflict broke out in South Kordofan state, which bordered South Sudan, and as a result of intense fighting, many people were died and injured, also hundreds of thousands were displaced from their homes. Although the ceasefire has been in effect since 2016, a number of refugees and internally displaced persons continue to live in evacuation destinations because no peace agreement has been reached. In the area that was the previous battle, there were many schools and hospitals that were severely damaged by shelling and air strikes, and the remaining school buildings were insufficient for children. The lack of equipment such as chairs is also significant. There are many children who died or separated from their families during the conflict, and even if they were able to attend schools, they could not bring school supplies such as notebooks and pen pencils, and many children dropped out in the middle of elementary school Absent.This year, in Sudan, the 30-year dictatorship collapsed, and along with the movement toward democratization, peace negotiations began to resolve domestic disputes. For this, a peace agreement is expected to be concluded. However, even if a peace agreement is concluded, it will take a considerable amount of time to reunite the families that have been separated due to the conflict and rebuild the living infrastructure including the school. In classrooms where the reconstruction of school facilities has not been completed and there is no blackboard, and children who do not have notes write on the sand and practice reading and writing.

2 - Activities / Outputs

In order to improve the educational environment of elementary schools, the following activities 1 to 5 will be implemented. 1. Consultation with related organizations on selection of target schoolsDiscuss with the county to which the elementary school belongs and the Board of Education, and select the five elementary schools that need the most support. 2. Support for school equipmentSupport blackboard and chalk for 8 classes from 1st to 8th grades at 5 target schools. (Blackboard: 2 pieces / classroom x 8 grades x 5 schools = 80 pieces in total) 3. School supplies supportSupporting school supplies such as notebooks, pens, rulers and erasers for the two most demanding schools (400 students) 4). Visiting guidance by educational advisorLocal education advisors visit five schools and give advice on how to operate and teach classes using the blackboard. 5. Collaboration with teachers and PTADiscussing school building repairs and measures to keep children going to school, and creating a system to support school education in the community.About twice a year, a place where teachers and parents gather to discuss school education is provided. [Direct results and effects]5 elementary schools that need the most support are selected after discussions with the Board of Education and governor in charge of the target area. Selected schools will receive blackboards and chalk, which are indispensable for effective class management, in the classrooms of each grade (eighth grade in the Sudan education system). In addition, when an educational advisor visits an elementary school and conducts a small seminar on board writing methods and class management, teachers can conduct effective classes using the blackboard, which helps improve the quality of education. In addition, children who have school supplies will note what they have learned so that they can review at home and promote their understanding. [Indirect results / effects]・ If the classroom environment is prepared and teachers conduct effective lessons, more children will attend elementary school and receive education.-Many children will receive education in an appropriate environment, which will lead them to continue studying in the future.・ Education prevents the loss of future job opportunities and increases the possibility of obtaining jobs other than becoming soldiers involved in conflict. In turn, it contributes to the creation of regional foundations for the realization of peace and stability.
