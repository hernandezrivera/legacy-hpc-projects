Project Summary

The project aims at providing lifesaving equitable as well as dignified, safe and sustainable access to basic WASH services for the vulnerable populations that are affected by drought and conflicts in Bardheere district of Somalia. In submitting proposed activities under the WASH cluster, the project aims at improving access to safe water through the extension of water distribution networks to drought affected settlement as well as host communities in the villages of Geriley, Darussalam, Dalsan, Dadshimbir, Dhomadhere, Jawhar, Wadajir in Bardheere district. in the Gedo region.
The current drought situation is worsening the already dire humanitarian situation in Somalia. According to the Food Security and Nutrition Analysis Unit’s (FSNAU) projection, almost 3.5million Somalis may endure food insecurity or depletion of livelihood assets, indicating a crisis level (IPC Phase 3) or even worse by the end of 2021.
The country is experiencing its third consecutive below-average rainfall season since late 2020, which is worsening the current drought conditions, particularly in the Southern, Central and Northern parts. According to FAO/SWALIM, these areas have received little to no rainfall since June due to delayed October to December 2021 Deyr rains.
The widespread dry conditions have resulted in increased human suffering and livestock deaths, with severe drought impacts reported in Jubaland, SouthWest and Galmudug states (Central regions) and parts of Puntland.
In Gedo region, a severe drought impact was reported especially in Bardhere and surrounding areas. The drought-affected people urgently need water assistance.
Access to safe WASH facilities will also remain an issue of concern, especially for children, women and vulnerable groups, such as persons with disabilities. More than 8.9 million people are affected by poor and deteriorating WASH conditions in Somalia, which have been further exacerbated by the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic affecting people’s health and livelihoods. The WASH Cluster estimates that 3.3 million people are in severe and extreme need of humanitarian WASH assistance and 1.1 million people are in catastrophic need of assistance. Water is also an enabler for agricultural and livestock production systems, for sanitation and hygiene, and helps to prevent conflict and stabilize communities.
Dry conditions will contribute to the likelihood of crop losses and deterioration in pasture and water availability in some areas.
The proposed activities will focus on the rehabilitation of strategic water sources as well as installation of water distribution networks, encouraging safe hygiene practices at the community level through awareness campaigns, and hygiene kits distribution including female specific hygiene items( sanitary materials) where sourcing for these kits shall be done through the use of local vendors.
This project will focus on Wadajir, Geriley, Dadshimbir, Dalsan, Darusalam Jowhar and Dhomadhere villages of Bardhere district of South Central Somalia where access to basic WASH services is very limited due to the servere droughts, locusts invasion, Covid-19 pandemic which continues to disrupt livelihoods and the constant conflicts due to scarcity of water and pasture resources affecting particularly girls, children, women, edlerly and disabled people. The project will focus on IDPs and vulnerable host communities, small scale farmers whilee putting the marginalized populations into consideration.

1. - Projects activities

Cluster Objective 1:Deliver life-saving WASH assistance to reduce acute needs among most vulnerable settlements and communities (including men, women, boys and girls) in a dignified manner.Activities include:Activity 2.2.1: awareness raising on handwashing at critical times, acquisition and use of toilets and mainstream COVID -19 messaging, reaching 10,800 beneficiaries in 7 locations (Geriley, Dadshimbir, Wadajir, Dalsan, Darusalm, Dhomadhere and Johar on a cycle of twice per months for 4 months.Activity 2.1.1: Distribution of 3600 number of hygiene kits in the following villages Geriley, Daresalam, Dadshimbir, Dalsan Dhomadhere, Wadajir and Johar.Activity 2.2.2: The establishment of a school hygiene club in Geriley school with 35 pupils engaged in hygiene promotional activities in their immediate environments.Activity 2.1.2: Distribution of 616 dignity kits to school girls in Geriley , Daresalam, Dadshimbir, Dalsan, Dhomadhere, Wajadir and Johar.Activity 1.1.4: Provision of life saving water through a water subsidy to 2100 Vulnerable persons @15liters per person per day for 30 days through water vouchers in Wadajir.Activity 1.2.1: Awareness-raising on household water treatment to 3600 persons who will receive Pur sachets at household level as part of the household water treatment, the households will also be provided with vouchers to redeem from the local vendors in the local communities in Geriley, Darusalaam & Daadshimbir who depend on the water supply from the three rehabilitated water reservoirs (Berkads). Cluster Objective 2:Continue to restore access to safe and equitable basic WASH services in targeted locations (including non-IDP men, women, boys and girls) in a dignified manner.Activities Include:Activity 1.1.1: Rehabilitation of (1) borehole in Wadajir Village through introduction of a hybrid system (solar and procured generator) and construction (2) water kiosks in Wadajir to serve 2100 IDPs and host communities. Activity 1.1.2: Rehabilitation (de-silting and fencing) of (2) strategic water catchments, (1) in Daresalam village and (1) in Geriley village of each 12000m³ to serve 7500 most vulnerable IDPs and poor host communities). Activity 1.1.3: Rehabilitation (de-silting, plastering, cementing and replacing the roofing) of (2) underground water Reservoirs in (1) Darusalaam, (1) Geriley villages targeting 3600 beneficiaries.Activity 1.2.2: awareness-raising of Water operators on seasonal (before and after the rains) disinfection of run-off water harvesting tanks and provision of initial stock.Activity 1.3.1: Capacity building of water committees for water catchments, storage tanks and boerholes. A total of 66 beneficiaries will be reached through workshops and trainings.Activity 1.1.5: Provision of Fuel and Lubricant support and spares to Wadajir Borehole WASH infrastructure for a period of 30 days at a rate of 15liters per hour for 10 hours per day. Cluster Objective 3:Maintain safety, equity and accessibility of the vulnerable groups (including women, boys, girls, elderly and people with disabilities) when accessing WASH services.Activities Include:Activity 1.3.2: ensure committees are gender-balanced, technical support to assess and establish affordable prices. 45% of women will be included in the community WASH Committees.
