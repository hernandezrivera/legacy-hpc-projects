Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to reach the most vulnerable children and their households in Borno by providing timely integrated (nutrition, protection, water, sanitation and hygiene) assistance to unaccompanied and separated children, malnourished children, and other children with protection risks. Nutrition activities will save the lives of the most vulnerable malnourished children and PLWs, by providing treatment for SAM cases and prevention of malnutrition through protection, promotion and support of IYCF practices. WASH interventions will be implemented I locations where SC is implementing nutrition program to ensure that the population has access to clean, safe water and sanitation facilities and promote essential hygiene messaging to reduce communicable diseases that exacerbate malnutrition
