Project Summary

In 2023, the humanitarian response in the field of education will focus on ensuring the continuity of inclusive and equitable education in emergency situations in order to ensure the right to education for every school-age child. The populations concerned are mainly those affected by natural disasters (and the material destruction they cause) and/or population movements. The World Vision’s response will target 45,536 affected people, including 45,000 children.
The interventions will consist of providing affected children with learning materials, organizing remedials courses for affected children who have had their schooling interrupted due to natural disasters or population movements. 536 teachers will also receive support. The interventions will be carried out, as a priority, in the most affected provinces. These are the provinces of Makamba, Ruyigi, Muyinga, Kirundo, Cankuzo, Rutana, Cibitoke, and Rumonge.
The response will offer packages of interventions tailored to the needs of the identified beneficiaries.
