Needs

Humanitarian actors and agencies lack safe transport to and adequate storage options in key areas of operation across Borno State, Nigeria. Additionally, partners require assistance with facilitating access and the free movement of cargo throughout Borno, and lack access to centralized logistics information.

Activities

The Logistics Sector will continue to offer humanitarian organisations access to common storage in eight locations in 2019: in Dikwa, Damasak, Banki, Bama, Ngala, Rann, Monguno and Maiduguri; while UNHAS will continue to offer a fixed-wing air service between Maiduguri and Abuja, as well as helicopter services in and around Borno State. Additionally, the Logistics Sector will continue to hold regular coordination meetings and produce maps and information products for humanitarian partners. Lastly, the Logistics Sector will continue to provide consolidation and facilitation of movement notifications for cargo and trucks throughout Borno, as well as providing advice and coordination for access related issues.
