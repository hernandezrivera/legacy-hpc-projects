Project Summary

The project works to secure 800 tents supported by the bloc and equipped with the basic needs of the 800 ( 880 fimale, 760 male, 1120 girls, 1240 boys) displaced families in Bennash (Tom) area, Afrin, Azaz, Dabeq
130 of them were completed in Tom camp and provided with the basic needs of the displaced families
A plot of land of 2 hectares will be rented on each site, readyed and prepared for the concrete tent
With an area of 6 m * 4 m , in addition to preparing the tent
with ground levelling and ground preparation
And equip the tent with 5 mattresses and 5 blankets.
In addition,
Train stakeholders in flexibility and capacities in providing quality oriented shelter / non-food skills.
The project was partially secured, and the rest was secured through ongoing campaigns.

4 - Needs

According to HNO The shelter situation in Syria continues to worsen, in terms of the available shelter capacity, the adequacy of shelter conditions, the ability of people to repair or rehabilitate their own damaged house, and in particular the aﬀordability of rent.• Te increase in shelter-related need is particularly acute in more densely populated urban areas including in areas with high levels of returns, with an increase in the number of people in need of shelter assistance of 13.7 percent to 4.7 million people since 2017.• There is significant overcrowding in several areas including in Idleb and north-western Aleppo. Shelter capacity in Idleb has been exhausted with no additional capacity to absorb further arrivals, while access to northwestern Aleppo is partially restricted.According to REACH Syria | Emergency Needs Tracking, Northwest Syria - 3 February 2020 The shelter was the most commonly reported priority need in communities across both Idleb and Aleppo governorates. New or additional tents were reported as a key need in 87% and 92% of assessed communities in Idleb and Aleppo governorates respectively where KIs indicated that shelter was a priority need. An estimated 10,325 households were reportedly squatting in open areas across the assessed communities.Winterization: Heating fuel and winter clothes were the most commonly reported winterization needs across the assessed communities where winterization was indicated as a priority need. In Idleb governorate, KIs reported that heating fuel was a need in all of these communities. • NFI: KIs in over 80% of the communities where NFIs were reported as a priority need, reported that cooking fuel is a key NFI needs.
