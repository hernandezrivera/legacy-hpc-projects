Project Summary

Through this project, UOSSM aims to provide life-saving integrated nutrition services by supporting 10 Rapid Response Teams (RRTs) in Harem and Jesr Alshugor ) sub-districts, targeting PIN Children under five (CU5) and caregivers including pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) and people with disabilities,
These RRTs will be linked to UOSSM’s health facilities in the targeted communities which will receive the referred cases. Each RRT is expected to reach 30 beneficiaries per day, so it is expected to deliver integrated nutrition services to a total of 93600 beneficiaries during project period. 62840 children U5(24336 male.38504 female) and 30760 caregivers including PLWs.
Multisector services (health, nutrition, protection. WASH, GBV) will be delivered by the RRT staff, diseases cases including malnutrition cases and beneficiaries with dangerous signs will be referred to the nearest health facility to be treated in coordination with health and nutrition partners in the same communities, in addition to screening and referring children under five years old and pregnant and lactating women or caregivers with signs of abuse/violence to GBV case management services centers and WGSS women girl’s safe space to receive services according their needs,
For SAM cases without complications, the RRTs will provide screening, treatment and follow up, and referring the complicated cases to the nearest SC to ensure the complete of CMAM program cycle.
UOSSM will receive the nutrition supplies through IP by its quarterly forecast. Supplies requests will be shared with UNICEF, prepared by nutrition program offices and UOSSM will ensure their warehouses' locations are shared and subject to TPM and IP monitoring. In the field supplies will be distributed to the targeted beneficiaries according UNICEF and nutrition cluster protocols.
At the beginning of the project, UOSSM will conduct IYCF & CMAM training according to UNICEF and Nutrition cluster protocols targeting RRT team’s members to enable them to deliver integrated nutrition services. In addition, UOSSM will support the training platform and deliver COVID-19 online training course to RRT’s staff in the field of the COVID-19 pandemic.
UOSSM will intimately coordinate with the Nutrition cluster, health directorates in NW Syria, UNICEF, and other key partners as required.
On the other hand, UOSSM’s M&E team developed the monitoring plan where the M&E team monitors implementation of project activities in the field through field visits, conducting beneficiaries satisfaction, controlling services quality, following up the progress of activities, and achieve indicators monthly and quarterly.
Nutrition programmatic adjustments will be implemented in the context of COVID-19 to mitigate the risk of infection while ensuring continuity of life-saving nutrition services. Furthermore, RRTs teams will be provided with PPE during delivering services.

4 - Needs

After 10 years of crisis, severity of humanitarian needs continues to increase as result of continued hostilities in localized areas, displacement, and erosion of communities’ resilience during over 9 years of crisis.According to HNO 2021, number of PIN to life-saving curative and preventive nutrition interventions is 4.9 million mothers and CU5 in Syria Most of nutrition needs lies in high severity areas, Nutrition vulnerabilities in Syria during 2021 are marked with 0.6 Million children who might lose their future learning and developmental potentials because of chronic malnutrition, over a million mothers who are anemic and/or malnourished. An estimated 141,540 children aged 6-59 months face an elevated risk of morbidity and mortality attributed to acute malnutrition, out of which 37,863 children are under the direct risk of death because of severe acute malnutrition and additional 103,677 moderately malnourished children are at risk of co-morbidities and development of severe acute malnutrition if they are not treated.Coverage of other essential nutrition services for children and women may also be affected as a result of COVID-19 transmission mitigation efforts.According Nutrition surveillance across northwest Syria report (February2021), the prevalence of GAM rate is (Idleb 3.1%, Aleppo 0.7%).The prevalence of moderate malnutrition among PLWs MAM rate (Idleb 16.6%, Aleppo 5.1%)During march 2021, it is clear that GAM rates in Harem, Jesr Alshogur, Jabal Samaan subdistricts were higher than the other areas in NW Syria.During 2020 UOSSM’s technical teams conducted malnutrition screening for PLWs and CU5 and statistics of malnutrition cases were as follow:SAM case CU5 213 MAM cases CU5 570 MAM PLWs 624

5 - Implementation of activities

Integrated nutrition services will be delivered by UOSSM’s teams to beneficiaries through supporting 10 RRTs in Harem and Jeser Al shogure sub districts, in coordination with the nutrition cluster. Each RRT includes 1 nutrition technician who will support nutrition response to children under five and pregnant and lactating women (strengthening early case finding, referral, and treatment of acute malnutrition). We expect that each team will deliver services to 30 beneficiaries through following activities: Screening, referral and follow up of children 6-59, pregnant and lactating women who are malnourished. Providing emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children. Distributing micronutrients for the prevention of anemia and malnutrition among children and pregnant and lactating women.Disseminating messages (IEC/BCC) on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices. Monitoring distribution and use of breast milk substitutes. Supporting health/nutrition education and promotion.Nutrition Supplements distribution, in addition to raising awareness by providing key messages (Hand Washing, Food Processing).Nutrition services will be integrated with protection through Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutrition screening.As for integration with health services, the health staff will do the following:Screening and referral of CU5 with dangers signs and medical problems.Screening and referral of PLWs with medical problems.Referral of medical cases to the nearby health facilities. RRTs will be linked to nearest heath facilities and GBV centers to refer beneficiaries according to their needs, and community health workers will follow up cases until full recovery.
