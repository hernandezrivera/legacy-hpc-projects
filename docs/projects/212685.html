Project Summary


The project aims to promote social cohesion, community engagement, and infrastructural development in 20 camps housing 25000 IDPs - 5000 households. Key initiatives include the formation of Community Mobilization Committees, capacity-building for community leaders, light care and maintenance of camp infrastructure, technical training for skilled workshop owners, fire mitigation and prevention activity, and support for community initiatives led by women and youth.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The formation of Community Mobilization Committees involves selecting members based on stakeholder analysis, with a focus on diverse representation. These committees play a crucial role in facilitating project activities, raising awareness, and overseeing camp management. Monthly coordination meetings between community leaders and camp management will further enhance communication. Capacity-building for community leaders and committee members will cover essential topics such as PSEA awareness, the Code of Conduct, and emergency preparedness, contributing to effective camp management. Light care and maintenance of camp infrastructure will be carried out periodically, involving community committees, GC, and camp management. Regular assessments, transparent procurement processes, and documentation of maintenance activities ensure inclusivity and safety. Establishing fire points and distribution of fire kits (extinguishers) in the targeted camps. Technical training sessions for skilled workshop owners aim to enhance their capacity, aligning skills with project requirements. Fire mitigation and PSEA awareness sessions and the provision of toolkits further ensure responsible and transparent practices in shelter upgrade activities. The project also supports six community initiatives led by women and youth, focusing on tree planting, installing road signs, garbage collection, and health awareness campaigns. Training sessions, collaboration with local authorities, and logistical support contribute to the successful implementation of these initiatives, creating a positive impact on the IDP sites.
