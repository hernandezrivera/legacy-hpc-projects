Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2024

End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

The project's purpose will be achieved by distributing NFI kits and one-off cash for winter assistance to the most vulnerable HHs in NWS and NCS. The intervention will improve the availability of core and seasonal non-food items and enable current and newly displaced HHs and vulnerable communities to meet their immediate non-food needs with dignity and flexibility, and will further support the overall intervention goal by addressing life-saving needs.
SR will provide 25,000 displaced individuals (5,000 HHs) across northwest and north-central Syria with in-kind NFI kits and 50,000 individuals (10,000 HHs) with one-off cash for winter assistance 180$. Through the cash for winter assistance, beneficiaries will have the flexibility to choose the items that meet their basic emergency non-food items needs with dignity from local markets. The harmonized transfer value for the cash for winter to these most vulnerable HHs is determined based on the recent SNFI cluster recommendation where any changes in the design and the exact MPCA value will be reconfirmed based on monthly REACH updates and reflected in REACH price monitoring reports.
The assistance will be provided to the most vulnerable HHs in NWS, it will be distributed according to a process of registration and verification using a scoring system.
The same target will be for 2025.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

SR is committed to providing targeted support to displaced individuals in northwest and North-central Syria, enhancing their resilience against winter conditions through a well-structured assistance program. This initiative will reach 25,000 individuals (5,000 households) with non-food item (NFI) kits and 50,000 individuals (10,000 households) with one-off seasonal cash assistance of $150 for winter needs. This cash assistance is designed for flexibility, allowing beneficiaries to purchase essential items from local markets, adhering to the SNFI cluster recommendations and adjusted based on monthly REACH updates for accuracy. The distribution of NFI kits prioritizes the most vulnerable households in NWS and NCS, utilizing a registration and verification process with a comprehensive scoring system. SR’s approach is grounded in its standard operating procedures and policies, ensuring transparent and equitable project implementation. The SR MEAL team plays a crucial role in overseeing the project stages, from registration to post-distribution monitoring (PDM), ensuring accountability and impact assessment. Protection and gender considerations are integral to the project, with SR undertaking rapid needs assessments and employing gender-sensitive strategies to ensure inclusivity. Female staff will be engaged in distribution and monitoring to facilitate access for all beneficiary groups. SR is dedicated to a transparent communication strategy, informing beneficiaries about the assistance process and ensuring a dignified service delivery. Mitigation strategies address potential protection risks, including sexual exploitation, abuse, and gender-based violence (GBV), with comprehensive training for staff and vendors, awareness-raising among beneficiaries, and strict adherence to a zero-tolerance policy for SEA. Safe and equitable access to services is a priority, with measures in place to ensure inclusivity for marginalized groups. Operational risks, such as market functionality, exchange rate fluctuations, and pandemic-related lockdowns, are managed through regular market assessments, price monitoring, and adherence to health guidelines. Security concerns, including robbery, looting, and conflict-related threats, are mitigated through the use of voucher modalities, strict safety protocols, and contingency planning for inaccessible project locations. SR’s initiative is a holistic response to the urgent needs of displaced populations in northwest Syria, focusing on winter assistance while prioritizing protection, gender sensitivity, and operational risk management to ensure effective and dignified support.
