Project Summary

The AHP Bangladesh Consortium is designed to contribute to the overarching DFAT Bangladesh Rohingya and Host Community Humanitarian Package (2020–2023). Partners will work towards all DFAT package high-level outcomes: basic needs, self-reliance, resilience and reform.
The project will meet the immediate life-saving needs of Rohingya refugees and host community members including persons with disabilities living in Cox’s Bazar district through providing quality education, emergency support on Water Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH) and protection with a specific focus on child protection and Gender-Based Violence (GBV) prevention. The project implementing area will cover 27000 individuals in camp 10 & 13 to meet beneficiary WASH needs with the coordination of clusters, Camp authority, local Government and existing AHP consortium partners (WVI, CBM, CDD and BGS). WV strategy will focus on operations and maintenance of all essential WASH facilities and services. WV will coordinate through camp WASH focal points an Camp-in Charge officials (CiCs) to provide operations and maintenance capacity, ensuring that WASH facilities remain functional and acceptable, and provide access to sanitation facilities to all.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

There are critical funding gaps in water, Sanitation and hygiene - without timely multi-year and flexible funding, WV will not be able to effectively address the needs of the refugee and individuals affected by emergencies.Needs are identified in line with sector based JRP strategic priority. The allocated area as mention below- 1. Regular supply of drinking water 2. Regular maintenance of latrine & bathing cubicle 3. Fecal sludge dispose and treatment 4. Solid waste management 5. Hygiene behaviour change. According to the Unicef WV will improve access to safe water to all Rohingya in camps 10 an 13 in coordination and in accordance with the recommendations from the Government of Bangladesh. Water networks in the targeted areas will be assessed and continually monitored throughout the project implementation. Hygiene promotion approaches will ensure equitable access to safe drinking water, maintain safe water chain and necessary adjustments will be done where needs are identified. Additionally, WV will continue to coordinate with Inter Sector Working Group on solid Waste Management and waste reduction in the camps.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

World Vision is working in 4 blocks in camp 10 & 13 from non UN fund. Since there is no other partners are working in these 4 blocks, there is no duplication of WASH activities. To avoid the overlapping of activities World Vision regularly participate in the WASH & camp coordination meeting on the respective camps and submit monthly report. In addition to that, WV is an active member of WASH sector in Coxs bazar and regularly attend meeting. World Vision is working in this response from 2017 and has plan to continue their operation till 2025 including camp 10 & 13.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

WV will ensure its staff, partners, volunteers and programs do no harm directly or indirectly to the people we support by practicing safeguarding mandates through screening, grooming and by developing a reporting system to avoid manipulation, exploitation and abuse of our beneficiary. The project strategy will fully comply with the global and national safeguarding policy. People & Culture Department (Human Resources Department) holds the responsibility here for safeguarding to coordinate and reporting on Safeguarding incidence following awareness building across programs and beneficiaries.

3.2 - Gender

WVB’s gender responsive programming seeks to move beyond addressing the needs of different gender groups and towards transforming gender relations across families and communities where it works. To ensure inclusive participation in decision-making bodies, all WASH Committees will be a representative body constituting of women, adolescents, and persons with disability.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

At the refugee response, WVB is an active member of the child protection and GBV sub-sectors and is committed to protection mainstreaming across all of its programs. At the global level, WV is a key member of the Protection Mainstreaming Task Team within the Global Protection Cluster and was a key author of the Inter-Agency Minimum Standards for Protection Mainstreaming.

3.4 - Diversity

The project will address the WASH need of everyone in the allocated area of World Vision regardless of their age, gender, religion, ethnicity, disability.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

Guided by the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS), communities will be made aware of their rights and entitlements under the project, and all complaints and community feedback will be gathered on a continuous basis and responded to promptly to ensure accountability to the beneficiaries. Adequate mechanisms, which are accessible to older persons and people with disabilities, shall be put in place to allow beneficiaries to raise their complaints in an accessible, safe and dignified manner.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

The project has some DRR/DRM elements such as repairing and maintenance of WASH facilities and slope stabilization of WASH facility sorounding area.
