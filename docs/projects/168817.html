Project Summary

1. Conduct protection situation monitoring assessments in Covid project locations
2. Conduct Staff capacity building training on GBV prevention and sexual exploitation and abuse
3. conduct Trainings on mitigating protection and Covid-19 related Risks targeting existing community based Protection Networks ,women networks.
4. Increase awareness raising sessions through small group discussions ,using microphone or Radio channels to mitigate Stigma, Social exclusion and protection risks against Vulnerable community members.
5.Distribution of dignity kits to women & girls survivors of GBV or at risk of GBV due to impact of COVID-19 : South Darfur, East Darfur, Kassala : , Total estimated beneficiaries = 40000
