Project Summary

1. Targeted groups:
the project targets:
The rural families who live in southern rural (Tal Al-Damaan and its surroundings) in governate Aleppo
2. Assisting needs
The southern rural suffered from violence that led to almost total displacement of most villages and towns, and the deterioration of the conditions of agriculture and Animals husbandry that people depend on to make a living.
When the security stability returned to these areas, IDPs returned to their original houses which were destroyed or damaged, their agricultural land neglected and damaged because of the careless of them for a period between one and three years with loss of all herds of animals they had been raising as sheeps ,cows and hens.
These reasons led to loss the main livelihoods of people they were able to work in. There were shy attempts at agriculture or animal farming, but they were very weak because of the inability of people to supply raw materials needed for agriculture (mechanisms, tools, seeds, fertilizers ... Etc.), as well as for the farming of animals (new herds and the necessary care they need). which caused a weakness of the ability of families to provide minimum main life necessities, whether necessary to fix the damaged houses or provide the household items which were lost such as furniture, cooking sets, clothing and other main needs. The need to provide livelihoods that enable them to live in the minimum limits and strengthen their existence in their villages has been increased
3. Project idea:
Based on the background of the target groups and their skills in animal raising, so the idea of the project is based on raising poultry for the following reasons:
3.1. Experience of targeted families in farming poultry, Despite the existence of gaps, the project needs to remedy them by raising the capacity of families by establishing workshops about poultry industry.
3.2. The large demand in both rural and city market,(Aleppo city), which has a large population thus larg demand for chicken (meat and eggs), because they have become main food and essential part of all habits of cooking in Aleppo as an alternative for mutton, which has become very expensive in the crisis also the lack of purchasing power of people to buy it.
3.3. Higher economic feasibility than raising sheeps and cows due to:
3.3.1. less purchasing cost means larger number of beneficiaries and more chickens for each family benefit from the project.
3.3.2. higher breeding average, means supplying the required amounts increasingly in the market. This ensures sustainability and continuity of the project.
3.3.3. Operational costs for poultry farming are lower due to the availability of raw materials and possibility of supplying them (household food leftovers from the same families which rise poultry)
3.3.4. The operational costs for poultry farming are easier (do not require care in the grassland, as poultry farming will be in the house and its very close surroundings). This will enhance the empowerment of woman-headed families and the possibility of raising by women and their young children. Also it doesn't require special barns as it in cows and sheeps raising that require relatively large areas.
3.3.5. Providing natural fertilizer for farming from chicken manure which can be used (thus saving its cost) or selling it at good prices (more expensive than livestock manure), This can be considered recycling of waste.
3.4. The southern rural were assessed through visits conducted by competent persons from AL-TA’ALOUF to different regions and focus groups were established with dignitaries of these regions who expressed the extreme need to revive this profession which has rapid feasibility the economic empowerment of the families of these areas
3.5. Presence of competent experts in farming poultry from AL_TA’ALOUF which enhances the ability to manage such projects
3.6. Experience of AL-TA’ALOUF with livelihood projects before and during the crisis.

Needs

The justifications of the project:• The project's activities must stem from the needs and priorities of the community and the beneficiaries. Due to the reality of the community of losing income because the large-scale agricultural and industrial projects that used to be in the area have stopped and the deterioration of irrigation canals which have led to the significant deterioration of agriculture; this has caused two forms of migration:1- The migration of some families to the city in search of livelihood, safety, and job opportunities.2- The migration of young men out of the country for several reasons, including the pursuit of livelihood, and the remaining of a large proportion of females.Raising chicken for obtaining their eggs and meat is very suitable in the targeted area compared to other professions. The beneficiaries of this project: Rural families where usually the females ratio is larger than males through the field reality of the target area.
