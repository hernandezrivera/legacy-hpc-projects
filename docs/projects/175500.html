Project Summary

ISAAC objectives in this proposal is to provide assistance to local communities in the resettlement centres of Pemba and Metuge to reduce health risks that include the provision of clean drinking water and sanitation facilities. Sanitation facilities will include waste collection instruments, disease control approaches including education and awareness raising.
The project will provide assistance to resettlement centres of Pemba and Metuge sheltering about 7500 displaced people
Conditions are being created by governmental organizations such as INGC, health institutions, the local government and non-governmental organizations to address this calamity. This project will fill an existing gap of services in the resettlement centres in the terms discussed above and will be implemented by a joint technical team, local officers and partners.
This activity goes hand in hand with the organization overall objectives in the areas of health, education, emergency, reintegration and development.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Provide safe/potable water, basic sanitation, and improve the knowledge and skills for adopting adequate hygiene education to resettlement centre of Pemba and Metuge through construction of household latrines, benefitting more than 7500 affected people in Pemba and Metuge. The project will provide assistance to resettlement centre of Pemba and Metuge sheltering about 7500 displaced people. This activity goes hand in hand with the organization overall objectives in the areas of health, emergency, reintegration and development.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Provision of safe drinking water: Interventions will include water treatment and trucking and distribution in resettlement Centres. · Providing supplies for water treatment, storage and distribution;· Making available latrines and sanitation services;· Preparing and disseminating information on hygiene, safe water and sanitation; Provision of hygiene education: this supplements the provision of safe water and sanitation facilities and is critical to prevent spread of cholera, activities include: Demonstration of low-cost solutions for sanitation and good hygiene practices such as proper hand washing and hygiene and sanitation promotion activities including theatre performances, radio messages and use of mobile units. Community water committees will be created or re-activated where necessary to ensure sustainable management of their hand-pumps. Handpump mechanic training and household contributions for spare parts are elements of community management to be reinforced. Increased awareness & access to sanitation: Sanitation for 7500 affected people in Pemba and Metuge improved through supply of latrines slabs & construction of families’ latrines, with accompanying hygiene promotion. Emergency latrines will be installed in accommodation centres to allow for private and safe sanitation. Latrine slabs, plastic sheeting and rope will be used to install shared emergency sanitation facilities. Personnel will be mobilized to keep these simple pit latrines clean and disinfected despite heavy use throughout the day and night. Latrines will be designated for women and for men and will be sited close enough to reduce risk during night use Hygiene promotion: 7500 people have increased awareness of safe hygiene & sanitation practices as a result of ongoing hygiene promotion campaign. 20 activists have been recruited from the communities and trained in PHAST as well as preventive and curative methods of most common diseases. These volunteers have been equipped with hygiene and sanitation promotion materials in order to enable them to carry out household visits and promotion gatherings. As a result, PHAST has been implemented in the target communities. Most of the people have understood the message and changes in hygiene behavior has been initiated. Capacity building: Associação ISAAC-Moçambique capacity to provide water & sanitation-related assistance to vulnerable populations is strengthened through appropriate training.
