Project Summary

The proposed project seeks to address the protection concerns of vulnerable Lebanese and migrants who are at-risk of or survivors of gender-based violence. The intervention adopts a comprehensive approach including both prevention and response activities undertaken by a staff of multi-functional protection practitioners. The target areas are Bekaa, Mount Lebanon and the South. The intervention is structured through a combined approach: individually-tailored specialized GBV response services, including case management, focused PSS, cash for protection and emergency cash assistance, which are provided to medium and high risk survivors and at-risk individuals through, in most cases, already established safe spaces at the Community Development Centres (CDCs);while prevention activities such as non-focused PSS, awareness-raising and information-provision services, are implemented through a community-based approach and are provided through mobile outreach and community mobilization. Outreach for these activities will be conducted by community-based volunteers, trained in inclusive targeting approaches and safe-identification and referrals.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

With a proposed staffing of, 9 GBV case workers and 10 community-based volunteers, INTERSOS anticipates that 270 GBV cases will be taken into Case Management over the course of the year; with 85% of those in CM accessing Emergency Cash Assistance and 60% receiving protection cash assistance. Focused PSS sessions (Emotional Support Groups) will be offered by psychologists to 189 individuals in GBV CM expected to access the service. Vulnerable Lebanese will constitute 95% of cases receiving individualised protection services while the remaining 5% will be individuals from the migrant community. GBV prevention services consisting of information and awareness sessions, and non-focused PSS conducted at community level by 6 mobile social workers will target 4,800 individuals, adopting a targeting breakdown of 95% vulnerable Lebanese, 5% migrant community (70% female, 30% male). Information and raising awareness will aim to strengthen capacities of communities to mitigate risks and empower them to claim their rights, including through improved knowledge on how to access services. INTERSOS will also conduct non-focused PSS activities. Sessions specifically aim at supporting the development of skills to deal with the challenges of everyday life and increase awareness of GBV to support the reduction of stigma at community and family levels. The proposed project is integrated within and strengthened by synergies with INTERSOS’ current protection programming.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

INTERSOS will ensure safety for the staff and management of business continuity through its standard operational tool tailored to each area of implementation: the Comprehensive Planning Process (CPP). This dynamic analytical, contingency and risk mitigation tool is complemented in Lebanon by a set of tailored procedures for all the main risks identified (SOPs for cash safety management, movements, Covid-19, centers safety, demonstrations, etc.), while also relying on set-ups that are built on past years lessons learnt (e.g., energy, exchange rate and fuel crisis management) to ensure business continuity through an agile and flexible adaptation to the context.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

This intervention upholds all four Sphere Protection Principles and the 9 Core Humanitarian Standards. INTERSOS strictly adheres to the do no harm imperative and strengthens people’s safety and dignity by adopting a beneficiary-centred approach. AGD Mainstreaming approach will be ensured, taking into consideration communities’ specific needs and risks. Accountability to Affected Populations is ensured in all the project implementation phases, and INTERSOS is committed to the beneficiaries' participation at all stages. To ensure accountability to PoC, complaints and feedback mechanisms will be available through different modalities to receive and respond to beneficiaries’ needs and suggestions. MEAL team will conduct regular monitoring, including field inspections and follow up with the teams, to ensure the activities are conducted properly. Anti-fraud measures and policies in place, as well as CP Safeguarding Policy and PSEA Policy that are signed by all staff, partners and volunteers.
