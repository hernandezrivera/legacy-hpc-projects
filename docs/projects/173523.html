Project Summary

The civil conflict that has been raging in East Darfur State did result in many negative aspects affecting the social fabric, daily routine, livelihood, Health and Education
The repercussion of the devastating conflict didn’t touch only the lives of the fighters, but the affected Population
Education is life-saving because it is the first well-structured social protection mechanism for children at risk and in early recovery, re-establishment of schools brings hope for future and stability which embraces collaborative learning, tolerance and other aspects of life skills and peaceful problem solving among children.
In a rapid survey by the Applicant Organization, in Abu Dangal , Shareya Locality, we found that many students are sitting under trees, on Stones, with no educational materials and lacking any health services, the class rooms are made of thatch and crowded and the school is having more than 8000 children boys and girls. The locality is having 74000 Population figure with returnees numbering 21400
The lack of well equipped schools in the locality, the education system and the absence of a comprehensive education program have far reaching consequence in determining the quality of education in the state and the seriously war-affected areas of the state. The schools in Shiariya Locality have been disrupted due to persistent war. The area dearly lacks a civil administration to look after programmatic planning and implementation. Like most of the other sectors, the education sector has no responsible body/department to monitor the delivery of services. . With the influx number of returnees to the locality, and the emerging new IDP/Returnee villages, the number of student population has significantly increased. The demand for better schools will also understandably increase. The lack of adequate school environment has an enormous impact on the aspiration of providing quality education at this very sensitive stage of childhood. It also limits the ability of the concerned education body to effectively respond to the post-war educational needs of the war affected and returnees’ populations. In this proposal, The National Initiative for Development Organization aims to Construct classrooms, distribute materials, and provide furniture and Recreational materials to improve the educational environment for more than 12000 beneficiaries in the locality
The education sector have identified providing access to basic education for the most vulnerable children in communities affected by conflict, displacement and natural disasters as the sector priority.
8%of adult Population received some formal or informal education.
since 88% of the Interviewed sample of population expressed their unhappiness about the educational Situation .What makes the Educational situation worse in East Darfur is the civil conflict that led to the dispersing of the already weak infrastructure for the locals and the Nomadic Tribes .According to the Ministry of Education in the State, Only 17% of the Children did complete their Basic Education, 8%of adult Population received some formal or informal education. According to the rapid Assessment conducted by the Appealing Agency, the main obstacles facing the Education and learning in the targeted locality could be summarized as follow: Lack of Basic schools infrastructure in villages and towns, Lack of Suitable Class rooms,Lack of Qualified Teachers on formal Education. Lack of Scholastic Materials

1 - Needs

According to the humanitarian dashboard, there are 4 million people who arein need for humanitarian assistance, of which 3,550,000 are living in Darfur (1.7million are in IDPs camps). School age children of these communities are in need for education for better future that brings peaceful life for them and their communities. Across Darfur, 40% of school age children (682,397 girls and boys) are out of school. While addressing the education needs for children living in the camps and rural areas, the sector would encourage partners to gradually shift to early recovery and provides durable solution through building capacity of local communities and government to take responsibility of delivery of basic education services. The project is planned to be implemented in Shiariya Locality in the following location: ABU DANGAL Shiariya Locality is situated in East Darfur State with a population of 74000, 21400 of them are returnees and there is only one school for this number. The co education school is having 8 class rooms made of thatch , No water, no toilets and no scholastic materials. In addition, the locals are contributing dangerously to the environmental and green cover destruction by cutting trees for herds and fuel, thus the issue would be addressed through training of the leaders on environmental issues such as preservation, conservation and replacement. his project is addressing the issue of Providing access to basic education for vulnerable children in communities that are affected by conflict, displacement, and natural disaster, meeting minimum standards for Education This project intends to improve the educational situation with special emphasis on Host communities and Returnees through provision of essential package, upgrading capacity of communities and locals and mainstreaming environmental protection Girls are among the most vulnerable groups. Women represent 47% of the state population, about 35 % of the total labor force in the public and private sectors and 55 % of agricultural labor and are generally regarded as subordinate to their male counterparts, have less voice, less autonomy, fewer opportunities and lowered self-esteem from childhood to old age. Scarce resources are less likely to be directed to their needs. The deprivations and suffering they face are not fully taken into account, and the significant contribution they make to household is undervalued. Although NIDO is one the very few agencies that has been on the field since the eruption of the conflict, working in the very localities that are targeted by this action, we still lack the adequate resources and funding to meet the extreme needs of the IDPs, Returnees , nomads and locals What is worsening the situation is that no immediate funding is at sight and our internal reserves wouldn't cover the proposed activities. Even the Government support in the targeted location which was provided, is far from enough
