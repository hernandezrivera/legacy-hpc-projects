Project Summary

Sindh province is prone to perennial emergencies involving floods resulting from heavy monsoon rains and droughts due to inadequate rain falls. District Sanghar are Badin are one of these flood and drought prone districts of Sindh which often face these disasters due to erratic climate change situation. The natural topography of these districts are such that they have mild slopes and soil condition which does not permit flood water to infiltrate into the ground resulting in standing stagnant water for months causing prolonged emergency situation.
The past Flooding of 2010, 2011, 2016 and 2019 showed that there was increase in number of the spread of water-borne diseases including diarrhea and Typhoid fever. Likewise, standing water caused by flooding also served as breeding grounds for mosquitoes, thus increased vector borne diseases including Malaria. The situation further deteriorates when there is significant population displacement, abnormal overcrowding, and a reduction in disease control activities. Children, women, elderly and disabled people are particularly vulnerable in these circumstances.
Flood prone Taluks of all these district were also declared calamity hit areas in 2009, 2010 , 2016 and 2019. Selected Taluka of these districts were also identified as most vulnerable areas under Rapid Needs Assessment 2020 conducted by UN in backdrop of Rain emergency. These floods create humanitarian crisis causing most of the population in accessing water and sanitation facilities. Access to portable water remains of high concern and is intensified by the water needs of livestock animals. Moreover, due to stagnant rain flood waters, most of the population go in displacement either in camps on road sides flood bund embankments for safe shelters.
The Humanitarian Country Team through allocated Pakistan Humanitarian Pooled Fund (PHPF) through reserved allocation also provided urgent humanitarian assistance for these districts in 2019 in response to rain emergency situation. These district are prone to flood emergencies and require urgent Emergency and rehabilitation Response in WASH sector during floods in monsoon season. Ultra Poor, poor and transitory poor households are always at high risk of vulnerability in the flood prone areas (Taluka) of these districts.
The proposed emergency response focuses on timely addressing the emergency WASH needs of displaced/affected people. SAFWCO proposed action aims to respond to reduce the mortality and morbidity of flood affected populations through the provision of lifesaving water, sanitation and hygiene related interventions in districts of Sanghar, Badin and Umerkot.
Gender will be mainstreamed all throughout the project cycle. SAFWCO will retain capacity to respond to WASH related crises for a maximum of 03 months. Widespread safe hygiene practice education will be provided to women through hygiene promotion session along with sessions on significance of hand washing at Household level with cross cutting awareness on prevention of COVID-19.

1- Project Activities

Provision of safe drinking water through water tankering including installation of community collection points.Water trucking will be provided where water is unavailable including camps and road side shelters where IDPs have taken temporary shelter. Chlorination will be ensured before trucking of water to the identified locations. Water testing officer under project will ensure such Arrangments according to minimum SPHERE standards in emergencies. He/she will be provided with Water testing kit which he will use it daily due water trucking to test water quality primarily for bacteriological contamination and to determine its safety for human consumption. The Water kit especially useful in emergency situations where testing and results can be determined quickly so that remedial action may be taken without delay. It will also be very suitable for water technical team for water testing regimes and standard tests for pH, Turbidity, Free and Total Chlorine, Temperature and Microbiological Quality. After ensuring water’s quality, water trucking will be allowed for transportation to the communities.Construction of Emergency LatrinesConstruction of emergency latrines will be supported aiming at ensuring a ratio of 1:50 persons per latrine. The points for installation of such emergency latrines will be decided mutually both in consultation with district Govt: for installation of emergency latrines at IDP camps and teams would also identify point in the locations alongside roads where displaced/affected communities have taken temporary shelter. With consent from community leaders from IDPs, raised latrines will be constructed with social and culturally appropriated design. SAFWCO project team will ensure following SPHERE Standards for emergency latrines: Availability of safe drinking water through provision of aqua tabs and PUR sachetsChlorination/Purification of water Families will be provided month water treatment solutions such as Aqua tabs for purification of water received from other sources other than water trucking which would be already chlorinated as per health minimum standards. Each family will be provided Aqua tab for one months. 30 sachets will be provided to each of family so that they may prevent from hazards of water with turbidity. Targeted families will be given awareness like (i)-removes or keeps drinking water free from all pathogens– bacteria, virus’, ova, cysts, (ii)-Keeps water stored safely without risks of contamination – containers should be covered and able to dispense water in a sanitary manner (e.g. with tap) Provision of hygiene/ NFIs kits (( mosquito nets, hygiene kits, jerry cans, tarpaulins, and PPEs)2,821 hygiene and NFI kits will distributed to the affected households to support safe water chain (bucket and jerry can) and improved hygiene practices (soaps). The hygiene kits will also contain menstruation hygiene items, mosquito net to prevent from malaria, mosquito repelling gel, mask against covid-19, personal hygiene items, tarpaulins to safeguard them from rain, cold in upcoming winter, scorching sun etc. Awareness raising and hygiene sessions on personal hygiene and precautionary measures to prevent the spread of COVID-19Community mobilisers will be conducting community level sessions with the support of PHP on hygiene promotion using IEC materials, organizing hygiene promotion events, cleaning campaigns, etc. with displaced/affected families both in camps and shelters sought by them along roadside. For wider purpose, PHP will also organize Children hygiene promotion activities. Each PHP will conduct C2C session with children in which refreshment for children will be provided under the project. Moreover to prevent from diseases like diarrhea, cholera, Dengue, Malaria, COVID-19, short awareness messages through direct contact will be ensured by PHP teams.
