Project Summary

This project aiming at enhancing the sustainability and safe access to suffeint quantities of water at institution and community levels, and assist in promote best sanitation practices and hygiene promotion, in three states (Kassala, Blue Nile, North Darfur and South Kordofan),
The project will be in coordination with relevant line misitries and CBOs at local, state and national level.
The key interventions include the followings:
The project will rehabilitate, upgrade and stablish water infrastructures (water yards, mini water yard includes solarisation and hand pumps). In order to improve the safe access of water of the target beneficiaries in the target locations in the four (4) target states. Location of water points will consider women accessibility through selection in consultation with women. IWRM approach will be applied and considered in the implementation of this activities to enhance the resilience and sustainability of access water to affected targeted communities.
Implementation of CLTs, water quality and quantity in participatory manner in coordination with communities and its organizations at locality levels.
Nutrition sensitive WASH interventions will be achieved through prioritizing area with high nutrition rates and providing sufficient quantities of clean water of household’s consumption as well as improving hygiene at community level in order to reduce the associated health hazards and avoid further deterioration in nutritional status especially among U5 children and PLW.
Community based WASH committees (women and men) will be stablished and trained to manage and maintain rehabilitated/established water sources (resourced from user fees) in order to enhance the sustainability. Women will be consulted in site selection and participated in water management committees, this is to ensure that women views and interests are considered and implemented. Water monitoring system include water testing and treatment will be stablished for different water points under the management of water committees to ensure water safety. Water harvesting infrastructure will also be introduced to improve water supply system (e.g. Simple check dam).
Hygiene campaign will be organized to raise the awareness about sanitation and hygiene at communities and institution levels (e.g. school hygiene promotion clubs and teacher training on CHAST). WASH package will also include public and school latrines and hand-washing facilities. These basic service are vital for keeping people healthy and productive.
Improving the availability and sustainability of safe drinking water will reduce the work and risks of violence on women and girls who shoulder bring of water to HHs. Also gender sensitive latrines will be constructed in schools, health facilities and markets with hand washing facilities and solar lanterns for protection and safety. Protection will be ensured through solar lanterns, separation men from women latrines.

1 - Needs

The Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene situation (WASH) is deteriorating due to worsening economic crisis, political instability, non-functional or aging WASH infrastructure, a five-fold increase in operation and maintenance cost of water infrastructures, insufficient revenue collections, poor budget allocation by the Government, and limited external investments. Most affected WASH population groups are IDPs, returnees, and other vulnerable populations battling food insecurity and extreme poverty who cannot afford WASH services and practices. In addition, these population groups are mainly under the threat of WASH-related epidemics, severe acute malnutrition (SAM), the COVID-19 pandemic, and water resource-related conflicts. As per the HNO 2023, 10.9 million people (excluding Refugees) who fall under the above categories need humanitarian WASH. Therefore, WASH Sector's overall objective is to increase access to humanitarian WASH services for 4.8 million most in-need populations with a focus on building their resilience to WASH-related recurrent shocks with also emphasis on community empowerment and engagement of women, girls, and young people as enhanced enablers.From the rapid assessment conducted in the targeted states including Kassala, North Darfur, South Kordofan and Blue Nile, it was found that there is gaps in improved water sources, poor sanitation services, and women and children are the most affected from the shortages of the WASH services infrastructure, this coupled with poor capacities of the communities and their organization in managing their water sources and sanitation services, additionally, the knowledge and better understanding of the sanitation and hygiene and its negative impact on people health among the target communities is very low which negatively impacted the access to WASH services negatively. So accordingly to address these issues rehabilitation of 4 water infrastructure supported by training the WASH community committees on water quality and distribution of disinfectant for those who use surface and unimproved water sources. It is worth mentioning that the deteriorated situation on WASH services has been worsened by the heavy rain and flood that hit most of Sudan states including Kassala, Blue Nile, North Darfur and South Kordofan, where most of the latrines has been destroyed and covered with water and water sources damaged and polluted, so more efforts is needed in this areas in order to recover the people form this shock as well.The project will adopt integrated approach for all WASH component to ensure the sustainability and continuality of community based WASH services at HHs level in the target localities in the 4 states. Additionally, the project will adopt the implementation of IWRM for all water related interventions to ensure the quality and sustainable access of water by all categories
