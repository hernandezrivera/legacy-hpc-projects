Project Summary

This project is geared towards delivering critical shelter and non-food item (NFI) aid to internally displaced persons (IDPs) and households within host communities across targeted sub-districts of Idlib governorates, with a total outreach aimed at assisting 1,100 households, encompassing approximately 5,500 individuals.
The assistance will be executed through the following key activities:
- Rehabilitation of 100 unfinished buildings, with a capacity to accommodate six households per building, thus extending support to a total of 600 IDP households (approximately 3,000 individuals). The estimated cost for renovating each room is $750, totaling $4,500 per building.
- Rehabilitation of damaged houses for 500 households (2,500 individuals) with an average estimated cost of $1000 per household. This aid will target light to moderate damage in shelters, ensuring privacy, protection from adverse weather conditions, and compliance with SPHERE minimum standards.
The project will span over a duration of 12 months, from January 2024 to December 2024, and will encompass sub-districts in Idlib where vulnerable populations are currently accommodated in damaged or incomplete structures, IDP camps, and individual residences. Priority for shelter repair assistance will be extended to IDP and host community households residing in inadequate shelters that fail to meet SPHERE minimum standards. Activities will include repairing or replacing doors and windows, installing partitions, sealing cracks and holes in walls, and addressing WASH facilities repairs.
Moreover, additional measures will be incorporated to cater to the specific needs of households with persons with disabilities (PwDs), such as integrating disability-friendly entrance and toilet facilities.
AHF is committed to ensuring that all project activities adhere to established standards and guidelines, including those outlined by the Sphere Project standards and SNFI Cluster Guidance. Staff will undergo comprehensive training on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and will collaborate closely with the Protection team to address any concerns regarding protection.
