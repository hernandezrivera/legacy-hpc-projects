Project Summary

This project is intended to cover the wide need of WASH among the vulnerable riverine community of Jowhar District of the Middle Shabelle region and Jowhar airport IDPs where the most disadvantaged Somali Bantu ethnic community of the region resides.

Needs

Needs in General For more than two decades, Somalia suffered anarchy which has led to the county becoming a ‘failed country.Years of prolonged conflict, piracy, and inability to deal with the cyclical famine from droughts & floods have left a substantial economic and social cost for the country. Poor access to safe drinking water and lack of adequate sanitation facilities coupled with poor hygienic practices are major threats for the survival and development of children in Somalia.The Food Security and Nutrition Analysis Unit (FSNAU) conducted in all regions of Somalia anthropometric and retrospective mortality surveys from October to December 2014.The survey found that an estimated 202 600 children under the age of five are acutely malnourished, including 38 200 who are severely malnourished and face a high risk of morbidity and death. Specific target Location need This project targets Riverine villages and IDPs in Jowhar Districts of the Middle Shabelle region- Hirshabelle state of Somalia that are principally inhabited by the Somali Bantu ethnic people who are the most disadvantaged people among the regional state resident community and having the same complication of water Hygiene and sanitation aspects as per the general need section of this project justification toward the need of responding the large need of the WASH related complications including the AWD/ Cholera as well as the other WASH related illnesses .

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

Project durable /sustainability The project target locations of the 30 Riverine villages are under control of the Federal Government of Somalia through Hirshabelle State of Somalia Administration as well as the elected local village leaders by respect village community in this regard the environment is safe and stable enough to implement a project like this.On the issue of durable solution or sustained project solution EVSO will focus on the under mentioned issues to be adapted by the local village community of the project target locations such as:# Conduct community engagement meetings prior to the project kick off # Encouraging female participation in planing and implementation discussion making of the project process # Creating of active participation atmosphere of all community members giving the first priority to the female interest and make them feel ownership of the project.# Conduct Joint pre-and post monitoring and evaluation actions with the project stake holders and the concerned government parties, the supporting institution and the implementing partner as well# Conduct hand over ceremony with the presence of the all villagers utilizing the project and publicly inform them that the project is not for One particular part , person or group, but for the whole village community and keeping the smooth running and sustaining is on the whole resident community

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

* Risks facing the target population: Floods , water scarcity, AWD/ Cholera * Mitigation measures: # Establishment of river embankment at the weaker banks of the river shabelle and strengthening the previous breakages and closing the open breakages,# Conduct transporting water/ water trucking ensuring the safety and cleanness of the water # promotion of hygiene and sanitation aspects # water treatment and rehabilitation of the malfunctioned water sources

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Risk on gender and age *The main risks facing the targeted population on gender and age is as under mentioned:# possible abduction during water collection period at far distance or going to have bowel movement at the forest # Discrimination on participation of project discussions making process * Mitigation measures: # Encourage the female participation on project planning, discussion making and project implementation process# Establishing the water collection points or water sources not too far from the village as well as the other sanitation facilities in order to call off going to the forest for having bowel movement and the long distance water collection points with the possible risk of abduction and other harassment actions against female

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The impact of armed conflict *The main risks facing the targeted population on armed conflicts and violence is as under mentioned: 1- Limited movement project implementation barrier, Injury, Death and worry. * Mitigation measures: # Creating of peace and stability atmosphere among the resident community and neighbors using the local community elders and the rest of the resident community members such as the Women and youth groups, religion scholars, chieftains and the other traditional leaders for peace promotion efforts to call off the impact of the armed conflict # liasas with the local authority on sorting out conflicts beyond the limit of the traditional leaders
