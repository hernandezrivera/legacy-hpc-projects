The overall objective of the project is to provide protective and qualitative education to the school age children boys and girls in most deprived locations, camps, gatherings and return areas.
1) Provision of protective and safe temporary learning spaces by using semi-permanent material linked with basic separated sanitation facilities to children, youth and teachers affected by acute emergencies 2) Capacity building and enhancement of Teachers, PTAs and Gos officials on EiE, child centred disaster risk reduction, early childhood development and on psycho social support and lifesaving message

1 - Needs

Hostilities continue to be reported in the Jebel Marra and prospects for the newly displaced remain uncertain. As of 28 February 2017, more than an estimated 92,000 people from Jebel Marra had arrived to Kabkabya and Kabkabya, the vast majority of whom are women and children. The influx of newly displaced children has exerted pressure on already inadequate education services in camps and gatherings and return areas which seriously suffered lack of education services. According to the interagency report in March 2019, it was estimated that 81% of the existing schools had to try and accommodate an additional newly displaced 14,000 student’s boys and girls ( North and Central Darfur some), which in turn affected the quality of education and effective classroom management, leading to an increased risk of drop out in schools due to lack of conducive learning environment for children especially for girls and children with special needs. Based on the feedback from the departments of education( state ministries ) in North, South, Central Darfur and South Kordofan that there is urgent needs to support establishment of TLs and provision of teachers in undeserved localities and camps . An assessment conducted by UPO across schools in Feb 2019 in Darfur shows that the education service coverage in these areas is below the minimum standard for EiE . One classroom is used by 180 pupils (boys and girls on average in Kalma IDPs 130 pupils in Kass locality and 96 in Belil locality. In all the locations, over 100 pupils, both boys and girls use a single latrine and the existing structures are not gender segregated . Although community volunteers have stepped in to assist, the teacher to pupil ratio is also far from the INEE standard of one teacher to 60 learners and many of these volunteers are urgently lack of capacity building, further affecting the quality of education. Overall, more IDPs make school absorption capacity even less and necessitates provision of semi-per classrooms and WASH facilities that meet immediate children boys' and girls' needs. The education sector in East Darfur also faces tremendous challenges due to a huge IDP caseloads and ongoing tribal conflicts, Based on the assessment conducted by UPO in Dec 2016 in close coordination with MoE Adila locality, gap has been reported, about 9,600 children both (5,000 boys and 4,600 girls) are identified to be in need lifesaving education. WASH in schools is a key component of safe learning environment and has been identified as an urgent need. It reported by the community worst indicators in terms of percentage of children in TL spaces without access to water and latrines. Teachers, PTAs also lack of capacity building. inflation and economic challenges impacted Sudanese people’s lives and their purchasing power decreases, many families cannot afford schooling costs. As a result, children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor

2 - Activities / Outputs

Provision of school learning materials and seating for 21,000 (11,000 girls &9000 boys) most affected school age children in camps and gatherings. 2 Establishment of 25 protective Temporary Learning Spaces TLSs targeting 2,000 (1,000 boys& 1,000 girls) newly displaced and conflict affected school age children in camps and gatherings. 3 Installation of 12 WASH facilities in school age children in camps and gatherings 4 Capacity building training for 900 teachers and PTAs members (300 female and 600 male). 5 Provision of psychosocial support sessions, recreational activities, lifesaving messages, and hygienic messages for 9,000 school age children (3,500 girls& 4,500 boys). To enable them mitigate the impact of conflict crisis. Rehabilitation of the classrooms and latrines in the targeted locations for boys and girls . conducting awareness raising on environmental protection
