Project Summary

The proposed project for 2023 will contribute to HRP 2023 through RRM response. Project will focus on providing nonfood item (NFI), emergency shelter kits and technical support for the recently displace people due to insurgency, damages of household by flood and fire outbreak. A multi sectorial rapid assessment will be conducted with in 72 hours from the alert received to identify emergency needs of women, girls, boys and men including disable people. 40,000 individuals will be benefiting from NFI and emergency shelter.

1 - Needs

According to interagency flood assessment report in Yobe state , 41% of people were supported with NFI and there was limited shelter assistance provided (4%). 6% who said they were sleeping out in the open and the 25% whose shelters had been destroyed during the flooding, there is an urgent need to provide shelter assistance to these affected households. The WASH sector may coordinate with the Food Security Sector to explore the possibility of using food or cash-for-work mechanisms to support affected households to rebuild or repair their shelters and other affected social infrastructures in the communities. The continued stay of households in dilapidated shelter conditions raises safety and protection concerns. The state government with the support of the humanitarian partners may need to embark on the task of verifying the shelter conditions of individual households to inform the specific support package required. On security incidences, 37% of the sampled households mentioned that the number of security incidences remained the same after the flooding, but 35% said the number had increased while 27% said it had reduced. Although most of the respondents (60%) mentioned there was no movement restriction as a result of the flooding, 29% said there was a restriction of movement. Also, according to the IDOM DTM tracking matrix, Between 14 and 20 November 2022, a total of 1,469 new arrivals were recorded in locations inAdamawa, Borno and Yobe States.

2 - Activities

Distribution of NFIs and Shelter kits will be done together with hygiene kits proposed under WASH sector to help women, men, boys and girls maintain their dignity during humanitarian crises. Preserving dignity is essential to maintaining self-esteem and confidence, which is important to cope in stressful and potentially over whelming humanitarian situations. Supporting women’s self-esteem and confidence also assists them in providing care and protection to their children. Generic hygiene kits will help people improve cleanliness (with items such as jerry can, soap, sanitary materials) and whilst NFIs and Shelter kits are similar to get access of shade and daily routine kitchen utensils hygiene in hostile emergency. All kits are designed in partnership with RRM operational group with affected communities to ensure the most appropriate items are included, and distribution is carried out in a manner that minimizes the risk of GBV against women and girls. Kits will be distributed in kind considering facts that IDPs and returnees will not be in position to buy, secondly proposed NFI/Shelter and Hygiene Kit’s items will be not available at local market also limited market accessibility in remote areas. Kits help target group by: • Contributing to the psychosocial and physical wellbeing of women and girls, by including items such as intimate wash and pads, and improving mobility of women and girls by providing specific sanitary items. • Contributing to the protection of women and girls, by including items such as flashlights, so that women and girls do not have to walk in the dark. • NFI/Shelter kits will enable HHs to build shade and to cook food for family, and household items for use such as mates for sleeping will be helpful. Activity 01: Distribution of emergency: shelter kits Emergency shelter kits will be distributed to the recently displaced IDPs to ensure their safety and security. Project team will coordinate with shelter and NFI cluster to support the affected population. A total of 2000 shelter kits will be distributed only for targeted most affected HHs in a location where shelter and NFI partners are not able to immediately access the locations. The shelter kits will consist of two tarpaulin sheets, one rope, umbrella nail packs, hammer, and wood. At the same time, AAH will coordinate with shelter cluster partners to support long term solutions for shelter. Activity 02: Distribution of emergency NFI kits: The NFI kits will be distributed to the recently displaced or returnee IDPs to ensure that they have access to basic non-food items. A total of 2000 CCCM/NFI kits will be distributed, and the kit includes three plates, three cups, four cooking pots, one kitchen knife, three tablespoons, three blankets, three sleeping mats, foldable mattress, seven laundry soaps, seven bathing soaps, four reusable pads, two 10l and one 20litres jerry cans, one 2l kettle, two 10litresbuckets, one 10litre basin, two mosquito nets, two serving spoons, one carrying bag and one solar flashlight.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

A program complaint response mechanism (CRM) has been, established that demonstrated to be an effective mechanism for identify issues. The CRM is a formalized channel to register, manage and respond by beneficiaries timely. ACF will leverage lessons learned and resources to set-up a simple and rapid response mechanism to attend to complaints in relation to the program. In addition, efforts to increase accountability will take place via translating the ACF charter into local language for effective service delivery. Capacities of health workers and CVs shall be built on ethics of referral to enable them refer GBV cases to specialized agencies for service delivery. Staff, vendors, stakeholder and all third-party agent are trained on PSEA with emphasis on ACF zero tolerance of SEA and the reporting mechanism in place. The organization has an existing policy of PSEA that in embedded in all contractual document.
