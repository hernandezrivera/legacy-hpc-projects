Project Summary

Based on the need to support ER&L sector and income-generating ways for affected persons and host communities then to promote community participation, Syrian Society for Social Development is planning to support people affected by war to restore their livelihoods through " Supporting Livelihood Restoration of Eastern Rural Aleppo "project which will include the following interventions:
• Supporting 100 farmers with seeds and fertilizers to planting their lands and generating income.
• Supporting 100 affected persons (prioritizing disabled persons and women at risk) from war with sheep with their feeder.
• Supporting 50 farmers with 10 thousand meter of irrigation pipes to help them planting by modern ways and save water which can increase their income and save natural resources.
• Supporting farming association by providing insecticide to 200 farmers which can provide the protection against harming herbs.
• Supporting farming association by providing farming heavy machines (4 tractors and one harvester) to help farmers to complete the agriculture process (500 beneficiary farmers).

Needs

The crisis made Eastern rural Aleppo one of the most vulnerable areas in Aleppo governorate where many villages were destroyed as a result of the hostilities due to their proximity to Quirres Airport, so during the assessment many needs were identified as the following: (Direct):• Most of people lost their livelihood due to the war.• Many farmers lost their ability to plant their lands.• Many caregivers lost leaving the families with no income source.• Limited and week roles of local farming associations. (Indirect):• The need to support the agricultural chain as rural Aleppo is one of the most fertile areas in Syria (this make many plants available in the markets with low price).• Increasing unemployment rate. The following assessment sources were used:• Agriculture directorate.• Agricultural Research Station in Hemaima.• Key persons from local communities.• SSSD teams.(livelihood and outreach)

Implementation of activities

The project activities will be as the following:• Case identification and assessment.• Procuring, inspecting and distributing seeds, fertilizers and irrigation pipes to beneficiaries in coordination with the agriculture directorate and according to the donor procedures.• Procuring, inspecting and distributing sheep and feeder to beneficiaries in coordination with the agriculture directorate and according to the donor procedures.• Supporting agricultural association with farming heavy machines in coordination with the agriculture directorate and according to the donor procedures.• Post distribution monitoring visits will be conducted to measure the impact and PDM report to be submitted.

Protection Risk Analysis

- The protection risks may include:• Child labour may be witnessed during the project activities.• Sexual abuse or harassment acts to teenager workers may occur if working hours were extended after dawn. • Misuse of power might happen by work leader.• Female beneficiaries may be violated through SGBV risks during distribution of materials. - Related mitigation measures should include:• Regular monitoring visits of SSSD staff to identify child labour, SGBV or misuse of power and advise the staff and local partners.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Risks and assumptions may include:1. Difficulty in getting the materials (that will be distributed) from the local market.2. Delay in obtaining the approvals from concerned authorities..3. Change in exchange rate for the US $ against the Syrian pound. - To mitigate the assumed risks above SSSD will:1. 1. Get the distribution materials form another near governorate (if needed).2. 2. Request the approvals from the starting day of project and coordinate with MoSAL and agriculture directorate to accelerate getting the needed approvals.3. 3. Buying the materials as soon as possible (regarding to the materials availability).
