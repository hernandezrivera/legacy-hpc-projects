The project will continue education for 2,300 children with 52% girls including 5% children with disabilities (on the basis of availability) of age group (4-14) in Rohingya. PRANTIC will utilize its existing 23 LCs in Camps Strategically, PRANTIC will strictly follow Edu-sector curriculum for Rohingya children (4-14). PRANTIC in parallel to its existing supervision, monitoring and evaluation system, will engage parents, community people, opinion and traditional leaders to strengthen child protection and assessment of progress from the perspective of children's attitude, behavior and life skills. PRANTIC is expecting core educational materials for (4-14) age groups from UNICEF as it received in the past. Without calculating this, the estimated costs per Rohingya children (4-14) is 100 USD.

Objectives

Objective 1: Access - Strengthen access to equitable learning opportunities for existing 2,300 children in a safe and protective environment for crisis-affected refugee girls and boys aged 4-14 years old. Objective 2: Quality - Provide quality inclusive education to for 2,300 children of age 4-14 years old in Rohingya community, aligned with Education Sector standards, and increase teaching-related professional development opportunities. Objective 3: Community Engagement - Ensure refugee community ownership and active participation and engagement in the education of 1,196 girls and 1104 boys aged 4-14 years old.

Needs

The proposed project will continue providing access to education for existing 2,300 children of age group (4-14) in Rohingya community.

Activities or outputs

The project will conduct a total of 17 activities to achieve the above three objectives of the project. Out of 17 activities, 3 activities shall be implemented to ensure equitable learning opportunities. These are 1.1. Continue provision of Basic education and life skills training for children (4-14) in Rohingya community, 1.2. Provide comprehensive education packages to children (4-14) in Rohingya community, 1.3. Recruit facilitators for Basic education and life skills training for children (4-14) in Rohingya community. Seven activities are planned to achieve Objective-2. To ensure quality inclusive education PRANTIC will provide four types of training and one refreshers course to all teachers and supervisory staff. These are: 2.1 4 Days Psychosocial Support (PSS) Training 2.2 4 Days Foundation Training on Educational Core Materials, 2.3. 2 days Health and Hygiene Training, 2.4. 2 Days DRM Training, and 2.5 Day long monthly refreshers training. In addition, PRANTIC will 2.6.1 Conduct survey of the immediate population living near the LCs to identify children with disabilities that will require extra support to receive education at the Learning Centers, and will 2.6.2 Provide special aid (such as hearing aids, wheelchair, crutches) from the allocated disability budget line to ensure children with disabilities can access education, as the need arises and to be determined on a case by case basis, 2.7 Conduct ongoing attendance monitoring especially to ensure retention of female students. Follow-up with house visits and parent meetings when drop-out occurs, particularly female drop-out, to promote female student retention. To ensure community engagement, foster partnership and gain feedback, PRANTIC will conduct 3.1 Monthly Guardian meeting and 3.2 Monthly LCMC Meeting regularly. To increase social cohesion among students, parents, and community, PRANTIC will organize 3.3 quarterly sports in each LCs and 3.4 quarterly community gathering in the community. 3.5. In addition, PRANTIC will organize FGD in every 6 month with the community to identify changes in attitudes, behavior and life skills among students. 3.6. Establish feedback mechanism for students where they can safely report concerns and complaints directly to field Monitoring Officers who will convey this to the Project Manager, 3.7 Monitoring and Evaluation Officer will coordinate surveys of students monthly to gain feedback on the project and recommend adjustments to the Project Manager accordingly

Key project/performance indicators

[1] 2,300 refugee (including 5% disabled) girls (52%) and boys (48%) aged 4-14 years old having access to equitable learning opportunities, including life skills and resilience programs, in a safe, inclusive and protective environment (Target: 414 girls and 368 boys of age group of (4-5), 782 girls and 736 boys including 115 disable children of age group (6-14)) [2] 2,300 refugee (including 5% disabled) girls and boys aged 4-14 years old receiving adequate education materials, supplies and equipment aligned with Education Sector standards (Target: 2300 Student) [3] 1,150‬ refugee parents and community leaders of age 20-60 years get engaged with 23 LCs to strengthen quality and support smooth running of school
