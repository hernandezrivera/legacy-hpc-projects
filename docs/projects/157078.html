Project Summary

CW aims to contribute to the HRP MYR 2017-2018 objective to protect the lives and fundamental well-being of civilians affected by conflict or natural disasters particularly in West Darfur and South Kordofan States. Emphasis will be on MYHS Outcome 2: Displaced populations, refugees, returnees and host communities meet their basic needs and/or access essential basic services while increasing their self-reliance. CW will implement the proposed intervention in line with the HRP MYR's outcomes and will underpin the protection of civilians from violence and abuse to ensure that further exploitation of the conflict will not affect further the population. CW will work with vulnerable, conflict-affected communities where the lack or low coverage of WASH services, poor sanitation and hygiene practices challenge sustainability. Emphasis will be to provide durable solutions for the targeted communities to be able to increase resilience and long-term sustainability. CW shall target IDPs, Returnees and vulnerable communities in Kulbus, Kreinik and El Geneina in West Darfur State, Elleri and Talodi in South Kordofan State and Abuzabad and Lagawa localities in West Kordofan State. Further, Concern shall also allocate resources and effort on HRP Outcome 3 in order to increased resilience of the communities . A complete package of WASH shall be rolled out to affected and host communities and shall cover water supply, sanitation and hygiene. Concern Worldwide intends to reach a total of 63,420 people though this program.

Needs

West Darfur State: In West Darfur humanitarian context the different elements perpetuate the humanitarian need including limited of livelihood activities in IDP camps and return villages , lack of basic infrastructures and facilities in return villages, disputes over land ownership, tribal conflict in addition to the impact on the current economic crisis and fuel shortage; it may cause more increasing in prices of all commodities particularly items coming from Khartoum and other states due to highest transportation cost. The funding constraints and the government bureaucratic of procedures might also affect sectors timely humanitarian response in case of any emergency. South Kordofan (SK): Concern has been presented in the region through life-saving nutrition and health intervention and during the operation of these activities observed the need of adequate and clean water source for all population. The most critical situation occurs for mothers and their children who visit some of the HFs that run by MoH and Concern with limited water sources. The domestic and HH level hygiene requires much improvement due to lack of information and positive attitude. On 5 June 2011 fighting broke out in Sudan’s South Kordofan state, centering on the Nuba Mountains area. The conflict set the Nuba Mountains section of the SPLM-N against the SAF and its aligned militias. Concern shall meet the needs of women and girls in manner that empowers them by putting a the leadership realm of water management, improving access to income and opportunities, improving learning environment for girls so as to attract, retain and improve achievement and performance for girls. These actions have the effect of removing all forms of vulnerabilities against women and girls.

Activities

- Establish 25 new water sources, upgrade/renovate 8 water sources- Organize 15 new water source committees, reinforce 10 existing water resource committees and set up/revitalize water charging system- Establish two (2) community water source (water yard) powered with both solar and diesel engines -spare part center and establish and train the Community water source spare part center Committee on sustainability of the center.- Establish a Community Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) program and roll out the program to provide HH and community latrines (e.g. market places) for 10 communities- Establish community latrines and handwashing facilities in 15 schools and form a School WASH/health club in each school to sustain the function of the facilities.- Organize community cleaning up campaigns- Organize community malaria vector control campaigns

Impact on environment/natural resources

The interventions shall comply with the Sphere Minimum Standards in Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene Promotion, Guidance Note 1, on the quantity, quality and source requirements and the environmental aspects. For Concern, establishing a water source is more involved than just digging a hole. This process includes a thorough site survey and assessment, installation of the water point, training of villagers in pump repair, and establishment of a water committee to oversee the use and maintenance. Throughout these processes, Concern recognizes the importance of a holistic approach to these water projects, including environmental awareness and impact and was fully equipped with technical resources, including human and material, to minimize hazards and optimize the water quality and amount. Concern specialized engineering and WASH teams shall give appropriate considerations to both the design, digging and materials used making sure adequate supply and quality of water and minimizing local pollution affecting the source and the environment around it. In All cases of unsuccessful drilling, all casings shall be removed backfilling of the holes shall be done to prevent possible contamination of the soil. The community shall be advised to construct latrines at least 30m away from the water source, put up latrines in the lower side of water point (downstream) and the community members also alerted to anticipate the kind of noise during drilling. Environmental project considerations include shall address the need to educate beneficiaries for safe disposal of human excreta creating the first barrier to excreta-related diseases, helping to reduce disease transmission through direct and indirect routes and protecting environment, and building latrines at least 30 meters away from ground water source - aligned with Sphere: Excreta disposal standard 1: Environment Free from Human Faeces. Concern shall provide appropriate gender-sensitive latrines for safe defecation and hygiene awareness as part of essential response for restoring people’s dignity, safety, health and well- being. As part of health education activities, parents and caregivers shall be provided with information about safe disposal of infants’ faeces, and laundering practices for effectively managing safe disposal. As a result of community education, community has become more proactive spreading the word on the need for better waste disposal, isolation of solid non-degradable waste from bio degradable waste. Clean up campaigns shall be followed by cutting of undesirable weeds/grass to stop the disturbances of the soil and natural vegetative cover in order to allow for growth of desirable crops and plants.
