Project Summary

The project will significantly contribute to reduce malnutrition among Children under 5 years, pregnant & lactating mothers and person with chronic disease ensuring comprehensive preventive nutrition services through both one to one and group sessions on IYCF, practical sessions and mass gathering. The project will also focus on improvement of nutritional status of person with chronic diseases.

Needs

"The level of both Acute and Chronic malnutrition in Rohingya refugee camps have been improved significantly since the influx in 2017, but still remain unacceptably high. According to the SMART survey conducted in Oct/Nov 2018 a total of 38.3% and 26.9%of children under the age of five years are stunted in Nayapara Registered Camp (NRC) and the Makeshift Settlements (MS) camps. Similarly, 12.1% and 11.0% of children under the age of five years are wasted in the respective camps. The stunting rates are still way above the WHO thresholds, which is a great concern as these children may never reach their full physical and mental potential. An “Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies” (IYCF-E) monitoring exercise conducted in March/April 2019 indicated a lack of desired behaviour related to breastfeeding and other practices, which aggravates the malnutrition situation. The results showed for example that 50.18% of children under 6 months were not exclusively breastfed and given pre-lacteals foods and complementary feeding (after 6 months) is relatively low at 61% for the range from 6 - 8 months. The Minimum Acceptable Diet (MAD) is only available to 26.51% and Minimum Dietary Diversity (MDD) to 46.18% of the households.And Host community peoples are nutritionally affected in relation to large gathering of Rohingya peoples through different dimensions like price hike of daily essentials, transportation, social clash, insecurity decreased income opportunities etc. and consequently nutrition situation is aggravating. In Ukhiya and Teknaf , Children 6-59 month with Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) respectively 1.2% and 1.2% and Children 6-59 month with Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) respectively 3.6% and 3.9%. And PLWs with Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) respectively 10% and 10%. The World Health Organization (WHO) estimates that 50% of cases of child under-nutrition are due to repeated diarrhoea and intestinal infections caused by poor sanitation and hygiene conditions or lack of safe water. Hand washing with soap is a critical determinant for achieving and maintaining good nutrition. This healthy behavior plays an important part in preventing micronutrient deficiencies, stunting, wasting, and deaths. In this regards, BRAC has designed a project to implement community nutrition interventions (mass gathering, practical sessions on cooking feeding demonstration, personal hygiene and hand washing) as part of preventive nutrition in Host Communities through 6 Unions of Ukhiya and Teknaf Upazilas under Cox’s bazar district. To enhance the nutrition intervention in FDMN 11 camps in a smooth manner, the project is designed to conduct Nutritional assessment of sick child and PLW, IYCF counselling, group session with family members, etc. "

Activities or outputs

"FDMN:1. Nutrition assessment of child and PLW during health facility visit for treatment (Z- score/ MUAC)2. Nutrition counselling for PLW and mother/ caregivers of sick child under 5 years at health facilities to reduce the risk of malnutrition due to sickness3. Referral of sick children and PLW with malnutrition to nutrition service centres.4. Nutrition counselling for patients with chronic diseases (TB, HIV, Hepatitis, Diabetic, HTN, etc.) 5. IYCF counselling of caregivers of 0-23 month children during routine immunization in BRAC Health centres6. IYCF counselling with family members after delivery at health centres7. Ensure early initiation (within 1 hour) of breast feeding for centre based delivery8. Upgradation of breast feeding corner at health facilities9. Joint field visit with GoB officials Host Community:1. Organize Cooking feeding demonstration (Practical session) at community level to strengthen complementary feeding practice. 2. Distribute 250 ml bowl to 5-23 months children to promote complementary feeding practice.3. Counselling and Practical session on personal hygiene and Hand washing at community level4. Mothers gathering focusing world breast feeding week at community level (union parishad)"
