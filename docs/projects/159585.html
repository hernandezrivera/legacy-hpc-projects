1 - Needs

The ongoing armed conflict in Eastern Ukraine has a negative impact on the socio-economic situation and psychophysical condition of the residents of Donetsk region. There is a sharp increase in the incidence of diseases, including such deadly diseases as cancer, tuberculosis, AIDS, hepatitis B and C. Regular preventive examinations are important for the timely diagnosis of a number of diseases. However, due to high unemployment, low social benefits and wages, degradation and cutting of many transport routes, many residents do not have the opportunity to be examined in a timely manner. Some residents due to daily attacks, poverty and remote living, and do not have access to medical facilities with the ability to conduct preventive examinations. According to selective sociological studies of young families in the area of the contact line, 23% of them do not have the financial ability to pay for their own health costs, 7% are forced to save on their own treatment, in which conditions, the prospects that these people will have the means for self - diagnosis and prevention of diseases are extremely small. To identify the disease before the first symptoms-means to give a chance for a complete cure without consequences for the whole body, to prevent the progression of the disease, its transition to the chronic stage and reduce the risk of comorbidities that can negatively affect the performance of a person, and his social activity, and the overall quality of life. In addition, an important advantage of timely diagnosis is the fact that timely treatment of the disease in the early stages involves lower financial costs.Among the vulnerable categories are single pensioners with minimal monthly assistance (150 people, men-61, women-89), families with children with special needs (100 people, men - 43, women - 57), low - income people (100 people, men - 42, women - 58), persons with special needs (150 people, men - 62, women-88). In addition, IDPs living in collective centres, the unemployed, single women raising minor children, children left without parental care, persons without permanent residence or who have lost documents. Thus, in General, the program is planned to cover 1,000 people (men-447, women-653). In order to make available early diagnosis of diseases that pose a serious risk to health and life, it is planned to create a mobile laboratory equipped with a set for blood sampling with subsequent delivery of vials to a stationary laboratory. With the help of a blood test, it is possible to assess the General condition of the body and immunity, as well as to exclude anemia, inflammatory processes; it shows the state of metabolism, identifies the risk group for atherosclerosis, detects metabolic disorders and the work of the relevant internal organs; allows you to exclude infections (HIV, hepatitis B and C); allows you to detect some types of cancer at an early stage.It is planned to equip the medical team with specialists of the following specialties: therapist, nurse.Sample list of recommended standard annual surveys for men and women. General blood test (allows you to assess the overall condition of the body and immunity, as well as to exclude anemia, inflammation, diabetes); - blood test for lipid spectrum and cholesterol (shows the state of metabolism, identifies the risk group for atherosclerosis); - blood test for dangerous infections (HIV, hepatitis B and C) (exclusion of relevant diseases and their carriers); - pressure measurement (detection of the initial stages of hypertension and hypotension); - blood test for individual tumor markers.In a special group it is necessary to allocate the diseases having gender signs with an unfavorable prognosis at untimely begun treatment. First of all, it is breast cancer in women, and in men, such diseases include prostate or testicular cancer. The project will be able to directly reduce morbidity and mortality among the local population in the most isolated and disadvantaged

2 - Activities & outcomes

Within the framework of the project it is planned to apply a set of measures, in particular: Formation, coordination of locations in which the program will be implemented (at least 20 locations located in the 20 km zone from the contact line);Selection of suppliers and partners of the program, conclusion of relevant contracts; Informing the needy population in isolated areas about the implementation of this program;Conducting appropriate field tests and informing the beneficiaries of their results (subject to the specifics of medical secrecy) and providing specific recommendations for treatment and reduction of medical risks, where necessaryOutput 1a) selection of health facilities willing to participate in the programme. Procurement of services, materials and equipment for the implementation of the program;b) selection of settlements for the project;C) informing citizens about the program.Output 2a) drawing up of the correct plan of inspection on the basis of primary data (sex, age, complaints, anamnesis, features of a way of life and working conditions), according to clinical recommendations of who, regulating thus both necessary volume of research, and multiplicity of the carried-out diagnostic researches;b) drawing up a work plan to reach the population (during the year, at least 1,000 people will receive assistance (447 men and 653 women), including persons without permanent residence, single pensioners with minimum monthly assistance, low-income, IDPs, people with special needs and other categories of citizens living in remote isolated areas on the contact lineOutput 3a) acquisition of necessary equipment and materials;b) complete set of the machine with the acquired equipment and materials;C) the establishment of a mobile team of medical specialists and daily visits to survey the population for the presence of the most common diseases in accordance with the coverage plan.d) informing the population of information on the preservation of health and prevention of the most common diseases.Output 4a) maintaining public feedback, a hotline and a contact center to ensure full information and work with the objections of the beneficiaries; b) monitoring during project implementation and post-monitoring;C) evaluation of the implemented project and preparation of the final report.

3 - Indicators & targets

To provide access to medical examination for frequently occurring health and life-threatening diseases for the most vulnerable groups of the population in areas of high military and social risk (in particular, with the complete absence of medical infrastructure and lack of communication with medical centers by public transport). Indicator 1: people who have been given the opportunity to be screened for the most common health and life-threatening diseases. Objective: 1000 Provide access to health information and prevention of the most common diseases for the most vulnerable people. Indicator 1: people who have been provided with access to information on health preservation and prevention of the most common diseases Objective: 1000.Beneficiaries are involved in the project at every stage: at the stage of project development, when this opportunity is provided by the donor, surveys and analysis of the needs of target groups are conducted in accessible ways, project activities are formed based on specific needs and aimed at solving specific problems. At the stage of project implementation, during verification and monitoring with a wide range of different forms of feedback, the need to adjust activities to achieve the goals is identified, at the stage of post-monitoring, satisfaction with the results of the project is determined, for planning further activities.The organization has mechanisms for filing and handling complaints in the form of a box in a prominent place in the office of the organization, a postal form on the organization's website and social networks, a telephone hotline, by mail, by e-mail. Also, there is a possibility to address the needs of the organization through various communication channels for individuals and legal entities, from such appeals formed a database of beneficiaries, numbering at the moment more than 3000 people in need, every month the organization receives more than 200 applications and appeals from beneficiaries.Members of local communities participate in the development of social projects, in which DDC participates with the help of its donors. Mechanisms for involving residents in joint humanitarian activities are gradually introduced, for example through the reception and issuance of second-hand, or the involvement of volunteers. Students are actively involved in the work of the organization for training practice, according to the results of which some of them are employed in the structure. Crowdfunding campaigns aimed at solving local problems are carried out in some cases.
