Project Summary

As September 2021, the Ethiopia National Displacement Report 10, highlighted anincrease to 4,239,636 displaced individuals nationwide due to several factors, such asconfl ict (85%), drought (7%) and seasonal displacement (3%). The MYR reported 14.8m ofpeople in need in Ethiopia, without including Tigray. In 2021, the triggers of humanitarianneeds in Ethiopia were armed confl ict, inter-communal tensions, climatic shocks, desertlocusts and disease outbreaks. In 2022, the humanitarian situation for Ethiopia is stillcharacterized by severe shocks, including the confl ict in north of the country, droughtconditions in southern and southeastern pastoral areas, and persisting infl ation (34.5%inJanuary 2022). According to Fewsnet, the sever poor precipitations during the deyr season 2021 (October to December) in the South and Southeastern areas aggravated by two anterior consecutive poor rainfall seasons in late 2020 and early 2021, have reduced pasture and water availability and resulted in extremely poor livestock conditions across much of the region. The identification of drought-affected areas, carried out by UN has focused on Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), which includes factors such as precipitation and heat. Findings show an estimated 6.8 million people living in areas meeting “dry” criterion. Somali and Oromia Regions are particularly affected and most notably in Doolo (Somali Region) and East Bale (Oromia). This project targets specifically East Hararghe Zone (Oromia) and Liben Zone in Somali Region. The project will deliver Shelter&NFI support to 5783 HHs (899 in Oromia and 4885 in Somali), approximately 31812 individuals (22% male, 23% female, 26% boys, 25% girls, 2% male elderly and 2% female elderly). Out of those, the project will deliver 868 kits for households living with a member with disability as designed by the cluster. Finally, the proposal includes cross-cutting activities related to the Prevention of Sexual Exploitationand Abuse (PSEA) including the training of staff and local stakeholders; the support to referral mechanism to specialized agencies. COOPI will mainstream gender throughout the implementation of the project by ensuring the participation of women and men in the selection committees, the use of gender sensitive selection criteria, and the collection of disaggregated data. AAP will be ensured through the establishment of feedback and complaints mechanisms whose functionality will be also assessed by the PDM survey.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

COOPI adhere to the humanitarian accountability principles and all COOPI Ethiopia staff is trained of the 5 commitments of the Accountability of the Affected Population. COOPI will make sure that beneficiaries know they have a right to equitable and safe assistance, where and how to obtain it.Following COOPI Standard Operating Procedures, to ensure informed decisions COOPI staff will involve partners, communities, local authorities, beneficiaries and especially vulnerable groups in the planning, implementing, monitoring and evaluating of this project. This includes engaging beneficiaries at the design stage by conducting focus group discussions and individual interviews with both men and women of various age groups, and ensure that assessments include the perspective of vulnerable groups, such as elders and people with disabilities. During the action, beneficiaries at all levels will take a leading role in the formation/consolidation of the committees described in the proposal (selection committees). These committees, jointly with local authorities and project staff, will co-lead in the selection of the recipients and in the monitoring of the action.COOPI will provide opportunities for women, people with disabilities and older people to hold positions of responsibility and be represented on community-based committees including as community focal points for assessment teams. An accessible, well understood and confidential mechanism will be set up for receiving suggestions and complaints, with a clear follow-up system on all reports.COOPI’s feedback and complaint mechanism includes information sharing on:• Project objectives, outcomes and outputs, implementing agency and donor.• Selection criteria and mechanisms to report issues for beneficiaries;• Composition of the ES-NFIs kit/entitlements. The validation and agreement with the main stakeholders on the selection of sites and beneficiaries of distributions will take place through meetings with community members, local authorities and cluster representatives.COOPI Project management team will strengthen local capacities, foster local knowledge and community coping strategies, as well as enhance protection mechanisms providing where possible psycho-social services to the community and beneficiaries.COOPI management team will make sure that field teams are sensitized and trained on the importance of making assessments tools age-friendly, by including questions on the needs and capacities of elderly and vulnerable people.Particular attention will be provided on PSEA. COOPI will provide training on its internal policies to the project staff and main stakeholders. The policy includes the concepts related with PSEA, preventive measures, referral mechanisms as well as feedback and complaints mechanisms. Besides that, COOPI will brief the staff also using the Ethiopia PSEA Network Training Package.COOPI will set up a feedback and complain mechanism to allow beneficiaries’ participation, improving monitoring and accountability reporting and promptly adjust the implementation of the project. Mechanisms to report issues will be done through:•Provision to local stakeholders of main contacts for COOPI staff (phone and email),especially for the SMT program staff • Provision of help-desk contact (help@coopi.org) • Establishment of complaints and feedback box and notice panel at the field (depending on level of literacy of beneficiaries)• Participatory selection of focal points at community level, for oral complaints/feedback.COOPI will record all possible complaints and feedbacks. According to the severity of the issue COOPI SMT will respond, in at least 4 days, involving different staff at different level:• Minor complaints/ request of information: social promoters/wash advisor/emergency lead.•Major complaints: Programme Manager/Emergency ResponseLead/CountryAdministrator/Head of Mission/HQ
