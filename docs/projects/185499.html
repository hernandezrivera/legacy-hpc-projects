Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon has affected over 2.2 million people as per OCHA sitrep! Shelter and NFIs needs have remained a major cause for concern to most IDPSs and host communities. In response to the acute Shelter/NFI needs of conflict affected populations of target vulnerable communities of the NW/SW regions of Cameroon, COHESODEC hopes to significantly respond to these emergency needs of vulnerable IDP populations in target communities of Bafut, Bamenda 2, Bali, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye, Kumba 3 & Mbonge sub-divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon in 2022. This targeted response will be improving on the Shelter/NFI situation 6,900 Vulnerable IDPs in dying need of Shelter/NFI’s support.
Also, during the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will ensure the provision of psychosocial support management services in the form of holistic support, community sensitization regarding prevention activities in affected communities. This will mainly target women, men, children and persons living with disabilities!
Moreover, COHESODEC will ensure the provision/assistance of 2000 households with essential NFIs, such as mattress, plastic sheeting, blankets, jerry cans, kitchen sets, solar lamps including market-based interventions for these items, ensuring that 2,000 households are assisted with emergency shelter materials/kits in targeted communities, and that 2000 households are assisted with tents to ameliorate their deplorable situations.
Also, COHESODEC will provide shelter support, through the construction of transitional shelter with construction materials including market-based interventions for these items involving 500 IDP households whose houses have been burnt down or destroyed as a result of the ongoing conflict across affected communities of these regions. These emergency shelters will go a long way to enable IDP households have some accommodation in the midst of this crisis and will help preserve/uphold their safety and dignity.
In addition, COHESODEC will be training 2000 targeted households’ members and communities on construction related skills and capacities in a bit to address their Shelter needs while also assisting 400 IDPs with Cash-for-Rent assistance as a subsidy to ameliorate their disturbing housing situation.
Furthermore, COHESODEC will ensure effective monitoring and evaluation of project activities during the implementation of this project! Project M&E Staff will constantly and regularly monitor and evaluate project activities to ensure implementation is in conformity with project action plan, deadlines and timelines stipulated. The M&E Staff will equally ensure proper accountability to affected populations!
Finally, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will put in place a complaints and feedback mechanism through a 24/7 Call-in line to enable beneficiary communities easily channel in their Complaints and also enable our management teams give timely Feedback to beneficiaries. Accountability to affected populations will as usual remain a key component for COHESODEC during project implementation!

1 - Besoins / Needs

Since the escalation of the ongoing conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon which has affected over 2.2 million people as per OCHA sitrep, Shelter and NFIs needs have remained a major cause for concern to most IDPs and host communities. COHESODEC, during the implementation of this project will ensure the provision of psychosocial support management services in the form of holistic support, community sensitization regarding prevention activities in affected communities. This will mainly target women, men, children and persons living with disabilities!In addressing these vital pressing Shelter/ NFI needs, COHESODEC hopes to conduct a baseline assessment, ensure the collection of data in registration of IDP’s, screen the data to ensure only the most vulnerable benefit, work in collaboration with community and religious leaders to ensure the most vulnerable in their communities are supported, recruit specialist suppliers of the required shelter/NFI needs through the usual bidding process where the best suppliers in terms of quality and cost are selected to supply these items for onward distribution to these target IDP’s in the target vulnerable communities. Some of the Shelter/NFI needs to be purchased include the following: Shelter>>>Polyester ropes, binding ropes (6mm in diameter, 20 m in length), (Hoe+ Handle, Shovel + Handle, Nails)>” through Voucher assistance due to government ban”, plastic Tarpaulins Reinforced, 4x5 M & wooden sticks.Meanwhile NFI’s Needs will include>>>Jerry cans-(non-collapsible- 10l), Impregnated Mosquito bed nets, synthetic sleeping mats(2x2m), mattresses, plastic sheetings, thermal medium blanket-fleeces, bars of soap-(Savon) 250g, , medium size--7 l- plastic basins, buckets, heavy duty plastic, 10l, 14l,15l, Stainless steel/ aluminum cooking pots, Stainless steel plates, Plastic cups, Plastic jars, Stainless steel serving spoons, Stainless steel spoons, Big Baco bag, polypropylene bag, weather proof and shockproof solar lamps / chargers to ensure significant support of these vulnerable IDPs and the host communities. COHESODEC will require the above-mentioned emergency shelter materials and kits; including market-based interventions for these items to adequately respond to these emergency Shelter/NFI needs of the vulnerable IDP populations and host communities. These intervention needs will also include cash-for-rents assistance and transitional shelter construction materials to effectively support these vulnerable IDPs who have lost their homes and properties as a result of the ongoing conflict to ensure their safety, security, protection and a dignified life in these communities.Moreover, COHESODEC will ensure effective monitoring and evaluation of project activities during the implementation of this project!
