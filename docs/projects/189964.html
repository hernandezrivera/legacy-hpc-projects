1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The insurgency perpetrated by armed groups in Cabo Delgado province, in northern Mozambique, has already caused the disbandment of large numbers of civilians who were forced to flee their areas of origin in search of peace and security. Most of these displaced people are living in foster care and resettlement centers with little or no minimum human conditions to live on. They miss everything, from food to basic hygiene services. In these resettlement centers there have been several cases of gender-based violence (GBV) and, as a result, there are thousands of women and girls in need of different types of support, especially psychosocial assistance. Thus, the project aims to intervene in the areas of GBV, case management, psychosocial support, economic empowerment and health, sexual and reproductive rights. It is in this context that we designed this project, with the aim of assisting girls (from 10 to 19 years old), women (from 20 to 65 years old) and people with disabilities to overcome these difficulties.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed Project identifies risks by conducting a risk assessment/analysis in order to anticipate and reduce the effect of harmful outcomes of adverse events and understand whether the risks will be outweighed by the benefits.This project identifies hazards, security gaps, estimates risks and determines next steps to eliminate weaknesses and control risks, classifies and compares the overall impact of risks on the project, as well as enhances communication and decision making processes related to information security. In this way, there is inclusion and empowerment of the different people affected.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project encourages significant access to assistance through self-support initiatives, as an example: life skill activities (handicrafts, agro -processing, carpentry and savings groups).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will identify people with disabilities through direct observation and through information and data acquired by Government partners (INGD, DPMGAS, SDMASS, DPS,...). The programmes developed/promoted in the project will be inclusive for all, i.e. there will be no discrimination of physical and psychological conditions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The environmental adversities that may impact the project are related to climatic problems, as some areas that we propose to develop the project are prone to rainfall, windstorms, high temperatures.
