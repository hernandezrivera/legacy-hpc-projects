Project Summary


This project seeks to sustain and scale up lifesaving GBV prevention and response services in the IDPs in Mogadishu and along Afgooye corridor to prevent and respond to protection risks/concerns experienced by women, men and children affected by droughts especially in Internally Displaced Peoples camps but also in the host populations. Gender based violence survivors through integrated cluster approach. The proposed integrated child protection and gender based violence interventions will address the most urgent and critical protection issues in Dharkenley, Daynile, Kahda district of Mogadishu and Afgooye corridor, Lower Shabelle region focusing primarily on the internally displaced population. SOYDA will ensure to intervene on timely, effective and holistic manner to the protection needs of mainly children and women of the displaced communities in our target areas. The project will address both child right violations and gender based violation through existing inter- agency referral mechanism. SOYDA runs community health services in 10 different locations in Dharkenley, Daynile, Kahda district of Mogadishu and Afgooye corridor, Lower Shabelle region; This project will be integrated in the existing health projects so that we strengthen both protection and health service provision. Mobile CPIE and SGBV activities will be undertaken in response to any new displacement and needs including when there is new displacement or influx. SOYDA has strong and solid outreach protection and health programming and this project will fit into that modality of programming. The project will significantly strengthen and significantly scale –up the on- going case management response services in targeted communities. The key focus will be to scale up prevention and response to separation of children from their families through family tracing and reunification activities and gender based violence survivors. Mainstreaming child protection to other service providers to ensure compassionate, confidential, and competent action for children in emergencies. The project will invest in building the capacity of the both protection and health sectors staff on provision of psychological first aid to the highly psychological distress community members as part of the first line emergency response. The project aims to provide immediate life-saving assistance to vulnerable populations. Beneficiaries will receive comprehensive case management and psycho-social support (PSS), including emergency care, referral to SOYDA medical teams trained in clinical management of rape (CMR), access tailored material assistance, basic emotional support, and follow up on the services. Capacity building of duty bares including formal and informal authorities to strengthen and improve identification. Mainstreaming child protection to other service providers to ensure compassionate, confidential, and competent action for children in emergencies.
SOYDA will closely working with all coordination structures such as Federal Government and States line ministries, protection cluster AORs and working groups in Mogadishu and Baido.

Needs

Despite improvements in the first half of 2018, major humanitarian needs in Somalia persist, particularly among IDP populations, due to residual impacts of drought, ongoing displacement, conflict and marginalization. 5.4 million people are still in need of humanitarian assistance throughout Somalia. The recurrent conflicts and drought in Somalia exacerbated the protection concerns of the most vulnerable sections of the community. In support of the family Women are forced to travel long distance in search of water and fire woods which makes them at high risk of gender based violence. The presence of mines and Explosive remnants of war (ERWs) are a sustained concern, and incidents of Gender-based violence (GBV) continue to be reported, especially in IDP sites. Abuses against civilians, including widespread sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), child recruitment, and limitations on the freedom of movement, forced evictions and displacement remain a pervasive feature of the conflict ict in Somalia. An estimated 3.6 million people are in need of protection services. The majority, 76 per cent, of reported GBV incidents were reported by IDPs. Vulnerable groups such as women- headed households, unaccompanied children, socially marginalized and discriminated communities are at particular risk and face specific protection concerns. From January to June 2018, the Country Taskforce on Monitoring and Reporting (CTMR), documented & verified 2,877 grave violations affecting 2,316 children (1,987 boys; 329 girls), compared to the 1,249 violations affecting 1,562 children (1,264 boys; 298 girls) 2017 reported in the first half of 2017. However, there has been an increase in incidents of child recruitment or use of children by 83% in 2018 compared to 2017. There is a crucial need to scale up, Strengthen and sustain the provision comprehensive case management, psycho social support, interim care and tracing services for these children, supported by the strong community mechanisms.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The following are the main protection risk related to exclusion of the vulnerable population upon displacement during nature adversity includes increased sexual gender based violence towards women and vulnerable girls, separation and lack of foster care among the children hence discrimination,abuse,child labor, rape,marginalization,diseases,ealier marriage by teenage girls and insufficient or/an undocumented vulnerable people needs and poor information sharing, lack of coordination as well as increase sexual exploitation among the girls and women in the IDP camp.SOYDA will be mitigating this through establishments of strong referral pathways during displacement as well as ensure boys, girls and women are given first priority in the emergency intervention, SOYDA will also provide integrated protection component within its intervention to ensure quick supply of prophylaxis to enable reduce the risk related spread of infection as well as liase with local authority in more coordinated version to enable bring the culprit of rape to the books and thus either be referred to the local authority for punishment.On mitigation of separated or unaccompanied children SOYDA will undertake reunification process by using the social media and electronic media as well as radio and TVs and this will ensure wider information sharing hence ease the reunification process for these affected families SOYDA will strictly respect the labor law of the country and will not engage in any activities that are going to facilitates abuse of children right, it will however, conduct wider community campaign and awareness to enable the community respect the children right and stop any form of child labor. It will also strive to provide equal opportunities for both girls and boys as well as marginalized, minorities and disable children hence improve the disparities among these in needs.SOYDA will also ensure proper information sharing and timing of session with target communities.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The following are some of the preventing risk associated with exclusion on gender and age such are, lack of consultation among the adolescent girls in the various camps about their sexual reproduction, safety and dignity hence this will create an avenue for oppressed girl child at the age of adolescent, also such exclusion as special group in decision making process in the camp might hinder proper advocacy and awareness among the disable, minority and marginalized population in the IDPs and host communities hence poor diversity and resilience building.For more effective and efficient mitigation SOYDA shall propose to have equal men, women, boys and girls in both services centered activities as well as employment within the target/operational areas in Afgooye and Mogadishu IDPs, Also on the issue of decision making. We shall route for proper representation of all interested group within the society such as disable, minority and marginalized as well as elderly and single mothers in these targeted IDPs and host communities.SOYDA will be will to cater the needs of both sex and age and shall strive to ensure all are equally served whether on employment opportunity or service provision with the target areas, it will also provide proper coordination mechanism that will enable the protection focal point within the community and project team work in harmoniously for preservation of beneficiary data without any alteration of their information or sharing with an external party to avoid any risk of discrimination among the beneficiaries.In the instances for the distribution of dignity kits, material assistance as well as non-foods items SOYDA will ensure equal share are provided by all the target vulnerable children boys, girls, separated, unaccompanied children & women within the target IDPs and host communities, will also ensure to advocate within the community in consultation with stakeholders within the project area that these children to prevent child soldiering

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The following might be some of the main risk facing the target population that may negatively impact to the beneficiaries that is ; high number of separated or unaccompanied children, high cases of SGBV, earlier marriage, poor beneficiaries data protection, violence and an armed youth within the IDP camps, as well as military and militia cross fire, explosion remnant of war and lack or rather limited information sharing and feedback of compliant mechanism in place to ensure safety and dignity of the vulnerable women,girls,boys and men in the target areas. This is coupled with separation and sexual exploitation as well as undocumented needs of the vulnerable group. To mitigate this SOYDA however, intends to carry out proper community leader’s consultation and involvement of all stakeholders men, women, girls and boys in ensuring children are reunified with their parent as well as provided alternative livelihood or dignity kits and material assistance. To prevent the rampant and attended sexual exploitation among the vulnerable girls and women in the camps, SOYDA will increase social mobilization and awareness aspect within the camps as well as train the community gatekeepers on the proper reporting of these verse hence improve coordination between the community leaders, staff and the local authority for prosecution and cultural solution for the such cases within the camps. SOYDA will ensure girls and women of reproductive age get equal representation and information as well as services that will be rendered in closer coordination and collaboration with government and other humanitarian actors within the camps.To enhance accountability and reduce the risk of violence SOYDA ensure any emerging or displaced communities, IDPs and camp are located in safe and secure location free from military and militia cross fire as well as explosion of remnant war far in the target location.
