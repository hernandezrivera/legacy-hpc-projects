Project Summary

The Project Summary:
1. Improved access to food in general to vulnerable communities by supporting them with monthly food vouchers to prevent further food insecurity and malnutrition
2. Restored community productive assets through the provision of livelihood inputs and developed alternative livelihood strategies for the vulnerable population, realized via the provision of farm inputs, vocational training, temporary job creation, and daily stipends for maintainers of productive assets.
3. The project will target mainly internally displaced households, refugee returnees, and very poor host community households severely affected by droughts and conflicts across the population's groups of IDPs, Rural Pastoralist, Agro-pastoralist in and out of the Wajid District
4. The project is also designed to mainstream protection principles and to promote access, safety, and dignity for all beneficiaries and in particular women and girls from IDP camps and others who have faced an increased risk of SGBV since the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic.
5. It will equally ensure gender mainstreaming approaches remains key to its implementation activities and the staff of the project will disseminate all messages in relation to all type of abuses through protection for sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and don’t harm approaches

1. - Projects activities

Output 1.1: Facilitated food access to 1100hh (6,600 beneficiaries) (480 men headed households and 620 women-headed households), that were affected by the triple crises emergencies like drought, desert locusts, COVID-19 pandemic effects for 3 months for communities in Wajid IDPs and reach areas or villagers. Activity 1.1.1: Provision of unconditional food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil, and pulses) to 700 hh (4200 beneficiaries) in an emergency (300 men headed households and 400 women-headed households) each household receiving unconditional cash transfer worth 71 USD per month in five months length. The total amount of food provision is 60 mt of Maize, 8 mt sugar,6 mt Oil and 9 mt Pulses. Activity 1.1.2: Provision of food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil, and pulses) to 400 hh(2400 beneficiaries) in IDP camps (120 men headed households and 280 women-headed households) each household receiving a voucher worth 71USD per month. The total amount of food provision is 20 mt of Maize, 3 mt sugar,2 mt Oil, and 3 mt Pulses Output 1.2: Increased minimum household basket for 800hh(4800 beneficiaries) (352 men headed households and 448 women households) who are extremely vulnerable through cash relief worth 100 USD for 2 months per year. Activity 1.2.1: Provision of cash relief worth 100 USD per month for 2 months to 800 hh (352 men headed households and 448 women-headed households) in communities in crisis focusing more on the most vulnerable individuals both men and women Output 2.1: Enhanced food production and assets for 800 households (4800 beneficiaries) for 1 year (260 men headed households and 540 women-headed households) through the provision of farm inputs (seeds, farm tools, and land preparation) for one season per year. Activity 2.1.1: 53 men headed households and 68 women-headed households will receive household metal silos and get trained on food storage and the importance of using household metal silos other than the traditional food storage and thus reducing harvest losses Activity 2.1.3: Provision of 4-hours Tillage support for land preparation to 100 destitute poor farmers/IDPs and marginalized groups (3 items; "shovel, Hoe & Rake" to each farmer) Output 2.2: Increased livelihoods of the community by development of community infrastructures by engaging 600 households (3600 beneficiaries) (264 men headed households and 335 women-headed households) in the rehabilitation of water catchments and feeder roads. Activity 2.2.1: Creating temporary job opportunities to 600 households (3600 beneficiaries)(264 men headed households and 337 women-headed households) just before the rainy seasons through cash for work activities and providing 5 USD daily rate to the laborers per 0.5 M3 for canals, 0.3 M3 for water catchments and 5 M2 for feeder roads working for 24 days a month. Output 2.3: Increased knowledge and skills to the vulnerable urban households by providing vocational training and start-up kits to 60 unemployed youths ( 38 young men and 22 young women) Activity 2.3.1.: Provision of vocational training to 45(20 young men and 25 young women) individuals especially the unemployed youth in an urban areas and provide them with materials to start up a business of their own. Provision of vocational training to 30 unemployed young men
