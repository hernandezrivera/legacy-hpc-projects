Project Summary

The Project will provide daily hot meals during 8 months for war survivors in Rural Damascus: Hirjalleh (Idlib's IDP's) and in Eastern Ghouta Harasta/Douma (returnees and vulnerable host community). The targeted beneficiaries are the most vulnerable in the selected region and have been entrusted to us by the Governorate of rural Damascus. The majority of the beneficiaries are women household and people with disabilities. In Hirjalleh the IDP's can be compared to refugees that are kept in a kind of guarded collective shelter where they have no surviving means besides the humanitarian assistance. The Project aims to provide the necessary food security consisting in one cooked hotmeal with 2100kcal x5 (2kgs) per day served with a bread bundle of 1.5kg per family. This provision is important to prevent dangerous coping mechanism from the population in need in seek for basic necessities. MSJM seeks to provide this temporary food security to this population with special care for age segregation needs, and repelling any gender discrimination and surveying the protection status and the resilience resources of the beneficiaries to monitor better our F&A activities.

Needs

1- The situation in Idlib is deteriorating and many families had to flee the ongoing violence. Among those who used the safe passages out of the Province, 540 families headed to Damascus and were sheltered in Hirjalled in the collective shelter. They have no freedom to move and, thus will rely to survive on humanitarian aid. The families are 45% women households with a high fertility rate. Those families have no parents in Damascus nor elsewhere and are obliged to remain in Hirjalleh until the resettlement in Idlib is possible. They are peasants and have no way to gain their daily bread. MSJM is running since march 2018 the Hirjalleh kitchen implemented by ADRA. For the lack of funds we have stopped running this kitchen. The Governorate asked us earnestly to continue cooking for this families who, in the case we do not help them, will face acute shortage of food security. 2- After the end of the violence in Eastern Ghouta many returnees faced precarious means of survival. With their houses destroyed and living inside rubble, or inside the shelter being without any resources to secure food and not having means to cook inside their makeshifts. . The Governor of rural Damascus asked us to secure a daily hotmeal for the most deprived local victims of the conflict. MSJM is also running a popular kitchen in Harasta but for the lack of funds we will soon stop this important activity that is providing food security for population with acute need. MSJM has a steady experience of running emergency popular kitchen that served in its peak 150 000 persons with daily hotmeals. The targeted beneficiaries are selected among the most vulnerable of an immediate post war situation where the means of resilience are equal to zero especially in Duma where there are severe restrictions of mobility.

Implementation of activities

For the implementation of the Project we have to keep in mind that MSJM is already running two kitchens: one in Hirjalleh and one in Harasta. Because of the shortage of funds MSJM will be obliged to stop its food security services. Our implementation is, thus, an ongoing activity shifted from one donor to another. MSJM has gain a local and worldwide reputation for its popular kitchens that we initiated in summer 2016 in rural Aleppo. After the liberation of Deir Ezzor and Eastern Ghouta and with the displacement of population in Afrin MSJM was handling 6 popular kitchen that were providing daily hotmeals for 150000 persons, proving its capacity to implement and run such projects with general satisfaction. Our current Project can be drafted as follow :1- Perform a new registration of the beneficiaries with a particular insistence on showing the age segregation and gender attribution of services2- Distribute to each family a plastic recipient that can contain 3- Distribution of non cash vouchers to exchange them every day against one hotmeal and one bread bundle 4- Hire the necessary staff to run the kitchens and distribute the hotmeals5- Launch a procurement to buy ingredients (dry and seasonal like vegetables and meat) to the best prices/quality and obtain permissions to buy gaz and fuel to the prices of the government6- MoU between the vendor/vendors and MSJM7- Receiving every month the dry ingredients and storing them in our warehouses duly kept by a warehouse keeper8- Receiving everyday the required fresh vegetables and twice a week meat (veal or chicken) and preparing them adequately with all the precautions of hygiene, standard nutrition facts and taste.9- Cooking of the hotmeals under the supervision of the specialized chef10- Distributing daily one family hotmeal (1;5-2kg depending the number of family members) and one bread bundle (1.5 kg) on situ outside of the kitchen for the population that lives in the vicinity or through a door to door on wheel distribution with a team of volunteers trained for the proper way to distribute and handle the population of concern.11- M&E visits to evaluate as indicators : the outputs, the satisfaction of the beneficiaries with special attention to age segregation and gender equality, giving the priority to those that are in more dire need or in particular situation of vulnerability and without any discrimination of any kind. 12- Mid-term narrative and financial report13- Final narrative and financial report considering the fact that there is no more emergency in these location.

Protection Risk Analysis

The situation in Hirjalleh and in Harasta/Duma is different.Hirjalleh is a closed recint where protection issues are more concentrated. Iguality men/women - Women dignity - some cases of domestic violence - rare cases of prostitution - 45 children are fatherless.In Harasta/Duma, there is a big promiscuity in the ruined houses. Many women head of households cannot afford to sustain their family and are victims as well as many children of abuses, manipulation, women and child labor and in many times prostitution. Young age marriages are the normal fate for little girls. We will take all the precautions to give back dignity to women and children.Even if we are not within a protection project our team will be very attentives for any case of abuse, GBV or child labor or manipulation. We will orient those victims to the best accompaniment. In the distribution we will take care that people with disabilities, women, children will be first served. We will not allow crowd irruption but will distribute following the voucher system where the beneficiaries are preregistered. Our team will conform themselves strictly to the MSJM Code of Conduct, being gracious and with a compassionate behaviour.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Crowd irruption - gaz shortage - possible disease of the cooking chief. We will mitigate those risks by : - Organizing the distributions by small districts with well trained volunteers- Put alternative energy to heat the cooking pots (mazout or wood)- Have always at hand a supplier of the chief
