Project Summary

HAGI is a national NGO implementing its activities in the greater lakes states. In line with the HRP 2020 protection cluster, HAGI wishes to support 8000 girls, women, boys and men in Yirol west and East Counties of Lakes states with critical GBV interventions to uplift and strengthen their lives.
This project is envisaged to empower in conflict-affected settings to create safer and healthier communities by addressing harmful norms that promote GBV against women and girls with and endeavor to foster dignity, equality, and non-violence. The project will aim at addressing harmful social norms and focus to promote women’s and girl’s rights as well as provide knowledge and information on available opportunities to poster women participation and to access GBV prevention and response services. The programme will also aim to engage communities in acting to prevent gender-based violence as well as take action in addressing and transforming social norms that perpetuate gender-based violence and remove barriers to women participation and decision making at all levels. This will include developing special measures in addressing social norms that perpetuate child marriages, engaging women and girls in identifying push factors and mitigating measures for child marriages, awareness raising on social norms including those that perpetuate child marriages and creating awareness and engaging families, communities and young people in addressing negative social norms that perpetuate GBV including decisions that influence child marriages and preventing women participation in peace building activities in their communities.
Based on sector objective 1, 2, and 3, this project is envisaged to ensure safe, equitable and dignified access to critical basic services to enable 8000 vulnerable women, men, girls and boys in these hard-to-reach counties of Yirol east and west meet their basic needs in locations of severity levels 3 & 4. This will be accomplished through an integrated approach to address the priority needs of targeted populations by:
• Specialized Psychosocial Support Services, recreational activities, livelihood services will be provided to women, men, girls and boys through trained community frontline workers . Women and girls friendly spaces will also be provided.
• To prevent and mitigate protection risks, enhanced CBP preparedness, resilience and protection risk mitigation, protection awareness raising and prevention messaging will be conducted to 8000 women, men girls and boys on GBV prevention initiatives activities. The following activities will be conducted
a) provision of dignity kits to women and adolescent girls who (disaggregated by age) as part of addressing differential needs and GBV risk mitigation measure
b) Training and supporting women and girls ( disaggregated by age) with Fuel Efficient Stoves as part of GBV risk mitigation measure
c) Training and strengthening 18 community mobilisers on protection risk mitigation, preparedness and early warning
• To enhance protection assessment and monitoring to inform protection and overall humanitarian response, protective accompaniment as requested shall be provided
The project is for the period of 12 months starting in January to Dec 2020
We therefore make an appeal to the GBV cluster to support these critical GBV interventions to uplift and strengthen the lives of 8000 target beneficiaries disegregated as follows 2000 IDPs (1500 female 500 males) and 6000 Host Communities (4000 female 2000 males) Of Age 10 to 60yrs (2000 women, 3500 girls, 800 men and 1700 boys)

1 - Needs

The crisis and conflicts that has been prevailing in south Sudan for the last several years has continued to drastically affect the living conditions of women, men boys and girls. The resultant effect of displacements and conflicts has negatively compromised the community resilience to mitigate their ability on basic need in the Lakes states region have aggravated since the July 2016 conflict and the resulting state in the 4 year period has resulted in many families in dire need of humanitarian support. In lakes State, the commun ities of Adior Payam, Lekakedu Payam, Malek Payam, Pagarau Payam, Tinagau Payam and Yali Payam in Yirol East and , Aduel, Akot, Atiaba, Cueicok, Maleng-agok, Paloch payams in Rumbek East respectively has continued to experience diverse and serious natural and human catastrophic effects. This has weakened their resilience in the mitigation of their livelihoods and coping capacities as many households are displaced, their means of livelihoods destabilized, markets have been disrupted, supply corridors have remained insecure . This resultant effects of these vices has increased effect of GBV and SGBV in these communities. The main drivers of these atrocities and insecurity is mainly by cattle raiding, displacement, economic decline, flooding , epidemic disease outbreaks, fall armyworm, community customs and the general lack of knowledge rights for women men boys and girls.In south Sudan Gender based violence including women discrimination and child marriage is a normal prevalence compounded by harmful traditional practices and social norms. Around 52% of girls in South Sudan marry before the age of 18 and community members describe the practice of marrying girls as young as 13 as common. The ongoing conflict and natural disasters has also worsened the situation due to break down of social structures and poverty that increased practices of GBV and child marriage, a practice related to high demand for cattle by the girl’s family in terms of bride price. Addressing gender-based violence in South Sudan requires measures to improve protective environment, through prevention efforts that include community mobilization and advocacy by community leaders, men, boys, women and girls that promote positive social norms and strengthen community-based prevention mechanisms and structures to mitigate risks of GBV including vulnerabilities related to gender inequality.This project is envisaged to empower people in conflict-affected and other humanitarian settings to create safer and healthier communities by addressing harmful norms that promote GBV against women and girls with and endeavor to foster dignity, equality, and non-violence. The project will aim to promote women’s and girl’s rights as well as provide knowledge and information on available opportunities to poster women participation and to access GBV prevention and response services. The programme will also aim to engage communities in acting to prevent gender-based violence as well as take action in addressing and transforming social norms that perpetuate gender-based violence and remove barriers to women participation and decision making at all levels. This will include developing special measures in addressing social norms that perpetuate child marriages, engaging women and girls in identifying push factors and mitigating measures for child marriages, awareness raising on social norms including those that perpetuate child marriages and creating awareness and engaging families, communities and young people in addressing negative social norms that perpetuate GBV. These will include decisions that influence child marriages and prevent women participation in meaningful activities in their communities.Based on sector objective 1, 2, and 3, this project is envisaged to ensure safe, equitable and dignified access to critical basic services for all gender.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

To address protection of the vulnerable populations especially the women Children and the elderly, a risk analysis is usually conducted to inform and to weigh in the best options of inclusiveness. Targeting of the beneficiaries and site selection are carefully considered so that the support assistance given mitigates risks and ‘Does No Harm’ to these populations. Special messages with warnings addressing forced recruitment of children or gender-based violence are communicated to ensure that these marginalized and vulnerable groups access assistance; with an understanding that services or support provided does not exacerbate tensions between different social & ethnic groupsIn addition, helpdesks shall be established at each point to allow for targeted households and/or other community members to raise complaints, ask questions or provide feedback.Optimization of inclusive participation – as part of the targeting and beneficiary selection process, communities will be consulted through AAP community forums to ensure that all the vulnerable and insecure families are included in the programInformation regarding entitlements will be clearly disseminated to ensure that the whole communities including vulnerable groups of women, men, girls and boys, elderly and disabled have a clear understanding of targeting, and selection criteri on all entitlements and utilization standards. This will iron out dissatisfaction which could arise from simple misunderstandings. All activities will start early in the day and the disabled, female headed households, the elderly and he children will be given special priority so that they can travel home in time before dusk.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

AAP Accountability to Affected Populations ( NEEDS ASSESMENT , MONITORING AND EVALUATION and FEEDBACK )A needs assessment will be carried out before the project starts . All affected populations will be party and will be included in the PMC and awareness will be created and information given to the beneficiaries while at the same time gathering their expectations on what and how and when as they want for the projects. This will comprise of: how they will access their needs,  the type assistance to be provided,  how they prefer to receive assistance Translation of languages into the local dialect shall be undertaken as well as pictures and images with signs to guide especially those who cant read and write. To monitor the project implementation an M&E, Framework and tools and an inbuilt feedback mechanism (help desks for feedback) to evaluate the progress of the project implementation and impacts for all activities will be undertaken . This will be carried out together with the affected populations and in close collaboration with other partners, to gather information on relevance and satisfaction of beneficiaries. The activity will be desegregated by gender, age and disability i.e, women, men, boys and girls, the disabled and the elderly and will evaluate in terms of timeliness, input distribution, relevance of the inputs and services offered and distributed, quality and quantity of services offered and overall organization and modalities of service provision including security and protection issues. The feedback from the findings will then be used to streamline and restructure the project to align with the needs and preferences of the beneficiaries. The set inbuilt mechanism will be confidential, accessible and responsive and will give information to the beneficiaries about the existing complaint and feedback mechanism. The feedback gotten will be used to adapt, align and redesign project assistance to meet the needs of the affected populations
