Project Summary

This intervention aims to promote a holistic approach for the well-being of children, with a special focus on IDP girls and adolescents in the districts of Ancuabe and Metuge. For this purpose, the project incorporates a multisectoral methodology including Protection and WASH components to target and address the most urgent needs of the beneficiaries with a strong approach to Protection in the Education sector. Outcome 1 seeks to increase the resilience and preparedness of identified schools towards disasters and conflictrelated social and emotional effects through PSS and Child Protection training of school administrations, teachers, and students. School administrations and teachers will receive specific training on PSS related to emergencies and crisis preparedness and response. Moreover, previously selected teachers will mentor a group of selected students in becoming ambassadors of youth by implementing PFA and peer-to-peer support for classmates in distress using the "I support my friends" toolkit. Outcome 2 aims to reduce children’s vulnerabilities by improving their access to civil documentation registration processes. This outcome will target children both in and out of school to ensure that they get their civil documentation in order to mitigate protection risks. Toward that aim, the project will closely work with local authorities to organize mobile civil registration units in the communities improving the accessibility rates to schools. Outcome 3 will entail an initial water assessment that will identify two schools in which to drill boreholes and construct a safe water supply infrastructure and hand washing stations. Alongside the construction of the water access points, two Water Committees will receive training and follow-up for the technical maintenance of the infrastructure as well as hygiene promotion activities at schools targeting students and caregivers. The construction of the water points will be strongly determined by a gender and disability inclusive design.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The PFA and PSS training will strengthen the overall community resilience and willprovide the schools, the teachers, and the students with the adequate tools toappropriately support the children and the communities dealing with psychologicalnegative experiences derived from the violence and the conflict-related social andemotional effects. The school training will also help the focal points and the teachersidentify cases that need to be referred to specialized services. The peer-to-peer supportfor friends in distress will facilitate access to vital PSS services for the students. It willact as a horizontal network that will help promote access to these services and raiseawareness among the youth. The project will also mitigate the exclusion ofundocumented children, especially undocumented IDP children, from accessing andreceiving public services such as education by supporting them in obtaining their civildocumentation. Easing access to water will also reduce protection and GBV risks forwomen and girls typically associated with fetching water, such as sexual aggressionsand transactional sex.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project incorporates a robust gender approach in all of its components. Regardingthe PSS and CP components, it will include a specific module on Gender-BasedViolence for teachers to be able to rapidly identify girls and adolescents that might beexperiencing it and provide them with the necessary tools and knowledge that will helpthem adequately refer those cases to the correspondent service provider. The “Isupport my friends” tool kit will also promote equal female participation, encouraginggirls to become network ambassadors through its selection criteria. A concertedpromotion of civil documentation of girls and adolescents will improve their access toessential public services such as education and health. In terms of access to safewater, the overall infrastructure will take into consideration gender accessibility in orderto reduce the risks associated with girls and women fetching the water from afar,including sexual aggressions and transactional sex. The hygiene promotion activitieswill include Menstrual Hygiene Management targeting girls and caregivers

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In order to remove barriers, the Child Protection and PSS training for the teachers andschool administrations will include a module on disability inclusion, in which they willreceive specific orientations on how to deliver tailored PSS support to children withdisabilities. The peer-to-peer PFA initiative will also promote ambassadorship amongchildren with disabilities. All Ambassadors will receive training on inclusiveness andtolerance towards children with different needs and backgrounds and encourage themto reach out and support peers with disabilities. The project will also facilitate theaccess of children with disabilities to civil documentation registration services bycoordinating and supporting mobile civil registration units in the communities.Children with disabilities that might have hindered their possibilities of previouslygetting registered due to long distances and expensive travel costs to reach the serviceproviders will be able to access these civil registration brigades for getting their civildocumentation in place. Also, children with disabilities in the schools' communitieswhere the two water pumps will be constructed will benefit from better and improvedaccess to safe water and hygiene promotion-related activities that will incorporate asolid disability inclusion component.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

For the water component, an initial water assessment will be carried out to evaluatethe viability of drilling boreholes in the different project locations and to adequatelychoose the best location to place them. The Water Committees will be set up in orderto ensure appropriate water management at the community level.
