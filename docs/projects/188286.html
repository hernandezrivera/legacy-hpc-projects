Project Summary

The main goal of the project is to deliver immediate humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable IDP people in/off camps in Azaz and Afrin in Aleppo It is planned to assist nearly 5,000 HH (25000 Men, Women, Boys, Girls) through multi-purpose cash assistance to cover their minimum survival needs. The value of the cash is planned to be $100 per Household (HH) per month for 2 rounds.
The beneficiaries will be selected in close coordination with the local councils and civil authorities with 100 % verification from the MEAL team in the targeted areas based on the vulnerability and eligibility criteria including displacement duration criteria since the newly IDPs are the most vulnerable groups to the shocks. Also, the project will prioritize the women-headed households as well as the households with children, disabilities, pregnant, and lactating women.
The financial service providers in the targeted area will be assessed and contracted for the redemption process. Market assessments will be conducted in the target areas. Considering the price of basic food commodities remains volatile and is subject to speculation, and disruptive events, specifically displacement and COVID-19 have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy and may result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the
delivery of unconditional/unrestricted cash assistance that gives households the agency to prioritize their most immediate needs. Therefore, the activity will help them to increase their food security and their most urgent NFI and WASH needs while reducing the negative coping strategies.
prior to the distribution, a rapid market assessment in terms of availability of the food items, prices, and accessibility will be conducted to make sure the local markets are functioning and to ensure all beneficiaries have good and equal accessibility to the different goods and services.
Accountability to beneficiaries’ is ensured through three main channels: (i) redress and grievance mechanisms; (ii) Post Distribution Monitoring Surveys (PDMS) and focused group discussions (FGDs) as needed; (iii) on-site monitoring activities.

4 - Needs

The main goal of the project is to deliver immediate humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable IDP people in/off camps and members of host communities (HC) in Azaz and Afrin in Aleppo and Maarat masrin in Idleb. It is planned to assist nearly 5000 families through multi-purpose cash assistance to cover their needs. The value of the cash is planned to be $100 per Household (HH) per month for 2 rounds. The vendors in the targeted area will be assessed and contracted for the cash distributions. Considering the price of basic food commodities remains volatile and is subject to speculation, and disruptive events, specifically displacement and COVID-19 have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy and may result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the adoption of a framework of priorities and actions that are focused on livelihoods. Therefore, the activity will help them to increase their food security and meet their most urgent NFI and WASH needs while reducing the negative coping strategies.

5 - Implementation of activities

The proposed intervention is designed to cover the basic food needs of IDPs in/off camps and vulnerable HC members through the distribution of multi-purpose cash assistance worth 100$. Following the necessary approvals obtained from local authorities in A’zaz and Afrin, the project will be introduced to the targeted people and relevant stakeholders such as vendors, camp managements, Local Councils. In camps, the blanket distribution will be applied in order to avoid tension among residents whereas vulnerability criteria will be applied for the Host Community members. Following the vendor assessment, the contracts will be done with them to conduct the cash distributions. Each family will receive their cash on a monthly basis for 2 rounds. Throughout the project, Covid-19 measurements will be applied by all stakeholders. Beneficiaries will be able to submit complaints and suggestions through the complaints boxes and hotline (WhatsApp), and through the M&E field teams
