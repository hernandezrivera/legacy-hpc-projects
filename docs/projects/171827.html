Project Summary

The overall goal of the proposed project is to contribute to improvement in conditions in
IDP sites through strengthening the predictability and effectiveness of service provision
and protection for displaced populations, with the aim to reach durable solutions through
strengthened community self-management and in addition to that the project will
strengthen coordination and information sharing between all stakeholders. Through this
intervention, DRC will ensure that all displaced persons are enabled to partake
meaningfully in the governance structures and management of the sites. This project will
also encourage camp residents to partake community led care and maintenance activities
aimed at improving safety and hygiene in sites, reducing protection and health risks. DRC
will work with the camp management committees to facilitate site maintenance through
cash-for-work while utilising members of the IDP community by enabling them to earn
temporary income. Through this project, DRC will establish or strengthen an inclusive
community governance structures and thematic committees at each site level, the
engagement of these structures in CCCM coordination mechanisms alongside active
humanitarian partners in the respective sites and representatives of relevant government
and local authorities, as well as through continued support for capacity building, mainly
through the delivery of trainings adapted to each type of participation (thematic IDP
committee members, site governance members, local authority representatives). The
proposed project will enhance community communication and feedback mechanisms
through construction of community information centers, employing community outreach
mobile teams, establishing complain feedback mechanisms, installation of information
boards, and conducting awareness campaigns.
This project aims also to support opportunities for displaced people to achieve durable
solutions, the project will contribute through community-based initiatives as well as
support communities in IDP sites towards self-reliance in locations where prospects of
obtaining land rights is achievable. The project aims to reach over 110,000 floods
and conflict displaced women, boys, men and girls in Somalia

Project Activities

The following activities make up the proposed project or in other words, will beundertaken, in line with the objectives of the Somalia CCCM Cluster Strategy: Continueand strengthen coordination role in CCCM Sub-National Cluster structure, in Bosaso,Galkacyo and Beletweyn, with strong quality and timely reporting, utilization ofcoordination tools, consistent meeting facilitation with consistent documentation andactioning of issues and follow-up, and a strong commitment to advocacy. participation indetailed site assessments exercises in 9 Districts in Somalia; conducting three intentionsurveys (in Mogadishu, Baidao and Galkacyo); establishment of 9 mobile sitemanagement teams (one in each of the districts targeted) for identification andengagement with community governance structures, improved monitoring, coordinationand support for site management; establishment/refining and training on CCCM principlesand community engagement for 120 site committees and Establish and strengtheninclusive site-level community governance structures; training on CCCM principles for 150government officials/local authorities; improvement and maintenance of 120 sitesthrough cash for work, formation of site maintenance committees and provision of 2000tool kits; installation of 100 solar lights in areas with high protection risks; distribution of1,500 solar torches; construct and furnish 10 community centers; establishment of 40outreach teams (outreach workers from the IDP communities) for information sharing andsensitization of beneficiaries on services and activities at site level; HLP awarenessraising interventions targeting 80,000 individuals; training of duty bearers on HLP (20committees); development of contingency plans for potential new IDP influxes in each ofthe targeted districts; advocacy with local authorities for public land for displaced people;mass sensitization through radio programming, sign boards, text campaigns in alltargeted districts; sensitize on the use of CCCM Cluster complaints and feedbackmechanism for beneficiaries in targeted communities.

Mitigation Measures

Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement The community leaders andrespective committees will be fully involved in project implementation, monitoring andrepresentation. DRC will ensure that project committees to be inclusive and thatsegments of the communities are well represented at all levels such as age and genderdiversity. DRC has a comprehensive community entry and targeting package thatguides staff. The project was designed in consultation with the targeted communities,and DRC strives to maintain these consultations throughout all phases of the projectcycle. In addition, DRC will implement the proposed project in collaboration with allstakeholders paying special attention to the beneficiaries. The government andrespective clusters will be involved in beneficiary selection, monitoring andimplementation. The project will be initiated with a familiarization meeting held withrelevant authorities and communities in targeted locations, to create awareness onproject objectives, activities, target locations, expected outcomes, scope, and type andselection criteria for beneficiaries. During community mobilization sessions, DRC fieldstaff with sound understanding of the community dynamics will provide detailedinformation on vulnerability-based selection criteria. DRC has a strong framework foradvocacy opportunities, on the issue of evictions from IDP settlements and collectivecenters and promotion of land tenure security for IDPs at the onset of an emergency.DRC will advocate with clan elders, regional and federal governments to provide landwith title deeds to enable this category of Displacement affected communities (DACs)access to durable solutions. With regards to community interactions and feedback,DRC has set number of mechanisms (such as complain feedback mechanism) whichis free of charge, easily accessible to everyone, and well-known throughout thetargeted communities uphold the accountability of the proposed project to theaffected communities. The system will be further optimized and expanded in view ofits use to serve the present CCCM project, and reports will be issued and shared withthe CCCM cluster and within the wider humanitarian community, once per semester.Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age: Protection will bemainstreamed throughout all phases of the project cycle and in the implementation ofall activities. Implementation of activities, including site maintenance and constructionworks, will be done allowing full opportunity for engagement to all site residents. DRCwill pay special attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerableor have difficulty accessing assistance and services through consulting them duringthe designing of the constructions. Separate side meetings will be convened withwomen and girls particularly to address their specific needs. Applied vulnerabilitycriteria for prioritization of assistance will include single women, single-headed HH,and elderly, especially if they live alone. DRC will also reinforce activities that focus onwomen and their role within communities, as an enabler for protection outcomes inconflict affected areas by leveraging women’s roles and leadership in expanding thehumanitarian space, ultimately promoting community resilience, and furtheringprotection of civilians and civilian assets. HLP rights for women will be prioritizedwhenever title deeds or land rent agreements are signed. Where cash for work will beimplemented, activities will be designed to allow participation of men and women, girlsand boys. Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence Despite theimprovements made against insecurity in Somalia and establishment of permanentpolitical institutions, paving the way towards a future with greater peace, this is yet,however, to translate into an improvement of protection needs of the vulnerable peopleas there are continued conflicts combined with forced eviction.
