Project Summary

Project Description: This project aims to support the rehabilitation of a minimum of 15 houses to allow families to return home from their various temporary accommodations. GER3 will conduct assessments to identify the most vulnerable households and work to repair homes that will positively impact the greatest number of people. The primary focus is to repair damaged roofs to dry-in additional living spaces helping to alleviate the current housing shortage on Carriacou. Operations will also include clearing debris from the home and completing critical repairs as needed to allow families to return home. GER3 will train and directly manage a local Cash for Work team of workers to achieve project deliverables as well as integrate local contractors to complete more technical components of the project. GER3 provides sound project management expertise to oversee all works and ensure that the affected homes are built back better to withstand future storms. Project Activities

• Conduct vulnerability and damage assessments of households affected by Hurricane Beryl, identify structures that will have the greatest positive impact once repaired., • Home rehabilitation – repair a minimum of 15 roofs and complete critical repairs to allow families to return home., • Install a minimum of 15 rainwater catchment systems on newly repaired roofs to provide a degree of water security for the immediate household with potential impact in the wider community, • Directly manage cash for work teams to achieve project deliverables and stimulate the local economy while also providing capacity building opportunities to augment the skills of local workers., • Sound project management – Ensure that home repairs are done in accordance with GER3’s philosophy of “building back better”.
