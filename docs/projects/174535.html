Project Summary

The overall goal of the proposed project is "Decreased levels of gender-based violence through greater knowledge and changed attitudes towards gender equality" which will be implemented in Peshawar, Lower Dir & Karak within a period of 12 months.
The project focuses on two-pronged strategies i.e. 1) Engagement with the government to respond to the needs of gender-based violence survivors and 2) Community Mobilization and Capacity Building to find local solutions to combat GBV. The following strategies will be used to implement the project:
1. Acquiring Support from Provincial / District Government / Departments for the GBV initiative-Coordination: KK has close coordination with Provincial Commission on Status of Women (PCSW) as well as with other provincial and national stakeholders. It has a dynamic management team that has engaged with the Government, supported it in various women related initiatives and hence has the credibility in the circle. KK plans to bank on these connections and would establish a regular (monthly/ quarterly) coordination mechanism to obtain their consent and supported actions towards the objectives of the project.
2. Harnessing Support in patriarchal society by engaging Men as Agents of Change: The project is about understanding, planning, using and changing the power relations towards improved gender relations and indeed, towards reducing GBV issues. However, this seems less plausible without engaging positively with men and within them, the various stakeholders with power. KK plans to engage with men particularly those having influence on the society including but not limited to religious leader, tribal leaders, civil activists, social media activists, pro-development government officials and others. KK strategizes to engage with them, enhance their knowledge and skills towards GBV issues based on traditional and religious teachings as well as constitutional provisions and also use their frame of influence towards reducing resistance to discussion on women rights as well as GBV issues.
3. Capacity Building and Ensuring its functional use: KK understands that the various actors engaged within the project particularly youth as well as those who will be engaging with GBV issues for the first time, need to have enhanced knowledge and skills on women rights, GBV, networking and referrals, functioning community platforms, positive activism as well as (social) ripple effect. This will be essential for the success of the project in the selected districts so that the knowledge and skills create the ripples beyond the projects immediate frame of action.
4. Dynamic Communication and Engaging Youth: Communication have proved a critical factor for the success of projects particularly those rooted in changing behaviours towards social issues. For this purpose, KK plans to engage a dynamic communication plan and a campaign at district and provincial level on social media (FB, TikTok, and other such platforms) to engage with youth (boys and girls) as well as men and women. The engagement will ensure that project messages reach the masses in an effective manner and that local level support is harnessed for the project at community level. KK plans to undertake visits of social media activists so that social media influencers can provide powerful messages at the local level. In addition, similar media campaign will focus religious and traditional influencers at local level as well as direct communication with them, to use their influence and enhance the rights of women as well as on GBV issues in light of traditions and religious teachings.
5. Regular Monitoring, Evaluation and Establishing System for Smoke Signals
