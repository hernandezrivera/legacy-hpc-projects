Project Summary

The project aims at addressing food insecurity and building resilience of the drought affected communities in lower Juba, Somalia through sustainable livelihood interventions.
The project targets atotal of 3,900 vulnerable households, particularly IDP's, returnees and vulnerable host community living in Afmadhow, Badhadhe and Kismayo districts. The proposed project seeks to meet 3 key objectices:
a) improve the immediate food security neeeds of the households affected by drought and conflict emergencies through the provision of cash for work and cash relief activities.
b) protect and restore livelihood assets of the drought and conflict affected households through restocking.
c) Support small scale agriculture through the provision of agricultural inputs and training.
Assistance will help affected households to meet their immediate and long term food and cash needs, as well as provide appropriate capacity building to the target communities.
Proposed cash for work (CFW) and cash relief provides immediate cash relief while setting the base for medium term recovery, It does this by debt payment, rebuilding both livelihoods and infrastructure. The project area has available essential goods and services but the population do not have income to purchase the available goods and services due to the recurring drought and conflict specially the IDP's, returnees and vulnerable host community. Cash relief and cash for Work ensure that vulnerable people remain in their communities, thereby avoiding more displacement and keeping social ties intact. When combined with other interventions, cash relief and CFW helps build the roots of stability that will prevent crises from recurring. According to study by FSNAU Somalia in 2011 indicates that most cash transfer programs in Somalia did not lead to noticeable price inflation. In addition, CFW is not expected to interfere with food and essential commodity markets. The most immediate benefits are freedom to choose the foods and basic necessities they prefer and working on infrastructure projects that rebuild community assets and set the ground for medium term recovery. Livelihood opportunities are created, and people’s skills are reinforced. WASDA will monitor the market on weekly basis to identify any changes and provide regular market information to traders and others involved in the market system so that they are able to respond appropriately.

Project Activities

Activities 1.1: provide conditional cash transfer through cash for work to 2,000 drought and conflicted affected house holds in Badhadhe, Afmadhow and Kismayo districts. The cash for work will be used to construct/rehabilitate key community owned infrastructure such as water pans, clearance of access roads, small irrigation canals, shallow wells among others.Activities 1.2: Provide unconditional cash transfer to 1,200 drought and conflict affected households in Badhadhe, Afmadhow and Kismayo districts. Cash assistance will be provided to drought and conflicted affected households facing urgent needs during the period of emergency. The value of individual unconditional cash grants is designed to enable targeted households to cover essential monthly needs, focusing on food and non-food items. The target beneficiaries will be vulnerable households who are not able to participate in cash for work activities such as people living with disabilities, elderly, house hold headed by women or children. Activities 2.1: provide support to 500 farming and Agro-pastoral households practicing micro-irrigation in Kismayo, Afmadhow and Badhdhe districts with farm inputs (seeds, fertilizers, farm tools and irrigation equipment) that will improve their production.Activities 2.2: provide training to 500 farming and Agro-pastoral households practicing micro-irrigation in Kismayo, Afmadhow and Badhdhe districts on improved agro-nomical practices (best agricultural practices) and farming as business. Activities 3: Provide livestock support to 200 vulnerable pastoral dropout households through restocking in Badhadhe, Afmadhow and Kismayo districts of lower Juba, Somalia. Through this vulnerable Households will be provided with productive livestock (10 goats) to build their livestock asset.
