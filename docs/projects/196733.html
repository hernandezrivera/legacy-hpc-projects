Project Summary

The project will be implemented by SKT Organization directly as the number of beneficiaries are included Men:2228, women:2573, boys: 2429, Girls:2354) =9584 IDPs, (Including 154 PWDs).
This project will work to implement the following interventions;
1-Distribution of hygiene kits; 1769 families will receive one hygiene kit, and one complimentary COVID-19 hygiene items to ensure well prevention measures among IDPs in camps.
2-Hygiene promotion campaigns; targeting the IDPs in the camp.
3-Provision of clean drinking water through the establishment and running of water networks: Across the 5 camps, all beneficiaries will get access to drinking water by contracted water trucking. " in the first 2 months, only 3 camps will be served". SKT will work on a more sustainable solution by building a water network within the camps and provide the water from a nearby water source (Public well). Also, SKT will build a central water tank near the targeted camps to ensure the continuous flow of water. SKT will ensure the water quality by chlorinating the water at the source in addition to water quality testing in the main tank and at the household level. Each beneficiary will get 40 liters of water tested as (0.2 and 0.5 mg/l FRC) before distribution and at the household level. This activity will work to install 46 (2 m3 each) water tanks to provide water per household level.
4- Solid waste management; this project will manage this activity on a daily basis in 5 camps. SKT will distribute 74 solid waste containers with a size of 100 liters across the targeted camps, and the collected waste will be dumped in a formal dumping site.
5-construction of latrines, according to the latest recommendation by the WASH cluster, ( 25 people per latrine), SKT will complete the construction of 96 latrine blocks (288 cells). the latrines design will take special consideration of protection issues as it is provided with a lighting system working on solar panels to ensure safe access during the dark hours to give safe access for both genders to these facilities. SKT is expecting 154 people with disabilities in the target camps, thus 7 cells will be allocated to cover their needs.
With a mass concrete base to ensure no leaking occurs or affects the surrounding land or population, 96 septic tanks will be constructed as well will the latrine blocks taking into consideration a safe distance.
The maintenance of these books will be managed as part of this project.
6-Septic tanks desludging;
SKT will desludging these septic tanks, during the project duration with a higher number of desludging during the summer.
7- Establish bathing facilities;
96 bathing facilities will be constructed as well taking into consideration the gender sensitivities by separating the facilities for men and women. The bathing cells will be part of the sanitation facilities, in which each block will contain 3 toilets and one shower. (detailed sketch will be attached to the proposal.
The proposed project aims to provide services for IDPs for 6 months and add one month for the preparation activities such as Baseline reassessment, engineering studies' preparation, contracting with contractors, recruiting staff, etc. The total project duration is 6 months.

3 - Needs

Based on the HNO 15.5 million people are in acute WASH need in Syria, and 1.125 million are located in Idleb governorate targeted by this action (HNO 2020). Within Syria, the worst situation regarding the availability of safe water is observed in Idleb where only around 13% of the population is served exclusively via piped water. And this situation has gotten even worse after the recent displacement. Due to the security situation in the south and middle of Idleb , the proposed project meets with cluster recommendations and objectives and ensures well implementation of COVID-19 prevention measures during WASH project implementation. The proposed project provides comprehensive WASH services for the targeted IDPs in the camps. SKT had designed this project to fill gaps and respond according to the required criteria. SKT conducted a needs assessment in the target project areas through key informant interviews (KIIs), focus group discussions (FGDs), and individual household interviews (HHIs), where the number of KII is 46 and FGDs 54 in the areas to be targeted.

4 - Implementation of activities

The project ensures continuous access of affected IDPs to essential quality WASH services in camps through the provision of life-saving and life-sustaining activities; through and in line with the priorities of the allocation strategy and the WASH sector, in particular, the action also provides safe water, water quality monitoring, repair and construct gender-segregated and safe latrines, constructing latrines for people with special needs, solid waste management, desludging of septic tanks, hygiene promotion as well as distribution of hygiene kits and hygiene complementary items in camps for the IDPs in Idleb. This intervention supports the main allocation strategic priority for the WASH cluster and matching the 1st, 2nd, and 3.1 cluster objectives support to water, sanitation/ sewage, and solid waste management system to ensure regular services for affected people in Syria. Deliver humanitarian WASH supplies and services and improve hygienic behaviour and practices of most vulnerable people and advocate and ensure that refuge migrants, IDPs people of concern who are particularly vulnerable to the pandemic receive COVID- 19 assistance.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

SKT acknowledges that certain challenges are unavoidable in the Syrian context and the risks to personnel and materials are multiple across the Syrian border. In light of risks, the scale and scope of program activities remain flexible and adjusted according to the context as well as the project ensures the equality of maintenance to facilities and ensures access to girls and women in a way that avoids gender risks that may happen. 1- Risk: New wave displacement to the camps targeted by the activities of the project Likelihood: High Impact: High Mitigation: Coordination will be conducted with the SCHF team in the event of a surplus in the budget or through coordination with the organization working in the field, under the umbrella and recommendations of the WASH sector. 2- Risk: The difference in needs as a result of the climatic conditions that may arise during the implementation of the project Likelihood: High Impact: High Mitigation: Communication and coordination with the SCHF team 3- RiskSome families want to install their own water tank. Likelihood: low Impact: High Mitigation: Meetings will be held with camp managers and beneficiaries to inform them of the project's activities, mechanism, and eligibility where the recommendations of the WASH cluster and SPHERE will be adhered to.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The staff of the project will have awareness sessions on GBV and PSEA to ensure that all of them are aware of dealing with these cases and able to refer them to the respective focal point in the area based on Protection Cluster shared the list of focal points. Female field staff will be hired to increase the protection of women and girls, and accountability boxes, as well as 2 complaint numbers, will be available.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks: 1- Application of humanitarian principles where the risk of aid diversion caused by armed groups exists although thus far not reported officially, SKT will report back to SCHF for any possible aid diversion incidents. 2- Major increase in fuel price and exchange rates. 3- Housing, Land, and property (HLP) agreement remain solid with the landowners. Mitigation: 1- Ensure proper coordination with the local councils and detailed written agreement are made with local authorities prior to the implementation 2- Close monitoring of the fuel prices in the implementation area, contracts with suppliers will be fixed in USD to ensure less deviation when fuel prices increase. 3- HLP agreement will be signed by the local councils as a third party to ensure that all parties commit to their responsibilities and duties High risk of bombing/ shelling/ work flexibility/ safety/ security: Justification: - Staff will stop working during periods of bombing - Collaboration with DoW (directorate of water and camps management) to gain legitimacy while working. - Collaboration with Camps managers to gain legitimacy while working. - Collaboration with the white helmets to receive necessary alerts, and develop evacuation plans.
