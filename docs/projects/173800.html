Project Summary

Following military confrontation between federal and regional forces in Ethiopia’s Tigray region refugees have been crossing the border to Sudan in Gedaref and Kassala regions. ACT Alliance has initiated a response under the lead of Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) focusing on filling gaps in humanitarian assistance to a targeted 20000 refugees in Kassala and Gedaref states primarily through WASH and GBV/Psychosocial assistance. The response is coordinated with Sudan Commission of Refugees (COR), UNHCR and respective sectoral working groups.
NCA moreover will respond to the current refugees settled in Al Radom, South Darfur with an integrated Food Security, WASH, Health, Nutrtion and Shelter/NFI. A total of 16,000 Refugees from South Sudan will be supported.
