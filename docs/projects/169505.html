Project Summary

Description of intervention: Selecting the distribution approach: Titi Foundation will give preference to direct delivery to the homes of beneficiaries. In instances where house-to-house will not applicable, distributions will be done from a safe location outside of the community (a school, health center, church compound) with only a few beneficiaries being called at a time to collect their materials. To prevent protection and security risks, all activities will be coordinated with officials. Titi Foundation will increase the number of distribution days and distribution points to reduce the number of beneficiaries gathering at each site. Community engagement and feedback mechanisms will be activated to reduce transmission, alleviate fear, misinformation, confusion, and tension. Existing community mechanisms will be leveraged to disseminate sensitization messages at the community level. Hygiene messages will be broadcast using megaphones and loudspeakers in simple/local language, taking into consideration the cultural context, as well as the special needs of marginalized communities.
PRP Pillar:
Geographical coverage: Yei, Kajo-Keji, Magwi, Cueibet, Yirol East, Bor South
Activity: Activity 1: Selection and Training of Hygiene Promoters: Titi Foundation will work closely with Community members, leaders and partners in to select and train a minimum of 25 hygiene promoters in the county. Individuals selected must be locals, can speak, read and write in Arabic and willing to remain in their community until the project is completed. Trainings will be facilitated using WHO, Global Health Cluster and South Sudan’s Wash Cluster standards and guidelines on Covid-19. A maximum of 5 trainees will be trained in staggered sessions, each session not lasting more than two hours. Topics covered during the training will be in line with UNICEF Hygiene Promotion guidelines in the context of Covid-19 including Household water treatment and storage practices, importance of maintaining social distance in public spaces, hand washi after coughing, sneezing, after visiting of public spaces, after touching surfaces using either sanitizers, chlorinated water or soap. During the training participants will be provided with masks and sanitizers and will be required to keep a distance of 2 metres from each other to ensure social distancing: Activity 2: Conduct house to house hygiene promotion: TITI Foundation will carry out house-to-house hygiene promotion though Hygiene Promoters. Hygiene Promoters will be provided with gloves, masks, sanitizers and loudspeakers during the door-to-door campaign. During the campaigns, hygiene promoters will be encouraged not to enter tukuls/ houses, to keep social distancing (2 metres from the other person), avoid shaking hands and to frequently wash hands using water and soap or sanitizers after each household visit. Hygiene Promoters will also use Information Education Materials, loudspeakers and megaphones to conduct Hygiene Promotion in markets, shopping centers, slaughter houses, water points and other places where people meet. Hygiene promotion messaging will include Menstrual hygiene management, water treatment and storage, Mode of transmitting Covid-19, Signs of Covid-19, social distancing, sneezing and coughing etiquette, personal hygiene, proper disposal of solid waste, symptoms and measures for assistance, how to do hand washing during critical times, and how to make, use and dispose homemade non-medical masks: Activity 3: Conduct training on production of reusable non-medical mask: The two modes of transmitting Covid-19 are contact or respiratory. Covid-19 spreads through body contact such hugging, shaking hands, kissing, through air droplets during normal conversation or when someone sneezes or coughs or touching eyes, nose or mouth after touching surfaces contaminated with the virus. In light of this, TITI FOUNDATION will train existing tailors on how to make homemade cloth masks to complement social distancing, hand washing and use of sanitizers to curb transmission of Covid-19. This will be done using specifications and standards recommended by Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). Training will be one on one. The trainer and the trainee will keep social distancing and will be supplied with sanitizers, soap and a mask. Activity 4: Provision of SNFIs to most vulnerable households: Titi Foundation will distribute SNFIs using in-kind and Cash Modality. To avoid congestion, Tarpaulins will be distributed to populations living in congested spaces. In areas with Functional markets, we will conduct Cash Based Interventions., Total estimated beneficiaries = 8000
