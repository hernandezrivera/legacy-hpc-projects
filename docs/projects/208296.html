Project Summary

The intervention provides support to vulnerable conflict affected households with Micro Enterprise and vocational skills trainings, and IGAs cash grant for Economic Recovery and Market System (ERMS) in four LGAs of Borno State which include Mafa, Konduga, MMC and Jere. The intervention targets vulnerable conflict affected HHs including IDPs, Host Communities and Returnees. The intervention aims to provide livelihood, promote the local market, and ensure self-reliance by the project participants. Selected households will undergo vocational and non-vocational training by SC in collaboration with Government MDAs and be provided IGA assistance through cash grant. The project participants will be further linked to artisan’s/master trainers at community level for apprenticeship, to enhance their technical know-how on the various skilled chosen by the project participants across the 6 implementing communities.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

As a result of the conflict in the Northeastern Nigeria, particularly Borno State, many Households have lost their means of Livelihoods/productive assets and as such having difficulty to meet their daily needs. There is need for IGA cash grant and capacity building for the affected people on entrepreneurship and vocational skills to improve in their income generating activities to be able to withstand feature shocks.

2 - Activities

The activities will include training of 4000 participants on Micro Enterprise and vocational skills to equip them with skills and knowledge to adequately run their income generating activities smoothly for growth and profit, formation of 100 VSLA groups and support with VSLA kits to encourage saving culture and access to financial services such as loan by the participants and also to promote social cohesion among the participants, provision of IGA cash grant to 4000HHs to support them with IGA startup capital which will contribute to the stimulation of the local market/economy.
