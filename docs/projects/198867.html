Project Summary

-Support the provision of access to primary healthcare services for displaced communities and affected host communities through integrated mobile clinics.
-Ensure linkage by adopting the essential package of health care provision for mobile brigades as per the MoH guidelines.
-Services will include outpatient consultations; life-saving emergency referrals as available based on Integrated Essential Child Health Care (IECHC) policy of Mozambique, Ante-natal care (ANC), Post-Natal Care (PNC), supplemental immunization activities (both routine and outreach), screening for malnutrition and treatment of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) and Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) with referrals to Stabilization Centre.
-Support to MoH during disease outbreak.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Gender and Protection analysis to be conducted / feedback and complaint mechanism to be set-up / FGD and KII to be conducted to adapt NUTRITION infra design/location and messages sensitization

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

A vulnerability based targeting approach will be used, where vulnerability criteria will be defined and/or weighted with supported communities through a committee elected by the community. For all planned locations of implementation, ACF will deploy a feedback and complaint mechanism (FCM), unless using a partner FCM when felt more relevant. ACF International Network Safeguarding Policy is currently under deployment, SEA automatic staff sensitization and charter signature being already in place. A gender and protection risks analysis will allow to better understand gender dynamics and protections concerns, for ACF to refine its strategy of intervention. ACF core targets groups are under 5 years old and pregnant and lactating women - baby WASH kits will be provided to GAM children.
