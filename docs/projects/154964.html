Project Summary

"In other to further support vulnerable households (HHs) in Loga, Baguaroua and Filingué departments, Samaritan's Purse will aim for the major objective of enhancing food access to HHs through coordinated food and agriculture resiliency sensitive assistance to save lives and livelihoods by increasing immediate food consumption among 2,979 HHs, providing animal support to 1,661 HHs and income Generating activities to 1,120 women. Project will focus on increasing the percentage of HHs with ‘acceptable’ food consumption score (FCS) strengthening capacity of vulnerable HHs to withstand shocks.
"

Needs

"• Extreme poverty, limited economic opportunities, recurrent environmental shocks and conflict contribute to hunger in Niger. The UN’s 2018 Humanitarian Response Plan for the country identifies 1.4 million and 1.7 million people in need of food and nutrition support, respectively. The insurgency linked to Boko Haram and the Islamic State of Iraq and Syria-West Africa in the Lake Chad Basin has displaced 104,300 Nigeriens within the country, while instability and insecurity have forced 58,300 Malian refugees and 118,900 Nigerian refugees to cross into Niger in search of shelter and protection. In addition, violence in western Niger is driving displacement and hampering normal economic activities; some populations in Tahoua and Tillaberi will face Stressed conditions through at least early 2019 and are increasingly reliant on relief assistance to meet their daily needs. Vulnerable populations who lack adequate access to assistance may be experiencing Crisis outcomes. • The Humanitarian Response Plan estimates a total of 1,481,155 people that will need food and nutrition support in Niger in 2019. Thus, through this intervention, Samaritan’s Purse (SP) intends to contribute to the sector target of meeting critical food needs of households in Loga, Baguaroua and Filingue departments providing animal feed and veterinary treatment kits to livestock herders. In Baguaroua Department of Tahoua region, SP will be targeting 3,045 beneficiaries (1,514 male and 1,531 female), 200 women with income generating activities, 435 livestock herders and 435 crop production households. In Filingue department of Tillabery region SP will be targeting 6,744 beneficiaries (3,526 female and 3,218 male), 320 women with income generating activities, 925 households with food assistance and supporting 740 households herders while in Loga department of Dosso region SP will be targeting 10,730 beneficiaries (5,098 female and 5,632 male), 600 women with income generating activities, 1,619 crop production Households and 486 households herders. "
