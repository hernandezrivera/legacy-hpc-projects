Project Summary

Purpose: In the wake of coronavirus disease COVID-19 pandemic created havoc
worldwide. The COVID-19 is an unprecedented calamity that has been affected millions of
people around the globe. Thus, a pandemic is much more than a health crisis; it's also an
unprecedented socio-economic crisis. Stressing every one of the countries it touches; has
the potential to create devastating social, economic, and political effects that will leave
deep and longstanding scars. Every day millions of people are dying, and the infected
people are in trouble, it has put a lot of pressure on health care services, emergency,
isolation, and quarantine, etc. The World Health Organization strongly recommended the
SOPs, the repeated hand washing, hand sanitizations, social distancing, and wearing the
mask. However, hand washing is considering the most important protocol to protect one
from COVID-19. Developing countries like Pakistan are also badly affected by
Coronavirus, It is precedent to observe a huge pressure comes on WASH services. Water
Sanitation and Hygiene are those important factors that need high attention at all. To
improve handwashing needs the provisions of water facilities, to overcome the burden on
sanitation complete sanitation services are the need of hours. Improving hygiene
practices is another important factor that needs further attention in this regard. To
devices, the WASH- services in the area of Hazara division( Abbottabad ,Kohistan and Torghar ) Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Province Pakistan are the selected districts of the
project and it is intending to provide a complete and concrete solution through WASH
interventions. The followings are proposed actions
1. Provisions of safe water supply, sanitation, and hygiene, and medical waste
management. the services are provided to those basic health facilities, hospitals, schools,
and communal places to be needed urgent WASH facilities. To deliver quality health
services, protect patients, health workers, and staff, and to prevent further transmission.
during an infectious disease outbreak, services should meet minimum quality standards
and be separated for infected vs. non-infected patients. Support is required to ensure that
services are not disrupted and products such as soap and alcohol-based hand rubs are
available. Temporary health care facilities and quarantine sites also need to provide these
services.
3. Communication and preparedness related to handwashing behavior change and
promotion, food hygiene, and safe water practices. Materials for hand washing and
hygiene may include the provision of fixed and portable handwashing facilities,
purchasing of soap and alcohol-based hand rubs, provision of water supplies for hand
washing, and point of use water treatment. Schools, workplaces, markets, transport
stations, and other areas where people gather all require easy access to water and soap
for handwashing. Proven behavior change techniques can help increase the frequency
and improve the practice of critical hygiene behaviors. To mobilize the public, civil society
actors to support the development of messaging and materials to respond to COVID-19
outbreaks in the area where the smart lockdown or partial lockdown imposed.
4. Rapid, low-cost water service provision for communities, health care facilities, and
schools that currently lack access to a reliable and safe water supply is critical to enable
handwashing, hygiene, and disinfection. Providing quick, just-in-time community water
access points/water kiosks (potentially including the provision of soap) in unnerved urban
and rural areas, and for unnerved health care facilities and schools. This would include: (i)
provision and operation of compact water treatment plants; (ii) construction and
operation of water points to delivering water in strategic urban or rural points; (iii)
provision and operation of trucks for water delivery (bottled, sachets) and water tankers,
including adequate water storage to service operators.
