1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to contribute to the realization of human rights, with special emphasis on the right to sanitation, by improving access to household sanitation and improving hygiene habits through the implementation of a gender-sensitive hygiene and sanitation promotion programme, which combines a community campaign and the training and accompaniment of at least 3 activists and 2 artisans in each selected community. At least 300 improved family latrines will be built with the participation of the artisans and activists, choosing low-cost solutions and a catalogue of low-cost sanitation solutions, adapted to the working context, will be developed. Finally, a stable financing system will be sought to access sanitation through economic actors. This approach is also intended to contribute to the reduction of morbidity and mortality caused by diseases related to lack of access to sanitation.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Preparation of the baseline and analysis of the sanitation situation at the household level in the area • Selection of low-cost sanitation technologies for use in the area: based on the information collected in the baseline, the most appropriate technological solutions is definedwith low-cost solutions for the poorest families. The solutions will also have to be adapted to the type of land in the area and to the preferences of the population and especially those of women and other groups vulnerable people such as the elderly or those with functional diversity.• Community organisation and selection of activists (health promoters) and artisans: The work of the activists will bebut will have some incentives such as visibility and communication materials and per diem. The artisanswill have training in low-cost technologies and initial materials and tools also as an incentive to promote their business, but will work independently as local service providers• Training artisans and activists in the Zonal PEC methodology (SANTOLIC, Sanitation Marketing, Hand Washing)• Implementation of hygiene promotion campaigns (handwashing, mass communication and community theatre) and SANTOLIC (generation of demand for sanitation) in the neighbourhoods• Development of financing systems and subsidies for the improvement of sanitation of the poorest households (promotion of exchange of labour for food): hygiene promotion will focus on hand washing and latrine use.• Creating a stable financing system through local economic actors and implementation of the marketing component of sanitation through artisans (financing, demonstration latrines with low-cost technologies, etc.): taking into account the difficulties that families in rural areas have in accessing capital in order to improving the latrine financing models will be developed to facilitate the payment of latrines in instalments by contracts signed with the craftsmen

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

There is an ongoing risk of armed conflict, which is mitigated by security precautions. There is also the risk of natural disasters, but these do not usually strike the project area. There is a risk of lack of community acceptance, which is mitigated by institutional meetings and meetings with the beneficiary communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

ONGAWA works directly with the district administration and local authorities to identify priorities for water and sanitation interventions. Then, after an objective data analysis, the intervention areas are chosen and meetings are held with the communities to agree the zones. In addition, training is provided to members of the water and sanitation committees, who will be responsible for the management of the water sources. Also, activists, who become people with a certain recognition within the communities for having been elected in a participatory way.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project, being the construction of family latrines, has a positive impact as the sanitation facilities are safer, with less seepage into aquifers that are a source of drinking water points.
