Project Summary

Première Urgence Internationale (PUI) undertakes around 180 projects per year supporting nearly 5 million people in 21 countries. PUI has been present in Maiduguri Metropolitan Council (MMC) since April 2016, and has developed a strong expertise to address the needs of crisis-affected populations through a community based comprehensive integrated response. An operational base was opened in Kukawa city, Kukawa LGA in 2018 to deploy health, nutrition and protection activities in Kukawa LGA, and more recently in Monguno. This presence enhanced PUI capacity to provide a rapid health and nutrition response following the inﬂuxes of IDPs which have occurred in late December. PUI seeks to implement live-saving activities, strengthen the local capacities, provide an appropriate response and give a primary place to the accountability to affected population in all implemented programs. Given the persisting humanitarian needs within Borno State, and the increased security threats towards the civilian resulting in aﬄux of IDPs in Maiduguri, Monguno and other perceived safe LGA, the proposed project aims to improve access to basic services through an integrated health and nutrition response for conﬂict affected populations, based on a community approach. PUI has been supporting a 24/7 reproductive health care services in two PHCC and ANC/PNC in three outreach sites in Bolori II MMC. PUI has plans to start sexual reproductive health in Monguno in 2020. Beside the 24/7 reproductive health care, PUI is also running ANC, PNC and under-ﬁve years immunization in the 5 health facilities in Bolori II in MMC.
PUI rolled out MHPSS activities in December 2018 in MMC and in February 2019 in Monguno. This was after PUI identiﬁed that IDPs seen at its supported health facilities are among the most vulnerable group to mental health issues. Over 62% of all consultations were delivered to IDP populations presenting with symptoms like somatic pains, sleeping diﬃculties and low mood.
Following the high burden of malnutrition in Bolori II, PUI is providing life-saving nutrition interventions since 2016 integrated with Primary Healthcare; Food Security and Livelihoods; Protection and Community Outreach; and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS). PUI implement an integrated nutrition package of CMAM and IYCF components. In MMMC LGA, PUI run a comprehensive CMAM services of Community outreach, OTP and SC services targeting 0-59 months children integrated with a community and facility based IYCF service packages including distribution of MNP for 6-23months to promote home fortification of complementary feeding. In Monguno, PUI CMAM activities will be limited to screening of 0-59months children from acute malnutrition while the referring all identified SAM and MAM cases to other partners running CMAM services in the location in line with the 2019 OTP sites downsizing strategy of the Nutrition sector.
To reduce exposure of COVID-19, delivery of nutrition activities at the community level will be essential to ensure affected population has access to the services and implement Covid-19 infection prevention and control measures across all Nutrition service points. PUI will adopt the nutrition sector proposed strategies to mitigate with the outbreak such as implementation of Mother-MUAC approach for continuation of screening, ensuring of protective measures are applied by Health care providers and volunteers and sensitization of the beneficiary communities on the preventive measures of Covid-19.

1 - Needs

As of January 2020, a total of 7.9. million people – more than half of the population in three conflict affected states of North-east Nigeria Borno, Adamawa and Yobe- were in need of humanitarian assistance. 2.8 million People, including 1.7 million children under five and 1.1 million women of reproductive age within the BAY states, needed life-saving nutrition services. Borno state is the most affected by the conflict and remains the central focus of the humanitarian response. Analysis of the current situation in January 2020 through Integrated Phased Classification (IPC)/ Cadre Harmonise (CH) exercise for 62 LGAs in BAY states classified that 22 Local Government Areas (LGAs), as Phase 3 (Serious) while the remaining 40 (including Maiduguri) are at Phase 2 (Alert). According to the IPC AMN classification Phase 3 requires the scaling up of treatment for affected populations. Phase 2 indicates the situation is progressively deteriorating and requires the strengthening of the existing response. The most recent findings of SMART survey conducted by PUI (December, 2019) in Bolori-II Ward of Maiduguri Metropolitan City (MMC) LGA in Borno State showed prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) at 11.1% (7.7-15.6, 95% CI), with Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) of 2.0% (0.9-4.2, 95% CI), stunting prevalence at 30.4%(25.0-36.5, 95% CI) and underweight at 21.8% (16.9-27.5, 95% CI). The afore-mentioned levels of malnutrition are of significant public health concern. The rates are classified as ‘Serious’ for GAM and ‘high prevalence’ for stunting and underweight respectively according to World Health Organization Standards. Similarly, another SMART survey conducted by Action Against Hunger (AAH) for whole MMC LGA (November, 2019) indicated the prevalence of GAM rate was 17.1 %( 13.8%–20.9% 95% C.I.) with a SAM rate of 3% (1.9%-4.7%: 95% C.I.) which is a critical level of GAM, while the prevalence of underweight and stunting was respectively 26.6% (22.9%-30.6% 95% CI) and 31.7%( 27.4% -36.3%, 95% C.I.) which are both at High level according to WHO classifications. More so, routine passive Mid Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) screening is conducted by PUI health workers at the health facilities, indicated that from a total of 30,406 boys and girls aged 6-59 months screened through MUAC measurement from July to December 2019, 2,310 (8%) were identified as having SAM. The identified cases were referred to and admitted in nearest Out-patient Therapeutic Care Programmes (OTP). The SMART survey also depicted sub-optimal uptake of recommended Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) practices in Bolori-II. Though rate of exclusive breastfeeding practices among infants aged 0-5 months have improved from 32.1% to 64.6% indicated in another PUI SMART Survey (November 2017) in Bolori II Although, the minimum acceptable diet for children aged 6-23 months is still sub-optimal as only 33.6% and 39.3% of children met the minimum dietary diversity and minimum meal frequency respectively. Similarly, in In Monguno Local Government Area (LGA), PUI Knowledge Attitude and Practices (KAP) survey carried on October 2019 indicated a sub-optimal practice in uptake of recommended IYCF practices, with only 38.6% of the interviewed mothers having initiated breastfeeding within one hour after birth, 65% of children 0-5 months were exclusively breastfed, while 13% of the children aged 6-23 months achieved minimum dietary diversity. 2 - Activities

The Nutrition activities are implemented closely with other inter-related sectors such as Health, Food Security and Protection, and focus on prevention, detection and treatment for the most vulnerable groups. To ensure relevant impact on reducing malnutrition with in the community, PUI delivers integrated Nutrition package of CMAM, IYCF and Micro-nutrient powder (MNP) distribution to tackle all forms of malnutrition which overlap.• IYCF interventions with focus on preventive Social Behavior Change Communication through a network of community outreach volunteers and Mother Care Groups as well as skilled based counseling of IYCF at health facilities mainly critical contact points in the OTP, SC and SRH services. Alongside with IYCF messaging, PUI also target MNP distributions for 6-23 months children to improve dietary fortification of essential micro-nutrients.• CMAM approach for timely identification and treatment of SAM and MAM in-line with WHO and MoH guidelines, through the provision of essential medicines and therapeutic supplies for management of malnutrition, and extending support to complicated SAM cases in the Stabilization Center.To reduce exposure of COVID-19, delivery of nutrition activities at the community level will be an important step to ensure affected population has access to the services and implement COVID-19 infection prevention and control measures. PUI will adopt the nutrition sector proposed strategies to mitigate with the outbreak such as implementation of Mother-MUAC approach for continuation of screening, ensuring of protective measures are applied by Health care providers and volunteers and sensitization of the beneficiary communities on the preventive measures of Covid-19.To ensure continued screening, identification and referral of acute malnutrition, the mother-MUAC approach will be adopted through training of the mothers of children <5years on how to asses acute malnutrition using MUAC screening and Oedema identification and referral of identified SAM cases to the nearest service providers. The approach significantly reduces exposure between the nutrition service providers and beneficiaries by avoiding gatherings for screening at the community. The mothers trained on the Mother-MUAC approach will perform MUAC screening for their children and will refer for confirmation and treatment for the identified SAM children. In the context of restricted gathering for screening in Covid-19, this will give mothers to identify the earliest signs of malnutrition.All PUI and MoH staffs working in PUI supported health facilities and in the community will be trained on the prevention measures of the COVID-19 infection to protect themselves and to their beneficiaries. Meanwhile all the facilities will adopt the infection prevention measures through applying Handwashing at entrances of health facilities, social distancing of 2 meters between service providers and between beneficiaries and establishing screenings sites at the gates of all PUI supported health facilities. Infrared Thermometer will be used by a trained health work to screen all patients, their caretakers and staff at the gate. Those found to be having high temperatures of >38 will directed to COVID-19 screening area from where suspected cases will be put in an isolation room and state surveillance team notified immediately.
