Project Summary

the project is going to improve the safe and equal access of quality inclusive education services in a protective and clean environment in 22 schools to 9650(4246boys & 5404 girls) children including 193 CWD inBennsh - Maaret Tamsrin sub-district, where targeted locations are considered as high severity (5) as mention in HNO2022. That will be done through conducting light repair to 75 classrooms and 25 wash facilities in22 schools, do light maintenance to the existing furniture in 22 schools, provide 22 schools with safety equipment (fire extinguishers and safety equipment), Provide maintenance and running costs, including cleaning material, stationery for school personnel.
Provide 22 schools with cleaning and disinfection materials and conduct weekly and monthly cleaning as preventive measure of Covid-19, contract with 445 teachers and supporting staff (22 head of schools, 329 formal, 44 non-formal teachers, 22 cleaners and 22 security guards, and 6 supervisors) and build their capacities in different areas including (Distance Learning & COVID19 preventive measures - Child Protection Principles - Modern Teaching Techniques - Integration of children with disabilities in the education response - Child safeguarding) to provide formal education services to 8492 children (3736 boys & 4755 girls) enrolled in formal education at the risk of dropout in 22 schools during academic year (October 2022 to May2023) ,non-formal education services during summer (May to August 2022) to 1158 OOSC children in catch-up classes using curriculum (B) physically with preventive measures of Covid-19, Conducting Back to learning BTL campaigns and awareness campaigns for children and their caregivers, and parents, and teachers, and community key members, conduct training on safety and evacuation for the 445( teachers ,supporting staff ),and 7545 children in the 22 schools, Conducting structured PSS and life skills for all students in formal and non-formal education .
Providing 8492 children with learning materials and school bags informal education. Provide 22 PSS workers on the PSS and Life skills to provide the PSS sessions and activities in the targeted locations,
provide awareness -sessions on Covid-19 and hygiene protocols to the children and caregivers, support the mid and end-year exams in the 22 schools. Provide teachers with teaching materials and resources.
As well, sadad will focus on mainstreaming the principles of child protection, and GBV by conducting awareness-raising sessions by sadad's protection team, in addition to PSEA awareness-raising sessions by Sadad's PSEA focal point.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1: Inaccessibility to education due to lack of safety and insecurity including risks of explosives and mines. Mitigating measures: Ensure flexible and context-informed mechanisms for school rehabilitation projects to adjust to a changing context. Ensure that relevant authorities are aware of and are engaged with the projects and are committed to ensure basic safety/ access for project implementation and ensure that staff members, teachers, and education personnel are trained in safety and security measures including in mine risk education. Risk 2: Insufficient safety and health standards at schools Mitigating measures: Close coordination with local education authorities, community leaders, and school management resulting in clear agreements on every actors' responsibility to create and maintain a safe learning environment. Regular monitoring to verify adherence to these agreements.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: organization’s Staff, volunteers, or associated people use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving. Mitigation and Prevention measures: 1. all staff, volunteers, and associated personnel sign the SHO's code of conduct which includes PSEA issues. 2. all staff, volunteers, and associates receive induction and (where relevant) annual refresher training on PSEA, and staff members are aware of their obligation to report SEA/misconduct. 3. HR and relevant hiring bodies check employment history during interview and questions related to PSEA asked during the interview.4. criminal records check is conducted where possible and new employees sign contracts which include PSEA and the organization code of conduct.5. complaints mechanisms developed to receive and respond to reports of SEA with train all SHO's staff, employees, volunteers, and contractors on these mechanisms and tools to make sure they are aware of how and when to use it. 6. The awareness-raising sessions on PSEA will be conducted for children by classroom teachers or schools' management. In case any violates the CoC/CSG; SHO will follow the investigation procedures in order to take the appropriate disciplinary actions based on OB’s CSG - PSEA policy.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigating measuresTargeted schools locations could be attacked by bombing or airstrike, Mitigation:SHO safety and security field team will conduct training, awareness-raising, and drills appropriate for the age of the involved students in addition to the staff. Topics include self-care, first aid, mine risk, and unexploded ordinance awareness, fire drills, evacuation drills, and stay-and-shelter drills. COVID-19 pandemic: Mitigation: SHO has developed policies to be followed due to COVID-19 including prevention measurements, conducting hygiene awareness, providing Schools with cleaning materials, dividing students into shifts in case the school is not able to apply the social distance between the students, in addition to following the recommendation of WoS re-opening schools. Re-Closure of learning facilities: Mitigation: Transform the physical learning to distance learning throughout SHO learning platform, train the education staff on the distance learning modalities methods, and approaches.
