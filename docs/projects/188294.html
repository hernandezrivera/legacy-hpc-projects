Project Summary

MWL will implement an integrated protection project which covers four sub-districts :Afrin/ Albab /Harim /Idleb, by expanding the protection interventions in 4 existing community centers, and mobile teams based on the huge and enormous needs in these areas. MWL's project meets the SO2 Enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks, and respond to protection needs through supporting the protective environment in Syria, by promoting international law, IHL, IHRL throughout creating a safer and more secure protective environment by providing an integrated set of General protection, Child Protection, Gender-Based Violence, and MineAction services.
The project targets the most vulnerable groups in the targeted communities so the CWDs, Orphans, separated children, adolescents girls, Widows especially living in camps, Children Headed Household to be prioritized, taking into consideration GAM.MWL will target 20,000 beneficiaries (M:3000, W:4000, B: 6000, G:7000) including 200 PWDs consists of four main components:
1.delivering general protection services to the host and IDPs including the following activities: awareness-raising, psychosocial assistance, targeted support to persons with specific protection needs and other community initiatives, through community centers and mobile teams
2. MWL will covers child protection issues to improve access to specialized child protection services and to child-friendly spaces by targeting children, youth, men, and women include the following activities:
*Sustained child protection programs, parenting programs, awareness-raising, and community events to prevent and respond to child protection issues, case management and training for men and women to raise the capacity about protection concept
3. Provide Risk Education sessions in addition to train people to conduct these sessions, conduct contamination surveys and Provide victim assistance services in areas affected by explosive hazards.
Workshop and meeting with community leaders and key community members will be implemented throughout the assessment, planning stages, implementation and close out of the project.
MWL will coordinate with other protection actors through service mapping prepared in MWL' team
The need assessment has been conducted in coordination with Local Councils, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. All the topics of protection awareness activities will be determined depending on the needed assessment results
Project MEAL activities will be conducted by MEAL field team . Work plan will be prepared in coordination with project program. Several tools (Surveys where Kobo and hard copy) will be adopted to monitor the project activities. Focus group discussion (FGD) on voluntary basis will be adopted to measure beneficiary’s satisfaction focusing on PWD, Children and CWD considering gender and Age. During COVID-19 MEAL team will use different methodologies to monitor and evaluate the activities including (BNFs survey, KII, Personal interviews and direct observation) using online apps and plat forms such as What’s app, telegram, etc.
