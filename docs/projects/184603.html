Project Summary

OCHA contributes to principled and effective humanitarian response in Somalia through
coordination, advocacy, policy development, information management, and humanitarian
financing tools and services. OCHA’s head office is in Mogadishu, the seat of the Federal Government of Somalia. OCHA maintains sub-offices in the state capitals of Somaliland (Hargeisa), Puntland (Garowe), South West (Baidoa), Galmudug (Dhusamareeb) and Jubaland (Kismayo). Sub-offices also exist in Hirshabelle State (Belet Weyne, Jowhar), Jubaland (Doolow) and Banadir Region (Mogadishu). The office in Nairobi continues to work as an integrated part of OCHA Somalia and provides day-to-day support to colleagues based in Mogadishu and in the eight field offices. The office also liaises with Nairobi-based donors and NGOs and provides support on issues related to humanitarian financing and response planning, including fund management support for the Somalia Humanitarian Fund (SHF) and the Central Emergency Response Fund (CERF).
