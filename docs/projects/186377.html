Project Summary

African Humanitarian Aid International (AHAI) through this program will build resilience of returnees, relocating from MMC, JERE to their local governments. the program will ensure sustain access to basic access to basic infrastructures and services ( health, nutrition services) through rehabilitation. in addition, the program will ensure returnees, especially women and girls' access to positive livelihoods opportunities via life skills capacity building and small grants to build resilience and advert negative coping mechanism ( selling of sex). Furthermore, AHAI will conduct peace building capacity sessions with host communities, community leaders for acceptance of returnees in communities.

1 - Needs

The prolonged crisis in the North-East Nigeria has caused massive displacement of boys,girls women and men into safer places in metropolitan cities in search humanitarian assistance. The crisis has disrupted means of livelihoods of men and women with untold consequences on their living standards. However, the outbreak of covid-19 pandemic with lock down including business closure, schools closure have worsen the condition of crisis-affected men and women, especially women , single female headed households with limited access to means of livelihoods. The insurgency and outbreak of covid-19 in late march 20202 in North East Nigeria have worsened the gender inequality among women and men in accessing livelihoods opportunities. Furthermore, The implementation of Borno State sustainable plan with the relocation of returnees IDPs of Jere, MMC camps into their various local governments areas (LGAs) necessitates provision of livelihoods opportunities to most of vulnerable households without means of livelihoods in their areas of returns. in addition, the overcrowding of returnees into existing infrastructures such as health, nutrition centers will overstretch the capacities of those basic and social infrastructures so as not to deliver quality basic social and communal services. there is need in Banki, Bama to rehabilitate dilapidated health and nutrition facilities in order to accommodate the new wages of returnees from Jere and MMC. In addition, there is need to strengthen peace as well as capacity building of host communities in social cohesion to enhance acceptance of returnees within the host communities.

2 - Activities

In order to create self-reliance of most vulnerable Returnees(IDPs) in Banki and Bama in Borno, AHAI shall;- assess skills of the layers of returnees households- strengthen capacities of most vulnerable women on life skills acquisition- capacity strengthen of targeted beneficiaries on saving and business scale up techniques- provision of livelihoods start up kits- provision of small grants to most vulnerable women and adolescent for business start up-rehabilitation of health and nutrition centers to ease access to basic services-capacity building of community members on social cohesion and peace building to enhance insertion and acceptance of returnees.
