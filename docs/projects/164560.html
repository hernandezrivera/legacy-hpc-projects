Project Summary

The Project aims to improve the food security and livelihood status of most vulnerable people through life-sustaining food assistance and income-generating opportunities ( linked to 1st and 2nd HRP 2020 targets) with special focus on female-headed households, and people with disabilities through distribution of Food vouchers targeted 2,050 Household in 3 high severity scales locations in Aleppo, Rural Damascus and Daraa, the amount of each voucher will be 45,000 SYP according to WFP Minimum Expenditure Basket for 8 Cycles according to Food Security Sector minimum standard,
In the purpose of providing income-generating opportunities, 30% out of the targeted beneficiaries will participate in specialized vocational training mainly of them PwDs and Female-Headed households for three months in the training centre in the same locations,hence100 beneficiaries out of the total beneficiaries who are targeted in the vocational training will participate in Cash for Work project which aims to provide temporary jobs in the targeted locations in close coordination with community committees in each location and provide 5 cycles of payments for each beneficiary, the amount of each payment will be 80,000 SYP, in order to support the well-being of the families, as well as providing income-generating for the most vulnerable people in the targeted communities.

4 - Needs

Nine years into the crisis, the food security situation in Syria has deteriorated compared to last year, with a total of 9.8 million people in need; of which 569,477 people are estimated to be severely food insecure; 7.3 million estimated to be moderately food insecure while 1.9 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity. This year the estimated number of people in need has increased by 8 per cent. The number of food-insecure people has also increased from 6.5M (2019) to 7.9M (2020), The number of people estimated to be food insecure is 7.9 million or 39 per cent of the total population of Syria. Of this, at least 569,477 (6%) are estimated to be severely food insecure. The majority of those are located in Aleppo, Rural Damascus and Ar-Daraa governates. Additionally, according to the conducted assessment in Jabel Samaan by TdH Staff: The samples size was 1028 HHS, the number of individuals was 5725 persons segregated to:55% Male and 45%, Female and the family size defined as;53% 5 or less than 5 household members,43% 6-10 household members,4%11 or more household members and the age group of headed households:2% Child (0-17 years),3%Youth (18-24 years),75%Adult (25-59 years),20% Elderly (60+ years)hence the age groups divided to:12%Young Children under 5 years,23%Children6-12 years,17%Adolescent13 – 18 Years,27 Young adults19-49 years,16%Midle aged adults 41-64 years,4% Older adults65 years and above, Persons with Disabilities: 8% out of the total individuals, PLW: 14% out of the total households, Chronically ill persons: 10% out of the total individuals. besides the percentage of job seekers is 21% divided to 36% Male and 64% Female where the most PIN works in many professions divided to 10% in Shoemakers,12% Tailors and 57% works in other professions( sewing, plumping, hairdressing...).

5 - Implementation of activities

The response project in Jebel Samaan in Aleppo, Eastern Ghota /Rural Damascus and Daraa City/ Daraa aim to improve the food security and livelihood status of most vulnerable people through life-sustaining food assistance and income-generating opportunities with special focus on female-headed houses, and people with disabilities (PWDS). Therefore, the activities will be in line with Food Security Sector in Syria and will be listed as below : 1st Activity: the project aims to meet the food assistance needs of 750 families in each location (three locations through the distribution of un-conditional / restricted food vouchers for 8 cycles on monthly bases (The amount of the food voucher is aligned with MEB and equal to 45,000 SYP) to provide a list of 50 essential food items according to WFP MEB. a Market assessment will be conducted through TdH field staff to identify and select the capable vendors and to build their capacity in term of the billing system and also to install ramps to facilitate the access and entrance for PWDs.2nd Activity: the project aims to implement Cash for work Project, which will be in close coordination with Community Committee in order to conduct FGDs with areas Job owners in order to define the appropriate jobs for the targeted beneficiaries mainly for Female-headed households and PWDs. The monthly payments will be 80,000 SYP for each beneficiary and will be paid through the selected job owners for five months targeting 100 beneficiaries in each location.3rd Activity: the project will build the capacity of (204) PWDs and female headed houses from each location through a set of vocational training in coordination with specialized training center under various subjects and topics based on individual assessment, then as an exit strategy and sustainability, Cash For Work component will be provided to 100 person out of them considering the cooperation with the job owners and community committees (5 month salary)

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

PR1: The targeted population may be under risk as a result of long-distance between the location of the vendors, lack of consideration for accessibility; lack of consideration of contextual information on the location and the local dynamicsM1: Liaise with the community and SCFPA to ensure that vendors are allocated in a Safe way for women and easy access for PWDs for (instance: no stairs) and it is compliant with the minimum standards for protection mainstreamingPR2: Discrimination of community groups and only a few groups benefit in the targeted communitiesM2: Engage community committee in beneficiaries selection ( with diverse members of the community to ensure diverse perspective and perceptions are considered, Select staff for all activities (especially volunteers) with adequate knowledge of the community, but also respecting balance in gender, age, social profile. PR3: Men and Women may come into conflict due to the mix of men and woman in the same distribution period, M3: Take into consideration the culture and norms in each location through divide the distribution creating a separate queue for women and men during distribution to avoid crowding or harassment, Separated rooms for any additional services. Ensuring there are female staff members from the implementing organization present during distributions, and setting up women-friendly spaces at asset distribution sites. PR4: Exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers when accessing assistance and services Establish a specific complaint and feedback mechanism 2.Conduct sensitization activities to reinforce the message that assistance and services are free

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

R1: Change in the security situation in the mentioned district/Governorate M1: TdH has aSecurity & Safty Focal Points in Aleppo, Rural Damascus and Daraa which provideDamascus office with updated information according to the security situation of dailybases, if necessary, TdH has the flexibility to move activities to alternate locations. R2:Tension of the relations between IDPs and host communities in the targeted areas. M2:Assistance is balanced and not directed only to one specific group. Identification ofbeneficiaries is done in partnership with local communities including IDPs and hostcommunities. Selected implementing partners are accepted by local communities andaware of conflict sensitivity. R3: Fraud Usage of one voucher by multiple households orduplication of vouchers. M3: Develop unique design for each voucher with differentcolours, including the name of the month, to ensure that all vouchers are related to thesame month of the predefined beneficiary. R4: Refuse the beneficiaries to participate inCash for Work project M4: Conduct separate FGDs with Job owners and separate FGDswith Job Seekers to define the appropriate job to fit both the job seekers and job owners.R5: Lack of training centres with appropriate training and equipment for PWD M5:conduct market assessment to define the appropriate training centers who eligible withthe needed training and criteria
