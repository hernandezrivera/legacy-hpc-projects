Project Summary


From 2016 up to this present moment, the issue that started as a struggle for equality and justice by citizens of the North West and south west Cameroon has spiraled into a full-blown crisis. Rebel groups seeking an end to what they consider domination of the Anglophone south by the francophone north have taken up weapons against the government’s security forces, causing hundreds to die while tens of thousands are forced from their homes. It is in this context of armed conflict (internal displacement of persons, shut down of schools, hospitals, poor sanitation, water scarcity, inferior water quality, inappropriate hygiene) and food insecurity that has to be addressed for over 437,000 people are internally displaced across the affected areas as cited by David Malan of the local Norwegian Refugees Council Office in an interview with Africa Renewal that this concept note is developed. it is hoped that the project will ameliorate the health situation of 2000 vulnerable households in the 7 sub divisions in Mezam.
