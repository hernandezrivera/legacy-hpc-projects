Project Summary

Formal education in Syria is in public or private schools of which attainment is regularized and accredited by the MOE In Syria.
Formal Education has 2 paths: curriculum A which is regular schooling and Curriculum B which is accelerated learning of 2 school years in one for out of school children or those who dropped out of school for more than 2 years.
The children at risk of dropping out are the children enrolled in formal education with an attendance rate of less than 60%, and children with low performance rate of less than 40% in at least 2 of the main subjects (Arabic, English, French, Math, and science). In addition to children participating in accelerated learning programme (CurriculumB)until they are enrolled in curriculum A in the appropriate age and level of education, and out of school children that re-enrolled in curriculum A due to unavailability to Curriculum B.In this education program GOPA will provide remedial education for children at risk of dropping out identified by the relevant DoEs , using the formal education materials with two categories of children :
The first : the children with the curriculum (B) program; a non-formal channel to strengthen children’s educational achievement and empower them to reintegrate them in formal education curriculum (A). Coordination will be held with relevant DoEs to eventually following these target children to pass their exams in curriculum(B) and enroll in regular classes of curriculum(A)
The second: the children with the curriculum (A) program that at enrolled in formal education with an attendance rate of less than 60%, and children with low performance rate of less than 40% in at least 2 of the main subjects (Arabic, English, French, Math, and science) .
GOPA will implement this program for 7000 children (8-18 years )in the several areas in Daraa , Quneitra , Aleppo and Rif Damascus will promote access to formal education for vulnerable students (50% male and 50% female between 5-17years of age), including through their reintegration into the good public education system (curriculum A) , and will conduct many events to promote the program and encourage the children to enroll in it. Furthermore, GOPA will provide the basic materials for this program in coordination with the MoE and Downer. One education focal point and one facilitator will be available in each 1000 children to To supervise the implementation of educational sessions.
And we will provide for children :
• Learning materials (stationary, pens, notebooks).
• transportation to school ( if necessary)
• textbooks for children ( if necessary)
• 4--supplementary learning materials for schools which help children to good learning.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project directly implemented by GOPA DERD that has sustained presence in the communities you are serving by relying on short visits, community centers in several cities.The project is divided into three main courses according to the academic year in Syria (The target will be divided into three groups in each city (two groups during the academic year, a group in the summer)1- We will choose the closest state school to the residence of most students at risk of droupt-out that we determeneted according need assement by GOPA-DERD, then we will take the approval of the Ministry of Education to use these schools to conduct educational courses in them.2-We will take student level exams to sort them into the appropriate grades.3- The program is divided to:-Remedial classes: aim at supporting students to be able to follow the Syrian curriculum and prepare for mid-year and final exams . Main subjects to be covered are Math, Biology, Physics, Chemistry, and Languages, for 15 hours per week over a period of 3 months. Beneficiaries of this program are reported based on the number of students who completed the entire period of the course (3 months).Qualified teachers with specific teaching skills will be employed to implement the education programme, and materials for remedial and exam classes and supplies will be delivered.The text books will be distributed to all children who cannot buy it (Extreme poverty is a key factor in child drop out because parents are unable to buy non-free textbooks in classrooms 7-9).Materials NotesNotebook 1 notebook to each subject Colouring pencil For students from 1 to 7 school levelsGeometric tools For students from 5 to 12 school levelsBooks of the Syrian national curriculum For out of school children and for 12th grade students4- At the end of the courses,we will make a test to the students to determine their progress . *During the entire program period, we will follow up the progress of students in the progress of students

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

It is very common that the children would be exposed to any kind of harassment during their way to school especially that there are many destroyed houses in the area; it would be very supportive if we could assign individuals to supervise on the children, take the attendance, and follow up their situation.It is most probably that there are mines in the area, and GOPA will seek to obtain the necessary approvals to provide protection to the children in the chosen schools.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The fluctuation of Dollar exchange rate set a great challenge in implementing the educational activities. A higher exchange rate of Dollar against the Syrian Pound, would led to a surplus that could be used in increasing the program’s target. In the contrary, the lower exchange rate of Dollar against the Syria Pound, we will attempt to reduce the expenditures to achieve the required target.If any sudden security situation or displacement etc.. occurs, GOPA will communicate with the donor to find alternative, appropriate, and close places.
