Project Summary

Under the project, HOPE will functionalize the proposed four government health in Tribal district South Waziristan and deploy four health teams for holding free medical camps (each team will consist of 1 Doctor, 1 LHV, 1 MT, 2 SMs and 1 female Psychologist. 561Static Medical Camps in all the three health facilities in 12 months and will reach to 27,000 individuals (7,020 men, 7,290 women, 6,218 boys and 6,472 girls). The teams will conduct OPDs to examine the 18,500 patients under PHC, 5,280 MNCH patients and 3,168 psychosocial support as well as 300 mothers will receive safe delivery kits and 300 baby kits to new born babies and provide the required medicines. During the OPD, Primary Health Care services will be provided to the patients including men, women and children from affected population in catchment of selected health facilities. The required medicines will be provided by the project from funds. The team will also provide MNCH services to the 5,280 mothers/CBAs about ANC, PNC, safe delivery, growth monitoring and safe delivery kits will be provided to pregnant women and baby kits for the new born babies. The reproductive health services will include family planning counseling, methods and delivery contraceptives etc. Referral mechanism will be established and strengthened with the nearby tertiary hospitals for complicated cases through ambulance service.
The project will be started from community mobilization and will form Health Advisory Committees, 2 for each health facility (male & female) with membership from 8 to 10 in the catchment area of the selected health facilities. The HAC members will be trained on record keeping, their role in project implementation and monitoring, brief orientation of PHC and MNCH services and will provide social support to the health facilities.
HOPE will train 30 health staff of the 4 selected health facilities and DoH in sessions on record keeping, medicine rationalization, Disease Early Warning System (DEWS) for early detection, notifying, response mechanism for outbreak with reporting and MNCH services.
300 Safe Delivery kits will be distributed to the pregnant women who want to get delivery at their homes. Similarly 300 Baby kits will be given for the newborn babies to save their health and care.
The Psychologist will identify the 3,168 patients in SMCs and conduct counseling to console and get them out from mental stress. The acute stress cases will be identified / treated and / or referred to the nearest trauma centers for further treatment.
For sustainability, the medical equipment and furniture will be provided to SMCs and will be handed over to the target health facilities after project completion to strengthen and support for provision of quality health services to the affected population. The staff of the health facilities will be trained on record keeping, medicine rationalization, patients’ management and DEWS reporting enabling them to provide quality services.

Needs

According to the Health Resource Availability and Mapping System (HeRAM) 2018 assessment, total number of Health Facilities (HFs) in tribal districts and Frontier Regions(FRs) are 996 out of which 135 HFs are located in Tribal District South Waziristan (SW). The report indicated that in Tribal District South Waziristan, 45 health facilities reporting to DHIS which are not totally destroyed whereas the rest of the health facilities were not providing services based on the absence of patients records in the month of the assessment due to fully / partially damaged or lack of staff availabilities. Health infrastructures in return areas of SW are severely damaged by the crisis and remain non-functional. The report further revealed that in Tribal District South Waziristan, 21 health facilities are fully damaged and 18 are partially damaged to the infrastructure. In term of accessibilities, among the functional health facilities, 8 facilities are inaccessible. The major reasons reported for inaccessibility of the health facility is that the health facilities are far and hard to reach for the people. The HeRAM report recommend that each BHU should have one Medical Officer (MO/WMO). Among the Tribal Districts, South Waziristan had the worst situation i.e. only 3 Medical Officer at the BHU level are available. Similarly, in South Waziristan the gaps for LHVs are 4. South Waziristan has the lowest number of technicians at the BHUs i.e. 13 (14%). In addition, findings from FATA Vulnerability Assessment exercise conducted in August/September 2017 revealed that majority of the communities in Tribal District South Waziristan faced difficulties in accessing health care facilities. Out of the women who had a child in the last three years, they didn’t seek any medical help or services during their pregnancy. A significant proportion reported delivery at home, mostly with the assistance of traditional midwife (Dai). The report further said that the returnee women and children in Tribal District South Waziristan have limited access to primary health services due to nonfunctional health facilities, no or deficient health care providers, lack of medicine and financial constraint. Many of the health staff who were displaced especially the female health workers have not resumed work. This has negatively influenced the provision of Reproductive Health, Mother & Child Health care services in Tribal District South Waziristan. A significant caseload opts to visit health facilities outside of Tribal District South Waziristan. This trend impacts negatively on the finances of the household and practiced by families who cannot afford to meet the expenses.

Activities

In order to rehabilitate and make the 3 target health facilities functional in the beginning of the project, the minor repair work including white wash/repair/electrification will be done and to cope up the load shedding/non-availability of electric supply, the solar panels will be provided to the 4 selected health facilities. The 4 health teams will hold static medical camps in each of the 4 government health facilities for 5 days a week (daily). Through the Static Mobile Clinics and weekly outreach camps, the OPD will be conducted and will provide first level treatment of the patients men, women and children for general diseases like URI, LRI, Diarrhea& Dysentery, Gastro Interties, Otitis Meadia (Ear Plus), Enteric Fever, Scabies, Dental carries, UTI, Worm infection, Dermatitis & Oral Trush etc. The patients will be examined and provide the medicines to the patients as prescribed by the doctor In case of high security threat in a specific area, with permission from UNOCHA, the particular SMC will hold Mobile Medical Camps in the nearby safer area/villages. The 4 Static Medical Camps teams will provide MNCH services to the mothers, PLWs/ CBAs about ANC, PNC and reproductive health services with provision of contraceptives/pills etc. The MNCH services will include antenatal, postnatal care and family planning will be on top priority under MCH services. Recognizing, stabilizing and referring obstetric, neonatal and other emergencies to the higher level and follow-up of the referred cases will be done. Community referrals for routine EPI for children including Tetanus Toxoid for pregnant women will be done through outreach services. Counseling on family planning and will be provided with condoms etc Maternal and Child Nutrition promotion and education will be done through counseling: including growth monitoring, exclusive breastfeeding, timely and appropriate introduction of complementary feeding, nutrition during pregnancies, management of Iron deficiency anemia and Vitamin A deficiencies, diagnosis and referral of moderate and severe acute malnutrition to appropriate centers. Assessment and management of common childhood illnesses like diarrheal diseases and ARI. Training mothers to prepare homemade ORS and use of ORS. The Psychologist will identify the patients in SMCs and conduct counseling to console and get them out from mental stress. The acute stress cases will be identified and will be treated and / or referred to the nearest trauma centers for further treatment. The Safe Delivery kits will be distributed to the pregnant women who want to get delivery at their homes. Similarly the Baby kits will be given to newborn babies to save their health and care in order to get the mother trained and get good habit and practice for future use. Referral mechanism will be established and strengthen with the nearby tertiary hospitals through the health field staff (LHW/CHWs) of the 3 health facilities. Linkages will be established with MSF Hospital is providing ANC, PNC and delivery services with nominal charges/free and similarly tertiary hospitals for referral purposes. Project will provide ambulance services to the patients with chronic diseases referred to tertiary hospital for further treatment. The project team and the health staff of the 4 selected health facilities will be trained on Disease Early Warning System (DEWS) for early detection, notifying, response mechanism for outbreak and weekly reporting regularly. In order to fill the gaps and strengthen the 4 selected health facilities, the medical supplies and furniture will be provided on need based on completion of the project so that these health facility are capacitated and strengthen enough to provide the required PHC and MNCH services to the patients from stayees and returned IDPs families.
