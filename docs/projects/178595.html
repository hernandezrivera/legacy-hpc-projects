Project Summary

Basma for relief and development (BRD) will support a planned established camps for the 5,400 newly displaced populations affected by the ongoing conflict (2 camps in same sub district - community), including forced evacuations, and provide CCCM services for the IDPs living in those 2 camps in addition to management of the data and information of the IDPs in those 2 camps as well as collect and management of the data of other non CCCM services provided to the IDPs including temporary shelter, non-food items, WASH, Food, Protection, Health and other in Idleb Governorate.
BRD will start providing CCCM activities and services for the IDPs as following:

4 - Needs

According to CCCM Cluster trucked displacement of 615K-116K to end of 2020 which Snapshot indicate, Idleb especially has experienced a significant IDP influx This inflow of IDPs is tied to North Idleb, which led to movement of up to 615K individuals to north of Idleb. Thus, residents in such camp-like settings are amongst most vulnerable, continuous influx of evacuees from South of Idleb has raised the demand for shelter, food, and NFIs. Additionally, the influx has led to a shortage of drinking water across Idleb, forcing people to rely on trucked water The humanitarian situation is further exacerbated by an ongoing shortage of basic food and non-food items, in particular for the evacuees from South of Idleb GBV and CP issues are present in most areas in NWS and seem to worsen within IDPs communities. According to 2021 HNO, child recruitment is a prominent concern in 42 % of 262 sub-districts and children are often the main breadwinners.CCCM cluster highlights the urgent need to provide coordinated, comprehensive assistance to the new IDPs in critical need of shelter, NFIs, food, hygiene, health, protection, and sanitation services. As hostilities persist, more IDPs will likely exhaust their household resources and take refuge in camps, informal tented settlements IDP sites have reached capacity or already have exceeded optimal population density conditions.Many camps damaged during winter, many tents burned, many camps getting double services, Irregular services, all those aspects directing BRD to deliver CCCM services in those 2camps about to be finished.Set in place proper staffing/management personnel to conduct CCCM activities, 2- Establishment contingency planning with firefighting system and others

5 - Implementation of activities

BRD will serve as the camp management agency, and set in place proper staffing/management personnel to conduct CCCM activities, including registration/data collection, vulnerability mapping, verification, establishment of gender-balanced camp governance/ participation management structures, and accountability mechanisms, routine site renovations Procedure of Reception of IDP’s in the camp Once IDP families arrive at the camp, the following procedures will be followed:The families arriving to the camp will pass through the registration reception tent to be registered, and undertake a profiling questionnaire with focus on identifying vulnerable and special cases. Registered families will also be given a registration card Vulnerable cases (disabilities, elderly) will be given priority during the process They and their family will be allocated a family tent near to access points. BRD will conduct workshop to start project. BRD will organize monthly planning and progress meetings and monitor activities through periodic field visits by MEAL team. The methods used for data collection and observing changes will be beneficiary interviews PDM after MEAL team will ensure community participation during the project implementation through activated feedback mechanism, where many channels will be available includes but mot limited, WhatsApp, email, skype, suggestion boxes and field team. Feedback mechanism will support the accountability within the project staff and targeted camps. Field coordinator will always be willing to listen from the BNs, Monitoring will focus on, but not limited to, cycle of activities, and how BRD is achieving the criteria of CCCM project. • Regular locations supervision carried out by Field Coordinator

Intentions surveys and/or NA will raise unrealistic expectations among IDPs living in camps. Targeting of communities and BNs selection will fuel unintended inter or intra community tensions. BRD will strive to employ women enumerators to achieve gender-balanced teams.BRD will engage target community through regular meetings, transparent information management and BNs selection, and throughout implementation Project will address potential protection risks with mitigating measures appropriate to intervention modality of projectBRD assesses risks to be in following areas- A:Inter-IDPs tension due to varied selection of items and HHs getting same aid. Abuse of authority by distribution actors–H/HBRD applies CoC/procedure on staff along with complaint mechanism to prevent abuse. Risk of Physical Harm distribution points–L/L Intervention is based on cash voucher hence greatly minimises such a risk Risk to humanitarian nature of the intervention by armed actors– M/H Strong coordination with local civil authorities, camp M, and community actors to prevent interference by armed actors. Lack of documentation may exclude certain IDPs–M/M Close coordination with Camp management authority to ensure no IDPs are excluded.
