Project Summary

This proposed project aims to sustain, expand the provision of improved WASH, food security and livelihood, and Education services to conflict affected IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in the localities (Al Kurmuk, Damazine, Rosaries and Geissan) in Blue Nile state. The project will address the WASH, FSL and Education needs of 25000 persons.
This project will enhance and sustain access to improved drinking water and basic safe sanitation through drilling and installation of new HPs, rehabilitation of nonfunctional HPs, training of HP mechanics and provision of hand pump maintenance tool sets. WASH activities will reduce overcrowding at host community water sources that are partially utilized by IDPs – i.e. the intervention will contribute to improved social cohesion between the IDPs and host communities. Aiming at enhancing access to safe basic sanitation facilities for the IDPs, especially those will be affected by AWD, the project interventions will include construction of school latrines; building the capacity of communities through training of WASH committees on water resources management; establishment and support of sanitation clubs; conduct of sanitation awareness campaigns; provision of hand washing facilities; training on CHAST approaches; and water quality monitoring and testing. Hygiene promoters training will be key part of the project activities to facilitate safe knowledge and practices transfers to lead beneficiaries towards sustainable behavior change and adoption of good hygiene practices. To preserve the natural environment, the project contains solid waste management, vector control, and environmental friendly technologies will be adopted. In FSL sector the project will provide varied interventions to support livelihoods of vulnerable displaced and host communities that have suffered widespread conflict and violence. The proposed project will achieve this through provision of emergency agricultural inputs and services which include distribution of food crops and vegetable seeds, tools and extension service; provide emergency livelihoods training and start up kits, free animal vaccination as part of emergency livestock inputs and veterinary services that will be provided to the IDPs, formation and support of women farmer associations, and natural resources conservation and management.
In Education sector, the project will focus on the construction and rehabilitation of both girls and boys government schools, aiming at creating a child and education friendly environment with maximum enrollment and minimum drop outs. Quality of education will be enhanced through teacher trainings and provision of reaching aids e.g. quality furniture for teachers and schools, learning and recreation kits and improved water and sanitation services.

1 - Needs

According to the last Blue Nile joint Needs Assessment which conducted in February 2018 revealed the education infrastructure in the communities is generally sub-standard. Classrooms for the primary grades are very often makeshift shelters made using poles and thatched roofs. Surveyed schools lack basic WASH facilities. No handwashing facilities. Lack of learning materials is problematic at schools. 35% of schools without hand washing facilities, 78.6% have no adequate seating furniture, 21.4 do not have drinking water containers. 12 schools were either destroyed due to the conflict in the targeted localities. The remaining schools those serving host community and IDPs are overcrowded. Additional classrooms are urgently needed. In addition 57 classrooms need rehabilitation. There is a shortage of qualified and trained school teachers especially female teachers. As result of this situation, enrolment rate is 33% (on average); 55 % male and 45% female. For WASH, there is Limited access to safe water; large number of the populations in Blue Nile State including IDPs doesn’t have access to safe drinking water. Water facilities are frequently braking down due to low capacity in operation and management followed with lack of necessary tool kits. Low community awareness on hygiene and sanitation, In FSL, due to the extremely high cost of production and transportation and persistent macroeconomic challenges. This will likely reduce household purchasing power particularly for poor households in food deficit and conflict affected areas that normally import staple foods from main producing areas, FEWS NET assumes the continued absence of subsidies for essential commodities, including wheat subsidies with high food and non-food prices are likely to persist through 2020, IRW will focus on extension and training services with local communities and their organization ) including farmer school to improve cultivation practices.

WASH: The proposed project will contribute to enhanced and sustainable access to improved drinking water and basic safe sanitation facilities to target beneficiaries through drilling and installation of new HPs and rehabilitation of nonfunctional HPs. HP mechanics training this will be coupled with provision of hand pump maintenance tool sets. The activities in this project will also reduce overcrowding in host community water sources that are currently partially utilized by IDPs this will tend to improve relationships between the IDPs and host communities. The project also aims to enhance access to safe basic sanitation facilities for the IDPs in the targeted areas affected by AWD by construction of school latrines; build the capacity of communities through training of WASH committees on water resources management; establishment and support of sanitation clubs; conducting of sanitation awareness campaigns; provision of hand washing facilities; training on CHAST approaches; and water quality monitoring and testing. Hygiene promoters training are part of the project to lead beneficiaries to sustainable behavior change and adoption of good hygiene practices. To preserve the natural environment, the project contains solid waste management, vector control, and environmental friendly technologies will be adopted..FSL: The project will provide varied interventions to support livelihoods of vulnerable displaced and host communities that have suffered widespread conflict and violence. The proposed project will achieve this through provision of emergency agricultural inputs and services this include of distribution of crops and vegetable seeds, tools and extension service, provide emergency livelihoods training and start up kits, free animal vaccination as part of emergency livestock inputs and veterinary services that will be provided to the IDPs, formation and support of women farmer associations, and natural resources conservation and management.Proposed project mainly contribute to the sector objectives by strengthening, reinforcing positive coping strategies & reduce vulnerabilities of targeted communities, empowering beneficiaries with improved human capital and livelihood options. Moreover the importance of these activities stem from their ability to enable targeted beneficiaries to pursue goals necessary for their survival and longer-term wellbeing, and thereby reduce the vulnerability created and exacerbated by the conflicts and insecurity. Education: The project intends to increase and sustain access to education in emergencies for IDPs and vulnerable host communities in Damazine, Rosaries, Kurmuk and Geissan localities. Through this fund IRW will provide access to safe learning spaces for 5,303 boys and girls through construction of 8 semi-permanent classrooms and rehabilitation of 8 classrooms. These activities will be coupled with rehabilitation of 8 school/teacher offices including furniture. A gender sensitive hygiene awareness campaign will be conducted as well as six educations in emergency awareness sessions. 8 schools will be supported by recreational facilities and health clubs, also 8 schools will be supported by teaching and learning materials. the learning opportunities and psychosocial and emotional wellbeing of girls and boys have been considered in the activities through providing psychosocial support training to 40 teachers. Besides that, 20 teachers will be receiving training in EiE technique to improve quality of instruction. To address the key child protection challenges this project will target to provide child protection awareness to the targeted comminutes as well as train and build capacity with the relevant people whom are in direct contact with the children and make the guardians /PTAs know about child rights and how to protect the children and safeguarding them in a very protective environment.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

This project is aiming at not to influence negatively on the natural resources in Blue Nile State. the materials that will be used in the WASH, Food Security and Education sectors are friendly to environment. For WASH, assuring water balance (availability, demand versus supply) IR will only upgrade HP boreholes with adequate water recharge to avoid overuse of existing water resources. Under FSL, the project will support farmers with environment friendly seeds (early maturing seeds). For WASH infrastructures, environment friendly supplies (e.g. solar systems) will be used whenever the context allows.

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

• Beneficiaries will be provided information from the holy Quran and Hadiths of the prophet will be shared. Specific topics on overall protections, GBV and SGBV will be added to different sessions on WASH and IRW project plat forms such as Health facilities, community and nutrition centers, and training of the agricultural extension agents and community members will be used to deliver messages on protection issues. Religious leaders in the communities will be engaged so that they talk about these issues especially in Friday prayer sermons. Involvement of the religious leader will divert the risk.• The violation and exploitation of childhood has increased steadily in the BN state because of the civil war where children and women vulnerability increased which compel families not care about child protection as a priority and even some families send their orphans / children daily to work to raise family income and satisfy financial needs.• The project will make every effort to enhance protection by making it mandatory to have dignity, respect and neutrality maintained in all committees and decision making processes. The project is aware of cultural issues concerning the participation of women together with men in committees. Female staff will address all issues relative to women and encourage them to take responsibility in mobilizing, sensitizing and taking active roles in community issues. Men will be equally sensitized to ensure voice is given to women, a strategy to break their silence. The project will also support women peace project to underpin their role in promoting peace. • The project is aware of contextual details particularly concerning the activities where women and girls are usually involved in. It has been observed that women folk preferably work in groups collectively, to a possible extend, out of the tribal and cultural complexities. For that reasons, it is believed that the activities related to awareness sessions, building capacities for better quality, which are arranged for the community in groups, will be help connecting them and reducing the risk of protection, conflict and tensions.
