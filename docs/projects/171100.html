Project Summary

The protracted multi-faceted armed conflict, recurrent natural disasters, and a resulting
upsurge in internal displacement have led to a highly complex crisis of protection in
Somalia. The protection needs of the IDPs and the vulnerable urban poor of the host
community in Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, and Galgaduud is very critical given the
overstretched nature of limited resources due to the influx of IDPs, the emergence of the
COVID-19 Pandemic. There are range of child protection issue in the target area; including child soldier, children living with disability, UASC, early marriage, FGM, and rape. People with disabilities face additional vulnerabilities.
The project operational area is the 8 districts of Galgadud, Middle Shabelle, and Lower Shebelle
regions targeting IDPs and poor vulnerable host communities. In 2020, NWO completed
protection projects successfully in Galgadud and Middle Shabelle funded by the SHF and
a total of 10,300 beneficiaries were reached. Protection measures are mainstreamed to all
NWO projects in its operational areas including implementation of this intervention.
Across its protection response, NWO will ensure integration into existing referral
mechanisms for life saving protection responses and support the establishment of the
same where these are not currently active. NWO will significantly strengthen its
community based protection engagement; providing affected communities with the
support, skills and information to strengthen their protection capacity.
The proposed child protection project will identify and address the heightened risks faced
by children and adolescent in areas targeted by this project. The strategy in
implementation of this project includes strengthening Community Based Child Protection
Mechanisms within conflict and flood affected communities besides facilitating access to
services to survivors of violations. This project will ensure full participation of children and adults regardless of their ability, including those with disabilities to promote social
inclusion.
NWO will endeavor to put in place specific arrangements to ensure and promote participation of these traditionally marginalised groups within the target locations; The project will conduct wide-ranging rights based public outreach and
awareness raising; Provision of appropriate assistance to children at protection risk; The project strategy is empowering communities to respond child protection violations through training, awareness raising, well coordinated case management and direct supports.
Adequate training for teachers and child protection staff in how to incorporate children living with disabilities into classroom learning and social service;
Assessing IDP settlements are for safety standards through safety audits, child safety
walks and child led participatory; Training CMCPMs on identifying and referring cases of children at risk in their camps and communities. Supporting community prevention on COVID-19 by providing protection messages including prevention and safe message for COVID-19.
Promotion of collective action on the centrality of
protection by ensuring complaint and feedback mechanisms, protection mainstreaming procedures, PSEA and child safeguarding focal points; Re-unification of separated girls and boys with their primary caregivers or in alternative care and proper case management.
Protection Case Workers and project staff to be trained will be composed of male and female. Men and women will be given equal opportunities when recruiting staff for the project. Persons living with disability and members from marginalised communities will
be given special consideration in starting. The project plans to reach 72,800 children and
adults through different well planned activities and community-led messaging on key

Mitigation Measures

New ways will collect and use data dis-aggregated by sex, age and diversity toeffectively inform programming. - New ways will ensure that assistance and servicesare reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritise the most vulnerable groups inthe community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalised groups – NewWays will ensure lack of documentation does not exclude individuals from accessingservices - New ways will make infrastructure adaptions to the safe houses so that it isaccessible to persons with impaired mobility. - New ways will provide culturallyappropriate mental health services by a trained staff. Where these services are notavailable will make sure that Community workers are aware of referral agencies andprocedures. - New ways will ensure that beneciaries know of available protectionservices and assistance where/how to obtain it, through various means to ensurecommunication reaches the broader community and consider literacy levels. Newways will set up mechanisms for submitting feedback that do not require thebeneciary exposing themselves to project staff. - New ways will respond tocomplaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place. -New ways will organize awareness raising sessions so that people know how it works.- New ways will consider a joint feedback mechanism with other sectors to minimizeconfusion. - New ways will make sure protection staff and community protectioncommittees will be representative of all groups within the community (e.g. gender, age,ethnicity, socioeconomic group, persons with disabilities) and all staff and memberswill receive protection mainstreaming training. - New ways will ensure all protectionstaff and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere toa Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarianstandards and the rights of beneciaries, including the condentiality of the victims.
