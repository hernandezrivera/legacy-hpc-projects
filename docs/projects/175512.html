Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

On top of the ongoing response following the impact of Cyclone Kenneth as well as conflict induced displacements, there is an urgent need to fill gaps in the existing humanitarian response in Cabo Delgado and the neighbouring provinces of Nampula and Niassa, to address the needs of IDPs and host communities impacted by the deteriorating situation.Building directly on its existing field presence and portfolio, with added value and comparative advantage on the response, IOM proposes to respond to the immediate humanitarian needs by providing emergency humanitarian assistance to 17,500 IDP families and 3,500 most vulnerable host families with residual needs from the cyclone Kenneth (with house repairs and retrofit to withstand harsh weather conditions). In implementing the response, the project will aim to improve the coordination between actors while creating the supporting tools and strenghthening the capacities of the shelter operations and humanitarian actors. As such, 20 “on the job” training workshops will be conducted around improved techniques for building sustainable and safer shelters, including bamboo treatment, IEC materials, pipeline management, market assessments and environmental impact of shelter interventions, etc.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

IOM is implementing the Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) which enables the humanitarian community to identify priorities and activities through multi-sectoral assessments that take into consideration gaps in humanitarian service coverage, including health, WASH, protection and shelter services. The DTM displacement monitoring and emergency tracking tools will further inform humanitarian programming of protection risks and needs for better targeted and evidence based interventions ultimately contributing to more secure settlements for affected populations.Regarding project design, IOM ensures that the shelter Kit content is tailor made to mitigate protection risks and takes into consideration the needs of vulnerable groups.Greater protection concerns are mainstreamed into the multi-sectoral teams (in addition to having protection representation within IOM structure), who conduct referrals and services specifically targeted for those vulnerable persons.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Community iddentification will focus on populations with highest scores of people with vulnerabilities, while detailed household needs assessments will be carried out in order to tailor the type of response needed for those vulnerable groups.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be targeted and have a higher score while selecting the most vulnerable houses. These people, if with no labour capacity, will receive technical and labour support for the construction of their shelters.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

IOM will seek stright adherence to the Sphere Standards for Humanitarian Assistance which identify the environment as a cross-cutting issue to be incorporated to all humanitarian assistance. In that sense, IOM will encourage the use of assessment tools to assess the environmental impact of the construction related program activities. This tools focus from the selection of the shelter sites, the construction of structures reviewing the possible negative environmental impacts, the management considerations while occupied and also considers the possible decommissioning or dismanteling of the structures to reutilize the resources. The use of this tools will engage local actors and communities to understand the different conditions particular to the working areas.
