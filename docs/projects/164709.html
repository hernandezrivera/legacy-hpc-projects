Project Summary

the project aims to strengthen the education services and decreasing drop-out levels in
Syria and sustain the educational activities in the targets areas, by the following:
Encouraging the students to back their learning via implementing BTL campaigns, from
girls and boys in the age of 7 to the age of 17,
Induce people to send their children to the ECCD sessions with the age of 3 to 5 years, and provide transportation for needed students.
providing assistance to formal primary school building, ensure the sustainability of the
teacher's stipends from women and men especially via Non-formal education by provision monthly incentives for both teachers and education personnel to sustain and help their financial situation.
Furthermore, the project intends to provide technical support for the teacher in order to make them better able to provide education in emergency NES situation.
As in general, the local authorities is committed to pay the salaries for the teachers in public schools and pay for schools running cost, IPV intervention in the public schools by coordination with LA, to avoid the duplication, would be via providing the school building with the required rehabilitation, school furniture to ensure that the schools itself is suitable and could be comfortable for students attendance further to equip the schools with the PWD equipment's from girls, boys, women and men, in addition, the running cost for the
schools is a very important component in the project for the schools and teachers by
providing school kits, teachers kits, textbook, stationery and hygiene kits, and the running
cost for the formal schools itself by providing drinkable water, electricity, heating fuel.
Developing the mentality of students will be strongly considered in the project by
provision the TVET sessions and equipping the learning centres by book banks, this
activity will be for youth and adolescents in addition to the primary school children from boys and girls.
The project also aims to provide assistance for non-formal education for OOSC and at risk of dropping out
children who are dropped out or may to drop out of school due to many reasons like displacement and
security issues especially girls, to reach for them IPV intends to conduct a detailed needs assessment in order to catch a respect data base to show the drop-out reasons and levels for drop-outed students, the percentage of children at school but at risk of drop-out, the main point of the proposed assessment, to identify the a appropriate intervention, where the project is committed to provide the neede education program for the targeted students,
(ALP and SLP) for the drop-out children
Catch-Up classes for the children at risk of drop-out.
Provision PSS and SRA sessions for the ALP students beside teachers to ensure their wellbeing. all the running cost for this non-formal program will be covered by the project, including the ALP teachers stipends, PSS facilitators stipends, the required materials for the PSS and textbooks for ALP program. furthermore, according to the needs to provide the capacity
building for the teachers there will be training for the education personnel from teachers
(women and men) in addition to add new component related to the classroom control and
develop the education process with the classroom level, by providing training to coaching the classrooms in the FE and for NFE program, the coaching program will take a very important role in developing the education process with the classrooms levels in the FE and NFE.
this will be supported with teachers learning circles " TLC" and PTA, and all the required
and related training will be provided.
provide transportation for needed students.
The project also intent to measure the developing of the student level by assessing their academic achievement by conducting ASER to measure the quality and placement - mid and final test for the targeted student aged from 7-14 ys to measure the learning, from boys and girls.

4 - Needs

according to the HNO-2020, the rate of displacement and the escalation of violence in addition to increasing the percentage of the risk of dropping out of schools for the children from boys and girls from the age of 7 to the age of 15, all of this factors have big role in designing the project in order to re-gap the affecting reasons, in addition, according to the HNO-2020, the barriers to accessing quality and relevant learning for children inside Syria are complex and include a wide range of protection and socio-economic issues, extending beyond the education sector. These complicating factors include the prevalence of child labour, child marriage, lack of identity and education attainment documentation, lack of safety both at home and in school, the cumulative psychosocial effects of the protracted crisis and extensive displacement. in addition to the needs of capacity building for the teachers from women and men from the age more than 18 to the elders age , all of the previous factors have been taken in much consideration in developing the project furthermore we have lessons learned and accumulative experience in implementing many educational projects in proposed areas. where we have always rapid need assessment to the areas like Al-Raqqa and its countryside, The needs assessment shows that the rate of drop-out students are in high level as consequence of the school were closed for than more three years under ISIS fighters role. 33% of public schools are partially damaged and 23% of certified teachers have left the education We are well known about the local environment where the project will take place, like the number of the functioning schools in the areas and the number of schools which are in need for the project activities, like the need for rehabilitation ,the teachers stipends, in addition to taking into consideration the role of the educational offices in the project areas and their updated inputs of the schools and education situation in general .

5 - Implementation of activities

Our organization, will implement the activities directly by its direct presence in the project areas, the implementation based on needs assessment for education taking care of the variance needs of the targeted groups from women, men, young children, children, adults and elders in the mentioned areas for the formal and the non-formal education programs, the implementations will be at the beginning by hiring all the involved staff then start the implementation of the activities taking into consideration the reasonable sequence of activities, this will be according to the technical recommendation from the technical persons, Exp, for the rehabilitation process, the technical and engineering advice is the base in implementing, then procure and provide schools with the needed school furniture, stationery, consumables. All required documents will be obtained by our field staff. Prepare all registry and document needed, and planning to receive students to provide formal education. schools administrators is responsible for daily Attendance sheet for both students and personnel staff. Register and documentation office prepare regular reports (weekly and monthly) then send to program manager via field program manager. more, there will be TLC for the teachers from women and men for all ages and PTA sessions on monthly basis the participant will be from women, men , boys and girls from children youth, and adolescents. for the NFE, hiring teachers from women and men and provide them with training for the ALP and catch up or remedial program, this program targets the dropped students from boys and girls from 9 yrs to 17. in the mid-year weekend, there will be training for the education personnel, for some related training to develop the education process and build the teachers capacities from women and men from adults who are in age more than 18 till the elders. some of the training proposed topics is PFA, TICC , child safeguarding training.
