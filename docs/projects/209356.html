Project Summary

ADRA aims to create relationships with affected communities through a community-based approach and mechanism. This will allow communities to understand their own protection needs and risks. To achieve this, ADRA will establish new and strengthen existing Community Based Protection Networks (CBPNs). The main objective of CBPNs is to enhance community-based approach by improving the community's knowledge of protection and the services offered. This will help to identify and ensure that the community is at the center of the protection program. The CBPNs will focus on GBV and CP and general protection and will be structured according to the existing needs of the community. ADRA, in collaboration with CBPNs, will develop awareness plans and disseminate relevant information to enhance community acceptance of the protection activities.
ADRA will provide personalized protection assistance and case management and execute community activities and projects through inter-cluster engagement with WASH and FSL to ensure appropriate access and customized response to address protection needs.
ADRA plans to target several Sudan regions, including Aj Jazirah, Blue Nile, Central Darfur, Khartoum, River Nile, West Darfur, and White Nile. The focus will be on localities with the most urgent protection needs, directed by cluster severity analysis and hotspot mapping. The priority will be for at-risk children and women, older persons, IDPs, IDP returnees and vulnerable crisis-affected host communities without community support, and people with disabilities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

1. SAFE PROGRAMMING/PROTECTION MAINSTREAMING PLAN The ADRA mainstreaming plan involves activities identified during the risk analysis. It details the mainstreaming activities and assists in keeping track of actions being put into practice. The following actions will be taken to ensure the safety and protection of beneficiaries: 1. Internal protection mainstreaming trainings will be conducted regularly for staff.2. Comprehensive assessments will be performed to identify vulnerable groups and evaluate community capacities.3. Active linkages to the Protection Cluster and other actors will be established.4. Feedback mechanisms will be put in place to gather beneficiary feedback and complaints.5. Gender-specific dialogue will be initiated with communities throughout the activity cycle.6. Field staff will be sensitized on protection issues and provided with gender-appropriate behavior change communication (BCC).7. Staff will be familiarized with policies for particular populations.8. Local capacities will be strengthened through supporting local health services and training of local health service staff. 2. EVALUATION Periodic assessment of the quality of the mainstreaming process, implemented by the project against the steps detailed above. In the future, this will include effectively assessing the impact of the Protection Mainstreaming Plan on the protection of the environment to inform future humanitarian interventions.Gender considerations are integrated in all phases of the project. Boys, girls, women, and men will be consulted throughout the project about concerns, specific needs, protection risks, opinions, feedback, and solutions to key issues. All data collected during assessments, monitoring and evaluation will be disaggregated by gender and age. Gender balance is carefully considered in recruitment to deliver services appropriately.All ADRA staff are briefed and trained on prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse. Staff and people seeking access to services are informed with the means to report should there be any cases of sexual exploitation and abuse.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

ADRA is an organization that values the participation of individuals in the decision-making process and actions that affect their lives and communities. ADRA is committed to ensuring that women, girls, and people with disabilities have an opportunity to express their concerns and shape the response, giving them a voice in the decision-making process and the ability to influence corrective actions if necessary. Additionally, ADRA focuses on meeting the unique needs of people with disabilities, older individuals, children at risk, and women by improving interagency referral and response mechanisms to enhance access to multisectoral assistance.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

ADRA is an active member of the Accountability to Affected Populations Working Group led by OCHA and co-chairs the El-Gezira sub-group. Together with other actors ADRA will work on refining and enhancing the different modalities and streams for feedback. The Beneficiary Complaints Feedback and Response Mechanism (CFRM) allows program participants to provide feedback on the design, implementation, systems, and staff of the program, allowing them to influence the decisions related to the program and motivate corrective actions if needed. Depending on the already existing and/or new modes of feedback, the communities will determine to use what best works for them and ADRA will support accordingly and work with the community to provide feedback and corrective action. One of the existing mechanisms that has been successful is the set-up of complaint boxes in centers and health facilities. ADRA also has established hotlines to ensure that complaints that come in through the hotlines are addressed adequately and confidentially. Traditionally, complaints also come through the local leaders, emergency rooms, and community elders, and in this proposed Action, complaints will also come through the CPBNs. ADRA appreciates that the CFRM must be community centered so that community members, especially women and PSNs will have the opportunity to determine any other modalities that suit them best.
