Project Summary

This Project will provide protection assistance in emergency/transition shelters and basic domestic items to internally displaced persons in the North West and Southwest Regions of Cameroon. The project ensure the most vulnerable displaced population and host families are reached with timely, appropriate life-sustaining emergency shelter support, the most vulnerable displaced population and host families are reached with timely, appropriate life-sustaining emergency shelter support, the most vulnerable displaced population are reached with rental subsidies and the most vulnerable displaced population and host families are reached with timely, appropriate life-sustaining with NFIs support
Specifically the project will
1) Meet basic needs and essential services, providing populations with basic domestic items. In this case the project will move from a baseline of 20% of persons in need to 33% of households whose needs for basic and domestic items are met. The project target 12,000 households to be provided with core relief items. 30% of these households will be provided with cash assistance in form of Voucher for NFIs. This represents 3600 household out of the 12,000. A Cash Feasibility study carried out by UNHCR revealed that Vendors of NFIs are familiar with Vouchers, considering that other partners like IRC have implemented Vouchers for NFI and Wash sectors successfully. UNHCR will therefore follow this method and Implement it Via Direct Implementation as a compliment to other shelter/NFI activities by our Implementation partners.
2) Shelter and infrastructure established, improved and maintained . This target reaching 10,000 households with a variety of shelter assistance including emergency shelter kits, rental subsidies and assistance in shelter materials for construction. Rental Subsidies will be provided to Urban IDPs via Cash transfer. UNHCR Cameroon is already in a contractual agreement with MTN Mobile Money, to facilitate cash based assistance. A feasibility studies to assist IDPs in Cash was conducted in November 2019 lead by a UNHCR cash based intervention Officer and a Multi-Functional team. The objectives were to access the feasibility of Vouchers, Cash transfers and Cash in envelop to support victims of SGBV and internally displace persons in need of Shelter and NFI . A market analysis suggested that vendors and suppliers of NFI items were more comfortable with vouchers as many have had previous experience with other projects within the Context of the Crisis. On the other hand, Cash transfers were found to be more suitable for rents assistance for urban IDPs.

This target reaching 10,000 households with a variety of shelter assistance including emergency shelter kits, rental subsidies and assistance in shelter materials for construction Rights Group: Basic Needs and Essential ServicesObjective: Population has sufficient basic and domestic items Problem Assessment, Comprehensive and Prioritized Response Problem Assessment, Internally displaced people in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon fled in despair, leaving everything behind, including basic household items, without means of subsistence . By moving to deep areas of the forest, the majority of these people (more than 80%) have found refuge in crowded makeshift enclosures, deprived of basic utensils. During an assessment carried out in September 2018, IRC found that these displaced people had priority in need of NFIs and shelter assistance. Thus, as part of the HRP / HNO's objective of "providing life-saving emergency assistance to displaced populations in the South West and North West regions, helping to reduce the morbidity and mortality linked to internal displacement ", UNHCR intends to respond to the basic and domestic needs of IDP households through supply, storage, distribution and distribution of NFI kits. Each kit will include 2 jerry cans, 2 mosquito nets, 2 sleeping mats, 2 blankets, 1 set of kitchen utensils and 1 plastic bucket (14l) The priority budget will cover the supply / purchase of NFIs, transport from warehouses to distribution points, distribution costs and post-distribution monitoring. Objective: Shelter and infrastructure established, improved and maintained This crisis has had dramatic and devastating consequences on the lives of IDPs who have lost property. According to several assessments, most IDP communities have settled in very remote forests, where they have no access to adequate shelter, without dignity for their families, or protection from animals and weather. A preliminary report of the joint shelter needs / WASH assessment conducted by REACH, in partnership with local NGOs in the last quarter of 2018, was presented to the shelter cluster on January 16, 2019. One of the main conclusions of the assessment was that a significant number of displaced populations come from communities where significant damage has been caused to homes and properties. In line with the Shelter Cluster's objective of providing vulnerable IDPs with emergency shelter in the short and medium term, UNHCR intends to acquire and distribute shelter kits (intended for approximately 50,000 IDPs) in the priority departments of the cluster, paying particular attention to the most vulnerable IDPs, selected according to the prioritization of vulnerabilities in the 'age, gender and diversity approach of UNHCR. Post distribution monitoring to ensure that assistance is used as intended.
