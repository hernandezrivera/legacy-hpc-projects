Project Summary

The successive years of droughts, harsh climatic conditions and local conflicts has severely aggravated the humanitarian crisis in Beletweyne district. Humanitarian needs have increased drastically due to limited rain, large-scale displacement, lack of access to basic services and, at its root, ongoing conflict. This project aims to reduce the vulnerability of about 24,786 drought and conflict-affected people (9,527 IDPs in Beletweyne town and Mataban and Mahas villages in in Beletweyne District) through provision of life saving safe water, sanitation facilities and hygiene practice for IDP’s and drought vulnerable communities in Beletweyne district by drilling 2 borehole and rehabilitation of protection of 3 strategic boreholes and 10 shallow wells extending the existing distribution network, operate and maintain piped connections to IDPs and disinfection with chlorine will be undertaken and the owners trained on proper chlorination and maintenance. Construction 100 gender friendly latrines and distribution of sanitation and hygiene kits will be part of the activities with main focus on IDPs. WASH committees will also be established/revived/streamlined and trained to strengthen their capacity to promote good hygiene practices and sanitation within their communities and manage the operation and maintenance of the developed/rehabilitated structures.

Project Activities

- Drilling and completion of 2 strategic boreholes with complete solar pumping system and water facilities in Beletweyne district to provide reliable and sustained access to sufficient safe water to IDP’s and most vulnerable host communities. Hydro-geophysical and environmental impact assessment is already done by GSA in the target villages. Construction of water facilities of each borehole will include construction of 30m3 elevated tank, generator house, caretaker room, animal troughs and water kiosks and Lay down a total of 2,800m piping water distribution network. Sites for construction of kiosks will consider do not harm principles to reduce conflict and will be identified in collaboration with women to ensure they are accessible and at secure places to reduce GBV. - Full rehabilitation and protection of 3 strategic boreholes and 10 shallow wells Beletweyne district that were heavily used for water trucking during the drought to serve host communities to provide reliable and sustained safe water access. Currently the target boreholes are operating with difficulties due to poor condition of equipment’s and infrastructures, weak management structures and challenges to access O&M tools. The proposed rehabilitation works include construction or rehabilitation of elevated water tank, provision of the generator set, submersible pump, change of GI pipes, control panel, cables, installing new water meter, PVC distribution water pipes, repair of existing animal troughs and kiosk, repair of the wellhead, the wells will also be fenced to control access and minimize congestion & contamination. Works shall be preceded by detailed technical assessments involving women in survey and design process.- Reduce water point breakdowns by at least 70% through formation and capacity building of 6 WASH committees- Construct 150 VIP gender sensitive latrines with hand washing facility for safe disposal of excreta to prevent AWD and other feacal oral diseases. Each latrine will serve 5 households (WASH minimum standard guideline and SOF). The project will ensure that the beneficiaries participate in sitting of pit latrines to guarantee gender and cultural issues (privacy and safety) are addressed. Designs of the latrines will ensure safety of children, privacy, dignity and protection of women and girls (lockable doors, solar light during night, separate latrines for men and women and marked based on gender), and possibility of desludging. To prevent effects of flooding the floor will be raised 0.3m above the ground level. Local artisans will receive on­the­job training and encouraged to allow women to provide unskilled labor to earn income. The project will support use of locally available materials to cut costs and promote replication.10% of the latrines will be placed in close vicinity to near communal infrastructures (e.g. schools, health center, and mosques).- Distribute of 1500 hygiene promotion kits, hand washing soap and Jerri cans in Beletweyne district.

Latrines will be constructed minimum of 30 person or 5 families for IDPs. (150 Unit).• 24,786 women, men, boys and girl are aware of safe hygiene practices, and the mean to prevent the spread of diarrheal disease through the effort of hygiene promoters.• 70 % of the targeted population will be practicing safe hygiene that includes hand washing with soap at critical times, safe faecal disposal and safe water handling practices and 1500 HH will benefit from hygiene kit. • A post distribution monitoring will be conducted and women, men and children will be consulted to identify the gaps of hygiene and factors that improve hygiene and sanitation practices to the target beneficiaries.

Mitigation Measures

Women, men, girls, and boys, community elders and the local authority within target communities will be directly involved at all stages of the project cycle in order to encourage ownership and participation in program activities. Community structures, networks, and support groups are fundamental to building resilience and are at the heart of the GSA’s programme strategy. Beneficiaries will be fully aware on the scope of the project and expected deliverables. GSA shall apply gender segregated community consultation meetings enabling men, women and girls to address their specific concerns, prioritize their needs and appropriately inform the development of proposed activities. To avoid any conflict or misunderstanding from arising the community members will be consulted during the selection of WASH committee’s members. At the initial stage of the project, GSA will organize and conduct community mobilization and sensitization with all relevant stakeholders to officially introduce the project and create awareness on project activities. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholder including M&amp;E roles will be discussed and agreed. GSA will also ensure that feedback and accountability mechanisms are integrated into organizations’ strategies, monitoring and evaluations, recruitment, staff inductions and training and this will be highlighted in reporting. The project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be sensitized on how to use the complaint /feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. Effective information sharing and communication channels will be promoted. Sharing information about GSA activities with local authorities, community leaders/representatives, the beneficiaries and other relevant stakeholders in a timely, accessible and inclusive way will allow communities to be in a position to understand and shape decisions that impact their lives as well as facilitate dialogue. GSA will adhere the principles of "do no Harm" through not creating any partial, nepotism, conflict oriented and sensitive issues within the project effaced population and beneficiaries.
