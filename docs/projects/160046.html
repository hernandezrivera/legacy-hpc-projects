Project Summary

The Somalia Humanitarian Fund (SHF) is a multi-donor country-based pooled fund that
was established in 2010 to support the timely allocation and disbursement of donor
resources to address the most urgent humanitarian needs in Somalia. The SHF has been
essential in enabling timely, coordinated and effective humanitarian action due to its
distinct comparative advantages – the unearmarked nature of the Fund; the established
and functioning accountability systems; integration within the existing coordination
systems; and flexibility.
