Project Summary

SOP intended by this project is to respond to the core needs of the BNFs in the targeted areas by providing (Core NFI Kits / Kitchen Kits / Winter clothing kits for both adults and Kids). It comprises essential NFI items. to conflict and earthquake-affected populations across Northwest Syria, for new displacements, residents in informal and formal camps, to meet the basic and NFI-related needs of vulnerable Syrian IDP families in Idlib (Dana, Ariha sub-district) through targeted 2,000 families (10.000 for NFI and 3800 for Winter Kits).
Winter Kits: Targeting 2000 Children and 1800 Adults / Adult kit estimated cost 33$ Children's Kit 28$
Initially, SOP will start this project by conducting needs assessments in the targeted locations in coordination with the SNFI cluster to identify the needs, gaps, risk factors, and capacities of the targeted populations, then submitting the needs assessment reports to donors. Based on the findings of the conducted needs, SOP will allocate final quantities of Kits to be distributed in the targeted location aiming to partially meet the pressing needs. Afterward, the allocated quantities of the kits will be transported gradually to SOP’s warehouses in the targeted locations, which are already identified, and well prepared for storing the NFI kits until all the quantities are distributed to beneficiaries.
SOP already has full access to targeted locations and coordinates regularly with local authorities on the ground; nevertheless, SOP will inform the local stakeholders of the proposed project in order to ensure a smooth implementation of the project and better identification of the most vulnerable groups who will be targeted with the assistance.
SOP usually conducts any distribution following a specific cycle that starts with SOP’s M&E teams visiting the targeted location, preparing initial beneficiaries lists aligned with donor vulnerability criteria, and sharing it with the SOP program team to be endorsed. Then, SOP distributes receipt vouchers, to the identified beneficiaries, which clarify the type of emergency kit\s that they will receive the contents of each kits (materials and quantities), the beneficiary’s ID number, and the signature of the field project manager. Afterward, SOP hands over the kit\s to the beneficiaries who have previously received receipt vouchers and asks them to sign the receipt vouchers, which will be restored by SOP to be documented later.
Finally, and along with this process, SOP uses also the beneficiaries’ distribution lists during the distribution that are used to collect beneficiaries' HHs data (Age, ID number, family members according to age and sex, vulnerability status, contents of the emergency kits, and signature of beneficiaries and field project manager).
Putting protection mainstreaming in place, SOP recruits female and male teams whenever it carries out any kind of distributions, where these teams distribute the kits for women and men separately in two locations in order to avoid exposing women and girls to any potential assault or harassment. Additionally, SOP sets up appropriate beneficiary feedback mechanisms through which affected populations can measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints.
In coordination with donors, SOP will start distributing the NFI kits, Kitchen kits and winter clothing kits, to the identified beneficiaries. The type of kit\s that will be distributed to each HHs will depend on the beneficiary's HHs’ needs.
During the distribution SOP’s M&E team will conduct regular distribution monitoring and PDMs and will submit them to donors on a monthly basis, as well as, SOP’s project manager will send donor narrative reports, beneficiaries lists, human interest stories HISs, and media documentation including videos and photographs of the whole project stages.
