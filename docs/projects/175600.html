Project Summary

The project, to be implemented in the three provinces of Cabo Delgado, Niassa, and Nampula, aims to provide displaced and vulnerable young adolescent girls (10-14 years) and women of reproductive age (15-49) in the Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) relocation sites and host communities, with life-saving and life-sustaining Sexual, Reproductive, Maternal, and Newborn health (SRMNH) services.
The project, to be implemented through Government and Civil Society implementing partners, aims to provide immediate life-saving access to SRMNH services guided by IASC Minimum Initial Service Package for Reproductive Health principles.
The project will also equip health facilities in the 3 provinces with essential reproductive health commodities and supplies in order to establish sufficient capacity for provision of Emergency Obstetric Care to prevent avoidable Maternal and Newborn deaths.
Lastly, this project will establish capacity for and support skilled birth attendance through training of reproductive health service providers in the three target provinces.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project, to be implemented in the three provinces of Cabo Delgado, Niassa, and Nampula, aims to provide displaced and vulnerable young adolescent girls (10-14 years) and women of reproductive age (15-49) in the Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) relocation sites and in host communities, with access to life-saving and life-sustaining Sexual, Reproductive, Maternal, and Newborn health (SRMNH) services. The project, to be implemented through Government and Civil Society implementing partners, aims to provide immediate life-saving access to SRMNH services guided by IASC Minimum Initial Service Package for Reproductive Health principles. The project will also equip health facilities in the 3 provinces with essential reproductive health commodities and supplies in order to establish sufficient capacity for provision of Emergency Obstetric Care to prevent avoidable Maternal and Newborn deaths. Lastly, this project will establish capacity for and support skilled birth attendance through training of reproductive health service providers in the three target provinces.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Conduct 2 integrated Mobile Health Brigades per week in each of the Districts hosting IDPs in the 3 provinces.2. Equip Health Facilities providing Sexual and Reproductive Health Services in the 3 provinces with essential reproductive health medical supplies and equipment3. Install and equip containerized units in health facilities in the 3 provinces for provision of Sexual and Reproductive Health Services.3. Support deployment of Maternal and Newborn (MNH) Nurses to Health Facilities providing SRH services in the 3 provinces.4. Provide support to strengthen the referral system for emergency obstetric cases in the remote areas with difficult access in the 3 provinces5. Conduct information sessions on SRH to increase awareness and access to SRH information and services6. Conduct capacity building training in the 3 provinces on the Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) and other key SRH skill areas including Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (EmONC), Contraceptive Technique Update (CTU), Clinical Management of Rape (CMR) etc

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

- To collect data on vulnerable groups such as pregnant women, breastfeeding women, women with disability, people living with HIV/AIDS and develop protection mechanisms- Gender mainstreaming and advocating for gender-responsive interventions across different clusters to prevent and respond to Gender-Based Violence- To involve the target population in every phase of project implementation and to use the feedback mechanism as part of communication with the affected population.- Cognisant of the need to involve men indecisions on family planning, the project aims to educate men on the benefits of family planning in order not to expose women to potential violence from male partners and spouses.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

In order to ensure complete access to sexual and reproductive health services, the project is designed to take these SRH services to the populations in remote locations through Mobile Brigades and mobile health units. The Mobile Brigades will visit relocation sites for internally displaced persons and remote villages to take SRH services to the women and girls. Sexual and Reproductive Health volunteers and activists will be used to support in identification and encouragement of visibly pregnant mothers to access Maternal Health services, and women and girls to access family planning and other SRH services.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

- To collect sex and age disagregated data (SADD) including data on people with disability (and their specific location at the IDPs camps)- To involve the peer group of people with disability in every activity to reach the targets because the peer group usually know the locations and people with disability will feel more comfortable to talk with their peers- To reach people with disability through mobile brigade services, home visits (visit the tents) for the people with difficulties to come to the health facilities- To modify and to adapt the IEC materials and information session’s methodology and to reach the target based on the type of disability (voice messages for people with visual impairment, using sign language, etc)- To consult with the group for additional items needed at the dignity kits to meet their specific needs such as ‘potty’, pampers? (for people with difficulties accessing toilet)
