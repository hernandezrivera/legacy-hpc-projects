Project Summary

The project endeavors to implement complemented activities in the humanitarian field in western rural of Aleppo province. This intervention will strengthen permanent protection assistance and services in IDP sites through the establishment of facilities inside and/or in close proximity to IDP sites and make a contribution towards Life-saving humanitarian assistance and protection to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on underserved areas with high severity of needs depending on the expansion of the activities of two existing protection centers WGSS specialized in GBV in Atareb and Kafr Karmin villages.
The project includes the following activities:
- Forming of three mobile teams to provide of psychological first aid (PFA), information/awareness, individual and emergency case management support, life skills program, GBV services, referrals to GBV services and other services. The teams will cover the Daret Azza and Atareb sub-district in rural Aleppo in addition to the surrounding camps. (every sub-team will consist of two mobile teams, two social workers and case management workers).
- Support the case management activities (Case fund) for 150 survivors. This assistance will be provided in kind and cash. The estimated amount for each beneficiary is 100 USD. Based on Cash Assistance in GBV Case Management Guidance Note June 2019.By GBV-sub cluster.
- Engaging 1760 women and girls with vocational activities in WGSS and ten point in camps.
- Establishment of eleven protection desks in camps inside and/or near IDP sites to provide specialized and non-specialized protection services, PSS support, Individual Protection Assistance and Emergency Case Fund in IDP sites.
- Provide 550 men, women, girls and boys with disabilities reached with protection services to specialized and non-specialized services like (peer-support, awareness raising, skills development, PSS, case management and Ambassador's programme to increase PwD participation in humanitarian action).
This intervention will support 23,715 beneficiaries affected by the Syrian crisis (4982 men,8414 women, 4364 boys and 5955 girls, 550 PWD) in Atareb and Daret Azza - sub-districts by promoting their accessibility to the protection assistance significantly. The project is designed to fit and be flexible to deal with conditions of constant changes and continuous of flows of IDPs who move between several locations.
Also, the project team will ensure data protection, confidentiality at all stages, protocols of information sharing and acquiring informed consent from beneficiaries. The project activities will be in line with Protection Cluster Recommendations, SOPs, Inclusive COVID-19 response - NWS Disability - TWG on Inclusion Guidance Note April 2020 and GBV SC guidance note on GBV service provision in the time of COVID-19 such as Hand sanitizing when conducting the sessions/ Gathering of five beneficiaries per session and social distance/Measure the temperature for each beneficiary as a precaution/ Plan has been put for online sessions/ Put awareness posters about Corona disease during sessions.
MASRRAT has been in the protection sector for seven years. MASRRAT is a focal point to provide GBV services in Jebel Seman district.
This project will complement the ongoing UNFPA project through covering of more area of intervention in Jebel Samaan ,where the two teams will work together under the same management and technical support, also the project will support the centers by vocational activity (sewing).
Each outreach team will be accompanied by a case manager who identifies the cases and coordinates with the GBV technical supervisor on UNFPA project to receive the cases in WGSS centers.

4 - Needs

MASRRAT conducted rapid need assessment to determine the best practices and most suitable methodologies to support the families in the protection areas in Jebel Saman in rural of Aleppo where the KIIs mentioned that: women and children are the most vulnerable groups or community, especially when it is related to the families' needs and the lack of work opportunities and the absence of many breadwinners, and 70% of women Headed-households suffer from lack of access to the basic food needs and non-food assistance especially older woman or women with special needs.35% of reviewed families have people with disabilities in a range of 1 person per family. Most of them are in need of protection support and social inclusion. Also, the KIIs stated that the most common problems that the women and girls faced are the lack of security, traditions and customs, early marriage, deprivation of the rights of education and inheritance, in addition to the parent's and husbands' control of their wives and girls.- About 51% of women and girls faced circumstances that prevent them to reach the protection services, such as transportation, family’s prevention, and traditions and customs in the region.- More than half of IDPs women and girls suffer from the lack of private sleeping arrangements, bathrooms, etc. People without civil documents face challenges in getting assistance.- Women and children are the most vulnerable groups or community, especially when it is related to the families' needs and the lack of work opportunities and the absence of the breadwinner, and half of women Headed households suffer from lack of access to the basic food needs and non-food assistance especially older woman or women with special needs.

5 - Implementation of activities

This project will support 12,925 beneficiaries affected by the Syrian crisis (2982 men,4414 women, 2224 boys and 2955 girls, 350 PWD) in Atareb and Daret Azza - sub-districts by promoting their accessibility to the protection assistance significantly.MASRRAT trained specialists will provide specialized services, structured and recreational psychosocial support (PSS), life skills sessions, awareness sessions, Psychological First Aid (PFA) and dissemination of information about services to HC and IDPs. The specialized services include case management, individual and group counseling, and referrals. The WGSS centers will provide static support that provides more specialized services than the mobile teams. Case management will include direct support using the appropriate case management tools (Informed Consent Form, Safety plan form, Suicide prevention form). Monitoring of activities will be aligned with agreed work plans, the log frame, and indicators performance tracking table (IPTT), and every activity related to this project will be documented and coded and all the staff will be trained in collecting data, protection-sensitive/issues, sharing information, and documenting.The project team will ensure data protection, confidentiality at all stages, protocols of information sharing and acquiring informed consent from beneficiaries. The project activities will be in line with Protection Cluster Recommendations, SOPs, Inclusive COVID-19 response - NWS Disability - TWG on Inclusion Guidance Note April 2020 and GBV SC guidance note on GBV service provision in the time of COVID-19 such as Hand sanitizing when conducting the sessions/ Gathering of five beneficiaries per session and social distance/Measure the temperature.
