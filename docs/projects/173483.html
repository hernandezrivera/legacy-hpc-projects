Project Summary

Due to our ongoing project under project number: SUD-19/HSD20/SA1/P/NGO/12946 funded by SHF under Protection sector (CP & GBV), the project name: (Strengthening resilience and empowerment of IDPs and vulnerable host community women, girls, and boys in the East Darfur States), which is serving IDPs, Refugees, and host communities.
The project targeted IDP, Refugees, and Host communities, the project serves about 35,500 individual in East Darfur by providing vocational training for 600 HH and planting 75,000 fruit trees, and constructed one CMC but it needs some additional unties like ( Walls, Latrines and to completed the local materials by permanent, Furniture and other facilities), also the project has provided a birth certificate for 265 children and engaged 300 children to the Schools and provide recreational materials for 2 Schools), besides that 420 people received PSS and 12,000 people received awareness on COVID-19 through Megaphone and IEC materials. Also, the project provides a Literacy course for the parent, 350 got ( Emergency Literacy Math ) ELM. The project was operated in Eldeain Locality but there is still a big gap in Eldaein and many other localities.
Due to the Social attitude of pastoralists, there is frugal violence against women and girls has reportedly intensified, consequent to the fragile economic and social structures, It has been proven many youths are not married, or the young girls marred just for a dowry, also when we look at the scope with low incomes; men unemployment results, which often catalyzes violent behaviors.
The main goal for this project is: to contribute to reduce the violence especially for the women and children and support the parent to increase their income and get knowledge in business skills, also to reduce child labor by supporting the children to join schools.
Project activities:
1. Provide livelihood assistance to 800 women households in handcraft and vocational training skills.
2. Provide Psychosocial Support and case Management for 600 women
3. Provide 8 Dressing machine for the Community Multipurpose Center in the Khore-Omer IDPs camp.
4. Provide 4 Bakeries for the women groups to assist their income and reduce economic inflation and contribute to the climatic change by reducing the cutting of trees by using the Environmental Friendly Stove ( EFS) by training 800 women in manufacturing and use the EFS.
5. Provide direct cash assistance to support the startup inputs for the most venerable host-community and IDPs camp. For the women groups through groups for one time as start-up kick. To procure the handcraft inputs.
6. Constriction the wall of Community Multipurpose Center and support the Child-Friendly Space by recreational materials
7. Provide the Women Center by public Latrine
8. Provide birth certificate for 500 child
9. Support 600 child to join the school
10. Training parents in outreach component of Early Literacy and Math (ELM) course by training them on simple ELM course to guide them in developing their child skills at home, Four-course 400 person.
11. Provide the CMC with Suitable Coved-19 preparedness and facilities to hosting the training.
12. Provide awareness-raising for the IDP, Host communities on, HIV/ADIS, and Drugs.

1 - Needs

Sudan covers a large protracted humanitarian crisis over the past 16th years ago, the severity and scale of needs remain far-reaching from the actual assistance needs. Although there has been an improvement in the security situation after the signed Joba Peace Agreement (JPA), but new displacement continues both from refugees and IDPs in some other area in Sudan. Still, there are more than About 9.3 million people live in protracted displacement camps in Sudan, especially in the Darfur States. (Humanitarian Overview 4 December 2019). The ongoing economic crisis continues to be a key driver of deteriorating humanitarian needs, which have been exacerbated by COVID-19. Control and containment measures increase the cost and time taken to deliver the basic humanitarian service. Flooding is a seasonal occurrence in Sudan and contributes to shelter and NFI needs as protection echo in very high. Heavy rains caused damage to houses, livestock, farmland, infrastructure, and services. Flood water and damage were recorded high in all States in Sudan. Inflation and currency depreciation have led to shortages of fuel and cash, price increases of imported key commodities, a reduced household purchasing power.There is a need for durable solutions (return and urban integration) for IDPs and returnees alike including a need to provide environmentally friendly transitional shelters, together with community training on building techniques and livelihood opportunities to promote self-reliance and resilience. Such as brick making, the weaving of traditional mats, provision of fuel-efficient stoves, and basic repairs. However, there are also negative coping mechanisms used by people in need of other services, such as health or education and livelihood intervention.Drugs and child labor is found with unregistered children and sexual harassment at the IDP and refugee camps.There is a gap in the parent outreach component for the Early Literacy and Math (ELM) course which improves the parent knowledge to reduce child abuse at home or at schools with support the parent to take their children to the schools. And to be aware of the risk of early marriage.
