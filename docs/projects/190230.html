Project Summary

The project intends to create a space that promotes the complete health (physical and psychological) of children and women; where they can feel protected, cared for, educated and able to overcome and to prevent different traumatic situations (armed conflicts, natural disasters, gender violence, and other) through educational and recreational activities.
The Ibo Foundation intends with this project to continue the work of promoting the living conditions of children, young people and women on the island of Ibo, through a set of actions aimed at boosting self-esteem, equality and livelihoods.
At CANI - Centro de Apoio Nutricional do Ibo/Ibo Nutritional Support Centre - weekly thematic lectures will be given to women (GBV, marriage and premature pregnancy, hygiene, health, nutrition,...). Pregnant and lactating women who are already assisted in terms of nutrition at CANI will also participate in this activity. At the same time, training and guidance for their empowerment will be organized.
Children from 2 to 10 years old will have a space on the CANI facilities where they will develop playful and educational activities that promote comprehensive health: physical, psychic and psychosocial – filling in the gap of short school hours and no crèches or pre-primary education in the area.
Likewise, daily activities for children/adolescents aged 10 to 18 will be organized in the EOI space.
From time to time, activities will be organized for groups of different age groups that include information, training and recreation.
The selection criteria will prioritize vulnerable people, with 50% of the participants being female and at least 30% displaced due to the armed conflict.
Prevent protection risks (including CP & GBV) of most affected and vulnerable populations in priority areas through enhanced preparedness and resilience capacity.

Ensure access of children, girls and women to friendly and secure spaces.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activities with the community and local authorities:- Presentation of the project to the community;- Quarterly meetings with community leaders and district authorities (SDSMA, SAAJ);- Information campaigns on the rights of children and women, especially in GBV and PSEA in the 3 districts of Ibo;- Conducting and making available a survey on the project and Foundation;Activities with women and young adults:- Literacy and math activities;- Recreational activities (handicrafts and crafts)- Empowerment activities through lectures and training in specific subjects;- Training and awareness in gender, nutrition, childcare, sexual / reproductive education, breastfeeding / pregnancy; - Awareness and information activities on GBV, PSEA;- Creation of a “Women's Association” that represents their rights in the community;Activities with children and adolescents:- Intellectual development activities through games;- Playful activities that increase creativity and motor skills.- School reinforcement in mathematics, literacy and reading.- Positive leisure such as: Theatre group, Painting/craft group, sports, videos,...;-Lectures with debate on the rights of children and adolescents: sexual education, violence and forms of abuse, early or forced marriage, pregnancy and use of contraceptives.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Various systems will be followed to identify potential risks to beneficiaries:- Carry out semi-annual evaluation and monitoring surveys;- Activate an anonymous suggestions/complaints box;- Permanent consultation with beneficiary groups during and through the activities;- Meetings with community leaders and community representatives;- Visits by activists/monitors to neighborhoods.All incidents will be recorded in order to find solutions for each problem identified and meet the needs of affected groups.To encourage participation in the project and encompass all those in need, dissemination campaigns will be carried out at different points of public access. Visits will also be made to homes in each neighbourhood to identify possible beneficiaries.Community leaders and different government bodies will also be informed about the project.The infrastructures selected to host the activities of this project are located next to the main infrastructures and public services. Any of the neighbourhoods is located nearby and has routes that do not go through military occupation or prohibited areas on the island. On the other hand, in additionto being guarded by guards, the spaces are fully walled and fenced in order to guarantee the safety and even privacy of all beneficiaries.The attendance of beneficiaries to the project will be promoted through different types of incentives, such as awards at the end of activities, food support, organization of events, etc.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The Ibo foundation has been working on Ibo since 2008 in permanent contact with local authorities, administrative services, religious and community leaders and, above all, with the community in general. All of its projects are designed together with different key actors, in which women, children and adolescents are represented. The foundation's experience and permanent presence on theisland of Ibo allow us to address the needs and problems of the community.Every 3 months, meetings are held in order to share the evolution of the project, its successes and failures. There will be meetings with religious and community leaders, with the Women's Association (created with the project), and with groups of adolescents. The meetings do not take place at thesame time as the most unfavorable groups end up not having a voice when in the presence of others.At the same time, the safe spaces created will have an inquiry always available to the community in general, to ascertain the degree of satisfaction with the Foundation, its members and this concrete project. These can be delivered in a box with complete anonymity.On the other hand, the project has an individual assistance office for women as well as children and adolescents. Here, each beneficiary, but also other members of the community, can be heard and assisted in a personalized way and with absolute secrecy.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Each project activist is responsible for weekly visits to three different families from the 3 neighbourhoods of Ibo (Cimento, Rituto, Cumuamba). These visits are intended to identify new beneficiaries, both female and male, as well as to raise awareness of project participation. At the same time, the leaders of each neighbourhood notify the activists of people with special needs.Priority in selection is given to the most vulnerable and minority people. That is, people with special needs, pregnant women, nursing mothers, orphans or other specific cases with needs such as displaced people, who are in greater numbers every day on the island.The inclusion of all people with special needs will be promoted and, as far as possible, activities will be adapted to their specificity.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Sensitization and awareness about respect for the environment. All waste resulting from the Foundation's projects are properly separated (each infrastructure has its own and properly identified locations), and then taken to the appropriate and prepared location on the island for this purpose. However, in all actions, the "three Rs" of waste management are promoted and taught: Reduce, Reuse and Recycle. In this context, some of the activities with children and teenagers are planned with only recycled materials. As a matter of principle, the Foundation gives preference to local products. If there is no product, the nearest market is used (the other islands, Pemba or Nampula), transporting the goods whenever possible by traditional sailing vessels (dhow). Also, at the level of employees, local people areprivileged in order to reduce the impact and expenses with travel.All the infrastructures that will be used for the project (CANI and EOI) are supplied with a rainwater collection system and its distribution so as not to overexploit the Ibo aquifer and preserve the water. Periodically, the Foundation's maintenance technician cleans and disinfects the tanks to ensure water quality. Likewise, the facilities are also provided with sanitation through latrines connected to septic tanks duly located and periodically cleaned.
