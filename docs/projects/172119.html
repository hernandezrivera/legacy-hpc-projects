Project Summary

This project is a fully-funded, multi-year project implemented from 01.05.2020 to 30.04.2022. All budgetary figures, targets, beneficiary data given here apply to the 12-month HRP 2021 period only.
Objective: Improvement of the capacities of 3 hospitals and a local civil society organization, to provide basic care through 22,000 consultations to children and pregnant/lactating women in Maracaibo.
Context and Needs:
UNHCR reports that with 4.5 million Venezuelans now living abroad this is the largest exodus in the region’s recent history. The INFORM index measures Venezuela's risk of humanitarian crisis in 2020 as medium at 4.5/10. The Humanitarian Response Plan 2020 identified 7 million Venezuelan people in need of assistance with 4.5M targeted for assistance. This project focusses on key sectors and target groups and is in line with the health cluster Indicators for 2021
The current situation in Venezuela is the result of a protracted socio-economic and political crisis. This has led to a collapse of services, deterioration of living conditions, and mass displacement. The population is increasingly poor while at the same time hyperinflation have led to limited access to food, medicines and other basic goods. The result is widespread disease, vulnerable to epidemic outbreaks, malnutrition, crime and high mortality rates.
In Maracaibo, people able to move, including up to 70% of medical personnel, have left. Health institutions are assisting with 20% or less of their previous capacity. Health infrastructure is deteriorating; electricity and water are often cut for days. Fuel, which was already difficult to buy before the COVID-19 pandemic, becomes even more scarce limiting the access to health services. (Source: MI Needs Assessment Dec. 2019 and partner reports 2020)
A main challenge identified by the health sector is the capacity of hospitals with shortcomings in their equipment, reduced human resources, low range of Telehealth or Teleassistance, a disjointed reference system and limited capacity of the outpatient system.
Target group (Based on:
Considered are gender/age marker as well as vulnerability criteria in accordance with the Humanitarian Response Plan Venezuela 2020 the Global Humanitarian Response Plan COVID-19) and the Venezuela COVID-19 Preparedness and Response Plan
- Children up to 14 years with focus on children <5 (52% are male and 48% female). The HDI under-five mortality rate has increased from 16.6 to 30.9 per 1,000 live births within 6 years. Often it is those children or teenagers taking care of their siblings while the parents are working abroad.
- Lactating/pregnant women from the low to middle-income catchment area of the hospitals, amongst them a high percentage of teenage mothers depending on medical services that are close and easily accessible.
- These target groups include approx. 6% of people with disabilities and 12,7% of people recognizing themselves as belonging to the indigenous population.
- This project also specifically targets women as decision makers building the capacity of the female-headed civil society organization, including three hospitals with strong female representation in higher management positions.
- Considering the current COVID-19 pandemic, this project also targets health care personnel not having access to personal protective equipment and modes to implement proper IPC.
- During medical outreach in communities with high vulnerability criteria, the medical team from the paediatric hospitals attends all patients in need, including approx. 4% people aged 50 and higher.
In summary, this constitutes approx. 21.600 consultations through the 3 pediatric hospitals, one including a maternal ward in 2021, 600 consultations of pregnant/lactating women receiving nutritional support in 2021 and 360 health personnel in 3 hospitals

During the assessments in the hospitals interviews were conducted to compile the information and design the project according to the necessities.

4.2. ¿El proyecto establece estructuras / mecanismos accesibles, confiables y confidenciales para recibir comentarios y quejas de la comunidad durante la implementación, y utilizar esa información para adaptar el proyecto?

Yes

4.2.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

This project follows a participatory approach, where the hospitals affected by the crises are involved in project design and in all other phases of the project cycle. This will be achieved through open exchange during stakeholder meetings/round tables as well as formal and informal beneficiary feedback. The same mechanisms will be used to channel information about the project and its activities towards all stakeholders ensuring its accountability. Additionally, anonymous feedback surveys are conducted regularly with patients and the health care personnel working in the three hospitals. This feedback is included in monthly project reports, addressed by project management, discussed between the partner organizations and stakeholder hospitals and adaptations carried out when necessary. Responses and adaptations are in turn communicated to the beneficiaries and stakeholders.

5 - CENTRALIDAD DE LA PROTECCION
5.1. ¿El proyecto analiza y busca abordar los riesgos de protección, incluyendo los riesgos enfrentados por personas generalmente más vulnerables (género, edad, discapacidad)?

Yes

5.1.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Sensitivity to protection issues and where possible referrals or creation of synergies with other programs is a cross-cutting issue in this project. The sector specifically mentions the need to strengthen and systematize the processes of referral to specialized services for children and their families. It is planned to sensitize the medical personnel and project staff towards patients with heightened needs in the area of protection and inclusion.

5.2. ¿El proyecto garantiza la seguridad y la dignidad de las personas beneficiadas y tiene en cuenta el principio “No hacer daño”?

Yes

5.2.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

The context is very sensitive with various overlapping vulnerabilities requiring assessing of the effects of each step taken within the project implementation. To identify any gaps in the initial do-no-harm analysis and rectify them in their early stages, close coordination with all stakeholders, participation and a functioning M&E and feedback mechanism are indispensable. This includes the reduction of potential access barriers. Thereby, the trustful dialogue with the hospitals and the target group are important factors in reducing conflicts or negative side effects.Additionally, hospitals and partnering civil society organizations carefully consider the coordination and information flow. Procurement procedures consider the dynamics of the local market to make informed decisions. Workplace safety is integrated in this project in order to provide a safe, appropriate and inclusive environment.

5.3. ¿El proyecto identifica las barreras y asegura accesibilidad universal e imparcial a la ayuda y los servicios por los diferentes grupos de población?

Yes

5.3.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

It is ensured within this project that all patients in need of medical care are accepted to the hospitals. All three targeted hospitals are public hospitals. The focus on pediatric hospitals, one also including maternal care, prioritizes vulnerable target groups to have access to the humanitarian activities. The physical access to the health facilities and appropriate appliances in the wards and their WASH infrastructure are included in the project design. Due to the diminishing of public transport and inflating fuel prizes, medical outreach to underserved parts of Maracaibo was included in the project, thus also reaching people with mobility restrictions and some above 50, which are treated despite the pediatric specialization. Also, in these parts of town, a high percentage of indigenous population could be observed.

5.4. ¿El proyecto incorpora un sistema para prevenir y combatir la explotación y los abusos sexuales (PEAS) que incluye una estrategia de sensibilización y mecanismos apropiados de denuncia?

Yes

5.4.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Sensitivity to SEA is included within the project design as cross-cutting issue. It is planned to sensitize the medical personnel towards SEA within their own as well as patient environments. Reporting and referral lines will be strengthened. The anonymous feedback surveys also include the topic, which will raise red flags within the monitoring systems in case issues are prevalent.

6.3.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Access for people with mobility limitations and other disabilities is eased through the provision of medical services by a mobile team installed with personnel of the partnering hospitals, equipment and transport provided by the implementing partner organization. During rehabilitation and construction activities inclusion and accessibility is also considered and included in the designs. Also, it is planned to sensitize the medical personnel and project staff towards inclusion of persons with disabilities through targeted capacity building.

7.1.1. Explique brevemente (máximo 1.000 carácteres)

This project aims to support and not replace the public health structures having localization strongly engraved in its design as it brings a strategic advantage to the program implementation.
