Project Summary

As north Syrian know is crowded with huge numbers of both host and refugees people reached to more than 3 million people, lead to limitation of income resources, caused huge demands for food, as the agriculture production is the main resource of food in the area, which basically depends on availability of water for the irrigation. Most of the farmers in this region used under ground water as main source for the irrigation, but because of high cost of pumping water to be used in irrigation enforced the farmers to use alternative source such as untreated wastewater to irrigate their crops. This process contributed to high level cases of water borne diseases (WBDs)
The project interventions will be in these main sectors:
- Water crisis management
- Community infrastructure
- Local governance and capacity building.
for this reason, the project targeting Azaz as high priority community, located in Aleppo governorate, since most of the farmers using wastewater to irrigate their crops, for conducting compatible panoramic interventions in this region starting form wastewater treatment, alignment with Syrian context; whereas lack of electricity/energy, unavailability of operational costs of projects and institutional public services bodies. Based in this situation, and for more sustainability, the best way to achieve this goal is to use anaerobic wastewater treatment methodology such as anaerobic baffled reactor (ABR). The ABR are septic tanks that have been upgraded with a series of baffles along the treatment chamber. The up-flow chambers provide enhanced removal and digestion of organic matter. As septic tanks, ABR are based on a physical treatment (settling) and a biological treatment (anaerobic digestion).

4 - Needs

The Syrian conflict is one of the most significant conflicts of the twenty-first century, unfortunately, it entered its eleventh year without finding opportunities to end hostilities and achieve peace , this conflict is accompanying with displacing of thousands of people, and this process leads to the changes in the environment with all components, now around of three and half millions of people are living in a small area and using the small amount of the available clean water for drinking and irrigation and drain eighty percentage of their drinking water as a wastewater to the open outlets, in addition to that, much of the water resources in northern Syria are used inefficiently and uneconomically. Diseases such as typhoid, fever and parasitic infections not only cause suffering but also have enormous economic disadvantages for those infected in the governorate of Azaz district, where the inhabitants are still suffering from water borne diseases (WBD), EWARN (Early Warning Alerts and Response Network) recorded in the last year was 72,689 cases of WBDs and 1758 cases of leishmaniasis. Climate change, high population growth and increasing urbanization in north Aleppo increase the pressure on water resources, resulting in localized groundwater depletion and pollution, that is driving the need for innovative and sustainable solutions to provide water for all purposes, alternative water sources are required to meet current and future needs.
