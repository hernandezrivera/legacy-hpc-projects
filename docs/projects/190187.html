Project Summary

The project addresses MHPSS and SRHR of people with disabilities, young girls / women affected by conflict in Cabo Delgado / Nampula Provinces. It builds the capacity of MH workers, equips local resource persons to provide MHPSS and SRH to local populations in PHC, Education, case identification, treatment, and referrals.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

REPSSI is a member of IASC, the Interagency Standing Committee on MHPSS. All our interventions and tools are guided by the do no harm principle that are stated on the agency´s manual. In addition to that, all REPSSI materials and tools undergo through a development, testing and review process that ensures that the end receptients of the interventions will not suffer re-traumatization as result of the intervention. Our staff and volunteers, are exceptionally trained and prepared to strictly observe these international and internally agreed norms. The mains objective of REPSSI intervention is to empower beneficiaries to become skilled, knowledgeable and resilient and be prepared to thrive. REPSSI has policies and procedures to identify risks, including country and organizational risk registers. The organization pays attention to possible risks and works hard to avoid these and the harm that could come from them. For instance, REPSSI adheres to the international guidelines and standards on PSEA. The organization has undergone PSEA assessment by UNICEF, has developed a PSEA policy, reporting processes and it is making sure that all staff do complete training on PSEA and adhere to the standards. Reporting mechanisms and safety measures to protect both the victims and those who report cases of violations have been developed. We have guidelines containing clear paths of what needs to be done in such cases and circumstances.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The development of the project was based on the information that is collected systematically by our REPSSI project officer based in Cabo Delgado. As well as those shared by our implementing partners and other stakeholders partnering with REPSSI in that province. However, once the project will be awarded to REPSSI, the organization will conduct a more in depth assessment and hold additional consultative processes as stated in the initial pages of this document. Feedback mechanisms take into a consideration a variety of aspects, including getting information from people with disability and those who are illiterate. This includes interviews, focus group discussions, monitoring processes such as visits to beneficiaries and other stakeholders and other forms of getting formal feedback using appropriate monitoring and evaluation formats. A steering committee that includes a variety of project stakeholders, including girls, boys and women, with whom REPSSI will consult about and to whom REPSSI will account for the project result will be created The proposed project will employ local people as activistas to work directly with local communities, have community conversations and encourage communities to support girls and women to access support. The project will train local government officials, including in the provision of services that have integrated psychosocial support and ensure that those with disability, young girls and others that are often marginalized are supported to access MHPSS and SRH services

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Our action focuses on inclusion of people with disability, including community awareness and education in regard to the needs and challenges of people living with disabilities. REPSSI does include training for its implementing staff to work with communities to identify and find appropriate ways for the inclusion of people with disabilities. On preparing events, such as trainings or any other activities, identification of participants with disabilities, as well as suitable venues and activities that allow inclusion are of utmost importance. People with disabilities will be identified by local community members and leaders, referred to trained and supported activistas and linked to services. People with disabilities will be encouraged to form support groups and to support one another to access services and support, including cash transfers from this project.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

This project has no direct impact on the environment, however during our interventions we will orient staff to observe some measures that could contribute to environmental degradation, like excessive use of sanitation material when accommodating in hotel, proper management of waste such as used plastic and other personal commodities, and also educate populations to improve their own surroundings by for example taking care of or planting trees.
