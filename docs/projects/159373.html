Project Summary

INTERSOS humanitarian aid organization proposes to undertake a life-saving and sustainable assistance to displacement affected communities through provision of emergency shelter kits (ESKs), non-food items (NFIs) and transitional shelters. The displacement affected communities herein is referred to as the internally displaced persons and Somali refugee returnees.
The proposed project aims at providing standard NFI and (ESKs) for the newly internally displaced persons and transitional shelters for the protracted IDPs and refugee returnees with secure land tenure agreement. The proposed project will be implemented in three regions of Bay (Baidoa and Dinsoor), Bakol (Xudur) and Middle Shabelle (Jowhar and Balcad) where INTERSOS has operational presence and prior experience in the sector of intervention. Majority of the envisaged targeted HHs for the ESKs and NFI kits are the ones who are living in 44 spontaneous IDPs settlement in Baidoa without current support from humanitarian actors. Likewise, INTERSOS will target the newly arriving vulnerable IDPs in other proposed locations who have not yet received any humanitarian support from other organization. Today Baidoa counts 435 IDP settlements. Moreover, despite INTERSOS distributed 400 NFI kits to 400 newly displaced HHs in Balcad district of Middle Shabelle region during the month of September, 2019, the number of new IDPs who have not received humanitarian assistance has increased over the last four months in the region and therefore a prompt response is required to ensure safe shelter. INTERSOS will therefore provide standard NFI kits and ESKs for all the newly internally displaced person across the proposed locations.
Furthermore, INTERSOS intends to provide transitional shelters for protracted IDPs and refugee returnees with secure land tenure agreement who are to be relocated to Barwaqo site in Baidoa as recommended by the local authorities. This initiative consolidates humanitarian efforts to find durable solution and foster reintegration status for those living in protracted crisis. INTERSOS has successfully implemented cash assistance project which facilitated construction of 200 transitional shelters for refugees’ returnees and IDPs in Baidoa. The project therefore capitalizes on established networks and data base stock of lessons learnt from previous projects to deliver the much needed humanitarian and protection assistance in the most effective and efficient manner in coordination with all the relevant stakeholders on ground. INTERSOS seeks to engage in promoting public private partnership in provision of essential services like water and electricity at reasonable tariffs for assisted HHs at Barwaqo site in order to hasten achievement of durable solution.
INTERSOS will develop comprehensive selection criteria based on vulnerability and age gender diversity to ensure humanitarian assistance goes to the most deserving ones. Vulnerable female headed household, families with many children, those living with disabilities, minority groups, aged people (70+ years) and child headed HHs will be given priority. Locally understood community feedback mechanisms that includes suggestion boxes, public posters for awareness creation and free hotline numbers will be put in place in order to address concerns appropriately and inform project cycle management. The community structures (committees and LA) will be given capacity injection through training to enable them contribute and assume leadership roles.

Project Activities

OBJECTIVE 1: Ensure that persons affected by conflict and natural disasters have protection from the weather and privacy through provision of emergency shelter and NFIs.Activity 1.1: Conduct community engagement and mobilization on activities to ensure their active participation in project planning, implementation and monitoring. A committee who are representatives of all sections of the community (persons with disability, women, minority groups and youth will be reconstituted in all project locations) in coordination with relevant government authorities in respective locations.Activity 1.2: Carry out rapid market assessment to establish capacities in the local market (both in terms of material and expertise) and prices for NFI kits and ESK taking into consideration existing guidelines/ criteria for cash based programming.Activity 1.3: Distribution of standard NFIs kits in kind for 1500 displaced vulnerable HHs in Bay, Bakol and Middle Shabelle; The standard NFI kit will consist of 1 plastic sheet, 3 blankets, 2 sleeping mats, 1 kitchen set, 1 mosquito net and 1 solar lamp or torch. Activity 1.4: Conditional cash assistance to provide Emergency Shelter Kits for newly displaced people; It will be given in two installment and payment of second installment is subject to monitoring and verification of the first installment. The beneficiary given opportunity to buy instructed material and after accounting for it provided with the second payment. The payment will either be used to upgrade their existing bulls or erect new ESK where applicable. In this regard, 1500 ESKs to be provided to 1500 HHs vulnerable displaced HHs through cash. The ESKs consists of plastic sheets, supporting poles and a rope for tying down structures. This provides the basic shelter which can offer protection from extreme weather. The ESKs helps the displaced HHs to put up by a 4M by 4M shelter structure with first few days of displacement.Activity 1.5: Conduct community mobilization workshop on the use, implementation modalities, monitoring of NFI, ESK and transitional shelters distribution. The different beneficiaries will be taken through different orientation sessions and sensitized on the established community feedback mechanisms.OBJECTIVE 2: Contribute to resilience and improve the living conditions of affected population through improved housing and related community infrastructure.Activity 2.1: Distribution of cash grant for establishing transitional shelters for 200HHs for displacement affected communities (refugee returnees & IDPs) in Baidoa with secure land tenure agreement. The design, technical composition and specifications for implementation of transitional shelters will be given during the orientation training for the selected beneficiaries. The beneficiaries will be provided with the design of the houses and type of materials to be purchased. They will be monitored and supported with technical guidance by the shelter specialist. The cash will be distributed in two installments and the first installment has to be monitored and accounted for before second installment is released.OBJECTIVE 3: Improve the quality of shelter and NFI assistance and ensure accountability through effective complaint and feedback mechanisms.Activity 3.1: Submit 3W matrix to keep relevant clusters informed about planned and implanted activities on monthly and quarterly basis for both in kind and cash based assistance.Activity 3.2: Put in place community based complaint/feedback mechanism (suggestion box and hotline numbers) to get feedback to support in addressing needs of the beneficiaries.Activity 3.3: Conduct comprehensive post distribution and post cash assistance monitoring during activities’ implementation and within 3 months after activities’ implementation in each of the targeted Districts. Take pictures of the implemented activities and record GPS coordinates of all project sites where applicable.

The transitional shelter will be planned in an area with eay access to sanitation services. Both transitional and ESK shelters will have lockable doors and lightings. The materials for construction will be inspected by technical engineer to ensure quality implementation.Barwaqo site is a new relocation area with an urban design and boreholes that will provide reliable source of water. The provision of transitional shelters will foster reintegration and reduce protection risks associated with access to basic services. The project will be implemented with due regard for dignity, safety and accountability by the affected communities. The project activities will be implemented in broad daylight in order to minimize protection risks. The gate keepers’ influence will be minimized in order to ensure transparent selection of beneficiaries. The communities will be provided with feedback mechanisms and sensitized on clear reporting mechanisms.The project will strengthen the existing community structures through advocacy and capacity injections. The projects aimed at improving the living standards of the displacement affected communities through provision of transitional shelters. 30% of the project requirement is designed to provide transitional shelters for IDPs affected by protracted crisis and have long term land tenure agreement. The project foresees establishing the shelters on a public land provided by the local authority at (Bawaaqo site). Providing transitional shelter to a beneficiary with secured land tenure agreement helps in fostering reintegration status in respective areas and preventing secondary displacement. The project will be integrated with other ongoing INTERSOS projects in area of intervention to promote access to basic services for the targeted communities such as food, education and health service in order to achieve results at scale.The project interventions are aligned with the national policy for refugee returnees and IDPs of the federal government of Somalia and with reference to principle 6 which reiterates right to an adequate standard of living through provision of basic shelter and housing.The existing community structures, for example IDPs umbrellas, camp leaders, women groups, youth, people with disabilities and relevant local authorities will be actively involved in order to promote community ownership and sustainability. This will be achieved through their active participation in project cycle and strengthening of feedback mechanisms with a view to inform durable solution programming.The communities will be engaged in advocacy meetings to shun away with donor mindset tendencies (behavior change) in order to articulate impact of the project beyond its lifespan. The proposed intervention is need based and intended to address challenges of displacement affected communities and maximize impact.The proposed project is aligned with addressing both national and state level priorities of addressing displacement crisis and fostering achievement of durable solution. The proposed project therefore foresees establishing collaboration and synergies with all relevant government line ministries in the process of delivering this aid.
