Project Summary

"Malnutrition, forced displacement, and health emergencies are recurrent in the Tahoua and Tillaberi Regions of Niger due to structural crises, poverty, and insecurity. The Humanitarian Response Plan for Niger foresaw a further deterioration of the humanitarian situation in 2019. The document estimated that more than 716,000 people in the two regions will needed assistance. This includes the 42,000 people currently displaced, and more than 55,500 Malians refugee who already live in the two regions. In 2020, similar numbers are probable.
UN agencies in Niger in concert with humanitarian partners have created a ‘Rapid Response Mechanism’ (RRM) to prepare for sudden emergency situations that require the mobilization of lifesaving humanitarian assistance.
The RRM is an operational, programmatic and partnership model designed to enhance capacity of Sectors to respond in a timely, coordinated and standardized manner to the needs of populations made vulnerable by displacement, disease and/or natural disaster in humanitarian/emergency settings.
It is meant to address:
- the most acute vulnerabilities;
- to access the most complex zones (insecurity);
- to access areas where few actors are present.
The intent of the RRM is to deliver aid within 72 hours of the report of an emergency.
"

"Project Management and AdministrationFinancial Budgetary and Human Resource ManagementUNDP NigerMonitoring and ReportingUNDSS will provide monthly updates to DSS HQ and the donor on:• Staffing and procurement status• Number of standing Ad Hoc SRM assessments completed• Number of ‘just-in-time’ Ad Hoc SRM risk assessments in RRM emergencies• Number of points-of-contact and local security actors included• Number of security briefings provided (and organizations attending)• Number of field missions conducted The HSST project will be directly overseen by the UNDSS Security Adviser for Niger, who monitor and evaluate the timeliness and quality of Ad Hoc SRMs and document delays and shortcomings, and receive feedback from the humanitarian partners on the quality of services provided. The input of data would be managed by local staff and the analysis performed by the Security Information Analyst. Overarching guidance and direction for the project will be provided by the UNDSS HQ Africa Desk and Threat and Risk Unit."
