Project Summary

The humanitarian crisis in the Southwest region of Cameroon has exposed the local population to large scale protection risks and human rights violations and impacted vulnerable households’ capacity to meet their emergency food needs.
Through this Action, the IRC seeks to improve the protection status of vulnerable people affected by the crisis in Southwest Cameroon, assisting the most vulnerable people to stay safe, access assistance, recover from violence and claim their rights. The IRC will do this through an integrated program of Protection prevention and response activities to mitigate further protection risks resulting from the use of negative coping mechanisms.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Conflict-affected populations in South West are forced to leave their homes and experience specific forms of deprivation, and an increase of protection risks due to the exacerbation of vulnerabilities, the disruption of social dynamics, the reduction of capacities to cope, and the increase of external threats linked to the high concentration of populations and potentially of armed forces. This violence peaked in 2018 and led to a massive displacement of the population. There are more than 409,173 people (female 48%, children 48% and 12% elderly person) are displaced within the two regions . Most IDPs are in shock and in need of access to basic services and restoring of a protective environment.Based on HIP 2021, more than 1,419,902 persons need protection and assistance in Cameroon. Conflicts and violence are having knock-on effects, hindering access to social services and challenging humanitarian access and the delivery of humanitarian aid.In South – West and North – West, the lack of protection and security for the population and especially in the areas currently affected by violence, is highly concerning. Threat to life and personal security counts for 36% of the protection incidents recorded in the NWSW regions from January to September 2020. According the Protection Assessment conducted by the IRC in September 2020, threats, movement restriction and most villages burned have been identified as the main protection incidents by 49%, 39% and 12% of responders in Meme, while in Ndian, the main risks identified are robberies/looting (40% of respondents), followed by movement restriction (35%), and torture for 25%. The proposed action is in line with the gaps and needs identified, with the protection response of DG ECHO, in particular the prevention of gender-based violence and sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA); the priority for HRP's NWSW of improving effective prevention and access to protective assistance, including psychosocial, medical and legal support; and put in place coordination and communication systems to enhance awareness. This action is also linked to IRC Cameroon's strategy and aims to meet the protection needs of the most vulnerable people, especially women, girls, men and boys and people with disabilities.IRC’s intervention aims to reinforce the community protective environment and to enhance access to basic services for most vulnerable people and at-risk individuals including GBV survivors. IRC’s objective is to strengthen community-based protection systems, prevention of protection risks and response to the protection concerns of internally displaced persons and other affected persons, with particular attention to those with specific needs and will provide direct assistance based on risk exposure and vulnerabilities for the individuals and targeted communities.
