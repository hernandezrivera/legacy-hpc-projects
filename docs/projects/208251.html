Project Summary


The aim of this project is to reduce child mortality and morbidity by strengthening health and nutrition services to deal with the humanitarian situation of children and adolescents, people living with HIV and other vulnerable groups, the elderly, girls, adolescents, the elderly and the disabled.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project is part of the 2024 humanitarian response to address the nutritional barriers faced by displaced communities and will cover about 9000 beneficiaries. The project will support the treatment of acute malnutrition in children under five and pregnant and lactating women and the elderly to reduce mortality and related morbidity, including those suffering from HIV and other vulnerable groups (adolescent girls and people with disabilities).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

During implementation, the project will conduct the social mobilisation intervention at host community and resettlement level for adherence to the provision of integrated health and nutrition services, in addition to supporting the APS as necessary for nutritional screening, nutritional counselling and provision of nutritional care and referral of at-risk groups (children under 5 years old). Support PHCs as needed in home visits to follow up on nutrition cases at the community level and provide support in the distribution of supplies and goods from the provincial level to the district level and from the district level to the facilities One of the a Ensure the quality of data collected through community actors, such as APSs, activities, and mothers' groups, from Chiure and Montepuez districts.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The risks will be identified at the field level and the potential risks to be identified and mitigated (places of provision of the project services; gender balance in the project; The type of product or service to be provided. )- Free and easy access to people: areas or places accessible to all beneficiaries, including people with disabilities, will be selected and conditioned to guarantee non-exclusion without exclusion. Gender balance in the project: this component guarantees that the project has complete data, for example: it is easier for a lady to talk to another woman to report something that is bothering her than with a man, and this prevents cases in the community. Service to provide: identify based on community needs, and avoid giving a non-food product without explaining how to use it. Promote lectures in the community to explain the project and all the possible services to be made available, this avoids a bad perception and creates risks during implementation.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Consultations will be carried out with individual people, including specific reflection with women, older men, children and people with disabilities who are most related, to understand the level of perception about nutrition in general and from that point, the team will promote lectures of sensitization in the communities with themes related to nutrition and chronic malnutrition, and a balanced and maintained diet of an unbalanced diet, and in the same way the community will be made aware of how to make meals rich in animal and vegetable proteins from the foods available locally, reducing the risks of malnutrition in the community, supplements and a package of advice will also be allocated to each identified family, on breastfeeding, water and sanitation, and others.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As it is considered an inclusive project, people in need will be the focus of Caritas, including the elderly to identify specific needs for infrastructure adaptation. The infrastructure will be adapted to these needs. Persons with disabilities will be one of the priorities during distributions. Distribution points will be If there are any people with disabilities and if they have some leadership character and ability to do some work, such as registering, organising beneficiaries, writing reports, they can be included in the field team serviced, ensuring ease of access for community members, especially people with disabilities and women.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is nutrition, so field agents will be trained to serve the target local communities, how to make healthy food for their families, keep the environment clean, avoid throwing garbage in inappropriate places, promoting the use of latrines to avoid water contamination, promoting advice on breastfeeding, among other practices, to ensure the nutritional well-being of communities.
