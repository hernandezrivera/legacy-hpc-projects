Project Summary

The assignment has the specific purpose of building the capacity and awareness of the target communities & schools’ students on WASH /COVID-19 and municipal services through its program of Community Mobilization for WASH Behavior Change. The main targets are school children along with child and women headed families, people with challenges, vulnerabilities and marginalized communities. Once implemented, the envisaged project will not only capacitate the target people in the adoption of the best WASH/COVID-19 practices and best utilization of the provided WASH facilities and services in their respective communities; it will also increase community's access to the improved WASH facilities provided under the Program. In all, the project implementation will work towards improving the health and quality of life of the poor and vulnerable (in particular women and school children) through their sensitization, education and adoption of the universally practiced best practices of WASH/COVID-19.
