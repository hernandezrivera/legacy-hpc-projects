Project Summary

This proposed project will be implemented in the counties of Lafon and Kapoeta East of Eastern Equatoria State of the Republic of South Sudan. It will contribute to the Protection Humanitarian Response Plan Objectives and indicators under objectives PC2,PC1 and PC3-PC3 which aim at ensuring that human rights are upheld and promoted for the persons of concern by strengthening coordination, advancing centrality of protection among humanitarian actors to reduce protection risks, and addressing coordination gaps/issues through close dialogue with partners and effective protection monitoring to inform programming, advocacy, and response and ensuring that human rights are upheld and promoted for the persons of concern by strengthening coordination, advancing centrality of protection among humanitarian actors to reduce protection risks, and addressing coordination gaps/issues through close dialogue with partners and effective protection monitoring to inform programming, advocacy, and response and - Promoting nexus to development and solutions as the transition between the delivery of emergency humanitarian assistance, systems building and the provision of long-term development assistance to meet peoples’ needs, mitigate risks and vulnerabilities, exacerbated by systemic and socially reinforced exclusion of women and girls and persons with disabilities.
The target for this proposed project is 8,152 that is 1,981 males and 6111 females distributed even between the two locations.(Women 3,451,Men 1,021,Girls 2,660 and Boys 1020), LGBTQ + minority and few foreign nationals.

HLSS will conduct KI interview at payam level to keep the protection cluster updated of what protection issues emerging every month in the project locations. The target is 6 men and 6 Women each.

1. Needs

Sexual violence (SV), physical violence and other forms of gender-based violence (GBV) remain a major protection concern in South Sudan as suggested by the South Sudan GBV Working Group. The decades of conflict, insecurity, environmental shocks and harmful traditional practices, such as, early/forced marriages, have increased women and girls’ risks of GBV. According to Initial Needs Rapid Assessment report conducted in Jie Payam of Kapoeta East county of Eastern Equatoria State ,South Sudan from 1st to 4th March 2023, following reports of four (4) deaths due to starvation that were flagged up by one of the local broadcasting media outlet Eye Radio on the link: https://www.eyeradio.org/four-starve-to-death-in-drought-hit-kapoeta-east/,the Deep Field Coordination forum for the Greater Kapoeta hurriedly convened a meeting on 27 Feb 2023 that brought together Cluster leads, co-leads together humanitarian agencies with operational in the area and RRC recommended an immediate IRNA Assessment mission team that left Kapoeta town on 1-4 March 2023 and returned to Kapoeta Town. All Clusters, FSL, Health/Nutrition, Education, WASH, and Protection joined the Assessment mission. Hence, the key protection findings were; lack of community-based protection networks to respond to SGBV cases in Jie, lack of protection facilities for responding to SGBV, such as police stations and child and women-friendly spaces, lack of conflict mitigation strategies for maintaining and strengthening peace to avoid inter-communal violence among the neighbouring communities of Jie. • Recommendations: Establishing community-based protection networks against all forms of abuse on women and girls, police posts and friendly spaces with its related services for women and children in the community, and establish and capacitate traditional local structures to carry advocacy and awareness raising on all forms of abuses in the communities during community meetings, traditional functions, and emergencies/disasters. There continues to be inadequate Gender Based violence response and prevention services across these two counties of Eastern Equatoria state of south Sudan, this continues to be seen as a critical gap for women and girls service delivery on lifesaving services which exposes them to further harm and consequences of the violence they have gone through which is a huge protection risk and exposure to their lives. Although there are GBV, actor on ground with GBV services in some of the parts of the state but one still finds out that there are some parts that indicate in adequate service delivery for women and girls. For example in Lafon county based on the reports from the current Primary Health Care project implementation progressive reports by HLSS in the County, under Health Pool Fund (HPF) funds as of Oct 2023 there is high need of GBV services in this county, which hosts both IPDs, returnees, Host communities and other affected populations. There has been increase on the number of women and girls who report for health service indicating during consultation that they were either beaten by their Husbands or intimate partners and other showing signs of been psychologically tortured for which there are no adequate GBV services where such survivors can be supported with case management or psychosocial support. Furthermore, reports from MHC in the various facilities indicate underage girls attending antenatal services, which is an indication of early marriage being common, and there are no services of information dissemination on consequences of such forms of Gender Based violence. With this project HLSS intends to cover the service gaps for women and girls which is a huge risk factor for life threatening cases, as the identified and mentioned locations have huge number of returnees, other affected population, IDPS and the host communities plus business population such as food vendors, suppliers of stationary, dealers in car hires ,and casual labourers among others.
