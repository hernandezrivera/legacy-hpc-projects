Project Summary

According to HNO 2024, 14.9 million people in Syria need life-saving primary and secondary health care assistance, and 1 in 10 people in Syria live with mild to moderate mental health conditions. According to NWS Sit.Rep March 2024. 89 % cent of over 2 million children require child protection assistance including psychosocial support. Based on a Rapid Protection Assessment led by the Protection Cluster, 65% of participants stated MHPSS services are not provided in communities across Northwest Syria. WHO has estimated that 1 in 5 people in Northwest of Syria were living with mental health conditions. According to Protection Analysis Update Oct. 2023, the earthquake's devastating impact, compounded by ongoing conflict and increased confrontation has caused intensive mental health and psychosocial risks and distress to the population. The extensive child and family separation has fractured support systems, amidst a breakdown of crucial social services, making it a collective responsibility to address the mental health and psychosocial well-being of the affected population.
This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining essential health services for all age and gender groups with attention to PwDs without any sexual discrimination among IDPs and HCs through 80 PSWs, 6 MHPSS supervisors, 4 Psychologists, and 6 mhGAP doctors to provide integrated MHPSS services through 33 MHPSS teams (20 HFs and 10 linked mobile units, 1 Mental health clinic in Kherbet Eljoz & 1 linked mobile unit, in addition to 2 CCs & 1 WGSS which provide MHPSS services targeting those at risk of protection). 10 of these HFs are already activated, while the other 10 proposed HFs will be selected later in close coordination with the health cluster and MHPSS TWG. locations of current static HFs are (HRO/Kherbet Eljoz !G0704034, HRO/Jisr-Ash-Shugur CC G0700171!, Partner/Hamama G0704036, Watan/Al Kher 3 center/Idleb G0700102, Hidaya/Qah G0703077, Onsur/Sandara b0204075, Partner/Bsheiriyeh G0704010, UDER/Sarmada PHC G0703011), and locations of mobile units include (HRO/Kherbet Eljoz !G0704038, Shafak/Arshaf 2!B0204102, Shafak/Aghtrin 1!B0204101, HRO/Hamama 1 !G0704039, HRO/Hamama 2 !G0704040).
MHPSS activities include Biopsychosocial assessment, PM+, PFA, identification and referral, diagnosis, psychotropics by mhGAP doctors, psycho-education sessions, follow-up and group MHPSS awareness sessions about (depression, epilepsy, psycho-behavioral problems, etc.). Most vulnerable groups (such as PwD, pregnant women, and older people) will be targeted through outreach visits, in addition to the MHPSS trainings that will target 240 health staff from different HFs in Idleb and Aleppo on different MHPSS topics in coordination with Health cluster.
Besides HRO will train 8 CHWs on different health topics according to the adopted WHO manual. As for health services, HRO is planning to expand its presence and reach by establishing 1 PHC in Aleppo targeting all community groups, besides activating 3 health mobile units (2 in Azaz and 1 in Badama) targeting older people. Health care services will be provided by 6 doctors, 4 midwives, 8 nurses & 8 CHWs, activities include diagnosis and treatment, Child health services, Prevention and control of communicable and non-communicable diseases, Reproductive health services including ANC, PNC and family planning, nurse activities such as minor surgery and follow up of related cases, prevention services including health education and raising awareness on different topics such as IYCF, NCD, RH, and child health, in addition to the COVID-19 & Cholera pandemic through conducting household visits by CHWs. All the health staff will be trained on GBV basics including identification and referral to specialized GBV services to enable the health facility to accommodate survivors. HRO through running these activities for the community members will contribute to improving the resilience of the overburdened health systems.
