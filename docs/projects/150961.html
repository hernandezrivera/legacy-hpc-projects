Project Summary

The project aims at increasing the institutional capacity of 15 new schools in GCA that have been implementing the distance education for schoolchildren from NGCA and CL in 2018-2019. It is envisaged to provide 4 different trainings (1 - organization of distance learning; 2 - using digital resources during the educational process; 3 - methodology for creating videoinstructions and interactive lessons; 4 - distance learning methodology) for 125 teachers and representatives of local divisions of education, institutes of postgraduate education in the Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts on the organization of distance learning, the use of gadgets, mobile applications, interactive free platforms and other digital resources during the educational process, on methodologies for creating video instructions and interactive e-presentations. It is planned in collaboration with the Luhansk Institute of Postgraduate Education to prepare and publish 3 methodological manuals (“Organization of Distance Learning”, “Collection of Lessons for Distance Learning of Primary School Students under the Programs of the New Ukrainian School”, “Instruction for Parents on Usage of Digital Technologies in Distance Learning”). 4 video instructions and 1 motivational video will be produced, the consulting support for teachers during the year on the basis of the created Distance Learning Center will be provided. In addition, the advocacy campaign aimed at improving the regulatory framework of distance education will be provided (namely the preparation of proposals and lobbying for the settlement of the issue of the load of teachers who work on the distance learning form and remuneration for their labour).

Needs

According to Ukraine Education Cluster (https://tinyurl.com/ya36728n) and NGCA, in 2018, 99,000 children and young people (including approximately 49% of girls and 51% of boys) need recognition of learning attainment, since the documents on education they receive in NGCA are not recognaized. In 2019, there will be 120,000 and in 2020 – 141,000 children of such kind. This creates barriers to obtaining a profession and employment outside the NGCA. One of the ways of obtaining the recognition certificates on education for these children is the distance education. The number of children from NGCA and CL, who are studying distance in comprehensive schools in the GCA area, is increasing. So, in the Donetsk oblast, the number of children studying distance/on an external basis has increased from 1,300 students in 2014-2015 up to 12,000 children in 2018/2019 year (http://openpolicy.org.ua/en/donbass-crimea/news/230/). In the Luhansk oblast, the number of this category of children has increased by almost 7.5 times from 112 to 809 students in 2018/2019 year. In addition, the development of distance education for children from CL provides children with the safe learning conditions. The implementation of distance education in the educational process requires to increase the potential of teachers (there are up to 90% of women employed in the educational sphere) regarding the acquiring of skills to work online, organization of distance learning process, use of gadgets, mobile applications, etc. Currently, there are actually no methodological works on the organization and implementation of distance education. In addition, according to the results of communication with the teachers who participate in the Foundation’s projects supported by UNICEF, the Embassy of the Republic of Lithuania, the French Embassy (http://openpolicy.org.ua/en/), they need a legal regulation framework for carrying out final academic assessment, criteria for assessing the academic performance, the payment of the work of a teacher who carries out the distance teaching etc. This requires the advocacy of amendments to the legal framework for distance education in part namely the preparation of proposals and lobbying for the settlement of the issue of the load of teachers, curricula who work on the distance learning form and remuneration for their labour.

Activities & outcomes

1. Educating 70 teachers of 10 comprehensive schools in the Luhansk oblast and 5 comprehensive schools in the Donetsk oblast, 30 representatives of offices and divisions of education and 20 representatives of institutions of postgraduate education in the Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts on the organization of distance learning, using smartphones, other gadgets, mobile applications, interactive free platforms and other digital resources during the educational process, methodology for creating video instructions and interactive lessons, distance learning methodology (4 trainings).Result: raised qualification of 120 teachers from Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts from GCA, CL. 2. Preparation and publication of the methodological manual “Cool Digital – Cool Digital Communications for the Distance Education of Your Children”, the methodological manual for schools “Organization of Distance Learning” and “Collection of Lessons for Distance Learning of Primary School Students under the Programs of the New Ukrainian School”. The placement of manuals on the website “Dostupna Osvita” (https://dostupnaosvita.com.ua/) and Luhansk Institute of Postgraduate Education (http://loippo.edu.ua/). Result: prepared and published 4 methodical manuals, which allowed to raise the level of knowledge about technology and methods of distance learning of 3,000 educators and parents. 3. Creation of the Distance Education Center (provision of consulting support in the form of webinars, modifications of the Facebook page “Dostupna Osvita” (https://www.facebook.com/dostupnaosvita/), groups in WhatsApp, newsletter).Result: created the Distance Education Center works which increased qualification 1,000 educators. 4. Preparation of 1 motivational video for parents and children “Dostupna Osvita” and 4 step-by-step video instructions “How to Prepare an Interactive Presentation”, “How to Create a Video Tutorial”, “Cool Digital – Cool Digital Communications”, “Development of Lesson Plans for Distance Learning”.Result: Increased knowledge and awareness of distance education facilities and methods of over 1,000 educators from GCA and 4,000 children and parents from NGCA, CL. 5. Conducting a press conference.Result: More than 5,000 residents of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts are informed about opportunities distance learning and receive recognized education certificates. 6. Advocacy of distance education (development of proposals and amendments to the existing regulatory framework on the implementation of distance education; provision of recommendations on distance education to the government and local self-government bodies, to Ukraine Education Cluster for its inclusion in HRP 2020).Result: schools that carry out distance learning will receive the standard of loading, curricula and remuneration for their labour.
