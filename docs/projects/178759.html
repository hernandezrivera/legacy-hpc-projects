Project Summary

This project aims to protect agriculture and livestock assets in Mhambal, Ariha, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts. Through the following activities:
- Distributing 500 agriculture vouchers with cost 150 $ per HH. 50 % of vouchers value to be redeemed from summer crops seeds enough for cultivating two donums. while the remain 50 % for getting on fertilizer, pesticides, or tools.
- Distributing cash vouchers for irrigation cost 80 $ per HH.
- Conducting extension visits for beneficiaries’ farms for five months. to provide farmers technical information about the best agricultural practices and follow up the progress of plants’ growing.
- conducting ten field visits to 1500 animals' breeders during ten months to check on the animals’ health situation and provide the required treatment, if needed, against the most common diseases, and consultations to raise their awareness.
- Targeting 50.000 sheep/goat and 1000 cows by six vaccination campaigns against Enterotoxemia (for twice), Sheep Pox, FMD, Pasteurella diseases, and lumpy skin diseases.
- Conducting artificial insemination for 100 cows by using seminal straws high-throughput strains.
- Organizing the pregnancy of 1000 sheep by using a vaginal sponge to increase birth rates for sheep.
- Training 30 veterinary technicians of persons for ten actual days. and distributing 30 veterinary kits consist of the main required tools to enable them of launching their special work.
- Conducting cash for work activity through involving those 30 trainees in the field visits to breeders' houses to increase their practical knowledge. where every 10 trainees will be participating for three months. and each one will receive 7 USD per workday.
- Distributing 800 fodder vouchers with a value of 50 $ for 800 sheep breeders to be redeemed from the required fodder. for four batches.
- Distributing 200 fodder vouchers with a value of 100 $ for 200 cows' breeders to be redeemed from the required fodder. for four batches. during the period of (Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec).
the agriculture activities will be implemented in communities of Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts. while the livestock support activities planned to be in Mhambal, Ariha, Jisr-Ash-Shugur sub-districts.
that integration in the planned activities aims to improve the food security for the vulnerable people Thus, reaching the largest possible segment of crises-affected people. Moreover, support for those both sectors, which constitutes a basic source of income for the majority of families in northern Syria.
Regarding COVID-19, a developed SOP that relates to the distribution process will be implemented during activities implementation. where, all health measures will be considered to mitigate covid-19 epidemic risk.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Protection risk 1: Child labor. Mitigating measures: IHR will be sure that the staff working in this project are trained and fully aware of child safeguarding, Child protection, and GBV principles. Protection risk 2: Lack/difficulty of accessibility for PWDs in the proposed activities. Mitigating measures: 1- Availability of wheelchair for PWD at the reception point, trained staff to deal with PWDs, Priority for the PWDs, included in the criteria, House to house distributions that will ensure that those with special needs that can’t reach the distribution points can reach the assistance. Protection Risk 3: Sometimes, crowding during the distribution of bread, may not provide the services at the desired quality, which might affect the BNFs dignity, especially women. Mitigating measures: Set up separate waiting and entry lines at registrations Raise the awareness of community leaders on GBV and child protection principles and make sure that all BNFs can voice their concerns through the designated channels. Protection Risk 4: Gender-based violence and sexual exploitation for women during the implementation of the project. Mitigating measures: Choosing appropriate time for distributions of bread and receiving of the wheat crop, voucher exchanging in Hawala agent will taking place during daylight hours to allow women to return home safely, ensuring regular security and incidence reporting, conducting awareness sessions for women about issues surrounding with GBV to minimiz damage.
