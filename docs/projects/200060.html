Project Summary

This Project is designed to provide life-saving nutrition services in poor neighborhoods with a high density of Lebanese, and Palestinians in a vulnerable situation through both community and facility-based interventions including:
1) Infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices optimization,
micronutrient supplementation distribution, and reporting of blanket
distribution of Infant formula
2) Screening, referral, and treatment of malnutrition (SAM without
complications and MAM) among children under 5 and PLW
3) Provision of education and awareness-raising activities to school-aged children and adolescents (at the school level).
4)Mother-support groups sessions raising awareness about breastfeeding, and young child feeding practices
5)Supporting the ECD of children under 5 by providing nurturing care through the inclusion of parents in early stimulation, growth, and development, and care educational programs.

The project will be implemented by Mercy-USA in the North, Mount Lebanon, and, Akkar Governorates. This project targets 22,900 people (Host communities 26,030 and PRL 1,370) disaggregated as follows:
- 11,800 Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs)
- 20,900 Children under 5 (10,450 girls - 10,450 boys)

12 Primary Healthcare centers will be supported under this project; outreach teams will be targeting each center's catchment area (localities within a 5 to 10 km radius) with Household visits.
26 outreach staff (13 IYCF counselors and 13 Community Health Workers) will screen, refer (when needed), and provide IYCF services for PLW and caregivers of children under 5.
The field teams will be directly coordinating malnutrition referrals with the assigned PHC staff.

Outreach and PHC staff will assess the nutritional status of children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them as needed. Additionally, IYCF services will be provided through:
Counseling for caregivers of children 0-6
counseling for lactating mothers
counseling for caregivers of children 6 to 23
Education and awareness raising for caregivers of children under 5 (including ECD)
Education and awareness raising for PLW (iodized salt)
Counseling for PLW – healthy maternal nutrition
Stunting in Palestinian camps
School-based programs (nutrition school-based
awareness sessions)
Screening, identification, referral, and treatment of CU5
Screening, identification, referral, and treatment of PLW
Provision of micronutrient supplementation to CU5 and Pregnant Women

All cases of violence against women and children will be referred to Mercy-USA's protection focal point.

This project will empower mothers and HH members by training them to screen their children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes. In addition to providing nurturing care through nutrition and educational approaches while managing severe and moderate acute malnutrition and micronutrient supplementation and fortification. As for the educational approach, parenting sessions will be conducted about the early stimulation growth and development of toddlers and young children while supporting caregivers in early stimulation and care (parenting programs) that play a major role in early childhood development; which is the most cost-effective equalizer to break the vicious cycle of inequity if evidence-based interventions are provided for all children and families, especially for those most marginalized.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1)Enhance the access and demand of Young children and their caregivers to services aiming at prevention of all forms of malnutrition and the associated developmental risks (0-5 years of age):-Optimum breastfeeding practices are promoted, protected, and supported in early childhood.-Optimum Infant and Young Child Feeding practices are promoted, protected, and supported with focus on 6-23 months of age.-Integrated Nutrition and Early Childhood Development are promoted, protected, and supported during the early years.-Children in their early years are provided with needed micronutrient supplementation. 2)School-age children and adolescents have access to and demand for services aiming at the prevention of all forms of malnutrition– particularly among girls:-School-age children and adolescents have promoted knowledge and skills on healthy nutrition and benefiting from anemia prevention. 3)Women and children have enhanced access to and demand for services aiming at the prevention of all forms of malnutrition– particularly during pregnancy and lactation:-Pregnant and Lactating Women have promoted knowledge and skills in healthy nutrition. -Children and Pregnant and Lactating Women have access to and demand services for the detection, referral, and management of acute malnutrition.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

All staff will sign a code of conduct for sexual exploitation and abuse, moreover, Mercy-USA has zero-tolerance against SEA. Staff will be trained on GBV prevention and response in emergencies, to ensure full adherence to the Do No Harm principle and to stress that PSEA and GBV issues will be dealt with firmly and expeditiously. Protection mainstreaming will be maintained in all project steps as follows:Close monitoring of the security situation and preparing all possible scenarios beforehand while putting in place mitigation measuresEnsuring safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of nutrition servicesA confidential protection reporting mechanism system will be designed and beneficiaries will be advised where to report incidents or violationsBeneficiaries will be informed that nutrition services are free of chargeMeasures are put in place to ensure that beneficiaries have a right to equitable and safe assistance including people with specific needs

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Mercy-USA will be targeting community members, including and not limited to women, the disabled, the elderly, and PLW, throughout the varying project stages. Project activities will be conducted at the facility and community (HH) levels by identifying and referring women and children (including PWDs) who display breastfeeding difficulties or have malnutrition to the to-be-supported health centers by Mercy-USA.Satisfaction surveys will be conducted using a random sample of beneficiaries via phone call.Accountability will be ensured through feedback and complaints mechanisms at both field PHC levels via locked complaint boxes with a complaint WhatsApp number available at each PHC; the WhatsApp number will also be disseminated by field staff through our project flyers.Nutrition activities are designed considering gender and the different needs of women, boys, and girls, including PWDs. All staff is trained to respect gender equality and justice in humanitarian service provision.
