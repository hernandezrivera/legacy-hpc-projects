1 - Needs

In its twelfth years, the crisis in the North-East Nigeria remains one of the most severeprotracted crises in the world. In the most severely affected Borno state and two adjacentstates, a population of 1.9 million are displaced as internally displaced persons (IDPs).One in four of the affected population is under 5 years of age. Women and children arethe face of the crisis, constituting 81% of the overall crisis population and 87% of the newdisplacements. The gendered dimensions of displacement are having a significant impact on the status of women and girls within the context of fragmentation of households,demoralization and rampant Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV). In addition,under spread of COVID-19, critical incidents and attacks continue and are even increasing,with 2,614 persons killed under the incidents or attacks in the three regions between thebeginning of 2020 to 17 October 2020, while the total fatality there in 2019 was 2,221.Despite the challenges, return of IDPs to the communities are ongoing in a number oflocations. Communities therefore have an opportunity to transition from a humanitariancontext, construct their livelihood systems, restoring access to public services, whilestrengthening their resilience for building and sustaining positive peace in the locations ofreturn. This aligned with the Humanitarian Response Plan Nigeria 2022. One of theapproaches emphasized in the plan is effective Humanitarian-Development-Peace (HDP)nexus. Through the approach, support for conflict-affected communities through theapproach should be further extended.

2 - Activities

The activities will be done for 3 priority areas as follows: 1) Restoration of basic servicesthrough the repair and reconstruction of infrastructure essential for the pursuit of liveswith dignity in communities that were devastated by conflict, and the strengthening ofbasic services provision. The restoration of basic services will be achieved throughpartnerships between the government and international actors for the provision of basicservices. Priority needs will be confirmed through consultations with communities,security groups, and local governments (both traditional and formal representatives). Keyactivities include: -Reconstruction/rehabilitation of housing and shelter for returnees -Construction/rehabilitation of key infrastructure (e.g., water, schools, clinic, markets) -Promotion of renewable technologies such as solar power for basic services andenvironment protection, such as waste management - Coordination of service provisionand capacity building, as well as the provision of effective maintenance systems PriorityArea 2) Livelihoods Stabilization infused with opportunities for immediate employmentand income generation including, but not limited to, Cash for Work (CfW) programmes.Interventions will focus on both emergency income generation measures (e.g, CfW ) and sustainable job creation. Key activities include: - Cash for work for construction/waste management labour - Grants for women and youth led small and micro-enterprise recovery. -Vocational training for vulnerable people, especially female-headed households. PriorityArea 3) Rebuilding social cohesion and peaceful coexistence among IDPs in HostCommunities, Returnees, and Host communities through systematic social engagementand community security initiatives. Restoring community stability, social cohesion andtrust is the most critical precondition to recovery and peace building, given the deepimpact of the conflict. The programme will strengthen community cohesion throughbuilding the sense of shared responsibility for and ownership of the reconstructionprocess and well as inclusive dialogues among different population groups in thecommunities Key activities include: - Strengthen the existing community mobilizationmechanisms, in particular with traditional conflict resolution mechanisms, and access tothe formal legal chain - Strengthening community-level peace-building activities, includingcultural activities, peace dialogues, joint communal activities and trust-building betweenthe community members and security/police actors. – Conduct stakeholder dialogue oncounter narratives on radicalization - Conduct sensitization for religious and traditionalleaders on radicalization - Research and knowledge development for prevention of violentextremism (PVE) Priority Area
