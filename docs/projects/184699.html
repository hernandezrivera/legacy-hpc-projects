Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in southern Somalia, SOS Children villages is proposing the following interventions to response to the extreme vulnerability that have placed girls and women in increased risk of gender based violence (GBV) in Bay, Middle Shabelle and lower Shabelle. While the humanitarian response are attending to the affected population, external shocks limits the population access to basic services increasing levels of vulnerability.
As a result women and girls remain high vulnerable and are forced to adopt risky and sometimes harmful coping strategies, including collection and sale of firewood and payment of debt through forced early marriage. In addition, Findings from multi-sectoral safety audits in 2021 indicate specific GBV risks for women and girls such as sexual harassment and rape caused
The main causes are inadequate fencing of IDP camps, poor shelters constructed with inappropriate and light housing materials (e.g. dump cloths, plastic sheets and sticks), poor lighting, lack of private spaces for sleeping, over-crowding, and faulty solar lanterns and torches. Furthermore, in such situations, person with face extremely hurdles in being recognized and accessing resources and remain highly vulnerable with gender based violence.
Considering the complexity of the operating environment, SOS CVS propose an intervention that will employ multifaceted approached that are tailored to each location. This include. Provision prevention and responsive measures through providing GBV services and case management in the target districts of Jowhar, Afgoi corridor, Balcad and Baidoa districts. The proposed project will also provide trauma counselling and PSS services to traumatized GBV survivors. In addition, the project will provide capacity building to health workers in case management and clinical management of rape survivors.
The project is envisioned to provide GBV prevention and response services to 22,700 persons in the proposed target districts.
