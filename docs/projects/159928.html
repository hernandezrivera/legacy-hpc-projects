Project Summary

In collaboration with the WASH Cluster, REACH will provide support to address gaps in information needed by decision makers through support of the FSNMS, infrastructure mapping assessments, and capacity building of WASH partners across South Sudan. Multi-sectoral analysis will highlight WASH needs and solutions across the country. The main beneficiaries will be WASH Cluster partners.

1 - Needs

Accessibility and security issues within South Sudan have impeded a systematic understanding of WASH needs in many areas of the country, and have created difficultiesin establishing a clear and centralized system for prioritizing the delivery of aid, therebylimiting the effectiveness of humanitarian planning and limiting the potential impact ofdonor funding. As this crisis continues to evolve, it has become increasingly important to fill information gaps to inform a moreeffective humanitarian response and planning for immediate life-saving WASH activitiesand contingency planning for durable solutions. REACH will conduct a series of overlapping, integrated activities to support the establishment of a WASH Cluster KnowledgeEcosystem for South Sudan and to assist WASH cluster partners in feeding into the 5Ws. REACH will directly support WASH Cluster with a dedicated,full time Assessment Officer and a supporting GIS Officer who will work with the Cluster and Partnersto analyze and map existing WASH related information to identify information gaps andprovide targeting information for REACH led interagency assessments, partnerassessment activities and WASH response prioritization. Using the WASH Baseline Toolkitestablished during 2018, REACH will provide support for partners conductingassessments in order to harmonize WASH assessments around a set of key indicators, byproviding a series of WASH partner capacity building workshops that focus on buildingpartner capacity to fill information gaps in the WASH landscape. REACH will providetraining to partners on assessment design and execution, mobile data collection, and IMbest practices that ultimately results in the execution of 6 county level assessments(locations to be determined by Cluster information needs), and the production of reportsand datasets. This actionable information will be used to identify priority areas and/orpopulations and the key WASH concerns, rank needs across the country to improvepriority targeting, and will also help shape what kind type of intervention should beimplemented.

2 - Activities

1) Produce at least 4 additional county level assessments for infrastructure mapping, with 4 resulting assessment reports and datasets2) Conduct regular capacity building with WASH partners 3) Update/maintain the WASH IM platform alongside the UNICEF WASH IM
