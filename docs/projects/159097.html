Project Summary

RRP through this interventions in the stated localities will work towards Strengthen and improve access to essential lifesaving health and mental well-being services aimed at reducing avoidable morbidity and mortality, reduction of maternal and child morbidity and mortality among and strengthen emergency preparedness and response capacity at all levels in order to mitigate and respond to communicable disease outbreaks in an efficient, coordinated, and timely for crisis affected and host populations in Beletweyne, bulaburte and jalalaqsi districts of Hiran region Somalia. As a way of working towards provision of Integrated life-saving primary health care services aimed at reducing the burden of morbidity and mortality; We will improve access to health care systems by providing health interventions to the residence within the target population to the nearest proximity.
RRP will work towards improving the vaccination coverage and measles immunization, pentavalent vaccination among other vaccine preventable diseases. AWD/cholera cases that affects mostly the area during droughts or flooding will be managed as well with integration to WASH and nutrition interventions in the area. Health care services through consultation and management of childhood illnesses with an aim f reducing maternal and neonatal mortality will be given focus. ANC and BEmONC with referrals of the CEmONC cervices will be provided to the pregnant mothers. Staff and the community will be equipped with adequate information to manage emergencies and outbreaks that may arise from the dynamic natural and conflict related concerns. EDEWS reports shall be shared with the in line MOH and the cluster including proper coordination so as to respond to emergencies like AWD/cholera, measles, and some haemorrhagic fever outbreaks. RRP staff will be equipped with trainings on ANC and BEmONC, IMCI, Emergency response preparedness and CHWs equipped with skilled on community management and targeting. Community dialogue will be key in adherence with the AAP framework. RRP is currently having and integrated project in Jalalaqsi of both health nutrition and WASH, to be able to have continuity of the intervention, we will ensure the the response plan captures the needs already identified in the area during the implementation.

Mitigation Measures

RRP will undertake a conflict sensitivity analysis that will assess issues of conflict and existing community structures that can contribute to peace-building and conflict resolution. The results of the analyses will serve as a guide for ensuring that project stakeholders understand the complex dynamics within each beneficiary community. We will also ensure staff; partners, and communities involved in the project are aware of and take into consideration actions that have the potential to cause conflicts within and between community and social groups. Gender disparities are visible in the operational communities, where women usually take a back seat in issues of community development. The project, through community mobilization and awareness, will ensure women are empowered and represented to influence decisions and outcomes.Gender mainstreaming has been considered in the design of this project at all levels. All targeted beneficiaries will be registered, profiled and data disaggregated by gender and age as for the following categories: 1. Women and pregnant women 2. Children under 5 years 3. Population under 18 years 4. IDPs resident in IDP camps 5. Access of men to transmissible diseases services During implementation and monitoring of the services a participatory and gender sensitive approach will be adopted to allow equal opportunity to all. The hygiene and health promotion interventions will involve hospital staff, both health workers and support staff, patients, relatives and visitors with specific sessions addressed to mothers of patients in pediatric ward and women in obstetric department. Gender specific group discussions and interaction are organized. Hospital staff recruitment will respect gender equality ensuring opportunities to all genders on the base of the professional qualification.

Please provide further explanation

The proposed project centres on increasing communities’ resilience to short-term and chronic emergencies by strengthening health systems and improving the overall health of the beneficiaries. The program will address the immediate need to improve health and nutrition outcomes while working towards the longer-term goal of sustainably integrating nutrition into the health system framework, thereby attracting government strategic and financial commitments that will enable continuous provision of quality health services. The project will implement activities alongside or directly through MOH staff to ensure that the capacity and responsibility for the provision of health and nutrition services remains within government facilities. Targeted trainings and supportive follow-up will strengthen local partners’ (e.g. CHVs, health workers, community leaders) capacity to deliver quality services in the long-term. The project will specifically invest in CHVs as these individuals will remain in their communities to further health and nutrition education and link communities to services. RRP will work towards linking the health systems with the in line MOH. RRP coordinating approaches and/or leveraging ongoing programs and complementary to counter the impact of limited presence of humanitarian actors in these zones. Piloting community centred approaches along with NGO, donors and communities presence in these inaccessible or hard to reach areas in order to enable protection programming at the community level and strengthening of communities’ self-protection mechanisms and resilience. Ensuring clearance operations, information sharing and risk awareness around explosive hazards; and ensuring more robust information sharing and evidence based advocacy on Protection of Civilians. Medical ethics on patients confidentiality will as well be adhered in the project implementation.
