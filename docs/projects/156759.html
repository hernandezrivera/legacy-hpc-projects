Project Summary

Since the beginning of the crisis in 2011, Syria has witnessed significant challenges in the humanitarian and security situation across the country resulting in an urgent need for humanitarian intervention to vulnerable people in all aspects.
After eight years of war, the situation continues to deteriorate in certain areas, with continuing violence, destruction for people and structures to infrastructure, economy and the individual livelihood. And the life-sustaining services such as water, sanitation and electrical systems as well as attacks affecting hospitals, schools and housing continue to affect the civilian life and the most important need for living.
On the other hand and after the changing of security and safety and situation in new big areas of Syria and the results of the reconciliation a significant number of IDPs were able or would like to return to their communities of origin increasing the need to repair and rehabilitate damaged houses to move to word these solutions.
Families members have been separated, kidnapped, tortured or subjected to physical or sexual abuse unemployment, loss of property, psychosocial, distress.
Even the same interventions help in mitigating their suffering but still the needs huge especially to reunite the family members by returning home to protect them (especially the children).
In the belief that shelter projects should be individual-centered, focusing primarily on enabling and assisting household self-recovery.
GOPA/DERD ensure that the interests and protection of beneficiaries are at the local of this project by rehabilitating their house; it’s tailored to the different needs of vulnerable IDPs, returnees and affected communities.
Total of 4.7 M people are in need for shelter assistance and 80 percent of Syrian population live under the poverty line leaving millions of people without sufficient means to care of their families.
Since the conflict in Syria before eight years all of the population has been forced to the flee their homes often multiple times which affected the quality of life and exposed them to multiple risks and violations of their rights, there have been countless deaths and devastating injuries, illness and no humanitarian dignity.
The most important need now is to prepare healthy house, a roof to protect them from cold, to enable them to start again life especially the children.
Thus and beyond survival, rehabilitation of house is necessary to provide personal security and human dignity to sustain family and community life and to enable affected population to recover from the impact of crisis. The main purpose of this project is to improve the living situation for most vulnerable families through rehabilitation of 500 damaged houses.
Our project will target the following areas with rehabilitation of private apartments as following: Aleppo: 125, Rural Damascus: 175, Al-Raqqa: 100, Deir Ez-Zor: 100

Needs

This year alone, the crisis has led to the displacement of hundreds of thousands of individuals and families in East Ghouta, Deir Ezzor and Rural Homs. Hundreds of thousands of IDPs in those areas are lacking adequate shelter, sanitation facilities, basic assistance and access to services.Shelter rehabilitation remains one of the most necessary support and a first step to help IDPs, returnees and other vulnerable groups return to their homes. It plays an essential role in reducing vulnerability and building communities' resilience. Shelter projects serve as a main component of humanitarian support because of their economic, social, and cultural importance, where the significantly deteriorated private apartments occupy the majority of the land in each city and town in Syria. Their situation has fallen below commonly-accepted minimum humanitarian standards.More so, shelter is far more than a roof and four walls; adequate space and physical conditions are important, but buildings should not be considered as the only or main dynamic in shelter, nor should building improvements be the only criteria for shelter intervention. This said, GOPA-DERD introduces a new shelter intervention in this proposal of private apartments. since it would be providing protection, financial security and recovery of their dignity, not to mention the re-establishment of a beneficiary's economic well-being and secure his/her livelihoods.Thus, and beyond survival, shelter is necessary to provide security and ensure personal safety and protection, and to promote resistance to ill-health and disease. It is also important for human dignity, to sustain family and community life, and to enable affected populations to recover from the impact of crisis.For this purpose, shelter that includes apartments for instance, should be addressed as a whole rather than separately. Since the rehabilitation of both are desperately needed to support crisis-affected communities.In the belief that shelter projects should be individual-centered, focusing primarily on enabling and assisting household self-recovery, GOPA-DERD ensures that the interests and protection of beneficiaries are at the center of this project. This project in accountable for beneficiaries by rehabilitating their apartments. It is tailored to the differentiated needs of vulnerable IDPs, returnees and affected communities.

Implementation of activities

The following procedures will be applied to the implementation of the project:1-Location: Important factors should be taken in consideration for the targeted location.Availability of sewerage, water, electricity. IDPs is returning to the area. Safety. Accessibly 2-Needs assessment: A field team have conducted a need assessment in the targeted area, the report indicates that the top priority needs is the rehabilitation of private houses due to the big number of IDPs 350000 IDPs from Deir zzour. 600000 IDPs from East Ghouta. 100000 IDPs from Aleppo.3-Beneficiaries selecting criteria: The following criteria should be applied in selecting beneficiaries such as in summary:-Poor household, widows, orphaned children.-Families with disabled, elderly.-Families with a sick member (chronic diseases).-Big number of families.-Household without fixed income, are female-headed household.4-Building criteria:According to the classification of GOPA/DERD for the damages of the houses:1-Totally damaged building: it is not accepted.2-Partially damaged building: it is accepted if the building is saved.3-The building sound: accepted.5-BOQ:- Usually, every house should have a sufficient BOQ consisting all the works should be conducted for repairing the house to be able for living.- The kind and quantity should be according To budget.- Rehabilitation should consist of living and bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom, W.C.- BOQs are being explained directly with beneficiaries to give them the right to share the work.- Some necessary modification may be made during the implementation phase as repairing needs.7- Announcement for bidding in a public newspaper.8- Implementation: The contractor start the works based on approved a time schedule 9- Take Over - Hand over: After all works are being completed a special committee take over, from the contractor the executed works ascertain (quality – quantity – time) acc. To contract condition.

Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: Increase tension between beneficiaries due to difference in assessments and in providing services, lead to feeling of discrimination. likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1) apply the need assessment to fixed criteria with all sector partners. 2)sharing the community representative in this stage of intervention. 3) activate the feedback with beneficiaries.Protection Risk: Risk of hurts to families community or owners due to unsound buildings or from ERW during preparing BOQ and implementation. likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1- Structural assessment for building by qualified engineers and ERW experts. 2- repair the building after assuring the soundness and safety of building. 3- clarify for workers about the dangers of ERW and to inform about any strange materials.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Unforeseen security situation in general which might affect the implementation of activates in general ( Low risk). Impact: Medium. Mitigating measures: GOPA will keep consulting with sector on monthly basis about the implementation and monitoring of action plan. Level of risk: Acceptable.Lack of access, no secured roads for shipping the materials to the designated places (Low risk). Impact: Medium. Mitigating measures: GOPA will try to provide adequate spaces in its warehouses to store all goods to ease the implementation. Level of risk: Acceptable
