Project Summary

The on-going “Crisis” in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon which breaks out since 2016/incorporated with Cholera outbreak in 2022, flood/landslide disasters in 2023 in NWSW regions due to climate change, has resulted in increased displacements of people, high risk of hygiene-related morbidity/mortality rates.

OCHA_SITREP_No:57_August 2023_report, 1.0M among the 1.3M PiN of humanitarian assistance in WASH/Health, 638,421 IDPs/442,580 Returnees as target, PIN-TARGET WASH HNO-HRP2024 of 20 Nov. 2023 is 787,8K.
ECHO Dec 2022 report on cholera: >14,885 cases/298 deaths recorded (week 47) (MoH) of Cameroon, as result of lack of water/sanitation. WHO Cameroon 2022 report: 6652 suspected cases including 134 deaths.
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 4 Subdivisions/4 health districts in 5 villages in rural, urban/semi-urban in Nkambe, Bamenda, Buea, Limbe, 3 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Mezam, Fako) in the NWSW Regions of Cameroon. Targeted: 10,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (1260 Boys/1274 Girls) <5yrs; (1260 Boys/1273 Girls)<18yrs; (1367 Men/1400 Women) 18-59yrs; (660 Men/673 Women) +60yrs, PWDs (413 Men/420 Women))
AIM:
To improved access to safe drinking water, hygiene related services to reduce cholera, death rates of children<5yrs & poverty by the end of June 2024.

ACTIVITIES
-Promotion of safe hygiene/sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people
-Construction of water points (boreholes, small water supply network...)
-Construction of gender-sensitive latrines in IDP sites/host communities
-Capacity-building of WASH actors in emergency WASH response/relative issues
-Ensuring availability of essential healthcare in areas affected by the crisis
OBJECTIVES
-Improve sustainable access to basic sanitation/safe drinking water to vulnerable people
-Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity/mortality of affected population in the crisis
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve WASH services/resilience of affected populations
BENEFICIARIES
Affected population (IDPs/returnee’s/host communities/minorities group) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 50% IDPs, 10% PWDs, 30% Host Communities/10% returnees
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations.
APPROACH: Community based approach/awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials including PSEA, collaboration with stakeholders/workshops, community/mobilization/engagement/construction/M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs/KII/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Engage MINEE/MINSANTE/Municipal Council.
SUSTAINABILITY
Trained (CPCs for continuos sensitisaton on HP; WMCs/supply with tools box; IDPs how to treat/reuse latrines). Improve sustainable latrines. Patient hospital bills pay/health units have access to water/latrines.
JUSTIFICATION
Increased # of improved (sustainable latrines/water points) to reduce cholera, diarrhoea, deaths/proverty.

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

NEEDS: 3,020 people living in poverty are in need of quality and sufficient quantity of sustainable clean water for drinking, laundry, preparing food, bath, washing of hands with soap for delivery of new-born to reduce maternal/neonatal deaths, cholera and diarrhea including deaths to children under 5 years for they collect water from an unimproved water point. Lack of improved sustainable sanitation facility (latrines) to homes. Lack promotion of good wash practices to 10,000 affected population. Trained the affected population on feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors on AAP. Lack of trained water management committees (WMCs) to manage their water supply and supplies of tools box with latrines management. Training of Nurses, community protection committees (CPCs) and community health workers (CHWs), volunteers and animators as health and hygiene promoters. Create community health clubs (CHCs) and trained. Lack of finance assistance for the payment of hospital bills to 20 most vulnerable people of marginalised/minorities gruops, IDPs, returnees and host communities members. 1 hospital or health center is in need of health facilities support such construction of one boreholes with solar power system and one improved sustainable latrines to 10 beds or 20 outpatients. NB : Direct beneficiaries are 3,060 and indirect beneficiaries is 6,940 NB: Construction of borehole underground water scheme is with small solar powered system of 4 taps per borehole. From the Sphere handbook 2018 Edition EN: householde toilet is 1 toilet for 5 persons or 1 family. i.e. 4 gender-sensitive family HH latrine x 5= 20 people while for hospitals/medical centre is 1 toilet for 10 beds or 20 outpatients.
