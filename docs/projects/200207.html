Project Summary

Currently, the multidimensional crisis in Lebanon led to a very challenging access to surgical care. Cost is consistently reported as the main barrier, and surgeries and diagnostic tests fees became unaffordable to families, who started to adapt negative coping mechanisms by deprioritizing health needs and delaying their required treatments.
This project aims at enabling access to general surgeries for vulnerable children, including coverage for urgent acute surgeries and for congenital disorders requiring urgent surgical intervention. The project’s implementation will adapt a comprehensive package, addressing complementary needs beyond the surgery itself for the child and his/her family, in a holistic and patient-centered approach.
Referral and eligibility of cases
Building on the existing connections of CDE, and with further development of this network, an introduction of the project will be delivered to the health sector actors through the health working groups, and through coordination with I/NGOs, hospitals, PHCs, syndicates… to explain our referral system and the coverage that we’re providing.
In addition, community-based organizations and community leaders will be outreached whenever possible, prioritizing the districts identified as highly vulnerable (level 4) in the latest MSNA.
A referral system will be put in place, with a hotline number accessible 24/7, allowing CDE medical department to receive referrals for vulnerable children needing surgeries, and to give approvals for the needed care.
Clear medical criteria will be defined in collaboration with HQ experts, and the main pathologies in the pediatric group needing general surgical care will be chosen, be it congenital disorders or acute conditions. Then, corresponding surgical codes will be mentioned and approved for each procedure.
Besides the medical criteria, vulnerability will be also briefly assessed by CDE team before giving the approval. Only cases which do not benefit from any other insurance / coverage will be included. CDE will cover 100 % of the medical/surgical costs linked to the pathology treated. 109 Lebanese, PRL and migrant children from birth till 17 years old will be eligible.
Hospitals Selection and Hospitalization Coverage
Through a transparent tender process for our surgical codes, hospitals will be contracted all over Lebanon, prioritizing public hospitals for sustainability purposes, while taking into consideration both the proposed quotations and the quality of care to be delivered. Then, the contracted hospitals’ admission team will be given the needed guidance to process with the medical approval by CDE.
The support will cover ER consultations fees when a surgery is needed, diagnostic tests fees (laboratory tests, imaging), surgical procedures with the needed proforma, in addition to hospitalization fees.
In addition, support will be provided to caregivers, through the preferred modality of communities, which will be determined at the inception of the project (for example coverage of transportation cost, etc.)
Post-Surgical Comprehensive Approach.
The project will include a multi-sectorial detailed vulnerability assessment for each family, in order to identify potential additional needs, in particular protection and to refer accordingly.
In addition, a psychosocial support will be offered by CDE psychologist to the caregivers (women and men) and to the children (if their age allows), be it individual or group sessions, depending on the needed intervention. In general, ten sessions will be offered for each beneficiary, extendable if needed. The participation is optional, however, our previous projects’ experience showed that this support is requested and really needed, with more than 85% of our beneficiaries participating and benefiting from PSS sessions post surgeries.
Post-surgical tests and imaging will also be covered as required for an appropriate follow-up.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

The project contributes to Strategic Objectives 1 and 3 of the Health Sector - More to precisely sector objective/outcome “Ensure access to life-saving and life-sustaining health services for those most vulnerable and in need in the context of worsening socio-economic conditions”.The project includes the following activity: “Patients supported with hospital costs and advanced diagnostics as a precursor to hospital admission”. The cost for surgeries and related pre/post care will be 100% covered for eligible patients. To a lesser extend, through the multi-sectorial social assessment done for each patient/family, the appropriate and fast referrals, the feedback and complaint mechanism and more generally the strong efforts to mainstream protection and AAP, the project also contributes to objective : “Provide enabling, cross-cutting support to coordinated, safe, timely and quality health responses with an emphasis on vulnerable communities”, in particular activity : “Enhanced protection environment in humanitarian health response”

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

CDE has a PSEA policy signed by all staff, with regular refreshers/sensitization received on this topic. Contracted hospitals also sign and commit to this policy.During the planning phase, CDE will undertake a specific assessment of the protection, gender, and social inclusion risks and opportunities in the project area. Then, during the implementation, a systematic detailed multisectoral assessment will be conducted for each patient/family after the surgery, to detect potential needs for additional services such as protection, and to refer accordingly.Finally, our surgical coverage is expected to support girls and boys equally, depending on the surgery prevalence. (some surgeries are more common for one gender than the other). From previous projects’ experiences, most caregivers participating in PSS sessions were women (mothers), however we encourage men to participate and benefit from these sessions, and whenever need be, some gender specific sessions are organized.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The affected population will be engaged in part of the project design : focus group discussions/interviews will be conducted with the community members, to better understand the needs and priorities of the families whose child needs a surgery, and address them through the family support component of the project. A community outreach will inform vulnerable families about the services, including translation to migrants preferred language if needed.An easy access to our existing feedback and complaint mechanism will be granted, allowing affected population to share views and opinions about the project, and prompt and comprehensive answers.Impact and satisfaction surveys are organized for patients and their parents at 1 and 6 months after the surgery and regularly analyzed.CBOs will be part of the referral system.The multisectorial social assessment allows to refer the child/families both to other health actors for specific complementary needs and/or to different sectors’ actors.
