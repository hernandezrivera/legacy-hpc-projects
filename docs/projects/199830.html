Project Summary

Partners access up-to-date, regular and systematic information to respond to humanitarian, peace and development response
The Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) provides support to the Government of Mozambique and humanitarian partners by establishing a comprehensive system that collects, analyses, and disseminates data on IDPs and returnees to ensure timely and practical assistance to the affected populations. In each round of DTM assessments, IOM works in coordination with a network of district focal points, Institute for Disaster Risk Management and Reduction (INGD) technicians and community structures to collate data in the field, including displacement baseline data at locality-level. DTM additionally conducts detailed assessments in displacement sites at regular intervals to ensure accurate and up-to-date information- collecting age, sex and vulnerability disaggregated data.

IOM has been implementing its DTM baseline assessments in the districts most affected by insecurity since February 2019 but modified its methodology in July 2021 to adapt the tools to the evolving nature of displacement in northern Mozambique. DTM covers all accessible districts in Cabo Delgado impacted by displacement due to insecurity, as well as the provinces of Inhambane, Nampula, Niassa, Sofala and Zambezia. IOM's DTM team has grown with well-trained and experienced DTM enumerators, a strong information management structure supporting robust data processing and analysis. DTM's field presence is based on dynamic and responsive mechanisms, capable of scaling up activities in a short space of time.
Under the proposed intervention, DTM will continue with mobility tracking assessments to gather and analyse data in order to disseminate critical multi-layered information on displaced and mobile populations' mobility, vulnerabilities, and multi-sectoral needs. As such, IOM enables decision-makers and responders to provide displacement affected populations with better and context-specific assistance. Through technical and operational advantages in the region, DTM will most especially support primary data collection for holistic, inter-sectoral analysis of crisis-affected population needs, vulnerabilities, causes and risks while strengthening the collection of data based on age, gender and disability disaggregation for the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) and Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) 2022.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Northern Mozambique is facing an ongoing complex crisis marked by a dire humanitarian situation. For over four years, northern Mozambique has experienced attacks by Non-State Armed Groups (NSAG) allegedly affiliated with the Islamic State, resulting in large scale displacements. The complex crisis has led to large displacement over the past four years. According to IOM, 784,319 internally displaced persons (IDPs) are currently internally displaced in northern Mozambique (Displacement Tracking Matrix - DTM, February 2022) . The dire humanitarian situation in northern Mozambique is therefore not only fuelled by the humanitarian needs of the displaced persons, many of whom have been forced to flee multiple times, but also by the needs of the host communities, who have continued to show solidarity in the face of increasingly scarce resources and stretched services. Indeed, humanitarian needs remain significant across all sectors, with a total of 1.5 million persons in need, according to the 2022 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO). As services have been largely disrupted in northern Mozambique since the onset of the crisis, IDPs as well as host communities lack access to the most basic services and items. IOM is proposing a comprehensive and integrated Health, WASH, CCCM, DTM, MHPSS and Shelter intervention, with the main objective to ensure dignified, safe and secure living conditions and basic services for vulnerable populations affected by disasters driven by natural hazards and conflict, and to contribute to empowered and resilient individuals and communities in northern Mozambique The project will build on the Humanitarian Development and Peacebuilding Nexus (HDPN) approach to reduce overall vulnerability and the number of unmet needs, strengthen risk management capacities, respond to critical needs while increasing resilience, addressing the root causes of fragility and displacement, and supporting long-term solutions.
