Project Summary

The conflict in Amhara region has inflicted heavy losses to livestock and poultry production and market systems. AGRI-BILCHA team field observation of the impact along the Debreberhan- Dessie corridor and information compiled from woreda experts and local leaders indicated that the poultry and livestock value chains are highly affected by the conflict, possibly making what was an already precarious health and nutrition situation in the region. With the seasonal crop and livestock decimated, millions of people in the region, particularly in South Wollo zone, are facing the impending crisis of livelihood insecurity with women and the young being most severely affected. Most farmers have lost their poultry and dairy animals due to shortage of feed and limited health care while still many farmers lacked financial resources to purchase inputs. With market systems affected, supply chains have become obsolete and farmers could not sell their animals and poultry. A targeted support will go some way in rebuilding depleted livelihoods and speedup the return to normal agriculture activities. This activity will immensely motivate semi-urban and rural poultry and dairy producers in South Wollo Zone to start poultry and dairy business once again, speedup the recovery process for sustainable food and nutrition security. This project targets 8000 household.
Specific Objective
The specific objectives of the proposed intervention include:
 To motivate the conflict-affected farm households to start production and market activities;
 To strategically identify the most affected producers and target them with appropriate inputs and follow on support for a speedy recovery;
 To create improved enabling environment for a resilient rural economy; and
 To introduce Good Agricultural Practices and benefit the community at large.

1. - Projects activities

The Food and Nutrition Sensitive Agriculture project will be implemented in conflict-affected woredas of South Wollo zone, Amhara Region. AGRI-BILCHA will provide much needed strategic support to targeted farmers and small enterprises involved in vegetable production, semi-commercial and commercial poultry and dairy farming to resume normal agriculture activities creating jobs, improving incomes, nutrition security and speedup recovery for a sustainable livelihood. The activities are deliberately selected for their potential impact in improving nutrition, reviving the local economy and likely impact on as many beneficiaries as possible in a space of few months. They are selected to revive agricultural production, improve food and nutrition and place the farming communities on a rapid recovery path to normal life. Considering the magnitude of livelihood loss and high population concentration, South Wollo Zone with four highly affected woredas is selected for implementing a package of activities with a total budget of $400,000. AGRI-BILCHA will target farmers and small enterprises involved in horticulture, poultry and dairy sectors with a set of activities that have been tested and proven to lead to measurable impact in a space of few months. The total expected number of beneficiaries is 40,000 or about 8,000 households. Based on the production potential, husbandry culture of the target communities, expected impact, project timeframe and the need to empowering women through enhanced nutrition and asset rebuilding technical and material support along with a package of technical support will be provided to smallholder producers and local enterprises of Kallu, Dessie Zuria, Kombolcha and Tehulederie woredas, South Wollo.Key activities include:• 10,000 Day Old Chicks supplied to poultry farmers • 800 quintal pullet feed and another 625 quintal layer feed distributed to poultry producers• Equipment like egg trays, crates, drinkers, de-bakers, etc supplied to poultry producers • 1,200 quintal feed supplied to dairy farmers• 55kg of tomato, pepper and onion seed distributed to cover at least 17.5 hectare landGiven that poultry and dairy products are nutrient dense, they will help children and women affected by under-nutrition and malnutrition during the conflict and its aftermath. National and global experience indicates that, a complete package of activities provided to poultry have multi-dimensional benefits, like gender empowerment, job creation and nutrition enhancement to alleviate malnutrition, increase incomes, create jobs and speedup asset rebuilding. In addition, investment on poultry does have a fast turn over possibilities. Given that poultry requires limited land and water resources for investment coupled with its limited impact on the environment, it is most ideal for smallholder farm households eking out a living on meagre resources. In this respect, the supply of poultry genetic materials (day old chicks with full packages of medicines, vaccines and feed) compound feed supplementation and critical equipment will be implemented following a cost-share scheme. It will immensely support particularly women and youth ultimately leading to a huge impact on the respective communities. Similarly, the provision of feed supplements for dairy targeting households with dairy animals will increase the supply of milk for domestic consumption, improve nutrition and further revive local business in milk and milk products in the target woredas. AGRI-BILCHA believes that through these complimentary interventions, a total of 8,000 beneficiaries will be reached in the space of four month to impact nutrition, improve livelihoods and consolidate peace and social harmony
