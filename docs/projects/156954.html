Project Summary

PUI during the project is planning to support 370,000 with better access to WASH services through :
• enhance the access to water supply through Water Network Rehabilitations & Rehabilitations of boreholes, where network needs to be enhanced to respond to the increased needs and where network has been damaged;
• better access to Sewage services through Sewage Network Rehabilitations, where network needs to be enhanced to respond to the increased needs and where network has been damaged;
• ensure better environmental health through Debris & Garbage Removal, targeting newly opened locations with returnee populations;
• Emergency Water Trucking, targeting areas experiencing severe shortage of water, where water networks have been damaged, where drastic population increases are reported due to displacements and returnees movement;
• Emergency onsite latrine and shower to respond to the urgent displacement and improve access to sanitation/sewage facilities and services.
• Emergency water facilities to respond to the urgent displacement and improve access to water.
• Distribution & installation of communal water tanks, targeting areas where drastic population increases are reported due to displacements;
• Distribution of HH level water tanks, targeting vulnerable households with access constraints to communal sources;
• Distribution of jerry cans, targeting newly displaced populations
PUI will ensue the full cooperation with relevant stakeholders, SARC, and WASH sector, to facilitate needed approvals, avoid overlapping between INGOs and share previous experiences and knowledge

Needs

As the crisis and armed conflict continues within the Syrian Arab Republic, an economic recession has emerged along with international economic sanctions all further exacerbated by the level of destruction across the country An estimated 15.6 million people lack access to adequate water and sanitation facilities, more specifically (HNO-2019 Draft) : • Access to sufficient quantities of safe water remains limited, with increased water quality assurances and support to water systems rehabilitation, operation and maintenance unconditionally needed. • At least 10 per cent of garbage is left in the public areas, which could leave people in some areas at increased risk of contracting infectious diseases such as Leishmaniosis.• As a result of the crisis at least 70 per cent of sewage is untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems are not functional, exposing communities to significant health risks such as Acute Bloody Diarrhea, Typhoid or Jaundice. • There is a need to maintain the provision of adequate WASH services and supplies for IDPs in the last resort sites, and to promote transition to more sustainable solutions while acknowledging that more emphasis on IDPs in collective centers and open areas is also needed. Assessment(HRP Draft 2019) reported differences, in terms of needs between age and gender groups, mainly in equal access to WASH facilities, Woman and girls, now are living within extended families, collective shelter and, with lack of well led gender segregated living spaces or WASH facilities, which will deprive them from privacy and endangering them to Gener Based Violence, additionally with no available link to public water network from inside the house, either due to internal damage in the house, or external damage in the water network system, women are forced to walk to long distances to secure water, all together made women and girls the most affected. As the context remains fluid and fragile, and political solutions are yet to come to fruition, humanitarian actors need to adapt their interventions to reduce the impact which eight years of conflict has had on the population – focusing on linking humanitarian emergency response the recovery in accessible areas.

Protection Risk Analysis

PUI is working in accordance with “Do No Harm” principle, seeking no further harm to the people in need. Therefore the proposed activities taking in consideration the potential risk that might unintendedly happen and, the measures that should be followed to mitigate them.More specifically for this project, based on our experience and WASH sector-PRA PUI proposed the following: - Risk of GBV might affect women and girls if toilets are not gender sensitive, and not available to all 24/7, To mitigate this PUI will built Gender segregated toilettes with locks and lights, to ensure safe access to women and girls.- Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials as mitigation measure PUI always builds a protection infrastructure to protect such materials, and communicates with the community stakeholders usually the municipality regarding the protection and maintenance works of them.- Inequality in distribution might increase the inter- community tension, to mitigate this risk PUI will communicate with all the stakeholders and the targeted beneficiaries to aware them about the intervention and that the field staff are available for any complain

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The following are seen as key assumptions: - Security situation remains stable, and humanitarian access consistent; accordingly PUI ensure through the intervention that all security procedures are in place and followed . - No major fluctuation of Syrian Pound; - No mass movements of populations into/out of intervention areas;- Financial transactions are still possible to Syria . Below are the main risks to the action and the measures PUI will take in response: - Local population does not accepts PUI´s intervention; to mitigate this PUI will ensure the full engagement of the community leaders and local stakeholders in project design and well introduce the benefits to the targeted community that will occur . - Lagging Return movements; it is not fully under PUI control, however PUI follows a multi sector integrated approach in programming to support the community resilience in a multi dimensions which might encourage more people to return to their original houses, if the lack of access to basic services is their main hinder to return . - Delay in receiving authorizations and clearances to quickly intervene. As mitigation to this PUI is fully coordinating with the relevant line ministries to facilitate the process of getting needed approvals
