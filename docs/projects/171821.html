Project Summary

A combination of serious flooding owing to above average Gu 2020 rains in most parts of South Central Somalia coupled by locust invasion, intermittent conflict and insecurity as well as socio – economic impacts of Covid -19 containment measures has significantly impacted onto food security status of the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and marginalized host communities in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions, Somalia (July/August 2020 post-Gu assessment ). The mentioned situation has significantly weakened already fragile food security status of the the most vulnerable and IDPs, returnees and marginalized host communities' (men, women boys and girls) in the project targeted locations. The situation is compounded by anticipated La Niña conditions associated with high likelihood of consecutive below-average rainfall seasons, while there is projected high risk that drought conditions will develop by early 2021 whereas Desert Locust could exacerbate crop and pasture losses. The plight calls for timely life-saving and life -sustaining Food security support through conditional and non conditional safety nets as well as restoring already weakened and fragile livelihoods and further strengthen resilience of the most vulnerable IDPs and marginalized communities to cope with the current and future shocks. The project targets a total of 4333 HHs or 26,000 beneficiaries/ persons (women 10816, men 9984, boys 2600 and girls 2600) in Middle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions, Somalia.

Project Activities

1. Rehabilitation of 8 water catchments and 6 feeder roads in the targeted locations in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions on cash for work scheme to benefit 1200 vulnerable and displacedpersons 2. Provision of food vouchers to 1500 most vulnerable and displaced women headed households and those with disability and health problems in the targeted villages in Bay and Bakool Regions 3. Provision of unconditional and conditional cash transfers for the most vulnerable and displaced 1500 women headed households, those with chronic illnesses, elderly and those with disabilities in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions 4. Provision of fodder for animals to 800 pastoral families in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions 5. Provision of emergency veterinary services (animal vaccination and treatment) to 3200 most vulnerable and displaced pastoral families in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions 6. Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by employing security staff/ guards while ensuring centrality of distribution point to limit the long distance covered by women and girls that expose them to risks of GBV. Furthermore the project implementation will ensure activities are completed before cover of the darkness to limit risks of GBV that normally occurs in the cover of darkness2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhere to it as well as putting in place SEA reporting mechanisms and conducting awareness on its utilization3.
