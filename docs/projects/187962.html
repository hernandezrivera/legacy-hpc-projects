Project Summary

Takaful aims to secure a safe environment for 78000 children through an integrated approach that focuses on providing life-saving education and protection assistance to crisis-affected children to protect and promote the healthy environment in which they are located through providing integrated and harmonious services in the education and protection sectors
supporting formal and non-formal education for 78000 dropped-out children or who are at risk of dropping out of school, in 220 schools, as follow
Providing three cycles of non-formal education through catch-up classes for 13000 out of schools’ children using curriculum B and each cycle will target 5000 children. The children will be identified through BTLs campaigns and meetings with families and local actors.
Supporting formal education for 65000 children by providing operational costs, learning and teaching materials, schools’ supplies, and heating materials, in addition to providing weekly PSS sessions to overcome the harsh experience of war conditions and to motivate them to continue their studies at schools.
-In order to increase access to children to the targeted schools, the project will work on increasing schools' absorption capacities by conducting light rehabilitation for damaged/unused classrooms based on accurate need assessment, in addition to providing TLSs to decrease overcrowding and facilitate social distancing and to receive new students
-To ensure safe and easy access for CWD (boys and girls), Takaful will provide schools with tools and equipment for CWD such as ramps, Iron stair handrails, special toilets, not connected desks and chairs with height adjustable function in addition to providing transportation for CWD
-Takaful will work to conduct awareness sessions to minimize the spread of the Corona Virus for all staff and students, together with sterilizing all schools before students get in.
-The project will provide 3500 teachers and education personnel with incentives in 220 schools. in addition to providing 3500 teachers and education personnel with training and learning circles
-To ensure the best community engagement, meetings will be held with local actors (males and females) of the targeted locations in order to describe the project's goal and hear their recommendations and collect detailed information about the specific locations of dropping out children and the reasons for their dropping out and their needs
Takaful Alsham uses oriented and flexible monitoring and reporting system in order to support and enhance the project implementation. The M&E system will enable staff of checking the appropriateness and effectiveness of the response and adjust specific activities in the response if the monitoring data indicate that the support provided is not meeting quality standards or is not as effective as it could be in responding to the priority of community needs
1. Students’ and teachers’ questionnaires will be conducted by Takaful's MEAL team in order to meet the needs of both men and women within the education staff as well as the students who receive Kits during the distribution process
2. Checklist Survey contains all distribution criteria to be filled by monitoring officers
3. Base endline survey for PSS activities
4. Narrative Distribution Reports after each distribution within a school
5. Photos verifying the distribution process
6. Pre and post-training assessments
7. Conducting Post Distribution Monitoring. The PDM activities will be carried out after each distribution activity. We select our sample randomly with a 95% confidence level and a 7% margin of error
8. Routine visits to schools to follow up on the implementation of the project. Bi-weekly reports will be composed and shared with the project management
9. Visits and checklist carried out for the PSS sessions
10. Pre-post surveys for protection activities
All the above activities will be implemented in 2022 and will be placed in the 2023 proposals to be implemented next year as well

5 - Implementation of activities

Takaful will support formal and non-formal education in 220 schools where those schools include 78000 students (boys and girls) and their fund has ended or will end soon. Teachers will be provided with incentives, as they don't receive any financial support from any other NGOs, monthly operational costs, and heating materials to face the cold of winter. PSS weekly sessions will also help children, who spent a long time away from school, ridding of the harsh experience of war conditions and motivate them to continue their studies at school. The 220 targeted schools will be equipped with safety and security tools, also, the project will involve 780 children with disability, who drop out of school, and overcome the difficulties that they may face by providing them with transportation services in addition to providing schools with tools for children with disability. Learning materials will be distributed to students, boys and girls. distributing teaching materials to teachers and teaching resources for the school. Moreover, teachers will receive light training sessions on non-formal education, curriculum B, child protection, and PSS.Takaful will support the educational process by providing 13000 Out of School Children (OOSC), boys and girls, IDPs, and HCs with non-formal education through catch-up classes using curriculum B.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Education personnel, Students participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Likelihood: LOW Mitigation: Learning facilities should have an enforced code of conduct against all forms of violence and unacceptable conduct, Teachers and school personnel should be trained on prevention and reporting of, Students and their caregivers should be sensitized on the prevention, and issues related to, physical punishment and bullying and the systems in place to prevent, identify and response to itRisk: Children are over-exposed to the risk of, or experience, harm by teachers, school personnel, organization staff or related entities.Likelihood: LOW Mitigation: Learning facilities should have Child Safeguarding polices and systems in place by the organization should be regularly monitored and corrective action taken when needed. Risk: Learning facilities or their surroundings are structurally unsafe or do not provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, dust, etc.) or from public health risks including Covid-19Likelihood: MediumMitigation: Learning facilities should be improved to ensure that buildings and grounds are safe and protected. The organization should ensure Child Safeguarding measures are in place before work start. Sufficient water and sanitation facilities should be provided and maintained. Learning facilities should have Covid-19 safety protocols in place


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk 1: The vulnerability of children and women in conflict circumstances highly increases and places them in higher risk of being exposed to exploitation, abuse and rights violations.Mitigation strategy: 1- Staff personnel will sign code of conduct and child protection policy.2- Most of the management and field staff had attended workshops to increase the awareness of PSEA cases.3- the compliance mechanism will be defined and shared with beneficiaries.Risk 2: There are not enough humanitarian staff (both male and female) at the distribution site monitoring the distribution process Mitigation strategy: 1- Ensure oversight of distributions by aid workers who are trained on PSEA, Codes of Conduct and how/where to report misconduct.2- Ensure field level PSEA Focal Points in distribution teams and/or that staff involved in distributions know how to report or refer any PSEA allegations received.3- Deploy gender balanced teams to support projects for disaster-affected communities.4- Consider wearing clearly marked organization logos on shirts/jackets/caps for ease of identity by beneficiaries.Risk 3 : when challenges and issues arose during distribution, they are not handled well, and there is no female employee if the beneficiary was a female1. Train field workers on PSEA2. Ensure female aid workers are present and involved in the resolution of the challenges3. Sensitize affected communities on PSEA and who/where to report


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Safety and security: locations are far from the front lines so change control is not expected.Risk: Changes in the security situation or authorities/groups in control of project areasmay make the project's activities less appropriate to the new situation.Likelihood: highimpact: HighMitigation:-In coordination with the Access Working Group, the security officer will help mitigate the risks through collecting information on security updates from field staff, the network of local councils, and civil society activists.-Ensure that staff members are trained in safety and security.-If an emergency arises that may threaten the safety of staff and/or beneficiaries, the staff is provided with directives to move to a safer location until the area is safe enough to continue with project activities or to ask for location change.- Preparing plan B, where if one of the targeted locations is affected by the military operations, it will be replaced with another location.Programmatic:Risk: Some of the targeted locations may be supported by another donor, which leads to duplication of services.Likelihood: LowImpact: MediumMS: -At the beginning of the project, we will re-check with related clusters and other networks to make sure that there isn’t any organization implementing similar programs in the targeted location.-If it appears that any organization was responded in one or more of the targeted locations, we will coordinate with the cluster to change the location.
