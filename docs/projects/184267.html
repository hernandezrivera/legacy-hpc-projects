Project Summary

The protection needs are very critical given the overstretched nature of limited resources due to the influx of IDPs, the emergence of the COVID-19 Pandemic. People with disabilities face additional vulnerabilities. CoDHNet will support the most vulnerable families in drought, locust and conflict affected internally displaced persons (IDP) camps and host communities of Somalia by addressing their urgent protection and prevention and response to gender-based violence (GBV).
The protracted multi-faceted armed conflict, recurrent natural disasters, and a resulting upsurge in internal displacement have led to a highly complex crisis of protection in Somalia. The purposes of this project is to fill the existing critical gaps in protection, GBV and CP through intervention to reduce the risks which are currently faced by the targeted children boys and girls in these areas, the incidents of SGBV, promote awareness on prevention and response to GBV and provide child protection services targeting 35,040 people (4,088 women 14,600girls, 14,600boys and 1,752men) including 3,504 people with disability.
The project will seek to increase the capacity of target communities to identify, report, and refer children with urgent child protection concerns as well as to provide psychosocial support services in the long term. This project will target 35040 people including (General protection activities to 11,914 people, CP activities to 11,563 children and GBV activities to 11,563 people) The project will conduct wide-ranging rights based public outreach and awareness raising; Provision of appropriate assistance to children and adolescent victims of SE; includes safety, medical care, PSS, referral to legal assistance; Provision of MHPSS, child protection, and GBV victim’s assistance services in the target areas; This project will provide protection activities of psychosocial support including specialized counseling sessions, group therapy, support groups, Medical referrals, legal aid counseling services, distribution of emergency social assistance (dignity kits), solar lanterns to survivors, awareness and sensitization on GBV prevention and response, capacity building for community structures on GBV prevention and response and comprehensive case management services at the women and children wellness centers and Safe houses.
Distribution of 50 wheelchairs and 50 support sticks for persons living with disabilities. There will be number of training and capacity building events (PSS and CP), targeting religion elders, women groups, youth, children, school teachers, police, and community-based protection committees; Adequate training for teachers and child protection staff in how to incorporate children living with disabilities into classroom learning and social service. Establishment of community-based child protection mechanisms training on raising awareness about existing referral pathways; Establishment of Child-friendly spaces, facilitation of psychosocial support for children including play therapy, art work, drawing, recreational activities and basic literary and numeracy.

1. - Projects activities

Act 1.1: CoDHNet will strengthen existing community structures on MHPSS prevention and response through training of 320 participants selected community members from different project locations Banadir and Gedo Regions on community-based protection mechanisms and also conducting awareness sessions at IDP and host community. Act 1.2: Provision of Psychosocial Support, Material assistance, Case Management, medical support Clinical Management of Rape for 2400 survivors of (women, girls, men and boys) in project target areas Dolow , Luq, Bardhere and Belet-Hawa of Gedo Region. Act 1.3: CoDHNet will establish 3 Women and Girls Safe Space (WGSS) based on the four regions of the project location the beneficiaries of the WGSS will be 2400 women and girls (1,680women and 720girls) Act 1.4: CoDHNet will conduct procure and distribution of emergency social assistance to 3,600 dignity kits and 3,600 solar lanterns to 3600 GVB survivors including people with disabilities who are at risk of GBV particularly at night in Dolow , Luq, Bardhere and Belet-Hawa of Gedo Region. Act 1.5: CoDHNet will carry out community sensitization; awareness sessions and forums at IDP sites to promote awareness on protection and Child protection risks faced by vulnerable community including women and children and will reach 35,040 people (4,088 women 14,600girls, 14,600boys and 1,752men) including 3,504 people with disability. Act 1.6: CoDHNet will develop and disseminate messages on GBV Prevention and Response service through radio spots in local language through local radio stations with wide coverage within the IDP camps and disseminate GBV and CP radio spots at prime hours to reach masses with the messages to 35,040 people (4,088 women 14,600girls, 14,600boys and 1,752men) including 3,504 people with disability. Act 2.1: CoDHNet will conduct psychosocial support, GBV and FGM, referral pathway in place with SOPs and service mappings training to 80 GBV service providers and Protection (CP/GBV) Staff. Act 2.2: Capacity building workshop training on protection and child protection to 80 (male and female) of community leaders and gatekeepers as champions of change both men and female. Act 2.3: Provision of MHPSS and GBV services to 3,504 people (1,402women, 877girls, 385men and 840boys) including people with special needs to protect and promote psychosocial well-being and treat mental disorders among survivors of GBV including people with disability from IDPs and host community. Act 2.4: Provision of Psycho social services on appropriate and timely support to 2,400 GBV survivors (Female/Male). Act 3.1: CoDHNet will construct 4 Child-Friendly Spaces (CFSs) for 4204 children (2102girls and 2102boys) in the target project areas including IDP Camps and facilitate psychosocial support sessions for children in CFSs in the IDP camps and host community who experience low to medium psychological distress including children with disability. Act 3.2: CoDHNet will conduct identification, documentation, tracing and reunification (IDTR) for 4202 UASC (2102boys and 2102girls) provided by trained and experienced IDTR/CP caseworkers in the three regions Act 3.3: CoDHNet will distribute 80 wheelchairs and 96 stick support for children/adult people living with disabilities; Act 3.4: CoDHNet will reach 35,040 people (4,088 women 14,600girls, 14,600boys and 1,752men) including 3,504 people with disability through community-led messaging on child protection risks, social norms, harmful practices, MRE on CP. Act 3.5. CoDHNet will conduct training to 168 CP actors [social workers, community volunteers, government officials, field coordinators] and non-child protection actors who have received CP training and demonstrate increased knowledge and skills in providing age- and gender-sensitive child protection services.Act 3.6: CoDHNet will conduct SPMS and include all vulnerable groups including minority clans and people with disabilities in all stages of the project

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance: Mitigation measures: (1) rotate distribution teams so no one team constantly visits the same settlements; (2) provide clear information to distribution actors and affected populations explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services / favours to anyone in exchange for receiving humanitarian assistance, ensuring that this information is communicated to women and girls. Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure and/or contamination by explosive hazards in targeted project area: Mitigation measures: (1) Consider the land tenure arrangements of IDPs, including statutory/legislative and customary access rights to land and inheritance rights. Advocate with land and property owners and local authorities to avoid eviction or disproportional rising in rent; (2) obtain permission before using or building on any land or property, in writing where possible; (3) avoid community tensions by providing the local/host community with access to camp services and assistance in situations where they are experiencing similar shortages of non-food items; (4) consult both displaced and host communities about humanitarian needs and assess whether assistance could cause tension, harassment, or conflict between the two communities; (5) keep lists of beneficiaries and personal data confidential and safe from unintended use – apply effective data protection measures.CoDHNet will include women, men, boys, and girls, vulnerable groups including minority clans and people with disabilities in all stages of the project like the decision-making meetings design stage and the project implementation, men and women will participate equally all the meetings.
