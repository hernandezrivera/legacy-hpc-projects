Project Summary

AHF, through the UNICEF continue maintaining the provision of nutrition services through stabilization center in Sarmada children’s hospital which considered as is one of the specialized referral hospitals at north east of Idleb governorate for health and nutrition cases, OTP in same location and Six RRTs covering the camps in commienties around Sarmada (in subdistrict Aldana), the staff of the centers and the RRTs is qualified and trained in many topics in nutrition cluster.
AHF- Sarmada Children specialized hospital provides 24/7 pediatric healthcare services include:
- Outpatients therapeutic Program Centre (OTP), which run by 1 pediatrician, 1 Nutritionist, 1 nurse and 1 data Entry cover OPD services on daily, providing assessment and management of outpatient pediatrics with malnutrition including SAM, MAM patient follow up, routine assessment and treatment for MAM and SAM without complication. So, receive referral from RRTs to OTP Centre.
stabilization center in Sarmada children’s hospital: which run by 1 pediatrician, 1 Nutritionist, 3 nurses (1 nurse every 8 hours , 3 shift)and 1 data Entry , 3 cleaner cover inpatient services on daily 24/7 , providing daily assessment and management of inpatient pediatrics with SAM with complication.
RRTs: six RRTs (each team includes: 1 Nutritionist,2CHW,1 data Entry) cover outreach services:
Screening, referral to OTP center and follow up children 6-59, pregnant and lactating women who are malnourished, Provide emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children
Distribute micronutrients for preventing of anemia and malnutrition among children and pregnant, lactating women, IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, monitor distribution and use of breast milk substitutes, Support health/nutrition education and promotion and Nutrition Supplements distribution
Target Area: Sarmada community , Camps and communities around Sarmada and between Sarmada and Aldana and near Bab Alhawa, Camps around Sarmada, Burdaqly, Babisqa, Al Kufir, Sarjaleh, Kafr Kafaldin.
Before the project implementation, M&E team will make a regular collecting the required data that help in making enlightened decisions. Monitoring and evaluation of the quality of the service provision through analyzing the collected data based on the Log-Frame, the project will be monitored and evaluated through three levels:
1) The operational, for which the project manager is responsible.
2) The technical, for which the technical team are responsible.
3) The objectives, for which the MEAL team is responsible.
Monitoring tools:
•Performance Indicator Tracking Tables (PITTs), issued in monthly basis.
•Satisfaction survey among beneficiaries and stakeholders will be analyzed and reported to stakeholders.
•Project Evaluation Report, by the end of the project-term.

4 - Needs

The latest escalation of violence in Northwest Syria is having devastating consequences on the civilians in Idleb. Since 16 December 2019, aerial bombardment intensiﬁed in southern Idleb displacing Hundreds of thousands of civilians from Ma’arrat An-Nu’man, Saraqab and the surrounding towns and villages. From 1 to 25 December, 244,000 people ﬂed their homes -mostly in southern Idleb with 80 percent of the new IDPs – 194,867 individuals – are women and children. Additional population may displace, if the hostilities expand to affect other parts of the Idleb area. According to Nutrition surveillance data, showed that there has been a 0.5% increase in global acute malnutrition among children under the age of 5 years during per/during one week of displacements. Moderate Acute Malnutrition (in children) has increased by 0.1 % and Moderate Acute Malnutrition among PLWs increased by 2.26%. This sharp increase in the cases in only one week, requires provision of high energy biscuits (for children and PLWs) and Lipid-based Nutrient Supplements (LNS) which function to prevent acute malnutrition . 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Risk of GBV against the staff (CHWs & Nutrition technicians) Propability: average, Impact: high Mitigation: staff will be selected form the targeted communities, each two CHW will conduct house hold visits together as a team and conducting visits will be done in coordination with local authorities, staff will be trained to identiﬁcation of GBV cases.2-Risk of Exploitation or abuse by staff when accessing assistance and services, Propability: Low, Impact: High, Mitigation: Clear explanation to the community about available services and targeted beneficiaries, reinforce the message that humanitarian services are FREE OF CHARGE and signal the importance to report abuse and exploitation to the service providers ensuring confidentiality of the reporting, consult periodically with the population receiving the services/assistance, through regular and targeted sessions, including separate sessions with different groups that may be more at risk, ensuring standards of data protection and confidentiality, design specific complaint mechanism, provide training to staff for code of conduct and PSEA.3-Risk of discrimination in providing services. Propability: Low. Impact: High, Mitigation: Ensuring that the designed phase focused on the accessibility of services by vulnerable groups with effective community-based complaints mechanisms, and ensure adherence to humanitarian principles, the project adopted a “Do No Harm” while providing services.4-Lack of consideration for the safety and security to access services and targeting of civilian infrastructure. Propability: Low, Impact: High. Mitigation: Consider mobility patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing a static facility or a mobile service or combine both; Conduct safety audit to ensure safe access by beneficiaries with decreased mobility, engage local authorities on safety and security in the community, select staff for all activities with adequate knowledge of the community.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

AHF will coordinate closely with the Nutrition cluster, as well as local authorities and humanitarian actors inside Syria throughout the proposed intervention, informing the cluster if and when adjustments are needed to the intervention. the Equipment are available, the place is available, the staff are available the required supplies and medication available the electricity is available by functioning electrical generators Risk 1: The close of borders which prevent the shipments of supplies and medications to access to facilities. mitigation: Develop a strong risk management strategy, (preparing an emergency plan /plan B/ according to the expected scenarios in conflicted areas) Risk 2: Conflict related threats including aerial bombardment, advancement of hostile actors and shifting alliances. . Mitigation: If any unseen circumstances happened, we will take the following actions: Postponing the services till safety is back or relocation if the project's activities can't be provided in the same locations..
