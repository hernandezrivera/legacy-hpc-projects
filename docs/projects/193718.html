Project Summary

Arab Ar-Rashayida is a village located in the eastern Bethlehem governorate and away from the center of Bethlehem city about 20 km.
The population is approximately 2,500 per capita (584 boys, 556 girls, 641 men, 614 women, 99 elders) distributed on housing within the community as well as in the surrounding areas as livestock breeders residing in the eastern slopes where they live in permanent homes (not tents), this will ensure sustainability and the maximum benefit of the water network services. They own over 30000 sheep and about 600 camels as well as several chicken farms (the largest number of sheep in the governorate).
While Za'tara is located east south Bethlehem, It suffers from low rainfall, so the population depends on purchasing water to provide it in areas that suffer from severe shortages, where the project will serve 2000 per capita (468 boys, 446girls, 514 men, 492 women, and 80 elders) distributors in the evangelical area as permanent residents of the area, also, 530 individuals beneficiaries as (126 children in the kindergarten stage, 301 children in the primary stage, 23 children in the secondary stage, 80 people make up the staff) school students and teachers from neighboring areas, with a large number of visitors to the public park that was established two years ago as the only park in Za’tara and the surrounding areas.
The project aims to provide Arab Rashaida, and Zatara villages access to safe and affordable drinking water to end the suffering of shortage water by providing proper water network services, through installing a new water pipeline (As water is available, but the population lacks a way to access it due to spread the resident in the area and un-linking it with a new water source by the Palestinian Water Authority(PWA) as in Zatara or the old network is worn out, as in Rashaida in a way that ensures the improvement of social, economic and health conditions in the targeted community.
According to the assessment, there is a significant deficit in water outreach, The following are the primary needs that the project will address:
1- Providing of domestic water services: It is about an extension Installation 1Km of 2", and 1450 meters of 1" of water network pipeline in Zatara as a new installation.
2- Installation and rehabilitation of the old existing water network by installing 4500 meters 3",2", and 1" pipelines of different diameters in Arab Ar-Rashayida in order to serve the whole targeted community.
The Bedouin of Arab Ar-Rashayida is the most vulnerable and marginalized community in the governorate according to the latest statistics by PCSB, which are represented by harsh and unstable conditions, which put more burdens on the community in general and on PWDA, and on women that will be on the expense of caring for her children, especially those with special needs who need to be integrated into society. Women play vital roles in dealing with WASH issues in households traditionally, they are usually responsible for providing drinking water to their houses from different water sources which are sometimes far away and dangerous due to the risks of settler attacks or wild animals.

Recently, increased numbers of Kidney disease cases were documented, this is mainly due to the contaminated water caused by the damages in the existing distribution network, this raised the need for renewing the existing water network in Ar Rashayda in addition to the high percentage of water loss and the high rate of rust to the highest level.

1 - Needs

According to the WASH cluster HRP goals, this proposal covered by Country Strategic Objectives: S.O.2: The basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through the provision of essential services, in accordance with the rights of protected persons under IHL. And falls within the WASH Cluster Objective 2: Ensure equitable, affordable, and sustainable safely-managed access to sufficient WASH services for the most vulnerable Palestinians. So it aims to Ensure acutely vulnerable Palestinians in Arab Ar-Rashayida, and Zatara in Bethlehem have access to essential services by improving access to WASH services by providing an appropriate domestic water services response for vulnerable women, men, girls, boys, and PWDA in fragile and critically underserved communities by ensuring access to clean drinking water in Zatara and Arab Rashaida in Bethlehem.AAA has conducted a refresh for the previous comprehensive needs assessment for the two communities with participating representatives of the village council, youth, and the women's center, the result revealed that there is a deterioration in the humanitarian situation due to the lack of any development or improvement in the last years on the water pipeline. the community suffers from a significant shortage of infrastructure and basic services and it needs rehabilitation in Ar Rashayda and the installation water pipeline in Zatara. AAA found that the core need of the targeted community is the provision of safe, affordable, accessible, and good-quality drinking water implemented to serve the community in a sufficient and sustainable way.The two regions suffer from low rainfall and consequently the inability to rely on wells, so water is purchased at high prices. Where the Evangelical School owns two wells with a volume of 950 cups, only 150 cups of rainwater are collected, while the school is forced to buy water at very high prices. The project will serve 3244 individual beneficiaries (795 boys, 722 girls, 834 men, and 798 women) and address communities' water needs by extending and rehabilitating the Za'tara and Arab Rashaida water network pipeline to improve water supply for human and animal use in the community. Both villages are considered disadvantaged in terms of basic requirements and services, particularly water and sewage facilities, with locations categorized as 100% area C and exposed to Israeli occupation breaches where they are located near the bypass. They suffer from an old water network in Ar Rashayda most of the lines require rehabilitation, rust appears clearly with part of the old water pipeline, and other areas require the installation of new lines, so water losses in the old network are very high and can reach up to 40%, and water supply capacity becomes very weak. In many cases, people in these sites receive water every 45 days during the summer and sometimes more as the women explained.The West Bank Water Service provides water to the people of the two villages via the public water network. These houses rely on rainwater harvesting wells for animal use (note that there is no sewage network and they rely on the absorption hole), and they purchase water tanks, whereas others rely on the network and tanked. In theory, the average water supply per inhabitant is less than 30 liters per day, and the cost of 1 cube exceeds 20 NIS. About 67.6 percent of the housing units are connected to the water network, 50 percent are dependent on the purchase of water tanks, and 0.9 percent are dependent on rainwater harvesting cisterns.The need assessment with Rashaiyda women showed that they suffer from the lack of water quantity and water quality for their households. This effect directly their cleanliness, health, and the health of their children, the health center was closed for a time this summer due to a lack of water, as the lack of quantity and pad quality of water increased diseases and infections. and women should be interested in that they should spend more time, effort, and money to bring more water with acceptable quality to manage their households. Do not forget also that women are responsible for livestock management at the farm (feeding, milking, and cleaning) and this needs a good quality and enough quantity of water, and women have to manage to bring water to implement their work at the farm. Providing water in an acceptable manner in an appropriate quantity reduces the risks that women face when they check the water at night or when they extend temporary connections to provide water to the house and raise the welfare of society and women in particular, as they will get rid of the burdens of transporting water, and the health level will improve due to High level of hygiene and sterilization. Water and sanitation are pressing needs for the community. All of these obligations and responsibilities affect the role of women and the adolescent girl and her social life in Rashaida, especially female students, as they may leave school because of obligations and duties and there are many cases of school dropouts that have been recorded over the last years.As an indication of the effect of the responsibilities, the number of first-grade students is 110, and the number of twelfth-grade students is only 15, both females and males.The following are the primary needs that the project will address: 1- Installation of 4500 meters 3", 2" inches, and meters 1" inch in the Arab Rashaida area.2- Installation of 1450 meters 2" inches, and meters 1" inch in the Za'tara area.There are 50 houses in Za'tara that require the water network pipeline, and primary and secondary schools, while in Arab Rashaida there are 2 schools and a health center.This improvement will reduce water loss, cost, time, effort, and water-related illnesses in humans and animals by ensuring better-quality water and constant supply. It will also raise the level of care and attention to people with special needs and those with chronic disease

2 - Activities

AAA re-conducted multiple meetings with important stakeholders in October 2022, covering the issues in Zatara and Arab Rashaida to gain a better understanding of their requirements and responsibilities. The project will reduce water loss (which is now around 40-50 % in Ar Rashayda) The council will be able to collect the water price, connect the network to residences, and manage network maintenance. and It will also provide the neighborhoods with a water network that is already missing in Za'tara.- A meeting will be held to announce the project and its' goal, activities, locations, and work principles, at the same time announcing the complaints channels through the complaints box and providing contact numbers and email. We will make sure that there are enough representatives of women and special needs. Where all complaints, especially those issued by women, will be dealt with in strict confidence, and women will be visited at home to ensure their satisfaction and to investigate their complaints cases. - A technical committee made up of members of the village council, the women's center, and a special case representative will be established to monitor the project's development. It will be made sure that the project will be implemented safely and won't result in any arrests, sabotage, or harm to the local populace or contractors due to the occupation. To avoid disturbing or obstructing the local community, specific hours will be worked to prevent harm to schoolchildren during their mobility and excavation operations, and barriers and warning signs will be put in place. In order to guarantee the safety of women as they labor around the house and take care of animals, the area and land will be returned to their original state. During and following the implementation phase, persons with special needs will be able to travel freely and safely. Restoring the pipes will increase the availability of water for human consumption and better their way of life. By improving the water level in the communities, the burden will be eased on women who take care of children with special needs, and the amount of water consumed by PWDA will increase by at least 30%. enhancing good networks of water will reduce water loss, costs, time, and effort—especially for women—as well as the risk of water-related illnesses in children and, will be improved, as well as their way of life. This will increase family and agricultural access to water. It will also ensure higher water quality. It enhances the mother's ability to care for her children, allowing her to bathe and wash them as necessary. - A number of meetings with pertinent parties will be held by AAA to hear different perspectives as the project is being implemented.The following are the activities that the project will address: 1- Installation of 4500 meters 3", 2" inches, and 1" inch in the Arab Rashaida area.2- Installation of 1450 meters 2" inches, and 1" inch in the Za'tara area.There are 50 houses, 1 school, and a wedding hall and services for the area in Za'tara that require the water network pipeline, and 30 houses, 2 schools, and a health center in Ar -Rashayda that require it.Output 1: The pipes in the targeted neighborhoods will provide access to clean and inexpensive drinking water for 1250 people in Arab Rashaida, and (2000) people in Za'tara who will benefit from safe and via pipes in the targeted neighborhoods.Output 2: There will be no water losses in the newly constructed pipes and new carrier line, which will reduce water loss in total (which is now around 50% in the hamlet) resulting in high water pressure in the pipes and improved water delivery, particularly to rural locations. This initiative will encourage future expansion to benefit the villages of Walidah, Khalat Hamad, and Al Rawae’n, where they will be direct beneficiaries.Output 3: Individual daily water consumption will grow by 45%, bringing it closer to the WHO's average for the entire world. Output 4: As the Arab Ar-Rashayida council's income rises, there will be less debt and better services for the public. The council will have the ability to collect water fees, link the network to homes, and oversee network maintenance. Output 5: No physical pollution of the water, which results in high water pressure in the pipes and improved water delivery, particularly to rural locations. The Khalat Hamad and Al Rawaen villages will directly benefit from this project's extension in the future.

3 - Indicators

A baseline survey will be done during the early phase of the project and will be updated until the final evaluation. Semi-structured interviews, household visits, and field observations will be used to gather data. On a monthly basis, regular monitoring will allow AAA to document the impact of the actions, identify obstacles and restrictions, and recommend appropriate solutions. Quality implementation will be prioritized, as will close collaboration with PWA, Zatara Municipality, Arab Rashaida Council, community leaders, and local protection committees. Prior to implementation, AAA will consult with the protection cluster and the NRC to assure legal advice, and a second legal opinion will be obtained to confirm that no risks to implementation exist. AAA will guarantee accountability by following up with field teams and recipients on a regular basis. The following are the project's expected KPIs and targets:- Installation of 4500M of water pipeline 3", 2", and 1" network to serve 1250 direct beneficiaries in Arab Rashaida (321 men, 307 women, 292 boys, 278 girls, and 50 elders) will benefit from enhanced access to drinking clear water. - Installation of 2450 M of water pipeline 2", and 1" network in the evangelical area to serve 2000 direct beneficiaries in Za'tara (513 men, 491 women, 467 boys, 445 girls, and 80 elders) will benefit from enhanced access to drinking clear water.- An increase of 20% in the amount of water reaching the surrounding areas due to the high pressure of the pipes.- Expenditure on the drinking water will be decreased by 45%.- In Arab Rashaida Za'tara, women and men required 50% less time and effort. - Cut water loss in the pipeline by half. Objectives: Reduce the water bill deficit in the community municipality by 10%. Gender components of the project: The specified indicators will be disaggregated by gender to guarantee the participation of women and other groups, as well as men, girls, and girls in schools, youth, people with disabilities, and the elderly. Women and girls bear significant responsibility for bringing and using water at home, as well as ensuring proper cleanliness for children. The intervention will guarantee that this role is honored and developed by relieving the physical work of delivering water on a regular basis from women.The implemented activities will take into account the needs of various groups of women and girls, such as the hygienic aspects that become more important with better availability of drinking water that is close to the community, and that bringing water will be much less of a burden on women, who usually bear this responsibility of work. By providing water, the well-being of the elderly will improve and the feeling of satisfaction will be enhanced among the population, especially those with special needs and their families Reports obtained on women's responsibilities will be capitalized and used in AAA women empowerment, as well as to help local AAA activities on policy reform towards neglected communities. M&E Indicators will be divided into two categories: women's health (access and utilization of water, disease and prevention, environmental health, water and fertility, health care management, health expenditures, maternal and infant health, mental health and risk behaviors, nutrition and water). 2- economic contribution (resource equity) related to water availability.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

By ensuring that everyone has access to clean, inexpensive drinking water, this initiative will aid in reducing the impact of human rights breaches. AAA's overall goal is to operate with a high level of humanitarianism while paying close attention to risk reduction, avoiding damage to the greatest extent feasible. Through the committee established to plan the project, which ensured that the proposed pipes will satisfy the urgent requirement to stop the deteriorating water crisis in the region, 7000 meters will in fact be implemented in the densest areas impacted by the water crisis.Furthermore, because 85% of the implementation area will be between communities and relatively far from direct contact with the occupying troops, neither the people nor the project will be at risk during this time. Furthermore, because it would be between communities, 85% of the implementation site will be relatively removed from direct contact with the occupying troops, posing little risk or damage to the population or project during the implementation time. Future development was considered while developing the project's operations and specifications to ensure the continuity of the provision of healthy water in the right amount and quality in the event of extension. AAA will undertake a risk assessment for proposed activity in the chosen site in order to identify potential concerns and provide remedies such as a community legal status: to determine whether there are any military orders or other legal considerations and to ensure that the planned action would not adversely affect the community legal case. AAA feels they have made meaningful measures in considering the Do No Harm policy by including many stakeholders in the community as well as other players from outside the community, including the population and owners. AAA will monitor any new difficulties in the region to avoid any unwanted consequences from project activity. AAA will host a monthly meeting with stakeholders to ensure a guaranteed complaint process in order to raise the voice of the local community's most vulnerable groups, including women, children, and persons with disabilities. Furthermore, a feedback box will be put in the village council to gather any complaints, recommendations, or notes regarding the implementation and beneficiary selection.
