Basic Info

Project Name

Improving Access to water, and enhancing hygiene practices through restoration of water infrastructure, WASH facilities, awareness on health and hygiene to ensure continuity of services for most vulnerable households and school students in targeted Governorate.

Start Date

01/02/2024

End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

A. Proposed Projects Hama Governorate:
1. Water Trucking to fulfill immediate water needs in 10 villages: In Hama Governorate, District Salamiah the 10 villages which are in dire need of water AKAH propose to truck safe drinking water on temporary basis until we restore the water infrastructure in the targeted 10 villages. This temporary arrangement will benefit approximately 10,000 individuals. AKAH will exit by the end of 2024 after rehabilitation of the Eastern Bari Water Network, and Rehabilitation and Solarization of Boreholes 3 additional boreholes in district Samalieh.
2. Rehabilitation, solarization and equipping no of Boreholes in targeted 5 villages In Hama Governorate, District Salamiah communities Jani Elelbawi, Tal Ada, Marj Matter, Dneibeh, Abu Hbeilat total 05 bore holes will be rehabilitated and solarized to get optimum water for drinking, cooking and washing. The rehabilitation and solarization of boreholes will benefit 5000 individuals in 05 villages.
3. Expansion and Rehabilitation of water sources this include (stations, networks and water tanks): In Hama Governorate District As Salamiah propose following rehabilitation work:
I. As-Salamiyeh expansion and rehabilitation of Alqantara pumping water station Phase -2
II. As-Salamiyeh rehabilitation of Alqantara water Tank
III. Eastern Bari Eastern Bari rehabilitation of Eastern Bari water Tank
IV. Tal Abdel Aziz rehabilitation of Tal Abdel Aziz water Tank
V. Saboura rehabilitation of Saboura water Tank
VI. Masyaf rehabilitation of Masyaf water Tank
The above proposed projects will benefit 100,000 individuals in district Salamiah improving their access to clean and safe drinking water.
B. Proposed Projects for Tartous Governorate:
1. Rehabilitation and Solarization of Boreholes: Rehabilitation, and solarizing and equipping 1 borehole in Malluah Tartous Governorate. The project will benefit approximately 1,000 individuals.
C. Proposed Projects for Aleppo Governorate:
2. Rehabilitation of Water Tank: In Aleppo, As-Safira for the village Ein Assan rehabilitation of Ein Assan Tank will benefit approximately 561 individuals improving access to safe drinking water.
D. Proposed Projects for Al-Sweda Governorate:
1. Rehabilitation and Solarization of Water Boreholes: Rehabilitation, and Solarizing and equipping 2 boreholes in Thaala village will benefit approximately 6923 individuals.
2. Rehabilitation of Water Pumping Stations: Rehabilitation PE100 pumping pipe DN110 and relevant infrastructure work. This will benefit approximately 6923 individuals in the Thaala village.
3. Rehabilitation of Schools and WASH Facilities: Will get a list from Ministry of Education to rehabilitate and equip 3-4 schools with missing WASH facilities etc. Also, sessions to promote health and hygiene practices among community in targeted villages above and students.
E. Proposed Projects for Homs Governorates:
1. Rehabilitation and Solarization of Boreholes: In Homs Al Makhrim, will benefit 15000
