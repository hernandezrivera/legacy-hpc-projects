Project Summary

MWL proposed project seeks to provide protective education environment opportunities to vulnerable children (boys and girls)in four sub-districts: (Harim, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin). They selected as the most priority to ensure expanding ongoing education services by availing inclusive protective learning spaces to ensure that no children are absent or drop out of school due to lack of learning spaces, learning and teaching materials, the economic capacity of the Education Directorate and the parents to cover all the schools & Student’s needs.
MWL proposed project aims to contribute to supporting 10000 (B:5000, G: 5000) aged 5-17 years, including 250 CWDs to get access to Education (formal and non-formal) where the number of targeted students in formal education is 5000 (B:2500, G: 2500) and also 5000 students (B:2500, G: 2500) will supporting in nonformal education, and raising the quality of education in line with EiE standards and inclusion Children with Disability through rehabilitation and equip schools, provision transportation and, awareness raising and inclusion activities. This project has no secured funds from any other partners.That will be achieved through;
1)Rehabilitation for 240 classrooms in targeted schools (34 schools) to raise their capacity to absorb more IDP students in Harim, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin sub-districts .
2)Established 8 temporary learning spaces (40 classrooms) set up in the form of tents, pre-fabs, rented rooms or lightly rehabilitated in Harim, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin sub-districts.
3)Providing Schools' furniture / Students' seats to enhance the capacity of schools for 34 schools and 8 learning space.
4)Training 300 targeted teachers and administrative staff in classroom management, and modern teaching methods in the targeted schools.
5)Provision stationery, teaching & learning materials, textbooks, and Student's kit for 1000 (B:5000, G: 5000).
6)Using the interim government curricula in formal education and curriculum B for non-formal education in the two semesters during the school year, and Providing literacy and numeracy skills, remedial & catch-up classes for t non-formal programs.
7)Conducting ASER to assess learning.
8) Establishing 2nd shift to providing formal / non-formal education in the school to raise the capacity to absorb new IDPs Boys and Girls.
9)Providing incentives for 300 teachers and administrative staff in Harim, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin sub-districts .
10) Conduct a winterization campaign by distributing Heating fuel and heaters in the Voucher Scheme to ensure the safety and security of the schools and prevent fraud & corruption in the procurement process for 34 schools and 8 learning space.
11)Establishing Parents teachers association PTAs to follow up with students’ education improvement and issues for 34 schools and 8 learning space.
12)Conduct awareness sessions and preventive measures on Cholera for 10300(5000B, 5000G, 150M, 150W) of the students and education team in the targeted schools and TLS in Harim, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin sub-districts.
13)Conduct PSEA awareness activities for10300(5000B, 5000G, 150M, 150W)students, teachers, and education personnel, also supported the PSEA reporting channel in the targeted 36 schools and 8 TLS schools.
The M&E Coordinator will be responsible for oversight of the complaint mechanism where beneficiaries will be able to provide feedback or complaint to ensure satisfied of beneficiaries Evaluation of the project will be conducted by the M&E staff throughout the project
duration that will start by conducting a “base-line survey”, monitoring and compares the progress development and conducting an “end-line survey” to measure the achievements of intervention outputs. A lesson-learning event will be conducted at the end of the project life cycle to draw some lessons and best practices from the project. The project will include the CVA modality for the heating liters distribution.

3 - Needs

Based on HNO 2023, 15.3 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance. School facilities have also suffered immensely, both as a result of attacks and collateral damage. . The project was built based on the needs of affected persons in NW Syria since MWL has targeted the locations based on the ASO1. MWL team conducted a needs assessment using KII & Filling Questionnaire with Education Directorates, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. In Afrin 3 communities (Trinda, Qarzihel & Afrin city), severity is 4.30 and school-age children in need is 17264 (B:7754,G:9510). In Dana 5 communities (Sarmada, Burdaqly, Hezreh –Hezri, Qah, & Dana), severity is 5 and school-age children in need is 20059(B:9465,G:10594), while teachers in need is 8281 (M:4027, W:4254). In Maaret Tamsrin children in need is 27015 (B:13272,G:13743).According to the information, in targeted sub-district schools absent due to harsh cold weather last year, with inability to provide fuel for heating for schools. 91% of teachers are working as volunteers. 46% of dropped-out children due to the financial conditions, while 42% due to the child labor. 58% of assessed schools are in need of rehabilitation.

4 - Implementation of activities

Based on a broad and deep understanding of needs and barriers to access to quality education for children in the target areas, MWL designed project activities. The project will implement the following activities:- MWL will provide 34 schools and 8 education centers with formal and non-formal education activities (ALP, remedial, and SLP) for 1000 ( 5000 Boys and 5000 girls) students aged 5-17 years including 250 CWDs where the number of targeted students in formal education is 5000 (B:2500, G: 2500) and also 5000 students (B:2500,G: 2500) will target in nonformal education in Harim, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin sub-districts.- 300(150 M,150W) teachers in the target schools will be provided with incentives and will be trained in interactive non-formal education programs.- Established 8 temporary learning spaces.- Rehabilitation of the 34 school’s infrastructure, equip Wash facilities and special needs bathrooms, with a gender-sensitive approach. - Contributing to protecting students and teachers from risks of fire by providing schools with fire extinguishers, in addition to providing schools with first aid kits.- Conduct awareness sessions and preventive measures on Cholera for 10300 (5000B, 5000G,150M,150W) of the students and education team in the targeted schools. - Conduct PSEA awareness activities for 10300(5000B, 5000G, 150M,150W) students, teachers, and education personnel, also supported the PSEA reporting channel in the targeted 36 schools and 8 TLS schools.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk1: Sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), including harassment against students, by educational staffMitigation: Train staff on Do No Harm, protection from SEA (PSEA), and child safeguarding; and sign and adhere to code of conduct that respect and uphold the rights of beneficiaries and PSEA . Risk 2: Contamination from remnants of war beside the educational centers.Mitigation measures: choice of locations done in close consultation and agreement with the community, conduction of safety audits. Risk 3: Difficulty faced by CWDs in accessing the schools/centers.Mitigation: Chose location close to beneficiaries, consult communities on the location, present or transportation support. Risk 4: Female workers including teachers may also face protection risks, including the risk of harassment and abuse, when traveling to/from centers or schoolsMitigation: Set up communication and complaints mechanisms; for ex. feedback desks, face-to-face interviews


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

- Signing PSEA and child safeguarding MWL’s policy by all staff.- getting training on PSEA for all staff.- PSEA awareness through brochures, and videos.- Assigning complaint mechanisms for SEA issues.- policies of Code of conduct, PSEA, and child safeguarding are mandatory for all staff and vendors working with MWL.MWL will adopt a restricted feedback mechanism to ensure a clear AAP. Effective participation by the affected persons will be ensured by getting their feedback via several channels. these channels are accessible for MWBG, PWD. The complaint Box will be fixed inaccessible places in addition to email, what’s app number, Mobile complaint boxes for outreach activities and direct contact with MEAL focal point. For BNFs who do not have access to internet, direct contact with MEAL focal point and FGDs on voluntary basis will be used to get their feedback. for illiterates, what’s app voice massage, FGD and direct contact will be used.To protect children, girls and beneficiaries from any exploitation, discrimination and SEA, posters will be published showing that all services are free of charge.Mechanisms will be well-announced on banners/posters in all targeted location and during distribution and awareness sessions. Complaints will be received by the accountability officer in Turkey. A separate hotline&email WhatsApp&Telegram, will be advertised for sensitive complaints, like, PSEA, and will be received by or referred to the protection coordinator/PSEA
