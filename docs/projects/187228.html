1 - Needs

The proposed response aims to provide cash transfers to support families to purchase food and fuel from the functioning markets. This will not be conditional but “labelled” or in cash parlance, does not come with strict conditionalities that are expensive to monitor or unrealistic to achieve, but rather comes with activities such as nutrition session with lead mothers participating in the cash program on preparing nutritious food available in the local markets.Humanitarian Cash Transfer: The transfer modality is unconditional cash which has proven to be effective and preferred in the target area. The value of the unconditional cash transfer is 3,770 NGN per individual for both food items and cooking fuel: 3,580 NGN is expected to be used for food items and 190 NGN to be used for cooking fuel. Those values are calculated using the food security sector and cash working group harmonized Minimum Expenditure Basket (MEB) valid for Jere LGA. The grant covers 70% of the food component of MEB. It is expected that the remaining 30% of caloric need is either produced in-kind through small-scale gardening or earned through market driven income generating activities by beneficiaries.Development activities: The development activities will be founded on community participatory activities such as transect walks and mapping to identify safe places for food production and eventual expansion to agricultural production as well as businesses which are needed by the market. Besides livelihoods, business skills and conflict mitigation/ peacebuilding training, the start-up kits, seeds and tools will be distributed through participating vendors. ZOA takes advantage of the RedRose platform, a digital system using biometrics, which significantly reduces misappropriation and fraud and improves efficiency of the distribution process. Distributions will be spread over two weeks and at different times of the day to adhere to SEMA (State Emergency Management Agency) regulations on distributions and social distancing.
