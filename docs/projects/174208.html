Project Summary

The Protecting Children Against Negligence and Harmful Practices (PANAH) project in North Waziristan, KPK is designed to provide technical support to community organizations, youth groups, teachers, schools and North Waziristan district/provincial level Government departments of KP province, in order to strengthen their knowledge and awareness of children’s rights, including protection from all forms of violence and exploitation. This will be achieved by addressing gaps in the enabling environment as a priority, including the fundamental absence of a child protection case management and referral mechanism at the North Waziristan district KP provincial level. Additionally, through this project IRP will address gaps primarily related to social norm and quality of service delivery through leave no one behind and do no harm approaches with faith lens. Focus will be placed on the most disadvantaged and excluded children, including girls. Project strategy will follow, Article 7 of Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) and necessary measures will be taken by IRP to ensure the full enjoyment by children with disabilities of all human rights and fundamental freedoms on an equal basis with other children. During the project life cycle it will be ensured that children with disabilities have the right to express their views freely on all matters affecting them, their views being given due weight in accordance with their age and maturity, on an equal basis with other children, and to be provided with disability and age-appropriate assistance to realize that right. IRP will work on developing the capacity of youth groups between the ages of 6 to 20 years and community based organizations (formed in RIHA-III project), with the objective of improving the knowledge and awareness of these groups on issues of child rights, protection, parenting skills and gender based violence. We will also establish youth groups at the village level with representation at the Union Council and District levels.
Moreover, a comprehensive training programme including communities counselling is part of the project design with an aim to spread awareness among stake holders and target groups regarding protection of child rights and to improve their knowledge, skills and abilities for better implementation of the child rights policies and on empowering Civil Society Organizations to effectively promote and protect Child Rights. Youth groups will also be provided consecutive trainings with members of Child Rights movement.
Child protection learning material will be designed and developed for teachers, school children, youth groups and community members. This material will highlights teachers, children and members of youth group’s roles and responsibilities, success indicators, tools and techniques of effective advocacy and networking to address child protection issues and influence the policy makers and government departments to improve the level of implementation with regards to child protection. In this training programme IRP will provides the participants with an understanding on child rights and sensitized the participants regarding child sexual abuse.
Furthermore, an advocacy and awareness campaigns will be implemented though different mediums, utilising various materials and methods including posters, radio messages, social media, community level street shows, for replication with short messages on child rights, child abuse, child protection, child safeguarding and gender based violence, parenting and adolescent risky behaviour. We will also conduct multi-stakeholder seminars at the provincial levels on child protection related issues and electoral reform. Added to this, a comprehensive research piece on child protection gaps at policy and implementation level will be conducted and policy brief will be launched and disseminated to policy makers including media, protection sector working groups and child rights movement etc.
