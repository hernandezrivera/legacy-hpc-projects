Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to promote a sustainable empowerment of vulnerable persons and households affected by the ongoing Anglophone crisis in Cameroon, through multisectoral livelihood activities. The project will strengthen the economic capacity of vulnerable persons and households through Organization of training sessions on vocational skills, capacity building on business and Disbursement of cash for business startup. The project will also include provision of psychological support to IDPs and returnees in order to limit their psychological distress and improve their coping mechanisms; and organize intercommunity dialogue to strengthen social cohesion. The project will also involve construction and rehabilitation of 4 water points and sensitization on COVID-19. The project geographical location is Mezam and Donga Mantung division in the NW and Bamboutos and Mifi division in the West region. The project is going to target community and youth leaders, 200 women and 200 youths. Before the end of 2022, the project is expected to improved sustainable empowerment of affected people; improve resilience and self-reliance among targeted beneficiaries. The project is also expected to strengthened social cohesion between communities and affected population, and IDPs and host communities

1 - Besoins / Needs

The Anglophone crisis that started since 2016 in the two English speaking regions of Cameroon escalated into an armed conflict and insecurity in 2017 and has rendered many people displaced from their localities. The increasing tension and hostilities has promoted insecurity and significant internal displacements. While humanitarian needs, joblessness, widowhood, crime and scarcity of food are on the increase, most farmers have fled from the villages into bushes, forest and a large number made it to nearby cities especially Bamenda in Mezam NW, and Bamboutos and Mifi dvision in the West. Consequently many have become vulnerable and in need of humanitarian assistance. Many IDP households and families in Bamenda are increasing being led by women as most men have been killed and many others recruited into the Non State Arm groups. This has made the situation more difficult for the women to provide basic need of food and shelter to their families. As earlier mentioned, subsistent farming which has been the means of survival in the past has been greatly affected as the displacement and violence increases hindering farming and even food harvesting. Despite the acknowledged importance of addressing these needs, little is presently being done.According to IOM, about 3000 children are suffering from severe acute malnutrition. Malnutrition is a critical risk in the NW region and good nutrition and access to adequate food remains a challenge to child survival especially in the context of the current sociopolitical crises. Reports of assessment by World Food Program (WFP) indicate that 900,000 people in the Northwest region are food insecure. In this population 13% of IDPs and 10 % of non IDPs are severely food insecure. 36.6% of IDPs and 29.9% of NIDPs are moderately food insecure while 38.2% of IDPs and 29.5% NIDPs are marginally food insecure. The continuous aggravating situation and sustained prevalence of critical Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) levels (2.8%) in the Northwest region suggests that general food assistance is needed to treat children with moderate acute malnutrition and prevent malnutrition. This could be as a result of the fact that subsistence farming which was basically the means of survival has been greatly affected due to farmers not having access to farms, unable to harvest their food and even continue farming or transport food to market. There is therefore a need for humanitarian assistance especially general livelihood assistance in the Northwest and west region.
