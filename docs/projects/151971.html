Project Summary

Peace building and reintegration training in some of the affected communities in Adamawa, Borno and Yobe. This training will target the returnees, IDPs in Host communities and the Host communities. This attempts to strengthen peaceful co-existence among community members, and provide the necessary trauma healing especially for those who were directly or indirectly affected in the insurgency. Other supporting training on counter violence extremism will be done to help the community members and the youth to stand for peace and promote it in their locality. Promoting the return and reintegration or local integration of IDPs and returning populations within local communities and ensuring their integration into local/area recovery and development plans and interventions.
Also, Promoting micro and small enterprise recovery through short-cycle business-management training, and the introduction of a new innovation called Micro Enterprise Fundamentals (MEF) which aims at training the beneficiaries the marketing strategies and ways in which a product can be sold and have much patronage within the community and its environ; not only that, cash grants, access to micro-finance schemes and coaching; and other income-generating activities targeting particularly women and youths. This will go a long way especially in Madagali where we have high rate of the returnees are female headed households who are susceptible to abuses and most of them go out of their way to seek succor and survivor sex. The project seeks to carry out a quick assessment on what the community will want as alternative source of livelihood seeing the communities are plagued with limited space of farming which is their initial source of livelihood and sustainability. This will also curb the prevailing drug abuse challenges commonly found among the youth which is traceable to idleness.
Following the sector needs assessments, it was gathered that some of the communities in Madagali LGA of Adamawa have no access to health care facilities due to the vandalization of hospitals and public structures during the insurgency hence limiting access to basic health care services. People have to trek miles to get only but limited services and in the event of emergency the person stands the risk of survival. This projects seeks to rehabilitate and reconstruct three damaged healthcare facilities in Madagali thereby making it a lot easier for the returnees and host communities gain access to basic health care services at a given appropriate time and affordable rate. These will alleviate suffering and drastically reduce the rate of casualties gotten from lack of proper and timely medical attention. With the high rate of teenage pregnancy, a well equipped heath care centre will assist in providing anti natal service, maternity, post natal care and malaria treatment especially in those locations were there no proper water channels, drainage systems which constitute stagnant water thereby making the community susceptible to Malaria

Needs

Based on the Early recovery humanitarian response plan as released, Data from recent multi-sector needs assessments and Livelihoods and Economic Recovery Assessments report on inadequate levels of access to basic services and public infrastructure relative to the size of the population, limited income and livelihood opportunities, and increased resource demands, leading to societal tensions between IDPs, returnees and host communities Some of the needs identified for which this project attempts to address are;* Conducting basic rehabilitation of primary social services, such as health care facilities considering possible ways to promote the sustainability of the services.* Promoting micro and small enterprise recovery through short-cycle business-management training, cash grants, access to micro-finance schemes and coaching; and other income-generating activities targeting particularly women and youths.* Promote inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDPs in Host Communities, Returnees, and Host communities.

Activities

*Conducting basic rehabilitation of primary social services in identified communities such as health care facilities and ensuring sustainability of the services.* Training on micro and small enterprise, through short-cycle business-management training, cash grants, Micro Enterprise Fundamentals (MEF), access to micro-finance schemes and coaching; and other income-generating activities targeting particularly women and youths.* Trainings/ workshop on inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDPs in Host Communities, Returnees, and Host communities.
