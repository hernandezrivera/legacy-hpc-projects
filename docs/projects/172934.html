Project Summary

The objectives of this project to improve the lifesaving WASH assistance and prevention spread of covid-19 to roughly 8,000 returnees, schools, detention centers and idp camps including other locations wherever humanitarian WASH assistance needed, whether east, west and south of Libya. Furthermore, due to the recent war on Tripoli last view months, were many people affected by humanitarian crisis, Covid-19 as well. The activities including provide of safe drinking water by water trucks supplies, lite rehabilitation of water networks, installation of water tanks, provide of safe sanitation facilities, distribution of hygiene kits, disinfection materials related to Covid-19, rehabilitation of toilets by install emergency latrines and hygiene awareness.
Also, our WASH program is established to help people in emergency cases, especially for women and children by deliver drinking water, hygiene kits and health awareness . Therefore, the main aim of rapid response mechanism in Libya to reach affected vulnerable groups and improve their lives.
