Project Summary

The Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC) and
Plan International Cameroon in collaboration with Norwegian Refugee Council will conduct an Education project that will run in parallel with the Accelerated Education Programme (AEP) launched in May 2020 with funding from ECHO and designed to cover a full primary school cycle of six years in three years in Kolofata and Logone Birni. This AEP will provide quality education and protection to children and adolescents in communities that are severely affected by displacement and conflict in Cameroon's Far North Region. Due to high insecurity, social and cultural norms that sustain harmful behaviors and Floods, children are unable to attend school and can face greater protection risks such as violence, early marriage, early pregnancy and exploitation. The proposed action supports vulnerable out-of-school children to return to education in a protective learning environment and catch-up classes and remedial classes for in-school children.
NRC and Plan will coordinate with child protection actors to support families and children to obtain birth certificates and identity documents, allowing children enrolled to take part in the national exam (CEP) and progress to secondary education systems. The action will draw on the lessons learned from the
previous and on-going ECHO EiE actions implemented in the Far North.
opportunities that include alternative learning platforms as part of the innovative approaches that they will adopt during the action. In addition, the proposed action will include an exit strategy within Kolofata and Logone Birni. Education impacts and benefits for out of school children are sustained in the longer term. NRC and Plan will work with key education community stakeholders to identify resources that could support AEP centers to continue functioning once the project ends.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Provision of comprehensive community sensitization on the benefits of AEPs and child protection prevention action: For AEP success and sustainability, community engagement is critical from the start. For this purpose, the engagement will come from the two Parent-Teacher Associations (PTAs) and Mother Association (MA) of AEP centres. The PTAs/MAwill provide essential links between the AEP centres and communities, ensuring that the programme is appropriately managed. Rehabilitation of Temporary Learning Space (TLS) for AEP centers including WASH facilities: To promote child friendly schools, NRC and Plan will rehabilitate 07 blocks of gender-sensitive latrines (with 02 cabins each) in AEP Centres. Separate latrines shall be rehabilitating for girls/female teachers and for boys/male teachers for AEP centres of Kolofata and Logone Birni.Provision of teacher professional development courses on subject knowledge and Accelerated Learning pedagogy: A total of 100 Teachers and MINEDUB staff will be trained on child safeguarding, gender in education, safe school declaration, Protection Against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and Psychosocial Support (PSS) through NRC Better learning program approach, in order to support and develop the emotional and cognitive wellbeing of childrenIdentification and enrolment of target students for either AEP/CARED:NRC and Plan will recruit 08 community mobilisers who will conduct sensitization and awareness campaigns in the targeted subdivisions (Kolofata, and Logone Birni) about AEP, catch up and remedial classes. They will help identify learners and facilitate their enrolment (especially the most vulnerable) and foster good relationships between PTAs/MAs, project staff and teachers, registered students for either AEP or CARED.Provision and monitoring of AEP classes to 2500 selected learners: Targeting out-of-school children AEP will focus on children who never attended formal school and those who have been out of school for more than one year, condensing the primary education cycle into three levels, with the option of taking the primary leaving certificate on completion of Level three.Strengthening of school governance and community structures (PTA) and recreational after-school clubs: The associations put in place (MA, PTA) will be mentored, trained and equipped to assist SMCs in supporting girls' education and keeping them in school. MAs' roles will include: ensuring safety of girls on the way to and on school grounds; promoting girls' enrolment in education; encouraging girls' regular school attendance and success through daily monitoring of girls' attendance; and monthly school performances; ensuring girls' and boys' equity; obtaining birth certificates for children, especially girlsDisseminate Schools safety protocols: NRC and Plan aims to support the setup of school safety protocols including measures to protect learners and teachers from attacks. Efforts will be made to ensure that all the channels are used to disseminate information on the school safety protocols to children. parents and communities while considering the needs of specific groups who might experience barriers to accessing information, care and support or be at higher risk of exposure and secondary impacts, such as children, especially girls and those with disabilities, who are often invisible and excluded.Market study and Risk assessment for the implementation of IGAs: Plan and NRC will conduct a feasibility study of Income Generating Activities and elaborate a business plan in order to determine thepossibility of carrying out an activity, to see the yield while considering the environment and the beneficiaries of the activity.Introducing PTA/MAs to Village Savings and Loans (VSL) methodology allowing to support AEP Centers needs: Village Savings and Loans is a holistic programming methodology/approach that offers households a strategy to protect assets, smooth cash flow, and increase income.
