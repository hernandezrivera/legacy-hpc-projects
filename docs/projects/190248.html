Project Summary

IRC assessment identified a significant number of livestock deaths, highly weakening livestock, and mass migration in search of water and fodder. As of January 2022, the number of reported livestock death was: 37,722 in Dawe Sarar, 368 in Lagahidha, and 847 in Dawe Kachen,. According to the July 2021 hot-spot classification done by the National Disaster Risk Management Commission, a total of 126 woredas in Oromia including targeted woredas, and 71% of the districts in the East Bale Zone are classified as hotspot priority 1and in need of urgent response.
The IRC proposed intervention will be implemented to support drought affected communities in three woredas (districts) namely Dawe Serer, Legehidha and Sewena woredas of East Bale Zone, oromoiya region, Ethiopia. The IRC has prioritized and select these woredas for project intervention after analysis the avialabe data and in consultation with the main partners - regional government bureaus. Some of the main rationales considered for geographical targeting includes complement and synergy with ongoing IRC projects for better impact, scale and magnitude of the drought and its impact, multifaceted crisis (drought, conflict induced IDPs and returnees), limited number of active partners and responses, etc.
The project aims People affected by drought have increased access to safe drinking water and improved their awareness on sanitation and hygiene and have improved access to food and meet other basic needs and minimize negative coping strategies through provision of unconditional multipurpose cash response in Dawe Serer, Legehidha and Sewena woredas of East Bale Zone. the project will be implemented in the coming Six months and 20901 individuals will be benefited.

1. - Projects activities

Rehabilitate and expansion of Three water schemes to increase access to safe water for Drought affected community, Conduct sanitation and hygiene promotion campaign including COVID-19 prevention & mitigations, Establish/ strengthen and tarin Water Management Committees.(3 WASHCO Committee) All the rehabilitated scheme will have a water committee and will be provided a water Management committee training, Provision of Multi-purpose Cash (MPC) to support the affected community meets their basic needs,targeting and verification, Provision of unconditional Multi-purpose Cash (MPC) to 900 (4500 individuals)drought affected HHs to meets their basic needs. The IRC will provide unconditional Multi-purpose Cash (MPC) through a selected Financial Service provider (Bank) for the most affected clients, in all the three targeted woredas and Conduct Post Distribution Monitoring.
