Project Summary

This CSP aims to contribute all year-round to: Crisis-affected populations to meet their basic food and nutrition needs during and after the crises; Food insecure school-age children, to access adequate and nutritious food; Vulnerable groups (children, pregnant women and adolescent girls) to have enhanced nutritional status; Food insecure populations and communities to have more resilient livelihoods and sustainable food systems; Capacity strengthening of National institutions; Humanitarian and development partners to access common services unti appropriate an sustainable alternative are available.

Besoins

"The 2019 national nutrition survey based on SMART methodology indicates that the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in Niger was 10.7%, which is slightly above the 10% serious threshold. GAM rates among children aged 6 to 23 months stood at 15.8%, above the emergency threshold (15%) compared to children aged 24-59 months (8.4%). Analysis show disparities between regions with four regions Tahoua (13.7%), Maradi (11.4%), Diffa (10.9%) and Zinder (10.9%) having rates above the national rate (10.7%). In Zinder region, six of the ten administrative divisions have GAM rates greater than the regional rate: Belbedji (13.8%), Tesker (13.3%), Kantché (12.0%), Tanout (11.5%), Dungass (11.4%) and Mirriah (11.0%). The rate of severe acute malnutrition (SAM), was estimated at 2.7% above the WHO critical threshold of 2% with four regions (Tahoua: 3.5%, Maradi: 3.4%, Zinder: 2.8% and Tillabery: 2.1%) having rates above this threshold. Chronic malnutrition remains above the 30 percent ""critical"" threshold with 45.7% at national level with all the regions except Niamey (24.3%) and Agadez (29.4%) having rates fall above the critical threshold. With the deteriorating securtiy situation in Niger's neighbouring countryies (Niger, Burkina Faso and Mali), there is a great possibility of split over of these crises in to Niger thus affecting and worsen the actual poor food security and nutrition situations in Diffa, Maradi, Tahoua and Tillabéry regions due to poor access to these areas. The Nutrition Cluster estimated that some 1.5 million people require nutritional assistance, including 396,539 children suffering from severe acute malnutrition (SAM), with 89,788 cases with medical complications. Some 828,994 children aged 6 to 59 months (boys and girls) suffering from moderate acute malnutrition, 627,485 pregnant and lactating women with an upper arm circumference of less than 210 mm, and 89,788 accompanying persons in the CRENIs and will need appropriate nutritional supplementation. Some 78,235 children 6 to 23 months (boys and girls) will need blanket supplementation to stabilise their nutrition status."
