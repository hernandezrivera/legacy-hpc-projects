Project Summary

Due to multi-hazard-drought, worsening economic situation, threats of civil unrest by citizens and possibilities of floods and disease outbreaks children will be displaced and become more vulnerable to abuses and exploitation. They will therefore be in need of legal services in cases of violation of their rights and when they may have been forced to commit offenses due to vulnerability or as a copying mechanism. Communities will need to be sensitised before, during and after such situation on available legal services in the event of violation of child rights in humanitarian situations.
