Project Summary

Water and sanitation challenges continue to pose serious public health issues in Zimbabwe with Mashonaland Central province being one of the most affected areas. According to the ZIMVAC report of 2018 and Service Level Benchmark 2019, Chitungwiza, Epworth, Bindura urban, Centenary and Muzarabani are in severity level 3 for access to WASH services. WHH has been operating in the Mashonaland Central for the past 2 years and has managed through DFID-UNICEF and Charity: Water funded projects to rehabilitate over 1000 Water points, 7 Piped Water Systems and 45 New water points. In the suburbs of Chitungwiza and Epworth in Harare, the situation is equally worrying, and many residents do not have access to safe drinking water. This constitutes a major threat in the case of another Cholera epidemic. In Harare, WHH is already implementing a project funded by GIZ, which mainly aims at stabilizing the water supply in Cholera-prone areas of Harare.
In response to the multi-hazard crisis currently facing Zimbabwe, WHH proposes to provide access to water through water points repair and solarizing high-yielding boreholes. Moreover, hygiene kits will be delivered to especially vulnerable households and waterpoint committees will be established and trained. Hygiene and nutrition education to at risk-communities and in schools will promote positive behavior change in order to battle open defecation and poor hand-washing practices.
