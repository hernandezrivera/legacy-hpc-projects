Mainly, shelter type grade 2 as Residential housing (single or multiple-story buildings), partially damaged or unfinished. 3 - Needs

According to HNO 2023, "The estimated number of people requiring NFI support has increased by 5% to 5.7 million. With funding shortages currently at a historic low, the NFI sectors’ limited response in 2022 is further expected to exacerbate needs as 2023 unfolds. Primarily in northwest and northeast Syria, more than 2.05 million IDPs reside in last resort sites, a marginal increase from 2.02 million in the previous year. Such locations lack crucial infrastructure and rely on emergency shelter solutions and continued humanitarian support. The majority of displacement is currently in excess of 3 years, and 37 percent of households have reportedly been displaced more than three times since 2011".The systematic targeting of social infrastructure including civilian homes has been a major driving force behind mass internal displacement. Families deprived of adequate shelter provision suffer from a wide spectrum of fundamental issues that erode their quality of life, ongoing economic limitations has negatively impacted household resilience across all aspects of basic living standards, further reducing families’ ability to pay rent, address shelter issues, and meet their basic needs, In addition to lack of safety, families endure psychological instability that arises from lack of privacy within degraded homes, HF had conducted a needs assessment for most of the sub-districts in targeted locations: It showed urgent need for rehabilitation for houses for the IDPs and host community.
