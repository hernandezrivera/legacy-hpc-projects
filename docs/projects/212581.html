Project Summary

The project aims to target: Total beneficiaries: 58,000 (5000W,2000M,21000G,20000B) with 10% PwDs. Azaz district, Al-Bab, Afrin, Idlib city, Harem, Al-Dana, Maarat Misrin. This will be through the following interventions:
1-CP: (5,000) G&B and caregivers who benefit from MHPSS services, and (17,000) unstructured PSS. (5000) Women and men will be reached through parenting programs.(20,000) individuals benefit from awareness-raising such as information and structured sessions for response to CP issues to enable them to reduce protection risks in their communities.
- (2600) G&B who receive specialized child protection services through CM. We will provide children in schools and camps to identify cases of children at risk and CwDs.
TAS will provide capacity-building training in CP for (670) to child protection staff.
Developing coordination with child protection committees
Coordination will be made with community committees, holding meetings with each committee, and discussing the child risk protectors by giving them training, and then determining priorities and implementing community initiatives by them. Community members will be invited to events and awareness campaigns, and they will also have role in identifying and referring.
2.-MA:Mine Action:people who will receive risk education from public service providers. humanitarian workers, civil servants, and teachers personnel trained to conduct explosive ordnance risk education.
The MA coordinator will be contacted to achieve a better response by outreach teams.
(25,000) people will receive awareness of EORE. (300) humanitarian workers, government employees, and teachers trained to conduct awareness of the EORE.
3.- Gender-based violence: Women and girls continue to face sexual harassment and assault from men on public roads, public parks, and streets, and when moving around their neighborhoods to get to school or the market [Voices from Syria 2023] We will provide gender-based violence response services , referral, psychosocial support, enhancing access of vulnerable groups to services and strengthening referral pathways
(500) cases will receive specialized services in case management and referral, with a focus on W&G with disabilities.
- (1000) women were reached through IGA activities.
- (25) humanitarian workers concerned with gender-based violence were trained.
(2,500) girls and women received awareness about the Tool Kit curriculum.
(12) women's initiatives and (25) safety audits will be carried out during 2024
(500) Dignity packages will be distributed
TAS is seeking to employ more females to handle any disclosure to make referrals. We also implement focus groups to identify the most vulnerable cases in the community.
- At the beginning of each project, TAS holds a meeting with the community and local actors, to ensure their participation in the project, explain the project objectives, and take their suggestions and feedback to develop the project activities and implementation. TAS will coordinate with the Protection Cluster and with other organizations in the same sector to reach the appropriate beneficiaries and avoid any duplication.
TAS focuses on ensuring the participation of local communities in project design and paying attention to complaints and feedback mechanisms. We give top priority to PwDs and enable them to integrate into our projects. Training and awareness are conducted on gender and political issues in each project.MEAL team monitors the mitigation of PSEA through Check List.theMEAL mechanisms:Information sharing with the community,Pre/post,hotline.Foraccountability,benefi ciaries consultations conducting to choose complaints mechanisms, and the M/E tool to monitor GBV activities.
and for CP MEAL plan we use 4Ws,CM reporting tool. - TAS has confidential and secure reporting mechanisms - beneficiaries can see AR materials on PSEA (posters and flyers)
and a hotline to report any protection activity (e.g. awareness sessions, community consultations.
