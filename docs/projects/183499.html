Project Summary

The HNO for 2022 indicated that there is a gap represented in the decline of Sexual & Reproductive Health Services (SRHS) in the Gaza Strip, which means that the lives of women & fetuses are at risk, especially women who are suffering from risk pregnancy, anemia & unmet FP needs.
Moreover, the results of the meetings with the local committees & different groups of the community, confirmed that women have difficulty access to SRH services.
Accordingly, the idea of the project responds to these needs as it will contribute to covering part of those gaps by providing SRHS to 15,100 women of reproductive age (including PWD) through 6 PHCCs located in all governorates of the Gaza Strip, in addition to Al-Awda Hospital in the northern Gaza Strip & Al-Awda maternity center, which will be operated in the early of 2022 in middle area of Gaza Strip.
For the successful implementation of the project, the local community will be communicated with information about the project’s activities & locations through: (i) Radio spot (ii) Facebook ads (iii) Meetings with the local committees.
Through the project the following SRHS will be provided for women in reproductive age including PWD inside the Al Awda’s health facilities: (i) antenatal care for 3500 women (ii) early detection, treatment & follow up for 1000 risk pregnant (iii) detection, treatment & follow up for 5000 pregnant or lactating women who suffer from anemia & malnutrition (iv) normal delivery & caesarean sections for 1500 women through internal referral to the Al Awda secondary health facilities or external referral to MoH hospitals (v) FP services for 500 women.
Knowing that all the project services will be provided free of charge to be affordable to the beneficiaries who are from the most vulnerable groups.
The Al Awda’s health facilities enjoy easy physical access, but some women may not be able to reach the health facilities due to their presence in remote areas, the lack of money to pay the transportation fees, or due to the restrictions imposed by the MoI on the movement of the population in link with COVID19 pandemic. So, the project staff will conduct home visits to follow up 800 pregnant women in their homes, including those with disabilities.
To ensure the provision of services of the required quality, the capacity of the project staff will be built by involving them in 2 training courses in PSEA, the mechanisms for providing SRHS in light of the COVID19 pandemic and providing health services for women with disabilities while ensuring their dignity during receiving services adding to referring mechanism of GBV cases.
As for community-based activities, the sensitivity of the community to SRH issues, including risk pregnancy, FP, anemia, malnutrition, the right to health and PSEA, will be raised by organizing awareness-raising sessions targeting 2800 persons (women & their husbands). Moreover, radio spots & Facebook ads that carry awareness messages on the aforementioned topics will also be published & reached 100,000 persons from the community. Adding to producing & disseminating 2000 leaflets about PSEA including information on rights & safe complaint channels available to beneficiaries.
In order to achieve the principle of AAP, the beneficiaries will participate in all the project cycle phases & will be informed about Al Awda Feedback, Complain & Response Mechanisms (FCRM) (which they have been consulted about) through publishing motion graphic video that will be interpreted to sign language.
It is worth noting that during the design of the project, coordination was made with the HC coordinator as well as with the Al Awda SRH Officer in order to enhance complementarity & avoid duplication of services provided. Noting that this project is also considered an integrative project for another intervention related to postnatal services that is implemented in partnership with UNICEF & targets women in all governorates of the Gaza Strip in (2021-2022).

1 - Needs

The humanitarian crises in the Gaza Strip affected all aspects of life in general & the Health sector including Sexual & Reproductive Health Services (SRHS) in particular, as these crises led to a sharp decline in services provided in the MoH’s facilities leading to a severe shortage of medication & medical disposable, which reached 41% & 23% respectively, while the percentage of shortages in MCH medication reached 61% (MoH July 2021). The humanitarian consequences related to the access to essential services increased risk for maternal mortality, morbidity & maternal complications among women. The MoH annual report 2020 indicated that the No. of women of childbearing age in the Gaza Strip from 15-49 years reached 504,021 women, or 24.3% of the total population of 2,077,357 people, where females represent 49.2% of the total population, the same report showed that the No. of new pregnant women who attend PHCCs reached 48,029 in the Gaza Strip. The report also indicated that risk pregnancies constitute 32% of the total No. of new pregnant women at the MoH, while at UNRWA, the percentage reached 26% of the total number of new pregnant women. The percentage of anemia among pregnant women in the Gaza Strip reached 40%, while malnourished pregnant women 18% & malnourished lactating women 14%, which making them more prone to maternal & neonatal mortality & morbidities. According to HNO 2022, 18 maternal deaths were recorded in the Gaza Strip during the year 2021, at a rate of 24.3/100,000 live births, which constituted an increase of 38.4% over the year 2020. While FP needs increased from 10.9% to 12.9%, knowing that stock is out of two items “Micrulot & male condoms”, as of May 2021 in Gaza which leads to an increase in the risk for unplanned and unwanted pregnancies, including risk for maternal & infant mortality, morbidity & unsafe abortion & lack of post abortion care. The result of needs assessment conducted by AL AWDA in Oct. 2021 to identify the most needs & priorities of women in reproductive age, which included 5 FGM, one group in each governorate, each comprising 10 women, including women with disabilities; showed that there are major problems facing women to reach SRHS, either due to the stopping of services from time to time as a result of the Israeli attacks, or due to prevention measures related to COVID19 pandemic, or fear of movement in light of the increasing severity of the pandemic, adding to their presence in marginalized areas, this also affected the inability of women to receive FP services due to the lack of some FP tools, & a large percentage of them reported that there is lack of nutritional supplements for anemia, & the participating women emphasized the importance of providing normal and SC delivery services for free or at almost with charitable prices because of the declining economic situation in the Gaza Strip & the increase in the poverty rate, while a group of women stressed the importance of providing RHS for women who suffer from risk pregnancy problems. Here, it is worth noting that the Al AWDA has implemented a project to provide SRHS in 2019-2020, funded by the OPT-HF, and during the participation of the women in the evaluation of the project, they expressed their satisfaction with the services provided & their desire to continue providing those services. Here lies the importance of continuing to provide SRHS, in light of the presence of large No. of pregnant women in the Gaza Strip, which coincides with the intensification of the difficult humanitarian conditions, including the crisis of the COVID19, which limits freedom of movement in addition to the lack of health resources or the discontinuation of SRHS from time to time, as women's access to SRHS has become very limited, which puts the life of the mothers and fetuses at risk & increases the possibility of complications that may lead to an increase in the No. of morbidity & mortality in pregnant women.

2 - Activities

The project will target the most vulnerable & marginalized groups of women who suffer from anemia during pregnancy or lactation, risk pregnancy & women with unmet need for FP, and women in need for antenatal services but couldn't access the service due to their hardship economic situation (including PWD). The project will contribute to bridging the gaps in the SRHS that are mentioned in HNO 2022, as the project services will be provided to women in reproductive age (15-49) (including PWD) free of charge through the 7 Al Awda’s health facilities deployed in all Gaza Strip governorates through the following activities: 1. Detection, treatment and follow-up of 1000 risk pregnant women (including PWD), the services will include clinical examination, ultrasound, lab. tests & providing them with nutritional supplements & necessary medications. 2. Detection, treatment and follow-up 5000 pregnant or lactating women suffering from Anemia (including PWD), the services will include all services that are mentioned in the 1st item with focus on providing them with nutritional supplements specifically folic acid 3. Providing 500 women (including PWD) with family planning services including clinical examination, ultrasound, lab. tests & contraceptive methods. 4. Providing routine prenatal services to 3500 vulnerable women (including PWD) who need this service but cannot afford the costs of follow-up & treatment. 5. 1680 Home visits (20 visits *12 months * 7 health facilities = 1680 visits) will be conducted to 800 women in reproductive age (including PWD) from the project beneficiaries who couldn't access the service due to the restriction on movement in link with the spread of COVID19 in the Gaza Strip or who couldn’t cover transportation fees to reach the health facilities. 6. Providing delivery services to 1500 women (including PWD) (1000 normal, 500 caesarian section) through referring the benefited cases (women with risk pregnancy or Animia) to Al-Awda hospital particularly those living in the northern area, while, others from middle area of GS will be referred to the maternity center which affiliated to Al Awda that will be functioned at the beginning of 2022, while beneficiaries from the southern areas of GS, will be referred to MoH hospitals. 7. Conducting 280 awareness sessions (10 participants/session) targeting women and their husbands (2100 women - 700 men) (including PWD), where the participants will be sensitized on the following topics: PSEA – signs of risk pregnancy - Prevention of infection with Covid 19 - Family planning - Anemia & malnutrition – right to health - Al Awda Feedback, Complaint & Response Mechanism (FCRM).8. Women and girls from the community (80000 women and 20000 girls) will receive awareness messages through 3 radio spots & 7 Facebook ads in the following topics: - Announcement the project activities (access to information). - Prevention measures of Covid-19 infection. - Risk pregnancy, Anemia, malnutrition & FP. - PSEA. – Al Awda’s FCRM.9. In order to involve different groups of society, including PWD, in information about Al Awda FCRM by beneficiaries, a motion graphic video with interpretation to sign language will be prepared and published on Facebook with a sponsored advertisement & will be displayed on screens in the waiting areas for beneficiaries in all Al Awda facilities.10. 2000 copies of the leaflets about PSEA including information on rights and safe complaint channels available to beneficiaries will be produced and distributed to the targeted group. 11. In order to achieve the optimum results, maintaining the dignity of the beneficiaries & preventing them from SEA & Covid-19 infection, 21 workers all of them are female in the Al Awda's reproductive health units will receive two training courses. The 1st course (12 hours, 3hours*4days) will be related to prevention measures of Covid-19 infection during providing services, and the second one (12 hours, 3hours*4days) will be on PSEA, the referring mechanism of GBV cases, dealing with PWD while preserving their dignity & protecting them from any exploitation. 12. PPE will be distributed to both the project staff & beneficiaries to prevent them from Covid-19 infection & to contribute in limiting the spread of the pandemic.

5.2 - Please briefly explain why the specific modality/ies for this response were chosen.

The results of HNO 2022 intersected with the needs assessment conducted by the Al Awda (oct. 2021) in determining the needs of women in the Gaza Strip, which confirmed the decline of reproductive and sexual health services and the difficulty of accessing them by women due to the frequent Israeli attacks and the increase in the severity of the COVID19 pandemic. Therefore, the project responds to these needs by providing sexual and reproductive health services in all governorates of the Gaza Strip through Al Awda health facilities with the aim of improving the health status of women and contributing to reducing morbidity and mortality rates, adding to enabling women to access high quality health services. Hence, the project is based on the service delivery modality. And it is worth to mention that all the services will be provided free of charge.

9.1d - Explain how you have ensured that mechanism are accessible to all population groups?

The Al Awda gives great importance to obtaining feedback & complaints from the beneficiaries, as they are considered a key partner, where they are involved in the planning, implementing & evaluating Al Awda activities. Several mechanisms have been allocated to receive feedback & complaints while ensuring that they can be submitted by all age & gender groups, including PWD (whether hearing, visual or physical disabilities) as the means of submitting the complaint varied in terms of written complaints directly to the complaint box (note that there is a special protocol for opening the box) or voice complaints through the use of the Toll-free No. which is a free No. specified for complaints, this in addition to electronic complaints via a generic e-mail, besides receiving complaints face to face or by filling out questionnaires. The Al Awda is currently developing a Feedback, Complain and Response Mechanism (FCRM) through the development of an electronic model that includes the following classifications:• Sensitive complaint.• Complaint & dissatisfaction.• Inquire about existing services.• Request for individual support & assistance.• Thanks & appreciation.Where the call center employee, through the electronic form, fills in all the details of the complaint and forwarded it to the MEAL manager, except for sensitive complaints that are forwarded to the Executive Director directly. As for the complaints on the Executive Director himself, they are forwarded to the Chairman. In order to response to gender needs, there will be call center workers of both sexes. It is worth to mention that, the beneficiaries of the Al Awda have been consulted about the FCRM and will be informed about it through publishing motion graphic video interpreted to sign language to reach to PWD.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

The idea of the project, its services and implementation mechanisms stem from respecting the protection principles stipulated in the Humanitarian Charter, including the right to live in dignity, the right to assistance and the right to protection. Moreover, these mechanisms are part of the values of the Al Awda within its strategic plan 2020-2024, where the Al Awda deals with its beneficiaries according to its slogan (the health service is a right for whoever needs it), in addition to avoiding exposing the beneficiaries to harm through the following pillars: 1- Safety: - The Al Awda will provide the project services through its 7 health facilities that are spread throughout the Gaza Strip. The geographical locations of these facilities are distinguished by their presence in the center of the governorates in populated places, and their streets are paved and lit at night, ensuring safety for the beneficiaries, making it easy to reach and are well known to the public since their establishment.- In order to prevent SEA while providing project services, project staff will attend PSEA training. In addition, their contracts will include a special clause on PSEA in accordance with the IASC Principles and Guidelines.- In order not to expose the beneficiaries to further harm, they will be educated about PSEA and the Al Awda’s FCRM.- Ensure that beneficiaries are not harmed while receiving services on the basis of dignity and protection. Here it is worth noting that 12 medical staff at Al-Awda Hospital of both sexes were trained on the Sphere and Do no Harm standards to create a positive deviation in the behavior of workers while providing health services to beneficiaries to avoid exposing them to harm.- Providing security and protection through security staff in the Al Awda Hospital.- Availability of an intensive care unit ambulance to refer emergency cases to MoH hospitals.- Providing insurance of work accidents for the project staff. - Providing health insurance for the project staff in the Al Awda’s health facilities. 2- Confidentiality and privacy:- Patient consultations and documentation are private and confidential. - Beneficiaries will be provided with all the necessary information on issues related to their health status and intervention mechanisms. 3- Adaptability and acceptability.- Gender needs will be considered by having a female doctor and nurses among the work teams to meet the gender needs of women and girls. 4- COVID-19.- To achieve protection from COVID19 infection while providing services, prevention and safety procedures are followed according to the directions of the MoH and the WHO, that includes providing work teams and beneficiaries with PPE.- As part of building the capacities of the Al Awda’s staff to confront the COVID19 pandemic, a group of them was trained on ways to prevent the transmission of COVID19 infection, while another group of workers in the Al Awda will be trained on mechanisms to prevent COVID19 infection while providing SRHS services through the activities of this project.- Avoid stigmatization in the case of dealing with cases infected with COVID19.

11. - Equal and impartial access to aid
11.1 - Equal and impartial access to aid

Al Awda adopts the principle of equality and impartiality in providing its services to beneficiaries, especially marginalized groups who need humanitarian interventions to improve their health and humanitarian situation. Al Awda provides its services without any discrimination based on age, gender, religion, race, or political affiliation. Knowing that through this project and to achieve the principle of impartiality in providing services, Al Awda will provide services to the targeted people on the basis that each patient has her own individual characteristics and special needs, especially PWD as the most vulnerable in society and who need special care and protection while maintaining dignity, confidentiality and nondiscrimination. Where the Al Awda provides its services according to the slogan (Health service is a right to whoever needs it) and since the project targets marginalized groups in society, the project services will be provided totally free of charge. The project services will be easily accessible to the target groups, as the Al Awda will provide project services through its 7 health facilities that are spread throughout the Gaza Strip. Project services will be provided to target groups, including PWD and GBV, in a safe and dignified manner and without any discrimination. To ensure that the targeted women have access to the sexual and reproductive health services provided under the project, especially women who do not have the money needed for transportation to reach health facilities, they will be followed up by arranging home visits. It is worth noting that the training courses that will be organized through this project, that will target health workers in the Al Awda, will take into consideration training them on the mechanisms and methods of dealing with PWD to ensure that the service is provided without compromising their dignity.
