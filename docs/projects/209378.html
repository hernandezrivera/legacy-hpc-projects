Project Summary

The State is characterize by long term poverty, under development, high malnutrition rate , cyclic diseases outbreaks (AWD, DF, Measles, chik, etc), high prevalence rate of endemic diseases (Malaria, TB, schistosomiasis ), Prone to natural disasters like floods (Algash river) and drought, Presence of refugees camps (83,071 refugees ), long and open international border with Eretria and Ethiopia.
Cholera outbreaks is repeatedly hitting the State, since October suspected cases reported. A
joint RRT from Kassala locality and SMoH was mobilized and investigated the cases, Samples were collected from all suspected cases, results showed a positive result for cholera by rapid test and confirmed by culture in national public health laboratory in Red Sea State.
Up to 12th November 2023 the cumulative cases reported are 36 with three related deaths and case fatality ration is 8%. The outbreak is still active. Major cholera outbreaks are reported on 2006,2007, 2016 and 2017, in 2006, the State has reported 862 cases with 38
deaths, and the case fatality rate (CFR) reached 4.4%. In 2007, the State again reported 282 cases with 7 deaths CFR 2.4%. In 2016 and 2017, the State reported major outbreak of cholera, 998 cases were reported included 28 deaths and case fatality rate was 3%. Some of
the risk factors that contribute to these outbreaks include; water contamination, heavy rainfall and flooding and population displacement. It has also been associated with poverty and closely linked to inadequate drinking water and poor sanitation.
Viral Hemorrhagic Fevers (VHF) is another cause of epidemics in the State. The State has witnessed increasing numbers of Dengue disease outbreaks in the last few years, in 2019 from August to December the State reported 3,960 Dengue fever cases included 6 deaths,
again in the period from September 2021 and March 2022 the State has reported 970 cases and no associated deaths reported. The co-circulation of different dengue serotypes in the State can use hyper- endemicity and is a predisposing factor for more severe forms of
disease presentation, including dengue hemorrhagic fever (DHF) and dengue shock syndrome (DSS). In addition, concomitant infection of Dengue and Malaria has reported in some cases during the outbreaks.
Chikungunya outbreaks occur in 2018 from August to end of December, accumulative of 19,897 cases were reported during the outbreaks.
Malaria is endemic disease in the State and the main cause of consultation in health facilities, and it causing epidemic during rainy season, during this year the State has reported 68,532 malaria cases with State incidence 257 cases per 10,000 population.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

UNOPS is prioritizing raising awareness amongst different groups of affected people on their rights concerning the humanitarian assistance and related issues; the aid workers code of conduct and the CBCM mechanism. UNOPS had developed induction and training materials on the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation, Abuse, and Harassments (PSEAH) for contractors and their workers. The material addresses potential GBV risk situations in and around UNOPS work sites and explaining the expected behavior from any of the individuals working on a UNOPS project site including UNOPS personnel.
