Project Summary

This project seeks to build sustainable livelihoods and self-reliance for 1,148 beneficiaries (792 women 356 men, aged 18 to 65) through a comprehensive program of technical skill-building for income-generating activities (IGAs) and life skills training. In consultation with the community, the project integrates gender equality, disability inclusion, and environmental sustainability into each component. Key activities involve hands-on training in IGAs, including homestead vegetable gardening, poultry and pigeon rearing, tailoring, fast-food preparation, handicraft production, toy making, and market agent development. These IGAs are designed to empower participants—particularly women—by enabling income-generating work that aligns with household responsibilities. Additionally, beneficiaries will receive start-up support for IGAs, creating sustainable income sources that contribute to household food security and stability.
Life skills and leadership development training will complement IGAs by enhancing resilience and social well-being through modules on family planning, child marriage prevention, nutrition security, and disaster risk reduction (DRR). The training includes a special focus on gender-based violence (GBV) prevention and social cohesion. Cross-cutting elements emphasize eco-friendly practices that meet Sphere standards, RRRC guidelines, and ISCG protocols, and they prioritize inclusivity for women, adolescents, and persons with disabilities.
Implementation will be managed by a skilled team, with strategic support from Pulse Bangladesh Society’s senior staff and technical experts to ensure effective coordination and impact. A robust monitoring and evaluation system will track progress in age- and gender-disaggregated data, ensuring inclusivity and safety.

4. Development linkages
4.1 Does your project promote self-reliance and resilience for refugees? If yes, briefly describe.

Yes, the project fosters self-reliance and resilience for refugee beneficiaries by building sustainable livelihoods through skill development for income-generating activities (IGAs) such as gardening and tailoring. Life skills and leadership training, covering topics like family planning, nutrition security, and disaster risk reduction, further strengthen resilience. Additionally, GBV prevention, social cohesion, eco-friendly practices, and adherence to Sphere and ISCG standards ensure inclusivity and align with the needs of women, youth, and people with disabilities, promoting both independence and community stability.

4.2 Do project activities in the host community link to development initiatives, and/or align with development priorities in the Cox’s Bazar district? If yes, briefly describe.If yes, briefly describe.

Yes, the project promotes self-reliance and resilience for the host community, including 30% of beneficiaries from Rajapalong, by focusing on sustainable livelihoods through skill-building for income-generating activities (IGAs) like vegetable gardening and tailoring. The project aligns with other priorities—gender equality, disability inclusion, and environmental sustainability—ensuring that these elements are embedded in each component. Life skills training on family planning, nutrition security, and disaster risk reduction (DRR), along with a focus on GBV prevention and social cohesion, enhances resilience. Eco-friendly practices and adherence to Sphere, RRRC, and ISCG protocols further align the project with best practices for inclusive and sustainable impact.
