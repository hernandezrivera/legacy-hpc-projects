Project Summary

This Project that Mandher Relief and Development Org,(MARDO) Proposing to implement in the targeted areas of the Region –Bakol will bring sustainable water supplies, improved water, hygiene and better sanitation practices to 13,000 people living in Rabdure and El berde districts- Bakol, in collaboration with UNOCHA Plan, that will deliver results through WASH clusters and Integrated existing Bakol regional water Stakeholders like Water Management Committee (WMC) app¬¬roach over the period of the 12 months. Specifically the project will:
1. Establish Water Management Committees at district level that represent the key stakeholders, and facilitate the development of rehabilitating water infrastructures (Shallow wells and B/holes), construction of communal and individual Sanitation Blocks (with access for PWDs) along side with distribution of Hygiene kits and WASH committee Plans through a participatory planning process and through provision of expert input to analyses the feasibility of different options
2. Provide secure access to safe water through renovation and construction of water points and infrastructure for enhancing groundwater recharge, as flowing from the sustainable methodology that IP and project Stakeholders as following.
3. Promote improvements in hygiene and sanitation practices
4. Document and share lessons learnt within and outside the Region that MARDO has extensive experience of working in the WASH sectors in the State of South West Somalia and the keep mobilizing rapidly due to strong links with communities and government particularly Minestry of Water and Mineral Energy ( MoWE) . that MARDO had good collaboration and worked together with other partners previously on other projects and have proven capability in learning from and supporting each other’s work.
To ensure sustainability, the project will work to strengthen sector management and co-ordination across the region districts, enabling the cost-effective approaches applied by the project to be continued and shared more widely in support of national goals. A common M&E framework that considers the requirements for payment by results will be designed during the beginning phase. Monitoring information will be used as input for progress reports, and will also contribute to internal learning. Monitoring will not only track the quantity and quality of outputs, assumptions, and context factors, but also dis aggregate data by gender, age, and locality; inclusive all persons with disabilities in the targeted region.

Project Activities

Rehabilitation of Wells, Construction of latrines (with modified access for PWDs), community capacity building, and distribution of WASH NFIs Kits, The proposed project fits neatly with the objectives of the Business Case. It will cover the emergency towards Development replicable water sources in Rabdure and El berde which is to result of building on lessons learned in the area as well as, among others, in west and North of Region. The project will actively seek to contribute to the improvement of water resource Management skills and approach in the targeted locations by the responsible authorities. In summary, the theory of change of the project can be formulated as follows: -By Improving and managing water resources in an integrated manner within rural catchment areas in Rabdure and El berde districts in a manner that ensures ownership by resident and (semi-)nomadic communities, local authorities and other relevant stakeholders and is based on a sound technical understanding of the catchment water balance, it will be possible to increase the reliability and availability of water for all stakeholders. Together with improved access to sanitation and hygiene, improved access to safe drinking water (and more stable livelihoods) will contribute to improved health status and improved general well-being of the targeted population. By documenting and sharing lessons learned, this specific project can contribute to the further development of WASH practice in districts and Red Sea, thereby contributing to an improvement in health, livelihoods and well-being, and a reduction in the potential for local resource conflicts. Mitigation Measures

The project has a system that ensures the beneficiaries report their concerns and complains easily through an installed committee that comprises of members of the project staff, our management teams and project community committee that will ensure no barriers between the beneficiaries and the complaint desk. During the selection of the project community committee will be formed from all groups of the community where women, youth, PWDs and the members the community are all represented and they will be included the decision making process throughout the project period. The community and designated officers from MARDO will ensure that no one is harmed during the registration of the project beneficiaries and identification of the project activities sites. • WASH services need to be delivered using a stronger protection lens which involves among other enormous efforts to ensure latrines and bathing facilities are gender separated, provided with lockable doors, lighted at night, ease of access for PWDs and distance to water point is reduced to agreed standards. • IDPs settlements (and host communities in crowded urban/sub-urban fringes), there is a need to gradually improve the quality and quantity of services delivered, including when promoting good hygiene practices and access to improve sanitation. Sanitary surveys need to be promoted to prevent, avoid and/or address contamination of water sources. There is a need to implement more sustainable solutions in the targeted areas particularly Washago, Yet, ATO and En-berde when the local environment enables it. • In drought and AWD/Cholera historical hotspots, there is a need to deliver more long-term WASH solutions both urban and rural areas of the REGION Bakol that enables to progressively improve the water and sanitation ladder. It also involves the improvement of existing water supply and sanitation systems and ensuring their environmental and functional sustainability. • In time of WASH-related disease outbreak of disasters, emergency temporary services are still required to address life-saving needs and reduce WASH-related mortality/morbidity, particularly in children. It will involve temporary solutions for safe water provision, construction of simple designed sanitation systems and the promotion of good hygiene practices
