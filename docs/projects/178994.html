Project Summary

In order to increase the access to education and provide education services with good quality, the proposed methodology will include: provision of non-formal education for vulnerable children ( boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community
Project will be implemented in non-formal settings in 11 locations in Damascus and Rural Damascus ( 45 % of severity scale 3, 55 % of severity scale 4)
• Cost per beneficiary is 67 $ noting that the total number of beneficiaries is calculated with no duplication and the services include cash intervention
• Estimated % of targeted beneficiaries that may have disabilities is 3%
• No funding was received in 2020 for AlTamayoz HRP project
• Total estimated number of people the organization reached in 2020 through partnership with UNHCR was 1400, and number of people reached under SHF fund in 2020 reached 10403, and about 3000 child benefited from different services provided by AlTamayoz.
• this project is not funded yet.
• Total estimated funding the organization received so far in 2021 for 2 locations in Rural Damascus is about 180000 $
the project will directly contribute to the Education Sector’s HRP objectives 1 and 2, and it will target children (IDPs, host community and returnees) due to the clearly observed need especially after the remarkable increase in all material prices,
Targeted locations are of severity scale 3, and 4 where AlTamayoz has access and needed approval and previous presence in some of them.
The project will address the beneficiary’s needs according to FGDs made by AlTamayoz targeted men, women, boys, and girls, and according to data illustrated in HNO 2021, in addition to needs shared by education sector, activities are tailored according to the need in each age group
The project contributes to increasing access to education for girls and boys of different ages,as it tatrgets children boys and girls aged from 3 to 24 years, in addition, it will support children with disabilities of different ages by providing suitable and easy access in the facility, deploying trained staff.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021: There are more than 2000 non-operational school premises across Syria.From total number of schools in Rural Damascus which is 1347, only 775 (50%) are functioning while 313 are non-functioning, and 259 need light rehabilitation.Younger children, adolescent, and children with disabilities face further challenges accessing learning services due to limited availability of ECE centers, secondary education services and vocational training opportunities.Many children are either behind in their learning, finding it difficult t return to school or at risk of dropping outA large number of teachers with qualifications left the country and most of rural areas still facing lack of teachers.Teachers are absent in a large number of rural school due to the lack of transportation support. As from AlTamyoz observations: Out-of-school children face an immediate risk of exploitation and abuse such as early forced marriage, child labor. Children’s sense of well-being is jeopardized and the impact of crisis on children and their development can have tremendous consequences for their adult lives if they are not provided with appropriate support and healing mechanisms. Increased poverty levels limits positive skills development opportunities for youth and adolescents, and their ability to continue their education and increases the exposure to risky and exploitative situations. Lack of income-generating activities have weakened the purchasing power of people living in Damascus and Rural Damascus.Altamayoz will contribute to increase the access to education and provide qualitative education services through provision of non-formal education services and supporting teacher training.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be directly implemented by AlTamayoz and will be in line with HRP framework and will contribute to HRP objective2.It will assure equal access to education for boys and girls and for children from all affected communities, special support will be provided to children with disabilities and those of special needs. AlTAmayoz has sustained presence in Damascus and selected locations in RD. A well database and reporting system will be in place, in addition to regular meetings with staff to evaluate the achieved results towards targets, Activities in Damscus, Duma and Harsta will be as an expansion to already existing activities where the need is very huge.Newly established learning centres in RD(Arbin,Babella,Kafr Batna,AlZabdani,Ein elfijeh,Hajar alaswad,Qudsiya, and Darayya)aim at expanding services to new geographic areas and new populations. Students will benefit from comprehensive package of services(non formal education sessions,PSS sessions and recreational activities, school supplies, school feeding,transportation, Cash assistance) in addition to BTL campaigns; supporting life skills training and TVET for those aged 17-24. this project is linked to another protection project where Mine risk education is provided to students and teachers, and referrals of students with protection risks to case management Activities will consider COVID-19 by assuring social distancing and preventive measures(awareness raising, PPEs) AlTamayoz will deploy well qualified teachers and PSS providers,pre and post tests to measure the course impact teacher will be provided with capacity building. Monitoring activities:consultation with communities,feedback mechanisms,pre and post assessments of students level,filed visit

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project will ensure safety of services through providing services in a protective environment away from all kind of violence, child safeguarding is ensured at the learning centres through different aspects (all staff including busses drivers has to sign a code of conduct that prevents any kind of violence.Safety system will be in place and will include: providing centers with fire extinguishers, training staff on first aid, separate bathrooms for boys and girls, centers will maintain a high level of hygiene through deploying a cleaner in each center.Risk: lack of safety and insecurity including contamination with mines. Mitigating measures: Ensure awareness raising among students, teachers, and parents.Risk: Violence against children in and around learning spaces: corporal punishment, bullying. Mitigation measures: AlTamayoz will set regulation for all staff and teachers that prevents all forms of violence, Children will be closely supervised to prevent any kind of bullying among students and teachersRisk: Tensions within the community or between the organization and community due to feeling of being unfairly treated, insufficiently consulted or listened to or concerns with the project. Mitigation measures: The community should be consulted and listened to through the life of the project (assessments, design, monitoring, corrective actions, evaluation, etc.). Risk: Children are over-exposed to the risk of, harm by adults such as teachers, organization staff, other adults or older students. Mitigation measures: Sufficient safe, functioning and clean bathroom facilities will be provided. Teachers and other education personnel will be regularly trained on positive discipline and child safeguarding.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk:lack of well qualified staff in the area of implementation due to the lack of education opportunities during the crisis years /Likelihood: high. impact: lowMitigating measure: capacity building for people from the implementation area, and deployment of part of the staff from DamascusRisk:lack of electricity and water in the Rural areas that might affect the implementation efficiency Likelihood: high. impact: medium Mitigating measure: support centers with generator, support centers with tank for waterRisk of staff refusal to work in far areas from their homes Likelihood: high. impact: high Mitigating measure: support transportation for staff Coordination:Project will be well coordinated with other organizations working in the same area through sharing information and provide complimentary services. coordination with other actors in the field like child protection actors (referral pathway to refer cases observed while providing education or PSS services) health actors, nutrition actors (allow Nutrition volunteers to perform screening and refer cases to specialized nutrition services,..) Accountability: Many focus groups discussion were conducted during planning phase included (women, adolescents, boys and girls children), interviews were also made in different areas in Rrural Damascus.all information collected were used to design the project frame work and to select the most needed activities and locations.During implementation, a tracking method will be in place to assure proper implementation through surveys and another type of focus group discussions. beneficiaries will have access to complain box
