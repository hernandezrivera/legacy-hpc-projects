Project Summary

Prolonged conflict and instability in Darfur and the surrounding regions has caused mass displacement and necessitated the establishment of Internally Displace People (IDP) camps.
The proposed project aims to intervene in the sector of livelihood, supporting 73,612 families become self-sufficient through income generation activities, targeting vulnerable households both IDP and host communities.
In Kutum Plan implementing peacebuilding and community stability project. This project is complementing mentioned peace project for optimal impact on affected people in the area.
Since the peace building working on community structuring and building relations between community groups, this project will use the same community structure for livelihood improvement which would push on enforcement of marginalized category for example empowerment of women will enable them participate actively in decision making and community dialogue and conversations the same will be for youth groups.

1 - Needs

The prolonged emergency situation and new displacements has deteriorated the humanitarian situation and created instability in the region. Humanitarian organizations that have been engaged in provision of services for more than ten years have been guided to gradually move towards early recovery phase and minimize the emergency services in IDP camps. Similarly, the government of Sudan, UN agencies and international organizations are intending to move gradually from the emergency phase towards early recovery programming. Many organizations are engaged in developing their early recovery plans; UNHCR/UNOPs which is Emergency Shelter (ES) /NFIs cluster lead is scaling down the services for IDP in Zamzam and similarly, WFP is scaled down its programme in Zamzam IDP camp. While international organisations are moving away from emergency aid, the escalation of periodic fighting in Jebel Marra is causing new displacements in North Darfur in Tawila in 2016, straining resources of existing IDP camp of project targeted beneficiaries.As the demand for livelihood services increases, resources remain scarce within project targeted areas

2 - Activities / Outputs

Expected Result 1: Resilience and self-reliance of IDP in Zamzam and Tawila IDP camps strengthened through increased access to appropriate livelihoods’ development and income generation opportunities, thereby reducing dependence on external aid. Conduct baseline survey to be used for progress detection for all project interventions.• Market survey to identify livelihood opportunities for youth m/f. Assessment results will be based for all livelihood interventions.• Construction and support 5 youth centers with furniture and facilities to be used as community compound for implementation of all project activities. • Establish and support 10 youth (m/f) IGAs groups for saving and loan Plan will support the groups with starting grants.• increased access to BEST programs and to employment/business development of 3 networks for youth, especially girls. • Establish and support women household garden with introduction of drip irrigation innovation for 80 households.• Individual cash grants for IGAs targeting 500 youth both male and female to start small enterprise business. Expected Result 2: Cash/food crops and livestock productivity increased.This will be through Improved knowledge skills and infrastructure and services the activities will be:• Provision of veterinary services include treatment and vaccination of at least 100000 head of small animals.• Goat restocking on revolving bases for 250 households with rate of 4 heads one male and three females. • Establishment of 3 farmers field schools• Seed multiplication and 3 seed bank establishment at the community level. • Introduce water harvesting techniques through production and distribution of 300 donkey plough and building of 500 Tarrases.• Introduction and support of 4 community vegetable gardens groups of 20 members. Expected Result 3: cross tribal community, youth, CBOs capable to mange natural resources and protect environment in targeted areasRelevant gender considerations will be mainstreamed throughout this project and periodic gender analyses will be conducted. Specific gender equality results on access and control of productive resources and decision making at household and community levels will be identified and addressed to strengthen the overall impact of the program and to demonstrate measurable progress made towards improving equality between men and women, this will be through: • Enhanced youth (m/f) skills in gender-sensitive decision-making, group-building, public engagement and peace building through training of 300 participants.• Introduce cross tribal community management agroforestry system using suitable local types of trees in 6 sites.• Purchase and distribution of 200 Gas stoves with gas Cylinder as pilot project for the purpose of reduce fire wooding for environment protection this action will done with consultation of local stakeholders and communities.• Non-fired Brick making targeting youth male and female. This activity has three dimensions; income generation; natural resource conservation and job opportunities for youth to reduce their involvement in conflicts. Each group of youth (5-10) will be provided with one brick making machine • Environment protection through local rules and regulations with involvement of youth group. This will be implemented through training of 200 leaders, women and youth groups.

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project will take a gender-integrated approach across the board by: a) ensuring that the voices of women and girls boys and men, people with special needs..etc are equitably heard throughout the project cycle with youth involvement and consultation of project targeted communities and stakeholders by specifically organizing consultations with women and girls in gender-sensitive ways, and by promoting women’s decision-making. b) Including a gender lens across project outputs including the market mapping to assess, not only the emerging social economic problems and protection issues but also gender related challenges, GBV, children access to education, access to clean drinking water, and barriers obstructing young women and children to participate to their full potential within their respective community in such project. Therefore, they can be aware and acquainted with their rights and process of learn to lead, and decide, as well thriving with dignity and respect within their own community. c) Skills training to ensure no gender stereotypes are reinforced that the targeted communities are empowered with an understanding of their rights and gender equality. And the trainings are offered in gender-responsive ways to ensure women, children, farmers, PTAs and local authorities understand gender issues and challenges faces women to prosper in their life, and their role in preventing GBV or exploitation.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Base interventions on gendered analysis of the causes and impacts of crises, the different need of young women and men, and design programs to meet those different needs, including collecting and having a regular analysis on SADDD, in order to target assistance towards those most at risk. • Look for opportunities to support young women and men in ways that contribute to the transformation of gendered power relations: lay the groundwork for gender equality and greater resilience to conflict and crises, by actively supporting women’s leadership and by challenging attitudes and beliefs that undermine women’s rights, including engaging with men and boys. • Promote the full participation and leadership of young women and men in all aspects of programming – from planning to evaluation. • Have gender-sensitive M&E system by developing gender sensitive indicators (quantitative and qualitative), as well as conducting regular tracking on the impact of the emergency response towards young women and men.
