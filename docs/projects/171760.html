Project Summary

In 2021 humanitarian response, Plan International will improve protection environment and promote child rights in Somaliland for IDP and vulnerable populations, particularly at-risk children. The country has one of the lowest child and gender indicators in the world with only one-quarter of girls – 24% aged 15-24 considered as literate, 45% married before 18 years and 98% subjected to female genital mutilation/cutting (FGM/C). The COVID19 quarantine measures to prevent and control the spread of COVID-19 exposed children and women to increased protection risks, particularly within the home, owing to increased stress caused by the outbreak, exacerbating existing gender inequalities and violence. Child Protection Areas of Responsibility in collaboration with UNICEF conducted a child protection survey in April 2020 showed more than half of the CP agencies (57%) reported an increase in physical violence particular on girls. There has also been increased exposure to violence especially to girls through FGM/C. According to the United Nations Population Fund, COVID-19 could have far reaching impacts on the effort to end FGM/C, with a potential 2 million FGM/C cases occurring over the next decade that could have otherwise been averted. Plan will work with CSOs and other partners to effectively promote and support improving community-based child protection mechanisms. Key interventions will target young girls and boys, their parents and other decision-makers to raise awareness on child protection and GBV, establish and strengthen referral mechanisms for case management and other services including MHPSS and financial support, and capacity building and advocacy with local authorities and for national agenda on centrality of protection (e.g. importance of child protection and campaigns on GBV, adoption of the positive discipline techniques, inclusion of children with disability) and distribution dignity kits. Plan and partners will also promote PSS for children and adolescents affected by crisis, support youth-led DRR initiatives, and establish girls groups to prioritize creating safe spaces for dialogue and peer friendships. Plan and partners will provide age-appropriate, gender-sensitive, and disability inclusive services that address the different impacts of emergencies on adolescents and young children, boys and girls and promote the potential for positive transformation of gender norms. Plan and partners will maintain child-friendly feedback and complaint mechanism and promote transparency across all activities.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

For Plan International whenever appropriate and possible, cash or voucher assistance should be the preferred form of aid over in-kind. In markets contexts where supply is adequate or prices are not dramatically inflated, providing cash or vouchers will save lives. Distribution team must investigate the gender considerations in order in making decisions for CTP to prioritize improving wellbeing of women and girls. From our 2020 response, we found multipurpose cash assistance to be good practice and an effective conduit to assist people in IPDs in Somaliland, and it boosted the economy, protecting the dignity while reducing social gatherings.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

In consultation with the Cash Working Group and other sectors in Hargeisa, Plan will ensure sufficient assessment of market functionality. This will determine whether target population would be able to procure and meet basic needs with what is locally available or if there are supply gaps, issues of inflation, or another reason to provide in-kind.

Mitigation Measures

- Plan will train on child safeguarding policy and ensure staff, partner staff, vendors, frontline workers, etc sign and commit to PSEA policy -Ensure consistent visibility of Plan with billboards, help desk, and flyers with phone/email contacts and in-person collection of child-friendly feedback and complaints. - Children with disabilities given special consideration for design/selection and prioritized for participation- Careful design and proper planning of needs, time, location – assess unique risks and barriers to participation - Include community members in project design and implementation- Include community members in selection criteria and process - Spot check at construction sites to ensure no risks to children or community - Ensure clear reporting/complaint process is displayed on site- Increased conflict sensitivity in programming- Careful selection and proper planning based on time and location - assess risks prior to activity, particularly distributions to ensure appropriateness of location and other considerations- Plan will also take environmental impact into consideration across project implementation with mitigation measures - collection of waste post distribution, long-term durable or recycleable materials as possible
