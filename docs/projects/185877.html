Project Summary

This project will address the dire humanitarian needs due to the continuing activities of the terrorist sect Boko Haram (BH) in Nigeria and North Cameroon. BH is a terrorist group based on extremist Islam, originally from Nigeria. The conflict is mostly concentrated in Nigeria with incursions in the Far North of Cameroun where in the past years they established also some operational bases. With tens of thousands of households displaced, and asylum seekers entering the Far North of Cameroon, LWF started a response in 2016 to alleviate the suffering of the most vulnerable households including Internal displaced People(IDPs), Returnees from IDPs sites, Refugees or local host communities, in this region of Cameroon by addressing their most pressing needs. In this project phase, the process will occur though a holistic and multi-sectoral approach including initiatives in Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) and psychosocial support and social cohesion activities to unite communities together in these hard times. LWF will implement activities through a comprehensive and participatory methodology towards rehabilitation, which include the rights holder at each level of the process. LWF will also work closely and support the local LWF member church, the Church of the Lutheran Brethren of Cameroon (CLBC), that is already responding to the needs of persons affected by the Boko Haram crisis. Other local churches within the community will also be supported in activities related with peace building. In addition, peace and social cohesion activities will be implemented in schools that receive displaced children through the establishment of "Peace in School" activities.
