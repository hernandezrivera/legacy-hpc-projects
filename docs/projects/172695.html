Project Summary

The Logistics Sector will aim to provide Information sharing and Coordination platform to
the humanitarian community operating in Libya where needed. The Logistics Sector will
facilitate humanitarian community access to common logistics services identified as
critical to implement humanitarian response plan in Libya, such services will be made
available where the most needed across Libya – with a focus on main hubs such as
Tripoli, Benghazi and Sebha.

1 - Needs

Based on consultations with humanitarian organizations, the major gaps and needsidentified are: 1)) A Logistics Information Management and Coordination platform that cansupport the collection, analysis and sharing of timely and reliable logistics information inorder to coordinate organizations interventions, avoid duplication of efforts and ensurethat available resources in the country are optimally utilized among the differenthumanitarian actors. 2)) A coordination mechanism in the technical area of logistics is relevant to support the implementation of the 2021 HRP in Libya.3)) Partners have highlighted the need to have access to up-to-date and reliable logistics-related information.

2 - Activities

The Logistics Sector activities will include providing information sharing and coordinationplatform -where and when needed – to allow humanitarian community (i) to have accessto up to date and relevant logistics information, (ii) make sure available in-countrylogistics resources are used, and (iii) avoid duplication of efforts. The logistics sector willsupport the implementation and access of common logistics services – where and whenaccess to sufficient logistics services on the local market has been identified as acommon gap or bottleneck by the humanitarian community in order to maintain anuninterrupted supply chain of relief aid. In case of identified common logistics gaps, theLogistics Sector Lead Agency, WFP, will act as “provider of last resort”. The LogisticsSector will provide mapping and assessment of key logistics infrastructures, including butnot limited to main entry points into Libya. The Logistics Sector will also support theidentification of customs related bottlenecks/constraints and will identify/advocate forways of improvement. These activities will support a multi-sectorial response approach.The Logistics sector will also conduct a Logistics Capacity Assessment (LCA) in Malta toidentify potential staging area at the free port to serve as a storage area prior to shuntingto Libyan ports.

5 - Mitigation measures
5.1 - For each risk mentioned under 4 above, please describe the ways you plan to mitigate, prevent and/or address these.

COVID-19 measures will be observed all the time during implementation. PPEs will be required to be provided to the project implementers to avoid contraction. Social distancing will be observed and enforced for sector members. Virtual meetings will be a key success factor for implementation. The Logistics Sector will comply with rules on UN staff movement in terms of fillingproper trip documentation of UNDSS website. The staff to get the security clearance tomove prior to any movement. Additionally, the Logistics Sector adheres to rules ofmovement within Libya and avoids areas which are not cleared from a securityperspective. Prior to any cargo movement, The Logistics Sector sensitizes marketintelligence such as transporters to obtain advise on the security of the route. Moreover,seeks information on routes from several sources such as agencies security, WFPSecurity, UNDSS and other humanitarian players.
