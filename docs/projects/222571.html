Project Summary

While taking in mind the humanitarian catastrophe that is lingering around South Sudan States, it is important that such a state of affairs shouldn’t be left to explode as the options are on the table to address it. It should be noted that the targeted State are the most contested states in the ongoing conflicts, floods and droughts and therefore it bares the brand of the most devastated of all states in terms of human displacement, destruction of property and loss of lives. For the moment it noted that the population are in need of humanitarian assistance and protection in order to rebuild their lives while also noting that even the host communities who had developed coping mechanism of rotating with in the conflict zones without necessarily evacuating all together are equally devastated and need humanitarian assistance because most of livelihood sources were destroyed or looted that has left the this states in absolute poverty. The fact that food security situation is very acute, and while noting that people rely on natural weather phenomenon to support agriculture, there is need to exploit alternative food sources like fishing by providing fishing nets to the households. This will not only provide the much needed supply of protein foods but it will act as a source of income when due training is given to the fishermen to learn simple fish preservation.
The presence of the water bodies will also be essential in establishment of the demonstration farms for vegetables by providing irrigation water.
Justification for the project geographical area:
• The project will be implemented in 2 states in South Sudan, which have been severely affected by floods conflict, displacement, and food insecurity.
• These states have some of the highest rates of malnutrition, poverty, and displacement in the country.
• The project will focus on rural areas, where access to basic services such as healthcare, education, and clean water is limited.
Justification for the target group:
• The target group will be vulnerable households, including internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities.
• These households will be selected based on their level of vulnerability, including criteria such as food insecurity, poverty, and lack of access to basic services.
• The project will prioritize households with children under the age of 5, pregnant women, and breastfeeding mothers, as they are most at risk of malnutrition

1. Needs

The targeted States emerged as the most highly contested area with the worst humanitarian crisis since the start of the conflict. The communities had to move to very remote areas most which were swamps to escape the fighting and all forms of livelihood initiatives like agriculture were abandoned, livestock was confiscated by the belligerents and used as food for the armed groups leaving the population unable to meet the most basic nutrition needs especially of the children. Malnutrition rates for children ran very high as there was no supply of protein and vitamin rich foods as the IPC Acute malnutrition analysis shows that the acute malnutrition levels deteriorate because of food insecurity. According to the IPC results of 2024 the nutrition situation has also deteriorated, with an estimated 2.1 million children aged 6-59 months suffering or expected to suffer elevated levels of acute malnutrition between July 2024 and June 2025, out of which 650,000 are cases of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM). About 1.11 million pregnant or breastfeeding women (PBW) are also suffering or expected to suffer elevated levels of acute malnutrition in the same period., Between September and November 2024, an estimated 6.3 million people (47 percent of the population analysed) are classified in IPC Phase 3 or above (Crisis or worse). Of this total, 1.71 million people are facing critical levels of acute food insecurity – classified as IPC Phase 4 (Emergency) – and a further 41,000 people are facing catastrophic levels of acute food insecurity – classified as IPC Phase 5 (Catastrophe). Nutrition information data reporting indicate that the number of children with Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) increased by 17% and Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM)increased by 35% between January and April compared to same period in 2018. The dire need of livelihood support has translated into challenges that do not only linger around economic ones but also social ones. Boys are being manipulated into joining militia groups because their personal need to survive through being provided with food or with a hope of acquiring a weapon which they can use to raid for livestock, men have become hostile because economic pressure resulting from the loss of their livestock and are displaced from their original lands where they could practice agriculture and even do not have the ability to start anew because of lack of start-up kits and farming implements like maludas and pangas to clear the gardens including the seeds for planting. The effects of this scenario has created psychosocial distress among most men that has translated into increased cases of GBV in homes to the extend that even the young girls are paying a price of early and forced marriage.
