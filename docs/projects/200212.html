Project Summary

The Strategic ERP objective adopted by this project is to provide essential humanitarian support to most vulnerable people affected by the economic crisis in Lebanon for them to meet their critical needs.
The project aims at increasing access to and retention in quality and inclusive education for the most vulnerable children (6-17 years) in the area of Beqaa, Baalbeck-Hermel by providing retention support, financial assistance, and protection interventions in schools/community centers responding to the high rate of drop-outs among the Lebanese population and migrant workers after the multifaceted crises targeting 1,000 school-aged children in total over 1-year duration. 

Description of activities:
The project activities include three main interventions:
Provide Supportive and Complementary Learning Opportunities through retention and remedial classes in schools/community centers following an integrated approach with child-protection measures.
Provide financial support to HHs to enhance retention through cash for education.
Provide teacher capacity building packages focusing on quality and inclusive education, and protection-related trainings, PSEA, CP, SRHR, SG, and GBV.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1)Removing barriers in access to education through cash for education:Targeting the households of 1,000 school-aged children in schools in Beqaa and Baalbek area, by providing the $20 cash-for-education component on monthly basis for one scholastic year following regular and legal processes of payments to beneficiaries. 2)Providing Supportive and Complementary Learning Opportunities:Conduct two Retention Support cycles, each targeting 500 learners in 10 target locations among Beqaa and Baalbek regions to cover learning gaps. This program includes transportation cost, parents’ awareness sessions, refreshments, and integration of protection-related measures. 3)Providing awareness & development sessions to parents/caregivers: Partners will, as part of their outreach efforts, raise awareness among parents/caregivers on the importance of education and available resources that will support children to enroll in school, continue their education and avoid any learning interruptions. In each cycle, the project provides three PAS to increase parents’ engagement and participation. 4)Teachers, education personnel and educators have enhanced capacities to provide quality, learner-centered pedagogy in public schools or learning spaces:The project focuses on providing intensive training for educators before and during the cycle focusing on new learning modalities, active learning, cooperative learning, and inclusion.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The project responds to issues of PSEA through the following measures:A comprehensive risk assessment will be conducted to identify potential protection risks and develop measures to prevent and mitigate them. All project (plan and partner) ill also receive comprehensive training on child protection, safeguarding, and PSEA.A robust complaints and feedback mechanism will be established to enable children and their families to report any concerns or complaints they may have about the project or its staff. The project will engage with the local community to build awareness of child protection and safeguarding issues.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project will engage affected populations, including children, parents, caregivers, and community leaders, through regular consultations, feedback mechanisms, and community meetings. The project will provide timely and accessible information and establish a complaints and feedback mechanism to address concerns and improve project implementation. Regular monitoring and evaluation will be conducted to assess the effectiveness of the project and collect feedback from beneficiaries and stakeholders through Post activity monitoring calls and post distribution monitoring calls, as well as pre and post surveys to assess project impact and beneficiary satisfaction
