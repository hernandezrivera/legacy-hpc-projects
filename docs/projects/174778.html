Project Summary

The armed conflict has imposed 271164 IDPs in urban centers and hardship among the populations. Most of the IDPs are girls 51% (64165) boys 49% (62663). More than 30% of women have become widowed. Imposed lockdowns have weakened the economy as the IDPs have lost their source of livelihoods and assets. Many have lost agricultural seeds. Many have lost business capita hence no jobs and immediate income earning opportunities. This situation gets worst with unskilled vulnerable population. These pictures leaves a population to engage into negative coping mechanism such as survival sex for girls and young women, boys are joining Non State Armed Groups (NSAG) as survival strategies. Sexual exploitation is on the rise. IDPs are further exploited by their host families and displaced further. Escalation of conflict as a hungry person is an angry person. Stealing and many other atrocities are increasing.
The purpose of this project is to contribute to the livelihood recovery of the affected population through; Increase skills in layer poultry farming, increase incomes of the target population through Cash For Work model and Increase sustainable livelihood activities among the vulnerable population through Start-ups kits and passing over the Gift (POG).
The target population will be selected and recruited to work in the layer poultry farm and gaining practical skill. While learning and providing these services they will be place in the Cash for Work Concept. This will permit them acquire prioritized basic needs. When the layers birds star laying eggs they are given 50 each as start-up kits. The birds will be laying eggs and they are selling and taking care of their basic need and likely to expand the business or permit them engage in their dream business. They are expected to support second line beneficiaries with ¼ incomes from the start-up in the POG concept.
Target population includes
Internally Displace Persons particularly women and girls: These are people displaced because of insecurity or rather looking for places where there is security and have lost their livelihood sources, accommodation and many separated from families.
Poor widows: both IDPs and host communities have many widows that have lost their husbands in the war and do not have any sustainable livelihood source
Women heading households; Many men have displaced in search of jobs and have abandoned their wives without any source of income
Teenage mothers, married and pregnant adolescent who are unable to secure basic needs for herself and child and disparate
Very poor women in host communities without skills to look for any suitable jobs
Women have lost their livelihood in the host communities.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Limited immediate income earning opportunities for IDPs and other vulnerable population in the community experience hardship. This project will put a layer Poultry day old chick farm. Identify and recruit very needy vulnerable population to be taking care of the layer day old chicks. While providing these services they will earn a payment within Cash for Work (CFW) model. They will be providing these services until the layer chicks get mature within 6 months and start producing eggs. The layer farm provides immediate income earning opportunities and beneficiaries are capable of earning money thereby increasing their purchasing power on their basic needs. Decide what to buy per their needs, live dignified life styles. This farm also provide opportunity for on the ground skill building in layer birds egg production combined with class room theory on disease prevention, vaccine, feeding etc. the skill building opportunity make them experts and they can exploit it and become macro layer poultry farmers and possibly generate jobs for others. During this skill building and cash earned, ¼ of it will be saved. Vulnerable target experience limited capital to star-up income generating activities. Provision of start-up kits: when the layers birds get mature and start laying eggs, the beneficiaries are given 50 of the layer birds each as start-up capital. The savings during Cash for work period will be used to prepare the brooding house for the layers birds. It is expected that at least 40 eggs produced per day averagely from the 50 birds and about 1200 eggs per month. Hence about 40 trays each month is produced. A tray of 30 eggs is sold for at least 1800FCFA locally in the quarters. It is expected that they harvest at least 72,000FCFA monthly from the poultry. These create sustainable income to these beneficiaries and generate income that they may reinvest in the layer poultry or take on business venture desired thereby injecting money in the local market. Beneficiaries acquire basic needs with ease. Creates reliability and empowered individuals to decide on their own. Empower households to decide on their purchase. Passing Over the Gift(POG); When the first beneficiaries of the Star-up kits are operational, they will be saving ¼ of the monthly earning and will hand it to support the Cash for work salary for the next batch of beneficiaries. Actually each first line beneficiary will hand the cash for work to the next line beneficiary so that culture of relay support can be built in the community. Some values and respect as well as building bridges (Social cohesion) for experience sharing in the days ahead after the project ends among beneficiaries of this project. A multiplier effect of injecting cash in the local economy increases. Beneficiaries have the power to re- invest in their community. Own processes in the community and get back the communities on rail. Overall this project help to; re-unite communities and mental relaxation; Maintain dignity of the affected populations; Empower individuals to spend money according to their needs and priorities. Affected populations participate in community processes; Shocks are mitigated. Reduce further Displacements of affected population.
