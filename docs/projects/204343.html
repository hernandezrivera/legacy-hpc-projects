Project Summary

The overall objective of this Project is to ensure that diabetic and renal failure patients in Sudan continue to have access to life-saving treatment, through procurement of Insulin and Adult Heamodialysis kits

1 - Needs

Currently in Sudan, there are over 7,000 patients with renal failure who are registered for renal dialysis and around 10-12% of people aged 20-79 have type 1 or type 2 diabetes in Sudan. Since 2017 UNDP was supporting National Medical Supplies Fund in procuring adult dialysis kits as well as Insulin. By 2022, UNDP was covering 50% of country need for the kits and 100% of the needs for Insulin. Funding was from the Government of Sudan, due to the current crisis, the government is not capable to fund those needs and needed population are at risk for loosing their lives.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Through existing long term agreements between UNDP and global supplier for Insulin and Adult Heamodialyis kits (Kits), and in close coordination with National Medical Supplies Fund, UNDP will do procurement and support distribution of both Insulin and kits. quantities to be procured is equal to 6 months needs of the country based on the current consumption rates of each item.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Dialysis services is done by dialysis centers in Sudan which has a country full coverage. The cost of the services is fully subsidized by the government. for Insulin, it will be distributed with a subsidized cost to the needy population. Both wide distribution and subsidization will enhance accessibility, preserve dignity and it will be mainstreamed within the current health system.
