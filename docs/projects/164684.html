Project Summary

The project aims at supporting vulnerable children ( boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community) with the needed education services
• Project will be implemented in non-formal settings in 10 locations ( 20 % of severity scale 3, 50 % of severity scale 4, and 30 % of severity scale 5)
• Cost per beneficiary is 49.6 $ noting that the total number of beneficiaries is calculated with no duplication
• Estimated % of targeted beneficiaries that may have disabilities is 3%
• No funding was received in 2019 for AlTamayoz HRP project
• Total estimated number of people the organization reached in 2019 through partnership with UNHCR was 1400, more than 10000 child benefited from different services provided by AlTamayoz.
• this project is not funded yet.
project is targeting children (IDPs, host community and returnees) due to the clearly observed need especially after the remarkable increase in all material prices, targeted locations are of severity scale 3,4 and 5, where AlTamayoz has access and needed approval and previous presence.
The project will address the beneficiary’s needs according to FGDs made by AlTamayoz targeted men, women, boys, and girls.
The project contributes to increasing access to education for girls and boys of different ages, in addition it will support children with disabilities of different ages by providing suitable and easy access in the facility, deploying trained staff.
If the funding received is lower than what requested, the BTL campaigns and recreational activities will be cut from all locations. the project will directly contributes to the Education Sector’s HRP objectives 1 and 2. activities will be clarified in the specialized part of the project due to the limited space in this paragraph, total number of beneficiaries is 16850 and it is counted as beneficiaries not as services.

4 - Needs

During AlTamayoz work in Rural Damascus especially in East Ghota from June 2018 to December 2019,many needs have been addressed in education perspective, children in Elementary education need more support as 65% of students in this stage are out of school comparing with 30% in the primary stage. The need to re-enroll students in school is important for both boys and girls (dropping out of school ratio is 74% among boys and 36% among girls) this high ratio among boys is due to poverty that forced boys to work in hard conditions to support their families with basic needs especially food, and sometimes due to the disability of the child because of some injuries. The percentage of elderly has noticeably increased in Rural Damascus with the increase also in the number of women house-headed families which increase the need to depend on children as a resource for income. Children not in school are at higher risk of sexual harassment,exploitation and exposure to violence,child labor,and early marriage.(AlTamayoz observation). Many schools in Rural Damascus deficit the adequate number of teachers especially in languages and information technology subjects (AlTamayoz observation). Children are often learning without textbooks and/or learning materials, in very poor learning spaces. The need to expand services in Damascus is very obvious, During AlTAmayoz work in Damascus, southern area, the number of students seeking for education services is around 30% of the total beneficiaries approaching AlTamayoz community center, noting that number of people seeking for different services is around 1000 beneficiaries per day, most of them are IDPs. Most schools in Damascus are overcrowded. More than 10 million people (5.2m men,boys/5.3m women,girls) need various forms of food assistance.Many parents have refrained from sending their children to school because of lack of food. Increased poverty levels limits positive skills development opportunities for adolescents, VT is important.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be directly implemented by Alamayoz and will be in line with HRP framework. The proposed project will assure equal access to education for boys and girls and for children from returnees community, special support will be provided to children with disabilities and those of special needs. AlTAmayoz has sustained presence in Damascus and selected locations in Rural Damascus except for Darayya where the approvals will be obtained soon. A well database and reporting system will be in place, in addition to regular meetings with staff to evaluate the achieved results towards targets, and management is informed for anyneeded correction action. Activities in Damscus, Duma and Harsta will be as an expantion to already existing activities where the need is very huge and couldn’t be met with the current activities. Newly established learning centres in Rural Damascus (Maliha, Arbin, Babella, Kafr Batna, AlZabdani, Qudsita, and Darayya) aims at expand the services to new geographic areas and new populations.Students will benefit from comprehensive package of services (non formal education sessions in addition to PSS sessions and recreational activities, school supplies, school feeding, and transportations for those who live far from the center to assure regular attendance) in addition to conducting BTL campaigns and supporting life skills training to those in need.AlTamayoz will deploy well qualified teachers and PSS providers to implement the activities, pre test will be conducted to determine the learning level of student before starting the activity, and pre and post tests will be conducted to measure the impact of the course on students learning outcome. Teacher will be provided with capacity building training regarding the active learning, PSS, classroom management, … to assure quality of services . Monitoring activities are:consultation with communities, feedback mechanisms, pre and post tests for learning courses and life skills trainings.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk:lack of well qualified staff in the area of implementation due to the lack of education opportunities during the past years Likelihood: high. impact: lowMitigating measure: capacity building for some people from the implementation area, and deployment of part of the staff from DamascusRisk:lack of electricity and water in the Rural areas that might affect the implementation efficiency Likelihood: high. impact: medium Mitigating measure: support centers with generator or led lamb, support centers with tank for waterRisk of staff refusal to work in far areas from their homes Likelihood: high. impact: high Mitigating measure: support transportation for staff (rent a bus to take the staff from Damascus and return them back according to agreed-upon working hours)Coordination:Project will be well coordinated with other organizations working in the same area through sharing information and provide complimentary services. coordination with other actors in the field like child protection actors (referral pathway to refer cases observed while providing education or PSS services) health actors, nutrition actors (allow Nutrition volunteers to perform screening and refer cases to specialized nutrition services,..) Accountability: Many focus groups discussion were conducted during planning phase included (women, adolescents, boys and girls children), interviews were also made in different areas in Rrural Damascus.all information collected were used to design the project frame work and to select the most needed activities and locations.During implementation, a tracking method will be in place to assure proper implementation through surveys and another type of focus group discussions.beneficiaries will have access to give feedback on the services provided, A complain box will be in each center, all complains will be followed up by the general manager to take the proper action,project will assure equity and encourage people with disabilities to be part of it
