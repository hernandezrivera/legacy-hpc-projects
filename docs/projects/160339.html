Project Summary

WASH services in these ten geographical areas or counties mentioned here are severely affected as result of the five years devastated conflict and this has contributed not only to disease outbreak, but also to increased malnutrition, food insecurity and increased gender-based violence among women and girls and other vulnerable groups. There is still inadequate of improved sanitation and limited knowledge of hygiene practices specifically for those communities displaced by current conflicts and the host communities and to mitigate against negative WASH impact in the above-mentioned areas
This project is to reduce morbidity, mortality, and suffering from protection threats and incidences of most vulnerable in severity level counties. Ensure safe, equitable and dignified access to critical cross-sectoral basic services to enable populations meet their basic needs in Akobo,Fangak,Pigi,Ulang,Uror ,Nyirol,Maiwut,Nasir,Leer and Juba POCs. If this intervention is implemented will enable target population of vulnerable people to recover from crisis, seek solutions to displacement, and build resilience to acute shocks and chronic stresses through targeted programming to support coping capacities and livelihoods in prioritized locations mentioned in this proposal.
The proposed activities include; WASH integrated interventions starting from the OTP/SC will target the couple mother/caretaker – malnourished children until the household level aiming to break the vicious circle of diarrheal diseases/nematodes with malnutrition, addressing also small stock open defecation affecting the young children. Provide comprehensive water points management for cattle and small stock requirement during the dry season, with promotion of appropriate hygiene practices to prevent animal open defecation down to the household level. WASH will work closely with Health to ensure lifesaving engineering for outbreak control (cholera/AWD, typhoid, HEV, EVD etc.) by transmission contexts (water-borne, intradisciplinary, school, market, funerals, health facilities upon specific demands) upon epidemiological investigations.
Nile Hope will provide WASH temporary and sustainable services will address the vital needs, public health and protection issues through an AAP community approach to target the acute vulnerabilities based on inter/cross-sectoral evaluations and analyses. WASH specific activities for GBV mitigation in and outside PoC/settlement will be based on after protection and need analysis conducted by female staff with women, girls and children for WASH activities adequately planned with the community input. The proposed geographical areas Nile Hope as already established a present with ongoing activities and will have added advantage
Nile Hope has already established a presence with well qualified local staff to ensure timely delivery of services. The targeted groups/ community women, children, boys, girls and men including IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable population Nile Hope will continue maintaining good working relationship with other cluster members, Education, Nutrition, health, food security/Livelihood and protection. This will include increased participation WASH coordination meetings as well various levels. Nile Hope will place more emphasis on sanitation and hygiene
