Project Summary

This project is aimed at providing early recovery opportunities for Bui and Boyo population affected by Anglophone crisis. This will be done through trainings on apiculture, agriculture, entrepreneurship, mushroom cultivation, ankara [traditional embroidery]. These trained persons will be supported with start-up materials in their various domain to start over. These person have been rendered homeless, displaced and have lost their sources of livelihood. It will involve transformation of wheat, corn and cassava flour to locally consumed food. This project target bee farmers, women and youths who are displaced to Bamenda and those based in Bui and Boyo Divisions.

Besoins / Needs

In Bui and Boyo Divisions of Cameroon many people have been displaced to other areas due to the Anglophone crisis. Those who are based in these areas or displaced to Bamenda lost their jobs and livelihood opportunities. They are poor, unemployed and lack shelter. Many have had their homes burnt down or flee because of conflict. They need early recovery opportunities in Boyo and Bui Division. We will also extend our activities to Mezam Division because this is where many persons from Bui and Boyo have found refuge. Our project will work on vegetable gardening, micro business development for women, craft from rafia bamboo, transformation of corn, wheat and cassava flour to various consumable food and traditional embroidery [ankara] which is the recycling of plastics and other used items to fashionable or decorative products.
