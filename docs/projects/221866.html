Project Summary

Education cluster estimated 1.6 million girls and boys between 3-17 years of age as in need of education services and teaching personnel needed to support provision of education to these learners aligning with South Sudan Education Cluster Strategy 2023-2025 for providing protective and inclusive emergency education services for children and youth affected by crisis
The impact of climate change, communal violence, flooding, economic crisis, insecurity and displacement across the country has severely disrupted the education system and impacted the school aged children. The adolescent boys and young men being vulnerable and most affected are the adolescent girls and exposed to increasing protection risks and are subjected to early forced marriage as well as Sexual Exploitation and Abuse.
Approximate of 42% girls and boys between ages 3- 17 years in conflict- and crisis-affected areas are estimated to have inadequate or no access to education in 2024 to 2025 including teachers and members of school management committees requiring humanitarian assistance. The target learners in need being approximately 650,000 children 3-17 years old boys and girls, teachers and SMC members and PTAs in areas affected by conflict, displacement or facing acute food insecurity levels including the project HRP2025 focused counties are supposed to be reached with various education services.
The closure of these schools due to the mentioned drivers not only interrupted educational progresses, but also affected and hindered children’s access to essential basic needs and services including school feeding, health information on disease prevention, protection and access to clean water and sanitation services.
The current estimated number of out of school children is 1.6 million with child protection cases increasing as well as the number of pregnant learners increasing in some schools. The schools are the key institutions to identify and refer children requiring protective services and case management to mitigate gender base violence threats.
Most schools in the project focused counties Aweil South, Tonj North and Twic has no strong referral mechanism in place and Insecurity is a significant challenge to school functionality. In 2025 HRP project focused counties Aweil South, Tonj North and Twic active conflict and recurring communal violence led to school closures.

1. Needs

Due to the floods in prone areas and conflict affected counties such Aweil South County in Northern and Bahr el Ghazal states disrupted education infrastructure especially the WASH facilities at schools affecting reproductive age school girls who are more likely than boys to be excluded from education.Climate change impact, conflict, economic shocks and flooding has affected schools and damaged school infrastructure which made schools inaccessible especially for the most disadvantaged.The increasing climate change, food insecurity, economic shocks and localized conflict exacerbate existing barriers, including inadequate school infrastructure, costs associated with accessing quality education, untrained education teachers and irregular and insufficient teacher payments. These barriers include lack of regular teacher salaries, damaged or non-existent school infrastructure, learners’ inability to pay costs associated with schools, hunger, significant household responsibilities and marriage and pregnancy. Teachers and members of school management dis aggregated by gender committees are in need of humanitarian assistance. Lack of trained PTA and SMC to manage school development plans as well as mobilizing learners to go to schools is among the priority needs affecting learning. Lack of qualified education authorities at state and county levels to enforce learning outcomes in schools, Lack of trained teachers in schools which is a priority need for effective learning.The learners both male and female will be reached through access to education, provision of school supplies and other services while teachers female learners benefiting from trainings such as pedagogy and subject specific pedagogy, life-skills and curriculum and materials, ECD trainings and PTA/SMC members from among the community education committees and local education officials will be trained in school management. School going children girls and boys between 3 and 17 years of age in conflict and crisis affected areas including the project focused counties Wau and Aweil South and in western and Northern Bahr el Ghazal states are estimated to not have access to per-school, primary and secondary education in 2025. Aid Support Community Organization has its geographical presence in Western and Northern Bahr el Ghazal states implementing education program in Wau and Aweil South Counties.The project will prevent and address Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, with a PSEA communication strategy and appropriate complaints mechanism. The HRP 2025 project will have ZERO tolerance on protection of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse based on Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA policy.

2. Project activities

The project will scale up the response in the focused counties to meet the priority needs of targeting 3000 beneficiaries male and female learners, the vulnerable host Communities, returnees, and displaced children by conflicts mostly constitute of the number of school aged children boys and girls including adolescents aged 3-17 with access to education in emergency as well as build their resilience and the number of preschool children aged 3-6 with access to play and early childhood education shall be given priority.The project will consider and navigate the activities across all three sect-oral objectives for 2025 targeting 3000 children ages 3 and 17 boys and girls as beneficiaries where most of schools in the focused counties were destroyed, damaged, closed and children being forced out of school based on different drivers. Objective one: Improve equitable access to safe and. protective education services for 670000 emergency affected IDPs, returnees, spontaneous refugees, and host community children including children with disabilities and youth girls and boys.The following are the activities that project will work on under cluster objective one• The project will ensure to establish temporary learning spaces and classrooms• Rehabilitation of temporary learning spaces and classrooms.• Establish gender segregated latrines, water points in schools.• Distribute or provide sanitation and hygiene materials especially hand washing soap, sanitizers, masks, etc. and PPE for students and teachers in schools Objective two: Improve the quality of gender and disability-inclusive education services and teachers' capacity to protect the well-being of all children and prevent the negative impact of the emergencies on learning environments.• The project will provide teaching and learning materials for ECD, primary, and secondary school education.• Training and update teachers and learners on PSS, CP, and referral mechanisms including child protection as well as Gender Base Violence, health and nutrition.• .Provide life-skills and basic vocational training to youth and adolescents. Objective three .Strengthen or enhance the education system’s response capacity of the school community and national/local education actors to mitigate the impact of existing emergency on learners, teachers, and communities.• Train SMCs, PTAs and education authorities on school management, governance, psycho social support PSS and Disaster Risk Reduction climate change..• Support prevention and mitigation of communicable diseases through training school communities by providing appropriate Information and Education and Communication IEC materials i.e. posters, leaflets.• Support technical and institutional capacities of national actors, including finance, procurement, HR, MEAL, governance and other areas.• Support conducting back to learning campaigns.
