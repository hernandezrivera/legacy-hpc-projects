Project Summary

The proposed integrated child protection and gender based violence interventions will address the most urgent and critical protection issues in Galgadud region focusing primarily on the internally displaced population as well as host communities. PDA will ensure to intervene on timely, effective and holistic manner to the protection needs of mainly children and women of the displaced communities in the target regions. The project will address both child right violations and gender based violation through existing inter-agency referral mechanism. PDA will runs community food security intervention in these areas; this project will be integrated in the existing livelihood projects so that it strengthen both protection and livelihood service provision. Mobile CPIE and SGBV activities will be undertaken in response to any new displacement and needs including when there is new displacement or influx. PDA has strong and solid outreach protection and livelihood programming and this project will fit into that modality of programming. The project will significantly strengthen and significantly scale up the on-going case management response services in targeted communities. Specifically, the project will response to by identifying and responding the needs of the SGBV survivor; separated and unaccompanied children (UASC), The project will invest in building the capacity of the both protection on provision of psychological first aid to the highly psychological distress community members as part of the first line emergency response; UASC will be provided interim community based care arrangement and foster care. The project aims to provide immediate life-saving assistance to vulnerable populations. Beneficiaries will receive comprehensive case management and psycho-social support (PSS), including emergency care, basic emotional support, and follow up on the services. Capacity building of duty bearers including formal and informal authorities to strengthen and improve identification. Mainstreaming child protection to other service providers to ensure compassionate, confidential, and competent action for children in emergencies.

Needs

Majority of the affected population in needs are women, girls and boys with a considerable proportion of households headed by women. Individuals with specific needs (i.e. serious health conditions, disability) and their families are at heightened risk of destitution, exclusion, and abuse, including extortion. Populations in areas affected by previous droughts, displaced communities and local residents may face also risks of violence and neglect as a result of their socio economic destitution and the insufficient services, in particular in areas where communities host a proportionally high number of IDPs.This is coupled with clan and ethnic groups which remains dominant of Somali society with comparatively less social capital, resulting in reduced clan-based protection. Armed conflicts and violence, as well as recent droughts disproportionately put marginalized social groups at heightened protection risk. The protection crisis in Somalia continues to be characterized by a multi-faceted armed conflict and inter-communal violence. It is also compounded by natural disasters, including droughts and flood, as well as governance challenges, weakened resilience and economic vulnerability of families and communities. Both protracted and new displacement have resulted in undignified living circumstances with severe protection implications for affected men, women, and childrenA total of 2.5 M people in need for the protection of which 37% are children <18 yeas,50% adult age between 18-59 years and 13% elderly person >59 years based on figures for population in situation of food insecurity (IPC 2 to 5 for IDPs, and IPC 3 to 5 for host populations) and in line with the assumption that food insecurity is also an indicator pointing to exposure to key protection concerns, such as insecurity/lack of freedom of movement, socio-economic destitution and discrimination. Geographical prioritization is based on data relating to food insecurity, malnutrition and child recruitment

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The protection risk related to exclusion of the vulnerable population upon displacement in IDPs settlement are, separation of families members, destruction of community asset, militarization of the health and other social amenities through displacement of the vulnerable people in needs, undocumented vulnerable people and poor information sharing, lack of coordination as well as increase sexual exploitation among the girls and women. In mitigation to the above problem PDA will be able to improve community and IDPs leaders consultation and communication on prior displacement of these vulnerable population hence this will alleviate the grave loss of the people properties as well as have alternative means to move, it shall also ensure that quick IDPs assessment are done whenever, such eventualities come to any existing IDPs with its project location. PDA will also ensure proper information sharing and timing of session with women and children in the displaced camps to enable capture the vital information for better programming within the target areas.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Prevention of risk associated with exclusion on gender and age are, lack of consultation among the adolescent girls, exclusion of special group in decision making process in the IDPs might hinder proper advocacy and awareness among the disable, minority and marginalized population in the Idps and host communities hence poor diversity within the communities. It is also highly luckily that women and girls will be excluded from the various employment and education opportunities within the camps hence this increase the disparities within the protection coordination cluster.On mitigation PDA shall propose to have equal men, women, boys and girls in both services centered activities as well as employment within the target/operational areas, Also on the issue of decision making PDA shall route for proper representation of all interested group within the society such as disable, minority and marginalized as well as elderly and single mothers in these targeted camps.it will also provide proper coordination mechanism that will enable the IDPs leaders and project team work in harmoniously for preservation of beneficiary data and without any alteration of their information to avoid any risk of discrimination among the beneficiaries.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

In the refugee camps the following might be some of the main risk facing the target population that may negatively impact the benefices in the camps these include; poor IDPs settlement plan, high cases of GBV, poor data protection, extremist among the youth,as well as military fire, explosion remnant of war and lack or rather limited information sharing and feedback of compliant mechanism in place to ensure safety and dignity of the vulnerable women,girls,boys and men in the violence areas. This is coupled with separation and sexual exploitation as well as undocumented needs of the vulnerable group.PDA will increase social mobilization and awareness aspect within the camps as well as train the community gatekeepers on the proper reporting of these verse hence improve coordination between the community leaders, staff and the local authority for prosecution and cultural solution for the such cases within the camps. However, in the IDPs set up there are extremist violence youth who terrorize vulnerable people hence create community tension as well as causing disharmony among the affected population this will however, be handle through youth awareness creation on their safety and dignity to shun away from engaging from such violence and as well focus their future PDA will accelerate the community active engagement with youth in the IDPs and as well create a better avenue for them to get empowered.To mitigate this PDA , intends to carry out properly IDPs leaders consultation and involvement of all stakeholders in ensuring the IDPs layout is of one that will ensure easy access to all beneficiary services within the camps, PDA will trained community leaders in the IDPs on simple surveying of the camps as well as management details of these IDPs hence improve the public relation on the target location. To prevent the rampant and attended sexual exploitation among the vulnerable girls and women in the target communities.
