Project Summary

Mundri east and West counties in western equatoria state has registered increasing cases
of malnutrition across all demographic cadres in the 2019 nutrition intervention as compared to previous
years thanks to the scale up efforts aimed at improving coverage.There is need for scale up of
nutrition services within the region so as to be able to reach a majority of the population as well as improve on the quality of services being delivered. ACEM is currently operating around 60% coverage within both counties .
Household food insecurity remains a challenge driven by heavy reliance on unpredictable rainfall which affected crop production coupled with the limited accessibility to land for cultivation owing to the prevailing insecurity in some payams.The current economic crisis that has greatly affected overall household income hence low household purchasing power.
According to round 23 FSNMS survey,the food security and nutrition situation is still below acceptable levels. The 2019 programmatic data indicates an increase in the number of beneficiaries compared to what was targeted.
In line with the nutrition cluster priorities,this intervention will seek to improve the nutrition
situation and prevent malnutrition related mortality through scale up and provision quality
life-saving management of acute malnutrition for the most vulnerable and at-risk under
five children and pregnant and lactating women,increased access to maternal infant and
young child nutrition (preventative services) and increased access to integrated nutrition
services in mundri east and mundri west counties.The project will target children under
five years and pregnant and lactating women as the most vulnerable groups. ACEM will
also consider beneficiaries with TB and HIV/AIDS and nodding disease that lie within the
admission criteria.
ACEM will deliver preventative nutrition services in line with cluster objective 1 involving maternal infant and young child nutrition (MIYCN).Pregnant and lactating women and caretakers of children 0-23 months will be targeted through counselling and behaviour change communication messaging. MIYCN counselling will target 2969 pregnant women,and 15,289 caretakers of children 0-23 months . 30,809 children under 5 years will be targeted with vitamin A supplementation and deworming
Live saving nutrition services which involves early detection and treatment of girls and boys under five years of age and pregnant and lactating women affected by acute malnutrition will target 1300 boys and 1400 girls for SAM and 2750 boys and 2956 girls for MAM . The intervention will also see 2969 PLWs treated for malnutrition.All this will be done through an integrated approach with other sectors of health,WASH,FSL and gender and protection
There will also be an aspect of promoting community participation through creation of a robust complaints and feedback mechanism within all levels of implementation.Community members will also be directly involved in planning and execution of nutrition programs and all other stages of the project ccle,ACEM will also continue to work hand in hand with the state ministry of Amadi state in order to ensure full accountability to the affected population

1 - Needs

Malnutrition among the most vulnerable groups (women,children) in Mundri east and mundri west counties of western equatoria state still remains a challenge. The prevailing malnutrition rates are attributed to a number of factors ranging from those that affect food availability,accessibility and utilization.The communities in mundri east and mundri west counties are still coming into terms with the violence that changed the entire context of the once bread basket of South Sudan, after populations were displaced,livelihoods destroyed and insecurity crept in.Agriculture has been the main source of livelihood over time and has been greatly affected over the years as there has been limited access to cultivation land due to insecurity as well as displacement of the cultivating population. Unpredictable rainfall patterns have also affected crop production and hence household food insecurity.The ongoing economic downturn,have significantly affected households’ income thus reducing the purchasing power which has in turn contributed negatively to the food security situation at the household level.Poor utilization of food brought about by ailments such as malaria,diarrhea diseases due to poor WASH practices has led to an increased prevalence of malnutrition in children under 5 years. Insufficient caring and feeding practices coupled by poor food quality with limited dietary diversity among children 0-2yrs has led to an increase prevalence in malnutrition in the age category.The IPC report issues in february 2019 (Jan-July) classified the food security situation in mundri to be under IPC phase 3 with an alert nutrition situation despite lower GAM rates of slightly under 6%.This means there is need for an integrated nutrition response in mundri that will help solve the malnutrition problem by improving access to health care services,reducing morbidity especially among the under 5s,improving diets consumption , care and feeding practices and improved hygiene and sanitation. As such ,this project will aim at ensuring that there is reduced prevalence of malnutrition among children under 5 years and pregnant and lactating mothers through an integrated approach. In this project, 2,700 children under 5 years will be targeted for treatment of SAM( 1500 in mundri east,1200 in mundri west), while 3835 U5s will be targeted for MAM in mundri east and 1871 in mundri west. The project will also address nutritional needs for 1507 PLWs In Mundri East and 1462 PLWs in Mundri west county. The preventative package (MIYCN) will target 10,220 caretakers of children 0-23 months through counselling on best MIYCN practices as well as BCC messaging in Mundri east and 10,100 in Mundri west county. Vitamin A and deworming campaign will have 20,709 children under 5 years in Mundri east and 10,100 children in mundri west county targeted for the two rounds of campaignACEM will also ensure that protection and gender needs of the target populations are met during implementation through gender and protection mainstreaming strategies.Linkages to other programs such as FSL,WASH,Health, will be given priority as well.Special groups such as people with disabilities and those living with HIV aids will be put into consideration.Men,women ,boys and girls living with HIV/AIDS and TB in Mundri east and Mundri west counties experience multiple stresses trying to cope with the disease effects coupled with inadequate food,access to proper healthcare and other important services thus exposing them to risks of malnutrition.

2 - Activities

In line with the nutrition cluster priorities for 2020 ,ACEM through this project will provide live saving treatment to children under 5 years and pregnant and lactating women who are most vulnerable,and also people living with TB and HIV in mundri east and mundri west counties. Preventative services that will address immediate and underlying causes of malnutrition will be enhanced and all this will be done through an integrated approach.Various activities that will make up this project are as follows:1. Prevention of malnutritionThis will include: Counselling of caretakers of children 0-23 months on best MIYCN practices which will target 10,220 beneficiaries in mundri east and 5,069 in mundri east.Creating at least 10 mother to mother suport groups in each countyVitamin A and deworming campaigns targetting 20,709 children under 5 years in mundri east and 10,100 in mundri westCarrying out World breastfeeding week campaigns in each county to sensitize the community on importance of breastfeedingEstablishing at least 1 demonstration garden in every facilityHold cooking demonstrations to caregivers of children 0-23 months and PLWs on preparation of healthy nutritious foodsGrowth monitoring for children U5yrs2. Treatment of malnutritionAdmission and treatment of children 6-59 months identified with SAM without medical complications in OTPAdmission and treatment of children 0-59 months identified with MAM in the TSFP programAdmission and treatment of malnourished children with medical complication in SCAdmission and treatment of Pregnant and lactating mothers with MUAC <23cm in TSFP programScreening for malnutrition in children and PLWs at facility and community levelConducting monthly community mobilization and active case finding within the catchment area of every facility ,TSFP/OTP siteTimely collection and sharing of program data to the cluster and relevant stakeholdersEnsuring program meets the set sphere standards (>75% cure rate,<15% defaulter rate and <10% death rate ) as treatment outcomesAccurate and consistent Reporting on funding secured through theestablished FTS system by OCHA3.Integration of nutrition into other sectors at nutrition sites i.eHealth: Distribution of LLTNs to OTP beneficiaries,Growth monitoring, Vitamin A and deworming campaignsWASH: Hand washing facilities established in all nutrition sites,Distribution of hand washing soap to OTP and TSFP beneficiaries,Counselling on best hygiene practices for caretakers under MIYCN packageFSL: Establish at least 1 demonstration garden at every OTP site,support in establishment of Kitchen gardens at the household level,Linkage of beneficiaries in the nutrition program to the existing livelihood programs at community levelProtection: conduct safety audits for all nutrition sites,develop referral mechanisms for GBVEducation: ECD kits distributed in all nutrition sites,

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

Yes- The project caters for all vulnerable groups including displaced populations .They will be able to access nutrition services just like any other group .Assessments will be carried out to determine the nutritional needs after which the appropriate support will be given which will include but not limited to distribution of emergency food ration (BP 5 and high energy biscuits) ,treatment of malnourished children and PLWs ,and also provision of IYCF-E support

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

Yes. This projects is actually build on an integrated approach which also focuses on establishing durable solutions .As such,beneficiaries in this projects will be linked to already existing structures and systems of government and at the community level.For instance ,linkages will be created for households with children treated for malnutrition to be able to get incorporated into existing livelihood projects within mundri as a way of empowering households to become food secure .

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

Yes,The national ministry of health is committed to providing affordable and well distributed integrated healthcare services through the Boma health initiative under the minimum basic healthcare package.This project is designed in such a way that it incorporates some of aspects through an integrated approach.For instance, this project intends to deliver Vitamin A and deworming campaigns to children less than 5 years,will also support growth monitoring activities, create linkages between nutrition and health care services etc

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

No,Mundri has not yet attained a kind of environment that would be termed as 'sufficiently stable"with regards to protection.Some areas especially in parts of Mundri East are relatively insecure especially with the on and off tension between the government,NAS rebels and IO rebels in the same locality.There has been increased insecurity incidents in the recent past where movement along mundri Juba road has been made difficult by gangs who hijack loot and burn vehicles along the road.The population in mundri are hence exposed to protection risks which in turn hinder access to services,such as health,nutrition ,WASH,food security ,education,etc Women and children are most vulnerable .Women travel long distances in search for basic services ,they are also predisposed to protection related risks most especially GBVWomen’s increasing role as a breadwinners, is a contributing factor to domestic violence in Mundri

6.4 - Does the project include sustainability/handover?

Yes,This project will have much of government involvement in all aspects of the implementation and most specifically the monitoring part whereby they will have an opportunity to determine what remains in order to achieve durable solutions with regards to nutrition implementation.Representatives of Government ministry of health at state level will participate in Project Steering Committee meetings, which review project progress, and also inform the design and adaptation of ongoing project activities. They will also participate in quarterly monitoring missions to nutrition sites that will help to influence planning.The indicators herein are also sensitive to the local contextFSNMS survey results and programmatic data will be shared regularly so as to help inform decisions of possibilities of hand over which will be based on having achieved an acceptable GAM of below 5% and that there will be no aggravating factors likely to cause a deterioration of the nutrition situation in mundri
