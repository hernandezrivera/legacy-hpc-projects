Project Summary

The proposed project aims at providing lifesaving and life-sustaining interventions which include the provision multi-purpose cash transfers. The project also proposes recovery interventions that aims to increase community resilience through improved agricultural production (livestock) through provision of seasonal livelihood inputs, creation & protection of livelihood assets.
Program targets the most vulnerable people that had recently displaced because of the conflicts in Guricel and other neighboring locations in Dhusamareb district as well as the droughts resulting from the sporadic rainfall of the Gu’ seasonal rainfall.
Overall, 400 HHs composed of 2400 persons (200 men, 200 women, 800 boys, and 1200 girls) will be supported through unconditional cash transfer and protecting/restoring the livelihoods related to food and income sources through conditional Cash transfers.
The food security project works in closely with stakeholders from other humanitarian organizations, local authorities and both the public and private sector to promote the cash programming support.
KAAH Relief and Development Organization will closely work with the community elders, local authorities, women and youth groups and involve them in decision making process. The organization will train and equip, Program Management Committee (PMC) nominated from the community to discuss implementation strategies, selection and targeting of beneficiary approaches with the predetermined selection criterion and provide oversight to the program implementation.
KAAH has mutual agreement with the telecommunication companies for money vending services to transfer cash safely and transparently to beneficiaries and thus ensuring dignified access to assistance.
Improved agricultural production is aimed to improve food security among the rural agro-pastoral communities inconsideration with seasonality. The community water harvesting structures rehabilitated increases water availability during the dry season. The program targets the hard to access communities to meet their basic dietary needs and non-food items.
KAAH localization approach in the program operational areas will ensure complementing of programs supported by our donors in the same geographical areas. To ensure food security and delivery of other complementary services within the program, the targeted households will receive awareness raising and sensitization on sustenance, good nutrition practices, safe and health food preparation, access to diversified food supplements, good water, sanitation and hygiene practices.
The provision of the life-sustaining assistance and the restoration of the communal assets is aimed to increase access to food among the food-insecure disaster affected households and improve household income generation through improved agro-pastoral production. This will help in building the community resilience throughout shocks and making informed decisions on protecting the environmental resources at their disposal.
Implementation of the cash transfer programme (CTP) will ensure consideration of agricultural seasonality and will also ensure market awareness to avoid disrupting local market through Market Based Programming (MBP). In general, the program aims to contribute to the national developmental plan 2020-2024.
KAAH organization constantly conducts assessments to identify poor households for recovery and resilience programs. The identified households are registered and supported with food security programs.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1: Provision of unconditional cash transfer to 400 HHs (2400 individuals including 200 men, 200 women, 800 boys, and 1200 girls) are selected and supported. Mainly targeting with vulnerability criteria including women, aged people, disable people, female headed HHs, lactating women, GBV survivors, and females with special needs. Activity 2:400 HHs (2400 individuals including 200 men, 200 women, 800 boys, and 1200 girls) from Dhusamareb who are food insecure are targeted with conditional cash support to meet the minimum Kcal requirements of each HHs and to support communal productive assets. They are engaged in cash for work initiatives for restoration and creation of 10 communal assets. The targeted beneficiary HHs for the activity include vulnerable households, minorities, marginalized groups, women headed households. The selection and consultation of the beneficiaries will be done in collaboration with respective committees and local authorities. However, KAAH will conduct independent verifications to ensure proper the needy people are met.Activity 3:Cash for work tools - 400 kits of Cash for wok kits will be procured and distributed to the CFW beneficiaries to help them undertake the CFW schemes. Materials will be procured locally in consultation with community committees and respective authorities and will be distributed to respective beneficiaries. Components of the kits are forks, shovels, wheelbarrows, embody sacks and borers, however this might change depending on community priorities and their preferences. Prior to the procurement, KAAH will conduct community consultations to identify the most appropriate cash for works tools that fit community priorities and particularly items that can be used for selected schemes. Anticipated communal assets include feeder water catchment rehabilitation, desalting of water sources, and supporting farmers on cultivation and cleaning.Activity 4: Provision of agricultural inputs to small farmers (seeds, and GAP training). This activity promotes rural agricultural production with the utilization of harvested water in the restored community assets such as water catchments, and Berkads through muti-storey and kitchen gardening. The activity aims to provide Agricultural inputs, forage seeds distribution, and GAP training of improved agricultural techniques for 200 households in Dhusamareb district.Activity 5: Provision of fodder for pastoral communities in Dhusamareb district of Galgaduud region. The fodder distribution is aimed to support 400 households and sustain their livestock during the dry season.Activity 6: Post Distribution Monitoring: KAAH will conduct a minimum of two PDMs to assess the performance of the cash for work and cash relief carried out two weeks after the first cycle and two weeks after the last cycle of the cash disbursement. 2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash transfer ensures accountable, dignified assistance and ensures that beneficiaries are not subjected to harm in anyway. The cash transfer is made through money vending telecom companies which are secure and helps to prevent the spread of Covid19 reducing contact as part of the health guidelines provided for social distancing.Cash transfer encourages multi-purpose usage of the funds received at household level, and supports family’s needs all round. However, the primary objective is to ensure food accessibility for the families, but the MPC often support the buying of nutritious and nourishing foods. On community specific preference, multipurpose cash transfer is priority compared to in-kind assistance.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

The project aims to increase food access and food security through improved agricultural production (livestock). The project is designed to revive the economic sector of the community by improving the living standards of the communities targeted. The project will help restore the community assets such as the water conserving structures that will help develop the agro-pastoral community during the extended dry season. The created/ restored community assets support the community productivity and increases marketing of their products, hence increasing their coping mechanisms.Sustained productivity helps the market functionality throughout the year and prolonged economic development of the community.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

4.1. Minority clans/ethnic groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance.- KAAH Proposed project will prevent risk of exclusion from humanitarian assistance for the most vulnerable, minority and marginalized groups. The organization will ensure access to assistance irrespective of ethnic, religion, political affiliation, gender, and status. The community participation in decision making and representation is ensured through the establishment of project committee. The committee is used for identification, targeting and selection of deservingly vulnerable households with the assistance of predetermined selection criterion. The committee is composed of the targeted community including women, minority, and marginalized groups with leadership position open to all. KAAH will ensure that all stakeholders including the local elders, and local authority are engaged in the implementation particularly during the project initiation. The community is involved in the planning and design of the proposed project. community involvement increases transparency and coordinates information to reduce duplication of resources, fraud, and corruption. Posters and visibility boards are established at the project sites while KAAH project personnel conducts regular community sensitization on selection criteria, entitlements, project specifics. Existence of complaint response mechanism (CRM) is informed to the community and are encouraged to call the call-centers and hot-line numbers provided. 4.2. Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance-To prevent any form of sexual exploitation and abuse, KAAH will enforce the organizational policies. - All personnel and affiliates of the organization are asked to sign the organizational PSEA policy and the code of conduct.
