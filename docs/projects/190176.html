Project Summary

The project is intended to respond to the current humanitarian crises aiming to restablish the availability and access to health care system in camps and resettlement areas and retaken areas. By combining static heath facilities and integrated mobile brigades, the project will prevent disease, respond to seasonal outbreaks

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project is aimed at using a combination of static health facilities, integrated mobile brigades, community health promotion, and mass vaccination. Emergency response through these facilities encompass health workers, medicines, medical supplies, measure to cover increased caseloads and disease surveillance. Patients with chronic diseases and outbreaks like cholera, measles, COVID-19 will be pursued.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The integrated primary healthcare package will be preferred, with emphasis on high impact interventions like:• Promote vaccination of children, • Promote timely treatment of the most common causes of morbidity and mortality, and antenatal care and skilled birth attendance. • Promote sexual and reproductive health to f specific sections of the population like women, children, adolescents, people with disabilities, survivors of GBV, HIV and TB. • Promote mental health and psychosocial support to vulnerable persons.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A conflict-sensitivity assessment will be carried out previously to the implementation phase in order to avoid inflicting harm through the action. In order to decrease ground for tensions and conflicts, both, host and IDP community will be participating in the project activites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project was designed with women’s groups targetd by Wiwanana in all its womens safe spaces across 7 districts of south Cabo Delgado. The earlier experience proved good practices of projects such as KIMICHI funded by KOICA and UNFPA. Hence, the foundation was inspired to draft this project to scale it up with other cooperation partners, with necessary adjustments, that meet specific terms of reference. The CP will work with the community engagement and accountability to affected population (CE/AAP) working group towards adopting standardized tools and jointly implementing strategies. Needs assessments will be conducted jointly with community leaders and key focal persons representing specific groups like women, adolescents and the elderly, and will apply methods like direct observation, key informant interviews and focus group discussions. Their views and recommendations will be included in the project design and implementation. At the local level, project and facility management committees will include community representatives. Healthcare workers will be recruited locally and from among the target population, when they meet required qualifications. More women will be encouraged to take up roles. Complaints will be monitored at service delivery points on a regular basis using complaints boxes and patient exit interviews and through a common feedback mechanism. Project results will be shared and client satisfaction assessment will be established and recommendations will inform best practices.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

It is estimated that Cabo Delgado has 609.000 peole with disability which is translated at 5.300 in each of 17 districts. Therefore, the CP will work with the SDSMAS to access and get updated records of type and number of disable people, their status of access to compensation tools and come up with a plan of action to improve their access to such means to enable them to live worthy live and contribute to the inclusion of of people with disability in the development plan of the communities where they belong and influence their life perspectives. Disaggregation by age and sex will be recorded to that all vulnerable people can access to tools and exercise their rights under this project.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Community health intervention can disturb the environment through mobile brigades, distribution of IEC awareness material, music and mobile radio. However, the CP will make sure that mitigation measures are in place to reduce the use of paper, plastic bags, plastic bottle, control the proliferation of syringes. Therefore, garbage bins will be allocated wherever the mobile brigade operates. Noise pollution will be moderated to prevention mental disturbance of the people. Therefore, the CP will hold the staff and beneficiaries to adopt measures to counter environment degradation occurring as direct result of the project
