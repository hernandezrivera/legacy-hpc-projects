Project Summary

WFP, as the lead agency for Logistics Sector, aims to oversee all the strategic objectives from the sector, namely: 1. provide logistics services (inclusive of surface transportation, transhipment, and warehousing) to humanitarian organisations responding to the Syria crisis. 2. maintain regional WoS interagency logistics coordination and information management in order to support humanitarian. 3. enhance capacity of humanitarian actors via dedicated logistics trainings and purchase of necessary equipments.

Needs

Key needs identified in consultation with partners include common transport and common warehousing across Syria, in order to provide a predictable and secure logistics supply chain in the midst of access and security constraints. The increasing trend of cross-border operations into Syria from Iraq and Turkey necessitates the extension of coordination activities and expansion of logistics hubs where required. Scarcity and rising costs of fuel require the maintenance of contingency fuel reserves, which will be especially critical during the winter months for emergency cooking and heating In line with partners’ needs to enhance logistics capacity, dedicated trainings will be continued in 2019, focusing on more diverse areas within logistics.

Implementation of activities

The Logistics sector will maintain common logistics services by making sure that warehouse contracts for the different locations are in place for 2019 in order to maintain sufficient storage capacity inside Syria, coordinating inter-agency humanitarian deliveries to newly accessible areas by communicating with the different actors for the process to go smoothly. The Logistics sector will also coordinate cross-border transhipment operations by setting contracts with the hub owners and taking all the necessary security measures so that the operations will not be interrupted. In addition to that, the Logistics sector will continue to organise meetings in different locations whether in Syria or in Turkey to better answer to the different needs of the organisations and provide partners with updates information on regular basis. While remaining focused on filling logistics gaps faced by the programmatic sectors in the Syria response, the Logistics sector will also bring subject matter experts to conduct trainings across Whole of Syria in order to enhance the capacities of humanitarian actors, enabling them to better implement sectoral response plans.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Logistics coordination remains pivotal in ensuring needs continue to be met while simultaneously maintaining capacity and resources to address any emerging issues. The main risk the Logistics sector might face is losing access. In order to mitigate that risk, the sector will continue to advocate for better access, explore different routes and assess the available options to reach different locations. In addition to that, the Logistics sector has long term agreements with different transport companies and contracts which allows better adaptability in case of any sudden change in the situation on the ground.
