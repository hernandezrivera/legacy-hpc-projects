Project Summary

The ClimaSmart Nutrition project in Lafon aims to enhance food security and nutrition through a multi-faceted approach that integrates climate-smart agriculture, nutrition-sensitive interventions, and education. This project aligns with the broader priorities of South Sudan's Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan (HNRP) for 2025, which emphasizes addressing immediate nutritional needs while building resilience against climate change impacts.
Priorities of Nutrition in the HNRP for South Sudan
The HNRP for South Sudan outlines several key priorities that resonate with the objectives of the ClimaSmart Nutrition project:
Addressing Micronutrient Deficiencies: The HNRP emphasizes the importance of providing nutritional supplements, particularly to vulnerable groups such as children under five and pregnant or lactating women. This aligns with the project's goal of improving child health outcomes through micronutrient supplementation.
• Agro-Processing Initiatives: Increasing the availability and accessibility of nutritious foods is a priority. The project’s focus on agro-processing supports this by enhancing local food systems, which is critical in areas like Lafon where access to diverse foods can be limited.
• Nutrition Education Programs: Educating communities about optimal dietary practices is crucial for improving nutrition outcomes. The HNRP supports initiatives that promote better feeding behaviors, which is reflected in the project's emphasis on nutrition education.
• Climate-Smart Agricultural Techniques: The integration of climate-smart practices is essential for building resilience in agricultural systems. The HNRP highlights the need for adaptive strategies to mitigate the impacts of climate change on food security, aligning with the project's objective to introduce sustainable farming practices
Target Populations
The ClimaSmart Nutrition project targets several key groups, which are also focal points in the HNRP:
• Children Under 5 Years Old: This group is prioritized due to their vulnerability to malnutrition and its long-term impacts on health and development
• Pregnant and Lactating Women: Ensuring adequate nutrition for these women is crucial for maternal and child health, a priority reflected in both the project and HNRP
• Smallholder Farmers: By focusing on this group, the project aims to enhance food production resilience, which is critical given South Sudan’s reliance on agriculture
• Community Health Workers and Local Food Processors: These stakeholders play a pivotal role in implementing nutrition-sensitive interventions and ensuring food system sustainability3
Integrated Approach for Sustainable Outcomes
The ClimaSmart Nutrition project’s integrated approach addresses both immediate nutritional needs and long-term resilience:
• Immediate Nutritional Needs: By providing supplements and education, the project tackles current deficiencies and promotes healthier dietary practices.
• Long-Term Resilience: Through climate-smart agriculture, the project enhances food production systems' ability to withstand environmental changes, ensuring sustained food security.
This comprehensive strategy not only aligns with the HNRP’s goals but also positions Lafon as a model for integrating nutrition and climate adaptation efforts in South Sudan. By focusing on these priorities, the project contributes significantly to reducing malnutrition rates and building resilient communities capable of adapting to future challenges.

1. Needs

The ClimartSmart Nutrition project in Lafon, South Sudan, is designed to tackle severe food insecurity and malnutrition through a multi-faceted approach that aligns with the strategic priorities of the Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan (HNRP) for South Sudan. The project addresses several critical needs identified in Lafon County: Acute Food InsecurityLafon County is experiencing severe food insecurity, classified as IPC Phase 4 (Emergency), which indicates significant food consumption gaps, high acute malnutrition, and excess mortality. This necessitates urgent interventions to prevent further deterioration of the situation67. Malnutrition among Vulnerable GroupsChildren under five and pregnant and lactating women are particularly vulnerable to malnutrition in Lafon. The county's classification as a priority 1 area for intersectoral humanitarian response underscores the need for immediate nutrition interventions to address critical levels of acute malnutrition Climate Change Impacts on AgricultureClimate change significantly affects Lafon's agricultural productivity, with irregular rainfall, frequent droughts, and potential flooding disrupting traditional farming practices. These climate impacts are a root cause of food insecurity in South Sudan, necessitating the integration of climate-smart agricultural practices to enhance resilience Limited Access to Nutritious FoodsThere is a lack of diverse, nutritious foods in local markets due to limited market access and underdeveloped value chains. This project aims to improve dietary diversity through agro-processing initiatives and better market linkages Inadequate Nutrition Knowledge and PracticesNutrition education programs are crucial for improving dietary practices and feeding behaviors in Lafon. The integration of these programs with food assistance is essential for achieving long-term food security outcomes Weak Agricultural ResilienceThe introduction of climate-smart, nutrition-sensitive food production techniques is vital for strengthening local agricultural systems against climate shocks. This aligns with the cluster's focus on emergency agriculture interventions and resilience-building activities. Limited Capacity of Local Health SystemsStrengthening the capacity of local health systems, particularly through community health workers, is necessary for effective nutrition service delivery. This effort supports broader strategies to enhance community resilience and service provision in South Sudan. By addressing these interconnected needs, the ClimaSmart Nutrition project aims to provide a comprehensive response to food insecurity and malnutrition in Lafon. Its integrated approach combines direct nutrition interventions with climate-smart agriculture and education, aligning well with the Food Security and Livelihoods Cluster's strategy for South Sudan. This strategy emphasizes integrating immediate assistance with long-term resilience-building efforts, making the project a strong fit for the HNRP priorities in Lafon County.

2. Project activities

PrioritiesThe ClimaSmart Nutrition project in Lafon County, South Sudan, aligns with the broader humanitarian response strategy and cluster objectives, addressing immediate food security and nutrition needs while building resilience to climate change and other shocks. Here are the key response priorities and activities: Food Assistance and Nutrition Support• Unconditional Food Assistance: Provide food assistance to vulnerable populations in IPC Phase 4 (Emergency) to prevent famine and improve food consumption and dietary diversity• Emergency Nutrition Interventions: Implement targeted nutrition interventions for children under five and pregnant/lactating women to address acute malnutrition.• Kitchen Gardens: Establish kitchen gardens at nutrition sites and households to enhance access to diverse, nutritious foods Climate-Smart Agriculture• Agricultural Kits Distribution: Distribute dry season and main season agricultural kits, including vegetable, crop, fishing kits, and tools, to support emergency food production• Training in Climate-Smart Practices: Train community members in climate-smart agricultural practices such as pest management and irrigation techniques• Multi-Cropping Techniques: Introduce multi-cropping techniques to increase resilience and productivity in agricultural practices Livelihood Diversification and Resilience Building• Vocational Training: Provide market-oriented vocational training and equipment for income-generating activities to diversify livelihoods5.• Cash/Food for Assets Programs: Implement programs focused on improving market access through infrastructure development like roads and bridges.• Storage Facilities: Establish facilities to enhance food security by ensuring food and seed availability during critical planting seasons. Capacity Building and Knowledge Transfer• Training for Health Workers: Train Community Animal Health Workers (CAHWs) and Community Based Extension Service Providers (CBESPs) to improve livestock management.• Post-Harvest Management: Conduct training on post-harvest management techniques and value addition in agricultural value chains.• Farmer Field Schools: Support the adoption of new agricultural practices through farmer field schools Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaptation• Flood-Resilient Infrastructure: Construct flood-resilient water points and sanitation facilities to mitigate disaster risks• Early Warning Systems: Establish or strengthen community-based early warning systems for climate-related hazards to enhance preparedness.• Ecosystem Restoration: Implement watershed-based ecosystem restoration approaches to protect natural resources Market Linkages and Value Chain Development• Strengthening Value Chains: Enhance agricultural value chains by re-establishing market linkages and providing equipment for value addition in various products like fish, meat, poultry, vegetables, fruits, milk, and honey. Cross-Cutting Themes• Inclusion: Ensure gender, age, and disability inclusion in all activities to address the needs of diverse population groups.• WASH-Specific Activities for GBV Mitigation: Implement water, sanitation, and hygiene activities specifically aimed at mitigating gender-based violence risks.• Protection Risk Analysis: Integrate protection risk analysis and mitigation measures into all interventions to safeguard vulnerable populations from exploitation or harm.• These priorities are designed to align with the Food Security and Livelihoods Cluster objectives, WASH Cluster objectives, and the broader humanitarian response strategy for South Sudan. They aim not only to address immediate needs but also to build long-term resilience against climate change impacts and other shocks.
