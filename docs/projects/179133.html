Project Summary

Al-Resala Foundation (RF) designed its 2021 WASH intervention based on the analysis of humanitarian needs of the affected-population in north Syrian, need severity for WASH assistance, and HNO2021 key messages, as well as, in line with WASH Cluster- COVID -19.
The project aims to restore functionality or keep water infrastructure at the minimum level of operation; and to carry out life-saving/ emergency WASH interventions including, water system rehabilitation and extending water network, as well as improvement of hygiene promotion, and raise awareness of COVID-19 pandemic precaution measures, targeting IDPs, host communities and returnees. also, include soap and detergent distribution. RF takes a new approach in this intervention to increase the access and reach more vulnerable people in need for water services, RF will be working with Vision For A Better Future organization (Vision) as a Sub-Implementer referred as SI in this proposal, which RF is going to distribute work load with Vision as 60 % RF, while 40% will be implemented through Vision, the same divided percentage will be used for the project components. Vision is going to operate the activities for Yazibagh, Qastal Jendal, where Vision will be responsible for the activities and resources in these 2 locations, Yazibagh C1565 Qastal Jendal C1521 while RF will take the responsibility of for running all activities in these 3 locations.

The project consists of three main components targeting 257,573.
*Connecting power lines electrical grid which come from Turkey with the water pumping stations in (Al Qastal, Maarin, and Yazi Bagh, and Diversion water station in Sharan) the power line has already connected to Kaljibrin. The boreholes are already connected with the water network in the four villages and Azaz city. Improve access to safe water, through the water network to minimize the financial burden on already impoverished families targeting 257,573 individuals in NW Syria are overburden due to high IDP numbers resulting from continued displacement in addition to limited functionality and efficiency of water infrastructure and services in addition to schools, health facilities, nutrition centers, and protection centers. Therefore, RF and SI Vision will provide sustainable power supply to the 5 drink water stations through an electric company.
The daily water per capita share will be 35 L/person
Based on this project fund RF and SI Vision will provide operational support for 90 days to supply water to the most vulnerable people, meanwhile, will develop tariff-based system management in coordination with relevant stakeholders (mainly the Local Council in the project area).
Chlorine pumps will be connected to each water station

4 - Needs

People in the targeted communities live now on Azaz district water trucking or closed wells to provide their unsafe water needs, many families, have to face difficulties in reaching the needed water where there is one private source with a high price and not available at all times. This situation seems difficult for people like widows, divorcees, and people with special needs because all people depend on this source (water trucking).Azaz district is suffering from a massive lack of drinking water supply, currently, it receives water in a limited time and during specific days of the week as this kind of services is not adequate to all families need and even it doesn’t reach faraway houses who had to buy water from tanks owners with a 1000-1500 SYP for each barrel 220 liters. 8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: health and safety of the community during implementation or rehabilitation of the water networks. Mitigation: ensure through regular monitoring of the rehabilitation site; awareness-raising meetings with the families regarding the ongoing work; well-planned interventions (phased intervention) and adherence to strong guidelines for safety and protection. Risk: theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by groups and used in conflict activities.Mitigation: MoUs with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities; promote cost recovery systems for longer-term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership
