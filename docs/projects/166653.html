Project Summary

• Adapt management of moderate acute malnutrition services for children, pregnant & lactating women, including those living with HIV/AIDS/TB in view of COVID-19 risk-mitigation measurements, and prepare training and supporting package adjusted to the social distance measure.
• Leverage WFP SBCC and messaging platforms to include messaging on protective measures against COVID-19 and safe hygiene to reinforce skills and good practices at community level. This includes communication on family feeding; reduction of food waste; recommended maternal, infant and young child nutrition practices (MIYCN); COVID-related messaging specific to PLHIV, the elderly and people with chronic illnesses; adapting health recommendations to the context of COVID-19; and tailoring the messages and communication channels to urban, peri-urban and rural contexts.
• Strengthen infection prevention and control procedures in Moderate Acute Malnutrition program, and community-based demand generation activities, ensuring adequate protection of staff and beneficiaries in line with WHO and Mozambican MoH protocols.
• Pre-position of specialized nutritious foods in country to treat the predicted increase on demand of these products.
• Preventive/protective fortified food ratio targeting nutritionally vulnerable groups (PLW, CU2, PLHIV, chronically ill and elderly) from high-risk food insecure households.
• Adapt and scale up nutrition-related monitoring and surveillance (e.g monitoring access to essential nutrition services; food prices, including of nutritious foods; purchasing power; consumption patterns; and nutritional status) as part of food security and socio-economic impact assessments, through the use of mobile technologies, to inform response options and allocation of resources.
