Project Summary

UPO will focus primary to support 20,000 families and ensuring that the most vulnerable Women, men, boys and girls are the center of UPO ESNFI response. ESNFI Needs assessment will continuously be conducted with support from other partners to understand the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programs addressing these needs. the Communities in the target areas are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support improving their lives to cope in managing recurrent shock and build resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs, UPO will continue with its ESNFI response in Darfur, Khartoum, Gadaref, Al jazirah , RedSea and South Kordofan. UPO will target the localities with it ESNFI interventions that scored 4/5 in the HNOs 2023 and since the recent events erupted in Sudan on the 15th of April between the Sudan armed forces and the Rapid support forces which caused mass displacement in all the above-mentioned locations and further exacerbated the needs and vulnerabilities. UPO will ensure close coordination with all partners throughout its interventions. UPO activities in target locations will include coordination of needs assessments, cash for shelter, reception centres, IES distribution, training, awareness raising on fire prevention, gender and environment protection and NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization of communities and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people.
The project will also focus on the awareness on the prevention of the flood and preparedness and hazard mitigation messages including Fire and droughts in the vulnerable areas. In addition to promoting peaceful coexistence and community shared management of the provided services to ensure ownership and sustainability of the provided services in these harsh times that Sudan is going through , UPO focuses on gender sensitive programming modality throughout the implementation and exit stages of all the projects ensuring complete community ownership and representation.

1 - Needs

since the 15th of April armed conflict started between the SAF and the RSF in what we can call a civil war that has wreaked havoc in the capital of Sudan Khartoum and in Darfur resulting in mass displacement and loss of lives and properties to thousands of families, thousands of thousands have lost their livelihoods and access to basic Services. According to the revised HRP 2023, its estimated that over 1 million have displaced and over 26 million people affected by conflicts since the War started in 15th of April,2023. its estimated over 250,000 affected in West Darfur and hundreds of thousands of others displaced to central and eastern States of Sudan with trends indicating continued displacement as armed violence Continues in Darfur, Eastern and Central States. The majority of people affected by WAR, in particular displaced, women headed households and those with no or limited assets, are living in a state of extreme poverty. humanitarian agencies made efforts to rescue people from appalling humanitarians’ conditions however, due to limited access, resources and immense needs across the country; it is far from reaching milestone of improving lives of all affected people. thousands of people are homeless and have no shelter living in open areas, public institutions, or hosted by families who already have limited spaces. Needs-based assessment will continuously be conducted in coordination with other partners to understanding the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programmes addressing these needs. Communities are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support improving their lives to cope in managing recurrent shock as result of the conflict, and flood disaster and build resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs, UPO will priorities localities that scored 4/5 in the HNOs 2023 will be prioritized to reach many people that are mostly in need of NFI and shelter including Cash for renting and communal shelters. UPO will ensure close coordination with partners throughout its interventions. UPO activities in target locations will include needs assessments, IES distribution, training, awareness raising on fire prevention, Flood, gender and environment protection, and NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people.

2 - Activities/Outputs

UPO will continue coordinating with partners to conduct participatory need assessments to determine NFI and Shelter needs and identifying the most vulnerable populations. the key activities will be implemented as follows : 1. provision of lifesavings NFI and emergency shelter materials to new IDPs, Returnees and host communities 2. Conduct community consultation meetings on risks and social cohesion related to NFI and emergency shelter response that will enhance coping mechanism 3. Conducting NFIs/ES needs assessment, verification and distribution of NFIs of newly displaced, protracted IDPs and other most vulnerable populations in timely manner. 4. Provision of Emergency shelter materials. 5. Training 1,000 community leaders on risks reduction and emergency management e.g. proper shelter construction, fire incident prevention etc. 6. Training women on fuel sufficient stove (mud) production skills to enable them to possess new income opportunity. 7 provision Cash for NFIs and shetler. 8. provision of shelter updates/cash services.11. Site management and planning services. conducting awareness on prevention of flood, fire and droughts and other preparedness and hazard mitigation related messages to 20,000 peoples
