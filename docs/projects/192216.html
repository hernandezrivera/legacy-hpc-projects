Project Summary

The situation in Lebanon continues to rapidly deteriorate due to the compounding factors of economic decline, political stalemate, protracted refugee crises, the COVID-19 pandemic, the Beirut Port Blast in 2020, and growing fear and uncertainty for the future. Since the political crisis began in October 2019, unemployment rates of migrants in Lebanon sky-rocketed while living conditions have significantly deteriorated. The Kafala System in Lebanon further exacerbates vulnerabilities and opens the door to employer abuse, and despite reforms, there is still a huge number of protection cases associated with migrants. In order to address the situation faced by vulnerable migrants, this project will partner with MSD in order to provide complementary services related to mental health, PSS in 3 community centers run by Tabitha, Aley, Batroun and Achrafieh.
Under this project, Tabitha will support a total of 200 cases in case management, in addition to 225 women who will attend PSS sessions and 108 children who will join as well PSS sessions. 150 cases will also benefit from Emergency Cash Assistance (ECA). These activities will be complementing the services provided by MSD related to legal assistance, counseling and representation in addition to medical care.
Tabitha will prioritize cases of trafficking and/or GBV. The beneficiaries will be coming to one of the 3 community centers and will receive a wholistic package of services.
Tabitha will utilize its reputation as a trusted protection-based organization with community outreach workers to ensure that migrants and Lebanese vulnerable beneficiaries are aware of the service and able to get support. While this project is focused in the locations where Tabitha has its community centers, Tabitha is open to referrals from across the totality of Lebanon.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

This project falls in line with the migrant sector objective 3, "To enhance timely, unhindered, and equitable access to protection assistance for migrants." The project will achieve this objective by providing mental support, PSS for adults and children in addition to ECA through case management conducted by social workers. Tabitha's social workers will link the beneficiaries with other services providers such as MSD the partner who provides legal counseling, support and representation.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Tabitha mainstreams protection in all its activities which allows maximization of the protective impact of the project through ensuring that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Social cohesion activities are designed to bring together individuals of different backgrounds. Activities create a space for discussion and peer mentoring which allows different groups to reflect on their similarities rather than differences. Project activities are designed to include any critically vulnerable individual within the population, regardless of their nationality, gender identity, disability, race, religion, or socioeconomic background. Staff will be sensitized to gender & diversity and should recognize that vulnerabilities differ from one person to another.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Tabitha creates opportunities for beneficiary involvement throughout the program cycle. This project’s activities have been designed based upon the feedback given by beneficiaries and stakeholder engagement, where possible. Tabitha has a robust two-way feedback mechanism with multiple channels like complaint boxes, email address, hotline, and SMS, and engages with stakeholders such as consulates and embassies of concerned vulnerable migrants. Within this project, Tabitha already has the complaint & feedback mechanism located in the 3 community centers. For those who do not speak Arabic, French, or English, Tabitha will locate community volunteers who can communicate with them to ensure the opinions of all beneficiaries are heard.
