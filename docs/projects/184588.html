Project Summary

Children in Somalia continue to face acute humanitarian needs due to conflict and environmental hazards such as drought and flooding. COVID-19 pandemic has compounded the challenges exacerbating vulnerability of the crisis-affected populations across the country. According to the 2022 HNO, 3 million children living in Somalia require urgent and sustainable education support during the year of 2022. The prolonged civil unrest, continued climatic shocks and impact of Covid-19 are all driving factors leading the education crises existing. Nearly 70% of school aged children in Somalia continue to be out of schools including majority of affected children living in rural areas. According to the Somalia education sector strategic plan, national gross enrollment rate (GER) for primary education is 23% in urban areas and 18% in rural while IDPs is at 16.6%.
The existing crises in Somalia has contributed furtherly to the vulnerability of education system which had impacted the future of school aged boys, girls and children with special needs. 70% of the Somalia children either out of school or soon-to-drop-out situation. Access to primary education is minimal and this contributes protection concerns among children. Lack of education services exposes children to greater risk of harmful practices specially recruitment by armed groups, child labor and child marriage. Health and demographic survey conducted in this year, education indicators in Galmudug shows very alarming situations. Limited access to education, week retention capacity and lack of institutional development has led greater number of children to be out of schools. Only 2% of the girls living in Galgadud have graduated from secondary school. This brings an alarming situation that demands to address the underlaying factors hindering access to and retention capacity of the schools in that state.
Given the recurrent emergencies across the country and specifically in Galmudug state, urgent humanitarian support for education is particularly critical to support school aged children get continued access of education services by addressing the underlying factors. In regards to this, Qatar Charity is proposing a project that will address immediate gaps existing in Galmudug education sector and will work to improve access, continuity of quality education and retention of crisis-affected vulnerable girls and boys in schools and promotes access to education services for all including children with disabilities by constructing/rehabilitating of safe learning infrastructures that can accommodate the different requirements of boys and girls in the school to improve academic performance and address gender related concerns in accessing equal opportunities. QC will Provide scholastic materials to the students to further motivate children attend the school regularly and ease financial burdens of buying scholastic materials. Further on this, project will work to Install gender responsive WASH facilities which enhances protection and the privacy of girls and promotes dignity and gender equality. Finally, to improve education quality, QC will build capacities of school teachers and will work to promote engagement of community education committees (CEC) in school management process to promote accountability in education sector.
QC will work with state level ministry of education, regional authorities and local communities, community level education committees and students in school to better identify existing gaps and prioritize the inclusion of minority children and those with disabilities in order to effectively plan responses. Consultation workshops will be organized with the different stakeholders groups for the aim of better tailoring project activities with the priorities and needs on ground. Also, QC will work to link the outcome of this project with other resilience and developmental initiatives implementing in the state to ensure that education services sustain for longer time.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

QC will work under the guidelines of integrated education child protection response framework that promotes child protection mainstreaming in education setting. QC will ensure that broader consultation meetings is made in the beginning phase of the project implementation where all duty bearers and relevant stakeholders are engaged to protect and support all children and ensure safe access to and at the school as per the Safe Schools Declaration. Also, project will sensitize all relevant stakeholders on project related information, inclusion of monitory groups and children with disabilities and contribute decision making process throughout the difference cycles of the project (assessment, implementation and monitoring). During these consultations, the project team will also identify gaps, assess the barriers to access education and record their inputs and suggestions to make education spaces a child friendly, safe and inclusive and promote retention. QC will consider the recommendations, needs and suggestions from the community education committee, local authorities and education ministry of Galmudug to improve the protection outcomes and maximize the impact of the interventions to the crisis affected children.Further on this, the project will stress and ensure the inclusion of children with disabilities in the project intervention and will initially engage with school management and duty bearers to assess the level of attendance of children with disability and existing barriers that hinder access to schooling after which the project ,based on their recommendations ,will work to further support the enrollment and retention of the these group of students to promote equitable and inclusive access to education by removing existing barriers. All project activities, special arrangement will be made to ensure that children with disability enable to access it. Construction/rehabilitation of classes will be done in a way that disabled students can access the class. QC will ensure improved safe and protective learning environments with access to protection services to reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen the safety of learners and to achieve this, the project will construct gender responsive WASH facilities where the boys and girls will access separated latrines and hand washing facilities and the safety of the children on their way to school and back to home will be considered in consultation with all duty bearers to ensure the children are learning in a safe and protective environment.The school teachers will be trained on inclusive education, protection and socio-emotional learning which will further mitigate protection concerns and the project will also engage with community education committee and provide training on children’s rights and a violent free learning environment with zero tolerance to all kinds of child abuses including bullying in and out of school. Additionally, the project will assign male and female teachers to identify as child protection focal point of the school, who will support students in need of protection support and establish referral pathways. QC will organize regular visits to the target schools and will meet with community education committee, teachers, children, parents and school management to identify any gaps and protections concerns and will solve them immediately if they are found.
