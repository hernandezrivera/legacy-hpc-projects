Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls, women, and men, with attention to PWDs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities with attention to IDPs and PWDs, targeting 198000 beneficiaries during the project period (73000women, 45000 men, 42000girls,38000boys) including all age groups; children, adolescences, and older adults.10000 of them PWDs.
This will be done through specific interventions focusing on:
• Provision of essential health care services to cover the health care needs of vulnerable populations in targeted communities, including RH services, Child Health care, CD, and NCD services. these services will be provided through:

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Although the security situation has somewhat stabilized, displacement persists, &significant needs persist, especially health situation remains fragile, worsened by the impact of COVID, cholera, and a recent earthquake. The catastrophic results have further worsened the humanitarian and nutritional situation, as indicated by the HNO-24 report, assigning a severity score of 4 and 5 to the project's targeted areas which is compatible with the HC strategy-24 to prioritize targeting the areas with severity levels of 4&5 SDI will implement this project for PIN in 9 sub-districts through the following:3 PHC,4MCs 2 hospitals,and 3 specialized center. The main activities will be provision of essential primary, secondary, and tertiary services.EHS comprise surgical and trauma services, RH,Nutrition,GBV,CH,MHPSS, diagnostic services,and specialized care for PWDs and in addition to prevention and treatment of CD&NCD.Also Provide HFs with essential medicines &supplies,including(PPE)and Strengthen the capacity of health care providers &CHWs to provide EHS.The M&E team will conduct visits to ensure service quality, verify data, administer BNs satisfaction surveys, and follow observational for addressing complaints.The reporting will be according to guidelines agreed by the TWGs of the HC (4Ws, HeRAMS,DHIS)The project will offer counseling to inclusivity diverse age and gender groups, including women&girls focusing on PWDs and vulnerable groups . SDI prioritizes AAP and stakeholder engagement (HC, NGOs, health directorates, local councils,WHO&UNFPA), with a focus on marginalized groups, is facilitated through a robust CFM accessible via multiple channels to facilitate greater sustainability. Additionally, SDI is committed to integrating protection measures, raising awareness among beneficiaries about potential risks, and consider protection risks and will mitigate these risks, through adherence to PRA guidance, mainstreaming protection, and conflict-sensitive programming. Gender balance is maintained through the hiring of female field staff for privacy enhancement, and the project ensures non-discrimination, awareness of GBV and PSEA issues, &verifies BNs satisfaction. Confidentiality is upheld for beneficiary information, and SDI's documentation policy prioritizes protecting beneficiaries' rights and dignity.The staff will be trained on communication, rights of PWDs, PSEA and GBV.R1: Physical accessibility difficulties. Mitigation: SDI planned and modified activities to meet the specific needs to enable access for PWDs. R2:Access to targeted areas becomes hindered or insecure. Mitigation: Monitor and address security challenges in targeted areas. Adjust activities to accessible alternatives in case of security changes..R3:Increase in influxes of IDPs result from more escalation in South Idlib. Mitigation:SDI always increase the operational capacity and provide more medical drugs through a private fund to respond to the extra need for most vulnerable
