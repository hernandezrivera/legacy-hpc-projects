Project Summary

The proposed project aims to strengthen the effectiveness of CUAMM's integrated humanitarian health intervention in Cabo Delgado by
- Strengthening the comprehensive support to targeted health facilities located in priority districts;
- Improving the delivery of quality essential health services to the most vulnerable populations and those most affected by the humanitarian crisis;
- Strengthen linkages between different levels of care: from community to secondary level;
- Improve coordination mechanisms between the different actors involved in the response through active participation in coordination fora (clusters, TwGs, etc.), reducing duplication of interventions and meeting the urgent and life-threatening needs of the most vulnerable categories.
CUAMM will adopt a human rights-based and people-centred approach, implementing tailor-made and flexible activities to respond to the most acute needs of the population.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The proposed project builds on the good results obtained thanks to the actions carried out in 2023, aiming to reinforce them and to strengthen the collaboration with local authorities in order to maximise their involvement and ownership of the action. The activities will be implemented in an unstable security and humanitarian context. The security situation in Cabo Delgado province, despite recent improvements remains, indeed, volatile and 2.3 million people are reported to be affected by the conflict, of which 2 million remain in need of life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance. Recent months have also been characterized by an important increase and growing trend in returns to the districts of origin while the number of IDPs remain high, with 850.599 individuals displaced in Northern Mozambique. Civilians continue to bear the brunt of the armed violence: conflict affected populations suffer from lack of food and water, inadequate sanitation and health services, insufficient shelter, and numerous protection risks. Moreover, serious violations of IHL and human rights conventions by all parties to the conflict have been reported, including SGBV, psychological trauma, killings, abductions and the forced recruitment of children into armed groups.In this framework of action, CUAMM adopts a conflict sensitive approach, considering its structural and social basis in designing the project's activities. Furthermore, being Cabo Delgado prone to epidemics for food and water born disese (cholera, thyphoid fever. etc.), CUAMM adopts a flexible approach in order to respond promptly to new crisis. The intervention proposes an integrated and comprehensive approach towards health aiming at addressing the most urgent needs of the most vulnerable categories by:- reinforcing the existing and functional health units located in the target districts overwhelmed by the IDPs influx;- supporting the delivery of life saving health services in IDPs sites;- wherever no health units are functional and operative, organize integrated health, nutrition, protection mobile teams and mobile brigades to reach people living in hard to reach areas;- improving the referral pathways, in particular for PLWs, U5 children and SGBV survivors;With the present proposal, CUAMM will reach: 26.700 individuals disaggregated as follows:SADD- 5.607 children (<18 years old);- 15.753 adults;- 1.335 elderly;- 4.005 PwDs;- 12.816 male;- 13.884 female.Considering the status of the beneficiaries, the present intervention targets:- 8.144 IDPs- 8.144 host communities;- 10.413 returnees.Due to the complexity of the humanitarian crisis in Cabo Delgado, CUAMM considers it fundamental to direct its activities towards all people in need, regardless of their status, paying particular attention to their level of vulnerability.-

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project is in line with the health cluster objectives and will work at different levels:- At Health Facility level: Cuamm will ensure essential lifesaving services to the most vulnerable groups, providing additional health workers (especially in the overwhelmed Health Centres close to IDP sites), medicines, medical supplies, etc. The direct provision of services will be complemented by capacity building activities, in order to strengthen the resilience of the system; - In IDP sites: CUAMM will support the reinforcement of integrated mobile brigades and, where necessary, temporary clinics to extend the coverage and increase (in terms of quality and quantity) of health services, in particular antenatal and postnatal care, U5 vaccinations, U5 malnutrition screening. A particular attention will be given to GBV cases to ensure prompt identification and careful management, taking into account not only physical but also psychological aspacts. Patients will receive appropriate clinical care and then be referred to the nearest health centre/hospital/specialised centres for psychosocial and legal support and follow-up, as needed. The activities will be carried out in close coordination with CCCM, health and protection clusters, to implement multi-sectoral assistance to support vulnerable IDPs in prioritized collective sites.- Staff will be trained and sensitised, and advocacy will be undertaken to ensure the continued availability of all essential medical supplies. The quality of care provided will be strengthened through technical assistance and supportive supervision, and monitored through improved data collection and analysis, functional for informed decision making to improve the current situation.The activities will be carried out in close coordination with the Health cluster, in order to avoid duplication of interventions and intervene in the areas of highest needs, and in close collaboration with the local authorities to strenghten their involvment and engagement in the response to the needs of the most vulnerables.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

CUAMM designs its projects in close cooperation with local authorities to ensure buy-in and active participation of local actors. Project activities are implemented through participatory approaches, ensuring that community members and beneficiaries are actively involved in the development and implementation of every project components, thus ensuring that their needs are effectively met. Prior to the implementation of all activities and regularly throughout the project, information meetings will be promoted at community level and with government services to ensure support and accountability. CUAMM, in coordination with the Cluster, will promote the establishment of effective project community feedbacks and complaints mechanisms, enabling beneficiaries and community members to provide feedbacks on CUAMM activities in a safe, non-threatening and accessible manner. Community feedbacks and complaints could be collected at service delivery points through focus group discussions with beneficiaries and community members. In addition, CUAMM will continue to promote access to 'Linha Verde' as an effective inter-agency complaint, feedback and information hotline, including for sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA).

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project's activities encourage meaningful access to assistance thanks to the implementation of tailored integrated interventions strenghtening both the demand side and the supply one. On the demand side, CUAMM will identify the difficulties, challenges and barriers encountered by the different groups of population in accessing assistance. Togheter with the beneficiaries and the local authorities, common, accepted and feasible meaures will be identified and put in place. In addition, community awareness activities will be carried out in collaboration with local civil society organisations and activists to develop better attitudes towards gender and disability among community members and leaders, and to ensure that women and girls are fully aware of the services available at the nearest functional and operational centres. On the supply side, CUAMM, in collaboration with the local authorities and the cluster, will contribute to facilitate the referral and follow up system for critical and complicated patients from communities to specialised services. In collaboration with local CSOs and activists, CUAMM will produce accessible IEC materials and participate to inclusive awareness campaigns promoting the active participation of disadvantaged groups. i.e. women, girls, people with disabilities and elderly persons. Finally, considering the inequalities affecting women and elderly persons in participating to the activities, CUAMM will implement specific measures to facilitate their active involvement and engagement in the intervention.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

CUAMM analyses the barriers, constraints and difficulties encountered by various demographic groups, impeding their equal access to humanitarian services and assistance. Specific attention will be given to identifying the barriers faced by individuals with disabilities (PwDs). A protection map will be developed by gathering community feedback and conducting an assessment of other partners/institutions. This will aid in identifying vulnerable groups and situations that may pose a threat to intervention goals. The map will assist in maintaining a focused approach, preventing the squandering of resources, and prioritising key areas and issues. In carrying out the initiatives, CUAMM will embrace and uphold a human rights-based and people-centred strategy to cater to the particular requirements of individuals with disabilities, encompassing women, men, girls, boys, and the elderly.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed programme aims to minimise its impact on the environment through sensitising healthcare workers and community health workers on the safe disposal of consumables, particularly personal protective equipment. Moreover, the project will prioritise raising awareness for environmental and personal hygiene, sanitation, and prevention/control of disease outbreaks for both host and displaced communities at the community level.During the project implementation, CUAMM will promote the integration of activities that involve vehicle movements, aiming to reduce the number of cars and motorbikes travelling to the same location and thereby decreasing fuel consumption.Additionally, we will ensure the careful monitoring of fuel storage and management to prevent any potential leakage.Waste products are hazardous to the surrounding environment. It is imperative to engage in discussions with the local health authorities on how to safely handle the products resulting from the drugs store dejunking.Regular exercises should be carried out to avoid wastage being left in the field, potentially contaminating water sources or cattle feeding spaces.
