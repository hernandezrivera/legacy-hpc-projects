Project Summary

Child Protection and Education sectors are working to integrate their projects to ensure that children get better and improved services and that long term effective solutions are attained for long term education. In this regard, this project presents an opportunity to implement a program that looks keenly into protection and education needs of the children in the localities of Al Abassya, Rashad and Kadugli (South Kordofan) locality (Al Mairam and Lagawa (West Kordofan) beside in Abyei PCA. The need for this project is embedded in the fact that a myriad of assessments conducted in these areas show a huge gap of children out of school and increasing access without working on protection issues that ensure retention and transition will not resolve the issues. The project will be implemented in the targeted areas through an integrated Approach that looks at Child Protection as a key element of Education and education as a fundamental right of the child. This project will ensure that children in the target localities attend and stay in school through innovative community based mechanisms that are easy to replicate and sustain. GAH will work with state and localities based family tracing and reuniﬁcation FTR systems and establish information desks to support FTR, Moreover, the action will strengthen the capacity of MOSA/MOE on case management and referral of children with specific needs. GAH will work with community based education and child existing community structures (PTA, CBCPN, Committees), supporting exciting child protection facilities (CFS) and integrate education and child protection in both CFS and schools , establishment of a very tight link between the social workers and surrounding schools and train the CBCPN and link them with PTA to complement their efforts.
The project will also target rehabilitation of class rooms which pose risks for the students and construction of new class rooms to facilitate hosting the huge number of school children. GAH is planning to rehabilitate and construct of school latrines especially for girls schools and advocate with the local community for school fencing and make the schools child friendly schools. To ensure the sustainable result, GAH will improve the capacity of teachers, PTA, children and parents and will sensitize them on CFS approaches. Project will target children on the move and enroll them in schools to reduce further movement that may lead to child abuse, neglect including child traﬃcking. A referral mechanism will be established/strengthened not just for education but with other sectors as well (Health, Nutrition and Livelihoods) The project would like to tackle girl child education through use of a peer to peer support group called the girl power clubs in schools where girls attending the same school are grouped together and sensitized to support each other. Child clubs and parent support groups will also be supported Special care will be given to children with disability through a well-planned disability friendly school facility improvement and parent support. The PTA will also develop SIP that will look into the immediate and future needs of the school and link the school to other community structures. At the end it is envisioned that the schools and children with their parents targeted by this project will have better access quality and attain a graded level of education while enjoying their fundamental human rights

Needs

The need for project is based on the assessments done in the areas targeted, the Inter Agency Rapid Assessment Report: Al Abbassiya Locality, South Kordofan, February 2018, Assessment Report Rashad South Kordofan State [12-26 October 2018] GAH protection Assessment in Kadugli September 2017 and the GAH RNA in Abyei and GAH RNA Al Mairam , Lagawa . The villages of Abasiya and Rashad in SK with pop estimates of 34,896 (11796, 14,080 and 9,020) IDP’s, returnees and host communities respectively (Women and children more than 60 %) remain inaccessible to humanitarian aid partners with no access to all basic services with minimal or absence of formal local administrative structures. Educational and child protection concerns identiﬁed were as follows; In Education, the school dropout is very high approx. 2000 school age child among the IDP children are out of school due to families unable to pay school & registration fees, buy school uniform and school feeding. Classrooms in existing schools are overcrowded with 70 to 100 students per class. Some children are working to support their families and most of the school is lacking school WASH facilities and 48 are in need for rehabilitation. In Lagawa , Al Mairam localities the school dropout among the nomadic children is very high according to the locality based MoE the rate is high among the locality children and signiﬁcant high among the nomadic population. Child labor, early Marriage, FGM, Children Lagawa and El Mairam. For child protection; there areas number of UA/SM among the IDP community, high rates of child labor exposing the children to different types of child exploitation and abuse. The CBCPNs exist in some areas but are missing/weak in some villages. Rashad with 22,324 IDP living has experienced cycles of displacement during which their assets , capital and social networking badly affected , the assessment indicates the following as the major educational and child protection concerns in the area , In education high cost of education such as school fees, cost of textbooks, learning materials and school uniform besides cost of school meals prevents parents from sending their school age children to school In child protection : dropout , USM, child labor , and forced child marriage and other types of child abuse is among the concerns in Rashad locality that need urgent intervention Kadugli remains a destination for all the IDP from Boram , Umdurain , Hiban El Reif El Garbi localities and at the same time a transit point for the IDPs intending to reach the bigger Sudan cities, the expected number of IDPs is 19,982 and the number increases on a daily basis. Abyei is completed underserved with most areas inaccessible and very few players. GAH with IOM have worked on some projects before but currently education is missing in intervention. In 2018 GAH started a protection project but the needs are too huge compared to the gaps. The project would like to utilize the CBCPN to reach out .
