Project Summary

Landmine (LM) and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) pose a significant threat to the safety and security of IDPs, returnees, at risk populations and humanitarian actors in South Kordofan and Blue Nile state. Women and children are particularly vulnerable. SK and BN states have the highest
number of land mine casualties in the entire Sudan according to IMSMA June 2020 monthly report EO casualties reported 659 casualties 200 victims were killed (60 x men 10 x women, 26 x boys,12 x girls and 92 Not Specified) , 459 victims were injured, (197 x men ,22 x women, 58 x boy,13 x girls, and 169 x Not Specified). In BNS IMSMA shows that’s 454 victims of EO registered in BNS 148 killed and 306 injured.
Furthermore in South Kordofan and Blue Nile delivery and access too much needed humanitarian assistance is complicated due to high ERW/mine contamination levels. The project aim to reduce the risk posed by LM/ERW, by deployment of two Multi Task Teams (MTTs) , tow Manual clearance teams (MCTs) and tow MRE teams in Blue Nile and South Kordofan to release 300,000 Square meter of land contaminated by land mines and Explosive Remnants of War . Build and Increase knowledge about dangers of UXO and ERWs among 60,000 beneficiaries from affected communities in Blue Nile (Kurmuk , Baw , Gissan localities ) and South Kordofan (Dalanj, Habila, Dalami and Abu Jebiha localities ) by contamination of ERWs, to enable them to identify and adopt measures that reduce the risk posed. The released land will be handed over to 76,614 (16220 men, 18340 women, 20487 girls and 21567 boys) IDPs, returnees, at risk population and humanitarian workers in SK and BN free of LM/ERW. JASMAR works in coordination with protection sector/MA sub sector in Khartoum/ South Kordofan and Blue Nile, in collaboration with National Mine Action Centre (NMAC) for tasking and facilitate the permits and engagement of men and women’s from affected communities throughout the project cycle.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Activities Activity 1: Recruitment of Staff Activity 2: Logistic preparation for MCT, MTT and MRE teams. Activity 3: Training sessions for new teams and refresher training for the current JASMAR MCT, MTT and MRE teams. . Activity 4: Tasks and Deployment of the 2MCT, 2 MTT and 2 MRE teams in Blue Nile , South Kordofan states. At least 8 tasks issued by NAMC and completed by JASMAR teams. Activity 5: Land release operations including Non Technical Survey (NTS), Technical Survey (TS) and clearance of mines/ERW (MC) at least 190,000 sqm cleared and released . Activity 6: QA and Field Monitoring visits. Weekly QA conducted by TLs and 8 QA & monitoring visits conducted by Mine Action JASMAR Operation manger Activity7: Coordination with project stockholders & other Organizations in project area. Activity 8: Provision of community Base Mine Risk Education (CBMRE) intervention. 80 local volunteers trained and equipped with mine risk Education package to conduct awareness to their communities every training is to include 10 participant COVID-19 safety masseurs will be applied during training. : Activity 10: Direct Mine Risk Education sessions to the mine affected communities, IDPs and at risk population in BN, SK states. 60.000 benefit from direct EORE sessions in SK & BN states every session is to include 15 - 20 beneficiaries, social distancing, on a regular basis considering COVID-19 safety masseurs.Activity 11: Environmental awareness and tree planting: The project staff will comply with JASMAR Code of conduct regarding the respect and saving human dignity of beneficiaries. Through regular meeting the CL will ensure the consultation/ participation of the affected communities in prioritizing the clearance and technical survey process, during operations and handover of the land. CLO ensures that women opinions on clearance and technical survey prioritization are heard and valued. CLO will liaise with, collect and share information from a wide variety of sources (e.g. communities, local authorities, Ministry staff, other NGOs & INGOS) using meetings, interviews, mapping and other participatory activities. JASMAR will respond to the feedback provided by communities through the Orientation & consultation meetings. JASMAR will make use of the physical presence and expertise of JASMAR in the fields of GVB, HIV/AIDS and COVID-19 to raise the awareness of beneficiaries about the danger . Regarding COVID-19 teams will be trained, as part of the refresher training, on how to protect themselves from the coronavirus and shouldn’t transfer virus contamination to the community therefore COVID-19 safety masseurs will be taken when teams conducting meetings, awareness sessions ,camping’s ,CBEORE TOT training and safety briefings.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Destruction of mines and UXO founds during LR operation will have impact of the environment including cutting trees , soil movement and disturb watercourse The teams will adhere to environmental conservation during LR operations by, the location of Central Demolition Side (CDS) to consider the environmental aspects. Items found during the clearance will be destroyed with minimum impact on environment, avoid burning/cutting trees & vegetation as much as possible ; if necessary mitigate the damage by adopting the control measures according to JASMAR SOPs, SNMAS & IMAS 10.04 & at the end of the operations all items removed for camping purposes will be returned to the original situation before intervention.
