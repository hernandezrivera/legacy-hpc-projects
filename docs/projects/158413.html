Project Summary

OUTCOME 1: Girls and boys aged 3 to 18 have equal access to inclusive learning opportunities in safe and protective learning spaces.
Output 1.1: Structural damage assessment carried out and schools selected for support provision
Activities:
• Develop detailed structural damage assessment tool in coordination with DPEDH and get approval/ endorsement from DPEDH
• Carry out the structural damage assessment jointly with DPEDHD
• Jointly agree on the priority schools/districts with education authorities
Output 1.2: Safe, protective and inclusive learning spaces (e.g. classrooms) are re-established or rehabilitated in affected areas in Sofala and Manica with gender-sensitive and child-friendly WASH facilities
Activities:
• Rehabilitation of damaged schools in line with MINEDH standards
• Establishment/rehabilitation of WASH facilities in schools (handwashing and latrines)
• Establishment/rehabilitation of drinking water facilities in schools (water points)
• Conduct accessibility audit and support the necessary adaptations in infrastructure
Output 1.3: Students (Girls & boys) and learning spaces receive grade/age/gender appropriate teaching and learning materials
Activities:
• Distribution of Classroom kits (SIB)
• Distribution of Teachers Kit
• Distribution of ECD Kit
• Distribution of Recreational Kit
• Distribution of Students Kit (Learners' Kit)
• Distribution of Hygiene Kit
• Distribution of Dignity Kit.
OUTCOME 2: Students (Girls and boys) aged 3 to 18 receive quality learning opportunities
Output 2.1: Teachers are oriented on EiE essential package and have an increased knowledge and technical capacity to respond to protective education needs
Activities:
• Orientation to teachers on effective management of distributed teaching & learning materials
• Training of ECD facilitators on use of ECD Kit and teaching learning practices
• Orientation of teachers on EiE package (PSS, Child Safeguarding, Positive Discipline)
• Conduct a baseline assessment of teachers’ skills, attitudes and knowledge on IE and teaching methodologies for children with disabilities and special learning needs and child protection measures
• Train teachers in the schools using trial modules, including a focus on practical skills, developing low-cost materials and improved learning outcomes
• Train Teachers in a selected CREI in the identification of impairments and practical approaches to teaching children with disabilities and special learning needs
• Provide specific training of supervisors to roll out a continuous close-monitoring support to trained teachers
Output 2.2: Learning Spaces with functional school safety plans are in place
Activities:
• Development of school-based Disaster Risk Reduction plans
• Development and dissemination of child-friendly IEC material (including hygiene promotion, water-borne diseases and disaster risk reduction).
Output 2.3: Parents are oriented on knowledge & skills to support their children during & post emergencies and are providing conducive learning environment at home
Activities:
• Train PEs from DPOs in conjunction to help engage with targeted families of children with disabilities and special learning needs regarding their child’s learning
• Conduct survey in collaboration with the PEs and student teachers to identify households of children with disabilities and special learning needs including needs analysis for prioritization
• Provision of assistive devices for Children with partial disability (eye-glasses, hearing aid, wheel chair, etc.)
• Establish a follow-up system with regular household support visits by PEs and student teachers to children with disabilities and special learning needs and their families
• Conduct awareness-raising campaigns in schools
