Project Summary

ADD International is implementing Inclusive Education Project at Gedarif State at three localities covering six locations include East Galabat ( Doka & Greeb) , Gorisha (Gorisha & Brbar) and Galaa Alnahal locality (Alnahal & Blous ) . The objective of the intervention is to provide quick response to COVID-19 for Children with disability and their families and community at remote rural areas of Gedarif State. The response includes four main component :
- Awareness-raising on COVID-19: Developing (COVID-19 ) inclusive awareness text and voice messages through mobile phones (accessible for deaf and visually impaired people). The messages will be delivered via Zain /MTN Telecommunication Company .
- Supporting vulnerable families of CWD in remote rural areas of Gedarif with food baskets during lockdown. Vulnerability will be identified based on disability, severe poverty, single women heading household with children at project schools, families with CWD. Children are forced to work to generate income and support their families .If they are given food during this period they are more likely to stay at home and avoid infection risks.( targeting 180 families )
- Support engagement or re-purposing of teachers as agents for community awareness and risk communication .Gathering and dissemination of information on COVID-19 and supporting CWD and their families in understanding COVID-19 and its consequences . Teachers’ will be trained remotely before starting the action .
- Distribution of Hygiene kits for families of CWD and other children of 13 project schools target 850 families .
Total direct target is 5100 persons including CWDs & families at 6 locations . Indirect approximately 15000: Gadarif State at 3 lcoalities East galabat , AlNhal and Gorisha : , Total estimated beneficiaries = 5000
