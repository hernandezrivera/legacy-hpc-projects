Project Summary

Attendance rates continue to be low and attributed to a variety of factors such as lack of appropriate learning facilities, no or minimal learning materials, untrained teachers and overcrowded classrooms.
Covid-19, conflict and natural disasters has further aggravated the situation and continues to undermined socio-economic development of communities and created vulnerabilities that demand lifesaving initiatives and reducing priority to education. Despite various efforts by international donors and NGOs out of school children face extra protection related risks, including forced recruitment, abuse, child labor and early marriage; thus, access to protective education can provide children a safe space. In some areas especially, rural areas there are no schools’ and where schools exist, they lack qualified teachers, water, sanitation and school feeding programmes. A Joint inter-cluster assessment conducted in September, 2020 in Hiran, 15% of the respondents mentioned that they do not have schools or learning spaces in their settlement.
Education committees lack capacity (financial & human) to manage schools effectively. Gender disparities exist in access to educational opportunities with boys being favored. Many families, when faced with costly school fees choose to educate boys rather than girls as the girl will be married off hence no benefit of educating her.
A recent assessment in Hiran showed that 39% of school going age children do not attend school because their parents cannot afford books even if school is free. 34% of the respondents also said they are out of school due to fees being unaffordable. According to a study by the Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MoEHE) in Puntland, children with disabilities are subject to neglect, abuse, stigma and discrimination, negative attitude and exclusion. Families of persons with disabilities are often faced with similar barriers of stigma, segregation, refered in derogative names and lack of access to services and opportunities. Many children with disabilities do not attend school and are kept at home. The barriers faced by these children are huge and are additional to those they already face due to conflict, gender or refugee status.
The project will target locations with most marginalized and deprived communities (displaced and extremely poor host communities) through an integrated approach in Beletweyne, Banadir; Bossaso, Butrinle, Caluula, Bandarbeyla; Berbera, Boroma, Burco, and Buuhodle, 22000 (South Central Somalia, 9300 (50% girls); Puntland, 6,500 (50% girls); and Somaliland, 6,200 (50% girls) with 10% being children with disabilities across. 90 (30:30:30) schools per area will be targeted across Somaliland, Puntland and South Central Somalia.
The project through community mobilization will focus on inclusion in education services, school infrastructure and learning environment to ensure friendly children spaces (classrooms and WASH facilities) for children with disabilities. Community sensitization activities, will promote value of educating girls to boost enrollment and retention of girls in school. This will be complemented by girls' involvement in school clubs, girls' forums and provision of sanitary kits to girls. The project will also ensure inclusion of female teachers and female CEC members in capacity building activities to ensure the female voice is heard. Massive public awareness on covid-19 prevention will be conducted through pictorial print messages which will be printed and distributed to all supported schools.
SC will construct temporary learning spaces for expansion, rehabilitation and furnishing of classrooms and gender segregated latrines with handwashing facilities accessible to children with disabilities and girls, provision of top up incentives for teachers and supply of Covid 19 kits including thermometers, masks and hygiene kits.

Project Activities

• Conducting Community mobilization towards re-enrolment (Go-back –to-school) of school aged children• Provision of dignity kits to 1,000 girls in the target schools• Construction of gender segregated and inclusive latrines with hand washing facility• construction and rehabilitation of TLS.• Furnishing classrooms with desks and other furniture• Procurement and distribution of supplementary school feeding programme for schools affected by emergencies• Procurement and distribution of trucked potable water to schools • Provision of gender sensitive and inclusive recreational materials to school children• Payment of teacher incentives through the Ministry of Education in Federal, Puntland and Somaliland• Training teachers on teachers in crisis context manual (TICC)• Training teachers on Inclusive education, child safeguarding, child rights, child protection• Training teachers and CECs in COVID-19 prevention measures, life skills, guidance and counselling to effectively respond to the changes of learners’ behavior. • Provision of TLM and basic scholastic materials to school children in targeted schools • Procurement and distribution of IEC materials with Covid-19 prevention messages• Support to Community Education Committees on school water management• Conducting Hygiene Promotion activities/campaigns in schools and communities; specific activities relating to prevention of AWD/Cholera, COVID-19• Purchase and distribution of WASH/Hygiene supplies to schools (liquid soap for students and teachers, hand sanitizers for teachers), temperature “Guns” for prevention of Covid-19 virus• Disinfection & Fumigation (from COVID-19) of supported schools• Provision of improvised child -friendly handwashing facilities• Provision of water storage (tanks) in schools with shortage of water storage facilities• Provide training for Community Education Committees (CECs) in education in emergencies, COVI 19 • support and conduct joint quarterly monitoring, assessment and supervision support to schools with MoE at different levels, 4 times during the project period• Rolling out Complaints and Feedback Sessions-including feedback sessions with learners and teachers and Community education committees.• Establish and strengthen referral pathways for children with protection concerns

Mitigation Measures

• The project will identify and address the heightened risks faced by people with disabilities in humanitarian contexts.• The project will identify and work to address barriers to participation for people with disabilities• People with disabilities and/or their representative organizations will be consulted during the design and implementation of the project.• The project will disaggregate beneficiary data by disability o During enrolment drive campaigns in the project target areas, the CECs will put special emphasis on the identification of children with disabilities and encourage parents to send them to supported schoolso To ensure children with disabilities actively participate in learning, all the physical facilities including temporary learning spaces, latrines will be constructed with ramps to facilitate easy access by children with disabilities. Teachers will also be trained on disability and inclusion during capacity building sessionso Save the children will involve children with disabilities and organizations during the design state in order to get vast views and specific needs to address for these beneficiarieso The project will provide truly disaggregated data of beneficiaries which includes age, disability, gender Identify safe residential locations and avoid placing facilities near military installations and conflictfrontlines. Save the Children will consider safety risks associated with landmines, explosive remnants of war (ERW), busy roads,exposed wires and dangerous materials on the ground. Save the Children will keep in mind the distances children have to travel and their mode of transport. and consider ways to reduce threats, including changing the location, accompaniment (eg. children walking in groups,crossing guard points) Save the Children will ensure that the timing of classes is safe for children to travel to and from school without exposure tofurther risk of harm. Classes should take place in daylight hours giving children sufficient time totravel home before nightfall. Save the Children will ensure to situate play areas in clearly visible and safe locations Save the Children will ask children directly (and especially adolescent girls) about the safety of their travel to and presenceat school.
