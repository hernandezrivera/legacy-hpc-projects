Project Summary

The escalating conflict between the government and the non state arm groups in the NW and SW has resulted to civilians facing a lot of human rights violation in the hands of both parties since November 2019. the complex nature of this crisis has led to an increase vulnerability of the civilian population especially on women and girls and has humanitarian consequences such as massive population displacement, loss of property, lack of access to basic services and serious human rights violation. About 3 million people have been affected by the crisis in these two regions and over 1.1 million displaced as of early 2021 within the two regions and also into other regions such as the West region, the littoral regions and the Centre regions. The number of IDPs increased from 530,000 in July 2019 to 1.1 million in March 2021 and humanitarian needs, coupled with ongoing complexity arising from human rights violations against civilians will most likely persist.
80% of schools have been shut down and over 1.1 million children have been forced out of school, numerous health centers have been closed intensifying the deteriorating health conditions for the over 5.2 million people living in the Anglophone regions and many displaced into other regions of Cameroon considered to be safe.
Donga Mantung Division is highly affected by the ongoing conflicts yet its largely underserved by humanitarian action based on a number of factors including physical access challenges as well as access in terms of acceptance of humanitarian actions by the parties to the conflict. The Division has a population of 337,533 inhabitants (2005 PNDP statistics) with over 5754 internally displaced persons. A harmonized analysis carried out in November 2020 estimate a total of 1 million people are food insecure. Other needs such as shelter, protection, livelihood are largely underserved as a result of a number of factors such as limited resources. This project seeks to close the nutrition, unemployment and dependency gap of underprivileged displaced groups of women and girls in a coordinated process of capacity building and economic empowerment. The goal of this project is to improve the living standards of 500 women and girls in AKO MISAJE NDU NKAMBE and NWA in the Donga Mantung Division.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Based on the Humanitarian Response Plan published by OCHA in March 2020 (HRP 2020) the North West and South West regions have been subject to a resurgence of attacks against persons, their properties and public infrastructure, including health centers and schools, along with continuing incidents against humanitarian workers and medical personnel. The number of people affected by the crisis in the North West and South West has dramatically increased in the second half of 2018, due to increasing acts of violence, and was one of the fastest growing displacement crises in Africa in 2018 - and is since continuously growing. The number of people in need in the South West and North West regions has significantly increased from 1.3 million to 2.3 million (HRP 2020).According to the various reports published in 2019 and 2020, there is the urgent need to enhance the livelihood 130,000 persons in the North West. IDPs and host communities living in Ndu and Nkambe in Donga Mantung Division are faced with problems of shelter, food insecurity, health and other basic needs caused by the armed conflict in the South West and North West Regions. The goal of the project is to enhance the livelihood of women and girls in Donga Mantung Division. this will be done through carrying out activities like:- Provision of income generating activities for women and girls in these communities- Strengthening the capacity of 500 women and girls through organizing of different Ergo-therapy activities- Support 500 women and girls with funds to start up businesses- Link these women and girls to local councils to continue support and mentorship
