Project Summary

The project is aimed to provide emergency life-saving and life-sustaining response through integrated water, hygiene, and Sanitation responses to the most vulnerable people living underserved and hard-to-access areas in Wanlawayn District. Main activities include Water trucking in locations that need urgent water supply using a phased response strategy, rehabilitation and/or extension of strategic existing water infrastructures, .Establishment of gender-balanced water management committees to ensure continuity of services, appropriate operation and maintenance as well as strengthen women participation in WASH projects, Installation of new of sanitation facilities with handwashing basins, distribution of sanitation kits, Hygiene promotion through House to house approach and campaigns focusing on hand-washing with soap and hygiene kits distribution, including female-specific hygiene items (sanitary). 21000 vulnerable people comprised of 4000 men, 4000 women, 6000 boys, and 7000 girls receive affected by the recurrent flooding, and conflicts in the Wanlewayn district are expected to benefit these services.

1. - Projects activities

-Tempera water voucher: As a temporary measure during the rehabilitation of the water sources KAAH will truck water through vouchers in locations that need urgent water supply using a phased response strategy. 6000 people who are composed of 1300 are men, 1300 women, 1500 boys and 1900 girls living in flood-affected areas that need urgent water supply in Wanlawayn district of Lower Shabelle. 7.5 lppd of safe chlorinated water (0.2 – 0.5mg/l FRC) will be provided each of the beneficiaries using a voucher approach for 45 days period while targeting the most vulnerable women boys, girls affected by the flooding. Female-headed HH, households with elderly members, HH with disabled persons, and Orphaned and Vulnerable children will prioritized in particular. Voucher implementations will be guided by Somalia WASH cluster water voucher guidelines.-Rehabilitation of shallow wells, 10 strategic shallow wells has been identified that require urgent rehabilitation. Identified sources will be rehabilitated while focus on increasing the yield through excavations, covering and upgrading minimize future downfalls: Designs will be consulted with communities, Special consideration will be given to women and girls to have easy access. An overall 21000 vulnerable people comprised of 4000 are men, 4000 women, 6000 boys, and 7000 girls affected by the recent flooding in the Wanlawayn district are expected to benefit this work.-Establishment & Training of gender-balanced water management committees to ensure continuity of services, appropriate operation and maintenance as well as strengthen women participation in WASH projects Training of WMCs: KAAH to ensure the maintenance of the rehabilitated water sources, 50 persons (5 in each water sources) will be trained on operation/maintenance of the water facilities, basic chlorination skills, DRR and household water treatment. 40% of the WMC members will be female where appropriate.-Shock chlorination: Rehabilitated sources and other surrounding bores will be tested upon completion through shock chlorination at sources level and distribution of purification tablets at the household level. This work will be done in collaboration with the southwest ministry of water. An overall 21000 vulnerable people comprised of 4000 are men, 4000 women, 6000 boys, and 7000 girls crises affected population in Wanlawayn district are expected to benefit this work.
