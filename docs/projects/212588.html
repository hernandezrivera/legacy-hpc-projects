Project Summary

Shafak Organization seeks to provide humanitarian health care services to 233,075 people in need and conflict-affected populations in northern Syria in 2024-2025. The proposed response prioritizes internally displaced persons and vulnerable populations, particularly women, children, and people with disabilities, in the targeted areas. The intervention will be implemented as follows:
Continue supporting a comprehensive Essential Primary Health Care Package to improve access to health care of the most vulnerable groups in northwest Syria. The motivation of this project is to continue providing health services in the already established and equipped centers to the population in Aleppo and Idleb governorates through static facilities and outreach teams, and referral activities to the higher level and more specialized health services.
The project will comprise primary health care services and mental health care at the facility level. The activities will be provided through eleven health facilities:
