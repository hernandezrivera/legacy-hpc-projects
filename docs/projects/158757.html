Project Summary

The project seeks to rehabilitate water infrastructures and promote water treatment activities in order to increase access to emergency, sustainable, adequate and safe water to vulnerable households. The project also seeks to promote sanitation and hygiene practices through awareness campaigns and construction of sanitation facilities. This project will ensure immediate access to clean water as well as hygiene ab sanitation facilities to a population of 4,894(826 households). This will involve the drilling of a new borehole in a settlement located in Abudwak that has been surviving on costly water trucking. Additionally, the project will construct 1 shallow well, 1 20,0000 litres elevated tanks and equip them with solar water pumping system and water kiosk as secure and convenient water collection points especially women and children and the disabled in both Gedo and Galgudud.
To promote appropriate hygiene and sanitation practices among the target communities, the project will construct gender, age and disability sensitive twin latrines with safety locks and hand washing facilities in schools and health centres. The facilities will also be equipped with 10000 litres plastic tanks to facilitate the safe storage of water for hygiene purposes. Periodic WASH promotions campaigns will be conducted in schools and the communities. These campaigns will also include waste management in school, promotion of water treatment methods, provision hygiene kits and distribution of local language IEC materials. This project will be implemented within a period of 12 months

Mitigation Measures

3c.External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: Exposure of displaced women and girls to sexual violence, caused by living in highly insecure settlements, distance to and location of humanitarian assistance and servicesThe project will1. Design and construct WASH facilities that preserve the safety and dignity of its users. 2. Build separate toilet and bathing facilities for males and females. The facilities will be clearly marked in pictorial form for illiterate users and work with community to ensure they are used by the indicated sex. 3. Provide secondary enclosures around facilities or put locks on the doors to latrines to ensure security of beneficiaries. This will be discussed with beneficiaries to consider their preferences. 4. Raise platforms and construct ramps at water points to ensure safe manual lifting to all, especially children, pregnant women, and persons with disabilities. 5. Consider safety risks to children. For instance the project will consider the size of drop hole; provide smaller jerry cans for children to collect water to avoid potential injury and consider their physical capacity in designing water pumps. 4b. Protection mainstreaming: Beneficiaries risk exposure to violence during registration/distribution 1. Ensurethe locationandaccessroutes to WASH facilities and distribution points are safe. Water sources will be located in visible, central locations and not more than 500 meters from settlement and will be in lighted places.2. Avoidplacing all facilities or distribution points near military installations or areas prone violent attacks. 3. Ensure that any weapon bearers should not be present inside distribution points.4. Educate women and girls to walk in groups to distribution points on fetching water to reduce threats to their safety and their dignitythis arrangements will protect beneficiarieswho may face theft, intimidation, sexual assault and extortion whilst taking their hygiene kits home. The hygiene kit will also be of appropriate size for easy transportation.5. Identify central locations for distribution points-beneficiaries should not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points. Additionally distributions will take place in daylight hours to give beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall.Where necessary conduct door-to-door distribution and to avoid large distributions which puts beneficiaries at risk of attacks and pillage. 4d The project will be1. Transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organisational procedures and processes that affect them. Additionally theproject will set up accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms. For instance Conduct feedback surveys while distributing hygiene kits, Installing feedback box at WASH facilities and provision of staff phone lines2. WASH staff and committees should be representative of all groups within the community(e.g. gender, age, ethnicity, socioeconomic group, disability) and will be given protection mainstreaming training so they can identify protectionissues proactive responding to them effectively.3. Ensure WASHstaff,implementing partners and volunteersworking with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conductstating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. 4. Ensureall staff, implementing partners and volunteersinvolved in distributions and in direct contact with beneficiaries have received training in prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA). 5. Provide clear information to the distribution actorsand affected population explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services/favorsto anyone in exchange for receiving WASHassistance.Especially women and girls.

Please provide further explanation

The Local administration, relevant District Ministry , community leadership and local IDP and Host community are key in identification of project beneficiaries, sites and resources. This will enhance legality of the interventions, enhance ownership as well as mitigate against potential conflicts among the clans, IDPs, and host communities. On one end, an engaged local leadership will offer sufficient protective environment to beneficiaries and the investments of the project. On the other hand, the local leadership will ensure the sustainability of the project upon handing over of the project. After each project is commissioned, an MOU stipulating ownership, management and maintenance roles of each project will be signed between all local elders, local administration and community groups. The proposed project is linked to the WASH objectives of Somali National Development Plan and Wash cluster targets with an emphasis on implementing the objectives of WASH in Emergency response and developing the resilience of the WASH sector to retain and accommodate more displaced and non displaced households.
