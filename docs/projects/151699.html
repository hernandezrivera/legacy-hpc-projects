Project Summary

PAH will establish new water sources, through drilling new bore holes and installing pump equipment, and rehabilitate the existing water supply systems, through installing pump equipment and replacing pipeline of existing centralized/decentralized water supply systems, Water quality testing will be also ensured. At the same time, sanitation and hygiene conditions in social institutions in mentioned locations will be also improved. PAH will implement the project in six locations in the 15 km zone along the contact line in Popasnianskyi, Novoaidarskyi and Stanychno-Luhanskyi rayons, Luhanska GCA.
The most vulnerable populations (mostly older persons, people with disabilities and chronic diseases, female-headed households and people with socio-economic hardships) residing in small settlements in the areas close to the “contact line” will benefit from the project.

Needs

The ongoing conflict continues to threaten water supply to those who live along the contact line. Regular water shortages increased from 12% in 2017 to 27% in 2018 and are reported by 27% of the households along the contact line. The pipes supplying the water to the households that are relying mostly on the water supply system that are in very bad conditions due to pipes being almost 70 years old as well as their inadequate maintenance without any improvements and replacements being done over that time. This results in bad water quality caused by pathogens and pollutants entering the water supply through damaged pipes. Households that are not connected to the water networks rely on the wells and bore holes.As hostilities are still very intensive, especially in Popasnianskyi, Novoadarskyi and Stanychno-Luhanskyi rayons, many people moved from the areas, depriving the local budgets from the taxes. Hence, water supply systems are in a crucial need of repairs that under these circumstances could be only repaired by humanitarian actors.Taking into consideration the above described situation there is a high need for alternative water supply and repairs of the existing one. There is also a need to regularly conduct water quality testing in mentioned locations.Moreover, the sanitation and hygiene conditions of sanitation facilities in social institutions in selected six locations are in critical conditions, PAH will make sure to have a holistic approach and cover diverse needs of vulnerable populations, improving sanitation and hygiene conditions in social institutions.The most vulnerable groups are older people, people with disabilities and female-headed households. The further needs assessment will aim to verify the needs of those groups with a focus paid to different genders. Needs assessment will be conducted at the first stage of project implementation.

Activities & outcomes

Establishing new water sources in three locations in Luhanska GCA:Activities:1) Conducting Needs Assessment;2) Selecting project locations;3) Receiving assistance requests from local authorities;4) Receiving supporting documentation from local authorities;5) Making respective calculations;6) Conducting procurement procedure;7) Drilling bore holes;8) Installing pump equipment;9) Connecting bore holes to the existing water supply systems (incl. laying new pipeline).10) To make a handover to local authorities. Outcomes:- 2250 people provided with access to sufficient quantity of water.- 60 liters per person per day are collected from all sources for drinking, cooking, and hygiene. Rehabilitating existing water supply systems in three locations in Luhanska GCA:Activities:1) Conducting Needs Assessment;2) Selecting project locations;3) Receiving assistance requests from local authorities;4) Receiving supporting documentation from local authorities;5) Making respective calculations;6) Conducting procurement procedure;7) Installing pump equipment;8) Connecting boreholes to the existing water supply systems;9) Laying new pipelines to replace non-functioning parts of pipelines.10) To make a handover to local authorities. Outcomes:- 2250 people provided with access to sufficient quantity of water.- 60 liters per person per day are collected from all sources for drinking, cooking, and hygiene. The most vulnerable populations residing in small settlements/villages in the 15km zone along the “contact line” will be assisted under the project. The assistance will mostly target older people, people with disabilities and chronic diseases, female-headed households and people with socio-economic hardships.People of different gender will be targeted under the project as establishment of new water sources and rehabilitation of water supply systems will be done at the community level. Water quality testing in six locations in Luhanska GCA: Activity: Conducting water quality testing.Outcome: Six locations in Luhanska GCA are covered with water quality testing. Water quality testing will be conducted in the mentioned locations every month throughout the year. PAH will make sure that water in newly established and rehabilitated boreholes is tested regularly and properly. Improving sanitation and hygiene conditions in social institutions in six locations in Luhanska GCA:Activities:1) Conducting Needs Assessment;2) Selecting project locations;3) Receiving assistance requests from social institutions;4) Receiving supporting documentation from social institutions;5) Making respective calculations;6) Conducting procurement procedure;7) Rehabilitating/establishing sanitation facilities in social institutions;8) Improving hygiene conditions in social institutions ;9) To make a handover to local authorities. Outcome:- At least six sanitation facilities are rehabilitated/established in social institutions in six locations.- Hygiene conditions are improved in at least six sanitation facilities in social institutions in six locations.
