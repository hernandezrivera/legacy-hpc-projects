Project Summary

Due to the current situation in Lebanon, around 1.2 million school-age children have had their education disrupted in 2020 alone (ERP, 2021). Of these 1.2 million, 400,000 children are left out of school as a result of poverty and other factors (ERP, 2021). As such, WVL will target the most vulnerable children/youth Lebanese, Palestinians and Migrants and their caregivers under ERP who are out of education (OOE) in Akkar, North, BML, South, Baalbak/Hermel and Bekaa. By doing so, WVL will ensure that community members including caregivers, especially the most vulnerable, are well informed on up-to-date education-related information and services available and aware of the importance of education. This will essentially include support in referral mechanisms and pathways for children who are OOE and provide them with additional information and support if needed. With the curtailing crises in the country, the overarching objective is to ensure that education is not de-prioritized in the face of the challenges and difficulties that families and their children are facing. As part of this response, WVL will also provide homeschooling support including transferrable skills, responsive caregiving and basic and digital literacy to parents /caregivers. WVL will leverage its presence in the areas and its long-standing partnerships in the communities and utilize previously implemented outreach strategies that have proven effective in reaching the Most Vulnerable children and youth among targeted population such as through local partners, door-to-door, media including social media and phone calls.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

WVL will begin by conducting needs assessments with other Education partners to identify 4000 out of education (OOE) school aged children/youth in the intervention areas. This exercise will be based on data provided from previous WVL programs, SDR and sector data and using unified criteria provided by the sector. As such, WVL will outreach to ensure that community members, especially the most vulnerable, are well informed on up-to-date education-related information and services available, aware of the importance of education, know how and where to refer children and get additional support if needed and continuously encouraged and assisted with enrolment in Formal Education (FE). WVL will conduct awareness sessions with caregivers and children to inform them about available services and providers and based on their identified needs and on the guidance provided by the education sector, WVL will be referring 2600 Children / Youth out of the 4000 identified to the available basic services. When possible, WVL will be following with children to ensure that they have received the service they were referred to and they are still enrolled in FE.Moreover, 200 Caregivers will cover sessions on managing stress, anger and other challenges, mitigating interpersonal familial conflict, child and adolescent development, positive parenting, CP GBV and risk prevention and arts to enhance self-expression and psychosocial well-being in addition to ways to support their children’s education.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

WVL will use a participatory method to ensure equal access for all and will ensure gender-responsive learning environments and training materials. The project will take into consideration identified barriers that create obstacles for school aged boys and girls, and male and female caregivers and community members when planning to participate and benefit from the provided sessions. Gender-responsive questions will be incorporated into the routine project monitoring and evaluation disaggregated and analyzed by sex and age to understand the benefits of the services and remaining challenges for both genders.WVL supports the inclusion of children with disabilities in its programs. The inclusion process focuses on lessening the barriers to disability by identifying CWDs using WGQ, raising awareness on the community level to create more understanding and acceptance while tailoring activities whenever needed.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

World Vision Lebanon will guarantee the presence of a functional and accessible Accountability system to enhance beneficiary and community participation as well as ownership of project design and implementation. Targeted beneficiaries will have access to information packages including information about World Vision, the project and the community response mechanism via SMS, flyers, helplines and/or information sessions held at field level. The Accountability team will ensure that a help desk is available during major activities. Feedback and consultation activities will be conducted during the project implementation to obtain input on the beneficiaries’ preferences and needs to inform programmatic decisions. Complaints will be confidentially handled and closed within the timeframe after proper actions and measures are taken to solve it. Finally, monthly reports will be drafted to provide updates on finalized activities, pending and solved complaints.
