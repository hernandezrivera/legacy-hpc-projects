Project Summary

The one (1) year Camp Coordination Camp Management (CCCM), Shelter and Non Food Items (NFI) project aims at provideding assistance to conflict affected IDPs, living in formal camps and informal settlements in Borno and Yobe State through dedicated static & mobile CCCM teams, one shelter technical officer, and NFI officers and Assistant. Almost half of IDPs Population lives in camps or camp like settings. Despite this, low resources for Shelter/NFI and CCCM, leading to below SPHERE standard living in camps/host communities, lack of coordination on camp level and lack of an existing referral system for beneficiaries to accommodate especially the most vulnerable protection cases.
The proposed project is part of SHI’s wider programming. SHI currently implements a CCCM project in MMC at Cheazcoan, Shuwari5, Doron Baga, Tausayi and Umara Bolori.
The proposed project will cover Shelter and NFI gaps and strengthen camp coordination mechanisms and site facilitation in Borno and Yobe state through the activation of CCCM, Shelter/NFI activities which will provide a clear referral pathway for beneficiaries in order to ensure that sensitive cases are being referred to the correct partner in a swift and respectable manner, it will improve the camp infrastructure through urgently needed site upgrades and the establishment of shelter/NFI camp committees; and lastly it will build capacity of communities and local authorities on CCCM with good inclusion of community on shelter maintenance and NFI gaps identification. SHI will deploy dedicated camp management teams to the proposed locations. SHI is proposing to implement this project in Maiduguri, Konduga, Bama, Pulka, Gujiba, Fika and Potiskum LGAs of Borno and Yobe respectively targeting together 55,300 beneficiaries. The locations have been identified in close consultation with other CCCM/shelter/NFI/DMS sector and complement SHI’s ongoing operations in MMC.

1 - Needs

Series of camps-camp like setting/host communities are housing displaced population as a result of increase in the trend of displacement, reintegration, and relocation to various communities even with the long insurgent in the northeast continues to evolve. with DTM figures of above 55,000 individual in Rann (Kala/Balge) displaced/cut off, with little or no access to humanitarian assistance, and over 25,000 influx into MMC, Konduga, Jere and above 25,000 displaced in Fika and Buratai environs, as a result of military operation to retake the affected LGAs affected and also chronic poor living condition which is rated below sphere standard. SHI will Establish CCCM activities and provide shelter and NFI assistance to displaced population in MMC Host Communities, Konduga, Jere, Bama, Pulka, Fika and Potiskum LGA. This activities are aim at complementing and Government, UN-Agencies and INGOs activities in these locations.

2 - Activities

Activity 1.0 Reception centere in Ran: SHI will ensure adequate reception Centere management is in place and comprehensive first contact response to new arrivals is done, in close collabration with IOM DTM SHI will assist in the registration of new arrivals during and after biometric registration/verification and also take vulnerability assesment and effective referrals for further humanitarian assistance as available ti needs. activity 1.1 SHI will directly and in close consultation Stakeholders (NEMA/SEMA, IOM) , IDPs leaders take gap analysis and need assesment to identify critical/urgent needs and share results with Sector. Activity 1.2 SHI will establish effective coordination and prompt information sharing mechamism: this is achiveable by setting up of coordination mechanism, and maximun usage of CCCM Sector formal camp monitoring tool and monthly reporting to the sector; also establish bi-monthly camp coordination meeting. With once in a month meeting of different camp committee established, this is to give the community a lead in all intervention. Activity 1.3 Camp improvement, SHI will ensure the improvement and upgrade of site after site assesment in consultation with NEMA/SEMA, IOM and Community leaders, priorities will be placed base on urgent/critical needs identified, the upgrade will basically to reduce enviromental risk such as flooding mitigation, fire mitigation and other associated environmental risks. Activity 1.4 Establishment of Complain Feedback Mechanism (CFM): SHI will maintain a good/confidential CFM to enable beneficiaries make inquiry about interventions, be a guide to planning and make necessary complain and get response from partners involve, this will includes the creation of Complain desk, suggestion box, resolves issues desk and unresolved issues desk for proper accountability to partners. Activity 1.5 Provision of Shelter Assistance by taking in accurate level of assessment to ensure proper protection and dignifying the lives of the IDPs. Activity 1.6 Provision of Non Food Items to Household with low income and little access to NFI/livelihood, to ensure Children and women are not expose to exploitation of any kind sue to hardship, with the help of our NFI committees we will identify gaps and household with disable people that required special attention.
