Project Summary

This integrated education and protection project has been designed to ensure that emergencies and crises-affected children and youth in Awdal and Lower Juba Regions of Somalia have access to safe and protective learning environments. The intervention plans to implement a set of integrated activities consisting of: teaching and learning materials, teacher incentives, construction/rehabilitation of learning facilities, emergency school feeding, safe drinking water, sanitary kits for female learners, psycho social support and will also establish referral system in schools/TLS and community-based Child Protection Mechanisms for quick and efficient referrals of children with protection needs. This includes training, mapping of services and focal points within community and school (community child protection committees, School Management Committees, school CP focal points); Provision of community and school based psycho social support to the affected children and their families through CFS, school clubs, community groups activities, procurement of recreational and dignity kits.; Case management – for Unaccompanied and Separated Children through documentation, immediate care, referrals and family tracing and response to GBV survivors & Capacity building and life-saving messaging on prevention of family separation, child recruitment Risk Education, and other protection concerns- for teachers, community members, CP committees. This action will provide and support safe and free education that will help mitigate the impact of conflict and disasters by giving children and families a sense of normalcy, stability, structure and hope for the future. This action will address toxic stress quickly to help prevent the long term impact on a crises-affected children’s cognitive, behavioral, and emotional development. Furthermore, by supporting the children to go to school, carers have time to rebuild their livelihoods, re-establish sources of income, or simply come to terms with their experiences. The program will be implemented in synergy with other ongoing programs in Awdal and Lower Juba regions. Apart from the 7,308 girls and boys being directly targeted by the intervention, a further 1,800 indirect beneficiaries are targeted. This includes parents of the school-going children, members of host communities, families of the teaching fraternity and CECs, contractors and their families, water transporters and dependents, ADRA staff among other beneficiaries. This proposed intervention is aiming to provide education services sustainably, ensure learning opportunities, support retention and improve the learning environment for vulnerable learners while also providing a critical protective environment against child related violations. Post-monitoring and assessment visits by ADRA for the ongoing ECW project indicates the need to support children currently in school, as well as expand access to quality education for children currently out of school. In supporting the close working partnership between the Education and Protection Clusters, there is also need to provide education which covers the holistic needs of children, including an increased focus on child and gender friendly WASH facilities, hygiene promotion with a specific focus on prevention of AWD/Cholera, and increased focus on ensuring protection in schools addressing physical and psycho social protection needs for children and youth in schools.

Mitigation Measures

In order to mainstream protection in this intervention based on the risks identified in the protection analysis, the project intends to undertake the following operational mitigation measures:Location of facilities: The intervention will ensure that all facilities being provided i.e. learning spaces, WASH facilities, recreational spaces and the access routes to these facilities are safe and secure. The play areas will also be clearly visible put only in safe locations.Secure learning environment: As part of DRR, teachers and CEC members will be trained on first aid (including psychological first aid) and first aid kits will be made available by the intervention.Target groups: The intervention will be designed to ensure that services reach the most vulnerable population, to prevent discrimination or exclusion. Further, special advocacy will be made to target girls for equal access to education.Respond to specific needs of vulnerable learners: As part of teacher training, the intervention will train teachers on how to teach children with disabilities. Teachers will be at the very least be aware of any special needs of children with disabilities to facilitate learning and will be mindful of discrimination by other children.Accountability to affected people: The intervention proposes to set up accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct that arise during the program’s implementation.

Please provide further explanation

To ensure sustainability and continuity of the program and subsequently the durable solutions process, the intervention has proposed to work with a local NGO whose capacity will be built to be able to sustain the program. ADRA will provide consistent guidance throughout the intervention to ensure that all key stakeholders and partners maintain a common understanding of durable solutions in the project’s areas of implementation. A protective environment will be ensured through prioritizing safety and dignity by among other issues, ensuring the location of facilities is within a safe and accessible area. The intervention has been designed to link to the education sector strategic plans and objectives for Somalia and Somaliland which aim to ensure that all vulnerable populations have an access to education.
