Project Summary

To increase capacity of SRCS in Cash for WASH, PGI and CEA through field practice in the refugee camps of
Gedaref state.
Specific Objectives:
By increasing capacity in these 3 cross cutting sectors SRCS will be able, to:
1. Run targeted, quality and sustained WASH activities specifically linked to the context of Gedaref;
2. Gather learn lessons to be used as a baseline for scaling the activities for future implementations, capitalize field experience and build track records.
3. Focusing on the needs of the most at-risk groups the project will focus on an integrated approach
that will enhance dignity and access to WASH services. Through a CEA and PSEA approach, the
participation and safety of affected population will be mainstreamed in the whole response. This will
be done by coordinating closely with relevant NGOs, INGOs, PNs and other humanitarian actors currently promoting CEA, PGI, Cash for WASH components in the Gedaref camps.

2 - Activities/Outputs

1.WASH outcome: Improved access to, and use of, sustainable sources of safe water in targeted camps and host communities 2.PGI outcomes: Women, men, girls, elderly, disabled and boys have increased access to sustainable and adapted sanitation facilities and items and have improved personal and community hygiene practices, including menstrual hygiene management, with emphasis on behavior change. People with disabilities are empowered and supported through volunteer tandems to be able to function well in their daily activity. 3.CEA outcome: Improved participation, inclusion and accountability of the community in the targeted camps and host communities through participatory and inclusive activities and actions. CASH for WASH outcomes:1. Improved a Dignified access to hygiene items, facilities, water and sanitation based on gender, age or disability needs. 2. Increased access of affected persons to safe water and sanitation Facilities, and improved practice in hygiene promotion that meet Sphere Standards 3. Increased awareness of affected groups through CEA linkages to PGI. 4. Improved quality of program implementation capacity 5. Increased access to clean drinking water through coordinated vending6. Increased community per capita income through engaging community members /refugees to conduct repairs, WASH construction work, duties for direct pay.
