4 - Needs

Following the figures listed in the "WASH_PIN_Severity_HNO_2021" documents (7390 in Harasta-Rural Damascus & 4170 in Harbanifse-Rural Hama) & the urging need for providing the necessary infrastructure services to the selected areas of returnees, and the request from the authorities & responsible municipalities, a needs assessment was conducted in November 2020, as the the Syrian Society for Social Development was implementing other projects in the selected areas, consultations were undertaken by SSSD's staff addressing different segments of affected population of families of returnees & host community, resulting in prioritizing the rehabilitation of considerable damages in the sewage lines forming a huge obstacle facing the beneficiaries returning to their original residing areas. Rehabilitation of the damages will participate in providing the necessary services & creating the base for other shelter projects for returnees & host community.

5 - Implementation of activities

Following the findings of the needs assessment, official approvals have been obtained for the rehabilitation of the sewage lines in the selected areas. Procurement of material shall be done through a tender to be announced in the local market. & once the ﬁnancial fund is secured, implementation of the activities (Repair, rehabilitation and operational support to sanitation/sewage and solid waste management systems) shall be realized using local contractors, with supervision by the staff of the Syrian Society for Social Development from the main office in Damascus & the branch office in Hama. Monitoring and Evaluation shall be conducted by SSSD's engineering team following the technical speciﬁcations presented in the tender and the sector's guidelines. The handover of the project shall be done to the municipalities in the selected areas.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk/Threat: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks)./ Mitigating Measure: Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming.Protection Risk/Threat: Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases.Mitigating Measure: Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.
