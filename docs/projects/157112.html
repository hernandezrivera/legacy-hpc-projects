Project Summary

This will be SORCs third year of implementing RRR project and will be building on from the results of year two in line with the durable solutions and recovery program. In line with Sudan integrated response planning framework to promote sustainable return and reintegration, (This framework for an integrated response, developed by the RRR (Recovery, Return and Reintegration) Sector, brings together efforts and expertise of humanitarian and development actors in selected areas of return. The protracted nature of Sudan’s displacement has demonstrated that it is not sufficient to implement humanitarian and development activities in isolation, but that a sustainable response in support of return and reintegration requires closer cooperation among all stakeholders. Due to the increasing number of returnees Umdukhun villages priotized by the sector. . There are many actors in the location as IMC, TGH, IOM, UNDP/IRDS and others that we will collaborate with during our intervention. Gender is taken into consideration throughout the project activities implementation, first and foremost by prioritizing women-headed households, elderly, disabled and chronically ill and the youth. Women are traditionally marginalized groups within societal decision-making structures and female-headed households tend to be more vulnerable to food and livelihood insecurity due to even less access to existing resources and support as a result of the cultural prioritization of others and traditional gender roles. Risk of gender-based violence increases with reduced access to resources as women are compelled to travel further in search of food or to find cooking fuel. To reduce the burden shouldered by women during conflict and disaster situations. The project directly addresses Do no Harm principles by ensuring extensive communication with the community (including leaders, women, youth, and children) to determine the needs that can best be supported by SORC in the implementation period. SORC will link the project in the four targeted durable solutions villages of Selele, Aboujaradil, Sorey and Marari with other RRR programming that it started implementing since 2017-2019 in Magan, Garaya and Baltabei, coordinate with the above mentioned partners at the field level. However, procurement of items will be done locally and items budgeted were analyzed based on market surveys done.The aim of the project is to develop a commonly agreed evidence based approach for durable solutions, this was done through discussions with groups of women, men, youth and senior citizens culminated in their developing comprehensive Plans of action to help them overcome their constraints to solutions. SORC will continue its response in the RRR under activities related to ESNFI sector as recommended by the previous RRR sector lead, of which TGH were recommended for Livelihood, Education and WASH, CRS Health and nutrition, UNDP/IRDS Protection. These needs were identified during consultations and series of meetings and workshops with the community leaders, shieks, durable solutions team and RRR partners at the field level and state level.

Needs

SORC field surveys indicates housing situation and life skills and livelihoods are still precarious due to influx of returnees in the region/locality. other newly return households come in large numbers with only alternative of chopping/ cutting down trees for shelter construction, use for cooking and other domestic use. The targeted intervention will have greater potential for sustainable impact, as the sector recommend using an area and community based approach by strengthening skills of the beneficiaries to build transitional shelters in an environmental friendly manner, generate income opportunities by producing and selling of SSB blocks as well as FES and grass mats making and possibly generating household income. These information’s were collected through the assessment tools which are key informant interview and focus group discussion which are very effective to meet with community members and community leaders with consideration to gender which were conducted separately. focus group discussion for them to hear their voices and ideas on how we can address their needs. SORC will ensure 50% women are involved in the implementation of the project including strengthening life skills of the target beneficiaries to construct of transitional shelters in an environmental friendly manner as well as training on SSB production and other relevant IGA/livelihood opportunities as well as establisment of tree nursery for seedlings. disaster risk reduction and environmental conservation will be undertaken to ensure resilience is built with consideration to role of gender, skills built and environment is protected. The beneficiaries will be identified from among returnee’s community’s protection standard as direct beneficiaries based on Verification and assessments. The proposed activities will contribute to long-term improvement among the targeted households. Additionally, SORC will conduct consultations with the community jointly with partners and Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) and relevant line Ministries and support in the selection of trainees. The items provided during trainings, materials and start-up kits provided will enable community targeted sustain activities after SORC hands over.

Activities

• Conduct Verification for beneficiaries selection for all interventions• Facilitation of 20 SSB machines for production of blocks • Training of 400 persons on SSB block laying in the targeted four villages of (Selele, Sorey, Marari and Abugiradil) 50% females involved. Committees will be formed in each village to manage and maintain machines and ensure it belongs to all• Conduct public environmental awareness sessions and facilitate develop environmental awareness materials and disseminate to the communities of the targeted villages of Umdukhun locality targeting 9,824 persons.• Conduct training to individuals on transitional shelter construction method in an environmental friendly manner targeting 400 persons 50% females included.• strengthen and Facilitate training of FES and grass mats making training for new 800 women in targeted villages of umdukhun• Conduct 8 awareness sessions on Fire, flood awareness, Gender, HIV/AIDS for the targeted communities, targeting 800 people 50%women participation • Establishment and running four tree nursery for protection of environment.
