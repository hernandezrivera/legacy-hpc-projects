Project Summary

This integrated project plans to as its main objective providing access to safe protective and quality education for conflict affected children (age between 6-12) in 4 Public schools in Bama town of Bama LGA of Borno state.
The boys and girls aged between (6-12) who from the assessment conducted at the 7 IDP Camps and 2 Public primary schools where discovered to lack access to safe and protective learning environment, KABHUDA intends to provide access to these children through the following strategies. The IDP Camps are:
1 Bakasi camp
2 Bukarti Camp
3 Ngro Yalaye Camp
4 Market Area Camp
5 Boarding Primary Camp
6 Kirikasamma Camp
7 Bama Central Primary school
8 Bama central 2 Primary school
1. Provision of temporary classrooms at the 7 IDP camps in Bama LGA (20 Temporary Learning spaces(TLS)- exact location for the TLS are Bakkasi Camp, Nguro layabe camp and Market Area Camp
2 Renovation of 22 classes in Bama central and Bama central 2 primary schools
2. Provision of 14 latrines and 10 hand washing kits in the 7 IDP Camps and 2 Primary schools for the selected pupils in Bama LGA
3. Provision of teaching and learning supplies to 7000 pupils in the selected IDP camps and the 2 Public Primary schools
4. Training of 40 teachers
5 Training of 20 School Management Committee members(SMC)
6 Renovation of 22 class rooms in Bama central and Bama central 2 Public Primary schools
7 Engagement of 40 Volunteer teachers to teach in the 20 TLS meant for the IDP Children for 10 months
8 School based Hygiene Promotion sessions in the 20 TLS and 2 Public primary schools. 20 rounds of Hygiene promotion would be conducted over the 10 months’ project duration
9 Provision of recreation kits to 500 primary 1, 2, 3 female pupils
The goal of this integrated project is to provide a comprehensive intervention in partnership with government and relative stakeholder to addresses school structure gaps, WASH gaps, gaps on teaching and learning supplies and lack of teacher capacity. COVID-19 activities would be implemented to protect pupils, teachers, school management committee members and the general public from the deadly virus
