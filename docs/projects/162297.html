Project Summary

The security situation in Central Mali has further deteriorated, with an increasing number of incidents affecting civilians. Intercommunal conflicts and tensions continue to lead to population displacements. Violence has been taking place between the pastoralist and farming communities in the Center, leading to loss of several lives. According to the latest CMP’s data for April 2020 there were 250,998 IDPs in Mali, with about 45% of these in the Mopti and Segou regions.
There is an increasing proliferation of Small Arms and Light Weapons (SALWs), Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) and Improvised Explosive Devices (IEDs) due to the presence of violent extremist groups targeting the concentration of national and international armed forces in Central Mali. Civilian populations, using the same roads as the militaries have been hit by IEDs with several casualties. Facing this situation, people need really to be made safer through Risk Education direct sessions and radio messages.
In addition to the above, the food security situation has continuously deteriorated since 2016. The frequency of shocks and aggravating factors (climatic, environmental, economic, security) accentuates the deterioration of livelihoods for poor and very poor households and increases significantly their level of vulnerability, while greatly reducing their resilience. According to the “Cadre Harmonisé November 2019”, in the Mopti region 422,198 people are under IPC phase 2 (Stressed) and 158,881 people are already under IPC 3 (Crisis). Consequently, poor and very poor households need assistance to face the lean season and in longer term perspective to improve their resilience to recurring shocks.
Overall Project Objective: To Build Safer Communities and contribute to social cohesion, resilience, and peaceful coexistence in central Mali.
Objective 1 (Protection Cluster/HMA Sub-Cluster): Communities and individuals are safer and social cohesion is strengthened.
Objective 2 (Food Security/Livelihoods Cluster): Communities are more resilient, and the most vulnerable people have improved livelihood and food security.
Objective 3 (Protection Cluster/HMA Sub-Cluster): Communities and CSOs have strengthened capacities to mitigate risks and violent conflicts and support economic recovery.
Target groups: Underserved and insecurity affected communities, IDPs and returnees, Women headed households, very poor farmers and pastoralists, CSOs. 60,000 people.
To reach these objectives, the following activities will be implemented:
SO1: ERW/IED/SALW risk education, community safety action plans, microprojects to strengthen community safety (pastoral tracks, pastoral calendars, social cohesion events, disaster risk reduction), early warning, mediation & dialogue forum.
SO2: Community resilience action plans development & implementation (resilience microprojects: in-kind distribution of seeds, farming and fishing equipment and mills, creation/rehabilitation of family gardening plots, clearing of irrigation canals via cash for work, ponds, distribution/repairs of pumps and water pumps ), distribution in kind and in cash or vouchers to vulnerable households during the lean period, vocational training for the youth, support for local businesses.
SO3: Training of social cohesion committees on participatory evaluation, community safety planning, conflict prevention and conflict management, training of community focal points on risk education, training of teams on Community Violence reduction and ERW/IED/SALW Risk Education, capacity building of CSOs Mine Action.
COVID component: To save lives by preventing COVID-19 infection and supporting the most vulnerable communities in conflict zones in Mali through sensitization of populations, provide emergency assistance hygiene kits to the IDPs and host communities and facilitate their access to food through cash assistance and IGA to endure socio-economic impact of COVID-19 outbreak.
