Project Summary

The project works to re-activate the guidance units in the areas of Bennish where the necessary technical support shall be provided through competent staff and field tours through which:
- Land features shall be developed within the scope of work of the guidance unit, which includes (the area of the public land within the geographical area of the guidance unit - arable land area - uncultivable land area - forest area - irrigated land area – rain fed land area – area of unwooded land – area of wooded land) - to provide some pesticide services.
- Carrying out field trips to survey the water bodies existing within the geographical scope of the work of the guidance unit - number of licensed and unlicensed artesian wells.
- Counting livestock in the area and organizing schedules of vaccines and preventive immunizations.
- Organizing a schedule of field visits to poultry farms, beehives and medium and large cows’ farms.
- Carrying out field trips to survey the cultivated areas and determine the area of each cultivated crop and the number of crops (winter crops, winter vegetables, summer crops, summer vegetables, wooded areas, number of fruit trees and cultivated varieties of figs, olives, pomegranates, etc.) and giving the initial estimate of production at the type and species level.
- Carrying out veterinary visits to livestock and poultry breeders, beekeeping, counting the final numbers, organizing livestock numbers, follow-up of veterinary activities according to schedule and provision of technical and logistical support available from vaccines.
- Analysis of the data collected and organized in the tables on the Excel program at the level of one crop and the preparation of a report of all those data for inclusion in the statistical group and drawing of agricultural plans based on it.
- General census of livestock such as cows, sheep, goats, horses, rabbits, poultry, pigeons, fisheries and beehives. Developing a preliminary estimate of production of meat, milk, eggs and honey.
- Comparing plant and animal statistical data, estimating the amount of fodder produced from plant waste, the amount of concentrated feed needed, and providing the Fodder Establishment of the same in order to develop a plan for the manufacture or import of feed in accordance with the requirements of breeders.
- Carrying out field days and meetings with farmers and breeders to convey the guidance information and modern technologies to them and keeping them informed of the newly introduced agricultural crops or some newly approved agricultural transactions in the agriculturally developed countries, modern irrigation methods and the use of modern agricultural machinery or guiding them on methods of prevention from the spread of some diseases that affect cattle herds and guide them to implement the types of preventive vaccinations for livestock and others.

Needs

According to HRP draft report 2019, 9 million people are in need. 4.3m male and 4.8 female. 6.5 m are food insecure and 2.5 m are at risk of food insecurity.- As a result of the ongoing war in Syria1. The Syrian Government has deprived the liberated areas of agricultural land as a result of the shelling, which increased the cost of agricultural production of seeds, fertilizers and pesticides. Thus, the prices of agricultural and veterinary fertilizers multiplied and the farmer bore additional financial burdens due to the inflated prices of fertilizers and veterinary equipment in those areas.2. Random farming due to the lack of one agricultural plan at the level of the province, which led to an increase in the proportion of agriculture for some secondary crops at the expense of strategic crops, such as the high rate of cultivation of black seeds in the province of Idlib to 50% compared with the decline of wheat cultivation to reach 15% in 2016 while the percentage of wheat cultivation in the province of Idlib in 2013 was more than 40% of the proportion of crops planted in it and this is a dangerous indicator leading to the deterioration of food security in the region.3. The deterioration of the livestock situation and the increase in the cost of raising cattle to the breeders as a result of the regime preventing the arrival of vaccines, immunization, vaccination and the nitrogen liquid to the areas out of control and as a result of the irregular prices of these materials in those areas, which led to the owners of livestock to sell these livestock at low prices to get rid of the cost of breeding and the cost of their supplies.As a result of the absence of guidance and veterinary units, agricultural plans developed by the local councils and civil society organizations used to be developed based on approximate statistical figures, which led to:- The spread of randomization in the cultivation of crops: We may find varieties of Syrian wheat allocated to the first zone of stability that is grown in the third zone of stability and this reflects negatively on the production and deterioration of the qualities of the cultivar being grown in an area contrary to the area that was adopted for cultivation.- The increase in the number of times of fighting diseases and pests to no avail of the fighting and thus increasing production costs and low yield in the unit of cultivated area.- The addition of fertilizers in large quantities and randomly and at inappropriate dates, which led to higher production costs as well as deterioration of physical soil specifications and exposure to salinization soon due to the absence of agricultural guidance and non-analysis of soil before the addition of fertilizers.
