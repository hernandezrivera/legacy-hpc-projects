Project Summary

As the whole world is facing sever challenges such as climate changes, drought, dissertation, plant deteriorations and water crisis. These challenges become more effective and much harmful in fragile countries like Syria due to ten years of crisis. As the agriculture is the backbone of Syrian economic before the crisis, since Syria was one of the most important countries in the world in agricultural products such as olive and cotton…etc. FAO report 2008.
The project is taking the whole agricultural system elements in north Syria as an integrated compatible system, from this perspective, realizing that there are huge needs for taking lead and initiatives in order to move and transit into cleaner agricultural, watery, environmental system.
decentralized wastewater treatment plant which established in Azaz with a low cost, simple technology, zero energy requirement and environmentally friendly t, about 400 cubic meter per day may be used in irrigation purposes, it is estimated that the project will benefit 200 farmers directly. These farmers will be using the reclaimed wastewater (according to the Syrian Arab Standards and Metrology Organization (SASMO), SNS: 2752\ 2008) for irrigation purposes.
EPA will benefit from the previous projects and take the lesson learnt in the consideration, especially supporting the establishing of the, and water user’s association (WUA), then enable the water user’s association (WUA) to organize the schedule of irrigation and furthermore, to take over the operation and management responsibility of the plant through the training sessions.

4 - Needs

About 16 percent of the interviewed farmers could not access any source of irrigation water, therefore, adopting rainfed agriculture only in the 2020/21 season. However, more than half of the farmers who were able to secure water to irrigate their crops, agreed that the availability of irrigation water from all sources decreased compared to the previous season. Decreased availability was partially attributed to the lack of rains and decreasing water table, but also to the scarcity and high prices of fuel needed to operate water pumps,The lack of irrigation water from dams and rivers infrastructure, led to increased dependence on private groundwater wells on which 40 percent of the farmers rely for irrigation. Fuel shortages were singled out as the main obstacle of extracting groundwater. Farmers in the areas with difficult access to irrigation water rely on purchasing water in tanks which have become less available and more expensive. 9 - Protection Risk Analysis

EPA will work to generalize the four principles of protection, the aim of which is to avoid, reduce and mitigate the risks associated with the provision of humanitarian aid, and to emphasize that protection is everyone's responsibility. Amal organization will work to: Giving priority to safety and dignity and avoiding harm: The organization will ensure that the services provided are in accessible and safe places for all age groups, as the organization will work to secure transportation for people with disabilities in the event of benefiting from the activities, in addition to that, it will work to rehabilitate all its centers and put a special stand for people with disabilities to facilitate their access to services. The organization also worked on analyzing the protection risk matrix for all project activities to ensure that individuals are not harmed during or during the implementation of the project activities, and to ensure that these activities are commensurate with the needs of the BNFs and ensure respect for their rights and dignity. The organization will also employ its teams of both sexes and will encourage women to apply for the required vacancies to ensure and observe the customs and traditions in the local community. Thus, women can communicate with female employees in the project and benefit from the service without fear of friction with men or fear of preventing people from accessing services.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Adhere to an organization's code of conduct to protect people from sexual exploitation and abuse: The organization has strict policies and procedures related to sexual assault and harassment, child protection policy and code of conduct. The organization will work to train all its teams on these policies and circulate them to them and ensure that all their teams sign the codes of conduct and ensure that it's is not violated. Emphasis will also be placed on the necessity of educating the beneficiaries and reporting cases of the exploitation of women or children by humanitarian workers during distribution operations. We will also work to activate the complaints channels well and train the BNFs on them. The employees will be assured that there are strict procedures in the event of any kind of exploitation, and this matter will never be tolerated. -Accountability: Increase transparency, secure feedback and complaints mechanisms, reach it easily, and encourage people to reach it Consultation with all segments of society to reach the marginalized The organization will work to deliver services to all categories of women, men, children, the elderly, people with chronic diseases and the disabled without discrimination and within transparent and clear standards.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Military escalation on the area of operation: Azaz is located on the Turkish-Syrian boarders and considered as relatively safe, however, majority of the equipment were designed to be removable, then to be transferred to another location knowing the BNFs can be relocated and continue their businesses. The distribution points will be chosen in consultation of the Safety and security department and to avoid crowded locations as well as the military headquarters. Sexual exploitation for women within the project. All the staff will be oriented on PSEA and will sign on code of conducted. All the contracted suppliers must sign on the code of conduct as well. Spread of COVID-19 among BNFs and staff: Both organizations' staff are trained on the protective measure of COVID-19. Sanitizers and cleaning materials will be procured for both staff and BNFs. Environmental harm resulted from the intervention: There will not be any chemicals or emissions within our activities, and all the activities were designed to be friendly to the environment. Conflict among IDPs and HC The BNFs will be mixed between IDPs and host community to avoid and conflict or sensitivity.
