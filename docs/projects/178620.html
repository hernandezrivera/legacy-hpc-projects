Project Summary

The Syrian conflict has dramatically influenced the whole Syrian community, whereas war and crisis has affected society in general and women in particular. Thus, millions of Syrians became IDPs who forced to flee their houses and internally moved to other areas beside to other who immigrated externally. Many vulnerable groups appeared as a component of Syrian society matrez. This project aims to target almost 25.000 beneficiaries both individual and families. The project is designed to include all target group taking into consideration GAM analysis ensuring the principle no one behind. The project designed to support people and provide them with their basics need insuring food security, since many people could not access to food resources regularly.
The project activities will support and cut cross with FSL cluster framework that will be implemented in Idleb, since the main activities will be like, provide emergency response to crisis affected vulnerable people with short- term food assistance, during distributing food voucher that match with people needs, moreover, Provide persons with disabilities and elderly with supplementary food assistance through exact amount of food packages and kits. In addition to, project aim to Supply flour to bakeries in order to produce bread that distributed freely. Finally, supporting small-scale food production since project looking for funding micro project-cash for work that could strength community resilience. Accordingly, the detailed action plan will be set, however set of certain activities will be implemented which are: 1- A base-line assessment will be conducted in the targeted locations to assess the exact needs and beneficiaries according to GAM analysis and appropriate modalities. 2- Signing MOU with the Local Councils. 3- Select the project team, the gender criteria will be taken into consideration. 4- Train the selected field team officers on basics of direct dealing with beneficiaries, mechanisms, protection list analysis. 5- Procurement of the required materials and products. 6- Provision of the technical capacity building for the beneficiaries 7- MEAL and reporting.8- Exit plan of project. Accordingly, this project come to reflect a set of activities as a combination of ongoing activities that Share foundation already got a certain amount of fund to cover the activities, it worth to mention that activities are implemented as campaigns to support families in Ramadan.

4 - Needs

Under international human rights law, the right to food is part of everyone's right to an adequate standard of living. "The realization of the right to food takes place when every man, woman and child, alone or with other persons, at all times has access to adequate food or the means to purchase it." This right consists of four main components: availability, accessibility, adequacy, and sustainability, which require the provision of food "in a quantity and quality sufficient to meet the nutritional needs of individuals, the food being free of harmful substances and being acceptable in a specific cultural context", and making food available "in sustainable ways. In addition to, according to the World Food Program, at least 12.4 million Syrians, out of an estimated population of 16 million, lacked food security, an alarming increase of 3.1 million in one year. FAO and the World Food Program estimate that 46% of Syrian families have reduced their daily food rations, and 38% of adults have reduced their consumption to ensure children get enough food. According to HNO 2021 report the most prioritized needs are food assistant, agriculture, livelihood, Livestock and Irrigation. Those needs are composed based on people needs to be addressed. Therefore, Share Foundation is designing the project based on FSL comprehensive needs to contribute to security food achievement. Share set activities according to our previous experiences and lesson learnt, our activities go around Cash for food, Voucher for food, and multipurpose cash in the areas where markets are functional. In addition to, Food basket, Ready to Eat Ration, Bread distribution and bakery support.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The risks arising could be under the following possible scenarios: 1. Some beneficiaries, as resident families, are excluded from the distribution of food aid and the service,which is limited to IDPs. This could cause a large gap between the people and greater sensitivity and problems. Therefore, the lists of beneficiaries must be prepared according to real standards that take into account the needs of everyone and ensure that no one is left behind. Also, the selection criteria must be clarified for the population and their participation also in setting standards, and this enhances their sense of social accountability. 2. The risk of discrimination on gender and the emergence of cases of domestic violence or sexual harassment or GBV, as women have become forced to search and access food sources, and this exposes them to possible violence from others, whether due to having to go to distribution sites or to move to far distances. Therefore, the groups must be involved in choosing the appropriate sites for them to obtain the aids and services provided. The project may look to provide mobile distribution points to ensure access to various target areas, thus facilitating access and obtaining aid. 3. The risk of excluding persons with disabilities and elderly persons due to the difficulty of their movement and access to the delivery sites. Therefore, from the stage of designing activities, these groups must be taken into consideration and there should be a survey for them to document their presence and their locations of residence, and the project must ensure that services reach them in order to preserve their dignity and privacy.
