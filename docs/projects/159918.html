Project Summary

The purpose of this project will be to increase food availability and food access for drought-affected communities through the provision of water sources in selected locations namely Bari, Galgaduud, Juba Hoose (Lower) and Nugaal.
The project will carry out the following activities:
1. Community mobilization
2. Identification and approval of the water infrastructure and allocation
3. Selection and biometric registration of the beneficiaries to specific Water Catchment rehabilitation areas.
4. Providing a biweekly photographic report of the training, work done; and payment of beneficiaries
The project is targeted towards achieving the following goals
1. Training of 10% of the direct beneficiaries’ in water catchment management for food security
2. To increase the area under irrigation within the affected areas that are close to the water catchment areas.
3. Improving livelihoods of 2700 direct beneficiaries and 11,000 indirect beneficiaries in the various households.
4. Improving the livelihoods of 30% of direct beneficiaries being women through the cash transfer program.
5. Reduced cases of sexual harassment cases in the regions due to improved access to water and sensitization through training and intervention measures in conjunction with the community leaders.

Project Activities

The project will be geared towards resolving the following two objectivesFOOD SECURITY OBJECTIVE 1: Improve households’ immediate access to food through the provision of conditional and unconditional assistance depending on the severity of food insecurity phases, vulnerability and seasonality of the livelihoodsFOOD SECURITY OBJECTIVE 3: Support household and community resilience to shocks, through rehabilitation and/ or restoration of productive assets, enhance livelihood strategies and disaster preparednessWater is life and food security and availability cannot be achieved without access to water and as such, this project will be geared towards ensuring access and expansion of water sources for food production and livestock use in the affected areas by rehabilitation and creation of new water catchment areas in the affected areas.The project activities will include community mobilization activities including informing communities about the project intent and scope as well as criteria for selecting beneficiaries and water catchments with food production capability. The selection of the beneficiaries will be on the basis of vulnerability (female-led households, IDPs, people with disability, e.t.c.) as well as a minimum of 30% of women participation per community in the identified areas. Project implementation activities will include the following activities:1. Community mobilizationThe activities will include:Liaising with SPs that will be implementing interventions of agriculture inputs distribution and of food sector support in the same areasMobilizing and sensitizing regional and district authorities Mobilizing and sensitizing village communities especially in relation to acceptance and supporting women and persons with disability Mobilizing and sensitizing the community on the importance of protection of women and children from sexual exploitation and harassment.2. Identification and approval of the water infrastructure and allocation Providing FAO with the list communities and targeted households3. Selection and biometric registration of the beneficiaries to specific Water Catchment rehabilitation areas Selecting and communicating with FAO household focal points and infrastructure to be rehabilitated with photographs taken before rehabilitation.Collection of information and location of persons with disabilities and cataloging a list of their needs which can then be cascaded down to the other partner agencies through open information sharing. Providing feedback mechanisms and being transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organizational procedures, and processes that affect them. Setting up accessible, confidential and well-understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improving programming, understanding community perceptions, promoting beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. E.g. place feedback surveys in food baskets, feedback box at the distribution site, phone lines Setting up mechanisms for submitting feedback that does not require the beneficiary exposing themselves to project staff. Responding to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place. Organizing awareness-raising sessions so that people know how it works. 4. Providing a biweekly photographic report of the training, work done; and payment of beneficiaries Supervising infrastructure rehabilitation work Recording of payment slips and receipts for paid beneficiaries Liaising with main food vendors in the markets where beneficiaries purchase their food items in order to ascertain the availability of basic food commodities and commodity price information Submitting an Interim Narrative Report and upon completion of 40% rehabilitation of planned activities. The report will include photographic evidence of the progress of rehabilitation works

Mitigation Measures

Lens 1: This problem can be mitigated by doing the following:Working with the community leaders to identify multiple distribution areas in the affected regions where people can access humanitarian aid.Identifying central locations to ensure beneficiaries should not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points. Ensuring that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable by identifying and prioritizing the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalized groups. Making sure beneficiaries know how to obtain food assistance and livelihood opportunities, where and when the distributions will take place so that they can select places closest to them.Communicating information through various means to reach the broader community and to account for different literacy levels Ensuring the location, distance and access routes to the distribution points is accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. Introducing special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or are unable to carry the rations.Lens 3: Blacklisting individuals who perpetrate sexual violence from receiving assistance and cascading the information to other donor agencies and groups.Ensuring that the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. Separating male and female queuing areas and ensure female staff presence at distributions so that they are accessible to women and girls. Monitoring and responding to potential exploitative practices or abuse of power by employers in livelihoods projects Being transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organizational procedures, and processes that affect them. Setting up accessible, confidential and well-understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understanding community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. Setting up mechanisms for submitting feedback that does not require the beneficiary exposing themselves to project staff. Responding to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place. Organizing awareness-raising sessions so that people know how it works. Considering a joint feedback mechanism with other sectors to minimize confusion. Ensuring that staff and relief committees are representative of all groups within the community and all staff and members receive protection mainstreaming training. Ensuring all our staff, implementing and working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. Ensuring all staff and volunteers involved in distributions and in direct contact with beneficiaries have received training in the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuseLens 4: Working with the local leaders and protection agencies to ensure that unscrupulous groups or individuals do not take advantageEnsuring the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution and plain-clothed manned security personnel. Identifying central locations to ensure that the beneficiaries do not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points. Ensuring distributions take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall. Blacklisting individuals who affect impartiality and cascading the information to other donor agencies and groups to ensure that undue influence is not encouraged this should be done in secret to avoid retaliation from such individuals and groups.
