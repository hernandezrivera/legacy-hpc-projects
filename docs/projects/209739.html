Project Summary

This project aims to address the urgent needs of Syrian women in Hama Governorate who have been affected by the conflict and earthquake impact.These women face various challenges including displacement,lack of access to basic services,limited economic opportunities,and psychosocial trauma.Based on the previous need assesment we did, we found that the situation of women in Hama affected by war, earthquake, and deteriorating economic conditions represents major challenge and requires urgent intervention to enable them to overcome these obstacles.The project seeks to empower these women through skills training,livelihood support,psychosocial support,and community engagement activities.Through building the resilience of Syrian women in Hama Governorate,the project aims to improve their well-being, enhance their economic opportunities,and promote their active participation in rebuilding their communities.
By ensuring that the project is also inclusive of PwDs and vulnerable populations “who may face challenges in accessing services and livelihood opportunities”, it promotes a more accessible and equitable approach to humanitarian assistance.This helps to ensure that all individuals,regardless of their abilities,have equal access to support and resources.many women in Hama Governorate also have disabilities or care for family members with disabilities.By addressing the needs of PwDs, the project can have a broader impact on the well-being and resilience of the entire community.This intervention aims to empower targeted women by preparing them for the labor market through training courses and workshops that teach essential skills.Additionally,the project provides home-based job opportunities and supports education to enhance women's skills and improve their future prospects.Furthermore,the intervention includes mental health support for women,offering psychological and social support,counseling.Through these activities,the project seeks to enhance the resilience and well-being of women.1.The project will organize training courses and workshops on various topics such as vocational skills,entrepreneurship,financial literacy,and personal development.These sessions are conducted by experienced trainers and experts in their respective fields to provide women with practical knowledge and skills that are relevant to the labor market.
2.Home-Based Job Opportunities: To create income-generating opportunities for women,the project facilitates home-based job opportunities such as handicrafts production,sewing,embroidery,and other skills-based tasks that can be done from home.This allows women to earn a livelihood while balancing their responsibilities at home.3.Mental Health Support: the project offers psychological and social support services for women.This includes counseling sessions,group therapy,stress management workshops.Throughout the implementation of the activities,the project conducts regular monitoring and evaluation to assess the impact of the interventions,gather feedback from participants,and make necessary adjustments to improve the effectiveness of the program.Protection Risk Analysis:in this context of interventions,we will identify and address potential risks and vulnerabilities that women may face during the implementation of the activities. Here are PRA for each type of activity:1.Training Courses and Workshops:Ensure safe and accessible training venues for women, considering security risks-Address cultural and social barriers to women's participation.2.Home-Based Job Opportunities:Assess safety for women working from home,especially in risky areas.Provide info on labor rights,safety measures,and reporting mechanisms.Establish communication channels for reporting concerns.3.Mental Health Support:Ensure confidentiality in mental health services.Train staff on trauma-informed care for survivors.Collaborate with local organization for specialized services.4. M&E:-Establish data protection protocols for participant information.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

PSEA Strategy: Here are some key components tailored to the interventions mentioned previously:1.Training Courses and Workshops:-Conduct comprehensive PSEAtraining for all staff,including trainers and facilitators, emphasizing zero-tolerance for sexual exploitation and abuse-Establish clear reporting mechanisms for any concerns or incidents of sexual exploitation and abuse during training sessions-Include information on PSEAprinciples and reporting procedures in participant materials and orientation sessions.2.Home-Based Job Opportunities:-Implement measures to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse in home-based work settings,such as regular monitoring visits and check-ins with women working from home.-Provide information on how to report any instances of sexual exploitation or abuse, ensuring confidentiality and protection for survivors.3.Mental Health Support:-Integrate PSEAprinciples into mental health services,emphasizing the importance of ethical conduct,boundaries, and respect for survivors-Develop protocols for handling disclosures of sexual exploitation or abuse during mental health sessions,ensuring survivors receive appropriate support and referrals-Provide training for mental health professionals on trauma-informed care and ethical practices when working with survivors of sexual exploitation and abuse.4.Monitoring and Evaluation:-Include indicators related to PSEAin monitoring and evaluation frameworks to track compliance with safeguarding policies and procedures-Conduct regular audits and reviews of PSEAmeasures to identify gaps and areas for improvement in protecting beneficiaries from sexual exploitation and abuse-Encourage feedback mechanisms that allow beneficiaries to report concerns or incidents of sexual exploitation or abuse safely and confidentially.Assumptions:1.Willingness to Participate:It is assumed that women will be willing and able to participate in the project and engage in activities aimed at building their resilience.2.Supportive Community:There is an assumption that the community in Hama will be supportive of the project and its goals to empower women.3.Supportive Environment:There is an assumption that the project environment, including staff, volunteers, and community members, will be supportive of targeted women and PWDs and their empowerment goals.4.Capacity Building:It is assumed that targeted women and PWDs have the capacity and potential to actively engage in empowerment activities and contribute meaningfully to the project.3.Sustainable Impact:It is assumed that the project's interventions will have a lasting impact on the resilience and well-being of participating women.Implementing this project to support and empower women is crucial due to the impact of the Syrian war, economic consequences, and increased responsibilities on women and disabled individuals. This project aims to provide opportunities for economic independence, enhance their skills, and promote gender equality for a better future.
