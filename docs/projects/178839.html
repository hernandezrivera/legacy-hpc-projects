Project Summary

Takaful Al-Sham Charity Organization will work through this project on life-saving services to IDP gatherings through providing multi-sectoral assistance, to improve their living conditions and help them to cope with harsh conditions of the harsh displacement and winter. Through this intervention, Takaful will conduct need assessments on a wide-range of sectors to ensure covering all needs and requirements inside IDP sites. The results will show gaps and will describe the actual quantities required to cover this gap. than Takaful will cover the needs according to available fund within the organization, but as for the gaps that cannot be covered by Takaful, a referral system will take place to ensure coordinating with other active NGOs to deliver the missing services, making sure to cover the targeted site with full package of services and coordinating with relevant clusters. This intervention will take place in 5 IDP site inside Maaret Tamsrin, Idleb, Harim, Dana, and Salqin subdistricts. Total estimated beneficiaries will be 3600 HHs.
Regarding the fire mitigation measures, Takaful is planning to cover 10 IDPs sites with full package of fire mitigation related services through constructing firefighting points inside the camps, as well as creating a team trained and assigned for fighting fire incidents with the required trainings and tools, finally spreading the awareness on how to decrease the accuracy of the fire incidents and how to protect lives when they occur. the project is planning to target camps in Daret Azza, Atareb, Al Bab, Afrin, A'zaz, subdistricts. Total estimated beneficiaries will be 3000 HHs (3375 men, 3600 women, 4350 boys, 3675 girls).
Takaful during 2021, beside delivering life-saving activities and emergency response, will work on increase the self-reliance and resilience of IDPs inside camps, through creating opportunities for them to secure a source of income to support their families, this is planned to take place within their residing sites (inside camps) through create short term job opportunities to camp residents through implementing maintenance and care activities to the infrastructure available inside camps, including maintaining latrines, roads, water networks, sewage networks, lighting, lands and basically all public service assets. The population benefitting from maintenance are 4000 HHs (4500 men, 4800 women, 5800 boys, 4900 girls). as for the population that will benefit from short term job opportunities are 400 individuals. the targeted location for these 2 components will be Maaret Tamsrin, Idleb, A'zaz, Harim, Dana, and Salqin subdistricts.
In the same context of empowering self-reliance, Takaful Is aiming In this project to support the self-governance of 20 IDPs sites, by supporting the creation of local bodies that are participatory management committees (including active participation by women). this will be followed with consistent support for these local bodies Including relevant trainings' delivery, handover of required tools and assets, etc.
Takaful will monitor the implementation of activities done by contractors or by contracted workers on a daily basis, to ensure quality and consistency of performance.

4 - Needs

The Syrian conflict enters its tenth year, there are more than 2.7 million IDPs in North West Syria (NWS). IDPs now represent 64 percent of the population living in NWS. According to the NWS Humanitarian Response Plan as of 4 September 2020, 2.8 million people need humanitarian assistance in NWS and 220,000 to remain at immediate risk of displacement. According to REACH, as they carried out a Camps NA in Feb 2020, only 48% of households reported that they were able to access markets inside camp to secure their needs.TAS conducted a Market NA in Dana and Idleb targeted camps, by meeting KI in Feb 2021 it was found out that the main source of all the residents is humanitarian assistance. KIs mentioned that none of the residents has received training for a capacity-building before and that they need it. KIs suggested stores that are needed for residents and community. Some of which: clothing selling stores, foodstuff stores, vegetable and fruit shops, pharmacies, meat shops, and shops for selling detergents.The need to provide camps with fire points and improving infrastructure comes from the large number of incidents that happen inside camps, according to CCCM just in March 2021 reported on wind incidents that impacted 168 sites as well as fires in 18 sites and floods in 15 sites. From 2019-2020, the number of Syrians living in IDP sites in NWS has increased from 868,486 IDPs (158,634 families) across 483 sites to 1,463,297 IDPs (280,698 families) living in 1,104 sites according to the CCCM Cluster. According to CCCM ISMM Report, number of people living in IDP sites is:Girls <18 426,978 , Boys<18 - 433,359 ,Women adoults 337,528 men adoults 297,552 totalal individuals ,1495,551 total families 287,071 PwD 23,174
