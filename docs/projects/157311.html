Project Summary

The proposed project aims to intervene in three sectors, education, WASH and Child Protection in targeted localities in North Darfur (ND) state (Zamzam, Tawila, Kutum, Melit, Elfasher and Dar Elsalam). The project will focus on strengthening the existing education systems and increase access to basic education, responding to the immediate and critical needs of children at school promoting to improve the school environment in three project targeted localities (Darasalam, Elfasher and Kutum). The project will provide different schools materials in addition to establishing, rehabilitating and constructing learning spaces, gender sensitive latrines and fencing (girls’ schools) in selected schools. The project will also provide trainings in core subjects for teachers, PTAs training and TOT training on main subjects in addition to schools awareness sessions in selected topics.
The project will respond to critical needs on Water, Sanitation and Hygiene in the targeted localities (Zamzam, Tawila, Kutum, Melit and Dar Elsalam) in ND. The project will work to increase the access to improved WASH services, through rehabilitation and construction of WASH facilities, including potable water Sanitation facilities, gender sensitive communal latrines in addition to hygiene promotion activities in targeted locations, thereby reducing the prevalence of related water and vector-borne disease. The project will Supporting state and non-state actors’ in ensuring access and continuation to safe gender responsive formal and non-formal emergency education services in line with EIE standards.
The Child Protection intervention will focus on delivering gender responsive lifesaving services to boys and girls affected by protracted emergency in line with CPMS and in coordinated manner in (Zamzam, Tawila, Kutum, Melit and Dar Elsalam) in ND and (Dalling, Elrief Elshargi, Habilla, and Kadogli, Talodi) in South Kordofan. The focus will be on strengthening the community systems i.e. community based child protection networks (CBCPN), work with Social Welfare to strengthen their capacity on prevent and response to physical and sexual violence against boys and girls through training on case management systems to provide quality assistance), promote positive behavior change within households and communities on issues of monitoring and reporting of grave child rights violations, child labor, trafficking, migration, sexual exploitation and abuse; building children’s confidence and understanding to decide on the specific issues which affect their protection and wellbeing , awareness sessions on child rights/protection including (importance of children’s physical, mental, social health and well-being as PSS, risks domestic violence and GBV, Traditional Harmful Practices such as child marriage and female genital mutilation (FGM), and other risks); ensuring better safeguarding for children and youth will be a core pat of the project. The intervention also ensuring Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) with focus on communication with communities (CwC) by assessing the existing communication channels/systems, documenting the good practices and lessons learnt on CwC for scale ups.

Needs

In ND, approximately 14,410 boys and girls out of 33,876 vulnerable people in (Tawila, Kutum, Mellit and Darasalam), are in need of humanitarian assistance in protection, education and WASH services due to the deteriorating situation in the context of protracted displacement. There are 32,851 (16,754 females, 16,097 males) children at school age in 55 schools, have poor access to safe and quality basic education. Nearly 41 schools out of the 55 school lack the INEE standards for teaching and learning, limited access to gender sensitive latrines (separate latrine for boys and girls), gaps in blackboards and recreational materials, lack of text books (4 to 5 per book), teachers are untrained in delivering the basic syllabus, most schools don’t have adequate drinking water, WASH facilities in schools not with minimum standards, lack of access to dignity kits where girls are obliged to miss class during the monthly periods. Gender roles require girls to spend most of their time in household chores limit their performance; while boys are withdrawing from school and engaged in child labor to provide financial support to families, women are less represented in Parent Teacher Association committee (PTA).In ND and SK children are in need of protection form all forms of abuse exploitation and neglect, this because the CP system is lacking serious reporting and responding mechanisms at community and locality levels including updating of referrals, social welfare and CSOs not able to management the individual cases of children in need of protection, children in need of community based PSS where life skills is delivered, adolescent boys and girls are the main drivers of labor for HH needs. Movement among children (adolescents) increased and puts them at risk of worst forms of child labor, exploitation, dropouts, recruitment, addiction, and drug abuse. Physical and humiliating punishments affected children’s development. CP awareness highly needed in a way that help communities better care and manage childhood and improve their socialization techniques.In ND, there are gaps in access to water and sanitation services at community level. Household (HH) collect approximately 36 liters of water per day per HH, i.e. only 6 liters of water (Person/day). Many communities use unprotected wells with poor quality of water without water treatment process, leaking of latrines close to the water points contaminate the water. Maintaining proper personal hygiene and sanitation habits is challenging given the scarcity of adequate and safe water sources, and behaviors of washing hand at critical times without soap; lack of sanitation facilities; common practice of open defecation; mismanagement of children feces; lack of water collection and storage containers; and unsafe control of domestic animals. The poor WASH situation results in spread of water borne diseases. Recurrent prevalence of diseases remains a risk for children and pregnant mothers.

Activities

Education Activities Provision of school materials/ Rehabilitating and construction of learning spaces/ Construction of gender sensitive latrines/ Fencing (girls’ schools) in selected schools/ Provide trainings for teachers, PTAs training/ Conduct TOT trainings on main subjects/ Conduct schools’ awareness sessions in selected topics.WASH Activities:Rehabilitation of water distribution pipelines/ Upgrade 20 Hand pumps/ Construction of 5 distribution points/ Construction and rehabilitate gender sensitive communal latrines in the targeted localities (1200)/ Training of community leaders and WASH committees using PHAST approach/ Distribution of personal hygiene kits including (540), hand washing facilities (450) and 2000 Jerri cans (targeting girls, vulnerable HHs and selected schools)/ Conduct 10 community awareness session by using GBV key message in target communities/ Conduct hygiene promotion campaigns (12 per Location)/ Conduction of 4 media/ radio messages and sessions on public health and hygiene promotion.Child Protection Activities:Train 100 (50F 50 M) CBCPNs members, 60 leaders and 60 women groups members on CR/CP, reporting and responding to CP concerns/ Train 60 (30 F 30 M) Social workers on case management and referrals/ Establish and train 4 children clubs on CR/CP, leadership and decision making and support them with necessary equipment/ Provision of recreational materials for community based PSS and life skills/ Conduct 40 awareness sessions on CP risks facing children/ Train 75 (36F 35M) CSO staff on safeguarding policy, code of conduct and do-no-harm/ Train 120 WASH and education volunteers of partners on development and use of key CP prevention messages/ Develop 2 reporting and feedback mechanism in coordination with all communities segments to agree on CRM system/ Provision of in-kind assistance for 35 vulnerable households in SK/ Provision of vocational training for 200 Girls and 200 Boys and provide start-up kits.

Impact on environment/natural resources

Project has medium impact on the environment:The construction of superstructures for latrines could possibility impact the environment as the targeted beneficiaries will be driven to cut more trees and affect the ecosystem negatively.The improper solid waste management and drainage could also risk contaminate the soil and pollution of the underground waterThe rehabilitation of the hand pumps and the distribution of the hand washing and drinking water facilities could possibly contribute to the over use of the water Mitigation measure:This project will support the environment, by decreasing the burden of communicable disease and reduce the flies, and mosquito through safe disposal of the solid waste through awareness creation on the negative impact of cutting trees in the long run, hence environmental preservation awareness sessions will be carried out alongside the construction of the latrines, so that the community members are advised to use some of the environmental friendly materials and especially non-fired bricks as well as using concrete slabs to significantly reduce the use of timber on the traditionally wooden slabs Environmental education and awareness components will be integrated into community sensitization programmes as well as integrated during awareness session on child protection issuesProper disposal of the menstrual hygiene itemsThere are many areas where natural environment is predicted to improve because of current policies and programs; it is expected that these improvements will have a positive effect on human health and the environmentRegarding the over-use of the water the project will include in the communities sensitization and mobilization awareness sessions on the importance of water conservation and water resource management to maintain sustainable water supply. Reuse of the grey waters for other purposes such as to irrigate some of the vegetable gardensInclude in the design good drainage system to reduce the transmission of the water prone diseases
