Project Summary

The project aim at providing shelter reinforcement kit and shelter repairs to meet the need of the most vulnerable households in need of shelter support in Maiduguri and Jere.
In MUNA DA'ALTI 2A, MUNA GARAGE EL BADAWE and FARM CENTER camps with estimated number of 450HH, 402HH and 153HH respectively (as per sector needs) in need of shelter repairs. The aim is to reach out to those living in the bad quality makeshift.
The project will engage the beneficiaries with carpentry training on shelter repairs and maintenance which is aimed at providing them with livelihood support through the cash for work strategy to enable them get cash to cover their needs.
An estimated 5025 individuals living in bad shelters in the three camps are in deplorable conditions as some household are already living in shelters with inadequate structures which is not inline with the sphere standard.
This project will target 15,000 beneficiaries for the shelter reinforcement/emergency shelter kit and NFI kit to fill gaps in IDP camps situated in Jere.
More so, their poor living conditions does not allow any privacy and consequently even the protection of the most vulnerable (mostly single women and children) is not guaranteed.

Activities

Activity 1: Identification of the beneficiaries among the most vulnerable families from within the camps Activity 2: Sensitization within the target communities about the limitation of the response and also on measures of protection against violence. Activity 3: Distribution of emergency shelter repair/kits materials and NFI Kit and repair work/ providing shelter repair assistance to respond to the specific shelter support needs of the affected population . Activity 4: conducting awareness session on shelter repair and distribution of shelter items to beneficiaries Activity 5: Monitoring of the project implementation process and reporting Activity 6: Overall assessment of project implementation and final report
