Project Summary

The project started in April 2017, it is funded by the German Federal Foreign Office. It was registered under the OPS ID number SYR-17/F/111664/R. For 2019 it received a topping up of 8,527,990 € (8,185,990 € from GFFO, 342,000 € WHH contribution). The project was prolonged until 31 March 2020. The total budget for the project since April 2017: 15,972,000 € (GFFO: 15,388,240 €; WHH: 583,760 €)
Plan for 01 Jan 2019 - 31 March 2020:
Overall objective: Improve the life situation of Syrian civil war victims.
Project objective: IDPs have access to food and the pressure on communities is reduced
Result 1: Improving the performance of local bakeries to provide sufficient basic food items to vulnerable households in conflict areas of Northern Syria
R1: Indicator 1.1: 2 bakeries are repaired, and the bread capacities increased.
R1: Indicator 1.2: Number of distributed bread rations
R1: Indicator 1.3: 21,500 HH (107,500 persons) have benefited from bread distributions.

Result 2: Improving the food security of the most vulnerable households and reducing the dependency on negative coping mechanisms through emergency aid
R2: Indicator 2.1: 10,850 HH (54,250 persons) received food support in form of vouchers for several months so that each family member had access to 1,930 kcal / person / day.
R2: Indicator 2.2: Harvest yields of 1,300 HH (6,500 persons), which were supported in the production of vegetables for their own needs.

Result 3: Establishment of a small emergency aid operations structure in order to be able to react more quickly to internal distortions.
R3: Indicator 3.1: Number of civil war victims who have been supplied with a certain number of relief supplies. With the reallocation, 18,600 HH (93,000 persons) food baskets, cloth vouchers, food vouchers, NFI kits, cooked meals, hygiene kits etc. (10,000 food baskets, 1,200 cloth vouchers and 1,400 food vouchers shall be provided for 2018 period.)

Needs

WHH TRK has close ties with relevant clusters like CCCM, FSL and WASH etc.. WHH prepared an a priori response plan for the winterization activities. The FSL Cluster coordinator informed WHH that in some locations in Idleb, approximately 9,000 HH have not received any assistance for an extended period. According to the recently released Idleb # 1 Situation Report on September 18, 2018, almost 67% of people in Idleb need assistance. Among those people, 37% are acutely food insecure and 30% are at the risk of food insecurity. Furthermore, 58% of the total population face obstacles in access to food, primarily due to safety and security reasons. For the winterization activities of 2018, WHH has planned the distribution of 10,000 food baskets, 1,400 HH will receive 3-month food voucher assistance and 1,200 HH one-time winter clothing activities are envisaged. The designated activities are to be implemented with Ihsan R&D and in close coordination with UN clusters and other stakeholders.

Implementation of activities

Activities: The free bread distribution, food voucher distribution, bakery rehabilitation, food basket distribution and micro gardening activities are the continuation of the existing project SYR 15.17. Additional activities are taken over by SYR 15.17 from the AA funded projects 25.15 and 26.16, because we want to separate the Turkey and Syria projects geographically.Free Bread Distribution: Bread is still highly demanded from people in need as a supplementary food. According to evaluation reports of the project SYR 15.17, targeted people are highly satisfied with the free bread activity and want this activity to continue. Therefore, it is envisaged to continue with free bread distribution with Ihsan RD (16,000 households) and PIN (5,500 hh) for the consecutive 12 months.Food Voucher Distribution: People in the targeted areas face difficulties in terms of accessing food. According to WFP standards, everyone should take minimal 2,100-2,500 kcal/day. Otherwise, they will be prone to serious health problems. Thus, based on Survival Minimal Expenditure Basket (SMEB) estimates, each vulnerable household shall be provided with monthly vouchers. This activity is the continuation of the ongoing project SYR 15.17 with existing Partners (10,860 hh) for 12 months.E-voucher Distribution: This activity will be a 3-month pilot activity with Ihsan targeting 1,000 hh. Vulnerability and other criteria will be the same except the modality of the implementation. The contracted vendors/retailers will be rather big shops as compared to paper based intervention.Bakery Rehabilitation: Idleb is experiencing one of the highest bread prices among the whole of Syria. The main reason for this is the scarcity of bread production. According to the evaluation report, bakery rehabilitation offered a sustainable and value-added solution for the people living in the targeted communities. Therefore, 2 public bakeries are foreseen to be rehabilitated with Ihsan RD and to be handed over to local councils.Micro gardening: People have difficulties to access vitamin and nutrient rich green vegetables on their diet. This activity is enabling people in need to have a more balanced diet. In addition to that, working with soil may also help them to improve their psychological resilience. Seeds, agricultural tools, irrigation support etc. shall be provided within the scope of this activity. This activity will be a continuation of the ongoing project with existing Partners (1,300 hh) for winter and summer season. Maintenance, clearance and installation of latrines: This activity will be handed over from another AA funded WHH project (SYR 25.16). Previously, 30 latrines have been installed in Deir Hassan camp and now they will be cleared and maintained under the project SYR 15.17. Since new IDP flow to this area are expected, 15 new latrines shall be installed. This activity shall be carried out with Hand in Hand (HiH).Hygiene Kit Distribution: Sanitation is a paramount problem in camps. With HiH, 2,000 hygiene kits are foreseen to be distributed under the emergency response budget line for the vulnerable people. Food Basket Distribution: This emergency relief activity will be carried out under the emergency response budget line in collaboration with Ihsan RD targeting 2,500 recently displaced people.Emergency Food Voucher Distribution: SARD and Luxembourg Caritas was a partner of another AA funded WHH project namely SYR 26.16. The activities of this partner are shifted to SYR 15.17. For 9 months people who are recently displaced shall be provided with emergency food vouchers via SARD in western Aleppo.Cooked Meal Provision: In western Aleppo, 375 hh in orphanage and health center shall be provided cooked meals by SARD. Rehabilitation of Shelters: One of the most important needs of vulnerable people is shelter. Therefore, SARD is targeting to rehabilitate houses (360 hh), shelter
