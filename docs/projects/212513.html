Project Summary

Needs in response to the WASH SO4: this proj is designed to meet returnees dire need based on WASH sector HNO 2024, Aleppo PiN 2,962,485, RDam PiN 1,717,593, Hama PiN 367,374.
we conducted several assessment rounds in the areas, info & data collected in collaboration with beneficiaries, local committees, municipality &Mukhtars.
We defined the actual needs for improving WASH conditions to the most vulnerable people in close coordination with WASH national & sub national sectors.
Activities: Coordination with MOLAE, DoWs & Govrs. - Procurement procedures, contracts- Site supervision & monitoring on materials & works, M&E, Field visits, beneficiaries’ feedback & surveys on assistance provided - Final works’ delivery & final narrative & finance reports.
Intervention in 3 Govs selected per subdistrict severity 3&4
Activity 4.1. Repair, Rehab & operation support to water systems: Maintenance of the existed Pipes in Yelda, Elevated Water Tank Rehab in Yelda & in Deir Alasafier/RDam provide solar system for Borehole.
Activity 4.3. Repair, Rehab & operation support to sanitation system (sewage lines Rehab in Hama city, RDam-Sbeineh & Aleppo-Tirbet Lala)
Activity 4.4. Repair, Rehab & operation support to solid waste management systems (Providing solar system with light Mechanical & Electrical maintenance for the Waste Water Treatment Plant in Al Heijaneh/RDam)
Safety & dignity are reinforced & transversal during Proj activities. Do No Harm is enforced via conflict sensitivity analysis to understand the context &identifying inter relations of different targeted groups. We act equipped with understanding these aspects to avoid negative impacts. elderly & people with chronic diseases are under focus assuring these groups benefits & needs are forecasted. The proj enables equal & impartial access as assistance will be provided on the basis of vulnerability (will avoid discrimination based on gender, age & community). GBV is strongly mainstreamed in this project; Safeguarding AAP by setting call center, CRM in place.
Protection risk: Potential exposure to explosive hazard, Like Med. Imp High
M Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area & conduct assessments/rehab works only in cleared areas, Conduct safety training to staff & contractors.
Prot R Health associated with construction works & exposure to raw sewage & gases Like High, Imp Med. M Ensure staff /contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage use PPE, conduct safety training for all personnel, M&E ensure project SOPs in place.
R SEA: Like Low, Imp High M: Rescate staff, partners & contractors sign the CoC directly refers to PSEA & GBV. As part of on-going efforts to improve AAP, commitment to implement CRM allow beneficiaries to raise concerns, make complaints & feedback on the services & work, PSEA &/or GBV issues will be addressed 1 by the Proj coordinator, share the concern with HoM to establish a solution to proceed to enhance the protection of the PSEA/GBV survivor. When needed, the HoM will scale the issue to HQ gender specialists’ team & referral with partners coordination for solution.
Oper R, miscommunication with different stakeholders with major consequences. Resources & response capacities being limited & far below known needs, there are perception of biased selection of response activities, thereby creating or exacerbating tension. Increased prices due to heavy inflation. M already accounted for in the budget, Contractor 25% down payment to purchase materials under our observation ensure quality, quantity & availability Fuel shortage & procurement supply delays are the Contractors’ responsibility & M to ensure immediate storage of the purchased materials. Approval delays foreseen in the proj duration & works schedule. Rescate has strong coordination with ST, stakeholders & communities to facilitate focused response with strong need-based approach.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This proposal aims to address the urgent needs identified in the WASH sectors, particularly in response to the HNO 2024, Aleppo, Rural Damascus, & Hama. Through collaborative efforts with beneficiaries, local committees, municipalities, & Mukhtars, we have assessed the critical requirements for improving WASH conditions, focusing on the most vulnerable populations. This project is designed in coordination with national & sub-national sectors to ensure a comprehensive approach to addressing the identified needs. 1. To Rehab & repair water systems, sanitation systems, & solid waste management systems in targeted governorates. 2. To strengthen safety & dignity throughout project activities, with a particular focus on GBV mainstreaming & prevention. 3. To mitigate risks associated with explosive hazards, health risks & SEA through comprehensive risk management strategies. Activities: Coordinate with relevant government bodies, DoW & Govs. Procurement procedures & contract management. ACT 4.1: Repair & Rehab of water systems: Maintenance of existing 380 LM pipes in Yelda, replacing & providing new pipes and valves Rehab of elevated water tanks in Yelda & Deir Elasafier. Installation of solar systems for boreholes in Deir Elasafier/RDam. ACT 4.3: Repair & Rehab of sanitation systems ~2200 meter of sewage lines in Hama city, RDam-Sbeineh, & Aleppo-Tirbet Lala. ACT 4.4: Repair & Rehab of solid waste management plant: Installation of solar systems with mechanical & electrical maintenance for the Waste Water Treatment Plant in Al Heijaneh/RDam. 2.R&M Conduct conflict sensitivity analysis to understand contextual risks. Provide safety training to staff & contractors. Ensure appropriate personal protective (PPE) for those exposed to health risks. 3. GBV Mainstreaming Establish a call center & CRM system to safeguard against GBV. Implement GBV prevention measures throughout project phases. 1. Safety & Security Collaborate with Mine Action actors to ensure work is conducted in cleared areas. Provide safety training to staff & contractors. 2. H R Ensure appropriate PPE for staff & contractors working with raw sewage. Conduct safety training & monitoring of project safety processes. 3. SEA CoC signed by all staff, partners, & contractors. Establish a beneficiary CRM system to address concerns & complaints related to SEA & GBV. 4. OPR Maintain strong communication with stakeholders to avoid miscommunication. Mitigate inflation-related price increases through careful budget planning. Ensure timely procurement & storage of materials to address potential fuel shortages & supply delays. This proposal outlines a comprehensive plan for sewage systems Rehab, water networks, & water treatment plants while addressing GBV activities & mitigating associated risks. By implementing these activities, we aim to improve WASH conditions & enhance the safety & well-being of vulnerable populations in targeted governorates.
