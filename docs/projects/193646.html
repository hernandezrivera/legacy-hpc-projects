Project Summary

The proposed action aims to enhance the livelihood resilience of vulnerable communities, living under severe conditions in crisis in the Gaza Strip, and who are unserved or underserved by WASH facilities, by giving them access to basic, equitable, affordable, and sustainable WASH services. Based on the WASH Vulnerability mapping, the PHG has performed a rapid need assessment shown that the people in El Zaytoon Area suffer from sewage flood resulted from deteriorated infrastructure, and allocated 450 vulnerable households live in crisis in several locations in Gaza and Deir Al-Balah governorates, who are in immense need to get their WASH facilities improved. Also, it was shown that those households suffer from negative environmental and social impact raised by lack of essential WASH services exacerbated by the communities' vulnerability and exposed women, children, and old people in particular to high risks of breakthrough of diseases and other illnesses.
The households in El-Zayton area have no access to basic infrastructure, and they have previously complained about public health hazards and environmental pollution risks resulted from the lack of infrastructure and sewage flood at their area especially in winter. The project will target nearly 750 people of which 50.1% are females. Moreover, the project will indirectly benefit all people in the target location through direct pollution prevention to the surrounding environment and groundwater. Accordingly, the project will contribute by supplying and installing 2Km of UPVC sewer lines including manholes and house connections. The project will contribute also to stopping further pollution of aquifer and prevent public health risks associated by the weak infrastructure and closing down cesspits, hence, ensuring no harm principle.
In addition, the project will improve the WASH facilities of 450 vulnerable households live in Gaza and Deir Al-Balah governorates, using the WASH vulnerability mapping, and under coordination with the Ministry of Social Affairs to allocate the people with acute need, and prioritize the beneficiaries based on their vulnerability. Several locations have been selected to make sure that the access of nearly 2700 inhabitants of which 1353 are women to essential wash services is improved. The number of beneficiaries in Gaza City are: El-Zaytoun 300, Al Sheikg Ejleen 300, Al Remal Al Janobi 300, Al Remal Al Shamali 300, Tal Alhawa 300, while the beneficiaries in Deir Al Balah are: block 1: 300, block 3 & block 4: 240, (block 1, block 2, block 5: 240), (block C: 300, Al Souq and Kalboush, Block L, and block f: 120). Accordingly, the project will contribute by rehabilitating their WASH facilities by supplying and installing internal WASH connections, sinks, water mixers, toilets, laundries and PE roof tanks.
This has been confirmed through deep coordination with the PWA and CMWU during coordination meetings organized on 29 September 2021, and is currently emphasized on 17 October 2022. This includes the WASH cluster coordination team, area focal points, technical focal points and governmental counterpart focal points.
The proposed intervention adopts gender responsive approach during the design and implementation of the project activities, and will reduce conﬂict in the household and neighborhood, improve quality of life, and signiﬁcantly decline the health problems (mainly for children).
This intervention responds to the SDG #6 aiming to ensure available and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all and includes targets for universal access to drinking water, sanitation and hygiene for all by 2030, and complies with the HRP 2023 strategy objectives and the WASH cluster objectives #2 and objective #3.
In general, this intervention will serve over 3,450 people in Gaza and Deir Al-Balah (893 boys, 847 girls, 769 men, 751 women, 95 elderly and 95 disabled persons), by installing sewage networks and also securing adequate WASH facilities for 450 HHs.

1 - Needs

Over the past decade, sewage systems are a life hazard that affects the lives in the Gaza Strip, and the problem was made worse by vulnerable economic situation exacerbated through the last years. The continued political and physical isolation of Gaza has magnified the severity of these issues, as the restrictions in place limit control over and access to its natural resources, thereby inhibiting its ability to cope with and adapt to these challenges. However, other issues such as insufficient electricity, import restrictions on equipment, and funding shortages to infrastructural reform have all exacerbated the presence of functional sewage systems in many areas. According the MSNA, July 2022, about 86.5% of households in the whole Gaza are connected to a sewage system for latrine waste drainage, with 10.7% of households reportedly using cesspits. The wastewater water crisis is reflected on the public health and on the surrounding environment causing serious illness and pollution due the overflow of the wastewater. Accordingly, an urgent intervention is required in areas with no sewerage infrastructure. The targeted locations have been facing a growing environmental crisis for many years now. Based on Gaza WASH Vulnerability Map 2022, PHG will target El-Zaytoun neighborhoods in Gaza area which is considered one of the densely populated and marginalized areas in Gaza, where thousands have no access to adequate wastewater networks. the deteriorated sewage infrastructure and cesspits contaminate the water aquifer and cause regular sewage flooding along the roads, which result in health risks for women, children, and old people in particular. The project aims to improve the environmental conditions through the installation of the sewage networks in the internal streets. In order to prioritize the areas with high humanitarian needs, PHG has carried out several meetings with the PWA, CMWU, Municipality of Gaza, Municipality of Wadi Gaza. Both PWA and CMWU have recommended Gaza governorate as priority area. In Gaza, 86 % of household are served by sewage networks, while large amount of people have no adequate mean of disposal. According to PWA and relevant Municipalities, there are frequent complaints of citizens in that region due to the daily over flow of sewage. The wastewater seeps through the soil layers to the aquifer causing deterioration in the groundwater quality and causing high levels of Nitrates. Consequently, the target community is suffering from serious health problems, especially the children. Furthermore, sewage floods in streets leading to critical environmental and health conditions on the population and the surrounding areas.According to MSNA 2022, 40.6% of the households in Gaza have reported using permanent sanitation facilities such as toilet seat, Niagara, handwashing station, bidet…etc.), where over 60% of the households need to have adequate sanitation and hygiene facilities at their homes, especially that over 81% of the households in Gaza face difficulties to cover their basic needs and they spend large amount of their expenditure on meeting their urgent basic needs. Those households suffer from negative health impact raised by lack of essential WASH services, vulnerability, and poverty. The frequent. PHG has conducted rapid assessment and arranged meetings with stakeholders in order to assess the needs and to respond to the newly emerging situation to enable those poor families to live in dignity. Without proper WASH facilities, those vulnerable households are exposed to real health hazards considering the high population rate. The HNO-2022 showed that over 1.35 million across the oPt are affected by public health risks associated with insufficient access to WASH services, and will be in immediate need of humanitarian assistance, considering the frequent Israeli escalations on Gaza as a major factor where 36% of the households reported that their typical monthly income decreased a lot. Thus, there is an immense need to provide adequate water and sanitation facilities for the vulnerable households. PHG has targeted the houses that needs WASH rehabilitation, more precisely will target 450 households for rehabilitating the damaged/inadequate WASH facilities at their houses to ensure safe drinking water supply and sanitation and hygiene conditions for those people which is highly urgent especially under the pandemic of COVID-19. Accordingly, PHG will target 450 households in (Al Zaitoon, Al remal Al Shamali, Al Remal Al Janoubi, Tal Alhawa, Al Sheikh Ejleen) neighborhoods in Gaza city, and (block 1, block 3 block 4, block 1 block 2 block 5, block C Al Souq and Kalboush, Block l and Block f) in Deir Al-Balah. In order to prioritize the areas with high humanitarian needs, PHG has carried out several meetings with relevant stakeholders, including the PWA, ministry of social affairs, and relevant municipalities. They have recommended Gaza and Deir AL-Balah governorates as priority area. Current situation has reduced household access to adequate water and sanitation services for these families and for some of them who are not able to mitigate the interruption of services, they are adopting negative coping mechanisms such as decreasing their water consumption and discharging wastewater. This condition has been worsened by the acute economic situation in Gaza and the need for essential items such as water tanks, or have their sanitation facilities and networks damaged, exacerbating the stresses among the families’ members and the needs for specialized WASH interventions to alleviate their suffering.

2 - Activities

All project activities and interventions have been reviewed, evaluated and coordinated with the relevant stakeholders, including cluster coordination team, area focal points, technical focal points and governmental counterpart focal points to avoid and overcome work duplication, as well this cooperation and coordination will be maintained and improved at implementation stages at all levels with all stakeholders and organizations. The approach of implementation for this project will starts by formulating a project committee that consists of 5-7 person , which will include representatives from the community, CMWU, women, municipalities, local committees, the role of the committee is to assist in collecting contribution if any (local members), also will facilitate work of the project staff in the field particularly to solve any conflict if raised through implementation period. The women whom representing CBOs and community will be active and expected to contribute equally in the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project side by side with the committee members. They will be given a significant role in implementation from the beginning. The design of the systems will be done under the supervision of PHG , and expected to be supported by CMWU ,PWA , Ministry of Social Affairs, Funder representative, and member form the community whom will be as members in a steering committee of 3-5 persons. They will be updated/reporting back in periodically meeting by PHG engineer / head of office/representative for the progress and obstacles faced through implementation, and recommendations form next periods, the SC will support and help to find solutions to improve work and progress. the tendering process will go through the PHG procurement system, procurement plan will be designed for this purpose. The M& E officer at PHG will follow implementation stage by stage through the M&E module and will insure achieving outputs and will measure indicator on regular bases. The main activities that will be implemented under the project which will target the neighborhoods (Al Zaitoon, Al remal Al Shamali, Al Remal Al Janoubi, Tal Alhawa, Al Sheikh Ejleen) neighborhoods in Gaza city, and (block 1, block 3 block 4, block 1 block 2 block 5, block C Al Souq and Kalboush, Block l and Block f) in Deir Al-Balah, can be summarized as follows: 1- Supply and installing of about 2Km of UPVC sewer pipelines with different diameters, 2-Supply and installing of about 70 concrete manholes with different sizes in the targeted areas 3-Supply and installing of about 80 house connections the targeted areas. 4- Rehabilitation of 450 WASH facilities for households live in crisis and under vulnerable economic situation. Beneficiaries of WASH facilities will be identified and coordinated with the ministry of social affairs in cooperation with WASH cluster. The WASH facilities rehabilitation will include kitchens, toilets with all relevant tools, fixtures and extensions as well as rooftop water storage tanks. The proposed modalities are will improve the sanitation services in the target areas, protect the aquifer from pollution and raise the economic standard of living for the region’s residents, to enable the poor families whose houses suffer from lack of adequate WASH facilities to live in their houses in dignity and safety in Gaza. The aim of these activities is to address the problems identified under the needs assessment which is related to poor water quality and water borne disease infections especially for children as well as those obtained from the women and men interviews during the assessment.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

PHG aim with all of its projects is to ‘Do-No-Harm’ and to maximize positive impacts. In conjunction with this policy, PHG has ensured these practices by focusing on female heads of household, empowering the most vulnerable of families. By doing community outreach, PHG is able to help normalize female participation in public spaces. By providing home visits involving female evaluators, women can feel more safe in their own homes. PHG’s two-fold aim with all of its programs is to ‘Do-No-Harm’ and to maximize positive impacts. For this project, PHG will ensure that beneficiaries, staff, and partners will be protected as much as possible from contracting or spreading COVID-19 by taking all safety precautions and measures throughout the entire project phases, including interviews, needs assessment, field visits, distribution of material, and technical implementation. Throughout the whole implementation phases and project activities PHG will be followed a clear protection approach to ensure a solid engagement at the community level by local community committees. Their role throughout the whole project will be that of guaranteeing compliance and respect of safety and privacy of each individual, especially for the most vulnerable groups (female-headed households, households with people with disabilities), moreover the name and personal data of beneficiaries will be kept confidential and safe from unintended use by applying effective data protection measures, prevention and response measures will be put in place in case of physical/sexual violence arising from lack of WASH facilities, in coordination with protection actors, Exposure to safety threats will be considered through the process of hygiene and dignity kits distribution, ensuring that beneficiaries will not be under any risk of theft, intimidation, sexual assault and extortion whilst receiving their hygiene kits home through door-to-door distribution of hygiene and dignity kits will be taken in account. Furthermore, the hygiene kit size will be taken in consideration, ensuring that beneficiaries are able to carry home with no additional burdens (physical or financial) and without the assistance for others to carry them and in case they need help, an assistance should be provided for free and the hygiene promotion sessions will be conducted based on the culture respect and religious inclusion and that there is a proportionate number of female hygiene promoters. To avoid discrimination the proposed actives were tailored to target the most marginalized and vulnerable groups in the community in terms of sanitations. It will be ensured that the process of beneficiary registration and documentation is inclusive through applying a systematic outreach, gender balance will be ensured in the distribution teams and hygiene promoters through assigning enough women available as a contact point for women in the community, specific needs of the vulnerable group’s women, girls, older persons will be checked and provided e.g. sanitary pads and adult diapers in hygiene kits and provide additional personal hygiene items for persons with incontinence or severe diarrhea, an inclusive orientation session will be conducted on WASH services and how to use hygiene kits to ensure equitable access to all types of activities, the information on WASH will be distributed through various communication means to reach the broader community and to account for different literacy levels eg. door-to-door, poster, radio, social media, use of pictograms and the hygiene Promotion messages will be simple to make it understandable for the children.
