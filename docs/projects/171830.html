Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing appropriate support to ensure dignified living conditions for 26000 displaced person (10816 women, 9984men, 2600 boys and 2600 boys) at the informal settlements in Bay, lower Shabelle and Middle Shabelle Regions Somalia. The targeted IDPs including people with disabilities are living precariously in crowded informal settlements in Bay, Lower Shabelle and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia. The recent climate shocks and insecurity affected IDPs are living under very poor and deplorable conditions in unplanned informal settlements with limited access to basic needs owing to inconsistent and unreliable service provision coupled by cases of exclusion from accessing humanitarian assistance. Women, girls and people with disabilities are further affected by unplanned nature of the IDP sites that limit them from accessing essential services such as latrines located within the sites which compromise their safety. The situation is exacerbated by poor coordination and management structures at the settlement owing to the fact that they are managed by gatekeepers who are not accountable and further keeps demanding for favors from them in the face of fears of rampant evictions. The highlighted plight calls for need for timely and appropriate site improvements to enhance safe and secure access to critical services available within the sites particularly for women, girls and people with disabilities in addition to very strong need for site-level coordination and management systems to ensure equitable access to services and increase protection of displaced persons. Furthermore there is need for information sharing with meaningful consultation and participation of affected IDP communities and People with disabilities to ensure their dignified living conditions sustainable impact

Project Activities

1. Improve and Maintain CCCM coordination structures in collaboration with appropriate government counterpart 2. Ensure through CCCM coordination that relevant responders are mobilized towards providing sectorial assistance to those identified as very vulnerable 3. Complete Monthly eviction risk data with community groups able to contact the respective HLP,CCCM and protection partners if a threat occurs 4. Conduct site verifications on bi-annual basis 5. Establish/update service mapping of partners operating in sites 6. Carry out service monitoring activities aimed at highlighting service gaps at the site-level andensuring that minimum standards are being adhered to 7. Conduct Detailed Site Assessment (DSA) on a yearly basis 8. Establish roving site management teams to ensure coordination with community governance structures 9. Provide capacity building to existing governance structures/focal points 10. Together with other clusters and partners, as well as the authorities, coordinate the establishment/ efficient use of referral pathways for both protection (including SGBV) and assistance/service delivery to persons with special needs. 11. support and Provide platform where IDPs can raise complaints and feedback provided 12. Provide trainings on site maintenance and site planning to camp committees13. Support community led site maintenance activities to ensure upkeep of sites (cash forwork, site maintenance committees, distribution of tools) 14. Implement emergency sites improvement projects to minimize protection risks and ensure safety in sites (flood mitigation, fire prevention). 15. Administer joint stakeholder site decongestion activities allowing for short-term improvements in settlement standards, minimizing protection threats and ameliorating COVID19 transmission 16. Establish/reinforce mechanisms for communication of multi-sector services available per geographical area or in IDP sites in the appropriate language through ( Communication/ information centers (in coordination with local authorities, and protection actors, Mobile outreach teams, Mass sensitization,Radio programming, sign boards, text campaigns) 17. Establish and maintain inter-sector complaints feedback mechanisms (CFMs) at the IDP site-level.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff/ additional guards as well as ensuring that assistance is provided at centralised locations where women and girls dont cover very long distance that compromise their safety on the way way back home2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as conducting awareness on SEA and the reporting procedures that doesnt expose the victims to the perpetrators.3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls and ensuring their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as active participation on all project phases/stages right from initiation throughout implementation including mornitoring, till completion5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
