Project Summary

HOPE Sudan will focus on Rokoro (North Jebel Marra) of Central Darfur based on poor indicators with regard to PHC, Nutrition, RH service provision, patient/service provider ratio and current service providers (SMoH, INGOs and NNGOs). The targeted sites in Rokoro locality include 49,992 IDPs (HNO 2018) with 1,597 under GAM.
This project contributes to the three HRP health strategic outcomes
This project allocated 25% of the resources to contribute to the first outcome which is Populations in areas affected by natural or man-made disaster receive timely assistance during and in the aftermath of a shock.
Hope organization is focusing on the second outcome allocating about 75% of the resource.
The second outcome is displaced populations, refugees, returnees and host communities meet their basic needs and/or access basic services while increasing their self-reliance.

Needs

In line with the Humanitarian Need Overview (HNO) 2019 confirmed that 3.66 million number of people in need of life saving health intervention. About 24% of the Primary Health Care (PHC) facilities in five Darfur states in Sudan are not fully functional either due to staff shortages or poor physical infrastructure.(FMoH/WHO HeRAMS July 2018). 49% Male, 51% Female. Segregated as 53% Children, 42% adult, and 5% elderly.HOPE Sudan will focus on Rokoro (North Jebel Marra) of Central Darfur based on poor indicators with regard to PHC, RH service provision, patient/service provider ratio and current service providers (SMoH, INGOs and NNGOs). The targeted sites in Rokoro locality include 49,992 IDPs (HNO 2018) with 1,597 under GAM. The last displaced happened in April 2018 when 11,425 individuals fled to Rokoro from surrounding areas due inter-tribal conflict (OCHA April 2018). Many health facilities have been destroyed, effectively leaving these places without government health services. Inadequate provision of and access to primary health care services (PHC) especially for women and girls across Rokoro remains the main constraint as these same areas have high mortality rates for infants and mothers. The reasons for this range from high levels of insecurity, poor transportation infrastructure and lack of funding from the central government. HOPE Sudan, through its partnership with SRCS, Majalas Alkhair and SMoH in Central Darfur, has access to health facilities are not functional in Rokoro as well as HOPE Sudan will fully coordinate with CRS, WR, IMC and UNICEF of Health partners in Rokoro.While no concrete data is available, increased fighting, inter-communal and inter-tribal conflicts have affected the safety and security of people exacerbating the risk of sexual violence especially when displaced. There has been an overall increase in GBV in affected areas. There is an increase in other forms of GBV such as domestic violence and early marriage of young girls. Women and girls continue to face risk of gender-based violence including sexual violence when they leave the villages. Fear of reprisals from attackers often prevents rape survivors from seeking medical help. The conflict in Darfur has also increased the number of women who are the sole providers for their families. Sudan also face issues with more chronic levels of nutrition with one in every three children under the age of five years affected. Decrease health impact resulting in both a shorter stature than non-affected peers as well as a significant impact on the development of the brain resulting in long term impact on IQ, learning potential, educational outcomes as well as poorer health in the immediate and longer term future. As a result, these children will have lower lifetime earnings, and lower productivity as adults, influencing the economic growth and potential of the entire Health in country.

Activities

ActivitiesThe targeted sites in Rokoro locality include 49,992 IDPs (HNO 2018) with 1,597 under GAM.Outcome 1:1. Provide primary care services including consultations for common diseases, free of charge consultation prescriptions, EPI, ANC, IMCI, RH and Referral services.2. Support monthly running costs of the one health facilities including the provision of water, clinic toilet, and necessary supplies .3.Minor Rehabilitation of 1 health facilities in Rokoro locality of Central Darfur.4. Train medical assistants and nurses and locality MOH staff on emergency preparedness 5. Procurement of drugs, medical supplies and furniture for needy health facilitiesOutcome 2:1. Provide raining on minimum basic package of primary health care services (including maternal & child health) & support referral to secondary health care.2. Train medical assistants and nurses on IMCI, RH Modules on collaboration with MoH,UNFPA,UNCEF3. Provide incentives for state Ministry of Health staff including : 1 Medical Assistants, 1 Pharmacist Assistance, 2 Nurses, 4 Midwives, Nutrition, and community health promotion 4. Training of health staff on psycho-social support of Rape Victims. 5. Refresher training for health facilities personnel on basic ANC, delivery, PNC and family planning.6. Maintain one central store for medicines supplies and RH commodities7. Support transportation of drugs to the health facilities on monthly basis .8. Conduct regular field monitoring on distribution and management of drugs in clinics.9.. Train medical assistants and nurses on IMCI in collaboration with MoH,UNCEF10. Training of 20 volunteer community health promotion group in targeted community engaging community health promoters in case findings.
