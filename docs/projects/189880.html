Project Summary

The project aims to improve IDPs' access to shelter and NFIs in temporary and resettlement centres in Cabo Delgado. 6,800 families will recive support with sphere standard shelters, as well as access to 2,500 NFI kits. In addition, IDPs, especially women and youth, will be capacitated in resilient construction techniques.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Provision of sphere standards Transitional Shelters: Through the proposed intervention the organizations will provide 6800 Sphere Standard Transitional Shelter (in kind contribution of 6800 6x4m tarpaulins) for households without any shelter and/or for HH with emergency or provisional shelter solutions During the last consultations carried out by CCCM actors and AeA after shelter interventions in different sites in Ancuabe and Metuge, families have reported a need for more space in the shelters due to the fact that the high number of people per household and that in most shelters they only have one room (around 8m2). The proposed models are approved by the shelter cluster, as well as the local government, and are extendable emergency shelter structures that are inspired by improved local construction customs includingd exterior dedicated cooking area and 2 rooms ( 3 for large families). Distribution of 2500 NFI (in kind: 1xsolar lamp, 1xbucket, 2xblanket, 2xmats, 1xkitchen kit) for new arrivals and people in need.Improvement of community construction skills and capacity within the resettlement centres and host communities.IDPs, Host communities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection mainstreaming is an integral part of AeA’s design strategy, this alongside the participatory methods that AeA use to collect information help greatly to identify the risks posed to each demographic. Staff working in the field are aware of the do no harm, a principle which assists in making the most suitable decisions for the populations in question. We prioritise preserving safety and dignity in the design of any installation, infrastructure or the planning of any event. Ensuring that there is equitable access for all and encouraging participation in order to empower those we are helping. A multi-sector risk analysis for each component of the project will be made to ensure that all risks are considered and mitigated. As an example early marriages (the families force minors to get married in order to create another HH and receive extra plot and shelter) are a frequent practice on the sites. In these cases a previous analisis will be carried out and provide extra shelter support for those large families and/or having children under this risk. In addition AeA will work alongside CCCM to locate street lights where necessary and assure the proper secure contitions to access shelters.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

AeA includes SEA as a human rights violation in it’s code of conduct offering both a regular channel for making complaints called WhistleB, a secure and encrypted vital tool to promote and ensure high standard of ethics, and to protect the trust that thousands of people place in Ayuda en Acción every day. AeA will continue to work to strengthen PSEA practices across each of it’s sectoral responses, integrating safe complaint and feedback systems, which ensure the confidentiality of the complaint, the security of the person making the complaint and ensure a swift and respectful response.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Specific committees of persons with disabilities and their relatives will be created, in order to voice out specific needs and gaps in the response. This will take into consideration the different types of disability as relevant to the implementation accessible service structures. This refers to enhances participation, as well as to sufficient physical assess to all necessary services.In addition during the implementation of the project Households containing people with disabilities will have priority for receiving assistance in terms of shelter/NFI and will receive extra support for the construction/rehabilitation of their shelters. Aspects such as functional diversity or other special needs will be taken into consideration when adapting the shelters to their users with measures such as enlarging the size of the doors for wheelchair access, direct access without obstacles or steps or other types of adjustments that are necessary to improve the comfort and life conditions of the people who will inhabit these shelters.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Before acting in development projects, AeA carries out a diagnosis of the territory in which it includes the environmental risk depending on its actions.Risk management, adaptation and mitigation are the transversal axis of our actions.Therefore, we try to undertake certain actions such as the planning of the territory, analysis of ownership or use in this case, analysis of environmental risks by sector (agriculture, fishing, services, etc.), are actions that define our work.AeA Will include Environmental mitigation measures through the replanting of identified areas close to the beneficiary populations with bamboo (in riparian areas) and trees, following the relevant distance metrics and regulations. In addition, AeA will carry out and analysis and identify specific areas for the collection of earth and mud completion of the shelter. Through this activity AeA aim to reduce the risk of erosion, floodings and other associated risks that could later affect the shelters. In addition this programme will be carried out with an holistic intervention in Metuge and Ancuabe having a wash and livelyhoods component which allows to optimize resources and the project footprint.
