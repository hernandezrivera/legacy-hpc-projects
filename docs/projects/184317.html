Project Summary


The project’s goal is aimed at improving the living conditions and protection of IDPs in Bosaso, Garowe, Gardo, Armo, Burtinle, CeelAfweyn and Laasqoray by ensuring equitable access to services and assistance of all persons in need, with a clear focus on moving towards durable solutions and enhancing GBV prevention and response.
The proposed project will be an extension of the ongoing Camp coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) Project being implemented by PSA and funded by UNHCR in Bosaso, Qardho,Armo, Garoowe and Burtinle with full participation of the displaced and host communities that are estimated population to be 170,660, with 13 camps in Bosaso (72,659), 20 IDPs camps in Garowe (31,273), 6 IDPs camps in Qardho (6146), 3 IDPs camps in Burtinle ( 4825) and 4 IDPs camps in Armo (3800).
Internally displaced persons (IDPs) in Somalia are struggling to cope with forced evictions, lack of opportunities, harsh livelihoods, water shortages and inadequate health-services. The country is a home to over 2.6 million IDPs. An estimated 1.3 million people were displaced in Somalia. In 2021, more than 60 per cent of the total IDP caseload (about 1.6 million) are considered to be the most vulnerable and require life-saving humanitarian assistance. Close to 80 per cent of those displaced are women and children.
IDPs are fronted with a lot of challenges comprising inadequate physical shelter structures in IDP camps, inadequate lighting in IDP camps, lack of/and inadequate locks for latrines are also secondary factors that predispose women and girls to sexual violence and abuse. In Puntland, women and girls in drought-affected communities with already have pre-existing emergencies such as the COVID-19 pandemic.
On disability inclusivity, PSA under the project will ensure that the rights of persons with disabilities are put into consideration throughout the project implementation; we will also ensure feedback and complaints mechanisms are designed to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities. Including our staff trained on inclusion of persons with disabilities and ensuring that measures to overcome barriers by any group are addressed. In addition, we will engage regularly with Persons with Disabilities to increase understanding of the general situation of persons with disabilities, barriers to accessing services and assistance, capacities and existing coping mechanisms, and actions that humanitarian organisations can take to strengthen inclusion.
The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity disaggregated data. An additional settlement mapping exercises from the previous one conducted by PSA will be conducted to get the information of all targeted settlements for necessary services in place and gap analysis, with our ongoing project we have noted various gaps that need to be addressed in the next phase of project, during our assessment the IDPs prioritized the following needs as urgent.
• Rehabilitation of solar lights
• Construction of IDP community centres
• Garbage collection and site maintenance such as constructing drainage systems
• Advocacy of Land and support relocations to public land when appropriate
Under the project, PSA will target 13 IDPs camps in Bosaso (72,659), 20 IDPs camps in Garowe (31,273), 6 IDPs camps in Qardho (6146), 3 IDPs camps in Burtinle ( 4825), 4 IDPs camps in Armo (3800), 7 IDPs camps in CeelAfweyn (8905) and 12 IDPs camps in Laasqoray (10,129) as well as the host community.

1. - Projects activities

OUTPUT 1: Strengthen safe access to multi sectorial services at site level through improved site management and coordinationActivities • Activity 1.1: Mobilization of relevant responders to provide sectorial assistance to those identified as very vulnerable and engaging authorities to advocate for increased service provision• Activity 1.2: Conduct monthly eviction risk data, this is a monthly activity where eviction threats are mapped, and data shared with nearest HLP and CCCM National cluster for further follow up.• Activity 1.3: Conduct site verifications on bi-annual basis , the data a map will be produced to show and highlight on the number of sites PSA manages in each district.• Activity 1.4: update service mapping of partners at the site and update service mapping to ensure no duplication of assistances for partners operating, mapping of existing community structures• Activity 1.5: Establish roving site management teams to ensure there is coordination within community governance structures• Activity 1.6: Coordinate the establishment/ efficient use of referral pathways: will support the functionality of referral pathways to assist in assistance/service delivery to those identified as vulnerable including SGBV cases through coordination• Activity 1.7: Covid-19 Prevention coordinated and structures awareness raising and disseminations of COVID messages among IDPs in line with the cluster approach and in coordination with other partners. OUTPUT 2: Improve living conditions of displaced people through site development, care and maintenance and decongestion initiativesActivities• Activity 2.1: Provide trainings to camp committees in order to roll out their roles and responsibilities in a more effective and efficient way• Activity 2.2: Conduct safety audits annually that involves evaluating site/section/block level protection/GBV risks associated to the camp layout, camp infrastructures and services as well as safety and security. • Activity 2.3: Implement emergency sites improvement projects to minimize protection risks and ensure safety in sites (solar lights, flood mitigation, fire prevention).• Activity 2.4: Administer joint stakeholder site decongestion activities through providing an impactful improvement to site conditions through small-scale community-led reconfiguration activities OUTPUT 3: Strengthen community self-management and access to information for displaced populationsActivities • Activity 3.1: Mapping and identifying existing governance structures by working closely with the community structures such as GBVWG, CPWG, and Protection Cluster• Activity 3.2: Provide core trainings (CMC Training Package) to community governance structures, which include training of the IDPs committees onsite maintenance• Activity 3.3: Establish/reinforce mechanisms for communication of multi-sector services • Activity 3.4: Community engagement and advocacy (information sessions), informing communities on what service is available within their sites through Community meetings and awareness sessions.• Activity 3.5: Establish and maintain inter-sector complaints feedback mechanisms (CFMs) at the IDP site-level.OUTPUT 4: Support opportunities for displaced people to achieve durable solutionsActivities• Activity 4.1: Implement site decongestion and improvement activities which comprise rehabilitation of communal infrastructures, basic site reconfiguration and improving sites• Activity 4.2: Coordinate/ conduct intention surveys with relevant partners and clusters to ensure the intentions of populations are understood and supported including ensuring inclusivity of minority groups• Activity 4.3: Support communities in IDP sites towards self-reliance in locations where prospects of obtaining land rights is achievable• Activity 4.4: Advocate with local authorities and landowners for additional land used for better planned IDP sites and more secure land tenure.
