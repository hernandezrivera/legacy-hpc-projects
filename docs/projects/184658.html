Project Summary

ASAL Youth Development Association will continue and expand its existing site coordination and management in Hargeisa District including Baligubadle IDP and Darasalam.
Specific activities includes: (a) Site planning and preparation to increase capacity (b) Site coordination/management – coordinate/lead improved provision of humanitarian assistance in IDP settlements; (c) Communication with affected communities, including complaint and feedback mechanisms, community sensitization/mobilization, ensuring participation of vulnerable groups (women/youth) and that the woman committees are maintained and functional; (d) Information Management – Through REACH, supporting ASAL CCCM activities, as well as additional surveys/assessments as necessary and agreed upon with the cluster. Variations in gender, physical ability and age that affect
Vulnerability and coping strategies are identified and taken into account for planning to prevent their situation from deteriorating even further. Additionally,
Contingency plans are developed and put in place to ensure ASAL can quickly adapt to and respond to changing humanitarian needs on the ground.
There are few rights for the IDPs including on the land they settle (lack of land tenure) with forced evictions being a regular feature in the IDP Camps. Many of the IDPs especially in Baligubadle city have been and continue to be at risk of being victims of multiple displacements.
Protection needs are acute especially for women and girls who are more vulnerable.
Protection of women and girls and also children remains a paramount need in the IDP camps to ensure security from gender based violence. Safe access for the IDPs to basic services and also to and from their camps is a very important need for the displaced in all, the indicated targeted district. The project will work with the CCCM Cluster and the GBV sub-cluster to conduct awareness workshop for the camp leaders and the local administration on GBV and its role in ensuring protection for the women, girls and children. The project plans to support the provision of CCCM services in order to reduce barriers to assistance, ensure equitable delivery of services, enhance the accountability to affected populations, and strengthen community engagement, participation and empowerment towards self-determination.

1. - Projects activities

The following activities will be undertaken, in line with the objectives of the Somalia CCCM Cluster Strategy, ASAL will set up in the targeted settlement mobile site management teams for identification, selection, implementation, coordination and for monitoring purposes. Improve community participation, living conditions and safe access to services and assistance in selected sites. Support formation of Camp Self-Management Committees (CMCs) in the targeted IDP camps. Provide training on CCCM principles to community governance structures. Establishment and training on CCCM principles and community engagement for 32 site committees and community governance structures. Conduct quarterly site verification-assessments including population numbers with gender and age break down. Facilitation and support to community meetings, in particular for women groups. Conduct advocacy and mobilization for the active participation of women in IDP leadership. Maintaining coordination through regular meetings, updates and communication flows with different partners working in IDP Site. Conducting site planning, verification and maintenance in collaboration with the CCCM committees. Provide technical assistance and guidance in establishing or reinforcing existing community representative/committee structures as necessary (including women’s representation) Support and help facilitate IDP governance meetings. Conduct safety audits on regular basis in coordination with relevant partners. Conducting site planning, verification and maintenance in collaboration with the CCCM committees. Regularly monitoring service delivery at site level to ensure that there are no gaps. Support the protection and restoration of livelihoods to strengthen resilience of communities ASAL Organization will support the Cluster with Detailed site assessments, assessing 3 areas in the Baligubadle, Darasalaam and Hargeisa IDPs. Support IDP communities to access land rights. Produce regular report (per site per month) on site management and conditions are provided and disseminated to relevant stakeholders Establish/update service mapping of partners operating in sites. Ensuring through CCCM coordination that relevant responders are mobilized towards providing sectorial assistance to those identified as very vulnerable. All protection concerns will be addressed accordingly on time with the help of the community. Conduct advocacy and mobilization for the active participation of women in IDP leadership.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Monitor high-risk zones regularly and at different times of day. I. Exposure to landmines or explosive remnants of war II. Communities are forced to leave their place of residence to access humanitarian assistance (humanitarian assistance as pull factor) 2. Exclusion and marginalization: I. Ensure that both women and men are fully engaged in the management of camp facilities. II. Involve and consult all categories and layers of the affected population in identifying and responding to camp issues. Different criteria may affect the power dynamics. III. Consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, older persons and marginalized persons during assessments to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences for the design and management of camp settings. IV. Set-up camp committees to represent camp residents in camp governance structures. Ensure members are representative of all groups within the community (ie. gender, age, ethnicity, socioeconomic group, disability). V. Establish appropriate and sustainable mechanisms for meaningful dialogue between camp Leaders/managers and camp committees to ensure beneficiaries are fully involved in decisions relating to their situation such as the development of camp/site policy, management and site closure. VI. Designate focal points within camp management structures and camp resident committees to incorporate the views of persons with specific needs in decision-making processes e.g. female representative, youth representative, and older person’s representative, representative of persons with disability.
