Project Summary

The Nigerian Humanitarian Needs Overview has projected 8.4 million people (of whom 2.2 million
are internally displaced) are in severe need of humanitarian and protection assistance in 2022.The
the report specifically highlighted that, the well-being and living standards of an estimated 3.03 million
people in Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe (BAY States) will be adversely affected by shortages of clean
water, and poor sanitation and hygiene practices in 2022. While 2.5 million people are targeted
with Nutrition response including 1.6million children, findings from the Nutrition and Food Security
surveillance (NFSS March 2022) points to the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) using
weight-for-height z-score (WHZ) among children aged 0-59 months was highest in Yobe (8.0%)
followed by Borno (7.7%) and Adamawa (6.1%). GAM by domain highlights Northern Yobe is at
(9.8%) as the highest with Karasuwa and Yusufari as LGAs in the domain while The GAM rate for
Southern Yobe is at (7.2%) with Potiskum as an LGA in the domain.
Given historical data from 2022 PiN: the populations of children in Yusufari, Potiskum, and Karasuwa
LGA of Yobe State with SAM burden (0-59M) requiring an outpatient therapeutic program (OTP) is
8995, 5377, and 11823 respectively. MAM burden (6-59m) is 22371, 13374, and 31427 respectively,
MAM PLW burden is 2494, 4397, and 1490, and MNP/IYCF burden combines 30976, 61072 and 18518
respectively.
This response aims to consolidate the achievements of Jireh Doo Foundation’s work in the project
locations having worked to improve nutritional outcomes while contributing to the reduction of
the proportion of people who suffer malnutrition commensurate with the five-year nutrition action
plan launched in 2021 titled “National Multi-Sectoral Plan of Action for Food and Nutrition
(NMPFAN) 2021-2025.
The primary target beneficiaries for the proposed nutrition-integrated project are 54,300 women,
men, boys, and girls prioritizing pregnant and lactating women and children age 0-59 months,
adolescents, persons with disabilities, and the aged who are the most vulnerable in emergency
situations. Emphasis will also be on persons affected by conflict and living in communities. The
target beneficiaries will be classified into direct and indirect beneficiaries.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The formation of mothers/women support groups for ideas exchange, moral support andinformation sharing as well as serve to encourage one another on ways of improving their familyand children nutrition. Ensuring the establishment of breast-feeding corners in health facilities toencourage pregnant and lactating women practice exclusive breast feeding. These practicalmeasures will continue to address gender, equity and sustainability in this program.Awareness raising among men will be done through focus group discussion, town hall meetings,house visits, speaking to father to father/men support groups during their meetings, social orreligious gatherings, on the importance of exclusive breast feeding for their babies and preparationof nutritious meals for their infants and young children to avoid acute malnutrition in their children.This awareness creation will serve to support, encourage, and mobilize men to serve as a supportto each other and their partners to initiate exclusive breast feeding for the first six months, and tosupport their households to take into consciousness the importance of good nutrition.JDF having over 9 years’ experience in the treatment of MAM with locally produced fortifiedsupplementary food, and will teach various stakeholders and groups in the targeted communitiesabout how to locally prepare it with available food items predominantly found in their communitiesand how best to prepare nutritious meals which will help to prevent future malnutrition amonginfant and young children and other members of the communities, this approach will serve as ameans of sustainability of the program.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Jireh Doo Foundation (JDF) as an organization with a heart for humanity and desire for effective andefficient project delivery and keen on upward and especially downward accountability is a member of theCHS Alliance and is also an active member of the AAP/CEAL Working group. JDF proposes an innovativeapproach to accountability to affected persons (AAP): The Community-Based Accountability and FeedbackSystem (CoBAFS).CoBAFS will have two basic components,1. The CoBAFS reporting library2. The CoBAFS feedback committee AKA Community-Based Accountability Committee (CoBAC).Under the CoBAFS reporting library, JDF will set up a project library (a physical metal cabinet) in an agreedsafe location (Bulama, Lawan, etc.) in its implementing community and an accountability board.The project library will contain JDFs project document including a large print implementation work plan.JDFs monthly reports will also be kept in the library for community access.JDF with support from Translators Without Boarders, will convert all her monthly reports into colorfulpictorial animations and charts in local languages such as can easily be understood by persons of alleducational backgrounds including children. These animated reports will be produced and distributedhouse to house for persons with disability (PWD) as would have been identified during community entryand engagement activities.The Accountability Board will be a large board (approximately 7ft by 4ft) it will contain a large print of JDFsdeliverables with their timelines in months. This will be securely locked but visible (glass) and the key keptwith the Community-Based Accountability Committee (CoBAC). The CoBAC with support from JDF willorganize a monthly Project Feedback and quality implementation review meeting. During this meeting, in the presence of community stakeholders, community members, implementing sector, AAP/CEAL WG,Government sector representatives and other partners.
