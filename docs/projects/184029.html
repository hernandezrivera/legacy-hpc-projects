Project Summary

This project is a multi-sectorial and multi-state set of interventions including social welfare and protection services under the protection Sub-Sectors (General Protection, Child Protection, and Gender-Based Violence) in addition to Education services and support for children in need.
Through this intervention, the provision of integrated Protection and Education Services for the vulnerable host and internally displaced populations will contribute to creating a protective and safe environment where particularly women, children and people with specific needs would access life-saving and life-sustaining services in safety and dignity. This project targets 10 localities in three states [West Kordofan (Lagawa, Nuhod, and Sunut), South Kordofan (AbuJebaha, Talawdi, Rashad, Habila, Altadamoun), and East Darfur (Al-Duain and Yassin)]. The main objective of this project is to provide essential, quality, and specialized lifesaving services to meet the protection and education needs of the targeted population with coordination and synergy between the sector-specific activities. The project will strengthen care for survivors and individuals vulnerable to violence through comprehensive Gender-Based Violence, General Protection, and Child Protection service provision.
This project will also provide a comprehensive package of education services to children in need, through improving the capacity of infrastructure and staff, as well as providing individual assistance to children. Combined with protection services, this will lead to beneficiaries getting better and improved services, and that long-term effective solutions are attained for sustainable access to protection, education, and basic needs.
The intervention in general protection will be through enhancing the essential welfare activities to provide people sufficient services to decrease the trauma of conflict and crises they have been through, and its effect on their psychological status. This will be through targeting communities, with a focus on PSN, WAR, PWD, CAR and EAR with diverse training, livelihood, capacity building and awareness raising activities. The project aims to establish CBPNs and build their capacity with the relevant knowledge and skills to undertake protection mitigation response. Providing the knowledge to vulnerable people to know their rights, assistance, and raising their awareness through psychological and psychosocial counselling, referrals pathways, and sessions will have an overall positive and tangible outcome in improving their coping mechanisms and solutions to the risks they face due to conflict, disaster and displacement. These activities will enhance the communities' capacity for sustained resilience, involvement, and community participation, namely through providing communities with a comprehensive CBP intervention leading to small scale projects to give them the ability to meet their needs while improving their skills and capacities in dignity. While benefiting from CBP activities, Women and Girls and also targeted within the GBV response of the project, which provides prevention, response and capacity building activities to reduce the exposure of women and girls to violence and support them in surviving from exposure.
The project will aim at improving access to response and prevention services for children at risk of violence, exploitation and abuse; affected children will be provided with structured psychosocial support services within safe spaces such as Child-Friendly Spaces and through other community and family-based approaches. GAH will also enhance the technical capacity of community-based protection systems and structures to identify, report, respond and prevent different protection risks and concerns that children are exposed to. Finally, community members including children themselves, adults and other stakeholders will be engaged, mobilized and sensitized on trights of children throughout the project.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

As a protection-based organization that specializes in children's and women's rights. This ensures that protection mainstreaming is not only visible in all of the project activities but also implemented. Consideration will be given to ensure that all services are offered with dignity. Given that there will be a clear strategy of Communication With the Community's vulnerable targeted beneficiaries will easily be reached the service centers. The project then develops referral pathways for support to the identified Protection of Civilians to enable them to receive other services from other sub-sectors this ensures an integrated approach. Within the organization will take into account preventing and minimizing any unintended negative effects of its intervention which could increase people's vulnerability and diminish the purpose of the intervention. GAH considers ways to minimize tension and conflict between beneficiaries, by ensuring that accurate information reaches all relevant community groups, including children, adolescents, older persons, and persons with disabilities, using different means of communication and dissemination of AGD appropriate information. GAH will hold general and refresher training on protection mainstreaming, GBV, and PSEA prevention for its staff at HQ and field level, as well as the relevant community workers. Specifically tailored gender mainstreaming processes will be performed for each intervention and reports will be shared with the Protection sector and GBV subsector, and included in the reporting of the project. AGD disaggregated data will be collected for reporting purposes in all stages of the project. All interventions especially interventions for GBV survivors and women at risk will be done confidentially to ensure the safety and dignity of the beneficiaries and avoid exposing them to further risks. Since GBV is known as a very sensitive issue in the targeted areas, GAH will avoid any approaches interpreted as inappropriate in the community context or insulting to local sensitivities. Project staff or key representatives of the project will be carefully selected to have strong knowledge of the community and a thorough understanding of the local political context. GAH will ensure that special arrangements (including but not exclusive to community outreach and volunteers) are made to provide meaningful access to services for those who may not be able to reach such as older persons and persons with disabilities. GAH interventions will not discriminate against any group and will consider whether men and women have different capacities to access the IGAs interventions compared with in-kind assistance. GAH is aiming to promote participation among the target communities and empower the community by making sure that beneficiaries know they have a right to equitable and safe assistance, and where and how to obtain it.

GAH will hold general and refresher training on protection mainstreaming, GBV, and PSEA prevention for its staff at HQ and field level, as well as the relevant community workers. Specifically tailored gender mainstreaming processes will be performed for each intervention and reports will be shared with the relevant sectors, and Protection sector and GBV subsector, and included in the reporting of the project.
