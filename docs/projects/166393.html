Project Summary

This project targets 26,071 beneﬁciaries and is focused on addressing COVID-19 crisis. The impact of this disease could be more devastating in densely populated and crowded settlements such as the Rohingya regugees camp in Cox’s bazar, Bangladesh, which is the largest in the world. As hand washing with soap and water and social distancing are the key targeted and recommended preventive measures to fight against the COVID virus by WHO, this project is designed mainly to implement the activities related to hygiene promotion that will help to avert infections and promote the health of the people. To achieve the goal of this project, the activities will provide both hand wash NFI supplies (HK, Gloves, Mask, Soap etc) and facilities like latrine, hand washing devices and water supplies through deep tube wells which will be repaired to ensure consistent water supply. The project will be implemented in camps 19 and 13 where WVB is already implementing WASH activities.

Objectives

1) Ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for women, girls, men and boy refugees living in camps and affected host communities to safe water for drinking, domestic needs and medical purposes.2) Ensure women, girls, men and boy refugees living in camps, host communities or affected by Covid-19 have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times. 3) Ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through hygiene promotion and distribution of hygiene items with strong focus on highly contagious diseases, for all refugees, affected or vulnerable communities of the district

Needs

For COVID-19, regular handwashing with soap is the most important practice that helps to reduce the spread of the virus. WVB will focus on ensuring handwashing supplies and water is available. From the findings of the COVID-19: Bangladesh Multi-Sectoral Anticipatory Impact and Needs Analys (April, 2020), 42% of the respondents reported that hygiene materials are not easily accessible, 47.9% use safely managed drinking water services,and 74.8% had handwashing facility with water and soap. In addition, 64.4% reported use of basic sanitation services.

Activities or outputs

1) Provide soap, hygiene kits, aquatabs both in camps and Host’s communities2) Provide adequate WASH facilities in health care facilities and isolation centers3) Increase coverage and operationalisation of hand-washing stations both in camps and host’s communities4) Strenngthen hygiene promotion focusing on Covid-195) Organise disinfection of WASH facilities and common spaces
