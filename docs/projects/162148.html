Project Summary

This project emphasizes on the importance of the Rohingya children who have been forced to flee their own ancestor land in Myanmar. This project’s goal is not only to bring the beneficiaries to access education but also to ensure quality, skill and discover other talents. This projects also gives importance to child protection and it also has a contingency plan in order to continue education without facing huge challenges and obstacles. This projects also aims at bringing all those neglected children to be enrolled through the education for all principle.

Objectives

"Access - Expand and strengthen immediate access to equitable learning opportunities, in a safe and protective environment, for crisis-affected refugee and host community girls and boys aged 3-24 years old.Quality - Provide quality inclusive education to refugee and host community girls and boys aged 3-24 years old, aligned with Education Sector standards, and increase teaching-related professional development opportunities.Community Engagement - Ensure refugee and host community ownership and active participation and engagement in the education of girls and boys aged 3-24 years old. "

Needs

Maintaining quality education for all the children, especially those more vulnerable under the age of 5 and 6, who have been living at the Rohingya camps is one of the key problems, where the teachers are needed to be highly trained so that they can teach these children properly. Landslide, flood, seasonal storms and many other disasters also putting these people into enormous problems. Core materials, supplementary materials and other teaching trainings are some of the important priorities to be addressed.

Activities or outputs

Provision of early childhood and basic Education, identification of and support to children with disabilities, equipment and upgrading of the existing learning facilities, conduct of teacher competency assessment and development of teachers’ competencies.
