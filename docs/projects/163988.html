Project Summary

In line with SO1 and SO2, this project aims at meeting the basic needs of the conflict affected population through improved access to safe and adequate water supply and improved sanitation services, while enhancing sustainability through rehabilitation of community level services.

4 - Needs

As very few pumping stations are currently functioning,this leads to low pressure in the network and low quantity of water reaching at the HH levelPublic water networks and boreholes are not functioning in many areas&the share of delivered water per capita varies across the different assessed locations.Controlled water trucking service is active in many neighbourhoods in the targeted governorates(Rural Damascus,Hama,Lattakia,Deir-ez-Zor,Dara,Aleppo) while in the other locations people buy water from private suppliers,pull from private wells or directly from the rivers.Due to the sewage leaking,probable infiltration of water networks and uncontrolled water sources,HHs store water in unsafe&unhealthy utensils causing poor water quality in many neighbourhoods.Many high water tanks are damaged and in urgent necessity for repairs to provide a sufficient storage of water with the efficient pressure to reach all the households in the affected areas.In many areas assessed, it has been reported that wherever the water network is not functional; waterborne diseases spread.Furthermore, access to basic water supply remains very limited&drinking water is mainly operated through private water trucking,which forces families to spend up to 15-20% of their incomes to secure access to a minimum amount of clean water.Main sewage networks were damaged during the conflict and still untreated, which caused flooding of wastewater in houses&streets&constitute a ground for disease-transmitting vectors. This inadequate sanitation environment poses significant health risks for vulnerable groups(women,boys,girls,the elderly&people with disabilities),while the newly displaced are often the most severely affected.Aside from considering the rehabilitation of damaged sewage infrastructure as one of main priorities in addressing public health concerns, there is still an urgent need for emergency WASH interventions to respond to the lifesaving needs in areas still experiencing active conflict.

5 - Implementation of activities

PUI will contribute to SO1 through the implementation of:•Water Network Rehabilitation:The intervention will be based on the needs, through the intervention PUI will rehabilitate 13 water network taking into consideration the following works:- Civil works-Installing new water pipes-All needed accessories.-Water tree connections for each building.•Sewage Network Rehabilitation:PUI will rehabilitate the sewage network in the targeted area taking into consideration the following works:-Maintenance&Rehabilitation of Manholes.-Execution of new Rain Water Manholes.-Execution of Rain Water pipes.-Cleaning of Manholes.-Cleaning of Sewage pipes.-In addition,executing new sewage manholes and sewage pipes.•Debris Removal.PUI will collect and remove the debris from the targeted area taking into consideration the following works:1-Debris removal from streets and roads,which includes:-Accumulation of the debris in the streets using shovel-Filling accumulated debris in the trucks&removing away to the outside landfills.2-Debris removal from building,which includes:-Accumulation of the debris inside the apartments-Removal of debris from the buildings,accumulating in front of buildings,subsequently removing the accumulated debris away to the outside landfills.PUI will contribute to SO2 through the implementation of:•Emergency Water Trucking. Based on the needs, through the intervention PUI will supply drinkable water for the targeted area taking into consideration the following works:-Supply drinking water by trucks to the targeted area.-5500m3 of water to the beneficiaries and filling water tanks based on needs and distribution plan.-Install water tanks and drinking system with all needed accessories if needed.-Sterilization of the water by adding disinfection materials and measuring the free chlorine, and testing the water to ensure the safety and quality.•Provision of 17 latrines and shower units for the targeted area along with all needed accessories.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1.Quality and/or quantity not adequate as per humanitarian standards- Likelihood: High- Impact: High- Mitigation measures: Ensure provision of safe water as per country Water Quality standards where possible and establish regular water quality monitoring including water source,water trucks &end-user. 2.Toilets present GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7-Likelihood:High- Impact: High- Mitigation measures:Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards&closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women &adolescent girls are consulted during design, implementation&monitoring phase of projects.Accountability mechanisms are put in place&received feedback from users is followed up.Lessons from previous experience are shared &learned.3.Potential exposure to explosive hazard during assessment/rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure- Likelihood: Medium- Impact: High- Mitigation measures:Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming.4.Children -18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organisation, its partners or contractors.Likelihood:High- Impact-High- Mitigation measures: Ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ labourers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labour in the contracts;check &register civil documentation.5.Risk of theft of rehabilitation/construction materials(generators/construction materials) by armed groups and used in conflict activities- Likelihood:Low- Impact: High-Mitigation measures: MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities; Promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

PUI’s activities are designed to ensure all individuals (women and girls, men and boys) targeted under these activities have safe access to clean, sufficient water supply to reduce the need for individuals to travel to obtain water, thereby decreasing all related protection risks. Also, PUI will support the most vulnerable female headed households to ensure safe, equal access to the water storage unit to the population.Some assumptions&risks are still under the spotlight and beyond the project’s control:Assumptions: Security situation remains stable, and humanitarian access consistentNo major fluctuation of Syrian PoundNo mass movements of populations into/out of intervention areasFinancial transactions are still possible to SyriaAvailability of material in the local marketAcceptance of communityRisks: 1.Local population does not accepts PUI´s intervention-Mitigation measures: PUI will ensure the full engagement of the community leaders&local stakeholders in project design&well introduce the benefits to the targeted community2.Lagging Return movements-Mitigation measures:PUI follows a multi sector integrated approach in programming to support the community resilience in a multi dimensions.3.Delay&change in the approval process- Mitigation measures:close follow-up with the stakeholder and take anticipatory approach.4.Volatile security situation-Mitigation measures:close follow-up with the stakeholder and locals to anticipate any deterioration in the security situation,conduct security training for the newly recruited staff to rise their awareness,ensure the circulation of the security SOPs within the staff5.Fluctuation in the exchange rate-Mitigation measures:keep the donor updated about the evolution of the context&report the impact of the fluctuation on the program in order to discuss the exit scenarios.6.Lack of qualified contractor-Mitigation measures:conduct constant survey to expand the list of qualified contractors working with PUI.
