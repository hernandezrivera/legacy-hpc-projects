Project Summary

The project will promote access to timely GBV services by provision of case management and psycho social support for women and girls who come to the women and girls friendly places. A total of targeting 8000 (5000 women, 1500 girls, 500 men, 1000 boys) beneficiaries will be supported in this project in line with the minimum standards in the GBV Inter Agency Standing committee guidelines (GBV IASC). Four women and girls friendly spaces will be established in It will provide community based psycho social support for women and girls in Kapoeta East .The project will expand access to psychosocial support for women and girls through case management by establishing women and girls friendly spaces (WGFS) in consultation with the community members.in Naita Hills,Katodori Payams and Mogo. To promote resilience, the project will support provision of small cash to women to enable them engage in income generating services while promoting protection of women in consultation with UNOPS which is providing cash for work of maintaining Narus road. The project will also focus on GBV prevention and mitigation through Community outreaches that will expand to engage the community in peaceful community co-existence. GRADO already has programing in peace building in Magwi and part of Kapoeta South and will build on this competency to expand this initiative to Kapoeta East

Needs

According to a joint Humanitarian assessment conducted by Relief and Rehabilitation commission and ARC including FSMNS Round 22 which GRADO Participated in greater Kapoeta, it was reported that women and girls were subjected to Domestic Violence, Early Marriage, rape and torture. While an approximately 3500 children were separated from their parents and some children were also recruited into armed forces and sexually abused. In Kapoeta East, only ARC has presence in Narus payam leaving the Naita Hills, Katodori Payams and Mogo with a very big gap.Although other services such as WASH is provided by Islamic Relief International, child protection by Save the Children, South Sudan Red Cross (NFI) Organization for Peace Relief and Development (OPDR) (Food) and UNOPS is providing cash for work of maintaining Narus road, many women and girls have to access for GBV related services. According to a Multi-sectoral Assessment Report for Kapoeta state lead by Nile Hope continually confirmed lack of GBV and general protection services for women and girls.
