Project Summary

Somalia is currently undergoing drought conditions with rains having failed/sub-optimal while some areas have had seasonal flooding. This situation is compounded by diseases outbreaks and conflict related displacement which further contributes to the increase in the number of people in need for WASH services. COVID-19, Acute watery diarrhea as well as cholera outbreaks have eroded the previous gains made in the provision of sustainable WASH services to the most vulnerable in Somalia. Recent studies show that rural areas and hard to reach districts have shown a significant increase in the number people in need attributed to increasing vulnerability due to limited access to water and sanitation among rural areas and IDP sites. Access to improved sanitation facilities in the target areas is very low with the JMCNA 2021 study showing that 57 percent of households do not have access to improved sanitation facilities. Additionally, 23 per cent of people across Somalia still defecate in the open, 49 per cent of which are in rural areas. Due to the drought, water sources are far from the households and this predisposes women and girls to GBV cases and assault while the disability affected population is fairing far much worse with their situation increased by these factors. Active in Development Aid which has been active in the target areas plans to target 15,000 vulnerable populations in Gedo and lower Jubba as well as Galgaduud regions of Somalia out of the estimated 333,848 people in need of WASH services. The projects key activities will include provision of emergency water services through water tracking, rehabilitation of existing water sources, rehabilitation and construction of household/communal and institutional latrines as well as hygiene promotion. each location will have water committees who will be chosen by the target communities following the WASH guidelines and will be trained on proper water management. The project will be implemented to ensure all members of the community including persons with disabilities have equal access to WASH services. ADA is similarly submitting HRP proposals in nutrition and livelihood in the same target areas hence the project will be integrated and have synergy with these other thematic areas. ADA will engage with the
community through FGDs for the different sexes and ages to ensure accountability and that their needs are catered for and able to benefit from the project. To ensure people with disabilities benefit equally from the project, ADA will design disability friendly infrastructure such as ramps, safer latrines, hand washing facilities among others. Data on usage of WASH services by the people with disabilities will be collected and shared with cluster/donor.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

ADA will institute various mitigation strategies to include but not limited to community engagement of all groups of people especially marginalized groups to ensure a joint transparent process in the response mobilization phase. Focused group discussions will be conducted with gender/age segregation so that people of different ages and sex are freely able to provide their inputs. ADA will seek to strengthen gender inclusive community-based protection mechanisms. Information campaigns will be boosted to ensure impartial and timely access to WASH services, especially in situations of displacement and at the IDP camps. ADA will as much as possible with community inputs, try to ensure water sources are away from military installations as well as reduce the distance between the water sources and human dwellings to ensure protection for women and girls. ADA will use evidence-based advocacy, especially with authorities responsible for the implementation of durable solutions for displacement affected communities. ADA will build trust in the efficiency of our Complaint and feedback mechanism to identify any situation of exclusion, and to condition implementation to be an equal, paramount and ensure direct access to all communities. We will ensure that complaints are followed up immediately and corrective measures are implemented on time. ADA will strive to provide of solar lights around water and sanitation facilities to reduce risk especially at night for girls and women. To provide safety and privacy, ADA will construct permanent and lockable toilets while separating the facilities for men and women. ADA will ensure to always follow the AAP and always prioritize the ‘do no harm’ principle. The organization will train all its employees and contractors on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse to ensure no beneficiary is exploited. For cater for disability, ADA will their input set up disability friendly WASH services.. Furthermore, for exclusion of ethnic groups and exposure of women and girls to sexual violence,including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance, ADA has mitigation measures in place. First of all, the project provides all target beneficiaries, equal access to project with no discrimination, creating a safer and more secure environment for children and women, beneficiaries will be clearly informed that the project is for the larger community through communty engagment meetings; This project is designed to have monitoring teams in place to assess, whether service delivery locations are adequate and are being used in a dignifi ed way;- A confidential feedback mechanism and protection reporting system will be designed and all beneficiaries will be informed about this mechanism and how to refer or report incidents or violations;-Measures are in place to ensure that beneficiaries understand that it is their right to receive equitable and safe assistance including people with specific needs or vulnerabilities;-All project staff will be trained on GBV prevention and response in emergencies and PSEA, inorder to ensure confidentiality and full adherence with the Do No Harm principle with strict dealing with Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and GBV issues.ADA maintains a zero tolerance policy towards PSEA in accordance with internationally-established protocols and standards for SEA protection. ADA staff is trained in PSEA protection, child safeguarding with clear protocols on reporting potential PSEA and child protection concerns. ADA will ensure that people with disabilities are given equal chances in any opportunities.-Additionally, ADA will give some preferential treatment for the people with disabilities with a target reach of atleast 3% of the total beneficiaries.ADA will ensure that services can be accessed by persons with non-mobility related disabilities. All the staff to be sensitizedand trained on the subject.
