Project Summary

SAPA (Sudanese American Physicians Association) is implementing a comprehensive health response intervention in Sudan to address the ongoing conflict's impact on healthcare. The purpose of these interventions is to provide essential primary and emergency health services, strengthen disease surveillance and reporting, and ensure timely responses to emergencies and disease outbreaks. The target population includes in Atbara (River Nile), in Port Sudan - Red Sea, in Northern State - Dongla and Halfa, in Karrari, and in Omdurman, and Sharg Elneel (Khartoum State). SAPA is implementing a comprehensive services packages which could be summarized on the following.
- Provide Essential Primary and emergency Health Care Services:
SAPA on its health services abilities is adopting the EMT Emergency medical team WHO initiative to synergies between primary care, outpatient services and emergency care through adopting in conflicts type 1 fixed and mobile.
- Establish healthcare clinics for primary care, trauma care, and Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (EmONC).
- Strengthen referral networks for effective patient transfers.
Expand Reporting Capacity of Early Warning Systems:
- Collaborate with local health authorities to enhance early warning systems for disease surveillance.
- Implement training programs for healthcare workers to improve timely reporting.
Strengthen Capacity to Investigate and Detect Disease Outbreaks:
- Conduct training for healthcare providers on disease surveillance, investigation, and detection.
- Establish a rapid response team for prompt control of disease outbreaks.
Support Health Authorities in Timely Response:
- Collaborate closely with health authorities to develop and implement emergency response plans where SAPA is operating.
- Provide resources and personnel for swift responses to emergencies and disease outbreaks.
Increase Access to Health Services:
- Implement mobile medical units to reach remote areas.
- Develop referral pathways for seamless transitions between mobile units and healthcare facilities.
Strengthen Capacity of Healthcare Providers and Community Health Workers:
- Organize training programs for healthcare providers and community health workers.
- Promote community engagement for active participation in healthcare.
Rehabilitate and Refurbish Health Facilities:
- Identify and prioritize facilities for rehabilitation, this activity will mainly be conducted in River Nile - Atbara identified health facility which serving mainly IDPs and done through a community effort. The support for this facility will be linked with the provision of support for facility renovation and necessary medical equipment. In This selected facility in Atbara, SAPA will be operating the health response part in collaboration with MedGlobal, where Med Global organization is going to cover the WASH and nutrition interventions to provide a comprehensive response for the IDPs and host communities in the center catchment area.
During the humanitarian health services interventions, SAPA is ensuring the protection mainstreaming policy Alignment and Training through align health response policies with Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) guidelines, specifically emphasizing the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). In addition, conduct comprehensive training sessions for all personnel involved in health response activities, including SAPA staff, local partners, and community health workers, to raise awareness about PSEA and enforce a commitment to its prevention, through its inclusion in all training and workshops sessions.
SAPA will establishment of Protection Mechanisms through develop and implement robust protection mechanisms within healthcare facilities and service delivery points to prevent and respond to incidents of sexual exploitation and abuse. SAPA is also ensuring gender-Inclusive Health Services through ensure that health services are designed and implemented with a gender-inclusive approach.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The SAPA project prioritizes protection mainstreaming throughout its design and implementation, aligning with interagency cluster coordination efforts and humanitarian standards. The following key strategies illustrate how the project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming:1. Robust Complaint and Feedback Mechanism: - SAPA is committed to implementing a culturally sensitive complaint and feedback mechanism that actively solicits the perspectives of affected populations. This mechanism allows beneficiaries to voice concerns and report protection-related issues, contributing to the identification and mitigation of protection risks.2. Community Engagement and Awareness: - Through community meetings, awareness campaigns, and the establishment of community-led monitoring systems, SAPA ensures active engagement with local communities. This participatory approach empowers individuals to understand protection principles, including Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), and provides them with a platform to express their needs and concerns.3.Protection Mechanisms within Healthcare Facilities: - SAPA is dedicated to establishing robust protection mechanisms within healthcare facilities and service delivery points. This includes confidential reporting mechanisms for survivors and witnesses, creating a safe environment where individuals can report incidents of sexual exploitation and abuse without fear of reprisal.4. Gender-Inclusive Health Services: - The project incorporates a gender-inclusive approach to health services, addressing the specific needs of diverse gender groups, with a particular focus on women and girls. Gender sensitivity training for healthcare providers ensures an understanding of gender-related issues and promotes inclusivity in service delivery.5. Integration of Protection in Health Assessments and services provision: - Protection considerations, including PSEA and gender inclusivity, are integrated into health assessments. By collaborating with protection-focused organizations, SAPA conducts comprehensive assessments to identify vulnerabilities and inform targeted interventions, reinforcing the project's commitment to protection mainstreaming.6. Alignment with Health cluster strategy on PRSEAH & APP Guidelines:SAPA aligns with WHO - PRSEAH's Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) guidelines. This involves building staff capacity on AAP principles, integrating AAP into planning and monitoring processes, providing timely information to communities, and actively involving affected populations in decision-making processes.7. Reporting and Advocacy: - SAPA generates reports on AAP interventions, feedback received, and actions taken. These reports contribute to collective advocacy for emerging protection needs/issues, fostering a collaborative approach with interagency cluster coordination efforts.The intervention above will be directly adherent toThe WHO Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.

SAPA is dedicated to implementing a robust, culturally sensitive, and accessible complaint and feedback mechanism as part of its Humanitarian Response Plan 2024 in Sudan. This mechanism aims to actively engage affected populations, ensuring their perspectives are heard and considered for the improvement of healthcare programming. This commitment resonates with health cluster's expectations for implementing Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) practices.Components of SAPA's Complaint and Feedback Mechanism:1. Dissemination of Comprehensive Information: - SAPA will provide beneficiaries with detailed information about available feedback and complaint mechanisms.2. Collection Channels: - Establishing hotlines for direct communication. - Conducting focus group discussions to gather collective insights. - Maintaining community feedback logbooks for written submissions. - Conducting patient exit interviews to understand individual experiences. - Forming community committees to foster continuous engagement.3. Alignment with Health cluster Guidelines: - SAPA recognizes and aligns with health cluster's AAP guidelines to ensure the active participation of affected children and families in decision-making processes.4. Staff Capacity Building: - SAPA will build the capacity of its staff on AAP principles to facilitate effective implementation.5. Integration into Planning and Monitoring: - AAP will be integrated into SAPA's planning and monitoring processes, including workplans, budgets, assessments, satisfaction surveys, and participatory development meetings (PDM).6. Provision of Timely Information: - SAPA commits to providing timely information to affected communities, ensuring safe, equitable, and inclusive platforms for consultation throughout the program.7. Community Sensitization: - SAPA will sensitize communities on available feedback channels and alternative face-to-face platforms.8. Reporting and Advocacy: - SAPA will generate reports on AAP interventions, feedback received, and actions taken. - Contribution to collective advocacy for emerging needs/issues in collaboration with health cluster coordination and other partners.SAPA will track the following APP indicators: - Number of people sharing concerns and asking questions through established feedback mechanisms. - Number of partner staff trained on AAP.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

SAPA's project within the Humanitarian Response Plan 2024 in Sudan is committed to actively supporting Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, with a specific focus on addressing Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) complaints. The project aligns with interagency cluster coordination efforts to ensure a cohesive and collaborative approach to accountability and feedback within the humanitarian response. Here's an outline of how SAPA will contribute: 1. Integration with Inter-Agency Cluster Coordination: - SAPA will actively engage with relevant Inter-Agency Clusters to align its accountability and feedback mechanisms with broader humanitarian efforts. This includes collaborating with health, nutrition, and protection clusters to ensure a comprehensive approach.2. Information Sharing: - SAPA will share comprehensive information about its feedback and complaint mechanisms with other agencies involved in the response. This ensures that the affected populations have consistent and accessible channels for raising concerns across various humanitarian services.3. Joint Training Initiatives: - Collaborating with other agencies, SAPA will participate in joint training initiatives focused on Accountability to Affected People (AAP). This includes building the capacity of staff from multiple agencies on AAP principles, including prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA).4. Harmonized Reporting: - SAPA will work towards harmonizing reporting practices with other agencies to streamline the collection, analysis, and dissemination of feedback. This includes contributing to collective reports on AAP interventions, feedback received, and actions taken.5. Cross-Cluster Collaboration on SEA Prevention: - SAPA will actively collaborate with clusters involved in protection and gender-based violence to integrate and reinforce measures for preventing Sexual Exploitation and Abuse within the project and across the broader humanitarian response.6. Sharing Best Practices and Challenges: - SAPA will participate in interagency forums to share best practices and challenges related to accountability mechanisms, seeking continuous improvement and learning from the experiences of other agencies.7. Collaboration on Community Sensitization: - SAPA will work closely with other agencies to collectively sensitize communities on available feedback channels, ensuring a consistent and coherent approach to community engagement and awareness.8. Establishment of Joint Mechanisms: - SAPA will explore opportunities to establish joint mechanisms, such as shared hotlines or community committees, in collaboration with other agencies to enhance the accessibility and effectiveness of feedback systems.9. Contributions to SEA Task Forces: - In alignment with Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) guidelines, SAPA will actively participate in SEA task forces.
