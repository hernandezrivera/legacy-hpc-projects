Project Summary

- The intervention in the project area is based on rehabilitation the water system in Heish town to provide drinkable water to the population who have started returning to town and neighboring villages and suffering from significant low daily share of water per capita(less than 10 L/Day) through the network and this forces them to rely on trucked water from untrusted sources which caused water-borne diseases like skin and digestive diseases in addition the high costs of trucked water, and to consolidate the process of supporting stability in the region and restarting economic life.
- Heish is a Syrian town located in Al Ma'ra district to the south of Idleb governorate, its population is now 30,000 including 10,000 IDPs, and the total population of its neighboring villages (Sahyan, Dar Al-Salam, Sheikh Dames, Amudiyeh, Maar Hattat, Kfr Mzadeh, Ameriyeh, and Armanaya) is 21,700, therefor the total number of project's beneficiaries will be 51,7000.
- Heish water system is consisting of 7 wells that were drilled by Water Establishment and distributed on two water stations (Southern and Northern water station), three water tanks and water network which covering all Heish town and working properly.
- After the crisis the water system needs rehabilitation for many reasons:
• The water network within the city is in good technical condition, but the flowing lines that connect between the main water tank inside city and neighboring villages is damaged and out of service, so the water doesn't reach to the neighboring villages of Heish town.
• There are two wells in southern water station out of service, so the station doesn't work with full operational capacity, therefor the quantity of provided water from the operating wells is insufficient for people's needs.
• Electricity is not available regularly in the city, it's available at an average of 3 hours a day, so it must provide an alternative power source.
• The population had increased to 30,000 people in Heish town including 10,000 IDPs, and 21,700 in neighboring towns.
- The project depends on the rehabilitation of two water station, as well as the necessary maintenance of the water tanks and flowing lines to achieve safe and stable water delivery to the beneficiaries across the water network as per the WASH cluster standards, in addition to monitoring the quality of the water, and periodically collecting and testing water samples from different points of the water network to conduct all kind of water quality tests.
- The project will support 100% of the operation cost of the water station in the first and second months, then by 50% in the third month while the Water Unit (which operates under the supervision of the city Local Council) should cover the operation cost’s gap from the collected fees then continue to collect fees, which provides sustainability of the project.
- After implementation of the project the drinkable water will be provided for the whole population in the city and neighboring villages across the water network in sufficient quantities (at rate of 50 L/d/c) which meets the needs of people and the standards of the WASH cluster.
- The project will also reduce the suffering of population especially women and children due to use trucked water for drinking, cooking and personal hygiene, thereby, improving their hygiene, and protecting them against skin diseases and other water-borne diseases and availability of drinkable water will positively be reflected on other activities of the population and will reduce physical and psychological burden for them.

Needs

Based on the study conducted from 07/01/2019 to 15/01/2019 needs were assessed in the form (Water Project Funding Request) as following:- The project will cover Heish town which its population is now 30,000 including 10,000 IDPs and some of neighboring villages of Heish as (Sahyan, Dar Al-Salam, Sheikh Dames, Amudiyeh, Maar Hattat, Kfr Mzadeh, Ameriyeh, and Armanaya), the total population of these villages is 21,700. - The total water quantity that's proximately pumped from all current operating wells for 3 hours a day is:35+40+45+30+30=180 m3/h180×3=540 m3/day = 540*1000=540,000 L/Day540,000÷51,700=10.44 L/Day/Capita which is very low, so population are suffering especially women and children due to use trucked water which is insufficient for drinking, cooking and personal hygiene and it is unsafe caused water-borne diseases like skin and digestive diseases, in addition the high costs of trucked water (about 2200 Syrian Pounds per truck, an average monthly consumption of a family is two trucks), which is exhausting the already consumed financial resources of both Host and IDPs community.- The town has 7 wells distributed on two water stations but two wells out of service need maintenance, three tanks (two high tanks and one ground tank) all of it need repair, the flowing lines that connect between main water tanks of Heish and neighbor villages are damaged and need maintenance and electricity is not available regularly in the city it's available at an average of 3 hours a day, so it must provide an alternative power source (providing generator). The needs assessment sources:Local Council and Water Unit of Heish city.Reports by Ballad Field MEAL personnel.Technical study by Balad engineering team.

Implementation of activities

1.1 Improved access to water as a result of repair and rehabilitation of water systems- In the first 15 days of the project our filed stuff will signing of the MOU with the Local Council – Water Unit, start recruiting the staff for the project and setting the technical specifications for soliciting bids for the necessary maintenance work.- In the end of first 15 days calling for tender to provide an alternative power source by purchase and install a generator to run the station when power is cut off.- Starting maintenance for wells in the southern and northern stations by providing and installing the required electrical and mechanical equipment (2 submersible pumps with capacity of 100 HP, Electrical cable 3×70 with length of 800 m and Galvanized pipes with length of 700 m), maintenance for southern station flowing line by providing and installing horizontal pump for the southern water station and all the necessary equipment (control panel, electrical cables, galvanized pipes), maintenance the flowing lines that connect between main water tanks and neighboring villages of Heish with all the necessary (L-pipes, valves, flanges….) and implementing necessary repairs and maintenance the northern and southern high water tank and the ground tank in the southern station. 2.1 Improved access to water due to provision of water disinfectants- Monitoring the quality of the water, by providing what is needed for water purification and periodically collecting and testing water samples from different points of the water network to conduct all kind of water quality tests, water Quality control for the produced quantities of water from the well, in addition to calibrate the needed chlorine level to purify the water, the project will also reduce the suffering of population especially women and children due to use trucked water for drinking, cooking and personal hygiene, thereby, improving their hygiene, and protecting them against skin diseases and other water-borne diseases also availability of drinkable water will positively be reflected on other activities of the population and will reduce physical and psychological burden for them.2.2 Improved access to water through the operation & maintenance support to the water systems- After completing all maintenance works the project will support the stations 100% of the operation cost of the water station in the first and second months, then by 50% in the third month by providing (fuel, engine oil, filters, stuff salaries and with all that is necessary, while the Water Unit should cover the operation cost’s gap from the collected fees.2.3 Improved access to water through establishment of water safety plans- The project works on the principle of sustainability and that will ensure the water security of the population, the sustain service will raise the population trust in the service body who will be responsible of operating the system.Water Unit will collecting fees monthly from each family, and with assumption of 2400 subscribers will participate in the fees collection (some most vulnerable families will be excluded), the monthly fees that will be collected will be enough for covering running costs of the water station including all needed operational supplies, staff, regular maintenance and also it will be save about 2,190 € monthly, which can use in increasing the quality of life for the city and its inhabitants, with better opportunities to enhance economic activities.These fees collection activities will be monitored to fulfill all gaps for better coverage percentage, involving all community’s main actors in order to create better awareness and commitment. During all activities M&E team will Monitoring and evaluation all maintenance works and of the collection work carried out by local councils - Water Unit.By the end of the project all assets will handed over to Water Unit.

Protection Risk Analysis

- Activity:Support to water supply systems - Protection Risk/Threat:1- Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empowers or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors –Medium.2- Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities –Low - Mitigating Measure:1- Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organize joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care.2- Include in monitoring formats; Ensure 3rd party monitoring considers analysis of inadvertent benefits to opposed groups and consequences.MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities.Promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. - Monitoring:1- Agency field monitoring and consultation with communities; Proper stock management system in place.2- Monitor safety of construction activities, condition of heavy equipment; Introduce Safety Checklist. - Acceptable Level of Risk:Yes

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Fuel Prices fluctuation and we can mitigate it by adapting the operation hours according to the price differences.- Temporary closure of border for transshipments at Turkey or within Syrian, may disrupt humanitarian response. There may be diversion of supplies especially in locations under the control of armed groups. Transport and handling of supplies may lead to damage of items. Earlier experience has shown the need for partner’s staff hibernation, restricted movements or suspension of activities is short term. - In case there is a military operation and a resultant escalation of conflict, there may be large displacement of people needing WASH assistance. Partners will ensure relevant local authorities are aware of response and commit to ensuring basic safety/access.- Well collapse during maintenance and we can mitigate it by Re-study the project by finding alternative solutions such as increasing the number of pumping hours to compensate for shortages.- Changes in the areas of control between: Turkey or Government of Syria or opposition groups. In case of unusual changes or diversion, donors will be updated on the situation; and necessary measures will be taken.
