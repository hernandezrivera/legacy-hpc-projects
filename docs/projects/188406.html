Project Summary

MWL's intervention will target 1500 families ( 6000 individuals) as: (Male: 1500, Female: 2000, Boys:1200, Girls: 1300/ PWD: 500) for 12 months through 3 interventions as follows:
1. MWL will target 19 camps in Dana, ldleb, and Maaret Tamasrin sub-districts through providing adequate drinking water by trucking water. (tanks) (35 L/Per/day) with constant water quality assurance through chlorination treatment and testing, aligned with distribution of plastic receptacles to transport water from to reservoirs.
2. Providing Hygiene kits for 1500 families (for one time) while the aim of distributing hygiene kits to cover the needs of infected families of Covid-19. MWL distribution team will be conducted the distribution by adopted door-to-door modality by taking into concentration all the prevention measures (hand sanitizer, wearing masks, keeping social distance, and wearing gloves. The team will deliver the families the kits without any touching or entering the BNFs homes. In case, the camps have more than one infected person MWL will target all the families by distributing the hygiene kits.
3. Awareness-raising sessions on personal hygiene and protection measures adopted in the camps (based on WHO recommendations) will be conducted in targeted camps and communities. The sessions will be provided physically by MWL team. They will be conducted in a hall or tent with a maximum number of participants 15 persons. The hall will be equipped with all protection tools such as masks, stabilizers in addition to ensure the social distance between people.
These sessions will be conducted in coordination with camp management and local councils, and it will be targeting local councils, camp management, and local communities’ key people such as teachers, principles, clerks and other effective persons. In each targeted camps MWL will conduct two sessions, 13 camps in the targeted communities, 26 sessions at the beginning of the project.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2022, in IDP sites access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for more than 2 million people in IDP sites. Water trucking services are the only source of water for 73% of IDP site residents. Despite the sector assistance, 24% of IDPs in sites couldn't access one or multiple hygiene items, 23% faced barriers to effective handwashing. HNAP COVID-19 vulnerability mapping - Round Six - October 2020 confirmed that the risk of contagion remains very high were 947,072 at high risk to Covid-19 in Dana sub-district.Mercy without limits' filed team conducted 28 interviews on 21st September 2021 in 28 different camps within four sub-districts as following: Dana, Sarmda, Maarrat Tamsren and Atareb. Out of findings, 90% of those informal camps were not supported by any organization, where people buying their water needs at high prices through water tanks periodically. The average water consumption per person on a daily basis according to the data collected at: 9.38 liters per day. Skin diseases such as leishmania, eczema, etc., are common due to difficulty of bathing and water scarcity. 8% of camps have public bathrooms which poses girls and women at risk of protection while they go and return.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Movements of water trucks in congested, camp-like settings L:H, I: L Mitigation: Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding location of distribution points, delivery route and its schedule; Avoid location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establish clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes, • Long distance to water collection points to be travelled not safe (exposure to GBV risk, UXO/ERW hazard). L:L, I: H Mitigation: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards(200-500m) and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consult separately girl, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

This project is built taking into account: Do No Harm, Age, Gender, and commitment to safe and equal access to assistance. The project team will set the necessary participatory mechanisms for different groups in the targeted locations, including men, women, boys, and girls, in addition to the PWDs, elderly people who are the affected people in the COVID -19 and decide which stakeholders need to be involved in the different implementation stages. MWL ensure preventing and responding to SEA acts by integrating PSEA in MWL policies and making sure that all personnel read and sign the code of conduct.
