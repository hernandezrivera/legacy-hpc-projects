Project Summary

GOALPrime Organization Nigeria is proposing to undertake an integrated Education in emergency Program in Gwoza, Jere, and Monguno, focusing on the congested camps. The project targets to reach 5000 school age children that are out of school or have not been in school before due to insurgency with specialised assistance in formal and non-formal education. These activity is targeting out of school children due to the effect of insurgency, newly arrived children, and other vulnerable groups that could not have access to formal education.
In the formal education part of the project, 2000 out of school children (young adolescent 10-14 years and Old adolescent 15 – 18 years) will be enrolled in formal education across Gwoza, Jere and Monguno LGAs. The enrolment drive shall focus on ensuring that adolescent boys and girls are enrol into formal education, while 1000 children (500, boys and 500 girls) are targeted across the three LGAs to enrol into safe and protective non- formal education. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will train community volunteer teachers in Education in Emergency subjects, supported by a community education coalition. In addition, sustainability will be a focus from the beginning of the programme by coordinate with other partners and government to ensure 100% mainstreaming of all the children that are reach with non-formal education.
This project shall be implemented with gender lens as 50% of the volunteer teachers for the non-formal part of the project will be female teachers and 50% will be men. The teachers will be train on how to manage and monitor children in school, especially the female ones to note their specific needs and situations that may hamper their performance in school activities.
To develop formal provision, minimal classroom renovation and gender appropriate wash provisions will be undertaken. The main investment will be in human resources through the dissemination of teaching and learning materials and teacher’s training. 50 teachers will receive training to teach at right level(T&RL), also 21 temporary learning space will build across three LGAs to facilitate the activities of non -formal education part of the project. The non-formal education classes and ongoing mentoring will technically prepare the children to be able to catch up with formal education class methodologies. GOALPrime shall through this project train and empower school 30 management committee members SBMC, 10 in each project community to protect education and its activities in their various communities.

Needs

Every child needs access to quality and free education. Every child deserves a better life, without education either formal or non-formal there will be difficult to encode and decode. An estimated 1.8 million people are displaced in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe, and around 40 percent (0.8 million) of the said population is comprised of school aged children between 3 and 17 years displaced across the three states. While the number of displaced persons and returnees increased compared to the previous year. The total number of children and education personnel in need decreased from 2.9 million for 2018 to 2.2 million for 2019 base on education sector estimates (EiEWG HNO, 2019). There is therefore, a need to put more efforts to take out every school aged child off the street and every other place of need and enrol them in formal or non-formal education.Recent data on the key education indices in the NE still indicate a dire situation in education with Yobe recording as one of the highest rate of children out of school at 42.2% compared with 27.2 of the estimated children out of school in Nigeria. Borno is reported in the survey (MICS 2016-2017) as having the highest number of girls out of school in Nigeria at 71%. Education sector therefore, faced with an upward task of infrastructural rehabilitation and expansion, education management stretching and need to look into combining education needs such as skills acquisition and protection against attacks. Evidence have shown that the deliberate and systematic destruction of learning institutions, killing and maiming of learners and teachers had adverse impact that is deeply imbedded in the communities. There is need for the enrolment of out of school children in both formal and non-formal education, rehabilitation of school structures as well as training of teachers and provision of learning material for the children. GOALPrime understand that most of the out of school children could not afford the school enrolment fee, therefore every child that will be part of the enrolment will be given complete school enrolment kits and it shall be design according to their age group and gender. We shall engage the parents, teachers and SBMC in the assessing need stage, designing of activities and reviewing and changes to have them make input and influence the decision in the project.

Activities

Activities: 1Profile and enrol 2000 out of school children in Gwoza, Jere and Monguno LGAs: GOALPrime shall identify formal schools in the three LGAs and profile school age children, distribute school uniform, bag, written material, and sandals to the beneficiaries and ensure that they are registered in school.Activity 2Identification of communities and camp sites in target LGAs and establish safe temporary learning spaces (TLS) with standards from the state school declaration of global coalition to protect schools from attacks. Activity 3 Training of community volunteer teachers with minimum qualification of (NCE) to deliver EiE learning component and live messages.Activity 4Enrolment of 1000 school children into informal education system and distribution of teaching and learning materials and school activities commence.Activity 5Training and establishment of community education coalitions for TLS management.Activity 6Advocacy with SAME for mainstreaming TLS structures and teachers into the government non-formal education system.Activity 7Construction of segregated latrines.Activity 8Rehabilitation of damaged classrooms and latrinesActivity 9Renovation of damaged class rooms.Activity 10Examination of students in the TLS to allow them to be mainstreamed into formal education.Activity 11Advocacy with the Education in Emergency working group to other partners to coordinate in mainstreaming the children in the non-formal education into formal system of education.
