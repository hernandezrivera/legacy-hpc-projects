Project Summary

Through this project Almassar with their partners (MOE) will respond to an urgent and critical emergency for people affected by the conflict in South Kordofan state, we targeting vulnerable,IDPs, undeserved children, teachers, PTA's, and host affected community.
The activities of this project include: Rehabilitation of 16 classrooms and 4 teachers offices in 5 schools in Abujubaiha ,Alaabasyia and Rashad localities using environmentally friendly soil-stabilized blocks (SSB), Rehabilitation of 5 school latrines each with 4 doors, handwashing facilities with water tanks for 5 schools, also Almassar will provide Education Materials Supplies and seating’s targeting 8216 student in Abujubaiha (3203) / Rashad (3266 )/Alabasyia (1747) (half of them female ), conducting training for 300 Teachers (100 teacher per locality),300 PTAs (100 per locality) (45% female) including psycho social support and other life skills (health and hygiene Education, Disaster Risk Reduction, child protection ) for 5 days (per locality) .
Almassar will intend to enhance the capacity of communities, Parent Teacher Association (PTAs)
Almassar will closely monitor - involve the ongoing activities through systematic observation and documentation of the implementation of the project, based on the project plan. Regular joint field visits will be conducted for monitoring, information collected and to assess the achieved results of planned project objectives. MOE will be through monthly progress reports, monthly staff review meeting and supervision visits to the field. Narrative and financial reports will be prepared and shared with Donors and MOE with all required information (monthly, quarterly and final report).

1 - Needs

This project aims to ensure that school age children affected by emergencies (including conflict, natural hazards), particularly children with disabilities, continue to get access to safe and quality formal and non-formal education with targeted interventions. The 2020 HNO has identified 1,461,032 people in need of access to educationthe statistics show that around 80 per cent are children 4-17 years, and 30 per cent are adults including, youth, education personnel and community members, however Children in emergency affected communities are adding pressure to existing education facilities which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources, Moreover, the economic crisis impacted people’s lives and their purchasing power, thus, many more households cannot afford schooling costs. As a result, children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor

2 - Activities / Outputs

Objective 1: The environment for basic education for children (Boys & Girls) improved and children have equally easy access to basic Education. 1.1 Construction / Rehabilitation of 16 classrooms and 4 teachers offices in 5 schools (Karima, Khalid-ibn Walid, and Kanjara schools in Abujubaiha locality and Al-eghiebish and Dar-el salaam schools in El-liri) using environmentally friendly soil-stabilized blocks (SSB), construction/rehabilitation of 5 school latrines each with 4 doors, handwashing facilities with water tanks for 5 schools, also Almassar will provide Education Materials Supplies and seating’s targeting 19400 student in Abujubaiha (13580) / El-liri localities (5820 ) (half of them female ). Objective 2: Quality of basic education improved and affected children have equal, sustainable access to quality basic services.2.1 conducting training for 400 Teachers (200 teachers per locality),200 PTAs (100 per locality) (45% female) including psychosocial support and other life skills (health and hygiene Education, Disaster Risk Reduction, child protection) for 5 days (per locality).
