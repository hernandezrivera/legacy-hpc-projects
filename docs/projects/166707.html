Project Summary

Provided technical support for IPC-WASH COVID-19 related activities in the Isolation Center (30 beds capacity);Provide medical and MHPSS support to patients affected by Covid19, including isolation, diagnosis, treatment and referral
- Perform a health needs assessment including capacity building supporting activities
- Support the identified health workers to diagnosed, treat, isolate and refer patients with covid19 adequately
- Identify and train psychosocial (pss) workers to provide pss support to patients and families
- Create and train staff on triage, medical and referrals protocols according to MoH and WHO guidelines
- Monitoring of prevention, diagnosis, treatment and referral of diseases like tuberculosis, malaria and HIV-AIDS
- Provide supervision and technical guidance on the covid19 response to the health workers identified
- Provide medical equipment and health commodities for isolations wards
- Ensure availability PPE and disinfectants to patients referred to home isolation
- Provide support on the diagnosis mechanisms available ensuring the safety of the health workers
- Coordination to ensure laboratory testing equipment, training on interpretation results and delivery package including the creation of protocols
- Provide PSS support to health and support workers
- Identification of ambulance services and strengthen patient referral mechanisms using existing community networks and private sector partners
- Coordinate and strengthened local health clusters organisation and referrals mechanisms;Provide Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) activities related with the COVID-19 Isolation Centre, and MHPSS Support to the community
- COVID-19 awareness training (Health and IPC) for community focal points
- Identification of IPC focal points at community level
- IPC training supported by IEC material at key locations within the community
- Development of IPC guidance for COVID-19 household level isolation
- Hygiene and social distancing promotion campaigns through mass media (radio, sms, digital, megaphone, etc.)
- Creation of a communication campaign regarding the isolation centre, including community actors and key messages about stigma and rumours
- Coordination with key stakeholders for the distribution of IPC kits for household level disinfection
- Creation of a MHPSS hotline for people affected by prevention and restriction measures
- Strengthen pathways for protection (GBV, children, people with MH conditions, older adults and others)
- Inform key communities and service providers about the updated pathways. Involve community members in the planning of the centre at all stages
