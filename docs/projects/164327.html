4 - Needs

Based on results from a countrywide household assessment, an estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. Food security exists when all people at all times have physical and economic access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food to meet their dietary needs and food preferences for an active and healthy life.” Some 70 per cent of the assessed households reported that food accounted for more than 65 per cent of their expenditure. More than 37 per cent of food insecure households reported having outstanding debt and 57 per cent of food insecure people had adopted negative coping strategies. (2020 Syria HRP_FSS draft).Based on the sector-based Food Security and Livelihood Assessment (FSLA) 2019 factsheets in ldeb governorate, the below table showcases the levels of food insecurity reflecting the high need for food assistance programs:Food Security Index (CARI): Severe Food Insecure 3.60%, Moderate 23.20%, Marginally 56.30%, and Food Secure 16.80 % while Coping Strategies: Emergency 28.20%, Crisis 19.30%, Stress 29.70%, No coping 22.9%respectively. Based on the mentioned figures, there is clear need for general food assistance with targetingthe most vulnerable groups such as female headed of households, young girls andchildren.

5 - Implementation of activities

All components of this project will be implemented by Mercy-USA contracted field staff under the direct supervision of Mercy-USA management from the Turkey office Mercy-USA will attend all FSL Cluster meetings in Turkey, submit 4W's, along with regular information and updates about the project, and the region. Efforts will be made to coordinate with any humanitarian actors on the ground to ensure duplication is avoided. Field staff will coordinate with local councils to emphasize neutrality, and adherence to humanitarian principles while ensuring all stakeholders are informed in the area. When possible, temporary staff will be hired from the beneficiary community to maximize efficiency and impact while building local capacities. - Food Baskets will be purchased inside Turkey, and shipped cross border to the target area. - Food baskets will be delivered to designated distribution points in each targeted location in coordination with the local council in each area. - Beneficiaries will be informed of this location and Mercy-USA staff will ensure protection measures are taken to ensure equitable access particularly for women, and young girls (separate lines, distribution during daylight hours, household delivery, etc.) - ***Household distribution will take place for those vulnerable beneficiaries who cannot receive their allocation at the distribution point. - Distribution schedule will be determined during the vulnerability assessment process in order to reasonably target the most vulnerable households first. - The program coordinator will oversee the project, compile and submit reports, and coordinate with the donor to ensure full compliance. In the field: - The project manager will be responsible for hiring and maintaining field staff with the direct supervision and approval of Mercy-USA management in Turkey.
