Project Summary

This project spans a duration of 9 months, comprising an initial 15-day preparation phase, a final 15-day period for tasks such as PDM,
Satisfaction Survey, endline survey and project closure, while the remaining 8 months will be dedicated to distribution activities.
The project is planned to provide life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance to 1150 of the most vulnerable HHs in the NWS within
Aleppo Governorate PC (SY02), Afrin district PC (SY0203), Raju sub-district PC (SY020303), in 11 camps and 3 villages (attached)
through cash-for-food distribution (funded by OCHA) for the first 5 months of the distribution period, followed by the distribution of in-kind
food baskets (funded by IHH) for the same targeted HHs for the next three months.
The proposed response will be delivered through the following two components:
1. Cash distribution (first 5 months): As per the recommendation of the FSL cluster and the Cash Working Group, five rounds of food
cash will be distributed to 1150 H.Hs each month for five months, $65 per family (for 30 days of food assistance) (in line with the SMEB
value).
In order to ensure rapid response and effective cash distribution, IHH will contract a Financial Service Provider (FSP) with a cash
distribution capacity of $373.750 (FSP criteria are specified in the activity section) in the targeted areas. The project and the grant amount
will allow beneficiaries to meet their food needs as this cash method will give them the flexibility to choose the actual products they
need. The distribution will be done through blanket modality to all beneficiaries in the targeted places.
2. Food basket distribution (last 3 months): 3450 food baskets weighing 58 kg and containing 10 items (attached items) that meet 70%
of calorie needs of a family of five for one month according to FSL cluster and WFP recommendation will be distributed to 1150 H.Hs
( 5192 individuals) on a monthly basis 1 time each for three months. These food baskets will be fully funded by IHH.
This project will also help to reduce serious food insecurity that can lead to malnutrition and other health related problems.
Distribution will be done with blanket modality covering the food basket standards according to cluster recommendation, considering
female-headed H.Hs, PWD and elderly, with 100% verification by the MEAL team , in close coordination with LC and local civil
authorities.Information and poster campaigns, surveys and monitoring will be carried out to ensure that beneficiaries are aware of and
have access to the CRM and PSEA service and that they access assistance in a safe and accessible, timely, accountable, and
participatory manner.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance to 1150 of the most vulnerable households (HHs) in Northwest Syria (NWS), addressing serious food insecurity and mitigating the risk of malnutrition and related health issues. Overview:Spanning a duration of 9 months, the project consists of a preparation phase, distribution activities, and project closure. It involves cash-for-food distribution for the first 5 months, followed by in-kind food basket distribution for the subsequent 3 months. Key Components: Cash Distribution (First 5 Months): Cash assistance of $65 per family will be distributed monthly to 1150 HHs for 30 days of food assistance. The cash distribution will provide flexibility for beneficiaries to choose the food items they require, ensuring rapid response and effective assistance. Food Basket Distribution (Last 3 Months): In-kind food baskets containing 10 essential items, meeting 70% of calorie needs for a family of five for one month, will be distributed to the same 1150 HHs for three months. These baskets will be fully funded by IHH, contributing to sustained food security and nutritional support. Targeting and Modality: Distribution will be conducted through blanket modality, covering all targeted beneficiaries. Special consideration will be given to female-headed households, persons with disabilities (PWD), and the elderly. Distribution will be verified 100% by the MEAL team, in coordination with local councils and civil authorities. Awareness and Accountability: Information and poster campaigns will be conducted to ensure beneficiaries are aware of and have access to Complaints and Response Mechanisms (CRM) and Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) services. Monitoring activities will be carried out to ensure assistance is provided in a safe, timely, accountable, and participatory manner. Expected Outcomes: Mitigation of Food Insecurity: The project will contribute to reducing food insecurity among vulnerable HHs, preventing malnutrition and related health issues. Enhanced Resilience: By providing both cash and in-kind food assistance, the project supports the resilience of beneficiaries, enabling them to meet their immediate food needs and maintain nutritional stability. Accountability and Transparency: Robust monitoring and awareness campaigns ensure that assistance is provided in a transparent, accountable, and inclusive manner, promoting beneficiary participation and safeguarding against exploitation and abuse.
