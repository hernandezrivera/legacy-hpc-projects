Project Summary

In 2022 humanitarian response, Plan International will improve protection environment and promote child rights in Somaliland for IDPs and vulnerable populations, particularly children with protection risks. The country has one of the lowest children and gender indicators in the world with only one-quarter of girls – 24% aged 15-24 considered as literate, 45% married before 18 years and 98% subjected to female genital mutilation/cutting (FGM/C). The COVID19 quarantine measures to prevent and control the spread of COVID-19 exposed children and women to increased protection risks, particularly within the home, owing to increased stress caused by the outbreak, exacerbating existing gender inequalities and violence. There has been increased exposure to violence especially to girls through FGM/C. According to the United Nations Population Fund, COVID-19 could have far reaching impacts on the effort to end FGM/C, with a potential 1.5 million FGM/C cases occurring over the next decade that could have otherwise been averted. Plan will work with CSOs and other partners to effectively promote and support improving community-based child protection mechanisms. Key interventions will target young girls and boys, their parents and other decision-makers to raise awareness on child protection and GBV, establish and strengthen referral mechanisms for case management and other services including MHPSS and financial support, and capacity building and advocacy with local authorities and for national agenda on centrality of protection (e.g. importance of child protection and campaigns on GBV, adoption of the positive discipline techniques, inclusion of children and policy formulation).
In August 2021, Plan International has commissioned an assessment that aims to analyse operational context (political, Social, economic, legal and governance) of the civil society and how power dynamics shape their strategic direction in Somaliland. The report findings will inform our CSO strengthening efforts and building influencing alliance. The most recent issue is the forced eviction in Lasanod region. Somaliland forced returns of internally displaced persons from South central Somalia which have had a devastating impact on displaced populations who were already vulnerable due to the intersection of multiple humanitarian crises including drought, conflict and COVID-19. The current situation has also increased the risk of Sexual Gender-Based Violence (SGBV) due to a breakdown in community structures, law and order, and a lack of physical protection. The situation has ignited a war of word between Somaliland authority and Somalia which could lead further eviction and retaliatory response. UN OCHA and the NGOs Consortium which Plan International is a member issued a joint press release expressing their regrets and concern.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

As interventions will be only in Somaliland, not Somalia - highest risks are anticipated as following with planned mitigation: - Plan will train on child safeguarding policy and ensure staff, partner staff, vendors, frontline workers, etc sign and commit to PSEA policy - Children with disabilities given special consideration for design/selection - Careful design and proper planning of needs, time, location – assess unique risks and barriers to participation - Include community members in project design and implementation - Include community members in selection criteria and process - Prioritize activities specifically for children with disabilities - Spot check at construction sites to ensure no risks to children or community - Ensure clear reporting/complaint process is displayed on site - Increased conflict sensitivity in programming - Careful selection and proper planning based on time and location - assess risks prior to activity, particularly distributions to ensure appropriateness of location and other considerations - Plan will also take environmental impact into consideration across project implementation with mitigation measures - collection of waste post distribution, long-term durable or recycleable materials as possible
