Project Summary

The project aims to respond to social cohesion and peaceful coexistence challenges in Qourqeena sub-district of Idlib. An inclusive and representative community committee will be established which will be responsible for overseeing the rehabilitation work, which involves marketplace rehabilitation through cash for work modality. The community committees will also involve in adopting the right approach in beneficiary identification to target the most appropriate beneficiaries. Additionally, the project will support small businesses through business support grants. The project also has a TVET component in the form of on-job training, where the businesses to be supported by the small grants will host the trainees to help them gain necessary skills. The project interventions were designed with the full participation of the communities. First, SYCAC engaged with communities to identify and prioritize their needs and agree on measures that could support them in strengthening their resilience, eventually promoting peaceful coexistence. The evidence collected, including community perspectives and priorities, informed the second phase, namely identifying project activities for community committee members which involves women, IDPs, people with disabilities, and youth to serve as a community-based management structure. The final phase will establish interventions based on community prioritization to improve living conditions in targeted areas, promote peaceful coexistence, and generating opportunities. These involve rehabilitating marketplace infrastructure by using cash for work modality, technical and vocational training mainly in the form of employability training for at risk youth, on the job training for at risk youth, and business support grants for small businesses with high potential for growth and job retention.
SYCAC prioritizes safety and security across all project activities. SYCAC will conduct regular risk assessment exercises to monitor the trends regarding safety and security risks. In the occurrence of a heightened military activity, the project activities will be immediately suspended until the security situation returns to normal. In the event of interference from local non-state armed groups, SYCAC will immediately inform the Donor organization and suspend its activities. SYCAC also designed the project activities in a way that minimizes the aid diversion risks as there are no activities involving transfer of goods or in-kind support.
In terms of GBV and SEAH, all project staff and stakeholders will be familiarized with SYCAC’s code of conduct, and PSEAH policy. This involves safe reporting and whistleblowing channels. SYCAC is committed to a Zero-Tolerance Policy toward sexual exploitation, abuses and all forms of unlawful harassment, including but not limited to sexual harassment. The staff recruitment process includes the vetting for records of prior misconduct and reference checking. SYCAC is also prepared to take other necessary measures to ensure that people involved in substantiated cases of sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment will not be recruited. SYCAC will also take all necessary measures to prevent sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment during cash for work activities, apprenticeship, and on-the-job training phases.
