Project Summary

The Project on Prevention and response to Gender Based Violence for conflict affected women, girls and men and boys in Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon in Northwest (Noketunjia, Mezem, Bui, Momo divisions) and Southwest (Limbe, Fako, Buea, Meme, Manyu divisions) of rgions of Cameroom is Plan International project which main objective is to contribute to a strengthened and protective environment and promote access to supported services for women and girls and men and boys affected by crisis, prevent GBV and provide appropriate gender and age support to victims. Also, this project will contribute to reducing the risk of gender-based violence and improve access to protection services for affected populations (hosts and internally displaced persons) through a coordinated humanitarian response based on humanitarian principles and prevention, community-based risk mitigation and survivor care activities.

Besoins / Needs

Background• Violent crisis that erupted in the Anglophone regions in Cameroon since Oct 2016 between the military and armed groups fighting for separation of the predominantly English-speaking North-West and South-West (NWSW) regions from the rest of Francophone Cameroon has driven over 530,000 women,men,girls and boys into internal displacement, including an estimated 35% women and 40% children (UNHCR/CP AOR, Apr 2019). The humanitarian situation continues to deteriorate with serious protection incidents, excessive human rights abuses, and violence against civilians. Displacement is increasing with the continuous destruction of villages and infrastructure by warring parties, fueling a climate of insecurity with the brunt of the conflict affecting the general population. Despite the massive scale of the crisis and immense needs, international attention has been scarce, leading the situation to be described as “the world’s most neglected displacement crisis” (NRC, Jun 2019). A joint Rapid Need Assessment (RNA) on gender, GBV and Sexual Reproductive Health was conducted by both Plan and CARE in 8 localities in the NW (Tubah, Bamneda 2, Kumbo, Belo, Batibo, Santa, Fundong and Bali) and 3 divisions in south west (Fako Meme and Kumba) with the objective to understand how the displaced female, male and children population are distinctly affected by the situation. The survey was designed with secondary data, in particular the OCHA IDP Populations Estimate and based on the report on security incidents reported. Existing Plan programming helped to identify the RNA target communities. Districts were targeted based on the number of IDPs, and communities were targeted based on Plan's knowledge of the general needs and their accessibility in terms of security.The RNA process consisted of primary data collection using Key Informant Interviews FDGs providing data around Child Protection (CP) risks, perceived needs, MHM understanding, and contextual practices from both genders. KII participants were selected based on their position within the community or official titles. These individuals provided specific information relevant to the situation of children in the communities. GBV needs • In March2019, the needs assessment conducted by Plan International and CARE International in the NW and SW regions have identified significant Child Protection and GBV risks, and many abuses committed against children including attacks on children and on schools, physical and psychological violence, sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), and risk of recruitment into armed groups, child labour, exploitation and neglect. Women, particularly young girls have been victims of rape cases and still continue to face high risk of SGBV, including rape, unwanted pregnancies and forced prostitution. Other issues identified include:-Women and girls face particular risk of assault, violence, abuse and exploitation in their communities.-Unwanted pregnancy-Early/forced marriage-Domestic violence-Lack of education-structural discrimination-Shame and stigma to report abuse cases-Lack of information on sexual reproductive health. Through the proposed project, Plan International will contribute to strengthening the protective environments and promote access to supported services for women and girls and men and boys affected by crisis, prevent GBV and provide appropriate gender and age support to victims. Also, Plan will contribute to reducing the risk of gender-based violence and improve access to protection services for affected populations (hosts and internally displaced persons) through a coordinated humanitarian response based on humanitarian principles and prevention, community-based risk mitigation and survivor care activities.
