Project Summary

It is aimed to support the maintenance/repair of existing water wells, new water wells and the continuation of the activity of existing water wells in Northwest Syria. In this way, it is planned to provide these aids for 5000 HHs living in Northwest Syria at the point of access to clean water. In this project, which is planned to be implemented especially in Idlip and its surrounding cities, IHH Humanitarian Relief Foundation does not have access problems in the region. In this project, it will work in coordination with the WASH Cluster.
This project will meet a critical need for those in need, as water access in the region is only possible through water distributions and water wells.
In addition, this project aims to achieve the following goals:
To provide a healthy and drinkable water source to the people of the region.
To prevent fatal cases caused by unhealthy water-borne diseases.
To prevent the loss of energy and time of people who have to walk for kilometers by providing easy access to a clean water source.
To direct the time and effort spent on water supply to education and a beneficial workforce.
To increase the livelihoods of the people of the region by making animal husbandry activities sustainable in all seasons of the year.

4 - Needs

There is a great need in the region for the maintenance/repair, support and construction of new water wells in the Sarmada and Maaret Tamsrin regions of Idlib. In this framework, a project is being considered to cover 5000 HHs in these regions where approximately 1 million people live.

5 - Implementation of activities

After the determination of the existing water wells and the report of the persons who will be assigned for the situation analysis, some activities will be carried out to solve the problems together with the determinations in these water wells. 1- It will be determined whether the existing water well supplies water in a sustainable way.2- Fuel support and generator maintenance/repair activities will be carried out for water pumps and generators providing energy.3- Maintenance/repair activities will be carried out for water pumps and solar panels providing energy.4- The quality of the water obtained from the water wells will be controlled.5- Chlorination activities will be carried out for the water obtained from the water wells.6- The design and cost items of new water wells may vary. Therefore, a long planning will be made on the estimated cost items.7- With the Monitoring & Evaluation activity, feedback will be received from the beneficiaries and improvements will be made.8- These activities will be regularly reported.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Disruption of water quality tests. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High. Against this possibility, ensuring the continuity of water quality tests will be supported.Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors. Likelihood: Medium Impact. Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organise joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care.Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities. Likelihood: Low Impact: High MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities. Promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership.Improper storage of hazardous supplies (chlorine and other) materials) - Possibility of explosion, human casualty and asset destruction. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High. Ensure proper storage of supplies with clear provision of care and safety consideration. Make sure that clear instruction for use is available for chlorine products primarily for household use.
