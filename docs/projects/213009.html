Project Summary

Elaf aims to target the urgent and evolving needs of communities in NW Syria affected by recent earthquakes and ongoing challenges. The project's multifaceted strategy enhances food security, supports the olive value chain in Jaindairis and Armanaz, and provides livestock feed support in Salqin and Raju. This initiative aims to improve agricultural livelihoods, foster economic resilience, and secure food sources, leveraging local capacities and resources for sustainable recovery and growth.
Detailed Activities:
Food Security Initiatives in Harim and Jebel Samaan :
Food Basket Distribution: Delivering 3,000 food baskets, each costing $60, across targeted districts to address immediate nutritional needs.
Cash Assistance for Food: Providing $65 monthly cash support to 500 families for nine months, facilitating access to essential food items.
Olive Value Chain Enhancement in Jaindairis and Armanaz:
Agricultural Inputs Vouchers: $20 vouchers for balanced soluble fertilizer, $200 cash vouchers for irrigation enhancements, and $30 vouchers for integrated pest management, targeting 500 olive farmers.
Cash for Work (CFW) for Harvesting and Pruning: Employing 400 workers at $8 per day for olive harvesting and 50 technicians at $10 per day for pruning, over a one-month period.
Olive Pressing Vouchers: Offering $100 vouchers to 500 farmers for pressing olives, facilitating oil and by-product production.
Livestock Feed Support in Salqin and Raju:
Feed Voucher System: Implementing a voucher system to provide high-quality feed, allocating $20 per sheep and $200 (10 vouchers at $20 each) per cow monthly, enhancing the health and productivity of livestock for 500 families.
Capacity Building Workshops: Organizing training sessions on optimal feed usage, animal health, and sustainable practices to empower livestock owners.
Market Linkages for Livestock Products: Strengthening supply chains and promotional efforts to improve market access for meat and dairy products.
Key Project Features:
Local Procurement: Prioritizing local sourcing to stimulate Syria's economy and support community businesses.
Inclusive Approach: Ensuring accessibility and benefit for all community members, especially prioritizing women, PWD, and marginalized groups.
Effective Complaint Mechanism: Establishing a transparent system for feedback and accountability.
Coordination with Clusters: Aligning with national and international efforts for maximized impact.
PSEA and Safeguarding Commitment: Upholding strict protection standards throughout the project.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Elaf is committed to leading the "Resilient Harvests" project, applying its expertise and resources to address the critical needs of earthquake-affected communities in Syria. By focusing on food security, supporting the olive value chain in Jaindairis and Armanaz, and providing livestock feed support in Salqin and Raju, Elaf aims to create sustainable solutions that empower local communities and contribute to the region's long-term recovery and development. Enhanced Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) Framework: Elaf will develop and implement a rigorous M&E framework, setting clear performance indicators for each component of the project. This framework will ensure continuous learning and adaptation, guaranteeing interventions are effectively meeting the community's needs.Community Engagement and Feedback Loops: Elaf prioritizes inclusive community engagement, establishing local advisory committees and feedback mechanisms to ensure all voices are heard and considered in project planning and execution. These initiatives will foster a sense of ownership and participation among the beneficiaries, enhancing the project's impact and sustainability.Capacity Building and Technical Support: Recognizing the importance of local capacity building, Elaf will conduct comprehensive training programs and workshops for farmers, livestock owners, and local workers. These sessions will cover modern agricultural practices, sustainable livestock management, and business skills to enhance productivity and market access.Environmental Sustainability and Innovation: Elaf is dedicated to incorporating environmentally sustainable practices across all project activities. This includes promoting water conservation, organic farming, and the use of renewable energy sources. Elaf will also explore innovative solutions to enhance agricultural and livestock production, such as mobile technology applications for market information and agricultural advice.Partnerships for Broader Impact: To maximize the project's reach and effectiveness, Elaf will collaborate with local governments, NGOs, private sector partners, and international organizations. These partnerships will leverage additional resources, expertise, and networks, creating synergies that amplify the project's benefits for the communities.Commitment to Gender Equality and Social Inclusion: Elaf ensures that gender equality and social inclusion are central to the project's design and implementation. Special efforts will be made to empower women, youth, and marginalized groups, providing them with equal opportunities for participation and benefit.
