Project Summary

Considering the high numbers of newly arriving people , there is a significant need for the provision of non- formal education support to minimize the gap in education provision for vulnerable IDP populations. Displacement is likely to have caused significant disruption, both to IDP and hosting communities. IDP children and youth put a significant strain on the capacity of host communities to accommodate increased education needs. Whilst there is willingness from host communities to support education needs of IDP children, school capacities are in many cases far overstretched. Overwhelming schools’ capacity to meet education needs through expecting them to accommodate new influxes of IDP children poses the risk of both undermining quality of teaching and learning for all school going children, as well as potentially ‘doing harm’ through raising tensions between vulnerable IDP and host populations. Considering the prolonged and violent nature of the conflict in northern Syria, moreover, there is a high likelihood that populations, both host communities and IDPs, have experienced significant war related stress/duress.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The security situation in Syria will allow the proposed programming with minimal interruption. SDH acknowledges that certain challenges are unavoidable in the Syrian context and designed the intervention with careful consideration of the complex operating environment. The risks to personnel and materials are multiple across the Syrian border. In light of risks, the scale and scope of program activities remains flexible and adjusted according to the context. high risk justification/Mitigation: Members of education staff will stop working during periods of bombing - Collaboration with DoE to gain legitimacy while working. Air attacks and indirect shots: Monitoring of news and information collection, Identification of health centers, Safe working area in the office, Hibernation, First aid training, Medical plan Corruption or fraud: Anti-corruption policy / training, credible control mechanism, procurement policy, code of conduct, check-in on staff and suppliers / Contractors \ financial policy and determination of powers COVID-19: Provide the schools with masks, sanitizer liquid, awareness sessions for the children by SDHs CCTCs health staff, triage point, Thermometer and social distance to ensure the safety of the children, plan B replace physical class by online class through using SDH platform. Chidren not attending the class, SDH will keep provide encouraging activities to keep them attending, as well providing them with needed school items and referral mechanism
