Project Summary

The project has 3 main objectives:
1- To reduce the risk posed by explosive hazards and the likelihood of exposure to them through implementing the EORE pillar of Mine Action Sector and raising the awareness and knowledge of civilians and humanitarian workers through implementing tailored EORE programming in 2 SDs: Afrin and Jandairis in Afrin district; note that Bahar has full access to Afrin district and has ongoing projects and previous MA projects there.
This objective will be achieved through conducting EORE sessions in the mentioned locations, targeting both children and adults in schools, Bahar's community centres, camps, and public spaces; as well as, conducting training for community focal points and humanitarian workers, as an indirect approach to disseminate the knowledge of EORE, providing victims assistance, and conducting contamination impact surveys in 4 communities, 2 in each of Afrin and Jandairis sub districts
2- Enhance equitable access to quality Child Protection services including (1) community-based PSS and AR, and (2) quality specialized CP services, mainstreaming children with disabilities, in Atareb sub district.
The CP objective entails CFS-based and outreach-based approaches; where CFS-based activities include:
- Establishment of 2 CFS in Atareb & Daret Azza SDs which will be tailored to be inclusive for CwDs through ramps and rehab of wash facilities, and providing transportation.
- Structured and sustained PSS program
- Parenting program
- Life skills for adolescents >13 years
- Case management & Referrals for children survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect, child labor, early marriage, children exposed to severe/extreme psychosocial distress, cases of CAAFAG, UASC.
While for outreach-based activities:
- AR for children and adults and IEC campaigns
- Semi-structured PSS
- Establishing CPNs.
- Referrals of children to the 2 CFSs and services in other sectors for a comprehensive response.
- Implementation of emergency response activities.
Note that there will be 4 outreach teams covering Atareb & Daret Azza SDs and implementing these activities and targeting schools, community and IDPs centers, and public spaces.
3- The GBV component aims to provide access for MWBGs survivors to life-saving specialized GBV response services provided at 2 WGSSs in Afrin and Ma’batli sub districts.
The activities will be:
At the WGSSs: AR program using the updated AR toolkit for W&G, and my safety- my wellbeing curriculum for adolescent girls, focused PSS for W&G survivors and at-risk of GBV, focused PSS and specialized case management supported by case fund, CMR, and referrals to other humanitarian services, in addition to VT and educational courses for the most vulnerable women and adolescent girls.
Through 4 outreach teams in Afrin and Ma’batli: Awareness raising program targeting men, women, boys, and girls (12 sessions per group), information sharing, PFA, referrals of W&G survivors of GBV to the WGSS.
Moreover, transportation for women and girls with disabilities to the WGSS to be provided with the available services given that the WGSS will be rehabilitated to be inclusive for women and girls.
Also, training will be provided to the GBV staff on GBV basics and CM, and they, in turn, will provide training to non-GBV health staff on CMR, PFA and referrals, and GBV basics.
To ensure effective coordination is in place, Bahar will continue to attend CP SC monthly coordination meetings and will submit 4Ws reports.
MEAL:
Pre-post test for the structured AR programs and training, observation sessions, Wellbeing and Satisfaction Surveys, Client feedback surveys for CM...etc. There will be also an appropriate AAP mechanism i.e. ensuring that the complaints and feedback mechanism is in place, as well as, the community is engaged during the design and implementation of the project. Bahar is depending on the M&E toolkit developed by the GBV SC for GBV projects' monitoring and evaluation.

3 - Needs

MA:Explosive accidents cause serious injury and death, leading to long-term physical and psychological trauma for survivors and their families and severely impacting their lives and livelihoods. According to the MA 2023 HNO, the estimated PiN in both Afrin and Jandairis is more than 175K. Also, the Protection sector 2023 severity scale for both areas is 4. Therefore, Bahar will provide MRE sessions, training for CFPs, victim assistance, and referrals of victims to PSS, and will ensure that the teams are capacitated to provide MRE services.CP:According to the draft 2023 HNO, Adolescent boys are more likely to be separated from family, recruited by armed groups or be involved in child labor, while adolescent girls are particularly at risk of early marriage, harassment and other sexual violence. According to the CP SC CiN, Atareb has more than 76K CiN, of whom 15.5 are CwDs, while Daret Azza includes 29K CiN, of whom 22.6% are CwDs i.e 6500 CwDs, and 46% are girls out of the total CiNGBV:Afrin is at 4th severity scale according to HNO 2022. According to Bahar’s needs assessment conducted in October 2021, IPV against women, early marriage against girls, emotional abuse against PwDs are the most common GBV types in Afrin; This matches with Voices from Syria and HNO 2022 findings stating that W&G are subjected to increased denial of economic resources and education, forced marriage. Thus Bahar will raise awareness on GBV, CM, and CB of workers to ensure quality interventions

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Bahar has a Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA policy that usuallyis attached to each employee’s job contract clarifying all the related procedures.Bahar will ensure the field teams attend PSEA\safeguarding trainings andrefreshing sessions provided by Bahar internally or externally. Bahar’s PSEA focalpoints responsibility is to ensure trainings are delivered to the project staff,informative materials (either soft copies in case of online or in-place approach ofimplementation) are distributed to both staff and beneficiaries, and updating anddevelop the PSEA policies including the complaints pathway/reporting mechanismregularly. The MEAL team will monitor if any danger is threatening the safety of thebeneficiaries and will report findings in order to take the appropriate measures.Bahar team will also inform the community, particularly men and youth, about thethe objective of the project and that its aim in order to mitigate exposing women andgirls to any potential harm or violence.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: COVID-19 pandemic may impose lack of BNFs’ access and participation inservices due to curfew restrictions, home lockdown, isolation, or people’s fear ofphysical interaction, as well as, the in-place approach might expose the participant to therisk of getting infected. Mitigation Measure 1: Bahar will provide sanitizers and PersonalProtection Equipment PPE to BNFs accessing the facilities and also during theoutreach activities and will ensure applying other prevention measures including socialdistancing, minimizing the groups sessions participants to 5-7 max. BNFs will beinformed also about measures taken to encourage them to access the services. Baharwas also a member of the GBV SC COVID-19 Task Force and will implement theassistance according to the guidance provided for GBV COVID-19 guidance and the otherSub sectors’ guidance and instructions as well when feasible. Finally, COVID-19 ARmessages will be mainstreamed and disseminated along the course of the project. Risk 2:Breaching Bahar’s CoC, safeguarding, and PSEA policies by the project staff against theBNFs in an intended or unintended way. Mitigation Measure 2: Bahar includesthese policies in the employees’ contracts and they are being delivered with orientationsessions by the HR department or the FP. In addition, Bahar has assigned 1 FP to conducttrainings on those policies, receive the allegations and act accordingly, ensure timelyresponse and investigate the allegations to take proper action.
