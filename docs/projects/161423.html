Project Summary

The project seeks to enhance the Livelihoods of food insecure IDPs, returnees and host communities in Yei River County Central Equatoria State. A total of 2,708 Households Equivalent of 16,248 Vulnerable Persons shall be Targeted to receive vegetable seeds, crop seeds and fishing kits. The intervention aims to Enhance the livelihoods of the vulnerable Households and subsequent improvement in food Security and to ensure that, women and adolescent girls are protected from exposure to Gender based violence,
A total of 500 women and 200 Girls shall be trained as TOTs on how to mould Fuel Efficient Stoves-an initiative that reduces the number of times women and girls go to the forest to look for firewood where they often get sexually abused. The project will be implemented directly by Mother and Child Development Agency for a period of one year in collaboration with local authorities, community leaders, farmers and farmers groups, Food Security and Livelihood Cluster as well as other humanitarian actors on the ground to build for synergy and avoid duplication of activities.

Currently 6.35 Million people representing 54% of the total population are classified to be in crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity. Among whom an estimated 1.7 Million are facing emergency (IPC Phase 4) and 10,000 People are in catastrophe (IPC Phase 5) compared to the same period in 2018 However, this situation is expected to improve slightly as seasonal harvest becomes available between September and December 2019. The food security situation is expected to deteriorate during the post-harvest period starting from January to April 2020 hence an estimate of 5.5 Million people will likely face crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity. The expected deterioration in food security situation is significantly attributed to Households food stocks depletion. Though the revitalized peace agreement signed in September 2018 is holding in most parts of the country, Yei river County still continue experiencing wave of Violence in the surrounding Villages causing displacement and food insecurity. It should also be noted that, in the past months yei County had been receiving Influx of returnees from Uganda and Congo. According to IPC projections released in August, most counties in central Equatoria are classified to be in crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Acute food insecurity therefore, Yei County has significant number of Vulnerable Households are in dire need of humanitarian assistance. Provision of emergency livelihood support is critical in averting from further worsening. This funding will be used to support food insecure households with vegetable seeds, crop seeds and fishing kits while at the same time training Households on better farming Methods the knowledge they can practice to increase food production and food options. The SSHF funding will go a long way to save lives of vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host communities. The proposed project will consider gender based violence mainstreaming through Fuel Efficient Stoves training to reduce exposure to SGBV risk among Women and girls. Data collection will be segregated to allow analysis by gender to help inform better the trends of food insecurity of different gender; hence this approach will help in providing specific intervention
