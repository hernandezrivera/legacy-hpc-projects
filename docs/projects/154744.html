Project Summary

The project is composed by a PHCC and an Emergency Hospital Room equipped with Pharmacy, Laboratory, X-Ray and Ultrasound, providing health care and assistance at Hassan Sham U2 IDP camp and surrounding camps (Hassan Sham U3 and Khazer M1) and host community. Targeting all the camp residents (approximately 5 thousand individuals), without any distinction based of gender, age, political affiliation or religion.
The hospital will be open 24/7, always with medical staff working. Also ambulance service is available for referrals of more serious patients who need to be transferred to Erbil hospitals.

Needs

The project started in July 2017 when a great influx of IDPs from Mosul fled from the comflicts. Initialy the project was for 8 months but due to the high need it contiued till November 2018, completing 1 year and 4 months of operations. Unfortunately, the IDP camps are still open without a date to closure. The number of famies decreased, however, due to the instability in the Iraq areas, every day new people arrive at the camps. The people residing at the camps (specially Hassan Sham U2, U3 and Khazer M1) depend on services provided inside the camp and it includes health services. These people can not be neglected and have the right to receive good care in the health sector. Befor ceasing it's activites in the U2 camp, ADRA made a brief assesment and figured out that, even with a local NGO covering the services of Primary Health Care, no emergency or more accute care is provided. The patients can be transfered to the neighbour camp, Khazer M1 where IMC has a more complete health facility, however, if referral to a bigger hospital is neede, IMC is not allowed to do it, while ADRA had no problem in referring any patient from different camps.

Activities

ADRA's health project meets the need of the people at the camp by providing primary health care and also hospital and emergency care. In cooperation with MSF who takes care of mental health and chronical diseases, ADRA provides all the communicable diases treatment, emergency response, image (X-Ray and Ultrasound) and laboratory diagnoses as long as dental care. ADRA is the only health facility to provide all these services. ADRA also complement the service provided in Hassan Sham U3 and Khazer M1. During the first year of operation, the Emergency Care was offered 24/7. Later the services were provided for 7 days a week but only during the day (from 9am to 4pm), however, if it's needed the services can be provided 24/7 again. ADRA plans to operate at the camp till it closes. On the event of closure, if the village around (Hassan Sham) is reestablished, ADRA is happy to hand-over it's facilities to the local authorities to use it in order to benefit the local community. In the event of not having the village reestablishment, ADRA is willing to transfer the portable devices to other project or donate it to any health facility in need.

Protection Risk Analysis

For being in the Ninewa province but under control of Kurdistan, the camp can be affected by land disputes, however, unlikely to happen. Also, if there is a new ISIS insurgency, the camp can be a target for them.

Mitigation Measures

Constant contact with authorities and securities forces in order to prevent and protect people in case of disputes of armed conflict. If the above mentioned cases happen, the services will be temporarily suspended till security can be assured.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Affected people are part of the project through a complaint/suggestion box, also through awarness sessions where they can talk to the staff ministring the session and their concerns/suggestions are taken in consideration and, when possible, always taken into consideration.
