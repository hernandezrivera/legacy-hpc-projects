Project Summary

A crisis will always leave its impact in education in one way or the other. Children in low income countries are most vulnerable to such situations. Schools serve as a unique environment for lifesaving services which include access to health ,safe drinking water ,food, AWD/cholera prevention and other protection mechanisms for reaching the most vulnerable and in this context the children. Safe learning ensures that children displaced or affected by natural disasters and pandemics such as COVID-19 are able to survive and recover from the crisis impacts. After report of first case and containment measures put in place including total closure of schools, this measure left 1.3million children missing out on education across the country from March to August 2020 .Girls are impacted by covid 19 since gender inequality practices and social norms reinforcement directly affects them. In some occasions the girl child has been burdened the duty of taking care of the households. This brings along psychosocial and emotional torture to the girl child and it likely erodes the dreams of later focusing on education hence eroding the efforts made over years in ensuring that equal genders of children have equal access to education without interruption. The project will seek to increase the capacity of target communities to identify , report, and refer children with urgent child protection concerns as well as to provide psychosocial support services in the long term. Activities will focus on the establishing community based child protection mechanisms training and mentoring them and raising awareness. According to SG report on children and armed conflict 2020, whereby children were recruited and others used by non-state armed-groups and girls exposed to sexual violence, CoDHNet will come up with an initiative of mobilizing these children to return back to school.
In line with the education cluster objectives the project will focus on IDP affected population in Gedo region with our target areas being Dolow district, Belet Hawa district and Luuq district. Through this grant CoDHNet will be able to reach out to 5620 school going students from 11 schools in the targeted areas. The project will majorly focus on gender equality giving a major priority to children with disabilities in order to address the challenges in access and learning outcomes between girls and boys in the above mentioned areas due to deeply rooted gender norms. This strategy is aimed at providing safe and equal access to education for both boys and girls through establishment of supplementary school feeding program and safe drinking water as this will help retain those who have reported and attract those who have not yet reported back to school ,rehabilitation of 22 classrooms to improve quality and protective learning environment for 5620 learners , construct 11 latrines soma will have handrails for support attached either to the floor or side wall on slightly raised level for children with disability and 11 handwashing facilities to cater for the 22 constructed classrooms, provision of sanitary kits to 1686 school going adolescent girls to ensure that the girl child is always in school , Provide emergency teaching and learning materials ,We will also initiate community and school outreach program and awareness raising for out of school children and their caregivers on child protection. CoDHNet will carry out identification and intervention process for children with special needs whereby we will identify children with abnormal patterns of development or educational potentials and devise special programs for them. Therefore we target to reach out to 562disabled children(309 boys and 253 girls), Teachers and CECs will be trained on children rights and safe school protocols and we will provide incentives to teachers as a motivation to them.

Mitigation Measures

1) Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation: There is passable integration and connections with WASH services to minimize distance travelled to access complementary humanitarian services. CoDHNet will locate water sources visible, central locations and not more than 500 meters from the classes and install lights near communal WASH facilities which significantly increase the security of female teachers and girls fetching the water. Involve women and girls insisting locations for communal WASH facilities considering their security and safety and ensure the location and access routes to WASH facilities and distribution points are safe. At the same time, latrines will be lockable and the location of the new latrines is discussed with the community to increase security in regards to GBV. Work in coordination with protection actors to put in place measures to prevent and respond to physical/sexual violence arising from the use of or lack of WASH facilities. 2) Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities. Mitigation: Members of marginalized community’s/ minority groups and people with disabilities are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance Likelihood and impacts are both High. CoDHNet will include women, men, boys, and girls, minority groups and people with disabilities in all the decision-making meetings during the project implementation, men and women will participate equally all the meetings. 3) Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms. Mitigation: CoDHNet will establish a feedback and complaint mechanism to record and act on the expression of satisfaction or dissatisfaction about the quality of the project services. The complaint mechanism contributes towards creating a safer environment for beneficiaries as well as increasing to listen to their voice during the implementation of the project. CoDHNet will make set up accessible, confidential and well-understood feedback mechanisms and ensure all beneficiaries are mobilized and the information reaches them regularly 4) Risk of explosive hazard contamination in project target area. Mitigation: Learning will take place during the day and end in the evening so as to give time for learners reach home safely. CoDHNet will carry out security measures training to all target area beneficiaries’ .We will also work closely with community leaders to advise and notify us on areas that might be insecure so as to take precautions

CoDHNet aims to reach out to 562 children with disabilities (309boys and 253girls). Children with disabilities face a challenge of unfriendly environment that is not accessible to them. To solve this, when constructing toilets, CoDHNet will set aside toilets for children with disabilities that will have handrails for support attached either to the floor or side wall on a slightly raised level.Setting up of disability clubs. Disability clubs have proved very successful at strengthening the role of both girls and boys with disabilities and young people in school, to improve their social interaction and facilitate their integration into school. Clubs also carry out disability awareness and prevention activities within the school and community.This is because education is the most important element in evolution of a nation which means with many people getting education in Somalia, over time it will bring along self-reliance and economic growth and this will help them survive in a competitive world independently
