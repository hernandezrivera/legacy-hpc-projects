Project Summary

the project aims to strengthen the education services and increasing drop-out levels in Syria and sustain the educational activities in the targets areas, by the following:
Encouraging the students to back their learning via implementing BTL campaigns, from girls and boys in the age of 7 to the age of 17,
Induce people to send thier children to the kindergarten by provision ECCD sessions; from boys and girls with the age of 3 to 5 years
providing assistance to formal primary school building, ensure the sustainability of the teacher's stipends from women and men who are from the young adults and adults , in addition to provide the school building with the required rehabilitation, providing the school furniture with essential supplies to ensure that the schools itself is suitable and could be comfortable for students attendance further to equip the schools with the PWD equipment's from girls , boys, women and men , in addition, the running cost for the schools is a very important component in the project for the schools and teachers by providing school kits, teachers kits,textbook, stationary and hygiene kits, and the running cost for the formal schools itself by providing drinkable water , electricity, and fuel for heating purposes.
Developing the mentality for students will be strongly considered in the project by provision the life skills sessions and equipping the learning centres by book banks, this activity will be for the youth and adolescents in the addition to the primary school children from boys and girls.
The project also aims to provide assistance for non-formal education for out of school children who are dropped out of school due to many reasons like displacement and security issues especially girls ,by involving them in catch up classes, remedial classes, ALP sessions and SLP sessions for the students from girls and boys from the age of 9 till the age of 15, who are dropped out of schools. there will be specialized services for the out of school children for the PSS sessions by specialized PSS facilitators and SRA sessions for the ALP students from boys and girls. all the running cost for this non-formal program will be covered by the project, including the ALP teachers stipends, PSS facilitators stipends, the required materials for the PSS and textbooks for ALP program, which is approved by UNICEF. furthermore, according to the needs to provide the capacity building for the teachers there will be training for the education personnel from teachers (women and men) in addition to add new component related to the classroom control and develop the education process with the classroom level, this will be by providing training to coaching the classrooms in the formal education program and for the non-formal program, the coaching program will take a very important role in developing the education process with the classrooms levels in the formal education and the non-formal education, this will be supported with teachers learning circles " TLC" and PTA, and all the required and related training will be provided by the project.
The project also intent to measure the developing of the student level by assessing their academic achievement by conducting ASER tool for the targeted student aged from 7-14 years from boys and girls.
The targeted areas of the project are :
Aleppo: AZAZ, Jarabulus, Al-Bab and Afrin.
Idlib city and its countryside where Alameen has very good access and ongoing education projects (Idleb, Ariha and Sarmada.
The camps of IDPs in both Idlib and Aleppo.

Protection Risk Analysis

Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target mentioned areas, affects the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in the project. Child safety incidents in school due to bullying and overburdened teachers, or due to the low knowledge of the teachers with child safe-gardening. the possibility of Attacks on schools in the mentioned areas, Risk of children to drop out of formal education to attend non-formal education, due to many reasons related to the children or family concerns. A limited number of beneficiaries in the program, which might cause inter-community tension and pressure on implementing staff, Increased risk of teachers/education personnel leaving formal schools to work in non-formal education, Some locations not accepting textbooks with the organization logos. interference from the armed groups in the project areas, risk related to safety and security for the humanitarian aid workers, and have the duty of care policy, security focal point and child safeguarding focal point, violence and bullying in school, inter-communal tensions.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Closely monitor the security situation in coordination with partners and relevant stakeholders and conduct a security assessments. They will ensure that staff members and teachers are trained in safety and security best practices. If and when the local security situation deteriorates: a) Immediately there will be informming internal emergency focal point on the situation; b) Coordinate with the other actors in the project areas , cluster and hibernate/suspend the operations if necessary. Training of partners and local teams on Childsafety, development of schools specific child safety policies; establishment of complaint mechanisms in school. Alternative locations (community centers,homes, etc…) and methodologies of education should be considered to avoid attack-related risks. Duty of Care policies are in place, and the staffare informed on the responsibilities as well as limitations of the organization. Security/safety training for the field staff and the education personnel, students and school staff. Using OCHA’s Deconfliction Mechanism as a method to hold attackers accountable for their actions.
