Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to contribute to addressing the winterization needs of the affected communities in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA along the contact line and NGCA).
The action will be implemented through a comprehensive set of winterization support activities, including distribution of coal and winterization kits for 15,750 beneficiaries, distribution of cast-iron stoves and heaters for 2,100 beneficiaries, conduction of winterization rehabilitation works in 40 selected social institutions benefiting 12,000 patients/visitors and distribution of winterization kits for 12,000 individuals that are patients/visitors of additionally selected social institutions.

Needs

Households who lack a centralized heating system are in need to buy coal to warm their homes. The Winterization Recommendations 2018-2019 developed by cluster highlights that the standard amount of the coal for one full winter season is three tons for the private household. Within IOM post-distribution monitoring (PDM) on coal distribution, carried out in April 2018, 68% of the surveyed beneficiaries stated that three tons of coal allowed to keep temperature above 18 degrees Celsius during the harshest time of the winter. Nevertheless, with the average monthly household income (long service pensions, salaries and social assistance) of USD 132, at least USD 41 (approximately RUB 2,700) should be spent to purchase one tone of coal that could be hardly covered by the most vulnerable households;According to the PDM on rehabilitation activities conducted by IOM in April 2018, infrastructure rehabilitation remains one of the top priorities for social institutions and healthcare centers, and represents 55% of the total estimated needs. Winterization-related interventions, including external insulation, heating and roofing, were mentioned as predominantly case by social institutions that have been directly affected by the hostilities and those that no longer receive public support;The majority of the centers supported by IOM with rehabilitation works confirm that during winter the temperature in the entire building or in parts of it is lower than 18 degrees Celsius despite the availability of electric heaters. Such low temperature is caused by cracks in the walls, old or damaged doors and windows, leaking pipes or lack of functioning heaters. The need of instalment of doors and windows was mentioned by 30% of respondents and the roof repair by 11% during the IOM PDM in April 2018;Data presented by REACH in Situation Overview: Winter Assessment of Government-Controlled Areas within 5km of the Line of Contact in February 2018 shows that only 38% of the households have access to central heating. Nevertheless, irregular electricity, gas and heating shortages are relatively common across this area. With harsh winters, such kind of shortages have a significant impact on the health and well-being of the members of the households;Lack of fuel and insufficient indoor warmth have caused 42% households to use at least one coping mechanism such as maintaining to reduce temperatures in the premises or heat only one living room in the building.

Activities & outcomes

Outcome 1. Vulnerable conflict affected persons in GCA and NGCA of Ukraine have increased access to essential commodities. Output 1: Vulnerable households are enabled to meet their basic heating needs for winter - Activities for Output 1:- Conduct verification and selection of beneficiary households in coordination with local counterparts;- Launch Call for Partners and select the coal supplier;- Procure winterization kits, cast-iron stoves and heaters;- Carry out distribution of winterization kits and coal to the targeted households;- Carry out distribution of the cast-iron stoves to the selected households;- Conduct post-distribution monitoring on the cast-iron stoves and heaters, winterization kits and coal distributions. Output 2: Critical infrastructure in key community sites and social institutions is functional and meets the needs of individuals seeking social services - Activities for Output 2:- Assess and select sites for rehabilitation and winterization kits distribution in coordination relevant de facto authorities in Luhansk NGCA, and with the IP’s involvement in Donetsk NGCA. Rehabilitation works and winterization kits distribution will be conducted in different social institutions;- Prepare the Bill of Quantities for rehabilitation works either by directly contracted construction company, or by selected IP, launch the tender and select the construction company;- Carry out the rehabilitation of targeted sites in Luhansk and Donetsk NGCA;- Procure winterization kits to be distributed among the selected social institutions;- Carry out distribution of the procured winterization kits among the key social sites;- Conduct post-distribution monitoring of rehabilitation and winterization kits distribution of the targeted sites in Luhansk and Donetsk NGCA. The beneficiaries of winterization NFIs will be identified based on agreed upon vulnerability criteria such as: single headed households, presence of elderly or persons with disabilities within the household, multi-child households. Public structures eligible for proposed winterization rehabilitations and repair works will be selected by prioritizing areas close to the contact line, those damaged in the course of hostilities, and those of the greatest importance for the local communities, accommodating the most vulnerable groups of population.
