Project Summary

The project aims to respond to 75750 individuals in both Aleppo and Idlib governorates, including the IDPs and vulnerable residents. The intervention aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support through the following emergency NFI activites:
Provision of core NFIs to cover 10000 individuals
Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs to cover 30000 individuals
And through the following emergency shelter activities:
Installation of new/ replacement of existing tents to cover 1000 individuals
Installation and/ or construction of temporary transitional shelters to cover 2000 individuals
Implementation of HH-level improvements to cover 10000 individuals and covering 5000 individuals with site level improvements/ infrastructure
Moreover, the project aims to cover 1250 IDPs with the service of Rehabilitation of collective centers
in special cases, and almost integrated with dignified shelter activities when needed, the project will cover 1000 individuals with cash-for-rent support
Furthermore, the project aims to reinforce an environment that enables protection and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure by covering 5000 individuals with house rehabilitation, 2500 for minor damaged houses, and 2500 for moderately damaged houses. Moreover, 3500 individuals will benefit from the rehabilitation of common areas; in addition, 10000 individuals will be targeted with the Supply and installation of HH-level solar energy systems. The project will conduct Adaptations for persons with specific needs to cover 200 cases.
During the implementation of activities, Shafak will maintain high coordination with the SNFI cluster through monthly submission of 4ws and contributions to winter planning and flood response actions.
Need assessment was already conducted by Shafak MEAL team to identify the project locations.
The project will consider the protection of beneficiaries as the distribution centers will be provided with firefighting equipment and brochures, and safety and security will be distributed to beneficiaries.
Shafak will pay close attention to people with disabilities, elderlies, and people with special needs, and for those, assistance will be delivered to home.
Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) rights of shelter sites, shelter beneficiaries, and house owners will be considered throughout all stages of shelter repair interventions, and an agreement will be signed to guarantee the rights of the beneficiaries.
