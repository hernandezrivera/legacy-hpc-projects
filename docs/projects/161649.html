1 - Needs

The effects of gender inequality is felt mostly by the rural women and girls who are held down by traditions and customs and are completely ignorant of what they are worth as women. Therefore the efforts towards achieving gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls should be highly encouraged at the rural communities. The prevalence of violence against women and girls has increased by 7.7% since the conflict began. Furthermore, GBV has increased in severity but also new forms of GBV appear due to this conflict. The conflict and its consequences is maintaining a vicious cycle of GBV that humanitarian community should take adequate measure to break through improving gender equality for women and girls while anticipating and mitigating unintended consequences. REHBI conducted a rapid assessment to further validate some of the secondary information that it had gathered from engagement with other actors and this further point to the overwhelming needs. The rapid assessment sort among other things to also try to improve gender equality for women and girls. Key findings from the assessment further validated the fact that there was huge gab in treating women equally in social, economic and all other aspect of society.

2 - Activities

This project is aimed at improving gender equality for conflict affected women and girls through;1. Sensitizing women and girls on their right to promote gender equality.2. Conducting capacity building training for women and girls support groups as an ambassadors to enable them promote right and address their needs.3. Promoting gender equality through radio programme4. Sthrengthening the existing women and youth groups within the communities to promote gender equality.
