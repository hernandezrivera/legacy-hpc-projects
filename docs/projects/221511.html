Project Summary

The socio-political crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions has led to a massive population displacement. According to the Multisectoral Need Assessment (MSNA) February 2023, round 8 the conflict has forced approximatively 1 million persons to be displaced in the Northwest, southwest, Littoral and west regions. The NWSW also count 569,311 IDPs 442,580 returnees and Cameroonians refugee returnees. HNO reports for May 2023 some 607,000 people in need of protection in the NWSW. Civilian population continues to face severe human rights violations, unlawful arrests and detention, destruction and extortion of property, threats to life, physical assaults, sexual assaults, rape and child abuse. The North-West and South-West regions continued to be marked by high levels of insecurity in 2023 with armed conflict, criminal offences against civilian populations, beside the lockdowns imposed by NSAG, and frequent roadblocks
The project aim at improving the protection and respect of fundamental rights for persons affected by crises in the NW /SW regions of Cameroon. The project will also make provision for psychosocial support and cash/livelihood assistance to vulnerable IDPs and affected population. The activities include: Conducting protection monitoring in crisis-affected areas; strengthening the civil documentation to conflict-affected people; providing targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (including livelihood); Sensitization on peaceful coexistence between host and IDPs communities and non-discrimination of IDPs; Build capacity of monitors, social and psychosocial workers to monitor human rights violation in standardized, rigorous way for credible, compatible documentation and supports advocacy and direct action effectively; Identify protection issues, monitor events, trend and changes; Collect, gather and analyze information related to physical, material and legal safety, vulnerabilities encountered by displaced and other affected population; Inform humanitarian programing through identification of needs, gaps and trends; Generate a responds from relevant stakeholders through referral and/ or advocacy actions; Identify individual protection cases for referral to specialized agencies (internal or external referrals based on services provided in the different locations); Disseminate information on available services to the displaced and host communities; The project location is in the Northwest region (Bui, Ngo ketunjia, Donga mantung, Momo, and Boyo divisions), and South west region (Ndian and manyu Division) of Cameroon. The project targets 28000 affected people from targeted communities

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Protection monitoring responses will involve protection services for IDPs including activities that prevent, respond to, and mitigate protection risks, including protection against GBV, child protection, family separation, case management and referrals for persons with specific needs or victims of protection incidents, mental health and psychosocial support. These monitoring mechanisms and systems will be regularly used for interventions, programming, resource mobilization and communications. 42 community monitors will be trained to conduct protection monitoring in crisis-affected areas and 21,000 persons of concern will be covered by protection monitoring, 700 referrals will be done to specialized services, 350 protection incidents reported through protection monitoring. The project also include Strengthening the process of issuance of civil documentation to conflict-affected people; 700 conflict-affected persons having benefited from civil or identity documentation support, 7 awareness-raising sessions on civil documentation. The project also includes providing targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (men, women, boys and girls, PWD); 700 conflict-affected persons having benefited from targeted protection assistance; Providing legal assistance to crisis-affected people on the protection of their rights; 1400 people reached with sensitization sessions on rights and obligations. Providing targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (persons living with disabilities to be included in social and economic support to livelihood); specialized services provided to 350 Persons living With Disabilities Sensitization on peace building, peaceful coexistence between IDPs, returnees and host communities; 1400 people actively engaged in peace building and social cohesion activities. Establishing and supporting the community based complains and feedbacks mechanisms; 70 community based mechanisms will be established with 70 focal points appointed. SUDAHSER will ensure the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), and accountability to affected populations through the implementation of zero tolerance policy. Our staff and protection monitors will be capacitated to conduct sensitization sessions in the targeted communities on their rights and reporting mechanisms
