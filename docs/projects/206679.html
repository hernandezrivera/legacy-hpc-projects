Project Summary

COHESODEC’s Nutrition response in 2024 will be realized in the following targeted communities of the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon: Kumbo West, Kumbo East, Benakuma, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Eyumojock, Mamfe, Kumba North & Kumba South.
This response will ensure targeted implementation of activities to promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for exclusive breastfeeding support involving 3,000 pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in the baby corners/baby tents and prepare a follow-up report on the implementation of the code on the marketing of breast-milk substitutes available.
The response will also be targeting the implementation of activities to promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for Complementary Feeding (including breastfeeding) involving 10,000 people who shall receive IYCF counseling / awareness as well as ensuring gender sensitive sensitization materials reach out to at least 1,000 targeted beneficiaries.
Moreover, COHESODEC will also be supporting community engagement for behaviour and social change involving 3,000 mothers and other caretakers trained on family MUAC activity while also ensuring that 500 people (M/F/Age) attend participatory sessions on program design/evaluation.
Furthermore, the project shall also ensure that Integrated management of severe and acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S) involving at least 75% of children aged 6-59 months are admitted for SAM management and discharge as cured with a positive recovery rate meanwhile 2 mobile clinics will be deployed for Child comprehensive response in these target vulnerable communities.
It should be noted that technical expertise of this survey will be mobilized through the following approach; COHESODEC shall sign a memorandum of understanding and work in collaboration with the National Institute of Statistics-(NIS), Recruit, orientate and work with trained Enumerators having background knowledge in statistics and surveys as well as involve specialized program staff.
Finally, the project will equally strengthen nutritional surveillance mechanisms with 1 SMART survey carried out during the lean season as well as 3 rapid nutritional assessments conducted within the following scope(Health Districts): Kumbo, Benakuma, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Eyumojock, Mamfe, Kumba North and Kumba South health districts.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will require funds for activities to promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for exclusive breastfeeding support through the mobilization, support and effectively counseling of pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in baby corners/baby tents as well as set up dedicated counseling units in selected priority health districts in a bid to increase access, coverage and use of lifesaving nutrition services and enhance protection of the nutrition status. COHESODEC team will also be identifying, registering, counseling and disseminating educative handouts and posters for wider community sensitization and ensure targeted implementation of activities which promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for complementary feeding including breastfeeding. Moreover, COHESODEC will identify, register and counsel mothers with infant and young children (M / F) and ensure that they receive IYCF counseling / awareness. The project will as well support community engagement for behavior and social change by identifying, registering and training mothers and caretakers on family MUAC activity. Further, this project will integrate the management of severe and acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S) involving children 6-59 months and ensure they are admitted for SAM management, properly treated and discharged with a positive recovery rate of at least 75% while equally ensuring that a mobile unit is established involving specialist for child comprehensive response in these target communities! Finally, the project will be ensuring nutritional surveillance mechanisms strengthening involving the following Health Districts in the North-West and South-West Regions: Kumbo, Benakuma, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Eyumojock, Mamfe, Kumba North and Kumba South health districts. Please, note that technical expertise will be mobilized through the following approach; Sign a memorandum of understanding and work in collaboration with the National Institute of Statistics-(NIS), Recruit, orientate and work with trained Enumerators having background knowledge in statistics and surveys as well as involve specialized program staff.
