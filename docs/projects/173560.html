Project Summary

The proposed action would enhance the self-reliance and resilience of IDP, Refugees, and vulnerable host communities in ND, ED, and BN through the restoration of traditional agro-pastoral livelihoods through the provision of seeds and agricultural tools, and goat restocking, and production-related capacity building. The proposed action would also provide kitchen garden training with a starter kit to those women with a SAM child and without access to land and other fundamental pre-requisite assets for an agro-pastoral means of livelihood to increase consumption of nutrition-sensitive foods. Home gardens been identified as a major potential change methodology to enhance household food security and enable women to directly support their children’s nutritional wellbeing and prevent relapses.

1 - Needs

The Food insecurity and humanitarian situation in Sudan have deteriorated rapidly in the first half of the year 2020. The devastating combination of recurrent climate shocks, conflict, economic downturn, disease outbreaks, exacerbated by the consequences of the COVID-19 pandemic and floods, has driven rising food insecurity levels across the country. Besides, the lockdown measures to prevent the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic significantly decreased commodity movement, market function, and cross-border trade, and compromised livelihoods, daily labor opportunities, reducing household purchasing power and food access of the vulnerable population. Hunger has increased across Sudan and over 7.1 million people are severely food insecure at the peak of the lean season (October to December). The figure, the highest ever recorded in the history of the IPC analysis in the country, represents an increase of 22 percent if compared with the same period last year. At least 1.3 million people are facing emergency levels of acute food insecurity (IPC 4), while around a 5.8million people are facing crisis levels of acute food insecurity (IPC 3). Another 17.4million people are estimated to be under stress (IPC 2) and any additional shock could push them to severe levels of hunger. As of March 2019 report, Darfur accounting for 45% of this population.COVID19-related restrictions starting in March and April 2020 harming food security and livelihoods of the most vulnerable households. Many households in the pastoral areas are likely to continue facing difficulty meeting their minimum food needs as a result of very high staple food prices, reduced access to labor, lower than normal terms of trade, and seasonally low access to animal products during the lean season.Based on various food security assessments, the most vulnerable groups are IDPs, returnees, refugees, and host communities. The highest severity of acute food insecurity is recorded in the different localities classified as IPC phase 3 among which North Darfur, East, and the Blue Nile States are included. Food security needs will remain at similar levels in 2021 as in 2020, with a likelihood of worsening of the situation as the underlying causes need to address.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

RI will ensure integrating protection at each level and in each process of the project implementation. This includes for instance, in the beneficiary selection process, the selection criteria are developed in a manner to be inclusive of those most vulnerable and needy households.As part of the ‘Do No Harm Approach’, prior to restocking activities, RI will assess risks associated with providing livestock to vulnerable households (e.g. safety and protection of recipient households, migration and grazing patterns, land tenure, vaccine and/or feed supply, and environmental impact), and ensure a participatory assessment is undertaken to mitigate risks. Women headed households will be one of the targeted groups, but given the common practice of economic out-migration, RI will consider factors such as receipt of remittances, the presence of another male relative designated to undertake HH decision-making, or whether the husband is returning HH decision-making remotely prior to engaging with goat restocking. During the distribution of assets, RI will also ensure that the accountability system is properly set for the community to reach out for feedback and complaint on services.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Relief International has a standard operating procedure (SOP) that guides the proper implementation and management of the accountability to community principles including safeguarding issues such as PSEA and child abuse. RI is working to strengthen the accountability system to be able to capture the needs and voices of the program recipients at each stage of the project cycle management: need assessment, designing, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation. RI has also rolled out its own Child Safeguarding Policy across the operation areas in order to reinforce existing policies such as Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) within RI and therefore increase and/or maintain a protective environment for beneficiaries, including children attending all RI-supported services. All staff receives mandatory training on this policy.Adding to existing feedback and complaint mechanisms, RI also aims to strengthen its accountability system putting a mechanism which enables coordination with the inter-agency to facilitate learning on best practices, complementarity in complaint responses such as referrals of GBV cases; and strengthening capacity of staff on management of complaints and feedbacks to inform project designing, improve service delivery, and, enhance program recipients participation in each state of the project management cycle, etc. As part of the existing accountability system, RI also documents community satisfaction on services which enable informed decisions on program designing and adjustment, budget allocations, and future programming.
