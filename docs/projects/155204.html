Project Summary

The project aims at providing safe and dignified living conditions for 43.000 Rohingya through the establishment, improvement and maintenance of shelter. Caritas Bangladesh will focus its intervention on durability, dignity & safety, transitioning away from the repeated cycle of emergency shelter interventions. The main components are: 1. Transitional Shelter Assistance, a community-led program that aims at empowering refugees, increasing capacity, skills and freedom of choice, and foster social cohesion. It will provide households with in-kind core items, a voucher/cash for optional items, training and technical assistance; 2. Midterm shelter which encompass a broad range of DRR features and provide a more durable solution that is applicable in newly developed camps and less congested existing camps; 3. Increasing durability of material by sourcing mature bamboo and piloting small-scale bamboo treatment facility. Throughout the project Caritas will generate livelihood opportunities for both men and women through cash for work and actively engage and support women and vulnerable households.

Needs

"Refugees are residing in makeshift shelters that are severely below basic humanitarian standards, overcrowded, and will not withstand seasonal climatic conditions. The low quality of the shelters and poor terrain, as well as the lack of privacy within these shelters has a serious impact on the physical and psychological well-being of the refugees, and especially for women, children and older refugees and refugees with disabilities. Women and girls consist of 52% of the Rohingya population in Bangladesh, and 55% are under 18. Women, girls, and boys are disproportionately affected by risks within the camps including safe access to services. As highlighted by the Shelter/NFI sector assessment carried out in August 2018, in addition to structural and material concerns, households raised lack of privacy (including for cooking and bathing spaces) and lack of ventilation as major concerns for their shelters – issues that disproportionately affected women, adolescent girls and other and vulnerable groups. Women and those with restricted mobility are more exposed to smoke and high temperatures given that cooking takes places within or next to shelters. Ventilation is limited to few windows that are rarely opened because of privacy concerns within the highly congested areas.Currently, the need for safe shelter and secure settlements are some of the paramount concerns for refugees. The monsoon has already severely damaged many facilities, shelter and community structures, that were built only with bamboo and tarpaulin. Moreover, bamboo has also deteriorated due to beetle infestation. Life-saving emergency response remains the priority with sectors focused on achieving coverage and improving quality. Access to basic services is still insufficient, with many installations of extremely poor quality that now require a second investment to upgrade. "

Activities or outputs

"Caritas Bangladesh shelter strategy for 2019 will center on durability, dignity & safety, transitioning away from the repeated cycle of emergency shelter interventions. Three main activities are thus proposed: 1) Transitional Shelter Assistance: Several families will require support again as their shelters were damaged during monsoon, rendering the structures unsafe. The untreated bamboo used for erecting the shelters only withstands the local weather conditions for about 3/12 months and needs to be replaced constantly. The transitional shelter assistance will be provided to households who previously received upgrade shelter kits (USK). This will include in-kind core items (treated bamboo when available, footings and rope) , a voucher/cash for optional items, training and technical assistance to provide resilience against adverse weather shocks. Transitional Shelter Assistance is a community-led program that aims at empowering refugees, increasing capacity, skills and freedom of choice, and foster social cohesion. 2) Mid-Term Shelters: The mid-term shelter is a more durable solution that is applicable in newly developed camps and less congested existing camps where the covered living space standard can be met. The design encompass a broad range of DRR features and consists of concrete columns with bamboo structure which will provide increased resistance against strong winds and increased durability.3) Bamboo: conduct market and supply chain assessment for sourcing mature bamboo, and piloting small-scale bamboo treatment facility.Caritas will proactively engage women to enhance their role and participation, particularly through full sex and age disaggregated data, verification of vulnerability criteria through segregated FGDs, sensitization messaging with men and women on equitable participation, segregated sessions for women for the HH level training, and freedom to choose materials to address main concern emerged in the shelter assessment. For EVIs, additional labour support will be provided to undertake the construction and maintenance of their shelters. Livelihood opportunities for both men and women will be generated through cash for work. "
