Project Summary

In Target areas GAM and SAM rates are critical.Its important to note that the nutrition situation has sustained above emergency threshold of 15 since 2013.(FSNAU,2018). Morbidity rates in the area is high,access to health services is low, including delivery by skilled attendant. Delivery of public health services is entirely delivered by external actors with limited government oversight. Coverage for immunization is below 80% recommended by Sphere. Integrated food security analysis shows that the food security situation in Handar is stressed. This remains fragile state as it depends on availability of pasture for animals.
CAFDARO therefore intends to intervene with a project that will help improve the nutritional status of women and children (Boys and girls) in the area, with a multi sectarian approach and addressing most aspects of both immediate and underlying causes of malnutrition. Interventions will focus identification and treatment of malnutrition and resilience activities like Promotion on optimal IYCF practices, micro nutrient supplementation and health and hygiene education. Active case finding, through CHWs, regular screening and mass screening, will be employed to improve identification of cases. The organization intends to greatly improve community participation through recruitment and involvement of the community in implementation activities. The organization will also form and work closely with community health boards, in decision making. This should improve accountability to the affected population and make projects more sustainable. The organization will also work closely with WASH, Health and food security cluster, and also integrated its nutrition, food security and livelihood projects, to ensure underlying causes of malnutrition are addressed. All nutrition services, treatment of MAM and SAM, will be integrated to maximize impact. The project will ensure that the environment is not degraded. Education of proper disposal of used packages of therapeutic food will be enhanced. This education is also include education on waster purification through use of solar/aqua tab rather that boiling, which destroys trees and other plantain used as fuel, of will be an emphasis. Involvement in cluster coordination and related activities will be high on our priority. The project will employ staff of all gender through a transparent process. More female compared to men will however be employed as IYCF and related staff, as most women prefer fellow women to offer IYCF support.

Needs

Hander are under 18 villages has an approximate population of 12845 and is also classified as a rural region with pastoralism as their main livelihood. GAM and SAM rates are critical.Its important to note that the nutrition situation has sustained above emergency threshold of 15 since 2013.(FSNAU,2018). Morbidity rates in the area is high,access to health services is low, including delivery by skilled attendant. Delivery of public health services is entirely delivered by external actors with limited government oversight. There is only one MCH in the area, near Beeled Xawa town. This population, who find the Dollow health center too far, prefers this MCH. Coverage for immunization is below 80% recommended by Sphere. Integrated food security analysis shows that the food security situation in Handar is stressed. This remains fragile state as it depends on availability of pasture for animals. Any major shock that could push it back to Crisis or Emergency (FSNAU, 2015). The major aggravating factors for acute malnutrition were high morbidity, reduced milk access and family splitting as men search for pasture, low immunization coverage, limited access to emergency health services and , safe water and sanitation facilities and gender based violations. Other Factors include poverty and illiteracy among women, the low value placed on women’s health, lack of female decision making power over their own health, and social and cultural norms associated with reproduction. These greatly affect health seeking behavior and consequently, positive outcomes for mothers and children (boys and girls) (UNICEF, 2017). There is widespread security tension in the area. Though intensity of this is not much; the magnitude is widespread.UNOCHA predicts that the nutrition situation is unlikely to change, at least for a few months, if all factors remain constant. Limited nutrition humanitarian activities in the area, are also likely to contribute to a sustained situation, if not deterioration

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Many living in the area will access services once they are availed. The only exception would be pastrolists and boys who sometimes have to move frommplace to place in search for pasture. These will be tracked using our mobile team and ronbust contactdatabase that will keep in touch since telephone coverage in the area is good.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Young boys are likely to be excluded from the program as they move along with their fathers in search of pasture. Men also who are significant others to their wives and relatives might be left out of programs that target improvement in maternal and child care outcomes hindering behaviour change. This will however be addressed by provision of mobile services with support group and health education for pastrolists. Elderly women who have a big say in infant and young child issues will also be included as they are often excluded.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The target population sometimes facesconflict as government solders engage non-state actir in neighbouring villages. This will be handled by close collaboration with local and state level administration. Staff , who are from the local community , will also be used to provide information on security situation along side government security briefs. Fewer contact will be made with beneficiaries so reduce security risk. The program will also use CHW after thoroughly equipping then as they are constantly on ground.
