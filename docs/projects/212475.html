Project Summary

The economic situation compounds the protracted crisis and recent natural disasters means that finances for and access to clean, safe water is a daily struggle for 13.6M Syrians in need of WASH assistance and waterborne diseases remain a concern across Syria. WASH infrastructure in Syria requires substantial repair and operational support, including O&M training for rehabilitated sites. The proposed project will provide life-saving WASH interventions at both the community and HH levels to increase water supply. Fuel/electrical supplies for infrastructure have become untenable. Medair proposes specific activities/per need in each operating location, such as rehabilitation and/or solarization of Water Pump Stations, boreholes, and elevated water tanks; fixing damaged sections of water distribution networks; Water trucking; and rehabilitating WASH facilities in clinics where Medair also conducts health activities. Complementing Medair’s health messaging via community health workers, hygiene awareness training is included. The health and hygiene messages will be delivered through house-to-house visits to ensure widespread coverage. Capacity building of Water Establishment staff in O&M will occur wherever water supply work is completed.
Protection Risk Analysis: RISK: Increased water supply is inequitably distributed in the network. MITIGATION: Community consultation and extensive, systematic mapping with various populations to ensure that no households are missed; consultation should include vulnerable populations and be done by appropriate facilitators (gender, age), MoWR staff O&M training. Continual M&E and timely response to feedback RISK: Safety hazards cause accident during construction phase MITIGATION: Explanation in advance to beneficiary communities; Contractual requirements for tidy, safe work sites with sufficient hazard warning signs and safety cordons. Pictorial signage (for children) to indicate hazards that cannot be cordoned off from community. Kept incident log, shows incidents or results of incident investigations RISK: Resulting system creates hazards for community, including vulnerable persons MITIGATION: Build to accessibility standards within Sphere/local sector standards. Ensure trained technicians and engineers are the implementers, Consult community, inclusive of vulnerable populations about perceived risks, existing mitigations. Quality assurance validation according to standards. Post-project monitoring for safety incidents
PSEA strategy: RISK: Risk of exploitation to and by Medair employees, prospective employees, contracted workers MITIGATION: Organization-wide PSEA awareness regularly trained and discussed; relevant PSEA mitigations in HR, accountability for prevention to managers, established staff feedback/reporting mechanisms. Multi-media communication with communities re: codes of conduct and reporting mechanisms, contractor sensitization to expected behaviours including PSEA. Training records, performance review indicators, feedback log.
Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures: RISK: Delays in planned procurement and/or importation MITIGATION: A procurement plan will be completed at start of project cycle to ensure sufficient lead-time for normal operations. Procurement is planned to maximise use of reliable in-country suppliers, transfer of responsibility for import issues to suppliers. RISK: Fluctuations in exchange rates, differences between black market and official rates and inflation impact suppliers, in turn affecting procurement and budget management. Also impacting amount of money held in the bank account, causing Medair to lose cash value MITIGATION: Monitor exchange rates and inflation, monitor % of funds that need to be in SYP, use rapid procurement processes. Monthly budget and inflation monitoring as well as use of forecasting spending. Appropriate clauses in supplier contracts, for mutual balance, but with clarity that Medair follows official exchange rates only.
