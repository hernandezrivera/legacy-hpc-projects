Project Summary

Burundi has just gone through more than a decade of socio-political crisis which has been characterized by the destruction of productive capital, massive population displacements, the decline in public aid, and the decline of the economy. At present, the impact of the cost of fuel has impacted the price of food and has created a controversy on the household budget in Burundi.
This project aims to:
(i) Increase productivity and agricultural production and development of sustainable production systems so as to restore, in the short and medium terms, the food self-sufficiency of before 1993 and in the long term to obtain a higher annual growth in food supply that of the population (3%);
(ii) Promote sub-sectors and agribusiness with a view to developing and diversifying sources of growth, including export and import-substitute products, in order to help increase household purchasing power and improve state financial resources. The sector's foreign currency resources will increase from 120 to 200 million US dollars while the rate of the population living below the poverty line will be halved (from 70% to 35%) by 2023;
(iii) Professionalize producers and develop private initiatives to help them defend their interests and be masters of their own development while supporting private individuals to get involved in agricultural supervision, development and diversification of profitable sectors;
(iv) Strengthen the management and sustainable development capacities of the agricultural sector in order to succeed in transforming our subsistence agriculture into profitable market agriculture managed by professionals;
