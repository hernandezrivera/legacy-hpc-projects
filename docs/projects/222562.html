Project Summary

This project aims to ensure that women lead and benefit from humanitarian relief and recovery efforts in Northern Mozambique, particularly in the districts of Palma, Mocimboa da Praia, Nangade, Muidumbe, Macomia, and Quissanga in Cabo Delgado Province. In 2025, the project will target 32,000 women and girls directly affected by conflict and facing multiple vulnerabilities, including internally displaced and residents in host communities, in return areas in Cabo Delgado (including women heads of households, women with disabilities, and young women/girls).

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will aim at:• Empowering displaced women and girls to be active players in planning, designing, building, and maintaining adequate, accessible, safe and resilient resettlement housing in fragile and conflict-affected areas;• Facilitating women’s and young women's access to sustainable livelihoods and socioeconomic opportunities; and• Increasing the access of conflict-affected women and girls to effective services and protection mechanisms. The project will target 93,400 women and girls directly affected by conflict and facing multiple vulnerabilities, including internally displaced and residents in host communities, in return areas in Cabo Delgado (at least 20,000 women heads of households, 500 women with disabilities, and 40,000 young women/girls).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In Palma and Mocimboa da Praia districts:- Elaboration and approval of adequate, accessible, safe and resilient Housing Models/typologies for women and girls affected by conflict in return areas; - Conduct a survey and analysis of appropriate housing models/typologies based on specific geographical and socio-cultural needs and context of conflict-affected women and girls in return areas - Train 3,000 returnees, prioritizing participation of women and young women, on the construction of adequate, accessible, safe and resilient housing (On-the-job training on construction);- Allocate 400 housing kits vouchers to women for the acquisition of conventional, mixed and local construction materials to construct adequate, accessible, safe and resilient housing in the return areas In Palma, Mocimboa da Praia, Nangade, Muidumbe, Macomia and Quissanga districts:- Design and implement a vocational training programme for 5,000 women and young women affected by conflict;- Undertake key employability skills development sessions for 20,000 women and young women and disseminate guidelines for employment seekers in line with the National Employment Policy; - Conduct business mentorship and financing initiative for 20,000 women and young women affected by conflict;- Facilitate business licensing for 10,000 women-owned micro and small businesses.In returning areas of Palma, Mocimboa da Praia, Nangade, Muidumbe, Macomia and Quissanga districts in Cabo Delgado Province:- Provide technical support to the integrated multisectoral mechanism to respond to sexual and gender-based violence cases to 30,000 women and girls at risk of and survivors of violence in return areas - Support the creation and/or operationalization of 8 safe spaces and referral services for women and girls at risk or survivors of violence in return areas.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project included a needs assessment at the design phase to identify specific needs of women and girls affected by the conflict in the six targeted districts. The assessment also helped to identify potential protection risks and mitigation measures.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project will target women and girls affected by the conflict in Cabo Delgado. Participation and empowerment of women and girls will be promoted throughout the project.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will target 250 women with disabilities to receive socio-economic support and GBV assistance in the targeted districts.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project’s plan to construct 200 housing units for internally displaced women and girls, as well as returnees, emphasizes the use of climate-resilient and environmentally friendly housing models. This approach ensures that the homes are not only durable in the face of climate-related challenges but also contribute to sustainability by incorporating green building materials, energy efficiency, and designs that minimize environmental impact.
