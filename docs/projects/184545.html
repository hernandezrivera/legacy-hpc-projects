Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing comprehensive protection services to the most vulnerable IDPs and marginalized non-IDPs communities women, men, boys and girls including those living with disabilities and the elderly that are high risk of violation of their rights as well as enhancing protection services for the targeted boys and girls in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Banadir Regions, Somalia. The need for protection services is occasioned by an unprecedented increase in the number of IDPs owing to conflict, insecurity and political tensions as well as recurrent climatic and shocks compounded by current severe drought conditions that are projected to worsen further till mid 2022 that have seriously impacted in to the lives of IDPs that live in informal and unplanned settlements under very poor living conditions while their plight is exacerbated further by increased exposure to violence, exploitation and other forms of abuse coupled by serious risks and threats to their safety and security as well as negative coping strategies that further expose them to the rights violation. Children endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights including family separation, sexual and physical violence, killing and maiming, psychosocial distress, child labor, widespread recruitment and use of children into armed groups, forced and protracted displacements, limited access to humanitarian assistance due to conflict, insecurity and/or discriminatory practices and exploitation. IDPs and marginalized host communities as well as those living with disabilities are further affected by impacts of Covid -19 that subject them to increased forms of discrimination to humanitarian assistance.
The project further seeks to increase protection of persons with disabilities and the elderly that face increased risks of exploitation, abuse and discrimination, isolation and denial of assistance meant for them hence need for their increased protection. The project will be implemented through the following activities: Provision of specialized MHPSS and victim assistance services to individuals, including persons with disabilities and older persons, provision of protection services during the eviction, conducting eviction monitoring services, provision of counseling as well as medical support for children and GBV survivors, building the capacity of caseworkers, provision of material support and access to essential services for other protection victims and child survivors, Prevention and Response services for unaccompanied and Separated Children. The project targets 14960 persons (4900 women, 5030 boys, and 5030 girls) including people living with disabilities at the targeted project locations in Hiran, Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Banadir.
Inclusion of minority and marginalized groups such as minority clans, women, IDPs, the elderly and Persons with disabilities in to the project will be ensured by involving them in consultations on the project right from needs assessment and prioritization of need at the time of project formulation as well as involving them in all stages of the project implementation including regular monitoring process where they will be part of key decisions during recommendations and suggestions for way forward whenever there are constraints to ensure that their needs and concerns and fully covered by the project.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Ensuring inclusion of minority and marginalized groups such as minority clans, women, IDPs, the elderly and Persons with disabilities in the consultations on the project right from the commencement and ensuring they are part of key decisions made regarding the project to ensure that their needs and concerns and fully covered by the project.2. Protection of women and girls from sexual violence by ensuring the distribution of humanitarian services are located at central locations that are accessible to women and girl without subjecting them to long distance beyond safety of the camps and homes. Zamzam Foundation will work closely with the targeted IDP committee members and other protection partners in the project targeted locations to ensure timely reporting of any land dispute related action for appropriate response by the authority/Federal government's line ministry. The project will in addition ensure strict adherence to PSEA policy and ensuring women and girls and boys and those with disabilities are fully aware of the project and clear expectation from the project to mitigate any form of sexual exploitation and abuse by project staff and local administration/government officials during the project. Furthermore, Zamzam Foundation will enusre that complaints and feedback platforms are in place and are accessible by the targeted beneficiaries/ women, girls and boys and other community members
