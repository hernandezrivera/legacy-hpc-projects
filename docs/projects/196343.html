Project Summary

Prantic Unnayan Society (PRANTIC), Bangladesh is working for the empowerment of adolescent girls, female youths and women living in Rohingya & Host Community, Ukhiya, Cox's Bazar. The proposed plan for the JRP 2023 targets the adolescent girls, female youths and women to engage in various self-reliant activity like-handicrafts, poultry farming, homestead gardening, duck rearing, pigeon rearing etc. in camp-4 and Raja Palong Union of Ukhiya, Cox’s Bazar. The targeted population in camp-4 shall be 25% of the PIN which is 8,000. The same criteria will also be followed in the host community and so the target population becomes 12,000. The variation in the target population in the Rohingya and host community is due to the dissimilarity in needs and feasibility of food security activity in the communities. It is due to the fact that, the percentage of food security in the Rohingya camp is more than the host community as per FSS-PIN. The activities of this project are planned to be implemented by the joint association formed by the men and women within the targeted area who will work together in this project. Considering the social practice within the Rohingya camp, the male members will include both the targeted men along with the male family members of the women involved in the association. The project also target gender based activity for men and women along with gender neutral activities. Overall focus of the project shall be to enhance self-reliance, and reduce mental dependency on relief, while specific focus of the project will include a) design gender transformative activity and address barriers to social cohesion b) encourage leadership and entrepreneurship within the community. C) Create sense of self-identity and self-esteem within the community d) engage the community members in self-reliant activity through providing training, and technical support e) create social awareness within the community against the social stereotypes and gender relations by conducting awareness sessions inclusive of adolescent boys and girls, adult men and women of the host and Rohingya community f) encourage climate friendly activities by inspiring and providing support to homestead gardening and also tree plantation. It is also to be noted that, PRANTIC is working in livelihood activities in Bhasan Char where both men and female are working together in Income Generating Activities like – gardening, fish cultivation, poultry farming, pigeon rearing, duck rearing.

3.2 - Gender

This project was created with a knowledge of how tragedy or conflict affects men and women, girls and boys of all ages, and how their needs, vulnerabilities, interests, capacities, and coping mechanisms vary. This research uses gender analysis to comprehend these discrepancies as well as disparities in responsibilities and workloads for men and women, access to and control over resources, decision-making authority, and possibilities for skill development. Because Prantic Unnayan Society strives to maintain impartiality and proportionality, care must be taken to ensure fairness between men and women and that all outcomes are equal. The employees and staff of PRANTIC Livelihood Team are committed to identifying the needs of all age groups in times of crisis and support timely.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

To assure that all children are safe from harm and have equitable access to fundamental services, this project has taken special precautions. This project will make sure that children's opinions and experiences are not only engendered during emergency assessments and planning but also have an impact on the delivery of humanitarian services as well as their monitoring and evaluation as children frequently are the majority of an affected population.

3.4 - Diversity

This initiative acknowledges the need for diverse populations, including women, girls, men, and boys, as well as the elderly, people with disabilities and other vulnerable groups, with appropriate confidentiality. However, the project is tailored to support the vulnerable females in the Rohingya Camp and host communities and their families. PRANTIC aims to help all gender, age, and diversity groups through the empowerment of women in underprivileged society by developing their skills and leadership qualities.
