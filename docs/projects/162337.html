Project Summary

The Project will be implemented over a period of four months and aims to improve food security of vulnerable displaced households and host communities in the Bandiagara cercle of the Mopti region of Mali. Given the shift in conflict locations to Mopti and Segou and the resulting humanitarian needs of the populations in these zones require urgent intervention in addition to the assistance provided by CRS through its Kisili Rapid Response Mechanism which provides immediate lifesaving support for the first month after displacement. This support isn’t sufficient for certain displaced populations who require longer term humanitarian assistance to set them on a path to recovery such as populations who remain displaced, host communities, and those who haven’t been displaced but have still suffered the consequences of natural and man-made disasters. Vulnerable households in the cercle of Bandiagara suffering from heightened food insecurity during the 2019 lean season will be able to meet their basic food needs as well as agriculture input needs in time to sow for the off season beginning in September/October.
Two communes, Doucoumbo and Sangha, will be targeted for this support and will include displaced households who have already received one month of food rations, WaSH, NFI, shelter through CRS’ KISILI project as well as communities hosting them. The most vulnerable displaced households will be selected to receive these conditional cash transfers and vulnerability criteria will include but not be limited to:
• Women heads of households
• Households led by an elderly person
• Pregnant woman in the household;
• At least two children under five;
• Households possessing less than one (01) hectare of land and engaged in off season activities
• Households having lost their livelihood in the last 6 months;
• Households displaced by conflict;
• Poor food availability at the household level;
This flexible support will in the form of unconditional cash transfers, one per month for three months of the 2019 lean season for 500 food insecure households.
In each target village, project staff will organize a community meeting in collaboration with local authorities, during which staff will explain the project goals and objectives. The population will modify and validate the selection criteria proposed above.
CRS is requesting support to bring life-saving support to 500 food insecure households in Bandiagara Cercle and specifically in Doucombo and Sangha communes, helping them through the lean season and allowing them to fulfill basic needs and maintain/obtain assets without resorting to negative coping mechanisms. The BLESS project will provide humanitarian support during the lean season through unconditional cash transfers. The RRM project, Kisili, has already provided lifesaving assistance to IDPs (80%) and host communities (20%) after rapid assessments. As part of this assistance, RRM-Kisili, funded by USAID/OFDA and FFP, provides Shelter, non-food items and WASH assistance to IDPs and host communities. However, the RRM project does not fund direct food distributions for more than one month, the reality is that many of the most vulnerable IDPs households are critically food insecure during the lean season including those in the two communes targeted by this proposed project. Both projects will address immediate and subsequent humanitarian needs while building a foundation for vulnerable households to increase their economic productivity and resiliency. RRM/USAID is an ongoing project and will be able to support any new IDPs during the implementation of BLESS in Doucombo and Sangha commune. Supporting host communities (who are just as vulnerable as the IDPs) through this support reduces the likelihood of social friction between the two communities, increases social cohesion as well as provides them with the opportunity to increase their agriculture outputs particularly given the losses in the previous crop season.
