4 - Needs

Orange and Misk have conducted needs assessment in the target area:- general need for waste management and collection in the area- supporting the livelihoods of returnees through cash for work- providing access to markets through road rehabilitation- protection against covid-19 protection.

5 - Implementation of activities

- gravle and waste management- cash for work supporting waste management activities- road rehabilitation - supporting the making of masks and distribution of personal protective equipment through municipal service unit. most of the activities of this project are addressed to meet the needs of the wider communities needs in including people with disability, however and whenever possible Orange will ensure the inclusion of people with disability in relevant activities like the municipal committee formation.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Prioritization of safety, dignity and no harm principle: In the selection of CFW beneficiaries it is important to prioritize HH where the problem of child labor is present and cases of severe economic vulnerability. Yet, considering that not all the vulnerable people can participate inheavy works it will be important to provide fewer heavy works opportunities to people with disabilities and to ensure adequate working hours.- exclusion of people with disability: the Orange will work with its partner to identify limitation and potential barriers preventing the inclusion of people with disability in the project activities in addition to ensuring their inclusion in the feedback and accountability mechanisms. - the accountability mechanisms will encourage benefices to self protect and provide themselves with anonymous and accessible means of feedback and complaints.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Roads were constructed before the establishment of the camps and are passing in privately own lands or agriculture lands, posing possibleproblems in terms of house and land property. Risk mitigation: Whenever possible, the project will prioritize pre-existing structures used bothby the host community and the IDPs with a particular attention to the environmental impact.Covid-19 Mainstreaming: This project follows the WHO guidance on prevention and response to COVID-19, in a rigorous effort to prevent theinfection spread, protecting staff and beneficiaries from this hazard. Social distancing, restrictions in gatherings and hygienic measures arein place within the project. In case of an outbreak, Orange will implement the following mitigation measures:- Compliance with local health authorities most updated instructions and procedures.- Provision of sanitization consumables and materials in activities that require in person contact, as in technician practical training andvoucher and distribution activities, considering minimizing personal contact as much as possible and preventing crowding of more than 5persons in proximity.- Risk associated with Cash handling and distribution for cash for work BNFs, Orange will follow its cash assistance SoP and contract pre-qualified agents.Sustainability: Orange has been in coordination with other partners that are planning a rehabilitation for a landfill area to be designated as the main waste collection location for the waste being collected from Ariha. On the other hand collected gravel will be Offred to be used as suitable in the road rehabilitation activity.
