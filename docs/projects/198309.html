Project Summary

The goal of the project is to enable 90,000 out-of-school children living in areas where schools are still closed due to the crisis to access learning opportunities through the radio education program.
In this sense, radios and other learning materials will be purchased and provided to communities to allow this category of out-of-school children and adolescent to follow lessons and messages recorded on USB keys under the supervision of trained facilitators.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the Northwest and Southwest, more than 200,000 children are still out of school for a variety of reasons, including school closures, insecurity, and access difficulties.Education partners have put in place strategies and approaches to enable out-of-school children to participate in learning opportunities, including the organization of community schools, digital learning where possible, and the radio education program. A total of 59,766 (51% girls) participated in the opportunities offered. This represents only 21% of the total number of children targeted under the HRP 2022. Given the poor coverage of the areas by the telephone and internet communication network, the radio education program has proven to be an important means of getting children to reach lessons in the main branches to help maintain a certain level of learning and to keep children interested in learning for future reintegration into school if the situation allows it. These children deprived of their education also have the right to education in the same way as their peers in areas where security permits. From 2021 to date, UNICEF and its implementing partners have reached 44,424 children, including 24,724 girls. However, this number remains insufficient for the magnitude of the needs. There is a need to extend this program to more children by also diversifying the lessons and messages according to the audience (grade level and age). 100,000 out-of-school children will be targeted in the two regions to participate in this program.
