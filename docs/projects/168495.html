Project Summary

In order to deal with the sudden Covid 19 crisis, the project will aim to empower women and girls as frontline responders in the prevention and response to the expansion of COVID-19 among refugee populations, people IDPs, returnees and the host community through a community approach that takes into account the central role of women.
IMPACT
Women and girls contribute and have greater influence as frontline responders from the prevention and response of natural disaster and sudden covid 19 crisis in humanitarian context
EFFET DU PROJET
Durable solutions are promoted for women and girls affected by protracted & slow onset Covid 19 crise
