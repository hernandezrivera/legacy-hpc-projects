Project Summary

One of the main sector plans is the expansion and rehabilitation of protective and safe learning environments including gender-sensitive WASH facilities in school and school inclusiveness for children with disabilities. In addition to provision of school furniture, supplies, stationary, heaters and water tanks.
The protracted crisis in Syria has resulted over 5.8 million school-aged children and over 120,000 teachers to be in need of education assistance inside Syria, 61 per cent of whom are in acute and immediate need.
Children who are out of school face increased protection risks including child marriage and the worst forms of child labour.
The project main goal is to provide support to vulnerable persons by addressing the most critical humanitarian needs. This project will support people in need, especially school-aged children via improved access to safe and protective spaces through small-scale rehabilitation of schools in the targeted cities through rehabilitation, equipping and furnishing of partially damaged existing schools in Deir ez-Zor, Al Raqa, AL Hasakeh, Dar’a and Al Quneitra to improve the learning spaces through implementing small-scale rehabilitation, including gender-sensitive/inclusive WASH facilities and ensure receiving safe and adequate access to safe educational environment.
The project is planned to target 15 schools with estimated number of 6000 students to be targeted.

4 - Needs

Due to a shortage of functional classrooms, classes are overcrowded and over one million children required attending double-shift schools. Teaching capacity remains overstretched. Children who are out of school face increased protection risks including child marriage and the worst forms of child labor. GOPA depends mainly on HNO 2020 as well as GOPA team has conducted a needs assessment aiming to provide an overview of the situation in the areas to address these gaps of providing the assistance. The needs assessment visits have a clear idea of the population dynamics and need. As well, through needs assessment GOPA was able to identify the most schools in need to be targeted through this project.One of the main sector plans is the expansion and rehabilitation of protective and safe learning environments including gender-sensitive WASH facilities in school and school inclusiveness for children with disabilities. In addition to provision of school furniture, supplies, stationary, heaters and water tanks.Children who are out of school face increased protection risks including child marriage and the worst forms of child labor. More than 40 per cent of the out-of-school children in Syria are between 15 and 17 years of age and have differentiated learning, skills development and livelihoods needs. GOPA's mobile team conducted needs assessment visits in these areas in order to in order to better identify the humanitarian needs and priorities of needed intervention, so that it can design a program that better meets these needs.GOPA needs assessment was implemented through its mobile team, the needs assessment included coordinating and meeting with community leaders and focus group discussion meetings with population of different age and gender.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will include the following stages:-Field visits will be conducted to each school to prepare all needed documents including BOQs.-The works will be carried out according to the tendering method followed by GOPA through a dedicated contractor.-The execution of the work will be by contractors who are experienced in the field of architectural and civil projects.-After all planned works are being completed, a special committee is formed to take over the executed works and to ascertain the quality in the specified time. The work will then be officially handed over to the concerned authorities (Directorate of Education).The rehabilitation will include implementing minor maintenance works of the schools including wooden works, aluminum works, electric works in addition to rehabilitation of WASH facilities taking into consideration gender-segregated. Also the project will provide Equipping & Furnishing for the targeted schools with the following items:- Class desk seats- Class tables - Class chairs - Whiteboard - Tables for administration rooms - Chairs for administration rooms - Wooden cupboard- Stationery (one per school / Yearly)The schools will be chosen upon the field visits to the planned targeted areas in coordination with MoE and Education Sector. The implementation will include rehabilitate about 225 new classrooms in 15 schools as estimated number of 6000 of children (3-17 years, girls/boys) will benefit from classrooms rehabilitated in addition to provide the targeted schools with school furniture.GOPA’s Accountability policy (AAP) , staff rely on the feedback from the local community and community leaders, both secular and faith-based, to provide input on the needs of the affected Syrians. GOPA engages with the community through focus groups discussions and separate consultations with the community leader(s) during the project’s design. GOPA uses various channels through which beneficiaries can submit their concerns.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: It is most probably that there are mines in the targeted areas. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigating measures: GOPA will seek to provide protection to the children in the chosen schools.Protection Risk: Risk of hurts to workers or children due to unsound buildings during preparing BOQ and implementation. likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1- Structural assessment for building by qualified engineers 2- repair the building after assuring the soundness and safety of building. 3- clarify for workers about the dangers of ERW and to inform about any strange materials.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The fluctuation of Dollar exchange rate set a great challenge in implementation.Impact:Medium.Mitigating measures: GOPA will coordinate with Education sector in order to increase or decrease the number of targeted schools according the budget. Level of risk: Acceptable.Unforeseen security situation in general which might affect the implementation of activates like accidents resulting from the explosive materials that have not been removed yet.( Low risk). Impact: Medium. Mitigating measures: GOPA will keep consulting with sector on monthly basis about the implementation and monitoring of action plan. Level of risk: Acceptable. Lack of access, no secured roads for shipping the materials to the designated places (Low risk). Impact: Medium. Mitigating measures: GOPA will try to provide adequate spaces in its warehouses to store all goods to ease the implementation. Level of risk: Acceptable.
