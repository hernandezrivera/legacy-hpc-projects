Project Summary

Needs:
The current food insecurity situation in Northwest Syria (NWS) affects approximately 5.7 million individuals, with an estimated 74% of families in Idleb and 61% in Aleppo experiencing food insecurity, most of which are women and children. A gap analysis report indicates projected coverage of only 53% and 44% for January and February 2024, respectively, yet actual figures may be considerably lower than projected. Furthermore, nearly 75% of female-headed households struggle to meet their basic needs adequately, as limited opportunities for livelihoods in camps and reduced income-generating possibilities contribute to about 60% of internally displaced person (IDP) households relying heavily on humanitarian aid. In addition, one-third of IDPs in planned and informal camps depend on assistance in the form of cash and vouchers, resorting to more precarious coping mechanisms such as borrowing, child labour, and sub-optimal diets, disproportionately impacting women and children.
In September 2023, the Shelter/NFI Cluster reported an urgent need for winter assistance among 2.1 million IDPs in NWS. Presently, there exists a funding shortfall of 67.1 million USD to address the winterization needs of 1.4 million people. A Winter Weather Vulnerability Analysis by the CCCM cluster identified varying degrees of vulnerability, with 31,726 individuals facing catastrophic conditions, 312,000 individuals in highly extreme situations, and 850,000 individuals enduring severe conditions in NWS.
Moreover, CARE's Multi-Sector Needs Assessment for 2023/2024, conducted its implementing partners, reaffirmed the critical demand for winterization assistance. A striking 81% of respondents identified it as their second priority, following food, underscoring the pressing need for immediate intervention and support.
Protection Risk Analysis:
Risk 1: Increased Gender-Based Violence (GBV) due to shifting household power dynamics from cash interventions.
Likelihood 1: Moderate - Impact 1: High
Mitigation measures 1:
- CARE will ensure cash distribution points are strategically located, with safe routes and timings, and preferably staffed by female personnel to enhance women's safety and comfort.
- will conduct awareness sessions to address concerns and promote understanding among men and women regarding the benefits of women's empowerment without threatening traditional roles.
Risk 2: Increased risk of exploitation and abuse, particularly among vulnerable populations, due to the influx of cash into communities.
Likelihood 2: Moderate - Impact 2: Moderate to High
Mitigation measures 2:
- Strengthening accountability mechanisms
- Community engagement and awareness
- Work closely with local law enforcement and protection actors to establish referral pathways and coordinate responses to cases of exploitation and abuse, ensuring swift and effective action.
Implementation of Activity:
- Overview: The project will be implemented by CARE Turkey through its IPs as a cross-border response in Northwest Syria and it is considered as a significant annual undertaking. The coordination efforts with all relevant partners are yet to be done since this initiative is implemented in emergencies and designed to address the urgent needs of the affected population in the region by providing cash assistance.
- Project Objective: To provide emergency relief to 10,000 HHs, impacting approximately 50,000 individuals.
- Project Modality: Distribution of $150.
- Selection Criteria: The project targets the most vulnerable individuals among internally displaced persons (IDPs) and members of host communities.
- Monitoring Tools: Distribution Monitoring - Post-Distribution Monitoring (PDM).
- Project Output: Empowering recipients to prioritize their specific needs, whether for food, shelter, healthcare, or other essential requirements. Moreover, the utilization of cash assistance actively contributes to stimulating local economies by encouraging purchases from nearby markets.
