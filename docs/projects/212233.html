Project Summary

Needs:
The dire food security situation in Northwest Syria necessitates urgent humanitarian intervention. With 4.1 million people in need of assistance, including 3.1 million acutely food insecure individuals, the THRIVE program by IhsanRD aims to alleviate suffering through targeted distribution of food vouchers and cash assistance. This intervention addresses immediate needs while mitigating risks and challenges inherent in the conflict-affected environment.
Intervention Strategy:
The THRIVE program seeks to increase access to food and necessities for internally displaced persons (IDPs) and vulnerable households in Aleppo and Idleb Governorates. By distributing E-food vouchers and cash for food, IhsanRD aims to cover approximately 70% of the Special Minimum Energy Basket (SMEB) for food, amounting to $65 per household. The intervention targets 4,300 households for cash assistance and 3,000 for food vouchers. Cash distribution will be conducted in two rounds simultaneously to overcome the challenge of limited availability of small denominations of USD banknotes.
Activities:
The intervention involves multi-round cash for food distribution to 4,825 households in Idleb and food vouchers to 4,500 households in Aleppo. These activities are aligned with the objectives of the Food Security Cluster and Cash Working Group for Syria, aiming to scale and sustain minimum food consumption needs through life-saving assistance.
Protection Against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA):
IhsanRD prioritizes the protection of beneficiaries by empowering them with information on risks and mitigation measures. The organization maintains contact with beneficiaries through dedicated reporting channels and implements a referral mechanism for severe rights violations. Measures such as gender-segregated waiting lines, multiple distribution sites, and female supervision during distributions prioritize the dignity and safety of vulnerable populations, including women, older people, pregnant and lactating women, and people with disabilities.
Risk and Mitigation Summary:
To ensure successful implementation of the intervention, IhsanRD identifies and addresses key risks:
Changing Security Situations: Mitigation includes monitoring security, suspending fieldwork if necessary, and coordinating with donors.
Hawala Centers Non-operational: Coordination with the GOAL team is essential for effective resolution.
Authorities Interfering with Beneficiary Selection: Early communication with local authorities and maintaining independence through MoUs are key mitigation strategies.
Delayed Funds Disbursement: Senior-level coordination with the GOAL team is crucial to secure advance payments.
USD Denominations Unavailable or Poor Quality: Mitigation involves advance notification to Financial Service Providers (FSPs) and adequate preparation.
Pressuring Beneficiaries into Payments: Mitigation strategies focus on raising beneficiary awareness and implementing feedback mechanisms.
Shopkeepers Not Adhering to Contract Terms: Training, monitoring, and corrective actions ensure adherence to contract terms.
Conclusion:
The THRIVE program by IhsanRD represents a critical response to the dire food security situation in Northwest Syria. The intervention aims to alleviate suffering and uphold the dignity and rights of affected populations amidst conflict and adversity through targeted distribution of food vouchers and cash assistance; coupled with robust protection measures, the intervention aims to alleviate suffering and uphold the dignity and rights of affected populations amidst conflict and adversity.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project will focus on improving access to basic food through Multi-rounds food assistance. A repetitive transfer is proposed based on scenario planning that indicates continued conflict and displacement in northwest Syria. Therefore, this immediate response to the most vulnerable households will continue to be a response priority by reaching 8,100 HHs through cash for food for 8 rounds and 3,000 HHs through E-food voucher for 8 rounds. IhsanRD will agree and establish BNFs selection criteria to ensure that the most vulnerable households will be targeted with multi-round cash for food and e-food voucher interventions. The cash will be unrestricted, while the food vouchers will be restricted for the items contracted by the contracted shopkeepers. IhsanRD will sensitize targeted households to ensure they are aware of the needs the interventions are intended to meet. Also, IhsanRD will closely monitor market dynamics through primary market assessments and secondary sources to ensure that CVA remains the preferred and best modality. CVA is a life-saving activity that needs to continue even in extreme circumstances. The value of assistance will be approximately 70% of the food SMEB, (equivalent to a daily calorific value of 1,512 kilocalories/person) and the FSL Cluster has recently agreed to change the value from $75 to $65/household. Accordingly, the transfer value for cash for food and voucher assistance will be $65 USD as the maximum transfer value per HH, to be in line with the coordinated transfer value recommended by the FSL Cluster, to achieve 70% of the vulnerable household food needs. IhsanRD will apply a sliding scale, considering $13 per HH member, with a maximum value of $65 per HH. This is in accordance with the FSL Cluster guidelines and in coordination with other actors distributing CVA for food in NWS.
