Project Summary

Cordaid South Sudan will implement project aimed at supporting local health systems transformation in South Sudan with a total population of 2,241,450. The project will aim to reach 1,067,794 population in need. The project will directly target to reach 586,939 people of both gender and ages. Currently Cordaid is implementing health system programmes in more than 180 facilities with funding from Crown Agents and UNICEF ,UNHCR and WFP. Cordaid as health programmes in 8 states and 15 counties in South Sudan.Cordaid in 2020 had a portfolio of 18.7 Millions USD ,It was implementing 24 project in the departments of Health,resilince,agribusiness and Strategic partnership. Cordaid in 2020 from all health project reached directly 1.5 Million patients and conducted 19,841 deliveries and 124,091 fully immunized children.The project will Supporting local health systems transformation among vulnerable communities in South Sudan with a specific focus on reducing maternal and child mortality, through.
(i)The delivery of essential package of health and nutrition services,
(ii)Promoting community participation and engagement in health as a public good and
(iii)Supporting local health systems transformation.
iv) Strengthen community infection and prevention controls of diseases and infections, including COVID-19 at all health services points and paying attention to disseminating appropriate information to the vulnerable communities.
The project will save Life by reducing morbidities and mortalities from preventable diseases through'; improving delivery and coverage of immunization services to all children, improving access and uptake of maternal and child health services, treatment of common illness and childhood diseases such pneumonia, Diarrhoea and Malaria. The project will also ensure inclusive design of mobile clinics to communities isolated by inaccessibility to health services and floods, delivery of outpatients and inpatients services by strengthening referral mechanism. This project will ensure establishing a team of highly skilled and resilient health care workers and training of health workers, routine joint support supervision to ensure quality delivery of health services following the ministry of health and world health organization guidelines, protocols, and standards. The project will ensure appropriate involvement of communities through strengthening of established grievance redress mechanisms and accountability to affected population providing local solutions to the problems identified.

Number of Mothers who received ANC1 and ANC4+Measuring success in the project will adopt the Success Case Method that looks at the most, and least, successful participants of a program. The purpose is not to examine the average performance - rather, by identifying and examining the extreme cases, it asks: 'When the program works, how well does it work? What is working, and what is not?'. Key question to be addressed in measuring success includeWhat is really happening?What results, if any, is the program helping to produce?What is the value of the results?How could the initiative be improved?The immediate results of conducting a Success Case Method study include documented stories of impact that can be disseminated to stakeholders, and a better knowledge of factors that enhance or impede business impact. There are five key steps in measure and documenting success1. Focusing and planning a Success Case study2. Creating an “impact model” that defines what success should look like3. Designing and implementing a survey to search for best and worst cases4. Interviewing and documenting success cases 5. Communicating findings, conclusions, and recommendations
