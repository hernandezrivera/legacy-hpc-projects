Project Summary

There will be a child-friendly space that provides children with free and organized play, entertainment and entertainment, as well as interactive learning activities. It aims to support the resilience and health of children who have experienced disasters, and to establish positive and supportive interactive relationships, thus helping the child to enhance his various abilities, reduce stress, and restore his rights to play and learn by creating a child-centered environment that supports his positive interaction with his peers and the surrounding community.
This safe space is a place to carry out kinetic recreational activities for children that help them learn through play and activities.
The general objective of the project: to provide psychosocial support in its “early stages” and to build local capacities to enhance the protection of displaced children and those affected by conflict and war, and to protect them from violence, abuse, exploitation and neglect, and to contribute to the promotion and development of a protection system that includes response and case management through:
1- Provide safe and equal opportunities for children to play, learn, build resilience and enhance communication.
2- Detecting the threats and risks to which children are exposed, and the fears that may affect them, and finding appropriate means of response.
3- Enhancing the psychological and social resilience of children.
4- Restoration of normalcy and routine of life.
5- Children exchange experiences and coping strategies through interactive activities of the space.
6- Find tools for personal safety.
7- Ensuring equality for all children (girls and boys), especially those in need of special attention and care.
8- Providing general care and services for children related to providing counseling and awareness on a number of issues, such as health and hygiene, in addition to psychosocial support activities.
9- Reaching a large number of children.
10- Facilitate linking children to available services, bearing in mind that a child friendly space is not a treatment center for specific cases.
11- Enhancing community participation through interactive activities carried out in the space.

In areas such as north-west Syria where two-thirds of the population (1.8 million people) have already been displaced, there is limited capacity to accommodate new IDPs and provide additional humanitarian assistance in camps. Currently, 1,234 of 1,420 IDP sites are unplanned, self-settled locations with high population density.More than six million children, including those with disabilities, are at heightened risk of violence, abuse and exploitation, and need critical child protection services. 7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks:1- Children are over-exposed to the risk of, or experience, harm by teachers, school personnel, organization staff or related entities. 2-Risk of explosives, as landmines, exploitations, harassment that students encounter on their ways to and from schools/learning facilities. 3- Aid workers or associated people use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving. Mitigation:1- Learning facilities should have Child Safeguarding polices and systems in place by the organizationshould be regularly monitored and corrective action taken when needed. The goal of child safeguarding is to create and maintain a safe learning environment through preventive actions that protect children from actions, or inactions that may lead to harm. 2- Reduce time/distance travelled by decentralizing learning facilities.Consider sunrise and sunset times when setting learning hours.Provide students, their caregivers and school personnel information on safer routes/times to travel.Provide students, their caregivers and school personnel information on what to do and what not to do if they face different types of threats.If appropriate, possible and practical promote caregivers accompanying students to/from school.Engage community leaders/members and change makers to support awareness raising.3- Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies should be in place.
