Project Summary

In Cameroon, from Jan 3 2020 to 10:07am CET, 7 December 2020, there have been 24,752 confirmed cases of COVID-19 with 443 deaths.
After first appearing in the Wuhan Province of the People’s Republic of China in December 2019, over time, the virus has gradually spread to at least 205 countries and regions, according to the WHO situation Dashboard, resulting in the death or quarantine of thousands of people.
Cameroon, like many States in the world, is not spared by this pandemic. At the onset of its emergence the Government of Cameroon implemented a prevention plan to stem the spread of this epidemic from the very first hours of its emergence. It should be noted, however, that the general population neither take the risk seriously nor follow the advice of WHO and guidance issued by national and local health authorities. Moreover, prevalent myths about the virus such as “ exposure to the sun or higher temperature will destroy the virus or drinking alcohol will prevent you from getting sick from the virus or only the older people are at risk. It is unfortunately difficult to implement the prevention plan.
In the midst of the ongoing socio-political crisis in the Southwest and North West regions which have lead to the so many IDPs being displaced to other part of Cameroon. While the government of Cameroon and its partner Humanitarian organizations are struggling to solve the problem of food security, health and sanitation etc the COVID-19 pandemic has added to already existing crises and if nothing is done and done fast to reduce the spread in Cameroon especially in rural areas where people are still resistance to the pandemic, the situation will aggravate with massive increase of mortality
The number of cases exponentially increases every single day and if urgent measures are not taken at the community level to prevent the fast spread of the disease. The suspected and confirmed cases are being stigmatised in their communities.“Sensitization and education on the Prevention of COVID-19 through community radios and the provision of preventive measures in Urban and peri Urban Communities in Ngoketunjia Division” is a major approach to slow the speed and help prevent the virus from spreading in the communities. Within the Divison public and private services, marketplaces are operational which are the main high-risk areas for the contamination. This project aims at responding to the Emergency prevention of the coronavirus pandemic in the Babessi, Ndop and Balikumbat council areas of the Ngoketunjia Division of the North West Region Cameroon.
CHASCAM will provide assistance in hygiene kits (hand washing materials such as: a 50-litre buckets with head taps, water collection basin/bucket and pieces of soap), face masks and hand sanitizers. Sensitisation and advocacy campaigns through three community radios with the use of English, Pigeon English and Local dialets/ languages. The team will equally target 08 market places and functional business hot spots within the identified subdivisions.
Strategic Objective; Reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen resilience of 70% of people affected by crisis
Specific objective; By the end of 2021, the use of negative coping strategies is mitigated for 4500 vulnerable people affected by crisis through COVID 19 inputs, sensitisation and education on protective activities.
Specific objective indicator; 4500 of targeted people (female and male) that received assistance through COVID 19 inputs, sensitisation and education on protective measures and activities.

1 - Besoins / Needs

With the ever increasing COVID-19 cases , Cameroonian are worried and living in the state of fear and panic, not knowing what to do. Most people have resulted in intensive prayers and others are busy eating different grasses and stuff as preventive measure to the COVID-19 pandemic. The 4500 population needs humanirian assistance to prevent themselves from this pandemic, this can be done through; sensitization and education meetings, provision of face marsks and wash hand materials to prevent this covid 19 pandemic from the displaced persons in Ngoketunjia Division.The Strategic Objective is to reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen resilience of 70% of people affected by crisis in Ngoketunjia Division.The Specific objective is that by the end of 2021, the use of negative coping strategies is mitigated for 4500 vulnerable people affected by crisis through COVID 19 inputs, sensitisation and education on protective measures and activities.Specific objective indicator; 4500 of targeted people (female and male) that received assistance through COVID 19 inputs, sensitisation and education on protective measures and activities.The activities include; • Sensitize and educate the communities about the COVID -19 through community radios and field trips to markets and community hot spots, explaining the signs and symptoms of COVID -19 and the alternative prevention measures as stipulated by the government and WHO. • Identify and provide to 130 hand washing points in the targeted markets and rural and urban hot spots. • Provide 5000 face masks, 500 liters of hand sanitizers to public offices • Train and support tailor on the production of face mask. • Produce three months radio program on the fight against covid 19• Produce 1300 information leaflets on COVID 19 .
