1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at reduction of acute malnutrition amongst children below 5years, Pregnant and Lactating women in vulnerable communities of Mocimboa, Quissanga, Metuge and Montepuez in the province of Cabo Delgado. This will involve: 1. Active case finding will be conducted amongst through strengthening screening of children below 5years at health facilities, during mobile brigades and at household level by facilitating Community health workers, equipping health facilities with anthropometric equipment. Activities will involve screening, referral, defaulter tracing, capacity strengthening of community health workers on IMAM and CMAM package (PRN), printing and disseminating key IEC messages at health facilities and during mobile brigades. 2. Management of acute malnutrition will involve collaboration with government nutrition technicians to ensure that all cases of severe acute malnutrition and moderate acute malnutrition identified amongst children below 5years, pregnant and lactating mothers are admitted on respective programmes and receive the recommended care up to when they recover. Efforts will involve capacity strengthening of health workers on integrated and Community based management of acute malnutrition as well as improving supply chain management for Specialised Nutritious at health facilities and during mobile brigades. 3. Delivery of gender-responsive infant and young child feeding messages for women and men caregivers will involve dissemination of IEC material, conducting sensitisation sessions at the health facilities and mobile brigade, conducting community level culinary sessions and teachings amongst the members of mother care groups and male change agents. Activists and APS will undergo training on the PIN package such that they can support the care group activities and also conduct home visits and offer specialised teachings to male caregivers of children below 5years. Theatre performances and radio talk shows will also be conducted more so during commemoration of key nutrition events. 4. To boost the administration of vitamin A and deworming for children 6-59months, Johanniter will boost the supply chain of Vitamin A and Deworming tablets such that there is adequate support of these at the health facilities and for mobile clinics. In addition, refresher training will be conducted for technicians about extended programme on Immunisation and logistics will be availed for technicians to be able to offer Vitamin A and deworming sites during mobile clinic activities. 5. Conduct routine surveillance through holding bimonthly meetings with Community Health workers, surveillance on key community nutrition data and participating in sector wide SMART surveys

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Screening children under 5years, Pregnant and lactating women at health facilities and mobile brigade sites• Community level screening, referral, follow-up and defaulter-tracing of children under 5years, Pregnant and Lactating women as they turn up for culinary sessions• Printing and dissemination of IEC material to health facilities, mobile clinic sites and community health workers • Transportation of Specialized Nutritious Foods, Vitamin A and Deworming tablets to peripheral health facilities from District stores• Training health workers on Integrated Management Acute Malnutrition• Training health workers on Community Management of Acute Malnutrition• Provide logistical support to technicians involved in conducting mobile clinic activities to boost uptake of Vitamin A and deworming alongside community management of acute malnutrition• Refresher training for health workers on Extended programme on Immunisation (EPI) package• Training of Community health workers on Prevention actions for Nutrition (PIN)• Conduct theatre performances and radio talk shows on preventive action for malnutrition• Conduct awareness raising and culinary sessions for mothers in the formed support groups with focus on recommended nutrition practices• Conduct quarterly household level monitoring of nutrition practices amongst the members of the mother support groups in collaboration with nutrition activists and APS.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project utilizes a thorough risk assessment and safeguarding strategy to proactively identify and mitigate potential risks, ensuring no harm to beneficiaries, especially those most vulnerable. • The project initiates with a detailed assessment of security, environmental, cultural, and logistical factors. Regular updates allow the team to anticipate and respond to shifting conditions, especially those impacting displaced or conflict-affected populations.• Community members, particularly women and leaders, are actively involved in risk identification. Feedback mechanisms enable beneficiaries to report concerns, raise issues, and provide recommendations, ensuring risks are swiftly addressed and interventions remain safe and effective.• Adopting a “do-no-harm” approach, the project emphasizes gender sensitivity and the protection of vulnerable groups. Activities respect cultural contexts while encouraging gender equality and health improvements.• Regular monitoring and adaptive planning allow adjustments to strategies in response to emerging risks, supporting safe delivery of WASH, health, and nutritional services. This approach maintains a safe, respectful, and adaptable environment, ensuring positive, risk-sensitive impact across target communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This project has 95% of target groups being women and children below 5years so all programming will rotate around identifying, management and prevention of acute malnutrition amongst children below 5years, pregnant and lactating women. Mothers will undergo teachings that vary from recommended nutrition practices to key life skills and integration of preventive actions for gender based violence. The proposed project fosters meaningful access to assistance and actively empowers women and elderly persons through targeted, inclusive strategies:• From the onset; women, elderly persons, and local leaders participate in decision-making processes to ensure that interventions reflect their specific needs and perspectives. Inviting women’s groups and elder representatives into planning sessions promotes interventions sensitive to their unique challenges.• This project will deliver a gender-sensitive humanitarian response by training women as model mothers to guide fellow women on recommended nutrition practices.• Regular feedback sessions will be conducted with segregated categories of community members including adolescent girls and boys, adult men and women, people with disabilities and elderly groups to encourage community ownership. These sessions allow participants to voice concerns, share challenges, and guide adjustments to project activities, ensuring responsiveness and accountability. This approach enhances immediate access to essential services, emphasizing the roles of women and elderly persons in community well-being and leadership.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will take an inclusive and proactive approach to identify individuals with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance:• The project will collaborate with community leaders, and health workers to identify individuals with disabilities within target areas. This will involve direct outreach, household assessments, and consultations to ensure these individuals are included in all project activities.• A thorough needs assessment will be conducted to identify the specific challenges faced by people with disabilities in accessing nutrition and health services. This will focus on physical, informational, and social barriers, addressing both environmental and attitudinal challenges.• Nutrition service delivery points, such as cooking demonstration points and health facilities, will have latrines that will be designed or adapted to be disability-friendly, incorporating features like ramps, handrails, and clearly marked pathways to ensure physical accessibility. Project staff and volunteers will undergo training on disability inclusion and rights, ensuring they are equipped to meet the needs of individuals with disabilities. Community members will also be sensitized to reduce stigma and foster an inclusive environment.These measures aim to ensure full participation and equitable access for people with disabilities, empowering them to actively engage in all nutrition-related project activities. Regular monitoring, feedback and adaptive measures will be implemented to address any emerging disability barriers and ensure continuous improvement in accessibility and inclusivity.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed project in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique, is committed to minimizing environmental impacts while implementing emergency lifesaving Nutrition interventions. Several measures will be taken to ensure the integration of environmental considerations:• The project will prioritize the local procurement of materials, where quality standards allow, to minimize transportation-related emissions and the use of plastic will be avoided wherever feasible, opting for sustainable alternatives to reduce waste.• Environmental education will be integrated into community engagement, focusing on efficient water use, proper hygiene, and responsible waste management. These practices aim to prevent environmental degradation, such as deforestation and soil erosion, which are prevalent in the region.• Regular monitoring will be conducted to assess the effectiveness of environmental mitigation measures. Project activities will be adjusted as needed to prevent potential environmental harm and ensure sustainable implementation.By integrating these environmental measures, the project aims to deliver critical nutrition support while preserving the natural environment and promoting long-term community resilience.
