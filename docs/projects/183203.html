Project Summary

Lebanon is facing a huge crises , starting from the COVID 19 outbreak, to struggling in accessing clean water and electricity , to running out of bread , fuel , medicine , paramedics, baby milk , and obstacles in acquiring hospital rooms .Similarly, all health facilities such as hospitals , dispensaries , primary health cares , pharmacies are all in danger, they are running out of electricity due to the lack of fuel, lack of paramedics and medicine. Let’s not forget about the enormous immigration of the medical staff and the financial crisis.
The COVID-19 pandemic is a major threat both to physical and mental health and to the well-being of entire societies.
Today Lebanon has registered more than 609,189 Corvid -19 cases, 8,129 deaths, and 563,521 have recovered from covid-19, unfortunately little to know about their health status if they are suffering from impairment on independence or from any disabilities that affect their life, since most of the patients are discharged directly home without referral to rehabilitation center.
For all of the reasons mentioned above, I came out with the idea of establishing a covid-19 rehabilitation home health care center, comprising from occupational therapy , physiotherapist , doctors, physicians, nurses, and aids that aims to help the vulnerable Lebanese and migrant’s population.
Studies has shown that post intensive care syndrome after critical illness with Covis-19 , 70-80% of patients show physical impairment or mental health illness ,Persistent impairment need 1 to 5 years follow up and 1/3 of them don’t return to work .
Thus, rehabilitation home health care center has an important role in the management and recovery of people with COVID-19, and it consider as one of the essential services defined within Universal Health Coverage.
Rehabilitation in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic is required for people with essential needs and to optimize functioning, minimizes the impact of impairment on independence and quality of life.
Impairments most likely to encounter:
o Physical deconditioning and muscle weakness, fatigue
o Impaired lung function
o Delirium and other cognitive impairments
o Impaired swallow and communication
o Mental health disorders and psychosocial support needs
o many organs can be affected due to covid-19 still long-term complications are unknown
vulnerable Lebanese and migrants have the right to receive the health care needed , but unfortunately in Lebanon , specifically in Tripoli , patients can’t afford the payment of private hospital and the government hospital are always full of patients .
At the end rehabilitation is important to reduce disabilities, social integration, prevents congestion in medical and acute facilities

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Objective 3: Respond to COVID-19 in Lebanon – will follow the pillars approach laid out in the WHO global Strategic. WHO had issued the COVID-19 Strategic Preparedness and Response Plan aimed at guiding the coordinated action that we must take at national, regional, and global levels to overcome the ongoing challenges in the response to COVID-19, address inequities, and plot a course out of the pandemic.More than 170 countries now have a COVID-19 preparedness and response plan based on the 10 operational and technical pillars set out in WHO’s Strategic Preparedness and Response Plan.According to WHO, pillar 7, had defined the case management, clinical operations, and therapeutics , approximately 15% of patients with covid-19 develop severe disease that requires oxygen support, and 5% have critical disease with complications such as respiratory failure, acute respiratory distress syndrome, sepsis and septic shock, thromboembolism, and/or multi-organ failure. Thus, COVID-19 rehabilitation home care center supported by mobile care teams’ center will support the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrants impacted by the socio-economic crisis. COVID-19 rehabilitation home care center has an important role in the management and recovery of people postCOVID-19 condition characterized by fatigue and cognitive impairments affects not only patients that have been hospitalized, but also those that had mild or moderate disease.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The COVID-19 pandemic is affecting the entire world population,but in all societies, certain individuals, groups or communities, face greater marginalization,inequality and violence than others.Such as people living in existing humanitarian emergency settings,Women and adolescent girls,Children,older people Persons with disabilities Migrants, and health care workers who are predominantly women .The COVID-19 pandemic has seen a rise in Gender-Based Violence GBV and sexual exploitation and abuse PSEA worldwide, a pattern that has been observed in Lebanon as well.Rehabilitation COVID-19 home health care center will coordinate with trained SGBV focal points and will: Identify and refer GBV and PSEA cases to health providers and clinical management of rape services, distribute dignity kits along with key messages on SGBV and PSEA for women and girls who are post COVID-19 condition,ensure all staff and volunteers are trained , and provide a list of referral services of PSEA to staff .

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

According to WHO, 15% of patients with covid19 are suffering from post COVID-19 condition characterized by impairment on independence, disabilities, decrease quality of life and difficulty in returning to work.Hence, the importance of establishing rehabilitation covid-19 home health care center to improve the quality of life for vulnerable Lebanese and migrant population. Therefore, we launched a strategy plan to start the home care center: Listening and talking to communities, is a fundamental part of our association. Establish Call Center to respond to specific needs.Ensuring accountability to integrate the home health care project into the countries’ strategies and programs.Evaluating, and recruiting staff inductions, , performing managements, trainings, partnership agreements, and highlight reportingDesign, monitor and evaluate the goals and objectives of programs with the involvement organization and populations.
