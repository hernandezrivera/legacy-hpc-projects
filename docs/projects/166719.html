Project Summary

Develop COVID-19-specific messages to mitigate exposure, Procure and distribute 2500 NFI kits to vulnerable households affected by COVID-19 to reduce the number of households sharing essential household items and to build up contingency stocks, Provision of 12 Quarantine Facilities in the Resettlement Areas, Temporary Shelter for overcrowded families (200), Provision of 4 solar powered handwashing facilities which will impact about 10000 people, Provide Psychosocial Support Services in partnership with other partners.
