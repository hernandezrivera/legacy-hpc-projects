Project Summary

In Metekel Zone, due to conflict, houses and other property were damaged, which was also accompanied by loss of life. This again resulted in displacing large number of people. Female headed households, children, elderly people and people with disabilities are among the major affected by the incident. This, as requested by the local authorities and humanitarian agencies, calls for an immediate response for food, Non food items and improvement of water and sanitation issues.
Therefore, this project tries to address the ES/NFI needs of 6,600 individuals (1,200 households) displaced by the conflict through the provision of Emergency Shelter (ES) and Non Food Items (NFIs). In doing so, essential protection mainstreaming, gender-inclusive approach, mitigation of gender based violence and sexual exploitation and abuse will be given attention. The support allows the IDPs and members of the family to meet their basic needs and to lead a relatively better life and contribute towards the attainment of the shelter cluster plan and priorities.
The project shall be implemented in Dibate Woreda, Metekel Zone in Benishangul Gumuz Region. The proposed project targeted 6,600 IDPs (1,200 HHs) and $200,000.00 is requested for implementation of the proposed project. Preparation phases including agreement with donor and government will be finalized until May 2022 and implementation of the project will be between June-October 2022.
Female-headed households, households with large family size, people with chronic illness, elderly people, people with disabilities and lactating mothers will be provided priority.

1. - Projects activities

Provide Emergency Shelter (ES) for 400 Households (2,200 individuals) 400 Households (2,200 individuals) will be provided an in-kind emergency shelter support. As per the cluster standard, two tarpaulin and one rope will be provided for a Household. Provide Non-food Items (NFIs) for 500 Households (2,750 individuals) 500 Households (2,750 individuals) will be provided NFIs. Out of the total figure, 70% will be assisted in-kind and the remaining 30% in cash. The item shall consist Minimum Standard Core Relief Items V1. As pert the cluster indication, most vulnerable households will be provided with the core relief items. Provide Shelter Construction Support for 100 HHs (550 individuals) Shelter construction support will be provided to 100 HHs (550 individuals). In the shelter repair assistance, the shelter cluster standard will be applied. Provide Cash for Shelter Rent assistance for 200 (1,100 individuals) Cash for Shelter Rent will be provided for 200 HHs (1,100 individuals). Based on the currently developed guidance note for transfer value harmonization, the suggested value for cash for shelter rent will be ETB 1,500.00/month. The rent will be covered for 4 months and a household head will be provided ETB 6,000.00 for the 4 months rent fee. Publication and Dissemination of Information, Education and Communication (IEC) Materials 2,000 IEC materials will be prepared and distributed to the target community on how to use the ES-NFI along with the kits. This helps to ensure that beneficiaries are aware of using the items. The project targets 10% of the host community who are in need of ES/NFI assistance. This helps to support a needs-based response approach and contributes in improving social cohesion and community structure rehabilitation in displacement-affected areas.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

In the implementation of the project, MIH ensures community engagement at all stages of the project implementation. This includes consultation and discussion with female-headed households, people with disabilities, elderly people, men, women, boys and girls. In doing so, needs of these groups will be addressed.In collaboration with representatives of the IDPs and respective governmental offices, complaint feed back mechanism shall be availed during pre and post distribution. Verification of beneficiaries will be conducted in coordination with community members and local government authorities to ensure vulnerability criteria are met and inclusion errors are reduced. In addition, after completion of registration of beneficiaries, coupons will be issued to beneficiaries for records and indication of assistance.Using the Shelter Cluster SoPs, joint evaluation will be conducted with the involvement of stakeholders such as the donor, government and community representatives. This will be done after completion of the Emergency Shelter and NFI support. In order to analyze the impact of the project, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will also be conducted after one month of completion of the support. The project will coordinate closely with National and sub-national Shelter NFI clusters as well as National and Sub-National Cash Working Groups. MIH will Participates in the periodical cluster meetings to provide updates about the status of the project and reduce duplication of efforts.MIH is a member and active participant of the Shelter Clusters both at the national and Sub National levels. Additionally, MIH is active participant of Cash Working Group at national level. Thus, MIH will continue coordinating with these clusters and local government authorities. The coordination will be conducted through sharing of information and lessons learnt in field operations, sharing of assessment findings and joint assessments and joint response whenever possible. MIH will also involve project beneficiaries in project design and implementation to ensure safe, meaningful, and equitable access. Thus,MIH will carry out discussions with project beneficiaries and representatives of the IDPs on the importance of participation of all community groups, implementation methodology, beneficiary selection criteria, distribution site identification and other protocols, and engage in close coordination with local government and other partners. MIH will also support the formation of community level committees to ensure participation and involvement of communities, information sharing, ownership, andadequate access. MIH will promote equal decision making between male and female household members. In all steps of the community involvement and cash distribution,MIH will consider minimum standards for prevention of COVID-19 spread through awareness raising, provision of personal protective equipment, and coordination with local health actors. MIH will continue to participate and coordinate at the Zonal and Woreda level actors to ensure that there will be no duplication of efforts. MIH will also endeavor to coordinate its activities with partners in the target area.
