Project Summary

The Project has planned to response the emergency with following strategies:
a- Rehabilitation of 15 flood effected schools in Jhal Magsi and Kachi District (10 partially damaged and 5 fully damaged)
The rehabilitation of 15 flood effected schools in the two district 15 schools will be focused out of which 10 in Jhal Magsi and 5 in Kachi. Out of these focused schools 10 partially damaged schools (7 Jhal Magsi and 3 Kachi) and 5 fully damaged schools (3 Jhal Magsi and 2 Kachi) will be focused. The schools will be selected based on high enrollment track record of school enrollment and population available around the schools. The priority will be given to Girls schools and Primary level. The Fully damaged schools will be provided with Temporary Learning Centers (TLC) including the toilet and hand washing facilities. the Schools may start functioning in these TLC so that the children may continue their education

The partially damaged schools will be repaired and equipped with missing facilities.
The schools will be selected based on detailed survey as per set indictors.
These focused schools will be equipped with furniture, tats, TLM, sports material, first aid kit and ECE material. Besides the PTSMCs will be revalidated and formed and capacitated in their role to respond the Balochistan Compulsory Education Act 2014.
The project will also contribute in safe opening of these focused school while responding COVID Under safe opening of schools while responding to COVID. Orientation will be given to community to build their confidence to sent their children to school and to be aware with their role in this critical situation. The washing facilities and washrooms will be rehabilitated and provided under missing facilities. The COVID messages will be displayed. Teachers and students will be oriented on proper social distance maintain and strategy will be developed school to school basis to minimize the contact of students and teachers. The teachers will be trained on the teaching strategies to be adopted in the COVID situation so that the children may continue their learning.
The Child Clubs will be formed at each school level and trained with their role to assist the teacher in proper functioning of schools and increasing the enrollment.
The School Develop Plans (SDPs) will be developed jointly with the PTSMC for all schools to mark the requirements of the schools and the efforts of the Community. The SDP will be rolled out in due spirit to achieve the objectives of the community participation and the rehabilitation of the school to proper function.
The community participation will be ensured by forming and strengthening the PTSMC and Mother groups for each school. The linkages of the PTSMC will be established and strengthened with the cluster schools to get the optimum benefit of the cluster budget allocated to these schools.
The Community will also collect the data of the children out of the school in their area and put efforts to get them enroll in the school to achieve the objective of the Article 25A and the Balochistan Compulsory Education Act- 2014. The project will mark the cases who achieve the maximum enrollment and put efforts to mark 100% enrolment achievemnt.
The linkages of the focused schools will be developed with the nearest health facilities to ensure the coordination among the health and education sector.
b. Safe Opening of Schools responding COVID
In response to the COVID the Safe opening school is required to mobilize the communities and aware them with the Safe School opening strategies.
The Communities will be oriented with the social Distance, Hand Washing and maintaining the temperature of the students and provision of water and mask to the children.
The Students and Teachers will be oriented on the hand washing, disinfection of schools, social distances and maintaining the temperature.
The COVID response messages will be displayed in the schools.
