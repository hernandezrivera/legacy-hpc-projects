Project Summary

Somali region is facing a prolonged drought after three consecutive failed rainy seasons since late 2020 affecting an estimated 3.3 million people. Ten zones in the region are affected by the drought. . In December 2021 Government estimation stood at more than 164,800 livestock mortality in Somali. Additional livestock is at risk across affected areas. According to the assessment conducted by Somali Regional Education and education cluster partners, a total of 1,867 learning spaces/schools are closed resulting in 382,403 (45 % girls) drop out of the schools. The huge pastoral and agro-pastoral movement of the communities in search of pasture and water due to the current severe drought has resulted family separation, anxiety, stress, and trauma. According to Deyr multi-agency needs assessment report, 55 % increase in domestic violence, and 69 % rise in FGM and early marriage was reported due to the drought emergency in the region
Based on report from Shabele DRMB , currently 230,000 people living in 135 kebeles are facing a shortage of water and are severely affected. Only 80 kebeles are receiving water ration while 55 kebele receives nothing so far. Lack of enough available water trucks and limited water sources, pose challenges to meeting the growing needs. Drought has displaced a significant number of people in Adadle, Elale, Berano, Gode, Ferfer and East Emay. According to the ETT (Event Trucking Tool) conducted by IOM, a total of 36,342 IDPs (6,057 HHs) has been displaced so far despite the data is covering a period between 12-18 February 2022.
Shabelle zone is one of the zones highly affected by drought.
OWDA under this project is applying integrated education, child protection and GBV activities Programmatic synergy of Protection and Education is aimed at improving desired outcome of protection and Education.
Project will focus strengthening the capacity of education institutions, education personnel and communities to provide crisis-sensitive education for emergency-affected girls, boys, including children with disabilities, provision of quality relevant and age appropriate education (formal and non- formal) for crisis-affected girls, boys, including children with disabilities and ensure equitable access to safe, protective and conducive (pre-primary and primary) learning environments for crisis-affected girls, boys including Children with disabilities. Empower and strengthen the community's especially women and girls' strategies to mitigate and cope with GBV risks, enhance access to multi-sectoral life-saving interventions for GBV survivors as well as vulnerable women and girls. Improve the resilience, capacity and well-being of girls and boys through age-and gender-appropriate community-based child protection reintegration services and ensure that children, adolescents and their communities benefit from quality, programmes promoting community-based and sustainable solutions finally project will focus also to reduce the vulnerability of girls and boys in the contexts of conflict and other natural hazards, through provision of lifesaving child protection services, while ensuring services availability to at risk displaced and non-displaced girls and boys, and caregivers and raise awareness and build the capacity of service providers, including duty bearers, community members and humanitarian actors, in order to identify and support the most vulnerable, prevent unintended harm and ensure safe, inclusive and accountable service delivery.
The project will be targeting of drought IDPs living in the Gode, Adadle and Denan woreda of the Shebelle zone in the Somali Region of Ethiopia. The project will provide access to educational and protection services to 1850 children affected and 13107 (women, girls, boys and men) respectively. Project will cost estimated project budget of 396,000 USD for period of 9 months.

1. - Projects activities

The Project aim to address one of the core challenges in education and protection services face by the people affected by the droughts. Project will provide access to education for children of 1850 children. At same time project will support children and women at risk by providing protection assistance like case management, establishing referral pathways, mental health and psycho-social support and awareness to community on the prevention, response and reporting abuse and exploitations happening at target areas. Project will implement the above mentioned tasks by conducting the following activities:Education projects: The key activities for the education project is to provide equitable to quality basic education to:1. about 16 semi-permeant temporary learning spaces classes rooms will be built to provide basic education access to 1850 out of school 2. Provide 350 combined desks 3. 100 Facilitators will be trained in Basic Pedagogy 4. Learning materials will be provided for 1850 students 5. Conduct community-based gender-sensitive and inclusive enrolment campaigns in the targeted woreda6. Provide training to 100 teachers to deliver an inclusive and gender-sensitive literacy program on the target woredaProtection 1. Training of 20 people on child protection case management 2. Provision of Non-food Items based on the needs of children identified as critical cases in the project locations3. Recruitment of Social workers 4. Strengthening child protection and gender-based violence community committees on child protection, cases identification, and referral through mentoring and capacity-building support.5. Provide awareness to 13,107 on CP and GBV 6. About 150 GBV survivors and vulnerable women and girls reached with core GBV response services including case management, PSS, legal aid and referrals. 7. Provide 1500 women and girls provided with dignity kits 6. Provide MHPSS to 2500 girls, boys, and women7. Provide awareness 13,107 on PSEA 8. Establish PSEA reporting mechanisms
