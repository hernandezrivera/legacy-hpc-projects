Project Summary

On the 11th year of conflict, the scale & diversity of needs, overall PiN in Dayr Hafir 19,350, which is complemented by figures presenting specific WASH needs related to water quality 3.3, quantity 1.4, sanitation 3.2, solid waste management 4.1, and heavy financial burden linked with purchasing water 7.5 or hygiene supplies 6.4 million people [HNO 2021]. Rescate is currently implementing a project of public market in Dayr Hafir which is under GoS since 2017. The security prevails and there are no access restrictions. Rescate aim is to provide integrated service in the town while safeguarding people's health. Severity scale is 3 and the current sewerage and sanitation systems in the targeted area are non-functional and flooding to the streets, putting populations at risk for epidemic-prone diseases HENCE need arises for lifesaving sanitation intervention. The situation in this neighborhoods is that sewage is flooding into the streets, hence vehicles and public transportation are eventually carrying the residues to outside the district which is spreading sickness and diseases to others. population in the target areas come from all walks of life, employees, students of all ages, workers, etc. who are prone to sanitation born diseases.
The main objective of this intervention is support sanitation management systems to ensure regular services for affected people in South & North districts in Dayr Hafir (160 and 116 hectares) inhabited by 5700 individuals including 600 elderlies, men 1110, women 1240, children and adolescents 2750 (boys 1450, Girls 1300), and PWDs 32 . The network rehabilitation is an urgent lifesaving need to ensure safe disposal & waste management. Lines of 2,210 ML are broken due to destruction to the conflict between 2012-2017 upon which people returned to the area and lack of maintenance. Replacement of the broken pipes will start with detailed inspection then cleaning the tunnels & re-cover the pipes per the approved technical standards. This will save local community & HHs returnees while the sanitation was not functioning from serious health risks.
Access to safe sanitation system is compromised based on the fact of lack of maintenance & rehabilitation of the networks which are not functioning due to un-addressed damages, consequently increasing the likelihood of waterborne diseases spreading due to poor sanitation & limited ability to practice good hygiene. The project impact with improved sanitation infrastructure & will support Covid-19 response as locality hygiene is a main requirement to ensure pandemic & contagion control. As Rescate is working on the public market project in Dayr Hafir, the main goal is to provide integrated response to help in building resilience & creating sense of stability for the returnees & population in the town. Rescate will deliver the services of pipelines rehabilitation through reinforcement of available service providers related to WASH sector. A candid review of the rapid technical assessment to ensure detailed BOQ inclusion in the tender call for the works. Rescate intervention in response to 3 severity score city (SYRIA 2021 HNO). This project will enhance beneficiaries’ resilience by enabling them to direct their resources to more tangible needs & to safeguard their health & hygiene under the current deteriorated health pandemic especially Covid-19. Rescate will enter agreement with experienced local contractors to provide works under close supervision of Rescate experience team. A claim mechanism will be introduced M&E officer will monitor implementation & prepare reports about progress, maintain track of works vs budget & action plan, & about beneficiaries’ segregation (gender, age & PWDs) & social impact. A questionnaire to assess beneficiaries’ satisfaction during & after the project implementation. Rescate maintains a schedule for financial & narrative, share info via WASH Sector.

4 - Needs

Rescate conducted field visits to the targeted areas in April 2021, including interviews with the targeted area municipality & meetings with residents to understand the actual environmental status and its impact on the returnees population who form 14% of the total residents 32,250 HNO 2021.Site inspection was conducted by Rescate with city council member. The North district is 160 Hectare 2500 Individual, South is 116 Hectare 3200 Individual. The sewage network in the two targeted districts were damaged to the conflict between 2012 and 2017 upon returns, no maintenance carried out coupled with lack of water. The networks need these works: replacement and expansion 6416 ML add 120 manholes with accessories, and switch pipes.Sewage residues flood and spread in the streets, which leads to high risk on environment and population health.Students living in these districts suffer from the difficulty of accessing schools in a safe and clean condition.Vehicles and transportation are carrying pollution from these districts to the neighboring areas.The negative effects resulting from the damage to the two networks constitute an obstacle for the City Council in their efforts to clean the city in order to limit the spread of epidemics, including Corona.The implementation of such a project increases job opportunities in the city, it bears job opportunities to workers.

5 - Implementation of activities

This project is a lifesaving intervention due to the high risks on population health and environment. Rescate had conducted the site assessment in full coordination with the local stakeholders. Activities are as follows: Coordination with the City Council and municipality for the project launch, coordination to receive approvals for project initiation, conduct field assessment for detailed BOQ,Technical review of the BOQs, with specialist team and equipment to finalize the tender call for contractors to bid for works,Process procurement to award contract with local contractor to implement the works,WASH works: soil excavation, installation of pipelines, rain sink, and hole tunnel coverage. Replacement and maintenance & rehabilitation of the whole network, Tests of the new network operation, sewer jet to ensure lack of blockages, clean tunnel, re-cover and deliver project works.Final receipt of works by the city council.Final evaluation survey to at least 15% of the beneficiariesMonitoring and Evaluation: final receipt reports, field visits and reporting to Donor and WASH sector.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Safety and dignity will be enforced and transversal during all the activities of the project through the principles of Do No Harm as follows: Paying attention to gender differences: Rescate and ST will ensure that women are represented in the Local Committee to ensure that their rights, needs, and views are well taken into consideration. Following up and monitoring: Rescate will first do a conflict analysis at the beginning of the intervention, to learn better about the context, and connectors and dividers in the targeted community. During the project implementation, Rescate staff will perform interviews with beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries to ensure that tensions are not rising in any way as an outcome of the project. A satisfaction survey will be held at the end of the intervention to assess the final valuation of the beneficiaries toward the rehabilitation works. The results will be shared with the donor. Facilitating a community-owned, managed and run project: Rescate will constantly inform to the beneficiaries and stakeholders about the advancement of the activities. Beneficiaries and stakeholders will have a voice in the project through complain mechanisms held in place: via email directly to Rescate Head of Mission, and through the committee meetings. Local cultural practices will be respected at all times Rescate will have a conflict sensitivity, first understanding the context where we will be intervening and identifying the relations between the different groups that will be targeting. Rescate will act upon understanding these interactions in order to avoid negative impacts.Rescate team & contractor ensure abidance to “Do No Harm” principal during works implementation.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

For operational, one risk is raising tensions with untargeted potential beneficiaries. In mitigation, Rescate will work in strong coordination with the City Council, municipality, LNC. Rescate had performed 1) conflict analysis before planning the response, 2) ensure the comprehensive need analysis is conducted with local stakeholders & LNC is documented & made transparent, 3) Ensure the areas in similar situation of need, at least within the geographical areas of response, are reached equally in future activities, 4) Efficiently deliver on the obligation of AAP, by keeping the City Council, municipality, LNC regularly informed of the project status, 5) Ensure a process of receiving complaints & of responses to complaints is put in place, 6) Have in reserve a strategy in case the planned response does unexpectedly create tension e.g. add-on of other activities or change in activities, in consultation with concerned stakeholders & LNC.Coordination with the LNC & stakeholders to inform on the reasons of the area selected. Rescate will keep formal & informal complain channels open at all times in case tension arises. Covid-19 is another potential risk that could disrupt the activities. The risk level of this to happen, & its consequences, are difficult to calculate beforehand. Rescate will take all the possible prevention measures from the start. In terms of financial risks, there is a high risk that the SYP devaluation continue, this would have grave consequences for the project. As mitigation, Rescate will start with the tendering procedure earliest possible, obtained approval to exchange inward fund in two halves, to overcome such obstacles. Risk of contamination of UXOs, City Council confirmed site clear.
