Project Summary

The project is a continuation of a series of projects funded by MTI, WHO and SCHF (through SRD and RF) in 2019. The objective of this project is to sustain access to primary healthcare services to the populations of Termanin, Sarmada, and Atareb in northern Syria through the following 3 facilities:
- Sarmada PHC (Sarmada PHC Center!G0703011)
- Termanin PHC (Tarmanin PHC Center!G0703028)
- Atareb PHC (Atareb PHC Center!B0200001)
The objective is to provide an adequate health response to the waves of IDPs arriving in northern Idleb, support the referral network and implement the essential components of the standard package of primary health care services, according to the package adopted by the health cluster in Gaziantep.
Under this project UDER proposes to continue addressing the critical needs for referral network and primary healthcare in northern Syria, by supporting the same facilities for 1 year. UDER will continue providing the health facilities with running costs, essential salaries, emergency maintenance costs and referrals for the period from 1 January 2020 to 31 December 2020. With this support UDER will ensure that the health facilities will continue to operate, staff will be retained, referrals will be followed and health services will continue to be provided to people in need in northern Syria. This is necessary to provide essential life-sustaining and life-saving primary healthcare services.
All the proposed 3 health facilities are referral centers for more than 100 community health workers providing services for the population of their communities. The PHCs are essential for ensuring effective community health services and for functional referral systems. Identification of cases that need further medical care starts at the community level, then the health facilities provide the necessary health services, which makes community health services and facility-based primary health care services complementary to each other. On a monthly basis, the community health workers refer the following to the 3 PHCs:
• 225 pregnant women for antenatal care consultations;
• 550 children-under-5 for vaccinations;
• 80 children-under-5 and 40 pregnant women for nutrition services;
• 130 patients for hypertension care
• 70 patients for diabetes care

4 - Needs

According to REACH, HNAP and CCCM, between 1 Dec 2019 and 2 Feb 2020, over 560,000 IDPs moved from southern and eastern Idleb towards Turkish boarders, where Dana, Azaz, Afrin and Atareb sub-districts are among the first intended areas for IDP influxes. Over 80% of IDPs are women and children.According to the humanitarian Access Team, the top 1 IDP arrival subdistrict in December 2019 was Dana, where Sarmada and Termanin PHCs managed by UDER are located. The 2nd top IDP arrival subdistrict was Atareb, where Atareb PHC managed by UDER is located.Sustained access to primary healthcare services for these people in need remains a major challenge. The health status of the population in northern Syria has been impacted by this lack of access to primary healthcare services. Health centres in northern Syria have been overwhelmed by the mass influx of IDPs and increase in population, in addition to the attacks on these health facilities. From January to September 2019, 65 attacks on health facilities reported, which resulted in killing 51 and injuring 84.Based on the above, UDER will ensure that the health facilities will continue to operate, staff will be retained, and health services will continue to be provided to people in need in northern Syria. This is necessary to provide essential life-sustaining and life-saving primary healthcare services to the increasing numbers of new IDPs in Idleb and Aleppo.

5 - Implementation of activities

UDER will provide primary healthcare consultations to the beneficiaries in Atareb, Sarmada, and Termanin. The staff and the essential infrastructure is already available in the PHC centers. UDER will pay for staff salaries and running costs for the three PHCs and provide technical support to the medical and non-medical staff to deliver essential primary healthcare services, manage the facility and ensure record-keeping and data collection for the supported PHCs. UDER's staff in Turkey will supervise and coordinate with the facility management and other actors to ensure that the services are provided at the best available quality. UDER will procure the necessary medicine and medical supplies according to the needs of the health facilities. UDER will also keep receiving donations from WHO and other partners and this amount will be combined with the procured medicine to meet the needs of the health facilities. UDER will monitor the services provided at the health facilities by conducting planned and spot-check field visits for quality and technical control of the delivery of services The Field Coordinator is responsible for collecting financial documentations and medical data on a monthly basis and will monitor the distribution of salaries to staff. He will do physical checks for the pharmacy and meet with beneficiaries to collect qualitative and quantitative feedback and receive any beneficiary complaints through complaint boxes and face to face interviews. The health-related data in addition to program reports and financial reports will be reported to donors on monthly basis. Additional analysis related to the health services will be provided to donors and to stakeholders to inform the decisions related to improving the quality of services in northern Syria.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. There is a risk of discrimination and inequality in providing services to particular groups or individuals. The probability: moderate; the risk severity: high. 2. The risk of harassment and sexual abuses during consultation. The probability: moderate; the risk severity: high. 3. There is risk for vulnerable (pregnant women, singles women and PWD) groups to access to the PHCs and benefit from Services. The probability: moderate; the risk severity: high. The mitigation action: - UDER Field coordinators and MEAL officer will conduct spot-check visits to the PHC. - Exit interview with beneficiaries - Enhance a confidential complaint mechanism including hot line and ethic point website for maintain anonymous complaints. - Conduct training for staff on PSEA and code of Conduct. - Sign PSEA, child protection and code of conduct policies and includes in the contract a very strict termination and dismissal action towards any misconduct Improve the PHC structure to be disabilities friendly place. - Activate the ambulance system for vulnerable people or ensure transportation. - Link the CH with PHCs for referral cases. - Train PHCs staff how to deal with PWD. UDER will monitor this risk by: - Monitoring and Evaluation surveys will be conducted by MEAL department including the provided services and staff treatment including PSEA questions - Share services map for MHPSS and GBV support centers - Recruit an agency to make the facility accessible or PWD and pregnant women.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: - Air strikes and military operations do not affect project progress in the operational area. - Access to project locations and community acceptance is maintained. - Sources for medical and non-medical supplies remain available. - Qualified staff continue to operate in the project location. Risk mitigation - Monitoring of activities is limited due to conflict-related access limitations. UDER will rely on local field-based staff to conduct monitoring activities. - Airstrikes closely or directly affecting the health facilities causing damage to the facilities or impacts on facility staff and patients. UDER will develop suspension, relocation and hibernation plans for each facility to limit the impact of air-strikes. Local alerts about potential threats will be monitored and the facility staff and patients will be advised to avoid the facilities during high-risk periods. - Active conflict in Idleb restricting staff and patient movement to and from the facilities. UDER will closely monitor the conflict dynamics in the area of operation and adjust the project activities according to the analysis of these dynamics.
