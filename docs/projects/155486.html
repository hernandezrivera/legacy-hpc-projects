Project Summary

The objective of the intervention is to provide treatment for severe acute malnutrition and contribute to the reduction of undernutrition amongst children under five in the North, Adamawa and East.
The project is articulated around four key results areas:
• Treatment and care for children with severe acute malnutrition (SAM)
• Infant and Young Child Nutrition
• Home based food fortification
• Data collection, knowledge generation, monitoring and evaluation

Needs

In the regions affected by the CAR crisis (East, Adamaoua and North), there are more than 166,300 people, including 51,400 Central African refugees, mainly children under 5 and pregnant or lactating women (FEFA), who will be vulnerable. At a minimum, 85,800 children under 5 years of age will suffer from Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) among which 26,400 girls and boys under 5 years of age affected by Severe acute malnutrition.Children under 5 years old affected by severe acute malnutrition are nine times more likely to die than their healthy peers. Women and young children are particularly vulnerable to malnutrition often exacerbated by poor living conditions and the accumulation of traumas. These can explain major dysfunction in the diet of infants and young children, especially in the ability of mothers to ensure optimal breastfeeding.It is essential for the most vulnerable populations to maintain and strengthen their access to quality case management, especially when the program is integrated into a health system that is already very weak.
