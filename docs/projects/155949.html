Project Summary

This project aims to spread the dissemination of the principles of protection in the camps area in the north of Syria , with a total number of direct beneficiaries 840 people , psychosocial support services will be provided to 300 children aged 6 to 12, including 40 children with special needs, in addition to provide children with referral pathways , these services will be provided to the children by 20 members of the organization team of specialists and experts who will receive specialized training on this subject , The project will also train 300 children (50% females) and 100 adolescents (30% female) in life skills (negotiation skills, team building and social responsibility development). Life skills clubs will be formed of children and adolescents. Community campaigns will be launched in the camps area. These clubs will include children and adolescents with special needs to support their integration with their peers and to stimulate their psychological positive factors , In addition, the project will target 60 persons of students parents from the camps, where training will be held on the principles of child protection and treatment of domestic violence, and other training for 60 camp managers and humanitarian teams on the principles of protection and humanitarian work. Two community committees will be formed of parents and camp managers and humanitarian teams that will raise awareness in the community ,
The training and activities will take place within the Zdeni Elman School in Act and Impact camp and will target the residents of the following camps (Sons of Homs (Abna Homs) , Qadisiya, Reef Hama Mankouba , Al Nour, Kura Mankouba ,Act and Impact)
There will be a simple maintenance of the place, providing the school with fire extinguishers and first aid. and putting wall stickers "with graphics that suit the ages, sex, wall stickers , including insulation of a child-friendly space with a shock-absorbing mat for the floor (rubber)
At the beginning of the project, activity will be held to introduce the project to people and distribute motivational gifts to the children to motivate them to participate. This activity will be repeated at the end of the project and gifts will be distributed to children and adolescents clubs that contributed to community campaigns.
Indirect beneficiaries
The six camp residents are indirect beneficiaries. awareness campaigns will be launched by the project to raise awareness in these camps. The number of residents of these camps is estimated at 4504 persons

Needs

Need Assessment As mentioned in the summary of the project, it aims to raise awareness about the issues of violence experienced by children. Comments and discussions within the protection sector have revealed the weakness of protection services in camps and besieged areas, lack of training and awareness raising campaigns in societyWe have chosen six camps suffering from this problem. (Sons of Homs (Abna Homs) , Qadisiya, Reef Hama Mankouba , Al Nour, Kura Mankouba ,Act and Impact) We have counted about 5,500 people, including 2879children and adolescents, who need to increase their knowledge about their basic rights, as well as raising awareness about their issues by their parents and camp managers to end domestic violence and to disseminate the principles of protectionThe need assessment showed that these children in this camps came from a war and violent environment. They needed treatment sessions and psycho social support so that they could get rid of the trauma they experienced. They could reintegrate into the community and overcome the bad psychological state they experienced.In addition, the evaluation showed that the parents of children need guidance and awareness sessions on how to deal with their children who were out of this violent environment and how to deal with their reactions caused by the psychological state they were livingAt the beginning of the project, we will meet with the residents and clarify the purpose and purpose of the project to motivate them and their children to participate

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption1: The camp's residents are open and accept this kind of projects. Risk A: lack of participation of women and children in the project's activities. Mitigation strategy: INSURYA will conduct an awareness campaign to community mobilization about the project purposes and importance of participation in its activities and reassurance to the civil community that the project is worthwhile and is something that would benefit and not harm their community. Assumption 2: The security situation is stable and IDPs in camps will not move to another place. Risk A: change of the security situation "air strike -clashes - armed attack"Mitigation strategy: according to the political agreements " Astana talks" the project area is a de-escalation zone. Risk B: change the security situation.Mitigation strategy: if there is a change in the security situation in the project's area we will go to plan B after discussing with the donor.
