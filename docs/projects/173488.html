Project Summary

Through this project SHPDO aims to contribute to the efforts to prevent and respond to GBV in three states ND, BN and WK. The overall target of the project is 21970 women, girls, men and boys. The activities include: 1\establishing and supporting women Centers where women will be supported by IGAs and will be able to access recreational activities, life skills activities, individual and group counselling, (PFA) and skills. And all skills building/vocational activities should attempt to avoid reinforcing existing gender stereotypes by offering activities on a variety of skills. Skills building/vocational activities should be undertaken within the framework of psychosocial support interventions for women and girls (and within a GBV Sub Sector framework); 2) Establishing CBPNs supported 4)awareness-raising and sensitization on GBV using various methods including targeted activities like workshops and discussion or campaigns utilizing TV/radio, theatre, engaging men and boys in preventing GBV. The topics of such awareness-raising activities may include different topics on GBV, domestic violence, child marriage etc. SHPDO will also foscus on meeting the urgent needs of women and girls through the distribution of dignity kits and the distribution is coupled with sharing information on GBV, available GBV services, GBV helplines numbers, and messages on PSEA. Dignity kits distribution is entry point to raising awareness and reaching women and girls.
