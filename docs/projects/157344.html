Project Summary

The project aims to establish 6 women centers/clubs to conduct workshops, events, lectures etc. to promote women empowerment and protection and raise awareness on GBV . The centres/clubs will be established in the localities of El Sireaf, Saraf Omra & Kutum in North Darfur, and Kulbus, Jebel Moon and Sirba in West Darfur. With a total PiN of 75461.
Through the established centers/clubs, SAO will aim to achieve multiple objectives through multiple activities like delivering of workshops, involving community leaders, training of 90 Women to be able to provide peer training to ensure sustainable outputs. the project will perform detailed assessments and documentation to Facilitate later interventions.

Needs

Targeted six localities of El Sireaf, Saraf Omra and Kutum in North Darfur, and Jebel Moon, Kulbus and Sirba in West Darfur are among most emergency places which had witnessed a severe GBV and human rights violations especially on women and girls. With a total PiN of 75461 for the six localities, The sector’s HRP matrix has a ‘5’ vulnerability indicator in all three localities in North Darfur, and '4' vulnerability indicator for all three West Darfur localities. GBV in these communities is rarely reported due to social stigma and fear of punishment from conflicted parties. Unfortunately, GBV and female abuse became a weapon or a punishment tool in the conflicted areas to humiliate & terrorize villagers to prevent them from getting involved or provide support to the other enemy party. As a result of these factors and many other factors an intervention to abandon GBV must be conducted. As a result of GBV in Darfur, women & girls are affected as follows:- Women became head of households & became responsible for families financial & care at a very young age. - Women in affected areas became refugees or displaced. - Women lose their basic sources of income. - Women in some cases were subject to killing, forced abortion, fear & terror. - Women in few cases were subject to slavery & trafficking. - Rape of women & girls is used in some cases as a punishment to the enemy.

Activities

The project will perform the following activities:- 1/ Establishment of a women club/center in each locality. 2/ Provide intensive training to 90 women on advocacy, problem solving, leadership and other subjects. These groups will be present throughout the projects duration, and will participate in the implementation of the activities, and will be prepared to become peer educators to achieve sustainable outcomes.3/ Identification of self-income generating projects for affected women & girls and provide guidance, training on small-business management and facilitate access to their project's financing. 4/ Prepare every center with equipment and trained personnel to provide physical and psychological health support for victims of GBV. 3/ Advocate for Capacity Development and Institutional Strengthening: This will be executed in relation to local judiciary system, legal procedures, rural court, judges and community leaders. The methodology is to tackle this issue through training of trainers and direct training to related parties, introduction of documentation culture and support electronic information system and tracking process at the women centers. 4/ Delivering of workshops, dialogues and involving of effective people like community leaders and Hakamas to ensure the inclusion of beneficiaries and stakeholders. 5/ Provision of legal advice and support to victims of GBV.6/ Raising awareness among women & girls about their rights and encourage them to speak out without fear or feel of shame through campaigns, lectures and workshops.7/ General detailed assessment and documentation to Facilitate future interventions.
