Project Summary

MWL proposed project seeks to provide protective education environment opportunities to vulnerable children (boys and girls) n four sub-districts: (Dana, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin). that selected as most priority to ensure expanding ongoing education services by availing inclusive protective learning spaces to ensure that no children absent or drop out of schools' due to lack of learning spaces, learning and teaching materials, economic capacity of the Education Directorate and the parents to cover all the schools & Student’s needs.
MWL proposed project aims to contribute in supporting 15000 (B:9000, G: 6000) aged 5-17 years, & 5000 (M:3000, F:2000) aged 15-24 years, including 60 CWDs and 300 (M:190, F:110) aged 25-59 to get access to Education (formal and non-formal) and raising the quality of education in-line with EiE standards and inclusion Children with Disability through rehabilitation and equip schools, provision transportation and provision PSS, awareness raising and inclusion activities. This project has no secured fund by any other partners.
That will be achieved through;
1)Rehabilitation for classrooms in targeted schools to raise their capacity to absorb more IDP students
2) providing Schools' furniture / Students' seats to enhance the capacity of schools;
3) Training the targeted teachers and administrative staff on CP core, PSS, classroom management, modern teaching methods;
4) Provision stationary, teaching & learning materials, textbooks and Student's kit;
5) Using the interim government curricula in formal education and curriculum B for non formal education in the two semesters during the school year, and Providing literacy and numeracy skills, remedial & catch-up classes for non-formal program
6) Conducting ASER to assess learning;
7) Establishing 2nd shift to providing formal / non-formal education in the school to raise its capacity to absorb new IDPs Boys and Girls;
8) Establishing CFSs inside schools in line with INEEE & CPMS that will provide structured PSS, sensitization that target students, caregiver, parents, community key members and mobile teams will conduct BTLs, awareness raising sessions on ERW (Explosive Remnant War)
9) providing incentives for teachers and administrative staff
10) Conduct winterization campaign through distributing of Heating fuel and heaters the through Voucher Scheme to ensure the safety and security for the schools, prevent the fraud & corruption in procurement process
11) Establishing PTAs to follow up with student’s education improvement and issues;
The M&E Coordinator will be responsible to oversight the complaint mechanism where beneficiaries will be able to provide feedback or complaint to ensure satisfied of beneficiaries Evaluation of the project will be conducted by the M&E staff throughout all the project
duration that will started by conducting “base-line survey”, monitoring and compares the progress development and conduct an “end-line survey” to measure the achievements of intervention outputs. A lesson learning event will be conducted at the end of the project life cycle to draw some lessons and best practices from the project.

4 - Needs

The proposed project was built based on the needs of affected persons in the northwest of Syria., MWL has targeted the locations with the most needs & highest severity based on the ASO1. Based on the HNO report 2021, in the Education Cluster, on level of sub-district. The severity scale in targeted location is very high. Those 4 sub-districts have been visited to conduct the need assessment in terms of vulnerabilities of children and their families, technical needs, security situation, complementary and GAM considerations. MWL team conducted needs assessment using KII & Filling Questionnaire focusing on Educational and Protection needs. The assessment has been conducted in coordination with Education Directorates, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. FGDs were arranged with different group of population to get wide range of suggestion to respond accordingly. the total number of FGDs is 25 of 138 (M:58, F: 77, PWD: 3). In Afrin 3 communities (Trinda, Qarzihel & Afrin city), severity is 4.30 and school-age children in need is 17264 (B:7754,G:9510). In Dana 5 communities (Sarmada, Burdaqly, Hezreh – Hezri, Qah, & Dana), severity is 5 and school-age children in need is 20059 (B:9465,G:10594), while teachers in need is 8281 (M:4027, W:4254)In Maaret Tamsrin children in need is 27015 (B:13272,G:13743).According to the information, in targeted sub-district schools absent due to harsh cold weather last year, with inability to provide fuel for heating for schools, schools’ administration forced to return back the children to their homes daily due to Severe cold, that’s leads the Education directorate concern from additional dropping out of schools Some deaths due to ERW-related causes were also reported.

5 - Implementation of activities

Based on a broad and deep understanding of needs and barriers to access to quality education for children in the target areas, MWL designed project activities. The project will implement the following activities: MWL will provide schools with formal and non- formal education activities (ALP and remedial), this will contribute in support the efforts to return the children into formal education, • Also, teachers in the target schools will be provided with incentives. • Teachers will be trained on interactive non-formal education programs. • MWL will Conduct BTLs campaigns • Project will work on rehabilitation the school’s infrastructure, equip Wash facilities and special needs bathrooms, with gender-sensitive approach 2- PSS services 3- Protection activities: identify and refer children with CP issues to appropriate interventions, as well as contributing to protect students and teachers from risks of fire by providing schools with fire extinguishers and putting wall stickers in schools for fire-safety and instruction manuals for using the extinguishers, in addition to providing schools with first aid kits, - Project will enhance the resilience, awareness and capacity of children and caregivers to prevent and respond to violations against children and their rights. Programmatic reporting provided from team leader on field directly to Program Coordinator & Project Manager. MWL is aware of importance of Accountability to Affected People can improve the intervention. Workshop and meeting with community leaders and key community members will be implemented throughout the assessment,Focus group discuss FGDs will be voluntarily conducted after getting the consent including school management, parents,and the students (CWD).

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

MWL committed to mainstreaming protection throughout its programming. The key to ensure the dignity and voice of the intended beneficiaries is the consistent consultation throughout project implementation.Improve access to quality and inclusive education and protection for children with disabilities is one of priorities of this project, where the target schools, and wash facilities will be friendly and accessible for CWDs, and they will be provided with ramps, special seats for those children. A holistic approach will be adopted to ensure that CWDs are effectively enrolled aa activities through awareness campaigns target the community and parents, and by advocacy to providing the children with hearing devices and wheelchairs to ensure their attendance at school. Transportation for CWDs will be provided to and from school to encourage and facilitate their access to education.MWL will ensure that staff from displaced and host community in recruitment process, MWL will encourage the recruitment of PWD in project teams. The trainers of the workshops will be women and men.PWD will have access to complaint mechanism thorough FGDs, MWL will consider and ensure gender-and age aspects throughout the project cycle.During the assessment, MWL will collect gender-and sex-disaggregated data. MWL will consider the safety of children & teachers as priority through distributing ( Safty and security kits).Furthermore, protection mainstreaming measures will be monitored to ensure protection principles are integrated in the project activities. MWL will conduct awareness sessions on PSEA. In addition, MWL staff will sign HLG code of conduct,PSEA and child safeguarding policies
