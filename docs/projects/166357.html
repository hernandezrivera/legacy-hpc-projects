Project Summary

This project is designed to address the critical WASH need for the people affected by humanitarian emergencies. The project will provide access to safe and sufficient water, sanitation and hygiene. The project will is targeting IDPs and host communities who are at risk of Covid-19 pandemic. The project will address the current Covid-19 pandemic issue regarding the WASH facilities in the targeted locations as well through strengthening the existing effort, particularly in awareness raising, hand washing practicing, social distancing. The project will use the WASH sector strategies and guidelines in all stages of implementation.

1 - Needs

As the Covid-19 pandemic continues to spread across the globe and Nigeria, Particularly northeastern part of the country, It is very critical for the affected people to gain access to basic WASH services, Taimako-CDI as a key partner in front line responder organization has also identified gaps within its operation areas, water facilities are still needed to satisfy the needs of communities . The majority of Latrines and bathing facilities remain communal and different feedback from the communities where the voice of women loudly demanded more privacy. At the same time, the functionality rate needs improvement besides the rationalization of quality and quantity. Hygiene promotion (Covid-19 virus prevention): it is essential to shift the hygiene promotion from message dropping to such kind of interactive approaches with communities thus the newly adopted strategies used to improve and change the behaviors showed good results, while the MHM remains a crucial area to continue to target women in reproductive health and adolescent girls. The hygiene kits distribution and monitoring the efficiency of utilization of these items is one of the gaps which need to be focused and addressed. The community WASH committee and their active involvement in the WASH program, also one of the critical areas, need to be continued with close consultation with the government and other stakeholders to adopt the participation and increase the participation level as well. The integration of the WASH program with other lifesaving sectors is crucial and necessary to quickly get the outputs(AWD, Safeguarding, WASH in schools and social inclusiveness...etc.) that support the humanitarian response. Therefore health/nutrition, protection, Education, and site management will be the critical sector WASH will work with to ensure the joint efforts are combined during the response and at different project cycle phases. The Targeted area used to face several types of emergencies such as cyclone, monsoon, and AWD, and other water-related outbreaks that need excellent preparedness and response plans. On the contrary, the first case of COVID-19 was identified in Nigeria about three months after the HRP finalized for this year in March. Even worldwide, COVID-19 took its pandemic size in March 2020. So, as the situation in the Northeast Nigeria is quite congested, and the risk of infection could be high unless special attention with critical interventions put in place. To limit the spreading of this disease, WASH partners intervened actively with measures that contribute in the prevention of this virus spreading such as social distancing, hand washing, and improvement of general hygiene and awareness campaigns using house to house messaging, while soap distribution to the entire community to support the hand washing initiatives remain a crucial intervention.

2 - Activities

To address these critical challenges, Taimako-CDI will implement the following activities in consideration of the Covid-19 WASH Technical guidelines made available by the sector, the project will prioritize Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) programming.Activity 1:- Awareness Raising: Taimako-CDI will raise awareness on Covid-19 and general Hygiene in the target locations. This activity will be conducted through design of communication materials, with key Covid-19 prevention and Hygiene messages including printing of banners, posters, airing of audio and video jingles and conduct awareness campaign to inform the affected populations and activate community networks as a key community mobilizers. Also MHM will surely be integrated. Activity 2:- Construction and Rehabilitation of water points: Taimako-CDI will provide access to safe and quality water to the vulnerable population who are in risk of Covid-19 pandemic. This activity will be conducted through construction of new solar powered borehole, rehabilitation of existing non-functioning boreholes. Activity 2.1:- Water Trucking: Taimako-CDI during lock-down, will provide access to portable, safe and quality water through water trucking in the targeted locations. Activity 3. WASH IPC programming: Installments of hand washing points in strategic places, such as healthcare facilities, markets schools, Taimako-CDI will establish hand washing points with alcohol based hand sanitizer, to mitigate the risk of Covid-19 virus spread.Activity 4: Distribution of WASH NFIs and PPEs : The project will distribute kits depending on the need and history of distribution. This also includes distribution of Covid-19 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) to the affected population.Activity 5: Construction and rehabilitation of latrines

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

For all projects, Taimako-CDI foresees an interim monitoring system, which includes verification on the achievements and on the status of the activities’ implementation every three months. Keeping these deadlines in mind, the accountability time frame must allow Taimako-CDI staff to collect and analyze information, and to disclose it to beneficiaries respecting the principle of “taking into account”. Another key point for a functional accountability system is the awareness of Taimako-CDI staff of the fact that the monitoring process is a continuum: since the planning phase, through all the implementation process, the gathering of information and feedback has to be continuous and carried out both in structured, specific moments, and in more informal contexts that can be found through the projects’ development. Regarding the opportunities and right times for collecting and sharing feedback, information and changes, Taimako-CDI staff have to be aware that the moments for doing this are not only formal and agreed (meetings, reunions with representatives, interviews, etc.), but can happen during other activities, even those that are not foreseen or implemented by the organization, but of which it has to be made the most.
