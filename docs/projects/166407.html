Project Summary

WV's overall Goal is to enhance livelihoods of 8,000 vulnerable host community HHs affected by COVID-19 in Cox's Bazar District, Bangladesh by December 2020. This will be achieved through provision of unconditional cash grants amounting to USD 66.7 per household per month, for 3 months, to meet target households’ food needs and other necessary basic expenses, and therefore support towards rebuilding their livelihoods. The project will intentionally target households with pregnant and lactation women, elderly, the widowed, persons living with disability and child-headed households. The project will also provide a one-time conditional cash grant amounting to USD 200 per target household, to boost the economic and income generating activities/small businesses. The project will ensure that at least 30% of the target beneficiaries will be women who engage in small businesses. In line with the Prime Minister’s directive on March 2020 on supporting food production activities as a priority during this lockdown, the project will procure and distribute assorted vegetable seeds, gardening tools and materials to 8,000 target households for nutritious and high-value vegetable production. Short technical orientations will be provided for the recipients before seeds and tools' distribution takes place. It is worth noting that orientations and distribution of seeds and tools will be done in coordination with, and seeking technical support from the Department of Agricultural Extension, Cox’s Bazar. The project anticipates to have 52% of the 8,000 targeted beneficiaries as women who will undergo nutrition education training covering on different types of food groups, food preparation based on a nutritious and balanced diet, sanitation and hygiene. In order to provide safety and protection to from line work force and beneficiaries, the project will procure and distribute protective materials (sanitizer, hand washing facilities, masks, gloves, etc). There will also be community-based sensitization on COVID-19 to 20,160 beneficiaries to ensure that they are aware of the preventive and protective measures of COVID-19.

Objectives

Objective 1: Expand support to improve food security and compensate for loss of livelihoods of the most vulnerable Bangladeshis in Cox’s Bazar whose livelihoods will be impacted by the pandemic, through food and cash support according to need. Objective 2: Ensure minimal disruptions in support of food production system, aligning with the government of Bangladesh department of agriculture extension policies and directives in order to ensure continuity of crop production and harvest throughout critical agricultural seasons.

Needs

The novel coronavirus (COVID-19) which emerged at the end of December 2019 has now spread globally, with high infection rates and huge economic impact. In order to mitigate the spread of the COVID-19 visurs, the Government of Bangladesh has issued ‘Stay at Home’ directives shutting down productive and commercial activities from 26 March through to16th May 2020 ( with possibility of extension), and international borders including trade routes, have been greatly restricted. The breakdown of food systems during the crisis could lead to years of decreased agricultural productivity and worsening of food and nutrition indicators among the host communities in Cox Bazar District. The district is one of the country’s poorest and most vulnerable, with a total Bangladeshi population of 2,650,000. Additionally, the administration predicts that more than 700,000 people in the district will be rendered immediately jobless due to the ‘Stay at Home’ directive. This loss of livelihoods, coupled with a decrease in access to the local market has left many poor people not being able to meet their basic needs and has resulted in extra support needs. Furthermore, the COVID-19 pandemic has not only resulted into disruption in food chain and other essential commodity supplies but also led to a reduction in HHs' income earnings and agricultural extension and private business services are on hold. Community are adopting neagtive copying strategies namely, selling off productive assets, skipping meals, including others.Provision of unconditional cash grants for the 1st three months to the host comunity's COVID-affected persons would help address basic safety net needs and speed the process of livelihood recovery. As per the Prime Minister and government's directives towards promotion of agricultural , livestock and fish production, the proposed intervention intends to provide conditional cash grants for the remaining five months to help boslter the loss of income/livelihoods among the 8,000 most vulnerable host community households. 30% of the cash grant will intentionally target women led business and IGA as one way of empowering women economic growth during this COVID-19 pandemic. The project will integrate complain response mechanisms including helpd desks, community feedback sessions to ensure proper accountability and transparent processes in all the project activities. Overall, the proposed intertention will provide short term to imtermediate supports towards rebuilding livelihoods and economic growth for the COVID-19 affected host communities.

Activities or outputs

Activity 1.1 : Conduct a rapid assessment to determine needs and those most affected among the host communities. Activity 1.2 : Identify and select 8,000 vulnerable host community households Activity 1.3 : Distribute unconditional cash support to 8,000 vulnerable host community households for a period of first 3 months Activity 1.4 : Distribute one time cash grant support to 8,000 vulnerable host community households for IGAs Activity 2.1 :Provide COVID-sensitive training on improved farming practices/techniques for 8,000 target beneficiaries (1 person from each of 8,000 targeted vulnerable host community households) Activity 2.2 :Procure and distribute assorted vegetable seeds and gardening tools & materials to 8,000 targeted host community households Activity 2.3 : Procure and distribute COVID-19 protective materials (sanitizers, handwashing facilities, mouth masks, gloves, etc) for 50 staff and 150 facilitators Activity 2.4 : Conduct community based awareness building/sensitization events on COVID-19 pandemic for 20,160 targeted host community households on COVID-19 pandemic Activity 2.5 : Conduct training on nutrition education for 4,160 vulnerable host community households
