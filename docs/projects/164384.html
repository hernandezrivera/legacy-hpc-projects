Project Summary

BRD will providing lending services for both men and women on favorable terms.
This project aims at firmly establishing work culture and principle of self-reliance in addition to providing job opportunities for people in need.
This project is directed towards providing an opportunity for those who have the skills, capabilities and willing to establish a private business.
BRD offer them liquid cash required to accelerate and promote their activities. This will help in creating new jobs and increase beneficiaries’ income.
BRD providing financial inclusion and lending services for 200 persons.
Returned installments that are paid by those persons will be given to other ones.
BRD aim to number of beneficiaries will reach 320 persons/borrower by the end of the year, using favorable loans system.
More than 76% og the Syrian citizens suffer from extreme poverty, 35% of them suffer from extreme poverty.
Each person, income ranging from 0 to 1.9 dollar daily as per definition of income line issued by world bank.
On the other hand, number of evacuees from south of Idleb have reached more than 520K (CCCM cluster) evacuee from DEC2019 to JAN 2020
Most of those evacuees are settled in north of Syria in Idlib, Afrin and Euphrates shield.
This project is offering means and tools of revenue generating. Moreover, those projects will emerge people from poverty, improving the quality of living and enhancing resilience.

4 - Needs

Many Syrian citizens strive to stay alive after their whole life was destroyed due to the conflict for nearly nine years (HNO) Millions of people are exhausted from adaptation strategy. Nearly 69% of citizens suffer from poverty and nearly 35% suffer from extreme poverty characterized by severe deprivation from foodstuff required for survival. The potential of having essential services stopped almost completely, including medical care, clean water and education. Some families resort to unsustainable and unsafe means to survive, including compulsory and/or early marriage, child labor and child soldiering to survive. On the other hand, increase of prices and decrease of currency value in 2019 obliged Syrian families to struggle to earn their living as well as the Unavailability of fuel for heating ....etc.Earn living opportunities decreased more than before; also loss of income affected millions of dependents. Nearly 85% of the Syrian citizens are below poverty line, while 69% of them suffer from poverty as 35% of them lives in extreme poverty. Those who suffer from extreme poverty are not able to afford essential needs for their families. It is likely that, family income may confront more pressure especially after the rise of fuel prices. This reality has created the need for operational solutions instead of temporary rescue solutions. Those solutions proved to be less effective over the long run, in addition to its negative psychological effects on the affected people desires to work and secure their life. Women, Men are willing to improve their skills to find new sources to secure their lives expenses, while no bodies supporting them in Afrin area to help the people establish their own business instead of providing them with services to fill the gap.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk High (Impact) (High) Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion; Timing and process of distribution of vouchers put women at higher risk of GBV. (Mitigating) Promote Establishment of adequate management plan; Promote involvement /engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback from different groups on package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among staff, whatsapp complains line, viber, email, brochures with how to use and communicate in case of any risk. Extortion Probability: Average Impact: High The employee responsible for the distribution of aid extorted women or children even men for personal needs before handing over aid or providing service to them and exploitation of the situation of disability and need. 1. Inform staff that this is contrary to regulations and laws and educate women and children in need to tell officials directly about any cases of extortion or harassment. 2. Use M & E and questionnaires mobilized by beneficiaries to control distribution and non-exploitation of those in need.There is a risk of discrimination if services are provided to specific groups or individuals and others are excluded. Probability: Average Impact: High Mitigation measures: 1. Ensure that there is a map showing the locations of the beneficiaries who are in real need of assistance so that communication with beneficiaries is proactive and based on knowledge. 2.
