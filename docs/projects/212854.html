Project Summary

According to HNO in NW Syria, humanitarian needs stem from conflict, economic crisis, water scarcity, and the ongoing drought. Security remains unstable, particularly in frontline areas. Displacement is driven by military operations and socio-economic conditions, with up to 400,000 people expected to be displaced. The return of refugees and IDPs adds complexity. Worsening economic conditions heighten vulnerabilities, with income insufficiency and a parallel economy posing risks. Protection needs persist due to gender and age vulnerabilities. Environmental shocks exacerbate water scarcity, with a severe drought impacting food security. Access to humanitarian aid is challenged by conflict dynamics. Public health risks, including waterborne diseases, strain the fragile health system. Overall, addressing these multifaceted challenges requires sustained humanitarian efforts and international cooperation.
ATAA will implement the project activities in Maaret tamsrin, Dana, Atareb, Daret Azza, Idleb, Atmeh, Albab, and Azaz communities targeting most vulnerable children and their caregivers with integrated protection services including CP, GBV, GP and MA services with a total target of 83986 BNFs.
The children will be targeted in schools or TLSs with MHPSS, life skills, awareness raising, case management services, in addition to targeting their caregivers with awareness raising and parenting skills. Also, women and adolescent girls will be targeted through Dignity kits distribution. Protection networks will be established / supported through engagement in project activities and capacity building opportunities in addition to training for protection workers and teachers. Outreach teams will conduct field visits to provide the awareness raising sessions to the BNFs on legal, CP and GP concerns.
Risks and mitigations:
Risk1: Conflict Escalation in Frontline Areas
Mitigation: Strengthen local peacebuilding initiatives and community reconciliation efforts to mitigate tensions and prevent escalation.
Risk2: Compounded Forms of Violence Against Women and Girls
Mitigation: Strengthen legal frameworks and institutions to ensure the protection of women's rights and enforce laws against gender-based violence (GBV).
Risk3: Limited Options for Durable Solutions
Mitigation: Advocate for the rights of IDPs and returnees to access durable solutions, including adequate housing, land, and property restitution, through legal advocacy and policy dialogue.
