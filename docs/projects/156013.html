Project Summary

This project designed to be implemented in five sub-regions: Atma, al-Madiq castle, A'azaz Salama and Ma'areen, Maarat al-Ikhwan camp, Darkush, al-Janoudiya, consists of five centers each center has an internal team and a mobile team to provide services for people Sub-areas outside the center.
Total beneficiaries: 31,330
Maram Foundation aims to combat the gender-based violence, which is a pervasive threat to women and girls in Syria through child marriage, domestic and sexual violence, and sexual exploitation.
The objective of the project is :
GBV specialized Services: case management, referral, counseling, PFA, PSS for survivors #of beneficiaries 1010 women, girls, men, and boys
Capacity building for project staff: # of beneficiaries: 50 women and men
Prevention, risk mitigation, and empowerment: for all communities # of beneficiaries: 30000 women, girls, men, and boys
Cash assistance (cases ): ( will be provided to survivors according to specific criteria after studying the case and referral pathways to services in far location )
Maram will move activities to the Euphrates shields and Afrin as Plan B if the region is at risk of attacks and instability commensurate with the movement of the beneficiaries.

Implementation of activities

Provide a safe space for women and girls.we will provide GBV services in three locations: Idleb: tow centers Atmeh and AL Janodiyeh, Hama, and Aleppo: Azaz and AL Salamah.so we have five centers: Each center has a team inside the center and a mobile team.The WGSS center will operate as UNFPA-defined women and girl safe spaces (WGSS) and have child-friendly safe spaces as defined by UNFPA guidelines, ensuring a full participatory approach of the female beneficiaries.-Provide specialized GBV services to survivors, including case management and individual psychosocial support. WGSS Center offer the provision of case management and PSS for GBV survivors, including PSS screening and 1-on-1 counseling, informing GBV survivors of resources, and case management.-Provide GBV empowerment and prevention activities: Which relate to the needs of women and girls" computer, English course and cooking"-Provide GBV programming for adolescent girlsWGSS Center offers recreational activities, including coffee meetings, weekly group support meetings, art groups, fun beautifying activities to build community support and trusted relationships.Boys, men, people with special needs and persons unable to reach through mobile teams will be targeted to provide awareness raising and PSS activities.The community will be targeted through capacity building activities "humanitarian workers within different sectors to ensure better quality and integration of services provided within safe areas.last year, we faced many cases related cash- services because there are many locations the services are not available to ensure effective referral, in addition to a large number of cases of sexual exploitation and harassment as a result of their inability To provide their needs so it provided cash service for a specific case.MF is committed, and has a strong track record, prioritizing the humanitarian aid guidelines and believes strongly in the “Do No Harm,” policy. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Security:Risk: Risks of targeting and attacking by aircraft to the center in Syria likelihood: Medium Impact: highMitigation Measures: Choosing center site will be according to risk analysis regarding the nature of the building, away from sensitive facilities in the area, also will be de-conflicted through OCHA de-confliction mechanism 2- Access: Risk: - Difficulty in the transfer and access of beneficiaries to activities due to the unstable security situation within the specific work areas- likelihood: Medium - Impact: HighMitigation Measures: A plan linked to security and safety department within the organization to protect workers and beneficiaries- Preventing women and girls from reaching centers to receive services within the center - likelihood: Medium - Impact: high- Mitigation Measures: Ensuring the provision of services for males and family members of the families and couples during the implementation of the project activities and in particular, awareness activities and advocacy campaigns in order to mitigate risks and reduce the risk of preventing the access of beneficiaries to services3- Category of beneficiaries Risk: Disability of persons with disabilities, older persons and areas away from centerslikelihood: High Impact: High Mitigation Measures: Mobile teams will be allocated to reach beneficiaries who cannot access the centers to participate in activities for people with severe disabilities and elderly people with mobility difficulties in remote areas. Beneficiaries will be facilitated by providing transportation as needed to ensure their effective participation.4- Community environment Risk: Lack of acceptance of society and the forces controlling the content of the activities provided they contain highly sensitive topicslikelihood: High Impact: High Mitigation Measures: The beneficiaries and local staff will be consulted about the possibility of implementing the sessions of the program and work on structuring the sessions in a contextual and specific manner when there is a need to address the topics indirectly and depends on the resources within the group and supports activities.
