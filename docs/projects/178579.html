Project Summary

In responding to the on-going crisis in NWS proposed intervention will provide life-saving and NFI interventions at household-levels to 12,250 target beneficiaries (2450)Families (1110 Female(0-17) – 1030 Male(0-17) –3680-Female(18-59) (3790-Male(18-59) (2640-Elderly (>59) (105-Persons with disabilities) by providing core NFIs. The project will be implemented in two districts where the needs are high: Harim and Jabl Samman. through providing the following activities:
Sector Objective # 1: Provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support
• Provision of (2650)core and essential NFIs (e.g. mattress/mat, plastic sheet, blankets, jerry can, kitchen set, solar lamps).
Relation to core NFI, these kits match the minimum standard for the Kit of the SNFI Cluster. Target: 2,450 HH - Target Individuals: 12,250 - Cost per kit: $185
The project will target ten camps in Jabl Samman are relatively new and 1,762 IDPs are currently taking shelter there. This camps are not covered by any NGO and is desperate need of basic items. The camps are too isolated for the residents to reach the nearest shops.
Also we will target the displaced in Idlib in three regions (Salqin - Harim - Kafr Deryan)
have received a huge number of displaced people which they are seeking safety away from their homes and their families.
In addition, this intervention approach will contribute in ensuring vacancies for youth through hiring a new staff for the project includes Ms and Fs.
MHD will identify the most in need families to be included in the project after wide and clear communication with all the stakeholders/NGOs in the area through conducting FGDs, surveys and awareness sessions with the local authorities, host community and communities representatives, alongside with 100% distribution monitoring with effective complaint mechanism in place, a monthly PDM and satisfaction report.
The project will insure females participation and inclusion as MHD will hire a female (one at least) in each distribution team, and as FHH is a selection criteria. Moreover, PWDs will be given proper attention through the beneficiaries selection process and has been prioritized as a selection criteria, and Muzun will assign a truck during the distribution only for FHH, PWD and elderly people to carry and transfer food basket to their living place (door-to-door) since the elderly beneficiaries considered the most at risk during the COVID-19 pandemic in NWS.
MHD will also conduct monitoring and evaluation processes throughout the project, as well as awareness sessions for COVID-19 and providing water tanks, sterilizers to protect against COVID-19 in the distribution locations taking into account the social distancing during all activities implementations. Also MHD will share information regularly with the concerned groups and raising awareness about the accountability mechanism to the affected population (consistent reporting).
Provide awareness session for GBV with PSEA team in same camps.
MHD will coordinate the multi-sectoral assistance through other sectors partners (food, health, protection, SNFI, education) and identify needs and gaps in services and share and advocate with SNFI cluster for improving service delivery. MHD will ensure sphere standards application, cluster’s guidance in assessing needs, providing and coordinating services. Addition to ensure equal access, prioritization of most vulnerable groups across all of their implemented and coordinated activities.
Female field staff will be hired to increase the protection of women and girls, and 6 accountability boxes, as well as 2 complaint numbers, will be available for all targeted IDP sites and communities’ residents.
The intervention is building on the experience, expertise and relationships from Muzun previous project cycles to reach the most vulnerable and in-need, with protection mainstreaming and sustainability at the core of its programme.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021, with the crisis in its tenth year an estimated 4,7 million NFI people in need . This need was increased about 35% of 2020 , staggering levels of need persist for people throughout Syria. The MSNA data also shows that female-headed households, youth, elderly and people with functional difficulties have reduced access to basic services including shelter as well as reduced livelihood opportunities and income that supports access to NFIs. Muzun runs the IDP Site Monitoring Team SMT which is focusing on collecting timely, holistic and precise information on all IDP movements in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. This project will support and enhance the proposed NFI distribution by providing regular new IDP arrival data on timely basis.After reviewing the most need locations to NFIs and according to our own assessment conducted in December 2020. Jabl Samman ten camps are relatively new and 1,762 IDPs are currently taking shelter there. This camps are not covered by any NGO and is desperate need of basic items. The camps are too isolated for the residents to reach the nearest shops; Muzun will therefore arrange to bring the goods to the camp. 90% of the communities report Female-Headed Households as the top vulnerability. The project will take into consideration their protection needs as well as participation in the implementation to address the gender sensitive issues. Project will include women participants to volunteer in distributing to women headed households.As for the displaced in Idlib, these three regions (Salqin - Harim - Kafr Deryan ) have received a huge number of displaced people which they are seeking safety away from their homes and their families.

5 - Implementation of activities

1-Provision of core NFIs: Supplying the items will be done by Contracting with suppliers. Shipping the items to Syria 2- Monitor the context: In case any emergency a RNA will be done within few days. 3- Coordination with the other humanitarian actors:The local communities play a good role in the safety and security of MHD team, we coordinate with them for the team and the items. 4-Distribution Process: distribution will in coordination with local councils and local communities in the targeted locations. The process including the final list of beneficiaries and their location, informing the beneficiaries about the date of distribution and the items that will be distributed , beneficiaries arrangement during distribution. MHD distribute the items according to the list that they have collected prior distribution, they call beneficiaries by names, check their IDs and let them sign the beneficiary list. All collected beneficiaries will be shared later with Gaziantep office to be checked and archived. 5.Monitoring Process: monitoring is taking place during all project phases through a direct follow up of all project activities by the program department based in Gaziantep. Monitoring is done by follow up meeting with team based in Syria and field visits of senior staff to the distribution sites. Muzun will have a team of trained enumerators (both male and female) conducting house-to-house assessments and distributions with oversight from the Programme Manager. A PDM will be conduct within a month after each distribution to monitor acceptability of the items provided and beneficiary satisfaction with the distribution process.The protection measures against COVID-19 set by Cluster will be taken into account in all project stages

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

• Societal tensions / inclusion and / or exclusion of a specific community group; This will be mitigated by engaging all community ethics groups during project coordination and activities. In addition to forming a field community committee consisting of three MHD field staff from various departments, to promote and support the principle of neutrality for the selected beneficiaries. This measure has been implemented in previous MHD projects and has proven successful and valuable. • Gender-related risks: Women encounter difficulties receiving group; this will be mitigated through; 1. Choosing a nearby, safe and easy to access location for kit distribution. 2. All NFI distributions have female staff present at the distribution site • Risk of physical harm at distribution points (security, safety) this will be mitigated through; 1. Well organized distribution to avoid overcrowding. 3. Pre-consultations with communities. 4. Ensure access to distribution points by all groups taking into consideration people with specific needs. 5. Application of checklist at distribution sites.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

There will be dangerous of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls. It is high Likelihood and high impact in the community.For mitigating measure, we we will establish a confidential complaint mechanism, train staff on code of conduct and add it in all staff contracts and address any PSEA complaints. Our monitoring staff and staff to analyze data will post-monitoring/ client feedback forms are used, include PSEA topic in regular supervision sessions. Also, there are risks of gender-based violence GBV because of women being treated as beneficiaries and they are primary stakeholders. They may be vulnerable to sexual harassment and sexual assault. If situational and risk analyzes are not systematically performed, these risks may not be identified and corrected. Here it is important that we take measures to mitigate these risks: the MHD team will include female staff on the project and will call for adequate assistance. We will include women, girls and other at-risk groups in the steps of project. Also must be consultations with women, girls, boys and men should be organized and community safety audits audited.There are a dangerous of exploitation to child.
