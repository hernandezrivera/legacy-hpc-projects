Project Summary

The project aims to increase equitable safe access to inclusive, quality educational services for children who suffers from learning difficulties, ADHD, and Psychological Disturbance in rural areas in the Northern Governorate; basically Nablus and Tubas. The chosen vulnerable areas in both cities are distributed as area (C). The direct beneficiaries of the project will be male and female students, aged between 7-9 years old. The project shall provide each school with an assistive resource room to ensure correct implementation to the project activities, and also to guarantee sustainability of the project. This project can be considered as a way of increasing awareness of the problem of learning difficulties, ADHD and Psychological disturbance for children and its impact. As a long term project, providing remedial education, and catching up learning support to children who have learning difficulties, ADHD and Psychological disturbance will avoid dropping out of school at elder grades/age. As a long term goal, the project will be providing remedial education and catch up learning support to children with learning difficulties, ADHD, and psychological disturbance to minimize dropping out of schools at elder grades/ages.

1 - Needs

Sometimes there are apparently inexplicable blocks to learning which prevent intelligent children from reaching their potential in the classroom.The blockages can be so difficult to pinpoint that no one is aware of the real problem. Studies show that up to 20% of children are not achieving the academic results that their intelligence indicates they should be capable of achieving. Local and regional field studies indicates that around 10% of the children in region, are suffering of learning difficulties and ADHD, where official reports showed the following impact of the mentioned difficulties (especially ADHD disorders):1- 50% of the drugs abusers have learning difficulties disorders.2- 30% of the drugs abusers schools students or School leakage children.3- 90% of the drugs abusers are under18 years old..4- In the year 2017, 53998 Palestinian students’ participated violations resulting in fatality, injuries, and detainment or incarceration, most of them become easy victims of the violence clashes with the Israeli forces, and other violence kinds. Despite of the few studies conducted to investigate the number and situation of children with learning difficulties in Palestine; here, we summarize the results of some of them:1.A study, that was conducted in 2000 on fourth graders students (males and females aged 9years old) who have intelligence percentage over 90% in governmental schools in Bethlehem governorate found out that the percentage of students who suffers from learning difficulties in Arabic language equals 11.58%.2. In 2012, a national survey was conducted about learning difficulties in Palestine in the main school subjects (Arabic Language, English Language and Math). The survey purposed 7,303 students aged between (males and females aged 7-14 years old). The results indicated the below percentages of learning difficulties distributed on subjects and students ages: *Arabic Language: - Second grade (7 years old): 28% - Third grade (8 years old): 30.4% - Fourth grade (9years old): 25.9% *English Language:- Second grade (7 years old): 26.7% - Third grade (8 years old): 24.2% - Fourth grade (9years old): 27.5% *Math- Second grade (7 years old): 15.5% - Third grade (8 years old): 27% - Fourth grade (9years old): 25.8% 3. A study conducted in 2013 about learning challenges in Palestine. The study purposed 2,921 students (males and females) aged between (7 to 12 years old). The study reflected that the school management, the educational system and student’s psyche are vital factors for student’ educational achievements. Whilst other factors such as family, society and even the health state of a student have less effects on student’ educational achievements. The study also revealed that teacher training, classroom capacity and spaces in schools empower schools in protecting students with learning difficulties. The ignorance of any of the aforementioned factors leads to decrease in educational achievements for students. Moreover, the problems with learning disabilities would be badly developed as long as a student get older. 4. Another study conducted in 2019 investigated the behavioral disorders in governmental school in northern governorates. A survey distributed for 40 learning difficulties teachers. the results showed that there are behavioral disorders for students along with learning difficulties in term of school system adaptation.**The previous studies show that the percentage of students with disabilities (males and females aged between 7-9 years old) is high, and shall be considered to avoid exposing students to further harm represented of leaving school education especially girls. There is a need to highlight the suffering of children with learning difficulties and ADHD to diagnose and treat them. Moreover, there is a need to work for decreasing the number of children who have learning difficulties, and protect their right in education, and remove all obstacles that impede their integration into mainstream schools with their typical peers. The need of the project comes from the need for sustainable projects in the field of learning disabilities that may decrease and solve the problem of thousands of students in Palestine. This project differs from other projects as it will diagnose the problem of learning difficulties from all aspects: medically, behaviorally and psychologically whilst keeping a student in his/her normal education environment. In addition, it aims to train the school teachers and school counsellors of the purposed schools, and also establish resources room in each school under the supervision of Rawan Association team to maintain more sustainability of the project. It is very expensive for most of the Palestinian families to enroll their children who have learning difficulties in rehabilitation centers and associations. On the other hand, when students with diabilities do not find support within the school, their educational achievemts will be kept at low level. This will be resulting in withdrawal of those students from schools, and denying their right of education and may be other rights as they will remain behind; especially girls; who would be forced to get married on early age.Hence, the proposed project meets the second education cluster objective that stipulates increasing equitable safe access to inclusive, quality educational services for vulnerable children. The project also goes in line with the national priorities and need related mainly to the Ministry of education, Ministry of Social Affairs and also the Ministry of Health. The issue of learning difficulties has been inserted in the strategic plan of the Ministry of Education since the percentage of students with disabilities male and females aged between (7-9 years old) percentage exceeds 15%. Those students affect the whole process of education, and that differs from class room to the other depending on the percentage of those students in each class room.

2 - Activities

1. Training of Teachers and Counsellors: the involved teachers and counsellors from all chosen schools will be given trainings by special education specialists on the main objectives and activities on the project; as they will be involved in the implementation of the project throughout the project period. It is planned to be given two separated trainings in total hours of 30hrs. The first training will be on learning difficulties and psychological disturbance, Whilst the second training will be on ADHD and Psychological disturbance. Besides, the teachers, counsellor and project team will be trained on PSEA & "Do not harm principles and guidance. The association will later engage them in the PSEA training and HCT OPT PSEA network.2. Diagnosis and determination of learning difficulties and ADHD: the trainee teachers and counsellors, with cooperation with experts, under the supervision of the project team at Rawan Association will make diagnosis to determine and identify students who have learning difficulties or ADHD or both of them; which will be accompanying with psychological disturbances in the six schools for children aged between (7-9). Individual development plans will be created for each student with involvement of parents. 3. Resource rooms: in each school, a resource room will be established, and supplied with equipment, furniture, computers and special education learning materials to respond to the inclusive education and protection needs of identified children4. Learning support session: weekly learning support sessions including individual remedial classes, classroom support and after school support. In addition to individual classes to be held for those children with significant special needs which cannot be met in the classroom.5. Monthly parent meeting: a monthly parents meeting will be held with parents of identified children to support them, maintain effective communication and address any issues or concerns from all parties involved. Parents will also be presented and guided to PSEA and "Do not harm" principles to increase their awareness and to help the team to maximize the impact of the project. 6. National Inclusive Education conference: Conduct national inclusive education conference with the participation of children, educationists, government and the community; to promote and advocate the right of children with special needs to education, PSEA, protection and participation. Conference hall to hold approximately 600 people and be divided into three sessions with lunch and coffee break. The conference will be divided into presentations by special education professionals and educationists, workshops on early detection, diagnosis and rehabilitation and children fun activities.7. Entertainment activities: the activities aim to engage student with learning difficulties and ADHD with normal students to overcome the psychological disturbances for them, as well as improving their communication and interaction skills. Entertainment activities will be including a summer camp, Drama workshops, music classes and other entertaining activities.
