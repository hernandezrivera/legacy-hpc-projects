Project Summary

This project aims at increasing access to education and providing education services of good quality in Damascus and Rural Damascus.
Targeted locations are of severity scale 3, and 4. the % of people that are within severity 3 is 53 %, the % of people that are within severity 4 is 47 %.
The project aims to meet a wide range of education needs that include ECE, TVET, school furniture, school supplies, training for teachers and educational personnel, support the NFE in several areas.
the project will directly contribute to the Education Sector’s HRP objectives 1 (Availability and access) and 2 (Quality), and it will target children (IDPs, host community, and returnees) with different kinds of activities in addition to targeting parents, and teachers. Activities are mentioned in detail with targets in another part of this proposal.
Targeted locations are determined according to AlTAmayoz work in those areas where AlTAmayoz is currently implementing part of education activities under SHF project that will be closed by the end of February 2022, and AlTAmayoz is willing to continue providing education services due to the huge need observed in those locations so to maintain the sustainability of the activities. Also, AlTamayoz has access and needed approvals and previous presence in the targeted locations where the need for education services is so high and where a high number of out of school children exists
The project will address the beneficiaries education needs according to FGDs made by AlTamayoz targeted men, women, boys, and girls and according to the methodology that will include the provision of non-formal education for vulnerable children ( boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community) and by providing a comprehensive package of services: ECE, TVET and life-skills, NFE, awareness raising related to mine action, COVID-19, and other hygiene and health issues, support teacher training, provision of PSS and recreational activities, support schools with furniture, school feeding, support parents with livelihood activities and support students with school supplies and materials,…
The project will be implemented in non-formal settings in Damascus and 12 locations in Rural Damascus and will support schools in some of those locations in coordination with DoE.
Activities are planned for 2 years to maintain the highest outcome of education and to achieve a distinguished impact on children’s education status especially those out of school.
The project will target students (boys and girls) of all ages from 3 to 24 (including adolescents and youth) and will support children with disabilities of different ages by providing suitable and easy access, deploying trained staff. this project is linked to another protection project where Mine risk education is provided to students and teachers, and referrals of students with protection risks to case management, It is also linked with livelihood activities that will support caregivers and reduce the family burden so they are encouraged to send their children to school. Activities will consider COVID-19 by assuring social distancing and preventive measures (awareness raising, PPEs) in addition to conducting awareness-raising sessions on different health topics. Activities will also include: NFE activities, ECE, BTL, TVET and lifeskills, providing 4 schools with furniture and solar cells, providing students with textbooks,school bags,supplies, recreational materials and PSS activities, supporting some schools with school feeding program, support the most vulnerable students with cash assistance according to specific criteria, support other vulnerable students with winter clothes, conduct MR education awareness sessions, train teachers and education personnel on different topics and teaching skills and support them with teaching materials

4 - Needs

As per Draft 2022 HNO SYRIA:The protracted nature of the conflict, economic duress and the Covid-19 pandemic, continue to impact overstretched education services. The formal system, is unable to retain all school age children.At the national level, there is an average of one operational classroom for every 54 school-aged child. The highest ratios are found inDamascus (1:101) and Rural Damascus (1:94)Existing services are unable to physically accommodate or meet the different learning needs of pre-primary children, adolescents, IDPs, returnees and children with specific needs. Most often education services are not flexible enough to accommodate children working to support their households. children face potential risks when traveling to school which may limit access to education services. Damasucus and RD are the governorates with the highest rates of students traveling longer than 20-minutes to school As per AlTamyoz observations in Rural Damascus: Out-of-school children face an immediate risk of exploitation and abuse.Children’s sense of well-being is jeopardized and the impact of crisis on children and their development can have tremendous consequences for their adult lives if they are not provided with appropriate support and healing mechanisms. Increased poverty levels limit positive skills development opportunities for youth and adolescents, and their ability to continue their education.Lack of income-generating activities have weakened the purchasing power of people

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be directly implemented by AlTamayoz and will be in line with HRP framework and will contribute to HRP objectives 1 and 2. It will assure equal access to education for boys and girls from all affected communities, special support will be provided to children with disabilities and those of special needs.AlTAmayoz has sustained presence in Damascus and selected locations in RD. A good database and reporting system will be in place, in addition to regular meetings with staff to evaluate the achieved results towards targets, Activities in Damascus,Duma Harsta will be as an expansion to already existing activities where the need is very huge. Newly established centers in RD(Zamalka,Babella,Kafr Batna,AlZabdani,Ein elfijeh,Madaya,Qudsiya,Dimas,Maliha and Darayya)aim at expanding services to new geographic areas and new populations. Students will benefit from comprehensive package of services(non formal education sessions, PSS sessions and recreational activities, school supplies, school feeding, transportation, Cash assistance)in addition to BTL campaigns; supporting life skills training and TVET for those aged 17-24, and supporting schools with furniture. AlTamayoz will deploy well-qualified teachers and PSS providers, pre and post teststo measure the impact teacher will be provided with capacity building. Monitoringactivities: consultation with communities, feedback mechanisms, pre and post-assessments, filed visit

Learning facilities or surroundings are structurally unsafe or do not provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, dust, etc.) or from public health risks including Covid-19 and other communicable diseases. Mitigation measures: Learning facilities should be improved to ensure that buildings and grounds are safe and protected.All works should be supervised and monitored by technical personnel. Conduct awareness raising sessions regarding NCD and COVID-19.Risk: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Students participate in bullying or act violently towards other students and/or to teachers. Mitigation measures: AlTamayoz will set regulations for all staff and teachers that prevent all forms of violence, Children will be closely supervised to prevent any kind of bullying among students and teachers. child safeguarding is ensured at the learning centers through different aspects (all staff including busses drivers has to sign a code of conduct that prevents any kind of violenceRisk: Tensions within the community or between the organization and community due to feeling of being unfairly treated, insufficiently consulted.
