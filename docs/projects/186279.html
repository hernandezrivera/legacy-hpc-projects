Project Summary

GOALPrime Organization Nigeria (GPON) targets to reach a total number of 47,000people; out of which 10,000 (80% girls & 20% boys) who are survivors of sexual assault will be reached with Comprehensive GBV case management and specialized services, psychosocial support services. 21950 (60% women/girls and 40% men/boys) people will be reached through house-to-house sensitization, on GBV prevention and principles that address negative social norms, 10,000women and girls will be reached with the distribution of material assistance and dignity kits while 5000 (60% women/girls and 40% men/boys) with peer support, mentoring/coaching, skills building and empowerment programs in camps and communities of Kala/Balge, Ngala, MMC, and Jere LGAs.20,000 Information Education and communication-IEC Materials will be printed through GPON Humanity Press and distributed to beneficiaries who will be reached with GBV sensitization as well as posted in all locations of engagement strategically. The IEC materials will be developed in Hausa, Kanuri, and English languages in collaboration with the GBV subsector, the Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development (MWSAD), and Translators without Boarder. 10% of the total number of beneficiaries to be reached is targeted at people living with disabilities.
GOALPrime in collaboration with the Ministry of Women Affairs and social development (MWSAD) will develop prevention and response jingles/messages on gender-based violence which will be aired consistently on the Radio Station with coverage reaching Kala/Balge, Ngala, MMC, and Jere LGAs. GOALPrime will also set up GBV child-friendly reporting mechanisms and zero GBV clubs in schools and camps of its location of implementation, as well as provide phone booths services for case management support for survivors especially in cases of emergencies.
GOALPrime will carry out capacity building quarterly for 50 women and girls in each location of its engagement throughout the period of the project implementation, empowering them as result to be the first respondents in their communities and camps, also GPON will work with the women leaders in these locations of engagements to create a “No GBV Women’s Network” in each location of engagement. GPON will also conduct capacity building and strengthening for GBV workers on proper case management at their field level.
Through Coordination with partners in the project locations, GOALPrime will identify livelihood actors for linkage of Survival of Sexual and Gender-Based Violence (SGBV) for livelihood support. Through this project, GOALPrime targets to identify 400 girls (SGBVs)with livelihood challenges and provide linkage to access livelihood support from livelihood partners across the project location.
GOALPrime seeks to strongly strengthen GBV referral pathways in each location. Through its ongoing GBV project in some of these locations, GPON has seen the need to strengthen actors' coordination and seeks to support quarterly coordination amongst actors in each of the locations. GOALPrime will be fully operational in all LGA throughout the implementation of this project. And continues assessment of camp and community response on the cases of GBV to inform subsequent project design and planning.

1 - Needs

An estimated 3 million people across Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe states need interventions for the prevention of and response to gender-based violence (GBV). The underlying drivers of the conflict and conditions of displacement continue to exacerbate the occurrence of GBV incidents. Half of this population in need consists of children(below 18 years) with the majority being adolescent girls. Sexual violence - including rape, sexual exploitation, forced and early marriage, survival sex and domestic violence, physical assault, denial of resources, and psychological and emotional abuse - are among the most frequently reported violations, recurrently perpetrated by intimate or former partners. Minors, particularly adolescent girls, are also at risk of GBV, which occurs mainly in the context of child sexual abuse and early marriage. Also, an estimated 2.7 million children and adolescents (1.5 million girls and 1.2 million boys) in northeast Nigeria are in need of protection and cross-sectoral services. Threats and risks to the well-being of girls and boys include family separation, gender-based violence including child marriage and conflict-related sexual violence, recruitment, and use of children, abduction, and other grave child rights violations. At least 6,000 children (more than 50 percent girls) are estimated to have been recruited, abducted, killed, or victims of violent attacks by armed groups since the beginning of the conflict. Of those, approximately 50 percent are survivors of SGBV. The crisis also has caused severe psychological distress among children. The conflict has not only disrupted the social fabric but has also negatively affected the ability of parents and communities to appropriately care for their children.The Humanitarian Response Plan for 2021 shows the critical Gender-based violence (GBV) needs in Kala-Balge, Ngala, MMC, and JERE LGAs, with a PiN estimate of30,198, 39,479, 156,665, and 113,939 respectively. These critical gender-based violence needs have also been confirmed by GOALPrime Organization Nigeria (GPON) through her activities of engagements in these LGAs and feedbacks from the community’s members, communities stakeholders, and camp stakeholders. Hence, the need to build the resilience of survivors of Gender-Based Violence by strengthening the prevention and response services through community sensitization, capacity building, PSS, and referrals for specialized services in Kala/Balge, Ngala, MMC, and Jere LGAs of Borno state.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

GOALPrime will continue to mainstream protection throughout the implementation of this project by aligning every approach with the protection and Do no Harm principles and PSEA, promoting equity, safety, and dignity of both the beneficiaries and that of employers and volunteers. GOALPrime, just like in our ongoing projects will continue to prioritize safety, dignity and avoid causing any form of harm to both beneficiaries and workers. The project will raise safeguarding awareness at the community level and put in place reporting and response mechanisms for redress in case of PSEA. The project will also conduct referral mapping for SGBV cases referrals across all the implementation locations GOALPrime will also strengthen already established mechanisms through which women and girls can be served effectively in target LGAs with systems that allow addressing concerns and complaints. GOALPrime will continue to ensure that the target population accesses the service in proportion to needs and without barriers and discrimination. GOALPrime will work with the community to develop sensitive messages to talk about SGBV, gender inclusion, and other concerns influenced by culture and environment. GOALPrime will continue to pay attention to different needs of girls and boys, women and men including girls and boys with disabilities who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services, hence, maintaining an environment that enhances integration and equal considerations of the needs of boys, girls and those with disability. In the Life-skills based psychosocial support services component of this project, GOALPrime will integrate SGBV/SEA Risk Mitigation activities to minimize or mitigate SGBV/SEA risk to beneficiaries. GOALPrime will train employees, and volunteers on child safeguarding and safe child participation, and work with men and boys as ambassadors for the safe participation of women and girls in our project activities. GOALPrime will continue to work consciously in compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches will align with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that this project does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NO HARM Protection Principle. GOALPrime will also ensure that all activities are carried out in compliance with the Nigerian Center for Disease Control (NCDC) and Federal Ministry of Health (FMoH) guidelines against the spread of, and community transmission of COVID-19. GOALPrime will ensure zero sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) via training and compliance of all staff, volunteers, contractors to GPON's PSEA Policy and code of conduct and also ensure that defaulters are held accountable via GPON's Investigation mechanism

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime will establish or strengthen new or existing feedback and complaints mechanisms in target LGAs. GOALPrime will hold bi-monthly feedback focus group discussions with girls and boys including adolescents’ beneficiaries and their caregivers to understand the project's impact on them and also receive suggestions from them on areas they want project improvement. The Focus Group discussions will however be in compliance with the COVID-19 regulations of maintaining social distancing and the use of face masks. GOALPrime will also carry out at least two child-friendly surveys –one at the beginning of the project and another one at the end of the project to collect perceptions and feedback from children that should guide project activities and strategy improvement. In addition, GOALPrime will still have a complaint and suggestion box kept at strategic positions within the project environment for beneficiaries and their parents to drop their complaints if they feel more comfortable with making anonymous complaints and also their suggestions on how they want the project to serve them better. GOALPrime will provide feedback of complaints to the beneficiaries, parents, and other relevant community stakeholders as soon as complaints are received and reviewed. There are suggestions also will be taken into consideration in the review of Programme approach. Effective and timely actions shall be taken to address concerns received through our established feedback mechanism. However, lessons learned from past projects are. that complaints received via the complaint box mechanism are mostly not related to the project being implemented. To address this, GOALPrime will intensify sensitization of the project to both the target beneficiaries and the community people so that they understand the expected project outcomes and make relevant complaints related to the project.
