Project Summary

Project Summary
This project aims to enhance water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) facilities in schools across Nkambe Central Subdivision, which has experienced a significant influx of internally displaced persons (IDPs) due to the Anglophone crisis. Nkambe has become a refuge for displaced students, as it has maintained safe and uninterrupted education since 2018. However, the increased student population has overwhelmed existing WASH infrastructure, creating urgent sanitation and water access challenges that impact students' health and learning environment.
Nkambe has been completely cut off from the national electrical grid for six years, resulting in the local water utility, CAMWATER, ceasing operations. The lack of reliable water access has put further strain on schools, which now depend on alternative sources. Our WASH assessment in March 2024, followed by a second review in September 2024, revealed that out of 80 schools assessed, fewer than five have access to a consistent water supply, and only two meet the criteria for safe drinking water.
While the Nkambe Council’s solar-powered water system at Marangong Water Catchment provides some relief, it is insufficient to meet the needs of the growing population. Many schools face chronic water shortages, lack of safe drinking options, and inadequate hygiene facilities, increasing the risk of disease outbreaks among students. This project seeks to construct and rehabilitate water points and hygiene facilities in Nkambe’s schools, ensuring access to safe water and promoting a healthier school environment for students, teachers, and staff. By addressing these critical needs, the project aims to create a sustainable, healthier, and more resilient learning environment that will benefit the community for years to come.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project addresses critical WASH needs in schools within Nkambe Central by improving access to safe sanitation and clean water. Many schools, currently overwhelmed by increased student populations, lack adequate toilet facilities, clean water points, and basic hygiene infrastructure, leading to poor health outcomes and increased absenteeism. The project will construct new toilets in underserved schools and rehabilitate existing ones to ensure safe, private facilities for both boys and girls, which is essential for inclusive and equitable access. Additionally, it will rehabilitate current water points, connect new ones, and provide boreholes where access to water is severely limited. Training WASH committees in schools will empower students, teachers, and community members to maintain these facilities sustainably, promoting hygiene practices and ensuring the longevity of the infrastructure. Through these activities, the project aims to provide reliable access to water and sanitation, reduce disease transmission, and foster a healthier learning environment for all students.
