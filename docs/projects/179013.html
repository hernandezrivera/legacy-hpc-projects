Project Summary

Food security remains a serious challenge due to continued localized hostilities, new and protracted displacements, increased numbers of recent returnees and the sustained erosion of communities' resilience after almost ten years of conflict, in addition to, loss the value of local currency with rising the food prices continuously.
The intervention will contribute to cover the gaps and respond to food needs of crisis-affected people in Aleppo rural province, through improving access to food. The project will target the most vulnerable families from IDPs & returnees (men, women, boys, and girls) who affected by the Syrian crisis.
The project will include one main activity of humanitarian intervention which is supporting the IDPs & returnees in Atareb district through distributing food kits for 1570 families for 8 months in Kafr Naseh Elatareb village (1450 from IDPs families and 120 from returnees' families), where there were 1450 families (7640 persons) in the random camps in the village (10 camps) according to the latest needs assessment and survey that conducted by MASRRAT team during April 2021.
The food kits will provide the families with 1470 Calories which is equal to 70 % per person per day according to Sphere standards.
This intervention will target the families who meet the selection criteria and at the same time we are going to focus on the families that have not received any food assistant during the last three months. The food kits' weight is approximately 80 Kg and contained the most basic materials such as (Rice, bulgur, lentils, sugar, oil, ghee, flour and other materials such as dates, jam, canned fish, olives, luncheon)
The food kits will be distributed to the IDPs & returnees to their living locations and will be procured inside Syria in order to support the local markets.

4 - Needs

According to the latest HNO 2021, The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 per cent in the last year and at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, an increase of 57 per cent compared to 2020, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. Food prices in Syria have increased dramatically in 2020 and WFP’s national standard reference food basket price in November 2020 was 251 per cent higher when compared to November 2019.Based on these data and through the wide coverage of MASRRAT Organization in the western countryside of Aleppo: MASRRAT team during April 2021 conducted many visits and needs assessment in Atareb district in Aleppo rural. Wherein, the results showed that the IDPs(1450 families) & returnees (120 families )suffering from a general shortage of basic food items due to the high price of foodstuffs. Also, all the surveyed people assured that food is the biggest need for them, in addition to shelter and especially the IDPs people who live in the random camps, informal camps and uncompleted or uninhabitable houses in Atareb subdistrict where, there are 10 random camps in Kafr Naseh Elatareb village and suffering from difficult living conditions because of the lack of support and the huge number of IDPs.As the result, this intervention considered one of the most necessary response through Providing the vulnerable food insecure populations with monthly food assistance through appropriate modalities to most vulnerable households.

5 - Implementation of activities

The Implementation plan of activities will start in 01 May 2021, the project duration will be 8 months wherein, the first month will include preparations, procurement and contracting with eight months will include the distributions. The staff will be recruited directly to carry out project implementation. The project will provide assistance for 1450 vulnerable families monthly for eight months. The food kits will provide the family with 1470 Calories which is equal to 70 % per day as per Sphere standards. The targeted locations will focus on the crowdy IDPs in the random camps and the returnees' families in Kafr Naseh Elatareb village who did not receive any food support during the previous three months. At the beginning of the implementation of the project, MASRRAT's team will sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MOUs) with the local council in the area. The process of engaging the Local Council will start with advertising about the project in the communities of intervention, explaining the mechanism of selection, and the number of potential beneficiaries under this project. T. Data collection of the beneficiaries will be conducted at the beginning of the project (in the preparation month). The data will be verified based on the criteria of selection like No. of individuals in the families, not receiving food aid, IDPs, existing of PWD and etc. Delivery of food kits will be according to paper voucher and according to distribution lists. Beneficiaries will sign on the distribution lists, and they will be verified through their IDs. The project will follow the procurement policy of MASRRAT, as the food items will be procured from local market
