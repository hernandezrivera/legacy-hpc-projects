Project Summary

Under-nutrition is a public health issue and has adverse consequences on immediate child survival and long-term well-being. Acute and chronic malnutrition in children and women causes irreversible damage to intrauterine growth of fetuses and leads to physical and mental disabilities in children under the age of two years. The North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions of Cameroon are characterized by a relatively low prevalence of acute malnutrition but high prevalence of chronic malnutrition, micronutrient deficiency and poor indicators related to infant and young child feeding practices. Prior to the ongoing crisis, the NWSW regions presented vulnerabilities to malnutrition with high rates of stunting of 36% and 28% in NW and SW respectively. Furthermore, poor indicators on Infant and young child feeding practices were reported with 42.5% and 58.3% of exclusive breast-feeding rates for NW and SW region respectively. Complementary feeding also appears compromised, with only 30 per cent of children 6-23 months consuming the minimum acceptable diet and over 40% (44.3% NW and 41.9% SW) of children aged 6-59months were reported to have anaemia. Caritas Kumbo current partnership with WFP on General Food Distribution and BSFP provide a unique opportunity and in line with the Nutrition Sector 2024 objectives to reach to over 45000 vulnerable and affected populations from the WFP 2023 targeting exercise; to reach to over 6000 children at risk of malnutrition, and ensure that over 300 SAM cases receive treatment according to national protocol and benefit from diets, practices and services that prevent stunting, wasting, micro nutrient deficiencies and overweight, including services for the early detection and treatment of all forms of life-threatening acute malnutrition and timely access to culturally appropriate, gender and age sensitive information that builds and improves on their nutritional status. Pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers’ benefit from diets, practices and services that protect them from under nutrition, micro nutrient deficiencies and anemia in humanitarian context and above all to ensure that People Living with HIV/AIDS (PLHIV/AIDS) and disabilities in conflict affected areas benefit from Nutrition Assessments, counseling, and support (NACS) services to prevent and treat acute malnutrition and improved adherence to ART treatment
This intervention targets most especially the hardest to reach communities within the implementation areas who due to the crisis have increase challenges to accessing health structures; through coordination with the Health System in place and the CHWs following the district approach the intervention will boast the health data as well as ensuring reducing gaps through continuously data reporting and participation in both cluster and district coordination meetings. Caritas Kumbo Partnership with WFP in the targeted divisions on GFD & Supplementary Preventive Feeding (SPF) offers a unique opportunity to do follow up and complement program interventions to reach more vulnerable population at a far reduced cost.
