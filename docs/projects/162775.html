Project Summary

In 2019, six years after the onset of the crisis in northern Mali, the security situation is characterized by "precarious stability" with the persistence of numerous incidents. It should be noticed:
 Targeted attacks or armed clashes of allegedly radical armed groups against national and / or foreign armed forces, particularly in the regions of Gao, Timbuktu, Kidal and Menaka.
 Some intercommunity tensions in these different regions, the most extensive of which is the tension between the Fulani and the dogons who have made numerous victims. In the circle of Gourma Rharous the communes of Banikane, Gossi and Hamzakoma, Bambrara Maoudé and Haribomo recorded inter-community tensions between the months of June and November 2018
 Several acts of robbery like theft of vehicles. The targets are vehicles belonging to NGOs, UN Organizations, the Malian State, but also individuals.
The consequence of the armed clashes and the inter-community tensions, is the displacement of populations. Also, it should note that the complex security situation in the Neighboring regions of Mopti and Gao since 2018, cause many population displacements toward the circle of Gourma Rharous. Thus, for recent period from January to October 2019, the RRM coordination system report more than 27692 IDPs in the circle of Gourma Rharous.
International Medical Corps, which is the main NGO partner of the Gourma Rhaous health district, is supporting this HD since 2013 through 5 successive one year-projects funded by ECHO, to strengthen and empower health facilities to provide qualitative basic health and nutrition service. With the total-free care, IMC helped to completely lift the financial barrier to access to care for the population. Even these projects included also an RRM component to address the needs for the IDPs, the coverage rate of this response can be improved. Also, the attendance at health centers has been limited by geographical and security barriers for some parts of the population. A part of the population in the intervention zone is nomadic pastoralists who, during the dry season, travel in search of fodder for their livestock. Some find themselves far from health centers. In the Gourma Rharous, around 28 villages are located more than 15 km from a CSCom; with an estimated population 13118 persons.
Regarding this situation, it is relevant to address these priors needs through mobile clinics interventions so that to strengthen access to basic health services in nutrition and protection for displaced and remote populations of health facilities in the Gourma Rharous health district. International Medical Corps proposes to implement mobile clinics to provide health access to uncovered population. The program strategy aims to improve the coverage of basic health care by providing qualitative health care, strengthen the technical knowledge of health staff and integrate community sensitization component. Beyond improving the coverage of the provision of care to vulnerable populations, the clinic will have a goal to explore the alerts and provide a rapid response to priority needs in the health and nutrition sectors. Indeed, when the CM intervenes urgently on a new site, it makes it possible to make a rapid assessment of the health situation of the populations affected by the crisis. Subsequently, more sustained actions can be envisaged to respond in a more sustainable way to the identified needs.This rapid and flexible intervention strategy with mobile clinic should be complementary to the actions already under way or envisaged by IMC in terms of structural support of health facilities for the provision of primary and secondary health care in the Gourma Rharous district.
