Project Summary

The Project will focus awareness raising and improving the living standard of orphans, Street children, Adolescents girls, person's with special needs, and women especially IDPs, asylum seekers and the families affected by the conflict and flood to improve their livelihoods and protect the life of the children.
Yusufari, Nguru and Damaturu are among the top Local Government that has Child protection concern in Yobe State/
A child born within Yusufari, Damaturu and Nguru LGAS has the lowest chances to access decent formal education, and even if they are enrolled in schools, there are still higher chances of dropping out before completion. This can be subtly traced due to their indecent living conditions and poverty within the community, For a child to stay in school, the caregivers must be able to provide basic care in terms of food and shelter, but most of these caregivers are not able to.
Most boys and girls are dropping out of school to try and earn a living, which has resulting to child Labour cases; the girls try to take care of the younger children and support their mothers in providing for the families, and once out of school, the eventuality is early marriages for the girls while some of the girls are becoming victims of rapes. this has become a vicious circle of poverty in these Locality, and with Children not attending schools, they remain practically illiterate. The children in this marginalized locality can only be able to join the societal mainstream if they are at least literate.
Our target is to provide support and strengthen the capacity of women, Children, Disabled person's and the most vulnerable families with businesses and train them on how to improve their farming system and to create massive awareness on Child Protection concern and support the victims of protection related concern and provide a mitigation measures that will enable the communities to know how to handle, and refer the victims of protection and the perpetrator.
We believed by supporting the victims and empowering the caregivers we can be able to reduce the rate of child protection concern's within the targeted communities.
Our mission is to render the services that will impact the life of children, women and most vulnerable people, to be self-reliance, and their will be a peaceful coexistent among others within the society.

1 - Needs

1. Community Education Programs: Raise awareness about the dangers of child trafficking, illiteracy, and child labor through workshops, seminars, and community events. 2. School Enrollment Campaigns: Encourage and facilitate school enrollment, ensuring access to quality education for all children. 3. Economic Empowerment: Support vocational training and income-generating activities for parents, reducing the economic pressure that can lead to child labor. 4. Legal Advocacy: Collaborate with local authorities to strengthen and enforce laws against child trafficking and labor, ensuring consequences for offenders. 5. Community Watch Groups: Establish community-led initiatives to monitor and report suspicious activities related to child protection concerns. 6. Social Support Networks: Create support systems for families in need, providing counseling, healthcare, and assistance to alleviate socio-economic challenges. 7. Partnerships with NGOs: Collaborate with non-governmental organizations working on child protection to leverage resources and expertise. 8. Media Campaigns: Utilize media platforms to disseminate information on child protection, emphasizing the importance of education and the dangers of child trafficking and labor. 9. Government Involvement: Advocate for government policies that prioritize child protection, ensuring resources are allocated to address these concerns effectively. 10. Regular Assessments: Conduct regular assessments of the community's progress in reducing child protection concerns, adjusting strategies as needed for continuous improvement.

2 - Activities

1. Access to Quality Education: Ensure that all children have access to free and quality education, including efforts to eliminate barriers such as school fees, uniforms, and transportation costs. 2. Economic Empowerment for Families: Provide vocational training and income-generating opportunities for parents to alleviate financial pressures, reducing the likelihood of resorting to child labor. 3. Community Awareness Programs: Conduct educational campaigns to raise awareness about the consequences of child labor and trafficking, emphasizing the importance of education for children's well-being. 4. Legal Support and Enforcement: Strengthen legal frameworks and law enforcement to deter and penalize those engaging in child labor and trafficking, ensuring justice for victims. 5. Healthcare Services: Improve access to healthcare services to address health-related issues that might contribute to child vulnerability and labor. 6. Social Safety Nets: Establish social support programs that provide assistance to families facing economic hardships, offering financial aid, food support, or other resources. 7. Community Engagement: Foster community involvement and ownership in tackling these issues, encouraging collective responsibility for child protection. 8. International Collaboration: Collaborate with national and international organizations and NGOs to leverage resources, share best practices, and access expertise in combating child labor and trafficking. 9. Child Helplines and Reporting Mechanisms: Implement and promote confidential reporting mechanisms for child protection concerns, providing a safe space for individuals to report incidents. 10. Monitoring and Evaluation: Regularly assess the impact of interventions, using data to refine strategies and ensure ongoing effectiveness in reducing child labor, trafficking, and illiteracy.
