Project Summary

The compelling need to support small businesses in Syria is evident in the profound economic challenges exacerbated by the protracted conflict and humanitarian crisis, impacting small and medium enterprises and resulting in the loss of livelihood sources for many families in these areas. The country's small enterprises, often the backbone of local economies, have been severely impacted which leads to job losses and financial instability for countless individuals and families. According to the HNO 2023, assessments and analyses conducted across all of Syria continue to show a general deterioration in access to livelihoods and the ability to meet basic needs due to increased inflation and a growing gap between income and spending. Livelihoods are reported to be among the top three priority needs of families in all countries. The overwhelming demand for assistance is underscored by the significant role small businesses play in fostering economic growth, creating employment opportunities, and contributing to community resilience. This project proposal aims to address these pressing needs by providing targeted support to small businesses, including financial assistance, entrepreneurship training, and access to markets. By revitalizing these enterprises, the project seeks to inject vitality into the Syrian economy, empower local entrepreneurs, and foster a sense of economic stability that is essential for rebuilding communities and ensuring the long-term well-being of the Syrian people.
The project will support the establishment/restoration of MSME in the following governorates: Idleb (Khan Shaykun), Aleppo (Jebel Saman), Hama (As suqaylbiyah), Dar’a (Sheikh Misken), Homs (Al-Qusayr)and Rural Damascus (Duma, Nashabiya).
The targeted locations were identified through the following :1. Prioritize areas with a significant degree of needs, focusing on the livelihoods and early recovery sector severity, in addition to conducting thorough assessments to gauge the intensity of requirements in these regions.
2. SSSD Identified locations with an urgent demand for small businesses, The intervention will promote the recovery of the local labor market, and address immediate economic needs.
3. the prior experiences of SSSD team in implementing MSME activities. This selection aims to leverage insights gained from previous interventions, ensuring better quality implementation of the program. By integrating lessons learned, SSSD aims for a more effective impact.
4. The majority of the targeted areas include active community centers engaging SSSD teams with the local communities in various types of interventions, which will ensure not only efficient implementation but also will satisfy monitoring requirements and strong engagement of the local community as decision makers and main stakeholders.
The MSME (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises) grant will support the establishment/restoration of a wide range of potential economic activities including the production, services, and retail sectors.
-Production-related enterprises (e.g. small-scale manufacturing, and construction, etc.)
-Services-related enterprises (e.g. shoe repair, auto repairs, computer/mobile maintenance, food catering, tailoring, etc.)
-Retail-based enterprises (e.g. grocery stores, clothes shops, etc.)

0 - Project Summary (extended)

implementation of activities:1.Labor market assessment (LMA):SSSD team will perform LMA in the targeted areas, employing various tools, including focus group discussions (FGD), and key informant interviews (KIIs). This aims to discern the specific requirements, identifying pinpointing types of small business that are currently absent and still essential to the local community. 2.Initial Selection of beneficiaries for MSME will be based on consideration of their vulnerability as well as their existing skills and capabilities (e.g. previous experience of business ownership). In accordance with age, gender and diversity (AGD) approach. 3.Entrepreneurship training:The ET will be composed of several sessions to a group of individuals with considerations to exchange experience among selected trainees and it is expected to incorporate the following key elements:• Basic accounting and financial management• Risk Assessment.• Marketing.• Developing business feasibility/project proposal.4.Final interviews and selection of accepted projects:SSSD will establish a Selection Committee to review and approve each MSME. The Selection Committee/Panel will include the relevant MSME Focal Point and SSSD MSME Coordinator and/or the Project Manager. 5.Items procurement by the beneficiaries and delivery of documentation:Delivery of MSME will be through cash modality, and over two transfers per MSME.Each awarded beneficiary will prepare, with the support of the MSME trainer, a business plan that will include the list of all items and tools needed to start his small business.MSME FP will request the price bids for all items and tools each beneficiary requests.The second transfer will not be issued unless the beneficiary provides the related receipts and required documentation for the items procured by the first cash transfer and confirmed by the MSME FP to be delivered.•Risks and assumptions: Risk: Financial Risks: Exchange rate fluctuations between the USD and SYP, Mitigation: Regularly monitor the exchange rate fluctuations and revisit MSME unit price for re-appropriation of budget .Risk: Lack of suppliers with identification documents and licenses in the targeted area. Mitigation: In the cash modality, the beneficiary will have the option to purchase from any supplier he/she chooses. Risk: Beneficiaries delay in procuring the tools and equipment after transferring the grant to them. . Mitigation: Will be avoided by transferring the fund in two payments and the second payment will be on hold until MSME FP verifies that the beneficiary has spent the first payment by procuring the tools and items needed to the business.PSEA strategy :All SSSD staff are trained on PSEA.they all signed Code of Conduct. SSSD regularly conduct refreshing sessions for staff on PSEA policy, and sensitize the community about PSEA and how to report misconduct. SSSD is working to ensure that all our suppliers and services providers are also compliant with PSAE and CoC principals.
