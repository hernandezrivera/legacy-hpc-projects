Project Summary

The population of the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon since 2016 have been undergoing crisis which has led to serious damages over the years, leading to violent armed clashes, loss of human lives, property, livelihoods, internal/external displacements and rising atmosphere of uncertainty amongst citizens living in the conflict-affected Regions. According to OCHA Cameroon Humanitarian Response plan, 2024, a total number of 1.140,300 people are in need of humanitarian assistance and protection.199,659 person are affected by the NW/SW crisis. 1M are internally displaced persons, 646K are returnees and 486K are Refugees and asylum seekers. about 0.5 million people are in need of House, Land and Property (HLP) services. 1.1 million people are in need of Shelter and NFIs and and about 1.1 million people are in need of Water, Sanitation and Hygiene promotion. The intentionally burning of housing affect almost 10,000 families in Cameroon. Severe SHELTER inadequacies are reported in Lagone et Charie, Bui, Donga-Mantung, Menchum, and Ndian Divisions. The persistent nature of the NW/SW crisis, new and repeated displacement, and insufficient humanitarian assistance are eroding people's already limited resilience. This is leading to an increase in negative coping mechanism including drugs abuse, child marriages and survival sex.
As is the case with any crisis, the crisis in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon has exposed the majority of its victims to life-threatening challenges especially non-communicable disease (NCDs) such as hypertension, diabetes, stroke, and mental disorder resulting from constant stress, anxiety, depression due to regular armed conflict in this area and generally unhealthy lifestyle imposed on them by their present predicaments. Since 2019, the Cameroon Baptist Convention (CBC) has been working in partnership with Mission 21, the Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC) and others, to provide interventions in Shelter/NFI distribution, HLP/ICLA sensitization, WASH/ Hygiene Promotion, Livelihood assistance to over 50,000 conflict-affected persons in the NW/SW regions of Cameroon. In 2023, the CBC implemented an 8-months project (July 2023 - February 2024) from the Norwegian Refugee Council funded by EU-NDICI to carry out several projects on HLP, Hygiene and Sanitation and Shelter/NFIs. This project targeted the distribution of WASH NFIs and Kitchen sets to the 650 vulnerable households in the four- communities in the NW region (Mbonso, Nwanti, Jakiri and Kumbo). 675 persons were targeted for Information, Counseling and Legal Assistance (ICLA) in all the project sites. 10,000 persons were targeted to be reached with hygiene promotion assistance (education and soap distribution).
The implementation of the NRC EU-NDICI project enabled the CBC to be aware of the dire humanitarian need of the targeted communities and surrounding communities. To response to these critical needs, this project seeks to: 1) provide life-saving shelter and non-food item (NFI) support; 2) improve sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water to vulnerable people; 3) improve the protection and respect of fundamental rights for persons affected by crises, prioritizing the most vulnerable groups and persons with specific needs.
The project shall accomplish these three-fold objectives through the following main activities in 6 targeted communities, three in the North West region (Bafut, Bali, Batibo) and three in the South West region (Nguti, Tinto, Ekondo Titi):
1. Provision of essential shelter and NFIs (including but not limited to: mattress, plastic utensils, blankets, jerry cans, kitchen sets, solar lamps) to 6,000 conflict-affected households.
2. Construction and rehabilitation of 20 water points/ water supply and 50 gender and disability sensitive latrines
3. Training of 600 local authorities and traditional leaders and civil society actors to effectively address HLP related disputes in the project targeted communities.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

According to OCHA 2024 Humanitarian Needs Overview, Cameroon has a total pin of 1.140,300, there is still high unmet needs in almost all the humanitarian thematic areas. However, the CBC has decided to focus on three thematic areas for this project: Shelter and NFIs, WASH and Protection in six conflict-affected communities in the NW/SW Regions with high humanitarian needs. These communities include three in the North West Region (Bafut, Bali and Batibo) and three in the South West Region (Nguti, Tinto and Ekondo Titi). In the domain of Shelter and NFIs, conflict-affected persons in the project targeted communities need humanitarian assistant in this area. The conflicted affected persons will be provided with shelter NFIs kit, comprising of household equipment including but not limited to mattress, Mosquito nets, plastic utilities, blankets, jerry cans, kitchen sets, solar lamps, etc. They also face challenges in paying or living in decent accommodations. People in these communities also need knowledge and skills in disaster risk reduction and construction related issues. In the domain of WASH, conflict-affected persons in the project targeted communities need water point for drinking portable water. The conflict-affected persons will be provided with low-cost sufficient water points following Sphere and WHO standard guide line for drinking portable water such as boreholes, small water supply network, etc. They also need gender and disability sensitive latrines. There is also a need for WASH kits to be distributed to boys, girls, men, women, families, persons with disability (PWD) and old people. Capacity-building of WASH actors in WASH relative issues is also needed with promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people. In the domain of HLP, individuals need to be provided with legal assistance so that they can exercise and enjoy their HLP rights. There is also a high need to provide individuals with counseling on HLP related issues. Sensitization of community, local authorities, and traditional leaders on HLP rights and obligations is also greatly needed. lastly, local authorities and traditional leaders and civil society actors should be train to effectively address HLP related disputes. To response to these critical needs, this project seeks to: 1) provide life-saving shelter NFI support; 2) improve sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water to vulnerable people; 3) improve the protection and respect of fundamental rights for persons affected by crises, prioritizing the most vulnerable groups and persons with specific needs; 4) Also persons affected by this crisis are in need of psychosocial support as they need to be impacted on positive coping mechanism. The project shall accomplish these three-fold objectives through the following main activities in 6 targeted communities:1. Provision of essential Shelter NFIs (including but not limited to: mattress, plastic sheeting, blankets, jerry cans, kitchen sets, solar lamps) to 6,000 conflict-affected households.2. Construction and rehabilitation of 20 water points/ water supply (boreholes and small water supply network with taps) and 50 gender and disability sensitive latrines. 3. Training of 600 local authorities and traditional leaders and civil society actors to effectively address HLP related disputes.
