Project Summary

This Project aim to support the population identified by the WASH sector HRP in area of Kubum locality whom currently estimated to be about 20,000 (4,080 Adults Females, 3,920 adults males, 12,000 children) people in needs. This project also will provide water and sanitation service as per WASH sector standard and strategies.

Needs

Kubum is one of the localities in South Darfur affected by the tribal conflict during recent years and therefore, the communities have moved away from their own villages for quite long time then their access to livelihood and other resources also negatively affected and as per HNO of this year, there are 20,000(4,080 Adults Females, 3,920 adults males, 12,000 children)people remained unserved and they are in need of Water and sanitation assistance. this proposed intervention will focus on enhancing the existing community structures at the locality and villages level to utilised their capacities in establishment of WASH program that could sustain and support the community ownership and resilience thus Community Led Total Saniation (CLTS) will be followed to eliminate the Open Defecation( OD)and as water access also one of the areas community have been reporting hence the project will focus on rehabilitation of the water sources beside the hygiene promotion actives as well.The issue of hygiene promotion specially the women and adolescent girls special needs(sanitary towels, soap for hand washing and disposal mechanisms) where women and girls seriously lack adequate facilities for menstrual hygiene management (MHM) including the unavailability of means to dispose of used sanitary pads and water to wash hands, means that women and girls face challenges in maintaining their menstrual hygiene in a private, safe and dignified manner.The project will also work on empowerment of Women girls to join the men in different WASH committees and participate in decision making while the project address many construction issues, this quite important therefore the project will train women, boys and girls together with men in managing the infrastructures that will be rehabilitated during the project including the whole steps of operation and maintenance.

Activities

1. Rehabilitation of water sources 2. construction of new water facilities with community participation and contribution. 3. water quality testing and monitoring. 4. latrines construction using CLTS approach. 5. provision of gender inclusive and age and disability friendly latrines. 6. enhance community solid waste management. 7. hygiene promotion at community and institutions. 8. pilot the RunAs approach in selective behaviour change. 9. training women and Adolescent girls on menstrual management and provide sanitary items. 10 Training of WASH committee including women, boys and girls . 11.Conduct KAP survey

Impact on environment/natural resources

During the assessment environmental issues will be clearly addressed and all the proposed of the project will be designed to friendly with the environment, the material use for latrines construction likely will contribute in deforestation thus the mitigation measures will be taken e.g to focus on seasonal tree that the ministry of forests and agriculture advise to use for these kind of facilities construction.The ground water discharge also may cause the depletion of the limited ground water thus the ground water monitoring will use and results will be use for optimum utilisation of the ground water.Solid waste management unless handled in good manner where the organics and inorganic materials segregated before any treatment then it will pollute the environment therefore this activity will be implemented as per WASH sector and MoH support.The project will document all these measure and work with line ministries and WASH sector and flag any issue may come on the surface.
