Project Summary

The proposed two years Project aims to improve the food security & livelihood status of most vulnerable people (linked to SO1, SO 2 and SO3 Food Sector 2022) through life-sustaining food assistance & income-generating opportunities with special focus on female-headed households, people with disabilities PWDs, elderly people, daily workers who lost their jobs during COVID-19 outbreak.
One of the project activities will be a food voucher distribution on 3600HHs (1800HHS per year) in tow locations in the highest severity scales locations categorized as 4 severity scale based on Food Sector Severity Ranking file in Hama and Deir-Ezzor.
The voucher amount is to WFP Minimum Expenditure Basket for 8 Cycles/Months for each year according to Food Security Sector minimum standard and Cash Working Group and in the purpose of Reducing coping Strategic Index of the targeted segment of society.
Rising unemployment rate is one of the most significant problems facing economies in the targeted project locations based on the need assessment conducted by TdH and its local partner, and income generation activities were one of the prioritize to the targeted communities as a long-term solution for poverty and lack of income, and based on the assessment results and conclusion, TdH will conduct specialized vocational training for 60 persons per year mainly for PwDs and Female-Headed households and daily workers who lost their work due to COVID-19 to Scale-up support to Market-driven Income-Generating Activities (IGAs) including vocational training based on beneficiary preferences, and this vocational trainings will be including agriculture topics , Also, and as an exit strategy to ensure the project impact on long term, TdH will distribute a livelihood kit after conducting the vocational training successfully .
The training topics will be identified based on the outputs of the conducted needs assessment in the targeted locations, also beneficiaries recommended list for the food vouchers distributions will be nominated by the community committees and verified through household visits by the volunteers in order to avoid any duplication with any humanitarian actors working in the same neighborhoods, and to ensure that the selected beneficiaries are meeting the vulnerability characteristics and criteria. Also, in order to improve localization for the humanitarian actors, the project aims also to build capacity in project staff and volunteers in different technical subjects to be able to provide the activities to beneficiaries based on the sector minimum standards and accountability methods, such as: COVID measurement, GBV, CP, data collection, need assessment , market assessment and PDM modality.
TdH will coordinate with other FSA actors to ensure there is no duplication in receiving the services.
TdH regularly participating to the cash working group, & we will share 5Ws report reflecting the achievements with food sector for more efficient & effectiveness in response & coordination.
All the activities will be supervised by TdH staff and will be carried out in coordination with the local partner to guarantee a continuous collaboration, also M&E staff will conduct field visits regularly, also, M&E officer will make active calls or personal interviews to a random sample of beneficiaries (20% of the targeted beneficiaries) to collect information, feedback from beneficiaries on how they feel about the project through PDM Process.
All the project activities will take COVID-19 Preventions measures in each step and empower the markets and training center in the targeted locations as well. All these activities will improve communities' resilience and capacity to sustain households’ livelihoods by improving and strengthen the capacity for the provision of essential services for local communities, in addition to the training component that will have a long term impact on humanitarian actors .

4 - Needs

Across Syria, hyperinflation and currency depreciation have significantly diminished households’ purchasing power,eroding the real value of salaries.Substantial harvest losses combined with a chronic economic deprivation have contributed to the mounting food insecurity as12.8Mpeople or almost 60%of the population, are food insecure– a57% increase since2019 and the highest number ever recorded in the history of Syria (FSLSector,HNO2022,DraftThis is extremely high and such families are unable to save and are barely surviving, as they live hand to mouth, with little hope in sight.The high food prices,coupled with lack of income-generating opportunities, will worsen the situationThe levels of acute food insecurity are projected to worsen in Syria in2022,due largely to the drought like conditions the country experienced in2021 and the subsequent poor harvest,shortages of essential inputs for farming combined with the overall economic crisis in the country, all of which reduce access to affordable food(FSA 2022-23 HRP Sector ChapterHHs headed by women have a poorer food security status than households headed by men.HHs with family members with disabilities have higher monthly expenditure than those without disabilities and only a Qtr of them report having sufficient income(WFP,SAR, Interim Country Strategic Plan,2022–2023Accordingly,TdH plans to distribute food vouchers to the most vulnerable and provide vocational training in Deir-ez-Zor and Hama,taking into account severity 4

5 - Implementation of activities

The proposed intervention is aligned with the SO1 ,SO2 and SO3, through systematic and sustained humanitarian response with ensuring immediate as well as consistent access to food to the most food-insecure people, thus the project activities will be in line with Food sector and the CWG priorities and recommendations as follows: Provision of food voucher assistance to food-insecure households in underserved locations with the highest severity levels of food insecurity for 8Cycles each year. Vocational training will be conducted for most vulnerable people according to the results of the assessment which will target PWDs & female-headed households in consulting with community committees in each location to provide them with specific Vocational Training where livelihood sector was reported as a priority needs in the mentioned communities. The targeted locations considered as a priority Food sector (4 Severity scale as HNO2022 draft). TdH will follow up closely the prices and variance in the market through regular and spot-check market assessment through the program staff and M&E officer to keep aligned with power of purchasing of the voucher and will coordinate with donor and local partner accordingly.
