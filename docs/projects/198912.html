Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to enable inclusive humanitarian assistance in the northen part of Mozambique. This purpose will be achieved not only scaling up advocacy efforts through the different layers of humanitarian actors but also improving coordination of persons with disabilities targeted interventions and building capacity of mainstream actors to implement activities that reach persons with disabilities adequately. The project is a response to reiterated complains about persons with disabilities, including women and girls, being left behind in different stages of humanitarian interventions or not having their specific needs identified and addressed adequately. Moreover, this project is an effort to place persons with disabilities and their representative organizations at the center of the humanitarian inclusion efforts, a good practice that has been proved in different contexts.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project aims to scale up advocacy and capacity building efforts regarding inclusion of persons with disabilities throughout humanitarian intervention initiatives. The project's purpose is to reduce the systematic barriers faced by different groups of persons with disabilities to access humanitarian support and the perceived inexistence of persons with disabilities in humanitarian spaces. Therefore, the project is an attempt to address cyclic report of discrimination and exclusion faced by persons with disabilities in different sectors and throughout the cycle of the humanitarian intervention. Moreover, the project builds on a proven good practice place organization of persons with disabilities at the center of the coordination, monitoring and capacity building efforts. Unlike previous projects, this intervention is led by organizations of persons with disabilities and inspired by previous successes in the same field. Therefore, the main target of the project is persons with disabilities, including, children and women affected by emergencies in the northern part of Mozambique.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

A - Advocate for inclusive emergency responses before Government duty beares, humanitarian angencies and all other relevant actors (Pemba - Nampula - Lichinga): (1) Produce technical notes; (2) Review working document and tools for inclusivity; (3) organize regular training sessions with mainstream humanitaria actors B - Coordinate interventions of the Protection Cluster Disability Working Group at national and local level (Pemba - Nampula - Lichinga): (1) Organize planning and reporting annual sessions of the DWG; (2) Conduct the day to day operation of the DWG, including regular meeting within and outside the Protection Cluster; (3) Define and update the operation strategy of the DWG C - Provide accessibility and inclusion technical support in facilities and services provided to persons affected by emergencies (Pemba - Nampula - Lichinga): (1) Conduct accessibly monitoring missions in facilities such transit center and resettlement centers; (2) Assist humanitarian actors to improve service delivery mechanisms for more inclusivity; (3) Organize regular trainings on inclusion and accessibility D - Raise awareness about disability inclusion (Pemba - Nampula - Lichinga): (1) Organize disabiliy rights awareness raising campaigns in emergency affected areas

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A risk assessment has been developed to make sure that the project included only activities that do not pose risk nor to the staff nor to beneficiaries, including contingency plans in case of any changes of circumstances on the field.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The projects core objective is to contribute to the use of a universal design approach in the conception and implementation of humanitarian interventions. This will benefit persons with disabilities and other groups that have specific accessibility need.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project is led by organizations of persons with disabilities, who have the finest expertise to identify persons with disabilities in the context of the implementation this project. The project has a strong component of barrier removal through capacity building, monitoring and collaborative interventions.
