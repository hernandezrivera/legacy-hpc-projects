Project Summary

The project aims to provide optimal access to quality education opportunities for 9000 children at risk of dropping out.
According HNO 2019 : There is 5,854,660 school age children in need to education in Syria.
We have chosen 31 different cities in Dar'a , Rural Damascus , Aleppo where there is 421,848 school age children in need to education(44% female , 55% males) , 512,113 IDPs , 106,893 Returnees , and the range of Education Severity 2019 is between 3 and 5 .
The areas in each citiy are :
In Dar'a : 15 areas (As-Sanamayn,Ghabagheb ,Masmiyyeh ,,Da'el,Dar'a,Jizeh,Kherbet ,Ghazala,Mseifra,Mzeireb, Hrak, Izra', Jasim ,Nawa ,Sheikh Miskine,Tassil )
In Aleppo :11 areas (Dayr Hafir, Rasm Haram El-Imam, As-Safira
, Banan, Hajeb, Khanaser, Nabul, Tall Refaat, Tall Ed-daman
Al-Khafsa, Maskana)
In Rural Damascus: 5 areas (Ghizlaniyyeh,,Harasta,Nashabiyeh, Arbin, Kafr Batna)Formal education in Syria is regularized and accredited by the MOE in both public and private schools.
Formal education has 2 types: curriculum “A" which is regular school and Curriculum "B" which is accelerated learning of 2 school years in one for those who are dropped out of school for more than 2 years.
The children at risk of dropping out are the children enrolled in formal education with an attendance rate of less than 60%, and children with low performance rate of less than 40% in at least 2 of the main subjects (Arabic, English, French, Math, and science). In addition to children participating in accelerated learning programme (Curriculum B) until they are enrolled in curriculum A in the appropriate age and level of education, and out of school children that re-enrolled in curriculum A due to unavailability to Curriculum B.
In this education program GOPA will provide remedial classes for children at risk of dropping out identified by the relevant DoEs , using the formal education materials with two categories of children :
The first : the children with the curriculum (B) program; a non-formal channel to strengthen children’s educational achievement and empower them to reintegrate into formal education curriculum (A). Coordination will be held with relevant DoEs to eventually following these targeted children to pass their exams in curriculum(B) and enroll in regular classes of curriculum(A)
The second: the children with the curriculum (A) program that are enrolled in formal education with an attendance rate of less than 60%, and children with low performance rate of less than 40% in at least 2 of the main subjects (Arabic, English, French, Math, and science) .
Total of 9000 children (8-18 years ) will be targeted in this project in the several areas in Daraa and Aleppo and Rural Damascus to promote access to formal education for vulnerable students (50% male and 50% female between 8-18 years of age), including through their reintegration into the good public education system (curriculum A) , and will conduct many events to promote the program and encourage the children to enroll in it. Furthermore, GOPA will provide the basic materials for this program in coordination with the MoE. One education focal point and one facilitator will be associated each 1000 children to supervise the implementation of educational sessions.
Each child will be provided by the following materials:
1- Learning materials (stationary, pens, notebooks).
2- transportation to school ( if necessary)
3- textbooks for children ( if necessary)
4--supplementary learning materials for schools which help children to good learning.

Implementation of activities

The program is divided into three main courses according to the academic year in Syria (The target will be divided into three groups in each city (two groups during the academic year, a group in the summer)1- The closest state school to the residence of most students at risk of drop-out will be chosen. The student will be determined according to the needs assessment conducted by GOPA-DERD. The approval of the Ministry of Education will be gotten to use these schools in conducting educational courses in it. 2-Students level exams will be used in sorting them into the appropriate grades.3- The program will include the following activities: -Remedial classes: This activity aims to support students to be able to follow the Syrian curriculum and to be prepared for mid-year and final exams . Main subjects to be covered are Math, Biology, Physics, Chemistry, and Languages, for 15 hours per week over a period of 3 months. Beneficiaries of this program are reported based on the number of students who completed the entire period of the course (3 months). -Homework café activity: will take place after schooling hours, 5 days a week, providing a suitable educational environment for the students to do their homework with the assistance of teachers.Qualified teachers with specific teaching skills will be employed to implement the education programme, and materials for remedial and exam classes and supplies will be delivered.The text books will be distributed to all children who cannot buy it (Extreme poverty is a key factor in child drop out because parents are unable to buy non-free textbooks in classrooms 7-9).Materials Notes:Notebooks - 1 notebook to each subject, Coloring pencil - For students from 1 to 7 school levels,Geometric tools - For students from 5 to 12 school levels,Books of the Syrian national curriculum - For out of school children and for 12th grade students,4- At the end of the courses,a test will be conducted to the students to determine their progress . *During the entire program period, the progress of students will be followed up in their official schools

Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: It is very common that the children would be exposed to any kind of harassment during their way to school especially that there are many destroyed houses in the area. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: GOPA will a ssign individuals to supervise the children, take the attendance, and follow up their situation. Protection Risk: It is most probably that there are mines in the area. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigating measures: GOPA will seek to obtain the necessary approvals to provide protection to the children in the chosen schools.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

-The fluctuation of USD exchange rate set a great challenge in implementing the educational activities. (low risk), Impact: Medium, Mitigating measures:A higher exchange rate of USD against the Syrian Pound, would led to a surplus that could be used in increasing the program’s target. In the contrary, the lower exchange rate of Dollar against the Syria Pound, we will attempt to reduce the expenditures to achieve the required target.Sudden security situation or displacement movement etc.. (low risk), Impact: Medium, Mitigating measures: GOPA will consult with sector about the shifting of planned activities to other provinces. Level of risk: Acceptable.
