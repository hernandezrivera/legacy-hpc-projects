Project Summary


This grant will bridged vital health gap on the ground matching with humanitarian response plan and cluster objective by providing lifesaving humanitarian health assistance for needy women and men giving extra attention for those elderly and people with disabilities. This crucial hospital is the only specialzed internal medicine hospital in all NWS and prioritized by health cluster. It will provide advanced and highly quality internal medicine services in Idleb city. These services have included outpatient services on daily basis except Friday. In addition to emergency internal medicine services 24/7 along with inpatient care and ICU services. This hospital has 8 ICU beds, 25 inpatient beds and 7 Emergency and first aid beds.
These internal medicine services were variety of specialized services as follow:
Cardiology, neurology, nephrology, Endoscopy, and gastrointestinal services and other NCD and CD diseases. The hospital have the following departments:
1-ICU
2-endoscopy unit including ERCP
3-emergency hemodialysis in ICU
4- outpatients clinics including: Cardiology, Neurology, internal services, Hepatology, and pulmonology
5-24/7 emergency department
6-emergency dialysis session in ICU
7-suplement services: radiology, Pharmacy and laboratory services
This hospital are playing a vital role in alleviating human suffering between IDPs and host communities within Syrian crisis due to fact that its services are Unique
This hospital is serving more than 311000 population and provided the 10100 medical consultation per month in average.
These services have been provided for targeted beneficiaries 24/7 regardless their ethnic or believes.
In addition to support five PHCs in line with urgent needs on the ground as follows:
Idleb, Gov, Harim district, Salqin district Azmarin community
Aleppo Gov Jebel Saman district, Atareb sub-district Abin
Idleb gov, Ariha district, sub-district Ariha, Orm Eljoz
Tow PHCs in Jisr Alshogour subdistricts: Besheria and Meshmshan
All of these PHCs have HERAMS Code.
Those PHCs will provide primary health care services including maternity and child health services, NCD and CD for targeted population. The services will include the outpatient services and temporary residency. They will provide 18000 medical consultations per month in average according to the last data that HIA collected.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- HIA will enhance the protection mainstreaming through several capacity building including PSEA by making sure that mentality of Syrian context will be taking into account. As such, all the nurses and midwives in the RH and child clinics will be female to ensure the acceptance and safe access.2- HIA will coordinate with health partners and access working group to be updated regarding the security aspects and make sure that the beneficiaries will continue have good access to health services.3- HIA will train the staff regarding the evacuation plan if any targeting happened: HIA directly suspend the work and inform the cluster and donors while the evacuation to other place will be prepared.4- if the health facility targeting HIA will suspend the work until the situation will be fixed while the incident report will be uploaded to the cluster and donors5- If the clashes between parties started, HIA will suspend the work to protect the staff and informed the donors 6- if any interferences happened through armed group on project payment HIA will directly report to donors and suspend the work and the contingency plan will be to replace the location of service delivery.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

HIA is an active member of NW Syria PSEA network and attends all meetings conducted by them.In order to prevent SEA, HIA promulgated a zero-tolerance policy to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) for all HIA’s employees and related personnel.Furthermore, The HIA's PSEA framework, affirms HIA's commitment to the UN Secretary General’s Bulletin on Special Measures for protection from sexual exploitation and sexual abuse (ST/SGB/2003/13) and to achieving full, ongoing implementation of the IASC Six Core Principles relating to SEA44. ​to create and maintain a safe environment, free from SEA, by taking appropriate measures for this purpose, internally and in the communities where HIA operates, through robust prevention and response work.In order to mitigate SEA cases, HIA has strong training, reporting, and awareness-raising policies in this regard.1- HIA systematically vets all prospective job candidates in accordance with established screening procedures.2- HIA holds mandatory induction and refresher training for all employees and related personnel on the Organization’s SEA policy and procedures.3- HIA has safe, confidential, and accessible mechanisms and procedures for personnel, beneficiaries, and communities, including children, to report SEA allegations and ensures that beneficiaries are aware of these.4- All HIA's contracts agreements include a standard clause requiring contractors, suppliers, consultants, and sub-partners to commit to a zero-tolerance policy


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Financial: - HIA will transfer money through PTT which is the only legal channel to protect fund – HIA will be in close contact with health staff to ensure that there is no interferences from any group in project payments Safety & security: -HIA will suspend the work if there is any targeting of the health facilities until the situation will be safe simultaneously, HIA will report directly to donors and health clusters. - if there is any clashes between armed parties, HIA will be in close contact with project staff making sure the safe work environment are in place for all field staff while the UNICEF will be updated in any case Operational: if any shortage of fuel, Or high price noticed, HIA will storage additional quantities to avoid work interruption of health and nutrition services depending on our stock Financial: if any delay in the project payment or fund, HIA will be in close contact with donors to inform them regarding this critical challenge while we will try to secure the fund from other donor Operational: if any new variant of COVID 19 spread, HIA from the beginning will ensure that all the staff are trained on IPC along with taking all the required measures: wearing Face masks, social distancingIf there is any increase in Fuel price HIA will storage the required amount to avoid any interruption in health services
