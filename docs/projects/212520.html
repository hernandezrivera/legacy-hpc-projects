Project Summary

• Needs.
At HNO 2024 The overall estimated Sector PIN is 15.4 million people. Agricultural production still does not meet the full national need. The drivers of low agriculture production include a continuous deteriorating economic situation, a decline in both the exchange rate and purchasing power of most people involved in agriculture-based livelihoods, high prices of agricultural inputs and low quality of available seeds, weather anomalies, water scarcity, destruction of irrigation systems and canals, scarcity of energy supplies, especially fuel, and electricity for various farm and off-farm activities, which impacts on supply chains and transportation costs, leading to an increase in production costs. The sector coordination project will play a vital role in ensuring timely, coherent, and effective food security and agriculture-related interventions, by mobilizing stakeholders to respond in a coordinated and strategic manner to the ongoing crisis in Syria. This will also help to provide partners with sound guidance on how to respond to the need in a technical sound manner and in an efficient way. FAO was asked to co-lead the sector and act as Coordinator for the FSA in the GCA, accordingly the need to provide the required financial support required for a better functioning of the coordination role to be played by FAO.
• Intervention, Implementation of activities.
Output 1: Accessibility of FSA information for stakeholders’ response planning improved. Activities
1.Support technical studies (HH Economy Analysis, CFSAM, IPC, ..etc) to support need analysis,
2.Provide input to (HNO) and develop cluster strategies (i.e. response plans) to guide cluster partners,
3.Setting standards indicators for monitoring the FS (e.g. the Outcome Monitoring Initiative),
4.Contribute to country level contingency planning and coordinate cluster/sector-wide emergency preparedness and response,
5.Monitor the implementation of the strategies and document lessons learned,
6.Developming regular FS reports covering the agriculture component of the sector, (Market bulletins, Agriculture seasonal bulletins, newsletters, etc.),
7.Participation in various coordination forums,
8. expand the sector support to sub-hubs to reach to all partners in Syria
Output 2: Capacity for national FSA Sector and AWG strengthened. Activities
1)Improve planning, agenda setting and time management of FSA and AWG meetings.
2)Regular information sharing and mapping of interventions to optimize complementarity of interventions, collaboration, and accountability.
3) Encourage stakeholders’ participation in FSA Cluster coordination platforms, including government actors, donors, and partners.
4)Strengthening institutional and technical capacity to support the functionality of FSA Sector,
Output 3: Capacity for subnational coordination of FSA sector built. Activities
1) Assign coordination experts at sub-national levels to co-lead the sector.
2) Assess coordination needs with relevant technical tools,
3) Train and mentor subnational sector/cluster coordinators, IM's and partners on technical guidelines, strategies, and cross-cutting themes.
* Protection Risk Analysis
