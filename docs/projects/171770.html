Project Summary

Combined impacts of devastating 2019 Deyr and 2020 Gu flooding and intermittent conflict has impacted to very high number of IDPs at the settlements in Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts as well as Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions where vulnerable IDPs and marginalized host communities live in congested unplanned settlements where they are exposed to high risk of violation of their rights. Their plight is exacerbated by increased exposure to violence, exploitation and discrimination to humanitarian assistance and other forms of abuse worsened by very serious risks and threats to their safety and security as well as negative coping strategies that expose them further to rights violation at the settlements. Of particular concern is the fact that children/boys and girls endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights including family separation, sexual and physical violence, killing and maiming, psychosocial distress, child labor, widespread recruitment and use of children, forced and protracted displacements, limited access to humanitarian assistance due to insecurity and/or discriminatory practices and exploitation. Furthermore, children/boys and girls are burdened with responsibilities at home that deny them opportunities of attending to schools while young girls are subjected to forced and early marriages whereas those with disabilities face additional risks of exploitation, abuse and discrimination and denial of the assistance provided hence need for their increased protection. It's against this background that the project has been formulated to enhance and ensure appropriate and timely protection services to children/boys and girls as well as those with disabilities in the project targeted locations in Lower Shabelle, Banadir Regions and Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts. To ensure durable solutions, the project will strengthen existing protection services by putting into account distinct protection needs of children/boys and girls as well as those with disabilities by consulting with them and including them in the project activities right from the project design face, throughout the implementation period including regular monitoring till project completion hence sustained impacts. The project targets total of 9180 beneficiaries/3990 boys and 5190 girls including those with disabilities.

Project Activities

1. Provision of recreational activities in targeted camps for psycho social support where targeted 7800 children (boys and girls) will be provided counseling therapy to overcome trauma and adopt the child friendly environment in Baidoa, Burhakaba, Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions2. Training of 60 Case Workers (male and female) on IDTR, case management, clinical management of rape in the project locations in Baidoa, Burhakaba, Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions3. Setting up of Child Protection Desks in Camps with Child attendants at standby for children by guiding and advising the children on appropriate action to ensure their safety and rights in Baidoa, Burhakaba, Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions4. Case management and referrals to other sectors by provision of Child Protection services based on CMR and SCA approaches in caring for child survivors 5. Provision of material support to enhance provision and access to essential services for 5000 child survivors at the project locations in Baidoa, Burhakaba, Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions6. Provision of specialized MHPSS and victim assistance services to children/boys and girls including those with disabilities at the project locations in Baidoa, Burhakaba, Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites such as guards as well as ensuring centrality and even distribution of sites for assistance to limit distance covered by women and girls as well as associated risks.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring beneficiary feedback and complains mechanism in place in addition to all staff signing PSEA code of conduct to eradicated SEA cases3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls on importance of their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as engaging them in the project right from the project start till completion including project regular monitoring exercises.5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
