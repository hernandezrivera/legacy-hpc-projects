Project Summary

According to HNO 2024, the food security situation in Syria continues to deteriorate. An estimated 15.5 million people (65% of the population) are in need of food or livelihood support, Or assistance from national safety nets. These estimates are based on a country-wide assessment of over 42,000 households which shows an increase in food insecurity compared to last year. At least 12.9 million people are in need of food assistance, if 2.1 million who live in camps are all food insecure, and a further 2.6 million are at risk of food insecurity. The driving factors of food insecurity are likely to remain in place in 2024 and if the required support is not provided there is a high probability that vulnerable people will fall into extreme poverty and that the number of people in need will increase further during the year. In addition, a scale up of food assistance and Agriculture assistance remains a priority.
The project aims to support families and farmers with food security and agricultural assistance. The most vulnerable people will be targeted with food baskets distribution while farmers will be targeted with appropriate agricultural interventions both in Idleb and Aleppo. The purpose has the following goal: 1) Supporting the most vulnerable populations IDPs camps in addition to the most vulnerable populations (IDPs).
HIHFAD will keep high level of coordination with FSL and CCCM clusters to ensure avoiding any potential overlaps in addition to keep the consideration for the coordination on the fi eld level.
2) Enhancing wheat value chain in two governorates (Idleb and Aleppo), and consequently enhancing food security in the target areas through distribution of agricultural inputs and training.
The project was built on achievements of the previous interventions of HIHFAD (irrigated farms and, a farmer association, agricultural inputs for the vulnerable households and free bread distributions) in the target areas.
The main activities are:
Distribution of cash for food, distribution of bread to IDPs in camps, supporting farmers with agricultural inputs, supporting households with small scale food production kits, rehabilitation of 15 bakeries and rehabilitation of irrigation system.
Protection Risks:
sexual abuse against women. Likelihood, medium. Mitigation measures : Team members will be males and females to contact women. All the team members will be trained on the code of conduct and PSEA. Effective complaint mechanism to receive feedback from the ground.
- Risk of discrimination and excluding of females from beneficiaries list: HIHFAD will apply clear selection criteria and prioritize female headed households and most vulnerable persons including PWD.
In accessibility of female headed households
Risks:
increase of Fuel Prices, Mitigation: - HIHFAD will sign a contract with fixed prices with Mills, bakeries and trucks from the beginning of the project based on general national tender. If Fuel prices increased significantly, in this case HIHFAD will try to import the fuel from Turkey. -Coordinate with the FSL Cluster and done to adapt the quantities according to the new prices.
Drought or low rain during winter: HIHFAD will follow up on the rain rate and provide irrigation cost whenever necessary, training on best practices on irrigation will also be conducted.
