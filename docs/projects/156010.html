Project Summary

Supporting livelihoods on both households and communities’ levels through the emphasis of the activities will be on supporting the communities’ own efforts at self-recovery and covering critical gaps in attaining decent livelihoods opportunities, access to basic services and repairing the social fabric. to strengthen the ability and capacities of the communities and households to reduce their vulnerability to the crisis-induced stresses and to mitigate the impacts of the current and future crises with a view to pursuing an integrated mode of resilience building.
Some communities have been surviving on life-saving humanitarian supplies for eight years and the likelihood of aid-dependency is quite high. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

During the project implementation all the targeted beneficiaries will be ensured equal access to the project intervention. They will be duly informed about the income generating activities inputs will be technical support provided. The necessary arrangements for each specific targeted group will be ensured (elderly, women etc.). Do No Harm will be a guiding principle for the project teams. For this purpose, they will be trained on humanitarian principle, human rights and other approaches that will ensure the successful implementation of the planned activities. In case any protection incidents occur during the implementation process, they will be duly reported to stakeholders. A focus on building partnerships with communities, beyond the direct provision of aid inputs, is a guiding principle in all work RF do.RF implements a framework for Accountability to Affected Populations that prioritizes leadership/governance; transparency; feedback and complaints; participation of communities and beneficiaries; and a design, monitoring and evaluation approach that engages affected populations and feeds learning back into the organization. Do No Harm framework that RF adopt holds the safety of staff and beneficiaries, as well as accountability to beneficiaries, as key operating principles. RF will promote the dignity, agency, and safety of beneficiaries at all times. Protection considerations – mitigating the risk of harm, abuse, discrimination, exploitation, and neglect of beneficiaries – will be overriding components of all activities as well as a specific objective of some activities, which will be implemented in line with SPHERE guidelines and other international standards. Beneficiaries selection is based on a community mobilization mechanism where the criteria of selection shared with all stakeholders and the project advertisements will be published in the community. After conducting introductory workshops for community to explain the project outputs. Also, the project will complement the input activities that are targeting the same intervention areas. RF has a community feedback mechanism to receive all complains from local community. Giving the vulnerable households the ability to resilience will increase the opportunities of children to learn. RF through the field team will advocate to this among the targeted beneficiaries and further data will be collected in the endline to have indicators.
