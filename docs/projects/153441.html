Project Summary

The project proposal is a one year Emergency Food security and livelihood support intervention with promotion of appropriate small scale resilience activities. The implementation period begins in January, 2019 through to December, 2019i The immediate objective is to enable vulnerable households to have access to food through their own production. The long term objective is to protect vulnerable households from shocks that will affect their ability to access food .The objectives are in line with HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2 and FSL cluster objective 4
To deliver the required results, the key program strategy is to throughdirect distribution of inputs, input trade fairs , training vaccination and treatment. To ensure Accountability to affected population, a committee would be formed to address these issues . The project will target mainly vulnerable households in host and IDP areas .60,000 vulnerable people will be supported mainly from Aweil West and Tonj South Women, boys and children that are mainly at high protection risk will be the main targets

Needs

The devastating war which erupted in December2013 has now become one of the greatest Humanitarian crisis in Africa. .The war has led to internal displacementof 2.1 million people ; 1.95 million as refugees WFP South Sudan Humanitarian Situation report #200. The Humanitarian situation continues to deteoriate with no significant improvement. The most recent IPC report in September, 2018 indicates that morethan half the population is facing hunger and starvation with an estimated 6.1 million people (59% of the total population2) faced Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity3 at the peak of the lean season (July – August), of whom 47,000 were in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5) and 1.7 million were in Emergency (IPC Phase 4).The severity of the food insecurity is mainly driven by the ongoing conflict which have affected crop production, led to loss of livelihoods, hyperinflation and soaring market prices, disruption of markets and trade routes . There are also other related disasters that have affected people’s access to food such as flooding, the incidence of fall arm worm reported all over the Country including Aweil where the severity is high. FEWSNET outlook (August 2017) report indicates that at the household level, food availability is now expected to be lower than previously projected. The report further indicates that national production is expected to the below five year average, which will led to low domestic supply in 2018 and a continuation of extremely high prices.CFSAM 2016 report that the conflict has disrupted natural resource based livelihoods and the 2016 cereal deficit of 500,000 MT is expected to be worse in 2017 and 2018, especially with the warring factions in the Greater Equatoria Region and the formation of the new rebel groups in Aweil. In Aweil West according FSNMS Round 22 report, the key shocks affecting food security are flooding(affected 4203 HHs (21032 individuals) ;assets lost includes houses, livestock (127 goats and 13 chickens, (FSL partners report, Aug 2018); Fall Army Worm which caused moderate level 54% of destruction reducing seasonal cereal yields; Millipedes reported to have caused mild damage to sorghum crops (FSNMS R22, July/Aug 2018); dry spell experienced in May and June, Mmost vulnerable HH lost cattle(a total of 88% HHs sold or slaughtered animal); Asset depletion of 66% emergency coping strategies. The Food consumption score is in Phase 4 affecting mainly poor households; household hunger scale is in Phase 3, Household dietary diversity in phase 4 and livelihood coping strategy in phase 4 . The GAM Prevalenceis in Phase 3 and at GAM level of 14.7%. Similarly, in Tonj South, the key hazards that affected food security are the eeconomic hardship compounded by continued inflation, Pests and diseases, dry spell, Fall Army Worm, livestock diseases outbreak ((Anthrax, CBPP, BQ, HS, PPR and Newcastle and Human sickness. 33% of households reported decreased income over the past year; 8% HHs reported pests and diseases,89% HHs reported their crops affected by dry spell, 37% HHs reported FAW destructions on cereals crop (maize, sorghum), 44% moderate and 46% slight,57% of HHs reported decrease in livestock due to disease outbreak and 94% HHs reported member sick of which 97% of malaria + poor HH diet + poor WASH. Food consumption score is in Phase 4, household diversity is in Phase 4 and GAM Prevalence in Phase 3 of GAM 11.4%. The above evidence indicates not only the magnitude of the food insecurity in the counties also shows the need to address the Food insecurity problems before it erodes into a catastrophic situation. Addressing the food security needs of these two counties in the two state is very much crucial. This could be addressed through livelihood support and support to protect livestock

Activities

Key activities that should be undertaken to address these problems are:Formation and training of Accountability to affected Population committeeDistribution of main agricultural season crops seeds , vegetable and fishing Kits and input trade fairs Distribution of lean season dry season vegetable seeds, tools , fishing kitsTraining of farmers in FAW surveillance and detection and prevention, improved vegetable production, net making and fish preservationVaccination and treatment of livestocks
