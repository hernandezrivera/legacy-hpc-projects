Project Summary

This project aims to provide the educational services to children with educational problems, such as school dropout or students who were unable to go to school earlier, students who are unable to read and write of both certificational ages i.e. ninth and twelfth grade. Students who find it difficult to understand their essential school subjects as well.
This project will provide educational services to 800 students and 100 teachers during the year of 2022, and 800 students and 150 teachers during the year of 2023.
Targeted areas are: Kafar Batna, Haza, Hamuria, Gisreen, Alaftarees, Alashaary, Saqba, Beit Sawa, Ein Tarma, Al Mohammadia, Darya, and Qudsaya.
CRS, the Child Right Society, has had a good experience in the fields of education since 2006. It also has had many partnerships with the UNICEF since 2015. And through its distribution in Damascus countryside (Eastern Ghouta centers - Qudsaya centers), CRS could afford its educational services to approximately 5000 children.
The centers of CRS will be used to serve this project and the following standards will be taken into consideration: low academic achievement, low standards of protection, low standard of living for the family, children mental health, residence state, displacement and returnees, people with disabilities and those without family care and juvenile.
Educational programs will be approved in line with the education sector's response plan, and the state curriculum.

4 - Needs

During the provision of educational provided by CRS, there noticed that most of the children in Eastern Ghouta have educational problems. In addition, children without parental care and juveniles, and high rates of school dropout which rates about 96% of children without family care and juveniles, and about 22% of children in Eastern Ghouta.A decrease in reading and writing skills was also observed for 91% of children enrolled in formal education in public schools in Eastern Ghouta, the poor teaching environment from teaching aids and the educational stimuli more than 90%, the spread of school violence, and the low ability of mothers to help their child in educational advancement. Low teaching performance and teachers' communication skills as well.

5 - Implementation of activities

The centers of the CRS will be equipped with 3 classrooms in Eastern Ghouta, 2 classrooms in Darayya, and 4 classrooms in the centers for those without family care and juveniles in Qudsaya. The classrooms will be equipped with tablets through which stories for early childhood children will be presented. They start hearing the story then read it. After that, there will be related questions to ensure their understanding and comprehension.Moreover, the intelligence games that allow the senses to move on and develop thought in a way that serves the educational process and the development of the child's personality helps facilitate the delivery of the idea and attract the child. All of these will be used with all children included in this project. Days of the week will be allocated as following: a day for science, music, drawing, theater, mathematics and mental arithmetic, sports, and time will be devoted each day to reading story and writing in both languages Arabic and English.Mothers will also be empowered with mechanisms to help their child advance in academic achievement.Educational services for the certificates (ninth and baccalaureate) will be provided in the form of intensive sessions in mathematics, physics, chemistry and both languages (Arabic and English).An evaluation of the quality of services provided will be conducted by specialists, in addition to evaluating the beneficiaries' development through pre- and post-tests, and periodic tests.
