Project Summary

The project seeks to alleviate poverty through training of farmers, provision of seeds, tools and start-up kits using new farming techniques, and improve young women skills on entrepreneurship. The goal is to reduce poverty and improve livelihood of the most vulnerable groups in the society. The Project will reach 5000 farmers of 30 women per group in Rubkona in Unity State. The project only focuses to reach the vulnerable groups such as widows, returnees, single mothers and disable people.

1. Needs

When fighting broke out in Sudan in April 2023, thousands of people fled into neighboring countries including South Sudan. Among the thousands fleeing into South Sudan are nationals returning through Panakuach as their transit point, with some settling in Rotriak because many villages in Unity are still flooded, or they do not have the means for onward traveling to other parts of the country. The government has allocated land in Block 17 of the Rotriak settlements for Returnees who choose to remain. From 20th April 2023 to date, IOM and UNCHR in collaboration with other UN agencies, INGOs & NGOs, formed a taskforce stationed at Heglig/Payangai (Panakuach) border to track, verify and register returnees and refugees upon arrival. The returnees are then immediately transported to Rotriak and refugees to Ajoungthok (Pamir) refugee camps. APEBA with other partners recorded over 20,000 Returnees have been transported from Payangai/Panakuach border to Rotriak. According to local authorities in Rotriak, some of the returnees have sent their families to relatives in other counties, either to settle or with intention to return to settle in Rotriak once they have constructed shelter there. These families experience difficult challenges as getting food and other basic amenities remains a serious challenge. The government provided small quantities of Sorghum, which was distributed to the returnees who arrive daily, but this support is insufficient and IDPs/host population do not have the capacity to support the needs of the returnees, as they are themselves vulnerable and in need of assistance. The main needs identified are food, nutrition and health services, Shelter and NFIs, Clean water and sanitation, core relief items for women and girls. The most critical recommendation from the Assessment Team is to provide immediate light humanitarian response including food, nutrition services, shelter materials and NFIs, health services, critical protection items especially dignity kits for women and girls, seeds and tools, advocacy with Government to protect the returnees from robbery and theft, increase clean water sources in Rotriak.

2. Project activities

APEBA as food security partner seeks to response to this critical need by doing the following activities. 1- community / beneficiaries mobilization, APEBA will works with community leaders and stakeholders to identify and register the returnees along with the host communities in Rubkona especially from the transit areas. This activity target 5, 000 beneficiaries mostly women, 2500 returnees, 2000 IDPs and 500 host communities. On each category 20% be people with especial needs, elderly, disables, widows among others. A special form be designed to capture and document their registration through local chiefs and of course in partnership with protection partners while doing the selection of the beneficiaries. 2. Provision of tools, after registration of the identified beneficiaries and identify those with farming ideas and trains them such that they can be provided with farming tools. the group would the advantage available water sources while it could be easy to grow some vegetables for their family needs,3,000 women groups would be supported under this activity and be guided on farming and fishing techniques and be trained on farming techniques, fishing skills including drying and preservation methods for those engaged in fishing. 3. Provision of seeds, the organized women farmers will be provided seeds of different varieties that are environmentally adaptive and can withstand flood pressure. in response to flooding impact, rice being seen as new farming that have good yielding and as such APEBA will training 1000 farmers on rice farming and the other seeds be distributed to the different groups with interest on vegetables, and others crops that can easily provide the best nutrients for the children and families. 4. Training of farmers, this activity will focus on building the capacity of the women farmers before providing them with the require tools and seeds. the training will be in three forms, (1,500 returnees, 1000 IDPs and 250 host) on entrepreneurship training, 1000 returnees, 500 IDPs and 200 host) on farming techniques and (500 IDPs and 50 host) on preservation techniques for those on fishing approach.
