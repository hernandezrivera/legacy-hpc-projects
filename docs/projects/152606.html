Project Summary

This project intends to provide integrated emergency lifesaving gender based violence (GBV) services to the vulnerable conflict affected population of Yei, Lainya, Morobo and outskirts of Juba in Central Equatoria State to reduce suffering of women, girls, men and boys.
The main project activities include; provision of comprehensive case management, Provision of psychosocial support (PSS) to GBV survivors and to unaccompanied and separated children (UASC). Safe referrals, including information dissemination on available services, provision of Legal awareness, assistance and counselling for survivors (including training police and justice. Establish Women & girls friendly spaces programming, including strengthened integration with child protection to support child survivors. These friendly spaces enable women to get together and discuss issues affecting them and use the centre as a place to conduct their IGA activities and get informal education on numerals. In this intervention child protection is integrated to the friendly spaces with an aim of supporting child survivors.
Conduct community-based protection activity through establishment of women safety committee. These group will be strengthened in protection coordination structures, empowering them with capacity on how to resolve GBV risks in their communities and to strengthen community referral system. Women led organizations will be strengthen in coordination structures. Community awareness raising and sensitization on women’s rights, the project will continue raising awareness in the community on women rights to participate in households and public decision making and rights to access services including freedom of movement among others, this can be done in social public spaces such as schools, markets, freedom rallies or important events, churches and through radio talk shows.
The project will ensure provision of protective livelihood programming for women. Through capacity building in the friendly space, women will be able to come up with income generating activities to support their household income which will further give them rights in the household as they also contribute to supplement what their husbands provide. In friendly space, women will discuss risks to them in terms of GBV while carrying their project and jointly work to mitigate the risks that might expose them.
Empower male youths to participate actively in raising awareness to the wider communities in the target locations on women rights. Conducting gender analysis of needs and aspirations of female and male youth in local community. By doing this, it will transport discriminatory attitudes and behaviours that contribute to inequality. Finally the project will engage women to activity participate in peacebuilding by conducting gender analysis of community relations and norms affecting peaceful co-existence
This project directly targets a total 30,000 vulnerable IDPs, returnees, Host communities and other group of people comprising trainers, promoter in Lainya, Yei, Morobo and Juba of which 60% (18000) people are IDPs, 25% (7500) people in the host, 10% (3000) are returnees and 5 % are the service providers including promoters of GBV services, caretakers who will be involved directly in the implementation of the project.

Needs

An estimated two million people remain internally displaced in South Sudan, many of whom have been displaced more than once. In Central Equatoria, July 2016 crises has led into destruction of life and properties leaving the situation dire. Aftermath of the crises often affect women’s lives and children in ways that differ from men, especially Young women are specifically targeted and suffered sexual abuse and violence and children are separated from their families, while the direct impact of violence falls primarily on young men who comprise the majority of fighting forces. The recent renewal of armed clashes in Central Equatoria that led to displacement of people have placed Women and girls in extreme vulnerability and at increased risk of gender-based violence. Counties affected are Yei, Lainya, Morobo, and Kajokeji. In these Counties, Gender based violence (GBV) issues such as Physical violence, sexual violence and domestic violence are of high concern due to the conflict and the worsening economic situation. Women and girls were sexually abused when fetching water, collecting vegetables and firewood. Juba County is also affected by these conflicts as many displaced people from neighboring counties came to settle in Juba. According to April-Jun 2018 REACH report state that, ” in Morobo 79% of the assessed population reported that they assess water within 30 minutes to one hours and for Central Equatoria state, the main protection concern reported by assessed settlements for both men and women in June was looting 21% and 19% of assessed settlements respectively. However, among women this concern was immediately followed by sexual violence (also 19%) and domestic violence (11%). ARDF had conducted Rapid needs assessment to identify protection gaps in these Counties. From the three (3) focus group discussion (FGD) conducted, it was found out that Forced and early marriages as a coping mechanism during conflicts is happening. Additionally domestic violence is the common practice in communities of these counties. The interviewees responded that their tradition accept men to beat their wives if they misbehave. Findings further underline weak traditional judiciary systems in these communities which does not resolved domestic violence fairly, ending up ignoring survivor’s rights. Similarly women feel shy when coming to sexual violence issues. Women have no rights for decision-making in the household, and are not also allowed by their husbands to participant in community led activities such as peacebuilding and democratic rights. Women have no say to issues concerning force and early marriages since women rights are not considered. Poverty, low economy and low education also contribute to domestic violence as women have no source of income that have can give them negotiation power in the households. In this intervention, ARDF will uses an integrated and holistic approach in the provision of Emergency lifesaving GBV prevention services to conflict affected communities of Yei, Morobo, Lainya and Juba. ARDF will look into deeply rooted social norms and problems that lead to GBV and seeks to address them before occurring, hence lead to the investment in younger population of male and female youth, younger women and adolescent school girls and boys. Moreover, there is needed to engage conflict affected communities in breaking the existing cultural and social norms which places women and girls at vulnerable situations, for example early marriage among girls, preference of the boy child as opposed to their female counterparts and negative attitude towards single female headed households. Additionally, there is need to empower young women, adolescent girls and youth to actively participate in peace building and transformation of discriminatory behaviors and attitudes that contributes to inequality

Activities

The following activities will be conducted in this project:1. Provision of comprehensive case management to gender bases violence survivors. This can be done by qualified providers trained by the project to manage GBV survivors. ARDF will strengthen the referral system in the communities for sexual gender based violence (SGBV) survivors. Other forms of GBVs can be managed in friendly spaces.2. Provision of psychosocial support (PSS) to GBV survivors and children who are unaccompanied and separated from their parents3. Conduct legal awareness, assistance and counselling for the survivors through training of police and justice authorities. Survivor will be counselled and supported on how to access their rights. Besides, Women & girls friendly spaces programming, including strengthened integration with child protection to support child survivors will be established. Confidentiality is maintain here. Project will support survivors to access their rights. 4. Project will establish women safety committees and strengthen this groups with knowledge in protection coordination structures. This activity is a community based protection strategy that will ensure protection concerns in form of GBV and child protection are identified and responded to. These groups will strengthen the community referral pathways to GBV survivors. The groups will be empowered to conduct meetings in order to provide update and identify GBV issues in the community which in turn can be used to raise awareness.5. Conduct community awareness and sensitization on women’s rights. These awareness can be done in social public spaces such as churches, schools, and markets. Additionally, the project will conduct radio talk shows to reach the wider coverage with messages on women’s rights and also out reaches to raise the awareness. Women committees/ Groups established will also be tasked to carry out awareness to their various communities.6. This project will also improve the livelihood of the affected women through provision of protective livelihood program for women such as empowering them with knowledge on how to conduct income generating activities (IGA), small scale business, Village saving loans associations (VSLA), Hair netting and growing of kitchen/household vegetable production which will be sold to increase on their income7. Conduct gender analysis of needs and aspirations of female and male youth in local community. The project will empower girls and youth women on self-esteems and to continue raising awareness on women rights. Male youths will be trained to continue providing awareness to the community this is to transform discriminatory attitudes and behaviours that contribute to inequality, negative masculinities, and accountable practices and women rights8. Finally the project will empower women to actively participate in peacebuilding in the communities. Through the established women groups and women’s organizations, the project will build their capacity on mediation and peaceful resolution of conflicts. Women groups and women leaders will be trained. Involving women in decision-making in Community Dispute Resolution, conflict mitigation, mediation & peacebuilding; Conduct gender analysis of community relations and norms affecting peaceful co-existence
