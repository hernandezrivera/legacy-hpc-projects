Project Summary

Oxfam and partners CLDH and Utopia have come together to propose an intervention that offers vulnerable migrants in Beirut, Mount Lebanon and North Lebanon comprehensive services with the objective of improving the overall protection environment. The three partners will adopt a community-based protection approach to ensure capacity strengthening and ownership over decision making within the migrant community. Oxfam, CLDH and Utopia will work with migrant informal community structures, where they exist, and support the establishment of migrant community structures where possible. While the intervention serves to address immediate needs of the community, the aforementioned support to community structures aims at introducing building blocks for more sustainable solutions and community independence.
The proposed project addresses all four outcomes of the Migrant Sector’s priorities for the ERP in 2023 and is line with Oxfam and partners’ country strategies and operating

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The socio-economic crisis has exacerbated preexisting vulnerabilities and amplified protection concerns for migrants and inhibited their ability to enjoy their basic rights. Protection risks will be monitored throughout the implementation of the project. There are risks of violence and exploitation by property owners, employers, market owners, community focal points etc. for migrants perceived or known to be receiving assistance, particularly if other community groups are excluded from accessing the service. There are risks of sexual harassment and abuse by service providers. There are risks of unequal access to assistance and discrimination in aid provision which could be triggered by accessibility barriers or lack of civil documentation. Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed; 2. 3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Oxfam applies an APP Framework that foresees multiple channels (in person, email, phone, website) for the community in order to report issues of conduct, safeguarding, and fraud related to Oxfam’s work. Oxfam maintains a national Community Response and Feedback (CRF) phone hotline that community members can use to give anonymous complaints and feedback. All project staff is trained to receive community feedback and enter it into Oxfam’s digital dashboard. Additionally, community protection focal points and community structures receive training on accountability mechanisms, safe identification and referrals, and safeguarding. Sensitive complaints (non-safeguarding) are shared and discussed with senior management. Safeguarding and fraud-related issues are addressed by Oxfam’s Safeguarding Focal Points and are investigated by trained safeguarding investigators. Communities and other stakeholders can also log complaints in a whistleblowing hotline run by a third-party organization.
