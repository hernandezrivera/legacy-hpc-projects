Project Summary

This project is to reduce food and livelihood insecurity of the extremely vulnerable 27000 persons through provision of agriculture & livestock inputs and livelihood in localities of Bindisi 9000 persons, Mukjar 9000 persons & Um Dukhun 9000 persons in Central Darfur State where duration of the project is 1 year.
SORR target of 27000 people among the total 106600 people in need and sector target total of 100882 people in the 3 localities above which will receive agriculture inputs and extension services as well as veterinary services that includes animal health services (including vaccination and treatment) and training of community animal health worker. Support return communities with agro-enterprise training to raise their marketing skills and diversify livelihood opportunities. Vocational training or business skills small targeting women in order to open market opportunity for them and generate new employment opportunities. Natural Resources Management to maintain environment .SORR will coordinate with community leaders, HAC, line Ministries and other stakeholders for project start up. HAC shall support SORR in the project implementation smoothly and successfully as they will facilitate in signing of Technical Agreement and travel notification. Currently there are limited humanitarian interventions in the targeted localities where SORR through the project developed will not cover the whole people who are in need from the sector targeted .SORR has valid registration and presence in Central Darfur State and well acquainted with the communities base on the previous experiences.

1 - Needs

IPC analysis (June – August 2019), estimated 6.3 million individuals are in need of food and livelihoods assistance to mitigate food insecurity. Central Darfur State among the States classified under Phase 3 and above, which reported a significant increase of food insecure people compared to previous analysis.FEWS NET August 2019 identifies population affected by food insecurity that is to say chronic food insecurity, livelihood and stability due to prolonged conflicts, environmental deterioration and other disasters such as a drought and floods.FEWS NET November 2019, the Sudanese pounds had continued to depreciate on a parallel market, reaching 82 SDG per 1 USD in November compare to 69 SDG per 1 USD in September and 76.5 SDG per 1 USD in October. This continues to to drive increases in prices of essentials, imported commodities such as wheat flour, medicine, fuel and agricultural inputs. In September 2019 price of wheat flour reached 48 SDG per kilogram compare to 45 SDG per kilogram in October. Overall wheat flour prices are 68 percent high than last year 2018.The scope and magnitude of this food insecurity crisis is well illustrated in the HNO 2019 which estimated 363822 people in need of food security & livelihood and targeted Food Security and Livelihood sector a total number of food insecure people in Central Darfur State estimated 343817.This project shall target three localities of Bindisi, Mukjar and Um Dukhun in Central Darfur State. In the localities above there are a needs of provision of agriculture inputs and extension services as well as veterinary services that includes animal health services (including vaccination and de-worming) and training of community animal health worker in order to support returned communities with agro-enterprise training to raise their marketing skills and diversify livelihood opportunities. Vocational training targeting women and youth to generate new employment opportunities including Natural Resources Management to maintain environment. Women traditional in Darfur are involved in firewood collection and making of charcoal for domestic use in urban and rural areas to enhance their livelihood and purchasing power which cripples forest and deteriorate the environmental patterns. In order to reduce negative impact on environment 60 % of women will be targeted and train on improved fuel efficiency stove and demonstrate use of brickets, bio fuel & bio gas using agriculture and livestock waste that can reduces forest destruction. The exposure women and ladies to risk of fetching firewood and making charcoal will reduce where it will give chance for forest regeneration, maintenance of natural resources and usage of solar power for water pump by small scale winter season vegetable producer which are environmentally friendly. In this project SORR will involve the community leaders and beneficiaries during implementation.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Activity: 1.1 Provision and distribution of agricultural inputs (seeds, tools and services) to 1500 HH Activity: 1.2 Distribution of agricultural tools to 50% of 1500 HH mostly female headed HH and persons with specific needs Activity: 1.3 Training of 60 agricultural village committee on extension services (Activity description: Communities mobilization & sensitization will be conducted, selection & registrations of the beneficiaries will be finalize with help of community leaders at the project site, tools and seeds required will be identified, distributions of seeds & tools to the targeted beneficiaries and training of VAC will be conducted).Activity: 2.1 Provision of livestock inputs (vaccines, drugs and services) to 45000 heads of animals(1500HH) Activity: 2.2 Restocking 300 female headed households with 900 milking goats Activity: 2.3 Refresher training of 30 Community Animal Health Workers (CAHWs). (Activity description: Communities mobilization & sensitization will be conducted, selection & registrations of the beneficiaries will be done at the project site, vaccines & drugs will be procured, training of CAHWs, vaccination & treatment of animals).Activity 3.1 Promotion of community awareness on regeneration of forests and fuel efficient stoves (FES) Activity 3.2 Procure and distribution of 150 FES frames Activity 3.3 Training of 600 women on FES Activity 3.5 Training of 300 women on non-wood forest products processing, packing, storage and marketing. Activity 3.6 Training of 300 vulnerable women on small business skills for the targeted beneficiaries (Activity description: Communities mobilization & sensitization will be conducted where selection & registrations of the beneficiaries will be done at the project site, provision of IGAs for skills enhancement and training on quality improvement and protection of environment).
