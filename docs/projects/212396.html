Project Summary

As HNO 2024,the escalating challenges in education resulting from the protracted crisis and the earthquake, have impacted 7.1 million school-aged children and teachers with a 4% increase in PIN compared to 2023 need urgent aid including repairing 3,700 schools and addressing the shortage of qualified teachers. Also, the shortage of qualified teachers worsen the situation,underscoring the necessity for interventions to ensure access to quality education and support for vulnerable children, including those with disabilities, orphans, and displaced individuals.Key interventions include re-enrolling 2.45M out-of-school children into safe, inclusive settings, focusing on early childhood development (ECD) and education,enhancing caregiver interactions,and promoting child well-being. Additional priorities cover support for adolescents transitioning to secondary education, assistance for children with disabilities or psychosocial challenges, and providing technical and psychosocial support to teachers and parents.In recent years AKF has been actively implementing its"Sustainable Model for Improved Learning Environment (SMILE)" initiative to address educational needs across different areas.In 2023 AKF has supported around 24,800 individuals placing a strong emphasis on education as a crucial lifeline for children and families during crisis.AKF focuses on ECD and education for the most vulnerable children by collaborating with UN agencies, government bodies, and local partners to expand its network of safe learning spaces, child community-based centers(CBCCs), and kindergartens. AKF adopts inclusive and integrated strategies that encompass protection, safeguarding, gender, and disability issues, aiming to ensure access to quality education and overcome barriers in emergency contexts.The initiative's strategic outcomes include strengthening institutional capacities, supporting vulnerable adolescents and youth, improving educational opportunities, enhancing ECD and education support, and providing nutritional aid. AKF is dedicated to constructing a resilient, inclusive educational ecosystem for Syria's children and adolescents, addressing the complex challenges posed by the current crises and fostering sustainable solutions for their well-being and development. AKF’s two-year plan focuses on enhancing the lives of those affected by the crisis, particularly in education for children and youth. The project aims building resilience by training local organizations, follow upon the existing 35 CBCCs (ages 3-6), and 22 Education Hubs(3–18), offering tailored learning experiences. Additionally, Initiatives include CBCCs and EHs in earthquake-affected areas and specialized teacher training for a holistic educational approach. Addressing the nutritional and health needs of vulnerable groups is also a key priority. This effort strives to alleviate the effects of violence, disasters, and economic hardships, ensuring every child has the opportunity to succeed. Protection Risk Analysis:AKF enhances WASH facilities to be resilient, gender-sensitive, and accessible,alongside hygiene practice training in line with local health and earthquake recovery efforts.AKF aims for transparent, inclusive facilitator selection, prioritizing qualifications and gender sensitivity to ensure effective support for community-based child centers and educational hubs.AKF employs strategies to promote a safe, inclusive educational environment, fostering resilience and empowerment among students and communities.AKF commits to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse(SEA)through its Global Safeguarding Manual, embedding PSEA across project stages, developing risk mitigation strategies ensuring accountability and community involvement in reporting mechanisms,adopts a zero-tolerance approach, and mandates Safeguarding/PSEA training. AKF prioritizes survivor safety, integrates PSEA into recruitment and partnerships, and emphasizes a survivor-focused approach in all responses to SEA
