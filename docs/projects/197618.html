Project Summary

In line with the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) strategic objectives (SO) and AKDN’s Global and in-country frameworks, the proposed project addresses basic humanitarian needs at educational facilities to ensure a safe, secure, and enabling environment for the students and teachers at school levels. The outcomes of the proposed project are:
a) Improved physical environment at schools, this includes providing the schools with solar systems for the functioning of lighting and operating the office devices and light rehabilitation of gender-sensitive WASH facilities.
b) Enhanced response to natural and man-made emergencies at schools. Under this outcome, school safety sessions including sessions on first aid, fire safety and disaster management will be arranged for teachers, students, and parents with distribution of first aid kits and fire extinguishers for each school. Also, a safety plan will be developed at the school level in participation with the teachers and students.
The proposed activities consider the short and longer-term needs of 30 vulnerable schools in Salamiyeh District in Syria benefiting over 15000 students, and 600 teachers.

3 - Needs

Major issue,highlighted in the acritical in the post-conflict situation was the poor physical conditions including access to WASH at school levels.Likewise, as per HNO 2022, 88%of children going to school were dissatisfied with the WASH in their schools.The ratio for Hama governate is about 75%. As per the need assessment done by AKAH in 2021, 2022, in Salamiyeh District,there are over 200 functioning schools, all of them are connected to the public water network,and they are under the rationing system when schools are receiving water from the public network once every 20 days for 12 hours.Local water resources are insufficient to meet daily demand due to severe fuel shortages and recurrent electricity disruptions. As a result, the schools are depending mainly on purchasing water from private water trucks.Similarly,schools are also lacking appropriate hygienic practices which are badly impacting the safe physical environment at the school level.As per the school principals, there are challenges in operating computers, printers etc.) without electricity.On the other hand, a large part of the eastern Salamiyeh countryside was exposed to many explosive munitions. Many schools are missing the fence when the playgrounds are connecting directly to contaminated lands.Unfortunately,in 2022there are many death cases among students as a result of choking,AKAH under this project aims to form and train school safety committees that can respond to such emergencies and save students' lives

4 - Implementation of activities

Rehabilitate /improve the WASH facilities in schools with the provision of PV systems for the purpose of lighting and operating educational equipment. The implementation will consider gender sensitivity in rehabilitating the facilities to offer an enabling learning environment for all ages and genders in the targeted schools2. Improve safe learning spaces by forming and training teachers, students, and parents (PTA members) on school safety including first aid, fire safety and environment: Under this activity, AKAH will form and train PTAs in each targeted school and they will receive school safety sessions on the topics of first aid, fire safety, evacuation planning, mine risk and school-based disaster risk management plans. In addition, a distribution of first aid kits, fire safety and evacuation kit.The PTAs will be responsible for transferring the received trainings to students through refresh trainings, simulations, and monthly drills to ensure the highest level of readiness and response to the different emergencies. All the selected schools have not been targeted before by any organization. However, the rehabilitation and maintenance works were stopped during the years of the war although these schools received huge number of IDPs and students especially for the elementary schools.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

A. Instability of the market prices and the sharp changes within short time, affect the implementation of the activities. The donor will be constantly consulted and informed on the situation and potential solutions. B. Lack of specialized workers to do the installation in the targeted sites. AKAH currently – in cooperation with AKF- is implementing R2R project targeting agriculture areas with solar system to improve the irrigation systems, the works are managed by technical local teams trained. Same modality can be utilized for this project. C. schools (students and principals) are not fully cooperative with the committees. AKAH will provide a capacity building to the committees’ members on behavior change related communication at school level and will rely on the existing structures to raise the communities’ awareness on the ownership and the participation aspects. D. The installed systems exposed to damage due to human or natural reasons. AKAH is considered in the design of the systems, to provide protection metal fence.
