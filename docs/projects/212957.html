Project Summary

The objective of this project is to support vulnerable populations in Homs to meet a variety of basic needs and reduce reliance on negative coping strategies. This will be done through the provision of Multi-Purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) to the most vulnerable households for a period of 6 months. Mission East (ME) will adhere to the transfer value and guidance recommended by the Cash Working Group. ME is present at the working group meetings to ensure proper coordination, collaboration, and adherence to recommended guidelines and practices. 2,250 individuals (450 households) will benefit from MPCA through this intervention.
In terms of location, this project will build upon ME’s existing ongoing programming in Homs with LAMSA that works with inclusive and integrated approaches to support sustainable early recovery and to rebuild longer-term resilience. ME has identified the district of Homs to be the target location for this intervention. This area is also targeted by the Cash Working Group, as 43,428 individuals have been highlighted as in need within the district of Homs.
ME and its partner will initially identify vulnerable households through both internal and external referrals. Once a referral is received, an assessment will be done with the household that considers factors such as number of household members, whether a household has a disabled member(s), whether the household is headed by a female or child, as well as other factors. The results of the assessments will guide ME and LAMSA in the selection of the most vulnerable households.
The cash modality is increasingly being used globally due to its numerous advantages such as giving beneficiaries the dignity of choice, as well as economic benefits such as the multiplier effect. In the contexts of Syria, where prices for basic commodities have soared and the currency is continuing to lose value, while at the same time salaries have remained stagnant, unconditional cash assistance is an effective modality that ensures beneficiaries can meet their basic needs and thus decrease engaging in negative coping mechanisms. The disbursement of cash will take place through financial service providers (FSPs). FSPs are commonly used throughout Syria, so targeted populations are familiar with the procedure. Nonetheless, ME, alongside with LAMSA will ensure that selected FSPs adhere to the code of conduct, as well as that beneficiaries are fully aware of their consumer rights vis-à-vis FSP field agents. The response is on best practices related to cash and voucher assistance, such as Sphere companion MISMA and Sphere’s “Delivering Assistance through Markets” and will build on ME’s ongoing CVA programming.

Implementing Partners

LAMSA: LAMSA falls under the umbrella of the Orthodox Archdiocese of Homs. The church was established in 1898 and began its work as a social actor in the area in 1970, with LAMSA implementing projects directly through its community center in Homs since 2018. LAMSA’s is one of ME’s key partners in Syria.
