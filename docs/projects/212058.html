Project Summary

In line with SO1 “Provide lifesaving and life sustaining shelter support” and SO2 “Reinforce an enabling protection environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure”,
PUI continues to undertake its comprehensive approach known as the Field-Oriented Community Operations in Syria (FOCOS) Strategy through its programming in a multi-year strategic way, with 2024 being the tenth year of implementation of PUI’s integrated-area based approach (IABA) in Syria. Through this approach, PUI makes sure to create synergies between the different interventions implemented in areas in need in order to respond to the needs of the Population of Syria as a whole and to help them to restore their pre-crisis standards of living by enhancing self-sufficiency and resilience in the long run and promoting social cohesion.
In 2024, people in need of shelter assistance are expected to increase compared to last year. This is due to many factors such as the ongoing crisis as well as the series of earthquakes in February 2023 that led to significant deterioration in shelter and housing conditions for affected population. PUI, as part of its shelter response, aims through this project to provide vulnerable and conflict-affected populations in Syria with access to improved, safe, and healthy living accommodations. Therefore, in line with cluster’s activities, the plan for 2024 includes implementing the following interventions:
• Rehabilitation of 885 damaged houses, benefiting a total of 4,425 returnees in several high-need areas in 4 governorates: Rural Damascus, Dar’a, Aleppo, and Lattakia.
• Maintenance and repair of 2 collective centers in Lattakia governorate, benefiting a total of 100 IDPs.
• Installation of 65 emergency shelter kits in Aleppo governorate, benefiting a total of 325 beneficiaries.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

PUI policies and operational guidelines align with IASC's Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations. PUI ensures accountability by placing affected populations at the center of all decisions and actions that affect their lives. Thus, targeted populations will be involved throughout all phases of the project to feed into the design of the interventions and guide the identification of both activities and beneficiaries based on PUI’s selection criteria. PUI aims to build a program to meet the needs of girls, boys, women and men living in intervention locations, taking into consideration vulnerabilities, to enhance their access to basic services, foster their self-sufficiency, but also promote social cohesion while ensuring that the program would have no negative side effects and that the risk for girls, boys, women and men to be exposed to any kind of violence when accessing assistance would be mitigated.
