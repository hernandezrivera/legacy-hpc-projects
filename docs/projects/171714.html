Project Summary

The project will target 10,000 (4000 women, 2000 men, 2000 girls, 1000 boys, 500 Persons with Disabilities and 500 including disadvantaged families and elderly persons) in emergencies to be provided adequate safe water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion services. We plan for construction of gender-friendly twin latrines and hand-washing facilities and distribution of WASH tools, essentials, Anti-COVID kits and Female Sanitaries.
Project undertakes Persons with disabilities baseline study on vulnerabilities, risks and capacities in determining holistic intervention. Study paper to be submitted to the cluster.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Multi-Purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) is seen as an effective conduit to aid extremely vulnerable populations, supporting a dignified assistance and value for money allowing the affected people to choose and buy their own food and non-food items from local shops across the country, helping to sustain the local economy in this critical time while paying off their loans. It allows a the tenet of the right to culturally acceptable choice and decision in humanitarian and development interventions while reaching variant populations without necessarily occasioning them to travel long distances for the aid. MPCA is also recognised as an active contributor to sector-specific programming, and in some instances, it can assist i.e. protection outcomes by alleviating stress caused by the lack of money. It’s a multi-player aid factor across all clusters.Cash transfer involves no pressure for quick payment from the hawala company, giving the NGO adequate time to review the documentation before signing the clearance statement. It lays ground for accuracy in data accountability and transparency in reconciling the payments while identifying the errors or misdemeanors. The reconciliation is effective as statements are released upon double verification and feed backs from beneficiaries.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

In the market risk analysis under the Cash based based assistance, the risk is reduced and transferred to the money transfer-market, as opposed to SORDA.SORDA considered the following:• Can we locate a safe environment in the area which can be used for cash distributions without further risking the already vulnerable populations?• Involvement of the local authorities, national and local Cash working groups in enhancing the project efficacy. The market analysis confirms loss of purchasing power of traders and end-consumers. Market recovery is delayed in most locations due to a lack of demand along the chain, caused by two factors: consumer demand is depressed, and a breakdown in the trading system itself as a result of demand failure within the market chain. The vendors and wholesalers surveyed reported accepting local and international currencies (USD), while mobile money transfers and cash-in-hands are the preferred payment methods, (99%) and (97%) respectively. The critical WASH goods at risk of stock depletion in the areas surveyed are: chlorine tablets and soap bars reported in (4 locations), drinking water (3 locations) and MHM in (3 locations). It is expected that economic pressures will continue to impact the access to emergency WASH goods and services as households typically relying on daily wages and the informal sector to survive are faced with widespread job losses. Cash and voucher assistance (CVA) for WASH programming in emergency confirms to the sectoral response. In contrast, on the supply side, market-based programming (MBP) is urgently needed to assist the market environment recover. For example, indirect assistance to markets through MBP, targeting WASH infrastructures can support the water market by facilitating the logistic and transportation of safe drinking water to affected populations. Markets confirm that women have less access to money across all social segments.We noted that the money transfer firms such as Dahabshil are functioning. Its secure to undertake cash transfer. The amount of money to be injected into the market does not affect the level of inflation. The market remains stable.
