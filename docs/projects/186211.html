Project Summary

The socio political crisis plaguing the North West and South West Regions has constrained access to basic social services like water supply. Rapid assessments carried out show that there is a precarious water supply situation in Bambili and Bambui which are student residential communities. Less than 25% of the population in these communities has access to portable water. Like 71 per cent of villages assessed in the anglophone regions face severe water crises. Collecting water requires more than 30 minutes walk regardless of the type of supply source. Regarding the sources of water supply, 70.5% of the affected population collect drinking water from an unimproved water source. The main challenges in Bambili and Bambui related to the drinking water supply include insufficient water points, insufficient containers in student homes and long distances to collect water.
Advocates for Social Inclusion and Community Development I (ASICD) a humanitarian and development organization founded in the North West Region of Cameroon hopes through this project to construct 10 modern boreholes in Bambili and Bambui, to build the capacity of 10 persons within the water user existing water management committee to manage the borehole water points, distribute 1,000 water jerrycans and water treatment tablets (aqua tabs) to1000 student households and conduct sensitisations to behavioural changes towards hygiene practices and encourage the adaption of healthy sanitation practices. The project will also integrate gender by not only giving women and girls water jerrycan, it will also give 500 heavy duty 15L buckets and 1000 bars of soap to 200 men and 300 women to help ease laundry. Upon completion, 3000 students and members of the host communities would have had access to portable drinking water through the boreholes, 1,000 student households would have benefitted from 1,000 jerrycans, over 5000 students and members of the host communities would have received sensitization messages for behavioural change and the adoption of proper hygiene and sanitation practices. Over 1000 students would have benefitted from water treatment tablets (Aqua tabs) and gained knowledge on how to use them. Lastly, over 500 women and men would have benefitted from 500 heavy duty buckets and 1000 bars of soap. The water user committees will ensure sustainability of the infrastructure realised. The NFIs include (Jerrycans, buckets and Laundry soap). The WASH are the water treatment tablets (aqua tabs)
ASICD is requesting both financial and material funding to help breach the gap in the water and sanitation crisis which has plagued Bambili and Bambui for more than 10 years and has been worsen by the crisis. The in-kind support requested includes 1000 water jerrycans, 100 cartoons of aqua tabs for water treatment. It We are also requesting 500 heavy duty 15L buckets and 1000 bars of laundry soap and financial support for the construction of 10 modern portable water boreholes in Bambili and Bambui. The total anticipated budget to realize this project is xaf 79,905,100, and equivalent of $145,282.
It is worth noting that the Bamibili Water cultural and development association evaluates the cost of providing portable pipe born water in the locality at over xaf 600 million. This does not include Bambui which is also a students locality and suffer the same water crisis as Bambili. In their cultural gathering to raise fund for the project, the elites contributed 77 million francs for a pipe born water project out of xaf 600 million, an equivalent of USD 360,000. It will take years for this money to be raised for the water project. ASICD provides a sustainable solution to the current water crisis through the construction of 10 portable water borehole in the two student localities to help alleviate suffering and reduce the spread of diseases.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Water is one of the essential commodities to human life. All living organisms, plants and nature in general depend on water for their survival. About 60% of human body is made of various types of fluids, which contain water. Some studies have shown that water supply has never sufficed the world demand. The UNESCO report of 1990 revealed that about 85 million people in urban areas around the world were without adequate water and hygienic sanitary facilities. The provision of water supply and sanitation systems in Tubah and Bambili by the Tubah local council remains a huge challenge like in most parts of the region. The scarce resources of the local council have been unable to meet the water systems maintenance demand of the local council. There is the need to implement less costly and cost efficient water systems for the benefits of the local and student population. Bambili has a local water authority run the Bambili water Committee Tubah the climate is hot and dry but with a moderate agricultural potential of mixed farming; 60% food production and 15% livestock. Bambili is greatly affected by the alarming water shortages which date back to early 2010 when the University was created from the ENS Bambili campus originally existed there along with site CCAST Bambili. The mass development of the areas through the construction of student’s hostels increase the residential population of the community as the university grew and is still growing. It should be noted that students sometimes go for two weeks without water flowing through the pipes. The issue of water shortage has resulted in other related problems. As a result of the acute shortage, people especially women are forced to fetch water of poor quality from streams and thus higher risk their lives. This puts them at risk of sexual assault. Women and girls are mostly affected as they tend to lack water for sanitary during menstruation, cooking and other house chores. Some people resort to buying bottled water which is very expensive and greatly affects their daily sustenance allowances. With the coming of the dry season, the risk of a cholera outbreak from the lack of potable drinking water has greatly increased. The problem largely affects the surrounding communities of Bambili. Students sometimes have to move down to Bambui to fetch water. This causes overcrowding in the local water sources of Bambui. Also, most of the students are exposed to risks. Boys are exposed to risks of attacks and harassment from state forces who usually seize their phones and subject them to ill-treatment. Some are taken to jails. Girls face the risk of being raped when they travel very far to fetch water. The water shortage also affects farming as farmers don’t get enough water for cultivation during the dry seasons. Women and girls are more disadvantaged when it comes to access to portable water in Bamibili and Bambui. Women and girls need water more for sanitation especially during menstruation. They need water the most because most household chores in our cultural context are performed by women and girls. Difficulty in access to water exposes then to common diseases during menstruation. Also, going long distances to fetch water in isolated streams exposes to sexual assault and related forms of GBV. Assistance will be tailored to meet the needs of everyone in the communities by constructing portable water points which are accessible to all, provision of Water jerrycans to women and girls to enable them fetch and store water in their houses. It will also provide these women and girls with water treatment tablets to reduce the risk of contracting waterborne diseases
