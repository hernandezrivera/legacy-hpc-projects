1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

These will be procured by JIA Under this project, 3500 HH of which 80% IDPs and 20% vulnerable host community HH, will receive basicNFI kits. directly and oriented on the basic NFI kit as defined by the shelter cluster. The intervention will focus on IDP HH that live in small dwellings within host communities and on the sorrounding host community within the district of Montepuez. The core activities will be: 1) Identification of vulnerable IDP and Host Community HH 2) Distribution of NFI kits and 3) PDMThe project will have a total duration of 6 months and will be implemented by JIA partner Wiwanana under tecnical supervision and support of JIA.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Identification of vulnerable IDP and Host Community HHThe most vulnerable HH will be identified through a mixed methodology containing a needs and vulnerability analysis, as well as Focus Group Discussions within the targeted communities. This way, it can be assured that distributions are needs-based, not status based. These FGD will also allow to adjust the composition of the NFI kit if necessary2. Distribution of NFI kitsShelter NFI-kits will contain tarpaulins, fixing materials, kitchen sets, sleeping mats, blankets and buckets. They will be complemented by 1 tool kit/5 HH. The distributions will target both IDP (~80%), as well as Host Community HH (at least 20%), whereas the composition of the kit might vary in accordance with needs.3. PDMAll distributions will be followed-up by a throrough statistically valid PDM. Results of the PDM will be circulated back to the shelter cluster.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A conflict-sensitivity assessment will be carried out previously to the implementation phase in order to avoid inflicting harm through the action. In order to decrease ground for tensions and conflicts, both, host and IDP community will be participating in the project activites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the entire project, which itself aims to reduce the exsposure to protection threats that arises from the lack of access to shelter NFI. Meaningful access is assured on the basis of a vulnerability assessment among the IDP population, which will be carried out prior to the implementation phase and which will inform the beneficiary selection. Targeting will thus be need-based, not status based and the project will consider the specific needs that arise from different vulnerabilities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Effective disability inclusion will be assured through identification of persons with disabilities including different disability status in an exhaustive vulnerability assessment and using the fundamental tools (Washington Group Questions). Persons with disabilities will be prioritized as especially vulnerable group.Specific NFI needs will then be discussed in FGD with persond with disability and/or their familily members, in order to assess if there is a need to adjust the content of the shelter NFI-kit.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project aims too reduce the environmental footprint wherever possible. As far as quality permits, NFIs are locally purchased in order to reduce transport and the related emissions and pleastic is avoided wherever possible. As such, e.g. plastic mats will be replaced by locally made sleeping mats.
