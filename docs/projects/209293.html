Project Summary

Since the 15th of April armed conflict has spiraled between the SAF and the RSF leading to mass displacement, huge causalities, and complete collapse in the basic service sector (health, water, energy, and food). This project targets 110,000 people with basic WASH services in Darfur, Gadaref, Khartoum, Red Sea, Aljazirah, South Kordofan and Northern States. The project will ensure that women, men and children have access to information and awareness-raising on safe household water treatment and the use of proper sanitation facilities. Women, girls, boys and men continue to be affected by protection-related WASH issues, especially now in light of the recent events and rise in protection concerns and reports of increasing violations, with gender-based violence occurring due to insufficient and unsafe access to water and sanitation facilities. • Sustaining access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene improvements, and awareness is critical to saving thousands of lives of children and women. Excretal and poor disposal, coupled with a lack of proper hygiene practices characterized by rampant open defecation, leaves a large portion of the population at high risk of preventable water-related diseases such as cholera and AWD.
UPO will ensure that women, men, girls and boys are consulted in the decision-making process and have equal access to safe water supply, sanitation and hygiene services to prevent potential public health risks as a result of using unprotected water sources.
sanitation and hygiene practices. • The consequences of poor water, sanitation and hygiene are stark, with more boys and girls dying from water and sanitation-related illnesses such as diarrhea; this exacerbates the health of malnourished boys and girls.
Inadequate access to safe water points also has implications for women and the education of girls in particular, as they have to spend considerable periods fetching the scarce commodity when they should be in school.
UPO believes that the provision of safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services to these vulnerable communities will cover aspects of social mobilization, rehabilitation of water points, sanitation facilities, emergency hygiene promotion, enhancing the capacity of the communities, and resilience. UPO will ensure the active involvement of women and men in decision-making in the provision of safe and appropriate water, sanitation, and hygiene services.
UPO uses and focuses on gender-sensitive programming and modalities by designing all activities through gender role frameworks and gender matrix frameworks to ensure safe and inclusive participation and sustainability of the project outcomes by creating a change theory in the communities, promoting gender norms and harmful practices, and promoting healthy habits and co-existing spheres.
