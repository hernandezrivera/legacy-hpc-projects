Project Summary

Project Summary
The project aims to assist the most vulnerable Women and girls who experienced heightened risks and compromised capacity to protect themselves from GBV. Project will increase the utilization of GBV prevention and protection services by women and girls through access to protection related services, which include GBV response and prevention, raising awareness on GBV, sharing advocacy messages against GBV, provision of dignity kits, Non Food Items to the GBV survivors and Capacity building of psycho-social counseling service providers. The protection rights and needs of estimated 3.2 million people, of whom 64 percent are children, and 48 percent are women and girls, are affected by armed conflict and violence and destitution triggered by climatic shocks, including prolonged drought conditions and floods. People in need of protection include households with significant proportion of persons with heightened vulnerability households with vulnerable members such as people with disability, medical conditions, children, elderly or pregnant/lactating women. The vulnerability is further increased if those members resume the role of the only household head. The proposed interventions under this project will address the current protection risks and build up the ongoing emergence response in these geographical locations under the regions of Bari (Bosaso and Qardho), Sanaag (Badhan, Erigavo), Sool (Lascanood, Caynabo).
Through these proposed interventions, CARE will contribute directly to the progress of the country toward the reduction of gender based violence by targeting the most vulnerable groups who are at risk of GBV in the IDPs of the above locations. Project will target 30% of the total PC target in towns.

Mitigation Measures

Mitigations measures 1. CARE will continually monitor the situation of the targeted communities and provide updates to the donor and the cluster members. CARE has developed a multi-hazard emergency preparedness and response strategy, including “current scenario, most likely scenarios and the worst case scenario”, the populations and regions forecasted to be affected. It has put in place deliberate effort to scale up its humanitarian response to reach out to new beneficiaries and geographical areas2. The project staff will continuously assess vulnerabilities to identify most vulnerable members of the community, such as ethnic minorities and the disabled persons. Members of the marginalization groups will be invited in the consultation meetings. Community awareness campaigns will be conducted in order to address the barriers. The safety of the needed people covered by the project will be prioritized. Through CARE‘s complaint and feedback mechanism (CFM), the project will provide a safe and confidential ways of receiving, managing and responding to the excluded and the marginalised communities. 3. CARE will involve committees/local leaders, local authorities in the monitoring of activities. Meetings will be held with communities to explain project purpose and the target groups. Most vulnerable groups which include women and girls will be consulted. Community leaders and the clan elders will be given full project orientations and seek their support for providing the assistance to the most needed people
