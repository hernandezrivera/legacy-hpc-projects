Project Summary

The project will be implemented by CAFOD and Caritas Hwange in the rural areas of Binga. The project is coming in the background of poor crop harvests in 2018 caused by the erratic rainfall of the 2017/2018 agricultural season, the predicted El Nino induced drought of the 2018/2019 agricultural season and the prevailing unfavourable economic conditions in the country. Thus, the project will support poor rural households to enter early recovery through support to cash for work on productive assets (short term emergency employment to rehabilitate community livelihoods infrastructure), support informal financial services (ISALs) and support district authorities in their response planning (subnational capacity for humanitarian response coordination and monitoring across all clusters that are responding to the crisis). Elements of microenterprise recovery and short-term vocational training will be assessed. The capacity of the local District Civil Protection Unit and relevant stakeholders for humanitarian response coordination and monitoring across all clusters will be strengthened.

Needs

Due to a protracted unfavourable economic environment and recurrent droughts there are serious needs for;1) Capacitating local authorities, community leaders and relevant responders with knowledge on early recovery from different shocks and stresses so that communities bounce back better. 2) Restoring communal livelihoods and assets for use.3) mainstreaming resilience in Disaster Risk management, particularly the Early Recovery stage.
