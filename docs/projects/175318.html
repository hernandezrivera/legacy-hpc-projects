1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

NeedsChad ranks 187 out of 189 countries on the Human Development Index, and 6.5 million Chadians live in poverty. COVID-19 has further reduced children's well-being and plunged at least 744,000 additional children into poverty. The 2020 SMART Survey estimates national level global acute malnutrition (GAM) at 10% with 2.1% of severe acute malnutrition (SAM). These figures. In addition to the food and nutrition crises that affect the well-being of children and their families, Chad faces problems related to population displacement due to instability in neighboring countries mainly Sudan crisis (conflict in Darfur), Central African Republic (Politico-Military Instability) and Nigeria (Bokou Haram). To date, about 80,000 Chadians have fled conflicts in Central African Republic to settle in Moyen Chari, Mandoul, Logone Oriental, Salamat and Mayo Kebbi provinces. In addition, 370,000 Sudanese in the East and 92,000 Central Africans in the South who have fled their country due to the violence in their respective countries are settled in refugees camp in Chad. These populations face many challenges and require humanitarian assistance, especially children under 5 years (more than 100,000) and women who are most vulnerable. Outputs: 1.) Procurement and distribution of 12,027 RUTF boxes and others nutrition supplies, materials and equipment to health facilities;2.) Ensure training and refresher training for 50 community health workers on the prevention and management of SAM; 3.) Provide training to 10 health workers on IYCF practices; 4.) Raise awareness of 1250 mothers on IYCF practices5.) Conduct monthly monitoring of nutrition status for children using Mothers MUAC strategy for early detection of SAM cases Results 1.) 12,027 children suffering from acute malnutrition are treated 2.) 50 Community Health workers trained in the prevention and management of SAM, disaggregated by sex and age 3.) 1,250 caregivers have improved knowledge on IYCF 4.) 4,379 children screened for severe acute Malnutrition
