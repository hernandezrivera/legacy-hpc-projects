Project Summary

New displacements are reported regularly due to armed conflicts, unprecedented natural disasters (i.e. flash flooding and drought) and evictions within Mudug and Galgaduud regions of Somalia in general. The displaced live mainly in makeshift shelters in congested informal settlements concentrated in the peripheries of major cities and towns, lacking security of tenure. Around 3.1 million people are in need of shelter and NFIs assistance. They are exposed to extreme weather conditions, forced evictions and other protection risks. They also lack basic household items.
An estimated 170,000 internally displaced people (IDPs) are in urban and peri-urban areas in Mudug/Galgaduud region. IDPs living in IDP camps in Mudug and Galgaduud regions face deplorable conditions, with limited access to basic services, precarious or no physical security and exposure to protection risks. The most vulnerable groups, who are exposed to the highest risks and are consistently socially excluded, include women, children, the elderly, child- and female-headed households, the physically and mentally disabled, people living in IDP. These conditions are expected to worsen during the dry season. In Mudug and Galgaduud, many households have substandard access to adequate shelter. Crisis-affected displaced persons face precarious living conditions having fled their homes, and are in urgent need of support in the construction and upgrade of shelters. In order to improve their hygiene and enhance their dignity, there is need for distribution of NFIs in kind and in Cash.
This project is aimed at improving the lives of 21,440 living in precarious crowded settlements, without sufficient access to basic services, with poor shelter conditions that raise protection and health concerns. This will be achieved through provision of Transitional Shelters and NFIs in kind and in cash to the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees, refugees and the host community in Galgaduud/Mudug regions.
The target population (women, girls, men, boys, the elderly and people with disabilities) living in the IDP camps who reside in small makeshift structures made of rugs, recycled cloth and card boards will receive NFIs intervention in kind. Those who are in hard to reach areas which are prone to armed conflict i.e. As attacks will receive assistance in cash through a 3rd party service provider to avoid targeting.
WISE will target locations in Mudug and Galgaduud region where it already has offices and a strong presence by incorporating the shelter project with other sectors of intervention like, CCCM, WASH, livelihood, economic empowerment and protection. The project is intended to fill the shelter/NFI gap that exists in the IDP camps.
The most vulnerable men, women, boys, girls and the elderly will be prioritized during the beneficiary selection process. WISE will Introduce special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the NFI kits. WISE will directly implement the activities. Upon distribution of the Nonfood items WISE will conduct post distribution monitoring to ascertain if the beneficiaries received their right entitlement of the items. In the PDM survey the beneficiaries will be asked how satisfied they are with the qualities of the items they received.
WISE has been on the front line when it comes to responding to the urgent emergency needs of affected displaced persons as well as address protection related issues.
