Project Summary

Based on MSNA conducted by OCHA in August 2021, need relative to drinking water, sanitation and hygiene represent the top need expressed by affected population in assessed villages in North West, South West Littoral and West regions. This project aims to provide an assistance to affected population in the four concerned regions by increasing access to safe drinking water, basic sanitation and hygiene practice mainly among displaced population. The project aims to reach 250 000 people for one year. Main interventions will include construction/rehabilitation of water and sanitation infrastructures, distribution of hygiene items.
The project will also include a component relative to support the WASH cluster coordination in North West and South West regions.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The humanitarian situation in the North-West (NW) and South-West (SW) regions of the country continues to appear to be worsening with frequency incidents reported among the non-State armed groups (NSAGs) and the military. The Multisectorial Need Assessment conducted in August 2021 reveals that drinking water is the second in rank as first top need expressed by affected population in assessed villages. This situation has worsening compare to Dec 2020 where drinking water was still at 2nd position with 24.3% of assessed villages. For 6% of assessed village, the top need is hygiene and sanitation. This situation is due to the fact that 27.7% of assessed villages used unprotected spring (27.8%) and surface water (23.5%) was primary drinking water. The most affected areas are Ndian and Lebialem in South West region and Donga Mantung, Boyo and Menchum in North west region. Main challenges include, lack of water containers, insufficiency of water point and long distance between water point and household. In 22% of villages, water points are non-functional. As far as concern sanitation, main places people go to defecate are pit without slab (71%), pit with slab 69%) and open hole 54%. This means that many people always used safe and unsafe sanitation practice. The unhygienic situation of latrines and its insufficiency are among the key problems faced by affected population. Concerning handwashing, few people have access to handwashing facilities. In the littoral and West region, the need of a drinking water is the top need (32% of assessed village) for urgent assistance. Hygiene and sanitation need were expressed by 6% of villages. Thus more than 1 person over 3 need wash assistance as top need. Most affected division includes Bamboutos, noun in West region and Nkam in Littoral region. Challenges faced by displaced population in Littoral and West region are similar to those reported in South West and North West region. Since: 27/10/2021 the South West region in reporting a cholera outbreak. Few weeks later, the health district of Bonassama in Littoral region also reported cholera cases in 2021. Thus, this situation is not good considering the presence of COVID-19 pandemic and frequency of cholera outbreaks in the South West region. On 25 November 2021, 84 cases and 17 deaths with a CFR of 4.92% were reported and only South West and Littoral regions were stills active. Last figure from South West region reveals that 209 people have been affected, 9 deaths reported and a CFR of 4.3%. sex ratio female /male is 1:2. Based on these needs, UNICEF and implementing partners plans to provide integrated WASH services using minimum WASH standards of SPHERE and/or national standards. An integrated package of WASH services will be provided to communities and institutions, with due consideration for solutions that are resilient climate and gender sensitive. Other transversal issues to consider includes. Protection, GBV and Accountabilities to Affected people.
