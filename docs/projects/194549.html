Project Summary

Summary
The overall objective of this project is to decrease excess morbidity and mortality rates from preventable illnesses, among 7770 (male 3892&female 3878) of Returnees /IDPs and host communities under Emergency (IPC4), due to circumstances including: intercommunal and cattle related conflict, drought, stress of food insecurity and essential service gaps. both counties have inadequate health infrastructure, underdeveloped and limited capacity of health system, including shortage of human resources for health, inadequate and inconsistent of medical supplies, exposing communities into negative coping strategies,
Among poor health indicators for tow county respectively Terekeka recorded 25% and Morobo 41 % of households had an under 5 passed away, 74% households had Experience health problem and in need of healthcare in the past 3 month in morobo , (71 % of households unable to access healthcare in the past 3 months in Terekeka 45 % of the HH suggested shortage of Specific medicine, treatment or service needed unavailable, in both counties beside Long waiting time for the service, 49 % of HH said Health facility is too far away, only 32% of HH experience of having access to ANC service (Source of information 2022 ISNO/HINO data shows) in addition to weak health referrals system in the two counties population, in vast geographic administration, and very challenging topographic, (streams and sump) which limiting access and logistic, for EPI, with very small coverage, due to capacity challenges 10. there is increasing number hernia cases identified in Tindilo, Rejang payam , 11. suspected measles cases were reported in the facilities;
Therefore, project will focus on provision of essential emergency critical lifesaving healthcare services through mobile clinic teams amongst the displaced population within the host communities, Ono aid will recruit qualified staff locally and deployed to the sites, and employ Minimum Package for Health Interventions in Rapid Response mixture approaches, (Mobile health clinic Mechanism, and Temporary & Static PHCU).
Main project activities,
The project activities will mainly focus HC Minimum Package for emergency settings including 1: continues Outbreak assessment, verification and response , and Outbreak prevention through health education with more emphasis on Surveillance 2: conduct EPI activities through the operational cold chain in the areas and do Mass vaccination of the targeted groups, 3: provide general consultation through OPD and Case management for all < 5 and >5 years , Diagnosis /treatment common illnesses, in compliance to national protocols and guidelines, includes: Screening and treatment of ARI and Diarrhea, Malaria RDTs and treatment , Other morbidity , Vitamin A supplementation , De-worming , Health promotion/education 1) 4) Provision of basic Reproductive health focused antenatal care, including support for GBV survivors , Care of uncomplicated delivery in a health facility, basic Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal care, Basic IYCF messages , Nutrition screening for PLW , Identification and referral of mothers. 5) provide refresher training on Basic IMCI, STI, M&E on Data Collection and reporting, Social Mobilization and Health Promotion/Education,

2 - Project activities

The project will focus on provision of essential emergency critical lifesaving healthcare services through employing Minimum Package for Health Interventions in Rapid Response mixture approaches, (Mobile health clinic Mechanism, and Temporary/ Static PHCU). Main project activities,1- Health Systems and Clinical Support the mobile health clinic teams will be involved conducting OPD consultations including diagnosis, treatment of common communicable, manage cases of SGBV (Clinical Management of rape) referrals of cases for further management, Nurses will provide basic nursing care, taking patient’s vital signs, dressing of wounds, establishing Oral Rehydration Corner and carry out vaccinations of children. The Lab Assistants will carry out malaria tests using RDTs in the current setting. The Dispenser’s role is to issue drugs to patients as prescribed by the Clinical officers.2- Conduct Epidemic disease surveillance, this will be done through general Health education and mobilization for routine, immunization, screening of under 5 for malnutrition with MUAC and lactating women for malnutrition, including rapid testing for malaria for under 5 and pregnant women as well as Screening for Tetanus vaccination of pregnancy, vaccination needs to all under 5 and TB Screening, the mobile health approach is employed to achieve rapid response to fragile health stations, communities under ( IPC 4) of this project areas to reach affected patients or provide referrals where necessary, this will reduces access to morbidity and mortality, in hard to reach . 3- Communicable Diseases ONO AID will work to prevent, detect and respond to communicable diseases including case management. In addition, the services carried out through the mobile clinics’ sites. National MoH treatment guidelines will be used for case management. RDT Para-checks for malaria and syphilis will be done at mobile outreach sites. Weekly reporting on data collected to Health Cluster at both State and National level on common morbidities like Malaria, Acute Watery Diarrhea, Measles and COVID-19 reported through EWARS and IDSR.4- Immunization Emergency measles vaccination for children 6 month to 15 years; During the mobile emergency health clinic consultations, children will be screened for measles vaccine and other antigens as well; vaccine will be obtained from health facilities. the activities for immunization will be done twice a week every Mondays and Thursdays until the project period. 5- Mother and child Health. ONO AID will provide maternal and child health care activities guided by MISP and BPHNS of the National Ministry of Health. At each mobile clinic site, a midwife will provide ANC, skilled community deliveries, post-natal care within 7 days, family planning, STI/HIV prevention and management, and GBV prevention & management. ONO AID will ensure that, other commodities like trauma and rape kits for management of rape cases, family planning commodities, Mama Kits are distributed, the midwife will conduct counselling on the birth preparedness plan and do screening of pregnant women for malnutrition during the ANC services and those found malnourished referred for nutritional services at the respective targeted Payams . 6- Community Health: ONO AID mobile health teams will provide health education sessions at all mobile clinic sites established and, in the communities, as well. Community Health workers will provide at the established mobile clinic sites while the Home Health Promoters will provide in the communities and household level twice in a week every Mondays and Fridays. Community mobilizers in their respective locations will mobilize community members to utilize health care services and for adequate turn up for efficient service up take. necessary topics covered will be diseases prevention and health promotion, ANC services, awareness rising on GBV issues, COVID-19 preventive measures, and personal hygiene

8.11. Mitigation Measures.

Upon the approval of the project ONO AID will engage all stakeholders involved in the project right from project inception to the closure. In order to ensure a sense of ownership. Consultative community meetings held with the community members and other stakeholders to inform them about the project. These stakeholders include County Health Department, community leaders, women’s groups, youth groups and religious leaders. Project beneficiaries will be encouraged to express their views, concerns and feedback regarding project implementation through regular biweekly meetings. Their expectations as part of the routine monitoring while ONO AID will provide regular feedback regarding key issues identied by these affected people and how they dealt with by the organization. Community leaders including IDP leaders used as a source of communication between ONO AID and the affected populations. Persons wishing to provide feedback are allowed through suggestion box provision at the established mobile clinic sites in all the targeted Payams of Reggo, Gemeza, Nyori, Tombek and Terekeka and a location to voice their feedback in person. This information will be displayed publicly, alongside the IEC materials used by the health promotion teams, and at the established mobile clinics sites. ONO AID has a Complaints Handling and Feedback Mechanism policy document that spells out how the rights of beneficiaries and ensures active consultation of beneficiaries from the project inception to phase out. The beneficiary feedbacks handled with confidentiality and shared only with relevant stakeholders without compromising quality of project implementation and without exposing the beneficiaries to protection risks. There will be active consultation with the beneficiaries including those with especial needs before, during and at project close out; their rights to accept or refuse certain project components considered
