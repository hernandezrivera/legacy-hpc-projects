Project Summary

-12 month project to build on previous achievements of similar project
-Will train CAR refugee and host community households in agricultural and livestock techniques
-Will provide women and youth with vocational training and business inputs
-Will create new savings and internal lending communities (SILC)
-Will promote social cohesion through collaboration between refugees and host communities in SILC groups, water management committees, and providing training on conflict mitigation

Needs

-CRS assessment found that CAR Refugee and host community HHs had limited skills and assets to grow or diversify their livelihoods. In CAR, the Central Africans practiced agriculture, animal husbandry, and commerce, however dislocation forced refugee HHs to shift away from animal husbandry to activities like daily labor, selling assets and collecting firewood, leading to the need for vocational training and livelihood support.-Demands for greater access to land and resources result in land disputes and communal conflicts that can compound existing mistrust between different ethno-religious groups.
