Project Summary

Providing training courses for caregivers of the elderly who are not specialists, and qualifying them appropriately theoretically and practically to care for their elderly, while providing them with the necessary support by providing some of the necessary devices for them to do their work appropriately (diabetes testing devices, oximeters, blood pressure measuring devices, etc.). ..), and carry out some recreational activities for the elderly, and some psychological support activities.
The project provides the necessary care for 220 elderly people (within specific cases) in the city of As-Suwayda:
1- Holding training courses at the organization’s headquarters for non-specialized elderly care service providers.
2- The practical application of the training course will be on the elderly for each case for a period not exceeding one month.
3- Providing some important equipment to the most vulnerable group of these elderly people, and making use of these devices after completing their need to provide them to other elderly people and according to the program that the organization is working on in cooperation with entities that provide similar services.
4- Organizing some recreational activities for the elderly and their companions.
5- Providing mental health services and social and psychological support during the practical training period for the elderly and the family who care for them.
Such courses can turn into an additional source of income for the trainees, and the Foundation will help with this for those who wish, due to its ability to communicate with the local community, the elderly and their families who may need such paid services.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

There are no specialized centers to care for the elderly in the governorate, and if there are, they are very expensive and most families cannot afford them. Therefore, most of the care for the elderly falls on their (untrained and economically incapable) relatives, which constitutes an additional burden on top of the other burdens placed on them. It is their responsibility.Therefore, this project is one of the new projects (at least in As-Suwayda Governorate). We have many statistics on these aspects, sourced from the Syrian Ministry of Health and a number of bodies working in the medical and charitable fields.The project includes the following steps:1- In the first stage, the indicators are determined by which the elderly with whom we will intervene and with their families will be selected (health status, financial situation, general family situation, ...), and based on that, the 220 people with whom the intervention will be conducted are selected.2- During the first phase, training programs for dealing with the elderly will also be prepared. These programs exist and are used locally and internationally. At this time, coordination will also be made with the specialized work team that will carry out the training operations. Determine what training equipment the team of trainers needs so that they can be purchased before the start of training.3- Based on the evaluation sessions in the first month, the necessary medical equipment for each patient will be determined and then purchased in full.4- People will be contacted to arrange the dates of the courses that will be held for this purpose on the institution’s website, and in order to coordinate the dates of the courses so that they suit their free time and will not be a burden on them:• One theoretical course requires 24 theoretical sessions. These sessions will be at the Rose Foundation.• The practical session requires one visit for each elderly person = 20 practical sessions. This process will take place in the month following the month of the theoretical course.• Two group psychological support sessions will be held every month for the families of the elderly and trainees.• Every month, one psychological support session will be held for each targeted elderly person.• At the end of each training session, an entertaining activity will be conducted for the trainees with the elderly and their families (who live with them).5- The work team consists of:• Specialized trainers for theoretical subjects.• Trainers for practical subjects from specialists.• Two specialists in psychological and social support.• Three employees from the organization: an administrator, an accountant, and a regulatory official.6- In the first month, the team needs to conduct a survey and evaluation process.7 - The institution will form a team of specialists to conduct monitoring and evaluation according to the institution’s existing procedure.
