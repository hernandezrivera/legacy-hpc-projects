Project Summary

Lebanon complex crisis (protracted Syrian refugees’ crisis, political instability, severe financial and fiscal crisis, Covid 19 pandemic, and Beirut port explosions) heavily impacted the health sector, with protracted delays in government payments of its dues to hospitals and severe shortage of medications and supplies in the Lebanese market, threatening access to health care including lifesaving care. The intervention aims to ensure access to life-saving hospitalization (COVID and non-COVID) and life-sustaining medications to the most vulnerable populations living in Lebanon.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under this intervention, there will be :- An increased access to life-saving COVID and non-COVID in-hospital care by covering patient cost based on pre-defined social vulnerability and medical criteria via Third Party Administrators (TPA): Best Assistance for COVID care; TPA to be selected for non-COVID care. Fund for COVID care is KfW carry-over.- A continuous support for nurse staffing of COVID units in 12 public hospitals via CRD, with addition of Oxygen generators and pediatric ICU beds. Fund is KfW carry-over and top-up.- A continuous access to acute medications through national PHC network, with direct bidding from WHO and storage at MOPH warehouse. Fund is EU carry-over.- A continuous access to chronic and psychotropic medications through national PHC network, procured, distributed and monitored via YMCA. Partial fund from EU carry-over and ECHO. Additional EU fund is committed for 2022. - Access to catastrophic medications and supplies, including oncology and hemodialysis, with storage and dispensing via MOPH warehouse. Concept notes submitted to Japanese and Italian Corporation.- A support to ESU with added staff on temporary basis, PCR kits, antigen testing and PPEs for increased COVID testing and surveillance capacity. This is ensured by KfW carry-over and top-up, and ECHO.- A support for public laboratories with testing supplies and materials.- Continuous coordination of the emergency health response via WHO

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Access to acute/chronic medications and inpatient admission is inclusive of migrant. Pre-defined social vulnerability and medical eligibility criteria clearly communicated with access to help desk 24/7 promoted and non-eligible vulnerable population referred by TPA relevant available support. Staff working on the reception and delivery of medications to the national Primary Healthcare (PHC) network, MOPH warehouse and COVID field case investigation as well as the staff conducting field monitoring visits will be asked to follow appropriate level of COVID preventive measures. Periodic staff care sessions, free access to a psychologist and personal accident covering COVID sick leave will be provided to nurses working in COVID units, in addition to facilitation access to COVID vaccination and training on code of conduct and PSEA. Facilitation of integration of new nurses in public hospitals, with salary scale kept within acceptable range compared to public hospital nurse salary scale.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Reception and distribution of medications to PHC centers as well as dispensing of medications from the MOPH warehouse directly to patients closely monitored using the new Logistic Management System (LMS). Patient satisfaction surveys/exit interviews conducted on periodic basis within the national PHC network, the MOPH warehouse and with the patients receiving in-hospital financial support. PHC centers have access to an electronic feedback form integrated within MOPH medical software (Phenics) on shortage of medications, allowing monitoring of timely distribution and availability of medications in these centers. Periodic field monitoring visits will be conducted. Communication and communication material provided to vulnerable population and relevant stakeholders to increase awareness about eligibility criteria and how to access available support. Beneficiaries consulted throughout the project via people participation groups. Project indicators closely monitored.
