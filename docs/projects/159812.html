Project Summary

This proposed project aims to contribute to the reduction of maternal and child morbidity and mortality among most vulnerable communities living in Qoryooley, Awdhegle and Kuntunwarey districts of Lower Shabelle region and to strengthen emergency preparedness and response to mitigate and respond to communicable disease outbreaks

Mitigation Measures

Building community structures including local temporary learning space that will ensure tension is minimized. Staff will be trained on human rights based approach where all people should be treated equally, especially those who ran from conflict and violence. Communities fleeing violence will be immediately supported through access to immediate Wash service without any discrimination. Where IDPs are involved, sites that support them will be built in consultation with them and the host communities. Marginalized groups will be prioritized so that they don’t feel excluded, which can otherwise aggravate their situation if they have run from violence

Please provide further explanation

The project is meant to build community resilience by strengthening local infrastructure and thus provide timely action, maintaining momentum of the response, and expanding reach in underserved areas promoting equity. Based on the cluster objectives, the response strategy includes the need to deliver a core package of integrated services, where priority will be given to the delivery of equitable access to basic Health service, SHAHRO will ensure community inclusion in the initial decision making of the project design and structure in this case the Health component will be prioritize the needs of community and in such will ensure to capacitate the existing community Health committee as well as the staff that will ensure the carryover/sustainability of the project after the expiry of the donor funds. SHAHRO will ensure to provide more responsive services to the affected population under the strict supervision and coordination with government and other humanitarian actors within its area of operation. The Health activities will consider the most vulnerable and disadvantage groups hence builds the community resilience to prevent recurrent and protracted emergency.
