Project Summary

Project is designed to support most vulnerable people with WASH services by providing sustainable solutions for water and sanitation, and reduce huge reliance on costly non-sustainable direct provision water trucking, desludging, etc. to improve living conditions of vulnerable conflict-affected HHs, with stress on prevention measures of COVID 19.
Project will target 2 communities (Beeret Kaftin, Maaret Elekhwan) and 7 camps (Jabal Kelly, Ataa Alkhair3, Ataa Alkhair, Ataa Alkhair2, Al Ainaa, Dar Alkiram(Kelly) and Al Baidaa) to ensure an improved, sustainable access to WASH services and supplies.
Targeted communities and camps are in Maaret Tamsrin (WASH Severity 4).
Project aims to provide water for 2538 HHs (13016 individuals, 2168 Men, 2583 Women, 4076 Boys, 4189 Girls) within camps, as there is a water network within camps and is connected to a reservoir, which is fed from a fully equipped public well that works by fuel and supported by an NGO covering the operating costs until the end of June 2021. Through implementation of this project, a hybrid system (solar energy, fuel) will be installed, as this will reduce operating expenses. Operating costs of station will be supported for 7 months (35 L/per/d) and 40L/per/d within summer months.
To enhance ability of beneficiaries to hygiene, especially with condition of COVID19, 2 HKs will be distributed for each family in the 3rd month and in 7thmonth and contents according to cluster guidelines. Also, HP sessions will be conducted in targeted camps, through trained mobile team (male and femal) about hygiene, COVID-19 preventions and IEC, taking into account the prevention procedures, social distancing and sterilization.
Beeret Kaftin town relies on water trucking, as there is no water station within town. Previously, water was pumped to town through Maaret Elekhwan water station, where there is a well designated for town. Since well was out of service and not been targeted by any organization. As for Maaret Elekhwan wells run on fuel and need maintenance.
In Maaret Elekhwan community, the residents get water through the main water network through the aid of an NGO covering the operating costs until the end of May month, after which the support will stop.
Through implementation of project 2095 HHs, (11500 individuals, 2432 Men, 3050 Women, 2998 Boys, 3020 Girls) will be supported:
Beeret Kaftin well will be equipped, and water station will be fully maintained.
Installing a hybrid operating system (solar energy + fuel), which contributes to reducing operational costs.
Maintenance and expansion of water network and high reservoirs within both towns.
Maintenance and expansion of sewage network within both towns
Support 100% of operation cost of water station in 1st month, decreased by 20 %, 40%, 60%. 80% in following 4 months, where local council will cover the operation cost gap from collected fees.
After completing all rehabilitation activities for water system, and starting operating, fees will be collected monthly from beneficiaries under the supervision of LC to secure operation and maintenance costs required to sustain project. In order to implement a proper cost recovery and tariff system BO will work closely with LC and beneficiaries to include them in design and planning phase of set up. Proper capacity building will take place for stakeholders involved in tariff system to ensure better managing of resources and water system. HKs for each family in 3rd and 7thmonth in conjunction with awareness campaigns of HP and COVID-19 preventions.
The project will provide sustainable solutions and reduce the costs necessary in order to obtain safe water permanently, through the establishment of a hybrid operating system and the promotion of the principle of fee collection within the communities. and training the relevant local stakeholders to manage the operation and maintenance of the water stations and networks, the cost recovery system, and infrastructure maintenance.
