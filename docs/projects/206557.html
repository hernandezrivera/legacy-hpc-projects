Project Summary

The project will provide equitable access to safe and protective education services to girls, boys, and children with disabilities of 6-17 years old in emergency through formal, non-formal and accelerated learning programs. The project will also improve the quality of education through providing emergency training to teachers and education personnel in addition to strengthening and improving the learning environment by ensuring access to clean drinking water and hygienic latrines for school going boys and girls in Cabo Delgado.
The project will target 107,260 boys and girls and 7,291 men and women in internally displaced persons (IDP) settings, host communities and returnee groups.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Considering educational needs has been increased in Cabo Delgado province, especially where many IDP children have returned to their home districts, the 12-month project will support vulnerable boys and girls to be enrolled into formal and non-formal education, as well as out-of-school children to be reintegrated into formal education, including children with disabilities. This project will be conducted in Macomia, Mocimboa da Praia, Chiure, Nangade, Palma and Quissanga districts in to strengthen quality of education and educational capacity. To address barriers to accessing and attaining quality education, the project will address the general shortage of supplies by providing learning and teaching materials, student kits as well as assistive teaching devices to improve the children’s reading culture which will increase their ability to catch up on learning lost time due to the movements caused by the conflicts. To ensure out of school children have access to education, accelerated learning programs such as catch-up classes and remedial classes will be offered to enable the children to recover lost lessons and keep up with others. To strengthen inclusive education, community sensitization messaging on the importance of inclusive education will be carried out at community level in partnership with community authorities and parents/caregivers. Additionally, teachers will be provided with professional development on various aspects, including training on psychosocial approaches, 'teachers in crisis contexts' packages and conflict sensitive education to ensure all children receive safe and quality education. The project will also conduct enrolment campaigns, including door to door, community dialogues and drives targeting out of school children, girls and children with disabilities, and learners at risk of drop out to raise awareness on the safe back to school and multiple learning pathways. The project will also strengthen and improve the learning environment by ensuring access to clean drinking water and access to inclusive and gender sensitive sanitation and hygiene facilities through construction of boreholes, gender sensitive latrines and promotion of good hygiene practices through campaigns.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

To provide equitable access to safe and protective education services to girls, boys, and children with disabilities (6-17 years old) in emergencies through formal, non-formal and accelerated learning programs, the project will:- Conduct community awareness about education importance, more focus on girls and boys affected by crisis, as well as children with disabilities- Distribute school kits for schools, including literacy and numeracy teaching resources for primary grades (1 to 6)- Distribution of student kits for according to grade children attend in school- Provision of assistive technologies to all school age children with disabilities in schools and communitiesTo improve the quality of education through providing emergency training to teachers and education personnel, the project will- Provide catch up and remedial classes for out of school children - Train non-formal education instructors (community facilitators) who will run the catch-up sessions; and remedial classes training for teachers and head teachers in schools with students in need- Train teachers on Ticc (Teachers in Crisis Contexts) package, including child protection, teachers' well-being, pedagogy and learning assessment.- Train District Education Officers on relevant TiCC content, including psychosocial support (PSS) and catch-up classes (CuC) approaches, as well as monitoring and supervision of activities.Geographical areasThe project will be implemented in Macomia, Mocimboa da Praia, Chiure, Nangade, Palma and Quissanga districts in Cabo Delgado province.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will monitor and analyse risks such as conflict development and its patterns on a regular basis- ensuring safe delivery and relevance of our assistance. For example, in light of the Cabo Delgado conflict, the project has also developed a series of strategies to ensure conflict sensitivity- conflict sensitive analysis, participation and inclusive targeting. Community engagement, (particularly consultations with children and most vulnerable groups) and conflict dynamics have influenced the project adaptations leading to ensure children’s safety, wellbeing and non-discrimination during their participation in specific activities. The community dialogues and consultations also promote community social cohesion to identify difficulties and define practical solutions to protect children. The project targets IDPs, host communities and returnee populations to minimize tensions and promote social cohesion. The project also includes specific, strategies strategies to ensure conflict-sensitivity. For example, in this response, SC has conducted conflict context analysis using the INEE guidelines to ensure contextualization of curricula and programming. The analysis is conducted with community members, education policy makers, education partners and the cluster. Based on the analysis, risk mitigation, gender sensitive and inclusive Conflict Sensitive Education activities have been developed, including training of teachers. Centrality of protection has also been embedded in all the project activities to ensure no harm.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project will ensure access to services is guided and based on a vulnerability assessment that is inclusive, and gender-sensitive considering age and ability. Assessments and inclusion criteria will be guided by the Washington Group Questions ensuring vulnerable groups including women, girls and persons with disabilities are consulted and included in the project. Save the Children will also collaborate with organizations for persons with disabilities (DPOs) for strategic planning, advocacy and implementation of activities. A mentorship and coaching approach will be employed for quality assurance, reaching higher numbers of children with disabilities (at least 5% of the total target) improving accessibility and responding to needs.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will ensure access to services is guided and based on a vulnerability assessment that is inclusive, and gender-sensitive considering age and ability. Assessments, registration and inclusion criteria will be guided by the Washington Group Questions ensuring persons with disability are consulted and included in the project. Save the Children will collaborate with DPOs including FAMOD, AIFO and UN family for strategic planning, advocacy and implementation of activities. Save the Children will work with DPOs to ensure the capacity strengthening of partners, communities and government on disability and inclusion standards, tools and approaches and general child protection. A mentorship and coaching approach will be employed for quality assurance, reaching higher numbers of children with disabilities (at least 5% of the total target) improving accessibility and responding to needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project is not expected to have major impacts on the environment. To address any unintended impacts of the project on the environment, project staff will be encouraged to prioritize the use of nature-based solutions. The project will also apply a climate change lens to the choice and design of all activities, mitigating the impact of activities through environmental risk screenings. Save the Children's environment and social sustainability screening tool will be adopted by the project.
