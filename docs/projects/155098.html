Needs

Reference to the education sector needs analysis and response plan, there is still a significant number of children and adolescents(boys and girls) not accessing any education, including over 39 percent of children 3-14 years and 97 percent of adolescents and youth aged 15-24 years. Over 2,000 learning facilities are needed to meet the existing needs of pre-primary and primary aged children 3-14 (Sector Gap Analysis/UNHCR MSNA). The provision of learning and skills opportunities for adolescents boys and girls requires urgent need as 97 percent of refugee adolescents and youth do not have access to any learning opportunities. Adolescent boys and girls equally need positive engagement activities so as to mitigate the risk of early marriage for adolescent girls, human trafficking, child labor for adolescent boys and adoption or engagement in other negative coping practices. There is also need for community empowerment and knowledge provision on the value for education and equal access as 40% of parents of adolescent girls and 33% of parents of adolescent boys reported that education is not appropriate for their children. Coupled with Cox’s Bazar’s lowest intake rate for the first grade of primary school at 72.6% for boys and 69.1% for girls, compared to the national average of 98%, there is urgent need for host community integration in the empowerment and knowledge provision interventions.

Activities or outputs

1.1 Enhance access to age appropriate and quality learning opportunities for children (3-14 years) and adolescents 15-18 years using the Learning and competence framework approach (LCFA) as developed by the education sector. The project will establish learning centres for boys and girls of 3-18 years. 3 year old boys and girls will be targeted for early childhood development while boys and girls between 4 – 14 years and adolescent boys and girls of 15-18 years will be targeted using the Learning Competence Framework Approach (LCFA) as guided by the sector. Activities will include: Construction of learning centres, Recruitment of teachers, ECD caregivers and teaching facilitators, Equipping learning centres with materials, Equipping learners and teachers with learning and instruction materials, Training teachers, teaching facilitators and instructures on child safeguarding, PSEA, LCFA, Life Skills Based Education (LSBE), Psycho-social Support (PSS), Psychological First Aid (PFA) among others. 1.2 Enhance access to pre-vocational and occupational skills training for adolescents. Pre-vocational centres will be established within the refugee camps where adolescent boys and girls between 15-18 years can access pre vocational training opportunities, occupational skills, peace building and conflict resolution skills. Activities will include: Construction of centres, equipping the centre with vocational training materials, recruitment of instructors, training materials, cash grants after trainings, peacebuilding trainings using the Empowerment of children and youth as peace builder (ECaP) project model among others. 1.3 Enhance community participation in education activities, access to information and knowledge on equal access and the education including child protection awareness campaigns. This will involve education campaigns targeting boys and girls, men and women within the refugee and host community. This will addressing equal access to education, school dropout, and the value for education among others and will be linked to child protection campaigns against all forms of violence against children including child marriage, child labor, and child trafficking among others. Other Activities will include equipping Centre management committees, parenting education, training community education committees, trainings, IEC material development and dissemination, Train teachers and resource persons on Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) The project will work with children, adolescents, community structures and parents to agree on a usual friendly feedback and complaint mechanisms to ensure accountability, ownership and sustainability. This will further enhance community participation. Resource persons from the community will be selected and trained. Both organization and partner staff will be trained. The project will work with the existing government department and the sector to ensure clinical supervision, on job coaching, mentorship and capacity building.
