Project Summary

- WWGVC plans to intervene in Aleppo, Deir-Ez-Zor, Ar-Raqqa and Suweida Governorates restoring community services in rural and underserved areas with a total budget of 3,223,993 USD.
- In line with the WASH sector's objectives, the interventions identified focus on resuming the supply of sufficient and safe water, and increase awareness on hygiene to 580,627 unique individuals in 2024, reducing the impacts of the financial burden of purchasing trucked water, and the health concerns associated with water-related illnesses.
- WWGVC will coordinate closely with the local water authority of each governorate to finalize the technical assessment of the infrastructure damage and the possibility of rehabilitation, solarization (if relevant), and resuming operations. Detailed assessments will also be conducted prior to the prioritization and selection of locations. The assessment will develop a baseline regarding the status of WASH infrastructure and services, the severity of its impact, and the presence of vulnerable individuals that will inform the prioritization decision at the community level.
- Water systems in the targeted areas are either out of service or partially functioning with a limited or no capacity of regular maintenance, or water quality monitoring. The improvement in service provision is achieved by the light rehabilitation and solarization of pumping stations, provision and repair of electromechanical equipment, rehabilitation of boreholes, water networks replacement/repair, and elevated reservoir rehabilitation ensuring water delivery to the affected population. Additionally, the activities will include awareness-raising campaigns on hygiene and safe water management in the same locations. These activities will ensure a holistic response to ensure water systems are operational in all the targeted communities of the project, delivering sufficient water to the households connected to the water networks.
- Moreover, WWGVC will also support the Water Boards in the targeted governorates on the operations of pumping stations through alternative power sources to diesel. This will be achieved through the solarization of pumping centers, or connection to a nearby grid where feasible and efficient. In parallel, WWGVC will work closely with the Water Boards of each governorate to build their capacity on water systems management and water safety planning, including training of technical staff and pumping stations operators.
- The above hard component activities will be integrated with awareness campaigns on hygiene promotion (HP) through a community engagement approach aiming at behavior change to all the targeted communities.
The project activities are targeting needs that are similar across all the subdistricts targetted in all three governorates. These include, but not limited to: 1. Damaged pumping stations and catchment areas of water sources prohibiting collection and delivery of water through the networks. 2. Damaged water networks reducing the water system's efficiency in delivering the needed amount of water collected. 3. Low efficiency of water systems due to lack of power by the water authorities. 4. Inability to afford hygiene items for the most vulnerable populations increasing the threat of spread of water-borne diseases. 5. Reduced awareness and care towards proper hygiene practices caused by the worsening living conditions and limited access to water.
The project will be implemented directly by WWGVC field staff in the selected governorates, in coordination with the relevant stakeholders including the WASH sector, SARC, Ministry of Water Resources, and Ministry of Education.
