Project Summary

The brunt effect of the insurgency in the North east brought a devastating result on the women, men, boys and girls, therefore, this project will facilitate to enhance Gender Base Violence (GBV) case management, provision of Mental Health Psychosocial support (MHPSS), and livelihood to survivors to improve their living standard and human dignity.
“Life At Best Development Initiative “will involves and get the services of clinical psychologist and social workers, which is key to provide support and to help stabilize living conditions of the victims, this will facilitate Mortality and morbidity to be reduced and a further spillover effect prevented. Life At Best Development Initiative, will purely engage community based approach by working with and through existing community structures such as women groups, youths groups, children clubs and with men to support services to communities through the integration of sensitization, awareness campaigns and Psychosocial support within its activities and adoption of a capacity building approach which empowers individuals and communities to take an active role in identifying and addressing the issues that hinders community level wellbeing.

Needs

The humanitarian crisis in north-east Nigeria continues as hostilities between Nigerian security forces and non-state armed groups enter their ninth year. Women, Girls and Boys. Still bear the brunt of the conflict that has resulted in widespread displacement, lack of protection, destroyed infrastructure, abduction of women, girls and boys and collapsed basic services. Many girls, boys and women were abducted and were forced into early marriages and as a result of this they were sexually assaulted, maimed, some suffered Vistula Vagina Fistula (VVF). Many of such victims, because of cultural barrier and dominance does not allow survivors to speak out and this leads to thousand of women, girls and boys suffer and being traumatized and stigmatized , hence the need of provision of Mental Health Psychosocial Support (MHPSS), case management and provision of livelihood support to improve their standard of living and human dignity. Women, girls and boys are at risk of abuse, neglect, violence, exploitation, discrimination, and marginalization, in general, they have limited access to food, water, health service, adequate shelter and supervision, clothing, mental health psychosocial support and education.The threat of psychological trauma continues to pose a serious challenge mental, economic and social well-being of the individual, family and society at large. Mental illness has become increasingly protracted affecting especially women boys and girls. Individuals who experience trauma and are not treated continue to experience loss of energy, desire to carry out day-today activities such as economic activities and social life. The chaos and trauma created by Boko Haram insurgency has seen a decrease in livelihood activities. This chaos and trauma have a profound effect on women, families and communities affected in one way or the other. Be it physical or psychological, trauma invariably affects women’s, boys, girls and even men’s well-being and the ability to carry livelihood activities. When a woman boys and girls is affected and proper care or management is not provided, the family is affected. When the family is affected, the community stands to be affected as well.

Activities

Capacity building of GBV for case workers and for community based social workers by Life At Best Developmet Initiative GBV Officer to provide case management, basic emotional support and lifesaving material assistance to survivors. Case workers ensured GBV is an issue to discuss in a safe and confidential manner in communities and at the GBV working group. Service mapping of key service providers (health, police, NGOs) finalized and GBV focal points appointed within Life At Best development Initiative multi-sectoral programmes (Education and livelihoods) to provide safe referral of GBV cases. Services awareness raising and engagement with men, women and youth from target communities, including elders, religious and other leaders.Provision of GBV services including case management, safe shelter, basic emotional support and health referral to the main health facilities in Bama, Biu, Dikwa and Jere Life At Best Development Initiavive will provide livelihood support to women, boys and girls with men to enhance their ability to care for themselves and families Women Boys and girls have access to recreational, social, learning and creative activities that reinforce their resilience and psychosocial wellbeingEffective livelihood activities by women have been severely affected by depressed market activity, widespread psychological distress; hence any planned intervention must take into account the myriad of problems faced by women, boys and girls
