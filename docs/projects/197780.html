1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Provide inclusive and gender sensitive life-saving water; sanitation; and hygiene services, items, and facilities for vulnerable populations affected by conflict, including displaced populations, returnees, and affected individuals in difficult to access areas. Promote protection of populations and prevent conflict and gender-based violence through provision of inclusive and gender sensitive water; sanitation; and hygiene services, items, and facilities for vulnerable populations, including host communities

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Beyond program design or activity selection, certain choices will be made in order to minimize the protection risks during the implementation stage and to integrate protection. Persons of heightened risk – including female headed households, very large families, child headed-households, single women living alone, single parents or caregivers, individuals with medical conditions including physical and mental disabilities, survivors of GBV or trauma, and others, will be carefully identified through community consultations ensuring a “do no harm” approach. Implementation of the activities will be community-based, and sensitasation will be tailored to support integration of vulnerable individuals and households. The Complaints Response Mechanism will be adapted to ensure that written, verbal, and confidential reports can be received regardless of access to mobile phone, charge, or literacy level.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

With power and information held at adult male level, despite women and girls being in charge of domestic work, it is critical to ensure consultation, participation and dissemination of information directly to women and girls, to encourage good hygiene behaviors. Designed to address the specific findings from primary data collection and secondary data review, the proposed response will seek to ensure safety and dignity, meaningful access, accountability and participation of women, girls and others vulnerable groups. Consequently, there will be a focus on ensuring service provision and access for the most vulnerable women or women-headed households. SI will continue to organize specific consultations with groups of women and girls throughout its program, and to rely on women representatives within the IDP/host communities, in order to involve them in the design and adaptation of its activities, notably for infrastructures to be built/rehabilitated. NFI kits and hygiene promotion messages will be adapted to women of different groups of age, and several of them will be especially designed to their intention (i.e. on menstrual practices and on the use of sanitary protections, etc.).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Where individuals targeted have physical disabilities or limitations, including the elderly, support will be provided for construction activities. All infrastructure included (including sanitation facilities) will incorporate additional needs for those with disabilities or who require additional support.
