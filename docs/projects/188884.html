Project Summary

This project aims to address the current gaps (according to the 2022 HRP) in returnee
response and emergency shelter/NFI needs identified by the Emergency Tracking Tool
(ETT) of IOM-DTM Unit, by:
a) Reinforcing the capacity of national and local authorities on emergency response
b) Creation of Early warning system and Strengthening the rapid assessment capacity
following natural disasters, including monitoring of IDPs
c) Distribution of Emergency Shelters and NFI response to returnees from the United
Republic of Tanzania and people affected by natural disasters, including cash-based
intervention to support during construction process
d) Providing Transitional Shelters and Repair Shelter kits to the most vulnerable
households returning from the United Republic of Tanzania and/or affected by natural
disasters, including cash-based intervention to support during construction process
e) Reinforce Emergency Communication with Community mechanism in country,
specifically the Emergency Hotline (109) hosted in the premises of the Burundian Red
Cross.
In addition, this project will reinforce the IOM’s Emergency Event Tracking system (EET)
for rapid assessment following natural disasters and other displacement events, including
the development of an emergency response team dedicated to assessing life-saving
shelter needs of returnees.
The project, as well as the Emergency Event Tracking (EET) will covering the 18
provinces of Burundi, namely Makamba, Rumonge, Bubanza, Cibitoke, Kayanza, Ngozi,
Ruyigi, Rutana, Gitega, Bururi, Mwaro, Murumvya, Karusi, Cankuzo, Muyinga, Kayanza,
Bujumbura Mairie, Bujumbura Rural.

3.1 S’il vous plaît ajouter plus de détails pourquoi vérifié cette option

The loss of shelter and land due to natural disasters causes desperation for the victims and can also cause tension between the displaced and the host community. Providing shelter and non-food items to those affected reduces tension and contributes to social cohesion. Similarly, providing shelter and NFI to returnees reduces the burden on host communities and contributes to social cohesion.
