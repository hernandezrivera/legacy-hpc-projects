Project Summary

This project seeks to capitalise on Mission East’s experience in Sinjar by delivering general protection services to a highly vulnerable peri-urban and rural populations in Sinjar, with an emphasis on overcoming psychosocial challenges of displacement and return. Mission East proposes to create 2 multipurpose Community Centres in Sinjar targeting youth and women. The centres will be equipped to provide multiple services, including PSS and activities aimed at bringing the community back together. The project will identify and strengthen existing community mechanisms and grassroots movements such as women’s groups and youth groups. The centre will also serve as critical information nexus for returnees to access referral mechanisms for other services.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Mission East is a CHS certified organization and follows the standards on quality and accountability. Mission East's accountability to the affected population can be broken down into the following commitments as listed below along with how they are put into practice: 1. Enable improved participation: regular opportunity for the local community to inﬂuence relevant decisions including inﬂuence the Mission East's policies, priorities, and actions at local, national level. 2. Information Provision: -Regular, timely and accessible information sharing with beneﬁciaries as well as stakeholders including government and the local community-Communicating in Arabic, and Kurdish respectful and culturally appropriate for different members of Iraqi society, especially vulnerable and marginalized groups such as Yazidis in the Sinjar location. - Utilizing pre-existing communication structures relevant to the Iraqi society. 3. Feedback and complaint mechanism: -Accessible and appropriate ways to different affected groups to access the formal feedback and complaints mechanism including emails, phone, face to face, text etc.-Regular review of barriers to giving feedback from the community. 4. Leadership and governance: -Mission East Code of Conduct is signed by all staff, volunteers and partners working on this project.-Mission East Child Safeguarding policy is signed by all staff, volunteers and partners working on this project as well as training of them.-Regular CHS training for Mission East staff and partner organisations 5. Transparency: -Sharing information about Mission East successes and failures more openly, to help promote a system-wide culture of openness and accountability.6. Design, monitoring and Evaluation: -MoUs with local grassroots/ voluntary groups at the village level to ensure relevance of programme and accountability to the community.
