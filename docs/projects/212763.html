Project Summary

HNO 2024 estimates 16.7 million people in need in Syria, with 6.8 million requiring shelter assistance and 6.2 million in need of NFI support. The economic crisis in Syria hampers the population's ability to improve shelters and access essential NFIs and limits access to basic services, protection, and physical safety. The Shelter/NFI intervention in Syria aims to provide lifesaving and life-sustaining shelter and non-food item (NFI) support, enhancing community resilience and cohesion by addressing housing and related infrastructure needs.
The project aligns with HRP Objective 1. NRC interventions will target vulnerable conflict and displacement-affected populations, including IDPs, refugees, host community members, returnees, and non-displaced households impacted by the conflict. The target locations are determined by severity, inter-agency coordination, and accessibility. Implementing multi-sectoral vulnerability scoring techniques where applicable. The key project Components will include:
Shelter:
• Rehabilitation of collective shelters in Hasakeh city to enhance living conditions for displaced families.
• Structural rehabilitation of housing buildings in Aleppo to reinforce protection and community cohesion.
• Camp infrastructure maintenance and repair.
• Dignified transitional shelter solutions in specific regions.
• Installation of new/ replacement of existing tents
• Supply and installation of HH level solar energy systems.
NFI Assistance: Winter assistance through blanket distributions in preidentified vulnerable areas, providing winter clothes to affected communities.
Winterization assistance (cash or in-kind) for affected populations.
Distribution of NFI kits.
NRC will implement the interventions through an integrated approach with joint program for Shelter and NFI activities, with modalities (emergency, transitional, and recovery) based on access constraints and capacity. Gender, elderly, and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) considerations integrated into design, implementation, and monitoring processes. NRC’s future Focus (2024/2025) will be exploration of housing and infrastructure projects linked with solar energy; pilot projects planned. Rehabilitation of community infrastructure such as markets, schools, and medical centers in coordination with relevant sectors.
