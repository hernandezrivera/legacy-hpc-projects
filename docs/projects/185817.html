Project Summary

Le présent projet prévoit de contribuer à réduire la mortalité et la morbidité des populations affectées par la crise sécuritaire et humanitaire dans les régions du Sahel et de la Boucle du Mouhoun au Burkina Faso en améliorant les conditions de prise en charge dans les formations sanitaires au travers de l'amélioration de l'accès à l'eau, l'hygiène et l'assainissement.
Les formations sanitaires ciblées dans la Boucle du Mouhoun et le Sahel seront dotées du paquet minimum WASH selon les données recueillies par l'évaluation des besoins.
L'objectif du projet est d'assurer pour chaque formations sanitaires appuyées:
- Disponibilisation de l'eau en qualité et quantité suffisante selon les normes (stockage de capacité minimum de 1000 L.)
- Mise en place de structure de gestion ;
- Disponibilisation de dispositifs de lavage des mains avec du savon
- Mise en place d'un système de gestion des déchets (vidoirs, brûleurs ou fosse à incinération, poubelles pour la collecte des déchets...)
- Dotation en kit minimum d'hygiène (Pour les questions environnementales, hygiène des mains, nettoyage du milieu, stockage et traitement de l’eau…)
- Réalisation/réhabilitation de blocs de latrines/douches séparés H/F/Agents de santé
- Promotion de l'hygiène (sensibilisations, affiches, prospectus,)

2 - BESOINS, ACTIVITÉS ET RÉSULTATS ATTENDUS
2.1. Décrivez les besoins, activités et résultats attendus

Among the 2,548,537 people with humanitarian needs in WASH in Burkina Faso, 25% are in the Sahel region. Dori is among the most affected cities requiring urgent humanitarian assistance in WASH to limit the deterioration of the People in need (PiN) physical and mental well-being and limit the risk of epidemics in the region. Some localities in the Séno and Oudalan provinces have rocketed and reached 5/5 in terms of needs severity. The Seno area has nearly 180 000 PiN. The main challenges in Dori are the lack of access to water to meet essential households needs for about 50% of IDPs and 50% of the host communities. Moreover, people dread the overloading of water facilities which leads to long-lasting queues and inevitable conflicts . In the targeted health facilities (Sahel and BDM regions), the major issues are the lack of maintenance and equipment. Dilapidation leads to the malfunctioning or non-functioning of equipment, already in bad condition. Due to the insecurity, the infrastructures are neglected and decreasing. In the Sahel region, 37 water supply systems were shut down due to vandalism, affecting as a result many health facilities in the region. The situation worsened as the needs : increasing breakdowns and disruption of existing water management and maintenance mechanisms, especially, for insecure areas; increasing attacks or vandalism on networks of water preventing the distribution of water; poor application/respect of national standards in sanitation infrastructures; poor access to soap, and bad maintenance and insufficient water cans available to IDPs and host populations; low level of knowledge of hygiene practices among people recently displaced. The project plans :- Provision of chlorine and hygiene kits/equipment in targeted health facilities - Provision of WASH kits for malnourished mother and child pairs at the nutritional education recovery Center of the advancer medical Center of Boromo and the Regional Hospital Center of Dori- Training of hygienists and health workers in WASH, infection prevention and control, production of chlorine solution in targeted medical facilities - Training/refresh courses for Community Health Workers on hygiene promotion and provision of awareness equipment in targeted health facilities - Promotion of good hygiene practices for patients and caretakers in targeted health facilities - Organization of periodic meetings with management committees on WASH issues in health facilities.- Construction/Rehabilitation of sanitation facilities in the targeted health facilities (latrines, showers, garbage bins and ash pits …)- Training of management committees on the drafting of action plans and the maintenance and use of water and sanitation facilities in the targeted medical facilities - Rehabilitation of the water network of the advanced medical facility in Boromo, Regional Hospital Center in Dori and urban medical centers in Dori- Construction/rehabilitation/repairs of human-powered pumps in targeted health facilities.All these activities will help to improve the conditions of access to water, sanitation and hygiene in the targeted health facilities of the HDs of Balés and Dori are improved.The whole population of the Balés province and Dori province is beneficiary of the project's WASH interventions (rehabilitation and construction of facilities): patients and accompanying persons who will attend the targeted health facilities, as well as medical staff.
