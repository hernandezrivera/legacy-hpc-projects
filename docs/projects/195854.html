Project Summary

After the influx of Rohingya in Bangladesh, Nabolok has been addressing lifesaving services since June 2018 to till today and we will have planned to continue these supports for the coming year of 2023 at camp-24 & 26. Nabolok had already implemented a few projects to provide humanitarian assistance for Rohingya in camp-24 & 26 & Host Community in Baharchara, Nhila, Teknaf Pouroshova,Teknaf sadar & Sabrang union under Teknaf upazila and delivered the assistance to them and this service is going on. From the beginning to work in the Rohingya response, we have been working in Site Management and Site Development (SMSD) by installing Solar Street Lights, and repairing & maintaining those under Site Development. So far, Nabolok had installed 171 solar street lights at camps- 24 & 26 for the Rohingya community to ensure their safety and security issues at night apart from this Nabolok have been also conducting 100% repair, maintenance and operation of this Solar Street Light to keep these lights functional. Furthermore, based on identifying gaps consultation with Site Management & Site Development Nabolok will have plans to install more than 20 nos of solar-based street lights in 2023 to address the existing need for the Rohingya refugee in the camp-24 & 26. Before installing a single light, we went to the field and discussed with the SMSD, CiC Office, Majhis and beneficiaries about demand collection, site selection, and so on. During the discussion, Nabolok has always prioritized the opinion of women and adolescent girls, older people, PWDs, and disabilities through consultation meeting at community level. So that they can get adequate lights to go everywhere in the evening through the installation of solar street lights. As a consequence, Nabolok installed most of the SSL at the places that beneficiaries suggested, especially the female and adolescent requirements. Besides these, we will plan to include all age group people with gender inclusion which will make our project more gender inclusive and participatory also. Nabolok installed most of their Solar Light beside WASH facilities, roadside, and many gathering places so that a wide range of beneficiaries (both primary and secondary) will benefit from our lighting facilities. As we mentioned above, we installed most of the Solar Lights beside the WASH facilities so that all age groups of beneficiaries have used the WASH facilities and moved from one place to another more comfortably with the help of lighting facilities during the night. Besides these, women and adolescent girls also came out of their houses to use the WASH facility even at night without fear of sexual and physical harassment or abasement as well as after lights were installed the rate of theft and rubbery incidents have reduced significantly. The project will also plan to ensure regular repairing & maintenance works on a regular basis to keep this light functional and those will give long-time services by collecting feedback from SDSM, Form Watch Group by Rohingya People and direct beneficiaries. Considering the feedback and requirement of beneficiaries and SMSD Nabolok will repair SSL at camp level Nabolok will repair by their vendor with communicate SD focal. And febore installation of new SSL, Nabolok will consult SMSD, CiC, Majhis and community peoples for keeping needs and select the proper site. After that Nabolok will install new Light through Vendor. This site development activity will increase the level of feeling safe during the night at the roadside and in the community.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

To enhance equal access to services for refugees residing within the settlements - through improved camp-level coordination mechanism, regular monitoring of service provisions, and conducting facility-wise assessment and considering the camp context, several field experiences, and identified gaps along with assessing the needs of beneficiaries regarding safety issues, especially at the evening. SSLs are essential for the Rohingya & host community people. We found that a significant number of the beneficiaries don’t get enough lighting facilities during the night. As consequence, the beneficiary doesn’t feel safe during the night especially when they are coming out of their house for using WASH facilities, and there always be a possibility of sexual & physical harassment and robbery offenses due to the absence of any lighting facility. Nabolok has the plan to minimize that problem at the camp level by installing an additional 20 nos of SSLs among the camps 24 & 26. Along with this, we will ensure the proper functionality by conducting regular repair & operation, and maintenance of the total 171 no’s for Rohingya and 165 nos for host of installations that we had installed in the previous couple of years through community consultations, especially with the female and adolescent girls of Rohingya community at camp-24 & 26 and Nabolok will repair and maintenance of existing and newly installed SSLs by own Vendor for the period of 2023. As well as ensuring a maximum number of beneficiaries will get lighting facilities at the hole period of night and ensured their safety issues. This project will help to improve site management and site development activities, to be more specific it will ensure safety, security during night, and protection issues. The all above-mentioned activity will be included in our project to ensure that 100% of targeted beneficiaries have proper lighting facilities at night as well as their involvement during site selection and regular feedback throughout the whole project time.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

Through installation of Solar Street Light and its regular repair & maintenence, safety and security of the beneficiary, especiall women and girls will be ensured during using of the latrine, bathhouse, water collection point, and getting to the market place or health centre, and medicine shop at the evening time. Usually at the evening time they are physically abused. Considering this situation, the project will continue the repair and maintenance of previously installed 171 & 165 Solar Street Lights (SSL) always functioning and protect this situation. It not only helps the community people but also helps reduced sexual and physical harassment of women and girls if we will keep the functions of the SSLs in the project areas.

3.2 - Gender

To ensure safe dignified access to the latrine bathhouse, water collection point, and go to the market place or health centre, medicine shop or any urgent needs in the evening time, the project will ensure enough lights into the communication road, besides the latrine and bathhouse and turning points of the road. It helps the people, especially women and girls for protect sexual and physical harassment and abuse, gender based violence (GBV) of women and girls. if we will keep the functions of the SSLs in the project areas. Due to the maximum Rohingya camp situated in hilly area in Tekbnaf region. Lights can support to the peoples for protect, identify the victims easily. Before the selection of the SSLs installation point, the project team conducted an assessment and conducted a Focus Group Discussion (FGD) with the Rohingya people. Females and adolescents get more priority for giving their opinion in the FGD session.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

The project will ensure safeguarding by maintaining safeguarding policies and procedures. The SSLs can provide support to movement & use latrine bath houses at night. Basically Women adolescent girls, Disability person protection measures first, To prevent and minimize any type of harmful situation in the evening, the project prioritized the lighting facility at night. The project ensured meaningful access with lighting support as much as possible to ensure reduced lighting vulnerability. All type of people i.e. Extremely Vulnerable individuals (EVI) - PWD, Lactating, pregnant mother, elderly people enjoyed by the SSLs support.

3.4 - Diversity

The project will find-out dark and vernalable places for installing the SSL at camp level through community consultation and group meeting with women & adolescent girls, elder person, disability people, third gender group and young peoples for selecting a new site of the SSL with the prior opinion of the CiC and SMS.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

The project will be accountable to ensure dignity and quality support for the affected people. For ensuring the quality of the support, the project will ensure the minimum of two types of accountability mechanisms are in place i.e. Meeting with different age (women, Adolescent girls, disability and Elder person) Complaint Box and direct or phonically system. Collect complaints from the community after Project staffs will play a positive and supportive role to ensure quality support in time. The beneficiary can share their suggestions, feedback, and complaints with the project staff verbally by the different ages meeting conduct. Besides, we will conduct regular monitoring and door-to-door visits, monitor the progress, identify the problem, and collect feedback about our services. The project will welcome complaints and feedback for further improvement and will be ready to respond to the different ages - complaints, and feedback on a priority basis.
