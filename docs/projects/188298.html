Project Summary

QRCS aims through the proposed project to ensure continuity and increase the coverage of life-saving preventive and curative nutrition services among children under 5 (boys and girls) and PLWs. QRCS has been supporting seven fixed OTPs centers and 6 mobile rapid response teams reported on monthly 4Ws in coordination with nutrition clusters, UN agencies, health directorates, and local stakeholders in which targeted areas are ranked as a high severity according to the HNO 2021 and nutrition cluster reports, at 29 communities including IDPs camps and host communities in Idleb and rural Aleppo.
QRCS will continue provision of quality curative and preventative nutrition services through running eight RRTs and deployment of community outreach activities in the targeted areas in Idleb and rural Aleppo, as well as QRCS, will continue to implementing programmatic adjustment in the context of COVID-19 to protect health care providers, mitigate the risk of infection and ensure continuity of life-saving nutrition services as the followings:
1-Continuity of life-saving nutrition services particularly CMAM, micronutrient supplementation, and IYCF-E under guidance for adaptations in the context of COVID-19 through the deployment of eight mobile rapid response teams and each RRT will visit 3-4 communities weekly.
2- RRTs will provide MUAC screening, refer complicated SAM cases and provision of therapeutic for SAM and MAM cased among children 6-59 months and malnourished PLWs as in 29 communities including IDPs camps.
3- Provision of Maternal nutrition and appropriate IYCF-E counselling and messages for pregnant women and mothers.
4- Screen, referral children and PLWs with Medical problems.
5- 16 CHWs and one team leader will conduct community outreach activities promoting IYCF-E, Maternal nutrition, health, and WASH-related topics and massages, and Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutrition screening based on NWS nutrition cluster and TWG recommendations in the context of COVID-19 using additional platforms such as mobile text messages, radio, TV, and social media to protect, promote and support IYCF-E.
6-Distribution of micronutrient supplementary to PLWs and children under 5 based on SoPs and nutrition cluster recommendations.
7-Conduct training on IYCF-E and CMAM programming in the context of COVID-19 for RRTs staff as well as identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs, referral pathway and COVID-19 pandemic.
8- Roll-out of a family MUAC approach for caregivers, PLW, and family members.
9- QRCS will conduct COVID 19 precaution during implementation through procuring protective personal equipment such as gloves, masks, sterilizers as well as staff will receive training on IPC and precaution measures. Also, QRCS will conduct online awareness sessions and distribute IEC materials via special channels in social media.
10- QRCS will coordinate with nutrition cluster, partners and local councils and coordinate with WASH cluster through the regular coordination between Nut. and WASH clusters if needed.
During the project -24 months- period, QRCS will provide supportive supervision and on-job monitoring and training through community health leader, field project coordinator and project management based in Turkey, as well as QRCS, will provide equitable access and quality of nutrition services to the most vulnerable population among 57,395 children under 5 (28,655 boys and 28,740 girls) and 12,320 PLWs reaching 99,090 expected beneficiaries including (55,160 Female and 43,930 Male), with 1,413 disables people, 56,480 displaced population and overburdened and 42,610 host communities. All estimated caseload and a number of targeted beneficiaries determined based on the capacity of QRCS and previous project finding, achievement and results which reported to nutrition cluster via monthly 4Ws.

4 - Needs

According to the HNO2022, 1.7M pregnant and lactating women and 3.3M children under 59 months of age in Syria are in need of nutrition assistance in 2021 in addition nearly 11% of mothers are malnourished in NW Syria. While the humanitarian community is scaling up its response to the ongoing crisis, gaps across sectors persist due to the scale of humanitarian needs. Nutrition cluster for N.W. Syria has been tracked nutrition and IYCF indicators through facility and community nutrition surveillance system. Community-based nutrition surveillance March 2021 report showed GAM 3% (0.9 – 5, 95% C.I.), and prevalence of moderate malnutrition among children under5 is 2.9%. Additionally, 17.19 % of the mothers in Jis-Ash-Shugur area are malnourished with GAM 4.22% among children under 5, 22.92 % of the mothers in Harim District are malnourished with 3.35 % GAM among children under 5, and 9.01% of mothers in Ariha sub-district are malnourished with 1.27 GAM among children under 5. Furthermore, the facility nutrition surveillance report conducted in March 2021, data showed a distinct level of MAM among children, especially between children 6-23 months, that is also associated with a high prevalence of stunting between children 48-59 months. The data shows a link between lack of exclusive breastfeeding and lack of dietary diversity or minimum acceptable diets to these nutrition concerns. Additionally, the malnutrition proportions among PLW were also high at 28.35 % in Idleb.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project is planned to provide life-saving nutrition services to the most vulnerable people in areas with high severity of needs at 29 communities, including IDPs camps, and enhance the resilience of affected communities most of them are women and children in high severity through provision through community engagement and outreach activities.This project will continue providing immediate and longer-term interventions to address the most vulnerable population's urgent needs and increase the coverage to the high severity and remote locations, including IDPs camps, as well as to increase equitable access to curative nutrition services through systematic and timely identification, referral, and treatment of acutely malnourished cases for pregnant and lactating women, and boys and girls under 59 months:• Provision MUAC screening, refer complicated SAM cases, and provision of therapeutic for SAM and MAM cased among children 6-59 months and malnourished PLWs as in 29 communities including IDPs camps. • Provision of Maternal nutrition and appropriate IYCF-E counseling and messages for pregnant women and mothers.• Promoting and providing health and nutrition key education messages on IYCF-E, dietary diversity, hygiene promotion, and COVID-19 using appropriate IEC and promotion materials. As a slightly longer-term intervention • Provision of nutritional curative and preventative supplementations to targeted beneficiaries.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Gender-Based Violence (GBV) affects accessing nutrition services. Mitigating Measures: Provide nutrition services in accessible easy to reach areas. Training of nutrition RRTs on Prevention and management of sexual violence. The presence of female staff in the nutrition teams’ increases access of women and girls to the nutrition services. Risk: Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) of women: Mitigating measures: Establish effective community-based complaints mechanisms, awareness-raising on PSEA amongst nutrition team members, with continuous training on the topic. Risk: Inequitable access to nutrition services by Vulnerable Groups. Mitigating measures: Implementation of standard essential services package across the sector interventions, including improved quality reporting and robust monitoring through standardized SOPs. Addressing the specific needs of vulnerable groups through supporting dedicated nutrition subgroups groups IYCF, CMAM and capacity Building


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy
