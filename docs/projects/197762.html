Project Summary

This Project seeks to ensure that targeted conflict affected children in the North-West and South-West Regions are absolutely protected from various forms of abuses regularly being perpetrated against children in these affected regions. The project will be targeting a total of 4,500 Children who shall be prioritized during this implementation.
In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be building the capacities of 50 Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms. Also, this project will allow COHESODEC to provide support in the establishment of 800 birth certificates to humanitarian crisis-affected children as well as sensitize at least 4000 persons among the entire population on birth registration.
Moreover, the project will ensure that 11,500 children have access to GBV risk mitigation, prevention or response interventions while also will ensure the identification and implementation of measures to prevent risks of violence, abuse and exploitation of children during this humanitarian context. This project will as well ensure that 14,000 parents, caregivers and community leaders are sensitized about child protection risks in targeted localities.
Furthermore, the project will permit COHESODEC to identify and ensure adequate alternative care arrangements and reunification of 75 unaccompanied and Separated as well as ensure that 675 UASC are accessing family-based care or appropriate alternative services while at least 75 unaccompanied and separated children are reunified or in contact with their families.
In addition, the project will allow us to provide children at risk of violence, abuse and exploitation in these humanitarian situations with child sensitive case management (multisectoral services such as psychosocial support, education and family economic empowerment) while also ensure that 1,850 children receive appropriate case management services.
Finally, the project will allow for the provision of mental health and psychosocial support to children and their caregivers in 20 functional child friendly spaces/other safe spaces while 2000 children [and caregivers] shall be able to have access to mental health or psychosocial support.
