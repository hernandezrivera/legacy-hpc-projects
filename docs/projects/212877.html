Project Summary

Through this project, ONSUR will undertake the following activities:
1) Assessment and repair of structural damage in the affected communities in Idleb Jisr Ash Shugur- Jisr Ash Shugur by targeting 310 households.
2) Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing in both Idleb-Jisr Ash Shugur Badama and Idleb-Jisr Ash Shugur Jisr Ash Shugur through the cash for rehabilitation - Self-help (type C) modality by targeting 250 households.
Assessment is the initial step, involving thorough inspections to determine the extent of damage to housing units and identify necessary repairs. Following this, a rehabilitation plan is developed based on assessment findings, outlining specific repairs and upgrades needed to restore units to a safe and livable condition. Structural repairs, particularly for units with moderate to severe damage, often involve reinforcing weakened elements like walls, floors, and roofs for stability and safety. Subsequently, plastering and painting may be required for interior and exterior walls to enhance aesthetics and protect against moisture damage. Windows and doors are maintained or replaced to improve security, insulation, and functionality. In regions with extreme temperatures, insulation materials are installed to enhance thermal comfort and energy efficiency. Quality assurance measures are implemented throughout the process to ensure work meets safety standards and adheres to the rehabilitation plan. Community involvement is encouraged, with opportunities for self-help initiatives, fostering a sense of ownership and resilience. Ultimately, the rehabilitation aims to provide safe and dignified living conditions for affected populations in Northwest Syria following conflict or natural disasters. In addition to the activities, a key aspect of this project involves implementing robust monitoring mechanisms. These mechanisms are designed to ensure the effectiveness and accountability of the rehabilitation efforts. Any issues or challenges identified during monitoring will be addressed promptly through timely corrective action as ONSUR aims to enhance transparency, accountability, and the overall success of the rehabilitation initiative.
3) Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished building common areas for the same beneficiaries of the targeted shelters for the same 250 HHs in activity 2.
4) Implementation of minor repairs to damaged or unfinished housing for 60 HHs
5) Construction of 1500 temporary transitional shelters in Aleppo Al Bab Al Bab. Each shelter, measuring 50 m2 and made of cement blocks, will consist of 2 rooms, a hall with a kitchen, and WASH facilities. Every 4 dwellings will form a residential unit supplied with a common courtyard. The shelter units will be equipped with all necessary infrastructure and supplied with energy through solar panels. Each household will have a water storage capacity of 800 liters.
6) Implementation of site-level improvements/infrastructure for the same locations where the temporary transitional shelters will be implemented. This activity includes interlock pavement, lighting poles, and sanitation and sewage systems. The shelters will adhere to protection core standards and be accessible by persons with disabilities (PWDs), ensuring no physical barriers hinder their movement within the shelter unit or in-camp roads.
7) Provision of seasonal and supplementary non-food items (NFIs), such as winterization kits consisting of 600 kg olive pomace briquettes, to be distributed in three batches within specific locations: Aleppo-Al Bab Al Bab Qabasin Ard Al Amal 3 camp established ONSUR (800 shares), in addition to 1500 shares for the prospected 1500 dignified shelters in Al Bab subdistricts. Eventually, 2300 winterization kit.
8) Distribution/ installation of emergency shelter kits/ materials ONSUR intends to distribute 300 shares in Idleb Idleb Maaret Tamsrin for 1500 individuals.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

According to HNO 2024 and SNFI cluster reports focusing on severity 3, and 4, there is a an immense need to shelter and NFI especially within the location which were affected by the 12 years old prolonged conflict and economic hardship, and the exacerbated circumstances occurred after a series of earthquakes struck northern Syria and Turkey on February 6th and 20th, 2023. These earthquakes displaced hundreds of thousands of families, resulting in nearly 5,900 fatalities and over 12,800 injuries in Syria alone. The quakes also inflicted severe damage upon shelters and infrastructure already weakened by the ongoing conflict. With many households losing their primary breadwinners due to either death or injury, the economic plight intensified, rendering millions even more vulnerable and unable to fulfill their basic needs. ONSUR plans to cover the following points: Installation/ construction of transitional shelters, and Implementation of site level improvements/ infrastructure for 9,000 IDPs: ONSUR transcended the typical ad-hoc responses by initiating a comprehensive strategy. This initiative began with a focus on urban planning. . These shelters, thoughtfully equipped with essential amenities and energy infrastructure powered by solar panels, seek to provide displaced families and individuals with dignified living spaces. Simultaneously, site-level improvements including interlock pavement, lighting installations, and sanitation systems are being implemented to enhance overall living conditions and ensure accessibility for all, including persons with disabilities.  Distribution/ installation of emergency shelter kits/ materials for 36000  Repair of structural damage and Implementation of minor repairs to damaged or unfinished housing for 360 IDPs, and host communities: At the core of the project is the meticulous assessment and repair of structural damage within targeted communities. • Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished building common areas, Rehabilitationof damaged or unfinished housing for 1500 IDPs, and host communities.ONSUR project in Northwest Syria aims to comprehensively address the pressing needs and challenges faced by communities, particularly in areas like Idleb and Jisr Ash Shugur. It encompasses a diverse array of initiatives geared towards rehabilitating damaged or incomplete housing, constructing temporary transitional shelters, and implementing site-level improvements to bolster living conditions and community resilience.Moreover, a pivotal aspect of the project involves the construction of 1500 temporary transitional shelters in Aleppo Al BabThe project encompasses the provision of seasonal and supplementary non-food items, such as winterization kits, to bolster the resilience of vulnerable populations during adverse weather conditions. By distributing these essential supplies, the project aims to alleviate immediate needs and fortify community resilience in the face of ongoing challenges.
