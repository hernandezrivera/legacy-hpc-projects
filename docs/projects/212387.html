Project Summary

This project aims to:
1-raising awareness of the local community about gender-based violence and protection
issues, including sexual exploitation and abuse, especially among the category of people
with specific needs, as they are more vulnerable to all types of violence and exploitation
2-strengthening the protection of persons with disabilities and integrating them into
society and empowering them through vocational courses and microfinance projects
based on the best interest of the child( for children aged 16-17-18 years) and the results
observed after the need assessment
The results of the assessment came as follows:
1-the need to strengthen protection and awareness of people with disabilities
2-the lack of appropriate opportunities to develop their skills and integrate them into
society and the absence of community support to provide job opportunities
3 - more than 50% of people with disabilities (mobility - deafness-other disabilities) over
the age of 16 have the ability and desire to engage in society and rely on themselves to
secure their various needs.
GBV awareness-raising sessions will be conducted by a mobile team consisting of 5
facilitators trained on gender-based violence topics within the designated areas
according to the previously mentioned assessment
Implementation of vocational courses and life skills courses targeting (children with
disabilities, their families, the surrounding community and some key people in the
targeted areas) in preparation for empowering people with disabilities later through
vocational courses and linking them with the local community to secure suitable job
opportunities for them in addition to financing small projects for about 150 beneficiaries
of people with disabilities
The mobile teams will assist in the social integration of targeted children with disabilities into local livelihood means that are of light work and do not affect their physical or mental health. The children will receive on-job trainings in the shops, barbers, libraries and other local income-generating small projects that will help them learn about the job. thus, the mobile team will accompany the children and facilitate the on-job trainings for them
