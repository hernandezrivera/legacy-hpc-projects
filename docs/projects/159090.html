Project Summary

In report of the Secretary-General at the seventy-fourth session, published on 06/08/2019, "Assistance in mine action" noted that “All kinds of explosive ordnance pose a deadly threat to girls, boys, women and men living in conflict-affected areas, hindering access to humanitarian aid and socioeconomic development and undermining the establishment of sustainable peace and progress towards the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.
According to twenty-seventh report by the OHCHR on the human rights situation in Ukraine which covers the period of 16 May to 15 August 2019, only during this reporting period mines and explosive remnants of war killed three civilians and injured nine.
Our Project proposal for "Technical Training" direct the development of a National Capability in Ukraine based on those current capacities current role of the State Emergency Services of Ukraine (SESU), which includes reacting to situations pertaining to the Explosive Remnants of War (ERW).
For to implement measures to ensure awareness-raising campaigns for the public about the danger associated with explosive ordnance, the project "Technical Training" will be held the сomprehensive 80-day courses developed by the leading international mine action center, "Praedium Consulting Malta (PCM) Ltd & Mine Action & Training (MAT) Center Kosovo".
All "Technical Training" will be conducted to International Mine Action Standards (IMAS).
All training on based "Training Centre of the Civil Defense Protection Operations and Rescue Service SESU & pyrotechnic units" in three main areas of expertise:
• A bespoke IMAS Compliant EOD ‘pyrotechnics’ Course for team members of the "State Emergency Service of Ukraine" - 20 people.
https://www.pcm-erw.com/technical-training/imas-conversion-course-imas-cc-humanitarian-mine-action-hma/
• The Community Liaison Officers course - 20 people (from public organizations and for employees of state administrations who are responsible for mine action).
https://www.pcm-erw.com/technical-training/community_liaison_officer_course/
This course focuses on the possibilities of mine risk education and public relations, as well as the skills necessary for effective training of the public in mine awareness.
This course complies with International Mine Action Standards (IMAS) and has been validated externally by the British Open College Network (OCN) - Credit4Learning NGO.
• The Humanitarian Mine Action Team Medics course - 20 people (from public health organizations).
https://www.pcm-erw.com/technical-training/humanitarian-mine-action-team-medics-course/
This course is intended for PLO humanitarian mine clearance specialists who are particularly interested in providing emergency care and treatment in remote areas, injuries sustained in aggressive environments and delayed evacuation.
MIRA Courses are approved and certified by the Royal College of Surgeons College of Advance Care Faculty (Edinburgh) and are specifically designed for those who provide medical care in harsh and hostile environments, including medical assistance for mine clearance operations, disaster relief and humanitarian assistance.
At the end of the course all students who have successfully completed “Technical Training”, an international certificate is issued.
We plan that a group of internationally certified specialists and instructors as a result of the "Technical Training", during 2019-2020, will be able to conduct training for about 20,000 civilians in areas affected by conflict and with a high level of mine danger on the Donbas.

1 - Needs

Eastern Ukraine is one of the most mined territories in the world. The presence of mines and ERWs in the region threatens the lives of millions of civilians, damages their daily lives and fulfills their professional responsibilities, including in the agricultural, municipal, coal, chemical, metallurgical and other fields.Also, as a result of the armed conflict in Donetsk and Luhansk regions, Ukraine is one of the most affected by war mines and explosive effects (ERW) of the countries of the world.Much of the liberated territory of Donetsk and Luhansk regions, with a total area of about 7,000 km2, is contaminated by ERW.The demining of the contaminated ERW territories of the Donbas can take decades, so there is a constant need to undertake measures to reduce the social, economic and environmental impact of the ERW on the life and activities of the population, in particular through information and training on safe behavior.In order to preserve the life of civilians and restore their normal functioning, it is necessary to undertake a set of appropriate measures such as:- Education and training of the national potential of mine action specialists to carry out information campaigns aimed at reducing the risks to the life and health of the local population.- Implementation of information campaigns aimed at reducing the risks to the life and health of the local population (at work, study and / or place of residence) by conducting ERW risk awareness measures, safe conduct rules in case of ERW detection.For the rest of the territory of Ukraine (except the east of Ukraine) the aim of the information campaign should be community associations, civil servants, government officials and donors around the theme of mine risk education, mine and ERW impact on the development of the affected regions and Ukraine in general.The action of mine action information campaign should be extended to Donetsk and Lugansk regions of Ukraine and additional measures to all other territory of Ukraine.The target audience of the mine action information campaign is as follows:1) Population of Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine:- children and adolescents of school age;- unemployed and / or working population - (women / men) whose work activity is directly related to places of potential danger;- local population living in the territory where the hostilities took place;- other civilians;2) Additional measures - population of the whole territory of Ukraine;3) Civil servants, statesmen, donors (in order to attract attention and highlight the need to implement mine action measures (MAM) as a public policy priority for donor assistance). 2 - Activities & outcomes

The activities under this project are primarily is to provide national capacity development through; technical advice, assistance, specialist training to improve, develop, expand & enhance the current capacity & capability of specialists in mine action for the implementation of the information campaigns is to reduce the risks to the life and health of the local population (at the place of work, study and / or place of residence) by informing about the danger associated with ERW, the rules for safe behavior in case of detection of ERW and aimed at solution of the following tasks:The task №1: Conduct & deliver a bespoke IMAS Compliant EOD ‘pyrotechnics’ Course for team members of the "State Emergency Service of Ukraine".The task №2: Conduct & deliver IMAS Basic "Community Liaison Officers course" from public organizations and for employees of state administrations who are responsible for mine action.The task №3: Conduct & deliver IMAS Basic "Humanitarian Mine Action Team Medics course" for public health organizations.The task №4: Start to conduct & deliver IMAS EOD 3 & EOD 3 to team leaders identified for advanced training.The task №5: Provide promotion of safe behavior in settlements where there is a constant risk of ERW through trained teams of mine action specialists. Raising awareness among the population of different social groups and ages to more effectively shape the overall idea of the danger of ERW.The task № 6: Drawing attention to the importance of identifying the dangers associated with ERW through the installation of warning signs, in particular that the damage / theft of warning signs endanger the lives and health of the population living in the affected areas and entail administrative responsibility.The task № 7: Organization and conduct of roundtables on hazard awareness related to explosive objects, safe conduct rules if they are detected, and to draw more attention to ERW threats, the importance of carrying out mine action activities, including information and clearance / demining of territories; bringing together donor societies around the topic of ERW risk to protect the public, the development of the affected regions and Ukraine as a whole.Expected results:Increasing public awareness of the risks of ERWs, reducing risks through the promotion of safe behavior among civilians, including those not controlled by the Ukrainian government, in settlements where there is a threat to the safe life of ERWs.According to the results of "Technical Training" should be prepared, no less than 60 internationally certified experts of pyrotechnics SESU and public mine action specialists.According to the results of the information campaign should be covered in the Donetsk and Luhansk regions at least:- population of 30 settlements located on the demarcation line (with coverage of at least 6000 people);- population of 20 settlements (no more than 3 settlements in one district (with coverage of at least 4000 people);- population of 40 settlements (no more than 3 settlements in one district) located in the other territory of Ukraine (except Donetsk and Luhansk regions) (with coverage of at least 4000 people);- pupils of 60 educational establishments (no more than 5 educational establishments in one district);- 4 enterprises in the water sector;- 4 enterprises of the energy sector of Donetsk and Lugansk regions;- 2 coal enterprises;- 2 enterprises of the agro-industrial sector;- 2 enterprises of the chemical sector of Donetsk and Lugansk regions.- 2 metallurgical enterprises.At least three mine action roundtables should be held:- in Severodonetsk, Luhansk region;- in Kramatorsk, Donetsk region;- in Kyiv

3 - Indicators & targets

The project provides for the development & introduction of relevant mine action EOD methods & techniques specific to ERW threat in Eastern Ukraine for mine action in line with international & national standards.This will be basis of any framework & recommendations to ensure that in mine action is more efficient, more productive & in line with International Standards, Best Practices & modern technology available to safely conduct & coordinate information companies for humanitarian mine action in Eastern Ukraine successfully.Measures aimed at reducing the social, economic and environmental impact of explosive objects on the lives and activities of the population (mine action) and informing the population about the dangers of explosive objects are based on the following regulatory framework for the implementation of the mine action information campaign:- Convention on the Prohibition of the Use, Stockpiling, Production and Transfer of Anti-personnel Mines and on their Destruction, of 18 September 1997;- Convention on Prohibitions or Restrictions on the Use of Certain Conventional Weapons Which May Be Deemed to Be Excessively Injurious or to Have Indiscriminate Effects, of 10 October 1980;- Protocol on Explosive Items - Consequences of the War on the Convention on the Prohibition or Restriction of the Use of Specific Conventional Weapons, which may Be Considered as Excessive Damage or Indiscriminate Effect, Approved at the Meeting of States Parties to the Convention in Geneva on 28 November 2003. ;- Law of Ukraine “On Access to Public Information”;- Law of Ukraine “On Mine Action in Ukraine”;- Resolution of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine of April 17, 2019 No. 372 “On Approving the Rules for Marking the Dangers Related to Mines and Explosive Objects - Consequences of War”;- IMAS 12.20 - Implementation of programs and projects for the education of mine risk and the effects of war;- IMAS 14.20 - Evaluation of programs and projects on mine risk education and the effects of war.The following communication style is used to implement the information campaign regarding the dangers associated with ERW and the rules of safe conduct in the event of ERW detection by: defining goals, objectives, communication channels, examining the current (at the beginning of the information campaign and after completion) public awareness situation the dangers associated with ERW; safe conduct rules in case of ERW detection.With population of Donetsk and Luhansk regions:- Convince and convey information that ERW is a major threat and danger and lay the foundations for safe conduct if ERW is detected;With the population of Ukraine, including internally displaced persons:- to convey information that ERW is a great threat and danger, to lay down the basics of safe behavior rules in case of detection of ERW in any territory of Ukraine, to form and introduce emotional and explanatory messages among people living in all territory of Ukraine, including emotions of sympathy, responsiveness, desire to help the affected population of Donetsk and Luhansk regions;With civil servants, statesmen and donors:To convey understanding, that the presence of ERW causes additional civilian casualties, including children, which has a catastrophic effect on the environment, impedes the socio-economic development of the region and the State as a whole. There is an urgent need to identify humanitarian mine clearance and mine risk awareness measures and ERWs as one of the main areas of national development.For feedback from the public - Facebook "Donbass without mines" public group: https://www.facebook.com/groups/353130225132991/
