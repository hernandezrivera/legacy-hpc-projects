Project Summary

By the end of 2023, during the implementation of one of Saed's projects within several camps in Jandairis subdistrict, observations provided by the project team, along with feedback obtained through feedback mechanisms and monitoring activities, revealed a severe gap in the camp management and coordination sector in 80 camps. These camps were newly established in response to the February 6 earthquake. Consequently, Saed's team conducted an initial assessment of the needs of these camps. Here is a summary of the results:
- There is almost complete absence of administrative structures in these camps, in addition to weak coordination and monitoring operations by the management of these camps.
- There are no measures to prevent or mitigate fires.
- There are numerous minor malfunctions and damages in the infrastructure of these camps that require immediate repairs.
On another note, in 2022, Saed implemented a project involving the provision of 1480 fire extinguishers in 55 camps in Dana, Harim , Idlib . These fire extinguishers need to be refilled for the continued benefit of this project.
The project aims to improve the living conditions for 76395 individuals (17956 men,18302women, 20841boys, 19296 girls) in 80 camps in Jandaris district, Afrin region, Aleppo governorate, by implementing a comprehensive set of interventions in camp management and coordination sector. Additionally, it seeks to continue enhancing the capacities of 126402 displaced persons (25,537 men, 29,730 women, 35,801 boys, 35,334 girls) in 55 camps in Dana, Harim, and Ma'arat Misrin subdistirct of Idlib governorate through supporting the sustainability of a previous project in the CCCM sector.
The objectives of this proposal will be achieved through the following interventions and activities:
• Enhancing humanitarian aid monitoring and coordination in 54 camps in Jandaris district by:
- Establishing trained and gender-balanced camp management structures in 45 camps in Jandaris district. This activity will be implemented during the first three months of the project (March 1, 2023 - May 31, 2024).
- Continuing to provide technical support to camp management committee members throughout the project duration (20 months).
•Strengthening the fire preparedness and response capacities of residents in 15 camps in Jandaris district by:
- Establishing 80 fire points and equipping them with recommended equipment by the cluster. (March 1, 2024 - May 31, 2024)
- Distribution of different fire mitigation tools for80 camps ( Supplying fire points with equipment )
- 54 Awareness sessions on fire mitigation/prevention for the camp population ( man and women)
- Form and train 80 community-based Fire prevention committees ( 40% Women ) and Continuing to provide technical support to fire response committees throughout the project duration and maintaining equipment and refilling fire extinguishers as needed. These activities will be implemented for a duration of 20 months (8 months in 2024 and 12 months in 2025).
• Enhancing the ability of displaced persons to maintain infrastructure, conduct repairs, and maintain facilities and services in 80
- Forming 80 repair and maintenance committees in the targeted camps, each consisting of 10 members, and training these committees on light maintenance and repair work, and providing them with basic maintenance tools. These activities will be implemented during the first four months of the project in 2024.
- Continuing to provide limited technical and financial support to these committees. These activities will be implemented for a duration of 20 months (8 months in 2024 and 12 months in 2025).
• Continuing to enhance the capacities of 126402displaced persons in 80 camps in Harim, Dana, of Idlib governorate to respond to fires by:
- Refilling and maintaining 1,480 fire extinguishers distributed previously in the framework of a previous project twice, first in 2024 and second in 2025.
