Project Summary

This project is aimed at adopting a more comprehensive approach to achieve sustainability by linking trainees directly with work, thus creating an integrated product cycle and self-funding later, by the making utmost use of 3 training centers located in Atareb, Abzemo, and Atma, which are fully equipped in the three target areas. Besides, we will launch a fourth center in Afrin.
In addition to investment in critical infrastructure for high-return which will directly contribute to the recovery of the living and economic situation in the targeted areas. This will contribute as a result to the provision of resources and money spent on imported goods and fuel for power generation without revenue. Moreover, the project provides strengthening resilience and creating jobs.
Some 17,000 direct beneficiaries will benefit of various services and trainings.
The intervention will be in 4 areas as follows:
Atareb and Atma
1-Provision of vocational and industrial trainings for 360 beneficiaries, 200 in Atma and 160 in Atareb.
2-Benefiting from the graduates of most trainings in the rehabilitation of schools, especially relevant trainings, through conditioned contracting with the contractors to make paid use of graduate trainees.
3-Establishing 2 workshops for the training centers to produce school, home and office furniture.
Through the mentioned activities, we will achieve:
-Linking trainees with the labor market and productive practical experience.
-Making use of the proceeds of productive workshops to have vocational training courses back, create a kind of sustainability, permanent jobs and reduce dependence on external funding.
-Improving educational services provided and providing a safer and more appropriate environment for the beneficiary children.
-Enhancing integration between displaced and resident trainees during the above mentioned activities.
In Afrin
We will launch a training center in Afrin and provide trainings for 200 beneficiaries. The theoretical trainings will be given to the trainees in this centre, while the trainees will be moved to Atma center near Afrin to receive practical training in order to take advantage of the technical and industrial equipment at Atma Center. This will reduce costs to minimum and provide education in a professional and practical way.
In Abzemo
1-Provide vocational trainings for 160 beneficiaries, including small business and grants for outstanding projects.
2-Benefiting from graduates of most trainings in the rehabilitation of infrastructure through conditioned contracting with the contractors to benefit from the graduates of the trainings.
3-Infrastructure rehabilitation and restoration projects will focus on ensuring the sustainability of the supply of water and electricity, achieving a financial revenue through them to continue the development of infrastructure as follows:
1) Drilling a well and establishing a water source that depends on renewable energy that provides its services free of charge to residents and sells water to commercial tankers.
2) The establishment of an electricity distribution center based on renewable energy to feed the commercial market.
3) Construction of a water well near agricultural lands, depending on renewable energy.
4) Using renewable energy for the wells in the south of town.
4-Forming a consensual management committee in the town of Abzemo to supervise the implementation of the previous activities, manage these projects and invest their revenues for use in other development projects within the town.
Through this committee, we will work to achieve:
-Allocating part of the proceeds of these projects to restore the vocational training cycle, achieve some kind of sustainability and reduce dependence on external support.
-Strengthening integration between IDPs and residents.
-Promoting the role of women and local community in a process of sustainable development and promoting resilience.
-Providing new and permanent jobs.

Needs

According to a report issued by the World Bank at the end of 2017, the number of unemployed Syrian people, or not involved in study or training, reached about 9 million people, which means that the unemployment rate reached 78%, mostly young people, especially in the liberated areas where there are the largest numbers of young people from the region or people fleeing from security pursuits or forced recruitment by the regime forces, especially in Atma town and recently Atareb and Abzimo because of the relatively good security situation compared to other areas and the resulting renaissance in the field of construction and reconstruction. Syria lost about 80% of its craftsmen, which is considered the biggest losses as the long time needed for Restoration and getting skilled labor.- The massive destruction of the liberated areas and the need for qualified personnel (in reconstruction) to Conduct this process in light of the stability in some liberated areas.In addition to the above and need assessment conducted by ACU about education, needs assessment for the 4 targeted areas conducted by Afaq and a focus group discussions were about the most pressing needs and activities that can be conducted to meet these needs in a more integrated and creative manner, creating as such a kind of complementarity and sustainability, so that it seeks to contribute to meeting the Existing needs of:- Rehabilitation of schools in Atareb and Atmeh which suffer from very high numbers of pupils due to high population density in those areas and a result of the large displacement in addition to the few numbers of good schools functioning and providing educational services.- Rehabilitation and development of the infrastructure and movement to renewable energy in Abzimo which suffer, like other regions, from lack of water resources or the availability of wells in remote areas as well as lack of power supply except for the energy generated by diesel generators, which cause air pollution and noise let alone the large sums of money spent without a benefit.- At the same time, it provides vocational and industrial training according to the needs of each region, linking most graduates directly with labor markets the thing which contributes to reducing rampant unemployment in targeted areas and creating new and sustainable jobs. - Forming a consensual administrative committee of displaced persons and residents of both sexes in the town of Abzimo to supervise the implementation of projects in the town and manage them subsequently, thus promoting social cohesion, which has been severely affected by the competition for resources and employment opportunities, in addition to strengthening the role of women and their involvement in society, and making use of proceeds to develop the infrastructure.- Utilizing the proceeds of the production workshops in Atareb and Atma as well as the proceeds of investment projects in renewable electricity energy in the financing of the vocational trainings later reducing as such the need for external funding for these trainings. - Lack of employment opportunities, expertise and resources for a very wide segment of women. According to a press report by the Financial Times in 2019, a significant change occurred to the role of women in the fields of employment leading them to work in fields that were recently the exclusive preserve of men. The decline in the number of men changed the shape of the labor market and led more women to enter this market. In recent years, however, companies and laboratories have been forced to hire women because there are not enough men looking for work, and because of the need for women to support their families.- Poverty rates have increased to unprecedented levels, reaching 85% of the total population.- 6.5 million people face severe food shortages, and 4 million people are at risk of becoming food insecure, according to the 2018 Humanitarian Needs Overview Report.
