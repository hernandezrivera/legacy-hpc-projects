Project Summary

The locations of Lakes and Western Equatoria continue to face inter-sectoral needs in the thematic areas of Food Security, Nutrition, WASH, Health and Protection. The agro – pastoralist nature of populations in the counties targeted has exposed population to severe food insecurity and malnutrition due to low food availability, diversity and access. The high levels of acute food insecurity in the counties targeted are driven by flooding, insecurity, population displacements, declined crop production, diseases and pests, the economic crisis, the effects of disease, limited access to basic services, and the cumulative effects of prolonged years of asset depletion and loss of livelihoods.
The project will target persons through cluster objectives CO2 and CO3 reaching 30,000 persons with much needed humanitarian support. The counties of Yirol East and Tambura are targeted for mainly the lean season, a period of 6 months (April to September 2025) with much needed support to the most vulnerable households.

1. Needs

The Food security and livelihoods situation in the states of Lakes and Western Equatoria continue to decline with projections indicting a worsening situation affecting mostly women, children and the elderly. According to the latest Integrated Phase Classification (IPC) report, in the first projection period of December 2024 to March 2025, an estimated 5.82 million people (48.3% of the population) will likely face Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity. During this period, an estimated 1.79 million people are likely to face Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity. In the second projection period. An estimated 7.24 million people (60% of the population) are likely to face Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, During this period, an estimated 2.47 million are likely to be in Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity. The most severe acute food insecurity conditions are in counties where chronic vulnerabilities have been exacerbated by a combination of shocks, including floods, insecurity incidents, the macro-economic crisis. In Lakes State Yirol East is classified as IPC phase 3, with phase 4 pockets anticipated. The high levels of acute food insecurity in the counties targeted are driven by flooding, insecurity, population displacements, declined crop production, diseases and pests, the economic crisis, limited access to basic services, and the cumulative effects of prolonged years of asset depletion and loss of livelihoods. The continuing currency depreciation and associated impacts on commodity prices and incomes coupled continue to erode the purchasing power of vulnerable households who rely on markets to purchase food and other basic needs. Seasonal scarcity of food, the flooding in 2024 (with limited recovery from the previous floods), and the much lower than required humanitarian food assistance in the face of increased needs, will likely result in an increase of acute food insecurity during the projection periods, especially during the lean season. About 1.4 million children under five years are expected to suffer from acute malnutrition in 2025 based on same season historical data of food security and nutrition monitoring system, SMART nutrition surveys and admission trends for 2024. This is the highest caseload for acute malnutrition since the start of the crisis. Further deterioration of the nutrition situation is projected during the lean season. The major factors contributing to acute malnutrition include high prevalence of diseases (up to 36%), poor quality and diversity of food (Minimum Acceptable Diet: 7%, Minimum Dietary Diversity: 15%). Furthermore, poor access to health and nutrition services due to heightened inter-communal conflict and flooding are also contributing to acute malnutrition. Access disruptions, including those rightfully implemented to curb infection rates, as well as changes in SAM and MAM referral protocols for children have exacerbated lack of access to services.Low food availability, diversity and access have all contributed to the worsened food security situation.Majority of the populations depend on livestock as a major livelihoods source. Low levels of vaccinations, treatment as well as loss of livelihoods through rustlings, death and displacement have all affected community livelihoods.A robust food security and livelihoods response is required in these counties in order to improve the living standard and well-being of targeted communities.

2. Project activities

Outreach Initiative plans mainly a lean season response (April - September) in the counties of Yirol East and Tombura through the following activities; - Provision of Emergency food & livelihood support through distribution of seeds, tools, fishing kits/ vegetable kits; rapid response kits (fish kit/ cow pea & three variety of fast maturing vegetable seed)• Livestock treatment and vaccination; livestock disease surveillance, Community Animal Health Workers Training and inputs support, cold chain establishment and maintenance, prevention & treatment. • Support to seed fairs using locally sourced seed; seed security & multiplication strengthening/ Support market functionality. • Cash & voucher-based transfers with food security/ nutrition objectives. • Support value chain addition e.g. through agriculture, fishery and livestock products/ by-products preservation & marketing• Training & capacity building: both adaptive and absorptive on-farm production (farmer/agro-pastoral field schools, demonstration plots), income generation (business skills), vocational training etc. - Prioritisation of HHs with high levels of malnutrition, victims of GBV, SEA and high disease burdens for FSL support, creating linkages for referrals as well as multi-sectoral responses to achieve robust impact. - Inclusive and participatory messaging on key drivers of vulnerability as a disaster risk reduction strategy, including messaging on safe community practices, hygiene, dietary diversification, livelihood opportunities, girl child and youth empowerment amongst others. - Safety audits, and AAP consultations conducted during the entire project cycle, from inception, implementation and closeout stages.
