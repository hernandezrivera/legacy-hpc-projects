Project Summary

Plan International aims at mitigating the impact of COVID 19 by enhancing awareness raising amongst communities including especially educators and learners of primary school-going age during this humanitarian crisis. Given that the lockdown period was preceded by sudden school closures, the movement restrictions, isolation, overcrowding and high levels of stress and anxiety are increasing the likelihood that children are most in dire of age-appropriate information that counter negative experiences, unprotective community environments including those that perpetrate physical, psychological and sexual abuse. Such is most likely to be impacting particularly those already living in violent or dysfunctional family situations. While on-line communities have made effort towards maintaining learning, support and play, it is also increasing their exposure to cyberbullying, risky on-line behavior and sexual exploitation. The situation is aggravated because children in most communities are currently isolated from the supportive individuals or structures that contribute to providing safe spaces within the community structures that facilitate access to resources and structured programmes usually designed through formal institutions such as schools.
It is without doubt that communities, learners and educators need to constantly be reminded of ways they can protect themselves and their closest contacts from contracting the disease. With a lot of false information and myths circulating on social media causing panic and anxiety, there is need to minimize this through dissemination of accurate information.
Plan International intends to achieve this through very close collaboration with the Ministry of Health and Child Care (MoHCC), Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE) and other stakeholders in utilization of community radios, national TV, bulk SMS/text messaging platforms as well as other forms of digital media. Although the beneficiaries of this intervention include people of all age groups who have access to a radio, TV , cellphone and digital media, children, educators and caregivers remain the main targets. Children will benefit from child-friendly, age appropriate and gender sensitive messages that will be disseminated especially through radio and TV. Infants and young children will also benefit from the preventative behaviors that their parents and guardians will gain and adopt after they receive the necessary information through our communications initiatives.
