Project Summary

According to the national level, the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) has remained Serious (10– 14.9%) over the past three seasons: 2021 Gu (11.1%), 2020 Deyr (11.8%), and 2020 Gu (11.8%). The FSNAU findings in Bay and Bakool confirms a global acute malnutrition (GAM) prevalence of 11.9 % (8.7 - 16.0 95% C.I.) while the prevalence of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) in the survey was 1.2 % (0.5 - 2.7 95% C.I.). DMO strategy prioritizes 14% of families with Persons with Disabilities, Minority clans, child-headed Households, the poorest, IDPs,Returnees and those affected by floods and droughts with child-bearing persons.
DMO works to reduce continuous loss of life for the overall 3.1 million of the most severely vulnerable people by decreasing hunger, acute malnutrition, public health threats and outbreaks, and abuse and violence among the stressed families.
Our major focus is:
• Provide community mobilization and sensitisation activities for screening and referral of malnourished children.
• Community based malnutrition detection and identiﬁcation of acute malnutrition through CHWs and Mother-led MUAC Screenings for children 6-59 months and PLWGs..
• Scale up community based Mother-to-Mother support groups for PLWGs.
• Screening of 7,000 children (0-59 months) of whom 3,000 boys, 4000, girls and 1,500 PLWGs for acute malnutrition
• Life-saving treatment of 5,500 children with SAM
• Life-saving treatment of 1500 Children with MAM
• Conduct individual and group IYCF protocol sessions including counselling among Pregnant and Lactating Women and caregivers of children aged 0 to 23 months.
• Administer lifesaving therapeutic treatment for Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) and Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM).
• Deworming,
• Execution of multiple micronutrient supplementation and Vitamin A as part of Outpatient management of acute malnutrition..
• Provide breastfeeding counselling and re-lactation in OTP centres for acutely malnourished children less than 6 months of Age.
• Provide Nutrition, Health, and Hygiene Promotion Training (NHHP).
• Capacity building of Nutrition workers and CNWs to implement CMAM through workshop trainings and on-job trainings.
• Supplementary feeding to prevent deterioration into acute malnutrition or relapse in children discharged from OTP .
• Setting up 7 fixed nutrition sites (4 OTP/TSFP, 3 SC) and two integrated mobile outreach services
• Capacitating / setting up Stabilization centers for impatient treatment of children with SAM complications.
• Awareness of how Sexual and Gender Based Violence along with gender discrimination leads to food and nutrition insecurity and disaster.
• Distribution of Covid 19 protocol mitigation package including masks and sensitisation.

1. - Projects activities

This project calls for urgent and timely life-saving intervention through nutrition among the extremely vulnerable under- five, and pregnant and lactating women who will likely face acute malnutrition and death. Focus may consider the elderly facing nutrition crisis. DMO prefers integrated approach for nutrition OTS, TSFP and Stabilization centers. Our major focus is:• Establishing 7 fixed nutrition sites (4 OTP/TSFP, 3 SC) and two integrated mobile outreach services• Setting up Stabilization centers for impatient treatment of children with SAM complications.• continuous consultation and treatment of under and over five children-curative• Screening of 7,000 children (0-59 months) of whom 3,000 boys, 4000, girls and 1,500 Pregnant and Lactating Women and Girls for acute malnutrition• Immediate life-saving treatment of 5,500 children with SAM • Immediate life-saving treatment of 1500 Children with MAM• Conducting individual and group IYCF protocol sessions including counselling among Pregnant and Lactating Women and caregivers of children aged 0 to 23 months. • IYCF protocol promotion, demonstration, counseling and messaging targeting 1500 PLW• Micronutrient supplement ion for children• Training of nutrition staff and CNWs• Sensitisation against Sexual Gender Based Violence this includes awareness of how Sexual and Gender Based Violence along with gender discrimination leads to food and nutrition insecurity and disaster.• Screening for COVID-19 and distribution of Covid 19 protocol mitigation package including masks and sensitization• Purchase and distribution of ANTI-COVID 19 PROTOCOL AID TOOLKIT• Receiving nutrition supplies (RUTF, RUSF) and PPE supplies from UNICEF and UNWFP for distribution to the targeted persons. As well as nutrition promotion, community engagement for IYCF promotion will contribute to improving the nutrition statuses of the vulnerable and marginalized communities in the supported regions. • Coordination with the MOH and UNICEF to ensure all children below 5 years are vaccinated ensuring a high coverage rate especially on measles, Polio• Purchase and distribution of sanitary kits to 5, 500 Women and Girls• All beneficiary households including those with Disabilities and affected by COVID 19, child-headed houses, Minorities, the Elderly, IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities.• Nutrition Workers (CNWs) to be admitted into the MAM program by the qualified nutrition nurses. Children with a MUAC measurement of ≥ 11.5cm and < 12.5cm or WFH Z-score ≥ -3 SD and < -2 SD and no bilateral oedema are designated as MAM and will be admitted into the TSFP. Children will also be referred into TSFP upon discharge from an OTP.• inpatient treatment services for children with SAM complications.• Installation of solar-powered electricity and increasing the operational tools and essentials such as beds, furniture, thermostats • Multiple micronutrients supplementation for pregnant and lactating women and Girls ; vitamin A, iron, iodine and folate are particularly common during pregnancy, due to increased nutrient requirements of the mother and developing foetus• Monthly and quarterly project monitoring and reporting to Nutrition Cluster and UNOCHA, project review, reflection, lesson learning and best practices policy sharing and makingTraining and Capacity building through • inpatient training (SAM with complications, Measuring and preventing Malnutrition• -Management of Acute Malnutrition with complications• Health Interventions in the stablisation centre• HIV-AIDS and Nutrition• COVID 19 protocols and mitigations, disposals to avoid environmental pollution• Gender effects and impact on food and nutrition security• Training of community nutrition workers
