Project Summary

The main purpose of the proposed project is to provide protective and social services to vulnerable children (boys & girls) and women in 2 target districts (Jhang & Rajanpur) of Punjab. Under this project, Sanjh Preet Organization will establish 20 Child Protection Centers (CPCs) in 2 targeted districts (10 in each district). The project will primarily focus on those children (boys and girls) who are at risk of violence, exploitation, neglect, abuse, discrimination and exploitation to provide them supportive and protective environment in the target communities. The proposed interventions will enhance the knowledge of target communities on child protection and empowered them on monitoring, preventing, responding and reporting on the child protection issues. The project interventions are designed in line with the child protection sub-cluster objective of the Humanitarian Response Plan 2021. The project will target 12,000 individuals including 10,000 children (52% girls), 2000 adults (1,200 women and 800 men) for the provision of protective services. SPO will establish 20 Child Protection Centers (CPCs) in two targeted districts (10 in each district) for the provision of protective and social services to children (boys and girls) and women including Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS), and recreational activities, delivery of child protection information and messages, life skills-based education and social services (identification and case referrals through monitoring, integration, community mobilization).
The project will also form 40 community-based Child Protection Committees (20 in each district) (20 M and 20 F) and 40 Adolescent Groups (20 B and 20 G) 2 in each Child Protection Center within the targeted communities. These CP Centers will be managed by 40 trained Child Protection Facilitators (preferably 20 M and 20 F). The project will also hire 10 Child Protection Monitors along with four Psychosocial Counsellors. These project staff will be engaged in identifying, monitoring, reporting & referral of vulnerable cases to service providers. The trained psychosocial counselors will identify children and women in need of psychosocial support through structured and appropriate psychosocial support services. The project team will organize orientation sessions on various child protection issues like child Abuse, Violence and child labor, missing, separated and unaccompanied children, child marriages, children conflict with the law, psychosocial issues. Women and girls will be given special attention in protecting them from abuse, violence, early child/forced marriages and from violence and abuse.
The CPM will also be responsible for monitoring the overall protection situation of girls, boys & women, identifying protection issues and finding out solutions to those issues in consultation with the Child Protection Committees, identifying vulnerable children and ensuring their access to basic services. They will conduct a mapping exercise to identify the existing service providers, make linkages with them and establish a strong referral mechanism in the target area. The most vulnerable children and adults will also be referred for specialized services to psychiatrist in nearby towns. They will also hold awareness sessions on protection issues and, disseminate information and promote dialogue among families and communities on stigma and Violence against Children (VAC) and GBV prevention.
Furthermore, SPO, throughout the project implementation, will build strong coordination at all levels including communities, local districts authorities, and the Political Administration of target districts. SPO will also maintain regular coordination at representative and technical level with CP Sub Cluster, Protection Cluster including GBV sub-cluster, and UNICEF in order to keep them updated on the project progress and related constraints and challenges.

The CPC’s will be equipped with structured, age and gender appropriate activities.2. Formation and strengthen of Child Protection Committees and Adolescent Groups: SPO will form 40 Child Protection Committees (20 M and 20 F) and these committees will comprise of project beneficiaries (direct & indirect), 400 active community members (Males 200 & Females 200) will identified to ensure the community participation in implementing of the proposed project and trained them on CPiE and community bases child protection mechanism. These committees would be involved in identification, monitoring and responding to the child protection issues. 40 Adolescent Groups (20 boys and 20 girls) groups will be formed and trained. 3. Provision of Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) Services: The project will register 10,000 children (52% girls) and 1,200 women in 20 CPC’s. These children will be provided with mental health and psycho-social support through regular sessions, counselling, recreational activities, indoor and outdoor games and drawings. Children (boys and girls) and women with psychological distress will be identified and their case history will be developed for further actions including psycho-social support or referral to service providers. 4. Disseminate information & promote dialogue among families and communities on stigma &Violence against Children/women: The project will develop & deliver appropriate messages/ information specifically related to the protection concerns of Children & women.
