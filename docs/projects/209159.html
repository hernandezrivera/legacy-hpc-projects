Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to contribute to reducing violations of children's rights and empowering children with regard to protection. Development of life skills and innovation in different area:
- Children and youth ( aggregated by age) in the targeted areas / communities will be well equipped with knowledge and skills to protect themselves from any types of exploitation and abuses.
- Children and and youth will lead change and contribute to the positive change in their communities.
- Children, youth and the community members will be psychologically treated and attended
- Surviving children will be able to recover by being referred to the relevant institutions, depending on the type of service they need.
- Knowledge and access to the existing services will be enhanced in partnership with relevant partners.
- Strengthening societal systems for child protection and responsive to children's needs and priorities
- Communities will be empowered to publicly address cases of violence against children.
Best practices will be documented and shared

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Gender: The project will address the different gender roles and division of labor of the diverse members of the host communities, Refugee and returnee including children and adolescents (dis-aggregated by gender and age). By looking at decision making processes with these three categories, opportunities and challenges will be highlighted well. Special attention will be given to female headed household needs. The project will ensure full engagement of women, men, children and adolescents in the design and planning stage of the program to ensure that their needs and priorities are addressed. During the implementation stage women and girls will be part of the activities and so is the management structures. Community protection networks and committees and monitoring teams will have women and adolescent membership. All training activities will be designed to meet the need of boys and girls and will have equal representation of male and female, boys and girls. The project gender strategy is based on:- Putting more emphasis on equal opportunities to girls and boys in accessing services making available by the project. - Integrating gender awareness in all project activities by organising meetings, discussions, and dialogue with the community in general and community leaders specifically. Equity:The project is designed to ensure an inclusive integration approach of protection mainstreaming ,all targeted communities’ members with focus on children and adolescents. This means that the project will be consider ethnicity, age and disability. YMCP has a written child protection policy which ensures equity of all children attending activities through actively promoting child protection awareness, good practices. Sustainability: working through the community-based child protection networks and building on the existing system of social works and native administration will provide a mechanism to ensure sustainability. The Seed fund and the linkages with the line ministries such as ministry of Education and Social Welfare will ensure a level of sustainability.
