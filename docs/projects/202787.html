1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1. Mental health: A program designed to reach individuals through awareness and outreach sessions including individuals as the community stakeholders/owners of the project implementation in 9 locations. The program aims at providing individual psychiatric consultations, therapy and treatment, as well as training sessions to mapped PHCs to ensure patient care continuity and follow up. Psychotropic and restricted medications will be prescribed by psychiatrists on individual basis based on each consultation on prescription sheets. Patients will be able to present the prescription to the assigned PHC to dispense their medications. 2. Insulation of tents for support of refugee families in Arsal: An emergency response to provide essential insulation to families during the climate crisis with temperature extremes. 3. An extension activity from the home healthcare program in 2021 and 2022: designed to provide vital primary care and treatment for chronic disease to disables patients in Beirut. The program targets patients with no access to healthcare (elderly, non-mobile) and provides patients with much needed lifesaving chronic medications. Patients who require further workup and interventions are paired up with PHCs for continuity.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Activities target vulnerable older patients and vulnerable youth. MedGlobal considers implementation of protective and inclusive approach as an ongoing process. Older patients are a vulnerable group susceptible to COVID-19 and Influenza, exploitation and abuse. In practice, our programs are implemented in teams (trained PSEA staff), providing consultations in the presence of family members. This means that for every new context in which the organization operates, the causes and various forms of risks will be assessed with respect to vulnerable groups and their safety provided in that specific context. For communicable diseases, deployed teams implement IPC measures, mask and sanitation compliance with each visit. Another factor in our programs is gender cohesions. The proposed projects take into consideration gender dynamics within the target population The proposed responses under these projects address and mitigate any gender differences in accessing the healthcare services.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

MedGlobal takes into account the community's feedback and opinion through satisfaction surveys to assess the project impacts and quality of response in addressing peoples' needs. We provide direct contact for beneficiaries to reach out to partners by phone for complaints or concerns that need to be addressed. Assessment of impact is done through initial and end of line surveys to better understand needs on the ground and plan for improving future interventions to cater to beneficiary’s specific needs.
