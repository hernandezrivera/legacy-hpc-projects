Project Summary

For years, the Northeast of Nigeria has been plagued by insurgency and a dire humanitarian crisis. The intense focus on humanitarian and counter-violence activities, coupled with increasingly complex conflict dynamics in the region, has exposed gaps in the international community’s response to new and emerging sources of violence. Tensions between IDPs and host communities, resistance of communities to accept former fighters and their victims, fear of IDPs to return home and face tensions with those who stayed behind – these are just several evolving sources of conflict. If these issues are not addressed in a timely manner through locally- owned solutions, they have the potential to intensify and worsen the on-going crisis in the NE. Therefore, WINN wishes to address existing and newly emerging tensions and conflicts to prevent Borno state from falling further into a vicious cycle of conflict and poverty.
WINN will use five main strategy to implement this project;
1.Strenghthen Community Resilience Group to identity early warning signs of conflict
2. Promote peaceful co-existence among Community and IDPs youth through Youth civil engagement programme.
3. Organise Dialogue Forum for community stakeholders and Government stakeholder.
4. Support youth and most vulnerable through skill trainings and economic empowerment.
5. Awareness rising and sensitization on Conflict management and stabilization

1 - Needs

Below are the needs to be addressed 1. There is an urgent need to restore livelihood and empowerment opportunities in-order for the returnees and IDPs to be (Youth, boys, girls, men and women) reintegrated to their normal life. This could include life skill acquisition trainings, self help micro economic skills, and micro and small business enterprises. This should be derived from community consultations. The essence of this is to enhance community development and recovery and also to contribute to ERL sector HRP.2. There is also an urgent need to promote social cohesion among the IDPs, Returnees, host communities, religious groups and ethnic groups, through dialogue forums, sensitization and Awareness campaigns, and youth civic engagement in their community. This is essential in other to address negative mindset and tensions between IDPs, host communities and returnees, this has leads to resistance of communities to accept former fighters and their victims and also the fear of IDPs to return home and face tensions with those who stayed behind. 3. This program is using a community-based approach with focus primarily on transforming relationships; and it seeks as well to bring together groups which have been divided due to conflict to work together to achieve sustainable peace. This process is a participatory community forum which will be used to build social capital in divided communities by providing safe spaces for interaction, communication and joint decision-making. Such processes can help to overcome mistrust and set a precedent for peaceful and constructive management of local disputes.

2 - Activities

The following activities are outlined to address the needs stated above. 1. Strengthening Community Resilience Group (CRG); WINN will identify community stakeholders and also strengthen the existing once. The committee will consist of traditional rulers, Religious leaders, Vigilante, CJTF, Youth Leaders, Women influential leader, LGA Representatives, Disabled representatives, Minority tribes . The group will be responsible for identifying potentials drivers of conflict, Create awareness in other to sensitize communities on conflict management and stabilization \2. Promoting Peaceful Co-Existence Between HC, IDPs, and Returnees through Youth Civic Engagement Programme (YCE): Both WINN and the community will develop joints projects/events between IDPs, Host Communities, and Returnees. These projects/events bring people from all sides together to accomplish a task that benefits them all. WINN will organize sport activities, artistic and cultural dance/events/displays, and creating new entities. In the process of working together on shared goal, people come to better understand each other, appreciate their similarities and diversities in cultural value as well as their difference, and begin to break down barriers that led to the conflict and exacerbated it before. This program will also involve exchange events between community youth and community role models.3. Community Dialogue Forum: A monthly dialogue platform and interactive problem solving (problem-solving workshops) for WINN and members of community stakeholders to discuss conflict related issues, pave way for problem solving, and action points for follow ups, learn to listen and understand the values, needs and interests of all sides in a more supportive way. This will also focus on analysing the fundamental source of intimidations and possible conflict threats and develop possible solution as part of action points and follow ups.4. Community Awareness Campaigns (Peace Education); Violence cannot be stopped by solely working with the elite at an official negotiation table, while that is an important aspect of peace building, the general public must also be informed about benefit of peace and ways to promote peace. A bimonthly awareness campaigns on peace building and reconciliation to limiting violence and intimidation, promoting peaceful coexistence amongst different ethnic and religious groups, IDP’s, HC and Returnees as well as other peace building topics will be conducted. These campaigns shall be led by members of the Community Resilience Groups CRG committee with facilitation and supervision from WINN.5. Skill Acquisition and Empowerment Program (SAEP): WINN will Empower Adolescent girls and boys (youths), women and disabled persons. Which will be done by identifying their priorities interest of individual to be empowered with different kind of skill acquisition, small scale enterprises, self help group for at-least 4 month. After the training the beneficiary will be empowered with a start-up capital.
