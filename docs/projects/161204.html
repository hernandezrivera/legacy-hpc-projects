Project Summary

Care for Humanity Organization aims to improve the sanitation situation in Warrap State. Warrap is divided into six counties namely Gogrial East, Gogrial North, Tonj East, Tonj North, Tonj South and Twic County.
CFHO will be targeting five payams including Rualbet, Aleik, Kirik, Akop and Awul. The proposed project
intends to contribute to the reduction of mortality and morbidity through the provision of integrated WASH services to vulnerable populations, especially women, girls, men and boys affected by armed- conflict and floods in the host communities.
This project will be targeting the groups most at risk
which are cattle keepers, households with pit latrines, community without protected boreholes that use unprotected water sources for drinking and displaced communities.
The direct beneficiaries of the project are 49,560 persons (Men: 10,750, Women: 12,430, Boys: 12,820 and Girls: 13,560).
The core problem that the project seeks to address is the extremely weak livelihood, education and health outcomes currently faced by target populations due to inadequate and unsustainable water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and practice in the target location.
Focus group discussions (FGDs) will also be utilized to trigger Community Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) to mobilize the community to stop open defecation in each of the 5 payams. Cleanup campaigns will be carried out in schools and health facilities because it posses risk to a large number of people.
A total of 60 frontline health care workers will be trained in case management for illnesses related to WASH.
To positively impact on the Water, Sanitation and Hygiene situation in Tonj North County, Care for Humanity organization will work with partners and other stakeholder to address priority result areas that it considers to be key in addressing the problem.
The following are the main objectives of the
planned project: Ensure the affected population has safe and sustainable access to sufficient quantity of water for drinking, cooking and personal use; Affected populations have access to safe, sanitary and hygienic living environment through provision of sanitation services that are secure, user friendly and gender appropriate.
The project seeks to create an impact through improved health as a consequence of better access to safe and sustainable water supply, basic sanitation and better hygiene.

1 - Needs

The Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation (MWRI) states that 30-50 percent of water facilities in the Country are non-functional at any point in time due to the lack of spare part supply chains, weak maintenance capacity, poor management and/or inappropriate choice of technology. Thus, the actual level of access to an improved water source in rural areas is estimated to be only 34 percent.Furthermore, only 13 percent of the population has access to adequate sanitation facilities. For those using an improved water source, 34 percent travel less than 30 minutes to and from the source while the remaining travel more than 30 minutes.the official statistics indicate that only 15% of household use sanitary means of excreta disposal, and 55% have access to safe drinking water. The picture is likely to be even poorer in rural areas of the country as well as in the overcrowded urban areas ofSouth Sudan. Although the government set a target of reducing the number of people who did not have sustainable access to safe drinkingwater and sanitation by 50% in three years from 2010, there appears to be neither government policy nor a safe drinking water programme in place to achieve this target. Coupled with the current conflict which started on the night of 15 December 2013 in the country, the little progress that might have been made in relation to improvements in water and sanitation provision, has been halted, if not reversed, in theparts of South Sudan most affected by the conflict.In August 2019, Cattle-raiding in Tonj South displaced approximately 13,000 persons to Tonj North. In August again seasonal floods have affected more than 364,600 people in different locations including some 300,000 people in Gogrial West, Gogrial East and Tonj North counties in Warrap worsening WASH conditions of the displaced persons.Tonj North is one of the counties in Warrap state with inadequate WASH services and the situations is worse with the ongoing conflict and floods as mostWASH infrastructures and sanitation facilities both at household and public places like health facilities and schools have been impaired.WASH-related diseases and associated conditions (dehydration, malnutrition) are the lead cause of under-five hospitalization and mortality. Over 60% of hospital visits in Tonj North are for illnesses related to water, sanitation and hygiene It is in fact estimated that 65% of all communicable diseases are water-related and hence constitute a major portion of health care expenditure. WASH is an essential foundation for the protection of public health and a life-saving intervention in any humanitarian crises. Sustained utilization of safe drinking water and hygienic latrines together with habitual hand washing with water and soap (at critical times) is effective at reducing WASH associated mortality and morbidity.

2 - Activities

• Trigger Community Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) to mobilize the community to stop open defecation. • Construct two stance latrine blocks block in 20 schools and 15 health facilities.• Rehabilitation and construction of community boreholes( hand pumps) in 10 schools and 5 health facilities.• Clean- up campaigns will be conducted to improve environmental sanitation in schools and health facilities since they are key areas where people can gather and transmit of infectious diseases.• Disseminate knowledge through awareness raising on appropriate behaviors to prevent and mitigate WASH related diseases and encourage good hygiene practices.• Identified 2,000 of the most vulnerable women and girls through using a participatory approach involving all stakeholders and these women and girls will receive menstrual hygiene management kits. • Training community hygiene promoters on hygiene awareness and promotion and 50% of them will be women. • distribution of WASH non-food items (NFIs) such as jerry cans, buckets, PUR tabs and soap.
