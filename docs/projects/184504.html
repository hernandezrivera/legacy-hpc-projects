Project Summary

The project aims are providing quality emergency and essential lifesaving primary health care services to reduce excess morbidity and mortality due diseases outbreak driven by recurrent climatic shocks and conflict, impacts of Covd-19 pandemic as well as improvement of their physical and mental wellbeing and case management for gender based violence among crisis affected IDPs, marginalized IDPs and vulnerable rural communities (men, women, boys and girls) as well as persons living with disabilities in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia through provision of essential primary health care services. The current climate shocks are exacerbated by outcome of current weather forecast that shows likelihood of below normal 2021 Deyr rains and increased risk of severe drought to till mid 2022 hence compounding already precarious food and malnutrition situation and (FSNAU and FEWS NET Post Gu technical report of September 2021).
The project targets the most vulnerable 20,776 Children under 5 years, 5,554 children under one years, 23,068 WCBA and 3,454 Mothers in Hiraan,Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions through the following activities by Facility/fixed and mobile teams: Provision of Essential PHC (EPHS) clinical care, Child health care, Emergency and trauma care, Communicable disease treatment/conducting immunization on common disease outbreak including EPI, NCD treatment, STD / HIV treatment, TB screening and treatment. The project will further ensure support to early detection, investigation and response to disease outbreak through Rapid response teams (RRTs), monitoring (EWAR) in addition HCW Training on trauma, MCM, case management, emergency management, provision of medical supplies including essential drugs and ensuring Health Staff and patient safety through provision of PPE, triage and regular hand-washing, , Conducting awareness on utilization of health services at the health facilities in the project locations through mobilization and sensitization meetings and forums, provision of Integrated Mental health / psychosocial support services (MHPSS)HCW and community training on MHPSS (PFA, PSS, MhGAP-HIG), Community and HCF based physical rehabilitation, Clinical management of rape services (CMR) and GBV / IPV services and referral and STD / HIV treatment and prevention from GBV cases.
The project will be implemented with close focus on persons/ men, women, boys and girls with disability and further ensure strong synergy through integration with WASH, Food Security and Livelihoods ,Nutrition, Protection and education intervention in the project targeted locations

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1.Ensuring inclusion of minority and marginalized groups such as minority clans, IDPs, women, men, boys and girls and the elderly as well as persons with disabilities in the consultations on the project to ensure their comprehensive participation in the needs identification such that their concerns are well covered by the project and that they are part of key decision in the project implementation including regular monitoring and key discussions and recommendation on the project till completion.2.Protection of women and girls from Sexual Gender Based Violence by ensuring their uninterrupted access to health services through at the targeted health facilities as well as through mobile teams to prevent them from covering long distance beyond the safety of their settlements and homes. furthermore, the project will protect the targeted women men, boys and girls as well as those with disabilities from any form of conflict and insecurity by working closely with the local administration structures for timely information and response. In addition, the project will ensure strict adhering to PSEA policy and ensuring women and girls are fully aware of the project and its benefits to mitigate sexual exploitation and abuse during the project. The project will further ensure that complaints and feedback mechanisms are in place for easy access by the targeted beneficiaries and other community members.
