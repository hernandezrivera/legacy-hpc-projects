Project Summary

This intervention aims to provide support and contribute to the protection and psychosocial wellbeing of vulnerable children in the targeted locations through the provision of direct services to the children (PSS, case management and referrals), strengthening the local CP networks and protection systems, building the capacity of humanitarian actors in the CP field, fostering an environment of inclusion and social cohesion while encouraging the positive development of children through the child friendly spaces where they can express them selves and prosper.

1 - Needs

In the past two years Sudan has witnessed increasing civil unrest and political changes and reforms.in spite of the transitional government efforts to prioritize peace and end the economic crisis, humanitarian needs continue to rise across Sudan.With the severe lack of basic services across Sudan, natural disasters like the floods and the novel corona virus situation in an already fragile economy 9.3 M people will need humanitarian assistance in 2020 (55% are women, 58% children and 15% people with disability). HNO2020The signing of the peace agreement between the transitional government and armed forces in October 2020 has led to some decrease in the fights and conflict situations specially in Darfur states but the results of years of conflict still persist and the situation of people remain unresolved with an estimation of (1.7 M IDPs, 1.1 M refugees and asylum seekers) that need humanitarian assistance and protection support in and out of camps and also among the host communities.these situations continue to take its effects specially on the vulnerable groups and their wellbeing, according to the monitoring andreporting mechanism of grave child rights violations, at least one formof grave child rights violation was recorded against 516 children (44per cent of whom are girls and During the first half of 2019, 9,338 unaccompanied and separated children (45 of whom were female) (UASC) were recorded by child protection workers. This number has more than doubled since the same period in 2018. Nearly 3 per cent (13,000) of refugee children living in Sudan are unaccompanied or separated, and about half are girls in addition s, 15 % of the child population are children with disability that, continue to experience physical and social barriers in accessing essential services, and are more vulnerable to violence, exploitation and abuse. These children are in urgent need of individual case management and access to targeted services to protect them from the criminal networks involved in smuggling and trafficking of people, sexual violence, child labor and being exposed to various forms of exploitation.In order to support 738,000 child (51% girls ) in need of protection against the above mentioned, and maintain their psychosocial wellbeing, we need highly skilled and professional practitioners, strong local child protection networks, effective child protection and referral systems and also induce social cohesion and community ad all stake holders participation.in addition to establish child friendly spaces for the children to express them selves, creating an enabling environment that supports children’s positive development.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Gofcw aims to work in seven states (21 localities) implementing the following activities:- Provide Case Management and referral services.-Establish or construct permanent safe spaces/multi-purpose (child and adolescents)- Run and Maintain of Child Friendly spaces - Furniture, stationaries and equipment (Solar Power System, computer).-Provide Structured and sustained PSS service- Community engagement and awareness activities.- conduct community based group activities ( includes support to child protection teams (manuals, kits, communication and transport)-Capacity building traiig workshops on mentoring, coaching child protection topics, for government, NGO staff and community based child protection structures.-Assistance to children with Disability all activities will be implemented after extensive orientation and consolation sessions and meetings with all stakeholders form the design phase to determine the needs of the targeted beneficiaries and to ensure inclusion and coherence.
