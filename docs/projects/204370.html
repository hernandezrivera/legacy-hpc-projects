Project Summary

Due to the escalating violence across the country since 15 April 2023. Fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has caused tremendous human suffering and exacerbated significant pre-existing humanitarian
needs amongst the civilian population in Sudan, also affecting urban areas previously not or only marginally covered by the HRP.
will be paused and, depending on contextual developments, successively re-established within the scope initially foreseen in the HRP.
South Darfur was one of the affected States that suffered from the armed conflict and this affection resulted in looting of properties, Women’s Violation, killing of civilians and thousands wounded, among them (women, children, men, youth, and elderly people, PSN). Since then, almost two months and there is no change in the situation therefore the assumption of high risk is remained because of escalation of the two conflicting parties. The project interventions will empower the health services in South Darfur state; Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) is proposing to improve the provision life-saving activities and access to safe environment for people, thus increasing accessibility of affected population to live with dignity. This in turn will ensure a smooth and continuous flow, while keeping people away from the danger caused by conflict and expose to explosive bodies. The project proposed interventions will enable Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) to obtain the key objectives of health services in emergency situation through integrated interventions targeting vulnerable communities in Beleil, Nitega ,Nyala Shimal and Nyala Janoub Localities. ECORD believes that tackling health issues through strengthening the capacity development of existing structures while increasing awareness among communities, and enhancing monitoring and linkages and integration to service provision for individual, households and community levels.
Through this proposed sector interventions, ECORD is hoping that by using a combined approach to solve the challenges faced by communities in Beleil, Nitega, Nyala Shimal, and Nyala Janoub in South Darfur State, ECORD will provide much needed lifesaving services, and set a stable basis for their sustainability through community outreach, appointment and finally adoption of key lessons for better self-reliance. ECORD will contribute to the general protection main Objectives in emergency.
ECORD, is the implementing agency and State Ministry of Health is the technical stake holder, all the involved partners will be having roles which will be shared in co-implementation presentation.
Beleil , Nitega and Nyala ( Shimal – jumbo ) has become the most affected locality in south Darfur due to the recent tribal and ethnic interest problems which caused clinically damages to not only livelihood assets but as well as tensions, insecurity, violence, and disruption of communities from their places with not destination, this has hindered the previous settlement confidence in the location. Sexual and Reproductive health were reported as one of the major vulnerable sectors in the location with high need of rapid respond.
Furthermore, the most common diseases discovered during the visit were The major diseases reported are respiratory infection and diarrhea. It is reported that due to the crowd of the area, more diseases are expected to outbreak if response is not taken immediately.

1 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Response Plan that at least 700 civilians have been killed and over 5,500 have been injured because of the conflictbetween SAF and RSF, as of 13 May. Active fighting is taking place in densely populated areas violating International Humanitarian Law principles of discrimination and proportionality without due consideration to the safety of the civilian population. Since the beginning of the current conflict, health service provision has been heavily impacted across the country, but specifically in Khartoum. Several critical programs have been discontinued due to violence and attacks on health facilities, disruption of supply chain, and poor accessibility to still operating facilities. Disruptions affected the power grid, water piping systems, fuel availability, and mobility of health staff rendered around 70 per cent of the health facilities across Khartoum out of service. Hospital facilities are severely impacted with mobile clinics activated.• Due to heavily centralized health system and reliance on the country’s capital for medical supplies distribution, performing laboratory tests, funding, and other aspects of the health provision across the country, all states were negatively affected by the violence that erupted Khartoum /sudan and spaicially South Darfur State .There have been widespread indiscriminate attacks targeting civilian infrastructure including markets and hospitals, as well as looting of homes, belongings, and humanitarian offices/assets in different parts of Sudan. Criminality is reportedly increasing. This portrays the breakdown of the rule of law and protection of civilian mechanisms. Similarly as the situation is terrible in Khartoum and may States, also South Darfur state is affected in the ongoing armed conflict and clashes and as a result of the fighting many civilians properties have been looted, Organization’s Offices and stocks were totally looted, governmental institutions were destroyed, which made the situation of humanitarian actors very difficult to provide services to vulnerable and most affected population, moreover there were civilians displacement from their locations to other locations, the displacement spread to include some South Darfur localities such as Beleil locality and other areas, which caused the population to be in IDPs camp and other left their homes to other safety places. Also the clashes resulted in spreading of reckless shootings that killed many civilians and other were seriously injured, in addition to houses damages and burning of properties, all these factors added the load on the economic situation which resulted in deterioration of the most vulnerable and affected population’s life, during the conflict there were assessments have been conducted by some NGOs and the situation of South Darfur has been determined in addition to the needs of affected people, therefore ECORD will propose this project to address the health needs in South Darfur State.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project aim is to enhance the health of population affected by the crisis through community-based and individually targeted Health interventions; The project aims at strengthen the delivery of health services and interventions for women’s and men’s with a focus on those most at risk of violence, exploitation and abuse in conflict affected locations, Increasing access to safe community spaces to empower women and children; Mental health and psycho-social service provision for Conflict-Affected person’s ; improving Psycho social resilience through Social Capital for IDP's and host communities, ECORD will provide the following activities in this project cycle:1- Conduct outpatient consultations targeting 1000 person .2- Support trauma consultations targeting 150 people .3- Conduct mental health consultations targeting 150 person .4- Conduct vaginal deliveries attended by a skilled attendant 350 person .5- Conduct postnatal sessions targeting 200 individual .6- Conduct physical rehabilitation sessions conducted targeting 20 session .7- Community based surveillance officers deployed and reporting targeting 30 . 8- Organize rapid response teams trained and deployed targeting 20 .9- Community health workers trained/re-trained on different health topics targeting 10 group. 10- Conduct community health workers trained/re-trained on different health topics targeting 10 group..
