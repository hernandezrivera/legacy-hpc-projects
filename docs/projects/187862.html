4 - Needs

HOMS : Where the crisis in it and surrounding areas made them as host communities for the displaced people for additional , many child protection problems were identified such unaccompanied and separated children violence against children , sexual harassment and abuse, child labour and very low economic situation affecting the enrolment of children in schools and causing child marriage and moreover-Al-Qusayer: located about 35 km west of homs , thousands of homes which were attacked and The majority of the populations of the two villages were refugees in camps inside and outside Syria .Hundreds of cases observed survival damage is in the critical psychological state of traumatized children due to the violence that they witnessedand remained without any treatment, many cases of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) bedwetting, flashbacks have been identified between the children and parents. Also, adolescents are suffering from frustration and loss of hope after the harsh conditions they have experienced, loss of trust and break-up of family ties. Palmyra: Located in the eastern countryside of Homs (about 160 km from Homs)After that horrible CRISIS period people were displaced to Homs and many other shelters ( including Al Rukban camp) because of the repeatedly attacks from ISIS and the area was closed for long time. For the time being the great child protection concern is the contamination of the UXOs as many lands are still affected and the risk is very high.
