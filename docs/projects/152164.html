Project Summary

This project is to provide reliable security coverage for the scaled up UN and partner presence and operations in newly accessible areas of North East Nigeria to the UN agencies working in this area. It is absolutely vital and relevant as majority of agencies have significantly expanded their existing programmes and staff presence in terms of capacity and geographical location. The existing capacity of UNDSS is not sufficient to meet the additional security burden this will place on the existing UNDSS organisation in NE Nigeria.

Needs

The security conditions in the North East region of Nigeria and especially in Maiduguri and surrounding areas continue to be volatile with ongoing military operation against Non State Armed Groups (NSAG). This has resulted in a generally unpredictable security environment. This requires enhanced security capacity to support the ongoing humanitarian operations. Additional capacity will therefore be required for 2019 to enable DSS to continue to provide critical security support and coordination for expanding humanitarian programmes. The UNDSS staff and the surge FSCO’s are expected to reach the remote new locations that keep on adding in priority lists and support UN staff and NGOs in seven humanitarian hubs out of Maiduguri as well as increase U N presence in Maiduguri. UNDSS staff are expected to assess these locations, provide Security Risk Management (SRM) and security recommendations to the humanitarian community for their operations in order for them to be carried out in a safe and secured manner and establish a physical deployment in eight humanitarian hubs in support of UN operations there.

Activities

Conduct security assessment of these newly liberated locations that keep on adding in priority lists as the military continue to make advances; • Provide SRMs and recommendations to the humanitarian community for their operations in a safe and secured manner; • Deploy and support UN and NGOs operations from eight humanitarian hubs and secure UN facilities in Maiduguri;• Keep track of staff travelling to these locations;• Maintain contact with the security forces in these locations to get updated information to pass unto staff in a timely manner;• Arrange escorts if required by the humanitarian community;• Conduct regular briefing and information sharing with the humanitarian community;
