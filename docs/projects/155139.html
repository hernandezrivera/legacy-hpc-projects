Project Summary

This projects aims at ensuring food security for Rohingya Refugees and their hosts by providing cooking fuel. FIVDB is working in Refugee camps in the sector since February'2018. Total targeted beneficiary is 68000 (Rohingya- 40000 and Host community-18000. Activities are around- Market Research, providing Gas Stoves & refills and ensuring post distribution support.

Objectives

To ensure Food security for Beneficiaries through Cooking Fuel Supply

Needs

According to the situation report Rohingya Crisis , 15 November'2018 only 13% of the Target population is supplies LP Gas. FIVDB is going to supply 3,000 gas stove in Camp-14 in December, the HHs receiving will receive refill till May'2018. Another 2,400 HH will receive CRH till May'2018. This Project aims at Supply of LP Gas Stoves to an additional 10,500 stoves (Including the HH receiving CRH) & continuation of refill supply to those who recieve stoves till December'2019

Activities or outputs

1. 10, 500 HH Received LP Gas stoves 2. 13,500 HH received LP Gas through out the year 2019
