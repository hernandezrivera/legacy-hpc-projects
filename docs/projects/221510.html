Project Summary

Background of the intervention:
Some 1.3 million people, including 650,000 children/youths, are now in need of some form of humanitarian assistance in the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon, as the security situation and living conditions continue to deteriorate. Around 450,000 of these people, half of whom are youths, are Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs). Children and their families are suffering amidst and fleeing armed violence, attacks on their homes and schools, abduction, sexual violence and recruitment and use of youths into armed groups. For many over aged girls and boys, it has been 6 years since they last stepped foot in a classroom. Due to the prevailing ban on education by non-state armed groups and attacks on education, 54 percent of schools remain closed during the 2022-2023 academic year and 36% of schools in 2023-2024 academic year representing over 3650 schools which are still currently shutdown. This has forced many children and youths to join non-state arm groups (NSAG), get into drugs, violence, and juvenile delinquency with high crime wave.
The crises in the NWSW regions have led to forced rural urban migration hence a high rate of IDPs and unaccompanied children in the urban areas. When these people move from their comfort zones to start a new life style in town, it becomes challenging especially when they cannot make ends meet. With the life stressors and frustrations some of them turn to drugs and substance abuse as a coping mechanism.
As a result of exposure to peer pressure, broken homes, internal displacements, most of these groups of young people cited above are at risk of falling into crimes, drugs or join the armed groups. Their situation is exacerbated by the fact that limited MHPSS is available to cater for their needs. As a result of this, CES intends to strengthen community-based Mechanism to provide mental health and Psychosocial response on children who have undergone severe physical and mental violence, unaccompanied and most-at-risk children in the ongoing Crisis in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon.
This project intends to provide MHPSS services to 10,000 children across the 13 divisions of the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon.
Specific Objectives
-Strengthen and establishment of 13 Mental Health clinics in all the divisions of the NW and SW Regions of Cameroon
-Provide case management/ MHPSS services to 10,000 identified children who have undergone severe physical and mental violence, Unaccompanied and Most-at-Risk children in the ongoing Crisis in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon.
-Carry out sensitization campaigns on the effects of conflict on children in all the 13 divisions of the NW and SW Regions

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The violence in these two regions are at its peak due to the high increase of unaccompanied children, children who have undergone severe physical and mental violence and children who are most-at-risk. To address this, CES intends to strengthen and establishment of 13 Mental Health clinics in all the divisions of the NW and SW Regions of Cameroon The issue of unaccompanied children, children who have undergone severe physical and mental violence, and most-at-risk children have increased Mental Health and Psychosocial (MHPSS) issues. Case management and MHPSS services shall be provided to 10,000 children. It is expected that, at the end of all the group and individual counseling sessions the clients will be mentally healthy and more focused to improve their lives and contribute to the development of their communities. Communications strategies and sensitization campaigns put in place shall go a long way to reduce the negative effects of children and communities and enhance prevention of violence on children. Sensitization campaigns on the effects of conflict on children in all the 13 divisions of the NW and SW Regions13 health clinics at the divisional level. Within this facility, children will find a safe space where they can be treated and their mental health restored. Two sensitization campaigns and distribution of visual materials on the effects of the crises on children in all divisions. Key messages of materials shall be developed, printed and distributed in schools and community structures.
