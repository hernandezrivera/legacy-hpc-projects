Project Summary

1. Research value chain for local materials within Northern provinces of Mozambique.
2. Procure bamboo (x 100 pieces) and construction poles (x 16 pieces) and other supplies for 15,000 families.
3. Procure 15,000 locally produced sleeping mats
4. Provide to most vulnerable IDP families, based on shelter cluster prioritisation.
