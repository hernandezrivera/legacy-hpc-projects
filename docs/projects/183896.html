Project Summary

Education in Sudan affected badly by the corona virous pandemic and its complications in people lives. The academic year in Sudan for 2020/2021 kept delaying until last September 2021 mainly because of because coronavirus and limited education national budget to support education COVID19 preventive measures and tools. Moreover schools are not ready because of the limited budget fail to provide the needed books and learning material before the opening of schools and final reform curriculum is not yet ready and handed to basic schools in Sudan (https://www.unicef.org/sudan/children-sudan)
The country and due to COVID 19 pandemic and the hardship economic crises suffers from lack of awareness about the importance of education, inability to pay fees prevents many poor families from sending children to schools, on top of that the culture traditional views that limits the role of women and girls as households’ holders and not to attend schools are all contribute to the poor schooling of boys and girls in Sudan specifically in 2020. Beside impacts of conflict in WD and BN and north Kordfan and political instability of the country that influence education in Sudan.
Sudan continues in receiving influx of refugees mainly from Ethiopia and South Sudan countries. Clashes in Ethiopia’s Tigray region last to November 2020 caused in an outflow of refugees into eastern Sudan via Hamdayet in Kassala State. 48,000 Ethiopian refugees in East Sudan and 7,500 asylum- seekers in Blue Nile State need to be supported as well as Sudanese IDPs and refugees in the areas. Sudan is host to one of the largest South Sudanese refugee populations in the region, with a total of 736,685 persons living in the country in December 2020. White Nile state host 276,070 as refugees and asylum seekers and the situation become worse when the flood hit the state last September 2021 and leads to displacement of more than 80,000 people of 50 villages and destroying crops, cattle and livestock. (Source: UNCHR). So humanitarian support including education is needed in these states to support quality education that is bellow standard due to poor schools’ environment, poor WASH facilities, in the classrooms and limited trained teachers.

1 - Needs

Considering current situation in Sudan of political uncertainty, economic stagnant and hardship, poor service provision, continued civil conflict and vulnerability to flooding and other natural disasters, 13.4 million people (29% of the population) are in need of humanitarian assistance according to the 2021 Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO). June-December 2020 IPC reported 9.6 million people and are experiencing high levels of acute food insecurity. These challenges in Sudan accompanied the outbreak of Corona Virus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) that affected up to date 39,550 cases and leads to death of 3,038 (Sudan FMoH October 2021) with limited financial resources, and medical and trained capacity to face the virous in the country. Situation in Sudan even become worse with the breaking floods across Sudan affected over 88,000 people across the country and a total of 4,800 homes were destroyed, another 12,780 homes were damaged, and an unconfirmed number of public infrastructure facilities and farmlands have been affected (OCHA 1 September 2021) out of 18 states flooding have been reported in 13 states, including Aj Jazirah, Blue Nile, Gedaref, Khartoum, North Kordofan, Northern, River Nile, Sennar, South Darfur, South Kordofan, West Darfur, West Kordofan and White Nile River Nile. Aj Jazirah, South Darfur, Gedaref and West Darfur are the most affected states. All these put heavy shadow on education and education needs in Sudan where OOSC estimated by three million children here, aged 5-13 years, UNICEF, are not in the classroom. Lack of awareness on the importance of education, and where culture, tradition, war and conflict limits girls from attending schools. Beside that limited country budget for education widening education gaps in schools regards to poor school infrastructure including classrooms, latrines, water sources accompanied with unqualified teachers 3,692 identified by FMoE. The project will contribute to fill education gaps to ensure affected children population have access to quality inclusive basic education and learning and WASH and child protection in the targeted areas,

2 - Activities/Outputs

To support education in 2022 and to reduce negative impact on education in 2020/2021 academic year in Sudan the project activities will include inclusive education activities in targeted schools including; primary school Kits, primary school Teacher kits, primary school Uniforms, primary school hygiene kits , primary school furniture, Primary school Classroom kits, Primary school Recreational kit, Primary school capitation grant, primary school classrooms constructed, primary school classrooms rehabilitated, primary school children ALP. The project will support construction and rehabilitation of schools latrines. The project will improve water sources and support WASH facilities in targeted schools with constructed or rehabilitated temporary and semi-permanent handwashing construction. To reduce children and teachers risks of corona virous disease and its impacts; the project will support schools with hard copies of COVID19 Schools Protocols. The project will train schools staff and PTA members on COVID-19 School Protocols, children and teachers in targeted school will be supported by face masks, and schools received COVID-19 cash grant to support activities minimize COVID19 negative impacts and promote preventive measure of COVID19 in schools. To minimize risks of GBV on girls mainly teachers and SMC members will be trained on school-related Gender-Based Violence Prevention and schools will be provided by enhanced with child protection referral pathway established.
