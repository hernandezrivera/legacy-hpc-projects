Project Summary

COHESODEC activities and response to community health related interventions in 2022 will be targeting 17,800 conflict affected populations in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. These target communities include the following sub-divisions: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Kumba 2 and Kumba 3! The project seeks to support Community health care in all targeted affected communities with various community interventions including mobile clinics involving Medical Doctors, Nurses and project staff functioning in health facilities for accessible areas who shall be involved in Mobile clinics in the NWSW regions of Cameroon to enable this project reach out to its target beneficiaries.
The project will enable 8,000 conflict affected people to be consulted by mobile clinics in the NWSW as well as enable COHESODEC reach out with support to 2 health facilities. COHESODEC will support 5% of epidemics preparation and response in all affected areas with alerts investigated in 72h. The project will also allow the organization prepositioned 1 cholera kit in risk areas while also supporting epidemics prevention in all affected areas with the involvement of 90 community health workers trained to raise awareness of good practices to reduce the risk of epidemics.
In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will also be providing health care to at least 500 people who have suffered trauma caused by firearms or white weapons as well as provide health care to 4% of people who have suffered trauma caused by firearms or white weapon while also making available 5 PEP Kits pre-positioned in crisis affected areas of these regions!
Moreover, during the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will ensure referral and counter-referral of at least 5% of GBV survivors to the protection sector while also supporting the strengthening of Mental health and psychosocial support interventions ensuring that at least 1,000 people receive mental health care or psychosocial support with the help of 4 mental health specialists deployed.
Finally, COHESODEC will ensure that at least 6% of crisis affected women have a safe delivery with the assistance of skilled health personnel while also ensuring that at least 5,365 women are received in antenatal consultation (ANC 1) in various health facilities across targeted affected areas as well as monitor 5% of SSA reported incidents in targeted areas.

1 - Besoins / Needs

During the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will need the services of Medical Doctors functioning in affected areas to support health care in functioning health facilities for accessible areas through, Mobile clinics in the NWSW regions. COHESODEC will also need financial assistance to make available to some severely affected health facilities in the conflict affected regions.COHESODEC will also need more field support staff to ensure at least 5% of epidemics preparation alerts investigated and response provided in all affected areas within 72h as well as ensure the availability of 1 cholera kits prepositioned in high-risk areas! Furthermore, COHESODEC will need 90 more support staff to ensure epidemics prevention in all affected areas. These staff (community health workers) shall be trained to raise awareness of good practices to reduce the risk of epidemics in badly affected areas.This response will also require the services of Psychosocial support specialists to provide health care to people who have suffered trauma caused by firearms or white weapon ensuring that at least 500 people receive trauma health care. These Psychosocial support specialists will also be providing health care to at least 4% of people who have suffered trauma caused by firearms or white weapon.Moreover, these project staff will ensure that at least 5 PEP Kits are pre-positioned in crisis areas while also ensuring that at least 5% of referral and counter-referral services are provided to GBV survivors to the protection sector. The project will also be soliciting the services of 4 mental health and psychosocial support specialists involved who shall be deployed in affected areas to strengthen the interventions with professional response services offering trauma health care to at least 1,000 people affected by the conflict.Finally, this project will require the services of 6 skilled health personnel who assist at least 6% of crisis affected women have a safe delivery while also ensuring through the services of Community Health Workers that at least 5,365 women are received in antenatal consultation (ANC 1) in various health facilities across affected areas as well as monitor and report at least 5% of SSA incidents in targeted areas.
