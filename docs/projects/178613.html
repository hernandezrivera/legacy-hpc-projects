Project Summary

In response to the FSL Sector SO 2&3, Support IGA including vocational training. Support to asset building and asset protection (barely hydroponic units), recovery of economic programmes, to provide asset replacement including fodder units to improve livelihoods opportunities and improving community capacities. The project is built in community-based approach to minimizing the conflict's impacts on poverty. It is natural resource management as well as social development approaches as community driven and community-based development. To alleviate the crisis impact on animal food supply resources and its impact on husbandry & dairy business. Field assessments deemed a rapid restoration of the most essential livestock needs for the people in Quneitra and Rural Damascus. The project is targeting returnees 85% and host community (farmers and livestock breeders) in two central areas in Quneitra, Al-Hamidiyah, and Nabe AL-Sakhr 11,200, and five central villages and in Rural Damascus, Hajira, Sbeineh, Bwedah, Sahya, and Bardeh where 100,000 of the population are returnees’ women form 60% of the caseload. The core aspect to initiate the project was livestock capital in Quneitra (4267 cows, 20,300 sheep, and 1,225 goats) & in RD (800 cows, 1500 sheep, and 2000 goats). The project objective to reduce dependence on food assistance, beneficiaries will be targeted by activities to improve food security and strengthening their livelihood opportunities via safeguarding livestock business. The 4 fodder units will be delivered to the MOAAR Farmers Units. Rescate will place increased attention on longer-term drivers of vulnerability and capitalize on opportunities to revive social and economic dynamics.
This project, however, aims to support restoration of husbandry and dairy production livelihoods for the most vulnerable women and children who form 60% of the country’s agricultural workforce, this is often their only source of income to feed their families.
Based on needs identified during a CVA for fodder in Quneitra when prices increase over 900%, hence the establishment of FSL inputs (Four Barely hydroponic units) together with technical capacity building and pilot projects on novel techniques will be implemented to improve the sustainable provision of barely along the year. Upon deed of donation, MOAAR will run the fodder units and will sell the products at rationalized prices, beneficiaries will have direct and sustainable access to fodder resources.
Training will be provided for 10 days on the units’ production technique. Six-month raw materials will be provided to ensure launch of the project until its sales will cover operation. The financial investment for IGA is justified by the fact that at the end of the project 20,200 HHs shall be able to have a sustainable source of income. Baseline and end line surveys of specific activities will enable Rescate to assess the degree of change associated with the project. Regular onsite monitoring will be conducted: Post Distribution Monitoring of inputs provided; FGD to assess in quantity and quality of the inputs; market prices monitoring; post-sales monitoring. Rescate will ensure that relevant groups are considered with disaggregated data throughout the project cycle from assessment and implementation to monitoring and evaluation.
Rescate will update the services mapping, revisit the field assessment results with the direct stakeholders to confirm final decision on sites is concrete and well-based. Rescate role is to enter into agreement with MOAAR for approvals and land allocation, Rescate will contract an experienced contractor to install the units, including equipment, and a supplier for raw materials provision. The training and liaison with Governorate & Farmers Units to select the workers (direct beneficiaries) from the community. Rescate staff will carry out daily supervision over the implementation of the project, including an Agriculture Engineer, MEAL, finance and procurement.

4 - Needs

Rescate field visits to Quneitra & RD based on the livestock capital and the income dependency on animal breeding and dairy production. PDM of previous project (CVA for fodder) report provided 36% beneficiaries requested fodder units to control spiking fodder prices which is a challenge to sustain business. In mitigation to negative coping, such as selling animals and production tools, the project identified beneficiaries who will have direct and sustainable access to fodder and resources to enhance men & women capability to support their income from livestock husbandry and dairy products, especially women who are the main animal breeders and dairy producers in rural areas. Fodder continuous supplies enhance HHs dependence on own income to feed livestock consequently families. Based on community meetings, farmers & breeders were identified at the respective pilot sites, a work plan with timeline and budget was developed which outlined the various activities. The population in the selected areas based on the life modality as being strictly farmers covering their living needs from farming, livestock breeding and products in market purchases and exchanges. The project will target these criteria to have access to the subsidized fodder in the 2 areas. They should be registered with the Farmers Unit; and are: Small and marginal owner-farmers: returnees; living in rural area at or below subsistence level, the families' main source of income is livestock breeding and dairy, Livestock Producers in the past and small farmers who lost their livestock to the events. FHHs, unemployed breadwinners; Farmers who demonstrated capacity to produce with the objective of generating reasonable income & utilize innovative practice.

5 - Implementation of activities

In response to the sector -SO.2: Support rehabilitation of relevant economic/productive infrastructures through appropriate modalities. Rescate FSL project is a combination of food security and livelihoods support. The project objective is increasing beneficiaries FSL security via increasing their resilience in response to any future breakdown. Changes on barely/fodder prices impact dearly on animals breeding hence dairy production. This project aims are securing “around the year” fodder supplies at rational price. Activities are as follows: - Coordination with MOAAR for land and staff nomination with Farmers’ Units - Set the criteria for the targeted communities/ beneficiaries in Quneitra, in the communities of Nabe al Sakher, and Al-Hamidiyah and in Rural Damascus in Sbeineh, Bwedah, Sahya, and Bardeh. - Contract 2 contractors to implement the works in support to asset building and asset protection (establish two barely hydroponic units based on BOQ) - Contract Supplier for 6-month raw material supplies.- Provide training on the technology and management of the barely hydroponic units for 10 days & 5 days supervision, - Deed of Donation to MOAAR/Farmers Units.- Provide training refresher on animal husbandry and selection types of fodder vs need, for milk processing, of for fattening, and feed production, para vet training and basic veterinary tools use and provision.- Provide Training and Equipment for Para Vets including on surveillance and early detection of infectious diseases.- Conduct PDM and surveys, in addition to submit scheduled reports to sector and donor.
