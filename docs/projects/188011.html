Project Summary

Continuing exacerbating humanitarian conditions, in Northwest Syria due to the prolongation of the crises in Syria causes shortage in the availability of WASH infrastructure services and lack of its efficiency and functionality in most cases. Both rural and urban communities are suffering due to the lack of WASH facilities and in some cases. MHD conducted various kinds of need assessment to detect those cases. MHD planned to perform interventions in those sites through a project which will serve 1773 IDPs/335 HHs. The first site is in Idleb/Idleb/ Maaret Tamsrin/Haranbush/Karm Alzyton(Haranbush)/Tayba Sectors: which is a self-settled camp. Tayba sector in Karm Alzyton camp is a totally neglected sector with the area of 8500 m2, located 700 m far from the original camp. This sector receives limited support, and in addition to its unmet needs, there is a lack of latrines and all WASH services except for potable water service. In this approach only Tayba sector will be addressed by constructing latrines 2 latrine blocks segregated by gender and sensitive to the PWDs accessibility, supported with relevant heating method-solar panels-for the affected 168 IDPs/27 HHs. Originally of the 657/125 HHs. It is planned to construct a 32 m³ collective septic tank on the southern side of the sector. It was designed to be desluged on a weekly basis. Cleaning materials for the latrines will be included in this approach as well, in addition to submitting garbage containers. In 2023 MHD will continue in desludging, submitting cleaning material, and regular maintenance. The Second site is in Idleb/Idleb/Maaret Tamsrin/Haranbush/Haranbush/Shohadaa Mardikh.It is a self-settled camp. 247 IDPs/55 HHs live in it. The resulted sewage fluids are linked to a huge septic tank (312 m³). However, no desludging is being conducted. Consequently, fluids overflow is being witnessed. Moreover,No potable water service is being submitted. The existing latrines serve only 100 IDPs. Thus, the camp needs latrine blocks. MHD is planning to conduct water provision, constructing latrine blocks, regular desludging, provision of cleaning items for the latrines, regular maintenance of the WASH facilities through the project life span continues till 2023. The third site is in Idleb/ Idleb/ Maaret Tamsrin/Haranbush/Haranbush/Maklaa Abo Hazem: in 2022 4 Latrine blocks 2 in each sector-2 septic tanks linked with the latrines in the western-southern sector of the camp in addition to conducting regular desludging and maintenance. Where, the northern sector-basic sector- of the camp will benefit from the already existing sewage network by linking its sewers to it. In 2023regular maintenance-desludging-hygiene kits-cleaning materials provision-sterilization campaigns. The fourth site is Idleb/Idleb/Maaret Tamsrin/Maaret Tamsrin/ Madajin Al Hamra. It is a self-settled camp it contains 280 IDPs/40HHs.In 2022 MHD plans to implement3 Latrine blocks,3 septic tanks (15 m³) for each one, in addition to conducting regular desludging and maintenance and provision of garbage containers. In 2023 regular maintenance-desludging-hygiene kits-cleaning materials provision for the latrines-sterilization campaign, and garbage removal. The fifth site is Idleb/Ariha/أMhambal/Hila/Hila/Al- Hamdan.It is a self-settled camp which accommodates 285 IDPs/53 HHs. This site contains neither latrines nor sewage networks. MHD is intending to implement a full WASH package in this site.4 Latrine blocks,3 septic tanks 15 m³ for each one, in addition to conducting regular desludging and maintenance, and provision of garbage containers. In 2023regular maintenance-desludging-hygiene kits-cleaning materials provision for the latrines-sterilization campaign and garbage removal. The monitoring activities will be conducted to ensure that services are delivered to beneficiaries on time and as stated in implementation plan. All COVID-19 measures will be applies during the project implementation.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021 and WASH cluster reports that there is a a big gab in WASH needs. MHD conducted tailored need assessments dedicated WASH needs in 5 sites. Then designed this approach accordingly. The assessments were conducted by MHD’s field engineers. The needs are as the following:1-Tayba Sector: In 2022 2 Latrine blocks -Sewage system- Collective septic tank-Desluding-Cleaning items-Garbage bins.In 2023 Regular maintenance-Desludging-cleaning items and sterilization. 2-Shohadaa Mardikh: in 2022-2023 water provision-Desludging-Regular Maintenance-Hygiene kits distribution.3-Maklaa Abo Hazem: in 2022 4 Latrine blocks 2 in each sector-2 septic tanks linked with the latrines in the western-southern sector of the camp in addition to conducting regular desludging and maintenance. Where, the northern sector-basic sector- of the camp will benefit from the already existing sewage network by linking its sewers to it. In 2023 regular maintenance-desludging-hygiene kits-cleaning materials provision for the latrines-sterilization campaign.4-Madajin Al Hamra: in 2022 3 Latrine blocks– 3 septic tanks- sewage network-cleaning materials for the latrines-sterilization campaign-Desludging.in 2023 cleaning materials provision for the latrines-sterilization campaign-Desludging-Hygiene kits-regular maintenance. 5-Al- Hamdan: in 2022 4 Latrine blocks– 3 septic tanks- sewage network-cleaning materials for the latrines-sterilization campaign-Desludging which will continue in 2023.

5 - Implementation of activities

• Latrine construction: each latrine block contains 3 toilets (one of them for the PWD which is supplied with a special shower for the PWD), and one shower. Water heating will be through the solar panels. The latrines will be supplied with lighting depends on the solar power, and 1 water tank (2 m3). Each latrine supplied with a slope in the entrance (2 latrine blocks in Tayaba, 4 in Maklaa Abo Hazem 2 in each sector, 3 in Madajin Al Hamra, 4 in Al-Hamdan).• Sewage network: (in Tayba MHD will implement a sewage network for the already existing shelters and the newly constructed latrines leading the fluids to a collective septic tank. In Maklaa Abo Hazem: there are 2 sectors, one of them will be linked with the already existing sanitation network, where the other will be supplied with a separated sewage network connected to 2 septic tanks. Madajin Al Hamra: each latrine will be supplied with an individual Sewage network connected with a septic tank which are 3 latrines. Al-Hamdan: will be supplied with an individual Sewage network connected with a 4 septic tanks).• Desluding: in all locations.• Water distribution:(in Shohadaa Mardikh, and A Hamdan 35/P/D chlorinated and tested).• Garbage bins distribution ( 5 in Tayba, and 8 in each of th left 4 sites to increase the number of the garbage bins as the assessment showed that they are inadequate • Regular maintenance: for all locations except for Shohadaa Mardikh• Hygiene kits distribution .• Sterilization campaign.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

During submitting water distribution in Shohadaa Mardikh Movements of water trucks can be congested, this risk can be mitigated by Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding location of distribution points, delivery routes and its schedule; Avoid location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establish clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Conduct safety training with truck drivers/operators.-Toilets present GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7;Access to toilets not available for all; MHD will Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned.Movements of desludging trucks in congested, camp like settings;Reliability of services.MHD will Consult/engage community regarding desludging services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Establish clear routes and schedule for desludging trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Conduct safety training (include operational safety/PPE) with truck drivers/operators;

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Aerial attacks or bombings might damage any currently implemented activities or cause delays in the appointments of completion. a contingency plan includes storage for the materials and evaccuation of the labors and beneficiaries will be prepared.COVID-19 casualties might spread causing paralysis of the implementation also a related detailed contingency plan will be prepared for such conditions. PPE will be mandatory for the contractors and labors in addition for the field team, and sanitization will be conducted regularly even in the second phase of the project in 2023. Latrines will be sanitized twice a week.Child labor: MHD will emphasize on child labor prevention. Any contractor will hire children will be warned then the contract will be terminated.
