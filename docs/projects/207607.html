Project Summary

Since April 2020, AAH delivers health, nutrition and MHPSS activities through mobile clinics in Meme Division.The project aims to reduce morbidity and mortality among the affected population in Meme Division (SouthwestCameroon) through an Integrated multisector emergency response in WASH, Nutrition, Health and MHPSS-P to children and adults affected by the crisis. Activities will ensure the continuum of care with referrals from community to primary health care (PHC) and secondary health care (SHC). The project will deploy Community Health Workers (CHWs) in communities, 2 mobile clinics (MCs) for PHC in hard to reach areas and support pediatric and maternity wards of The Kumba Regional Hospital Annex - hereinafter Kumba hospital - including the stabilization center (SC) and the neonatal service. Activities will include preventive and curative care for all vulnerable beneficiaries, with specific nutrition interventions for children U5 and PLWs, and sexual and reproductive health services for women of reproductive age. MHPSS package will be integrated to health intervention to limit distress and trauma among the most vulnerable aged above 6 and adapted protocols will be implemented for adolescents, PLWs and GBV survivors. Rape victims will be referred to protection partners. GBV interventions are limited to Psychosocial Support consultation for the survivors and ACF assures the referral for other services in other to provide holistic care. The project will also integrate WASH activities for all admitted SAM cases and existing WASH systems and infection prevention and control (IPC) in the hospitalwill be improved.
Action Against Hunger will mainstream protection principles and promote universal, meaningful,dignified and safe access to quality healthcare.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

In coordination with the HD and NGOs/UN in the region, Action Against Hunger is positioning to cover part of the gap left by MSF and is shifting to a public SHC facility, namely the Regional Hospital Annex, Kumba. The intervention will be coordinated with IOM, UNFPA, IRC, EFORA, CODAS CARITAS, FAO, WFP, ICRC, DRC and NRC. AAH will focus on continuum of health care through direct and primary offer of health/nutrition/MHPSS services through 2 MCs and support of paediatric services (including the stabilization center and neonatology), and the maternity. In addition, WASH systems will be monitored and improved in the supported HF.Health and Nutrition:The health package includes support to the BPHAS (Basic Package of health services) at theMCs’ sites and SHC facility.At the MC / community level:-Preventive care during pregnancy, with iron/folic acid supplementation for pregnant women, the administration of intermittent preventive treatment with sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine, supplementation with vitamin A and deworming as defined by the national health policy during ANC.- PNCs with a focus on IYCF in emergencies for PLWs.Women of reproductive age and adolescents will be offered consultations for contraceptive methods and referred to the SHC facility for IDU insertion, if indicated/wished.These commodities will be procured in partnership with MoH and UNFPA.- Mothers, caregivers and women at reproduction age will be trained to perform screenings for SAM.- Preventive care (growth monitoring, deworming, vit A supplementation, etc), screening and medical managementof SAM, curative care based on IMCI, and emergency referrals (described in the activities) for U5.- Preventive and curative medical consultations will be made available for all patients, regardless of their genderor age, for common acute diseases for above 5 years old.- Communities will be sensitized on IYCF, hand hygiene, SRH, MHPSS, child care and AAH project activities through CHWs, at the MC sites.- SBGV survicors will be treated with PEP kits (in partnership with UNFPA) if they are assisted under 72h after the assault and all SBGV survivors will be provided an empiric treatment for STIs. Survivors will also be referred to IRC.- Vaccinations will be provided at MC sites in coordination with the EPI at district level to deploy the HD outreachstrategy (vaccinators of the health district will be deployed in MCs’ sites)The support to the SHC facility includes:- management of referred obstetric and paediatrics cases.- support of the stabilization center for complicated SAM cases.- support to the maternity (management of complicated pregnancies, referred vaginal deliveries).-improvement of basic water, sanitation, hygiene, and waste management facilities as well as basic environmentalcleaning practices.The AAH supervisor (experienced nurse) will support in the management of the facility (drugs management,analysis of monthly drugs consumptions, patients’ registers, etc). Based on the assessment done at the SHC facility in December, and to ensure the implementation of these activities, one paediatrician, one nutrition nurse and onemidwife will be hired. Patients suffering from non-communicable diseases requiring long-term care and follow-upwill be referred to appropriated medical services at the SHC facility.WiN kits will be distributed for children admitted in the SAM and their care-givers in MCs (admission in OTP) and SHC (at discharge from the stabilization center). CHWs will monitor the proper use of the distributed material,together with sensitization. The project deploys an integrated approach with an MHPSS component that aims to prevent malnutrition and to stabilize emotional distress among children and adult. The MHPSS component willtarget children over 6 years old and adults. Specific attention will be given to vulnerable people including PLW,adolescents and children that are exposed to greater violence.
