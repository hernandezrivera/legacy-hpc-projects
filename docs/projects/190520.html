Project Summary

The conflict between Ethiopian government forces and Tigray armed groups has seriously affected the education sector. The current data collected by Amhara Region Education Bureau shows that a total of 1660 schools (primary & secondary) and two colleges are damaged and looted. Accordingly, the education of 1.3 million children is affected due to damage of educational institutions. They are at verge of drop out and will be forced out of the school. This project targets 152,879 (77,000 Females) crisis affected children live in nine (9) Woredas and one city Administration of North Wollo Zone of Amhara Region. Children with disabilities are also included in this project. The project will make an effort to further align the proposed actions with the Education Sector Objectives and priorities. The main aim of the project is to ensure equitable access to safe, protective and conducive (pre-primary and primary) learning environments for 152,879 (77,000 Females) crisis-affected girls, boys including Children with disabilities. Children will get school meal services and scholastic materials. Adolescent girls will receive dignity kits. The project ensures gender equality and promotes inclusion. The project is planned to build capacity of local government partners that work on coordination of education. Teachers and education officials will be trained on teaching methodology, gender based violence, trauma healing.

1. - Projects activities

Ethiopia School Meal Initiative (ESMI) is a national non-profit organization primarily working on School feeding program focusing on school age children. ESMI is a former organization having a tremendous experience in introducing and implementing a well established, responsible, and accountable structure that can coordinate the school feeding program. The project has been designed in line with the key strategies and priorities of the Ethiopian Humanitarian Response Plan 2022, the Ethiopian Education Sector strategic objectives and ESMI's main focus on implementing the school feeding program. ESMI is targeting the most vulnerable and crisis affected children in need of emergency life-saving assistance in North Wollo Zone of Amhara Regional State, focusing on its commitment education in emergencies interventions to respond to the critical needs of those affected by the crisis. With an effort to further align the proposed project interventions with the Education Sector objectives and priorities, the project is going to implement and contributing to the following Education Sector response strategies: i.) Provision of school meals; ii.) provision of scholastic materials; iii.) provision of dignity kits; iv.) capacity building training for the school leadership on coordination of school feeding services and facilitation of training on teaching methodology, trauma healing, inclusion and gender based violence for teachers. Through these response strategies, the project is aimed, particularly, at increasing access to quality and inclusive early childhood development education in safe and protective school environment. In order to successfully implement project interventions and to achieve the project objectives, Ethiopia School Meal Initiative will follow its participatory approach and work in close collaboration and coordination with relevant education stakeholders such as Ministry of education (MoE), National and Regional Education Clusters, Local Government Education Authority, Steering Committees, EiE Task-forces, and School Based Management Committees including the wider communities. To ensure the sustainability of the project intervention, ESMI follow participatory as well as a community and right based approach during the course of implementation of this project.

In addition, joint monitoring visits will also be organized with key government offices including encouraging the regional government signatory bureaus to undertake independent evaluations by their own teams – this includes their participation in conducting the final evaluation.
