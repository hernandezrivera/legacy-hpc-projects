Project Summary

The integrated Somalia nutrition response aims to save lives and mitigate further suffering among children, pregnant and lactating women and their families through accelerated nutrition recovery and sustained rehabilitation of 196,787 beneficiaries affected by malnutrition. The interventions will be adopt a preventive and curative approach. Preventive nutrition intervention aims at promoting good nutrition among, pregnant and lactating women, children under five and the community at large. These interventions will include micronutrient deficiency control (vitamin A, Iron and folate supplementation), promotion of optimal IYCF practices, nutrition and hygiene promotion and linkage with WASH, livelihood and health interventions aimed at sustaining the rehabilitation. The interventions will also provide high impact health and nutrition interventions aimed at mitigating malnutrition and its consequences among vulnerable groups. Actions will include deworming, and home fortification of foods including distribution of micronutrient powders).
The curative approach will be aimed at providing treatment to malnourished children under the age of 5, pregnant and lactating women. Timely treatment will be initiated for the malnourished children through active case finding done through nutrition screening of most at risk groups, promote and strengthen family participation in nutrition surveillance. Through Family-led MUAC, Household members will be trained and supported to carry out MUAC screening at the household level and facilitate self-referrals for treatment. Moderately malnourished children pregnant and lactating women identified through active and passive screening will be referred for nutrition rehabilitation through outpatient treatment of moderately malnourished beneficiaries. Case management of moderate acute malnutrition will be integrated and linked to targeted supplementary feeding program facilities with support from WFP and partners in the respective locations .These services will be provided at 61 fixed and 7 outreach sites located in 17 districts across 7 regions in Puntland, Somaliland and Southern Somalia. At 54 of the targeted 61 fixed sites (Health centers) WVI in collaboration with WFP supports bother TSFP and MCHN activities which provide both MAM case management and preventive maternal and child nutrition interventions, these will facilitate holistic nutrition care to SAM beneficiaries admitted at these health facilities for SAM care, enabling easy beneficiary transition from one phase of nutrition treatment to another.
Severely malnourished children without medical complications will be provided with therapeutic nutrition treatment at outpatient therapeutic feeding program (OTP) sites. Severely malnourished children with medical complications will be admitted for specialized care support at 3 stabilization care centers that will be managed by this project and integrated with the health interventions.
Households with children on malnutrition treatment will be linked to the food security and livelihood interventions aimed at improving household access to basic food. IYCF knowledge, support and practice will be promoted through campaigns, mother to mother and father to father support groups. These nutrition interventions will further be promoted through timed and targeted counselling (ttC) that focuses on 10 key messages during scheduled CHW home visits with the emphasis message informed by the beneficiary physiological stage. WVI through collaboration with UNICEF and MOH will also support accelerated country wide micronutrient supplementation and deworming campaign in support to the MOH deworming strategy.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

WV will put robust measures to address barriers that hinder access to health services or prevent health-seeking behaviors especially among youth, disabled, illiterate and poor, GBV and protection affected beneficiaries. WV will coordinate its activities and identify disadvantaged and vulnerable groups (IDPs, minority clans, other marginalized) that may face unequal access to health, preventing unintended harm working in close collaboration with other community resource persons. Their feedback will influence realignment of response to continuously adapt and cater to health needs of all groups including GBV and protection survivors’ .In consultation with community members and with MOH, WV will be responsible for selection of beneficiaries. WV will promote active and representative participation of different groups in communities to influence decisions that affect their lives. To ensure transparency, communities will be sensitized on available services, health priorities, their rights and what is expected of community members and the complaints and feedback mechanisms in place.WV will further work together with MOH to strengthen coordination mechanisms, which will ensure effective service provision and prevent duplication of efforts while reallocating and prioritizing resources to underserved areas. WV working with MOH will provide timely information to target population regarding project objective, interventions and timeline to make informed decisions and provide relevant comments. Inception meetings will be held both at regional and community level to share information on all aspects of interventions including support for SGBV, protection and evictions, Accountability and Feedback System: The project will take into consideration Inter-Agency Standing Committee commitment to affected populations. Functional feedback system will be established for continuous collection of feedback from community. The project will leverage on existing WV Community Response Map platform that facilitates online recording, tracking, compilation and visual mapping and feedback received from community members daily. A 3 digit Toll free hot line number already in use in the target locations and will be strengthened and integrated with Interactive Voice Response system, suggestion boxes and community help desk teams to facilitate timely feedback. WVI will enable PLWDs access health care services through the disability friendly health facility set up including latrines provisions .The project will utilize CHWs who will do home visits and follow up on PLWD HHs , facilitate their referrals and movement to the health facility to seek health care services. Special emphasis on health promotion and home follows up will be done targeting pregnant women and HHs with children living with disabilities. Moreover, WV through continuous engagement with community leaders, disability associations and the protection unit will sensitize communities to raise awareness on health rights of PLWDs to mitigate stigma. In each community engagement, WV will ensure and facilitate representation with 15 % of participants being PLWD during all community engagement forums. Boys and girls, men and women living with disabilities attended to will be closely followed up to ensure continuity in care thus transforming their health and well-being.
