Project Summary

Hundreds of children in the Northwest and Southwest (NWSW) regions of Cameroon have been brutally killed in schools, massacred, molested, or kidnaped in the streets, on their way to school or back from school, in villages and towns, as children aged 4 – 17 have become exceedingly vulnerable since 2016. This is due to the socio- political tensions and crisis that have drastically caused the two English-speaking regions of Cameroon, into what is known as the ‘Anglophone Crisis’. Many children have abandoned school and forced to flee into more safer zones, while many are also internally displaced, lost their parents consequently could not attend school over 5 years now.
In turn, education has been distorted and ruptured in the lives of several children both girls and boys who are either: unaccompanied children, separated children, Orphans and Vulnerable Children (OVC), Children living with disabilities and children from the most vulnerable communities identified in the hard-to-reach areas and communities by CAMHELP. This crisis caused tension threat of life’s, violence, road block, ghost towns and other spontaneous call for shutdown of all activities including education, sometimes the shutdown lasted for several days and having a huge impact on children’s ability to attend school and learn. Some schools in Limbe were attacked as teachers and students went naked in the public without any clothes, pants, or underwear on their body.
According to the Safe School Declaration agenda especially the 4th international Conference on the Safe Schools Declaration held in October 25th-27th 2021 placed emphasis on ensuring Safe Education for All. The Declaration is committed to practice Safe School and Education. Despite of the above catastrophes and attacks perpetuated towards children, pupils/Student, teachers, including parents attending schools, education can’t wait.
The School learning environments have to be accessible, safe and protected from all form of attacks and violence in school infrastructure, to enable an environment free of violence, tension etc. In this light alternatives to Non Formal temporary learning centers should be given priority options especially children living the bushes in the hard to reach areas and children who drop-out of Schools some 4years of drop out , 2 years, 1 years, missed out etc. should be taught and re-enforce at home or other temporary learning spaces where it is more protected with fence and safer since children run from hot zones to red zones., some ran from the gun to relatively safe urban areas of the restive regions, while others have defiled the ban on schools and the environment of insecurity and risked tragic consequences. Disruption of education, Besides the education ban and the resulting insecurity, other factors including false alarms and social media misinformation have led to further disruption of school activities.
The purpose of this project is re-enforced Education cannot wait learning packages which suit the education in emergencies programming and it’s another way to create more temporary learning spaces form School drop-out, catch up classes, some missing -out, some pupils/student rape and suffer sexual violence by armed parties during displacement. Due gun shoots from any angles, some children become disable, they break their legs, some killed in class while learning in school, some of the pupils /students, teachers, school personnel were asked to remove their cloth and walk naked because they were found learning and teaching in Schools, this includes, attacks on Higher education and child recruitment from Schools/Universities .
Covid-19 was officially declared a pandemic in March of 2019. The pandemic increased humanitarian needs and further strained an already fragile health and education system in the NWSW Region of Cameroon and worsen the fact that curriculum and syllabus became to difficult for teacher to complete at the of regular school year.
