Project Summary

The project aims to target: Total beneficiaries W:4035/M:1000/G:5800/B:5800 (16635)Azaz including 15% PwDs, Al-Bab, Dana, and Maarat Tmisrin subdistricts. Through the following interventions:
-General Protection: We will provide GP services within the 2 centers in the targeted areas. 25 employees.each center will have a mobile team to provide legal awareness raising on(CSD-HLP).TAS’s mobile teams will provide IDPs beneficiaries to identify at-risk cases and provide services.(24) community-based initiatives and community-based protection structures supported.
in the centers, people will reach out with individual assistance for specific protection needs(PSS, referrals). reach through group-based activities to support psychosocial well-being, and recreational activities through community centers focusing on PwD.
- Build technical capacity targeting humanitarian actors on identification, referral, and psychological first aid.
-Conduct protection monitoring and identify protection needs.(13)communities will reach with protection monitoring.
-GBV: Early marriage continues to increase in Syria, with close to 19% of girls aged 15-17 (Voices-2022). Women and girls continue to face sexual harassment and assault from men on public roads, public parks, and streets and navigating their neighborhoods to reach a school or a market [Voices From Syria 2022].
In Dana and Azaz we will provide GBV response services, CM and referral, and PSS, enhance vulnerable groups' access to services and reinforce referral pathways. Providing empowerment activities for women. Awareness raising for W&G.
A survivor-centered approach will be used, in the WGSSs and we will adopt GBV SoPs and guidelines in addition to using GBV subcluster monitoring tools. The team will consist of 5 caseworkers and a supervisor, we will provide them with CM training, referral, and disclosure, as well as ethical closure.
-Mine Action: people who will receive risk education from public service providers. humanitarian workers, civil servants, and teachers personnel trained to conduct explosive ordnance risk education.
The MA coordinator will be contacted to achieve a better response in Dana-Maaretmisren by out reach teams. Beneficiary satisfaction is measured after each session.
Child Protection: G&B and caregivers that benefit from MHPSS services, structured PSS, and adolescent initiatives with focusing on CwD in all activities. also women&men will reach through parenting programs.
individuals benefiting from awareness-raising to prevent and respond to CP issues to enable them to reduce protection risks in their communities.
adults and children groups/committees supported to ensure the community’s active participation to prevent and respond to child protection issues
-CP Capacity building will be provided to CP actors.
-At the beginning of each project,TAS hold a meeting with the local community and local actors, ensuring their participation in the project, explaining the project goals, and taking their suggestions and observations to develop project activities and implementation. TAS will coordinate with the Protection Cluster and other organizations in the same sector to reach the appropriate beneficiaries and avoid duplication.We will report 4w monthly.
TAS asks children about their preferred complaint mechanisms, and we hang posters of CoC in child-friendly language in our safe spaces.
We consider gender by focusing on employing females in protection projects, and targeting vulnerable groups.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

In case the complaint was related to SEA classifi cation we share it with our trained field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the GeneralManagement immediately, in order to investigate of the case, and take the required action with the employee according to SEA Code of conduct and refer the case to GBVspecialized to follow up with the survivor, as well as we report the caseto PSEA Network. We classify it under the sensitive complaints about sexual exploitation orabuse (SEA).TAS consider following points to reduce SEA risks:-Explanation of theCode of Conduct for all employees and requesting their signature-Raise employee awareness and zero tolerance policy-Monitoring risks-Coordination with PSEA Network-Activities are carried out in a safe space and accessible environment foreveryone, including PwDs-The ability of staff to refer or receive referrals for protectionservices-Case management services are delivered in a secure, private and confi dentialspace.-As far as possible, there are both male and female staff in the protection teams.-The organization has clear confidential and secure reporting mechanisms-Beneficiaries can see PSEA awareness materials (eg posters and fl yers) and a hot lineto report any protection activity (eg awareness sessions, community consultations,FGDs).in case CM services are not carried out in a safe and confidential space. TAS will Train PSEA/personnel to handle SEA cases &AR for beneficiaries.
