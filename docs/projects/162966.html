Project Summary

The project offers solutions proffered to the array of challenges and prioritize practical programmes and/or activities that adapt to effectively address the differential needs and support for girls, boys, young women and men are facing to access quality and inclusive education as a result of the drought disaster. Plan will strive to enhance the Government of Zimbabwe’s school feeding initiative. The right to adequate food and to be free from hunger forms part of the contemporary International Bill of Human Rights as adopted by the United Nations. In that regard plan will capacitate school structure to ensure that feeding at school is introduced where children are facing food shortages to enhance attendance and concentration. Working with Childline, Plan will establish community supported child and adolescent friendly spaces (CFS) which will be used to provide safe, confidential Psychological First Aid, age and gender appropriate PSS activities, awareness activities, identification and referral for children/adolescents in need of specialised counselling services. Pan will enhance a safe learning environment by ensuring adequate access to sanitation and health facilities through adequate WASH facilities for learners. This will enhance Menstrual Hygiene Management for girls especially in areas that suffers intermittent water shortages. Support through Menstrual pads will ensure that girls whose priority has been shifted to meet food needs due to income shortages are met.Program implementation will be done in partnership with government structures like the DCPC, DCP and establishing local consortiums (like the One Stop Shop for Child Protection included in this project). This will build their capacity to cope with the effects of the emergencies as duty bearers. Continuous Monitoring will be done jointly with the working group comprising of key stakeholders. Scheduled meetings will be platforms for lessons learnt sharing and identification of areas that needs improvement and resilient building for disaster risk reduction. At schools and in the communities, DRR structures and Plan will be the targets for Disaster risk reduction and strategic planning.

1 - Activities

Objective 1: An emergency schools feeding scheme for 179 747 (93 468 girls and 86 279 boys) children affected by drought in 3 districts in Zimbabwe by December 2020. • Training of 430 Teacher on emergency sensitive school feeding;• School feeding for children(93 468 girls and 86 279 boys) that are most at risk of hunger, not benefitting from school feeding • Conduct training on Disaster Risk Reduction in 430 schools for teachers, learners and community members Objective 2: Accelerated learning and re-integration support for 179 747 (93 468 girls and 86 279 boys) children not enrolled (ECD-Grade 7) in 3 districts in Zimbabwe by December 2020. • Training of 430 teachers on emergency sensitive teaching and learning methodologies• Conduct Community awareness on the importance of education all communities of the 3 targeted districts• Community mobilization of children particularly orphans and those with disability to return to schools in the 3 targeted districts.• Purchase of teaching and learning materials for 430 schools from 3 targeted districts. Objective 3: Psycho-social support for 179 747 (93 4368 girls and 86 279 boys) orphans and vulnerable children in emergencies in 3 districts in Zimbabwe by December 2020.• Training of Teachers and SDC from 430 schools from the targeted districts on PSS• Provide psycho social Support and counselling in schools to all identifies traumatized children from the 3 targeted districts.
