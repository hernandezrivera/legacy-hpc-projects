1 - Needs

Kubum , is one of the localities in South Darfur affected by the tribal conflict during recent years and therefore, the communities have moved away from their own villages for quite long time then their access to water and other resources also negatively affected Over the past two years, the general humanitarian situation gradually became critical as the communities are increasingly suffering from different types of humanitarian gaps, such as water shortage for example especially from October 2019 update due to funding gaps for SAO regarding WASH and project in Kubum, which resulted negative impact within the local community. and there is high demand for water, Sanitation and hygiene services. therefore SAO is planning to implement this project to cover there are 20,000 people identified by the WASH sector HRP in area of Kubum locality and this conclusion was drawn from numerous field trips and inter agency missions. SAO aims to provide access to proper sanitation facilities for the targeted population, mainly focusing on the vulnerable groups (women, children, and disabled people). The lack of sanitation facilities affects the general condition of women because they cannot defecate during the day; defecation at night exposes them to security risks. They are also exposed to rape and death during water collection due to the far distances between the camps and the water sources. Child safety is also a concern, not only because they are often with their mothers but also because they are exposed to feces (outdoor defecation) which in the long term could lead to diseases and an increased risk of mortality. In addition, children are more likely to be affected by water-related diseases. and the needs of women are access to safe water with very close distance, sanitation services as well as hygiene promotion; there are 20,000 people remained un served and they are in need of Water and sanitation assistance. this proposed intervention will focus on enhancing the existing community structures at the locality and villages level to utilised their capacities in establishment of WASH program that could sustain and support the community ownership and resilience thus Community Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) will be followed to eliminate the Open Defecation( OD)and as water access also one of the areas community have been reporting hence the project will focus on rehabilitation of the water sources beside the hygiene promotion actives as well

2 - Activities / Outputs

Proposed Activities:a)Provision of safety on drinking water of which 20% of the total population will benefit from this activity.•construction of new water facilities with community although the general fore man shall be from Nyala duty station of S.A.O 2% of the community beneficiaries shall contribute to this activity.•Rehabilitation of water sources will be under the monitoring and evaluation team of S.A.O 1% will contribute to the rehabilitation activity of the water source.•Conduct 10 water quality training course for 50% of the total population of the benefeciaries.•Conduct training of operation and maintenance to Water User Committees. To the entire community for 100% of the beneficiaries•Capacities of communities to manage and operate WASH facilities and services improved in areas of operation by the end of 2020•Conduct 6 vector control spraying campaigns. b) Sanitation.•Conduct Community action for total sanitation (CATS).•Construct solid waste campaigns where the populations are higher.•Conduct Disaster and Risk Reduction Training for Community Leaders as ToTs.•Conduct house to house visits for sanitation focuses.•Construction of house hold latrines. using CLTs approach •Conduct 6 vector control spraying campaigns.•Distribution of 36 sets of cleaning tools.c) Hygiene Promotion..•Conduct monthly cleaning campaigns in kubum focusing on public areas and villages.•Conduct 7 hygiene promotion workshops for community and community leaders.•Conduct house to house visits through women groups.•Carry out 3 ceremonies of Global hand washing day. Conduct 30 hygiene promotion sessions in water places and public places.
