Project Summary

The priority is to provide farms with critical inputs that are essential for increasing production as well as crop productivity. Attention will be given to production of nutrient rich foods. Inputs include soybean seeds, vegetable seeds, small farm equipment and fertilizers/pesticides to increase protein-rich soybean and nutrient-rich vegetable production and small livestock. Distribution is combined with training to increase productivity, improve agriculture practices and mitigate disaster risk.
