Project Summary

Title: Expansion of PHC, SHC, and MHPSS services for most vulnerable families in Latakia city and its countryside.
Objectives/ goal : Supporting the pre-existing clinics of Ayadina association in Latakia and improving its services, and supporting the mobile Health team.
Improving the overall physical and psychological health of the target groups and beneficiaries and reducing stress and suffer attached with inaccessibility of comprehensive services thus ameliorating healthy living standards.
Expected outcomes:
⁃ Access to and provision of Primary health care services including medical consultations, medications, and diagnostic investigations is strengthened, referral to lab-tests and to X-Rays, performance of echo-graph and ECGs and malnutrition.
⁃ RCCE: Health promotion and education on primary health care issues are enhanced through spreading key health awareness messages and engaging with the local community.
⁃ Increase access to essential medicines to decrease disease complications.
⁃ Advanced medical treatment under Secondary care is boosted on a referral basis.
⁃ Facilitate timely-manner Trauma Care Management including referrals. Target: 54 services
⁃ Provision of assistive devices for people with disabilities is reinforced. Target: 228 services.
Our association will cover the costs of surgeries, referrals, assistive devices , treatments and medications .
It monitors the patient's condition through the monitoring system to ensure that the required procedures and the best medical results are achieved.
The services will be provided to the host community and IDPs In order to provide services in the best way
All services will be provided by the medical team in Ayadina clinics (the community center) and by the mobile medical team.
