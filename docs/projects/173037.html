Project Summary

The proposed intervention will seek to address immediate protection concerns and provide emergency assistance to vulnerable households and individuals through the provision of multi-purpose cash assistance with the aim of improving access to basic services, commodities and essential household items. In line with the guidelines of the Libya Cash and Markets Working Group (CMWG) the project will provide emergency cash assistance to vulnerable migrants, as well as multi-month cash assistance to vulnerable IDP households in Tripoli.
The sought outcome is to enable targeted households and individuals to meet their basic needs such as food, water, household items...etc. and to reduce the use of negative coping strategies among the population of concern.

1 - Needs

As of August 2020, there are 584,509 migrants and 392,241 Internally displaced persons in need of humanitarian assistance in Libya as reported by IOM's displacement tracker. in addition, Libya continue to host 46,247 refugees and asylum seekers. With 97,552 IDP’s and 83,140 migrants reported in Tripoli, where the proposed intervention will be implemented. The use of negative coping mechanisms and the risk of exploitation remains high particularly among both Libyan and Non-Libyan vulnerable populations who have no or limited access to cash or income generating opportunities and as a result are unable to meet their basic needs, according to the multi-sectoral needs assessment carried out by REACH in 2020, 50% of Libyan households reported not being able to cover at least one of their basic needs in the last 30 days. Due to the physical shortage of Libyan dinars and other hard currencies, the Libyan Central Bank has restricted the distribution of cash in the country, significantly impacting people’s ability to cover their needs in what is a predominantly cash- based economy. In addition, due to COVID-19 related movement restrictions and closure of boarders, access to livelihoods was significantly affected further reducing vulnerable populations abilities to access cash, 20% of Libya with members working reported that their workplace closed due to COVID-19 while more that 40% of migrants reported their work place being affected by OCIVD-19 were their employer was either forced to close, has reduced the salaries or stopped paying salaries. Political instability and poor economic situations coupled with COVID-19 restrictions are expected to continue to further exacerbate the needs of vulnerable groups in Libya.

2 - Activities

The proposed intervention will provide cash assistance to vulnerable IDP’s and Migrants in Tripoli, the assistance will be delivered in the form of pre-loaded electronic cards that allow direct purchase from more than 2,000 selling points in Tripoli, providing access to a wide range of household items, food, hygiene and other basic items. Below are the main project activities that will be carried out under this intervention; Beneficiary targeting The project will target vulnerable IDP families as well as vulnerable refuges, asylum seekers and migrants. For the selection of IDP households in need of assistance NRC will work closely with local offices of the ministry of social affairs as well as with municipalities and local councils to identify potential beneficiaries, in addition the program will receive internal referrals for both Libyans and non-Libyans through NRC’s call centre which was established in Aug 2020 and aims at providing information and referral pathways to vulnerable populations in Libya. , the program will also work with other cash and protection actors in Libya to allow referrals of IDP’s and migrants identified as vulnerable and in need of assistance. Once potential program beneficiaries are identified, NRC will conduct an assessment looking at vulnerabilities with the purpose of selecting families and individuals to receive assistance, the vulnerability assessment will be done using the CMWG score card and will be in line with the sector guidelines and tools. Provision of cash assistance Emergency cash assistance will be provided to migrants and for IDP’s multi-month cash assistance will be provided in 3 separate monthly instalments using e-cards as well. The cash transfer value per card will depend on the size of the household and will be as set in the cash transfer value matrix developed by the CMWG. The distribution process will consider NRC’s safe distribution procedures to ensure mitigation measures against the spread of COVID-19.
