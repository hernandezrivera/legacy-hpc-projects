Project Summary

The action will focus to create an accessible and inclusive response to GBV survivors, from referrals and follow-up of GBV cases, including case management, and at the same time will set an enabling and inclusive protection environment to prevent and mitigate GBV risks within most vulnerable crisis-affected population in Cabo Delgado. The action will be mainly addressed to People with Disability, through accessible information (various format) and promote protection of most vulnerable groups through community awareness. Moreover, the action will enhance resilience capacity, involving DPOs, women with disabilities, community members and local government authorities throughout its implementation.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The action covers some of the districts most affected by the displacement of people in Cabo Delgado Province (City of Pemba, Metuge, and Montepuez) and aims at enhancing community and government actors’ capacity on inclusive and accessible GBV prevention and response, focusing on women and girls with disabilities. Through this action, access to information and services barriers will be identified and removed barriers, advocating for universal access to life-saving and basic services. Moreover, stakeholders will be supported to implement inclusive and accessible approaches for GBV prevention and assistance. The main target groups will be women with disabilities (of any age), who are the most exposed to GBV risks and experience multiple barriers to access life-saving services. The project will directly involve PwD who, duly trained, will identify, refer and manage cases of GBV survivors with disability and raise awareness within communities to enhance protection of the most vulnerable groups. 12 Community officials, of which 6 women with disability, will be trained to provide information and assist survivors. 6 sites providing inclusive and accessible case management and GBV support mechanisms will be established/supported/refurbished – 3 in Pemba, 2 in Metuge and 1 in Montepuez. In order to empower survivors with financial autonomy, 55 GBV identified survivors will be provided with professional training and financial and technical support to start up small IGA.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) Train 12 Community Official (of which 6 with disability) on protection and GBV prevention and response mechanism. 2) Develop/integrate or strengthen inclusive and accessible referral pathways for women and girls with disabilities; 2) Establish 6 inclusive and accessible case management sites to provide GBV case management and referral services for women and girls with disabilities; 3) Provide training to stakeholders and other implementing partners on disability, inclusion and protection of PwD, focusing on GBV; 4) Elaborate various formats of IEC material on GBV (radio messages, printed panphlet and brochures etc.). The use of different formats will ensure the reaching out to most audience targets. Key messages will be focused on protection of persons with disabilities at community level as awareness about accessible and inclusive reporting mechanisms.; 5) Conduct accessible GBV awareness raising sessions within communities. Sessions will be carried out by trained community officials; 6) Provide professional training and IGA in kind materials to selected GBV survivors with disability.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims at establishing inclusive and accessible GBV protection mechanisms through accessible information, risk prevention, reporting and response, creating synergies with main specific protection and assistance providers and stakeholder, including CSOs and local institutions. The development and strengthen of disability inclusive and accessible referral pathways for women and girls with disabilities will encourage meaningful access to assistance among one of the most marginalized and vulnerable group. The partnership DPOS, in particular DPOs of women with disabilities, will guarantee their active participation and involvement in the action.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

AIFO works within and with communities, always in strict collaboration with local partners, taking into consideration the specific needs of the different population groups, with a particular focus on women and girls with disabilities. Consultations with them was guaranteed by the longstanding partnership between AIFO and the local DPOs in the project area. Community members and beneficiaries of the project will be provided with assistive tools and information in order to establish an inclusive GBV prevention and assistance mechanism, taking into consideration the “Twin-track Approach” (combining disability mainstreaming with disability specific support) and age-specific considerations. The feed-back mechanism will be based on Focus Groups and regular meetings with community members and beneficiaries, including peer-to-peer dialogue with women and girls with disabilities. In addition, activities of case management of GBV survivors will be connected and integrated with existing hotlines such Linha Verde and Linha Fala a Criança.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities (PWDs) are at risk of being most excluded from protection mechanisms because of multiple barriers: physical/architectural, attitudinal/socio-cultural, economic, communication/information, organizational and others. Despite the existence of specific disability laws and policies, line Ministries – at central, provincial and district level – are not fully aware of PWDs rights and they don’t have the tools to apply policies. This affects program design which are not inclusive, such as GBV prevention and assistance interventions. The project is designed specifically to guarantee the access to GBV information and services by men and women with disabilities on equal basis with others. PwDs will be identify with the collaboration of local DPOs. AIFO has a longstanding experience in the field of disability in Mozambique, especially in Cabo Delgado. During 2021, AIFO has been leading numerous capacity building activities in Cabo Delgado, involving local government (district and provincial level), civil society actors and community structures on inclusion of PWDs and Inclusive Planning in different sectors.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in the respect of AIFO Environmental and Sustainability Policy, included in AIFO Manual of Operational Policies- Code of Ethics and Conduct 2020. According to the essential points of AIFO Environmental Policy, an environmental assessment will take place and it will consider also gender aspects: women living in rural areas are often more vulnerable to environmental degradation due to inequality in access to land. The project will tackle community preparedness to natural disasters, as the geographic area is exposed to catastrophic natural events, as part of the Disaster Risk Reduction framework. Within this framework, environment assessment elaborated within the catchment area will be taken into consideration.
