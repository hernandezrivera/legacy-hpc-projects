Project Summary


The “Strengthening Foundations: Integrated Initiatives for Land Security and Conflict Resolution” project aims to strengthen local capacities, enhance sustainable resource management, and secure land tenure. Through objectives such as HLP capacity building, advocacy at government levels, and improved access to documentation and rights, the project employs participatory methods, involving diverse populations. Key actions include training, advisory council support, conflict resolution mechanisms, community demarcations, and natural resource management committees. These initiatives collectively aim to foster community development, address land conflicts, and establish partnerships with administrative and district governments, ensuring a comprehensive approach for lasting impact.
The project integrates three pivotal objectives to enhance community development in Cabo Delgado and improver the access to HLP rights among displaced and returned communities.
1. HLP Capacity Building at Community Level, the focus is on strengthening community capacities through participatory methods, involving diverse age groups and vulnerable populations in activities such as advocacy agent training and community sensitization besides strengthening conflict resolution mechanisms)specially about land tenure, at community level.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

O1. HLP capacity building and awareness raising at community level For the strengthening of community capacities, a series of training sessions, lectures, and community mobilization activities will be conducted. The participatory methodology will be employed, ensuring the inclusion of all age groups, especially women, youth, the elderly, and PwD. A minimum of 20 participants is expected for each training session across the ten communities, with a gender-sensitive approach. Key activities include:- Capacity building for Advocacy Agents- Community sensitization for project participation and ownership- Lectures on the importance of land ownership- Social preparedness- Selection and training of Community Facilitators.O2.HLP capacity building and advocacy at local, districal and provincial government levelTo strengthen and engage local authorities, revitalization, training, and support for advisory council sessions in three locations and administrative posts (two in Palma and one in Mocimboa da Praia) are planned. The goal is to address land conflict issues and develop sustainable methodologies for their reduction. Due to a lack of understanding of their role in representing communities, there is an urgent need to empower these bodies. As the governance in Mozambique starts at the grassroots level, and development plans are based on the discussions and decisions of these councils, engaging at least 60 members from the advisory councils in the two districts, considering gender balance, is anticipated.O.3. enhanced and improved the access to HLP documentation and rights.This component is divided in three different actions:1. Community demarcationsTo ensure secure land tenure for communities, there is an urgent need to support the demarcation of communal lands demarcation is planned including the provision of official certificates (certidoes oficiosas). Ten communities will be demarcated (5 in MdP and 5 in Palma). Public meetings will be conducted to promote the benefits of demarcation and the importance of community and local government involvement. Community Facilitators will be selected to mobilize and sensitize communities, ensuring project engagement and ownership. Twenty facilitators will be chosen, with two per community. These individuals, possessing comprehensive knowledge of their communities and influence, are expected to enhance project outcomes.2. Establishment and strengthening of natural resources management committees.To ensure sustainable management of natural resources, support will be provided for the establishment and capacity building of ten natural resource management committees. Each committee will comprise twenty members. These committees will be legalized and officially published in the Boletim da República.Furthermore, comprehensive training will be provided to committee members regarding their roles, , duties, rights, agrarian laws, and sustainable natural resource management. The selection of committee members will be conducted through a transparent and public process, promoting inclusivity and community engagement.3. Development of Community AgendasTo obtain community development plans that reflect the aspirations of each population, agendas will be created outlining the historical profile, , zoning, , mapping of natural resources, infrastructure, existing projects, and prioritizations.To achieve this, all social layers, will be included in the process. This process will culminate in the creation of the agendas, which will be submitted to the administrative and district governments for potential partnerships in community development. In total, ten agendas will be developed, reproduced, and distributed to various stakeholders.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

AeA ensures that, during the design phase of its projects, information is gathered on the specific needs of each gender and various vulnerable groups. To achieve this, AeA conducts a socio-spatial analysis to identify the requirements of each demographic (considering age, ethnicity, gender, disability, etc.). This involves exploratory walks, interviews with key informants, focus groups, and direct observation to pinpoint the challenges and risks, including access to public spaces and services, associated with each specific group.AeA then analyzes this data to target groups where it can have the greatest impact, actively coordinating with relevant stakeholders to avoid duplication of efforts. Subsequently, the activities are customized to address the actual needs observed in the field. AeA also monitors these activities, placing a strong emphasis on using a gender-aware lens throughout the process.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project objective is also to empower women with knowledge of their rights regarding Housing, Land, and Property (HLP). Additionally, the goal is to highlight disparities in accessing these resources and shift roles and modes of operation towards more just and equitable systems. All activities will be conducted with an equal representation of men and women, working collaboratively to incorporate the perspectives of all groups and facilitate access to decision-making spaces.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Although the project does not have a direct focus on people with disabilities, it will ensure their active participation in the development of activities. The project adopts a community-based approach aimed at promoting the early recovery of the returned populations from Mocimboa da Praia and Palma. To achieve this, the first step involves identifying the limitations and challenges in terms of protection, specifically focusing on Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) for each population group (gender, ethnicity, age, disability, etc.). By doing so, the project aims to facilitate access to legal assistance, training, and active involvement in all sessions for at least one representative group of people with disabilities. This approach is designed to incorporate the diverse concerns and limitations they may present, ensuring that the project is developed inclusively and addresses all existing community-level issues. The inclusion of these groups will take place through community mappings (social, physical, and spatial), focus group discussions (FGDs), committee formation training, awareness-raising sessions, and all subsequent monitoring and advocacy activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Mitigation Measures for Potential Environmental Impact: Mitigation Measures for Potential Environmental Impact: 1. Creation of Natural Resource Management Committees: - Mitigation: Establish committees with a strong emphasis on sustainable resource management. - Action: - Transparent Selection Process: Ensure the selection of committee members is transparent, promoting inclusion of individuals with a commitment to environmental conservation. - Comprehensive Training: Provide thorough training on sustainable practices, emphasizing the importance of balancing resource use with conservation goals. 2. Clarification of Community Demarcations: - Mitigation: Prevent land-use conflicts and unauthorized resource extraction. - Actions: - Demarcation of Communal Lands: Clearly define communal boundaries to minimize the risk of encroachment and unauthorized land use. - Public Awareness Campaigns: Conduct public meetings to raise awareness about the benefits of demarcation in preventing environmental degradation and disputes. 3. Development of Community Agendas: - Mitigation: Integrate environmental considerations into community development plans. - Actions: - Natural Resource Mapping: Include mapping of natural resources in community agendas to identify ecologically sensitive areas and prioritize their protection. - Prioritization of Sustainable Projects: Emphasize the inclusion of projects that promote environmental sustainability in community development plans. Overall Strategies for Environmental Mitigation: - Community Engagement and Ownership: - Mitigation: Foster a sense of community responsibility for environmental stewardship. - Action: - Selection and Training of Community Facilitators: Train facilitators to mobilize communities, emphasizing the importance of sustainable practices and environmental conservation. - Inclusive Participation: - Mitigation: Ensure diverse perspectives in environmental decision-making. - Action: - Inclusive Agenda Development: Actively involve all social layers, including youth and persons with disabilities, in the development of community agendas, promoting a holistic approach to environmental issues. - Legal Framework for Committees: - Mitigation: Provide a legal basis for sustainable resource management. - Action: - Legalization of Committees: Formalize natural resource management committees legally, giving them authority and accountability in environmental decision-making. By incorporating these mitigation measures, the project aims to minimize potential negative environmental impacts, enhance community resilience, and promote sustainable practices in natural resource management and community development.
