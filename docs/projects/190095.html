Project Summary

FHI 360’s response to the emergency in Cabo Delgado through integrated approach (WASH, Health and Protection) in Ancuabe and Metuge district. FHI 360’s goal for the proposed program is to improve the wellbeing of conflict-affected and displaced populations through an integrated, multi-sectoral response. FHI 360 will collaborate 4 essentials elements of protection principles into its operational and all program cycle management. All IRAMP staffs including volunteers in the target area will get the protection mainstreaming training and produce the action plan of protection mainstreaming. There are huge need and gap of MHPSS amongs effected population due to the crisis in Cabo Delgado. FHI 360 therefore embedded MHPSS activities into our activities. Staffs will be equipped with psychosocial support (PSS) and psychological first aid (PFA) skills. Affected population in the FHI 360 target area will be benefited with PSS and PFA services and safe ad ethical referral services to specialized MHPSS.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

FHI 360’s response to the emergency in Cabo Delgado through integrated approach (WASH, Health and Protection) in Ancuabe and Metuge district. FHI 360’s goal for the proposed program is to improve the wellbeing of conflict-affected and displaced populations and host community through an integrated, multi-sectoral response. This will be achieved by using the successful experience of the earlier phase of this project, working with the district government system to recruit and cover salaries of Contractados young technical health professionals while they are in the process of entering into the government payroll system, organizing them into IMMUs, equipping them with essential medicines and supplies, managing their daily operations and transportation and supervising them as they provide clinical care in areas with disrupted health systems. Concurrently, FHI 360 will provide surge capacity to the district health in two existing health facilities by providing essential emergency equipment, supplies, and medicines, working with local community radio in local languages to mobilize the community to utilize these facilities and delivery key health and nutrition messages. All healthcare activities will align with the Mozambique Health Sector Strategic Plan (2014-2019) and meet SPHERE minimum standards. The provision of healthcare will further be enhanced by theAgentes Polivalentes Elementares (APEs) as they provide community-based care for Integrated Community Case Management of Childhood Illnesses (iCCM) conduct active case finding and referral, and provide basic, preventative health education. FHI 360 will support APEs with the required supplies and essential medicines to implement iCCM in their communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Activities will include integrated management of childhood illness (IMCI); Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH), including Family Planning (FP), Sexually Transmitted Infections (STI), Antenatal and Postnatal care (ANC/PNC), normal deliveries, and Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal care services (EmONC); Non-Communicable disease (NCD) and Communicable Disease (CD) case management; basic laboratory services; and referral to higher level care services. 2. Service provision through IMMUs: FHI 360 will support one IMMU, one for three days a week in each district to provide immediate care to affected communities and surge support to government health facilities. The IMMU will target communities where there are large number of IDPs, an overwhelming number of patients at the health facilities, and limited access to other health services. Primary Health Care Services will be in alignment with the Mozambique Health Policy Sector Strategic Plan and operations based on the FHI 360 Standard Operating Procedures for IMMUs.3. Support to static health facilities to provide Primary Health Care Services: FHI 360 will support additional two existing government health facilities (either Type 2 or Type 1 Rural Health Centers) that are overwhelmed due to increase patient load from IDPs. At each supported health facility, FHI 360 will provide the health staff with a monthly salary based updated government payroll system of carriers and remunerations in the public sector.. Supported services will focus on basic primary care to maximize efficiencies in treatment and decrease the need for more expensive, secondary care. If budget permit, FHI 360 envisage to be able to provide emergency transportation from supported static facilities to referral hospitals apart from the ones done by the IMMU. However, FHI360 will coordinate with the community and partners to establish some form of emergency transport, though it may be through private vehicles and require out-of-pocket expenditure by the patient4. Improvement of infection prevention and control and healthcare waste management: With the aim of ensuring safe operating environment for health workers and patients, FHI 360 will continue to provide support to improve IPC and healthcare waste management (HCWM). This will include the provision of supplies to enable segregation and transportation of waste.5. Conduct minor rehabilitation to the health facility building and the WASH systems within: With the view to improve the built-environment and hence the space, ease, and safety of providing health services, FHI360 will conduct minor non-structural rehabilitation to the health facility blocks ranging from improvement to privacy screens, partitions, windows, and doors, etc. FHI 360 will Additionally include minor rehabilitation to the WASH systems in the health facilities, establish hand washing stations with appropriate drainage systems, provision of running water to key sections of the health facilities and provide clinical waste management systems and facilities

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

FHI 360 uphold commitment to gender equality, diversity and inclusiveness. All staffs will get the induction on gender, diversity and inclusiveness. FHI 360 integrated gender into project cycle management phase. We ensure that the specific needs, capacities and priorities of women, girls, men and boys are identified and that assistance targets the persons and groups most in need. Ensuring that program activities are safe and accessible to vulnerable populations, FHI 360 will prioritize gender and inclusion mainstreaming as a continuous process during the design, implementation, and monitoring of program activities. Deliberate efforts will be made to ensure that women, people with disabilities, and other vulnerable populations are equally and meaningfully represented in all capacity-building efforts and community engagement structures to ensure their voices are considered, and they have opportunities to participate in decision-making related to program design and implementation including operational and management.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

FHI 360 conducts situation and context ananlysis and risk assessment to develop risk mitigation measures. FHI 360 is held accountable for integrating key actions to reduce risks and incorporate 4 essential protection principle elements through out the project cycle management. Prioritize safety, dignity, and avoid causing harm: Protection activities will seek to prevent unintended negative effects of all action such as exposure of the survivors to further forms of harm by maintaining confidentiality. Meaningful Access: As part of protection intervention, FHI 360 will identify affected population with accessibility issues and address issues appropeiately to their need and diversity. FHI 360 will arrange for their access to assistance and services. Training will be conducted for staff from all sectors to ensure internal referral is strengthened, which will allow the non-protection sectors to safely refer all program participant including those with accessibility issues to access services. FHI 360 promote program participant’s level of their evolving capacity of participation and empowerment. All actions of our project are based on the result of program participants' feedback. FHI 360 respects the needs and wishes of program participants. We build friendly environment for program participants to share, lead and thrive.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

FHI 360 uphold equity, diversity and inclusiveness. FHI 360 will use inclusive designs to improve access to services for the disabled. FHI 360 will work with the staff at each facility to accommodate physically disabled persons. Each facility will develop simple protocols to physically assist program participants into the facility if necessary and provide care without requiring them to move about the facility. Adequate spaces are provided for people with special needs. IASC Guidelines on the Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities in Humanitarian Action, will be used to assess the specific needs disabled person, so that they receive the necessary support. FHI 360 will collaborated with available district Disabled Persons Organization (DPO) to link with DPO representatives at the field sites who will directly support protection activities by interpreting sign language and assisting the physically challenged and ensuring that their voices are heard, and their needs are integrated into activities.
