Project Summary

- Alseeraj will support 34 poultry farms selected in each region according to specific criteria determined by the Alseeraj in terms of the technical condition of the poultry and its readiness to work and provide the necessary tools for breeding as well as the living situation of the owner of the poultry and all this will be under the supervision of local councils.
 purchase 4,000 little chicks per poultry, each cycle provides nine poultry farms with:
1. 7 tons of Charcoal for each poultry farm.
2. fodder supplements to support the chicks for good growth and health. Each poultry farm will be supplied with 5 liters of vitamins and 5 liters of sugar suppliments for each poultry farm.
3. The task of veterinarians will be to supervise the feeding process and to monitor the overall condition of the poultry.
4. Alseeraj through its staff, which includes specialized veterinarians, will provide health care for the little chicks in this poultry. The doctors will also carry out immunization during the life of the little chicks according to the following vaccine program.
5. Cooperation with the local councils will be the most important point in terms of advertising in the regions about this project and ensure that all owners of the poultry meet the criteria that will be selected.
6. These steps will have a significant impact on the reactivation of the poultry farms that have stopped working because of the circumstances of the conflict because their owners are unable to cover the costs.
7. Reactivation of this sector will provide great employment opportunities because of the multiplicity of entities whose work is related to this sector, which will reduce the unemployment rate widespread in the region.
8. Through the activities of the project the number of deaths in chicken will be decreased as a result to the services provided by veterinarians.
9. Increase production and availability in the market, which contributes to increasing market movement and low prices
10. Support the local product and its availability in the market
11. After selecting the breeder that we will support according to our criteria, we will follow and supervise all the poultry breeding stages by the veterinarians in the team. In addition to the monitoring and evaluation of all those stages.

Needs

Large-scale population movements; the widespread destruction and contamination of agriculture-related infrastructure and value chains such as markets and bakeries, depletion of productive assets and savings, increasing debt; and limited economic opportunities have all contributed to socioeconomic hardship and the disruption of livelihoods.The humanitarian context in the targeted area stated that 60% of the population are people in need divide in 25% in an acute PiN (people in need) and 35% at risk pin. A rapid needs assessment was conducted in five communities in Jabal Saman District through surveying the local councils regarding the overall situation. 159,500 IDPs are living in the surveyed areas. While the local councils stated that the possibility for the IDPs to return to their home communities do not exceed 10%. 50% of the people in those areas have access to humanitarian assistanceThe survey showed that the three main complaints of the people were about the insufficiency of humanitarian assistance, and the poor quality. Which reveals a significant issue regarding people thinking towards their livelihood.Regarding the Poultries, the survey revealed that 70 of poultries are not active in the surveyed areas This project is designed based on the fact that the poultries production in the area is not enough for the local consumption, in addition to support the local market and participate in reducing the chicken meat prices. Considering the project will provide sustainable source of income for 34 families when the local councils estimate the percent of families with a stable income as 7% of all families.The project will participate in reducing the food gaps through distributing chicken meat for vulnerable families. The needs assessment showed that about 50% of the people cannot cover their food needs for the coming 10 days.

Implementation of activities

Activity 1: Sign MOU with the local council and main stakeholders if applicable.Activity 2: contract with staff include veterinary expertise who have experience in dealing with the people, and renting a car and an office for the team and prepare the necessary equipmentActivity 3: Receive applications from breeders through the veterinarian and the team members then selection 34 poultry farms breeders according to a specific selection mechanism by AlseerajActivity 4: Ensure that the poultry is ready before the chicks are introduced by the M & E team.Activity 5: Buying and distribution of Charcoal and fuels, Sawdust and chicks and distribution the inputs. Activity 6: Regular visits by veterinarian and trainees to provide treatment, medicines, and healthcare services for chickens and important advice and supervision of chickens’ vaccination.Activity 7: Mini field training sessions with breeders to increase their experience and answer their questions Activity 8: Perform immunization according to the specific program.Activity 9: Cleaning and packing of chicken for distribution after that Distribute chicken for the needed families with Monitoring and evaluation for clearing and packing process.Activity 10: Invitations and announcements by local councils on the dates and times of training sessionsActivity 11: Implementation of the workshops over 3 months for men and women and for 2 days in each month.Activity 12: carry out the final project line to measure the impact of the project
