Project Summary

In line with the Priority Needs that cuts cross the BAY states as revealed in the 'Guidance on the GBV Humanitarian Response Plan in Northern Nigeria (Borno, Adamawa and Yobe States)( GBV Sub-Sector document 2019), GZDI proposes to provide a comprehensive assistance to Gender Based Violence (GBV) survivors in Adamawa and Borno states Nigeria and facilitate needed referrals for specialised cases.

1 - Needs

The project will address the following Needs:  Survivors of GBV needs for safety, confidential, survivor-centred (multi-sectoral) services, made available and are continuous–given the context and associated vulnerabilities. Women of reproductive age need predictable provision of standardised dignity kits (like other assistance packages) to enable them continue their daily routine, with dignity and respect. Survivors with safety/security concerns require safe shelter facilities to provide life saving remedial care. Survivors of GBV usually have shelter problems as there are none available whenever the need arise.  Children survivors need places to relate with other children and activities to enhance PSS provision and reduce psycho -social turmoils.

2 - Activities

In line with the identified needs GZDI proposes to carry out the following activities for Implementation:*Service mapping of Actors.*Educating communities on GBV prevention and response through Sensitisation and Awareness creation: targeting the community stakeholders on protection awareness, rights awareness, GBV responses and knowledge of available assistance at designated place(s) where survivors can go to receive assistance and services without stigma or discrimination.*Outreach GBV issues. *Case management of GBV survivors.*Provide Psycho-social Support (PSS) and Counselling for survivors and families.*Provide Dignity kits for survivors of GBV.*Strengthen and Establish Community structures to prevent,mitigate and respond to GBV issues.*Economic Empowerment —includes skill acquisition, income generation for GBV survivors and other vulnerable women that focus on building support networks, vulnerability reduction, reintegration into communities and confidence building.*Facilitate provision of identified needs, referrals for survivors of GBV: Psycho-social, Health/Medical services,Safety/ Security, Legal/Justice,etc.*Building the capacity of key GBV Personnel, Community Heads, Religion Leaders and Community Volunteers on GBV Prevention, Response, Community participation/Care and Support.*Equipping the proposed set-up Child friendly spaces (CFS) and Safe Spaces/ Safe homes (proposed to be constructed). Hence the proposal for the survivors of GBV to have 'Safe houses' to be constructed in the selected States where they can access secured Shelter, Case Management, needed First -aid -PSS, Dignity Kits, Skill acquisition and empowerment, Documentation and records will also be safely kept in the houses. Where specialised services are needed,facilitation of the Survivor will be made. The construction of the proposed 'Safe Houses and Children Friendly Spaces' have been submitted to the Early Recovery and Livelihood Sector for consideration while furnishing and equipping is included in this budget. Children survivors can relate with other children during activities in the proposed CFS thus enhancing their PSS provision and reducing their psycho social turmoils. * Liaise with relevant Government Ministries, Agencies, Departments, Donors,INGOs, GBV Actors, NGOs for multi-sectoral support for the GBV Survivors.*Documentation and record keeping of GBV Cases.*Monitoring and evaluation of the GBV activities.

7.3 - In which LGA are you planning to undertake Nexus activities?

The project plans to undertake HDN (Humanitarian Development Nexus) related activities in: Adamawa State: Mubi North, Mubi South, Michika and Madagali LGAs. Borno State: Maiduguri and Gwoza LGAsMost of the proposed activities mentioned are related activities that will aim at greater collaboration and coordination between the humanitarian and development actors, to be done with respect for humanitarian principles. It aims at reduction of risks and vulnerability,to improve the lives of the most vulnerable people and ensure that development progress is accessible to all communities as well as those affected by crises.
