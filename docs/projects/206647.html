Project Summary

This Project seeks to ensure that targeted conflict affected children in the North-West and South-West Regions are absolutely protected from various forms of abuses regularly being perpetrated against children in these affected regions. The project will be targeting a total of 26,261 Children who shall be prioritized during this implementation.
In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be strengthening 50 existing Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms. Also, this project will allow COHESODEC to increase advocacy targeting local authorities on issuance of birth certificates for vulnerable children involving 341 birth certificates to humanitarian crisis-affected children as well as sensitize at least 3,000 persons among the entire population on birth registration.
Moreover, the project will ensure that 597 children have access to GBV risk mitigation, prevention or response interventions while also will ensure the identification and implementation of measures to prevent risks of violence, abuse and exploitation of children during this humanitarian context. This project will as well ensure that 3,000 parents, caregivers and community leaders are sensitized about child protection risks in targeted localities.
Furthermore, the project will permit COHESODEC to identify and ensure that 1,885 UASC are accessing appropriate alternative services and family reunification or in contact with their families. The project will allow us to provide children at risk of violence, abuse and exploitation in these humanitarian situations with child sensitive case management while also ensuring that 341 children receive appropriate case management services.
In addition, the project will allow for the provision of mental health and psychosocial support to children and their caregivers in 50 functional child friendly spaces/other safe spaces while 15,000 children [and caregivers] shall be able to have access to mental health or psychosocial support.
Finally, this project will Provide Humanitarian beneficiaries with PSEA measure by providing accessible safe channels for reporting SEA to at least 597 humanitarian beneficiaries.
