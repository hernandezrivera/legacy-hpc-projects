Project Summary

The 2022 HNO PiN for the General Protection is 2.7 million, of whom 63 per cent are children, and 49 per cent are women and girls. BADEF propose to focus in 5 from more than half (39) out of 74 of the total districts and also from 10 regions; 2 Lower Shabelle and Middle Shabelle, of most acute protection needs with ‘emergency level’ needs at severity score 4. Child protection MRM CTF for instance reported recruitment appears to be increasing. In 2021, 1,797 grave violations affecting 1,392 children (330 girls; 1,062); CP AoR on number of unaccompanied and separated children (UASC) continued to rise, with more than 11,235 (5,802 girls; 5,230 boys) UASC documented from January to August 2021. At least 40 per cent of child protection incidents reported to caseworkers in 2021 were related to mental health, physical abuse, and neglect. While GBVIMS report of 2021 indicate 62 per cent of reported GBV incidents were physical assault, 11 per cent rape, 10 per cent sexual assault, 8 per cent denial of resources, 6 per cent psychological/emotional abuse, and the remaining 4 per cent percent forced and early marriage.
The project aims to implement set of interventions that focus on strengthening the child protection system (both formal and informal elements) at the community level, and subsequently empowering communities, families and children. Through comprehensive case management per the Somalia Case Management task-force guidelines BADEF will focus on family reunification for UASC, community based reintegration of Children formerly associated to armed forces (CAAFAG) or at risk of use by armed forces as well as children in need emotional support / psycho-social support. BADEF will also engage with and facilitate capacity building to formal and informal authorities while strengthening / establishing community based child protection mechanisms ensuring they comply with PSEA and code of conduct to ensure that all interventions are safe for children. On child protection BADEF will target up to 8,170 beneficiaries (anticipating 50% to be female) and ensure service include and are friendly to people living with disabilities.
On GBV the project will work with vulnerable women and girls (including those living with disabilities and from minority clans) from, IDP camps and host communities to access safe, confidential, timely and quality services which are coordinated with relevant actors on GBV/FGM services through integrated community referrals. Additionally the GBV approach will engage the humanitarian actors and vulnerable women and girls (including PLWDS and minority clans) access gender, age and culturally sensitive information on GBV coordination, mitigation, prevention and response which is anticipated will reach 6,600 beneficiaries in the 5 locations of the project.
The general protection aspect will address the acute protection needs of individuals, including persons with disabilities and older persons, facing life-threatening risks of abuse, violence, exploitation, injury, severe distress; work with Non-IDP and IDP communities, including persons with disabilities and older persons, receive quality and timely response services and benefit from risk reduction and prevention measures; coordinating with CP AoR on facilitating assessments on for safety standards through safety audits, child safety walks based on child-led participatory mapping exercises and provision of protection training and awareness of rights initiatives to formal and community-based protection mechanisms to improve the protection environment for communities, in particular minority communities, against violence, coercion and abuse. General protection anticipate reaching up 6,400 beneficiaries (50% to be female).

1. - Projects activities

On child protection the activities will include;• Improve the access to quality CP service for boys and girls most at risk• Case management for individual children at risk• Safe alternative family-based care for unaccompanied and separated children• Family Tracing and Reunification• Provision of community-based reintegration services for CAAFG, or those at-risk categories.• Focused PSS, Life Skills interventions for children and adolescents; e.g. group sessions• Support to caregivers and families (MHPSS, positive parenting and other kind of support) of children.• Preventing and responding to grave violations of children’s rights through advocacy and referrals for services such as medical and rehabilitation for injured children• Increasing the capacity of community-based child protection mechanisms: child/adolescent/caregiver clubs• Community Engagement- awareness raising activities targeting community wide on prevention messages.• Capacity Building for child protection staff and other actors• Strengthening CP information management for regular Child Protection trends analysis and evidence-based responses through inter-agency collaboration For GBV, the activities will included;• Ensure access for women, girls, men and boys to benefited from age appropriate and quality services of clinical management of rape services in GBV one stop centres in line with minimum standards.• Facilitate access for GBV / FGM (women, girls, men and boys) to age appropriate psychosocial support for GBV one stop centers and safe spaces in line with the minimum standards.• Facilitate access to recreational and livelihood skills including GBV/Reproductive Health education to women and girls who access and benefited from Women and Girls safe spaces. • Support access of vulnerable women and girls to age appropriate and gender responsive legal aid support/Education and information• Provide dignity kits, menstrual hygiene kits and solar lanterns to vulnerable women and adolescent girls (including those living with disabilities and from minority clans).• Strengthen capacity of national actors (government officials, security personnel, NGOs ) and cluster field coordinators with knowledge on implementing GBV mitigation, coordination , response strategies and PSEA for vulnerable women and girls (including PLWDS and minority clans)• Provided awareness and mobilization to women, girls, boys and men (including those with disabilities and from minority clans) on key GBV/FGM information, service availability, prevention and mitigation measures. For general protection • Directly address the acute protection needs of individuals, including persons with disabilities and older persons, facing life-threatening risks of abuse, violence, exploitation, injury, severe distress on case by case basis • Ensure both Non-IDP and IDP communities, including persons with disabilities and older persons, receive quality and timely response services and benefit from risk reduction and prevention measures through routine awareness , mobilization and complimentary PSS when applicable. • Provision of protection training and awareness of rights initiatives to formal and community-based protection mechanisms to improve the protection environment for communities, in particular minority communities, against violence, coercion and abuse
