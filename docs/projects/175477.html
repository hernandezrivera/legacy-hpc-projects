1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The Project will be realized in the districts of Ibo and Pemba (cidade); Ibo District has been heavily affected by cyclone Kenneth suffering from a high percentage of houses distruction and uncovering of the houses (95% of houses uncovered on Matemo island). Moreover, due to the situation of insecurity on the coastal area of Cabo Delgado Province , both Ibo District and Pemba city are facing high influxes of IDPs looking for safer places to settle down.The intervention will target:- In Ibo District both host community, affected by the cyclone Kenneth, and IDPs- In Pemba (cidade) the IDPs recently arrived from the coastal area of the Province of Cabo Delgado.The objective of the project is to support with shelter and NFI the target beneficiaries of the two areas indicated above. The project will support the IDPs recently arrived with Distribution of tarpaulins and assistance to the most vulnerable families in securing the tarpaulins with basic wooden frames. The assistance will be given by teams trained on purpose. Such approach has been validated after the cyclone Kenneth resulting in a tuchable improved shelter situation for vulnerable families;Support to most vulnerable IDPs families with construction kits (construction material such as poles, bamboos, rope, nails) and technical support to adopt some basic resilience measure;Support to the host community in building permanent houses with resilient construction tecniques for those vulnerable families whose house has been totally destroyed by the Cyclone and are currently hosting high number of IDPs Support to the host community with housing rehabilitation for for those vulnerable families whose house has been partially damaged / destroyed by the Cyclone and are currently hosting high number of IDPs. The rehabilitation / repair will favore the reuse of the structure and the existing construction material. The project will also disseminate resilient constructions know-how and best practices among the community, while working with community involvement and with local labor force. The most vulnerable familie are normally female headed HH, families with disabilities, and families with elderly. It will be utilized an inclusive and sensitive gender based approach in the design, implementation and monitoring, to assure inclusiveness and the reduction of vulnerability and insecurity. Clearly the most vulnerable and poor children, children with disabilities, living in these accommodation centres or within their own families have hardly a chance to access the messages or lessons, radio and TV messages. Staying at home or in the temporary accommodation sites increase the risk for being exploited for domestic tasks, for being confronted with domestic violence and/or sexual abuse, in particular girls. Target groups: 1.200 IDPs vulnerable families in Ibo district2.242 IDPs vulnerable families in Pemba300 vulnerable families among the hosting community in Ibo district458 families among the hosting community in Ibo district

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

- 600 vulnerable households (IDPs) in Ibo District (all islands) supported with assistance in securing tarpaulins with basic wooden frames;- 2.842 vulnerable households (IDPs) supported with NFI kit distribution and upgraded Shelter retroffiting (2.242 hh in Pemba and 600 in Ibo District)- 150 vulnerable households among the hosting community in Ibo District supported through reconstruction of resilient houses (75 in Ibo and 75 in Matemo islands);- 150 vulnerable households among the hosting community in Ibo District supported through house repairs applying resilient reconstruction tecniques (75 in Ibo and 75 in Matemo islands);- 458 HH rechead with improved know how on reconstruction techniques;

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.The provincial and districtal infrastructures services DPOPHRH and SDPI in the selected districts for implementation of the project activities will work with assistance from the shelter cluster and active partners in Cabo Delgado and utilize existing feedback mechanisms such as Linha Verde and Linha Fala Criança for which specific pre-formulated questions and replies already do exist. Therefore, the DPOPHRH, SDPI and partners will be aware and can follow up about any comment on the distribution or availability and quality of the services and products provided, and of specific requests for information.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The main risk is the “Possible distrust and difficult acceptance from the part of the beneficiaries of the adoption of resilient construction techniques”.The project identified the following solutions:● The introduction of resilient construction practices will be made after consultations with the principal stakeholders and community leaders ensuring a better acceptance from everyone. ● Involvement of the community leaders on the diffusion of the messages. In fact, they have significant potential to influence the cultural norms. ● Holding single-sex, age segmented focus discussion group sessions on constrains to their behavior change.● Resilient construction techniques inserted in the communities’ practices valorizing traditional knowledge and local productive culture and keeping in consideration taboos, preferences and construction patterns.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Local Institutional stakeholders will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities.The technical support offered to the vulnerable families will take into account the special needs of people with disabilities, conceiving the shelter structures accordingly to the evaluation of the specific situation.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will take into account some environmental considerations that can come up during the NFI distribution (such as the production of waste) or linked to the construction material provenience.The project identified the following solutions:● During distributions activities, the population and other beneficiaries will be taught the correct way to dispose the waste produced (such as plastic bags and packaging) in a way that would not be prejudicial to the environment.● All the construction material will be provided assuring its sustainable provenience and avoiding to encourage the illegal traffic of wood, in order to prevent the exploitation of the resources of the QNP (Quirimbas National Park)● The project will avoid to promote material coming from non-renewable resources and it will ensure a minimal use of non-re-used material to reduce mass waste production in refugee accommodation sites and host communities.
