Project Summary

this emergency intervention aims at improving the most vulnerable host communities.
the program will ensure that 20,000 community members whose sanitation were destroyed by the flood have sustained access to safe , segregated and dignified latrines through new construction, rehabilitation, and maintenance. in addition, the program will ensure 10,000 community members( women) including adolescent girls of reproductive age have access to NFI ( hygiene kits, sanitary kits ), menstrual hygiene management , hygiene education ( campaigns ) for improvement of hygiene behaviors and practices change.

1 - Needs

The protracted crisis in North East Nigeria has led to waves of displacements of boys, girls, men and women to safer places in search of humanitarian assistance. The insurgents attacks coupled with military operations have posed threads to people movements and substantially destroyed partially or totally WASH infrastructures with negative impact on livelihoods of men and women. The sand storm and flooding (June, 2022) has partially or totally destroyed water and sanitation infrastructures in some local government Areas (LGas) in Borno ,Adamawa and yobe states. the opening of the dam in Cameroon (far North) has completely cut off the town of rann into two leading to flooding with unprecedented consequences on WASH facilities. The Water severity classification (WSC) assessment conducted in (February- March ,2022) through mixed methodologies ( direct observation and quantitative method, questionnaires , FGD) showed the following in respect to access to sanitation infrastructure in Shani: 20% ( critical), Rann:25 % ( critical), Madagali 30% ( critical) thus showing congestion and practice of open defecation with correlative risks of likelihood of cholera outbreak and spread of communicable diseases. Sanitation functionality is 25, 40 and 55% across the 23 LGAs assessed. The aggravating factors contributing to limited access to sanitation facilities are continued insecurities, especially in Madagali LGAs in Adamawa state, and limited access for contractors for rehabilitation resulting from insecurity. Still according to the water severity classification(WSC) assessment, means of defecation in the 23 assessed local Government Areas (LGAs) are: 40% emergency gender sensitive latrines, 30% IDPs shared latrine with community members, 15% used open pit, 10% open defecation, 5% in the nearest bushes. However, the fulfillment of Borno state plan of closing camps in 2021 ending : relocation of all IDPs within their communities of origin as well as the continuation in last two months in 2022 has overstretched the existing wash facilities capacities (latrines, bathing spaces) in both Maiduguri and other host communities in LGAs of resettlement. More than 50,000 thousand relocated IDPs in assessed LGAs are in dire needs of emergency sanitation in addition , 30,000 thousand most vulnerable IDPs reported (WSC) that they have not received hygiene kits for the past three(03) months. The disruption in supply is due to limited funds and insecurity disrupting the supply chain. Maiduguri camps closure has widened the gaps in access to WASH services while deepening the severity of the needs. The Government Counterpart ( Ministry of water resources) coupled with WASH sector and its partners have been providing assistance to the most vulnerable IDPs living in both resettlement areas and host communities. However, limited funds and huge gaps require more hands ( partners) with experience for construction of new sanitation and promotion of good hygiene behaviors and practices.

2 - Activities

- the program will ensure that host community members have a dignified life by accessing safe and gender segregated latrines with pictorial images through distribution of materials for construction of community types latrines . in addition , the program will ensure that targeted beneficiaries access safe and segregated latrines through :- distribution of age appropriate sanitation kits ( buckets for bathing, buckets for laundry, brooms, gloves etc.. potty for children .). the program will also ensure that beneficiaries capacities are built for latrines maintenance ( small repairs on roofing, doors etc..). furthermore, the program will carry awareness raising on critical keys age appropriate hygiene promotion messages for hygiene behavioral change and practices. in addition, hygiene kits shall be distributed to community members including children. However, adolescent girls of reproductive age including women shall benefit from capacity strengthening on menstrual hygiene management as well as distribution of re-usable/ disposable sanitary pads.
