Project Summary

The project Youth, Empowerment and Peace Building in conflict situations in Cabo Delgado Province is aimed at supporting the development and education of youth and women by addressing their most urgent needs during this time of conflict and ensuring that they are knowledgeable and provided access to their rights. Youth and women will be at the center of dialogues and conversations on peace using the capacity strengthening they receive through this project to support addressing the emergency conflict and strive towards development.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project will support the provision of safe, quality access to education for crisis affected children and children with disabilities. Beneficiaries will include 17000 children and youth and 340 teachers.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Teachers in need of support to ensure continuity of education. Youth (in particular girls) in need for information to support the preventioin of GBV. Children with disabilities need for back to school support in Pemba, Ancuabe, Mecufi, Chuire, Meconta and Nacala districts in Cabo Delgado Province

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Political and Socio-economic instability a potential risk to be considered in the implementation of this project. Currently, Cabo Delgado province is caught up in a security challenge with national, regional and international implications. The situation not only endangers the lives of the local population (especially young people and girls), but is also destabilizing the upper north-eastern parts of Mozambique In the context of the implementation of the escalation of attacks in Cabo Delgado and non-adherence to the project's objectives by beneficiaries can be to be a potential risk. In addition, the lack of connectivity in selected learning centers can influence negatively the development of distance learning centers. To mitigate, Risk Assessments will be conducted, as well as regular communication and collaboration with the Education Cluster to understand situation. Before implementation a situation analysis will also be conducted.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This project will build on the support of existing partners and UN response in the North within the four strategic priorities (SP) of the UNSDCF (2022–26), namely: human development (SP1), economic diversification (SP2), climate resilience and sustainable use of natural resources (SP3) and peacebuilding, human rights and inclusive governance (SP4). Through SP1 Human development, the UN aims to ensure that people have access to and utilize high-quality, gender sensitive and inclusive basic social services and protection. Through SP2 Economic diversification and sustainable livelihoods, the UN ensures more people, especially women and youths, participate in and benefit from a more diversified, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth based on increased production, productivity, and greater value-added chains. This program will build on and complement the implementation of ongoing UNESCO’s projects and Program, namely CapED Program, O3 Program and Rapariga Biz which aims to promote literacy, numeracy and life skills and equip adolescents and young people with knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values that will empower them to realize their health, well-being, and dignity.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNESCO has worked closely with the Ministry of Education, Ministry of Science and Technology and Higher Education, Secretary of State of TVET, Secretary of State of Youth at Provincial and district level to improve quality education services and skills development for youth across the country. In addition UNESCO maintains an extensive network with a range of stakeholders from government, CSO, media, Local Education Group, and UN agencies. UNESCO has substantive experience in the implementation of projects and programs targeting youth and vulnerable people with focus on Education for Sustainable Development, Comprehensive Sexuality Education (CSE), Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH), inclusion, human rights and gender equality. In addition, UNESCO has an active presence within the United Nations Country Team (UNCT), and leads UNCT coordination and response on issues directly related to its mandate, for example the protection of Persons with Albinism (PWA).

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Through SP 3, Climate resilience and sustainable use of natural resources, the UN aims to ensure the most vulnerable are resilient to climate change and disasters, and benefit from more sustainable management of environment and natural resources, and resilient infrastructure and human settlements.
