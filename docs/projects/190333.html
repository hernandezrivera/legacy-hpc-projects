Project Summary

Ethiopia is grappling with multiple ongoing crises in a year of unprecedented emergencies and humanitarian needs. The country has experienced chronic internal displacement due to drought and flooding, and has also seen a steep rise in inter-communal conflict-related internal displacement since 2018, against a backdrop of significant political change. The military conflict in the Tigray region, which began in November 2020, has caused additional forced displacement and led to social tensions in other parts of the country.
There is a prevailing need for accurate and up to date information that allows humanitarian actors to tailor their planning to the beneficiaries' needs. Indeed, the complex and dynamic nature of the crisis has been requiring a strong and effective humanitarian coordination supported by accurate information management services. Additionally, since the HRP review and the new crisis in Tigray and Metekel zone (among others), the scale of the humanitarian needs has increased, requiring sustained information management services to the humanitarian coordination bodies and actors specifically regarding needs assessments efforts.
This project aims at providing the humanitarian ecosystem with IM support to coordinated and joint assessments as well as at providing them with Humanitarian Situation Analysis Reports focusing on each crisis-affected area in Ethiopia. In addition, this project will strengthen the coordination capacity related to the protection cluster through dedicated IM support. Through this project and these activities, the analysis, needs assessments and coordination capacities will be strengthened in order to enhance the response capacity of the humanitarian actors.

1. - Projects activities

Through this project, information will be more accurate and timely shared among actors in order to improve decision-making and response capacity. This project will also contribute to a better understanding of the context of the crisis as well as to the multisectoral humanitarian needs in the country through the provision of quarterly Situation Analysis reports. Utilizing its expertise and knowledge gained from its long-standing support for coordination mechanisms as well as from its early success of an ongoing project related to COVID-19 situation analysis, iMMAP aims to strengthen and empower humanitarian partners to leverage and optimize IM tools, technologies, platforms and best practices in their response to a humanitarian crisis. To help achieve this, iMMAP will conduct three core activities: 1.support to multi sectoral needs assessments, 2.production of situation analysis reports that all aim at addressing different critical information needs and gaps that have been reported by the actors in the country, 3. provide dedicated IM support to the Protection Cluster as well as its sub-sectors as Protection needs to be mainstreamed in every crisis situation in the country. The teams that will be dedicated to these different activities will be working together to ensure transversality. The IIMSU will work closely with other actors conducting similar activities to ensure complementarity and better geographic coverage The IIMSU will be therefore supporting existing initiatives and providing support where information gaps are identified to ensure the operational actors have the necessary information to adequately design and implement their assistance to the most vulnerables.
