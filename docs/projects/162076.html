Project Summary

HEKS will implement activities with area focal agencies, camp focal points in coordination with site management and CiCs for service improvement, preparedness and response measures, consolidation and rationalization. The strategy focuses on life saving WASH assistance, resilient building, community engagement, safety, dignity and ownership with speciic focus on the consultation and interest of women and girls. In the host communities, modified Community-Based Total Sanitation approaches will be scaled up to construct household latrines and water points. WASH services in the health care facilities and schools will be improved.

Objectives

Objective 1: To ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities to sufficient and safe water for drinking and domestic needs. Objective 2: To ensure all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation (including household waste) facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times. Objective 3: To ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through access and exposure to innovative hygiene promotion (including hygiene items) for all refugees living in camps and affected host communities.

Needs

Equitable access to safe water: 73% of hand pumps are functional, with a ratio of 1 water point to 47 persons. While this is well within the standard of 1 water point to maximum 250 persons, water quality issues, highly uneven coverage, yield and quality of shallow tube wells mean that overall targetstandards for water supply have not yet been met. Tube wells are vulnerable to Floods and landslides. Access to sanitation: There is still an insufficient amount of safe and dignified sustainable sanitation facilities. 53% of households have access challenges including distance, overcrowding, location, and overflowing. 49% of girls and 40% of women reported feeling unsafe using latrine facilities, 40% and 34% respectively for bathing facilities. Women and girls are adopting coping mechanisms in the form of makeshift bathing areas within their living shelters and not using latrines at night.Hygiene and community engagement: Hygiene practices remain low as evidenced by the lack of soap for hand washing (35% of interviewed households) and evidence of open defecation, especially by children (65% of households). This is compounded with a high population density and overcrowding which increase the risk of disease. The coverage of hygiene promoters, particularly females, across the population remains low due to, among other factors, the skills and language required to communicate and engage the Rohingya communities. The coverage of hygiene top-up kits (consumable items like soap) and menstrual hygiene management materials remains sub-optimal.

Activities or outputs

1. Construction of deep tube wells- 50 nos: The local government and village development committee will be engaged in the site selection process through proper assessment in the community. The village development committee will be formed with at least 50% of female representation. Female voice is very important as they will be the main user of the water points. During construction, female workers will be engaged with an equal wage rate. Highest priority and security will be given to the women and adolescent privacy and security. 2. Construction of twin offset pit toilets- 500 nos: The local government and village development committee will be engaged in the site selection process through proper assessment in the community. The village development committee will be formed with at least 50% of female representation. During construction, female workers will be engaged with an equal wage rate. Highest priority and security will be given to the women and adolescent privacy and security. 3. Construction/renovation of WASH and IPC facilities in health centers- 20 nos: This activity is targeting men, women, boys and girls as all these age groups are getting treatment support from the primary health care centers. All the targeted health centers will be well equipped with the WASH and IPC facilities following WHO standards. WASH Facilities Improvement Tools will be used and followed in every steps of the improvement process. 4. Construction of WASH facilities in the schools- 20 nos: The local government and government education department will be engaged in the school selection process through proper assessment in the areas. The school management committee will be engaged in the development process of the school from the very beginning. The school will be equipped with safe water and sanitation access. Children will learn the good practices in the school and will apply at their home. Menstrual hygiene management facilities will be ensured in the school for the adolescent girls.
