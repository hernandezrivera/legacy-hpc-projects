1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNICEF has been working with some of the most vulnerable populations, including IDPs and host comunities in the high burden distrits, in the provinces of Cabo Degado, Niassa and Nampula. The different emergencies, affecting the province, like Cyclone Kenneth, Covid19, and the conflict in Cabo Delgado had exarcebated the vulnerability situation of the population. Diseases, lean season and all the above mentioned factors are increasing the risk of malnutrition in a region that is known to have highest acute malnutrition prevalence in Mozambique and one of the highest prevalence of chronic malnutrition. Through this project UNICEF expects to reach those nutrition vulnerable populations, particularly, children under five with severe acute malnutrition. Strategic approach includes active case finding and support delivery of services by the MoH (sole proider of acute malnutrition treatment services). The objective is to provide life-saving nutrition interventions to the IDPs and host communities that have been affected by the conflict in northern Mozambique. It is estimated that at least 15,632 children with SAM can be reached and admitted for tretament through this program. UNICEF supports Integrated Mobile Brigades approach to assure the provision of life saving nutrition interventions at community level, both in communities with high influx of IDP’s and in accommodation centers/ resettlement sites. UNICEF also support optimal breastfeeding and complementary feeding practices, and overall Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) best practices in these vulnerable populations with support to MoH programs and with the implementation of IYCF corners in the accommodation centers/ resettlement sites. UNICEF and WFP are covering the same geographical areas as the roles are complementary.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Restore and expand capacity for life-saving nutrition interventions through health facilities and outreach activities for children under five suffering from Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) either through the integrated Mobile Brigades (IMB) or through the Rapid Response Teams (RRT), in communities with high influx of IDPs (including host communities) and accommodation centers/ resettlement sites, respectively; • Treatment of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) of children under five, both from IDPs and host communities living in the affected districts;• Provide nutrition supplies for therapeutic feeding including, RUTF and therapeutic milks (F75 and F100) and other supplements and nutrition equipments;• Support the supply chain logistics for nutrition;• Improve the quality of the inpatient treatment in pediatric wards thorugh training and mentoring with the Mozambican Peediatrics association (AMOPE); • Support Human Resource sourge to address higher needs at the health system to support the IMB and other outreach interventions;• Strengthen the CHWs (APEs and ACS) in the PIN (Nutrition intervention package), and treatment of acute malnutrition at community level; • Active case finding thouth MUAC screening for detection, referral, and follow-up, and laso monitoring of the nutrition situation;• Undertake nutrition assessments/ surveys for assessing the nutritional status of the populations at risk, and for Integrated Phase Classification (IPC) acute food security and nutrition analysis; • Implement active case finding and referral of malnourished children and monitor the nutrition situation through MUAC screenings;• Strengthen the CHWs (APEs and ACS) in delivering micronutrient supplementation at community level;• Support in the IYCF corners in the acomodation centers/resslement sites;• Promote optimal breastfeeding and complementary feeding practices, and overall Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) best practices messages for caregivers of children under two either though the awareness sessions and cooking demonstration in the communities with high influx of IDPs (including host families) or through IYCF Corners that will be associated with the health fix posts at the accommodation centers/ resettlement sites.
