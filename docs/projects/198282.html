Project Summary

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
OVERVIEW
The on-going “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the NWSW Regions of Cameroon which outbreak since 2016, incorporate with recent Cholera outbreak/diarrhoea has resulted in increased (displacements of people to the West region hosting majority of displaced population from the NW region/deaths). 1.4M among the 1.9M people in need of humanitarian assistance in Water sanitation/Hygiene (WASH) will be targeted/includes about 0.4M IDP.
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 2 Subdivisions in 15 villages in rural, urban/semi-urban in Babadjou, Mbouda in Bamboutos Division in the West Region of Cameroon. Targeted: 15,000(dissag. by gender, age, disability of (2,556 Boys/2,684 Girls) <18 yrs, (2,374 Men/2,407 Women) > 18 yrs, (1,507 Men/1,657 Women) +60yrs, PWD (889 Men/926 Women)).
AIM:
To improve access to safe drinking water and hygiene related services to the vulnerable populations by 30th June 2023.

ACTIVITIES:
-Promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people
-Construction of water points (boreholes, small water supply network...)
-Construction of gender-sensitive latrines in IDP sites/host communities
-Distribution of WASH kits to boys, girls, men, women, families, PWD and old people
-Capacity-building of WASH actors in emergency WASH response/relative issues
OBJECTIVES:
-Improve sustainable access to basic sanitation/safe drinking water to vulnerable people
-Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity/mortality of affected population in the crisis
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve WASH services/resilience of affected populations
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 60% IDPs, 10% PWDs/30% Host Communities.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through community based approach, awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials, collaboration with stakeholders/organised workshops, community mobilization/engagement, open space distribution/homes delivery/M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Engage MINEE/MINSANTE/Municipal Council.
SUSTAINABILITY
Trained (CPCs for continuos sensitisaton on HP; WMCs/supply with tools box; IDPs on HWTS/how to treat/reuse squatting slabs). Jerry cans for storage of water at HHs level/distribution of Aqua tabs. Installation of HWFs/improve sustainable latrines.
IMPACTS
-Improved (water supply/latrines) addresses water/latrines need on a long-term basis for 20yrs/reduces cholera, diarrhoea/deaths.
OUTCOME
-80% of HHs have access to chlorinated water between 0.2/0.5 mg/L for all uses, reduces cholera/deaths.
-50% of women/girls use improved latrines/feel safe
JUSTIFICATION
Increased # of improved water points (borehole of underground water scheme with small solar powered system) will reduce cholera, diarrhoea/deaths

1 - Besoins / Needs

NEEDS: 10,350 people living in poverty are in need of quality and sufficient quantity of sustainable clean water for drinking, laundry, preparing food, bath, handwashing with soap (HHWS) for delivery of new-born to reduce maternal/neonatal deaths and to reduce cholera and diarrhea including deaths to children under 5 years for they collect water from an unimproved water point. Lack of improved sustainable sanitation facility (latrines) to homes, lack of water containers to collect and store water, insufficient handwashing facilities and soaps, and WASH kits. Need promotion of good wash practices to 15,000 affected population. Trained the affected population on feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors on AAP. Lack of trained water management committees (WMCs) to manage their water supply and supplies of tools box with latrines management. Direct beneficiaries are 10,350 and indirect beneficiaries is 4,650. NB: Construction of borehole of underground water scheme is with small solar powered system of 4 taps per borehole.
