Project Summary

The global pandemic has remarkably hunted down nation's economy, In Borno, displaced people due to the Boko Haram insurgent and host community largely depends on the government and humanitarian community for survival owing to the fact that all source of livelihood have been cut off and as such many cannot put food on the table, for an average family that farming has been the only source of survival presently living in camps with no access to farm land, having access to factual information is another challenge as every information seems to be a rumor, with almost half of these population under low standard of living condition, this life saving project aims at providing CCCM Covid-19 Inclusive Service to vulnerable IDPs/Host community through dedicated static and mobile site facilitators in informal sites and host community SHI is proposing to implement this project in Maiduguri, Konduga, Jere LGAs targeting 30,000 beneficiaries. The locations have been identified in close consultation with CCCM/shelter/NFI/DMS sector and complement SHI’s ongoing operations in MMC, where SHI is currently providing CCCCM support to NEMA and SEMA in Shuwari5, Cheazcon, Tausayi Camp, Doron Baga and Umara Bolori with technical support from CCCM Sector and funded By NHF through IOM.
This project will support and establish strong coordination with Health Sector, WASH sector and active referral on life threatening issue. by establishing strong community engagement and active participation, creating awareness on COVID-19, community capacity building on preventive measures, engagement of community leaders in host community and border community active participation of various stakeholders on need to avoid further spread of the various in camps and camps like setting is essential.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Series of camps-camp like setting/host communities are housing displaced population as a result of increase in the trend of displacement as the long insurgent in the northeast continues to evolve, couple with the global Pandemic of COVID-19, living standard in these locations is below sphere standard. with DTM figures of above 50,000 individual are with little or no access to humanitarian assistance, and over 30,000 influx into MMC, Konduga and Jere as a result military operation to retake the affected LGAs affected and also chronic poor living condition which is rated below sphere standard. SHI will Establish and construct Reception Center in Konduga, for proper screening and immediate response to need and gaps identified and create a good referral pathways to Health/WASH and managing immediate protection cases and needs. Have a dedicated CCCM teams in all the LGAs for adequate site facilitation and site maintenance with effective coordination, Create maintain a special Covid-19 awareness strategy, Build Capacity building on Covid-19 Prevention mechanism

2 - Activities

Activity 1.0 Reception centere in Konduga: SHI will ensure adequate reception Centere management and Covid-19 screening is in place and comprehensive first contact response to new arrivals is done, in close collabration with Health Partners, IOM DTM SHI will assist in the registration of new arrivals during and after biometric registration/verification, Covid-19 Screening and also take vulnerability assesment and effective referrals for further humanitarian assistance as available to needs. activity 1.1 SHI will directly and in close consultation Stakeholders (NEMA/SEMA, IOM) , IDPs leaders take gap analysis and need assessment to identify critical/urgent needs and share results with Sector. Activity 1.2 SHI will establish effective coordination and prompt Covid-19 related information sharing mechanism: this is achievable by setting up of coordination mechanism, and maximum usage of CCCM Sector formal camp monitoring tool and monthly reporting to the sector; also establish bi-monthly camp coordination meeting. With once in a month meeting of different camp committee established, this is to give the community a lead in all intervention. Activity 1.3 Camp improvement, SHI will ensure the improvement and upgrade of site after site assesment in consultation with NEMA/SEMA, IOM and Community leaders, priorities will be placed base on urgent/critical needs identified, the upgrade will basically to reduce enviromental risk such as flooding mitigation, fire mitigation and other associated environmental risks. Activity 1.4 Establishment of Complain Feedback Mechanism (CFM): SHI will maintain a good/confidential CFM to enable beneficiaries make inquiry about interventions, be a guide to planning and make necessary complain and get response from partners involve, this will includes the creation of Complain desk, suggestion box, resolves issues desk and unresolved issues desk for proper accountability to partners..Activity 1.5 SHI in close collaboration with Health/WaSH sector will build capacity of community leaders on Covid-19 prevention MechanismActivity 1.6 Participate in setting up washing hand stations in host community, camp and camp like setting.
