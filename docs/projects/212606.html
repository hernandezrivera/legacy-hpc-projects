Project Summary

The intervention aims to respond to the significant need to Enhance access to essential services and infrastructure, strengthen community resilience, improve livelihoods for HC and IDPs, and contribute to enhancing access to livelihood opportunities in Dana and Mhambal Subdistricts,
Based on the previous experience in implementing similar activities, Global Communities will provide critically needed upgrades/rehabilitation/construction to the neglected and crisis-affected infrastructure as follows:
- Rehabilitation of 10km of the main roads including a new asphalt layer with all the manholes and drainage infrastructure and lighting required.
- Implementing infrastructure rehabilitation through cash-for-work approach by hiring 10 teams of 6 members each to implement the planned works
- Vocational training for 100 skilled workshops with connecting them to the local market
Lack of access to employment opportunities, increasing the risk of intra- and inter-community tensions involving residents and displaced people, the deteriorated economic situation, inflation and lack of services and infrastructure, and resumption of economic activity all are reported as main burdens for the population in NWS. In 2024, the ERL sector estimates 15.4 million people in need of early recovery and livelihood support, the vast majority of them are in the 3rd or 4th level of the severity scale, where out of the 4M targeted people in NWS, 50 percent are estimated to be women, 1.7M are children, with 700K persons living with disabilities.
In 49% of households in NWS, loss of a job or unemployment was reported as a barrier to meeting basic needs; furthermore, over 70% of families are unable to afford basic needs, including shelter, food, and medicine, with household incomes at 85 USD and expenditures at 183 USD per month, a 53.5% shortfall, based on MSNA data.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Global Communities will implement: - Rehabilitation of 10km of the main roads including a new asphalt layer with all the manholes and drainage infrastructure and lighting required. - Implementing of Road rehabilitation through 60 cash for work workers - Vocational training for 100 skilled workshops and startup kits with connecting them to the market. The activities will include; 1. Community/facilities selection and outreach: collecting detailed information about the specifics of intended infrastructures that require rehabilitation, through FGDs, KIIs, and Observations with all the stakeholders. 2. Topographic surveys for the selected locations, developing the BoQsand the technical specification. 3. Procurement process: through a transparent procurement process, advertising and receiving offers from the bidders, opening bids, TEC, and signing the contract with the selected contractor. 4. Implementation of the proposed rehabilitation and construction, and supervising the implementation of the rehabilitation and construction works. Along with implementing the activities, we will have ongoing monitoring for the implementation. Meanwhile, the selection of CFW workers, and skilled workers will be through the beneficiaries selection approach and modality while the process will include registration and verification in light of the preselected selection criteria, considering the most vulnerable cases, their dues will be transferred in cash.GC will consider the different protection considerations and needs of older persons, mobility-impaired, women, children, and at-risk youth in implementing the proposed activities, ensuring that we prioritize these vulnerable households for assistance. GC will promote women’s participation in the program to the extent feasible and appropriate without endangering the safety the staff or beneficiaries.GC will work closely with service providers and contractors to train them on gender and protection considerations, including PSEA and GBV prevention and mitigation including protection staff for providing training for all camp management, conducting FGDs on GBV Risk Mitigation, conducting safety audits, providing referrals, and leading awareness raising sessions on protection issues. We will ensure that field teams include male and female representatives, where feasible, to ensure that the assistance is accessible to all households, including vulnerable families such as female-headed households.
