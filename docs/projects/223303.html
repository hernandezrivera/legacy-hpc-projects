Project Summary

The Nutrition action will provide life-saving nutrition services, address root causes of malnutrition, and preventive measures. The
planned activities will leverage in addressing malnutrition by providing a comprehensive CMAM package both at the facility and
community levels. The project will use existing government systems - PHCs at the community level to refer treatment services to
nutritional vulnerable groups identified with uncomplicated severe acute malnutrition SAM through the outpatient therapeutic feeding
programme.
The project will target 1,100 children U5 with MAM across all the locations (400 in Dikwa ,400 in Damboa and 300 Kala Balge) for the
Tom brown approach in close collaboration with the OTP programme and appropriately linked with other health and nutrition services
such as MNP supplementation, routine immunizations, and medications and with appropriate complementary referral mechanisms
between both programmes in place. This will strengthen the referral of SAM children with complications to the stabilisation centre
through the facilitation of transportation for timely access to services to prevent mortality. Similarly, 1,100 caregivers of the identified
MAM children will receive monthly cash support of N10,000 to promote their access to locally available fresh nutritious foods. Through
HFs, 600 pregnant women (200 in Dikwa, 200 `Damboa and 200 in Kala Balge) will be supported with the monthly cash transfers as well.
