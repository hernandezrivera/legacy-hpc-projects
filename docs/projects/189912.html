Project Summary

The project entitled as “Livelihood Restoration Support for Conflict affected Communities in North Gondar zone of Amhara region” is proposed to implement interventions that contribute to sustain the lives of the severely conflict affected communities in north Gondar zone 4 (Four) woredas ( Addi Arekay, Beyeda, Debarik and Telemit . The proposed interventions focus on bringing the conflict affected communities to its agricultural practices and its livelihood activities. The aim is to restore the weathered livelihood resources like farming cattle, cereal crops, agricultural inputs and related income generating activities (IGA).
The proposed project intended to implement animal health and feed supports; agricultural input supports (Improved farming seeds and forage seeds); conducting different capacity development workshops and trainings specially on agronomic practice and post-harvest management, livestock management , Motivational, Inspirational, life skill and Entrepreneurship trainings; restocking of farming cattle and small ruminants. For those who were working on pity trade will be supplied seed money to start it again.
The target groups are selected from each affected woredas including displaced people, returnees, women and disabilities, youth and adults in need. The total number of target groups is 26,468 (Adi arkay 7731, Beyeda 8286, Debarik 5713 and Telemit, 4738).

1. - Projects activities

Project activities are framed with respect to the two agricultural cluster objectives. These are to protect and sustain core-agricultural livelihoods of climate and conflict affected households through humanitarian interventions and to enhance vulnerable households’ ability to withstand recurrent and future shocks through early recovery and resilience building. The project followed triple nexus approach (humanitarian response, development and peace building & advocacy) activity design to benefit 15,000 households. Proposed activities of objective one includes: provisions of different types of improved seeds in order to re – engage the target community in its farming practices and fulfil its food demand; introduction of various types of forage seed/grass; Provision of oxen for needy farmers; Renovation of veterinary clinics and supply of essential drugs; furthermore, capacity buildings on different thematic areas such as livestock husbandry, forage production and promote destocking and controlled grazing, improved agronomic practices, gender, Health and nutrition, advocacy of peace, etc… The intended activities of objective two are focused on Capacity and resilient building. Major activities are: Advocacy workshop on the climate actions for people from faith institution and other very relevant community and government segments; encourage local community to participate on watershed management practices by providing training on FMNR (Farmers Managed Natural Regeneration) and supply of farm hand tools to construct physical structures; provide motivational inspirational, life skill, and entrepreneurship training; provide IGA support for poor women and youth establishing Self-help groups or VSLA . M&E, Visibility and communication activities are included in the project life cycle. The proposed activities are critically linked with food self-sufficiency, nutrition and WASH. Because activities are vested on restoring livelihoods and NRM. Such condition will initiate vibrant collaboration with the WASH, Nutrition and food cluster. The Physical and biological soil water conservation practices will increase surface and ground water recharges and protect infrastructure from siltation. The planned activities contribute to the restoration of the ecosystem through the development of the watersheds; Promotion and utilization of renewable energy. Furthermore, IGA selection criteria will include environmental parameter that beneficiaries’ IGA plans will be evaluated against its impact on the environment. The above justification will show the project’s environmentally friendliness.DICAC made the intended activities to be conflict and Gender sensitive. As PSEA activity, involvement and consideration of women and girls as they are disproportionately affected by the humanitarian emergency is critical. The project will also impose its efforts for the greater social cohesion using enlightened volunteer clergies, Sunday school youth enabled the church on a better ground for social reconstruction. By adhering to humanitarian principles, the project will not be the cause for conflict between beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries. Relevant government and community level structures including beneficiary representatives will involve in the promotion and management of PSEA. Additionally, rapid protection analysis (including factors that heighten the risk of GBV, the capacity of local authorities to mitigate risk and support survivors) will be done. PSEA reporting and case handling mechanism will be in place. Finally, AAP is ensured by listening and responding to beneficiary needs, perspectives, and priorities. It always focuses on Participation of beneficiaries throughout the project implementation Information provision, Consultation, and placing complaints and feedback mechanisms. Service providing staffs are also guided by the organizational code of conduct to use their power responsibly to apply responsible use of power.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

This project will be implemented by Ethiopian Orthodox Church Development and Inter - Church Aid commission (EOC- DICAC) which is a local SCO in partnership with FAO. Other Government signatories and generic partners and signatories will involve in designing, planning, Implementation, M&E and closing of the project. Different advocacy groups such as religious institutions, Youth forums, community care coalitions, child right commutes in each woreda will participate in all process of the humanitarian response. EOC-DICAC will have project agreement with concerned bureaus of regional government and roles and responsibilities of each will be articulated in the agreement. Other advocacy groups will sign Memorandum of understanding with DICAC and clearly identify their role and engage in a meaningful way. The generic partners like Amhara Regional, Zonal and woreda level Bureau of agriculture, Bureau of Finance, Regional Disaster Risk Management Commission, regional Food Security and Job Creation, women and youth office etc… This project also identified community level partners and stakeholders like Community Based Association, Faith Based Association, Youth Association, Women Association, Elders and Micro Enterprises Associations. The generic partners of this project will be responsible to play facilitative roles in accessing various services such as livestock health services, agricultural inputs services, training facilitation, IGA assistances, and technical supports in restoring livelihoods, joint monitoring and supervisions, Market linkages, review the project implementation, project appraisal and endorsement. They also play the role of collaboration, legal protection for the project as appropriate, engaged in the mobilization of local resources using their governing structures, lead review meetings, undertake supportive supervision, take measures from the inception of the project till the end addressing sustainability issues and finally takeover the project activities following to the completion of the project. Specialized service providers like Technical and Vocational skill training centres will provide capacity building training for the target groups. The networking and partnership with all these partners will be tied by memorandum of understanding defining the role, responsibilities and contribution of each actor.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

In order to ensure the collection of feedback from the most vulnerable community members, there are several plat forms or arrangements at the community level that need to be strengthened and functional and help in doing so. The most important plat forms are community care coalitions (CCC), Child Right Committees (CRC) and other grass root level volunteers and partners. So that they follow, technically assist, involve in the case management, and involve in overall process of project implementation starting from the target selection up to case closure of the target groups. The project will work closely work with this plat forms to collect feedback from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels. EOC-DICAC will establish compliant handling mechanism, where target beneficiaries encouraged to exercise their rights and provide feedback to EOC-DICAC on the humanitarian response project. As the development and Humanitarian wing of the Ethiopian Orthodox church, DICAC uses religious fathers, archbishops, Clergies, Sunday school students, to promote and catalyze development and humanitarian assistance in the community and closely work with various Religious institution to foster development and humanitarian assistance.Religious leaders have the capacity to maintain human psychology to withstand different life threating challenges and live with hopes in most religious institutions in Ethiopia. They preach, consult in all situations, hear confessions from followers and provide appropriate spiritual counseling and order to do some humanitarian and good things. No one is allowed to lay these fathers and they are highly confidential to maintain all the secrete they hear from the community members. Generally, it is common to hear individual confidential cases by Religious fathers and provide Psycho spiritual supports in all places in Ethiopia. The project intended to use this role of the religious fathers to collect feedback using as age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channel. Furthermore, EOC-DICAC will use its feedback loops that is functioning starting from the community level to project office, from the project office to Coordination offices and from the coordination office to the head offices levels. Appropriate feedback data collection mechanisms will be developed and will be conducted regularly to improve the project transparency accountability and performance by handling all the vulnerable community concerns and claims in timely manner.EOC-DICAC further strengths the engagement of the government signatories to play proactive role in ensuring all the planned activities are delivered to the community with all safety, protection and humanitarian principles as well in a timely fashion. They are authorized to monitor and evaluate the project interventions and all the necessary deliverables. They will have the mechanism to collect data from the most vulnerable communities using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels.
