Objectives

Objective 1: . Improve equitable access to and utilization of quality lifesaving and comprehensive primary and secondary health services for crisis-affected populations with special focus on sexual reproductive, maternal, neonatal, child and adolescent health; mental health and psychosocial support; and non-communicable diseases. Objective 3: Encourage healthy living, improve health seeking behaviour and utilisation of essential service package among refugees and host populations through community engagement, with special attention to gender considerations and vulnerable groups.

Needs

According to health sector the health status of the population in need remains fragile. Adherence to minimum standards for services and staffing and quality of care in health facilities is a concern and availability of NCD treatment, MHPSS services remain limited. Newborn care is poor, especially at community level and referral linkages need to be improved. Family planning needs remain unaddressed, particularly for the most vulnerable populations and support is required to strengthen neonatal care. The SRH needs for high-risk and special need populations including sex workers, survivors of gender based violence and adolescents are yet to be addressed. Progress was made in routine immunization but coverage remains insufficient in some areas.During 2019 Tdh will improve access to primary health care for the crisis-affected populations by ensuring access to primary health care services through the HP in camp 13 and PHC in camp 27, as well as community outreach in both locations. Tdh will stabilize services offered by the HP and ensure the provision of sector recommended minimum service package, improve the quality of the services provided, with a strong focus on SRH and aSRH, EPI as well as MHPSS. Additionally, the referral system will be reinforced both with other health facilities, and other sectors (nutrition, protection and WASH). Emphasis will be put on strengthening the community outreach through capacity building of community health volunteers and safe motherhood promoters. The plan for the response was prepared to address the most pressing needs of the community identified by the sector and Tdh, through monitoring the ongoing response. As a part of commitment to accountability to the beneficiaries Tdh will establish a feedback mechanism for both facilities operated in 2019. At the moment funding is secured to cover the activities for the first quarter of 2019, but Tdh is currently working on proposal to secure the funding for the rest of the year.
