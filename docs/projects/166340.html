End Date

Project Summary

This project is targeted at providing support to prevent spread and transmission of coronavirus among learners, teachers, Parents and SBMC in MMC, Jere and KalaBalge and Damboa LGA of Borno state through reaching 100,000 school children Boys and girls, 5000 parents (men and women), SBMC and stakeholders (ie. Principals, teachers, students, parents, PTAs) with Coronavirus preventive measures massages, mode of spread massages, response strategies and Corona virus update within six months.
GOALPrime shall Development, print, and distribute appropriate awareness messages on coronavirus prevention and referral to schools factoring in the language and visual impairments targeting 200, Formal and informal education structure which include Temporary learning structure (TLS) in IDP camps, Qur’anic schools, Nursery and Primary schools, and Secondary school across MMC Jere, Kala-Balge and Damboa LGA, as well as dissemination of preventive messages in and around school communities through Mass Campaign and house hold sensitization.
GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall Mobilize and sensitize 5000 Qur'anic school (Tsangaya and Islamya) owner on preventive measures of COVID 19 across MMC, Jere, KalaBalge and Damboa LGA and Mobilization and sensitization of SBMC and stakeholders (ie. Principals, teachers, students, parents, PTAs).
GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall through EiEWG MoE, SUBEB and LEA identify 100 teachers across the targeted LGAs and train them on Mental Health Psychosocial support to enable the have the required capacity to support the school children to build resilience to cope with the challenges of COVID 19.
Through this project GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall carry out disinfection of 50 schools across the target LGAs to ensure the safety of the school environment to facilitate safe reopening of schools. And Carry out Back to School Campaign to support the 10,000 out of school Children to return back to school by the end of coronavirus spread with the support of Ministry of Education, Local Government education Authorities and other Education in Emergency Working Group and education partners across the project targeted locations.

1 - Needs

The Coronavirus pandemic and its strategic response across Nigeria and particularly northeast affected 4.2million Men, Women, Boys and girls in the education system, which includes school children, parents, education officials and the communities across the state, following the ongoing emergency in the state, It is well noted that government has close down schools and ask everybody to stay at home to reduce the wide spread of Coronavirus and enable easy tracing of the transmission. There are numerous unreliable information sources on COVID 19 and lack of implementing partners/actors involved in COVID19 awareness activities leading to Lack of reliable information and misinformation on coronavirus and possible fear/reluctance of children (boys and Girls) returning to schools, and women and men not absle to play their expected roles to prevent the spread of COVID 19, Therefore it is important to create structured information dissemination on Coronavirus preventive measures to reach women, men, young people and Boys and girls. Not all children will have access to the digital platforms, therefore alternatives need to be developed to make education available for hard to reach children and vulnerable children and youth. To complement the effort of government and health actions to contain the spread and treatment of Coronavirus, there is need to develop, print, and distribute IEC materials (i.e. posters, leaflets, comics) on coronavirus prevention in local language, Development and dissemination of prevention key messages through pre-recorded radio messaging and lessons from ECD to Senior Secondary level, Development and dissemination of prevention messages through SMS/text messaging, Mobilization and sensitization of Parents, SBMCs and community/tribal leaders (i.e. Principals, teachers, students, parents, PTAs) - Refreshments/megaphones/posters etc. Provision of psychosocial support (i.e. counselling, group activities, awareness) incl. recreational activities in family.
