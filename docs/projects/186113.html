Project Summary

The social crisis in the Anglophone Regions that began in late 2016 has eroded many years of social and economic development efforts, increased levels of risk and violence, breakdown in social infrastructures like schools, health, water, and sanitary infrastructure pushing the populations to a situation of poor hygienic practices. According to IOM/OCHA data, as of September 2020, some 409,173 people were displaced within NWSW Regions, and 360,547 are considered as returnees. The female population accounts for 48% of the total displaced population, children 44% (51% girls and 49% boys), and older people for 12%.
The outbreak of COVID 19 has further compounded the situation. In most localities there is poor access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water to the masses of displaced persons and the host communities that is exposing the affected population to an increase morbidity and mortality. This situation can be put under control if water points are constructed or rehabilitated, latrines constructed and basic WASH materials made available to IDPs and Vulnerable host community members.
This project aims to:
-Improve sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water to vulnerable people in high concentration localities.
-Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity and mortality of the affected population in the crisis.
This will be achieved through:
-Construction as well as rehabilitation of non-functional water points in affected localities,
-Construction of community latrines with showers to serve the masses thereby guaranteeing 100% confinement of faecal matter,
- Engaging in hygiene and sanitation promotion and supporting individual households with financial and technical support to enable them to construct their household latrines.
This project plans to:
Make available 10 water points with functional user committees, put at the use of communities 25 public gender disaggregated latrines with showers, distribute 5000 WASH kits to vulnerable households, support 50 households realise family latrines and reach out to 50000 people with behaviour change communication on safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people.
Accountability to beneficiaries will be ensured through education and sensitization throughout the project, involvement in scheduling of activities, as well as through beneficiary complaints and feedback mechanisms that will be put in place. Accountability to the partner will be through regular sharing of reports.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The project plans to address the following needs through its activities: Gender-sensitive latrines with showers: 10 community gender-disaggregated latrines with showers (cabins) will be constructed in IDP sites and vulnerable host communities. Water points: 4 water points (boreholes, water taps, small water networks) will be constructed and 4 non-functional water points rehabilitated in IDP sites and vulnerable host communities to give at least 6000 people access to a safe water source (2500 men and 3500 women). WASH and Menstrual hygiene kits: 1,000 WASH kits distributed to vulnerable households in the target communities. Water user committees: 8 water user committees will be established/ revamped in crisis-affected localities where water the water points will be constructed or rehabilitated. Safe hygiene and sanitation practices: 6,000 persons will be sensitized on safe hygiene and sanitation practices including COVID 19 preventive measures and each receive soap. Household latrines: 50 households will benefit from financial and technical support to realize latrines in their homes to serve about 500 persons.
