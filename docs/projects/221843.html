Project Summary

Project summary:
The project aims to provide a quality education that promotes innovative self-actualization for vulnerable individuals living in Lac Chad, N,Djamena, and Abeché provinces. The Community Centre (CCC) will offer valuable opportunities to acquire knowledge and skills that empower them to overcome life’s challenges and improve their circumstances through handicapped-friendly activities and services that accommodate their disabilities. It will also encourage awareness within the Chadian communities and decision-makers, local authorities, and NGOs about the positive reforms, economic and social contributions of individuals with disabilities.
The CCC will create a supportive environment with diverse roles and tools designed to foster positive behaviors and morals. These efforts will encourage community members to share their ideas and visions, contributing to the achievement of sustainable development goals. The Community Centre is part of our initiative to help vulnerable people become more independent and to combat the various forms of exclusion they have experienced over time.
Project goal:
We are committed to empowering individuals with disabilities to participate in sustainable development projects through our Community Capacity Center (CCC). By strengthening schools for disabled children, we aim to support vulnerable individuals in refugee camps and hosted communities. The project will also provide to government with a reform that provides rights-based support to people with disabilities for long-term goals.
We enhance self-help capabilities through education, improving the well-being of children with disabilities in line with human rights principles. Raising community awareness is crucial to changing the negative perceptions of disabled individuals, encouraging their active participation instead of viewing them as unproductive community members.
SVO is dedicated to creating sustainable projects and providing ongoing support to vulnerable individuals, serving them with excellence and compassion.

1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

Background: The Sudanese Volunteers Organization (SVO) was founded by Sudanese refugees who have experienced life in refugee camps or internally displaced IDP camps. Operating under the principle of "REFUGEE FOR REFUGEE," SVO is a volunteer-based, non-governmental organization (NGO) active in Chad and Sudan, and remains neutral, non-political, non-religious, and non-commercial. SVO aims to empower individuals to provide voluntary services to vulnerable people in eastern Chad and Sudan. Officially registered in Sweden, France, and Sudan, it is active in 8 refugee camps in eastern Chad: Kounoungou, Mile, Am-Nabak, Touloum, Iridimi, Farchana, Aboutangue, and Metche with over 30,000 members across these camps. While we lack the resources of larger NGOs, our unique perspective as refugees drives NGO and our commitment to assist the most individuals with vulnerabilities, including the elderly and people with disabilities. We frequently encounter marginalized individuals living in inhumane conditions, often deprived of basic care, clothing, and bedding. Our experiences show that the target groups of people with disabilities, elderly people, and individuals with mental illnesses are target groups living in inhuman conditions without observation of their needs or rights. Overview of the project:The project aims to provide a quality education that promotes innovative self-actualization for vulnerable individuals living in N,Djamena, Abeché, and Lac Chad for start. The Community Centre (CCC) will offer valuable opportunities to acquire knowledge and skills that empower them to overcome life’s challenges and improve their circumstances through handicapped-friendly activities and services that accommodate their disabilities. It will also encourage awareness within the Chadian communities, local authorities, and NGOs about the positive economic and social contributions of individuals with disabilities. SVO has provided services and activities for people with disabilities for over 8 years in 5 refugee camps. In 2024 we launched a data collecting project for collecting data of people with disabilities in refugee camps so it will be easier for us to design the necessary project and provide service and assistance. Also, we started 2028 school children with disabilities in the refugee camp of Kounougou. The school is today the only place providing school and social services for individuals with disabilities in the whole of Chad. Now we have numbers of people with disabilities, people with mental illness, and elderly people in 6 refugee camps: Meteche, Aboutangue, Farchana, Mile, Touloum, and Irirdimi. The data project enables us to identify individuals and the needs of target groups. We have reached and shown that there are now any kind of systematic services from the public sector or private sector for the target group in Chad. The CCC will create a supportive environment with diverse roles and tools designed to foster positive behaviors and morals. These efforts will encourage community members to share their ideas and visions, contributing to the achievement of sustainable development goals. The Community Centre is part of our initiative to help vulnerable people become more independent and to combat the various forms of exclusion they have experienced over time. Needs:1. Meeting these needs is crucial for improving independence and quality of life for individuals with disabilities. What do we want to do? (Project activities)We are dedicated to establishing community centers in named provinces that provide essential care and support to residents. Our goal is to integrate individuals into society while offering vital services and opportunities. Our centers will be staffed by committed volunteers and will collaborate with local organizations (OHDC- Organisation Humanitaire de Développement et de Coopération ) that share our vision of enhancing the quality of life for people with disabilities. We will offer rehabilitation, social care, and educational programs for individuals with disabilities, and workshops to aid mental health recovery. Additionally, we will ensure that people with disabilities and the elderly receive necessary hygiene support. Through our CCC program, we empower target groups to assist their neighbors and support the most vulnerable, building a stronger and more resilient community. What happens if we do not do that?Elderly and disabled individuals are often marginalized and lack access to essential services. If we do not provide the right support now, they will remain confined to their rooms, isolated from society, services, and their basic rights.SVO geographical areas of intervention 2025 in the Republic of Chad:N,Djamena, Abeché, and Lac Chad. Construction of the centers will begin one week after funding is received, starting 1st February 1st April. From April 2st to May 2st, the centers will be prepared to receive assets and learning materials. The second phase will focus on teacher training and organizing activities at the centers. The project is set to conclude on June 30, with monitoring and supervision throughout the timeline.
