Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to give dignity to the life of the people who are the most vulnerable people in Northwest Syria thru giving them short-term work opportunities, vocational and skills training, basic housing repair, rehabilitation of social infrastructure, and other early recovery and livelihood activities thru out 2022 and 2023.

4 - Needs

1- Empowering women to have decent training and skills can have a big impact on their future. Giving them opportunities like this can help them boost their morale and create short-term work opportunities that can change their life and can apply further. As we all know, new variants of the noble coronavirus are still being discovered so this project can all help at least a tiny fraction by producing facial masks that will be distributed in the health centers around North Syria. 2- Home Maintenance and Repair of vulnerable families with a woman run-household (orphans) as a basic housing repair support and giving solar-powered panels for them and others who are in need to have access to basic utilities in Idleb and Azez rural areas due to lack of access of it. It covers the families of oppressed and needy Syrian refugee orphans in Syria. 3- Grocery Store Project for families with a woman run-household (orphans) to give them entrepreneurial support for them to stand on their own. 4- Renovation of Kefer Lusin Health Center in Idleb. At a time when the covid virus is spreading so fast, the health center is very important and its repair will be great morale for the village people.

5 - Implementation of activities

1 - Training women in northern Syria and supporting them with the materials needed to make masks.selecting about 60 women from 15 villages in the Sahel Al-rouge area in Idleb. The training duration will be 15 days after these 15 days we will select just 50 women give them a sewing machine and generator to help them to make masks in their home and we will support them by (fabric, strings, diesel, needles (for a month and produce 50 masks per a day. After one month we will collect the masks from these women's homes and we will distribute this mask to the health centers in the north of Syria. A sewing machine can also be used for other purposes. 250 people will benefit from this project. 2- Within the scope of this project, it is planned that 150 people will benefit from this project by helping poor and struggling families. 20 families will be supported by rehabilitation of their house and 10 families will be given a solar-powered panel for them to have basic utility access such as electricity. 3 - We will gonna provide materials to the woman run-household (orphans) such as groceries for them to open their own mini grocery store as entrepreneurial support. We all know that it is hard for a woman run-household to have work outside especially with children. 50 people will benefit from the project. 4- Health is always one of the most essential needs for people. The repair of the health center, which became useless as a result of the war, will meet the health needs of the area.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Abuse of Authority by distribution actors such as discrimination by picking only the people that they know but not the one who is in need. Materials for the project are not being equally distributed for each household. 2. non-beneficiary households in the area can be jealous and feel left alone if not given the same materials such as a solar-powered panel. 3. Looting in the mini grocery store can be a problem and the security of the cashier or the people in charge of the store.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The risk related to sexual exploitation and abuse for this project is minimal to zero due to the project will train and support JUST women. Risk can occur when the beneficiaries are traveling to the assigned place of the workshop unaccompanied.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. The market value of the said materials for making the masks might differ due to the high demand for them because of the noble coronavirus.2. High case of coronavirus might enable us to distribute the made masks for the health centers and facilities. 3. Exchange rate fluctuations between Turkish Lira / US Dollar / Syrian Pound.4. Weather conditions in the area.
