Project Summary

The project aims to provide protection, child protection and GBV prevention and
response services to the most vulnerable MWGB through community-based interventions
and protection emergency response mobile teams in Northern Syria (North Aleppo) and
Northwest Syria The project will cover WGSSs, CFSs, widows camps, support WLOs, community centers ,Mobile teams (most of those teams are emergency response protection mobile teams to response for the new displacement movement and to
conduct the assessment needed to respond for those IDPs).
. The main objective of the project is to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs
through: * supporting the protective environment in Syria. * provision of quality and
integrated protection services with a focus on community-based approaches, including
awareness-raising, psychosocial assistance. * IPA targeting
people with protection, CP, GBV concerns *support other community initiatives through
community centers and outreach mechanisms to address particular protection
issues as identified through protection monitoring. Provision of PFA support
and structures PSS support, including CM *Provide legal
counseling &legal representation on civil status documentation /registration as
well as on housing/land/property issues. *Expand the availability and quality of
protection" CP and GBV services" in targeted location accordance to strategic priorities of
the 2022 GP, CP, MA, and GBV. As well to ensure the protection of the population affected
by the crisis is improved through community-based and individually targeted protection
interventions and advocacy with duty bearers, Increased access for boys and girls to
quality child protection interventions in targeted locations in line with the child protection
minimum standards in Humanitarian Action"by providing Specialized child protection
services (CM) for girls and boys.
Provide quality specialized GBV services for survivors to prevent and mitigate risks of GBV, e.g., • Case management including cash and voucher assistance for survivors or women at risk
• Focused PSS for GBV survivors (only)
• Individual and group counselling/Focused PSS
• Legal services
• Other referrals of GBV survivors to GBV related services
• (PFA)
• Referral to other services ( legal services ,PSS, Medical, non GBV services)
Enhance strategies to empower W&G and prevent GBV, with a particular focus on adolescent girls by providing awareness raising sessions to MWGB by using the awareness raising toolkit with emphasize using M&E toolkits for all GBV services ,focusing on the roll of each part of MEAL and CWs and measure the quality of the services such as survivor satisfaction surveys in GBV, and satisfaction service survey in CP cases.
The project will target the most vulnerable people such as the older women, PWDs including CWDs
IhsanRD has a MEAL plan for all protection sub-sectors to be implemented during the project time frame.
the project aims to reduce the impact of explosive hazards. The project aims to strengthen the capacity of humanitarian actors and
duty-bearers at the national and community levels to assess, analyze and address
protection risks and needs and to respond to child protection concerns in Syria
CB component is a main part of the project, and all staff will be trained on protection specialized(CP, GBV,GP)topics along with targeting humanitarian actors by build the capacity for all the staff with periodical supervision by specialized technical supervisor in Turkey and Syria levels in each AOR. All protection facilities will be rendered to be accessible for PWDs along with provision of transportation service to PWDs and elderlies.
IhsanRD has good communication and coordination with all protection actors in both level by update SM, meeting with stockholders in the areas, signing MOUs and protocols.
IhsanRD is committed to submit the 4Ws for protection clusters.

4 - Needs

As SCI reported on the Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment Report, the most prevalentthreats and risks for children were reported as child labor 61%; school dropout 40%;diseases 21%; followed by additional risks such as violence, physical hazards, warremnants/UXOs and early marriage 10%. According to Northern Syria CP Situation Monitoring Dashboard in March 2021: Almost 78.49% of KIs said that children in NWS need psycho-social supportservices. Almost 86.29% of KIs said that children in NWS are engaged in works that prevent them from joining schools. Almost 36.17% of KIs said that children in NWS are facing separation from usual caregivers. According to Voices from Syria 2020 Report and WoS GBV sub-cluster, for voices 2022, in NWS some of the typesof GBV violence incidents’ occurrence is reported high prevalence of Psychological/emotional abuse 34%. Physical assault counts for 29% of reported incidents, whereas Denial of resources and opportunities counts for 24%, rape and sexual assault add up to a total of 10% whereas early/forced marriages count for the remaining 4%. Based on the needs assessment conducted recently by IRD in the most severe locations, there is a high need for protection services, along with interfering in the Widows’ Camps. IRD aims to cover the gaps regarding to need assessment by providing CP, GBV prevention and response, GP response services. The resources are from HNO 2022,VOICES 2022 and IhsanRD need assessment.

All protection services designed to be accessible to PWDs and adapted to a COVID-19 situation. IhsanRD is the protection emergency response focal point in Jebel Seman district. In coordination with protection actors, monthly meetings will be conducted at both Syria and turkey level to share information. CB component is a prom part of the project.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

IhsanRD adopts a zero-tolerance approach towards sexual exploitation and abuse.And it is committed to the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse both within IhsanRD and within the framework of IhsanRD programs and their beneficiary.To this aim, IhsanRD’s Policy Against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse: • Encourages prevention; • Promotes detection; • Sets out a straightforward investigation procedure; • Sets out a mechanism of sanctions IhsanRD fully upholds principles of freedom of speech and protection of victims of sexual abuse and exploitation. As such, IhsanRD has developed mechanisms that can contribute to preventing sexual exploitation and abuse such as • Signing of the Code of Conduct by the staff in order to clarify unacceptable behavior and in particular sexual exploitation and abuse; • Information to the beneficiaries, staff, and all stakeholders about the existing Complaints and Response Mechanisms - CRM which can allow them to send their concerns and complaints in a confidently way without fear • Implementation of training and awareness sessions to train and empower the teams, particularly for staff exposed to working with minors or the most vulnerable population; • Identify risk factors on an ongoing basis and in particular throughout the project cycle; • Put in place evaluation procedures for IhsanRD staff, partners, and stakeholders by carrying out due diligence, particularly with regard to their ethical commitments and past behaviors.
