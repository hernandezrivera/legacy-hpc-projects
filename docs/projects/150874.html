Project Summary

WRRS therefore intends to intervene with projects that deliver lifesaving to a community caused by diverse catastrophic needs ranging from floods, and civil conflict . Life saving to save lives of nearly 1/5 of children(Boys and girls) 6 59 months and pregnant lactating women , who are acutely malnourished and at risk of either contracting diseases or dying due to malnutrition. This will be done through scaling up treatment of moderate acute malnutrition and both complicated and uncomplicated severe acute malnutrition. To increase access additional Mobile and fixed sites will be put in place. Mother MUAC screening will be adopted to the program for early detection of mulnutrition by the care givers. More community health workers and volunteers will also be employed to increase access, as they hail from the community and thus frequently interact with them, offering services as needed, and appropriately referring those who need specialized assistance. They also follow up those receiving services thus promoting use of health and nutrition services. The project will use the organisation's health facilities as fixed sites , so as to encourage integration. Prevention services will also be employed as a way of reducing incidence of malnutrition. These will include provision of micro nutrient to children 6-59 months and PLWs, Nutrition Health and hygiene promotion support .
WRRS will carry out the implementation of these interventions in the following localities: Afmadow District : dobley and Blis Qooqane, in Badaadhe district Kolbiyo, While in Kismayu via Afmadow: Bolo Gaduud.
The project will work with nutrition cluster partners to avoid overlapping and with health, WASH and food security partners to link beneficiaries to those projects as much as is possible. Community consultations will be key to minimize on the risks and for accountability.
WRRS with its capacity and presence in the region will implement the interventions in this area as well as appeal for the funding for the implementation.

Needs

FSNAU September technical release 2018 indicate that the overall nutrition situation has improved due to a combination of improved food security, reduced disease outbreaks and sustained humanitarian interventions. However, despite this improvement, nearly 954,007 children are expected to be acutely malnourished, including 173,594 who are expected to be severely malnourished in the coming months. The surveys indicate Critical levels of GAM (15-29.9%) in 12 out of 33 populations surveyed (7 IDP and 5 Rural) while Critical levels of SAM prevalence (≥4-5.6) were only seen among IDPs. Similarly, the Crude Death Rate (CDR) rates are Critical (1 to <2) in 3/33 population groups surveyed while CDR rates are Serious (0.5 to <1) in 11/32 and U5DR rates (1 to 1.9) in 6/32 population groups surveyed, respectively. Lower Juba is one of the areas in Somalia with chronic high levels of acute malnutrition beyond the emergency threshold. It is also a district where the IDPs report high prevalence acute malnutrition according to FSNAU surveys. Almost 1 in every 2 children suffering from a communicable disease, limited access to health facilities, very few women with access to Maternal and rates are high (>20 %) in many parts of the country but highest among population groups surveyed in northeast and central parts of the country. Overall, the median GAM is 14 per cent which is an improvement from Critical in 2017 Gu (median GAM of 17.4 per cent). Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) prevalence is Critical. From a protection perspective, Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs are disproportionately affected with critical prevalence of acute malnutrition (Global Acute Malnutrition of more than 15 per cent) in seven out of 15 locations surveyed as well as some rural livelihoods. WRRS will target 45,000 beneficiaries from the target population to over BSNP to reduce on the population prevalence of acute malnutrition. Coordination with all stakeholder will be followed for proper service delivery.

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

The project for sustainability and durable solution will focus on mitigating the effects of the ongoing catastrophes in an integrated manner. The project intends to integrate treatment of malnutrition by treating Moderate acute malnutrition,Un complicated severe acute malnutrition, and complicated severe acute malnutrition all which have reported increased prevalence in the last few months in most areas of Somalia. These will integrate other health and WASH related integration like hygiene promotion, and education treatment of common illnesses and expanded program for immunization (EPI). WRRS runs an maternal child and health clinic (MCH) / out patient department (OPD) linked together. These interventions are life saving interventions because they focus on those at risk of death due to any additional shocks. The project will also target children under five , pregnant and lactating women as the most vulnerable and should be prioritized in emergency settings.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Protection will he key in the project. We will ensure that girls and women that face abuse discovered in our centers get help. Our project will also treat all beneficiaries equally and protect the dignity of our beneficiaries.We will ensure that our project involves most community groups so as to demystify operations and avoid rumors of the projects' activities. We will also protect our staff by collecting security updates regularly and sharing with them in time so that they do not go to areas where their lives will be endangered

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The project intends to involve the community both male and female, boys and girl of all ages in all stages of the program cycle, so that they understand the rationale behind decisions made.The project intends to use already existing community governance structures and use them as health committees. Health committees will be involved in recruitment of staff, community mobilization and monitoring of the project.Their presence will represent the interest and role of the community in successful completion of the project. The project will also have a feedback mechanism , from which complaints and comments from the community will be collected, and fed back to the same. This will be done in a highly confidential manner with very few staff in the management allowed to interact with this process. Complaints will be addressed immediately while compliments will also be shared with staff.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

WRRS has been working in areas where security and conflict has been a major factor affecting the beneficiaries. Accountability for violations is limited, with traditional or formal mediation and justice mechanisms having been disrupted, or having become disrespected or inaccessible. As a result of general insecurity, inter-clan conflict escalations, indiscriminate attacks as well as military offensives and withdrawals in the context of the conflict between AMISOM and AS, and supporting actors. This is further aggravated regarding violations for marginalized groups where societal inequalities result in reduced protection. WRRS will work closely in collaboration in providing education on Disaster Risk Reduction, and how to go about in areas with explosive remnants of wars.
