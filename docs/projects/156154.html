Project Summary

Through this project, Ataa will be reaching 20,107 beneficiaries (57%female-43%male) - (58% under18-42% above18) in:
1- Idleb, Harim, Dana, Tal Elkaramej.
2- Aleppo, Jebel Saman, Atareb, Kafr Naha.
The targeted communities population size is about 3,500 HHs, 40% Host communities and 60% IDPs from: (Hama–Homs–Aleppo–Damascus). In average, two families are living in the same house under the same roof and using the same items, and the reason for host high number of IDPs in the targeted communities is the security situation is relatively safe.
The targeted communities are in need for such project as a few organizations had worked there since a year under WASH sector and very few humanitarian organizations had worked there under other sectors, therefore it is very essential for the target communities to have affordable, long lasting water source as well as to have a sewage system, solid waste management and hygiene promotion, all of that will greatly help in preventing many diseases to beneficiaries and other life forms in the targeted community.
This project aims to reach the most vulnerable people who have no access to clean and drinkable water for almost two years, and which houses are not connected to the sewage system and hygiene items and reduce of diseases throughout the waste collection and oversee the transportation of waste to landfill on a weekly basis.
55% out of 3,500 HHs will be able to use water through the water network and directly from their houses and 6 wells after rehabilitation will pump the water through the networks, the other 35% have access to the water network already.
55% out of 3,500 HHs, their houses will be able to connect to the sewage system instead of sewage pits, and the other 40% have access already to the sewage system.
100% out of 3,500 HHs will receive hygiene gender sensitive kits along with hygiene promotion activities.
71% out of 3,500 HHs will help with solid waste management activities.
Ataa will conduct field visits to the rehabilitation sites for all water networks and sewage systems to follow up with the rehabilitation process and will conduct a final inspection after the rehabilitation is finished.
Ataa will advertise in the local communities and social media for (water station, hygiene promoters) staff and technicians, 3,500 HHs will be provided with hygiene gender-sensitive kits, along with raising hygiene awareness sessions.
Specific monitoring activities include: rehabilitate water networks and chlorination in the communities, confirming distribution of hygiene items through onsite monitoring, monitoring report, confirming beneficiary verification for their access to cleaned and chlorinated water, feedback and satisfaction according to beneficiary lists.
Ataa will activate cost recovery mechanism by collecting monthly fees from every HHs in the targeted communities, and the local council in each community will be responsible for collecting the fees from HHs. MoU will be signed with the local councils.
Ataa will conduct FGDs with targeted communities to inform them about goal, outcome, and, activities of the project. Brochures and banners will be used to share information with the communities.
Ataa will set a community – based mechanism to make sure that community members can submit their complaints and feedback. feedback tracker will be prepared on a monthly basis.
Local councils and other stakeholders in the targeted communities will be consulted to oversee all aspects of the project in the target communities, especially the following tasks:
prioritizing of the list of beneficiaries, advising on the availability of local labor, construction teams, and contractors, informal monitoring on-site during construction. As well as participate in the commission that would undertake the final technical inspection.

Needs

According to HNO 2019: “Idleb Governorate as well as surrounding NSAG controlled-areas have become extremely crowded amidst the significant displacement to and within those areas. Increased pressure on already overstretched WASH services has resulted in increased dependency on alternative and often unsafe water sources that significantly increase the risk of waterborne diseases. Piped water systems, though dysfunctional in some areas, continue to operate at varying levels of efficiency, with urban centers generally better served. Limitations in regular operations, maintenance and repair have had a significant impact on the systems efficiency, leading to a higher risk of contamination. The results indicate that the water chlorination level remains insufficient in many areas and that many people are still forced to complement water received from the network or solely rely on alternative sources. The WASH Household assessment indicates that at least 10 per cent of garbage is left in public areas, potentially contributing to the increase in leishmaniosis cases observed since the beginning of 2018 in some areas. In addition, some places used for garbage dumping are not prepared and managed well that could contribute to increase vector breading.” PiN:15.5 million-Acute PiN:6.2 million By Sex: Male:7.7M; Female:7.8M BY AGE: Young Children (<5):1.5M-Children (5-17):4.6MAdults (18-59):8.6M-Elderly (>59):0.7MAtaa conducted a rapid need assessment, with 4 KIIs and 32 HHs in each community, Key informants who were (members of the corresponding targeted communities, water engineers, water technicians, and IDPs representative) had been interviewed as they considered knowledgeable on their geographic coverage and whose understanding could present an overall situation of the targeted locations. The targeted sample were defined by snowball methodology and the interviews were conducted in a door to door manner. Prior to the interview, the data collectors followed an already applied protocol that required them to introduce themselves and Ataa, and the purpose of the interview. An oral informed consent was taken as well. 4 partner enumerators (2females and 2males) conducted data collection in the targeted communities, this assessment aimed to assess respondents’ main needs and focusing on the following areas: (Water System-Sewage system-Most needed Hygiene Items-Solid waste management-Hygiene Promotion).Based on RNA findings, the water stations in targeted communities need rehabilitation and replacing some equipment. also, 60% out of the population don't have access to the sewage system and they are using sewage pits, 60% of the targeted communities are using water trucking and their houses have no access to the water network. while the other 40% don't afford to buy their needs of water. Due to the high hygiene items’ prices and lack of jobs, both of distributing hygiene kits and conducting hygiene promotion are needed. It appears also that there were 6 wells in the targeted communities (3 in Kafr Naha - 3 in Tal Elkaramej), the 6 wells are connected to the old network and providing water for 40% of the targeted population, the approximate production capacity for wells measured by (m3/day) are as followed: (880 in Tal Elkaramej, 760 in Kafr Naha), Ataa will operate the 6 wells for eight hours and each water station will provide 50 L for each person per day in targeted communities, the 6 wells will provide water to 3,500 HHs for all HHs’ water uses purposes. All wells need minor rehabilitation and operation materials (fuel,engine oil and chlorine). In relation to SWM, in all targeted communities waste collectors should be recruited, bins should be provided and solid waste trucks to transport the waste should be assigned and ensure there is a weekly (minimum) waste transportation.

Implementation of activities

Ataa assesses the needs of the targeted communities for water and will conduct infrastructure assessment for the water network and prepare estimated costs and quantities. A request for quotations will be made in the targeted communities or other surrounding areas if needed and contractors will be selected according to the best offers.Ataa will start extending the water networks, and support visits to the working areas will be carried out on a daily basis in addition to a final inspection for the networks and start pumping the water through the water networks. Ataa will install a 110mm polyethylene pipes for 4,500m as a main pipe and connect it with the existing main pipe and will connect a 75mm polyethylene pipes for 3,500m with the new main pipe and to the houses as a sub-network. A request for quotation for the hygiene gender sensitive kits will be made in the targeted communities and contractors will be selected according to the best offers and the kits will be procured.Ataa will start distributing 3,500 hygiene gender sensitive kits. Ataa will advertise in the targeted communities and through the social media for its need for hygiene promoters, shortlisting and interviews will be made, the promoters will be selected, and their contracts will be signed. Ataa will distribute monthly incentives to the promoters, the promoters will conduct hygiene awareness sessions in the targeted communities. Ataa assesses the needs of the targeted communities for water sources and will conduct an infrastructure assessment for the 6 wells in the areas, a request for quotation will be made for the rehabilitation and operation of the wells, the contractors will be selected according to the best offers. Ataa will start the rehabilitation process of the 6 wells, support visits to the water stations will be conducted on a daily basis, final inspection will be made to finalize and handover the process.Ataa will provide the water stations with monthly running costs to cover operating, maintenance, consumables costs … Ataa will advertise in the targeted communities for its need for station staff (engineers, technicians, workers), shortlisting and interviews will be made, selection will be made, and contracts will be signed with the qualified staff, Ataa will distribute monthly incentives for the water station staff. Ataa will start pumping the water from the stations and through the water networks in the targeted communities. Ataa will assesses the infrastructure needs for the sewage systems in the targeted communities to prepare estimated costs and quantities for the sewage system extending process, request for quotations will be made, contractors will be selected according to the best offers. Ataa will start the sewage systems extending process, a daily support visits to the work location will be made, a final inspection will be made after finishing the extending process. A process of drilling and filling of 4,400m will be implemented and installing a 1.5m length and diameter of 0.4m for 3,400m and 1.5m length and diameter of 0.3m for 1,000m as a sub-network to the houses. Ataa will cover the sewage pits with reinforced concreate and provide an iron top cover to the sewage pits, provide and install asphalt for the top covers. Ataa with support from local councils will identify locations near dwellings and designate them as solid waste disposal areas for bins. As much as practically possible, Ataa will encourage to utilize local labor sources (including beneficiaries and other community members) to undertake community waste collection and maintain waste disposal collection sites and oversee the transportation of waste to landfill. Ataa will provide adequate supplies and tools to ensure the safety of workers. Ataa will ensure there are weekly (as a minimum) waste collection and transport of solid waste to designated landfill.

Protection Risk Analysis

During the rehabilitation process, Ataa will ensure safety in the work environment to avoid any physical safety risks. Ataa will provide hygiene gender sensitive kits to the most needed people and the most vulnerable. as well as conducting a hygiene promotion awareness session to reduce risks related to bad hygiene practices. Ataa will establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes and avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school. Ataa will provide adequate supplies and tools to ensure the safety of workers. Ataa will ensure there are weekly (as a minimum) waste collection and transport of solid waste to designated landfill sites. This project aims to preserve the beneficiaries dignity by extending the water network, hence they will not have to use water trucking or travel long distances or wait in lines to bring their water especially women, elders and people with disabilities. both of water networks extension and sewage systems extension will decrease the morbidities rates in the targeted area.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities: Ataa will sign MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities, and Ataa will promote cost recovery systems for longer-term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. - Gender insensitive contents verities of kits with inconsistent items: Ataa will harmonise and standardise gender friendly hygiene kits among the sector members and indicate quantity and instruction for use. - (Late) Distribution of expired unhealthy items: Ataa will establish and monitor expiry date of supplies prepositioned in the warehouse, ensure relevant storage provision and carry out timely disposal of damaged/expired items.- Movements of garbage trucks in congested, camp like settings; Reliability of services: Ataa will consult community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Provide household level solid waste containers/plastic bags to ease disposal of waste from H/H to the public SW collection points. Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school.- Health risk associated with garbage removal: Ataa will ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors- Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors: Ataa will establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organise joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care.- Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to chemicals: Ataa will ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with chemicals wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.- Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organisation, its partners or contractors: Ataa will ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ labourers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labour in the contracts; check and register civil documentation.
