Project Summary

This project is designed to meet the needs of 169000 IDPs of the total beneficiaries targeted. Gender will be mainstreamed into the project through the specific consideration of the different needs of women, men, boys and girls during the design of activities. The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis-segregated data. Settlement mapping exercises will be conducted to get the information of all targeted settlements for necessary services in place and gap analysis, the settlement mapping exercise data will be shared with CCCM cluster lead, which will be useful in creating a master list of the area. This master list will then be posted online for the ease of access to all humanitarian actors, which will be used by different humanitarian partners for their future planning and response.
The majority of IDPs settle in informal and unplanned settlements in the target Regions have poor management which has been appropriated by the ‘gatekeepers’ (informal managers), with living conditions being poor, observed high rates of Gender Based violence. There are few rights for the IDPs including on the land they settle (lack of land tenure) with forced evictions being a regular feature in the IDP Camps.
There is need to provide IDPs with alternative livelihood means by offering skills training and labor opportunities through Cash-for-Work (CfW) opportunities in undertaking site improvement targeting mainly women and youth. The project also plans to work jointly work with Durable Solution Unit(s) and/or other similarly designated entities to initiate actions to address access to secure housing and land tenure, shelter upgrading materials and shared household gender friendly latrines for the IDPs in the 2 target regions.
Protection of women and girls and also children remains a paramount need in the IDP camps to ensure security from sexual and gender based violence. Safe access for the IDPs to basic services and also to and from their camps is a very important need for the displaced in all the indicated two targeted regions. The project will work with the CCCM Cluster and the GBV sub-cluster to conduct awareness workshop for the camp leaders and the local administration on GBV and its role in ensuring protection for the women, girls and children. The project plans to support the provision of CCCM services in order to reduce barriers to assistance, ensure equitable delivery of services, enhance the accountability to affected populations, and strengthen community engagement, participation and empowerment towards self-determination.

Project Activities

1. Undertake vulnerability assessment in the targeted 2 regions to help identify those in IPC 3 and 4 for prioritized support. 2. Support local awareness on the issues of eviction to minimize the negative impacts of IDPs evictions especially to women, youth and marginalized Waqooyi Galbeed and Togdheer Regions. 3. Undertake, jointly with the Local District Administration, a detailed and comprehensive gender context site assessment every 3 months as per CCCM requirements of the targeted 10 IDP Camps in Hargeisa and 6 IDPs Burco in Togdheer Region. 4. Provide legal aid support for the victims of GBVs cases through counseling and referral pathways at community level.5. Capacity building Training for the community level working groups (self-help groups, community committees) on human rights protection, monitoring, reporting and documentation of GBV cases, referral and case management6. Establish a Master List of the IDP Camps with information on locations (and GPS), basic services points, capacities and current and immediate future plans, population supported especially of women, youth and marginalized. 7. Coordinate and conduct gender sensitive intention surveys that also consider protection concerns/aspirations especially for women, children (boys and Girls) and youth with relevant partners and clusters working in the 2 target Regions to ensure the intentions of populations are understood and supported. 8. Identify existing governance structures and focal points for the targeted 16 IDP Camps in the 2 Regions of Waqooyi Galbeed and Togdheer . Women and youth level of inclusion and participation will be identified and supported. 9. Monitor service delivery at site level to ensure that there are no gaps or duplication of activities. 10. Facilitate the setting up of IDP Camp Management Committees (CMCs) as focal points for Camp Management and support activities with 50% of the membership being women. 11. Provide capacity building on the CCCM principles to newly-formed and existing governance structures/focal points in the IDP Camps. 10. Provide for site infrastructure improvements including road and drainage utilizing limited Cash-for-Work (CFW) to provide labour (prioritizing women for labour opportunities), site improvement kit distributions and public light improvements in all the IDP Camps in the targeted areas.

Mitigation Measures

Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement, the community leaders and respective committees will be fully involved in project implementation, monitoring and representation. ASAL will ensure that project committees to be inclusive and that segments of the communities are well represented at all levels such as age and gender diversity. ASAL has a comprehensive community entry and targeting package that guides staff. The project was designed in consultation with the targeted communities, and ASAL strives to maintain these consultations throughout all phases of the project cycle. In addition, ASAL will implement the proposed project in collaboration with all stakeholders paying special attention to the beneficiaries. The government and respective clusters will be involved in beneficiary selection, monitoring and implementation. The project will be initiated with a familiarization meeting held with relevant authorities and communities in targeted locations, to create awareness on project objectives, activities, target locations, expected outcomes, scope, and type and selection criteria for beneficiaries. During community mobilization sessions, ASAL field staff with sound understanding of the community dynamics will provide detailed information on vulnerability-based selection criteria.

Please provide further explanation

Durable solutions are one of the key pillars of ASAL Somaliland programming, and continuous efforts are directed towards promoting durable solution and self-sufficiency of the displaced and vulnerable people across Somaliland. However, ASAL recognizes the complexity and long milestones required to reach this vision. This project will contribute towards supporting opportunities for displaced people to achieve durable solutions given people cantered response, capacity building for both the government and IDP communities, linking community priorities with national government plans and as well as implementing community-based initiatives that can promote community recovery. IDPs have limited options for durable solutions to their displacement.The project activities as designed will contribute to substantially improve living conditions of IDPs as per CCCM Cluster Objective 4, through the establishment of governance systems, increased Housing, Land and Property Rights and social, economic and political inclusion. The integrated activities will contribute to the strategic, targeted and sustainable approach, tailored for the respective regions that takes into account specific displacement and socio-economic context (which varies from region to region) to ensure better governance, basic services access and accountability in supporting the IDPs. The strengthening of the local governance structures within the local authorities and IDP Camps will enhance accountability mechanisms in order to make relevant offices and institutions accessible to IDPs and enable authorities to assume their primary responsibility to gradually enable durable solutions processes in Waqooyi Galbeed and Togdheer Regions.
