Project Summary

Bonyan Organization designed its 2024-25 ERL intervention based on the analysis of MSNA data in NW Syrian, need severity for SNFI assistance, and key messages of HNO 2024, as well as, in line with SOs of HRP and SNFI Cluster.
[*] Taking the assessed needs as a base for the intervention, In 2024, Syria faces heightened humanitarian needs, affecting 16.7 million people, with 5.5 million displaced significantly higher than 2023. February 2023 earthquakes worsened the situation, causing fatalities, injuries, and infrastructure damage. Worsening economic indicators drive population movement, intensifying poverty and reliance on aid. Ongoing military operations hinder access, especially in conflict zones. From January to October 2023, 454 civilians lose their lives due to conflict. Critical infrastructure, severely impacted post-earthquake, leaves communities without vital services. Even before the earthquake, the North-West faced substantial humanitarian needs, including food insecurity, limited healthcare access, and a scarcity of basic services. Vulnerable populations, especially women, children, and the elderly, were already at risk of exploitation, abuse, and neglect.

For 2024 The project is planned to benefit total 38000 INDV (13,295 Boys, 15,200 Girls, 3,800 Men, 5,705 Women with 5% PwD) in 13 subdistricts in Aleppo and Idelb governorates.
SNFI Assistance Project is a comprehensive effort to address urgent needs in IDP camps. It starts with community engagement and NA to identify shelter and NFI requirements, and cash provision facilitates financial assistance. Beneficiaries will be chosen based on vulnerability assessments, also, agreements will be formalized, and transparent procurement contractors will be selected to meet standards.
The details of NFI response activities and emergency shelter activities are included in the extended summary paragraph.
[*] Brief on PSEAH prevention measures
To fortify against sexual exploitation and abuse, Bonyan Organization institutes a robust Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) strategy. PSEA Focal Persons are appointed to address allegations promptly, ensuring investigation and reporting follow standard procedures. Persons with disabilities receive PSEA awareness and direct communication access. Call center numbers and PSEA-related materials are promoted in project areas. Survivor-centered approaches guarantee information access and immediate assistance.
Risk: SEA Violate from one of the staff, Mitigation includes staff training, community feedback, and safe community centers for domestic violence. Mandatory PSEA training, CoC, and complaint mechanisms.
[*] Key Risks and Mitigations:
Bonyan is following the cluster risk matrix as well as developing its own risk matrix when operating in certain areas.
Risk01-Increased risk of inter-community tension due to varied assistance packages across members /Likelihood:H - Impact:H/ Mitigation: 1. Use the Cluster guidance on minimum standards and recommended content. 2. Use recommended minimum standards in quality and specification of items.3. If different to recommended standard, identify, and exclude from variance any items particularly prone to create tensions.
Risk02- Aid diversion by armed groups, including looting of warehouses. /Likelihood:L - Impact:M/ Mitigation: 1. Invest on sound security measure at the warehouses 2. Train the staff on how to deal with aid diversion 3. Establish timely reporting mechanism and info sharing with other actors on the ground, donors and clusters

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Throughout the project, BO will select the target communities based on scoring and prioritizing system in coordination with SNFI Cluster, then will engage the selected community in all phases of the project. Also, BO will ensure that the support will be provided to the most vulnerable people through transparent process based on clear vulnerability criteria (HLP verification will be included). All activity will be documented, lessons learnt will be collected to measure impact of the activities and the community initiatives. Principles of Do No Harm and AAP as well as humanitarian standards are incorporated in the project design and will be ensured during the project timeline.-----------------------------------As for the NFI component Bonyan will pre-mobilize its resources in order to be able to provide rapid response to various situation whether it is winterization support or a response to violence escalation. Bonyan estimates that in 2024 the response will cover 6,000 HHs and will be provided with core NFIs, seasonal and supplementary NFIs kits.-----------------------------------For the emergency shelter component Bonyan planning to target 1,300 HHs The activity ranges from:Repairing, Replacing, or establishing new tents for 350 HHs Establishing temporary transitional shelters for 200 HHsImproving living and sheltering conditions on both households and site level for 750 HHs-----------------------------------Lastly for the improving housing activity, Banyan planning to conduct minor repairs to damaged housing for 300 HHs.-----------------------------------MEAL team of Bonyan will conduct ongoing monitoring of project activities to verify achievement of indicators and to correct any deviation.-----------------------------------[[**]] As of 2025 Bonyan expect to expand its SNFI activities by 40% reaching 10,000 HHs with a total budget of 4,000,000 USD.
