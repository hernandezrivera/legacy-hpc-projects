Project Summary

In 2020, Action Against Hunger will serve the COVID-19 information and communication needs through its CwC information centres to address the gaps and ensure that the voices of the refugees and host communities are heard, their feedback is taken into consideration in programme design. The information centres will also raise awareness on COVID-19 and hygiene promotion along with context specific information and communication needs on nutrition, health, livelihoods, mental health, gender, gender based violence, protection and SRH.

Needs

"Overcrowding in the Rohingya Camps and adjacent host community areas makes the communities uniquely vulnerable to COVID-19, since social distancing is a challenge. The loss of livelihoods of poverty-stricken people due to COVID-19 causes preeminent social disorder among the host and Rohingya community. Moreover, the spread of rumors increases panic, elevates unhealthy behavior and creates information gaps, making the community more vulnerable to the crisis. Lack of access to proper information can be deadly in such crisis. From 2018 the information centres/hubs have been working as a catalyst in the humanitarian response to the Rohingya crisis by not only ensuring that people had access to vital information and communication sources but also enabling a space for people to share their inputs, give feedback and lodge complaints. In JRP 2019 midterm review, it was reported that 92% of refugees say that they have adequate information to make decisions about their daily lives, compared to 23% in late 2017 and 84% in July 2018. This is an impressive progress where the CwC Info hubs have played a vital role as part of the overall humanitarian response. However, the information and communications needs are constantly changing due to changing context such as the COVID-19 pandemic and other factors like repatriation, monsoon and so on.In view of the growing tensions, low levels of social cohesion and interaction between the Rohingya and host communities, due to COVID-19 crisis, CwC including behavior change initiatives are needed to avoid potential conflict and bring social cohesion among new refugees, old refugees and host community. Considering the poor literacy and comprehension skills, ACF will continue to develop and disseminate COVID-19, cyclone preparedness, social cohesion and other context specific easy-to-understand audio-visual contents to disseminate vital messages in person and through centre based and outreach activities. The strategic locations of ACF’s Info Hubs within Nutrition and other program integrated centres also allow both direct and indirect female beneficiaries of all programs to access the Info hubs. This project will directly address COVID-19 information needs by running CwC info hubs for both Rohingya and Host communities. Empowering Rohingya refugees and host communities to provide feedback is key to enable relevant, conflict-sensitive and localized response. Using established feedback systems supplemented with additional feedback through targeted outreach and engagement activities will help provide data that can be consolidated and shared with CwC Working Group (CwCWG) to support the sector’s decision-making. This project will also undertake COVID-19 research activities to understand different kinds of barriers that still influence affected communities to access information services or provide feedback. ACF remains flexible about the research design and objectives as the CwC needs and context are dynamic. ACF will consult with the CwCWG during the research process to maximise the quality and impact.Acknowledging the importance of catering to different needs based on gender and age ACF will continue to collect disaggregate data by sex, age and disability; ensure gender balanced teams (staff and volunteers); use mechanisms and create safe space and opportunities to engage community members and ensure equal access of women, girls, boys and men in the design, implementation and evaluation of ACF’s integrated programs. In 2020, ACF will continue serving the information and communications needs through its CwC Info hubs to address the gaps and ensure that the voices of the refugees and host communities are heard, their feedback is taken into consideration in program design. The Info hubs will raise awareness on COVID-19 and also meet information needs on nutrition, health, livelihoods, mental health, gender, gender based violence, protection and SRH to ensure overall wellbeing of the targeted populations."

Activities or outputs

"COVID Compliant Community Engagement:• Increased access to information- awareness and knowledge of COVID-19 for both refugee and host populations;• Opportunities provided for women and adolescent girls to co-design Hygiene Promotion, nutrition and protection programs and services;• Strengthen mechanisms through Communication with Communities (CwC) initiatives to systematically gather input from women and adolescent girls regarding COVID-19 and their needs and proposed solutions;• Collaborate with women and adolescent girls to co-design programs and services that respond to their self-identified needs and priorities;• Ensure SADD- sex, age and disability disaggregated data;• Provide information and support related to Gender, GBV, Protection and SRHR through women and adolescent-friendly spaces, spaces for elderlies, home visits (for persons with special needs, eg: PLWs and persons with disability), Mens’ Forums;• Achieve and ensure 100% gender balance in information centres/hubs staffing (including volunteers);• Outreach activities including community mobilisation on COVID-19, cyclone and monsoon preparedness, social cohesion etc. COVID Compliant Information and feedback service centers/facilities:• Use advance data management and monitoring system including case-management (DHIS2-PDA software) to analyse progress on integrated program and will be shared and dicussed through consultation with/as per guidance from CwC WG;• Strengthen complaint and feedback mechanisms;• Strenthen referral mechanisms to coordinate among sectors and working groups; COVID Relevant Communication Resources:• Develop information content on Gender, GBV, Protection and SRHR in addition to other integrated programme content (eg. COVID-19, Hygiene Promotion, Nutrition, Health, WaSH, Mental Health, Care Practices);• Active participation in coordination meetings and sector initiatives (eg. for advocacy, communication). Capacity Developement:• Capacity building of staffs on maintaing Gender Equality and GBV amidst COVID-19 situation for Action Against Hunger and it’s partners;• Training and skills development of staff and volunteers; COVID-19 Related Assessment/Research/Studies:• Undertake research and conduct analysis to inform strategic planning for CwC initiatives. The research design will be defined in consultation with the CwC Working Group to address the changing needs and context of the CwC sector;"
