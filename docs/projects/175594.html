Project Summary

The project plans to assist the most vulnerable population displaced by violence in Cabo Delgado Province through measures to ensure the provision of nutritional services and treatment to reduce malnutrtion and access to improved feeding practices in the districts of Metuge, Pemba, Montepuez Chiure and Ancuabe.
TOTAL: 10.000 direct target. // Indirect target 20.000

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the nutritional situation of people displaced by violence in the province of Cabo Delgado by implementing two types of actions: Those aimed at Facilitate access to treatment services for Acute Malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies, such as mobile brigades foused in nutrititional screening and treatments, distribute supplement feeding to health units and IDPS's site camps that need it assinting the logistic strcuture of Cluster, detect new cases, and continuously monitoring in the targeted districts. (limited target) On the other hand, those actions destined to improve the attention to the target population in the area of nutrition by increasing their knowledge and improving their general health status through hygiene promotion activities linked to nutrition, in this direction it wl be developed Trainings of CHWs (APEs and ACS) on topical areas related to IYCF and HIV to provide one-on-one sessions at household, and also cooking demonstrations that will be carried out in the health units with the aim of teaching mothers to use local nutritious, cheap and usually abundant products to improve their feeding and that of their children, training on the correct handling of food, or the creation of health committees that among other responsibilities will develop a continuous screening in search of new cases of malnutrition to report to the health agents during the visits. (Higher target), in addition it will broadcast radio spots, novels and other media products in programmes related to IYCF and HIV, in local radios. Likewise, control and prevention measures against covid19 will be implemented in all planned sites.Pemba City – family Hosts IDP`s : ( 1.663 Individuals)Metuge district (IDP`s Site Camps) : ( 1.450 Individuals)Montepuez District (IDP`s Camps & family hosts) : ( 1.840 Individuals)Ancuabe District (IDP’s Site Camps) : ( 1.100 Individuals)Chiure District (IDP’s Site Camps) : ( 1.184 Individuals)TOTAL: 7.237 direct target. // Indirect target 12.000

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection Describe how the proposed project indentifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm? How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people? (maximum 200 words) The joint and coordinated work between local authorities, Cluster Leading international Agencies (UN) and the beneficiaries themselves is essential for a successful, flexible and continuously developing risk analysis and management and screening it.For this reason, will be carried out a previous identification of potential risks , which will also include a risk and management analysis , through the implementation of a collaborative coordinated work system between the beneficiaries, district and provincial health authorities and with the different clusters involved in the project (CCCM, Nutrition Cluster (UNICEF)). These results will be contrasted with the previous risk analyses identified by the Mozambican authorities in charge for the assistance to the displaced people (INGC, DPS, Gender Social Assistance and Gender Directorate), as well as the district administrators and the corresponding DDSMAS). The empowerment of people will be one of the crosscutting goals that the project aims through individual and familiar nutrition and health promotion activities, cooking demonstrations and involvement of people within the activities themselves, making them part of the decision making process, and not just recipients of the activities, making people the protagonists of their own well-being. For this process will be highly involved the health committees created in each sites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Accountability to Affected People Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?(maximum 200 words) At the beginning of the project, together with the baseline of nutrtion needs, it will be conducted an initial survey to gather needs and expectations of the beneficiaries, as well as to report them on the activities planned. Activists will use the regular monitoring visits and cooking demonstrations to maintain an ongoing dialogue with the beneficiaries and report about the ongoing results and coming activities. Quarterly meetings will be held with local leaders and health committees in each of the places planned, to evaluate together the progress of the project and collect complaints, suggestions etc. At this time it is essential to take advantage of the fact that many displaced people arrive at the resettlement camps previously organized in communities, which means that exist a real possibility of talking with local leaders and involving them in the dialogue with the beneficiaries. The project will take advantage of this circumstance to facilitate the management of complaints in conjunction with the reception of individual complaints collected on a regular basis by the activists during their family visits and in the nutritional screening visits. Finally will be carried a final survey to analyze and communicate the project results, measures taken, and gaps and lessons learnt.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disability InclusionExplain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance.(maximum 200 words) People with disabilities will be located and incorporated into the activities as priority beneficiaries through the implementation of a baseline census of the beneficiaries at the beginnig of the project conducted among health promoters , community leaders, and the collaboration of other national and international partners directly related to the work with vulnerable people. will be placed an special emphasis on the active incorporation of these persons in the activities, being prioritized when receiving health care, and in the development of hygiene and well feeding promotion and cooking demonstrations. At the same time, will be developed a map of existing barriers to identify and eliminate these, or at least to manage it correctly to allow a full and fair access to health services for all. Finally, will be carried out a permanent screening of people with disabilities and special vulnerability during the visits of the health promoters and with the collaboration of community leaders and health committees, that also will colleboarate in screening and notification of malnutrition undetected cases .

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Respect for the environment will be ensured throughout the project, avoiding any type of environmental pollution, such as forest degradation, and the contamination of aquifers that could lead to a deterioration in the quality of life of the people who will benefit from the activities of this project. The collection and adequate use of existent resources used for coking demonstrations will be ensured, following the international regulations dictated by the Mozambican authorities and UNICEF. Likewise, the development of activities that promote an adequate environmental sanitation of the sites camps and homes that they host will be encouraged, aiming to guarantee a healthy, clean and hygienic environment. It will be included in all hygiene and cooking demonstration promotion activities as a cross-cutting element of environmental conservation and environmental health, highlighting the impact that environmental degradation has on people quality of life, and why it is so important to avoid deforestation, preserve water sources and what measures can be taken to respect in daily life.
