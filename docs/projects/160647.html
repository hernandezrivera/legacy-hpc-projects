Project Summary

This project is designed to provide emergency support to vulnerable households living within 20 km from the contact line. 25 locations have been selected in Donetsk Oblast (GCA). The aim is to help address immediate needs and prevent a deterioration of existing conditions of vulnerable households, as well as build the resilience of people.
The project offers:
1. multi-purpose cash assistance to 600 people to help address immediate needs and prevent a deterioration of existing conditions of vulnerable households. These vulnerable people will be selected using a vulnerability assessment questionnaire and home visit questionnaire, agreed upon with the FSL and CTP clusters.
2. livelihood grants to 150 vulnerable households willing and able to conduct agricultural activities with the purpose of becoming more self-sustainable.
3. trainings in agriculture to 50 vulnerable household able and willing to start agricultural activities and build a sustainable livelihood.
4. psycho-social services in the form of self-help groups to increase the resilience of the affected population (200 people).
Dorcas has physical access to the affected population in the proposed project locations, having worked in that region previously. The proposed activities are in line with Dorcas expertise in cash

1 - Needs

According to OCHA Humanitarian Needs Overview 2019, 5.2 million people are affected by the conflict, while 2.3 million people are reported to have an ongoing humanitarian need. According to REACH Economic Security Assessment, most areas with the highest humanitarian need lie within 5 kilometers from the contact line, both in GCA and NGCA. The most common reported type of vulnerability is pension age (64%), chronic illness (19%) and disability (12%). Population aged 60 and older make up to 37% of the rural population. 74% heads of households reported to have at least 1 type vulnerability from the mentioned list. Such factors, in combination with continuous life threat, often expose people to stress and distress, increasing their (psychological) suffering. According to Dorcas' MPC post-distribution monitoring report and as well as Reach Economic Security Assessment, food remains one of the most needed types of support, requested by 27% of the respondents.Dorcas plans to address immediate needs through cash assistance, as a life saving support (this aid modality is requested by 31% of the beneficiaries, living in 0-5 km from the contact line).In Dorcas PDM report, cash was named as the most preferred modality due to a limited access to ATMs, therefore, Dorcas will use cash transfers through Ukr Poshta, as well as transfers to beneficiaries’ bank cards, if necessary/requested.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Activity 1: provision of 600 emergency cash grants for the most vulnerable conflict-affected people. The aim is to meet their immediate needs, which will alleviate their suffering and prevent further deterioration of their situation. The support period is 4 months; transfer value is 40 USD per person per month.Activity 2: provision of 150 cash grants for agricultural activities (500 USD per household, one transfer). The aim is to increase self-sufficiency and subsistence farming capacity of the most vulnerable households in Donetsk region to cover their immediate basic needs by providing small size agricultural grants.Activity 3: trainings in agriculture to 50 vulnerable household able and willing to start agricultural activities and build a sustainable livelihood.Activity 4: monthly psycho-social support meetings (self-support groups). The aim is to improve the psychological well-being of people, strengthen communities and increase the resilience of the people. Dorcas will run protection sessions in around 5 locations, 7 meetings per location. Topics to be discussed in these groups include:1. Available Services and Access to them2. Stress Management and Depression3. Non-violent Communication4. Conflict Resolution5. Positive Parenting6. GBV prevention7. Human TraffickingAccountability to Affected populations will be reached through involving people into all stages of the project cycle though focus group discussions, surveys and a feedback mechanism.
