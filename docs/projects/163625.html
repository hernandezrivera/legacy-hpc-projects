Project Summary

Through the project WFP will provide seasonal food assistance to the most vulnerable local populations in targeted areas of the Far North regions. Assistance will be delivered through unconditional in-kind food distributions for 6 months. In-kind food assistance will support households’ access to adequate and nutritious food, therefore prevent them from adopting negative coping practices. The half ration of the in-kind distributions will provide 1,050 kcal energy per day per person. The food basket and the daily rations will comprise 175g cereals, 50g pulses; 17.5g vegetable, and 2.5g salt.
WFP food assistance in-kind will contribute to ensure food security of the households during the period of hardship. During food distribution special attention will be paid on households with people specific needs and necessary support will be provided taking into consideration of the situation of the head of the household (whether male or female or child); the presence of a person with specific needs (pregnant woman, elderly person, disabled person etc.) in the household also has an impact on food security. Regular monitoring activities will be conducted during food distribution and monthly data collected against performance indicators for reporting. During post distribution monitoring, attention will be paid on the role played by men, women, girls and boys including decision-making on food consumption and food security in the household. Targeted vulnerable local populations will be closely involved in various stages including beneficiaries targeting process, food distribution also through complaints and feedback mechanisms they could freely express their views and concerns on the project implementation. In line with WFP accountability to affected populations, beneficiaries’ complaints are treated respectfully, and their views are considered for projects adjustment. WFP together with local authorities and partners, conducts assessments to identify most vulnerable people among the local populations to be considered in the projects based on vulnerability criteria. These criteria include household size, presence of under-5 children, the elderly and handicapped, women-headed households, assets. Women and men will be equal participants, decision-makers and beneficiaries to enhance resilience. WFP ensures that women, men, girls and boys are involved in various stages of the project from conception to evaluation. For the conception of projects, beneficiaries’ opinions are regularly consulted through focus group discussions, gender and protection evaluation field missions, post distribution monitoring exercises and others. These gave beneficiaries opportunities to state their preferences for food items and transfer modalities and opinions on targeting criteria.

Besoins / Needs

The Far North Region has been experiencing increasing humanitarian crises mostly caused by Boko Haram armed groups. IDPs affected by the crisis are living with host families in various safer locations in villages or towns, putting enormous pressure on already scarce community resources and the local economy. The most affected divisions of Logone-et-Chari, Mayo Tsanaga and Mayo Sava are the most food insecure areas of the far north region. The presence of over 400,000 displaced people mainly IDPs (63%), returnees (23%) and out of camp Refugees (11%) has put more pressure on the natural resources and other economic assets of these areas. The arrival of IDPs in food-insecure areas has severely affected host communities. Boko Haram attacks have disrupted the economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in the Far North region, causing a depletion of livelihoods and household assets. Therefore, the existing chronic vulnerability and structural food insecurity have been aggravated. The region is predominantly arid and regularly hit by drought and seasonal floods. The most recent floods in October 2019 left about 500,000 hectares of cultivated farmland destroyed. The October 2019 Cadre Harmonisé highlighted that 1.3 million people in the Far North are food insecure. Out of this number, 232,934 people are severely food insecure, which means that they have limited or no access to enough, nutritious food required to live a healthy life. The Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) reports that vulnerable families in the conflict-affected areas of the Far North Region are experiencing ‘Stress’ level of acute food insecurity due to flood and pest-related crop damage, and also attacks by Boko Haram. As food insecurity affects differently men, women, girls and boys of various ages, Food assistance will prioritize the most affected local vulnerable populations. In addition, the limited access to land, resources, income-generating activities; credit, seeds have an impact on the economic situation of the household and on food security. The local vulnerable people are in a dire need of food support while there are busy with farming activities. The assistance will support households’ access to adequate and nutritious food, therefore prevent them from adopting negative coping strategies during the lean season.
