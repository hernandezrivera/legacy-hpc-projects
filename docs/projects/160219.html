Project Summary

In the framework of this project proposal, Emmanuel aims at addressing immediate winterization needs of the most vulnerable conflict-affected population living within 0-5km of the LoC, (GCA) through distribution of solid fuel and/or distribution and installation of solid-fuel/electric heaters or electric radiators.
Choice of beneficiaries: Due to high number of families unable to prepare adequately for the winter season and limited resources, Emmanuel will use an individual case management approach and apply multiple filters in selection of most vulnerable beneficiaries. Emmanuel will apply scoring system on vulnerability criteria keeping in mind prioterization criteria proposed by Shelter Cluster. At the same time, Emmanuel will make sure that its activities in the framework of this project are complementary to the assistance provided by state agencies by verifying that the potential beneficiaries are not receiving state assistance. Emmanuel seeks to facilitate participation of affected people in all its humanitarian projects by ensuring that the project design, implementation as well as monitoring and evaluation processes are informed by and reflect the views of affected people.
Procurement: Procurement in the framework of this project will be carried out by the process of tendering. All prices used for budgeting are based on preliminary market assessment of 5 various vendors of suggested goods and equipment.
Access: Emmanuel has been working in conflict-affected eastern Ukraine since the start of the conflict in 2014. All of its humanitarian activities have been implemented through 32 volunteer centers located along the line of contact in close cooperation with local state administrations. Emmanuel has previously installed 2,221 solid-fuel heaters and distributed 2,847 tons of solid fuel in partnership with “Pope for Ukraine” Initiative.
Monitoring: Emmanuel’s staff will monitor the project by implementing monitoring field trips at least 1 per month.
PROJECT ACTIVITIES:
Activity 1. Distribution of solid fuel (3,4 tons of solid fuel briquettes per HH, according to the winterization recommendations of Shelter Cluster) will be distributed to 1,600 most vulnerable households in settlements within 0-5 km to LoC (based on the letter of request from Department on Civil protection, Mobilization and Defense).
Activity 2. Distribution and installation of 12kW solid-fuel heaters for 300 most vulnerable households of Pavlopil and Novotroitske (Volnovasky Rayon) where the gas supply has been interrupted (based on the letter of request from Department on Civil protection, Mobilization and Defense and letter of request from Pavlopil Village Council). Emmanuel will recruit local brigades of construction teams to make sure that the solid fuel heaters are properly installed and ready to operate.
Activity 3. Distribution and installation of 6kW electric heaters in 600 most vulnerable households of Zolote and Hirske (Popasniansky Rayon). According to the letter of request from Luhansk Oblast State Administration, 3.368 apartments of Zolote and Hirske will be disconnected from centralized heating and will be in need of individual heating solutions for the winter 2019-2020.
Activity 4. Distribution of 2kW electric radiators among 100 most vulnerable beneficiaries living in apartment buildings of Zolote and Hirske (Popasniansky Rayon). According to the letter of request from Luhansk Oblast State Administration, 3.368 apartments of Zolote and Girske will be disconnected from centralized heating and will be in need of individual heating solutions for the winter 2019-2020). Electric radiators (2 items per household) will be distributed in cases where centralized heating is no longer available and installation of electric heaters is not possible due to technical reasons.

1 - Needs

Five years of the protracted conflict in eastern Ukraine continues to affect an estimated 5,2 millions of people of whom 3.5 million people need humanitarian assistance and protection (Ukraine Humanitarian Needs Overview 2019). The most vulnerable groups in need of humanitarian assistance are the elderly, single-headed households with low income, people with disabilities, and children. Of the 3.5 million in need, 30 per cent are above the age of 60. These men and women face severe hardships when accessing essential services because they experience higher rates of disability and immobility and are often separated from their families. They are also more susceptible to abuse and neglect, have specific health and nutritional needs, and are highly prone to economic insecurity. The lives of millions of people living in Donetska and Luhanska oblasts have had to be re-organised, placing additional burdens on already over-stretched services, such as health, education, water, electricity and heating. Most vulnerable people are increasingly forced to buy food on credit, cut health expenditures. Loss of income and lack of livelihood opportunities are the second main concern among conflict-affected communities, which is closely correlated with increased alcohol and drug use, particularly among young people. This has contributed to the increase in domestic violence. One in five elderly people living along the ‘contact line’ reported experiencing at least one type of violence and abuse, 76 per cent being women (HelpAge. Humanitarian Needs of Older Women and Men in GCA). WINTERIZATION NEEDS. Harsh winters in Ukraine continue to pose a threat to life and well-being of people living along both sides of the “contact line”. Almost 30,000 vulnerable households in both GCA and NGCA are struggling to adequately prepare for the coming winter and were identified as a priority for receiving winterization assistance by Shelter/NFI Cluster. For the 2019-2020 winter period, in GCA there is a gap of 5,531 vulnerable households (or 13,385 people) in need of winterization assistance, according to Shelter Cluster Winterization Recommendations 2019-2020. Many such households depend on coal, wood or other types of fuel for heating, which are often unavailable or costly, especially in isolated settlements. Based on this information, experience and available resources, Emmanuel plans to address the immediate winterization needs of most vulnerable conflict-affected population living within 0-5 km of LoC, GCA, aiming at meeting the distinct needs of women and men in order to ensure equal opportunities and access to assistance.
