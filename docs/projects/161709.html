9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Aiming at ensuring the implementation of monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning (MEAL) strategies, INTERSOS has developed a harmonized MEAL system, including indicators follow up, consolidated data bases, monitoring toolkit, reporting and evaluation workplan and a feedback and complaint mechanism. Under the supervision of the M&E Manager, the Accountability team is being set-up and accountability mechanisms are being put in place to further guarantee beneficiaries’ ability to share their feedback on INTERSOS projects. Beneficiaries feedback will be gathered through several sources, including face-to-face with field staff, WhatsApp, suggestion boxes, help desk, and email. Feedback received from adults and children in the community is gathered and organized by project and sector. This information is sent to the INTERSOS office in Maiduguri, where it is added to the Complaints Response Mechanism Register and referred to the most relevant person. MEAL team members follow up with the person who shared the feedback to ensure he or she is satisfied with the answer given. According to SOPs, the Action must respond to the complaints, based on the type of compliant the SOPs indicate what response is appropriate.In addition to the aforementioned set-up, the community-based approach, through active involvement of men, women, boys and girls, is at the base of the project design. Since the onset of INTERSOS action in the region, it has become well-known and respected within the targeted communities, positively engaging with both the hosting and IDP communities. INTERSOS conducts regular focus group discussions with target beneficiaries to improve its programming, taking into consideration the suggestions coming directly from the affected communities.
