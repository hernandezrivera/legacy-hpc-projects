Project Summary

The outbreak of fighting in Sudan on 15 April 2023 has caused an influx of people fleeing the country to the crisis affected South Sudan. More than 259,451 individuals have fled from Sudan into South Sudan. Nine out of ten of those arriving are South Sudanese; of those fleeing, 50 % of the new arrivals are women and girls, nearly half of the Women and girls fleeing are below the age of 18 and 4% of the Women affected by the Sudan crisis are women over 60 years (UNHCR 2023). The primary cause of movement of people, particularly women and girls, from Sudan to South Sudan is to find Safety/protection.
However, in South Sudan, sub-national and localized violence is ever-present; manifesting in clashes between State and non-State armed groups, community-based armed militias, and groups of displaced civilians (HNO 2023). Without political stability and peace, communities will continue to be affected by violence leading to multiple displacements. This Results in many community members having problems with their lands, Housing and Property. To resolve this, there is need for Awareness raising on HLP rights, Training of State and County Authorities on Citizen rights on HLP, carrying out protection monitoring and reporting, engaging communities to reconcile and build inter-communal synergies for peaceful resolution of conflict. Identification and assistance of women, men, girls, and boys with protection concerns during static and IPMT, general protection awareness-raising, giving Legal Aid for HLP cases, Conducting training on conflict resolution, mitigation, mediation, and peacebuilding, carrying out outreaches to people of concern through peacebuilding awareness-raising activities, individual, group, and specialized PSS, awareness raising outreaches on access rights and challenges and training Partners on HLP (Due diligence, and access rights and challenges). Humane Aid for Community Organization (HACO) intends to reach 7,623 individuals (50% IDPs, 30% Host and 20% returnees) in Upper Nile and Unity States.
