Project Summary

Over 100.000 persons from the Lake province in Chad have been forced to displace from islands to IDP sites, due to the insecurity caused by the exactions of the armed group Boko Haram. Oxfam has been present in the area since the beginning of the crisis in 2016 to provide humanitarian and rehabilitation aid to affected communities. In the last few months, access have just been secured to some island areas where communities have received very little humanitarian aid and where IDPs have started to return. The risk is high in food insecurity, waterborne diseases and protection incidents, due to the loss of their productive assets, the very bad 2017-18 agroclimatic conditions, the lack of WASH infrastructures and the high presence of men in arms coupled to a low level of rule of law.
The general objective of the action is to support affected communities to meet their immediate needs.
The specific objective is to focus on the needs of displaced, returnees and hosts in the sectors of food security, WASH and protection, in the departments of Fouli and Kaya.
the protection activities will cover : strengthening of community-based protection and advocacy to stakeholders in protection at community, local and regional level

Needs

Between january and september 2018, 1106 protection incidents have been reported by the cluster protection, among which on average 33% are violation if property rights, 20% gender based violence(GBV), 23% violation of physical integrity,10% violation of liberty, 8% psychological violence. Authority represent almost 1/· of perpetrators and the lack of identification papers put IDPs particularly at risk from illegal taxation. Intra community conflicts are increased by displacement, espacially access to land and GBV.Young men regularly return to the islands for fishing, which exposes them to protection risks( illegal taxation, killings, abduction).This situation also favors emigration and radicalization of young men as well as the increase domestic violence. Approximately 20% of Households are led by women(migration or killing of men) which exposes them to higher food insecurity( no access to ressources, gender discriminations, illiteracy).Highest needs are sensitization of communities and authorities a better access to idnetification papers, and holistic support to survivors( complaint, referencing, legal and medical assistance, follow-up etc..)

Activities or outputs

Distribution of security kits to women and girls ( solar torches that will facilitate and make safer their movements on site during the night time( especially wash needs)- Support to the registration of births among IDPs( to facilitate the delivery of identification papers)- Support to GBV survivors and to people leaving with HIV( IGA, Cash grant, support to access legal and medical assistance)- Mass sensitization campaign on protection( what are protection risks, how to prevent them, how to refer cases and how to assist survivors, diffusion in community and media, advocacy tools)- Support to community protection committees(CPC)( structures put in place on IDPs sites to reduce intra community conflicts exacerbated by displacement anf prevent protection risks and GBV)- Coordination of local stakeholders of the protection sector ( organization of round tables and meeting with representants of communities( especially Women and young persons, on protection, existing prevention and support mechanism and monitoring- Study in the impact of humanitarian aid on protection issues( how does it contribute to improving or worsening protection issues at the Households, community and regional level- About Mainstreaming Protection , those activities are planned: - Analysis of Protection risks linked to project activities: before implementing each activity, potential protection risks will be analysed and mitigated (e-g-will cash distribution creates domestic violence and how to reduce this risk)- Identification of ethnic minorities at risk so that their specific needs are taken into account - Encouraging different voices (female, male, youth, elderly, minorities) in focus groups and shared discussion spaces in deciding activity implementation modalities- Increasing communities´ knowledge of their own rights and the accountability of NGOs- Strenghtening the ratio of female staff, especially when hiring animators and daily workers, and training staff on safeguarding and Safeprogramming( mainstreaming protection)
