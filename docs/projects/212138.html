Project Summary

HEKS/EPER Strategy in Syria focuses on three basic needs: Water, bread and work. HEKS/EPER prioritizes strengthening the work of the pre-existing structures without substituting them. Therefore, HEKS/EPER mainly implements infrastructure support to ensure sustainable impact and restore the initial standards of living.
Under this project, HEKS/EPER is planning to rehabilitate the following infrastructure to restore access to basic water services for around 367,186 PoCs across six governorates as follows:
A) Aleppo:
1- In Aleppo city: Sewage networks in Midan and Hamidiyeh
2- In A’zaz, Tall Refaat sub-district:
a) Sewage network in Tall Refaat,
b) Two Pumping stations and high water tank in Sheikh Issa village,
c) Karmin Pumping station rehabilitation,
d) Water and sewage networks in Dier Jmal,
3- In Menbij, Maskana: Sewage network,
4- In Al-Bab, Dayr Hafir: sewage network,
5- In As-Safira, a sewage network in Tal Aran and a pumping station in Kafr Hamra.
B) Hama:
1- In Hama city:
a) Two sewage networks in Hawarneh and Frayeh neighborhoods,
b) Borehole in Sawaek neighborhood,
c) Water main pipe in Al-Fayha & Al-Qussor,
2- In Suran:
a) Elevated water tank,
b) Elevated water tank, in Tiba Elemam,
c) Solarization of a borehole in Murak,
3- In As-Salamiyeh:
a) Sewage network in As- Salamiyeh town,
b) In As-Saan, sewage network and solarization of water treatment plant,
c) Rehabilitation of Tal Khazneh High Water Tank,
C) Rural Damascus: in Yabroud, Jobbeh, Sewage treatment plant (green, using plants),
D) In Damascus, Sewage network in Tadamon,
E) In Lattakia, Salanfa, two water networks in Balata and Khamila, and in Qalmun, Water network,
F) In Tartous,
a) Tanita, Sewage network rehabilitation in Tanita
b) Tartous, Water network rehabilitation in Tartous City
The total estimated cost for the above activities is around 2,937,530$ while the total project cost is 4,024,014$. HEKS/EPER secured around 1,700,000$.

Project Assumptions: 1) Stable security situation 2) Consistent humanitarian access 3) No mass movements of populations 4) Availability of material in the local market 5) Acceptance of community.
Based on its previous experience and context awareness, HEKS/EPER has identified the following risks and prepared the adequate mitigation measures:
Operation:
Risks: 1- Delays in obtaining needed approvals for assessment/implementation - Mitigation: keep close coordination with the line ministries at different levels to speed up the processes
2- Lack of capacity of authorities to operate or manage the infrastructure - Mitigation: provisioning of technical training, spare parts, and tools for public staff
Protection:
1) Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials- Mitigation: Coordination with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities.
2) Safety incidents during or after the execution of the works – Mitigation: 1- Install caution signs around the work sites 2- Contractors sign HEKS/EPER’s safety measures. 3- Install railings and fire extinguishers in the infrastructure
3) SEA risks: Due to the nature of the planned activities (Infrastructure works), the interactions with beneficiaries are almost entirely nonexistent and thus the risks related to Sexual Exploitation and Abuse are almost totally avoided, nevertheless the HEKS/EPER staff are trained and obliged to adhere to the code of conduct & safeguarding principles.
