Project Summary

This project aims to address the emergency and urgent needs of vulnerable populations in the northwest of Syria, to response and support the GBV risks in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates. Through WGSS and ambulance units center in Aleppo and Idleb Governorates, Azaz, Harim, Idleb and Jisr-Ash-shougur districts, Azaz, Dana Armanaz, Harim, Salqin, Bennsh, Idleb maart Tamsrin, and Badama sub-districts; GBV services are to be provided to community members in selected locations, as well as referral services through the ambulance system that is functioning in selected locations in Aleppo and Idleb governorate. To strengthen its response to the complex and multispectral needs of survivors. The GBV intervention in this project aims to "Survivors of GBV and individuals at risk of GBV have increased access to safe and confidential GBV services through 4 WGSS. The GBV staff in will target women, girls in particular, including women and girls who live with disabilities, and the elderly women.
Violet will ensure to conduct a risk assessment and analysis to understand the safety and accessibility of the services and develop clear and targeted recommendations and action points while establishing GBV services. Also, Violet will strengthen privacy and confidentiality for all women who come for health services through infrastructure improvements. Violet will use GBV Case Management Standard Operating Procedures including GBV CM tools, Data Protection Protocols, and update the GBV service map that is available by the GBV Sub-Cluster in NWS and update the relevant referral pathways for multi-sectoral support in the operating area. Violet will do refresher training on the already existing protocol on CMR and ensure compliance with it, for Doctors and Midwifes and other humanitarian workers are already existing in the targeted location. Also, ensure/improve educational materials available to women and girls on topics related to GBV in the WGSS and other facilities. Violet will collaborate with the GBV SC to participate in the case management coordination efforts to benefit from inter-agency capacity development opportunities and already developed tools related to case management and data management.
3500 women, girls, and men will be targeted by Violet GBV intervention within 4 WGSS, starting from training non-GBV staff on GBV core concepts and safety referral. Whereas the GBV staff will be trained on Community-based Protection, PSEA & CSG, and PFA, and in-depth cases management to GBV cases workers and PSS counsellors, targeting a total of 60 staff members. GBV Case Workers will provide specialized GBV services to survivors and the at-risk cases based on survivor centered GBV Case Management to a total of 450 women and
girls' cases.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Violet MHPSS counselors will provide cultural and age-specific individual and group mental health and psychosocial support sessions targeting GBV survivors and at-risk cases in a total of 2500 women and girls. Violet YMC facilitators will provide it and sessions to women and girls, targeting 350 women and girls. Moreover, 120 women and community committee members will be trained on GBV basics, Safety Referrals, and Enhancing the community acceptance and support for GBV issues and needs, in addition to the response to more than 150 survivors by CVA and small grant opportunities through empowerment sessions and business skills training and coaching. The priorities are for the high-risk cases and based on the type of violence and the roots of the problem to ensure ending or reducing the violence against women and girls' survivors and/or at-risk cases. The GBV survivors who live with disabilities and elderlies will also be included in the priorities.
