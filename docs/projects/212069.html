Project Summary


This proposal aims to target the the most vulnerable families of returnees, host communities and displaced through provision of summarization NFIs in Deir Ez Zor city and its countryside, Abu Kamal city and its country side and in Al Mayadin town and its country side, with special focus on vulnerable groups, youth, women headed households and people with disabilities, who have lost their livelihood means and sources, and who do not have any stable income to properly survive due to lack of work opportunities, thereby increasing their reliance on host communities support to survive, and as they do not have access to humanitarian lifesaving assistance and services provided by the limited number of humanitarian actors present in the area of intervention and its operational capacity which is not commensurate to meet the scale and scope of the crisis and its impacts on affected families in Deir Ez Zor governorate. And due to these negative impacts of the crisis on all sectors, combined with deteriorating underlying factors such as water, sanitation and heath care services, electricity supply and food security, BICA, through the medical teams and the assessment teams and its volunteers has conducted several home visits to majority of these families at their residence areas where the initial assessment at the targeted geographic areas estimates, there are more than 6,400 of families of 38,400 people are in urgent needs of humanitarian assistances.
As the targeted families are now experiencing the worst summer, the proposal recommends providing summer NFIs to alleviate the suffering of these vulnerable groups in summer as the climate of DZ is characterized by severe dry weather and high temperature in summer which reaches more than 45, and lasts more than six months, and as the project proposal was initially approved last year by the NFI sector, it is expected the distribution operation of the assistance items to the targeted beneficiaries will be launched by April 2023.
Moreover, As of the Electricity Company, the Governor office, SARC and DoSA as well local communities, Deir Ez Zor city and its countryside currently suffering of shortage of the electricity supply, in which the rate of feeding of electricity supply does not exceed 2% of the total of electricity produced at Syria level. This situation has affected negatively on living conditions and wellbeing of the poor, sick, people with disabilities. And despite these summarization and essential core NFIs are available at the local markets but these families unable to get it for its high prices, as a result to the depreciation of the Syrian pound against the US$ where the exchange rate of the US$ against Syrian pound has exceeded SP 3,500 in the local blanket markets, leading to more declining of purchasing power of the crisis affected families, in the face of skyrocketing prices of the basic living commodities including NFIs.
This project proposal has been designed in consultation and coordination with the humanitarian actors in the targeted areas, concerned line ministries especially DoSAL and SARC, UN agency especially with HCR, Local and international NGOs, NFIs sector, as well, communities and beneficiaries where effective beneficiary and community participation is practically indispensable to render a project successful where beneficiaries were involved in information sharing, consultation, decision making, and initiating action in the program activities. To reach this level of coordination, the assessment teams conducted several meeting with the representatives of beneficiaries and targeted communities, then their views are taken into account and consequently is explicitly incorporated in their objectives, and approach and methodology, related to their urgent needs, plan of action and the response to their NFIs needs.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

It the proposal outlines a comprehensive plan to provide humanitarian assistance, specifically Non-Food Items (NFIs), to vulnerable families in Deir Ez Zor city and its surrounding areas. The focus is on supporting various groups such as youth, women-headed households, and people with disabilities who have been impacted by the crisis in the region. The proposal highlights the urgent need for assistance due to the lack of stable income and limited access to essential services. The project aims to distribute summer NFIs to alleviate the suffering of these vulnerable groups, especially considering the extreme climate conditions in the region. The distribution operation is expected to be launched by April 2022. The shortage of electricity supply in the area is also mentioned as a significant challenge affecting the well-being of the population, particularly the vulnerable groups. The proposal emphasizes the importance of coordination with various stakeholders, including humanitarian actors, local authorities, and communities, to ensure the success of the project. Beneficiary and community participation is highlighted as a crucial aspect of the project design, with efforts made to incorporate their views and needs into the planning and implementation process.Additionally, the proposal acknowledges the impact of the depreciation of the Syrian pound against the US dollar on the purchasing power of crisis-affected families. The skyrocketing prices of basic living commodities, including NFIs, have further exacerbated the challenges faced by vulnerable groups in the region. The project proposal has been developed in consultation and collaboration with various stakeholders to ensure a coordinated and effective response. This includes engagement with local and inteThe assessment team conducted field trips to targeted intervention locations in DZ countryside, which identified by HNO 2022 and paid visits to the residence of potential beneficiaries; poor, female headed families, PWD and families who lost their livelihoods, The assessment showed that there are 6,400 HHs/38,400 persons out of population, meet the criteria and in need to the following total summer items of NFIs: 1. Solar Lamp: 6,400 ones (a lamp/HH). 2. Rechargeable Fan: 6,400 pieces (a fan/HH). These items to be distributed to intervention areas: - DZ city and its Eastern (Khasham, Hatla, Marat, Mathloum, Al Huseinieh and Al Tabieh) and western rural area (Al Bghelieh, Ayyash, Al Shmetieh, Al Msarab and Al Tebni) : 900 solar lamps and 900 rechargeable fans benefitting 900 HHs (5,400 people). - Abu Kamal town and its rural area (Jalaa, Sabkha, Tothiyeh, Qataa, Abbas and Salhiyeh): 2,600 solar lamps & 2,600 fans benefitting 2,600 HHs of 15,600 people. - Al Mayadin town and rural area (Sbekhan, Mehkan, Al Ashara): 2,900 solar lamps & 2,900 fans benefiting 2,900 HHs of 17,400. Needs assessment sources: assessment teams of the Association and secondary sources (SARC, DoSA, local municipalities).
