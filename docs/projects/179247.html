Project Summary

The project seeks to ensure the continuity and increase the coverage of life-saving nutritional services, in high needs and remote locations through activating Rapid Response Teams (RRTs), who will target areas of low coverage or have very high rates of acute malnutrition and therefore require urgent scale-up.
Those teams will introduce integrated nutrition, protection, health and WASH services to both host and IDPs, where two teams cover one of the following five locations: Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Badama, Harim, Marret Masrin and Salqin sub-districts in Idleb, in addition to Qabasin in Al-bab sub-district in Aleppo.
Each team, consisting of a nutritionist, a nurse, and 2 or 3 community health workers, in addition to data entry.

4 - Needs

Based on the nutrition cluster assessment, there are many sub-districts and communities either poor or have a medium coverage from the rapid response teams RRT. SEMA in partnership with UNICEF is supporting RRTs in these locations but still needs to initiate more teams to integrate the work in Jisr-Ash-shugur, Badama, Harim, Salqin, and Qbasen in Al-Bab district. The mentioned areas received huge waves of IDPs in the first quarter of 2020. The total population in these locations is 394847, where the total number of PLWs in need is 40305 and the total number of CU5 in need is 78042. Furthermore, about 11706 from the children between 6 and 59 months have disabilities.SEMA conducted a rapid needs assessment RNA in April 2020, which showed that 65% of the CU5 are feeding exclusively by breastfeeding and the rest of 45% are feeding either through milk or milk with breastfeeding. About 17.5% of the mothers provided other supplements with their milk to the infants. Besides, 84.44% of the mothers did not receive any IYCF messages or consultations. 12.61% of the lactating mothers do not have the needed privacy during the breastfeeding, hence, SEMA will distribute the breast covers. Over 69% did not receive any community health services through the CH teams.According to the RNA, there was a gap in the humanitarian aid in many different locations in Idleb due to the rapid increase in the number of IDPs who fled from the conflict areas also, due to the significant decrease in the Syrian Lira value, which contributed to the increase in the levels of poverty either among the IDPs or the host communities. Poverty has led to a surge in the number of malnourished cases in the last period.

5 - Implementation of activities

Each team of the RRTs will provide the following activities:Nutrition: Screen Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) and Children under 5 (CU5) to detect and refer the malnourished cases to health facilities, provide IYCF-E counselling for mothers with moderately malnourished children, distribute micronutrients for preventing of anemia and malnutrition among children, pregnant and lactating women, disseminate oral messages on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, and monitor distribution and use of breast milk substitutes, support health/nutrition inclusive education and promotion refer the needed cases and follow up the referred cases. Besides, Breast covers will be distributed to the PLWs to encourage them feeling more privacy and to raise the awareness about the importance of exclusive breastfeeding even in the camps.Also, the RRT will roll out a family MUAC approach through training the caregivers to measure the MUAC of their children, in addition to strengthen household follow ups for SAM and MAM cases. All the RRT will be trained to take the right precautions during the work and the project will cover the PPE for the staff and beneficiaries (masks, gloves, sterilizers, big board for explaining the awareness messages).Protection: identify and refer the cases exposed to abuse or violence via the visual signs on the children under five and PLWs’ bodies, where the discovered cases will be referred to the child protection CP or GBV case management teams in coordination with CP specialist in SEMA and field teams on the ground according to CP and GVB service map.Health: Detect and refer CU5 and PLWs with danger signs and Medical problems according to guidance developed in coordination with the nutrition cluster.
