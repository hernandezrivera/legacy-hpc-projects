Project Summary

This project will contribute to preventing and respond to the S/GVB in Diffa region through case management to girls and young adolescents survivors, strenthinning of community based mechanisms, reiforcing local services providers to promore quality care to survivors including technical and logistic support to community based mechanims. It will an other part focus on improving services provived to children survivors of sexual violence by mapping what services are provided in each areas through mobile and static approaches to ensure reachable and hard to reach areas are covered. Survivors of abuses, will be provided integration support and playschool support while ensuring positive parents skills to both care givers and foster families. targets will be girls, boys, young women DPs, refugees and returnees. Though mobile and static CFS and safe places to provide adequate PSS/PFA. A coordination with other actors from sub-regional and community levels will be strengthened to promote efficient referrals.

Needs

In Diffa Region, according to the last figure review for population affected, 50 % of them are mainly girls, adolescents and young persons and in need of protection services. S/GVB working group, protection monitoring actors have reported that in the last 6 months, most of incidents recorded are mainly gender based violence in areas like Tomour, Nguigmi, Ngurti, etc. According to Plan International research report in the " Adolescents in the Crisis of Lake Chad", girls are highly exposed to all forms of gender based violence such sexual violence, denial to access opportunities, early/ and forced marriage and others. In those locations, mostly in the hard to reach locations due to security or distance, actors are not present and protection services are not available or others are not of quality. Community based protection structures are almost absent and those available lack resources to prevent S/GBV. Other girl mothers have developed negative mechanism such sex for living since no reintegration support after being abused. Local service providers such the health centers, the foster parents, the police,etc need to be more supported on providing quality services.
