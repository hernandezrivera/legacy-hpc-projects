Project Summary

BINAA will establish of inter-cluster advisory group includes representatives of CCCM, SNFI, WASH, ERL, and Logistic clusters, representatives of the NGOs operating in Idleb, and BINAA focal persons. The group's mission is to report emergencies requiring a response from the RRT, in addition to coordinate to avoid overlapping. This group will continue to conduct assessments include technical details for the potential locations of damages, in addition to the database of people could not reach out in each location throughout Idleb governorate to determine the places most exposed to winter and flood damage. Based on findings, BINAA RRT will cover these needs ensuring rapid response for residents in Idleb governorate to emergency maintenance work for both roads leading to camps and communities.
Rapid response mechanism training will be conducted for each sector, including project supervisors, engineers, contractors, and local community representatives, to ensure clear understanding and prompt response to emergencies.
The project will observe the principle of “Do No Harm” through monitoring the markets, high coordination with project stakeholders and providing opportunities for the participation of host communities in the project design and activities and coordinate with ER cluster other NGOs to avoid duplication/overlap.
If COVID-19 risks persist prevention procedures will be taken according to Cluster guidelines BINAA will also consider preserving the environ

4 - Needs

BINAA carried out Need Assessment for IDPs sites between 16 March and 09 of April 2021 , 10 data collectors (5 females – 5 males) had been worked and trained for conducting the need assessments over targeted 100 IDPs sites . data collection methodology was used In-depth interview with KIIs, a total of 100 KIIs interviews including people with disability had been conducted, the main findings were found that all KIs reported that the IDPs facing an access issues during the rainy season due to bad situation for main roads to the nearest communities, and this bad situation prevent pass to nearest communities and camps. KIs reported that 30% of assessed roads are graveled in bad condition, 22% still dirt roads and need to be rehabilitated, 14% are asphalted and need a heavy rehabilitation, 15% are asphalted and need a light rehabilitation.
