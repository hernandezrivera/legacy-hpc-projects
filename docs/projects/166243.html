Project Summary

The Government of Zimbabwe announced on 17. March 2020 the early closure of schools on 24. March 2020 due to the Coronavirus outbreak. Due to the uncertainty around the development of the coronavirus transmission, the Ministry of Primary and secondary Education (MoPSE) is envisaging opening of schools in a phased approach starting end of June 2020, the reason being more time is required for logistical purposes and planning so that schools ensure safe return to education. According to UNESCO, Over 90% of enrolled learners (1·5 million young people) worldwide are now out of education. MoPSE recognises that one of the major specific challenge posed by Covid 19 in Zimbabwe which will threaten safe back to school is unavailability and inadequate water and sanitation facilities at schools especially in remote parts of some districts including satellite schools.
To bridge the identified gap CARE will undertake the following critical WASH activities so as to reduce the transmission rates and flatten the curve amongst learners and teachers in Bikita and Gutu Districts. CARE will provide clean water, for drinking and hand washing, sanitation facilities and supplies (handwashing soap, sanitary wear and toilets) and personal hygiene education and capacity building of existing WASH structures at school level (SHCs, School health coordinators, water point committees and activation of school emergency plans. If WASH activities at school level are not addressed to support the safe return to quality learning for teachers and learners there is high possibility of increased transmission rates, risk of drop out and psychosocial stress, anxiety and stigma resulting from coronavirus crisis negatively affecting teachers and students hence the proposed project will come in to complement MoPSE initiatives.
The initiatives will target 20,000 people (learners (boys and girls) and teachers) in Bikita and Gutu Districts. The initiative will be implemented from July 2020-December 2020. A full team of staff with expertise including project management, WASH, monitoring, evaluation and education will be coordinating and implementing activities.
As the project is proposing to conduct WASH activities in schools, that may be taken care of by the WASH cluster. The project will deliberately target districts with no Wash In Schools (WINs) interventions supported by the WASH cluster and also will prioritise disadvantaged schools which are usually located in remote parts of the targeted districts focusing on satellite schools with limited or no WASH infrastructure development at the moment so as to increase the number of education institutions ready to bring back learners to education safely.

1 - Activities

The response seeks to build the resilience of communities through the coronavirus preparedness and response activities, for example through the establishment of school emergency plans, the dissemination of health messages and the upgrading of WASH facilities and interventions for all learners to access. The following activities spelled out in the cluster strategy to complement the HRP will be will be prioritised: Provision of safe and adequate WASH facilities ( boreholes, latrines) Capacity building of water point committees, school health clubs,  Provision of adequate WASH supplies (i.e, soap, sanitizers) Establishment ,strengthen and activation of school emergency plans

2 - Indicators

The project will be measuring the following indicators• Number of schools with repaired or rehabilitated water points• Number of schools provided with access to safe and adequate WASH facilities(Sanitation facilities and Handwashing facilities)• Number of schools provided with adequate WASH supplies (Sanitisers,soap)• Number of schools with established school emergency plans • Number of boys and girls provided with hygiene kits• Number of established school health clubs Thes indicators will help in monitoring the progress of the project and ultimately its success in meeting the objectives.
