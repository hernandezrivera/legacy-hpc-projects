Project Summary

The project is going to provide response on protracted and complicated crisis in the East of Ukraine. It requires complex approach in several sectors connecting both immediate response and stabilization approaches. Based on needs assessments, the intervention will secure acute needs of beneﬁciaries in hygiene and health services as well as improve sanitation conditions for elderly people. Project will provide assistance to 416 elderly people (188 men and 228 women) living in rural area 0-5 km along the contact line, GCA. This total number includes 96 laying elderly people who need home care, 320 elderly people with disabilities who need access to essential infrastructure however 400 of those 416 people reached will be assisted with hygiene kits. Hygiene kits modality will be effectuated in a sensitive manner with regard to age/disability status. Two different set of kits will be distributed to beneﬁciaries who can walk and to those who are laying with severely restricted mobility To stabilize coverage of basic needs of the chosen target group, even after project ends, the intervention is based on local markets and community activation. Beneﬁciaries will obtain access to essential infrastructure in the nearest towns due to transportation services to be implemented by the project. 12 local activists will provide beneﬁciaries with home care and 4 activists (1 per a location) will support them with transportation opportunities.. Local authorities will provide activists with a cabinet and Caritas will equip them with oﬃce equipment and supplies. Project undertakes professional training for local communities to provide highly needed services for elder people. Project starts on the 1st of January 2020, and has the duration of 12 months. The project will be fulﬁlled under coordination and support of national oﬃce (Caritas Ukraine) by 4 local Caritas Centers. Caritas Mariupol will operate in Novohnativka (Volnovakhskiy rayon). Caritas Zaporizhya will operate in Novobakhmutivka ( Yasinuvatskiy rayon), Caritas Kramatorsk will operate in Luhanske (Bahmutskiy rayon), Caritas Severodonetsk will operate in Zolote4 (Popasnyanskiy rayon). Selection of the locations is based on preliminary assessment. The intervention is coordinated with Help Age and Adra as well as Health and WASH clusters. The indicators are divided mostly equally between 4 local teams who are experienced in operating along contact line. The project will have complementary approach with other Caritas humanitarian projects. It means that project beneﬁciaries will have opportunity to be assisted with heating materials for winter season, medical cash/vouchers assistance, multi-purpose cash assistance, water supply, psycho-social support. Complex assistance to targeted beneﬁciaries will be both in immediate humanitarian support and protection assistance The project intervention is align with HRP collective outcomes and clusters objectives as well as with Grand Bagrain goals and UHF strategic objectives. Based on new edition of Sphere Standards, project will go align with Core Humanitarian Principles, commitments and technical standards. Protection of beneﬁciaries will be ensured by incorporation of accountability mechanism, safeguarding measures preventing abuse or conﬂicts, addressing assistance, community based approach with attention to gender and speciﬁc needs of beneﬁciaries. First month of the project is dedicated to preparation (procurement and supply, selection of activists and creation their capacity). The last month of the project is dedicated to ﬁnal evaluation, lessons learned and improvement of program strategy.

1 - Needs

Intervention is based both as on general assessments and detailed assessments of multisectoral needs of the elderly people in the settlements of intervention (0-5 km, GCA). Protection and access to health care services are of particular concern because 30% of those affected are older people (60 years old and over), and 60% are women and children. Persons with disabilities and GBV survivors are also particularly affected by lack of access to health services. According to HelpAge International, 97% of older people have at least one chronic disease and over half (53%) require assistive devices . The health system in the conﬂict-affected areas of Donetsk and Luhansk regions faces several distinct patterns of disruption. Approximately 38% of households in the 20 km area along the contact line and 57% of households in the 5 km area along the ‘contact line’ reported having problems accessing healthcare. The cost of medicines, distance to health care facilities, and the cost of travel were the most reported diﬃculties. (EXPLORING ACCESS TO HEALTH CARE SERVICES IN UKRAINE, July 2019). Despite increase of pensions during in 2017-2018, elderly and unemployed people could represent the socioeconomic group at highest risk. In particular elderly people living alone and those living in couple are among the most vulnerable, as they cannot share expenditure with other members of HH. (WFP: Socio-economic and market overview - 2018) According to the 2018 Food Security andLivelihoods trend analysis, between 2013 and 2015, the percentage of the population living with income below the actual minimum subsistence level increased from 20% in 2013 to 74% in 2015 in Luhansk oblast and from 22% to 66% in Donetsk oblast. Rural households in both the 0-5km area and the 5-20km area GCA had lower incomes than urban households. Households in the 5-20km area had overall higher incomes than households in the 0-5km area. The majority of respondents that reported facing additional problems accessing markets in the winter indicated the barrier to be higher prices in the market (63%) followed by weather conditions affecting travel to markets (43%) (REACH: Economic Security Assessment, Government-controlled areas, March, 2019). Only 22% of respondents mentioned that they have stable waste disposal service. 79% of settlements researched have no access to quality drinking water. (Caritas Ukraine: NEEDS ASSESSMENT OF THE WATER, SANITATION AND HYGIENE SUPPLY , March 2019) Caritas has provided fast evaluation of situation in different settlements in 0-5 zone, GCA in the 1st week of August, 2019. The data was collected by local teams by phone. The key respondents were local authorities, volunteers, workers of local health facilities. The situation was analyzed in 13 settlements. There were selected 4 main rural settlements for project intervention and additional 4 small nearby villages. They are Katerinivka with nearby Rodyna-4 in Zolote, Popasnyanskiy rayon, Luhanska oblast (713 residents), Luhanske in Bahmutskiy rayon, Donetska oblast (2562 residents), Oleksandropil with nearby villages Panteleymonivka, Novobahmutivka, Novoselivka in Yasynuvatskiy rayon (1845 residents), Chermalyk and Novohnativka in Volnovahskiy rayon (1603 residents). The overall population in the settlements 6720 residents, 57% females and 43% males. Elderly people (60+) group is 15-20%. Among them, there are 10-15% of elderly people have disabilities and serious diseases. Nearly 15% of hard severely elderly people are lonely and 5-7% are laying. The main acute needs of the most severe elderly people are: absence of life-saving medical services, medicines, specialized tools. - absence of needed hygiene and sanitation measures, drinking water - absence of needed nutrition The main barriers are high prices and inability to access necessary markets and facilities. Local health facilities, communal agencies have also limited capacities both in access and appropriateness of response.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Health and Nutririon Objective - Elderly people with disabilities will improve their access to quality and affordable services by obtaining individual home care and transportation services to essential infrastructure, especially health facilities. As project targets also the most vulnerable laying elderly people, they will have opportunity to obtain medical services at home from doctors transported to the settlement. Outcome 1 - Most vulnerable elderly men and women with disabilities have improved their physical health condition during 12 months. With regard to that, starting from 2017 Caritas Ukraine has been using SMART indicator which means that iin the end of the project, at least 50% of beneficiaries will improve their physical health condition. Indicator is rendered by base line/end line surveys. Output 1.1 - Beneﬁciaries have obtained regular home care assistance from local trained activists. Activities - Provision of at least 2 times per week individual home care services to 96 elderly people with disabilities during 8 months by 12 trained local activists. Output 1.2 - Beneﬁciaries have obtained at least 12 transportation services to access essential infrastructure related especially to specialized health facilities during 12 months. Activities - Provision of 10 return transportation services(hereinafter - RTS) to essential infrastructure per month in 4 project locations during 12 months by local service providers. Besides laying persons (96), every location hosts 80 elderly people who can walk. Providing 10 RTSs monthly in every location, on average 8 persons per 1 RTS, assures that every person from 320 targeted will visit essential infrastructure at least 1 time per month. Caritas will also look at surrounding hard to reach villages and will try to assist ﬂexibly with additional transport if beneﬁciaries are identiﬁed living there or if a need becomes apparent nearby. Caritas will try to ﬁll vehicles: if there is any space remaining then Caritas will allow other vulnerable groups to use the transport, for example parents of small children. Output 1.3 - Beneﬁciaries have obtained medical services from doctors transported at least 1 time per month to project locations. Activities - Provision of 5 whole day travels per 1 month for doctors to 4 project locations during 12 months to immobile elderly people. 4-6 lying persons in every location will be visited by doctor during 1 whole day travel. To laverage the option, other vulnerable groups (single-parent families, with infectious deseases, etc.) will also have opportunity to access specialized health services from transported doctors in the spare time of their visit. All others, who can walk, will be effectively covered through RTS option as described in activities of Output 1.2, filling the spare seats and places in the vehicles. WASH Objective - Elderly people with disabilities will cover their immediate needs in hygiene with in-kind modality assistance as the most appropriate one for such group. The most vulnerable population of the settlements will beneﬁt in sanitation and health conditions from local activists specially equipped for provision of services related to WASH. Outcome 1 - Most vulnerable men and women with disabilities have improved their living conditions in sanitary and hygiene during 12 months. Output 1.1 - Beneﬁciaries have obtained 1 hygiene packages for 3 months period 4 times. Activities - Distribution of 1600 hygiene kits to 400 elderly people with disabilities within period of 6 months. Overall number of beneﬁciaries includes 96 lying sick people and 304 elderly people who can walk. Hygiene kits modality will be age/disability - sensitive. Due to that, there will be 2 different types of kits available with different set of items. One type is for persons that can walk, another is more speciﬁc, for mostly immmobile, lying beneﬁciaries.
