Project Summary

The project aims to address the MoH and East Jerusalem Hospital Network (EJHN) needs in responding to COVID-19. MAP is continuously coordinating with MoH and EJHN regarding their needs during emergencies including the clashes that started in April 2021 in West Bank and Jerusalem as well as the COVID19 pandemic.
Through this project, MAP will procure and deliver medical supplies and consumables according to MoH and EJHN needs focusing on the needs of the groups most affected by COVID-19.

1 - Needs

In the West Bank, the threat of COVID-19 has presented monumental challenges due to inadequate clean water/hygiene facilities and vulnerable populations. The occupation, displacement and funding limitations have left the health system woefully unprepared for the emergency. With the recent surge in case numbers, health facilities lack the infrastructure, skilled workforce, essential equipment, drugs and supplies needed to manage the pandemic.According to the COVID-19 Situation Report issued by WHO on 07 October 2021, the number of active COVID-19 cases was 5,441, as there was a significant rise in cases between July and September of 2021, despite the increase in number of people vaccinated. This is due to the new varieties, which led the MoH in September 2021 to warn against a fourth wave of the disease. MAP started responding to COVID-19 in 2020. Initially, we focused on the procurement of hygiene kits, antiseptics, disinfectants and personal protective equipment (PPE) for frontline health workers, aiming to control transmission of the virus and protect those health workers and the patients they cared for. This remains a hugely important component of the COVID-19 response but, as transmission and hospitalization increased , we expanded our focus providing CPAP machines and training MOH staff on their use.MAP continues to work closely with MoH and EJHN to be always updated on their needs in responding to COVID-19 cases. During developing the list of items to be procured and their specifications, MAP will ensure to prioritize the requirements to the needs of most vulnerable groups particularly people with disability. People with disability have limited access to vaccination and awareness towards COVID-19 which puts them at higher risk.

2 - Activities

MAP has extensive experience in emergency procurement supporting the MOH and EJHN as it has implemented several emergency response interventions in 2020 and 2021. Interventions included supporting the health system with their consumables needs to cope with the pandemic. MAP has developed a good network of suppliers to provide medical supplies in a timely manner. Outcome 1: MoH and EJHN have access to urgently needed drugs, disposables and lab reagents needed for COVID-19 case management. Output 1: Needed medical supplies and disposables are procured and delivered according to MoH and EJHN requirements. Activities: • Develop lists of needed medical supplies and disposables in coordination with MoH and EJHN. • Initiate tendering process and contact suppliers. • Analysis of offers in collaboration with MoH and EJHN. • Sign contracts with selected suppliers. • Deliver items to agreed locations.• Conduct interviews, case studies with photographs.

9.1c - How will feedback be used?

MAP will document and follow up on all received feedback. MAP will address any complaints related to the project implementation, quality or quantity of supplies. 9.1a - Specify the mechanisms for feedback and/or complaints

e - Field visit,f - Other

9.1b - If 'Other', please specify :

Upon the delivery of items, MAP will meet with the teams of MoH and EJHN teams to get their feedback on the project and procurement process. MAP will also provide health workers with emails and phone numbers to reach out for any feedback.

9.1d - Explain how you have ensured that mechanism are accessible to all population groups?

For this project, MAP will collect feedback directly from health workers through different channels. These different channels ensure confidentiality and security of information. Moreover, they allow health workers to reach MAP through verbal and written feedback, and allow for feedback to be in-person or anonymous. MAP will follow up on indicators measuring the numbers of each group benefiting from the provided supplies. Since different age groups are affected differently by COVID-19, MAP will make sure that procured items respond to the needs of particularly vulnerable groups including pregnant women, young children and older adults.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

MAP conducts due diligence of suppliers before contracting them and audits all supporting documents before transferring payment. During the tendering process, MAP strictly follows its procurement manual and ensures that all suppliers are committed to it, and excludes any ineligible applicants.MAP staff has received training on safeguarding and shares it’s safeguarding policy and code of conduct with all partners and suppliers as relevant. MAP’s partners understand that any actions taken that could result in a breach of any of the provisions of this policy may lead to an immediate termination of the agreement. MAP and the Partner will take all measures possible to ensure that all activities are always conducted according to MAP’s Safeguarding Policy and to ensure the safety and protection of children and vulnerable adults participating either directly or indirectly in the project. MAP will work with the partners to ensure that:1. the partners safeguarding policy, code of conduct, investigation procedures and beneficiary feedback/complaints mechanisms are consistent with global and regional guidelines/best practice.2. All the partners’ staff, trustees and volunteers sign the code of conduct in relation to safeguarding beneficiaries and fully understand their responsibilities within the code.3. All the partners’ staff, trustees and volunteers are provided with suitable safeguarding training 4. All services provided for beneficiaries are assessed for the potential risk to those beneficiaries, with review of the risks/preventive action incorporated within the project’s action and monitoring plans.5. There is a shared understanding of what safeguarding concerns need to be reported to MAP immediately: a. Any allegation or disclosure of abuse that concerns a beneficiary.b. Any allegation of abuse made against a staff member, trustee or volunteer.c. Any observed or reported breach of the safeguarding code of conduct.d. Any other safeguarding concern they may have in relation to our project/beneficiaries.6. There is close collaboration between the partners and MAP in any safeguarding investigation.

11. - Equal and impartial access to aid
11.1 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The support and supplies will be provided to MoH and EJHN which serve all patients according to need. MAP commits to its code of conduct and the principles of humanitarian interventions including equality, impartiality and independence. MAP will ensure that all community groups are involved in the project and receiving services equally. MAP will follow up on indicators measuring the numbers of each group benefiting from the provided supplies. Since different age groups are affected differently by COVID-19, MAP will make sure that procured items respond to the needs of particularly vulnerable groups including pregnant women, young children and older adults. Moreover, MAP in collaboration with MoH and EJHN will consider disability inclusion measures during the announcement, collection and analysis of tender documents. This includes using different communication channels to allow people to communicate with MAP’s team, ensuring accessibility of location and tools used in analysis sessions.
