Project Summary

According to UNESCO’s Institute for Statistics (UIS) data published in February 2018, roughly 260 million children, adolescents, and young people worldwide (one in five) are not enrolled in school . Sudan has its own share in this learning crisis,Sudan has both the largest number and the highest rate of out-of-school children in the region.
The project targets wo localities in West Darfur, Which is Jebel Moon and Sirba. The two localities together has a total of 21229 sector PiN. Schools enrollment rates are affected by armed conflict and subsequent economical and cultural factors. Either shortages of female and male teachers or lack of skills negatively affect the quality of the education.
The project aims to attract and maintain the # of students in the selected localities by: A/ Improving overall school's environments. B/ Create and/or improve child-friendly and protective learning spaces. C/ Create or/and improve WASH facilities and raise awareness of importance of general and menstrual hygiene D/ Conduct training for teachers and school's management on different aspects.
The project will implemented by The Sudanese Organization For Combating Violence Against Women & Children (SAO) And AlZubair Charity Organization

Needs

West Darfur selected localities of Jebel Moon and Sirba are among the most affected localities by armed conflict and humanitarian emergencies. According to the education sector matrix, the two localities have vulnerability indicators of 4 and 4 for Jebel Moon and Sirba respectively. The localities have a total of 21229 sector PiN. Schools enrollment rates are affected by armed conflict and subsequent economical factors as boys and girls families could not afford education for their children. Children are obliged to work and attracted to join fighting groups. Enrollment rates are also affected by cultural and religious factors, especially for girls. Shortage of female teachers makes the situation worse for girl students. shortages of well-trained teachers decrease the quality of education that students receive. and decrease the school enrollment rate because teachers are unable to identify the unique needs and the problem of their students. Poor skilled management fails to attract new students or maintain the current. Inability to continuously improve teachers abilities. The nonavailability or poor WASH facilities in primary school is one of the reasons that makes students situation worse, especially female students. Some schools also do not have a fence or gate to protect students; girls reported increased cases of sexual harassment and interruption from people passing by. All these factors together make education an inconvenient choice for students and parents in addition to increasing rates of early marriages, child labor and children recruitment to fighting groups.

Activities

The project will aim tackle the barriers and the obstacles to ensure continuity of education. The project will register out school children by facilitating the registration process and attracting students through improving the overall school environment. The project will provide basic school kits which contain stationary material for 4960 students in 24 schools. The project will repair or construct WASH facilities/services and raise awareness on the importance of general hygiene and menstrual hygiene, especially for girls. Overall maintenance of protective learning spaces including painting, doors, windows etc. Construction of latrines or rehabilitation of existing ones.Conduct training for teachers and school management on psycho-social barriers that leads students to drop out and training on how to help students with special needs and learning difficulties and classrooms management.Improve students-to-class ratio by building new classrooms for the schools that have students outnumber classes.Establish a child-friendly environment. By building a fence with a front wall or provide maintenance if existed. We aim to build small sheds for breaks, playing and praying spaces for every school or improve it if existed.The project will also involve community-based engagement through PTA meetings and supervision of their school's affairs. Special attaention will be devoted to capacity building and tranining of teachers and work with education Authority to fill shortage gap of teachers especially for female teachers and prepare safe accommodation for female teachers from out of the local community.
