Project Summary

Needs
In 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced, including over 2 million who live in last resort sites.
The humanitarian situation in Aleppo and Idleb has been further complicated by a major earthquake in February 2023, resulting in over 10,000 fatalities and injuries, as well as substantial damage to buildings and infrastructure. This has increased the demand for humanitarian aid and recovery efforts. Access to WASH services is facing severe challenges due to conflict damage, limited maintenance, and weak management, exacerbated by climate change and economic downturn. Unsafe water sources affect 52% of the population, with significant water systems often non-operational. Economic barriers hinder vulnerable communities, leading to sanitation issues, health risks, and challenges for children, women, PWDs, and the elderly.
Sadad recent needs assessment conducted in late 2023 from the Sub-districts of Raju, Bulbul, Ma’btali, Sheikh El-Hadid and Sharan indicated the need to reoperate a 9 vital water stations working on fuel and replace it to the solar energy, ensure sustainable delivery of safe water for over 13K individuals. The project also recognizes schools as vital community hubs and targets 79051 students and teachers, ensuring 100% access to WASH facilities and implementing hygiene awareness campaigns in over 314 schools in 15 Sub-districts all over Idleb and Aleppo.
Intervention, Implementation of activities
Sadad aims to enhance WASH services in communities, IDPs sites and schools, increase community engagement, facilitate recovery through this project which is designed to respond to the WASH needs of 95484 people (Male: 47321, Females: 48914) 62986 IDPs, 32498 people, 4405 people of them are people with disabilities during 2024-2025 based on two phases:
Phase 1 involves a smooth and continuous delivery of water through water tracking to 314 supported schools.
Phase 2 involves converting 9 non-operational water stations from fuel to solar energy in 5 Sub-districts (Sharan, Raju, Bulbul, Ma’btali, and Sheikh El-Hadid), benefiting a total of 13675 individuals.
The following activities will be implemented during the 2024 – 2025:
1- Installation of the solar power system of the 9-water stations in Afrin District.
2- distribution of 12500 one-off consumable hygiene kits to the most vulnerable households.
3- Water tracking to 314 schools in 15 Sub-districts for 9 months.
4- Conducting 314 hygiene promotion campaigns and risk reduction and community engagement at the Sub-district level.
5- Rehabilitation of the wash facilities in 314 schools affected, which including but not limited to (installation of PWDs wash facilities, construction of new latrines, maintenance of sewer system)
Protection Risk Analys
Provision of NFI packages.
Risks of Inequality in distribution; Timing and process of distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV.
Promote Establishment of adequate management plan; Promote involvement /engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct.
Support to water supply systems.
Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation).
MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities.
Child labor
Children under 18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organizations, their partners, or contractors.
Ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ laborers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labor in the contracts; check and register civil documentation.
Assumptions
All targeted locations are accessible to Sadad and the situation is stable and no threats.
