Project Summary

ALIMA is present in Nigeria and in Borno State since mid-July 2016, and was the first NGO to launch operations in Monguno where 6,407 children suffering from SAM have been treated and over 56,423 pediatric consultations were delivered.
In Maiduguri, ALIMA is operating an outpatient pediatric treatment center on the eastern outskirts of the city where tens of thousands of displaced persons are living both among the host community and in makeshift shelters. This clinic also manages children under 5 suffering from severe acute malnutrition (SAM) through an ambulatory service, while training the mothers/caretakers to detect malnutrition on their children with MUAC tape.
The present proposed project aims to strengthen ALIMA’s response in contributing to reduce the mortality and to alleviate the most acute suffering IDPs and host communities (mainly children under 5 and pregnant women), in Borno State.
ALIMA’s intervention will be focused on reducing the mortality among children under 5 linked to sever acute malnutrition.
In Maiduguri ALIMA will provide inpatient management of severe acute malnutrition with complication at University of Maiduguri Teaching Hospital.
ALIMA will complement the management of patients under 5 suffering from SAM by addressing the intermediary causes of malnutrition through activities related to Infant and Young Child Feeding and behavior change (IYCF).
In Monguno, ALIMA will continue to provide emergency ambulatory and in-patient management of SAM for children under 5, outpatient care and provide Basic Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal care. All mothers and caretakers targeted by the project will be trained to detect SAM with MUAC tape. . In coordination with the early recovery program aiming to support the health facilities, ALIMa will guarantee the prevention, the primary and secondary health and nutrition care for IDPs and host community. At least, ALIMA will be able to deploy an emergency team to provide an adequate response to outbreak (measles, meningitis, cholera) and/or manage an IDPs/wounded influx of individuals within the IPDs and the Host Community, in the areas where ALIMA is already implemented.
