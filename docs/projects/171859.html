Project Summary

The project is designed to provide emergency medical services and primary medical treatment to conflict affected communities people living in the 5-km area from the contact line and in the “gray zone” as well as for those crossing the contact line both ways in the Luhansk region.
1. Supporting InterMediCare's existing capacities for emergency and primary health care services to people crossing the contact line in both directions and living in the 5-km area along the contact line in the GCA and in the "gray zone" in Luhansk region (3 emergency medical care posts at the Stanytsya Luhanska, Zolote and Shchastya EECPs) and 3 mobile emergency medical care teams (ambulances).
2. Establishment of 3 standard emergency medical care posts at the 3 EECPs opposite Stanytsya Luhanska, Zolote and Shchastya EECPs in the NGCA to provide emergency and primary health care services to people crossing the contact line in both directions.
3. Supporting of local efforts to combat COVID-19 in particular through thermal screening and health referral proceedings at the Stanytsya Luhanska, Zolote and Shchastya EECPs as well as through mobile emergency medical care teams' capacities.
4. Establishment of 39 workplaces for the population of the Luhansk and Donetsk regions through full or partial employment of health professionals, regardless of their age or gender in the project.
The project focuses primarily on target groups living in isolated settlements in the 5-km area along the contact line and in the "gray zone". Medical assistance will be provided to everybody on the basis of medical ethics and medical secrecy, regardless of gender, age, race, nationality, linguistic choice or presence of mental illnesses, including to the LGBT community representatives and people with HIV/AIDS.

1 - Needs

As of October 26, 2020, there is 1 functioning EECP at the 182 km long contact line in Luhansk region – "Stanytsya Luhanska". The opening of 2 additional EECPs in Zolote and Shchastya is scheduled no later than November 10, 2020. The only first-aid post deployed at a checkpoint in Luhansk region is the emergency medical care post operated by the InterMediCare. 4 staff members work in it to date: senior paramedic, 2 paramedics and a nurse.The InterMediCare's modus operandi depends on the EECP's work schedule and the security situation on the contact line. Our staff continued their work during temporary closure of the EECP in October 2020 (01-05.10.2020 and 19-31.10.2020).In January-September 2020, 9080 EMC, PHC and OPC services have been provided at the Stanytsya Luhanska EECP (children (<18 yrs) – 236, adult (18-59 yrs) – 2795, elderly (60+ yrs) – 6049, male – 2762, female – 6318, residents – 8976, people with disabilities – 1999). 45% of emergency cases were registered; 2724 people were referred to hospitals.About 140 thou people live in 62 settlements within the 5-km zone from the contact line and in the "gray zone" in Luhansk region. Access to healthcare services remains challenging for people of all ages, especially for the elderly and people with disabilities in rural hard-to-reach settlements close to the contact line. Two InterMediCare's ambulances have entered the duty on August 15, 2020 at the Stanytsya Luhanska EECP (1 ambulance is now in reserve). Ambulances will be attached to each medical post after official opening of Zolote and Shchastya EECPs. They will serve people living in the 5-km zone from the contact line and in the "gray zone". Each mobile team will be function within an operational zone of 130 km (60 km lengthwise and 5 km in depth from the contact line).People have access to a mobile EMC, PHC and OPC services from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. 7 days per week, even if the EECP's functioning will be temporarily suspended.In August-October 2020, 126 EMC, PHC and OPC services have been provided by mobile teams (children (<18 yrs) – 0, adult (18-59 yrs) – 82, elderly (60+ yrs) – 44, male – 42, female – 84, residents – 126, people with disabilities – 24). 106 people were referred to hospitals.InterMediCare supports local efforts to combat COVID-19 pandemic in particular through thermal screening for all target groups at the Stanytsya Luhanska EECP. In June-October 2020, 1379 thermal screening were provided at the Stanytsya Luhanska EECP.Project needs1. The operational support to 3 medical care posts (Stanytsya Luhanska, Zolote and Shchastya EECPs) and 3 ambulances, supplying them with medical equipment, essential medicines and POL, providing employees’ salaries.The operational and technical capabilities of the InterMediCare`s post at the Stanytsya Luhanska EECP are covered by SlovakAid, Swiss Cooperation Office (SCO) in Ukraine and V4 East Solidarity Program until the end of 2020. The post has necessary equipment to provide EMC, PHC and OPC services in epidemic conditions. SCO in Ukraine has allocated funds for acquisition and installation of 2 emergency medical care posts at the Zolote and Shchastya EECPs. They will be installed in early November 2020.In July 2020 the Government of Slovakia has provided InterMediCare with 3 equipped emergency medical vehicles as humanitarian aid. The operational capabilities of ambulances are covered by SCO in Ukraine until the end of 2020.2. Acquisition and installation of 3 medical care posts at the 3 EECPs opposite Stanytsya Luhanska, Zolote and Shchastya EECPs in the NGCA, supplying them with medical equipment, furniture and medicines, providing employees’ salaries.The posts will be operated following the model of the InterMediCare first-aid post at the Stanytsya Luhanska EECP.3. Acquisition of personal protective equipment for medical personnel, sanitary disinfectors, non-contact infrared digital thermometers and COVID-19 test kits.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

Activities1. EMC, PHC and OPC services for people living in the 5-km area from the contact line and in the “gray zone” as well as for those crossing the contact line both ways.2. Support of health referral procedures.3. Gathering information about medicines needs of people living within the 5-km zone from the contact line and in the “gray zone” as well as those crossing EECPs.4. Thermal screening in medical posts at the EECPs as a preventive measure and warning to respond to COVID-19.5. Dissemination of materials covering both basic information of COVID-19 and hygiene promotion messages.6. PSEA training for the personnel of InterMediCare medical posts on the contact line and mobile emergency medical teams (ambulances).7. Joint monitoring missions with representatives of local authorities, UN agencies and donors to different areas of project implementation in order to provide technical support and services quality control. Outcomes 1. Fully operative, including the COVID-19 context, 6 standard medical posts at the 6 EECPs on both sides of the contact line with appropriate medical equipment, furniture, essential medicines and personnel.2. 3 mobile emergency medical care teams (ambulances) which provide mobile EMC, PHC and OPC services to the population living within the 5-km area from the contact line (GCA) and in the “gray zone”.3. Health referral procedures in the isolated and hard-to-reach settlements.4. Identification of medicines needs of the population living within the 5-km zone from the contact line and crossing the EECPs, particularly in the coronavirus pandemic environment.5. Raising local people’s awareness of COVID-19 in the GCA/NGCA.6. Support of local efforts to combat COVID-19 in particular through thermal screening at the EECPs.7. Raising medical staff's awareness of PSEA policy to ensure zero tolerance to any signs of sexual exploitation or sexual abuse.8. Generating of 39 workplaces for the local population.
