Project Summary

Maram is proposing to provide Shelter support to based populations in northwest Syria (Idleb) in order to achieve its objective of increasing access to Safe and Adequate housing and supporting stabilization in target area. for displacement and conflict-affected populations. Under this intervention, Maram will provide assistance to 3,500 individuals (650 households) in Al Maara district (Telamnas , Maar Shurin , Maar Shamsheh and Eastren neighborhoods of Ma'arrat An Nu'man communities ) . This will be achieved through the following activities:
- Shelter Rehabilitation/ Improvement: Vulnerable HHs living in unfinished or damaged buildings, returnees and newly arrived IDPs or IDPs living in extremely overcrowded conditions moving to shelter extensions and newly rehabilitated or finished shelters.
- HLP (Housing Land Property) Rights /prepared by Shelter Cluster/: Vulnerable individuals have increased knowledge and understanding of HLP rights and how to exercise them.
This project will build on Maram’s existing work in Idleb area and enable it to expand its operations to provide essential services to under-served populations in Idleb Governorate. All proposed activities are based on gaps and needs identified in collaboration with the CCCM and Shelter Clusters coupled with primary needs assessments conducted by Maram.

Needs

According to SNFI-HNO 2019 , 750,931 people suffered from Shelter Severe problem in south Idleb governorate especially in Al Maara district with a 47.2 percent increase over compaing with 2018 .As a basic reference, Maram field team conducted a field visit to Eastern Al Maara countryside to verify from the needs and take a close look about Shelter situation where Maram field staff visited ( Telamnas , Maar Shurin , Maar Shamarin, Maar Shamsheh and Eastren neighborhoods of Ma'arrat An Nu'man)More than 650 HHs in needed to Shelter rehabilitation where most of the houses were partially damaged and others were incomplete.Maram’s Shelter programme will target vulnerable populations affected by conflict and displacement living in the most sub-standard living-conditions in Al Maara district with through rehabilitate their houses with cost 1000$ for each house. As defined by the Shelter and WASH clusters, the most vulnerable populations include those living in Collective Centres and damaged/unfinished buildings, in addition to returnees from the camps who living in tents or without shelter, where shelter activities will be implemented through contractor

Implementation of activities

Maram Foundation plans to implement rehabilitate shelters to sphere standards and confirm the quality of repairs, according to the follows activities:1. Coordination:Maram team will coordinate and contacts with local authorities for initial identification and assessment.2. Assessment and preparing:- Preparing and confirm the technical criteria to be used during the implementation.- Technical assessment to identify the vulnerable beneficiaries list and modality of support.- HLP assessment and work with landlords, landowners, and local councils to secure tenancy for the most vulnerable residents, ensuring they are not evicted or taken advantage of- Prepare BoQs based on the HH technical survey3. Identify the contractors, including prepare PRFs, announcement and selection (including technical evaluation and scoring process)4. Implementation of Rehabilitation: Provide all rehabilitation needed to improve shelter and create privacy and ensure a protective, safe environment for women, children, and men.5. monitoring the project activities:MF staff will monitor project operating directly and provide reports for THF and 4Ws to WASH and CCCM Clusters6. Evaluation:Maram Foundation will evaluate the project after complete all the works.7. Handing over the work and exit strategy:Maram team will hand over the work to the beneficiaries by coordination with the local authorities, including signing all the MoUs and agreements required
