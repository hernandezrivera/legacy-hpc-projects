Project Summary

The project support on improvement of existing WASH facilities and installation of new facilities on requirement to provide safe, adequate and dignified WASH services to Refugee in the camps. All available WASH facilities especially the latrines and bathing units, in targeted camps/area, will be improved and rehabilitated meeting emergency WASH standards. It is aim to provide water supply access with installation of bore hole as per need including pumping and distribution network on selected area. Likewise, decentralize Fecal Sludge Management (FSM) unit will be constructed and regular desludge will be carried for sustainable management of sludge generated. The activities will be implemented in coordination with the community; WASH committee will be formed and capacitate to able to construct, operate and maintain the facilities. Hygiene promotion activities will be intensified with better access to hygiene product to improve the hygiene practice contributing towards the behavior change and to increase health assistance seeking behavior. Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) session will be target to women and adolescent girls and community base solid waste collection and management activity will initiate with this project. Access pathway, lighting system at WASH facilities will be ensure by project to increase access, dignity and protection.

Objectives

Objective 1: Ensure effective, adequate, dignified, sustainable continuous provision of water and sanitation services for children and their families Objective 2: Ensure that all targeted women, men, girls and boys have the means and knowledge to practice improved person and environmental hygiene practice and encouraged to adopt individual and collective measures increasing health seeking behaviors to mitigate public health risksObjective 3: Ensure that all WASH assistance promotes the protection, safety and dignity of targeted people, and WASH Committee are able to maintain facilities and receive support for, on requirement.

Needs

Basic provision of the water supply, latrine and bathing units are available on the working area. All available WASH facilities especially the latrines and bathing units need to improve and rehabilitated. Most of these facilities were built at first phase of the response hasn't meet the emergency WASH standards. Access pathway, lighting system at WASH facilities including the male: female latrine ration require improvement to increase access including the appropriate space for the special need of children, women, PLW, people leaving with disability. Further to ensure the long term regular operation of the latrines; there are need of decentralize Fecal Sludge Management (FSM) unit and continue desludging activities throughout the year. Engagement of the beneficiaries/community Particularly children, women, girls and old age person from planning and operation is utmost important for sustainable operation. Furthermore, community base solid waste management is one of the needs observed at camps. Hygiene promotion activities need to intensify with better access to hygiene product to improve the hygiene practice contributing towards the behavior change and to increase health assistance seeking baheviour. MHM campaign is required among the women and girls with reproductive age girls. Skill of women and adolescent girls need to enhance on production of the reusable menstrual pad for sustainable access to those requirements. Further there is need of water supply system with distribution network to provide adequate and safe water to the population, closer to household and other facilities in camps. Special consideration need to provide to GBV, protection and equity during design and implementation of the WASH program.

Activities or outputs

Water supply:- Assessment- Regular maintenance and support on operation of the installed hand pumps and water distribution systems- Installation of deep tube wells with submersible pumps and water distribution points. - Water quality testing at source and household level- Functionality survey Sanitation:- Assessment- Maintenance and improvement of the existing latrine and bathing unit - Decommission the non-functional sanitation facilities and Construct of gender segregated latrine and female friendly bathing unit - Construction and maintenance of drainage near water points and latrines - Construction of Fecal Sludge Management Unit and its operation- Carry out regular de-sludge activities- Provision of small scale appropriate solid waste management activities through active community participation- Functionality survey Hygiene promotion:- Selection and training of community volunteers on key hygiene messages and methodologies for girls, boys, women and men- Conduct hygiene promotion sessions (including menstrual hygiene management) at cluster level, including engaging women, girls, men and boys- Organize mass campaign and house to house visit to promote improve hygiene practice - Distribution of hygiene kits, household handwashing kit, dignity kits and soaps-KAP Survey, PDM, PTMEnhancing protection, safety, dignity of people and management capacity- Maintain and install solar light installed on WASH facilities-Ensure the latrine and bathing unit are properly separated for male and female-formed and capacitate the WASH committee to be able to operate and maintain facilities -Stockpile preposition items to response any emergency response in-between- slope protection work around WASH facilities- Improve the access/pathway to WASH facilities
