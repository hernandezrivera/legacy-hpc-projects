Project Summary

The overall project objective is that vulnerable youth refugees, IDPs, and host community members impacted by displacement crises in urban contexts have access to livelihoods opportunities through cash grants for new and existing business and entrepreneurship training that safely and sustainability meet their needs and contribute positively to their communities in the Far North.

1 - Besoins / Needs

As global humanitarian crises are leading to growing displacement and mass movements of populations, the vulnerable situation in which many displacement-affected populations find themselves is becoming less sustainable. As more and more populations flee conflict, disaster, and insecurity, refugees and internally displaced people (IDPs) are forgoing traditional camp-based resettlement settings in lieu of greater perceived economic and social opportunities in cities and urban areas. The majority of the world’s refugees and internally displaced people are now living outside of camps. Furthermore, displaced people often resettle in neighboring countries that are already struggling economically; 80% of displaced populations resettle in developing countries. Alternatively, some displaced populations resettle in developed countries such as in Europe, where cities, too, have struggled to maintain economic growth amid large influxes of new populations.Urban centers across all these contexts represent both opportunity and difficulty for vulnerable populations, in particular youth, where with sufficient access, knowledge, and support, economic integration is possible. Without due support, however, youth continue to struggle with some of the highest rates of unemployment, social stigmatization, and an inability to integrate positively in their new markets and communities.
