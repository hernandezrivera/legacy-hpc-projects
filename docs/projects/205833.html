Project Summary

Action Against Hunger in this 2024 project concept proposes to extensively provide contextualized interventions to the targeted Counties within its' 3 operational States. These Counties include; Aweil East, Aweil South, Fangak, Uror, Duk and Twic of Northern Bahr El Ghazal, Jonglei and Warrap States respectively. The interventions are aligned to the WASH cluster strategic plan focusing on the 3 key sectoral cluster objectives as well as inter-sectoral objective of; 1. Well, Being Reduce Morbidity and Mortality, 2. Basic Services Provide safe, equitable and dignified access to services, 3. Build resilience. Action Against hunger intends to carry on with ensuring integration of with its' nutrition sector within the organization especially as a result of the Nutrition cluster rationalization of one county, one partner according to our recently completed mapping of nutrition sites and WASH facilities/service status in the above mentioned and targeted Counties, focus will be to ensure provision of comprehensive WASH services at facility and community level. among the proposed activities are; rehabilitation of non-functional water points of all types, drilling of new deep wells especially in our nutrition sites, carry on with ensuring, disability friendly sanitation facilities within our sites are constructed, supporting women with dignity/menstrual hygiene kits targeting SAM/MAM caregivers, conduct participatory hygiene awareness involving affected communities to ensure best hygiene practices. with the continues influx of returnees, transiting refugees as well as displaced persons due to Sudan crisis, floods and sporadic inter-communal conflict such as in Warrap state, Action Against Hunger will ensure, WASH facilities are provided in consultation with women, girls and the disabled to mitigate GBV linked to access to WASH services/facilities. In some of the location particular Aweil East, Twic, Aweil South and Fangak, Action Against Hunger will use cash modality for provision of WASH non-food items tapping in from the expertise within the organization on the ongoing ECHO CCD supported cash project. Prior to engaging in this activity, market assessment of basic and essential communities will be conducted including financial risks and measures put in place to mitigate.

1. Needs

South Sudan continue to struggle to cope and recover from the subsequent conflicts of 2013 and 2016 which not only let to displacement internal and outside the country of its' civil population as refugees but also the impact of the prolonged floods especially in the greater upper Nile region (Jonglei, Unity and Upper Nile) states. among some of these hardly hit Counties are Fangak, Duk, Gograil West, Aweil East and Aweil Center. these are Counties we have been and continue to operate in providing multi-sectoral humanitarian and or lifesaving services (WASH, Nutrition, Food Security & Livelihood, Health and Protection/GBV). Action Against Hunger is one of and among the few humanitarian organization in South Sudan employing two operation responses modalities i) MET/SET, Multi-sectoral Emergency Team/Surveillance Emergency Team and Static programing. According to the WASH cluster 2024 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) there 8.5 Million people in need of humanitarian services out of which 5.5 million requires WASH services. According to several SMART surveys we conducted, for instance in Aweil East of 26th October 2023, The prevalence of combined GAM was 24.9% and SAM was 5.7%, About 45.5% of the households had no sanitation facilities and for those with these facilities, hand-washing facility was the most predominant (28.1%), majority (68.4%) of the households do nothing to drinking water whereas other households used different water treatment options ranging from boiling to straining, only 11.3% of the households reported that they have latrines. In Fangak as per the May 31st SMART Survey report, This survey has shown some deterioration in the nutrition status of the population of Fangak County. Besides this survey, the most recent survey indicated the GAM in Fangak County was 17.5% with the highest confidence interval limit of 21.8%, River water was the main source of water (80.2%), only 23% of the households reported that they have latrine out of these 23%, 53.8% was a shared or communally owned whilst individuals owned 46.2%. The type of latrines used by the households or the communities were mostly without slabs (69.9%). The deterioration in the nutrition status could be attributed to the the poor state of food security in the county largely caused by the flood water from the river that has covered much of the arable lands and deprived the population of livelihoods that could be acquired through farming. The floods further affected the other existing assets like livestock that further exacerbated the vulnerability of the population. As for Gogrial West Smart survey July 2023 report findings, The prevalence of combined GAM was 29.1% and SAM was 6.2%, only 4% of the households reported that they have latrine, About 68.4% of the latrines were without slabs, As the County witnesses deterioration of nutrition status especially of women and children, The poor nutrition situation in the county was mostly attributed to the current war in Sudan that has led to the rise in the price of market commodities for food. Furthermore, the flood in 2022 has destroyed majority of the planted crops and nor or minimal harvests were made by households. While in Duk and Kassingor, between June/August, there were reported cases of suspected cholera cases which triggered deployment of SMoH/WHO/UNICEF for verification. our finding through the assessment we conducted found inadequate access to safe drinking water, poor hygiene practices as well as luck of health facilities was the main attributing factor to the increased diarrhea cases. With the increasing influx of population into our areas of operation, Fangak, Aweil Center/East and Gograil West, the existing WASH facilities are over stretched and witnessing frequent breakdown. with the outbreak of Hepatitis E in Fangak and Measles in Gogrial West, the situation is only getting worse.

2. Project activities

Base on the above needs identified through our SMART surveys as well as in line with May 2021, WASH severity classification exercise, Fangak, Duk, Gogrial West, Aweil East, Aweil Center and Kassingor were classified between phase 3,4 and 5. directly implying limited access to safe water sources, risk to WASH related outbreaks, rampant open defecation, high GAM rates/malnutrition, increased GBV cases linked to access to water sources especially for women and girls, poor hygiene practices including lack of hygiene materials which could be due to high prices of such items within the local markets or cut-off roads to markets besides destroyed livelihood activities of vulnerable communities in these Counties due to prolonged floods, continued inter-communal conflicts leading to displacement of population etc.Action Against Hunger will provide, life-saving response/intervention as per the WASH cluster guidance to directly contribute to the sectoral objectives, among the activities are;1) Provision of safe water through Rehabilitation of non-functional water points of all types at both facility and community level, targeting areas of high malnutrition rates. for sustainability, affected communities capacities will be built in a form of water user management committees as well as local pump mechanics equipped with special and or standard Indian Mark II installation tool kit, water quality monitoring will be conducted routinely to ensure prompt actions. the focus will be on bacteriological parameters. in areas such as Duk, Fangak and Gograil West, ACF will identify flood affected/prone water points to be converted into flood resilient platforms to ensure they withstand and are accessible for use year around.2) Ensure safe accessible sanitation facilities at nutrition/health centers as well as in location host displaced through construction/rehabilitation of gender-segregated blocks of latrines. for inclusive access, disability add-ons will be installed equipped with handwashing station and soap or ash.3) To mitigate/prevent relapse of SAM/MAM children, the caregivers of these children will be provided with WASH kit as defined by the WASH cluster, in addition most vulnerable women/girls within the reproductive age will be provided with menstrual hygiene kits/dignity kit to improve their person hygiene.4) To improve hygiene practices and eventually mitigate poor hygiene related outbreaks/mortality, Action Against Hunger, will work with local communities to mobilize men and women (Youth) who will be trained and form part of the team as community hygiene volunteers, these team will be deployed to conduct extensive hygiene awareness sessions both at facility level as well as at the community through door-to-door visits. they will also aid in demonstration/distribution WASH NFIs to affected communities and ensuring their usage is monitored either through their routine visits and or post distribution monitoring.
