Project Summary

In 2020, Help a Child in collaboration with the IOM, the Red Cross, OCHA, the national platform for disaster responses and the Ministry of solidarity, gender and social affairs facilitated the closure of 3 IDP relocation sites and the reintegration of 1082 IDP households affected by natural disasters in commune Ntahangwa situated in Bujumbura Mairie with shelter support, NFI kits, food aid and loans for income generating activities.
In 2021, Help a Child would like to build on its previous responses with the launch of Hembura Project II. A Project that aims to provide 1500 IDP households (approximately 8,700 people) to most affected by natural disasters in communes Mutimbuzi and Mukike (Bujumbura), Ntahangwa (Bujumbura Mairie), Murwi and Buganda (Cibitoke), Rumonge (Rumonge), Busoni and Bugabira (Kirundo) with support to access emergency shelter and 3 months’ rent fees to facilitate their reintegration in permanent shelters. The package will also include NFI kits, sanitary pads for IDP households with women and girls, mobile phones and loans for income generating activity groups.
