Project Summary

In 2023, the WASH sector will target globally 146 562 people, including 21 600 internally displaced persons following natural disasters or other shocks as cholera epidemies, 124 962 vulnerable and members of host communities. The assistance will be carried out according to two sectoral objectives which are as follows:
• Provide emergency WASH assistance to people affected by epidemics and natural disasters including host communities. This objective aims to provide drinking water and emergency sanitation infrastructure (latrines, showers) as well as to distribute hygiene kits to affected people. These interventions will make it possible to respond to essential WASH needs in order to guarantee survival considered to be physical and mental well-being, but also the protection and dignity of vulnerable people.
• Ensure access to WASH services for 146 562 people including internally displaced persons, members of host communities as well as people living in areas of return of returnees and those at risk of water-borne diseases. This objective aims to improve WASH services for communities in order to reduce the use of negative adaptation mechanisms such as the use of surface water (river and lake water), air defecation practices which constitute a risk of pollution of the sources as well as other bad hygienic practices favoring the occurrence and the persistence of epidemics.
