1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aim to assist IDP and non displaced people of Montepuez district, targeting 2880 people among, IDP and host communities providing food assistance and building the livelihood capacity through market approach and development of life skills

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Oxfam will provide monthly food assistance to acutely food insecure population through the most feasible and contextually appropriate modality (in-kind food baskets or equivalent cash-based transfers).Food assistance to 2880 ppl (male/female) (576 hh). The families covered will receive food aid for 2 months. Food ration will meet the minimum calory requirement of 2100 calories pp/day and has been designed based on the food security cluster recommendation. The target group for the Action prioritises internally displaced persons fleeing conflict including returning IDPs and host communities among which we will prioritize the most vulnerable families with pre-existing vulnerabilities before the crisis, considering the Minimum Expenditure Basket, and who have suffered human loss, loss of assets or livelihoods.
