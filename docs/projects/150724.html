Project Summary

Overall objective of this intervention is to improve food security status for conflict-affected population in the Gaza Strip. The intervention aims at enhancing economic access to food and livelihoods for vulnerable households of young graduates and unskilled laborers by increasing their income as well as improving their skills and quali cations which will increase their independence and recognition within the community. Young graduates and their families will be targeted through creating temporal employment opportunities in civil society organizations (CBOs) and the private sector through a cash for work (CfW) modality. The project target population is 400 young graduates and unskilled laborers from food insecure households taking into consideration targeting female graduates with at least 55% of the targeted graduates and female headed households as the most priority The project aims to create 50,000 paid working days through temporary employment opportunities for target beneficiaries.

Needs

Gaza Strip is on the brink of collapse as most of the Palestinian people remain affected by the protracted crisis resulting from Human Rights and International Humanitarian Law (IHL) violations and lack of protection, that contribute to a coercive environment, progressive isolation and forcible transfer of the Palestinians. The crippled economy resulting from Israel’s decade-long blockade, with deteriorated purchasing power, the impact of additional cuts in incomes in Gaza may result in a full-blown humanitarian crisis. The unemployment rate stands at a staggering 44% feeding into the perceptible sense of hopelessness and despair. In order to prevent further deterioration as well as building resilience humanitarian support is needed. Additionally, it has become necessary for third states (like International Non-Governmental Organizations (INGOs) and human rights organizations) to accelerate their efforts in order to foster economic growth and promote sustainable development, including through ensuring predictable and sufficient energy supply to improve access to basic services, including healthcare, education, clean drinking water, and proper wastewater treatment. (The Association of International Development Agencies (AIDA) Report March 18). On the other hand, Food Security Sector (FSS) Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2019 workshop in addition to “FSS Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) Priorities and Response Reports 2019”, which considers Cash-Based Interventions (CBI) as the most priority of Immediate relief actions. CBI ensure direct access to food for families , and immediate response to shocks, furthermore, purchasing power is enhanced and existing economy revitalised through CFW modality. (Reference: HRP 2019 Priorities FSS). According to “Economic Monitoring- Ad hoc Report” released by World Bank on September 2018, one of the immediate objectives is to increase household purchasing power to enable a return to basic economic activity, the report also specified that cash for work scheme would make use of the large pool of unemployed individuals and provide a ready cash ow to support the wider economy.

Activities

The main goal is to contribute to increased protection for the communities and people acutely affected by protracted crises in the Gaza Strip through the Cash programming approach. The intervention will contribute to enhancing economic access to food for vulnerable and protection threatened Households (HHs) of skilled and unskilled persons (mainly: Fresh graduates) through temporary employment opportunities in civil society organizations and the private sector. CfW modality will be used, which is considered efficient and effective in provision of immediate direct access to food, and response to shocks and thus an immediate relief to economic/poverty stress among population. Main Activities: • Community sensitization and bene ciary selection with gender consideration The lists of potential bene ciaries will be nominated from the database of Islamic Relief in addition to Ministry of Social Development (MOSD) nomination list which is extracted from the waiting beneficiaries (BNF) list and in consultation with FSS partners. Priority given to: female headed household, those who have no source of income, no person working in the family families with disabled family members and elderly and families living in rented houses. Gender perspective will be promoted where both genders will be given the opportunity to benefit from this project. In particular; female graduates will be given higher quota (60%) due to higher unemployment rate among them. The BNF selection mechanism will ensure no one is neglected, abused, discriminated or exploited. Older people, women, men, girls, boys, minority groups and individuals will be identi ed in the needs assessment. • Selection of hosting organizations/businesses Islamic Relief has a network of over 200 local CBOs and private sector companies to host the graduates. In particular CBOs that care for female graduates and working women will be given priority • Identi cation of CfW beneficiaries • Employment of recent graduate bene ciaries The beneficiaries will be given temporary work placements at local civil society organizations, women organizations, and private sector companies and agricultural CBOs / farms for periods from 3 to 6 months, with various levels of wages. Female employment process will ensure safe and secure conditions in addition to social acceptance through different contexts. The employment periods, and wages will be according to the following preliminary breakdown depending on rates approved through Food Security Sector and Cash Programming Working Group • Mainstreaming of gender and raising awareness on human rights and inclusion and protection among the community through workshops and community meetings The intervention will include advocacy activity that promotes raising awareness regarding Gender based violence (GBV) • Monitoring, feedback and evaluation The eld o cers will pay regular and irregular visits to workplaces, to follow up attendance, assure the daily hour's works, resolve any issues, and con rm adherence to labour law. Islamic Relief will disseminate the complaint and feedback policy among all the project stakeholders, i.e. bene ciaries, hosting institutions, etc. Project information will be shared among stakeholders though brochures, website, etc. which aims to explain the project purpose, targeting and benefiting criteria and clarify any ambiguity. Midterm and nal evaluations will be conduct by M&E department to learn lessons and improve performance.

Indicators

- # of individuals benefiting from CfW: 400 individuals will receive temporary CfW work opportunities for periods ranging from 3 to 6 month.- The project will create around 50,000 working days, resulting in enhanced economic access to food and livelihoods for vulnerable and protection threatened households and ultimately contributing to improving food security of vulnerable and food insecure communities in the Gaza Strip.- The impact of the prevailing food insecurity crisis is alleviated for 400 households, and their purchasing power is increased and they become able to meet their basic food needs- The project will contribute towards maintaining the dignity of the most vulnerable groups and confidence as well as financial support to beneficiaries in conflict and post conflict areas and saves the family situation.- This intervention is a vital component toward the overall food security strategy and it will enable beneficiaries to receive assistance with dignity.- The project will support around 100 hosting institutions, where they get free labor and hence improve their services and quality of work.
