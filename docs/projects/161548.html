Project Summary

This project seeks to provide wet feeding program for 3 days and 3 nights for Returnees at Pulka Transit Camp. The Target number is 5000 comprising of females, males and Children. Safe energy stoves would also be provided to 3000 heads of households after formal training on how to use the efficient energy stoves

1 - Needs

Kanem Borno Human Development Association(KABHUDA) finished a wet feeding food assistance in September 2019 with support from NHF. The project conducted a post implementation survey in October 2019 and during the course survey we noted that hundreds from returnees kept coming to our wet feeding center thinking that our project was still on-going The IOM Dashboard also kept on showing arrivals of returnees to the Pulka transit camp suggesting that returnees keep coming but as at the time of this proposal no partner was on ground to provide a stabilization wet feeding program to returnees and thus this proposal In addition to the wet feedings needs, KABHUDA post implementation survey showed that Safe energy needs amongst the new arrivals is relatively high and hence the inclusion of safe energy programming in this proposed project

2 - Activities

KABHUDA receives IOM screened and registered returnees and provides them with wet meals for 3 days and nights, meaning 9 rounds of meal comprising of breakfast, lunch and dinner and each meal with provided with table water. Each leader of a household would be given 1 efficient energy stove. Stove beneficiaries would be given an informal training on how to use the stove before distributionAfter 3 days of wet meal assistance, returnees are then weaned for possible assistance by partners who provide dry food assistance The wet feeding comprises of morning breakfast meal which includes Groundnut paste porridge served with fried beans cake and/or bread and beans cakeLunch: Comprises of Rice and stew with beefDinner: Semoliva with local vegetable soups( okro, baobab leaves or spinach) Safe activities: When wet feeding beneficiaries are weaned, a head of a household( child, female or male headed household) is selected for distribution of a one piece efficient energy stove. However a targeted training on how to use the stove is given to the beneficiaries on how to operate the stove The safe energy training would be a formal training 1/2 day program where beneficiaries are trained in batches of 100 Accountability to Affected persons: Beneficiaries would be ask be involved in selecting the meal variety combinations provided such combinations fall within allocated budgets Protection mainstreaming: Wet meals distributed would be done in an orderly and gender segregated manner. When meals are received beneficiaries would be directed to a eating halls where meals would be taken in an organized manner The practical sessions for the safe energy stove usage would be held in an open space Beneficiaries would not able allowed to access the kitchen area save the dining halls PSEA. Project staff and vendors would be required to commitment to PSEA documents and violations would attract sanctions Project staff would be trained on PSEA and protection mainstreaming
