Project Summary

1: To provide immediate household access to food and increase purchasing power to the most vulnerable populations in emergency throughout the year and to people in crisis during the livelihood lean season by providing monthly food vouchers and unconditional cash relief/transfers
2: To restore community productive assets through provision of livelihoods inputs and develop alternative livelihood strategies for vulnerable population, realized via provision of farm inputs, vocational training, temporary job creation and daily stipends for maintainers of productive assets.
3: To Improve food access to school going children in vulnerable communities by providing them with daily meals to prevent further food insecurity and malnutrition

Project Activities

Output 1.1: Facilitated food access to 1800hh ( 10800 beneficiaries) (806 men headed households and 994 women headed households), for 3 months for communities in south central region.Activity 1.1.1: Provision of food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil and pulses) to 1300 hh (7800 beneficiaries) in emergency (586 men headed households and 714 women headed households) each household receiving a voucher worth 71 USD per month. Total amount of food provision is 60 mt of Maize, 8 mt sugar,6 mt Oil and 9 mt Pulses. Activity 1.1.2: Provision of food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil and pulses) to 500 hh(3000 beneficiaries) in IDP camps (220 men headed households and 280 women headed households) each household receiving a voucher worth 71USD per month. Total amount of food provision is 20 mt of Maize, 3 mt sugar,2 mt Oil and 3 mt PulsesOutput 1.2: Increased minimum household basket for 800hh(4800 beneficiaries) (352 men headed households and 448 women households) who are extremely vulnerable through cash relief worth 150 USD for 2 Months per year. Activity 1.2.1: Provision of cash relief worth 150 USD per month for 2 months to 800 hh (352 men headed households and 448 women headed households) in communities in crisis focusing more on the most vulnerable individuals both men and womenOutput 2.1: Enhanced food production and assets for 3370 households(20220 beneficiaries) for 1 year (1443 men headed households and 1928 women headed households) through provision of farm inputs (seeds, farm tools and land preparation) for one season per year Activity 2.1.1: Provision of seeds (maize seeds, sorghum seeds, cow peas, mung beans, onion ,tomatoes, lettuce) farm tools and land preparation to 3150 hh( 18900 beneficiaries) (1390 men headed households and 1760 women headed households); 120 households(720 beneficiaries) Activity 2.1.2: 53 men headed households and 68 women headed households will receive household metal silos and get trained on food storage and the importance of using household metal silos other than the traditional food storage and thus reducing harvest losses Activity 2.1.3: Provision of 4-hours Tillage support for land preparation to 100 destitute poor farmers/IDPs and marginalized groups (3 items; "shovel, Hoe & Rake" to each farmer)Output 2.2: Increased livelihoods of the community by development of community infrastructures by engaging 600 households (3600 beneficiaries) (264 men headed households and 335 women headed households) in rehabilitation of water catchments and feeder roads. Activity 2.2.1: Creating of temporary job opportunities to 600 households( 3600 beneficiaries)(264 men headed households and 337 women headed households) just before the rainy seasons through cash for work activities and providing 5 USD daily rate to the laborers per 0.5 M3 for canals, 0.3 M3 for water catchments and 5 M2 for feeder roads working for 24 days a month. Output 2.3: Increased knowledge and skills to the vulnerable urban households by providing vocational trainings and start-up kits to 60 unemployed youths ( 38 young men and 22 young women) Activity 2.3.1.: Provision of vocational trainings to 45(20 young men and 25 young women) individuals especially the unemployed youth in urban area and provide them with materials to start up a business of their own. Provision of vocational trainings to 30 unemployed young men in fishing communities and provide them with boats, fishing nets and other equipment to enable them start up a business for themselves.
