Project Summary

The project seeks to mitigate the impact of the Anglophone crisis on women and girls through a snail domestication livelihood project. It shall consist of building a snail multiplication facility which will serve as a seed bank and hands-on training grounds. The project shall also provide rescued victims of sexual slavery with hands-on training in all aspects of snail domestication, including pen construction, snail husbandry, processing, packaging and market penetration strategies. The project will also provide start-up support for the creation of individual and/or group farms and ‘seed’ snails to populate the farms.
Increasingly NSAG in the rural communities enslave girls in their camp to perform domestic chores and sexual servitude. Others have been victims of rape and other forms of sexual slavery from the regular military as a way of revenge against NSAGs. In rural communities controlled by NSAG, young girls and women take turns in the camps of NSAG to serve them. Once released traumatized victims travel out of their villages as unaccompanied child IDPs. These young women and girls are in dire need of livelihood and support activities which our initiative seeks to contribute in giving them.

Besoins / Needs

The increasing number of IDPs caused by the crisis in the North West regions of Cameroon is exerting enormous pressure on the host communities. This is fast weakening the community support chain of host communities in Bamenda. As a result most IDP especially unaccompanied children, victims of sexual exploitation girls are forced to fend for themselves. Some have been abused by pedophiles and sex traffickers while other embrace survival sex work (prostitution).Our initiative seeks to reduce the vulnerability of these sexually exploited women to further abuse through a sustainable livelihood initiative of snail domestication. It shall contribute in solving the challenges faced by victims of the crisis while providing them with psycho-social support. Snails are an outstanding source of proteins and a delicacy cherished by a majority of urban duelers in Cameroon and beyond. Through the provision of vitals skills for snail domestication and marketing, the project shall empower the beneficiaries both economically and nutrients wise.
