Project Summary

We are writing this proposal to improve on the level of awareness of Covid-19 and measures to prevent transmission of the virus. Due to data collected and analysis from our last Covid-19 rapid need assessment, we turned to realize the areas of slowdown of responsibilities regarding preventive measures are very low in rural communities unlike urban where there exists high level of awareness. But neither urban nor rural communities have been putting in to practice preventive measures. Covering face with mask is almost the only measure known by these communities. As a means of mitigating the effect of the pandemic, we intend to impact positively in 50 communities within 4 Divisions and 8 Sub-divisions in the South West Region with special note on hard to reach areas where there is less concern and increase in vulnerability of the population. While some of us have learned to live with mask and social distancing, as well as handwashing after handling packages and touching surfaces, we need some basic sensitization activities to improve level of awareness and preventive measures in order to live in a more stable and healthier environment. Covid-19 pandemic has caused more harm in some of our studied communities such as psychological trauma, stigmatization, loss of lives, and high level of urban-rural migration increasing the rate of internally displaced persons. Our target beneficiaries are 50 students, 100 teachers, 50 parents and 5 educative staffs. After impacting in the communities positively increasing awareness and preventive measures, we shall equally sensitize beneficiaries of the importance and available protection services that have been put in place such as hotlines or toll numbers, counselling services, case management services, child friendly spaces, institutional care not leaving out social worker groups and their inputs regarding overall management of affected or suspected cases.

Plan fields

1 - Besoins / Needs

Program activities;• Sensitization through physical seminars with teachers, students, parents and host of others inclusive of distribution of COVID-19 preventive kits.• Placing of wash kits at entrances of at least 50 streets in some selected rural areas/villages with sensitization to the village counsels in order to make it continual as a means of sustainability. • Physical seminars with care givers, community leaders, teachers, students, parents and host of others with 2M social distance to implement. • Distribution of sanitizers to at least 800 vulnerable households as a means of educating them on the essence of using such measures to prevent the effect from the pandemic• Distribution of durable face masks to at least 800 vulnerable persons with special notes on disabled children within sampled communities.  Special support;• Purchase of wash and hygiene kits to be given to 10 orphanages in rural communities.• Purchase of wash and hygiene kits to be given to 3 handicap centers in rural communities• Purchase of wash and hygiene kits to be given to 3 deaf centers• Putting in place hotlines for reported cases.• Psychological support to affected families.Program monitoring and evaluation: The monitoring and evaluation will take a community based approach and will start from the beginning and to the end of the project. Extraction of data from the Google sheet (data collected during the project) and run analysis using Excel and Power BI (Microsoft Power Business Intelligence) in order to come out with reports that can be used to understand overall reached and predict future change/sustainability. After overall analysis, a project report will be communicated to donors. This report can be communicated or shared through PowerPoint, words formats, pictures, videos and any other form of available feedback not leaving out proposals from donor. At the end of the evaluation period MICHAFOUND will make an overall reports showing planned, reached and gap analysis.
