Project Summary

This project will target 7 communities in the Idleb and Aleppo which are classified among the areas most of the need according to moving of IDPs which depend up on the report of CCCM cluster of IDPs movement tracking.
The percentage of IDPs in the targeted areas reached 71% of the total population of each area in average, due to the recent displacement happened which the total number of IDPs up to end of Jan 2020 are 984K according to CCCM cluster information.
BRD aims to improve the food security of people suffering from food insecurity and provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable displaced families, focusing on areas with high needs.
The beneficiaries will be provided with monthly food assistance for 8 months (2100 KCAL) as per FSL cluster guidance and BRD will take into consideration the COVID-19 situation and take the preventive measures to deliver the food assistance, (Masks, sanitizers, social distance and distribution points..etc) , which will contain basic food items. The weight of each basket will be 60 kg, which consequently will contribute to alleviating the financial burdens and costs of living on the targeted families in the project.
Total number of HHs will be assisted: HHs 3200 and individual: M 3290 , W 3450 , B 4710 , G 4550 .
BRD logistics team will purchase food items from inside Syria exclusively to be packaged in the warehouses in Hazzano.
The baskets will be transferred to the targeted areas in Aleppo and Idlib after identifying the spots of IDPs especially in open-lands, collective centers and informal settlement in the targeted locations on a daily basis according to the distribution plan and under the supervision of the Field Project Manager, the M&E team, the Logistics Team, and the Security and Safety team and then deliver it to the beneficiaries.
BRD's team will cooperate and coordinate with the local councils in accordance with the needs assessment conducted previously by preparing lists of beneficiaries that will be approved by the local councils according to predefined selection criteria:
- Displaced families (a key priority for choice, especially for newly displaced families)
- Family income must be less than $ 50 per month.
- The breadwinner is a woman whose husband is lost, injured or disabled.
- Poor families that have (elderly or young children under 15 years or widowed women).
The M&E team in collaboration with the field team will verify the names of the beneficiaries who will be provided with cards containing (the village code and beneficiary code) certified by the organization before starting the distribution.
Field Distribution Teams will begin the distribution process according to the beneficiary name lists and under the supervision of the distribution supervisors within a pre-defined comprehensive distribution plan for the targeted villages.
The people in NWS facing critical situation of cholera, and the number of people who affected are: 30114, so BRD will coordinate with LC and partner who running cholera center to referral the ne who affected in case.
The fund for this project not secured yet.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

BRD will start implementation by provide 28800 FBs (8 months) for 3200 HHs of IDPs (Individual 16K) in targeted villages to16K IDPs persons, distribution will be through several points of distribution in targeted villages. The proposed FB will be enough for families who consist of 6 individuals which were weight 60kg and is composed of 14 items (bulgur, rice, lentils, sugar, chickpeas, white beans, tomato paste, vegetable oil, dates, halva, noodles, ..etc) FBs will be purchased from selected suppliers within Syria and agreement setting out all the conditions necessary to ensure quality of products and delivery and payment mechanisms The process of packing and packaged FBs in boxes with the BRD's logo placed on FBs so that the packaging and packing BRD will ensure accountability for AP and prevent fraud and exploitation. MOU will be signed with LC explaining project activities and objectives. Verification of BNFs lists through field visits by M&E Teams. BRD will also print voucher for whole targeted 3200 HHs on monthly basis for 8 months, and distribute them to BNFs at least 3 to 5 days in advance, specifying date of distribution, time and location, making them be able to receive FBs from distribution points safely and easily. Project will enhance protection through, stop child labor, women not facing abuse or harassment to get food assistance, all targeted people have access to assistance, will be phone number to complaints, and system to receive the baskets.
