Project Summary

Under the Rapid Response Mechanism, ACF aims to assist, nearly 23,800 conflict affected individuals, IDPs as well as returnees, with Emergency Shelter and NFI assistance. ACF maintains capacity and flexibility to mobilize response in multiple districts in northern Cabo Delgado, based on presence and coordination with the cluster, Macomia has been provisionally indicated in this project sheet as projections on new displacements is dynamic and evolving. The rapid response under RRM is based on three pillars 1. Humanitarian Monitoring and Rapid Needs Assessments (RNA) 2. Integrated and multi-sector rapid response and 3. Advocacy for Complementary Responses.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In response to the urgent and increasing humanitarian needs in Cabo Delgado, this project employs a comprehensive and timely approach to provide life-saving assistance through provision of emergency shelter and non-food items for 23,800 individuals affected by a shock, either conflict or natural disasters. ACF aims to enhance early warning mechanisms, ensuring a coordinated Rapid Response Mechanism (RRM) for an immediate response to the vulnerable population in a timeline manner. ACF aims at targeting IDP but also returnees to provide tailored support for the populations recently returning to their place of origins . A key focus lies in strengthening capacity for Rapid Needs Assessments (RNAs), harmonizing efforts to comprehensively understand immediate and critical needs in vulnerable households. Overall, the project aims at implementing an integrated approach to support the most vulnerable following a shock- including the identification of referral pathways at the first stage of the intervention (needs assessment ) to ensure an holistic approach along with the actors and stakeholders present and active in the area of intervention and capable of providing services beyond ACF's services (ex: child protection) for a complementary approach.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The overall objective is to contribute to alleviate people’s suffering by restoring access to essential multi-sectoral life-saving services for affected populations in Cabo Delgado. ACF Provides coordinated multi-sectoral and life-saving humanitarian assistance to affected populations in Cabo Delgado. The project seeks to ensure that vulnerable households in Cabo Delgado receive emergency multisectoral support aiming at saving-lives and alleviating the suffering of those most-in-need, while promoting the dignity, health, wellbeing and empowerment of communities. This will be achieved by strengthening RRM mechanisms, providing emergency shelter and essential non-food items , as well as mainstreaming protection and environmental considerations. The project will aim at prioritizing the delivery of essential humanitarian aid to severely impacted districts such as Macomia, Meluco, Quissanga, Metuge , with potential expansion to other areas depending on the evolving conflict dynamics ( Chiure, Muduimbe )and the coordination with partners, clusters and existing mechanism (such as JRP) to ensure a complete coverage of the needs. It ensures a standardized minimum response package is consistently delivered across all targeted districtsKeys activities are implemented : 1- Enhancing early warning mechanisms and Context monitoring:The RRM through the focal points network, and in close collaboration with other 1st line partners and Clusters, will ensure close monitoring and analysis of humanitarian context. RRM alerts will be triggered in various critical situations, including conflict-induced incidents, violence, disease outbreaks, and preparedness natural disasters such as cyclones, floods, and droughts, recent sudden return movements. Following the alerts, Rapid Needs Assessments (RNA) will be conducted (jointly or individually by ACF, FDC and SI), taking into account factors such as security, safeguarding, Do no harm principle, protection risk analysis, access, operational presence, and team availability. 2- Alert and RNA: Publishing alert, through the validation of the Operational Coordination Unit (OCU) and then conduct of the Rapid Needs Assessment. and the report is shared among the humanitarian communities, including clusters and donors to ensure an operational coordination to cover the needs for the affected population assessed (Elaboration and dissemination of quality post-RNA information products)3- Based on the RNA results, Provision of essential life-saving multi-purpose assistance distribution of Survival kit, emergency shelter, essential non-food items accordingly to the RNA and the prior needs , targeting the most-affected and vulnerable population.4- Conducting Post monitoring distribution and satisfaction survey to ensure the quality of the services received and provided, to monitor the use of the distributed items, to continuously collect complains according to the mechanism in place and to ensuring adressing it

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

ACF consistently adopts a do no harm approach throughout the implementations of all projects. The project will mainstream protection, addressing the protection risks and needs of affected people with the aim of safe guarding their dignity while strengthening the protection environment through collective and multi-sectoral action in line with commitments to gender equality principles, the centrality of protection, and PSEA across the humanitarian response. The project aims at supporting the most vulnerable, without creating any tensions between communities and beneficiaries and in accordance with the protection cluster guidance of vulnerability based targetting. To do so, selection criteria will be thoroughly explained to the communities and communities’ leaders, and the population involved in the conduct of the activities whenever possible. As main risk, the escalation or aggravation of the conflict hindering the humanitarian access and access to assistance for the beneficiaries is the biggest. In case of security situation jeopardizing the possibility to intervene ACF is taking several measures accordingly to the level of gravity from daily follow up on security to temporary closure or reduction in activities is safety and security cannot be guaranteed for the team and for the beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

RRM interventions are designed with the goal and purpose of providing meaningful assistance to displaced persons and those affected by the conflict in a fair and equitable manner, ensuring access to aid for all individuals affected by displacement and the crisis, regardless of age and gender. Therefore, ACF conducts an assessment that takes into account the needs of households, and the kits distributed integrate the specific needs of women (e.g., menstrual products). If specific needs beyond ACF's capacity and the RRM response are identified, ACF refers individuals to appropriate partners or services, particularly second-line actors working in the shelter and protection sectors. These actors may also be present during distributions to support the identification of protection cases and facilitate care, as well as to raise awareness on these specific and sensitive issues among the supported communities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

If individuals with specific needs or persons with disabilities are identified, the project provides an appropriate response in various ways during the needs identification and distribution process: individuals are prioritized during distributions (to minimize waiting times), ensuring that a caregiver, relative, or community member can assist in carrying the kits to the household (while upholding the principle of do no harm and under the supervision of an ACF staff member), identifying additional needs beyond ACF's capacity (e.g., access to health or protection services), and triggering the referral pathways

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Regarding environmental considerations, the following measures are prioritized: A waste management system in place and limitation of plastic use;Minimizing international transportation;A waste system for chemicals to prevent groundwater pollution;Procurement of more environmentally sustainable relief items;Use of renewable energy when possible;Developing innovative and alternative solutions for boreholes in the future to address water scarcity.Pooling of resources and transportation with other partners if possible and necessary.
