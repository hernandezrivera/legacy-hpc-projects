Project Summary

The is aimed at providing basic entrepreneurial skills and peace building capacity to 200 women and girls in Damaturu LGA Yobe State. The project will help address the issue of women and girls vulnerability resulting from the impact of insurgency by Boko Haram which left some LGAs with so many families poor and vulnerable in addition to the Internally Displaced Persons to which women and girls (children) are the most vulnerable of all. These horrific attacks brought about challenges which necessitate the need to sensitize and sanitize women and girls on the dangers it pose on them and how to counter violent extremism using women and girls as tools for peace building and resilience to violent extremism as they are more influential. Women have the capacity to counter ideologies and calm situation providing counter narratives and resilience to any form of violence.

1 - Needs

The destructive effect of the activities of the Boko Haram insurgents over the years has created a huge humanitarian challenges in the areas affected by the insurgency. The killings of bread winners and destruction of sources of livelihood of affected families left many individuals in a dire need of humanitarian assistance. The most affected group in this crisis are women and girls (disabled and minorities inclusive) who are most vulnerable members of the communities. Most women and girls are struggling to survive as they cannot cater for their basic needs while some resolved to begging and young girls forced to go for street hawking making them vulnerable. This necessitate the need for this intervention especially in Damaturu. The project target to address 1. The fear and Distrust of one another among community members. 2. Women who lost their jobs due to insurgency who are now idle and vulnerable have become potential targets. 3. Those that had no jobs before now are even more vulnerable 4. Due to poverty some of the parents especially widows could not afford to send their children to school and those who use to were forced to with draw them. Most of these children especially the girls were forced to street hawking in order to provide income for their families thereby increasing their vulnerability. There is an urgent need to empower women and girls (including disabled and minorities) who have lost their loved ones (husband, parents and guardians) and their sources of livelihood.

2 - Activities

The identified needs will be addressed by the project through the following activities 1. PRE-IMPLEMENTATION MEETING a. To conduct series of meetings to map out plans for successful project implementation and staff briefing. b. Identification of logistics required and staff allocation of resources needed for the administrative arrangement. Outline duties and responsibilities to each member of the project team. 2. ADVOCACY a. Organize visit to traditional and community leaders, religious leaders and government agencies (Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development) 3. MAPPING STUDY AND DESIGN WORKSHOP a. Conduct mapping study to provide baseline for the intervention and also facilitate stakeholders engagement. b. Organize a design workshop to validate assumptions of the project and develop clear understanding of project approaches and deliverables. 4. SELECTION AND IDENTIFICATION OF STAKEHOLDERS IN THE COMMUNITIES a. Community sensitization sessions with stakeholders (Religious, Community, Ministry of Women and Social Development, Women and Youth Leaders, LGA officials and Security Agencies) 5. CAPACITY BUILDING FOR 1000 WOMEN AND GIRLS ON PEACE BUILDING a. Selection of beneficiaries and organize capacity building workshops on peace and conflict management 6. EMPOWERMENT OF 1000 WOMEN AND GIRLS a. Identification prioritization of marketable hands skills b. Identification and engagement of master craft persons within communities. c. Training of beneficiaries on prioritized and selected hand skill/trade d. Procurement of craft materials equipment. 7. MONITORING AND EVALUATION a. Follow up and monitoring of beneficiaries b. results and lessons sessions c. Final assessment/submission of report d. Follow up on project to government and relevant agencies for sustainability.
