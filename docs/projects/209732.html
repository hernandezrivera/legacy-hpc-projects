Project Summary

Summary Project Proposal: Missed People and Dead Bodies Management with Environmental Health Integration
Project Overview:
This project is designed to ensure the proper and dignified management of dead bodies and facilitate the identification process while concurrently addressing critical environmental health concerns in disaster-affected areas. It will be executed in collaboration with key stakeholders, including the Humanitarian Ambassadors Organization (HAO), the Sudan Forensic Medicine Association, local authorities, and organizations such as the Federal Public Prosecution, the Federal Ministry of Health (FMOH), the Red Cross (ICRC), and the Sudanese Red Crescent (SRC).
Project Objectives:
Promote Proper Dead Body Management: Implement procedures outlined by the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) for the management of dead bodies after disasters, including assigning unique codes, utilizing identifying methods (fingerprints, DNA, dental examination), photographing and recording data, and orderly temporary storage.
Support Displaced Individuals in Finding Missing Family Members: Establish a robust system for reporting and tracking missing persons, providing support to displaced individuals until they are reunited with their families.
And The following approaches will apply to ensure that all reached dead bodies identified, managed, stored in mortuaries or buried in graves in a timely and dignified manner. And reported missing people well hosted in safe and secure places, treated with full respect till they connected with their family members.
• Allocation of a unique code to each body.
• Unique identifying method (whether fingerprints, DNA or dental examination) or other techniques.
• Taking photographs and recording data about each body as soon as possible.
• Placing each body in a body bag.
• Orderly temporary storage of the bodies.
• Creation of database for all reached dead bodies and reported missed people.
The donors will receive quarterly progress reports of implementation of the project. Initial approval for this project has obtained in advance from Federal Public Prosecution.
Integrated Environmental Health Interventions:
a. Vector Control and Campaigns: Implement vector control measures to prevent the spread of diseases, including those transmitted by vectors attracted to dead bodies. This involves proper disposal of dead bodies, management of equipment, chemicals, and labor for vector control campaigns.
b. Solid Waste Management and Campaigns: Develop strategies for solid waste management, including the disposal of dead bodies, supplies, equipment, and waste bins in a manner that minimizes environmental pollution and health hazards. Conduct awareness campaigns to promote proper waste disposal practices.
c. Support for Medical Waste Management: Ensure proper disposal of medical waste generated in health facilities, including dead bodies, supplies, and equipment, in compliance with safety and environmental standards.
d. Mass Media Campaigns: Utilize mass media platforms to raise awareness about environmental health issues, including the importance of proper dead body management, waste disposal practices, and vector control measures.
Importance of the Project:
This integrated approach addresses both humanitarian and environmental health concerns in disaster-affected areas. Proper dead body management is essential for public health and dignity, while addressing vector control and waste management issues mitigates environmental pollution and health risks. By implementing these measures in conjunction with the management of dead bodies and missing persons, the project aims to create a cleaner and safer environment for affected communities, promoting resilience and well-being in the aftermath of disasters.
