Project Summary

Conflict in South Sudan has had a negative impact on the populations in South Sudan. The country was on upward trajectory soon after independence from Sudan but thing changed to the worst after 15th December, 2015 when the former vice president was accused of a attempted coup.
A peace agreement was signed in Addis Ababa but it never lasted for long time. on the 12th June 2016, fighting erupted in the capital and soon spread to other parts of the country. This greatly affected the communities with killings and displacements of the populations having a negative impact on the population.
Key infrastructure and WASH facilities have been left in deplorable situation leaving the affected population without adequate clean and safe water for drinking and domestic use.
Terekeka county in central Equatoria State was not left out in the crisis and WASH facilities were destroyed and left unattended for for a long time,
This has left the populations in this county in urgent need of intervention to help alleviate the already dire situation.
Water borne diseases are a major issue in this county the latest outbreak of cholera was reported in 2017 and it claimed several lives. as of February 2017, there were 8 deaths according to the national ministry of health statistics. This is a trend which can be averted with with proper sanitation facilities and proper human waste disposal.
Lack of knowledge on the importance of good sanitation has affected many people in Terekeka County and has led to spread of preventable illnesses contracted through contaminated water.
lack of sanitary pads for the women and school going children has had detrimental effect on the dignity of this vulnerable population and has had a negative effect on the learning circles of the school going girls who are forced to stay at home during the menstruation period.
This project will help the affected population by construction and rehabilitation of water points, construction and rehabilitation of latrines, distribution of hygiene kits, menstrual hygiene kits and aqua tabs.
The project will establish water management committees and also train the health and nutrition workers on the minimum WASH package activities in order they can disseminate key wash messages to the affected populations.
All health and Nutrition facilities will be supplied with clean and safe water for drinking and mothers to the acute malnutrition cases will be given hygiene kits in order to ensure that the malnutrition cases don't get worse from infections related to contaminated water.
Reporting of the project activities to the relevant cluster and ministry will be done to improve service delivery and policy making.

Needs

Terekeka County has a burden of recurrent waterborne infections like bloody waterlily diarrhea and cholera, the recent outbreak was reported in early 2018. This calls for behavior change communication to help the population refrain from open defecation which is a major source of water contamination leading to continued disease burden due to human waste contamination.Cases of violence and sexual abuse against women have been reported in the past in Terekeka County. This has been due to lack of sanitation Facilities as women are attacked when they go to the bushes to relieve themselves. The project will address this by constructing and rehabilitating WASH facilities in the county and ensure that they are gender sensitive with locks and doors. This will be done in consultation with the women and girls.The health and nutrition sites lack essential WASH facilities and even lack clean and safe water for drinking. This therefore exposes the already vulnerable populations in danger of increased disease burden. the project will construct and rehabilitate wash facilities in health and nutrition centers. lack of menstrual hygiene kits have increased suffering to women and girls in school. this is a need which will need to be addressed to allow more school days for the girls and more activity to the women without fear and preserving their dignity.

Activities

1. Distribution of menstrual hygiene kitsMenstrual hygiene kits will be distributed to mothers of childbearing age and school going girls. This will preserve the dignity of the mothers and the girls and will also ensure that the school going girls don’t miss classes during the menses period.2 Distribution of hygiene kitsThe distribution of hygiene kits to the vulnerable populations will ensure that the communities practice safe water storage and water treatment tablets in the hygiene kits will ensure that there is clean and safe water for drinking and for domestic use.3. Construction and rehabilitation of water pointsWater points like boreholes and the other water sources will be constructed and those destroyed during the crisis will be rehabilitated in order to ensure that the beneficiaries get clean and safe water to the required standards of 15 liters per day per person. Water points in health facilities and nutrition centers will also be rehabilitated to ensure that all the pregnant and lactating mothers and children with acute malnutrition have access to safe water for drinking and domestic use.4. Construction and rehabilitation of latrinesThe project will construct and segregated latrines which will help in reducing cases of open defecation and reduce disease burdens caused by water contamination. The latrines will also help reduce cases of gender based violence and sexual abuse by ensuring that women and girls have a safe place to relieve themselves.5. Training water and latrine management committee members. To ensure functional of all the water facilities, TAYA will re-training all the water management committee on the management for sustainable purpose. 6. Hygiene promotion and sensitization. social mobilization and awareness session that involves all age women, men, girls, boys ensuring active participation of every on major hygiene campaign sessions will be done 7. Regular reporting by Wash coordinator
