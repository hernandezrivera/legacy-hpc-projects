Project Summary

Nabolok ensure the lifesaving services are being addressed since June, 2018 to till date and planning to continue for the coming year of 2023 in camp-24, 25 & 26 at sub-block-D, E & F under Camp-24; A4 & A5 under Camp-25 and E4, E5, E7 & E9 for Sanitation and F-8, 9, 10, G-3, 4, 5, 6 &H-10 under Camp-26 respectively for Rohingya and host communities at word no-8 & 9 in Nhilla Union in Teknaf, Cox’s Bazar. Considering the camp context, field experience and assessing the needs of beneficiaries regarding WASH services, we found that a maximum facility has already been installed. Now need to ensure the proper use, operation & maintenance with continuous repair of those facilities as well as quality, regular, sufficient, equitable, and dignified access to WASH facilities for all women, men, girls and boys, inclusion of all age groups and persons with disabilities in the Rohingya camps and targeted host communities and need to address the new challenges or demands. Besides some latrine need to decommission and installed the new one. Bearing in mind the above issues we are planning to address the currents need of targeted beneficiaries Just as: supplying sufficient safe water to targeted Rohingya and host beneficiaries from ARO Sea Water Desalination Plant (The first ever Sea water treatment plant for Rohingya and host Beneficiaries) and Boreholes. The sanitation and hygiene activities through regular repair & maintenance of every single WASH facilities Just as: Twin-pit Latrine, Bathing Cubical, Double chamber Dustbin for organic and non-organic waste, Drain cleaning, Latrine dislodging, maintaining of Fecal Sludge Management (FSM). Aware the beneficiaries about hygiene knowledge through regular hygiene promotion session, individual HHs visit, group session and Mega phone and distribution of ICE materials etc. In addition, our beneficiaries plan to conduct an evaluation to collect feedback. We may conduct some repair and renovation work on existing wash facilities so that people of all age groups, genders and PWDs can use our wash facilities very comfortably. Disinfection activities & fumigation spry and Key message forwarding have included in our plan considering COVID-19 pandemic and Dengue Fever. In every stage, we will collect beneficiaries’ feedback before going to implement any activity and we will design our plan as per their demand so that we can ensure inclusion of all age groups, genders and persons with special needs to use WASH facilities. As most of the facilities has already constructed that’s why we won’t scope to re-install those facilities rather we will conduct some repair work and install some components with the existing facilities to make those facility gender and PWD user friendly so that to all age groups, genders, and persons with special needs get access to use those facilities quite easily. All of above mentioned activities are included in our plan to ensure that 100% of targeted beneficiaries have proper water, sanitation and hygiene facilities throughout the project duration.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

Improve water service: As per WASH sector analysis 12% of Rohingya and 26% of HC reporting not having enough water to meet domestic needs. Teknaf area don’t have the access of safe water and water scarcity is very high here in Teknaf region as it’s a saline area and underground water is also contaminated with saline. Access to water in Teknaf still remains a major challenge during the dry season with water trucking and water diversion required again during 2022. The People are depending on the rain water of this area as a consequence during dry season water scarcity becomes intense here. So ensuring safe water for 100% beneficiaries is a challenge and need to fulfill this gap as well as who has access to safe water, need to maintain the quality, quantity and supply of water regularly.Access to Sanitation: Sanitation: As per WASH sector analysis, 0nly 8% of fecal sludge treatment plants meet standards while 7% of sanitation infrastructure require major maintenance and 51 Rohingyas on average share bathing facilities due to lack of space. Regarding waste management: As per WASH sector analysis only 51% of Rohingya are disposing waste in designated area resulting of 27% of household reporting garbage near their shelters. Although the gap in bathing spaces has reduced, there has been a significant increase in private spaces, with 86% of households reporting private bathing space inside or attached to their shelter for but those bathing places sometimes associated with laundry spaces and dishwashing places. It is very often those spaces are unhygienic and poorly maintained or used as alternatives for urination and defecation Women and girls face issues of overcrowding and lack of privacy when using latrines and bathing spaces during the day, while at night they risk harassment and violence with One of the major concerns regarding sanitation is the quality of the faecal sludge treatment as facilities require upgrades and improvements. Solid waste management is still a gap in our targeted area. Hygiene Promotion and Community Engagement: as per WASH sector analysis of Hygiene: Only 66% of women and girls accessing MHM kit as per standards. Regarding outbreak, cases of scabies and dengue have respectively been multiplied by three and by twelve in 2022 compared to 2021. Hygiene knowledge and access to soap remains high although behavior change remains an issue that requires further research to assess the impact of knowledge on hygiene habits. It also requires a wide scale of Hygienic Practice monitoring, staff capacity building and meaningful community engagement. On the other hand, host’s communities have been less supported by hygiene items and awareness raising initiatives.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

Considering safety and security and protection and dignify access of safe water, the project will increase 3 boreholes, 11 tap-stands and 7 water distribution tank by the nearest area of the beneficiary house s so that they will receive their support within a short time with zero/ minimum cost. Prevent and minimize as much as possible any unintended adverse the project ensure safe water through tap-stand and ensure and water distribution volunteer in to the distribution places, he or she will ensure easy access and enough and meaningful access to assistance and services in proportion to need and without discrimination, gender, disability or age-related barriers. The project will pay special attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services, e.g. Persons with disabilities or disabled friendly, elderly, children, etc. Ensure health and hygiene for all latrine and bathhouses.

3.2 - Gender

To ensure safe dignify access of safe water, Sanitation, reduce gram diseases by the water and rotated solids waste the project will increase water distribution water and water distribution points, ensure the waste bins to the households and continue the awareness building activities i.e. Group session, house to house visits. Repair and maintenances work will continue for the latrines and bathhouses, cleaning the drains and dustbins. We well known that the females and adolescents are suffers for access the WASH facilities, so the project team will go to field assessment for site selection, and the household selection and they will talk with beneficiaries, especially females because we always emphasize the female and adolescent girls' opinions to set up/installed the facilities. It will be reduced trafficking and sexual and physical harassment and gender based violence (GBV) of women and girls.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

The project will ensure safeguarding by maintaining safeguarding policies and procedures. To ensure easy access to water from the water distribution point, the project ensures easy access road and protects hits from sunlight ensuring a shed on the tap stand. The water distribution volunteer will give priority to the Extremely Vulnerable individual (EVI). The tap stand and water distribution points will be in a safe and secure place with a shed for all, they will enjoy for receive their services. Desludging work will be continuing on a daily basis. The Solid Waste Collection volunteer will collect waste from the house, and the awareness program will continue at the household level, the aware households will keep their waste in the nearest dustbins. So that the families will be safe and enjoy for using the facilities without any harm.

3.4 - Diversity

The project will conduct a household assessment for select beneficiaries, and households; select the sites to establish/install new WASH facilities those families will urgently need of the WASH service from Nabolok. Besides of this the projects also conduct the FGDs regarding this. Considering the women and adolescent comment, women-headed PWD families, ethnicity, different cultural backgrounds, and various lifestyles will be selected the place by the consent of CiC and SMS.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

The project will accountable to ensure dignitary and quality support for the affected people. The project is committed to addressing the crisis and reducing the water crisis and ensuring sanitation facilities for Rohingya households and 25% of the host community and support to ensure solid waste management. For ensuring the quality of the support, the project will ensure the minimum of two types of accountability mechanisms are in place i.e. Complaint Box and direct or phonically system. Projects staffs will play a positive and supportive role to ensure quality support in time. The beneficiary can shares their suggestions, feedback, and complaints with the project staff verbally, and in writing using a complaint box. Besides we will conduct regular monitoring and door-to-door visit and collect feedback about our services. The project will welcome complaints and feedback for further improvement and will be ready to respond to the complaint, and feedback on a priority basis.
