Project Summary

The project will be implemented in some of the districts with communities affected by cyclone Idai as well as districts with porous borders and highly mobile populations. The project will support approximately 50 235 people from the target districts of Beitbridge, Chimanimani, Chipinge, Chiredzi and Nyanga. Focus of support to be provided by the International Rescue Committee (IRC) will be on inclusive access to humanitarian assistance for women, girls, boys and men in the targeted districts.

1 - Activities

The IRC will carry out the following activities: 1) Support community focal persons to conduct GBV awareness raising and sensitization in target communities; 2)Train Community focal persons to provide basic psycho- social support to GBV survivors and provide information on available clinical care services and specialized support services; 3) Support women and youth groups to support village savings and lending Associations (VSLAs) and gender discussions to improve women’s access to economic opportunities, increase their decision making power and reduce GBV ; 4) Provide education on sexual reproductive health, awareness and education on GBV prevention for in and out of school girls.

2 - Indicators

The project's success will be measured through the following indicators: number of women, girls and men attending awareness raising sessions; number of GBV survivors who receive psycho-social support; number of GBV survivors referred for specialized support services; number of functional Village saving and lending associations for i) women and ii) youth; number of in and out of school adolescent girls receiving education on sexual reproductive health and GBV prevention
