Start Date

Project Summary

To provide lifesaving cash and economic support to maintain critical household assets, combined with food assistance through food vouchers in Djenné, Goundam, Ténenkou and Niafunké (Timbuktu).

Activities

"Activity 1: Distribution of unconditional cash transfer to 10,109 vulnerable HHs to meet their essential food needs during 2017 and 2018 lean period.Activity 2: Cash transfer to vulnerable HHs to protect their livelihoods and make them more resilient to future shocks. 40% (4,044) of targeted HHs – approximately 24,264 individuals –, especially vulnerable returnees who have lost their productive assets will participate in Income Generation Activity (IGA) trainings - petty trade, craft, farming, animal breeding Activity 3: 60% of HHs targeted by unconditional cash transfers—6,065 very poor HHs of approximately 36,390 individuals, will receive food vouchers to meet their essential food needs during the lean periodActivity 4. 75% of targeted HHs with children under 2 years old and/or PLW - 7,582 HHs/ 15,163 individuals - will receive nutrition supplementsActivity 5: Strong community approach for active screening, referral and treatment of children with MAM at community level through: (i) training of community agents, “Lead Mothers” (LM) to conduct active child screening, share their experience with other mothers, conduct awareness sessions and MUAC mothers who will be trained and equipped to conduct MUAC measurement of children aged 6-59 months" Indicators and targets: Unconditional cash transfer---- 10,109 HHs 60,654 individuals (Wommen 30,934; Men 29,720)Food vouchers ----- 6,065 HHs 36,390 individuals (Women 18,559, Men 17,831)Unconditional cash transfer --- 4,044 HHs 24,264 individuals (women 12,375, Men 11,889)
