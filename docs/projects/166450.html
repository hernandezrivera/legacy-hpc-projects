Project Summary

The project is designed to contribute to a reduction in WASH related public health risk specific to covid-19 through improved access for refugees and affected host communities in the area of Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Promotion.

Objectives

The Project will contribute in the achievement of the following sector objectives:1. Ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities to sufficient and safe water for drinking and domestic needs ad medical purposes.2. Ensure all refugees living in camps, Host Communities or affected by COVID -19 have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times.3. Ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through hygiene promotion and distribution of hygiene items with strong focus on highly contagious diseases, for all refugees, affected or vulnerable communities of the district.

Needs

UNHCR is the area focal agency for 14 camps (1E, 1W, 2E, 2W, Kutupalong RC, 3, 4, 4 Extension, 5, 17, Chakmarkul, Nayapara RC, 26 and 27) and Kutupalong Transit Center covering 40.4% of refugees in Ukhia and Teknaf Districts. As per now, COVID -19 has already been detected in Cox's Bazar district (39 confirmed cases) and UNHCR has been scale-up the response in camps and host's communities. Need for safe water is increasing for hand-washing. Good behavior need to be reinforced through hygiene promotion, new facilities and distributions of hygiene items.

Activities or outputs

WASH JRP 2020 remains critical activities, however, specific activities related to COVID-19 for WASH should focus on: 1) Provide soap, hygiene kits, aqua tabs both in camps and Host’s communities2) Organise disinfection of WASH facilities and common spaces3) Provide adequate WASH facilities in health care facilities and isolation centers 4) Increase coverage and operationalisation of hand-washing stations both in camps and host’s communities.5) Strengthen hygiene promotion focusing on COVID-19 6) O&M of water facilities. Extraction and distribution of chlorinated water through solar powered networks as well as ground water levels will be monitored through the setting up of real time monitoring system. Keys tube-wells will be maintained using community approach and solar powered water systems and water treatment plants will be operated and maintained by trained skilled operators
