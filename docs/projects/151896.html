Project Summary

Action Against Hunger has provided 24,948 households with emergency shelter kits and standard NFIs, benefitting 174,636 IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities who affected by displacement and disease outbreak in Borno and Yobe State. The activities are planned for 3 years.

Needs

Nine years of conflict between the Nigerian military and non-state armed groups in northeast Nigeria has taken a heavy toll on the population. Approximately 1,926,784 individuals (364,635 households) remain displaced in North-East Nigeria and the ongoing conflict is the primary reason (94%) for displacement. Borno state, is the most affected and hosts the highest number of IDPs (1,441,635) and in Yobe State there are 137,588 IDPs (IOM DTM Round 24, 2018). As per the IOM DTM Round 24, August 2018, 54% percent of IDPs needing assistance are female, 27% are children under five, 81% are children plus women. The host communities support 91% of the IDPs, placing burdens on households and existing infrastructure that are already stressed. According to the Nigeria Center for Disease Control (October 2018) a total of 39,540 suspected cases of cholera, including 804 deaths with CFR: (2.05%) had been reported around 20 states, including Yobe (1,905) and Borno (5,465) states. According to IOM DTM tracking matrix, Shelter needs were assessed in 286 camps and camp-like highlighted 13 sites (hosting 5,448 families) in Borno (11), Bauchi (1) and Yobe (1) states as having households living without shelters, with the number of families in need of shelter being lower than 25 percent of total IDPs on site. Additionally, households in 203 sites (hosting 118,711 families) were living in makeshift or self-made shelters; in 54 of these sites, over 75 per cent of the total IDPs on site were living in makeshift shelters. 50 sites have more than 75 per cent of IDPs on site living in such emergency shelters.

Activities

Activity 01:100,000 affected male, female, boys and girls will received standard 14285 NFI kits. • Registration of beneficiaries for standard NFI kits • Distribution of standard gender NFI kits distributed for man, women, boys and girls• Post distribution monitoringActivity 3: 100,000 affected man, women, boys and girls will received standard 14,285 emergency shelter kits• Registration of beneficiaries for emergency shelter kits • Distribution of standard emergency shelter kits for man, women, boys and girls• Post distribution monitoring Description: A rapid assessment will be conducted during the first phase of emergency to identify the specific needs of the NFI (Kitchen items) and shelter of the affected population. Needs of most affected individuals such as women, children and separated girls will be identified and to reduce the risk of gender based violence, women and girls with high needs will be priotize for the distribution. Special consideration will be taken to support the separated girls and female headed households with WASH hygiene kits to ensure that they received the basic items related to menstrual hygiene management.
