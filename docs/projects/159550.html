Project Summary

The proposed project aims to improve vulnerable households’ access to food and essential basic needs and promote the protection and restoration livelihoods to enhance early recovery and resilience of households and communities. The project will target 10,400 (62,400 individuals) conflict and drought-affected households including drought-affected pastoralists, urban poor and IDPs in Puntland, Galmudug,and Banadir. Specifically, the project will prioritize vulnerable groups such as female-headed households, households headed by or has persons with disabilities, child-headed households, households with older persons, households with high number of dependents who are vulnerable and households who lost productive assets among other vulnerabilities. The project will achieve this through two main objectives:
Objective 1: Improved access to food and other basic needs for 4,500 vulnerable households through provision of multipurpose cash transfer and short-term gainful employment. Under this objective, the project will support:
• 3,000 vulnerable conflict and drought-affected households who are in crisis and emergency through multi-purpose cash transfers to enable them tmeet basic needs and avoid negative coping strategies. Transfer values will be based on recommended transfer values by the cash working group and will be coordinated with Cash working group to ensure interventions are coordinated with FSC and Cash working Group partners. Markets remain functional and are able to provide need basic commodities but people lack the cash to purchase needed. The intervention will therefore provide unconditional cash transfers to provide beneficiaries with flexible options to purchase their most pressing needs. IRC will monitor markets and will conduct post-distribution monitoring to understand market changes and beneficiary perception to provide timely corrective actions
• Creation of short-term temporary employment for 1,500 individuals who are stressed or crisis through cash for work initiatives to create income opportunities during crisis to enhance their purchasing power and improve access to basic needs. The CfW interventions will not only improve household income but will also support target communities in rehabilitation of key communal infrastructures such water catchment sources, market access roads and public facilities-schools, clinics, community centers that are critical for early recovery and resilience.
Objective 2: Productive livelihoods and livelihood assets for 5,900 households are protected and/or restored through provision of appropriate inputs/services, skill training and income generation. Under this objective, the project will support:
• 10,000 poor pastoral households through massive animal treatment/vaccination campaigns to treat livestock against infectious and endemic diseases including Sheep and Goat Box (SGB), Contagious Caprine Pleuropneumonia (CCPP), tick-borne diseases and parasitic worms such as helminthes to protect livestock and improve animal health and productivity. Treatment/vaccination campaigns will be coordinated with FAO and relevant ministries of livestock in Puntland and Galmudug to avoid overlap and duplication in treatment. In addition, the project will establish, train and equip 100 community-based animal health workers to undertake animal health services at the community level enhancing available sustainable services for pastoral communities.
• Fisher folks along the coast of Hobyo, Eyl and Bandarbeyla face significant challenges including limited fishing skills, inadequate fishing gear to catch and store fish and limited information of marketing. The proposed project aims to target 300 fishermen through training on best fishing practices, marketing, consumption and provision of appropriate fishing gear to increase fish catch, promote safe storage and enhance fish market and consumption.
• 500 women and youth from the communities will be trained on entrepreneurial and market relevant skills.

Project Activities

1.0 Provision of multi-purpose cash transfers to 3,000 ( at least 50% female) vulnerable conflict and drought-affected households In Bari, Nugaal, Mudug, Galgaduud and Banadir for a period of 3 months with priority given to female-headed, disabled, elderly and child-headed households, as well as IDP households to enable them meet basic needs and avoid negative coping strategy 1.1 Identification and engagement of 1,500 individuals for short-term gainful employment through cash for work initiatives 2.0 Conduct 2 organized animal health campaigns to treat/vaccinate over 500,000 livestock belonging to 10,000 poor pastoral households in pastoralist target areas of Sanaag, Bari, Nugaal, Mudug, and Galgaduud to protect livestock and improve animal health and production 2.1 Establish, train and equip 100 community-based animal health workers to undertake community-based animal health services 2.2: Provide fishing inputs and seasonal fishing based training and other transferable skills to 300 fisher folks along with the coastal bands of Mudug, Bandarbeyla and Eyl districts 2.3: identify and train 500 (at least 50% female) urban poor and IDP households through market-relevant and entrepreneur skills training and provision of start-up grants to enable households to generate income and assets for self-reliance and self-sufficiency.
