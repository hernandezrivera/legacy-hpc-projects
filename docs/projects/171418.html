Project Summary

The project is designed to contribute to achieve the HRP SO2: The basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through the provision of quality basic services & improved access to resources, in accordance with the rights of protected persons under IHL.
The emergency situation after the COVID-19 Outbreak in the Gaza Strip late of August 2020 in addition to that the situation is worsened due to lack of power – Gazans have lately been surviving on 8 to tw12 hours of electricity a day. This has caused the local authorities to impose lockdown which will threaten vulnerable communities’ livelihood and access to essential services including health care and WASH services. According to OHCA latest situational report, the spread of COVID-19 within the community has generated additional challenges for WASH service providers. These include the shortage of disinfection materials and PPE. WASH services in Gaza have been further undermined by the power outages. As a response to appeals received from WASH services providers, PARC conducted RNA (rapid need assessment) in the northern governorate of Gaza Strip (Jabalya, Bait Lahya, Bait Hanoun and Um Al Nasser), to address the main challenges these providers are facing. The RNA results indicated that all of North Governorate`s municipalities are suffering from urgent lack of resources including but not limited to fuel, maintenance materials, and sanitizing tools, in addition to the incredible decrease in fees collection (decreased by 90%) which put the continuity of the WASH services of these municipalities are providing at serious risk.
This project is designed to support CMWU and the targeted Municipalities (water and sanitization service providers) in collaboration with CMWU and in line with PWA (Palestinian Water Authority) to ensure continuity and quality of services, avoiding deterioration or collapse of essential services during the COVID-19 crisis, through implementing four main activities as follows: First, the provision of chloride used in the disinfection of the domestic water. The quantities are estimated to cover 196,000 liters. Second, activity will be provision of PPE (Personal protection equipment) and disinfecting materials which include (Manual spray machine, plastic gloves, Protective plastic Suits, Medical masks, medical gloves, sanitization gel, protective outwear; shoes Reflective Vest and so on). This equipment will serve 440 field employees of all targeted municipalities. Furthermore, provision of 388 administrative employees with masks and sanitization gel. Third, provision of operational equipment as spare parts for WASH facilities and installation/maintenance for small facilities which include chloride injection pumps and needed accessories to support operation of water and sanitation station. Last, awareness raising sessions will be provided to field employees and to the public via radio spots and publications.
these activities will directly affect the vulnerable groups (women, elderlies and PWD) positively by enhancing their accessibility to the basic WASH services.
The project is intended to conclude an agreement with local supplier to conduct the assigned rehabilitation works required in worksites. the local supplier shall be able to provide maximum safety conditions in the work sites under Covid19 settings as well as show well developed protocol for safety measures in worksites.
Important to mention that PARC will be applying the special Covid19 Standards of Operations for safety measures. This SoPs were developed in Partner ship with Premiere Urgence international in Gaza.

1 - Needs

On 24th of August 2020 local COVID-19 cases have been detected outside the quarantine centers in GS and the imposed curfew with strict measures. PARC received appeals from several community actors and individuals, including the WASH service providers specially in the north Gaza, this led PARC to conduct a rapid need assessment in the form of questionnaires to the WASH service providers (CMWU/ Related targeted Municipalities) to address the Gaps, potential needs as well as the field of interventions. The rapid need assessment (RNS) was conducted between 12/09/2020 to 17/09/2020 and targeted Jabalya, Bait Lahya, Bait Hanoun and Um Al Nasser Municipalities, as they are highly affected by COVID-19 outbreak (classified as red zone). The assessment concluded, Jabaliya is Inhabited by 262,717 capita ( more than 100,000 in Jabalya camp), 21,900 HH out of the camp, with 60 % poverty and 40 % unemployability rate. It is reported that 18,217 residential units are connected to the sewage network while 3,704 are units not yet connected furthermore, 33 sewage pumps are operating the network, 9 of them operate by fuel. In regards to solid wastes about 15 to 200 ton are daily collected from the city. As for water provision is it reported that 16,016 residential units are connected to the water network while 2,000 units are not yet connected. The network provide water from 25 water wells, 5 of them need a pump to collect water which works on fuel, this network is in urgent need of chloride material for water disinfection and fuel for pumping water to continue its services. This need also is elevated due to the fact that fees collection was decreased by 98 % according to the municipality. For Bait Lahya that is Inhabited by around 100, 000 capita, 20,000 HH, with 70 % poverty and unemployability rate. It is reported that 16,000 residential units are connected to the sewage network while 4,000 units not yet connected,25 sewage pumps stations are operating through the network, 6 of them depending on fuel, for solid wastes about 90 ton are daily collected from the city. In regards to water networks provided by the municipality it is reported that 19,000 residential units are connected to the water network while 1,000 units are not yet connected, the network is provided with water from 33 water wells, 12 water pumps needs fuel to operate. In conclusion and knowing that collection fees is downsized by 90%, there is a need of chlorine and fuel to operate the water and waste services. As for Bait Hanoun that is Inhabited by around 59, 500 capita, 10,000 HH, with 70 % poverty and unemployability rate. 4980 residential units are connected to the sewage network while 850 units not yet connected, 6 sewage pumps stations are operating in the network, 2 of them depends on fuel, for solid wastes about 80-90 ton are daily collected from the city. Regarding water services, 5,700 residential units are connected to the water network, the network provides water from 22 water wells, 8 of which work on fuel. In conclusion and due to the fact that collection fees are decreased by 80%, Bait Hanoun municipality needs chlorine and fuel to operate its services. Finally, UM Al Nasser that is Inhabited by 5, 200 capita, 700 HH, with 90 % poverty and 85 % unemployability rate has two sewage pumps stations that are operating the network, 1 of them depending on fuel the others depends on electricity, for solid wastes about 4-5 ton are daily collected from the city.650 residential units are connected to the water network, the network is provided with water from 3 water wells, 1 of them work on fuel. The fees collection was decreased by 95 % according to the municipality. All that adds up to report the weak capacity of Municipalities to provide water Sewage services, in which there is an urgent need for fuel, chlorine, regular maintenance, and provision of Personal protective Equipment (PPE).

2 - Activities

The project aims to achieve the overall objective “Contribute to enhancing the continuity of WASH service providers in the most affected communities by COVID-19”.Project outcome aims at “Enhancing the Operational capacity of 4 targeted municipalities to cope with service provision shortages due to COVID-19”The project is designed to target 4 Service WASH service providers in the red zone. PARC will work jointly and in participatory manner with the targeted Municipalities and relevant line ministries and PWA and local communities under targeting by using all possible means of communication whether face to face meetings, consultation, interviews to inform their feedback or through virtual meeting if in person meeting were not possible. The project will be achieved by implementing the following activities:1. Formulating a project Technical committee PTC: PTC will be formed in each locality that will include representatives from Community member/ leaders, Municipalities, CBOs, CMWU, governorate representative and other relevant stakeholders. The PTC will be leading and guiding the project in the target locations2. Organize for the coordination and work approval with the local authorities/PWA. 3. Organizing for inauguration/info sessions: field info sessions in the 4 target communities to inform the community of the project information, type of interventions and complaint mechanism.4. Updating and verifying the most urgent needs with CMWU and Municipalities, this include the detailed BOQ for the needed materials/maintenance.5. Coordination with WASH cluster and PWA to avoid duplication with other stakeholder interventions. 6. Initiate the baseline survey: Baseline line survey will be conducted to the 4 service providers, this include their workers and employees. the Baseline will be used as benchmark to measure the project progress and final success. 7. Signing the contracts/agreements with the targeted Service providers.8. Preparing tenders and awarding: Announcing and awarding the maintenance and supply process to local contractor to lead the maintenance works and the provision of inputs with to targeted service providers. The contractors shall be familiar to the targeted location, provide commitment to invest local workers whenever possible, assure the application of Covid19 safety measure and Special SoPs in implementation of projects activities in the worksites that covers the targeted service providers and workers. 9. MEAL activities: the project will have a MEAL plan and MEAL frame with clear and well-set progress indicators. This will include well-established complaint mechanism, satisfaction and impact survey…etc. 10. End-line report: Conduct an End line survey/report to compare the situation and measure impact of the project that will provide main findings, challenges and lessons learnt to feed the final report and next project cycle. Close out and final reporting.

8.6.c - How will feedback be used?

PARC has a mechanism regarding accountability and receiving feedback from beneficiaries; Handling complains and ensuring accountability measures are implemented since the design phase of the project until the handing over of all activities, Feedback is ensured via HAP mechanism that is adopted by PARC which ensures to provide a secure space for targeted population to express their dissatisfaction. This mechanism includes a well-designed complaint form that will be tracked through PARC’s MEAL officer and documented through the MEAL documentation system. All project activities will be followed by the MEAL officer who collects data to develop the lesson learned on two levels; during the implementation and after achieving the project in order to be taken in consideration for achieving the rest of the project in a good manner and the upcoming projects. In 2015, PARC developed its guidance to HAP policy systems with Christian Aid Britain, which includes participation, information sharing, and complaint and response policies. A HAP complaint mechanism will be elaborated at targeted communities and audience will be encouraged to claim their rights. The mechanism allows beneficiaries and community members to provide their feedback and report to PARC in a formal and confidential manner. This is promoted and implemented in coordination local committees (LCs). Hence, a complaint and feedback box will be introduced to VCs, placed in the targeted communities in addition to a generic e-mail, field visits and satisfaction survery, and is easily accessible to community members (males and females). Of course, PARC team will ensure that health and safety measures are taken into consideration throughout the project life cycle.the tools that will be used are mainly the : satisfaction survey, baseline and end line surveys, observation field visits.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

PARC was one of the first organizations to declare and published clear instructions on how work shall be under covid19 settings. Clear instructions were regularly shared with the staff, stakeholders and beneficiaries to apply the safety minimum precautionary measures. Hygiene, disinfectant materials including masks and gloves were placed in the entrance of the PARC premises and all work offices, PARC also distributed disinfectant packages to its local CBOs and field offices. Meeting were decreased to minimum while virtual/online meeting were organized once required. In addition, PARC has developed its Standards of Operations for the safe implementation of activities with commitment to precaution measures and enhanced mitigation approach of risks. PARC staff received special training on how to apply these standards while in office and in field work. Special HR staff member was designated to monitor the process and make sure that the staff apply the social distancing and measures are well applied. PARC continues sharing clear instructions with its staff as well as its beneficiaries about how vulnerable people and people at risk due to be elderly or with chronic diseases and/or have immunity issues to keep safe in their home and that PARC will reach them to provide the required services. PARC has used the from-door-door modality in order to keep minimum people movement to get the assistance. For this specific intervention, PARC will use the contractor modality to minimize people movement. PARC already developed standards of operations for the worksites with the Municipalities. Also, the contractor will be asked to provide certain safety measures for the field workers and any involved actors. PARC is a HPA certified NGO; meaning that PARC is experienced in Core Humanitarian Standards and imposed strict policies for information sharing to ensure do no harm to its target groups and people at risk. PARC also have well developed policies for community engagement and accountability principles and code of conducts.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

PARC has formed a solid popular base among a myriad of communities; urban, peri-urban, rural, Bedouin, refugee camps, etc,in Gaza Strip. This base has been formed through carrying out several projects and voluntary campaigns that created a network of trustees who aid PARC's operations throughout the Gaza Strip, which in return landed PARC a prominent status and well established reputation. Also PARC designed this project in participation with local stakeholders in the targeted locations, that will form the local project committee which will facilitate accessibility of PARC team. PARC will implement the proposed project in full coordination with 4 targeted municipalities, PWA and CMWU and based on a pre-developed COVID 19 emergency plan.
