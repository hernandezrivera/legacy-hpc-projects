1 - Needs

Pre-existing poor infrastructure is exacerbated by damaged roads resulting from the protracted crisis and unprecedented floods in 2022 leaving several bridges collapsed in its wake has significantly increased the need for logistics capacities in the three affected states in northeast Nigeria. Movement of relief items into key operational areas remains limited, access for humanitarian staff is restricted, and some areas remain completely inaccessible by road.

2 - Activities

The Logistics Sector aims to ease logistics bottlenecks and support the humanitarian actors with sufficient and reliable logistics services and consolidated information related to logistics capacities, to foster the capacity of the humanitarian community to deliver relief items to hard-to-reach locations. The Logistics Sector will continue to facilitate access to common storage in six locations in 2023 in Dikwa, Damasak, Banki, Bama, Ngala, and Monguno; while UNHAS will continue to offer a fixed-wing air service between Maiduguri, Yola, and Abuja, as well as helicopter transport, medevac and evacuation services throughout Borno State. Logistics Sector will continue to hold regular coordination meetings and produce maps and information products for humanitarian partners while continuing to provide training and equipment loans to humanitarian partners, as well as providing advice and coordination for access-related issues.
