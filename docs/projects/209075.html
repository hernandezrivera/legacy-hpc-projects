Project Summary

WHO project aims to decrease avoidable under five morbidity and mortality related to Severe acute malnutrition through improved access and quality of inpatient care for the Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) with medical complications in 160 stabilization centers. The project includes curative, preventive and promotive measures integratedly. The activities will address the clinical life-saving interventions in Stabilization centers targeting the children with SAM and the mothers/caretakers through Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) counseling inside the SCs, strengthening baby friendly hospital initiatives, growth monitoring and nutrition promotive interventions including raising awareness of mothers/care takers to lower relapse and prevent SAM cases. The projects’ activities and costing factored in 41 SCs supported by NGOs in Sudan. The SCs Supported by NGOs will require only medical kits (1st and 2nd line medicines and medical supplies) and technical support. Main activities under this HRP project included to: Training of the health and nutrition staff of 124 SCs (all except the ones Supported by NGOs, however the NGOs supported staff can be included based on the request of NGOs). The training included to ToT, initial training (7 days) and refresher Training (5 days). The project will support provision of operation cost to certain SCs (based on fund availability) provision of guidelines, Job Aids and IEC materials to all participants from the 124 SCs (excluding 41 NGOs supported SCs). WHO will procure and distribute medicines (SAM inpatient Kits – first and secondary level) and medical supplies to 160 SCs; based on the needs identified together with MOH. To improve the quality of care and recovery rate in SCs, WHO nutrition and public health officers present in states will conduct joint supportive supervision with SMOH and NGOs parterns on regular bases targeting all SCs including provision of on the job training, coaching to improve adherence to the national treatment protocols. Routine nutrition surveillance to know the trends, identifying hot spot areas, effective planning of activities will be enhanced through training, provision of recording & reporting tools and anthropometric equipment in 18 targeted states. The system is built on the existing information system for better integration into HFs reporting mechanism making sure the sustainability. WHO nutrition team will provide technical and operational support to the MOH at federal and state levels to regularly analysis the nutrition data (using the agreed performance indicators) dis-aggregated by sex and age. The information will be disseminated to relevant stakeholders through nutrition cluster platform. Effective coordination of WHO and partners will promote an improved referral system between the SCs, TSFP, OTP, and community. If the situation permits, the project will undertake small repairs & renovations of 10 Stabilization Centers (SCs) to ensure better environment for work and improved hygiene in the SCs. The total budget for this project is $56,170,806 it doesn’t include the resilience activities which is not included in the total HRP budget.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

WHO has a protection mainstreaming policy that all staff in the field have been trained in, at field level WHO partners with organizations whose programming is wholly protection focused to ensure that beneficiary protection needs are properly addressed. Equally, there is a country child protection focal person who gives the child protection focal points remote support, this ensures WHO is equipped to capture and refer appropriately the protection issues to the best-placed protection partners. World Health Organization during the design phase, implementing SAM inpatient programs and activities, protection implies implementing projects in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the SAM children receiving treatment from malnutrition with medical complications. The principles of protection mainstreaming will be incorporated during the Monitoring and Evaluation. Assessment of the project incorporates the key elements of Protection Mainstreaming, which include Avoiding-causing harm and prioritize safety and dignity, Ensuring meaningful access, Practicing accountability and Promoting participation and empowerment of the stabilization center staff and beneficiaries. A confidential complaints and protection reporting mechanism system is in place and beneficiaries know where to refer or report incidents of rights violations. Feedback on the complaints should be regularly be provided to the beneficiaries
