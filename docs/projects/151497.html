Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing comprehensive protection services to conflict displaced children and women affected by the conflict between the local community and non state armed actors in Harardheere district. It targets families, children and women displaced from Harardheere who have settle in IDP settlements in Galgadud and Mudug regions. The displaced families have scattered in various areas in the two regions. Following the conflict, an inter agency assessment was conducted where it was noted 28,000 people were displaced from Harardhere who fled to Hobyo and Adaado districts. TUOS proposes to provide protection services to the displaced children and women in Hobyo, Dhusamareb and Adaado under the project. Of the displaced more than 400 were unaccompanied and separated children, women, children and the elderly. Cases of GBV have been reported in some of the IDP settlements where the community have settled with lack of WASH facilities, it increase’s protection risks further making them more vulnerable to rape. Following the assessment a number of rape, attempted rape have been reported with the community which also lack access to medical services for the survivors. In addition children are traumatized with fear of retaliation of the militants from the community. In provision of the services TUOS targets to train a core group of women and men as community based child protection committee who will assist in referral of survivors to TUOS and other service providers. Also the project proposes to train a core group of social workers who will be engaged by TUOS in case management and awareness raising. In addition TUOS will also conduct trainings to health workers on clinical case management to enhance and provide timely medical services to survivors in the three target areas.
Furthermore the project will provide interim care centres for unaccompanied and separated children who fled from their families to prevent recruitment by the forces and ex children associated with armed forces and groups who escaped. The centres will provide the children with all their needs until they can be reunited with their families. In addition the project also targets to provide GBV services to affected women and girls from the community and the displaced families. Apart from the services trainings will be conducted to capacitate the community in developing home base solutions in the prevention of GBV, provision of solar lamps and dignity kits. The project will work with all members in the community to enhance protection mechanisms. Also child friendly spaces will be developed in 11 major IDP settlements in Adaado, Hobyo and Dhusamareb. TUOS proposes to construct 6 child friendly spaces which will be used by 5 IDP settlements in Adaado, 3 IDP settlements in Hobyo and 3 in Dhusamareb. The child friendly spaces will be constructed at strategic places where all children from the displaced settlements can access them.

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

Following the displacement of 28,000 people from conflict between militants and local community in Harardhere village, who have moved to various areas in Galgadud and Mudug Region, the proposed project aims at providing protection services and comprehensive GBV services to women and girls and also provides interim care for unaccompanied and separated children and children who have fled from armed groups. TUOS has selected three districts where most of the affected community have moved to where there is security but there is fear of reprisal from the militants. The project activities and objectives are in line with the government strategic plans on promoting safety and protection to vulnerable groups. The project will work with line government authorities and community stakeholders to build on community system or mechanism towards child protection and protection concerns at large. TUOS has been providing interim care centres in Adaado and Dhusamareb where with the experience will be an asset in leading and providing IDTR and UASC services to the affected children from the conflict. The project targets to strengthen linkages to service providers with the community and also train community focal point persons who will be vital in linking survivors with service providers. In addition community representatives will receive training on child protection mechanism and be encouraged in building local systems to promote child protection and reduction of GBV within the community. Before the implementation of the project consultation meetings will be conducted to inform on the community stakeholders on the planned project which aims at providing comprehensive protection services to conflict displaced children and women and strengthen the community resilience exposed to protection risks, and prevent further abuse. Though the project activities will improve the capacity of the displaced community on GBV protection methods hence improving sustainability and reduce risks and increase protection mechanisms and systems.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

South and Central regions of Somalia are gradually recovering from years of sporadic armed conflict, widespread human rights abuses, lack of economic recovery, severe droughts, endemic humanitarian needs, minimal health care and education and population displacement. During this period, many people are displaced: women and children suffer various forms of human rights violations and abuses including rape and other forms of sexual violence, physical violence, abductions and denial of humanitarian access as a result of increased militarization. Moreover, the prevailing conditions forced communities to resort to coping mechanisms that exposed children to child labor and exploitation, recruitment into fighting groups, abandonment and domestic violence. Besides the above protection risks, girls in Somalia are still subjected to harmful cultural practices of Female Genital Mutilation/Cutting - FGM and child marriages. Somalia faces a dire humanitarian situation, having endured drought and famine as well as continued fighting and heavy rains, all of which have aggravated the conditions of IDPs. The IDPs leave their livelihood moving to safe areas without the basic amenities to life and are at risk to protection concerns due to lack of such needs like shelter, education, WASH, health and protection. With sporadic fighting it hinders or prevents humanitarian agencies to provide the much needed assistance to affected communities hence making the situation further complicated. TUOS aims at working with other humanitarian agencies in reducing and preventing GBV and provide protection services to women, girls and children in Adaado, Hobyo and Dhusamareb. The organisation plans to work with the community especially women and girls in designing the implementation of the activities and committees to work with TUOS and the community in the provision of GBV services hence building and bringing about gender inclusion in the project implementation period.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Lack of GBV services have failed to reach the most vulnerable groups in the community leading to more harm. The risk of exclusion based on gender stereo type and age is prevalent in the community. The project targets women, children, girls, elderly, boys and men of all ages. The project targets to provide GBV/protection services to all gender and age groups in the target communities. Unaccompanied, separated and children who fled armed militia groups have been considered where they will be provided housed at the interim care centres which will provide all their basic needs and provide their protection needs protecting them from further risks. Women and girls will be informed on the protection services provided by TUOS and also strengthen linkages to services providers for comprehensive GBV services. In its work experience best lesson learnt by the organisation is the inclusion of all groups in enhancing and reducing protection incidences where women and girls and children are given more assistance for an impactful outcome. Inclusion of male in protection is vital since they are the head of the family and decision makers who will protect their female members from incidences of GBV and work towards enhancing or strengthening security concerns to reduce risk of GBV incidences. TUOS will conduct trainings to enhance the community capacity in reduction of risks and development of community mechanism for protection

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The project target areas especially the target villages are safe to enable the project implementation to its end. However, security situation in the region is volatile thus sporadic conflict and attack might happen. To mitigate the effect of being adversely affected by the conflict when it occurs, TUOS will be working with community security team to protect women and children from attack. Every morning TUOS staff will be updated on security concerns on the target areas to avert any risks to the staff and beneficiaries. Also working with community focal points and stakeholders there will be will analyze security situation and advice all the concern parties. TUOS will work with focal point people who will be from the community who will link GBV survivors for the provision of services reducing security risks to the survivors and the staff. As mitigation the community will be trained on community prevention mechanism on protection will be vital in the reduction of risks. Safety issues are of most top priority at TUOS where we have been conducting protection, nutrition and health projects in Galgadud and Mudug regions hence the same criteria and procedures will be followed in the implementation of the proposed project.
