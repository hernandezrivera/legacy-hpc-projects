Project Summary

The project proposal aims to provide integrated response based on the actual need for the most vulnerable groups in North Syria districts to improve the living conditions for IDPs and response to the rapid displacement waves and the crisis-affected population. Project objectives shall be reached through integrating a set of consistent activities in line with CCCM objectives and under their coordination matrix.
It also will continue provision of humantiatrian live-saving assitances provided in Temporary Transit Centers; Maaret Al Ekwan RC that is managed by SCA to contain rapid displacement waves as it is the sole shelter available for IDPs at the very first times of displacement.
The proposed interventions aim to improve the resilience of IDPs, especially newly IDPs through improving the basic humanitarian infrastructure in camps by the following activities:
- Rehabilitation and maintenance of main and secondary roads inside camps.
- Establishment, rehabilitation and maintenance of rain and floods water drainage systems.
- Tents’ ground insulation using gravels and blocks.
- Camp management training and management activities.
- Maintaining RC and improving living conditions in existing IDP sites.
- Provision of live-saving multi-sector service in RCs, such as temporary shelter, food, protection, tracking IDPs and wash and drinking water besides WASH services.
- Installing solar lighting poles at 9 camps to further assists protection mainstreaming of provided services in camps, and most vulnerable groups of population on camps mostly women, children and people of special needs.
- Establishing and preparing fire points in displacement camps.
This proposal will positively affects population life directly through limiting the spread of diseases during winter, promote people access to facilities, helping children access schools safely, improving the access of children, elderlies and people of special needs, and securing the access of women and girls to facilities at night.
On the long term, the project will limit and reduce negative coping strategies such as joining arme groups, early marriage and abstaining from joining schools.
SCA ensures commitment to SPHERE Standards, International Red Cross Code of Conduct, and Non-governmental Organizations along with the minimum standards of CCCM Cluster.
Also, SCA Committed to full coordination with other sectors in Gaziantep and international humanitarian partners in the same area during the project implementation phase to prevent any overlap or duplication or existing of any uncovered gaps in accordance with 2020 Standard and reserve Allocations.
SCA has the necessary technical and technological experience to implement such activities and has vast experience in CCCM sector , besides, wide experience in implementing such projects in 2019-2020 in 40 camps, 2 districts.

4 - Needs

Based on OCHA's latest updates in late Jan 2020, about 2.5 M are internally displaced in Syria, and 388,887 People displaced in northwest Syria between 1 Dec, 2019 and 27 Jan 2020. This has made too much pressing needs on temporary shelter, as RCs capacity are in deficiency. Besides, many IDPs were forced to desperately seek makeshift camps that lack the basic infrastructure in agricultural lands, suffering from severe access to services, food, water supplies or accessing schools or medical centers when needed. Besides, being hit by floods and rain water during harsh winter conditions. In order to obtain more clear information about the affected population needs, SCA team conducted a realistic needs assessment in Harim and Jisr Ash Shogour districts by following methods: - Reviewing SCA experience and Learned lessons in managing RCs.- Discussions conducted with various groups of newly IDPs. - CCCM floods-reporting tool- Some camps’ managers.After surveying 42 camps in the two districts of 58712 IDPs (10360 HHs of them 13319 men, 16669 women, 13517 boys, and 15313 girls) and found the following:- Extending current camps, RCs or establishing new sites for newly arrivals of IDPs.- Improving the basic infrastructure of makeshift camps to improve IDPs access and protect them from floods, provide winterizations and facilitate helping themselves with water provision, markets access, access to medical points in case of emergencies and to schools by gravelling roads, establishing of culverts and drainage systems to drain floods and rain water out of sites.- Promote resilience and protection of IDPs by lighting the main roads in camps and provide fire points along with forming and training of firefighting committees.- IDPs' Sites management activities for RCs and other existing camps, and tracking displacements and sending updates to Cluster. - Support, train, and implement exit strategies for existing sites.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project aims to provide integrated response based on the actual need for the most vulnerable groups in displacement sites in Idleb and Aleppo Gov to improve the living conditions of IDPs of the crisis-affected population inside the suggested locations. Project objectives shall be reached through integrating a set of consistent activities in CCCM sector. The proposed interventions aim to improve the resilience of IDPs, especially newly IDPs through improving the camp management and implement emergency response to the existing sites- Rehabilitation and maintenance of main and secondary roads inside camps.- Site management activities including management training, IDPs receiving, registering and tracking of displacement. - Installing solar lighting poles further assists protection mainstreaming of provided services, and most vulnerable groups of population on camps mostly women, children and people of special needs.- Supporting, planning and implementing exit strategies from RCs and other sites. - Save squares for children, women and elderlies. - Promoting sites protection and safety by establishment, or rehabilitation of fences. In case of any probable new displacement waves SCA will report to Clusters about the deviation, for mitigation:- Increasing project activities and securing other funds.-Reducing number of targeted sites-Reducing the duration of project.This proposal will positively affect population life directly through limiting the spread of diseases during winter, promote people access to facilities, helping children access schools safely, improving the access of children, elderlies and people of special needs, and securing the access of women and girls to facilities at night.On the long term, the project will limit and reduce negative coping strategies such as joining armed groups, early marriage etc.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: Security Tensions in project implementation areas due to military hostilities and inaccessibility to project implementation area. Impact: HighProbability: low Mitigation: Security situation in the area will be closely monitored and evaluated continuously by SCA team, also, work sites shall be shared with OCHA access team to indicate those locations so in case of any high security risks, consultations with CCCM cluster and OCHA shall be made to make the most suitable decisions of whether to stop or relocate the project to another locations. - Risk: Workers got injured due to engineering and construction works. Impact: low Probability: medium Mitigation: Workers and contractors shall be obliged to follow safety and security procedures and provide protective clothes. - Risk: Decrease in prices, especially diesel due to military, financial and political strikes in North West Syria and the variation of exchange rates.Impact: LowProbability: MediumMitigation: Contracting with contractors and service providers all over the project duration, and in case of any immense change of prices in markets and inability of contractors to meet their obligations, re-examination of current market prices and reconciliation with contractors will be reached. - Risk: Delay in implementing the activities in camps due to weather forces. Impact: LowProbability: MediumMitigation: Restoring infrastructure activities shall be started early, and in case of any delay in implementing the activities, workers numbers and working hours shall be increased with working within holidays. - Risk: Difficulties in money transferring to Syria contractors, and facing delays or obstruction in implementation of project activities.Impact: highProbability: low Mitigation: Contacts shall be made with only contractors who have PTT account in Syria, and this term shall be listed in the contracts and tenders between SCA and contractors.
