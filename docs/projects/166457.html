Project Summary

Save the Children (SC) strategizes its response activities after the COVID-19 appears as pandemic. To ensure the continuity of WASH services in the refugee camps, SC plans to maintain the already constructed Decentralized Waste Water Treatment system (DeWATS) and water supply systems, and desludge latrines. Based on the presumption that refugees have an adequate supply of soap and clean water, SC intends to strengthen the provision of community outreach on hygiene practices through maintaining social distancing. Additionally, SC involves volunteers to disinfect the surfaces to restrict the spread of COVID-19.

Needs

The 855,000 Rohingya Refugees who are currently residing in 34 overcrowded, makeshift camps in Cox’s Bazar are highly vulnerable to COVID-19. The overcrowded and unhygienic conditions increase the potential for the rapid spread of disease. The use of communal hygiene facilities can be the potential mortality and morbidity risk associated with COVID-19. All water and hygiene facilities provided in the camps are public, and these are often overcrowded conditions which increase the risk of COVID-19 transmission. As disease prevention hinges on firmly entrenched WASH practices, there is a critical need to ensure improved water service, sanitation, hygiene promotion and community engagement. The provision of safe water, sanitation, hygienic living and environmental conditions are essential to protect human health during all infectious disease outbreaks, including the COVID-19 outbreak. To ensure smooth water supplies and hygiene practices, repairs and maintenance of existing WASH structures along with distribution of soaps and detergents are highly needed for the refugees to limit the outbreak if it occurs. Additionally, installing or repairing of handwashing facilities in the proximity of functional water sources and at multiple service points (health posts, learning centers, community spaces, nutition or food distribution points, etc.) are highly required. Moreover, correct handwashing with soap is considered sufficient to ensure clean hands and to prevent the spread of germs.

Activities or outputs

In addition to regular WASH activities in the camps, SC is planning to implement the following activities in responding COVID-19 crisis - • Operation and maintenance of DeWATS• Operation and maintenance of water supply systems• Desludging of latrines• Distribution of laundry soap• Distribution of handwashing soaps• Door-to-door hygiene messaging (maintaining social distancing) through volunteers• Procurement of HTH (Chlorine) containers• Disinfecting of surfaces through involving volunteers

Key project/performance indicators

(a) # of DeWATS maintained (Target: 1). (b) # of times water supply systems repaired and maintained (Target: 180). (c) # of bathing spaces and latrines repaired (Target: 360).
