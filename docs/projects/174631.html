Project Summary

Provide a humanitarian response for effective community resilience, reduce the risks and mitigate the impact of the explosive ordnance and weapons used in the Malian conflict in the regions of Gao, Kidal, Menaka, Timbuktu, Mopti, and Segou, by assisting the population affected by the conflict.
MAG aims to achieve this through 1) delivering person-to-person risk education, 2) conduct mass media campaigns, 3) identifying suspected contaminated areas, 4) facilitate the referral of victims, and 5) promoting sustainability through local capacity.
The entire population is at risk from explosive ordnance and weapons in the conflict areas. However, road users, internally displaced persons, returnees, refugees, nomadic groups, road users, and children, and NGO workers are considered to be particularly at risk and will be targeted accordingly.
Firstly, person-to-person risk education will aim to change behavior by providing information on the threat posed by explosive ordnance and weapons and how to mitigate them. These risk education sessions will be gender and age-sensitive and tailored to be delivered to women, girls, boys and men by gender balanced teams.
Secondly, to increase the outreach of risk education messages even in the most remote areas, MAG will launch a mass communication campaign to share safety messages to further encourage the development of safe behavior toward explosive devices and small arms and light weapons.
Thirdly, MAG will conduct a non-technical survey to identify areas suspected of being contaminated by explosive ordnance which will reduce immediate risk for the population, and facilitate clearance. This exercise will also help identify areas which may be more at risk and thus requiring special attention.
Also, MAG will facilitate referral to appropriate assistance for victims of explosive ordnance and weapons and their families. This will be achieved through direct support, and by providing information to service providers allowing them to also provide information directly to the victims and their family.
Finally, MAG will develop the outreach and sustainability of gender-sensitive initiatives. This will be achieved by developing and strengthening the capacity of Malian civil society organizations and to work with the education system, public, private and informal in topics related to mine action and weapons.
