Project Summary

1. Diagnosis /Rapid Assessment on existing community self help groups;
2. Assess disability and gender inclusion in existing self help groups
3. Strengthen mainstream self-help groups through specific trainings, including on disability inclusion and GBV
4. Support integration of persons with disabilities, including women with disabilities, in self help groups
5. Develop a Community Alarm System (CAS) to identify and report cases of violence, violation of Human Rights and rights and discrimination in the acces of services for Persons with Disabilities, including women and girls with disabilities, through trained community Activists and self help group members.
6. Strengthen knowledge and capacity of local DPOs, governement and local authorities on disability inclusion (monitoring and advocacy), and on the CAS.
7. Community Sensitization through radio programes on PwD rights and Inclusion
