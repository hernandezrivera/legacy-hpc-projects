Project Summary

This overall project aims to complement the existing emergency coordination and programming in relation to the Ethiopia refugee response while building on best practices from UN partner’s humanitarian interventions in Sudan. The central component of this project is the establishment of multi-purpose Safe Centres in each of the refugee settlements to address Ethiopian refugee emergency protection issues with a particular focus on prevention and response to SGBV. Building a space for all humanitarian and refugee response partners to work together creates a coordinated, gender sensitive and survivor centred approach to emergency SGBV prevention and response through holistic services for: awareness raising, psychosocial and legal aid services, conflict mitigation and social cohesion related interventions. This 2-pronged ‘GBV prevention and response’ approach to carrying out protection activities aims to redouble existing efforts by creating a safe and secure space that incorporates Ethiopia and local Sudan culture and traditions.

1 - Needs

This project will focus on the critical emergency SGBV components of the overall UN Women project in addition to ensuring that gender is mainstreaming throughout the refugee response: legal aid; psychosocial support; peaceful co-existence, protection from sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and strengthening of gender equality programming, including gender responsive camp management, governance and coordination

2 - Activities/Outputs

The expected result is the protection/empowerment of displaced Ethiopian refugee women and girls in Sudan with the aims to meet the immediate, lifesaving needs of refugee women and girls while laying the groundwork for survivors of such violence, their families and their communities to recover as well as access services and adapt to their new lives in Sudan. The expected outputs will include: • Women and girls access survivor centred emergency protection including SGBV and livelihood services.• Improved access of refugee women and girls to safe, equitable and trusted mechanisms to prevent and to respond to SGBV and SEA violations.• Strengthened gender responsive camp management and service provision to refugee women and girls (alternative formulation) Strengthened engagement of women leaders and women’s organizations in refugee camp management and coordination for a gender-responsive refugee response.These activities have been defined as follows: • Direct court representation of survivors accessing legal redress• Referral for emergency services including health and psychosocial• Training of women leaders on mediation skills• Community dialogues on conflict resolution and mitigation• Capacity building for refugee community management structures to support women and girls in decision making• Conduct legal awareness to facilitate access to legal remedies• Convene monthly forum for women to advance issues affecting women and girls in the refugee camps • Training on IASC guidelines on PSEA• Train refugees on peer counselling• Screen 1000 refugees for SGBV psychosocial support

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project will focus on the critical emergency SGBV components of the overall UN Women project in addition to ensuring that gender is mainstreaming throughout the refugee response: legal aid; psychosocial support; peaceful co-existence, protection from sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and strengthening of gender equality programming, including gender responsive camp management, governance and coordination. The project sees the engagement of men and boys as vital to addressing SGBV concerns especially physical and emotional violence, sexual abuse and assault and early/ forced marriage. This project will engage refugee and host community men and boys through SGBV awareness-raising sensitization activities, convening facilitated dialogues to resolve conflicts; hold discussions on their experiences and concerns; and will have access to the information and services to address issues as they arise.
