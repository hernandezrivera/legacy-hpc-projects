Project Summary

ChildFund Mozambique, based in Maputo City, has its interventions in the Zavala district, Inhambane Province and in the Kamavota district, Maputo City. The implementation of activities in these places is carried out through two local partners, the Txivuno Tchavanana Association (ATTV) in the Zavala district and Reencontro in the Kamavota district. ChildFund interventions are focused in the areas of Economic Empowerment, Health, Nutrition, Parental Education and Child Protection.
ChildFund will ensure that affected communities have access to safe, child friendly and confidential mechanisms to raise cases of child abuse and should be aware of how to report cases of neglect, exploitation and other forms of violence. Furthermore, ChildFund will partnership with the Child Helpline, ECD Network, ATTV, Reencontro, Department of Gender, Children and Social Affairs, PRM and Centro de Atendimento Integrado.
The activities are as following:
(1) Capacity building Session on Child Protection Risks and solutions, Child Safeguarding mechanisms and mental health Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) needs for children. Participant are Health Technicians, caregivers, community leaders and Community Development Agents.
(2) Produce and allocation of complaint boxes.
(3) Train Child Protection Community Committees on complaint boxes management in coordination with Child helpLine.
(4) Produce Spots, community Radio messages on CP related risks, child help line and exiting hotlines (including Child helpline – 1116) that children and other community members can use to report cases related to violence against children in the context COVID-19 interventions as well as to provide PSS.
(5) Produce and broadcasting COVID-19 prevention message targeting Early Child Development (messages on PSS for children and caregivers, Nutrition, Hygiene and Child Protection, Organization and planning of the Daily routine, and playful activities that parents can develop at home, Education). In coordination with ECD Network (RDPI).
(6) Produce IEC materials on Child Protection friendly messages (Poster, Flyers, Brochure, Roll-ups, banners, billboards).
(7) Support capacity Building of Law and Enforcement Agents, including PRM and community -based policing structures in the context of State of Emergency and Human Rights.
(8) Purchase and distribute Basic Basket Kits Support for 1500 Vulnerable Children (Rice, Sugar, beans, Peanut, Salt, Oil, Soap -Bars, Bleach, Buckets - with Taps, Masks) in coordination with Reencontro and Department of Gender, Children and Social Affairs. This activity is to address the economic and social vulnerability of vulnerable children such as children with disabilities and those who are heading families to respond the food insecurity and other basic children needs.
