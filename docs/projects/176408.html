Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to mitigate famine by providing emergency life-saving and resilience building livelihoods support to 3200 HHs ((1600 HHs in Ngauro and 1600 HHs in Loudo) in Budi county where the cumulative effects of natural stressors including flooding, outbreaks of desert locust, fall armyworm and COVID-19 have pushed over 24556 people into IPC level 3.
Inline with the FSL-cluster's HRP objectives, SSARO seeks to provide emergency food production kits (vegetable seeds, cereal crops seeds farming tools) and training on pest and disease control and management to 3200 households in Nguaro and Loudo payams of Budi county.
The vegetables will help households diversify diets required to mitigate malnutrition and related metabolic diseases among the children, elderly, lactating mothers and people with disabilities. The cereal seeds will help the households fill the grain gaps that cause food insecurity. The farming tools will improve household farming efficiency and pest and disease controll trainings will help the household save on-farm yields in the midst of yield devasting desert locusts and fall armyworm.
Ngauro and and Loudo payams lie in the remote highland forest of the greenbelt agroecological which receives an average rainfall of 1600 mm per annum. However, the rainfalls were the heaviest in 2020 leading to flooding and destruction of crop yields.
The highland forest in the area provides conducive habitats to desert locusts (DL) and fall armyworm (FAW) to colonize, feed and lay eggs in the area. The net effects of these pests led to complete devastation of crop yields in 2020 leading to severe humanitarian needs (level 4 of HNO) in Budi county
The food insecurity is also compounded by disruptions associated with COVID-19 which prevented shops restocking, household incomes and flow of essential household inputs including seeds, fertilizers, pesticides and labourforce
Besides the provision of household food production kits, SSARO team will provide vegetable farming trainings by establishing demonstration plots in all bomas of the payams to help housholds maximize yields.
.The project is designed to only target payams of Budi county that lack road networks required to deliver FAO provided seeds. Bomas like Mehira, Bakka and Lorema lack road networks. However, SSARO team has a great experience working in the area, provideing desert locust awareness to the local communities throughout the whole of 2020. The project is designed to get the synergy of ongoing SSARO desert locusts control and management program that has the partnerships of FAO and Crop Protection Department of the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security (MAFS).
SSARO will use protection against sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA), gender-based violence (GBV), human rights, transactional HIV as the cross-cutting themes in this project if it is awarded.

1 - Needs

Over 18324 HHs live in acute food insecurity crisis or phase 3 of the IPC. Of these households, 4832 HHs are found in the remote areas of Ngauro and Loudo payams of Budi county where humanitarian needs is severe.(IPC, 2020 ; HNO, 2021). The key drivers of acute food insecurity crisis are subnational violence, outbreaks of desert locusts (DL) and fall armyworm (FAW), flooding, disruptions associated with COVID-19, economic crisis and lack of road networks to the area. Subnational violence is triggered by cattle raiding among the Didinka and Topoda tribes. In 2020 alone, more than six cattle raids occurred creating insecurity which impeded subsistence farming, yet subsistence agriculture in the area accounts for 90% of grain and seed requirements (FEWNETS, 2020). Currently over 3200 households in Ngauro and Loudo payams face famine in the lean season of April to July 2021. The food insecurity crisis in the area is compounded by outbreaks of desert locusts DL) and Fall armyworm (FAW). Report from FAO suggests that desert locusts account for 30% yield destruction while fall armyworm accounts for 60%yield losses (FAO, 2020).The infestations in Nguaro , Bakka and Mehira is enabled by the highland forest which serves as the habitats for desert locusts (DL) and fall armyworm (FAW) feeding, ovipoistion and colonization (FEWNETS, 2020).Recent desert locusts impact assessment indicates that local communities lack skills in integrated pest management approaches, yet desert locusts monitoring and surveillance tools of FAO still project that the desert locusts (DL) and fall armyworm (FAW) may continue to colonize the area in the next two years if no appropriate measures are taken . This calls for an urgent need for integrated households pest management trainings that empower the locals with cost-effective and farmer- friendly pest control and management approaches. The disruptions associated with COVID-19 forced the households to deplete virtually all the coping mechanisms to the breaking thresholds. There were disruptions in household incomes, and flow of essential household inputs including seeds, fertilizers, pesticides and labourforce. In Ngauro, household incomes is obtained through gold mining, this was interrupted by COVID-19 imposed travel bans. Similarly, essential household incomes including seeds and pesticides come from neighbouring Uganda and Kenya in small quantities that are affordable to individual household members, however, border restrictions impeded movements across regional borders.(FAO, 2020). Budi is poverty prone area, statistics from the national bureau of statistics indicate that the poverty prevalence in the area stands at 64% which is much higher than ever estimated (NBS, 2019). The poverty trends have been on the rise since 2016 to date. According to humanitarian needs overview, 4 out of 5 persons in Budi county live below the international poverty line (HNO, 2021). The hard hit areas are Loduo and Ngauro county which lack road networks. The poverty rates is due to worsen due to consistent crop failure and declining on-farm yields The yields of key food security crops such as sorghum and maize have dropped to 0.4 and 0.5 t/ha (FAO, 2019). The declining yields can only be overcomed by providing early maturing and high yielding seeds of cereal staple crops while diversifying household diet sources through the provision of vegetable seeds. Farming efficiency needs to be imooved too by providing farming tools to 3200 HHs in poverty prone payams of Budi county. The growing poverty rates often aggravate gender-based violence (GBV), sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), spread of transactional HIV, crimes rates and cattle wrestling (WHO, 2020). It is estimated that 1 in every 3 women in Budi experiences violence in her lifetime. SSARO seeks to mitigate these problems by providing PSEA, GBV, human rights as cross-cutting themes in this project.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

SSARO uses PSEA as a thematic area in all it's running project. We have a specially designed PSEA manual at desks of our staff. We have empowered all our staff in spotting PSEA and reporting them in real time to SSARO administration. This is enabled by use of whistleblowing email and telephone lines. SSARO is a partner to Durch Caolition of Humanitarian Innovation (DCHI) which dictates that all member organizations prioritize PSEA and GBV. SSARO provides that all staff relay the PSEA instantly on occurrence to the top administration. We also offer PSEA training to our beneficiaries. We give them tailored questions and answers to enable spot potential PSEA cases at the all locations and project sites

SSARO has always excel in receiving and incorporating opinions of people with disabilities into its project planning and running.
