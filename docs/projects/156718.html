Start Date

28/02/2019

End Date

29/12/2019

Project Summary

Provide life-saving assistance to local communities in targeted area, suffering from illness, health conditions, and conflict-related injuries, through launching and running 60 ambulances covering targeted areas in northern Syria, to improve access of the conflict-affected population in Idleb governorate to the essential health care and referral.
The support of an ambulatory system will allow a maximization of the existing health resources in a conflict situation by providing life-saving services to the patients, including:
1. Increasing the coverage scope of medical evacuation using the ambulatory system.
Enhancing the efficient utilization of the referral system through applying TRIAGE to hospitals to emergency cases only.
Enhancing the response speed by linking the ambulances’ systems with a communication network that would contribute to a better use of existing resources and increased coordination.
This project suggests the formation of an ambulatory system of 60 existing ambulances.

Needs

Throughout 2018, health needs in Syria continued to be exacerbated by access constraints, damage to health facilities, and insufficient human resources. The latter are a particular barrier to health care in hard-to-reach areas where 8 per cent of people in need are currently located. In addition, many areas that witnessed escalation in hostilities and changes in control are suffering from disruption of critical health services, access constraints, and an increased need for humanitarian interventions. The health system struggles to respond to trauma and rehabilitation needs. High numbers of trauma-related cases continue to be reported across Syria, particularly in north-western and north-eastern Syria. An estimated 10.2 million Syrians are at risk of explosive hazards, further increasing vulnerability of people to traumatic injury. as it appeared in Health Sector HNO Narrative draft, A lot of medical and paramedical staff displaced to another area caused a big gap in ambulatory services and life saving services and system. In addition, in 2019, an escalation of hostilities is anticipated in a number of areas in north-eastern and north-western Syria, necessitating further support to trauma centres and referral systems. The ambulance system will be implemented in Idlib for the following reasons: 1. Security conditions are relatively stable. 2. A high number of people in need who are not receiving adequate health services.3. Large numbers of IDP's in the targeted locations.4. The volume of vulnerable groups, like women and children, who are in urgent need of health service. 5. High poverty levels in the targeted communities which hampers their access to paid health services.

Implementation of activities

Output 1.1: Supporting and running of ambulance network of 60 ambulance vehicles(which is working now in idlib governorate under the umbrella of idlib health directorate) that will provide emergency treatment to trauma injuries, critical medical cases referral and transportation between medical points, PHCs and hospitals in rural Aleppo. Activity 1.1.1: Running ambulatory system including ambulances, equipment, operation room, communication network, and recruitment ambulatory service providersActivity 1.1.2: Mobilize the patients to hospitals and provide the essential lifesaving procedures for them during transportation.Output 1.2: Building capacity, resilience and strengthening the referral system by enabling ambulatory service providers to acquire latest related knowledge and practices allowing them to respond to beneficiaries’ requirements in line with project objectivesActivity 1.2.1: Conduct basic life support training for ambulance service providers Activity 1.2.2: Conduct advanced training in emergency aid and mass causality response
