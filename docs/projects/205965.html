Project Summary

In the proposed action, Save the Children aim to implement integrated WASH interventions to reduce the burden of WASH related diseases as aggravating factors for malnutrition locations presenting an extreme WASH vulnerability and high prevalence of malnutrition. Services provided will break the transmission cycle of main communicable diseases (i.e. diarrhea), thus improving food intake of malnourished children and contributing to the sustainability of health and nutrition. The proposed action will also seek to address WASH needs in during emergencies such as response to floods, returnees and disease outbreaks. The proposed interventions will target WASH Cluster priority 1 and 2 counties of Akobo Abyei, and Lankien in Nyirol counties in which Save the Children has already established good operational presence.
The proposed WASH activities include; Distribution of Dignity/Hygiene kits among SAM/MAM children and caregiver families, hygiene promotion sessions conducted at Nutrition sites and catchment areas, construction /rehabilitation gender-disaggregated latrine facilities at nutrition site with consideration of People with Special Needs based on based on area analysis and if required, latrines constructed/rehabilitated as per WASH Cluster guidelines with flood resilient designs, construction /rehabilitation of water sources/boreholes at nutrition site and catchment areas with consideration of People with Special Needs based on based on area analysis and if required, water sources/boreholes will be constructed/rehabilitated as per WASH Cluster guidelines with flood resilient designs. water quality monitoring, safety audit for construction of borehole/handpump/water yard drilling/rehabilitation, mobile Sanitation Teams deployed to clear environment from faeces, dissemination of IEC material and using mass media for hygiene promotion, MHM kits distribution to IDPs and returnees distribution of WASH Emergency Kit (buckets, aquatabs, PuR Sachets, filter cloth and soap) during Emergency response Safe water sources developed (rehab/new) as per the WASH Cluster guidelines for flood resilient structures, barrier analysis conducted for disability inclusion, hygiene promotion and construction of latrines in communities using CLTS approach
Save the children targets a total of 191,887 beneficiaries (M: 32,621, F:36459, B: 61,403, G: 61405) across the five counties. The estimated cost to deliver the proposed WASH interventions is USD 2,329741.

1. Needs

In South Sudan, WASH needs have been exacerbated by displacements, insecurity, returnees, economic downturn and historically low coverage of water infrastructure. Increasing insecurity, both ethnic and politically motivated conflict, coupled with localized violence and disputes over land, public health issues (including waterborne diseases), and extreme climatic events (i.e. flooding and drought) has been an underlying driver of WASH conditions in almost all states, limiting the ability of service providers to deliver WASH services while also restricting people’s access to existing WASH infrastructure. According to the HNO, 9.4 million people need assistance (24% women, 54% children, 15% People with Disabilities (PWD). Large-scale displacement is also affecting the country (2.3 million Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs). Furthermore, due to Sudan’s conflict (April 2023), 363,458 individuals crossed the border to South Sudan (50% female, 50.34% children); of those, 83% are South Sudanese returnees. About 84% of new arrivals enter through the Joda/Renk border (most common final destinations include Upper Nile, and Jonglei). The situation is compounded by the arrival of returnees from Ethiopia (fleeing Gambella and satellite refugee camps due to intercommunal conflicts and lack of assistance), mostly going to places of origin in Jonglei and Upper Nile.

2. Project activities

The proposed WASH activities include; Distribution of Dignity/Hygiene kits among SAM/MAM children and caregiver families, hygiene promotion sessions conducted at Nutrition sites and catchment areas, construction /rehabilitation gender-disaggregated latrine facilities at nutrition site with consideration of People with Special Needs based on based on area analysis and if required, latrines constructed/rehabilitated as per WASH Cluster guidelines with flood resilient designs, construction /rehabilitation of water sources/boreholes at nutrition site and catchment areas with consideration of People with Special Needs based on based on area analysis and if required, water sources/boreholes will be constructed/rehabilitated as per WASH Cluster guidelines with flood resilient designs. water quality monitoring, safety audit for construction of borehole/handpump/water yard drilling/rehabilitation, mobile Sanitation Teams deployed to clear environment from faeces, dissemination of IEC material and using mass media for hygiene promotion, MHM kits distribution to IDPs and returnees distribution of WASH Emergency Kit (buckets, aquatabs, PuR Sachets, filter cloth and soap) during Emergency response Safe water sources developed (rehab/new) as per the WASH Cluster guidelines for flood resilient structures, barrier analysis conducted for disability inclusion, hygiene promotion, construction of latrines in communities using CLTS approach, soap distribution and mitigation of Gender Based Violence, GBV through training of WASH staff on basic skills to provide survivors with information on where they can obtain support and GBV messages (including where to report risk and how to access care) are incorporated into hygiene promotion and other WASH related community outreach activities using multiple formats to ensure accessibility. The proposed action will adopt mitigation measures that address barriers to access to WASH services for people with disabilities and the elderly. To better understand the needs of the people living with disabilities and address their barriers to accessing WASH services, the WASH cluster Disability Inclusion checklist will be updated, and the findings used to inform the program design. Priority communities and villages will be selected in consultation with WASH cluster subnational coordinator, WASH cluster partners and with support of the Nutrition partner. Communities and villages with nutrition facilities with and no/limited access to WASH services, highest GAM prevalence and diarrheal disease incidence based on data available at nutrition facilities and sourcing drinking water from an unsafe source with a high risk of contamination with human excreta will be selected. Within the selected communities and villages, the most vulnerable households will be targeted. The most vulnerable households within the community are the households with a pregnant and lactating woman, female-headed household, households with a disabled and/or elderly, etc.
