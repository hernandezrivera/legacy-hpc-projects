Project Summary

This project aims to mitigate the rising rate of drug abuse among students in 10 schools in Buea, Cameroon, through a $50,000 targeted intervention. Its purpose is to educate students, teachers, and parents about the dangers of drug abuse as negative effects of the ongoing anglophone crisis, build capacity for prevention and early intervention, and promote positive engagement through extracurricular activities.
The initiative will train peer educators, establish anti-drug clubs, provide counseling and support services, and engage students in sports, arts, and debates to foster a drug-free culture. Stakeholder workshops will empower parents, teachers, and community leaders to collaborate in addressing drug abuse. The project targets over 5,000 students, creating a sustainable framework for preventing and managing drug-related challenges in schools.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project aims to mitigate the rising rate of drug abuse among students in 10 schools in Buea and reaching out to over 10,000 students by addressing the following assessed needs: 1. Trauma and Mental Health Challenges:Many students, particularly those displaced by the ongoing Anglophone crisis, experience significant trauma due to violence, displacement, and loss of family members. These experiences drive some to seek solace in drug use. This project will address this need by providing psychosocial support through counseling services, peer education, and safe spaces for emotional expression. 2. Lack of Awareness About Drug Abuse Risks:Students, teachers, and parents often lack adequate knowledge about the dangers of drug use and how to prevent it.Awareness campaigns and educational sessions will increase understanding of drug-related risks and equip stakeholders with tools to counter peer pressure and misinformation. 3. Inadequate Support for Displaced and Vulnerable Children:More than 200 displaced, unaccompanied, or separated children who have fled from Mamfe, Muyuka, and the environ of Buea face heightened risks of isolation and substance abuse due to the absence of parental guidance and economic hardships. This project will specifically target these groups through inclusive activities, mentorship programs, and access to counseling services. 4. Prevalence of Unsafe and Unregulated School Environments:Incidents such as a student attempting to stab a teacher, hallucinating in class and violence as recorded in GHS Buea while under the influence of drug, highlight the urgent need for safer school environments. Training peer educators and school staff will enhance early detection of at-risk behaviors and promote conflict resolution strategies. 5. Limited Extracurricular and Engagement Opportunities:A lack of structured extracurricular activities leaves students idle and more susceptible to negative influences like drug abuse. This project will address this by organizing sports, arts, and debates to positively channel students' energy and foster a drug-free culture. 6. Insufficient Support for Parents and Teachers:Parents and teachers are often ill-equipped to detect and respond to drug use or support traumatized students. Stakeholder workshops will build their capacity to create supportive environments at home and in schools. 7. Stigma and Fear Around Seeking Help:Many students may avoid seeking help for drug-related issues due to stigma or fear of punitive measures or being regarded as outcasts. By establishing non-judgmental counseling services and referral systems, this project will encourage early intervention and support for at-risk students. By addressing these needs, this project aims to reduce drug abuse, enhance school safety, and foster resilience among students, ultimately creating a healthier and more supportive school environment.
