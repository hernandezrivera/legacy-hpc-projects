Project Summary

The project seeks to alleviate hunger by meeting immediate food needs of the most food inscure households while investing in actions to support livelihoods recovery. The project targets men, women, girls and boys from the most vulnerable families with both conditional and unconditional cash transfers and cash for work initiatives. The project will also incorporate capacity building initiatives on household food production using climate smart agricultural practices and food utilization. The project also recognizes the gendered dimensions of food insecurity hence will seek to address gender equality with regards to access to food, household income and productive resources.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

The project is premised upon the Humanitarian Needs Overview and other assessments like the rural and urban ZimVAC. PIZ has also conducted various assessments alone and in partnership with other organizations e.g. the April 2020 Cash and Voucher Assistance Feasibility Study which was done in partnership with ACF and IRC. Such consultative assessments have formed the basis of this intervention and PIZ will ensure accountability to communities and prioritise the integration of community feedback into the response. PIZ will use multiple child friendly, safe, gender-sensitive and accessible channels for community feedback and complaints mechanisms (CFMs) which will include; community dialogue sessions, Focus Group Discussions (FGD’s); suggestion boxes, help desks and toll free lines. These channels will ensure that all beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries in all their diversity have an opportunity to provide feedback on the response. All feedback will be categorized to ensure quick identification and rapid attention to all concerns raised especially those on safeguarding and SEA. To ensure that the processes are secure, transparent and independent, PIZ will work together with the respective community structures such as Village Development Committees (VDCs) and will have clear systems for documenting, reporting, referring and responding to communities during community meetings

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project is not expected to have a massive negative impact on the environment as most support will be provided through cash based transfers. However, a few livelihoods activities might have minimal impact on the environment hence mitigation measures will be put inplace. The project will start by conducting an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) for all livelihoods activities such as rehabilitation of irrigation schemes or construction of wier dams. The Environmental Management Agency will play a leading role in the conducting of the EIA. The project will also ensure that soil and moisture conservation works are put in place and also create designated places for safe disposal of waste.
