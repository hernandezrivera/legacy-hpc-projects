Project Summary

In line with the strategic objectives of the HRP, SR project for 2024/2025 aims at i) providing equitable access to education ii) supporting quality education in protective environment, and iii) strengthening education systems, including empowering
communities and building partner capacities to support education service delivery.SR currently supports 89 under Manahel in Ariha,Jisr Ashugur and Maaret Tamsrin till 2027 and 9 under AFNS in Ariha and jisr Ashugur until Jul-24. The project seeks to support 8 schools in Aleppo(Atareb subdistrict) and in idleb(Bennsh,Dana,Idleb,Janudieh) marked with 4 PiN Severity.The 8 schools have not been supported by any actor for 3 years.The staff work as volunteers and children receive education services at min. From Jan-24 Dec-25 ,the project aims to deliver FE(SiG curricula) to 5434 children(2042 girls, 3392 boys graded 5-12,aged 11-17 including 108 CWDs) during the academic year.The same 5434 children will have NFE(remedial/catchup)in the summer club June-September in each year.The schools will receive BTLs to advocate for education,capacity building to the staff, winterization for a warm school environment,minor repairs to improve the access with a focus on the needs of the CWDs,provision of furniture and school supplies, solar panels and safety equipment,and monthly stipends to the school staff. The areas may witness military escalation by GoS that lead to injuries/victims. The likelihood of such incidents remain a matter of conflict status.For the other contextual risks like inter-armed clashes, IED explosions and kidnapping they currently remain in their almost minimal levels due to the de facto authorities control over different aspects of life including security issues. However, still the risk of theft and burglary are generally highly present in Greater Idlib whenever/wherever points of weakness can be found and made use of.
SR will ensure the project staff attend 3 days GESI training on gender and social inclusion.
The reporting will include data disaggregated by gender. A safe and accessible places will be available in the project locations for all genders and groups as there is no mixing in the schools. As per ED directions, schools are separate for boys and girls.So,all genders will have safe and accessible space.As the project has adults of the education personnel and adolescents aged 13-17,SR will refer any GBV cases during the project activities to the protection actors in the area to follow the GBV cluster SoPs and referral guidelines.All 177 teachers will receive training on SIR to ensure applying "Do no harm” through maintaining confidentiality of GBV cases and keeping their relative databases anonymous.
SR provides communication methods to enable all BNFs submit their feedback, complaints, suggestions by WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype, and email for all groups,including PWDs.PSEA complaints will have a different channel as there are 2 emails through which the PSEA FPs Turkey Office can be reached directly.PSEA complaints will be discussed directly with management to make a proper action,the same for CSG and GBV complaints.SR will recruit 1 male MEAL staff and 1 female accountability assistant to reach the different targeted groups including women, girls, men, boys, illiterate, PwDs, and elderly by open a dialogue.This action aim to receive the feedback from BNFs if they are not able to reach the accountability channels.
The assumptions are that schools open and children are already enrolled,security conditions are stable with no school attacks ,EDs are supportive,parents are cooperative. To mitigate any potential risk, SR will coordinate with the stakeholders(Cluster,NGOs,Eds,local community),sign MoUs with education authorities,raise awareness on evacuation and safety measures,advocate for the importance of education through the BTLs and the awareness messages.PTAs will be established in each schools and have 10 representatives from the parents,kids,staff.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The schools will absorb 5434 IDP out of school children 2042 girls, 3392 boys ,108 CwDs aged 11-17 graded 5-12 from both genders, in addition to155 teachers ,4 PWDs, where the teacher pupil ration will be 35 children per Teacher ,8 head teachers, 8 cleaners and 8 guards who will be recruited based on a fair hiring criteria.Teachers will be paid monthly incentives according to days worked as tracked by timesheets. As a plan A, SR will deliver physical education based on the EDs decisions taking into consideration the hygiene practices for children and staff.However, home-based education will be as a plan B if an emergency situation occurred(distance learning,homebased education) .SR will provide a capacity building through trainings and improved learning circles to school staff as relevant and needed and further coaching and mentoring to improve professional development of teachers within each school, with the collaboration of teachers, school management and relevant education authorities.FE(SIG curricula) will be delivered during the academic year for the 5434 children who will have NFE(remedial/catchup)in in summer Jun-Sep.SR will provide 5 remedial classes (2 Arabic, 2 Math and 1English) in collaboration with the ED, in addition to at least 1 recreational PSS session and1 SEL skill session that will be done either online using online platforms for children with access to electronic devices.The CWD will be able to access learning through the proper infrastrucure like ramps and handrails and the suitable toilets in the WASH facililties.CWD will be placed in the front desks to see well and follow up better.Teachers will pay a special care to the CWD to make them like the school and socialize with their classmates. BTL campaigns will target the 8 schools for key messages on the importance of learning.There will be classroom Observation for overseeing the teachers and support them.Teachers will form Teachers Learning circles in each school for exchanging the experiences and teaching CWD will have an access to education through including them in the schools among their peers. There will be minor repairs for the schools infrastructure,focusing on the needs for the CWDs: ramps and reels for them in addition to special WASH gender sensitive facilities.The children will be provided with the school supplies,stationery for teachers and children,school furniture to improve their access to schools. SR will supply the schools with the winterization in the cold winter to ensure the attendance of children and mitigate the dropout rates. The PTAs will be platforms for sharing the feedback of the parents in a democratic way for improving the learning outcomes.
