Project Summary

Based on the HNO 2022 draft, and under the 2nd strategic objective for the HRP, the project aims to increase the safety and dignity for 17,560 of the most vulnerable people in need in Rural Damascus and Al Hassakeh in alignment with the protection sector priorities and severity scale (4, and 5) in addition to the MSNA 2021.
The project implementation will be in two phases:
The first phase for 2022: will be focused on increasing awareness and providing immediate support to vulnerable people:
The work structure in each location will include: local Partner mobile team and a stationed teams inside safe space accessible to PWDs via soft-rehabilitation with TDH technical supervisors and field Coordinators), in addition to SARC distribution teams in Camps.
The first step will include
- Series of technical trainings for 20 volunteers to enhance their capacity on GBV, MRE, PSS, and disability inclusion to enable them to conduct the activities with high quality.
- In addition, in this step TDH will establish Child and Woman Safe spaces in Al Kiswah, and Al Zabadani,
- Procurement for the 6000 dignity kits for AL Areesheh and AL Hol camps, which have been selected based on the location severity scale, TdH access and plans, Local partner access.
The second step will be done through the trained Local Partner’s volunteers, and TDH will provide them with support, supervision.
Activities will include:
General protection: (Rural Damascus)
- Recreational and Structured PSS activities for 1300 beneficiaries, these activities will aim to increase social skills, emotional wellbeing, and improve coping mechanisms.
- Protection Monitoring activities: TDH will conduct Need assessments for protection needs in Rural Damascus, these needs assessments will overview the protection environment of the concerned population to Assist the Protection sector and NGOs in prioritizing the affected areas needing further protection.
CP activities: (Rural Damascus)
- Awareness-raising sessions for 6000 BG, topics will include topics such as child rights, and child labor.
- case management for 100 children, including referral to services.
MA activities: (Rural Damascus)
awareness-raising sessions for 3000 WMBG on Mine risk education. all materials used will be from approved sources such as UNMASS.
-TdH will provide CASH assistance for protection (CASH specific sector/CASH for protection) for 40 victims affected by explosive hazards, in alignment with the CASH Working Group, for 8 cycles.
GBV activities: (Rural Damascus)
- awareness-raising sessions for 6000 WMBG including topics such as violence against women, and early marriage.
- Focused assistance for 100 women via providing MHPSS support, referral to Psychiatric/psychotherapy specialized services
GBV activities: (NES)
- Dignity kits:6000 dignity kits will be distributed in Al Areesheh&Al Hol camps, the kits' content will be based on “Report on Menstrual Hygiene Practices of Women and Girls in NES, September 2021”
The Second phase 2023
This phase will focus on increasing resilience and the coping mechanism for by providing vocational training, and specialized services for identified people in need especially PWD and Women facing protection risks, unitizing the findings of the Protection need assessments and the project lessons learned,
in this phase, activities will be more focused on delivering more specialized assistance services, including:
-Parenting skills trainings for parents,
-TDH will provide empowerment activities for women and girls including skills building and vocational training,
-victim assistance via Vocation training.
-Continuing the Awareness-raising, on CP, GBV, MRE, and PSS activities.
-M&E plan: All the activities will be supervised by TdH PM and M&E staff will conduct field visits to assess implementation while ensuring active participation of beneficiaries through establishing feedback mechanism, ensure accountability to beneficiaries through conducting FGD, surveys, and PDM.PDM.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

TDH as an origination adapted a zero-tolerance policy against sexual exportation and abuse, and to ensure al PSEA standard are respected: • All TDH staff have singed the Code of conduct upon recruitment. • All TDH staff are oriented on PSEA upon recruitment on PSEA topic via induction sessions • TDH have it own internal reporting mechanism on PSEA, and have clear reporting channels that are accessible for all staff • All TDH staff including field staff that are working directly with beneficiary are regularly trained on PSEA, and reporting channels. • Beneficiaries are oriented on PSEA reporting Mechanism, through multiple channels (lines, Posters, email, Focal points). • TDH includes Gender-based violence (GBV) and child protection services in its intervention to support women facing sexual Abuse


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

R1: Local partners lose access to the project locations and are unable to implement activities, M1: SARC has displayed an outstanding ability to implement humanitarian projects despite constraints and limitations, However, if SARC lost access to project locations, TDH would either identify other suitable partners or, in coordination with them and with local actors, identify other locations with same high needs, where the programming could continue.R2: bureaucracy and delay in getting needed approvals from local authorities, M2: TDH already obtained approvals to work in the project locations (attached with the project documents) and TdH already has and on-going projects and access in the targeted locations, in addition, TDH will advocate through the local partner to have the needed approvals based on the signed MOU.R3: Continuation of restriction of movement for Humanitarian workers in the camps as COVID 19 measure.M3: Take necessary permission from the authority, keep the team updated with government directives and safety and security information. COVID-19 related govt.’s rules and guidelines will be followed. R5: Inflation of Syrian Pound and its effect on the markets and the difference rate between the Syrian central Bank and the USD exchange rate.M6: any significate change will be discussed with the donor and modify the budget via the amendment process
