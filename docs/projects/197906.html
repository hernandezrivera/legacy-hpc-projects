Project Summary

Borno State in Northeast Nigeria has continued to face constant threat from Violent Extremist Organizations such as Boko Haram and ISWAP. The constant attacks across key LGAs which directly link the city of Maiduguri has led to the shortage of food and key economic activities. Despite the untold suffering it has caused on the civilian population, recent attacks across vulnerable communities around the state by VEO continues to increase vulnerability among community residents especially the youth who can easily be manipulated, brainwashed and recruited. Despite efforts of government and the humanitarian community in supporting Internally Displaced Persons(IDPs) in major camps and host communities within the state, the need to engage young people and providing platforms for them where they could interact, dialogue with one another and clearly express their views as well as grievances to stakeholders regarding their situation is of key relevance to maintaining peace, stability and reducing the risk of recruitment among the youth by VEOs in this fragile state.
Borno State Government has committed to promote the welfare of youths in development while at the same time strengthening effort on law enforcement across the State. With the recent ban of all Political thuggery across the state with priority on Biu , Maiduguri and Gwoza The state government is committed to reducing youth’s vulnerability to Violent Extreme organizations (VEOs). The readjustment of the state curfew time within the Maiduguri Metropolis is a determination toward building a peaceful community.
The need for open governance platforms to constantly engage the youths is of key relevance towards addressing issues of socio‐economic grievances, political marginalization, poverty, unemployment, lack of basic services and functioning governance, environmental shocks, high population growth, displacement, understanding youth plight as well as lending a listening ear to ways/approaches the youths can support to strengthen the security of the state and building their resilience to refute all forms of recruitment by VEOs across the selected locations.
In consolidating the efforts of the state and sustaining the peace in the Mega Local Government areas of Biu and Jere, promoting civic participation is of significant importance to maintain law and order in such communities and to have youths as key stakeholders in advocating and amplifying messages of Peace across the selected locations.
CATAI will support the process of building and promoting civic participation across the selected location in line with pillar 9 Peace and security of the Borno state 25-year Development plan with focus on strategic objective
1. Instill in a comprehensive, workable, and self-sustaining Early Warning Response Mechanism that is community driven.
2. Strengthen the traditional structure as a security system linked with the formal mechanisms.

1 - Needs

Borno State in Northeast Nigeria has been under constant attack from violent extremist organizations such as Boko Haram and ISWAP. The constant attacks across key LGAs in the state have led to a shortage of food and other key economic activities. Despite the untold suffering, it has caused the civilian population, recent VEO attacks on vulnerable communities throughout the state have increased vulnerability among community residents, particularly young people, who are easily manipulated, brainwashed, and recruited. Despite the government's and the humanitarian community's efforts to assist internally displaced persons (IDPs) in major camps and host communities in the state, the need to engage young people and provide platforms for them to interact, dialogue with one another, and clearly express their views, as well as grievances to stakeholders regarding their situation, is critical in maintaining peace, and stability and reducing the risk of recruitment among the youth by VEOs in this fragile state. The Borno State Government has committed to the welfare of community members in development while at the same time strengthening efforts on law enforcement across the State. With the recent ban of all Political thuggery across the state with priority on Biu1, Maiduguri, and Gwoza2 The state government is committed to reducing youth’s vulnerability to Violent Extreme organizations (VEOs). The readjustment of the state curfew3 time within the Maiduguri Metropolis is a determination toward building a peaceful community. The need for open governance platforms to constantly engage community members, especially youth, is of key relevance towards addressing issues of socio‐economic grievances, political marginalization, poverty, unemployment, lack of basic services and functioning governance, environmental shocks, high population growth, displacement, understanding youth plight as well as lending a listening ear to ways/approaches the youths can support to strengthen the peace architecture of the state and building their resilience to refute all forms of recruitment by VEOs across the selected locations. In consolidating the efforts of the state and sustaining the peace in the Mega Local Government areas of Biu and Jere, promoting civic participation is significant to maintain law and order in such communities and to have youths as key stakeholders in advocating and amplifying messages of Peace across the selected locations. CATAI will support the process of building and promoting civic participation across the selected location in line with pillar 9, Peace and security, of the Borno state 25-year Development plan4 and the objectives of the platforms created for this activity which is to facilitate dialogue and provide a space for communities to address trust deficits (intergenerational and political), isolation, and underlying grievances that make communities vulnerable to VEO influence. CATAI will therefore complement the "State objective" in this project through the successful implementation of the three project objectives stated in the proposal below. At the State level, there are structures such as the State Emergency Management Agency (SEMA), Department of State Security (DSS), Agency for the coordination of Sustainable Development and Humanitarian and Response, North East Development Commission, etc supporting peacebuilding, Coordination of post-disaster/humanitarian assistance and rehabilitation and reconstruction efforts. CATAI works directly with these structures as part of the Localization approach in the Northeast and shall work closely with these agencies as well as their structures at LGA level to implement this project.

2 - Activities

1. Community Mapping and Vulnerability assessment in Two wards, Six communities within Biu and JereThis activity shall be jointly facilitated with the MEL team of the North-East Connection Program. A community mapping exercise shall be conducted in the form of a workshop at the LGA level. This workshop shall bring together all critical stakeholders (CBOs working within the locality, community and traditional leaders, religious leaders, and security agencies both formal and informal) from the different communities, wards, and districts. The workshop shall be in the form of a facilitated discussion where the MEL team will employ a mixed method using different qualitative research techniques (KIIs, FGDs, and discourse analysis) to explore information on the current contextual situation, conflict profile, and dynamics in each community, ward, and district. This will provide us with evidenced-based validated and revalidated information to effectively map and identify communities of intervention. 2. Capacity building of key selected community stakeholdersProject stakeholders and community members, including youth, women, and traditional and religious leaders, will be trained to build their capacity to mitigate any grievances that make their communities vulnerable to VEO influence. The training will also help the stakeholders organize and facilitate dialogues that provide a safe space for the target and neighboring communities to address any trust deficits, and isolation between citizens and their community leaders as well as elected officials. A two 2-day training shall be conducted in each of the two project wards. A total of 50 stakeholders (a breakdown of participants is provided below) will be invited to each Ward training, making a total of 100 stakeholders who will be trained under this activity. and countering violent extremism. 3. Community Dialogue for Peace Forum This activity will provide a round table discussion between selected youth leaders, female leaders, traditional leaders, religious leaders, and representatives of the host communities and their neighboring communities within the same LGA to dialogue by sharing ideas, concerns, criticisms, and learning points on key governance and social protection issues affecting their communities and offer suggestions on on key governance and social protection issues affecting their communities and offer suggestions on how they can collectively identify ways of addressing these issues before it deteriorates into violent extremism.A dialogue session will be organized at the community level. One session with fifty (50) participants will be organized in each of the 6 project communities within the first quarter, so they can inform the mega town hall meetings at the local government level (activity 4). Therefore, a total of 6 dialogues will be conducted across the six communities with 300 participants.4. LGA-level Townhall DialoguesThis activity aims to provide a platform for community members and stakeholders trained under activity two to interact with their elected officials and duty bearers in the middle of the project in the respective LGAs of Biu and Jere (Maiduguri) and a central townhall bringing together selected participants from the two LGAs in Maiduguri. The townhalls will include round table discussions with stakeholders across various sectors who are directly affected by the threats and activities of violent extremist organizations in Borno State to share ideas, concerns, criticisms, and learning points on key governance and social, economic, security, and protection issues affecting their wellbeing.
