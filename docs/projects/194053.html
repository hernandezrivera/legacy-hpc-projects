Project Summary

Based on the Cluster objectives and HNO of the Cluster, the WASH interventions in the 2 States shall focus on improved access to water services ,sanitation and hygiene which will seek to reduce vulnerability of IDPs and host communities to waterborne and vector-transmitted diseases resulting from deficient environmental health conditions, unsafe disposal of faeces, water-borne diseases, poor personal and household sanitation and hygiene practices. The approaches will seek to address needs related to accessing quality and safe drinking water , access to basic sanitation and hygiene services and facilities, while also establishing/strengthening community structures for improved self-reliance capacities. The intervention shall also have a focus on building community resilience to WASH-related recurrent shocks with emphasis on community empowerment and engagement of women, girls, and young people as enhanced enablers. On sanitation and hygiene elements, the project will focus on supporting communities through Community Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) interventions to empower women, girls, boys and men to use latrines and live in Open Defecation Free (ODF) communities; rehabilitation/construction of gender segregated and disability-friendly sanitation facilities in schools and primary health centres; conducting of hygiene promotion interventions in the communities and in schools and health centres with focus on handwashing with soap at critical times including menstrual hygiene management in schools. The project will also strengthen community WASH structures through establishment and empowerment of Sanitation and Hygiene Committees to manage sanitation services in all the targeted communities. The interventions shall adopt the following approaches:
Action-oriented rapid survey: This will be the initial step to establish status of critical WASH services, facilities and practices in the target localities. A multi-agency approach will be undertaken to promote wide ownership of the rapid survey. Data from the action survey will also be shared with other sector partners to support in addressing gaps and emerging needs.
Capacity building: This will be through establishment of and training of Hygiene and Sanitation Committees, Water Committees in target villages and training of Hygiene Promoters. This approach will seek to enhance community ownership and participation, thus anchoring future support for project activities. Capacity building will also encompass undertaking the communities through the CLTS processes.
Rehabilitation/Establishment of facilities: These will be through rehabilitation or construction of new sanitation facilities in communities and institutions. The intervention shall also construction and or rehabilitate Water Yards equipped with solar system and construction of water treatment plants to improve access to clean water including distribution of PUR sachets.
Awareness campaigns: This is an approach that will seek to change behaviour and attitude of community members on safe personal, households and environmental hygiene practices.
Provision of WASH NFIs: This will include provision of basic WASH facilities such as soap, water buckets/jerry-cans, mosquito nets and ibriks

1 - Needs

According to the WASH Cluster HNO, the Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene situation (WASH) is deteriorating due to worsening economic crisis, political instability, non-functional or aging WASH infrastructure, a five-fold increase in operation and maintenance cost of water infrastructures, insufficient revenue collections. poor budget allocation by the Government, and limited external investments. Only 30 percent of the population have access to basic sanitation with prevalent open defecation practices at 33.3%. which is especially concerning for women and girls. Hand washing with soap (as the main indicator for hygienic practices) is estimated at 14.3%. Unsafe water, inadequate sanitation and poor personal and environmental health and hygiene have considerably increased the threat of contracting a growing number of WASH-related diseases such as acute watery diarrhoea (AWD), cholera, dysentery, hepatitis E, typhoid, vector-borne diseases including malaria, Dengue Fever, Rift Valley Fever, Chikungunya Fever and acute respiratory infections. COVID-19 is also linked to poor handwashing facilities with soap and water. The Education Sector- WASH in schools infrastructure report of October 2020 estimates that approximately 30% of schools do not have any functional latrines and fewer than 50% of schoolchildren have access to soap and water for handwashing at school. Temporary handwashing stations, latrine and handwashing construction and long-term sustainable solutions for the provision of clean water to schools are required. North Darfur was ranked as one of the most affected States from a survey of 18 States in Sudan. The Cluster HNO estimates that approximately 36% of the displaced population report unaffordability of water. Women and girls who mostly fetch water for households from distant water sources are more susceptible to GBV. Lack of gender-segregated latrines, undignified, unlit, and insecure sanitation facilities pose an additional risk of GBV for girls and women. Menstrual hygiene management is often not prioritised, negatively impacting girls' attendance and enrolment in schools. Diarrhoea kills one in ten children. Over 3 million children suffer from acute malnutrition, 50 per cent of which is associated with repeated diarrhoea or worm infections related to poor WASH conditions. WASH needs for vulnerable populations are expected to increase due to natural emergencies including dry spell, floods attributed to climate change.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Output 1: Targeted individuals are enabled to practice safe excreta disposal with dignity in a secure environment. Activities : This will be achieved through operation and maintenance of shared family, construction of ( communal, institutional or Household latrines, conducting Community-Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) triggering sessions targeting communities , distribution of menstrual hygiene management kits distributed and PUR sachets.Output 2: Vulnerable individuals have knowledge and appropriate behaviour to prevent and mitigate WASH related diseases and practice good hygiene. Activities : This will be achieved through distribution of WASH NFIs (Each household to receive a set comprising 2 jerrycans and 2 buckets and 2 bars of soap 400g per household per month), and capacity strengthening of sanitation and hygiene structures in the target locations such as Hygiene Committees and Hygiene Clubs in schools through trainings and provision of tools and rehabilitation of Hygiene Promotion Centres. The project will incorporate messages on prevention and control of COVID-19 and cholera, conducting trainings through Children’s Hygiene And Sanitation Trainings (CHAST) model (20 children per school) and conducting hygiene promotion and waste management campaigns. Output 3: Vulnerable households have improved access to safe and clean drinking water . Activities : This will be achieved by distribution of PUR sachets, Construction/ rehabilitation of Water Yards and formation and training of water committees and Construction or rehabilitation of water treatment plants. The implementation approach shall ensure that communities and affected populations are consulted or engaged in assessments, designs, implementation, monitoring, and inclusion of women and girls in all project activities . Efforts will be made to strengthen feedback mechanisms and post-distribution surveys. In line with the cluster resilience approach, activities will also contribute to making the population/communities more resilient to WASH-related recurrent shocks though training, capacity building and community empowerment where women, girls, and young people are enhanced enablers. The inventions shall also be implemented in consideration of cross -cutting elements. Interventions will ensure that the communal and institutional latrines are gender-segregated, safe with latches on the doors, and easy to access secured places. linkages shall also me done for women and girls to income-generating opportunities , for example women's groups and girls shall receive training on making reusable sanitary pads and soap. Latrines shall be constructed in consideration of needs of Persons With Disabilities.
