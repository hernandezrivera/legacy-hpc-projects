Project Summary

Since 2017, the Non-state Armed Groups (NSAG) have demanded the closure of schools. They threatened or burned down establishments that have remained opened. As a consequence, as of December 2019, 83% (5,307 out of 6,379) schools are closed, putting 81% (841,330 out of estimated 1,033,000) children out of schools and 73% (21,128 out of 28,866) teachers not reporting to work owing to the life-taking threat posted on them and children as well as attacks on education facilities.
Schools have been shut down in most of the difficult and hard to reach villages in the Anglophone Regions for the past five years. Parents who had means displaced their children from these villages to attend schools in urban areas in the English speaking Regions where schools were ongoing while others moved to French speaking Regions. The very vulnerable parents and children who lacked the means to seek education out of the very difficult and hard to reach crisis zones have been out of schools for five academic years, this has led to child recruitment into arm groups and early marriages, early and unwanted pregnancies, drug abuse and high level of juvenile delinquency.
An estimated 1,033,000 school aged children have been affected by the crisis. They were denied the opportunity to learn and develop themselves. As Schools officially resumed for the 2021/2022 academic year, an improvement in the number of schools that resumed as compared to last year has been registered. However, the doors of majority of public schools in some hard to reach areas in the NWR of Cameroon are still closed. As a result parents and communities have organised learning centres for their children from nursery, primary and secondary levels to enable them enjoy their fundamental right to education.
According to the analysis of the MSNA round 5 for the NW and SW regions of Cameroon the number of schools in these Divisions that opened their doors is far below the number of schools available in these divisions (Bui total number of schools 636, operational 56 giving 9%, Ngoketunjia the expected number of schools is 250, operational 20 giving 8%, Momo expected number of schools is 255 operational 19 giving 7%, Menchum expected number of schools 216 operational 89 giving 41%)
Due to the extremely high risk operational environment in the North West Region, for which education is used as political instrument in the conflict, some communities have developed strategies to grant children access to education through learning centers.
The project seek to support and Promote the functioning of 9 learning centers within 4 Divisions for 1490 boys and 1510 girls (3-18years), in the NW(Bui, Ngoketunjia, Momo, and Menchum) Region of Cameroon for 12 months
The specific objectives
• Provide school learning kits to 90 teachers and 3000 children (3-18 years) in 9 learning centers within 4 Divisions in the NW(Bui, Ngoketunjia, Momo, and Menchum) region of Cameroon
• Sensitized 3000 children (3-18 years) on effective COVID 19 safe protocol in 9 learning centers within 4 Divisions as in the NW, and provision of COVID-19 safe materials for 9 months
• Assist 3000 children (3-18 years) with school feeding programs in 9 learning centers within 4 Divisions in the NW(Bui, Ngoketunjia, Momo, and Menchum) for 9 months.
• Train 18 Teachers and education Personnel in 9 learning centers within 4 Divisions in the NWR (Bui, Ngoketunjia, Momo, and Menchum) on psychosocial support to offer psychosocial assistance in their learning spaces.
• Actively engage Community participation through creation of feedback committees, hotlines, and complaint and feedback mechanism for 9 months.
• Training and supporting 150 school dropout youths on income generating activities

1 - Besoins / Needs

Access to education remain a challenge with the going Anglophone crises. Since 2017, the crisis has a substantial social and humanitarian impacts on children and education in the NWSW. The conflict has also had repercussions for the education. The whole system has failed in providing access to inclusive learning. An estimated 1,033,000 school aged children have been affected by the crisis. They were denied the opportunity to learn and develop themselves. Failing in achieving Sustainable Development Goal (SDG), goal 4: ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. The current system is leaving more than 955,700 children behind due to the extremely high risk operational environment in both Regions, for which education is used as political instrument in the conflict, some communities have developed strategies to grant children access to education through learning centers. The creation of learning centers in hard to reach villages in crisis affected Divisions in the NW (Bui, Ngoketunjia, Momo, and Menchum) has offered opportunity to many vulnerable children to have access to education and support affected population to meet their basic need of education. Many children have also drop out of school and they are willing to learn a trade. The main activities of this project will be • To provide school learning kits to 90 teachers and 3000 children (3-18 years) in 9 learning centers within 4 Divisions in the NWR school learning kits (Chalk, Chats, text books) for teachers, (exercise books, pens, pencils, rulers, slates) for children - Transportation of learning kids to learning centers - Distribution of learning kits in learning centers - To sensitized 5000 children (3-18 years) on effective COVID-19 safe protocol in 9 learning centers in the NWR and provision of COVID 19 safe materials - Organise COVID -19 sensitization session for teachers who will in turn sensitize the children in learning centers - Distribution of COVID-19 information materials (posters, brochures) in learning centers. - Purchase of face masks, buckets, soap and hand sanitizers for 9 learning centers - Transportation of face masks, buckets, soap and hand sanitizers to 9 learning centers - Distribution of face masks, buckets, soap and hand sanitizers in 9 learning centers • To assist 3000 children (3-18 years) with school feeding programs in 9 learning centers - Purchase of snacks (biscuits) for 9 learning centers - Transportation/ distribution of snacks (biscuits) to 9 learning centers - • To train 90 Teachers and education Personnel in 9 learning centers on psychosocial training. - Organize one day psychosocial training in each Region - Feed 90 teachers and other education personnel during training - Payment of transport allowances to 90 teachers and other education personnel - To create 9 feedback committees, to manage complaint - Creation of feedback committees - Training of feedback members on how to receive and treat complains Training and supporting 150 school dropout on income generating activities project results • 3000 emergency affected children and youth (5-18 yrs old) attend learning spaces in affected areas • 300 emergency affected children (3-5 yrs old) attend learning spaces in affected areas • 9 learning spaces provided with learning materials in the in the targeted locations. • 9 learning spaces provided with education kits in the targeted location • 3000 children received food in 9 learning center • 9 learning spaces provided with adequate hand washing facilities • 90 teachers/other educational personnel in affected areas trained in psycho-social support. • 500 children (3-5yrs old) taught by teachers trained in psychosocial support services • 3000 children (5-18 yrs old) taught by teachers trained in psycho-social support services 150 children trained received income generating activities
