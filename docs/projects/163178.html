Project Summary

MICHAFOUND aims at reducing the problem of unemployment within some IDPs in the SWR of Cameroon.
Targeted population is at 11. These IDPs are resulted from the current Anglophone crisis ongoing in Cameroon for the past three years.
This project aims at providing employment facilities thereby giving out capital and business plans for small scale businesses base on the know-how of vulnerable persons.
Some IDPs related to unemployment as a problem based on data collected shall be called to secured venues and proposals / trainings shall be made as planned.
Project execution will be done chronologically that is from one location to another for proper monitoring.
Feedback will be collected in-house through short paper surveys for general reporting. This shall rebuild livelihood and play positively in the minds of the vulnerable.
.
