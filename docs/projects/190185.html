Project Summary

Save the children, International (SCI) plans to provide emergency response in 40 Woredas in Amhara, Afar, Tigray, Somali & Afar regions. SCI in line with the cluster recommended response strategy would propose a full package WaSH response that ensures that the conflict-affected communities will access supports of essential WASH services. This will address the identified needs & gaps of short term emergency water trucking, rehabilitation of water facilities (shallow/hand-dug wells, springs & pipelines), replacement of damaged/non-functional electro-mechanical items, provision of water treatment & testing supplies, training of water management committees & provision of a maintenance kit. SCI will provide gender-sensitive sanitation facilities including emergency pit latrines with hand washing to IDP settlement & semi-permanent latrines in selected priority HCFs with cleaning products. SCI will also train CHVs on key good hygiene practices and cascade awareness raising through hygiene promotion to the IDP & host community through house-to-house visits, health education at HCFs, and mass campaigns. Selected most vulnerable households will be provided with basic WaSH-NFI supplies including Jeri cans, soaps, buckets & water treatment chemicals.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

The accountability system for this project will include all three components including information sharing, participation, and complaints and feedback for all communities and children. The project will provide regular, timely and accessible information to children and communities about the project, implementing organization, staff behavior and compliance and feedback mechanisms. Children, adults and vulnerable groups in communities where the project works will be provided with opportunities to express their views and influence decision-making across the programme cycle (e.g. discussing together, deciding together and working together through child focus group discussions). Save the Children will present the opinions of the children and adults with whom we are working to adapt the project. Participants and non-participants will have access to the SC program accountability system, with feedback and actions taken recorded in the M&E system. Under this system, complaints or feedback can be entered through four channels: 1) Compliance or Appeal Committee); 2) through a toll-free hotline number that is managed by SC; 3) through suggestion boxes; and, 4) through direct reporting to SCI either face-to-face. Suggestion box will be feasible at school compound whereas face to face and appeal committee will be installed at community level. The toll-free hotline system is managed at country office and anyone who have access to cell phone can forward his/her compliant and feedback freely and anonymously. The MEAL and program staffs are responsible to monitor and evaluate the progress of the project and accountability system.
