Project Summary

Provision of Donbass residents in need with additional water resources for uninterrupted supply of heat to homes and social facilities.
The providing of residents of the region suffering from armed conflict with quality drinking water from new and abandoned wells and draw-wells.
The providing of residents of the region affected by the armed conflict with individual water treatment and storage systems, household water heating devices.
Reduce the risks of infectious diseases associated with the degradation of water resources in the region.

Needs

In the context of the ongoing military conflict on Donbass and the isolation of certain areas of the region, the problem of the shortage of quality water resources is increasingly aggravated, although the issue of water shortage is traditional, historical character existed even before the outbreak of hostilities. However, now the problem with water in the region has reached such a scale that in the autumn of 2018 in the central cities of the region mass outbreaks of intestinal infections with dozens of hospitalizations are already recorded due to the degradation of water purification systems. The following the future development of the problem of water shortage may lead to the experience of large-scale technogenic catastrophes in the level of event in the autumn of 2018 in the Crimea.The municipal infrastructure of water supply, heating and sanitation in most social institutions in the region has been severely degraded and has not been updated for a long time, which is a separate segment of the water problems in the region. In the context of the humanitarian and socio-economic crisis in the region, to update this socially important infrastructure, the more there are no available budget resources. There are separate locations with the most socially vulnerable people, where the same problem is critical, simply because of the dysfunction in the buildings of public infrastructure systems (for example, in the hostel of the blind), in some settlements basic utilities are absent for several years (eg. in Krutaya Balka).Regular interruptions in the supply of water to individuals and key social institutions, as well as regular violations and risks in the work of the Donetsk Filter Plant, create additional threats of water deficit and violations of basic humanitarian rights. In such conditions, of particular importance are not only the measures of infrastructural strengthening and improvement of the water supply system in the areas, but also situational measures of operational assistance with devices of water treatment, water heating, water accumulation and storage of water, as well as basic hygiene, to ensure the fundamental conditions of human health and dignity in extreme conditions of water deficit. According to the report of the OSCE monitoring mission "The access to water in the conflict-affected areas of Donetsk and Luhansk regions" for 2015, up to 1.3 million children and adults in the territory of Donbass need hygiene and quality drinking water.At the same time, the region is rich in ground and mine waters, which, without being used as a promising source of water supply, are a constant factor of additional costs and environmental threats on the macro-regional level. For their reduction, there is a potential and in a number of experimental sites (including in neighboring regions) successfully tested practice of useful use after treatment of mine waters, their use as water reservoirs and wells.

Activities & outcomes

Within the project it is planned:1. Improve the access to water for conflict affected populations, within the framework of which it is planned to:• Carry out laboratory research of mine waters to assess their use as a coolant in heating systems. Select the most suitable mining company with the appropriate mine water flow rate of appropriate quality. To develop a technical specification for a pilot plant for the treatment of mine water intended for the use of a boiler house nearby to the mine as a coolant. To set up a pilot plant for the treatment of mine waters in the region. To enter in the experimental-industrial operation of the plant for the treatment of mine waters. Publish the results and technical documentation of the project for scaling by other entities.• Perform the restoration of previously operating wells and draw-wells located in the region and capable to provide to the population with quality drinking water.• Conduct geological and sanitary-epidemiological research of water resources in order to assess the possibility of commissioning of new wells for drinking water. To put into operation a number of new wells to obtain drinking water in the most areas of water scarcity.2. To prevent the outbreaks of diseases and poisoning related to low quality and water deficit of water resources by:• Identify and provide a number of the most vulnerable people in the region in need of water treatment, storage and heating devices, as well as personal hygiene products.3. To make the improvement of water disposal systems (primarily of storm drainage and sewerage systems in a critical situation and infrastructure of public sewer) in the settlements along the demarcation line, in the neediest and high-risk areas with the aim of reducing the risks of deterioration of the epidemiological situation. To improve the quality of drinking water and water supply to key socially important objectives through modernization, reconstruction and installation of water supply, heating and sewerage in the neediest social objectives.
