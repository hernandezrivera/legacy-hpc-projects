Project Summary

Knowing the importance of education and ensuring learning opportunities to all children in the country especially to those who have been deprived due to several factors and since almost two years their right to a regular school life, Caritas Lebanon project aim to directly support the community by providing the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant children in ML, Bekaa, and Baalbek El Hermel governorates the possibility to access education and to continue learning despite the complexity of the crises that everyone is facing at a national level.
Through this intervention, under strategic objective one, CL team will work on preventing dropouts as well as getting children back to education through outreach, awareness session and cash for education. One of Caritas Lebanon strategic plan to respond to the crisis is the Education Trust Fund. CL team with Oeuvre d ’Orient has developed database of around 300 schools and includes critical school-level data covering students, teachers, gender statistics, refugee status and types and level of payments provided by students. In addition, there are the notes from F2F interviews with school administrators that clearly show the deteriorating situation in the schools over time and the impact on teachers and students.
With this in mind, and given Caritas Lebanon's positive relationship with the 300 school principals, the team might be a valuable partner in executing and completing many of the activities of the Education Sector ERP's first strategic objective. Using our relationships, contact information, and communication skills with a variety of schools, we will be able to reach out and identify students who have dropped out of private schools due to financial reasons, allowing us to follow up with them and ensure that the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant children, including children with disabilities, are reached. We can currently report that we have extensive interaction with at least 90 schools in our target areas: 24 in Bekaa, 6 in Baalbek El Hermel, and 60 in ML. Caritas Lebanon, with the help of education awareness assistants, will continue to raise awareness among 1,500 students and at least 500 caregivers (95% Lebanese, 5% migrants), as well as refer Out of Education Children (OOEC) to essential services in order to aid them in returning to school. Cash for education will be a critical instrument to employ and supply to 400 most vulnerable households (90% Lebanese, 10% migrants) in order to help them stay in school while still receiving other forms of social cash assistance.
On the other hand, it is well known that special needs students in Lebanon continue to face social, financial, and physical barriers to education, particularly now that the country is in an emergency situation and neither parents nor schools can afford the high costs of materials, equipment, and assistive technologies for disabled children. Changing this is critical because investing in special education has beneficial spillover effects, such as job creation and allowing children with special needs to realize their full adult potential. CL believes we must strive for an inclusive education system that enables all children, whatever their ability, background or faith to realize their ambitions, providing learners with greater opportunities to learn from, and relate to, their peers. As such, under this same objective, CL will support 40 students with physical disabilities (36 Lebanese, 4 migrants) learning in CL’s center (Patriarch Sfeir, Zahle – Vocational school owned by Caritas, supported by MoSA or other CL's centers or external cases referred to CL) children with hearing impairment with assistive technologies to enable their access to and retention in education. The device will be a tool to ensure school retention for children in formal education and with disabilities and ensure that the lack of device does not impair their education.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under Objective 1. 1.1 Train Education Awareness Assistant (EAAs) to properly identify Out of Education Children (OOEC) and provide awareness – CL will recruit and train 6 EAAs and provide the necessary equipment.1.2 Outreach, awareness raising & referrals to bring them back to education: This activity consists of following up with school administrators to identify dropouts and students who left the school. EAAs will coordinate with parents and caregivers about students’ enrolment for the scholastic year 22-23. They will be also responsible to plan for awareness campaigns through different modalities and methodologies of sessions. 1.3 Provision of assistive technologies for children with disabilities. This activity consists of reaching out 40 children with hearing impairment and providing them with assistive technologies. The tools will be procured based on accurate medical assessments of an audiologist covered by this project, who will perform a test and measure hearing loss to assess the type and degree of loss.1.4 Cash for Education targeting vulnerable Lebanese and migrant families to enable retention in education: This activity consists of providing 400 households with cash for education. The amount per household will be approximately USD 20 and will be confirmed following the sector guidance and instruction in this matter. 2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Caritas Lebanon staffs have been trained by partners on GAM and GMS ensuring appropriate integration of protection, gender and GBV issues within the design, delivery, and review of the project. Gender is mainstreamed in the design of the project which will ensure at least equal access to the provided services to females, males, girls and boys. Eventually, it is worth mentioning, Caritas Lebanon is a strong actor in the Protection sector where it has been co-leading the sector and is significantly involved in all decisions related to protection issues. Moreover, protection is central to all CL interventions, both as a standalone programme area, but also mainstreamed across all sectors, alongside safeguarding best practices. The proposed project has protection and disability inclusion integrated, with the explicit aim of ensuring prevention and mitigation of protection risks to the most vulnerable and marginalized including women, girls, the elderly, and persons with disabilities

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Caritas Lebanon will actively engage communities, local authorities, and MEHE within the frame of the project. Through this engagement, CL is ensuring the project is designed and implemented based on the priorities. Caritas Lebanon has operationalized the organizational PSEA and safeguarding mechanism as well as a Feedback and Complaint Handling Mechanism which provides staff, communities and others, with multiple channels to raise concerns and provide feedback including through the complaint boxes and hotlines, and through the MEAL process which include accountability questions and satisfaction surveys. Caritas Lebanon will fully incorporate Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) modalities into all stages of this project while strengthening the approaches to reflect the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and protection mainstreaming.
