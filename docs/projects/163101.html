Project Summary

The proposed action aims at providing tailored Shelter and/or Non-Food Items (NFI) assistance to the most vulnerable conflict-affected populations identified through needs assessments. The modality of intervention and vulnerability criteria will be in line with the NFI/Shelter Cluster guidelines and standards.

Besoins / Needs

The humanitarian community is still facing challenges as to assess the whole magnitude of displacements and related needs in the North West and South West regions. IOM has conducted two comprehensive DTM rounds in the two regions in 2018 and 2019. Latest figures from the September 2019 report of the ongoing DTM exercise indicate the presence of 485,784 IDPs and 203,634 Returnees in the two regions as well as in neighbouring localities of the West and Littoral regions, which are also impacted by the crisis. 35% of them are living in makeshift shelters or agricultural buildings, and a majority is also facing important challenges in accessing Non Food Items. IOM in Cameroon is part of the NFI/Shelter Cluster for the South-West/North-West regions and will contribute to deliver assistance to these vulnerable populations as well as keep working on better understanding the displacement trends, their priority needs and the follow-up on the support displaced populations have received or not.
