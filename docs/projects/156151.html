Needs

AlSeeraj relied on several assessments that have been conducted in the planning phase to design the project outcomes, activities and targeted population on an evidence-based approach:• Key informants reported that host population and IDPs access to health facilities is limited, due non-availability and high cost for accessing health services particularly for IDPs. • Number of IDPs increased significantly and most of them considered in need of basic humanitarian aid. • Humanitarian assistance provided to the community is not adequate and not meeting with priority needs. • The current security situation is normal and people there can move freely and easily. • Majority of IDPs are staying with family and friends in host community, and collective centres (many schools have turned into collective centre).• IDPs and host communities are being affected by increased food prices, access to NFIs and health care for IDPs are high priorities.• Most vulnerable groups are: IDPs, females, U5 children, chronically ill patients, and elderly people.• The assessments showed that the age distribution in Darbasiyah host population (30,500) was as following: Children (0-5):15.3%=5033- Children:(6-14):22.8%=6954- Youth:(15-24):9%=2754- Adults (25-59):33.6%=10248- Elderly(60+):17.1%=5215, while the gender distribution was: females: 53.5%(16,318) and males:44.79%(14,182).• The assessments showed that the age distribution of IDPs in Darbasiyah (4500) was as following: Children (0-5):16.25%=689- Children:(6-14):24.6%=1107- Youth:(15-24):11.5%=518- Adults(25-59):29.6%=1332- Elderly(60+):21.6%=972, while the gender distribution was: females: 55% (2475) and males: 45% (2025). (The sum of the percentages are not 100 exactly as they represents the means of the estimated number of people within each category).• Approximately, there are about 270 female headed households and about 130 male headed households in Darbasiyah.• According to the LA in Darbasiyah, the average percentage of age and gender distribution for males and females in Darbasiyah was: males >18: 27%, females >18: 33%, boys <18: 18%, girls <18: 22%. Number of total households in Darbasiyah: 5725.• Women in reproductive age: 17.4% of the total population ( 6090 in Darbasiyah).• Indeed, there was no accurate estimation of the number of the chronically ill patients according to age and gender. (please refer to Darbasiyah rapid health & nutrition assessment).• The estimated percentage of Pregnant and Lactating Women PLW in Darbasiyah communities is about 14.5%.

Implementation of activities

• Support Primary Healthcare Center in Darbasiyah countryside.• Support comprehensive healthcare center in Darbasiyah center.• Provide Thalassemia treatment services for Hasakah Patients.• Provide Primary Healthcare services to IDPs and Refugees in two camps in Hasakah governorate.

Protection Risk Analysis

We will follow our guidelines in case of any exposure happens. Our staff are informed about the best practices and any one can reach our complaints email or WhatssApp number.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The main risk is the unstable control of the area and the possible changes. We will support out staff decision, and take all the available measurements to legalize their status with any change.
