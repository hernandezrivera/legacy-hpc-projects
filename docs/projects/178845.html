Project Summary

Takaful has designed this program aims to contribute in improving the level of food security of farmers and their families in north west Syria through supporting wheat value chain for one hectare for 3,500 HHs (HHs and IDPs); 17,500 individuals (M,F,B,G) of the most vulnerable families in the assessed sub-districts in Azaz, Afrin, Sharan, Jandairis. Targeted locations were identified based on need and market assessments. The project will achieve its objectives through a combination of 5 interventions: Preparing farmers’ lands, providing the farmers with agriculture inputs (seeds, fertilizer, pesticides) through $250 restricted value E-voucher, providing farmers with theoretical and practical extension training sessions, Supporting farmers in marketing their crops through distributing packaging bags, assist farmers in weeding and Sunn pest management through CFW activities, which will help in supporting the vulnerable women, who are heading their households, with temporary income generation, that will help them to counter the harsh conditions of the life in NW Syria.
This project will encourage farmers to receive E-Vouchers, be able to plant wheat, as a strategic crop as many farmers may prefer to plant cash crops, the assistance will be provided through a combination of 5 interventions which TAS has used and perfected with the many years of experience and lessons learned. The intervention aligns with FSL CLUSTER Objectives to improve the food security status of assessed food insecure people through lifesaving and life-sustaining food assistance, and support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives.
Moreover, the project aims to enhance the quality and the productivity of wheat crops, by taking the advanced agriculture Tech in Turkey, and the availability of the required inputs with very high quality in Turkey, which is close to Afrin, and the structure of value chain will give the sustainability of the wheat growing sector in Syria, therefore this project will address the continuous need for the Wheat flour in Syria. Finally, the project contributes in saving and protecting the environment, through better use of the developed methods in growing wheats.
In addition, Takaful will work on addressing the agriculture challenges and supporting the olive value chain by providing 1000 farmers in Idleb, and Salqeen with agricultural inputs and services aligned with capacity development through theoretical and practical training on the best practices in olive breeding, and supporting the Agro-CFW in targeting women to do olive harvesting for the targeted farmers. And also, promoting the agricultural planning within the target communities through establishment and management of two farmers’ cooperatives involved in the olive value chain.

5 - Implementation of activities

1- Distribute agricultural inputs, such as seeds, fertilizer, pesticide and equipment; and provide related training as the follow: Takaful will support 1400 farmers(males and females),7500 individuals who are the most vulnerable and have already a cultivated land with wheat at least 1 hectare by providing them with the needed agricultural supplies for 1 hectare (fertilizers, pesticides, supplementary irrigation support)using restricted vouchers, as well as, income Generation through the marketing of all harvests of the project's beneficiaries in fair price 2- Support income-generating activities including vocational training as follow: Cash For Work component, which will provide vulnerable 126 females in A’zaz and Afrin with temporary income to support wheat value chain through working in weed control and Sunn pest management, this way is considered as an environmentally friendly way for handling with such problems that affect the growing of wheat, the impact of this way is very high, because it is 100% accurate, where the workers insure that they only target the Sunn pest without affecting the plants.The workers will be provided with the required tools and equipment, to maintain their safety, Takaful will also ensure that CFW tasks are adapted to the beneficiaries’ abilities and define working hours taking into consideration the flexibility needed, especially for women-headed households with children. Workers will be provided with a session describing safety procedures related to working in field in addition to emergency procedures.
