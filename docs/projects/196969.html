Project Summary

The project aim at: (i) improving access to education for boys, girls and CWD 5-18 affected by the crisis and deprived from the right of education due to conflict; (ii) supporting teachers and members of schools communities affected by the crisis to detect risky situations for students, increasing their skills in communicating with children to reduce the effects and consequences of trauma and/or stressful events.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aim at: (i) improving access to education for boys, girls and CWD 5-18 affected by the crisis and deprived from the right of education due to conflict; (ii) supporting teachers and members of schools communities affected by the crisis to detect risky situations for students, increasing their skills in communicating with children to reduce the effects and consequences of trauma and/or stressful events.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Outreach activities and support to Back to learning campaign: The campaign will focus on identifying IDPs school aged children and OOSC with disabilities or special needs to be enrolled, sensitizing communities on the right of CWDs to fully access their rights (including the right to education). 2. Provision of emergency learning and teaching materials together with hygiene supplies: basic stationery will be delivered to students who will be indicated by teachers and pedagogic directors of the schools according to the needs. Pre-identified criteria are: children with specific needs, newly enrolled students (not enrolled in 2021), students attending the first 3 grades of primary education, 3. Train trainers and teachers on EiE, psychosocial support and psychological first aid including provision of focus sessions with student and teachers on prevention of VAC,GBV and PSEA: improve the quality of teaching and the ability of teachers to deal with complex situations in their classrooms as children have experienced a significant academic gap, they are often traumatized and are continuously exposed to protection risks. The training enable teachers to detect risky situations for students, increase their skills in communicating with children on problems that they do not usually discuss with adults, reducing the effects and consequences of trauma and/or stressful events.4. Children participation field test: Children in selected schools will be able to be informed on key questions that describe the quality and relevance of the ongoing response. Tools and selection criteria will be agreed with the Education Cluster on the basis of the results of the pilot test implemented by Cluster coordinators (UNICEF,SCI)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will consider gender issues according to the following:a) In terms of pedagogical support, GBV’s training for teachers, activists and schools’ directors will be developed as well as the revitalization and training of gender focal points at the level of the DPE and SDEJTs. In general, lessons will take into account the inclusion of gender aspects, with no discrimination in the selection of the beneficiaries.b) In terms of participation of children (6 to 12 years), girls' club will be created or revitalized and trained about rights, gender and child protection. Involved in this action will not be only grils but boya and professors too with the aim of developing and implementing sensitization activities for their schools and communities; c) In terms of community engagement, completely involved in the project will be the School Council which members are from the school and the local community. Sensitization campaigns and trainings will be promoted about gender, rights and child protection.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project has been designed in consultation with communities and relevant local authorities where We World-GVC is implementing its ongoing programme. Needs and vulnerabilities have been identified along the current year with the constant envolvement of DPE, SDEJT and School Councils whose members belong to schools and to local communities. The project will therefore adopt a protection mainstreaming approach in all the components and activities envisaged. In particular, with respect to accountability, mechanisms for measuring the adequacy of interventions and collecting / responding to problems or complaints will be made available to beneficiaries.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be identified according to the following strategies:- Involvement of the multi-sector team for the definition of indicators’ criteria for the identification of people with different disabilities;- Production of an instrument for collecting data on indicators of people with disabilities, taking into account the criteria of defined indicators, with the involvement of the multi-sectorial team;- Training enumerators to collect data from people with disabilities who are both included and excluded from the formal education system;- Adaptation of the activities in order to include completely people with disabilities.
