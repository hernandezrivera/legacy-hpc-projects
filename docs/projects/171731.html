Project Summary

Direct Aid HRP 2021 project will focus on improving the physical protection and dignity of IDPs, and poor host communities across Somalia through the provision of locally purchased Non Food Items (NFIs) and Emergency Shelter Kits (ESKs) through in-kind modalities with community driven or owner driven approaches, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) exercises for NFIs & ESKs will be conducted after distribution of ESKs and NFIs is done in all target locations.
Beneficiary’s registration will be conducted and prioritizing the Extremely Vulnerable Individuals (EVIs) including female and single headed HHs, orphans, chronically ill, aged and child headed households. Gender will be mainstreamed into the project through the speciﬁc consideration of the different needs of women, men, boys and girls during the design of activities. The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis-segregated data. DA will target a total 24,000 individuals (50% females) consisting 3000 Bay, 3000Hiiraan, 3000Banadir, 3000Lower shabbelle , 3000Middle Shabelle, 3000Gedo,3000 Mudug and 3000Waqooyi galbeed regions through different Shelter and ESK interventions throughout the year 2021.Shelter and NFIs project will target 24000 individuals who are 2000 HHs who will receive complete NFIs and Emergency shelter kits and same beneficiaries will utilize ESKs and NFIs.
DA staff will ensure that the provided ESK and NFIs has been utilized for intended purpose by coordinating with the beneﬁciaries themselves and conduct follow ups with the support of the community leaders, local authorities and the relevant clusters. A Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) exercises for NFIs and ESKs and Post distribution Monitoring exercises for Shelters and ESK will be conducted to analyze outcomes, the beneﬁciaries’ level of satisfaction and draw lessons learnt for future programming shifting this modality to another or beneficiaries are satisfied with program.

Please provide further explanation

Direct Aid Shelter and NFIs activities included in this project proposal have a solid contribution to durable solutions and resilience. In kind Based Interventions will enhance the resilience of affected populations. Through active engagement with traditional formal and informal leadership structures, traders, and women and youth groups, effective partnerships will transfer expertise and knowledge to improve their living conditions and equip the community with skills that they can use in the current location or wherever they move, including if they return to their areas of origin. DA will maintain a commitment to accountability to affected populations, including through transparent identiﬁcation and notiﬁcation of the project, inclusive and active engagement with the community during project design and implementation through focus group discussions and key informant interviews. Through multiple and continuous discussions, A will engage directly with the community to better understand their intentions in recognition of the converging crises. The community will play a bigger role in almost all aspects of programme implementation, proving a crucial partner in the process of selecting Vendors, sensitizing beneﬁciaries and the wider community, responding to feedback/complaints mechanism and organizing the skills trainings. Allowing the community to assume increased responsibility for the improvement of their living conditions will restore their dignity and strengthen the self-suﬃciency of the affected population. This applies to distributions for ESK and NFI assistance for both newly and protracted populations but also for those beneﬁting for trainings and other initiatives. In order to ensure in-kind ESK and NFIs activities are sustainable and do not create dependency, DA will liaise with durable solutions stakeholders, including DA Durable Solutions team, to guarantee beneficiaries, while building towards the achievement of durable solutions for displacement affected population. Similarly, Da's Shelter project will be integrated with other sectors (WASH, , Education and protection) and will adopt area based integrated programming to have maximum impact and have more sustainability, resilience and durability .Disasters, extreme climate events, conflict and humanitarian emergencies disproportionately affect people with disabilities who often experience barriers in accessing information, protection and assistance. People with disabilities are less likely to participate in community disaster risk reduction processes, more likely to be left behind, be injured, and be separated from family and caregivers during an emergency, and face extra barriers in accessing humanitarian assistance.Most of the IDPs live in the sites that are overcrowded and are not planned, leading to the increased risk of fire outbreak, spread of diseases including COVID 19, GBV related incidences and flooding due to the absence of or poor drainage system. Persons with disabilities face additional challenges due to narrow and irregular streets and difficulties in accessing services ,Direct Aid project beneficiaries will be 10% people with special needs and accessibility to the distribution will eased.
