Project Summary

Based on community participation, the project is designed based on the following:
Increasing the food security and enhancing the livelihoods of IDPs and host communities (50% IDPs, 50 % host communities) in Idlib and Aleppo Governorate through the following:
• First stage: (compost production).
This intervention will seek to produce organic fertilizer (compost) through recycling residues of tree leaves and weeds, pruning, animal residues (manure) and food residues, to achieve self-sufficiency from fertilizers on the family level.
• The second stage: (cultivate strategic crops).
This intervention will seek to support these households to begin to restore this production, as main sources of livelihoods and to enhance their resilience by enabling them to have more food availability through personal production of the crop products In addition to improving the crops value chain by increasing production, this would also energize local markets.
• The third stage: (Supporting the productive assets of livestock breeders by distribute (sheep, cows and dairy processing equipment) in addition to supporting basic value chains)
This intervention will seek to support beneficiaries to establish or improve income -generating activities by providing them with productive inputs.
• The fourth stage: (Supporting livestock breeders with fodder and veterinary services (vaccines and treatments).
This intervention will seek to decrease the number of livestock deaths, raising the health level of animals, increasing animal production and increase income generation for families and helping them to keep their livestock as a productive asset by providing fodder and veterinary services (vaccines and treatments).
• The fifth stage: (Establishing a veterinary laboratory to support animal breeders and veterinarians by providing appropriate diagnosis and treatment quickly and effectively).
The third intervention will seek to assist the veterinarians in diagnosing infectious animal diseases and confirmation of the diagnosis of suspect or clinical cases and confirming that an animal population is free from infectious disease and promotes the responsible use of antibiotics; thus, enhancing not only the control of animal diseases but also food safety and public health.
All stages involve arrangements for appropriate storage, handling, distribution, treatment and vaccination services.
The project will directly benefit about 1000 HH farmer with (compost, extension services, agricultural inputs and Support the marketing), 500 animal breeder and Smallholder farmers with (sheep, cows, and dairy processing equipment), about 60000 of sheep and goat and 7000 cattle by providing fodder and veterinary services (a total of approximately 5000 family) and about 10000 HHs by providing diagnosis and treatment.
Totally the project will indirectly benefit about 3.55 million animal breeders, milk and meat consumers as well as the dairy industry and local economy.

4 - Needs

Ataa conducted RNA by M&E and our technical tame in order to get more clarity and information on current situation in NWS.50 interviews were conducted with farmers, livelihoods KIIs and households from the local community and authority. The assessment covered 39 communities in 14 sub-districts in 7 different districts (Jarablus, Afrin, A'zaz, Ariha, Harim, Idleb, Jisr-Ash-Shugur) in Aleppo and Idleb governorate.Based on assessment results the Key Findings was as following:•Farmers experiencing a lot of agricultural problems especially regarding land degradation, the inappropriate use of inorganic fertilizers, soil poverty with mineral elements and a decrease of soil biodiversity.•There are many areas that mainly depend on agriculture production (strategic crops) and contain irrigation sources.•High prices of agricultural inputs (fertilizers, fuel, and pesticides) led to the reluctance of farmers to cultivate their lands.•Lack of modern techniques and laboratory service for the diagnosing infectious animal diseases increase the period and price of treatment, decreases the productivity of animal and the daily income of breeders. •Epidemic and infectious diseases, e.g. PPR, FMD, LSD, smallpox lead to increase the mortality of animals.•Weak livestock support services and low numbers of well-trained field veterinarians which lower livestock productivity.•Low quality of drugs and artificial insemination, though this may be the result of incorrect application or administration.•Inadequate grazing land and animal feeding as a result of high price of fodder that led to decreases in quantity and quality of output.•Poor application for supply value chain.•Lack of veterinary guideline to breeder and farmer.Eventually, It must be noted due to military advancement in Idlib province there is high IDPs movement towards Aleppo province. And there is high percentage of IDPs forced to selling their livestock partially or fully to adjust to the new circumstances.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project is also aligned with and contributes to the strategic objectives of the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) 2020 FSA-SO2: Support the self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating Income-generating opportunities to save and sustain lives through: 1- sustainable increase in small-scale food production, build resilience and prevent food insecurity and malnutrition of crisis-affected families through producing organic fertilizer, provide agricultural inputs, livestock distribution and livestock health services (vaccines, treatments and laboratory services). That positively impacting on their food security and income. 2- Support the self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income-generating opportunities to save and sustain lives.The Project will be initiated through a baseline to help refine beneficiary selection criteria. The procurement will be done through Ataa procurement procedures to ensure that the quality of all the requirements conformity to standards.Prior to project implementation, the Monitoring and Evaluation team will communicate M&E tools with the project team (logical framework and indicator plan), which will reflect all the project components. Will explain the project’s objectives, outcomes expected from the project, the indicators will be communicated as well as the means of verification, that will provide an overview of the performance of the projects, the results obtain and the impact upon key stakeholders. These results will be measured through spot visits to the projects, interviews with the beneficiaries, and reviews of project documents.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project was designed in a manner that benefits all categories of the target community, including women, children, people with disabilities and the most vulnerable categories, and the project will focus on the woman, especially the woman who heads a family.Equality will also be achieved in the recruitment process and personnel selection.The protection risks:1- Sexual exploitation and abuse including workplace harassment and child abuse by ATAA personnel, its partners, services providers/suppliers toward the beneficiaries especially women and girls. The mitigation:- Mainstreaming PSEA/ workplace harassment policy, in addition to CSG policy including training ATAA`s personnel.- requiring ATAA`s personnel, partners, services providers/suppliers to sign a commitment of the protection policy.- Activate the complaint mechanism and penalty procedures.- Awareness-raising sessions to the beneficiaries about sexual exploitation and abuse, and their rights, and aware them of complaints mechanism.- Assign a protection officer to monitor all protection standards during the implementation of all of the project activities2- During the distribution of agricultural inputs, the risks of GBV may emerge. The mitigation:- Allocation of special days for women regarding the distribution of inputs and ATAA will ensure that women &_men on the field staff have the necessary awareness of these risks.- Activating the mechanism of referring survivors of SEA/GBV to the specialist services- The distributing vouchers will help organize work and mitigate this risk.3- Community tension due to beneficiaries' selection process leading to a feeling of discrimination. The mitigation: - Clarify the criteria and procedures for choosing the beneficiary - Establish a complaint mechanisms at community level.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Markets are functioning and all the inputs are available. The crops varieties are stapled and adapted to the environment. There is social acceptance for the project and the people are prepared to interact with it. ATAA will contract with many local markets to avoid fraud Where risk has been classified into protection, environmental and security risks. Environmental risks: 1-Plant diseases will be addressed through the distribution of pesticides in a timely manner in addition to strict observation by the competent agronomists through continuous tours of farmers' fields to investigate any diseases and advise farmers accordingly. 2- Fire during the harvest season: there is a high risk during the harvest due to factors such as high temperature or neglect, in addition to the Deliberate shelling during harvest. Raising the awareness of the local community about the dangers of fire and methods to mitigate it by: -Removing the weeds on the roadside -Work the lines of fire (firebreaks)between farmers' lands to prevent the spread of fire from one field to another. -Coordination with the Civil Defense -Distribute awareness-raising brochures to the community about the importance of cooperation to avoid fires. -Security risk: This will be processed with strict follow-up by the S&S Department.for livestock intervention the risks will be addressed as follows:A.In the case of difficulty in securing the quantity of required sheep from one location:We will purchase from more than one supplier according to the approved procurement policies and alternative supply plans.B.beneficiaries may negligee sheep and the occurrence of some acute diseases that cause mortality or poor production:This risk will be addressed based on the beneficiary training and scheduling visits by team and vet according to the location of the beneficiary so that the sheep are closely monitored to avoid disease and to find means of communication for emergency response.
