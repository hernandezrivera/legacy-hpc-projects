Project Summary

The project’s aim is to provide a range of protection services to migrants in need, strengthen links between targeted community groups and services available, and enhance knowledge on migrant population groups in Lebanon. Services will target both male and female migrants, with a higher caseload of the latter group due to their increased number in country. Beneficiaries will typically be working age, between 18 and 40 years old. The services that IOM will deliver, either directly or through partners, include legal assistance, mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), safe and temporary shelters for vulnerable migrants, provision of non-food items, multipurpose cash assistance (MPCA), and voluntary humanitarian return. These services will be made accessible to migrants by scaling up IOM’s pre-existing case management services, supporting enhanced capacity of, and referral with partners, strengthening translation and interpretation efforts to diminish language barriers facing migrants, and expanding community-level networks to strengthen targeted communities’ awareness of and access to IOM and partner services, as well as wider issues of concern. Finally, enhancing knowledge of migrant populations will involve nationwide data-collection efforts via key informant interviews in order to inform humanitarian planning. The project will be implemented by the IOM in partnership with civil society organizations (CSO) and community-based initiatives, with some services such as shelter, legal assistance referred from IOM. IOM will work with local service providers to strengthen their capacity, to enhance the overall protection network available for migrants in Lebanon. Onward referral to multisector assistance such as food or health support will be mainstreamed across the project.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Sector Objective 1: Provide protection services to migrants in vulnerable situations. • Provide legal assistance including awareness, counselling, and legal aid. • Establish grassroot support systems, training on Psychological First Aid and victim-identification.• Provide MHPSS services offering counselling support, workshops, therapy. • Establish and train interpreters to enable delivery of MHPSS and legal services in migrant-specific language. • Translate awareness leaflets into migrant specific languages.• Provide humanitarian assistance to irregular migrants returning to Lebanon from attempted maritime migration attempts. Sector Objective 2: Provide multi-sector lifesaving assistance to migrants in vulnerable situations. • Provide multi-purpose cash assistance to 1,500 migrants affected by ongoing crisis.• Provide non-food items to 1,500 migrants• Provide safe / temporary shelters to 500 migrants. • Conduct data collection across Lebanon.• Compile, and analyze data and publish report for humanitarian planning. Sector Objective 3: Facilitate the voluntary humanitarian return of migrants to their country of origin. • Provide voluntary humanitarian. To include counseling, pre-departure health checks, liaising with embassies and General Security to secure travel documents and exit permits respectively, airport escorts, in-flight and post-arrival escort for children, the elderly, sick or disabled passengers, pre-departure and post-arrival cash assistance.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

As part of its implementation strategy, IOM will focus on promoting resilience and sustainability as well as ensuring IOM’s programming are safe, dignified, and accessible to all. This will be achieved by ensuring women’s and girls’ voices and migrant-led initiatives are promoted during all phases of the response; enhance community-led initiatives by engagement of outreach volunteers to expand awareness of, and referrals to available services; train partners and outreach volunteers on protection, GBV core concepts, PFA, humanitarian principles, risk identification and mitigation measures, data protection, PSEA, AAP and code of conduct; support evidence-based response and learning between partners using data collected through direct assistance.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

IOM’s approach toward meeting AAP standards, will be guided by a range of frameworks and tools developed by IOM HQ, and designed to see key principals put into working practice. These frameworks cover a range of themes and issues, from operating in crisis contexts, gender equality, mainstreaming protection, and humanitarian principles. IOM’s AAP approach, will see a critical attention paid to upholding several core operational principles, including do no harm, non-discrimination, zero tolerance for sexual abuse and exploitation, data protection, principled humanitarian action, and protection.
