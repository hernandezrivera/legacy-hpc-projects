Project Summary

After 12 years of hostilities and persistent economic crisis, at the beginning of 2024 and
based on the HNO 2024, 15 million people are assessed to need humanitarian Protection
assistance such as (PSS, CP, GBV).
Regarding the last general need assessment which conducted by Research and
Development unit in GOPA-DERD 2024, the protection risks in Syria continue to have a
multifaceted and differential impact on all population groups especially after Earthquake
in affected areas. Displaced women, men, girls and boys in collective shelters and
informal settlements are particularly vulnerable and live in some of the hardest places to
reach. (GBV) continues to increase in the country, affecting women and girls’ coping
strategies and depleting their resilience, in addition of the risk of GBV has increased due
to the instability of the security situation and according to the Protection Assessment,
female heads of households, pregnant and lactating women, and persons with disabilities
are among the most vulnerable groups affected. Based on the recommendation of the
last General need assessment which conducted by (R&D) unit in GOPA-DERD the
protection assistance will raise the level of awareness and community participation to
enhancing the process of reintegration of survivors of violence and even people with
psychological disorders into society again after receiving the various services available.
GOPA-DERD will conduct protection activities for the affected families through
community engagement, awareness campaigns, and support for women's empowerment
initiatives, provide specialized psychological services, including trauma management and
psychosocial support, implement awareness campaigns to educate affected populations
and humanitarian workers about the risks of sexual exploitation, and available support
services, provide referral and case management services and ensure the continuation of
providing services on a long-term basis to ensure that. The severity scales of selected
locations:
- Rural Damascus - Jaramana, PIN are 300,628 the severity scale (3-4)
- Idlib - Khan Shaykhun, PIN are 242,290, the SS is (3-4)
- Hama - Mharda, PIN are 375,320, the SS is (3-4)
Accordingly, we will carry out the following activities: case management - structure groups - campaigns - psychiatrist and psychotherapist referrals and cash for protection for all vulnerable families.
• PSEA strategy: GOPA-DERD provides immediate and confidential mechanisms for reporting potential or actual SEA through a specific e-mail used solely for this purpose, by calling the hotline, where they will receive the appropriate follow-up Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures: The ability to operate freely and impartially due to unwanted interference by stakeholders and access in providing services to the people most affected by the crisis in terms of need and vulnerability, Analysis of all potential outcomes including Do-No-Harm analysis, Stay transparent from the very beginning, Strong negotiation capacities. • GOPA-DERD staff and related above-mentioned figure(s) are completely aware of the core principles of PSEA policy.
Risks: Content of message is not fully adapted to the social and cultural environment and creates misunderstanding or even tensions and has repercussions on the organisation implementing the campaign: Consult with population groups and communities on most effective way of delivering messages and their contents; consult with line ministries and departments on the organisation of mass campaign.
Exclusion of some groups due to the choice of the location: Consult with communities in the preparation of the messages as needed to context also based on their feedback after dissemination.
Tension within the family, including risks to physical violence or safety and disruption of the previous family dynamic (esp. On cash assistance): Establish adequate selection criteria mechanisms based on vulnerabilities

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The activities which will be conducted: Case management: GOPA-DERD will utilize the inter-agency CM guidelines to provide care and case management services to the general vulnerable in humanitarian settings. GBV CM services within each family safe space Center and community-based (non-facility) CM through mobile units staffed with case workers and community outreach staff. These mobile units will additionally support improved reach to those PWDs who may not otherwise be able to access relevant information or services. We will enable Vulnerable people through case management and referrals to different services internally or externally with focusing on developing the skills of women, girls, men and priority will be given to PwDs to train in promoting skills and refer them toother organizations or facilities. In order to ensure that vulnerable people can be safely linked to multi-sectoral services, the Family safe space will have referral pathways in place and will coordinate with other organizations including the GBV, PSS, CP. Through these referral mechanisms, the vulnerable can access supportive and competent services such as case management, health/MHPSS, and police assistance (if applicable) after providing their informed consent. Each case will have a developed plan with the case manager based on the need. Group Structure: Group sessions on PSS/ GBV. These sessions will address topics of positive parenting skills, protection, and support for parents and children, as the curriculum is divided into different developmental age groups. The activities for girls exposed to early marriage in adolescence, up to 21years of age, in order to raise awareness in terms of sexual, physical, and psychological health and dealing with children for dealing with the elderly in the region to enhance their role and participation and enhance their status. Distribution of dignity kits for women and girls, elderly, and kits for mother and baby at the end of the group sessions. International Days Events: International days and weeks are occasions to educate the public on issues of concern, to mobilize political will and resources to address global problems, and to celebrate and reinforce achievements of humanity. Cash for protection: The cash provision service is one of the most important services provided, especially due to economic conditions, the high cost of living, and the inability to meet basic needs. The service will be provided through Al-Fouad or Al-Haram centers by providing transportation for the beneficiaries to reach the center and receive the money, the required need, exit and safety plan will be assessed based on selection criteria. Referral to a psychiatrist and psychotherapist: After the psychosocial evaluation is provided by the case managers, the referral is made to the psychiatrist and psychotherapist to provide the specialized service, especially since the psychological pressures, economic conditions, natural disasters(earthquake)
