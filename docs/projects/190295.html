Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to the rehabilitation of livelihoods of 40,000 households to pre-conflict levels through the support of seeds, fertilizers, farm tools and traction power, essential animal drugs and vaccines for use in the upcoming primary cropping season locally called meher (June-September). This intervention is expected to reduce the need for emergency food assistance starting from the next harvest season around the end of 2022. FH will target only 50% (4,000) households in five zones (Central Tigray, North Gonar, Wag Himera, North Wollo, Kamashi and East Wollega) of the FAO-prioritized areas severely affected by conflict. These six zones are located where FH has an ongoing emergency and development programs.
FH will provide crop and vegetable seeds), chemical fertilizers (urea and NPS), different farm tools and traction power support, animal drugs and vaccines. FH will implement the project in partnership with the interim administration at the Woreda, Zone and Regional levels and in coordination with other humanitarian actors and ECC (Emergency Coordination Center). FH’s ongoing emergency and development projects in these zones and the established offices and connections with the local administration and target communities, and the long established relationship with federal and regional seed enterprises, and transport companies are expected to provide significant leverage for the successful implementation of this project.
FH’s and its local partner (EKHCDC) staff in these locations will manage the interventions using existing project offices, facilities and charge a proportional amount to this project. Additional full-time staff will be hired for technical support, and to distribute all agricultural inputs.
This proposed project will complement the ongoing USAID/BHA, WFP, DRA funded emergency food assistance, MPC, shelters, WASH projects. It will help maintain the gains of the immediate food assistance by rehabilitating the food production to the pre-conflict levels to prevent further deterioration of the food insecurity situation.
The total proposed budget requested from potential donors is US$ 2.6 million.
