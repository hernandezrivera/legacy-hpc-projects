Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to contribute to strengthening prevention, mitigation and response to GBV issues as well as strengthening quality of care for GBV survivors. This will build on the already gained milestones from 2019 that has already enabled AYA to provide community based women and girls friendly spaces in Mundri East, and Ibba in Western Equatoria State. Also continuous provision of case management services. However, these established structures have become few and capacities of communities to run the centers themselves is still weak which needed strengthening for sustainability.
AYA intends continue running and equipping women and girls friendly spaces established in Mundri East, West and Ibba and work on establishing two new centers. AYA as active GBV prevention organization, is also planning strengthen its EMAP capacities and continue engaging men to address the root causes of Gender Based Violence in the said communities of South Sudan. Community outreach activities will form cornerstone of activities, PSS services, case management services and livelihood opportunities for sustainable peace in communities shall be part of the project. These will create change in communities that the project aims to support. AYA will create awareness through women and girls friendly spaces, the awareness shall be on available services, small income generation activities as part of psychosocial support, recreational activities tailored to needs of each community and support building capacities of women and girls especially the most vulnerable women and girls in vocational skills for improved livelihoods.
There is already existing referral pathway for Mundri East and Mundri West, AYA will continuously update it and create the referral pathways for Ibba County.
AYA during the previuos projects, have built the capacities of staffs who will do mobile or static response activities. There is also full set of prevention staffs who disseminate messages on radios, who are trained on Engaging Men through Accountable Practices (EMAP) and AYA is one of competent few NGOs implementing EMAP in the country. AYA will therefore, only work on strengthening the capacity of its project staffs.

1 - Needs

South Sudan is one of the emergency protracted Countries in Africa. The conflict that started in 2013 re-erupted in 2016 after the fighting that took place in the Capital Juba and this also led to emerging of other rebel groups like NAS that started in March 2017 and United Democratic Front (April 2017). These posed threats on people at various levels.Ibba County is one of the counties found in the newly created Maridi State. The County is hosting 9112 Internally Displaced Persons and 6340 returnees. Ibba along with Maridi County has been the center for 2016 crisis. Though during the aftermath of the conflict, there has been no assistance to the people living in the area, Internally Displaced Persons and the Returnees who came back and found that their houses were destroyed and others occupied.In Mundri East, continuous conflict and simultaneous return by the Internally Displaced Persons who were in Mundri West and Dolomayi has been still the major challenge. The county is currently hosting 7086 returnees and 2718 Internally Displaced Persons who are currently found in Lui, Kedi'ba, Wandi, Magyiri and Bekenye. The conflicts have left lasting effect on the people. This conflict led to loss of properties, torturing, killing, rape which is also employed by the armed actors, denial of humanitarian access by the warring parties, forceful recruitment of women and children.In Mundri West, Ngiri is one of the cantonment sites in Western Equatoria. It is located on Southern side of Mundri West. The creation of cantonment sites has problems associated with harassment of women and girls, occupation of civilian homes, land grabbing also reported and this is potential for further conflict. Furthermore, the exploitation of women and girls sexually is also imminent. Not only has this but Mundri been center for Internally Displaced Persons. The County hosted the IDPs from Kedi'ba that came in December 20161. Mundri recently received more than two thousand returnees from Maridi, Yambio and Nzara. These people were left in disarray and now been hosted by some relatives and friends. The congestion at home is high and no privacy respected.Given the above scenarios, women and girls are the most affected people. The elderly, persons living with disability and the children face the huge burden of the conflict related abuses. In Internally Displaced Camps, almost 60% of individuals are women and girls. This clearly indicated that they are the sufferers in the face of conflict. As witnessed from the returnees especially in the above locations, 60% are also women and girls. Challenges related to cantonment are also endured by women and girls. Despite the fact that women face the burden of the conflict related abuse, the men and the society continually degrade the position at which women and girls are placed. The traditional roles assigned by the community affect mostly women and girls. Primarily, women and girls have no choice in relation to matters concerning them. They are to raise children, cook food, fetch water and firewood, wash clothes, wash dishes and at the end of the day forced to marry regardless of whom to marry. This therefore, calls for the involvement of men and boys in talks against violence that affect women and girls and turns around to have ripple effect on the community.Though women and girls face the huge challenge, there has been limited capacity to respond to issues pertaining to women and girls. AYA is the only organization responding and conducting GBV prevention activities in Mundri East, Mundri West. In Ibba, there has been no GBV partner implementing GBV related intervention. Therefore, need to intervene for the better support to the communities that have gone through many and repeated atrocities. There have been no adequate psycho-social support activities, case management activities in Ibba. While in Mundri East and West, AYA has been providing but with also limited coverage.

2 - Activities

1. Individual and Group Psycho-social Support: AYA team of trained Social Workers will conduct both group and individual Psycho-social support to women, girls, boys and men who have experienced the violence. The support will be done in structural or unstructured manner. 3000 beneficiaries will be reached with 60% women and girls while 40% men and boys will be targeted. 2. Case Management: AYA is planning to support case management services in the selected communities of Ibba, Mundri East and Mundri West Counties. The supports will be through the equipment of the areas with the items for case management such as lockable cupboards for the Ibba, while in Mundri East and West there will only be continuous referrals, support and linking of the survivors for the available GBV services. In 2020, 75 individuals are targeted to benefit from the case management services conducted by the Active Youth Agency. 3. Develop and Disseminate GBV Referrals pathway for Ibba, update referral pathways form Mundri West and Mundri East: AYA will conduct service mapping, establish contacts with all the service providers, rewrite the GBV referral pathway for Mundri East, and disseminate to the community in the areas of the project. The referral pathway includes the contact details of the available focal point for each available service with their time frame. In doing, the service providers will be called in a two days development and dissemination sessions. 4. Training of community volunteers/Mobilizers and community leaders: AYA protection officers and the community leaders from the operational areas will select 30 community Mobilizers, 60 community opinion leader as chiefs, youth leaders, women leaders, church elders/pastors, and healthy workers to be trained together with AYA Social workers on GBV guiding principles for working with GBV survivor, PFA, and GBV referral pathway for easier accessibility of services for clients. This training will therefore last for three (3) days with 60% female and 40% male participants from each community. 5. Creation of peer support groups and support them on weekly basis: Due to the stressful situation communities of Ibba, Mundri West and Mundri East. These groups will be strengthened and become the community protection committees. They will comprise of women in their separate group, community leaders especially men in their separate groups and youths both boys and girls in their separate groups. Each group will be given two days training and continuous mentorship by AYA project staffs. These groups will act as eyes and support each other at the face of the conflict. 20 persons per group and four groups in each county. 6. Conducting bi-weekly awareness raising in the communities. The awareness shall focus on GBV risk mitigation, community re-integration, early warning systems and services available for the GBV survivors. The awareness shall be organized in a large form and music dance and drama performance is one of the key information dissemination methodologies AYA shall use. 7. EMAP programming for addressing root causes of Gender Based Violence. AYA as trained EMAP implementing partner would like to have EMAP started in Mundri West. The county is now stable for implementation of EMAP. Therefore, 5 men dialogue group and 5 women dialogue groups will be started and 8 weeks sessions for women will commence and 16 weeks sessions for men will also be done. 8. Establishment and running of women and girls friendly spaces. AYA intends to establish two women and girls friendly spaces in Ibba, continue run one women and girls friendly spaces in Mundri West and continue running two women and girls friendly spaces in Mundri East. Therefore, together 5 women and girls friendly spaces will be supported in 2020. Multi-sectoral activities will be conducted. Including Income Generating Support to 300 women and girls.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

Proper planning, consultation of every group of beneficiaries, disseminating PSEA messages, strengthening community complaint mechanism, repeated dissemination of humanitarian principles to the key stakeholders, training for staffs also on key GBV guiding principles and conducting assessments prior to the distribution of items.AYA will orient all of its volunteers on six principles of PSEA. This will include the part-time volunteers and contractors. Community awareness shall be done to communities on issues pertaining to the sexual exploitation sexual abuse. The contacts for the focal points shall also be given to community members to enable reports of any act the constitutes SEA. Thoroughly investigation shall be summoned when any SEA related incidence reported. Therefore, proper feedback mechanism to the community shall be established to generate and disseminate some feedback to the communities.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

Through targeted focus group discussions with the persons with special needs to communicate their level of satisfactions and communicate their grievances. AYA has already established complaint mechanisms that gathers, analyze and disseminate the results for decision making. The complaint desk is established in each women and girls friendly spaces and a dedicated personnel is assigned to run it. AYA also have hotline used for documentation and follow up of the complaints raised against the services AYA provide and others that are provided by other agencies. Ative Youth Agency is the organization that is sexual violence free. The organization have trained PSEA Focal Points and their contacts are availed to community to report any SEA issues. AYA will therefore, only capitalize on the full usage of the existing focal points and continue providing the services. AYA will also pass PSEA information to other humanitarian organization that operate in the same location. This will enable the other organizations also to support reducing the impact of SEA on communities.There shall also be a focus on the persons living with disabilities. These people will be contacted and seek from them specific feedback in relation to the services offered to them. AYA as one of organization working with persons with disability will also use its networks to generate feedback from the persons of that same category. AYA will also work through other NGOs implementing Disability programs to get feedback that will inform future programing or adjustment on the existing programs.
