Project Summary

This school feeding project is covering the nutrition needs of school-going children. A locally prepared healthy sandwich and a fruit or a vegetable are distributed in nine schools in Jebel Saman sub-dstrict in Aleppo. Through this specific provision, parents are convinced to send their children to school again and not to keep them at home, so that they can pursue their education and through that gaining a perspective for their future and lay the basis to become productive citizens in the future. The healthy sandwiches and fruits or vegetables also contribute to a balanced diet of the children and ease the financial burden on the parents.

Needs

The conflict in Syria grew out of what started as the civil unrest in 2011. Since then several million of civilians have been displaced, some have sought refuge in neighboring countries others have undertaken the journey to Europe in search for a newer, saver home. Specifically to Aleppo, around 30% of HHs mis at least one member (Humanitarian Needs Assessment Programme for Syria (HNAP) June 2018. Amongst the civilians that remained in Syria 6.1m have been displaced, some of them already several times due to changing front lines (HNO2018). In 2018 1,250,956 Syrians returned, the top sub-district that welcomed returnees were Jebel Saman, Aleppo (MNM, Dec 2018). 89% of them returned to their own house, often needing to restart their lives there completely. In this fluent, uncertain situation children and women bear the burden of the consequences of the conflict. Many families live in dire needs and parents struggle to provide, because of lost livelihoods or unemployment. This significantly reduces parent's ability to provide for themselves and for their families. In the meantime, savings are depleted after several years of conflict and poverty is on the rise. A frequently observed coping mechanism is that due to family's economic needs, boys are kept at home so that they can assist in earning income, and girls are taking over the domestic role of their mother. This negative coping mechanism severely hampers the future perspective of this generation of school going children. If children miss out on classes, it becomes increasingly difficult to re-enter them into school again, as they have lost motivation, interest and don’t see the purpose of going to school compared to working or being out of school. This situation lead to the significant needs of children in Aleppo, sub-district Jebel Saman, where 525,830 children and 10,955 teachers are in need (PiN) (HNO2019). The severity ranking of it is at 2 as still substantial needs remain uncovered despite joint efforts throughout the last years by humanitarian actors focusing on this area (HNO2019).

Implementation of activities

Healthy sandwiches are daily prepared in a central location in Aleppo by trained volunteers. From there the healthy meals are distributed to the nine schools included in the project. Healthy sandwich plus a fruit or vegetable will be handed out to every child that attends school. This project of school feeding intends to convince parents to send their children to school and not to keep them at home, so that they can pursue their education and thus gaining a perspective for their future as productive citizens. Healthy sandwiches and fruits of vegetables also contribute to a balanced diet of the children, enable them to concentrate better during school and ease the financial burden on the parents.Dorcas has already signed a funding contract with a private foundation for this project, it is fully covered.
