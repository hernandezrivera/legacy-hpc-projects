Project Summary

The project " Child Protection through improving the well-being of vulnerable household at IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities in South Darfur." aims to ensure the provision of preventive and response programmes and the capacities of local communities to improve the well-being, resilience and protective environment of vulnerable households with girls and boys at risk as well homeless children in selected 8 localities and 5 IDPs camps in South Darfur.The protracted conflicts in Darfur with reoccurrences of natural disasters including floods, climate change and dry spells as well the economic crises with unseen strategies for solutions exacerbating the poverty rates among most households in South Darfur , these multiple factors continue to affect the life and livelihoods of many vulnerable children in South Darfur as consequences to family separation, child labour and economic exploitation, sexual violence and gender based violence , psycho-social distress and trauma. The future of young girls and boys are at risk, where most families are unable to cover school expenses for their children, moreover the miserable living conditions forced families to send their children for work as child labour with the aim of generating additional income and contribute on family’s , the thing that depriving many children from enrolling in regular school classes , as well there is uncontrolled governance over their kids and this is because of willingness of children for employment, and the most destructive thing is the involvement of children on drugs use and exploiting them on the activities of drugs promotion.In a wake of these miserable circumstances; children continue to be at risk of GBV and in particular child labour which exposing them to sexual violence in addition to school and domestic violence. Young teenage girls are particularly facing to various forms of violation; relative torture and violence and many cases ending with death in some localities in South Darfur, the insufficient income in the households could potentially lead to survival sex among young adolescent girls, homeless children are exposure to all forms of violence, rape and sexual exploitation because of their social status as no family carrying about, children at homes are also experiencing various types of domestic violence, lack of sanitary pads and sanitation services for adolescent girls at schools is a huge concern.
These factors show the depth of the problem and the needs in the state and the gap in child protection.
Sudan Social Development Organization (SUDO) seeks to strengthen preventive and response programs, enhancing the capacity of local community mainstream community-based approach in South Darfur to provide multi-sectors programming of advocacy and delivery of child Protection services, GBV prevention, Resilience and Access to Services for vulnerable and homeless children at IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities, improving the quality of community-based child protection interventions and specialized child protection services of case management and referrals, optimizing child protection outcomes through engagement with other sectors including education, strengthening and broadening existing Child Protection committees and networks at the community level to facilitate more sustainable, community-led CP risk mitigation and response measures.
In its work,SUDO tends to provide comprehensive case management support, including alternative care, family tracing and reunification, psycho-social support, advocacy and equipped with knowledge and life skills that reduce their vulnerability and enhance their resilience.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project will prioritize safety and dignity of beneficiaries mainly children, women and girls through the provision of psych-social and case management services, group psycho-social activities at the community child protection centers to provide beneficiary centered services. In addition this project will provide access to assistance and services without discrimination of sex or age and also ensuring that most vulnerable groups and groups with special needs are reached and assisted with appropriate confidentiality. This project through advocacy, empowerment of targeted groups as well as use of community based committees to enhance population’s ability to reclaim their rights. Increased awareness on prevention and child protection as well the consequences of GBV and enhanced utilization of available services for women, girls and boys affected by conflict. The community based support teams will support the GBV survivors reintegrate back in the community and reduce stigma attached to violence. To build resilience in communities by enhancing the capacity of service providers to support women, men, girls and boys affected by conflict.Do No Harm (DNH)/HAP:Within all it is interventions, SUDO is integrating DNH and HAP principles as part of human rights policy and to make sure that its interventions do not harm or negatively affect the children or community members involved with or targeted by project activities . Intergroup relations between IDPs, local communities and Refugees as well as different ethnicity, minority groups, gender and age categories in addition to people with disabilities are all at center of project activities.SUDO will strive to ensure that before commencing the project implementation to put consideration to its actions (theft, market effects, distribution effects, substitutions effects, legitimization effects) and the staff behavior (power, disrespect/mistrust, impunity, different values, powerlessness, belligerence/tension/ suspicion, publicity) analyzed carefully in the 9 principles HAP approach to minimize the negative impact of our work and maximize the positive impact accordingly.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

On Accountability to Affected Populations: SUDO will work to ensure accountability to the affected population (AAP) especially through participatory approaches and routinely consult and engage with the communities in general and children, women and girls in specific. Whilst monitoring will be progressive, evaluation will be mid-term and final and accountability reports (both financial and narrative) will be generated and shared as appropriate. Some specific measures/activities undertaken/planned to integrate Accountability to Children (Boys and Girls), Women, among Affected People in the project design, implementation and monitoring shall include the following: i) Consultation, and participation of, beneficiaries during the establishment of the project including vulnerable families and children (both girls and boys) will be actively involved in selection a space for child’s friendly spaces, identify the types of activities they want to happen at the child’s friendly spaces and monitor the progress of the program periodically, ii) Pre-implementation stakeholder workshops and locality-level cluster meetings;iii) Case study profiling and documentation of learning themes;iv) Use of community-based structures e.g. community leaders, child protection committees) vii) Community-led campaigns and advocacy initiatives during the child’s rights/child protection day and other events.viii) Use of dedicated local staff (knowledge of cultures, local contexts and operational environment, cost-effectiveness, sustainability…);ix) Collaboration with State/Locality authorities and partnerships with local actors and Child Protection Working Group.SUDO project staff will work closely and in full coordination with community-based protection mechanism through the approach of Monitoring Analysis and Reporting Arrangements (MARA) gathers information to identify protection concern (physical safety, material safety, legal safety issues, freedom of movement and residence) and most vulnerable individuals for assistance through this project, referrals and advocacy activities. The findings of the protection monitoring can be also consolidated and put in a locality level protection monitoring reports to be shared with protection sector at state and national level in both the MRM and MARA.
