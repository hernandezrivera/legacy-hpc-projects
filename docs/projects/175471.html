Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The Project will be realized in the districts of Metuge, Mecufi and Ibo. These districts were heavily affected by climatic shocks (such as the cyclone Kenneth, floods and heavy rains due to tropical storms), violence and extremist attacks, which triggered a huge internal displacement of the population and consequent increase of food insecurity, due to a greater competition for food, and loss of livelihoods. The objective of the project is to improve food security of the target population, through improved capacities on climate smart techniques, adapted to climate changes, making the beneficiaries more resilient and resistant to climate shocks, increasing food availability and providing livelihood opportunity. The distribution of tools and seeds will help to develop livelihood among IDPs and to improve the productivity of the hosting communities’ farmers. The development of home gardens will facilitate the production and access to food by IDPs and people who have reduced access to land.Female headed HH, families with disabilities, families with children under 5 years old, are most vulnerable to the lack of food security and opportunities of livelihood. For this purpose Oikos will use an inclusive and sensitive gender based approach to define most vulnerable potential beneficiaries, providing greater security and equality in the region. Target groups: Resident farmers of the target area and IDPs supported in agricultural activities;District extension officers that will receive technical trainings; Population of the target areas and IDPs that will benefit from an increased food security.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will support livelihood diversification and food security through:Distribution of agricultural inputs such as staple crop and vegetable seeds, and equipment; and related training (districts: Metuge, Mecufi and Ibo);Capacity building for farmers and IDPs on climate smart agriculture techniques (production, pest management, harvesting, post harvesting, storage and processing - districts: Metuge, Mecufi and Ibo);Capacity building for District extension officers (districts: Metuge, Mecufi and Ibo);Promotion the cultivation of home gardens for IDPs population and resident vulnerable population/groups who lack access to land (districts: Metuge, Mecufi and Ibo);Awareness and capacitation messages disseminations on agricultural livelihoods (radio, banners, manuals, etc.);Rehabilitation of agricultural related infrastructures including irrigation systems, postharvest structures and markets.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The main risk is the “Possible distrust and difficult acceptance from the part of the beneficiaries of the adoption of new adaptive agricultural techniques”.The project identified the following solutions:● The introduction of new agricultural practices will be made after consultations with the principal stakeholders and community leaders ensuring a better acceptance from everyone. ● Involvement of the community leaders on the diffusion of the messages. In fact, they have significant potential to influence the cultural norms. ● Holding single-sex, age segmented focus discussion group sessions on constrains to their behavior change.● Climate smart techniques inserted in the communities’ practices valorizing traditional knowledge and local productive culture and keeping in consideration taboos, preferences and food consumption patterns.The action follows a participatory approach for the design and implementation of activities. The action will ensure meaningful access to assistance through activities such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection.The project will empower the women to act as the first agent of change in their own communities. Specifically, the intervention methodology aims at increasing the capacities of the women to interconnect and transfer knowledge and experience by building their capacity development and awareness on the importance of utilizing climate smart techniques that are more sustainable and resistant to climatic shocks.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Local Institutional stakeholders will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities.All the activities have been structured with the aim of guaranteeing access to disable people, such as the selection of the location for the training and the project staff will guarantee all the support to allow them to participate in the actions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will provide capacity building for resident farmers and IDPs and extension officers on climate smart techniques, more sustainable and resilient to climate shocks, and on sustainable agricultural practices, that help to conserve the natural resources. Therefore, the project will have beneficial consequences for environmental sustainability.
