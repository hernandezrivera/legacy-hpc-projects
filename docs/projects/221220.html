Project Summary

The proposed project, titled "Strengthening Livelihoods for Conflict-Affected Farmers in the South West Region," aims to address the devastating impact of the socio-political crisis on the agricultural sector and livelihoods in Cameroon’s South West Region. The crisis has caused widespread displacement, loss of income, and food insecurity among farming communities, which depend heavily on agriculture for their survival.
The purpose of this intervention is to rebuild and strengthen agricultural livelihoods by equipping farmers with the tools, resources, and knowledge necessary to restore productivity and resilience. By focusing on conflict-affected households, particularly vulnerable groups such as women and youth, the intervention will promote sustainable agriculture, enhance food security, and support economic recovery in the region.
Target Population and Region
The project targets 500 conflict-affected farming households in selected communities within Ndian Division and neighboring areas of the South West Region of Cameroon. This population includes individuals who have lost access to farmland, agricultural inputs, and markets due to displacement and insecurity. Special attention will be given to vulnerable groups, including women-headed households, unemployed youth, and elderly farmers, who face greater barriers to recovery.
Core Components of the Intervention
Provision of Agricultural Inputs:
The intervention will supply farmers with high-quality seeds, tools, and fertilizers necessary for re-establishing crop production. These inputs will focus on staple crops such as maize, cassava, and vegetables, ensuring food security at the household level and providing surplus for income generation.
Livelihood Diversification:
To reduce reliance on crop farming and enhance income stability, farmers will be trained and supported to engage in alternative livelihoods, such as poultry production, bee-keeping, fish farming, mushroom farming, snails farming. These activities will complement traditional farming and provide a buffer against economic shocks.
Capacity Building on Sustainable Practices:
Training workshops will equip farmers with knowledge on sustainable and climate-smart agricultural techniques, including soil conservation, pest management, and crop diversification. These skills will increase productivity while minimizing environmental degradation.
Market Access Support:
The project will facilitate connections between farmers and local markets by organizing cooperatives, providing transport support, and strengthening value chain linkages. This will ensure farmers receive fair prices for their produce and reduce post-harvest losses.
Monitoring and Evaluation:
An M&E framework will track the progress and impact of the intervention, ensuring accountability and providing insights for continuous improvement.
Expected Impact
The intervention seeks to achieve the following outcomes:
Restored Agricultural Productivity:
Farmers will regain the ability to produce sufficient food to meet their household needs and sell surplus for income.
Improved Food Security:
By increasing crop yields and diversifying income sources, households will experience reduced food shortages and improved nutritional outcomes.
Strengthened Resilience:
Farmers will adopt sustainable practices that mitigate the impact of future shocks, such as climate change and conflict.
Enhanced Economic Opportunities:
Market linkages and alternative livelihoods will provide farmers with stable income streams, reducing dependency on external aid.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The intervention targets critical needs in the South West Region of Cameroon, particularly among conflict-affected farming communities. The socio-political crisis has disrupted livelihoods, exacerbated food insecurity, and reduced access to essential agricultural resources. Below is a description of the identified needs and the corresponding activities the project will implement to address them: 1. Agricultural Inputs and ResourcesAssessed Need:Farmers have lost access to quality seeds, tools, fertilizers, and other essential inputs due to displacement and insecurity. This has resulted in reduced agricultural productivity and widespread food insecurity. Project Activities:Distribution of high-quality seeds for staple crops like maize, cassava, and vegetables.Provision of essential farming tools such as hoes, shovels, and watering cans.Supply of fertilizers and pest control materials to improve crop health and yields.2. Restore LivelihoodsAssessed Need:Conflict-affected households face significant income loss due to the destruction of farmland, loss of livestock, and market disruptions. Many farmers are unable to diversify their income sources, leaving them vulnerable to economic shocks. Project Activities:Introduction of alternative livelihood options, such as poultry farming, bee-keeping, and fish farming, with training and startup kits provided.Support for farmers to establish small businesses linked to agricultural value chains, such as food processing or local seed production.3. Food Security and Nutrition ImprovementAssessed Need:Displacement and reduced agricultural activity have resulted in food shortages and poor dietary diversity, particularly among vulnerable populations like women and children. Project Activities:Establishment of community and household gardens to provide fresh, nutrient-rich vegetables.Training on nutrition-sensitive agriculture to promote the cultivation of diverse and high-nutritional-value crops.4. Need for Knowledge and Skills on Sustainable PracticesAssessed Need:Farmers lack access to knowledge on sustainable agricultural techniques, which are critical for improving yields, conserving soil, and adapting to climate challenges. Project Activities:Training workshops on sustainable and climate-smart agricultural practices, such as intercropping, soil conservation, and drought-resistant crop varieties.Capacity-building sessions on post-harvest handling and storage to reduce losses and enhance food availability.5. Access to MarketsAssessed Need:Conflict has disrupted local and regional markets, making it difficult for farmers to sell their produce and access fair pricing. Transportation challenges further hinder their ability to participate in value chains.Project Activities:Facilitation of market linkages by organizing farmer cooperatives to strengthen collective bargaining power.Support for the transportation of produce to local and regional markets, reducing logistical barriers.Training farmers on basic marketing strategies and value addition to increase the profitability of their produce.6. Community Resilience Against Future ShocksAssessed Need:Farmers remain highly vulnerable to future crises, including climate change impacts and prolonged conflict. There is a need to build resilience through sustainable systems and practices.Project Activities:Promotion of crop diversification and agroforestry to enhance environmental and economic resilience.Establishment of farmer cooperatives and community groups for shared resources, skills, and risk mitigation.Development of disaster preparedness strategies tailored to agricultural contexts.
