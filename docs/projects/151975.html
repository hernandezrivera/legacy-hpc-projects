Project Summary

The insurgency has left much negative impact on the various communities in the BAY states and due to the continuous insurgency, many of the IDPs remain in host communities, many have returned to their LGAs and only to meet devastation and are in despair of how to rebuild their livelihoods. This is also the challenge faced by many within these host communities. It is imperative that critical and strategic interventions with multi-layered approaches needs to be deployed to support these groups of people. LHI's objective through the implementation of this project is, 'Supporting livelihoods and income recovery’ for community members especially women and adolescent young boys and girls of age between 15 -55 years of age in Jere, Maiduguri, Bama, Gwoza of Borno State and in Potiskum, Fika and Fune of Yobe State, targeting IDPs in the host communities, Returnees as well as members of the host communities. LHI shall deploy an integrated response approach to ensure that IDP in the Host Communities, Returnees as well as host communities have their livelihoods improved. The proposed activities will aim to address needs identified under the Livelihoods needs, income support and generation and social cohesion needs. The activities will provide immediate opportunities to increase individual and household income base, create an enabling environment for wealth creation through group savings and loan association, dissemination of appropriate business management knowledge and financial literacy, market dynamic information, vocational and technical skill training, empowered and peaceful environment as well as a viable government socio-economic policy.

Needs

The recently concluded and released Multi-sector Needs Assessment & Livelihood and the Economic Recovery Assessments revealed that there are limited income and livelihood opportunities, and increased resource demands leading to societal tensions between Internally Displaced Persons [IDPs] within the host communities, returnees and the host communities. These has impacted on young adolescent boys and girls negatively, leaving them virtually with no proper education, no set of economic skills that can support them for any profitable economic life and living; women and men are faced with uphill task of finding noble path for survival and growth, left largely without opportunities, which invariably affect their levels of participation in the decision making processes within the household and in their immediate environment. This has further fueled the tension, the frustration and anger within and without, created untold hardships and despair. To address these underlying needs among these affected people through this project, Life Helpers Initiative [LHI] shall be implementing targeted interventions that contribute to immediate income and livelihoods support through individual skills development, provide a mutual and collective platforms to share and create wealth as well as setting up mechanism towards longer-term development designed at strengthening the self-reliance of affected populations, especially women, young boys and girls and strengthen social cohesion against immediate and future vulnerabilities. This shall ultimately set the path for recovery and guarantee improved livelihood for the selected beneficiaries,
