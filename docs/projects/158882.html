Project Summary

Project Objective: To enhance the capacity of agricultural assets of vulnerable farmers in the southern governorate of the Gaza Strip who are subjected to humanitarian crises to restore livelihoods.
Special objectives:
1. To increase agricultural assist productivity of 360 Dunums of open field holed by (at least 10 female farmers their age between 20-65 year and 170 males' of the headed household farmers their age between 20-65-year-old).
2. To rehabilitate agricultural water wells and install a 75 kW solar power for three wells in the targeted area (at least 3 female farmers their age between 20-65-year-old and 57 male of the headed household farmers their age between 20-65-year-old).
3. To provide 9000 M3 of water storage by rehabilitating 100 ponds (at least 5 female farmers their age between 20-65-year-old and 95 males of the headed household farmers their age between 20-65-year-old).
The project's objectives match the HRP 2020 (part of 2018-2020 HRP) oPt, Strategic objective country #( 3): The ability of vulnerable Palestinians to cope with the protracted crisis, including environmental threats, is supported, while solutions to violations and other root causes of threats and shocks are pursued.

1 - Needs

Two million people living in Gaza are witnessing a continuous decline in their living conditions, the level of food insecurity remains alarmingly high in Gaza, where an estimated 62 % households are severely or moderately food insecure(HNO2020), also increased the rates of unemployment, which exceeded 52% in 2019( PCBS 2019). the funding crisis is represented also by the FSS HRP funding appeal shortfall, with only 53% of the overall FSS budget being funded as of mid-year 2019,farmers are still facing a deficit of water for irrigation due to the deficit of power supply for the functionality of their water wells, for the re-use of wastewater, as well as for other basic agricultural operations throughout all value chains’ stages. The irregularity of water availability caused severe losses to farmers as the production sharply decreased in terms of quality and quantity. Besides, the Great March of Return has greatly affected the lives of farmers in border areas (ARA) who are still suffering from limited access to their lands to cultivate, also the damages derived from the GMR. According to the Ministry of Agriculture, about 400 farmers have been directly affected due to the GMR demonstrations since March 2018(HNO 2020). Al Nakheel assessed humanitarian needs using several methods; semi-structured interviews executed with headed house females farmers their age between 20-65 years (random samples), field visits to some owners of the water wells, three focusing group discussions were conducted. In Deir Al Balah,Khan Younis,Rafah, with participation of MoA, women' associations representatives, 18 small headed household farmers (4 females and 14 males) with different marital status, their age between 20-65 years, this age group have been selected because they forms the most economically active age group, 2 of the participant is people with disabilities.Some of them are landowners or land hold and some of them work with their relatives in their father's or grandfather's land ownership. Participants concluded their needs as follows: 1.(95) % of the participants focused on the low productivity of open fields due to man-made and natural disasters. They focused on the necessity of increasing their land production capacity as well as protecting their plants from stray dogs and loafers. 2. (80)% of participants indicated agriculture water availability and accessibility issues as an urgent need due to the high price of cubic meters of water (about 2.5 NIS for one M3). 3. All the interviewed agricultural water wells' owners complained about the high cost of operating their wells by fuel. 4. The people with disabilities indicate that they are actually in the bad economic situation and their needs for the source of income for secure food to their families while most of the income is spent on healthcare and they could not invest in the rehabilitation of their farms .5. Female-headed households participants complained form that food insecurity among them; even they have some production assets like house backyard. They expressed their needs for agricultural water accessibility to produce fresh food to match their family's needs. 6. Females focused on their rights to benefit from agricultural assets of their fathers or grandfathers - equal with the males. They expressed their urgent need for Al Nakheel to design the project activities to sustain the livelihood of target the beneficiaries. Notes: According to information released by MoA, the number of female farmers who are holding the agricultural assets is equal to 5 % of Gaza farmers. Moreover, the unemployment rate among females is 1.7 times than men, being 68.3% for women and 39.7% for males. in addition, Female-headed households, pregnant and lactating women are particularly vulnerable to food insecurity and malnutrition Women in agriculture are particularly disadvantaged, despite being a significant unregistered portion of the workers in this sector (HNO 2020).

2 - Activities

This intervention aims to increase the production capacity of the agricultural assets owned by the small male and female farmers who are most vulnerable to humanitarian crises in Rafah, Khan Younis, and Deir Al Balah. The activities are designed in coordination with MoA and FSS to eliminate the duplication of the beneficiaries from other agriculture projects, which may be implemented in the same place, this project will target women farmers who own or rent or hold agriculture assets from their fathers or husbands. Moreover, in the implementation stage, the coordination with MoA will be in each stage, Also, the unemployment rate among females is 1.7 times than men, being 68.3% for women and 39.7% for males. in addition, Female-headed households, pregnant and lactating women are particularly vulnerable to food insecurity and malnutrition Women in agriculture are particularly disadvantaged, despite being a significant unregistered portion of the workers in this sector (FSS HNO 2020). Activity # 1: Rehabilitation of agricultural assets for 180 farming households (at least 10 female farmers). Al Nakheel will create opportunities for vulnerable and poor farmers, two dunums for each farmer to rehabilitation 360 dunums( 100 Rafah, 160 Khan Younis, 100 Middle Area), through tilling the soil in addition, to fertilize the soil by natural manure on 6 cubic meters per dunums and extension of irrigation network for each dunum and providing vegetable seeds. Output 1# 360 dunums of high productivity soil and 1800 tons at least of vegetables. Activity # 2: Rehabilitation of water wells. Partial Rehabilitation for operating three agricultural wells to improve the efficiency of discharge water and installing renewable energy for the agricultural water wells. (1 Rafah, 2 Khan Younis) each well will serve 20 farmers; this activity will target 60 farmers (at least 3 female farmers and 57 males of the headed household farmers). Outputs#2: improving water access to 300 dunums at a low cost. Activity # 3 Rehabilitation of 100 water ponds. The plastic water harvesting ponds rehabilitated with plastic sheeting, shadow net, and safety fence ( 25 Rafah, 50 Khan Younis, 25 Middle Area) - 100 farmers (at least 5 female farmers and 95 males of the headed household farmers). Outputs: # M3 of water storage will be provided. Three local farmer's committees will be formed. At least 50% of the members will be women. and representing people with disabilities ( PwD).The local committees will participate in planning, implementing, and monitoring and evaluating the project activities. This will ensure good participation of women in all the project cycle

8.6.c - How will feedback be used?

Al Nakheel adopted Mechanisms Accountability to Affected Populations to know and claim their rights and increasing the ability and accountability of individuals who are responsible for respecting protecting and fulfilling rights, with special focus on women on project designing, implementation, monitoring and evaluation phases of a project. Al Nakheel will organize 2 community meetings the first one at the beginning of the project implementation phase involving related actors and affected persons to describe and explain the project’s objective, outcomes. the second meeting at the end of the project to discuss the result of the project and final evaluation and discuss the lessons learned. Al Nakheel already establishes an accountability mechanism including complaints form and suggestion boxes placed, at the Al Nakheel office as well as a telephone line and Email the senior management have access to it. The first stage project staff will be informed and trained on the kinds of feedback mechanisms available, how to collect and forward the received feedback and concerns. Any recommendations, suggestions, and concerns of the beneficiaries, along with the mitigation measures will be discussed during the project the senior management is aware of all issues and that the issues are followed up on and appropriately resolved. As an example, from our previous projects, the beneficiaries suggest changing the types of agricultural inputs for the rehabilitation of open fields during the meeting of the local farmer's committee. The project team redesign the intervention and adopt the farmers' suggestion to increase the profitability. Information will be published on the Al Nakheel website explaining the project objectives, outcomes, outputs and activities to affected community members. The affected person will participate in project final evaluation, the satisfaction of target groups, efficiency, effectiveness, impact, and sustainability; lessons learned and best practices

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

The association and through its nature of work understand the concept of "do no harm" and consecrate their effort to provide the received an agricultural asset to the local community to strengthen peace among them. Therefore, the staff is highly recommended not to use their power during the project implementing stages and not to be an element of the conflict. And not to use this agricultural input to support their sides in the conflict to prevent harm to the community. The association takes the following steps:1. High coordination with MoA in order names of affected and registered in it and the FSS to make the cross-checking to avoid any duplication.2. Explicit and crucial selecting criteria announced to the community.3. Conduct workshops in the presence of local community representatives, include women association, key persons, and farmer of both sexes and Al Nakheel representative.4. Forming neighborhood committee to assist the project management in reviewing applications, intervene as necessary. 5. Preparing application form embodied the main items of selected criteria.6. The ranking system will be used to determine the most needed beneficiaries.7. Transparency and good govern policy and procedures are followed and receive any complaints, register it, analyses and replay the complainers as soon as possible8.Preventing any interest of conflict during the procurement process9.Keeping female and male farmers' dignity and spirits 10. Provide the agricultural input according to acceptable standers.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The project aims to provide service to the most vulnerable farmers with coordination with MoA and FSS according to a set of selection criteria ensuring equality, impartiality and with no discrimination moreover, The project activities will be published and communicated in the targeted areas to ensure all farmer target are have equal impartial access to these activities.Al Nakheel will give equal opportunities to apply through announcement of the project in the target area by various outreach tools including (printed announcement, social media pages, Al Nakheel website, etc..) the registration will be through open applications to ensure registration of all potentially eligible breeders, the selection criteria will be included in the announcement. Additional priority in the selection will be given to the female-headed household as well as people with disabilities to empower them and strengthen their ability to face their challenges.
