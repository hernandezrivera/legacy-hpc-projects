Project Summary

Summary
The ten year insurgency across the North-Eastern part of the country has triggered serious security instability and escalated the food security situation in the Northeast. With the return of relative peace in many communities in Adamawa state, the returnee IDPS are confronted by challenge of livelihood considering the fact that they are predominantly peasant farmers and their farming activities have been truncated by the insurgency. Food insecurity and children suffering from malnutrition remains a major concern with 5.2 million people in need of life-saving food assistance. The IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) Round XXIII Report (June 2018) indicates that 83% of the total number of 1,743,839 Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) are located in Borno State, with Adamawa State accounting for 10% and Yobe State for 7%. The Federal Government has declared a food and nutritional crisis in Borno, where over 2,219,000 people are severely in crisis phase, 886,200 are in emergency phase and 58,500 are in famine phase. A growing number of people are in urgent need of food assistance and without any sustainable means of livelihood.
This project is aimed at creating sustainable livelihood and support the re-establishment of community asset depleted by shocks to 5000 beneficiaries representing 1000HH comprising of IDPs, returnee and Host community in Damboa LGA of Borno State and 6000 beneficiaries representing 1200HH in Bama LGA Borno State will be targeted in a manner that will contribute to ensure protection of the most vulnerable women, children and men amongst the IDPs and host families in the community.
This project is specifically directed to 5,000 beneficiaries representing 1000HH comprising of IDPS, returnee and Host communities in Damboa LGA of and 6000 beneficiaries representing 1200HH in Bama LGA Borno State
The target focus is on 2200HH
The various livelihood projects will include crop production. Milling machine, food processing, knitting and embroidery, livestock rearing, construction of market sheds and construction of communal pit latrines in Bama and Damboa

1 - Needs

The nine-year-long conflict in North-East Nigeria has resulted in escalating levels of insecurity, the massive displacement of people, food insecurity and significant human, social and economic losses, in Yobe, Adamawa and Borno State with the epicenter of the crisis. Host communities are also affected as they have weak access to the needed resources for their own food production, face the highest levels of poverty and malnutrition rates in the country and bear an additional burden as they provide in-kind support to IDPs. While livelihoods in Borno State remain particularly affected by the conflict, improvements in security and access have improved market conditions and enabled the resumption of livelihoods, especially crop and livestock production, fisheries and aquaculture. Host communities, IDPs and returnees are able to engage in farming by accessing land through rental arrangements and communal landholding managed by traditional leaders (Bulama and lawan). Provision of seasonally appropriate and livelihood specific inputs will increase the productive capacity of rural livelihoods; invest in the construction of rural livelihood assets to prevent further deterioration and/or restoring households and community productive assets to build resilience to withstand future shocks remains the goal of the projectThe Project will provide seasonal support to vulnerable households through investing in livelihood assets designed through community consultations consisting of men and women to ensure that assets constructed are appropriately aligned to seasonality and linked with efforts that promote resilience, building such as nutrition, health services and production in Damboa and Bama. The provision of seeds/tools distribution will be carried out to boost local production. Farming is a time-sensitive activity; therefore it is time-critical that affected populations will require timely support to access agricultural inputs in order to take advantage of the rains and dry season, Income Generating Activities are also designed and implemented are to build resiliency by addressing the medium and longer-term vulnerabilities of female-headed households exposed to shocks using income generation activities through community engagement. These activities are targeted for women-headed households and abled youth group in Damboa and Bama

1. Social infrastructures 1.1 Construction of 2 communal latrine stances with 2 compartments each one for boys and one for girls at Damboa and Bama Central Primary School which will Rebuild Households food security through cash paid for work 1.2 Construction of 6 market shad blocks of 10 stalls each (60 stalls) and the construction of 2 Latrine stances with 2 compartments each one for Men and one for Female in Damboa and Bama LGA Central Market.2. Crop production Provision of quality agriculture inputs for (rain-fed) which will provide food and income for household’s sustainability. 3. Income generating small businesses Provision of milling machine to 50 Female-Headed Households which will provide them with income and food for their household sustainability in Damboa LGA. Provision of income-generating activity (food processing and Knitting/Embroilment) for 100 Female-Headed Households which will provide them with income and food for their household sustainability in Bama. 4. Livestock FarmingProvision of small ruminant animals to 200 and 150 households in Bama and Damboa respectively to rebuild household assets and livelihood depleted by shocksEnhance poultry production by provision of vaccinated chicks to 100 Households each in Bama and Damboa 5. Capacity building Training on result-based planning, monitoring and evaluationTraining on CBPP approach for project implementationTheoretical and practical technical trainingsProject activities planning arrangements YAI/SHF will use the community Based Participatory Planning Approach to plan livelihood interventions in each selected communities. In preparing community specific plans through CBPP, the following activities will be conducted which will ultimately enable us to identify interventions and endorsed by the community; • Community sensitization: YAI/SHF will sensitize the community on the intervention mix• Establishing required platforms at LGA and ward level: LGA Chairman/LGA Secretary in addition to Ward chairmen will be engaged as government stakeholders for their buy in.• Identifying Wards where livelihood interventions have to be implemented: Using the CBPP assessment report YAI/SHF will clearly identify specific livelihood intervention to be implemented• Conducting quick livelihood and market assessment or use available study findings• Organizing Community Based Participatory planning consultations at the last planning unit level (community)• Advocacy, awareness and sensitization: YAI/SHF will engage communities and beneficiaries on purpose of livelihood to aid early recovery, objective of livelihood programme, strategic plan, process of targeting, criteria and expected target groups. This will be a continuous activity throughout the different stages of project implementation;• Formation of community targeting committee; YAI/SHF will ensure that election of community targeting committee will be by the full participation of the general community representing men, women and youth. Targeted list of beneficiaries will be presented and endorsed by the general community in a meeting where at least 60% of the community members are represented. The committee will verify list of household enrolled, develop targeting criteria and do the actual targeting and verification of households. The committee will ensure the representation from the different wealth groups, which include very poor, poor and moderate if need be they could include from better off category. • Preparation and submission of beneficiary households list: The process of selecting household will be done in a transparent manner using set targeting criteria. The final lists will be shared immediately after the community meeting and posted in a public place . YAI/SHF team will facilitate the community-based targeting; will also give attention to any manipulation in the list or selection process that assists selected households to share assistance in the future.
