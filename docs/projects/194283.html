Project Summary

South Darfur state is one of Sudan,s States that affected by natural disasters(floods), in 2022 rainy season, and there was many areas and camps within the state has been fully damaged, therefore ECORD is proposing a project to address the needs of the targeted areas, by taking The principals Objectives Of the Emergency Shelter and-Food Items (Es/NFIs) sector in consideration to save lives , provide protection from harsh weather elements and restore a sense of dignity to persons of concern ( PoCs) By ensuring the timely provision of Es/NFIs. PoCs include families displaced due to conflict, disaster ( e.g floods) ; and returnee / reintegrated households.
the main Objectives are:
1-Provide timely multi- sectoral life-saving
assistance to crisis affected people to reduce
mortality and morbidity
2- Improve vulnerable people’s access to
livelihoods and life-sustaining basic services
3-Mitigate protection risks and respond to
protection needs through humanitarian action

1 - Needs

Darfur crisis is considered as one of the biggest ongoing conflict, after the conflict started in mid of 2003 with a lot of consecutive fighting violence’s sparked up between the two main opposition groups and the Government of Sudan(GoS), in the region, the consequences of those straggles leaded to huge number of people released from their places to another places which coasted them to have shelters to protect themselves from the hot, cold and rain , destinies, displacement, increasing the cases of the needs of shelter and NFIs at all levels of IDPs camps, host communities, refugee’s settlement and affected rural villages. Currently the results of previous scaling up fighting between the government and rebels in greater Darfur and specifically South Darfur added more violence on the rural communities whom were living in conflict and surrounded cluster villages by fights. This situation indicated that new IDPs arrived in to the existing camps and they added new IDPs camps in South Darfur state and that is one of the areas of witnesses’ different modules of fighting, between government and rebels, between rebel groups themselves, tribal fights between one ethnic group or between different ethnic groups. This factor of lasts fighting increased number of people displaced around big town, Nyala as a capital of South Darfur State is currently accommodating more than 7 IDPs camp with approximate number of 254.000 IDPs .All IDPs are fully dependent on relief, the failure of peace agreement in Darfur and drop of security hindering humanitarian access, all these acted as additional factors in prevailing the need of shelter and non-food items in the communities, and also the failure of juba agreement on 2019, and 25 October 2021 military coup made The situation more complicated which coast the security, economic situation worse, and With inter-communal conflict on the rise, overall political turmoil and negative impact of the economic crisis, the needs and affected people will increase. Furthermore, climate change and environmental degradation threaten to raise the risks of more severe flooding while failing infrastructure and inadequate housing make people more vulnerable to natural disasters. Deteriorating situations in neighboring countries, may further increase cross-border flows of refugees. While the S/NFI Cluster struggles to keep up with both the rate and scale of new displacement in the context of chronically low funding, the succession of crises has led humanitarian response to focus on immediate and life-saving needs only. Furthermore, with limited development funding to offset deepening socio-economic vulnerability or address root causes of the crisis (e.g., poor governance, lack of public financing for failing infrastructure and public services, HLP issues, etc.), the need for more durable, longer-term solutions become ever more pressing. While protracted insecurity and worsening economic situation continue to negatively impact household resilience through all areas, it is crucial to break the continuous cycle of recurrent emergency response and invest in more permanent housing, public facilities and DRR infrastructure in order to mitigate the impact of both man-made and natural disasters. On 2022 South Darfur State was affected by the floods that occurred in rainy season, therefore an inter-agency assessment mission led by OCHA, conducted in many areas and camps in South Darfur state and they determined the needs of the affected population, on first of September 2022, ES/NFIs team concluded that the flood affected around 40 percent of IDPs population in Al Sereif camp which include around 495 houses that collapsed and 600 HHs which were partially damaged. The overall impact of the heavy rain is expected to be 60 percent of the figures communicated to partners by HAC and local sources, the same team conducted another assessment to Dereige IDPs camp, and Al salam IDPs Camp on fourth of September, and identified the needs.

7.1.1 - Please specify other means to collect/share feedback or information if there is any

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Accountability to the affected population is core in the ECORD response and will be strengthened through continuous consultation with the affected population on the response modalities as well as their satisfaction with services provided. In addition,ECORD is an active member of the national PSEA technical and coordination team and leads the survivor support task team and together is working with the team members to ensure that GBV referral mechanisms are accessible to survivors of SEA. ECORD will equip implementing partners and targeted communities with information regarding the SEA, and how to report and access services including health, legal and psycho-social support services. ECORD’s partners and beneficiaries play an active role in the decision-making process in regards to the planning, implementing, and evaluating of activities.This is done by conducting focus group discussions with women and girls in women centers. The project will support the development of self-protection capacities and aim at improving beneficiaries’ daily lives through improved formal and informal protection mechanisms. This is considered the most effective way of tackling gender inequality as the root cause of GBV.
