Project Summary

Through this intervention, Islamic relief will contribute to improve basic services for South Sudan and Ethiopian refugees in WK, Gadaref, South Kordofan and Blue Nile by delivering integrated durable and sustainable solutions across all sectors to refugees and host community. The strategy is to deliver multi sectorial services for refugees in all their locations to ensure satisfaction of services. This integrated approach will enable Islamic Relief to work on filling the gaps and need at once in all sectors, and assure that both South Sudan and Ethiopian refugees rights and dignity are fulfilled at all levels.
In West Kordofan, the project will target SSR through multi interventions in Protection, Education, FSL and WASH targeting the in and out of camp refugees in Kailak, El Mairam, Lagawa, El Nuhud. In regard to protection, the intended project will raise awareness on general protection topics to reach around 8,506 refugees in targeted localities of Kailak, Lagawa, El Mairam and El Nuhud, manage to establish eight Community-Based Structures for CPBNs, community reconciliation committees, and youth networks and build the capacity of 160 service providers on general protection topics as well as cash under MPCA at full to around 2,000 refugees. In Education the project will target both SSR and Ethiopian refugees in WK, SK, BN and Gadaref to support both primary and Secondary Education through comprehensive package of activities include; increase learning space for refugees by construction of 16 units of primary school classrooms and rehabilitate 12 units of classrooms. WASH in schools will be improved by construction of units of 8 hand washing stations in targeted schools. Gender sensitive and segregated latrines will be constructed at target school and all schools will be supported with furniture and seating. 7,312 students in target school will be provided with uniform, school supplies and learning materials. 120 teachers will be trained on COVID 19 prevention and control, and will be supported with primary kits and furniture. 120 teachers in targeted schools will be supported with monthly incentives to encourage them for delivery of quality teaching services to refugees.
In health intervention, IRW will target SSR in WK through delivery of quality primary health service including; conduct occupational visits to provide essential primary and secondary health care services, inpatient treatment to provide essential primary and secondary health care services, increase access to health services by supporting mobile medical units and referral pathways and strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services.
In WASH, the proposed project will deliver WASH services for both SSR and Ethiopian refugees in WK, SK, BN and Gadaref through intensive activities covering; drilling and installation of new hand pumps, rehabilitate hand pumps, construction of Hafirs and water treatment points, conduct hygiene promotion at households and at communal set ups, construction of communal latrines and solarization of water sources to reduce running costs. The project will provide and distribute personal hygiene materials especially for women, provision of soap to HHs, and solid waste and disposal systems, in addition to building the refugees capacities on different WASH related trainings.
In regard to FSL, the project will support SSR and Ethiopian refugees in El Nuhud and Basundah through delivering FSL activities including; provision of agricultural and livestock support, vocational trainings, and provide cash based interventions as well as support the activities for youth issues

1 - Needs

Referring to UNHCR recent updates and reports, refugees’ needs both South Sudan and Ethiopian, were increasing across all sectors and partners are recommended to continue supporting the humanitarian needs through providing of FSL and income generating support to build the refugees resilience and maintain their reliant upon availability of income opportunities. This was recommended as priorities to enhance and support provision of income generating chances through conducting vocational trainings. In Education, the reports are recommending to focus on school constructions and rehabilitations to increase coverage and availability of classroom spaces, provide support to improve education quality, work on access to school retention support and financial assistance for education, and provide school feeding at schools to support retention and children’s well-being. More focus is recommended for secondary education for Ethiopian refugees in the East of Sudan. The needs to support South Sudan refugees in is also very high due to their significant numbers within target Localities, and as of UNHCR updates, it was revealed that the refugees situation in this regard need immediate intervention and support. The reports are indicating, worse conditions of water facilities and poor and total hygiene and sanitation conditions which require urgent support and interventions as well. The updates and reports are also highlighting low health coverage and showing the available health facilities are very limited in targeted Localities and lack capable staff to run the facilities for provision of quality health services to refugees and to the resident hosting populations. The sanitation and hygiene situation is getting worse especially on the risk of COVID 19 pandemic occurrence and continuous floods crises which impacted most facilities and affected the wellbeing of the community at all levels including refugees. These factors justifies the necessity to intervene and contribute for reducing the sufferings in services for both refugees and hosting community. In regard to protection, UNHCR reports are recommending to focus on protection issues including awareness raising campaigns to address early marriage issues, establishment and promotion of women’s centers and community sensitization initiatives to enhance women’s participation in community decision-making and inclusion of women in livelihoods and vocational training opportunities, and access to financial services to support women’s economic empowerment. In regard to food security and livelihood, the needs are highlighted to continue provision of immediate support in income generating activities and vocational trainings. In regard to WASH the focus is highlighted on rehabilitating the water points and expand in exploring more opportunities to access safe adequate water and sanitation facilities including solarization of water sources, WASH in schools and hygiene promotion. In Education the needs are recommended to focus on school constructions and rehabilitations to increase coverage and availability of classroom spaces, provide support to improve education quality, especially for secondary education for Ethiopian refugees. The school package will include seating, school supplies and school learning materials for both refugees targets.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Conserving natural resources is a basic requirement for sustainable development andimproving the quality of human life. To assure this principle is adopted to target projectactivities, the project will aim to not reverse the trend towards resource degradation, andwill consider giving greater attention and priority to ecological aspects. The project activities were designed in a way as not to impact the environment and will not affect the geographical and context of both the problem statement and nature of target groups (South Sudan refugees and host community). The multisector activities are clearly planned to dig deep into the roots of the context and seek durable solutions to address the basic needs of refugees and vulnerable host communities in West Kordofan.This trend support inclusion of positive environmental aspects and sustainable change.

6 - Protection mainstreaming
6.1 - Does the project incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity for the beneficiaries?

4=Fully in place

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection will be a key feature within planned interventions. In all planned activities, woman and girls will be sensitized about the protection issues to reduce the risk of abuse, interaction and violence. Water facilities and water points will be constructed in safe locations that minimize risks of various types of GBV. Pitching water in most part of remote areas in Sudan is the responsibility of women and young girls. However, this project will ensure sparing women an ample time and give them the opportunity to carry out other activities and participate in the development process of their communities, state and country. Women and children will be the project focus to support through provision of direct support especially to refugees UASC children and women of special needs.
