Needs

Nigeria’s North-East continues to experience intricate and multi-faceted humanitarian crisis in the water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) sector, because of the ongoing protracted conflict, unpredictable and continuous large-scale population displacements, recurrent large-scale cholera epidemics and seasonal flooding and fierce storms and winds. Ongoing and protracted displacement creates continued need for WASH and puts an additional stress on existing WASH services and facilities, especially in 43 LGAs across Borno and Adamawa states. With congested populations in displacement sites, especially in 15 LGAs and wards in Borno State, water-borne diseases spread rapidlySpiraling cholera outbreaks have taken hold each year. In 2018, the cholera outbreak has affected 18 LGAs with a total of 4,250 cases . In addition, 1.5 million people including women and children in the affected LGAs , including IDPs and host communities, are considered at risk and require cholera prevention interventionsFlooding, fierce winds and sand storms have resulted into substantial damages of WASH infrastructures. In 2018, at least 20 per cent and 28 per cent of IDPs living in camps in Borno and Adamawa states respectively have been seriously affected as a sizeable number of latrines, showers, handwashing points, and solar panels got partially or destroyed, affecting access to WASH facilities and services.New arrivals from hard-to-reach areas, especially women and children, are particularly at risk and need urgent WASH support. They include at least 100,000 children under five and 30,000 pregnant and lactating women across 9 LGAs in Borno State and 2 LGAs in Yobe State.

Activities

1. Provision of immediate life-saving WASH facilities and services.2. Provision of safe water through new construction and rehabilitation of water systems.3. Provision of safe water and sanitation in institutions (health and nutrition centers, schools, public places), through new construction and rehabilitation of infrastructure.4. Provision of WASH NFIs or vouchers to malnourished children admitted for SAM treatment, and their parents/caregivers. 5. Provision of sex-segregated sanitation facilities (latrines, showers, handwashing stations) in formal and informal IDPs settlements: Provision of household (HH) latrines in host communities, returns areas and long-term established IDPs communities: 6. Maintenance of provided WASH facilities.7. Garbage collection in formal and informal IDPs settlements.8. Promotion of tailored hygiene messages to strengthen hygienic behavior and practices.9. Provision of basic hygiene items including top-ups.10. Use of cash-transfer programming (CTP): Capacity-building among sector partners.11. Strengthening and expanding coordination and partnership.
