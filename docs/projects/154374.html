Project Summary

BCF will support HRP strategic objectives for 2019 through implementing activities aligned with Education cluster objectives.
In view of the importance of the educational sector in the improvement and development of communities and its effective impact on the building of personality and its contribution to the progress of humanity and also in view of the situation that occurred in the case of students and students in Nineveh province, especially returnees. The inability of parents to provide the basic needs of their children,
This project is aimed at the people of Nineveh governorate (the center of the governorate and all its districts). BCF will provide Education materials to the vulnerable students.
This project is of great importance to the people of Nineveh Province and it is not less important than the provision of food and other basic services. The reason is that this project is mainly concerned with the educational aspect and works to bridge the gap by providing study materials and also encourages parents to facilitate the students and encourage them to go to school.
Thus, this project benefits the public and contributes to minimizing the effects of suffering and alleviating the damage that may be inflicted on new generations.

Needs

Around 1.5 million of returnees in Mosul are in high need for emergency educational kits such as stationary and school bags and provide them with legal assistance through providing them with their lost documentations, without these materials it will be hard for them to continue going to school.The project aims to improve the conditions of students by providing studying materials of bags and stationery to encourage them to attend their schools and alleviate the suffering of parents and encourage them to send their children back to schools.The project has far-reaching goals in contributing to the development of new educated generations, contributing to literacy, and building an educated and educated society that contributes to improving the peaceful coexistence environment away from ignorance and backwardness and to avoid a lost generation.Also, the project aims to provide sustained, structured, psychosocial support for the project beneficiaries through conducting resilience activities and training to make them passionate about their studying and pay more attention and importance to the future rather than living in their past, and to feel like there is someone taking care of and your future as its well known that kids are the most affected during conflicts or any crisis.

Activities

Laying out the foundation of the project by documenting Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), establishing community Accountability and Reporting Mechanisms (CARMSs), coordinate and Communicate with all Key Stakeholders, Establish an M&E and Quality Assurance Plan and Protection Referral Mechanism Identifying and Registering Project Participants (TLS & Formal Education Sites), Communicate General Program Information with Stakeholders, Protection training to the staff Distributing emergency school teaching/learning materials to 2,016 students -provision of sustained, structured, pyschosicial support through providing resilience activities for months on different period for 26836, provide training to teachers, social workers on positive discipline, school code of conducts, PSEA and gender based violencecoordinate with local authorities for exit strategy, carry out post distribution/delivery monitoring surveys

Protection Risk Analysis

- Ongoing conflict and safety of distribution, tensions in the areas of distributions - Sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA)- Quality of kit based on item durability- Ensuring equal treatment and appropriate distribution to beneficiaries within a site- Sexual abuse against

Mitigation Measures

Prior to a distribution, we will make sure that distribution sites and routes to/from distribution sites are free from explosive hazards, we will do so by consulting security forces, local authorities and mine action organizations- Communicate before and during distribution with local officials/leaders, school administrators and the community about our role, procedures, criteria - Designate one staff at each site responsible for security decisions- Ensure items size and weight can be hold by children - Train staff on Do No Harm, protection from SEA (PSEA), and child safeguarding

How is your project accountable to affected population

This will be happened through various channels of communication, most importantly the accountability to these population will meet (transparency, participation of the community, feedback and complaints). Information provided to the community will be accurate, timely and understandable by everyone. Establishing Complaint and feedback mechanism in order to provide a mean for the community to voice complaints and provide feedback on areas relevant to operations in a safe and dignified manner. This will also include to establish procedures for recording, referring, taking actions and provide feedback to the complainant. Complains about any aspect of project will go through five stages: acknowledge complaint, asses/gather information/resolve, provide reason for decision, implement outcome actions, close complaint and keep records. All complaints will be dealt in a respectful and confidential way. And more importantly a quick response in no more than 2 working days. Most of complains will be dealt with by the project manager, a hotline number will be also shared among the students. A clear referral mechanism will be in placed for issues related to gender and protection, issues related to this will be dealt by Gender and Protection officer.
