Project Summary

According to the Humanitarian Programme Cycle of March 2021 for Cameroon, the level of insecurity in North-West Region is still high. The presence of national security forces (police, gendarmerie, army) is concentrated along the main roads and cities, leaving most rural areas under the influence of Non state armed groups (NSAGs).
The report further states that the socio-political crisis in the North-West, now entering its fifth year, has led to massive population displacements. As of August 2020, the North-West and South-West crisis has displaced over 1.1 million people, an increase over almost 200,000 people in comparison to December 2019, when 930,000 were displaced.
Among these people, there are also a growing number of unaccompanied and separated displaced children who suffer from malnutrition and have very limited educational and social opportunities.
The loss and lack of civil and legal documentation puts the affected population – particularly boys, girls and men at risk of arbitrary arrest by law enforcement forces. This lack of documentation especially personal identification cards has trapped most of these groups of persons in the rural communities since they are at risks of being arrested on transit.
Furthermore, the difficult access to basic healthcare facilities, education including child protection services remains a key risk factors especially for children in these rural areas and most remote localities.
Moreover, approximately hundreds of children in these communities under the age of five are likely to be acutely malnourished.
In the Bamenda II municipality, the absence of good road infrastructure, portable water, electricity, health among others are some of the challenges brought forth by the Quarter Heads. This was during a come together between the Bamenda II Council authorities and all the Quarter Heads from the municipality in January 2021 to strategize on how to improve upon the livelihoods of all resident within this community. While calling on development partners to join hands for the best interest of the community member, “what has come should not be allowed to go back. We should work together so that the projects can be realized for the benefit of all." Mayor Chenui pointed out.
According to statistics from the Regional hospital in Bamenda II in June 2021, some desperate 64 patients from the rural communities of the Bamenda II municipality were supported by the council in paying for drugs and doing lab tests. Some were mothers who put to birth prematurely and did not have the means to sustain the welfare of the children.
In regards to markets in the Bamenda, in Mankon, there is an increase in the prices of basic commodities coupled with a rise in the prices of fruits, and vegetables such as tomatoes, potatoes, onions, cabbages, bananas, lemon, and mangoes due to insecurity limiting supply from rural areas according to market reports of October 2021.
Following the rapid needs assessment exercise of September 2021 that was carried out in 5 quarters of the Bamenda II council area( ALA-nki, Alabukum, Mulang, Ngulung and Alamatu) organized by the local traditional authorities and supported/facilitated by CAFEDO, 356 displaced persons ( 102 women, 74 men, 124 girls and 56 boys) were contacted. From the analysis of the data that was collected, their first 6 challenges are ranked as follows: security, feeding, health, education, documentation and employment.
In response to this, CAFEDO in line with the FSC & HRP 2022 objectives, seeks to improve households’ immediate access to food, health and facilitate access to identification documents in other to protect them from police arrest while also to ensuring their free movement to seek for other opportunities. This will be done through unconditional cash transfers and the provision of agricultural seeds in these rural and semi-urban communities of the Bamenda II council area.

Plan fields

1 - Besoins / Needs

Objective 1: Improve households’ immediate access to food through the provision of unconditional assistance in the rural and semi-urban communities of the Bamenda II council municipalityActivities:- CAFEDO will prioritize unconditional cash transfers especially for 200 vulnerable households (200 HHs: 70 male, 130 female) in the internally displaced population for eight months. CAFEDO will prioritize cash in areas where markets are functional; are physically and socially accessible to all segments of the population. Cash transfers will be done through mobile money as it will not only ensure the safety, dignity, and integrity of recipients but limit fraud and diversion of funds. It should be noted that this cash transfers will increases their access to food, reduce dependency rates, reduce the rate of sexual exploitation and abuse of teenage girls and young women and also assist some of in them in the process of re-acquiring their lost documents that will enhance their free movements to look for other opportunities. Furthermore, these assistance will provide basic needs for these households and the rate of child labour and abuse will be limited as the number of children who go out to look for food will also be greatly reduced.Objective 2: Protect and restore livelihoods, related food through the provision of seasonally appropriate technical support and farm seeds to farm families in rural and semi-urban settings. Activities: Provision of livelihood specific inputs. CAFEDO will distribute the seeds package to the 290 farm family households (HHs) (130 male, 160 female). In line with FSC guidance, each household will receive 10kg which includes: improve maize seed (2kg), and beans seeds (2kg). Before the distribution of the livelihood inputs, the farmers will receive a farming training to improve their yield. The livelihood inputs will be done once (during the planting season).Upon distribution CAFEDO will also conduct a Post Distribution Monitoring of the cash transfers and farm seeds. This Post Distribution Monitoring will also be disaggregated by persons with disability analysis of barriers faced by persons with disabilities and any instances of exploitation or other protection risks created through the delivery of assistance.With regards to the COVID-19 pandemic, CAFEDO will avoid large gatherings and ensure that a one week distribution allowance is provided to avoid overcrowding and not to keep people waiting for a long period of time. The distribution sites will set up a supervised hand washing area with enough water soap alongside a body temperature checks point to be manage by a health workerProject Indicators 1.
