Project Summary

Title: Bar COVID-19 into Njikwa Sub Division.
2. The project will provide standard Face Mask to the General Population of Njikwa.
3. The project will provide 259 Cartons of soap to the various institutions where buckets are placed.
4. The project will also provide Spraying Cans, Sanitizers and Disinfectants to the 9 palaces, 14 Health Posts and 72 Churches in Njikwa
5. Given the fact that there are no vehicles on standby in Njikwa, no telephone network, access road diplorable, the project will train community healthcare and sanitation officers on COVID related issues and provide with carrying Stretchers, Special Aprons and Protective Gloves to trained community Committees to transport COVID-19 suspected cases to the hospitals. The Health posts too will have additional Stretchers
6. The project will embark on serious sensitization and demonstrative campaign on measures to curb the spread of COVID-19. Posters carrying messages will be posted in all public places in the various villages and 9 campaign teams of 5 people each will be sent out with translators of the local languages.
