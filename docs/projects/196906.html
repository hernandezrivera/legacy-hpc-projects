Project Summary

Silk Road will address the issues that emerged in the need assessment of Silk Road by implementing activities that ensure multi-faceted intervention, where awareness will be raised among conflict-affected groups that are in urgent need of humanitarian intervention through the implementation of awareness-raising activities with the aim of publicize the knowledge about SEA, whereby outreach staff who have extensive experience in presenting such raising awareness sessions will be hired so that staff will conduct field sessions with groups of beneficiaries within the targeted communities giving practical examples of SEA issues drawn from the reality experienced by the population inside Syria and based on the relevant humanitarian standards. In addition to what was previously mentioned awareness brochures that include key messages about SEA will be distributed. Those brochures are provided with pictures to be visible even to illiterate people within the targeted communities those communities are Harim, Bsanya, Kafr Hum, Ariba, Salkin, Azaz, Suran, Mare, Harbal, Salama, Sejar, Maarin, Karamil, Kafra, Qazal and Yahmul. Silk Road will work to resolve all gaps/risks related to the organizational management by utilizing the WoS PSEA Network Checklists. As it will be utilized by management and HR to review existing organizational policies and procedures related to SEA. The field checklist also will be utilized in the field to assess the organizational situation and fill the gaps accordingly. The organization team will be attend SEA training to enhance the importance of complying with SEA prohibitions and reporting any incidents. New staff should always be briefed when starting work. The organizational PSEA Code of Conduct is signed by all staff. The Code of Conduct is displayed in prominent places in offices. Silk Road will ensure designate a staff members as a PSEA Focal points with providing them with the needed support. Silk Road will comply to establish a complaints mechanism and publicize how staff can report concerns or suspicions of SEA. Together with the PSEA Network and local populations, establish how beneficiaries / local populations can report SEA. As this complaints mechanisms will be in the same line with other organizations operating in a given location, rather than the Silk Road own system. Giving the ability of giving feedback on a variety of issues, and not just SEA, in order to encourage people to use the complaints mechanism and to avoid stigmatization of the complainant. Silk Road will establish clear procedures regarding how to handle and direct complaints.

3 - Needs

According to HNO 2022, 14.6 M People in need, as the Syrian crisis has entered into its tenth year, with having seen the emergence of new and complex socioeconomic difficulties that are further exacerbating the humanitarian aid need situation of families across the country. As according to HNAP 2021, seventy-six percent of all HHs across Syria reported receiving assistance or accessing nonpaid services, this property on was highest in NWS (82 percent). Where the increase in poverty, unemployment and the constant need for a humanitarian response is considered an appropriate environment for appearing a SEA cases for the vulnerable groups within Syrian societies. Where 5 percent of HHs nationwide that received humanitarian assistance stated that they or members of their HH were asked for favors in exchange for assistance or services. 4 percent in NWS of HHs who received assistance stated they had been asked for a favor, such as requests for cash; labor; personal relationships. As according to HNAP 2021, 83 percent of HHs on the national level who were asked for any kind of favor in exchange for assistance or services stated that they did not report the experience through an existing complaint mechanism. Only 16 percent stated that they reported the experience and 1 percent were unwilling to answer. Similarly, of the HHs that were asked for a personal relationship at the national level, 92 percent did not report the experience through an existing complaint mechanism.

4 - Implementation of activities

Directly after signing the project agreement, Silk Road will start Key activities preparation including announcing the project for the targeted areas and explain the roles of each party, the purpose of the project, its objectives and activities (Signing MoUs) with the local authorities. Mainly, Silk Road will target the people who are living in camps with targeting the vulnerable people inside communities. As per our Rapid assessment, no PSEA activities have been implemented during the last year in the targeted areas with the existence of a big number of IDPs live in the camps there. So we will do a quick registration for the people inside the communities to assess the vulnerability and prepare the final lists of the participants inside communities, while no need for the registration to those people who are living in the camps, as the people in the camps considered a vulnerable people who are basically in need for the human aid. Mainly the awareness sessions will be provided to the head of the family who will be able to transfer the knowledge to his family members. The awareness sessions will be provided by the Silk Road reach out personnel who have an experience to do this kind of activities. The awareness sessions will be provided in the location by gathering small groups of people to receive the sessions taking into consideration the preventive measures of COVID19 during providing the sessions. The attendees for the sessions will be provided with brochures as well.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Silk Road has zero-tolerance for some special cases such as any acts related to terrorist or radical groups, PSEA related issues including rape, assault or sexual exploitation, blackmail, possessing a weapon, threatening or assaulting another person or corruption. These cases necessitate the most severe types of disciplinary actions besides the termination of employment with the possibility of legal prosecution in the critical cases that require this. This applies to employees, beneficiaries, workers, suppliers, partners and any party that may be related to Silk Road work (All these groups are requested to sign and understand the code of conduct of Silk Road before proceeding in any type of work with Silk Road). Bullying, harassment (including sexual, gender and racial harassment) and discrimination are not tolerated, along with any unwelcome comment or behaviour that is offensive, demeaning, humiliating, derogatory, or any other inappropriate behaviour that fails to respect the dignity of an individual. Silk Road requires all team members and partners to immediately report suspicions of sexual exploitation or abuse. Silk Road encourages all beneficiaries and community members to report suspicions of possible sexual exploitation or abuse of or by any team member, partner or visitor. Any staff member who has concerns about the behaviour of another staff member should raise these immediately with the line manager, HR or another senior manager, or the CEO.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

According to our previous experience in the targeted area, we can summarize the risks as following:● Code of Conduct and/or Child protection incidents:Silk Road Organization has a COC and Child protection and child safeguarding policies in place that have been signed by all the staff focusing on the frontline workers in prior of starting direct activities with beneficiaries, including all procedures for reporting directly to the head office. In order to increase their awareness to the cases they might face in the field and know correct practices and procedures they have to follow and reduce the risks on themselves.● Discrimination between IDPs and the host community:Silk Road Organization staff are well trained to be professional in involving both categories of beneficiaries (IDPs and host community) in the same sessions that will create a smooth communication channels between them and increase the communication flexibility to share their emotions and support each other. ● Inflation, may result in higher than planned prices. If prices spike too high, then SRO may not have the budget to complete the project / Market System Disruption: Silk Road will monitor inflation and prices and include this risk in its contracts with service providers. If inflation rises, they will work with the donor to re-align the budget and program accordingly. Include the ability to ensure procurement as a criterion in the contractor's bid analysis.
