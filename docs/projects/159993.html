Project Summary

Due to the dry and harsh Jilaal (January March) season and a significant delay in the start of the 2019 Gu season rainfall, Moderate to Severe drought conditions prevailed in most parts of Somalia between March and mid-May. Accordingly, the 2019 FSNAU Post Gu nutrition assessment findings showed marginal decrease in the Median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) of 13.8% down from 14% of post Gu 2018 and 17.4% of post Gu 2017. The corresponding median SAM prevalence are: 2.3% (Gu 2019), 2.2% (Gu 2018) and 3.2% (Gu 2017). The FSNAU projects the GAM in Bardhere district at 11.9 % and SAM at 1.7 % while the prevalence of GAM and SAM in the neighboring Bardhere IDP, Kismayo Urban and Kismayo IDP are 14.4%, 11.7% and 10.5%; 2.7%, 1.1% and 1.5% respectively. The Nutrition Cluster estimates caseloads of about 750 SAM and 3,900 MAM annually in Bardhere district with an estimated population of about 85,000 UNICEF is supporting the delivery of nutrition interventions in Bardhere district through implementing partners.
The same areas have done issues with floods and this makes them prone to water and sanitation health issues. Bardhere is situated in the southern region of Gedo, therefore, the proposed emergency response is designed by URDO is to complement other ongoing efforts to avoid any retrogressing any achievements gained before. The program comprises of integrated life-saving health and nutrition interventions, coupled with safe water, sanitation, public health education, distribution of essential WASH NFIs and support to meet the immediate needs of both IDPs and host communities in Bardhere district. While the basic needs and services continue to be an issue in the project area, it is expected that an integrated Health, Nutrition and WASH program with a community-led approach will reduce the current morbidity and mortality risks faced by most vulnerable particularly boys and girls under five and pregnant and lactating mothers, the elderly, the disabled and the destitute, but also work towards improving the community’s capacity to a sustainable, resilient future.
The project interventions and activities are linked to the national priorities, Nutrition policies and workplan.URDO will provide a holistic package of essential basic nutrition services packages(BNSP) which include management acute malnutrition, micronutrient support, IYCF counseling, maternal nutrition, de-worming, IYCF promotion, Immunization services and food fortification and prevention. IYCF activities will be guided by the Somalia IYCF policies and guidelines and will monitor violation of the Breast Milk Substitue (BMS) in the area. URDO will use the updated Somalia IMAM guidelines,IYCF guidelines and other related manuals for implementation. Liasing and engagement with the Ministry of Health and relevant national and local offices and community leaders will be prioritized during implementation.
URDO takes action in response to emergencies and at the same time promotes respect for international humanitarian law and its implementation in national law. URDO has a code of conduct to ensure that displaced people are treated with the humanitarian principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence. As such the highest regard for the protection of human life and nutrition will be provided to all people needing and seeking help with clear policies and procedures in place for URDO staff and partners to endure that not protection risk is incurred

Project Activities

Bardhere like many parts of Somalia has been majorly affected by the long standing conflict resulting and climatic changes that have had a debilitating effect on all social services infrastructure. Increasingly, gaps in the coverage of essential and life-saving health services are aggravated by inadequate access to safe water and sanitation, mass displacement and eroded livelihoods. FSNAU and FEWSNET September 2018 brief projects that between August and December 2018 an estimated 294,200 children will likely face acute malnutrition, including 55,200 who are likely to be severely malnourished. It is further estimated that of the 8,200,000 people in need, 5,368,274 people will be in need of humanitarian WASH services in 2018. The lack of essential WASH services means serious public health risks and surge of preventable deaths. This program shall promote the equitable participation of both men and women in the planning and decision making processes, this will ultimately help to ensure that the entire affected population has safe and easy access Health, Nutrition services as well as access to better water supply, sanitation facilities. Health and Nutrition posts in the operational areas are one of the cost effective and community owned approach that leads to better outcomes and enhances sustainability. The WASH intervention is meant to complement the Health and Nutrition interventions to further reduced water based and water washed diseases which increase the morbidity and mortality in the area. URDO intends to rehabilitate wells and boreholes and carry out a rigorous health promotion activities. URDO will work closely with the communities and establishing community committee’s e.g training water management committees to manage infrastructure; and organizing village health teams to model good sanitation and hygiene practices and have a well maintained CHW network who are well trained on Nutrition Health and Hygiene Promotion (NHHP) and screening of malnutrition.

Project Indicators

URDO will identify areas where it can find synergy and demonstrate Value for money in implementing the emergency Nutrition program in Bardhere district. All activities in line with the Health and Nutrition services will be provided under one roof. The medical and nutrition team will provide primary health care to children under five and pregnant and lactating mothers and will also carry out screening of malnutrition of the same target groups. URDO in meeting the above focus will recruit and train 20 health care workers (of which 50% are females and 50% males) in basic emergency obstetric care (BemOC), focused antenatal care (FANC) and Essential newborn care (ENC) and Community based management of Acute malnutrition (CMAM) URDO is proposing to implement integrated curative and preventive nutrition interventions under this project through establishment of 6 OTP and TSFP in Bardera. The preventive nutrition interventions will mainly focus on Behavior Change Communication (BCC) targeting mothers and fathers of children less than two years in the target population and routine immunization. In addition, mothers of children in the Outpatient Therapeutic care Program(OTP) will be enrolled to mother support groups in their area of residence where women in the neighbourhood meet and discuss issue related to child care and Infant Young Child Feeding (IYCF). These sessions are led by community nutrition volunteers and will run 8-10 weeks until all essential IYCF topics are discussed. The mothers who complete these sessions will be encouraged to establish similar groups in their neighbourhood to pass the message to other mothers in the area. Malnutrition levels throughout Bardera district and villages remain high. The high rates are related to variations in right to accessibility and utilization of a diet of adequate quantity and quality, poor public health conditions (including water and sanitation), low levels of access to quality Nutrition services for vulnerable groups and lack of basic knowledge on diets.

Mitigation Measures

URDO being an indigenous organization is committed to safe programming and will make sure that any of its program activities should not cause benefit and no harm. URDO is aware that limited livelihood opportunities are also a cause of conflict in Somalia, therefore URDO will as much as possible be working with the community and will be cognizant of the fact that the community is subdivided into host and IDPS, women, men and youth and children. Whenever it comes to activities that involve communal structures that might later on bring conflict problems like hiring of staff, reconstruction of health and nutrition posts, Boreholes and wells URDO will make sure that all persons within the community is part of the decision making, this includes even people with disability. The final decision will be shared through public announcement. URDO is also very much aware of the “Heyr” cultural system in which many disputes are settled, therefore to avoid URDOs direct involvement in whenever there are conflicts that might affect the program activities. It should also be appreciated that many Youth and idle Somali men and women will involve themselves in conflict, therefore it is anticipated through the program course that Youth men and women will be engaged in the program through community activities and will receive trainings.
