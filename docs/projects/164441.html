Project Summary

The project aims to address the local causes of conflict in Afrin, Syria and seeks to create “safe spaces” for local identity groups to discuss their concerns openly and enhance mutual understanding while resolving some of the outstanding disputes. Social cohesion is to be enhanced at the community level among citizens pertaining to the host community to help them better articulate their common interests and strengthen their capacity to represent their group. Through training programs and advocacy work, the community will be better equipped to actively and harmoniously contribute to peacebuilding, community dialogue and ensure a more effective bottom-up impact of efforts. Furthermore, the focus will be directed towards developing initiatives that promote collective action and integrate peacebuilding concepts into society.
Also, The project consists of the activities as follows:
1-conduct a deep analysis of the context to understanding the context in which Bahar is operating in the target community/communities during the first two months of the project, the anylisis will focus on : the context profile (socio-economic and political information, emergent issues, history of conflict .. ), conflict dynamics , potential sources for consensus building … etc. Based on the findings of the analysis, the intervention will be built.
2-Form 3 representative committees to strengthen collective identity and create in-group cohesion, and support them to be involved in the decision making process The groups will be made of 20 to 30 people, and they will be categorised in Women group, Men group and Youth group After six months of such exercise, the three groups will reunite in a common discussion, The main goal of this activity is for the groups to be able to solve their problems in a peaceful way and use non-violent resolution to overcome the conflicts within their own group and with other groups, and prepare the Community projects and initiatives like (sports clubs for youngsters, cultural or educational festivals, field school for adults, food processing initiatives for women) . 3 Initiatives with 15.000 $
3-Provide capacity building programme to community representative committees and support them in becoming CBOs, Several trainings and workshops will be provided with topics related to conflict nature and causes, dialogue facilitation, mediation, interest-based negotiation, conflict analysis and advocacy ( 6 Trainings will conducting with 4 days for each training)
4-Create a conflict resolution network to solve conflicts, to collect information, design social initiatives and coordinate local efforts for reducing tensions, which will enclose representatives of the committees/CSOs, The goal of the network will be to work towards establishing local peace mechanisms starting with preparing an annual plan, which will be made with contributions coming from all parties, The network will have a permanent secretariat made of 5 members elected through free and transparent election every year. Meetings will take place on monthly basis.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: The local authorities will place no restriction on the work of civil society organizations/groups rendering on their effective contribution to activities. There will be no major security risks interfering with project implementation and the situation will keep calm. The youngsters will show interest in the activities. Risks: Risk 1: The heightened conservative discourse in some of the target areas is detrimental to rebuilding social trust and rebound people. Mitigation 1: The project is implemented with the consent and collaboration of local community representatives and having the authorization from the local authorities, who will take the necessary measures to ensure the continuation of the activities. The project relies on a right-based approach and non-threatening language to disseminate its messages about social cohesion and work collaboratively with young leaders to counter the effects of negative conservative discourse. Risk 2: There will be a low level of women involvement in the project as the area is highly conservative. Mitigation 2: The team will communicate with tribal and local leaders and will explain the importance of women involvement in the project activities. The registration of beneficiaries will target females and the project locations will ensure safe participation for them in the actions. Risk 3: The community actions plans will not be realistic fixing too high targets and the selected projects will rather amplify the tensions in the community In order to keep the plans close to the needs but feasible in term of achievements, Bahar team will provide mentorship and it will hire trainers capable to enhance the skills and knowledge of the beneficiaries, preparing them to write the plans and implement the subsequent activities.
