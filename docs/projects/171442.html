Project Summary

The project aims at improving sanitary situations (known as WASH) in the selected Areas of the suburb of Shweikeh town (Tulkarem Governorate) and 6 schools located in Area C of Jenin Governorate. The improvement of the sanitation situation in the target areas is essential for a stable life with basic services that will help the residents and the students of these schools to suffer less and to gain better education when town and school is served with sanitation facilities. Based on a recent survey conducted by House of Water and Environment (HWE), more than 2473 people will benefit from this project directly (1510 males and 963 Females). It has been estimated by House of Water and Environment that more than 3000 people will be benefit from the proposed project indirectly. The targeted areas of Shweikeh town are located in Zone C. These lands are outside the boundaries of the areas normally served by Tulkarem municipality. These areas suffer badly from a lack of wastewater collection system as 95 % of their residents rely on septic tanks and cesspits, which cause unsanitary conditions and pollution of soil and groundwater resources in addition to affecting negatively on the public health and safety of the women, men, children elderly and disabilities of these areas. Therefore, there is an urgent need to mitigate the bad sanitary conditions in these areas through installing wastewater collection system to serve the targeted population in Shwaikeh area and to promote good access to environmentally sound municipal wastewater services. The targeted schools require their sanitary facilities and drinking water facilities to be rehabilitated. It is proposed in this project to build new sanitation units. So that, those six schools will enjoy having access to adequate sanitary and water units with services for disabled students. Public awareness campaigns will be conducted to address hygiene practice issues at the household level to improve the capacity of the families in the targeted areas to effectively respond to emergencies and reduce tensions across the communities. It worth to mention that, transmission of COVID-19 and other water borne diseases will be lowered. The proposed interventions ensure protection mainstreaming incorporating protecting principles and promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity; accountability as well as participation and empowerment through conducting regular meeting and focus groups.

1 - Needs

Over 60 % of the West Bank is considered Area C. Under Oslo II Accord, Area C is under total Israeli civil and military control and is subject to extensive restrictions seen to hamper Palestinian development. Recently, due to increasing Palestinian population in the West Bank, and consequently the expansion of residential areas, these is an crucial need to build a live area C to become vital and significant for human living conditions sustainability. In addition, Palestinians move to live in Area C, due to land low price comparing to the rest of the lands in areas A&B. In addition, the construction of the separated wall by Israel impedes access to water, food and sanitation services. Palestinians in area C, lack of basic services and needs, because of Israeli obstacles and inability of Palestinian local government councils and municipalities in offering such services in Area C. Inadequate WASH facilities expose women and girls to threats and burdens associated with meeting their personal hygiene needs, undertaking basic domestic chores, managing household water needs, and securing the needs of children disabilities, the elderly and the chronically ill. In addition to secure the locality from spreading COVID-19 and in decreasing the number COVID-19 cases. As result of this inadequate wash situation, an awareness campaign will be conducted to disseminate the importance of the project HWE has conducted need survey for all targeted communities in Area C, this survey was driven by coordination between PWA and HWE. In Addition to the need assessment conducted by Wash Cluster team with full coordination with them, one potential location was selected and screened in Tulkarem (Shweika) and 6 schools located in Jenin Governorate were selected: Shweikeh, is a suburb zone located within Tulkarem municipality borders, where 11417 people live (PCBS 2017). Shweikeh is an expansion area of the normal growth of the city. The urban area of Shweikeh is restricted by Israeli separation wall in the west where construction of new infrastructures prohibited. Some lands can’t be served by municipality because their location in Area C. About 1058 capita: 532 males and 526 females (Tulkarem Municipality and HWE, 2020) suffer from a lack of wastewater collection networks. 2453 households in the targeted area are not connected to sewerage networks. They rely on septic tanks and cesspits, which cause unsanitary conditions and pollution of soil and ground water reservoir that is used to be close to the ground level. Lack of access to sewer system, affects women and girls (18-65) ability to engage in income generating activities as most of them work in agriculture. Target area approximation to separate wall, leads to limit community ability to access their farms. Thus, there is a need to mitigate bad sanitary conditions. In addition, using cesspits in these target areas forms heavy economic burdens on men, due to high cost of emptying cesspits. Children (0-18 years) and elderly people (+65 years) from mixed-sex group are sensitive to unhealthy conditions caused by improper behavior in wastewater disposal and expose the community to be affected by COVID-19 Virus. Violent activities and social disputes are common among communities, especially young people (19-55 years) from both sex groups. To solve this non-healthy condition, it is required to build/install wastewater collection network to serve the targeted population. 1- Six schools, located in Jenin Governorate. Limited access to WASH services is observed in the six schools four of them (Jenin Secondary for boys (332 boys), As-Shaheed Abu Ammar Males (186 boys), Secondary Birqin for girls (400 girls) and Al-Yamun Primary boy’s school (419 boys) need to construct and rehabilitate the sanitary units . So, at all, those six schools have no access to adequate sanitary and water units. in addition to installing two drinking systems in two schools: Imreeiha primary school (25 boys and 21 girls), Dhaher Al Malih Primary shcool (17 boys and 15 girls).

2 - Activities

coordination with PWA was done to define target group needs. Proposed interventions and activities can be categorized as follows: 1) Construction of sewer lines in Shweikeh Suburb: This intervention is contributed totally to WASH cluster and consists mainly of installation of sewer networks. The proposed networks to be installed are detailed as:a) Length: 750 m, Diameter: 8 inches, Number of House connections: 71,Number of Beneficiaries: 334 (168 Males and 166 Females) b) Length: 880 m, Diameter: 12 inches, Number of House connections: 154,Number of Beneficiaries: 724 (364 Males and 360 Females)2) Conduct Public awareness targeting the community to to promote household connection to municipal networks and improve hygiene behavior Number of Beneficiaries: 1058 (532 Males and 526 Females) Proposed intervention includes: design of sewer line, excavation works, PVC pipes installation, manholes installation, filling and compact excavations with sand and base coarse layers. Number of beneficiaries from this activity is estimated at 1058 beneficiary, distributed as 532 Males and 562 Females. 2) Installation of sanitation and drinking units in six schools: Main activities are:a- Construction of 3 sanitary units in Jenin Secondary for boys, As-Shaheed Abu Ammar, Secondary Birqin for girls schools and rehabilitation of sanitary unit First Al-Yamoon Primary boy’s school. b- Installation of drinking units Imreiha primary school and Daher Al Malih school. Number of beneficiaries from this activity is estimated at 1415 beneficiary, distributed as 979 Males and 436 Females. All proposed activities will ensure protection principles on safety and dignity, meaningful access, accountability, participation, empowerment and claiming of rights.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

Safety plan and measures will be taken into consideration to avoid harm for community and workers, by identification of safety and health risks taking into consideration the precautionary safety measures in relating to COVID 19 virus crisis. In addition to the mitigation measures. This plan generally will consist of the following protection activities: 1) Responsive activities: immediate and urgent violation and effects are to be predicted, immediate actions to treat with such effects are to be defined. Example 1:- immediate effect: sudden collapse in a trench - Immediate action: Evacuation of people around the collapse, consolidation of collapse edges to prevent expansion and first aid for any injury, if any. Example 2: Immediate effect: Cut in water pipe inside trench. Immediate action: Fix it as soon as possible. Example 3: Immediate effect:Violation between two neighbors on their cross border. Immediate action: Cooperate with representatives of community to solve such problems, and try to avoid creatingviolence between neighbors. 1) Remedial activities: Long term effects are to be indicated; remedial plan is to be set. This activity will also include forming a committee from community (5-7 people) to facilitate project implementation and solve any upcoming problem with community. Example 1: Remedial effect: House owner explained about crossing sewer line his house sidewalk because during time of implementation he will has social activity such as wedding party. Remedial action: negotiation plan and awareness activity. 3) Environmental activities: Define activities to create safe work environment for workers and community.2) Example 1: Installation of warning signs to warns from any expected harm, e.g. warning from falling in a trench. Example 2: Considering disabilities needs in sanitation units (for students).Example 3: During installation of sanitary units and drinking units for schools, there will be no sanitary and drinking units to be used by students. To overcome this problem, this activity should be implemented in vacation days or use movable sanitary and drinking units
