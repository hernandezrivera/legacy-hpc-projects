Project Summary

This intervention will contribute to cover the gap of food insecurity especially that -This year the estimated number of people in need has increased by 8 percent comparing to last year. The project will respond to food needs of most vulnerable people and develop essential households’ livelihoods assets through improving access to bakeries by conducting the needed repairs, creating income generating (temporary jobs). This project will aim to support men, women, boys and girls who are affected by the Syrian crisis to experience less impact of the conflict and economic crisis by safely accessing humanitarian assistance. Massive depletion of livelihoods infrastructure, high levels of unemployment and poverty coupled with high food prices and inflation have all contributed to low purchasing power, as households struggle to meet their daily food needs. The project will contribute to food insecurity through rehabilitation of partly damaged bakeries in NES and Rural Damascus. Rehabilitation and repairing of the needed works in bakeries is vital for the families needs in the targeted areas. This procedure is considered as the back bone for living in the targeted areas as it will have great positive impact on securing income sources and empower the returning families.

4 - Needs

Ongoing violence and high levels of insecurity after almost a decade of conflict have impacted all aspects of the country and its people, with significant consequences on the pillars of food security. Unprecedented numbers of displacement have meant that almost two thirds of the Syrian population have been forced out of their homes causing a detrimental effect on the availability and access of food. With an increase of 8 percent in the people in need since last year, food security and poverty remain to be deeply intertwined as economic deterioration becomes more persistent over time. Despite being one of the most resilient pillars of the economy and key to the national food security, the agriculture sector has had significant losses since the beginning of the crisis in 2011 affecting both crop and livestock production with a noted decline of 49 per cent and 51 per cent respectively. Widespread damage to irrigation systems and shortages of labor and quality inputs such as seeds, fertilizers and fuel due to imposed sanctions on Syria has affected the country’s production. Bread is a strategic commodity in the Syrian Arab Republic which the Government subsidises through the sale of subsidized wheat flour to selected bakeries belonging to the public sector which have to sell the bread at a government regulated fixed price, this is known as public bread. Commercial bread instead is produced by private bakeries and is sold in shops. Commercial bread tends to be of higher quality and its price is not fixed. According to HNO 2020, the number of people estimated to be food insecure is 7.9 million or 39 percent of the total population of Syria. Of this at least 569,477 (6%) are estimated to be severely food insecure. The near one decade crisis in Syria has had an impact on the livelihoods and food security situation of Syrians throughout the country. At least 37 percent of the food insecure population are in North West Syria.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will include the following stages: -Field visits will be conducted to the targeted areas to prepare all needed documents including BOQs. -The works will be carried out according to the tendering method followed by GOPA through a dedicated contractor. -The execution of the work will be by contractors who are experienced in the field of architectural and civil projects. Workers from the local communities will be provided by job opportunities through work in rehabilitation works under the contractor supervision -After all planned works are being completed, a special committee is formed to take over the executed works and to ascertain the quality in the specified time. The work will then be officially handed over to the concerned authorities (MoLA). The rehabilitation will include implementing minor maintenance works including wooden works, aluminum works, electric works. Total of 6 bakeries in the most needed areas are planned to be targeted. The targeted bakeries will be chosen upon a needs assessment in the targeted areas in coordination with Food sector and concerned authorities.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: It is most probably that there are mines in the targeted areas. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigating measures: GOPA will seek to provide protection to workers in the chosen areas. Protection Risk: Risk of hurts to workers due to unsound buildings during preparing BOQ and implementation. likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1- Structural assessment for building by qualified engineers 2- repair the building after assuring the soundness and safety of building. 3- clarify for workers about the dangers of ERW and to inform about any strange materials. All our activities use a rights- based approach to provide a protective environment that promotes equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social speciﬁcation of targeted populations. Also, all our volunteers have already received a package of training including: psychological ﬁrst aid, non-violent communication, protection induction, basics of SGBV, ﬁrst aid, PSEA and risk management in case of any emergency, as well as other basic information that enables them to act towards any person in need with total respect and most importantly to deliver assistance while protecting the dignity of the person.
