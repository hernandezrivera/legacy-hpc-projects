Project Summary

"Poverty is one of the main challenges of the FDMN camps as well as the local population living in the remote regions where the camps are located. Without having any sustainable income sources, they often sell portions of their food rations and Non-Food Items to buy most necessary goods for their families. Rohingya refugee people get food support from WFP and some international and national NGOs. At the same time, employment and livelihood is the most affected sector for host community people due to the Rohingya influx. Their incomes have fallen by almost 50 percent for casual labors because Rohingya men are available in the market with lower wage. On the other hand, their daily expenditures have increased. So Rohingya influx has made very crucial situation for the economically challenged host community families in Ukhiya and Teknaf sub district.
Within the context of the humanitarian assistance for Livelihood and Food Security, which aims to "improve the income, food and nutrition security of the poor host community people and Rohingya refugee also, a pilot initiative "" small scale of Income Generating Activities and Nutrition support for Rohingya youth by pigeon and hen rearing and technical skill development on camp based . This initiative aims to improve their access to finance, services, and markets and consequently fulfill their nutrition demand. The project’s another aims to improve production, market, credit and innovative technology access, access to climate-resilient techniques and practices in order to increase economically challenged host community people’s productivity and income in a sustainable way. The project specifically targets women and youth in the region of poverty reduce. The project intends to increase work opportunities for Rohingya and host community youth’s, however targets specifically, youth and women.

1 - Identified Needs

"Community need assessment on Income Generating is a systematic process for determining and addressing gaps between current conditions. The discrepancy between the current conditions or wanted condition must be measured to appropriately identify the need. The need can be desire to improve current performance or to correct deficiency. The idea of the project comes as a result of seeing the Rohingya and host community people’s lifestyle, capacity, experience and scope of intervention implementation in both host and Rohingya community. Many youth men and youth women with their family members live in poverty. Community needs assessment aimed to improve youth’s and women’s income generation in both community through IGA training and support using their own resource they have in the community in order to reduce poverty by increasing their income in their households. Community Needs Assessment was carried out in corroboration with host community and Rohingya youth and women in both area and different techniques were used to assess the needs for a new intervention. COAST has conducted an assessment on livelihood opportunities where we have found that in host community area they have many sources for income generating but in camps it has limitations. Because a lot of people are living in a very small area in camps. So they have very minimum excess to start income generating activities. On IGA need assessment in host community we have gotten ten community and market actor suggested activities. Those are goat rearing, cow rearing, vegetable production, tailoring, paddy cultivation, driving training, micro business, mobile servicing, electrical training and chicken rearing. In Rohingya camps there have very few opportunities for starting a new IGA. On IGA need assessment in Rohingya community we have gotten five community and market actor suggested activities. Those are pigeon rearing, chicken rearing, tailoring, mobile servicing and micro business in camp.
