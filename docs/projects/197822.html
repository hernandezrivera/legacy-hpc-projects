Project Summary

The project aims at improving access and quality of Education in the most affected by conflits areas in Cabo Delgado Province: Pemba, Ibo, Quissanga, Montepuez, Balama, Namuno; in favor of 26,173 People in need.
The strategy will be developed in the following Objectives and Activities
ob.1 Improve ACCESS:
- Accelerated Classees for out of school children
- Provide learning supply to IDPs and Host communities children.
- Provide supply to teachers and school directors.
- Provide WASH facilities in schools
- Provide Hygiene supply
- Sensitize families and population on BTS themes
Ob. 2: Improve Quality:
- Strengthen the education system for through training for teachers in catching-up methodologies and pedagogic.
- Reinforce catch-up and remedial courses for children;
- Teachers training in order to strengthen the protection system in school for a better identification and charge of vulnerable cases, including GVB, abuses and other violations of children’s rights though: capacity building of teachers, school’s councils’ members and parents on MHPSS, life skills, PSEA, VAC and GBV
- Provide MHPSS services for vulnerable children.
Coordination will be guarantee by participating at Education Clusters at national and provincial level.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained and community members sensitized on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, children selection creiteria will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations and that educational service will be inclusive. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. A prioritization in the selection process will be given to children with disabilities. Child protection component is considered also within the following activities: teachers training on MHSPP and Psychosocial Support, sensitization campaign, referral of CP cases. Thought other ongoing projects, Protection monitoring, as part of the activities, will be guarantee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Participatory approach will be guarantee throughout all the phase of the project:Need assessmentProject implementationProject monitoringProject evaluationThis project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and re-oriented on the need to seek the views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries, children and teachers, and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints, as well as to be directly engaged in the implementation of the activities (sensitization campaign; mobilization). This project will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria for children and schools to be supported will be developed in consultation with the communities and educational authorities. AVSI will conduct protection qualitative assessments including children and teachears KOBO in order to evaluate their satisfaction to the program and give input for corrections and adaptation to their needs. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disability will be a priority condition and criteria under selection of beneficiaries.Activities will be adapted according the specific needsProject will be evaluated through evaluation activities which will take into consideration opinions, suggestions and evaluation from people with disabilities.n the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people and children with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. Target group includes 1.301 children with disabilities, who will participated to the AVSI program for children with disabilities, already implemented in the target area throught UNICEF’s funds. AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI will adopt all regulations and policies presented in its Environmental policies.The majority of the project will be a person to person intervention with minimum environmental impact. To ensure there is limited waste produced by the project, reusable supplies will be purchased whenever possible and limited consumables, particularly single use plastic goods, will be purchased throughout the activities.Most staff of the project and volunteers will be recruited locally and in proximity communities in which they will work, to minimize the number of times vehicles are plying in IDP settlement centres and host communities.
