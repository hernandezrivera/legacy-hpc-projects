Project Summary

The project aims to provide capacity building activities and organize coordination meetings for Syrian NGOs who are members of WATAN Network, in which there are 32 active members. The activities are planned to be conducted on monthly basis and includes field research via scientific methodology that aims to search in deep the most important security threats that the aid workers are facing in northwest Syria. The research will have the mixed research methodology including quantitative and qualitative research techniques. That will drive to have more comprehensive results and outcomes.
As a result of research analysis a set recommendation will be listed and published, and a tailor-made workshop will be later conducted for the hub members. This research will cover the following topics and aims to address the needs through a list of recommendation;
- The current situation of the CSOs in Northwest Syria
- Challenges of coordination
- Emergency respond capacities
- Localization
- Remote management challenges
- Fragile security that humanitarians are working in
- Challenges and opportunities faced by CSOs
- Host community vs IDPs
The project will include meetings with coordination bodies with such as UN OCHA, Syrian unions and networks who are like umbrellas under which many CSOs, NGOs, LNGOs are existing. Meetings will be conducted both in Syria and Turkey. As result of field research tailor-made training and workshops will target over 40 active members of WATAN Network and to more than 150 Humanitarian workers
WATAN Network services:
Coordination and Networking: provide monthly coordination meeting between members to facilitate engaging, building relationships, and experience exchange. Advocacy and Representation: Plan, Evaluate, and Implement joint advocacy campaigns with the humanitarian active actors as well as nominate members for representation in occasions.
Capacity Building: Plan and conduct capacity building for members through workshops and training sessions. Media Promoting: by publishing and promoting members’ activities and news through SADA, the first Arabic website concerned with the civil society. Legal and Logistics: Inform members of the latest
updates of both legal and logistics affairs through email bulletins.
Information Management: Support members making their strategic decisions and effectively engage
with key actors through designing, collecting, and analyzing information of requirements and needs

4 - Needs

People in northwest Syria are living through some of the worst crisis since the war in Syria began. More than 800,000 people have been displaced since 1 December 2019, due to intense conflict infreezing weather. The humanitarian community is doing everything it can but is overwhelmed by thescale of needs. An immediate cessation of the violence is critical. More resources, includingfunding, is immediately needed to save people’s lives and alleviate their suffering (OCHA situation report no.8) Due to recent updates and heavy conditions the needs enormously increased. UN agencies and NGOs are doing the best to cover the urgent needs and respond the emergency. As a result of hostilities and displacement, the provision of humanitarian assistance is severely hampered. Many humanitarian workers are themselves displaced with their communities, undermining the human capacity to provide humanitarian assistance as well. As the needs are growing every day, the need of capacitized CSOs and humanitarians are also growing. It is highly important and essential to build and/or strengthen the capacity of CSOs and humanitarians as well as provide a better coordination to increase efficiency and more access of the implemented projects. This project will contribute to increase the capacities of CSOs, humanitarians and provide better coordination.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will provide WATAN Network members the tailor-made trainings, focused trainings targeting the needs of gaps of CSOs, based on the field research and other following; Strategic planning, Donor relation, Human resources, Management, Governance trainings. 10 field researchers in Idleb and north Aleppo will receive focused training on conducting FGDs, questionnaires, and other data collection methods. The data collection is referred to have reliable and confidential data that mainly assists and feeds needs assesment efforts and defining gaps and issues to be addressed. WATAN team in Gaziantep, will analyse the data. There will be a data visualizing in place and relevant stakeholders will be included in the process of generating the final report . WATAN already has a well trained and experienced field team in Syria to implement the aimed activities. The planned activities under this project are; 1- Provision of dialogue between the WATAN network member NGOs and other 9 networks under OCHA Turkey hub recognition UN OCHA and interested INGOs working in northwest Syria. There will be a number ofdialogue sessions between these NGOs (number of meetings, number of training, emailcirculars, and online groups). 2- Field research to better understand the needs and gaps of CSOs 3- Tailor-made trainings that will be provided for all partners on coordination, access and security topics which will be the outcome of the data analysis. 4- Advocacy campaigns that will come out for the protection of humanitarian workers.

Likelihood: High. Impact: High Mitigating measures: Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and who services are for. Provide regular training to staff and service providers for code ofconduct and PSEA. Protection risk: Violating the confidentiality of the individuals while gathering data for field research. Likelihood: Low. Impact: HighHigh mitigating measures: All staff working on the data collection will be trained and asked to sign code of conduct and relevant WATAN policies. The whole process will closely monitored by WATAN HQ and field team.Protection risk: Collected data and information is shared to third parties Likelihood: Low. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: Changing the security situation that interferes with the implementation of the project. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High. High mitigating measures: WATAN will establish a response plan that can be activated according to the situation. Designing the activities in a flexible way where can be revised according to the situation to achieve the expected results. Risk: Communities and key stakeholders reject the project/plan. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High. High mitigation measures: WATAN will include the local and IDP community in the design and the implementation of the activities of this project, conduct community mobilization ahead of the project promoting to its proposed activities and ensure the community acceptance. Risk: The project location is not safe and/or accessible due clashes on the ground. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High. High mitigation measures: WATAN will prepare project evacuation plan and also remote control mechanism when applicable. Risk: Late financial payments by donors might affect the timely delivery of services and activities. Likelihood: Low. Effect: Medium. Mitigation measure: Early coordination with donors and agreement on disbursement dates and conditions.
