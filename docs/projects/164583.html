Project Summary

According to HNO of education for the year 2020, 6,800,000 children between the ages of 3-17 years old are in need of education support, in addition to children who are at risk of dropping out of school, and a significant number of children who do not have access to learning opportunities fall behind in meeting the basic early grade learning outcomes.
HRS has over 6 years of implementing education programs experience in Deraa, Eastern Ghuta, Idleb and Aleppo, HRS team has developed, tested and will implement, evidence-driven, cost-effective, scalable and adaptable quality interventions based on needs with the purpose to improve cognitive & social -emotional skills of children. By introducing psychosocial support, literacy and numeracy initiatives through libraries (NFE spaces) and in-service professional development for school staff; and creating opportunities for children, including marginalized children who fled from the southern Idleb to the north of Idleb and western rural of Aleppo, and lost opportunities to access formal schools by enrolling in NFE spaces.
The purpose of the proposed intervention is to reflect HRS 2020-2023 strategic plan to contribute to making a child safe, well and educated through creating pathways for out of school children to have access to appropriate and safe learning opportunities, and improve the learning outcomes and resilience of children.
HRS team also understands that the benefit to children will occur both through their direct access to learning opportunities and indirectly when working to change the parent/caregiver skills, therefore the intervention includes three main target groups: Caregivers through Hurras' other sectors activities, children and school staff through education.
The proposed intervention is supported by the established relationship between Hurras and 101 schools in the NW through ongoing child protection and quality education activities in those schools.
The districts of the proposed intervention in Harim and Jisr-Ash-Shugur were selected based on the daily security updates from the field teams where there are potential schools in areas for rehabilitation and can accommodate the education needs of the IDPs from southern Idleb considering the gender disaggregation the inclusion of children with disability when re-constructing latrines, designing the school’s entry.
Using the summary of HNO for education as a secondary source to identify that in Harim and Jisr-Ash-Shugur districts there are over 490,000 children have education needs and over 16,000 education personnel are in need, so the project is targeting and aiming to support 900 (495 female- 405 male) school aged children with non-formal education (through literacy and numeracy intervention ), during the summer club, then they can start the formal education starting from the academic year, In addition the project would offer stipends to a number of 90 schools personnel ( 50 male- 40 female) in Harim and Jisr-Ash-Shugur districts with 3 schools.
The proposed project would be in line with the current intervention under Manahel fund, but to expand to more schools supporting the improvement of reading, numeracy and social-emotional skills that will be measured using standardized baseline and end-line assessments. In addition, Social and behavioral change of parents and school staff to both assess and deliver better quality of services will be also measured.
HRS safeguarding policy is made visible to the community, with the codes of conduct and allegation reporting system made available in all schools Hurras run so all stakeholders can easily report complaints or child protection allegation to the field office and /or HQ.

4 - Needs

According to OCHA situation report of NW Syria in late Jan-2020, nearly 390,000 people were displaced in northwest Syria, mostly from large population centers such as Saraqab and Ma’arrat An Nu’man, as well as smaller towns and villages in other parts of southern Idleb governorate this unfolding situation impacted the learning of 59,000 school aged children and only 23% of the children have been reached lately and leaving a huge number of 44,000 still in need of education support.Those affected internally displaced children started to lose their access to quality learning opportunities in formal education settings due to living in temporary accommodations like camps and shelters in northern areas in Harem, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Azaz and Afrin districts where over 900,000 school aged children have severe and critical needs of education support, the proposed project would include rehabilitating and running additional learning spaces inside schools to accommodate the new arrivals of children in those areas and facilitate their access to education continuing to provide non-formal education using contextualized literacy and numeracy interventions, and awareness raising to parents on how to support their children reading at home through the direct contact between the school's teaching staff and parents/caregivers, with a distribution of literacy booklets that could be used at home.The spaces within which formal education, psychological support, literacy and numeracy programs are to be applied. respond to children who have been dropped out of schools due to reasons of internal displacement, lack of support by parents/caregivers.

5 - Implementation of activities

The proposed project would focus on rehabilitating and operating schools to offer non-formal education through literacy and numeracy interventions to new arrivals of children who were denied access to education lately,so they would be easily enrolled in formal education, the project would include the following activities :-Rehabilitating schools: HRS' team have identified several schools in Harim &Jisr-Ash-Shugur districts that are partially damaged and need rehabilitation, out of which 3 would be rehabilitated under this project, which would consider gender disaggregation, and inclusion of children with physical disabilities -Running schools: HRS would support running the schools by providing the monthly costs,-Stipends: HRS would provide stipends for 90 adults (50 males- 40 females) personnel in schools, including the positions: head teacher, lead teacher, teachers, safeguarding officer, librarian, janitor, to cover a percentage of education personnel in need as per the HNO (16,000 in Harim &Jisr-Ash-Shugur districts),-PSS and reading: HRS would establish a library inside each school, that will offer 1 reading and 1 PSS session per week. Each library will have a librarian to deliver the sessions, to a targeted number of 900 (495 female- 405 male) school aged children, -Non-formal education: within schools' literacy and numeracy interventions, where Each child will receive 3 literacy sessions and 2 numeracy per week for 12 weeks to complete the cycle of intervention, the total target is 900 (495 female- 405 male) school aged children, HRS has an established monitoring and evaluation department with dedicated field M&E team, in charge of evaluating the output and outcome level and would apply M&E tools already tested ,to assess the improvement of the children’ learning outcomes, in addition Hurras would ensure to assign safeguarding officers and librarians to implement safeguarding and psycho-social support activities to keep children safe in schools.
