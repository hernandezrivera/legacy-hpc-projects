Project Summary

Proposal Summary
1.1. Title: Strengthen the provision of essential health care services to the vulnerable groups and IDPs in Deir Ezzor, Al-Haskeh, Daraa and Rural – Damascus governorate
1.2. Objectives/ goal:
Decrease avoidable mortality and morbidity among affected people in Deir Ezzor, Al-Haskah, Daraa and Rural – Damascus governorate
1.3. Expected outcomes:
• Primary health care services including medical consultation, medication and diagnosis investigation are provided to 316800 Patients.
• Secondary health care services including surgical operations are provided on referral basis to 1140 Patients.
• Trauma care services including treatment for patients with trauma injuries and provision of assistive devices to 2664 Patients
• MHPSS services to 39360 patients
1.4 outputs: the intended activities briefly like:
• 16 medical mobile teams running in Deir Ezzor, Al-Haskeh,Daraa and Rural-Damascus Governorate
• 362412 PHCCs supported by 4 mobile teams.
• 316800 provisions of medical consultation
• 45909 procure and provision of medication
• 23040raising awareness sessions Conducted on PHC subjects, etc.
• 1140 referral services supported under secondary care.
• 2664 trauma Care services Supported on referral basis.
• 39360 of MHPss services provided.
1.5. Proposed Budget: USD 1,562,702
1.6. Proposed Duration: 12 months from 1/3/2024 -28/2/2025
2. Health needs Analysis:
2.1The health Situation in the targeted location:
Deir Ezzor
The public health system has been harshly disrupted during the crisis period. According to Health Resources Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS), 16 public health centers classified as partially functioning in in Deir Ezzor governorate.
Al Sham Association for Health strengthened access and delivery of life-saving and life-sustaining health care services including Primary, Secondary and Trauma health care services to the most vulnerable groups in Deir Ezzor governorate in cooperation with UNICEF in 2019-2023 through the deployment of 3 fix health clinic and two medical mobile teams targeting Deir Ezzor and AlBokamal and AlMyadeen for 12 months period.
Moreover, Al Sham Association for Health strengthened access and delivery of life-saving and life-sustaining health care services including reproductive health care services to the most vulnerable groups in Deir Ezzor governorate in cooperation with UNFPA in 2020-2023 through the deployment of 1 fix health clinic, three medical mobile teams and net of midwives in Rural alMaydeen and AlBokamal targeting Deir Ezzor, AlBokamal and AlMyadeen for 12 months period.
Consequently; there is a huge need not only essential health care services especially under primary health care with specialties on internal and chronic diseases, child health, gynecology and RH.
Al-Hasakeh
The public health system has been harshly disrupted during the crisis period. According to Health Resources Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS), 5 public health centers classified as partially functioning in in Al-Hasakeh governorate in the targeted area in this agreement (AlShadadi , Markadah, Al-Hol city and Tal Hamis).
Al Hasakeh, is classified as 3 on health severity scale with an estimated number of people in need as 202,862 out of 289,803. The percentage of persons with disability is 47 % in Al Hasakeh.
According to WHO / Health Resources and Services Availability Monitoring System, there is no fully functioning public health center NES, 43 are classified as non-functioning distributed, while the number of partially functioning public health centers is 57 in Al Hasakeh.
As for public hospitals, off a total 5 public hospitals in Al Hasakeh governorate, there is only 1 hospital reported as fully functioning, 1 as non-functioning and 3 as partially functioning.
