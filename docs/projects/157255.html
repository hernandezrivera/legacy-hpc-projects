Project Summary

In South Kordofan, Blue Nile, West, North and Central Darfur states, GAH conducted needs assessment in December 2018 to review the humanitarian situation. The assessment covered the localities of Provision of WASH services to the vulnerable communities in Atadamon in Blue Nile, Nertit and Golo in Central Darfur, Elserief in North Darfur, Abujubeha in South Kordofan, Elgeneina and Sirba in West Darfur States in Sudan. According to the findings of the assessment reflected that there humanitarian needs in these areas, 40 to 60 percent of assessed boreholes and 70% of the hand pumps are dried up due to shortage of rainfall, people are traveling long distance to collect water (30 minutes to 8 hours) and 80% of the assessed population are not practicing proper hand washing practices during critical times, In addition, only 4% to20% of the assessed households have latrines and the remaining percentage are practicing open defecation including the IDPs, host communities and SSR. They suffer from lack of enough safe drinking water and basic sanitation facilities and inappropriate hygiene practices. According to the State Ministry of Health (SMoH), the international NGO (INGO) and the World Health Organization (WHO) report, some 240 cases of suspected AWD in the targeted Areas, The major causes being limited water supply and inadequate personal hygiene practices. This confirmed that water quality is a public health issue among the targeted communities. The planned intervention is needed to contribute to reduce morbidity and mortality associated with WASH related diseases among IDPs, host communities and vulnerable groups, especially children under 5 years and pregnant/lactating mothers and elderly. Additionally GAH conducted assessments in Dec 2018 and Jan 2019 to rural targeted areas to identify the most critical WASH needs and there is urgent need of WASH services as well as other needs like health and immediate food and non food items assistance. In Dec 2018. GAH participated in two different inter-agency assessments lead by UNHCR to the targeted Localities, The main finding of these assessment are summarized as follows, The main assessment findings in WASH sector highlight needs for (i) safe drinking water by repairing the existing Hand pumps, Haffirs and upgrading some to mini-water yards, (ii) support sanitation/hygiene including school sanitation/hygiene, and (iii) explore options for new water supply sources. The socio-economic situation in the targeted areas had been broadly affected by the conflict in South Sudan and Civil wars in Darfur as the entire displaced populations sought refuge in villages within the targeted areas putting additional pressure on existing available WASH facilities, hence posing a threat of possible conflict outbreak among different communities, people currently depend on unprotected open water sources for drinking water and these sources are not protected, and water declines significantly during the dry season. In late 2018, GAH conducted joint assessment with WASH partners in targeted localities. The heavy rains and floods in July and August 2018, affected many houses in these areas and caused collapse of many latrines, affected water supply facilities and increased breeding of diseases borne vectors. A total of 156 latrines completely collapsed that need replacement and 242 latrines partially affected. Water supply facilities were also affected by floods, 30 water points needs rehabilitation.

Needs

According the 30 September 2018 OCHA's Humanitarian Snapshot, "the UN and the actual NGO implementing partners estimate there are 240,000 IDPs in Central, West and North Darfur and South Kordofan States. There are 183,500 new South Sudanese refugees in 2018 bringing their total to 423,000." The 8 October issue of OCHA's Humanitarian Bulletin reported that "there have 35,000 cases of Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD) causing 200 deaths among the targeted areas." Also, the authorities are reported newly emerging areas in Central Darfur areas where access has not been available for agencies to provide basic necessity services. Many from these localities are wandering searching for water,food and shelter. The implication is a tremendous need to help the Sudanese Government to provide even the most basic life-saving services to these traumatized wandering IDPs, refugees, returnees and vulnerable host communities, along with the increasingly over-stressed host communities. Needs assessment missions conducted by GAH 24-27 November 2018 concluded that there was a overwhelming need for all sectors, especially Nutrition, Health and WASH. It was cited the need for safe drinking water and AWD mitigation training and convenient sanitation locations as key priorities. WASH urgent needs: 1.Sustainability of water services in Geneina, Improved Environmental health in West Darfur, Behaviour change through hygiene promotions and improved environmental health (Vector Control) in Abujubeha in SK, construct defecation facilities for the communities in Alsereif in North Darfur State, Improved defecation facilities in Al Tadamon in BN, as well as in Golo and Nertit in Central Darfur needs improved defecation facilities.

Activities

Rehabilitation of 40 Hand Pumps, Distribute jerry cans to reduce water contamination which will minimize water related diseases, implementation of CLTS for the construction of latrines for HHs and schools with community participation to eliminate open defection, Complementary systemic hygiene promotion with water and sanitation facilities such as hygiene awareness, trainings and supporting materials, Construct 25 HH latrines in line with WASH sector and sphere standards, Construct 1 Segregation latrines in schools for students and teachers, Construction of 2 hand washing facilities for student, Conduct 10 hygiene awareness sessions for community, Conduct 10 cleanup campaigns inside school and 10 for community, Construction of 1 teacher latrine in school (F/M), Procure and distribute 2 Water tanks for student for Drinking water at school, Distribution 8000 hygiene kits to vulnerable women, Construct 50 household latrines. (Consider 10 child-friendly latrines included), Provide 16 water points (water tanks), Rehabilitate 50 communal/public latrines, Conduct 80 awareness raising sessions on how to proper treat drinking water points and best hygiene practices, Conduct 80 cleanup campaigns, Provision 500 lights (solar energy light) at latrine and shelters, Ensure participation of the Refugees and IDPs in WASH activities (CLTS), Maintenance of the existing non-functioning tap stands, Chlorination and conduct training on use of recommended household water treatment technologies, Train 50 Hygiene Volunteers on improved hygiene and sanitation practices, and follow-up of volunteer’s hygiene promotion activities, Conduct Health, hygiene and sanitation education sessions at HC and in strategic community places, Distribution of 2,000 hygiene and water treatment kits to 250 households, Printing and distribution of 2,000 hygiene promotion leaflets and posters, Conduct 3 mass clean-up per location, Conduct SWASH activities (5 schools), Construct of 10 hand washing facilities.
