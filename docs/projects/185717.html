Project Summary

Currently, COAST Foundation has one project mainly focused on improving livelihood and food security in host community areas in Ukhiya and Teknaf. In addition to continue the services and work with more poor and vulnerable households in affected host community areas the coming year 2022, this project has developed. The project has prioritized women involvement in design, management and monitoring. To address most vulnerable people livelihood and food security needs the project will mainly target women, and disable people. The project’s major activities includes; Community consultation meeting and selection of beneficiaries, Baseline and End Line Survey of the targeted households, Project Inception meeting, Skill Development Training Agriculture and Non Agriculture, Cash Grant for starting IGA and small business, Courtyard sessions and follow up the IGA and livelihood activities, training on PSEA, CRM and Safeguarding Issues, training on DRR tree plantation/reforestation and Environmental Sustainability, Climate Resilience Adaptive Technologies, Contingency Fund to Response any disaster and emergency, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) and beneficiaries feedback event, learning sharing meeting and Capacity Building of Project Staff. Total 1000 families from selected two unions will be the direct beneficiaries of this project. Women headed households, families have low families income, and having person with disabilites and gender diversity in families and suffering from overall financial crisis due to Rohingya influx get priority. Through baseline survey, the targeted households will be identified. To improve the food security and livelihood options of affected host community, COAST will work on their socio-economic development for 1000 household’s age 19 to 49 years (female 58%, male 38% and 4% person with disabilities and gender diversity) in host communities’ areas and they will receive skill development training and later part of grant cash grant support to start their IGA and small business. 500 households will receive skill development training agricultural in host community. They will also receive grant support for special IGA goat, poultry, pigeon rearing, kitchen gardening, vertical gardening, demonstration farmer field etc. Another 500 host community households will skill development training non-agricultural livelihood support. After the training part, 500 households will be supported by cash grant to start that IGA. Program staff will conduct regular courtyard sessions and follow up the development. Community will be well aware of PSEA, complain response mechanism and safeguarding issues. Project will take regular feedback from the community. COAST Foundation will ensure the Accountability to the Affected Population (AAP) conducting community consultation meeting, involve them in project design and monitoring and influence on project activities. Besides, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will be carried out by hiring enumerators after distributions. This activity will help the project to know the community perception, feedback, further recommendations and impact of the distribution in the community. At the initial stage of the project, a baseline survey will be conducted to know the status of community people and how the project will support the most effective ways. Later end line surveys will be conducted to compare the charge made due to project intervention and what is the status of targeted outcome. As COAST Foundation is local organization and born in Cox’s Bazar, so this project will demonstrations of localization by engaging local community in implementation of the project. COAST is active partner of FSS sector and attend coordination meeting at sector. We will take part of FSS sector coordination meeting in regular basis. 5W, Sitrep and other required reports will be shared for better coordination.

1 - Identified Needs

Based on the findings from J-MSNA 2021 published by ISCG, the project will address the needs in Rohingya and affected host community. The timeframe of this project is proposed one year and that will enable COAST Foundation to contribute on achieving the objectives set by ISCG FSS sector through addressing the needs of the Host population in Ukhiya and Teknaf. Community need assessment on Income Generating is a systematic process for determining and addressing gaps between current conditions. Community needs assessment aimed to improve youth’s and women’s income generation in both community through IGA training and support using their own resource they have in the community in order to reduce poverty by increasing their income in their households. COAST has conducted an assessment on livelihood opportunities where we have found that in host community and Rohingya communities both are most vulnerable in terms of livelihood. As many actors are working in camps areas, so COAST Foundation will address the needs of affected host community areas and continue the services. Deputy Commissioner (DC) office also suggested to work more in vulnerable host community areas. According to the assessment, COAST have found that host community don’t have enough income to live. Both 76% respondents from host communities reported that they have don’t enough income to live and not good food for their families. 56% respondent informed that they have lack of skill to earn in their house like IGA and another 50% respondents have no financial ability to start any IGA. Women are most vulnerable in case of poor financial conditions. They had to reduce the number of meal compare to male.

2 - Activities or outputs

Community consultation meeting and selection of beneficiaries: The project will conduct community consultation meeting and most affected families will be selected as beneficiaries. However, there are some criteria that will be followed for selecting beneficiaries i.e. affected by the influx, lost everything or partially, not receive similar services by any actor etc. COAST will conduct community consultations meeting for setting beneficiaries selection criteria. The project will also take surveys to know the beneficiaries needs by the section criteria.Baseline and End Line Survey of the targeted households: At the initial stage of the project, a baseline survey will be conducted to know the status of community people and how the project will support the most effective ways. Later end line surveys will be conducted to compare the charge made due to project intervention and what is the status of targeted outcome. Project Inception meeting: At the initial stage of the project, a total two Kick off workshops/inception meeting will be organized with the community at union level. This activity will ensure the project information to community people and affected populations and stakeholders. Project inception meeting will give detailed information about project objectives and target beneficiaries and overall services of the project. Skill Development Training Agriculture and Non Agriculture: To enhance the capacity of vulnerable community, target beneficiaries will receive skill development training on agriculture and non-agriculture. For agriculture, goat rearing, cow rearing, vegetable production, paddy cultivation, chicken rearing will be provided and for non-agriculture training, tailoring training, micro business and small shop training will be provided. Cash Grant for starting IGA and small business: To start the business, targeted households will receive cash grant support in against their training. This fund will be transfer by using mobile banking like Nagod. Court Yard Session and Follow up the IGA and Livelihood Activities: When all the training will be done and cash transfer to target people, project staff will conduct courtyard sessions and follow up the IGA and livelihood activities.Training on PSEA, CRM and Safeguarding Issues: The project will provide PSEA, complain response mechanism (CRM), safeguarding training. These training will help the communities to prevent themselves from any negative incident by the project staff and community people by awareness and actions. Training on DRR tree plantation/reforestation and Environmental Sustainability: Every year, cyclones, floods and landslides occur in these areas. Besides, host community people often faced by severe natural disaster like flash flood, landslides, cyclone and others. To get respond from community people and better preparation, COAST will provide DRR and environmental sustainable training. These training will help the communities to reduce the loss resources due to disaster. 20 Hectares of lands will be reforested by tree plantation initiatives. Climate Resilience Adaptive Technology: Cox's Bazar is also one of the most vulnerable climate change areas in Bangladesh. To avoid negative impact of climate change, the project will initiatives some alternative climate resilience adaptive technology like saline tolerant seeds, sack method vegetables etc. Contingency Fund to Response any disaster and emergency: To respond to any disaster including COVID-19 or any other disaster occurring in the project period, this fund will be used to support the affected community in the period of disaster.Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) and beneficiaries Feedback: After distributing the cash to the targeted households, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will be carried out by hiring enumerators. This activity will help the project to know the community perception, feedback, further recommendations and initial impact of the distribution in the community. Learni
