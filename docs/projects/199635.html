Project Summary

Green Graduation programme as a graduation Programme will include four main pillars: social protection, livelihood promotion, financial inclusion and social empowerment. It will provide a comprehensive and sequenced package of support over a fixed period. This simultaneously will improve livelihoods and income and provides access to basic services including financial services. As Green Graduation, the Programme will apply a green lens to every stage of programme cycle and strive to address social and economic inequalities whilst taking a transformative approach to tackling negative environmental practices. It will ensure a Do No Harm approach [to the environment] whilst improving livelihoods and building the resilience of communities. Green graduation programme will provide an integrated support that includes consumption support via regular cash transfers, business training, coaching, increased knowledge on nutrition, asset transfers to help beneficiaries develop their own sustainable income-generating activity (IGA), savings assistance through establishment and running of viable Voluntary/Village Saving and Loan Associations; support environment protection initiatives; and additional trainings including Men Engage (topics on household governance, gender-based violence, working together as partners, making decisions as a team, sexual and reproductive health-HIV, education of children…), Women life skills, livestock rearing, improved farming practices, awareness on environment and other natural resource protection. The programme will be implemented in different phases/cohorts and activities will be the same for each cohort. Due to lack of viable partners in Sila, Concern will use direct implementation strategy. Yet, the organization will explore ways to engage with one active Association that works on social cohesion and pacific cohabitation and provides capacity building training to improve their capabilities; we already have a list of preselected local associations (that worked with DIZA Programme and PIRCS) and that the final selection process will be undertaken at the beginning of the project. The programme will also work closely with government representatives in charge of overseeing the different intervention in the province through MOUs.
