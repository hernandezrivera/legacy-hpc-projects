Project Summary

The proposed 12-month project will support provision of quality and high impact lifesaving nutrition interventions to internally displaced people in South and East Darfur states. ARC will provide nutrition services integrated to the primary health care facilities supported by ARC in South and East Darfur states. The nutrition activities will be implemented in an integrated approach with health and WASH. The goal of this project is to contribute to the reduction in nutrition-related mortality and morbidity, and improve access to high-quality multi-sectoral life-saving nutrition interventions for children under five and pregnant and lactating women who are below the emergency threshold. The project will support 23 Targeted Supplementary Feeding Programs, 23 Outpatient Therapeutic Programs and 3 Stabilization Centers (SC) including referrals to secondary health care and provision of essential drugs.
ARC will use the mother support group strategy to promote appropriate Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) practices and Social Behavior Change and Communication (SBCC) among the target communities. ARC will also enhance the technical capacity of ministry of health staff through conducting training on CMAM package, IYCF and training on monitoring and reporting of the nutrition activities.
All activities will be implemented in close collaboration with local institutions/ line ministries and communities such Community Relief and Development Committees (CRDC) to ensure sustainability and active community participation.

Needs

As per the draft of Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2019, under outcome 2 about 1,450,493 children and Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) across the country suffer from wasting annually, of which 331,529 children suffer from Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM), 833,180 children suffer from Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) and 385,784 PLW suffer from MAM. Outcome 2 targets vulnerable residents, protracted IDPs, non-South Sudanese refugees and returnees. Per the findings of 2013 S3M survey, both South and East Darfur have state level GAM (Global Acute Malnutrition) rates above or very close to the WHO 15% emergency threshold for acute malnutrition at 18.3% and 14.9% respectively. The SAM rates are 4% and 5.2% respectively. The percentage of children aged 0-6 months who are exclusively breastfed is only 79.9% and 81.2% in South and East Darfur States respectively. The percentage of good IYCF practices is only 41.9% and 40.3% in South and East Darfur states, respectively. As per the SMOH Nutrition Database, between January – December 2018, ARC admitted and treated 8,523 children with SAM without medical complications in Outpatient Therapeutic Program (OTP) in East and South Darfur. An additional 847 children with SAM and medical complications were admitted and treated in ARC’s three stabilization centers (SCs). The program also admitted and treated 14,326 MAM cases of 12,747 children and 1,579 PLW in South and East Darfur. Given the economic situation of the country due to inflation rate, it is expected that the humanitarian situation to be deteriorated and among the target population which will lead to increase the malnutrition rates during the proposed grant period especially during the hunger gap period when the food is depleted.

Activities

This project will implement the following activities:Conduct MUAC screening to children 6-59 months and PLWAdmit and treat children 6-59 months with SAM without medical complications in OTPsAdmit and treat children 0-59 months with SAM with medical complications in SCsAdmit and treat children 6-59 months with MAM in TSFPsAdmit and treat pregnant and lactating women with MAM in TSFPsRefer SAM cases with medical complication to inpatient Stabilization Centre (SC)Run Mother support groups in the targeted locationsConduct IYCF counselling to the mothers/caretakers of children 0-59 monthsProvide micronutrient supplement to children 6-59 monthsConduct CMAM training to the nutrition staffConduct monitoring and supervision of the nutrition activitiesProvide the drugs and nutrition supplies to the nutrition centers These activities will be implemented in line with Sudan CMAM operational guidelines and IYCF strategies.
