Project Summary

Through this project, ADRA aims to improve access of population affected by the conflict in Eastern Ukraine to essential services as well as critical civilian structures by repairing infrastructure. This will be achieved by increasing their access to clean water through delivery of water chemicals and water trucking/bottled water, testing water quality and distribution of sanitary water storage vessels. this will be combined with sustainable emergency repairs of pipes, tanks, as well as the supply of essential material, equipment and pumps of decentralized water supply systems and in schools and health facilities. The project will also address waste management problems by emptying septic tanks as well as distribution of essential hygiene items with hygiene promotion
The project will be implemented in GCA Donetsk Oblast with main focus on settlements near the contact line (0-5 km), and will be targeting vulnerable groups with focus on elderly people, persons with disabilities or injury, persons with chronic illness, and women & girls.
The project proposal is prepared based on Humanitarian Response Plan 2019 (HRP). The project will contribute to the implementation of HRP Strategic Objective 2 (SO2): Provide emergency assistance and ensure non-discriminatory access to quality essential services for populations in need. The project will also contribute to the WASH Cluster Objectives 1 and 2: WASH Objective 1. Ensure immediate and sustainable access to sufficient safe water, and minimal levels of sanitation provision, for conflict affected people. WASH Objective 2. Provision of critical WASH-related supplies and information for the prevention of water- and sanitation-related diseases.

Needs

The protracted crisis in Ukraine & Military actions are ongoing, with frequent & unpredictable fighting along the contact line.Vulnerable population,& IDPs in particular,keeps increasing.The territories with the most challenging living conditions are those in the “buffer zone”,situated along the frontline. Water infrastructure continues to come under constant attack in eastern Ukraine.In 2019, 21 incidents have been registered,impacting critical water & sanitation facilities, & the access to water to millions of people in both GCA & NGCA.The stoppage of the water supply could lead to severe consequences,including the blockage of heating systems,which rely on an uninterrupted water system,during the harsh winter season.Many settlements & municipalities continue to highlight water supply as the most pressing need for 2019.In 2018, at least 27 % of people who live close to the 'contact line' experience daily or weekly water shortages. The situation remains critical for major water infrastructure such as pipelines, pumps, & treatment plants. Most of villages along the contact line rely on decentralized water supply systems as they are not connected to the main adduction system.Water collected from their boreholes is not treated & thus not drinkable.According to a water quality analysis done by ADRA in Q1 2019, this water has usually traces of heavy metals & hardness & it tastes bitter or salty in many locations.Without treatment,this water is not desirable for drinking or cooking. Usually the source of water in these villages is not close to the settlement & financially capable families are buying it from local dealers who provide it by water trucking system ,quietly expensive (0.7 to 3 UAH/liter), which represent a huge burden for vulnerable economy of families that have to struggle in a daily basis with unemployment & poverty. According to ADRA assessment, many children have no access to drinking water in their schools & the major part of health institutions do not have a proper water supply system.In these cases, water is provided by truck which represents a high cost for the budget of these facilities. The same situation occurs in EECP where hundreds of people, mainly elders, cross these points in a daily basis & are forced to wait sometimes for hours.Access to drinking water is one of the priorities that is in risk to be disrupted. The situation is far to be easily solved as any water supply infrastructure can be implemented along the contact line:neither the military forces give the permission to work there nor the local companies are willing to work under a constant threat of being shelled. There is no doubt that the advanced age of the entire water system, which cuts across the 'contact line' continues to negatively affect people on both sides.Sewage networks & wastewater treatment facilities, which may be 70 years old, are also failing, which may lead to water contamination.Within the 5-km range of the 'contact line', some 56 % of people have a toilet connected to a sewer network.However, 18 % have a septic tank & 32 % have an outside toilet, rising to 69 % in rural areas. people are facing difficulties to empty septic tanks near the 'contact line', & some are instead using 200 liter drums & other means to empty septic tanks manually.This is more difficult,as pump trucks refuse to visit these highly volatile areas, or charge an increased price for the risk incurred. In 2018,a market study by the ACCESS consortium,NRC & SCI found that some hygiene items were not available in all locations in Donetsk & Luhansk oblasts, with the least available items being adult & infant diapers,only available at 53 % & 29 % of surveyed locations respectively.Meanwhile,the cost of hygiene items increased on average 10 % from Nov 2017 to Feb 2018.All those needs are even crucial for disabled & elderly people (27 % of affected population)

Activities & outcomes

• Activity 1: Repair/maintenance/replacement of pipes, tanks, pumps, etc. of existing & new decentralized piped water supply systems.This activity will be mainly focusing on replacement of old water pipes & pumping equipment/water tanks, which are long aged or/& are out of service. Equipment & pipes will be purchased & delivered to the settlement by ADRA. Local authorities in targeted locations are expected to complete all construction & installation work in accordance with the agreement For the durable & qualitative operation of the pumping equipment during its installation. The laying of pipelines & the installation of plug forming armature should be carried out in accordance with the applicable norms & rules. • Activity 2- Water Trucking/ Distribution of bottled water - Operation & maintenance of water distribution points at checkpoints. For some settlements, the question of drinking water supply remains the matter of life & death as it may solve the destiny of the total village. In coordination with local authorities & through a 9-points need assessment , ADRA has identified more than 10 settlements along the line of contact most at risk due to absence of drinking water & also identified 2 exit/entrance checkpoints – Pishevik & Mariinka to provide with clean drinking water according to the sphere st&ards (2-3 litres per person/day) for several months until the more sustainable resolution . In locations where water suppliers refuse to provide services due to security constraints, ADRA team will deliver water by their own truck . As a result, the intervention will reduce the risks of displacement for 8,000 people & prevent the spreading of various diseases in these communities. Also , 53,000 people moving from GCA to NGCA will receive clean drinking water. • Activity 3 - Improvements to the reliable provision of sufficient water of an adequate quality, at social institutions (wells & boreholes)This activity is to install water purification systems in schools, kindergartens, medical institutions, where there is urgent necessity for clean drinking water. While the administration of the social institution prepare a placement which has access to water supply & sanitation & meets the st&ards of the State Sanitary & Epidemiological Service &, will ADRA assumes the following obligations: 1. Analysis of water in the sanitary station for selection of equipment.2. Purchasing & installation of equipment.3. Analysis of water after cleaning.4. Further maintenance of the equipment during the year. The targeted social institution will be selected in coordination with local authorities & through an assessment with eligibility criteria including: Urgency of needs Close location to the contact line  Ability to complete all works & to provide documentation. In institutions where latrines require rehabilitation, ADRA will identify contractors to do the relevant repair works. • Activity 4. Distribution of Hygiene kits & Hygiene promotion events Healthy planet is a group event focused on the promotion of hygienic practices among children through theatrical performances. It includes training children in hygiene skills, promotion of healthy life style & distributing child hygiene kits. ADRA implemented this component in schools & kindergartens of Donetsk & Luhansk oblasts & received a lot of positive feedback from administrations of the institutions & parents. Healthy planet was also praised by cluster partners as a very successful event. In the frame of the project, it is planned to organize 32 events with the participation of 3,840 children & their parents. Additionally, 1000 HHs (2,300 beneficiaries) will receive hygiene kits. • Activity 5 Desludging of septic tanks Especially in areas close to the 'contact line', where access has been limited due to active hostilities, & where costs of removal are now high, ADRA will identify suppliers to desludge septic tanks & cover the relevant expenses.
