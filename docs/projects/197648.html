Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving food assistance to the newly displaced persons in Dana subdistrict that was affected by the latest conflicts and in urgent need for food assistance as a response in line with HRP 2023
Elaf will be responsible for delivering life-saving food supports through implementing a project mainly related to the aforementioned targeted areas due to the need that existed and the difficulties in accessing food for reasons mentioned in the needs assessment paragraph
.
activities enrolled in this proposal include;
1- Assisting 2000 HHs with 2000 food baskets which is designed according to cluster recommendations in Dana subdistrict and matches Sphere guidelines for 3 months in a row.
2- Providing 3000 RTER for 3000 HHs for newly displaced in open areas or in camps that are most vulnerable and most in needs for RTER
The weight of RTERs is 34,5 KG covers 2128 KCAL and the weight of food baskets is 48 KG and covers 1545 KCAL.
RTER packages for 5 days for a family of 5/6.
As for the implementation method, Elaf is planning to execute these activities directly through its field team in Syria. baskets are going to be purchased and distributed in most effective methodology to ensure timely response to the newly displaced.
MoUs would be signed with the Local Councils (LC) within the targeted areas since they are considered to be the formal authorities and the main communication channels. LCs will provide Elaf with the primary list of beneficiaries and the M&E team will be responsible of completing the verification process to ensure that all registered names in the final beneficiary list have met the project's criteria. When it comes to the targeted groups, Elaf will target the most vulnerable HHs to guarantee supporting the eligible beneficiaries regardless of their gender. The eligibility criteria for food baskets include the following:
1- Poor households with no/little income below 50 USD.
2- HH Caring for elderly, sick, disabled or injured family members.
3- Female-headed household.
4- Child-headed household
5- Elderly-headed household
6- HHs with pregnant/ lactating women
The eligibility criteria for RTEs include the following:
1- HH is newly displaced in the past month or affected by floods or bad weather condition.
2- Individuals or households with no or limited access to cooking facilities
3- Individuals or households with limited mobility or access to markets
4- Female-headed household.
5- Child-headed household
6- Elderly-headed household
7- HHs with pregnant/ lactating women
Dana communities can be reasonably deemed to be safe and secure comparing to other sub-districts in Idleb governorate. Despite this, the security situation across Syria can be rapidly deteriorated as the incidents have proved over the last years. Generally speaking, the security situation was, still and will always be the main risk of which all humanitarian projects consider. For this reason, Elaf will be assessing the security situation before and during the activities implementation to secure the safety of the field team, beneficiaries, suppliers and all other involved parties. The mitigation measures include the continuous evaluation of the security situation and avoid any potential risky incident. Elaf has complete access in each sub-district enabling it to accomplish the proposed project especially if MoUs are signed with authorities. Elaf has. For the association capacity, the field team in Syria has shown full capacity and implemented different types of activities and among them livestock activities which were all accomplished smoothly.
Baskets will be bought from within Syria with best price as the market assessment showed that all the material are available and to avoid the risk of border closure with Turkey

3 - Needs

The humanitarian situation for people in northwest Syria is at the most critical points due to ongoing hostilities, harsh winter conditions, and existing needs that were already severe. As populations continue to be uprooted from their homes, the need to provide them with safe and decent accommodation in a dignified manner remains a major undertaking. According to HNO 2023.Families who recently displaced to Dana and other districts which are safer are deprived of basic food assistance in light of huge number of displaced persons with low resilience and multiple displacement had forced them to use their resources and starting using negative coping mechanisms to obtain basic needs of food Moreover, Close to 11 years into the crisis, the resilience capacity of people in the most affected communities in Syria has been severely eroded. An estimated 83 percent of Syrians live below the poverty line. A monthly food ration with staple items costs at least 80 percent of an unskilled laborer's monthly salary and 50 to 80 per cent of a public service employee’s monthly salary, demonstrating the existence of “working poor” in Syria. FSS cluster published priority paper for 2023 showed the needs for the food baskets and RTER in Dana and other subdistricts as it is the basic need for the newly displaced persons. Many vulnerable groups in all covered communities suffer in securing the basic needs of food.

Risk a: There is a risk that NGOs may be accused of supporting the political aims of one group or another and lose neutrality. There is a riskthat armed groups may respond by taking a harder line, including restricting access of humanitarian groups in specific areas.
