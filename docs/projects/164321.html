Project Summary

In line with SO1 “Strengthen access to livelihood by creating income generating opportunities and by improving access to production and market infrastructure to restore local economy recovery”, PUI will support vulnerable conflict affected populations toward self sufficiency. The project will aims at developing and enhancing income generating capacities through small business projects (SBP), vocational training, and distribution of Professional Tool Kits (PTK) in addition to the rehabilitation of Economic Infrastructures. PUI activities will cover various neighborhoods in five governorates across Syria (Rural Damascus, Deir-Ez-Zor, Homs, Aleppo, Lattakia).

4 - Needs

Identifying the needs was based on initial data collected from different resources including the HNO, but more specifically through assessment conducted by PUI team in the targeted locations. Rural Damascus: -Daraya:+10,000 PoCs returned to Daraya and with the lack of services and access to markets there, a need for support to re-start small businesses is identified.-Duma: As result to PUI assessment,44,850 PoCs are residing in Duma.Due to long besiegement and the impact on the intensive fight, people are in need for VT & support to re-start business activities is also identified.Aleppo: -Dayr Hafir: With around 14,500 PoCs residing there,the need for supporting the small businesses,agricultural activities&rehabilitating the irrigation channels was identified.-Tall Refaat:+37,500 PoCs live in Tall Refaat and needs of VT&support to re-start of small business has been identified.Deir-ez-Zor: Currently, PUI is active in Sheikh Yassin, Jbeilleh, Al Orrdy and Harabesh within the city. Around 193,000 PoCs live in the city. Main needs identified are the rehabilitation of one bakery&support to re-start of small businesses.Homs:-Al Qaryatein,8,000 PoCs residing there are in need of rehabilitating of the irrigation system,support for small businesses & agricultural activities.-Mahin,With the lack of services and availability of goods in the markets, the 3,800 PoCs residing there need support for their livelihoods through small businesses &tool kits.Lattakia: PUI gained initial access to Qastal Maaf in mid-2019 through the OOR intervention. This access will be used to facilitate further interventions specifically targeting the rural areas, which have not been accessible for humanitarian interventions in over three years. With around 10,000 PoCs in the area, the need to support agricultural activities and small businesses was identified by the residents themselves.

5 - Implementation of activities

PUI will contribute to SO1 through the implementation of the following activities: 1.3. Vocational and skills training provided: The Vocational Training approach targeted vulnerable Syrians and refugees with the aim of building new skills in order to support in professional insertion, enhancing their self- sufficiency and income generating opportunities. 1.4. Entrepreneurship supported.PUI will implement this activity through the provision of Small Business Projects (SBP) and distribution of Professional Tool Kits (PTK). This activity aims at helping skilled people in Syria to (re)start/develop their professional activity in order to enhance self-reliance and generate a sustainable income through supporting the start-up of small businesses involving the distribution of productive assets and the support of business start-up. 1.6. Rehabilitation or repair of basic, local economic infrastructure, PUI will implement this activity on local economic infrastructure such as Bakery, VT center, Irrigation system, in collaboration with the Ministry in charge of the facility. = All activities will target IDPs, host communities and returnees in five governorates (Rural Damascus, Aleppo, Homs, Lattakia and Deir-Ez-Zor)

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:Acceptance of local population for PUI´s interventions remains high,Humanitarian access to the proposed locations continues,Approvals for the proposed activities are obtained,Security situation remains stable,No mass movements of populations into/out of intervention areas,No major fluctuation in exchange rates.Risk:Lack of accountability creates high unmet expectations-Mitigation measures:Strategic communication, explaining entitlements attached to beneficiaries&rapid assessment will ensure targeted and realistic interventions.Risk:Aids diverted to armed groups-Mitigation measures:Intervention areas are assessed&perceived safe by PUI&its stakeholders;PUI has the capacity to follow the full process of activities implementation;PUI increase its capacity of visibility by selecting activities where direct monitoring is possible.Risk:Limited facilitation by partners contributes to a delay in implementation-Mitigation measures:PUI maintains regular formal&informal contact with all partners at field&mission level;PUI continuously advocate to further expend the presence of staff in the field.Risk:Variations in exchange rate negatively impact local procurement-Mitigation measures:PUI times procurements in line with overall exchange rate trends, and will delay/re-schedule purchases as needed; exchange rate terms are also elaborated in contracts with suppliers.Risk:Independent access to beneficiaries is restricted during monitoring stages-Mitigation measures:PUI relies on standard methodologies&data management systems to confirm implementation&continuously monitor progress.Risk:Beneficiaries awarded for the SBPs are unable to obtain necessary authorisations to establish businesses.Mitigation measures:This process will be launched prior to the procurement of productive assets.PUI will connect beneficiaries to local community centres in case of additional support needed.
