Project Summary

The Project which is containing response of shelter and NFIs distribution in kind is targeting 750hhs (500 Female), ( 250 Male) and ( 330 Children), in the three districts of Marka, Qoryooley and Wanla Weyn of Lower Shabelle region, the population targeted are IDPs of conflict, droughts and floods, in addition who lost their shelters and NFIs.
The response will majorly focus on the activities of Selection and registration of beneficiaries, broken down by gender and age for the NFI beneficiaries, Procurement / Distribution of in kind NFIs kits for the beneficiaries. Selection and registration of beneficiaries, broken down by gender and age for the Shelter beneficiaries. Advocate for / Process / Acquire land tenure documentation as to get land for the project, in line with the community elders and local authority. Procurement / Distribution of in-kind Emergency Shelter kits to the beneficiaries. Conduct Settlement (re)planning trainings. Distribute settlement planning tools. Conduct settlement planning activities. Conduct Infrastructure mapping exercises. Drafting mid-term and final reports.
AYUUB expects the Project response will meet with the beneficiaries needs and save lives of the targeted group as planned, the organization by applying all experiences gained from the previous projects, the project will achieve all set goal and objectives respectively.

Needs

Generally, People in Somalia are facing the compounded impacts of a multi-layered and protracted humanitarian crisis: widespread conflict and inter-clan violence as well as climatic shocks (draughts, floods, cyclones) has let to chronic and high levels of vulnerability, especially of 2.6 million internally displaced people. However, since famine alert in January 2017, Somalia has consequently had the wettest season in recent years, boosting the harvest of cereals to the highest levels since 2010. The projection of an above average rainfall for the coming Deyr season is likely to result in further food security improvements. The FSNAU Early Warning – Early Action Dashboard indicates that number of indicators in alarm phase are back to the level of 2015 and 2016. Evidence from the JMCNA 2018 data that was collected in 58 districts in Somalia show that 80% of the IDPs live in makeshift shelters, temporary emergency shelters, tents, or under trees; 35% reported damage to their shelters in the last three months; 70% reported that the shelters they live in have no internal separation; Lower Shabelle is one of most vulnerable areas with high movement of IDPs for conflicts, drought and floods, in Multi-Sectoral Assessment Report for AYUUB , noted that over 9960 households IDPs are living in unprotected shelter in Marka, Qoryooley and Wanla Weyn, for Qoryooley, more than 8000 households ( 52.000 individuals) washed away their shelters and NFIs by floods and destroyed their crops, Marka: 1420hhs (9,295 individuals) of the recent conflict between SNA liberating the town and AS of that ruling it for years in August where Wanla Weyn hosts 540hhs ( 3510 individuals) of a protracted people of drought affected populatiom, children, women and elderly people are the most vulnerable groups, living under a critical shelter condition by establishing traditional cottages of clothes and sticks, lacking basic shelter, exposing to direct sunlight, rains, and cold.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The main risk can be identified are thefts of shelters and NFIs after distribution, fires can brake out in constructed shelters, WASH outbreaks/ AWD outbreaks if the sanitation and hygiene are not properly managed. this can be mitigated through community mobilization and establishing committee and enhancing internal communal communicating channels and feedback mechanisms in the IDPs camps, Settlement planning and layout, training the community on leaving appropriate spaces in the settlements, safety from fire brake outs, community mobilization on improving the sanitation and hygiene in camps and as well the government role in the security of the overall areas is also in place.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

There Is delivering Shelter and NFIs to the target group, the main risks the beneficiaries can face is improper selection if not applied a proper selection criteria, AYUUB in applying its selection criteria served in the previous projects, will employ its tool to capture the right benefices, beside this AYUUB team will be directly involve to make sure the selection and overall project service respects the gander balance in respective with the project criteria selection.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The area where the project is planned to deliver the services is full control of SFG, there is no other actor who can create armed clash, there can be small intra- clashes in SNA forces, but this will not be target to the beneficiaries of the project and AYUUB in applying its Risk assessment and imitation policy will do mobilization to both beneficiaries and project staff in the respective area when need calls.
