Project Summary

In line with the strategic recommendations of the UN InterAgency SEA Working Group in Burkina Faso, Viamo Burkina will set up a channel for feedback and complaints related to SEA via the dedicated Affected Communities Chapter on the 3-2-1 service – an interactive voice response hotline containing essential information in French and local languages on Health, Livelihoods, Education, Weather etc.
The 3-2-1 service is available 24h/day and can be reached for free (4 free calls per month, 25 FCFA per call after the free calls) by Orange-Burkina subscribers (11 M) by calling the shortcode 321 via a (simple) mobile phone. Once on the service, the caller can navigate between theme and topic menus by using his phone keypad, listen to messages and/or respond to questions. The service has existed in Burkina since May 2017 and receives (November 2022) around 650.000 calls per month.
The Affected Communities Chapter on 3-2-1 is oriented towards refugees, internally displaced persons and host communities in Burkina Faso and contains messages on GBV, Child Protection, PSEA, Documentation, Security, Social Cohesion and Livelihood Opportunities which have been developed and validated by the Community Engagement and Accountability Working Group (CEAWG), financed by UNHCR and published on the 3-2-1 service in April 2022.
Together with the Burkina Faso SEA inter-agency network working group, CEAWG and the CBCM/CFM Inter-agency Steering Committee led by UNHCR, Acted, WFP and Tin Tua , the SEA messages in 6 languages on the 3-2-1 Service will be rewritten and further elaborated. Listeners to messages on the Affected Communities Chapter and/or specifically on SEA will be asked if they have encountered situations of SEA. If so, the listener will be registered as a potential victim or witness of SEA and assigned a ticket. A dedicated Feedback Mechanism Manager will verify the registrations/tickets and refer them to the appropriate respondents in the concerned regional humanitarian hubs who will further handle each ticket. An alert per ticket will be sent to the respondents, who will indicate on the platform if and when they have accepted to start working on the ticket.
The feedback mechanism will be available 24h/day and will be promoted via regular broadcasts of the SEA messages by local radios in the high insecurity zones of Burkina Faso, via flyers in the refugee camps and the host communities, and via demonstration sessions for women and girls in the refugee camps. Local Community Complaint Committees (total around 340 committees) will be sensitized on the Feedback Mechanism and will an important role in channeling complaints and feedback through this channel (or any other collective feedback mechanism in place).
Statistical and demographic platform data on listenership and SEA tickets (number of listeners, number of tickets, number of listened messages; gender, age and geographic distribution etc.) will be used for analysis and improvement of the CFM, and quarterly mobile surveys will be executed in order to complement the user data with user satisfaction-related data.
The feedback mechanism has been piloted between July and November 2023 on and has generated 11.541 tickets during that period – of which 4.601 wishing to report an inappropriate behavior. Since the objective of the pilot was essentially to test whether users of the 3-2-1 service would submit feedback, not all tickets have been verified – but the accessibility of the channel has been demonstrated.
