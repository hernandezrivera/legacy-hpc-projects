Project Summary

This project is designed to respond to existing food security situation due to combined effects of the prevailing conflicts, recurrent climatic shocks, impacts of COVID-19, and increasing poverty. The Somalia HNO 2022, shows that a total of 7.7 million people require humanitarian assistance, and out of those 3.5 Million people need food security assistance. In addition according to the FSNAU-FEWSNET 2021 Post Gu Technical Release, approximately 3.5 million people in Somalia face acute food insecurity Crisis, and approximately 1.2 million children are likely to be acutely malnourished. The report showed an estimated 2.9 million Internally Displaced Persons across Somalia are poor with limited livelihood assets. Also, the agropastoral communities have faced erratic rainfall and conflicts hence substantial crop losses and low income. In riverine communities, they have faced seasonal floods which have destroyed their farms and displaced people, leading to crop losses and loss of income too. AWI aims at preventing further deterioration of the affected population and offer timely and appropriate assistance. AWI will offer interventions that meet the immediate needs and build resilience by starting with food Aid (Rice, Sugar, oil, and Pulses) to vulnerable households through vouchers in IDP camps for two months.The beneficiaries will comprise pregnant and lactating mothers, children under five with malnutrition, elderly and people with disability. Food vouchers will be distributed on a monthly basis. The project will identify and select local vendors with a capacity to supply the food items in each location. Security measures to safeguard both the beneficiaries and vendors will be put in place (i.e. beneficiary identification must match on list provided to vendor) and discussed with both beneficiaries and vendors. We will also seek to restore acceptable household food consumption and complement the household income of the affected households. Unconditional cash will be given to extremely vulnerable households (women-headed households, elderly, sick, disabled) of 120 USD a month for two months. Cash assistance has been shown to assist vulnerable households as well as have a positive impact on household wellbeing. Cash assistance can reduce poverty and vulnerability by enabling people to have food access as well as mitigate the effects of COVID 19 which has really affected the economy and most people have been struggling to make ends meet. Cash relief is effective form of support to people who are struggling and don’t have any other means of support during times of crisis and it can enable them to rebuild their lives. Due to the lack of proper infrastructures and poor maintenance, a reflection workshop with the irrigation committees will be conducted to work out modalities of restoration and maintenance of these infrastructures. Through cash for work activities, there will be the creation of temporary jobs for men and women just before the rainy seasons,providing a 5USD daily rate to the laborers for two months which will increase their income. The infrastructures will contribute to the provision of water and also facilitate the transaction of businesses through feeder roads. The food assistance and cash for work will allow beneficiaries to free up some of their meager income and establish a quick economic boost which acts as a supplement to their household income helping them to afford appropriate food and establish an income for further investment in their livelihoods while reducing the level of vulnerability. AWI will provide households with agricultural inputs before Deyr and GU rains and train farmers on improved farming techniques to give them knowledge and skills on how to improve crop production. The target beneficiaries are drawn from both the agropastoral and riverine communities who have been affected by the prevailing circumstances and are in need of assistance to prevent further deterioration of their livelihood situation.

1. - Projects activities

AWI will directly target 2,112 households (12,672 people) in need including IDPs. The following activities will be the core milestones in the project. 1. Provide food vouchers (40kg Rice, 6kg Pulses, 5kg Sugar, and 4 lts Oil) to 600 Households (3,600 beneficiaries) in Balcad and Jowhar IDPS with over 50% going to women-headed households. To help them meet the immediate need 2. Unconditional cash to 300HH extremely vulnerable households that are not able to work (women-headed households, elderly, sick, disabled) to IDPs in Balcad and Jowhar 120 USD a month for two months .3. Provision and distribution of farming inputs, fertilizer, crop, legume, and vegetable seeds providing 20 kgs per household and land preparation of 1 hectare per farmer to 720 riverine HH (4,320 beneficiaries) in Middle Shabelle (Jowhar and Balcad). 200 Selected farmers will be trained on modern farming techniques and safe food storage and they will in turn train people in their respective villages. 4. Creation of temporary job opportunities for 292 Households (1,752 beneficiaries) in Balcad and Jowhar through rehabilitation of irrigation schemes (canals and water catchments) and feeder roads rehabilitation.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

As for AWI, the key component of interventions is to do no harm. This applies to both the beneficiaries as well as the personnel of AWI. One of the key advice is to reduce contact between the AWI staff and the beneficiaries and also between the beneficiaries themselves especially this time of Pandemic. Through cash and voucher system it will reduce gatherings and any other inconveniences. Also, the cash can be transferred directly through the mobile without the need for people to have close contact. By doing this AWI will be complying with the government regulations and at the same time ensuring people are safe by avoiding large gatherings where the virus can easily spread. Also with this method is more effective and efficient in reaching all who need support.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

The project design will ensure that market assessments are conducted before the interventions as well as monitor market prices in case of any changes. The areas that the interventions are taking place have efficient markets for commodities as well as cash transfer systems.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

There are many causes that cause displacements among communities, it might be conflicts, violence, violations of human rights or even disaster. In all these causes, one of the affected things in their lives is lack of necessities such as food, shelter and water and most lose their normal livelihoods and sources of income. Displacements affect individuals in different ways and most of them are marginalized within communities and are often overlooked when assessing needs of the community or even in programme planning. In ensuring these marginalized groups are included AWI will ensure that we work with all the relevant stakeholders to prevent excluding affected groups. In assessing the needs and programme planning AWI will ensure the most affected in the community that’s is IDPS, Returnees and even the vulnerable host communities are targeted as they are most affected and discriminated. Conflicts and displacements affect individuals in different ways depending on their gender and age. Children and older persons are often marginalized and excluded from the formal decision-making processes. Also, during the displacements many women and children are left as the heads of households and this forces them to assume different roles and responsibilities. These groups enjoy less social, economic and political power due to the inequalities within the communities and as a result their particular needs are often ignored. Children, in particular when separated from their families, are often at risk of neglect, abuse or exploitation, forced recruitment into armed forces or armed groups, child labor, deprivation of access to basic needs and other violations of their rights. Since some of them have the responsibilities of taking for their young siblings its quite hard for them as they lack the resources and support of managing a household. Sexual violence and Exploitation among women and children is always a common feature as a result of conflicts and displacements too. To counteract these issues AWI will ensure when collecting our data, we ensure inclusivity of all in the community and even disaggregate by age and sex. Also, we will enhance security at the project sites by employing security staff/ guards while ensuring the centrality of distribution points to limit the long distance covered by women and girls that expose them to risks of GBV. AWI will ensure all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhere to it as well as put in place SEA reporting mechanisms and conduct awareness on its utilization. AWI will employ participatory approach in all the programme stages to incorporate the capacities of the different age groups and gender. During community engagements and even forming committee’s different age groups and gender will participate equally. Armed conflicts and violence have quite devastating effects on the lives of people as well as access to essential basic services is disrupted. In our programming and delivering our services to the communities we will ensure adequate representation of the marginalized groups in the community. AWI will also ensure that the interventions carried out will be addressing the needs of the different groups in the target area. So, in all our interventions we will be keen to ensure that they promote and respect human rights and that they will not result to any discrimination, abuse, neglect or violence. AWI will establish beneficiaries complaints and feedback mechanisms. To avoid major security problems, it is in our plan to work with the local administrations, leaders from the local community and security agencies.
