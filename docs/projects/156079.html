Project Summary

Ana Kader project is one of The Trust programs, targeting children in elementary and junior high schools, whether displaced children, returnees, host community, or refugees in order to enhance the capacities and skills of the child and develop the abilities of adults (school staff and parents) by activating the role of the school in the life of the child as well as the integration of children with each other, and the acceptance of displaced children by children from the same area.
In order to achieve this, the project will work on empowering the school community in all its components, starting from the child, working with educational staff and reaching the parents in an accumulated process, where the project will enshrine certain activities such as:
- capacity building for Teachers.
- Events implementation
- Non-classroom activities
- Initiatives
In conclusion to all mentioned above, a coronation of the children achievements takes place to enhance their feeling of accomplishment, thus to assure the importance of what they have achieved.

Needs

Learning is a child’s basic right, regardless of the circumstances the country is facing. It’s an obligation to maintain the flow of education not only because it is a basic need, but also for it being the only guarantee for the future of the country throughout offering the correct and needed support to further continue and develop education in its correct forms and methodology so that every child is educated. Since the start of the Syrian crisis in 2011, Syria witnessed many challenges that negatively impacted the humanitarian and security situation in Syria. Most importantly, the widespread challenges related to education, due to the increase in educational costs, depletion of savings, destruction of school buildings, limited access to learning and life skills, low quality educational services, and high numbers of dropout children with great possibility of exposure to risks, which in turn led to the inability to provide its educational services. According to HNO 2019, the number of children, teachers, and personnel staff in need for educational support and capacity building varies between the targeted governorates. For instance, there are 494,032 in Damascus, 779,930 in Rural Damascus, 90,630 in As-Sweida, 336,372 in Lattakia, 234,643 in Tartous, and 1,358,478 in Aleppo. For that reason, Syria Trust responded to these obstacles through a series of open programs that have worked to inflict real positive and long-term change in the field of learning and developing life skills.

Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented in the Syrian governorates (Damascus, Rural Damascus, Sweida, Aleppo, Lattakia and Tartous during school year through several activities, initiatives and events as follows on a different scales :- On scale of child empowerment: non-classroom activities are offered during the school year as part of a cumulative process of information, skills and values, in addition to allowing children to plan and implement number of initiatives, which are conducted by all children in the same class at the end of each school year. In addition, these initiatives help to empower the child at the end of his journey during ninth grade, to be an explorer of himself and his surroundings, more confident, and able to make a difference in his personality and the world around him.The project also offers some summer activities at school to connect the child more to his school and to enhance the number of skills, values, and information he needs. And also, to work on events at the beginning of the school year to provide entertainment and unload his\her energies. furthermore focusing on welcoming new children along with accepting their presence and providing support and assistance to each other.- On scale of educational staff and parents: conducting a number of training workshops (skills for dealing with children, training of trainers (TOT)....etc), that contribute to the process of developing their skills to serve the educational process and activate their role and participation in improving the work of the school and implementing the activities of the project.This process is culminated through the formation of a committee in each school composed of teachers and parents working on the capacity building of its members in a number of training workshops and regular meetings in order to activate the committee’s role in improving the work of the school and to ensure that during the empowerment process, enhancing the participation in the committee members' proposals and developing their perceptions to constantly improve schools work. The second step is to plan and implement the proposals which the committee members have developed. The committee’s role is not limited only on that, but also it continues working during the project’s presence and after finishing the project’s execution at the school.last but not least step is to create Ana Kader Club that allows the child to express him\herself and to do his\her role in the most appropriate way in order to develop his\her skills and abilities. The activities will be summarized in:-Capacity building for Teachers.- Events: implementation of number of events through both occasions of family & teacher days which will target a specified group of beneficiaries, along with recreational activities planned by both children and teachers.- Non-classroom activities: performed during school year. these activities are characterized in a cumulative journey of information, skills and morals; in addition to giving the children the opportunity to plan and enforce number of initiatives.- Initiatives: as a result of children going through the events and non-classroom activities, they will be capable of identifying the challenges they face whether in or outside the school in order to come up with initiatives which shape the solutions needed to complete his\her full journey of Ana Kader.In conclusion to all mentioned above, a coronation of the children achievements takes place to enhance their feeling of accomplishment, thus to assure the importance of what they have achieved.

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1 : Community based local inequality (tension between IDPs, Returnees, host communities). Inter or intra-community tension due to service delivery locations or types of services offered if one community is served and a neighboring community not served/cannot access or if information offered is not consistent between communities as there may be perceptions of discrimination or favoritism. M1: conducting activities that are designed to mitigate and reduce the tension between served communities implemented by trained staff. Risk 2 : exposure to physical insecurity\ violence; This includes risks to safety and disruption of the dynamics or encouragement of negative behaviors in schools and neighboring communities (e.g. children dropping out from school to support family members).M2: Ensure safety measures is conducted to ensure safe access (route and location) by boys, girls, women and men, elderly and populations. along with maintaining constant coordination with concerned authorities to ensure that the activities are conducted in safe environment (schools). Risk 3: exposure to abuse & exploitation (deprivation of dignity).M3: raising community awareness through focus group discussions FGDs & awareness sessions. Risk 4: exclusion (access, discrimination...etc) M4: Ana Kader project orientation for the community in order giving the opportunity for all community groups ( IDPs, Returnees, host communities) Risk 5: Protection risks of mixing different age groups in the same center/class.M 5: Ensure that Ana Kader activities are implemented taken into consideration the age group.
