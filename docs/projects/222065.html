Project Summary

The proposed project is for 6 Months response to provide food assistance and livelihood support to 54,240 (28,058 Males and 26,182 Females) food insecure population Kapoeta North County in Eastern Equatoria Counties during the lean season. The project intervention activities will contribute to famine prevention and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies of target beneficiaries in IPC 4 and 3. The project will also seek to enhance emergency food production and contribute to the reduction of dependency on food and agricultural inputs. Organization for Peoples’ Empowerment and Needs (OPEN) will adopt a mix of intervention approaches including but not limited to Assets Creation and Livelihoods (ACL), capacity building tailored on agronomic practices, and resilience building at the household level. The proposed project will also be implemented in synergy with other sectors implemented by OPEN in the target locations such as health, nutrition, WASH, gender and protection related programming to achieve Cluster set Objective 1: Provide food assistance to prevent famine and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies for vulnerable populations (in IPC 4 and 5), Objective 2: Enhance and sustain emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop livelihood inputs, fishing and livestock and Objective 3: Reduce dependency on food and agricultural inputs to support and strengthen households’ ability to absorb & adapt to shocks & contextual changes to address the needs in these three selected Counties in Eastern Equatoria State.

1. Needs

Eastern Equatoria State population in greater Kapoeta Counties are mostly pastoralist communities and the trends of movement is seasonality drives such as cattle-related conflict, insecurity, weather-related disruptions to infrastructure and the functioning of markets, high food prices and highway robberies/ambushes thus these incidents are expected to limit household movement and access to food and income sources resulting to food insecurity. During the lean season projection period, Kapoeta North is priority 3 county classified in Emergency (Phase 4) acute food insecurity because of high staple food prices linked to limited supplies and low production, coupled with insufficient income from crop sales and other opportunities according to IPC Acute Food Insecurity and Malnutrition Analysis September 2024 - July 2025 Published On 18 November, 2024 with projection is estimated to be 165,000 people (45% of the population) As part of the 2025 HRP, Organization for Peoples’ Empowerment and Needs (OPEN) aims to reach the most vulnerable affected IDPs and host populations in Eastern Equatoria State with Assets creation and Livelihoods (ACL) activities to support and strengthen households’ ability to absorb shocks. The food security situation in the targeted counties is very critical in terms of food consumption, dietary diversification, reduced coping strategies, level of hunger and livelihood coping strategies. The IDPs, returnees, and host communities are particularly vulnerable and many of them switched to negative coping strategies including both selling their assets and decreasing the number of meals per day per person. Organization for Peoples’ Empowerment and Needs in partnership with Diakonie has been implementing projects in the target locations but huge food gaps still exist and the communities need immediate support. The project actions were done in a proper manner and more so where community cohesion and mobilization, empowerment of women and youth development is a priority in addressing tensions between IDPs and host communities that influence IDPs-host community relations, limiting the local protection space and opportunities for interdependence. Such tension results from the use of land, natural resources, firewood collection, access to medical, educational and infrastructure facilities, family disputes, the overstretched absorption capacity of the conflict-affected communities. Therefore, Organization for Peoples’ Empowerment and Needs (OPEN) aligned strategies to achieve Cluster set Objective 1: Provide food assistance to prevent famine and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies for vulnerable populations (in IPC 4 and 5), Objective 2: Enhance and sustain emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop livelihood inputs, fishing and livestock and Objective 3: Reduce dependency on food and agricultural inputs to support and strengthen households’ ability to absorb & adapt to shocks & contextual changes to address the needs in these three selected Counties in Eastern Equatoria State. Reference Sources. South Sudan: Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2024 (Issued November 2023) | OCHA (unocha.org) South Sudan | IPC - Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (ipcinfo.org).

2. Project activities

Project activities include: Providing food assistance to 54,240 population in Kapoeta North County of Eastern Equatoria State targeting (28,058 Males and 26182 Females) to prevent famine and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies for vulnerable populations) through Assets Creation and Livelihoods (ACL) and quick maturing vegetable seeds. Support emergency food & livelihood, fishing kits/vegetable kits, rapid response kits (fish kits/cow pea & three varieties of fast maturing vegetable seeds). Livestock treatment and vaccination, livestock diseases surveillance, community Animal health workers training and inputs support, cold chain establishment and maintenance, prevention & treatment especially zoonoses eg RVF, Anthrax) and provision of timely food security information to the targeted population. support to seed fairs, seed security, & multiplication strengthening/ support market functionality eg. Assist traders for resupply markets/local procurement for FAO ITFs and Aggregation of produce for WFP assistance, Support value chain addition through agriculture, fishery and livestock products/by-products preservation and marketing, Training and capacity building both adaptive and absorptive on farm production (farmers/agro-pastoral field schools, demonstration plots, income generating skills ( business skills) vocational training, Multi-sectoral models support, livelihoods, nutrition-sensitive food security, hygiene and sanitation, Reducing pest and crop diseases eg FAW & DL surveillance, detection, prevention and eradication, diversification and restoration of livelihoods; small grant for petty trade, village saving & loan schemes, Use of local biomass briquettes & fuel-efficient stoves.
