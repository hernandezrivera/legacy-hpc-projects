Project Summary

The Syrian population faces critical food and economic security challenges, with 65% in need of food or livelihood support. The economic decline, reliance on negative coping mechanisms, and lack of basic services contribute to vulnerability. NRC underscores the urgency of scaling up food and agriculture assistance to prevent an increase in extreme poverty.
The Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC) outlines a multifaceted approach to contribute to HRP Objective 3 by integrating responses with Multi-purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) and early recovery and livelihoods assistance. This approach aims to create synergies that enhance the effectiveness of interventions and support sustainable solutions. Key components of NRC's strategy include emergency response, livelihood support, and food security and agriculture interventions.
Emergency support to Crisis-Affected Individuals
NRC envisions a two-fold strategy involving short-term food assistance and MPCA for the most vulnerable. The organization emphasizes unconditional cash where functional markets permit, with the goal of reducing negative coping strategies. To enhance the impact of assistance, NRC plans to accompany interventions with information campaigns promoting dietary diversity.
Livelihood Support and Economic Recovery:
NRC's economic recovery initiatives cover diverse activities such as small business grants, conditional cash, Cash for Work (CFW), and vocational training. The organization seeks to empower individuals for long-term self-employment and improve access to short-term employment. Additionally, infrastructure rehabilitation, such as grain silos and water/irrigation systems, is considered to enhance community resilience.
Food Security and Agriculture Interventions:
The food and agriculture sector focuses on restoring micro-small businesses, supporting agriculture production, and enhancing economic resilience. NRC plans to provide inputs, grants, and infrastructure rehabilitation, addressing the needs of vulnerable households. Tailored support interventions are designed for specific vulnerable categories, including female-headed households and persons with disabilities.
Target Group: NRC prioritizes areas and communities based on vulnerabilities and needs, aligning with the HNO 2024 categorization. Specific interventions include the distribution of agricultural inputs, vocational and skill development, and rehabilitation of critical infrastructure. The organization emphasizes a gender, age, and disability inclusion lens throughout its activities, with a focus on tailored livelihood packages for vulnerable categories.
Implementation: NRC's primary focus in 2024 is the revival of economic and livelihood activities, agriculture, and food security. The organization adopts a holistic approach to building recovery and resilience capacities. Implementing a variety of interventions, NRC aims to support vulnerable crisis-affected populations, combining consumption support with livelihood assistance through graduation programming.
NRC directly implements food security and agriculture programming. The organization emphasizes a comprehensive approach, conducting thorough needs and vulnerability assessments for targeted areas and community selection. Monitoring and evaluation plans include post-service monitoring, after-action reviews, and tracking output/outcome indicators to inform program adjustments and ensure effectiveness.
