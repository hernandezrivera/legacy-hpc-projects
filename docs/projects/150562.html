Project Summary

Comprehensive Protection Response through provision of psycho-social and mental health services for conflict affected population in the Gaza strip" project aims to contribute to alleviating the impact of conflict on the psychosocial and mental health wellbeing of affected women, men, boys and girls in the Gaza Strip. Aisha has already started working on providing services for the affected people from Great march of return .The project that Aisha already works on targets about 4,950 affected people of the march of great return , till this moment number of injured "or generally affected " people has increased based on the updated statics .There is a noticed need to work with those cases and provide them psycho-social services or any additional services.. Through working with affected groups The main outcomes of the project are: 1) enhanced access to quality psycho-social and mental health services to conflict affected women, men, boys and girls in the Gaza strip and 2) Targeted communities are able to provides rapid and quality response and enhance the copying mechanisms and resilience for the families of affected population in the Gaza strip. Aisha will work to achieve these outcomes through various activities starting from coordination with main stakeholder to outreach the conflict affected persons during the Great march of Return since 30th of March till his moment and networking with partner CBOs near conflict areas to host the project activities. Then, providing structured psycho-social and specialized mental health services and recreational activities for acutely conflict affected women, men, boys and girls, and detect the severely affected person in need for advanced psychological, mental health service to Aisha mobile clinics (5 clinics, North area mobile clinic, Gaza , Middle area clinic, Rafah and khanyouins clinic- each clinic has a psychotherapist and supervised by Aisha psychiatrist) while cases in need for additional services such as physical therapy, legal and medicine, assistive tools and health services will be referred to Aisha and offered these services whether inside Aisha or through networking with other service providers such as GCMHP and UHWC according to a signed MOU with them. The mobile clinics will operate within the internal and external referral system, where coordination will be carried out with the basic institutions in five areas in the Gaza Strip. The cases will be transferred to the five clinics through psychological support sessions. Aisha will provide psychosocial support and case study from the social and psychological aspects . Some cases may be in need additional services so we can refer those case for "Governmental Shelter , court ,Child protection network or health ministry . There will be a previous coordination with those sides in order to refer the cases and activate the external referral system .. On the last level, Aisha will develop the capacity of service providers who are engaged and works directly with the conflict affected persons on providing specialized psycho-social services, case management, detection and referral pathways.
On other hand, Aisha will apply the previously developed manual Hawwa Zahra developed according to IASC guidelines, that was revised and mainstreamed among the protection cluster and it sub- working group member Also, AISHA has already a referral mechanism in which all referrals and needed information are followed up and traced. It is worth mentioning that AISHA is already a part of the SOPs' manual managed by UNICEF and includes all referral pathways.

Needs

Since the beginning of "Great March of Returns " number of injured people among men, women and children is increasing more and more through the protests near the borders Till this moment as the statics from " Al Mezan Center For Human Rights" refers to more than 16686 people were injured . Aisha has already started to work with 4,950 cases through providing psycho-social support , individual counselling, integrated self-care ,awareness sessions and additional services of intervention. On other hand the allocation strategy stated that the violence experience at the fence- whether through being injured directly, witnessing an injury or death, or knowing someone injured or killed- can significantly impact the mental health and psychosocial well-being of a person both immediately as well as over the long term. MHPSS is one priority responses of the protection cluster in the HRP 2018-2020, on other hand, UN OCHA (2017) in its HNO presents a number of gender-based vulnerabilities, concerns and associated needs where gender dynamics in the Palestinian society are key in shaping the realities, opportunities and status of men, women, boys and girls, as well as in shaping their experiences of the post and during conflict, humanitarian situations and coping strategies. The serious need of psychosocial support for women and children is evident in such an abnormal situation of loss, distress, insecurity and violence. AISHA reached women "Victims of violence" who never have the chance to leave their houses before. On the other hand, gender dynamics in the Palestinian society are key in shaping the realities, opportunities and status of men, women, boys and girls, as well as in shaping their experiences of the post and during conflict, humanitarian situations and coping strategies. Furthermore, the lack of specialized and structured PSS services, social stigma toward the women approaching mental health services or centres, and the deteriorated economic situation and lack of quality and comprehensive services prevent women and children from seeking for mental Health or other fundamental services. The GBV Mobile Clinic of Aisha responds to the above-mentioned needs where it facilitates women’s access to psychological services, it offers home visits, family counselling, and transportation for women who attended the group sessions and the individual counselling. Thus, it is important that comprehensive services include psychosocial, legal, economic, and rehabilitation services through case management approach to women and their children as part of the humanitarian response. Through monitoring and evaluation two focus groups conducted by Aisha with both "affected people that Aisha already works with and provide psycho-social support to " and the other one with Aisha "psychologists and psychotherapists " The first focus group with target people addressed the real needs of those families for targeting additional number of affected people . Mothers of injured people addressed their problems and their relative problems where Aisha witnessed the increase of the needs. They asserted specific humanitarian needs under the current crisis in Gaza including psychosocial group counseling; individual counseling sessions, available protection measures, equal access to services, transportation fees to facilitate their access to medication and other services, and finally, financial support where most of them were the breadwinner for their families and now there is no income resource for food security or basic health services. Women of one of the groups in Rafah city were really happy that the finally have a space to speak out and address their problems. The second focus group with Aisha psychologists and psychotherapists addressed the problems that they has already noticed through their working "with those cases whether throughout the psycho-social support group sessions or throughout the individual sessions with those cases need individual interventions.

Activities

Activities of the project include :(1000) of conflict affected women " mothers and wives of injured persons) received structured group psycho-social support services via Hawwa Zahra manual (total of 66 group; 15 women/group where each group attend 10 sessions), (80) women referred to Aisha mobile clinics to receive specialized psycho-social services including consultation, individual counseling, mental health services and medications,(40) of these targeted women (who participated in structured group psycho-social support, and in need for in-depth intervention, received multi secretarial services: case management, rehabilitation, legal assistance and referral to health and protection services,(10) Recreational activities and open days organized for the targeted women,(300) of conflict affected children (150) girls of age 9-12 yrs, and 150 boys of age 9-12 yrs) received structured group psychosocial support services via Hawwa Zahra manual (total of 20 group; 15 children/group where each group attend 10 sessions),(3) Recreational activities for boys and girls,Well being, self-care, and integrated security workshops for 500 injured men (each workshop 20 men, total of 50 workshop each workshop 5 hours where each group attend 2 days workshops (using the Well being, self-care, and integrated security manual for men),1500 women and men (1000 women, 500 men) families of the injured persons raised their awareness on gender, stress management, loss, disability and violence, (total of 60 workshop, each workshop target 25 persons for three hours),300 of conflict affected adolescents boys (13-18) years received structured group psycho-social support services (total 15 adolescents each group),(20) affected children (10 girls, 10 boys) referred to Aisha mobile clinic to receive specialized psycho-social services including consultation, structured individual counseling, mental health services and received multi sectoral services: case management, rehabilitation, legal assistance and referral to health and protection services,(30) service providers who are engaged and work directly with conflict affected people in the Great March of Return build their capacities to provide specialized psycho-social services, case management, detection and referral pathways for acutely affected women and girls during and post crises (2 groups, each group attend 25 training hours),(5) mobile clinics of mental health established in CBOS in different areas of Gaza Strip to receive cases ,Provision of clinical supervision for AISHA staff working with conflict affected persons ,
