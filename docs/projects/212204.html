Project Summary

In 2024 Shafak’s WASH plan, intervention plan will focus on immediate emergency life-saving WASH
interventions and support the resilience of existing WASH systems (water supply and
sewer networks, wastewater treatment, solid waste management) and WASH in
institutions, such as schools and healthcare facilities, ensuring coordinated and cohesive
response and provide comprehensive WASH services for affected people in northwest Syria who living in IDP sites and host communities.
The intervention will focus on providing emergency WASH services in the short term and
a sustainable long-term service to the affected people in Aleppo and Idelb governorates.
Shafak, through this intervention, will provide emergency WASH services for 650,000 individuals living in camps and communities. Shafak will provide a full package.
WASH emergency services to provide rapid responses and strengthen the community resilience to adapt to living conditions in the affected communities
The intervention plan will ensure safe access, availability to basic WASH services, quality assurance based on WASH cluster guidelines, and Cholera and COVID-19 mitigation measures
for the planned activities. The intervention will comprise the following components:
a) Rehabilitation/upgrade of 20 water pumping stations, including equipping the stations with electrical and mechanical equipment and installing renewable energy systems to reduce the running costs, benefiting more than 200,000 Individuals. In addition, support Water
Association Units (WAUs) provide monthly running costs for the stations (fuel, electricity subscription, chlorine, engine oil, and worker incentives) and promote a cost recovery system. As
well as train the local communities and apply water safety plans.
b) Provision of WASH emergency services for 450,000 individuals living in camps, including water trucking and water quality monitoring, water containers, construction/repair of emergency latrines, providing cleaning materials for latrines and latrine cleaning, desludging of septic tanks, and solid waste management.
c) Rehabilitation of sewage networks to connect the remote sites, camps, and public facilities(schools and health centers) and replacing the damaged sewer lines benefiting 150,000 Individuals.
d) Rehabilitation of water networks to connect the remote sites, camps, and public facilities (schools and health centers) and replacing the damaged water lines benefiting 150,000 Individuals.
e) Supporting the local operators and municipalities to continue the garbage collection and disposal of the waste from 20 communities (around 150,000 individuals), schools, health centers, and surrounding IDPs sites. This support will cover providing fuel for garbage vehicles, maintaining/repairing the broken ones, and supporting
workers’ incentives.
f) Conducting hygiene promotion sessions to improve hygiene practices and behaviors for 50,000 individuals.
g) 22,000 hygiene kits will be distributed to the IDPs and host communities who can’t secure hygiene materials.
h) provision of WASH services to 5 education centers and 5 health facilities. Shafak will ensure support the innovative solutions as possible in its proposed interventions for dealing with water drought issues by supporting solar-powered
systems, wastewater treatment. In addition to applying environmental improvement projects through developing solid waste management treatment and elevating the bad impacts of neglecting SWM support.
As for 2025, according to Shafak's strategy and fund availability, it is expected to implement the same activities with the same size and budget.
