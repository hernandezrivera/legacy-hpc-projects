Project Summary

The project aims at providing quality and timely life-saving nutrition services for the targeted boys and girls aged 6-59 months and Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) through systematic identification and treatment of severe acute and moderate malnutrition caseloads as well as enhancing preventive nutrition services for the vulnerable population particularly the marginalized PLWs and children (boys and girls) aged 6-59 months including those living with disabilities in the project targeted locations in Banadir, Hiran , Middle Shabelle Regions.
Worsening malnutrition caseloads in targeted locations is quite alarming with serious GAM rates above 15% threshold. The situation is attributed to compounding impacts of recurrent climatic shocks, diseases outbreaks, insecurity, conflict and political tensions that continue to affect vulnerable communities that are living precariously under diminishing coping mechanism. The mentioned plight is compromised further by current weather forecast that indicate likelihood of below normal 2021 Deyr rains that will significantly worsen food and nutrition outcomes till mid 2022 (FSNAU-FEWS NET 2021 Post Gu Technical Release of September 9, 2021). The situation is further exacerbated by limited and poorly distributed health and nutrition services that are clustered in urban areas, poor breast feeding patterns among lactating mothers, poor diet diversity and complimentary feeding practices among boys and girls aged 6-59 months as well as limited access to safe water and poor hygiene conditions and practices owing to frequent cases of water borne diseases that worsen already precarious malnutrition status among boys and girls and PLWs in the project targeted locations.
The project targets a total of 13486 children (5168 boys and 5168girls) aged between 6-59 months and 3150 Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) including those living with disabilities that bear the brunt of discrimination and isolation and neglect from assistance by enabling for their protection and further engaging them directly to enable and strengthen their participation and inclusion in the project planning process as well as throughout the entire project implementation stages including monitoring hence covering their special needs by the project.

1. - Projects activities

1. Screening of 13486 children (5168 boys and 5168girls) aged 6-59 months and 3150 PLWs through MUAC and Weight - height measures and treating them through OTP/SFP program. Treated OTP caseloads are referred to SFP while untreated OTP are referred to SC programs while treated SFP caseloads are discharged in the project targeted locations in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.2. Prevention and management of common diseases: anemia, diarrhea, malaria etc through Vitamin A supplementation, deworming, multiple micronutrients, supplements, iron/folate supplements to the targeted 13476 children (5160 boys and 5160 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 3156 PLWs at the project targeted locations in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.3. Promotion of optimum IYCF with strong emphasis to exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding and diet diversity targeting 3150 PLWs in project locations in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.4. Dissemination of hygiene promotion at the nutrition sites in the project targeted locations through key promotion messages and practices to the targeted 3150 Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.5. Training of 60 OTP/SFP staff (30 men and 30 women) in case detection through MUAC measurements and weight measures treatment and referral protocols at the project targeted locations in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.6. Training of 120 Community health workers (60 female and 60 male) as well as volunteers on nutrition education to enhance their knowledge in Malnutrition to mitigate increasing malnutrition caseloads among children aged 6-59 months and PLWs at the project targeted locations in in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.7. Conducting 12 Community education sessions for 120 community nutrition volunteers (60 female and 60 male) to increase awareness on malnutrition at the project targeted locations in in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Banadir Regions.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Ensuring inclusion of minority groups and the targeted vulnerable PLWs, men, boys and girls as well as the elderly including IDPs and non IDPs such as minority clans and people living with disabilities in the consultations on project needs identification and ensuring they are part of key decision made regarding the project such that their needs are put into consideration in to the project and they duly benefit from the project. In addition, involving them in them in regular project monitoring and ensuring that their views and concerns are put into consideration for key decision during the project implementation period2. Ensuring protection of PLWs from GBV cases by working in close coordination with protection partners for timely reporting of the incidences and appropriate responses in addition to enhancing awareness on GBV services by the protection partners. Furthermore adhering to PSEA policy and ensuring PLWs are fully aware of the project and their rightful entitlements in terms of nutrition treatment to mitigate sexual exploitation and abuse during the project. In addition, ensuring that complaints and feedback mechanisms are in place such as suggestion boxes and toll free hotlines for easy access and use by the targeted beneficiaries.
