1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project would like to continue providing life-saving food assistance to internally displaced people (IDPs) who are fleeing from violence due to radical insurgency in Cabo Delgado. We aim to reach IDPs living in camps and those in host communities who have fled their homes leaving everything behind and who are in desperate need of food assistance. The project aims to supply a one month ration of food which would contain: 50 kg of rice***, 14kg of pulses, 8 liters of oil, 1K salt, and 1 bar of soap. The work will be done in Metuge and Pemba***in the case that rice is not available the project will provide flour

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Provide regular food assistance to food-insecure vulnerable households maintaining the WFP nutritional recommendations.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The program will hire unarmed guards to assist in the safe distribution of food. Local government officials and representatives are invited to participate in the distribution of food given they have rapport and respect with the local community.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Iris Global has been working in coordination with the local government and the FSC to coordinate and design our efforts with the most accurate statistics and understanding the local needs. The needs are taken into consideration and also address livelihoods and sanitation through other projects.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

There is no discrimination of food assistance on the basis of disability. There are workers on the ground who are prepared to assist the disabled in receiving and carrying the food.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project is mindful of protecting the environment and cleaning up all waste at our distributions. Aside from regular carbon emissions from our truck used to transport the food, there are no concerns beyond that. The workers are instructed to use latrines and to properly dispose of trash in designated trash receptacles.
