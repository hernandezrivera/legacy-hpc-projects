Project Summary

MICHAFOUND aims at reducing the problem of malnutrition within SWR of Cameroon.
Targeted population within 0-50+ all age groups and sex. We are looking at a total of 1000 babies (male & female), boys and girls 500 and adults 500. These are both non IDPs and IDPs. This project shall provide seminars on malnutrition for youths, adults and quality supplements for all age groups and baby food such as vitamin, calciuim,, cereals as examples. For us to meet up above target and plan, our seminar shall provide measure on how to prevent malnutrition. Below shall be following points to note; dietary changes, such as eating foods high in energy and nutrients, support for families to manage underlying factors affecting the child's nutritional intake, treatment for any underlying medical conditions causing their malnutrition, vitamin and mineral supplements, high energy and protein nutritional supplements – if the other treatments aren't enough on their own. We shall attribute an average budget to spend per family while we shall equally give a breakdown on expenses related to seminars. Project execution will be done chronologically that is from one location to another for proper monitoring. Various towns/cities involve are Kumba, Ekondo titi and Buea.
Feedback will be collected in-house (proposed venue) through short paper surveys for general reporting.
This shall reduce hunger, rebuild livelihood and reduce death rate. Total budget is at 68,686USD beginning 01/01/2020 – 31/12/2020.

Besoins / Needs

The findings from emergency food security assessment (EFSA) that WFP and partners conducted showed a proxy GAM of 4.4% and 5.6% for the NW and SW respectively. However, access constraints hindered the survey and there is possibility of higher nutritional vulnerability in hard-to-reach areas. Compounded with the aggravating factors including food insecurity, very limited access to health services and poor water and sanitation, the nutrition situation is expected to deteriorate if immediate nutrition response is not provided. Furthermore, survey results from WFP-led mobile vulnerability analysis and mapping suggest that the proportion of children who receive the minimum acceptable diet is as low as 18.2% and 6.9% in the NW and SW regions respectively. This implies that majority of the children do not have access to nutritious food, thus are not meeting their nutrient need and consequently compounding growth. With these findings, there is urgent need to scale up the response in line with the cluster prioritiesIn an attempt to reduce the rate of malnutrition at the level of food insecurity, poor feeding practices and both child and adult care i.e. male and female, we propose to give out cereals (food), vitamin to babies and also calcium and vitamin to adults as well as creating seminars to sensitize women/caregivers on the procedures of preventing malnutrition. By so doing we shall facilitate the reduction of this ongoing problem.
