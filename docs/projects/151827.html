Project Summary

The project will support and improve the living condition of vulnerable people including child and female headed household, women, people living with disability and caregivers in host communities and internally displace persons through inclusive recovery response in the highly populated two LGAs of Borno State (Mafa and Konduga) due to their severity ranking in North East Nigeria as a result of the Arm Conflict by Boko Haram.

Needs

Konduga and Mafa LGA In North East Nigeria of in Borno State, have suffered tremendous severe negative impact of the arm conflict in these communities which has experienced enormous lost of lives and destroyed physical, social and government buildings, which has led to a drastic reduction in basic Education services and livelihood opportunities. Already marginalized groups have become even more vulnerable due the damages to Government and Private Establishments including schools, markets and primary health care centers as a result of the arm conflict by the Boko Harm group which is adding problem to the already experienced under-development in these LGAs. The Early Recovery Severity Map indicates that there is high severe need in Konduga and Mafa LGA, among the people affected, it is stated that over 80 per cent were already living below the poverty line (NBS 2005). Nearly 2000 homes and Government infrastructure were damaged or destroyed and 75,000 people were displaced in these two LGAs (NEMA 2016 report). The livelihood of the communities workers has been affected which were classified as vulnerable and underdeveloped before the insurgency.In the aftermath return of peace, the efforts of the Government of Nigeria, supported by the humanitarian community, have by and large succeeded in preventing a full blown humanitarian crisis. The Borno State government launched the Ministry of Reconstruction, Rehabilitation and Reintegration without rapid provision of early recovery activities addressing immediate support to restores livelihoods in the form of short term employment creation, provision of market based skills including vocational training and micro and small enterprise for youth and women derived from local community base opportunities. In addition, the capacity building support to the Local government authority will be essential to restore essential basic services and ensure that sufficient conflict management expertise is available to enable an effective rights-based approach to reducing risks to people living in unsafe areas and mitigating the impact of future insurgency. As relief activities start to scale back to the areas, the focus must now be on providing the most vulnerable with short-term support and rehabilitation of education and health care centers to boost income so people no longer need to rely on emergency aid in a way that supports longer-term recovery. Progress on early recovery and livelihood support over next year will be crucial to avoid a resurgence of humanitarian needs in these areas.

Activities

Improve Access to Infrastructure and Basic Social Services through Rehabilitation and Reconstruction of Public Service Facilities through:• Conducting basic rehabilitation of 8 Blocks of Primary schools, 4 in konduga community of Amarwa and 4 in Mafa Community of Simari Including reconstruction of 2 Blocks PHC facilities in Simari ward of Mafa and Malari ward respectively to promote return and sustainability of the services;• Building the capacity of 200 people including women on enterprise development and to understand how to access services such as health care and education, and to contribute to maintaining these services. Support Livelihood Stabilization and Income Recovery through: • Promoting micro and small enterprise recovery for 4 women groups through formation of Village and Savings Loan Association (VSLA) with 15 to 20 members through short-cycle business-management training, cash grants, and access to microfinance schemes and coaching. and other community based accepted income generating activities targeting particularly women and youth groups• Providing emergency employment opportunities to the already trained and mentored VSLA members from the rehabilitation of community infrastructure.Promote inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDPs in Host Communities, Returnees, and Host communities through;• Creating awareness and sensitization on access to justice and civil protection to ensuring displaced and returnee populations and women in particular are not victims of discrimination in relation to their rights in both LGAsSupport Restoration of Local Governance Administration and Services through;• Conducting capacity development for Local Governments for effective coordination and monitoring of existing Recovery Plans and Programs;• Strengthen local governance capacity through provision basic infrastructure to resume the delivery of basic public services
