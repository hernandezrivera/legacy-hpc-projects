To illustrate wefaq increase the team ofremote modalities as a result of increasing load. Recently, in 2017, UN Women published adocument regarding GBV services in Gaza Gender alert. Their investigation showed clearlythat the hardship and poverty exacerbate the GBV in Gaza Strip. Their study alsohighlights the contributing factors of GBV in Gaza Strip including perpetrators rarelyfacing legal penalties; violence against women being socially acceptable within marriageand a prevailing culture of silence to preserve marriages at the expense of victim's rights.Their examination recognized the need for formal training in GBV sensitization, capacitybuilding and the support of income generation support for GBV survivors [R3]. In 2017, oPt Humanitarian Fund (HF) funded projects tackling GBV in Gaza Strip. These projectssupported at least 12,800 people, 77 % of whom were women, mainly those headinghouseholds, disabled, internally displaced and other survivors of GBV.The reality ofdisability in Palestine during the Corona pandemic According to the 2017 statistics fromthe Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics (PCBS)1 released in July 2020, the number ofpersons with disabilities was 255,228, constitute 5.8% of the total population in Palestine;127,266 in the West Bank 5.1% and 127,962 in the Gaza Strip 6.8%. The disability rateamong males was 6.3% and 5.4% among females, and mobility are the most acommon type of difficulties, which reached 32.2% with a prevalence of 2.9% among thepopulation. In this context, persons with disabilities in Palestine, particularly in the GazaStrip, face a number of challenges in acquiring basic human rights, obtaining basicservices such as health, safe access to services and information, and poor access forpersons with disabilities in the public and the governmental services centres. The mostdemanding issue is that RAFAH IS THE ONLY GOVERNORATE WITHOUT QUALIFIEDHOSPITAL and few numbers of GBV actors and specialized organizations.
