Project Summary

This project aims to continue the delivery of CMAM services for SAM and MAM cases within the existing health system in Khartoum(umbada,Sharg Elnile,and Jabal Awlia locaities), S Kordofan (Abujubiha,Dilling,Liri,Kadugli and Alabasia localities) /W Kordofan( Lagawa) , E Darfur(Assalaya,B.Elarab,Eldeein,Elfardous,Yassin,and Abujabra localities) /N Darfur (Melliet, and Alfasher localities) States, through the provision of full package of treatment and prevention of acute malnutrition among (boys & girls) U5 and PLW. The project targets acutely malnourished children (SAM with or without complication and MAM) either through treatment or rehabilitating services. Improve the capacity of MOH staff to provide quality services and community volunteers ,CBOs to mitigate the risk towards food insecurity and reduction of malnutrition, the project enhancing the need analysis of the nutrition situation through conducting rapid nutrition surveys involving the target communities in identifying the needs , planning and in implementation as well as monitoring of project on regular basis. Moderate Acute malnutrition and the underlying causes of malnutrition will be managed through participatory nutrition education activities using the Nutrition Impact & Positive Practice approach (NIPP) through targeting predominantly mothers and fathers of moderately malnourished children. The NIPP circles emphasize behavior change, communication & counseling (for improved awareness), livelihood (for improved food/nutrition security) and cooking demonstration using locally available foods (for improved feeding and care practices). Community screening of cases will equally target boys and girls & depending on their nutritional status they will be referred to the OTP or NIPP circles based on the admission criteria.
Mother to mother groups where a group of mothers with malnourished children, grandparents group) to help empowering Mothers to solve issues related to the nutrition with focus on IYCF practices and knowledge and coordination with relevant sector (for multi-pectoral nutrition activities). Mainstreaming child protection through reflecting in the assessment, design, monitoring and evaluation of the project. Girls and boys of all ages and their caregivers, especially pregnant and breastfeeding women and girls, have access to awareness about women and child rights for the prevention of malnutrition Gender, HIV/AIDS and the environment are mainstreamed throughout the project activities.
The ultimate goal of the project “save the lives” of acute malnourished children U5 in IDP ,host communities and refugee sites at the targeted states.
In addition the project is seeking to provide the medical treatment and referral for the vulnerable populations in the targeted states, through construction or rehabilitation of existing health facilities, purchase of medical equipment and referral of cases to the health facilities in the areas.

In the context of the humanitarian crises in Sudan the most affected population groups include vulnerable communities,IDPs, returnees and refugees.There were an estimated 151,000 IDPs in need (that is, children < five and PLW) and approximately 30,000 returnees (that is, children < five and PLW). As 2019 began, 8 million people were in humanitarian need in Sudan. After a year of civil unrest and political change, humanitarian need continues to rise:this HNO estimates some 9.3 million people 23 % of the population willbe in humanitarian need in 2020.The situationofpeople displaced by decades of conflict is unresolved:the 1.87 million IDPs in Sudan make up 20% of the people in need, and they,refugees,and returnees continue to need humanitarianassistance and protection support, both in camps and communities.During 2019,heavy rains and flash floods have affected thousands of people in 15out of18 states.Communities in Sudan are exposed to significantly increased risk of diseases as heavy rains account for a rise in various vector/water borne diseases including malaria, dengue fever and chikungunya.The political events of 2019, as well as the worsening economic crisis, have affected people directly, raising immediate protection concerns as well as impacting households’ ability to buy food and cover basic needs. Basic service provision, already weak and underdeveloped, has been dangerously degraded bythe economic crisis. In addition,IDPs, refugees,andreturneesalsohavespecificneeds.Politicalchange,ongoingconflict,andenvironmental deterioration, most of all, the deteriorating economic situation, all have profound consequences on people’s living standards, as well as their physical and mental well-being. 5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Environmental mainstreaming will be integrated through the entire project management cycle, according to Almanar 2020 strategy. Environmental mainstreaming will be integrated in the activities to ensure that environmental harm is reduced or eliminated and environmental benefits are maximized. Key environmental concerns will be built into the project in order to reduce deforestation in Sudan. Almanar will attempt to switch away from fired bricks to alternative construction methods such as stabilized soil blocks (SSB) or concrete blocks (CSB) depending on the prerequisites of the region. Environmental mainstreaming will be included during monitoring and evaluation of the project outcomes of the response to ensure that the effects sought was indeed delivered, and to help identify possible negative environmental effects in time in order to adjust activities accordingly. It is a necessary part of ensuring that the principle of do no harm is fulfilled. Furthermore, to help identify best practices and lessons learnt that offer valuable insight for making improvements in future aid efforts. Almanar will work with local NGOs, CSOs and communities including working with the national and state governmental authorities, since Community consultation and participation is a key part of environmental management. Environment is taken into consideration as a specific activity to be implemented and across all health and nutrition activities, as environmental sanitation and hygiene cannot be separated from healthy practices at the household and community levels, and thus is often correlated with high rates of malnutrition.Throughout the project cycle gender will be considered to access to, and management of natural resources to receive consultation from both men and women. The combined information will give more accurate information on the total need for the community and ensure equally assessing.

4=Fully in place

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Almanar through coordination with the local government authorities and humanitarian partners will incorporate protection principles with DO NO HARM and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity in the implementation of this project by avoiding causing harm preventing and minimizing as much as possible any unintended negative effects of the project activities which can increase people’s vulnerability to both physical and psychosocial risks, ensuring equality by arranging access to impartial assistance and services without discrimination and paying special attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services through the vulnerability assessment and identification vulnerability criteria. Through the CBPCs and consultation sessions Almanar will ensure accountability to the beneficiaries, where they can address their concerns and complaints. Through the awareness session and training workshops, networking and forums Almanar will support the development of self-protection capacities and assist people to claim their rights and empowerment.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

In the all stages of the project the communities are kept engaged and involved through community dialogue meetings, project dissemination. Accountability will be ensured across the activities in this project through establishment of complaints and response mechanisms at project level aimed at ensuring that the voices of the affected populations will be heard. These committees are expected to be gender and ability sensitive with the inclusion of both males, females, elderly and people with disabilities to ensure that all voices, including those of the marginalized are heard, equality and equity are realized with regards to access to services that will be provided by the project. Project staff will ensure adequate consultation with the communities to ensure that existing complaints handling mechanisms are incorporated into the complaints and response mechanisms to be established by the project.
