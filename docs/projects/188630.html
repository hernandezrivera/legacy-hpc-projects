Project Summary

This project will assist in the investigation and treatment of acute malnutrition among conflict-affected children U5 and PLWs in Darat Azza and surrounding communities, Aleppo Governorate, Syria. Through two mobile teams of health workers trained and qualified for this task.
The goal of the project is to support breastfeeding, spread awareness, prevent acute malnutrition, detect and follow up cases, facilitate early detection, and accompany severe cases with complications in inpatient care. The project will target all children under the age of five and pregnant and lactating women in easily accessible communities including informal settlements in the area. Exclusion criteria will not be added. The selection of beneficiaries will be fair and impartial. Both the host communities and the internally displaced will be targeted, and preventive and curative supplements will be provided for infected cases, follow-up and necessary counseling, and training of mothers of the infected to follow up the measurement and record the results.

4 - Needs

Orkide through the project is trying to minimize the gap of preventative and therapeutic/curative nutrition needs among children under five and pregnant and lactating women (PLWs). Many other NGOs are also working in the same context under the umbrella of Nutrition cluster in Gaziantep. Orkide is coordinating with the cluster to define the most urgent gaps and to implement in harmony with all other stakeholders.And by coordinating with the nutrition clusterer and getting the areas where the gap is. The high rate of malnutrition in these areas, according to the information provided to the clusters by the nutrition monitoring teams working in these areas, a goal has been set to reach 60,000 thousand indirect beneficiaries, including 35,000 direct beneficiaries of the project, where direct services will be provided to them during 2022

5 - Implementation of activities

ORKIDE staff started to get the program's approvals from the local authorities of the targeted locations (Daret Ezza) in rural Aleppo and follow up the approvals progress. When we obtained the required approvals.We coordinated with the Nutrition Cluster in Turkey (Gaziantep) to find out the areas that lack the service provided by us, which are community health and nutrition (PLWs and Children under 5). We were directed to areas that are not covered by any organization, and after we conducted field visits, some camps and some areas were also in need of these services so we added these areas. The plan has been developed to visit it periodically and follow up the cases in it and transfer some cases when necessary.As for the project activities, there will be: *Screening and Referral(1- children 6-59 months screening for acute malnutrition2-PLWs screening for acute malnutrition).*Distribution of preventive as well as curative nutritional supplements for detected cases of malnutrition and follow-up until recovery.(MAM treatment + out-patient SAM treatment)*Provide community health workers caregivers (including PLW) letters/education on appropriate IYCF-E.*Conduct awareness sessions about IYCF.*Family MUAC Approach(training and follow up of mothers on FMA).*Provide caregivers (including PLW) one to one counseling on appropriate IYCF-E.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Orkide will pursue gender equity in project staffing (unless the nature of the project and culture require mainly female staff) and compensation. The project will have:• 8 community health workers (CHWs): all CHWs should have been trained on CHWs guideline "Gaziantep, WHO" and IYCF and CMAM protocols and have at least 2 years of experience. Preferably they have a medical background certificate. • 2 team leader: the team leader should have a university degree, be familiar with or preferably from the targeted area, have no less than 3-year experience with social work, including at least 1 year as a team leader, full knowledge of CHWs program, and excellent knowledge of managing records. • 1 Project Officer: This person will manage the project. He is already part of the Orkide crew. He has a medical university background, more than two years of experience in implementing nutrition projects, ability to manage projects and plan activities, coordinate with technical bodies, and strong knowledge in providing technical support to teams.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

 All the staff will be oriented about the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policy and procedures.  The beneficiaries (BNFs) will be informed about the feedback mechanisms and the mechanisms will be shown in a proper way during the field visits.  A hotline and a complaints box will be used and will be announced for all BNFs to address any complaint raised.  All complaints will be tracked according to Orkide’s complaints tracking system, to make sure that each complaint is resolved and the BNF is informed about the solution.  Each complaint will be handled according to its severity scale, as conducted by Orkide.  A bi-weekly spot check will be conducted by Orkide’s MEAL officer: a total of 6 field assessments should be completed and a monthly M&E report.  All complaints will be handled by the field MEAL officer then raised to the HQ meal officer, who will also track the implementation and provide his report.
