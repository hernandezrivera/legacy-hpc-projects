Project Summary

It would not be wrong to say that in several school centres in North-West Cameroon, students take courses sitting on kitchen benches, on small stools and others on the floor. Teachers who stand throughout the day and use the windows of their classroom as the table and place to store the teaching materials.
In the face of all this, I am afraid that the emergence 2035 advocated by Cameroon will only be a shame for these young people who cannot distinguish their left hands from their right hands. The deplorable situation in this area is to be feared because of the armed conflicts that have made the Northwest a West lighthouse.
Thus, in the face of this alarming situation, OFPMAS has acted as the voice of these young people and voiceless teachers to implore all humanitarian actors to be sensitive to the needs of these children and their teachers. Certainly their dream cannot be fulfilled without you. And let us not forget also that an outstretched hand can break the deadlock a family, a people, see a whole country. In view of all this I ask myself a question: how could we think of a prosperous future without these children and how could they hope for a successful life without us? I hope for them that their cries will reach your ears and that your hearts will be sensitive to the needs of these children and their teachers who are waiting only for you to come out of primitivity.
Their situation is even more chaotic when, according to the report, the internally displaced have filled the city of BAMENDA thus fleeing the abuses of rebels and bandits who cause countless deaths.
To this end, I invite all humanitarian actors to take a look at this region in order to meet this challenge that awaits us.

Besoins / Needs

The North-West region having experienced a chaotic situation in the field of education on the fact that several schools were set on fire by the enemies of education, Support in the field of school infrastructure would be crucial for the area’s next generation of students. So many questions will remain unanswered if the world remains sterile to the educational situation of children in the North-West region.Since OFPMAS cannot list the needs of all the education system of the area, it has concentrated on the benches, chairs, desks and storage cabinets which need urgent. The six schools that were the subject of our evaluation will be able by your help to improve education at half-mast in the sector.However, let us know that these children will only be able to know the value of education if we put to their needs the means to follow the courses sitting on comfortable tables. And that their teachers have at their disposal: desks, chairs and cabinets to store their teaching materials.- Number of school centres requiring assistance: 06;- Number of classrooms per school centre: 13.Total classrooms for the 06 school centres:78. Estimated needs for the six school centres:-Number of bench tables for students in the 06 school centres: 1872-Number of office tables for teachers in the 06 school centres: 78-Number of storage cabinets for teachers in the 06 school centres: 78-Number of chairs for teachers in the 06 school centres: 78May we take this new generation of students to heart, knowing that our help will enable 3744 students to enjoy the values of education. And to 234 teachers of these schools to fulfill their duty in peace.I took you that when you smile, think that these children and their teachers also need to smile. Wherever you dream, believe that their dream will only be possible through you.The beneficiaries of this project are:Kindergarten students (girls and boys);SIL students in the fifth grade (girls and boys);Adult teachers (women and men);Single teachers young adults (girls and boys)
