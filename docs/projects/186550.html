Project Summary

Bridging the gap in ending Gender Based Violence in Adamawa State a project to be carried out in Lafiya, Lamurde, and Gyawana Communities, Lamurde Local Government Area, Adamawa State, Nigeria.
The project overall goal is to address root causes of GBV and proffer solutions through improved access to essential multi-sectoral services. The objectives are; i) To provide direct support to vulnerable women and girls by empowering them to access the necessary assistance to meet the basic protection standards, ii). To
increase awareness and enhance systems that will bring an end to GBV through mitigating risk factors and strengthening community protection strategies, (3) To sensitize the community on the importance of reporting GBV. The strategies to be employed include human right based approach, survivor centered based approach, community centered based approach, town-hall meetings, drama and road shows, jingles and print media. Our project direct beneficiaries include women and adolescent girls in general, survivors of GBV, women and girls with disabilities.

2 - Activities

Against All Odds Foundation (AGAOF), as a grass root based NGO intends to proffer solutions to the GBV issues in the selected communities of Lamurde LGA. Our focus is on both preventive and response using survivor centered approach to create a long term transformation impacts. We intend to promote preventive measures using both Human Rights Based approach, Survivor Centered approach and Community based approach. We will use : i. Sensitization and awareness on the need to prevent the occurrences ofviolence ii. Sensitize women and girls on their rights and to advocate for themselves and as well save otherwomen iii. Sensitize men and boys on their responsibilities to defend women and girls as agent of changeiv. Advocate for harmonious relationship between boys and girls in schools, places of worship, markets, social gatherings based on respect and equality. v. Establish improved services for GBV survivors and case management. vi. Economic empowerment programs for the women. i. Using the human rights-based approach, we will analyze and address the root causes of discrimination and inequality. Everyone regardless of their gender, age, disabilities, ethnicity or religion, has the right to live with freedom and dignity, safe fromviolence, exploitation and abuse, in accordance with principles of human rights law. We will assess capacities of affected population, identify immediate barriers and underlying causes that prevent people from realizing their rights. We will assess capacities and imitations of duty-bearers (e.g. government) to fulfill their human rights obligations to the beneficiaries, as well as develop sustainable strategies for overcoming the limitations of duty-bearers. We will monitor and evaluate outcomes and processes, guided by human rights principles and standards. ii. Using the Survivor-centered approach. In line with theguiding principles, survivor’s rights, needs and wishes will be prioritized. This is becausekey elements of the survivor-centered approach build on the guiding principles of safety,confidentiality, respect and non-discrimination. iii. In using the community-based approach, we will from the earliest stage of the project ensure that critical stakeholders participate in making decisions, have access to information and expect transparency throughout. This could be achieved by ensuring that affected population are actively engaged as partners in developing strategies related to their protection and the provision of humanitarian assistance. This will be done through the process of direct consultationand dialogue with members of communities, including women, girls and other at-risk groups. Participation within a community-based approach will involve women, adolescent girls and community leaders as beneficiaries to be empowered as partners in the policy and strategy development, as well as program design and implementation. We will use these approaches to achieve result through the: i. . Provision of material assistance that aims at supporting vulnerable women and girls unable to access services, the assistance such as emergency material support, dignity kits distribution and re-integration packages. ii. Provision of health care and medical services for survivors of GBV through investigations, treatment, emergency contraception and antibiotics for STI treatment. iii. Provision of case management support for GBV survivors to assess, plan, implement, coordinate, monitor and evaluate available resources, options and services to meet an individual survivor’s needs and to promote quality, effective outcomes. iv. Town hallmeetings, drama/road shows, jingle and print media to enable beneficiaries relate freely and have confidence to open up on GBV issues affecting them.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

i. We will maintain the tenets of Right-based approach of safety, confidentiality, non-discriminatory style in all our interviews, interactions, discussions, and counseling sessions with survivors and respondents. ii. We will seek the consent of parents/guardians when using children and minors to get information iii. Names of rape/sexual abuse victims will not be mentioned in public to avoid stigmatization iv. Services of local security outfits known in the locality will be employed to avoid peoples fear and assure them of their safety v. Will employ do-no-harm approach in all our activities. vi. Men and women would be given equal opportunities to access GBV related services so that definition of GBV will be increased, reflecting the desire that all members of the community will be involved in preventing and responding to all forms of GBV cases. vii. Training of survivors in small business management skills; engagingthe private sector and diaspora business leaders, particularly women, who may be able to assist in the rehabilitation of adolescent, also increasing coordination between GBV response actors and livelihood programmes focusing on the outcomes and impact of other project interventions.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Stakeholder analysis tool will be used to engage key stakeholders by conducting a stakeholders analysis especially when facilitating focus discussions and key informants interviews with the stakeholders. The stakeholders category are: i. Community members (GBV Survivors, vulnerable persons (women and adolescentgirls), community leaders, religious groups, vigilante, community women associationetc) ii. Government Actors (Local Government Personnel/staff, politicians, police, civil defense, Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development, etc) iii. Other NGOs/CSOs present in Lamurde Local Government Area and other key-stakeholders in the community. The contribution of the stakeholders is the collection of useful and accurate information that will guide baseline data collection. Our approach of engaging stakeholders listed above always requires the use of Right - based and Community - based /Participatory approach. The integrated use of these approaches for data collection increases stakeholder capacity and ownership of the programwithin the local community. The M&E Plan will be attached at the end of the project for reporting.
