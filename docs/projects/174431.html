Project Summary

HEADS has proposed WASH assistance for 24,000 floods affected population in district Shangla to cope up with urgent WASH needs. Proposed villages are finalized based on identified needs through the involvement of PHED, local communities, and coordination with district administration to ensure maximum transparency and accountability. An assessment conducted by HEADS further guided the planning of culturally sensitive and locally relevant activities for the proposed areas while the proposed activities are in line with sector objectives for HRP 2021. The focus of the proposed interventions is on WASH support for which includes Repair/Rehabilitation/installation of water supply systems including handpumps, pressure pumps and dug wells, Installation of user friendly latrines, defection trenches, bathing cubicles and washing places, Promotion of critical WASH-related information using interpersonal communication, social and mass media, radio jingles, leaflets, posters, billboards and radio & television shows and Provision of appropriate WASH installations at schools and healthcare facilities. HEADS will ensure all compliances i.e. SPHERE, Gender Marker, Core Humanitarian Standards during the implementation phase.
