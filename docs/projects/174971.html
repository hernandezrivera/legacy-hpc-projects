Project Summary

Pakistan is a disaster-prone country mostly faced with floods, droughts, earthquakes and is currently passing through the second wave of COVID pandemic. Most disadvantaged districts of Balochistan, Sind and KP are more prone the emergencies, which have already having very poor development indicators including health. Ongoing COVID19 Pandemic has further deteriorated the situation as the country is faced with both direct and indirect morbidities and mortalities due to COVID pandemic. Health services in the country are already resource constrained and there is a need to support national government for strengthening essential health services with more focus on MNCH and essential lifesaving immunization. The Following interventions will be prioritized.
1. Essential Health Services Strengthening: The project will strengthen Lifesaving RMNCH and Immunization services in public sector PHC facilities in target districts through capacity building of health care providers (on COVID, IPC, hand and hygiene promotion, Self-protection , triaging , MHPSS, addressing stigma & discrimination in health care settings) provision of equipment and supplies including PPEs (Personal Protective Equipment); home based newborn care, IMNCI and management of pneumonia and diarrhoea along with supplies. The project will also strengthen community outreach of life saving immunization and MNCH services through outreach teams (LHWs, Vaccinators, CMWs) and engaging local CSO partners. Provision of Psychosocial Support (PSS): This is primarily aimed at those individuals or group of people who require care and support including those in isolation or quarantine in case of the current COVID-19 situation. Digital platforms will be used for delivering of PSS while there are movement restrictions in place. The Psychosocial support will be provided at three levels tailored to the needs of target population:
2. Community based integrated (Nutrition, WASH, Health) essential MNCH and Immunization services will be established for the most disadvantaged population , with support from LHW Program and involvement of local CSOs focusing on provision of life saving essential health services at the door step of communities.
3. Risk Communication and Community Engagement: Risk communication and community engagement is another area, where awareness raising campaign on COVID and IPC will be launched through community-based health workers like LHWs, CMWs and other CHWs. engaging local Influential and religious leaders orienting them on COVID-19 prevention and importance of seeking healthcare.
4. Programme monitoring and knowledge generation: Monitoring continuity of MNCH & immunization services through filed visits, desk reviews and review meetings. The project will also support generation of evidence around MNCH & Immunization in the context of COVID 19.
