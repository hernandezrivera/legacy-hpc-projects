Project Summary

The project implementing areas are famous for pistachio farming, it's include 6000 Dunums planted with Pistachio(about 65000 trees).
In the last few years, the pistachio trade has experienced a significant decline due to the war in Syria and the separation of cities according to the parties control of each region. The targeted areas has experienced major conflicts between the Syrian government and the opposition factions, which have resulted in the destruction of large areas of land planted with pistachios and the destruction of infrastructure of pistachio trade.
The targeted areas lost 20% of its property of the golden tree "Pistachio Tree" due to the war in Syria, where 900 dunums were lost due to the uprooting, burning and breaking due to the war that was in the region, in addition to the loss of 300 dunums have been uprooted directly by farmers due to the large damage due to pistachio capnodis insect, because of the poor ability of the farmer in the fight against this insect under conditions of war and displacement and the absence of any governmental or non-governmental entity that provides material and technical support to farmers in the field of control of this insect.
The remaining area of 4800 dunums, which constitutes 80% of the total area of the pistachio groves in the town, suffers from general weakness and insect infection (Bsilla, Capnodis, chaetoptelius vestitus, as well as Karamana insect, which appeared almost 10 years ago) and fungal infection, the most important of which is Septoria leaf blotch and the fruit rotting.
Due to the damage caused to the pistachio tree as a result of the war, the productivity of this tree began to decline until 2016 to reach 14 kg per tree compared with 82 kg for the tree in 2010. The wages of workers increased significantly from 2.5 dollars per hour per worker in 2010 to 3-6 $ per hour per worker depending on the security situation of the pistachio field, which has become a burden on farmers in addition to the high cost of agricultural inputs for production (fertilizers, pesticides) and low quality. All these reasons led to a decrease in the farmers' revenue, which negatively affected their standard of living and the service of the pistachio trees.
The Project will contribute to the revive of pistachio sector through:
First: the marketing sector:
The marketing infrastructure of pistachio will be reconstructed by:
1. The establishment of a peeling line to separate the fruits of pistachio from its external green peel, then packaging and equipping for marketing or storage.
2. The establishment of crushing line to separate the fruits of pistachio from its external wooden peel to extract pulp from the fruits and then packaging and marketing.
3. The establishment of a packing line to prepare pistachio for marketing after the removal of external green peel and drying it or after the removing of wooden peel and extracting the pulp.
4. Giving the product a special brand where a special name for the product will be chosen in agreement with all parties involved in the project, considering that this brand name is owned by no one before and has no religious, ethnic, sectarian connotations etc.
Second: Reviving the pistachios office through:
Re-activate agricultural communities in the region and training and rehabilitating them administratively and financially in order to contribute to project management and follow-up project activities and sustainability after the end of the project period. Where the focus will be on this gathering includes agronomists, who have experience in serving the pistachio tree in addition to those who have previous experience in the preparation and marketing pistachio.
Third: Serving the pistachio trees:
1. Support the harvesting process of pistachio nuts by providing workers (Cash for Work) and technical supervision.
2. Provision of organic and chemical fertilizers.
Fourth: Distribution the wood peel of pistachio nuts to the vulnerable people to use it in heating.

Protection Risk Analysis

Risks: 1- Community tension arising from the beneficiaries’ selection, where the IDPs protest to not selecting them to receive the agricultural inputs2- Humanitarian workers’ male-dominated. Mitigation measures:1- Community- based beneficiaries’ selection, as well as selecting the daily workers from the vulnerable people from both IDPs and host communities.2- Recruit female staff and train them.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk Description:1.The sale of agricultural inputs by some beneficiaries2.Late completion of purchases due to the status of crossing border or availability of agricultural inputs How to mitigate :1.The inputs will be distributed in stages during the implementation of the project, and the monitoring of the beneficiaries at each stage to continue receiving support and awareness that the sale is a violation of the conditions and in this case, Elaf will deprive the beneficiary of receiving assistance in this project and any other project carried out by Elaf 2.Conduct a market assessment in Syria and Turkey and start the procurement process once the project is agreed upon, with an alternative plan for the procurement process
