Project Summary

Considering the economic challenges nowadays in Sudan , in South Darfur and South Kordofan in particular and based on the need assessment done by El Ruhama field staff in the targeted areas mention above the assessment reveal that there is food insecurity facing IDPs, residents, refugees and returnees it is expected to be more worsen, in addition to above mentioning factors, women and girls experienced (GBV), including rape .sexual assault, intimate partner violence, sexual harassment, trafficking, and sexual exploitation and abuse of girls, early married, FGM this specific considering violence is socially ascribed intended to control female body and live them in subordinate position , moreover, women and girls are economically and socially vulnerable and have a higher incidence of poverty, they lack job-based skills and means of production which increases their vulnerability to GBV; Elruhama recognized that returnees, and Host communities in South Darfur and South Kordofan still have challenges in accessing services of SGBV survivors, Survivors of S/GBV are not support properly . There is a need to offer more support to these vulnerable targeted communities and m the social workers and services provider need more training including Psychological First Aid (PFA), there is also demand for formation of SGBV protection mechanism . Elruhama and will implement this project in close collaboration and partnership with key stakeholders like local community CBOs, HAC, locality leaders in particular the administrative leaders , popular committees and other effective key persons and line miniseries. This project will use a community based approach to reach the protection needs of the most vulnerable population especially women, and girls ,its planned activities will targeted South Darfur and South Kordofan the intervention will be done after need assessment to a void overlapping and duplication .The activity foster to enhance protection services to most vulnerable women in the above targeted areas through establishment/ rehabilitate of women centers with local materials to serve multi- functional purposes such as strength protection committees , provision of in –kind supports for women , strengthening the capacity of the services provider , social worker and local CBOs ,provision of protection services and motivate the community protection networks through community engagement and capacity building along with related line ministry to insure the quality of the service delivery to vulnerable / survivor women and for project sustainability..

Needs

The inter-agency assessments conducted in areas such as SD and SK reveal uncovered massive humanitarian needs in protection especially with GBV , some of the concerns related to ability to respond to humanitarian needs. The status quo of sporadic fighting during 2018 also meant that the numbers of displaced populations continue to create vulnerabilities, with the majority being children and women. respect for the rights of individuals. Needs extend beyond material assistance, and require the provision of specific counseling and humanitarian protection activities. For example, training of social workers, construction or rehabilitation of women centers for multi -function will be deployed to ensure that community-based protection mechanisms extend its protection responsibilities the project intervention will include , establishment of women centre by local materials which will served for multi- functional services for women and female empowerment , for family tracing and reunification, for reintegration of children from state and non-state armed forces and armed groups, for referral pathway for improving the monitoring and reporting mechanism on violations among conflict affected children and prevent and respond to GBV against women and female

Activities

Activity 1 : conduct base line survey/need assessment for two targeted locality SD (Dimsu & Al Radom), SK (Kadoguli and Al Miram)Activity 2: Establishment / rehabilitation of four women centers from local materials to serve as multi -functional purpose enters ,such as referral pathway, services provision, market for women small business, information and learning center …etc . the targeted direct beneficiary will reached 2000 (1000 each locality)Activity 3: 4 capacity workshops targeted 200 persons (gender sensitive )for services provider, social workers, line minsters, community leaders …etc to provide services properly Activity 5: formation of four protection mechanisms /committees / networks targeted 200 persons (100 per each locality–gender sensitive)to overseas services inside the center , guarantees the quality of service delivery and bear other responsibility related the GBV services and for project sustainability Activity 6: conduct 4 advocacy events (possible 16 days of activism ) for combatting the GBV and stop violence against women and female specially with incidents that related to FGM, early marriage , domestic violence , trafficking and rape. the events will targeted 2000 persons. Activity 7: provision of SRHS kits targeting 1000 female (250 per each area)Activity 8 : conducting four workshop for food processing and market –based skills targeting 200 female (50 per each area) to be handle at the centers with possible advance TOT training for the best to run the activity within the centers and to oversee the women economic groups that will be formed by the end of the workshop.Activity 9 : provision of start –up kits for 200 female as evolving fund and for resilience . Activity 10 : conduct 8 sessions targeting 200 persons including media, ministry of social welfare, stakeholders , the session will be about women rights, gender, GBV, VAW, reproductive health rights , HIV/AIDs and STD (sexual transmitted disease ),FGM,
