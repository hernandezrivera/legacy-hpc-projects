Project Summary

The overall project objective is to prevent and mitigate the impact of violence on vulnerable communities, and to increase the protection of population affected by conflict and natural disasters in five Counties where AFAA has both static and mobile presence . In Panyinjiar AFAA Mobile Team implement emergency flood response project through integrated mobile clinic which has a component of protection whereby our mobile team do referrals of protection concerns cases while in Leer AFAA has mobile focal point who used to participate in local actors coordination meetings and shared protection updates and in Upper Nile ,Malakal town and Longechuk County, AFAA is implementing community-Led monitoring project focusing on monitoring, documenting, and reporting of HIV/AIDS rights violations whereby AFAA Share reported violations with concern actors to address such violations, and in Maiwut AFAA used to access such location with its mobile team based in Longechuk who do Monthly protection assessments to identify protection risks related to HIV/AIDS human rights violations, these activities, are in line with this project which will focus on addressing the most critical protection needs through static and mobile response by conducting monthly protection assessment through FGDS and KIIS , weekly patrols, protective accompaniment or referral for available services, these measures will prevent violence and ensure safe access to services by communities. Referral pathway strengthening , conduct workshops, do advocacy on policy changes targeting key leaders and stakeholders, this help transform attitudes and secure community participation in risk prevention and mitigation. AFAA focus on capacity building will ensure communities are resilient to, and can recover from violence/harms without AFA’s future support, trainings and assessments will focus on existing coping strategies to support communities’ capacities identifying and responding to protection risks especially in high-risks areas, community based protection structures will also be trained, equipped, and involved in project activities; this encourages community participation in identifying their own protection risks, come up with sustainable protection strategy , AFAA shall facilitate community dialogues and meetings that promote peaceful co-existence , local security actors and other authorities. These will lead to enhanced understanding, trusts between communities, resolve issues peacefully, and reduce risks of harm in case of violence. In line with the local capacity building strategy, violence prevention, mitigation and response workshops will also be provided to communities, using training manuals on peacebuilding, and conflict mitigation. AFAA will facilitate centrality of protection in humanitarian operation by providing training/technical advice to partner agencies when there are identified gaps , use its unique access to provide regular assessments on ongoing protection crises, and thereby provide critical information to humanitarian responses, mobile team deployment shall be coordinated with state Protection cluster, and once response is triggered by PC’s clear criteria, mobile teams can be immediately deployed to where violent conflict happens or where there is no presence of protection actors to respond. AFAA has unique expertise in facilitating community-driven protection, static team in community and present 24/7 to address protection needs. AFAA relies on strong relationship-building, resulting in community trust and acceptance that helps AFAA negotiate unique access to areas. AFAA staff includes advisors in protection, women peace & security, social cohesion, safety and security and M&E guiding AFAA to be effective and efficient whilst ensuring safety. Operational support systems are in place to ensure effective and timely actions for programming and reducing operational risks. The project will directly benefit 39,136 individuals disaggregated by sex ).

1 - Needs

Ten years after independence and three years after the signing of the revitalized peace agreement, people in South Sudan continue to face deteriorating humanitarian conditions. Their situation is worsened by endemic violence, conflict, access constraints and operational interference, public health challenges such as direct and indirect effects of COVID-19 and climatic shocks resulting in the dual phenomena of extraordinary flooding and localized drought, which have a severe impact on people’s livelihoods, hampers access to education and water, sanitation and hygiene and health services. Protection concerns remain high, with people impacted by violence having limited access to justice and the rule of law. In 2022, the humanitarian community in South Sudan estimates that more than two-thirds of South Sudan's population, 8.9 million people, are in need of humanitarian assistance, an increase of 600,000 since 2021. According to AFAA Panyinjiar field site team who participated in intra- agencies assessment late last year here attached, Unity state is among the worst affected with an estimated 480,000 were displaced while in Panyijiar county 8178 HH (Nyal 3,739 and Ganyliel 4439) people were reported affected, coupled with the slow recession of the 2020 floodwaters, and the onset of the 2021 seasonal rainfall last July, the number of floods affected households in Panyijiar county increased spontaneously with an estimated (52,654) people (27683 female, 24971 male) were affected in 2021 in ten worst-affected payams of Pachienchok, Yai, Ganyliel, Pachar, Katieth, Kol, Nyal, Pathiel, Mayom,Tiap,Maluok and Thornhom . Snake bites cases and cattle raiding cases between Panyinjiar and Mayendit Youths was reported during the assessment and was said to be causing protection concerns to the already fragile situation in Panyinjiar County. There was report of limited information on protection services by those displaced to Islands and high grounds., for example, in Panyinjair County, Women and girls are exposed to many protection risks especially risks of GBV since there is no privacy at the crowded places, women and girls walked long distances inside water to collect firewood from relatively non-flooded villages or high grounds, this is due to lack of awareness raising on protection risks. This Increased cases of exposing women and girls to sexual exploitation and abuse while in Longechuk County Upper Nile, IRNA assessment reports shared by AFAA mobile team that the assessed populations in and around Udier, Pamach, Mathiang, Warweng, Jangok, Malow and Belwang payams are consistently reported to have protection concerns such as lack of 1) Food, 2) NFIs and 3/ protection needs due to reported community tensions and clashes among groups who either defect to either government or IO due to negative political influences, there was report of recent insecurity in Longechuk, Mathiang , in Late February 2022 where population in Mathiang and around it fled to nearby forest due to fear of attack by defectors , there is a huge gap in protection services in both Longechuk and Maiwut Counties, while in Leer villages like Padeah, Guaat, Gandoor,and Juong over 2000 population are reported displaced to Leer TPA during the recent clashes in Mirmir between IO forces and some local youth from Koch . The local population there live in fear to return to their original villages because anticipated return of the same Youth in future is worrying. To address theses identified protect concerns AFAA intends to facilitate community dialogues, provide capacity building trainings to youth and local leaders on peace building , raise awareness on protection risks between Leer & Koch Youth and also use its unique access through its static and mobile response to regularly conduct monthly protection assessments on ongoing critical protection needs across named Counties in order to prevent and mitigate anticipated protection risks .

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

AFAA shall design, monitor and evaluate the goals and objectives of project with the involvement and feedback of affected populations views by incorporating them in all stages of the project implementation. This information will also feed back into planning processes and implementation during the project period, thus ensuring that proposed activities of the projects will be community-driven, and are in line with the principles of Accountability to Affected Populations and such feedback shall be adapted and integrated into relevant future organizational processes such as; assessments, community meetings, project proposals, project monitoring and evaluations, staff recruitment, and inductions, trainings and performance appraisals, and reporting in a manner that is participatory to the community members views and needs. For the project design and implementation, beneficiaries’ feedback shall be constantly collected through engagement with project participants via different platforms such as community meetings, focus group discussions, trainings, workshops, and key informant interviews . AFAA shall focus on always consulting local community beforehand to get their views on how best activities could be conducted in line with the beneficiaries feedback and ensure that the feedback are adapted where relevant and in turn use such important information for the design of future projects in the same community.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

AFAA has a policy in place for protection against sexual exploitation and abuse , and AFAA project staff shall have the responsibility of raising awareness of the community members on sexual exploitation and abuse, so that they know that no AFAA Staff or any other humanitarian staff can sexually or otherwise exploit project beneficiaries. All project beneficiaries and affected persons will be encouraged to use complaints mechanisms and AFAA shall have a focal points internally and will have also other community structures focal point which will first be trained on PSEA including reporting on incidences in case an incident occurs. The information provided to AFAA on PSEA shall be treated with confidence and such complaints will be addressed without causing any harm.

8.11. Mitigation Measures.

In order to prevent and mitigate anticipated risks on project beneficiaries and project personels, AFAA shall first conduct pre- risk exercise before carrying out any planned registration and distribution activities and AFAA shall work very closely with the concerned humanitarian partners and local leaders to identify the safety of the distribution site including the distance between the location of targeted population and planned distribution site, and space of distribution site. Also AFAA will help identify potential protection risks during the registration and distribution exercise and will respond without delay, for example, providing to local leaders technical assistance in the form of capacity building training on anticipated risks and how to respond . AFAA will also established safe survivors centers – especially for those living in hard to reach areas who have challenges in accessing referral services due to lack of enough coverage of the said services, experienced social stigma, and/or not being able to afford transportation cost to where services are available, also community members will be made aware of available services and where and when to access such services when in need , they will be made aware through information sharing during community meetings and other community gatherings. Community members will be equipped with knowledge on available referral pathways and post-incident support. Information will be disseminated in local language to reach the majority of the community members in places where there are large gatherings. AFAA will also engage influential leaders, religious leaders, and youth to secure their support in addressing the socio-economic barriers for survivors to access post-incident care services. Furthermore, AFAA will work very closely with other stakeholders and use the available referral pathways and always conduct coordination meetings with protection local actors to review and upgrade the accessibility and quality of available services. Finally, AFAA will make a consistent effort of including marginalized groups such as PwDs (persons with disabilities) and Older persons, women, boys and girls into its consultations, and community decision-making processes and/or meetings and also offer strong support to community protection structures through capacity strengthening . At project start, AFAA will engage host community leaders to set a peaceful ground for IDPS and returnees by conducting sessions on peaceful co-existence and , in cases of any disputes, meetings will be called as soon as possible before the anticipated surfaced and resolve into violence. AFAA will also conduct regular communication channels with relevant actors, closely engagement local authorities and available community protection and local peace committee structures available, and build their capacities on violence prevention and peaceful co-existence , this will act as additional measures for preventing community tensions and mitigate anticipated conflicts/or issues.
