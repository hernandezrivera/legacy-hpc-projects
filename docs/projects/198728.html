Project Summary

This project will cover a period of 12 months. The project will start in January 2023 and will be concluded in December, 2023.
This project will be working closely with youth groups at various strata of the northeast society to explore their potentials in managing conflict, reactions to VEOs misinformation and peace building. The project is also designed to provide gender awareness, leadership induction, advocacies and trainings directly benefitting youths and advocates media campaigns through Radios, Printed Media, and Bill Boards. This media campaign will reach to almost all youth generation in the target area and raise awareness about their active role in civil society and policy initiatives. The project will adapt a cultural sensitive strategy, where all the activities will be planned and conducted in a way that the negative impacts will be avoided. Youths, which are both males and females, will be the target and they will be reached during the course of this project. Based on our team observations, our society is now ready to accept that youths are integral part and change agents of the society. The project will take advantage of this new emerging feeling of the society and build upon the concept that this is the time where Nigerian youths must play a great contribution to bring prosperity to the war-affected society (Boko Haram Insurgency) by influencing the policy initiatives. In this generation, media has become an important part and plays a vital role in our society. Additionally, the stronger role of media is now obvious in Nigeria; it has broken the shackles of the centuries-long self-imposed superstitions in almost every walk of life. People feel empowered with the rising level of awareness on various issues. The media can eliminate the existing deprivation of youths and the steps could be taken towards their empowerment and their role in the Nigerian civil society and policy initiatives. So through broadcasting of radio programs (Phone-in) and messages about youth empowerment and their role in the society, they will be aware and sensitized in the society. However, this project will make the targeted youth groups fully empowered through various capacity building trainings and empowerment programs on peace building and Violent Extremism towards building community resilience to emerging conflicts. Care Aid will use it technical skills and expertise to encourage them to take part in community development activities, managing conflicts and better understanding of their role in peace building, conflict transformation agenda, networking, learning and communication.

1 - Needs

A decade of conflict and insecurity, hunger and massive displacement in Northeast Nigeria has left the populace in extremely vulnerable and at high risk of violent extremism situation. While the humanitarian response in the region has stabilized the condition of small number of the previously affected populations, perpetual external shocks limit the population’s access to basic services causing fluctuating levels of vulnerability. As a result women, youth and children are forced to adopt risky and sometimes harmful coping mechanisms, including listening to campaign by JAS/ISWAP group to recruit vulnerable community members through religious sermons, cash inducements, loans, gifts and provision of food items. Pressure for survival intensifies the potential continuous violence. Communities in NE Nigeria have a long history of shared culture, values and customs that are unique in fostering community bonds and solidarity which is reflected in the peaceful nature of communities especially in the pre-insurgency era in the region. However, while infiltrating communities, VEOs seek to divide the communities and drive them towards conflict by providing negative messages that undermines government policies, misinterpretation of religious provisions and teachings. This reinforces ethnic tensions, stereotype, diversity as well as creating loopholes for VEOs to utilize. Under this project, YOYICOD will engage traditional and religious leaders, youth and women groups, professionals that directly engage with community members like the department of social services, public health, teachers etc to support in popularizing alternative narratives to VEOs negative messages through different campaign approaches and strengthen community cohesion to increase citizens consciousness for peaceful coexistence and tolerance in Bade through the following approacheso Build and sustain resilience at the community and individual level: For many of those who have experienced terror events, psychological trauma is the immediate result. This invisible wound has adverse, pernicious and long-term effects on the victims and the communities. o Strengthen victims’ roles in preventing violent extremism: Victims of terrorism, whether direct, secondary or indirect, have credible voices to add to the counter-narrative debate, from policy formulation to prevention and intervention. By sharing their own stories, victims give a human face to the effects of terrorism, thus providing a damning critique to the terrorist discourse.o Contribute to inter-faith, intra-faith and intercultural dialogues: One of the aim of this project is to bring young victims together, from different communities and different religions, to promote peace-building and foster resilience. Encouraging dialogue between victims of terrorism, who have different backgrounds, experiences, stories, faith; helps contribute to their resilience and foster tolerance. They feel less isolated as they share a common experience. That way, they understand that they are not alone and they develop empathy by interacting with others

2 - Activities

Community entrance and Joint advocacy to the State and Local Governments of the targeted LGAsHigh-level engagement with the Governments authorities will be made in the places where this intervention will be carried out. To gain buy-in from the relevant stakeholders to achieve full community ownership and sustainability of the initiative in the future, advocacy visits would be organized for the identified key influencers such as traditional and community leaders, religious leaders, ward development committee heads, wealthy individuals, as well as associations in communities.  Government Authorities (National Emergency Management Agency – NEMA, Sate Emergency Management Agency – SEMA, Nigerian Army): Care Aid has developed good relationships with all the Government authorities at STate and LGA levels. Although the humanitarian capacity of these official entities is extremely limited, YOYICOD will maintain the same level of cooperation with them, whose support is essential to ensure a proper project implementation. Village leaders/ Lawan & Bulamas (key informants, religious leaders, elderly people): YOYICOD as an CBO regularly works with the village leaders, who use to play a key role in identifying targeted beneficiaries within their communities, as well as in sharing information, monitoring activity progress and providing feedback. Following an owner-driven approach, YOYICOD will keep on involving the village leaders, who will be mobilized to ensure the participation and cooperation of their communities throughout the project implementation. Joint visit to the communitiesYOYICOD in consultation with local authorities will plan joint field visits to the communities in Bade. These joint visits will mainly focus on raising awareness, identification of project beneficiaries for interventions, and planning of field activities on monthly and quarterly basis Mobilisation and sensitisation of communities on peaceful coexistence, tolerance, violent extremism and survivors of Boko haram: this will create awareness in the community of their right and role in identifying and setting priorities on preventing violent extremism, counter radicalization and survivors’ re-integration towards peace building through their participation in the community Multi-stakeholder Dialogue Sessions: There will be dialogue sessions to enable the stakeholder understand the Disbandment deradicalization and VEOs messaging and misinformation process and contribute their quota in preparing communities participation. Capacity Building workshop: Capacity-building sessions will be held on monthly basis for 8 months curriculum to support selected youth to be peace champions and mentors to other youth. Topics may include, among others: SDG 16 and the role of youth, VEOs misinformation, leadership, good governance, conflict management and peace building, civic responsibilities, behavior change communication, advocacy, human rights, gender and women’s empowerment. This will build the capacity of traditional and religious leaders, social welfare officers to facilitate sensitization process such as youth platforms and radio programs. Radio programmes would focus on the positive role young people can and do play in conflict prevention, the prevention of violence, and the promotion and consolidation of peace. This session will create opportunities for youths to share goals and aspirations with adults as well as among each other, to capitalize on their experiences and assets, and to engage in multiple areas, including social, emotional, moral, spiritual, civic, vocational, physical, cognitive, personal and cultural development. Increase, understanding and opportunities for cooperation among Youths, parents and elders, in order to act jointly to prevent and resolve violence and transform conflicts.
