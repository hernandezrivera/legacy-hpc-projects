Start Date

01/11/2019

End Date

31/07/2020

Project Summary

The project is in response to clear and evident deterioration of Mali complex humanitarian crisis. The intervention will protect 6,000 girls and boys, aged 1 to 18, inBandiagara and Mopti Circles, Mopti region, from violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect through prevention interventions and referral support. This will be achieved through 1) creation of child-friendly space for which children can attend and grow without fear, and 2) establishment of community-based child protection mechanism, in which community members will be capacitated and aware of child protection concerns and work to protect their own children.
