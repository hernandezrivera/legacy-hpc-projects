Project Summary

The project “Improving Resilience and Living Conditions for Conflict-affected Population through Integrated PSS care” aims to address the problems of people living in eastern Ukraine in the Bakhmut and Avdiivka regions in Donetsk region. The project will secure reconstruction of 5 and equipment of 15 educational facilities and centres targeting children, parernts and educators. Residents of the Bakhmut and Avdiiv region will be assisted in addressing their psychosocial problems. Approximately 350 people (including boys, girls, women, men and youth, the elderly) will receive professional support for strengthening their resilience of post-traumatic mental health. At least 1,000 children who live in conflict areas will be able to develop in a safe environment and under the supervision of professional caregivers through access to regular didactic free-time activities. At the same time, at least 200 parents (including mothers and fathers) will receive expert psychosocial counselling focusing on "positive parenting" and addressing challenges in bringing up their children in the difficult conflict environment. The project will tackle the consequences of the long-term armed conflict and contribute to creating better conditions for the socio-economic development of Ukraine.
The project is submitted and implemented by People in Need Slovakia, which is the independent and stand-alone organization and the organization receiving primary funding.

1 - Needs

The project is responding to the needs of population living within 20km of the “contact line” and creates the access to integrated psychosocial service that help to restore population mental health and wellbeing of the conflict-affected population. Continuous exposure to hostilities, mine contamination and fear of military presence have a significant impact on the mental health of the population living in these areas. Children in particular are at risk of abuse, neglect, exploitation and violence. The project is particularly targeting children, youth, and families with small children.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

Activities and Outcomes: In relation to the main objective of improving the resilience and living conditions of people affected by the conflict in eastern Ukraine through material and psychosocial support the project raising up two main objectives: 1. Improve the living conditions of children living in conflict areas through access to renovated and equipped social centres2. Increase the psychosocial resilience of people (including children) living in conflict areas through quality integrated psychosocial support and counselling.The expected results of the proposed project will serve to achieve the following outcomes: • Children living in conflict areas have access to renovated and well-equipped social centres• People living in conflict areas have access to quality psychosocial support and counselling • Parents and children living in conflict areas are more psychosocially resilient thanks to didactic activities and positive parenting lessons.

3 - Indicators & targets

The project indicators are set as follow: • 5000 children will improve their living conditions thanks to access to 5 renovated and 15 equipped social centres• 1550 people (including children) will have access to psychosocial support and counselling, including:o 200 people will receive consultations focused on the resilience of post-traumatic mental healtho 150 people will receive consultations focused on solving work problems and issueso 1000 children will benefit from didactic activities in a safe environmento 200 parents will benefit from positive parenting lessons
