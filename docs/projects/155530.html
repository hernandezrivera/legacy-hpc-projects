Needs

The Anglophone crisis has deteriorated the security situation and affected the local economy in the Northwest and Southwest (NW/SW) regions. More than 430,000 Cameroonian have become internally displaced persons (IDPs) in the Northwest, Southwest, Littoral and West regions. Unemployment of people in the NW/SW regions and IDPs in the four regions is alarming and social cohesion and the mutual confidence in the NW/SW have been negatively influenced by the conflicts between communities and the government and among communities. The Anglophone conference planned in November 2018 has been postponed and the new date of the conference has not been fixed yet. Many IDPs in the Littoral and West regions rent houses. Basic services (e.g. water, health, education) are available but they need to pay for them. IDPs and vulnerable people, in particular in host communities need revenues to keep their life with basic services. Social cohesion and mutual confidence need to be improved. Women are more willing to participate in dialogue and focued groups than men who are scared by security forces and activists/separatists. The government, CSOs and media need to strengthen their capacity to respond to the crisis with promoting peace culture.
