Project Summary

The municipality of Bani Suhaila, and particularly the Abu Nasser valley, is an area at risk of flooding affecting more than 10,000 people including women, children, men, traders, teachers, passer-by etc. Each year in winter, recurrent floods damage houses adjacent to the valley and form stagnant water pools which make the access to public and UNWRA schools very difficult for children. As a flood mitigation measure, PUI will rehabilitate Abu Naser valley in Bani Suhila by building 1 km of drainage system with rain-water harvesting gullies and transferring the collected water to Khan Younis lagoon. Furthermore, PUI will use heavy machines to support municipalities to evacuate the stagnant storm water from the low level/flood-prone areas in the targeted area. During focus group discussions and key informant interviews, officials and community members expressed the urgent need to prevent the risk of flooding in the Abu Nasser valley. The planned Action has been coordinated with the WASH cluster, local municipality, CMU and INGOs working in the same sector, as well as the WASH focal point in Khan Younis.

Needs

In September 2017, PUI updated its assessment conducted in 2016 on Abu Naseer valley in Bani Suhaila. In collaboration with PUI, the municipality of Bani Suhaila held a consultation meeting with a number of people living and working in the areas surrounding Abu Naseer valley to discuss the issue of the frequent flooding, identify the needs, and propose solutions. The meeting took place in the meeting room of the municipality and was attended by local people living in the area, community leaders, men and women, head teachers of the affected schools, mayor of the municipality, municipality's engineers and PUI's representative. The main findings of the meeting were:• Changing the course of the valley due to the last wars is the main reason for the flooding and the forming pools of stagnant water. The pools of stagnant water led to the spread of insects like mosquitoes as well as infectious diseases.• The flooding of the valley threatens the passers-by, especially children, women, elderly and people with disabilities. The recurrent floods hinder the movement of people and vehicles into and out of the affected areas.• The limited access to the schools caused by the flooding of the valley led to the disruption of the educational process in the surrounding schools.• The flooding causes severe damages to the houses, properties, schools, and other public facilities.• The main facilities affected by the flooding include 3 public schools, 2 UN schools, kindergartens, mosques, poultry farms, car repairing workshops and a primary health care centre. More recently, PUI conducted a needs assessment in 14 municipalities in the Access Restricted Areas (ARAs) during August and September 2018. The main findings of the assessment indicated that some neighbourhoods located in the assessed communities are vulnerable to floods, which require an urgent need of implementing emergency response activities to mitigate the impacts of these natural disasters on people’s lives and livelihoods. In particular, children, women, and farmers are highly impacted by these floods as the flooded roads negatively impact students’ access to schools, women’s access to health care centres, and farmers’ access to their farmlands. Through the assessment, the municipalities were requested to identify the main challenges facing the population and the municipality during winter. They reported the following: flooding of rain water in the low-level areas; flooding of sewage manholes; flooding of the main streets during the heavy raining storms due to the blockage of drainage system caused by throwing solid wastes/garbage into it; flooding of agricultural not paved and muddy roads during winter which makes it difficult for farmers to reach their lands; and leakage of rainwater into houses covered by metal/asbestos sheets. Furthermore, the lack of fuel needed to operate drainage pumps and water wells especially during emergencies and the lack of manpower to carry out the routine maintenance works and/or the emergency tasks related to floods were raised by all the assessed municipalities.

Activities

PUI exchanged technical information on the needs of affected population with Action Against Hunger (AAH) as Focal Point of the WASH Cluster in the southern areas. PUI will work closely with its local partner NGO (Palestinian Environmental Friends), local CBOs volunteers in the 4 targeted localities and the municipalities of Bani Suhaila, Khuzaa’, Abassan Al Kabira and Al Fukhari. PUI will implement the following activities:Activity 1: Upgrading of Abu Naseer Valley in Bani Suhila through the construction of a rainwater harvesting system in collaboration with CMWU and the municipality (10,000 Individuals/1786 HHs).PUI in close coordination with the municipality of Bani Suhila will conduct a deep technical assessment of the needs in the targeted area (Valley Abu Naseer) in order to prepare the final designs and BoQs for the required construction works (rainwater harvesting and drainage system). The proposed system will be constructed through carrying out the following activities:1- Levelling works for the valley with spreading of Kor-Kar gravels to reach the design level.2- Supply and install 1km of 500 mm (20 Inch) UPVC pipes, manholes, and concrete gullies (catching water basins).3- Supply and casting concrete B300 for culvert rooms, ground beams and retaining walls to reinforce the valley sides in order to protect residence homes and assets.4- Supply and casting reinforced concrete 15 cm thick ground slab in the beginning of the valley in order to protect the valley’s entrance.5- Connecting the proposed rainwater drainage system to AL Qrara rainwater drainage system which delivers the collected rainwater to Khan Younis Al Amal lagoon. Activity 2: Support 4 local municipalities in targeted areas to enhance their emergency response capacity through the following activities (8,000 individuals / 1429 HHs):1- Rent of heavy machines for emergency interventions; a contractor will be hired to support the targeted municipalities with front loader machines to evacuate the stagnant water from the low-level areas and muddy streets, make trenches, and build sand dams to prevent storm water from reaching home and assets.2- Provision of Kor-kar gravels in the targeted muddy areas in order to reduce the possibility of water stagnation and to ensure the smooth movement of people, especially women, children and elderly, to their workplaces, schools, and primary health centres.3- Provision of fuels to operate the rainwater suction pumps and the tools needed for emergency response activities related to floods.

Indicators

PUI and partners’ M&E teams will develop a monitoring and evaluation plan at the beginning of the project to manage the process of assessing and reporting progress against project targets. This plan will describe the whole M&E system, including indicators, tools, breakdown of responsibilities and communication channels within the organizations and between them.• Monthly monitoring visits will be conducted to follow up the progress of project activities and to ensure that the activities are being implemented in accordance with the work plan developed at the beginning of the activity. Written report summarizing the main findings will be issued after each monitoring visit.• Introductory and information meetings with targeted population and community representatives will be arranged to provide accessible and timely information on organizational procedures, types of project activities, beneficiary selection process, criteria of selection, etc.• A complaints and feedback mechanism will be set up to make sure that the opinions of target population is heard and taken into consideration. Complaints regarding the beneficiary selection process or suggestions related to the design or the implementation of project activities will be timely responded to.• Focus group discussions and/or semi structured surveys will be carried out to assess the impact of project activities against baseline survey results.Indicators and targets:- Number of people protected from flooding (Target value: 18,000 individual including 800 students).- Number of kilometers of the pipeline constructed for the rainwater drainage system (Target value: 1).- Number of locations where gravels, tools, fuel and heavy machinery were provided to municipalities in order to mitigate the effects of floods in the low level areas (Target value: 4)
