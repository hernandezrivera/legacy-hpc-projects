Project Summary

Prior to April 15, 2023, fighting broke out between Sudanese armed force (SAF) and Rapid support force (RSF) in Sudan at 9 am in Khartoum after four hours later, the calashes spread-out in six (6) different states which including North state, North Kordofan, North west, central and south Darfur. Though Nyala is considered as the most coexisted in peace manner of it it’s community with dynamic in cultural and social structure that a unique state although, the escalated conflict between the two forces, devastate Nyala city. As a result, electricity was cut, businesses Centre was burnt down, looted and area that are around city center were plus INNGOs warehouse, Cars and valuable items were been looted too.
Mainly one of very serious challenging situation with the IDPs is that, they cannot support themselves during the fighting. Gender based violence (GBV) is experienced as one of the most serious and widespread protection threats to women and children in in Nyala. The report indicated, that in and around the towns of Nyala, there were various accounts that showed a clear pattern of forces unlawfully targeting civilians; - killings, rapes, random arrests, disappearances, torture, beatings, harassment, looting, burning and destruction of their property. The emotional effects of these challenges is immeasurable particularly for the women and girls, given the limited or unavailable emotional coping mechanisms for these categories of people. There are almost no direct group based and individual counseling services offered due to the porous situation on ground. Other emergency medical support services for specifically the rape survivors have been limited.
This project will provide group based and individual counseling to 2100 conflict affected survivors of gender-based violence, other vulnerable groups of women, girls, men, boys and children including war widows, orphans, former child soldiers, those with disabilities, those living with HIV/Aids and severely depressed persons. As a sustainability strategy and for community ownership of the project, 70 selected GBV survivors will be trained on basic Trauma counseling skills so as to continue serving as community peer counselors within their communities. 70 key stakeholders including the police, local chiefs, Islamic leaders, social workers, women, youth and community local leaders shall be trained on clinical management of rape as well as basic principles for providing emergency psychosocial support to the survivors of GBV. They will also be acquainted with the available referral pathway for GBV survivors. Community GBV male engagement groups and GBV clubs in schools shall be formed as a grass root-based advocacy and awareness strategy against Gender Based Violence. Peace on earth Sudanese Organization has conducted rapid assessment on GBV services during conflict period and has been implementing health, nutrition and protection activities in Nyala town including health centers (Nyala teaching Hospital and Turkish-Sudanese Hospitals). Peace on Earth, will be the services, through which GBV survivors will access relevant information and services including guidance and counselling sessions, dignity kits, Family planning, HIV/Aids etc.

1 - Needs

According to the assessment conducted, the team observed that there were key GBV findings. The most affected are children and women (separation, trauma, social destruction, abuse, exploitation, violations), in addition to deterioration of psychological health of women and girl. Also the closure of health facilities and private hospitals caused some risks for women , girls and those who have chronic illness, moreover those whom deliver babies, they faced a challenge to have access to health facilities, in addition to that there was internal displacement for some families from Hai Elwadi Area to Karrari area and other localities, the most challengeable factors that, faced humanitarian actors to deliver services during the clashes, was the looting of their stocks in their warehouses , offices beside the lack of resources caused an enormous gap in GBV. Similarly, the absence of confidential corner is seen as one of the biggest challenges to the referral pathway, and the other individual counseling. During the clashes there were many types of violence that had been committed included the following: • Economic violence for women and girls worked in markets, and burning of houses neighboring the compounds and clashes areas.• Physical violence for women and girl exposure to shoots and injuries. • Psychological violence for women and girls suffered from fear of guns sounds, and for those whom lost their relatives and family members during the armed clashes.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

The project aims at improving the physical and mental health of GBV survivors, increase community awareness on GBV, promote unity and peaceful coexistence through community social support groups and connecting survivors to vital support services and support areas. The project will also build the capacity of the key stakeholders to advocate against Gender Based Violence while being able to respond to and manage the needs of GBV survivors in their communities. It is designed to bridge the gaps in prevention of GBV, capacity of stakeholders and communities on GBV and strengthening the physical and emotional wellbeing of GBV survivors. The project will consolidate grass root GBV awareness and prevention initiatives through; community awareness raising and advocacy platforms to include community dialogues, formation of GBV groups and school based GBV clubs
