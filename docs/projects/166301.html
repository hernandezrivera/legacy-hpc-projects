Project Summary

The proposed project, to support hygiene promotion in response to Covid-19 and livelihoods stabilization among IDPs, returnees and host community, will target 50 schools and 8,500 women and girls, men and boys, from 8 Local Government Areas in Borno and Adamawa States. The project has three components:
1. WASH facilities for Covid-19 prevention – a total of 50 health facilities and schools will be jointly identified with relevant government bodies across Adamawa and Borno state. These health facilities and schools will be provided with water and sanitation facilities for hand washing to prevent the spread of corona virus.
2. Livelihood support - urban livelihood entails entrepreneurship training for 1000 selected youth (50% Male, 50% Female) across Adamawa and Borno state. The selected beneficiaries will range from ages 18 and older will be jointly identified and selected by the community and will be supported to establish small business.
3. Awareness raising for covid-19 - will reach out to 7500 women across the8 local government areas in Adamawa and Borno states. Each of these beneficiaries will be provided with information on symptoms and prevention measures for Covid-19 disease. A total of 1,500 households will be reached.

1 - Needs

Damages done to affected populations as a result of the Covid-19 global pandemic outbreak in Nigeria, especially northeast BAY states with already most vulnerable conflict-affected people, have led to loss of livelihoods, access to food and other basic necessities, by many.Lockdown measures to curtail the spread of corona virus has led to loss of income and poor economy as people could not go about their daily job which is the only/major source of income; people in the rural area, such as farmers could not access their farmland, hence leading to a reduction in production, resulting in food scarcity and inflation of food prices. An increase in crime rate has occurred, as people could not make ends meet due to loss of livelihoods and hunger.It is expected that food consumption will continue to deteriorate further as the vulnerable households approach the lean season, with the peak yet to come around June, July and part of August 2020. This exposes the vulnerable households to various coping strategies including the dangerous and irreversible ones such as selling off assets, debt, early/forced marriage and forced prostitution.Over 1.6million vulnerable people targeted in Borno state will be at risk of not accessing food if the lockdown continues, as partners and vendors could not reach beneficiaries on cash voucher and food interventions due to lock down in the state.

2 - Activities

Provision of WASH and Covid-19 prevention materials and supplies for health and school facilitiesJRS will work with relevant government authorities to identify health facilities and schools that are lacking water ad sanitation facilities and they will be prioritized based on needs. WASH facilities, including water points, hand washing liquid and hand sanitizer will be distributed across the selected health facilities and schools. Setting up of WASH facilities in health facilities and schools will help to promote good hygiene and washing of hand habits at the health facilities, among pupils and teachers, which will prevent the spread of corona virus at the health facilities and among pupils in school. Entrepreneurship / livelihood support Urban LivelihoodsJRS will provide business training to vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host community youth on the production of face mask, re-usable cloth sanitary pads, hand washing liquid and sanitizer. In addition to the business skills training, reconciliation activities through capacity building and experiential learning will also be included in the curriculum. The beneficiaries will be supported with start-up kits which will enable them practice what they have learnt and earn their livelihoods. The trained beneficiaries will also be engaged to produce part of the items to be distributed under close monitoring to ensure quality production. JRS programming will provide a secure market for the newly trained entrepreneurs as we will purchase the produced materials for distribution within our programs in the form of hygiene kits for vulnerable households. If successful, the funds received through HRP will complement our existing program plans of providing entrepreneurship training to 3,000 youth in Borno and Adamawa States. Awareness and dignity-promotion towards covid-19 preventionTo create awareness on covid-19 and its prevention, JRS will conduct awareness campaign where printed awareness materials on covid-19 will be distributed across project locations. Also, JRS will distribute protective items such as face mask, hand gloves, liquid hand wash and hand sanitizer to the identified beneficiaries. All these are aimed to reduce and prevent the spread of corona virus among the beneficiaries and within the community of operation at large.
