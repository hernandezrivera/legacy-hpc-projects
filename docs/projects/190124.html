Project Summary

OWDA will carry out this wash emergency response project in the Somali region's Horshagah and Garbo woredas. The project will last 9 months and will address the critical needs of vulnerable communities, primarily IDPs and drought-affected host communities, who live in the proposed woredas. The project will serve 38,200 IDPs and 10,816 host communities in the wash sector. This project has a total budget of $ 700,000 planned. 70% is planned for water supply scheme rehabilitation, 25% for sanitation, 5% for hygiene promotion campaigns and cross-cutting issues such as trainings on gender mainstreaming, AAP and PSEA.The project's major activities include the construction/rehabilitation of two shallow/boreholes and twenty communal latrines, the provision of WASH NFIs, the promotion of sanitation and hygiene, the strengthening of WASHCOs and latrine cleaning committees, and the provision of support for COVID-19 prevention.
