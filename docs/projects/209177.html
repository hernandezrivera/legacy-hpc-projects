Project Summary

#PRELUDE:
After War crisis in 2023 and up to now many Refugees and Sudanese People were displaced to a near boarders areas at Northern , Southern, Eastern and Western States of Sudan. This have add burden upon the Host communities, especially on Water demands, Sanitation and Hygiene.
During last June 2023 up to December 2023, EnvI and its partners carried simple need assessment and quick emergency need for WASH and Health, and Environment Interventions, specially within the House Settlements and Communities Hosts. The project also targeted the Non displaced communities as direct and Non -direct beneficiaries.
The main results and emergency needs were;
- To Ensure and Provide Sanitation and Hygiene services, with dignity and Gender Provision.
- Distribution of Hygiene kits.
- Solid Waste Sorting and Management.
- Environmental and Advocacy Campaigning.
- Intervention with Innovations and Environmentally Friendly Nature based Solutions.

#Grant Request JUSTIFICATION:
For better WASH, Health, Environment, and Gender through Advocacy and campaigning, Mobilizing the communities with fulfilling Education and Raising Awareness in the respective activities targeted, which are Highly needed in the selected locations. Despite of that “EnvI” has a huge experience with Environmental, WASH marker, Gender mainstreaming , and Elders perspectives within the aims of SDGs, will assure the Success and fulfill of this controversial Project with a Strategic vision, considering our Vision and Mission.
#Link with allocation strategy :
The proposed intervention will contribute to HRP objective SO1. Providing timely WASH lifesaving assistance to crisis affected & reducing mortality & morbidity; and SO3. Mitigate protection risks and response to protection needs through humanitarian access.
EnvI will develop effective feedback system to consider all voices of the target population, will activate the complaint mechanism to provide immediate decisions and will document the best practices & leaned lessons and will share them with all stakeholders & donors.
# The LOGICAL FRAMEWORK
Overall project OBJECTIVES:
In Targeting IDPs & host communities, and Non displaced People at 4 Localities Areas (Kosti, Tendalti, Port Sudan, and Um Dum), to access better water supplies & owns effective healthy sanitation services, and educate communities towards peace buildings. The project goal to have adhesive and concrete activities for the essential WASH needs. The project also classifies the Indicators for the activities targeted, with Info Analysis and Accountability System Mechanism.
#ACTIVITIES:
1. Installation & Rehabilitation of Water Pipelines & Systems:
Installation of New hand pumps, Solar bens, and Rehabilitate of hand pumps and its platform, and existing wells.
2. Construction and Rehabilitation of Emergency latrines, Bathing , Institutional and Communal hand washing facilities.
3. Construction and Provision of Waste Management and Water drainage Systems with New Innovations and Environmentally Friendly Techniques.
4. Provision and distribution of multipurpose cleaning tools; Soaps, Jerrycans, buckets, ibrigs, Menstrual Hygiene Materials (Reusable sanitary pads), disinfection and operational support for environmental cleaning at health/nutrition, education, and care facilities.
5. Training & Capacity Building:
6. Environmental, Health and Hygiene, and Public and Mass media Campaigns.
