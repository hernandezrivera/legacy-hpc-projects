Project Summary

This project will assist in the transition to recovery by improving vulnerable people’s access to shelter solutions, as GAH will be providing through this project transitional shelters to protracted vulnerable households and providing durable shelters for returnees and vulnerable people in North Darfour (targeting Alfashir, Allaiet, and Dar Alsalam),South Darfour (targeting Beliel and As Salama localities), and South Kordofan (targeting Abukarshola, Abujubiha, Dilling and Alabassya localities), GAH will also replenish the NFIs in the same states.
This project will also aid in the mitigation of the negative impact of hazards and ensure preparedness of disaster affected people in areas anticipated to be affected by crisis, as the project will be ensuring the sensitization of community members on best practices to mitigate the impact of natural hazards (floods) in Blue Nile state and will also raise the level of preparedness by The prepositioning of life-saving NFIs in key locations for timely emergency response in Blue Nile state and Khartoum state. the preparedness training for communities and the prepositioning of life-saving NFIs will also include Abyei area as conflicts tend to erupt regularly between residing tribes.
The project will also provide timely shelter solutions and NFI assistance to people affected by crisis such as floods in Blue Nile and Khartoum or armed tribal conflicts in Abyei area.

1 - Needs

The ongoing economic crisis continues to be a key driver of deteriorating humanitarian needs in Sudan Despite significant reduction in conflict in recent years, overall needs have grown considerably due to severe economic crisis, which has been further exacerbated by Covid-19, and flooding’s that affect 0.75 million people across 17 out of 18 state across the Sudan. The ongoing macro-economic challenges resulting in high inflation and currency depreciation which has led to shortages of fuel and cash, price increased of imported key commodities and medicines and subsequently reduced household purchasing power. The estimate of the number of internally displaced persons was revised upward to 2.4 million in 2020 – including newly accessed populations and estimates on displaced people in non-governmental controlled areas such as Abyei.There has been significant decline in armed conflict since 2015 with a declining number in new displacement in specific hot spots in Darfur especially with the inter-communal conflict between Massalit and Arab tribesmen in and around El Geneina town, state capital of West Darfur. However, in 2020, after initial progress key locations witnessed recurrent displacement such as in West Darfur Ag Geneina (9849 IDPS are estimated to be at risk according to the sector severity ranking), Kereneik(8905 IDPS are estimated to be at risk according to the sector severity ranking), and sporadic inter-tribal conflict in East Sudan among the Bani Amer and Nuba tribes Red sea state in Port Sudan and Sawakin ,At least 25 people have been killed in clashes between ethnic groups in a port city in eastern Sudan, according to the central committee of doctors. those clashes caused a new waves of displacement.Abyie still presents a unique humanitarian gap, its remoteness to service are barriers to the provision of basic services to north of Abyei communities, seasonal migrants, people displaced from South Sudan. In addition to the dispute between the two governments of Sudan and South Sudan, the area also has a multi layered chronic conflict involving the Misseriya nomads and Ngok Dinka farmers. This makes dire service gaps in all sectors. Sudan also continues to host over 1 million refugees. All populations are affected by floods annually, with 2020 being the worst rainy season in a century causing damage to houses, service facilities and destroying family properties. The Sudanese Ministry of Interior Affairs reported that the torrential rains and floods in the country have killed 114 people.on the other hand, Sudan is also suffering from disasters such as seasonal floods which poses an additional threat to the already vulnerable communities and causing displacements, injuries and potentially death.The Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) reported on September 2020 that the heavy rains and floods have affected more than 557,000 people in 17 out of 18 states. Floods and rising water levels have been reported in Sinja in Sennar State along the Blue Nile.Internally Displaced People (IDPs) – new, protracted, integrated and newly accessed, returnees and refugees need Shelter solutions and NFIs - plastic sheeting, plastic mats, jerry cans, blankets and cooking sets. These items help restore a minimal sense of dignity and protection against exposure to the elements, mitigate health risks and provide some privacy and security to those in need. More sustainable shelter solutions for returnees and protracted IDPs affected by not only recent conflict but also natural hazards need to be addressed.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Output 1: are prepositioned in key locations.GAH will start prepositioning of 1320 NFI kits in Blue Nile state and Abyei anticipating any upcoming natural or manmade disasters.Output 2: communities are sensitized to mitigate the impact of natural and manmade disasters.The next activity will be the sensitization of community members on best practices to mitigate the impact of natural hazards in the same localities, as GAH will conduct a training focusing on floods preparedness and response in Blue Nile state targeting localities most at risk such Tadamon and Rosaries, the training will also target Abyei area to include areas where tribal conflicts usually occur, the training for both areas will target 2000 individuals. Output 3:Disaster affected people receive timely assistance through NFIs and emergency shelters.GAH will procure and distribute 2601 NFI kits and 211 emergency shelters in Blue Nile and Khartoum states responding to floods and assisting newly displaced people (caused by armed conflict) in Abyei area. As those areas are considered high risk areas and often affected more by crisis and require urgent intervention. GAH will also convene regular Cluster meetings with all partners and attend relevant meetings at site level to share information with stakeholders, ensuring continuity and efficiency of response relative to the broader humanitarian response.Output 4: protracted vulnerable communities are provided shelter access:GAH will select vulnerable households through accurate assessments in collaboration with all relevant stakeholders in North Darfour (targeting Alfashir, Allaiet, and Dar Alsalam), South Darfour (targeting Beliel and As Salama localities), South Kordofan (targeting Abukarshola, Abujubiha, Dilling and Alabassya localities) for replenishment of 2311 life-saving non-food items and the Distribution of 1430 transitional shelters. GAH will also provide 520 durable shelters (material and tools) in Darfour and Kordofan states targeting returnees and vulnerable residents in an attempt to improve their shelter options for more sustainable ones.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

GAH as a protection based organization will ensure that protection mainstreaming is not only visible in all of the project activities but also implemented to the later. Consideration will be given to ensure that all services are offered with dignity. GAH will ensure that protection and GBV mainstreaming in all project activities, GAH will prioritize safety, dignity and avoid doing harm in all services are offered. GAH will avoid any discrimination in targeting, the community will be familiar with criteria of selection and services delivery process which will be consistent with their religious, cultural and traditions as will as the site will be located in a safe area with adequate supervision, safe spaces for women, safe areas for children waiting for their parents and safe and confidential complaints mechanisms in place to respond for any reported incidents of intimidation, violence and sexual exploitation (that have been committed by community members and/or staff).GAH will ensure gender balance in the project team to ensure that there are enough women available as a contact point for women in the community.GAH will provide special consideration to people living with disabilities, older people, children, women with caring responsibilities and others to be able to benefit from project interventions.GAH will avoid and monitor all unsafe livelihoods practices such as child labour, commercial sex work and risky movement to collect firewood or access markets.The nutrition services and facilities are designed to taking into account the needs of diverse groups in abyei, GAH will provide Nutrition staff with sufficient knowledge and skills to working with children.
