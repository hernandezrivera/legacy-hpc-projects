Start Date

End Date

Project Summary

Address the immediate causes of food and nutrition insecurity and improve access to sexual and reproductive health services for more than 35,000 of the most crisis-affected people in the Mora district (Mayo-Sava) and Kousseri district (Logone-et-Chari) Far North region."

Plan fields

1 - Besoins / Needs

Output 1 : will aim at improving access to nutrition, sexual and reproductive health services for young people, women and children, while placing the beneficiaries at the heart of the intervention, through a community approach. About 6,800 people will benefit from this Output.Activities : - Identification of beneficiaries for food assistance during lean season- Distribution of unconditional unrestrictive food assistance during lean seanson- Emergency unconditional and unrestrictive food assistance after RRM multisectoral assesments- Community sensitization on cash for food usage and the accountability mechanism - Monthly market monitoring Likewise, being a direct cause of food insecurity, the WaSH sector will be addressed by Output 2 to promote hygiene and increase potable water supply, to rapidly reduce morbidity and mortality as underlying causes of malnutrition. Approximately 40,000 people will benefit from these activities in new and former villages. Activities : - Signature of MoU with the Commune- Distribution of wash kits to households with a malnourished child- Awareness raising on good hygiene practices by community focal points- Construction of water points/ boreholes in health centres- Rehabilitation of water points- Recyling and training of community water point management committees- Water analysis Output 3 of this project will offer more sustainable solutions to socio-economic vulnerabilities to build resilience of households with acute social vulnerabilities or at protection risk, consisting of developing short-impact livelihoods and an emergency food assistance for recent IDPs affected by a shock (post RRM response). Approx 1,500 people will directly benefit from these activities. Activities : - Identification of beneficiaries- Establishment of an inventory of IGAs to be restored- Competency assesment- Capacity building of beneficiaries- Distribution of vouchers/cash/in-kind for IGA's kits - Follow up of IGAs and training- Training of VSLA promotors- Distribution of didactic material to te VSLA- Follow up of VSLA groups Output 4 aims at more equitable and better defined access to land, which is a core milestone in reducing protection needs. Local customary and decentralized administrative authorities will be capacitated, and ad hoc mechanism for the recording of local/traditional land transactions and existing land-based conflicts will be designed. More than 7,500 people will benefit from these activities. Activities : - Capacity building of local customary and decentralized administrative authorities - Designing an ad hod mechanism for the recording of local/traditional land transactions and existing land-based conflicts- Strengthening and duplication of village and communal consultation frameworks for the prevention/management of agro-pastoral conflicts and land rights- Identification of voluntary acquired rights by vulnerable host and refugee populations and mapping of land in rural areas- Formalization of agreements for access to land and monitoring of compliance with commitments- Micro-zoning of villages most at risk of conflict As a cross disciplinary theme, the project will reduce pressure on natural resources (water, land, etc.). That will contribute to the environmental sustainability of the intervention area in the medium and long term, and will put in place upstream a NEAT+ approach in the area.
