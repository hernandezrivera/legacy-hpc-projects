Project Summary

According to HNO 2021, there are about 2.9 million internally displaced persons in Somalia majority of whom have self-settled in over 2,400 sub-standard and unplanned IDP sites with poor living conditions and little access to basic services in urban, semi-urban and rural areas across the country. These people are living in precarious conditions with the majority not having their basic needs met due to inconsistent service provision or exclusion from accessing humanitarian support.
With a large proportion of humanitarian aid and support in the country targeting these IDP sites which accommodate majority of the most vulnerable populations in the country, CCCM partner expertise is required for coordination of site-level activities in addition to general service monitoring and site management support. HAPEN as one among the leading agencies over the years in the provision of services to displaced populations in camps across Somalia is proposing this project to reach out to 150,000 IDPs where it will focus to utilize its engagement with these camps to expand its role to include site coordination and management in Lower Juba and Gedo regions. The project will focus on IDP camps located in Kismayo, Afmadow, Doolow and Luuq districts.
The overall objective of the proposed project will be to contribute to improvement in conditions in
IDP sites by strengthening the predictability and effectiveness of service provision and protection for the displaced people. The project will seek to strengthen the coordination and information sharing between all relevant stakeholders.
With this project, HAPEN intends to establish new and inclusive community governance structures while strengthen the existing ones in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders in the aforementioned districts like the local authorities, other agencies and the cluster. The project will focus on creating coordination structures and mechanisms that will help the beneficiaries’ access different services in their settlements. Enhancement of communication channels to boost information sharing in the camps with other service providers in the targeted locations. There will also be the development of service mapping mechanisms to address the population movements, trends of new arrivals, exiting individuals and returnees into the IDP camps. This will be enhanced by engaging focal points in the camps - the focal points will conduct service mapping and record all the essential needs/gaps in the camps. HAPEN will conduct safety audits in the camps and share with the cluster on quarterly basis. The proposed project will enhance community communication and feedback mechanisms through construction of community information centers, employing community outreach mobile teams, establishing complain feedback mechanisms, installation of information boards, and conducting awareness campaigns.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

4.1 - Minority clans/ethnic groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance: The community leaders and respective committees will be fully involved in project implementation, monitoring and representation. HAPEN will ensure that project committees to be inclusive and that segments of the communities are well represented at all levels such as age and gender diversity. The project will be implemented in collaboration with all stakeholders while involving the beneficiaries. During community mobilization sessions, the project staff will provide detailed and an in-depth information on vulnerability-based selection criteria. Special attention will be given to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services through consulting them during the designing of the interventions. 4.5 - Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure and/or contamination by explosive hazards in targeted project area. HAPEN will seek to mitigate against this through integrating HLP and eviction mitigation activities into the project programming such as monthly eviction risk data collection, HLP/conflict resolution trainings for CMC members and referral mechanisms in case an eviction notice has been posted. HAPEN will also work with the cluster to seek to mitigate evictions and collaborate with protection partners, local authorities, landlords and communities to find alternative solutions.
