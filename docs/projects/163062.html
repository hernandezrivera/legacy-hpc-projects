Project Summary

The proposed action targeted cyclone affected, displaced persons in Chipinge and Chimanimani whose shelter units were destroyed by Idai. The proposed action will reach 7500 (3525M;3975F) individuals in need of support in rebuilding their shelter. Post cyclone livelihood restoration has been affected by severe food insecurity and harsh economic environment limiting government to support cyclone affected houses with shelter. Coming of the rainy season, will compromise housing conditions of vulnerable households failing to reconstruct their destroyed houses. The proposed action will provide material for transitional shelter construction and rehabilitation. A set of NFI that include a shelter repair/ maintenance kit. The action will provide capacity building on shelter construction making use of locally available resources. The process will involve provision of shelter repair tool kits.

1 - Activities

[Activity 1: Vulnerability Assessment, shelter construction training and provision of shelter construction tool kits].An assessment will be conducted to evaluate identify vulnerable households, map local resources, agree on shelter models that can be up-gradable into permanent shelter. Beneficiary participation will be guided by the age, gender and diversity approach to ensure a whole society approach that ensure individual rights are uphold. Targeted able-bodied men and women will be trained in shelter construction to build local capacities as well as provision of shelter construction and repair tool kits. [Activity 2: Support provision of and rehabilitation of transitional shelter to 1500 household].Cyclone affected households are either living in worn out emergency tents or in some shelter units in need of rehabilitation being at risk of destruction by the coming rainy season often characterized by violent hail storms. Most affected households lost their livelihood capacity and are in need of assistance to access building material for building decent accommodation. Guided by a shelter needs assessment covering nearly 2000 households, standardized up gradable transitional shelter units will be developed. A total of 1500 transitional shelters will be constructed and or rehabilitated, using locally trained builders. The action will provide construction material that include roofing material, timber, among other items, and individual targeted households will also mobilize local resources agreed upon. Highly vulnerable socio-economic groups will be supported fully with the action.
