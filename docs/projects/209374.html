Project Summary

The project will provide integrated WASH, FSL, General Protection and NFI support to South
Sudanese and Ethiopian Refugees in White Nile and Gedaref States. The project will
address the immediate needs of the refugees with the following interventions:
Awareness raising on CP and supporting Child protection networks
Capacity Building on CP and GBV mechanisms
Providing Household NFIs
Providing Households with clean and sustainable energy
Construction of latrines and providing hygiene promotion services

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

CAFOD is concerned with the broader impact of its interventions on safety and dignity of community members and their meaningful access to the assistance. To reduce misunderstandings that can cause conflict and mistrust and to avoid causing harm, strong communication and sensitization at all times so that all relevant stakeholders have a shared understanding about exactly what the objective of project is and how it is implemented, including its rationale/purpose, beneficiary selection, stakeholders’ roles and responsibilities, timelines, locations, and the rights of community members and beneficiaries. The data including access to assistance will be collected disaggregated by age, gender, and location or specific community to ensure safe access to everyone. To ensure meaningful access, special care and support will be provided in the case of child and women-headed households. This project looks to address gender issues and will ensure that girls and women including widows, female-headed households with two or more children under five, and pregnant and lactating women who don’t access to enough food on a daily basis are selected to benefit in project socialization, HH assessments, beneficiary selection and verification.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with individuals (in homes) through aid workers ,Face to face with individuals (in other venues/locations) through aid workers,Face to face with group consultations with aid workers,Face to face with community leaders with aid workers,Email,Complaints / suggestions box

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The feedback will inform the next steps in reviewing the delivery approaches of the project as per the community suggestions. This will include revision of delivery approach, selection of beneficiaries, and monitoring of activities with community support.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

To guarantee accountability and full participation of the affected population including children, the community will be consulted for culturally acceptable, safe, and accessible mechanisms in terms of diversity. This consultation will be done in form of rapid needs assessment, community stake holder's meetings, and service mapping.Moreover, CAFOD and its partners will establish various community project management committees to support the intervention. Training to be conducted in the project will provide specific inputs and will be devised to enhance women, girls, PWD, and elderly capacities while ensuring all groups are adequately represented in the groups of trainees and beneficiaries of project supplies and facilities. Increasedknowledge/awareness of the project and details of key contact persons will be provided on the strategically affixed signboards in the target project villages.Moreover, to further ensure inclusive participation of all crisis-affected people CAFOD will designate complaint and feedback focal points – one male and one female, in both locations to respond to traditional barriers in communication between women and men, limited access for persons with disabilities, and persons with chronic illness, and low literacy among the population in intervention. The feedback collected will be used to improve program quality as well as plan for future improvements in gaps that shall be identified. The feedback is intended for CAFOD to respond to sensitive complaints and establish a safe and confidential strategy for safeguarding communities from any forms of exploitation and abuse. The feedback/complaints out of CAFOD’s scope of work shall be used for lobbying and advocacy with partners as well as referrals.
