Project Summary

AMAN (Association for Mass Advancement Network) has developed the Shelter NFI for JRP 2022 with objectives to Provide safe and dignified living conditions and contribute to environmental sustainability Provide life-saving emergency Shelter/NFI support to households affected by disasters and other sudden onset events and Ensure that Shelter/NFI activities are inclusive and responsive to community feedback, promote peaceful coexistence between refugees and host communities, and enhance resilience. AMAN focused camp 17 as for MTS and Repair and maintenance of previously constructed shelter. Major activity for the project are; New Mid Term Shelters (150 sq feet- 300 sq feet) (estimated target 30% of total population) and maintenance of the existing MTS; Provide training: DRR/shelter reinforcement (estimated target Individuals 500) inclusion of youth and women in the training; HH level site improvement (including accessibility for people with disabilities) (40% of all HHs); Provide NFI through vouchers modality, through in kind distribution (40% of population) including support to EVI with porter assistance; Provide of HH level solar lights to Rohingya Refugees. (estimated target 100%); Provision of NFI or cash for NFI to HC (estimated target 1000); Provide emergency shelter support -RDV and distribution of shelter material (including EVI support with porters and labour) -(assumption for target: 20% of the population); Provide emergency NFIs according to RDV (including support to EVI with portering) assumption for target: 15% of the population and Involving women, older people, people with disabilities and other forms of gender diverse population of shelter activities (estimated target 3% of cfw). RDV NFI distribution is coordinated and distributed according to protection needs.
Next Year AMAN has the initiatives to enable the Rohingya refugees and vulnerable host communities in Cox's Bazar through providing Provision of HH level solar lamps as per the guidance developed by EEWG, HH level site improvement, Provision of NFI for emergency- for the protection cases to ensure privacy (curtains) and winterization (blankets) to 2000 HHs Rohingya community and 1625 HHs Host Community Value of the NFIs is calculated based on the GIK value above all involving women, older people, people with disabilities and other forms of gender diverse population reaching to 18671 individuals in the Camp 17 and Palong Khali - Ukhiya out of which 9549 host community individuals. Targeted beneficiaries will be selected base it on an independent assessment to follow a “Do No Harm Approach”. Separate data sheet will be used through questionnaire and need assessments also. To reach these targets, AMAN’s estimated budget is $676,130.
AMAN has experience in assisting refugees with shelter and NFI support since 2017. Next year AMAN is planning to build 500 MTS in camp 17 with the provision of treated bamboo based on the approved design. AMAN always emphasizes on capacity building training and its concerned staff has already attended in different training by the initiatives of Shelter /NFI, other sectors focal and stakeholders that is the strength of AMAN to achieve the targeted objectives to ensure project sustainability. Moreover, AMAN’s field staff will be trained in PSEA to ensure proper protection mainstreaming.
Gender responsive and peoples with disability with elderly people inclusion will be considered when and where applicable.
Systematic process of program Monitoring using participatory methods is what AMAN will do next year to deliver the project and to ensure beneficiaries inclusion in the project design and implementation. Before intervention any projects, we will conduct baseline survey, FGD, KII, etc. Collect and analyze and use disaggregated data and analysis on gender, age and diversity and equally consult with women, girls and marginalized groups, including during assessments and in the overall response monitoring.

1 - Identified Needs

Gaps have persisted across Shelter/NFI assistance for Refugees. Roughly three quarters of households reporting issues with their shelters, as well as roughly one in ten households reportedly having to make rent payments, and large proportions reporting having insufficient NFIs according to J-MSNA, 17 Oct 2021. This can be explained by several factors: a reduction of shelter activities due to COVID-19 containment measures (thus shelters were not repaired/upgraded for a year), heavy rainfall and winds during monsoon season and Cyclone, and the ongoing use of lightweight and temporary materials such as untreated bamboo and tarpaulin.Refd. to JSMNA 2021; households per shelter & NFI LSG severity score: None/minimal 15%; Stress 12%; Severe 72% and not classified as 1%. Households (72% of) reported shelter issues at the time of data collection; 12% of households reported having had to make rent payments in the 6 months prior to data collection; households reporting expenditures-on rent 9%, on fuel 51%; In the 3 months prior to data collection-on shelter repair 29%; On household items/clothing 55%; 36% of households reported not having made improvements to their shelter in the 6 months prior to data collection despite having reported issues. Lack of sufficient shelter assistance to make repairs was a common concern. Some shelter reported to have been completely destroyed (collapsed/flooded) – in particular, shelter located on hillside and in low-lying areas reported to be at risk. Households lack money to repair shelter themselves–some save on food or borrow to afford shelter repairs. Moreover, considering the increasing of population and limited land area, the construction of MTS (durable materials such as Iron footed treated bamboo) and accessibility with disability based on the approved design and in respect of the site planning is a need-based strategy.NFI likes Blankets, curtains, solar lights and emergency need according to RDV to be provided among targeted people. Due to the lack of electricity and malfunctioning of some solar panels, poor lighting in the camps creates an increased protection risk for women at night time. Gender Diverse & Inclusion of people with disabilities: The normative framework that governs the lives of Rohingya refugees unequally affects women and girls by limiting their mobility, their ability to make decisions about their lives and access lifesaving services, and deprioritizes their needs and demands compared to those of men. Women are excluded from decision-making systems in the community, and their lack of access to reliable information, consultations and feedback mechanisms limits their ability to influence both prevention and response plans. The inclusion of women leaders, networks and volunteers in the planning, implementation and monitoring of response plans will have a strong impact on making these right-based strategies, inclusive and effective. AMAN will ensure the most vulnerable households, including female-headed households, still have access to materials and support to make shelter improvements and to prepare for the cyclone season. Through the proposed project, AMAN will provide culturally and gender appropriate NFIs, including for those people with disabilities and other forms of gender diverse population.As per gender age group adolescent and women along with elderly will be provided training on GBV issues and above all social cohesion thus they will be aware of their rights and their roles in the Communities in regards of achieving Shelters and NFI objectives.
