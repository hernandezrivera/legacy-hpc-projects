Project Summary

This project aims to support the overall humanitarian response by enabling and enhancing access to beneficiaries and project implementation sites through a provision of air service.
Its main objectives are:
• To provide NGOs, UN agencies, donor organizations and diplomatic missions in Cameroon with safe, effective and efficient air service;
• To transport light cargo, such as medical supplies, information and communication technology (ICT) equipment;
• To provide adequate capacity for medical and security evacuations of humanitarian staff.
UNHAS was established in Cameroon in November 2015 to provide air transport services to the entire humanitarian community and their implementing partners principally NGOs, UN agencies, red cross/crescent movement, donor organizations and diplomatic missions. Government restrictions on UNHAS flights and destinations started in 2020 and culminated in the suspension of all flights in June 2022. Flights have resumed at the end of December 2022 but to the Far North region only. UNHAS will continue its advocacy in 2024 to offer safe humanitarian flights to all crises affected regions in Cameroon.
As the continued insecurity, vast distances and poor road conditions remain the main constraints to the provision of assistance by aid organizations, the continuation of UNHAS in 2024 will be vital in enabling humanitarian organizations to access the populations in need and project implementation sites.
