1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The intervention will target both host community and IDPs in Ibo. The objective of the project is to support with improved access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and a safe and healthy environment, contributing to reduce the risk of transmission of WASH related diseases for about 53,000 people in Ibo

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

- Rehabilitation of 3 existing wells and construction of cement paths for improving water points access in Matemo Island- Rehabilitation of the Rainwater Harvesting Systems (RWHSs) of two schools, construction of 2 new community RWHSs in Quirimba and Matemo Islands and improvement of the RWHSs for IDPs at HH level in Quirimba, Quirambo and Matemo Islands- Promotion of water filtration with handmade textile filters, water purification with Moringa Oleifera and solar water disinfection (SODIS) in Quirambo - Monitoring and support of the Water and Sanitation Committees activity in the entire Ibo District- Construction of emergency latrines and bathing facilities for IDPs in Quirimba, Quirambo and Matemo Islands- Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines in Quirimba and Matemo Islands and feces controlled disposal in Quirambo and Ibo Islands- Hygiene Campaign with a specific focus on Menstrual Hygiene Management- workshops for production of hygienic items - Monitoring and maintenance of the functionality of the 32 hand-washing stations installed by Istituto Oikos in community’s key points - Construction of household latrines for people with disabilities in the entire District

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Identification of potential risks is based on oikos experience in the area (present in Ibo since 2014), deep and trustfull relationship with local community and local authorities. All the activities have been verified on the ground and discussed with relevant stakeholders.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

people with disabilities have already be identified by OIkos in the framework of previous intervention in the DIstrict. List will be updated in collaboration with community leaders and local authorities. They will be specifically involved in the activity related to the construction of ad hoc sanitation facilities.
