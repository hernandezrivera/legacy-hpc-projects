Project Summary

8million people in south Sudan are faced by extreme effects of prolong violent conflict, climate change and devastating impact of COVI9-19 pandemic. 54% of the population are children faced with acutely malnourished, Women and girls continued to face extreme levels of GBV and psychosocial distress (SSHNO20 )incresed (DTM, IOM2020)returnees. Protection cluster targets 800,000 children experiencing extreme risk and threats and are in urgent need of accessing critical child protection services and humanitarian assistance7% persons with disabilities. Panyinjiar has been a home for the most displaced population from Southern Unity who moved through the river with canoes and this led to exacerbate the already worse off humanitarian situation. Mayendit County has in the previous years been experiencing sporadic community conflicts related to cattle raiding that led to displacement of people thus resulting to intensified communal violence and revenge killings (HNO2019). Leer County witnessed a high inux of returnees through the Adok port Medair-SMART survey, April 2019). Overcrowding due to displacement pose high health risks and inscurity,in n Mayendit county, the effects of devastating flood which has displaced over 80% of the local population mainly from southern part, destroyed livelihoods and increased the prevalence of water borne diseases in the county (IPC 2020) In Panyijiar County, over 5 health centre submerged by water have been abandoned and the displace. The the selected counties of LEER, Rukbokana Mayendit and Panyijar will continue to face acute food insecurity (HNO 2021) (IPC 2020) predicts households inability to meet food needs. In the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, children inability to access basic service such as health, psychosocial services, education further heightens children’ vulnerabilities, and pushes children and their families pushing families to negative coping mechanism such as child labour, early / forced child marriages, and child on the street (CPoAR ,CPISM2021). UNIDOR will adopt static Inclusive Child protection and Mental Health response plans to child protection concerns involving caregivers; aiming to protect children from violence, exploitation and abuse through improving access to lifesaving services. A total of 19,500 beneficiaries in 5 counties of LEER, Koch, Mayendit, Rubkona, Panyijiar, with data disaggregated by gender as Male=6750, Female=12,750. The project seeks to compliment, strengthen, expand and improve the already existing child protection interventions in Koch, Panyinjiar and Leer, in Rubkona UNIDOR is already implementing GBV protection /COVID-19 response, that requires additional finance capital human capital. It also focusses to expand child protection to Mayendit where UNIODR has active presence with Health and Nutrition projects. The project will strengthen community case management structure and build capacity of care givers and provide psychosocial support to adequately prepare and response crisis involving their children as well participate to monitor their children wellbeing during COVD 19 pandemic. UNIDOR will use its existing CFS to enhance comprehensive case management and cash based supported referrals for child SGBV survivors and support to child tracing and family reunification while ensuring child protection monitoring and mainstreaming into other sectors to improve the overall equitable access to basic services and Providing psychosocial support services through provision of integrated multi sectoral intervention. covid 19 will be mainstreamed

5.1. Cash and Voucher Assistance (CVA)?

Yes

5.1.2. Explain how the project design considered market functionality

Transport and transportation system is very weak in the selected project - hard to reach areas, water transport is majorly used by many. Road transportation become very expensive where it is available, and with constrained economic situation and lack of income for parents and caregivers of vulnerable children and GBV child survivors, it prove difficult for the families to afford /pursue medical or access to justice . Cash supported case management will be effective for vulnerable children and care givers meet this need,

5.1.1. What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash supported referral cases of SGBV- sexual gender base violence children survivors to assist the child access medical and legal aid. Most villages where the project will operate are very remote and means of transport are very limited. where they exists, they are charged exorbitantly that SGBV child survivors are most likely not able to afford, and with constrained economic situation in many rural families it will no longer be priority for the caregivers to pursue medical and legal response to child SGBV cases. By providing unconditional cash assistance to child SGBV /GBV survivors, the child survivor and care giver will be supported to follow up medical attentions as required within 72 hours and also be able to pay transport and any charges related to medical and legal aid-access to justice for the survivors.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

The project will be guided by UNIDOR PSEA policy to ensure beneficiaries are protected against sexual exploitation and abuse-PSEA. staffs including field staff and community volunteers will be trainned on PSEA to further create awareness to the beneficiaries on -SEA. during such training the information on reporting and communication pathway will be outlined and made accessible by the community . The PSEA focal person and project team will conduct PSEA awareness to the community and unveil SEA communication and feedback mechanism for UNIDOR. The organization through project provide information of PSEA focal point person and hotline( mobile contacts, for reporting allegations and incidences of SEA towards beneficiaries.All partners in contact with beneficiary must be informed of PSEA and child safeguarding policies. People with organizations contracts (Suppliers, Contractors, Consultants) must also be informed and sign PSEA and child safeguarding policies to protect beneficiaries.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

The project will ensure functional inclusive communication and feedback mechanism is in place. through diverse selection of communication and feedback mechanism that will address any communication barriers assessment and facilitate unique communication needs of the identified beneficiaries including those that may reduce access to information for persons with disabilities and older persons. Project staffs and particularly those managing -AAP information/protection desk will be trained on disability inclusion to best facilitate inclusions of persons with disabilities.There will be awareness raised to the general public on disability inclusion and how to facilitate communication and feedback barriers of reporting incidences and cases affecting all gender and ages.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

In reference to the organization's' accountability, communication and community engagement framework, the protect team will ensure engaging community through out the project cycle. from the participatory needs assessment through to evaluations and learning and dissemination of project lessons learned. By Conducting project launch and involve key stakeholders to discuss objectives and project planningorganize project review and feedback meetings with the community's active participation. provide feedback communication boxes where communication/suggestions , boxes will help the beneficiaries to drop their Feedback and complaints, the boxes should opened and messages dropped reviewed on regular basis, and feedback communicated within reasonable time as guided by organization communication policy and as appropriate. Complaints should be addressed appropriately using the defined communication protocols which should be easily accessible by the community. The organization will engage Accountability Affected Persons officer(AAP-officer to monitor and coordinate accountability process, laisse the organization, project and community communication feedback.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

The feedback and all information gathered from beneficiaries will be recorded, analyzed and action taken to response to the feedback. beneficiaries will be involved in the response process keeping confidentiality principle at all times. The reports will further inform the project of success realized, risks and threats faced by beneficiaries to better plan mitigation strategies depending on types of feedback received.
