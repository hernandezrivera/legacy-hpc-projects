Project Summary

Phase 1:
a. In-person and remote protection monitoring using data-collection tools in hard-to-reach communities for the indentification of persons with heightened vulnerabilities, protection needs ans trends as well as gaps in the response. Production of protection monitoring reports and dashboards for regular dissemination with all humanitarian partners and Government.
b. Two-way communication with communities and strenghtening acocuntability to affected populations. Engaging affected communities, community leaders and key informants in the collection of protection data, community feedback and identification of vulnerable individuals for referral to available services. Facilitating in-person or remote focus-group discussions and household level assessments where appropriate.
c. Leveraging technology and innovative solutions, such as remote protection data collection in inaccessible communites using technological tools, building and keeping up to date community-level key informant database for collection of protection data and rapid collection of data on needs following incidents of violence.
Phase 2:
a. Referral of individuals with identified needs to available services using existing referral mechanisms. In adidition referral pathways and case management systems will be kept up-to-date, strenghtened and capacity of case management actors enforced to ensure adequate follow of cases of persons with specific needs (PSN).
b. Identification of protection gaps and needs in the provision of follow up services to identified protection cases and capacity building of key institutional actors and law enforcement agents on key aspects of Protection.
In this regard, UNHCR will partner with and support OHCHR in deployment of dedicated expertise to guide assessment and capacity building requirements and engagement with relevant justice institutions
Phase 3: Community-Based Protection (CBP)
Selection and training of community-based protection focal points (PFPs), both in person and remote. Capacity building of PFPs to dentify and refer cases within the community to service providers. Dissemination of life-saving information within communities, especially among persons with heightened vulnerabilities. Strenghtening community engageent mechanisms, such as protection desks, focal points, focus group discussions and awareness raising on existing community feedback and complaint mechanisms.
and ensuing advocacy, leading the Prot cluster)
Phase 4: Distribution of CRIs as part of the protection response of UNHCR, with special focus on individuals with heightened vulnerabilities as identified through monitoring activities.
Phase 5: Advocacy on the centrality of protection, human rights and specific needs of IDPs within the humanitarian community and with authorities. Strategic leadership of the Protection Cluster and the National Community Enagegement and Accountability to Affected Populations Working Group to mainstream protection within the response.
