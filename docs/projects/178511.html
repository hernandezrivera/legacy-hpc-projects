4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021, regarding Food security at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure population as 1.7 million people living in camps are deemed to be 100 percent food insecure, and 1.8 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity.SKT conducted a rapid need assessment (RNA) in 41 camps in Harim and Salqin locations. The methodology of data collecting included key informant interviews (KIIs) with camps managers, members of the administrative committees, sector experts, and representatives of local authorities (LA). The selection of key informants was based on the nature of each section in the assessment and took into consideration that the local authorities, community leaders and camps managers are commonly trusted resources for this information. As per the findings: 1- Almost all people in surveyed camps faced difficulties accessing enough food due to: - No resources/income to purchase food. - High bread prices. 2- A majority of people purchased on credit or borrowed to obtain bread. 3- Looking for income opportunities, sharing items, borrowing items, and taking credit / going into debt were the most common things people do when they could not meet their basic food needs. 4- When asked about their needs and priorities, the majority has defined bread bundles as their priority need. Based on the above, SKT will provide life-sustaining food assistance (GFA) to 8691 HHs in need of critical and sustained food assistance in 41 camps through GFA. The GFA will be supplemented with bread bag distribution for each family for a period of three months. Regarding the targeted beneficiaries.

SKT always works in close coordination with communities, LCs, and relevant stakeholders to ensure fair selection of the beneficiaries using selection criteria and therefore, maintaining a good relationship with a range of concerned stakeholders and strengthening transparency as well as SKT will "1- SKT will start by announcing a tender/quotations to select the best bakery in the target area. 2- Printing bread distribution cards with a serial number that matches the SKT database.3- Delivery the cards by hand for each HHs on a monthly basis, 4- Complete the procurement process of needed flour from Turkey, and transfer it in collaboration with the Turkish Red Cresent. 5- Re-handover the flour in the Zero point in Bab Alhawa gate, by SKT team to be transferred to the bakery, 6- SKT will provide the operational cost for the bakery and the needed to provide the nylon bags.7- Complete the distribution of bread for 23 days per month in the targeted locations for 8 months.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk1: SKT during implementing the activities of the project may face these risks according to the project's activities. Protection risk 1: crowding of women during the distribution of bread, Likelihood: Low, Mitigation: SKT will hire females for the distribution process. Risk 2: Risk of harassment of women and girls by the humanitarian workers. Likelihood: medium, Impact: high. Mitigation: establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and enhancing accountability mechanisms besides training the staff to deal with beneficiaries in addition to providing workers with training on humanitarian standards and signing code of conduct. Risk 3: in distribution area we may face a risk of harassment for female beneficiaries by males beneficiaries.Likelihood: High . Impact: High . Mitigation: we will conduct separated distribution timed for males and females.
