Project Summary

According to the most recent global overview of the humanitarian situation (HNO 2022), 4.2M People are in need, of whom there are 1,013,548 Internally displaced persons (IDPs),446,415 refugees and 557,886 returnees, while 500,000 people are affected by floods.
In 2014, Cameroon had one of the highest maternal mortality rates in the world. It stood at 782 maternal deaths per 100000 live births (WHO 2014). Thanks to the efforts of the government in collaboration with multilateral donors like WHO, WB, GIZ, KOICA and UNFPA, within the confines of the ‘campaign for the acceleration of the reduction of maternal mortality (CARMA), this figure reduced to 406 deaths per 100000 live births (WB 2022).
This Action aims to implement life-saving activities for internally displaced and vulnerable women and girls at risk, in the context of armed conflict and COVID-19 in Cameroon. The interventions under this Action aim to reach 1,013,548 internally displaced persons and 2,000,000 members of host communities with comprehensive sexual and reproductive health services (ANC, Emergency Obstetric and neonatal care, mobile clinics, maternal waiting homes and family planning). Furthermore, the Action will ensure the implementation of infection prevention and control measures (IPC) in 100 more health care facilities (in the 10 regions of Cameroon including humanitarian regions) to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality and morbidity as well as the risk of transmission and spread of covid-19 and HIV in hospitals and in local communities
The Actions interventions will cover the 10 regions of Cameroon ( IDPs are now found to be in all the 10 regions of the country).
Through this Action, 1000 midwives and other service providers from 100 health facilities will be recruited, trained, and redeployed. Two hundred newly recruited Medical Doctors in the targeted Health Districts will benefit from capacity building in modern cesarian section techniques, IPC, prevention of postpartum hemorrhage by the systematic use of misoprostol. Commodities and equipment for infection prevention and control will be procured and distributed to the health facilities. Emergency reproductive health kits for the management of rape and the safe management of deliveries and their complications will be procured and distributed to the last mile of all the targeted health facilities. This will also include the renovation and equipment of 100 labor and delivery rooms to improve the technical platforms of 100 health districts
In addition, this Action will help to facilitate access to quality remote and timely medical and reproductive services using online app(cloud technology) thus linking the IDPs and host communities to qualified health care providers

1 - Besoins / Needs

Cameroon Humanitarian Response Plan 2023 Fields map1 - NeedsThis project aims to reduce Maternal mortality and strengthen the quality of the SRH response in Cameroon as a whole and the humanitarian regions. Outcome 1: Integrated SRH services are available and accessible to women and girls.Activity 1.1: Provide SRH services, including remote service delivery, where appropriate; Activity 1.2: Provide health facilities with inter-agency emergency sexual and reproductive health (IARH), including emergency contraception (EC) to prevent unwanted pregnancy and post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) to prevent HIV infection;Activity 1.3: Organize integrated mobile clinics to provide services to hard-to-reach IDP sites where beneficiaries do not have access to services; Activity 1.4: Set up safe equipped and functional maternal waiting homes; Activity 1.5: Conduct community outreach activities focused on access to services, especially available SRH services. Activity 1.6 Recruit, train and redeploy 1000 midwives to 100 health facilities to provide high quality delivery services, making sure every delivery is attended by a skilled personnel.Activity 1.7: Organize refresher courses for 200 medical doctors in EmONC, FP,Post abortion Care and MISP,Activity 1.8: Procure 100000 doses of Misoprostol tabs for fight against PPH,Activity 1.9: procurement of 200 smart phones for remote consultation and Data collection. Outcome 2: Engage men, boys and community leaders in dialogue to improve the use of SRH services including family planning services and encourage them to accompany their wives for ANC.Activity 2.1: Community dialogues are conducted in targeted communities with community and religious leaders to ensure community engagement in the fight against unwanted pregnancy and abortion. Activity 2.1: Conduct capacity building activities on SRH service delivery by traditional birth attendants in the detection of risk factors and early referrals, available services and human rights approach to promote social change in favor of women's sexual reproductive health rights, targeting specially men and boys as allies in SRH service utilization. Outcome 3: Creation and running of functional EmONC networks to enhance high quality service delivery, and early referrals as well as risk mitigation. Activity 3.1: Conduct EmONC monitoring in targeted localities. Activity 3.2: Organize mentorship programs amongst these networks to encourage continuous hands-on training and formative supervision. Outcome 4: Cash voucher assistance to 2000 vulnerable pregnant women to encourage service utilization. Activity 4.1: Provide vouchers for transportation, access to services and feeding in temporary maternal waiting homes. Activity 4.2: support the running of the maternal waiting homes with basic furniture and stipend for the attending midwives. Outcome 5: Increased capacity of local women-led organizations to implement Maternal and neonatal death and morbidity audits and response programs. Activity 5.1: Support academic institutions and professional societies involved in women's health and rights organizations; to enhance mentoring, coaching and professional development. It also aims to strengthen SRH coordination through information sharing, capacity building, advocacy and synergy between actors.
