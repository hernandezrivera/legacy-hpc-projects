1 - Needs

In the midst of the Boko Haram insurgency, covid-19 pandemic has suddenly emerged with potential threats to millions of livelihoods and thousands of households in Borno State. As at 03.05.2020, 75 confirmed cases have been reported in Borno State with 11 deaths (NCDC). This could get worse with the effect of the insurgency even more severe, food very scarce while access to WASH facilities and healthcare almost impossible for even the most elite. The people at the receiving end are the most vulnerable being women, children and youths who constitute the most poverty stricken and at risk population through livelihood assistance and ensuring that peple's food needs are adequately met. Furthermore, the closure of schools has led to many children most especially girls at risk of dropping out of the education system and being subjected to a more prolonged domestic work.

2 - Activities

The project aims to support Livelihood stabilization for marginalised populace ; Increase capacity of local partners, host communities to identify, analyze and respond to early warning signs of violence, conflicts and hazardsPromote inclusiveness & Peaceful co-existence among IDPs, Returnees and host communities within the host community in Borno State through the following:-Community awareness and campaign on hygiene and safe waste management and disposal initiatives-Providing entrepreneurship training for the vulnerable people-Establishing small scale businesses with cash grants or in kind support-Food and livelihood for the marginalised and most vulnerable populace-Capacity building and support for local government & civil society staff to enhance governance & service delivery Other activities include: 1. Procurement and Dissemination of preventive COVID-19 messages, flyers, posters, stickers on COVID-19 in 4 LGA's in Brno State 2. Procuement/ Distribution COVID-19 prevention kits (liquid hand wash(sanitizer), plastic buckets, soap bars, face mask and hand gloves to at risk Communities (5000 for each of the 4 LGAs) in Borno State 3- Cash transfer of $50 per Household to assist the lockdown effect on the community of the LGAs (100 beneficiaries per LGA as a cushioning effect and as a pallative measure to the lockdown. 4. Radio/Social media campaigns to create more awareness on the covid-19 scourge5.Provision of educational support for our of school children via the radio and social media-Procurement of Covid-19 prevention bill boards in target communities-at least one banner in local language per community.
