Project Summary

PROJECT DESCRIPTION :
The on-going “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the North West/South West (NWSW) Regions of Cameroon which break out since 2016 has resulted in increased displacements of people.
OCHA sitrep_nwsw_august_and_september_2022_final data : 2.0 M People in need of assistance ; 1.4 M Targeted for assistance ; 600,336 IDP ; 419,734 Returnees (former IDPs and refugees) ; 85,967 Cameroonian refugees in Nigeria. Sources : Humanitarian Needs Overview/Humanitarian Response Plan 2022 ; MSNA NWSW, OCHA, August 2022 ; MSNA West, Littoral, OCHA, September 2021 & UNHCR, September 2022.

DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION : Plan intervention in 09 Subdivisions of 20 villages in rural, urban/semi-urban in Nkambe, Ndu, Ako, Batibo, Widikum, Bamenda II, Wum, Upper Bayang/Mamfe in 5 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Momo, Mezam, Menchum/Manyu) in the NWSW Regions of Cameroon. Targeted Population: 30,000(dissag. by gender, age, disability of 5,980 Boys/6,020 Girls; 5,500 Men/6,000 Women) >18yrs; Elderly (1,980 Men/2,020 Women) +60yrs, disability (1,240 Men/1,260 Women).

AIM: To improve the capacity of the community member’s knowledge’s, local authorities, traditional leaders and civil society actors on HLP rights by the end of 31st December 2023.
Affected population need an emergency humanitarian assistance for live-saving/alleviate suffering/have the right to (life, dignity, protection, security/to receive humanitarian assistance on the basis of need without discrimination), avoid exposing them to further harm/leave no one behind.
INDICATORS :
# of individuals sensitized on HLP rights and responsibilities
# of individuals receiving case-specific counselling on HLP
# of persons trained on HLP related matters
These will ensure the affected population to known their HLP rights and responsibilities and counselling on HLP related issues.
ACTIVITIES:
-Sensitization of community, local authorities, and traditional leaders on HLP rights and obligations
-Provision of individual counselling on HLP related issues
-Training of local authorities and traditional leaders and civil society actors to effectively address HLP related disputes
STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES:
-Reduce the protection needs of people affected by crisis
-Improve the protection/respect of fundamental rights for persons affected by crises, prioritizing the most vulnerable groups (including PLWDs, elderly, women and children).
-Build capacity of Cameroonian authorities and civil society actors on the protection of affected populations to ensure respect for rights.
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs, returnee’s/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 50% IDPs, 10% PLWDs, 30% vulnerable host communities/10% Returnees.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations.
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through community based approach, use collaborative/ADR procedures i.e. (Mediation, negotiation/Arbitration), awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization), radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials, collaboration with stakeholders, community’s mobilisation/engagement. Organized workshops/M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers, local management, ownership. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs, KIIs, field visist/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Engage MINJUD, MINAS, Municipal Council/advocacy actions.
SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained (CPCs for continuos sensitisaton on HLP rights and responsibilities ; local authorities, traditional leaders and civil society actors on HLP related matters).
JUSTIFICATION :
The community member’s, local authorities, traditional leaders and civil society actors lack the knowledge’s on HLP related matters. Need more capacity building on HLP related issues.
