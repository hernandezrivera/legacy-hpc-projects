Project Summary

The Project aims to continue supporting pre-existing Youth and community center in Northern countryside of Aleppo, the center provides several activities in the mean of empowering members of the community especially youth and increase access to moderate values with enhancing their resilience and increase the positive engagement and involvement within their community.
SOH carefully conducting activities in which helps reaching for the previous principals,
the main activities of this project is :
- Provide 16 Life skills trainings (Langues, administrative skills, Managment skills, leadership and computer) for 400 youth male and female (16-24) years old.
- establish two networks/groups from youth (male and female) from the age of (18-25) and provide them with civic education trainings.
- fund and support to two initiatives developed and conducted by the two networks/groups .
- provide 12 cultural lectures/ seminars at the center .

Needs

Depending on the needs assessment conducted by SOH, NGOs and the UN agencies, all of the outcomes and recommendations stat that focus should be shifted towards youth.Space of Hope have been working in Education for the past 4 years, trying to help youth to continue their study but unfortunately the absence of universities for some areas caused many adults to stop going to schools. The situation led us to think about vocational training for those who need to be offered an income generating solutions. Vocational Training workshops will be providing essential knowledge for Adults to encourage them to build their future which will keep them away of military groups (for boys), and of early marriage (for girls). Aside of the vocational training we will provide cultural & social learning, which would help our communities to think properly about the necessity of being responsible towards their country, and how important is saving people lives while you can but not knowing how to make it.If we check all educational activities during the conflict, we can notice the weakness and shortness in developing civilians specially those who are in low level of Income. No opportunities nor realistic solutions have been used to save those people’s future. We believe that our civic educational efforts can improve others opportunities as well (others=similar initiatives), and will give better understanding for the uneducated people to become responsible & collaborated, and the most important thing: people will learn how to pick their choices of building their future away of extremism.

Implementation of activities

depending on the experience gained by implementing similar kind of projects, and taking into consideration the pre-existing canter, the first step will be conducting needs assessment in coordination with the local authorities to determine the specific vocational and life sills trainings needed in the area of implementation.preparation and development of activities with specific work plan will be the phase two of the project,beneficiaries will be chosen and have a role in these phases, the center will be basically rehabilitated and fully equipped with the needed materials .a schedule/program for the center will be set and be followed closely by the staff and the activities will start accordingly .monthly reports will be prepared and submitted with closely work of the M&E staff.the activities that will be provided is : - Provide 16 Life skills trainings (Langues, administrative skills, Managment skills, leadership and computer) for 400 youth male and female (16-24) years old.- establish two networks/groups from youth (male and female) from the age of (18-25) and provide them with civic education trainings.- fund and support to two initiatives developed and conducted by the two networks/groups .- provide 12 cultural lectures/ seminars at the center .

Protection Risk Analysis

Mainly these kind of projects contains a lot of complexity since it is evolve youth, our plan is to depend on the communities needs and demands for an economical improvement, this may cause a stable and continuous level of semi informal education for those youth without forgetting that the security situation can force any project in any area to delay/stop.
