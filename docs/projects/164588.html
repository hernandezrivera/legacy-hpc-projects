Project Summary

The project aims to support female-headed households and women with special needs in Syria, through vocational training and small business projects.
100 beneficiaries of female-headed households aged between 18 to 40 years of host community and IDPs will be selected to learn vocational and skills for three months, this training will enable them to increase their opportunities to access decent jobs and supplement family incomes.
three months of training will be organized in Sarmada and Bab Alhawa.
The average time for each course is about 100 - 125 training hours.
The project will provide the following courses:
-livestock and dairy production
-Textile and garment manufacturing
- cooking includes ( making deserts - cookies....etc)
-Art includes ( handicraft making, drawing, Hairdressing, …..etc)
after the end of the training, INSAN CHARITY will support 50 small -business projects for 50 trainees who participated in the training. The cost of each project is 1200 $. Also, INSAN CHARITY will follow – up all the trainees after the end of the training and try to integrate them into the labor market through Communicating with the local market, partners, and donors.

4 - Needs

Almost a decade into the crisis, the main drivers to food insecurity are conflict and protracted displacement, loss or lack of livelihoods and reduced production capacity and economic sanctions on the country. These factors have resulted in limited physical and financial access to food, soaring prices and inflation, contributing to reduced purchasing power and continuous livelihood depletion for the most vulnerable. This is evident from the recent market trends which indicate that the national average price of a standard reference food basket at USD 77 based on formal exchange rates is more than 8 times higher than pre-crisis levels. In December 2019, the reference food basket had increased by 17 percent month on month and 43 percent year on year while the Syrian pound had depreciated by 43 percent on the informal market in December 2019 compared to December 2018 and by 24 percent compared to the previous month. Overall the Syrian pound has depreciated by more than 1700 percent since the crisis began highlighting the overall impact on market prices and reducing purchasing power as food prices remain high. According to need assessment done by INSAN CHARITY for female-headed households in different areas In Idlib governorate about small - business projects priority.45 % of women stated that they prefer training on livestock and dairy production while 30% of women stated that they prefer textile and garment manufacturing10% of women stated that they prefer hairdressing15% prefer others

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

risk: sociocultural norms that limit women to work.mitigation measure : this project respects the privacy of all women who participated in this project by providing work inside the house and allow including members of the family to help the headed of the household.risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls: mitigation measures :1. Establish a confidential complaint mechanism2. Train staff on code of conduct 3. Code of conduct in all staff contracts4. hiring female staff. Monitoring the behavior of workers on site, and making sure they are abiding with humanitarian principles and code of conducts Address any SEA complaintsrisk: difficulties for women with special needs to access to training centermitigation measure: transportation to and from the center will be available for freerisk of child labor: mitigation measure :- the project empowers women to work to prevent any exploitation of their children at work. - The project will target just women who age between 18 to 40 years old. It will not accept any female under 18.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1) risk: Dropping Outs of some participants from the training.mitigation measure: Sign MOU with the trainees that includes the necessity of commitment to attend the training to the end.2) some trainees are not qualified to learn or to work.mitigation measures :selection of participants will be based on specific criteria. 3) Unavailability of qualified and technical expertise as instructors. mitigation measure: Share and consult with vocational training institutions and use references for trainers who will conduct training.4) Unstable Security may cause delays in training.mitigation measure: relocate project for a safer area.5) Loss of interest, motivation in training and lack of commitment to attend training for some trainees. Mitigation measure: (encourage participants through informing them of providing grants to best trainers )6) difficulties in marketing products of small - scale projects.conduct marketing courses through the training that explains ways of marketing for participants.7) selling the items of projects.mitigation measure: signing MOU with participants that refer to refusing of selling any items of the project for one year. and in case of inability to complete the project activities, the project will be moved to another beneficiary.
