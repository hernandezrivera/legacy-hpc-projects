Project Summary

Immediate Life saving Activities:
• Immediate kick starts of mobile clinics/Brigades by restoring access to primary health care services through mobile brigades (Mobile clinics) among the affected population especially in the hard to reach areas.
• integrating WASH activities to include waste management, hygiene promotion and infection prevention & control in to the supported health facilities and mobile sites in the hard to reach areas.
• Enhancing the availability and utilization of reproductive health and family planning services, incorporating PMTCT, with a future intention of referencing SCI common approaches on Saving New-born Lives, Integrated Community Case Management and FP/PAC.
• Supporting elements of community WASH, to prevent WASH related communicable disease outbreaks.
• Supporting the Immediate mitigating risks around endemic diseases and common conditions among affected population including malaria, pneumonia and diarrhea/cholera.
• Working with Health cluster and partner’s organizations to ensure proper organization of immediate life saving the activities and that the needs are met.
Aiming to target 305.000 people
