Project Summary

Aseel Women Organization (AWO) is a Sudanese NGO founded in 2017 by a group of women in different parts of Khartoum, and was registered in 2019 and became a national organization in 2021. The organization is working in women and children issues, raising awareness and educating the community including capacity-building and support for women and children from displaced individuals.
AWO aims to develop a mapping and data analysis solution for displaced individuals gathering points across several states in Sudan. This project seeks to address the critical need for accurate and up-to-date information on gathering point in the region to assist NGOs in providing essential services to displaced individuals.
The solution will be a comprehensive system to map and manage data related to gathering point.
The platform will include Google Maps integration, Geographic Information System (GIS) mapping, and a centralized database containing vital information about each gathering point, including (GP name, total number of displaced individuals, segmentation, contact information of officials, detailed gathering point information and messaging and reporting features for emergency communication), additionally, the platform will facilitate data entry forms for health, education, shelter, psychosocial support, livelihoods, income, food and nutrition, and demographic data. These forms will enable regular data updates for analysis and reporting purposes.
Sending Reports & Emergency Message:
1. Sending Reports:
The "Sending Reports" feature enables gathering point officials to compile daily reports that capture events and progress occurring within the shelter gathering point. These reports encompass vital particulars across various facets of life and society within the gathering point, encompassing aspects like health conditions, nutrition, housing, and daily activities. For instance, these reports might encompass details such as the count of meals distributed, recorded health instances, and recreational undertakings. Subsequent to drafting these reports, they can be uploaded onto the platform, where concerned organizations and relevant authorities can access and download them. This enables a comprehensive evaluation of the prevailing situation, facilitating the formulation of necessary interventions.
2. Emergency Message:
The "Emergency Message" functionality empowers gathering point officials to instantly compose messages intended for immediate dissemination during emergency scenarios or critical incidents transpiring within the gathering point. These messages effectively convey swift and imperative information, ranging from health crises and security issues to any developments mandating an instantaneous response. Once an emergency message is composed, it is promptly transmitted to the platform. This ensures direct access for beneficiaries and authorized entities, enabling them to swiftly retrieve the information and take requisite actions.
Project Objectives: the primary objectives of this project are as follows:
1. Create an accurate mapping and data analysis system for displaced individuals to easily locate gathering points
2. Ensure the collection and continuous updating of information on gathering points
3. Develop a comprehensive database accessible to NGOs, containing crucial and current information
4. Enable streamlined data analysis to enhance the effectiveness of NGO interventions
Expected Outcomes: upon successful implementation of this project, we anticipate the following outcomes:
1.1 Increased and Improved accessibility for displaced individuals to locate gathering points
2.1 Timely update ensuring information remains current and relevant
3.1 Improved coordination and response times
4.1 Increased accessibility for NGOs to critical data facilitating rapid decision-making

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection mainstreaming is a crucial aspect of our project design and implementation to ensure the safety, dignity, and well-being of displaced individuals in Sudan state. The following elements highlight how our project incorporates protection mainstreaming:1. Inclusive Data Collection:The data collection forms embedded in our platform include specific categories for demographics, including information on women, men, and children. This allows for a nuanced understanding of the composition of displaced populations, by capturing data on vulnerable groups, such as women and children, our platform ensures that the unique protection needs of these individuals are recognized and addressed in the provision of essential services.2. Privacy and Confidentiality:The platform emphasizes the importance of privacy and confidentiality in handling sensitive information about displaced individuals, access to the system is restricted through password authentication, and data is only accessible to registered and authorized organizations.gathering point officials are trained on the ethical handling of personal data, ensuring that information related to vulnerable populations is treated with the utmost care and respect for privacy.3. Gender-sensitive Reporting:The "Sending Reports" feature allows gathering point officials to provide daily updates on various aspects of life within the displacement gathering points, this includes reporting on health conditions, nutrition, and housing.The platform encourages gender-sensitive reporting by requiring data on women, men, and children separately. This ensures that the unique needs and challenges faced by different gender groups are recognized and addressed.4. Emergency Communication and Safety:The "Emergency Message" functionality is designed to enable gathering point officials to communicate urgent information during emergency scenarios. This includes issues related to health crises and security incidents.The swift dissemination of emergency messages through the platform ensures that timely and critical information reaches the relevant authorities and beneficiaries. This contributes to the overall safety and protection of displaced individuals.5. Comprehensive Gathering Point Information:The detailed information provided on each gathering point page includes key personnel responsible for overseeing operations. This information ensures that there is clear accountability and a designated point of contact for protection concerns.6. Training and Capacity Building:Aseel Women Organization, in collaboration with partner organization, will conduct training sessions for gathering point officials on protection principles and mainstreaming, this includes understanding the specific protection needs of women, children, and vulnerable populations, by building the capacity of those involved in the project, we aim to enhance their ability to recognize and respond to protection issues during the implementation.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The site mapping and data analysis project recognizes the valuable role of community feedback in enhancing its effectiveness and responsiveness. The following strategies outline how the project will actively seek, analyze, and incorporate community feedback to continually improve and adapt its activities:Establishing multiple channels for community feedback, including but not limited to community meetings, suggestion boxes, and dedicated communication platforms within the platform itself.Utilizing local community leaders and influencers as intermediaries to gather feedback from different segments of the displaced population, ensuring a diverse range of perspectives are considered.Conducting regular community consultations to discuss the functioning of the platform, understand community needs, and identify challenges faced by the displaced individuals.Ensuring these consultations are inclusive, with a focus on engaging vulnerable groups, women, and children to capture their unique experiences and perspectives.Designing the platform with a user-friendly interface to encourage active participation and feedback from gathering point officials and community members.Implementing periodic usability assessments to identify any challenges faced by users in navigating the platform and addressing these issues promptly.Establishing a dedicated team responsible for analyzing community feedback systematically.Generating regular reports summarizing key feedback trends, concerns, and suggestions, and sharing these reports with relevant stakeholders, including NGOs, government agencies, and the displaced communities themselves.Use community feedback as a foundation for adaptive programming, allowing the project to modify its activities based on the evolving needs and priorities of the displaced population.Integrating a flexible project design that can accommodate changes and improvements based on the feedback received during different phases of implementation.Conducting capacity-building workshops for community members and gathering point officials to empower them to articulate their needs effectively and participate actively in providing feedback.Ensuring that community representatives are included in decision-making processes to foster a sense of ownership and involvement in the project. Establishing transparent communication channels to inform the community about how their feedback has been received and acted upon.Providing regular updates on the project's progress, changes implemented based on feedback, and future plans to maintain trust and accountability. Integrating cultural sensitivity in feedback collection methods, recognizing and respecting cultural norms and practices that may influence how individuals express their opinions.In conclusion the project team will ensure that feedback mechanisms are thorough accessible to all community members, regardless of linguistic or cultural differences.
