Project Summary

IDPs in South Darfur State remain the most vulnerable people in the Greater Darfur due the old humanitarian situation and the new confrontation of the sides of the Government troops. According to the humanitarian assessment reports issued by Un Agencies, INGOs and NNGOs during the period of 2022, IDPs in camps settlements in and around Nyala town and the localities still lacking physical WASH, FSL, protection including housing. The recent inter-government war between SAF and RSF occurred in April 2023 in all Sudan, made the life situation of IDPs critical. More than 600,000 IDPs in the 6 camps of Nyala and about 450,000 citizen moved from their houses to seek refuge with their families or elsewhere in the South part of Nyala and other localities in South Darfur such as Kas, Edel Fursan, Mersheng, Bileil, Niteiga and Elsalam localities.
The WASH project will target those who are vulnerable based on needs identified by need assessment and verification process conducted by NNGOs initiative, HAC and State Ministry of Health and SMoWF conducted on May 14th and 15th, 2023 in Nyala city and the surrounding localities: Kas, Elsalam, Edelfursan, Bileil, Niteiga targeted HH 30,613 (153,613 persons) including 67% female households, 7% Children households and 26% male. This assessment report is shared with OCHA staffs, IOM and other humanitarian Agencies which still have staffs in Nyala. During the confrontation started on April 15th, 2023, many water facilities are consequently damaged or destroyed. IDPs are lacking sanitation and hygiene services in where they took refuge.

1 - Needs

Great Family Organization (GFO) together with Humanitarian actors conducted assessments in the targeted areas of protracted IDPs and newly displaced where located in South of Nyala town, North of Nyala town, and the localities of Kass, Edel Fursan, Niteiga, Bileil and Elsalam in South Darfur State. The findings of the recent assessment conducted by the humanitarian actors who are in the area: NNGOs Initiative, HAC camp coordinators and the line State Ministries: Social welfare and Health indicated that the situation of the vulnerable people including IDPs and Nyala citizens who newly displaced around the town and to other localities is very weak. Due to the reduce of the humanitarian donation the lifesaving assistance particularly water and sanitation make the life situation very hard in addition to lack of food and NFIs/shelters .153,065 (HH 30,613) vulnerable people are found lacking water and sanitation services including others humanitarian needs as reported in the assessment findings.The project is designed to address the needs of this population to access clean and safe drinking water including sanitation and hygiene services. Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) situation is deteriorating due to worsening economic crisis and political instability.The majority of the water points are found non- functional in the three localities because of lack of rehabilitation services because of lack of humanitarian donation and also the poor budget allocation by the Government or the local community because most of the population in the areas are either IDPs, returnees or poor host community are mainly women and children who have no capacity to repair the water points. 2 - Activities/Outputs

The project is designed to respond to the WASH need of IDPs and also these population groups are under the threat of WASH-related epidemics, severe acute malnutrition (SAM), the COVID-19 pandemic, and water resource-related conflicts. As per the HNO 2023, 10.9 million people (excluding Refugees) who fall under the above categories need humanitarian WASH. Therefore, WASH Sector's overall objective is to increase access to humanitarian WASH services for 4.8 million most in-need populations with a focus on building their resilience to WASH-related recurrent shocks and an emphasis on community empowerment and engagement of women, girls, and young people as enhanced enablers. GFO have sub- offices in the 7 localities with full personnel to address these activities in time. The project will target the vulnerable protracted IDPs in old camps and the newly displaced people from Nyala town during the Government confrontation in South Darfur State, in the most severity affected indicated by the revised HRP of 2023.Great Family Organization will conduct activities of lifesaving through provision of water supply by: 1- Rehabilitate of 30 Hand Pumps in the 7 IDPs’ location.2- Rehabilitation of 17 Mini Water yards 3- Construction of 4 water yards 4- Provision of water quality control material: Water Chlorination material for HH and water points.5- Improve sanitation and environmental health interventions to vulnerable IDPs, returnees, refugees and host communities in the 3 localities through capacity building.6- 153,065 persons will access basic water services.7- 153,065 persons of crisis-affected populations will have access to hygiene promotions and sanitation services.8- Beneficiaries' committees are formed and trained in water safety and water source management. 9- Sustaining water quality and quantity through forming spare parts center and water tariffs.
