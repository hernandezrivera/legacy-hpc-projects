Project Summary

The worsening conflict along the Amhara-Tigray regional border is further increasing the number of IDPs. Currently, an estimated 225,000 IDPs are located in volatile and conflict-prone areas in North Gondar, Central Gondar, South Wello, South Gondar, Awi zone, and various areas. According to the NDRMC around 4M affected are in needed of urgent support while some are in areas inaccessible. There is a limited humanitarian relief presence in North Wello and South Gonder, where urgent humanitarian needs are being reported. Access is especially constrained in areas that are still under siege and there is little information to guide planned interventions.
The impact of displacement has rapidly exhausted positive coping mechanisms and affected well-being. Limited access to WASH NFIs, inadequate sources of safe water, inadequate sanitation facilities and prevalence of poor hygiene practices can render the populations living in collective centers/sites at risk of preventable diseases and overcrowded and congested IDP sites also pose a high level of risk to waterborne diseases, such as cholera. Assessments indicated that integrated WASH services are critical to ensure that facilities are maintained at a good standard and can be scaled up in case of population influxes, both in terms of the provision of safe water and increased hygiene promotion to prevent disease outbreaks.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1.1: Conduct introductory meetings to share clear communication about the selection criteria and ensure that all stakeholders understand the process. The Project prioritizes IDPs living in the collective center and the cluster also recommend supporting this. The meetings will involve Woreda and Kebele representatives, IOM, DPO and representatives of the affected communities (both IDPs and host communities). This meeting will take place initially before listing any potential beneficiaries, transporting of items and providing assistant to the specific kebele. Key points to be addressed in the introductory meeting include:• Explanation of the purpose of the assistance: all stakeholders should be clear on who the target group is and why.• Identify and agree on targeted locations within the Woreda. The meeting should priorities and select areas for the specific registration of beneficiaries. • Agree on committee members for the actual registration of beneficiaries and prepare a plan/schedule for registration in in each location. Activity 1.2: Beneficiary identification, registration and verification will take place once a committee is established for registration of beneficiaries. The relevant parties from local authority woreda DRMO, kebele officials and committee will work as a team, with DPO, to do the registration. This will reduce challenges during verification; however, independent verification will be conducted by DPO. Complaints and feedback mechanisms will be incorporated into the selection process.Activity 1.3: Transportation of WASH NFIs: IOM will procure and provide the hygiene kits to DPO. Activity 1.4: Distribution of WASH NFIs: 1,000 hygiene kits will be distributed for 2,000 IDP and 5,000 non displaced HHs. All team members will be trained in safe distribution practice which also considers COVID-19 prevention and mitigation during distributions. During the distribution, DPO will install hand washing facilities at the gate of the distribution site and also, provide hand sanitizer to ensure beneficiaries and distribution teams either washes or sanitizes their hands. Activity 1.5: Conduct PDM survey After at least 2 weeks of actual distribution of hygiene kits, DPO will conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) survey to assess the effectiveness of kits utilization and its suitability. Sample population from the beneficiary group will be presented with follow-up FGDs and HH based questionnaires to gather actual first-hand data on the feasibility of intervention. Furthermore, beneficiary complaint mechanisms put in place will be further monitored which will serve as an input for enhancing future program implementations. The survey report will be shared with the relevant stakeholders.
