Needs

The province of Idlib is hosting the highest number of IDPs in Syria . It is estimated that almost half of the 2,9 Million people in Idlib are internally displaced and half of them are children. Most of the families live in poor housing conditions (informal settlements/unfinished buildings) with no regular electricity or water supply and also the provision of food and basic medical health facilities is inadequate. Moreover the host communities are overwhelmed by the numbers of IDPs. The infrastructure and the administration/financial systems are overstretched and are completely insufficient to cope with the huge burden of IDPs. More and more informal settlements announce that they cannot take any more refugees. It is estimated that 1,39 Mio people in Idlib are in need of humanitarian assistance and 350.000 people are in dire need of humanitarian help. An assessment done by the WASH cluster in 2018 revealed the complete insufficient provision of drinking water in Idlib and North Aleppo. It is saying that where only around 13 per cent of the population is served exclusively via piped water The study shows that water trucking is the only source of water for 35-45 per cent of the population in Idlib. This situation also results to an increase in water-borne diseases. Studies of the CCCM for 2018 show that WASH interventions have the highest priority in almost 30 % of the informal settlements

Protection Risk Analysis

risk: Risk that target areas are inaccessible or insecure; or that staff or beneficiaries may be injured, killed or kidnapped by armed groups during the implementation of activities because of advance of armed groups and/or aerial shelling • Mitigating measures: 1) Strategic targeting of areas which offer highest humanitarian needs with highest potential for accessibility based on conflict trends analysis. • Constant monitoring of the security situation; extensive acceptance measures to sustain good relations with communities for a continuous understanding of local context and to facilitate unimpeded access to target areas; engagement with WoS (whole of Syria) to devise contingency access plans. Risk : Risk of theft of NFIs and chemicals such as chlorine/Sodium Hypochlorite which may be attractive to armed groups inside Syria.AN will provide feedback and accountability mechanisms to users;Work closely with all partners on the ground; Risk: Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities.MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities. Risk: Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases.Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Fighting between opposition groups:AN will suspend activities until fighting stops and the situation becomes clearer. AN staff will hibernate until further notice. 2- Government forces shelling:AN will freeze all activities immediately. All staff will hibernate at the safest and nearest location until further notice.3- Local hostility towards INGO’s:AN will immediately suspend activities and hold discussions with local actors and authorities on the ground.4- the Specific targeting of Arche nova staff members: AN will suspend activities immediately and discuss the situation within the organization and with the local actors on the ground.
