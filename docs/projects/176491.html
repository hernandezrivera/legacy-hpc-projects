Project Summary

Lake states is one of the 10 states of south Sudan that has experienced a lot of conflict arising from house hold incidents to organized large inter communal conflict and revenge killing. This has left the communities divided in lakes state. Women and children suffer more the effects of violent conflict. In march 2020 clashes among communities in Rumbek North county resulted to displacement of 2738 individuals (408 households) who fled and found refuge in Den-Nhial site due to cattle raiding that made many people to lose their lives and property. The Conflict is motived by different issues such as high bride price for marriage forcing youths to launch attacks in order to acquire cattle for marriage , conflict about grazing lands, water sources during dry season, farming land, prejudices existing between different clans for long years without being resolved in peaceful non violent ways, weak law enforcement in place to promote accountability and respect of diversity within the communities . The same cases of violent conflict continue to affect Rumbek East county of Lake state in past years arising within Rumbek East county or from bordering counties of Yirol East, Yirol West, Wulu and Rumbek center which is bordered to Panyijiar County of unity State. Humane Aid for Community Organization based previous peace building activities in lakes state intends to facilitate the promotion of peaceful co-existence and respect of diversity in Lakes State through various activities including establishing community dialogue platforms, conduct series of awareness on negatives consequences of violent conflict and advocate for nonviolent means of conflict resolution, Conduct training for community leaders on peace building, conflict Management and prevention, Conduct conferences and public lectures on traumatic issues that contribute to conflict such as land and property disputes, Organize peace festival for youths including exchange visits in different payams to promote peaceful co-existence and respect for one another, organizes series of engagements meeting on peace with different groups to better their understanding on social coexistence, Strengthen local existing intercommunal structures to mediate conflict in nonviolent ways with focus on South Sudan relevant laws that protect land and other properties and legal ownerships. As well as Dissemination of key messages within communities on human rights and information that promotes peaceful co-existence and changing attitudes that prolong and increase violent conflict in the communities in Lakes State.

1 - Needs

The Repeated clashes and localised violence in Lakes State have affected the peaceful coexistence between communities. In July 2020 there were reported clashes between two communities, resulting to 16 fatalities, 04 wounded and 03 females reported to have been raped, and displacment of 2,098 Households. The displaced population comprising mainly women and children, hosted in the nearby villages of Pulapuot, Liether, Amenduol, Majok, and Adou village of Mapourdit Payam, the biggest number of the displaced persons were reported to have camped in St. Daniel Comboni Primary School, located in Mapourdit Payam. The most affected affected areas include Mapourdit Payams, Aluakluak Payam, Mabui and the Jur area of Mvolo (IRNA-Lakes State, July 2020). The main driving factors for the armed fighting are cattle raiding and retaliatory attacks. The aforementioned instances indicate there is growing conflict among communities of lakes state majorly inter-communal conflict (Localised Conflict/violence) such as cattle raiding, revenge killing and attacks, fighting about grazing land rights, prejudices on historical matters and high pride price forcing young men to look for cattle to get married in ways resulting to increasing and exporting violence; cattle reselling including crossing to neighboring states counties with the same issues. The Localized violence also includes States bordering lakes State and in some cases displacing or importing insecurity to the neighboring states. The Conflict has forced people out of their homes, schools have been disturbed for children, girls and women have been raped or forced for marriage as compensation of lost ones and general economic activities have weakened, thus undermining peaceful coexistence. HACO aims to promote peaceful coexistence among communities conflicting in lakes counties of Rumbek North and Rumbek East including their neighboring counties through encouraging all communities participation in peace activities that will restore hope among them and live dignified lives with full respect of human rights.

2 - Project activities

Humane Aid For Community Organization will conduct the following activities to promote and support peaceful co-existence programs among lakes communities 1) Conduct engagement meetings with different groups of people in different payams to understand communities understanding on peaceful co-existence and also to inform them of the involvement of all members of the communities to participates in the peace process,2) Conduct training on conflict prevention and response using nonviolent means, also inclusive will be topics on land and property rights according to South Sudan Laws and ownership since land disputes is identified as one of the main sources of conflict in South Sudan,3) Conduct a series of dialogues through community participation in dialogue platforms to discus on issues that promote violent conflict such cattle raiding , land disputes, prejudices among others 4)Conduct conferences and public lectures on peace by involvement of expertise on conflict resolution and prevention including in the area of land disputes resolution, family conflicts on inheritance that fuel tension later, cattle raiding among others to transform community attitudes to value individual rights,5)Organize youth exchange visit program including foot ball matches organized, traditional dances organized in different locations to promote coexistence and respect of diversity,6) Establish and support Peace committees in each location through regular coaching and mentoring to act as be the platform for peaceful coexistence activities and promote peace within the community.7) Conduct series of awareness raising campaigns on negative consequences of Violent conflict through radio programs and outreach programs with key messages on preventing and responding to issues that fuel conflict within communities using appropriate measures such as legal process or alternative dispute resolution mechanisms,8) Strengthen community structures on mediation , conflict resolution, especially on the contradicting traditional laws and National laws and policies that protect individual rights through workshops and seminars. 9. Support Womens and youth Groups (as Agents of peace) with CASH vouchers. 10. Protection monitoring and assessments.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

HACO SEA focal person in Juba will establish PSEA Committee at field level to create awareness and dissemination of information and consequences of SEA and reporting procedures. Beneficiaries will be informed on their rights to humanitarian assistance which is free of charge throughout our program implementation activities.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

Focal persons for each complaints and feedback desk will always be from local project implementation site where the project is taking place. The project deliberately targets, women, girls and persons with disabilities as principle victims of localised violence. For those who will not read or write, they will be given oral confidential information through the focal person and monitoring team will conduct outreaches to reach others by adhering to the principle of “leave No One Behind".

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

The project will be introduced to the beneficiaries about the overall goals, objectives and implementation plan for the purpose of transparency ,and accountability with involvement of key stakeholders. Beneficiaries will be knowledgeable of all the setup of implementation and established complaints and feedback desk to receive information and respond to beneficiaries on any new challenges and improvement during the implementation

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

HACO program team will collect the information from the Complaints and feedback desk and analyse them and later respond to the needs to avoid affecting implementation of the project by allowing community participation in decision making for improvement in program outcomes.
