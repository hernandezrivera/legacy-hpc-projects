1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The nature of the conflict has displaced populations from several villages to hard-to-reach forest areas, as well as to neighboring towns where they have little access to health care services and live in very poor conditions. In addition, several health facilities in both regions (NWSW) are no longer functional because of attacks and prolonged shut downs. Many health facilities in the NWSW regions are non-functional due to insecurity and attacks on health care, and these health facilities no longer provide vaccination services . This has greatly affected the health system. It has disrupted health service delivery and disease surveillance capacities in these regions. Throughout this intervention, DEMTOU Humanitarian will leverage its expertise in epidemic preparedness and response. DEMTOU will train 250 frontline health workers in order to detect, verify, and respond to public health events through training and mentoring on disease surveillance and community surveillance . The project will provide smartphones to frontline health workers for data collection and reporting. It is envisage to enhance timeliness and completeness of health facilities reporting to district and regional levels through regular supervision visits and mentoring .
