Project Summary

The project of Improve access to water and promote sanitation & hygiene services among IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in Central Darfur State which is 1 year project. This project will target 14,000 people (male 3276, female 3612, elderly 1512, boys 2744 and girls 2850) as follows 5000 returnees (male 1170, female 1290, elderly 540, boys 980 and girls 1020), 7000 IDPs (male 1638, female 1806, elderly 756, boys 1372 and girls 1428) and 2000 residents (male 468, female 526, elderly 216, boys 392 and girls 2408) in Um Dukhun, Mukjar and Nertiti (West Jebel ) localities of Central Darfur State. The 14,000 targeted people ( 2585 people (male 605, female 667, elderly 279, boys 507 and girls 527) will receive water services, 3415 people (male 799, female 881, elderly 369, boys 669 and girls 697) will be provided sanitation services and 8000 people (male 1872, female 2064, elderly 864, boys 1568 and girls 1632) will receive hygiene services within and outside the camp). The project will focus on rehabilitations of non-functional hand pumps, up-grading of the hand pumps to mini water yards, training of hand pumps mechanics, refresher trainings of water committee, drilling of bore holes and equipping and provision of water facilities & campaign awareness. There will be provision of sanitary kits including towels for 1500 (women and adolescents girls in and out of schools) through core pipelines in the 3 localities of Central Darfur State each 500 beneficiaries per locality.

Needs

HNO 2019 indicates that the impact of the conflict in Darfur, South Kordofan and Blue Nile States has been widespread displacement with some 1.86 million IDPs (male 911400 and female 948600).According to recent estimates from the government of Sudan as of November 2017,the number of IDPs has reduced from 1.99 million to 1.86 million.Based on revised estimates there are about 324000 returnees(158760 male and 165240 female) who have voluntarily returned to their places of origin.In Darfur over 624000 people(305760 male and 318240 female)returned to their place of origin since when conflict in 2013 started. Inter-communal conflict particularly in parts of Darfur report by UNAMID 2018 also decreased in intensity and conflicts between farmers and agro-pastoralists communities over access to and use of resources primary land and water.It is estimated that out of 2.8 million people in 11 states need humanitarian WASH assistance which includes people without access to potable water,sanitation and hand washing facilities.These were caused by factors such as the current economic situation,malnutrition,diseases outbreak and limited water resources. Across Sudan,WASH remains an urgent humanitarian priority.Surveys done at IDPs camps indicate that water service varies from 7.5 to more than 20 liters per person per day,with an important number of them are still below the global SPHERE standards.This involves prioritization of water use within the household and resorting to use of unsafe drinkable water.There are humanitarian needs for construction of new and rehabilitation of households, public and communal latrines which are gender sensitive and consider disability and age in the same localities mention blow.The hygiene promotion was identified as a priority for targets with at least 1 hygiene promoter for 500 persons.This project will target 14000 people(male 3276,female 3612,elderly 1512,boys 2744 and girls 2856) in Central Darfur State.The project target in Mukjar 3285 people(male 1610 and female 1676), 1600 people(male 784 and female 816) in Um Dukhun and 9115 people (male 4466 and female 4649) in Nertiti localities each will receive water, sanitation and hygiene services collective. SORC will work in coordination with UNICEF,IRW,TGH,NEF,CRS and IOM in the targeted localities.Women and children are the primarily the ones who fetches water among IDPs,returnees and vulnerable host communities in Central Darfur state.Long distances to water points expose them to potential physical and sexual abuse within this context of instability.Lack of latrines at the household and community level has resulted in increased risk of across contamination and the spread of illnesses,special consideration in design of water sources for disable,children, elderly and lactating women for easy access.The loss of trees affects soil fertility,contributes to erosion and reduced groundwater level which resulted to degradation in the environment.

Impact on environment/natural resources

Cutting of tress for construction of pit latrines at household's level affects the environment where loss of trees affects soil fertility, contributes to erosion and reduces underground water level which resulted to degradation in the environment. Upgrading of water sources to water yards requires power. SORC will mitigate by introducing technology of using solar panels for energy to pump water avoid air pollution, disseminate environmental messages to raise awareness during trainings and campaign on benefits to support sustainable use and maintenance WASH facilities. SORC further will encourage use of environmental friendly materials for constructions and rehabilitation where compensation will done by planting additional trees.
