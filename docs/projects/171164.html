Project Summary

The projections of recent FSNAU assessments reveal that there is a dire need to gear the humanitarian efforts towards addressing the risk of malnutrition and similarly the projections done earlier in 2020 showed that malnutrition levels were above emergency threshold level in most of the parts of the country and correspondingly the national median GAM prevalence in children was reported to be around 13.8% and this translates to 0.96 million boys and girls. Out of this 162,000 are affected by life threatening severely acute malnutrition while around 800,000 are moderately acute malnourished and at the same time the programmatic data shows that of the total admissions, 54% are girls. In addition to children under five years of age, a further 571,159 pregnant and lactating women are estimated to be in need of either preventive or curative emergency nutrition assistance. The last Post Deyr assessment showed that out of 24 IDP sites, 5 and 13 sites showed that the GAM levels in children under five was at critical and serious levels respectively thus showing that children in the IDP camps are disproportionately affected and hence needs lifesaving nutrition interventions. The Somalia 2020 Micronutrient indicates that around 40.2% of non-pregnant women and 47% of pregnant women are anemic and similarly around 43% children below five years are anemic, and both these indicators reveal a serious public health challenge.
while this is crucial to curb the resulting consequences of increased acute malnutrition, morbidity and mortality rates, the need to also tackle the underlying causes of malnutrition, and thus looking at long term nutrition interventions, has recently become a major priority. Focus on sustainable long-term nutrition interventions such as promotion, protection and support of optimum infant and young child feeding practices will go a long way in the contribution to the reduction of infant morbidity and mortality rate.
Currently ISDP is implementing Integrated primary health care and nutrition services targeting women in IDP communities in Bosaso district funded by UNICEF(2020-2021)with budget $626,490.46; WFP funded Nutrition Program (TSFP/MCH/BSFP) in Bari region (2020-2021) with budget $380,578, and UNICEF global fund Behavioural Change and Communication Strengthening Malaria Control and Health Systems project in Bari, Nugal and Mudug regions (2020-2021) with budget $128,218.22.
Hence Integrated Service for Displaced Population (ISDP) is proposing to implement the project that aims at providing integrated lifesaving nutrition services to boys, girls, pregnant and lactating mothers with Wasting and affected by COVID 19 priority given to IDP and rural communities in Bosaso District. The major objectives of the project will be to strengthen life saving preventive nutrition services and provision of equitable access to lifesaving curative nutrients among PLW, boys and girls. The intervention will scale ISDP missions to additional beneficiaries from 38 IDP camps include (100 Buush,Inji,Shabeele B,Absame A,Absame B,Baalade,55aad,Biyo-Kulule A, Tuurjaale,Saylada A, Buulo-Eeley,New Biyo Kulule,Shabeele A,Mingis A,Buulo-Qodax, Girible, Raf & Raaxo, Banaadir A, Abow A, Far Jano, L-Khayr, Saylada Gawraca, Bariga Bosaso, Xaadoole, Ajuuraan A, New Shabeele.) in Bosaso district and rural poor host communities targeting 74749 beneficiaries.
The proposed activities are: -
a) Training of community workers for training on malnutrition and IYCF
b) Training of health workers at clinic level on malnutrition and IYCF
c) Training of Community Health Workers (CHWs) or peer counselors
d) Formation of mother to mother support groups) centers
e) Screening boys and girls (6-23 months) and PLW for preventive service through supplement nutrition
f) Screening of boys and girls 6-59 month and PLW with Severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and moderate Malnutrition (MAM)
g) Forums/community dialogues
h) Awareness and sensitization Radio program

Project Activities

Activity 1: 1-day Start-up Consultation meeting bring together 15 different community and government representatives from Bosaso district – During the meeting, ISDP will inform participants of the proposed project, its objective and to seek support and cooperation from them observing WHO COVID 19 prevention rules. Activity 2: Selection of 60 community workers/caseworkers, peer counsellors and health workers for 3-day training in Bosaso on malnutrition to enhance their knowledge and skills to conduct sensitizing on nutrition and IYCF at community level. The training will capture skilled IYCF counselling, nutrition, infant growth and development, counselling techniques and criteria for making referrals among other topics. Working with MOH a selection criterion will be developed prior and followed during the selection of community/caseworkers, peer counsellors and health workers. Activity 3: Formation of mother to mother support groups centers: - 5 centers will be formed in 5 IDP settlements to enhance community knowledge towards appropriate infant and young child feeding practices. MTMSGs will form a platform for information sharing, discussion and peer influence/support towards improved IYCF practice. The centers will be equipped with IEC materials, toys for children, food samples etc. 10,000 women are expected to benefit in the center activities Activity 4: Screening boys and girls (6-23 months) and PLW for preventive service through supplement nutrition targeting 39586 beneficiariesActivity 5: Screening of boys and girls 6-59 month and PLW with Severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and moderate Malnutrition (MAM) targeting 35194 beneficiariesActivity 6: Carry out 4 Forums/community dialogues on breastfeeding and nutrition bringing together 30 women and young adolescent boys and girls among Bosaso RAS/IDP communities per session to mark World breast feeding week held annually August 1st to 7th :- It will also involve key influencers (i.e. doctors, midwives) to create awareness on benefit of breastfeeding and nutrition. The forums will give chance to women and men from surrounding communities mobilized to teach the adolescent girls and young men on how to breastfeed and learn to prepare ration into nutritious meals using local food and also to know more on malnutrition, benefit of breastfeeding, eating of balance diet, weaning, demonstration of breastfeeding etc. Activity 7: Quarterly Radio program: - working with MOH, ISDP will air three (3) radio programs in three quarters on nutrition and malnutrition. The radio program will be aired using a national radio targeting more than 300,000 people in Puntland. Activity 8: conducting 2 IYCF survey
