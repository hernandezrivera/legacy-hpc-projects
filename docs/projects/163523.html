Project Summary

This project aims to provide assistance to the populations affected by the Lake Chad Basin crisis in the Far North region of Cameroon, particularly in the divisions of Mayo Sava, Mayo Tsanaga, and Logoné-et-Chari. The ongoing violence carried out by armed opposition groups has had widespread impact on livelihoods and food security, limiting communities’ ability to meet their basic needs and exposing them to various protection risks.
Previous funded projects have allowed IRC to develop an in-depth knowledge of beneficiary communities and the humanitarian needs confronting them. Based on the successes and lessons learned from its experiences working with these communities,the IRC plans to support and strengthen established structures and its local relations in order to improve the quality and scope of actions, while maintaining its capacity to react to spontaneous movements with mobile services that enable access hard-to-reach areas in persistent and precarious environments.
The proposed intervention will continue the implementation of activities already underway by the IRC through, aiming to provide direct support to beneficiaries to meet their basic needs across multiple services areas. These include provision of drinking water, promotion of hygiene and sanitation, the restoration of livelihoods and the introduction of actions to promote protection and prevent gender-based violence (GBV).
In terms of provision of unconditional food, cash transfers to refugees, IDPs and local vulnerable population, the project aims at covering basic needs of vulnerable households through:
Activitiy 1: provide assistance to IDPs and host population to improve their food and nutrition security through agricultural livestock and fishery support (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture) as:
- Number of months: 9 months;
- Number of people directly benefiting: 4,900 (700 households); and
- Total USD amount: $124,250.
Locations: Mayo Sava, Mayo Tsanaga, and Logoné-et-Chari
Activity 2: Provide unconditional food, cash transfers to refugees, IDPs and local vulnerable population as:
- Number of People Targeted: 1,225 individuals (175 households)
- Number of IDPs Targeted: 700
- Total USD amount: $107,029.
Locations: Mayo Tsanaga and Logone et Chari

Besoins / Needs

The crisis and the displacement of populations also have dramatic consequences on access to basic services which are overstretched and create tensions between displaced and hosting populations. As indicated in the 2019 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP), more than 50% of the local population need humanitarian assistance. For almost one quarter of households, at least 65% of expenses are used to meet food needs, of which 13% spend more than 75% of their expenses on food.As a result, 40% of the population suffered from chronic malnutrition in 2018 while economic barriers pose particular risks for women and adolescent girls, who may resort to negative coping mechanisms such as commercial sexual exploitation to meet their families’ basic needs —exposing them to increased risk of gender-based violence (GBV), including early and forced marriage, sexual harassment and assault, kidnapping for ransom, and rape, and exploitation. Given the multidimensional nature of the crisis and the limited chances of a rapid resolution of the conflict, the needs remain very important and range from immediate life-saving to recovery.
