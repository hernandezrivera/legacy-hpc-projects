Project Summary

The project aims to support healthcare services in Bab Al Hawa Hospital by providing financial support along with providing all medicines and medical consumables needed, to provide people in need with all required services, this includes local communities and IDPs, women, children and elderly people.
Bab Al Hawa Hospital is considered the biggest specialized hospital in NW of Syria, and it’s now considered the main referral health facility for all primary health facilities in NW of Syria.
The services provided at the hospital includes but not limited to: Emergency unit, Renal Dialysis unit, Scanning and Imagining unit, GI Endoscopy unit, Laboratory and Blood Bank, Neonatal unit, Neurology unit, Cardiology unit, Ophthalmology unit, and Outpatients Clinics, and Specialized Operations unit which includes General Surgery, ENT Surgery, Neurology Surgery, Orthopaedic Surgery, Thoracic Surgery, Maxillofacial Surgery, Urology Surgery and Pediatric Surgery, which will be all supported by this project.
The hospital facilitates the services provision for women by preparing a specialized reception utilities and isolated room for privacy, in addition to the availability of women in the hospital staff.
In addition, the hospital is the authorized referral point to Turkey for the most complex cases.

Implementation of activities

The project will support the provision of healthcare services at Bab Al Hawa hospital by supporting and renewing staff contracts to sustain their efforts at providing the required services. The project also will provide the hospital with the required medicines and medical consumables necessary for the medical procedures conducted within the different departments of the hospital. In addition to the maintenance for all hospital departments as needed. The project will support all the specialized departments of the hospital such as General Surgery unit, Neonatal unit and Paediatric Surgery which is very rare at NW of Syria, Cardiac Specialized Center which is one of the most advanced cardiac centers in the area, Ophthalmology unit, Emergency Department, Imaging and Scanning unit and other specialized departments, with medical devices and consumables as needed. The hospital provides its services to patients with all ages as it contains all the departments required for this as mentioned above. These departments contribute to the provision of essential primary and secondary healthcare services in the area by providing the required consultation for inpatients and outpatients throughout the different and various clinics and departments, and through the specialized surgeries and operations. The hospital receives all the complex medical conditions whether form war related trauma or the referred cases form the NW of Syria, and provide the required medical services for all patients. And for the more complex cases that are beyond the ability of hospital capacity, the hospital refers these cases to Turkey as it’s considered the main authorized referral point to Turkey. Part of the implementation activities is to collect feedback data from the beneficiaries throughout simple and clear complaint mechanisms, the data collected through these mechanisms are analysed and reviewed by the quality team, and the necessary actions are undertaken to solve any issues faced by the beneficiaries regarding the provision of the service. The project currently is funded by King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre till 21/9/2019. This fund supports all salaries and running cost, and about 25% of the need for medicines and consumables. The needs of the hospital for medicines and consumables is always increasing due to the increasing number of beneficiaries.
