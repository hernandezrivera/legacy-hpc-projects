Project Summary

The situation in the North West and South West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon remains grave as communities are caught between two emergencies; the four-year old socio-political crisis which turn violent November 2017 and the corrent COVID-19 pandemic. Parties to the conflict have largely ignored calls for a ceasefire and violence is ongoing, leading to continued displacement. In April 2020, 7,773 persons from 1,365 households were forced to flee from their villages as a result of violence . In February 2020, the humanitarian situation in the North West and South West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon continued to deteriorate as a result of violence related to the February 2020 elections. Several waves of displacements were recorded throughout the NWSW. In February 2020, about 1,800 persons from 257 households were displaced in Fako and Manyu Divisions of the SW. In the NW, an estimated 8,457 persons from 1209 households were displaced in Menchum, Boyo, Donga-Mantung and Ngokentunjia Divisions as a result of increased violence and fears related to the elections. Majority of these displaced persons are children, women and girls of the reproductive age.
Mindful of the above situation and the vulnerability of these women and girls, our current project is design to help these women and girls understand their sexual and reproductive health and rights through raising awareness, strengthen the capacity of health service providers and improve the accessibility of contraceptives.
