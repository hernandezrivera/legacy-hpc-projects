Project Summary

The overall project goal is to Under the partnership, increase access to quality learning environment, support with the education and Child Protection system strengthening as well as strengthening the Prevention and Response to Violence, Abuse and Exploitation of children in schools through Child protection, GBV/PSEA services for Conflict-Affected Children Across 51 schools in MMC and Jere LGAs of Borno state.
The expected outcome is that Conflict-affected children and adolescents including Women, Parent/Caregivers have increased access to quality protection and response services in safe and healthy environment. NADA will through community and stake holder’s engagement ensure that all children have access to quality and inclusive basic education, mental health and psychosocial support and vocational opportunities for crisis-affected girls and boys in safe learning environments in North East Nigeria as well, ensured the capacity development for SBMC, CBCPC, Teachers and Parent of Children on CDRR, Risk/Psycho-education, protection and GBV/PSEA reporting mechanism. The organization will support in re-establishment of all school’s structures including protection and GBV/PSEA reporting mechanism in schools across the targeted schools within MMC, JERE, Damaturu and Yobe State.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The project will significantly contribute to gender equality, including across age groups, members of marginalized community’s/minority groups, including people with disability will be included in consultations at all phases of the project. Women and girls will be included in consultations and decision-making processes at all stages of the project including youth in consultations and decision-making processes.NADA will ensure that the project have mechanisms to prevent and address sexual exploitation and abuse, with a PSEA communication strategy and appropriate complaints mechanism. NADA will consider protection of all persons affected and at risk when planning humanitarian response and protection is central in NADA’s preparedness and lifesaving activities throughout the duration of project. The population at risk would be identified considering the specific vulnerabilities that underlie these risks, including those experienced by men, women, girls and boys, and groups such as internally displaced persons, older persons, persons with disabilities, and persons belonging to sexual and other minorities. The proposed action is defined by daily interaction and collaboration with affected Schools/communities, including support to Schools/community-based structures and their participation in public awareness-raising, referral processes and collaboration in identifying and implementing initiatives which contribute to improving the protective environments.
