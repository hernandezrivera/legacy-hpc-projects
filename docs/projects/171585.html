Project Summary

According to FSNAU post Gu 2020 assessment report indicated that 5.1 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse outcomes through December 2020 in the absence of humanitarian assistance across Somalia this was due to the combined effects of widespread and severe flooding, Desert Locust infestation, socioeconomic impacts of COVID-19 and the cumulative impacts of previous shocks , In addition, 849,900 children under the age of five are likely to be acutely malnourished through August 2021. The 2020 Gu season exhibited erratic performance. The rains started as early as late March in many parts of Somalia, then intensified and expanded in April, with heavy rain leading to riverine and flash floods. However, rainfall in May was widely below average. Severe riverine and flash floods in April and early May caused significant population displacement and damage to property, infrastructure, farmland, and crops.
Despite multiple shocks in 2020, high levels of sustained humanitarian assistance and government support have played a critical role in stabilizing and preventing worse acute food insecurity outcomes in Somalia. According to the Somalia Food Security Cluster, an average of 1.85 million people received food assistance monthly from April to August. Approximately 400 000 people also benefitted from government-led rural and urban safety net programmes. In April, government has also enacted a 20-100 percent tax exemption on imported food commodities in order to mitigate the socio-economic impact of COVID-19 on the population. Based on results of assessments conducted in July and August 2020, approximately 1.3 million people are currently facing Crisis or worse (IPC Phase 3 or 1) higher) outcomes through September 2020 in the presence of humanitarian food assistance. An additional 2.5 million people are Stressed (IPC Phase 2), bringing the total number of people experiencing acute food insecurity to 3.8 million
sustained and large-scale humanitarian assistance and government support are currently preventing more severe outcomes in many areas. Desert Locust continues to pose a serious risk of damage to both pasture and crops at least until the end of 2020. The 2020 Deyr (October-December) rainfall season is likely to be below average to average across the country, which could lead to drought and trigger a worsening of the humanitarian situation if the 2021 Gu (April-June) season rainfall is also delayed or performs poorly.
PDA project will therefore, intends to provide immediate access to unconditional and conditional cash assistance to 6,400 most vulnerable women, girls, boys and men in IDPs and host communities, it will also provide capacity enhancement, farming tools as well as farm inputs to these target farmers in Beletweyne,Jalalaqsi Hiran region, Jowhar and Balcad of middle Shabelle and Dhusamareeb and Cadado district in Galgadud region as well as well as agricultural seed inputs and tools to increase their farming production thus improving the nutrition of the target population. The project will provide through cash for work will enable to provide immediate access to household income thus improving the overall well-being of the target communities. PDA will ensure to coordinates the foods security and livelihood activities closely with other humanitarian actors and concern ministries at regional and federal level to ensure better information sharing among the regional states in the target district

Sensitize vulnerable household benefiting from unconditional cash transfer on nutrition, health and hygiene promotion for food preparation to enable reduce malnutrition and disease hence improve community living standard. 4.Conduct training to capacity building farmers (80 men and 40 women household headed) on proper preparing, harvesting as well as management of farm inputs storage hence enhancing their knowledge and thus inculcate skills for their livelihood in future5. What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

PDA proposes to providing unconditional cash assistance and conditional cash grant is the most appropriate intervention at this immediate response stage and will allow beneficiaries to priorities their most pressing need. Additionally, affected communities and local government authorities will be engaged in identification of the beneficiaries most in need. The selected beneficiaries will be registered and provided with unique cards for future verification and monitoring purposes. The cash will be transferred through mobile company/financial service provider which will be selected based on availability of network and/or services, readiness and ability to provide a prompt delivery, and reasonable service charge. A proposed list of beneficiaries (from community consultations) will be validated by the project in a door-to-door beneficiary verification exercise. Beneficiary selection shall not be restricted by documentation provided available with the beneficiaries and solely the selection criteria shall be followed. Local authorities and relevant government departments will be consulted for final selection of beneficiaries at the targeted areas. Therefore, the project will particularly target these vulnerable groups, which includes women, children, elderly persons and ethnic minority groups in Hiran, Middle Shabelle and Galgadud regions in Somalia, people with disability, and provide them with lifesaving assistance to protect them from further harm and refraining them from adopting negative coping mechanisms. Direct beneficiary’s selection will be ‘inclusive’ and based on the consultation with communities by the respective local administration following the humanitarian principles.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

PDA will ensure provision of life-saving and life sustaining cash response to the affected population in Hiran,Middle Shabelle and Galgadud region target location through vouchers for unconditional and conditional cash transfer to vulnerable household. Providing a market based approach also strengthens the sustainability of the action by ensuring the operation and maintenance of the assets by either the targeted communities’ relief Committees. The project will use market bases intervention aims to responds to IDPs and host communities affected by drought coupled with the COVID-19 pandemic and locust infestation that have hindered the livelihood of the already in crisis communities in South central Somalia,

Mitigation Measures
