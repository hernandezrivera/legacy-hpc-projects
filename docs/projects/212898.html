Project Summary

Almost 13 years have passed since the conflict in Syria. The current situation is getting worse especially in education. According to the preliminary estimates, 2.1 Million of school-aged children are no longer able to read and write, resulting in school drop-out just as much as lack of education. The significant reason behind this is the inadequacy of competent teaching staff along with the deterioration of the economy. The salary hardly reaches $15 per month which is, of course, not enough. This indicates that these problems have not just been limited to students but also teachers.
This project aims to build the teachers’ capacity so as to ensure that most of the children enjoy high-quality and inclusive learning opportunities. In this project, teachers receive training on student-centered teaching methodology and foundational teaching skills.
In short, the project will include several stages:
Stage One: Teachers will attend the training courses, which will be in partnership with specialized parties in this field. The training course will include:
- Implementation of lessons: How the teachers should deliver the information to children easily
- Classroom management: Teachers will learn some strategies that empower them to give the lesson in a friendly atmosphere
- Teaching ethics: Its real importance lies in the correlation with what we are going through, such as abuse
- Computer course: Teachers will learn basic computer skills in addition to Word, PowerPoint and Excel
Stage Two: Teachers are subjected to an assessment, so that each teacher is evaluated periodically twice a month according to criteria, in which we measure the teacher’s performance and its impact on the student and this is done in cooperation with Monitoring and Evaluation Officials. The evaluation will coincide with the provision of basic services for teachers and students. Some of these services include: supplying teachers with educational resources along with cash assistance to let the teachers know they have the support they need. Moreover, students are provided with stationary and other school supplies as well as PSS.
The expected results are the following:
1. 160 teachers will be selected for the training courses. Every teacher attends 50 sessions. Each session lasts for 180 minutes. Each session will include about 15 teachers
2. Psychosocial support activities will be held for these 160 teachers to ensure that they are well-equipped to identify children. Every teacher attends 4 sessions, each lasts for 180 minutes
3. These 160 teachers will receive cash assistance
4. Educational services and remedial classes will be provided to 300 children, aimed at enabling them to finish their school year at the best level (formal Edu.)
5. 300 dropout children will be targeted in the Individual Education Plan that determines the extent of the children’s need for literacy lessons (non-formal Edu.)
6. Stationery and school supplies will be delivered to 600 children (formal and non-formal Edu.)
7. Schools will be supplied with projectors, laptops and other in accordance with the needs
8. 2 community initiatives will be planned in order to address the problems children face
We will strive to provide these educational services to the following target groups: IDPs, CWDs, OoSC, adolescents, crisis-affected children and children without family care. The project will be implemented in Damascus, Kafar Batna and Qudsiya.
Our response, together with MOE, will be in public schools as well as our centers, which are equipped with surveillance cameras and basic educational equipment like boards, projectors and educational games.
Finally, and most importantly, CRS has had good experiences in educational programs and most recently in capacity-building for teachers.
We ensure that the primary beneficiary of the services is the children. That is why when we support the teacher financially, professionally and morally, this will significantly be reflected on their students.
