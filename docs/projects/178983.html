Project Summary

the project is planned to be implemented in NES- DEZ and Al-hasakeh governorates, to address the needs for protection services for the most vulnerable communities from IDPs, Returnees, host communities from women and men, boys and girls, and PWD. there will be establishing two CC in Al-hasakeh and DEZ for in and outreach proposed below services;
the project includes general protection service for 6000 people from women and men, boys and girls, PWD and elders: by1-improve and raise awareness of the target communities on the needed protection aspects as shown in the needs assessment and HNO 2021 which is related to improve the community awareness for child protection concerns, PSEA, GBV core concepts and enhance information dissemination for the services provided by the working actors in the areas through updated and live service map documents. 2-establishing referral pathways in DEZ and Al-hasakeh and ensuring they received their referred needs with safe access and quality service with transportation service needed ones which will be determined according to their disability, vulnerability statues like women headed household, child-headed household, SGBV survivals, taking into most consideration ISP to avoid any expose for further risks. Under GBV: 1-the project includes Case management “CM”services SGBV for 50 cases including all the stages of CM process , ensure coordination with service providers in the targeted areas to have the maximum benefits for the case including in-kind assistance for the needed ones, IPV received CM trainings from NES-PWG and has 2 year experience in CM and has the capacity to have opened cases under medium and High risk. 2-vocational training programs for 200 women and girls who are above 17 yrs, for literacy and numeracy and other vocations from sewing, knitting, etc,. the project enhances the provision of structured PSS services for 200 women.
Under CP:PSS Sessions 2000 girls, boys under 18 yrs. children PSS program include creative sessions for children from boys and girls as this has a positive impact on their mental and Psychosocial wellbeing. 50 CM services for children will be and 25 people from project staff will be trained on CPMS.
the project emphasizes building the capacities of the working staff for all the needed aspects from protection mainstreaming, GBV core concepts, PSEA mandatory training. facilitation skills, PSS program pieces of training, CM training, CPIE, CPMS training, SIR training. the project mainstream CP and GP by CSG, PSEA which are reflected in mandatory COC for all the working staff and all the related works people. the project will be implemented with full coordination with NES protection working groups and protection coordination admin offices in NES to avoid and duplication in the services provided. the project will take COV-19 outbreak into most consideration by ensuring the awareness-raising regarded, provide PPE for the working staff and all the BNFs people from the project. the total number of people per session will be no more than 8 people per session. and the established centre will be cleaned and sterilized on daily basis in addition to ensuring water availability in project centres for safe WASH services. all the project activities will address the inclusion of PWD from women, men, and girls who are under 18.
All of the proposed activities will be delivered in the fixed and mobile centres according to the needs and BNFs access to services. IPV is implementing such protection services two years ago in NES and well know ME tools to ensure proposed quality, like IASC tools for CM and Referral services, all the safety measures and must have consent before any targeting on BNFs. ISP and data protection is the core of the implementation program, taking into most consideration the BNFs do no harm concept to avoid any exposure for any further risks.

4 - Needs

The project aims to address the protection concerns which the communities In NES suffering from under: GBV-PSEA-CP-GP. According to HNO2021, Women and girls are particularly vulnerable to gender-based violence because of gender inequality and discrimination in Syria. Women consequences responsibilities do not automatically translate into decision making power. This contributes to increased pressure and stress for women to provide for their families, often in absence of any other support from family members. Consequently, they are exposed to increased risks of violence against them within the family and at their workplace. Violence, protracted displacement, ongoing violations of IHL and human rights law, extensive destruction of infrastructure, and inadequate access and services are likely to continue impacting the protection landscape in Syria and this will be worth in Northeast Syria NES. These protection concerns are caused by lack of awareness, bad-livelihood situation. through IPV experience in DEZ, the rate of CP concerns from Physical abuse, child labour, and forced early marriage has been increased. further to bad livelihood situation which increased risks exposure for women and children as they are most vulnerable. the conflict has negatively affected the communities by negative adaptive mechanisms from social norms especially related to vulnerable women and children from boys and girls, PWD, elders from women and men. IPV is proposing this project through its unique access to DEZ and Alhasakeh as it is hard to reach area for many working actors in NEs added to being with high severity scale beside to being marginalized area with total people in need in DEZ is 0.6M and 0.8M in Alhasakeh "HNO2021"

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be implemented under full coordination with the working actors and coordination admin offices in the areas to avoid any duplication in the efforts. the addressed needs are reflected by the diverse needs of women, men, children boys and girls under 18, PWD and elders. the project is designed and will be implemented under the DO-NO HARM principle and the humanitarian principles and standards to provide humanitarian assistance in quality, equity and gender equality. the project activities will address avoiding any expose for further risks for all BNFs. the proposed activities are reflected by community participation and accountable by the feedback mechanisms. the working staff will be from women and men taking into account social norms and community acceptance for the activities to be tailored and contextualize further to accepted staff to ensure project expected results. the project staff will be from local communities in the targeted areas to help in improving the livelihood situations in the area. the project activities will be implemented after providing all the capacity building needed to the working staff to have quality and safe services, with ensuring the mainstreaming of PSEA- CSG and all related feedback mechanisms and accountability ways.

Yes

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

the project includes SEA risks that could be taken place within working staff, or due to some working staff with BNFs from women or children or even within the BNFs level. SEA risks could occur during the staff or BNFs journey to the project centre location by some people from the community.IPV have set many mitigation strategies to address PSEA, which include: the PSEA mandatory training and related COC-PSEA and CSG, which the staff must align with and obtained, with the clear clarification that the IPV have zero tolerance with any SEA accidents. IPV has PSEA focal points in the working area and there will be focal points in the project centre for any reporting needed or support in PSEA mainstreaming. IPV will do awareness-raising for PSEA which include community level, working staff level and centre level which could include: brochures and banners, videos, awareness raining messages and hotlines. IPV will ensure that all the working staff is wellknown about the internal reporting and feedback mechanism taken in place in case of SEA occurrence cases. all the project's service providers or contractures who has direct contact with project beneficiaries from children or women must be trained and signed COC. IPV will ensure that during any conducted sessions there will be a female member to ensure beneficiaries safety, especially from children and women.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

IPV will ensure that all the AR topics are reflected by the community and stakeholders participation in the project design phase. to start up the project, IPV will conduct lunching project campaign and information sharing with the targeted communities in the project area to ensure their full knowledge of the proposed activities. the established CC will be in safe, accessible, not far away from the city centre to be safe accessed for the targeted people from children boys and girls, and women. case management activities will be provided with the consent of the caregivers or the case if she/he is above 18 years old. the CM work will be supported with the local stakeholders from comeen, protection admin office in the project area to promote. the community acceptance for these types of activities and their importance. the sessions groups will be gender and age-sensitive by women and men, boys and girls. During the COVID-19 pandemic curfew, the activities will be continued even by door to door - out reach methodology or by using social media.
