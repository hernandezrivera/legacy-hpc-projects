Project Summary

Household food security and livelihood severely affected. Household food availability and utilization of food is challenged along with economic and Physical access to food. Due to inundation and communication breakdown, market & local storage facilities are disrupted. Cluster priorities include protection from food insecurity through food assistance with distribution of fortified food commodities (FSC Food Assistance package) to address both hunger and nutrition to the most vulnerable including marginal groups (e g Female headed household, HH with disability etc) as complement to the government effort ensuring respects to religious and cultural traditions. Priority life-saving activities include Food assistance to the most vulnerable monsoon flood affected households, Emergency lifesaving Nutrition services to the under 5 children, adolescent girls and PLW, Immediate livelihoods support to most vulnerable and economically challenged households affected by the monsoon flood, Cash for work to repair breached embankments and essential community infrastructures, Livelihood recovery assistance for the most vulnerable economically disadvantaged female headed HH and Persons with Disability, and Restoration of appropriate nutrition service delivery for PLW and under 5 children's
