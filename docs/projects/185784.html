Project Summary

After influx of FDMNs in Bangladesh, Nabolok has been addressing lifesaving services since June, 2018 to till today and we will have planned to continue these supports for the coming year of 2022 at camp-24, 26 as well as surrounding host communities under Teknaf upazila in Cox’s Bazar. Nabolok had already implemented few projects to provide humanitarian assistances for Rohingya in camp-23, 24 & 26 and we also included the nearer affected host community in Teknaf upazila and deliverd the same assistances to them and this services is going on. From beginning to work in Rohingya response, we have been working in Site Management and Site Development (SMSD) by installed Solar Street Lights, repaired & maintenance of those under Site Development. So far, Nabolok had installed 359 solar street lights at camp-23, 24 & 26 for Rohingya community and Baharchora, Teknaf Sador, Sabrang and Nhilla Union for host community to ensure their safety and security issues at night apart from this we have been also conducting regular repair, maintenance and operation of this Solar Street Light to keep these lights functional. Farther more, based on identifying gap Nabolok will have plan to install more 70 nos of solar based street light in 2022 to address the existing need for the Rohingya refugee in the camp-24 & 26 and also for host community. Before installing a single light, we went to field and discusses with the beneficiaries about demand collection, site selection and so on. During the discussion, Nabolok has always prioritized the opinion of women and adolescent girls, older people and PWDs so that we can meet their lighting needs through the installation of solar street lights. As a consequence, Nabolok had installed most of the SSL at the places where beneficiaries suggested. As it’s continuity we will keep this practice for the upcoming year beside these we will plan to include all age group people with gender inclusion which will make our project more gender inclusive and participatory also. Furthermore, Nabolok installed most of their Solar Light beside of WASH facilities, road side and much gathering places so that a wide range of beneficiaries (both primary and secondary) will benefited by our lighting facilities. As we mentioned the above, we had installed our most of the Solar Lights beside of WASH facilities so that all age group of beneficiaries have used the WASH facilities and move one place to another more comfortably by the help of lighting facility during at night. Beside these, women and adolescent girls also came out from their house for use WASH facility even in night without fear sexual and physical harassment or abusement as well as after lights installed the rate of theft and rubbery incident have reduced significantly. Apart from this, we will have plan to focus on DRR issue during site selection for install solar light just as: to choose a site near narrow bridge, a site near a safe places just like cyclone shelters, schools cum cyclone shelter and other important places so that if any natural disaster will be occurred then the beneficiary especially the elder, PWDs, women and adolescent girls can easily reach the safe places by using this solar lighting facility etc. The project will also plan to ensures regular repairing & maintenance works on a regular basis to keep this light functional and those will give long time services by collecting feedback from direct beneficiaries along with regular field monitoring by the both field level staff as well as a full time Monitoring Officer. This site development activity will increase the level of feeling safe during night at the roadside and at the community.

1 - Identified Needs

Considering the camp context, several field experiences and identified gap along with assessing the needs of beneficiaries regarding safety issue especially at night. We found that around 45% beneficiaries don’t getting enough lighting facility during night. As consequences, beneficiary doesn’t feel safe during night especially when they are coming out from their house for using WASH facilities and there always be a possibility of sexual & physical harassment and robbery offences due to absence any lighting facility. In this project we have plan to minimize that problem at camp and host community level by installing additional total 70 nos of solar based street light among them 50 nos for camps-24 & 26 and 20 nos for host community under Nhilla Union. Along with, we will ensure the proper functionality by conducting regular repair & operatio and maintenance of total 428 nos of installation which we had installed previous couple of years including newly propose 70 nos of installation. As well as we can ensure maximum number of beneficiaries will get lighting facility by our installation, meet up safety issue during at night besides we need to address the new challenges or demands regarding this issue. This project will help to improve site management and site development activities, to be more specific it will ensure safety, security during night and protection issues. The all above mentioned activity will be included in our project to ensure the 100% of targeted beneficiaries have proper lighting facility at night as well as their involvement during site selection and regular feedback throughout the whole project duration.

2 - Activities or outputs

Installation of number of solar based street light for Rohingya refugee after assessing the gaps at the camp-24 & 26 -50 Nos; Installation of number of solar based street light for Host community under Nhilla Union of Teknaf Upazilla -20 and To ensure the regular Repair, operation & maintenance of total 428 nos of solar based street lights including newly installed 70 nos and previous 358 nos at the camp-24 & 26 and nearer host community's for next five years -428 Nos.
