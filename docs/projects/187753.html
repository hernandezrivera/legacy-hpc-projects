Project Summary

SARD First Line Emergency Response targets the 4000 most vulnerable IDPs families (20.000 IDPs approx.) scattered in open fields and informal settlements with rapid emergency WASH assistance including, Hygiene Kits, and emergency Latrines (including water storage).
SARD also focuses under this action on more sustainable solutions, looking at community level, targeting both IDPs and local communities where 10 water networks are planned to be rehabilitated/extended benefiting up to 3000 families (15.000 individuals approx.). Rehabilitation and extension works will also include sewage systems, and additional 10 systems to be rehabilitated targeting 3000 families (15.000 individuals approx.).
Water and sanitation facilities also fall under this action where 12 facilities are planned to be rehabilitated (6 schools, and 6 health facilities), benefiting up to 30.000 individuals (direct and indirect).
It is estimated that for rehabilitation works of public infrastructure facilities (water/sewage networks, schools, and health facilities) are 50% local population and 50% IDPs.
All SARD WASH activities are implemented with a protection lens, with a well-structured complaints mechanism.

4 - Needs

SARD Yearly Needs Assessment in NW Syria showed that one in three households (33%) can barely cover their water needs (22% of host, 36% of IDPs) and 4% reported insufficient access to cover their needs (3% of host and 4% of IDPs). Over two in five households (42%) had faced water scarcity and/or shortages in the month prior the assessment. Quality of water was considered as satisfactory to excellent quality by 95% of households. The assessment also showed that one in five household (20%) do not have access to safe latrines, mostly due to a lack of gender-disaggregated facilities, lack of safety and security or lack of facilities in their location. Additionally, and for the same reasons, 26% do not have access to safe bathing spaces. Due to economic downturn unaffordability of some key hygiene supplies reported by up to 43% of HHs in communities further deteriorate effectiveness of infection prevention and control (HNO Syria). WASH systems in NEW Syria have suffered from damage owing to hostilities. Additionally, WASH needs in schools and health facilities remain very high and could be linked with dysfunctional public water and sanitation systems the facilities are connected to, water crisis and economic downturn. Over 40% of caretakers in households with children attending school received complaints from children on WASH-related issues , and such factors may contribute to the overall 2.4 million children out of schools (UNICEF Regional Director MENA- 24 January 2021).

5 - Implementation of activities

Under its First Line Emergency Response (Result 1) SARD will target the most vulnerable IDPs families scattered in the open fields in informal settlements and under trees with rapid emergency WASH assistance including:- Hygiene kits for each family for three months. Compositions of the hygiene kit will be designed based on cluster recommendations, discussed, and agreed with people of concern (beneficiaries).- Emergency Latrines (toilet and bathing), with water storage. Latrines will be gender-segregated, with access for PWSN (when required). Latrines will be installed directly by SARD mobile team and linked to leach pits. Latrines will be dismantled and the soil will be naturalized soon as the IDPs left the location. Of note, the emergency latrines are not designed to cope with long displacement. The lifespan of an emergency latrine is expected at 8-12 weeks.Under result 2, SARD focuses on more sustainable solutions, looking at community level rather than a family level. Activities under result 2 include: - Rehabilitation and extension of existing water and sewage networks.- Rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities in schools and health facilities. Prior to site works, SARD technical team conducts site visits and studies to prepare BoQs and SOW. Site works are implemented thereafter through contractors under close supervision by SARD site inspectors. SARD WASH activities are implemented with a protection lens, with a well-structured complaints mechanism.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Due to the nature of the project, household visits are required to assess the needs and deliver the services which may expose the most vulnerable individual to sexual exploitation and abuse.Likelihood: low, Impact: HighMitigation Measures: Through SARD's a) MEL team, and established accountability and complaint mechanism, SARD ensures a quick response in the case of any incident. B) constantly check the efficiency of the reporting pathways and review SARD’s PSEA policy; c) Employs staff aware of the PSEA policies and run regular refreshing training; d) conduct awareness-raising activities to the beneficiaries of the program. Risk: The socio-cultural context in the area may impact the relationship between SARD employees which may result in abuse of power or sexual harassment.Likelihood: low, Impact: HighMitigation Measures: a) SARD value a diverse and transparent working environment, therefore, it ensures all staff is aware of the PSEA and CoC; b) review the CoC and PSEA on regular basis and updates them accordingly; c) review the complaint mechanism and ensure all staff is aware of them by running refreshing training on regular basis.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

One of the risks that the project might face is the steeling of NFIs, SARD Has a dedicated staff to look after the warehouses and a well-observed storage system, SARD maintainer standby to provide feedback to the relevant sectors. Distribution of expired unhealthy items, SARD has a technical committee to review any purchased items right after the tender and selection process, SARD choose according to specific criteria that suit the activity intervention and time of implementation, one of these criteria is the expiry date and the storage conditions, SARD team monitor the status of the items periodically to ensure its validity. Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empowers or strengthens the position of armed groups or other actors, SARD Establishing strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organise joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care. Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases, SARD team and contractors are always under observation considering the usage of PPE, SARD has a safety rehabilitation working SoP and ensuring the applying of it at all SARD operations area, SARD provides safety training to its engineers and makes sure that the contractors are aware of such safety measures.
