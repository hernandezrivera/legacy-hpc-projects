Project Summary

The project is designed to provide life-saving interventions, including food baskets, ready-to-eat meals, hot meals, and free / subsidized bread distribution, targeting the most crisis-affected individuals in Northwest Syria. Specifically, it will be operated in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates, focusing on districts (Maaret Tamsrin, Ariha, Dana, Salqin, Harim, A’zaz, Afrin, Ma'btali, Albab, and Jarablus).
This project aims to stabilize communities by addressing immediate basic food needs and empowering (women, men, girls, boys) for both IDPs and host communities. By providing monthly emergency food provisions to recently displaced households and vulnerable host communities, the project seeks to enhance food security, support marginalized group and mitigate tensions between different groups. With approximately 45 % of the funding secured from QC fund, the project aligns with Sector Objective 1, which aims to improve the food security status of assessed food insecure populations through life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance. Through these efforts, the project aims to contribute to enhancing the overall food security situation in Idleb Governorate throughout 2024.
QC will provide the same interventions:
1.1: Distribution food basket (FB): (10.000) for 1250 for displaced households and vulnerable host community members, individual 6250 over 8 months, Number of food basket, 10,000*$75=$750,000
1.2: Distribution Ready-To Eat Rations (RTERs): 5000 RTERs for 5000 HHs of newly displaced households, Number of RTERs, 5,000*$30=$150,000
13: Distribution Cooked meals for 1,000 HHs, individual 5,000 foe 30 days, # of cooked meals, 30,000*$2.5=$75,000
1.4: Distribution free and subsidized bread for 32,000 HHs of IDPs and HC, individual 160,000, Distribution of bread will be over 7 months, Number of MT provided flour 5,500* $450=$2,475,000.
QC will contract with the bakeries for free bread distribution and will coordinate with the LCs who respond to the bakeries and subsidize bread.
QC will be responsible for flour producing/ purchasing and distribute it to the bakeries in addition to the yeast.
Modality of distribution will be in distribution points and door to door for the most vulnerable people (women and PWD) as the following criteria:
- women headed household.
- living in the camps.
- have a more of six members.
- in the family one member or more from the PWD, or has chronic disease.
Through these interventions QC will target 196250 BNFs during 2024.
The total of goods' cost 3,450,000 $, and 45% of it is secured.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

In Northwest Syria, a QC plans to contribute in strengthen the food security through various interventions. This includes distributing food baskets, RTEs and bread, as well as cooked meals during Ramadan, targeting vulnerable households. QC will ensure accountability, compliance with protection measures, and mitigation of risks, particularly related to gender-based violence and exploitation. Vulnerable groups like persons with disabilities (PWDs) are reached through door-to-door assistance, while COVID-19 safety measures are implemented during distributions. Staff are trained in protection principles, including prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA), with clear reporting mechanisms in place. QC will emphasize zero tolerance for PSEA and promotes a culture of accountability. Programming is designed to mitigate risks, including those related to shelter layouts and kit components. Comprehensive operational and HR measures are implemented to safeguard beneficiaries and maintain organizational integrity. Through these strategies, the organization aims to ensure equal access, safety, and dignity for all beneficiaries while addressing food insecurity in the region.QC will ensure coordination with FSL Cluster before RTE meals mobilization to align with FSL cluster recommendations, avoid duplication, and target food insecurity effectively. Food basket content and caloric coverage meet Sphere project and FSL Cluster standards of 2200 Kcal, ensuring nutritional adequacy for vulnerable households. Quality control measures in bread production adhere to FSL Cluster standards of 300g/ person per day, including inspections, ingredient monitoring, and hygiene practices. Distribution monitoring tracks timely delivery and satisfactory quality, with beneficiary feedback mechanisms in place.QC will establish coordination and referral mechanisms with humanitarian organizations and sectors in NW Syria. Partnerships with health organizations address medical needs, education agencies support schooling access, and protection cases address gender-based violence and exploitation. Referral pathways ensure comprehensive support, and information-sharing facilitates collaboration and best practice exchange. Regular coordination meetings and communication channels foster a cohesive and integrated response to beneficiaries' needs.
