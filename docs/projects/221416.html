Project Summary

In the Mbonge sub-division of Cameroon, the ongoing conflict and socio-economic instability have led to a dramatic decline in foundational literacy and numeracy among primary school pupils. Since the crisis began in 2016, over 90% of schools were closed, and as of the 2022-2023 academic year, only 46% of schools are operational, with just 54% of students enrolled in the North-West and South-West regions. This disruption has resulted in overcrowded classrooms, with pupil-to-teacher ratios soaring to 200:1, severely compromising the quality of education and leaving many children without essential learning skills.
The lack of trained educators further exacerbates this crisis, as 80% of facilitators in these learning environments have received no formal training, and existing curricula are not adapted to the emergency context. Consequently, many children are falling behind; recent assessments indicate that a significant percentage of pupils lack basic literacy and numeracy skills. For instance, a pilot project on the Teaching at the Right level approach implemented by NADEV and Street child of Cameroon in 2021 demonstrated that TaRL could yield positive results, with 57% of participants improving their numeracy skills and 53% improving their literacy within just seven weeks.
To address this urgent issue, this project focused on enhancing foundational literacy and numeracy in Mbonge is essential. This initiative will train 45 teachers in effective pedagogical methods through the Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) approach, and provide supplementary learning resources. The project aims to uplift 1500 pupils in 15 schools, ensuring they acquire the critical skills necessary for their future success and resilience in a post-conflict environment.
This project represents a vital step towards restoring educational quality and equity in Mbonge, ultimately contributing to the broader goal of sustainable development in Cameroon.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

This project seeks to address the needs of 1500 pupils in Mbonge Sub division whose foundational literacy and numeracy has been decline due to the crisis resulting to low learning outcomes, low self-esteem, loss of years out of school. Most of these pupils who have been out of school for long and some have been re-inserted but cannot meet up with their peers making it very difficult for them to catch up in class. Also most teachers cannot meet the varying learning levels of these pupils who have been out of school for long. A critical need identified is the lack of trained educators capable of delivering effective literacy and numeracy instruction. With 80% of facilitators lacking formal training, there is a pressing demand for professional development programs that equip teachers with modern pedagogical skills and strategies tailored to the unique challenges of the local and emergency context. Many schools in Mbonge face significant shortages of learning materials and resources. This scarcity hampers the ability of both teachers and students to engage meaningfully with the curriculum. Providing resource kits, including books, educational games, and teaching aids, is essential to enhance the learning experience and support diverse learning styles. Assessments reveal that a substantial number of pupils are struggling with basic literacy and numeracy skills due to prolonged school closures and inadequate instructional time. There is a clear need for targeted interventions, such as after-school tutoring programs, to provide personalized support for these students, helping them catch up and achieve grade-level competencies. There is a need for initiatives that educate parents about supporting their children’s learning at home and encourage community engagement in school activities to foster a supportive educational environment.By addressing these assessed needs, the project aims to create a robust educational framework that not only improves immediate learning outcomes but also promotes long-term resilience and community involvement in education in Mbonge
