Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the recent escalation in NWS to respond to immediate needs for IDPs. In light of the October escalation in the Idleb governorate, our initiative seeks to alleviate the hardships faced by the affected population.
As part of the response, the NGO has launched a Multi-Purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) program. This program will provide a one-off assistance of $150 to each targeted support targetting 814 households in 2024. These households will be meticulously identified, registered, and verified by the EER team to ensure that the assistance reaches those in genuine need. Moreover, 200 cases will be referred directly to the EER team from the Protection team, ensuring that those within the same affected community receive the necessary supporthousehold.
The conditions in the Idleb governorate have been drastically impacted by recent events, making many residents heavily reliant on external financial assistance to cover basic necessities such as food, shelter, and medical care. Our tailored MPCA program is designed as a direct response to these needs. By furnishing the affected households with this financial support, our goal is to enhance their resilience and capacity to navigate the current challenges, ensuring their well-being and stability in these trying times.
Risks and Assumptions:
1. Security: We acknowledge the fluid security situation in NWS and have implemented a proactive approach to adapt to changing conditions. The partner will closely monitor security levels and maintain flexibility in our activities, allowing us to shift locations or adjust modalities as necessary to ensure the safety of our staff and beneficiaries.
2. Humanitarian access: We are committed to reaching all targeted beneficiaries in the designated areas. The partner's strong communication and coordination with relevant stakeholders, active participation in NWS working groups, and our reputation for neutrality and independence will enable us to stay informed about contextual changes and overcome any access challenges that may arise.
3. Coordination: Effective coordination is essential for the success of this project. The partner maintains robust collaborative relationships with program partners, humanitarian actors, Clusters, local authorities, and beneficiaries. This collaborative approach ensures efficient information sharing, harmonization of efforts, and avoids duplication of services
