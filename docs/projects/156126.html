Project Summary

This project aims to provide a series of services that will promote the protection of children through improving the quality of community-based child protection through support to community structures and psychosocial support interventions; and expanding the reach and improving the quality of child protection specialized services for children most at-risk and survivors of violence, exploitation, neglect and abuse (this includes children at risk of recruitment, child laborers, child GBV survivors and at risk of GBV unaccompanied and separated children, among other vulnerable groups). In addition the project aims at building the capacity of community members on child protection topics.
This project will create new opportunities for positive mechanism to the children to support their development and wellbeing through community centers and awareness activities. Therefore, International Medical Corps will provide structured psychosocial support (PSS) and non-formal education activities and remedial classes to children and youth in order to improve their key life skills and knowledge. At the same time IMC will continue providing rehabilitative services for children with intellectual disabilities and delays, and will train their parents/caregiver on assisting their children mental, physical and educational developments. Youth Empowerment program will also be provided to youth to become active members and agents of positive change in their community. This can also contribute to empowering families to protect children and to enhancing the resilience of the entire community.
Moreover, to help families to better understand their children’s developmental stages, parents/caregiver will have the opportunity to learn more about the condition of their children and practice new skills to better support their development. Parents also learn how they can themselves assess their children's development and offer them stimuli in each developmental stage through early childhood development and portage training for parents/caregivers of children with disabilities.
IMC will provide specialized child protection services through case management and referrals. Trainings for community members on child protection prevention and response and inclusion of children with disabilities in community programs will also be conducted.

Needs

Children constitute a high rate of the affected population groups of crises and many risks faced by both boys and girls greatly affect their well-being, security and their future. Protracted conflict in Syria has led to the disruption of services, breakdown of community structures, safety nets and rule of law institutions, and the proliferation of weapons. The strain on resources continues to lead families and individuals to harmful coping mechanism such as early marriage, school dropout for labor, and generates situation of exploitation and abuse. The whole of Syria needs assessment 2018 indicate that 160 communities (66%) were assessed in Rural Damascus governorate. 94 per cent of the assessed communities, reported that child labor was an issue of concern. Adolescent boys 15-17 years (92%) followed by adolescent boys 12-14 years (88%) were considered most affected population groups. 55 per cent of assessed communities, respondents reported child recruitment was an issue of concern. Adolescent boys (34%) followed by boys. 81 per cent of assessed communities, respondents reported unaccompanied and separated children was an issue of concern. Adolescent boys (67%) followed by adolescent girls (62%) were considered the most affected child population groups. 78 percent of assessed communities respondents reported that family violence was an issue of concern. Adolescent boys (71%) followed by adolescent girls (70%) were considered the most affected child population groups. 87 per cent of assessed communities, respondents reported child marriage was an issue of concern. Adolescent girls (86%) followed by adolescent boys (59%) were considered the most affected child population groups.

Implementation of activities

IMC will be delivering these services within the three existing facilities; one community center, one static clinics of IMC in Jaramana and one static clinic Masaken Barzeh and MHPSS mobile teams in Rural Damascus, and through establishing one new community center in Rural Damascus in Al Nashabieh or Qatana which are ranked 5 on severity scale IMC is looking for the needs of the population on the ground in Rural Damascus, and will coordinate to get access to the most affected areas. The project will support children (including children with intellectual disabilities and delays), youth and adults including caregivers through structured psychosocial activities and programs in order to strengthen the resilience of the families and community. Protection Risk Analysis

Activity 5.1; Threat; Risk that existing traditional practices and community structures conflict with child protection principles (e.g. child rights messaging is perceive as a threat). Mitigation strategy: IMC will ensure full participation and consultancy with community members and leaders with the community when developing child protection materials in order to avoid any cultural inappropriateness. Activity 5.2; Threat; Risk of doing harm or perpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys (and their caregivers) through the provision of specialized child protection services if service providers are not adequately trained/ services are of low quality. Mitigation strategy: IMC will build capacity for staff to demonstrate the needed skills and knowledge for the provision of specialized services. Also IMC will ensure continued supervision and support during the implementation period. IMC will raise community awareness as well as training community members and leaders on child protection topics, which will avoid any stigmatization for all beneficiaries who will benefit from the specialized services. Activity 5.2; Threat; Risk to the wellbeing of child protection service providers due to exposure to violence (e.g. secondary or vicarious trauma). Mitigation strategy: IMC has developed a staff care policy and peer to peer support groups, to support and protect the wellbeing of its staff. Activity 5.3; Threat; Limited knowledge among stakeholder responsible to make key decisions regarding the protection of children. Challenges in measuring the impact of capacity development; compromised quality of trainings due to contextual constraints; high turnover of staff Mitigation measurement; Ensure training programs are designed to assess impact of the trainings 2. Link with other capacity building initiatives to ensure synergy 3. Support development of a cohort of core child protection trainers in Syria 4. Encourage follow-up, mentoring and coaching initiatives; 5. Ensure appropriate supervision by relevant authorities is in place

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Unstable security situation, which might limit the access for the supervisor to reach the locations and do direct monitoring and supervision of the implementations of the activities. Mitigation strategy; prepare a detailed plan for activities and remote supervision plan to supervise the activities accordingly. Shortage of skilled staff: based on the health department experience in the implementation area, MHPSS department has some concern regarding finding capacities in that area, like ECD trainers, PSS counselors, Educational specialists and other specialties. Mitigation strategy; provide specialized training to recruited staff and building capacity of the community in the targeted area. Difficulties of finding suitable places that comply with protection needs for the beneficiaries, also, getting the needed approvals on time. Tension in community, for perceived inequality, discrimination, favoritism, including risk for project staff. Mitigation strategy: IMC will establish adequate selection criteria mechanisms based on vulnerabilities, communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and for whom they are intended. Moreover, IMC will avoid the exclusion from assistance of persons with no identity documents within the community center and women safe space
