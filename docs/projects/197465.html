Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in northeast Nigeria in the three BAY states for over a decade now has been a big threat to education and poses many physical and psychosocial threats to children and their caregivers. The 2022 Humanitarian need overview shows that 8.4 Million people are in need with 58% percent projected to be children, these population of children required urgent assistance because of the criticality of the protection and education needs of children that are Displaced, separated and unaccompanied children that are at heightened risk of abuse, exploitation and neglect.
children were increasingly used as tools for various unholy acts due to the facts that they are either out of school or neglected to just go about their life with no supervision of adults, children are used as explosive device bearers by non-state armed groups, with number of children recruited and used for attack and various terrorist act increasing actively, children recruited and used by armed groups, and face rejection, inter-communal tensions and other stresses when reintegrating.
FRAD Foundation is focused on implementing integrated programming in Borno and Yobe states Between its Child Protection and Education departments. FRAD Foundation will have integrated Protection and education programming in a holistic approach to ensure children with various protection concerns are addressed and they have access to school and build their resilience to feel safer in their homes and communities.

1 - Needs

2022 HNO shows that 8.4 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance in the northeast, 84% of the people in need are women and children which makes them to be at risk of abuse with so much unforeseen vulnerabilities. the recent flooding that occurs during the raining season has further increase children vulnerabilities as it has led to the displacement of families and the use of schools as shelters to households affected by the flooding thereby making learning inaccessible to children. Children face various forms of protection risk ranging from child labor, sexual abuse and exploitation, denial of resources and poor access to health and care. these has increase the threats children boys and girls are facing at homes and in their communities. with this complex needs, it is certain and clear that this children require protection from violence, abuse, exploitation, and violations of rights, access to quality education for them to learn and continue learning, and also protection from mine risk while going to school and also in school. the current impact of climate change and ongoing conflict and continued displacements and resettlement that is currently ongoing will further increase out of school children while they move to their new settlement, it could also further lead to increase in children separation, which means they are in need of immediate, integrated case management and other psychosocial support to increase their protective factors.The risk of recruitment and kidnapping for adolescent boys and girls remains high with the breakdown of family and community protective mechanisms. The majority of the conflict-affected children and their caregivers have experienced brutal violence, loss of family members, friends and property, accumulated and protracted stress, deterioration in living conditions, increased military presence, social disruption in communities and lack of access to social services, such as schools and healthcare. These experiences have a significant impact on the development of children and the psychosocial wellbeing of adolescents and caregivers. other impact of these is that children may not be willing to go to school due to reasons from their end or from the end of their caregiver. These Project will support vulnerable children and other children living in conflict affected locations and new areas of return to attend critical education services, caregiver through the intervention will receive support to enable them manage stress and use good coping mechanism and render positive parenting to ensure children go to school and also remain in school. the project will also provide case management services to children identified with protection concerns and work towards addressing those concerns to ensure they are safe in school and communities. The project will also include Mine risk education to children in school and also at the safe space to ensure children can identify landmines and stay away from it, also Assistance will be provided to children and women that are victims of explosive ordinances

2 - Activities

Child ProtectionCase Management:FRAD will provide case management services to vulnerable children, including UASC, children at risk, children that have experienced abuse, neglect or exploitation. SAFE/SHLSFRAD establish 4 safe and healing learning space for children in 2 community of return, and rehabilitate safe spaces in communities. the safe spaces will be used to carryout SHLS activities, SAFE (supporting adolescent and their families in emergencies). SAFE Adolescent Children Support:FRAD Foundation will support adolescent children after graduation them from the SAFE Curriculum and life skills curriculum, the support will be in form of small grants that will support adolescents boys and girls to continue school while they make small income to be able to remain in school. SAFE Caregivers CAFAAG Support:FRAD will take caregivers of adolescents girls participating in the SAFE session in caregivers session, caregiver of CAAFAG will also be taken through the Growing stronger curriculum which is a curriculum specifically targeting caregivers of CAAFAG and other vulnerable communities. Education Back to School Support to ChildrenFRAD Will support out of school children from its Case management case load to go back to school and address all future challenges that will make them stop school, focus will be paid specifically on children formally associate with armed groups. Teachers TrainingFRAD Foundation will target school teachers to train them on Digital skills, this training will equip teachers with modern skills to access resources and the use of modern technologies to support and facilitate learning. The training will include practical simulation of EdTech software's that will enable learning in emergencies and learning for persons with disability. Scholastic Materials Support:FRAD will support enrolled children back to school with all necessary school materials that will support them in learning and also developing interest to continue in school and also remain in school. Community Learning Support:FRAD will develop a model that will support children to learn from their communities, Community Volunteers will be train to facilitate learning of small group of both out of school and in school children, the learning support will be an avenue where out of school children will develop interest in school and they will be enrolled. Advocacy Visits:FRAD will consistently conduct advocacy visits to prominent traditional leaders and head of ministries, and religious leaders to advocate for education enrollment of children back to school. Mine ActionEORE SessionsFRAD will conduct EORE sessions with children in schools and also safe spaces where children usually access to ensure they don't become victims of explosives ordinances, EORE sessions will also be conducted in communities with community members to train beneficiaries on EORE Support to Victims of explosive ordinancesFRAD will provide livelihood support to victims of explosives ordinance and provide equipment support to victims who become disabled as a result of explosive ordinances. Training of Trainers on EOREFRAD will conduct training of trainers targeting key critical stakeholders that will continue to administers such training to communities and schools to ensure sustainability.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

FRAD Foundation Protection/ PSEA focal person will support training staff and partners in order to ensure the sustainable incorporation of protection principles in all the projects FRAD will also work with local stakeholders in Borno State to inform the assistance we provide and help ensure that protection considerations are integrated into the full package of services for children, caregivers and communities. FRAD will deploy Protection Mainstreaming Monitoring and Evaluation System that will guide the project’s protection mainstreaming interventions. The project will integrate four key elements of protection mainstreaming which are:meaningful access to assistance and servicesprioritizing safety, dignity and doing no harmaccountability and participationempowerment of beneficiaries. FRAD will ensure these elements will be promoted through updating the current protection mainstreaming strategy at project inception. The update will be informed by findings from beneficiary assessments, staff capacity assessments, and findings from various project activities. Foundational capacity-building activities for project staff will be conducted to strengthen and promote a shared understanding of protection mainstreaming, including child safeguarding principles, and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse. FRAD will sensitize the community on our accountability mechanism for quality services and on the ways they can provide confidential feedback.
