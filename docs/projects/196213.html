Project Summary

MF aims through this project to enhancing the food security for the most vulnerable communities in Aleppo and Idleb Governorates.
The proposed intervention will support the most affected population of 11,750 IDPs - 2,165 families/ HHs in total, including (3200 G, 3050 B, 2800 W, 2700 M, 689 PWD), through providing food security in the most in need areas.
Mainly in (Idleb - Maaret Misrin sub district/ Aleppo - Azaz & Albab district), including three location; ( 3 planned camps/ MARAM RHUs Newly sites); in Idleb (Kafr Jales and Shiekh Bahr PC), and Aleppo (Ihtemlat PC), in northwest Syria (NWS).
MF intervention will be through regular food assistance by providing Cash for food, where MF will target about 2150 HHs (11,500 beneficiaries/ IDPs) with cash for food, the cash intervention/ round will be on monthly basis ( 8 rounds), with 60 $ to be allocated for each family as per the FSL cluster and Cash Working Group recommendation.
Also the project intervention in the targeted communities will be through the distribution of wheat seeds and sheep to the most affected and needy farmers to ensure their food security and support their livelihoods in the long term.
The project will also support the local market by purchasing all project inputs from the target areas.
Where the Selection priorities:
1. most affected farmers from host communities and have more than 10 dunums. As we
cannot target IDPs because of their lack of adequate land ownership
2. Agriculture is the main source of income for beneficiary households
3. Farmers directly or indirectly affected by the ongoing crisis
4. family affected and headed by a woman.
The project will enable most affected population to resist poverty and gain resilience to fight the severe economic conditions, to overcome challenges caused by conflict, to become independent from humanitarian aid and move towards self-reliance.
this project will provide an opportunity to female-headed households and engaging them to create sustainable income and lives in dignity.
MF monitoring and evaluation team will follow up and verify all activates and distribution processes.
As well as the accountability mechanism to the affected population, COVID-19 awareness sessions and precautionary measures, GBV and PSEA awareness session - where all will be took place throughout the project cycle.
Maram will endeavor to work based on FSL Cluster guidance and will keen continuous coordination with the Cluster and the other relevant agencies for more effective coordination and to avoid any duplication or overlapping of intervention.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Physical security of girls, boys, women, and men's is put at risk as a result of the violence associated with civilian populations/spaces being targeted by armed parties to the conflict; a lack of consideration for the safety and security to access/receive services; lack of consideration of contextual information (e.g. aerial bombardment) and targeting of civilian infrastructure in route to, during and directly after service provision. - Likelihood: High - Impact: High - Mitigation: Consider conflict patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing a fixed site or mobile service provision. - Ensure safety audits conducted to ensure safe access (route and location) by boys, girls, women and men,elderly and populations with decreased mobility (due to physical, visual/hearing deficits, psychological issues). - Engage with local authorities on safety and security in the community.- Training on protection mainstreaming for Humanitarian Actors.PR: Unrealistic expectations are raised amongst IDPs on the quality of service that NGOs should provide, there by causing them to prevent services Likelihood: Low, Impact: Low, Mitigating Measure: Tailor each training module to the context in Syria. Activity: Promote equal access to service in IDP camps to all residents, PR: None, Likelihood: Low, Impact: Low, Mitigating Measure: This is part of the commitment of humanitarian actors and its main aim is to mitigate risks in Settlements and camps.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

R1: Organization Acceptance by beneficiaries, communities and other stakeholders: M: Maram Foundation will prepare a community outreach plan, will attending and conduct coordination meetings with target community members and the local authorities to explain project activities. R2: Deterioration of safety and security context: M:MF maintains a security risk management system for its areas of operation in order to adapt and respond to changes in context - MF maintains a full stakeholder and power analysis for its areas of operation in order to better understand the context -MF on a daily basis reviews security information related to its areas of operation and monitors staff movement as per the current security conditions. R3: Exchange rates change significantly and therefore the budget is insufficient to reach the targeted number of beneficiaries M: MF will maintain a regular dialogue with donor in the event of change -MF conducts regular price monitoring. R4: Potential Issues with the money transfer agent / CVA.As the money transfer agent should following all MF policies, and methodology.M:The money transfer agent will be requested to provide the beneficiaries’ signatures and beneficiaries’ original vouchers along side the invoice requesting payment. The transfer agent will not be reimbursed unless he gives the original vouchers and the signed lists.Post distribution monitoring and verification of payment, Complaints and feedback mechanism will be in place for reporting.
