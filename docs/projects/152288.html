Project Summary

Mercy Crops has been supporting communities in the northeastern part of the country reaching more than 165,000 people including ongoing activities in Damboa and Biu LGAs for the past several years, with capabilities of maximizing benefits to the targeted communities through creating synergies. MC will be supporting vulnerable people from both IDPs and host communities in the two LGAs. The project will focus on livelihood activities and rehabilitating social infrastructure with aim of contributing towards early recovery through training and vouchers distribution to start small businesses as well for emergency income generation and rehabilitating social infrastructure mainly access roads, primary education facilities, health facilities, water sources and drainage networks, and other community owned properties through training and cash-for-work. The targeted population will be selected among most vulnerable people to be engaged in the project activities.
A total of 7,400 households will be engaged or 51,800 household members are expected to benefit in Damboa and Biu on project activities during the year.

Needs

Damboa LGA is classified in the severity needsa map as High with 73,946 with a returnees population size of 25,227 and a total of 102,356 which makes more than 72% are displaced that shows the high levels need for support interventions. And, although Biu is classified as Medium on the severity map, it has a total population of 102, 659 out of which 54,870 are remaining needy people to make the number of needy people more than 53% of the total population. Furthermore, until very recently, not much humanitarian aid organizations were based in Biu LGA in support of the vulnerable people.This project will build on what has been done by MC to address the needs through supporting micro-business opportunities to women with training programs to target beneficiaries. It also provides opportunities of emergency income and job creation to the most affected people in the LGA. It will also create opportunities to rehabilitate community infrastructure through provision of emergency employment opportunities.

Activities

Supporting livelihood stabilization and income recovery through supporting small businesses focusing on women that involves training on small business management and cash grant to a total of 1,300 targeted people (this same 1,300 beneficiaries will be provided with vocational training mainly in micro business management). Improve access to infrastructure and basic social services through rehabilitation of public service facilities such as rehabilitation of primary education facilities, health care facilities, water networks through training and cash-for-work. This will involve 3,250 people of the two LGAs in cash-for-work activities for 108 days following basic training on rehabilitation and maintenance to community members. Providing opportunities of emergency income generating activities from rehabilitation of community infrastructure that involves 2,850 people working for 108 days.A total of 7,400 households will be engaged or 51,800 household members are expected to benefit in Damboa and Biu on project activities during the year.
