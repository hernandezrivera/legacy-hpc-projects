Project Summary

The project aims at contributing to the education of survivors of conflicts especially Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) considering the conflict and Covid-19 threat. This is taking place at a time WILPF Cameroon has completed a Gender Conflict Analysis which enabled the organization to report on the difficult conditions in which the IDPs live in host communities. Generally, with conflicts ongoing, host families in the Littoral region are overwhelmed by new arrivals of IDPs from North-West and South-West regions. The overcrowded living conditions and poverty in the families do not allow a real intergration and observation of COVID-19's preventive barrier measures. The economic situation of IDPs and their host families has also deteriorated with the multiplication of cases of abuse on their rights.
The purpose of the projet is to raise awareness among IDP communities on peace and social cohesion, sensitize on the prevention of COVID 19 infection in host communities and bring adequate support to address some of their needs. Key intervention priorities include:
- Giving IDPs the space to speak out about their condition and experience of their rights. This is done through our Call center, with the tool free number 8243;
- Holding community awareness campaigns educators on GBV, social cohesion and provide information on the progress of the situation around the Covid-19 pandemic;
- Providing IDPs with necessary protection materials for the pandemic and basic needs during meetings ;
- Providing particularly women and girls IDP with dignity kits.
WILPF Cameroon intends to take advantage of the sympathy assets built up during meetings with IDPs ans part of the GCA to conduct targeted sensitization for their benefit. This action will make it possible to reduce vulnerability to Covid-19 and address some of the specific needs of IDPs in relation to their integration. The project will be implemented in 10 months in the year 2021, in two localities (Douala and Melong) in the Littoral region.
Baseline:
1- WILPF Cameroon has an early warning mechanism on elections, GBV and human rights, with an operational call center with a tool-free number;
2- WILPF Cameroon has undertaken a small-scale survey in Yaoundé and through the Call center, from April to August 2020, to assess the impact of Covid-19 on IDPs' rights.

1 - Besoins / Needs

With the project coordinators leading the two project locations, we will conduct community awareness action and support on a sample of the most vulnerable IDPs to first assess their situation in relation to GBV, human right abuses related to COVID-19 crisis and provide them with the information and support likely to enable them to improve their condition. Then we will provide them with materials for their protection, dignity kits for women and girls, basic needs. The project will be implemented based on the Call center that freely sets a space for IDPs to speak out, as we have experienced while implementing a project on assessing the impact of Covid-19 on IDPs' rights. The following activities will be carried out: a) Preparatory phase- Sampling- Elaboration of an interview and reporting form with IDPs, with key messages specific to IDPS on GVB, human rights and COVID-19 - Briefing of WILPF Cameroon and members; local project coordinators and IDP community focal points on the processes, to better involve beneficiaries in project management- Communications around the call center and Covid-19 protocols. b) Operational phase - Exchanges with IDPs on GVB and human rights related to COVID-19 and collection of their issues, challenges and needs in the context of conflicts and the pandemic - Distribution of protection kits to IDPs in Douala and Melong, Littoral region.- Counselling on preventive measures to be taken for their protection and safety. Counselling is done on the basis of individual assessment- Community awareness meetings in target localities on project issues- Identification of children and IDPs in the target localities facing schooling challenges to educate them and their famililies on social cohesion in conflict and Covid-19 settings. c) Reporting - Monitoring and evaluation of the project based on the approved indicators; - Comprehensive report of community awareness and support, with recommendations for advocacy. COVID-19 is one more threat and one too many for those most at risk who are already struggling to survive and make their voices heard in Cameroonian society. It is expected that this project provides more concrete support to improve the daily lives of IDPs and help them prevent COVID-19 infection in their host communities. Targeted IDPs will also be able to claim their rights, which have been severely impacted by the health crisis.
