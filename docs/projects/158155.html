Project Summary

Pending confirmation from grovernment, Linha Verde the tollfree Idai reponse hotline can be expanded to also serve the areas affected by cyclone Kennetheth until the end of December. To promote accountability to affected populations (AAP), Linha Verde is designed to support three core AAP elements: information provision; complaints, feedback and response mechanisms; and, community participation. Linha Verde will be operated by a team of multi-lingual national operators, with at least 50% of the call centre operators being female. Through its different communication channels, the platform will systematically share and capture data whilst taking account of the different needs within communities from an age, gender, and diversity perspective. Needs and priorities will be circulated to response partners through agreed feedback referral pathways, which include guidance on response time to feedback to ensure feedback loop closure, document programme adjustment and to build trust with affected communities. As AAP is a key connector in the emergency, early recovery and development nexus, Linha Verde will facilitate evidenced-based decision making.
