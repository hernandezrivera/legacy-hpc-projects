Project Summary

The humanitarian crisis in Syria has been exacerbated by the widespread use of explosive ordnance (EO), posing significant risks and requiring different mitigation strategies to reduce its impact on the civilian population and the humanitarian sector delivering aid to those in need. Although the scale and scope of EO contamination in Syria remains undefined, according to the 2024 Summary HNO, more than 65% of the Syrian population (14.4 million people) are at risk from EO contamination, which represents over 20% increase from 2023 (11.5 million people). According to OCHA access analysis of June 2023, the presence of mines and EO remains a major access impediment to the safe delivery of humanitarian assistance in 146 sub-districts (54 percent of all sub-districts). EO contamination is also acknowledged in the UN Common Risk Register as a high residual and inherent, strategic and operational risk to UN operations.
Mine action is a key humanitarian priority and enabler for the wider humanitarian response and is critical enablers for safe delivery of humanitarian assistance. In the absence of comprehensive technical survey and clearance, continuous hostilities for the last 12 years have been constantly creating new layers of explosive contamination. Based on open-source data (ACLED), from 1 January 2017 to 31 December 2023, a total of 71,289 EO incidents were recorded. This is an average of 31 Explosive Ordnance incidents per day.
Interventions: The overall aim of UNMAS project is to reduce the impact of EO on the civilian population and humanitarian workers through effective sector coordination and implementation of humanitarian mine action activities, in line with the framework of agreements between the Syrian State and UN agencies, funds and programmes, as well as Security Council resolution, S/RES/2672(2023). Under this project, UNMAS will provide advisory support to the national authorities, and technical and operational support to the HMA actors, including through accreditation and quality assurance of HMA activities. UNMAS will also continue coordinating the HMA Area of Responsibility (AoR) at both Whole of Syria (WoS) and Damascus Hub levels, ensuring the integration of the HMA needs into UN strategic plans and advocacy documents, and disseminating relevant EO data and analysis.
UNMAS will follow an integrated approach in implementing HMA interventions, where it is capable of prioritizing and implementing several activities simultaneously, to ensure a comprehensive response. Furthermore, HMA activities will be planned jointly with the humanitarian and early recovery partners to fulfill their role as integral enablers of their operations.
All components of humanitarian mine action (risk education, victim assistance, survey clearance) are critical, but UNMAS will focus on expanding survey and clearance, as the only way to permanently remove the threat posed by EO.
UNMAS operations will be fulfilled through the deployment of non-technical Survey (NTS), Multi tasking Teams (MTTs) and integrated Explosive Ordnance risk education/ Victim Assistance (EORE/VA)teams. The teams will support ongoing and planned humanitarian and early recovery operations of the UN and the wider humanitarian and early recovery community and establish the extent of contamination in their areas of deployment.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

UNMAS objectives is to support ongoing & planned humanitarian & ERL operations, establish the extent of contamination in the country through: Coordination: As the MA AoR coordinator, UNMAS will ensure that HMA is integrated within the humanitarian coordination mechanisms & the EO threat integrated within strategic planning. UNMAS will provide technical expertise to the HMA partners & humanitarian and ERL actors. UNMAS will continue to manage the Information Management System for Mine Action (IMSMA),which generates custom-made information products. UNMAS will ensure the quality of activities implemented by HMA operators in the country are in line with the IMAS through regular QA & monitoring visits & technical accreditation of international & national actors.Survey & clearance: UNMAS will deploy Multi Tasking teams (MTTs) within UN hubs, prioritizing(Aleppo, Damascus & Homs),that are mobile & able to deploy rapidly in response to EO risks. The MTTs will conduct both survey and clearance, survey will include desk assessments, data analysis and field based community liaison. The survey helps to define the presence/absence of EO, marks & records them to ensure appropriate planning for the safe delivery of humanitarian aid & services. The MTT will then remove&destroy the items found. MTTs will have gender parity. EORE/VA: To complement the work of the MTTs, UNMAS will deploy integrated EORE/VA teams, to deliver EORE to civilians &humanitarian field personnel. They will identify EO victims, refer victims to available services, provide basic VA services, collect information on EO presence & incidents in support of UNMAS survey efforts. Through direct EORE sessions, people in EO-affected communities receive life-saving information to reduce the likelihood of EO incidents. To maximise reach, UNMAS will train EORE facilitators from humanitarian& ERL actors& develop EORE education materials & messages that are tailored to age, gender, profession, social responsibility & localised threats. UNMAS will provides EO awareness & focal points training to humanitarian workers to support safer delivery of humanitarian activities to ensure integration of EORE messages in those activities. VA operations will prioritise persons with injuries &disabilities living in EO contaminated areas, including victims of EO incidents& their families (Children, elderly & female-headed households are given specific attention). The VA response includes:1)data collection & analysis,2)access to specialised health services 3)disability inclusion. All VA activities are guided by the twin-track approach recommended by the IASC: A) Access to VA Services is enhanced through a holistic& multidisciplinary approach;where the VA services provided include rehabilitation (physical & sensorial)assistive products& prosthetic devices & PSS.Operational activities will take place from the Syria hub. project submitted at WoS level given the WoS coordination function that UNMAS will continue.
