Project Summary

The proposed project aims to provide health services for both Male and Female in Aleppo and Idlib Governorates through a package of services as follows;

1-Support Shamarin specialized hospital code: B0204014 to support 17400 (8526 male, 8874 females, 696 elderly, 4350 with disabilities) beneficiaries in A'zaz district. The main services are necessary specialized medical services, especially in the following fields:

# ER cases 7200
# Specialized clinic consultations 7200
# Major Orthopedics operations 960
# Minor orthopedics operations 1920

2-Support one rehabilitation and Prosthetics center B0204009 in Azaz: the center will provide free-of-charge prostheses to people with amputation or paralysis to mitigate their physical, social, and psychological suffering, besides training sessions for them.

# of prosthetic prostheses delivered 360 (167 male, 133 female)
# of patients receiving rehabilitation services 400 (196 male, 204 female)
# of physiotherapy sessions. 2400

3-Support the specialized Mental health facility in A'zaz (B0204003) to provide secondary mental health care to both OPD & inpatients for acute and chronic severe mental health problems, and Substance abuse disorders rehabilitation unit for 1,612 (978 male, 634 female).

# of MHPSS consultations provided 6,100
# Of patients who received PM+ sessions 200
# of hospitalizations 600

4-Support Al-Madina paediatric & Maternity hospital (G0700140).
This key hospital is CEMONC facility providing SRH & Delivery services and essential pediatric and neonatal services for 41,702 (2502 male, 39,200 female).

# of OPD consultations provided 75,000
# of vaginal deliveries attended by skilled attendants 3000
# of C-sections performed 350
# Of hospitalization 3,000

5-Cover the management technical activities of the EPI central team in Aleppo governorate (Supporting effective and equitable routine immunization services to eligible children):

EPI immunization activities inside N. of Syria are implemented through vaccination teams, which administratively belong to NGO implementers, and technically follow the SIG technical committee. In Aleppo governorate, and in absence of government, a supervisory team is formed to manage the technical issues of the program (governorate supervisor, in addition to logistic, social mobilizer, data officer, and finance officer), in addition to governorate level there are 5 supervisory teams at district level ( district supervisor, logistic, social mobilizer, and data entry ), these supervisory teams manage the technical aspects of EPI in the field under supervision and support of SIG technical committee in Gaziantep in Turkey. This work is technically supervised and supported directly by WHO experts.

•Children under one year of age for one year to complete the vaccination schedule (67,068)
•Pregnant women (67068)

6- Support the service provision in two Primary Healthcare centers (PHCs).
PAC is currently supporting Ehteimlat (B0204126) and Ein Shib (G0700175) PHCs, providing essential healthcare services according to the approved Essential Health Service Package (EHSP). These centers are integrated with OTP and RRT services to provide CMAM, IYCF, and MAMI services for PLWs and children in the target locations through facility-based and community-based interventions.
# of OPD consultations 72,000
# Beneficairies received at least one healthcare service (Unique) 40,000
# Of patients who received at least one treatment procedure 57,600

7- Run one Hemodialysis center in Jarabulus code B0208014: This center will serve 35 ESRD patients with both acute and chronic kidney diseases by supporting a hemodialysis center in Jarabulus district and will provide 3000 hemodialysis sessions.

All health services are integrated with nutrition, protection and WASH.
M&E plan using the project indicators and measures will be developed and followed monthly. PAC M&E officer and 4 field officers will supervise the plan implementation.
