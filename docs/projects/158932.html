Project Summary

Dan Church Aid DCA\NCA with Ahli Arab Hospital (AAH) will support trauma patients by providing much needed surgeries for them. AAH will target 130 patients who will receive 200 surgical procedure regarding Limb Reconstruction, Ilizarov application, Ilizarov readjustments for referred cases, tendon repair cases, external ﬁxators, bone grafts, skin grafts, nerve repair, Intramedullary nail and bone cement, AAH will also conduct physiotherapy for the cases as each case will have 5-6 sessions or according to the need, where the total will be 1000 physiotherapy sessions. As Trauma GMR patints were identiﬁes by the HNO as a vulnerable group, AAH hospital efforts are fully permitted and coordinated with the MOH, and mentioned in the MOH's emergency plan as a pack-up hospital, moreover there is on-going referrals from MOH, to AAH. AAH-MOH relationship is led by the AAH senior management and MOH's deputy minister. Additionally the AAH is represented in the MOH screening committee by it's senior orthopedic consultant surgeon. Furthermore, DCA\NCA will make sure quality assurance of the program implementation provided by DCA/NCA, monitor the implementation by attending partner’ activities on a regular basis and by conducting periodic review meetings. The monitoring will focus on project progress/ tracking sheets, logistical frameworks, ﬁnancial, procurement progress of activities, delivery of outputs, and achievement of short-term outcomes as described in the project document.

1 - Needs

According to the Health Cluster, conflict-related trauma cases are still burdening Health Care System in Gaza. Since the start of Great March of Return (GMR) that has started end of March 2018 until the end of June 2019, around 31,000 have been injured. Moreover, over 44% of the casualties arriving to the hospitals have gunshot wounds where 139 amputations have taken place with the numbers expected to further increase. These are complex and serious injuries that require specialized treatment and/or rehabilitation. Meanwhile, rates of bone infection amongst injured patients have continued to grow, increasing the risk of amputations and even death. Furthermore, a significant proportion out of the 31,000 injured patients’ still need rehabilitation (postoperative care, physiotherapy Therefore, and according to the Health Cluster Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO)/ Humanitarian Response Plan HRP 2020 since January 2019, a total of 3,730 people has been inured as 1,700 injured people with need for Limb Reconstruction (LR). According to the WHO August report, the total figure of people injured stands at 31, 3386. 18,549 casualties arrived at the emergency departments (ED) of MoH and NGO hospitals, 1,221 were children. 7,545 cases were gunshot injuries; these account for 41% of the total casualties arriving at the hospitals. Out of 7,545 gunshot injuries, 87% are limb gunshot injuries. Since the demonstration started, AAH targeted the most critically affected patients, and those in need of lifesaving surgeries. Furthermore, the need for inpatient surgical care at AAH level is essential to avoid losing lives and preventing disability. This includes complex reconstructive wounds and orthopedic care, intensive care, blood transfusion and Infection Control. Since the Great March of Return (GMR), AAH has achieved 463 surgical trauma procedures for 333 patients as around 150 of them were Limb Reconstruction (LR)/ Complex Trauma Cases. AHH medical assessment showed that many cases still face excessive damage to the bone, irreversible damage to neurovascular structures and extensive soft tissue damage. Managing these injuries requires high resources from the AAH specialized health medical team. The medical team indicated that more than 200 referred cases in 2018 who received LR surgical procedure still needs Ilizarov application, Ilizarov re-adjustments Tendon repair cases, external fixators, bone grafts, skin grafts nerve repair, Intramedullary nail, bone cement based on their Health indicators by targeting 130 patients who will receive 200 surgical procedure regarding Limb Reconstruction. 200 surgical procedures will be as each patient might need 1-2 surgeries. In addition, for each case there is a need 5-6 of physiotherapy sessions or according to need where the total will be 1000 physiotherapy sessions. It is estimated that that between 25 and 40 per cent of patients with such complications go on to develop some form of bone infection over a 12-month period following the injury. The urgency to invest in services that can prevent amputations and restore lives must not be underestimated. Proper wound management and infection control has become a major issue in Gaza. Multiple factors, such as nosocomial infection, over crowdedness, lack of appropriate environmental hygiene and lack of resources, result in increased rates. AAH has managed wounds for hundreds of injured patients. However, some of the wounds were not healing due to infections. Infection control is crucial to minimize complications such as osteomyelitis and to prevent amputations and even death.

8.6.c - How will feedback be used?

DCA/NCA and AAH will work closely to ensure quality of health care for trauma and limb reconstruction inured people. To ensure accountability to patients several mechanisms are set in place: First, a functional complaint /feedback mechanism is set in place. Second, DCA/NCA will randomly meet patients to have their impression and satisfaction on the level of service provided. Third, DCA/NCA will monitor the partner practices and their compliance with CHS benchmarks especially the beneficiary complaint and feedback mechanism and supports them to enhance such practices at both the project and organizational level. The complaint handling systems are emphasized in all DCA/NCA’s/NCA’s cooperation agreements with its partners. Recently, DCA\NCA conducted a workshop to AAH and its partners the complaints and feedback mechanism which provides safe and easy access for the targeted patients communities to ensure the mechanism in place, the system have a to raise their thoughts, positive or negative feedback, and minor or major complaints at project implementation level. In addition, the mechanism will enable the targeted beneficiaries to participate effectively in the project implementation and monitoring processes and to inform the decision-making process. The receiving feedbacks either by online link, verbal feedback or recording complains received by other channels such as the hotline and feedback boxes. The system includes clear procedures to guarantee the accuracy of receiving, recording and documenting the complaints. The system includes different categories of complaints with different authority levels to deal with the complaints, decide about the complaint and inform the complainants about the decision. In addition, the system includes restricted procedures to ensure privacy, confidentiality and information security and includes very specific procedures to deal with gender-sensitive complaints in order to maintain the privacy and confidentiality of the provided info

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

The proposed intervention has been designed to fill the specific critical gaps in LR services. Further assistance is needed based on the Health conditions of patients to address the outstanding needs as revealed by the AAH current assessment through appropriate preventive strategies. In addition, the rehabilitation program will be implemented in helping patients to recover better from their injuries and regain mobility and functioning. This ongoing support is vital to treating the high number of Palestinians suffering shattered bones. through proposing a set of integrated activities and adopting approaches that respond to the identified needs while maintaining the dignity, safety, and security of the beneficiaries. The project design recognizes the rights and needs of beneficiaries in terms of safety, security, and dignity and proposes activities that foster the realization of these needs and rights. The provided activities will support to alleviate the public health risks that the targeted patients are facing especially women and girls that includes of Right to Health and Right to live, as those people rights are violated. Regarding the trauma patients and limb reconstruction needed patients who are mostly affected by GMR, their rights to prompt access to health care which is not fully met as the MOH capacity is limited and health care system in Gaza is collapsing. As a result, DCA/NCA and AAH through the HP fund will maintain their rights and mainstream protection by providing the needed services. As well, AAH will minimize the referral to outside which is also maintaining the right to access health care. DCA\NCA Partners women organizations (WAC and CFTA) who are willing to cooperate and support AAHs operations, both have a good access to GBV referral system. This will enable AAH to response quickly in case of detecting GBV cases. In this project, AAH will refer the detected cases of GBV to relevant service providers. Addressing the GBV cases during the implementation will be dealt with confidentially and will be refereed to specialized agencies to protect the GBV survivals. The project considers gender aspects and disaggregates data during all phases from the need assessment, and planning to implementation, monitoring and reporting.AAH have a specialized, mostly female, data collection and analysis team who have been trained on the tools and techniques of gender sensitive data collection and analysis. A complaint mechanism will be in place to enable women to express their concerns and to provide feedback on the project activities, AAH also have database systems that document sex disaggregated data. DCA/NCA’s s partners (WAC and CFTA) will support AAH to enhance the capacity of health Medical team to be able to detect the GBV cases and refer them to appropriate services considering the national the national protocols and the protection cluster guidelines. DCA\NCA has gender-based violence strategy and updated gender policy to detect any violence against women, through the reporting period, DCA\NCA and AAH committed to its gender policy, anticorruption policy, and code of conduct.All necessary safety and security measures will be taken into consideration as usual when implementing the project activities to prevent incidences and reduce safety and security risks AAH and DCA/NCA will also keep close monitoring for the security situation in the Gaza Strip and will advise the donor and partners in case any emergency indicators arise. AHH and DCA/NCA will also adjust timeframe and activities in case any emergency happens to avoid security and safety risks.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

According to the Health Cluster HNO, trauma cases related to GMR are considered vulnerable group so, any such case is considered vulnerable and will be accepted based on AAH capacity. Also, any GMR referred or coordinated case from MOH that matched AAH capacity will be considered. AAH in partnership with DCA/NCA has designed the project in a gender sensitive manner where women, men, boys and girls will be served at AAH in terms of limb reconstruction surgeries. In addition, DCA/NCA will use its women empowerment network to support women and girls in case of GBV so, they will be referred to either WAC and or CFT according to the need and geographical place.
