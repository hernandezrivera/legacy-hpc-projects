End Date

30/10/2020

Project Summary

UNICEF as the Sector Lead for Nutrition will facilitate bi-weekly nutrition cluster coordination meetings, provide technical support for optimal IYCF-e in the context of Covid-19. Capacity building for caregivers and families of children under the age of five years to conduct MUAC screening at home. UNICEF will also procure essential lifesaving therapeutic foods to preposition in every district and health facility to facilitate treatment with acute malnutrition across the country . Expansion of the scope of communication for development on IYCF in the context of Covid 19 to ensure greater reach of pregnant and lactating women with nutrition messages. Coordination will be strengthened to ensure a standardized nutrition response. Optimal Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies (IYCF-e) in the context of Covid 19 will be supported . Facilitate continuing provision of lifesaving nutrition interventions in view of the drought emergency with emphasis on Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) targeting 606,349 children, pregnant and lactating women affected by drought and COVID-19. Integrating early identification and treatment of children with acute malnutrition into COVID_19 assessments and preventive services. Moniitor possible deterioration of food and nutrition security due to impact of COVID-19 . Facilitate integrated risk communication and community engagement on IYCF in the context of Covid 19 . Support will also be given to improve the quality of the IMAM programme.
