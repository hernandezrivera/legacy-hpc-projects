Project Summary

The activities listed under this plan are aiming to improve living conditions for IDPs in Atmeh Camp through water supply, solid waste management, sanitation system supply, and hygiene promotion activities. Under this plan, GC will be able to respond to the essential WASH services for the anticipated camp population of 140,000 people due to the ongoing conflict.
The activities also are aiming to improve living conditions for host communities and IDPs through sanitation system rehabilitation for the new informal settlements in Northern Maaret Masrin and Azaz area, where the plan is to assist 140,000 people, where the sewage from several villages flowed into this natural drainage because of the lack of sewage drainage system. The sewage was able to penetrate the soil and the natural drainage (river) was usually dry in the summer. Now with the size of newly established settlements and the surrounding population, the flow into the natural drainage has increased and it is wet year-round.
