Project Summary

Based on the WASH assessments that were conducted in Jun-Aug-19 (HRP 2020), The assessments revealed that, despite overall slight improvement of WASH conditions in Syria, vast WASH-related needs continue to exist in many parts of Syria, affecting various population groups
Access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations, IDPs living in IDP sites
MF in Turkey office is remotely managing the delivery of integrated WASH assistance with main-streamed protection and advocacy in Idleb Governorate. Timely humanitarian relief and enhanced protection to vulnerable is delivered to those affected by emergency situations in the settlements. Basic WASH assistance is delivered to the targeted community by taking into account the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls, in addition, to take into consideration the special needs for disabilities people, especially during the infrastructure designs.
Due to the emergency security situation that has occurred in Idleb Governorate in recent months and referring to emergency needs in addition to camps management request from WASH cluster in order to provide the main WASH services in Idleb camps through INGOs and NGOs, Maram Foundation seeks to provide emergency needs as much as possible to help the people in need in different locations at Idleb camps with coordination and collaboration by WASH cluster, local authorities, and camps management in the area.
Where Maram Foundation has full access and works since the beginning of 2013 in Idleb in different sectors including WASH, in the same context, in addition to that Maram Foundation will respond to affected IDPs from the recent emergency situation in Idleb area through main WASH services.
Maram Foundation is applying for HPC to provide WASH emergency response in many locations in Idleb area as a result of the large influx of IDPs and limited access to services.
Since Maram has a current WASH project to provide around 20,000 individuals (4,000 households HHs) living in nine camps in Idleb governorate including six camps in Harim- Al Dana and three camps in Idleb -Maaret Tamsrin sub-district with basic WASH services till the end of March 2020. So, MF is applying for HPC to continue the project and provided the emergency WASH services at the same level, in addition, Maram will constantly be targeting new arrivals at all the project's phases.
The provided WASH activities will be drinking water, solid waste removal, de-sludging latrine septic tanks, construction or rehabilitation latrines, Hygiene kits distribution and hygiene awareness sessions. The project design will be according to SPHERE standards and WASH guidance in line with current priorities (note that Al AMAL and Kafar losien camps will be provided only with HK and rehabilitation latrines because it is covered by Maram with other funds).
Furthermore, the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults. The provision of sanitation services will be consulted with the targeted camps and communities, and take into account the specific needs of each group. Maram Foundation also revised the content of hygiene kits by involving the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during previews implemented projects.
The Project also designed to provide disabilities, elderly and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups needs into consideration during the design phase of these infrastructures, with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘People with Special Needs’, in addition, to provide access to proper gender-sensitive and disability WASH facilities in camps, schools, child-friendly spaces, and health facilities.

4 - Needs

As mentioned in HNO 2020 WASH sector, access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations and IDPs living in sites, in particular those in informal or overcrowded sites. Newly displaced populations require a timely and quality response based on sufficient preparedness. IDPs in formal sites and informal settlements are highly dependent on continued humanitarian assistance. Furthermore, and behalf of UN agencies REACH, Idleb and surrounding areas witnessed an exponential intensification of aerial bombardment precipitating mass displacement across the region. The majority of arrivals were reported in Dana sub-district.MF conducts continuous assessments in the camps in which it operates to update the beneficiaries' data and register new arrivals, in addition to evaluating the services provided and registering the new uncovered needs. So MF conducted a WASH rapid assessment using different sources of information on Feb -20. It has been confirmed that there are large numbers of new arrivals (around 3500 IDPs) due to the recent events in Idlib in all camps where Maram provides emergency WASH services there.Which means, there is still a need to continue covering the basic WASH services provided by Maram (drinking water, garbage collection, and desludging septic tank) in seven camps (Lastom Wahdakom, Al Sultan, Al Gofran, Al konitrat, Al Farhan, Al karkat, and Has camps), in addition, to provide those camps, Al Amal, and Kafar Lusien camps with hygiene kits distribution and hygiene awareness, the total beneficiaries 20,000 IDPs including new arrivals.Additionally, as mentioned in the assessment, these camps need to increase toilet blocks to reduce the number of people using toilets, to be 25-30 people/one toilet.Moreover, Maram will provide all WASH needs in these camps to cover all the IDP's needs including new arrivals.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

There are several protection risks that Maram can face during the implementation of the project, including:1-Protection risk: Toilets represent GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7; Access to toilets not available for all; Likelihood: High, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: Ensure context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during the design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned. 2- Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks). Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with staff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming. 3- Protection risk: Health risks associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases. Likelihood: High, Impact: Medium Mitigating Measure: Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors. 4-Protection risk: Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion; Timing and process of distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV. Likelihood: High, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: Promote Establishment of adequate management plan; Promote involvement communities in distribution planning and feedback on package for improvement.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions, risks, and mitigations:Risk 1: Organizations Acceptance by beneficiaries, communities and other stakeholders:CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: Maram Foundation will prepare a community outreach plan, will attending and conduct coordination meetings with target community members and the local authorities to explain project activities, targets, and purposes.Risk 2: Deterioration of safety and security context:CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: In general, the security situation will be acceptable because of the project located in the demilitarized zone, but any way Maram Foundation, in addition to the procedures that are always taken into consideration in all projects:- MF maintains a security risk management system for its areas of operation in order to adapt and respond to changes in context- MF maintains a full stakeholder and power analysis for its areas of operation in order to better understand the context- MF on a daily basis reviews security information related to its areas of operation and monitors staff movement as per the current security conditions.Risk 3: Exchange rates change significantly and therefore the budget is insufficient to reach the targeted number of beneficiariesCONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: - MF will maintain a regular dialogue with its donor in the event of a significant change- MF conducts regular price monitoring, utilizing existing information where available- MF will contract with the contractor in the dollar to avoid the risk.Risk 4: MF unable to maintain access to the most vulnerable populations despite changes in conflict dynamics inside SyriaCONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: MF has adopted a Whole-of-Syria structure in order to provide multiple entry-points to vulnerable affected populations and to enable a more flexible response.Risks 5: HLP clear documentsCONTINGENCY / MITIGATION: MF staff will verify all the HLP documents available through the local council and actual community members.
