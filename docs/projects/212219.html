Project Summary

Needs.
The population of the targeted locations by this project proposal (Duma - - Arbin) is about 275,076 people including, 130,637 children under 18 years.
In Rural Damascus, there is a large percentage of returnee children who are vulnerable, all facts increased protection risks including physical violence and sexual exploitation. Deteriorating living conditions, frequent exposure to violence and repeated displacement have profoundly affected children and adolescents’ psychological well-being. There has been a significant increase in the proportion of girls and boys displaying signs of distress, Mosaic has three centers providing limited educational and child protection services in the Eastern Ghouta of Rural Damascus governorate/ Duma (Harasta, Misraba, and Meidaa), in addition to two health centers.
According to the needs assessment carried out by Mosaic in 2023 for the beneficiaries of these centers, it was found that more than 80% of children don’t have their right to a dignified, safe life, education, food, and health, and they suffer from forms of deprivation, neglect, and violence, which exposes them to the risk of school dropout, child labor, and begging.
In line with UNICEF mandate to uphold the rights of all children, and work to ensure every child is protected, healthy and educated, focusing on the children left behind by wider economic and social progress. And depending on Mosaic capacity, community acceptance and needed approvals from relevant authorities to implement the different and various programs in many Syrian governorates, we are submitting this proposal. Moreover; Mosaic has Signed a renewable Memorandum of Understanding with Education ministry valid to the end of 2024.
Intervention.
1- Provide children with Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) for the children aged 3-5 years through early childhood motivation activities.
2- Provide children and youth with non-formal education programs, including remedial classes and Self-Learning Program (SLP).
3- Provide Child protection services including GBV awareness and prevention.
Implementation of activities.
• Education including early childhood activities and CP activities will be provided through two centers for each intervention and outreach community teams in close coordination with Rural Damascus Education directorate, Education sector and protection sector.
• The above package of the interventions will be conducted in two sub-districts that administratively belong to Rural Damascus governorate (Duma – Arbin) sub-districts.
• Total and breakdown of beneficiaries are provided below.
The most prominent protection risks that children suffer from in the targeted locations includes (Various deprivations - School dropout – child labor and its accompanying exploitation and abuse - Domestic and social violence - Neglect and lack of care. - early marriage, not only girls but boys as well - sexual harassment)
The major challenges that have exacerbated protection risks, including Deteriorating financial conditions. and harsh environments, overcrowded homes, and schools affected the healthy and balanced growth of children, their psychological well-being, and their opportunities in future life, especially children who suffer from psychological or health problems and those with special needs.
Mosaic all staff well trained and have the proper knowledge of PSEA rules with regular refreshment on its regulations and application.
Moreover, reporting mechanism is clear and published in all Mosaic working sites.
