Project Summary

The protracted crisis in the NWSW has distorted social, cultural, and community structures, has affected women, girls, boys and men differently. They all face common and specific protection risks. Gender-based violence, including sexual violence, physical assault and forced marriage, primarily affect women and girls. They face oppressive gender norms and ever greater poverty and hardship as household heads and family providers confronted with the killing or exiling of their husbands. Protection monitoring data indicate that males represent the vast majority of those exposed to torture or inhuman treatment, theft, extortion, destruction of personal property, arbitrary or unlawful arrest and/or detention (around 80 per cent in 2021 and 2022). Most children especially those delivered within the crisis period lack civil documentation (birth certificates) as many people are displaced
The project interns to provide an integrated life saving protection support to crisis affected persons especially women and children who are most vulnerable. This will be realized through the following activities;
GENDER BASED VIOLENCE RESPONSE
 Provide PSS to at least 200 GBV survivors
 Organize 6 GBV and peace building campaigns in 3 divisions
 Create GBV men and women support groups in 10 communities to continuously carryout GBV sensitization in their communities
 Provide safe shelter to at least 100 GBV survivors
 Create mobile safe spaces for GBV survivors in 10 hard to reach areas
 Economically empower 100 female survivors through trainings and support with direct cash to carryout income generating activities to reduce their vulnerability
 Distribute vulnerability kits and dignity kits using vouchers
PYSCHOSOCIAL SUPPORT
 Provide mental health and psychosocial support to 500 crisis affected persons in distress
CHLID PROTECTION
 Establish birth certificates for 200 children who do not have them in accordance with civil status registration

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The protracted crisis in the NWSW has distorted social, cultural, and community structures, has affected women, girls, boys and men differently. They all face common and specific protection risks. Gender-based violence, including sexual violence, physical assault and forced marriage, primarily affect women and girls. They face oppressive gender norms and ever greater poverty and hardship as household heads and family providers confronted with the killing or exiling of their husbands. Protection monitoring data indicate that males represent the vast majority of those exposed to torture or inhuman treatment, theft, extortion, destruction of personal property, arbitrary or unlawful arrest and/or detention (around 80 per cent in 2021 and 2022). Most children especially those delivered within the crisis period lack civil documentation (birth certificates) as many people are displaced The overarching objective of this project is to reduce the vulnerability, increase the safety and alleviate the suffering of crisis affected persons in Mezam, Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions. This will be achieved through a combination of protection activities as followsGBV response- Raise awareness on GBV through sensitization and organization of campaigns on GBV, peace building to atleast 10000 people in the 3 divisions.- Case management and referral to ensure appropriate care for 200 GBV survivors - Provide safe shelter to 100 survivors of GBV - Create 6 mobile safe spaces for women and girls to come together and to widen the support networks available to survivors of SGBV.- Economically empower 100 female survivors through trainings and support with direct cash to carryout income generating activities to reduce their vulnerability - Distribute vulnerability kits and dignity kits using vouchers to the 100 survivors at the safe shelter- Create 6 GBV community committees in the 3 divisions to carrying out awareness-raising on GBV and available services for GBV response, provide psychological first Aid , liase GBV cases for further management at the level of the office and monitor gender-based violence cases PYSCHOSOCIAL SUPPORT- Identification and provision of psychosocial support to particularly 500 crisis affected persons in distress Civil Documentation - Establish birth certificates for 200 children who do not have them in accordance with civil status registration- Monitoring and evaluation of project - Establish feedback and complain committees in the 3 Divisions, and suggestion boxesThe project will be carried out for 8 months from March to December 2023 . Through this project we expect the following results;10,000 people in Mezam, Bui and Donga are aware of various forms of GBV, where to access care and prevention strategies GBV case management provided to 200 survivors 100 GBV survivors provided care at the safe shelter 6 mobile safe spaces provided to survivors in Mezam, Bui and Donga Mantung Division.100 survivors trained and supported on income generating activities 100 women receive vulnerability and dignity kits 500 vulnerable persons in distress provide PSS through one on one and group counseling sessions Birth certificates established for 100 vulnerable children 10% of the beneficiaries will be persons living with disability and 80% women
