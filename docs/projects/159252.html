Project Summary

The multicomponent project, designed for protection and advocacy of rights and freedoms of the population affected by the conflict in Eastern Ukraine and consists of 4 components:
Component 1. Operating Hotline for the conflict-affected population on wide range issues (
Component 2. Legal support for the conflict-affected population
Component 3. Expert support to the governmental bodies and Advocacy of rights and freedoms of the conflict-affected population
Component 4. Trainings on protection and advocacy of non-discrimination
