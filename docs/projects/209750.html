Project Summary

Based on HNO 2023, approximately 4.7 million people are in NWS, including 1.8 million IDPs, with an estimated 4.1 million in need of humanitarian assistance.
The health system functionality is hampered by deteriorating socio-economic conditions, poor infrastructure, lack of electricity, and inadequate WASH conditions in healthcare facilities. Only 57% of PHCs are fully functioning, limiting access to healthcare.
Food insecurity, water crises, and unhealthy dietary habits contribute to increased waterborne diseases and worsening malnutrition among children and PLWs. MSNA data indicates that 40% of interviewed PLWs consume sub-optimal diets, elevating the risk of adverse pregnancy outcomes.
As per BINAA conducted Need Assessment, FGDs, and end-line assessment for the ongoing project:
- The majority of assessed camps face challenges in accessing healthcare services.
- On average, each household earns less than 45 USD per month, insufficient to cover basic food needs. A high percentage can only afford one meal a day, with limited access to fruits or meat.
- The results underscore the importance of sustaining the provision of nutrition and health services.
The proposed solution to response to this problem is to improve the availability, access, and utilization of essential quality health and nutrition-integrated services to children, adolescents, PLWs, and the population including PWDs through a comprehensive approach to health and nutrition.
These interventions are strongly aligned with the recommendations, priorities, and guidance from the health cluster.
This project aims to support a comprehensive package of health and outreach services including Primary Health Care and community health awareness services in seven of the existing health facilities in NWS and NCS and through outreach activities in the surrounding areas:
1- G0703144 / IDA Hezreh PHC/Idleb, Harim, Dana, Hezreh - Hezri
2- B0204126 / Ehteimlat PHC/Aleppo, A'zaz, Suran, Ehteimlat
3- B0204068 / AlResala PHC/Aleppo, A'zaz, A'zaz, Shamarin
4- G0703048 / Aljolan camp PHCC/Idleb,Harim, Dana, Qah
5- G0703206 / Serjebla PHC/Idleb, Harim, Dana, Bab El Hawa
6-k1102023 /tell Abiad health center/Tell Abiaed/ Ar-Raqqa
7-k1102016/Muheisen PHC/Al-Hasakeh
Those centers were selected based on coordination with the Health cluster.
The motivation of this project is to continue providing services in the already established and equipped centers and through trained teams to the population in rural Idleb focusing on the most vulnerable people (women, boys, girls, and PWDs) with outreach and referral activities to the higher level of health services. The project will comprise two convergent components: primary health care and outreach services.
PHC centers provide a package of health services as per the essential primary health care package; these services include pediatric services, Reproductive Health services, nutrition activities, management of communicable and non-communicable diseases, and essential medicines and medical supplies.
Through the proposed project, BINAA will continue supporting the outreach component, which will comprise services related to raising awareness and referral activities according to WHO Guidelines.
