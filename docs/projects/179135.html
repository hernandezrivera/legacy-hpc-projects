Project Summary

WWGVC plans to intervene in Aleppo and Deir-Ez-Zor governorates restoring community services in newly accessible and underserved areas. A set of interventions identified is to provide sufficient access to safe water to 65,600 people in need, allowing communities to cope with the expected increasing number of returnees. Water systems are out of service or partially functioning and unable to guarantee sufficient standards. The light rehabilitation of 5 pumping stations and sections of damaged water networks will ensure access to water to host communities and possible newcomers. Whereas the light rehabilitation of 5 learning centers/schools and 27 WASH facilities will facilitate safe and inclusive access to schools. Hard component activities will be integrated with awareness campaigns on hygiene promotion (HP) and Covid-19 preparedness and prevention for the most vulnerable communities. The action also includes capacity building to technical staff of the Water Board (WB).

4 - Needs

Despite efforts to restore or maintain water systems’ functionality and countrywide support to water treatment processes, equitable access to sufficient quantity of safe water remains challenging. According to Syria HNO 2021, Up to 36% of the surveyed general population is relying on alternative water supply modalities to meet or complement their water needs. In addition, a detailed needs assessment was conducted between December 2020 and January 2021 by WWGVC. The assessment result was then triangulated with information from the WASH severity scales, WB feedback on areas most in need, HTR and Newly Accessible Areas. In Aleppo, access to an insufficient amount of water was mentioned by 80% of respondents, showing that quantity of water is critical. This has direct consequences on hygiene conditions, as 95% reported not able to adopt adequate hygiene practices while 70% mentioning that hygiene products are not available at the local market. All key informants reported that they are facing challenges in accessing 15 to 24 l/p/d.While in Deir Ez Zour, The quality of water pumped into the distribution system is affected by insufficient treatment points. The quantity of water is also reported not to be sufficient to fulfill households’ needs. Water from the network is received on an irregular basis with frequent shortages and cuts of the service. To cope with the lack of service, residents rely on water trucking or they collect water directly from the Euphrates river thus leading to poor quality water and possible contamination. This situation particularly exacerbated in underserved and newly accessible areas. Since February 2020, a total of 4.095 households have returned to the communities according to municipalities.

5 - Implementation of activities

A1 Light rehabilitation of 27 Schools WASH Facilities in Aleppo and Deir Ez Zour including improving gender disaggregation and access for people with disabilities. The repair supply of tanks, water supply and plumbing works, repair or replacement of sanitary fixtures, civil and architectural works.A.2 Rehabilitate 5 water pumping stations to ensure access to adequate quantity of quality water to the population from controlled sources,. The works include cleaning and removal of debris and damages, civil and finishing works, as well as electro-mechanic depending on the need of each pumping station in coordination with the water board. .A3 Light rehabilitation and repair of existing water networks to restore system distribution and efficiency. A4 Increase awareness and good hygiene practices and prevention on COVID-19 of the population through community hygiene campaigns and the provision of family hygiene kits A5 Capacitate WB’s technical staff in cleaning, assessing and testing wells located in newly accessible areas; A6 Monitoring, evaluation, and learning. The MEAL system will ensure: Routine M&E activities based on a systematic reporting activity and implementation of the M&E plan, Development of data-collection tools, establishing information management mechanisms leading to an efficient use of data and informative reports aiming at strengthening internal decision-making process.All activities will be implemented in GoS controlled areas. For the sub-districts which are not fully under GoS control, WWGVC will only access and intervene in the sections of the territories that are under GoS control.
