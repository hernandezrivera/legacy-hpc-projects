Project Summary

The Logistics Sector will continue supporting cross-border activities from Türkiye into the northwest of the country by facilitating access to free-to-user transshipment services to the humanitarian community at Bab al-Hawa border crossing, pending extension of the current UN Security Council Resolution 2462. The Logistics Sector will continue to facilitate inter-agency convoys from Aleppo availing partners to free-to-user transport and handling services for dispatches into NWS. Explore humanitarian needs for augmented storage capacity, in Qamishli and other locations if required, where access is more challenging while working closely with partners to identify alternative solutions to meet long-term logistics needs in the region. Priority will be given to make use of local available logistics capacity and advocate for the pooling of resources among partners when and where possible. The Logistics Sector will preposition extra storage capacity in key locations across the country as a preparedness measure and make them available for loan when and where needed. Coordination and information management activities will continue to support sharing of up to date relevant information, best practices, efficient supply chain planning, addressing logistics bottlenecks, common advocacy etc.
UNHAS will continue to provide passenger and light cargo air services for the humanitarian community to hard-to-reach destinations inside Syria.

3 - Needs

Based on ground realities and analysis provided by the stakeholders, coupled by the Gaps and Needs exercise (GNE) as well as feedback from regular consultations with partners, key needs identified for 2023 include the provision of access to common cross-border transshipment services subject to the UNSCR extension in January 2023, and facilitation of cross line inter-agency humanitarian convoys. In 2023, the sector response aims to phase out free-to-user common storage in the whole of Syria, including services in NES. Based on common identified needs and evolving operational context, the Logistics sector will explore possibility of emergency storage support for humanitarian partners. The analysis will be reinforced by a robust logistics coordination and information management platform with regular coordination meetings at various hub and field levels which is needed to ensure a predictable and secure supply chain for responding organisations. The GNE also found a need to strengthen partnership with government counterparts, such as SARC and development-focused stakeholders, establish locally led technical working groups to address identified technical supply-chain related challenges, and encourage partners to use local available capacities and to share assets and pool resources.The sector will maintain a flexible approach that allows it to adapt its services, taking into account the situational needs, and augment or reduce capacities as required .

4 - Implementation of activities

WFP-led Logistics Sector aims will phase-out storage services by end of 2022; however it will preposition extra storage capacity in key locations as a preparedness measure and support humanitarian partners when and where needed. It will continue to facilitate cross-border transshipment operations by maintaining coordination functions among relevant actors and managing the transshipment hub, should the UNSCR be renewed, keeping the necessary security and COVID-19 precautions. Facilitation of Crossline humanitarian inter-agency convoys.Meetings in Damascus, Gaziantep, Aleppo, and other locations as required, will continue to be organized to better respond to the localized needs and provide partners with relevant logistics information on regular basis. Information management functions and platforms will continue. Capacity strengthening of local actors will also be key to localize the overall sectoral response over 2023. This will enable a suitable transfer of coordination and information management mechanisms to local entities. UNHAS will continue passenger and light cargo air services for the humanitarian community using medium size jet aircraft CRJ200 in compliance with UNAVSTADS. UNHAS Steering Committee will provide overall governance on the smooth running of UNHAS and the User Group will promote interest of users in the movement of passengers and cargo in order to fulfil the humanitarian needs.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Despite timely deconfliction efforts and a robust coordination mechanism in place, for cross-line convoys, partners are notified on security alerts in case security incidents happen. The LC coordinates with the Security in WFP and UNDSS to mitigate possible threats to minimize risks exposure.Delays in local authorities approvals causes delays in movement of inter-agency cross border convoys from Türkiye into NWS. The LC works closely with OCHA to ensure that approvals are disseminated at widely resulting in smooth and uninterrupted passage.Storage locations can be exposed to natural hazards and fire. The LC mobilizes security personnel and fire fighting equipment to protect assets and structures.
