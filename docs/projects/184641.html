Project Summary

FSNAU, Sept., 2021, indicates that about 7.2 million people; 3.5 million people in Crisis (IPC Phase 3)1 or worse outcomes, of whom 640,730 people are in IPC4,facing large food consumption gaps and 3.7 million people expected to be stressed (IPC2),facing depletion of livelihood assets through the end of the year, in the absence of humanitarian assistance. Humanitarian assistance must be sustained through December 2021 to prevent Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Emergency (IPC Phase 4) outcomes for 3.5 million people. Livelihoods support is also required for people that are Stressed (IPC Phase 2) or worse. Among them, 1.2 million children under the age of five are facing acutely malnutrition, with 213,400 of them likely to face severe malnutrition despite humanitarian food assistance and government support since January 2021.This situation is driven by combined effects of poor and erratic rainfall distribution, resulting in 60% and 63% harvest below the 1995-2020 average, in Southern and Northwest, respectively; flooding, conflict and multiple declines in food and income sources in rural areas..
The poor rains led to below-average Gu poor crop production and harvest in agro pastoral livelihood zones Southern and Northwest Somalia, respectively. In pastoral livelihood zones, poor households are facing large food consumption gaps due to below-average or poor milk availability, a limited number of saleable animals, and increased expenditures on food and water. In agro pastoral livelihood zones, poor households experience large food consumption gaps due to crop losses and low income from agricultural employment. Of those people facing crisis, 2.9million are Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) with limited livelihood assets, few income-earning opportunities, low communal support and high reliance on external humanitarian assistance. As a result, a significant proportion of IDPs (in both rural and urban settlements) facing large food consumption gaps.
Forecasted increased likelihood of below-average rainfall during the 2021 Dyer (October-December) season, will adversely affect food security and nutrition outcomes. From October to December 2021, food insecurity is expected to further deteriorate among poor rural, urban, and displaced populations due to the impacts of anticipated, below-average 2021 Dyer (October-December) season rainfall, continued insecurity and other food security related risk factors, including rising food prices and cost of living, declining availability of milk for both consumption and sale, and a likely reduction in agricultural employment opportunities during the forthcoming Dyer season. Desert Locust continues to pose a serious risk to both pasture availability and crop production across Somalia.
The project targets 5,500 households in Togdhere Region, Somaliland in IPC 3&4( including IDPs) with; i)Unconditional cash transfer for immediate food access(1,000 hh); ii)cash for work for 1500hh (iii) Protection of productive livelihood assets for those in IPC 2 -provision of supplementary livestock feed -3000hh, 1500 for animal health services.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

VSF Germany bas been implementing CBT for over 6 years, through digital platform using money vendor company ,Telesom in Somaliland. Cash transfer will be done. Currently VSFGermany is using same system to support households in food crisis in Awdal region, Somaliland The use of cash transfer recognizes that that at any given time the households that are affected by crisis experience multiple needs. Beneficiaries are best placed to decide what their immediate household needs and the cash gives them the flexibility to choose according to their own diverse needs and priorities. Beneficiaries targeted for Food Access through the conditional and unconditional cash transfers will be vulnerable households- with members who are chronically ill or have disabled members, Elderly-headed households or child-headed households or female-headed households, IDPs, unsupported returnees and host communities and destitute pastoralist households. Supplementary livestock feed provision will primarily target pastoralists with not more than 25 flock size, cannot access adequate feed due to damaged pasture by Desert Locusts/drought and require nutritious feeds to produce milk for home consumption, in targeted regions/locations in Somaliland State. Priority will be given to female-headed households, child-headed households, family with at least a disabled member in the household, chronically ill members or heads of the household. These households should own between 10-25 Sheep and Goats (shoat) and depending on their animals in milk for food. Animal Health Services will target both households under stressed (IPC 2), crises (IPC 3) and emergency (IPC 4) situation in Togdhere Region. Priority will be given to female-headed households, child-headed households, family with at least a disabled persons in the household, families headed by disabled persons, chronically ill members or heads of the household. Unconditional Cash Transfer (UCT) and conditional cash transfer (through cash for work) will target households who completely lost their livelihoods and productive assets due to conflict particularly IDPs and women head households with high number of children, disable and elder households. The UCT aims to enable poor households have immediate food access and other basic needs. The amount of UCT will be as per FSC guideline for each targeted region. Cash for Work will target households that have lost their crop or livestock or lost their home but have strong members capable of doing manual work for income; households with no family support, no access to remittances, households with members who are chronically ill or have disabled members, elderly-headed households or child-headed households or female-headed households. Households with more than 8 members and only one member with income capacity, IDPs, returnees and host communities, destitute pastoralists communities and those willing to work as per the pre-determined work norms. The labour rate will be based on the Cash for work guidelines for each targeted region. Working hours will be made flexible for women who are ready to take part to enable them attend to other household chores, e.g. 2-3 hour of work per day and be paid prorata.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

The project design takes into account the market function as provided by FSNAU through the CWG on quarterly basis. This report gives the availability, prices and access of essential goods and services. The project will derive the value of amount to be transferred based on recommendations, of the market analysis report for each targeted region. These values serve as Somalia's standard minimum expenditure basket (MEB), to humanitarian organizations implementing cash-based interventions in Somalia. The project will conduct profiling of selected beneficiaries and establish their status at the onset of implementation. The project will work closely with the CWG transfer value recommendations for populations classified in the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) phases 3 ('acute') and 4 ('emergency'), in addition to an overview of market performance for the period of July-September 2021. The food Security and Nutrition Analysis Unit (FSNAU), also provides price data.The project will also do market assessment to ensure availability of basic foods and the market prices.
