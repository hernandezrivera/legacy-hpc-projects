Activities or outputs

"1) Site Maintenance Engineering Project (SMEP) 2.0:In response to requirements validated by the SMEP Technical Working Group, SMEP conducts the following range of activities:1. Planning, repair and recovery works of critical infrastructure2. Land optimization3. Planning and works for drainage channels4. Improvement of accessibility through identification of roads and potentially bridges for construction5. Capacity Development Program NOTE: SMEP provides engineering services in response to requirements identified and validated through the SMEP Technical Working Group, which is comprised of WFP, IOM, and UNHCR. The operational area of responsibility of the WFP component of SMEP encompasses the entire refugee population. However, which specific subgroups of that population will end up being targeted, and their location/demographic attributes, is only known once a requirement is identified, validated, and selected for inclusion in the SMEP workplan. 2) Disaster Resilience and Public Works:1. Renovation of bridges, footbridges, access roads, footpaths steps & stairs, handrails, flags and signs etc. 2. Risks mitigation through slope protection, terracing, perimeter fencing, constructing dams, clearing drainage canals etc.. In both projects WFP will have a targeted and gender-transformative approach in its cash-for-work activities, enhancing the capacities of the most vulnerable groups within engineering and DRR activities. In order to increase inclusion of women within these activities, mobile childcare facilities, flexible working hours and creation of an all-female buddy-system are the approaches being used by WFP to ensure a gender and protection-sensitive project. WFP’s Emergency Telecommunication mobile application (ETC App) alongside its help-desk and hotline will be used in capturing beneficiary feedback, complaints and concerns as part of monitoring and evaluation processes to ensure “Do No Harm” and Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) principles are adhered to.
