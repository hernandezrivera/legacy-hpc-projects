3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project is designed to contribute significantly to gender equality. Gender mainstreaming and targeted actions are fully reflected in the project at all levels and in all stages of planning, decision-making, and implementing activities in all of Al-Manar’s interventions through: • Collecting and using gender-disaggregated data in all interventions • Ensuring the involvement of all groups of the community at all stages of project planning, implementation, and monitoring • Promoting female staff progression and development • Ensuring that the community-based activities consider the cultural difference in the roles of men and women and use different approaches to reach both genders. • Creating gender awareness with both women and men in the communities where Almanar is working • Consulting women and men about the location and/or appropriateness of services in the community • Ensuring all health and nutrition care activities are accessible to all groups of the community through identification and tackling of barriers to access to the different groups of the community.The objectives and contents of project activities (needs, objectives, target groups, activities) address the different needs of various groups (boys, girls, women, men).The project activities throughout execution, will aim to ensure community participation through the project cycle, capacity, and vulnerability analysis, reduce the level of exploitation of women, and promote the protection of girls in communities and families with the support of legal and traditional protection systems. The project will use and advocate for the Minimum Standards of Protection package amongst the targeted communities throughout the project implementation. This project through the referral pathway will ensure that women, girls, and boys’ victims of S/GBV, the elderly, the disabled, and their families and guardians are fully aware of the available nutrition and health services around them. Almanar will ensure the services locations are safe and accessible to all without discrimination and ensure that the beneficiaries receive services with respect to their dignity and confidentiality.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with individuals (in homes) through aid workers ,Face to face with group consultations with aid workers,Complaints / suggestions box

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

All project activities will continues in consider accountability, meaningful access, prioritizing safety and dignity and avoiding causing harm and community participation and empowerment principles, Almanar engaged men, women, girls and boys desegregated by gender and age groups (SAAD) into need identification, project design through meetings and group discussions, and will engage them in implementation and monitoring. Accountability will be ensured across the activities in this project through establishment of complaints and response mechanisms(complain boxes) at project level aimed at ensuring that the voices of the affected populations will be heard. The established nutrition and health community committees and community leaders networks including volunteers will facilitate community engagement at all levels of the project including planning, management, monitoring and evaluation. These committees are expected to be gender and ability sensitive with the inclusion of both males, females, elderly and people with disabilities to ensure that all voices, including those of the marginalized are heard, equality and equity are realized with regards to access to services that will be provided by the project. In addition, AMVO will ensure that all staff/volunteers are trained on PSEA policies and procedures which are in line with UN's protocols

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

The Complaints handling mechanisms will be established in the targeted area and fully inform the community about the complaint mechanisms, their purposes and use, and what constitutes a complaint to allow people confidentially report their concerns and with developing monthly tracker to ensure that communities complaints/feedback are comprehensively analyzed and responded to with the beneficiaries getting feedback on a monthly basis. Project staff will ensure adequate consultation with the communities to ensure that existing complaints handling mechanisms are incorporated into the complaints and response mechanisms to be established by the project. I.e.(complain box, a complaint committee or direct complaints to project staff members trained to act as “focal points,” receiving complaints sensitively and without judgment, and ensuring the complainant’s safety.
