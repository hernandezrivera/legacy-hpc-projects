Project Summary

Climatic extremes, which have in the recent past been characterized by unprecedented flooding and prolonged dry spells, coupled with increasing waves of inter and intra communal violence, bureaucratic impediments including escalating Sudan conflict with increasing influx of refugees, returnees and asylum seekers crossing into South Sudan and deteriorating local currency and high food prices have continued to negatively impact vulnerable boys, girls, men and women, including people with disabilities in Unity, Jonglei, Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Central Equatoria among other States/ administrative areas in the country.
These have had consequent implications on multiple displacements of people, prolonged congregation of livestock in marsh lands with consequent impacts on increased livestock disease outbreaks and mortalities in addition to submerging of agricultural land contributing to unprecedented food insecurity levels, with the lean season projection period of April to July 2024, where food security situation will deteriorate and an estimated 7.1 million people (56.3 percent of the population) will be severely food insecure (IPC Phase 3 or above). 79,000 people are likely to be in IPC Phase 5 (Catastrophe) while an estimated 2.34 million people are likely to be in Phase 4 (Emergency) (IPC November 2023 Report).
Many vulnerable host, returnee, refugee and displaced families, especially women, widowed women, child headed households, the elderly, boys and girls including people with disabilities; solely depend on livestock, fishing, vegetable and crop farming among other coping strategies. VSF Suisse 28 years of experience in the targeted states, presence on the ground and proven capacity will deliver high quality programming by leveraging on strong grass root networks, local knowledge, understanding of seasonal and location specific contexts. Proposed actions will target 187,000 beneficiaries (30000 men, 30000 women, 63500girls, 63500 boys) who shall include elderly and people living with disabilities. The action will be aimed at ensuring cost efficiency through complementarities by linkages with FAO pipeline for livestock vaccines prepositioning and agricultural livelihood kits - crops, vegetables and fishing kits in order to promote preparedness, household resilience and self-reliance through protection of livelihood opportunities.
Proposed actions are in line and correspond with the FSL Cluster 2024 HRP Cluster Objectives Supporting CO2: Enhance and sustain emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop livelihood inputs, fishing and livestock and Cluster Objective 3: Reduce dependency on food and agricultural inputs to support and strengthen households’ ability to absorb and adapt to shocks and contextual changes.
Proposed actions will establish synergies with other sectors to maximize the response impact, to ensure complementarity and integration with other clusters’ interventions, starting with the “Famine prevention clusters” (Health, Nutrition and WASH) and the Protection cluster. This is in addition to promoting a nexus between humanitarian response, peace building (conflict sensitivity) and resilience programming. The project will ensure quality programming, cost efficiency in addition to mainstreaming cross-cutting programmatic approaches that include application of Accountability to Affected Populations principles ,protection including Gender Based Violence mitigation measures, Do No Harm, conflict context analysis, gender equity, HIV/AIDS awareness and prevention, environmental conservation and sustainability.

1. Needs

Climatic extremes, which have in the recent past been characterized by unprecedented flooding and prolonged dry spells, coupled with increasing waves of inter/ intra communal violence, bureaucratic impediments including escalating Sudan conflict with increasing influx of displaced people crossing into South Sudan and deteriorating local currency and high food prices have continued to negatively impact vulnerable boys, girls, men and women, including people with disabilities (IPC November 2023). These have had consequent implications on multiple displacements of people, limited grazing land and pastures with prolonged congregation of livestock in marsh lands with consequent impacts on increased livestock disease outbreaks/ mortalities. Households have been resorting to distressed sale of livestock for fear of disease outbreaks/ decimation. 2023 VSF livestock flood assessments have indicated increasing livestock disease outbreaks more so in flood prone areas like Rubkona, Panyijiar among others with livestock disease incidences like Contagious Bovine Pleuropneumonia, Foot and Mouth Disease, Trypanosomiasis, haemorrhagic septicaemia among others (VSF Reports 2023; REACH Updates 2023).Continued influx of displaced people due to the ongoing conflict in Sudan will likely exacerbate already dire humanitarian situation, coupled with disrupted cross border trade with Sudan; has had significant impacts on livelihoods/ incomes in addition to increasing food/ commodity costs (WFP/VAM Market Bulletins 2023). This is in addition to submerging of agricultural land contributing to unprecedented food insecurity levels, with the lean season projection period of 2024, with an estimated 7.1 million people will be severely food insecure (IPC Phase 3 or above). 79,000 people are likely to be in IPC Phase 5 while an estimated 2.34 million people are likely to be in Phase 4 (IPC November 2023). 2. Project activities

• Prepositioning, transport and distribution of crops, fishing kits and vegetable kits among 187,000 beneficiaries (11,000 HHs) – comprised of 30000 men, 30000 women, 63500girls, 63500 boys) who shall include elderly and people living with disabilities.• Set up at least 60 vegetable demonstration plots/ kitchen garden demonstration sites at nutrition facilities and conduct nutrition sensitive feeding and cooking practices and agronomic trainings. • Carry out cash transfers supporting 500 displaced populations • Conduct agronomic trainings and postharvest preservation techniques training for 11,000 household heads • Training/ Refresher training for 400 Community Based Animal Health Workers/ Stock Persons (100 female, 300 male) • Conduct refresher training of 40 Stock Persons and Animal Health Auxilliaries on cold chain management and monitoring to support safe storage of veterinary vaccines/ integrity.• Install and maintain 20 solar vaccine fridges for livestock veterinary vaccine handling in Central Equatoria, Unity, Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Jonglei. • Carry out livestock disease investigation outbreaks in collaboration with line ministries, FAO among other key stakeholders. • Carry out livestock treatment and vaccination campaigns targeting1,000,000 heads of livestock against endemic livestock diseases in Unity, Northern Bahr el Ghazal, Central Equatoria and Jonglei. • Support 5000 households through input trade fairs using locally sourced seed inputs in Northern Bahr el Ghazal.
