Project Summary

As the refugee influx crisis enters into its 4th year, the focus of this project and Oxfam’s WASH strategy for 2021 onwards is to consolidate and sustain the gains and learnings from the last three years, and subse-quently provide technical and strategic inputs to address sustainable & resilience WASH service provisions. This implies enhancement of the most appropriate and feasible operation & maintenance (O&M) models adjusted to cope with complexities in the context due to unprecedented occurrences such as the COVID-19 pandemic that started in 2020.
For the refugee camps, Oxfam aims to focus on 3 main areas; Enhancing performance & efficiency of un-derperforming water-networks through technical and social inputs, capacity building and facilitating tran-sition to sustainable O&M. This will be done through feasibility studies on pilot selected water networks, with appropriate modification and adaptation; and established O&M approaches embedded in strong user-centered community engagement (UCCE) approach for meaningful participation of targeted end users.
Improving overall performance of environmental sanitation through capacity building in O&M of FSTP, desludging and sludge transfer, design and modification inputs and performance monitoring FSM systems & effluents surveillance in camps. DPHE and Oxfam are also reviewing DPHE/WB FSM with a view of collab-orating on improved design and technical inputs, capacity building and where feasible to support with sludge transfer network design.
A collaborative pilot study (by Oxfam and DPHE) of about 22 DPHE/ADB water networks currently in camps being operating by contractor under BOT basis to identify and apply best operating model in such situa-tions; and transition to transfer of O&M and sustainability.
For the host community, Oxfam will support NGO forum to scale up community led inclusive sanitation and improved water safety & surveillance plans.
Oxfam will also develop a comprehensive capacity building plan with the CXB WASH Sector along with the results of ongoing global clusters gaps analysis, for both sector partners and affected community in camps and host community. Focus will be on rapid response FSM design; construction and O&M; and appropriate community engagement approaches.

2 - Activities or outputs

Overall Output 1: Enhancing performance & efficiency of underperforming water-networks through technical and social inputs, capacity building and facilitating transition to sustainable O&M. (7 activities listed below)1. Scoping study of 22 22 DPHE/ADB water networks with review and modification (via a consultancy). 2. Water network design and O&M 3-days workshop to share findings and learnings with other agencies. 3. Assessing and proposing remedial action both engineering and social aspects to establish the appropriate funding and implementation mechanism jointly with DPHE. 4. Establishing meaningful participation of end-users, forming/revising water users’ group, training and capacity building on O&M.5. Piloting a sustainable transitioning approach – in this case a contractor/private sector managing the WNP as BOT (build operate and transfer) basis- to a social entrepreneur/utility and or joint community/utility managed transfer and sustainable O&M 6. Water safety plans in the host community in Teknaf that includes sanitary protection to community & household owned shallow and surface water sources, community based sanitary survey and surveillance. 7. Capacity building of Oxfam partners and other agencies on O&M using appropriate community engagement approaches. Overall Output 2 (a): Improving overall performance of environmental sanitation through capacity building in O&M of FSTP, desludging and sludge transfer, design and modification inputs and performance monitoring FSM systems & effluents surveillance in camps. (6 activities below)1. Survey & Design support to sludge transfer pipe networks.2. Modification and upgrading support to existing FSMs in the camps. 3. Capacity development through on-job coaching to teams managing the FSM for camps. 4. Training SUSANA/EWAG on managing the FSM by DPHE in the host community. 5. Development of technical guidelines for FSM design and O&M in CXB camp context.6. Capacity building and knowledge sharing with other sector partners on FSM. Overall Output 2 (b): Improved Sanitation scale-up using appropriate community engagement approaches in host community. (3 activities below)1. Social architecture approach to support meaningful participation of the women, elderly and disabled persons in the design of their sanitation facilities. 2. Sani-tweaks approach to ensure the inputs from various user groups are used to adapt existing sanitation facilities, including the scaleup of these modifications for increased access and use of the facilities in the host community. 3. Capacity building of various end users’ group in host community on O&M of their sanitation facilities. Overall Output 3: Community stakeholders are enabled to prevent the spread of diseases (such as COVID-19, AWD etc) in both camps and host community. (6 activities below)1. Coordinated and integrated COVID-19 response within the WASH programming (done in collaboration with the health sector). 2. Enhanced risk communication community engagement (RCCE) and infection prevention control (IPC) of COVID-19 activities.3. Supply and installation of Oxfam handwashing stations to encourage handwashing with soap practice. 4. Disease outbreak & disaster preparedness and response plans embedded in the WASH programming. 5. COVID-19 mobilization kits for Community-Based Volunteers working in host & targeted camps.6. Upgrading of existing latrines (substructures: replace 30-inch with 48-inch diameter RCC rings, superstructures: metallic frames and steel sheets, and inclusion of features to increase access to all including PWD and women). Consultation with end users to determine need for new improved designs for bathing facilities.
