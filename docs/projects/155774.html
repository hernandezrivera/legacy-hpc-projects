Project Summary

SHED will collaborate with key stakeholders including District Education department, Schools Head teachers, Taleemi Islahi Jirga/Parents and Teachers Counsel (TIJ/PTC), District Administration and Village Organizations in order to get their support in the implementation of the project. Need assessments of the boys and girls primary schools in the targeted Tribal District Kurram will be conducted for installation of Pre-Fabricated structure and provision of teaching and learning material. Training Need Assessment (TNA) of TIJs will be conducted to design appropriate training manual and modules for the TIJs training. Qualified Para teachers will be identified from the local areas to facilitate the teaching with regular school teachers in girls primary schools. The project activities will improve access to quality education with capacity building of TIJs, provision of teaching and learning material to 26 schools and installation of pre fabricated structure in 04 fully damaged boys and girls primary schools in the targeted Tribal District Kurram.

Needs

A total of 455,290 families have returned to their homes in various areas of FATA /Tribal districts till 31 October 2018 according to UNOCHA report (UNOCHA – IDPs Factsheet as of 31-10-2018) out of which there are 46,201-Kurram, 36,759-Mohmand, 31,824-Orakzai and 96,883 families to Khyber tribal districts. The returnees in both the aforementioned tribal districts are facing numerous issues in rehabilitation. The major issue is the resumption of education of their children (boys and girls) where the community infrastructure especially besides from their houses, the schools are either fully or partially damaged. SHED designed the project activities on the following need assessment:-1) Preliminary findings – FATA Vulnerability Assessment 2017The Rehabilitation and Reconstruction Unit, FATA Secretariat, reports that currently 868 educational institutions are damaged (209 completely and 659 partially) in the five IDP returning agencies. Some 500,000 children in the 4-9 age group are reportedly out of school in FATA. The UNHCR IDP factsheet (September 2017), states that an additional 43,549 families including 107,130 children (48% girls, 52% boys) still need to return to these agencies which will put further pressure on existing facilities and thus require additional immediate resources to provide opportunities for all school aged children in their areas of origin. Proposed Actions:• Some 868 schools need immediate reconstruction/rehabilitation in the five agencies to cater to the educational needs of 104,160 school going children. In the interim, alternate facilities (i.e. Temporary Learning Centers) are required to support resumption of education.• Social mobilization and massive enrolment campaigns need be initiated to encourage and support girls’ education and reduce their dropout rates.• Equip schools with basic teaching-learning materials, provide WASH facilities, and financial incentives to support girls’ and female teachers’ transportation.• Train teachers on pedagogical and psycho-social issues.• Form TIJs or School Management Committees to ensure community participation to increase enrolment, address issue of teachers’ absenteeism, and improve school environments

Activities

1. SHED will provide and install solarized pre fabricated structure in altogether 04 fully damaged government primary schools in the selected Tribal District Kurram. 2. SHED will provide teaching and learning material, learning kits (school bags with stationery) recreational materials, white boards, setting material, teacher ‘s table and chairs in 26 (04 fully & 22partially damaged schools (13 girls and 13 boys) and will cover 4,640 students (2,650 boys and 1,990 girls). 3. Hiring and 05 days training of 05 Para teachers (02 female and 03 male) 05 day long non residential training workshop will be organized for Para teachers (female). The training will focus on equipping them with knowledge and skills of the teachings on child friendly teaching methodology and English medium of instruction. 4. The project will revitalize and provide capacity building to 26 TIJs/PTCs (104 female and 104 male) which will cover total 208 TIJs/PTCs members (104 female and 104 male) TIJs/PTCs members. 26 training workshop will be conducted for the TIJs/PTCs. Following this, it will arrange a 02 day long non residential capacity building workshop focusing on the various thematic areas.5. SHED will provide monitoring support and building capacity of DoE/AEO office
