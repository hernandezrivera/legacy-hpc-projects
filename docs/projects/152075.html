Project Summary

The overall objective of the project will be to support the early recovery and restoration of livelihoods security for vulnerable population in the Yobe state. The project will build on the humanitarian assistance already provided in the targeted areas by ACF and other actors as well as the Government to gradually contribute to the efforts made and lead towards recovery, resilience and sustainability. Focus will be on ensuring a transition between emergency and development in a sustained manner. Since this transition is rarely a linear chronological process, the project will follow a flexible and phased approach where immediate needs will be supported at the same time as recovery and longer term interventions are implemented. The need for applying such an approach is accentuated by the fluidity in the security situation in the locations selected. The flexibility and adaptability of the project will be ensured by a multi-scenario planning considering possible evolutions of the security, return or displacement situations. The intervention aims at supporting livelihood stabilization and income recovery in Borno State (SO03) and promote inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDP, returnee and host communities.

Needs

The proposed action responds to the need of building resilience and sustainability in Borno and enhancing the capacity of public institutions and affected communities to anticipate and react to the challenges of poverty, social cohesion, climate change, and crisis/security. The main objective of this comprehensive project is therefore to enhance the resilience of conflict affected populations and state actors and initiate early recovery and reconstruction with the following Overall Objective: Contribution to long-term resilience among populations and public institutions in North East Nigeria.

Activities

Responding to the needs of the conflict affected populations in Borno requires a comprehensive and integrated intervention across multiple platforms of service delivery and system strengthening. The project will therefore cover multiple sectors at once in the targeted area including social protection, livelihoods and income generating activities. The Specific Objective of the project will be as follows: Strategic Objective 2 - Support livelihood stabilization- Improve access to infrastructure and basic social services through rehabilitation and reconstruction of public service facilities- Promote inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDPs in HC, returnees, and host communities - Support restoration of local governance administration and services The project will build on the humanitarian assistance provided to gradually contribute to efforts by the Nigerian authorities and other major actors to initiate sustainable reconstruction and recovery work. Focus will be on ensuring a transition between emergency and development in a sustained manner. Since this transition is rarely a linear chronological process, the project will follow a flexible and phased approach where immediate needs will be supported at the same time as recovery and longer term interventions are implemented. The need for applying such an approach is accentuated by the fluidity in the security situation in the locations selected. The flexibility and adaptability of the project will be ensured by a multi-scenario planning considering possible evolutions of the security, return or displacement situations.In order to build resilience, the proposed action will seek to ensure sustainability is built in all of its components. The project will be linked to existing systems and protocols at both state and local level, and work alongside authorities to ensure they are strengthened to be able to deliver the basic services to their population. While the ultimate expected level of sustainability will be dependent on factors such as resources and security, the project plans to work closely with national and community structures to ensure that they have the technical capacity to deliver an effective response without external support. This will not resolve the shortfall of resources or security; however, the intervention will ensure key stakeholders do consolidate their service capacity delivery alongside the intervention. Beyond authorities, the project will seek to empower conflict affected populations, in particular women, to make them more resilient. Action Against Hunger participate in various coordination groups for humanitarian action and early recovery one to streamline the efforts and inform other partners of our work. The Early recovery is entirely led by RRR Commissioner while the rest of the activities has SEMA/NEMA and other partners. Complaints response mechanism is established with a view to improve the programming, assisting in understanding the beneficiary and community perceptions, promoting beneficiaries empowerment and assisting in the early detection of problems such a targeting criteria, misconduct including sexual exploitation and abuse, food diversion and fraud. AAH tries to provide meaningful access to vulnerable household, and we do not discriminate against any groups.
