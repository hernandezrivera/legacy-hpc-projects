Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to an overall improvement of the nutrition and health status of IDPs and host communities affected by conflict in Cabo Delgado, by strengthening nutritional and health services at the District level (Mobile Brigades, Inpatient facilities (IPF), Community Health Workers (CHWs), etc.), through the support of teams working in hard to reach areas.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The nutrition project is integrated with a multisector approach, that aims at reducing morbidity and mortality for conflict-affected populations in Cabo Delgado. The project focuses on a sustainable, community-based approach, in order to restore basic access to immediate, quality care and assistance to targeted, vulnerable populations.The project is focused on the operational strengthening of existing Mobile Brigades, to enable them to reach more locations more frequently and to strengthen community-based management of acute malnutrition, common infections in children and reproductive health issues in mothers, via technical support. ACF will also implement additional integrated mobile clinics to provide basic health and nutrition services, psychosocial support and hygiene promotion to vulnerable populations in Mueda district.The reduction of malnutrition rates will be targeted through a hybrid approach of farming support and complementary nutrition trainings to women on healthy dietary practices for vegetable preparation, child-feeding practices and marketing to increase household income, thus providing additional access to other nutritious foods. The community based-approach (with the involvement of the activists and CHWs network) will complement the outreach efficiency, and allow the identification of vulnerable groups to benefit from the livelihoods and agriculture activities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

ACF will support the districts to form additional iMB to expand the coverage and increase the frequency of services. Through the community health promotion approach, regular health education on different primary health topics will be conducted, multipurpose activists (APE) will do community case management for common illnesses, and active case finding and follow up of patientsThe package of services will be designed to address access, quality and utilization of health services through health system support, support of provision of primary health care services (which will include both curative and preventive care for children under 5 years of age as well as for pregnant and lactating women), management of public health emergencies, community health and support the provision of medicines and medical equipment, stationary in supported HFs.Main activities:- Provision of human resources support for the mobile brigades- Provision of operational support to run the mobile brigades - Provision of technical support to Mobile Brigades staff - Provision of technical support to CHWs and volunteers - Provision of logistic and human resources support to a network of stabilization centers- capacity building of the hospital staff who manages SAM complicated cases- Provision of support for the referral systemGeographical focus: the bulk of the activities will be conducted in Mueda. Mobile brigades will also be supported in Macomia, Muidumbe, and Nagade. ACF will also have a rapid response component to potentially respond to urgent needs in other districts.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

ACF will mainstream gender and protection into the humanitarian response in several ways to reduce the vulnerability of IDPs and host communities, as well as mitigate the risks they face. For instance, the voices of women and girls will be enhanced throughout to promote their safety and access to humanitarian services. They would actively participate in decision-making on subjects such as community-based activities, health services.The project will promote linha verde service and foster its use among the beneficiaries. Promotion activities will include the scope of the service, costs and practical examples. The project will consult with affected populations (not only community leaders) to identify specific needs for vulnerable groups. Updates on the project will be shared with the affected populations through the available feedback mechanism including community meetings. The project will be monitored by the MEAL department, which will also support it in a risk analysis in view of the situation close to the beneficiaries, in order to guarantee respect for accountability, the PSEA policy, the staff's code of conduct and the policy on child protection.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

ACF will work with the community engagement and accountability to affected population (CE/AAP) working group towards adopting standardized tools and jointly implementing strategies. Needs assessments will be conducted jointly with community leaders and key focal persons representing specific groups like women, adolescents and the elderly, and will apply methods like direct observation, key informant interviews and focus group discussions. Their views and recommendations will be included in the project design and implementation. At the local level, project and facility management committees will include community representatives. Healthcare workers will be recruited locally and from among the target population, when they meet required qualifications. More women will be encouraged to take up roles. Complaints will be monitored at service delivery points on a regular basis using complaints boxes and patient exit interviews and through a common feedback mechanism. Messages will be communicated in the local languages through the community leaders, FM radio stations and information, education and communication materials. Data of beneficiaries for health services will be disaggregated by gender and age, and all best practices will be documented and shared regularly with communities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The implementation of the inclusive approach will be guaranteed in a transversal way to the project. Inclusion, on the implementation of all project activities will include a follow-up on the "triptych of inclusion", thus taking into account the aspects of gender, age and disabilityFor the inclusion and advocacy activities, coordination within the Disability working group will be ensured with the Pemba protection sub-cluster, OCHA Pemba and Maputo disability working groups. ACF will implement the WGs recommendations, and guarantee equal access to services for all.Key messages disseminated during training sessions with health/nutrition staff and filed visits will emphasize the importance ofn achieving equivalent health outcomes for both girls and boys, and PWDs. As part of their onboarding process, ACF staff will be trained and sensitized on the importance of narrowing the gender gap, the inclusion of PWDs and shifting attitudes toward gender-based violence, exploitation and harmful societal and cultural norms.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Specific sessions will be implemented during the sensitizations activities: importance of waste management and its impact on the environment and health, importance of water source and water conservation, importance of care and feeding practice in a sustainable way.Rehabilitation activities will promote the use of local materials and sustainable structures.Medical waste management will be carefully discussed and monitored with medical staff in the HFs and IPFs, in order to ensure health and environmental standards. Specific training will be provided.
