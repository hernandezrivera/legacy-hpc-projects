Project Summary

Child Protection concerns remain for the population in SWS due to the increased conflicts and natural disasters, mentionable of the ongoing military operations for the SNA and AMISOM against AS in parts of SWS, recent floods in Baidoa, the airstrikes and clan conflicts, contributing in the worsening of the protection situation of the population, exposing over 9000 HHs to protection and GBV risks.
According to Protection HNO report 2020, The lack of protection from violence, abuse and neglect remains a key consequence of the current humanitarian crisis in Somalia for up to 3.2 million people. Out of those, 1.9 million are living in displacement and 1.2 million people belong to host communities with 35% of them being children. Whether directly exposed to armed conflicts or violence, or the destitution resulting from climatic shocks, affected children face serious risks to their physical safety and integrity, and access to child rights.
READO needs assessment August 2020 revealed that the number of children in need of protection services has increased since 2018 by 23%. SWS has the most IDPs, due to recurrence of conflicts, droughts and increased military operations led by the SFG against AS. From January to June 2019, the Country Task Force on Monitoring and Reporting (CTFMR) verified and documented 1,473 cases (1,272 boys and 201 girls) of violence against children, with abduction and recruitment/use of children accounting for 41% and 55 % of the overall number of children affected.
A multi-sector joint assessment on IDP Influx in South West State September 2020 indicate that Major child protection issues noted in the community include FGM (70 per cent), restriction of movement (69 per cent), recruitment of children by armed forces (60 per cent) and gender and sexual violence (51 per cent). Of the 199 respondents, 67 per cent of the respondents reported witnessing or hearing of children being recruited or used as soldiers/fighters, all of the respondents identified Al Shabaab as the perpetrator that is recruiting child soldiers. High levels of child protection concerns have been recorded. The child protection needs in Baidoa includes PSS to crisis affected children, Case management of reported violations against children and awareness and campaigns on child rights at community and institutions to support children at school and community level. The target beneficiaries are boys, girls including adolescents, children with disabilities, facing life-threatening risks of abuse, neglect, violence, exploitation, and severe distress from IDPs and host communities.
The Project proposes protection services that comprises of Child protection case management services through reporting of violations affecting the newly displaced people affected by the ongoing military operations, airstrikes and floods in SWS, and supporting these IDPs with Child friendly spaces, psychosocial support (CP), referral responses for GBV cases affecting individuals to the services and facilities in the areas.
The project will also enhance the activities with community mobilization targeting the various community members, respecting gender equality inclusion, disabled children, adolescent children and other special needs as orphans.
The project will provide Family tracing & reunification (CP) to the separated children during the displacement and providing Child friendly spaces service awaiting to reunify with the caregivers and parents. The Project targets 2333HHs or 14000 individuals (4200 girls, 2940 boys, 4480 women and 2380 men in Bay- (Baidoa, Bardaale, Dinsoor) and Bakool-(Waajid, Xudur). The project in collaboration with Child Protection sub-cluster will be delivering CP Referral and Response services, Community based CP Awareness activities in the IDP camps and furthering child safeguarding activities by other programs and agencies in the locations targeted.

Project Activities

ACTIVITIES:Objective: To ensure equal respect for the rights of individuals without discrimination and as provided for in national and international laws. Activity 1: Assessment, monitoring, awareness raising and outreach (Child Protection) and reporting of displacement and general protection violations from the IDP camps in Bay- (Baidoa, Bardaale, Dinsoor) and Bakool-(Waajid, Xudur) over the project period.Activity 2: Access to Basic General Protection Services including Case management, family tracing and reunification for minors lost during conflict including reintegration for ex-child soldiers and referral services to 14000 beneficiaries (8,400 females) vulnerable and IDPs in Bay- (Baidoa, Bardaale, Dinsoor) and Bakool-(Waajid, Xudur) over the project period. Activity 3: Protection oriented assistance (on off Cash based assistance, solar lamps and dignity kits) for 2,100 HHs with specific needs or heightened vulnerability targeting Bay- (Baidoa, Bardaale, Dinsoor) and Bakool-(Waajid, Xudur). Activity 4: Establish Child friendly spaces and psychosocial support in Bay- (Baidoa, Bardaale, Dinsoor) and Bakool-(Waajid, Xudur) to reach some 2100 boys and girls at community and school levels.

Mitigation Measures

Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms: READO will provide drop boxes at all project sites and avail a toll free line for all complaint reporting. The complaint mechanisms process shall apply based on READO policies and procedures.Risk of land disputes and tenure insecurity in targeted project area: READO in coordination with all stakeholders will identify issues relating to land tenure and resolve them before the project starts. This will include a written legally binding agreement with land owners.Risk of explosive hazard contamination in project target area: READO in collaboration with government security agencies will work on receiving updated information on road safety and mine risk areas. The coordination will include agencies working on MRE to provide necessary support to the project in terms of risk identification and resolution.
