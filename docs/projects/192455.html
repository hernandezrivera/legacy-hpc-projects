Project Summary

The main purpose of this project is to ensure children that are out-of-education or are affected by the economic crisis are provided relevant, inclusive and quality education adhering to national and international standards on education in emergencies and child protection. The target population in this project is the most vulnerable children and youth and parents/caregivers from Lebanese and Migrant backgrounds in North Lebanon and Akkar. The project aims to provide learning support, food, referral to other sectors, SGBV and protection awareness sessions and procurement of learning materials, stationary and low cost solutions to the targeted groups. Moreover, the project include 800 most-vulnerable children enrolled in catch-up learning support programs addressing learning losses in public schools. The project includes a main outreach component to identify out of Education Children, refer children and youth to basic services and implement awareness sessions to children and youth on the importance of education and being in education. The number of target group ranged across activities as identified in cluster/sector later.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Flyers and brochures that illuminate on Al Fayhaa Association PSEA and child protection safeguarding incidents reporting mechanism are designed in child friendly in Arabic distributed across girls and boys, women and men in our programmes. Moreover, trained child protection officer and social workers will be available to communicate with beneficiaries as needed. Children, youth and parents/caregivers might be referred to other programmes based on their identified needs. Coordination across NGOs and agencies will be made to ensure beneficiaries are successfully placed in needed programmes. Facilitators will be trained on GBV and SGBV topics to ensure messages of protection in a do no harm approach is being communicated with beneficiaries. Facilitators in liaison with the inclusion officer will be trained on strategies and pedagogical approaches to ensure inclusion of children and youth of special needs mainstreamed in the remote classes. Aiming to ensure safe learning environment.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

This project will ensure accountability for affected populations through multiple processes. Primarily, Al Fayhaa Association management team will ensure feedback is taken from children and parents enrolled in the designed remote programmes. Feedback will include consulting parents/caregivers on the timing of homeschools and PSS-SEL sessions. Moreover, frequent satisfaction surveys will be implemented across the project period to inform the management team on the needs and challenges that might be faced by children and parents/caregivers. Feedback channels such as hotlines and helpdesks will be available. Through feedback channels children and parents/caregivers can provide any complaints. The awareness sessions implemented will ensure Al Fayhaa Association feedback and complaint mechanisms is explained in appropriate language to the affected population. Trained staff will be available to analyse the data of the surveys and respond to the feedback and complaints in a timely manner.
