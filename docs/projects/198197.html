2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activity 1. Support 1000 farmers for improved livestock production through provision of animals, feed, and training for diversification of animal feed/veterinary assistance service and income. Partners will provide support to the targeted HHs to improve access to adapted breeds, local feed resources and animal health interventions are available, along with improved and adapted technologies that include sound animal husbandry, on and off -farm product preservation and value-adding product processing. Activity 2. Support 3000 farmers for improved agricultural productivity through provision of seeds, agricultural tools, extension services for enhanced cultivation and harvesting techniques, for diversified income. Oxfam will implement activities that will assist 3000 farmers to increase production of crops like maize, sorghum, cowpea, fodder and cassava and engage with private sector and market players to. Partners in collaboration with district agricultural office and targeted community will select most vulnerable HH with access to land and practice farming and support them with provision of seeds, agricultural tools, extension services for enhanced cultivation and harvesting techniques, for diversified income. 3 .Cash support to small business and provide training on entrepreneurship and business management.100 businesswomen and 100 businessmen in 4 targgetd districts of Cabo Delgado and Nampula will receive technical and financial support that will help them to improve household income and building sustainable self-resilience. Implementing partners will conduct an assessment to evaluate the market potential, livelihoodsoptions and baseline capacity of target beneficiaries;Food Voucher program with food fair modality for 3 months using targeted approach. In coordination with Cabo Delgado sub clusters and local government authorities: Oxfam will conduct a rapid assessment on the need, food consumption and market to respond to critical needs caused by new influxes of IDPs, identified gap in an existing IDP settlements/camps or other natural disasters causing new IDP influxes. Partners will distribute food assistance for the targeted 5000 HH through voucher system and will ensure to increase the participation of women by selecting at least 30% women headed, at least 10% households with people with a disability.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project acknowledges the centrality of protection and adheres to the 4 core Sphere protection principles. Community engagement will follow the Acceptance Approach and activities will be informed by risk analysis with assistance provided across communities based on need without discrimination in accordance with the Do No Harm principle.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project will conduct a gender analysis of the multi hazard risk assessment and contextual analysis. Based on the findings of the assessment, the project will take a transformative approach in tackling the root causes that cause vulnerability to different groups of people in IDPS and host communities. The project will ensure transformation of gender relations and social and gender norms at household and community level by engaging men and boys to encourage and promote ownership of resources and productive asset and promote the empowerment of women, girls and excluded groups of people The project will work with existing governance structures in the target communities, and in some stages facilitate the creation of new ones, where women and girls are included as active participants in influential planning and decision-making position. Oxfam will Conduct gender analyse in all project target districts to trigger the prioritizing of intervention based on specific needs of women, girl, boys and men. Deliver gender in emergency and resilience development programming package to the Project civil society implementer stakeholder to ensure mainstreaming of gender issues on each thematic topic of intervention and project cycle
