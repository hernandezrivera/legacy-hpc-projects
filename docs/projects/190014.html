Project Summary

IDPs affected by heavy rains and floods by Tropical Storm Ana are safe while in collective sites and supported to return quickly with adequate shelter and NFI assistance.
To provide adequate and accurate data for displaced and affected people for efficient and effective response.
Provision of shelter and NFI kits to the most vulnerable individuals displaced by Tropical storm Ana
