Project Summary

Also will targeted Deir-ez-Zor in Hajin, Abu Hamam, Hajin , Bahra, The severity level according to HNO 2019 is 4 for these communities.
Each beneficiary (out of a total of 34,000 will cost 64.71 USD).
The percentage of target beneficiaries with disabilities is about 1%.
The cost of the project is 2,200,000 USD.
WHOSD received about 500000 USD for education services and served more than 20000
students and education personnel staff through integrated education and WaSH projects.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020, that is not officially published, The enrollment rate in formalschools are approximately 63 per cent with overall parity between boys and girls at theprimary level and an increased rate of enrollment for girls in the secondary level. Childrengenerally, start primary school at six with a sharp increase of reported attendance atseven years old. Attendance peaks and plateaus when children are between seven and 11years old and then sharply declines. Within this overall trend there is a slight comparativeincrease in the attendance of teenage girls compared to teenage boys and slightcomparative increase in the attendance of young men in tertiary education. The sharpdownward trend in enrollment between primary, secondary and tertiary education mayhighlight difficulties families have investing in their children’s education over time and theincreasing pressure for children to contribute to the household as they get older. Thedownward trend happens once adolescence has started, when children are exposed toincreased protection threats such as child labor, early marriage and recruitment byarmed groups. Education needs are highest in areas of the northwest where conflict anddisplacement is concentrated; areas of northeast where chronically unmet andunder met education needs are now being exacerbated by increased displacement andconflict and the south where a slow re-establishment of the education system iscompounded by returns. Endemic poverty and crisis-related stress and loss continues tocompel most families to focus on short-term immediate needs. These needs are oftenbalanced against the quality and relevance of the education provided and its likelihood toresult in future income and possible risks some children face going to school and being in school.

5 - Implementation of activities

Project duration is12 months: 8 TLS center will host 12000 children in the 12 months (3 cycle each one 4 months with total 4000 children)and 8 Mobile team will reach 12000 children in the 12 month (4 cycles each one 3 month with total 3000 children) and all target will receive non-formal education as following: 10,000 children will be enrolled in catch-up classes.4,000 children will receive basic literacy.10,000 children will receive language courses /English –Arabic-Kurdish.100 classrooms in 10 schools in Ar-Raqa rural will be rehabilitated to provide a safe learning environment, and increase access to schooling for girls, boys and learners with physical disabilities. Rehabilitate and restore 17 schools in the aforementioned areas in the four target communities in Hajin – DEZ (12 in Hajin community, 3 in abu alkhatir, 1 in Abu Hasan, and 1 in Bahra) with total 140 classrooms.The project will improve and ensure safer learning space for the currently enrolled 3000 students, as well support the enrolment of approximately 1000 new students to the newly renovated classrooms, with the target of 4000 learners reached in total. 160 teachers at 8 TLS centre and 8 mobile team will have the opportunity to participate in professional development. White Hats staff will implement the training with teachers and staff after participating in TOT conducted Teacher Training Program, whichincludes four modules: learner-cantered approach, inclusive education, protective education, and community-based psychosocial support BTL campaigns for children return to school Distribution of education supplies including 24,000 schools’ bags to children.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

WHOSD adheres to the protection mainstreaming guideline including: 1- do not harm:WHOSD will make sure that none of the beneficiaries will be subjected to any harm due tothe service delivery. WHOSD will consult closely with the target communities to avoid anypotential harms resulted from the service provision. 2- Meaningful access: WHOSD willensure that all the target beneficiaries will have safe and meaningful access to theavailable services. Gender balance and access to feedback mechanisms will be takeninto account. 3- Accountability: The beneficiaries will be informed and given access tofeedback and complaint tools. 4- Participation and empowerment: WHOSD, through thisintervention, will strengthen the resilience of the target communities and will focus moreon the long-term impact of the project to achieve this objective.
