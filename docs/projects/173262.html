Project Summary

The project will address the need of shelter and NFIs for Rohingya refugees living at Camp-13 in Cox`s Bazar. The aim of the proposed project- to improve the living condition of Rohingya refugees by providing safe and adequate shelter and necessary NFI assistance. To achieve the aim of the project, ISDE will provide TSA 2 Shelter assistance to 300 Rohingya Refugees, Capacity building training on disaster risk reduction (DRR), shelter upgradation/ construction to 300 Rohingya refugees, household level solar light distribution to 300 Rohingya HHs and NFI assistance to 1000 Rohingya HHs at Camp-13.
Transitional shelter assistance will not replacing all materials in the existing shelter, but replacing the damaged materials, allowing for an incremental process towards more durable shelters. The material assistance is composed by a mix modality of a minimal in-kind distribution and a conditional voucher support. Treated Bamboo will distribute to the beneficiaries for long lasting of Shelter. ISDE will purchase treated bamboo from BRAC/DRC/IOM. ISDE will engaged local skilled and unskilled labor force for all shelter construction. All households who will receive transitional shelter assistance, receive training on shelter upgradation and DRR. It is advisable to provide training prior to the provision of materials. Training will include revised Key Messages particularly raising bamboo using footings. Special attention will be given to the DRR features such as retention walls, drainage, bracing, tie down and fire protection (especially cooking areas). Upkeep and maintenance of shelter will be part of training as it goes a long way in ensuring durability of shelter. Special trainings will be provided to women. Diversity determinants (i.e. include older and disable persons) need to be considered while providing training, as all members of the community can play essential role in shelter maintenance. Trained Community Volunteer will be developed for shelter assistance. Community Volunteer will support the community members in implementing transitional shelter assistance. ISDE will arrange orientation on protection against sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) for project staff and volunteer. ISDE will request Sector support in PSEA training.
ISDE also provide Household level solar light and NFI materials to the Rohingya households in camp-13 as per sector guidance. Female will be given preference to enlist as beneficiary of this action especially widow, divorced, separated, lactating and pregnant women. Elderly and person with disability will also be given priority for this support. ISDE will maintain complaint mechanism in the project to raise any complaints, feedbacks, suggestions etc. A hot line number will be disseminated among the communities of project locations to generate complaints, feedbacks, suggestions etc. and will be recorded accordingly including solutions. During the COVID-19 pandemic, ISDE will conduct all activities with maintaining social and physical distance and health rule. ISDE will monitor the project Activities through senior staff of ISDE. Monitoring officer and Senior staffs will monitor the project activities regularly. Direct feedback mechanism from beneficiaries to Executive Director via mobile and inspection from HQ will established. ISDE will prepare progress report and submit to donor, sector and government department regularly.

1 - Identified Needs

Based on the MSNA2020 Shelter for the majority of Rohingya refugee families is still a common concern, with about one-third of Rohingya families buying shelter materials to repair their shelter. More than a quarter of Rohingya are unable to improve their shelters due to limited access to materials. According to the Phase 3 Shelter Progress Report (October 31, 2020) of Shelter/NFI sector, 85% coverage for Transitional Shelter at Camp-13 has been reached, with 15% of households still un-reached. So, TSA II shelter support /repair and maintenance activities are very much important in the next year. Besides this, Refugees are residing in makeshift shelters that are severely below basic humanitarian standards, overcrowded, and will not withstand seasonal climatic conditions. The low quality of the shelters and lack of privacy within these shelters has a serious impact on the physical and psychological well-being of the refugees - especially for women, children and older refugees and refugees with disabilities. Upgrades to shelters and site improvements are urgently required prior to the next monsoon and cyclone season. Also, the lack of adequate lighting in shelters and public spaces was reported as a top safety concern in the camp areas. So, the aim of this project is to provide transitional shelter solution, necessary NFIs and Household solar light to Rohingya refuges at Camp-13 in Cox`s Bazar.
