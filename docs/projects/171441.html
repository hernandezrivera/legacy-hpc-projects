Project Summary

The spread of the Coronavirus diseases and the resulting school closures have caused a disruption in education for Palestinian school children. To counteract the loss of learning due to the disruption in physical classes caused by the Pandemic, PSD proposes a holistic intervention that enables a smooth transition from in-person eduction to remote learning through grade-specific Interactive Learning Packages and support services to both educators and parents. These interactive packages will be standards-aligned, organized by course/concept and grade level, and each package will cover one month of course material as designed by pedagogical experts.
During the distance learning period that took place from March to May of 2020, the mode of teaching and assignments remained as they were during proximate learning which was not effective. PSD will help schools to adapt to remote learning through developing online/offline interactive packages that are specifically designed with blended learning needs. The packages will provide students with the opportunity to practice and apply key academic skills. The skill practice opportunities will be grade-level and developmentally appropriate, most importantly, they will be easily student-driven. PSD will create both digital (online/offline) and non-digital versions of the packet to ensure equity in access. In addition, the audio and visual aspects of the interactive packages ensues that children with disabilities can equally benefit from these resources.
The packages will be designed to:
 Provide students with continuous learning opportunities that reinforce critical standards
 Minimize instructional loss
 Help students remain engaged and connected with their learning
In addition, PSD will provide training to teachers and parents to facilitate a smooth transition between proximate and online learning as needed. The learning packages will serve as a tool for teachers to use both in the classroom and in on-line classes.
The intervention will benefit school children from grades 1-11 in the West Bank and Gaza, 300 teachers, and 4000 parents in marginalized communities. To do so, PSD will work closely with the Ministry of Education, Save the Children International, and Mothers' School Society. PSD has presented the Ministry with the project idea and has received written confirmation regarding the MoEs' need of Interactive Learning Packages. Following PSD's process of developing educational content, the supervisors from the MoE will assist in developing learning assignments and evaluating the final learning packages and SCI will provide technical knowledge regarding Early Grade Reading and Math skills as well support towards developing digital books for grades 1-4. PSD will work with Mothers' Schools along with local authorities and Community Based Organizations (CBOs) in marginalized communities to ensure that students have access to devices and/or printed packages and to facilitate parent training.

1 - Needs

The spread of the Coronavirus diseases and the resulting school closures have caused a disruption in education for Palestinian school children. The Ministry of Education (MoE) plans to utilize blended learning (face to face and remote) for the 2020-21 academic school year. The closure of schools in early March of 2020 meant that 1.43 million children across Palestine needed to access distance learning, while 360,000 children in isolated poor communities are without internet connection and in need of home based learning materials according to OCHA. The nature of the crisis and the large probability of its continuation in the current school year is beyond the capacity of school systems to address efficiently due to the lack of eLearning resources. PSD’s ten years of educational content development and use of technology to transform learning meant that the organization has been well poised to act swiftly during school closures to enable students to access quality educational resources. To counteract the loss of learning a disruption in physical classes can cause, PSD proposes grade-specific remote learning packages. These packages will be standards-aligned, organized by course and grade level, and each packet will cover one month of course material and will be designed by pedagogical experts. During the distance learning period that took place from March to May of 2020, the mode of teaching and assignments remained as they were during normal education, indicating lack of know-how among teachers to transition to online learning using relevant pedagogy. Parents were also unable to cope with the demands of remote learning. The Interactive Learning Packages along with tailored support will enable teachers and parents to facilitate a seamless transition between in-person and online learning and for students to thrive.

2 - Activities

PSD proposes Interactive Learning Packages as a holistic intervention to provide equitable quality educational resources to students through: 1. Developing Learning Packages: PSD will develop and digitalize learning packages for grades 1-11 in the following subjects: Math, Science, English, and Arabic and will enrich the Ministry of Education's Abbreviated Units with multimedia resources and self-learning materials, assignments, and assessment tools. The packages will provide students with meaningful activities that include engaging projects they can work on, individually and/or in groups, and that reinforces grade-level standards, allowing students to develop their understanding of concepts and apply their knowledge in new ways. PSD will utilize already developed resources including:a. Interactive digital books that include resources, videos, and simulations for fifth-ninth gradesb. Educational and assessment games that allow students to enjoy the learning processc. 1800 Khan Academy Arabic language videos aligned with Palestinian curriculumd. 1009 study collections in partnership with GooruThese resources will be mapped with grade-specific learning packages, and PSD will create additional resources to cover any gaps.2. Distribution of Learning Packages:These packages will be available in online and offline formats. The developed packages will be distributed on-line through the MoE portal, NETKETABi portal, and other educational sites. The offline copy will be made available on educational devices, students PC’s, and smart phones. PSD will also provide students without access to devices a hard copy of all the developed materials which will include printed instructions for parents of students in relevant grades to enable them to work with their children. 3. Teacher Training:PSD will train 300 MoE teachers from marginalized schools on how to implement distance learning effectively, how to design learning assignments, and what tools are available for their use. Although teachers have gained some experience during the last closure, the methods they practiced mirrored those that they used in face to face instruction. PSD will familiarize them with distance learning pedagogy. 4. Support Families with Self-Directive LearningPSD will work with marginalized schools and CBOs to identify parents in need of support. Through CBOs and youth volunteers who will be trained by PSD staff, workshops will be held for targeted location. These workshops will guide parents on how to use specific digital tools utilized in online learning and how to access digital and non-digital learning materials. The workshops will also provide parents with the know-how on supporting their children through this process by promoting focus, self-directed learning, etc. For parents in remote communities, instructions on packages will be sent through text messaging. PSD will work extensively with CBOs and youth volunteers in remote communities to provide them with the tools to assist families in their communities through technical support or tutoring services. PSD also proposes working with local radio and TV stations to air existing educational content developed by PSD. 5. Distribute NETKETABi Devices:PSD will provide an in-kind contribution of 100 NETKEABi devices to the most marginalized communities. NETKETABi is a learning device with customized technical specifications to meet the needs of its young users. Within the device, students have access to the digitized Palestinian curriculum, access to thousands of educational material through 1800 instructional videos in partnership with Khan Academy, 1009 study collections in partnership with Gooru, and educational games. Students with no access to internet connectivity are able to remain engaged in the learning process through the offline educational content made available on the devices. PSD proposes that these devices be made available to students or community centers in marginalized communities. To do so, PSD will conduct an assessment to determine students that do not have access to tablets or smart phones whether at home or through local centers.
