Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining essential health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls, women, and men, with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities through trained 44 PSWs on different MHPSS topics such psychological-behavioral problems of children and adolescents, techniques (Relaxation, and Stabilization, and psychosocial support through art), group facilitation skills and Self-care and stress management to provide integrated MHPSS activities linked with 14 health facilities (of our partner health organizations) and 10 MHPSS MCs, selection of these facilities will be in close coordination of health cluster and MHPSS TWG, locations include: Badama and Kherbet Eljoz!G0704038, HRO MHPSS clinic/center!G0704034, Kafr Takharim G0703021, DanaE0503008, Sarmda G0703105, Janudiyeh and Hamama G0704036, Darkosh G0700148, Sejr Mobile clinic 1!G0700147, Sejr Mobile clinic 2!G0700148, Idleb G0700102, Qah G0703077, Atma G0703071, Atma camp G0703063, Sandra b0204075, Arshaf B0204102 ,Azaz and Aghtrin B020410, Afrin and Ariha (new MHPSS activities will be launched starting of June 01st 2021 and funded by IOM). MHPSS activities include initial evaluation sessions, PM+, PFA, identification and referral, psycho-education sessions, follow up and group MHPSS sessions to address topics such as (depression, epilepsy, parental skills, psycho-behavioral problems, PSS sessions for adolescents and children). Most vulnerable groups (such as PwD, pregnant women, and elderly) will be targeted through outreach visits. In addition to the MHPSS trainings that will include training of 640 frontline health workers from different health facilities in Idleb and Aleppo governorates on suicide prevention in coordination with Health cluster to select those trainees. Besides HRO will train 10 CHWs on different health topics according to adopted standards manual developed by WHO to provide services. As for health services, HRO is planning to expand the MHPSS services with adding health (3 PHCs, and 3 Medical Mobile clinics) in the following locations: Aghtrin and Azaz B020410, Janudiyeh G0704036, Badama and Kherbet Eljoz!G0704038, Darkosh G0700148, Sandra b0204075, Arshaf B0204102. Health care activities will be provided by 9 doctors, 6 midwives, 12 nurses and 10 CHWs, activities include diagnosis and treatment, Child health services, Prevention and control of communicable diseases, Prevention and treatment of non-communicable diseases, Reproductive health services including ANC, PNC and family planning, nurse activities such as dressing and minor surgery and follow up of related cases, prevention services including health education and raising awareness on different topics such as IYCF, NCD, RH, and child health, in addition to the COVID-19 pandemic through conducting household visit by CHWs. All the health staff will be trained on the basic principles of GBV including identification and referral to specialized GBV services to enable the health facility accommodate survivors. HRO through running 3 PHCs, training 640 health actors, and conducting raising awareness sessions for the community members will contribute to improving the resilience of the overburdened health systems during COVID-19 outbreak. All activities will be implemented considering COVID-19 protective measures, besides the health staff will receive a training on COVID-19 IPC. 57,300 beneficiaries will be targeted with the project’s activities including 34,380 Females, 22,920 Males (5730 Elderly, 19,482 Adults, 14,325 Children, 17,190 Young Children, and 573 PwD). HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the health NGO partners, local councils, and camp mangers throughout the project period. Community members will be involved in implementing the proposed activities, and multiple channels will be in place to help them report suggestions, complaints, and PSEA concerns.

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2021. health workers alike experienced increased stress, placing additional demand on mental health services. Non-COVID-19 outbreak risk remained present, due to cumulative effects including fear of exposure to COVID-19, cost of health services, over-crowding and long wait times affect beneficiaries especially PwD. Disrupted medication, particularly for psychotropic drugs and NCD medicine were previously identified as a gap in health system. Geographically, the health sector severity scale reveals that areas of catastrophic need are primarily located in parts of Aleppo, Idleb in 2021, while areas of extreme need have remained primarily in NW and NE Syria. The disrupted healthcare system in areas of high severity significantly affected the wellbeing of women and girls who are unable to access essential SRH services. There is a need to focus on PHCs to respond to GBV, child health, integrated maternal health, management of NCDs and MH conditions. As per PIN of Global Health, targeted locations were scored as extreme need of health with total PIN in Idleb 1583465(Female 807567, Male 775898, Childs 712559, Adult 807567, elderly63339 and PWD 395866), While in Aleppo 608218(Female310191, Male298027, Child273698, Adult310191, elderly24329 and PWD 152055). Based on HRO RNA/Feb 2021/ conducted in All planned locations, 84% of respondents mentioned that people suffer from Psychological problems (83% Distress and Depression), HRO RNA/Sep 2020/in Bdama: 45% in need for NCD. HRO RNA/April 2021/in Darkoush: 86% of respondents do not receive sufficient medical services (55% SRH and 79% NCD).

5 - Implementation of activities

The project aims to train 44 PSWs on different MHPSS topics such as psychological-behavioral problems of children and adolescents, techniques (Relaxation, and Stabilization, and PSS through art), group facilitation skills, Self-care and stress management, then the trained PSWs will provide integrated MHPSS activities including individual consultations such initial evaluation, PM+, PFA, identification and referral, psycho-education, follow up, and group MHPSS sessions on topics such as depression, PSS for adolescent and children, and Parental skills, through 24 MHPSS teams (14 HFs and 10 linked mobile units), Most vulnerable groups will be targeted through the mobile units. Also, MHPSS trainings will include training 640 of health staff from different HFs in Idleb and Aleppo on suicide prevention in coordination with Health cluster. Besides HRO will train 10 CHWs on different topics according to WHO manual. The activities will include Running 3 PHC units and 3 mobile clinics to provide PHC activities by medical staff including 9 doctors, 6 midwives, 12 nurses, and 10 CHWs. Activities will include diagnosis and treatment, Child health services, Prevention, control, and treatment of communicable and non-communicable diseases, ANC, PNC, family planning, prevention services including health education on different topics such as IYCF, NCD, RH, child health, andCOVID-19 diseases by conducting household visits by CHWs. Field visits will be conducted by M&E team to ensure the quality of services, in addition to verifying the raw data before preparing HIS report, conducting beneficiaries’ satisfaction surveys, following complaints, where the team will consist of 2 M&E assistants and 3 Data entry.

In addition, staff with specific responsibilities (e.g. managers) will receive additional training commensurate with their role. 3. Risk: Employees in direct contact with children are accused of SEA cases previously.Mitigation: Managers and Human Resource team in HRO ensure robust recruitment and screening process for all the personnel, particularly for persons who will have any direct or indirect contact with children, in addition to checking the employees’ history through the references provided to ensure that there are no recorded SEA cases against any of them.
