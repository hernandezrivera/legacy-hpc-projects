Project Summary

The project aims to enhance the steadfastness and survival of the people of the countryside of As-Suwayda Governorate in their villages, especially in light of the internal displacement movement that occurs in the governorate from villages towards cities, which has caused an increase in unemployment and unemployment. People left to work in agriculture and animal husbandry. Hence the idea of ​​our project was launched by distributing 400 animal projects during the years 2002/2023, at a rate of 200 sheep projects and 200 cows projects during 2022/2023, at a rate of 200 projects each year / 100 cows and 100 sheep / for families who are able to work and have previous experience in raising livestock, where we will By distributing these grants to families who are able to work after conducting training for them on the principles of education in order to enhance their information by specialists. In remote villages in As-Suwayda Governorate, which will secure a stable income for these families and enhance their survival in their areas and not turn them into families seeking help

4 - Needs

The project seeks to provide support to families affected and threatened with internal displacement from the countryside to the cities due to the weak financial resource by providing a grant to raise sheep or cows for those who are able to work and have previous experience in this field, and for women without breadwinners who help their families by working in order to secure an acceptable income for them. The project seeks to provide 400 grants during 2022/2023, consisting of 10 sheep or cows, according to the needs and experience of each family.Families in As-Suwayda Governorate, especially in remote villages, have experience in working in this field, and the appropriate environment for education is available in As-Suwayda Governorate, according to the statistics of the Directorate of Agriculture in As-Suwayda Governorate and the FAO.

5 - Implementation of activities

Initially, the targeted villages of the project will be identified, which are the remote villages in As-Suwayda governorate that are suitable for education and whose people are threatened with displacement due to poverty.Second, communicating with local actors and community figures to identify the families that will be targeted in this project and that meet the project conditionsThird, verifying, through personal interviews, that the applicants match the conditionsFourth, conducting a special course for raising sheep and cows for the beneficiaries by a veterinarian to inform them of some basic details and remind them of themConducting a tender to purchase the required quantities through the sealing of livestock breedersFinally, the livestock was distributed to the farmers
