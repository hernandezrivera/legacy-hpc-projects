Project Summary

CFL is currently working in 5 communities in Dondo, approximately 1550 households. We have assessed all of these households and have calculated the needs of these villages based on our assessments. 100% of these homes have sustained damage or have been completely destroyed due to cyclone Idai. These Families are in need of assistance so that they can resume essential daily routines (like cooking, sleeping and personal hygiene) in a dignified and appropriate manner.
Project Activities:
Roofing repair: using zinc and wood supports for 500 households in Dondo district.
Thousands of families in these communities are struggling with lack of safe shelter. Families can improve their shelters to provide at least minimum level of safety, security, privacy and dignity by repairing roofs.
Home repair materials will be supplied to those who are able to repair their homes or for temporary shelter until a permanent structure can be built. Supplies include wood support for new roof, zinc roofing materials. These supplies will be delivered to each family and use of the materials will be monitored.
