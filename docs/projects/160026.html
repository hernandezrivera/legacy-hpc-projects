Project Summary

The objective of the project is to reach 30001 beneficiaries by addressing and targeting to 14,544 moderate Acute malnourished (MAM) in both IDPs and Non-IDPs, and 2993 of Severe acute malnutrition in both IDPs and Non-IDPs and 3464 Pregnant and Lactating women who have burden both IDPs and Non-IDPs and 5043 for prevention of children under five (5) both IDPs and Non-IDPs as well as prevention 3957 of pregnant and lactating women in both IDPs and Non-IDPs in the four selected locations.
During the implementation SYPD will reach 8,502 beneficiaries in Buulo-burte district (6371 as a treatment target and 2131 as prevention target), in Jalalaqsi district SYPD will target 7199 beneficiaries (5410 as treatment target and 1789 as a prevention), in Qoryole district SYPD will target 9699 beneficiaries (6,406 as a treatment target and 3,293 as prevention target), in Ceel barde district SYPD will reach 4601 beneficiaries (2815 as a treatment target and 1,786 as a prevention target).
The project will ensure that the most vulnerable groups including children, adolescents and communities in the selected localities are able resilient and adopt appropriate practices to reduce their vulnerability.
SYPD will ensure gender and age considerations by targeting the most vulnerable women and children in IDPs settlements and host communities. Each activity will contain a list of age and gender-disaggregated data to enable monitoring of progress towards gender and age equality.
SYPD will strengthen community and beneficiaries' involvement and accountability to affected people by forming project committees which will be in charge of receiving any complaints or feedback and dispute resolution. The project committees will be drawn from the project beneficiaries and will be trained on project management and coordination with other stakeholders.
At the inception of the project, SYPD will also hold meetings with the main stakeholders such as community leaders and government officials to create awareness of the project activities, enhance partnerships and strengthen coordination.
SYPD will coordinate with other partners in the same locations the Nutrition cluster to ensure better coordination, complementarity and avoid duplication.
SYPD will establish an effective monitoring and evaluation system where every activity will be tracked on a monthly basis and reporting on achievements shared with the Nutrition cluster on time. SYPD will actively participate in the Nutrition cluster coordination meetings and update all activities regularly to the cluster

Project Activities

NeedsMalnutrition remains high in Somalia. Urgent treatment and nutrition support is needed for approximately 170,000 acutely malnourished children, including over 50,000 who severely malnourished. Integrated support interventions should be sustained to maintain recent improvements as well as prevent further deterioration in the nutrition situation. Over 1.5 million people facing acute food security Crisis (IPC Phase3) or worse need urgent humanitarian assistance and livelihood support. Livelihood support is also required for 3.1 million people who are Stressed (IPC Phase 2) - FSNAU September 2018. Hiraan remain one of the hostspots with 114,00 people in Stress phase – IPC2, 42,000 under crisis, 11,000 under emergency phaseand 2,000 in catastrophe phase. The worst affected areas include North Gedo Riverine, Beletweyne District (Reverine & Agro-pastoral), South Gedo Agro-pastoral, South Gedo Riverine, Mataban, Jalalaqsi and Bulobute districts of Hiran Region, Juba Riverine, IDP Populations: Bosaaso, Garowe, Galkacyo; Qardho, Mogadishu; Baidoa, Kismayo and Dolow -FSNAU – September 2018. Activities# Scale up of existing Community Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM) programs to contain and arrest critical levels of acute malnutrition – Output: Reduced acute malnutrition in priority IDPs locations – Hiraan Burlo Burto # Screen 25% of under-five 35,000 girls & boys aged 6 to 59 months and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) for malnutrition # Scale up the existing preventive health programs (routine vaccination program and vitamin A supplementation) in IDP settlements that have registered high levels of stunting, underweight and under-five death rate (U5DR) – # Train community nutrition volunteers (CNVs) on CMAM and MUAC screening. Output: Improved capacity of community-based nutrition screening # Admit & treat 25% of severe acutely malnourished girls & boys aged 6-59 months, through Community based Management of Acute Malnutrition. Activity 6: Train staff on Community Management of Acute Malnutrition(CMAM)# Admit and treat of 25% MAM children aged 6 to 59 months, PLW and elderly. # Micronutrient supplementation for vulnerable groups (PLW and U5 children) including Vitamin A, zinc/ ORS,# Conduct integrated Food based acute malnutrition prevention through emergency Blanket supplementary feeding programs (eBSFP).# Community nutrition volunteers training on IYCF counseling and running Mother Support groups. # Conduct 2 surveys of IYCF # Conduct community mobilization and sensitization on CMAM and IYCF on quarterly basis# Distribute behaviour change communication IEC materials. # Establish 12 OTP/SFP centres

Mitigation Measures

Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement1. SYPD will incorporate inclusion considerations into project design by prioritizing internally displaced persons, returnees and host communities in food security interventions including establishing and strengthening community committees that are balance in terms of age, gender and clan diversity. 2. During the project startup, SYPD will organize consultative meetings with all stakeholders including minority groups to avoid social exclusion errors.3. SYPD will carry out a needs assessment to identify the needs of displaced people and other vulnerable groups. The beneficiary registration will ensure needs analysis brings to the fore all dimensions of exclusion and village relief committee will participate in beneficiary selection and registration. 4. SYPD will establish inclusive community communication mechanism by forming beneficiary feedback and complaints mechanisms to ensure that all vulnerable groups have a voice in the project implementation. The project implementation will mainstream capacity building on all dimensions of exclusion.5. SYPD will carry out community consultations in order to identify obstacles which may hamper the full participation of IDPs, including from marginalized communities in the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the projects. IDPs, host communities and refugee returnees will be included to prevent aggravation of tensions between IDPs and host communities.6. SYPD with the consultation of local authorities and elders will constitute a joint village committee and train on knowledge to oversight and manage the rehabilitated community infrastructures, the committee will solve the conflicts among the community. Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age1. SYPD will carry out needs analysis during the beneficiary selection and registration to ensure that all vulnerable people have access to assistance without discrimination. 2. SYPD will conduct constant community consultations meetings to identify barriers to the participation of women, girls, marginalized and minority groups and youth to ensure that risks regarding exclusion based on gender and age are identified and mitigated in a timely manner. 3. The project will include the formation of a feedback mechanism that involves women, men, and youth to gather information from beneficiaries’ access to assistance and provide timely feedback on the project achievement. 4. SYPD will carry out a deliberate inclusion of women, girls, youth and other vulnerable groups in the project design, implementation, and monitoring. Project committees will be all-inclusive of gender and age. 5. SYPD will train gender balanced committees to strengthen the capacity of vulnerable groups in participating in project implementation. 6. SYPD will manage assistance provision proximity to the dwelling places and in daylight to allow safe travel back home. Distributions will be managed through effective crowd control mechanisms and separate lines for men, women, and people with special needs. Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence1. SYPD will ensure that beneficiaries are not exposed to armed conflicts and violence by the nature of assistance provided by undertaking community consultations at all project stages. 2. SYPD will conduct pre-intervention assessment and do evaluation to identify any risks and community dynamics that may lead to violence or armed conflict. 3. SYPD will ensure the participation of all on affected communities, inclusion of all stakeholders in food security projects and coordination with government authorities.4. SYPD will also undertake pre-context analysis to identity the existing government institutions like the presence of judicial frameworks available in the area of intervention.5. SYPD will avoid overcrowding during the distributions as it may arise protection concerns

Please provide further explanation

Building of resilience of food insecure people will be a key component for the project implementation to tackle the key risks and address the underlying causes of food insecurity and vulnerability for IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in terms adequate and sustainable food security and livelihoods. SYPD will work with vulnerable people including forcibly displaced people, both refugees and IDPs and host communities to support resilience building through capacity building in agropastoral practices, income generation and asset creation. Even in the context of protracted displacements, the environment in Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Bay region, middle Shabelle still presents opportunities for durable solutions and resilience building. SYPD will work with the community leaders, relevant ministry at the local and national level, and local government authorities to find durable solutions including secure land tenure, capacity building and income generation activities, community assets, irrigation systems and hand over mechanisms for community based projects.
