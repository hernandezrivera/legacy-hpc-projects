Project Summary

- Based on the studies and reports submitted by international organizations and the questionnaires we obtained from the local community, it was revealed that there are several cases of domestic violence, gender-based violence, cases of bullying among children and adolescents, the marriage of underage girls, child labour, poverty, and many people with special needs, It is a case of domestic violence, where the father abuses girls in particular and prevents them from working or studying. It was also found that there were cases of exploitation and harassment by some groups of males over the age of 15, especially within large families that live together. The work team also found several cases of bullying and violence against children with special needs due to the parents’ fear or hatred of these cases. Through the study conducted on the ground, it was also noted that there was severe neglect of the care of children under 12 years, whether from a health, educational, or even psychological, which led to cases of violence among children under the age of 12 years and cases of harassment, in addition to the deteriorating health situation due to neglect of personal hygiene.
- in the protection sector, we offer a group of specialized programs to meet the needs, including awareness programs for women and girls, programs for fraternal care and early intervention to discover special cases, both negative and positive, such as cases of autism or talents, in addition to several vocational training programs that aim to Enhancing self-confidence and improving the living situation. These programs include all target groups, including women, girls, children, adolescents, and people with special needs. Our programs also include special courses to raise awareness of gender-based violence. Our programs also include the establishment of special clubs for women in which partnerships take place between the targeted women and girls and the training team to qualify women and girls to influence and lead in society. As part of the child protection programs, our organization is developing an action plan to build safe spaces for children to play, by renovating and preparing a public park that includes green spaces and children’s games under the direct supervision of caregivers, in addition to a closed place within the park for educational games.
- In our strategy, we face several risks that vary in degree of severity and probability, including:
o Difficulty in reaching some areas due to the lack and cost of transportation, which is a high and certain risk. We seek to provide a means of transportation for the organization to facilitate movement and reduce transportation costs
o Another high-level and confirmed risk is the lack of electricity and communications in many of the targeted areas in Idlib Governorate. If the organization provides a means of transportation, we can provide a mobile generator or battery and a transformer to provide electricity during the implementation of the programs
- The strategy may be exposed to the risk of exploitation or gender-based violence. All employees and volunteers have been trained on the risks of exploitation and gender-based violence, and a response plan has been developed that includes a direct hotline with the project management, in addition to training a team. A special person makes periodic visits to the targeted areas, communicates directly and confidentially with the targeted people, and learns about any problems. A code of professional conduct has also been developed for the employees and volunteers in the organization.
- Protection risks were analyzed during the implementation of the programs within the response plan, and the results were simple, and the degree of risk and probability was minimal. This is because most programs aim to provide services collectively or at the family level, and the work teams include a group of girls, women, and males, and each group is headed by a person from the public administrat

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Qaderoon Foundation’s humanitarian response plan, within the 2024 strategy, is based on the protection sector. Training programs for women with breadwinners, widows, and divorcees are based in response to the need to protect this group from exploitation and violence. These programs include psychological support and human development programs, in addition to several vocational qualification courses for recycling work and some professional crafts.The strategy of the Qaderoon Foundation for Development for the year 2024 also includes special programs for children from the age of 3 to 17 years. These programs include standards for exploring talents and special cases, which provides the opportunity to develop the talents and creativity of this group and treat special cases such as autism and learning difficulties.Qaderoon Foundation's strategy for 2024 focuses on supporting people with special needs, whether mental or physical, through treatment and training programs for cases of mental disability, such as Down Syndrome, cases of hypoxia, and cerebral retardation, and other programs specific to physical disabilities, such as physical and occupational therapy.All the activities proposed within the project are the basis of our organization’s work, and we work on these activities mainly because they are the core of our work, so we ensure that these services reach those who deserve them.
