Project Summary

In rural areas of Balochistan, if rights of women are violated, there is no option but to take the matter to Jirga. Jirgas are run by men who are mostly gender insensitive. In a majority of cases, women victims are not consulted before announcing a decision. Because of lawlessness in under developed areas of Balochistan so domestic violence, honor killing Child &forced marriages, Sexual, emotional or psychological violence, Physical punishment, Intimate partner violence are very common gender issues. Karo Kari became a business. Women avoid approaching police stations mainly due to cultural restraints HOPE believes that without engaging men, we cannot guarantee rights for women. If the male head of the family is sensitized on women’ rights and its benefit to the household and community, more women will be supported in their education and become empowered. Another important thing HOPE will do is to engage the tribal Sardars or leaders. Most Sardars are Ministers or occupy important positions in the provincial government. With men on board, acceptability for women’s empowerment initiatives increases. On other hand due to the social and cultural setup of Balochistan, women feel more comfortable to share their issues with women. This makes a strong basis for having both genders in project activities.,
HOPE will involve main stakeholders including political parties, judiciary, parliament, bureaucracy, Police and Levies officials’ civil society, and media. The project will focus on reducing gender-based violence (domestic and sexual violence perpetrated against women, and girls) in the home and in the society at large Project activities involve sensitization of different segments of society through utilizing different SBCC approaches., capacity building of law enforcement authorities, live stock business support at micro levels,. The project will be implemented in 3 Districts (Awaran, Washuk &Kharan).
Survivors of gender-based violence, women & girls, law enforcement official, youth, the general public, frontline workers (teachers, nurses, police officers, social workers and community leaders, Jirga heads) in proposed districts will be main beneficiaries
The main and ultimate goal of the project is to reduce the gender based violence in proposed districts
Total project budget is US$ 60000 for the period of project spam (6 months).Project Manager Salary will be paid by HOPE as a cost share .So amount requested from donor agency will be US$ 57200
HOPE has already physical presence in all proposed districts so existing HOPE office in propose districts will be used as project office. HOPE office in District Washuk will be used as a head office to coordinate with district offices

1- Project Activities

• Lobbying & advocacy of the Domestic Violence Act • Training of 150 police officers (50 from each proposed district for 3 days capacity building sessions )on the DVA, handling sexual offences, new sexual offences legislation (when passed) and related gender-based issues, Public education sessions with all SHOs (Police station heads) in all proposed districts • Gather information from reports Made to police stations in the various circles to conduct survey for the purpose of verification of activities as a means of improving the lives of women• Awareness sessions- [1to 2 hours ] with staff from health centres, women groups, youth groups, religious groups, jirgas &tribe head to sensitize them to issues of gender- based violence and remedies to such acts including the use of the DVA .Sessions will be held at union council level 1 session in each union council• Public education activities through print media and social media. Print media publications will be made on monthly basis since start of project till project ends while social media channels will be utilized on daily basis throughout project duration• Production and distribution of pamphlets, brochures, flyers, posters etc. that address the issue of domestic violence and gender-based violence• Livelihood support. Goats will be provided to 120 identified vulnerable women (40 in each district). It is proposed that 2 goats will be provided to each beneficiary

2 - Project Indicators

• More people would be aware of the Domestic Violence Act .Increased number of victims seeking assistance• More people able to secure effective implementation of legislation; increased awareness about gender- based violence leading to better use of the laws to obtain relief & prosecute offenders and alternatives to violence are disseminated and adapted Greater knowledge of the skills and attitudes needed to intervene successfully in domestic violence• More women would be encouraged to make reports and to use the services of the police• A greater number of persons use the DVA (THE BALOCHISTAN DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (PREVENTION AND PROTECTION) ACT to obtain justice• People begin to consider adapting a different and non- violent way Increased public awareness, Increase in the number of protection & other orders applied for under the DVA, Increase in the demand for legal aid services• Financial condition of 120 households improved
