Project Summary

According to the HNO 2021 draft issued by the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, there are 13.4 million people in Syria in need of humanitarian assistance - an increase of 21% compared to 2020 - as needs are increasingly exacerbated by the deteriorating economy.
Statistics indicate an increased exposure to ongoing hostilities, particularly in the northwest. Limited access to services; Displacement, which often separates children from family members, including primary caregivers, disrupts the education of 2.45 million children aged 5 to 17 years out of school, increasing the protection risks reported by parties to the conflict. In addition, children and primary caregivers have vulnerabilities associated with direct risks of the worst forms of child labor, disability, physical / psychological injury, severe neglect, family separation (including expected separation due to COVID19), child recruitment and child marriage. The project aims to reduce the aforementioned protection risks for displaced children and the host community in northwestern Syria in Jisr Al-Shughour in Al-Junudiya area within the AHF CP Center, in line with minimum child protection standards, as the center will present campaigns to increase awareness, case management and build the capacity of workers In the field of child protection to provide case management services through
1. Awareness teams that hold awareness sessions for women, men and children. With regard to CP issues and work to alleviate them. 2. Implement case management services that address the vulnerabilities of children and primary care providers associated with direct risks of the worst forms of child labor, disability, physical psychological injury, severe neglect, family separation (including expected separation due to COVID19), child recruitment and child marriage. These services will be provided through referrals from the community protection committees and through the monitoring team, according to joint procedures between the authorities. 3. Building the capacity of child protection workers in case management. The project will target host communities, IDPs, returnees, community centers and camps. The center will serve the most vulnerable children. All activities are aligned with the 2021 Humanitarian Response Plan as well as the Strategic Objectives of the Protection Cluster. The services will be provided for one year. The project will coordinate with affected populations and stakeholders. The project aims to include children with special needs, and all activities will be implemented according to the recommendations of the Protection Cluster and the WHO in order to deal with Covid-19 and the necessary preventive measures to protect the beneficiaries and humanitarian workers. .
The project will ensure accountability of affected populations and stakeholders through transparent accountability, complaints mechanism, feedback mechanism, and workers' competency monitoring.
The monitoring plan will ensure that:

2. Track and collect project indicators to measure project progress at the output level

4 - Needs

A comprehensive needs assessment was conducted in Jisr Al-Shughour to provide basic data to obtain a comprehensive understanding of the current living situation, where 588 men, women, boys and girls were interviewed, and the results were as follows:All respondents said that Shaqour Bridge had children under the age of 18 who had child laborThe causes of child labor were: 79.2% of the respondents reported that it was due to the death of a primary care provider 61.1% of respondents stated that this is due to poverty and the high cost of living. 69.4% of respondents reported that due to the continuing displacement, the main reasons that led to the presence of unaccompanied children were: 39% of respondents reported that because of separation / divorce or abandonment of the family. 73.2% of respondents said that it is due to the death of a primary care provider. 73.2% of respondents reported that because of displacement. 48.8% of respondents reported this due to war and instability. Effects of Disability on Persons with Disabilities: 87.5% of respondents reported that deprivation of education. 75% of respondents said distress and mental disorders. 87.5% of respondents reported being denied employment opportunities. 75% of the respondents said that social isolation. 25% of respondents said that bullying is by others The main causes of mental disorders were: 59.1% of respondents reported that it was due to family violence. 88.6% of respondents stated that this is due to war and instability. 18.2% of respondents reported this because of displacement. 18.2% of respondents reported that it is because of leaving school. 18.2 of the respondents said that because of exclusion from social life.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: COVID-19 pandemic and its impact on awareness activities, case management and training probability: high Mitigation: - Maintaining social distance between beneficiaries and the project staff - Use methods of protection, including masks, gloves and sterilizers - Avoiding meetings that contain many beneficiaries - Spreading awareness on ways of protection around the project implementation locations and on transportation means of the project - Transfer project personnel in batches within transportation means to reduce congestion - If necessary, implementation will be terminated in the field and follow up on cases that have a high-risk caseRisk 2: CP activities are suspended due to the conflict. probability: medium. Mitigation: in the event of attacks or invasion of the region, the service center will be moved to the Jisr Al-Shughour area in coordination with the local authorities to ensure the safety of project personnel and beneficiaries and after acquiring the required approval Risk 3: Council local intervention and impeding the implementation of the project or Interference by armed groups to prevent the implementation of child protection activities that are related to early marriage and Child protection issues in conflict situations probability: Medium Mitigation: signing a memorandum of understanding with the local council to avoid obstacles, interference or external dictates by armed groups - Raising awareness of the community and primary caregivers to cooperate in mitigating the risks of protection. - Establishing community protection networks that are made up of community leaders and influencers to support unhindered child protection activities.
