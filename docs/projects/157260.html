Project Summary

The project aims to build capacity of target communities to support their vulnerable including those affected GBV,PLWD, the elderly and other PSN through community based gender sensitive and culturally appropriate interventions that built community resilience, multiplier effect and sustained results. It offers to tackle general protection plus a very sensitive neglected area of protection that of GBV including SGBV and would like to try innovations and practices to reach out to communities to achieve its goals. Women and children bear the greatest brunt in any conflict and more so with GBV where communities settle their scores using women, are still practicing harmful cultural practices and are not open to discussion on women rights and empowerment.
the project will focus on women and children, it aims to reinforce community-based structures and social services, identification and referral of vulnerable people, awareness-raising on protection risks and rights, conflict resolution, harmful practices and hygiene promotion to men, women, girls and boys. The community centers will provide fullback age of PSS,case management,referral and awareness).
The project envisions that giving women a safe space, conduct thresh hold activities and have psycho-social support from trained and experience protection workers has far reaching results in ensuring women and girls get support from each other and from the communities. The safe spaces will also provide a place for further support to SGBV. GAH will build community based structures that support the frameworks created and ensure longer term impacts, safety and security for women and girls through advocacy to tackle issues like FGM,early marriage,girl child education,women voices and rights recognition and participation of women in communities.
As protection oriented organization, GAH has the sufficient capacity and competences to undertake the proposed activities under this HRP. GAH has well established offices in all mentioned locations and has been implementing protection related intervention since 2012.GAH has sold M&E system, financial system and procurement manual which recognized by UN and international donors.
during 2017 and 2018 GAH has supported more than 100 PLWD with assistive devices, moreover more than 500 children and women received cash support (cash grants, vouchers)
This project will focus on the displaced population in difficult and hard to reach locations, including Alabassiya, Abujebeha, Leri, in South Kordofan. Gerida, Marshang, EJM in South Darfur, Abujabara, Abukarinka in East Darfur, SerfOmra in North Darfur and Abyie PCA. The target beneficiary is 22500(13,500Female 9,000 Male) Reached through activities 13WC, 10 locality level CBPN and 180 community awareness sessions.Project will conduct WC activation (literacy classes, handicrafts, life -skills, PSS) TTT for women and DTD for men and peer and clubs for adolescent girls and boys.
Despite the lasting impact of sexual violence on survivors, including those with children born as a result of rape,no reparations have been paid. Victims of sexual violence are very often fearful of reporting the crime or seeking assistance, further compounding their suffering. Because sexual violence is vastly under reported, the lack of reported cases cannot be equated with the absence of violence. This project will work with communities to increase level of service seeking behavior for victims but most importantly to impact on behavior change that has reinforced the violation that have continued over time.
The situation with the economic crisis in Sudan has had a great impact on families and has further pushed more people to be vulnerable and this implies protection issues will increase with families(women) sometimes seeking negative coping mechanisms. As situations worsen PSN can no longer be supported with the existing community systems as resources are scarce and thus become destitute(PLWD,Elderly,vulnerable female head HH)

Needs

The protracted crisis in these target states and Abyie PCA, has had major social and economic impacts, making millions of people displaced, causing significant loses, destruction of livelihoods and infrastructure and negative access to public services. Patterns of widespread and systematic sexual violence have devastated the lives and livelihoods of women and girls in these protracted conflict areas, and the perpetrators continue to operate in a climate of impunity. Given the general lack of data for Protection in general, most of the information herein is inferred, the data is even more scarce for GBV and information here is based on interactions with the community and other actors , assessments by other sectors, some protection assessments, program reports and mission reports.According to assessments done in these areas; Inter agency rapid assessment for South Kordofan (Alabassiya, Abujebeha and Alliri) February 2018. inter agency rapid assessment in South Dafur in Marshang in May 2018, and in Nyala North January 2019, Voluntarism Return Verification Mission in Saraf Omra and El Sireaf Localities December 2018 and 3,000 HH visits in Abyie and according to the protection HNO, the protection situation in these areas is dire and immediate action is needed. The assessments find a lack of basic services; shelters, water, food, health services and insecurity which in essence means vulnerable people are exposed to protection concerns. In most of the assessed areas over 55% of the HH heads are women some of whom have been violated GBV/ SGBV, FGM, early marriage, Women’s financial status force them to work in unfavorable and insecure environments that expose them to violence and protection risks (working in gold mines, collecting wood and water and caring for the family). In return areas, it was observed that there were issues with safety and dignity especially in North Darfur where there is insecurity for the returnees which justified constructing of a police station. In Abyie it was only in 2018 that programming in protection was started and the gaps vis-a -viz the support is huge. There is no single police station, women center or CFS. In all target local there is lack or very limited care given to PLWD , elderly or other PSN. The referral mechanisms are weak or not linked to services and need strengthening in addition to strengthening community based structures. Gender discrimination also contributes to the denial of opportunities to basic services such as education that can be a cause or consequence of violence and exploitation against vulnerable women. Social acceptance of the general lack of education for girls in these areas has a huge bearing on low literacy rates (approx. 70%). Vulnerability to exploitation is further increased due to the current economic situation that is increasing vulnerability and reducing economic independence with fewer families able to cope.
