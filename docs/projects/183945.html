Project Summary

Alight began working in South Darfur in 2004 with emergency humanitarian aid to people affected along the Nyala-Gereida and Nyala-Tulus corridors. In 2014, Alight expanded its operations to East Darfur, with activities reaching 7 localities in the state so far supports, South Sudanese refugees, IDPs and the host community.
Food security in Sudan has been very much deteriorated due to a combination of factors include and not limited to; conflict, spread of COVID-19 epidemic, climate change and economic instability has contributed to food insecure across the country and exacerbated the situation in Darfur in particular south and east where many IDPs and returnees are living. The major livelihood activities are farming, pastoralism, and trading. However, the livelihood activities are significantly affected by the protracted displacement in the State since 2003.
The prevention measurements have been taken to cope with COVID-19 outbreaks such as; lock downs have reduced the access to the food commodities, market functional and border trades these compromised livelihoods and reduced purchasing power at house hold level in addition to decrease of daily labor opportunities.
Over all; The impact of the conflict has had a destructive effect on the limited agricultural infrastructure, values chains, crop and livestock production and food security.
Through this project Alight will contribute to:
1- Improve the food security status of assessed food insecure people through life-saving and life sustaining food assistance
2- Support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives
As per Sudan HRP 2021; the number of people in need are 7.1 million and 6.2 million will be targeted by FSL partners , Alight intervention will contribute to reach overall targets goal 175, 938 catchment population in targeted areas of out 28,531 in South Darfur (Asalam + Gereida ) and 147, 407 in East Darfur Yassin and Sharia localities .The project will directly target total 8,600 people . the communities in these areas are returnees, IDPs and host communities ,vulnerable individuals affected by conflict and other disaster such fire outbreaks, communal conflicts expose to food insecure due to food unavailability and access in addition to small-scale farmers who face major constraints in farming because of high costs of production, post-harvest losses, access to farmlands and etc. The Alight response is building upon the ongoing humanitarian response and real needs of the people and this will be in line with the cluster priorities and synergies with the country humanitarian plan. The project intervention will focus on rehabilitation of community assets through cash base interventions and in kind support , Procurement and distribution of agricultural inputs and seeds include livestock and vocational trainings and assessment.
There will be additional cross-cutting issues mainstreamed through the proposed intervention: - gender - women will be consulted and will participate in the whole project cycle (design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation), women-headed households will be prioritized for the proposed activities, and the market analysis will seek for a feedback related to women's special needs, security and an access to the market; - vulnerability and underserving - will be the key criteria for the beneficiaries selection, and apart from female headed households the project will prioritize IDPs and host community members that are handicapped, chronically sick and elderly people, but also those with a large number of children, no income and access to assets, poor shelter conditions and not covered by the WFP distributions networks; - protection - including a ‘do no harm approach’ and ensuring secure and safe access to food security and livelihood and markets for the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host communities.

1 - Needs

Alight FSL project will focus on 2 states of South and East Darfur, 4 localities will mainly be targeted; Shiaria and Yassin in East Darfur in addition to; Alsalam and Gereida in South Darfur. The proposed targeted localities identified as high levels of food insecurity, through the IPC acute food insecurity analysis that indicated that Sharia, Yassin, Gereida and Alsalam localities are recognized among the highest percentages of crisis and emergency levels of food insecurity (IPC phase three and above). According to HRP report of 2021. The communities in this areas are conflict affected IDPs , returnees and vulnerable host communities who lost their assets and livelihoods due to conflict hence destruction of community assets ,poor food production , harvest losses , lack of capacity are the maijoir challenges facing the communities in these areas . The project will target total direct beneficiaries of 8, 600 people in two states mainly returnees, IDPs and host communities. The project intervention focus under two main objectives of 1&2 (Improve the food security status of assessed food insecure people through life-saving and life sustaining food assistance) and (Support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives). Under objective 1; the project intervention will focus on food for asset in-kind-cash and vouchers through supporting household on cash base intervention and in kind to maintain the community assets by rehabilitation and constructing them , emergency agricultural inputs and support this will be through procurement and distribution of seeds and agricultural tools as well as livestock support to the targeted groups that affected by conflict and natural disasters and require an urgent assistance among Returnees, IDPs, and vulnerable host and resident communities. Alight will support cash assistance, and emergency agricultural and livestock interventions which aim to improved productivity and availability of food and other economic activities.Under objective 2; Alight intervention will focus on activities that minimize the needs of the target groups at communities and households level helping them to be able to adapt with negative coping strategies, the project will provide capacity building assistance through support on construction of local infrastructure to restore agricultural and livestock productions reduce post-harvest loss through improved storage, development of food processing techniques and support to small-scale farmers through support on vocational trainings and extensions . Alight will continue working with key stakeholders from local communities, government, CBOs and civil society organizations that are involved in the identification, design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of projects to build the project ownership and ensure its sustainability, in addition Alight will approach community base initiatives on beneficiary selections during activities implementation where alight provide standard guidance and criteria and give option for CBOs to select benefices as they are better placed to know the contextual and dynamics of the communities .

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The major environmental issues in Sudan currently include, wanton destruction of theenvironment/deforestation, poor environmental sanitation, pollution and poor land use planning at both State and national level. Therefore, Alight will focus on environmental friendly activities and conduct awareness raising on environmental issues, waste disposal, hygiene promotion and advocacy on proper land use and planning. Construction activities within the FSL and other Program sectors will consider effects on the environment (e.g. sites for construction, as well as collection of construction materials like sand and gravel). Debris and left over materials will be disposed of safely and sites will be leveled before buildings are used. Backfilling will be done on areas where brick-making activities create depressions to prevent breeding of mosquitoes during rainy. In meetings and trainings for the communities, conservation of natural resources and environmental protection issues will be practiced and explained such trees cutting etc.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

(Institutional and financial arrangements for gender mainstreaming in agriculture and rural development National economic policy in the Sudan has traditionally not included any provisions for women in agriculture, and this has not changed significantly under the current transitional government. The Sudan’s three-year National Economic Growth Policy, adopted in 2020, which was based on 15 sectorial workshops, includes “developing new policies for improving production and productivity” as a major pillar for economic reform and mentions seven important aspects to be considered under this pillar, none of which refer to women. Overall, the economic policy does not address women’s inclusion, nor does it consider the recommendations of the “Women and Economy” workshop organized by women’s networks in collaboration with the Ministry of Labour and Social Planning. The three-year National Economic Plan has 10 strategic objectives. Women are addressed in objective number 5, which reads, “Ensuring and promoting women’s rights in all areas with fair and deserving representation.”) - FAO Gender Report for agriculture 2021 Alight will play a crucial role in enhancing the gender mainstreaming during implementation, the women play vital production role than the men in the field of agriculture in Darfur, the FAO gender reports shows that women participation is over 60% in all phase of agriculture, planting, harvesting, chiming. etc As cross cutting themes, projects that focus on the training of staff on protection mainstreaming, humanitarian codes of conduct, child protection policies as well as in Do No Harm.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

In regard to AAP;Alight will employ the AGD policy that will be implemented through community based, participatory approaches that are already well established in the communities. The Project will ensure that participation, feedback and complaints mechanisms are integrated in all strategies and plans all through the project cycle. This is to ensure the project decisions and actions are responsive to the expressed priorities, needs, capacities and views of all persons of concern irrespective of Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. This is aimed at ensuring meaningful participation, enhanced communication and transparency, improved feedback and response. ALIGHT will ensure a balanced boys, girls, women and men in all project activities with relative 60% female and 40% male. Data for beneficiaries will be disaggregated by gender, age groups and location, as the project activities are tailored for different groups in line with developmental stages, needs and vulnerabilities and basing on relevant parameters appropriate in reverence to heightened protection risks of the persons of concern. The Project will ensure at all persons of concern have equal and non- discriminatory access to protection, assistance and solutions, this will build on their resilience and recovery.The project will engage much better with boys, girls, men and women while in their safe spaces. Integration of other Alight protection projects will be enhanced such that activities like awareness raising, dialogues, will be integrated in our FSL programming approach.Alight will maintain effective feedback systems (including comments, suggestions and complaints) using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non- sensitive feedback e.g. use of complaints boxes, toll free hot lines. In regard to PSEA;All Alight staffs have committed to and signed PSEA policy, Child Protection policy, Code of Conduct as well as the engaged community based networks Alight is a member of National and State levels PSEA task force and regularly coordinates with other partners in relevant PSEA forums. Under the Project, we will conduct Safeguarding and SEA risk assessments to mitigate any potential or identified risks of SEA. Alight has contributed to Inter Agency Action Plan for the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and will ensure Zero Tolerance - No Excuse to SEA in all its operations and project. Jointly with the PSEA task force, we will develop relevant SEA IEC materials and distribute them in communities, raise awareness on SEA, strengthen community based complaints mechanisms, enhance reporting and ensure appropriate measures are undertaken for investigations.
