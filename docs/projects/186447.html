Project Summary

The intervention is to build resilience of the target local government areas (LGA) of Bade, Nguru and Yunusari in Yobe state, Nigeria. Beneficiaries will perform task in the communities of the 3 LGA rehabilitating communal assets and get payment after the work each month, beneficiaries with existing small scale business or newly established will be supported with cash to boost their business. The beneficiaries will be attached with master Trainers for skills training and be supported with in-kind start-up tools, 84 VSLA will be established covering all the 2,100 people. To strengthen the local government system, 25 staff each of the 3 LGAs will be trained to improve on their capacity in the areas of management, accountability, efficiency and planning, this will also include topics on GBV , PSHEA and fraud prevention. To enhance the functionality of the LGA, the project will support each LGA with 50 chairs, 50 tables, 10 computers set, 1 projector, 3 printers, 250KVA generator, 3 projector screen, 10 pair of air conditioners and 3 mobile communication sets. This project will be an integrated one, the different sections of the project will be running at the same time. It will be for 12 months, the cash for work and small scale business project beneficiaries will be mobilized to join the Village Savings Loan Association (VSLA), this will help them to have access to saving and loan facilities. The groups of beneficiaries in the VSLA will have access to training on leadership, business development and financial management. The project target majorly the adult for direct support with a special focus on 70% female due to their high vulnerability. Households headed by women, widow, children and people living with disability will be given priority in participants’ selection. Household with pregnant and lactating women will be given preference in targeting. Plan has strong mechanism for Accountability to Affected Population (AAP), Plan International will work with community structure in the project location, this community structure will be carried along during project planning, implementation and evaluation. Responses from stakeholders will be received through a dedicated phone line and response will be given while issues will be resolved as soon as possible, issues that may need referral will be referred to other partners for support. Plan International has zero tolerance to GBV , there will be a Gender and Inclusion staff who will ensure prevention of gender discrimination in all interventions and monitor the prevention of gender based violence that may come up in the project. All stakeholders will pass through PSEA training, sign the organization PSEA policy. All contract will have PSEA policy as part of the document to sign. All cases of SEA are to be reported immediately. All training sessions will have a short PSEA session as part of the agenda. This project is part of our Plan Emergency Response Plan strategy, all the interventions will be continued for long impact even after the end of the project period.

1 - Needs

The project will address the needs on community assets and social facilities which were destroyed during the conflict in Yobe state. Some of the infrastructure are in dilapidated state, some are completely un-useable, such as roads, bridges, schools, hospitals and town halls. Nguru and Bade Local Government Areas (LGA) has blocked drainage that often lead to flood, there is high sanitation and refuse disposal challenges in the communities that can be addressed through cash for work. In Yunusari LGA, the local government facility is in pathetic state that need urgent support, the effect of conflict from insurgency attack has affected many social infrastructures such as schools building, hospitals and others. Other needs identified in the 3 LGAs are the inadequate skills of the young women and men in vocation that will assist in improving their livelihood, most affected people are not having gainful source of income to sustain their livelihood. Some have skills but not enough to help them in competing in the trade and needed to be upgraded with more knowledge. Many are using sub-standard tools for their vocation. Those in business have inadequate capital to boost their trade to get enough profit. The business cannot generate enough profit for re- investment, many are not having good financial management capacity to maintain the business, majority have lost all their business tools and working materials. The people in the areas are not having access to saving and loan scheme to help them in their business. They have no collateral for formal bank loans, many are not comfortable to patronize formal financial institutions. The local government systems are having gaps in resources, the human resources are not adequately trained to be highly efficient in their duty, the female staff are specially are not so much at advantage for training opportunities due to traditional household tasks; the knowledge base for staff to perform at expected level is low and needs to be upgraded. Training on the most important tasks of each staff are not been provided as expected. The local government funding for facility, equipment, furniture, office tools are very poor. Majority of the staff are working with obsolete tools and sub- standard facilities. There is epileptic supply of electricity from the national grid which affects the performance of the local government services. There is no high voltage electricity generator for office use, those in use are not well maintained for good output of needed electricity supply. The office computer for staff use is below standard, there are inadequate personal laptops for staff use.

2 - Activities

These activities were chosen based on gaps identified in the areas by Plan International during our field surveys. Plan International has presence in Yobe state and have been running projects in these communities, we are part of the community and we feel the gaps and also have passion to help in filling the gaps when possible. During our discussion with the community, we have reported cases of the sub-standard communal social facilities that need to be rehabilitated, there are able body men and women that can be facilitated through cash for work approach to rehabilitate these infrastructures, the opportunity for cash support will also reduce the negative coping strategies, provide cash to meet households basic needs and address other areas of life like health, hygiene and education for their children. The unemployed young women and men are many in these communities, some have no skill for gainful engagement in a profitable business while some in business has low capital for re-investment, the project will therefore support with skill development and business management training. The project participants will be supported with necessary tools after the training, the beneficiaries will be given follow up and facilitated to join the Village and savings association (VSLA). The VSLA groups will be supported with kits to have successful operations, through the groups, more training will be given to the members, the groups will be supported with seed money to boost the fund for loaning the members, this will support the members business and lead to income for sustaining their livelihood. The government at local level has not been able to deliver expected mandate as the needs arises, this is due to low staff capacity and working tools. The project will focus on the staff capacity development, the women will be targeted more than men due to past disadvantage position they have been in the past. This project will provide training on accountability, sexual abuse, fraud prevention and standard management practices. This training will be facilitated by competent trainers who are experienced in these areas, this will improve the delivery approach of the staff, increase their competence and will lead to high outcomes in the community. The local government working tools are not adequate, this project will support the system by strengthening the facilities; equipment such as generators, working tables, more comfortable chairs, environmental friendly air conditioners, multi media computers projectors, projector screen, personal laptops and communication sets will be provided as support through this project. The provision of needed working tools and equipment will help to make the staff to be more efficient and effective in carrying out their daily work. This will translate to more development in the target community.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The project will follow protection guidelines, interventions will be carried out in safe spaces chosen by the community, all activities will take place during the day time to prevent attack that usually occur in the dark of the day. Activities locations will close to the beneficiaries houses to prevent long distance travelling. There will constant awareness creation on protection, prevention of sexual abuse, exploitation and abuse during all activities. There will constant feedback mechanism put in place to address grievances and risks that may come up during project implementations. All travelling will be approved by security manager before take off. All categories of people including women, people living with disabilities, marginalized section of the community will involved in project planning, implementation and monitoring. All stakeholders will pass through Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) training, sign the organization PSEA policy. All contract will have PSEApolicy as part of the document to sign. All cases of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse are to be reported immediately. All training session will have a short PSEA session as part of the agenda.
