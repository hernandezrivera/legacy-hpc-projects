Project Summary

This project objective is to reduce food and livelihood insecurity of the 50420 people who are extremely vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host community through promoting agriculture & livestock production and livelihood in localities of Nertiti, Bindisi, Um Dukhun & Golo Central Darfur State where duration of the project will 1 year.
SORC target of 50420 people in need (10084HH) in the localities above will receive agriculture inputs, animal traction and extension services as well as veterinary services that includes animal health services (including vaccination and de-worming) and training of community animal health worker. Support return communities with agro-enterprise training to raise their marketing skills and diversify livelihood opportunities. Vocational trainings targeting women and youth will open market opportunity for them and generate new employment opportunities. Natural Resources Management to maintain environment.SORC will coordinate with community leaders, HAC, line Ministries and other stakeholders for project start up. HAC shall support SORC in the project implementation smoothly and successfully as they will facilitate in signing of Technical Agreement and travel permits. Currently there are limited humanitarian interventions in the targeted villages where SORC through the project developed will not cover the whole IDPs and returnees who are in need from the targeted groupsSORC has valid registration and presence in Central Darfur State which will make it easy when approved the project, SORC will immediately seek HAC in Central Darfur for signing the Technical Agreement in short time as possible to in order to have effective implementation.

Needs

Continued conflicts and fragile peace agreements in Darfur are major factors that will put more than 3.6 million people in crises IPC Phase 3 or worse with the recurrent conflict highly populated fertile productive volcanic areas in Jebel Marra since mid-January 2016.FEWS NET Food Security Outlook report January 2018 to January 2019 staple food prices are expected to remain very high through January 2019, even though harvests starting in October 2018 could lead to small declines. Food security outcomes are expected to improve starting in October, following seasonal improvements in livestock productivity and as households' access own-produced foods and earn in-kind income from agricultural labor. However, the number of people requiring emergency food assistance is likely to remain at above normal levels during the harvest period. The scope and magnitude of this food insecurity crisis is well illustrated in the HNO 2019 which estimated 5674216 people in need of food security & livelihood and targeted FSL sector a total number of food insecure people in Sudan at 4390709 dis aggregated by IDPs 1864195, refugees 608401 and returnees 324328. All Interagency assessments and household food security consumption score and copying mechanism survey conducted between December 2016 to September 2017 for IDPs, returnees and host communities in Nertiti, Umm Dukhun, Bindisi, and Golo localities indicates delayed rainfall, lengthy dry spell in parts of Central Darfur State resulted to crop pasture failure and reduced seasonal production by 47% in the most parts of the localities.In the localities above there are a needs of provision of agriculture inputs, animal traction and extension services as well as veterinary services that includes animal health services (including vaccination and de-worming) and training of community animal health worker Support return communities with agro-enterprise training to raise their marketing skills and diversify livelihood opportunities. Vocational trainings targeting women and youth will open market opportunity for them and generate new employment opportunities. Natural Resources Management to maintain environment.Women traditional in Darfur are vulnerable as they face severe exclusion and inequality.70% of women are involved in firewood collection and making of charcoal for domestic use in urban and rural areas to enhance their household livelihood. In order to reduce negative impact on environment 70 % of women will be targeted to improved fuel that includes FES, bio fuel & bio gas using agriculture and livestock waste that reduces forest destruction, their exposure to risk of fetching firewood and give chance for forest regeneration, maintenances of natural resources can be mitigate by involving women on manufacturing improved fuel efficiency stove which reduces cutting of trees for meal preparation and usage of solar power for water pump by small scale winter season vegetable production which are environmentally friendly

Activities

Activity: 1.1 Provision and distribution of agricultural inputs (seeds, tools and services) to 4000 HH (2000HH of IDPs & 2000 HH returnees)Activity: 1.2 Distribution of agricultural tools to 50% of 4000 HH mostly female headed HHActivity: 1.3 Training of 100 agricultural village committee on extension services(Activity description: Communities mobilization & sensitization will be conducted where selection & registrations of the beneficiaries will be done at the project site, tools required will be identified, seeds will be requested from FAO core pipeline, distributions of seeds 7 tools to beneficiaries and training of VAC will be conducted)Activity: 2.1 Provision of livestock inputs (vaccines, drugs and services) to 70000 heads of animals(2800HH) Activity: 2.2 Restocking 800 female headed households (500 IDPs & 300 host community) with 2400 milking goats Activity: 2.3 Training of 80 Community Animal Health Workers (CAHWs). (Activity description: Communities mobilization & sensitization will be conducted where selection & registrations of the beneficiaries will be done at the project site, vaccines & drugs will be requested from FAO core pipeline, training of CAHWs, vaccination & treatment of animals)Activity 3.1 Promotion of community awareness on regeneration of forests and fuel efficient stoves (FES) targeting 1500 IDPs and 1500 host community Activity 3.2 Procure and distribution of 160 FES framesActivity 3.3 Training of 1000 beneficiaries (750 female and 250 youth) on FESActivity 3.5 Training of 1000 women on non-wood forest products processing, packing, storage and marketing. Activity 3.6 Training of 1000 HH on small business skills for the targeted beneficiaries(Activity description: Communities mobilization & sensitization will be conducted where selection & registrations of the beneficiaries will be done at the project site, provision of IGAs for skills enhancement and training on quality improvement and protection of environment).
