Project Summary

Due to the conflict in Syria, it is estimated that 11.5% of the population within Syria have been killed or injured, more than 5 million people have fled to neighboring countries and over 6.5 million are internally displaced. Idleb and Aleppo governorates have been disproportionately affected by the crisis, by intensifying Russian airstrikes and other conflict actors. As a result, there are now 1,960,000 IDPs in these governorates to address these needs, IYD proposes a twelve-month project to improve WASH, in this project Insani Yardım Dernegi (IYD), will rehabilitate water infrastructure in Idleb and Aleppo governorates, reaching48,000 beneficiaries. This will include training for local authorities to support ongoing operations and maintenance of the established facilities, as well as hygiene promotion events in schools and communities.
Total population in the targeted area is more than 4 million including 1,960,000 IDPs in both Idleb and Aleppo governorates – The clashes between the Government of Syria (GoS) and various organized armed groups (OAGs) throughout Northern Syria have created large waves of displacements adding to the hundreds of thousands who had been displaced by Russian airstrikes at the end of September 2015, the Attacks against hospitals, water networks, electricity plants and markets are common.
IYD conducted several needs assessments in the proposed area of operations in September 2018. WASH assessment was conducted. The major objective of this assessment was to ascertain the current WASH situation in terms of access to water Supply, solid waste management and hygiene promotion practices. The assessment also aimed to collect data on emerging WASH humanitarian assistance needs identified by the specialist teams in collaboration with local council and local people
We can summarize the activities that we planned in 2019 in WASH activities:
Activity 1.1.1 Rehabilitate water infrastructure within the Idleb governorate:
Three main water stations will be rehabilitated in the proposed locations the main work will be providing the required equipment such as generators sets, water pumps, and electrical and mechanical parts of the water stations in addition to rehabilitation the buildings of the water stations’ buildings
Activity 1.1.2 Conduct water testing and water quality management:
In each of the targeted villages IYD will trained local staff from the local people in how to test the water quality those staff will be part of the water units that IYD is planning to establish in each targeted villages the water technical staff will be responsible for physical, chemical and biological tests in the water stations
Activity 1.1.3 Operation and maintenance support to water infrastructure:
Through providing fuel, filters, engine oils and light rehabilitation after rehabilitation of the water stations and during the operation and maintenance period IYD will establish the water units from the local people
Activity 1.1.4 Rehabilitation of water services in schools:
In the targeted villages IYD will repair the WASH facilities in the schools such as the toilets and handwashing points and water storage tanks
Activity 1.1.5 Training of local authorities in water quality and tariff system for the long-term sustainability of water facilities

Needs

NeedsIYD conducted several needs assessments in the proposed area of operations in September 2018. WASH assessment was conducted. The major objective of this assessment was to ascertain the current WASH situation in terms of access to water Supply, solid waste management and hygiene promotion practices. The assessment also aimed to collect data on emerging WASH humanitarian assistance needs identified by the specialist teams in collaboration with local council and local people as a result of the assessment the following activities is needed: Rehabilitate water infrastructure within Aleepo Idleb governorate:Due to a lack of staffing capacity and financial resources, the local Water Authorities in the Bisakla and Rasha and Albab areas have been unable to support and maintain the water infrastructures within the areas. The proposed intervention aims to provide safe and clean water to a total of 48,000 people, dispersed among 8 villages, at a regular supply rate of 35 liters per person per day. The primary source of water for the population has been water trucking, creating a reliance on water trucking which remains problematic due to the financial constraints this creates. IYD completed a full assessment of the water system to gain a specific understanding of what the system needs. Outside the boreholes, submersible and horizontal pumps will be installed, which are required to pump the water to the surface and the required distances. Galvanized pipes and polyethylene pipes. Tools will be required to repair any cracks or leaks in the network. Polyethylene (PE) pipes will be used outside of the actual pump area, underground. Clamps, valves, etc. are used to piece together the PE pipes. water testing and water quality management: Water in these villages needs to be treated through the provision of chlorination equipment (including dosing pumps), provision of chlorine (liquid or powder) and the provision of testing kits (Delagua kits which test for free residual chlorine), pH (potential of Hydrogen), and bacterial content), to ensure the safe consumption by beneficiaries. Operation and maintenance support to water infrastructure: Operationalization and maintenance (O&M) of the water stations is a critical need. Once rehabilitated, through supporting the water stations with diesel and fuel for the generators to ensure they are able to function continuously, the provision of maintenance costs for any minor repairs, the provision of spare parts (including filters, seals, injectors oil and fuel engine pumps) on a monthly basis, Activity 1.1.4 Rehabilitation of water services in schools: During the Rapid WASH Assessment conducted in Idleb, an assessment of the schools was made in addition to the general community. As with the problems that face the communities, it was found that the below rehabilitations were necessary by school locations: 1- Sanitation Accessories: 2- Maintenance of buildings3- Rebuilding of bathrooms4- Maintenance of tanks5- Sanitation pipes: 6- Maintenance & Painting of walls7- Maintenance of building8- Rebuilding of bathrooms Training of local authorities in water quality and tariff system for the long-term sustainability of water facilities: It has been found that a critical need in those locations was to create water committees to establish a tariff system from which the payment of staff salaries and the purchase of spare parts and other general maintenance will be supplied. There are needs of training for the water committee in key skills such as bookkeeping, tariff management, establishment of policies and standard operating procedures to ensure they have the skills to manage the tariff system. Additional technical capacity building in testing and monitoring of water quality.

Implementation of activities

IYD’s response to the Syria crisis is led in Turkey and Syria by the Program Manager (PM) who is responsible for the operational and strategic management of the organization’s programmed in both countries. The Syria Project Officers are responsible for programs’ quality and provide support and technical advice for the implementation and management of the actions. The Quality and Program Development Coordinator leads IYD’s MEAL, CRM, program development and information management teams. The HR officer oversees the support functions of HR, logistics and administration. Finances are overseen by the Finance Manager (FM), assisted by a Finance Officers and Assistants. In total IYD has over 40 staff based in Turkey in various roles. In Syria, IYD has a team of over 200 staff who are based in the 2 Governorates in Aleppo and Idleb.IYD’s Implementation plan will be as the following steps:• Needs Assessment: WASH rapid needs assessment conducted via our MEAL and WASH team in coordination with the local councils and the community representatives in the proposed areas• IYD will hire the required staff to coordinate with local authorities and to communicate the project’s activities.• BOQ and Estimates: for the proposed activities rehabilitation of water station and other activities using standard tools/formats preparing detail estimates.• Conduct the procurement/Bidding Process as per IYD’s Procurement policy.• Review of agreements/Purchase order by project Coordinator or relevant staff and signing of agreements by supplier/IYD's Purchase order.• Site Layout/commencement of work: WASH Officer and WASH assistant to perform site layout for the supplier/contractor.• A sample of Preparation and approval: Supplier to submit the sample of all materials and get it approved by WASH Officer before any supplies to a site, WASH Officer to document the samples approval with a report describing the quality of material along with pictures for the record.• Conduct quality control/monitoring visits for the commodities.• Completion and Close-out: WASH Officer to check all the works completed at site and ensure compliance with standard and specifications in BOQ and Service Agreement, then prepare the handover letters in presence of the formed committee to the local authorities.

Protection Risk Analysis

IYD has a security and safety team that conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils and civil society activists, as well as by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released by the international community. IYD has a good reputation as a nonprofit organization and works side by side with the community to empower and encourage the community ownership of facilities. Accordingly. For emergency relief projects, when a security situation arises that threatens the safety of staff, staff are redirected to a nearby, safer location until the area is safe enough to conduct the aid or distribution project. For non-relief projects, an assessment of the security situation is conducted in order to determine the location of the project. While very few locations in Syria are completely safe, IYD takes time to advise its staff and partners of ways to mitigate against security threats – such as avoiding specific routes, avoiding certain groups, or selecting locations or building types that are lesser targets.IYD will coordinate together in the implementing process and both of the teams will work together to ensure the safety and security of the team in the field. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

the border between Turkey and Syria closes sometimes which might affect the delivery of some required items into the proposed locations especially the capital equipment such as water pumps and electric generator sets and to mitigate this access restriction, IYD has increased local procurement of project supplies inside Syria, recruitment of staff from inside Syria further access controls on sending supplies to the project area would make it extremely difficult to implement the project activities. Therefore, this proposal presumes some kind of continued access to the project area or the available of supplies and equipment in the local market. Sudden clashes or air attacks which probably happens during the project period. To mitigate these risks, IYD staff have received mine awareness training which will be repeated on a regular basis during the project period. IYD cross border security group provides a forum for IYD staff at different levels in the organization structure to analyse security risks and to update the security management plan. Security groups are used for tracking of staff movements within Syria and for dissemination of information about security incidents to all staff.This proposal presumes that the local authorities in the targeted area will continue to allow NGOs to implement projects according to the humanitarian principles without undue interference in the selection of beneficiaries and staff.IYD will not pay fees or taxes to armed parties to the conflict inside Syria or to ISIL or other sanctioned groups as per the Joint Operating Principles. Beneficiaries are made aware that goods and services under this program are coming from IYD rather than from the local authorities.IYD is managing risk and potential fraud by ensuring compliance with and adherence to IYD’s approved and documented policies and procedures in addition to these policies and procedures, IYD conducts regular internal audits of its country offices and every country is subject to an annual external audit. Particular attention is paid to recruitment of staff and identification of suppliers. Where another humanitarian organization is given as a reference for staff or suppliers, IYD will check with that organization at the highest possible level to check the performance of the staff or supplier before signing a contract.
