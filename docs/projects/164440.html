Project Summary

In order to mitigate and reduce protection risks and vulnerability for persons in Idlib and Aleppo governorates, Maram Foundation proposes the present year-long intervention to increase the provision of legal support and counseling and increased legal awareness among the affected population on how to access civil and HLP documentation. The project will take place in target areas of Idlib and Aleppo Governorates.
The aim of the project is to increase legal awareness for 4,800 individuals (1,700 men, 2,200 women, 350 boys, 550 girls), provide legal training to 320 individuals (160 men and 160 women, including service providers, humanitarian workers, and community members), and to provide individual legal counseling for 400 individuals (200 men, 160 women, 10 boys, and 30 girls) from affected populations.
MF will reach the target beneficiaries through assembling, training, and dispatching four (4) mobile legal teams and two (2) mobile legal clinics " Based on 2 community centers in those locations" to provide legal awareness and information sessions on CSD to conflict-affected populations and to conduct HLP and CSD training for humanitarian workers in Northwest Syria. this project will also include HLP and CSD training for humanitarian workers in Gaziantep, Turkey. The mobile teams will be comprised of paralegal staff to provide legal awareness and information sessions on CSD and HLP, and the legal mobile clinics will be staffed by experienced lawyers and judges to provide individual legal counseling on CSD and HLP. Teams will cross-refer cases to one another, as well as to existing local authorities (where available) based on identified needs. Thus, this project aims to support the goals of the Protection Cluster and HLP TWG and to improve the lives of displaced people and affected populations living inside and outside of camps.
The mobile teams will travel outwards from these two central locations in order to reach identified communities in need. Each central location will serve as the hub for two mobile teams and one mobile clinic team. Using existing Maram centers as hubs will support an estimated 80% of total office costs and resource requirements.
Maram Foundation has proposed the locations for the outreach teams and legal clinics based on the above up-to-date needs assessment, which is correlated with existing locations and networks of MF in Syria in order to reach broad catchment areas among the communities in most need for the legal protection interventions. Using existing centers and offices as hubs and launch points for the mobile teams, Maram will draw on its existing working relationships and networks with local authorities and local communities to recruit and hire local experts for the mobile teams and support staff, to ensure access and successful outreach and services to beneficiaries and to contribute to a well-coordinated response with NGOs and local actors.
To avoid any overlap or duplication, the targeted locations determined through the assessment will be continually coordinated with the Protection Cluster and HLP TWG based on the priority locations, as well as in close coordination with field staff and other NGOs and authorities working on the ground in Syria. This will be continually updated during the implementation phase.

4 - Needs

MF conducted a needs assessment using primary and secondary sources to evaluate the specific CSD and HLP needs and awareness among communities in the districts and sub-districts within the target areas: Idlib and Aleppo Governorates, NW Syria. The purpose of this assessment was to identify target locations and beneficiaries and used the following combined methodologies: 1. A thorough desk review of secondary sources2. Mapping existing service provision and partners providing legal service interventions3. Assessing the needs of the local communities, including IDPs and host communities.4. Identifying the most vulnerable communities and areas using combined vulnerability and needs assessment.5. Correlating mapping results with the locations of existing Maram Foundation offices and services The results of this assessment identified a significant need in the districts of Harim (Dana sub-district; Qah, Sarmada, Dana, Aqrabat, Atma) in Idlib Governorate and in Azaz (Azaz sub-district; Azaz, Bab AlSalameh, Jarez, Kafr Kalbein, Kaljibrin, Krum, Salama, and others) in Aleppo Governorate.The impact of the Syrian crisis on individuals and communities has been severe. One of the least visible, yet far-reaching, impacts of the crisis has been on people’s inability to access civil status documentation, such as birth certificates, marriage certificates, identity documents, and others. Lack of documentation as left hundreds of thousands of Syrians without valid documentation. Possessing these documents gives people the right to move, to work, to travel, to own property, to access education, health, and other benefits and basic services. When documents were lost, destroyed, or unable to be obtained, individuals’ lives are severely impacted.
