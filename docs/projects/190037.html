Project Summary

The project will focus on two components, food security and livelihoods. In the Food security componente the project will consist of the distribution of food for 6 months, pending the harvest season. In the Livelihoods component the project will consist of the acquisition and distribution of seeds and agricultural equipment. The total number of beneficiaries for both components of the project,t will be 3000 people, resettled in the districts.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Assuming that the project will serve two components, food security and livelihoods, in one districts of Cabo Delgado, it aims to serve 3000 peoplein both components in the district of Ancuabeequivalent to a total of 3,000 people, which means that all families will benefit from food assistance, on the one hand, while they wait for the harvest season, on the other side. The food security will consist of he distribution of food kits (25kg of corn flour, 25 kg of rice, 4 liters of oil, 7kg of beans and 2kg of salt). As a way of curbing food addiction from humanitarian organizations, the project will create conditions for agricultural practice for all resettled displaced people, consisting of the purchase of agricultural inputs and distribution to all displaced people, allowing them to produce within 4 months and harvest the food. The livelihoods component will consist of purchase and distribution of seeds (corn 10 kg, beans nhemba 5 kg, and agricultural instruments (1 machete, 2 hoes and 1 ax).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The first activity in making this project feasible is to contact the government and local authorities to get lists of IPDs in each district. The activities will take place during the period of six months that coincides with the agricultural season, to allow families that receive the seeds to wait for the harvest. The roll of activities to be cared out are as the follow:Physical verification of the lists of beneficiaries to be provided by the government in all districts involved - this activity will consist of the validation of the lists by the Caritas’ technicians to certify the eligibility of the beneficiaries.Procurement process for food and agricultural inputs in the local market or in Nampula - This activity will consist of seeking quotations and proceeding with the acquisition and purchase process.Reception and preparation of kits for distribution - after receiving the inputs, these will be prepared in kits (placed in a bag, to facilitate the handling process). The food will be distributed according to the quantities that Caritas has been distributing: 25 kg of corn flour, 25 kg of rice, 2 kg of salt, 7 kg of beans and 4 liters of cooking oil.Distribution of kits to beneficiaries. The kits will be transported in rented trucks to the distribution sites that will be cared out following the list previously prepared for this purpose. It should be noted that the agricultural inputs will be distributed only once, while food will be distributed 2 times (monthly) Post-distribution monitoring - Ensure that there was no deviation in the application of the seed as well as assess the level of satisfaction of the beneficiaries. Reports. Two financial and descriptive reports will be presented in the middle and at the end of the project.All these activities will be taken in Ancuabe distrit.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The identification of risks will be done through involvement and dialogue with local leaders,it is known that the target group belongs to several ethnic groups. To seek to know, togetherwith the leaders, what are the main priorities and immediate needs for the beneficiaries. Alltarget groups will be included to avoid possible confusion between them

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Caritas as the humanitarian arm of the church, serves all the needy without anydiscrimination. If there are people qualified to support the distribution, Caritasinvolves them in their work and in return, they gain experience in the work ofassisting the needy, which they can use in the future.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be one of the priorities during the distribution, reasonwhy within the community consultation, the field officers will be trained tounderstand the modality of targeting for the most vulnerable beneficiaries such aspeople with disabilities with leadership character and ability to do some sort of work,they will be included in the team. Distribution points will be conveniently locatedensuring ease of access by community members particularly people with disabilitiesand women.

Some sessions of individual and collective hygiene willbe carried out by the activists and good use of latrines, water sources.
