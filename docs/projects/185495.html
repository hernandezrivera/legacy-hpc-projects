Project Summary

COHESODEC will in this emergency response be scaling up education response in vulnerable communities to IDPs children as well as children of host communities. COHESODEC has since 2017 been ensuring that children study in 45 Learning Centers across Mezam Division of the North-West Region. The relevance of this project cannot be overemphasized as it will enable COHESODEC scale up education in the North-West Region to combat illiteracy and promote the fundamental right to education which has been violated following the on-going conflict in these affected Regions of Cameroon.
Almost a million children since 2016 have been out of school owing to the vulnerabilities of this conflict. Given that COHESODEC has already negotiated humanitarian access with both state & Non-State Armed Groups, it is imperative through this project ensure that the current low rate of children studying in conducive environments without being targeted by the warring parties.
This project seeks to target 74,450 out of school children between the ages of 3 to 24 to ensure these children have access to quality education in the midst of the conflict in the following communities: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda 1, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Santa, Tubah, Batibo & Mbengwi subdivisions of the North-West region of Cameroon in 2022.
In addressing these educational needs through this project, COHESODEC will be constructing 8 classrooms in some of the above-mentioned subdivisions to accommodate vulnerable out of school children. Also, in a bit to reduce the level of illiteracy & enhance knowledge among out of school IDP children and host communities, COHESODEC will be putting in place Radio Education Programs & digital learning platforms to enable girls, boys & adolescents affected by the crises able to attend non formal education thereby enabling them have access to alternative education.
COHESODEC will equally carry out advocacy & social dialogue with stakeholders in the education sector i.e (communities, teachers, administrative & school officials, Partners and arm groups) to foster education in preprimary, primary, secondary, professional or vocational training in order to allow all children have access to education including girls, boys, adolescents & children living with disabilities.
Moreover, COHESODEC will be carrying out capacity building sessions with teachers, staff & school administrators to enhance their capacities in Inclusive Education so as to ensure our learning spaces are gender, age, race & ethnically inclusive & child friendly.
In addressing the severe problems of lack of school materials/needs such as bags, books, pens, pencils, etc.), COHESODEC shall purchase & distribute adequate learning materials to boys, girls, adolescents & children living with disabilities to ensure better learning while in school or learning spaces as well as at home. These learning materials will comprise of a variety of different learning items at different levels & according to the learning needs of the children.
COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will equally be providing grants to schools & training institutions & the most vulnerable families & children, training of teachers, supervisors & other Education staff in life skills, psychosocial support & other topics related to education in emergencies, training teachers/facilitators & supervisors in Psychosocial Support, Conflict & disaster risk management as well as Child Protection to ensure girls, boys & adolescents affected by the crisis do feel safe in schools, learning & training spaces.
Finally, in implementing this project, COHESODEC will ensure the training of school managers in the minimum standards of Protection & Education in Emergencies, Coordination & Information Management while also addressing educational needs of the vulnerable communities-(students, parents, teachers/facilitators, supervisors & other education staff by so doing creating awareness & sensitization on COVID-19 Preventive measures.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Since the outbreak of the crisis in the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon, Education has been one of the most affected sectors by the conflict resulting to a lot of displacements and causing many children to stay out of school. It is in light that it is imperative to address this critical educational need. COHESODEC will be ensuring the construction of 8 classrooms in the affected communities to address these needs as well as the construction of 20 latrines in educational facilities to curb the deteriorating WASH needs in some educational facilities that lack these WASH facilities. COHESODEC will equally be running a Radio Education Programs & digital learning in the midst of this crisis to ensure that 6,000 children benefit from distance learning in targeted communities in the earmarked subdivisions of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. COHESODEC in ensuring that children attend school/learning space freely and safely, will carry out advocacy and social dialogue involving 20,000 stakeholders in the education system (communities, teachers, administrative and school officials, partners and arm actors) to foster the access of all children to education, including girls and children living with disabilities. Also, COHESODEC will carry out capacity building sessions involving 150 teacher/facilitators and supervisors in Inclusive Education, Psychosocial Support, Conflict & disaster risk management & Child Protection to ensure a great number of children feel safe while learning. COHESODEC will equally provide learning materials to 300 children living with disabilities and 5,000 boys, girls and adolescent children to ensure a smooth learning process and build confidence in the learners as well as ensure they feel safe and dignified. The organization through this project will also be providing grants to schools & training institutions & the most vulnerable families & children, training of teachers & supervisors in Psychosocial Support, Conflict & disaster risk management & Child Protection and will equally training 150 school managers in the minimal standards for Protection & Education in Emergencies, Coordination & Information Management to ensure the children are protected while at the learning spaces. Finally, COHESODEC will carry out awareness raising and sensitization campaigns in vulnerable communities of the targeted subdivisions on COVID-19 prevention measures to end the pandemic.
