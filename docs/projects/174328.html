Project Summary

This project takes into consideration the gender dimensions projected by the conflict in Northern Nigeria. The protracted nature of the conflict continues to reinforce pre-existing gender inequalities (with women having lesser opportunities) across the (Borno, Adamawa, Yobe) BAY states as it continues to record worrying rates of gendered violence, especially sexual violence. Besides, COVID 19 and the resultant lockdown served to increase GBV risks for women and girls as women found themselves locked up with perpetrators and had less access to essential GBV lifesaving services. While the conflict drags on and the consequences of COVID emerging there is need for quality lifesaving GBV services, MHPSS, adolescent and girls reproductive health need and gender transformational models to address risk mitigation and prevention of GBV.
The result framework responds to the immediate and long-term consequence of GBV on women and girls as a result of the current crisis by providing crucial GBV and mental health services. Presently women's risks continue to increase as funds decrease as humanitarian actors are grappling with a notable decrease in funding in the wake of the pandemic notwithstanding the war that has dragged on. This project is a scale-up of NCA's existing GBV operations which provides lifesaving one on one case management services and remote toll-free telephone GBV services for vulnerable populations in two LGAs of Borno State ( Mun'guno and Pulka). Whilst building the capacity of partners to offer quality timely GBV services in Pulka where NCA is the GBV field level sector lead.The main objective of this intervention is to enhance timely access to critical lifesaving GBV services for vulnerable individuals in Pulka and Munguno and referrals to lifesaving health services, legal and other necessary referrals that aid in recovery and empowerment of GBV survivors.
NCA will seek to improve the well being of adolescents affected by war through psychosocial care besides psychological therapies for adolescents with PTSD to aid in healing and recovery. To this end NCA will ensure timely mental health care and psychosocial support is functional, available and acceptable to women, men, boys and girls in host and IDP populations in NE Nigeria. NCA will ensure providers deliver quality clinical care, mental health and Psychosocial support services to GBV survivors. This will be achieved: Training staff on MHPSS, support in updating the referral pathways, set up supportive community structures to address the stigma around those accessing MHPSS services, train teachers in school to support in linking pupils to service providers.
NCA will seek to continue integration of ASRH and GBV services by ensuring women and girls of reproductive age access acceptable, affordable, equitable, and age-appropriate quality reproductive health services—provision of safe delivery kits for pregnant adolescent girls to support safe delivery Adolescent girls programming will Provide one-on-one phone-based and mini-group information sessions on sexuality education using my safety, my wellbeing" curriculum and modern family planning.To reduce acceptance and prevalence of violence against women and girls, NCA will conduct outreach and prevention activities via awareness-raising events; ,regular listening sessions; and community-led activism and dialogue sessions with community leaders. NCA will conduct safety audits to document women and girls' voices and the risks they face to inform advocacy with community leaders and other stakeholders.o address social norms that reinforce VAWG and to challenge the profound normalization of violence that hinders women and girls to seek services or speak out against violence,NCA will introduce the male the module offers a model for working constructively with with men to examine the gendered impact of conflict and how they have been and how they have been socialized,which are crucial steps in creating a violence free world

1 - Needs

Response services in both host and IDP community continues to remain inadequate amidst high GBV prevalence rates. Often actors lack technical expertise in case management- a service audit revealed 66% of case workers had not received training on caring for child survivors of sexual abuse, while a majority had not been trained on responding to IPV. There is a growing number of sexual violence presenting after 72 hr according to 2020 mid year report many rape cases remain under reported due to an interplay social factor like stigma, shortage of service points and bottle necks of accessing justice.In addition, GBV incidences also continue to be under reported due to fear of and actual abduction, harassment, and sexual violence keeping women and girls from moving around freely; further limiting access to service information, social support networks, and safety alternatives.The protracted conflict and COVID19 pandemic have rendered most people unable to perform normal functions and in need of MHPSS .Despite the growing need for these services,very few organizations are currently offering MHPSS.Most service providers offering sexual reproductive health did not have adolescent friendly services negatively affecting uptake of service, despite the criticality of reproductive health services especially for the adolescents.Further still, there were low levels of referrals with very few partners mentioning a satisfactory collaboration with non GBV actors. Lack of knowledge about GBV, the existing GBV referral pathways weak coordination among multi sector actors escalate the challenge. NCA will be seeking to address the above challenges that would hamper the recovery of GBV survivors as well as interfere with the development of children as a result of undergoing child sexual abuse through capacity building partners in responding to child survivors off sexual abuse, responding to intimate partner violence. It is worth noting that physical assault was the most reported form of GBV in the past 3 quarters as per the GBVIMS hence a critical need to enhance service provider capacities in response, while CMR is an immediate need for survivors of rape, timely access to the service is greatly hampered by inadequate service points and where the providers are present most of them are ill equipped to offer quality services, hence the need for capacity building on clinical care for sexual assault survivors.NCA will also seek to build the capacity of partners on adolescent friendly service provision to encourage uptake of age appropriate ASRH services.GBV referrals is an integral part of effective multi-sector approach to response of GBV services yet confidentiality is not guaranteed while some actors have not been able to keep the referral pathway vibrant, while non GBV actors have been shy to use the referral systemTo reduce acceptance and prevalence of violence against women and girls, NCA will conduct outreach and prevention activities via awareness-raising events; regular listening sessions; and community-led activism and dialogue sessions with community leaders. NCA will conduct safety audits to document women and girls' voices and the risks they face to inform advocacy with community leaders and other stakeholders.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

All activities will have protection mainstreaming lens, promoting safety, dignity, meaningful access, participation and empowerment of beneficiaries.8.6 Protection considerations underpin all our interventions in all stages of the project cycle. All activities will have protection mainstreaming lens, promoting safety, dignity, meaningful access, participation and empowerment of beneficiaries (women and girls, men and boys).Our project will feminist informed aiming to achieve gender equality hence reduce violence through engaging men, boys, the youth and older men in gender transformational programs.Ensuring our services are survivor centred by placing clients at the centre of our interventions. A survivor-centred approach aims to create a supportive environment in which the survivor’s right are respected, and the person is treated with dignity and respect. We prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm through our programs for instance risk mitigation measures like joint safety audits are meant to identify and address safety concerns for women and girls, the older women and women and girls living with a disability.A recently concluded safety audit recommended providing gender-segregated WaSH facilities would minimize GBV risks for women and girls. Meeting menstrual hygiene needs would increase women and girls visibility in the public spaces and raise their self-esteem.Increased access to water would lessen distance covered by women and girls cover in search of water, nor will they resort to negative coping mechanisms to access water.We will encourage meaningful access through consultations with the community before and during implementation on what best suits them in terms of service delivery. Additionally, our services will be culturally appropriate.NCA aims to enhance accessibility by having service points in locations considered safe by the community, have services closer to the community and ensure service provision is within time frames appropriate for women and girls. Women and Girls safe spaces will have child-friendly corners to allow women to come with their children as the majority of the women cannot afford childminders bringing along their children would enable them access services offered. Age gender and diversity is prioritized in all stages of the project we maintain accountability to all our clients through quality service provisions that consider age gender and diversity; we are accountable to our beneficiaries and participation and empowerment. NCA will aim to mainstream disability in the project to achieve inclusion and participation. NCA will accomplish this by designing disability friendly services, buildings and raising awareness and changing attitudes.NCA will conduct Consultation of different groups (women, men, the youth, the elderly, people living with disability) will be consulted during the assessment, project kick-off, during implementation and planning on exit strategies.NCA will tailor the project activities to address concerns of different groups; for instance, consulting girls and boys in designing adolescent-friendly ASRH services, mainstreaming disability, thereby addressing their concerns.NCA will design feedback sessions to incorporate all groups in the community and timely feedback shared.Community engagement mechanism will include engagement with field-level leadership. NCA will provide Mobile services to address issues of mobility specifically for the elderly and those living with disability. Additionally, NCA will engage in contextualizing and translating trainings on PSEA, trafficking, and GBV core concepts in significant languages spoken in the field, mode of delivery will be interactive and culturally appropriate. These critical trainings will aim to include everybody in the community (women, girls, men and boys)NCA will relay on the voices of women and girls guide a feminist informed project while relaying women voices to men and boys to enhance accountability

9.1a - Specify (and give URL link if possible to) the relevant needs assessment document.

Safety audits conducted and rapid gender analysis conducted in NE Nigeria, the HRP and GBVSS 2020 mid-year report and GBVIMS statistics provided inputs on gender needs. The assessments deduced the following;Although there are some reasonable efforts towards protection of civilians by the army in some parts of North East Nigeria, the gendered perspective on safety and protection has an alternative dimension in this context. Nigerian men, boys, women, and girls possess varying amounts of power within their community, which are deeply rooted in conservative social and cultural norms and fundamental religious believes. Nigerian men are the main decision makers in the family, recognized as household heads who have the final say, empowered to adjudicate all matters affecting family members. Women, on the other hand, are responsible for the household, children, and domestic work, traditionally subservient to the authority of their husbands and often their in-laws. Women are hamstrung and debilitated by deficiencies imposed upon them by this strictly patriarchal society. Menstrual health management needs are unmet, discussing such needs is taboo, and meeting those needs is not a priority for male heads of households even when they are made aware. Women have insufficient access to water, lack areas to dry menstrual hygiene items, and lack those menstrual hygiene items themselves. Other prevailing practices placing an undue burden on NE Nigerian women include the expectations that, as soon as menstruation begins, girls should stay indoors, cease their education, and stop interacting with males. Girls are also eligible for marriage once they reach puberty.Women experience barriers to movement and access to and control over resources; girls’ access and freedom of movement are restricted once they reach puberty. https://reliefweb.int/sites/reliefweb.int/files/resources/NE-Nigeriahttps://docs.google.com/document/d/1cDLYScGvCL0VDit3VJtp4juRz_ooyTxJ/edit

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Feedback will be collected in the women safe spaces by engaging women and girls through listening in sessions, FGD’s and discussions session will also enable women and girls give and receive feedbackSafety audits will also be used as a tool for collecting feedback from the community through FGD’s conducted.Clients satisfaction survey will be used to collect feedback on client’s satisfaction with services given.Adolescent girls curriculum my safety my well will also be used to collect feed back from girls and their care givers.NCA operates a feedback and accountability mechanism supervised by a dedicated officer, operating a dedicated phone line and email address which is disseminated to all affected populations. Suggestion boxes are also put in strategic locations which are also manned by a dedicated CRM officer. A Quality and Improvement Committee (QIC) will be constituted and regular meetings will be held where issues of concerns and areas of needing programmatic improvement will be presented to a team of NCA staff. The QIC will be constituted by a cross section of community members. Decisions to solve issues raised will be taken and implemented immediately. With issues that requires higher level decisions, they will be referred to the senior management team at NCA who will endeavour to provide timely solution to the issue. Regular training will be conducted for local partners as well as beneficiaries and key stakeholders on our CRM procedures, phone lines, suggestion boxes and email address. Complaints and suggestions are addressed in a timely manner with results relayed back to beneficiaries.
