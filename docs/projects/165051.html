Project Summary

Syria in the midst of ongoing crisis is faced with serious social issues. The culture of violence, social disintegration, disturbance of cohesion and lack of coexistence challenges stability of the country and expose Syrian people to challenges. Abuse of women rights and Gender Based violence has been cause of serious harm to the Syrian women. The armed conflict has added to the vulnerabilities of general public and posed serious challenges to their protection. On the other hand, lack of awareness about their rights and limited capacity of the duty bearers have further complicated the situation leaving the society absolutely vulnerable.
Given the current Syrian context, there is need for humanitarian response as part of the protection cluster. The vulnerable people, women faced with GBV and communities/individuals experiencing disputes are in need of legal awareness, counselling and guidance in order to avail the formal justice system. The awareness raising will ensure that the communities look for resolving issues through formal justice mechanisms instead of looking for vigilante justice which can proliferate tensions in the country. Similarly, women faced with domestic and gender-based violence are in need of support vis a vis protection and safety.
UNDP Syria Country Office envisages to implement this project in order to provide legal awareness, counselling and guidance to the people in need of support. Under the protection sector, UNDP will be implementing interventions as part of objective No. 1, Activities No. 1 and 2 and objective No. 3, activity No. 2. The project will be focused on raising awareness of the beneficiaries in five different governorates of Syria that are in need of humanitarian assistance.
The project will be implemented as per UNDP project implementation modality. The UNDP country office will be responsible for design and technical oversight of the project activities. The area offices of UNDP through a project team will implement the project. UNDP country office operation teams will provide operational support to the area office. The project will be monitored at three levels. The first level will be that of the area office and project team level ensuring that the project is implemented as per the project proposal. The second level of monitoring and evaluation will be conducted by the UNDP country office programme team. As a third layer, a third part monitoring team, already recruited by UNDP, will undertake independent monitoring of the project activities.

4 - Needs

The Syrian crisis resulted in social issues such as a culture of violence, proliferation of arms, drug use among youth and community level tensions. Mass displacements has caused community level disputes with a potential to escalate to violent conflict. Women are facing psychological pressures in the shape of domestic violence. families lost its male members putting the responsibility of economic sustenance on women with minimum conducive environment. Women lack most civil documentation. UNDP conducted a context analysis of justice sector in 2018 which found that problems included criminal public disorder; civil documentation; sexual and GBV; and HLP issues. A community needs assessment was conducted in 2019 across 10 governorates showing lack of awareness about state laws, their rights and judicial procedures as causes of social tensions, disputes and violence. This hindered capacity to reach justice service providers and seek solution of issues through formal justice system. This confirmed UNDP’s Justice Sector Context Analysis which valued lack of legal awareness as the highest road bump in people’s access to justice. It was also causing domestic violence and abuse of women rights. Syria was ranked third amongst the world’s 10 topmost dangerous countries and second on non-sexual violence against women. UNDP’s Preliminary Examination of Local Drivers and Capacities for Sustaining Peace in Syria, produced in June 2019, has highlighted justice as one of the drivers of peace and stability in the country. Hence, legal awareness raising is a priority issue for consideration of the UN agencies’ humanitarian response in Syria. UNDP proposes to undertake a comprehensive legal awareness initiative to facilitate access to justice, eliminate GBV and assure promotion of respect of human rights. The intervention focuses on organization of awareness raising workshops, community outreach through volunteer youth, offering legal counselling and attending to women legal needs

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Security of Staff: Given the current unpredictability of security situation in Syria, the project staff will be exposed to security threats at all points of project implementation. The project team will have to strictly follow UNDP security guidelines at all times to minimise probability of the security threat. Women victims of GBV: The women victims of GBV seeking legal assistance and pursuing formal justice process for security of their rights could be at risk of facing retaliation from the perpetrators. Survival of GBV: Since the project is providing legal awareness and consultation, The survival of GBV seeking the aid for security of their rights could be at risk of facing stereotype and retaliation from the perpetrators. UNDP in collaboration with gender will be addressing these risks by: -- Maintain high level of confidentiality for all cases seeking the consultation. - Maintaining easy and unexposed access to the consultation center by survivals; strict criteria will be applied. - The service providers (of awareness and legal consultation) will be trained on basic GBV as well as impact of implemented activities on power-dynamics. - considering the sensitivity of the topics, Gender and GBV topics are part of other type of awareness session. All project activities are considering required government approvals are received. Inter/Intracommunity conﬂict: Some of the communities/groups/individuals are already in a state of dispute. The beneﬁciaries receiving legal awareness and as a result approaching state legal system for dealing with issues could be at risk of facing retaliation from the opposite parties.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Government Support: It is assumed that the authorities at the local level will support implementation of the project activities as per the plan. In order for the assumption to materialise, UNDP will closely coordinate project activities with the local authorities, and they will be kept informed about the project activities. Security: The security situation remains unpredictable in Syria and some of the project target areas have experienced instable security situation in the recent past. The risk of security deterioration expectedly remains true and may challenge implementation of the project activities. In order to mitigate the risk, UNDP will constantly undertake security risk analysis and take timely actions vis a vis security situation in order to ensure timely delivery of the project outputs with least exposure of the project team and beneficiaries to security threats. Project Staff deployment: UNDP has experienced constant staff turnover in the projects. The staff turnover is a medium level risk that could disturb smooth implementation of the project. UNDP will try to ensure staff retention and in the case of staff turnover, will take timely actions to avoid any gaps. Support of Communities: It is assumed that the community representatives will support implementation of the project activities. Support of community representatives remain important for mobilisation of communities and insurance of participation of the local communities in the project activities.
