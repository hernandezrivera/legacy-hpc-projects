Project Summary

Two successive rain seasons, that is Deyr rains (October- November) 2018 and Gu rains (April-May) 2019 have registered below average amounts (FSNAU Port Gu report 2019). Leading to water deficit and poor pasture conditions (FSNAU Technical Release September 2, 2019) for pastoralist livelihood groups in Central Somalia. This has resulted in Galgadud Region being among areas classified as being in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) while other Mudug Region’s rural region areas falls in the category of areas classified as being in Stressed. Central Somalia has yet to recover from the significant livestock loss occurred during the 2016/2017 drought, with the many drop- out pastoralists identified as being reliant on labor as an alternative income source after losing their animals as CPD Market Assessments for Hobyo, Adado and Dhusamareb undertaken with the support of Diakonie Germany (for Hobyo District) and Somalia Stability Fund project for Adado and Dhusamareb Districts Indicate. The availability of saleable animals’ remains among the local pastoralists is low, constraining the ability of poor households to feed their families and purchase water for their animals. The pastoralist populations in Galmudug Regions (i.e. Galgadud and Mudug Regions) shall require livestock related interventions to enable them access water, pasture as well as veterinary care critical for the survival of theirs herds. CPD proposes the provision of integrated interventions to support the pastoralists with supplementary feeding, basic veterinary care and water trucking for animals, alongside collapsible tanks to hold and supply water in strategic locations.
In Lower Shabelle Region, the successive rain seasons failures have led to a below average harvest being realized for the Gu 2019 season crop (50 percent below the long-term average according to June 2019 Monthly Rainfall and NDVI Report). The region has also faced intensified conflict as the Federal Government forces and AMISOM have intensified their war against the Non-state actors in control of many of the Districts and locations in Lower Shabelle Region. The combined effects of poor harvests, increased food prices, low cereal to animal exchange rates will increase food access difficulties and also subsequent capacity of the farms to produce as they have reduced farm incomes. Women and children have borne the brunt of the noted food access challenges with localized high malnutrition rates being observed within the IDP camps. There is also a window of opportunity created in the newly liberated areas in Lower Shabelle Region especially among the agro-pastoral and riverine livelihood groups which require support to rehabilitate their farms, farming inputs and trainings and also the rehabilitation of irrigation canals damaged through many years of deliberate destruction and also lack of maintenance.
IDP Populations in Mogadishu, Adado (in Galmudug Region) and Galkayo (in Mudug Region) are among those indicated as being in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Stressed (IPC Phase 2). The IDPs will need lifesaving unconditional cash transfer, skills training and start-up capital for alternative livelihood means especially for women from women headed households. The cash transfer will support the women and youth from resorting to unsustainable and vulnerable survival livelihood means. CPD through information generated by Vulnerability Assessment Survey in Mogadishu’s Dayniile and Dharkeynley Districts in January 2019 indicated that 87% of the interviewee IDP households have 1-3 children under 5 years in the family and 52% of the interviewed households have 1-2 over 60 years old members in their families. At the same time though many of the IDPs rely on waged labour for their livelihood (57% of HHs), the incomes from waged labor at an average of US $50 per months was below the recommended Minimum Expenditure Basket for Banadir which is currently at US $93 (MEB for urban markets September 2019).

Project Activities

1. Undertake gender informed vulnerability survey to identify the most vulnerable IDPs and Host communities IPC 3 (stress) and IPC 4 (emergency) and their needs in the target regions (7 target Districts) and develop a vulnerability criteria to aid in their selection for targeted effective support.2. Unconditional cash transfer (UCT) targeting 3,000 IDPs and identified drop-out pastoralists’ households in Mogadishu and Galmudug (Dhusamareb, Adado and Hobyo Districts). 3. Conditional Cash Transfer for early action communal projects rehabilitation.4. The project will utilize conditional transfers for 660 laborers, prioritizing women to undertake rehabilitation of 20 kms of irrigation canals (primary and secondary) that will involve removing accumulated silt in Afgooye Districts. 5. Carry out restocking of goats, sheep and pack animals for 660 most vulnerable agro pastoralists and pastoralists in Galmudug State regions (Dhusamareb, Adado and Hobyo Districts) with special focus to women headed households (7 shoats per HH and 1 Pack Animal per HH).6. The project will support the rehabilitation of 13 strategic water points used by livestock, specifically waterpans in Adado and Dhusamareb (Galgadud Region) and Hobyo District (Mudug Region) through CFW targeting 645 labourers of skilled/unskilled with women being prioritized.7. The project will also target the tractor land preparation support for 275 River Shabelle riverine farmers whose farmland land lies abandoned in Afgooye Districts. The farmers will be supported with 12 Kgs of certified seeds per farmer.8. The project will also support training on modern crop husbandry to help increase production of crops. 9. Fodder cultivation support for 275 riverine farmers and agro pastoralists in Lower Shabelle Region (Afgooye) and Galmudug State regions (Dhusamareb, Adado and Hobyo Districts).10. The project will support the adoption of modern Hermetic Storage Technology (HST) to 275 riverine farmers to cut down on post-harvest loses in Lower Shabelle region (Afgooye Districts). 11. This will be through training and provision of bio pesticides to control armyworm and other crop pests to eliminate them from farmer’s fields. 12. Support vocational training 360 IDPs in Mogadishu’s Districts of Dharkeynley, Daynile and Kaxda) and Galmudug State regions (Dhusamareb, Adado and Hobyo Districts), prioritizing women to help them gain an alternative livelihood means while also supporting their communities. 13. The project will facilitate the formation of gendered Community Resource Committees (CRCs) in all the areas the project targets take up proactive roles in the management of communally owned resources. 14. The CRCs formed in Lower Shabelle (Afgooye Districts) and Galmudug (Dhusamareb, Adado and Hobyo Districts) will be trained in community based preparedness with women being prioritized in the training.

Please provide further explanation

The projects proposed are in line with National Development Plan and also Economic Pillar for Somalia. These projects are going to involve the community together with their leaders and the local authorities. The rehabilitation of water structures and training and support of beneficiaries on farming will ensure that food security is increase for long term gains. The beneficiaries will also be trained and supported on alternative livelihoods that will ensure they have food on the table even during droughts. Committees elected to manage the water structures will be given the mandate to run the assets on behalf of the community after training through a proper hand-over of the assets. The committees will be registered with government/local authority agencies to give them legal basis to manage the assets on behalf of the communities.
