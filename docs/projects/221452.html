Project Summary

The on-going “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the North West/South West (NWSW) Regions of Cameroon which break out since 2016 incorporate with recent outbreak of Cholera in South West Region has resulted in increased displacements of people/deaths and children are not going to schools. 21% of the children the HHs in the NW do not have access to education. Ngo-Ketunjia has the highest percent of children (7%) who had access to distance education and least being Boyo. from OCHA SITREP of 19 June 2024 ) 2.3M among the 3.4M PiN of humanitarian assistance in Education.
OCHA sitrep_nwsw_august_and_september_2022_final data: 2.0 M People in need of assistance; 1.4 M Targeted for assistance; 600,336 IDP; 419,734 Returnees (former IDPs and refugees) ; 85,967 Cameroonian refugees in Nigeria. Sources: Humanitarian (Needs Overview/Response Plan) 2022; MSNA NWSW, OCHA, August 2022; MSNA West, Littoral, OCHA, September 2021 & UNHCR, September 2022.
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 2 Divisions in rural/semi-urban and in hard-to-reach areas Ngo-Ketunjia and Boyo in the North West Region of Cameroon. Targeted population: 5,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (856 Boys/868 Girls) <18 yrs, (851 Men/862 Women) > 18 yrs, (450 Men/500 Women) +60yrs, PWD (300 Men/313 Women)). Targeting 30 Schools
AIM:
To improve access to learning environment with basic sanitation facilities, hygiene related services and learning material to vulnerable IDPs by 30th June 2025.
ACTIVITIES:
-Construction of school and training infrastructure (classrooms, latrines, water points, temporary learning spaces
-Purchase & distribution of learning materials (bags, books, pens, pencils, etc.)
STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES:
-Schools/education services serve as a platform for accessing basic Water, Sanitation, hygiene/health promotion for crisis affected children/communities.
-Vulnerable children/teachers in schools/communities access basic teaching/learning materials for improved quality EIE by 30th June 2025.
-Reduce mortality by 75% /morbidity by 75% of people affected by crisis.
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people & PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability & Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 60% IDPs, 10% PWDs, 30% host communities.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitizations.
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through community-based approach, awareness raising campaign (school-to-school/door-to-door sensitization), radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials, collaboration with stakeholders, community’s mobilization, engagement & inclusively school children. Open space distribution, organized workshops, M&E. Extension of water from communities to schools’ latrines constructed and stand taps in schools. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers, local management/ownership. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs, key formant interviews, field visit, report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Engage MINEDU, MINEE & Municipal Council.
SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained (CPCs for continuos sensitisaton on hygiene promotion; schools administration on how to treat/reuse squatting slabs). Installation of handwashing facilities & preventing COVID-19 with medical needs for wider population. Improve sustainable latrines and ensuring education continuos with learning materials.
JUSTIFICATION:
Latrines reduce GBV cases that are latrines related. Poor & vulnerable groups of children are not assisted with learning materials/schools’ fees.
