Project Summary

The Logistics Sector 2022 is an internal coordination platform with UN Agencies and NGOs, in order to collect, compile, analyze and disseminate information on supply chain procedures to be followed for shipping humanitarian cargo to Gaza with detailed information and documentations required for transporting aid contributions to Gaza.
The Sector is establishing a web information page to support operational decision making and to improve the efficiency of the Gaza logistics response; and also a digital data platform collection containing the information related to all the logistics partners involved in the Gaza response, for the benefit of the entire humanitarian community

1 - Needs

The Palestine Logistics Sector was established just after the May 2021 escalation, because the delivery of humanitarian relief and humanitarian cargo in the Gaza strip were facing several challenges due to restricted access. Kerem Shalom crossing point has been the main entry for cargo movements in Gaza. Movement of cargo have been limited to only certain type of items like food, NFIs and drugs & pharmaceutical products; and from August 2021 there was also a significant increase of construction material allowed to cross in Gaza. Many other items like spare parts for WASH infrastructure, rehabilitation, solar energy systems, education equipment and tools, maintenance spare parts, health equipment, generators and technical vehicles remain restricted and require sometimes lengthy coordination processes that are not adequate to respond to the emergency needs in Gaza. The Logistics Sector was locally established to scale up and augment the existing logistics capacity, to provide coordination and information management to the partners involved in the Gaza rehabilitation projects, and to overcome potential logistics bottlenecks related to the cargo importation mechanisms. The Logistics Sector approach was established to provide a single coordination structure to the different humanitarian actors (UN Agencies, INGOs, NGOs) that have humanitarian, recovery, and development projects in the Gaza strip; and to enhance predictability and efficiency of the humanitarian response under the logistics coordinate approach

2 - Activities

This project will support the HRP coordination strategic objectives through the following:SO 1: Access support provided to humanitarian staff and programmes 1) Guidance and Procedures – Provide overall supply chain guidance on procedures to be followed for shipping humanitarian cargo in Gaza and helps partners in navigating through the different importation mechanisms: three of these are UN established mechanisms: UNRWA, ACMSP and GRM. While the remaining two: Bilateral agency coordination and Direct coordination by private sector; are both used by NGOs and UN Agencies 2) NGO support – Support I/NNGOs who are the most affected by the border closure for a harmonized and informed process through: •facilitating engagement with UNOPS on the GRM mechanism • support engagement and dealing with relevant authorities • assessing & evaluating the possibility of renting a warehouse (managed by a UN Agency) in order to release the partners’ shipments goods from Ashdod port, avoiding the need to pay high warehouse fees, and making the importation process easier and faster for partners relying on private contractors 3) Best Practices, Coordination Meetings and Workshops with UN Agencies, I/NGOs and Red Cross societies on: • best importation practices • Yuval and GRM system (when & how to use one or the other) • tips and suggestion to logistics focal point and heads of operations and how to avoid bottlenecks and entry denials SO 2: Coordination meetings are held at country and operational levels to ensure a common situation awareness and timely, appropriate and effective humanitarian response 4) Data Platform and Improving Humanitarian CargoAccess – In order to maximize the efficiency of the responses carried out by different actors and to improve the coordination among the logistics partners; the Palestine Logistics Sector built a multisectoral digital data collection platform that: • Provide an overview of the different partners projects • Identify the critical sector of interventions • Strengthen the coordination and support the gathering, production and sharing of information required for an effective logistics response • Produce an advocacy report that helps partners that are facing entry denialsCoordination of the projects data collection platform exercise built with the support ofthe Global Logistics Cluster, to collect, compile and disseminate key logistics information 5) Information Sharing, Analysis and Coordination Meetings - Support and providegeneration and analysis of data and information for the planning and coordination of thelogistics interventions. Engage the partners in sharing projects logistics data on planning,priorities, and bottlenecks faced during the importation of cargo in Gaza. Assess gaps toavoid duplications, and to promote a harmonized logistics response. There is noexpectation of opening of the borders crossing to function as normal (before May 2021)in the coming period. The data collection platform built by the Logistics Sector shouldcontinue to work, and increase the partners participations, especially I/NNGOs. 6) Sector Information Dissemination - Provide information management services with a dedicatedLogistics Sector webpage, through situation updates, meeting minutes, assessments, andlogistics snapshots with the aim of supporting operational decisions making to improvethe efficiency of the logistics response with: • logistics gaps and bottlenecks • logistics infrastructures geographic information systems/mapping tools and products • operational information on roads conditions, borders closure & entry points, and Israeliauthorities’ official communications 7) Reporting and Information Management- On quarterly basis, produce an operation infographic overview of the different organizationsinvolved, the sectors of intervention, the type of materials imported, the importationmechanism used, and the common challenges faced by the logistics partners. Produce amonthly SitRep on the analysis of the partner’s data explaining trends in comparison withprevious months SO 3: Strategic humanitarian response is updated at all levels andsectors, as needed, before end 2022 and Preparedness measures for humanitarianemergencies are in place 8) Preparedness Measures – Invest into partners’ logistics capacity strengthening in Gaza on a regular basis, building on Warehouse Management training first offered in late 2021 and new trainings on emergency preparedness, emergency response, medical logistics and health supply chain will be held on 2022; these will also contain PSEA info.The Audience of the capacity building and workshops are logistics focal points and head of operations working with Un Agencies, I/NGOs and Red Cross Societies that have logistics projects and operation in Gaza. 09) HRP update – The update will be based on:-partner’s lessons learned from their projects and activities-data collected and analyzed during the year by the Logistics Sector-logistics best practices and lessons learned shared and collected during workshop and sector meetings The HRP plan will be updated with the relevant activities aligned with the Logistics Sector strategy in order to support partners and beneficiaries in the most effective way10) Cross-sectoral Inputs - Contribute to inter sectorial coordination at the central levelwith the Identification of needs and response modalities that may require inter-cluster ad hoc harmonized response. The identification of needs and adaption of the response will be part of the information management and data collection process from partners. Based on the customs situations and crossing border operation feedbacks; we’ll be able to adapt the plan to better support the Palestine Logistics Sector’s partners11) Support Fundraising - Develop and support resourcemobilization and partnership strategies to strengthen relations with other clusters, UNagencies, governments, donors, I/NNGOs and other key partners

The Logistics Sector approach is to engage the entire logistics humanitarian community in order to respond in the most effective manner.To do that, the Logistics Sector collects needs and requirements from partners on the ground through different mechanisms and approaches such as: coordination meetings, bilateral discussions, workshop, survey and field assessments.All relevant information is then collected, compiled, analyzed, and the key results disseminated to the entire humanitarian community through the ICCG and HCT, in order to find the best way to support beneficiaries’ needs in the field.The sector includes representatives of UN Agencies, I/NNGOs and the Red Cross societies to ensure provide one of the right supports, at the right time, through the right approaches, to different humanitarian partners.Individual I/NNGOs and UN Agencies that are partners of the Logistics Sector build their own programs based on the input from the beneficiaries they serve.

The Logistics Sector is coordinated through the World Food Programme, and accordingly adheres and implements WFP’s Accountability to Affected Populations Strategy and Guidance. The sector operationalizes this guidance through ensuring that affected populations’ input and feedback are taken into account to inform the sector plans and implementation. These AAP control and risk management mechanisms are effective tools to mitigate the risk of abuse, protect the rights of beneficiaries and WFP’s accountability towards them. The Logistics Sector uses WFP Palestine’s several channels for any feedback or complaint: complaint boxes that are available in shops participating in general food assistance activities, feedback and complaints hotline telephone system which is available to all Palestinians (beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries).

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

The Logistics Sector will emphasize with the different partners the needs and obligation to respect Do No Harm principle while implement their projects in the field. We would prepare a survey to be sure that all humanitarian actors that have logistics operation in Palestine adopt a specific policy on this subject. Additionally, in adherence with WFP policies the logistics sector facilitates the activities of the entire humanitarian community based on need alone, without discrimination, and contributes to the safety, dignity, and integrity of beneficiaries by giving them a voice in decisions that affect their lives. The Logistics Sector will also share with his network of UN Agencies, I/NGOs, Red Cross societies and Suppliers key messages and information related to protection mechanisms on PSEA
