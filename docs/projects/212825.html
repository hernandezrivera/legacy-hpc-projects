Project Summary

The action plan is based on recent needs assessments conducted by the organization with external consultants, other stakeholders, and cluster gap analysis reports. Primary data collection methods, including key informant interviews and household surveys, were triangulated with secondary data sources and field observations. Vulnerable groups such as children, the elderly, and people with disabilities were given special attention. The earthquake exacerbated challenges in education and health facilities across Northwest Syria. Pre-earthquake, attendance rates were 88%, but seismic damage has rendered 452 schools non-functional. Over 1 million school-aged children need education support, and more than 25,000 individuals require school rehabilitation. Health facilities also suffered, with 55 damaged, leading to a 28% increase in morbidity. Women, girls, and people with disabilities face barriers in accessing health services. the organization technical report highlights structural damages in preselected schools and health facilities, indicating compromised safety and functionality during seismic events. The action plan aims to address these urgent needs, focusing on rehabilitation and strategic planning to rebuild education and health infrastructure with a resilient approach.
The organization will support the restoration of the education and health systems in NWS, focusing primarily on facilities located in the districts of Harim, Idlib and Jisr Elshogur, in Idlib governorate as well as Jebel Saman in Aleppo governorate. the organization has pre-selected the areas of implementation based on critical needs of rehabilitation for educational and health facilities, for host communities and IDPs.
This project invests in rehabilitation and construction, and consequently has a large budget dedicated to rehabilitation of schools and health facilities as well as water supply and sanitation infrastructures. The Shelter Team will undertake the responsibility of preparing all pertinent documents, encompassing Bill of Quantities (BoQs), design drawings, and other essential paperwork. This team is already trained to develop designs suitable to a post-earthquake response. the organization would also engage an external consultant to conduct technical assessment of the EQ damaged buildings (moderate to heavy damage) and use the data generated to carry out the structural analysis of the buildings to assess the gaps in the existing structural system and produce a retrofitting design to address those gaps in accordance with the organization "Build Back Better" approach. The project will use contractors to carry out the construction/rehabilitation work, which will be supervised by the organization teams. The budget also includes provision of various protection mainstreaming equipments and furniture for the facilities
The risk analysis and contingency plan for the project involve identifying various risks and outlining mitigation measures. Risks include restricted physical access, technical limitations due to remote management, violence escalation, natural disasters, political influences, economic challenges such as increased material prices, and social issues like assistance duplication. the organization, confident in its regional experience, plans to monitor and adapt to the security situation, maintain transparent decision-making, and address economic fluctuations through existing agreements with suppliers. The contingency plan involves temporary closures or activity reductions in response to escalated violence, constant security monitoring, and engagement with local authorities. Assumptions for successful implementation include stable security conditions, unimpeded physical access, cooperation from local authorities, sustainable material prices, availability of construction materials, contractor compliance with recommended construction methods, absence of major natural disasters, and effective coordination to avoid aid duplication.
