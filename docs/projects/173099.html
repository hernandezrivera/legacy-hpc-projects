Project Summary

SCEFA project activities are in line with the protection sector response strategy, activities and indicators. Through SHF funding SCEFA will respond to an urgent and critical emergency for people affected by conflict in South Kordofan ,Khartoum , East Darfur and Kassalla , SCEFA targeting children undeserved especially girls , disability children , Women and PTAs .
SCEFA project aiming to increase the live saving concepts and enhancing the protective mechanism and and advocate against GBV and raising awareness on peaceful coexistence and conflicts resolution which can account for cross cutting issues targeting the most affected groups from inter-tribal conflicts which took places in Khrtoum , Nuba Mount in South Kordofan and East Darfur.
SCEFA intervention will in four levels which are live saving, protection , resilience, and durable solutions through awareness campaigns, focus group discussion, formation and training of family support groups , the beneficiaries will be 13,280 in the targeted areas ( 25% in Khartoum , 25 % in South Kordofan , 25% in East Darfur and 25% in Kassala).
The general expected out come in targeted area will be secured and enhanced live saving spaces and concepts, and protected children, women and elders , the resilience of community backed up and achieved durable solutions

1 - Needs

Despite the significant political successes in achieving peace made in 2020, instability remains in certain areas of the country. Intermittent inter-communal tension and violence in parts of Darfur, eastern Sudan and the Kordofan state heightened protection and rule of law concerns amongst the population. The target areas East Darfur, Kassalla state, Khartoum and Nuba Mount and specifically the targeted geographical areas are the panels of Sudan and the boarders of Sudan all the dwellers are vulnerable communities whom are absent of support of Governmental planning, allocations and provision of services, as well as in the International community all INGOs are neglecting them in term that they are out of their focus. All the above mentioned indicating that they are more susceptible and vulnerable to natural or manmade disaster; this is clearly reflected in targeted states in four localities in each states. The grant requested we are committed to use it in strengthening different weaknesses , and to use the strengths and to increase protection activities for empowering them to achieve durable solutions and to backup capacities of communities to prevent or respond to GBV and to address on standby bases different cross cutting issues towards saving lives and well being of communities .

2 - Activities/Outputs

Activities: 1 – Conduct 12 awareness campaigns 3 in each state targeting 12000 participants from the community (women’s –men’s- girls – boys and children) to raise awareness about child protection and human rights.2- Conduct 8 workshop capacity building on child protection, including mentoring and coaching, for government, NGO staff, and community (35 participants in each workshop half of them female)3- To advocate against GBV through the conduction of 4 awareness sessions in each state (two localities from each state), targeting 400 person half of them female.4- Formation and training of family-supporting groups two in each locality in the targeted states it will be trained in peace building and conflict resolutions and how to attain and maintain durable solution as bottom-up approach (each family-supporting group composed of 30 members half of them female).5-Conduct 3 training workshop in each states on issues of the role of the law of protection and child protection, live-saving and the mechanism of mitigating to attain durable solutions using their wisdom and culture targeting community leaders, Hakama, and elders (360 Pearson,50% female )Outputs:1- 12 awareness campaigns conducted in three states concerned with child protection and human rights.2- Eight workshops conducted on child protection and child protection structures3- Four awareness sessions conducted, advocate against GBV.4- Formation and family support groups were conducted.5- Three training workshop conducted about the role of the law of protection and child protection
