Project Summary

TThis project aims to contribute to respond the impact of Natural disaster (Tropical Storm or Cyclone) in 15 districts in Nampula Province. The project will focus on natural disaster response with focus on preparedness actions and response that include procurement and stock of kits, distribution, post-distribution monitoring). The target is people affected by Natural Disaster in Angoche, Cidade de Nampula, Erati, Larde, Liúpo, Meconta, Mecuburi, Memba, Mogincual, Monapo, Mossuril, Muecate, Nacala-a-Velha, Nacarôa and Rapale, Nampula province.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The projects will contribute to improve living conditions in particular to affected by natural disaster in Angoche, Cidade de Nampula, Erati, Larde, Liúpo, Meconta, Mecuburi, Memba, Mogincual, Monapo, Mossuril, Muecate, Nacala-a-Velha, Nacarôa and Rapale, Nampula province. The project will focus on preparedness actions and response that include procurement and stock of kits, distribution, post-distribution monitoring). The target is the people affected by Natural Disaster, through improved access to sustainable and quality WASH services in affected communities, behaviour change related to safe water consumption, handwashing, improve access to safe drinking water, to people adopting safety practices of hygiene and sanitation and to reduce their risk of water related diseases, particularly malaria and cholera.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities will be:1. Procurement of familiar hygiene and dignity Kits.2. Stock of familiar hygiene and dignity Kits.3. Distribution of familiar hygiene and dignity Kits.4. Post-distribution monitoring These activities will be implemented depending of the demand in affected districts.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The integration of protection measures is a fundamental component of the project's design strategy, emphasizing the preservation of safety, dignity, and equitable access. HELVETAS upholds the 'do no harm' principle, with its staff extensively trained to identify and implement solutions in accordance with these principles. Additionally, HELVETAS maintains close coordination with protection actors, actively addressing issues such as gender-based violence (GBV) and other protection needs through effective referrals. This proactive approach underscores HELVETAS's commitment to safeguarding vulnerable populations.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project has been developed and planned together with provincial and district authorities, with coordination with WASH Cluster based on the information received based on planned affected people. It will be implemented with involvement of local authorities. It also has a participatory approach regarding the community aspect of its work, where it aims at empowering communities to maintain and restore water supply and sanitation and hygiene facilities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Staff of HELVETAS and its partners are oriented about protection against sexual abuse specially of women and children. As per the decision of the WASH Cluster in Mozambique, WASH hygiene kits are always accompanied by dignity kits and this project adheres to this decision. Women and older are a specific target group in terms of care givers and decisions makers in households. The project is aware of the different gender roles related to WASH and addresses the resulting specific needs but does otherwise not target specific genders. However, in the sense of social inclusion, the project is working with the most vulnerable.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Overall coordination of the response is managed by the government under the leadership of the Province level. The WASH Cluster is coordinating the humanitarian community to engage with local government authorities in assessment, coordination and mobilizing relief items to the affected communities. HELVETAS is in direct contact with the national authorities and the relevant provincial authorities. This includes the DPOP, where WASH is included, the DPS as well. HELVETAS is very well connected with authorities on district level.
