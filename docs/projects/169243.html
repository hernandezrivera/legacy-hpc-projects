Project Summary

1 - Needs

According to the Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MEHE) 159 public (92) and private (67) schools and 20 TVET schools serving around 50,000 children and youth have sustained minor to significant damage. Assessments of the extent and cost of the damage are ongoing. The project will start with a rapid needs assessment for the targeted schools, students and teachers, public, private and semi-private schools as it will agreed and appointed by the MEHE and in coordination with the sector to avoid any overlapping and over budgeting.The schools will be subject to light rehabilitation with the provision of needed equipment relevant to learning activities. For a total of 20 public, private and semi-private schools.The rehabilitation will be done through a close coordination with the school administration and MEHE but also informing parents and students for acceptance and support.This will be completed with distribution of education kits ( stationery notebooks, pencils and pens and backpacks for children in primary education n grade 1 to 6 and classroom education kits for also primary classes as they will need to do activities in classroom in support of their learning. IRC will also support through providing PSS and SEL capacity building of teachers and school counselors at the DOPS/MEHE that are covering the 10 public schools and also integrate teachers and school counselors from 10 private and semi-private schools. IRC will link the support and trainings to the ongoing work on the Inclusive education and Child protection policies provide by MEHE.

2 - Activities

1.MInor Rehabilitaion of damaged schools with relevant education euipments. 2.Cash for Education: Ccash support to cover immediate needs of families and children to be able to access to education. The cash for education support will be coordinated directly the schools and social workers in the semi-private and private schools and paid in one shot an amount of 400’000 LBP in support for children and their families with priority to children semi-private schools as they will be consider as a vulnerable group of children and at risk of drop-out.3.Dirstibute Education kits to targeted children in the selected schools with back-packs and stationary according to age and level or class + Classroom kits with learning material, stationary and recreational material that can be used by the whole classroom.4.Capacity building of teachers in PSS and SEL activities as well as school counselors appointed by DOPS/MEHE with provision of needed material and coaching support. Boys and girls will benefit from various activities included within this project in the selected public, private and semi-private schools. 20 schools will be targeted including students and teachers but also working with the DOPS team at MEHE to support the school counselors through capacity building for PSS and SEL trainings that they can cascade to the schools in the areas they cover and most specifically in the affected areas.
