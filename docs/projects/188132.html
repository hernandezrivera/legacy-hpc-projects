Project Summary

The project will improve access to adequate food through cash vouchers to all targeted beneficiaries in the targeted locations, providing life-saving assistance to those who need it most, especially children and pregnant and lactating women for 2022 .This project will target 756 families of widows, orphans, and special needs in north Syria in Aleppo, /Idlib governors, camps too, so we will be providing them a $ 70 voucher monthly for 8 months (8 rounds) that helps them to get their food needs, and emergency needs, especially for families with children suffering from malnutrition or milk Shortage. The cash idea was chosen to give more flexibility to families to get their needs. Al-Resala Foundation (RF) will target 756 families according to the eligibility criteria according RFs" policies, who are widows, orphans, and special needs, the elderly and vulnerable families The beneficiary selection process will be in coordination with civil society representatives, and based on several criteria, Including duration of displacement, vulnerability and eligibility, priority will be given to female-headed households, as the case of RFs” widows and people with special needs, coordination will be at a high level between RFs” coordination bodies to avoid any possible interference. Al-Resala Foundation (RF) has a PTT account in banks in North Syria, where RFs” transfers funds by Turkish post, which will be used within the framework of this project to transfer funds and distribute them to the beneficiaries. Before distributing the cash, a quick market assessment will be conducted to ensure
availability of supplies, prices and access to local markets. Cash vouchers will be printed in a way that prevents fraud and forgery through thermal stickers, purchasing process will be from Turkey, which include serial number and Barcode for each beneficiary name, as they will be distributed to the beneficiary families. When the cash amounts are distributed and include their information, and it’s recovered at the end of the project The number of widow’s families in Aleppo/ Idlib is 756 HHs and the number of individuals is Aleppo/ Idlib is 756 HHs and the number of individuals is 4158, there are database of names, where RFs" has implemented a special project of bread distribution for them, in addition to the distribution of dignity baskets and provision of sacrificial meat. These families live in Azaz , Afrin Babiqa , and their living situation is very difficult. There is no donor provides them with support, and most women work to cover the needs of their children. special needs number is 240 HHs and the number of their family members is 900, they were previously targeted by RFs” through distributing bread, food baskets and sacrificial meat too. 4 - Needs

The Syrian crisis is entering its eleventh year, humanitarian needs are deepening. Ongoing insecurity and the compounded impact of displacement, combined with worsening socio-economic conditions characterized by sharp currency depreciation, record-level price increases, and cuts in subsidized goods have prompted an almost 20 percent increase in the number of people in need compared to early 2020 - with a disproportionate impact on women and children. As of January 2021, HNO 2021 The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 percent in the last year. • At least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. Food prices in Syria have increased dramatically in 2020 and WFP's national standard reference .The agriculture sector, which was once a cornerstone of the Syrian economy, has been severely impacted, with half (i.e. 50 percent) of national grain requirements met through imports. • According to Food and Agricultural Organization (FAO), high cost and limited availability of quality crop and livestock production inputs, damaged irrigation infrastructure across the country, degradation of livelihoods and income-earning opportunities, target Beneficiaries is 4,158
