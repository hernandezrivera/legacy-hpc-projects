Project Summary

This project is designed to provide access to basic protective education services for vulnerable children affected by crises and emergencies in Middle Shabelle Region and Banadir. The target population of this project is 6708 children (3152 girls and 3556 boys) either affected by droughts and other vagaries of weather or displaced by ongoing conflicts in the country. The project targets five existing schools (2 in Balcad and 3 in Jowhar) and will establish fifteen in Banadir (8), Balcad (5) and Cadale (2) to provide access to basic protective and lifesaving education to vulnerable children in need.
The purpose of this project is to use school attendance as important factor for the prevention of child protection risks, particularly the girl-child. The project will employ interventions such as provision of teachers incentives, training of teachers on child-friendly pedagogy, training of teachers on basic child protection skills and counseling, construction of gender sensitive latrines for schools, provision of safe drinking water, the establishment Child-to-Child clubs, provision of teaching and learning materials-including recreational materials, capacity building of the Community Education Committees to ensure sustainability of schools and creation of child protection and GBV awareness sessions for women groups and men in the target villages and conducting routine hygiene promotion to raise public awareness on the prevention of Acute Watery Diarrhea/Cholera. These interventions are envisaged to prevent child protection risks and protect children and women from abuse, exploitation and violence such Gender Based Violence, sexual exploitation, early marriages, child labor and recruitment of children into the armed groups.

Mitigation Measures

IDPs children particularly girls and children living with disabilities may be at risk of exclusion from access to education. Aid vision, through this project aims to advocate for and enforce the equal access to education and protection services, regardless of gender, social status, disability, or economic situation of a child. At risk of exclusion are also minority communities who may be locked in decision making by predominant communities. Aid Vision pay attention to this ensure such communities cushioned from exclusion and are present at decision making table. Aid Vision will negotiate with the local authorities and community leaders to ensure children’s equitable access to education, especially girls and those living with disabilities. Where practicable, the project will advocate for 50-50 gender representation in all positions of leadership in the project, for example: teachers, CECs, Protection Focal Points etc. The other strata of the community like representation of the youth and elderly will also be considered for community capacity building and awareness sessions.
