Project Summary

This project aims at responding to reach assessed food insecure people with a harmonized selection criteria to meet their monthly food needs at least 8 rounds in a year through distributing 850 in kind food baskets(each food basket is sufficient for one family of 5 individuals) .This will cover a minimum of 1600 kcal person/day (76% of daily recommended standard) as the rest of the 500 kcal per person/day is expected to be covered by other partners/or their own sources (such as bread from bakeries) in Salqin sub-districts .The project will supports total number of individuals disaggregated approximately 813 men, 888 women, 1772 boys, 1942 girls and 50 person with disability (In Khairat, Alhamziyeh school ,Alqaws, Maamal Faroukiyeh, Al Tarahum, Altoulul Camps).
In addition, this intervention approach will contribute in ensuring vacancies for youth through hiring a new staff for the project includes Ms and Fs.
-MHD will identify the most in need families after communication with all the stakeholders through conducting FGDs, surveys and awareness sessions , alongside with 100% distribution monitoring with effective complaint mechanism in place, a monthly PDM and satisfaction report.
-The project will insure females participation and inclusion as MHD will hire a female (one at least) in each distribution team
-PWDs will be given proper attention through the beneficiaries selection process .Muzun will assign a truck during the distribution only for PWD and elderly people to transfer food basket to their living place (door-to-door) since the elderly beneficiaries considered the most at risk during the COVID-19 pandemic in NWS.
-MHD will deliver FBs through the tendering/contracting with the qualified contractor to purchase the materials in Turkey and ship them to Syria. Then providing trucks that will transport the commodities from Muzun warehouse to the places of distribution. Theses warehouses are the ideal location to store the foodstuffs (ventilation - lighting - humidity)
-MHD will also conduct monitoring and evaluation processes throughout the project,6 accountability boxes, as well as 2 complaint numbers, will be available for all targeted IDP sites and communities’ residents.
-MHD will provide awareness sessions for COVID-19 and providing water tanks, sterilizers to protect against COVID-19 in the distribution locations taking into account the social distancing during all activities implementations.
- Provide awareness sessions for GBV with PSEA team in same camps.

4 - Needs

As HNO 2021 report , 2020 having seen the emergence of new difficulties that are further exacerbating the food security and livelihood situation of families with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food assistance, representing 68.6% of the total population. All people living in camps are deemed to be 100 % food insecure. The results show a sharp increase from last yearabout 60% . Based on the allocation paper & PiN for 2021: food insecure increased caused by a combination of factors: currency depreciation and associated loss of purchasing power, considerable hikes in food prices, large population displacements, lack of livelihood ,losses in agricultural production and assets, all compounded by ongoing conflicts and the COVID-19 pandemic. Also, Protection cluster report a steady increase in the adoption of harmful coping mechanisms( Child Protection and Gender-based Violence).This project is directly serving the purpose of this Allocation (SO2) : Contribute to most urgent life-saving winterization interventions in high-needs and underserved areas.And FSL Cluster-specific objectives contributing to ASO2 : Strengthen and scale-up life-saving activities through food, cash and voucher transfer modalities to assist IDPs in winter, particularly in areas with critical needs and currently underserved by the cluster (Linked Syria HRP CSO 1)According to MHD's need assessment and the intervening in 24 sites(in Salqin)to provide NFIs and strengthen basic infrastructure and shelter. So these locations need to FBs This area of intervention is considered as border area, relatively secured .This location is already mentioned as a priority in allocation paper to deliver the people with needed services.

5 - Implementation of activities

1-Identifying beneficiaries: Beneficiaries selection criteria's will be determined, which will focus on vulnerable IDPs and a specified percentage of host communities. Self-application will be made, and beneficiaries will be selected according to weighted scores for each criterion, then beneficiary's registration list will be established included list of all selected beneficiaries divided upon locations.2- Procurement and provision of Food baskets through tender process mainly from Turkish companies which have access to Syria from Border, to ensure the quality, validity, storage, packing and packaging3-Arrange regular shipments from food baskets' supplier(s) from Turkey to MHD warehouses in Dana Sub-District.4-Receiving all food baskets monthly in Sarmada warehouses and carrying out all logistical work until the food baskets be received by beneficiaries.5-Providing monthly regular food basket to 850 IDPs' HHs for 8 times on monthly basis.6- Conduct MEAL assessments in each distribution location everyone month.7-Monitoring all the activities (selection process, distribution), conducting FGDs, satisfaction surveys and PDM for all project's activities.8-Conduct segregated awareness session for males and females for COVID-19.9-MHD will hire a third party company to monitor and evaluate the activities of the project. 10-The monthly report will be produced on the end of each month, and the final report will be produced on the last month of the project.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

There will be dangerous of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls. It is high Likelihood and high impact in the community.For mitigating measure, we we will establish a confidential complaint mechanism, train staff on code of conduct and add it in all staff contracts and address any PSEA complaints. Our monitoring staff and staff to analyze data will post-monitoring/ client feedback forms are used, include PSEA topic in regular supervision sessions. Also, there are risks of gender-based violence GBV because of women being treated as beneficiaries and they are primary stakeholders. They may be vulnerable to sexual harassment and sexual assault. If situational and risk analyzes are not systematically performed, these risks may not be identified and corrected. Here it is important that we take measures to mitigate these risks: the MHD team will include female staff on the project and will call for adequate assistance. We will include women, girls and other at-risk groups in the steps of project. Also must be consultations with women, girls, boys and men should be organized and community safety audits audited. There are a dangerous of exploitation to child.For mitigating of risk security and safety on Beneficiaries and MHD staff, we will: Organizing the distribution at the door of home to avoid overcrowding , taking into consideration people with specific needs and women . Pre-consultations with communities and application of checklist at distribution sites. In all the steps of project we will establish a confidential complaint mechanism, train staff on code of conduct. Our monitoring staff and staff to analyze data will post-monitoring/ client feedback forms are used.
