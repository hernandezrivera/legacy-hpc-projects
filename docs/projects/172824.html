Project Summary

Providing a safe and healthy environment suitable for children with attention to people with special needs as well as providing specific school tools for children to help them receive a better education. We will be working on 8 schools in the entire Benghazi municipality.
These schools were chosen because they are in places that the service has not yet reached in the required manner, and it was not a priority for the state and they need service because there are large numbers of children.

1 - Needs

The conflict in the country has badly affected the education sector in the eastern region of Libya. Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) Round 32 records that a total of 40 schools have been damaged including 15 schools destroyed in Benghazi. Additionally, 3 schools in Benghazi had been occupied by the Internally Displaced people (IDP) and the WASH facilities in those schools have been damaged and are not functioning properly. These schools require the rehabilitation of school infrastructures including WASH facilities. Adequate and properly functioning WASH facilities are very essential to create enabling environment for the students and teachers stay in school and continue teaching and learning. This is very important specially in the COVID-19 context. The failure to timely repair and maintenance of the schools infrastructure will result to low attendance, school dropout and will pose the security threat both physically and mentally. Similarly, the poor and non-functioning WASH facilities with inadequate hand-washing facilities will additionally increase the risk of COVID-19 infection among students and teachers

2 - Activities

The project targets to conduct light repair and maintenance of the damaged classrooms and WASH facilities in the eight affected schools in Benghazi that require urgent repair and maintenance. BREEZES will work with the Ministry of Education, Municipality and School management committee to conduct the school rehabilitation activities. Under the rehabilitation work, the project will also provide the schools with very basic schools furniture including whiteboards and desk for students. This will help students learn in the safe and protective learning environment. The project will also consider the special needs of the students with disabilities while conducting the rehabilitation interventions. Disable friendly furniture will be provided to the schools that have disable students.
