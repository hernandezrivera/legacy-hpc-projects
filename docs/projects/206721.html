Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon has displaced over a million citizens in the country and has rendered most of these vulnerable populations helpless and food in-secure. Due to the protracted separatist conflict in these regions, the displacement of populations continues.
The internal displacement further worsened the deplorable living conditions of a significant proportion of the populations of these regions rendering many going to bed on empty stomachs. It is for these pathetic reasons and much more that COHESODEC hopes to carry out interventions in some of the most vulnerable targeted areas of Momo and Menchum divisions of the North-West Region which includes the following: Fungom, Menchum-Valley, Wum, Batibo & Mbengwi sub-divisions to improve on the deplorable food in-secure situation of these displaced populations.
COHESODEC as usual will strong coordination with the FSC NWSW and will as well continue to report monthly their activities to the FSC NWSW.
Furthermore, COHESODEC through this intervention will ensure the provision of Agriculture assistance to IDPs and host communities to improve their food security situation through agricultural livestock and fishery support (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture) benefiting some 2,000 targeted people (female and male).
Moreover, COHESODEC will ensure the provision of unconditional food, Cash transfers/Cash+ (Emergency food Production) to some 2,500 IDPs and local vulnerable communities of these targeted people. We strongly believe this emergency food production will go a long way to ameliorate on the basic food security needs of these affected populations in 2024.
The assistance at this point in time is of utmost importance to the affected populations given that the agricultural support activities as a livelihood enhancement will empower them economically and will sustain them significantly over a longer period of time.
Finally, COHESODEC will in this intervention be providing seasonal Food in kind through voucher transfers to some 2,500 I.D.Ps and targeted local vulnerable populations in a bit to temporally ameliorate on the food security situation of these conflict affected populations of the targeted areas in the North-West Region of Cameroon.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The deplorable living conditions of conflict affected communities of the North-West Region remains a cause for concern. As per the recent Cadre Harmonisé results of 2023, statistics show that over 3 million people are severely food insecure (5% increase in comparism to last year) which includes almost 336 000 persons in extreme/emergency food insecurity situations, an increase of 32% in 2023. For the projected period, over 2.3 million people will be in severe food insecurity. The rising cost of food in most local markets made it harder for households in these conflict affected communities to easily get food on their tables. According to WFP’s price monitoring system, the tuber’s price increased by over 15% in the North-West Region has further exacerbated the already bad situation. The cost of food was 9.5% higher as compared to last year. The main outcomes of this project will include the following: 1) Ensure that at least 85% of the beneficiary households increase their consumption of vegetable, eggs and mushrooms in targeted communities; 2) Ensure that at least 75% of the beneficiary households improve their livelihoods from selling their production surplus; 3) 100% of beneficiaries (male and female) IDPs and local vulnerable targeted populations receive unconditional food assistance; 4) 100% of beneficiaries (male and female) IDPs and local vulnerable targeted populations receive cash assistance; 5) 8% of the total targeted beneficiary are persons living with disabilities; Key project outputs: • Provide agricultural assistance to 2,000 IDPs and host populations to improve their food security situation through agricultural livestock and fishery support (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture. • Provide unconditional food, Cash transfers/Cash+ (emergency food production) to 2,500 IDPs and local vulnerable population. • Provide Food-in kind seasonal food transfers to 2,500 targeted local vulnerable populations.
