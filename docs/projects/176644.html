Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2021

End Date

Project Summary

IOM proposes to maintain, strengthen and expand IOM’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) in Mozambique to support the coordination of humanitarian activities and the provision of life-saving assistance to IDPs. This will be achieved through baseline and ETT assessments in all relevant accessible districts in Northern Mozambique.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

IOM is at a critical stage of continuing its DTM assessment tools in the most affected districts and diversifying its portfolio to respond to information management needs and requests from the Government and the humanitarian community. Thus far, IOM’s DTM activities have succeeded in covering all 17 districts of Cabo Delgado Province as well as affected districts in Nampula and Niassa provinces by 9 rounds of baseline assessment between March and December 2020. Additionally, the DTM rolled-out its Emergency Tracking Tool (ETT) methodology in key areas presenting rapid and important movements of populations, in order to ensure the tracking of population on the move and to identify areas of displacement and settlements and support the identification of critical needs. This aims to inform trends of displacements on a weekly basis and complement and feeds into the DTM larger exercise.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In order to support an informed and well-coordinated humanitarian response, the following key activities will be implemented: 1. Regular collection of data on displacement through systematic implementation of the DTM Baseline assessments. This will include the tracking and monitoring of displacement, production of sex and age disaggregated data, service delivery, and key needs and gaps. 2. Continued operation of the Emergency Tracking Tool (ETT) aimed at collecting information on large and sudden population movements to provide urgent rapid updates on displacement numbers and needs to facilitate a timely e-ffective humanitarian response to the aff¬ected population.3. Implementation of ad’hoc surveys based on needs identified by Government and humanitarian partners. These surveys will be sectoral, thematic (i.e. return intentions survey), or target a specific geographical area;4. Regular sharing of information with Government, humanitarian, and development partners about the numbers, location, profile, displacement trends, reasons and periods of displacement and needs of IDPs. This will include DTM reports, dashboards, raw data, and maps including the information collected in the various DTM exercises.5. Operational support to the Government of Mozambique. The DTM will continue to provide operational and technical support the Government of Mozambique, at both the provincial and district district level. This support will mainly be in the form of joint data collection, technical support on specific topics, and data management in order to improve the delivery of services and living conditions of IDPs.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The DTM enables the humanitarian community to identify priorities and activities through multi-sectoral assessments, which include protection and child protection indicators, as well as sex and age disaggregated data (SADD). The DTM displacement monitoring and emergency tracking tools will further inform humanitarian programming of protection risks and needs for better targeted and evidence based interventions. Furthermore, IOM is working on increasing the number of Protection indicators and to enhance the quality of the information collected to better identify the protection needs, risks, gaps and vulnerabilities of the displaced. At the request of Protection partners, IOM could collect information on other sectors, including Protection and Child Protetion, as the DTM is alredy operational and cover most of the affected districts. The DTM has already conducted ad hoc surveys focusing on thematic issues, including Child Protection. Finally, DTM enumerators have been trained to refer protection cases to the IOM multi-sectoral teams, who can provide direct assistance or further refer cases to the right entity.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The DTM is a participatory tool that involves and includes community sub-groups including girls, boys, women, men and marginalized individuals/groups. These groups are engaged meaningfully in the process using a combination of participatory approaches, such as key informant interviews and focus group discussions. In most instances, multiple key informants are used as a proxy for the displaced population in each location, in order to involve them and to better understand their needs and living conditions. As a way to increase communities’ capacities to provide feedback, access information, and report incidents and complaints, the UN created a hotline (Linha Verde – 1458). DTM enumerators are aware of this tool and will promote it in communities in order to provide access to the UN’s feedback and complaint mechanism. An analysis of the collected data will highlight the most relevant assistance, thus relaying the information provided by interviewed displaced persons. In addition, to the extent possible, the DTM incorporates the feedback and recommendations of humanitarian actors, as partners are the primary recipients and users of DTM products.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

DTM tools include indicators related to vulnerable persons, including persons with disabilities. In addition, ad hoc surveys conducted by the DTM collect more detailed data on disabilility and the access to services for persons with disability. IOM will continue to reinforce its protection indicators, including those targeting disability. In addition, DTM enumerators have recently been trained on the selection of Key Informants, and have been instructed to collect information from direct interviews with persons with disabilities, when and if possible.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The DTM has a viturally inexistent environmental impact as it does not provide physical inputs or materials. Enumerators are based in the district in order to reduce transportation needs to a minimum, and the use of paper forms have been reduced as much as possible for the useof tablets, hence reducing the impact on the environment. In addition, the DTM plans on conducting a Disaster Risk Management survey in the Cabo Delgado province in the coming weeks.
