Project Summary

The project will focus on the rehabilitation of water and sanitation services in buildings categorized under Level 2 (Moderate damage) according to the classification of damages done by the multisectoral assessments carried out by Wash and Shelter sectors. AAH will work in close collaboration with the Shelter Sector to rehabilitate the plumbing system inside the apartments but also inside the communal areas of the buildings. Also, in case previous interventions during Phase 1 didn’t repair the household connection to the water distribution network, AAH will also reinstate the connection to the system. In the same line, if further damages are detected in the reservoirs and they hadn’t been addressed before, AAH will also support on providing household water tanks for the apartments. AAH estimations considers than only around 50% of the HH in the area of intervention will need support, either because the appartments have been already repaired or because the damage in some appartments didn't reach level 2. This assumption has been taken into consideration to calculate the number of HH and beneficiariaies benefitting from the intervention: Target population for awareness activities: 1.044HH/ Target population on building and apartments: 522HH (total disaggregated data: 0-2 years: 70 females; 70 males/ 3-17 years: 184 females: 184 males/ 18-64 years: 1321 females; 696 males/ 65+ years: 364 females; 243 males).
As it has been discussed in different coordination forums, this intervention needs to be a combination between Shelter and WASH sectors in order to reestablish a minimum standard of living conditions, and AAH is aligning with that strategy. In this sense, AAH will also apply to the Shelter Flash Appeal, so the combined intervention inside the building (WASH + Shelter) will be fully undertaking by AAH, facilitating the process for the victims and the coordination with the different actors implicated in the Beirut Blast Response.

1 - Needs

Both WASH and Shelter Sectors have carried out multisectorial assessments in the different neighborhoods affected by the blast to identify the level of damage of each building and categorize the damaged areas into 3 levels: 1) Light Damage, where the WASH infrastructures are in good shape and only a Shelter rehabilitation will be needed. 2) Moderate damage: basic services to the houses are affected, including access to water, wastewater and electricity services. A combined intervention between Shelter and WASH is needed, in order to reestablish a minimum standard of living conditions. 3) Severe damage: Buildings too close to the blast have been severely damaged, including structural damages, and a light or medium rehabilitation from the WASH/Shelter partners is not applicable.This project will focus on bulding and apartments with damages in level 2.

2 - Activities

1.1 Technical visit and elaboration of the BOQ’s: Based on the assessments, AAH Shelter and WASH teams will visit the apartments to confirm the needs of the first assessment and establish a detail BOQ for the rehabilitation of the apartment and the plumbing connections.1.2 Rehabilitation of wash infrastructures at 522 apartment. This will be undertaken by external contractors in order to accelerate the process. This way, AAH can control the quality of the works and supervise that the contracted company follows the BOQ’s established by the team.1.3 Rehabilitation of wash infrastructures inside 87 buildings (if there is a need to do so). In case there is any need to rehabilitate any water or wastewater connection to the distribution and collection network, AAH will take in charge this rehabilitation too.1.4 Awareness with 1.044 HH for good practices and prevention control for COVID 19
