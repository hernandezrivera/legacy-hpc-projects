Project Summary

This Project aim to support the population identified by the WASH sector HRP in the area of Elsalam locality who are currently estimated to be about 110,676 (55,338 Adults Females, , 18,446 adults males, 36,892 children) people in needs. This project also will provide access to safe drinking water , sanitation and hygiene services as per WASH sector standard and strategies.
This project will provide wash awareness and preparedness for targeted host communities and refugees in Elsalam locality targeting 5 clusters in critical need (amzur, Faio, Abuajura, Bulbul Timbisko and Abujazu ) that were assessed and identified as vulnerable in WASH services , by the proposed funding SAO will construct 5 hand pumps and of the 2 boreholes motorized solar energy systems in Elsalam locality.
SAO will also provide sanitation facilities to schools in need and hygiene kits to targeted estimated beneficiaries.
Water:
The project will include provision of access to safe drinking water by drilling of 5 hand pumps and upgrading of the hand pumps to motorized solar system, expansion and extension of water to areas of IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities, schools will be provided with access to safe drinking. SAO will formulate water management committees that will be trained on operation and maintenance, the committees will be formulated from all categories of the communities including IDPs, Returnees, Host communities and refugees representatives (50% of women will be included in the committees), school member , farmers and Nomads will be included in the committees in order to strengthen the sustainability of the project. Regular water monitoring will be conducted as well as water chlorination services. The water points will be divided between human and animal basins will be constructed.
Hygiene.
SAO will provide hygiene kits full packages to the most vulnerable communities including school boys and girls , SAO will also conduct hygiene sessions in the 5 targeted locations to target community leaders and workers , this will include PTAs committees, mobilization of hygiene campaigns exercises at community level , public areas such as markets , and schools , this will be mainly targeting school boys and girls to show examples after awareness , primary health centers will be targeted and covered to ensure safety of people in Needs , People Living With Disabilities will put under consideration in the distribution of hygiene kits.
Sanitation.
SAO will implement this project in full WASH component not forgetting sanitation , SAO will construction household latrines to at least 1,200 most vulnerable households including IDPs, newly returnees , south Sudanese refugees and most vulnerable host communities, SAO will train the communities on the standard of CHAST, PHAST, and CATs this will involve provision of training and materials such as slabs, plastic sheets for fencing , and angle bars , the community beneficiaries will dig the latrines and construct under supervision of SAO WASH program manager, at least 20 units of public latrines will be constructed in order to avoid contamination and open deification around markets areas and public places 4 schools will be provided with school latrines , hand washing facilities and sanitation kits.
Covid-19.
SAO will put the covid-19 component as one of the main intervention in the project cycle , all trainings will conducted in consideration to covid-19 prevention standard , social distance , sanitizers will be provided , face masks, hand washing soaps. A special session will be conducted for the covid-19 preparedness and prevention approaches.

1 - Needs

Elsalam locality The total population is approximately 110,676 although the whole locality is hosting more than 16,118 internally displaced people since 2003.a total of 110,676 people, the majority of them are women and children. Inter-tribal tension makes this area vulnerable to insecurity which has resulted in a dynamic population movement. These people are restricted within the area and they lack basic humanitarian needs (such as water, sanitation, and hygiene)because there is limited and unequal access to safe drinking water services. It is important to note that the distances to the water collection points are very long and prove to be difficult, especially for women. Over the past two years, the general humanitarian situation gradually became critical as the IDPs are increasingly suffering from different types of humanitarian gaps, such as water shortage for example especially from April 2020 update due to funding gaps for SAO regarding WASH project in Elsalam locality , which resulted negative impact within the local community. and there is high demand for water, Sanitation and hygiene services. therefore SAO is planned to implement this project to cover 55,338 women and 18,446 men and this conclusion was drawn from numerous fieldtrips and inter agency missions. SAO aims to provide access to proper sanitation facilities for the targeted population, mainly focusing on the vulnerable groups (women, children, and disabled people). The lack of sanitation facilities affects the general condition of women because they cannot defecate during the day; defecation at night exposes them to security risks. They are also exposed to rape and death during water collection due to the far distances between the camps and the water sources. Child safety is also a concern, not only because they are often with their mothers but also because they are exposed to feces (outdoor defecation) which in the long term could lead to diseases and an increased risk of mortality. In addition, children are more likely to be affected by water-related diseases. and the needs of women are access to safe water with very close distance, sanitation services as well as hygiene promotion; for the men their needs are income generation activities to support their families and for the girls and boys protection and accessing to safe water with close distance to their homes. this project will actively address gender imbalance to prepare gender mainstreaming plans and to ensure that community committees include women, men, girls, and boys. The project will also focus on gender segregated data assessments, day to day monitoring through daily monitoring forms, consulting all community structures in any intervention (consult women, men, girls, boys, youth, and old peoples). SAO will work closely with WES , the local community to address environmental issues and to ensure that the environment is safeguarded by focusing on integrated water resource activities and natural resource management. For example, latrines are designed to mitigate natural resources degradation by reducing the use of wood and tree branches as latrine pit covers. This will complement WASH activities. SAO will work closely with WES and concerned UN agencies (UNICEF -UNEP) and other INGOs working in the same areas and sector to establish appropriate sustainable natural resources management approaches.
