Project Summary

The proposed project will significantly contribute to implement the COVID 19 Preparedness and Response Plan of ISCG Health Sector, Cox’s Bazar developed in light of Country Preparedness and Response Plan (CPRP) for COVID 19. The project is designed to continue the existing essential primary healthcare service that will ensure the sexual and reproductive health services and also will create provision for early detection of suspected COVID 19 cases, referral, awareness building initiatives and provide support for the prevention of COVID 19. A total of 11 BRAC Health Facilities (2 primary healthcare center and 9 health posts), distributed in 10 Rohingya camps will be equipped with trained Health Service providers and necessary logistics support for COVID 19 response. At the same time project also will develop the capacity of health service providers of Ukhia and Teknaf Upazila Health Complexes to be engaged with COVID 19 Response. For the establishment of Isolation and Treatment Center for COVID 19 patients’ in Cox’s Bazar the proposed project will support Government initiatives. As the referral system of Upazilla Health complex is not optimum to support the existing patients, it will be more jeopardized with the increased need of COVID-19. For this reason project is proposing to provide three Ambulances to three Upazila Health Complexes in Cox’s Bazar for emergency transportation of COVID 19 cases to designated treatment centres. In addition, under the proposed project, 135 community health workers will be trained and equipped for community awareness raising on prevention of COVID 19 and referral of suspected COVID 19 cases to nearby health facilities.

Objectives

Objective 1: Reduce to the extent possible the morbidity, mortality and spread of COVID-19 by rapid identification and isolation of all cases, including most vulnerable providing them with appropriate care, and tracing, quarantining, and supporting all contacts.Objective 2: Prevent transmission and amplification of COVID-19,by enhancing infection prevention and control in community and health care settings and effectively communicate critical risks (counter misinformation) to men, women, boys and girls.Objective 4: Ensure continuity of equitable access to and utilization of quality lifesaving and comprehensive primary and secondary health services for all crisis-affected populations with a special focus on sexual, reproductive, maternal, neonatal, child and adolescent health; mental health and psychosocial support; and non-communicable diseases while adapting services to the current context.

Needs

"The Rohingya camps are the most densely populated camp setups throughout the world where overcrowding is already a huge problem which is unmanageable. In the added crisis of COVID-19 outbreak the risk of casualty of this community will be multiplied by thousand times. If there is outbreak of COVID-19 in a large scale only the critical essential health care services from Primary Health Care centres along with field hospitals will run in the camp beside the Isolation and treatment centre. So there will be very less number of health centres in the camp to provide general care and SRH services. It will much critical to run the health services to reduce the morbidity and mortality from treatable causes. One of the important pillar to prevent COVID-19 is awareness building that is very much essential where the CHWs are the front line fighters. The CHW’s will disseminate the COVID-19 awareness messages among the camp dwellers. This workforce can also play role in home based care for the mild COVID-19 cases and also an important source of COVID 19.The Government efforts to combat COVID-19 outbreak in the host community is moving forward slowly but due to lack of resources the efforts are not running forward quickly and the planned ITC beds are not appropriate as per the projected need. Recently health sector assessed the Teknaf, Ukhiya and Ramu Health complexes where Government is planning to prepare ITC but due to resource constraint they are not fully functional. Patient referral system of the Upazilla health complexes are not meeting the expectation of the community in the normal situation where COVID-19 related patient load will add extra burden on it and the system may dysfunction that need to be addressed. "

Activities or outputs

"Objective 1:1.1: Support to Government initiatives to combat COVID 19: The proposed project will significantly support government’s initiatives to establish and operational of Isolation and Treatment Centers for COVID 19 patients in Cox’s Bazar by providing medicine, logistic, equipment and referral support. 1.2: Screening and referral of suspected COVID 19 case: The proposed project will train and equipped the health services provides of both BRAC and Government Health Facilities for early detection and referral of suspected COVID 19 cased to designated Isolation and Treatment Centers. There will be 2 existing ambulances in the camp and 3 new ambulances in the host community (Teknaf, Ukhiya and Ramu Upazilla Health Complex) to transfer suspected COVID 19 cases round the clock. Objective 2:2.1: Awareness building on COVID 19 prevention: In the COVID 19 context community health workers will be equipped with training on COVID 19 and provided with preventive equipments for community awareness raising and referral of suspected COVID 19 cased to health facilities. Objective 4:4.1: Continuation of essential primary health care services: Project will support critical essential Primary health care and SRHR services to reduce mortality and morbidity from general illness other preventable and treatable causes as there will be shortage of this care amidst of COVID-19 outbreak. In the health facilities, there will be separate space for women, children and person with disabilities. Project will collect reports according to age and sex aggregation of service recipients. "
