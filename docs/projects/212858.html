Project Summary

• 300 tons of soft wheat seeds (to produce bread) and 87.5 tons of Sham 6 (to be sterilized and used for planting) will be purchased (after testing seed samples using Standard Operating Procedures before purchases) from local farmers who were selected to receive an agriculture basket and will harvest their crop in June 2024 to support their wheat production and their income, hence support their resilience.
• 400 Tons of wheat seeds (75%soft and 25%durum) (300 tons of soft wheat from the farmers and 100 tons of durum wheat from the market) will be stored at ICO warehouses under controlled conditions and later will be sent to mills to produce 300 tons of flour that will be baked to distribute 574,500 bread bundles (600 g bundle weight) to 2527 HH (14814 individuals) in 16 camps in Harem-Qourqeena and Dana with 5054 bread bundles on daily basis 26 days monthly up to 4 months, from the beginning of August 2024 to the end of November 2024 by well-trained ICO distribution team with great and proven track record dealing with BNFs using a well-controlled mechanism to ensure accountability and transparency.
The distribution criteria will be:
HH with 1-3 individuals will receive one bread bundle.
HH with 4-6 individuals will receive two bread bundles.
HH with 7-9 individuals will receive three bread bundles.
HH with 10 individuals or more will receive four bread bundles.
Each ton of flour can produce 1915 bread bundles; each bread bundle weighs 600 g.
This project also will support resilience at medium- and long-term large scale for strategic wheat chains. Leading to an increase in the economic returns from this investment, leading to using the savings later by families (especially for children and women) for health, education, and food supplies, to 700 vulnerable and small size farmers. Those 700 vulnerable farmers from local communities or IDPs including families headed by women and/or disabled, to receive an agricultural basket that contains: 125 kg of Sham 6 wheat seeds, 100 kg NPK fertilizer, and 150 kg Urea 46% N-fertilizer (applied at two phases, mid-February and March as recommended by FSL Cluster), 50 g narrow leaves herbicide, 250ml insecticide, 500ml fungicide, 100g rodenticide and cash voucher (65 $ /farmer) to harvest, will be distributed to each farmer. The agricultural baskets will be distributed at 6 different villages in Ma’aret Tmasreen and Bennish subdistrict.
