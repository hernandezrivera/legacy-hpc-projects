Project Summary

Community Development Planning is designed to facilitate interaction between the formal and informal security sector and the communities, in order to improve relationships both within the forum and the larger community. A community development planning, sensitization and mobilization comprises of all parties, who can help to jointly identify and address key security challenges, preventing crime and promoting community cohesion. It can also serve as an open and transparent community complaints and resolution bureau'

1 - Needs

The goal of this project is to create and enhance community accountability structure in Yobe state – so as to support and strengthen the role of citizens in the deepening of democracy, human rights, access to justice and promote good governance.

2 - Activities

1. Advocacy meetings with Community leaders, Religious leaders, Police, Civil Defence Corp, Women groups, CJTF and vigilante group members in the target LGAs. These meetings are designed to informed stakeholders about goals and objectives of the CDP;to have them project buy-in and request them to nominate a person to represent them at the Steering Committee. Selection is by choice of each representative group though conscious effort is made to achieve a balance of representatives.2. Selection and training of principal officers for CDP Steering CommitteeThis is a training workshop for Steering Committee members of CDP in the two locations. A local consultant will be engaged to deliver trainings on conflict sensitivity, conflict management, and gender sensitivity. 3. Inauguration of the all-inclusive forum in Yobe LGA. This is the formal inauguration of an all-inclusive two steering committee members in the LGAs. 4. Meeting between the Steering Committee Members and local government executivesThis is a meeting between the steering members and local government officials to discuss support (cash in kind) for the CDP meetings e.g. venue, chairs, stationeries and light refreshments to ensure sustainability.5. Organizing and facilitating monthly CDP meetings..
