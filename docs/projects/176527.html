Project Summary

Zimbabwe is prone to a number of rapid and slow onset disasters which have generated acute humanitarian needs that are aggravated by the current political and economic crisis. The delivery of a targeted and efficient humanitarian response relies on the capacity of humanitarian actors to collect accurate and regular information on the locations and needs of population of concerns (IDPs, returnees, host communities). In order to support advocacy and inform operational planning and response, a data collection system is implemented in the country. This system includes various data collection tools which have been elaborated in close collaboration with humanitarian partners and which are currently used to collect information on populations affected by the Cyclone Idai, other natural disasters and to collect information about mobility trends in regard to the COVID-19 pandemic. The DTM tools are flexible and can easily adjust to respond to any emergency crisis, from Flow monitoring, Baseline assessments, Rapid assessments, PMM, Multi sectorial and village assessments, Return intentions, Socio-economic surveys, etc.
The continuation of the data collection system is crucial to ensure an efficient and targeted humanitarian response. DTM has being systematically deployed in medium to large-scale.
humanitarian response operations since objective analysis of primary data is key to effective advocacy and a well-informed humanitarian response. DTM will serve as a coordination and key accountability tool to regularly inform analysis by other humanitarian and policy actors.
DTM assessments will highlight persistent gaps and serve to inform relevant actors about where assistance is most needed and how the interventions are impacting in the population in need.
DTM will enhance disaster preparedness and migration crisis response through better integration of systems and actors, strengthening national and local capacities on displacement tracking, and enhancing predictive analytics, ethical data collection and accountable data use promoting accountability towards the affected populations.

1 - Activities

• Draft data collection tools and establish assessment methodology in close collaboration with concerned authorities and humanitarian partners. • Undertake Mobility Tracking assessments in areas affected by sudden/slow onset disasters, health crisis and conflicts in order to collect data on the presence and locations of populations of concern (IDPs, returnees, affected populations). • Carry-out detailed multi-sectoral location assessments to collect data on the numbers, demographics, needs and mobility dynamics of populations of concern.• Establish Flow Monitoring systems to quickly collect information on internal displacement caused by particular events. • Facilitate joint analysis and dissemination of key information products.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Key protection stakeholders were consulted during the design of the project. In addition views of communities gathered during other activities being implemented by both IOM and its partners were taken into consideration during the design of the project. Key community leaders would be capacitated to raise awareness within their communities. vulnerable and at risk members of the community. IOM has been working to strengthen existent toll free numbers as feedback mechanisms. To this end IOM will promote use of the toll free line through posting information on the toll free line in public spaces in the target districts and through other IEC materials developed by the project. IOM will also periodically conduct community feedback meetings at which project beneficiaries and the community at large will be given the opportunity to raise their concerns and be provided with feedback on the project.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Project activities will be carried out in a manner that will not impact on the environment. IOM will promote the use of reusable materials and will encourage communities to recycle materials procured under the project. In addition communities will be sensitized on environmental protection issues including proper waste disposal.
