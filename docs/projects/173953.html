Project Summary

In 2021, OCHA will continue to focus on the humanitarian crisis in the Borno, Adamawa and Yobe (BAY) states, with the centre of the operational response in Maiduguri, while maintaining a commitment to support the HC, humanitarian partners and the Government at federal level in Abuja, including for emergency response and preparedness as dictated by events and circumstances.

1 - Needs

Nigeria has a complex humanitarian coordination structure, with federal level mechanisms managing the national interface with the humanitarian community on all strategic issues. The Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs, Disaster Management and Social Development (FMHDS) is the main interlocutor for the international humanitarian community. At the operational level, the humanitarian actors interact with autonomous state structures with devolved functions whose mechanisms and governance structures do not necessarily mirror the federal level mechanisms. However, the mechanisms are closely interlinked and constant engagement with government oﬃcials at both federal and state levels is crucial for the success of the humanitarian response. OCHA plays a strategic role within the overall humanitarian architecture in discharging its core functions (coordination, advocacy, information management and resource mobilization) in support of the Humanitarian Coordinator (HC) and the Deputy Humanitarian Coordinator. OCHA services are required to support coordination of over 86 humanitarian partners currently operating in the BAY (Borno, Adamawa and Yobe) states. OCHA will continue to consolidate its presence in the BAY states. OCHA will continue to support both the Operational HCT and the ISWG to become more effective in the BAY states. For better coordination at the sub-state level, OCHA will continue providing the necessary support to coordination at the Local Government Area (LGA) level. OCHA will continue to explore ways to promote and sustain humanitarian access, where feasible, and reinforce CMCoord mechanisms at federal, state and LGA levels. OCHA has three operational sub oﬃces providing services in the BAY states. The oﬃce in Maiduguri supports the sub oﬃces in Yola and Damaturu and consolidates operations as one BAY response. The Deputy Head of Oﬃce based in Maiduguri oversees all three oﬃces and acts as the head of OCHA operations in the BAY states. OCHA’s presence in Abuja under the leadership of the Head of oﬃce continues to support ﬁeld operations—but its emphasis is also on supporting the Humanitarian Coordinator, UN agencies, donors and NGO partners located in the capital. This includes the HCT and other inter-agency coordination mechanisms at the Abuja level. The OCHA Abuja oﬃce is further responsible for ensuring strategic engagement with relevant federal government ministries and key interlocutors, including the FMHDS, Ministry of Budget and Planning, and the North East Development Commission (NEDC). The OCHA Humanitarian Financing Unit (HFU) based in Maiduguri manages the Nigerian Humanitarian Fund (NHF).
