Project Summary

Support most vulnerable wheat farming families in three governorates through assistance with production inputs to cultivate 1,500 ha of wheat as a food security stabilization factor, the project will provide wheat seeds, fertilizers, irrigation and harvesting support to most vulnerable farmers as in-kind materials, the project will purchase the yield with a subsidized price after receiving back the agricultural inputs value, each farmer will be supported to cultivate 1ha.
75% of the purchased wheat yield will be screened and stored to support the project sustainability, while the 25% of the yield will be grained to produce flour will support bread production, the expected quantities will be 1,500 MT of bread flour, the project will provide flour to support bakeries in communities which suffer from food insecurity, bread will be distributed to 12,500 HHs for 4 months in daily base.

Needs

According to HNO 2019; An estimated 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity. From the assessed households, an estimated 40 percent of Syrians spend more than 65 percent of their expenditure on food, 57.2 percent of food insecure households reported to have outstanding debt and 65 percent of food insecure people are adopting crisis or emergency negative coping strategiesEight years into the crisis, the main drivers to food insecurity are prolonged displacements, loss and lack of livelihoods and reduced production capacity which in turn resulted in limited physical and financial access to food, high prices and inflation contributing to reduced purchasing power and continuous depletion of livelihoods assets of the most vulnerable populations. Almost 40 percent of the surveyed households report to be an IDP or returnee of whom 74.5 percent reported to be displaced for more than 12 months. Among them 478,282 displaced people living in last resort camps are of particular concern as they have limited access to a diversified diet or income opportunities. With an 83 - 89 percent people living below poverty lines , and with cumulative GDP loss of $226 billion between 2011 – 2016 out of which $16 billion in losses and damages attributable to agriculture sector alone, the relationship between poverty and food security is becoming inseparable. A monthly food ration with staple items costs at least 80 percent of an unskilled laborer’s monthly salary and 50-80 percent of a public service employee’s monthly salary thus demonstrating the existence of “working poor” in Syria. The worst drought in 30 years affected cereal production in 2017/2018 winter season in Syria. This has come as an additional burden on the prolonged crisis with the lowest wheat production at 1.2 million metric tonnes since 1989. There is a shortfall of wheat to meet domestic requirements (food use) of 3.4 million metric tonnes. The people in the project locations are suffering from high cost of food including the basic foods such as bread which reaches a price of up to $1 per kg. bag. The wheat farmers face difficulties in selling their crop due to monopolies by some traders which lead some farmers to abandon cultivation all together thus become reliable on relief assistance.The majority of household headed by women do not have income generation, for all these reasons, this project will be phase four in 2018.

Implementation of activities

ِActivity 1: Assist 1500 farming families with agriculture inputs (10% of them are women-headed families), each beneficiary household cultivates an average of 1 ha.Activity 2: purchasing the wheat production from the targeted farmers with 5% subsidized price Activity 3: Milling 2500 tons of wheat and supporting the mills with flourActivity 4: distributing bread with supported price
