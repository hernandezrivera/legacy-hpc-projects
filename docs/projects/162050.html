Project Summary

"From September to October 2019 Uttaran piloted 200 shelters up-gradation work through Transitional Shelter Assistance (TSA) in camp 22 where IOM provided shelter material and Islamic Relief Worldwide provided operational and staff cost for implementing the mentioned project. In 2020 Uttaran will upgrade 500 shelters in camp 22 and 2000 shelters in camp 14 under IOM’s TSA programme. This time shelter materials will be provided by IOM and operational cost, office cost and staff cost will be provided by Islamic Relief Worldwide.
Uttaran has been providing humanitarian assistance to the Rohingya community in Camps of Ukhiya and Teknaf of Cox’s Bazar district since July 2018. Partnering with IOM, WFP, Islamic Relief Worldwide and Misereor, Uttaran’s humanitarian response in Rohingya crisis both for Rohingya refugee and the host communities includes food, livelihood, NFI, Shelter up gradation, DRR, WASH etc.
In Camp 22, as a TSA common pipeline partner of IOM, in collaboration with Islamic Relief Worldwide, Uttaran provided TSA with treated bamboo and repairing and upgrading 200 shelters.
Uttaran has been working in humanitarian sector since 1985 and one of the key priority areas of the organization is shelter. Over the years the organization provided shelter support to thousands of families in the coastal areas of Bangladesh during small, medium and large disasters. Since 1988, more than 11,000 HH had been provided housing with support from DFID, Bangladesh Bank, Oxfam, Canadian CIDA, Trocaire, Misereor etc.

Needs

"According to a recent study by the Shelter/NFI sector in Cox’s Bazar, over 22 million sticks of bamboo have been used by humanitarian actors for construction to date. Because of dwindling supply, much of this bamboo has been low quality with viability of only 8 months to 2 years, meaning that it already has been damaged. The JRP 2019 have targeted 145,000 HH in Camps to benefit from transitional shelter assistance (TSA) against the need of safe shelter for 949,000 people, or 180,000 households. Till July 2019, only 23,415 have been achieved that remain the Shelter needs in large scale with a pressing nature. Rohingya refugees in Camps are still living in below any human standards and vulnerable self-built, makeshift shelters. In Camp 14, a total of 6,582 and in Camp 22, another 4257 Rohingya refugee families resides. They need shelter up-gradation support. Uttaran collaborating with IOM and Islamic Relief Worldwide will be started TSA with treated bamboo for 500 Rohingya families of Camp 22 and 2000 families living in Camp 14. In the current shelters, families with PwD and aged people and pregnant women are particularly at risks of access, mobility and safety. "

Activities or outputs

"i) Coordination: At the beginning of the project, Uttaran will coordinate with the Shelter/NFI Sector Coordination Team, Camp in Charge and Site Management authority of Camp 22 and Camp 14 to select the households those who will get TSA support. Additional assistance for the most vulnerable HH i.e. PwD, women/children/aged etc. will be provided. ii) Participation: This project will promote participation of women, children and Person with Disability. iii) Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR): The targeted households will have drainage, bracing, slope protection support which will ensure Disaster Risk Reduction. iv) Staff training: Uttaran staff will be provided with the training in line with the scope of work of IOM’s ToR for TSA Programme. Technical staff of the project will be deployed for supporting the beneficiaries during and following the upgrade process, including on the implementation of the key messages in the ‘Shelter Maintenance Handbook’. Staff capacity will be assured to maintain humanitarian standers and principles and to ensure mainstream protection and gender responsiveness including inclusion of PwD. v) Beneficiaries training: Selected beneficiary HH head will be trained on shelter upgrades. The training content will include ‘Shelter Upgrade and Maintenance’vi) Carpenter Training: A group of local carpenters and workers will be trained and oriented on TSA to provide support to the most vulnerable who are not able to do their own shelter work. Special focus will be given to PwD and elderly people. vii) Distribution of in-kind and voucher materials: Required staff, logistic, transport and warehouse facilities will be established and maintained in line with the IOM rules and regulations. IOM guidance will be instrumental in establishing the voucher system.viii) Accountability: The project will to ensure principles of accountability are mainstreamed throughout the project planning, implementation and M&E work as well through specific accountability activities to improve the information sharing and transparency, feedback communications with beneficiaries and ability to handle complaints. The project will dedicate a MEAL Lead directly reportable to the Director of Uttaran. He/She will be responsible for establishing effective monitoring and evaluation systems and conducting regular independent monitoring on programme implementation across all of our field sites. For the proposed project, a comprehensive monitoring mechanism will be devised across all planned results. Monitoring of the proposed intervention will involve regularly reviewing and planning work against the indicators of each intervention, assessing whether activities are carried out as planned, and identifying and dealing with problems during implementation."
