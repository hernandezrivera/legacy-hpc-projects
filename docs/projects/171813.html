Project Summary

ACTED’s proposed action will response to the needs of IDPS and rural communities in Baidoa, Kismayo, El-Afwayne, Badhan, Diinsoor and Buurhakaba districts who are impacted by floods and drought, lack sustainable livelihoods and at risk of displacement. This project will establish, reinforce and strengthen governance, management and coordination mechanisms at appropriate levels; Increase site safety through site maintenance and improvement works; and capacity build Camp Governance, CCCM stakeholders and local authorities working towards self-management.

Project Activities

1. ACTED will support participation of affected population to the humanitarian response through the assessment/set up and strengthening of inclusive and representative camp/site level governance structures. ACTED will establish and support camp inclusive and representative management committees (CMCs) for the sites. ACTED will support and promote the capacity development of the CMCs members through regular monitoring visits, meetings and dedicated training.2. Capacity building of local/national actors on CCCM and/or planning of services to displaced populations- As part of the AGORA approach, ACTED will provide specific capacity building support to local institutional actors in and across targeted sites/settlements hosting displaced populations.3. Improve safety and protection for affected population through site improvements and site maintenance, and support to site planning- ACTED will maximize the protection impacts of its intervention by conducting site improvements works, maintaining existing infrastructure and supporting site planning activities to reduce risks connected to site layout, environmental hazards or presence of dangerous infrastructures, taking into account minimum humanitarian standards. ACTED will facilitate installation of solar light and fire point materials will also be handed over to CMCs. 4.ACTED will work at the different levels of the humanitarian coordination structure to improve the quality of life, safety and dignity of displaced people through coordination activities. ACTED CCCM teams will facilitate site-level coordination for improved humanitarian service delivery, based on reliable and up-to-date information gathered by the CCCM Teams. 6. CCCM team will collaborate and coordinate to ensure targeting of the most vulnerable IDPs for additional assistance, to carry out information and awareness campaigns (CwC activities), and establish regular service monitoring systems at the site level to ensure 3Ws and other essential site level information is available to both the IDP population and other humanitarian actors. Additionally, CwC activities will target pressing issues affecting IDP sites such as flood mitigation, COVID19 awareness, and Community Feedback Mechanism (CFM) awareness. 7. ACTED will establish multi-sectoral CFM. The CFM utilizes a combination of information desks, a hotline phone number, and where appropriate, mobile team visits.8. ACTED will conduct regular safety audits with active coordination and involvement of relevant partners and stakeholders in seeking to monitor, address risk and vulnerabilities of women, girls and boys. Additionally, ACTED will conduct regular site monitoring, providing an accurate account on the gaps and needs within targeted sites.9. REACH will provide regular updates in areas affected by displacement, notably across camps and camp-like settings, by monitoring service provision and access to markets, as well as using comparative vulnerability indicators to strengthen inter-sectorial prioritization and gap analysis, in the form of Humanitarian Situation Monitoring (HSM).10. The Detailed Site Assessment aims to inform evidence-based multi-sectorial interventions in IDP settlements in urban areas across Somalia through collecting multi-sector information on the availability of, and access to, basic services in IDP settlements in urban areas as well as identifying displacement trends of IDPs in assessed urban areas. In 2021 the DSA will be one of the primary sources of data for the monitoring of internal displacement and cover both IDP’s in camps and IDPs in informal settlements. It will also seek to use remote sensing for triangulation of IDP population figures and locations of the IDP settlements across the country. Data for the 4th round of the DSA will be available in April 2021 and data collection for the 5th round of DSA will begin in November 2021.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1: Through conducting safety audits, ACTED will be able to identify protection risks and assess specific vulnerabilities of women, girls, boys and men to those identified risks. ACTED will also install street solar lights with the location of the solar lights identified based on focus group discussions with community members and meetings with CMCs and newly formed SMCs. Female-only FGDs will be carried out to enable women identify areas that pose risks or areas where lighting will increase access and mitigate GBV threats. In addition, ACTED will seek guidance from other protection/GBV partners that may have knowledge about locations that are known to be problematic. Preference will be given to areas along footpaths, communal areas and latrines. In order to increase safety in the IDPs sites for vulnerable members, as follow up of safety audit activities, ACTED will provide portable solar lights to vulnerable households. Risk 2: Through conducting safety audits, ACTED will be able to identify protection risks and assess specific vulnerabilities of women, girls, boys and men to those identified risks. The analysis of findings from safety audits will be used to inform actions including putting in place risk mitigation measures and advocating for the service providers to improve the physical safety of sites, the distribution of and access to services, or security. Also based on findings, specific site maintenance interventions required to reduce GBV risks will be integrated in the site maintenance plan. Risk 3: ACTED’s internal policies adopts a zero-tolerance policy on sexual exploitation and abuse and fraudulent actions by both staff members and beneficiaries. This internal policy signed by all staff members, ACTED partners, suppliers, site management committees and camp management committees promote the protection from any contrary actions by all stakeholders. The policy strategy is read together with various policies including, conflict of interest policy, anti-fraud and bribery policy, U.N. Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Task Force on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse in Humanitarian Crises. ACTED has an existing policy on Sexual Exploitation and Abuse which is readily available to all staff members. This document is part of the code of conduct handbook signed by all ACTED staff and volunteers when working with ACTED. Strict adherence to this policy is expected within the organization and in case of violation, internal reporting mechanisms exists which employees are encouraged to utilize in the event such incidences occur. With regard to employee to beneficiary interaction, the PSEA also provides clauses on this and repercussions in case ACTED employees infringe on this. Beneficiary hotline numbers exist and shared with all beneficiaries and posted in all sites which ACTED is active.
