Project Summary

To contribute to the early recovery of affected populations in host or return communities in the Far North region of Cameroon, IOM will raise awareness among populations on social cohesion and conflict prevention through activities and trainings and also provide capacity building trainings to local authorities on crisis prevention, mediation and crisis response in order to adress potential growing tensions among IDPs, returnees and host communities. IOM will also provide multi-purpose cash assistance to assist in providing livelihood opportunities and contribute to provide durable solutions to displaced populations.

Besoins / Needs

The population of returnees has been constantly growing since 2017. Many returnees find themselves lacking support and facing challenging conditions such as fractured social relations, damage to properties, limited access to livelihood, to economic opportunities and to basic services. Displacements also put pressure on host communities, who sometimes have limited access to basic services which are overstretched for them as well, creating additional tensions. IOM aims to reduce such tensions by facilitating social cohesion activities among displaced and host populations and provide adapted trainings to local authorities in order to reinforce their capacities in crisis prevention, resolution and mediation. Another trigger for tensions is the inequal levels of assistance received by all affected populations. IOM will furthermore raise awareness among the humanitarian community on this issue. IOM will thus select priority localities thanks to IOM's stability index and DTM assessments, where returnees and IDPs are numerous and pressuring basic services ; where assistance has not been considered as sufficient or adapted to the needs of the populations affected ; and were tensions have been reported among IDPs, returnees and host communities. IOM will also provide multi-purpose cash assistance to assist in providing livelihood opportunities and contribute to provide durable solutions to displaced populations, in accordance with the guidelines of the concerned cluster.
