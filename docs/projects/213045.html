Project Summary

In light of the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2024, which outlines a critical escalation in humanitarian needs across Syria, the Elaf project's role in catalyzing early recovery and sustainable development is more vital than ever. With the HNO highlighting that 16.7 million people, including 7.5 million children and 2.8 million individuals with disabilities, are in dire need of assistance, Elaf's initiatives are crucial. The aftermath of recent earthquakes has devastated communities, compounding challenges already intensified by socio-economic decline and environmental crises. Elaf's commitment to vocational training, capacity building, and infrastructure rehabilitation in Jaindairis, Salqin, Hazano, Kelly, and Azaz directly addresses these urgent needs. By focusing on empowering female-headed households and rehabilitating essential services, Elaf's efforts are instrumental in fostering resilience and ensuring a sustainable path to recovery for millions affected by Syria's protracted crisis.
Elaf is dedicated to catalyzing early recovery and sustainable development in Syria through a series of targeted interventions aimed at economic empowerment and infrastructure rehabilitation. The project focuses on vocational training, capacity building, and direct support to foster entrepreneurship and improve community services in Jaindairis, Salqin, Hazano, Kelly, and Azaz. By enhancing skills, providing financial support, and rehabilitating essential services, Elaf aims to strengthen the resilience of female-headed households and communities affected by the crisis.
Detailed Activities:
Vocational Training for Female Engineers in the Solar Sector:
Objective: Target 50 female engineers for vocational training to enter the solar energy sector, building their technical and financial capacities.
Selection and Support: From the initial group, the best 25 participants will be selected for additional financial support of $2,500 each to establish their businesses as entrepreneurs. The training costs $300 per participant.
Rehabilitation of community facilities in Hazano and Kelly:
Objective: Addressing critical needs, Elaf will rehabilitate four community facilities, including health centers, public markets, and schools. Elaf will prioritize community preferences during the selection of the targeted facilities, benefiting approximately 12,600 individuals in Kelly and 9,850 in Hazano.
Cost: The rehabilitation will cost $36,000 in Hazano and $27,600 in Kelly, aiming to enhance resilience and empower the local economy.
Support for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs):
Objective: Strengthen 25 MSEs in Azaz and Salqin through capacity building and financial support.
Grant: Each selected MSE will receive a grant of $1,000 after completing the capacity-building program, encouraging sustainable business growth and economic resilience.
Key Project Features:
Local Empowerment: Prioritizing female-headed households and local entrepreneurs to enhance economic empowerment and gender equality.
Sustainable Development: Focusing on sectors with long-term growth potential, such the renewable energy, to ensure lasting impact.
Community Services: Rehabilitating community facilities to improve community well-being and access to basic needs.
Holistic Approach: Integrating capacity building with financial support to maximize the success rate of new ventures and strengthen the local economy.
Through "Pathways to Prosperity," Elaf seeks to lay the groundwork for sustainable recovery and resilience in affected communities. By empowering women, rehabilitating essential services, and supporting local businesses, the project aims to create a ripple effect of positive change, fostering economic stability, and improving the quality of life for thousands of Syrians.
