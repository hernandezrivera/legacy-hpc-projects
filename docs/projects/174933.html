Project Summary

Learning English/French remains a real problem for primary school children. This is a systemic problem in Cameroon schools. At the national level, more than 70% of students cannot read after three years of schooling. In the 04 regions including East and Adamoua regions, it is noted that out of 100 students, more than 91 are concerned. Already at the national level, more than 51% of students experience enormous difficulty in understanding text after six years of schooling. In the ZEP, 80 out of 100 students are in this situation, over 80% of students have problems with grammar, conjugation, spelling and vocabulary.
Students also have difficulties in learning mathematics. In Cameroon country, more than 43% of pupils fail to complete addition and subtraction operations after three years of schooling. In the ZEP, at least 64 out of 100 students are in this category. 44% of pupils fail to identify basic geometric forms after three years of schooling. This proportion is more than 86 out of 100 students in the ZEP, more than 64% of pupils experience great difficulties in numbers and numeration, measurement and geometry after six years of schooling. 85 out of 100 students are involved in ZEP.
Teaching and learning through ICT could help to improve outputs which here is students learning both in quantity and quality. Many researches show that, ICT has introduced qualitative changes and increased productivity and changed the overall style and functioning of the educational system and its governance. ICT-enhanced learning environment facilitates active, collaborative, creative, integrative, and evaluative learning as an advantage over the traditional method.
We are support basic education ministry for setting up “Connect my school” which is an innovative pilot initiative aims at improving learning and teaching trough ICT technology in the Cameroon primary schools. This project will help to scale up this initiative in the east and Adamaoua regions in this resilience context with more refugees’ children in school. The COVID-19 context is also another motivation to explore using technology for learning and teaching, this can help to reduce the impact of this pandemic which oblige to reduce the number of children in classroom and impose double flux with reduction of time duration of learning and teaching in classroom.
