Project Summary

In Sool and Sanaag regions of Puntland, the drought has serious consequences for children and the protection of their rights, leading to abuse, violence, neglect, and exploitation. Key risks for children are family separation and child recruitment, arbitrary arrest, an elevated exposure to GBV including early marriage, trafficking, psycho social distress, a lack of access to education, and hazardous child labour and other forms of child violations. Somalia Humanitarian Dashboard – September 2019 (issued in 22 September 2019) indicated increasing displacement due to drought increased from 11,000 to 18,000 new displacement in September. Further, more than 173,000 individuals were evicted from their settlement in the year including 22,600 in September. This reflect a growing need for protection services especially the children in the IDPs, drought, hard to reach and conflict affected populations. The report highlights only 21 per cent of the target have been reached with Family Tracing and Reunification during Sep 2019 while gaps in protection still remain high in some regions including Sool and Sanaag regions.
The funds from the project shall be channeled to provide essential CP services which include preventive and responsive services not limited to support access to medical services/assistance to children Survivors, re-unification of Unaccompanied and Separated Children (UASC ) to unite with their families and mitigate child rights violation including material support to the children unified with families or care givers providing interim support. We are going to conduct community engagement targeting men, women, youth, school going children and elders with CP messages in order to strengthen community based approach and enhance CP system. We intend to also establish a safe house in each district to provide interim support to UASC while we are tracing families or cargivers. Each district shall also have Child Friendly Spaces to provide avenue for counseling , guiding and advising the children on the steps and child protection services available to ensure their safety and rights. We will strengthen the capacity of staff and CP actors to ensure enhanced services and case management especially during emergencies.

Project Activities

Activity 1.1 Family reunification: PYPEER will provide family tracing and reunification, alternative family care and arrangements including community-based support to identified 150 Unaccompanied and Separated Children (UASC) in target areas, by covering the cost of transport and living costs (including Food for the child 2Kg Sugar, 5Kg Rice and 5Kg Flour).The food will be given to the relatives or the care givers that the child is staying before he/she is reunited with their family.Activity 1.2 Capacity building: We intend to provide training on child protection especially during emergencies, Identification Documentation Tracing Reunification (IDTR) and case management to 50 members ( from LNGO staff, service providers /Case workers and duty-bearers from the line ministries), particularly those supporting emergency response teams and women activists in target districts.Activity 1.3 Protection monitoring. Through focal points and case workers will undertake a comprehensive mapping of existing referral pathways for children how they access to the services and challenges in remote target locations and maintain monitoring and reporting of child violations, abuse and GBV incidents for prevention and response actions. Community sensitization shall be done on child protection concerns to community members reaching 100 people(20 participants from school children group, 20 participants from women group, 20 participants from youth group, 20 participants from Men groups and 20 participants from religious/traditional leaders’ groups.) in the four locations per month. A total, 4800 people (960 participants from school children group, 960 participants from women group, 960 participants from youth group, 960 participants from Men groups and 960 participants from religious/traditional leaders’ groups.) will be reached to enhance child protection awareness and provide platform for community dialogue and community action plans to prevent and respond to child protection concerns.Activity1.4: Establish 4 safe houses to provide interim care and support for Unaccompanied and Separated Children (UASC) pending reunification with their families and caregivers and 4 CFS to expand child protection services including counseling, guiding and advising the children on child protection services available to ensure enhanced safety and rights.Activity 1.5: Provide medical support and other referral services such as legal services to enhance child protection to survivors

Please provide further explanation

We have designed the interventions to take care of protection environment, linkages with the government/community priorities , sustainability , access to to services among the displaced communities. The protection environment has been addressed by ensuring even the most vulnerable community members can be accessed in the hard to reach areas designed through enhanced community approach and through focal points within communities. The interventions are highly linked with the government /community priorities especially envisaged envisages the pillars in protection as well as protection cluster objectives. The interventions seeks to provide long terms gains to protection and realizing sector resilience through approaches which seeks to enhance access to protection services while considering the most vulnerable populations including children, women and displaced populations which anchors the sustainability. Designed in the activities include comprehensive and effective approaches to reach displaced populations including hard to reach populations through surveillance from focal point and case workers as well as visibility and awareness to the services accessible through the interventions.
