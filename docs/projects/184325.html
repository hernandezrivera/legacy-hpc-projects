Project Summary

The Somali population continues to experience some of the worst health outcomes in Africa, being highly vulnerable to acute shocks – like floods or COVID-19 – steeped onto the protracted crisis and repeated environmental disasters. Over 3.9 million Somalis require lifesaving essential healthcare and health protection services. Excess mortality in Somalia is driven primarily by malnutrition, disease outbreaks (e.g. COVID-19, AWD/cholera, measles, malaria) and violence; as well as non-communicable diseases, including complications of pregnancy and chronic disease. These drivers are compounded by displacement due to conflict and natural disasters, marginalization of vulnerable groups and lack of healthcare, including preventive services such as vaccination. Somalia reported its first case of COVID-19 in March 2020.
CoDHNet will improve the access and utilization of health services for women, men, boys, and girls including people with disabilities living in the most crisis-affected populations in IDP settlements and the vulnerable urban and rural poor in Belet-Hawa, Dolow and Baardheera Districts of Gedo Region. Static facilities and mobile outreach teams will be set up and strengthened through provision of medical supplies and the improvement of staff capacity. Remote villages will be reached through mobile clinics; community mobilizers and CHWs will be recruited and trained. The proposed activities will include supplemental vaccination campaign to under5 children (boys and girls) and women of child bearing age (WCBA); Provision of antenatal and post-natal care to pregnant and lactating mothers, strengthening physical and mental healthcare system. Through this intervention in the target locations will strengthen and improve access to essential lifesaving health services aimed to reduce morbidity and mortality, maternal and child morbidity and mortality among. The activities will seek to contribute to improvement in the physical and mental wellbeing of the population affected by conflict and displacement; including people with disabilities. Further, seek to improve equitable access to quality emergency and essential lifesaving health services for the crisis-affected population IDPs, and nondisplaced population. Health care services through consultation and management of childhood illnesses with an aim f reducing maternal and neonatal mortality will be given focus. ANC and BEMONC with referrals of the CEMONC cervices will be provided to the pregnant mothers. Treatment and control of communicable diseases targeting the most vulnerable boys, girls, PLW, WCBA, and men; 31,800 people (6,678 women, 4,452 men, 9,540 and 9,540 boys and 1,590 elderly) including 2769 people with disabilities from both host and IDP communities with healthcare services; Strengthening of a referral system between Primary Healthcare facilities, the community (remote villages) and the regional hospitals; Establishment of mobile clinics; Creating Disease Surveillance and Response plan; Training of health workers on clinical management of childhood illness, Basic Emergency Obstetric Care and disease surveillance and response. Training will be conducted for CHWs in integrated community case management on communicable diseases such as pneumonia, diarrhea, and malaria. CoDHNet will undertake stakeholder engagement targeting beneficiaries in the target villages, their leaders, local authority and MoH officials to provide an overview of the intended project including the objectives, key activities, indicator and the target beneficiaries. Participatory M&E will be employed with regard to frequency, keeping track/ assessing the effectiveness of the interventions with the aim of working within and where necessary adjusting the work plan to enhance efficiency and realization of project objectives. Proper coordination with partners, as well as the zonal and the national team will be adhered to avoid overlap and share the 4W Matrix report to the Health Cluster.

1. - Projects activities

31,800 people from (17,142 IDPs and 14,658 Non-Displaced people) including 3,180 people with disability will get access of life serving health care services to reduce excess morbidity and mortality due to disease outbreak driven by climatic shocks and conflict. Activity 1.1: CoDHNet will set, procure and equip with essential drugs to fully operationalize 3 health facilities and 3 mobile clinics in Dolow, Baardheera and Belet-Hawa Districts of Gedo region; The health facilities requires to be setup to the standard to offer the service as per the EPHS standard to minimize occupational health safety and make it a disable friendly facility, procurement of essential medical supplies and equipment for the three (3) outreach teams and three (3) health centers; Activity 1.2 The setup health and clinic facilities will benefit 31,800 people (6,678 women, 4,452 men, 9,540 and 9,540 boys and 1,590 elderly) including 3180 people with disabilities to provide life-saving health care services including, treatment of childhood illnesses, immunization, Anti-Natal Care (ANC), Post Natal care (PNC), Basic Emergency, Obstetric and Neonatal Care (BEmONC) with referrals of the complicated cases to respective referral hospitals. Activity 2.1: CoDHNet will conduct training to 12 Community Health Workers (6 male and 6 female) on workers on Emergency Obstetric Care for 5 days, the training package will be as per the approved MoH guideline on the EmOC (Emergency Obstetric Care). The staff will be drawn from the Health Centres and Outreach teams. Activity 2.2: CoDHNet will conduct training for 6 Health workers on Neonatal resuscitation on the job training. The training package will be as per the approved MoH guideline on the New-born care. The staff will be drawn from the Health Centre and Outreach team. Improved case management and referral services for 100 survivors of sexual or gender-based violence, also prevention of AWD/cholera cases and COVID-19 pandemic. Activity 3.1: Provision of sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services (including for SGBV) to the vulnerable population including people with disabilities and marginalized groups in 3 districts Belet-Hawa, Dolow and Luuq in Gedo region. This service will be in line with the MoH guidelines. Activity 3.2: CoDHNet will conduct training to 6 Health workers on Case Management and Referral (CMR). Activity 4.1: CoDHNet will carry out public health awareness, social mobilization, education, and advocacy activities on MHPSS, disabilities. Improve equitable access to quality essential lifesaving and emergency health services for the crisis-affected population to 31,800 people from (17,142 IDPs and 14,658 Non-Displaced people) including 3,180 people with disability at health facilities and community awareness level. Activity 4.2: CoDHNet will conduct routine Immunization services at twelve (12) outreach sites in rural villages of Belet-hawa, Dolow and Luuq and three (3) fixed health centers within the Belet-hawa, Dolow and Baardheera districts. The vaccines will include BCG, Pentavalent, Measles and Oral polio vaccines. The villages will be served weekly. The team will work with the CHWs drawn from the ten (12) villages. Target will be 996 children under1 years. Activity 4.3: CoDHNet will conduct training for 12 Health workers on Immunization services for five days. The training package will be as per the approved MoH guideline on the immunization services. The staff will be drawn from the 3 Health Centres and 3 Outreach team. Contribute to improvement in the physical and mental wellbeing to 31,800 people from (17,142 IDPs and 14,658 Non-Displaced people) including 3,180 people with disability of the people affected by conflict and displacement. Activity 4.4: Training of 6 Health Workers and Community Health workers on Mental Health and Psychosocial support.
