Project Summary

The proposed project entails to contribute to reducing the incidents of water rates (borne, based and washed) diseases and diseases caused by poor and inadequate sanitation and hygiene practices. This can be achieved by increasing access to safe drinking water, safe excreta disposal and improved hygiene practices among displaced and other vulnerable communities in targeted states.
HA-UK with its local partners plans through providing sustainable access to safe water supply, improve sanitation coverage and enhance hygiene practices to IDPs and people effected by the ongoing conflict BN and SK economic crisis, climate change (flood. drought), malnutrition, and food insecurity in Blue Nile and South Kordofan states. HA-UK will ensure appropriate and cost effective technology products and clean energy such as Solar energy are installed for safe drinking water. Safe handwashing facilities will be provided in schools, hospitals and villages. Demo latrines will be constructed for the most vulnerable families to encourage all the families in villages to have latrines and avoid open defecation. There will be monitoring of water treatment at the source to ensure safe water is provided to the communities. Most vulnerable families will be provided hygiene kits. Massive hygiene promotion campaign will be launched in schools, villages and health services.
The project proposal will be included:
• Water yard rehabilitation 5 in communities with vulnerable conflict-affected populations, IDP, and host communities.
• Livestock trough construction to keep livestock out of human water sources
• Support to operation & maintenance of 5 hand pumps through the distribution of spare parts to ensure vulnerable populations (IDP, rural post-conflict, etc.) have sustained water access and to prevent hygiene and sanitation-related emergencies or outbreaks
• Training of 25 individuals in the community-based hand pump mechanics to support the operation and maintenance of hand pumps
• Construction of 5 Temporary School latrines to prevent the spread of disease (AWD, Cholera) and to increase hygiene and sanitation behavior in fluid population areas
• Distribute water quality supplies at 1250 HH (at point of use - Aquatabs/PUR)
• Formation/ strengthening of hygiene clubs in the 10 schools
• Do hygiene campaigns in communal set ups (Include Nutrition and Health facilities, schools, or other communal setups) cover 1000 plus people per campaign.
• School hygiene education for the prevention of AWD and the improvement of WASH services in IDP and Returnee education centers. (Target 5 schools)
• Women’s hygiene education to prevent the spread of disease (AWD, Cholera) in IDP, returnee, and rural underserved areas (Target 5 schools)
• Formation/revitalization of Water, Sanitation & Hygiene committees (WASH Community)) to maintain the hygiene of water sources as well as implement preventive measures.
• Community Hygiene Worker Training and Support to promote improved hygiene and sanitation behaviors to prevent the spread of water and faucal borne disease (AWD, Cholera, dysentery, etc.) in rural, post-conflict communities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the project, making sure at‐risk groups’ specific needs are taken into consideration and risks mitigated where and when possible. These groups (who could be constituted of women and girls, but also men and boys, elderly, unaccompanied children, minorities, IDPs are at risk of conflict‐related or sexual violence, forced conscription, looting, destitution and general insecurity. The crop and vegetable kits distributed through the program are designed to be lightweight to enable women to carry/transport them back to their homes easily. HA staff will ensure compliance with the five Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) defined by the IASC Task Force As part of its strong commitment to AAP, HA is engaging IDPs and other persons of concern in the program cycle through formation, training and strengthening of community-based structures and networks who take leading role in project implementation. HA has distinctive community based and AGD approaches that promotes the active participation of targeted population particularly girls, women, disability and minority and ensures engagement of all people of concern across the interventions levels. The people of concerns and other stakeholders are also able to share their opinions and complaints through feedback mechanism. This ensures greater accountability to people of concern and support the project sustainability. The opinions and feedback are regularly collected, analyzed and processed to inform project adjustment and improvement. This has largely reflected in building trust with the people of concerns and host communities as well as led to client satisfaction. Through its presence and capacity support to counterparts.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with group consultations with aid workers

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

HA will strengthen the sharing of information regarding the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) in the targeted states. This will be achieved by raising awareness among the target communities about their rights and establishing effective feedback and complaint mechanisms, including specific channels for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse incidents. HA will ensure that humanitarian interventions are responsive, inclusive, and transparent. In collaboration with community members, including camp committees and vulnerable groups such as women, youth, and persons with disabilities (PWDs), HA will develop or adopt information, education, and communication (IEC) materials on PSEA and disability inclusion. HA-UK will also establish a complaint and feedback mechanism in water points rehabilitated by HA. Additionally HA will place focal points to ensure that there are multiple complaints and feedback pathways in place, either through frequent field visits by focal points or over the phone. Reporting: We will prepare detailed reports that provide a comprehensive overview of our project's progress, achievements, challenges, and financial utilization. These reports will be shared with our donors, partners, and other stakeholders to ensure transparency and accountability.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

HA-UK has established multi-sector Community Feedback and Response Mechanisms (CFRM) in all its operational sites including in the proposed project location (Blue Nile and South Kordofan) through which the beneficiaries will be informed on their rights to launch complaints or give other forms of feedback in regard to the implementation process and the quality of services they are receiving from the HA-UK. The existing complaint and feedback mechanism will be used during the project period with beneficiaries being sensitized on beneficiary accountability standards. Communities will also be informed about the focal point in the HA-UK to handle any compliant report and feedback. The reporting procedures will be displayed in a visible place in the project location and complains and feedback boxes will be placed at distribution points and project site offices for those who are literate to use. Views and feedback will be collected from women, girls and community beneficiaries including those living with different disabilities and the minority, authorities from the start of the project to the intervention and monthly thereafter. Feedback and complaints will generally be collected using FGD’s with groups of men, women, boys, and girls; during community sensitization and awareness sessions; and through the use of suggestion boxes. HA’s community feedback mechanism allows for a range of complaints to be received and managed, including a mechanism for confidentially fast-tracking concerns of a serious nature such as Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. Through this discussion, the information and the feedback obtained from the beneficiaries of the HA-UK will explain its procedures, decisions and allow women and girls to hold HA-UK accountable. Awareness raising on existing PSEA Networks or Focal Point(s) will be included to ensure that beneficiaries are well informed of existing reporting mechanisms.
