Project Summary

IOM WASH is proposing to contribute to the provision of emergency and sustainable water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services to overall 350,000 beneficiaries living in vulnerable settlements and communities in multiple regions in Somalia. Twenty strategic water sources will be constructed and/or rehabilitated which will provide reliable and sustainable safe water and mitigate water shortages as result of displacement and recurrent drought/flooding in hotspots districts to be identified. IOM further proposes to construct 2,000 new emergency latrines and rehabilitate/desludge another 2,000 latrines in targeted settlements.
The project will also strengthen the capacity of the federal/state government officials, community leaders and water committees/technicians for better coordination and sustainability of the constructed/ rehabilitated WASH infrastructures. In addition, the project will nurture community participation in good hygiene practices through training and community sensitization campaigns. The project is aligned with protection mainstreaming to ensure safety and dignity of affected populations without causing harm. Affected populations will further have meaningful access, accountability and participation of activities/beneficiaries design, implementation and feedback mechanism for WASH services.
Furthermore, the project will be a part of an IOM integrated emergency response program and resilience building, where possible, efforts will be made to integrate IOM health, nutrition, shelter, CCCM and protection activities.

Mitigation Measures

2a - Members of marginalized community’s/ minority groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistancei. IOM will gather data (age, sex, diversities) of communities and beneficiaries for effective and informative programing. This will help identify vulnerable and marginalized persons for planning, inclusion purposes and engagementii. A systematic outreach process will be done to pre-register marginalized/minority vulnerable groups to avoid exclusioniii. Efforts will be made to ensure that information regarding provision water will be disseminated to all members of communities (including women, girls, boys, men) through appropriate channel of communication such radioiv. An inclusive feedback mechanism that will include all beneficiaries will be put in place which help evaluate selection criteria and overall services gaps.v. In all water collection points, a mechanism will be established that will give priority to those who cannot stand in line for long periods, such as older persons, persons with disability, unaccompanied children, pregnant and lactating women, so that they wait for less time. 2b- Women, girls and boys are not included in consultations and decision-making processesi. Women, girls and boys will be consulted during programme design to gather accurate information about their specific WASH requirements and preferences for location and design of WASH facilities. Issues of privacy and access to persons with disability will be discussed. ii. Since women and girls often fetch water they will directly be engaged to contribute on decision about safety of the location of and routes to the water collection point.iii. Efforts will be made to include women in water committees and community hygiene promoters so that they activity contribute to the overall program implementation at community level.iv. The project will hire female staff members at project location to ensure that women and girls will be free talking to them and provide feedback mechanismv. Girls and boys will actively be engaged in children hygiene promotion groups/compaigns 3b- Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance.i. Water locations will be put in central, visible places and not more 500 meters away from settlements. This will help protect women and girls from risks of sexual violence/exploitation when accessing servicesii. Separate toilet (and bathing) facilities with internal locks and secondary enclosures will be constructed for women and men with clear labels such pictorial/signs to ensure that they are used by intended sexiii. In setups where threats are anticipated frequency of hygiene kits distribution will be modified and door-to-door distribution considered, to avoid sexual abuse and extortion while carrying kits. iv. Coordination will be done with Shelter/NFI partners to put solar light installations at WASH community centers to deter any risk of violence against women and girlsv. Distributions of water will be done during day light to allow beneficiaries who often women and girls reach home early.

Please provide further explanation

Despite the recurring of droughts, flooding and AWD/Cholera in the country, there are a multitude of opportunities to build upon existing Somali structures and innovations to increase the overall resilience of the population. The project design and implementation will take into account environmental protection to avoid over-exploitation and contamination of the environment and sustainability. This includes design and installation of the water pumping system based on pumping test to avoid depletion of aquifers. Constructed and rehabilitated boreholes will be equipped with elevated water tanks, distribution systems with household connections when possible and hybrid energization systems. Latrine construction/rehabilitation will use standard designs to avoid contamination of ground and surface water. The project will be linked to and built on ongoing resilience WASH program in close collaboration with government institution, local communities to ensure stable and safe environment. IOM will continue to strengthen the capacity of staff from Federal Government and Regional States. WASH committees will be established and a cost recovery system introduced to ensure proper operation and maintenance of water sources, community ownership and ease of access for all population groups. Private enterprise has thrived in many areas of the Somali economy, and the private sector accounts for a large proportion of WASH service providers, the project will promote private Public partnership (PPP) approach to ensure sustainable and affordable WASH services. All the project activities are also linked to and are contributing the National Development Plan.
