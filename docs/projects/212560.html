Project Summary

Currently 6.7 million children and 0.2 million adults are in need of education assistance across Syria, and it is estimated that 2 million children are out of school. According to the HNO 2023, 12 years of war resulted in a dramatic worsening of the education needs: 73% of the sub districts are severely affected, 24% extremely affected and 2% catastrophically affected. In NES, all sub-districts except three are either severely, extremely or catastrophically affected. 60% of the population live in communities where illegal activities are reported as a coping mechanism for boys, and begging is more frequent among women and children in Al-Hasakeh, Ar-Raqqa and Deir-ez-Zor than in other parts of the country.The project is located in DeirEzZor "DEZ" and Alraqqa governorate consisted of three key component areas: 1-preparing currently out-of-school children(boys, girls) to return to formal schools, 2-securing equitable access to NFE -FE provision for children and youth in DEZ, and 3- supporting TVET for Youth girls and boys. The project will reach 2000
children per year (Boys, Girls)in the age of 7 - 17 including children with disability CWD under formal and NFE in 5 targeted schools and 400 of youth who are between 18-27 Yrs. 5 ‘Back to Learning’ campaigns will be undertaken as well as project information dissemination. All the three-component include comprehensive supporting environment services for education quality under provision textbooks, stationery kits for all targeted students, minor rehabilitation work, providing schools furniture and gender and disability sensitive wash facilities, potable water, electricity, and fuel winterization. This work also forms part of the recruitment strategy for the emerging Parent/Teacher Associations, to secure
ongoing community support and participation. For OOSC, NFE services will be provided in each of the project locations. based on children needs assessment, two cycles of SLP, 2 cycles ALP, two groups of BLN targeting children with minor gaps or more than 2 cycles according to their education needs, therefore likely to transition to formal education for the coming academic year. Under the NFE we have: PSS program and PSS recreational kits for schools, teachers’ incentives. Additional ongoing support will be provided throughout the academic year once children have transferred to formal schools; this will support their retention in this provision through remedial classes and homework support as appropriate. Children will be identified by the placement test according to needs/age. the project includes establishments of TLC in case of absence of education environment to cover OOS children who are above 12 and under 18 from boys, girls. security and safety plans will be established and resourced at each location. For FE-NFE: Professional development and capacity-building to enhance performance and quality of education personnel for 90 pers under CSG, positive discipline, peer coaching and SEL. The project includes doing a pilot for SEL in the formal and NFE schools by the trained teachers and providing all needed materials. The project includes supporting youth from M/F 16-27Yrs –PWD-women headed HH and girls under risk of Early marriage and improving the socio-economic situation by supporting NF TVET school in DEZ, by keep supporting the TVET center and targeting 400students, equipping it, provision of all needed staff and educational materials for the five professions determined through market assessment. And support the graduated students with startup vocational kit and incentives. 4 NF-TVET cycle. provision of life skills and PSS. Finally, all the hygiene promotion preventative measures shall be taken into account while implementing the project activities. the children will have participated through monitoring program and feedback mechanisms, complaint boxes, and classrooms level initiatives for more engagements in activities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

IPV will implement the project in coordination with the local authorities and actors in the targeted locations to avoid overlapping and have comprehensive assistance. IPV will be committed to implementing the project activities building based on SOPs, DO-NO HARM, no lost generation, to ensure the quality indelivering response. the selection BNFs consider gender sensitivity and equality. improve the access of girls and children PWD to a safe education. Safety and dignity are clearly marked in rehab work obtaining the EWG SOPs incorporating visible access for pwd. Meal activities for quality and safety for the center and BNFs, high insurance for PWD inclusion , ramps, suitable latrines. PRA:risk : the possibility of Attacks on schools in the mentioned areas, If and when the local security situation deteriorates, Mit: A)Immediately there will be informing internal emergency FP on the situation(IPV has assign one emergency FPper location) b) Coordinate with the other actors in the project areas , working group and hibernate/suspend the operations if necessary. Risk of children to drop out of formal education to attend non-formal education, due to reasons related to children or family concerns, and A limited number of BNFsin the program, which might cause inter-community tension and pressure on implementing staff, Mit: coordination with Key persons to advocate and limit this issue, Risk: Some locations of the project may not accept textbooks with the organization logos. Mit: there will be no logos in the copies of the textbook. Risk: interference from the armed groups in the project areas, risk related to safety and security for the humanitarian aid workers, Mit: have duty of care policy, security focal point and CSG focal point, Risk: violence and bullying in school, inter-communal tensions, Mit: training education personnel on the CSG policy and have COC- CSG is mainstreamed through the design and operation of the project as well as through the content of the curriculum and programs. PSEA:Risk: Harassment among the project staff on the one hand, as well as possible incidents of sexual exploitation between teachers and M/F BNFs. IPV have set many mitigation strategies to PSEA, include: PSEA mandatory training and COC-PSEA and CSG, and clarify that IPV have zero tolerance with any SEA accidents. IPV has PSEA FP in the working area and there will be FPs in project for any need in PSEA mainstreaming. IPV will do awareness-raising for PSEA on levels :community , working staff and centre by brochures and banners, videos, awareness raining messages and hotlines. IPV will ensure that all the working staff is wellknown about the internal reporting and feedback mechanism taken in place in case of SEA occurrence. all the project's service providers or contractures who has direct contact with project BNFs must sign COC. IPV will ensure that during any conducted sessions there will be a female member to ensure BNFs safety, especially from children
