Project Summary

Project aims to assist affected population by Venezuelan Crisis in Lara State, Venezuela, by providing necessary primary and sexual and reproductive health care services as well as psycho social support to vulnerable population, specifically targeting women and girls but also including men and boys. The assistance is directed to health care centers in Lara State, which PUI will support by providing basic medicines and medical supplies, capacity building and infrastructure rehabilitation (this component in consortium), further PUI will implement community activities targeting vulnerable populations in the suburban areas of Barquisimeto by organizing health campaigns directed to sexual and reproductive health care and primary health care activities in schools targeting children in vulnerable areas. PUI will conduct its activities with local partners, AVESSOC - Asociasion Venezolana de Servicios de Salud de Orientacion Cristiana.
Targeted throughout a local partner, AVESSOC, the 2 health facilities were assessed: ADS Centro Monseñor Alejandro Zaini - dispensary in Duaca; ADS Centro Maria Auxiliadora Dispensary in Barquisimeto and 3 schools: one pre-school of ADS Centro Maria Auxiliadora Dispensary and 2 Fe y Alegria high schools in Pavia (Irribarren Municipio) and Carora (Torres Municipio).
The beneficiaries in health facilities will be children, adolescents and youth up to 18 years old and pregnant and childbearing age women, in schools, children and adolescents of both sexes and within the communities, girls and women in child bearing age. The medical staff and management of the 2 health facilities will also benefit from capacity building; health staffs will be coached on clinical protocols and community agents will be trained in IEC/CBC technics, health and hygiene promotion.
