1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

WHO will focus on providing lifesaving and life-sustaining accessible essential health services to targeted populations, directing to the main causes of morbidity and sexual and reproductive health. It will provide quality care for people with physical injuries, disabilities, and mental health needs. The project will target preparing for, detecting, and responding to epidemic-prone disease outbreaks. All these are in line with the three core HRP objectives. These interventions will be provided in partnership with SPS/ DPS, SMDAs and other health partners. The services will be delivered through fixed health facilities and mobile clinics/mobile health brigades. This project will be targeting all age groups composed of girls, boys, women, men, adolescents, elderly, child and female-headed households, persons with disability and other vulnerable groups identified during the periodic assessments. It will be implemented in the 3 provinces (Cabo Delgado, Niassa and Nampula).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities will be conducted to cover all 17 districts of Cabo Delgado Province, 20 districts in Nampula and 13 districts in Niassa Provinces. The activities will:1) Provide leadership to Health Cluster strategic planning and decision-making to ensure health response needs are appropriately reflected in sectoral and multi-sectoral discussions2) Advocacy for resource mobilization for all health partners, ensuring a people-centered approach including MHPSS3) Monitoring of health cluster response through the collection, validation, analysis and dissemination of reliable information for effective decision making 4) Provide regular updates on the health services functionality through the Health Resources and Services Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS) platform5) Provision of essential medicines and supplies based on emergency response to bridge gaps observed in regular drug supply 6) Support assessment and monitoring of e-IDSR performance and support gaps and needs7) Conduct and enhance e-IDSR training to improve timely detection and response to disease outbreaks & health conditions (e.g., cholera, AWD, measles, malaria, malnutrition, MHPSS)8) Guide the SPS/DPS in the implementation of the COVID-19 strategic preparedness and response plan (institutional and community-based level)9) Support COVID-19, cholera, polio and measles vaccination campaigns and reach the targeted vulnerable groups 10) Scale up laboratory testing capacity and rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs) (COVID-19 & cholera)11) Advocacy for the in-depth situation and needs assessment of laboratory infrastructure status and technical equipment needs to be allied to control and prevention of infections at the health unit level12) Support partners’ service delivery by working to identify and respond to priority needs and gaps, including participation in joint assessments and revitalization of district coordination forums13) Reinforce the integrated mobile brigade approach for easy reaching of the IDP population and continuous provision of a comprehensive set of health services,14) Strengthen MHPSS services at health facility level.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The protection risks for all the women and girls, boys and men, will be identified through the focused group discussion for each group. The AAP approach will be included in all the integrated/join mission assessments by partners. WHO through the health cluster will work closely with the protection cluster to understand the protection concerns outlined in their periodic field visits. The findings will be used to ensure the participation of everyone in project implementation. Staff deployed to all locations will be sensitized on WHO non-discriminatory policy and all humanitarian core principles.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The persons with disabilities will be identified through screening while health services are being provided. Responders will be trained on how to screen persons with disability and categorize them by age and gender and document them in the appropriate registers. Community-based APEs will be oriented on screening persons with disability for enrolment into the targeted health interventions such as mass vaccination campaigns and provision of health interventions

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project activities that may impact the environment include the generation of injection wastes during outreach consultations or measles, polio, and cholera vaccination campaigns. These activities have well-outlined mitigation measures or policies on waste management using safety boxes and incineration processes in place. The implementation of activities within this project will be conducted in line with protocols that protect the environment from the disposal of hazardous waste.
