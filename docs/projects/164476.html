Project Summary

Shafak protection strategy in 2020 aims to strengthen protection interventions to support the overall protection cluster objectives in addition to sub-clusters GBV towards the provision as well as the response of integrated GBV specialized services with focusing on most vulnerable people such as adolescent girls, elderly and PwDs. Shafak through this project will continue to support GBV activities within women and girls safe space (WGSS) centers called Makany at Maarat Misrin, and Qurqania, in addition to two community center called Makany and Alkarameh at Idleb city; also, within RH facility in Maarat Misrin; finally, at Azaz reception center at Azaz city. Shafak will deepen the commitment to protection programming, to projects that support GBV, Women Empowerment, and social GBV advocacy projects to support women and girls at risk in NW Syria. The GBV delivery points will be able to provide response and mitigation services to a total of 5000 women and girls on a monthly basis. The project activities will include prevention, mitigation, and response GBV activities where the outreach teams under prevention/mitigation activities will be responding across the locations, wherever the need arises by conduct the following activities:
1- safety audits in the sector of GBV informed camps in the target locations (two camps each location, quarterly bases),
2- PSS non-structures sessions where the recreational activities will be part of PSS (a total of 2000 girls, 2000boys, 500 women, and 200 men will be targeted),
3- GBV awareness-raising sessions/campaign to target women, men, boys, and girls (the teams will use GBV tool kit who develop by GBV sub-cluster as a method to target men and adolescent boys) a total of 1000 girls, 500 boys, 1000 women, and 200 men will be targeted),
4- Furthermore, as part of response activates the outreach teams will conduct Psychological First Aid (PFA) within camps, reception centers, and other IDPs collective places.
On other hands, the centers teams will deliver comprehensive GBV services including prevention, mitigation, and with focus on GBV specialized response services as follow:
1- PSS structured /non-structured services (2000 women, and 1500 Girls),
2- Women and girls’ empowerment activates (life skills, vocational training) (2000 women, and 500 girls),
3- Young mothers club activity (500 girls),
4- GBV case management (there will be three caseworkers each center and they deal with 60 GBV cases each month),
5- Individual and group counseling. Shafak will engage men and boys in GBV prevention activities by mobile GBV activities, which aimed to mitigate the GBV risks, besides, to make the community more supportive of GBV issues.
Shafak GBV response strategy is in line with the protection needs overview and in coordination with the GBV Sub-Cluster. Shafak is committed to coordinate with women-led organizations in the target locations who operating GBV projects and respond closely with their needs specialty technical GBV capacity building training.

4 - Needs

The impact of Syria crisis for 2019 on women and girls is still wide where women continue to be exposed to many risks, including limited access to reproductive health and GBV services, while girls suffer problems no less than women such as forced/ early marriage, and education denial. GBV continues to impact the lives of women and girls, with adolescent girls, women-headed households, especially those divorced and widowed, bearing the brunt of the crisis. The fear of sexual harassment, including on the way to school, in the market, and at distribution sites, as well as sexual violence, often associated with kidnapping, is a concern raised by consulted women and girls and contributes to psychosocial stress and limits their movements. Additionally, various means of technology, such as texting and social media, are used to sexually harass and facilitate early/forced marriages. Adolescence, in particular, is a time when exposure to new forms of violence increases. GBV emergency assessment report (May-Aug 2019) has indicated that 69% of interviewed KIs in three districts (Harim- Idleb- Jebel Samaan) reported that there has been an increase in security incidents affecting women and girls since the onset of the emergency in North West Syria (NWS). Moreover, Voices from Syria report for 2019 findings that GBV – particularly sexual violence and sexual harassment, domestic violence, family violence against women and girls, and early/forced marriage – continues to pervade the lives of women and girls, particularly adolescent girls. Women, girls, boys, and men have confirmed that GBV occurs in homes, at schools and universities, in the market, and on the street. According to FGD participants – and in line with global trends – movement restriction was the primary obstacle to accessing GBV-specialized services. These constraints limit women's and girls’ access to services, humanitarian aid and generally infringe upon their wider human rights.

5 - Implementation of activities

Project activities will be delivered through existing five GBV delivery points, in addition to GBV activities within RH facility as an Integration with health/SRH, in addition to mobile teams will be formed in each center. The project activities will include prevention, mitigation, and response GBV activities; where the outreach teams will be conducting Safety audits in the sector of GBV in formed camps, PSS non structures sessions, and GBV awareness raising sessions/campaign (the teams will use GBV tool kit develop by GBV sub-cluster as a method to target Male, and adolescent boys) in addition to (PFA) to target newly IDPs. The centers teams will deliver focused GBV specialized services as follow: PSS structured /non-structured services, Women and girls’ empowerment activates, Young mothers club, GBV case management, and Individual/group counseling. Shafak will conduct GBV mainstreaming training to target 40 humanitarian works. Shafak will train their newly recruited staff on CMR, GBV SOPs, GBV risk mitigation, referral pathways, and case management principles. Moreover, to ensure strengthened collaboration between GBV delivery points, and health facility Shafak will use a referral pathway to refer GBV cases between the existing GBV delivery points and RH services. The RH staff will train on the GBV basics concept and PFA. To ensure the inclusion of Persons with Disability (PWDs) at the WGSS Shafak will rehabilitate the centers to be friendly with PWDs, also the project staff will attend online training on PWDs. From previous experience, Shafak will deal with the male survivor according to the principle of survivor-center approach where the male survivor will determine the gender of the case manager, and the location of the case management. Finally, MEAL activities will included feedback and pre/post-tests for PSS activities, in addition to applying the complaints mechanism. the GBV supervising activity will be in plac to measure the quality of GBV case management
