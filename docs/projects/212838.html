Project Summary

HRO aims to continue providing CP services for IDPs & HC in our already existing CCs in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur & Darkosh), besides planning to establish new CCs (Maaret Tamsrin, Harim, Dana, Afrin & Akhtarin) in Idleb and Aleppo, and new CFSs in Badama & Suran, & through outreach visits. HRO will target children with CP CM, including emergency case fund, CVA, & IPA to reduce the cases of child labor, CAAFAG, exploitation & other issues that they may be exposed to, based on HRO's previous experience in providing CP services in emergencies (CPFP of emergency response in Darkosh).
HRO will train employees & provide services targeting children, their families, caregivers, & local community, including IDPs, HC & the most vulnerable groups such as, UASC children, CwDs, and those who are at risk of protection through awareness-raising activities for community & caregivers on CP, parenting programs, PSS structured & semi-structured for children to enhance flexibility & adaptation skills for them, and several structured programs to children & parents together. CM for children is in line with SOPs, including CM of UASC based on FTR standards, & UNICEF for response, reunification, or alternative care support.
6 community committees for children & adults will be established (10 members each) and will be trained on basic CP and identification & referral. Also, humanitarian actors will be trained on CP minimum standards.
M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, and regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the field level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be conducted to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
HRO will provide transportation & adapt the activities & centers to fit the needs of PwDs & older people through installing the needed tools such as ramps, toilet rails, extra lighting in the corridors & WCs for those with poor vision, etc., in addition to targeting them in all project activities including IPA & case fund.
48,000 BNFs (31,200 IDPs & 16,800 HC), (26,400 F, 21,600 M), (1000 Young Children, 20,000 Children, 23,000 Adults, 4,000 Elderly) of them 2,400 PwD, through 8 CCs and 2 CFSs by 50 CP staff (30 outreach, 20 case managers). There will be 3 CP supervisors to supervise case management teams ensuring quality performance, staff well-being, & the regular monitoring & improvement of the project’s progress.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

After ensuring the preparedness of the project logistics & recruiting procedures, staff will be trained on CP basics, CP CM, PFA, PSS, and inclusion of PwDs. Afterward, we will conduct awareness raising on CP-related topics, CAAFAG, & including children with disabilities in center & outreach activities, PSS & structured PSS activities for children, conducting CP monitoring assessments in the target communities, training widows’ camps managers & security & safety officers on CP prevention & referral system, besides training women committees in widows’ camps on CP prevention, PSS, referral, & suicide prevention. HRO will target separated children outside camps with CP CM, including emergency case fund, CVA, & IPA to reduce the cases of child labor, CAAFAG, exploitation & other issues that they may be exposed to.
