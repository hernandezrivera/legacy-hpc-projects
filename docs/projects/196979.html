Project Summary

The goal of the proposal is to promote inclusive education in 3 Regions in Cameroon (West, Littoral, Central) on a 12 months pilot project to be replicated – later on, with following interventions - to the whole Nation, prior further evaluation.
Beneficiaries of the action will be Children with Disabilities, vulnerable children and IDPs, currently not attending or drop out from the education system.
As a first step the initiative will target schools, Disability People Organizations (DPOs) and local NGOs staff promoting ToT sessions by international experts to create a solid and permanent group of trainers and expertise on inclusive education and education in emergency, able to replicate and multiplicate the competences and the results on the ground. Will follow a vast training program targeting at least 60 schools in the 3 target Regions to create and consolidate the skills on inclusive education and education in emergency on the teaching staff. Specialized services with additional staff (coaches, Peer Counselors, Educators, Supervisors) will be activated involving local DPOs and NGOs to support the school educational path on inclusive education for CwDs, IDPs and vulnerable children. Education accessibility for CwDs will also be improved through structural rehabilitation and proper equipment for schools. The mobility for CwDs and vulnerable children will be strengthen through purchase of special vehicles, especially in rural areas. Technical laboratories will be set up and managed by local DPOs for the purchase, maintenance, personalization of aids for CwDs attending the schools. International exchange and additional training program will be put in place between Cameroonian teachers and Italian, Palestinian schools and DPOs. Learning materials will be distributed towards the most vulnerable students. A system of grants will also be promoted toward schools, training institutions and the most vulnerable families and children.
A research on the ground, involving Cameroonians and Italian Universities will be implemented with the involvement of beneficiaries, stakeholders, partners and institutions, as a baseline for the identification of a pilot Strategic Plan for the improvement of inclusive education in the whole Nation of Cameroon.
Awareness and sensitization initiatives will be realized to fight the stigma on CwDs and vulnerable children and to share knowledge, potentialities and result of the inclusive education program.
The work methodology sees EducAid leading a consortium of local and international entities with competences on inclusive education, services implementation and research activities, focused on a multidisciplinary approach.

- RIDS (Italian Network for Disability and Development) is a network promoting rights for PwDs in Italy and in Developing Countries. EducAid is a founding member of RIDS. RIDS is working in partnership with EducAid in Palestine, El Salvador, Kenya, Italy and in the international cooperation sector with other international NGOs promoting inclusion and PwDs rights. On the present proposal will realize training programs and remote follow up on ToT on school inclusive education for CwDs.
- CEIS. Inclusive school and educative center promoting innovative approach for the full inclusion of CwDs. It is also promoting training and capacity building programs towards other schools and in the framework of international cooperation projects. It is a co-founder of EducAid with whom is working in partnership for the promotion of inclusive education in Palestine, San Salvador, Kenya and other developing countries. On the present initiative will implement ToT on coaching services to promote inclusive education in pre-school and primary schools
- International Living Centre: Palestinian DPOs managed by PwDs, promoting services, Human Rights and inclusive education for PwDs in Palestine. Co-founded by EducAid, it is the only Palestinian NGOs started up and managed by PwDs for PwDs. An international exchange is foreseen for training implementation, tools individuation, assessment and workshops on inclusive education, also with the aim of good practices exchange in the framework of complex contexts.
- Vento di Terra and ALA: Italian NGOs implementing humanitarian project in Cameroon since 2008, with specific expertise on education in emergency and inclusion of vulnerable people such as: IDPS, conflict victims, drug abuse victims, prostitution and sexuality related problems, psycho-social issues. On the present initiative will implement inclusive education training and services for vulnerable children and school support activities for a full educative inclusion.
- Universities: EducAid is historically collaborating with Bologna, Firenze and Macerata Universities in the realization of research activities, Strategic Plan, Policy Papers in developing countries for the promotion of rights and services for PwDs. It is also foreseen the involvement of local universities such as Yaounde, Douala, Dschang - among others, for the realization of the research and Strategic Plan on inclusive education in Cameroon.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Cameroon defines children with special educational needs (Acts No. 2010/003 and 2005/006) as children with significant learning difficulties due to some form of disability/disadvantage. This category includes children from areas that are remote and far away from schools; displaced, disadvantaged and poor populations; children from marginalized populations; nomads; and students in overcrowded classrooms. It refers as well to children with disabilities; children belonging to an ethnic minority; street children; children from displaced or refugee families; children living with HIV or AIDS; children affected by psycho-social and behavioral problems (drug and sexual abuse among others).The needs assessment of the present proposal was carried out in partnership with ANOPHAC, coordination body of Cameroonian NGOs/DPOs dealing with disabilities, with the involvement of the University of Yaounde - Special Education Department, and on the basis of the work developed by the Task Force on Inclusive Education led by UNICEF and the Ministries of Education. In addition to the involvement of partner NGOs, in particular present in the West and Littoral Regions in relation to the (lack) of educational inclusiveness of IDPs and other children affected by specific problems, such as, between others: victims of prostitution, stigma, drug abuse and in situations of psycho-social problems.In the three target regions (West, Central and Littoral), there are serious problems in relation to educational inclusion. Schools (pre-schools, primary and secondary) are often inadequate both in terms of structure, equipment and competences of the staff to welcome children with disabilities in an inclusive way. The families with children with disabilities, as well, do not have the resources to promote the inclusion of their children, due to lack of knowledge, finance, transportation and general awareness. For the most part, CwDs do not have adequate and personalized aids also capable of promoting their inclusive path. There is an overall lack of services, skills and means to support CwDs and their families.Furthermore, the large presence of IDPs, particularly in the Littoral and West regions, has put the educational pathways of the most vulnerable children at serious risk, exposing them, in connection with a multi-problem situation linked to extreme poverty and to the ongoing conflict in the regions at the border with Nigeria. Issues such as child prostitution, spread of STDs, drug abuse, find a fertile ground in such situations. The lack of adequate services, counseling, referrals, psycho-social support, results in a lack of positive response to multiple problems and the concomitant drop out and/or non-inclusion in educational paths. CwDs in groups of IDPs and individuals with psychosocial problems are the most exposed to this situation.Stigma it is also a relevant issue, hitting family with CwDs, IDPs, family and children with vulnerable situation. Social stigma is an obstacle to the social and education inclusion, based on fear, ignorance and lack of understanding of complex situation.From a strategic point of view it is also underlined a lack of a general Strategic Plan, based on research and involvement on the ground of Disability People Organizations, NGOs, PwDs, family with PwDs members and vulnerable situations, school staff ect… to design a path to improve Inclusive Education in Cameroon. Covid 19 it might still an issue to focus on, on the side and together at the up mentioned situations.
