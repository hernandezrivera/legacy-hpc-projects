Project Summary

Nigeria recorded her first case of COVID-19 on 27th February,2020 and as at 2nd May, 2020, 2,388 cases have been confirmed with Borno having 75 cases of the total cases.
The purpose of the intervention intends to provide basic awareness and sensitization on the Covid-19 Pandemic and precautionary measure to bridge the gap in containing the spread of the Corona Virus. this shall be done in the community and IDP camps. There shall be a strategy to Monitor the impact of the COVID 19 situation on the mental well-being of children to enable planning for post-pandemic MHPSS services based on lessons learnt. In addition to the positive parenting skills sessions, support shall be provided to parents/caregivers to help them cope with anxiety and stress.
With the emergence of COVID-19 pandemic, the already fragile humanitarian setting will be exaggerated because humanitarian settings pose a significant risk to disease prevention/control and overcrowding coupled with poor hygiene and food security as obtainable in Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) Camps will make compliance to the prescribed global preventative measures to curb spread of COVID-19 difficult to implement. Also measures to curb spread will lead to loss of livelihood for low income earners, diversion of resources to the pandemic with its antecedent effect on limited access to services like the Psychosocial support and case management for children.

With the current global crises, Relief Rescue Initiative intends to bridge the gap in high risk communities/settlements(IDP Camps) for spread of COVID -19 in Borno State, through clear and consistent messages on the preventive measures and hand/respiratory hygiene especially in this COVID-19 Pandemic as well Monitoring the impacts of the pandemic on the well being of the children at risk and most vulnerable.
The project will span for 6months with an estimated budget of 10,000 USD while ensuring it aligns with the child protection sub working group long term goals for children.
