4 - Needs

The sector animal Production in Syria is considered one of the most important economic sectors that many citizens depend on, of all kinds (sheep, cows, goats, buffalo, and poultry)..Livestock witnessed a serious decline, which led to a significant decline in their numbers during the nine years of the war,According to the statistics of the Ministry of Agriculture and Agrarian Reform in the government of the Syrian regime, the number of sheep declined from 18 million heads in 2012 to 15 million heads in 2018.The number of cows decreased from 1 million heads to 300 thousand heads during the same period, while the number of goats decreased from 2. million to 1,500 million heads during the same period.As for the numbers of poultry, they decreased from 25 million birds to 17 million birds.Thus, meat production decreased from 400 thousand tons to 300 thousand tons, milk production decreased from 200 million tons to two million tons, and egg production increased from three billion eggs to one billion eggs during the same period.The north of Idlib and the north of Aleppo is a region that relies mainly on agriculture and livestock, and its people have good experience in this field, but because of the war that has been going on for 9 years, the proportion of breeders has decreased to a third, meaning that the number of current breeders is approximately 30% of their number before 2011 .Unfortunately, as a result of the massive displacement during the previous period, the number of breeders increased in a narrow area (northern Syria) and in the camps, without an increase in pasture. . This resulted in a decrease in the number of sheep headsAnd he pushed the breeders to reduce their livestock numbers because there was no space for grazing and the inability to buy feed
