Project Summary

Relief International through the food security and livelihoods support to conflict and flood affected households in upper Nile State project seeks to alleviate the suffering of 4,904 vulnerable community resulting from natural shocks such as floods, occurrence of crop pest and disease outbreaks and drought besides man-made shocks such as conflict. Thus, the project intends to create livelihoods opportunities and alleviate the suffering of 4,904 vulnerable households in four (04) counties of Upper Nile State (Maban, Longechuk, Melut and Nasir) through enhancing access to agricultural production assets (inputs) through conducting targeted distribution of grain crop (main season) and vegetable kits (dry season) response as well as establishment of kitchen garden both at household and selected facility nutrition sites to address the anticipated critical malnutrition situation as projected in April to June 2025 in 2024 to 2025 report. The project as well seeks to create economic opportunities and diversification of income among communities by promoting asset creation among community through enhancing livestock re-stocking. this intervention envisages to reduce over reliance on humanitarian aid, contributing to household economic incomes to 400 persons in the four counties of Upper Nile State (Maban, Longechuk, Melut and Nasir). Relief International intervention seeks to promote agricultural production through enhancing access to quality agricultural information by training 120 community-based extension workers on Agricultural extension for improved agricultural production and minimize post-harvest loses. In addition, the food security and livelihoods support to conflict and floods affected households in Upper Nile state seeks to enhance vulnerable people’s capacity to withstand the impact of shock through creation of skilling opportunities hence Relief International seeks to Provide Technical and Vocational Training (TVET) opportunities to 400 youth and women on market driven vocational skills supported with start-up kits, the vocational trades include plumbing, electrical installation, blacksmithing and catering and bakery. Relief International intends to Support and strengthen the local capacity of four (04) DRR committees by enhancing early natural disaster alertness, preparedness and response mechanism through the anticipatory Actions (DRR, Climate change adaptation) to minimize the impacts of floods and promote resilience in the four counties of Upper Nile state. the anticipatory seeks to facilitate the dissemination of downscaled climate and flood early warning information through radio programs, including radio shows and solar radios as well as supporting social safety nets and labor-intensive cash for work community led anticipatory Actions (DRR, Climate change adaptation) initiatives.

1. Needs

1. Limited access to food and income as revealed by a Relief International FSL needs assessment conducted in September 2024, the assessment revealed that 70% of communities in Maban, Longechuk and Melut reported not having enough food and having widespread food insecurity with 71% in Maban and 17% in Longechuk. 2. Acute malnutrition rate as revealed in 2024 to 2025 IPC report, this has resulted into poor food consumption in Maban, Luakpiny/Nasir and Longechuk. the main drivers include flooding resulting into low crop yields while inadequate access to quality agricultural productive inputs such as seeds. 3. Limited number of households owning livestock due to economic constraints yet there is a strong reliance on animal products such as meat and dairy products being consumed by the communities of Maban, Longechuk and Melut, Relief International needs assessment reveals that 24% of Longechuk and Melut communities consume animal products. 4. Limited training opportunities on greens skills to boast income generation activities. 4. Over reliance on crop production as the main source of livelihoods5. Frequent occurrence of flooding in the area affecting lives and livelihoods as revealed by the IPC 2024 to 2025 IPC reported being reported as the main drivers of food insecurity. The above aforementioned gaps and needs are as of the 2024 to 2025 IPC report and Relief International FSL needs assessment conducted in the month of September in the three counties of Upper Nile state counties of Maban, Longechuk and Melut.

2. Project activities

Relief International September 2024 FSL needs assessment revealed that 70% of households in Maban county reported insufficient food while 12% reported facing severe shortages thus Relief International seeks to enhance access to agricultural production assets to 2,500 farmers through input distribution to promote self-reliance, 600 (300 in Maban, 100 in Longechuk, 100 in Melut and 100 in Nasir) with vegetable seeds (Okra, jute mallow, eggplant, Amaranthus) for dry season response while 1,900 (700 in Maban, 400 in Longechuk, 400 in Nasir and 400 in Melut) with grain seed for main season response, the inputs will target 85% female farmers having access to cultivatable land. Relief International will as well support the establishment of 250 (100 in Maban, 50 in Longechuk, 50 in Melut and 50 in Nasir) kitchen gardens at households and community sites, the kitchen gardens will target children and mothers discharged from the nutrition units. Relief International seeks to Promote Technical Vocational and Educational training (TVET) to 400 youth and women in market driven TVET trades such as Mansory, plumbing, welding and metal works fabrication. the project will target youth between the age of 18 to 40 with female given higher consideration. Relief international will as well enhance the capacity of unity-based extension agricultural extension workers targeting 120 persons. Relief International FSL needs assessment reveals that 36% and 30% of households in Maban and Melut respectively own livestock while 49% of households report no livestock, reflecting economic constraints, particularly for refugees thus Relief International seeks to Promoting livestock re-stocking among livestock farmers to 400 (100 in Maban, 60 in Longechuk, 80 in 80 in Nasir), each farmer will be given 2 goats (1 billy and 1 Nany) to enhance income diversification. Relief International seeks to strengthen early warning signs alertness, preparedness and response through cash for work community led initiatives and interventions. Relief International will support 140 (50 in Maban, 30 in Longechuk, 30 in Melut and 30 in Nasir) with 2.7 USD per day for a period of 15 days for a duration of 4 months to enhance dyke creation, water channel opening to protect farmlands for an enhanced crop production.
