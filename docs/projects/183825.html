Project Summary

The Darfur region of Sudan has suffered from protracted conflict since 2003, resulting in the internal displacement of approximately 2.1 million individuals, creating a vacuum in the provision of basic services by local authorities, and contributing to the deterioration of social cohesion. With the full withdrawal of the United Nations-African Union Mission in Darfur (UNAMID) in 2021, intercommunal violence in all five states has increased. Flooding is a seasonal occurrence in Sudan and as it contributes poverty , as well it negatively affects the shelter and NFI sectors, thus the need protection is high. The heavy rains caused damage to houses, livestock, farmland, infrastructure, and services. Flood water and damage were recorded high in all States in Sudan. Inflation and currency depreciation have led to shortages of fuel and cash, prices increases of imported key commodities, a reduced household purchasing power.
Number of People in need are 4.2 m, the gender segregation is Boys 2.4M 26% Girls 2.9M 32% Men 1.6M 17% Women 1.9M 20% according to. (Sudan Humanitarian Overview 8 August 2020). ( Sudan Humanitarian overview December 2020).
The Government has established a cash based transfer ( THAMARAT) for all Sudanese states but it has not covered all the people yet also it is very small grant which is equal to $5 per month for each person.
Newly arriving IDPs come mostly without their belongings and are in dire needs of non-food items and shelter to restore dignity and address emergency response needs. Additionally, IDP in protracted situations require replenishments of NFIs based on protection assessment/findings, as access to livelihoods is limited. Furthermore, IDP and venerable Host communities require shelter assistance, both in terms of emergency and transitional shelter.
In this Proposal AOSCD is planning and seeking durable solutions , AOSCD is to link between Protection of IDPs, Returnees and Venerable host Communities by providing a ES/NFIs assistance intervention to reinforce dignity and to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals SDG by the following measures under the supervision and donation through Sudan CBPF - SHF:
• Distribution of NFI kits (new arrival kit; replenishment kit (every two years); personal hygiene kits; soap (every quarter or six months for the latter); from core pipeline based on assessment.
• Distribution of emergency shelter kits;
• Distribution of permanent shelter upgrade kits, or permanent shelter kits; mad by Cement SSB ( Stabilizer Solid Bricks)
• Supervision and direction of community members to construct their own shelter
• Post-distribution monitoring;
• Planting and distribution of 3,000 seedlings to support the environment
• Provide full basket of NFIs
