Project Summary

This intervention aims to promote a holistic approach for the well-being of children, with a special focus on internally displaced (IDP) girls and adolescents in the districts of Ancuabe and Metuge. For this purpose, the project incorporates a multisectoral approach including Protection and WASH in Education to address the most urgent needs of almost 18,000 children in 7 schools in Metuge and Ancuabe districts. Outcome 1 seeks to increase the resilience and preparedness of identified schools towards disasters and conflict-related social and emotional effects through Psychoscocial Support (PSS) and Child Protection trainings of school administrations, teachers, students and caregivers. In 2023, a total of 212 school administrations, teachers, members of school councils and emergency focal points of the Education, Youth and Technology District Services (SDEJT) and the Health, Women and Social Action District Services (SDSMAS) received specific training on PSS and Psychological First Aid (PFA) related to emergency and crisis preparedness and response. For 2024, these previously trained teachers and members of the school councils will mentor a group of selected students from each participating school to become Ambassadors of the "I support my friends" initiative, through which these Ambassadors will be trained on Protection and PFA to create a peer-to-peer support network for classmates in distress. Under the supervision of their mentors, the Ambassadors will also lead school gorups to conduct PSS activities while conducting awareness raising on topics such as Child Protection, Mental Health, Prevention of GBV, and more. The school gourps will also create Do It Yourself stress-relief kits that the children will take home for use in emergencies that could potentially trigger the anxiety. Outcome 2 will entail the continuation of the capacity building of the schools in hygiene promotion. For this purpose, in addition to the two Water Committees that were trained on 2023, IsraAID training 5 more Water Committees from the rest of the schools, including teachers, members of the school councils, focal points from the SDEJT and the Infrastructure Planification District Services (SDPI) and students. The Watter Committees will be in charge of promoting good hygiene practices in the school and the communities and will be responsible for the technical maintenance of the water and sanitation infrastructures in their respectives schools. The hygiene promotion will activities will include a strong spproach on gender and disability inclusion .

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

All the activities will be conducted in both districts of implementation, namely Ancuabe and Metuge districts. Outcome 1: 17,980 children in 7 schools in Metuge and Ancuabe have improved psychosocial well-being Output 1.1.: Capacity of 220 teachers and 21 school administrators to promote children’s well-being and resilience is enhanced, and they are able to provide adequate first response to Child Protection violations (through trainings, mentoring and supervision, and other follow-up activities) Activity 1.1.1. Training, coaching, and monitoring support, including quarterly 2-day refresher sessions with the trained 35 Focal Points from Phase I, including school administrators, teachers and representatives from district services; in addition, 1-day follow-up support visits to the 185 teachers trained in CWB Phase 1 at their respectives schools.Activity 1.1.2.: Child Protection and MHPSS awareness-raising activities with 5,993 caregivers and other community members in consultation with school and community leaders, including the use of visibility materials at schools. Output 1.2.: 14 teachers, 14 members of school councils and 70 students acquire knowledge and tools to provide effective Psychological First Aid (PFA) to other students ("I support my friends"). Activity 1.2.1.: 4-day training of 28 selected teachers and members of the schools´ councils to become Mentors of the “I support my friends” initiative (4 per school), including the design of a mentorship action plan and timeline for the teachers to accompany the selected ambassadors of the project. Activity 1.2.2.: Creation of 7 School Groups led by the Ambassadors to promote contents and activities around Child Protection, Child Rights, MHPSS, prevention of GBV, well being, resilience building, and more, and to provide direct peer-to-peer support to classmates in distress. Activity 1.2.3.: 1-day quarterly refresher sessions with the 28 mentors and the 70 Ambassadors to review the contents of the training, share their experiences and address questions and challenges faced. Outcome 2: 7 WASH Committees effectively promote WASH standards and behaviors. Output 2.1.: 7 WASH Committees improve their own skills and knowledge. Activity 2.1.1.: Identification and selection of 100 members for 5 new WASH Committees (20 per school), and 4 days of training on WASH, including hygiene promotion. Activity 2.1.2.: Quartlery 700 KAP surveys to identify needs and the results to be used to design hygiene promotion activities and continuously train the 7 WASH Committees. Activity 2.1.3.: Quarterly community-based hygiene promotion sessions and activities targeting students and caregivers. Activity 2.1.4.: Quarterly 2-day refresher trainings with the 7 WASH Committees to review contents from the training, share experiences, address questions, and discuss challenges faced.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The PFA and PSS training will strengthen the overall community resilience and will provide the schools, the teachers, the students and the caregivers with the adequate tools to appropriately support children and adolescents dealing with negative social and emotional experiences derived from conflict-related and other forms of violence. Through the capavity building sessions, school personnel is going to be prepared to identify cases that need to be referred to specialized services and to raise awareness about protection-related issues amongst the caregivers and the overall communities. The child-led peer-to-peer networks for supporting friends in distress will facilitate access to PSS services and vital information for the students. Throughout all the capacity building sessions, gender and prevention of GBV, and inclusion of people with disabilities will be transversal themes.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project incorporates a robust gender approach in all of its components. All the trainings and quartlery refresher training sessions include a specific module on Gender-Based Violence to be able to build the sensitiveness of the trainees around these topics and for them to always apply a survivor centered approach. The training will also build their capacity to identify girls and adolescents that might be survivors to provide them with the right information and tools and knowledgeand the support in case they would like to be referred to the correspondent service provider. Through the “I support my friends” initiative, it will also be promoted female participation and leadership, encouraging girls to become Ambassadors. Finally, through the trained WASH Committees and the hygiene promotion activities, there will be awareness raising on the associated risks for women and girls to certain types of activities (e.g.: fetching water from afar, no light in latrines, etc.) for the community leaders and other relevant stakeholders to adress them within their respective communities. The hygiene promotion activitieswill include Menstrual Hygiene Management targeting girls and caregivers

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In order to remove barriers, the Child Protection and PSS trainings will include a module on disability inclusion, in which they will receive specific orientations on how to deliver tailored PSS support to children with disabilities. The peer-to-peer PFA initiative will also promote Ambassorship among children with disabilities. All Ambassadors will receive training on inclusiveness and tolerance towards children with different needs and backgrounds and encourage them to reach out and support peers with disabilities. Also, during awareness raising and hygiene promotion sessions, community members (including community leaders) and caregivers will be encouraged to advocate for inclusive WASH infrastructure design.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The Water Committees will be trained to ensure appropriate water management at the community level.
