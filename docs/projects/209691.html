Project Summary

The project aims to provide primary and secondary health services, in addition to raising community awareness through a medical point in the eastern countryside. The village of Tal Zubaida and its neighboring villages, in addition to a mobile medical team to reach places far from the medical point. For the most needy groups, the displaced and affected, and for all groups and ages, especially women. Children, the elderly, people with disabilities. These are the most vulnerable groups that are exposed to various types of exploitation. This is done by meeting the needs that lead to their protection, especially women and children who are most vulnerable to exploitation and societal gender-based violence. The project provides services in a fair manner that guarantees them protection and opportunities to improve the health status that gives them life. Happy families and strong, healthy bodies protect them from all kinds of exploitation. Especially after the deterioration of the economic, social and humanitarian situation in Syria. The eastern countryside in general suffers from an almost complete absence of medical services and health workers and a severe shortage of healthy drinking water, as village residents depend on unsanitary transported water. The high cost of transportation to the city of Homs, which constitutes a great burden on the people of the eastern countryside. Inability to purchase medicines, especially after economic inflation, which has led to an increase in disease cases among different groups and ages. Through the medical services provided in the project, beneficiaries are given opportunities for a decent life, and meeting health requirements helps them return to practicing their normal lives, which ensures protection for them from the risks of exploitation, limiting the spread of diseases, enjoying the strength of bodies capable of working, and returning to doing business. To earn a living and meet life's requirements in a new and effective way.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The health situation in Syria remains generally poor, and more than 14 million people will need life-saving primary and secondary care services in 2024, including the most vulnerable groups (girls, women, children, the elderly and the disabled). Most primary and secondary care facilities are partially or non-functional, based on the Hormuz sector report in Syria. If they are functional, they do not provide services related to non-communicable diseases and mental health, despite their prevalence in the population. The continued economic and social deterioration and the earthquake incident led to the spread of mental illnesses, as the World Health Organization report in Syria stated that one in ten lives in a moderate state of mental health, especially children. There is a widespread spread of infectious diseases for various reasons. The absence of a health system led to an increase in the death rate, according to the cholera report sitrep/wos awd.Most of the population suffers from the inability to obtain medicines due to the significant increase in their prices, as well as the inability to obtain the necessary medical consultations. The project aims to provide primary and secondary health services, in addition to raising social awareness by opening a medical point in the eastern countryside. In the village of Tal Zubaida, there are clinics (children's, women's, internal medicine), in addition to psychological support providers and raising community awareness through community awareness activities and lectures. . Services include all neighboring villages in addition to a mobile medical team to reach remote places. For the most vulnerable groups, especially women. Children, the elderly, people with disabilities. Which are exposed to different types of exploitation. This is done by meeting the needs that lead to their protection, especially women and children who are most vulnerable to exploitation and gender-based violence. Services are provided to them in a fair manner that guarantees them protection and opportunities to improve their health status, and gives them a healthy life that protects them from all types of exploitation. Especially after the deterioration of the economic, social and humanitarian situation in Syria. The eastern countryside in general suffers from an almost complete absence of medical services and a severe shortage of healthy drinking water. The high cost of transportation to the city of Homs constitutes a great burden on the people of the eastern countryside. Inability to purchase medicines, resulting in an increase in medical conditions. Through the medical services provided in the project, the beneficiaries are given decent life opportunities, and meeting health requirements helps them return to practicing their normal lives and protecting vulnerable groups from all types of exploitation.
