Project Summary

The most needy with vulnerable host communities and IDPs in Somalia will be covered by this project. Kismayo hosts some of the most vulnerable and protracted IDP in Somalia
,most facing risk of multiple displacement. Hobyo, Jariban and Badhadhe host rural communities that are far to reach and marginalised with some not able to access services for several years past (SAF UK 2019; FSNAU 2019; UNOCHA 2019). Most households in these areas have limited access to
health services and poor WASH status (REACH 2018) exacerbating disease and malnutrition. GAM rate is
above emergency threshold (FSNAU,2019). IYCF Indicators still score low in these areas (Save the children 2018; SAF UK, 2017) especially in Hobyo and Badhahde where such interventions have been lacking and few health partners offering maternal and child health service thus presenting missed opportunity to
strengthen IYCF at an early stage.
Hobyo and Jariban nutrition situation is critical for respective districts with aggravating
factors. The areas have recorded below average rains presenting scarcity of
water thus increased prevalence of water borne diseases like AWD /Cholera cases,low
vaccination coverage,limited access to health services and a critical Food security
situation. The situation expected to deteriorate for all areas if nothing is done especially if
rains fail again (FSNAU,2019;UNOCHA 2019).
SAF UK will therefore focus on life saving, Resilience building, protection activities in hard
to reach areas and most vulnerable populations e.g children under 5(Boys and girls),
women, elderly,HIV/TB patients, IDP and remote rural population. Activities that support prevention and treatment of malnutrition and resilience building for affected population will remain a primary focus.
This will tie in a strong community component that will promote community participation and Information
sharing, to improve access to those in need and provide a platform for surveillance. SAF UK has developed strong links with communities and conducts biannual community conversations with affected population including them in programming in all stages of the program cycle. The organization has developed community structures that ensure services are devolved to clusters of 15 households especially IDP, in line with organizations community strategy and Accountability to affected population (AAP) Framework.
Programs will integrate with health, WASH and food security and livelihood activities.This will include integration with basic health and maternal services, including Expanded program for immunization (EPI), BSFP, hygiene promotion including hygiene kits.The project will also seek to improve health seeking behavior though community mobilization. Significant others to the target beneficiaries, especially Men and grandmothers, will be targeted for health promotion. Children,adolescents and the youth who have traditionally been neglected when it comes to programing will be full included at all stages of the program cycle to ensure sustainability and mutual inclusivity. Peer support groups will also be included to promote peer learning. Multi sector approach that included involvement in multisector coordination forum like cluster coordination will be key to 2020 programs.
The project will employ and train staff (Men and women) including community workers(Men and women) through a transparent process. community volunteers (Men , women and youth of both sexes) will be engaged so as to ensure sustainability of interventions and inclusivity.
Government officials will take lead in most key decisions and will be involves through the cycle for sustainability and contribution to capacity building.
The programs will adhere to set government strategies and policies related to interventions , and will calso contribute to achievement of cluster 2020 HRP working closely with government entities and the cluster.

Mitigation Measures

For IDP the main risk is displacement. for this the organisation hasmaintained a database for all beneficiaries with telephone contacts and that ofrelatives. For those in rural Mudug the risk is spontenious clan clashes. While it isdifficult to deal with this, we have ensured that services are provided for the differentclans in their areas equitably. Interclan clashes therefore have minimal effect ourprograms. in all location the organisation will seek to provide safety nets byimplementing BSFP . Victims of GBV will also be provided with an organisational GBVfocal point who will link survivors to appropriate assistance. Programs will also ensureIDp have access to basic services in an equitable and dignified manner with equalityalso observed in job allocation in projects serving IDP population. Among IDP the riskof exclusion based on tribe is real. Most IDPs in Kismayo are from a certaincommunity who found it hard to access services in the recent past based on their tribeand skin color. The organisations' policy does not allow exclusion and actuallyreaches out to all community members to receive services. Recruitment of staff is notbased on tribe but professional competence. This has produced staff from a hybrid ofcultures who have learned to work together well. Some positions are also reserved tothe target communities and community groups that are marginalized like IDPs andwomen so that equity is achieved.All gender will be awarded opportunity to benefit in equitably. Women and youth whoare mostly left out in recruitment will be considered in all projects and positions will beawarded equitably. Needs assessment will include all community groups so that an allconclusive, agreeable and fair assessment is reached. both men and women leaderswith an additional youth representative will be part of community health board as perorganisational policy. This will ensure decisions made are all inclusive. SAF UKpolicies have sought to put men at the helm of needs affecting their wives, daughtersand female relatives. The organisation's APP framework seeks to ensure menparticipate through affirmative action for men ensuring that men are not excludedfrom interventions targeting women beneficiaries. Children who are often left out willnot only be seen as beneficiaries but their views on service delivery ad need willoccasionally be sought by a professional child specialist so that the whole communityparticipates in program design and implementation. Community diagnosis,community dialogues, evaluations and assessment will seek to include all communitygroups to ensure all are reached. Dialogues, evaluations and conversations will include women, children and youth with their own forum so as to ensure their views are considered and used to programing. 2020 programs will seek to improve access to women and girls , who are key beneficiaries of proposed interventions by scaling up services to be within a few kilometers walk. Far and hard to reach areas in rural settings will be served by mobile teams so as to reduce risks and hazards faced by these groups enroute to accessing services.

Please provide further explanation

In all proposed areas the environment is sufficiently safe to enable implementation ofresilience activities. All our projects have a good working relationship with the governmentand other relevant stakeholders. Some of our facilities were handed to the organisation bythe government and work under strict supervision of the same. Others run by the organisation are community owned but supervised by MoH, possessing long term agreements witnessed and endorsed by government. In all proposed districts, project involve government entities in all activities and receive several monitoring and supportive supervision visits. The organisation is also not only registered by government to operate in the locations but also have Memorandum of understanding with clear terms of engagement including relationship with government, commitment to serve and deliver, allegiance to total transparency and accountability, and commitment to deliver positive outputs and outcomes. The organisation also has strong working relationship with communities we serve working closely with all age groups who are involved right from needs assessment. In all areas, community health boards have been formed not only to represent the community in matters health but also provide leadership and guidance. Sustainability continues as our focus area in 2020 and the same is being entrachanced in our policies.As part of our policy and APP framework, beneficiaries are informed of their entitlement to quality service delivery and access to utilities as per project requirement. This aspect is currently operational with a strong, non- bias and functional feedback mechanism in place.
