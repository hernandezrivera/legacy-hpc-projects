Project Summary

The proposed intervention seeks to provide comprehensive emergency Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) services to IDPs residing in camps while improving access to water and sanitation facilities in surrounding communities. The primary purpose is to address the urgent needs of IDPs and HHs by ensuring they have access to safe drinking water, adequate sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion activities.
These interventions aim to mitigate health risks associated with inadequate WASH services, reduce the incidence of waterborne diseases, and enhance overall well-being in both camps and communities.
The overall target is 669402 beneficiaries (341396 male, 328006 female), including (294536 children and 133878 PWD), 59% of them are IDPs in camps and communities hosting displaced populations.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The intervention comprises three key components: 1. Emergency WASH Services for IDPs:This component involves providing full-package emergency WASH services within IDP camps. It includes delivering safe drinking water, installing sanitation facilities, solid waste management, Desludging, Latrine cleaning, and promoting hygiene to foster healthy practices among camp residents. 2. Water System Repair, Upgrading, and Rehabilitation: The project will focus on improving access to water by repairing, upgrading, and rehabilitating existing water systems in communities and camps. This rehabilitation for the water stations, providing and installing solar systems for the water stations, expanding and rehabilitation for water networks, and providing operation and maintenance support to ensure continuous functionality of water systems, besides providing capacity building for water unit staff on O&M and water safety plans and providing training on tariff collection system activation training. 3. Sanitation System Support: Access to sanitation services will be enhanced through the repair, rehabilitation, and upgrading of sanitation infrastructure, including sewage networks and wastewater treatment plants.
