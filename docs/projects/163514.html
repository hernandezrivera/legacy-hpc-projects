Project Summary

The Anglophone crisis that started in 2016 as a result of marginalization of the English speaking North West and South West Regions of Cameroon by the majority French speaking has intensify for close to three years now and has witness increasing killings by both the separatist arm men and government forces on men and youths. This has lead to the affected and non affected families to flee the area and seek refuge in nebouring towns and villages in the French speaking Regions in Cameroon. The increase population especially women and children is largely driven by poverty.
Women and girls make up about 52% of the Cameroon population and the gross inequalities and inhuman conditions they endure are both as a result of the crisis, the situation is particularly worst and presents real concerns for their fair treatment, access to food, livelihoods and income generating activities.
The situation of displaced women and girls from North West Region to Bangurian subdivision in Noun Division of the West Region is deplorable as they have indulged into prostitution and other forms of criminal behavior like stealing of food crops from host farms etc.
A critical element of hardship emanates from the women’s increasing roles as providers of basic needs or amenities to the members of their households
The Anglophone crises have created gross poverty and unemployment levels among the Internally Displaced persons (IDP) especially women and girls both in urban and rural communities in Banguran and Jakiriof Subdivisions the West and North West Regions. These challenges amongst other issues have precipitated a vicious cycle of poverty and hopelessness from which many women and families find impossible to overcome.
The project will focus on capacity building for agricultural production, livestock rearing and start up material support for women and girls to enhance job creation, livelihood and sustainable income generation. Women and girls are sexually abused because of their social and economical incapacities to meet up their needs because they are internally displaced persons in Banguran subdivision in Noun Division West Region and in Jakiri subdivision in Bui division of the North West Region.
The strategic Objective of this project is to Reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen resilience of 5000 of people (mostly women and girls) affected by crisis in Jakiri and Banguran subdivisions of the North West and West Regions of Cameroon.
Specific objective; By the end of 2021, the use of negative coping strategies is mitigated for 5000 vulnerable people affected by crisis through inputs, capital and skills for livelihood activities.
Specific objective indicator; 5000 of targeted people (female and male) that received assistance through agricultural, livestock and fishery support (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture)

1 - Besoins / Needs

The effects of displacement are often different for women, men, girls, and boys – each face unique protection risks within the humanitarian context. There are continued needs to consider alternative and flexible income- generating schemes to address livelihoods problems.The protracted Anglophone crises in the North West Region has highlighted unique challenges and opportunities facing humanitarian efforts suggesting a need for greater innovation to respond effectively to the evolving economic and protection challenges faced by displaced persons. Economic insecurity has increased the protection risk for the displaced as they seek income-generating opportunities, creating a need for a more integrated approach to livelihoods initiatives. In a community where 80% of the population depends on agriculture for livelihoods and food securities, the absence or shortage of short term innovative agricultural practices is a serious threat to livelihoods and food security for these vulnerable IDPs especially women and girls and their host communities.The project will work with women and youths to economically empower them by building their competencies and capacities to participate constructively in development processes through provision of training courses for income generating activities including Market gardening, cassava production and processing and small livestock/fishery processing and marketing.
