Project Summary

This project aims to establish 25 rooms for planting the hydroponic barley, and the room's capacity will be about 400 KG green fodder on daily basis according to many factors including controlling of humidity and climate inside the rooms. The criteria of selecting the beneficiaries will be according to their experience in cows breeding and the vulnerability factors including the women-household as a priority number one. The total daily production from hydroponic barley will be about 10,000 KG, this daily production of hydroponic barley will be distributed to a pre-selected 600 cows and 2000 sheep.
IYD will support the selected cows' breeders with pellet fodder for four months, and healthcare for the cows against the common diseases and treat the expected reproductive system’s problems for the cows to be able to get insemination during the project period, in addition, conducting the healthcare and reproductive follow up for the selected sheep to increase their numbers in the targeted areas.
IYD seeks through the project to help beneficiaries to secure income for their families and the dissemination of a culture of green fodder among livestock’ breeders through the following:
Define 25 beneficiaries, half properly headed by women based on a certain criterion to produce the hydroponic barley fodder on their rooms, each targeted family will provide a room 25 square meters as a contribution for the beneficiaries as per their capacities and willingness to enhance the cost-sharing idea.
IYD will rehabilitate these rooms by insulation them to control the humidity and temperature inside the rooms and provide them with the needed equipment such as the ventilation, electronic systems, and other tools like boxes with their iron holders to produce the hydroponic barley. Each room will produce about 400 KG of green fodder on daily basis, the total daily production resulted from the 20 rooms will be about 10,000 KG.
The other part of the project is to choose 600 cows and 2000 sheep which owned by vulnerable families (50% headed by women) and support them with inputs as the following:
1 –8 kg of the pellet on a daily basis for each selected cow to secure the required protein, energy, and minerals in the fodder ration.
2- Healthcare follow up for the 600 selected cows and for 2000 selected sheep, the treatment including the vaccine programs and follow up the daily diseases to improve the health situation.
3- Follow up the reproductive system for the selected cows and sheep and treat the diseases if any, to be able inseminating them to increase their numbers and the milk production in the targeted areas.
4- Distributing 10 kg of hydroponic barley fodder on a daily basis for the selected cows and 2 kg for the selected sheep to improve the health of the digestive and increasing the daily milk production and increasing the fertility of the flock after using the hydroponic barley.
The feeding of cows on hydroponic barley will cause many benefits to cows, where the production of milk for each cow will increase by 10%, but the most important benefit will be improving the health of cows, including the digestive system because the good effects when using the green fodder in feeding the cows which will improve the monthly income of the breeder through the reduction of monthly treatment expenses which paid by the breeder.
In addition, the green fodder is rich with minerals and vitamins which will resolve some problems of the reproductive system and motivate to get the new-born and will help the breeder to increase monthly income.

Needs

Now in its eighth year, the humanitarian crisis in Syria has led to the internal displacement of atleast 5.9 million people, and left an estimated 13 million in need of humanitarian assistance.According to the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) Food Security Sector 2018 Mid-YearReview, food security further deteriorated during the first six months of 2018, with the numberof food insecure people increasing from 6.5 million to 6.7 million. The number of people at riskof food insecurity has also grown, from 4 million to 4.5 million, bringing the total up to 11.2million Syrians who need urgent life-saving and life-sustaining food and livelihoodsassistance.Insecurity and conflict are among the major drivers of food insecurity in Syria, causingwidespread, often multiple displacements and constraining physical access to food and markets.Vulnerable populations such as people with disabilities, single-headed households, and theelderly often face particular challenges physically accessing food, for reasons ranging from alack of mobility to threats to their physical safety. In many communities, an influx of internallydisplaced persons (IDPs) and returnees contribute to socio-economic pressures that also createvulnerabilities for host communities. This is particularly true in urban areas, which host roughly84 per cent of all IDPs in Syria. Food insecurity in Syria is also driven by a lack of financial access, as high food prices, low purchasing power, and the collapse of income earning opportunities have hampered mostSyrians’ ability to acquire food According to FAO meetings from 2017 until now in livestock working group (LTWG), the livestock owners’ have very limited access to the animal health services and quality animal production inputs persists, which decrease the livestock productivity. The livestock sector suffered from high damages due to animal deaths, amounting to over USD 2 billion – a third of the total amount of damages to the agriculture sector. The actual number of animals per household has fallen dramatically, especially for cows and sheep. The main reason for the decrease in animal’s ownership is the sale of animals for income generation, in addition to the increasing costs of maintenance. The greatest reason of loss animals is due to the poor living conditions that most of the Syrian families live.According to the Rapid Needs Assessments RNAs which have been conducted by IYD team in the targeted areas before implementing any project previously and from our field team comments in our conducted projects, the families are depending mainly on the livestock as the main source for their income. And the breeders can no longer bear the burden of rearing animals due to the absence of the green forage fodder and the high price for other types of fodder. In addition, the spread of diseases is a high risk among the animals because of the lack of healthcare due to the high prices of the health follow-up and lack of vaccines and insemination.The cows are mainly suffering from the following diseases; entroximea, foot-and-mouth disease, mastitis, reproductive system disease, blood parasites...etc. While the sheep suffering from entroximea, blood parasites, low of productivity and fertility, in addition, there are doubts about spreading the new dangerous disease which is the PPR.The absence of the green forage fodder led to problems in the digestive system and decrease in milk productivity.

Implementation of activities

IYD after selecting the areas will inform the breeders to participate in the subscription process which will be conducting by IYD field team to benefit from the project. The informing process will be conducted through many scours where the advertisements will be distributed in the targeted area, and the informing process will continue about 5 days then the subscription process will start after coordinating with communities leaders. IYD seeks through the project to help beneficiaries to secure income for their families and the dissemination of a culture of green fodder among livestock’ breeders, and help the livestock breeders to increase their monthly income. during selecting process IYD will apply the following:1- Define 25 beneficiaries, half properly headed by women based on a certain criterion to produce the hydroponic barley fodder on their rooms.2- Define 300 cows' breeders (600 cows), half properly headed by women based on a certain criterion to support their cows.3- Define 100 sheep' breeders (2000 sheep), half properly headed by women based on a certain criterion to support their sheep. IYD will rehabilitate the rooms which provided by rooms' owners to insulation them to control the humidity and temperature inside the rooms and provide them with the needed equipment such as the ventilation, electronic systems, and other tools like boxes with their iron holders to produce the hydroponic barley. IYD is bent on doing several types of training:The first one will be a seminar on planting the hydroponic barley, where an announcement will be distributed in the targeted areas, including an invitation to attend to the seminar about hydroponic barley and its benefit in feeding the cows and sheep for one day in each target area. IYD during the seminar will distribute brochures about hydroponic barley for all attending.The second training will be two days of theoretical training for beneficiaries who were selected for producing the hydroponic barley.The ways of planting the hydroponic barley and its benefits and the lessons learned from the previous projects will be explained in order to develop the skills of the beneficiaries.The third training for the selected beneficiaries will be after the preparing of the hydroponic barley rooms where the practical training will be conducted for the hydroponic barley planting for seven days. IYD technical team will working daily with the selected beneficiaries during the training to explain the full details of the planting.The fourth one will be a training for the selected livestock owners about rearing their animals and to correct malpractices that occur during the rearing the livestock in the field. IYD will buy the hydroponic barley production from the 25 producers of hydroponic barley and distribute it daily for three months by rent 6 distribution cars to deliver the green fodder to the selected beneficiaries. The distributing amount of hydroponic barley fodder will be 10 kg on a daily basis for the selected cows and 2 kg for the selected sheep to improve the health of the digestive and increasing the daily milk production and increasing the fertility of the flock after using the hydroponic barley. In addition, IYD will distribute 8 kg of the pellet fodder for the selected cows to secure the required protein, energy, and minerals in the fodder ration. IYD will follow up the healthcare for the 600 selected cows and for 2000 selected sheep, the following will be including the vaccine programs and follow up the daily diseases to improve the health situation.IYD will Follow up the reproductive system for the selected cows and sheep and treat the diseases if any, to be able inseminating them to increase their numbers and the milk production in the targeted areas.

Protection Risk Analysis

- The risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers. IYD will mitigate this by establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and referral mechanisms, addressing the SEA complaints, training staff on PSEA and GBV, asking all staff to sign code of conduct, creating females and males’ queues separately, having female staff. Behaving with beneficiaries as do no harm and respecting their dignity. - Increased risk of inter-community tension between community residents and host communities due in part to unequal level of assistance given to community residents in comparison to host communities. IYD will mitigate this by ensuring that the community are all needs based, and that community residents are amongst the most vulnerable. - Sexual harassment from the beneficiaries because of the overcrowded distribution location. The mitigations are by creating females and males’ queues separately and having a committee for organizing the distribution process. - To avoid Gender exploitation. IYD will mitigate this by including all the age and gender needs accordingly within the project designing phase. Clarifying all the project objectives and beneficiaries’ selection criteria to all the local authorities. Asking the local authorities’ members, vendors and services’ providers to sign the PSEA code of conduct. - To avoid the gender discrimination, IYD will provide suitable and safe distribution environment such as: accessible ramps for people with special needs, drinking water, waiting seats, umbrellas, secure distribution center and placing it away from military installations or threats such as attacks. - Child labor risk, the mitigation is to make the service provider committing to the listed conditions which provided to not make any work with him if he was breaking the signed contract with IYD by practicing the child labor. - Child food ration receiving risk. The mitigation is by not delivering the food ration to the child and having a mobile distribution team to deliver the ration to this child house in case the householder is not able to come to the distribution center.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption, The Pregnancy of cows will go smoothly.Risk Description, Pregnancy will not happen due to low quality of sperm that used in industrial insemination.Mitigation, We will apply the following protocol in the Artificial insemination:1 - Check the reproductive system of cows, diagnosis their genital's situation and provide treatment against reproductive system diseases.2 - After the treatment of the reproductive system diseases, IYD technical team will inseminate each cow twice at the same time.3-After the insemination the vets will be monitoring the pregnancy for 60 days if the cow is not pregnant, they will inseminate the cows again. 4-If the cow is not pregnant after the second insemination, we will use the natural insemination by bull and it will solve the problem. Assumption, The climatic conditions will be stable.Risk Description, External climatic risks (drought, diseases, physiological impacts…)Mitigation, Technical supervision by prevention and suitable treatments procedures. Assumption, Cows are living in a good condition.Risk Description, Cows exposure to disease.Mitigation, Regarding the spread of the epidemics and diseases, IYD will take care of these kinds of diseases. Assumption, People are aware of green fodder.Risk Description, Lack of the expertise in hydroponic barley project..Mitigation, IYD will choose the beneficiaries through Specific criteria and conduct training about the hydroponic barley planting. Assumption, The security situation will be stable during the project period.Risk Description, Change in the security situation.Mitigation, IYD will coordinate with the active actors in the ground to pick the safest area to implement the project, additionally, IYD safety and security officer will follow the security situation and send reports to the field team to ensure their safety.
