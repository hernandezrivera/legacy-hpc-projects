1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The aim of the project is to provide WASH assistance for IDPs and native population of the host communities in Pemba and Mecufi districts. It forsees the implementation/rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities with specific focus on gender and disabilities, sensitization campaigns about water-related diseases and strengthening the partners’ coordination for a better implementation of the WASH actions in both districts. Pemba DistrictTarget beneficiaries: 18,800 (60% women, 40% men) Mecufi DistrictTarget beneficiaries: 3,510 (60% women, 40% men)

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

A1-WASH sector coordination established in Pemba and Mecufi: coordination in the WASH sector among all partners operating in the areas will be necessary. Sectoral meetings will be held in the two district, involving local authorities and local/international partners that work in the area. A database will be realized and updated weekly. WW-GVC will lead the process and share data with the partners.A2-Access to safe water of appropriate quantity and quality is guaranteed to 21,000 IDPs and 3,000 host families in Pemba and Mecufi districts: in partnership with the district authorities (administrations, SDPI and SDMAS), an evaluation of the updated necessities will be elaborate and the host communities where to construct/rehabilitate wells will be selected. The process will be lead by WW-GVC’s civil engineer. A3-Access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities is provided to 3,000 host families and 21,000 IDP in Pemba and Mecufi districts: in partnership with local authorities, an evaluation of the necessities will be put in place in order to select the host communities where to build VIP latrines integrated with MHM and facilitate access for people with disabilities. A4-Dissemination of information on safe water, sanitation and hygiene and NFI to prevent spread of WASH related diseases in Pemba and Mecufi districts: sensitization campaigns in the host communities and district schools will be implemented through communication material (flayers, graffiti (“paredes falantes”)) and artistic movements (dances, theatre, games for kids).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The actions foreseen in this project are design to avoid as much possible to harm the target population, limiting causes of conflict between the different categories of beneficiaries. Particular attention will be focused on the beneficiaries’ selection criteria, that will try to put in the same line both the IDPs and the native population, in order to not discriminate anyone. Sensitization before, during and after the building of new facilities will be developed and will involve the whole community in order to reduce the wrong use of the constructions but mostly to create a sense of ownership about them. The facilities will be of the community so they will have to preserve them, so know how and allow everyone to use them. Committees for the management of water points and latrines will be created, involving both women and men equally.The sensitization campaigns, as well as the creation of communication and visibility material (graffiti, radio spots, flayers, theatre, dances, games, etc.) will be discussed with specialists in different fields (WASH, protection, education, gender, disability, etc.) in order to create an inclusive communication and lead the message reach all the population categories.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

WW-GVC is already present in the target district so it was facilitated in the design of the project, already knowing the basic necessities of the population. Moreover, the organization is in constant relation with the local authorities (WW-GVC leads the Mecufi WASH Cluster) allowing to have an update vision of the population needs. This relationship allows WW-GVC to have official data and information about the number of IDPs, host families and host communities, following the ongoing changes that, due to the situation, are very common and most of the times cause disrupted knowledge of the reality. All the data is collected distinguishing information about sex, age, status (IDP or native), origins and actual location of each project’s beneficiary.The areas where the wells and latrines will be built will be selected and agreed in collaboration with the local authorities as well as with the target communities. The project’s staff will visit these areas at least three times at week to guarantee the right execution of the constructions. WW-GVC will put in place the Green Line’s service, adequately advertised with visibility and communication material. Complain boxes will also be present each time WW-GVC’s staff will be in the field.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The first stage of the project will the identification of population’s updated needs as well the final definition of beneficiaries. This will include the distinction of IDPs and native population discriminated by age, sex and disability. It is common knowledge that many times people with disabilities are hided by their families or communities. At the beginning of the project, WW-GVC will put in place both an arising campaign about the project and a broad data collection that will include all its beneficiaries.For what concerns the hygiene facilities, both the water points and the latrines will be built in order to be accessible to people with disabilities, with slopes and structures with enough space for the movement of wheelchairs. Latrines will be equipped with grab-bars and larger doors that will consent its simple use. All the communication and visibility material used for the sensitization campaigns will be developed including specific focus for people with different kind of disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

For the building of hygiene facilities, contractors will have to take a geophysical study in order to certificate the right position of the water points as well as of the latrines, avoiding the contact between the water table and contaminated water. The wells will be provided of discharge channels in order to avoid water stagnation and the development of water-related diseases. Latrines will be build using local material and taking attention to the particular soil. As a matter of fact, sandy soils will need a more specific technique to avoid them to sink. In general, all the actions will have a minimum impact on the environment and everything will be done to avoid it. The process will be lead by the WW-GVC’s civil engineer.
