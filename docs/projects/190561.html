Project Summary

The project is in line with the government priorities and seeks to prevent and mitigate protection risks and their consequences through wide-ranging intervention. While dealing on child protection and GBV in the emergency crisis, it works to make a lasting contribution to improve the living condition and development perspectives of the target community. Interventions are destined to ensure protection and access to services through combined efforts of the project, community structures and government institutions. Reinforcing the existing child protection and GBV prevention coordination mechanisms in the intervention areas is an essential part of the approach. It will have a strong capacity building, networking, and community mobilization components to promote child protection and GBV issues. It aims to address protection risks and gender-based violence on women, girls and boys affected by drought through establishment of temporary women friendly spaces for life saving psycho-social supports and strengthening referral pathway, coordination, and provision of dignity kit.
The project will strengthen the livelihood of vulnerable women and girls to reduce their exposure to negative copying mechanisms, exploitation and build economic resilience. It will strengthen multisectoral coordination through tailor made capacity building, technical back up supports and development of GBV standard operating procedures. It is designed to respond to the multi-faced violations of rights of children in the target areas affecting their survival and development. Improves access to strategic services for children and their care providers through integrating protection and GBV in an on-going joint humanitarian response using GBV guidelines. Frontline service providers will be trained and coached on case management, psychosocial support, and clinical management of rape.
The project will strengthen government and community structures to mobilize resources and provide unbroken psychosocial supports for boys, girls, and women.
The project along with the protection cluster will contribute to enhancing access to multi-sectoral lifesaving interventions for GBV survivors as well as vulnerable women and girls. It will mainly target GBV survivors, girls, women, and children (boys and girls) who are affected by the conflict and suffered life-threatening violence, abuse, or serious neglect. Works to restore the person's capacity to live a safe/dignified life by reducing the vulnerability of persons at heightened risks of violence and exploitation. It will raise awareness and build the capacity of service providers, including duty bearers, community members, and humanitarian actors. The project will contribute to cluster objective on and strategic priority through multisectoral life-saving interventions, empowerment measures and risk mitigation through GBV integration.
The project is meant to address protection and GBV risks such as violence, exploitation, harmful traditional practices and improve access to GBV/ protection services through direct provision and referral system for girls, boys and women impacted by the drought in three selected districts of Tigray region namely Mekelle, Enderta and Wokro . Targets a total of 21000 beneficiaries (Mekele 9000, Wukro 6000 and Enderta 6000)girls and boys through referrals, livelihood support and training. Furthermore, it will reach 21,000 men, women, boys, and girls indirectly through community awareness programs, IEC, and BCC. In the process, inclusive and participatory process will be adopted to make sure PWDs are not overlooked. GOAL Ethiopia has previous experience of implementing EHF funded project and is currently implementing multi-sector projects which will provide competitive advantage to ease implementation and achieve envisaged results.
