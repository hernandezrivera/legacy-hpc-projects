Project Summary

This project intends to strengthen CCCM site management structures in Banadir, Galmudug, Lower Jubba and Gedo Regions with increased coordination among the service providers within the settlements and host community to streamline humanitarian service delivery. The project shall also ensure that the population can access key services through ongoing advocacy to the partners with a special emphasis on people with specific needs. Through this project, NoFYL shall work in close coordination with the local authority to strengthen the existing governance structure and fill in the identified gaps while encouraging the active participation of both IDPs and the host community’s leadership. Cluster coverage will be expanded through NoFYL capacity to carry out assessments and monitor the displacement conditions in target locations where there are currently no CCCM coordination structures in place or no IDP assessment of any kind having been conducted in the last one year. Coverage will also be expanded through coordination meetings with camp committees and camp leaders.
NoFYL will implement emergency site improvement projects to minimize risks of exclusion and high incidence of protection violations in IDP sites, as well as achieving durable solutions. NoFYL will also conduct settlement planning training for settlement leaders; create coordination structures and SOPs for emergency displacements; conduct capacity building training for government actors and relevant stakeholders; establish/reinforce community mechanisms for communication channels among others.
Other planned activities also include site maintenance & decongestion activities, Contingency planning, awareness raising campaigns through local TVs and Radios on COVID prevention and response, update and disseminate referral networks, Strengthen site level coordination meetings

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1.1.1: One Site Verification exercise covering all sites in the target regions will be conducted with support from other humanitarian partners and the local authorities to provide multi-sector information on access and availability to basic services in the target sites to ascertain gaps in service provision and monitor services being provided, the verification will be conducted by NoFYL's multi-functional mobile teams, this will help to produce detailed information on the population of each site, its perimeter, access and services available in sites. NoFYL will use this data and contribute to its dissemination to inform humanitarian response at each level. The site verification will also inform the selection process of the target camps.Activity 1.1.2: Establish effective and representative Site governance and for the promotion of IDPs’ participation in decision-making and the daily life of the Site, including the development of Site rules. In addition to gender representation, minority groups, people with special needs will be assured equal representation. Community leaders and representatives that are selected will be given clear functions and written, agreed-upon roles and responsibilities that will be utilized as a possible entry point for other humanitarian partner’s activities that require community consultation. Activity 1.1.3: Implementation of service mapping and service monitoring and coordination in the target locations. NoFYL will coordinate monthly updates of the service mapping to ascertain availability of service (or lack thereof) of services in camps and disseminate it to the partners and communities. NoFYL will ensure regular coordination with humanitarian agencies, working closely with Protection to ensure Do No Harm approaches are realized. Activity 1.1.4: Settlement planning training will be conducted for 300 participants from across the 4 regions. This will target community leaders, elders, and CMC and equip them with skills that will facilitate participatory management and maintenance of public spaces. The site planning training will cover the importance of emergency planning and preparedness for communities that are particularly vulnerable to various shocks. Activity 1.1.5: Establish mechanisms for communication of services available in the target locations/camps, this can be achieved by establishing information centers in coordination with local authorities and protection actors, this will enhance the community knowledge on available services and referral mechanisms by disseminate key information regarding the settlement including information on services. Activity 1.1.6: Strengthening CFM structures - Complaints and Feedback Mechanisms are operational within target locations- With a continued commitment to enhancing accountability to the site population – whereby site residents can easily access the opportunity to raise concerns or complaints related to any sector within the site. Complaints are collected and registered by trained staff at the NoFYL information desks. Complaints are then referred to partners with a request for an action to be taken. Activity 1.1.7: Support community-led site maintenance activities to ensure the upkeep of sites through cash for work activities, site maintenance led by the site maintenance committees in target settlements. 200 persons in each of the 4 target regions will be targeted for the conditional cash for work, beneficiaries who will support in site maintenance through digging drainage systems, pit-holes for garbage collection, bush clearing and clear pathways. Activity 1.1.8: Joint stakeholder site decongestion activity will be conducted in the target sites as part of a broader strategy geared towards decongesting sites, the on-site congestion causes a high risk of flooding and further spread of COVID-19 due to congestion.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Beneficiaries risk exposure to violence during registration/distribution, mitigation measures being as follows; 1- Separate the process of registration and distribution, never register beneficiaries at the same time conducted distribution. 2- Engage the authority for clearance and provision of security during the distribution exercise. 3- Do not construct distribution in an open ground, use community centers or fenced compound to restrict access to only listed beneficiaries. 4- Where distribution is conducted at the agency's office, provide transport allowances to beneficiaries back to their camps or homes. 5- Use cash/food vouchers where beneficiaries can visit a supplier anytime to receive their share instead of open distribution. Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure in targeted project areas: Mitigation measure being the following; 1- Assess the ownership of land if government owned or private before establishing any facility on the land. 2- Engage local authorities on the allocation of public land with lease of over 5 years to ensure long term sustainability of projects 3- On private land ensure agreement is signed with the land owners for a lease of over 5 years to establish community structures like community centres in an IDP camps. 4 - Engage directly with the owners of the land rather than gate keepers to understand land tenure processes. 5 - Validate with the regional authorities tenure documents produced by private land owners to ensure claimed land owners are known before proceeding in any engagement.
