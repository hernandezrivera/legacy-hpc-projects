Project Summary

This project will increase the resilience of communities in conflict affected areas of South , East Darfur and Khartoum through playing, learning and exploring their skills of coping, and will stood as voice of the most at-risk communities and PSNs to establish the bases of future leaders, it will also encourage working together the create the positive sense of brotherhood between different community background, therefore we are expecting at the end of this project communities will be able to change negative behaviors and believes and consider human rights based approach in their daily culture practices.
This will provide required protection services for the children and support preventing Gender Based Violence in South Darfur, East Darfur and Khartoum States, it will help communities to cope with challenges and harsh situation they faced, the anticipated demand to at least satisfying their currently urgent needs, while enhance the capacity of community member and community based organizations towards collecting efforts in order to make a solidarity attitude in protection and other intervention tools or mechanisms such setting up child protection system, Case Management, Gender participation, Human and child rights and referral mechanism, enhancing referral mechanisms, capacity building, vocational trainings.
This intervention will focuses on General Protection, Child Protection and GBV as REMCO with full capacity to intervene in the suggested activities, providing that REMCO worked with UNICEF in East Darfur providing Child Protection for 5 years and active in GBV sub-sectors where REMCO implement all its project with focuses on GBV issues

1 - Needs

Since the process of the signing of peace agreement in conflicting areas in Sudan, the Darfur villages which witnessed displaced people have been receiving a return from IDPs camps to the areas of origin specially farmers due to the slight stability in the security situation. The increasing number of the return particularly in the past two years has added value to the situation for the serious level of food security in all conflict affected state despite the states still suffering from lack of resources to respond effectively for growing protection needs. However, and in such a situation, children, women, PSN and adolescents are often at highest risk for being directly affected by negative coping mechanisms with few protective strategies available to them with the multiple change of school calendar resulting from the spread of COVID19; and increased exposure to violence expected with high drop out of schools which will effect on their development and well-being . Although there are no clear figures due to the lack of counselling services, it is well known that during the return, many children went through traumatizing experiences and were separated from their families. As children’s well-being and development depends very much on the security of family relationships and a predictable environment, many children lose touch with their families and have no idea of fathers and mothers’ whereabouts. Given the fact, the vast majority of the children and women in are now living in very unsafe and difficult conditions affecting their abilities to develop on physical, cognitive, emotional and social aspects, which have obviously taken away Life's normalcy and development conditions. During REMCO assessment in the suggested localities most participants reported that while their children previously had support, the lack of case management and basic PSS continued to be insufficient. Although some aid originations have succeeded to the provision of basic needs, UASC are still lacking support and further protection assistance and counselling services that help them to overcome the situation they passed through. REMCO staff in the three suggested areas (South, East Darfur and Khartoum) highlighted the need to severe humanitarian situations and need for an immediate scale up response to the critical situations of particularly in South East Darfur and Khartoum. Protection partners are concerned that this situation will exacerbate the vulnerability of families to danger level. The proposed project will respond to the growing humanitarian crisis of returning communities in General Protection, Child Protection and GBV to provide lifesaving protection assistance and services to most at risk boys, girls, women and men and adolescents. The project will increase the resilience of children through playing, learning and exploring their skills of coping, and will stood as voice of the most at-risk children and PSNs to establish the bases of future leaders, it will also encourage working together the create the positive sense of brotherhood, the activities will be based on human rights approach therefore we are expecting at the end of this project return communities will be able to change negative behaviors and believes and consider human rights based approach in their daily culture practices.
