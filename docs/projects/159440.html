Project Summary

The project will focus on improving the physical protection and dignity of IDPs, refugee returnees and poor host communities across Somalia through provision of locally purchased Non Food Items (NFIs) and Emergency Shelter Kits (ESKs) through cash/voucher/in-kind modalities, construction of Transitional and Permanent Shelters through community driven or owner driven approaches, settlement (re)planning training, conducting Post Construction Monitoring (PCM) exercises for Shelters and Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) exercises for NFIs & ESKs and participation in infrastructure mapping exercises led by Shelter cluster or any other partner.
For the beneficiaries’ selection and registration, NRC will use an electronic mobile data collection tool called Kobo tool box, which provides an accurate and real time data. The data received through Kobo is easily manageable and transferable. 20% to 30% Poor host community members will also be selected to ensure 'DO NO HARM' principle ensuring other humanitarian principles including humanity, neutrality, impartiality and
independence. For the provision of NFIs, each HH will be provided with a cash of $100 as per cluster agreed standard NFI kit while for the provision of ESKs, each HH will be provided with a cash of $145 (80% or $116 as first installment and remaining 20% or $29 as second installment) as per cluster approved ESK design. NRC will acquire the services of local telecom companies (viz. Hurmuud telecom in South and Central Somalia and Somtel & Golis in Puntland and Somaliland) for the cash transfers. NRC will provide a complete list of selected beneficiaries to respective telecom companies for their cross checking purposes. This list will include all details of selected beneficiaries including their telephone numbers and token numbers. After reviewing and cross checking, respective telecom company will transfer specified amount of money to selected beneficiaries through mobile transfer facility.
Beneficiaries will be selected through predetermined beneficiary selection criteria prioritizing the Extremely Vulnerable Individuals (EVIs) including female and single headed HHs, orphans, chronically ill, aged and child headed households. Gender will be mainstreamed into the project through the specific consideration of the different needs of women, men, boys and girls during the design of activities. The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis-segregated data. NRC will target total 111,300 individuals (50% females) consisting of 54,900 in South and Central Somalia, 43,200 in Puntland and 13,800 in Somaliland through different Shelter interventions throughout the year 2020.
NRC staff will ensure that the provided cash is to be utilized for intended purpose by coordinating with the beneficiaries and conduct follow up visits with the support of the community leaders, local authorities and the relevant cluster partners. A Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) exercises for NFIs and ESKs and Post Construction Monitoring (PCM) exercises for semi-permanent and permanent Shelters will be conducted to analyse outcomes, the beneficiaries’ level of satisfaction and draw lessons learnt for future programming.

Mitigation Measures

1b. Protection of civilians: In a rural context, communities or groups are either forced into temporarydisplacement because of climatic shocks or conflict, or might join urban centers in search of better access to humanitarian aid (pull factor), access to services and economic opportunities. During slow onset and long-tail crisis such as the recurrent drought and conflict, the same communities may partially displace to neighboring towns. Since all the Shelter interventions will be channeled through cluster approach and involvement of relevant local authorities, thus the impact of armed conflict andviolence will be mitigated through proper coordination, advocacy at national and international level, media and referral mechanisms. 2a. Exclusion and marginalization: TThe Federal Government of Somalia has committed to women’s empowerment and eradicating violence against women, recognizing that without institutionalizing the equal rights of women, the country will never be able to stabilize and develop in a sustainable way. Somali women and girls continue to bear an unequal burden of the hardships occasioned by poverty, conflict and clan-based culture, which promotes strict male hierarchy and authority. This is further exacerbated by cultural limitations on the role and status of women in Somali society. Women with disabilities are often marginalized on account of their disability and their gender. They may be more vulnerable to poverty and social exclusion, and often have limited social, political and economic opportunities and lack of access to basic services. Women with disabilities may also be at greater risk of sexual and physical violence and abuse. Girls with disabilities often experience discrimination, for example in education and family life. This project will ensure engagement of people, especially women with disabilities, and the elderly at the co-management level and will ensure that programs interventions do not generate negative side-effects.. The beneficiary selection will be conducted in a participatory approach with the community leaders and the local authorities that will identify the most vulnerable people. Women and Child headed households will be given special priority. 2b. Exclusion and marginalization: Targeted assistance will be provided to the most vulnerable groups including women, girls and boy. Beneficiaries will be selected through a community-based approach, which will identify the most vulnerable based on certain criteria as female or child-headed households, elderly, disabled, large families and the chronically ill. The beneficiary selection will be conducted in a participatory approach with the community leaders and the local authorities that will identify the most vulnerable people. Women, girls, boys and Child headed households will be given special priority. 4a. Protection mainstreaming: Protection mainstreaming will be done in all shelter interventions. Proper land title deeds will be provided to recipients of permanent Shelter to avoid evictions at the later stage. Similarly, for the recipients of the transitional shelters, at least communal title deed will be provided to avoid any eviction. Local authorities and settlement leaders will be involved in the process of title deeds, who will serve as witness in case of any dispute.

Please provide further explanation

The project is linked to longer-term durable solution initiatives. Through Shelter activities in settlements, unplanned settlements will be planned through site planning activities, whereby adequate spaces will be left for necessary facilities including schools, hospitals, play areas, mosques, roads and other infrastructures. Through these site planning activities, the objective of longer-term durable solutions activities will be achieved. Similarly, through construction of transitional and permanent Shelters and well as different infrastructures, the project will contribute to durable solution objectives.
