Project Summary

The WASH condition in Sudan is deteriorating from time to time. Access to WASH services becomes more challenging for communities. Several WASH infrastructures remain in disrepair due to lack of spare parts and lack of maintenance budget and chronic socio-economic and political problems. East Darfur, South Darfur, West Kordofan, Al Gezira, Al Gedaref and Kassala states are amongst the highest affected states with more than 93% localities having WASH severity 4 & 5. In these states, lack of WASH services is exacerbating the health and nutrition problems. Reports showed that severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and WASH-related diseases are still very high among IDPs, returnees, and other vulnerable populations. The condition needs humanitarian intervention and working on long-term sustainability of WASH facilities.
Following the war erupted between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) on 15 April 2023, Sudan is facing one of fastest unfolding crises globally, the humanitarian situation in Sudan is dire, with 24.8 million people in need of assistance, including 6 million internally displaced. The main sectors of need are health, nutrition, WASH, protection, and FSL, which are driven by multiple factors, such as conflict, insecurity, social tensions, climate shocks, poor governance, and disease outbreaks. The ongoing conflict has worsened the situation for all families, but especially for the vulnerable groups, such as new and protracted IDPs, refugees, and returnees. These groups live in overcrowded conditions and face overcrowded conditions and face repeated emergencies and displacement. This project aims to contribute to lifesaving of targeted communities in Khartoum,
Aljazeera, Al Gedaref, Kassala, East Darfur, South Darfur and West Kordofan through provision of timely WASH services to cope up current emergencies and respond to future emergencies. Alight will provide inclusive WASH services that respond to the immediate needs of affected people and implement activities that contribute to the long-term sustainability of services.
The following are main activities:
• Repair, Maintain and Rehabilitation of existing nonfunctioning water supply systems
• Upgrade (solarize) and extend/expand water supply systems.
• Installation of Emergency sanitation services for emergency affected communities
• Establish emergency water supply systems through treatment of surface water or
through distribution of water treatment chemicals.
• Distribution of essential WASH NFIs.
• Support operation and maintenance of the water supply systems, and sanitation facilities.
• Conduct water treatment and ensuring water qualities.
• Increasing awareness and behavioral change on hygiene, sanitation & environmental practices.
• Promoting community participation and improving sense of ownership.
• Support communities and local governments in improving the management of O&M of water supply systems.
The specific target locations (sub-localities and villages) will be selected through joint need assessments and identification of hotspot areas for emergencies to maximize the impact; the project prioritizes service delivery for IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable populations affected by emergencies (including WASH-related epidemics and water resource-related conflicts) and facing severe acute malnutrition (SAM) & high food insecurity. This project contributes to overall national and state level humanitarian assistance initiatives by implementing the humanitarian WASH response activities responding to their immediate need of emergency affected people and building resilience to future emergencies, i.e. by implementing community & local government empowerment activities. The project aimed to reach 841,645 most in-needed people in the targeted areas. The project facilitates community participation especially women, girls, young people, and marginalized groups and engage throughout the different stage of project assessment and implementation.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The WASH response activities are designed based on safe programming criteria. For example, latrine construction designs will take into consideration protection needs of different segments of users based on Gender, Age and Diversity (GAD) as it affects the Safety, Access and Dignity (SAD) of users. Designs consider the diverse needs and ensure privacy, dignity and security of women, girls and children, and give easy access for pregnant women, the elderly and people with disability. WASH facilities will be constructed at safe places with the consultation of the community. Latrines will be near to homes so that they can be accessed safely at all times of day and night. Institutional latrines will be gender segregated and ensure privacy and dignity, will have features such as lockable doors and solar lights where possible. The sitting of latrines will take into consideration water sources to avoid possible contamination. To ensure safe access, water point's site will be selected in consultation with community. As much water points as possible will be placed at public places (open space) and be close to the living places which will reduce distance as well as prevent incidence of GBV. Furthermore, we construct conventional emergency shelters with lockable doors and ventilation and equipped with light during night that provide good protection in use for people with special need (PSN) in IDPs camp and for others with extreme vulnerability.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

In regard to AAP, Alight will employ the Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability (AGD) policy that is implemented through community based, participatory approaches that are already well established in the IDPs camp and host community areas. The Project will ensure that participation, feedback and complaints mechanisms are integrated in all strategies and through the project cycle. This is to ensure the project decisions and actions are responsive to the expressed priorities, needs, capacities and views of all persons of concern irrespective of Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. This is aimed at ensuring meaningful participation, enhanced communication and transparency, improved feedback and response. ALIGHT will ensure a balanced boys, girls, women and men in all project activities with relative 60% female and 40% male. Data for beneficiaries will be disaggregated by gender, age groups and location, and project activities will be tailored for different groups in line with developmental stages, needs and vulnerabilities. The Project will ensure that all persons of concern have equal and non- discriminatory access to protection, assistance and solutions, this will build on their resilience and recovery. Activities like awareness raising, dialogues, will be conducted in different WASH trainings and in the communities, specific needs andvulnerabilities identification and culturally sensitive issues discussed separately. Alight will maintain effective feedback systems (including comments, suggestions and complaints box) using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non- sensitive feedback e.g. use of complaints boxes, toll free hot lines.
