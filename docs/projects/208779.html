Project Summary

Protection without Borders (POWIB) seeks to address the pressing Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) needs in Bama(Banki) and Ngala LGAs of Borno State, Nigeria. The project, Enhancing protection environment for displaced women and girls through an Integrated HLP and shelter solutions. aims to improve access to HLP rights for conflict-affected populations, fostering sustainable returns, preventing forced evictions and enhancing protection of women and girls headed households in the targeted communities.
The conflict in Borno State has left a significant impact on the HLP sector, creating urgent needs and gaps. These include the necessity to advocate for fee waivers in land document processing, advocacy on permanent land allocations to expand garrison towns, and finding durable solutions to forced evictions at informal settlements. The identified gaps include insufficient HLP actors in areas where Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) are returning, lack of pre-and post-eviction cash assistance, and challenges in accessing legal representation and assistance for HLP violation and related cases.

The project will strengthen HLP actors and relevant community stakeholders Including women groups on dispute resolution mechanisms especially alternative dispute resolutions (ADR) in areas/communities witnessing an influx of Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs), provide pre-and post-eviction cash assistance, and support access to legal representation for HLP violation cases.
The project is an integrated HLP and shelter response to be implemented in Bama(Banki) and Ngala LGAs. POWIB as the lead partner will implement the HLP component and will sub-grant the shelter components to PRIDE. The project aims to provide an integrated response in Bama(Banki), and Ngala LGAs, encompassing Housing Land and Property, and shelter solutions, prioritizing the most vulnerable women and child girls’ rights to housing protection. The lead partner, POWIB will be responsible for the operational oversight of the program and closely monitor the shelter components sub-granted to PRIDE as the sub-Implementing partner. The shelter response will focus on supporting the most vulnerable Women and girls including persons with disabilities facing housing challenges; either at the risks of eviction or living in a congested shelter or sharing spaces with relatives in camps. the project will also once-off cash-envelope as a rental subsidy to support the most vulnerable affected families living in the host communities but couldn't afford to cover for the rental cost, they could also be supported with emergency shelter assistance where there is availability of land for shelter construction.
The success of the project will be measured through indicators such as reduced land document processing fees, increased allocated land, decreased forced evictions, Increased awareness on HLP matters and the establishment of functional community oriented dispute resolution mechanisms as well provision of housing assistance to the most in need. POWIB aims to create sustainable solutions, empower communities, and protect the fundamental rights of conflict-affected populations in the targeted Locations.
To ensure lasting impact, the project incorporates a sustainability plan, involving the establishment of local committees for ongoing community engagement, training of key stakeholders Including women and girls groups to advocate for HLP rights, and documentation of best practices for sustainability and continuity.
