Project Summary

Based on HNO 2024, now in its 13th year of crisis, Syria is grappling with a catastrophe of unprecedented magnitude, with new emergencies adding to existing crises and taking a heavy toll on the people living in Syria. Protracted displacement, economic decline, global inflation, escalation of hostilities, and the aftermath of the earthquakes that struck south-eastern Türkiye and northern Syria in February 2023 are some of the biggest challenges Syrians currently face.
The number of people in need of humanitarian response in Syria has increased from 15.3 million to 16.7 million since 2023. Overall, 268 out of 270 sub-districts are now classified as being under severe or extreme conditions.
The main goal of the project is to deliver flexible humanitarian assistance (regular multi-purpose cash MPC) to the most vulnerable IDP people in the subdistricts Azaz and Afrin in Aleppo and Maarat Tamsrin in Idleb. It is planned to assist nearly 1500 HH (7500 Men, Women, Boys, and Girls) through multi-purpose cash assistance to cover their minimum survival needs. The cash value is planned to be $100 per Household (HH) per month for 6 rounds.
The beneficiaries will be selected according to the need and vulnerability in coordination with civil authorities to reach the most vulnerable HHs and 100% verified by the MEAL team in the targeted areas based on the vulnerability and eligibility criteria, including displacement duration criteria, since the new IDPs are the most vulnerable groups to the shocks. Also, the project will prioritize the women-headed households and the households with children, disabilities, and pregnant and lactating women.
The financial service providers in the targeted area will be assessed and contracted for the redemption process. Market assessments will be conducted in the target areas. Considering the price of basic food commodities remains volatile. It is subject to speculation and disruptive events, specifically displacement; COVID-19 and Cholera have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy and may result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the delivery of unconditional/unrestricted cash assistance that gives households the agency to prioritize their most immediate needs. Therefore, the activity will help them to increase their food security, and their most urgent NFI and WASH needs while reducing negative coping strategies.
Market assessment will be conducted using tools approved by the Cash Working Group, where the capabilities and capacities of the market will be assessed in terms of material availability, accessibility, storage, price stability, quality of goods, cash availability, support for local products, and prevention of damage or inflation and to make sure the local markets are functioning and able to replenish stocks without causing a shortage in supply and to ensure all beneficiaries have good and equal accessibility to the different goods and services.
Accountability to beneficiaries’ is ensured through three main channels: (i) redress and grievance mechanisms; (ii) Post Distribution Monitoring Surveys (PDMS) and focused group discussions (FGDs) as needed; (iii) on-site monitoring activities.
The SDI’s plan for the year 2025 is similar to the year 2024, as the activities will be similar to the general budget for 2024, with a focus on continuing the same activities and services provided.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The main goal of the project is to deliver immediate humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable IDP people in/off camps and members of host communities (HC) in Azaz and Afrin in Aleppo and Maarat Tamsrin in Idleb, who have been suffering from the deterioration in their ability to meet basic needs as the food and basic need prices increased by since last year’s according to Joint Market Monitoring Initiative, especially under the lack of or insufficient income or job opportunities leading to negative coping mechanisms including dept and selling important assets. It is planned to assist nearly 1500 families through multi-purpose cash assistance to cover their needs. The cash value will be $100 HH per month for 6 rounds. The vendors in the targeted area will be assessed and contracted for cash distributions. Considering the price of basic food commodities remains volatile. It is subject to speculation, and disruptive events, specifically displacement, have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy and may result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the adoption of a framework of priorities and actions focused on livelihoods.R1: SEA cases – M1: Train staff on PSEA, establish a Code of Conduct, display PSEA banners at sites, and conduct awareness campaigns. M2: Inform BNFs of free assistance and provide CRM support. A3: The BNF list is accurate, but R3: it could be corrupted. M3: Verify lists with different teams and monitor selection via vulnerability assessment. A4: SDI can be implemented without interference, but R4: there are threats to staff.
