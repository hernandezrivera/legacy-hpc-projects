Project Summary

NPA is supporting its implementing partner União Provincial de Camponeses de Cabo-Delgado (UPC CD) in the implementation of Foods Security and Livelihoods Programme in northern province of Cabo-Delgado. The Programme aims to provide life-sustaining emergency livelihood support to crisis affected persons in Metuge district in the Cabo Delgado province, in line with the response priorities in the 2023 Humanitarian Response Plan.The programme also aims to restore and strengthen people’s livelihoods to improve their sustainability and thus their resilience. The programme’s main objective is to provide access to quality agricultural inputs (seed and tools) for increased household food productivity, increased access to sufficient food in quantity and quality to meet dietary needs and enable livelihoods access to food through production (agriculture) or income. The agriculture inputs assistance will enable target households to meet their immediate needs in initiating life sustaining food production (6 weeks cycle for horticulture inputs and 3 to 6 months for cereals), diversify and improve their nutrition.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

NPA is supporting its partner UPC CD in the implementation of Foods Security and Livelihoods -FSL program in northern province of Cabo-Delgado. The program is providing humanitarian aid to Internal Displaced People (IDP’s) affected by insurgency attacks in the resettlement center of Ntocota in the district of Metuge. The program consists of distributing agricultural inputs, farming tools, training on agriculture and livelihood strategies, disaster preparedness measures, distribution of non-food items and technical assistance. The program will provide assistance to 600 vulnerable households (approximately 3000 individuals). While the targeted population will be mainly IDPs, host communities will be included when and if relevant, as a conflict sensitivity measure. NPA will target IDPs communities who have officially had land allocated to them within the resettlement camps in Metuge district. NPA and partner will also prioritize households using the high vulnerability index and the following elements will be prioritized; female-headed households, households with a high number of dependents, households headed by older people or people with disabilities or chronic illnesses or households with limited income.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The program is providing humanitarian aid to Internal Displaced People (IDP’s) affected by insurgency attacks in Metuge. All the activities that the program will implement will cover 3000 IDP's located in Ntocota resettlement site. The FSL program will support IDP’s by ensuring an increased household food productivity by supporting access to quality agricultural inputs (seed and tools). The agriculture inputs will enable the beneficiaries to meet basic dietary requirements through food production and diversify and improve their nutrition. Furthermore, the program will conduct trainings on various thematic areas that include management of small businesses, conservation agriculture strategies and livelihood strategies, as well as disaster preparedness measures and smallholder farmer trainings for promotion of increased and diversified household incomes. The program will also support creation of savings and loans groups and farmer's associations. In addition, the program will distribute NFI kits consisting of essential non-food items, including cooking pots, cutlery, plates, buckets and drinking glasses. Furthermore, technical assistance will be provided to beneficiaries to closely monitor progress of activities and increase efficiency of results.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

By working with local partners NPA has established community acceptance over a long period of time and is able to access vulnerable populations as a consequence of the trusted position they have pre-dating the current conflict. Through this understanding and analysis NPA and its partners will ensure they do no harm and have a conflict sensitive approach. NPA and its partners have a well-established operational approach to community engagement through the ongoing work with small-scale farmers and civil society in rural and urban areas. The long-term presence of NPA’s partners in the target area and the support provided to host communities through existing programming will form part of a localized conflict mitigation approach. NPA will strengthen mplementing partners’ capacity on safeguarding protection, Code of Conduct, Security policies Do No Harm principles and feedback and complaints mechanism to avoid harassment or exploitation of beneficiaries. NPA’s humanitarian programs are developed and implemented in line with internationally recognized humanitarian principles and standards. Ensuring that we ‘do-no-harm’ in humanitarian settings in Mozambique, NPA will examine how aid interacts with conflict dynamics, the power structures, gender relations or other aspects of the context we work in, and ensure that our programs, as well as staff and partner behavior, are conflict sensitive.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Mozambique has severe inequalities between men and women that characterize the country. NPA recognizes that the majority of the IDP’s are the women, children, elderly and people with disabilities, whose life dignity and protection have been completely disrupted. NPA together with its partners is committed to reduce these inequalities by implementation of the proposed project. NPA recognizes the role of women in resolution of conflicts in the resettlement areas, humanitarian response and post-conflict reconstructions, and will support women’s meaningful participation throughout the humanitarian program cycle. The different needs and vulnerabilities of men, women, boys, girls as well as elderly and persons with disabilities has been considered. Severity of needs is also considered which includes, amongst other factors; female-headed households, households with a high number of dependents, households headed by older people or people with disabilities or chronic illnesses, households with limited income. Emphasis will be to prioritize response to the needs of the most vulnerable households according to the vulnerability assessment in Metuge district. This will prioritize particularly female-headed households, child headed households, households with a high number of dependents, households headed by older people or people with disabilities or chronic illnesses, households with limited income. The partner will ensure the use of a variety of participatory methodologies such as needs assessments and local community engagement in order to map out community structures and available services to concretely understand the context of people of concern as well as their needs. This is to ensure that the program is able to provide assistance specifically targeting and delivering services to populations with identified vulnerabilities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Persons with disabilities are disproportionately affected in disaster, emergency, and conflict situations. The project will ensure that persons with disabilities are consulted throughout the project cycle that will ensure identifying and prioritizing of the work that needs to be done to improve their inclusivity and accessibility of the project.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will focus on providing training to beneficiaries on how to mitigate against impact of climate change by adopting Conversation Agricultural principles for sustainable food security and improved livelihoods.
