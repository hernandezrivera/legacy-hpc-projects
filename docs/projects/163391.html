Project Summary

Through the project WFP will provided unconditional food support to 63,000 Nigerian refugees in the Far North region including 3,000 contingency caseload. In line with WFP Cameroon Country Strategic Plan (CSP 2018-2020), life-saving assistance will be provided Nigerian refugees in Minawao camp. The transfer modality of food assistance to the beneficiaries will be in-kind food through unconditional monthly food distributions to support refugees’ households’ access to adequate and nutritious food, therefore prevent them from adopting negative coping practices. The full ration of the in-kind distributions will provide 2,100 kcal energy per day per person for the beneficiaries who entirely rely on humanitarian food assistance The daily food rations will comprise 350g cereals, 100g pulses 35g vegetable, and 5g iodized salt.

Besoins / Needs

For the past 9 years, the Far North Region has been experiencing increasing humanitarian crises mostly linked to the Lake Chad Basin crisis caused by Boko Haram armed groups. People affected by the crisis are struggling to survive in strenuous conditions in villages or small towns, putting enormous pressure on already scarce community resources and the local economy. The Logone-et-Chari, Mayo Tsanaga and Mayo Sava are the most affected divisions with more than 400,000 persons in situation of displacement. Whilst the majority of this mobile population is IDPs (63%), the other part is composed of returnees (23%) and out of camp Refugees (11%). The displacement has increased in the third quarter of 2019, while the returns slowed down in the same period. The arrival of IDPs in food-insecure areas has severely affected host communities. Boko Haram attacks have disrupted the economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in the Far North region, causing a depletion of livelihoods and household assets. Therefore, the existing chronic vulnerability and structural food insecurity have been aggravated.The region is predominantly arid and regularly hit by drought and seasonal floods. The most recent floods in October 2019 left about 40,000 people displaced and above 500,000 hectares of cultivated farmland destroyed. The October 2019 Cadre Harmonisé highlights that 1.3 million people in the Far North are food insecure. Out of this number, 214,324 people are severely food insecure, which means that they have limited or no access to sufficient, nutritious food required to live a healthy life. The Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) reports that vulnerable families in the conflict-affected areas of the Far North Region are experiencing ‘Stress’ level of acute food insecurity (IPC 2) due to flood and pest-related crop damage, and also attacks by Boko Haram
