Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing comprehensive protection services to the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees, refugees, asylum seekers and marginalized host communities that are high risk of violation of their rights as well as enhancing protection services to boys and girls in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Banadir Regions, Somalia. The need for protection services is occasioned by an unprecedented increase in the number of IDPs owing to conflict and serious flooding since June 2020 while the IDPs live in informal and unplanned settlements under very poor living conditions while their plight is exacerbated by increased exposure to violence, exploitation and other forms of abuse coupled by serious risks and threats to their safety and security as well as negative coping strategies that further expose them to the rights violation. Children endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights including family separation, sexual and physical violence, killing and maiming, psychosocial distress, child labor, widespread recruitment and use of children, forced and protracted displacements, limited access to humanitarian assistance due to insecurity and/or discriminatory practices and exploitation. IDPs, returnees, asylum seekers, and marginalized host communities are further affected by Covid -19 containment measures that subject them to increased forms of discrimination to humanitarian assistance. The project further seeks to increase protection of persons with disabilities that face increased risks of exploitation, abuse and discrimination and denial of assistance meant for them hence need for their increased protection. The project will be implemented through the following activities: Provision of specialized MHPSS and victim assistance services to individuals, including persons with disabilities and older persons, provision of protection services during the eviction, conducting eviction monitoring services, provision of counseling as well as medical support for children and GBV survivors, building the capacity of caseworkers, provision of material support and access to essential services for other protection victims and child survivors, Prevention and Response services for unaccompanied and Separated Children. The project targets 25300 persons (10525 women, 9715 men, and young girls, 2530 boys, and 2530 girls) with specific needs including people with physical, sensorial, intellectual and mental disabilities, adolescent girls and boys, pregnant and lactating women and girls, female and child-headed households, unaccompanied and separated children, persons from minority clans, older persons without caregivers, persons missing documentation, persons facing eviction, persons living in areas with armed conflict and violence.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites such as guards as well as having distribution sites not far from the homes of beneficiaries to limit distance covered and associated risks.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring beneficiary feedback and complains mechanism in place in addition to ensuring that all staff sign PSEA code of conduct3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences and ensuring they are well aware of them4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls on importance of their inclusion and participation in decision making processes and the entire project to ensure their active involvement right from the project inception throughout during project implementation including monitoring exercises till completion.5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
