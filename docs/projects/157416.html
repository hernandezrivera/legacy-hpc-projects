Project Summary

This project will serve 24300 individuals including 8500 IDPs and host 1600 host community inside Kebkabiya town, 4000 host community in rural area, and 10200 returnees in Kebkabiy a rural with basic WASH services including safe water supply , sanitation and hygiene promotion.
The beneficiaries are distributed by age group as follow:
6816 women, 6549 men, 5358 boys and 5577 girls.
The interventions include:
O&M of of 6 boreholes in Rural Kebkabiya
Upgrading of 6 open dug wells to mini water yards in Kebkabiya rural areas
Rehabilitation of 3 open hand dug wells
construction of 3 new open hand dug wells and 2 hand pumps
Rehabilitation of 2500 meters of pipelines inside Kebkabiya town
Training of 10 water management committees of 120 members in urban abd rural Kebkabiya
Construction of 150 improve households latrines with cost sharing approach
Apply CLTS in 7 communities in Kebkabiya rural
provision and distribution of cleaning campaigns tools
Conduct 36 environmental cleaning campaigns
provision of hygiene promotion materials including hygiene kits for 2400 vulnerable families

Needs

KSCS together with Oxfam America regularly assessing the situation to identify gaps in basic services. In November 2018 the two partners conducted need assessment covering Kebkabiya as working area for both partners. The assessment covers basic services of WASH and livelihood. The assessment reveals obvious gap in water supply and access to sanitation facilities in both rural and urban areas the assessment explore the the gap in areas reporting returning back of IDPs to their origin.
