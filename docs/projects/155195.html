Objectives

Objective 1. Providing life saving NFIs to new influx of refugees or households affected by natural disasters or other shocks and to existing people in need.

Needs

The Rohingya refugees are almost completely dependant on external humanitarian assistance to meet basic day-to-day needs, including essential household Non-Food Items (NFIs). IOM's Need and Population Monitoring Round 12 Report of the refugee camps indicates that kitchen sets and torch/solar lamps are among the most frequently requested household items. The lack of lighting in houses is identified as a major shelter related safety issue by the refugees, particularly for women who spend a significant amount of time indoors as cultural, social and religious norms restrict the mobility of Rohinhgya women outside of their homes.

Activities or outputs

Concern will conduct distributions of essential NFI items to Rohingya refugee in the 9 camp where we operate our emergency nutrition programmes. Subject to government approval and NFI Sector recommendation, Concern will seek to introduce cash for NFIs programming in 2019. Key activites and outputs include:- Assessment of beneficiary NFI needs from field experience and in coordination with the Shelter and NFI Sector, with particular emphasis on meeting the needs of women and chilren who are the targeted beneficiaries of Concern's emergency nutrition centers. - Community consultation meetings to identify beneficiaries- Procurement, transportation, warehousing and distribution of NKI Kits to affected population- Distribution of cash grants for NFI items, targeting women refugees as the direct recipient of the grant and conducting participatory community awareness sessions to increase the decision making power of women in the household about the utilisation of the cash- Post-distribution monitoring with at least 5% of NFI Kit/Cash recipients, with responses disaggregated by sex and age
