Project Summary

As a response to the increasingly needs on the SHC and Trauma services, AHF established Hazarin hospital in June 2016 as a secured specialized trauma referral underground hospital, it is fully equipped to deal with all the medical trauma cases.
This project will provide the SHC services and Trauma health Services, to the affected population, through supporting a health facility in Hazarin in southern Idleb, in this project we will support supplying the hospital with the required running costs and staff salaries of the health facility.
We will receive through the hospital a large number of wounded people in the emergency department, and will perform orthopedic, general and vascular surgeries by a high-qualified staff.
We will provide the medical consultations to the patients in urgent cases as well as after the major surgeries.
The project will include the provision of the needed medications for the patients, and will provide the Lab tests too.
Through coordinating with "Sham ambulatory system" we will receive the medical cases from the surrounding HFs and refer the complicated cases to Turkey.
All our services will be free and we expect to reduce the numbers of patients who cross the border seeking medical care in Turkey.
The capacity of the Hospital is as the following:
1- Operation theater with 2 rooms. (there are 2 more operation rooms but not functioning)
2- Hospitalization and rehabilitation department with 22 beds.
3- Outpatient surgical clinics (2 clinics: general and orthopedic).
4- Laboratory.
5- X-Ray section and CT scan Machine.
6- Sterilization department.
7- Emergency department.
The staff consist of 66 persons including 6 specialized doctors and 6 resident surgeons.
The beneficiaries in many cases are multiple trauma patients and every minute can make the difference between life and death for them.

Needs

Field staff had conducted a rapid need assessment in March 2017 in Idleb city and some rural Idleb area. The method of the assessment was local authorities' interviews and the direct observation. The general finding was:1- There is a need for the Trauma and SHC services in the region of the project.2- The most of functioning HFS do not have a specialized surgical service like the neurosurgical and vascular surgeries.3- Many centers have faced difficulties in obtaining the needed drugs and the major reason that let medical staff flee out is decreasing in support for medical centers especially for the running cost (especially the staff salaries). Based on this assessment we agreed with the Health Directorate, that our Safe hospital is highly needed. According to the reports of the Health Directorate in Hamah and Idleb This project is highly needed and well known for the health cluster as one of the referral hospitals in Idleb and Hamah, and the only HF, which provide the vascular surgeries in the region of (southern Idleb and northern Hamah).Based on Hamah Health Directorate reports our HF is serving about 45% of total health care in the governorate. According to the medical reports of the hospital in the last project for 3 month (funded by WHO) we have: 1167 major surgery, 3409 dressing and minor surgery, Laboratory services 8238 Radiology services 1435. In the last few weeks (07-08 / SEP / 2018) three health facilities in Hama governorate were attacked by airstrikes (Latamneh Hospital, Kafr Zeita Hospital and Has Hospital "Sham") and one ambulatory system center in Khan Shikhon was attacked too.Also, Madeeq castle town got a lot of shelling which has a hospital in the front line, so a lot of injured are currently being transferred to Idlib hospitals due to lack of healthcare services in the region, and we received calls from doctors in Idleb asking us to operate Alsham Central Hospital with full capacity to relieve pressure on them.Now, there are only Alsham Central Hospital (which works with a volunteering staff with the minimum capacity and with no running costs) and Kafr Nobol Hospital (Orient) are providing the surgical health services to the northern of Hama population, after that the nearest hospital in Idleb is in AlMa'ra about 20 Km away.
