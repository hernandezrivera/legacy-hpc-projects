Project Summary

the following interventions targeting all 97 camps which both WASH and Shelter interventions are taking place in addition to complementary with other NGOs working in the camp site secured funds:
1.fire and safety response and mitigation measures through contingency planning, Safety, and Security through providing 1240 fire extinguishers and 382 first aid kits in 97 locations beside deliver trainings to the security points members.
2. sites support with care and maintenance activities through site maintenance and emergency works through hiring 28 workers and equip them with proper tools.
3.Support improvement measures to camp management structures and systems through delivering training to the camp management members in 97 camps.
4. providing humanitarian life-saving, multi-sectoral assistance to ensuring access to facilities (latrines, showers) for women and children, mitigating protection risks by improving and maintaining lighting and new solar lights.
5.Conduct a multi-sector need assessment in the targeted camps before the winter season to identify the gaps in these camps
6. multi-sectoral gap analysis of last resort IDP sites published (ISIMM).
7. 7. providing to CCCM Cluster and WG members, Clusters and other operational actors on sudden displacements monthly based that the direct information will provide on-time updated for better response.
Under the proposed project, BINAA will remain accountable to affected populations through open dialogue and feedback/complaint mechanisms provided to these populations so will have a direct channel to BINAA.
Furthermore, BINAA will routinely consult and engage the communities and create a safe place for beneficiaries and the wider community to provide feedback on the project in general. BINAA staff will also introduce the complainant channels to the beneficiaries and follow up with on resolving their and emerged issues.
BINAA through CCCM intervention will be able to provide valuable information to the CCCM cluster through ISIMM, ISIMM Plus, and 4Ws tools.
BINAA with coordinate with the camp management to ensure smooth implementation for the project.
All the activities have a high priority to be implemented because of the bad living conditions in the targeted camps which contain a huge number of IDPs, that BINAA belive that the implementing of this project will mitigate the mentioned conditions, safe lives, and provide better organization to the targeted camps beside providing income-generating opportunitie to the IDPs.

4 - Needs

*The CCCM has identified close to 1,766,874 people as being in need, including an estimated 1.5 million people in the north-west and almost 266,874 in the North-east Syria (NES) living in IDP sites, often with inadequate access to basic services. And the incidents continue to impact IDP sites, exacerbating existing challenges and vulnerabilities. Over 100 incidents in NWS. BINAA had conducted the needs assessment survey between 16 March 2021 to 12th of April 2021 which aims to assess the WASH, shelter, Early Recovery Livelihood (ERL) and CCCM needs of the population in need at the camp level for 240 camps in Idleb and Aleppo governorate to provide information on statistics of the affected population in addition to monitoring the condition of existing WASH services, housing needs and its types, infrastructure situations and basic services rehabilitation and restore.( Attachment_N01_MSNA_Needs Assessment Report) The main key findings out of 240 IDPs sites in CCCM cluster were:*97% stated lack of streetlights and power sources for their camps sites which consist mainly challenges to their HHs related to protection issues.*75% reported their camps need for firefighting equipment and trained response team to save their HHs lives during fire incidents which caused injuries and life lost for number of individuals and children during winter. All the activities have a high priority to be implemented because of the bad living conditions in the targeted camps which contain a huge number of IDPs, that BINAA belive that the implementing of this project will mitigate the mentioned conditions, safe lives, and provide better organization to the targeted camps beside providing income-generating opportunitie to the IDPs.
