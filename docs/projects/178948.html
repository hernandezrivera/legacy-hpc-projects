Project Summary

The project aims to increase the resilience of the flood-affected communities in Harim, thus reducing their dependence on aid, and securing them increased access to livelihood opportunities, which are among the most vulnerable groups of society. The project will focus on the displaced and the host community as well.
The project aims to implement major infrastructure rehabilitation projects to improve access to basic services and basic infrastructure for the most vulnerable IDPs and the host community and those living in Harem with the aim of accessing livelihoods through the creation of Quick Impact Work Opportunities (CfW) for 300 displaced families from the most vulnerable population In Harem camp (200 men, 100 women; 40 of them are people with special needs).
At the beginning of the project, the Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation will sign memoranda of understanding with the civil bodies responsible for the camps and then make announcements about the project and clarify its goal and call for the beneficiaries to be registered in the Cash for Work (CfW) program for people in the targeted sites who can work in the priority sectors, then The verification process to select the beneficiaries according to the criteria that will be announced, including the most vulnerable groups (without an income, a family headed by a woman, etc.). The Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation will provide basic training for the selected beneficiaries.
The Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation will also establish community participation teams in the Harem camps, a team for each site consisting of (9 members for each team + 1 team leader) and train the teams for at least 3 days on work mechanisms, improving self-reliance and effective community participation, then the “Al-Sham Foundation” will hold Humanitarian "Meetings with community members to share the results of the community assessment and consult with them to prioritize the community's needs for major infrastructure projects. In cooperation with the groups, the results are validated
Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation will prepare a detailed work plan for the projects and prepare all supporting documents such as (bills of quantities, detailed budget, plans, timelines, procurement and supply plans ...) to implement the rehabilitation of the main infrastructure approved by the CfW.
The focus of this project will be on implementing the following projects
Rehabilitation of public markets
Rehabilitation of facilities related to water, sanitation and hygiene: such as water tanks
Rehabilitation of public service facilities: such as the fire station
Rehabilitation of rainwater drainage canals
School rehabilitation
Rehabilitation of important roads
Rehabilitation of waste disposal sites
Rehabilitation of public transport facilities: such as bus stations
In connection with COVID19; The project will follow the recommendations of the ERL and Health Group during the implementation of the project, by conducting awareness sessions for the beneficiaries and project staff, while providing the necessary personal protective equipment at work sites, maintaining social distancing measures, identifying suspected cases between workers, and coordinating with the medical sector in the Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation. To carry out the necessary examination and follow-up with the great experience and services that the institution has in this field and the hospitals and isolation center it supervises.
Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation will strive to achieve the goal of the project through a set of activities and operations, taking into account accountability to affected people, mainstreaming protection and gender-based violence / protection from sexual exploitation and sexual exploitation, risk analysis and mitigation, monitoring and evaluation, and effective coordination with stakeholders, And presenting an exit strategy that contributes to the sustainability of the project.
