Plan fields

Needs

• To ensure that 60,000 targeted women, girls, men and boys including people living with disability and the elderly have the means and are encouraged adapt individual and collective measures increasing health seeking behaviours and practices to mitigate public health risks by the end of the response.• To ensure that 10,000 school going girls and boys in target learning centres receive age appropriate health and hygiene messaging on health seeking behaviours and good hygiene practices to mitigate public health risks by end of the response. • To ensure effective, sufficient and context appropriate provision of life-saving water and sanitation services for 20,000 targeted women, girls, men and boys including people living with disability and the elderly by the end of the response.

Activities

Hygiene Promotion Services• Provision and installation of hand-washing stations in key locations.• Distribution of hygiene NFIs and IEC materials, accompanied by key messages.• Distribution of ‘point of use’ water treatment products – aquatabs or water guards.• Cholera awareness campaigns• Training of community volunteersWater Services• Rehabilitation of water points/sources• Provision of portable water quality testing consumables• Testing water samples at water points/sources and HH levels• Testing FRC on water samples per day• Water trucking (only in worst case scenario)Sanitation Services• Clean-up campaigns• Promotion of demand led total sanitation
