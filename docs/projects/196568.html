Project Summary

Youth Charity based on these reports carried out its own assessment by studying and analyzing the situation through visits by the survey team of the association to ALyarmouk area of Damascus governorate and meeting with key people to reach an accurate study and determine the need optimally, and an important reason that helped in the ease of access to execute survey is that some of the team members are from the population of the targeted areas, and which helped to gain more confidence.
ALyarmouk is a city one of the affected areas by the Syrian crisis, with a damage exceeds 75% of the area including medical centers and related health sector facilities. The area is now considered under government control
ALyarmouk is an area that has a population of over 7,500 out of this most of them are returnees: 45 % women, 25 % men, 17 % boys and 13 % girls. Most residents are currently very poor and internally displaced after ten years of war and , resulted in most of them need medical care and services. High percentage of the population are children and families who rely on women as their main breadwinners. The whole targeted area has no hospitals nor medical centers which means lack of medical services, and the medical situation there is very poor with no existence of the clinics for people and very poor medical services.
The project aims at establishing Mobile Medical Clinic in AlYarmouk area in order to provide free medical services will provide it to 5,500 of the aforementioned. (Primary Health Services, Secondary Health Services and Trauma Services) with referral system, in addition, providing free medication, and find job opportunities to jobless people of the area (mainly women). The number of beneficiaries was determined according to a specific logical study, where approximately /50/ patients are received per day in one clinic. Those patients in return receive medical consultations by specialized doctors in addition to medical analysis, surgeries, and trauma services.
The location of the mobile medical clinic will be located in AlYarmouk at the main square.
And referral system will be executed in coordination with local hospitals in Damascus for the to receive secondary health services. And medical services include free medication and the source of medicines is the Pharmacists Syndicate. Trauma services will be provided in the emergency clinic in mobile medical clinics in the following cases: emergency wounds, fractures, and abscesses.
YC maintains provision of the aforementioned health care services to all who seek medical care without ethnic, religious, gender, and/or political discrimination, as all medical services are being

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The risk of failure to provide full free service, and this will be addressed through permanent monitoring and daily contact with beneficiaries and by implementing active strict quality procedure and penalties and if impossible to deal with this case in Daraa without the budget we will transfer the case for executive management team of the YC association In referral system there is the risk of the non-availability of a specified medical service and doctors in the local area, and this will be addressed by transferring referrals of beneficiaries to Damascus and provide beneficiaries with medical attendance cards to facilitate mobility. The risk of continuous power outages and this will be addressed by providing generator to mobile clinic.The risk of lacking commitment of some beneficiaries of the continuing treatment process and will be addressed by daily follow-up medical system and conducting daily questionnaires to beneficiaries and closing each case separately. The issue of non-occurrence of gender-based violence (women & children), violence against children, and child labor will be pursued, The raising in exchange rates due to the economic situation in the country.
