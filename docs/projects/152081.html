Needs

The ongoing armed conflict in the East of Ukraine has a negative impact on the socio-economic situation and psychophysical condition of the residents of the Donetsk region.High unemployment, low social payments and wages, administrative restrictions have sharply reduced the quality of life of the inhabitants of the region. Daily attacks, the dilapidated infrastructure of cities and towns in the region, the tension in society aggravate the mental health of citizens.In such emergency, residents often become addicted to alcohol and/or drugs, receive PTSD, or are simply exposed to the risk of physical damage from military attacks and mines.This project aims to support and promote healthy lifestyles of vulnerable residents of the Donetsk region, through health group sessions and trainings for such citizens, as well as support the infrastructure to help them, strengthening the relevant budget professionals and volunteers of public organizations

Activities & outcomes

1.To develop a set of training activities in full-time, remote and institutional ("training of trainers") forms aimed at ensuring a healthy lifestyle, first aid skills, first psychological assistance and mine safety (at least 3 types of teaching materials).2.Provide remote and full-time, and also institutional (training of future thematic teachers and strengthening of their material and technical base) introduction of the specified above competences (not less than 50 participants of the program of trainings of trainers, not less than 2000 full-time and 50,000 remotely passed through the program of needing persons.The methodology for selecting beneficiaries will be community activists, community leaders, educators and residents of the most vulnerable areas to mines and shelling, as well as IDPs and those directly affected by military conflict, while respecting gender balance standards.Within the framework of psychological assistance, the needy will be able to better analyze their own mental states and acquire with the help of professional psychologists basic skills to provide themselves and others with the first psychological help. Monitoring of the project is provided within the framework of preliminary verification of those in need, measuring their feedback immediately on the results and subsequently after the completion of the project through face-to-face questionnaires and telephone calls.Within the framework of procurement procedures, mass purchases of specialized handouts and training products are planned, which allow to deploy appropriate classes, primarily in the field of teaching first aid on the basis of local educational institutions in the most vulnerable and high-risk locations.

Indicators & targets

Not less than 3 open-access applied adapted training materials, not less than 50 trained thematic teachers, not less than 2000 people full-time trained in the basic competencies of medical resilience and self-help, not less than 50,000 people, distance learning. The target is the creation of mass mechanisms to reduce the risks of mine threat, the introduction of first aid, overcoming the consequences of PTSD, the foundations of a healthy lifestyle. Long-term improvement of competence of the most needy segment of society on the specified skills.
