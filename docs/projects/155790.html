Project Summary

The Govt. of Pakistan launched military operation Zarb-e-Azb in North Waziristan Agency (NWA) on June 15, 2014, targeting non-state armed actors’ hideouts to curb the menace of terrorism. This prompted large scale displacement to neighboring districts in KP province. Return to NWA officially started from April 2015 till Oct. 2016 . According to the FDMA’s (FATA Disaster Management Authority) Situation Report of TDP’s return as of October 10, 2018, out of the total caseload of 107,391 families, 92,618 families have returned back to North Waziristan Tribal District (NWTD) erstwhile NWA. The erstwhile FATA EMIS report for 2016-17 shows that more than one million (1,038,933) children in the age group of 04-14 are out of school in the tribal areas. The overall drop-out rate in Government Primary Schools over a cycle of 6 years is 76% (74% Boys, 80% Girls).
As per the FATA Vulnerability Assessment (August-September 2017) report around half of the respondents expressed dissatisfaction with the quality of education due to lack of basic facilities, lack of teachers, teaching materials and damage to school infrastructure. In terms of damaged infrastructure, the RRU data shows that 943 educational institutions were partially and fully damaged in five tribal districts. According to the FATA Education department Statistical information of 2016 -17, a total of 218 schools are damaged including 91 girls’ schools in different Tehsils of North Waziristan Tribal District (NWTD). The Education Cluster in its meeting on 20 November, 2018, has agreed on a case-load of 100,000 children (50% girls) to be supported in 2019 for continuation of education.
The proposed education project envisages re-construction and re-building of 08 fully damaged Govt. primary/secondary schools (4 being girls schools) in NWTD particularly in Tehsil Miranshah and Ghulam Khan where ADF is currently active in WASH sector response. Moreover ADF will focus on the following activities for its proposed project;
•Provision of safe and child-and adolescent-friendly temporary school facilities/rehabilitation of damaged institutions with latrines and water facilities that are gender and age sensitive; (08 Schools)
•Provision of recreation materials for psychosocial support to raise children’s self-esteem, reinforce resilience and provide an opportunity for self-expression, including early child development materials to 08 schools.
•Cash stipend/incentive to encourage enrolment and retention of children at primary level, and for girls at secondary level as per Education cluster's approved incentive package.
•Provision of school supplies (school-in-a-box), recreation kits, school bags and stationery for 08 schools.
•Hiring of teachers on contract basis to reopen closed schools, if needed in schools with teacher deficiency
•Formation of school management committees for improved school-community linkages in the villages of the target schools.
•Accelerated Learning Programme for over-age dropped out children.

Needs

The proposed education project envisages re-construction and re-building of fully damaged Govt. primary or secondary schools in the target Tehsils of North Waziristan. Each school will be provided with a Pre-fabricated model having 2 classrooms (size 24'x15') with veranda (size 6'x48') and 2 pour flush latrines. Repair work of the school boundary wall, play ground, renovation, etc will be carried out as per on ground real time assessments. Drinking water facility will be either made functional if exists or installed anew. A total of 1,040 children (50% being girls) aging between 0-14 years will receive access to quality education in friendly environment with reasonable facilities to help them regain their momentum. The shortage of school teachers will be fulfilled with induction of Para- teachers from the nearby community. Already existing and new teachers will be provided a basic training of 10-days at the start of the project on child- friendly teaching, lesson planning, teaching / learning methodology, classroom management, class healing approaches, multi-grade teaching and teaching English as a medium of instruction. A 2-days refresher training on the mentioned subjects will also be arranged towards the end of Winter vacations to teachers. Separate male and female TIJs will be established/reactivated for each school. ADF through their professional staff will establish/revitalize 10 TIJ (05 female TIJs) each comprising of 15 members (10x15=150 members) and subsequently its capacity building on social mobilization processes to facilitate mainstreaming education through their effective involvement. ADF will provide necessary supplies and learning materials in schools; such as blackboards, floor mats, Desk/chair, tables, school stationary, chalks, admission, attendance and stock registers, etc. school-in-a-box with items like bags for children, necessary stationery for children, stationary, school uniform, a school recreational/sports kit, health and hygiene kits for students.
