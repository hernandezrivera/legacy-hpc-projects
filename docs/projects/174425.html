Project Summary

Over 12 months, the proposed intervention will assist the Government of Pakistan to increase the sustainability and impact of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government’s COVID-19 response plan. As of 9th December 2020 the number of confirmed COVID-19 positive cases in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is 46,604 and 1,355 deaths are reported. The response will address the needs of communities through an integrated approach that incorporates interventions in water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH), Food Security and Protection. The action has been designed to complement synergies of government departments and adhere to the protocols of government and the World Health Organization (WHO) while keeping the approach dynamic and adaptive to enable resources to be adapted in the evolving COVID-19 situation. Overall, the project aims to save the lives of covid-19 affected families in KP by supporting them to sustain their livelihood and improving their living condition and resilience. This objective will be achieved through i) introducing COVID-19 relevant WASH infrastructure at appropriate places; ii) behavioral change through dissemination of context-specific and culture-sensitive health & hygiene messages iii) integrated household food system assistance iv) ensuring the most vulnerable population have access to protection interventions. Through this proposed project, Cesvi will target vulnerable communities in six calamity affected districts of KP that have been prioritized under the HRP; Swat, Nowshera, Charsada, Peshawar, Khyber, North Waziristan. In total, the project will support over half a million people.

1- Project Activities

WASH 1.1 Promotion of critical WASH-related information using interpersonal communication, social and mass media, radio jingles, billboards and radio & shows.Under this activity, it will be ensured that people have the life-saving information they need to protect themselves and others from COVID-19 pandemic and to reduce its impact on their health and social life. Efforts will be made to have consistency and coherency in the awareness messaging and language to avoid misinformation. Channels will be identified to reach audiences e.g. mass media, radio listeners, social media platforms such as Twitter and Facebook etc. Cesvi will ensure that all communication is inclusive and sensitive to the needs of people with disabilities. Cesvi has provided RCCE support across KP in 2020 and plans to continue the interventions in 2021 based on the evolving needs during COVID-19 pandemic. Direct interaction with women even through women staff members is challenging in KP: the monitoring team will opt alternative possible means to measure this indicator with women e.g. involvement of LHW or lady teachers to interview the women who received session on hygiene promotion.1.2 Provision of appropriate WASH installations at schools and healthcare facilities Our assessment indicates schools and basic health facilities lack sanitation facilities resulting in difficulties for students (especially girls) and patients (especially female). In 4 districts of KP, schools and healthcare faculties will receive WASH installations, including hand washing stations and sanitation facilities. Cesvi will also provide drinking water supply schemes at schools and healthcare facilities. Activity 1.3 Installation of hand washing stations at key public places and distribution of soap.One of the key preventive measures for COVID-19 is to practice frequent hand washing with soap. Provision of such facilities is also an important goal in the Pakistan Preparedness and Response Plan. Cesvi will support the government in this sector through the provision of paddle-operated hand washing stations. Cesvi has provided no-touch paddle operated hand washing stations in KP, including NMDs, and in the process of installing hand washing stations at public areas and communities affected by floods in Sindh. Therefore, Cesvi is well positioned to provide the assistance in response to COVID-19 in districts with unmet needs.Food Security 1.1 Cash/food/Integrated Household Food Systems assistance will be provided to those affected by multiple shocksCesvi will assist returnees and IDPs who have lost their source of livelihoods as a result of conflict and/or COVID-19 pandemic. Cesvi will restore microenterprises/small business of affected communities in districts North Waziristan and Khyber through provision of support to develop business plans and in-kind grants to set up the businesses such as grocery shops, tailoring businesses and fruit shops. Cesvi is currently implementing programs to restore livelihoods and respond to COVID-19 in the proposed districts. Protection 1.1 Economic resilience and livelihood support for women affected by emergenciesDue to COVID-19 outbreak and challenging cultural norms, women in KP are in urgent need of livelihood support. To mitigate the risk, Cesvi will establish micro businesses for 200 women in remote districts of KP. 1.2 Disseminate information and promote dialogue among families and communities on stigma and Violence against Children (VAC) and GBV preventionIn coordination with local government, Cesvi will provide protection sensitization trainings in areas of intervention. Cesvi will conduct ToTs at district level and the master trainers will cascade the trainings to communities. Cesvi will also develop the training module on child protection which will be utilized in the training sessions. Cesvi will also sensitize the communities on the use of existing child protection services, hotlines and referral mechanisms.
