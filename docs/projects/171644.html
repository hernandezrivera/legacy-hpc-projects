Project Summary

Through this proposed project, Aid Vision will operationalize one health facility and further scale up Garasbaley and Shangani MCH that will offer life-saving primary health care services that includes but not limited to: free consultation and treatments for both under-fives and over five at the outpatient departments, immunization services, early detection and treatment of communicable diseases, free 24hours skilled, Free antenatal and postnatal care, early detection and treatment of communicable diseases both at the facility and community level by the trained health care workers. Through this initiative, community health workers will also be engaged who will active case finding, defaulter tracing, COVID-19 awareness, hygiene promotion and messaging for the hard to reach area in the target districts, further Capacity build trainings will be conducted for both qualified staff as well as community health worker to ensure quality services are offered to the vulnerable communities in the target area. For purpose of the sustainability of the project, Aid Vision will employ community based participatory approach through strengthening community capacity by capacity building own staff, local Authority staff, MoH staff and training of CHWs and established community health committees while observing gender inclusion in the top leadership of the designed committees. This approach ensures at the end of the project a strong cadre of community-based structures are built for community ownership and sustainability of the program. Further, Aid Vision will closely work with other agencies in the region by collaborating with other humanitarian key stakeholders in the area of Nutrition/WASH/Education/Food security and protection for better coordination and avoid duplication.

Mitigation Measures

Members of marginalized community’s/ minority groups who are not often included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance will be deliberately targeted to participate in decision making. Clear stakeholder analysis will be commenced to ensure inclusivity at decision making levels of the project. Women, girls and boys are often the ones left out in consultations and decision-making processes because of their age due cultural barriers. The project will ensure community decision makers will be representatives of all, young and old, men and women, boys and girls to ensure the specific needs of each are adequately listened to and addressed. The project will address the risk of exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance. This will be done through: staff and vendors signing code of conduct that expressly prohibits the above violations and makes consequences of the same clear. Exposure of displaced women and girls to sexual violence, caused by living in highly insecure settlements, distance to and location of humanitarian assistance and services will be addressed through scheduling of distributions, meetings and consultative forums in such a way that they do not pose risks to beneficiaries, particularly girls and women. The distances covered by girls and women to seek services will also be compliant to Sphere Minimum Standards and other best health best practices. Synergies and collaborations will be developed with other partners in the field shelter and camp coordination to ensure there is complementarity in addressing needs of IDPs in holistic manner. Aid Vision runs nutrition and Education projects in Garasbaley of Deynile and Shangani District through the support from UNICEF and WFP. The proposed project will be complemented with nutrition/Education and protection projects in the target Districts
