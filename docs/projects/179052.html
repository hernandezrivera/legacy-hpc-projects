Project Summary

The project is located in DeirEzZor "DEZ" governorate and consisted of two key component areas: preparing currently out of school children(boys, girls) to return to formal schools, and securing equitable access to education provision for children and youth in DEZ. The project will reach 1500 children (Boys, Girls)in the age of 7 - 17 including children with disabilityCWD.
5 ‘Back to Learning’ campaign will be undertaken in all areas and will focus on the social, economical or safety barriers which children suffer from in the project area to access education. This will raise awareness with community leaders, parents, children and other stakeholders about project activities, and will encourage students enrolment with a non-formal provision as well as securing retention in formal provision; reminders of the advantages and opportunities of education as this was addressed in HNO 2021.
As well as giving practical details of services, this will also inform parents of the project’s efforts to mitigate the costs of school attendance and enhance children to back to schools (eg: by providing textbooks and stationery). the project includes provide minor rehabilitation work for three formal schools- provide schools furniture and gender and disability sensitive wash facilities. . This work also forms part of the recruitment strategy for the emerging Parent/Teacher Associations,to secure ongoing community support and participation in the provision of stable education services.
For children not currently in school, non-formal education services will be provided in each of the project locations. based on children needs assessment, there will be two cycle of ALP one June-September targeting children with minor gaps in their education progress, therefore likely to transition to formal education for the coming academic year. The second cycle will be from Oct- Feb, supporting children with longer periods out of school and therefore in need for more education support before transitioning to formal education. Additional ongoing support will be provided throughout the academic year once children have transferred to formal schools; this will support their retention in this provision through remedial classes and homework support as appropriate. Children will be identified by the placement test for according to needs/age (difficulties with learning, access to learning spaces outside of school, at risk of dropping out. the project includes mobile education modality which is tailored to provide education due to the absence of formal schools and education actors to provide education or establish TLS or home based centers with mobile and fixed teachers to cover OOS children who are above 12 and under 18 from boys, girls . Non-formal education programs will integrate Child Protection activities (through the provision of non-structured PSS sessions/ recreational of 3 sessions, with the possibility to include SEL component with PSS activities ) and referrals were needed, both within IPV service network and externally to other NGOs and service providers. security and safety plans will be established and resourced at each location. Stationery and textbooks will be provided for children and teachers. Professional development and capacity building activities will enhance the performance and quality of education personnel. This will include Child Protection training for all personnel. All teachers will develop their capacity for working in crisis contexts (through programmes in establishing child- and trauma-friendly environments, classroom management, effective pedagogues etc).
The project also includes provision of incentives for the teachers (both formal and non-formal teacher). Through all these components, the project will therefore improve the access to and the quality of formal and non-formal education NES. Finally, all the COVID 19 preventative measures shall be taken into acount while implementing the project activities.

4 - Needs

according to HNO2021: An estimated 6.9 million people (97 per cent children) needhumanitarian education assistance. in 2020, an estimated 2.45 million children were out of school and 1.6 million children were at risk of dropping out. Due to the cumulative effect of the conflict, the closure of learning facilities, and/or reduced learning hours due to COVID-19, worsening economic conditions, increased population numbers and displacement, the numbers of children out of school, and children who are at risk of dropping out, is almost certain to increase. and according to HNO total number of people in need in DEZ is about 0.6M people from children and adults. DEZ governorate with the proposed locations sub-districts are under-served and the lack of education services leaves children without access to quality formal or non-formal education. in addition, the rate of OOSC is high due to the bad economic situation and social, access, safety barriers. the project area suffers Due to the lack of education services, children are exposed to risks and abuse such as child labour (mainly boys?) and early marriage (mainly girls). the area suffers from a lack of capacities and qualified teachers which reflect the critical need to build the capacities for the teachers for formal and nonformal and proposed educational program as well.

5 - Implementation of activities

All the targeted locations are ranking 4 according to severity scale the selected activities are aligned with the most education priorities mentioned in the needs assessments which IPV will conduct, as well as the standard activities of the education working group.IPV will implement the project in coordination with the local authorities in the targeted locations in order to obtain the official permits for the project activities, as well as the coordination will include the main humanitarian actors in the project location to avoid the overlapping on one side and to benefit from the other provided services in other hands (FSL, Protection, Nutrition, NFI and Health).IPV will be committed to implementing the project activities building on the approved SOPs by the relevant authority for each activity, and under the principle of DO-NO HARM further to the full obtain of humanitarian standards and principles in delivering the humanitarian response. Finally, the selection beneficiaries from the teachers and children process shall take into account the gender sensitivity to be equal for female and male, and improve the access of girls and children with disabilities to a safe education. Safety and dignity are clearly marked in the rehabilitation work in the schools, incorporating visible access for people with disability, appropriate sanitation facilities for girls and boys.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Risk: Harassment among the project staff on the one hand, as well as possible incidents of sexual exploitation between teachers and their male and female students.IPV have set many mitigation strategies to address PSEA, which include: the PSEA mandatory training and related COC-PSEA and CSG, which the staff must align with and obtained, with the clear clarification that the IPV have zero tolerance with any SEA accidents. IPV has PSEA focal points in the working area and there will be focal points in the project centre for any reporting needed or support in PSEA mainstreaming. IPV will do awareness-raising for PSEA which include community level, working staff level and centre level which could include: brochures and banners, videos, awareness raining messages and hotlines. IPV will ensure that all the working staff is wellknown about the internal reporting and feedback mechanism taken in place in case of SEA occurrence cases. all the project's service providers or contractures who has direct contact with project beneficiaries from children or women must be trained and signed COC. IPV will ensure that during any conducted sessions there will be a female member to ensure beneficiaries safety, especially from children and women.
