1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Food insecurity is soaring as conflict and repeated displacement, compounded by climatic shocks, have disrupted communities’ agricultural activities and livelihoods in Cabo Delgado. According to FEWS Nets , in 2024, Crisis (IPC Phase 3) outcomes will likely persist in the remote semi-arid areas of southern and central regions due to a likely below-average harvest, high food prices, and limited market access by the very poor households who will continue to engage in coping strategies to meet their food needs in the harvest period. In Cabo Delgado, the ongoing and planned distribution of humanitarian food assistance in secure areas will continue to support Stressed! (IPC Phase 2!) outcomes, with Crisis (IPC Phase 3) likely in more conflict-affected areas.From October to December 2023, strong El Niño phenomena is expected to delay the start of the 2023/24 rainy season by around one decade (10 days) with mixed performance, especially in southern and parts of central Mozambique. Irregular rainfall and reduced access to agricultural inputs will likely affect the area planted, affecting agricultural labor opportunities for poor households. Government and donors should prepare now for rising food assistance needs in 2024. The project intends to enhance the food security and livelihoods of IDPs and host communities. The target group are mainly vulnerable IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in the districts.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1.0. 35500 both IDPs and host communities affected by conflict in district of Montepuez, Mueda, Meluco, Mocimboa da praia and Palma to be provided with emergency life-saving assistance to ensure their nutritional needs are met. The following activities will be undertaken: 1) Rapid assessment of people in need and alternative livelihood resource availability.2) Provision of emergency food and water assistance to people in need3) Cash assistance to help meet basic needs.4) Support to protect livelihoods.5) Support that restarts disrupted livelihoods and small business activities6) Household and livelihood vulnerability analysis7) Asset creation (creating local assets that reduce food insecurity and build livelihood opportunities).8) School meals and horticulture gardening activities.9) Train staff and government officials.10) Identify IDPs in need of life-saving food assistance.11) Plan and set beneficiary figures for food distribution in consultation with appropriate stakeholders.12) Provide recipients with distribution plans with information on distribution points, quality, and quantity to be provided.13) Distribute life-saving food assistance to targeted IDPs.14) Monitor and report distribution of life-saving food assistance.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will conduct security risk assessments to identify potential risks that may harm project personnel. Social and Environmental Responsibility assessment will be contacted to assess any possible negative impact of the project. Through, community consultations, possible sources of conflict and risk will be identified.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Identification of potential communities and beneficiary and the selection of vulnerable people will be conduct in consultation with community leadership and government officials. This will be conducted to ensure that everyone in need is properly identified and included. The project will conduct vulnerability assessment with the use of the Households vulnerability assessment tool (HVAT). The toll will enable project to identify different household vulnerability situation and determine those in need to be targeted.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

ForAfrika will be guided by the IASC Guidelines on the inclusion of Person with Disability in Humanitarian Action (2019) which encourages organizations to promote meaningful participation, remove barriers, empower persons with disabilities, and disaggregate data for monitoring inclusion. Specifically, ForAfrika will:1. Review organizational policies, guidelines, and tools to ensure they affirm the rights of persons with disabilities to access and inclusion.2. ii Ensure feedback and complaints mechanisms are designed to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities.3. Train staff on the inclusion of persons with disabilities, familiarize staff on IASC guidelines, encourage staff to identify persons with disabilities and involve them in implementation and propose measures to overcome barriers. 4. Seek advice, guidance, and support from organizations, and coordination platforms specialized in working with persons with disabilities and mainstreaming inclusion.5. Ensure that all data collected is disaggregated at a minimum by gender, age, and disability, and analyze data accordingly.6. Ensure that persons with a range of disabilities participate in key informant interviews, FGD, and in needs assessment teams. Guarantee representation of persons with disabilities by including girls, boys, women, and men.7. vii. FORAFRIKA will understand the needs, capacities, and difficulties of persons with disabilities and their caregivers.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed project will have some medium environmental impact due to waste generated from life-saving food assistance and the effect of livelihood interventions on the environment. To minimize the environmental impact of the project, ForAfrika will ensure:1. Conduct environmental screening assessment of the project.2. Prepare an environmental plan to mitigate the finding of the screening exercise.3. Carry out mitigation measures, including:  Environmental awareness sessions with the target beneficiaries, other stakeholders, and other implementing partners Plan the solid waste management scheme in collaboration with local authorities to avoid contamination of water sources, agricultural land, diseases, and odors. Encourage climate-smart agriculture practices that promote Water resource management, soil preparation, and agroforestry. Consult the beneficiaries to get their views and meaningful, involve them in new environmental coping strategies. Promote tree planting projects in the target locations. Identify the environmental impact on human livelihoods and promote the best practices that have less impact on the environment.
