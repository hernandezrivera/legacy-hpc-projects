Project Summary

Concern will rehabilitate five water supply systems in Aleppo governorates, Concern will conduct a need assessment in order to have a clear picture of the situation closely, the need assessment will target the vulnerable groups as well including women, elderly and people with disability, the need assessment will use three approaches including baseline survey, FGDs and KIIs. Based on this assessment. Concern will ensure that each water system is functional by installing of solar panels and have the capacity to deliver a safe water by installing chlorination systems and supporting each station with enough chlorine for six months. Through this request, Concern will identify two affected sewerage system and target them by rehabilitation, this will include replacement of the old and damaged pipes, construction of manholes, and clean the existing and functional pipes.
Based on assessment and the recommendations from the WASH cluster, Concern suggested to rehabilitate the follwoing water stations 1)Hersh Al Kaldiah (with a total number of beneficiaries 2,265 IDPs living in the camps), 2) Basuta (with a total number of beneficiaries 644 IDPs living in the camps and 5,800 in the communities), 3) Ain Dara (with a total number of beneficiaries 926 IDPs living in the camps and 2,200 in the communities), 4) Basma (with a total number of beneficiaries 2,591IDPs living in the camps ) and 5) Taibah water station (with a total number of beneficiaries 652 IDPs living in the camps and 4,922 in the communities) in Afrin district, Afrin sub district, while the sewage system will be implemented in Al Basuta and Taibah to connect the closed camp with the sewage system

3 - Needs

After over a decade of violent conflict, humanitarian needs across Syria remain severe with the continued need for emergency humanitarian assistance to meet the needs of over 13.5 million in need. Concern in partnership with Syria Relief completed a short term project to support the IDPs living the camps in Afrin by water trucking and sanitation emergency intervention in response to the cholera outbreak, the project was completed in Dec 2022, and reported to the WASH cluster as gap. During this period, Concern designed the proposed activities under this request for long-term solutions. Along with that, Concern built this request based on the secondary data and assessments, mainly based on the cluster recommendation, severity report, and coordination with other partners on the ground. In response to these needs, Concern suggested through this request to rehabilitate 5 water supply system targeting the most affected communities, this rehabilitation will include the installation of five chlorination systems in order to make sure the water safe for drinking. In addition, Concern will rehabilitation of two sewerage system.

4 - Implementation of activities

Activity (1): Repair and rehabilitation of water systems: Concern will identify the affected water systems and will be shortlisted according to several criteria’s including but not limited to the urgency of needs, damages of the system, number of beneficiaries, number of cholera cases in the community and cost recovery existence. In order to maintain the functionality, Concern will install Solar panels beside the diesel generators, the solar system will decrease the operation cost needed for those system, which can be reflected to a stable cost recovery system. Concern will conduct a training sessions to the targeted water departments for general and periodic maintenance needed and support them with tool kits and PPE (Personal Protection Equipments). Activity 2: Support to water quality assurance, operation and maintenance of water supply systems: Concern will maintain 1 mg/L at the water source (water station) in order to have enough chlorine demands at the end of the network. Concern will hire a water quality monitors to ensure the FRC at HHs level are meet the standard and provide enough storage of chlorine. Activity 3: Repair, rehabilitation and operational support to sanitation/sewage systems: Concern will target the most affected communities by rehabilitation of the sewerage system. The rehabilitation will include fixing/Replacing the damaged municipal sewage pipes and manholes to ensure the functionality of these systems.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empower the position of armed groups. Mitigation measures include: Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organise joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Risk of Improper storage of hazardous supplies (chlorine and other) materials) - Possibility of explosion, human casualty and asset destruction. Mitigation measures include: Ensure proper storage of supplies with clear provision of care and safety consideration. Make sure that clear instruction for use is available for chlorine products primarily for household use. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Risk of theft of chemicals such as chlorine Hypochlorite which may be attractive to armed groups. Mitigation measures include: Work closely with all partners on the ground; Promote distribution stock between multiple warehouses in the target regions (for large quantities of stock). Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organisation, its partners or contractors. Mitigation includes: Ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ labourers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labour in the contracts; check and register civil documentation; Likelihood: High, Impact: High

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk of low quality of items expose survivors to further harm, discrimination, and stigmatization. Mitigation measures: Technical speciation on place (standard), samples along with the offer, random check comparing with the offer. Risks related to the location of service provision (safety of movements for beneficiary, contamination from remnants of war, proximity with sensitive buildings). Mitigation measures: choice of locations done in close consultation and agreement with the community, conduction of safety audits. Risk: COVID 19 and Cholera: Not adherence to preventive and IPC related measures can increase the risk of transmission to beneficiaries. Mitigation measures include monitoring of adherence to preventive measures; reduction of beneficiary's numbers for group activities provision of PPE, trainings, suspension of service provision if increase in the number of staff with confirmed/suspected infection occurs
