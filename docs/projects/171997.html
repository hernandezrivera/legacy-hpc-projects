Project Summary

Within the Bangladesh Preparedness and Response Plan (BPRP) for COVID-19, the “Contact Tracing and Reducing Community Transmission” pillar identifies the CST initiative as one of the key interventions to reduce the spread of COVID-19 by ensuring syndromic quarantine of probable COVID-19 cases and their entire households. The CSTs can also aid mask distribution and reinforce proper mask-utilization behaviour in the communities. The “Risk Communication and Community Engagement” (RCCE) pillar also identifies the CST initiative as an important platform to raise community awareness about COVID-19 and drive behaviour change at the community level.
Community Support Team (CST) strategy is an extension of the Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh Preparedness and Response Plan (BPRP) for COVID-19, and lays out the conceptual framework, results chain, and multi-agency coordination efforts already underway to help
communities better control the spread of COVID-19.
This inter-agency collaborative encompasses diverse expertise for providing targeted health, advocacy, and food support to communities through a needs-based and risk-based approach that is sensitive to gender, health status and socio-economic differences. The overall aim of this platform is to continually adapt to the changing COVID-19 situation and needs of communities to strengthen the resilience of Bangladeshis in the face of COVID-19 and in response to new and emerging health threats.
Key activities include the Community Support Team initiative; Infection Prevention Control and Case Management; Risk Communication and Community Engagement; Research and Information Management
