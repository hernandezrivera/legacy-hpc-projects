Project Summary

The proposed project is aimed at strengthening the WASH response system in Borno state through rehabilitation of non-functional latrines, and showers, distribution of hygiene kits, COVID-19 Personal Protection equipment, and gender-sensitive, age-appropriate, and community tailored hygiene promotion to reach a total of 70,000 internally displaced men, women, boys and girls, construction of 100 handwashing points in IDP camps and communities across Kala/Balge, and Askira Uba LGAs of Borno state within one year.
From the total target aforementioned, 10,000 vulnerable household heads (60% men/adolescent boys & 40 women/adolescent girls) will be reached with sensitization on positive hygiene habits via a door-to-door approach in Kala-Balge LGA while 50,000 individuals will be reached with bi-monthly awareness-raising outreaches on positive hygiene habits using a motorized speaker & megaphone approach in Askira Uba and Kala-Balge LGAs. Key messages in both approaches will include prevention and control of COVID-19, Cholera, and Lassa fever.
GOALPrime will establish and strengthen the existing gender-incisive WASH committee with a 60:30:10 ratio of women, youth, and men in Askira-Uba and KalaBalge LGA respectively. All will be trained in construction, maintenance, repair, and management of the WASH facilities.
GOALPrime through the support of the WASH sector common pipeline will distribute 10,000 hygiene kits and PPEs to vulnerable household heads which will be reached with door-to-door sensitization on Chorale, Lassa Fever, and COVID-19.
GOALPrime will rehabilitate 150 non-functional latrines to reach a total of 900 individuals, and 100 household showers to reach a total of 600 individuals across the LGAs.
All WASH facilities will be designed considering the disposal of used menstrual hygiene products, accessible to children and the physically disabled, sex disaggregated, and clearly marked. They will be locale from the inside and well-lit, especially at night. Lighting will be done in such a way as to reduce the gathering of youth and men at WASH facility sites.
GOALPrime is in KalaBalge providing rehabilitation of toilet and shower support and have identify the gaps this project is programmed to address in 2021 response and will work with WASH sector, partners and Community leaders and households head in KalaBalge and Askira Uba LGA to ensure the WASH need of vulnerable community and IDPs are met.

1 - Needs

In Borno, one in four of the affected population is under 5 years of age. Women and children are mostly affected by the crisis as they constitute 81% of the overall crisis population and 87% of the new displacements (2020, Humanitarian Needs Overview). While whole families and communities are affected; children, women, especially pregnant and lactating mothers, the elderly, and persons with disabilities remain more vulnerable. The COVID-19 pandemic which is affecting Borno state has presented new challenges to WASH Interventions. Excerpts from the WASH Sector’s Needs Analysis in the 2020 HRP Addendum for COVID -19 Response Plan advises that the traditional sensitization of persons in groups is strongly discouraged and recommendations are made for door to door hygiene promotion, which requires an increase in personnel and volunteers to provide much-needed outreach activities as well as motorized speaker and megaphone awareness raising approaches. Also, the distribution of hygiene kits and other WASH NFIs have to adopt options that minimize contact, avoid crowding, which efforts will require additional logistical arrangements. As of 7 December 2020, 69,255 COVID-19 cases have been confirmed in Nigeria, with 1,180 deaths. In Borno, 745 have been recorded in the same period. Factors that contribute to the spread of COVID-19 are a weakened health system, multiple disease outbreaks, high population concentration in specific IDPs camps and Host Communities, lack of access to safe potable water and sanitation infrastructure, inadequate awareness on preventive measures, and traditional practices. Reference to the Borno State COVID-19 Preparedness and Response Plan, IDPs are a high-risk category for the spread of COVID-19. Kala-Balge appears to be among the LGAs with possible high risk of COVID-19 community transmission because of border town to an affected country, weak health system and lack of access to potable water; therefore, effective door to door hygiene promotion, preparedness and response are needed. From the There is a serious shortage of hygiene kits and cleaning supplies for latrines, homes, and health facility disinfection. There is great need for installation of handwashing stations in strategic and public places within the camp, support sanitation and rehabilitate non-functional latrines. In Kala-Balge, Terres Des Hommes (TDH) is providing boreholes and water point chlorination services hence gaps in other essential WASH services. GOALPrime has been on ground and constructed 25 latrines 1000 Hygiene kits through the support of the WASH sector and the Nigerian Humanitarian Fund. However, this is contributing to just 0.3% of the need in Latrines rehabilitation and Showers Hygiene kit distribution. More also, Borno state is faced with the realities of congested camps and the people have demonstrated poor hygiene practices over the years in the camps and host communities. If we must reduce the risk of transmission in amongst the internally displaced persons in camps and host communities, it is expedient to support the people of concern with soaps, water, and hygiene kit as well as increasing the number of water points in camps and host communities to reduce their risk of contracting the virus. There is a need to scale up hand washing drills amongst the IDPs so as to foster improved hygiene practices.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime’s accountability to the affected populations spans through the Program Cycle from the Rapid Specific Vulnerability Assessment through to design, through to implementation, and through to monitoring. The proposed project prioritizes constant creating of opportunities for review and feedback from beneficiaries, community leaders, partners present in location and donors. This integration of feedback and input from communities closely aligns with both the SPHERE Standards; Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Minimum Standard, and guidelines set forth by the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC). The pre-design assessment on the feasibility of this project in target locations collected information from local beneficiary populations, IDP camp coordinators, host community Bulama and a review of what other partners are doing in target LGAs via the WASH sector ReportHub partners update from target LGAs. As a result, the proposed project uses direct contributions in its proposed structure, scope, and proposed activities to allow a more community informed implementation process. Throughout the implementation of this project, GOALPrime shall engage participatory Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning (MEAL) and establish a platform that will allow participation and feedback from the community on project goals. GOALPrime shall conduct regular feedback surveys among the beneficiaries to gauge levels of satisfaction and solicit suggestions for project implementation and improvement.
