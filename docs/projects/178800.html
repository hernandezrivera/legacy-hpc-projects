Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to lifesaving and life-sustaining health assistance in NWS with focus on the most vulnerable groups, including IDPs and host communities in the target area. Another aim is to contribute in recovering the largely disrupted public health services across areas of displacement. The project will contribute to the following two objectives of Food security cluster: Objective 1: Increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable humanitarian health services for those most vulnerable and in need. Similarly, it is related to 2021 HRP Strategic Objective 1: Provide life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people, with an emphasis on those in areas with a high severity of needs.
The project will start off with a thorough field needs assessment, consulting with health facilities and personnel, communities, local stakeholders, and health directorates. The assessment will include collection of data on infrastructure, staff and available specializations, current health support provided, available medical equipment and assets, and type of medical supply needs. One of the main challenge to overcome is the recruitment of female health workers, due to that there is a relatively small numbers of whom are certified. Nevertheless, IR Turkey will aim to recruit around 25% female workers, depending on the availability of female workers at the time of signing the MOUs. Moreover, the assessment will also focus on protection risks faced by the target population and communities, and collection of SADDD will be ensured throughout.
The Project has the following main components:
• 32 health facilities providing services including children and maternal healthcare services, PHC services, cardiac surgery, dialysis as well as thalassemia services will be supported with drugs, equipment, and disposables.
• 13 Health facilities from amongst those supported with stipends and running costs.
• 2500 children under 12 months will receive 4 packs of baby milk.
• 2000 newly born children will receive a new born kit composed of hygiene items, drapes, and clothing for child and mother.
• Outreach workers will raise awareness on PLW and hygiene related issues at 5 health facilities
• 6 referral vehicles + workers will be supported at 6 different health facilities
• 170 healthcare staff will be trained in data management, warehouse management, orientation on the complaint mechanism, GBV case management, protection and inclusion.
• Maintenance of 10 healthcare facilities in the promotion of inclusion and the strengthening of established referral systems will be carried out to promote protection and inclusion.

4 - Needs

10 years of crisis have caused huge damage to the Syrian population eroding its resilience and causing immense suffering. The needs are now gigantic. 12.4 Million people are now in need of health assistance. The project will be implemented in order to address the most urgent needs of the communities of Northwestern Syria in particular.According to the HNO 2021, “Dramatic increases in food insecurity can worsen already-elevated levels of anemia in Women of Reproductive Age (WRA), posing a risk to adverse health outcomes for both mother and child, and, when coupled with diarrheal disease, can exacerbate the risk of poor malnutrition.” This particular intervention will address this need by providing critical multivitamins, medicine as well as baby milk for children.According to the HNO 2021, the integration of many sectors (WASH, nutrition etc…) inside the health sector increase the resilience of the health system. The proposed project will be implemented in this way by including construction activities increasing privacy and dignity of women, girls, PWDs and the elderly especially whilst visiting health facilities as well as nutrition and protection related activities such as baby milk distribution, dignity kits distribution to needy women and children especially in IDP settings.According to the HNO of 2021, “lack of medicines, supplies or health staff; and fear of exposure to COVID-19 have replaced distance, over-crowding and long wait times as the main self-reported barriers to health access” this project aims at providing free of charge drugs and disposables as well as stipends to health staff which will increase the targeted communities access to healthcare.

5 - Implementation of activities

IRW will implement the project through the following indicators: 1.1.7 # of cases referred for specialized treatment (between levels of care inside Syria, cross-line and cross-border): This will be achieved by supporting 6 referral vehicles at 6 different health facilities operating in IDP majority areas. The referral vehicles will be composed of a vehicle with its driver as well as the referral worker which is a health professional (nurse, pharmacists, etc…) with GBV case management training. They will coordinate with the administration of the health facilities in order to provide the referral service to the beneficiaries with no access to transportation. Also, coordination with local councils as well as camps management will be established in order to provide timely service to beneficiaries at their households. On a monthly basis, 600 referrals will take place for an estimated 7200 referrals supported during 2021 under the project.1.5.1 # of treatment courses delivered to health facilities by UN Actors (drug treatment for one disease, one medical procedure such as dressing, dialysis): A procurement list for the medicine and disposables most in need for PWDs, women (PLW especially), girls, boys, and the elderly will be prepared with the beneficiaries as well as qualified health staff (paediatricians, gynaecologists, etc…). The procured items will then be distributed to 20 health facilities in order to support the provision of healthcare at those health facilities. The main procedures supported will be: C-section and natural deliveries, orthopaedic surgeries, general surgical operations (cholecystectomy, appendicectomy, etc.). The treatment courses will be provided for an estimated 500,000 beneficiaries.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Violence against health facilities: Preparing emergency plans in case of high-risk environment (increase of violence affecting the area of the facility) namely providing guidance on the staffing, equipment relocation and facility schedule. Communications established with the safety and security department of IR and the administrators of the facilities. This communication is to update immediately in case of any risk or emergency affecting the area of the health facility.Gender Based Violence cases during travel to health facilities: Under this project, UIYV will train the health workers on management of GBV cases which will increase the number of personnel ready to handle such cases at target health facilities. Also, UIYV will support referral system with designated staff and transportation to assist the GBV victims. This will increase access to specialized medical services for the GBV victims. Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse: All IR employees shall sign UIYV code of conduct prior to starting their activities, this code of conduct includes sections on proper behaviour and measures taken in case of disorderly conduct. Also, a whistleblowing policy inside IR office is clarified to all personnel ensuring that any complaints pertaining sexual exploitation or abuse is taken seriously and confidentially. The contact details for reporting such incidents will made available for UIYV partners and target communities. The agreements and MOUs signed with the health facilities will include prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse. Complaints hotlines and contact details will be made available at every health facility that receives support through this project.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Conflict settings place women and girls at acute risk of poor health outcomes and sexual and gender-based violence. Health services are often the first, and, on many occasions, the only point of contact for GBV/SGBV survivors seeking assistance. Complaints and feedback communication hotlines and boxes will be put in place at the project locations and IR Turkey follow up response to the complaints and feedback collected.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The existence of many checkpoints inside Syria, which may limit the access to the target area:IR will coordinate with local partners and local committees who will help in facilitating the movement of the items to the target areas. All required documentation will accompany all shipments of goods along with experienced staff who are familiar with the situation and the checkpoints.No access to certain target areaIslamic Relief can cancel the inaccessible target areas from the distribution plan. Islamic Relief will reallocate the goods to more secure and accessible areas. On certain occasions, delaying the distribution for a limited time until accessibility is again possible.COVID-19 in project area becomes out of controlThis will greatly reduce the number of beneficiaries visiting the supported centers due to the lockdown measures that will be implemented. During this phase, the prioritization of PPE distributions to the health facilities will be key to prevent a shutdown of the health system due to infections.Closure of the Turkish borderIR will coordinate with Turkish authorities and the Turkish Red Crescent to enter the goods through relief cross points and to have exceptional border passing permissions.Escalation of hostilities and targeting of the medical facilities supported.IR will support development of a contingency plan or evacuation plan for each health facility in case the health facility becomes unsafe. The contingency plan will assist in the relocation of the health facilities to safer places or replace the health facilities with the accessible ones. Diversion of aidIR team is implementing a health program inside Syria by involving all the relevant stakeholders
