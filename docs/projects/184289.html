Project Summary

To strengthen emergency preparedness and response capacity at all levels in order to mitigate and respond to communicable disease outbreaks in an efficient, coordinated, and timely manner; New Ways will enhance the early warning and response for epidemics and other communicable diseases for men, women, Girls and Boys and expand in the targeted sentinel sites in the targeted districts of Banadir, L/Shabelle, Bay and Galgadud regions; by Pre-positioning of supplies and rapid establishment of Oral Rehydration sites in affected communities as well as Provision of vaccine to under 5 years children and tetanus toxoid to women of child bearing age (WCBA) in target areas.. In order to upgrade basic emergency Obstetric care, New Ways will strengthen maternity and Paediatric units of the targeted Districts to improve accessibility and availability of maternal and neonatal and child health care services as well establishing a comprehensive Emergency Obstetric care. To improve access to essential lifesaving health services for crisis-affected and host populations aimed at reducing avoidable morbidity and mortality; New ways will also establish Mobile integrated health services to enhance AWD/cholera, other communicable diseases case management and safe motherhood in target areas with particular attention to making sure equitable access to the services for disables (15 % ) and population from minority clans, through provision of basic life-saving health care services including detection and treatment of endemic and epidemic diseases, through provision of full course treatment for uncomplicated cases treatable at community level, treatment of people with common mental disorders within the general framework of available health services, ensuring that primary healthcare workers are able to apply key psycho-social and behavioral science skills, as well as ensuring an efficient referral system for those who require more specialized care, health seeking behavior promotion. New ways will organize training of qualified nurses on mental health care.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

At the start of the project, NWO will conduct community mobilization and awareness-raising meetings with all stakeholders, including local authorities, to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholders will be exposed. Women will be part of the participants of the mobilization workshop. NWO will hold special meeting with women, girls, boys, people with disabilities and minority groups to know their needs. NWO will involve the target community through its leaders and engendered committees in the first stage of planning of the project to ensure community participation. The organization will establish engendered committees (women participation will be strictly adhered) that will have together working relations with the project team and beneficiary communities NWO will Ensure the location and access routes to health facilities and mobile teams are safe. NWO will: - Locate mobile teams inside the targeted villages. NW will avoid placing facilities or mobile teams near military installations or threats such as attacks stray bullets, crossfire or violence. Weapon bearers will not be allowed to be present inside health facilities or mobile team installation sites. NW will Identify central locations for mobile teams - beneficiaries should not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access the health service. - health service will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries enough time to travel home. - facility latrines will be separate between female and male and will be lockable inside and outside to keep the privacy of women and girls. NWO will hold at the inception of the project, community mobilization, and sensitization meetings with all stakeholders including the local authorities to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholder including M&E roles will be discussed and agreed. NWO will develop elaborate beneficiary complain and feedback tool, project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be given 1-day workshop on how to use the complaint/feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. The feed complain mechanisms is accessible to everyone including women, children, people with disabilities and minorities.Children and people with disabilities are among the most vulnerable, marginalized groups within Somali society because of the attitudinal, environmental, and institutional barriers they face. This practice means they are unable to interact freely and receive appropriate service they deserve. NWO has given priority to PLWD in its all programs, in term of accessibility and inclusion. Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and Sexual Harassment are unacceptable breaches of fundamental human rights. 46 staff trained including senior management officers on PSEA and its procedures, NWO has zero tolerance on PSEA. Reporting mechanisms that are safe and trusted are in place. NWO adapts the strategy of AAP by working transparently and effectively sharing information with communities, giving communities influence over decision making in a way that accounts for their diversity, and allows the views of the most at-risk to be equally considered. Giving communities the opportunity to assess and if appropriate sanction our actions.
