Project Summary

The project will aim to provide the primary healthcare services for people in Hard to Reach HtR areas
in Al-Hasakeh governorate through a comprehensive Primary Health Care PHC center in Darbasiyah
sub-districts in Ras Al Ain district. This project will be implemented by. Beside increasing the area of
coverage, and hence, targeted population, the principle objective of this project is to enhance the
current public health status among people in the targeted area prioritizing the most vulnerable groups.
The main approach of this project was designed to meet the main health needs in Darbasiyah basing
on several assessments that have been conducted by Al Seeraj. This project activities,
outcomes, and targeted population was developed basing on the Essential Healthcare Service
Package EHSP working group guidelines and recommendations that took place in Turkey - Gaziantep
2016. In addition, this project has been developed and tailored to meet the health cluster objectives
and indicators taking into account the current humanitarian context in the targeted area. The activities
will last for 12 months from November 2016 until October 2017. The project will utilize the activities to
meet the needs of vulnerable groups and to ease the safe access and delivery of these services to
women, under 5 children and people with disabilities in addition to the referral service that will be
offered practically for the patients. Health reporting system that is planned to be applied in this project
is designed according to EHSP final template of reporting that represents the impact of the project on
the different age and gender categories and according to the vulnerable groups classification in the
targeted area. By this project, several basic healthcare services packages will be provided to the
beneficiaries especially with regards to maternal and reproductive health services. The capacity
building component is one of the most important outputs of this project through several courses of
training that are designed by different working groups of health and nutrition clusters in Turkey -
Gaziantep in a manner that took in consideration the current humanitarian context in Syria. In general,
Al Seeraj, and through their ability to access to the targeted sub-districts, will provide a
comprehensive approach of healthcare services.

Needs

AlSeeraj relied on several assessments that have been conducted in the planning phase to design the project outcomes, activities and targeted population on an evidence-based approach:• Key informants reported that host population and IDPs access to health facilities is limited, due non-availability and high cost for accessing health services particularly for IDPs. • Number of IDPs increased significantly and most of them considered in need of basic humanitarian aid. • Humanitarian assistance provided to the community is not adequate and not meeting with priority needs. • The current security situation is normal and people there can move freely and easily. • Majority of IDPs are staying with family and friends in host community, and collective centres (many schools have turned into collective centre).• IDPs and host communities are being affected by increased food prices, access to NFIs and health care for IDPs are high priorities.• Most vulnerable groups are: IDPs, females, U5 children, chronically ill patients, and elderly people.• The assessments showed that the age distribution in Darbasiyah host population (30,500) was as following: Children (0-5):15.3%=5033- Children:(6-14):22.8%=6954- Youth:(15-24):9%=2754- Adults (25-59):33.6%=10248- Elderly(60+):17.1%=5215, while the gender distribution was: females: 53.5%(16,318) and males:44.79%(14,182).• The assessments showed that the age distribution of IDPs in Darbasiyah (4500) was as following: Children (0-5):16.25%=689- Children:(6-14):24.6%=1107- Youth:(15-24):11.5%=518- Adults(25-59):29.6%=1332- Elderly(60+):21.6%=972, while the gender distribution was: females: 55% (2475) and males: 45% (2025). Implementation of activities

The overall objective of this project is to provide equitable access to healthcare services in order to protect health and provide lifesaving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance, including referral system, to IDPs and host community in Darbasiyah sub-district – Al-Hasakeh governorate, prioritizing the most vulnerable, namely infants and young children, chronically ill patients, females and elderly persons among. Moreover, this project aims to strengthen the coordination between health partners, improve diseases prevention and increase access to healthcare services. In addition, the project will strengthen the targeted community resilience and response capacity.
