Project Summary

The project aims to cover 620 HHs (3100 individuals) from the most vulnerable IDPs in Idleb camps in Harim and Salqin sub-districts by providing of the basic WASH services (secured water, hygiene promotion, and solid waste services)
The project duration will be for 6 months and the targeted communities will be Azmarin, Salqin, and Kafr Hum commities in Harim and Salqin Sub-districts.
The targeted camps are Al-Izza camp and Abnaa' Al-Ghab camp in Kaf Hum community, Al-Taa'khi camp in Salqin community, and Al-Shorfa camp in Azmarin community.
A full coordination has been done between SKT and PAH (which are working now in these camps) while PAH will go through out of the targeted camps at the end of February and SKT will start implementation in the targeted camps.
All the project activities had been designed based on needs to fill gaps and respond for the significant newly displacement and according to SPHERE criteria.
The main activity in our proposal project will be to ensure safe water for targeted beneficiaries through providing of 45 liters for each person per day through water truck while the project staff will provide the required percentage of chlorine to water provided for targeted people which will be 0.2 FRC and it will be checked by the project staff on a daily basis. As well as the project proposal will work to provide 64 water tanks to fill gaps with capacity of 1200 liters for each water tank and each water tank will be for four HHs.
The project will work to provide 13 cells of latrines with technical holes and provide maintenance for 74 cells of latrines existed in the targeted camps.
The latrines that proposed to provide had been designed to save dignity and privacy for women, and disabilities.
As well as the proposed to promote hygiene though provide trash removal on a daily basis, desludging service, and providing of 80 trash containers to fill gaps. In addition to proposed to enhance the cleanliness of latrines by clean the latrines by volunteers from the targeted camps on a periodic basis.
Furthermore, the project proposed to raising awareness through outreach campaigns for the targeted beneficiaries while the outreach workers will work to raise awareness and outreach of the hygiene importance specially after the wide spread of water born diseases through the outreach sessions and distributing of brochures.

Needs

Comprehensive WASH assessments were conducted in mid-2018 to inform the 2019 HNO, including an Individual household level survey at sub-district level (~24,600 samples from 265 sub-districts across Syria) and a household survey focused on IDP camps and informal settlements in northwest and northeast Syria (~1,950 households from 156 camps and 100 collective centers). Both assessments included water sampling and free chlorine (FRC) testing at the end-user side. Based on WASH assessments there are an estimated 15.6 million people who require WASH assistance (people who are at elevated risk of consumption of unsafe water, including people vulnerable due to displacement), 6.2 million of whom are considered in acute need (displaced people in camps, informal settlements and host communities). Furthermore, according to 1st standard allocation paper in 2018, people who have been forcibly displaced from rural Damascus and Homs had to leave with very little in terms of belongings. They have also had limited access to basic WASH services.The influx of additional people into an area where social infrastructure is already threadbare means that additional resources are required to improve access to water, sanitation and hygiene services. As well as, according to Final Population Data on 10.06.2018 that shared by OCHA, the population of Harim sub-district has reached to 51530 people divided into; 27946 host community population (54%) and 23584 IDPs (46%) living in camps and collective centers, and the population of Salqin sub-district has reached to 140055 people divided into; 51004 host community population (36%) and 89011 IDPs (64%). Based on need assessment conducted by SKT in June 2018, in Salqin, Kafr Hum, and Azmarin communities in Harim and Salqin sub-dsitricts, newly and old IDPs most in-need to basic WASH services that support their resilient according to saving their lifes (safe water, solid waste services, hygiene promotion, latrines).SKT had been designed this project based on triangulated the previous results to fill gaps and response according to required criteria.SKT conducted a needs assessment in the target project areas through key informant interviews (KIIs), focus group discussions (FGDs), and individual household interviews (HHIs). The main providers/consulters for information and data included key informants and the community members (i.e. vulnerable IDPs)The baseline mainly borrowed from adapted the household economy approach (HEA), which was used to assess WASH a status for affected households based on contributions to their hygiene kit and other variablesThe need assessment that conducted by SKT team confirms that there are 3 camps in Dana sub district that contain 300 households in need to reach the number of beneficiaries to 1475 people included ( 391 women with age above of 18 years old, 376 men with age above of 18 years old, 361 girls with age under of 18 years old, and 347 boys with age under of 18 years oldSKT takes into consideration vulnerability assessment of HHs in need assessment The Gender division of the targeted community is approximately: 48% male (37175) and 52% female (40273). While, the Age disaggregation of the target community is; - Men are: 25% of the community = 19362- Women: 27% of the community = 20911- Boys: 23% of the community= 17813- Girls: 25% of the community= 19362 The targeted camps had been covered by PAH organization for period, since 6 months this camps with no safe water and still suffer in access to basic WASH services. Implementation of activities

The distribution activity will be fulfilled by SKT (self-implementing) without any mediators.SKT will communicate in advance the selection criteria and the outreach plan with all stakeholders including local councils, community leaders and camp management to make sure that the selection process and activities are transparent and clear.SKT always works in close coordination with communities, LCs, and relevant stakeholders to ensure fair selection of the beneficiaries using selection criteria and therefore, maintaining a good relationship with a range of concerned stakeholders and strengthening transparency.SKT will work on the first month to hire project staff and conduct and sign contracts with supplires in addition to provide supplies, then it will implement the direct activities for 6 months according to work plan.
