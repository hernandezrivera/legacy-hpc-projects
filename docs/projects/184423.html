Project Summary

As per the HNO 2022 report, over 3.9 million people in Somalia need urgent action to maintain their right to health and wellbeing, and to assure their equitable and dignified access to health care. The health sector is overburdened, remaining fragmented, under-resourced and ill-equipped to provide life-saving and preventive services. The COVID-19 pandemic places the population at increased risk and stresses the capacity of the healthcare system. Displaced and marginalized groups inequitably face barriers to healthcare and higher exposure to disease.
In light of these, SOS CV propose to addresses barriers access to health and factors that increase risks of poor health and diseases including COVID-19 and provision of health services support to person living with disabilities. SOS will provide basic life-saving primary health care services to 242,500 highly vulnerable IDP, host-community and hard-to-reach populations in Banadir, Afgoye Corridor, Lafole Baidoa, Jowhar Diinsoor, Balcad with a strong focus towards women and children. SOS will facilitate access to target populations in identified catchment areas safely access comprehensive primary healthcare at no cost, including health and social behavior change messaging and community outreach through CHWs, designed to reduce the spread of communicable diseases. Disease outbreak surveillance, reporting, response and prevention to reduce morbidity and mortality from common diseases.
Integration of MHPSS in to the primary health care to improve the physical and mental wellbeing of communities. Clinical management of rape services to SGBV survivors to reduce rape consequences. Outpatient consultations will primarily focus on clinical diagnosis and treatment for communicable diseases, treatment of common ailments, Antenatal and Post-natal care visits (ANC/PNC), immunizations, skilled birth attendants specialized obstetric and newborn care and ambulance referral service.
The proposed project will support people with disabilities who will be provided with Health emergency response which is designed to improve the health and protection of the target groups through provision of Health services and mental health support.
