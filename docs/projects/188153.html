Project Summary

The proposed project will contribute to achieve the SO1 through improved access to humanitarian lifesaving emergency WASH services, and with improved hygienic behaviour and practices of vulnerable conflict-affected households, with stress on prevention measures of COVID 19.
In light of the high population density in displacement areas and camps, and the dependence of civilians on alternative sources such as water trucking, which are absolutely unsafe, new concerns arise from the spread of diseases due to the lack of water, especially among children. therefore, there is a strong need to provide personal hygiene items.
The project intends to provide personal hygiene items for about 60000 IDPs (10000 family) in camps of NWS that hosting IDPs.
IAC has good experience in distributing in-kind assistance due to its previous experience in distributing food baskets and non-food baskets since 2018 in several areas within Syria.
Project also will provide containers for solid waste, communal garbage bins and small garbage bins for each tent, also trucks will periodically collect garbage and transport it to designated places to be processed by sorting the contents so as not to damage the environment and surroundings.
Project also includes conducting hygiene promotion sessions to raising awareness about personal hygiene, COVID-19 prevention and IEC, taking into account prevention measures, social distancing and sterilization. The campaign will be done by trained team on HP principles and presentation skills. Throughout session team will distribute materials printouts and stickers in public spaces in targeted camps, in addition to distributing three rounds of hygiene kit for every family in targeted camps(Kits ‘contents will be according to cluster guidelines).

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2022-2023 the number of people in need has reached 13.2M in2022 and will reach to 14.5M in 2023 and they require WASH assistance, including more than 2 M living in IDPs last resort sites that require comprehensive and continued WASH assistance to survive.In addition to need to access to safe water, and adequate sanitation, the solid waste management and hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations and IDPs living in sites, in particular those in informal or overcrowded sites. IAC has conducted a rapid needs assessment in many camps in several locations as Daret Azza (severity 4), Atareb (severity 5), Jisr-Ash-Shugur (severity 4) according severity scale of HNO 2022 According to IAC team we found:Dret Aazza:11 camps 7885 IDPs (2314G, 2285B, 1716W, 1570M, 1377HHs, 416PWD)Atareb:49 camps 70030 IDPs (9976G, 17215B, 17425W, 15414M, 11952HHs, 1613PWD)Jisr-Ash-Shugur:Badama:51 camps 21399 IDPs (5326G, 5907B, 5298W, 4868M, 5039HHs, 787PWD)Darkosh:28 camps 12983 IDPs (3666G, 3677B, 3129W, 2511M, 2792HHs, 443PWD)Janudiyeh:19 camps 8676 IDPs (2205G, 2302B, 2232W, 1937M, 2005HHS, 220PWD)Although most of the displaced received some WASH assistance, but about 50% were not fully satisfied with it, mainly due to insufficient quantity or quality of it, and receiving it once or a few times in irregular periods, 60% do not have the ability to secure personal hygiene items on a permanent basis and sufficient. So IAC is planning to meet this need.

5 - Implementation of activities

- At the 1st month, IAC will conduct a meeting with the local actors and IDPs representatives (males and females) in each targeted location to explain the project and its goals, take any suggestions which help in the success of the project and ensure their support. - Distributions of hygiene kits or each family on three rounds according to WASH cluster guidelines.- Installation of solid waste containers (100 liter capacity), provision of communal garbage bins (100 liter capacity) and periodically collect garbage and carry it to a designated place. (one container for every 10 families).As well as will conduct Hygiene promotion campaign through trained team of male and female hygiene promoters, the main topics will cover the self-hygiene as per the COVID-19 related recommendations and water conservation and rational consumption.the team will use the recommended IEC materials by WASH cluster.In addition, they will install wall panels contains messages on protection and awareness of the COVID-19, directed and easy to understand for children.The project is considered as a planned and will target two locations through two phases:1- Aleppo governorate during 2022.2- Idleb governorate during 2023.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

To prevent any PSEA issue from occurring during the implementation period of the project activities, especially the activities in which the project staff are in direct contact with the beneficiaries, such as the distribution team. All field activities have been reviewed and assessed for risk factors for PSEA with the use of the PSEA Network Field and Organizational Mitigation and Prevention Checklists and the following prevention and mitigation measures will be taken: 1- IAC will make sure that All staff, and volunteers understand and sign the Code of Conduct. 2- Field staff with direct contact with beneficiaries will receive PSEA training 3- IAC will review internal reporting mechanisms and ensure all staff know about reporting mechanisms 4- female staff will always be present at the distribution site and involved in the distribution process 5-Benefeciaries will be given clear information on timing and content of distribution 6- Beneficiaries will be given information that aid is free7- PSEA messaging and awareness raising will be done though pasting PSEA posters and placing PSEA banners at the distribution site or other service place.
