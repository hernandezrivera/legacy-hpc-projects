Project Summary

HRO aims to continue providing CP services for IDPs & HC in our already existed community centres in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur & Darkosh) & in Aleppo (Afrin), besides planning to establish new CCs in Idleb (Harim, Salqin, & Kafr Jales) & through outreach visits to the widows’ camps (1 in Janudiyeh & 2 in Darkosh) to target the female widows & children with different CP activities, also HRO will target those children outside the camps who were separated from their mothers due to exceeding the age specified for them to stay in these camps, as they will be targeted with CP CM, including emergency case fund, CVA, & IPA to reduce the cases of child labor, CAAFAG, exploitation & other issues that they may be exposed to, considering that HRO has its own previous experience in providing CP services in emergency situations & is the focal point of emergency response in Jisr Ash Shugur.
HRO will train employees on (principles of CP, CP CM, FTR, Structured PSS, Inclusion of PWD) & provide services to children, their families, caregivers, & local community, including IDPs, HC & the most vulnerable groups such as children, UASC children & children with disabilities & those who are at risk of protection. Awareness-raising activities for community & caregivers on CP will be provided including CP services in crisis, parenting skills, early marriage, child labor, importance of education, & any other suggested topics by beneficiaries considering cultural context. Also, women & men will be engaged in parenting programs. PSS structured & semi-structured for children to enhance flexibility & adaptation skills for them, with several structured programs, including resilience Program for Children, which is provided to children & parents together. CM for children in line with SOPs, including CM of UASC based on FTR standard, & UNICEF for response, reunification, or alternative care support.
10 community committees for children & adults will be established (10 members each) will be trained on basic CP and identification & referral. Also, humanitarian actors will be trained on CP minimum standards.
M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the field level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
HRO will provide transportation & adapt the activities & centers to fit the needs of PwD & elderly through installing the needed tools such as ramps, toilet rails, extra lighting in the corridors & WCs for those with poor vision, etc, in addition to targeting them in all project activities including IPA & case fund.
19,860 BNFs including (11,916 IDPs & 7,944 HC) will be targeted including 10,923 F, 8,937 M (150 Young Children, 7,100 Children, 10,610 Adults, 2,000 Elderly) among them are 1,000 PwD, through 7 CCs including 32 CP staff (18 outreach, 14 case managers).
There will be 1 CP supervisor in each governorate to supervise case management teams ensuring the quality performance, staff wellbeing, & the regular monitoring & improvement of project’s progress.
Also, HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the child protection NGO partners, civic local bodies & stakeholders throughout the project period. In addition to the provision of MHPSS services through our ongoing projects to ensure the integration between our CP and MHPSS services considering the WHO standard COVID-19 and Cholera preventive measures throughout the implementation period. HRO is a member of the protection cluster and is committed to updating the protection 4WS monthly.

3 - Needs

According to draft HNO 2023, the scale of children in need of humanitarian assistance is on the rise, including those with disabilities, are at heightened risk of violence, abuse, and exploitation and need critical CP services. Grave violations against children remain a major concern including risk of being killed, injured, recruited, used in hostilities, tortured, detained, abducted, and sexually abused. By September 2022, 1,895 grave violations were verified by the MRM across all 14 governorates at generally increased levels (about 70% of grave violations are in NWS). Also, insecurity and economic hardship exacerbate CP concerns, fueling harmful coping mechanisms.According to north-west Syria child protection situation monitoring dashboard, 81% of KIs who reported children in need of focused non-specialised services, 54% of KIs who reported children in need of specialised services, 69% of KIs who reported parents (or other caregivers) in need of specialised services, 83% of KIs who reported children engaged in type of work that prevents them from going to school, 41% of KIs who reported children living in child headed households (CHH) engaged in child labour, 71% of KIs that reported children living in households with persons with specific needs (PWSN) engaged in child labour, 39% of KIs who reported children being separated from their usual caregivers, 52% of KIs reported violence against children ( in 2022).

4 - Implementation of activities

After ensuring the preparedness of the project logistics & recruiting procedures, staff will be trained on CP basics, CP CM, PFA, PSS, inclusion of PwDs. Afterwards, we will conduct awareness raising on CP-related topics, CAAFAG, & including children with disabilities in center & outreach activities, PSS & structured PSS activities for children, conducting CP monitoring assessments in the target communities, training widows’ camps managers & security & safety officers on CP prevention & referral system, besides training women committees in widows’ camps on CP prevention, PSS, referral, & suicide prevention. HRO will target separated children outside camps with CP CM, including emergency case fund, CVA, & IPA to reduce the cases of child labor, CAAFAG, exploitation & other issues that they may be exposed to.Key messages & IEC materials on Cholera will be disseminated at the end of each session. Also, in case of severe Cholera situations awareness-raising sessions will be conducted online. M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with KIIs at the field level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions, in addition to beneficiaries satisfaction surveys. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, photos, videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented.
