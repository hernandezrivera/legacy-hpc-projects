Project Summary

In addition to the original WASH proposal, GOAL have further added activities in support of COVID-19 preparedness and response, including provision of handwashing facilities in public spaces, mass media awareness raising, and provision of PPE.

1 - Activities

• Support the setting up of hand washing stations at various public places.• Support mass media campaigns on COVID-19 prevention using various channels of communication (mobile vehicle billboards, radio shows, radio jingles etc) taking into account literacy levels and access of different groups.• Providing access to critical handwashing facilities including soap and alcohol-based sanitizers, WASH-related hygiene kits.• Ensure field and front-line staff engaged in WASH humanitarian responses have adequate access to PPE and practice key risk reduction practices like social distancing.
