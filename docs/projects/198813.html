Project Summary

To improve the economic prospect of people affected by displacement, program focus will be on skills development (entrepreneurship, employability skills and soft skills), providing business inputs (start-up kits or start-up capital), access to financial services and regular business coaching/mentoring.
To ensure early recovery and restoration of livelihoods, IDPs, returned, and host households will be supported in reviving agro-pastoral systems, in order to resume their production activities and thus improve their food security and thereby their self-reliance. This will primarily involve providing agricultural inputs and tools, as well as strengthening the technical capacity of the beneficiaries according to their needs, preferences and experiences.
Beneficiaries of this action will be targeted in MMC/ JERE and Adamawa

1 - Needs

Agriculture remains to be the main source of livelihoods in the North East and North central Nigeria, particularly in the rural and less urbanized areas, however the ongoing hostilities and insecurity continue to restrain community access to land for cultivation. Up to 80% of people in north-east Nigeria are employed in agriculture, but the prevailing conflict impairs every stage of production, from obtaining inputs to working the fields or guarding the herds, to harvesting safely and marketing any surplus, IDP’s ability to access land or other livelihood opportunities in the BAY states is highly constrained by insecurity – particularly the fear of venturing beyond secure areas. . Many farmers cannot invest in agriculture and lack access to land, assets, capital and key inputs. Mass displacement of people has further reduced the labour pools and caused lands to be abandoned. As a result of all this, the agricultural value chain seizes up. Moreover, the NSAGs’ steady theft of cash, products and equipment, plus extortion of farmers and transporters of their produce, shows that they are targeting the agricultural sector for cash and food. The lack of employment and livelihoods opportunities is a major cause of frustration factor which compound social polarization and openness to radicalization .
