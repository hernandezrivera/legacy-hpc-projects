Project Summary

Building Resilience in Complex Crisis (BRICC) programme will build the resilience of 26,875 households in Yobe State, increasing their ability to cope with the shocks and stresses of conflict, climate change and complex crisis, and their ability to transform the underlying causal dynamics to reduce the risk of future conflict.
The Overall Objective is to support early recovery from, and build resilience to, conflict in affected and vulnerable communities in Yobe State.
The Program is being implemented by a Consortium of Mercy Corps(Lead Agency), Danish Refugee Council and COOPI

Plan fields

1 - Needs

BRICC Program will provide 26,875Household with basic needs via Unconditional Cash Transfer, strengthening community resilience including improved community conflict resoluation capacity, women, youth and adolescent have greater access to financial services, essential market system functions restored and improved resulting in greater access for vulnerable population to market for quality input and services and improved on-farm agricultural production, Increased access for youth to diverse income sources from off farm employment

2 - Activities

Formation of Community Resilience Groups (CRGs),• Transferring unconditional cash to vulnerable households• Providing technical assistance to Government on social protection• Supporting the rehabilitating of community assets and water access points• Training community leaders in conflict analysis, negotiation, and mediation• Facilitating multi-stakeholder dialogue sessions between community groups, LGA, and State officials• Training and supporting communities on savings and loans groups (SLGs) and financial education• Linking SLGs to financial institutions• Strengthen crop and livestock small-holder farmer associations/cooperatives• Facilitating the training-of agriculture of extension workers in Smallholder Farming Systems and dispute resolution• Facilitating the training of small-holder farmers in climate smart practices• Supporting the dissemination of agriculture related knowledge through radio and community mechanisms• Transfer conditional cash or subsidy for purchase of improved seed products by small-holder farmers• Facilitating the linkage of premium-priced traders to mature farmer associations• Facilitate business support services and linkages for seed suppliers to improve access and availability of improved seeds• Supporting the youth entrepreneurship and vocational training• Transfer small-business cash grants to select youth beneficiaries• Facilitating capacity building and technical support to vocational institutions
