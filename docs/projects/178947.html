Project Summary

The project approach aims to improve access to essential health services including primary health services, reproductive health and dialysis care services, and increase the capacity of medical staff through supporting six fixed primary health care centres (three level4, and three level3), four mobile clinics, one CEMOC centre, and one dialysis units.
Primary health care: The proposed intervention aims to implement the WHO’s EHSP which has been validated from Gaziantep Health Cluster considering the specificity of the region and the NES and HWG capacity and recommendations, to increase access to quality primary health-care services. primary health care services will be provided through six fixed primary health care centres and four mobile clinics. Supporting dialysis unit.This component aim to enahance the quality of hemodialysis care through supporting one dialysis unite at Al-Kesreah.This support includes providing the emergency and regular dialysis sessions, nephrology consultations, and regular blood test for all ESRD patients.
Supporting CEMOC centre: Al-Hol CEMONC centre aims to provide the comprehensive reproductive and maternal and newborn health services in Al-Hol town.Supporting a medical training centre in Ar-Raqqa city. This component contributes to the rehabilitation of medical personnel working in north-east Syria in order to achieve stability in the health sector and raise the level of competencies of health workers and develop their skills. This aims to provide the health sector with qualified and trained personnel, compensating for the acute shortage of medical personnel.
The proposed project encompasses four different outcomes and several activities:1-PRIMARY HEALTHCARE: increase the access to primary health care in the targeted communities of North East Syria(NES)•Activity 1.1PHC services provision through primary Health Care Centres and mobile clinics •Activity 1.2 Provision of awareness household visits through community health worker teams•Activity 1.3Provision of PSS groups and individual awareness raising sessions at fixed primary health care centers and at mobile clinics2-DIALYSIS: improve the access to dialysis in the region of Al-Kasrah :•2.1 Provision of dialysis session3-MOTHER AND CHILD: Improve the access to paediatrics and gynaecological services in targeted communities•Activity 3.1 provision of Comprehensive Emergency maternal Obstetric Neonatal Care (CEMONC) services in AL-Hol centre 4-TRAINING CENTRE: Improve the access to medical training for professionals in Raqqa •Activity 4.1 provision of training for medical staff in Ar-Raqqa training centre.UOSSM always seeking to engage the humanitarian response of health cluster with social cohesion, for that we provide full support for UOSSM HPCCS centers which include salary and training for staff, rehab the facilities, and most important value which is engage all potential stakeholder in all project-level which necessarily includes the most medical needs of area. We ensure all project-level under safe control whith coordination with local authority.

4 - Needs

According to the Humanitarian Needs Overview 2021 by OCHA; 1.9 million people in Ar-Raqqa, Deir-Ez-Zor and Al-Hasakah are in need of humanitarian assistance. 58% of hospitals and 53% of PHCs Centres fully functional, the COVID-19 pandemic has exacerbated weaknesses in the health system. And as showcased in (REACH Resource Centre, Humanitarian Situation Overview in Syria – NES, 2019) “the most commonly reported barriers in Ar-Raqqah, eastern Aleppo, and Al-Hassakeh governorates were the perceived lack of health facilities available in the area and the high costs of transportation to the facilities”. Therefore UOSSM France search to ensure continuity of support for 6PHCs and 4MMUs in NES.The HNO2021 report mentioned “Access and referral to specialist health services are similarly affected. This includes dialysis. In this regard, the needs assessment conducted by UOSSM France in May2019 showed that the dialysis services were not covered at all in the NES, specifically in Deir Ezzor. Therefore, the dialysis unit was supported at Al-Kasrah Hospital since Aug2020, and as of the beginning of August2021, the center will need funding to ensure the continuity of service provision. Under the same rationale, pediatrics and gynecological services are two others much needed specialized services that are insufficiently provided in NES. And based on the recommendations of the health sector working group, UOSSM France plans to continue its efforts in building the capacity of the health sector in NES. "The measures such as task-shifting and training must be complemented by longer-term efforts to address retention and shortages of health care workers such as capacity-building and production of clinical staff" (HNO2021)
