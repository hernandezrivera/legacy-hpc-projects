Project Summary

The deadly conflict between Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) which erupted on April 15, has further exacerbated the already precarious situation in Sudan, putting millions of lives and livelihoods in danger. According to the 2024 Humanitarian Response Plan, 24.8 million people – or every second person in Sudan – will need humanitarian assistance. While this overall figure remains equivalent to that estimated in 2023, the severity of needs has increased. A “catastrophic” severity of needs has now been observed in 17 localities (in comparison to 14 localities estimated in May 2023). – almost half of which are in the Darfur region.
As a result, some 20.3 million Sudanese (ie 42% of Sudan’s population) are now experiencing high levels of food insecurity of IPC Phase 3+ (crisis and above), driven by conflict and displacement across several states particularly in Khartoum, and the Darfur and the Kordofan regions. The conflict is also affecting the relatively stable eastern and northern states as communities are hosting high numbers of IDPs. The most severe impacts on household livelihoods and acute food insecurity continue to prevail in conflict hotspot urban areas experiencing heavy fighting, as well as in areas hosting high numbers of IDPs. In most rural areas, the impact of the conflict continues to be more indirect as insecurity along trade routes is reducing trade flows, input supplies and causing prices to rise above seasonal levels, thereby negatively affecting household access to staple foods and related commodities at a time when reliance on market purchases is typically high.
The situation is also compounded by climate shocks and hazards and economic down-turns/crises manifested due to subsidy withdrawals, devaluation, and inflation. The conflict has triggered a large-scale displacement of 7.6 million people across the country, according to OCHA.
The situation has compromised the food and livelihood security of the most vulnerable and threaten to worsen the conflict dynamics. Apart from growing criminality and insecurity in conflict-affected areas, unprecedented spike in acute food insecurity has been induced by previous dry spells and erratic rains, high price inflation for food, fuel and other commodities, floods, and persistent disease outbreaks.
According to the HRP 20024, available evidence suggests a correlation between conflict intensity and the levels of severity and prevalence of food insecurity. The highest levels of severity and prevalence of food insecurity are reported where the conflict is more intense, namely in the Greater Darfur and Greater Kordofan regions and Khartoum State. The large-scale displacement and extensive disruption to agricultural production are likely to significantly hamper the seasonal improvement in the food security outlook period between November 2023 and April 2024.
This project will provide critical life saving and life sustaining food security and livelihoods assistance to over 469,900.00 (of which 30% women, 30% men, 20% girls 20% 2,850 boys). These include 40% vulnerable host community members: 40% IDPs and 20% refugees and impacting other groups of concern including returnees. The overall objective of the project is to save lives by protecting the food and livelihood security of both displaced population and vulnerable host community member across 13 States in Sudan. It will focus on emergency agricultural support, livestock Support, livelihood cash. Skills development and income generation, support using Cash for Work modality.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The intervention will mainstream protection concerns in food security and livelihood activities in order to address, mitigate and reduce risk to particular vulnerable groups especially women. UNDP will prioritize safety, dignity of vulnerable groups and avoid causing harm. UNDP will also ensure a transparent selection criteria and participatory processes, promote equitable and impartial access for all groups, enhance accountability. UNDP will also specially target and promote the participation and empowerment of the most vulnerable groups especially the Youth and women and take steps to address structural obstacles to their meaningful participation.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.
4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The implementation and monitoring of the project will be driven by community-based structures known as the Community Management Committees (CMCs) to be established and maintained by UNDP. As part of the project feedback mechanisms, the project will organize review meetings with the CMCs as well as the general communities to receive feedback on lessons learned to improve implementation and for corrective actions. Beneficiary feedback and complaints can be provided through the CMCs, through the community review meetings or through a dedicated project complaint hotline. Information received through the above mechanisms will be analyzed, reviewed and incorporated into project implementation and future design.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

UNDP participates actively in the FSL cluster coordination activities and will adopt an inter-agency and collaborative approach to accountability to the affected people and feedback. mechanism. UNDP will implement joint monitoring mechanisms including joint field monitoring visits with other agencies. UNDP will also invite other agencies working in the same locations to the review meetings to ensure coordinated approach to accountability and beneficiary feedback. UNDP will also strive to share insights and trends emerging from the feedback received to inform other agencies including on sexual exploitation and abuse.
