Project Summary

Due to the overwhelming drought in Puntland region there is excessive displacements. . According to interagency assessments conducted by FSNAU, the Puntland Humanitarian and Disaster Management Agency and NGOs in mid-2021 highlighted that 55% of Puntland’s regions are facing drought conditions. Based on the most recent IPC projections in the region (October 2021 to January 2022), KAALO therefore propose to work in IPC 3 and 4 locations, namely the Bosasso and Galkayo IDP camps which host 19,962 and 9,979 households respectively (CCCM cluster verification September 2021). The proposed project will be implemented in Nugaal, Bari and Mudug regions in Puntland. The project targets 4000 beneficiaries. KAALO will provide lifesaving GBV services including medical treatment, psychological support, legal support in 3 GBV stop centers. The organization will scale up both prevention and response services to ensure that GBV survivors have access to multi-sectoral responses. KAALO will use survivor-centered and community-based approaches to cover the needs of victims of GBV with dignity and privacy. The project will also improve the capacity of staff to provide a higher quality of services, strengthen the resilience of Internal Displaced Peoples (IDP) and poorer members of host community through education and awareness. The organization will also distribute dignity Kit to estimated 3000 drought affected peoples in Nugal, Bari and Mudug regions. In addition, 20 staff will be trained on Clinical Management of Rape. At the end of the project, A total of 30,000 affected women, girls, boys and men will have access to integrated multi-sectoral GBV interventions.
KAALO will put more emphasis on the vulnerable persons including women, children, persons with disabilities, persons with minority clan affiliations and women groups and will be involved and be part of the project design and during implementation as well as through whole the process. KAALO will therefore provide greater consideration to these neglected groups of the society who are always subjected to exclusion.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

This project is conceptualized based on protection risk analysis and will mean-stream protection at every level. KAALO is mainstreaming protection in all its programming by ensuring that the most vulnerable groups such as women and girls and others such as PWDs, elderly, receive protection when they are providing information at any stage of the project cycle and that the whole process is confidential. Ensure accountability to affected populations • KAALO will be transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for residency, organizational procedures and processes that affect them. • KAALO has accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. • KAALO will Ensure camp/site managers and staff/volunteers working in the camps understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. • KAALO ensures all staff, implementing partners and volunteers involved in distributions and in direct contact with beneficiaries have received training in prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA). • KAALO will also provide clear information to distribution actors and affected population explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services/favour in exchange for receiving services or accessing facilities. This information will particularly communicated to women and girls. • In cooperation with protection actors, enhance the capacity of camp staff and managers to monitor, report and refer protection concerns (such as abuse and exploitation) and set-up camp referral systems to provide an appropriate response to persons with specific needs. Strengthen participation and empowerment • Ensure that both women and men are fully engaged in the management of camp facilities. • Involve and consult all categories and layers of the affected population in identifying and responding to camp issues. Different criteria may affect the power dynamics. • Consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, older persons and marginalized persons during assessments to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences for the design and management of camp settings. • Set-up camp committees to represent camp residents in camp governance structures. Ensure members are representative of all groups within the community (i.e. gender, age, ethnicity, socioeconomic group, disability). • Support and build their capacity in camp coordination and management and train members in protection mainstreaming. • Establish appropriate and sustainable mechanisms for meaningful dialogue between camp leaders/managers and camp committees to ensure beneficiaries are fully involved in decisions relating to their situation such as the development of camp/site policy, management and site closure. • Designate focal points within camp management structures and camp resident committees to incorporate the views of persons with specific needs in decision-making processes Eg. Female representative, youth representative, older persons representative, representative of persons with disability. Monitor high-risk zones regularly and at different times of day.
