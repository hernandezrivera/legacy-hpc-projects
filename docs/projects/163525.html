Project Summary

Vocational trainings is a veritable educational alternative for adolescents while menstrual hygiene management is an integral part of a woman’s health and has lasting impact on education, livelihoods and the security of women, guaranteeing their empowerment . Unfortunately it is not taken into account in humanitarian response to the Internally displaced persons. OWED has as one of its objectives to carryout menstrual hygiene management campaign. The sociopolitical crisis wrecking the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon has caused untold suffering and poverty to the entire population. So, the absence of awareness about menstrual hygiene management and difficult access to sanitary pads has forced the internally displaced women to use unhygienic cloth and often skip some of their daily activities while the displaced youths keep idling around the quarters . This project will be carried out in five Sub Divisions of the north West Region. The goal of the project t is to address menstrual hygiene challenges in the IDPs in these two regions and support them with menstrual cups and vocational training. These cups are beneficial because they are hygienic, non toxic, and non allergic since they are made from silicone and don’t react with other materials. They are eco-friendly since no trash is created and economical as a single cup can be used for 8 to 10 years. An independent consultant in the domain will be recruited for the evaluation of the project. In this light OWED also intends to enroll these adolescent displaced youths in the different vocational training workshops/centers within the urban municipalities.

Besoins / Needs

The project will train beneficiary women and girls on menstrual hygiene management and the usage of sustainable menstrual cups. Organize 20 sensitization campaigns on menstrual hygiene management and on the usage of the sustainable menstrual cups in the 5 sub divisions. Acquisition of the sustainable menstrual cups.and also provide sustainable menstrual cups to 2000 IDP women and girls in the 5 sub divisions in the North West Region of Cameroon.The project will provide training fees and a meal per day for the adolescent youths while also providing basic hand tools and start-up capital for the most vulnerable.The project therefore aims at training these internally displaced women and adolescent girls and boys on vocational skill development in other to create better lives for themselves and their families. OWED has as one of its objectives to mobilize and encourage girls to undertake activities and projects that will help them generate income for sustainable development through handicraft and vocational training.The impact of this project is contributing towards gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls affected by the crisis. This empowerment will give them confidence and ability to obtain a job and generate income for the entire family
