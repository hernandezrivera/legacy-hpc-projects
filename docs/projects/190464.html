Project Summary


The project targets four most affected woredas and prioritised by the regional Bureau, namely Elele, Kohle,Tuliguled & Danot in Shabele, Afder, Doolo & Fafan zone respectively and benefiting a total of 32,579 individuals (16,913 F, 15,666M). This proposed intervention will enable IRC to address the ongoing need based acute humanitarian assistance through water trucking, two new drilled boreholes pump and generator installation with on spot water delivery facilities, hygiene promotion and multi-purpose cash assistance response. Based on above outlined needs, the IRC propose lifesaving WASH and unconditional multi-purpose cash response to save lives of the affected people and support them to meet their basic needs. IRC will work and conduct discussion with the partners at woreda level partners to ensure the integration and coordination for effective delivery of service for clients.

1. - Projects activities

Deploy five water trucks with a capacity of 20 M3 size tank for three months in the prioritized kebeles of the two woredas (Elele and Kohle of shebell and Afdeer Zones) of drought affected community. The water drop of points are a temporary storage commonly used during water trucking (Birkads and small ponds with plastic sheet), to ensure safe drinking water IRC will procured HTH chlorine and chlorinated the water using water truck monitor and daily monitor the residual chlorine of the water fetching point, dumping site to make sure the residual chlorine is in the range of World Health Organization (WHO) recommendation 0.5 mg/litter. Totally 9000 M3 of water will be transported and distributed for 13,335 drought affected people (average of 7.5 litres per person per day for three months).Rehabilitate/maintain two motorized water schemes through installation of pump and generator. Riser pipe and Temporary generator house, roto tank supply and installationThe two boreholes are in Fafan and Jarar zone, Tuliguled (Jidile) and Danod (Shaxda Buhodle) respectively.
