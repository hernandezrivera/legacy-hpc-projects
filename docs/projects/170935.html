Project Summary

According to draft 2021 HNO that revealed that 3.15 million people are in need of shelter and NFIs assistance due to prolonged humanitarian issues caused by recurrent drought, conflict, and floods and due to inadequate shelter conditions, overcrowding, economic, hardship, and lack of security of tenure.
In consideration of severity factors, this project is to strengthen the protection and address life-saving needs of IDP families and poor host communities through Shelter and NFIs response in Kaxda and Daynile districts of Banadir region, Lower Afgoye and Marka districts of Lower Shabelle region, Baidoa district of Bay region, and Beledeweyne of Hiiraan region in South and Central Somalia including women-headed household families, extended families, families with disabled and elderly persons who are most in need of humanitarian assistance to mitigate risk and its consequences on crisis-affected populations at the target areas. The main objective is to improve the living conditions and safe access to services of IDP families including refugee returnees and poor host communities in the targeted locations. HINNA aims at providing 4800 ESKs and NFI kits to 4800 HHs (28,800 beneficiaries/Individuals) while 2,880 (10%) people of them will be poor host communities through in-kind distribution to improve the living conditions of the target beneficiaries who faced inadequate shelter/NFIs conditions. The reason we can't use cash transfer programming for this project is that we have had several meetings with the communities in some of the project target areas and most of them have told us that this type of program can cause insecurity, family violence that can lead to family breakdown and other things including missing kits or rising market prices.
Through this project disaggregation of gender will be promoted in full consideration with protection mainstreaming. The total beneficiaries of the project (28,800 people) of the drought, floods, and conflict-affected populations in Banadir region (Daynile and Kaxda districts), Lower Shabelle region (Afgoye and Marka districts), Bay region (Baidoa district) including (4320 women, 4320 men, 9504 boys, and 9504 girls) including 1152 elderly people (4%) will directly be receiving any benefits of the project based on their need of shelter and NFIs assistance through selection criteria set by the shelter cluster.

Project Activities

1. Construction of 4800 ESKs for 4800 HHs (28800 individuals) of the Crisis-Affected Populations including IDPs, refugee returnees, and vulnerable host communities based on selection criteria of the cluster including new arrivals affected by drought, flooding, and conflict as well as recently forced evictees, and protracted of those in need of privacy and protection from weather conditions in the project target regions - Banadir, Lower Shabelle, Bay, and Hiiraan. 2. Distribution of 4800 NFI kits available to 4800 IDP families, returnees, and vulnerable host communities (42,000 Individuals) who received ESKs. 3. The project will implement site planning and decongestion in full participation with target communities, landlords, local authorities to reduce or prevent risks that may cause transmission of the Virus of COVID-19, fire outbreaks, issues of GBV and flooding.4. Conduct PCM/PDM exercise to ensure the quality and/or issues related to services provided on quarterly basis.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and services (1.1)- Before registration, community mobilization will initially be conducted in participation with site leaders, sub-camp leaders, the established community committee from all levels of the community including persons with disabilities, women-headed households, marginalized groups, to know more about the project and selection criteria will be shared with them to understand and be part all the works of the project including registration, distribution and etc, local authorities will also be informed about the planned distribution with all required details. Secondly, the project will select safe distribution locations carefully close to beneficiaries and the beneficiaries will be notified in advance. During the distribution, HINNA will use enough staff members for a smooth distribution process and provide support to elderly people, pregnant women, people with disabilities, and sick as required. 2. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities. (2.1)-Marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys, and girls are the main part of project designing and implementation and they are included in the consultations and decision-making process. Apart from these, any important information about the whole project will be shared with them to avoid any action that may possibly make them excluded. The full participation of these people in the project is crucial to the achievement of objectives, the project will give more priority to the equality of gender, and "Do No Harm principles will be adopted among project stakeholders. 3. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms. (3.1)-To ensure beneficiaries' accessibility to CFM, a call center (Short Number) will be made available to them to use for any issues that arise during/after the implementation. 4. Risk of land disputes and tenure insecurity in the targeted project area. (4.1)-The project will support the most vulnerable IDP communities. The risk of dispute on landowner and land tenure may rise but the two-side agreement with a speciﬁed period of time at least 3 years between landowners and established IDP community committee will be developed in the presence of district commissioners/local authorities to ensure the land tenure.
