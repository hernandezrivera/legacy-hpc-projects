Project Summary

After years of crisis, the continuing lack of access to services and economic and livelihood opportunities have had an increasingly strong and ramified impact on the Syrian population. The risk of negative coping mechanisms, such as reducing food consumption, deferral, if not cancellation, in seeking medical care, reduced hygiene practices, children engaged in harmful work and / or early / forced marriages are constantly increasing and consequently, the need for interventions increases. The social centre of Zahret Al Madan (ZAM), partner of the initiative, was created with the specific task of assisting families in which there are children and adolescents with physical and/or cognitive disabilities. The central activity of the association is the creation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for minors, to which have been added those on which the impact of the conflict was particularly dramatic, both from a psychological and physical point of view (e.g.: minors with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder - PTSD). The strong reduction for families of the possibility of accessing social and health assistance services, both due to the lack of structures in the suburbs and the lack of economic resources, makes it necessary to strengthen the services offered - free of charge - by the ZAM Centre. The project here presented aims to increase the food security of some of the most vulnerable families of the targeted communities. Armadilla is proposing in the food security & agriculture sector an intervention based on:
i) distribution of food parcels to the most vulnerable families of the Area;
ii) distribution of agricultural inputs (and related training) with the aim to develop house vegetable gardens and partially reduce dependence from humanitarian aid.
The project is integrated with activities proposed under the protection and health sectors umbrellas.
Armadilla and its partners, Zahret Al Madan, and in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders (communities and MoSA ), has identified three mains area of problems/constraints that the Syrian society is facing: economic, social and of life conditions. Therefore, notwithstanding the importance of providing vital relief and immediate support to the most vulnerable people, Armadilla believes that its actions should be always oriented in build the basis for a better and protected future for the people of Syria, creating a medium-term strategy that frame the activities to be implemented in a path of linking the relief with the development.
At this aim, the activities to be implemented in the years 2021-2023, will be focused on three main topics interconnected and governed by the main protection driver:
a) saving lives, through activities of emergency in support of the most vulnerable (emergency component).
b) improving the access to basic social services, working on enhancing both the quality and the quantity of the services provided by local associations and institutions (resilience component).
c) improving the capacity of the local communities and individuals in reinforcing their livelihoods, in order to increase their capacity to cope with the prolonged crisis (early livelihood component).
Beneficiaries of the intervention here presented are families that see the presence of one or more children with disabilities in the household. Moreover, a substantial percentage of families (78%) are managed by women. The project integrates activities of “saving lives” through the distribution of food and delivering an essential service (therapeutic pathways for children with disabilities) for the vulnerable families, with others which stimulate the resilience of the families, such the development and implementation of vocational training for women, social inclusion and inclusive education of children with disabilities, etc. In this way, children and the hh they belong to, may be assisted at 360° and participating in several activities driving them to abandon the mere assistance.

4 - Needs

In Syria, an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68.6 per cent of the total population. This highlights a deterioration of the context, caused by several factors: currency depreciation, hikes in food prices, lack of livelihoods, COVID-19 pandemic. Even areas that seem to have a low prevalence of food insecurity, such as Rural Damascus and Damascus, records levels of 44.5 per cent and 46.9 per cent, respectively. In the area of intervention, access to food, is limited by the scarce economic resources of the families, both due to the household loss of income and the growing increase in food prices. The reduced availability of food has led to a worsening of nutritional conditions, which has caused worrying effects especially among the most vulnerable categories: children, pregnant women, elderly, people with disabilities. 92% of the families targeted belong to the southern suburbs of Damascus, and the crisis in progress has added to a pre-existing condition of high vulnerability. The community sees a significant presence of children with disabilities, on whom the impact of the conflict has been particularly dramatic, having significantly reduced the possibility of access to social and health care services. Recent surveys conducted in October 2021 on a sample of 450 families show a prevalence of FCS borderline result (345 families, equal to the 58% of the total sample).

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented mainly in Midan, where the Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) Centre lays, even if beneficiaries of both the programme of house vegetable gardens, and fod parcels distribution will be targeted among various communities of Damascus and Rural Damascus. Also, a quota of potential beneficiaries reaching the centre referred by the Ministry of Social Affairs, as Armadilla and ZAM work under the coordination of the Ministry (regarding Armadilla: a specific MoU with the Ministry has been signed since 2006 and renewed till the year 2022).Project activities will be implemented by Armadilla and ZAM staff. ZAM volunteers will be coordinated by Armadilla’s agronomist and her agricultural extensionists, which will train the beneficiaries of the agricultural inputs.List of activities to be implemented:1) selection of beneficiaries of food parcels, according to their social-economic vulnerability score;2) tender for the purchase of goods;3) distribution of food parcels; 4) Evaluation of the impact5) Selection of beneficiaries of home gardening project, according to their social-economic vulnerability score and possibility to create a home garden;6) Training on house vegetable gardens to groups of women;7) Distribution of agricultural inputs;8) Continuous monitoring and assistance to beneficiaries of the programme of house vegetable gardens (supervision and follow-up of the activities).

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Access to the Area of the activities The Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) Centre, located in Midan, where the activities will be implemented is situated in an area so far considered safe as it never witnessed heavy clashes. However, the Organisation will be in continuous communication with the Syria Logistic Cluster and will organize activities and movements only after consultation with it. Availability of goods: Goods will be purchased on the local market, looking for the best vendor in terms of economy and capacity of guarantee the provision of service. A back-up list of potential suppliers guarantees the possibility of finding the goods in case of problems. Lack of permission to enter in Syria for expatriate staff: Local staff of the Organisation will be able to perform the proposed activities, thanks also to the long collaboration with the local partner which has a proven capacity in implementing similar projects.
