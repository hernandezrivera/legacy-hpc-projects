Project Summary

The ongoing armed conflict in eastern Ukraine since 2013 has greatly impacted access to safe and quality education for boys and girls, particularly those living in non-government-controlled areas (NGCA) and in the vicinity of the contact line 0-20km (CL) with limited access to humanitarian assistance.
This project aims to improve access to safe and quality education for children and youth from NGCA/CL (assessed to be around 63,500 affected children) by strengthening the institutional capacity building of 15 schools and the “Donbas-Ukraine” Education Centers in GCAs of Donetsk and Luhansk regions through distance learning activities and by conducting an information campaign through a hotline in 2020.
The distance learning activities will include 4 trainings for 120 educators (100 teachers, 10 local education departments representatives, 10 educators from in-service teachers’ training institutes). The trainings will focus on promoting digital learning technics; using digital tools, gadgets, mobile applications, interactive free platforms; adapting school curricula for distance learning; creating video lessons and interactive e-presentations. Creating and publishing 3 methodological manuals (for teachers, managers, parents) together with the Luhansk Institute of Postgraduate Pedagogical Education. Establish Distance Learning Center. Create a network of mentors and “ambassadors”. Involve educators & students who have become Green STREAM mentors & ambassadors (285 educators & students have been trained in International Green School in 2017-2019 by Open Policy Foundation (OPF) projects, which were supported by the Embassy of France Embassy of Lithuania, USAID). The International Green School is one of the OPF’s projects (http://openpolicy.org.ua/en/igs/ ) in Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast. It works as a center of non-formal education for students and educators. Last 3 years, about 300 educators, incl. from GCA, trained here. In 2018, an agreement was concluded with the Luhansk Regional Institute of Postgraduate Pedagogical Education. And teachers and administrators are training common with Institute (integrated STEM/STrEAM education methods, distance learning, digital tools). Employees of the “Donbas-Ukraine” Education Centers are also trained here on information campaigning, strategic planning, and fundraising. Employees of the Ministry, regional and district education departments also take part in training at the school. In 2019, USAID representatives carried out 2 program visits to the International Green School. The monitoring results are positive.
Produce 2 video instructions and 1 motivational video. Conduct press-lunch with educators and MoES. Advocate of distance education. Inform 3,000 residents of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts about opportunities of distance learning and obtaining recognized education certificates. Improve standards of distance education for 15 schools.
The information campaign activities will include training for 50 educators and 40 students from “Donbas-Ukraine” Education Centers focusing on effective information campaigns, involving students/”ambassadors” from NGCA/GCA/CL. They will serve as agents of influence (network of ambassadors) to conduct an active information campaign using their social media networks to target more than 600 000 people, including the population of NGCA. During the trainings, proposals will be made for national legislation regarding the work of the “Donbas-Ukraine” Education Centers and the simplified admission system. OPF staff will advocate for them during working groups at MoES and meetings with representatives of relevant MoES directorates and members of parliament. Information national hotline will be established. During admission campaigns, it will work together with MoES. The project will promote cohesion with the objectives defined by the Protection Cluster in terms of endorsement of the right to education, access to documents, and freedom of movement.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

The number of children and youth who received school certificates in NGCA since the beginning of the conflict is 120,000. A generation of school graduates in NGCA are at risk of diminishing opportunities to continue higher education outside the NGCA and enter the job market. https://www.humanitarianresponse.info/sites/www.humanitarianresponse.info/files/documents/files/1_ukraine_education_cluster_strategy_final_23.09.2019_eng.pdf, p.11). The equal access for children to education, the simplified entry system to vocational schools and higher education institutions has been implemented for enrollees from NGCA. This system has benefitted 5,323 residents in NGCA/CL in 2016-2019. The number of participants through the Donbas-Ukraine educational centers has almost doubled. https://mon.gov.ua/ua/news/1600-vstupnikiv-z-donbasu-ta-265-z-krimu-cogorich-vstupili-do-ukrayinskih-vishiv-za-sproshenoyu-proceduroyu-ce-najbilshij-pokaznik-za-ostanni-4-roki. Also, distance education is an important way to obtain the recognition certificates. So the number of schoolchildren NGCA/CL, who are studying through distance/externat in schools GCA, is increasing. It also provides children with safer learning conditions. 19,000 of girls and boys need distance learning programs (https://www.humanitarianresponse.info/sites/www.humanitarianresponse.info/files/documents/files/1_ukraine_education_cluster_strategy_final_23.09.2019_eng.pdf, р. 14). The implementation of distance education in the educational process requires increasing teachers’ potential (https://www.humanitarianresponse.info/sites/www.humanitarianresponse.info/files/documents/files/1_ukraine_education_cluster_strategy_final_23.09.2019_eng.pdf, р. 10) and acquiring advanced skills to work online, use gadgets, mobile applications, etc. There is a lack of methodological approaches to modern digital learning space in schools. Also, there is a need for legal framework for final academic assessments, criteria for assessing learning performance, teachers’ payments, etc. All this requires advocacy for improving of legal framework for distance education. OPF started this advocacy in 2015 (http://openpolicy.org.ua/en/). However, without information support and a national information hotline, children and youth from NGCA/CL will not be able to access these opportunities. Therefore, OPF together with MoES established an education hotline from 2016 to 2019. In 4 years, more than 6,000 calls were received from NGCA/CL. The number of calls has increased yearly. The highest number of calls from was received from NGCA/CL in 2019 during the university admission period. During a period of 1,5 months, more than 1.5 thousand calls were made to the OPF Hotline working in partnership with MoES with UNICEF support. This represents a three-fold increase from last year.Main questions of residents from NGCA/CL using the hotline in 2017-2019 focused on the simplified admission procedure 55% and distance learning 27%.The information materials (audio, anima videos, roadmaps, leafets etc.) are produced and distributed, and made available on ofcial websites of MoES and the Regional State Administration, in https://dostupnaosvita.com.ua/, in “Ukrzaliznytsia”, checkpoints, as well as on radio and television channels, in city transport in the GCA, CL. The dissemination of information on educational opportunities should be provided at the national, regional, and local levels during the entire year. The dissemination of information products with the simultaneous education consulting on the “hotline” will enable children and young people on equal access and wider recognition of learning attainment and continue their studies at vocational schools and universities in the Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts in GCA. However, the impoverishment of the population leads to the lack of funds to finance the transfer and other expenses. According to the calls to the hotline of the OPF, there is a lack of information in the NGCA.

2 - Activities & outcomes

1. Train 120 educators and create a network of mentors&”ambassadors” on distance learning (100 teachers from 15 schools in GCA Luhansk&Donetsk oblasts, 10 representatives of education departments and 10 representatives of institutions of postgraduate education). Conduct 4 trainings (distance education using smartphones, mobile applications, interactive free on-line platforms, creating video instructions and interactive lessons etc). Result: 120 educators from GCA/CL trained on distance learning and network created. Conduct 2 training courses for educators from educational institutions (incl. professional vocational schools and colleges) in Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts, and 2 training courses on an information campaign development and holding for students from “Donbas-Ukraine” Education Centers. 4 training courses will be held in the International Green School in February-March 2020. During the admission campaign (April-August 2020), educators and students will implement communication strategies developed during the first training courses, and OPH’s staff and trainers will act as their mentors. 2. Produce 3 methodological manuals (for teachers “Cool Digital Teaching”; for educational managers “Organization of Distance Learning”; for parents “Cool Digital Learning”) and “Collection of Lessons for Distance Learning of Primary School Students under the Programs of the New Ukrainian School”. Posting on dostupnaosvita.com.ua and site Luhansk Institute of Postgraduate Education - loippo.edu.ua. Result: production and publication of 3 methodical manuals, which will raise the level of knowledge about technology and methods of distance learning of 3,000 educators and parents. 3. Establish a Distance Education Center to train mentors&ambassadors (consulting via webinars, Fb “Dostupna Osvita” - https://www.facebook.com/dostupnaosvita/, WhatsApp groups, etc). Result: Distance Education Center created and 1,000 educators trained. 4. Promotion and Advocacy. Create 1 video for parents&children, 2 step-by-step video instructions “How to Prepare an Interactive Presentation” and “Cool Digital Teaching”. Conduct a press lunch. Result: Information covered for 20,000 residents from NGCA/CL, GCA (inc. 4,500 children&parents from NGCA/CL). About 3,000 residents of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts are informed about distance learning opportunities and received recognized education certificates. 15 schools improved standards for distance education&curricula.Regional and national information campaigns will be conducted by development, production and distribution of 2 types of booklets, 1 audio and 1 video clip, 2 video tutorials and 1 banner on issues of admission to educational institutions. The information campaign will be accompanied by the education hotline activities. Results: Updated work the national hotline (free calls, including messengers). For the period of the entrance campaign, organized the hot line together with MoES. The “Dostupna Osvita” site, together with the MoES updated. The project also foresees an advocacy campaign on improving the work of the “Donbas-Ukraine” Education Centers and the simplified admission system. According to the annual Admission Requirements, there is an additional session for NGCA entrants, as well as a simplified procedure of CL crossing. All lists of entrants are encrypted. In 2019, local authorities organized free shuttle buses from EECPs to educational institutions in GCA. The OPF plans to advocate: amendments to the law on secondary education in supporting distance education; participation in the development of conditions to enter displaced educational institutions in 2020; that the monitoring in 2020 will not result in their destruction; the model of hotline financing at the expense of state and local budgets; appropriate amendments to laws; development of a model regarding financing by regional budgets; collection of normative and legislative acts that regulate these issues.
