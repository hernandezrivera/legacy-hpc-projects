Project Summary

The project is responding to the cluster HRP 2021 SO 2; The basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through the provision of quality basic services and improved access to resources, in accordance with the rights of protected persons under IHL.
TARGETING THE VULNERABLE HHS IN vulnerable communities, camps and Bedouin communities prone to flooding and weather extremes who have concerns from shelter inadequacy to withstand severe weather conditions, and put a high focus on those with high risk of covide-19 and lack of adequate privacy living spaces to females, elderly and PWDs.
The project aims to "Protection of the Most Vulnerable households exposed to harsh weather and protection concerns through minimum standards rehabilitation with provision of NFIs and winterization/ summarization needs " the project is prioritizing the vulnerable households
This would be identifies through the findings of the recent shelter vulnerability assessment conducted by MoPWH revealed 21,500 substandard units, which need rehabilitation of essential components to provide minimum requirements of waterproofing, hygienic living space, privacy, and protection from weather extremes. Overcrowding compounded with unhygienic living conditions is common in the substandard shelters in Gaza. however FAFD has conducted field visits and interviews to 500 HHs of the targeted communities assessing their Needs of upgrading, NFIs provision and W/S repairs.
need assessment of each HH using the shelter& NFIs minimum standards scoring system that clarify the critical needs of each HHs through identifying the socio-economic level . The project team will make consultations with HHs, and discuss upgrading works and rehabilitation Applying self-help approach to maintain more identified needs of each HHS by her/ himself. With 120 shelters will be supported with estimated amount of 2500 $ based on the actual BoQs. Of the needed works of rehabilitation and NFIs, however, the critical cost for each will be identified clearly during the end line assessment. Additionally other 150 HHs will be benefited with estimated amount of 210 $to supporting winterization/summarization repairs and vouchers of NFIs assistance such Nylon kits, fans, nylon, etc. And household NFIs assistance, NFIs Including bedding sets, kitchen NFIs, and lighting units, and hygiene kits including COVAID-19 Hygienic and dignity kits and materials. Average per beneficiary, the actual amount will vary for each beneficiary based on precise assessed needs at household level. The upgrading works and W/S repairs will tend to be durable sustainable solutions /works with at least 5 years of stability and protection shelters. It will consider the improvement of living conditions including spaces and rooms, and providing adequate privacy spaces of females, children, elderly at risk of protection, and adaptation to PWDs. capacity building of FAFD and it's partner staff " Rural women Association" who will receive a training about PSEA and ISAC principles, humanitarian aid main principles and service delivery mechanisms. As well as the good practice In regard to covid19 prevention and protection. the training will target 20 participants of both males and females. producing a visual materials and brochures to enhance PSEA among community and /beneficiaries to reach about 20000.

1 - Needs

According to MoPWH recently large scale assessment of substandard shelter, revealed that out of 44,832 assessed housing units, 21,219 units are very substandard and in need for rehabilitation. Provision of Shelter rehabilitation based on self-help approach of sustainable shelter assistance is essential, including waterproofing materials for the roofs, walls and windows. as well as maintaining f living space, adequate privacy .and hygienic.. Provision of sustainable shelter assistance is essential, including waterproofing materials for the roofs, walls and windows. as well as maintaining f living space, adequate privacy .and hygienic.The action will be supported by the mainstreaming on an Integrated Protection Approach (IPA) to ensure that the rights of all family’s members are respected with full dignity and safety, with a specific focus on women, children and people with disabilities. Threats to safety, security and well-being of children at shelter level will be diminished through compliance with The Minimum Standards for Child Protection in Humanitarian Action (CPMS). Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) and communication with community (CWC) will be also ensured through regular contact and visits to beneficiaries, needs and vulnerability assessments, engagement of households in the intervention design, adaption of the intervention according to the households’ needs and comprehensive Complaint Response. for the leagal issues related to rented house units and others, FAFD will recruit a legal consultant as well as in coordination with NRC, it will provide the best solutions to target such beneficiaries and to protect the rights of both HHs and client. In response to this appeal ,FAFD has conducted a recent baseline -need assessment on March 2020, where 503 Bedouin and vulnerable HHs identifying their needs of shelter rehabilitation and upgrading including kitchen and bathroom, and NFIs of Kitchen NFIs, bedding sets, safe lighting units winterization& summarization NFIs, and hygiene kits including dignity kit. The assessment targeted HHs from Gaza Governorate from 1) the appeals received on our social media websites in the last 6 months, 2) others registered on the waiting list since 2018 assessment conducted, and 3) other HHs were assessed by our local Partner Rural Women Association at Al Mugraqa. the assessment targeted 6 localities; Al Shujayia, AL Tufah, Al Shate’ camp, Al Zaytoon, Juhor El Deik, and AL Mugraqa through field visits conducted by social workers. 358 (71%) of the HHS are male where 145 (29%) are female, 99 of them are PWDs, 57 (58%) are male HHs and 42 (42%) are female HHs. The Gender Age Marker of these HHs family members are assessed into 3 categorists from 1 to over 10 persons. 183 (36%) HHs family members are between 1 to 6, 229( 46%) are from 7 to 9, and 91 (18%) HH are over 10 persons. Total of 342 of the targeted HHs are Bedouin whereas other 40 are refugees and 121 are non- refugees(citizens). FAFD worked in partner with it’s local partners at al Mugraqa and the Community Protection Committee( CPC) members in four localities as they facilitate to reach the targeted HHs of each area with FAFD field staff. Protection mainstreaming is highly considered as the HHs were asked about if the house is with lack of a adequate privacy spaces of females and children or adapted for PWDs. The need assessment resulted in revealed that 333 of HHs are in need to shelter rehabilitation and upgrading, 306 are in need to kitchen and bathroom repairs, where 243 HHs are in need to maintaining or upgrading spaces regarding Gender protection.; females, PWDs and children. The project will provide a sustainable rehabilitation and shelter upgrading to substandard house units with a comprehensive approach to - winterization consideration., improving an adequate privacy spaces, and adaptation to PWDs
