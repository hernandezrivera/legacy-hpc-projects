Project Summary

South Sudan years’ long crises have left 9.4 million people in critical needs of humanitarian assistances in 2023. Despite the formation of the new government by the South Sudanese warring parties, yet expects to have the situation deteriorating due fresh conflict, natural calamity, epidemics and climate instability. Food insecurity and lack of basics services. Humanitarian Need Overview (HNO) revealed there will be insufficient access to clean drinking water, WASH NFIS, WASH NUTS, hygiene and dignified sanitation facilities in 2023, and 6.0 Million people in South Sudan will be adversely affected.
Since year 2017, Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) has been intervening in emergency WASH through deployment of frontlines volunteers in hard to reach areas, delivering lifesaving WASH NFIS to both conflict and disaster affected persons in Koch Panyijiar, Baliet,Panyikang,Panyikang, Malakal, Akobo, Canal Pigi and Ayod counties Since 2017. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) has been playing crucial role completing most IRNAs, KAP, Village surveys and multi-sectoral need assessments with static partners and in coordination with OCHA, and aftermath delivering of emergency assistances to the crises affected persons in many counties. In 2023, Hope Restoration South Sudan will aim at improving timely, equitable integrated access to WASH services to the epidemics, conflict and disaster affected persons in Koch Panyijiar, Baliet,Panyikang,Panyikang, Malakal, Akobo, Canal Pigi and Ayod counties.

1 - Needs

Displacement of populations and the aftermath looting of their properties among many others abuses, complicated humanitarian situation in Baliet, Koch, Panyijiar, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo, Rubkona and Ayod. Women and girls have been attacked by gunmen on their way to the water points unmasking, them to protection and GBV risks especially in Canal Pigi and Fangak counties. They are harassed, raped, abused and exploited. While perpetrators normally go at will. The judiciary system in most parts of locations targeted is paralyzed due to many factors including availability of small arms in the hands of bandits or unauthorized agents, who seem to undermine legal redress. Communities in Baliet, Koch, Panyijiar, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo, Rubkona and Ayod have experienced displacement associated to natural disasters or flooding and fresh conflicts. The conflict and floods which resumed in June and intensified in the midst of September, then triggered the displacement of thousands of households and destroyed WASH infrastructures. This left households vulnerable. Women, men children became malnourished, as evidenced in the (FSNMS report 2022). Heavy rainfalls coupled with the floodwater from the River Nile and its tributaries, added pressure that submerged many villages, whereas both water and sanitation infrastructures affected, including households latrines, and thus caused pollution to the human environment, which resulted to diarrheas and water-borne diseases. Water storage items; jerry cans, buckets and without taps lost during the displacement. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) and other humanitarian agencies on the ground and in collaboration with UNOCHA, conducted IRNA in Canal Pigi, Panyijar, and Fangak counties, between October and November 2022, and underscored that 80% percentages of the population have been displaced by fresh conflict and flooding and additional to other driving factors, deteriorating WASH conditions of the communities. IRNAs and KAP conducted in 2022) proved beyond reasonable doubt that, 85% of the total population in Pigi and Fangak and Panyijiar practiced Open Defecations (OD). Attacks by gunmen on the roads to the water points, considerably reported to have threatened women and girls. In light of this contextual analysis, humanitarian situation in counties mentioned, community living conditions are confronted by multiple factors: malnutrition in women and children U5 prolonged by poor feeding and lack of WASH infrastructures. There is a Shortage of Households water storage and water treatment tablets such as buckets with taps and without taps, jerry cans, Aqua tabs, PUR sachets, and water filter clothes. Poor hygienic conditions both in camp-like and camp settings. There are few sanitations or latrines and water infrastructures in most locations. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will address the needs of conflict and disaster-affected persons in areas targeted, through multi-sectoral approaches; WASH health, NUTS, WASH NFIS such as households water storage; buckets, jerry cans, and households water treatment tablets are necessary to improve water storage and treatment systems to the women and children admitted for SAM/MAM and other affected households in project locations. Distribution of soaps to increase hygiene practices and reduces diseases. Construction and rehabilitation of latrines or sanitation infrastructures, installation of hand washing stations in nutrition and health facilities in all locations. Champion hygiene promotion by training community hygiene promoters in all locations. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish or strengthen the complaint complaint feedbacks desk in efforts to track beneficiaries’ feedback on concerns related to satisfaction protection and establish feedbacks desk in efforts to track beneficiaries’ feedback on modalities employed. AAP committee will be also established.

2 - Project activities

Activity 1.1.1 : Community consultative meetings: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) and other static partners, will conduct community consultative meetings with community members drawn from the population groups to participate in the consultative meetings in a pursuance to establish vulnerability and identify the project beneficiaries. Population groups represented in these consultations, will comprise of : Local authorities, women leaders, representatives of religious groups, representatives of persons living with disability, representatives of persons living with chronic illness or HIV&AIDS, persons without effective community links, members of affected households, chiefs, teachers, These meetings, will inform the project team to understand the community dynamics. These community leaders, will serve as focal and entry point to the project locations. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRS) will use these consultative meetings to mobilize and sensitize community about the project scope. Meetings are also mandatory to justify the core pipeline request, which is very crucial to WASH cluster and core pipeline agencies for the purpose of Core pipeline approval. Activity 1.1.2: Formation and training of AAP committee: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish, train Accountability to the affected population AAP committee in all locations. This committee members work as focal points to the vulnerability locations. It helps the project team to identify need and address vulnerability also establish project beneficiaries. Activity 1.1.3: Establishment and strengthening of complaint feedback desks: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish or strengthen existing complaints feedback desks in all location of the intervention. Complaint feedback desk will inform the team about the level of satisfaction and dissatisfaction served from the targeted population or population groups or response modality. Activity 1.1.4: Distribution of households water storage minimum packages, water treatment tablets to the women and children admitted for SAM/MAM and other affected persons and fetching water from untreated sources: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will conduct distribution of households water storage and water treatment table; Aqua tabs, PUR sachets and water filter clothes to the affected households in the areas highlighted and to the women and children admitted for SAM/MAM in Canal Pigi county, Fangak, Ayod, Malakal, Panyikang, Rubkona and Baliet counties and other locations targeted in the project such as; Koch and and Panyijiar counties Activity 1.1.5 :construction and rehabilitation sanitation facilities latrines in health and nutrition facilities: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will construct and rehabilitate 45 latrines in efforts to improve access to the dignified sanitation and elimination practices of Open Defecations along with its predisposing factors. Activity 1.1.6: Installation of 45 hand washing stations in nutrition facilities: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will install (45) hand washing stations in Baliet, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo, Rubkona and Ayod 5 each location Gender separated in attempt to improve of affected persons, and in response to reduceCOVID-19 transmission. Activity 1.1.7 : Recruitment and training of 90 community hygiene promoters: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will recruit and train( 90 ) hygiene promoters with 10 promoters drawn in each location total (90) promoters, to conduct regular hygiene promotions in public places such as: Nutrition facility, health facilities, markets and schools in compliance with COVID-19 WHO protocol Activity 1.1.8 : Awareness and preparedness sessions on Ebola Virus with beneficiaries and partners: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will conduct training to increase awareness of community on the Ebola preparedness and prevention in all locations targeted by the project.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) is women based organization and its core values are the protection of rights of women, children, girls ,boys and men in South Sudan to equal participations in all and deserve equal access to business and humanitarian and other opportunities. Sexual exploitations and abuse is a grave misconduct and treated with extreme contempt it deserves and fought off zero tolerance. We embrace "DO NO HARM principles" and our organizational code of conduct stipulated clearly. Therefore, project this project will build common mechanisms to eliminate unlawfulness behaviors that violate human rights and beneficiaries rights and perpetration of Gender based violence in our intervention areas and beyond. This is done through awareness raising, reporting cases, monitoring , strengthening community protection based committee with each member drawn from all actors and population groups, to be very focal and vocal to lead great role in the prevention of GBV. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will coordinate with other actors including government, UN agencies international and NNGOS and private sector to fight off GBV and its associates. The project will also engage with the community and stakeholders to support and strengthen the existing referral pathways. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will Champion awareness through community based protection committee(CBPM) and Accountability to the Affected population committee (AAP ). Hope Restoration South Sudan(HRSS) Code of conduct demonstrates zero tolerance and signed by all its staff.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

Establishment and strengthening of complaint feedback desks in the distribution sites or near WASH facilities: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish or strengthen the existing complaints feedback desks in all locations of the intervention such as : Baliet, Fangak, Panyikang, Malakal, Akobo, Ayod, Koch, Panyijiar, Canal Pigi and Rubkona counties of Uppernile, Jonglei and unity states . The Complaint feedback desk, will inform the team about the level of satisfaction and dissatisfaction served during the response. This includes response modalities, selection of distribution sites, Time and date. The safety audits and tools employed. Installation of handwashing facilities was installed and with soaps chlorinated water in the distribution sites in response to COVID-19 Ebola . Hiring crowd controllers to separate the queues between men, women, Girls and boys in efforts to mitigate the GBV concerns against women and girls receiving WASHNFIS. This means distribution sites shouldn't be planned to take place near the military barracks or locations proximal to the armed groups. Feedback tracked from project beneficiaries, will be adapt to strengthen the referral systems and to adjust modalities employed or improved in the next response. We will use the feedback to include the beneficiaries feedbacks in the design and delivery while considering the culture, political dynamics, language barriers of our beneficiaries
