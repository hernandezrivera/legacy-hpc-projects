Project Summary

In line with the Emergency Response Plan (ERP), World Vision in Lebanon will address the unmet immediate basic needs, including food needs, of the most vulnerable Lebanese and Migrant populations in the areas of BML, South, Bekaa, Akkar and North. The current situation in Lebanon including the multi-faceted socio-economic crises, increase in cost of living and breakdown of essential basic services in the country has taken a toll on the most vulnerable individuals, especially persons with specific needs including children and adults living with a disability. The amount of available funding in relation to the growing needs on the ground is becoming increasingly divergent. This has been the case with WVL’s WFP funded cash and in-kind assistance program in Akkar and Bekaa whereby the number of self-referrals through WVL’s hotline is far exceeding the budgeted targets for assistance. The same was observed in BML when a cash assistance program in response to the Beirut Blast was coming to an end as the economic situation is deteriorating. As such, WVL will leverage its presence in the areas and its long-standing partnerships with municipalities and communities, and its internal existing structure to ensure the provision of relief food and basic needs assistance to the most vulnerable individuals not covered by on-going and planned interventions until June 2022. To assess potential beneficiaries, WVL’s will apply its vulnerability assessment and scoring process and tool, while applying a validation process and quality control on both data and process. WVL will also make sure to conduct an inter-agency de-duplication exercise to ensure that the eligible families are not receiving similar assistance from other actors.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

WVL will provide relief food and basic needs assistance to individuals not covered by ongoing and planned interventions until June 2022 through monthly cash assistance to 2120 individuals and in-kind food assistance to 4600 individuals. WVL will build on outreach lists from households who reached out to the WVL hotline and weren’t referred to the WFP projects due to missed deadlines and non- eligibility. The outreach list will then be assessed using WVL’s vulnerability assessment, while applying a validation process and quality control on both data and process. Once households are confirmed, distribution plans will be developed. For in-kind assistance, a message will be shared with each household describing the type of assistance as well as the place, date and time for distribution and documents needed for identification. For cash assistance, selected households will present their identification cards to the service provider and receive an awareness and information session on how to cash the assistance. WVL has an agreement with a cash service provider that allows transfers in USD and LBP and that has access to the Sayrafa platform which offers best exchange rates for LBP transfers. All distribution sites will then be set to ensure accountability, transparency, and dignity for all beneficiaries. By the end of the project, WVL will implement post-implementation monitoring while collecting necessary information for the evaluation of the intervention.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Women and girls among others are subjected to greater protection risks and discrimination based on gender, nationality, age and capacities. They lose opportunities to access life-saving services, are excluded or not prioritized to receive assistance and bear risks of being exposed to various types of exploitation. WVL will ensure that distribution sites are gender sensitive and will prioritize gender considerations like the weight of the parcel, gender training of staff, ensuring the presence of male and female staff to support men and women. Gender-responsive questions will be incorporated into the routine project monitoring and evaluation disaggregated and analyzed by sex and age. WVL supports the inclusion of people with disabilities in its programs. The inclusion process focuses on lessening the barriers to disability by identifying PWDs using WGQ, raising awareness on the community level and tailoring activities whenever needed.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

WVL will guarantee the presence of a functional and accessible Accountability system to enhance beneficiary and community participation as well as ownership of project design and implementation. Targeted beneficiaries will have access to information packages including information about WVL, the project and the community response mechanism via SMS, flyers, helplines and/or information sessions held at field level. The Accountability team will ensure that a help desk is available during major activities (including all distributions). A hotline is available for complaints including a grievance mechanism for the project, validation and referral as needed. Feedback and consultation activities will be conducted during the project implementation to obtain input on the beneficiaries’ preferences and needs to inform programmatic decisions. Complaints will be confidentially handled and closed within the timeframe after proper actions and measures are taken to solve it.
