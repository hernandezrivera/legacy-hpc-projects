Project Summary

The project intends to provide life-saving and preventive nutrition services for 3440 children (1720 boys and 1720 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 1052 Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) through selective feeding and nutrition treatment and further increase evidence based BNSP services linked to Nutrition, Health, WASH, Food security and protection in the targeted locations in Caadado and Cabudwaaq Districts Galgaduud Region as well as Ceelbarde and Wajid Districts in Bakol regions Somalia through the following activities: screened and treatment of the targeted malnourished through OTP/SFP program, promotion of optimum IYCF with emphasis to exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding targeting at the nutrition centers in the project locations, prevention and management of common diseases (anemia, diarrhea, malaria etc) through Vitamin A supplementation, deworming,multiple micro nutrients, supplements, iron/folate supplements and dissemination of hygiene promotion messages and practices to the targeted children aged under 5 and PLWs. The project will in addition equip nutrition staff, community Health workers and Nutrition Volunteers with essential knowledge in case detection through MUAC measurements and weight measures as well as treatment and referals hence management of malnutrition on the project targeted locations.

Needs

Past devastating drought that nearly degenerate into famine in Somalia coupled by successive seasons of erratic Gu 2018 rains attributed to sustained high malnutrition rates, increased morbidity and mortality among children aged 6-59 months and Pregnant Lactating Women (PLW). The situation is exacerbated by severe food insecurity, very limited access to safe water attributed to frequent cases of outbreak of AWD/cholera and other water borne diseases amongst IDPs and vulnerable Host communities in Caadado and Cabudwaaq Districts in Galgadud Region as well as Ceelbarde and Wajid Districts in Bakool Region Somalia. The situation is compounded by limited functional nutrition and health centers and low immunization coverage owing to vast distance covered to access the services coupled by poor breast feeding patterns and among pregnant lactating women and poor diet diversity and complimentary feeding for boys and girls aged 6-59 months coupled by precarious food insecurity situation and poor hygiene practices attributed to frequent hygiene related illnesses. Despite the above average Gu rains that improve food security and replenished existing water sources, malnutrition still remains sustained in Caadado and Cabudwaaq at GAM 14.4% and SAM 1.6% as well as Ceelbarde and Wajid at GAM 17.7% and SAM 3.6% (FSNAU September 2018 Nutrition assessment report). Nutrition assessment further confirmed that there is no significant difference between sex of girls and boys in the locations, therefore malnutrition rates between boys and girls are identical at the rate of 50-50%.. The project targets 3440 children (1720 boys and 1720 girls) acute malnourished aged between 6-59 months and 1052 PLWs for treatment of malnutrition.

Promotion of optimum IYCF activities conducted at the nutrition centers particularly with emphasis to exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding targeting 1052 PLWs in project locations in Caadado, Cabudwaq, Ceelbarde and Wajid3. Prevention and management of common diseases (anemia, diarrhea, malaria etc) through Vitamin A supplementation, deworming,multiple micro nutrients, supplements, iron/folate supplements and dissemination of hygiene promotion messages and practices to the targeted 3440 children (1720 boys and 1720 girls) aged between 6 59 months and 1052 Pregnant Lactating Women4. Training of 40 OTP and SFP staff (20 men and 20 women) as well as volunteers in case detection through MUAC measurements and weight measures5. Training of 60 Community health workers (30 female and 30 male) on nutrition education and to enhance their knowledge in Malnutrition to mitigate increasing malnutrition cases among children aged 6-59 months and PLW6. Conducting 12 Community education sessions for 60 community nutrition volunteers (60 female and 60 male) to increase awareness on malnutrition
