Project Summary

With the entry of the Syrian crisis into its eleventh year, which brought with it many negative aspects, which had a direct impact on the poor families, as it made the families that were able to some extent to secure their daily food to become below the poverty line, and with the increase in the economic situation, the worsening and deteriorating security situation in the governorate As-Suwayda made many families below the poverty line. The project aims to distribute 5,000 food baskets to the poorest families according to a specific need for each family, which will reflect positively on their living situation and reduce their financial burdens, especially that the project targets families that contain children, pregnant women or single-parented women. Where the field teams will verify the level of need for each family targeted by this project in complete impartiality without any sectarian, ethnic, sectarian or sexual considerations

4 - Needs

Market The project is based on distributing 5,000 food baskets to the poorest families in As-Suwayda Governorate, which consists of a large number of family members, or has children, pregnant women, or families who have lost breadwinners, after verifying the level of need through field teams that will conduct field visits to these families and verify the The extent of entitlement The level of need today in As-Suwayda governorate is great, due to the deteriorating economic situation and the bad security situation, which has caused a decline in job opportunities and the spread of unemployment, as the number of these families is more than 30,000 families in need, according to the estimates of the Red Crescent, where it targets approximately 10,000 families per month. The rest of the families are waiting for an opportunity to register with non-governmental organizations, and the United Nations has estimated the proportion of poor families in Syria to be more than 80% of the Syrian population.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Due to the deteriorating security situation in As-Suwayda Governorate, the main danger that the working group may be exposed to is exposure by some outlaws. This danger can be overcome in coordination with the people and the local community to avoid any problems. Also, the center has a good reputation and the ability to reach all areas of the governorate


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The project works without discrimination between any sects, race or gender. It also imposes on all workers to respect the beneficiaries and deal with them within the humanitarian provisions stipulated in the moral charter of the center. It will also publish methods of complaints about any abuses that may occur, preserving the privacy of the complainant.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The security situation worsens, which will reflect negatively on the reality of work more and with it the unemployment rate will rise significantly. The implementation of such programs constitutes a basic guarantee for families in the event that the situation gets out of control, as this danger will be avoided in coordination and cooperation with the people
