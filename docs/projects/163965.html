Project Summary

The project will address the urgent health needs of both IDP and Host Communities in Idleb and Aleppo governorates (western Aleppo countryside) with an emphasis on continuity of current projects and integration of mental health and psychosocial support services (MHPSS) with primary and secondary health care in line with WHO and IASC guidelines. Integrated MHPSS services will be delivered at health facilities in both static and mobile modules. In addition, the project aims to enhance the quality of medical services through provision of technical support via capacity building opportunities, equipment, medications, supplies and operational costs (i.e. salaries, fuel, etc.) within 6 primary health care facilities, 2 maternity and childhood hospitals, seven mobile clinics and the provision of immunization services through fixed and mobile teams. There will be a health system strengthening component through the continuation of the existing referral system and provision of capacity building trainings on infection prevention and control (IPC), non-communicable disease (NCD) management and pharmacy management. The project will ensure and improve health access for the elderly and persons with disabilities (PWDs) through modified accommodations and mechanisms.
SRD maintains a strong working relationship with Local health authorities, humanitarian actors in the area in addition to close collaboration with OCHA, WHO, and Health Cluster and respective TWGs,
SRD is in constant and continuous coordination with NGOs (HIH, IDA, SAMS, SEMA, UOSSM, SR, DDD, Al-Sham Foundation, Shafak, SDI, REA..) and INGOs (RI,WVI, IMC, MSF, Islamic Relief, IRC, QC, HI). SRD proposed project complement the health services provided by operating partners within referral system, NCD, IPC and mhGAP activities.

4 - Needs

During 2019, nearly 1 million IDPs left their homes & belongings and moved to safer areas in NWS, which greatly increased load & need for health services. According to CCCM reports, around 517k people fled their homes toward NWS between the period of December 1, 2019 to January 29, 2020. In 2019, SRD's Health program responded to needs of enhancing health systems especially unified referral system along with the provision of quality health services to communities in the form of primary and secondary care. In addition, SRD ensured availability and access to routine, emergency, and specialized health services in order to meet the needs of the most vulnerable groups in Aleppo and Idlib gov. Based on a health needs assessments carried out by SRD, the most prominent health needs were identified as follows: - improve access to general health services for Host and IDP communities, including vaccination provision, malnutrition management, screenings and referrals to PHCs -ensure access to family planning (FP) and other reproductive health services including normal deliveries, caesarian sections (C-sections), prenatal and postnatal care, and post-abortion care-provide acute and chronic (NCD) care, including treatment for complications in such cases. Conditions specified include hypertension, asthma, diabetes, measles, polio, acute watery diarrhea, COPD, depression, psychosis, and substance abuse-provide surgical services, including: amputations, post-trauma complications, war/non-war related injuries, and cerebrovascular accidents (CVA)-improve emergency and non emergency patient transportion-facilitate access to gender based-violence services, specifically cases of rape, harassment and domestic violence -provide medications,ie. NCDs, SRH and psychotropics The target beneficiaries dis-aggregation calculation are in place according to the services and location in addition to project type also based on previous projects data and current IDPs movement.

5 - Implementation of activities

SRD activities in 2020 are in line with health sector priorities including:-Enhancing health systems through implementation of NCD, IPC and emergency and non-emergency referral system between all care levels, as well, comprehensive service mapping.-Improve access to primary&secondary health services through existing facilities in Idleb and NW Aleppo including 2 Maternity and Child Hospitals (Harem,Daret Azza) and 6 PHCs (Abin, Kafrtaal, Azmarin, Mills, Hur, Afs) with MHPSS integration by mhGAP trained doctors and PSWs (MHPSS, PM+, CBT) along with outreach teams to expand mental mental health and psycho-social support service capacity and coverage.-Expand availability of the essential primary health service package, with particular emphasis on gaps such as availability of essential medicines and diagnostic services, as well as treatment of NCDs.-Support 5 ambulance cars within the emergency referral system, as well as trauma and emergency services with 20 non-Emergency patient transportation vehicles to ensure access of patients to health services.-Provide comprehensive RH services, including (MISP), first-line care for GBV survivors and tailored services for adolescents in all SRD facilities.-Support 7 mobile clinic and 4 RH mobile teams, particularly to newly displaced populations and under-served areas with non-functional or partially functional facilities. -Ensure a reliable safe, quality medicines and medical supplies.-Continue routine vaccination (by static and mobile teams) for children in SRD's 5 PHCs.-Capacity building of health facilities staff on Referral system, IPC & NCD also provide training health care workers and community health workers and support community health programming, particularly to vulnerable and high-risk populations.The beneficiaries engaged in many phases by assessing needs (needs assessments), feedback and suggestion (feedback &complaints mechanisms) that are in place in all SRD's projects to achieve projects objectives.
