1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Throughout the action, which will be closely coordinated with the CP AoR, UNICEF, the national and province-level Reference Groups on Child Protection, Combating Human Trafficking and Irregular Migration (RGs) and other CP partners, IOM will target key child protection services, including those providing MHPSS to children, IDP children, adolescents and caregivers. IOM will aim to contribute to addressing key gaps in child protection knowledge, services and quality, which have been identified in the UNICEF-IOM Child Protection Need and Risk Assessment. The project will focus on further strengthening the RGs on Child Protection and Combating Human Trafficking at Province and District levels in Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa, increasing their capacities to protect children in the current emergency setting. Particular attention will be paid to strengthening coordination capacities and forging linkages with Community Child Protection Committees. In order to improve children’s access to MHPSS services, IOM will work with the Child Protection AoR and MHPSS TWG to strengthen capacities of child and social services at province and district levels in Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa, as well as health services and NGO/INGO partners, as well as to increase the referrals of IDP and host community children to such services.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Building the Capacity of Reference Groups: IOM will expand its support to the identification and coordination capacities of Reference Groups and sub-committees at province and district levels through training, coaching and support to provide direct assistance to child beneficiaries. In particular, in Niassa, which has so far not been supported in a targeted manner, the non-served district will be supported in the set-up and basic training as well as targeted support of the Reference Groups. In Cabo Delgado and Nampula, IOM will work to further improve their work as well as coordinate with Community Child Protection Committees. 2. Strengthening MHPSS services for Children: Based on its global expertise and local capacities in providing MHPSS to the IDP and host community population, IOM will strengthen its community-based MHPSS interventions focusing on children, including building children’s capacities through awareness-raising and focused MHPSS activities, and build the capacities of child and health services to improve the access of children to such services through targeted training, on-the-job coaching and coordination efforts.Above mentioned activities will be implemented in the following provinces and districts:Cabo Delgado: Ancuabe, Balama, Chiure, Pemba, Ibo, Metuge, MontepuezNampula: Meconta, Memba, Nampula CityNiassa: Lichinga, Marrupa

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

IOM employs an integrated protection-MHPSS approach which ensures intersectional considerations of protection and MHPSS needs as well linkages with health programming in a whole-of-community approach. IOM identifies individual and structural-level protection risks through outreach to IDPs through its activists and by conducting regular protection/safety audits in the districts and in the provision of referrals and assistance.IOM’s community-based activists are trained to identify vulnerable groups, including children and adolescents and the elderly, refer them to available services, advocate and assist them in gaining equal access to available humanitarian aid and protection services. IOM follows strict data protection rules and assistance standards in order to ensure meaningful access, not to cause further harm and empower individuals and communities assisted. IOM’s Protection-MHPSS Programme ensures awareness raising and capacity building on PSEA at the field level. All of the community workers and team members are trained on PSEA by IOM PSEA focal points and/or members of the PSEA Network. In line with that, all IOM programmes are targeted with PSEA training and refresher sessions conducted by IOM PSEA Focal Points. In addition, IOM Protection-MHPSS Programme conducts regular PSEA awareness-raising sessions not only for the communities but also for government counterparts at the district and provincial levels. IOM will continue increasing its and partners’ capacity in PSEA while ensuring communities’ access to the reporting and complaint mechanisms.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This project aims to increase access of IDPs to the protection and MHPSS services. IOM has a multi-disciplinary core team and community field workers working at the site/district levels. The teams identify protection risks and needs and address them either through direct service provision or referral to the more specialized services. Male and female community workers conduct door-to-door outreach to reach individuals at the risk of marginalization due to limited access to the services (e.g., elderly, persons with physical disabilities, persons with chronic diseases) and the individuals who live in distant locations. Moreover, all community workers were selected based on their language capacities in cooperation with the social services to ensure that IDPs can express their needs, concerns, and requests in their native language. In addition, IOM cooperates with and supports the government entities to reduce the barriers in access. For example, IOM conducts joint mobile brigades with government health and social services to target individuals with limited or no access to the facilities. In order to implement these activities, IOM works very closely with the community leaders and members in the sites and host community settings. The activities at site level are designed in cooperation with the communities to ensure that their needs are addressed in culturally appropriate manner.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

IOM Mobile Teams and community-based protection activists identify and refer people with special needs, including individuals with physical and mental disabilities, to adequate services. IOM’s trained MHPSS professionals are able to provide counselling and mitigate some mental health impacts of displacement. IOM pays special attention to ensuring that persons with disabilities, incl. albinism, are protected from human trafficking, abuse and exploitation under the integrated health-protection-MHPSS activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed project does not include any construction work or other activities with significant environmental impact. In IOM’s community-based MHPSS activities strive to use environmentally friendly, recycled or reusable materials, and respect for the environment is included in some awareness-raising sessions with communities.
