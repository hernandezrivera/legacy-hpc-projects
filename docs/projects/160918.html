Project Summary

In South Sudan more than 7 million people are in need of assistance, a number increasing due to returnees. The prolonged conflict, climatic change and its effect are both contributing to humanitarian situation in the country. According to the August 2019 to April 2020, Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) analysis conducted, an estimated 6.35 million people (54% of the population) in South Sudan are classified as being in Serious Crisis (IPC 3+); While the Prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) in children have increased across the country in comparison to 2018. The Overall GAM for children has increased to 16.2% (2019) compared to 13.3% in 2018. In Western Bahr el Ghazal the prevelance of GAM for children has increased from 10.4% (2018) to 15.5% (2019); however in Lakes the GAM has reduced from 12.2% to 11.2 and in Warrap the prevalence of GAM for children have reduced from 15.3% to 15.1% though above the WHO threshold (FSNMS round 24). In 2020 over 1.3 million children 6-59 months will suffer malnutrition and close to 292,000 with SAM. With this situation Peace Corps is planning to work with Partners (INGO) the government (MOH) and the lead agency within the selected areas to support the vulnerable community of Warrap, Lakes State and WBeG as earlier mentioned. This project will contribute to reduction of morbidity and mortality related to malnutrition among vulnerable populations within the proposed project areas, to approximately 8% . In order to increase equitable access and utilization of quality preventative nutrition specific service delivery for children, adolescents and women, Peace Corps plans to provide preventative nutrition specific services to 29,967 (women, men, boys and girls) This shall be done at House Hold, community and the facility. Male and Female Staff will be recruited this includes community nutrition volunteers (CNV); mother to mother support; qualified staff (nutrition assistance and officers) who will be trained and provided with necessary working tools; at community level they will conduct growth monitoring, provide specialized food where applicable. The same staff will be used for behavior change communication through use of nutrition information IEC material; counselling and the establishment of Mother to Mother Support Group (MTSG). In prevention of malnutrition through provision of micronutrients. Children, women and adolescence will be provided with folic acid; provision of deworming supplements and Vitamin A as per the country guideline, this will includes 30610 children reached through campaign. In the second objective Peace Corps plans to provide nutrition curative and preventive services, through improvement of staff knowledge:- the recruited staff will be trained on CMAM/ MIYCN context to enable them provide evidence based care; staff will be provided with working tools including job aid, guideline and screening material. Children, between 6-59 months will be screened and treated through relevant programs; the project targets 1197 children with severe acute malnutrition (SAM) thorough out-patient therapeutic program (OTP); 1730 moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) through targeted supplementary feeding program (TSFP) moreover and 1378 PLW with moderate acute malnutrition will be enrolled and treated through (TSFP). In the third and fourth objective:- Peace corps plans to integrate this with the current WASH and Education project within Raga, however in others area, the organization will work with other partners to ensure services are integrated. The program will ensure malaria testing and provision of WASH kits to all children identified with SAM; GBV awareness creation and working with protections partners. Peace corps will collect and submit routine data to partners to ensure compliance.

1 - Needs

Generally the government is supposed to take responsibility for the basic services within the country; however with economic instability within the country due to effect of conflict has led to the government struggle with funds to address the nutrition gaps. According to the recent IPC report (September 2019) an estimated 6.35 million people facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity besides the proposed project area like WBeG food insecurity is driven by household food shortages, which has been further exacerbated by population displacements caused by the additional needs from returnees. The Prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) in children have increased across the country in comparison to 2018 and the Overall GAM in Western Bahr el Ghazal has increased from 10.4% (2018) to 15.5% (2019); the project plans to support Raga county and the projected case load for the area in 2020 is Raga ( SAM under 5 is 1250; MAM under 5 is 2308; MAM PLW is 900; MIYCN is 6750 and Vitamin A (13,283) and BSFP Under 5( 3800). In Lakes the GAM had reduced from 12.2% to 11.2 however the projected cases loads in the target locations 2020 is; in Cueibet ( SAM under 5 is 6979; MAM under 5 is 9515; MAM PLW is 9396; MIYCN is 16,835 and Vitamin A (60,250); Yirol East( SAM under 5 is 4298; MAM under 5 is 7714; MAM PLW is 6840; MIYCN is 17,725 and Vitamin A (37,609); BSFP under 5 (16835) and BSFP PLW (60,250). Moreover, apart from the general Country conflict; Warrap State have been frequently affected by resource related or inter- communal conflict; mainly in Tonj North; likewise the state have been affected by climatic changes including drought early 2019 and currently in the recent September Inter Agency Assessment (IRNA) the area have been hugely affected by floods. With the floods it is project deterioration of nutrition situation; because of significant increase of underlying causes of malnutrition i.e increased disease; Increase cases of malaria, pneumonia and diarrhea reported from health facilities, reduce house hold food consumption due to low harvest since Crops in low land areas have been affected and sharing of malnutrition supplies due to low harvest; Poor hygiene and Sanitation due to reduced access to water supply since some boreholes are submerged in the flood water which brings contaminated water additionally. In Warrap the GAM rate has reduced from 15.3% to 15.1% though above the WHO threshold; moreover, due to the impact of flooding on household crop production, the level of GAM is likely to remain high. This is expected to deteriorate further in the first quarter of 2020, the selected county caseloads Tonj North ( SAM under 5 is 2624 ; MAM under 5 is 13,961; MAM PLW is 4380; MIYCN is 8,207 and Vitamin A (143,947); BSFP under 5 (650) and BSFP PLW (780).This number has informed target beneficiaries. Moreover according to Food Security and Nutrition Monitoring System (FSNMS 24), this is expected to deteriorate further in the first quarter of 2020 with the early onset of the lean season as most households are expected to deplete food stocks from own production, seasonal changes in disease trends also with the continuation of rainy season, that has effectively set in, is likely to result in increased incidences of diseases such as malaria and acute diarrhoea. Both of these factors are immediate causes of maternal and child Under-nutrition. Furthermore, the existence of other chronic disease like TB/ HIV AIDs and Kalaazar within the country also contributes to malnutrition to both male, female, boy and girls including persons with disability.

2 - Activities

Peace Corps intends to work with other partners mainly the International organization, Cluster, the lead agencies in nutrition and other stakeholder to achieve the proposed plan. In the first objective; Prevention of malnutrition through provision of specialized food. Preventive activities will be done in three areas (Household; Community and the Facility); Peace Corps will recruit key staff that includes community nutrition volunteers (CNV), qualified nutrition staff (Nutrition assistant, nutrition officers and nutrition nurses. Train the staff on provision and utilization of specialized food at the community and household level. active case identification at the house hold, growth monitoring and utilization of supplementary food and follow-up of cases enrolled in the program to ensure there is compliance in utilization of the specialized food at household level. TB/HIV patients will benefit on this as per the protocol.Children between the age of 6 – 59 months; pregnant and lactating women (PLW) will benefits on this. The project intends to promote behavior change communication through use of nutrition information IEC material; counselling of adolescence girls, boys, women, men including care givers on nutrition that includes Maternal Infant and Young Child Nutrition Promotion (MIYCN)on (EBF) and complementary feeding. Peace Corps will encourage the use of Drama, songs and dances at the community level, the establishment of Mother to Mother Support Group (MTSG) since mothers are integral part of care and nutrition in children. In prevention of micro-nutrient deficiencies, children, women and adolescence access micro-nutrients, this includes provision of Vitamin A supplements to children, folic acid to prevent anemia to girls, women of child bearing ages and PLW; provision of deworming supplements and iodize salt to women of reproductive age. Timely, accurate and credible information sharing will be done. In the second objective - CNV, MTMSG are expected to refer screened and eligible cases to the respective treatment programs; qualified recruited staff will be trained on CMAM/ MIYCN- Emergency context to enable them provide evidence based care; staff will be provided with treatment protocols and guidelines, working tools. Peace Corps will screen, admit and rehabilitate 1197 children between 6-59 months with severe acute malnutrition (SAM) through out-patient therapeutic program (OTP); screen and rehabilitate 1730 children between 6-59 months with moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) through targeted supplementary feeding program (TSFP) moreover 1378 PLW with moderate acute malnutrition will be enrolled and treated through (TSFP). In the third objective, Peace Corps is providing WASH and Education intervention in Raga and therefore the nutrition intervention will maximize the opportunity of integration through access to and supply of WASH kits to children with SAM. This activities will be integrated with WASH intervention to ensure, hygiene promotions/ education; health education. The program will ensure malaria testing to all children identified with SAM; the organization intends to work with FAO partners, to promote Kitchen gardening to MTMSG that will be established; In relation to education the program intend to create child friendly space. In regards to GBV, the health education at community level will involve GBV awareness creation through use of GBV partner in the area, display of GBV IEC Materials in the facility and use of role plays and demonstration to create awareness to women, children, and men. In the fourth Objective, the organization will ensure timely and appropriate data collection and submission through the laid down protocol. Additionally, the organization has been participating in the National Nutrition Cluster meeting, this has given it an upper hand of acquiring information, learning to ensure the program complies to cluster requirements and other policies within the country.
