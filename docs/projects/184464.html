Project Summary

Save the Children (SC) aims to provide life-saving WASH assistance to among disaster affected vulnerable settlements and communities (including men, women, boys and girls) in order to reduce excess morbidity and mortality rate from preventable crisis-driven diseases and public health outbreaks among most vulnerable people. The project will also provide and restore sustainable access to safe and equitable basic WASH services in targeted locations (including non-IDP men, women, boys and girls). SC will provide a holistic response to ensure affected families and their children have access to necessary WASH services to prevent water related disease outbreaks and address acute water shortage, sanitation and hygiene needs among disaster affected communities in Lughaya, Zeila, Borama, Iskushuban, Qardho, Banderbayla, Bossaso, Garowe, Beletweyne, Wajid, Baidoa, Diinsoor, Burhakaba, Qoryoley, Garawe, Banadir, Burco, Caynabo, Ceerigaabo, Gabiley, Hargeysa, Kismayo and Galkayo. The WASH activities are designed to be implemented at household and institution levels considering people with disabilities, the elderly, and safety of children as well as dignity and privacy for women and girls. The household level approach will target families affected by floods, drought and conflict. The health facilities level approach will contribute to improve access to quality healthcare and improve nutrition outcomes. The target community will have access to safe water supply through emergency water supply with temporary distribution systems, pipeline extensions, rehabilitation of existing water supply systems (shallow wells, boreholes, berkads) with proper water quality monitoring, well disinfection and chlorination. Moreover, SC will construct sanitation facilities targeting communities who do not have any access, and health/nutrition facilities and public schools, desludging of pits/septic tanks where applicable, conduct cleaning campaigns (solid waste removal and disposal), conduct hygiene promotion campaigns that will focus on community involvement in hygiene sessions, household visits and community awareness campaigns including promotion of Household Water Treatment and Safe Storage (HWTSS) and provision of hygiene kits to the most vulnerable groups. Additionally, the project will build community resilience and increase sustainability of WASH services by enhancing the capacity of village level water and sanitation management committees to manage, operate and maintain water and sanitation facilities. SC will also ensure accountability to affected populations through improving access to complaint/feedback channels. SC will use Market Based Programming (MBP) approach mainly voucher system for the provision of water trucking kit where the right market conditions exist.
SC will ensure participation and active engagement with people with disabilities in all stages of the programme. The project will address barriers and risks faced by people with disabilities when accessing WASH services through consultations, organizing meetings in physically accessible venues and programme outputs are accessible to children and adults with disabilities, such as WASH infrastructure and distribution of hygiene kit distribution sites are accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. Moreover, Hygiene promoters will deliver key hygiene messages through various communication means to reach people with disabilities through door-to-door visit.
The project targets 100,000 people (20520 women, 15480 men, 36480 girls, 27520 boys) of which of which 15000 (8550 female, 6450 male) are people with disabilities.SC has extensive experience targeting and seeking positive impacts for gender equality through its programming. SC will promote the full participation of women to ensure that their needs are addressed adequately at all stages of project design, implementation and monitoring. 1. - Projects activities

Deliver emergency water services and provide / restore access to basic water supply Activity 1.1: Conduct emergency water supply with temporary distribution systems through vouchers where appropriate to 66,666 people Activity 1.2: Conduct well disinfection and chlorination. Water testing will be conducted at water sources, collection points and promote household Water Treatment and Safe Storage (HWTSS) will be done. Activity 1.3: Conduct 15 pipeline extension/rehabilitation of existing water supply system. Activity 1.4: Provision of 10 water containers for transport and storage. Activity 1.5: Monitoring of water quality at HH level and/or community level. Activity 1.6: Rehabilitation of 10 shallow wells to ensure they are protected in compliance with cluster standards Activity 1.7: Rehabilitation of 11 boreholes with full compliance to cluster guidelines. Activity 1.8: Improvement of 10 berkads (sizing, cover, lining, water extraction, etc.). Activity 1.9: Establishment of 36 water committees to ensure proper maintenance of water services. Deliver emergency sanitation services and provide/ restore access to basic sanitation including institutions Activity 2.1: Construct 500 sanitation facilities targeting communities who do not have any access. Activity 2.2: Construct 30 gender segregated VIP latrines with hand washing stations in health/nutrition facilities and public schools. Activity 2.3: Desludge 100 pits/septic tanks where applicable. Activity 2.4: Conduct 75 cleaning campaigns (solid waste removal and disposal). 4.6 - Mitigation Measures

• Use of the Somalia WASH cluster guidance on mainstreaming protection. Services will be accessible to avoid exposing beneficiaries, particularly women, and provided with dignity • Interventions will consider culture and respect values and religious practices.• Ensure community involvement through community committees and community volunteers • Both IDPs and host communities will have access to services without any discrimination. Special attention to special groups such as the disabled and elderly will be prioritized. • Latrines will be constructed in a way to ensure protection and safety of the users and minimize any risk of collapse. Each latrine will have separate blocks (one for men and one for women) and have lockable doors to ensure privacy and protection of women using the facilities. • The monitoring of water quality will ensure protection of the target population from diseases. • Taps stand will be located within a radius of 500m to reduce distance and enhance security of women and girls who often collect water to avoid cases of SGBV• The hygiene promoters are from the same community and most of them women, which eases communication with HHs, and are culturally acceptable. Home visits will be conducted at an appropriate time to ensure privacy of the HHs.• Beneficiaries will be fully made aware of the scope of the project and expected deliverables. Monthly Focused Group Discussions will be conducted regularly on the course of the project to measure the beneficiary satisfaction with the progress and contribution made by the project. • A feedback and response mechanism consisting of a hotline, suggestion boxes, and community feedback sessions will be put in place and managed by the MEAL team.
