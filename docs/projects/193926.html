Project Summary

The project aims at improving sanitary situation in the selected Areas of the suburb of
Shweika in Tulkarm Governorate where 11417 people live. The improvement of the
sanitation situation in the target areas is essential for a stable life with basic services that
will help the residents to suffer less when Suburb is served with sanitation facilities.
The proposed project serves the western area of Shweika, north of Tulkarm city, which is
located along the separation wall (about 5 km2 located in area C and 2 km located in A
and B areas) with a total estimated population 4.500 (Turkarem Municipality 2021).
Based on a recent survey conducted by House of Water and Environment (HWE), about
2700 (1326 female and 1339 male) people will benefit from this project. These areas
suffer badly from a lack of wastewater collection system as 90 % of their residents rely on
septic tanks and cesspits, which cause unsanitary conditions and pollution of soil and
groundwater resources in addition to affecting negatively on the public health and safety
of the women, men, children elderly and disabilities. Therefore, there is an urgent need to
mitigate the bad sanitary conditions in these areas through installing wastewater
collection system to serve the targeted population in Shwaikeh area and to promote good
access to environmentally sound municipal wastewater services. It is worth to mention
that the proposed HWE interventions in the targeted area will contribute to HRP WASH
Cluster Objective 2 (Ensure equitable, affordable and sustainable safely-managed access
to sufficient WASH services for the most vulnerable Palestinians).Public awareness
campaigns will be conducted to address hygiene practice issues at the household level
and the High schools to improve the capacity of the families and students in the targeted
areas to effectively respond to emergencies and reduce tensions across the communities.
It worth to mention that, transmission of COVID-19 and other water borne diseases will be
lowered. The proposed interventions ensure protection mainstreaming incorporating
protecting principles and promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity; accountability
as well as participation and empowerment through conducting regular meeting and focus
groups. HWE will implement the project with the partnership with Tulkarm municipality
and highly coordination with PWA to achieve the project outcomes and outputs in
addition to ensure the sustainability of the project.

2 - Activities

Needs assessment has been conducted by HWE with the coordination with TulkaremMunicipality for the targeted areas, this includes identifying the targeted areas and theproposed activities. A high coordination with PWA was done to identify the plannedinterventions. It is worth to mention that the coordination with Tulkarem Municipality andPWA will be continued during the project implementation phase. In addition to the dataobtained from WASH cluster teams. Proposed interventions and activities can becategorized as follows: Activity 1: Installation of sewer connection network and HHconnections. This activity consists of the followings: 1.1 Construction of sewer lines inShweikeh Suburb: The proposed networks to be installed are detailed as: Length: 2450 mNumber of House connections: 300 Number of Beneficiaries: 2500 (1240 Males and 1228Females) Proposed activities include: design of sewer line, excavation works, UPVC pipesinstallation, manholes installation, filling and compact excavations with sand and basecoarse layers. Activity 2: Hygiene promotion Awareness campaign: 2.1 Conduct Publicawareness targeting the targeted community and 4 high schools to promote householdconnection to municipal networks and improve hygiene behavior. Consultive meeting withTulkarem municipality, the representatives of the communities and schools’ principals andteachers will be conducted to design the awareness campaign. HWE will developcommunication plan identifying the key messages, the most popular social media topublish the messages in the targeted area. The key messages will focus on the positiveimpacts of the connection to sewer networks on conserving the environment, public healthand the economy. Sanitizers will be distributed to the beneficiaries. All proposed activitieswill ensure protection principles on safety and dignity, meaningful access, accountability,participation, empowerment and claiming of rights. During the Design and the implementation of the project, HWE team will develop a safety plan that includes all the necessary measures to prevent any harm for the community, taking into consideration: gender equity, age groups needs and disabilities special needs. This will be implemented using the following health and hygiene measures to mitigate risks and create a safe construction site:• Announcing the implementation of the project for the community before it starts. • Assigning a committee from the area that will represent a focal point to reach if any inconvenience occurs during construction, and to enable citizens to be more involved in the project. • Contractor will be responsible for following all safety instructions at site. • Avoid excavation works during early/ late hours (rest time of day) to minimize noise and dust emissions.• Display signages clearly (ex: warning signs, signs to control speed limits, etc.). • To prevent accidents and injuries, clear and proper pathways will be installed so the people will have easy access into and out of their homes. • Workers will continuously clean the site to avoid any hazardous material. • Always maintain a clear access route throughout the worksite. • Storing hazardous material and machinery in a secure site.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

Installation of wastewater network will ensure a safe and hygienic disposal ofwastewater. Therefore, cesspits flood, odour, groundwater pollution, waterbornediseases, accidents risks esp. for children and disabled people and conflicts betweenneighbours will be prevented. HWE team will develop a safety plan integrating all thepreventive measures to avoid any harm to the targeted community. Further, allpreventive measures to minimize the risk of the COVID 19 pandemic will be taken intoconsideration. Hygiene kits and equipment will be provided and distributed to workteam, contractors and community as needed. HWE will follow a participatory approachto ensure the equal participation of the vulnerable people taking into consideration theneeds of women, children and disable people to strength their roles and equality.These groups will be enhanced to participate in decision process of proposedinterventions and the monitoring phase of the project. HWE will conduct a variety ofcommunication tools to protect the community esp. vulnerable groups, ensure genderequality and their participation. Mainly the public awareness session, workshops andfocus group will addressed the do no harm principles to avoid the negative impact ofpotential conflicts. In addition, key massages on WASH project will be disseminatedthrough social media and posters. This Safety plan generally will consist of thefollowing protection activities in line with all humanitarian principles and needs: 1)Responsive activities: immediate and urgent violation and effects are to be predicted,immediate actions to treat with such effects are to be defined. Example 1: Immediateeffect: sudden collapse in a trench Immediate action: Evacuation of people around thecollapse, consolidation of collapse edges to prevent expansion and first aid for anyinjury, if any. Example 2: Immediate effect: Cut in water pipe inside trench. Immediateaction: Fix it as soon as possible. Example 3: Immediate effect: Violation between twoneighbours on their cross border. Immediate action: Cooperate with representatives ofcommunity to solve such problems, and try to avoid creating violence betweenneighbours. 2) Remedial activities: long term effects are to be indicated; remedial planis to be set. This activity will also include forming a committee from community withequitable gender representation (5-7 people) to facilitate project implementation andsolve any upcoming problem with community. Example: Remedial effect: House ownerexplained about crossing sewer line his house sidewalk because during time ofimplementation he will has social activity such as wedding party. Remedial action:negotiation plan and awareness activity. 3) Environmental activities: Define activitiesto create safe work environment for workers and community. Example: Installation ofwarning signs to warn from any expected harm, e.g. warning from falling in a trench.HWE will set a complaint and feedback mechanism to treat confidentially with anycomplaints esp. complaints related to sexual abuse and exploitation. This mechanismwill be stated by HWE code of conduct.
