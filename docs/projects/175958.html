Project Summary

The project aims to provide dignified and safe access to WASH in transit and relocation sites, as well as in the surrounding host communities in order to achieve ODF environments and reduce and prevent tensions between host and IDP communities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project focusses on providing dignified and safe access to WASH for IDP and host populations in Ancuabe and Metuge districts through a threefold approach1) Immediate response to the most urgent WASH needs of newly arrived IDPs in 3 transition centersIn correspondence with Objective 1, life saving support will be provided in transit centers, responding to the most urgent needs in the first weeks upon displacement. As such, protection risks with regards to safe access to Water and Sanitation services will be reduced and the spread of waterborne diseases such as Colera and AWD will be prevented in the densely populated transit sites. 2) Implementation of WASH services in 2 relocation sitesUpon multisectorial assessments and community engagement to ensure relocation complies with the minimum standards and is voluntary, CLTS HH sanitation facility construction will be mobilized and good hygiene practices will be promoted through community structures. 3) Improved access to WASH and complete sanitation in host communities surrounding Transit and Relocation sites.In order to support a complete sanitation approach, as well as to reduce tensions between host and IDP community, CLTS will be promoted in host communities, as well and water systems will be reinforced through the rehabilitation of the exising non-functional water systems.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) In order to respond to the most urgent WASH needs of newly arrived IDPs in transit sites in Ancuabe and Metuge the following activities will be implemented:· Construction of 500 Emergency latrines and bathing facilities· Punctual Water Trucking to bridge water supply gaps· Distribution of 1,500 Hygiene and Dignity kits· Installation of 250 handwashing stations· Training of WASH committees and activists in all sites· Distribution of Water treatment products to newly arrived IDPs (10,000 HH) 2) HH in 2 relocation sites in Ancuabe and Metuge will receive the following support· Support to the CLTS self-construction of 1,500 HH latrines and bathing facilities including material support for the most vulnerable HH· Training of WASH committees in all sites for a strong community engagement component· Hygiene promotion on community level through community meetings and talks 3) Host community support to communities adjacent to IDP sites/centers in Ancuabe and Metuge Districts· Support to the CLTS self-construction of 500 HH latrines in host communities adjacent to IDP sites/centers for a complete sanitation· Rehabilitation of 2 water points in host communities with limited access to water

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the entire project, which itself aims to reduce the esposure to protection threats that arises from the lack of WASH.Meaningful access is assured on the basis of a vulnerability assessment among the IDP population, which will be carried out prior to the implementation phase and which will inform the beneficiary selection. Targeting will thus be need-based, not status based and the project will consider the specific needs that arise from different vulnerabilities.In addition, a conflict-sensitivity assessment will be carried out previously to the implementation phase in order to avoid inflicting harm through the action. In order to decrease ground for tensions and conflicts, both, host and IDP community will be participating in the project activites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities. Therefore, Focus Group Discussion with committees of different vulnerable groups will be held on the implementation of every infrastructural change. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through community mechanisms. Village leaders, as well as members of different committess (WASH, women, older people etc.) will be informed on planned activities.Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process.All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responded to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Effective disability inclusion will be assured through identification of persons with disabilities including different disability status in an exhaustive vulnerability assessment and using the fundamental tools (Washington Group Questions). Persons with disabilities will be prioritized as especially vulnerable group.Consultations will be done with individuals with disabilities and older persons to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

In order to reduce the impact on the environment, plastic will be replaced by regenerative natural resources as much as possible and construction materials will be locally sourced.Local materials, such as wood and bamboo will be purchased from certified vendors with national sourcing permits.
