Project Summary

Humanitarian needs have continued to increase in South Sudan due to decades of civil unrest, recurrent climatic shocks, ongoing public health challenges, and COVID-19.The nutrition situation in South Sudan remain alarming about 2 million children under five years are in need of nutrition assistance . The burned of Acute malnutrition among U5 children is significantly increased in 2022 compared to 2021 from nearly 1.4 to 2 million. Of the six counties IRC is implementing Aweil south and Panyijar counties the recvecnt SMART survey reveals that the nutritional status in both counties were at Critical level with GAM of 23.1 % and 15.7% respectively. According to FSNMS round 27 Aweil east and Yirol west counties have serious nutrition status with proxy GAM rate of 13.1%and 9.2% respectively and the remaining two counties have poor nutritional status with Proxy GAM rate 7.8% and 6.5% in Rumbek north and Rumbek eat respectively. Despite the low GAM rate in four of the counties Multidimensional cases that aggravate the situation is observed that includes, flooding sever food insecurity, sub optimal practice of Maternal infant and young child feeding, and high rate of under-five mortality and morbidity that attributes worsening of the nutrition situation in the counties in the coming seasons
The proposed intervention aims to increase coverage and access to the most vulnerable community through provision of equitable and accessible nutrition prevention treatment services. The nutrition services will delivered through scale up the emergency nutrition responses through 87 static OTP/TSFP (17 in Aweil east, 10 in Aweilsouth,18 in Panyijar, 23 in Rumbek east 7 Rumbek North, and 12 in Yirol west ), 6 Stabilization center(two in Pnayjar, one in Aweil south, 1 in Rumbek eats, 1 in Rumbek north, and 1 in Yirol west) and through 6 RRM mobile tam (2 in Aweil east ,2 in Aweil south, 2 in Panyjar,). The IRC will implement integrated CMAM and MIYCN in all sites. Promotion of optimal nutrition and prevention malnutrition among young children (0-23 months through implementation of MIYCN to ensure vulnerable children have optimal growth and protected from irreversible malnutrition. Strengthening community participation and use of community structure through active engagements of the affected community through strengthening of MtMSG, increase engagement and role of CNV in prevention activities and regular community sensitization sessions. Scaling up of the existing Nutrition response to ensure timely service availability at all times. Scale up family MUAC to ensure early detection of acute malnutrition that contributes to early detection. An estimated of 20,031 SAM children,62,277 under five and 49,716 PLW MAM will be reached through this action across all implementation area in OTP and TSFP, respectively. Continue with the capacity building of CHD, and Primary health care service providers including IRC nutrition staffs. Train health and nutrition staffs on CMAM and MIYCN to ensure the nutrition serveis is provided timely. Rapid response team will be deployed to hard-to-reach area to ensure nutrition services is accessible and vulnerable groups have got timely treatment subsequently mortality and morbidity associated with malnutrition will be reduced, Integration of nutrition activities with other sectors will be also implemented that includes Integration of health and nutrition education messages at triage conduct Malaria Test and treatment , promotion of hygiene and sanitation provision of training on GBV and CP for nutrition staffs and conduct kitchen garden and cooking demonstration to ensure dietary diversification of children 6-234 months are optimized .
