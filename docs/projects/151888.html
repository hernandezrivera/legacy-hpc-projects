Project Summary

The project aims to provide life-saving WASH assistance to (20,000) (female 8000 12000 male including 4000 children) migrants and refugees where humanitarian WASH assistance is needed (such as Zwara, Azzawya, Tripoli). The project will also preposition essential WASH supplies, sufficient to respond to some 20,000 people in case of emerging humanitarian WASH needs across the country in a timely manner.

Needs

In 2018, a total of 267,000 people including 106,000 children have been identified in need of humanitarian WASH assistance. As a result of overcrowding, all government-run detention centres for refugees and migrants need to improve water, sanitation, garbage collection and hygiene situation, especially those hosting persons with special needs, children and women. Assessments at affected detention centres in/around Tripoli during the hostilities of August-September 2018 revealed that at times there was statistically only one latrine and shower per 200 individuals, and that sewage tanks were insufficient for the needs, leading to overflooding of the holding cells, leading to alarming and life-threatening sanitary conditions for the close to 7,000 detained refugees and migrants. As it concerns urban refugees and migrants, about a third of the refugees and migrants who responded to a late-2018 assessment noted that they lack access to sufficient drinking water, highlighting the need for dedicated outreach of WASH interventions to urban refugee and migrant communities.

Activities

The proposed intervention intends to closely work with Department for Combating Illegal Migration (DCIM) under Ministry of Interior in order to address following major gaps and provide minor rehabilitation of BASIC WASH facilities which need immediate assistance: Following are the proposed general activities for achieving the project objectives (these are only proposed activities and they will be specified per each selected center based on initial WASH assessment): • Initial Needs Assessment conducted in selected detention centers in order to identify the needs and set clear priorities for reconstruction/ rehabilitation based on available budget allocation, selection of centers to be supported through minors wash rehabilitation • Minor rehabilitation/Restoration/Construction of the damaged water supply reservoirs and toilette • Provision of the water storage tanks for drinking and sanitation purposes. • Rehabilitating and equipping the existing water pipelines and network. • Restore the existing and install additionally latrines to ensure equity and to meet the sphere standards. • Rehabilitation and/or construction of the hand washing facilities • Restoration of the damaged drainage lines for removing stagnated water to mitigate health risk. • Repair of the septic tanks and sewerage networks including desludging. • Installation of waste collection & disposal points in centers. • Fumigation and spraying campaigns. • Hygiene promotion/ awareness raising activities targeting mixed migrants and detention centers staff. • Capacity building and designing sustainability/ maintenance system involving DCIM HQ department/ DC managers in order to maintain implemented rehabilitation.All the activities and places of intervention will be coordinated with other organisations like UNHCR, IOM and NGOs.
