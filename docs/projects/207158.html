1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Rehabilitation and construction of water points, construction of emergency latrines, family latrines, distribution of sanitation slabs, Wash'em assessment and CLTS promotion, hygiene promotion and capacity building training.Target groups include : crises affected community, Health facilities and internally displaced people in three district of Cabo Delgado province.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Mueda District: Rehabilitation and/or improvement of water supply systems in health facilities; rehabilitation/construction of latrines in health facilities; rehabilitation and/or improvement of medical waste management systems in health facilities; hygiene promotion in affected communities and health facilities and distribution of hygiene kits and Certeza, capacity building training. Maccomia Districts: Rehabilitation and construction of water points, construction of emergency latrines, family latrines, distribution of sanitation slabs, Wash'em assessment and CLTS promotion, hygiene promotion and capacity building training. Quissanga Districts: Rehabilitation and construction of water points, construction of emergency latrines, family latrines, distribution of sanitation slabs, Wash'em assessment and CLTS promotion, hygiene promotion and capacity building training.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Gender and Protection analysis to be conducted / feedback and complaintmechanism to be set-up / FGD and KII to be conducted to adapt WASH infradesign/location and messages sensitization

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

A vulnerability based targeting approach will be used, where vulnerability criteria willbe defined and/or weighted with supported communities through a committeeelected by the community. For all planned locations of implementation, ACF willdeploy a feedback and complaint mechanism (FCM), unless using a partner FCMwhen felt more relevant. ACF International Network Safeguarding Policy is currentlyunder deployment, SEA automatic staff sensitization and charter signature beingalready in place. A gender and protection risks analysis will allow to better understandgender dynamics and protections concerns, for ACF to refine its strategy ofintervention. ACF core targets groups are cries affected displaced people, under 5 years old and pregnant and lactating women - WASH NFI kits will be provided to GAM children.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

A gender and protection risks analysis will allow to better understand genderdynamics and protections concerns, for ACF to refine its strategy of intervention. FGDand KII to be conducted to adapt WASH infra design/location and messagessensitization, including people living with disabilities participating to it. WASH infrastructures would consider easy and friendly access to disabled and elderly people to use the services.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Specific attention to environment impact by nature of the project - IPC at health facilitywith safe waste disposal / respects of SPHERE WASH standards. Also ACF will conduct environment impact assessment (NEAT+) before and during interventions.
