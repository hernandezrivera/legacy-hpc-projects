Project Summary

In response to the critical needs in Northwest Syria, Elaf is undertaking a comprehensive intervention in the water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) sector. Our primary objective is to rehabilitate and enhance existing infrastructure, establish sustainable systems, and provide emergency response services to ensure access to safe water and sanitation facilities for the affected communities. All our activities are aligned with the recommendations of the relevant clusters and aim to improve the overall well-being and resilience of the population.
Infrastructure Rehabilitation:
Water Station Rehabilitation: We are prioritizing the rehabilitation and polarization of two water stations, one in Jaindairis and the other in Harim. These stations serve as vital sources of water for the communities, benefiting approximately 32,000 individuals. The rehabilitation efforts aim to improve the functionality and reliability of these stations, ensuring consistent access to safe water for drinking and other domestic purposes.
Cost Recovery System: In parallel with infrastructure improvements, we are supporting the establishment of a cost recovery system in both locations. This system will enable the communities to cover operational costs associated with water supply services, ensuring the sustainability of the infrastructure interventions. By engaging the community in financing the operational expenses, we are fostering a sense of ownership and accountability, which are essential for long-term success.
Water Network Rehabilitation: Another critical aspect of our intervention involves the rehabilitation of water networks in both communities. This includes the refurbishment of approximately 2.5 kilometers of water pipelines, as well as extensions to facilitate direct access to water sources for an additional 4,300 beneficiaries. By enhancing the infrastructure, we aim to reduce water loss, improve distribution efficiency, and ultimately increase access to safe water for the population.
Sewage Network Rehabilitation: Addressing sanitation challenges is equally crucial for safeguarding public health and environmental sustainability. To this end, we are focusing on rehabilitating approximately 2.2 kilometers of sewage networks, including extensions to eliminate the use of cesspits. This intervention will benefit around 1,600 individuals by providing access to improved sanitation facilities and reducing the risk of waterborne diseases.
Elevated Tank Rehabilitation: Additionally, we are undertaking the rehabilitation of an elevated tank in the Jaindairis community, connecting it to the water network. This upgrade will enhance water storage capacity, improve water pressure, and contribute to the overall reliability of the water supply system, benefiting the local population.
Emergency Response Services:
In recognition of the urgent needs in emergencies, we are providing comprehensive support for water trucking, solid waste management (SWM), and desludging services. This initiative targets 7,600 beneficiaries living in nine camps in the Afrin and Harim districts. Over nine months, we will ensure regular water deliveries, manage solid waste effectively, and undertake desludging activities to maintain hygienic conditions in the camps.
Throughout these interventions, Elaf is committed to collaborating closely with relevant clusters and partners to ensure alignment with sectoral standards and guidelines. We prioritize community participation, engagement, and capacity-building initiatives to foster sustainable solutions and empower local stakeholders. By addressing critical WASH needs comprehensively, we aim to enhance the resilience and well-being of the affected communities in Northwest Syria.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Community Engagement and Participation: Elaf recognizes the importance of involving local communities in decision-making processes and project implementation. Community engagement sessions, participatory assessments, and feedback mechanisms will be integrated into the project to ensure that the interventions are responsive to the needs and preferences of the beneficiaries. Hygiene Promotion and Behavioral Change: Alongside infrastructure improvements, Elaf will conduct extensive hygiene promotion campaigns and behavioral change communication activities. These efforts will focus on educating community members about proper hygiene practices, water conservation, and the importance of sanitation facilities in preventing disease transmission. By fostering positive behavior change, we aim to enhance the overall health and well-being of the population. Capacity Building and Training: Elaf will invest in capacity-building initiatives to strengthen the skills and knowledge of local stakeholders, including community leaders, water management committees, and hygiene promoters. Training sessions on operation and maintenance of water systems, hygiene promotion techniques, and disaster preparedness will empower community members to take ownership of the infrastructure and sustain the benefits over the long term. Monitoring and Evaluation: Rigorous monitoring and evaluation mechanisms will be established to track the progress and impact of the project. Key performance indicators will be regularly monitored to ensure that the interventions are achieving the desired outcomes. Feedback from beneficiaries and stakeholders will be incorporated into adaptive management processes to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of the project. Elaf recognizes three potential risks: security instability, community acceptance, and environmental sustainability. Security instability in the region could hinder project timelines and safe access for implementation teams. We counteract this by coordinating closely with local security entities and employing risk assessment tools to plan safe operations. Community acceptance is crucial for ensuring the interventions are welcomed and sustained; hence, we engage with community leaders and stakeholders from the outset to align our efforts with local norms and expectations. Environmental sustainability is addressed by integrating green technologies and practices, like water-saving fixtures and waste recycling, to mitigate negative environmental impacts. Gender and protection considerations underpin our approach to ensure inclusivity and safety in our WASH interventions. We prioritize the creation of gender-appropriate facilities, recognizing the specific needs of women and girls, thereby enhancing their privacy and security. Special emphasis is placed on training sessions for women, enabling them to lead WASH-related community initiatives. Protection efforts extend to ensuring that facilities are accessible to all
