Project Summary

• The North West and South West Regions of Cameroon have been experiencing instability since October 2016 because of the conflict between the Anglophones and the Cameroon government. This has led to insecurity, and the situation is deteriorating as the days go by. From the OCHA Situation Report No. 12 as of 31th October 2019,“The total level of displacement from NWSW stands at over 700,000, including those displaced as refugees in Nigeria as well as in other regions in Cameroon.”
• Global Community Rescue (GCR) since the beginning of this crisis has been intervening in the sectors of WASH, Child protection, shelter & NFI and livelihood assistants in part of Mezam Division, Donga Mantung Division and now to carry out a project of CPiE activities, in Civil Documentation Assistant to Widikum Subdivision, Momo Division.
• This projects will provide CPiE assistance to the affected population displaced in the North West region of Cameroon. GCR will provide support to issue Birth Certificate to conflict-affected children ( within and after legal declaration deadline) infants, children (Boys and Girls) including People with Disability ( PWD).This will be obtain from our selected communities where the needs are very high ( See “OCHA situation Report No. 12 as of 31th October 2019” ) .
• Since the beginning of humanitarian intervention in the North West Region most Local Non-Governmental Organization ( LNGOs ) and International NGOs have been focusing all their activities in Mezam Division, Ngo-ketunjia Division, Bui Division and other areas where there is access excluding Momo Division on the CPiE activities. There even seem to be duplication as most of the LNGOs and INGOs implement the same activities in the same locations. In Momo Division there are people in needs with some living in the bushes but they have been neglected. They Child protection is an issue, as children no longer attend school, civil documentation. This gives the impression that, we are not reaching the most vulnerable. This project therefore intends to bridge this gap before the situation even gets worst in Momo Division for child protection on civil documentation.
