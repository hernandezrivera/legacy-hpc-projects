Project Summary

Silk Road intervention will support the people in the in Aleppo, Idleb and Ar-raqqa country sides with agricultural food items with many of projects which provide sustainable services for the farmers and the vulnerable people by supporting the productive assets in terms of agricultural and livestock production with providing food items.
In terms of the livestock support projects, a cow farm will be established for the purpose of dairy and meet production and increase the cow flock in the farm. We seek to create dairy value chain which start from the milk till the marketing for the final products in the local market.
Stable with a suitable space in the targeted location will be rented and equipped with all needed items, with specialist veterinarians will be employed to monitor the health of the cows on a daily basis.
Qualified workers will also be employed to serve the cow farm under the supervision of the specialized veterinary medical team. Fodders will also be purchased and prepared to be placed in dedicated rooms within the stable. On the other hand there will be another place to for dairy productions manufacturing, where the total amount of milk will be divided to produce yogurt and cheese this place will be equipped with the needed equipment. The dairy product will be free distributed for the vulnerable people in the targeted communities as two groups of beneficiaries will be targeted during the project period.
The last step of the chain is marketing and selling the final production which is milk, that will happen after finishing the project period and handing the project as Silk Road will continue participating in asset management with the other parties as there will be quality assurance monitors to keep having the same level of quality for the productive asset.
In terms of the agricultural support. We seeks to support potato, lentil, barley, vegetables and olives farmers in Idlib, Aleppo and Ar-Raqqa country sides with agricultural inputs to give them the ability to cultivate their lands that are difficult to invest at the present situation, that due to the high cost of agricultural inputs and damage caused by the lack of balance between production costs and its price in the market, so that a lot of farmers stopped cultivating their lands, that led to lack of the agricultural product in the local market, so we seek to support farmers with most of the agricultural inputs help them get ideal yield. These inputs are seeds, fertilizers ( organic, urea, amino acids and compound fertilizers), land ploughing, pesticides ( Insecticides and fungicides) and yield mechanical harvesting .
Technical support will be provided also through farmers schools establishment, , beside that field visits will be conducted by our technical agricultural expert team to follow up the crops case with the farmers step by step taking into consideration all technical issues related to the plant breeding and pests control. Agricultural brochures also will be distributed to the farmers to raise awareness and guide them to the correct scientific methods which must be applied. Projects will support livelihoods in the targeted areas, where farmers will be supported with the labor for harvesting. That will give considerable number of un employees the chance to work improving their families financial case, those workers will be selected according to specific vulnerability criteria.
Thus, these projects provides integrated services to the farmers so that they are guided, directed, and assisted in all stages during the season to achieve integration in the project and ensure the results.
Furthermore, the IDPs in the targeted locations will receive a rations of the final production by taking a part of the total production from the targeted farmers according to agreement signed with them, those amounts of agricultural products will be distributed for the most affected people in the communities and camps as well.

5 - Implementation of activities

Regarding livestock project- Rental and equipping of cattle- breeding buildings: A building of sufficient space with an external courtyard with a stone wall will be rented where each cow needs.- Rental and equipping dairy production manufacturing: this building will be rented near to the cattle breeding building this building will be provided with the equipment which is needed to produce the dairy products. - Purchase the feeder which needed for cattle breeding.- Purchase the group of vitamins, medicine and vaccinations required for the cattle health: this group of items will be purchased and kept in a special room with in the breeding building to be used at the time. - Manufacturing the milk to produce the dairy production.- Distribution the dairy production in for the vulnerable people: there will be two phases of distribution for two different groups of beneficiaries.- Building the capacity of the local council in the targeted community: this will be achieved by technical training courses for the local council in the targeted location. As the agricultural projects will be implemented as the following. - Distribution of potato seeds for the farmers high quality Hybrid seeds will be distributed to farmers by in-kind distribution modality for seeds.- Distribution the pesticides (insecticides- fungicides) and agricultural fertilizers (Organic fertilizers-urea fertilizers - compound fertilizers - amino acids).- Farmers will be supported also by lands ploughing for two times.- Farmers will be supported with yields harvesting services by hiring daily workers. Specific vulnerability criteria will be applied to choose workers. -A strict contract will be signed with the farmers to give up a portion of the crops they produce for Silk Road Organization to support vulnerable families. - Farmers' awareness will be raised through activities that increase their technical knowledge like field days, brochures distribution and field visits.
