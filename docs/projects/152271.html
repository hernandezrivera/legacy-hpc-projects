Project Summary

The project on NFE will be implemented by PARE in Michika and Madagali LGAs, Adamawa State for a period of 9 months. PARE will work very closely with donor agencies to achieve the overall objectives of the project which include:
Establishing non-formal learning centres and manage teaching & learning of literacy, numeracy, basic science, social studies & psychosocial support (Social and Emotional Learning) in Michika, Madagali and Mubi North LGAs, Adamawa State.
Under this objective, PARE will facilitate the establishment and management of learning centres. This will involve teaching & learning of literacy, numeracy, basic science, social studies & psychosocial support (Social and Emotional Learning) to Returnees’ children. The NFE to be established and managed will cover children from ages 6-12 for a period of 9 months. The expected size of the class is 50 pupils comprising males and females.
To manage literacy, numeracy and life skills of girls and youth through the development of support program that addresses the needs of girls and boys in the communities and Returnees’ children in Michika, Madagali and Mubi North LGAs, Adamawa State.
The second objective deals with the strengthening of literacy, numeracy and life skills of girls and boys ages 13-17 in Yola-South Local Government Area. Program that specifically addresses the needs of girls and boys in host communities. PARE will manage provision of literacy, numeracy and life skills activities at the centres. It is expected that, about 560 to 600 learners will be enrolled in Adolescent Girls Learning Centres (AGLCs) while 280 to 300 made up of male and female youth will be enrolled in Youth Learning Centres (YLCs)
Following the enrollment and literacy, numeracy and life skills training, this will lead to:
i. Enrolment of 6000 learners with improved knowledge and skills accessing education opportunity, formal, alternative or non-formal.
ii. Increased conflict mitigation competencies.
iii. Improved civil society and community engagement in school management.
iv. Increased provision of safe and protective temporary integrated learning centres.
v. Improved quality of instruction to enable learners identify letters, numbers, symbols, read and write.
vi. Increased knowledge and skills on income generating activities
The third objective deals with strengthening Community Coalitions to play leadership role in advancing the concept of NFE and mitigating conflict in the communities in Michika, Madagali and Mubi North LGAs, Adamawa State. PARE will work very closely with CCs to ensure they help in identifying community needs and proffering solution, enhance community awareness, stimulate community involvement, develop community-wide action plans, foster networking and collaboration with CBOs and assist in resource mobilization. This will enable the CCs to play an effective leadership role within their communities.

Activities

1. One day orientation for the PARE StaffOne day orientation workshop will be organized for key project staff and key stakeholders at inception of project implementation to acquaint them with the goals and objectives of the project. The workshop will also serve as a forum for discussion on the type of information that should be maintained by the project as well as familiarization with project reporting format that will be used. In addition, the workshop will discuss and adopt an implementation strategy for the project and clarify roles and responsibilities.2. Advocacy visitsEssential to project implementation at community level is the support and cooperation of community leaders. Thus, advocacy visits in each IDP community will be conducted to target community leaders to inform them about the project and solicit for their cooperation in its implementation.The advocacy activities will likewise be extended to traditional and religious leaders, LGA, Local Government Education Authority etc.3. Sensitization meeting with communityOne of the most important start-up activities will be sensitization meetings with target communities to discuss and identify best strategies for efficient project implementation. These sensitization meetings will be organized focusing on specific objectives. The first sensitization meeting will be organized once advocacy visits have been concluded. Community leaders will be requested to invite all community members for the meeting to inform them about the project, its objectives and strategies.4. Support to Community Coalition (CCs)Another key activity to be undertaken is the formation and strengthening of CCs to enable them provide the required leadership at the community level. Therefore, PARE will among other things provide the CCs with required Secretariat services, facilitate development of their Action Plan, monitor and supervise and support their activities.5. Mapping/survey of Returnees’ SettlementsPARE will conduct pre-enrolment mapping exercise at the IDP settlements to capture the condition and the characteristics of the various communities. This include availability of pupils, physical structures and distance between the location of communities and nearest school etc. On the whole good mapping exercise will identify some of these problems & issues as: social problems, economic, political, educational, geographical and administrative problems.6. Enrolment and RegistrationEnrolment and registration of children into the Learning Centres is one of the activities to be undertaken by PARE. This will include enrolment and registration of children such as adolescent girls ages (13-17), into 15 AGLC, Youth (boys and girls 13-17) into 15 YLCs, girls only (6-12) into 30 GLC and boys & girls (ages 6-12) into 60 NFLCs.7. MonitoringThis is one of the critical activities to be undertaken by PARE. Effective monitoring is central to the successful implementation of the project by enabling documentation and tracking of activities. Thus, routine monitoring will be conducted by the PARE M&E officer and the project manager to ensure collection of qualitative and quantitative data using the monitoring tools.
