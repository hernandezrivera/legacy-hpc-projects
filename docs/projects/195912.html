Project Summary

This project has been designed to respond rapidly to education needs of crisis-affected populations in Tal Abiad district through rehabilitation/reconstruction of deteriorating school facilities and conduction of catch-up/remedial learning programs for out-of-school children.
The project’s activities revolve around the following components:
1stCOMPONENT Rehabilitation of deteriorating school facilities: Through this component, the project will target the 12 schools & more than 15,000 children will be a benefit from the Rehabilitation activities
ONSUR understandas that availability of the above-mentioned equipment and facilities are extremely important in order for schools to provide the safe, protective and inclusive learning environments where students can recover from conflict and fulfill their potentials. Therefore, the project will utilize in order to carry out the following in the 12 schools:
• Repairing separated toilets for girls and boys,
• Repairing and painting classrooms’ walls
• Repairing and painting boundary walls around the schools
• Maintenance of electrical installations
• Installing water tanks
• Repairing/building sanitation and washbasins
2ndCOMPONENT : Targeting 1200 children (600 girls, 600 boys, ~4% CwD, 5-17 years old) in 12 Schools in Tal Abiad district for non formal eduction
The activities are:
1)1200 children (600 girls, 600 boys, ~4% CwD, 5-17 years old) in 12 Schools will supported by cash-transfers"who regularly attend school"
2)Conduct awareness sessions and preventive measures on Cholera to increase awareness among parents and local communities on the importance of education and how to be protected from epidemics
2)Conduction of back to learning (BTL) campaigns:conduct awareness-raising campaigns to sensitize the students’ parents and local communities in the target areas.Campaign messages will be focused on the value of schooling and safety of targeted learning facilities.Moreover, students’ parents will be informed of all the operational, educational and recreational supports that are provided by the project to the targeted schools. As part of the BTL campaigns, parents and local communities will also be sensitized about Cholera outbreak and precautionary sanitary and hygiene measures to prevent infection .
3)Each school will be provided with required Operational Supplies: Recreational kits, Supplies for PSS Activities, Stationary, Supplementary Materials, and Winterization for classrooms heating. Children will undergo pre and post learning assessment to measure their improvement.
4) Conduct structured PSS and Social and Emotional Learning programs via daily sessions using War Child’s “I DEAL”.
5) Promote community engagement by activating the role of PTAs in improving and monitoring the project activities.
6) Conduct Capacity Building programs, to enhance quality of education, benefiting 130 teachers and staff (65 Men, 65 Women).
7) Conduct an in-school integration activities targeting CwD via CwD-inclusion-officers.
All 12 schools will have an established AAP/PSEA reporting channel as per UNICEF Guidelines. The channels will be introduced to children and adults (parents and teachers) to be more familiar with and confident using it.

3 - Needs

Syria is in a relatively protracted humanitarian emergency with little prospect of recovery in the short term. More than ten years of crisis have inflicted immense suffering on the civilian population, who have been subject to massive and systematic violations of international humanitarian and human rights law. More recently, the accelerating economic deterioration and impacts of climate change have increasingly become additional key drivers of needs, compounding vulnerabilities even further. 6.9 million people (97% children, about 7% with disability) need humanitarian education assistance in the whole of Syria with approximately 1.9 million in northwest Syria(HNO 2023 ). With around 4.5 million children have been out of school and 1.6 million at risk of dropping out, the fate of an entire generation is at stake [UNICEF, 2022]. The project target area, Tal Abiad, is a district of Ar-Raqqah Governorate located in northern Syria. The district is home to 120,894 people, including 10,881 [9%] internally displaced persons (IDPs). According to OCHA, more than 82% of this population is in extreme need of humanitarian services as they had been exposed to long-lasting hostilities and multiple displacements [Humanitarian Needs Overview, 2022].

4 - Implementation of activities

This project supports Education Objective1&2and3: Increased safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children and youth through :1- Support the provision of Non-formal education to 1200 children (600 girls, 600 boys, ~4% CwD, 5-17 years old) in 12 Schools in Tal Abiad district.2- Conduct Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns to 1200 children (600 girls, 600 boys, ~4% CwD, 5-17 years old) in 12 Schools in Tal Abiad district.3- Conduct parent-teacher associations (PTA) in 12 Schools in Tal Abiad district.4-Conducting Payment of incentives for 135 teachers and staff (65 Men, 65 Women) in 12 schools.5- Conducting training135 teachers and staff (65 Men, 65 Women) in 12 schools on PSS, educational subjects, and child-protection principles and practices.6- Provision of stationary supplies for 12 schools on monthly basis targeting 1200 children (600 girls, 600boys, ~4% CwD, 5-17 years old).7-Hygiene promotion and hygiene awareness campaign using agreed UNICEF-WHO messages to preventive measures of Cholera to increase awareness among parents and local communities on the importance of education and how to be protected from epidemics.8- Establishing PSEA/Feedback Reporting ChannelsTargeting 12 Schools.9-Conducting MEAL Activities :include monitoring and evaluation activities both operational and educational, to monitor the achievement of project.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1 :Lack of girls' engagement in the NFE activity.Mitigating measures : Consultations & raise awareness with LC and parents and community members on opportunities for education activities suitable for girls.Risk 2 : Gender-based violenceMitigating measures : the activities will be designed in consultation with the students, teachers, PTA members so that the activities will be planned accordingly. The implementation of the activities will be gender-balanced which means the student body is balanced as much as possible boys and girls including CWDs in order to guaranty inclusion among students.Risk 3 :Exclusion of CWDsMitigating measures : the activities will be designed and tailored based on the needs and level of students, there will be activities in the campaigns tailored according to the CWDs needs that enhance their enrollment and integration into the education process, as well as teachers, will be trained about TICC focusing specifically about inclusion of education.
