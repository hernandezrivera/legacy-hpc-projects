Project Summary

Every year, many people die from preventable diseases as a result of inadequate and unsafe water, poor sanitation, and unsafe hygiene practices, which are the main causes of diarrhea and cholera outbreaks in the region. Thousands more suffer from regular bouts of diarrhea or parasitic worm infections that ruin their lives. Women and children are the main victims burdened; they must also endure the indignity, shame, and sickness that result from a lack of hygienic Sanitation. Continued disruptions of livelihoods coupled with outmoded systems resulted poverty level aggravate, which subsequently is impairing their productivity and other coping mechanism.
MARDO, as a local Organization with strong field presence and has valid registration, build a strong working relationship with the local communities over the years. MARDO is also an affiliate member of the Health, Nutrition, WASH, Food Security, Protection and Education Clusters. With these, MARDO enabled to carry out a rapid need assessment on the continued adverse impact of the prolong drought on various livelihood zones in Huddur and Elbarde districts.
Based on this assessment, life-saving Food Security, WASH, Health and Nutrition responses included the most pressing need of these communities. Particularly, the WASH sector needs urgent interventions, as concluded by many key respondents and in many focus group discussions during the assessment. “Water is very essential and without proper WASH intervention, the other sectoral need responses, whether for livelihoods, health, food security or general economic development; may not be released effectively” said one respondent.
In the pastoral and the agro-pastoral areas, the situation was worse in terms of availability and access to water supply for both human and animals. Generally, there was no water for animal in nearby in most areas assessed; some animals were to be trekked for 35-45 KM. The sedentary agro-pastoral villagers are not saved; they trek long distance with their animals too. The few functional shallow-wells found were uncovered with unclean water risk for human consumption; thus, MARDO identified that many shallow-wells in the area require cleaning out and rehabilitation before covering and installing them with hand-pump.
In these areas, poor practice of water and dam managements have caused them lost their traditional coping mechanism, dams require regular maintenance to sustain their lifespan, water quality and quantity and to prevent costly structural failures. Similarly, MARDO identified communal dams require cleaning out and rehabilitation as silt and accumulated sediments affected water quality and decreased the dam capacity by far.
Subsequently, MARDO proposed an appropriate emergency WASH service intervention which will have a diverse positive impact on the targeted drought affected population by rehabilitating and upgrading important WASH infrastructures through Cash for Work program. The proposed infrastructures include; 10 shallow-wells (3 in Elbarde and 7 in Huddur), 6 communal water dams (4 in Huddur and 2 in Elbarde), 6 public latrines (3 in Elbarde and 3 in Huddur), 13 hand-wash facilities (7 in Elbarde and 6 in Huddur) and 6 refuse pits (3 in Elbarde and 3 in Huddur). Out of the 57,000 persons who will most likely be in crisis (IPC 3&4) through December this year (FSNAU-FEWS-NET- Post-Gu-Technical-Release – Sept 2019), 10,000 will directly benefit from this project, where over 20,000 more others will indirectly benefit from the project too.
The project aims to reduce the risk hygiene malpractices hence minimize the outbreak of water borne diseases such as cholera. The project will also improve the community future water supply and their access to safe drinking water. The project will similarly inject a substantial amount of money to the local economy; while through the Cash for Work program beneficiaries will have immediate access to food and other household needs and reduce their debt.

Project Activities

Proper and successful community mobilization and the formation of the project community committees include the opening and start up activities for every new project that MARDO implements and this project will not be an exception. However, the following are the proposed WASH project activities; • Community mobilization and sensitization over the project program, formation of the project community committee and re-identification of the project activity sites • Rehabilitation of four most important communal water dams in the agro-pastoral areas of Elbarde and Huddur Districts• Rehabilitation of 10 shallow wells - the rehabilitation activities include installation of hand-pumps to the uncovered wells (3 in Elbarde and 7 in Huddur district) Bakool region• Construction of hand-wash facilities in 13 health and education centres in Elbarde and Huddur districts • Community sensitization on hand-washing program in 13 health and education facilities in Elbarde and Huddur districts of Bakool region • Construction of fifteen compost pits through CFW along with bimonthly hygiene promotion sessions for the drought affected and the IDPs in Huddur and Elbarde districts• Construction of 6 public toilets 2 at the major water points 2 in the market places and 2 for the IDPs and other poor urbanite and pastoral drop-out settlements

Mitigation Measures

The project has a system that ensures the beneficiaries report their concerns and complains easily through an installed committee that comprises of members of the project staff, our management teams and project community committee that will ensure no barriers between the beneficiaries and the complaint desk. During the selection of the project community committee will be formed from all groups of the community where women, youth and the members the community are all represented and they will include the decision making process throughout the project period. The community and designated officers from MARDO will ensure that no one is harmed during the registration of the project beneficiaries and identification of the project activities sites
