In terms of the geographical characteristics of the area, it is characterized by a great diversity in the nature of its land uses, as a large proportion of its land is irrigated by two irrigation sources, private wells and/or surface irrigation channels,

The cultivation of vegetables in Deir-Ez-Zor depends on the traditional and inherited method of agriculture, which is one of the challenges that agriculture suffers from in the region, in addition to the suffering of farmers from climatic weather changes, lack of water for irrigation, low water level in the Euphrates and low yield. Summer and winter vegetables are grown on the Euphrates river bed with areas ranging from 1 to 5 dunams for each farmer, and most likely the family works and serves the land, and as a result of the high fertilizer prices, the tendency to use organic and municipal fertilizers has begun in a good way, and this gives an indicator to accept the idea of the project and help spread this method of cultivation, given that it has become an urgent need for farmers to secure vegetable requirements from Fertilizers and control materials at simple prices in the first place, and food-safe and environmentally friendly in the second place.
Overall project objective
This project aims mainly to support and ensure the sustainability of the livelihoods of farmers in Deir Ezzor Governorate,
The project will achieve integration by supporting the agricultural and animal sector, as well as opportunities to obtain income and sustainability in generating income with self-sufficiency and ensuring protection and dignity for the beneficiaries.
Accordingly, there will be a significant impact and expansion of the impact of food security
