Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in the North-West Region of Cameroon has over the past eight years affected over 2 million people as per OCHA sitrep! Shelter and NFIs needs have remained a major cause for concern to most IDP’s, Returnees and host communities. In response to the acute Shelter/NFI needs of conflict affected populations of targeted vulnerable communities of this affected region of Cameroon, COHESODEC seeks to significantly respond to the emergency shelter/NFI needs of vulnerable IDP populations in the following targeted communities/localities: Kumbo, Menchum valley, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda I, Bamenda II, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo and Mbengwi localities of the North-West Region of Cameroon in 2024. This targeted response will be aimed at improving on both the Shelter and NFI situation of 5,200 Households of Vulnerable IDPs, returnees and affected host communities with emergency Shelter/NFI’s support.
In implementing this project, COHESODEC will ensure the provision of 1,000 core and essential NFIs, such as mattresses, plastic sheeting, blankets, jerry cans, kitchen sets, and solar lamps that that meet environmental criteria. The project will also be providing 1,000 emergency shelter, emergency shelter materials and kits; including market-based interventions for these items. Also, COHESODEC will be ensuring the provision of 500 shelter support, in the form transitional shelter construction, provision of construction materials including market-based interventions for the items.
Moreover, COHESODEC will ensure the provision rental assistance (either-free rent, cash-for-rent, or subsidy) to some 100 vulnerable I.DP’ households who were forced to flee from their burnt down villages and now squatting in urban areas. Also, the project will be ensuring the training of targeted households and communities on disaster risk reduction and construction related skills and capacities involving the training of 50 people in construction and in disaster risk reduction.
Furthermore, COHESODEC will ensure the effective monitoring and evaluation of project activities during the implementation while ensuring that project M&E Staff constantly and regularly monitor and evaluate project activities as per COHESODEC M&E policy to ensure that the implementation is in conformity with project action plan and according to deadlines and timelines stipulated.
The Project management overhead cost which includes-(personnel support, transportation, logistics, 7/7 free call-in lines set up and operational cost) will constitute 7% of the total project cost which stands at $44,797. Procurement of Shelter/NFI kits to be distributed as usual will be done through a selection process as per COHESODEC procurement policy where the bidding process is advertised, tender files are received, a selection committee is set up to select the best suppliers who meet the procurement criteria in place.
Finally, COHESODEC will ensure that the project promotes gender equality, including across age groups in a bid to achieve SDG 5 which seeks to ensure gender equality. Also, accountability to affected populations will be ensured through the complaints and feedback mechanism using a free 7/7 Call-in lines are effectively put in place to enable beneficiary communities easily channel in their Complaints while also making sure that management gives timely Feedback to beneficiaries. Accountability to affected populations as in all projects will as usual remain a key component in COHESODEC project implementation strategy!

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

With regards to Need Assessments conducted by IOM and that conducted by COHESODEC involving burnt houses in the North-West Region, there are over 148,754 IDP’s, 199,659 Returnees and 58,235 Host and non-displaced population directly affected by the crisis in need of Shelter assistance. According to the assessments, shelter severity needs indicates: 12% extremely inadequate, 48% severely inadequate, 37% moderately inadequate and 4% minor inadequate. Given the above mentioned disturbing situation of the North-West Region, COHESODEC seeks to ensure that the needs of these affected populations are ameliorated upon in 2024. This project will ensure that the needs of men, women, girls and boys are met as the organization hopes to distribute emergency Shelter and Core relief item kits per household for use by the entire household. Moreover, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC shall prioritize persons with disabilities by ensuring that at least 15% of persons living with disabilities are supported with emergency shelter and core relief item kits. Also, during the implementation of this project, we shall use part of the overhead budget cost to set-up and put in place a 7/7 free call-in line for beneficiaries use complaints and feedback mechanism with which to receive and address complaints. Also, in implementing this project, COHESODEC shall ensure that overhead cost which includes-(personnel support, transportation, logistics, 7/7 free call-in lines set up and operational) will constitute 12.4% of the total project cost which stands at $380,548. Procurement of Shelter/NFI items to be distributed as usual will be done through a selection process as per COHESODEC procurement policy where the bidding process is advertised, tender files are received, a selection committee is set up to select the best suppliers who meet the procurement criteria in place. Meanwhile, we shall also sensitize the beneficiary populations before effective distribution on best practices while also pointing out harmful practices that are prone to destroy the environment which they must avoid as they install their shelter kits or use their core and essential NFIs.
