Project Summary

This project has three components:
1-Mercy-USA aims to target 12,870 HH with food assistance by using mixed modalities and There are three categories of beneficiaries:
1) For 9,770 HH monthly unconditional food assistance by using value-based vouchers, each HH will receive a minimum value of USD$30 per month for up to 3 individuals. Each additional HH member will add USD$10 USD to the voucher value for 11 rounds starting from January to December 2022 and 5 rounds from January 2022 till May 2022. According to FSL and CWG guidelines (This is partially secured fund to be implemented till June 2022). These are separate awards and current beneficiaries, so the assistance will be ongoing until their food security status is maintained without deterioration or to have graduation approach towards livelihoods, this is why we have 11 months from 2022 in one award and 5 months in the other award (3 months were covered in 2021), but in both cases the minimum assistance is 8 months out of 12 months as per FSL guidance.
2) For 2500 HH with one-off supplementary food assistance in form of meat share
3) For 600 HH, each HH will receive E-voucher with value 60 USD one time plus top up 20 USD for big families and PLW as supplementary for the holy month of Ramadan. (This is secured fund to be implemented during Ramadan)
The distributed vouchers will be to be redeemed with list of allowed food commodities at the contracted vendor shops.
The proposal is integrated between FSL, Nutrition, and ERL clusters.

4 - Needs

Across Syria, households are now reverting to negative coping mechanisms more frequently than before. This includes child labor and child marriage and the sale of productive assets – all of which increase protection needs and/or reduce households’ capacity for self-sustenance in the future. Across the country food insecurity has grown – with an estimated 12.8 million severely food insecure people, Syria ranked amongst the ten most food-insecure countries globally by mid-2021. In November, the national average price of WFP’s standard reference food basket reached its highest ever recorded level since monitoring started (2013), increasing by five percent between October and November 2021 and reaching SYP 213,223 (USD 85 at the official exchange rate of SYP 2,500/USD). This is the third consecutive month that the national average food basket price breaks its previous all-time high level. The national average price of a standard reference food basket increased by five percent between October and November 2021, reaching SYP 213,223. The national average food basket price has increased by 27 percent in the past six months (May 2021) and by 115 percent in the last year (November 2020) (Figure 1). This is the third consecutive month with record levels for the national average food basket price. The continued fuel shortages across Syria have further contributed to the food basket price increase. (Syria HNO draft 2022)

5 - Implementation of activities

This project will be implemented by Mercy-USA field staff under the direct supervision of Mercy-USA management from the Turkey office Mercy-USA will attend all FSL Cluster meetings in Turkey, submit 5W's, along with regular information and updates about the project, and the region. Efforts will be made to coordinate with any humanitarian actors on the ground to ensure duplication is avoided. Field staff will coordinate with local councils to emphasize neutrality, and adherence to humanitarian principles while ensuring all stakeholders are informed in the area. When possible, temporary staff will be hired from the beneficiary community to maximize efficiency and impact while building local capacities. The regular food assistance component will be provided via monthly value-based vouchers for food with contracted markets in the targeted locations - Beneficiaries will be informed of the contracted markets and redeemed food list. Mercy-USA staff will ensure protection measures are taken to ensure equitable access particularly for women, and young girls . Options for home delivery of the needed food commodities for vouchers which will be redeemed on behalf of vulnerable persons (e.g., PWD who cannot access the markets, elderly, etc.) - Distribution schedule will be determined during the vulnerability assessment process in order to reasonably target the most vulnerable households first. - The program coordinator will oversee the project, compile and submit reports

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks: 1.Heightened visibility for beneficiaries/exposure 2.Cash/voucher overly spent on non-food items, thus negatively impact food security outcomes 3.Cash/voucher not being equally shared/distributed within the HH Mitigation measures 1. Restricted voucher for food 2.Provide e-vouchers for the regular food assistance and cash for the emergency one-off 3.Engage with relevant authorities as appropriate. 4.Vary the payment days 5.Food security assessments to ascertain/inform needs 6.Ensure close monitoring by female aid workers Different CFM channels will be in place such as WhatsApp, hotline, email, phone as well as physically by MEAL team. All complaints will be received, verified and combined by CFM officer and action will be taken accordingly by referral to the respective department and reply to the complainants and close the complaints within specific period based on the categorization and urgency of this complaint


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

SEA Risks: • Overcrowded during the food distributions/ redemption which may cause SEA • Mixed distribution lines for males and females • All distribution staff are males • The distribution is not accessible to all gender groups • No shared clear information about timing and content of distribution • No clear procedure regarding loss of card/voucher • Taxation on the food assistance • No complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) in place Risk mitigation: • Ensure extend distribution days to avoid overcrowding • Separate lines for males and females in all distribution/ redemption process • Ensure gender balance in the hiring process, ideally to be 50% of the team are female • The distribution sites will be chosen to be accessible to all gender groups especially female HH and disables • Clear information about timing, contents, and procedures about loss of cards/vouchers of the distribution will be shared with all beneficiaries beforehand and will be existed in the banners in the distribution sites • Sensitization about the scope of the project to all communities with highlighting “This assistance is Free” and this statement will be printed on the beneficiary cards. • PSEA awareness materials (such as posters and pamphlets) and reporting hotline will be visible to beneficiaries at the distribution site and/or are included in distribution kits. Different CFM channels will be in place such as WhatsApp, hotline, email, phone as well as physically by MEAL team.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

ssumptions: -Markets are functioning well in the targeted locations. - Security Situation is good with no huge displacement -Stable exchange rate with stable prices of food commodities - Cooperation from local councils -Facilitation and acceptance from major regional stakeholders/actors (Turkish Government, OAG groups, etc.) - Timely fund dispersal -No huge spread of COVID-19 in NW Syria Risks: -Markets are not functioning which may affect the voucher redemption -General daily security threats including robbery and looting. - Macro level conflict related threats including aerial bombardment, advancement of hostile actors, shifting alliances, border closure, and Chemical attack. -Danger to staff and beneficiaries including conflict between beneficiaries. - Delays in fund dispersal - Exchange rate fluctuations between USD and TRY and SYP which affects the price in NW Syria -Uncontrolled Spread of COVID-19 pandemic in NW Syria which may cause complete lockdown Mitigation: -Regular price monitoring and markets assessment to ensure this intervention will not cause harm to the local markets - Following Mercy-USA safety and security guidelines and established safety protocols. n the event of major macro level conflict related threats, Mercy-USA will take appropriate action commensurate with the scale of the Threat. This may include: o Suspending operations temporarily Shifting resources and operations to more secure locations
