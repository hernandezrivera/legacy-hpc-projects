Project Summary

The project aims at improving access and quality of Education in the most affected by
conflits areas in Cabo Delgado Province: Pemba, Ibo, Quissanga, Montepuez, Balama,
Namuno; in favor of 26,173 People in need.
The strategy will be developed in the following Objectives and Activities
ob.1 Improve ACCESS:
- Accelerated Classees for out of school children
- Provide learning supply to IDPs and Host communities children.
- Provide supply to teachers and school directors.
- Provide WASH facilities in schools
- Provide Hygiene supply
- Sensitize families and population on BTS themes
Ob. 2: Improve Quality:
- Strengthen the education system for through training for teachers in catching-up
methodologies and pedagogic.
- Reinforce catch-up and remedial courses for children;
- Teachers training in order to strengthen the protection system in school for a better
identification and charge of vulnerable cases, including GVB, abuses and other violations
of children’s rights though: capacity building of teachers, school’s councils’ members and
parents on MHPSS, life skills, PSEA, VAC and GBV
- Provide MHPSS services for vulnerable children.
Coordination will be guarantee by participating at Education Clusters at national and
provincial level.
