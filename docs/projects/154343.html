Needs

1,283,292 Returnee & 69,960 IDPs living in various places in Anbar are suffering from lack of WASH services as follows:1- Poor sanitation2- Lack of safe water (Drinking and domestic ).3- Lack of hygiene awareness4- Diminishing waste collection services These are combined factors posing significant public and environmental health risks.WASH services will contribute to the contraction of water-born and communicable diseases as well as a rise in vector-transmitted diseases. Assessment Sources.1- DTM of IOM (Report October 30, 2018)2- Our organization assessment (Since 2016 our organization has worked in the field of WASH continuously as a partner with UNICEF in Al Anbar governorate and its districts, and we have a good understanding of the reality of WASH services in all regions and what are the people need there).

Activities

1- Coordinate and follow up WASH ongoing services.2- Coordinate with other clusters interventions.3- Establish cooperation mechanism with the concerned Municipalities/service providers’ directorates. 4- Receipt daily people WASH complains and follow up on response with partners and/or concerned local authorities.5- Carry out water quality monitoring (Water stations, water tanks in schools & health centers).6- Carry out needs assessments and gaps surveys and share with WASH cluster7- Response to onset WASH emergencies8- Maintaining the existing WASH facilities (latrines, showers, RO stations, Water tanks, etc) in the targeted regions( public, schools and health centers).9- Ensure that the latrines or showers segregated by gender, or family units that are safe for women, girls, men and boys to use at all times.10- Active disease surveillance and increased vigilance on water quality and sanitation practices during disease outbreaks.11- Coordination with municipalities and local councils to carry out waste collection campaigns or disposal of Water pools in the targeted areas.12- Distribute of washing materials and sterilizers to schools when needed.13- Hygiene promotion and community mobilisation to promote safe hygiene and health-seeking behaviors, and to empower people to take an active role in WASH operations

Priority / Category

High

Protection Risk Analysis

1- Women and children are at particular risk of disease violence and loss of dignity in a context of inadequate, inappropriate, unsafe, and non-segregated sanitation facilities, as well as lack of hygiene and sanitary items.2- For the security situation, sometime it will be very difficult to move between the locations because of close the roads and this may be continued for many days, for this case staff cannot perform activities in these locations as planned.3- The lack of awareness of the IDPs and returnees of the importance of hygiene would expose them to many diseases and epidemics.

Mitigation Measures

1- As mentioned in our activities, we will work to ensure that women and children can use latrines or showers segregated by gender, or family units that are safe for women, girls, men and boys to use at all times, therefore, they can access to WASH facilities without any problems.2- Our organization will recruit the staff from the targeted regions and in the situation of closing the roads will not be a hindrance in the implementation of activities in the timing of the plans.3- As mentioned in our activities, We will work to ensure that people will have a good understanding of safe hygiene and health-seeking behaviors and to empower people to take an active role in WASH operations

How is your project accountable to affected population

Our project accountable to affected population with the principles of (Neutrality, Transparence, Confidentiality and Non-discrimination).The project team will be working to ensure that the beneficiaries will know very well the services which must be provided to them in the project and clarifying the material possibilities of the project Affected people will participants in the result of the assessments, monitoring, and implementation, the project team will review the feedback of the affected people to check if the project activities have a response to their needs.This is done through direct interviews with them and receiving complaints and follow up the results and the project activities performance.
