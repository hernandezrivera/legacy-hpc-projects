Project Summary

The project aims at establishing an iclusive and accessible health environment for Persons with Disabilities, involving community members, service providers and government, through multidisciplinary rehabilitation health teams (technicians), Community Based Rehabilitation and accessible health services, including inclusive and dedicated MHPSS for PwD who prensent an increased risk of problematic mental conditions, especially in conflit context, and a triple burden of disease.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Through this action, AIFO and its partners, will ensure the establishment of and accessible and inclusive health environment through the accessibility of basic services and structures and provision of specific rehabilitation services withinh HC and community for PwD, including the attribution of assistive devices for selected beneficiaries. Selected staff of the HCs involved will firstly receive a general training on disability and inclusion and soonafter, they will be trained on evaluation of disabilities and rehabilitation practices, so to be able to provide specific services to PwD within their area. At the same time, selected community members of Community Health Committee, will be trained on disability and inclusion and then on Community Based Rehabilitation practices so that they can daily follow up with patients prescriptions. The project will also establish mechanism of coordination between the health sector and structures involved and the Community members trained in order to guarantee a propoer and dedicated follow-up of PwD served. 100 PwD targeted within the action will also receive assistive devices according to evaluation and prescriptions. Moreover, an assessment of the HCs and its services will be carried out thorugh a participatory accessibility auditing, which, taking into account the social and urban environment, will set priorities to turn HCs accessible to all, including PwD.Key target groups:• # of PwD residing within the areas of intervention• 100 PwD per District (400 in total) receiving CBR services of which 60% are women and 40% are children• 100 PwD receiving assistive devices of wich 60% are women and 40% are children The action aims also at establishing or improving MHPSS services within communities and focusing on PwD specific assessment and needs.Through the project, 8 Mental Health Technicians, will be trained on MHPSS support to PwD and then allocated to the 4 intervention districts. These technicians will be in charge of training and coaching, along the duration of the project. Local health staff and community members (APEs and other members of the Community Health Commitees) to provide case management and psico-social support to IDPs and host communities affected members, with a specific focus and attention on PwD. In this way, coordination mechanism between Health Units and communities will be established in order to guarantee an olistic and coordinated protection environment related to mental health services and assistance. Key target groups:• Ancuabe: 362 people of which 70% IDP population, 60% women, 15% PwD• Pemba: 271 people of which 70% IDP population, 60% women, 15% PwD• Montepuez: 182 people of which 70% IDP population, 60% women, 15% PwD• Metuge: 235 people of which 70% IDP population, 60% women, 15% PwD

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project is based on a multi-phased approach that goes through the following steps:1. Technical tranings to clinicians, technicians and community members (APEs or ACSs) on inclusion of PwD and rehabilitation services; 2. General trainings on Inclusion and disability to health staff; 3. Establish Community Based rehabilitation support mechanism; 4. Provide Accessibility of HC and services.5. Provide Assistive Devices to selected beneficiaries.6. Assessment phase based on participatory action research strategies;7. Planning and implementation of community based MHPSS services with the support of ground organizations, such as DPOs, women and youth organizations;8. Advanced trainings on MHPSS for mental health technicians with a specific focus on disability and inclusion; 9. Training of community actors on disability and Mental health related issues10. Case management (trhough direct identification or referral) of PWD with MHPSS need, interlinking services and communities

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The action aims at creating an enabling an inclusive protective health environment in the communities of intervention.Before starting all trainings and project activities, AIFO will conduct an accessibility auditing of Helath Structures and services, involving DPOs, community members, Civil Society Organizations and local authorities, to determine their accessibility, according to the perspective and context of the community, and propose ja ointly agreed set of priorities to turn the structure and services accessible to all.This exercise, will contribute to reduce potential risks of exclusion from protection mechanisms and of harm will be identified and duly addressed. Community sensitizations sessions carried out by trained community members will encourage meaningful access to assistance and protection. The active partnership with CSOs and DPOS will guarantee the active participation of people with disabilities in the action.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

AIFO aligns to the IASC Gender with Age Marker and acknowledges that the impact of the crisis is different for women and men, depending also on age, education level, location, disability and other factors. The action is addressed to contribute overcoming health protection risks within communities, with a specific focus on women and girls with disabilities. These groups are the most vulnerable groups, because they face double discrimination: the first related to gender and the second one related to disability. As a consequence, women and children with disabilities are more at risk of direct violence, and they are more excluded from basic services, such as health, education and MHPS (Mental Health and Psycho-Social Support), also because services are poorly adapted to their specific needs. The action will put emphasis on gender and gender/disability specific needs and vulnerability, addressing them with a specific attention in the content of training, sensitization and awareness activities, both at community and government level. The disability focus will be crossed also with an attention to different ages, taking into account the specificity of women with disabilities’ vulnerability according to their age. Therefore, accessibility assessment will take into account specific gender, disability and age groups’ needs to remove the identified barriers.The gender/disability focus will then take into account also the specificity of vulnerability according to age.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Target beneficiaries of this action are precisely PwD, who are particularly vulnerable among communities, living ostracized and excluded from social life. They are often excluded from information and health services and not provided with the support to help them develop to their full capacity. The action will take into account PwD specific needs concerning the accessibility to health services ans care and will directly benefit them by contributing at creating an inclusive and accessible community and health environment.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in the respect of AIFO Environmental and Sustainability Policy, included in AIFO Manual of Operational Policies- Code of Ethics and Conduct 2020. According to the essential points of AIFO Environmental Policy, an environmental assessment will take place and it will consider also gender aspects: women living in rural areas are often more vulnerable to environmental degradation due to inequality in access to land.The project will tackle community preparedness to natural disasters, as the geographic area is exposed to catastrophic natural events, as part of the Disaster Risk Reduction framework. Within this framework, an environment assessment will be conducted with SDAE (District Services of Economic Affairs) for the IGAs that should have minimum environmental impact, guaranteed through the inclusion of this specific criterion for determining grant awards.
