Project Summary

This intervention will benefit the population of up to 68,427 across several rural communities including the three locations that were already supported in the first phase; Upper Khuneiz, Tal El Samen Dahham and Abarra for which the assistance will continue to be provided by distribution fully equipped of water tanks. Additionally, four other locations in Ar-Raqqa governorate will benefit from the services provided under this intervention along with 11,407 households residing in Ar-Raqqa city. Direct beneficiaries will be 11,407 households, which make-up the total population of the targeted rural communities and partially of the Raqqa city in addition to 3 other locations Jalaa, Hfeiret Elsoqur and Alrawda. The intervention will increase access to integrated health and address the root cause of waterborne illnesses by mitigating water contamination, in addition to improving public health through environment friendly solid-waste.
Indirect beneficiaries will include the surrounding areas who will benefit from the overall increase in health services and solid waste management which amounts to 81,000 persons.

4 - Needs

In several communities across Ar-Raqqa sub-district. Due to the lack of water tank maintenance and damage sustained during the war on Daesh, most household water tanks in the area are damaged, resulting in wide-spread contamination of potable water. The lack of clean water is causing multiple water-borne diseases. 3.1. Supply and distribution of household water storage tanks: 2,000 household water tanks will be provided and installed. Contractors and plumbers will be deployed and supervised by Bahar’s WASH team to ensure proper installation. All pipes and accessories will be provided to ensure relate water supply. Selection criteria for households will be:- Households living without water tanks using unsafe storage utensils.- Households with 3 children under 5 years old.- Households with 8 family members or more. 3.2. Provision and distribution household water purifier units: 2,000 household “Gravity Filtration System 2-liter size” will be provided. Contractors and plumbers will be deployed and supervised by Bahar’s WASH team to ensure proper installation.Solid waste management by renting trash collection trucks, waste collectors, waste removal, and proper disposal of solid waste contamination: In Raqqa, to reduce diseases caused by solid waste contamination, transitional waste management plans will be developed for ownership by the communities. 4.1. Supply of waste management equipment and safety tools: rent of 25 trash collection trucks, pickups and tractors with trailers, support collection and disposal of solid waste through cash for work (CfW) scheme for managed by Bahar, 100 workers will be allocated for Ar-Raqqa city, Alrawda, Jalaa and Hfeiret Elsoqur. 4.2. Identify and develop proper solid waste dump areas and medical waste management system: In coordination with community leaders, develop municipal waste management plan for safe and environmentally appropriate disposal to improve overall health of residents.

5 - Implementation of activities

1-Provision of clean potable water to 2,000 households in targeted communities across Ar-Raqqa sub-district. 2-Solid waste management by renting trash collection trucks, waste collectors, and proper disposal of solid and medical waste contamination. 3-Implementation of hygiene campaigns.
