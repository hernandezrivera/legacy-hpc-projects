Project Summary

Saed plans to implement a set of coordinated and integrated SNFI , WASH, , and CCCM interventions to o bridge the gap for 78,605 beneficiaries (15818 men, 17231 women, 20241 boys, 25315 girls including 1101 PWDs) in 34 IDPs sites closely to each other in Dana and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts within a limited geographical space, where gaps were determined based on multi-sectoral needs assessment that Saed conducted at 2021 considering the current and previous efforts and interventions done by other humanitarian partners in proposal operation area.
SNFI:
upgrade 8 IDP sites and ensure safe and dignified living conditions for the most vulnerable people in Idleb - Harem district - Dand sub district , with a focus on appropriate preventive measures to alleviate the effects of flooding and prepare for winter, In addition to contributing to protecting the most vulnerable groups from the consequences of the winter season for 2022 and 2023.
Through SNFI component Saed suggests implementing:
1- Conducting improvements to the infrastructure in 8 camps, reported flooded last winter, in Dana through establishment of closed and open rain drainage systems, rehabilitation of roads and pathways inside camps; where Saed has conducted topographical, hydrological, and geotechnical studies for these camps and designed an integrated, and inclusive interventions to drain all rain water in the area through which targeted camps.
Activities will be implemented within a timeframe of maximum 6 months before 1 November 2022. Total beneficiaries from this component is: 4000 families ( 23200 IDPs , 42350 men, 4760 women, 7045 boys, 7045 girls)
2- winterization : winter cash top-up( one -off) for 2000 family During November 2022 in order to enhance the resilience of the residents of the 5 camps during the winter of 2022-2023 .
3- shelter repair for 966 families (20% from total families ) during six months in 2023. The beneficiaries of this activity will be determined through a realistic reassessment at the beginning of 2023 and through clear criteria for shelter units that need repair.
The proposed interventions (temporary and sustainable) in the shelter component will contribute to protecting 4000 displaced families in 8 camps ( 23200 IDPs , 4350 men, 4760 women, 7045 boys, 7045 girls) from the consequences of the winters of 2022-2023 and the winter of 2023-2024, and these goals are part of the general objectives of the multi-sectoral proposal, which is to preserve the lives and dignity of the displaced in camps Northwest Syria (Idlib)

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2022:Despite an improved security environment, the deteriorating economic situation, compounded with the implications of COVID-19, has led to further intensification of the severity of need for NFI support. As such, the overall NFI PiN has increased by 5 per cent in 2022 to 4.91 million.According to the maps of severity of need published by the SNFI custer , sub district Al-Dana is considered one of the most in need of assistance. In the middle of 2021 Saed has conducted a multisectoral needs assessment for two geographic locations including 21 nearby camps in Dana and 16 in Maaret Tamsrin Sub-districts.Based on the previous procedures, 14 camps in Dana found suffering gaps in one or more sectors of Shelter, WASH and CCCM.After determining targeted camps, Saed had made another round of field needs assessment to determine IDPs needs and determined interventions based on sectors with wide participation of all age and gender groups including PWDs through the following:- Field survey for all camps, and conducting sessions with CM- Conducting FGDs with 7 groups representing populations divided as per age and gender including women, children, elderlies and PWDs.SNFI Need assesment results:- 10 camps need infrastructure interventions: establishment of drainage system, road rehabilitation using gravels, and 1050 tents in need for ground insulation. -20% of shelter units need to repairs.- Most of the IDPs in these camps do not have access to heating fuel.
