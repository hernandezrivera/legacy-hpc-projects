Project Summary

Since 2018, Mopti region, especially Bankass, Koro and Bandiagara circles, are suffering a significant security deterioration. Inter-community conflict has quickly led to a complex humanitarian situation characterized by a weak humanitarian actors presence, mainly health and nutrition workers. Following this sudden deterioration, Première Urgence Internationale carried out field assessments (December 2018 and April 2019) which revealed a health coverage decrease in Bankass circle, mostly due to the insecure and economic degraded situation.
As a result, in May 2019, PUI began an intervention to restore access to health care for populations severely affected by the conflicts. Thanks to OFDA funding from September 2019, PUI was able to provide support to the populations in Bankass Circle by supporting 10 health areas (project ends in August 2020). Its current success has allowed the provision of emergency health care to vulnerable populations affected by the conflict. It also helped strengthening the failing health system so as to restore primary health care access for local populations, especially the most vulnerable (children under 5 years old, pregnant and lactating women). PUI also worked to build health workers and ASACO members capacities. At the same time, close collaboration with local health authorities helped to strengthen monitoring capacity.
Aware that the security situation is worsening day after day, but wanting to avoid an emergency assistance that could further weaken the health system, PUI has developed an exit strategy with the management staff (Circle level) from the project conception.
So as to continue the efforts already made to improve health care access of a community still suffering from the ongoing conflict, Première Urgence Internationale wishes to propose a second phase of this intervention thanks to USAID/OFDA support. This project aims at strengthening healthcare access and reviving the health system in order to ensure the permanence of health coverage in Bankass circle. This project also plans to strengthen the health system actors capacities (ASACO, health workers, community) with a focus on a progressive financing, monitoring and supervision empowerment, when basic requirements will be in place (level of competence, human and financial resources, infrastructure, procedures).
