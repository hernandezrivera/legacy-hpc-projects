Project Summary

According to the 2023 Humanitarian Needs Overview, the overall NFI PiN has increased by 15 percent to 5.7 million. Unaffordability is the main driver of need. In 2023, the estimated number of people requiring NFI support has increased by 15 percent to 5.7 million. With funding shortfalls currently at a historic low, the NFI sectors’ limited response in 2023 is further expected to exacerbate needs as 2024 unfolds.
Before the crisis in 2011, the city of Deir ez-Zor was inhabited by more than two million people, while now no more than 300,000 people have returned to it, and the reason they are returning is that their homes are destroyed, and some of Their homes need restoration.
is project is designed to improve Syrian families living in poor livelihood conditions, focusing on those living in their damaged apartments facing harsh weather circumstances without any protection and privacy.
In Deir Ezzor governorate, more than 135,000 people are in need of shelter (Shelter HNO2023). In addition, SYRIA AL YAMAMA identifies a high need for the rehabilitation of damaged houses in the neighborhoods of Deir Ezzor

Syria continues to face a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian and protection emergencies.
