Project Summary

The project will support 7,540 HHs, 45,239 individuals to restore income-generating assets & increase bread and agricultural production, and improve economic conditions and labor market opportunities.
Bahar intervention will improve the wheat/bread production capacity of 10 bakeries and one mill in the targeted locations, and purchasing power, where 800 MT of wheat will be purchased from 400 farmers in planting season 2022-2023 and will divide into two parts, the first: 171.2 MT will be sieved into 160 MT of pure wheat (93%) and 11.2 MT impurities (7%). The 160 MT pure wheat quantity will be allocated as wheat seeds and distributed to another 800 farmers for next season 2023-2024. The second part, 628.8 tons will be
milled into 503.04 tons of flour with 80% rate extraction and will be supplied to the rehabilitated bakeries. Consequently, approximately 710,000 bread bundles with a weight of 800 gr will distribute for 29,579 IDPs individuals free of charge for 3 months duration/78 distributing days based on the daily survival needs (250 g
bread/person/day). On the other hand, 1000 new farmers will benefit from 1- 600 wheat farmers from the distribution of wheat inputs (fertilizers, pests control materials), including irrigation and harvesting costs, 2- 400 vegetables farmers from the distribution of vegetables inputs (seeds, fertilizers, pests control materials), including establishment of 50 greenhouses, accompanied with capacity building related to Crops integrated management through How-to-Do approach. Moreover, 250 CFW (200 semi-skilled, 50 un-skilled) will implement activities related to bakeries and mills rehabilitation/maintenance works, bread distribution, berms, earthen barriers, irrigation canals, pumps, gates rehabilitation actions, existed greenhouses maintenance, existed eggs hatcheries maintenance and DRR including fire and vole pest-fighting.

3 - Needs

10 KM of irrigation canals, including berms, earthen barriers, maintenance of pumps and gates in urgent need to be rehabilitated to enhance the access of farmers to the irrigation water sources. Meanwhile, 15 bakeries assessed based on the owner of the bakery, status of the bakery (open or closed), production capacity, available cost and cost recovery. The overall situation was: 80 % need rehabilitation intervention. The average cost is approximately 7500 $ per bakery. whereas the current productivity reaches 36% of the full capacity. On the other hand, a wheat/vegetables value chain assessment was conducted to get a better understanding of the respective domestic agricultural value and supply chains and the challenges which inparticular smallholders are facing along the chain. The main findings were: Wheat and vegetables account for the bulk of Agri activity, Namely 75% Agricultural is the main source of income, 80% Securing reliable seeds represents a major challenge. Cultivation inputs cost doesn't affordable for the majority of farmers. Fires during the harvesting as a challenge, ensure the existence of firefighting teams. The average tenure is 10 dunums per farmer. Irrigation support be critical within the current climate change conditions. Cash vouchers are preferable for harvesting and irrigation costs, while In-kind support preferable for inputs. A lot of Agri-production infrastructure such as poultry equipment and greenhouses in need to be rehabilitated/maintained

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although all Syrian population has been heavily affected by the current crisis, due to traditional local norms, women are exposed to additional protection-related risks. All types of violence are cited as occurring in opposition areas of Syria, with domestic violence, sexual exploitation, and child marriage as the top three. While sexual exploitation is somehow new, child marriage and domestic violence are oftennormalized and ingrained in the culture. Women and girls are exposed to structural and societal risks as the lack of safe spaces and public transportation, limited freedom of movement, poverty, unemployment, displacement, the stress associated with war, gender role change, lack of documents. All these pose enormous pressure, men feeling they failed in protecting and securing a good life for their families. Withmen perceiving women as a threat to their control due to gender roles change, domestic violence has increased in Syria. Whereas men's actions are explained as a result of stress, women are often blamed for provoking the violence or allowing it to happen. The planned intervention will mainstream protection principles throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls,therefore before starting the activities. Bahar will provide a package of training for field staff including GBV guiding principles, child protection minimum standards, referral pathway, and protection mainstreaming


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The following elements will be taken into account in all project activities: Bahar will prioritize safety & dignity and avoid causing harm: Bahar will prevent and minimize as much as possible any unintended negative effects of our intervention which can increase people's vulnerability to both physical and psychosocial risks. Ensure that women and children are consulted in regard to the design and implementation of this project (throughout the process) - The beneficiaries will be informed about the dates, times, and locations of planned distributions well ahead of time. It will be ensured that women-headed households and unaccompanied and separated children are given ration cards in their own names and that they receive distributions of the inputs as necessary, in a way that does not cause further separation. During the distribution, Bahar will set up separate waiting and entry lines at registrations, distributions, and fairs so that vulnerable individuals such as pregnant women, people with infants,unaccompanied children, and the elderly, sick, and those with disabilities are helped first. It will be ensured that there is always female staff assigned to work with communities. It will be ensured that all staff including those working in the distribution of the inputs have signed up to and been trained in a code of conduct or other policy which covers child safeguarding. Bahar Will inform child protection actors immediately about observe any protection concerns for children.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Political, and seeking to control international routes, are likely to remain a source of conflict. Therefore, many Syrians will be either refugee or internally displaced. Increased contact and exchange of animals between flocks from different areas will increase the risk of disease transmission and outbreaks.
