10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

- In case the complaint was related to SEA classification we share it with ourtrained field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to theGeneral Management immediately in order to investigate of a case and take therequired action with the employee according to SEA Code of conduct and refer thecase to GBV specialized to follow up with the survivor as well as we report the caseto PSEA Network. We classify under the sensitive complaints about sexualexploitation or abuse (SEA).TAS consider following points to reduce SEA risks:-Explanation of the Code of Conduct for all employees and requesting their signature-Raise employee awareness and zero tolerance policy-Monitoring risks-Coordination with PSEA Network -Activities are carried out in a safe space and accessible environment for everyone, including PwDs-The ability of staff to refer or receive referrals for protection services-Case management services are delivered in a secure, private and confidential space.-As far as possible, there are both male and female staff in the protection teams.-The organization has clear confidential and secure reporting mechanisms-Beneficiaries can see PSEA awareness materials (eg posters and flyers) and a hotline to report any protection activity (eg awareness sessions, community consultations, FGDs).Risk:CM services are not carried out in a safe and confidential space.Mitigation:- Trained PSEA/personnel to handle SEA cases&AR for beneficiaries.
