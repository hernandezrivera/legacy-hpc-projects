Project Summary

This project will target vulnerable children at risk, through the identification and registration of vulnerable children, such as those who have been orphaned, separated from their families, or are at risk of exploitation or abuse. Once identified, vulnerable children will be provided with individualized support, which includes assessments, care plans, referrals, and follow-up under the case management intervention. Case managers will work with children and their families to address the identified needs, such as access to education, healthcare, and withdrawal from child labour, and psychosocial support. Concern will also ensure the creation or upgrade of child-friendly spaces to provide a safe and supportive environment for children to play, learn, and receive psychosocial support, including structured activities and emotional support to children. Concern will also conduct awareness-raising activities in the targeted communities, to raise awareness of child protection issues and to encourage communities to take action to protect children. The topics will include positive parenting, as well as other topics to challenge negative social norms like early marriage and child labour. Through these discussions and sessions, Concern will aim to increase the engagement of the community in identifying and responding to child protection risk. Concern will also work with local partners in the targeted areas, with capacity building plans to enhance their ability to identify and respond to child protection issues in their respective areas of intervention. Concern is currently conducting an identification and assessment exercise to select local partners to work with on protection projects. Until now, the potential partners include Mouvement Social for CP activities and LECORVAW for GBV activities.
In brief, the activities will include child protection case management, focused psychosocial support services targeting children and their caregivers and awareness sessions, campaigns at community level as well as cash assistance such as emergency cash assistance (ECA) to ensure that the targeted populations has the capacity to overcome the faced risks. The project will target 360 children and 540 caregivers in awareness sessions, and 100 children with case management. The targeted population will include vulnerable Lebanese, Syrian and Palestinian refugees, as well as migrant children at risk. For the migrant children, a needs assessment will be conducted to further understand the community needs and the associated risks, to ensure the project responds to them. This would include exploring needs for child friendly spaces, access to education and legal services as well as cash assistance to meet basic needs – including food and non-food relates needs.
Overall, this project, and under the Lebanon ERP priorities, would aim to prevent and respond to child protection risks, promote the well-being of vulnerable children, and ensure that they are able to access essential services and support.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Concern will implement activities including:1- Child protection case management: For children affected by violence, exploitation, abuse, or neglect, Concern will support through a structured and coordinated approach that involves different stakeholders, including CP service providers, social workers, and community members, with a detailed comprehensive case management plan to address the identified needs of the child, from educational to health-related, psychosocial and legal needs. 2- Awareness raising activities: In the aim of increasing community knowledge about children’s rights, and the responsibilities of communities of identifying risks and protecting children, Concern will provide awareness sessions with caregivers and community members. The topics will include children’s rights, positive parenting and identification of child abuse.3- Emergency Cash Assistance: This will be provided as a one-time off assistance to ensure immediate risk is alleviated and that the child’s basic needs are provided, including food, shelter, clothing, and healthcare, and in the aim of preventing children from being exploited or abused by reducing the economic pressures that families may face during crises.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Concern worldwide has its own policies for child safeguarding and Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse, and Harassment, as well as the code of conduct. These policies clearly outline Concern’s commitment to preventing and responding to sexual exploitation and abuse, and will be disseminated to all staff, partners, and beneficiaries to ensure everyone is aware of the zero-tolerance approach towards such abuse. Concern will also conduct training and capacity building on PSEA to partner staff and beneficiaries, and engage communities to raise awareness about PSEA. Concern will also ensure that the reporting mechanism are clearly established, especially the Complaints and Response Mechanism (CRM). Concern will also conduct regular monitoring and evaluation activities for the project, to ensure that it is being implemented according to standards.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

As part of the organizational accountability commitments, all project participants will be informed of Concern’s Complaints Response Mechanism (CRM). Concern’s CRM exists to receive complaints and feedback from all project stakeholders including beneficiaries, contractors, and others as relates to the implementation of activities, conduct of Concern’s staff and other issues within Concern’s control. The CRM also contributes to Concern’s M&E and continuous learning. Complaints are received via a dedicated phone line, a complaints box, or in person to Concern field staff. Investigations are carried out where appropriate and all complainants are informed of the outcome. By the end of the programme, there will be evidence that feedback and complaints have been considered within programming, as evidenced by reports, complaints log, and responses given.
