Project Summary

Social Welfare/Caritas Kumbo is an indigenous, faith based, but all-inclusive Non-Governmental 0rganization who through their interventions seek to provide community based solutions to development and humanitarians needs of individuals and rural communities especially the vulnerable and the underprivileged.
It has as mission "Bringing God's Love and Restoring Hope for the less privileged" and vision "Enhancing sustainable community-based solutions for all".
Situated in the context of the ongoing socio-political crisis, the population of these two divisions continue to remain vulnerable with children and women affected most. Sexual and Gender Based Violence, recruitment and use of children, separation or loss of contact with family or primary caregivers, other worse forms of violence and abuse of children, deprivation of children from basic rights and protection services continue to remain a call for concern in most communities within Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions.
According to the MSNA report 2024, shelter, protection (case management), NFIs, livelihood support, WASH, health services, education, access to food and farming tools remain top priority needs in Bui and Donga-Mantung divisions. Caritas Kumbo seeks to through this project contribute in addressing the assessed needs through the following interventions;
- Provision of Mental Health and Psychosocial Support to conflict affected children through establishment, equipping and running of 10 child friendly spaces and reaching out to at least 2500 conflict affected children with MHPSS services.
- Improve and expand community-based child protection services by carrying out refresher trainings of p
07 existing CBCPMs and establishment and training of 13 additional CBCPM mechanisms with more focus on communities identified as extremely affected by the conflict.
- Carryout comprehensive case management for 100 UASC through foster care and family reunification, including children, victims of other serious forms violence and abuse.
- Create and support 10 adolescent clubs and provide life skills training enabling at least 200 adolescent boys and girls to adapt and cope in conflict situations.
- Support 50 vulnerable adolescent boys and girls to enroll in vocational training centers to learn trades and improve their access to resources thus reducing vulnerability.
- Prevent, mitigate and respond to GBV risks through strengthening community referral systems (GBV Focal Points training and support to identify and refer GBV survivors for response). This includes sensitization of at least 30,000 women, men, girls and boys using different media like community radio stations, community-based sensitizations and visibility materials (posters).
- Monitor, report and respond to grave violations against children (at least 50). This will be achieved by identifying, training and supporting Child Protection Monitors to monitor and report grave violations.
- Carryout key interventions for at least 20 children within the first 1000 Golden years like screening and referral for MAM and SAM treatment, vaccinations and nutrition support.
- PSEA risk mitigation and response through sensitization of at least 30,000 persons and expanding the CFM and improving access to safe channels for reporting SEA. Caritas Kumbo will also carry out a refresher training for all staff, PSEA key messaging in all sensitizations and training programs including other projects than child protection as specified in the PSEA policy.
Caritas Kumbo shall while using the capacity built from experience and capacity building programs also avail themselves to any capacity building opprotunities that will further enhance their skills in effective and efficient project implementation.
- Ensure effective and efficient project management through timely reporting at all levels including 5W, PSEA reporting, monotoring and reporting of grave violations and use of referral pathways.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

According to the MSNA report 2024, shelter, protection (case management), NFIs, livelihood support, WASH, health services, education, access to food and farming tools remain top priority needs in Bui and Donga-Mantung divisions. Caritas Kumbo through this project will focus on the following assessed needs: - Protection: Children affected by armed conflict face heightened risks of violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect. They most often than note lack safe spaces and mechanisms to report or escape harmful situations. Activities will address these risks by establishing CFSs, providing MHPSS and strengthening community-based protection mechanisms to ensure children's safety and well-being. - Non-Food Items (NFIs): Displaced and conflict affected persons lack essential items including clothing, beddings, hygiene kits to maintain good living standards and protect children from harsh conditions. Through comprehensive managment procedures, individual needs established and NFIs provided where and when there is need. - Livelihood. Studies have shown that families struggling with instability due to conflict are unable to provide for their children's basic needs, increasing their risks of child labor and exploitation including risk of SGBV for especially girls. Activities will include vocational training for vulnerable adolescents, support for income generating activities/livelihood support to some families of vulnerable children to empower families to meet the needs of their children and reduce their reliance on harmful coping mechanisms and exposure for other forms of violence and abuse. - Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH)Children in conflict zones face poor access to clean water and sanitation facilities, exposing them to water and hygiene related diseases and risks including poor menstrual hygiene management and school absenteeism. Activities will include provision of basic wash facilities like handwashing buckets and soap to CFSs, Menstrual Hygiene Management kits to vulnerable adolescent girls alongside awareness raising campaigns to promote healthy practices amongst children and caregivers. - EducationThough schools have resumed effectively in some parts of the conflict zones, thousands of children still face severe disruptions to their education due to displacement, destruction of schools, lack of resources and insecurity. The project through comprehensive case management of vulnerable children provide baisc learning kits and educational support to improve access of the children to education. Learning programs will also be incoporated in the CFSs to provide opportunities to out of school children accessing MHPSS in the CFSs to have access to some minimal learning opprtunities. - Health careChildren in most affected zones suffer from limited access to essential healthcare services, including immunization, malnutrition treatment and other preventable diseases and injuries including mental health challenges for both children and caregivers. Project activities will include improving access to primary health care services tailored to meet children's needs through identification and referral to SAM treatment centers and vaccination centers. MHPSS will also be provided to caregivers of children identified with SAM other nutritional related health issues. Priority will also be given to providing health education to caregiver to enhance prevention care. Other forms of health assistance will also be provided to UASC and other extemely vulnerable children need health care. This will include support with medical bills and maternity care (ANC, delivery bills and related health bills) for children survivors of sexual violence who got pregnant while being abused.
