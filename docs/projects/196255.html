Project Summary

Human Appeal (HA) in line with Health Sector strategic objectives proposes to support and sustain the functionality of three healthcare facilities one maternity and pediatric hospital, one primary health care center, and one mobile clinic in Harim district( Dana sub district) for 12 months, namely:
• ‘’Iman’’ Sarmada Maternal and pediatric hospital in Sarmada/ Dana sub-district/ Harim district/ Idleb governorate (HeRAMS identifier code: G0703086).
• Mashhad Ruhin PHC in Mashhad Ruhin/ Dana sub-district/ Harim district/ Idleb governorate (HeRAMS identifier code: G0703160).
• Human Appeal Mobile Clinic in Sarmada IDPs camps/ Dana sub-district/ Harim district/ Idleb governorate (HeRAMS identifier code: G0703203).
HA support will include provision of technical support, training, facilities’ staff incentives, pharmaceuticals, medical and lab consumables, rehabilitation costs, electricity, and other operational costs needed to ensure smooth implementation of the proposed activities.
The above facilities will continue providing primary (EHSP) and secondary healthcare services for people in need within catchment areas as follows: RH services ( ANC, PNC, family planning, treatment of STI…etc.). Child health consultations. General medicine consultations. Nutrition services for children and pregnant & lactating women. Community health awareness services in all three facilities. Provision of essential medicines. Referrals. CEmONC services including C section, blood transfusion, as well as in-patient healthcare services for the women of reproductive age and newborns in Iman Hospital. Laboratory services, EPI, trauma and MHPSS services in Iman Hospital and Mashhad Ruhin PHC.
Besides referring to the health cluster/ Turkey hub dashboard health facilities map, and for the purpose of ensuring coordinated and non-duplicating intervention, HA during the design and implementing phase was very keen to coordinate and communicate with the partners and coordination bodies relevant to project including WHO, IHD, SRH TWG, and local authorities.
To ensure integration of COVID-19 and Cholera related preparedness and response measures, Human Appeal proposes to operate COVID-19 triage stations and Oral Rehydration Point ORP at the entry of Iman hospital and Mashhad Ruhin PHC during the project period. The triage stations will screen the visitors ( staff and beneficiaries) who enter the facilities and ensure providing save health services in line with Do no harm principle, whereas the ORP will to treat simple cases only (on oral treatment) and to refer severe cases to CTCs or CTUs.
Through the proposed project, HA will enhance the physical access to the facilities to those most in need, especially elderly people, people living with disabilities ,this will be achieved through implementing some adjustments to the facility to be more accessible through removing architectural barriers, installing grab bars in the toilets, installing handrails to the stairs, establishing ramps at the entrances and doors, having assistive devices, beside transportation for elderly and PwDs.
Monitoring the services delivery and activities implementation through random technical and M&E filed visits using standard checklists and tools, beside conducting satisfaction surveys periodically. Feedback Response Mechanism developed by Human Appeal collected via various designated channels allows for relevant and timely intervention, to respond to the acute needs of the most vulnerable groups of beneficiaries, to adjust its intervention accordingly. As HA has a zero-tolerance toward sexual exploitation, abuse and child abuse, the accountability team will verify the validity of the complaints and reporting it to a committee with verification methods for handling the complaints according to HA (PSEA) Policy. HA also has advanced information management system which produces sex and age disaggregated health data which will be shared with the relevant parties.

3 - Needs

Twelve years into the conflict, the humanitarian situation in Syria remains alarming as violence and displacement continues to have a devastating impact on the lives of millions of people. According to draft HNO 2023, more than 15.3 million of population in Syria are in dire need of life-saving health assistance, of them 2.2 million children U5, 4.2 million women of reproductive age, and more than 700,000 elderly people. Dana sub-district (Harim district) on top of the list across all 26 sub-districts in Idleb governorate in terms of population content with 1.125 million individuals (85% IDPs). According to the HNO data, the severity level across all sectors in Dana between 4-5 out of 6 with more than 80% of its population are in need of some type of humanitarian assistance. The fragile health system in Syria continues to face huge challenges such as COVID-19 pandemic, and recently cholera outbreak which was declared by MOH Syria in Sept 2022 with, 13,059 suspected and 900 confirmed cholera cases including 60 associated deaths as of the 4th of October. Food insecurity and the water crises have also contributed to an increase in water-borne diseases and the deteriorating malnutrition status of children and pregnant and lactating women(WHO Syria). According to draft HNO 2023, SRH and safe delivery, child health, EPI, care for CDs & NCDs; MHPSS; emergency services, referral, and outbreak prevention and control and response measures are major projected needs.

4 - Implementation of activities

Human Appeal will implement the following activities: Recruitment of health facilities staff, provide staff incentives, operational costs. Provide essential primary (according to EHSP) and secondary health care services (C section, in-patient services, neonatal care…etc.) including trauma care, antenatal care, psycho social support, and referral through the project health facilities. Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies, including personal protective equipment (PPE). Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services. Provide routine immunization services to all children <2 years of age. Provide case management for only simple cholera cases through ORPs integrated in the health facilities. Screening of children U5 and pregnant & lactating women for malnutrition in addition to community health awareness services. Provision of PSEA and safeguarding training to all staff. Monitoring the services delivery and activities implementation through random technical and M&E filed visits using standard checklists and tools, conducting satisfaction surveys periodically. Feedback/complaints Response Mechanism collecting Feedbacks/complaints via various designated channels and circulating them to relevant staff to respond on time to the acute needs of the most vulnerable groups. Information management system is in place which produces sex and age disaggregated health data.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Unsafe closure of facilities and protecting data. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High. Mitigation: Human Appeal HA trained the staff on withdrawal and closure procedures, including ways to protect and/or destroy data and information, in addition to adopting standard procedures to ensure electronic data and information are properly protected and backed up. Risk: Women of reproductive age, elderly, and People with Disabilities and other vulnerable groups might face challenges accessing health services. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High. Mitigation: Human Appeal selected the facilities locations to be inside the communities and camps area which enhances access , also provided transportation as needed to elderly people and PwDs beside mobilization of some activities to the communities through outreach team and mobile clinic, trained all the staff on humanitarian principle including engaging with vulnerable groups, kept Analyzing and disaggregate all data by sex, age and disability with analyzing vulnerable groups needs. Risk: COVID-19 may affect staff, beneficiaries, and decrease the access to health services. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High . Mitigation: Human Appeal will provide sufficient supply of Personal Protective Equipment PPE and encourage the vaccination among the staff, as well as monitoring the continuity of services carefully and ensure disrupted services are restored.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Beneficiaries are subject to PSEA risk. Likelihood: High. Impact: High. Mitigation: Human Appeal has PSEA policy describes standard of behavior for organization’s staff, beside Code of Conduct. Human Appeal trained all staff on PSEA and continue to check their understanding on regular basis. Effective recruitment and performance management of all humanitarian staff is in place. Effective and clear complaints mechanisms are available in the health facilities, feedback/complaints box, posters introducing the services including victim assistance and beneficiaries rights, posters showing feedback/complaints channels and enhance the awareness of both the health facility staff and the beneficiaries regarding PSEA with hotline number. Human Appeal to protect from sexual exploitation is keen to hire female health staff such as midwives, doctors and nurses to deal with female patients. Human Appeal will ensure confidential and independent proper investigation and follow up of each reported case, including referrals to specialized GBV and health service providers including MHPSS. The employees who in question are immediately suspended until the investigation concludes, then one the sexual abuse is confirmed immediate dismissal will be applies as per HA policies and Code of Conduct. Provide information to communities and people affected by crisis about the organization, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: Non-renewal of UN cross-border resolution. Likelihood: High. Impact: High. Mitigation: Adopting of OCHA contingency plans including create an alternative body to ensure continuity of fund and entry of goods across borders. Human Appeal has multiple channels of funding in addition to good cross-border access for staff and commodities to Syria through its coordination with local authorities. HA also relays on gifts in kind and better financial management of resources. Risk: Deterioration of security situation puts project staff at risk such as (attack, kidnapping, arrest, Inter fighting among Armed groups, clashes, VBIED, car incidents, threats,…etc.). Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High. Mitigation: Continuous monitoring of the security situation through different indicators and preparing evacuation plans in advance. Maintain good relations with local authorities. Respect local culture and building acceptance with host communities. Training the staff on security topics and first aid. Follow the common and safe roads. Risk: Corruption/fraud/aid diversion. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High. Mitigation: Aid diversion trainings are provided to direct and indirect staff in HA facilities. Segregation of duties in the procurement process. Facilities managers are made aware that interference in program/activities from third parties is not tolerated. Using PTT to pay for vendors and staff.
