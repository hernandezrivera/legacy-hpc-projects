Project Summary

In Lebanon, with the deepening economic crisis collapse in services and impact of the pandemic, child rights’ violations including child marriage, child labor, and violence are increasing drastically with a heightened risk of detrimental physical, emotional, and mental health consequences. Along with the increased distress levels of the caregivers, children are now at further risk of exploitation, abuse, neglect, and distress at the cost of their wellbeing, education.
Aim and strategy of Intervention
Ensure children are protected and have access to mental health and psychosocial support. This includes mental health and psychosocial support, providing safe spaces for girls and boys and case management interventions. We plan to provide female support kits to adolescent girls benefitting from case management services, non-focused PSS, focused PSS, and provision of child protection kits.
• Establishing safe spaces and/or use existing community spaces for children receive psychosocial support and provide them with the opportunity to integrate with other children,
• Conducting case management intervention for children at medium and high-risk cases including the safe referral of GBV risk of children and adults,
• Providing mental health and psychosocial support for children and youth,
• Providing child protection kits which will include educational and recreational materials for children identified via CM and CP activities.
Ensure caregivers have access to psychosocial support, support group sessions to promote for their children’s safety and well-being, and increase community members’ awareness on child rights, SGBV concerns, and IYCF support.
• Conducting caregiver support group sessions to provide them with the needed support and ensure children at risk are being identified and responded to at the safest way possible,
Provide emergency cash assistance to children and households supported through case management and other child protection activities through Haddi Program.
Launch child protection events and initiatives to address and mitigate child protection risks in communities. SC, with the support from our Child Rights Governance team, will conduct 3 main campaigns and/or events to be conducted that will focus on the reintegration of various communities while addressing children’s rights and mitigation measures, where children led activities will also take place so children can voice their concerns and advocate for their rights.
Intervention areas, targets, and segregation:
SC and partner will aim to reach 260 children through case management specialized services. A total of 2250 children are expected to be supported via community based child protection activities across all 3 offices, while 208 children will receive Focused and Specialized Psycho-Social Support, as well as 658 caregivers are expected to participate in support group activities promoting safety and wellbeing of children. SC and partner will aim to refer 1040 individuals to benefit from Haddi cash support that are to be identified via case management services. SC will also aim to lead on 3 initiatives and/or events advocating for children’s rights across all areas of intervention reaching an estimate of 600 individuals. SC will provide Female Support Kits to all girls and adult females participating in our activities. In order to ensure inclusion of children and adults with impairments, SC and partner will aim for at least 15% of total reach to be targeting individuals with impairments.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Provision of integrated child protection case management services:This includes mental health and psychosocial support for children in parallel with specialized case management interventions. While also caregiver support group sessions will be provided. Female support kits will be provided to female children and adolescents, along with provision of child protection kits. Referrals for ECA (Haddi Program) will be taking place in parallel with CM services.Strengthening child protection community -based networks and provision of community-based PSS services:Establishing Safe Spaces and/or use existing community spaces for children of different age groups to participate in activities that promote their psycho-social wellbeing and reintegration within the community, along with launching 3 main initiatives that will include the participation of children and caregivers in advocating for children’s rights.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

At the start of all projects SC undertakes a risk assessment, including a focus on ensuring the protection and safeguarding of children and adults. All staff are trained on PSEA and safeguarding and sign up to SC’s code of conduct & safeguarding policies, with regular refreshers on reporting procedures. SC is committed to mainstreaming diversity and inclusion across all its projects and programs. Gender Equality Markers are completed for all project and use of GBV Minimum Actions at the design stage with a close monitoring and on-the-job coaching at the field level. SC works towards creating gender-sensitive to transformative and inclusive/accessible programming with strong focus on women, girls, and diverse groups' empowerment. SCI's approach includes conducting regular gender and disability analysis, framing the analysis recommendations and outcomes into practical action planning, setting gender equality and social inclusion indicators in the MEAL framework.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

An accessible and inclusive complaints management system provides opportunities for all beneficiaries to give direct feedback, to make complaints about the delivery of program interventions and to request assistance for SC referrals service. All feedback from beneficiaries is logged, investigated, and addressed systematically through an automated online application, aiming at immediate resolution. Monthly CMS reports are issued and shared with SC leadership and teams for immediate corrective actions. Moreover, accountability business cards will be distributed to all beneficiaries. The team informs beneficiaries about SCI complaints and feedback process. In addition, project brochures, include the main information about the project and the eligibility criteria, will be posted as part of the accountability system.
