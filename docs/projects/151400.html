Project Summary

The proposed project will be implemented in Nugaal, Bari and Mudug regions in Puntland. KAALO will provide lifesaving GBV services including medical treatment, psychological support, legal support in 3 GBV stop centers. The organization will scale up both prevention and response services to ensure that GBV survivors have access to multi-sectoral responses. KAALO will use survivor-centered and community-based approaches to cover the needs of victims of GBV with dignity and privacy. The project will also improve the capacity of staff to provide a higher quality of services, strengthen the resilience of Internal Displaced Peoples (IDP) and poorer members of host community through education and awareness. The organization will also distribute dignity Kit to estimated 3000 drought affected peoples in Nugal, Bari and Mudug regions. In addition, 20 staff will be trained on Clinical Management of Rape. At the end of the project, A total of 30,000 affected women, girls, boys and men will have access to integrated multi-sectoral GBV interventions.

Needs

According to FSNAU report in September 2018, about 4.6 million people in Somalia require emergency humanitarian assistance and protection. In addition, an estimated 1.5 million people, 13 percent of the population, are expected to be in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Emergency (IPC Phase 4) through December 2018. The devastating drought combined by the conflict in Puntland has led to increased displacement to major urban centers such as Garowe ,Bossaso and Galkayo districts. According to the UNHCR led Protection and Return Monitoring Network (PRMN) indicate that around 23000 displaced across the country. GBV remains a critical protection concern for women and girls in Puntland and specifically rape accounts 22% and sexual assault which accounts 14% of the total GBV cases(GBV report 2017). In addition, stigma and fear of retaliation contributed to lack of reporting of GBV incidents among women and children. Furthermore, KAALO conducted assessment between 3-4 October 2018. the report indicated that majority of IIDP camps lack of lighting, overcrowding and humanitarian services are limited . in addition, Safety remains weak for Internal Displaced Persons and civilians affected by the drought. Women and girls from IDPs are at risk of GBV incidences. To response , KAALO is proposing to scale up its GBV prevention and response through three GBV on stop centers in three regions in Puntland state of Somalia targeting 30,000 vulnerable groups with equal access to girls, women ,boys and men, KAALO will integrate this project with ongoing livelihood, WASH and education projects.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

limited access to services st target pose risks to international displaced people. Affected individuals may walk long distance in search for services and as consequences they might face incidences like sexual assaults , violence and rape. Perpetrators may intend to violate their human rights due to lack of protection mechanism. KAALO will apply adopt gender dis-aggregated community consultation that will allow women and girls to raise their concern and take appropriate prevention measures to address their specific concerns and prioritize their needs. KAALO will scale up an integrated health and protection services to fill these gaps. KAALO will ensure the capacity of local community and resilience improved. The organization will focus on both prevention/curative services and building local community structure and system that will enable community response to risks and sustain basic services in long run

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Garowe and Bossaso host the largest internal displaced people in Puntland and estimated about 300,000 people. The displacement is driven by drought and conflict. These population live in overcrowded settlement and number are increasing with a limited service available. The main risks reported by partners include sexual assault , violence and rape. To mitigate these risks , KAALO will scale up community based services , provide services inside IDP camps. Safety and security measures will be taken into account by close consultation with women in program design like GBV outreach services to be established close to their settlement. The organization will apply do no harm principles and other humanitarian principles that must be upheld throughout the project period.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The absence of protection mainstreaming in both humanitarian and development sector has increased risk of GBV and in particular, sexual violence remains a critical protection concern for women and girls at target locations. Both state and non state armed men are the largest perpetrator group, (50%) of sexual violence and majority of cases were physical assault, accounting 50%, of the cases. To mitigate the impact of armed conflic and violence , KAALO work with the government and UNCHR in prevention and response to violence and GBV. KAALO is working with UNCHR in provision of legal aid services to refugees and asylum seekers and the provision of GBV services including medical, livelihood and psycho social support
