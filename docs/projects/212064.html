Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to achieving the Strategic Humanitarian Objectives (SO1, SO2, and SO3) in northwest Syria: to 1- Save lives, 2- Enhance protection, and 3- Increase resilience and access to services, under the FSA Cluster objectives:
1- To Scale-up and sustain minimum food and diversified consumption needs.
2- Support the self-reliance of affected households by providing urgent and time-sensitive emergency agriculture inputs.
3- To improve communities’ resilience and capacity to sustain households’ livelihoods.
based on the humanitarian needs assessment conducted by humanitarian partners at the level of WoS and reflected in the HNO 2024.
Al-Bab sub-district was targeted by our project based on the HNO 2024 / FSA Chapter general assessment indicating that People in Need / PiN are (143,541) with a severity category estimated at extreme (4), additional primary needs Assessment was carried out in February 2024, which reached specific communities in Al Bab Sub-district (e.g. Wqah, Olan, Hazwan ...etc.)
The project will prioritize the most vulnerable HHs (stated in WoS strategy / Thresholds 1, 2, and 3) in coordination with FSL Cluster and Al-Bab Local Council and will start in March 2024, all humanitarian deliverables (Baskets, Bundles…etc.) will be standardized according to FSL Cluster technical recommendations, The following breakdown provides a brief about the planned activities:
Objective 1:
Activity 1.2: Provide (300 assessed food-insecure HHs) with monthly food assistance (8 months) through appropriate modalities. (In-kind and Cash)
Activity 1.3: Provide (25 cases of people at risk of malnutrition), children, Pregnant and Lactating Women, Elderly, and Persons with Disabilities with supplementary food assistance through appropriate modalities (complementary to 1.2)
Activity 1.4: Supply (daily bread rations) directly to (500 HHs) for the period of (8 months) through appropriate modalities. (In-kind and Cash)
The "modality" of assistance for (PWDs/PSNs) will be "In-kind" delivered to their doorsteps, The FB "value" is based on approximately 70% of the daily kcal intake as per the FSS guidance, while HHs who have access to the market with no physical disability will be assisted by "cash" transfered directly to their Ptt accounts. The "targeting principle" will address food insecurity at two levels geographical (endorsed by the cluster) and households (based on primary needs assessment). Quality control certificate will be requested from the factory supplying the in-kind food items with site visits conducted periodically.
Objective 2:
Activity 2.1: Distributing of agricultural inputs for (200 Farmers) for crops cultivated in AlBab Sub-district (e.g. Wheat, Barely...etc.) - The inputs will include the core needs of the selected farmers (e.g. seeds, fertilizer, pesticides, and equipment; coupled with related training)
Activity 2.1.A: Providing protection Food Rations (FRs) to (200 Farmers) targeted by the activity 2.1.
Farmers will be assisted by "cash" transfered to their Ptt accounts. the cluster guidance on targeting and prioritization for beneficiaries SO2 will be followed, and the response package will be applied (10-25$/dunum)
Objective 3:
Activity 3.2: Support light rehabilitation and maintenance of (the irrigation network, well, and system) in AlBab Sub-district to support the whole community.
Activity 3.4: Maintain/Strengthen collective productive unit at the community level (food processing, renewable energy) in AlBab Sub-district to support the whole community.
The organization will coordinate with the FSL Cluster and all humanitarian actors to ensure the humanitarian assistance is directed to the most needy people without duplication, the cluster tools (5Ws, meetings, trackers...etc.) will be utilized, and the local councils will be consulted for close on-site coordination.
10% of the project fund is secured so far, and 90% of the fund is planned (expected) to be secured in April 2024

Protection Risk AnalysisProtection Risk/Threat 1: The theft of specific food items (e.g. sugar) from the basket during temporary storage, or delivery to distribution sites, Likelihood: medium, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure: follow strict selection criteria for staff and services providers, enhancing monitoring and control over warehouses and transportation trips, Random check for food baskets contents just before handing over to beneficiaries, and inform beneficiaries about the existing feedback and accountability mechanisms to report any misconduct. Resources: Dedicated staff member and good storage management. Protection Risk/Threat 2: Reselling of food basket by beneficiary to third entity may expose them to risks (e.g. unbalanced bargain, harassment by buyers or drivers) Likelihood: medium, Impact: medium, Mitigating Measure: Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the reasoning behind the sale/exchange of assistance and solicit their feedback with regards to solutions, PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategyPSEA Risk/Threat 2: the attempt of sharing telephone number (by staff member) with female beneficiaries during registration or distribution, Likelihood: low, Impact: high, Mitigating Measures: provide training for SOH field staff, and avoid 1 to 1 connection with beneficiaries (SOH front-end team will consist of 1 male and 1 female at least), and ensure they commit to humanitarian principles and code of conduct signed after recruitment, and distribute brochures with Food baskets including hot-line numbers for reporting PSEA incidents PSEA Risk/Threat 1: beneficiaries sexually harassed or abused in exchange for Food baskets, Likelihood: low, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure: Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the safest mechanism for handing over the food baskets, Monitoring: Enhance communication: (i) distribute brochures with Food baskets including hot-line numbers for reporting PSEA incidents (ii) avoid 1 to 1 connection with beneficiaries (SOH front-end team will consist of 1 male and 1 female at least), and (iii) provide training for SOH field staff, and ensure they commit to humanitarian principles and code of conduct signed after recruitment. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measuresThe project took into consideration the following risks (beyond the project's control): 1- Mass displacement from Idlib to Aleppo based on conflict escalation (SOH contributes actively with local stakeholders during crises and has plan B to shift operation to Turkish/Syrian borderline). 2- Markets may be un-accessible because of insecurity (modalities will be changed if necessary according to local security conditions/challenges). 3- Some humanitarian actors may conduct uncoordinated distribution for households targeted by our project (coordination with the local council and FSL cluster will take place before implementation).
