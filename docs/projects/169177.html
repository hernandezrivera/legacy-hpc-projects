Plan fields

1 - Needs

According to UNICEF, 100,000 children have had their homes damaged and are displaced in Beirut, while 120 schools serving 55,000 children are in various states of damage. World Vision note, if the number of homeless families housed in schools is substantial, the resumption of classes in September might be affected.

2 - Activities

Providing light rehabilitation services to damaged classrooms and toilets in affected Public schools which includes plastering, paint works, fixing damaged windows and doors and toilets seats in addition to lighting and electricity works Providing rehabilitation services to extracurricular facilities in damaged Public schools. the schools to be Rehabiliatated will be decided based on recomendation from the regional education department Beirut. Rehabiliation of scholls will be managed by Plan Intenrational . Distributing educational kits including stationery supplies to affected girls and boysBack-to-school kits include stationery and essential scholastic year requirements to support a smooth return to the public schools following the recent deteriorating economic situation in the country that has been specifically affecting the Education sector schools and learning centres. This would allow affected children to still access learning wherever they are. Distribution of remote education kits, including tablets to affected families. A tablet will be distributed to each family whose child/children is registered in the targeted Public school, for a total of 750 families. Given the severity of the COVID-19 situation in Lebanon and the fact that the number of cases is increasing significantly, the Lebanese Ministry of Education and Higher Education is considering the remote learning option for an extended period of time and maybe even to last for the entirety of the academic year 2020 / 2021. The remote learning kit will include a tablet to be distributed to each benefitting household so the children can make best use of the online learning opportunities and expand their knowledge in terms of technological advances.The objective is to ensure that the students are well equipped to be able to pursue their online Education. 750 tablets will be distributed to households. The distribution of education kits will be done in partnership with the local actor Ana Aqra
