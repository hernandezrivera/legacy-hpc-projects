Project Summary

The Lebanese Council to Resist Violence Against Woman (LECORVAW) is proposing to implement an 11 months project targeting vulnerable women and girls residing the the area of T5.
The purpose of such project is to urgently prevent and respond to the critical GBV and protection risks experienced by the most vulnerable communities in Lebanon especially women and girls through prompt integrated service delivery in case management, legal counselling, PSS (Focused / non Focused) as well as provision of awareness raising activities.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under this project, LECORVAW will provide women and girls in the targeted areas with a GBV package of services through 1 static safe space and 4 mobile safe spaces Under the CLPRO/CA1: Provision of GBV risk mitigation and prevention services (non focused PSS, distribution of dignity kits, information and awareness on services. LECORVAW will apply the GBV risk mitigation tools (safety audits, service mappings, FGDs) as well as providing women and girls with info sessions, GBV & legal awareness sessions, life skills and skills building classes (Non Focused PSS). Under the CLPRO/CA4 :Provision of GBV Response service (focused PSS, case management, legal and cash assistance) LECORVAW will provide focused case management for women and girls GBV survivors as well as focused PSS using tools developed by the International Rescue Committee (Arab Women Speak Out - Positive Parenting, Life Skills Through Drama and Early Marriage Tailored Package) and legal service provision (legal consultations and court representations) On the other hand, LECORVAW will be requesting 1000 dignity kits for women and girls to be used as an entry point during the outreach process, this will also be useful especially during the current economic crisis.(it is worth mentioning that during the distribution process, LECORVAW will provide PSEA awareness sessions for the dignity kits beneficiaries). in addition to that, LECORVAW will provide cash assistance through the case management service.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

During 2020, LECORVAW started developing it's PSEA Policy which is used and incorporated in all aspect the work at the moment. LECORVAW will make sure to provide PSEA awareness raising sessions for all women and girls that will benefit from the services mentioned above as well as share with them the reporting mechanism required for them to make a complaint or to report a PSEA incident. On the other hand, LECORVAW will implement safety audits in all of the areas of implementation and will make sure to share the service mapping with all beneficiaries and refer them to services that they might be needing such as education, health, WASH and livelihood.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

LECORVAW will ensure the project accountability for affected populations by different means. The outreach will be done using the methodology of the "I'm Here approach tool" which targets the most vulnerable population in each area and by applying the community engagement to establish the mobile safe spaces. The activities will be monitored using the SGBV M&E toolkit (Developed by the SGBV task force) which will allow beneficiaries to submit any complaint and cases will be reported on the GBVIMS platform as well as the activity info platform. LECORVAW will also provide PSEA awareness sessions for all the beneficiaries reached through this project and provide them with the feedback means (hotline, complaint boxes)
