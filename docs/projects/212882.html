Project Summary

Water pumping stations in Al-Hasakah Governorate are considered extremely important facilities for achieving stability and ensuring decent livelihoods for residents and farmers. These stations are considered the first step in delivering water to residents for use in drinking and hygiene and providing well water consumption for agricultural purposes, as agriculture in the region has been disrupted due to water shortages.
This intervention will lead to the rehabilitation of a water station in the Makhroum area to provide residents with a reliable and safe water source and provide irrigation water to farmers. This intervention has a main goal, which is to ensure that the pumping station provides a sufficient amount of clean drinking water from the source to the people of the area and surrounding villages for daily use (drinking). . - cleanliness.
Makhroum station supplies 9 villages with drinking water, which are: (Makhroum Al-Shamali - Makhroum Al-Janoubi - Al-Sirb - Ain Al-Hara - Ebb Al-Shouk - Tuwainan Village - Al-Nawfiliya Village - Souda and Abd Village - Al-Hafayer Village), where water is filled through water tanks from the opening of the station, which is now It has stopped working, and the people of the area are suffering from a severe water scarcity due to the station not operating.
According to a source from the region, its population is more than 17,500 people (10,360 of them females and 7,140 males), including 3,755 females under the age of 18 and 5,740 males under the age of 18. It is the only source of water for people with disabilities, whose number is estimated at about 70 people of different ages
