Project Summary

The on-going “Crisis” in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon which breaks out since 2016 /incorporated with multiply event: Cholera outbreak in 2022, flood /landslide disaster in 2023 in the NWSW regions due to climate change, has resulted in increased displacements of people, high risk of hygiene-related morbidity/mortality rates of children <5yrs. 1.0M among the 1.3M PiN of humanitarian assistance in WASH including 638,421 IDPs/442,580 Returnees/PIN-TARGET WASH HNO-HRP2024 of 20 Nov. 2023 is 787,8K will be targeted.
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 6 Subdivisions in 15 villages in rural, urban/semi-urban in 6 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Boyo, Ngo-Ketunjia, Momo, Menchum/Manyu) in the NW Region of Cameroon. Targeted: 15,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (2,556 Boys/2,684 Girls) <18 yrs, (2,374 Men/2,407 Women) > 18 yrs, (1,507 Men/1,657 Women) +60yrs, PWD (889 Men/926 Women)).
AIM:
To improve access to safe drinking water and hygiene related services to the vulnerable populations by 30th June 2024.

ACTIVITIES:
Promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people
Construction of water points ( boreholes, small water supply network,..)
Construction of gender-sensitive latrines in IDP sites / host communities
Distribution of WASH kits to boys, girls, men, women, families, PWD and old people
Capacity-building of WASH actors in WASH relative issues
OBJECTIVES:
-Improve sustainable access to basic sanitation/safe drinking water to vulnerable people
-Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity/mortality of affected population in the crisis
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve WASH services/resilience of affected populations
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 60% IDPs, 10% PWDs/30% Host Communities.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisation/drilling
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through community based approach, awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials including PSEA, collaboration with stakeholders/organised workshops, community mobilization/engagement, open space distribution/homes delivery, construction/M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs/KII/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Engage MINEE/MINSANTE/Municipal Council.
SUSTAINABILITY
Trained (CPCs for continuos sensitisaton on HP; WMCs/supply with tools box; IDPs on HWTS/how to treat/reuse squatting slabs). Jerry cans for storage of water at HHs level/distribution of Aqua tabs. Improve sustainable latrines.
JUSTIFICATION
Increased # of improved (sustainable latrines/water points of boreholes)/hand hygiene practices reduces cholera, diarrhea/deaths

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

NEEDS: 7,060 people living in poverty are in need of quality and sufficient quantity of sustainable clean water for drinking, laundry, preparing food, bath, handwashing with soap (HHWS) for delivery of new-born to reduce maternal/neonatal deaths, cholera and diarrhea including deaths to children <5 years for they collect water from an unimproved water point. Lack of improved sustainable sanitation facility (latrines) to homes result to open defecation which contaminate streams, wells, lake. Lack of water containers to collect and store water and menstrual hygiene management Kit to women and girls for personnal hygiene up keep including hygiene kits to boys and mem. Insufficient soap for handwashing and WASH kits. Need promotion of good wash practices to 15,000 affected population. Lack of training to the affected population on how to report feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors on AAP and PSEA. Lack of trained water management committees (WMCs) to manage their water supply and supplies of tools box with latrines management. Direct beneficiaries are 7,060 and indirect beneficiaries is 7,940. NB: Construction of borehole of underground water scheme is with small solar powered system of 4 taps per borehole. From the Sphere handbook 2018 edition say 1 toilet for 5 persons or 1 family i.e. 12 gender-sensitive household latrine x 5= 60 vulnerable people using latrine.
