Project Summary

Nabolok is implementing a project in camp 26 under Teknaf upazila from October 2018 and it will be continued up to 30 November 2020. On this project have 3 (three) component like WASH, Solar light installation and LPG Distribution. Under this project we have been distributed LPG among Rohingya (2500) and Host community (650) and it have been distributed in camp-23 and Shamlapur union respectively. In addition, an unannounced lock-down is being carried out by the Government of Bangladesh in Cox's Bazar district as well as across the country to curb the spread of the strongly infectious epidemic COVID-19. Since, COVID-19 is a strongly infectious epidemic; the only way to get rid of this disease is to stay at home; avoiding public gatherings as much as possible with maintaining proper personal hygiene. Analysis of data from various health organizations in the country and abroad has shown that older people infected with the COVID-19 have a lower recovery rate due to lower immunity. And so the only goal of our project is not to bring the elderly out of the house but to deliver LPG cylinders to their homes. As a consequence, social distance can be ensured by keeping the elderly at home by preventing them from coming out of the house. Because if they get LPG gas for cooking while sitting at home, then they will not have to come out of the house for this work during this epidemic.
