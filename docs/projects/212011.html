Project Summary

The proposal aims to combat economic disempowerment and food insecurity in Al-Rai town, affected by conflict and natural disasters. It focuses on supporting small and medium-sized enterprises in agriculture, food security, and WASH sectors through training and financial aid to promote recovery and sustainable growth. The strategy includes:
Business Support: Providing in-kind support and financial grants to 17 selected SMEs to help them improve or restart operations.
Capacity Building: Offering training programs for employees and business owners to enhance skills in various areas, including safety, ethics, and management.
Employment Creation: Subsidizing salaries for new hires to encourage employment among marginalized groups, such as women, youth, and IDPs.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This proposal outlines a strategic intervention aimed at addressing the critical challenges of economic disempowerment and food insecurity in Al-Rai town, which have been severely exacerbated by the ongoing conflict, environmental crises, and the recent devastating earthquake in February 2023. Our organization has conducted a baseline assessment and a subsequent feasibility study to identify the most effective approaches to support recovery and sustainable development in the local economy, with a particular focus on micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the agriculture, food security, and WASH sectors, through training and a onetime input or financial support to enable the businesses to restart or improve/augment and then maintain their production.Comprehensive Baseline AssessmentObjective and Methodology: Our baseline assessment was meticulously conducted to map the existing challenges within the targeted sectors. Utilizing a combination of direct surveys, in-depth interviews, and participatory workshops, we engaged with local businesses, community leaders, and residents to gather actionable data. Secondary data was sourced from local NGOs and international development agencies to enrich our findings.Following the baseline assessment, a detailed feasibility study was conducted to analyze the local economic environment, availability of resources, and the potential for sustainable business growth. This study underscored the urgent need for integrated support mechanisms that encompass financial access, skills development, and market linkages.Detailed Project Activities1. Business Support and Development:• Target: 50 SMEs within the agriculture, food security, and WASH sectors.(each business size ranges from 1 to 50 employees), with a primary focus on struggling businesses and those that are new, stable, or experiencing growth in the agriculture, food security, and WASH sectors in Al-Rai city.Modality of Implementation:o Generating a participants long list: Evaluate existing businesses based on criteria including potential, capacities and gaps (needs), gender, business size, and sector.o Formulating the final participants list: Engage in participatory analysis and conduct quantitative and qualitative survey for each business, finalizing the selection.o Signing an MoU with the business owners to define the roles and responsiblities, and to ask them to sign the code of conduct and the PSEA policy, And to define the relation with the empolyess and the future plans after the project is over.o Tailoring improvement plans: Collaborate with each business to develop a personalized three-month improvement plan, specifying detailed support, through mentorship and to focus potentially on business development plan, an operational plan.o In-kind support: Offer tailored assistance to selected businesses based on each business’s requirements, addressing specific needs such as tools, rental fees, and essential resources.
