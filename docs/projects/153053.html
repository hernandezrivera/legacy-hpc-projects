Project Summary

The ASCO WASH project will focus on 2019 WASH objectives and consequently the lifesaving needs to the affected beneficiaries Wau and Aweil South Counties in Western Bahr el Ghazal State as geographical location for response.
ASCO will respond on integrated programming with WASH to help other sectors coping with the unanticipated scale and spread of the cholera outbreak, responding to the protection crisis GBV to provide integrated services for survivors of gender-based violence and dedicated services for women and girls with protection concerns
The key drivers for integrated emergency WASH response in the current context are the IDP population, GAM rates and displacement puts a strain on existing water sources and increases WASH needs such as cholera outbreak and High malnutrition rates are also linked to poor WASH conditions, as exemplified in the diarrhea-malnutrition cycle prevalent in vulnerable populations
WASH will integrate with Nutrition partners to ensure that the OTP and TSFP toilets has water and soap for washing hands after visiting the latrine, children with SAM in the OTP sites provided with WASH kits. Ensure that soap for mothers with children of SAM and MAM cares provided in nutrition sites, Ensure there is no garbage in the nutrition sites and WASH messages provided in the OTPs site by ensuring water for appetite test is treated with aqua tabs. ASCO will ensure availability of soap and water in hand washing stations.
ASCO responding on WASH needs will train nutrition staff to provide WASH messaging at Nutrition sites. Will ensure Nutrition and WASH community volunteers monitor practices related to integrated WASH, Health, FSL and Nutrition messages at household level.
ASCO will ensure the affected beneficiaries male and female access equitable safe water, sanitation, hygiene promotion and prevention of malnutrition through provision of sanitation services to Children admitted for SAM/MAM treatment with a WASH minimum package of activities at household level that are secure and appropriate to IDPs, returnees and acutely vulnerable host communities.
The differential needs, concerns and priorities of women and girls, men and boys of different ages and disparities will be addressed basing on the three main cluster objectives notably: objective one: Provide timely equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs, host communities and returnees Objective two: Mitigate WASH-related related GBV and objective three: WASH in Nutrition for famine prevention minimum package of interventions.
WASH interventions at household and community levels for displaced population and host community in the areas with highest malnutrition rates GAM coupled with highest famine risks on objective three. The areas at highest risk of protections issues within GBV are focusing on objective two and WASH interventions at cholera etc. transmission contexts levels for displaced population and host community in the areas at highest risk, rate of cholera outbreaks and WASH-related diseases focusing on objective one.
Taking into account the WASH lives saving approach to affected beneficiaries IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities coordinating with the ministry of Water relevant clusters for project integration, local authorities, and community leaders in design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of project activities by involving male and female.
Right based approach will be used in selecting targeted 36000 beneficiaries constitute of Women 12000, Girls 9000,Men 7000 and Boys 8000 to undertake different roles and activities of the project. Male and female boys and girls shall be given equal opportunities in project design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation to measure the three WASH cluster objectives.

Needs

The displaced populations are in need of support to access safe water and basic sanitation facilities Wau and Aweil South Counties in Western and Northern Bahr el Ghazal states. The protracted conflict, economic decline, diseases and climatic shock in Northern Bahr el Ghazal State has resulted in multiple displacements in Wau and Aweil South Counties compounding the WASH needs vulnerable population. The remaining coping abilities have been further eroded by prolonged food insecurity, economic collapse and disease outbreaks especially cholera.The widespread lack of safe drinking water, sanitation facilities and poor hygiene practices has left a large proportion of the population at risk of preventable WASH related diseases. IDPs remain among the most vulnerable groups with the most acute and vulnerable populations found in Western and Northern Bahr el Ghazal With limited basic WASH needs. ASCO will provide timely equitable access to safe, water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs/host communities and returnees. WASH needs are also closely linked to Nutrition and health needs. The overall WASH situation is magnified by the endemic nature of cholera and other water borne epidemics in Wau and Aweil South Counties which disproportionately affects children under 5 years. High malnutrition rates are strongly linked to poor WASH conditions exemplified in the diarrhea-malnutrition cycle which is prevalent in vulnerable populations. ASCO will respond to enhance WASH in Nutrition sites, which both serves to keep children in nutrition sites and schools and helps to prevent further outbreaksDisplaced women and girls continue to be disproportionality affected by protection related WASH issues, with GBV occurring due to insufficient and unsafe access to water and sanitation facilities. Wau and Aweil South Counties being the most severely affected, the protracted fighting and cattle raids has adversely affected the communities particularly on water hygiene and sanitation situation with low standard sphere of WASH. Provide latrines with locks and design improvement agreed through focus group discussions conducted by female staff with women and girls to mitigate WASH related GBV risksProject will focus in Wau and Aweil South Counties by providing lifesaving services to IDPs, vulnerable host communities’ needs and response services targeting 36000 beneficiaries’ Women 12000, Men 7000, boys 8000 and girls 9000 as direct beneficiaries.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Access to WASH assistance is allowed since Wau and Aweil South Counties in Western and Northern Bahr el Ghazal states is relatively calm and most agencies operate in this focused county. According to the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission Coordinator, all humanitarian agencies are absolutely allowed to provide humanitarian assistance in the county to the affected and vulnerable population.There are no access constraints in Wau and Aweil South Counties. Currently the Counties is primarily physical in nature. The road network is functional and with regular flights in Wau and Aweil town. The airstrips are in good condition; however it is a significant distance from affected populations and is operational for humanitarian and commercial services. All WASH actors and other humanitarian actors are encouraged to carry out humanitarian activities, assistance to the vulnerable population within the focused counties. Humanitarian specifically WASH supplies are secured with the responsibility of local authorities and the organizations managements.
