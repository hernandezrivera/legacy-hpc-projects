Project Summary

The project aims to provide nutrition opportunities and to improve the food security situation of at least 5,500 people residing in locations near the front-line (0-5 km, 5-20 km) through the step-by-step provision of 6 rounds of cash-based transfers assistance during the 10 month period. Out the whole range of beneficiaries, at least 5,500 people will receive cash/vouchers and at least 2,000 of them will be provided with 3 rounds of trainings (for each beneficiary) on nutrition and family accounting (food for trainings component).

Needs

The ongoing conflict in the Eastern part of Ukraine had negatively influenced the normal living conditions of the most livelihoods, and persons specifically. Many people who used to have access to normal everyday life conditions, including employment, had changed their social category to vulnerable. In the same time, through the occurred economic crises, most of those who considered to be vulnerable, had become the most vulnerable. Since March 2016 the NGO “Responsible citizens” had been working on the territories of the Government-Controlled Areas. Mainly the support of the organization is provided to the citizens residing on the territory of the front-line locations (incl. “grey zone”) and in the locations of the IDPs’ compact residence at Donetsk and Luhansk oblast.Both types of population can be characterized with:- unemployment;- hard access to social payments;- unbearable increase of prices on food products, potable water, hygiene and medicines, resources for home heating in winter periods.It should be also mentioned that most of those who are suffering are women of a different age and especially with IDP status. It can be explained through next options: - some of men had stayed on the territories of the origin residing (NGCA) taking care of property and others, - IDP men, coming to a new places of residence, finding no employment in most cases become addicted (especially in age 40-60),- men, belonging to the local population, losing their employment through the economic situation, are not able to feed their families (especially in age 40-60).Despite such a situation, the monitoring shows that the most active among the suffered population are still women, but in the same time, they need much support. Especially in case of a big household – with two and more children.According to Humanitarian Needs Overview 2019 by Food Security & Livelihoods Cluster 89% of IDPs, who were actively seeking employment reported facing difficulties, with some 80 per cent of them representing women.For woman in age 40+ it’s very hard to find work. According to statistics from Ukrainian employment centers, this category of citizens most often becomes registered, but does not find a workplace. In addition, this trend is visible through open online recruitment resources, where employers indicate a woman’s age for admission to work up to 40 years. It should be noted that according to the research of the State statistics service, women in Ukraine receive a 22–40% lower salary than men under equal working conditions. The part of the population, that used to belong to the vulnerable categories before the conflict, appeared to be in a depressive situation now. Among such people there are retired lonely people with low income and now side support, disabled people and people with chronical diseases. These people have to use the most part of their hardly obtained funds directly on medicines and utilities. As the practice shows those people’ thoughts about the norms of their nutrition are the last in the que.IDPs are also the ones who, despite the ongoing programs of support from national and international humanitarian agencies, continue to require support not less as from the beginning of the conflict. Despite the fact that some percentage of them had managed to move to the furthers from the front-line regions most of the continue to residing in the cities near their former home locations. It gives them an opportunity to take care of the apartments and relatives, left on the NGCA. But among the nearest locations more than 60% are cities with depressive economies. Aside from this the economic situations stays to wish better and doesn’t provide any new opportunities for unemployment reduction. There is also existing a social problem – the negative attitude of the local population towards IDPs as the most attention in support providing is still mostly paid to IDPs.

Activities & outcomes

It is planned that 6 rounds of support will be provided to about 5,500 of beneficiaries via food for training. The schedule for the RC’s implementation of the proposed activities:February: Development of beneficiaries’ lists for not less than 2000 of beneficiaries (group A).March: Providing the I round of trainings and the I round of support to first 2000 of beneficiaries (group A). April: Next beneficiaries’ lists for 2000 of beneficiaries (group B). The II round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (A).May: Next beneficiaries’ lists for 1500 of beneficiaries (group C). The II round of trainings and the III round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (A). The I round of trainings and the I round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (B).June: The I round of trainings and the I round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (C). The IV round of support and the II round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (A).July: The III round of trainings and the V round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (A). The II round of trainings and the III round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (B). The II round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (C).August: the VI round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (A). The IV round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (B). The II round of trainings and the III round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (C).September: The III round of trainings and the V round of support to 2000 of beneficiaries (B). The IV round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (C).October: The III round of trainings and the V round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (С). The VI round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (B).November: The VI round of support to 1500 of beneficiaries (С).December: Reports’ providing. All the beneficiaries will belong to vulnerability criteria: elderly people living alone, elderly people living in couple (elderly people part of larger households should not be included), single women with children (to include divorced, separated and living alone with children), disabled and chronically ill, female-headed households, IDPs.Locations: Donetsk region: Maryinsky rayon – 900 beneficiaries, Volnovakhsky rayon – 600 beneficiaries, Yasinuvatsky rayon - 1000 beneficiaries, Bakhmut rayon - 1000 beneficiaries. Luhansk region: Popasnyansky rayon - 800 beneficiaries, Novoaydarsky rayon - 500 beneficiaries, Stanichno-Luhansky rayon - 700 beneficiaries.Before and during the implementation of the project, RC will coordinate with other organizations which provide assistance in these areas to avoid inefficient distribution of aid.Food for trainings component includes three trainings on the topics of nutrition (balance diet, safe food) and family accounting. During the monitoring, the indicated topics of the trainings were selected by the beneficiaries as interesting and potentially useful. Trainings will be attended by at least 2000 beneficiaries and at least one adult person per household with vulnerable categories. Each participant will receive a handout with the main information and recommendations on the training topic.To provide a successful work of the Project on each of the locations, the RC is going to involve local NGOs and volunteers, local authorities and institutions in the fields.The activities of the Project, are: conduction of pre-and post-monitoring on every stage of the project implementation;cooperation with stakeholders in the project locations;development of cooperation with local retailers’ net;conduction of beneficiaries’ identification and verification;timing control – measures undertaken to be sure that each of the beneficiary had obtained 6 rounds of support, each for each month and 825 UAH/beneficiary, on the total amount 4950 UAH;control and accounting of the beneficiaries’ funds and funds obtained for the project implementation;permanent hotlines at all project locations and at RC head office for receiving requests from beneficiaries;RC head office email for receiving requests.

Indicators & targets

1) Target:About 5 500 of beneficiaries had improved their nutrition conditions.Indicators:-not less than 5500 people will receive of 6 rounds of cash-based transfers assistance from March till December 2019,-not less than 80% of all beneficiaries are living at 0-5 km area along the ‘contact line’, -60% of beneficiaries are women of a different age,-not less than 20% of all beneficiaries are IDPs,-not less than 2000 beneficiaries will be interviewed during the monitoring on issues of improving the food situation during the project.2) Target:Not less than 2000 beneficiaries will be provided with 3 rounds of trainings (for each beneficiary) on nutrition and family accounting from March till November 2019:-not less than 1 adult person from each family/household who will get cash-based transfers are visiting 3 trainings,-not less than 60% of training participants reporting improve in knowledge on the nutrition and family accounting.
