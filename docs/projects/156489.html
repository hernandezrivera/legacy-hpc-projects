Project Summary

This project aims to support 2500 farmers in three locations with agriculture inputs as the following:
• Support farmers in Atareb and Al-Bab through reducing the financial burden by supporting 500 local farmers who can plant one donum agriculture lands but they are in need for agricultural inputs through supporting them to plant one donum with seeds, fertilizer, pesticide, fungicide, modern irrigation network, and fuel to plant summer vegetables (tomato, eggplant, and cucumber(. Plant one donum with summer vegetable crops will produce nearly (2.5- 3) ton vegetable crops that will provide the basic need from summer vegetable in the targeted areas.
• Support 500 potato farmers in Atareb and Al-Bab with (tubers, fertilizers, pesticide, fungicide, modern irrigation network, and fuel) to plant one donum with potato crop in Autumn season, the potato crop is the second strategic crop after wheat in Syria, this crop is grown in vast areas in the governorates of Idlib, Homs, Aleppo, and Hama. Because of the ongoing crisis in Syria, the cultivated area decreased by 50%.
• Support 500 olive farmers in Bennsh, Idleb province with agriculture services regarding pre-harvesting practices (supplementary irrigation to increase the resistance to high temperature and improve the composition of olive oil inside the olive fruit), harvesting process (manual and mechanical harvesting, extraction process, branding and labelling) and post-harvesting process (post-harvesting tillage, intercropping, and fertilization in addition to marketing support).
olive farmers will be targeted in an average of 3-4 donum for each which mean almost 50 olive trees for each.
• Support 500 wheat farmers with seed and fertilizers to plant one Hectare for each farmer in Atareb. Wheat is the first strategic crop in Syria and planted 500 Hectare with wheat will produce about 1750 -2000 ton of wheat which will enhance the food security in the targeted area.
• Support 500 farmers in Jarablus to plant one hectare with rainfed barley crop through providing them with barley kits which contain (250 kg barley seeds, 150 kg fertilizers, 5L herbicide). Barley is considered feed crop and planted 500 donum with barley will produce about 1000-1200 ton of barley which improving the livestock feeding.
• Rehabilitation of the irrigation canal of Jarablus through recruiting 50 workers as Cash for Work (CFW) to clean and maintain the canal edges. The workers will work for twenty-two days per month, and each skilled worker will be paid for 7.5 USD per day and 6 USD for the unskilled ones. It is worth mentioning that IYD will provide these workers with agricultural equipment set so that they will make use of it after the end of the project. These wages earned by these workers will be redeemed according to their need in a flexible way. In addition, the operational costs of the vehicles, which will be used to implement this activity, will be covered. In this way, we will ensure that 500 hectares will be watered and irrigated, which means increasing in the production of crops and decreasing their prices.

Needs

Now in its eighth year, the humanitarian crisis in Syria has led to the internal displacement of at least 5.9 million people and left an estimated 13 million in need of humanitarian assistance.According to the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) Food Security Sector 2018 Mid-Year Review, food security further deteriorated during the first six months of 2018, with the number of food insecure people increasing from 6.5 million to 6.7 million. The number of people at risk of food insecurity has also grown, from 4 million to 4.5 million, bringing the total up to 11.2 million Syrians who need of urgent life-saving and life-sustaining food and livelihoods assistance.The agricultural sector, which normally serves as a major engine of the economy and a source of cheaper, more abundant food, is also threatened by factors such as land contamination, lack of availability of and/or financial means to access essential inputs, and damaged infrastructure. Given this year’s poor cereal harvest, the seed is very likely to be in extremely short supply for the coming season (starting from October 2018). concerning the Potato production in the country is now down to about two-thirds of its pre-crisis levels, largely because of the reduced availability and high cost of seed potatoes and required inputs. Depending on the availability of funds, GOSM imports between 10 000 and 15 000 tonnes of certified seed potato each year from Europe. Regarding the livelihood trend, lack of employment, resulting in low purchasing power, is the main economic challenge faced by most households. IDPs reported working multiple jobs and resorting to child labour as commonly employed coping strategies to meet their needs. Women have become the breadwinners for many families where men are not present, given the vast extent of the destruction in both urban and rural areas, major investments will be required to rebuild livelihoods in the coming years, the unemployment rate is estimated at about 50 per cent (although precise statistics are missing), up from about 10 per cent at the beginning of the conflict. At the same time many, civil servants, in particular, continue to juggle several jobs to be able to cover the high cost of living. According to the rapid need assessment conducted by IYD's MEAL team in the second half of 2018, the information indicates to:1- Atareb, in general, contains large tracts of fertile agricultural land and farmers with small agriculture land. These farmers are planting winter crops because they cannot cultivate their land due to the high costs of agricultural inputs and irrigation.2- Atareb is the western rural side of Aleppo, contains large tracts of fertile agricultural land and irrigation wells. According to follow table, the percentage of cultivable land in the targeted area is 65 %.3- The rainfall rate in the Atareb subdistrict is about 350-500 millimetres annually and the percentage of irrigated land is 42 %.4- The farmers of Jarablus suffer from lack of water for irrigation and the percentage of irrigated land decreased significantly especially in the last three years because the main irrigation canal has been out of service for seven years. Only in Jarablus, this issue alone led to drop-in cultivation by 35% since 2011. Therefore, cultivation decreased throughout the area, which has had a negative impact on the local economy.In other side, After nearly 7 years of the conflict in Syria affected negatively the olive sector in Idlib governorate, especially the areas surrounding the military sites and points where there was fierce fighting because of its strategic location such as Bennesh city due to their location near Foah city, this has led to the fact that a wide range of olive lands is not invested.

Implementation of activities

Summer vegetables and potato: this project will support 1000 farmers, 500 of them who have a small scale to plant one donum with summer vegetables and 500 farmers to plant one donum with potato crop. This will be by providing the farmers by agriculture inputs including seeds, fertilizer, pesticide, fungicide, irrigation system, and support harvesting. Also, the project aims to save water by using modern irrigation network by the beneficiaries in the targeted area, this modern irrigation network will improve the quality of irrigation and increase the yield. Moreover, IYD will hold training sessions on the new agriculture methods by using new network irrigation so IYD will choose fields which are owned and ran by expert farmers from the benefited families, these five fields will be the training locations on the GAP training and these expert farmers will be divided into many groups to be trained based on their experience. Therefore, the beneficiaries will be able to see and learn from the agronomist who will do the experiment in front of them.Concerning olive farmers, IYD will support 500 farmers in Bennsh as follow:a. Harvesting process: the following activities are planned to be implemented:i. Field workshop on proper harvesting practices and on mechanical harvesting by testing the new harvesting machines were provided to the local council to be used in olive harvesting.ii. Supervising the harvesting process by scheduling the process to have all BNFs harvesting their field within 1 month maximum starting first of November 2018, olives will be collected by using plastic boxes (25 kg capacity), 50 BNFs will be selected for mechanical harvesting and the rest will benefit from manual harvesting, contracting with an olive mill to extract the olive oil under technical supervision by the team, labeling the product by using a label with an information about the quality and the circumstances of production. Some harvesting tools will be provided to ease the harvesting process like the ladders, rakes, olive oil stainless steel tanks and collection sheets.b. Post-harvesting process: the following activities will be implemented:i. Post-harvesting tillage for all BNFs will be carries out to ventilate the soil and to avoid the compact impact of harvesting process.ii. It is known technically that the pruning of olive is very important process after harvesting and/or during February, pruning tools will be provided to the BNFs and they will be trained on pruning to enable them to prune their trees.iii. Intercropping activity by providing legume seeds (Peas and barley) –according to the standards - to the farmers to be cultivated under olive trees where it will support the soil fertility and structure. Cash for work: 1000 workers will be targeted by cash for work activities, where 500 workers will work for one month in manual harvesting activity and 500 workers will work for one month also in irrigation process, some of them will work also in the extraction process (loading, uploading activities, labeling …etc). this project aims to upgrade the irrigation status throughout: 1- Rehabilitation of the irrigation canal of Jarablus through recruiting 50 workers as Cash for Work (CFW) to clean and maintain the canal edges. In addition, the operational costs of the vehicles, which will be used to implement this activity, will be covered. In this way, we will ensure that 500 hectares will be watered and irrigated, which means increasing in production of crops and decreasing their prices. 2- The farmer's awareness about water saving concept will be promoted through conducting raising awareness sessions, as well as distributing modern irrigation system to farmers enough to cover one donum per farmer. Moreover, training on how to maximally and efficiently make use of the modern irrigation methods, will be done to these farmers with supporting them with irrigation costs.

Protection Risk Analysis

- The risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers. IYD will mitigate this by establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and referral mechanisms, addressing the SEA complaints, training staff on PSEA and GBV, asking all staff to sign code of conduct, creating females and males’ queues separately, having female staff. Behaving with beneficiaries as do no harm and respecting their dignity. - Increased risk of inter-community tension between community residents and host communities due in part to unequal level of assistance given to community residents in comparison to host communities. IYD will mitigate this by ensuring that the community are all needs based, and that community residents are amongst the most vulnerable. - To avoid Gender exploitation. IYD will mitigate this by including all the age and gender needs accordingly within the project designing phase. Clarifying all the project objectives and beneficiaries’ selection criteria to all the local authorities. Asking the local authorities’ members, vendors and services’ providers to sign the PSEA code of conduct. - To avoid the gender discrimination, IYD will provide suitable and safe distribution environment such as: accessible ramps for people with special needs, drinking water, waiting seats, umbrellas, secure distribution center and placing it away from military installations or threats such as attacks. - Child labor risk, the mitigation is to make the service provider committing to the listed conditions which provided to not make any work with him if he was breaking the signed contract with IYD by practicing the child labor. - Child food ration receiving risk. The mitigation is by not delivering the food ration to the child and having a mobile distribution team to deliver the ration to this child house in case the householder is not able to come to the distribution center.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption, The agriculture inputs have high quality.Risk Description, Low productivity per donum because of weak plant resistance to the surrounding environmental conditions.Mitigation, coordinate with technical team to check seeds after that supply seeds of high productivity and resistant to environmental conditions. Assumption, The climatic conditions will be stable.Risk Description, External climatic risks (drought, diseases, physiological impacts…)Mitigation, Technical supervision by prevention and suitable treatments procedures. Assumption, All the agriculture inputs are available with stable price.Risk Description, Lack of availability of certain materials or changes in prices during the project period.Mitigation, The financial study of the project took into consideration the change in the exchange rate as well as the writing of long-term contracts with suppliers will mitigate the damage resulting from price change. Assumption, The exchange rate will be stable during the project period.Risk Description, Change in the exchange rate for the Syrian Pound against the US Dollar.Mitigation, The financial study of the project took into consideration the change in the exchange rate as well as the writing of long-term contracts with suppliers will mitigate the damage resulting from the price change. Assumption, The security situation will be stable during the project period.Risk Description, Change in the security situation.Mitigation, IYD will coordinate with the active actors in the ground to pick the safest area to implement the project, additionally, IYD safety and security officer will follow the security situation and send reports to the field team to ensure their safety.
