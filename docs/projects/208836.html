Project Summary


This project has been designed to address the most critical Education needs of the IDPs in camps/gathering sites & most affected host populations. However, special attention will be given to children who got displaced after 15th.April 2023.The project will provide increased access to inclusive and protective lifesaving education for the children (boys and girls) affected by emergency. Efforts will be made to improve the quality of education and ensure continuity of learning during and after emergency for the most vulnerable school aged children. The project will specifically support the provision of Emergency Education services to (boys and girls 6-18 years) in the conflict affected communities & those in IDPs camps. In North Darfur state, the following localities will be targeted (Al Fasher, Al Lait, At Tina, Kernoi and Kutum localities) while in South Darfur the following localities will be considered (Beliel, Gereida, Mershing, Nitega and Nyala Janoub localities). The project will target children at risk of dropping out of schools due to the ongoing crisis. The project will ensure retention in school for displaced and host community's children, from disadvantaged backgrounds and in hard-to-reach areas.
The Humanity for Development and Prosperity Org (HDPO) aims to serve the most vulnerable groups of children in the targeted localities through provision of qualitative and protective minimum standard for education. The most affected children’s (boys and girls) will be more effectively protected from violence, risks and exploitations. The project will be able to mitigate external shocks and ensure continuity of learning. This will be achieved through the following main activities: provision of recreational materials, distribution of dignity kits and MHM Education, establishment or rehabilitation of temporary learning space (TLS) using semi-permanent materials and alternate learning modalities, installation or rehabilitation of handwashing facilities (installed temporary), provision of psychosocial support, training of teachers, parents and teachers associations (PTAs) on mental, health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), safe schools, gender based violence (GBV) and child protection, provision of teaching and learning materials and training clusters partners and Ministry of Education officials (MoE) on emergency preparedness, response planning and coordination.
11,550 individuals (7,500 IDPs & 4,050 host communities) are expected to benefit from this project activities directly as follow: Female (1200 ), Male(1090), Children( 8,900), Adult(210), Disabilities (150) and 25700 individuals indirectly (13,500 IDPs & 12,200 host communities) as follow: Females( 2,500), males (2,500), Children( 20,000), Adults(350) and Disabilities ( 350) with total estimated budget of 413,200 $ to ensure the above identified critical needs been addressed in effectively and timely manner.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project will ensure the protection, dignity and safety of the beneficiaries receiving the services. The project will prevent any discriminatory action and promote equal accessibility to the delivered services. HDPO recognizes that understanding the conflict dynamics is crucial to ensure at the very least a Do No Harm (DNH) approach is taken. Beneficiaries education and awareness raising is also another component put in place for understanding the needs of the community and appropriateness of delivered services which will further ensure their protection. The project will ensure that particularly women and children protection needs are met through designating of gender when accessing the education services

The information obtained from beneficiaries and stakeholders will be used to take corrective action, ensure quality assurance and inform decisions to be taken by the project steering team.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

In order to ensure the targeted beneficiaries number is reached and the intended services are provided equally among the most vulnerable populations to save their lives in timely and effectively manner, HDPO will adopt all the safety, protection and accountability to the affected population related measures including DO NO HARM policies and procedure at all levels of the project implementations.:- 1- In order to ensure that the communities are aware of the project activities, the communities (women, men, boys and girls) were involved from the very beginning of the project that include needs assessments, project designs and will be involved fully in implementation of the project. 2- HDPO will work closely with the affected populations during selection of beneficiaries so as to ensure fairness, equal distribution of services by considering gender needs and sensitivity and to avoid any conflict of interest that might occur among the affected populations. 3- Joint monitoring team will be formed by HDPO with involvement of key IDPs community leaders, local authorities at state levels. The team will meet on quarterly basis to review the effective implementation of the project activities. This mechanism will enable the target populations to discuss and evaluate the performance of HDPO and provide feedback, complaints and recommendation where necessarily needed. Their feedback, complaints and recommendations will enable the organization management to take effective steps toward the implementation of the project and its impact of the target beneficiaries. HDPO will follow up the guidelines and procedures that provide instructions on how to set up and run interagency-based mechanism to handle reports of abuse and to provide victim assistance and scaling up protection from sexual exploitation and abuse and focusing on encouraging victims to come forward by ensuring that safe, trusted and accessible mechanisms for reporting and promoting a culture of zero tolerance for SEA.
