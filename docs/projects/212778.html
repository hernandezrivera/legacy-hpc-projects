Project Summary

The association owns a building (fully build) consisting of 5 floors (2 underground and 3 above) with a floor area of about 2000 square meters in Um Elamad in the eastern countryside of Homs. The association obtained all official licenses, completed engineering studies, and completed all construction work. During 2024, the association aims to secure the necessary funding to complete the rehabilitation and equipping operations of the hospital.
It is planned that the hospital's capacity will reach to 45 beds and includes several main departments, including ambulance, intensive care, radiology, laboratories, and other specialized departments, the most important of which are the women's, children's, and dialysis departments. In addition to 2 operating rooms to perform all types of surgeries, an outpatient department, and several logistical departments. All departments will be equipped with the necessary equipment according to the approved standard specifications for hospitals.
Finally, the project aims to provide integrated medical services to the beneficiary families of the poor at nominal fees and free of charge to the families most in need. Regarding the geographical scope, the project aims to provide medical services to all residents of the villages of the Al Makhrim region (+100 villages), reaching the remote areas located in the Syrian desert to the east.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The entire eastern countryside of Homs was subjected to large-scale military operations that led to the destruction of many health facilities, including most areas of Al Makhrim. The targeted areas are considered to be in high need of medical services. The population exceeds 60 thousand people (60% women and children). The region also hosts dense concentrations of internally displaced persons (IDPs), who constitute 33% of the population. On the other hand, there is no public or private hospital in a circumference of more than 10 km (40 km) in all directions since before the war. In addition, there are no health centers, clinics, or specialized laboratories in the region, especially dialysis centers, radiology centers for MRI and CT scans, or an incubator center.Residents of these areas are forced to go to hospitals in the city of Homs to obtain medical services, in conjunction with the lack of means of transportation and high transportation costs. Most of the region's residents are considered poor families in need of health care and unable to afford medical treatment (more than 90% of the population).The association will commit to providing previously prepared studies, engineering plans, and tables of quantities of materials required for rehabilitating. During implementation, periodic follow-up reports will be provided in cooperation with the engineering team. On the other hand, the project will consider all safety and security standards to protect workers from potential risks while carrying out operations and train them on dealing with hazardous materials. The project is expected to face several economic and logistical risks during implementation, which will be dealt with according to the previously prepared risk matrix.
