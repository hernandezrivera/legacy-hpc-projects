Project Summary

After Cyclone Idai struck Zimbabwe in March 2019, affecting about 270,000 people in 12 districts of Manicaland and Masvingo provinces, there are still approximately 104,360 individuals having insufficient shelter in the following districts that is Chipinge, Chimanimani, Buhera and Mutare. The raining season started with strong winds and hailstorms that affected the already vulnerable districts increasing emergency and shelter needs. Help is scaling up efforts to provide shelter and Non Food Items assistance to 3.000 most vulnerable households through rehabilitation of damaged shelter and new constructions in Chipinge and Chimanimani Districts. This will be done through the provision of shelter building materials and labour fees to support rehabilitation of shelters and new constructions, so as to improve long-term usage of constructed shelters. Help is adapting its response to shelter needs based on lessons learned and good practices from the past. The activities are implemented in cooperation with IOM, Government departments responsible for shelter and other NGOs.
