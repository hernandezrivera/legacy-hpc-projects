Project Summary

Considering the high numbers of newly arriving people to Dana and Maaret Tamsrin(NWS), especially in (Burdaqly, Deir Hassan - Darhashan, Harbanush, Shekh Bahr) which consider as most overcrowded areas), and high severity for the risk of dropping out due to no schools, no educational staff ..etc).
support to minimize the gap in education provision for vulnerable IDPs populations and Displacement is likely to have caused significant disruption, both to IDPs and HCs, for
IDP children and youth put a significant strain on the capacity of HC to
accommodate increased education needs, Whilst there is willingness from HCs to support education needs of IDP children.
Overwhelming schools’ capacity to meet education needs through expecting them to accommodate new influxes of IDP children poses risk of both undermining quality of teaching and learning for all school going children, and potentially ‘doing harm’ through
raising tensions between vulnerable IDP and HCs.
Considering the prolonged and violent nature of conflict in NWS, moreover, there is a high likelihood that populations, both HCs and IDPs, have experienced significant war related stress/duress.
BRD will go ahead to cover education needs in 4 schools:
Shekh Bahr school–1st stage
Haranbush school–1st.
Alhuria school–1st, 2nd stage
Burdaqly school–1st
BRD aims to support Education opportunities through delivering Educational and mainstreaming protection services in NWS for most vulnerable children (G, B, PwDs), through curriculum B and SLP, ALP as NFE.
Project designed to enhance the quality of Education through rehabilitate WASH facilities in school taking into account difference of S and G.
Building capacity of educational personnel, covering monthly stipends, running costs, teaching and learning materials, PSS sessions, school bags(G, B, M and W), and in regard to System strengthening, the project will respond to gap of school textbooks by printing, considering sensitivities of Syrian crisis. Intervention is planned to cover Education objectives after 11 years of crisis that imposes education as urgent priority to prevent a lost generation.
BRD will start project through BTLs, rehabilitation for schools (Latrines blocks (con\rehab), furniture, recruitment, textbooks, with school will be distributed.
Formal education
BRD will run those 4 schools with formal education in schools time and Education process will start through ASER test for children to determine their levels, training the teachers (ALP, CM, EIE, INEE, referral mechanisms)
BRD will Prepare a room in each school to be used as CFS, BRD will conduct special activities for children and PWDs such as inlusion (Learning through play, psychological support, integration activities.. etc),
NFE:
in summer vacation BRD will run those schools with NFE
In Summer period BRD will deliver NFPs (ALP for and catch up class for same children) for 1200out of 2365
BRD will continue with formal education for 2365 students (6 to 17 years - who dropped out - lagged behind their peers) for the children who are regularly attending the schools or dropped out for a years.
BRD will deliver those services for 2365 (B:1339 and G:1026) (236 out of 2365 (10%) PwDs) and educational staff (M:27 and W:31)
Grades in those schools from 1st -6th grade, BRD will encourage F/M children to attend in those schools equally through BTL campaigns to ensure equal access to children, BRD will hire teachers, schools managers. etc, M/F in equal to ensure Gender equality.
BRD will facilitate attending class for PwDS, through special exit and entrance, special toilets and washing basin, as well as inclusion activities with their peers.etc
BRD will deliver physical activities for children in schools and will take into consideration COVID-19 which ensure social distancing, ,,etc, in case education cluster and DoE decided to close schools temporary, BRD will use its online platform to deliver educational services for children as plan B.

3 - Needs

In 2022, The impact of hostilities is particularly severe on W and C who constitute 77% of the population in NWS, of which over 53% are C. Further, PwD are disproportionately impacted by ongoing conflict. Over 180K school-aged children are in need of education services. 2.1M Children in Syria are OOS, and a further 1.3 million are at risk of dropping out (HNO23)More than 1 in 3 schools are damaged, which causing overcrowding in operational schools, 65 to 75 child per class (HNO23). As the conflict continues, effective teaching becomes increasingly challenging due to over-crowded and lack of resources. C have been expose to psychological trauma, displacement and family upheaval generated by crisis, many C and teachers are affected by psychological trauma. NA conducted by BRD (in mentioned locations) shows the C are in need for education services which more than 1900 child out of 2300 (B:1044, G:1256) are not going to schools (Dropped out), no salaries for teachers, all schools need to be rehabilitated.etc, in mentioned locations.RNA resulted the following:- 74%of assessed schools aren’t supported for the new academic year (2023-2024) - Most of the assessed schools are affected from the suspension during Covid-19 - There are 8281 children (4658 IDPs & 3713 hosts) including 75 CwD enrolled in the formal education till the end of academic year 2022-2023- There are 383 new displaced children enrolled at the schools in formal education during this academic year.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Learning facilities could be attacked, • Reduce time/distance travelled by decentralizing learning facilities.• Provide students, their caregivers and school personnel information on safer routes/times to travel.• If appropriate, possible and practical promote caregivers accompanying students to/from school. highlight the importance of safety and related solutions. Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment,Recruitment processes for teachers and other education personnel should evaluate candidates soft skill competencies along with pedagogical skills. Teachers should receive sufficient remuneration so that it is more likely that trained and experienced teachers and education personnel remain in-service. Systems should be in place to ensure sufficient monitoring during recess periods, in hallways, in bathrooms.Students, their caregivers, teachers and school personnel should be encouraged to report issues and have safe access Aid workers or associated people use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving., ensure a survivor centered approach that ensures the safety and confidentiality of the affected person. The survivor should have a say in the processesLearning facilities or their surroundings are structurally unsafe, Unsafe learning spaces and insufficient/non-existent WASH facilities contribute to children not attending learning facilities


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

BRD a PSEA network member and strives to serve all vulnerable people to attain a better life and maintain their dignity. Through set protection policy and procedure, BRD actively discusses methods to protect the dignity and image of BNs during implementation. BNs are treated equally with respect and dignity, regardless of religion, G, S, or political affiliation. BRD conducting gender mainstreaming to identify how project could impact lives of vulnerable groups in area. The project tries to touch on specific risks faced by boys such as labour and recruitment, by G such as early marriage and labour, and the disabled such as isolation and limited mobility, by reaching out to communities and promoting the value of education, by improving the learning environment. BRD discovered immense benefit to vulnerable W and (PwDs) BRD is assisting the most vulnerable groups gain access to centers. To further protect these groups (C, W...etc) during the implementation BRD will prepare centers to receive children with DAs and complaints box will be there with all communication channels to have contact with PSEA and MEAL staff,, through ramp, exiting and entering tracks, separate toilets with locks and lamps, and other for PwDs BRD will aim to 50-50% F/M representation. BRD will prioritize hiring people who live near centers—as longer distances often limit W’s ability to travel to work due to cultural norms and/or safety concerns. BRD has PSEA policy which include in all aspects of project.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The security situation in Syria will allow the proposed programming with minimal interruption. BRD acknowledges that certain challenges are unavoidable in the Syrian context and designed the intervention with careful consideration of the complex operating environment. The risks to personnel and materials are multiple across the Syrian border. In light of risks, the scale and scope of program activities remains flexible and adjusted according to the context. high risk justification/Mitigation: Members of education staff will stop working during periods of bombing - Collaboration with DoE to gain legitimacy while working. Air attacks and indirect shots:Monitoring of news and information collection, Identification of health centers, Safe working area in the office, Hibernation, First aid training, Medical plan Corruption or fraud:Anti-corruption policy / training, credible control mechanism, procurement policy, code of conduct, check-in on staff and suppliers / Contractors \ financial policy and determination of powers COVID-19: Provide the schools with masks, sanitizer liquid, awareness sessions for the children by SDHs CCTCs health staff, triage point, Thermometer and social distance to ensure the safety of the children, plan B replace physical class by online class through using BRD platform.Chidren not attending the class, BRD will keep provide encouraging activities to keep them attending, as well providing them with needed school items and referral mechanism
