Project Summary

This project will target 7 communities in the Idleb and Aleppo which are classified among the areas most of the need according to moving of IDPs which depend up on the report of CCCM cluster of IDPs movement tracking.
The percentage of IDPs in the targeted areas reached 71% of the total population of each area in average, due to the recent displacement happened which the total number of IDPs up to end of Jan 2020 are 984K according to CCCM cluster information.
BRD aims to improve the food security of people suffering from food insecurity and provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable displaced families, focusing on areas with high needs.
The beneficiaries will be provided with a three-time food basket during the project, which will contain basic food items. The weight of each basket will be 29,350 kg, which consequently will contribute to alleviating the financial burdens and costs of living on the targeted families in the project.
BRD logistics team will purchase food items from inside Syria exclusively to be packaged in the warehouses in Hazzano.
The baskets will be transferred to the targeted areas in Aleppo and Idlib after identifying the spots of IDPs especially in open-lands, collective centers and informal settlement in the targeted locations on a daily basis according to the distribution plan and under the supervision of the Field Project Manager, the M&E team, the Logistics Team, and the Security and Safety team and then deliver it to the beneficiaries.
BRD's team will cooperate and coordinate with the local councils in accordance with the needs assessment conducted previously by preparing lists of beneficiaries that will be approved by the local councils according to predefined selection criteria:
- Displaced families (a key priority for choice, especially for newly displaced families)
- Family income must be less than $ 50 per month.
- The breadwinner is a woman whose husband is lost, injured or disabled.
- Poor families that have (elderly or young children under 15 years or widowed women).
The M&E team in collaboration with the field team will verify the names of the beneficiaries who will be provided with cards containing (the village code and beneficiary code) certified by the organization before starting the distribution.
Field Distribution Teams will begin the distribution process according to the beneficiary name lists and under the supervision of the distribution supervisors within a pre-defined comprehensive distribution plan for the targeted villages.

4 - Needs

through the Nine year, the humanitarian crisis in Syria continues and thus the scale, severity, and complexity of needs across Syria remain overwhelming and remain high. Syria Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO 2019), indicates some 11.7 million people in Syria require humanitarian aid and assistance, of them, 5 million considered in acute need in addition to the increased number of IDPs to reach 6.8 million peopleFood Needs in Syria are driven by many crisis related factors such as lack of financial access, as high food prices, low purchasing power, and lack of means of income or livelihood opportunities which badly affected the economic and living situation of people and led to food insecurityMost families suffer from many obstacles such as difficulty in accessing food and relief due to poverty, lack of livelihoods and income-generating sources, lack of adequate financial resources, increasing prices of food items in general, lack of jobs and income. These families’ needs to food baskets in the current situations to alleviate the suffering and ensure the existence of food security for them and to ensure the provision of basic human needs to maintain the safety and life, where most of them have fled their homes due to the escalation of hostilities within South of Idleb, The recent displacement upto Jan 2020 which the total number of IDPs reached up to 520K. IDPs in North Syria experiencing very difficult economic and social conditions, with the increase in the number of "poor families" headed by a woman / husband who is missing, a detainee, a deceased or a husband with special needs who cannot work, where estimated to reach more than 53,875 households about 43.5% of the total population as indicated through the Multi-Sectoral Needs AssessmentThe lack of basic food items, absence of income resources and rising food prices led to the deteriorated situations of those families in their last resort where they seek refuge in camps, open lands and in collective centers

5 - Implementation of activities

BRD will start implementation which BRD will provide 16000 food baskets for almost 16000 families of IDPs in the targeted villages to 92000 persons of IDPs, the distribution will be through several points of distribution in targeted villages. The proposed food baskets will be enough for families who consist of 6 individuals which were weight 29.350 kg and is composed of 14 items from (bulgur, rice, lentils, sugar, chickpeas, white beans, tomato paste, vegetable oil, dates, halva, noodles, and canned cheese) The food baskets will be purchased from selected suppliers within Syria through a written agreement and agreement setting out all the conditions necessary to ensure the quality of the products and delivery and payment mechanisms The process of packing and packaged food baskets in boxes with the BRD's logo placed on the food baskets so that the packaging and packing BRD will ensure the accountability for the affected population and prevent fraud and exploitation. MOU will be signed with local councils explaining all project activities and objectives. Verification of beneficiary lists through field visits by the M&E Teams. BRD will also print cards for the whole targeted beneficiaries, 16000, and distribute them to the beneficiaries at least 3 to 5 days in advance, specifying the date of distribution, time and location, making them be able to receive the food baskets from the distribution points safely and easily The distribution supervisors will be supervising the work of the distribution workers, monitor the progress of the distribution process. Once the distribution process is completed, the M&E team will conduct visits to ascertain the satisfaction of the beneficiaries.
