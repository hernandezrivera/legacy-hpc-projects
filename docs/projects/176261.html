Project Summary

Panyijiar County at present is home to a total of 117,000 individuals and continues to face numerous challenges, namely ; insecurity due to cattle raids, Revenge Killing, chronic food insecurity, yearly flooding from the river Nile, high rate of malnutrition, water borne diseases and gender based violence accross many households. The 2020 flooding which begun in Mid June has affected women, men, girls and boys accross the County, curently affecting more than 5,791 HH (more than 3577 in Nyal and more than 2214 in Ganyliel). The flood has displaced several households in Tiap Payam, Jiech, Chuk, Malouk, Kanhial, Pachar, Pachienjok, Maluak and Pachar. Ganyliel, Thoanhom, Tiap, Jiech, Pariel, Pachar, Pachak, Pachienjok and Yai. 90% percent of the population is affected by flood and 85% percent make do with open defecation and the GAM rates are over 30%. The number of boreholes that were submerged in water or flooded were as follows: Ganyliel =7, Pachienjok=4, Yai=3, Pachar=5, Pariel=3, Pachak=5, Thoanhom=4, Tiap=3, Jiech=2, the total number of boreholes submerged are 36 in the whole of greater Ganyliel. The number of latrines collapsed because of flood or flooded are 521: Ganyliel=400, Pachienjok=15, Yai=17, Pachak=14, Pariel=16, Pachar=15, Thoanhom=25, Tiap=10, Jiech=9. Although there has bed interventions in WASH sector, HACO proposes continued access of affected community to Water Purification tabs, Rehabilitation of boreholes, construction/rehabilitation of communal latrines and hygiene promotion.

1 - Needs

Panyijiar and Mayom Counties suffered multiple shocks since mid 2020; localized violence, floods and crop destruction. This resulted in the two locations being placed under IPC 4.The high rates of malnutrition are attributed to several factors including high levels of food insecurity, poor infant and young child feeding practices as well as high morbidity due to a weak health system in the counties. In order to forestall the aforementioned crisis, HACO in concert with other Humanitarian actors intends to provide timely and equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs, host communities and returnees through sustainable solutions. HACO response aims at mitigating WASH related GBV risks by providing gender appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities and tackling issues surrounding gender, safety and WASH including provision of dignity kits and NFIs for Hygiene, Sanitation and rehabilitation of Water points. Due to high GAM rates (over 30%) in the Project areas, HACO will integrate WASH in Nutrition to provide caretakers of SAM/MAM children at OTP/SC with famine prevention kits.

2 - Project activities

1. Provide timely, access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene for IDPs, host communities, and returnees (Rehabilitate 50 Water Points): WASH temporary and sustainable services will address the vital needs, public health, and protection issues through an AAP community approach to target the acute vulnerabilities based on cross-sectoral evaluations and analyses 2. Mitigate WASH-related gender-based violence: WASH-specific activities for GBV mitigation in targeted communities based on Protection assessment conducted by female staff with women, girls, and children for WASH activities adequately planned with the community input. (PSEA sessions, FGDS, and construction/rehabilitation of 60 Latrines and Hand Washing facilities) 3. Integrate WASH in nutrition response through famine prevention minimum package: WASH integrated interventions of SAM/MAM admissions at OTP/SC targeting caretakers of the malnourished children up-to the household level aiming to break the vicious circle of diarrhea diseases/nematodes with malnutrition, addressing also small stock open defecation affecting young children. WASH activities will be conducted through an integrated and multidisciplinary approach involving WASH user committees coordination with Hygiene Promoters (Hygiene Promotion sessions at household and public gatherings).
