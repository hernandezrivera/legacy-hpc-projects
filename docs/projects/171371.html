Project Summary

The project aims are providing quality emergency and essential lifesaving primary health care services to reduce excess morbidity and mortality due diseases outbreak driven by climatic shocks and conflict as well as improvement of their physical and mental wellbeing and case management for gender based violence among crisis affected IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities (women, men, boys and girls) in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia through provision of essential primary health care services. The project targets the most vulnerable 19,776 Children under 5 years, 4,554 children under one years, 22,068 WCBA and 7,454 Mothers in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions through the following activities by Facility/fixed and mobile teams: Provision of Essential PHC (EPHS) clinical care, Child health care, Emergency and trauma care, Communicable disease treatment/conducting immunization on common disease outbreak including EPI, NCD treatment, STD / HIV treatment, TB screening and treatment. The project will further ensure support to early detection, investigation and response to disease outbreak through Rapid response teams (RRTs), monitoring (EWAR) in addition HCW Training on trauma, MCM, case management, emergency management, provision of medical supplies including essential drugs and ensuring Health Staff and patient safety through provision of PPE, triage and regular hand-washing, , Conducting awareness on utilization of health services at the health facilities in the project locations through mobilization and sensitization meetings and forums, provision of Integrated Mental health / psychosocial support services (MHPSS)HCW and community training on MHPSS (PFA, PSS, MhGAP-HIG), Community and HCF based physical rehabilitation, Clinical management of rape services (CMR) and GBV / IPV services and referral and STD / HIV treatment and prevention from GBV cases.
The service modality: The project activities will be conducted by the project staff at the Health facilities/MCHs run MCH by Zamzam Foundation while outreach services such as EPI will be provided by the mobile teams at the project targeted locations in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions. Activities such as conducting awareness on utilization of health services will be conducted by project staff with strong collaboration by the community members including elders, religious leaders and the MoH staff of the FGS / Hirshabelle and South West States.
Durable solutions to the project will be ensured by equipping health staff including CHWs with requisite training on integrated management on illnesses that will be put into practice beyond the project time. Furthermore, the existing health facilities will serve the targeted beneficiaries at the entire communities in Hiraan, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions beyond the project time. Moreover, already used medical equipment will be disposed and burnt regularly at the designated locations set aside for the particular purpose hence protection the environment from pollution by the such used equipment

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff as well as ensuring that project activities are carried out at before the cover of darkness to enable vulnerable women and girls ample time to get back to their homes and further limit the risks of GBV and related violations.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as conducting awareness on SEA and the reporting procedures that doesnt expose the victims to the perpetrators.3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4.
