Project Summary

As a result of the conflicts coupled with the COVID-19 epidemic, the existing health system and social platforms have been further weakened to respond to the Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR) needs of women and adolescent girls; to provide clinical management of rape survivor’s services. Significant number of health facilities in the northern part of Ethiopia are looted and damaged due to the conflict.
As a result of the drought in Somali, Oromia and SNNPR, there is high number of drought induced internally displaced persons as a result of which significant number of the health facilities that are found in the host community are overburdened. The health facilities are depleted of reproductive health commodities, personal protective equipment and medicines and medical equipment that are key for providing life-saving SRH services. There are reports of pregnant mothers delivering in the bush, in IDP camps, and at home due lack of access to health facilities and service providers. The health service referral pathways are not strong enough to enable timely access to appropriate services due to the overwhelming number of IDPs who are living within the host community in the collective IDP sites.
The interventions will aim to enable health facilities, institutions and health care providers to implement Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) for SRH that are lifesaving services during emergencies. Moreover, the activities will strengthen the capacity of health offices and health facilities to build a strong health system that is resilient to shocks and capable of responding to the SRHR and GBV needs. These include various capacity building training, equipping the facilities with the necessary equipment and commodities, deployment of midwives, establishing/strengthening mobile health teams, strengthening the referral and linkage between the community and the health facilities, between the host and the refugee/IDP community and their service provision platforms.
UNFPA is a pipe line manager of inter-agency RH kits for the emergency response, and is expected to important the emergency RH kits to the health cluster prioritized zones and districts
Community sensitization, awareness creation and mobilization activities will be undertaken to increase the health seeking behavior in general and SRH in particular; increase referral linkage from the community to health care facilities.
