Project Summary

The WASH humanitarian response will address the urgent needs of entire population and communities of the impacted Islands of Petite Martinique and Carriacou as well as those in shelters across Grenada. Special priority will be given to pregnant and lactating women, infants and young children as well as to girls, adolescents and other vulnerable groups, which will access to critical lifesaving WASH services and supplies. The WASH response also aims to ensure access to sufficient and clean water for drinking and domestic purposes, basic sanitation, bathing spaces, appropriate management of excreta and solid waste led by government authorities and hygiene services, including handwashing facilities, hygiene messages, hygiene supplies and menstrual hygiene management. When market access and functionality are appropriate, cash-based interventions will be considered as a modality for WASH supply distribution, especially when linked to the existing social protection programmes. Project Activities

• Support emergency water trucking/distribution in areas without supply., • Support safe water provision with water treatment, community water points and water quality monitoring.,• Distribution of household water treatment and storage supplies for most affected families, • Risk communication and community engagement for promotion of life-saving WASH practices (i.e. hand hygiene, water consumption, treatment, environmental health and behavior practice)., • Distribution of family hygiene kits,• Improvement and rehabilitation of water supply systems, including the provision of water supplies as tanks, chlorine, etc., • Installation or rehabilitation of hand hygiene points., • Provision of services and supplies to support access to adequate sanitation, according to minimum standards., • Distribution of family hygiene kits and family cleaning and disinfection kits, • Promotion of WASH adequate practices in shelters and training of shelter committee and sheltered families and WASH life-saving priorities under child protection focus., • Emergency Cash assistance to vulnerable households to ensure basic WASH needs are met
