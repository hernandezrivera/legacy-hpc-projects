Project Summary

The project seeks contribution to enhance the community’s resilience towards COVID-19 pandemic by implementing activity in ERL cluster in the humanitarian field in western rural of Aleppo province in Jebel Saman district in Atareb sub-district which is considered as high-needs and underserved areas, for both host community and IDP. This intervention will contribute to develop the household’s livelihood through establishing sewing workshop as CFW activity for 20 women from host community and IDPs to fabricate 750000 masks wherein wearing mask is an essential preventive measure to break the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic nowadays for specific where the daily statistics from NW Syria about the numbers of people contracted with COVID-19 do not bode well, as the numbers are increasing at an alarming rate that threatens the entire society, which already suffers from poverty and neglection.
Thus, the CFW activity for 100 women form the most vulnerable households from both host community and IDPs by daily 5 working hours and daily wage 5 $ according to the CWG, by establishing sewing workshop for fabric re-usable masks according to the Public advisory on the use of fabric facemasks in the context of COVID-19 with low cost , and distributing the produced masks (4 masks for each beneficiary), according to the COVID-19 task force recommendation to, camps and host community. There will be 3 sizes of the produced masks, small size suitable for children from 3 to 10, medium size suitable for adults 11to 18 and the big size suitable for adults18 years old and above.
This project will support 186,875 beneficiaries affected by the Syrian crisis (40080men, 42145women, 51015boys and 53635 girls by promoting their accessibility to the humanitarian assistance significantly.
All the people in the target locations and markets will benefit directly/indirectly from the project where the purchasing of the needed materials of both activities will be form the local markets.
The project is designed with consideration to the clusters and WHO recommendation on prevention and mitigation measures related to COVID-19 especially during the distribution of support. Also, protection cluster recommendations were taken into consideration, wherein all the population in the targeted locations will benefit from the awareness session of MASRRAT protection team in terms of GBV referral cases, early marriage and child labor risks awareness raising sessions.
The target locations:
Aleppo governorate / Jebel Saman district / Atareb sub-district/

5 - Implementation of activities

We start with the conduct of community mobilization process (including the criteria of purchasing, specification and supported prices) to choose 100 women workers to receive CFW opportunities for sewing 750000 masks. Register and verify of potential beneficiaries, based on registration criteria in the targeted location for CFW activity, as well as the CFW workers. Rent a workshop in the targeted location contains at least eight Sewing machines and purchase of masks materials. Conducting of training to the workers on sewing masks. Conducting of sewing masks process to produce 750000 masks. Distribute the masks to the local beneficiaries. PwD will be encouraged to apply to the masks producing activities (sewing on the sewing machines) when their disability doesn’t prevent them from the work, wherein during the announcement of the activities, PwD (especially PwD who have previous experience in sewing or are able to work in this work) will be addressed and considered during the selection.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

MASRRAT will hire at least one female officer to ensure that vulnerable populations such as women and young girls, along with female-headed households are able to provide information and feedback easily and to deal and reduce any potential GBV cases. MASARAT will assign a special phone to report any harassment or blackmail that will be referred to a special employee to address these issues, and distribution points will be safe and accessible, and special points will be allocated for women and people with special needs. The information-sharing will be conducted by MASRRAT team using 8 group discussions in the community mobilization sessions beside to hang 40 project announcements to provide information about the activities.MASRRAT will provide accessible and functioning feedback/complaint mechanism by proper ways (i.e. Beneficiaries can submit their requests or complaints using mobile phone numbers or the direct contact with the field staff or by using complaints boxes in the distribution points where these boxes will be separated for men and women) or by informing the M&E field officer directly, moreover MASRRAT will facilitate a Help Disk in the area of implementation in order to provide the affected population with all the required information. Wherein, MASRRAT’s MEAL team will conduct 10 sessions to explain the instructions on how to provide feedback to the targeted population providing them with all the methods to complain, all beneficiary data will be kept confidentially and will not be shared for any reason. Feedback and complaints that received will be sent to the accountability focal point in Turkey, and the accountability focal point will enter the FB&Cs in the FB&C database, disaggregate them.
