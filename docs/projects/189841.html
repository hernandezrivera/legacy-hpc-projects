Project Summary

Support 30,000 children affected by insecurity to continue their education by providing school feeding in districts that received most IDPs in Cabo Delgado and Nampula. With a duration of two school trimesters, the project will target children in transitional learning spaces (TLS) and children attending primary schools in host communities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will support 30,000 children affected by insecurity to continue their education by providing take-home rations in learning spaces in Cabo Delgado and Nampula. With a duration of two school trimesters, the project will target 26,000 children in transitional learning spaces (TLS) supported by UNICEF and in primary schools in host communities in the Districts of Pemba City, Metuge, Montepuez and Chiure (Cabo Delgado) and 4,000 in Meconta (Nampula). Targeting has been guided by the number of IDP population in districts and consultations of Provincial Education Directorates. School feeding will contribute to meet the protection and food access needs of displaced children and support them to pursue their education. The project will also assist children in host communities where already scarce resources have been further stretched by the sharp increase in Internally Displaced People (IDPs). Learning spaces will receive handwashing stations to improve WASH conditions, promote the safe resumption of classes, and ensure the safe distribution of food to children. Take-home rations distributions will also be used as a platform to engage with school communities on child protection and to raise awareness about specific protection risks faced by girls, including child marriage. The project will be implemented in partnership with Provincial and District education services that will be involved in school targeting, community mobilisation and participation in distributions. It will also strengthen these services’ capacities to deliver school feeding in emergency conditions that have repeatedly affected these provinces in recent years.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Provide school feeding to children affected by insecurity to meet their nutritional needs and support in continuing their education. Given the infrastructure limitations in learning spaces in terms of access to water and storage capacity, the intervention will provide school feeding in the take-home rations modality. Monthly distributions of food baskets consisting of 5kg of rice, 1kg of beans, 1 l of fortified oil and 1kg of ionized salt will be carried out in learning spaces, in coordination with the school community. In districts where TLS are being created, these spaces will be targeted. In districts where IDPs are integrated into host communities, school feeding will be channeled to established schools, benefiting IDPs and host communities. Distribute WASH materials to learning spaces such as handwashing facilities, to better equip schools for the safe return to school and to enable the safe monthly distribution of take-home rations. Strengthen capacities of local education services, school staff and school council members on the implementation of take-home rations in the context of an emergency. These teams will also be trained on the protection of children, specially of girls, including linking them to essential child protection referral pathways established in the province. Engage caregivers on child protection and prevention of Gender-Based Violence (GBV) by using take-home rations distributions as a platform to raise awareness of caregivers and school community on these themes.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Measures to ensure children’s protection will be integrated into the training provided to school teams and school councils and the communication materials distributed in schools, with focus on protective measures for students receiving take-home rations in the monthly distributions. To ensure children are not exposed to any potential risks during distributions and at the same time ensuring their attendance in classes, parents and caregivers are registered and responsible for collecting the food basket on behalf of their children.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

WFP has been working closely with the local governments in Cabo Delgado and Nampula to assist IDP families and host communities through food assistance. Provincial education services have also been consulted on the proposed geographic targeting for the project. On the behalf of the HCT, WFP led the establishment of the inter-agency tollfree hotline for the humanitarian response, Linha Verde da Resposta a Emergencia (1458) as one channel to receive feedback from communities regarding the response. ‘Linha Verde 1458’ provides an opportunity to beneficiaries to contact WFP directly, to request information or report issues concerning the humanitarian response. ‘Linha Verde 1458’ is free of charge and accessible in the main local languages to ensure accessibility by different populations. Parent Teacher Associations/ School Councils and School management will also be expected to raise any concerns to WFP via the hotline or directly to WFPs Provincial focal point who engages on a regular basis with school management. Communication material will include information on the right to provide feedback through the channels noted above as well as key programme information such as: duration, entitlements, coverage, and targeting criteria to prevent opportunities for abuses of power. Linha Verde 1458 works in close collaboration with the child protection hotline, Linha Fala Criança (116).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

WFP and local education services will work with school communities to identify children with disabilities or whose parents have disabilities to map barriers they may face to benefit from programme assistance and address them accordingly.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The high number of IDPs in programme districts have stressed resources in a region that was already affected by lack of access to water and extreme weather events. Furthermore, with COVID-19 schools are requested to have increased access to water to follow hygiene protocols and ensure a safe learning space.
