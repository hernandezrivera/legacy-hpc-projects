Project Summary

Kassala health Citadel is a new intervention sponsored mainly by the Italian Government and includes some existing facilities in addition to new facilities. In all these facilities there is no proper methodology of waste management especially the medical waste. All existing facilities inside the citadel were rehabilitated and /or renovated. Those existing facilities were under huge pressure after the displacement of citizens from different regions/ states of Sudan e.g. Khartoum, Darfur and lately from Gezira state. In order to help protect the health of vulnerable community in Kassala it is of invaluable importance to provide a proper waste management system. This shall include the provision and installation of a medical waste incinerator that can deal with infectious waste, pathological waste, sharps, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, genotoxic waste, radioactive waste and any non-hazardous waste.
