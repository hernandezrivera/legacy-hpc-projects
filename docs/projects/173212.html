Project Summary

This intervention aims at providing equitable access to protective quality education services to vulnerable crisis-affected boys and girls(3-17) of primary 4 -6 in Monguno and Gwoza Local Government areas (LGAS) of Borno state. This intervention purpose is to enhance access of crisis-affected boys and girls of low incomes households in host communities to equitable protective and quality and inclusive education. AHAI will improve access of crisis-affected boys and girls to equitable education through cash transfer assistance to 20,000 boys and girls of primary 4-6 as per $50 per child for enrollment and supply of learning materials, safe go back to schools awareness campaigns. in addition, the program will improve access to quality education through capacity building of 50 teachers (M/F), supply of teaching materials as well as incentives to 50 teachers (M/F); however, the program shall also strengthen access to protective learning environment through rehabilitation of 10 dilapidated government primary schools and construction of 50 Temporal learning spaces (TLS) at the cost of $4,500 each in mnguno and Gwoza LGAs; in addition; the response shall ensure access to protective learning environment through distribution of PPE to 20,000 learners and 50 teachers in monguno and GWOZA.

1 - Needs

The Education rapid needs assessment conducted by AHAI in October 2020 in selected Local Government Areas (LGAs) of Monguno and Gwoza in 55 households with schooling age boys(3-17) and girls(3-17) and low incomes of crisis-affected boys and girls in 30 primary schools revealed that: 60% vulnerable parents and care givers of crisis-affected boys and girls lack means of livelihoods to enroll their children to schools. of the assessed households, 45% were reported single female headed households with limited access to income generation opportunities; there is need to give grants ($50 ) as per child to households with schooling students. in addition, 67% of boys and girls of assessed households have limited access to learning materials; vulnerable crisis-affected boys and girls need texts books, uniforms and bags. in addition, 30% schools were destroyed in monguno and Gwoza local government areas ( LGAs) of Borno, meaning there is need to rehabilitate dilapidated schools and construct TLs in Monguno and Gwoza to enhance boys and girls access to education. Furthermore, 70% of conflict affected teachers in monguno and Gwoza reported that low pay as they have less than $100 monthly, there is need to provide incentives to crisis-affected teachers, while 34% of interviewed teachers reported the fear to attend classes due to insecurity thus affected the schools results of learners. there is therefore a need to build resilience and access to quality and protective education by building capacities of teachers on Psycho-social ( PSS) and teaching at the right level(TARL).
