Project Summary

This CSP aims to contribute all year-round to: Crisis-affected populations to meet their basic food and nutrition needs during and after the crises; Food insecure school-age children, to access adequate and nutritious food; Vulnerable groups (children, pregnant women and adolescent girls) to have enhanced nutritional status; Food insecure populations and communities to have more resilient livelihoods and sustainable food systems; Capacity strengthening of National institutions; Humanitarian and development partners to access common services unti appropriate an sustainable alternative are available.

1. Besoins
1 - Besoins

The 2022 national nutrition survey based on SMART methodology indicates that the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in Niger was 12.2%, which is slightly above the 10% serious threshold. Analysis show disparities between regions with four regions Maradi (13.6%), Agadez (13.7%) and Tahoua (13.4%) having rates above the national rate (12.2%). In Dosso region, GAM rates vary from 8.1% (Loga) to 16.4% (Dogondoutchi) and with GAM rates above 10% in Dioundiou (10.4%), Tibiri (11.9%) and Dosso department (11.3%). The rate of severe acute malnutrition (SAM), was estimated at 2.4% above the WHO critical threshold of 2% with five regions (Maradi: 3.9%, Zinder: 2.9%, Agadez: 2.2% and Dosso: 2.2% ) having rates above this threshold. Chronic malnutrition remains above the 30 percent "very high" threshold with 47% at national level with all the regions except Niamey (18.5%) having rates far above this threshold with peaks in Maradi (61.7%) and Zinder (51.5%). With the deteriorating securtiy situation in Niger's neighbouring countries (Nigeria, Burkina Faso and Mali), there is a great possibility of split over of these crises in to Niger thus affecting and worsening the actual poor food security and nutrition situations in Maradi, Tahoua, Agadez and Maradi regions due to poor access to these areas. The Nutrition Cluster estimated that some 2,096,715 people require nutritional assistance but will target 1,351,567 persons including 430,487 children suffering from severe acute malnutrition (SAM). Some750,000 children aged 6 to 23 months (boys and girls) suffering from moderate acute malnutrition and 39,318 caregivers (accompanying persons) in the CRENIs will need appropriate nutritional supplementation. A total of 150,448 children 6 to 23 months (boys and girls) will need blanket supplementation to stabilise their nutrition status.
