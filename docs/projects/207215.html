Project Summary

The goal of this project is to improve access to basic social services in health, nutrition and WASH for vulnerable communities affected by population displacements in the Goulfey health district.
ACF has implemented projects in the area of Goulfey and Kousseri, under ECHO, French Embassy and SIDA funding since 2017 and enjoys a great acceptance in the area.
This current project aims to meet the urgent health needs in the Logone et Chari division of Cameroon, through the provision of emergency and quality primary and secondary health care services integrating MHPSS to populations affected by the crisis and particularly children under five and pregnant and lactating women (PLW). A mobile clinic will be deployed in the supported areas providing primary health care to beneficiaries, with a focus on children under 5, PLW, but also delivering services to adults for most common pathologies. ACF integrates into its intervention support for health centers and one district hospital and the implementation of a community approach through identification and training of CHWs. The CHWs will be trained and supervised to provide community management of acute malnutrition without complications. This project will support the rapid response mechanism in the Far North region by deploying its mobile clinic and supporting the health center to preposition stocks, prepare SOPs, train staff and deploying evaluations after the displacements and/ or flooding and rapid health, nutrition and mental health responses, including supplemental vaccination campaigns and distribution of hygienic kits and NFIs.
At the MC site, all children aged 0 to 5 years, PLW and adults will be offered free medical and preventive consultations by qualified medical staff and sensitization awareness sessions will be held by the CHWs and ACF staff. The staff will provide treatment for common childhood illnesses using national guidelines / and standardized IMCI protocols. The immunization status will be assessed and linkage to the EPI program. People living with disabilities will have access to the services by dissemination messages adaptable to their conditions and linkage to specialized services.
Additionally, as preventive consultations, children will be growth monitored, screened for malnutrition using MUAC (6-59 months), weight, size and oedemas, dewormed (12-59 months) and provided with vitamin A supplementation (6-59 months). Children (0-23 months of age) will also benefit from IYCF counselling provided to their mothers and caregivers.
The package of care will also include medical and MHPSS care for GBV victims (including rape clinical case management), especially women and children, and referral to appropriate facilities for those whose post-rape condition requires further in-depth care, including, for example, social reintegration.
A map of GBV actors and services in the various intervention areas will be created or updated based on the map produced by the GBV sub-working group. A referral system will then be set up or integrated in areas where it already exists to facilitate the referral of survivors to other GBV service providers. This referral pathway will be formalized through SOPs.This map of GBV stakeholders and services will be disseminated to project teams in addition to the capacity-building they will receive on GBV, the clinical management of GBV cases and the referencing of GBV cases.Through an active participation in working groups and sub-working groups at the regional and local levels, collaboration with other GBV service providers will be strengthened in order to ensure that the referral mechanisms put in place are properly coordinated.
This project will target all people affected by the crisis: men, women, PLWs, children aged 0-5 and above, adolescents, IDPs, host populations, people living with a disability, the elderly, health staff from supported health centers and district hospital and community actors.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

As of 30th of September 2023 Logone et Chari has registered 11.626 newly internally displaced persons due to the conflict. The persistence of violence is the main cause of displacement, exacerbating the pre-existing level of vulnerability resulting from poverty. According to the Rapid Response Mechanism (RRM), 30,000 people have been newly displaced by insecurity. Three attacks on health facilities have been reported, notably in Oudjila (Mayo-Sava), Goldavi and Ouzal (Mayo-Tsanaga). Despite a reduction of rainfall marking the end of the rainy season, the waters of the Logone river are gradually rising in the departments of Mayo-Danay and Logone et Chari. ACF has conducted an exploratory mission in August 2023 in Goulfey HD (health areas of Hilele, Afadé, Goulfey and Goulfey_Gana) and Kousseri Health District (HD) (especially in health areas of Madiako, Kalakafra and regional Hospital Annex of Kousseri) which has confirmed the prevailing humanitarian needs in the division.
