Project Summary

Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) demonstrates unwavering commitment and active engagement in providing vital emergency support, particularly focusing on Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFI) within Upper Nile, Jonglie, Warrao and Northern BharGhazal states of South Sudan. Our response strategy is founded on our extensive experience in emergency interventions and our ongoing efforts in NFI, Multi-purpose Cash, and Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) programs. With this project, NCA's targeted goal is to assist approximately 58,000 individuals, specifically aiming to reach 18,000 males and 40,000 females within these target areas. Our strategic project focuses on cash voucher assistance, the distribution of shelter materials, and household non-food items, integrating these interventions within our broader emergency response initiatives. Cash voucher assistance allows affected communities to determine by themselves their exact needs at their household level while aiming to reduce the response time. Access to an active market system will allow the affected community to purchase the shelter and household needs. In kind shelter materials and non food items are part of NCA’s stock preparedness to ensure the hard to reach areas and communities affected where there is a non active market still is able to receive protection and preserve their dignity during crisis. By implementing these strategic interventions, our project seeks not only to provide immediate relief but also to instill resilience and contribute to the long-term recovery of communities grappling with these crises. NCA's approach remains guided by the urgency of the situation, ensuring that our interventions are precisely tailored to address the most pressing needs of vulnerable individuals and families. In doing so, we aspire to kindle hope and actively participate in rebuilding lives amid the ongoing crisis in South Sudan

1. Needs

South Sudan faces a worsening humanitarian crisis, with conflict, climate challenges, and displacement pushing 7.1 million people into acute food insecurity by the 2024 lean season. Of these, 79,000 are projected to experience catastrophic conditions. The hardest-hit areas include Jonglei, Unity, Upper Nile, and Lakes states, where the majority of residents need urgent assistance (IPC, 2024).The Sudan conflict has further exacerbated the crisis, displacing over 1 million people to neighboring countries. By November 2024, South Sudan had received 364,818 arrivals, primarily South Sudanese returnees (83%). Most entered through Wunthow/Joda in Upper Nile, Panakuach in Unity, and Kir Adem in Northern Bahr el Ghazal. Overcrowded camps and insufficient resources have led to unsanitary conditions and heightened security risks.A rapid assessment conducted by Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) in November 2024 highlighted critical gaps among returnees. Ninety percent of respondents lacked proper shelter, with inadequate protection against the elements, while 85% reported missing essential kitchen items. Cash-based assistance emerged as the preferred form of support.In response, NCA is targeting 58,500 individuals, including 40,500 women and girls and 18,000 Men and boys, in Northern Bahr el Ghazal, Upper Nile, and Warrap states. The program combines cash vouchers, shelter materials, and household non-food items to address immediate needs. These efforts aim to ease pressures on border regions, where resources are already overstretched due to the influx of displaced people.With decades of experience in South Sudan, NCA remains focused on delivering effective assistance, informed by ongoing assessments and community engagement to adapt to the growing challenges.

2. Project activities

The proposed project will reach 58,500 individuals, prioritizing inclusivity and efficient resource allocation. It will assist 30% women, 20% men, 25% boys, and 25% girls across targeted regions. Some beneficiaries will access multiple forms of assistance to address their diverse needs. Key activities include: Initial Assessments and Verifications: Conducting detailed assessments to identify eligible households and verify their needs.Non-Food Items (NFI) Distribution: Delivering essential household items such as cooking tools, bedding, and hygiene supplies to support immediate recovery.Emergency Shelter Support: Providing materials and technical guidance for constructing temporary shelters to improve living conditions for displaced families.Cash-Based Assistance (CVA): Implementing multi-purpose cash transfers to empower households to address their specific priorities, including food, health, and education. Cash disbursements will use secure mobile platforms or physical vouchers, with clear communication and training to ensure recipients understand how to access and utilize the assistance.Capacity-Building and Training: Offering training sessions on shelter construction, financial management for cash recipients, and effective use of distributed NFIs.Close collaboration with UNHCR and other humanitarian actors ensures efficient coordination and delivery. Emphasis is placed on timely distribution and support for vulnerable groups, including women, children, and those with special needs, ensuring they receive adequate assistance in a manner that respects their dignity. This structured approach focuses on addressing immediate needs while equipping affected communities with tools to regain stability and self-reliance.
