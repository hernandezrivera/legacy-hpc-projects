Project Summary

Through the proposed intervention, TGH will contribute to achieving objective 5 of the 2024 Protection Sector’s Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for Syria through providing quality, integrated and sustainable protection services through community-based approaches to children, young adults and women. Psychosocial support (PSS), including the identification of cases facing protection risks, their access to specialized services and case-management will be proposed. The project will also include awareness raising on a high range of protection concerns such child protection (CP) and risk education (RE) toward explosive hazards. The proposed intervention will be part of an integrated programme linked to Education project in public schools for the school years 2024-2025; which will consist in providing support in the communities given that both sectors’ needs are intertwined. TGH is already implementing CP programs under Education sector in schools in Aleppo, Hama, Idleb, Dar’a and Rural Damascus and aims to scale up its intervention through providing assistance to communities at risk to a total of 7,937 conflict-affected children (3,883 boys, 4054 girls) in Nashabiyeh (4), Kafr Batna (4), Duma (4), Arbin (4), Maliha (4), Markaz Darayya (4) (Rural Damascus), in Jebel Salman (4) (Aleppo), in As-Suqaylabiyah (3), Eastern Bari (4), Suran (3), Hama (3), Madiq Castle (3) (Hama), in Khan Shaykun (3), Ma'arrat AnNu'man (4) and Tamanaah (4) (Idleb) and in Izra’ (3), As-Sanamayn (3), Busra Esh-Sham (4) and Kherbet Ghazala (3) (Dar’a). The intervention will provide approximatively 7,937 children with CP in public schools and 60 adolescents in safe spaces referrals and case management at school and protection awareness campaigns among the parents and the community. These sub-districts were selected based on their level of accessibility and theseverity of needs as per the 2024 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) with all the localities being rated over 3 (66% of level 3, 34% of level 4), TGH 2023 & 2024 field assessments and coordination with municipalities SARC and other partners. TGH already received the approval of the MoE to conduct Protection-related activities within the schools, including case management, as TGH has a daily access to the targeted schools for implementing educational activities. The proposed intervention will consist in:
GP: Providing quality and integrated protection services, with a focus on community based approaches including awareness raising, psychosocial assistance to teachers through schools;
CP: Providing structured PSS to children enrolled in NFE classes based on the national MHPSS manual; providing case management and referrals inside the schools to the most vulnerable children enrolled in TGH's NFE sessions.
Mine Risk Education: Conducting risk education regarding explosive hazards in schools for teachers, education personnel and children;
GBV: Distribution of dignity kits to crossline girl students taking their national exams in centers located in GoS, in coordination with other partners and the MoE/DoE. All girl students enrolled for the exam will be targeted by the distribution.
TGH will consider and ensure gender-and-age aspects across all sub-sectors of focus –CP, GBV, and general protection. Cultural preferences and sensitivities will be considered through consultations with the staff – the majority of whom will be from local community and the affected populations. Specific needs of children, people with disabilities and othervulnerable groups have been considered.
