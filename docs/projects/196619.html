Project Summary

In light of the harsh conditions experienced in Suburbs of Damascus and Rural Damascus Governorate after seven years of war, siege and destruction, health sector in the region has been seriously affected; in addition to the spread of epidemics and diseases.
In view of the large number of inhabitants and the continued displacement to the region, which has generated increased needs.
Suburbs of Damascus and Rural Damascus is an area that has a population of over 5,000,000. Most residents are very poor and internally displaced after seven years of war and siege, resulted in huge number of people who need medical care & services, taking into consideration the limit availability of medicals centers with insufficient medical services.
High percentage of the population are children and families who rely on women as their main breadwinners.
Most people in the area have no capacity to pay for proper health services due to their financial situation.
The project aims to offer free medication with referral system and conduct health raising awareness sessions (children, women and men) in Union’s facilities by specialist doctors.
Health needs
The whole area with a population of over 5000,000 has no enough medical care points. The population have highly vulnerable segments including women dependent families. The provision of free medical services and offer free medication with referral system in addition to conduct health raising awareness sessions is a necessity, taking into consideration the raising medical needs by specialist doctors.
Project Beneficiaries
The following are the main segments of beneficiaries in the area:
1- Internally displaced community: They are very poor with no or very little family support. High percentage of the population are families that are dependent on women as their breadwinners.
2- Vulnerable local and nearby poor population of the area who became unable to bare proper medical services.
The planned number of beneficiaries will be as follows:
- Direct beneficiaries from medical services (referrals) will be 54,600 beneficiaries (with recurrence)
- Indirect beneficiaries: Families of the beneficiaries and their local community

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The risk of transmission of infection from any patient to another patient, such as corona disease, for example. Risk of electrical short circuit or fire. The risk of having a large number of children accompanying patients in the waiting room. Blackouts delaying the provision of a health service, which leads to a worsening of the patient's condition, the presence of a bad clinical condition that requires immediate referral to the hospital. Withdrawal of one of the medical center staff without prior notification. Work injuries, wrong medical diagnosis of a patient. Huge crowding in the center, which leads to the transmission of diseases Carrying out unhealthy habits and spreading them among children in the area.
