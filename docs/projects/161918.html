Project Summary

The project will provide sexual and reproductive health services like ANC, PNC, family planning, STIs, menstrual health management etc. and undertake prevention and response to gender-based violence activities. The project activities are informed by CARE’s on-going gender analysis and operational expertise and are adapted to meet the differing needs of men, women, boys and adolescent girls. It has been observed that there are lowest use of contraceptives, highest maternal mortality rate, significant level of lack of knowledge about sexual reproductive health due to lack of awareness, misconception, sensitization and demand side barriers. CARE proposes an integrated approach to sexual and reproductive health and gender-based violence that will reach 56,837 people (31000 women and 25,837 men) living in Camps 12, 13, 14 and 16 in Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh.

Needs

There are critical gaps in Sexual reproductive health (SRH) care services in the camp. The SRH service seeking behavior is also very low among the displaced population which is worsened by mobility restrictions among women, low knowledge on SRH services with no autonomy over their sexual reproductive health. Adolescent sexual reproductive health is a needed services in curbing the early marriages, morbidities and mortalities associated with unintended pregnancies and its consequences, early pregnancies and childbirth and gender based violence. Inadequate provision and uptake of antenatal care services need to be strengthened to ensure positive pregnancy outcomes and promote facility deliveries. There is still unmet Family planning needs especially of the Long Acting Reversible Contraceptives and low level of sexually transmitted infections prevention and their management.

Activities or outputs

- Offer Antenatal care services, menstrual regulation, postnatal care, family planning, neonatal care, sexually transmitted infection prevention and management and basic basic health services at CARE's run clinics- Offer ASRHR services through WGSS. - Provision of intergrated SRH and GBV services by maintaining minimum standard of health sector of emergency response. - Ensure EMOC services by ensuring ambulance and establish linkage and referral with 24/7 service providing agencies, PHC and field hospitals. - Community engagement and awareness-raising activities will complement service uptake by addressing informational and societal barriers to women’s access to health services. - Provide information through coutyard session, especially to women and girls and men of reproductive age who may otherwise be excluded from information.
