Project Summary

1. SHPDO will Provide 12 Awareness Raising Session Via Local Radios & TV Messages in Each State using the local Languages each state for 4 days per a month on issues related to safety and security of women and girls, general treatment of survivors of violence and access to services and support in proper and timely manner. (Main issues will be discussed are: GBV related issues, early marriage, FGM GBV networks, domestic violence, and violence against sex, psychological support, referral).
2. SHPDO will conuct 12 Mobile awareness providing Covid 19 impacts on GBV Messages in local Markets Days in each State.
3.SHPDO will Conduct 10 TV and Radio Dialogue on psychosocial support and referral pathway for service providers.
4. Procurement and distribution of dignity kits for women and girl of reproductive age in conflicted affected areas.
5. Rehabilitation of 1 women center in each locality to be prepared as a safe space.
6. Life skills activities / trainings: Activities that support the development of "abilities for adaptive and positive behaviour that enable individuals to deal effectively with the demands and challenges of everyday life and are relevant across cultures.
