Project Summary

The project objective is to improve access to inclusive quality basic education for 31,624 girls and 21,083 boys, including children with disabilities, who are affected by conflict and natural disasters in 12 localities in North Darfur, Kassala, North Kordofan and South Kordofan states. This will be accomplished through improving facilities and furnishing schools affected by heavy rains and floods. The school facilities will be designed, constructed, and maintained to be resilient to flooding. The learning will be supported by the provision of teaching and learning supplies, recreational materials, as well as menstrual health and hygiene kits for adolescent girls. Safe water and sanitation facilities will be constructed that are gender sensitive and friendly for children with disabilities and teachers/PTAs will be trained on COIVD-19 School Protocol. Teachers will be trained on Package for Teachers in Crisis Contexts and provided teaching supplies. PTAs will be strengthened to participate in school governance, develop School Improvement Plans, and carry out awareness raising activities on the rights of all children to education and identifying and addressing oppressive norms that hold girls and boys back. The target schools will be linked to child protection, psychosocial services, and child protection referral pathways will be established.

1 - Needs

In North Darfur, South Kordofan, North Kordofan, and Kassala states 784,633 conflict and disaster-affected basic education (primary school) and secondary school-aged children have been identified by Sudan Education Sector in needs humanitarian assistance to full their right to education. Plan International Sudan will target 52,707 internally displaced and non displaced vulnerable primary school-aged children (31,624gils and 21,083 boys) in 4 states of North Darfur, South Kordofan, North Kordofan, and Kassala, with detailed as follows:- North Darfur: 19,032 internally displaced (11,419 girls and 7,613 boys) in basic schools in localities of Melit, Kotum, Kebkabiya. - South Kordofan: 11,279 internally displaced and vulnerable non displaced children (6,767 girls and 4,512boys) of basic education.- North Kordofan: 8,732 of vulnerable non displaced children (5,239 girls and 3,493 boys) of basic education- Kassala State : 13,664 non-displaced vulnerable children (8,198 girls and 5,466 boys) in basic schools of 3 localities of Rei Hamashkureib, Rei Nahr Atbara, and Rei Shamal Adelta.Plan International Sudan identified the target by this response according different needs assessment conducted by Plan and other partners and in consultation with state ministries of education in target states, as well considering Education Sector Severity ranking as the project target localities ranked very high and high ( 4 and 3 ).Children’s education in the target localities are challenged by poor access, poor retention and poor learning outcomes resulting from complex and mutually reinforcing patterns of disadvantage including: poverty, social norms that lead to gender inequities in education, disability, conflict, and displacement. The majority of target schools are in rural areas and already suffer from a shortage of clean water and lack gender-sensitive sanitation facilities, which affects both attendance and learning. The quality of education is generally poor with many children in grade 3 struggling to read simple familiar words and less than half comprehending what they read. (Ministry of Education, 2018 National Learning Assessment report). School environments are often unsafe, with cases of bullying, gender-based violence, abuse, and corporal punishment happening in schools. Additionally, due to COVID-19, floods and the instability of schools in Sudan, it is expected that the most vulnerable children, particularly children living in areas affected by conflict, will not be able to return to school or to progress in school unless significant support is provided to mitigate against this long absence from school.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Plan International maintains high standards of Safeguarding and PSHEA (preventing sexual harassment, exploitation and abuse), more-so in emergency situations. Plan’s Safeguarding Policy and procedures are implemented during the design and implementation of project interventions with additional vigilance and we adapt and strengthen existing interventions to identify and reduce risks to girls, boys, children, young people and beneficiaries. Safeguarding and PSHEA risk analysis and assessment of project interventions will be conducted with mitigation measures/contingency plans identified for each risk. Plan will work to ensure staff, partners, and beneficiaries and communities are aware of the safeguarding policy, its minimum codes of behavior, and requirements to uphold high standards of professional behavior. The implementing partners were assessed to ensure the adequacy of the partner’s safeguarding and PSHEA policies and guidelines. Mitigation strategies take account of particular risks or vulnerabilities associated with girls, boys, children and young people. Children and young people are respected and their safety and protection is not compromised when obtaining and using information on and images of them.
