Project Summary

• Rehabilitation and disinfection of 27 existing water points: the activity will target affected wells located in Matemo, Ibo and Quirimba Islands. Three main intervention solutions have been identified based on wells characteristics: 1) open wells: increment of wells depth, cleaning of the bottom and the walls, closing and installation of a manual pump; 2) improved wells without pump: improvement of the covering and installation of a manual pump; 3) improved wells with pump: substitution of the broken manual pump. Rehabilitation and disinfection of 15 existing water points affected by flooding: the activity will be implemented among the 10 selected Aldeias in Metuge district, considering: i) open wells; ii) improved wells without pump; iii) improved wells with non-functioning handpump.
• Development of 2 existing water points in Matemo Island: the majority of water in Matemo is breakish water (wells conductivity ≥ 1,500 µS/cm). Based on a survey conducted in June and July 2019 Oikos has identified 2 wells with low conductivity (0,66 mS and 1,3 mS). The project will allow to develop those wells in order to provide good quality water to the population of Ngamba and Palussança villages. Intervention will include the rehabilitation and cleaning of the well, creation of a coverage, installation of a pumping system, reservoir and distribution point. Development of 2 new well in Nancaramo (8 m) and 2 new boreholes in Nanlia (40m). These Aldeias suffer from water scarcity from long time. The floods caused by the cyclone exacerbate this situation. Particularly in the primary school in Nanlia, where the water is missing during most of the dry season.
• Solid waste management (including collection, removal, disposal and drainage) in Ibo Island to reduce health and environmental risks: the intervention will consist of cleaning campaigns to reduce waste accumulate due to effects of the cyclone (ruins, garbage, trees), improve the condition of dumping site effected by the cyclone and setting up a pilot for the implementation of a sustainable waste management system in Ibo Island.
• Promotion activities for self-construction of 100 household latrines and garbage pits/ sludge drying beds for sludge disposal and treatment. Activity will target the already identified most vulnerable families / households in Ibo, Matemo and Quirimba Islands.
• Community and household level hygiene promotion activities in Ibo District and related institutions in order to reinforce all the above activities.
• Installation of rain water harvestin systems in 7 schools: Schools are often built far from the centres and do not have access to enough water all along the year. This solution will give extra water during the dry season (20,000 litres each school).
• Realizatioin of 10 protected laundry areas: Often these space are missing or located close to unused wells. The activity will involve the whole community and is aimed to improve hygiene and to create a safe place for womens cleaning their laundry.
