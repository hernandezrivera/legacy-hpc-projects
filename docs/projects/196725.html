Project Summary

Through this project, Violet aims to establish Camp Management Mobile Team to Build the Capacity of Site Representatives and improve living conditions by implementing minor infrastructure and technical maintenance in 12 IDPs Sites in Northwest Syria.
The intervention aims to is to ensure the capacity of the targeted 12 IDPs sites in order to continue the provision of the humanitarian assistance in the targeted IDPs sites and access for humanitarian response by enhance the capacity and knowledge of the camps management about the standards and guidelines for the activities related to the CCCM includes (but not limited) coordination mechanism in accordance to SPHERE standards, dealing with the disasters and reducing the negative impact of it such as fire incidents and the first aid sessions.

3 - Needs

A large proportion of IDP sites in Northwest Syria are informal settlements, also known as “spontaneous” or “self-established” camps. These usually consist of groups of tents or other shelters that were set up by IDPs themselves or other actors. Permission to use the site chosen is usually informal and requires renegotiation. They are usually too dense and sometimes too large, requiring phased upgrading to meet international standards and local and international good practices, including introducing firebreaks, surface water drainage, and infrastructures such as schools, distribution centers, water supplies, and recreational areas. Crucially, these sites are not managed by anyone and often have no legal claim to the land on which they were established. In these sites, the delivery of dignified protection and multi-sectoral assistance cannot be guaranteed by humanitarian actors, a critical issue for vulnerable groups, and particularly for women- and child-headed households. Planned camps, on the other hand, are managed by accountable humanitarian organizations that are responsible for these services. The same applies to transit and reception centers, which are temporary structures established for very short-term displacements. Accordingly, Violet is aiming to support the camp management in 12 informal settlements in Northwest Syria through CCCM mobile teams with a particular focus on CCCM approaches and community participation.

4 - Implementation of activities

Violet will start implementing the project by reaching out to the agencies which are managing the targeted camps in order to coordinate the access to the IDPs site and initiate the discussion process with the Camp Management Team inside each site and discuss the project approach and the planned achievement during the project implementation.The activities which will be carried out during the project duration are as following:1. Violet will coordinate with all concerned stakeholders in order to obtain the needed documents and inform them about the project. 2. Establish CCCM mobile team that will be responsible for covering all the identified sites and conduct online CCCM, Sphere Standards, disaster risk reduction and community mobilization; fire safety and first aid training and practical training on fire safety.3. Conduct detailed assessments for the 12 targeted IDP sites in Northwest Syria in order to identify gaps and existing capacities of camp management and fire safety in coordination with the MEAL team and come up with detailed response plan.4. Upon the completion of the assessments in the IDP sites, Violet will establish site management committees in a participatory approach through nomination or election and conduct focus group discussions with the camp management to discuss the TOR for the Site Management Committee. Each site management committee will consist of 12-28 member.5. Conduct minor infrastructure and technical maintenance proposed by the Site Mana

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Violet has a protection department and is a member of the protection clusters and sub-clusters. Violet will establish CCCM Committees in IDPs sites as the main activity in order to increase community engagement and ownership, they will be selected from volunteers from men, women and PWDs according to elections in the camps. Violet takes into consideration while implementation all protection risks as well as mitigation measures to PSEA and child safeguarding. Avoid any crowdedness and keep the social distance between the beneficiaries. Moreover, hire female staff to respect the dignity and privacy of the beneficiaries. Violet adapts the complaints and feedback mechanisms according to type of project. Violet will conduct an accountability survey about the services provided, where it is not only focused on the services but also PSEA related and the ways they see fit to communicate with them. Violet's PSEA and safeguarding focal point will receive the complaints via the PSEA email or WhatsApp. These will be immediately reported to the protection coordinator and Violet CEO will nominate an investigation committee. The committee will conduct a risk assessment mitigation measures and investigation plan.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: most vulnerable groups such as women and PWDs are not involved in the project.Likelihood; Medium.Impact: Medium Mitigation measure: Violet is carefully and thoroughly integrating protection mechanisms to understand the needs and risks of the target group and to address those sufficiently. To achieve gender equality and to ensure gender mainstreaming, Violet is integrating gender mainstreaming throughout the whole project cycle including needs and context assessment, participatory planning and design, implementation as well as monitoring and reporting. Through that, Violet is ensuring the involvement of women at all time as well as addressing their gender particular needs thoroughly. In the project at hand, this mainly includes the involvement of female field enumerators to ensure that both male and female KIIs – or HHs, if applicable – are able to adequately communicate and interact with the Sites Monitoring team. Risk 2: IDPs that have fled to areas where CCCM members are not present are ignored, which may lead to lack of minimum life-saving assistance and services provision for displaced IDPs.Likelihood; Medium.Impact: Medium Mitigation measures: Encourage all humanitarian responders to contribute to the collective IDP tracking.
