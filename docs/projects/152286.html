NARRATIVE ON NEEDS OF THE FOURTH LGEA (MADAGALI, MICHIKA, MUBI NORTH, MUBISOUTH) Adamawa State is among the three states affected by the Boko Haraminsurgency which led to the declaration of Emergency in 2015 for Borno, Adamawa andYobe Staets. The destruction made by the insurgency has affected seven Localgovernment Areas in Adamawa they include Madagali, Michika, Mubi-North, Mubi South,Maiha, Gombi and Hong. The most destroyed LGEAs where Boko Haram occupied for overeight Months and destroyed every social amenities, killed lives in Madagali, Michika, MubiNorth and Mubi South LGAs. The communities left their areas and run to safe areas likeYola, Numan, Toungo as IDPs needs of the communities as they return includeclassrooms as schools were destroyed. Learning materials, instructional material, books,chalk, desk Teachers chairs and tables, exercise books, erasers etc where lacking in all theschools. In Madagali and Michika pupils at both Primary and JSS are learning under treesand seating on stores hence need child friendly environment. The teachers aretraumatized and required psychosocial training, Classroom Management and improvisionof constructional Materials, the urgent need for such capacity building cannot be overemphasized. The SBMC need to be reactivated so that they take the ownership and goodgovernance, School Development plan is needed in rehabilitation of the schools.Therefore 40 Primary School and ten JSS will be covered under this project as follows:- 1.Madagali LGEA 25 Pry and 3 JSS 2. Michika LGEA 15 Pry and 3 JSS 3. Mubi Morth LGEA5 Pry and 2 JSS 4. Mubi South LGEA 5 Pry and 2 JSS TOTAL 40 Pry JSS 10 = 50 SchoolsOn the enrollment of Pupils especially girls most of the parents are traumatized and areafraid to send their wards some parents engage their wards on street hawking, hence thecash transfer will enable the parents to send their girls to schools.
