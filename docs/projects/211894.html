Project Summary

There is an acute shortage of Emergency, Shelter and NFI support for 6.8 Million out of which, 2.9 Million resides in Aleppo. The latest HNO 2024 shows that PINs severity levels for shelter are at 3% stressed, 70% severe and 27% extreme. Like wise NFI reported 30% stressed, 55% severe and 15% extreme. A vast majority (90%) reported unaffordability to cope with SNFI needs, coupled with inadequate shelter. As informed by HNO and through our surveys, there has been an increase of 19% from 2023 to 2024.
The proposed action will provide response to emergency SNFI needs caused by conflict related displacement, earthquake and other shocks and protect the wellbeing and thermal comfort of highly vulnerable IDPs and host communities in NWS, Aleppo Governorate. We
plan to focus on 4 sub-districts (Afrin, Al-Bab, Mare’ and Suran). The planned programming intends to be achieved through an integrated program of assistance focused on providing targeted, mixed modality CVA assistance to the most vulnerable host and IDP households (HHs) in areas with greatest severity of winter need. - Winter Response planned to target 15040 HHs for the winter response component and seasonal and supplementary NFIs by implementing CVA for one time (valued at $180 each for HHs).
Shelter Response (Target 350 HHs) – For Shelter Response, we intend to reach out to 350 most vulnerable HHs (3,850 beneficiaries), in need of dignified shelter. The assistance would be direct contracting assistance, which is expected to generate gainful
employment for able men and women as well. Modality – We will utilize a mix modality CVA to achieve the targets set for
2024. Primary modality will be CVA through provision of a onetime Winter Assistance valued at USD 180 per HH. Households that have received CVA assistance will not receive in-kind assistance from any donor. The CVA would be for targeted winter response items. Direct
assistance will be provided for Shelter Assistance by deployment. The selection of beneficiaries will be based on our already developed vulnerability criteria considering protection issues as well. Data Collection and Monitoring - The monitoring will be through our well defined M&E systems. Monitoring will be diligently executed starting from Verification, Distribution Monitoring, Post Distribution Monitoring and Satisfaction Surveys. The data will be collected online, primarily using tablets and Kobo tools. We deploy several tools to
include Excel and SPSS to analyze and report. A robust system is in place for complaints receipt and redressal, with dedicated teams available for handling and resolving complaints. A robust system of lodging complaints is already in place through communications tools such as WhatsApp, SMS and email. Further, the complainant can lodge a complaint via hard copy and drop it in the drop box at our office locations. Reporting – We will ensure prompt reporting on all of our projects through regular filing of donor and 4W reports and ad hoc reporting requirements.
Big Heart coordinated with other actors before design of the program. The coordination was done with humanitarian actors in Afrin, along with AFAD and Local Councils.
CVA Support Systems – A fully automated and online Cash Voucher System developed internally and utilized for last 6 years will be deployed. The system is flexible to use paper vouchers or cards and can be of single or multiple use. To complement our voucher
programs, we have framework agreements with Fund Service Providers that provide cash distributions through formal arrangements. Due to this system, our response time for emergencies range from 5 – 7 days from the onset of emergency.
