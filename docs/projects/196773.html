Project Summary

INSAN aims through the proposed project to continue the provision of preventive and curative nutrition services for IDPs in the camps in Maaret Tamasrin sub-district which ranked with high severity according to HNO 2023 PIN severity scale . Two mobile clinics with the following HeRAMS codes: (G0700149 & G0700150 cover 85 camps (including 15074 HHs with 76795 individuals) in Maaret Tamasrin sub-district in the communities: Kelly, Kafr – Kafrehmul, Maaret Elekhwan , Kaftin , Haranbush , Sheikh Baher , Kafr Jales , Kafr Nabi , and Ma'arrat Tamasrin.
These clinics have been established to reach underserved communities who face challenges to access health & nutrition services due to distance and transportation difficulties, especially children, women, and people with disability.
Team consists of 2 GP-2 midwives-2 lab technicians and 2 CHWs based in the mobile clinics and four rapid response teams consisting of (a field coordinator - 2 nutritionists and 2 CHWs -1 data entry ) will provide the following services :
- assessment for PLWs and children under 5 by MUAC and Z- scores.
- Screening PLW on infant feeding difficulties and provide facility and community-based skilled counseling on WHO recommended Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies (IYCF-E) and maternal nutrition practices, health, and WASH-related topics inside the mobile clinics and through community outreach activities in camps, schools , mosques....etc
- Providing micronutrient supplementation to PLW, children U5 and adolescent girls to prevent iron deficiency anemia.
- providing supplementary feeding including LNS and HEB .
- referral complicated SAM cases and provision of therapeutic for SAM and MAM cases among CU5 and PLW.
- Training and capacity building for the staff on basic nutrition skills, IYCF and CMAM.
- integrating Cash for Nutrition using the cash plus approach in line with Nutrition Sector CVA guideline.
The mobile clinics will move weekly between the camps to reach all CU5 and PLW in the targeted camps.
INSAN will submit 4w report on a monthly basis and Coordinate with the Nutrition cluster and other organizations working in the same area to avoid any overlapping.
INSAN will coordinate with the Referral system to refer complicated cases to stabilization centers for treatment.
INSAN will coordinate with the health and RH technical working group and follow up the standards and guidance related to RH during the implementation the project.
INSAN will coordinate with the Nutrition cluster, FSL cluster, and Cash working group to ensure adherence to the guidance on any CVA for Nutrition intervention in NWS.

3 - Needs

The project was accepted in 2022-2023 HRP .7,835 CU5 and PLW have benefited from nutrition services.71 CU5 and PLW with MAM or SAM cases referred to centers for treatment. 232 individual and group sessions were conducted to 998 beneficiaries.According to the SMART survey conducted in June 2021, the prevalence of chronic malnutrition among children aged 6-59 months increased from 19% to 24.5 % between May 2019 and June 2021 in Northwest Syria in Aleppo and Idlib governorates. The prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition GAM increased from 0.9 percent to 4.7 percent over the same period. And the combined GAM prevalence was 6.0%. Among PLWs, the prevalence of acute malnutrition across NW Syriais 12.4 percent. There are also significant differences across NWSyria, Idleb more than Aleppo. According to the same survey, (IYCF) practices were found to be suboptimal. The high burden of stunting in children is linked to sub-optimal uptake of appropriate IYCF practices, which are crucial for healthy child development and survival. Only 53.4% of children under 6 months are exclusively breastfed, while only 10.8% consume the Minimal Acceptable Diet (MAD) in NWS. Micronutrient deficiency is another major problem; NWS 2021 SMART survey showed that the anemia rate was 53.8% among targeted WRA women of reproductive age (15 -49 years) and 46% among children 6 – 59 months.According to nutrition PIN severity scale, Maaret Tamasrin severity score is 4, and severity stunting is 4.
