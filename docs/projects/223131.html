Project Summary

The intervention "Strengthening Community Resilience and Enhancing Protection for Children and Women Impacted by Conflict in BAY States" is designed to address the vulnerabilities of children and women affected by ongoing conflicts in the BAY (Borno, Adamawa, Yobe) states. This initiative focuses on enhancing protection mechanisms and building community resilience to safeguard these at-risk populations effectively. A primary goal is to establish a robust Protection Information Management system to timely assist those in danger by compiling and analyzing data that informs stakeholders, aiding in the strategic optimization of response efforts.
Furthermore, the project emphasizes the importance of enhancing community-based protection mechanisms by empowering local structures to actively participate in crafting protection solutions. This is achieved by fostering social cohesion through structured dialogue forums, ensuring the community's involvement in peace-building and conflict resolution. In addition, collaborating with national actors to incorporate protection measures in development programs and creating comprehensive, one-stop centers offering protection services are key components of the initiative aimed at providing lasting solutions for those affected.
Several activities shall be implemented to meet these goals, beginning with Protection Monitoring and Reporting, which involves partnering with community based child Protection Networks to assess and report on protection trends and vulnerabilities. Concurrently, the intervention focuses on Protection Information Sharing and Awareness by designing informative campaigns and engaging the community through interactive sessions to raise awareness of protection risks.
Capacity building for partners and duty bearers is also prioritized, with workshops aimed at local authorities and leaders to instill effective protection strategies. Specialized Protection Services shall be provided through case management systems offering psychosocial support and legal assistance, alongside establishing referral systems for specialized protection services. Moreover, support for Community-Based Protection Networks is fortified, particularly in Borno and Adamawa States, stimulating local structures to address protection challenges through community-driven action plans.
Additionally, the initiative integrates livelihood and skills acquisition activities to enhance economic resilience. Livelihood training is incorporated alongside protection management, and there is collaboration with the Early Recovery and Livelihood Sector to align these efforts with broader initiatives. Through these comprehensive actions, the intervention seeks to foster a safer environment and empower communities in the conflict-impacted BAY States.

1 - Needs

The project targets key needs by aligning its activities with goals to enhance protection and resilience in vulnerable populations. The initiative aims to improve Protection Information Management by creating a strong data collection system, enabling timely aid for at-risk individuals through regular analytical reports. The project aims to empower local community structures and strengthen Community-Based Protection Mechanisms by fostering participation in protection solutions through organized dialogues. This approach enhances social cohesion and ensures communities actively contribute to relevant, accepted protection strategies (IASC, 2018). The project focuses on sustainable protection solutions by collaborating with national stakeholders to embed these measures into development initiatives. It establishes one-stop centers for comprehensive and accessible support services in partnership with institutional providers, easing access and enhancing service delivery (Sphere Project, 2011). The project uses Protection Monitoring and Reporting to systematically track protection trends, establishing a strategic basis for interventions. This aids in generating detailed reports that highlight key issues and guide decisions on protective measures. The project addresses information sharing and awareness through campaigns focused on risks from non-state armed groups, livelihood safety, and flood preparedness. Using educational materials and workshops tailored to the target audience improves impact by conveying vital information effectively (IFRC, 2015). To bolster local capacity for managing protection issues and promote sustainability, this project holds workshops to improve stakeholder collaboration. Training on protection strategies and psychosocial support is provided to partners, duty bearers, local authorities, and community leaders (IASC, 2010). Specialized Protection Services ensure individuals receive tailored aid, emphasizing the importance of customized support like case management, psychosocial and legal assistance. Providing dignity kits and referral networks addresses broad needs for at-risk people, offering comprehensive protection (OCHA, 2020). Supporting Community-Based Protection Networks is vital for strengthening systems that tackle protection challenges, especially in Borno and Adamawa states. This involves engaging communities to collaboratively create action plans for ongoing dialogue and proactive involvement (UNICEF, 2014). Livelihood and Skills Acquisition Activities are vital for merging economic resilience with protection case management. By supporting broader initiatives like the Early Recovery and Livelihood Sector, this project helps displaced populations gain skills that promote economic independence, enhancing self-reliance (UNDP, 2016).

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The project will implement several modalities to ensure protection mainstreaming. Protection Monitoring and Reporting will involve coordination with stakeholders like CPAoR and PSNE to strategically place monitoring efforts, generating comprehensive reports on protection trends and vulnerabilities. Information sharing and awareness campaigns will target protection risks associated with non-state armed groups, livelihood safety, and flood preparedness, with direct engagement through face-to-face sessions. Capacity building will be achieved through workshops with partners, community-based child protection committees (CBCPCs), and duty bearers, emphasizing stakeholder collaboration and psychosocial support. Specialized protection services will include case management systems offering psychosocial support, legal assistance, and dignity kits, along with referral systems for specialized needs. Community-based protection networks will be supported by strengthening local structures and facilitating engagement through collaborative action plans. Finally, livelihood and skills acquisition activities will integrate economic resilience building, coordinated with broader initiatives by the Early Recovery and Livelihood Sector.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

To ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members, our project employs several strategic components. Protection Monitoring and Reporting (1.1) involves coordinating with CPAoR and PSNE to strategically position monitoring efforts, ensuring these efforts are focused where they are most needed. Additionally, comprehensive reports (1.2) are generated to identify key protection trends and vulnerabilities. Through Protection Information Sharing and Awareness (2), we design campaigns and conduct face-to-face sessions (2.2), directly engaging the community. Capacity Building (3) includes workshops (3.1) to enhance local authorities' and leaders' ability to gather and understand community feedback. In providing Specialized Protection Services (4), we implement systems for case management (4.1), ensuring individual needs and feedback are directly addressed. Support to Community Based Protection Networks (5) and Livelihood Activities (6) emphasize strengthening existing community structures, facilitating continuous engagement, and integrating economic resilience programs to capture and respond to feedback effectively.
