Project Summary

The overall objectives of this project is to enhance women participation and leadership in humanitarian response, provide awareness on GBV issues to promote protection of women and break the culture of silence in the 7 identified project sites ( IDP camps) in Sanaag, Sool and Togdheer and and create platforms and linkages with key stakeholders on GBV issues
The ProJet will contribute to the cluster objective through establishing/promoting community-based GBV/gender mechanisms in support of timely service delivery and prevention of GBV, provision of material assistance, promoting social-economic reintegration, access to justice, and empowerment and finally the project will raise community awareness of protection issues.
Protection will be cutting across all aspects of the project stages. ActionAid will ensure protection is taken into consideration during program designing and implementation as well as in the overall operation. ActionAid staff working in the project will be reoriented on protection issues at the field level and at program level.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1: Some of the religious leaders are not supportive and have different opinions on the issue• Organize different religious sects to ensure they have common understanding on the issue• Awareness raising that GBV is a national phenomena that is affecting the communities at large• Exposure visits to countries that made progress on similar cases Risk 2: The survivors not visiting the stop center due to cultural issues• Community awareness raising on the functions of the stop center• Address cultural barriers that survivors share their problems with the service providers in the centers• Train stop center attendants to encourage survivors to visit the center Risk 3: Lack/inadequate female legal advocates• Establishment of women and children desks in the police stations• Train police and prosecutors on GBV case management and investigation, women’s rights Risk 4: Survivors become voluntary relocations or refuse giving information• Attain full information, including contacts, during registration processes• Increase awareness on the cultural related stereo types. Risk 5: Lack of access to conflict areas (GP)• Mobilize and use traditional elders to facilitate access to affected communities• Conflict resolutions trainings to youth groups, CSOs and women groups on skills of conflict management/resolutions and peace building
