Project Summary

Improvement of WASH services to conflict affected IDP is continued to be top priority for protracted IDP gathering Schools under the continued humanitarian crisis in North Darfur, NOHS will support water, hygiene and sanitation program in Tawila locality and Malite. The main objective of the project is to reduce the vulnerability of displaced people and host communities to water in targeted areas, prioritizing the most vulnerable groups including women and children particular girls. This will be achieved through: improved hygiene knowledge and practices, increased access to safe drinking water and increased access to cultural- and gender-sensitive sanitation facilities. The project activities will include water quality and quantity visits, rehabilitation of water sources to motorized water scheme, rehabilitation and construction of latrines, provision of hygiene kits, regular hygiene promotion sessions, and spraying campaigns for vector control. The expected results of the project are decreased risk of water related diseases, improved hygiene and decreased incidence of diarrheal diseases.
This project will be implemented through a well-established local partner, in continuous coordination and close monitoring with sector partners. The proposed response will fill outstanding gaps in the targeted IDP and host community areas and, supporting the needs of the affected population to reduce vulnerability and maintain human dignity.
Intervention strategies will work to increase the capacities of local communities to ensure they can effectively manage WASH facilities. Gender equality considerations will be incorporated throughout detailed project design and implementation strategies, including emphasis placed on ensuring the participation of women and children as well as girls in appropriate activity design { gender-sensitive latrines} and engagement in leadership roles by representing in WASH committees in camps and gathering areas.
