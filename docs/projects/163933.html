Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls, women, and men, with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities with attention to new IDPs in northwest Syria, targeting (65000 women, 62000 boys, 65000 girls, 28000 men) including all age groups; children, adolescences, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults.
This will be done through specific interventions focusing on:
• Provision of essential services to cover the health care needs of vulnerable populations in targeted communities, including Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, and Child Health (RMNCH), as well as continuity of treatment for CD and NCD. these services will provide through:
1- one maternity and child hospital in Salqin (G0703109)
2- one PHC center level 3 in Afrin, (B0203052)
3- one PHC center level 2 in Atareb (G0703109)
4- and four mobile clinics, (Temp code)
Providing gynecological and obstetrics services, address emergency obstetric and newborn care cases (B&CEmONC), post-surgical care for mothers and other women, and providing incubator services for newborn,
These services will provide through Save the Soul hospital in Salqin in Idlib governorate.
• Ensuring comprehensive general health services and referral pathways for acute and post-acute management phases through one General Hospital located in Harim –Idlib. (G0705021)
• Support and operate Swasia center (B0203062) for prostheses, orthosis, and physical rehabilitation in Afrin (Manufacturing and installation of artificial limbs and splints, Providing physiotherapy services, with include educating the beneficiaries and their caregivers about the beneficiaries’ physical disabilities and impairments, tailored facility-based as well as home exercises, also donate of assistive devices and mobility aids, provide psychosocial support services and counseling to patients to suit their needs.
• Expanding access to specialized services for hostility-affected populations, including Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) services through:
1. Support and operate an Inpatient specialized MHPSS health facility in Afrin, in Aleppo (Temp code).
2. Integrate MHPSS services including MHgap intervention and focused - non-specialized services by PSWs within SDI health facilities, also at the community levels in Atareb, Afrin, Salqin, Marret Tamsrin, Harim subdistricts
• Providing blood products that are very important to save patients' lives who have major surgeries, acute hemolytic anemia, acute hemorrhages, chronic anemia, thalassemia, etc, for Afrin health facilities.
• Disseminate and promote health-seeking and basic hygienic behaviors, 20 CHWs will provide services at the community level.
• Integrate nutrition and health activities through running RRTs to respond to the new IDPs

4 - Needs

Prolonged conflict in Syria has affected many people being exposed to extreme levels of violence, most of them lost their livelihoods and their homes, which led to a significant deterioration in mental and psychological health. Further, people with disabilities are disproportionately impacted by the ongoing conflict,Many of these families have been displaced multiple times before, depleting any existing resources that they may have had, rendering them extremely vulnerable with no option to return to their home;Moreover, displacement patterns indicate that the majority of newly displaced people have moved to areas that are already densely-populated by people displaced into these areas earlier and are themselves in urgent need;The last two months about 520,000 people – around 80% of whom are women and children – have been displaced in northwest Syria.This project will target six subdistricts, and the number of new IDPs arrived at these subdistricts as following: Afrin 49,211, Atareb 27,290, Jandairis 14,698, Salqin 22,349, Maaret Tamsrin 59,496, and Harim 7,717 according to last update of CCCM 02/02/2020. this project support specific health services or facilities in the targeted location; Save the soul hospital for child and maternity car is the only CeMONC facility in Salqin subdistrict and provide 4500 OPD consultations, 400 deliveries (Vaginal and C-section), 350 admissions for neonate, children, and women Prostheses & Orthosis and physical rehabilitation services center is the only center that provides such services in the Afrin district, it provides 30 artificial limps, 500 physiotherapy sessions, 50 Assistive devices per month, where SDI support this facility since August 2019. also, the project will support the only functioning blood bank in the Afrin district where SDI supports this facility since June 2019. According to the mental health working group of health cluster, there is no inpatient mental health facility in the Afrin district.

5 - Implementation of activities

SDI will implement this health program for people in need in seven subdistricts Afrin, Atareb, Jandairis, Salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, and Harim subdistrictsThrough the following: Two PHC, one PHC center level 3 in Afrin and one PHC center level 2 in Abin Saman in Atareb subdistricts, functioning daily 8 hours per dayTwo mobile clinics, one in Atareb subdistrict and one in Salqin subdistrict to provide health services for IDPs in the camps, and other IDPs settlements. Save the soul hospital for child and maternity care in Salqin subdistrict, functioning 24/24 to provide (B&CEmONC) services, the hospital has many departments OPD, ER, IPD hospitalization for child and women, incubators, labour and delivery, and operations departments, as well as, laboratory and pharmacyThe General hospital in Harim subdistrict, functioning 24/24 to provide trauma management for injuries and acute medical emergencies required immediate surgical intervention (Appendicitis, Perforated Ulcer, Arterial Injuries (Artery ligation, and advanced trauma life support; Fractures Fixation, Tracheostomy, head Injuries Management, Chest Drainage, abscess Drainage, Splenectomy, intestine Anastomosis, and thoracotomy, the hospital consist of ER, operations, hospitalization, outpatient departments, as well as laboratory, pharmacy, and basic radiology services.Swasia center for prostheses, orthosis, and physical rehabilitation in Afrin (Manufacturing and installation of artificial limbs and splints, Providing physiotherapy services, also donate of assistive devices and mobility aids.Inpatient specialized MHPSS health facility in Afrin, in AleppoIntegrate MHPSS services including MHgap intervention and focused - non-specialized services by PSWs within SDI health facilities, also at the community levels in Atareb, Afrin, Salqin, Marret Tamsrin, Harim subdistricts, in each facility, there are two PSWs male and female to respond to the need for both genders.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Some women and adolescent girls, especially those who may have physical limitations and/or are socially isolated, such as divorced women and widows, may not be able to access basic services. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigation measure: more female CHWs will be trained to provide services at the community level and mobile clinics will visit the targeted communities to facilitate access to services and refer cases to receive appropriate and specialized care, female staff is about 70%, that increases access of women and girls to health services.Risk related to GBV on CHWs when visiting the homes, also risks of GBV on beneficiaries and health staff in health facilities Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High, Mitigation measure: each two CHWs will work together, training of health staff on providing first-line support, identification of GBV cases, safe referrals, and ensuring access of the affected population to the Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) for reproductive health, Identification of safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBV cases connecting health services and clinical management with social services, Risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers of women and girls, Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High, Mitigation measure: Establish a confidential complaint mechanism, including victim assistance, train staff on the code of conduct, including the code of conduct of all staff contracts and address any SEA complaints, SDI is a member of PSEA network. Risk of discrimination or if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others. Likelihood: Low, Impact: High Mitigation measure: the project design encompasses accessibility of services by vulnerable groups (physical accessibility, disaggregated areas for women and girls, availability of female staff, Signboards) with effective community-based complaints mechanisms.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption:All beneficiaries especially those who may have physical limitations are able to receive services.Risk: Physical accessibility difficulties Mitigation: SDI planned activities meet specific needs to enable access for all children with disabilities to services in accordance with their specific situation, SDI had modified the health facility including ramp and entrance with handles, WC for disabled.Assumption: Access to targeted areas remains possible and safe.Risk1: Access to targeted areas becomes hindered or insecure, Increased checkpoints and road closures Mitigation: Monitor the security instances in the targeted locationand the routes leading to the area to ensure staff access. If the security situation changes in a certain area, we can wait or postpone activities for a few days or change targeted areas to another accessible area in need of support. Plan B: Relocatee the activities to a more safe area Assumption :No more deteriorating security situationRisk: Increase influxes of IDPs result from more escalation in South Idlib Mitigation: SDI always increase the operational capacity and provide more medical drugs and consumables through a private fund to respond to the extra need for most vulnerable peopleMonitoring:- Observational checklists/ spot checks (once-per-month) - Beneficiary satisfaction surveys - Photos/ Videos: (During the field visit)- beneficiaries Logs Monitoring: (During the field visit)The reporting will be according to guidelines agreed by the technical group SRH, MHPSS, NCD, CHW, of the health cluster (4Ws, HeRAMS, and other reports), Also, SDI will report monthly for dHIS2.SDI has coordinated with:local councils and health authorities in target locationsSDI has coordinated with the referral network to refer to the cases that need further servicesSarmada hospital for mental health. SDI staff will identify the GBV cases and refer it to GBV centers belong to global communities, MRFS and HRO.
