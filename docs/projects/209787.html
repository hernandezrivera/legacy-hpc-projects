Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving shelter and non-food item (NFI) assistance to internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host community households in sub-districts of Aleppo and Idlib governorates, targeting a total of 9,214 households, equivalent to 46,070 individuals.
The assistance will be provided through the following activities:
1. Rehabilitation of 380 unfinished houses, with totaling 425 IDP households (2570 individuals). The estimated value of the rehabilitation per house is $1500.
2. Provision of NFI assistance, specifically winter labeled cash, with a value of $180 per household, provided in December. This assistance will benefit 5,000 households (30,000 individuals), following SNFI Cluster Guidance.
3. Flood response activities encompass replacing tents with dignified shelters single unit, constructed from concrete, for each family., conducting leveling, constructing gravel pathways, building culverts, and implementing all necessary infrastructure activities, including the installation of solar-powered lighting and electrical connections. These initiatives aim to target over 50 camps and benefit 1140 households (5,700 individuals), ensuring safety, flood protection, and dignified living conditions. It's noteworthy that the specified number of shelter units consists of 1140 concrete shelter units cost per household is estimated at $1,575, And with all needs of Infrastructure cost per single unit is estimated at $380, each catering to a single family, and with installation of solar energy systems cost per unit is estimated at $430.
4. Shelter repair for 1300 households (7,800 individuals) with an average value of $670 per household. This assistance will address minor to medium damage in shelters, ensuring privacy, protection from the elements, and compliance with SPHERE minimum standards.
The project duration is 12 months, from Jan 2024 to December 2024, and will cover sub-districts in Idlib and Aleppo where vulnerable populations are sheltered in damaged or unfinished buildings, IDP sites, and individual houses.
The shelter repair assistance will prioritize IDP and host community households living in inadequate shelters that do not meet SPHERE minimum standards. Works will include repair or replacement of doors and windows, installation of partitions, sealing of cracks and holes in walls, and repairs to WASH facilities.
Additionally, for households with persons with disabilities (PwDs), additional works will be included to address their specific needs, such as disability-friendly entrance and toilet facilities.
BINAA will ensure that the project activities adhere to established standards and guidelines, including Sphere Project standards and SNFI Cluster Guidance. The staff will receive training on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and work closely with the Protection team to address protection concerns.
Overall, BINAA has extensive experience in shelter rehabilitation and cash assistance programs, ensuring effective program management and continuous learning for improved implementation of future projects.
