Project Summary

Somalia is grappling with increased humanitarian needs due to food insecurities, evolving political tensions and security situations, therefore having reliable air access for the humanitarian community is critical. With the infrastructure gaps existing in Somalia and no countrywide safe and reliable air services, the WFP-led United Nations Humanitarian Air Service (UNHAS) remains vital as the only travel options for humanitarian personnel needing to reach beneficiaries to provide timely and efficient assistance. UNHAS provides safe, reliable, efficient, and cost-effective air transport services (regular scheduled and special flights) to hard-to-reach locations in Somalia. UNHAS facilitates access for over 100 UN agencies, NGOs and Donor organizations to provide life-saving humanitarian assistance in Somalia.

1. - Projects activities

Provision of air services for the humanitarian community: To address the infrastructure gaps and limited availability of safe commercial air services across Somalia, WFP will continue to run UNHAS to enable humanitarian workers access to reach and respond to needs through the implementation and monitoring of their projects. UNHAS will also continue the transportation of essential medical supplies and equipment and essential cargo to hard-to-reach areas and provide timely medical and security evacuations for the humanitarian community. The planned activities are as follows: UNHAS will provide coverage for;• Regular and ad-hoc passenger flights, • Urgent/essential cargo flights travelling on regular and ad-hoc basis, and • Security relocations and medical evacuation flights. In the event commercial services are established which meet UN aviation standards, UNHAS would stop or reduce its schedule accordingly.
