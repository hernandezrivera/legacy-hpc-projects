Project Summary

"Objectives
(a) To restore access to sufficient water of appropriate quality and quantity to fulfil basic needs,
(b) To increase awareness of safe hygiene and sanitation practices, with a focus on participatory health and hygiene education (PHHE) and water conservation and
(c) To provide access to critical WASH related Non-food items (NFIs), with a focus on the most vulnerable families in the targeted areas."

Needs

Life-saving interventions to Restore Access to Safe and sufficient water and improving hygiene practices in 3 drought and cholera affected districts (Hurungwe, Makonde and Mudzi) of Mashonaland West and Mashonaland East provinces

Activities

"Rehabilitation/upgrading/ construction of strategic water points with a focus of maximizing use of perennial water sources to cover needs of institutions (schools, health centres) and vulnerable communities:• Rehabilitation/repair of 160 broken down water points; including head works; • Rehabilitation/upgrading/construction of 1 solar powered community managed piped water scheme; • Water quality testing; • Provision of field water quality testing consumables; • Provision of basic village pumps mechanic tool kits; Conducting Participatory Health and Hygiene Education and awareness in institutions (health facilities, schools) and communities with high malnutrition rates. This will include training of 30 Environmental Health Technicians (EHTs), 150 Village Health Workers (VHWs) and Community Health Workers (CHWs), 60 school health teachers, supporting community based counselling of caregivers on infant and young child feeding practices on critical lifesaving WASH messages / hygiene practices. Formation and training of 30 school and 60 community Health Clubs, Promotion of affordable sanitation and hygiene technologies (self-sponsored uBVIPs and tippy taps), Promotion of menstrual hygiene management for adolescent girls in schools and safe disposal of sanitary ware; Distribution of hygiene enabling kits targeting care givers of severe and moderate acute malnourished childrenStrengthening coordination and surveillance mechanisms at national and sub-national levels"
