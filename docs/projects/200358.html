Project Summary

Lebanon is facing a devastating combination of complex crises that are fuelling a rise in the number of children experiencing abuse, exploitation, and violence, with other children’s rights becoming compromised across the lifecycle of the children. The multiple crises the country has been through since 2019 have deepened the vulnerabilities of all populations residing in Lebanon(Syrian/Palestinian refugees, migrants, Lebanese), affecting a much larger population, including those living outside previously recognized most vulnerable localities. The deepened vulnerabilities and rapid impoverishment of families, combined with a lack of access to essential social services, social safety nets and livelihood opportunities amongst displaced populations and vulnerable Lebanese, contributed altogether to an alarming deterioration of the protection situation of children.
As a general objective, UNICEF, together with its implementing partners, aims at increasing access and quality of CP services by strengthening the community-based protective and supportive environment free of violence, exploitation, neglect and abuse.
Based on the above, UNICEF will work in collaboration with line ministries, UN agencies and partners to address the psychosocial needs of more than 22,000 affected girls, boys, adolescents and caregivers, using a multi-layered approach through the provision of an integrated package of services, including community-based and more focused psychosocial support activities, case management and access to specialized services, such as mental health psychosocial support services.
The programme will be linked to broader services that vulnerable households will need, particularly child protection. Extensive community engagement, outreach and communication on the programme, efficient payment channel, and clear grievance and accountability mechanisms will be crucial.
UNICEF will also include timely and consistent AAP mechanisms (Accountability toaffected population) to ensure that the programme responds to girls', boy's and caregivers' needs.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

To address the worrying CP trends, and within the scope of the Lebanon Emergency Response Plan, UNICEF, with support from its implementing partners, and in close coordination with the Child Protection working Group, PSS working Group and the Case Management taskforce will: - ensure provision of sustainable, quality, and integrated continuum of social services and actions to address specific CP concerns among the most vulnerable girls and boys through the provision of community based psychosocial support for children and caregivers, focused non-specialized psychosocial support, case management, and referral to specialized services including legal, medical, mental health, etc.. ensure the presence of a strong multi-sectoral referral system that aims to maximize outcomes for children. The envisioned activities will ensure outreach to the most vulnerable populations residing in Lebanon. Lastly, social cash assistance will be provided to 50,000 children through the child grant program

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

UNICEF is an active member of the National PSEA TF and has set up a number of mitigation measures to ensure the prevention of SEA. Under this project the following will be ensured:- Review of internal complaints handling procedures to ensure existing feedback mechanisms are functional and can respond to reported issues- Ensure affected communities, especially women and girls, are consulted on changes to complaints and feedback mechanisms, while ensuring that changes are communicated to affected communities in appropriate formats.- Promote safe, confidential complaints and feedback mechanisms with affected communities, making increased efforts to ensure those who are particularly at risk are aware of and have access to safe and confidential means for them to report concerns over sexual exploitation and abuse and other types of misconduct.- Continue roll-out of mandatory PSEA training for UNICEF

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

To strengthen its AAP efforts, UNICEF systematically incorporates processes for affected people to promote participation and equitable, two-way communications between communities and service providers. Moreover, UNICEF builds on existing planning, monitoring and evaluation capacity in development, humanitarian preparedness and response work, before, during and after a crisis.By leveraging local, national and international partnerships aimed at expanding the reach and scope through coordinated responses, UNICEF continuously supports vulnerable and affected people to strengthen their resilience, and the capacity of service providers and authorities to respond effectively to affected populations' needs. More specifically, under this project, through its outreach work, UNICEF will increase access to timely, accurate and reliable life-saving information for th most vulnerable population in urban and rural areas and strengthen community engagemen as agents in their own survival and recovery
