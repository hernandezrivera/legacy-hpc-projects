Project Summary

The project aims to assist vulnerable IDPs (women, girls, men, and boys) as well as members of conflict-affected host communities. SEMA through this project will continue providing preventive and responsive services. SEMA will continue to manage two offices in North West Syria namely one in Idleb/ Salqin and one in Aleppo/Shammarin. These two offices will provide the logistical support to the outreach teams (four mobile teams in Idleb governorate – Harim district and two teams in Aleppo governorate, A’zaz district) and also provide the static/private points to conduct the complementary comprehensive case management where beneficiaries already well be informed about the available services. In addition, there will be CM center in Idleb which will provide comprehensive case management and Psychosocial rehabilitation for PwDs.
. The following services will be provided through the project cycle:
• Providing Psychological First Aid (PFA) and recreational activities to reduce acute distress.
• Identifying, referring and managing most vulnerable GBV and CP cases.
• Provide integrated, community-based protection services, including awareness raising and psychosocial support sessions.
• Information dissemination on essential services.
• Case management activities will continue to address Protection concerns in Idlib and Aleppo.
• Continuing to provide comprehensive case management, referrals to the needed services and specialized psychosocial support for PwD in addition to providing parenting skills for caregivers of children with disabilities in Idleb and Azaz.
• Continue to provide awareness raising sessions to affected communities on Protection concerns, and COVID-19 preventive measures and risks mitigations.

PSEA focal points are responsible for mainstreaming PSEA into all departments and projects, through developing the measures and practices including feedback and compliant mechanism along with concerned departments.
