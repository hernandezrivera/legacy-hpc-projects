Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to strengthen prevention, response services for Children in Mundri East and Terekeka County.
AYA in its efforts to support this conducted assessment in Mundri East and the result of the assessment finding indicated that children face multiple challenges ranging from lack of psycho-social support services where there is no active child friendly spaces in the area, no case management services, abuse of children based on gender persists, recruitment into armed groups is one of the key challenges children face. If the above challenges are not addressed, children’s development will be compromised.
AYA intend to reach 9,100individuals of which 60% children and 40% of the beneficiaries women and men. Through the following activities, 1- case management and psycho-social support, establishment and running of five community based child friendly spaces, creation and updating of child protection referral pathways, awareness raising on topical issues including MRE, CAFAAG, FTR and key Child Protection topics using posters, drama and other methods of information dissemination, establishment and strengthening of community based child protection networks in Mundri East which comprises of community leaders, church leaders, members of women and youth groups, parents and other key influential community members, and establishment of child support desks in child friendly spaces, distribution points, three schools in Mundri East and capacity strengthening of members of child support desks. Lastly, AYA will conduct child protection monitoring, coordination and assessments representing CPSC and disseminate the information using the key Child Protection Minimum Standards on information sharing protocols.

1 - Needs

South Sudan at international level had been considered the fragile and worse place for children and women to reside in, since 2013 crisis on track1. Communities were exposed to repeated violence scenarios and many lost hope and went to neighboring countries as refugees. Many reports said up to 7.1 million South Sudanese left their place of origin. Mundri East faced the worst situation special from 2015 up to date. Community settlement was disorganized by both state and non-state armed actors, many sources of livelihood were destroyed and several villages were displaced including burning down houses and raping of girls and women, children witnessed forceful recruitment, sexual abuse, child labour and child neglect. This year in February, community of Minga Payam run to Rokon as IDPs and these people are still there without humanitarian attention. Though some of them have returned home, the children still remain at large without support. The returnees from Mundri to Kedi’ba, Wandi and other areas have never accessed humanitarian support since their return. The finding of assessment recently AYA conducted revealed that the people are in need of humanitarian assistance, children have never been supported with psycho-social support services and other child friendly services.No functional schools for children in the area. The only two schools in Kedi’ba and Wandi have been overstretched because of increased number of children attending. The presence of cantonment site in Mundri West has posed increased challenge to children and women. The luring of children to join the ranks of the armed men has been on rise. The communities decry the vulnerability of their children to such situation. SEA special to girls is also reported as presence of cantonment. The presence of the holdout rebel group also pose threat to the children in terms of forceful recruitment, abduction and torture of children. All these increase their vulnerability and affecting their positive cognitive development.Terekeka County in Central Equatoria State is one of the counties which had been calm throughout the conflict. However, in July-August, famine broke out in the county which made people to leave their original land. The children especially young girls are reported to face numerous challenges. Some of these people are found in Mundri East County- Kedi’ba. Therefore, programming to target the County is of key advantage.Therefore, as AYA had been working with IsraAID on UNICEF funded project from 2016-2018, the organization have improved capacity to implement CP projects. UNICEF and Save the Children conducted series of capacity building on CPiE, Case Management and how to conduct PSS to the children affected by the crisis. AYA currently have trained Case Workers who works effectively with the children and the organization is planning to integrate child protection in all its GBV projects in Juba and Raja.This therefore, gives AYA the opportunity to implement its projects when given the opportunity. The organization has presence on the ground, coordinating GBV activities in Greater Mundri and active member of Protection Cluster. AYA currently have active GBV projects in Raja and Juba. AYA also have capacity to establish and run its activities without delay. This has been witnessed by implementing two GBV projects- one funded by RRF in Lobono’k and SSHF in Raja. These projects progressed well.

2 - Activities

1- Conduct comprehensive case management services to children affected by the crisis or prone to protection risks. Enter the data to the CPIMS as necessary and conduct regular follow up with the children enrolled for case management.2- Establishment of 5 community based child friendly spaces: AYA will establish five community based child friendly spaces in Mundri East and Terekeka. The spaces will be established in Kedi’ba, Wandi, Wiroh, Tindilo and Tali. These are the current hot spot areas that children are suffering a lot. AYA will in consultation with the children design activities to be performed in each child friendly spaces. The activities will focus on traditional CFS activities which includes, recreational and life skills activities. There are already recreational kits supplied by UNICEF in April 2019 which is not yet used. AYA will use the spaces for identification of vulnerable children and conduct PSS and referral to other services such as health, education, nutrition, livelihood through the spaces. Parents will also be included in the design of the spaces and parental sessions will also be conducted to the parents. All the activities will be in line with CP Minimum Standards. 3- Establish and strengthen referral pathways: There will be creation of CP specific referral pathways for Mundri East and Terekeka. The referral pathway will include the major services such as health, specialized counseling, and legal services though very weak in the county, and security services that will focus on strengthening linkages with partners providing purely protection and police that is the primary security service providers to the citizens. AYA will organize workshop that will bring together all the service providers and sessions like guiding principles for working with children will be shared. Key roles of every service provider shall be agreed upon and sharing of best practices of working with children. This meeting will therefore culminate to the development of the CP referral pathways.4- Community Child Protection awareness and prevention messaging: AYA will conduct weekly community awareness raising on topical child protection issues. The awareness raising will be in line with CP Minimum Standards and various means will be used 1- use of posters, AYA team will use the posters developed by CP Sub-cluster and context appropriate posters developed in the location (Mundri East and Terekeka) to disseminate information on Family separation, CAFAAG, MRE in Schools, Case Management services available and other forms of child abuse. 2- Music dance and drama, the organization will pass CP prevention messages to the communities through the use of its focused music dance and drama it conducts in the community. 5- Establishment of community based child protection networks and working to strengthen child protection desks in schools and any location of humanitarian assistance: The organization will form and train members of community based child protection network in Mundri East and Terekeka. The members will consist of chiefs, members of women groups, members of youth groups, influential parents. There will also be establishment of child protection desks in five Primary Schools (3) in Mundri East, (2) inTerekeka and training for the members of the child protection desk will also take place. 6- Child Protection monitoring, coordination and assessments: AYA will conduct regular child protection assessments in the community and participate in County based Inter-Agency Assessments to document Child protection concerns. The assessment results will be communicated with CPSC at National Level to inform humanitarian programming related to children. AYA Management will also pay regular visits to project sites as part of activity monitoring. This will improve the quality of services to the survivors of child abuses.
