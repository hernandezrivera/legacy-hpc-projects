Project Summary

international Medical Corps intend to provide adequate Water, Sanitation and improved Hygiene (WASH) services to saves lives and to uphold the health and dignity of the populations in Damboa LGA. The provision of water and sanitation infrastructure and services, coupled with the promotion of good hygiene practices will reduce the risk of water-related disease transmission as seem daily from hospital and clinics records, 85,000 beneficiaries in six informal camps and Host communities will be targeted with life saving WASH intervention
