Project Summary

This project will contribute to addressing the urgent needs for food security and livelihood of the most vulnerable HHs in the newly accessible locations in South Kordofan State, El-leiri, Rashad and Talodi localities. The targeted localities are in urgent need of emergency food security interventions agricultural & non-agricultural (livestock inputs and services), food assistance, income-generating activities and natural resource management. This will be followed by a selection of 7749 HHs (46,498 individual, 6,345 men, 15,228 women, 8,418 boys and 12,309 girls) of the most vulnerable households among new and protracted IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable and destitute host communities as direct beneficiaries. Selection criteria such as number of children under 5, women/children headed households, food insecure households with more than 6 members, households with malnourished children, disabled, elderly men and women, landless (for IGAs), chronically poor and minority groups will be part of the selection criteria. Beneficiary (300 HHs) selection for goats restocking will focus on female-headed HHs, HHs with malnourished children, vulnerable HHs with children under the age of 5 and elders; tools distribution (for 6,300 HHs) will focus on vulnerable farmers IDPs and returnees who have lost their livelihood assets due to conflict and displacement; provision of grain millet and oil extraction machines (200 HHs) will focus on IDPs, vulnerable host communities and returnees men and youth from the targeted communities including persons with disabilities; fuel efficient stoves training will target mainly vulnerable women, girls and youth from IDPs, returnees and host communities including persons with disabilities

1 - Needs

Displacement and refugee influx created a heavy burden on both IDPs and host communities in SK State. The majority of the IDPs have been unable to re-establish the livelihoods they used to practice before being displaced. Most of them are farmers, pastoralists as well as agro-pastoralists and those who lived near urban centers practiced other local crafts and trades. Regarding food security, the IDPs are suffering to provide enough food for the household consumption due to the steep increase in food prices in the market. For example, the price of Sorghum, which is the main staple food crop has doubled compared to the same time last year. An Inter-Agency Assessment was conducted on (12-26 Oct.2018) and the information compiled based on the inputs provided by partners on the ground including; government authorities, affected communities/IDPs and agencies; showed a total of 10,206 individual 4,591 Male and 5,669 Female, are living as Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) in El Leri according to HAC. Only 50% of the IDPs are registered as some of the areas where IDPs live have been inaccessible. El Leri population is estimated by 38,882 individual which was further boosted by the arrival of 14,000 refugees from South Sudan since 2013 to-date. Care International Switzerland, Save the Children (CIS) CONCERN and Al Manar have humanitarian response activities in El Leri Locality. They support sectors as Health, WASH and Nutrition as well as providing cash grants to South Sudan refugees (SSR) and number of vulnerable IDPs to cover part of household expenses. Rashad locality is inhabited by 95,919 people (22,874 HHs). Currently there are 22,324 IDPs composed of 3,576 HHs living in displacement in Tandla, Kajmala, Kaboos and Rashad city. They are comprised of 12,520 female, 9,794 male and 4,702 children under-5. Presently Mercy Corps Scotland (MC-S), Global Aid Hand (GAH), Sudanese Red Crescent Societies (SRCS), Alsalam Organization for Rehabilitation and Development (AORD), SOS Sahel Sudan have offices in Rashad. Other partners INGOs, UN agencies and NNGOs operate remotely. They are providing food, health, WASH and nutrition services to the IDPs and some vulnerable community members. Talodi locality has 13,968 IDPs (2,328 HHs) mainly living in 5 locations, 4 of which are inside Talodi Town, and one in Al Tugula Village, 16 kilometers east to Talodi Town. The IDPs were displaced from Mandi, Angarto, Al Gardod, Mafloua, Kindirma, Al Salamat and Taboli which experienced several attacks by both SPLM-N and the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF). Most of those villages are no longer under government control. Some people from these villages initially fled to Talodi and Om Dual village which have been evacuated and later moved to AlTugula. The displaced people did not form displacement camps but were absorbed into existing villages by relatives and other people in Talodi. The total population of Talodi is 48,862 people, including IDPs.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Provision of quality assorted crop seeds (Sorghum, sesame & legumes) and vegetable seeds to 6,300 farmer HHs (37,800 individual: 6,010 men, 13,608 women, 7,182 boys and 11,000 girls): Under this activity each HH will be provided 20 USD worth package of assorted seeds including: Sorghum, sesame, water melon, okra and tomato seeds, pigeon pea seeds and chick pea seeds will be provided to the targeted beneficiaries in consultation with the communities in Rashad, Eleri and Talodi to select the suitable package. Procurement of assorted seeds will take place in Khartoum through tender process including certified suppliers. - Provision of 6,300 tool kits (consists of hoes and rakes) to 6,300 HHs or100% of beneficiaries targeted by seeds distribution. - Formation and training of Village Agricultural Committees (VACs) in seeds management, storage, pest control, community mobilization and distribution of agricultural inputs: - Extension training of 300 HHHs farmers in best agricultural practices Livelihood: - Provision of goat package (4 She, 1 He) to 300 female-headed most vulnerable HHs (1800 people: 288 male, 640 female, 342 boys and 530 girls), provision of (10) grain millet and (5) oil extraction machines for 150 HH (men & youth) 900 beneficiaries , divided into 15 groups consists of 10 beneficiaries, each 1 machine for a group. - Arrangement of TOT of 30 HHHs men and youth, (on molding mixture, materials ratio, production, maintenance and operation technologies of Stabilized Soil Block machines. - Provide training of 30 HHHs, 180 beneficiaries - Arrangement of TOT of 300 women and youth HHHs (1,800 people: 288 male, 666 female, 342 boys, and 504 girls) on molding mixture, materials ratio, production, and maintenance and operation technologies of Fuel Efficient Stoves.
