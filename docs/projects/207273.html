Project Summary

The on-going “Crisis” in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon which breaks out since 2016/incorporated with Cholera outbreak in 2022/flood disaster in 2023/landslide in 2023 in the NWSW regions due to climate change, has resulted in increased displacements of people, lead to acute malnutrition, lack of water/information on humanitarian program planning/highest death rates of children <5yrs. 1.0M among the 1.3M PiN of humanitarian assistance in Nutrition/WASH including 638,421 IDPs/442,580 Returnees/PIN-TARGET WASH HNO-HRP2024 of 20 Nov. 2023 is 787,8K will be targeted.
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 5 Subdivisions in 5 villages, rural, urban/semi-urban in 4 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Boyo, Mezam, Menchum) in the NW Region of Cameroon. Targeted 5,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (856 Boys/868 Girls) <18 yrs, (851 Men/862 Women) 18-59yrs, (450 Men/500 Women) +60yrs, PWD (300 Men/313 Women)).
AIM: To improved healthcare feeding to children aged 6-59 months admitted for SAM management and capacity-building to family MUAC activity to reduce neonatal death rates with access to drinking to reduce cholera by the end of 31st December 2024.
INDICATORS:
-# of mothers and other caretakers trained on family MUAC activity.
-# of information products distributed to the affected population through a variety of mechanisms on humanitarian program planning, functioning and progress
-% of children 6-59 months admitted for SAM management and discharge as cured (Recovery rate)
-# of boreholes constructed
-# of taps constructed
-# of men, women, boys and girls provided with sustainable access to safe drinking water (disaggregated by geographical area)
-# of water user committees established/redynamised
-# of water user committees trained
-# of community mobilisers (community health workers, volunteers, animators..) trained on WASH issues.
ACTIVITIES:
-Establish and strengthen national and regional capacities for the coordination of the nutrition sector group, other sectors and information management that mainstreams protection and AAP.
-Support Community engagement for behavior and social change
-Integrated management of severe acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S)
-Construction of water points (boreholes, small water supply network..)
-Capacity-building of WASH actors in emergency WASH response/relative issues

OBJECTIVES:
-Improved healthcare to children aged 6-59 months admitted for SAM management for recovery by 75% to reduce malnutrition/stunted growth.
-Improved healthcare capacity on family MUAC activity
-Improve access to safe drinking water to vulnerable people.
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve WASH services/resilience of affected populations

BENEFICIARIES: IDPs, returnee’s/host communities of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations.
APPROACH: Community based approach, awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials including PSEA, collaboration with stakeholders/organised workshops, community mobilization/engagement /construction/M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs/KII/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Engage MINEE/MINSANTE
SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained (CPCs Nurses/CHWs for continuos sensitisaton on nutrition programmes/HP; family on MUAC activity ; WMCs/supply with tools box). Nutrition unit in hospital will have access to water.
