Project Summary

Given that HIHFAD is currently operating 4 primary healthcare facilities across northwest Syria (3 within Idleb Governorate and 1 within Afrin, Aleppo governorate, HIHFAD seek to continue operating those facilities to support the primary healthcare services for people in need within their respective scope areas. Each of the facilities is planned to operate 8 hours per day, 5 days per week. All the facilities are planned to be operating for the whole year of 2019 as a response to the mounting healthcare needs within their areas. In addition, HIHFAD is planning to establish three mobile clinics to operate within Afrin area supporting the aforementioned PHC fixed facility as well as the health system in Afrin area as a whole entity. The services planned to be provided through the facilities entail:
• Outpatient general medicine, pediatric and reproductive health consultations.
• Outpatient mental health services (MhGAP).
• Basic obstetric and neonatal care BEmONC services.
• Basic trauma care (Dressing) services.
• Laboratory services.
• Provision of essential medicines.
• Imaging services (Plane X-ray and ultrasound imaging)
• Ambulance referral services.
• Facility and community-based vaccination services in line with the expanded program on immunization.
• Facility and community based dental care services.
• Ophthalmology outreach services.
• Outreach nutrition assessment (Mid upper arm circumference MUAC) and referral of undernourished cases to the appropriate facility. Needs

Increasing access to humanitarian life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for those most vulnerable and in need is the first objective of the health cluster. In addition, and among the drafted priorities communicated by the health cluster is increasing access to primary health care through the provision of essential primary health services to cover the health care needs of vulnerable populations, including Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, and Child Health (RMNCH), as well as continuity of treatment for non-communicable disease.HIHFAD seeks to support the aforementioned trend of intervention through operating multiple primary healthcare facilities; fixed and mobile ones; across northwest Syria in areas that are considered among the most conflict-affected ones (The healthcare severity levels in the areas the intervention is planned to be implemented are 4,3,3,3 in Dana, Saraqeb, Ma'arrat An Nu'man and Afrin respectively).

Implementation of activities

• Provide essential primary and secondary health care services, including trauma care, EmONC and referral to specialist services: the healthcare services will be provided on daily basis and round the hour. The primary healthcare services will be provided in line with the essential primary healthcare package adopted by the health cluster- Gaziantep hub. Those services entail outpatient consultations for children (0-18 years of age) and women of reproductive age (15-49 years of age). In addition, those services entail the provision of services that address the communicable and non-communicable diseases not to mention the provision of basic obstetric and neonatal care BEmONC services as well. The service package will also entail the provision of MhGAP services in addition of providing other specialized services like dental and ophthalmology services for those in need.• Provide routine immunization services to all children <1 years of age: All children under 1 year of age as well as any children under 5 years of age eligible for immunization within the scope area of the facility will be offered the vaccination services in line with the expanded program on immunization led by the Syrian immunization group SIG. The vaccination services include the administration of Penta3 and MR vaccines among all of the other adopted by the SIG and will be provided at both the facility and the community levels.• Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies: HIHFAD will ensure that the facilities consistently have adequate quantities of the essential medicines and medical supplies that are required for the implementation of the planned activities, where that will happen through the funding channels HIHFAD has established. Also, the provision of such items will be promoted through the coordination with WHO and UNFPA since they provide those items through their supply lines.• Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services: HIHFAD consistently assesses the gaps in terms of medical technical capacities of the staff and conduct the required trainings accordingly. Those trainings include and are not limited to integrated management of childhood illnesses, infant and young child feeding in emergencies, reproductive health-related trainings. In addition, HIHFAD will ensure that the community health workers attached to the facility are provided with the standardized trainings adopted by the community health working group- Gaziantep hub on the community health topics and modules.• Increase access to health services by establishing functional health facilities and mobile medical units and supporting referral: HIHFAD will provide the facilities with well-equipped ambulances to ensure timely transportation of any referral case that require further medical intervention to the appropriate nearby or remote facility.health information system in all facilities are in place , and the data is shared on regular basis with turkey offices for analyzing the data to get information and reporting to the stockholders

Protection Risk Analysis

The list of the key protection risks includes and not limited to the followings:(Abbreviations: LCs: Local councils/ HDs: Health directorates/ L: Low/ M: Medium/H: High)1. Beneficiaries are affected by conflict related incidences while seeking healthcare at the Facility.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Facility are based in areas included in the de-escalation zones agreement at adequate distances from gatherings of armed groups. Coordinate with the local authorities (Civilian LCs and HDs) to support the exclusion of the health facility from any possible clashes. Technical support by HIHFAD safety and security team. Training of staff on contingency plans. Ensure about the establishment of emergency exits within the facility.2. Patients are subject to harm due to low-quality healthcare services.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: HIHFAD follows strict HR recruitment procedures in terms of the qualifications and expertise required for medical personal. Also, HIHFAD consistently evaluate any gap of the technical capacities for its medical staff. Feedback mechanisms to be made available for the beneficiaries to ensure their participation in identifying any gap in the staff technical capacities that need to be addressed. In the same context, HIHFAD consistently seeks to ensure the standardization of the services provided in terms of medical equipment, medical supplies and pharmaceuticals.3. Children undergo vaccination induced side effects after receiving EPI vaccines.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: M to H• Mitigation: provide EPI staff with the necessary capacity building. The children vaccinated are observed for appropriate duration after receiving their scheduled vaccination. The vaccination services are introduced through a facility that can handle pediatric emergencies. In addition, HIHFAD to utilize HeRAMS to identify the required Facility and referral pathways required for promptly respond to emergencies related to vaccine administration.4. While at Facility, Beneficiaries are abused, harassed, undergo compromised privacy or dignity.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Child safeguarding training provided for all staff. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. Ensure individuals of vulnerable groups (children, women, elderly and people with disabilities) are accompanied while at the Facility. Ensure adequate female staff representation. Application of privacy screens and separation of medical examination theatres. Establishment of breast-feeding corners. Separation of the bathrooms by sex. Label tags to be demonstrated within the Facility. Establishment of adequate waiting areas and ensure adequate lighting of the facility is in place.5. Confidentiality of the information provided by the Beneficiaries is compromised.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: staff of the Facility adapt HIHFAD code of conduct. Tags about confidentiality of patient records and information to be demonstrated within the Facility. Maintaining patient information through well secured and strictly monitored modalities inaccessible to public. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. 6. Communities are affected by medical wastes produced at the Facility.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Securing the medical wastes prior to disposal. Application of medical waste management pathways for the purpose of proper disposal of them.7. Staff/ patients and their companions acquire nosocomial infections while at the facility.• Likelihood: M/ Impact: M to H• Mitigation: programmed use of disinfection measures. Programmed microbiological screening of the Facility. Establishment of clean and well-ventilated waiting areas. Proper handling of medical wastes. Use of personal protective equipment when indicated. Children presented at the Facility to receive EPI vaccines are offered the service within the outpatient department to ensure minimum contact with hospitalized patients.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption #1Cross-border operations and humanitarian space are sufficiently preserved to allow implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Risk/s: Security incidents impacting border-crossings, terror attacks, cross-border shelling or clashes on Turkish soil. Consequences:Possible damage to relations with communities.Complications with assists transfer and delivery including medical consumables and pharmaceutical itemsDelays or stoppage of programming in multiple areas Mitigations:Options for procuring the needed supplies from inside Syria.Explore alternate official border crossings.Maintain good relations with local communitiesSecure stock in HIHFAD warehouses for emergency measures. Assumption #2The safety and security situation remain relatively stable in the project implementation areas in a way that allows staff to travel to and operate sufficiently. Risk/s:Increased suspicion/tension or armed clashes between the parties to the conflict.Airstrike raids or shelling bombardment. Consequences:Death or serious injury to the staff or the beneficiaries. Mitigations:Delays/stoppage of programming Reduce exposure to areas that are known to be targetedEnsure contingency plans are in place for cases of aerial bombardment and indirect fireEnsure that the facility has a safe room equipped as a shelter for the staff in case of conflict-related incidents. Assumption #3Targeted populations maintain adequate levels of cooperation, commitment and willingness to get engaged in the project activities. Risk/s:Rise of anti-NGO activities and loss of acceptance within the targeted communities. Mitigations:Insure that feedback and accountability mechanisms are in place Ensure stronger communications and relations with the local civil authorities.
