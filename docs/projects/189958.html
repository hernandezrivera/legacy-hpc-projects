Project Summary

The project proposal aims to increase access to water for 1800 Households (HHS) or 9000 IDPs living in 6 communities of the Administrative Post of Macomia-Sede. Dorcas will provide water treatment materials to contribute for the reduction of waterborne diseases including help to reduce the outbreak of acute diarrhea in the IDP families. The project will also promote activities for self-construction of households’ latrines. Community and household level hygiene promotion activities will also be being implemented and hygiene volunteers will be trained to carry out the promotion activities.
Women and girls are particularly impacted by WASH conditions. Latrine conditions and the management of menstrual hygiene can be an on-going struggle, particularly for school-aged girls. So the Project will conduct distribution of hygiene dignity kits to young girls and pregnant women. The COVID-19 has also put personal and environmental hygiene at the forefront as frequent hand washing. As such, the project will implement a robust set of interventions to mitigate the spread of COVID-19 infection. This beneficiaries of this project 60% are women.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

To identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm, the project will mobilize the targeted communities, raise awareness about the project objectives and organize a community Project Management Committee to ensure ownership and community leadership. The communities will be consulted and involved in project interventions. During the mobilization session, the communities will also be informed of their rights/entitlements and responsibilities. The Project Management Committee (PMC) will be trained to indemnify potentials risks. The project will apply accountability measures throughout its operations, involving local authorities. Feedback and complaint mechanism including help desk, hotline will be established. Beneficiary participation during key phases of the project cycle, e.g. beneficiary screening, implementation, & monitoring through PMCs will be prioritized. The feedback and complaint mechanism to be established will include help desk, hotline. Information from complaint boxes will be collected on a regular basis, processed and responded timely.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project will mobilize the targeted communities, raise awareness about the project objectives and work closely with IPD leaders to ensure ownership and community leadership and the communities will be involved in project interventions. During the mobilization session, the communities will also be informed of their rights/entitlements and responsibilities. The IPD leader and activists will be trained to share messages on gender-based violence and referral mechanism through the appropriate government institutions.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Additionally, the project will work with IDPs and community volunteers to increase outreach and share project information during the mobilization, sensitization and training sessions.The project will ensure greater inclusiveness of all targeted vulnerable groups in IDP relocation sites/host communities and especially women-headed households, orphans and people living with disability and the elderly. Gender equity and equality will be integrated and mainstreamed in all project actions, providing equal opportunities and promoting the role of women in all project positions and interventions.Vulnerability criteria will be applied to identify and select the most vulnerable and needy beneficiaries and avoid inclusion and/or exclusion errors. A participatory process will be applied involving community leaders and local government authorities. The final master beneficiary list will be endorsed by community leaders. Host community members will be explained the benefits of the programme and especially the interventions that extend benefits to the community at large in WASH and mainstreamed interventions.
