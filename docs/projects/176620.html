Project Summary

Cabo Delgado province has over 329,000 internally displaced people as a result of an armed conflict in 7 northern districts. The project will provide protected and sustainable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities as well as a safe and healthy environment to at least 28,000 people including hosting communities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

People affected by cyclone Idai and associated floods have protected and reliable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and a safe and healthy environment, contributing to reduce the risk of transmission for WASH related diseases.FH will provide both emergency and long term WASH services to conflict affected IDP families living in accommodation / transit centres and resettlement areas including the hosting communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Water supply activities will include; • Setting up and operation of emergency water distribution points in accommodation / transition centres by connecting to existing water networks (FIPAG). In critical circumstances, water trucking will be used as a short-term approach• Rehabilitation/emergency repair of existing non-functional water sources. • Construction of new water points in resettlement and hosting communities (Metuge, Ancuabe and Chiure) • Establishment and training of water committees to ensure community participation, ownership and ensure that effective for operation and maintenance mechanisms are in place.• Point of use water treatment 2. Access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities, including management of solid waste • Construction of emergency latrines and bathing facilities that provide dignity, privacy and proection in accommodation centers• Support self contrcution of HH latrines in resettlement communities to reduce the risk of disease transmission• Operation and maintenance of temporary latrines including decommitioning of sanitation and hygiene facilities when necessary• Construction of improved drainage where necessary and ensuring collection, removal and proper disposal of solid waste. 3. Promotion of proper hygiene and sanitation practices including distribution of WASH NFIs for the prevention of WASH related diseases.• Distribution of non-food items (hygiene kits) for the most vulnerable populations • Hygiene promotion activities such as community engagement sessions, mass sensitization campaigns, door to door visits, • Training Community Volunteers to lead the community engagement sessions • Reproducing and distribution of IEC materials

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Consultations will be done with community leadership structures and the most vulnerable groups to identify and enlist all potential protection risks with more focus on women and children. Protection awareness sessions will be promoted at all levels and feedback mechanisms will be put in place to ensure that community members are able to identify and report any protection issues arising. Close coordination will be maintained with the Protection Cluster for additional assistance.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project was designed in a high participatory approach where the beneficiaries have defined the needs and activities, so, the empowerment has already started and the approach will be used and enhanced during the implementation phase.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will promote proper management and handling of solid waste to avoid environmental pollution and ensure safe living environment.
