Project Summary

CARE International (CI) will focus on 50,000 beneficiaries of Pishin, which was severely affected by COVID-19 Lockdown, Drought and Locust Disaster. Most of the small holders and farmers lost their main source of earning in global pandemic and lock down. Care has proposed the agriculture and livestock support for the affected population and lifesaving and livelihood support to extremely vulnerable population of district Pishin. The targeted beneficiaries will include Men, Women, Boys, Girls, and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs). The proposed area has been selected in view of recently conducted RNA, IPC, Socio-economic framework, and other assessment. District Pishin falls in the districts nominated for HRP 2021 as one of the vulnerable district affected by several disasters in recent years. The project interventions are designed to provide emergency response to address the food security, agriculture and livelihood needs of vulnerable covid-19 affected communities of Pishin, Baluchistan. Immediate emergency response activities are the strategic objective of the food security and agriculture working group and government priorities for the affected families. The objective is to provide life-saving food security, nutrition and livelihood support to vulnerable target communities with a special focus on women and girls. This will be in line with the RNA findings of FS&LWG August 2020, Baluchistan drought needs assessment and consultations with FAO, WFP and Food security and agriculture working group at national and provincial level. All farming households in the district keep livestock for subsistence and income. Women play an active role in livestock rearing at the household level. Livestock farmers are confronted with forage and fodder shortages, particularly during the drought years, as well as frequent livestock disease outbreaks.
The interventions will include provision of poultry packages, goat packages, Kitchen gardening kits, Mott grass fodder cutting and fertilizer and training for each and C-19 IPC via radio and face-to-face sessions and distribution of IEC materials, which will cover the key messages approved by the government and WHO. CARE will finalize beneficiary identification criteria and registration through community mobilization and engagement. Care will reach a total 50,000 COVID-19 affected individuals. The project will also provide training on nutrition, hygiene, entrepreneurship and PSEA with a special focus on women and girls. The FSL component will address the food, nutrition and livelihood needs of the families as the poultry kits will provide sufficient output of eggs and poultry meat for each family. The goat package will enable households to meet their dairy dietary needs sufficiently including women and girls. Kitchen gardening activity focuses on provision of vegetables to COVID-19 affected HH that will improve access to seasonal vegetables. Mott-grass fodder cutting farming will ensure provision of fodder for livestock all year round particularly because it does not require repeat sowing. The production will be increased through provision of agriculture and livestock inputs, which will lead to the increase in income of the beneficiary households. Training organized for nutrition, Hygiene, PSEA, GBV and COVID-19prevention. The WASH component of the project will include COVID-19 risk awareness, PSEA, entrepreneurship, and nutrition. CARE will ensure compliance of PHPF Gender Marker 2a and SPHERE.
CARE will use people-centered approach & collaborate with Livestock and dairy development, Agriculture Department and FSCs for creating linkages and create farmers/kitchen gardening committees. The project will focus on hygiene education and COVID-19 risk information dissemination. CARE will ensure compliance of Gender Marker and SPHERE. CARE will provide food, hygiene kits and COVID-19 risk awareness in the same UCs in Pishin with its own funds ($75,000) that will complement the grant and scale up.
