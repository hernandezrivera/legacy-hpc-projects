Project Summary

This project delivers a strategic and integrated set of interventions to restore the local economy in 7 camps in the Idleb governorate and the communities around it in sub-districts Idleb &Ma'arat Tamsrin and the communities of Idleb city Ma'arat Tamsrin city and Shihk Bahr Camps, in the aftermath of protracted conflict further aggravated by the impact of the C-19 pandemic and deteriorated economic situation. The project formulation has been based on needs as identified in multi-cluster RNA/REACH/HNAP data and MHD's market assessment. Besides, firsthand consultations, conducted specifically for this project with local stakeholders, have also informed the design of this proposal. By pursuing the referred main objective, the project aims to achieve the following outcomes:
Targeting 500 people including (IDPs and Host communities) (500 individuals 300M / 200F and %60 from Him IDPs in 2022 and we will aim 250 People in 2023 also ( 150M and 100F and also 60 % of them IDPs .
- vocational Training: MHD will conduct VT training for 500 Beneficiaries (300 f/200M) from IDPs and host community members around Hair, dresser - Dairy - Pickles and Jams - Solar energy - Mobile phone repair - Sewing clothes - Detergent Industry Project - Men's shaving - the plumbing - home electrician which determined based on the MHD's labor market assessment "attached". the theoretical training will take place for three full days after that trainee will involve in 20 working days of on-job training / Apprenticeship - the training beneficiaries will also receive business development support (BDS) training to complement their newly acquired technical skills and increase their livelihood opportunities. as a final stage, 150 out of 500 will get business grants to start implementing their projects (based on the specific selection criteria) the selected beneficiaries will receive either cash or in-kind business grant of USD 900 as an average.
The VT training will be considered HAZARD ANALYSIS AND CRITICAL CONTROL POINTS (HACCP) as a condition for the Dairy industry - Pickles and Jams Industry pieces of training.
These grants for 150 people will be considered as CVA and we will coordinate with CWG and all implementing partners in the area to avoid any overlap or duplication,
On the other hand, your need assessment was according to the market survey. To avoid any harm in the market after delivering the grants and beginning the work, MHD with full staff will monitor the activities and fully coordinate with the CWG and IPs.
Through the project, MHD will work to move the market by injecting the revitalization of the professions that we have trained in, which will provide good job opportunities and secure potential job opportunities for 350 people who take care of the family and secure job opportunities and a small project for 150 people with a cash grant to help them start work To establish the profession in which he trained
Therefore, after studying the needs in the two areas, the impact will be positive for these professions and will not cause harm and damage to the owners of the old professions whose opinions were taken in the design of the project

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The priority will be given to safety and dignity and avoiding causing harm by the MHD team. To ensure that, all the locations of implemented activities will select to be far from military points and clashes to ensure the safety of the staff and beneficiaries.-Identify and monitor barriers that may appear during implementing the project activities and take steps to remove them, to ensure that project and program are accessible to women and PwDs. -To ensure gender inclusion, Muzun’s team will use vulnerability selection criteria and will specifically target the women and PwDs affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. These vulnerability groups will include women-headed HHs with no male aging 16-18 able to work, PwDs, survivors of GBV, HHs whose children are not attending school and/or engaged in dangerous types of child labor that lead to the new types of threats on children (e.g. recycle collection from landfills, agricultural work, ...e.t). -MHDwill ensure that all PwDs in target communities will have the opportunity to take part in all project activities. -The data collection and analysis process is disaggregated by sex, age, and disability. Furthermore, ensuring that data ethics and protection principles (including confidentiality, provision of information, informed consent, security) are respected whenever data on persons with disabilities are collected and used. -Female staff will be present at the activities site and involved in the process,

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

All risk and mitigation measures will be taken into account throughout the project's lifecycle. R1: Positive Cases of COVID-19 are registered in NWS, L: Medium/high, M: MHD will follow the instructions issued by the Health Cluster/WHO and follow all protocols. It will include but is not limited to lockdowns/curfews. MHD can change all meetings in the project to be done remotely via the internet. The precautionary measures will be followed by all staff and contractors during the implementation of all activities. In case these measures will cause any delay, MHD will notify HFU and ask for advice on how to proceed. R3: Local authority interventions in the project, L: Low I: Medium, M: MHD gets daily security briefing from our field team and attends/coordinates with the Access Working Group (AWG) of OCHA. Any intervention will be documented as an incident and sent to OCHA’s AWG as well as the HFU team right away. R 4: Community cohesion breakdown due to projectL: Low I: MediumM: MHD will conduct a consultation process that is inclusive and transparent to make sure we avoid any conflict or community tension.R5: Escalation of hostilitiesL: MediumI:Medium/highR6: Beneficiary lists are corrupted by local authorities, L:High ,I :High M:All people on the list will have a vulnerability assessment carried out by MHD and MEAL team and the final selection will be based on vulnerability score, verification of the final beneficiary list will be carried out by MHD MEAL
