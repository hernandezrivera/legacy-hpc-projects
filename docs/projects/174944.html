Project Summary

The project targets 90.000 children and adolescents in school and vocational training. It will build the capacities of 1.900 teachers from primary and secondary schools on PSS and C/DRR, for them to use adequate pedagogical and in-class practises. In order to generate foster school-based risk informed humanitarian action and evidence generation, the project will also conduct a Protective Learning Environment study.
The needs need to be expanded: how many children are in primary and secondary school and what are the challenges faced by teachers who work in context of insecurity?

1 - Besoins / Needs

The target population consists of pupils and teachers from seven subdivisions in the Far-north region.
