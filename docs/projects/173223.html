Project Summary

this project will increase the resilience and coping mechanism of children in return areas of Central Darfur through playing, learning and exploring their skills of coping, it will build the capacity of the community of child protection issues through awareness campaign and will stood as voice of the most at-risk children and PSNs to establish the bases of future leaders, it will also encourage working together the create the positive sense of brotherhood between return children communities and the children's of communities who doesn't experience displacement , the activities will be based on human rights approach therefore we are expecting at the end of this project communities will be able to change negative behaviors and believes and consider child rights and child protection in their daily culture practices. This will provide required protection services for the children in Central Darfur to cope with challenges and harsh situation they faced, the anticipated demand to at least satisfying their currently urgent needs, while enhance the capacity of community member and community based organizations towards collecting efforts in order to make a solidarity attitude in protection and other intervention tools or mechanisms such setting up child protection system, Case Management, GBV, Human and child rights and referral mechanism and strong CBCPN. the collaboration with the SCCW will be closed specially in identifying UASC and place them in alternative families. children at risk will be identified and support them or refer them to institution of concern.

1 - Needs

Since the process of the negotiation to signing of peace agreement in conflicting areas in Sudan, the Darfur villages which witnessed displaced people have been receiving a return from IDPs camps to the areas of origin specially farmers due to the slight stability in the security situation. The increasing number of the return particularly in the past two years has added value to the situation for the serious level of food security in Center Darfur state despite the state still suffering from lack of resources to respond effectively for growing protection needs. However, and in such a situation, children and adolescents are often at highest risk for being directly affected by negative coping mechanisms with few protective strategies available to them with the closer of schools resulting from the spread of COVID 19; and increased exposure to violence expected with high drop out of schools which will effect on children's development and well-being . Although there are no clear figures due to the lack of counselling services, it is well known that during the return, many children went through traumatizing experiences and were separated from their families. As children’s well-being and development depends very much on the security of family relationships and a predictable environment, many children lose touch with their families and have no idea of fathers and mothers’ whereabouts. Given the fact, the vast majority of the children in Central Darfur are now living in very unsafe and difficult conditions affecting their abilities to develop on physical, cognitive, emotional and social aspects, which have obviously taken away children’s normalcy and development conditions. During REMCO assessment in Golo and Rokero , most participants reported that while their children previously had support, the lack of case management and basic PSS continued to be insufficient. Although some aid originations have succeeded to the provision of basic needs, UASC are still lacking support and further protection assistance and counselling services that help them to overcome the situation they passed through. REMCO staff in the three suggested areas (Golo and Rokero) highlighted the need to severe humanitarian situations and need for an immediate scale up response to the critical situations of children particularly in Central Darfur. Protection partners are concerned that this situation will exacerbate the vulnerability of children to danger level. The proposed project will respond to the growing humanitarian crisis of returning children in Central Darfur to provide lifesaving protection assistance and services to most at risk boys, girls and adolescents. The project will increase the resilience of children through playing, learning and exploring their skills of coping, and will stood as voice of the most at-risk children and PSNs to establish the bases of future leaders, it will also encourage working together the create the positive sense of brotherhood between communities and the activities will be as a mediation tools to bring the children of return communities and stabled communities who doesn't experience displacement, the activities will be based on human rights approach therefore we are expecting at the end of this project return communities will be able to change negative behaviors and believes and consider human rights based approach in their daily culture practices.
