Project Summary

Zimbabwe is facing threats from the COVID-19 outbreak. As of the 7th of May 2020, the WHO reported that globally, the pandemic has resulted in 3,634,172 confirmed cases and 251,446 confirmed deaths in 206 countries, areas or territories with cases. Ministry of Health and Child Care (MoHCC) reports that as at 6th May 2020 Zimbabwe has reported 34 confirmed cases and four lives have been lost as a result of COVID-19. The first case was reported on the 21st of March 2020. Local transmission started on the 24th of March 2020. The provision of safe water, sanitation, and hygienic conditions is essential to protect human health during this period of the COVID-19 outbreak. Ensuring good and consistently applied WASH and waste management practices in communities, homes, schools, marketplaces, and health care facilities will help prevent human-to-human transmission of the COVID-19 virus. (WHO 2020). According to the ZIMVAC (2019) assessments in both rural and urban areas, a total of 7.7 million people (including 3.7 million children) were in urgent need of humanitarian assistance in 2019. For 2020, the situation is expected to worsen and this has seen the UN and wider humanitarian community, in coordination with the government revising the 2020 Humanitarian response plan with significant scale-up of response projected to address the significant level of humanitarian needs. This COVID-19 preparedness and response project is specifically targeted at WASH needs arising from and aggravated by the current COVID 19 outbreak in the targeted districts and areas. Christian Care, one of the leading local NGOs in Zimbabwe aims at facilitating project implementation in Gutu Rural districts of Zimbabwe. Christian Care intends to among other things:
• Support the setting up of hand washing stations at various public places.
• Support mass media campaigns on COVID-19 prevention using various channels of communication (billboards, radio shows, radio jingles etc) taking into account literacy levels and access of different groups.
• Support the health system to ensure availability of basic water and sanitation services in the key prioritized health care facilities.
• Providing access to critical handwashing facilities including soap and alcohol-based sanitizers, WASH-related hygiene kits.
• Ensure field and front-line staff engaged in WASH humanitarian responses have adequate access to PPE and practice key risk reduction practices like social distancing.

1 - Activities

The project will strengthen the Water Provision; Provision of NFIs and Health Promotion aspects. These aspects will be implemented through the existing WASH structures within the targeted district. The District Water and Sanitation Sub Structure will spearhead the implementation process. the following activities will be implemented:• Support the setting up of hand washing stations at various public places.• Support mass media campaigns on COVID-19 prevention using various channels of communication (billboards, radio shows, radio jingles etc) taking into account literacy levels and access of different groups.• Support the health system to ensure availability of basic water and sanitation services in the key prioritized health care facilities. • Providing access to critical handwashing facilities including soap and alcohol-based sanitizers, WASH-related hygiene kits.• Ensure field and front-line staff engaged in WASH humanitarian responses have adequate access to PPE and practice key risk reduction practices like social distancing.
