Needs

The ongoing war in Syria has led to a deterioration in the humanitarian, livelihood, service, and health situation. The recent events have reflected an increase in the needs of all kinds. According to recent estimates and the estimates provided from various resources international, local and civil society resources, more than 2.35 million people in Rural Damascus have been displaced .The estimates also point out that 1.3 million displaced people have returned to their homes, in addition to original residents in conflict areas.Furthermore, nearly 48% of hospitals serving the targeted area, are out of service according to assessment provided by many international and local parties. Also, more than 25% of medical centers were completely destroyed and are out of service due to the war, and more than 60% were directly targeted with theft and lack of supplies, and are operating at a minimum capacity. Needs Priority in the Targeted Area: 1-Primary, secondary medical care and trauma: urgent needs are at 65% of people in urgent need of medical coverage. The needs of children, women, and elderlies in those areas have reached to 81%. According to HNO statistics for 2017, the project targets area where an estimated nearly 200.000People in need for medical help reside. 700.000 people are in immediate need for medical care according to assessment provided, which was based on statistics accumulated through local reports, WHO reports, government and local reports, and monitoring visits for Mobadaret Ahl Al Sham. According to HNO, nearly 41% of people in need of medical care are between the ages of 1month – 17 years, while 53.9% of people in need of medical help are between the ages of 18-59. According to HNO, 51% of those in need of medical care are females.The details for the special study for the medical project “Amaluna” based on the private assessment of Mubadaret Ahl Al Sham to assess the needs of the beneficiaries for the medical project. Implementation of activities

First, Preparing the location: Th project needs a stable location, which is a rented premise for 6 months with the center 150m.The place will be ready within 20 working days.Second, contracting with 5, physicians of various specialties and with 1 lab specialists.Third, contracting with 1 manager and 4 employees (3 females and 1 male). Forth, targeting policy: targeting people who fit the criteria for the relief intervention, taking into consideration the criteria from the private data back of the project. Those beneficiaries are registered at the international relieve agencies and the civil associations in their area. The project plan by groups aims to: 1. Elderly women are among the bigger group of the targeted PoCs through the project, and because women’s mobility is limited in these area to leave their towns around and seek treatment, and also 51% of the population in those towns is females and the project groups them by most vulnerable and poor as per the international criteria. 1) Women-headed household, 20%2) Affected women of low income returning from displacement, 15%3) Women with special needs, 5%2. Children, the project targets children (boys and girls) under 12 years of age, 18%3. Elderlies, (males and females) : 1) Who fit the criteria with no supporter, 10%2) Who fit the criteria with special needs, 5%4. Adolescents, boys and girls between the ages of 13-24:1) Girls, 15% of those who fit the criteria, 2) Boys, 7% of those who fit the criteria, 3) Male adolescents, 5% of those who fit the criteria, Fifth, contracting with hospitals, for Artificial Limbs, Radiology and Optometry centers .Sixth, conduct weekly medical sessions at the center according to the following: Social studies with 20 work hours per week. Medical sessions with the medical staff with 14 hours every week. A total of 280 hours of medical sessions divided over the period of 20 weeks, except for the time needed to prepare the location. Protection Risk Analysis

1. Gathering the beneficiaries and workers in the project during the medical sessions,Risks that affect beneficiaries and workers in the project such as crowdedness during the medical sessions, which could be uncomfortable for the female beneficiaries and the staff working in the project such as harassment.The Odds:Average /Impact High Mitigation Measure:The project will use entry cards for the medical sessions through which beneficiaries are called after having completed their social profile, ensuring limited number for each session.Monitoring:The mechanism used in this project is by issuing entry cards for the medical sessions as per a suggestion by the social worker prior to the medical sessions which will control and organize the beneficiaries in a way that doesn’t affect the course of the session. Required Resources :Social workers,Center security,& the project manager. Level of Risk :Acceptable 2. Security:Risks that might affect the beneficiaries & the working staff in the project ,Odds and impact :lowThese area have been declared completely safe of conflict. The safety status is determined through reports submitted prior to the medical sessions and are submitted to the project management to be taken into consideration for any changes to the medical sessions.The safety status is evaluated by various governmental resources and associations of the civil society in the areas prior to the sessions. Acceptable.3. Financial:Fluctuations in the currency exchange rates in the market, which could affect procurement of the medical supplies such as hearing and visual aids, mobility and heart devices. Odds :High/Impact:Average.At the beginning of the project, a contract will be signed with a center for visual, hearing, and mobility aids which could be directly affected by procurement. This can be avoided by contracting on a fixed exchange rate with the centers. Conducting market assessment and prices, to learn about the daily, weekly, and monthly exchange rates, and maybe making reliable predictions.The procurement committee of the project will adopt a procurement mechanism in cooperation with the project management in which the prices are not affected by the fluctuations of the market prices. Acceptable.4. Logistics :Risks that affect the workers and beneficiaries from the project include the difficulty in reaching the medical center. Odds:low/Impact:Average.A study has been conducted on the targeted area on the basis of providing proper medical response in a way that doesn’t affect the access of beneficiaries and workers.The location for the center has been carefully chosen to ensure easy access of the workers and the beneficiaries from the catchment area, and to cover the surrounding area as well.The logistic risks management are the responsibility of the project management and the center management.Acceptable 5.Technical:Technical risks might indirectly affect the beneficiaries such as making referral for a beneficiary in need of prescription glasses. In such case, the risks might include either a delay in receiving the glasses, or the glasses are don’t match the prescription. Odds&impact:low. The project is integrated in that the coordination between the centers will be achieved through mechanism that ensures the beneficiaries receive the aid through the centers only.The beneficiaries receiving such services will be contacted regularly to inquire about the condition of the aid received in a timely manner and in good quality. A complaint box will be located in the center to follow up on the complaints and evaluate the results.The quality and evaluation team of the organization will follow up on all complaints and ensure complete confidentiality, and the beneficiaries will be contacted by the project management, and reports will be filed accordingly Acceptable .

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Protection: the beneficiaries and the project staff might be at risk of harassment when being in isolated places such as waiting rooms, bathrooms. The Odds: low The Measure: prepare the center and separate males from females, while giving the bigger space for the females since the majority of the targeted population are females. As for the bathrooms, there will be 3 bathrooms at the center with locks and proper ventilation, which can be used in cases of emergencies. The 2 bathrooms will be assigned for the females and 1 for males. The organization working within the project will adhere to international agreements and will specially provide complete protection for the beneficiaries and the working staff of the project. The organization will also adopt its own regulations and resort to laws and the governing rules of the Syria Arab Republic in case of harassment, abuse, or assault. 1. Protection: beneficiaries of all nationalities could be subjected to sexual or physical abuse. The Odds: average The Measure: the center is equipped with surveillance cameras that cover the center, except for private rooms such as bathrooms and the examination rooms. There are also confidential box for suggestions and complaints. The organization operating in the project shall adopt all international agreements and in particular ensure the full protection of beneficiaries and the working staff in the project. The organization will also adopt its own rules and resort to the governing laws of the Syrian Arab Republic in the event of any harassment, assault, or abuse; including physical or sexual. There will be constant surveillance by cameras, in addition to a confidential complaint box for beneficiaries and another one for complaints and suggestions for the staff participating in the activity. The organization’s staff will directly monitor the activity, and there will also be an external party for monitoring contracted with privately to conduct monitoring and evaluation through visits to the medical sessions and the social workers.
