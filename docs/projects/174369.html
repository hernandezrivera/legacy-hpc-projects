Project Summary

District Mirpurkhas is multihazard district of Sindh Province badly effected by Locust and Flood 2020 and as of 10th December 2020 flood water is still remained in many parts of the district Mirpurkhas and adjacent districts. Flood 2020 badly effected agriculture lands and orchards of mangoes. It increase the vulnerability of people depending on agriculture products for their only earning source. HelpAge International planned to provide humanitarian support to most vulnerable people including older people, people with disabilities, alone woman headed house holdsand child headed households in the district. The project aims to provide cash grants for restoration of livelihood and supported them in iniating alternate sustainable sources of income generation by providing cash grants and livelihood support. Cash grants support will be conditional with a training aim to build capacity of reciepents on sustainable and diaster resilients sources of income generation.
