Project Summary

KSCS proposed interventions to provide an essential Non Food Items and cash for shelter solution and and provide technical training and capacity building on shelter construction and awareness raising session on fire, flood and risk hazard for 4,200 HHs (21,000 individuals), (1000 HHs with cash for shelter solutions, 2000 HHs with NFIs including ( 1625 HHs PSNs and 375 HHs rain and flood affected in rural Kebkabyia) and 6000 persons with awareness and training) from protracted IDPs, returnees and vulnerable residents, including women, men , girls and boys affected by heavy rain and flash flooding that inundated Kebkabyia town on 08/ August/ 2020, to protect them from hazard and elements and other environmental related diseases as immediate life saving, based on the inter agency mission assessment findings conducted IOM, UNHCR, FAO, WHO, WFP and other INNOGs and NNGO including KSCS led by OCHA, and in coordination with government line Ministries , community leaders, the assessment conducted on 13/8/2020 to 16/ 8/2020, the key finding results that: About 13,969 HHs were affected by rain and flash flooding in Kebkabyia town ( 6,670 HHs were reported completely damaged and 7, 299 HHs were reported partial damaged), in addition to 375HHs were affected in 11 villages of rural Kebkabyia, IOM provided Non Food Items to the 6,670 caseload HHs , but no and shelters provided for any of those affected caseloads( 14,344 HHs) including urban and 11 villages of rural Kebkabyia, about 30% of these caseload were still hosted in schools and other in open yards and with their children and elders groups among them and due to nearest Winter season which might be harmful to them, therefore KSCS as a first line res ponder and based on Kebkabyia as well as one of ESNFIs partners

As KSCS is signed in Protection from Sexual Exploitation and abuse of Power /PSEA Joint framework Group as a member of this group , KSCS committed to adopt PSEA policy in all of its programming included the beneficiaries, staff, volunteers and all partners and stakeholders, in addition KSCS standard MEL system will promote accountability to affected beneficiaries, donors and stakeholders. To give community members a voice and contribute to effective dialogue with staff during implementation, KSCS will utilize beneficiary accountability that emphasizes the importance of listening and understanding the needs of beneficiaries.
