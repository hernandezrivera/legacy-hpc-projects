Project Summary

The association has a large land area of 29,000 square meters, on which the school will be constructed with floor area of 15,000 sq metres spread across 3 building blocks, as well as other spaces for the school's facilities. The school is planned to have 70 classrooms with a capacity of 3,640 students. The association has obtained all official approvals and all the necessary engineering studies and plans have been completed and construction work has started, but the completion rates are minimal due to lack of financial liquidity.
The project aims to take advantage of the good reputation of the association's schools and attract high-income students to enroll in this model school as well as rent out school facilities such as playgrounds. to achieve a sustainable income sufficient to create free education opportunities for children of households in need. The project also aims to contribute to alleviating school dropouts in the target area while decreasing the congestion in the few schools in which it is located.

3 - Needs

All areas of eastern Ghouta have witnessed large-scale military operations that have destroyed a large number of schools. Since 2018, some schools in several areas of eastern Ghouta have been rehabilitated to support survival. However, the number of rehabilitated and currently functioning schools is still insufficient to meet the region's need for returning families. This has exacerbated school drop-out rates, given the poor capacity of schools in the target area.Data from educational authorities in eastern Ghouta during the current academic year indicate that the number of out-of-school students due to lack of capacity reached 6,231. Out-of-school students in basic education are divided into 1,570 males, 2400 females and in high school to 835 males and 1,420 females. Likewise, the West Ghouta areas near the project site have 3,220 out-of-school students in basic education to 1,550 males, 570 females and in high school to 750 males and 350 females. This means that the target areas are classified as areas of extreme need.

4 - Implementation of activities

Our organization will oversee the cladding and equipment of the three buildings dedicated to this project and will ensure the buildings have all necessities for a fully functional school.Upon completion of the first stage, the organization will continue to run the school with students (educational years 1 - 12) from within the target area.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The possibility of commitment to sexual assault and violence within these areas is probability due to the poor living conditions and because of the psychological pressures during the Syrian crisis and the displacement movement. this project aims to avoid these risks. Therefore, those in charge of the project or the executors must be obligated to sign a written pledge that such risks will not occur, or to conduct awareness sessions against these risks for the workers, and the families.And work to restrict communication between workers and residents of the targeted area and keep work during the daytime due to the lack of electricity.
