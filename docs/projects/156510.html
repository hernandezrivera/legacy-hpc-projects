Project Summary

The project aims to establish fully equipped catheterization laboratory in Bab Al Hawa hospital to provide the needed therapeutic and diagnostic catheterization procedures needed for patients with cardiac conditions. In addition to providing all medicines and medical consumables needed for these procedures and operations, and the recovery services after the operation.

Needs

Entering the seventh year of the crisis, the scale, severity, and complexity of needs across Syria remain overwhelming. Some 13.1 million people in Syria requires humanitarian assistance. 1 Of these, 5.6 million people are in acute need due to a convergence of vulnerabilities resulting from displacement, exposure to hostilities, and limited access to basic goods and services. 2 Conflict continues to be the principal driver of humanitarian needs, with the civilian population in many parts of the country exposed to significant protection risks which threaten life, dignity and wellbeing on a daily basis. The destruction of life-sustaining civilian infrastructure and services such as water, sanitation and electricity systems, as well as attacks affecting hospitals, schools, housing land and property have continued to undermine support structures in urban and rural areas, ultimately endangering civilian lives and hampering the return of the displaced populations after the cessation of hostilities. The Health Resources Availability Mapping System (HeRAMS) implemented by the Health sector in Syria, indicated that of the public health facilities across all 14 governorates and 105 health districts, (including hard to reach and UN-declared besieged areas) more than half of public hospitals and health centres have either closed or are only partially functional.11.3 Million people in need for healthcare services in Syria, high percentage of them are in the NW of Syria, (5.5 M Male, 5.8 M Female – 1.3 M Children 0-4 years, 3.4 M Children 5-17 years, 6.1 M Adults 18-59 years, and 0.5 M Elderly >59 years).(HNO 2018)This situation had led to the destruction of all hospitals capable of conducting catheterization procedures in NW of Syria, and forced the people to be referred to Turkey for such health care services. Without any hospitals capable of providing catheterization procedures in NW of Syria, patients will suffer from the lack of this service and the rate of mortality will continue to rise due to the complications of lengthening the time of intervention.This center will cover the lack of catheterization service of Idlib and Afrin as there is no other free centers providing this type of medical service. And we in Bab Al Hawa Hospital (BHH) have all the trained and qualified medical staff who is capable of running the center as required. The cardiac department in BHH can provide the necessary supporting services for patients who need catheterization procedures and can be fully integrated with this center. The cardiology department provide its services for about 1400 patient per month and for the time being all cases that need catheterization procedures are referred to Turkey, and by establishing this center all these referred cases will be able to get these services without being referred to Turkey.The project now is waiting for fund approval from the donor, and as soon as it gets approved the project will cover this lack of catheterization services needed in NW of Syria.

Implementation of activities

The project will provide BBH with the equipment required to establish a fully equipped catheterization lab to provide therapeutic and diagnostic catheterization procedures for patients with heart conditions.The medical staff at the center will receive all cardiac cases from Bab Al Hawa Hospital and the cases referred to the center from NW of Syria, and will conduct the necessary procedures whether it is a diagnostic or therapeutic.Patients will stay for at lease 6 hours after they have the catheterization operation, as a recovery period, they will be monitored and evaluated for any possible risks. After that and if their condition is stable, they leave the center.The project will provide the lab with all the medicines and medical consumables needed for these procedures. The center will provide this service to all patients in NW of Syria, as Bab al Hawa Hospital is considered the main referral point for the secondary health care service, and it’s for now the most advanced medical facility in the NW of Syria.The center provides its service to all patients with all ages, men and women, even though the highest number of beneficiaries are expected to be from the adults and elderly people due the nature of the medical services provided in the center.Part of the implementation activities is to collect feedback data from the beneficiaries throughout simple and clear complaint mechanisms, the data collected through these mechanisms are analysed and reviewed by the quality team, and the necessary actions are undertaken to solve any issues faced by the beneficiaries regarding the provision of the service.
