Project Summary

The project will include training for jobs that are done physically (based on the situation) or online if the condition is suitable for physical work. The main training topics will be ( proposal writing Web-Design, E-Marketing, and Media Production), training programs that are required for the office jobs (Languages, MS. Office), business skills (Entrepreneurship and Introduction to NGOs), Maintenance training programs (Mobile maintenance and Computer maintenance),
The training will offer a "blending" of theoretical and hands-on teaching methods (upfront teaching, visuals, group discussions, practical exercises, learning tests, on-site visits, etc.) and combine different methods in order not to lose the attention of the audience.
Since most training modules will require both conventional classroom teaching and practical/hands-on exercises, IHR will seek the most appropriate training venue for each training subject that offers closed and open-air spaces (workplaces visits, labs, etc.) for the practical exercises.
After the Vocational Training end, three hundred (300) beneficiaries will preparing thiers ipersonaly or integration propsalas the best 200 project will be selected and each proposal will receive 700 $ for each participant to support their activities.

4 - Needs

The NW region is facing huge humanitarian and development needs. The unstable situation in the target area caused by the ongoing war in Syria, the migration crisis, and the additional effects of the COVID-19 pandemic has increasingly adverse effects on the economic, economic, and social situation. Both IDPs and HHs struggle to find a job, the inability to support their family, and difficulties in obtaining education for children and young people. The tenth year of the humanitarian crisis is marked by worries about the economy and its impact on the country's stability. Frustration is growing due to the prolonged stay of displaced persons and the limited number of returns, which results in long-term economic, social, and environmental problems in NW. At the same time, it is worth mentioning that most need assessment conducted in NW stated that the lack of lasting security in Syria, the lack of shelter, lack of access to services, and earning opportunities are the main barriers against the rotation of the lifecycle. Even before the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic, there were mass closures in response to the economic crisis that erupted in September 2019. This led to a catastrophic increase in unemployment and poverty.In Afrin district, 80 % of population numbers are IDPs the youth, women, and PWD are considered the most vulnerable groups in target areas, furthermore, due to the abovementioned bad economic situation most youths in Afrin are an easy to target for recruiting in the army group and the PWD and women will stay under GBV risks so so IHR believe that the center will serve a broad segment.Therefore, based on the above information, IHR believes that the creation of training centers will provide young peo

Protection risk (2): Lack/di􀂨culty of accessibility for PWDs in the proposedactivities. Mitigating measures: 1- Availability of wheelchair for PWD at the reception point(IRD contribution), a trained staff to deal with PWDs, Priority for the PWDs, included in thecriteria, House to house distributions Protection Risk (3): Sometimes, crowding duringdistribution of bread, may not provide the services at the desire quality, which might affectthe BNFs dignity, especially women. Mitigating measures: :Set up separate waiting andentry lines at registrations Raise the awareness of community leaders on GBV and childprotection principles and make sure that all BNFs can voice their concerns through thedesignated channels. Protection Risk (4): Gender-based violence and sexual exploitationfor women during implementation of the project.
