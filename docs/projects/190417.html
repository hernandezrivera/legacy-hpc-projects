Project Summary

The proposed intervention is envisioned to improve GBV through provision of immediate and comprehensive lifesaving services to survivors, and GBV risk mitigation for vulnerable women and girls through the following interventions: Clinical management of Rape (CMR), case management (CM); psychosocial support services (PSS) including psychosocial counselling, psychosocial first aid (PFA), social networking and access to information on available GBV services, through women and girls friendly spaces/safe spaces, and establishment and strengthening of GBV referral pathways (to enable access to comprehensive GBV services including legal, medical and livelihood services) to most vulnerable and displaced female headed and people living with disability special need in the target communities, including IDP and Host communities in the target locations of Amhara, Tigray and Somali. Alight will also strengthen GBV risk reduction through strengthening and engaging community protection groups, distribution of dignity kits, whistles to vulnerable women, girls and mobility reaching Aids and Grabbers for PLWD, provide capacity building and trainings to community volunteers/workers and GBV service providers to enhance service provision and risk reduction for women and girls, and conduct safety audits for GBV risk assessments to inform the project implementation and decisions, among other interventions. The proposed project will therefore increase accessibility, quality and coordination of GBV, and Disability services for conflict and drought affected communities in Amhara, Tigray and Somali Woradas. Alight will work with various stakeholders to ensure women and girls are protected from GBV and receive support when they experience harm. Some of the stakeholders include line ministries at state government level, the local administration, the police, and women leadership at community level, community and religious leaders and will establish protection networks for women and girls. Community action plans and protection mechanisms will be developed and implemented to reduce protection risks for women and girls including those in IDP settings.
The project will assist in capacity building local authorities, IDP committees etc. on IDP rights/obligations. In addition, the project will also aim to mainstream protection across sectors through advice on effective implementation of protection-sensitive and rights - based approach into the relief assistance for other humanitarian actors/ clusters – PWG/All sectors, training and sensitizing duty bearers and other key stakeholder on protection concerns including GBV, and freedom and safety of movement.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1) Establish Friendly women and girls’ Safe space: Establish safe spaces to provide support socializing, resources and provide a platform for information sharing, awareness raising, peer-to-peer networking and support, obtain vocational skill training, PSS provision including counselling, referrals, and linkage to community protection structures. Activity 2) Training of duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff (GBV): Alight will provide Clinical Care for Sexual Assault Survivors (CCSAS) capacity building opportunities with WHO guidelines to health and GBV staff and provide Health facilities' supplies, guidelines, protocols, and regulations to support timely and quality GBV service delivery. Activity 3) GBV and Disability Awareness raising advocacy and public outreach campaigns on GBV prevention, response and mitigation (GBV): Alight will engage communities in discussing GBV, child protection and disability risks including the identified risks and ways of addressing them. Awareness activities will be intensified during the marking of international days like the international women’s day, sexual awareness month, HIV/AIDS day, disability day in relation to GBV and the 16 days of activism. Activity 4) Provision of risk mitigation items including Mobility Aid Kits for disabilities, Solar light, whistles and solar lamps installation: Alight will procure disability mobility kits to respond to the need of people living with special need, disability is estimated 9.5% of general population in Ethiopia due to violence prone and recent conflicts.Activity 5) Strengthen protection assessments, analysis, data collection and reporting 6) Capacity building to duty bearers and others: Alight will support train key duty bearers, key stake-holders, IDP committees, leaders and other clusters on GBV basic principles, IDP rights, obligations and principles of camp management. Activity 7) Protection advocacy Initiatives: Based on the assessment data, the project will advocate against forced evictions of displaced population in Tigray and Amhara and ensure the freedom of movement, and protection of other key rights in coordination with UNHCR, UNCEF and UNFPA.Activity 8) Strengthen the protection mainstreaming within the humanitarian actors in Somalia: Alight will conduct a series of protection mainstreaming trainings for international NGOs, Local NGOs and CBOs staff from their various sectors. The primary objective of this activity is to ensure that all humanitarian interventions are provided in a safe, dignified and in accountable way. Activity 9) Identify and train GBV community activists: In line with the recommendations of the safety audits and protection assessments, Alight will identify and train GBV activists at IDP site level. Activity 10) Create and train women vigilant groups: Alight will identify and train women activist groups in Amhara, Tigray and Somali IDP camps. The established groups will be trained for GBV protection, reporting mechanisms and will be the first point of contact for GBV survivors at the regions. The activist groups will monitor GBV incidents and support the survivors to report and seek services. Activity 11) Conduct Safety Audit Assessment for 60 IDP camps in the target Woradas. Activity12) Provide Survivor – Centered Psychosocial support Services Activity 13) Provide Case Management capacity strengthening to local actors and GBVWG.Activity 14) Unconditional Cash Transfer (UCT) to 300 survivors or vulnerable female households.Activity 15) Procurement and distribution of dignity kits to vulnerable women and girls including survivors.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

The proposed project will operate a comprehensive set of organizational policies and procedures in line with international best practices to ensure Accountability to Affected Populations; this ensures participation of affected populations at all stages of programme design, development and delivery. An initial consultation has been conducted with communities, to confirm their commitment to participation in the project, and to ensure ownership over project activities and outcomes. ALIGHT will use several mechanisms to solicit the perspectives of affected populations to improve programming, ensuring that these mechanisms are appropriate, robust and streamlined. We will consider contextual challenges including (i) creation of safe spaces for beneficiary feedback (given the age and gender dimensions of the project and increased exposure to risk as a result); (ii) clear and consistent communication around beneficiary feedback to mitigate safety and security concerns related to reporting; and (iii) design and delivery of verbal and visual beneficiary feedback mechanisms appropriate to children, adolescents and adults in low literacy, low resource environment including individual interviews, group discussions, narratives, maps, pictorial representations and role plays. Following the selection and set up of participation mechanisms, We will (i) offer specific orientation on selection criteria and the scope of the programme to communities; (ii) set up a project file in communities, containing information about project resources, planning and implementation, and promoting accountability to affected populations; and (iii) measure, at regular intervals, awareness of and access to feedback amongst beneficiaries. All information will be shared in local languages, and community focal points, project staff and other service delivery team will be trained on key communication techniques and terminologies to ensure engagement of local level leaders, including religious leaders; The proposed project includes the establishment of Community Protection Committees and activists who will meet once a week with project personnel present to offer feedback, raise concerns or questions and propose recommendations. In line with our Accountability to Affected Populations [AAP] policies, all meeting minutes will be recorded and shared with communities to optimize accountability and transparency, and a complaints mechanism will be instituted in each of the communities to allow for anonymous reporting.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

ALIGHT will ensure accountability of the interventions provided by setting up appropriate mechanisms through which affected populations can measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints. These mechanisms will include following up and assessing the effectiveness of established Complaint Mechanism including placing a complaint box in all service centres and informing beneficiaries, creating hotline for complaints and feedback, Kuja Kuja customer feedback and conducting survivor exit interviews to assess the satisfaction of survivors with the services provided, assess staff attitudes towards beneficiaries, including survivors charged with provision of services, as well as garner information on how to improve services. Further, the Protection sector will work with the Health and WASH sectors to support the development of self-protection capacities and assist people to claim their rights. Overall, ALIGHT’s key interventions will promote age appropriate, inclusivity in decision making and accountability processes, such as including marginalized females into PFAs and CFs and safe space Activists, GBV focal points and CBPNs committees at camp levels. Staff will be sensitized on how to identify and ensure that members from marginalized groups are accepted and treated with the dignity they deserve; providing infrastructure that caters to their wellbeing, such as constructing ramps at entrances to the health facilities and at the latrines for those with mobility issues; promoting recruitment of persons from these groups by ensuring that all recruitment advertisements bear clear information encouraging the receipt of applications form qualified persons including those with disabilities; and developing IEC/BCC materials that promote community understanding and inclusion.
