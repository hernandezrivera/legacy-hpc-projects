Project Summary

Child malnutrition, women and girls and person with disabilities are in urgent needs of assistance in Rubkona and Renk counties as these county received large number home newly arriving women and children. 1. Needs

The arms conflict in Khartoum on 15 April 2023, came with humanitarian crisis along with refugees and thousands becomes homeless, crippled the economic stability in South Sudan and Sudan. The Refugees and returnees arriving to South Sudan suffered from hunger, disease, homelessness multifaceted conflict with women and girls suffers from SGBV when fleeing the war. Many displaced persons, refugees and returnees from Sudan are severely reeling behind poverty, land disputes , SGBV in conflict related sexual violence (CRSV), separating families and caseload of unaccompanied children, and Persons With Disabilities (PWDs) are reeling behind nothingness, hunger, disease, lands disputes, lack of emergency dispute resolution to deescalate tension between refuges, returnees and host communities Unity and Upper Nile. The project will provide an emergency assistance to most vulnerable returnees, refugees and host communities in Rotriak Payma -Rubkona County and Renk – North, Refugee Camps through provision of emergency GBVs assistance, shelter, legal referral services. The project is anchored on general protection objectives that address the needs of refugees, returnees and host communities. DRI will coordinate with prevention network in the communities through awareness-raising sessions conducted with women, men, boys and girls receiving GBV risks knowledge and reporting as well as Women, men, girls and boys reached through GBV prevention initiatives (e.g. SASA, EMAP, community cares programming) and Men, women, girls, and boys supported with cash and voucher assistance will probate safety of returnees durable solution, and provision of emergency shelters, protection awareness on arrival entry points and border areas and promotes community base protection solution. The project is in -line with the Cluster Strategic Objectives which emphasis the emergency assistance for Women, men, boys, girls, and other marginalized vulnerable groups such as persons with disabilities. While basic cultural premises of returnees, refugees and host communities may not change, effective linkages can change a culture from antagonistic and mistrustful outlook to trusting and responsive to the interests of the vulnerable community. As refugees and returnees begin to understand that conflicts can be resolved and protection, justice and peaceful living can be obtained fairly by methods that address the people’s needs, the overall level of tension is reduced, trust increases and society becomes more peaceful. Thus increasing coordination and improve linkages between the stakeholders network to link legal aid lawyers, paralegals, and other justice sector actors, including those working with customary justice mechanisms, both with each other and with legal aid providers. Prevention of SGBV; land disputes and empower communities on PSS, group, and individual counselling and mitigate protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience. DRI shall strengthen mobile and static presence accompanied by provision of critical protection assistance and services, including specialized response services, tailored to the specific needs of women, girls, boys and person with disabilities and gender age groups in emergencies situation. The project will provide assistance to total number of 19659 beneficiaries disaggregated as (4,000 women on GBV, 5000 men, 5000 boys, and 5000 girls) will be reached in Rubkona –Rotriak Payam in Unity and Renk -North, Upper Nile. This will provide legal aid services to 1659 PWDs, men, women, girls, and boys during the project period. DRI will continue to raise communities’ awareness on HLP rights, legal framework, and available mechanisms for disputes resolution for returnees; IDPS. The project establish GBV, GP, and CP confident centres for reporting while the project breakdown shall be as follow, GBV 30% GP 40%, CP 20%, and 10% PWDs.
