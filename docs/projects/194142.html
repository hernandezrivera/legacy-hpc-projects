Project Summary

Mara and Elradoom IDP and Refugees camps since 2018 providing education , Livelihood, Sanitation and child protection activities to affected communities, in 2021 the interventions were mainly education and Protection . This project focus on responding to the needs of the newly displaced communities in East Jebel Mara and Elradoom localities in South Darfur, more than 15,000 new IDP will benefit for multi-Sectoral response that will cover Education, protection with focus on (child protection and GBV) and Education. The affected community is targeted by the two sectors and RDO will establish complain mechanism to ensure issues of concern are raised by beneficiaries and stakeholders and addressed timely and properly.
Protection – child protection and GBV:
The child protection will focus on responding to psychologically distressed children affected by the emergency through establishment of 2 child friendly spaces CFS to address the psychological distress of 2000 children age 3-18 years (1200 girls/ 800 boys) through outdoor activities in the afternoon, through the project hygiene and harmful traditional practices will considered to ensure the provision of prevention activities. Provide family tracing and reunification FTR to 20 unaccompanied/ separated and missing children through community based mechanisms and the States Council of Child Welfare, the project also seeks to provide assistance/kits for the most vulnerable including 80 children with special needs (45 male, 35 female). The GBV seeks to provide direct support to 1500 community members (750 female and 750 male) affected by the Gender based violence and to ensure that they are recovered from the effect of the violence; the intervention focus on establishing and strengthening the referral pathway to ensure access to appropriate services available in the community, it also support provision of personal hygiene kits and recreational kits based on need of intervention. In this regards community based mechanisms will be established to respond to the child protection issues, community support group is created and effectively contributed to the response of the affected people and monitoring children issues. It will also raise the awareness of key protection messages among displaced and host communities (men, women, girls and boys, teachers. Topics will cover, GBV, CP, Case management, FTR and HIV/AIDS and COVID measurement among children and their communities.

1 - Needs

According to humanitarian needs overview 15.8 million vulnerable residents , 3.7 million internal displace people (IDPs) and 0.9 million returnees in Sudan they are in need of humanitarian assistance and protection support in camps ,return areas and host communities . South Darfur is one of the states with the highest needs in Sudan . East Jebel Marra and Elradoom still struggle with issues around access to protection services. EJM struggle with unending conflict and the Ministries of Education and Social welfare therefore struggle to ensure that appropriate teachers and social workers are sent to the area ,for Elradoom the logistical constrain during the rainy season is affecting the movement of the teachers from the capital to the deep field to fulfill their task in addition to the big number of out school children in the two localities and the fact of using the children in the mining activities ,no place or activities for out school children and child married still practiced by communities and FGM as well . in Elradoom the waves of the refugees from Central Africa due to the instability in neighboring villages in Central Africa that let the communities to flew their village and to cross the boarders to Elradoom (cross boarder tribes) to stay with their relatives in the same camps . the internal conflict in EJM have worsened and hindered access to services in addition to that availability of basic services in both East Jebel Mara and Elradoom is extremely limited and few actors are operational in the two localities .all those factors let RDO to prepare the needs assessment for this project.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Objectives by address emergency humanitarian protection needs of newly displaced persons and other affected population, including women and children and supports Saving lives of vulnerable people affected by conflict and disaster; it also provides humanitarian protection to affected populations; and strengthen the Community Child Protection Networks . The proposed Education interventions designed to fit with sector specific objective to sustain and expand protection and GBV services for IDPs and seriously affected population through provide quality of protection services (sector objective) and expand to implement at institutions and community level to maximize benefits and increase access to protection and GBV services. Multi-sectorial is also ensured by adopting sectors interlink approach in the implementation to ensure greater impact for the benefit of the affected children and women in the two localities. For instance, Child Protection sector will consider children below 6 years and will provide psycho-social training for teachers, case management and PSS for children. For GBV will focus in providing specialized training for GBV service provider and strengthening the women capacities through the income generation activities followed by startup kits for 150 members (75 M – 75 F) and the hosting workshop on EORE for aid workers. RDO will Coordinate with partners from WASH sector in the two localities to conduct hygiene promotion awareness and help with its technical expertise to establish WASH facilities in the targeted CFS.
