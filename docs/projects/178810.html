Project Summary

The proposed project targets Azaz and Aghtrin sub-districts,
GAN aims to enhance resilience of affected communities in Mare and Aghtrin through provision of livelihood opportunities and to have community resilience strengthened:
GAN aims to support the livelihoods of vulnerable crises- affected communities, by starting with Market mapping which aims to identify market gaps and needs, and then announce and selection of participants according to eligibility and vulnerability criteria and giving priorities to women headed HHs, PWDs when possibile and later provide vocational training for 160 persons on topics that will be identified according to the market mapping with giving priorities to vocations related to PPEs production and other locally produced general covid19 protection related items such as hygiene items.
After finishing the VT, GAN will deliver SME trainings for the successful applicants considering their attendance percentage, tests results and trainers’ evaluations according to which each participant will submit a business plan which will be evaluated by a formed committee.
Each successful business plan will be supported with cash grants of 1200$ for the first 95 best plans to be paid on three tranches. While the remaining 65 participants will be supported with job related kits of 400$ each to help them engage in the market and work.
These participants attended all the vocational training and the SME training, but their business plans could not get high scores to be funded will be supported with job related kits.

4 - Needs

13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance, with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline. The Syrian pound is in virtual freefall having lost 78% of its value since October 2019, while price increases for staple goods are at an all-time high. More than 90% of the population is now estimated to live below the poverty line. Summary of Needs in 2021 OCHAThe COVID-19 pandemic has accelerated this economic downturn by further reducing already sparse income-generating opportunities in a context where 50% of the work age population is now estimated unemployed. Summary of Needs in 2021 OCHAIn particular, there was a decrease in employment of 18.2% for males and 19.7% for females. Most enterprises lost significant revenue ranging from 40% to 100%, and about 13% had to close their operations.ERL needs remain considerable in scale, severity, and complexity across the country, with an estimated 11.6M people needing dignifying early recovery and livelihood support.While hostility induced displacement in early 2020 generated additional needs amongst the population in Syria for internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees and host communities.Facing deteriorated living standards, families are increasingly adopting harmful coping mechanisms. Seventy-one per cent of households and 75% of female-headed households have taken on more debt since August 2019. Summary of Needs in 2021 OCHA28% of families now adopt ‘crisis’ or ‘emergency’ food related coping strategies, including withdrawing children from school to have them work instead, selling property, migrating due to lack of food and early child marriage. 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

GAN support equal and safe access to life-saving assistance without discrimination of any kind. pay particular attention to not exclude women, girls, people with disabilities, and other people belonging to disadvantaged or marginalized groupsWithin this operating context and COVID-19,a key Do No Harm principle is to avoid exacerbating additional tensions and conflicts in communities already competing for finite resourcesBeneficiaries may face problems particularly with sexual assault and robbery including at official or unofficial checkpoints while going to and from a distribution point, so the distribution points in the targeted communities will be inside the communities, during daylight hours and safeguard that there is appropriate segregation for women to create a culturally sensitive and safe operational environmentGAN team will include female members to make sure that women feel able to comfortably discuss their needs and constraints, the feedback mechanisms will be available and accessible for all persons with disabilities with specific regard to persons with communication difficulties, where will be providing written, verbal and assisted modes for complaints and feedbackGAN intends to prevent abuse by implementing procedures to protect beneficiaries through best recruitment practices, staff induction, creating an open and aware culture and assigning clear management responsibilities, ensuring that victims are supported, and perpetrators held to account.Information on the referral pathway will be circulated through posters and banners at the distribution points and training centers.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Beneficiaries may be exposed to exploitation by project stakeholders, especially vulnerable women.It will ensure that the project activities are adequately explained and the community is aware of their rights and duties towards the project and towards some of them to mitigate these risks.In addition, the mechanism of feedback and permanent monitoring of the reactions of stakeholders will contribute to knowing any risk and determining the mechanism for dealing with it.GAN is a member of PSEA network attending a monthly meeting and following the guidelines, so to mainstream the protection from sexual exploitation and abuse in the project GAN have developed a policy stating standards of conduct including acts of SEA and a work plan to implement the policy in place, GAN will ensure all staff members are signed and adhered to a code of conduct policy, PSEA and will support the members to access relevant pieces of training (PSEA), ensuring that non-humanitarian stakeholders who will be in touch with beneficiaries such as (suppliers) will be informed and adhere to the humanitarian principles and code of conduct, PSEA posters will be available.
