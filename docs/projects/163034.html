Project Summary

Provision of safe water, NFIs and Hygiene and Sanitation awareness to vulnerable targeted populations of Buhera District, Chipinge District and Mbare, Waterfalls and Highfield, Harare.

The targeted districts of Buhera and Chipinge in Manicaland Province and Harare suburbs are districts that have been identiﬁed in need of support for the current El Nino season as well as the ZIMVAC 2019 findings. Cyclone Idai further exacerbated the situation in Buhera and Chipinge. Water coverage remains low in Buhera district, as of the June 2019 ZIMVAC Assessment report, showed that 16 per cent of households travelled more than 1km to fetch water from the nearest main water source and 32 per cent over 500m, still above the Sphere standard. GOAL will target wards 24,25,26,27,28 and 29 of Buhera district.
The reduction in water availability is leading communities to compromise on safe sanitation and hygiene thereby increasing the risk of WASH related diseases. GOAL ZW has been operational in Chipinge district for over 15 years and Buhura and Harare for over 2 years, with livelihoods and humanitarian interventions and will use existing structures to ensure that the WASH needs are met in the targeted wards.
Rehabilitation of water points combined with key messages on hygiene and sanitation will be the main focus of this intervention, water point committees will be established and reinforced. IEC materials and NFI’s will be provided by UNICEF in particular in case of a cholera outbreak.

1 - Activities

GOAL’s key proposed activities for targeted districts are:• Borehole rehabilitation and repairs/Borehole drilling • Hygiene and Sanitation awareness provision • Provision of IEC materials • Provision of NFI’s in case of Cholera outbreak Although GOAL will not be channelling funds to local responders, GOAL will directly implement the proposed activities in coordination with and support from local line ministries. GOAL has been present in Chipinge district since it first began operations in Zimbabwe in 2002, in Buhera for 2 years and our country headoffice is based in Harare. GOAL has been delivering cholera response and food security programmes in Harare urban for the past two years, including in the target areas. Based on GOAL’s experience in the target districts, targeting carried out with the involvement of communities will be key to ensure that those most in need will be identified and targeted. GOAL's Complaints Response Mechanism (CRM) and verification exercise will also contribute towards ensuring that the most in need have been targeted. GOAL has an established complaints and feedback response mechanism with an established toll-free line, email address and posters in the local languages that are clearly displayed during distributions. These mechanisms are clearly explained at the onset of the intervention. Due to GOAL’s established presence, areas where gaps exist within the WASH sector have already been identified with the WASH cluster and the communities at district level. Barriers to access for vulnerable beneficiaries (elderly, chronically ill) have been identified and will be addressed during the targeting and implementation phase to ensure that these beneficiaries are not left behind, by sensitising the community and increasing their participation i.e. (support from neighbours for the physically impaired). Barriers specifically related to access of the elderly and the chronically ill to services and inputs have been identified and will be addressed through focused targeting implemented at community level. Women and adolescent boys and girls will be included in the consultation processes and the implementation aims at least 50% of the beneficiaries targeted to be women, and young boys and girls. GOAL's Gender Advisor and Protection Officer are also part of the design and implementation of the project to ensure that key messages are imparted onto the community on protection and GBV. All GOAL staff undergo training on GOAL’s Code of Conduct and sign the document at the onset of their contract. A robust Monitoring and Evaluation process combined with the CRM and involvement of the Social Welfare Department will ensure that issues related to protection and GBV are identified and acted upon swiftly. GOAL will work through established committees at village/ward level and organisations in the district that work with people living with disabilities to ensure that these beneficiaries are included in the consultation and implementation process. Our M&E team will conduct verifications at field level to ensure this objective has been met. Barriers have already been identified during the cyclone response that were directly related to physical ability to participate in consultation processes or to collect inputs. The proposed project aims to ensure that the people identified with disabilities are able to provide their voices through their own identified family members or neighbours, through a signed proxy.
