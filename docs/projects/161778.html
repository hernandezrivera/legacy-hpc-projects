Project Summary

This project aims at providing of Community-Based Integrated Maternal, Neonatal and Child Health Services. It will focus on reproductive health services, through a mobile hub able to breach the gaps that are aggravated by the lack of accessibility. The maternal health aspect seeks to meet the medical needs of pregnant and lactating women by provision of routine antenatal care, post-natal care and management of childbirth, while the neonatal and child component will provide medical care to new-borns and children ensuring that they are immunized and that their illnesses are treated. On top of these activities, Tdh will also conduct awareness raising sessions on reproductive health and conduct training.

1 - Needs

Despite a significant need for specific reproductive and child health in Mafa Central, the area remains under resourced with only 1 Health facility providing Primary Health Care which is supported by UNICEF and is opened from 8am – 4pm daily. There are no secondary or tertiary health facilities in Mafa LGA with the nearest referral hospital located 45minutes drive (1hr 30Min due to several check points on the way) and no availability of public transport. There are currently no referral pathways or networks in Mafa Town from the primary healthcare facility to either secondary or tertiary healthcare facility.

2 - Activities

1) Conduct Reproductive Health education and awareness sessions dedicated to women in reproductive age 2) Conduct Reproductive Health individual consultation throught trained and skilled CHW to women in reproductive age. 3) conduct pre and post natal education and awarness sessions to women in reproductive age 4) conduct pre and post natal individual monitoring consultations (focused on mothers and new born needs) throught trained and skilled CHW. 5) Develop referal system capacitities for safe delivery (MoU with Public Health services and InGO services - medical staff : doctor, midwife and nurses dedicated to consultation pre reference). 6) Realize sensitization sessions on sexually transmissible infections according to the universal precautions 7) Conduct a survey related to hygiene practices through Community health workers8) Conduct community awareness and sensitization. 9) Set up a referal system

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will provide accessible and timely information to the target population, on organizational procedures, structures and processes that affect them.Accountability and Quality Improvement Committee (AQIC)/Health Committee will be constituted by key stake holders within the community, with representatives from different sectors in the community such as adolescent boys and girls, chair ladies and chairmen from the different communities, Bulamas and Lawal, etc. This committee will meet once a month to discuss issues about the services provided, how the services can be improved and how we can continue to work collaboratively.
