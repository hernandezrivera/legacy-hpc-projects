Project Summary

In the 2019 flash appeal Save the Children Zimbabwe (SCZ) plans to support children across the Zimbabwe who face the risk of starvation and malnutrition owing to the forecast El Nino weather phenomenon. Weather expects have warned that the El Nino effect will bring extreme weather condition in 2019 result in drought and high temperatures. The toll will be great on children who are supposed to have uninterrupted education even during emergencies. Save the Children (SC) plans to support 20 of the worst affected schools in 8 districts that include Binga, Hwange, Matobo, Beitbridge, Gokwe North, Gokwe South, Mbire and Rushinga. The project will reach out to 96000 children in the districts above. SCZ plans to promote an emergency sensitive teaching and learning environment. which will include meeting some of children’s dietary needs resulting in improved attendance. The project will work to improve health and sanitary condition in schools to avoid incidences of the spread of communicable diseases like cholera. The project will work to enhance the capacity of the Ministry of Primary And Secondary Education (MoPSE) to manage coordination of interventions of this nature during emergencies.
The specific needs for the children include food so that they are able to continue with their schooling. Water will be a big necessity as this period is associated with very high temperatures resulting in dehydration particularly and not limited to districts like Binga, Beitbridge and Hwange. The increased scarcity of water will also mean that schools will need support with water to ensure that sanitary facilities are clean and handwashing in emphasised. This will be imperative given the continued cholera outbreak across the country. This very difficult time for children requires that we provide Counselling, Life Skills and Psychosocial (PSS)Support to help build resilience among children and hence help them to cope with the trauma of this very difficult time. Children will need further support to attend schools consistently. Over and above school feeding awareness will be required among community members to encourage children to go to school. Dignity kits will be provided for girls so that they do not miss school. Children may not be able to pay school fees or bring sufficient stationery kits as required by the school. Teachers will need support with teaching materials which may be in short supply given the non-payment of school fees. There will be a need to increase the capacity of District Education Officers, Teachers, SDC members, Parents, Students leadership groups and the community to be able to help the changing circumstances of the community and children’s lives. Several training will be required in emergency sensitive teaching, PSS, school feeding, DRR and basic school health and hygiene.
To address the stated children’s needs Save the Children will do some of the following activities, School feeding, providing school water supplies, Cleaning of sanitary facilities, Hand washing, Counselling of students, Psychosocial Camps for children, Psychosocial camps for Girls, Supporting school attendance, Teacher training on emergency sensitive school feeding, Teachers and SDC training on PSS and counselling, Teachers training on emergency sensitive teaching and learning methodologies, District Education Officers training on emergency sensitive monitoring, Supporting girls with dignity kits, School health clubs strengthened and Referrals of children to health support services. The project is expected to cost $2.000 000.
