Project Summary

The project proposes to increase access to lifesaving health for a total of 7397
hard to reach and undeserved people of Aweil North County, targeting 1090 under 5 years children, 3241 children from 5-17 years, 2530 18-60 years and 536 above 60 in the county. The organization will provide integrated health outreach services to both host and internally displaced people of Aweil North County, with two outreach (Mobile Clinic) teams that will operate in various sites on a daily basis. To implement the project activity HEF will recruit 2 clinical officers, 2 nurses, 2 nurse assistants, 2 drug dispensers, 2 nutritionists and 34 Home Health Promoters who will be site based. The project will use a holistic, integrated and multisectoral approach in delivering the health package activities, in which much as it will be a Health Project, program will take into consideration and address some of the health related nutritional, WASH, GBV and Protection concerns especially where there is nexus. Ultimately, the project will aim to immediately reduce morbidity and mortality among the most vulnerable children and caregivers, while promoting longer-term behaviors change adaptation and resilience measures into their communities. Major activities will include consultation and treatment of ailments, integrated management of childhood illnesses (IMCI), Antenatal care (ANC), immunization and referral of complicated cases. Nutrition activities will major on screening and referral of malnourished children U5, pregnant and lactating women to relevant programmes operated by nutrition specific partners and provision of Maternal, infant and young child nutrition (MIYCN) messages and counselling sessions for caregivers, pregnant and lactating clients. WASH integration will entail strategic promotion of hygiene and sanitation practices to establish behavior changes that will serve to decrease the burden of disease on these communities. The programme will support active surveillance for epidemic prone diseases listed by the health cluster as Malaria, ARI, Measles, cholera and meningitis

1 - Needs

Access to health services has remained limited to communities in Aweil North County of Bhar El-Gazal State. In total 41096 people in the county are in need, 6057 U5, 18009 5-17 years, 14055 18-60 and 2975 above 60. According to the (REACH 2020) Area of Knowledge-Neighborhoods (AOKNeighborhoods) assessment conducted in September 2020, 35% of the households experience health living standard gaps (LSG) while 51% and 2% experience WASH LSG and protection LSG respectively. The reports states that 65% of households could not access health services at the existing health facilities and therefore resorted to other coping mechanisms such as delaying to seek for treatment. Majority of people who manage to seek for treatment spend more than three hours to reach the health facilities. Currently, Aweil North has 38 health facilities including five PHCCs and 33 PHCUs. While HPF covers 15 PHCUs and 5 PHCC, Concern Worldwide supports 28 facilities to offer nutrition services both outpost and static sties. A total of 17 health facilities and two community centers are not covered to offer health services while 10 facilities do not provide nutrition services. This means the communities within the catchment areas of the 17 non-functional health facilities are so disadvantaged that they are either under-served or are completely cut off from receiving the health services to live a healthy and productive life. Thus, HEF with its mission to improve health equity among the disadvantaged and vulnerable people affected by poverty, disasters and conflicts across the country will provide the most needed essential health care services to these less privileged communities within the catchment areas of these 17unsupported health facilities like Markot Maluak, Kangkuot, Marengakok, Kiir Kalama, Majak Boy, Kiir Adem, Riang Achol, Mabior Nyang, Gualang, Makuei, Jorbioch, Titagor, Luethlual, Pandit, Mapr Ngok, Lueth Ngor, Rumading and Warojeng where two Mobile Clinic outreach and referral services
