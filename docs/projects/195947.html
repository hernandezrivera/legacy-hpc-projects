Project Summary

The aim of SECD annual plan for 2023 is to support IDP resilience and social cohesion between host communities and IDP families in camps through conducting certified vocational training on basic camp infrastructure rehabilitation, skill training for future jobs, roads rehabilitation in camps and communities that host the IDPs.
SECD is planning to engage more with Early Recovery Cluster as needs are exacerbating and the need to implement activities with sustainable impact is obligatory.
Vocational training will be conducted and dedicated kits will be distributed for 320 trainees, males and females, not less than 10% of them are PWD, about self-maintenance of basic infrastructure in their community/IDP camps, after every workshop SECD will conduct a test and choose two trainees or more, and soft skill training will be conducted for 30 better trainees, to help them to get opportunities for a long term job.
15000 meters of roads rehabilitation will be implemented over the proposed interventions for the next year, considering CFW modality to generate work opportunities.

3 - Needs

The continues suffering and no root solutions have resulted in exacerbating the needs in north west of Syria. Despite the multi-sector intervention, the needs are still increaseing in the absence of sustainable interventions.The need to increase resilience of IDPs and Host community is a must in which, aid-relaince is obvious and is affecting their daily living to cover the need. Jabal Saman, Jisr Al-shoughour and Idleb districts are ones of the top districts that have high intensity of IDP camps and IDP population. The camp residents are continually suffering from lack of livelihood opportunity to generate income and cover essential needs. Based on the Humanitarian Needs Overview 2023, there are almost 14 million people in need across Syria territories. and about 4 million of them are residing Idleb and Aleppo governorates and a high rate of them are in need for ERL and ERL with multi sectoral activities.

4 - Implementation of activities

For 2023, SECD will focus on increasing resilience and supporting cohesion between IDPs and Host people. Campaign training by professional trainers and experts will be conducted for 320 individuals(males and females, and about 10% of them PWD), in 3 districts of Jabal Saman, Idleb and Jisr Al-shoughour.Each campaign will include vocational training in addition, to the distribution of a career kit.The selection of trainees will be based on selection criteria that will be developed with the trainers to make sure most of the possible participants are able to start their careers with proficiency with a certificate of completion. The campaign will be designed to ensure effective participation from local councils, and soft skill training after the vocational training as a part of the campaign will be conducted for 30 better trainees, to help them to get opportunities for a long-term jobs. Based on the selected vocational training interests, SECD will identify what kits will be delivered after successfully completing the training. Vocational training on WASH infrastructure maintenance in IDP camps will recive a kit that they can do the job with it.SECD will ensure half of the trainings will benefit the host community and the other half are IDPs.15000 meters of roads will be rehabilitated within 29 community mainly:- the roads linked between IDP camps and the community.- Within community center affected roads.Lightening will be considered with small led solar lights.
