Project Summary

The socio-political crisis in the North west and South west regions of Cameroon have been ongoing since 2016. Schools have unfortunately been one of the main targets during this crisis. During the 2021-2022 academic year, 66% of schools in both regions were still closed and for the 2022-2023 school year, 54% of schools still remain closed, causing the displacement of about 200,000 school aged children most of who remain out of school. According to the Cameroon Education Cluster Strategy of 2023-2025 an estimated 1,033,000 school-aged children have been affected by the crisis in the North West regions. They have been denied the opportunity to learn and develop themselves, failing in achieving Sustainable Development Goal (SDG), specifically, the goal 4: ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. The current system is leaving more than 955,700 children behind. The impact of the crisis has deprived thousands of older children and youth an education. Displacement being in a child-headed household, being an ex-child soldier or being disabled compounds many of the losses suffered by those deprived of an education. For many of these children and youth, the experience of the crisis has made their participation in education a challenge with the disruption of education due to insecurity and attacks on schools. As a result, the crisis in the NW/SW has had a significant gap in their schooling.
The Accelerated Education Programs are flexible, age appropriate programs that promote access to education in an accelerated time frame for such disadvantaged groups specifically, for out of school, over age children and youth excluded from education or who had their education interrupted due to the conflict. The essence of this program, is to provide out of school, over aged children and youth with equivalent competencies as those in the formal system in an accelerated timeframe, with them transitioning back into the mainstream education. In this context, the AEP will fill a critical gap in the provision of essential educational services to the conflict affected populations and ensure learners receive an appropriate and relevant education responsive to their life circumstances.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Project OverviewThis project aims to provide quality education to conflict-affected children in the Northwest Region of Cameroon. It will focus on creating safe learning environments, providing educational materials, and offering psychosocial support to help these children recover from the trauma of conflict and build a brighter future. Project Needs 1. Infrastructure:Construction of TLS: Due to the fact that several infrastructures have been damaged, this project seeks to construct 30 TLS to house Oosc in this region.Another pressing need is the lack of School Furniture: Supply desks, chairs, blackboards, and other essential furniture for classrooms will enhance learning in these hard to reach communities. 2. Educational Materials:The crisis has had a devastating effect on the economy of this region. Textbooks, stationaries and Workbooks are of great need to foster education. Teaching Aids: Another pressing need air teaching aids to enhance the teaching and learning process. 3. Teacher Training: The need here is Curriculum Development and Professional Development of teachers to improve their teaching skills and knowledge.The children are equally in need of Psychosocial Support, Counseling Services and Play Therapy.Community Engagement: Involve parents and community members in the project to create a supportive network for children.
