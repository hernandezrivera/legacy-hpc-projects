1 - Needs

Recent CCM sector assessments indicate that we have over 10,000 out of Camp IDPs in Maiduguri and Jere and thus there is a gap which requires CCM interventions to out of camp IDPs in Maiduguri. Thus this proposed project intends to provide the COVID-19 compliant CCM out of camp services to about 5000 IDPs in Maiduguri

2 - Activities

Beneficiary selection in partnership with IOMProvision of COVID-19 Compliaint CCM interventions to 5000 out of camp IDPs in MaiduguriProvision of COVID-19 response inteventions including sensitization, distribution of face masks and hand santizers
