Project Summary

The main objective of the project is to provide access to basic protection services over a two-year period (2022-2023) to bring about more profound change than short-term projects can achieve for 122,630
IDPs, returnees, and overburdened at-risk groups in the highly neglected (GoS) sub-districts of the areas listed below:
Saraqab, Sinjar /Idlib Governorate
Kafr Batna, Meliha/Rural Damascus governorate
Abu Kamal, Khasham, Tabni, Deir Ezzor, Jalaa / Deir Ezzor Governorate.
The consequences of the conflict and the ongoing Covid-19 are particularly severe, as many parts of different governorates have become hard-to-reach areas where people suffer from lack of basic needs, loss of livelihood and income, increase in unemployment, and lack of essential goods.
Girls and boys experience high-risk violence, including physical, emotional, sexual, and domestic violence. Insecurity, worsening socioeconomic barriers, and the ongoing impact of Covid-19 exacerbate child protection and gender-based violence issues for women, boys, and girls. Explosive hazards endanger the lives of already vulnerable communities. All of these factors have led to harmful and negative coping mechanisms, as well as increased child labor and forced child marriage, leaving adolescent girls particularly vulnerable.
This protection assistance will improve conditions for the basic protection of 122,630 people in areas with high displacement rates and expand protection coverage to mitigate the impact of COVID-19, the risk of explosions, potential protection risks for women, girls and boys, and people with disabilities. We do this by strengthening protection aspects through conducting awareness-raising events, providing assistance for specific protection needs, supporting community-based initiatives, and providing psychosocial and socioeconomic support. We also provide GBV response services to survivors of GBV and women and girls at risk, conduct GBV training for GBV actors, and provide GBV prevention activities. Raise awareness of COVID-19, train humanitarian workers who are not GBV actors to mitigate GBV risks, educate them on the risk of explosive attacks, introduce victim support services through coordination with UNFPA and protection actors on service map. Girls and boys and their caregivers benefit from MHPSS services and parenting programs. Raise awareness of child protection and support community committees to ensure active community participation in addressing child protection issues. Girls and boys receive child protection services through case management and conduct child protection training in accordance with the Minimum Standards for Child Protection. RSRP has memorandum of understanding with STD. Yet regarding implementation of activities, it worth to mention that RSRP will implement directly all proposed activities. As well, RSRP will consider stablish of community centers and outreach volunteers through campaigns within targeted local communities.
It should be noted that the project is consistent with HNO 2022-23 and the Sector Strategic Objective for the ERL (SO1 and SO2) and the priorities:
Strategic Objective 1: Provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs.
Strategic Objective 2: Enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs through supporting the protective environment in Syria, by promoting international law, International Humanitarian Law (IHL) and International Human Rights Law (IHRL) and though quality, principled assistance.
The intervention areas may be increased according to the increase in the areas that will beunder control in the years 2022-2023

4 - Needs

Due to the humanitarian crisis, an estimated14.6 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance. Lack and loss of livelihoods, massive destruction, of homes and property at 80%.The vulnerability of the population has worsened compared to last year due to the impact of the COVID19 pandemic. The overall poverty rate is estimated at almost 90%.In addition, the income deficit of households where the head of the family is a person with a disability, including female-headed households ,is estimated at nearly71%,explosive hazards endanger the lives of civilians and restrict safe movements. The result is increased need for protection and resort to negative coping mechanisms such as gender-based violence,84% child labor, child recruitment,75% various forms of exploitation and forced marriage, psychosocial suffering among children26%. (HNO 2022) According to HNO 2022 and the assessment conducted in the field,14,211,426 people were reported as vulnerable in the targeted areas. Therefore, the RSRP aims to reduce vulnerability through protection activities and raise awareness of protection issues among target communities. Priority will be given to expanding assistance to persons with disabilities and women with their own households to increase access to protection, in high-need areas where access to protection is most limited ,where there are high levels of destruction, and where there are returnees/strong influxes of internally displaced persons

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

PRA1: There is a risk of discrimination if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: -Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed. PRA2: There is a risk of inter-community tension from excluded beneficiaries might lead to conflict between target groups and complaints against the humanitarian partner. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: -Clear and comprehensible communication of the selection criteria to the community, community leaders and beneficiaries.-Complete impartiality in informing people within an affected community. PRA3: Possibility of positive Covid-19 cases between aid workers Mitigation Measure: RSRP will supervise and monitor the activities and the application of safety measures daily, to ensure practices are being followed, however, any employee suspected of having Covid-19 will be given sick leave and replaced with another. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

PR1: Misconduct by humanitarian workers while realizing the activities. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High Mitigation Measure: 1. Inform and train humanitarian workers on all aspects related to protection and develop strategies to address the risks of GBV and PSEA during all project-stages. 2. Selection of competent and professional staff and signing of a code of conduct by all humanitarian workers. 3.Establishment of an effective feedback and complaint mechanism. 4.Assigning PSEA focal point to deal with all PSEA cases that may occurred. PR2: Targeted communities including victims, do not report allegations of SEA, which results in RSRP being unable to refer the allegation to investigation, hold personnel to account and assist victims. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High Mitigation Measure:1. Train staff on PSEA policy and procedures. 2. RSRP will work on raise awareness of targeted communities about PSEA and how to report misconduct.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

R1: Access limitations could prevent the humanitarian actors from aiding the affected population in a timely manner. Mitigation Measure: Constant monitoring of the security situation on the ground and capacity to mobilize all resources into another project area if required. Likelihood: low Impact: High Risk R2: Delayed, denied or revoked GOS approvals which prevent delivering the assistance in a timely manner or prevents implementation of the project. Mitigating Measures: Transparency with government and local stakeholders in the areas of intervention, in addition to strong communication and collaboration prior to project implementation with local partner. Likelihood: Medium Impact: Medium R3:Unavailability of materials on the local markets due to closure of the border for commercial shipments or SC resolution not being renewed. Mitigation Measure: Detailed market analysis in areas of intervention and development of contingency plans to provide the materials from local markets. Likelihood: Low Impact: High R4:Exchange rate fluctuations of the SYP increasing the cost for the project. Mitigation Measure: Inform donors of any effects experienced by these fluctuations, arrange procurement of materials in the shortest time frame, to minimize the risk of prices increasing. Likelihood: Medium Impact: Medium
