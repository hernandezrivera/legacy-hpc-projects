Project Summary

PAH will provide psychosocial support to 3600 highly vulnerable conflict-affected older immobile people residing in Luhanska and Donetska oblasts (GCA). The proposed project will focus on protection through psychosocial support services with components of MPCA, food security and WASH interventions. Beneficiaries residing in GCA will be assisted in three rounds, and PSS will be provided once a week during each of the rounds (four months). Provision of PSS will be supplemented by MPCA. At the same time, 500 older people will be provided with agricultural vouchers. WASH intervention will be supplemented by rehabilitation of health and social institutions in GCA.
PAH community-based volunteers will provide social home-based care to highly vulnerable older people residing in Donetska NGCA. At the same time, there will be distribution of food and WASH NFIs kits.

Needs

PAH assessed current needs of most vulnerable older people residing within 0-7 km along the “contact line” in seven rayons in Donetska and Luhanska GCAs through Multi-Sector Needs Assessment. In total, 1047 older people were interviewed by PAH in the period from January 21 till February 11, 2019. Among those who were interviewed, 77.5% were women, and 22.5% were men. 98% of all the respondents were older people at the age of 60+ years old. According to the needs assessment, 73,6% of total female respondents and 62,3% of total male respondents have mobility restrictions, where 29,2% of both older men and women reported that they are home-bound. Almost 93,6% of interviewed older people have chronic diseases. 18,4% of surveyed have disability. More than half of the respondents (58%) live alone, almost 40% have nobody else to live with. For 98% of the respondent’s pensions are the main source of income, where reported income per household during the last 30 days was equal to 3,264 UAH (121 USD). The rest of the respondents primarily rely on aid from agencies (local, national or international organizations) and income of family members. The main expenditures of interviewed older people are as follows: food – 47,2%, medicine – 32,8%, winterization – 22,5%, medical treatment – 15,3%, utilities – 14,7%. 54,6% of the interviewed believe their income loss should be linked to the armed conflict. PAH conducted a WASH Assessment focused on sanitation conditions in health and social institutions in Luhanska and Donetska oblasts (GCA) from March, 25, to April, 5, 2019. The Needs Assessment proved high needs for WASH rehabilitation. Majority of assessed institutions have never received assistance in the WASH sector either from humanitarian organizations or from local authorities. The latter is linked to lack of funding or to other bureaucratic issues. According to the findings, 53% of the assessed institutions have lavatories, which are partially out of order. Due to this fact, some of lavatories were closed, so that the patients access to them was limited or inaccessible. In order to assess needs of older people residing in Donetska oblast (NGCA), PAH conducted Rapid Multi-Sector Needs Assessment in three small settlements of Donetska NGCA, from September, 24 to October, 26, 2018, where 243 interviews with older people were held during home visits. 76,9% of respondents were women, and 23,1% were men. Only 26% of respondents answered that they have no mobility restrictions and can move anywhere independently. At the same time, 35% of all the interviewed older people were home-bounded. 98% of interviewed older people were affected by chronic diseases, where the share of women with chronic diseases was 98,3% and for men — 96,4%. For 98% of the respondent pensions was the main source of income. 98% of the interviewed did not receive humanitarian/charitable assistance during 3 last months.

Activities & outcomes

Activity 1: Provision of psychosocial support services(PSS) to highly vulnerable older people.Outcome 1: 3700 highly vulnerable older people are provided with PSS.PAH will provide PSS to 3700 highly vulnerable older people in Luhanska and Donetska GCAs by community-based volunteers trained by PAH. The project will target the most vulnerable older people, who suffer from stress, depression and anxiety. Additionally, 1000 most vulnerable home-bound older people will be provided with assistive devices, which will contribute to the improvement of their PS well-being.Activity 2: Provision of social home-based care to immobile older people or those with mobility restrictions.Outcome 2: 2800 immobile older people or those with mobility restrictions are provided with social home-based care.Community-based volunteers will provide social home-based care to 2800 immobile older people or those with mobility restrictions residing in Donetska NGCA, which includes inter alia assistance with shopping, changing a light bulb, cleaning, paying bills, cooking etc.In addition, 350 most vulnerable home-bound older people will be provided with assistive devices, which will make them less dependent on others. Activity 3: Provision of multipurpose cash assistance (MPCA)Outcome 3: 1500 highly vulnerable older conflict-affected people are provided with MPCAPAH will provide the monthly amount of 970 UAH (approx. 35 USD) according to CWG to each of 1500 beneficiaries residing in Luhanska and Donetska GCA during 3 months with regard to increase of needs during winter season. MPCA will be provided in 3 tranches under each round of provision of PSS support. Activity 4: Distribution of additional food kits to highly vulnerable older people.Outcome 4: 2800 older conflict-affected people are provided with food kits.PAH, through its partner in Donetska NGCA will cover most vulnerable older people in need of food assistance with food kits during December, January and February 2020, who will be also provided with WASH NFI kits. PAH takes into account food basket value recommended by FSLC while implementing the given activity. Activity 5: Distribution of WASH NFIs kits to highly vulnerable older people.Outcome 5: 2800 highly vulnerable older people are provided with supplementary WASH NFIs kits.The most vulnerable conflict-affected older people residing in Donetska NGCA will be provided with WASH NFIs kits. The content of WASH NFIs kits is based recommendations of both WASH Cluster and Age and Disability TWG. The content of the WASH NFI kit will be gender-sensitive. Moreover, 100 elderly people will be provided with hygiene diapers. Activity 6: WASH Rehabilitation of health and social institutions in Donetska and Luhanska GCAs.Outcome 6: 526 care institution residents along the contact line in Donetsk and Luhansk GCA directly utilizing improved sanitation servicesFive health and social institutions selected according to Needs Assessment conducted by PAH will be rehabilitated in terms of WASH. While implementing the Activity, PAH will address needs of people with disabilities. Moreover, safety requirements will be fulfilled, following the regulations of those institutions which ensure protection. Most of the lavatories will be separate for men and women and lavatory entrances will be adapted to people with disabilities, where possible.Activity 7: Provision of trainings in agriculture and agricultural vouchers to the most vulnerable people residing in Doentska and Luhanska GCAsOutcome 7: 500 highly vulnerable conflict affected older population residing in Donetska and Luhanska GCA received agricultural vouchers. Trainings will be provided in winter to avoid overlap with gardening season. During the obligatory training sessions, PAH intends to distribute conditional value vouchers. Distribution will be one-time. Vouchers value is anticipated to be 140 USD, which is to be spent during March-April 2020 to use them during agricultural season.
