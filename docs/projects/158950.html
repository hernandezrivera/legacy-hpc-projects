Project Summary

The overall objective of the project will contribute to education cluster objective 2 of the HRP strategic objective, by supporting and coordinating a coherent and effective Education humanitarian response. This includes the coordination of needs assessments, development of a response strategy, long-term planning, cross-sector collaboration and advocacy. UNICEF in partnership with Save the Children co-leads the Education Cluster in Gaza and in the West Bank.
Specifically, the project will ensure uninterrupted implementation of the core cluster coordination functions including:
1. Supporting service delivery and coordination
2. Informing strategic decision-making of the HC/HCT for the humanitarian response
3. Planning and strategy development
4. Advocacy
5. Monitoring and reporting
6. Capacity building in contingency/preparedness planning
In the context of oPt, authorities lack national coordination mechanisms for addressing the enormous challenges facing the education system - each education system, in East Jerusalem, Area C, and Gaza has different duty bearers and face different significant protection challenges. The Education Cluster will support the relevant duty bearers to ensure that children (age 8-12, 50% girls), youth (13-18) and teachers in the most vulnerable communities can safely access schools that meet minimum humanitarian standards and provide a protective environment in the context of protracted conflict. All interventions planned through the 2020 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) will be closely coordinated with local communities and meet the requirement of the strong protection mainstreaming and community engagement especially in the needs assessment as well as the implementation of activities.
The Cluster is also planning to strengthen the partners’ level of engagement on gender through joint cluster needs assessments on gender specific needs in the field, joint HRP mid-review and related gender analysis, strengthening partners capacity on data collection and reporting of disaggregated data related to special needs of women, men, girls and boys. Partners will jointly identify the cluster priority interventions and responses based on the gender disaggregated needs assessments, reports and testimonies.

1 - Needs

Across the oPt, an estimated 0.5 million Palestinian boys and girls are in need of education humanitarian assistance, around three quarters of whom live in Gaza. Against the unique contextual backdrop of the oPt, there is a continued need for coordinated humanitarian response and advocacy. There is a high demand within the local humanitarian community, as well as in the broader international community, for support to develop a robust, detailed, evidence-based common situational analysis; for the coordination of advocacy and operational humanitarian responses; for the management and monitoring of country-based pooled funds; and for the development and monitoring of strategic needs analyses and response plans. With over 40 implementing partners having participated in the 2019 HRP, and financial requirements reaching over USD 15 million targeting 0.4 million people in 2019, there is a clear strategic need for continued coordination of humanitarian action in oPt to maximize achievements, while reducing gaps and duplication in the provision of humanitarian assistance. Education Cluster unique role in facilitating and coordinating these streams of humanitarian work continues therefore to be of critical importance.

2 - Activities

UNICEF in partnership with Save the Children co-leads the Education Cluster in Gaza and in the West Bank. Under this project, the following activities will be implemented. 1. Coordinate meetings, at national and sub-cluster (Gaza) operational levels, to ensure a common situation awareness and timely, appropriate and effective humanitarian response. This will involve conducting regular education cluster meetings for partners involved in education in emergencies.2. Strengthen needs analysis through improved trends analysis and reduced information gaps, as well as facilitate and support coordinated gender sensitive assessments and monitoring, data collection and analysis of key vulnerabilities.3. Promote cross cutting interventions in the humanitarian response including WASH, health and child protection.4. Ensure the bridging of humanitarian and development actions through engagement with the MoE, Education in Emergencies Working Group and the higher level Education Sector Working Group. 5. Implement joint advocacy events among Cluster partners as required. 6. Support service delivery for acute vulnerabilities of populations in Gaza and West Bank according to humanitarian caseload through coordination of education partners including government authorities and development actors.7. Support strategic decision making of the HC and HCT for the humanitarian response, while strengthening Sub-National Education Cluster Coordination in Gaza.8. Contribute to planning and strategy development of the humanitarian response, including in particular the development of the HRP as well as developing Education Clusters Response Strategy. The Cluster will make sure that these documents and strategies take into consideration different needs of girls and boys including children with disabilities and responses are planned accordingly. 9. Strengthen national capacity of MoE in contingency planning and emergency preparedness, as well as in integrating EIE in education sector plan and budget.10. Conduct joint monitoring and evaluation on the general education humanitarian response.
