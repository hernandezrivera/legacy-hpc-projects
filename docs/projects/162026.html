Project Summary

The project seek to build up and expand the ongoing the currative, preventive and promotive primary and secondary health care services (including SRH and MHPSS services) to refugees and surounding host communities. Outreach services will be provided through Community Health Workers and Community Psychosocial volunteers. Facility based primary healthcare (including MHPSS and SRH services) are implemented through 22 camp based PHCs and HPs which cater for the needs for women, men boys and girls; services provided are aligned to the essential health service package endorsed by the health sector and MoH. Medical referral support to access secondary, and tertiary health care will be provided for 50% of the camps. Outbreak/emergency preparedness and response will be strengthened, procurement of medicine and medical supplies including the contingency stocks will be undertaken. Health sector coordination will be supported through CHWG, MHPSS WG, field coordination and other sector coordination forums.

Needs

The health status of the population in need has improved since the beginning of the influx but remains fragile. Under 5 mortality rates have reduced from 1.22 deaths/ 10,000/ day to 0.68 and CMR from 1.36 to 0.2 deaths/ 10,000/ day between October 2017 and October 2019 (source: SMART survey), no differences were observed between sexes. UNHCR is currently supporting 21 primary health care facilities through its partners, in the first 9 months of 2019 a total of 410,499 consultations (61% are females and 39% are male). Approximately 33% of all consultations are children under 5 years of age and 3,622 patients referred to secondary health care facilities. Key morbidities are upper respiratory tract infections (16%), skin diseases (16%) gastritis (14%), fever of unknown origin (7%), and diarrhea (5%) (source: UNHCR HIS). Access to non-communicable disease (NCD) diagnostics and treatment remains constraint with only 5.7% of consultations provided for NCDs. Mental health and psychosocial support services are provided through nine facilities as well as at community level through 200 volunteers but efforts to raise awareness and enable access need to be intensified. Skilled delivery uptake remains constraint with more than 53% of deliveries being conducted at home, decision making is complex and linked to culture, traditions and health seeking experiences. Similarly, only 25% of women access the Antenatal Care during the first trimester of the pregnancy (source: UNHCR HIS) and newborn care and complete antenatal care coverage is poor . Access to SGBV services including clinical management of rape has increased but access for male survivors is constraint. The needs of key populations (sex workers, LGBTI) are addressed by UNHCR through dedicated drop-in-centres in close proximity to the camps but are presently unable to cater for all the needs.

Activities or outputs

The project will provide comprehensive primary health services for men, women, boys and girls in line with the essential package of health services (ESP) recommended by the government and health sector. The proposed project will employ a strategic approach of building partnerships and capacity building, with a view towards sustainable solutions and inclusion of host communities. 1. Provision of comprehensive primary health care services (Objective 1)• Provision of curative, preventive and promotive health care to women, men, girls and boys in line with the ESP through 10 health centres and 12 health posts with integrated specialized services such as dental care and physiotherapy • Provision of essential medicines including NCD and psychotropic medication• Staff capacity building, expansion of health committees to enhance community engagement and dialog • Strengthen data collection to monitor quality of care, access and utilization through irHIS 2. Support access to sexual and reproductive health (SRH) and HIV services (Obj 1)• Provision of SRH service to women, men and adolescents through implementing partners and the existing cooperation with UNFPA • Provision of Clinical Management of Rape for female/ male survivors, linkages to other supportive protection, legal and social services• Provision of counselling, SRH and protection services to key populations (female sex worker, LGBTIQ) through Drop-In Centres. 3. Enhance existing referral system and access to secondary/ tertiary health care services for host community and refugees (Objective 1)• Maintain of 10 ambulances for emergency medical referrals • Support patients with translation, obtaining services and conduct payment of hospital referral charges through dedicated referral worker, incl. support for life-saving emergency referrals to non-UNHCR partners in UNHCR’s area of responsibility• Construction of outpatient department in Sadar hospital, provision of human resources to Sadar hospital 4. Integrate mental health component in the primary health and protection services (Objective 1)• Maintaining psychologists and psychosocial counsellors at health facilities and community-based points of access and care including in community centres and client homes.• Maintain continuous supervision and monitoring of MH trained and newly recruited Volunteers of health and protection partners to assist in identification and referral of refugees with complex needs • Maintain supervision and monitoring of 100 refugee lay counsellors to provide basic counselling5. Building community capacity for healthy living through a community-based health promotion and surveillance programme (Objective 1,3)• Continuous training of 300 existing and 30 new female/ male community health workers (CHWs)• Awareness raising through home visits incl. neonatal care visits, community gatherings and other methods, patient referral 6. Support for management of major public health risks including outbreak and disaster preparedness and response (Objective 2)• Support emergency response during outbreak response through enhancing community awareness, early case identification and referral through intensive health messaging by CHWs and mobile medical teams • PFA by CHWs, provision of mobile medical teams, camp wide referral through ambulance system to functioning facilities•Maintain stocks for emergency response (IEHK etc)6. Support health sector coordination and information management efforts (Ob 4)• Continue active contribution and engagement in health sector coordination, strategic advisory group and working groups• In support of the health sector and in close cooperation with relevant partners, co- chair Community Health and MHPSS working group• In support of the health sector, strengthen field level health coordination through dedicated health coordination focal points
