Project Summary

SECD, during this proposal, aims at increasing resilience and affected people’s access to basic Education based on the needs assessment conducted by SECD during a full academic year (12 months). Also, the project focuses on the areas with high severity (3,4 and 5) and priority schools, through the following HRP objectives: Increased safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children and youth; SECD, through this proposal, is going to access indicators that will include increased enrolment rate to formal and non-formal education, back to learning activities, rehabilitation and maintenance of existing education facilities as well as establishment of new temporary education centers, provision of teaching and learning materials, gender sensitive and disabled friendly WASH interventions, provision of school supplies. SECD is planning to provide access to education for students with disabilities and vulnerable students through providing transportation services and improve school environment and provide targeted assistance within the schools in order to encourage them to continue their education and integrate them with their peers.
Enhanced quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth; through training activities for educational personnel such as COVID-19, CSG, Vocational trainings, policy, planning, data collection, and INEE Minimum Standards and PSEA and also provision of incentives for them.
Within the proposed project, SECD is going to support 20 schools divided into 10 schools at Idleb governorate, Jisr Ash-shughur district, sub-districts of (Badama, Janudiyeh, and Darkosh) and 10 schools at Aleppo governorate, Jebel Saman district, sub-district of Atareb and Daret Azze. Due to COVID-19, SECD will be committed to do periodic sterilization process for classrooms and WASH facilities, awareness sessions for students, training for teachers and educational staff, and provision sterilization materials for all schools.
The targeted number of beneficiaries is disaggregated as 15,400 students (7430 boys and 7970 girls) 350 (0.02 %) of them are CWDs, and 200 teachers and educational personnel (90 males, 110 females) will receive education assistance.
SECD, in case the fund is lower than the required, will prioritize the rehabilitation, construction and that under COVID-19 activities in Badama, Atareb and Daret Azze where the severity is 5 with focusing on the most vulnerable groups such as PWDs and poor students.

4 - Needs

Based on the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO 2021) quality education remains a critical need in Syria in general and North West of Syria in particular. Data analysis demonstrates that 6.9 million children (97%) and school personnel (3%) are estimated to be in need of humanitarian education assistance, an estimated 2.45 million children are out of school, 1.6 million students are at risk of dropping out in addition to that more than two-out- five schools are partially damaged, destroyed sheltering displaced families. Based on (Needs and Response summary 2021), it is estimated that over 2.6 million children remain displaced and separation from caregivers is a reported child protection issue putting children at risk of exploitation, abuse, or neglect. Also, Covid-19 outbreak forced all schools to move between physical, remote, and blended learning modalities. According to all the mentioned above, SECD conducted a needs assessment in 5 sub-districts affiliated from both Idleb and Aleppo; Idleb ( Badama, Janudiyeh, and Darkosh) and Aleppo (Atareb and Daret Azze) to assess the educational situation there, so based on this needs assessment the proposal is going to address the following needs: back to learning activities , rehabilitation and maintenance of existing education facilities as well as establishment of new temporary education centers, provision of teaching and learning materials, gender sensitive and disabled friendly WASH interventions, provision of school supplies. Provision incentives and trainings for the educational personnel. SECD won't target the ECCE in this proposal as the focusing will be more on COVID-19 and WASH facilities’ rehabilitation activities and other activities that suits the current situation.

5 - Implementation of activities

SECD is going to implement the activities through the HRP objectives; availability and access, quality, and systems. To increase access for education, SECD is going to support 15,400 students (7430 boys and 7970 girls) through rehabilitate 319 classrooms. The rehabilitation will include repairing 20 schools in the 5 districts of Daret Azze, Atareb, Janudiyeh, Badama, and Darkosh. The rehabilitation works will consider the needs of girls and CWDs in regard to WASH facilities; constructing additional latrines blocks to include 1 latrine in each school for CWDs including ramps. Installation of solar power for 20 schools. Furniture schools. Conducting BTL and COVID-19 awareness sessions 15,000 students. Provision of learning materials (Schoolbags, stationery kit, teacher's kit) for 15,400 students (7430 boys and 7970 girls) with regard to the difference in color and design between males and females and 200 teachers (140 males, 260 females). Enhanced quality of education through providing in-class trainings to the educational personnel about COVID-19, child safeguarding, CoC, INEE, and PSEA for 200 teachers (females 55%, males 45%), also vocational trainings to (400) students (150 males,250 females) aged from 18-24 in the non-formal schools. Provision incentives for 200 teachers and educational personnel. Involve parents in the educational process through conducting monthly meetings for PTA members to set mechanisms for formal and non-formal schools. MEAL team will conduct regular visits to the targeted locations to monitor the activities. Furthermore, conduct FGDs with beneficiaries. Conducting PDMs after distribution of materials and also conduct PIM. CRM will be in place through tracking the all types of complaints.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1: Unable access to women and girls during the distribution of some services or materials. Risk Management: SECD put this into account and will recruit female staff from the same areas of the project.Risk 2: Sexual Abuse or exploitation to beneficiaries.Risk Management: SECD signs Code of Conduct with the organization staff and, suppliers and educational personnel and will train them all as part of the proposed activities.Risk 3: Violence, punishment or bullying against students by teachers or the educational personnel Risk Management: All the teachers and the educational personnel will sign code of conduct elaborating SECD policy against any kind of punishment with students.Risk 4: Chaos withing the community in regard to distribution materials for beneficiaries. Risk Management: SECD will put clear selection criteria to avoid any misunderstanding to whom the materials for.Risk 5: Barries may face disabled or vulnerable students such as transportation or access to WASH facilities.Risk Management: Support these categories by transportation and build ramps in all the latrines that will be constructed.Risk 6: In some schools there is no separated latrines for girls.Risk Management: This risk will be managed through building new blocks for more privacy and dignity for girls.

7 - Does the project include a PSEA strategy ?

Yes

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies are in place, practiced and monitored. Policies will cover standards of behavior for the organization’s staff and representatives, specifically forbidding sexual exploitation and abuse, and include accountability measures. Policies will address risks at the organizational level and the program level. Trainings and refreshers on PSEA will be done systematically. The targeted communities and learning facilities should be aware of SECD policies and procedures. SECD staff and targeted communities will have access to safe and effective reporting systems including knowing when they will get a response to their report and having the right to report anonymously. SECD will ensure a survivor centered approach that ensures the safety and confidentiality of the affected person. The survivor will be an active participant in the processes. A poor handling of allegations of sexual explanation and abuse can put the accuser/survivor, alleged perpetrator and others at risk.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: The targeted areas are stable, and no large-scale military operations are anticipated, procurement and shipment roads/lines are stable, and relations with the Education Directorates and local authorities are accessible.Risk 1: COVID-19 pandemic outbreak in the project targeted areas. Mitigating measures: SECD had already measures from the beginning of the outbreak of the pandemic in the North-west of Syria through social distance, sterilization of the centers and schools. Furthermore, in this proposal will be distributed PPE kits.Risk 2: Out the blue new wave of displacement, Likelihood: medium Impact: High mitigating measures: The security situation in the project locations will be followed, and integration with other stakeholders to provide emergency services.Risk 3: Sudden targeting to the project areas: Likelihood: High Impact High mitigating measures: Activation of the e-learning through supporting the teachers and students with internet packages.Risk 4: Stealing materials such as schools’ assets Likelihood: Low, Impact: high, mitigating measures:MoUs will be signed with the local councils or the local authorities to protect the materials.Risk 5: Sudden closure of the cross border with Turkey Likelihood: medium, Impact: high, mitigating measures: Some materials are available in the local market yet there are missing materials, coordination with the Turkish government to allow the humanitarian assistance to be delivered.
