1 - Needs

Humanitarian need remains critical for 3.66 million people in Sudan, due to ongoing conﬂict between government armed forces and rebel groups; inter tribal ﬁghting and the recent unanticipated inﬂux of refugees from South Sudan 1.2 Millions of south Sudanese refugees are currently taking refugee’s settlement in Sudan. The displaced live in camps, among host communities, and in rural areas leaving most reliant on humanitarian assistance and placing an additional burden on the existing resources within host communities. On the other hand the conﬂicted affected people in South kurdufan are in high need to health services. In the four CIS operation areas ( South Darfur, East Darfur, South Kordufan, and Kassala states) the health facilities are not suﬃcient compare with the population, and the health facilities which are running are lacking drugs, staﬃng, and operation cost. CIS health project will contributed effectively in the live saving through provision of quality health services to IDPs, returnees and host community, girls, women, boys, and men; all Age groups will beneﬁted from the health services, these include young children, children, Adolescents, Young adults, Middle-aged adults, Older adults,

2 - Activities / Outputs

CIS Health project is planned to undertake the following activities: • Deliver basic package of primary health care services, including disease treatment and prevention. • Supply the health facilities with medical supplies and equipment, including provision of essential and live saving medicines and supplies including medicine for chronic illness. • Expand and maintain disease surveillance system with early warning component. • Support Reproductive Health services including Emergency Obstetric, new born care. • Support routine/outreach immunization, including routine or acceleration interventions for immunization. • Support referral to secondary health care. • Provide response to diseases outbreaks in affected areas. • Provide Basic Construction / Rehabilitation of health facilities. • Provide capacity building to facility staff through regular health training. • Conduct health campaign to improve community awareness in diseases control and prevention. • provide stabilization center services in East Darfur (Eddaein SC), South Darfur (Kass SC), and Kassala (Kassala SC)

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will be will contribute in environmental and natural resource protection; CARE ensures that 100% of supported health facilities, are implementing environmental protection measures; reliable sanitation services, safe waste disposal Standard Operating Procedures, and maintaining functioning incinerators. CARE will ensure all the relevant staff (especially nurses and cleaners) are trained in infection control and waste management, using the safety boxes to transport waste to the incinerators. in facilities where there is no incinerators, CIS will construct quality incinerator to ensure proper medical waste management. also CIS is planning to plant trees in all health facilities,
