Project Summary

The project thrives to promote access to inclusive quality education for 52, 969 learners (21, 187 Boys & 31, 782 Girls) and reduce the risk of children dropping out of school. It will support primary and secondary schools in 4 prioritised districts categorized under severity ranking 5 with block grants as well as directly provide teaching and learning materials and other supplies towards establishment of conducive teaching and learning environments. The project will promote learner enrolment and retention through provision of educational subsidies and PPEs aimed at mitigating the spread of COVID 19 in schools. Refurbishment and repair of school boreholes will be prioritized to ensure constant supply of clean and safe water to 26 276 learners (10 510 Boys; 15 276 Girls) and 656 teachers in 4 districts ( Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare).. It will also complement effort to improve attendance and wellbeing for 21, 187 boys and 31, 782 girls through provision of Cash Transfers and commodities to sustain the School Feeding Programme.

1 - Activities

• Community mobilsation and engagement towards enrolment of Out of School learners in primary and secondary schools as well as in NFE programs – 4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare).• Provide PPEs in schools to enhancing safety, protection and reduce anxiety of not attending school amongst learners as well as teachers resulting from COVID 19 – 4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare).• Distribution of teaching and learning materials and educational subsidies (school fees/levies, uniforms, dignity packs for girls) to curb dropouts and sustain retention of learners –4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare).• Teachers capacity enhancement on quality delivery of education (including gender sensitive pedagogy, inclusivity, catch up/accelerated learning), PSS, Child Protection, Safeguarding and SRHR services – 4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare).• Provision of “Temporal Learning Spaces” to ensure an enabling learning environment and maintain COVID 19 social distancing regulations in situation where schools infrastructure sustains damages that render them unfit for occupation - 4 districts ( Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare).• Facilitating “Block Grants” and training of SDCs towards improvement of the learning environment through rehabilitation of damaged schools infrastructure – 4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungwiza; Gwanda; Harare). • Provision of safe and clean water and establishment of nutrition gardens in needy schools through rehabilitation of boreholes and facilitating water reticulation – 2 districts (Chitungwiza; Harare). • Provision of Cash Transfers and where necessary commodities for the School Feeding Programme in selected primary schools – 4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungiza; Gwanda; Harare). • Faciltate training of teachers on School Feeding Guide-lines – 4 districts (Bulilima; Chitungiza; Gwanda; Harare).
