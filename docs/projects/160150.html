Project Summary

Protection issues remain a major concern for the actors in Somalia. The country remains one of the unfriendlier countries to the safety and opportunity for women, girls and children. UNHCR continues to report daily violent violation of children and women related to abuses especially sexual abuse and the violation of the rights of children through child-unfriendlier practices. The project targets the protect children and women from all forms of SGB and protection risk through multi-propronged. The project will directly benefit about 33,000 people mainly through information provision, campaigns and other related information transfer practices. These include, but not limited to, community interventions, case management, addressing individual child abuse cases such as child labour, gender-based violence just to mention a few. These multi-pronged, multi-sectoral approach. Capacity building training will target about 250 key notables, policy makers and other influential figures in the society to perpetuate continuity and sustainability. It will also undertake strategic strengthen community level child protection mechanisms for reporting, case management for the survivors. Capacity building and engagement of the notables will seek to address the rampant issue of child labour in the targeted locations known for their perpetuation of the vice. Other strategic protection approach planned include Reports FGM cases through PRMN project in Bari region, Spread FGM/C awareness to the Six districts in Bari region and Bossaso/Qardho IDP’s. help FGM/C victims to get medical support they might need in Bossaso & Qardho. Referral, Advocacy & FGM/C workshops with communities & House to house visit.

Project Activities

HADO will implement the following activities;(i) Information Provision, capacity building, campaigns and advicacy targeting the community notables; religious leaders, elders, local administration, businessmen and other notables through action-oriented, results-based approach.(ii) Community interventions, cases management and referrals for -based violence including SGBV.(iii) Strengthen community-level child protection mechanisms for reporting, case management for the survivors. (iv) Reports FGM cases through PRMN project in Bari region, (v) Spread FGM/C awareness to the Six districts in Bari region and Bossaso/Qardho IDP’s. (vi) Help FGM/C victims to get the medical support they might need in Bossaso & Qardho. (vii) Referral, Advocacy & FGM/C workshops with communities & House to house visit.

Project Indicators

The project indicator will be;(i) The number of community notables reached through protection advocacy and messaging.(ii) The number and Commitment approaches made by the community notables including religious leaders, elders businessmen and other actors towards entrenching child and women protection practices.(iii) The number of cases successfully managed and referred.(iv) The number of community protection mechanisms established for reporting, case management for the survivors. (v) The number of FGM Reports processed and presented to PRMN , (vi) The number of awareness on FGM/C undertaken (vii) The number of FGM/C victims helped to access medical support they might need (viii) The number of referral, Advocacy & FGM/C workshops conducted.

Mitigation Measures

FGM and protection issues still remain a major challenge in Somalia because in most part the practices are entrenched in the local socio-cultural fabric and as such approach that seeks to address these issues may be misconstrued by a section of the community which may eventually exacerbate some level of protection risks. The organization is however experienced in grassroots engagement of the notable stakeholders so that proper communication is made and the intention of the project communicated and proper information on how the information accords with the local culture outlaid.
