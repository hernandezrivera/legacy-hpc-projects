Project Summary

The project is multi sectoral to achieve the needs of Vulnerable returnee, IDP and resident population in As Salam and Ar Rawat locality in White Nile Most of the populations are farmers and shepherd living in extreme poverty and illiteracy especially women who are seized with bad cultural norms and habits, children are dropped out of school and their childhood are stolen and are employed child laborers, poverty steal their childhood innocence, even those who are lucky to be in school they have to walk for long distances and they are Exposed to harassments and violence especially girls which exclude them from continuing their study In terms of education BPW will provide knowledge of violence and sexual abuses for primary school’s students via target 4 boys and girls schools, Conducting Safety Education workshop, children learn to assert their rights, understand and respect their bodies, and stand up for the rights of others. also works with school staff to ensure that schools are safe for both girls and boys and that teachers are equipped to respond in cases of abuse and violence, To distribute reading materials ( Books) for … and technical support as writing materials for….. student in each school .to encourage methodology of knowledge as well as support the protection system. As well as provide 10 package of plants seeds for support education environment. To plant four trees one in each school of PROGRESSING THE REDUCTION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND GIRLS ( to grow to be a SOUVENIR FOR ENDING THE CYCLE OF GBV), Rehabilitation of 4 schools 8 classes total 32 classes. This model focuses on wellness, effective child friendly communication, interpersonal and community resourcefulness, displacement and protracted crisis.
The project designed to prevent and decrease VAW through empowering women by rehabilitating women center and child friendly spaces to operate protection service and provide Technical Vocational Education Training TVET (life skills activities training to….. women and girls on vocational skills and support them with startup capital to put their skills into productive use) including recreational activities, individual and group counselling (non-specialized), Psychological First Aid (PFA) within the context of protection services for (boys, girls, women, men), addressing sexual and gender-based violence prevention and response e.g. psychosocial and counselling;; GBV case management of psychosocial support programs, awareness for the society in gender equality mainstreaming and support community protection networks. Youth will be engaged through outreach Strategies capacity building of 2000 women and 200 men “leave no-one behind, to prevent GBV (awareness’s campaigns information/sensitization on GBV and health, domestic violence, child marriage.
For Water, Sanitation and Hygiene BPWVO will construct 24 latrines holes 14 hole for girls &10 hole for boys and 4 water points one for each school and back age of hygiene facilities (points, Basins, pipes and hygiene club for children to increase access to education for the most vulnerable disadvantaged children especially girls. Forming school clubs and raising awareness of children education providers and stockholders and community on hygiene promotion will reduce the negative impact of displacement and poverty and provide prevention of disease in conditions of high density living., Installation of hand washing facilities and provision of basic hygiene and sanitation items for existing protection center as part of COVID-19 risk response Distribution hygiene kits.

1 - Needs

Even before the conflicts and pandemic of COVID19 there is low enrollment particularly in targeted area especially girls of school aged in White Nile The affordability of school fees is one of the root causes that hinder access to education. Other factors are existing education are the inadequate security and emergency situations such as floods and the long way, which discourage them to send their children to expose them to harassment. As a result, children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor, critical gap in teachers in operated schools which are overcrowded and classes have more than 100 children, the dropout rate among girls is far higher than boys. This is due to the high rate of early marriage and social norms that do not support girls’ education. The illiteracy rate among residence women is 80 % they are ignorant about their rights and most of them are victims of abuse, exploitation and GBV.Their families decide for them for they are not allowed to make any decision They need to be empowered Economically, socially and to know their rights and to earn their income to have voice and choice and participate in decision making The school environment has been affected by the inadequate sanitation facilities associated with poor access to clean water, and poor hygiene and save latrines privacy for girls is not affordable
