Project Summary


The project seeks to provide 360 beneficiaries (Females/ Males) with the most popular professional and industrial skills required in the labor market. That will enable them to find job opportunities or start their own business and will provide them and their families with a source of income in light of the difficult conditions that Syrians live in this region.
The project also seeks to supply the industrial sector with appropriate competencies to compensate for the increasing shortage of industrialists and professionals as a result of migration.
The project will also create 20 job opportunities for qualified engineers and engineer assistants from the town of Atmah, which will help them improve their families’ economic situation.
We have a pre-equipped training center with the best equipment and tools. It includes two training rooms and four mechanical and electronic laboratories. The center is located in the town of Atmah, which includes the largest gathering of camps in Syria.
Due to the big number of the people with disability, the center and the facilities are designed to address barriers to participation for people with disabilities, in addition to that the people with disabilities will be consulted during the implementation of the project in order to facilitate the participation for people with disabilities.
The trainings are divided into three programs, each program contains a set of courses and specializations according to the following:
Electricity and Electron Program:
1- Courses in Electron and Alternative Energy (125 hours).
2- Courses in Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (125 hours).
3- Courses in Household Electrical Wiring (60 hours).
Electricity and Mechanics Program:
1- Courses in Automatic Control (125 hours).
2- Courses in Pneumatic and Hydraulic Control (125 hours).
3- Courses in Automotive Electricity and Mechanics (125 hours).
4- Courses in Winding and Maintenance of Electric Motors (125 hours).
Computer and Mobile Program:
1- Courses in Mobile Maintenance (125 hours).
2- Courses in Computer Maintenance (125 hours).
3- Courses in Web Programming (125 hours).
The duration of each specialized program is 4 months, including the advertisement, registration, and exams process.
The training is launched in three stages, and each stage includes six different courses (20 trainees in each course), so that the total number of beneficiaries in each stage is 120 trainees within each phase, which lasts for 4 months.
At the end of the training, the successful trainee will receive a bag of equipment for the profession in which he has been trained, at a cost of 50 dollars, to encourage the trainee and facilitate his involvement in the labor market. The bag of equipment include the main tools of each profession as it was recmomended by trainers such as ( Electric Soldering Iron tool, Tin Wires, TEST PENCIL, Duct Tape, pliers, Pliers Wire Cutter, CUTTER KNIFE, Screw driver set, Plastic Toolbox).
A trainee who has completed a full training program will receive $75 at the end of the program as a transportation compensation ($25 for each month).
The training and job opportunities that the project will provide to 380 beneficiaries will contribute to reducing the possibilities of migration or engaging in violence for these young people, and overcoming the economic obstacles that face them, and thus enhancing the resilience and stability of society.

4 - Needs

Atmeh town did not mainly have adequate basic educational facilities, as well as vocational education facilities. Like the rest of the Syrian areas outside the control of GoS, it lost thousands of young competencies.We have conducted a study in Atmeh and its camps to identify training needs and develop the concept of the project. The study included four main tools: 1. Group discussions. 2. Interviews. 3. Field visits. In the study, the team gave a presentation about the Afaq Institute in Atmeh, asking for more ideas to improve it. Key findings: - There are very few vocational training centers in and around Atma town offering specialized technical training - Providing shorter intensive specialized pieces of training - Making use of the available equipment to focus on specialized pieces of training - Providing advanced computer training - There are many qualified trainers in the mechanical, electrical and electronic fields but many of them work in other jobs and sometimes below their level of education due to the lack of relevant training centers or appropriate opportunities. The project justification - Reducing unemployment. - High rates of population growth in the region, population inflation and IDPs in large numbers - Low levels of education and the absence of an industrial projects. - The available resources are wasted considering the inability to invest the human resources of the youth - Youth joining the military factions, which reflects on civil society - The deviation of youth to crimes and thefts to meet their needs in the absence of available alternatives - Reducing Immigration - Lack of qualified industrial cadres-Absorbing the energies of youth and their personal aspirations

5 - Implementation of activities

-The duration of the project is 12 months: divided into three consecutive programs.-The duration of each program is 4 months, including the preparation period and exams.Implementation Process:1-Conducting employment interviews and signing contracts.2-Work mechanisms and criteria for selecting the trainees:The launch of the specialized programs will be announced on social media and through local channels.The trainees selection process includes the following criteria and steps:-The applicant must have the primary school certificate as a minimum and pass the entrance test.-There is a priority for the displaced applicants and the people without a breadwinner.3-Launching the training in three stages, each stage (120 students 17-30-years-old) lasting four months and contain 6 specialized courses, as follows: 1st stage: 1-Electron and Alternative Energy 2-Mobile Maintenance 3-Automotive Electricity and Mechanics 4-Computer Maintenance 5-Automated Control 6-Pneumatic and Hydraulic Control2nd stage: 1-Electron and Alternative Energy 2-Mobile Maintenance 3-Automotive Electricity and Mechanics 4-Computer Maintenance 5-Air Conditioning and Refrigeration 6-Pneumatic and Hydraulic Control3rd stage:1-Electron and Alternative Energy 2-Web Programming 3-Pneumatic and Hydraulic Control 4-Automated Control 5-Electrical Wiring 6-Winding and Maintenance of Electric MotorsAt the end of the training, the successful trainee will receive:1- A bag of equipment for the profession in which he has been trained, at a cost of $50.2- $75 as a transportation compensation.Most of the trainings and activities will be carried out in open environments with full regard for the safety measures recommended by the WHO against COVID-19.
