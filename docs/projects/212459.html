0 - Project Summary (extended)

IHR is embarking on a strategic initiative to provide Multi-Purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) to the most vulnerable families in Northwest Syria (NWS) as a regular intervention for four months (100 USD per round per HH). Building upon the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) of 2024, this project aims to address the critical needs identified within the region, particularly in light of the ongoing emergencies that plagued the area in 2023. Through comprehensive needs assessments, both quantitative and qualitative, conducted in collaboration with local partners and communities, the project will target vulnerable households with tailored assistance to ensure their basic needs are met and their resilience strengthened. Identified Needs and Assessments: The needs assessment conducted by the IHR team as part of the project identified several key areas of concern within the targeted locations in NWS. These include but are not limited to: Food Insecurity: Many households are experiencing severe food insecurity due to disruptions in livelihoods, limited access to markets, and inflation.Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFIs): A significant number of families lack adequate shelter and essential NFIs, exacerbating their vulnerability, particularly in harsh weather conditions.Healthcare and Hygiene: Access to healthcare services and hygiene items remains a challenge, contributing to health risks, especially among vulnerable groups such as children and the elderly.Livelihoods: Displacement and economic instability have severely impacted livelihood opportunities, leaving many families without sustainable income sources.In addition to the HNO 2024, the project team conducted organizational and market assessments in proposed locations to ensure alignment with local capacities and market dynamics. Insights from these assessments informed the design and implementation of the MPCA program, ensuring its relevance, effectiveness, and sustainability.
