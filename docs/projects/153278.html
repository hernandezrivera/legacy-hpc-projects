Project Summary

The project aims to support humanitarian and development actors' response in South Sudan by undertaking critical infrastructure works identified by the Logistics Cluster. Maintenance of priority delivery infrastructure such as roads, bridges and airstrips is key to enable access to remote areas and key social services, improve humanitarian response, as well as to link up farmers with markets.

Needs

South Sudan's transport system is deficient and there is a great need to build and improve infrastructure in order to increase accessibility, especially in remote locations. Transport costs in the country are among the highest in the world, making it difficult to provide humanitarian aid and to promote development. Adding to the already dilapidated road conditions, about 60% of all roads become inaccessible during the rainy season, which leads to humanitarian organizations airlifting life-saving supplies and other goods to support vulnerable populations.
