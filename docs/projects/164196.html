Project Summary

The project aims to support healthcare services in Bab Al Hawa Hospital by providing financial support along with providing all medicines and medical consumables needed, to provide people in need with all required services, this includes local communities and IDPs, women, children and elderly people.
Bab Al Hawa Hospital is considered the biggest specialized hospital in NW of Syria, and it’s now considered the main referral health facility for all primary health facilities in NW of Syria.
The services provided at the hospital includes but not limited to: Emergency unit, Renal Dialysis unit, CT-Scan and X-Ray unit, GI Endoscopy unit, Laboratory and Blood Bank, Neonatal unit, Neurology unit, Cardiology unit, Ophthalmology unit, and Outpatients Clinics, and Specialized Operations unit which includes General Surgery, ENT Surgery, Neurology Surgery, Orthopaedic Surgery, Thoracic Surgery, Maxillofacial Surgery, Urology Surgery and Paediatric Surgery, which will be all supported by this project.
The hospital facilitates the services provision for women by preparing a specialized reception utilities and isolated room for privacy, in addition to the availability of women in the hospital staff.
In addition, the hospital is the authorized referral point to Turkey for the most complex cases. Bab Al Hawa Hospital has provided over the past six years from its founding in 2013 to the end of 2019 more than 1.200.000 beneficiaries.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will support the provision of complete medical services at Bab Al Hawa Hospital,and all patients will be received and then distributed to departments as needed,according to:Outpatients will go to all types of outpatient clinics:paediatric,internal medicine,general surgery,ENT,Ophthalmology and other clinics.If the patient is an urgent case,he will go to the Emergency department,and he will be provided with service according to his condition,with a special advantage in the Bab Al Hawa Hospital,which is the presence of the intensive cardiac care department.Patients with renal failure are also accepted into the dialysis unit and conduct dialysis sessions.Also,newborn needing incubators will be admitted in the incubators unit.As for the surgical operations,the surgical patients will be sorted according to the specializations,and they will be prepared for the operations and then conduct the surgeries.All patients requiring investigative procedures will be served by a radiography,C-SCAN tomography,ultrasound and laboratory units.Also,the blood bank and thalassemia unit will provide blood to the patients.Referred patients will be received from other health facilities and hospital services will be provided to them.As for patients in need of services more than the capacity of Bab Al Hawa Hospital,they will be transferred to Turkey hospitals.MEAL team will make a regular collecting the required data that help in:Making enlightened decisions based on the collected data.Monitoring and evaluation of the quality of the service provision through analyzing the collected data.The project will be monitored and evaluated through three different channels,and on three different dimensions:The operational dimension,The technical dimension,The objectives dimension.Monitoring tools: PITT,issued on a monthly basis.Satisfaction survey will be analyzed and reported to stakeholders.Project Evaluation Report by the end of the project-term.
