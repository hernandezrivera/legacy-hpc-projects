Project Summary

Strengthen gender mainstreaming throughout the HPC by strengthening knowledge and capacity of humanitarian partners (including UN agencies, international and national NGOs, and CSOs). This will be done through training and capacity building sessions, development of contextualised and translated tools, and awareness raising activities.
This will be further supported through the revitalisation of the inter-agency Gender Technical Working Group in Ethiopia which will facilitate, coordinate, and advocate for gender mainstreaming throughout
all humanitarian planning, decision-making, and response processes. Furthermore, the GTWG will provide technical leadership and support on gender-sensitive humanitarian programming and response, working in close collaboration with Government, UN agencies, international and national NGOs, CSOs, and donors.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1.1.1: Establish an inter-agency Ethiopia Gender Technical Working Group including UN agencies, national and international NGOs, Civil-Societ Organisations (CSOs), Women-Led Organisations (WLOs) / Women Rights Organisations (WROs), and Government. Activity 1.1.2: Develop GTWG strategy and work plan for 2022 and seek endorsement of GTWG members. Activity 1.1.3: Coordinate bi-monthly GTWG meetingsActivity 1.1.4: Develop an annual Gender Accountability Framework (GAF) report in coordination with cluster partners and WROs/WLOs.Activity 1.1.5: Ensure HPC decision-making and prioritization processes are informed by Sex and Age Disaggregated Data (SADD) and gender analysis. Activity 1.2.1 Train humanitarian stakeholders, including cluster partners and WLO/WRO members on Gender in Humanitarian Action, SADD, the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Gender and Age Marker (GAM), and planning and conducting Rapid Gender Analyses (RGAs)Activity 1.2.2 Provide training of trainers (ToT) on Gender in Humanitarian Action (GiHA) for humanitarian stakeholders including cluster partners, NGOs, CSOs, and WLO/WROs.Activity 1.2.2 Train CSOs and WLO/WROs on the humanitarian architecture to increase their engagement in humanitarian decision-making processes and response. Activity 2.1.4 Facilitate a learning session with the ICCG on tips for gender responsive assessments.Activity 1.3.1 Develop, print, and distribute Information, Education, and Communication (IEC) materials on gender equality for humanitarian stakeholders and crisis affected communities in local languages.Activity 1.3.2 Conduct information and awareness raising campaigns on gender equality targeted at humanitarian stakeholders and crisis-affected communities. Activity 1.4.1 Conduct rapid gender analyses after a humanitarian shock hits or there is a significant change in the humanitarian context.Activity 1.4.2 Generate bi-monthly Gender Alerts to generate gender-sensitive data and evidence and advance the importance of gender across humanitarian advocacy and information efforts in Ethiopia.Activity 1.4.3 Develop and roll-out dissemination plans to ensure gender data and evidence is properly understood and applied by humanitarian partnersActivity 1.4.4 Develop an annual humanitarian gender analysis report to inform system-wide humanitarian coordination and planning processes.
