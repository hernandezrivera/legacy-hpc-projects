Project Summary

The project will provide integrated training programs for young people aged 18-25 who are not enrolled in schools and universities to invest their skills and engage them in society.
Section I: includes 3 training programs:
1. Vocational training 3 months (ICDL-designing - mobile maintenance-hairdressing)
2. Management training 2 months: (Project Management- MEAL - English – Secretarial -Writing Reports - Principles of Humanitarian work)
3. Community awareness and democracy workshops 2 month in parallel with Management training
Section2:
- First is to provide specialized kits in the field of training for 50 % participant to open a workshop in which he could work and we will follow up for 3 months to ensure the success of the work and provide consultancy.
- Secondly: Provide opportunities to volunteer for 50 % of trainees in the management training in organizations and pay incentives for 3 months, ensuring that they have a good income and practical experience that will enable to continue.

Needs

- Poverty and unemployment aren't appropriate environment for participation in democratic life, especially people who will be searching for a living instead of thinking about their rights and taking part in the electoral process, this can make them a tool in hands of those who buy votes and put them in the wrong side The contribution of the project to securing job opportunities is not sufficient enough so we will conduct awareness seminars to raise the awareness of participate in democratic life. - The problem we are trying to solve is: A whole generation is vulnerable to loss and delinquency, especially among young (males and females) the reasons are: 1- - The lack of secondary schools. 2- - The difficulty of returning to school due to the long drop out of education. 3- The lack of voluntary Courses that are concerned with young (males and females). The project will provide integrated training programs for young (males and females) aged 18 - 25 who are not enrolled in schools and universities to invest their skills and engage them in society. The needs assessment sources:Local Council of Azaz city.Reports by Ballad Field MEAL personnel.

Implementation of activities

3.1 Vocational and skills training provided 300 trainees will be chosen as the following:100 females from hosting families, age from 18 to 25 year.50 females from IDPs families, age from 18 to 25 year.100 males from hosting families, age from 18 to 25 year.50 males from IDPs families, age from 18 to 25 year. - 200 trainees will receive vocational and administrative training and 100 will attend community awareness and democracy workshops through the following activities: 1- signing MoU with the local council 2- contracting with a training center 3- preparing the center with the lakes 4- launching the project and announcing for the applicants to register in the training 5- selecting the beneficiaries according to the criteria 6- starting the two training programs 7- distributing the training kits after finishing the training 8- distributing the incentives to the trainees after finishing the training 9- following the trainees after finishing the training for three months. 1.1 Short-term work opportunities created - 50% of vocational trainees will get amount of money to start their own project as following:20 females.20 males. 50% administrative trainees were able to provide permanent work for them by provide temporary employment opportunities within local organization to get more experience and covering their salaries for 3 months as following:30 females.30 males.

Protection Risk Analysis

Resources and response capacities being limited and far below know needs, there will always be perception of biased targeting of beneficiaries or of biased selection of response activities, thereby creating or exacerbating tension-MEDIUM. Mitigating Measure:1- Ensure that a comprehensive needs analysis informs the response formulation, and that this is openly documented and made transparent.2- Communicate the planned response, and the rationale behind, to the local authorities and communities concerned.3- Have in reserve a strategy in case the planned response does unexpectedly create tension (e.g. add-on of other activities or change in activities, in consultation with concerned communities. 4- Manage donor expectation on how long it takes to gain community trust and set up activities. Monitoring:1- Apply strong M&E 2- 3. Ensure communities fully understand – and subscribe to – the proposed programme response, and are empowered to participate in M&E. Acceptable Level of Risk :1- Yes, as it is an unavoidable risk in the conflict environment, but that risk can be managed – to a reasonable extent - if the indicated mitigating measures are applied.
