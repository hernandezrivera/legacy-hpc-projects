Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the WASH needs of 269,119 people (Men: 51753,
Women: 53611, Girls: 82871, Boys: 80884),( 123171 IDPs, 125946 hosted people, and
returnees 20002 ) with 2316 people of them are people with disabilities during 2022 and 299,001people during 2023, to respond to their
protracted needs by ensuring good access to sustainable WASH service, The project is designed to respond to the needs of IDPs, Returnees, and hosted people, stayed in the north of Syria in the communities and the IDPs camps .to cover their needs by delivering humanitarian WASH supplies and services to most vulnerable people: men, women, boys, and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions, the project will be transferred the water station from working by fuel to working by a hygiene system most of it solar energy.
One of the causes of the spreading of Leishmania is bad solid waste management in place and this is another factor that can increase the threat on the vulnerable people SECD is aiming to solve this long-known problem by undertaking by supporting the local council to activate good solid waste management which is reducing and eliminating the Leishmania causes.
Through this project in 2022, SECD is planning to support the targeted communities with the
following activities:
-Installation of the solar power system to turn the operating system of the water stations:
41 water stations. and conducting a water safety plan, and establishing a cost recovery system to ensure the suitability of the project. The solar system will be used for both good operation and/or for pumping stations
-Distribution of 20000 consumable hygiene kits (second round) for the most vulnerable people the component of the HKs are linked with (WASH Cluster - Hygiene Kits v2 final -
COVID 19).
-Conducting 81 hygiene promotion campaigns, SECD will target the entire community with
at least COVID 19 awareness. The hygiene promotion campaign will focus on
Coronavirus-2019, SECD will adhere to the recommendation of the WASH cluster in the
implementation of the hygiene promotion and will focus on using the recommended
methods, also during these campaigns SECD will distribute PSEA for bene¦ciaries to
increase the knowledge and access to the PSEA reporting hotline.
Supporting sanitation works which include but are not limited to :
-Provision and installation of 11050 garbage bins 0.1m3 size for the small camps.
-Rehabilitation and or /providing of the solid waste truck.
-Support SWM unit with salaries and fuel for 6 months, and uniforms for the workers, masks, gloves to protect the workers of coronavirus-2019 in this service with Conducting solid waste campaigns to encourage people to participate in this humanitarian effort.
-Rehabilitation of the sewer system which includes but is not limited to Rehabilitation and
extending of Sewer system network ( (HDPE 30cm diameter ):6800m,- (HDPE100 cm diameter ):8600m.
SECD will be in a close position with the local council, the beneficiaries, the IDPs, and the host community and will involve them in all the project stages from the planning till the closure of the project to adhere to the community engagement approach by inviting them to regular FGDs and open all the channels to receive their feedback, complaints, and opinions. the proposed project ensures that the sanitation service would be provided to schools, hospitals, children-friendly centers, Women empowerment centers, and public bakeries. SECD have regular access in Non-GoS controlled areas: in Aleppo governorate in the area
controlled by Turkish Armed forces and National Army and In Idleb governorate in the area
under controlled by non-state armed groups
SECD will conduct a deep KAP assessment prior the hygiene promotion campaigns to ensure that the chosen subject is not covered by other NGOs / radio / TV (COVID-19 prevention measures)

4 - Needs

The provision of a comprehensive WASH package throughout the year must be unconditionally sustained for over 2 million IDPs living in displacement sites across NW and NE Syria. Resilience-focused, more sustainable, durable, and cost-effective solutions like connection to existing water networks, the establishment of simplified water and sewer networks, the introduction of wastewater treatment or solarization should be considered, where possible, preferably in 2022 to reduce the operational costs in the following year of the HRP. Similarly, the construction of HH-level/shared family WASH facilities, currently a privilege for only around 40% of HHs in IDP sites, should be supported to mitigate the GBV risks and vandalization of communal facilities. The proposed interventions depended on the findings of primary data collection by SECD staff during October 2021 who collected the data by using two methodologies: KII and rapid need assessment with IDPs and hostedpeople and deep technical assessment, The results showed the importance of supporting the targeted communities who are suffering from the lack of the resources to ensure access to the basic life-saving WASH needs, This assessment also aims to get information on the estimated population and vulnerability. 9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Activity 1: Support for water supply systems.Protection Risk/Threat: Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empowers or strengthens the position of armed groups or other actors.Likelihood: Medium Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organize joint planning culture, and agree on the minimum standard. Activity 2: Supporting solid Waste Management (SWM)Protection Risk/Threat: Movements of garbage trucks in congested.Reliability of services.Likelihood: High.Impact: low. Mitigating Measure: Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes, and schedule; Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school. Activity 3:Conduct and rehabilitate sewage systemThe risk: Health risks associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases.Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating Measure: SECD will Ensure staff and contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff and contractors.

SECD conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils and civil society activists, as well as by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released by the international community.
