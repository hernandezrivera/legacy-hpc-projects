Project Summary

The present proposal is an intervention aimed at scaling up the Health response. In line with the ERP strategy (Objectives 1 and 4), and building on the assessments carried out by ARCS and AMEL, the intervention will increase preventive health care awareness and services, and bridge the gap between primary and secondary/tertiary health by giving access to advanced diagnostics services, including transportation to health facilities. A total of 7000 direct beneficiaries will benefit from the proposed intervention.
Around 6000 beneficiaries (Lebanese and Migrants) from Saida, Halba, Sour and Nabatiye will benefit from preventive healthcare services, awareness sessions (at least 50% women, and about 11% elderly above 65 y.o..), and the provision of needed PPEs.
Additional 1000 beneficiaries from Sour (Sour and Bazourieh), Marjayoun (Khiyam) and Hasbaya and Baabda (Bourj el Barajneh) districts will access advanced diagnostics services and awareness sessions on preventive healthcare, including transportation to/from hospital facilities. AMEL will guarantee the needs of the most vulnerable patients through screenings at their PHCCs in the target areas, while ARCS will manage the referrals from AMEL by giving access to and covering advanced diagnostics tests. In parallel, during the screenings, AMEL medical staff will identify cases of malnutrition and will refer them internally and externally for management, while ARCS will provide targeted awareness sessions on nutrition and preventive health behaviors to the beneficiaries accessing advanced diagnostics.
ARCS will conduct baseline, midline endline MEAL activities, particularly surveys and FGDs, to ensure adaptive management throughout the implementation.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Objective 1 (ERP - Health Sector): A1.1: Medical screenings at PHCC level and identification of advanced diagnostics needs for vulnerable patients in Sour, Bazourieh (Sour district), Khiyam (Marjayoun district) and Hasbaya and Bourj al Barajneh (Baabda district);A.1.2: Access to advanced diagnostics services in Sour, Bazourieh (Sour district), Khiyam (Marjayoun district) and Hasbaya and Bourj al Barajneh (Baabda district) and coverage of transportation to/from hospital facilities. Objective 4 (ERP - Health Sector): A.4.1. Provision of PPEs to the governmental hospitals of Halba, Saida, Nabatieh and Sour;A.4.2 Community awareness on preventive healthcare through door-to-door activities and information hubs in the districts of Halba, Baabda, Saida, Nabatieh, Marjayoun, Hasbaya and Sour - during outreach activities, referrals will be processed to activities A1.1 and A1.2. MONITORINGARCS will ensure constant monitoring of the project and the measurement of progress against the indicators and the real impact on the beneficiaries through:- beneficiaries’ database, gathering disaggregated data on target beneficiaries and vulnerability scores;- internal monitoring reports to track progress on activities progress;- base-, mid-, and end-line surveys to targeted beneficiaries to assess the impact and quality of the services;- weekly field visits by ARCS to the target locations to follow up with the partner.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Within the intervention, men and women will have equal access to the services provided. During the satisfaction surveys conducted at the end of the activities, ARCS will ensure to incorporate gender-sensitive questions into the survey to measure satisfaction to the assistance provided and how it affected men and women differently. A GBV referral mechanism will be implemented in case hardship cases are identified,to be referred through the existing referral mechanism put in place by the Protection Sector and through RIMS platform.Furthermore, local partner’s capacity and expertise in delivering other health and protection services will guarantee a better integration of services to reduce target beneficiaries' vulnerability and ensure access to healthcare to a larger part of the population. Local partners have been selected based on a Capacity Assessment Tool developed by ARCS to ensure that partners, among other standards, have in place mechanisms to prevent PSEA.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

By applying the AGD approach, ARCS is able to assure direct involvement in each stage of the project of the communities and at the same time to be accountable. A trustworthy relationship with the affected population will be guaranteed by the engagement of dedicated staff and volunteers from the community. AMEL staff and ARCS volunteers are well-trained on PSEA, conflict sensitivity and non-violent communication and additional training on Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) will be provided as part of the capacity development component. ARCS will adopt an anonymous feedback and complaint form for the affected population. At project onset, ARCS will activate a Hotline to increase its AAP capacity. ARCS will ensure referrals are filled and managed through the RIMS platform. At the onset and at the end of the project, ARCS staff will assess the impact through surveys with target beneficiaries.
