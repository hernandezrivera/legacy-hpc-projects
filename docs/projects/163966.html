Project Summary

In the poultry sector:
The objective for this sector is to contribute to the reactivation of poultry farms for reproduction and food security, and to support the poultry sector through the following activities:
1- Alseeraj will support 70 poultry farms selected in each region according to specific criteria
2- Purchase (chicks - sawdust - medicine - fodder supplement - charcoal - power source (solar power)- vitamins and sugar supplements.
3- The other sub-activities will be conducted are(supervise the feeding process, monitor the overall condition of the poultry, provide health care for the little chicks, carry out immunization during the life of the little chicks according to the vaccine program.
* A portion of the production estimated at 56,000 chicken (70 * 800) will be returned and prepared for distribution in camps for IDPs and people in need according to the selected criteria by Alseeraj.
4- Support owners of the poultry farms by multi-training.
5- Distribute hatchers to breeders working by solar power, which have the egg-laying chickens.
In the livestock sector:
The activities under this section aim to support the livestock herders, living in Syria. which provides to preserve the health of livestock, increases production and grants economic security for the working families.
1-support 500 families have livestock according to criteria.
2- Alseeraj will provide these families with necessary supplies for the livestock, such as fodder, fodder supplements (Salt and vitamins) for three months, vaccines against foot and mouth disease and veterinary services.
Training for the herders will carry out by veterinarians; The training provides the herders with the information about The care for livestock, the methods of forming the fodder mixing, and the main diseases that affected the livestock and its treatment.
In the vocational training sector:
in order to alleviate the unemployment rate, which is significantly widespread in northern Syria, and provid high quality local products
Training (500) beneficiaries, especially the female ones, through a series of vocational courses in order to meet the local market need
In the agriculture sector:
1- Cultivation of 500 hectares of wheat, chickpeas, Bean,.….ect
2- Cultivation of 4 hectares of various vegetables(cucumber, tomatoes, okra, zucchini, pumpkin green pepper)
3- Cultivation of 150 hectares of mix-fodder for animals. The crops are Jalban and Barley.
3- Increasing of the agricultural experience for the BNFs and reduce of unemployment.
In Food sector:
The objective for this sector is to provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people to alleviate suffering.
1- Distribute food basket for 50,000 families in Syria.
2- Distribute ready to eat ration for 50,000 families in Syria.
3- Distribute cooked meals for 50,000 families in Syria
The fund for the Project is secure for 10% and unsecured for 90% and it is 100% FSL sector.

4 - Needs

Nine years into the crisis, the food security situation in Syria has deteriorated compared to last year, with a total of 9.8 million people in need; of which 569,477 people are estimated to be severely food insecure; 7.3 million estimated to be moderately food insecure while 1.9 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity.According to the Reach report, 25 Feb 2020 the most emergency need was The lack of food and the subsequent adoption of negative coping mechanisms as a result (i.e. people eating one or less meals per day) – with an estimated 12,000 households eating one meal or less per day.- This year the estimated number of people in need has increased by 8 percent. The number of food-insecure people has also increased from 6.5M (2019) to 7.9M (2020), approximately 20 percent increase from last year, with a decrease of almost 0.7M in people at risk compared to last year – those at risk have likely become people in need this year.(Need Assessment Report 2020 OCHA) - Approximately 2.5 million people displaced across North West and North-East Syria. Within this group, some 760,889 are residing in the last resort camps. During recent assessments, 82 percent of IDPs surveyed reported being displaced for more than 12 months.(Need Assessment Report 2020 OCHA) - In the North East, the impact of the conflict could affect at least 1.2 million people as a result of the creation of the 32km buffer zone. Currently, there are 496,590 displaced people across three governorates with fears of further unrest if Syrian refugees were forced to move into the territory from Turkey. (Need Assessment Report 2020 OCHA)- All population groups have been affected by the protracted crisis. In particular, children, under 5 and pregnant and/ or lactating women face the highest risk in terms of a deteriorate. (Need assessment 2020 OCHA)

5 - Implementation of activities

The proposed activities are expected to start in Mar - 2020 and end in December 2020. The project start date has been set taking into consideration the urgent need for support livestock and chicken herders during the winter months. During the lean period, farmers relay on animal feed for their livestock also, cultivate the multi-crops for themselves also, we will distribute food Basket for the emergency status for IDPs .All project activities will be implemented directly by Alseeraj within the target areas and will involve and cooperate with local partners in all stages of the implementation process. A team will be assigned including a project manager and field coordinators (agricultural engineers and veterinarians) and we will work to include employees in the staff for supporting the families (most of which being female-headed). Contracts will be made with specialized instructors to provide support in training workshops.Purchases will be carried out in a transparent manner and in accordance with the procurement strategy approved by Al-Seeraj and will be overseen by the relevant specialists to get the best quality.The local team will be responsible for the implementation of all phases of the project _ procurement, distribution, monitoring and evaluation, the preparation of periodic reports and enacting any necessary updates at every stage.Trainers will be hired to add value to the planned business plans and ensure quality standards. Follow-up training will also be undertaken up to several weeks after the training to assess if beneficiaries have continued to practice their livelihood activity with good demonstrated skills.Alseeraj will work closely with the project manager to fill the gaps that occur because of the region's unstable conditions and any amendments it deems appropriate for the continuity of the project.Periodic reports by the project manager and the surveillance team in addition to financial reports to ensure the implementation of the project as

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: community tension due to service delivery locations/types of services offered not consistent between communities. Mitigating Measures: Ensure local authority actively engage with communities to communicate to the local population the reasons for prioritizing the selected areas. Monitoring: SDH will continuously monitor the reaction of communities to services provided, and mitigate any disputes by reaching out through FGD's, communities and local leaders. Risk: Barriers to access for marginalized groups Mitigating measures: Specific measures are taken to ensure input flow from marginalized groups; Feedback informs planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Mitigation measures are brainstormed with the community to ensure equal access to SDH activities. Monitoring: FGDs, feedback mechanism Risk: SCI or partner staff violating SCI CSG rules harms children; damages the reputation of the organization; Mitigating measures: All SDH staff, volunteers and representatives are trained on and sign the Child Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct, as well as on reporting mechanisms; Risk: Affected populations and SC staff and volunteers, are placed at risk of sexual exploitation and abuse, including harassment. Mitigating measures: Training will be provided to SDH staff and volunteers partners on SDH ’s Anti-Harassment Policy and Code of Conduct and the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) through SDH ’s PSEA focal points. Overall Monitoring: As some activities harbor high risks for both beneficiaries and humanitarian workers (including risk of sexual exploitation or harassment), SDH will be trained on potential risks and how to mitigate them and provided with confidential complaint mechanisms to report in case of an event

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: - Stakeholders will take part in the project with all the power they have.- The security situation of the region is relatively stable.- The project steps will be implemented within the designated time frame of the projectRisk: Security risk, Some diseases occur, The quality of chicks are bad, Lack of materials in the areamitigation: Provide vaccinations, health care and supervision of the poultry farms by veterinarians, We will establish the center in relatively safe areas, Permanent sterilization and no access but to specific persons, We will check them through our team of doctors, Dealing with dealers can secure these materials. Assumption: Availability of materials and livestock inputs in the area.Risk: Lack of some materials or livestock inputs in the area.mitigation: dealing with suppliers that provide and deliver livestock inputs in the target area.Assumption: Dealing transparently with the team and the beneficiaries or traders.Risk: Problems between staff and stakeholders.mitigation: Selection of trained personnel to deal with beneficiaries.Assumption: Improve the health of livestock and increase production.Risk: Death of livestock and low productivity due to disease outbreaks.mitigation: Regular vet check-ups for livestock and immediate treatment in the event of an outbreak of disease.Assumption: Cultivation of land and harvest of crops.Risk: Occurrence of land fires and theft of harvests.mitigation: Periodic visits by field coordinators and the M&E team to ensure that the process of agriculture is going well.
