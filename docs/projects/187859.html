Project Summary

The proposed project will strengthen access to lifesaving and life-sustaining essential health services and COVID-19 response in northern Aleppo and northern Idleb. By keep running 4 functional primary health care facilities for a duration of 11 months. These primary health care facilities are part of the health facilities map and coordinating and reporting its activities to the health cluster on a monthly basis. The primary health care service provided in these facilities as per the Essential Health Service Package ESHP and considering MHPSS services and COVID-19 services as it is recommended.
The proposed facilities and target areas are as follows;
1- A’zaz subdistrict 5.2 high priority areas; Alresala Camp. 1 Fixed PHC level 2. Code: B0204068
2- Afrin subdistrict 4 .3 high priority area: Afrin City. 1 Fixed PHC level 2: code: B0203061
3- Afrin, Sharan camps subdistrict 4.1 high priority area; 1 Mobile Clinic: Code: B0203071
4- Harim, Dana subdistrict, Al Manarah camp and nearby settlements 5.0 high priority area; 1 Mobile clinic.: Code: G0703157
The main project components include.
1- Provide primary health care services for two PHCs and two mobile clinics based on the essential package of services target population of 51093 IDPs and 35,504HH.
2- provide the Mental health care services of PHCs, and the mobile clinics on the
essential package of services for target population of 44,675 IDPs and 28,563 HH
estimated number of people with chronic illness or psychological condition 2044 people.
The proposed facilities maintain high coordination with the local referral system for higher management facilities and share monthly reports with health cluster 4Ws and the EWARN system.
A- These proposed 4 Primary health care facilities will provide the recommended services for level 2 PHCs as ( GP, Midwifery, pediatrician, community awareness, deducting and referring COVID-19 suspected cases, and all these health services will be the CHWG, screen the community for CD and NCD and refer vulnerable people to health facilities using community referral cars. They will identify PWDs who do not have assistive devices and provide them with the devices after completing a consultation with health facilities
B- Mental health and psychosocial support
The MHPSS services will be provided in fixed PHCs in Aresalah camp/A’zaz and Afrin city for 3 days per week. Each of the 4 facilities will be supported with 4 MHCWs full time to provide outreach mental health awareness and referral.
-Screening of acute malnutrition and treatment of moderate acute malnutrition and severe acute malnutrition without complications, referral to care for severe acute malnutrition with complications, and counseling for Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF),
-GBV first-line support with referral to specialized GBV services,
-NCD care,
-Provision of facilitated referral to diagnostic services.
The Information about the project will be channeled through the local authorities and the health directorates. Dissemination of information will be done collaboratively with health directorates, other NGO actors, and the health cluster. reporting mechanism 4W, Cluster and the MHPSs TWG, in addition, information through DHIS2
The following routine monitoring and tools will be used:

- Exit surveys will be conducted of patients at PHCs to assess their satisfaction about PHCs.
- MEAL plan is designated to support the collection and tracking of sex and age disaggregated data in line with proposed health interventions and is undertaken directly by MEAL in collaboration with the health and nutrition department. Monitoring methodologies include needs assessments, baseline, and end line, and activity monitoring.
