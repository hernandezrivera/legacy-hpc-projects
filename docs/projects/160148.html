Project Summary

The project intervention is geared toward preventing and responding to eviction, monitoring of threats to evictions by assessing potential eviction sites, monitoring, documentation and reporting to relevant authorities and relevant clusters to mobilize support for advocacy. The advocacy and campaigns will aim to ensure actors facilitate lawful eviction processes. Capacity development targeting settlement managers, local authorities on Housing, Land and Property rights, advocacy on HLP rights and forced evictions.

Project Activities

Activity 1.1.1 Assessment monitoring threats of eviction by landlords, authorities and settlement managers, the assessment will also cover land tenure use, status of rental agreements, and forced evictions, assessment to be conducted by trained.This activity involves undertaking a rapid assessment of the current ownership and tenure rights of IDP communities in Banaadir, Lower Juba and Gedo and a detailed study of property state, legal documentation and tenure rights in case and/or to motivate a return to areas of origin once the conflict has ceased. Activity 1.1.2 Establishing 4 Information Centers within the targeted areas. The aim is to create local systems that will reduce forced evictions through an enhanced commitment of authority towards HLP issues and through improved local knowledge and practices related to HLP issues including legal frameworks and rights on rental and contractual procedures and arrangements. The centers will be established as part of structure within the targeted area and will include a paralegal team and field monitors who will be reaching out to IDPs, landowners, and local authorities. The paralegals and field monitors will closely coordinate with other organizations outreach teams like NRC, IOM, and UNHCR to ensure synergies and avoid overlapping of activities. Activity 1.1.3 Awareness Raising is one of the key functions of the information centers which will be to increase local awareness on HLP issues and legal rights on land rental and contractual procedures and arrangements. The campaigns will target different beneficiary groups; IDPs, host communities, camp management, landowners, and other agencies providing other services within the same camps. This will be conducted by the development of IEC materials (flyers, posters, banners, factsheets on HLP, T-shirts and caps. The community outreach teams (paralegals and monitors) will use these materials during their weekly community outreach to inform the target communities on HLP issues. Activity 1.1.4 PSS support for children and families affected by forced eviction Provide age-appropriate Psychosocial Support (PSS) through emotional support groups for 1,000 traumatized children and their families, through mobile and static teams of PSS Workers, this intervention targets families affected by forceful evictions. Activity 1.1.5 Building cohesion between IDPs and host communities, Peacebuilding community committees established to support prevention of conflict and response efforts to enhance peaceful coexistence. (Weekly dialogue sessions with community leaders (Women Group, Elders, Religious Leaders, and Youth Group) on social cohesion and peacebuilding and then establish 8 committee groups to meet weekly for 6 months, each group with 12 participants and 2-month action plan and implementation in all the targeted areas. Activity 1.1.6 Training of community leaders, camp leaders, local authorities and paralegal interns in Law schools on Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) issues such as security of land tenure, forced evictions, displacement and durable solutions.

Mitigation Measures

2a. Exclusion and marginalization: Members of marginalized community’s/ minority groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance - Mitigation measures include the following;1- Design assessments to enhance participation - include representatives of diverse groupsas key informants (eg. women, older persons, persons with disabilities, single-headedhouseholds, minorities, and other marginalized groups)2- Aim to have a proportionate balance of male and female enumerators to interviewboth male and female respondents and respect cultural practices. 3- When engaging with children, obtain parental consent; use age-appropriate questions;and ensure enumerators are trained in interviewing children4- Use local knowledge to determine the cultural appropriateness of the questions;5- Collect Sex, age and diversity disaggregated data whenever appropriate 3b. External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance - Mitigation measures includes the following;1- Ensure assistance is being offered to the most vulnerable groups to prevent them fromresorting to negative coping strategies.2- Ensure the provision of livelihood programs does not increases or creates protection risks.3- Identify the main obstacles for women/girls/older persons/persons with disabilities inaccessing health facilities (e.g. distance, cost, lack of female staff, cultural restrictions,mobility restrictions, lack of specialized services)4- Put in place mechanisms to provide assistance to persons who have difficultyaccessing the facilities (eg. mobile teams, transportation services)5- Train service providers to detect and refer to protection cases and put in place a system to refer and report protection-related cases. 4a. Protection mainstreaming: Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure in targeted project areas - Mitigation measures includes the following;1. Establish a referral process for legal disputes involving IDPs residents and landowners.2. Map HLP stakeholders in the selected regions.3. Establish referral mechanisms and, if possible, legal assistance for disputesarising from the tenure agreement between landowner and shelter beneficiary, to ensure thatdisputes are taken to the proper venue.4. IEC materials on HLP rights and distribute to communities; thesecan be translated into local Somali language5. provision of alternative land (private/government) for relocation
