Project Summary

Needs

The overall security situation in South Sudan has deteriorated significantly since mid- December 2013 to date. The situation in Jonglei State, Unity State, Upper Nile State, the Bahr El Ghazal, northern border areas and the Equatorias in South Sudan have become more hazardous because of intermittent but severe armed conflict between rival forces in many locations. The implementation of the Revitalized Peace Agreement may bring further challenges to the security environment/program delivery. All areas with humanitarian and other UN program operations require constant ‘security risk management’ (SRM) by security to provide access without jeopardizing the security and safety of humanitarian personnel and assets. SRM are deemed necessary to ensure updated first-hand knowledge and to ensure that information is provided on all areas where UN and Partners work through continued support of a wider security system using information-based analyses and security interventions. UNDSS plans to conduct frequent Security Assessments which will require air movement. It is also expected that UNDSS will facilitate medevac/relocation (or as situation arises) for injured/sick UN personnel in remote or areas with limited access. Many areas require joint program/security assessments and in many cases these responses must be conducted within a short period of time due to critical emergencies or limited safe windows of opportunity.
