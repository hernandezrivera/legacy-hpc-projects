Project Summary

the project is mainly about providing monthly unconditional cash assistance for people in need in Homs city to cope with the huge economic inflation and the deterioration of the economic situation and obtain their basic needs of food or non-food items.
the project will identify the most in need families in the city through the help of the association database and the coordination with the most active social actors in the city.
about 10000 families in Homs city will receive a cash assistance of 50 $ on a monthly basis for 1 year to enhance their resilience through the current harsh economic situation that the country are going through.
the transfers will be done using money transfers company spread in the city.
this kind of assistance will be provided to the most vulnerable families and it will save their dignity and help them purchase their basic needs freely like any others.

3 - Needs

11 years into the Syria crisis, humanitarian needs are deepening. Ongoing insecurity and the compounded impact of displacement, combined with worsening socio-economic conditions characterized by sharp currency depreciation,And as the economic situation continues to deteriorate, its impacts are being acutely felt by virtually all populations. By population group, it is the “vulnerable residents” category defined in the HNO - those that have not been recently displaced, that show the greatest increase in humanitarian needs: increasing from 6.4 million in 2020 to 9.2 million in 2021 – suggesting strongly that economic deterioration is now a major driver of needs in areas historically less directly affected by hostilities and displacement. ( 2022 HNO) therefore the Association is aiming at providing as much as possible of direct cash assistance to the most in need families in the city to help them meet their monthly basic needs and mitigate the devastating effect of their increased poverty due to the current economic situations.

4 - Implementation of activities

1- the association will identify & register the most in need families around the city through the help of local charity organization and its own database. 2- cash transfers to families on a monthly basis through money transfer companies. 3- continuous monitoring of the families to detect the project impact.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk AnalysisRisks: 1- There is a risk of discrimination or perceptions of discrimination if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will provide its services to most in need families with no discrimination, clear criteria will be considered to choose the beneficiaries according to the socio-economy study. 2. Inflation: likelihood: medium. Impact: High. Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will sign contracts and make agreements with some vendors in advance to provide basic materials with low prices to PINs in order to ensure stability in prices as much as we can. In addition, Al-Birr may increase the cash amount according to variance in prices. 3. decreased capacity of FSP. Likelihood: medium. Impact: high. Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will contract with more than one FSPs in order to lower this risk, and lower load on each FSP.
