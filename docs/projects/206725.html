Project Summary

COHESODEC will in this educational emergency response be scaling up education response in vulnerable communities involving IDP and host community children. Given that COHESODEC has since 2017 been ensuring that children study in 45 Learning Centers across Mezam, Momo and Menchum Divisions of the North-West Region, we shall ensure that this project significantly ameliorates on the study conditions of IDP children as well as encourage all out of school children to enroll in schools or community learning centers. The relevance of this project cannot be overemphasized as it will enable COHESODEC to scale up education in the North-West Region to combat illiteracy and promote the fundamental right to education which has been violated following the on-going conflict in this affected Region of Cameroon.
Almost a million children since 2016 have been out of school owing to the vulnerabilities of this conflict. Given that COHESODEC has already negotiated humanitarian access with both state & Non-State Armed Groups, it is imperative through this project to ensure a significant increase in the current low rate of school going children.
This project seeks to target 35,820 out of school children between the ages of 3 and 20 to ensure they have access to quality education in the midst of the conflict in the following localities of the North-West region of Cameroon in 2024: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda I, Bamenda II, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Fungom, Furu-Awa, Menchum-Valley & Wum.
In addressing these educational needs through this project, COHESODEC will be constructing 20 classrooms and 20 latrines in some of the above-mentioned localities to accommodate these vulnerable out of school children. Also, in a bit to reduce the high rate of illiteracy & to enhance knowledge among out of school IDP children and host communities, COHESODEC will be putting in place a Radio Education Programs & digital learning platforms to enable 4,310 girls, boys & adolescents affected by the crises able to attend non formal education thereby enabling them have access to alternative education.
COHESODEC will equally be carrying out an advocacy & social dialogue with stakeholders in the education sector involving 4,300 community members, teachers, administrative/school officials and Partners to foster the access of all children to education, including girls and children living with disabilities. Moreover, COHESODEC will be carrying out capacity building sessions with 180 teachers, staff & school administrators to enhance their capacities in Inclusive Education so as to ensure all learning spaces are gender, age, race & ethnically inclusive & child friendly.
This project shall also ensure the purchase and distribution of adequate learning material supplies to 500 children which are inclusive & sex specific to children living with disabilities and affected by the crisis while also addressing the severe problems of lack of school materials/needs for 13,750 children such as bags, books, pens, pencils, etc.). These learning materials will comprise of a variety of different learning items at different levels & according to the learning needs of the children.
COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will equally be providing grants to 3,500 children in the form of financial support to schools & training institutions as well as the most vulnerable families & children. The project will also be training 180 teachers, supervisors & other Education staff in life skills, psychosocial support & other topics related to education in emergencies.
Moreover, the project shall be ensuring the revitalization of 60 School Management Committees as well as the training of 180 parents, teachers, supervisors & other Education staff in the mission of School Management Committees & other topics related to school governance.
Finally, in implementing this project, COHESODEC shall be ensuring the sensitization of 13,750 students, parents, teachers & supervisors.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Since the outbreak of the crisis in the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon, Education has been one of the most affected sectors by the conflict resulting to a lot of displacements and causing many children to stay out of school. It is in this light that it is absolutely imperative to address critical education needs. COHESODEC will be requiring funds to ensure the construction of 20 classrooms in some of the affected communities to address these needs as well as the construction of 20 latrines in some educational facilities to curb the deplorable WASH needs in schools and community learning centers lacking these WASH facilities. Furthermore, COHESODEC will equally be acquiring radios for the implementation of a Radio Education Programs & also ensuring digital learning in the midst of the crisis to ensure that 4,310 children benefit from distance learning in targeted communities in the earmarked subdivisions of the North-West Region of Cameroon. In a bit to ensuring that children attend schools/learning spaces freely and safely, COHESODEC will also be requiring sensitization materials, the use of local community radio stations as well as other community transmission mechanisms of advocacy and social dialogue involving 4,300 stakeholders in the education sector such as community members, teachers, administrative/school officials and partners) to foster the access of all children to education, including girls and children living with disabilities. Moreover, COHESODEC will require financial resources with which to carryout Seminars/Workshops during capacity building sessions involving 180 teacher/facilitators and supervisors in inclusive Education and Psychosocial Support. The project will equally provide learning materials to 500 children living with disabilities and 13,750 boys, girls and adolescent children to ensure a smooth learning process and build confidence in the learners as well as ensure they feel safe and dignified. Finally, COHESODEC through the implementation of this project will also be providing grants in the form of financial support to schools, training institutions & the most vulnerable families & children.
