Project Summary

The project aims to strengthen childhood education in the fragile context of Sila Province, Eastern Chad – one of the poorest regions in the world. The project will provide quality childhood education for around 4,000 girls and boys over 2 years. Moreover, the project will ensure the durability of the support over multiple years. The duration of the project will be 27 months, from 1st of May 2022 to 31st of July 2024.

1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

The education system in the Sila Province is particularly weak, and not well supported by the central authorities. The centralised system should ensure financial allocations and paid teachers in every school. In reality, most schools do not receive any support, teachers are not present or not paid. Schools buildings (if they exist) are in a state of disrepair, there is little equipment or school furniture, and hygiene infrastructure and access to water is limited. Support for teachers is also lacking with little support from local authorities or access to trainings or evaluations. Under these conditions, school attendance is very low and as a result the education indicators and educational performance of children is abysmal.
