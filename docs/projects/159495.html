Project Summary

Based on coordination with MOH there are around 9,000 cases who are in need for elective surgeries. Among them, there are 2,500 minor E.N.T cases and around 2.000 major and moderate cases. Moreover, MOH has asked Arab Ahli Hospital (AAH) to support them minimizing the load of other kind of surgeries such orthopedic cases, vascular, urology, surgical and gynecology as AAH has qualified full time consultant’s surgeons. Also, AAH in partnership with LWF/AVH has the capacity to perform 2,300 surgeries per year. Currently, AAH has two Operating theaters and another room to increase the numbers of surgeries. AAH will use MIS (Minimal Invasive Surgeries) techniques whenever possible to minimize patients’ pain, complications and staying in hospital as well as maintaining body image especially for women such as cholecystectomy. Furthermore, LWF/AVH has partnership with AAH that includes empowering the infection prevention control at AAH where AAH has formed an IPC committee. LWF/ AVH and AAH has many successes in the IPC area that saved lots of injured people from amputations and was published at WHO trauma report that covered GMR from end of March 2018 to March 2019. Therefore, AAH will conduct 2,300 elective procedures that are referred form MOH as following:
1) E.N.T surgeries (750 Minor, 650 Moderate and 150 Major)
2) Orthopedic and tendon repair surgeries 200
3) General and Gynecology Surgery 300
4) Urology 100
5) Minimal Invasive Surgeries 100
6) Vascular Surgeries 100

1 - Needs

According to MOH 2019 and as indicated in the Health Cluster HNO for 2020, there are around 9,000 cases who are in need for elective surgeries while MOH is not able to do them on time related to so many factors. These factors are: 12 years blockade on Gaza, 2014 war, the internal Palestinian division and recently the Great March of return which is depleting the MOH resources. GMR is overburdening the MOH health care system on both scale and scope as more than 33,000 injured people have been affected while more than half are in need of surgeries and one third are in need for limb reconstruction. These factors has made a domino effect and shifted the elective scheduled cases who are in need for surgeries to months and even years especially E.N.T cases. According to OCHA 53% of Gaza population are under poverty line so, it is expected that at least 4,500 of the mentioned 9,000 elective cases will not be able to do their surgeries in a reasonable time which will impact their daily life activities and their quality of life.

c - Complaint box

8.6.c - How will feedback be used?

The intervention has been formulated in a participatory approach between LWF/AVH, AAH and MoH. The project design is based on the cluster priorities and humanitarian allocation document that includes MoH and health cluster.LWF/AVH and AAH will work closely to ensure quality of health care for people in need for elective surgeries. To ensure accountability to patients several mechanisms are set in place: First, a functional complaint /feedback mechanism is set in place. Second, LWF/AVH will randomly meet patients to have their impression and satisfaction on the level of service provided. Third, LWF/AVH will monitor the partner practices and their compliance with CHS benchmarks especially the beneficiary complaint and feedback mechanism and supports them to enhance such practices at both the project and organizational level. The complaint handling systems are emphasized in all LWF/AVH’scooperation agreements with its partners. LWF/AVH and AAH will pay a specific attention to increasing the capability of the complaints and feedback mechanism to inform decision-making, enhance accountability and transparency, and increase the ownership of beneficiaries over the project. This will be ensured through engaging beneficiaries in addressing the expected risks and challenges and empowering them to participate in the monitoring and evaluation process of the project.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

The proposed intervention has been designed to fill the specific critical gap which is long waiting list at MOH and lack off accessibility and affordability by patients to receive such services. AAH is a well-known surgical hospital that provides quality of services where AAH follows WHO safety checklist pre-intra and post operation. AAH will sensitize its staff to be able to detect the GBV cases and refer them to appropriate services considering the protection cluster guidelines.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

Health Cluster has indicated in its HNO for 2020 that 9,000 patients who are in need for elective surgeries should be prioritized by health providers with focus on E.N.T. Therefore, such group is being vulnerable and cases will be referred by MOH based on coordination through referral system.
