Project Summary

Selecting the project area to be one of the areas affected during the crisis, where the students had lost the chance to continue their educational process as a result of displacement. The target beneficiaries will be 600 students (boys and girls) who dropped –out their classes and who have clear weakness in main subjects as a result of the crisis
The project outcomes:
• Improve the level of 600 targeted students at the end of the project to become at their normal educational level in order to follow up their peers.
• Reintegrate the students into their schools and regular classes.
• Raising the level of students' educational achievement after receiving a remedial education curriculum and psychological support for one year
• Prevention of many mental illnesses that can affect the student who is deficient in learning.
• Raising the level of performance of the educational process in the classroom as a result of the students' learning at a very consistent level of knowledge.
• Raising the level of education and educational achievement for students in the project area in general
• Reducing illiteracy rates and school dropouts in the project area.
• Targeting students who are the most vulnerable children.
• Targeting students who are from women headed families.
• Targeting 50% female students and 50% male ones.
• Targeting 50% IDPs students and 50% hosting ones.
• Targeting 10% PWD students.
• Perform ASER test for these students to determine each level and divide them into groups according to the level rather than the age.
Finally, reintegrate the successful students in their regular classes after we have bridged the gap between them and their peers.

Needs

The project area includes many communities about 30000 populations-IDPs and hosting communities. There are around 10 primary schools, most of them were target of war planes and shellings which led to the dropout of the students.• There is a gap in the level of students per class, whether in one or more of the basic subjects such as Arab - mathematics – English.• This variation in levels is often caused by partial drop-out of students due to displacement. This disparity weakens the educational process in one class and hinders its progress in a good and effective manner. This results in a deterioration in the level of education in general.• Psychological damage to students resulting from the state of default and weakness in some subjects compared to their peers.• The large prevalence of this phenomenon of variation in the level of students per class due to the dropout of the school because of war and displacement.• Rehabilitative education should be considered as the best method of education to compensate for drop-out among students who are excluded from school and to be reintegrated into their appropriate classes.that is based on rapid need assessment provided by our field team

Implementation of activities

During this remedial educational process will take 6 months. The number of the targeted beneficiaries is 600 students (boys and girls) who meet the selection criteria. Based on that each student will receive intensive educational teaching which will focus on the basic subjects (Arabic - Mathematics - English). This process will be led by highly qualified teachers( male and female) who have a good experience in remedial education. The target students will be provided with stationery and all educational requirements. In addition to the transportation service from and to the center. as well as the target students will be provided with daily meal (milk, eggs, juice, sandwiches….etc ). Moreover, protection sessions will be conducted to mitigate the psychological affects which the students suffer from. Recreational activities (drawing, hand crafts, vacations and chess) will also take place in order to motivate the students to overcome their psychological trauma.the main activities:• Signing the memorandum of understanding with the local councils in the targeted areas.• Announce project activities.• Provide appropriate construction of the educational process with all the necessary tools and equipment.• Recruiting of the technical implementation team of the relevant teachers in the project area.• Targeting students from grades (1st to 5th grades) who need support in basic subjects (Arabic - Mathematics - English).• Registration of students who suffer from a weakness in some or all of the school materials in the project area based on the incoming lists of these schools from the first to the fifth grade.• Conducting ASER test to determine the level of these students to classify them according to their levels of education.• Launching the educational process with follow-up and evaluation for a full educational year and two consecutive semesters• End-of-year testing- ASER- to determine students' level, evaluate work overall and reintegrate students into their natural classes.• Shut down the suspensions and submit a final report.

Program activities are designed to be culturally relevant and appropriate, and tailored to the specific context, while preventing competition over resources. IACO with corporation with partner will integrate protection topics, including preventing sexual exploitation and abuse, and prioritizing the safety and dignity of beneficiaries.
