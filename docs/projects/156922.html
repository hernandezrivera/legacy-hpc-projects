Project Summary

Yalda, Babila and Beit Sahim or Southern Damascus area are a part of Rural Damascus governorate. Located near to Damascus airport highway. It was Ruled by the opposition militias from 2012 – APR 2018 . During the past 5 years the people in this area faced many difficulties started with the lake of food and medicine then, beside many military actions. Until 2014 and the first conciliation in YBB which suspended all the military actions between SAA and opposition militias in YBB. In 2015 ISIS invaded Al-Yarmouk camp, and many people fled from Al-Yarmouk to YBB. This decampment increased the population in the area and made the humanitarian situation more difficult. After that, the Syrian government entered YBB in April 2018.
More than 311000 people live there, they are in a bad need for health services (primary and secondary health care), only the Syrian MOH and SARC work there (as mobile teams) -no governmental or private medical centers and some NGO's mobile teams.
UNRWA also works there (as mobile teams) for Palestine refugees only. The number of those who are returning is increasing and a larger number is waiting for a compromise with the government to be set up.
There is a bad need for treatment of hundreds of patients daily (Non-Communicable Diseases - Pregnant Women – children – scabies, etc…).
In Al Qunaitira, and similarly to YBB a very bad need for Medical Care both Primary and Secondary was reported.
The estimated number of population there is over 150000 with almost 100000 people in such a need.
The end of 2018 witnessed an increased number of people returning to their homes that lack the most essential Medical Services (With only one governmental hospital). Al Qunaitira which consists of three main sectors (Northern - Central - Southern) with three centers (Hadar in the north - Khan Arnaba in the center - Sweisa in the south). Last of which had been accessible recently.

Needs

Background information:Yalda, Babella & Beit Sahm (YBB) and Al Qunaitira were affected by the crisis and witnessed since 2012 a severe humanitarian deterioration at all sectors mainly in food and health associated with movement restriction. Recently and following the conciliation agreement, civilians were able to move freely between the three communities in Rural Damascus and in Al Qunaitira (Khan Arnaba, Swisa And Hadar) the population there is increasing as many people have started to return to the area and YBB received several IDPs from neighboring Yarmuk camp. Public transportation was still not functional in Al Qunaitira. Estimated number of population is more than 311000 with more than 211000 people in need for medical care in YBB. The Estimated number of population is more than 150000 with more than 100000 people in need for medical care in Qunaitira. Health situation analysis:Reference to a recent update from the health working group on the health situation in YBB And Qunaitira, a visible absence of health coverage for local population has resulted in deterioration of health conditions was reported. There is a high number of NCD cases (including diabetes, hypertension) anemia and infectious diseases especially skin diseases (children) and degenerated diseases such as lower back pain, joint pain, and arthritis. Absence of laboratory services is common. High number of women is in need for RH services.

Implementation of activities

Response plan: In line with HNO strategic objective for health 2019 in terms of providing life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian health assistance with an emphasize on those most at risk and in need; Nour Foundation for Relief & Development plans to support the provision of the Essential Package of Primary Health care Services to cover essential health needs of vulnerable populations and IDPs in Yalda and Qunaitira surrounding areas through supporting:• 2 PHC centers, one center in Yalda And one center in Sweisa each one consists of 3 clinics: gynecology, pediatric and internal diseases. The centers will operate in two shifts from 9 am to 7 pm. Medical consultation and medication will be provided at the center while diagnosis investigation and surgical intervention under secondary and trauma care will be supported on a referral basis to the following health facilities: Mahaini Modern Hospital, Mahaini specialized Hospital, and AlImam Al Sadar hospital and Shams Medical Center. PLEASE NOTE:In Yalda there is a PHC center that is supported by WHO to provide the previously mentioned services but lacks the needed Mobile Teams Activites and this proposal will cover the fund for the Mobile Teams and their activities in the area.In Al Qunaitira we neither have a PHC center nor Medical Mobile Teams. (And here is where most of our planned budget would be used.)

Protection Risk Analysis

The only issue we have in consideration is that the targeted areas are freshly accessible but the fact that our mobile teams are has successfully completed tasks that were assigned to them in the mentioned areas without any risk-related issues.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

It's very well understood that accessing those areas will be needing more and more security approvals some of which are already obtained (All the areas in Al Qunaitira are now accessible).
