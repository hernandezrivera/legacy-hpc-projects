Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in Syria with no end in sight has resulted the most complex humanitarian crisis since the Second World War, where millions of people are displaced most of them women and children, and the majority are in need of humanitarian assistance. The conflict impact expanded overall the community sectors, Over the last six years, the education sector in Syria has been severely damaged. The fighting has destroyed schools and many teachers are seeking to survive by resorting to neighboring countries or seeking new opportunities in other countries. Schools have also become shelters for families displaced by the fighting, which has been reflected in a large extent on the absorptive capacity of schools and classrooms and negatively affected the student in terms of comprehension and understanding.
Degraded living conditions in most areas also affect the education due to the lack of interest in children education by the parents who are spending most of their time being preoccupied with handling the living of their families under the heavyweight of war life.
All the above-mentioned factors led to severe deterioration in education process in all Syria generally, Hence the importance of the project which aims to improve children’s access to quality education and protection support to improve the educational level and well- being of the children under the supervision of trained staff.
Therefore, OB would operate 10 Learning centers in order to response to the needs of students through the provision of educational services (Informal and remedial education) according to the curriculum followed in official schools, where all the 10 learning centers will provide educational sessions for children based on the formal curriculum being taught in official schools.
The location of centers will be as following:
(Idleb): Ain el bayda, Killy, Kaftien, Hazzano, Harem, Ehsem and Kafar Darian.
(Aleppo): Atareb, Dara Ezza and Abzemo
SO, OB will establish these centers to deliver the educational and PSS services. Firstly, OB will rehabilitate the 10 mentioned centers in Idleb and west Aleppo, then OB will provide them with furniture, OB will start the training for the educational staff and PSS facilitators.
OB staff will conduct back to learning campaign (BTL) to reach the children who dropped out from schools and register them, OB will go ahead to conduct the placement test to identify each child level.
OB will print and distribute the Books, Bags and all needed items (copybooks, stationary, …Etc) for the children.
OB will target the children with hygiene campaign through distributing hygiene kits, to start the educational process.
OB will conduct at the end of each term (two terms).
After each term (first and second one) the succeed children will be referral to the formal education schools so through processing these activities OB will encourage the chidlren to go back to formal education to follow up thier peers in same grade.
through implementing these activities OB will fill the gap of the children during the war happening in Syria to compensate them in both education and PSS sessions.
By implementing this project OB will fill the gap of the children who dropped out from the schools to encourage them that go back to the formal education schools

Protection Risk Analysis

The most expected risks that beneficiaries or humanitarian workers may be exposed to through the implementation of the project’s activities are:1. Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target areas, affects the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in project. This includes the risk of targeted attacks and bombing of schools/ communities and the risks of mines, airstrikes and cross fire.2. Child safety incidents in school due to bullying and overburdened teachers.3. Risk of not having enough WASH facilities for boys and girls. This might cause lack of privacy to the children.4. Risk of long distance and young children walking alone. Children may get lost, be attacked or lose interest in learning. 1- the related mitigating measures that will be adopted:Closely monitor the security situation in coordination with partners and relevant stakeholders and conduct a security assessment. They will ensure that staff members and teachers are trained in safety and security best practices, including mine risk education.2- If and when the local security situation deteriorates: a) Immediately inform internal emergency focal point on the situation; b) Coordinate with partners, cluster and hibernate/suspend the operations if necessary; c) Examine the possibilities of adopting different modalities to implement the project. All important documents will be saved as soft copies, so that they will not get lost due to an attack. Decisions are taken in advance3- Work in collaboration with WASH section who will establish WASH facilities in schools.4- Training of partners and local teams on Child safety, development of school’s specific child safety policies; establishment of complaint mechanisms in school.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Threats:1- Air attacks and indirect shots (High risk)2- IEDs or suicide bombs (High risk)kidnapping the staff (Low risk)3- Riots and demonstrations (Medium risk)4- Arrest and detention (Medium risk)5- Conflict between political /ethnic/religious groups (Low risk)MITIGATION MEASURE(S):1- Monitoring of news and information collection, Identification of health centers, Safe working area in the office, Hibernation, First aid training, Medical plan2- Gathering information and situational awareness, providing appropriate security advice, first aid training, medical plan.3- Standard mobility procedures, friends system, and security/locks on homes and offices.4- Safe setting, sharing information, providing appropriate security advice, good assessments of information, knowledge about the community, knowledge about the region/alternative routes.5- Follow the rules and regulations, keep in contact with local authorities, and social networks.6- Preparedness for Hibernation, keep neutral, orientation to safe places.
