4 - Needs

The prevalence of malnutrition is a significant public health challenge in Syria. The main groups affected by malnutrition are infants, young children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women (PLW). The different forms of malnutrition include wasting, that is, severe acute malnutrition (SAM) moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) as well as chronic malnutrition or stunting (low height for age) and micronutrient deficiency especially iron-deficiency anaemia which occurs in young children, PLW but in girls of reproductive age. According to the Child Nutrition Report (WOS, 2020) the percentage wasting in children 6-59 months was 12%. Modelling estimates indicated that the prevalence of stunting in children 6-59 months increased from 23% in 2012 to 30% in 2020 while iron-deficiency anaemia affected one in three women (2019). The rate of exclusive breastfeeding in infants 0-5 months was already low but declined further from 43% to 29% (2014-2020). In 2021, 85 subdistricts across Syria had 25% of children suffering from stunting (severity 3 classification). In 44 subdistricts there were 46% children 6-59 months with iron-deficiency anaemia (severity classification 4) this was coupled with poor infant and young child (IYCF) practices including low minimum acceptable diet (MAD) in children 6-23 months. according to HNO 2022 nutrition severity (azaz, Afrin, Dana) Stunting Rate 25%, GAM Rate 4.7%, SAM Rate 0.8%, children under 5 MAM Rate 3.9%, PLWs MAM Rate 12.4%, disability rate 15%, ANEMI

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The situation in Syria is exposing the population to a significant lack of coverage of basic needs. Additionally, it is characterized by a lack of institutional capacity to protect the civilian population. This does not only refer to female community members but also other marginalized or excluded groups such as PWDs or elderlies. nutrition assistance to the most vulnerable supports in increasing social tensions, the need for negative coping mechanisms and GBV exposed to especially women. Most important, IYCF & CMAM can Saving lifes provide dignity, safety, and protection through preventing exploitation, abuse, violence, and injury for those at risk. AlResala Foundation is carefully and thoroughly integrating protection mechanisms to understand the needs and risks of vulnerable community members and directly target those. AlResala is committed to mainstream protection in all activities and considers the protection principles,Do No Harm, accessibility, accountability, and participation. AlResala will continue to develop referral options as part of the accountability system for GBV and child protection issues with agencies operational and with technical expertise in these fields utilizing specific referral pathways. AlResala will identify and train Child and Gender Protection Focal Points.AlResala will ensure the complementarity with SEA prevention for this project aiming at decreasing risks of SEA for all categories of the affected population as it is a member of PSEA Network


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

RF remain accountable to affected populations through open dialogue, FGDs and feedback/complaint mechanisms provided to these populations. All IDPs in the target camps will have a direct channels for and PSEA hotlines through WhatsApp number, email, Facebook, and field staff. Channels were announced through posters and brochures that were posted in social media, local council offices and camp management offices. Also, effective systems are in place to prevent and respond to acts of sexual exploitation and abuse by incorporating protection from sexual exploitation and abuse into RF policies and ensuring that all employees read and sign the Code of Conduct, and that all contracts with partners / suppliers / contractors include clauses on PSEA. RF will ensures that complaints filing mechanisms are accessible to report sexual exploitation and abuse (complaints, WhatsApp, e-mail) and that the contacts receiving complaints understand how to perform their duties. The complaints mechanism will explaine to the beneficiaries, and whoever desired could photograph the complaints brochure considering Covid-19 precaution measures. Beneficiaries will be inform of the malnutrition screen process and the micronutrients distribution date prior to distribution by posting advertisements within the camp and in coordination with the camp administration, to inform all beneficiaries of the distribution date one day before the distribution.
