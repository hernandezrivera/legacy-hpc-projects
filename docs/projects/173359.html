Project Summary

This project intends to strengthen the capacity of boys, girls and parents to prevent abuse against children in their communities. The project will target beneficiaries from Blue Nile i.e. Kurmuk & Baw (level 3 severity ranking of protection needs, HNO 2021) and South Kordofan i.e. Al Leri & Talawdi (level 4 severity ranking of protection needs, HNO 2021).
The proposed CN is aligned with the following HRP 2021 Protection Sector Objectives:
- Provide protection services to vulnerable crisis-affected people, particularly those with special needs.
- Strengthen community-based protection mechanisms to prevent and mitigate protection risks, and to address protection needs where feasible
- Contribute to a favorable protection environment through engagement of and support to authorities, humanitarian and peace building partners
Key interventions will include:
1- Undertaking service mapping on protection activities and technical capacity assessment of government departments in Blue Nile and White Nile. The study aims to identify gaps in the current services provision, technical and human resource capacities and recommendation to address these gaps.
2- Strengthening child protection protection services to support the most vulnerable and at risk people in need in BN and SK states through a static children’s center, that will provide specialized services i.e. case management for children with trauma that will provide specialized services i.e. case management for children with trauma survivors; structured and sustained PSS activities for children, adolescents including recreational activities and counselling (individual and group); as well as vocational and life skills courses for adolescent girls (including sewing/tailoring, handicrafts, knitting, IT, literacy, English courses, etc.).
3- Building on lesson learned, HA will mobilize communities and conduct community-based protection interventions by strengthening the existing and establishment of three child protection patrol and network committees across various neighborhoods of both states in target localities. These committees will be linked to available CP services and coordinate on awareness raising and referrals.
4- To reach the most vulnerable children in the wider Kurmuk, Baw, Al Leri, Talawdi localities district and increase their access to services, specifically for those unable to travel to capital state in BN and SK, HA will strengthen its delivery through a mobile approach. 4 Mobile Teams will undertake outreach services in the neighboring communities to provide awareness raising, identification and referrals. They will also utilize a mobile Ideas Box to deliver PSS, educational and recreational activities to children and adolescents. The mobile approach would include also case management for women and children, awareness-raising sessions on CP response.
5- Through both static and mobile approach HA’s legal protection team will continue providing legal assistance, representation and legal counselling to vulnerable children (girls and boys), their families/guardians survivors to ensure self-reliance, supporting access to birth registration or/and any missing civil documentation that may hinder children from being able to exercise their rights.
6- Capacity building, coaching and mentoring of the government and non-governmental organizations (NGOs) on child rights and supporting conflict-affected children in BN with legal documentation and/or birth registrations etc.

1 - Needs

Four per cent of children aged 1-14 years experienced various forms of violent discipline. And children, particularly boys, are actively participating in armed conflict. The ratio children in detention reached 238/100,000 of the whole Sudan’s child population. More than 32,407 children across all 18 states (19,224 boys, 13,183 girls) were in contact with the law as victims, witnesses and alleged offenders. While diversion outside the judicial system increased, children in contact with the law still face lengthy judicial procedures and can be placed in pre-trial detention even for petty offences.Sudan is also a country of origin, transit and destination for children, with more than 2.2 million internally displaced people and refugees from neighboring countries, and war-affected countries. Children are on the move more and more in Sudan due to conflict, economic pressures, trafficking and migration. Children on the move are frequently unaccompanied or separated from their families and are exposed to violence, abuse and exploitation.There is various type of harmful traditional practices in Sudan that poses risks on children physical and psychological health and wellbeing. 6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Due to limited assistance in targeted areas, populations are forced to engage in risky behaviors, including walking long distances to access services, exposing them to theft, assault or forced recruitment perpetrated by armed soldiers. Lack of access to services disproportionately affects persons with disabilities, female headed households, PLW and child headed households. Strengthening community protection mechanisms and improving access to services through information dissemination on available services, identification of the most vulnerable and referrals will be prioritized. Protection issues will be mainstreamed and considered during planning and implementation of the protection intervention to ensure that beneficiaries are protected and that services are easily accessible.
