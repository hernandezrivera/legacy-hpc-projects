Project Summary

The Joint Humanitarian Complaint Feedback Mechanism (JHCFM) aims to enhance accountability and responsiveness in humanitarian operations. Building on the successes of the pilot phase, this second phase will focus on scaling up the initiative to reach more beneficiaries, improve feedback processes, and strengthen partnerships among stakeholders.
The initiative will ensure that affected populations, especially people with disabilities (OPDs), women, girls, boys, and men affected by the three complex crises, make their voices heard and receive timely and confidential responses.
The system ensures centralized data management, where feedback and complaints are collected through various channels (e.g., help desks, hotlines, suggestion boxes) and managed in a centralized database. This allows for comprehensive data analysis and reporting.
It also ensures joint coordination and quality assurance, with participating organizations jointly overseeing the effectiveness of the feedback mechanism to ensure feedback is addressed promptly and appropriately. Additionally, it improves accountability by enhancing the mechanism's responsiveness to affected populations, ensuring their feedback is considered in programmatic and strategic decision-making. The system will promote efficiency and cost savings by combining resources and efforts, reducing duplication of efforts and associated costs, such as staffing and training.
Overall, the JHCFM aims to make humanitarian assistance more responsive and adaptive to the needs and concerns of the communities it serves.
