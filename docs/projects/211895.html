Project Summary

The project is designed towards Child Friendly School with easy and equitable education for children in age groups of 3-17. As informed by HNO, Aleppo has 2.7 Million (38%) PIN out of the total 7.2 Million PIN.
A recent rapid needs assessment conducted on 285 schools, by Big Heart during February 2024, in Aleppo Governorate covering Afrin, Jenderis, Bulbul, Mabatli, Raju, Sharan and Sheikh Hadid. The selected schools reported a total of 98,496 Students ( 48,741-49% Males, 49,755-51% Females, 2,349-2% Orphans, 747-1% Disabled). The total number of teachers reported was 1,497 (66 Students per teacher average). The total classroom reported was 1,774 (56 Students per Classroom).
The schools cumulatively reported need for an additional 97 classroom. In the sample of 285 schools, 190 (67%) schools reported to have playground, out of which 181 (95%) required repairs/renovations. 137 (48%) schools reported having water, through public water/Water Trucking/Wells. Only 104 (36%) schools have electricity through Public Grids (41%), Generator (3%) and Solar (56%). 155 (54%) schools reported requirement for renovations. 225 (79%) schools reported having toilets but requires repairs/renovations.
There is an acute need to provide required necessary infrastructure in terms of water, electricity, classrooms and playgrounds to make the schools sustainable for early grade and secondary education. All orphan students (100%) and most teachers (72%) reported not having adequate resources to have students / teacher kits. Outside the orphan students, only 47% of the remaining students reported having adequate student kit.

UNESCO Education Standards sets students per classrooms at 40 (16.5 sq. ft) per student. The samples selected were 40% above the recommended standards. Similarly the student to teacher ratio should be 30:1. In the selected samples, the student to teacher ratio is 66:1, more than double the international standards.
The main issues that requires immediate attention are additional classrooms, toilets, school renovations, electricity, Student kits, teacher kits and recreational kits. In addition mental health of students can vastly improve, if provided with adequate playground facility to promote healthy activities. In the sample selected only 67% of the schools reported having playgrounds, with majority 95% reporting playgrounds needing repairs/renovations.
Our design will be based on three prong approach, 1. Addressing the needs of students (student kits), 2. Addressing the needs of teachers (teacher kits) and 3. Addressing the need of schools (school renovations, recreational kits, toilets, water, electricity).
Our approach will be based on total inclusivity, with schools empowered to make their own decisions on needs, vetted by Big Heart team. In this regard, we plan to award performance grants to schools, based on progressive payments. The school will have a choice to prioritize their needs and execute in collaboration with communities they serve. Big Heart will attempt to revive community boards in school for improved ownership and sustainability. Likewise, students and teachers will receive student and teacher vouchers, which can be redeemed at selected shops carrying school materials. Big Heart will sign MoUs with the shopkeepers to ensure that fair and stable prices are charged throughout the school year. Based on our previous experience, this approach is most suitable, as giving out kits in one go results in students and teachers losing kit material and are back to square one. With this approach, they will acquire only things that are needed and in progression, which will have better impact. Big Heart plans to implement the program in 42 (15%) schools that were included in the survey. The student and teacher vouchers will be provided on vulnerability criteria and only those students and teachers will be provided vouchers that are in greatest need, with first preference to orphans.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The program design is based on empowerment for schools, students and teachers. We have suggested a Grant/Voucher modality. Schools will be provided varied grants as per assessed needs. The selection of schools will be based on the severity of needs at school, the catchment area it serves and number of students. The student voucher support will be provided according to vulnerability criteria. In order of preference, the orphans and disabled students will be prioritized. The second applied criteria will be households headed by females or PWDs. The third applied criteria will be households having earnings of less than USD 18 per household member per month. All three criterions will be applied to the households in order to be eligible for assistance. Under the design during 2024, 15% (42) schools, 10% (10,000) Students, and 17% (250) teachers will be provided voucher assistance. The program will also support stipends to 17% (250) teachers for the entire remaining school year. In addition stipends will be provided to 150 other school staff. The grant to the school will be an average of USD 20,000 per school (one time). The student voucher value will be USD 75, whereas the teacher voucher value will be USD 50. The vouchers can be redeemed in tranches. Further the program will support the selected 42 schools with recreational and library kits to inculcate heathy physical and reading habits. The program is designed to address students in the age range of 5-17. The program will support formal education grade 1-10, with awareness campaigns to bring at least 10,000 out-of-school children back into the folds of formal education. PSS support will be provided to all students in all selected schools. Special emphasis will be PSEA violations. Big Heart has a well defined complaint redressal mechanism in place to report and refer PSEA violations. Where possible, the program will encourage teachers to hold informal classes for out of school children after the school hours. The student and teacher voucher incentive will only be used for school and teacher supplies. No cash will be provided to students or teachers for the kits. Big Heart will deploy its tested and tried redemption approach and MoUs with the shopkeepers will be executed locking the price and availability of items throughout the school year. Our education experts will impart limited pedagogical training to the teachers. Early Grade Reading will be tested and reported as an impact of library initiative. Big Heart will deploy its own Voucher Management System through custom designed CodeReader system. The distribution of vouchers will be at the school, with proper distribution and post distribution monitoring. All beneficiary verification will be done at the household level with 100% verification approach. The program will be designed as an all inclusive engagement of all stakeholders, to include governmental departments, AFAD, community and local councils.
