1 - Needs

Since the process of the negotiation to signing of peace agreement in conflicting areas in Sudan, the Darfur villages which witnessed displaced people have been receiving a return from IDPs camps to the areas of origin specially farmers due to the slight stability in the security situation. The increasing number of the return particularly in the past two years has added value to the situation for the serious level of food security East, South and Central Darfur states despite the state still suffering from lack of resources to respond effectively for growing protection and education needs to children. However, and in such a situation, children and adolescents are often at highest risk for being directly affected by negative coping mechanisms with few protective strategies available to them with the closer of schools resulting from the spread of COVID 19; and increased exposure to violence expected with high drop out of schools which will effect on their development and well-being . Although there are no clear figures due to the lack of education services, it is well known that during the return, many children went through traumatizing experiences and were separated from their families. As children’s well-being and development depends very much on the security of family relationships and a predictable environment, many children lose touch with their families and have no idea of fathers and mothers’ whereabouts. Given the fact, the vast majority of the children in East Darfur State are now living in very unsafe and difficult conditions affecting their abilities to develop on physical, cognitive, emotional and social aspects, therefore most of them joint the market labor to change their conditions , due to the schools closers resulting from COVID 19, children have lost the interest of schooling . During REMCO assessment in the three targeted states , most participants reported that while their children previously had support, the lack of classroom, education kits and the school cluster for long time deteriorate the social acceptance to children to join schools, where more girls forced to get married, more boys joined Rapid Army Force. Although some aid originations have succeeded to the provision of basic needs. REMCO staff in the three suggested areas highlighted the need to severe humanitarian situations and need for an immediate scale up response to the critical situations of children particularly in South, Central and East Darfur. Education partners are concerned that this situation will exacerbate the vulnerability of children to danger level. The proposed project will respond to the growing humanitarian crisis of returning children to provide Education assistance and services to most at risk boys, girls and adolescents. The project will increase the resilience of children through playing, learning and exploring their skills of coping, and will stood as voice of the most at-risk children and PSNs to establish the bases of future leaders, it will also encourage working together the create the positive sense of brotherhood, the activities will be based on human rights approach therefore we are expecting at the end of this project return communities will be able to change negative behaviors and believes and consider human rights based approach in their daily culture practices.
