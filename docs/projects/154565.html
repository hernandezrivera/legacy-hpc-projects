Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to strengthen situational awareness and enhance humanitarian actors’ knowledge on access, risks, and vulnerabilities of populations affected by the ISIS operations and promote principled, coordinated and standardized approaches to information management across clusters and sectors for diverse recipient groups, including maintaining common data and information repositories.
The project also aims to rapidly assess and analyze the level of threat/contamination and the security situation to set up safety, security and risk-free management systems in new contexts and provide rapid onset emergency response information to Humanitarian Actors (HA) involved in the humanitarian responses. In addition, the project aims to provide information ﬂow channels to the IDPs, returnees and host communities about the explosive hazard threats, in order to enhance their security and provide a safer environment.

Needs

The needs are primarily related to disproportional dedicated resources to this ﬁeld of work and the perception of it not being the ﬁrst line or even the second line response. Moreover, hard-to-reach areas and areas which were under the control of ISIS are not yet completely assessed, using iMMAP’s analytical security incidents database interpolated with satellite images will promptly facilitate to establish a current common operating picture of the scope and scale of contamination/threat and allow for effective threat mitigation systems to be established and applied. Such threat mitigation systems should be the base-line and corner-stone of the humanitarian operation given the experience so far with liberated areas. The RMIM IM will collect, analyze, customize and report on security incidents that pose the major threat to the Humanitarian actors HAs and including also IM for Mine Action and desktop survey data which will facilitate security coordination, safe access and coordination of operations. iMMAP will be providing regular detailed IM situational maps, online real-time dashboard, snapshots, infographics and situation reports on routes frequently used by HAs, routes occupied by returnees and IDPs, areas around the camps and areas of humanitarian response highlighting accessibility, security threats and IED threats customized to speed up the access of humanitarian organizations to hard to reach areas including areas retaken from armed actors and newly accessible areas, provide risk information to IDPs, returnees and host communities about the explosive hazards that pose threats and challenges on their way. iMMAP will also be providing capacity building sessions to the national humanitarian aid workers on humanitarian principles, normative frameworks, and/or assessments tools and methodology and specific capacity building sessions using its eclectic field experience on awareness raising sessions related to humanitarian access, safety, and security.

Activities

The RMIM center will collect, analyze, customize and report security-related and explosive hazard threats, which will facilitate security coordination, safe access and coordination of operations. iMMAP will be providing regular detailed IM situational maps, online real-time dashboard, snapshots, infographics, and situation reports on routes frequently used by HAs or occupied by returnees and IDPs, areas around the camps and areas of humanitarian response. The objective will be to improve the accessibility of humanitarian organizations to hard to reach areas including areas retaken from armed actors and newly accessible areas; in addition, the project will provide risk information to IDPs, returnees and host communities about explosive hazards threatening their movement. Special attention will be also given to people deemed most vulnerable including girls and boys, women and elderly persons, through advocating for activities to be carried out immediately in their areas to protect the safety of their movements. 1 iMMAP through its RMIMC aims to enhance understanding of protection concerns and operational constraints. To achieve this goal, the RMIMC will do the following activities: Development and dissemination of dashboards, bulletins, infographics, snapshots, weekly and monthly situation reports to HAs, UN agencies, I/NGOs, local authorities and other concerned actors. Deliver emergency safety information. Disseminate IM products highlighting security risk and humanitarian access on regular and ad hoc basis. Develop more online IM resources to provide emergency safety information such as online dashboards and snapshots. Integrate IM information with desktop surveys (Including but not limited to Satellite Imagery, IDP Matrix, and IDP camps) to present composite analytical IM products. Integrate/layer datasets for clusters and other I/NGO speciﬁc needs. 2 The second objective of the RMIMC is to increase awareness about Explosive Hazards threats in the vicinity. To achieve this objective RMIMC will do the following activities: Conduct capacity building sessions to raise awareness of HAs about humanitarian access, safety and security and will conduct the Hazards/Contamination assessments of priority locations and routes frequently used by humanitarian partners, IDPs, returnees and host communities to raise their awareness about the security threats posed their ways. Update explosive hazards database on daily basis using open sources and disseminate the outcomes when necessary. Conduct risk/hazard mapping exercises and disseminate the piloted IM maps immediately to humanitarian partners responding to the emergencies.Train humanitarian actors on mobile data collection MDC, data clean-up process and visualization apps to promote their capacity and collect explosive hazards/threat/contamination information then share the outcomes immediately with the concerned HAs. Conduct damage assessments in hard to reach and inaccessible areas using satellite images interpolated with desktop survey data and produce composite analytical reports then share them with HAs for their prompt actions. Update contamination maps and disseminate to coordination entities. Through mapping of existing data identify areas which require further contamination assessments. 3 The third goal iMMAP RMIMC strives to achieve is to increase the eﬃciency of protection concern assessments of populations affected by the security situations, to achieve this goal, the following activity will be carried out: Identify the need for risk education (RE)with partners and local populations in accordance with ﬁnding of explosive/hazards contamination analysis through inclusive IM maps showing the potential areas for RE and conducting workshops for the specialized RE (risk education) organizations on the MDC ﬁndings in order to raise the awareness and prevent harm to IDPs, returnees and humanitarian partners from all types of explosive hazards threats. 4 The fourth objective is to increase capacity building of humanitarian partners both National and International actors responding to the crisis through:-Carrying out assessments tools and methodology or multi-cluster needs assessments. Developing mobile data collection solutions for partners in the CCS cluster and other cluster partners in order to facilitate household level protection monitoring. -Providing training to humanitarian partners on IM data collection mechanisms and assessment tools. -Providing training on data quality processing services (cleaning, validation, integration) to humanitarian partners. - Providing training on data visualization.

Protection Risk Analysis

The project aims to enhance protection for IDPs and returnees, through providing to the humanitarian partners responding to the ongoing crisis, comprehensive and visualized information to improve their programming and risk mitigation measures. The project will not potentially create protection risks although the project will target protection risks that are mainly determined by the exposure to residual explosive hazards, ERW, and daily security incidents. The risks are therefore physical other than psychological and psychosocial. These types of protection risks are different on the basis of age and gender but could affect the whole population indiscriminately. Total beneﬁciaries: 500.000 girls, boys, women, and men among which humanitarian actors involved in the humanitarian response including the government authorities, UN agencies, local NGOs, INGOs, donors and other actors engaged in the response; IDPs, hosting communities and refugees living in the target areas. Female: 175,000 Male: 325,000 The project is not gender or age focused but targets all ages and genders equally. Speciﬁc attention is given to the most vulnerable categories of boys and girls, women and elderly persons as detailed in the activities description.

Mitigation Measures

The project aims to target the above-described risks by producing online IM data including regular detailed IM situational maps, onlinereal-time dashboard, snapshots, infographics, and situation reports on routes frequently used by HAs, routes occupied by returnees and IDPs, areas around the camps and areas of humanitarian response highlighting accessibility, security threats, and IED threats,customized to speed up the access of humanitarian organizations to hard to reach areas including areas retaken from armed actors and newly accessible areas. The project will also provide riskinformation to IDPs, returnees and host communities about the explosive hazards that pose threat to their movement. The IM products will be shared with the humanitarian community using various platforms to ensure that the number of beneficiaries reached is optimized.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Through this project, the RMIM center will work to utilize its broad-based and accredited information management data on security incidents about the existence of explosive hazard threats, contamination areas, ERW, and affected infrastructure assets. The evidence-based data will then be processed at the RMIM center to produce analytical reports, and then will be shared with the concerned HAs. The project guarantees accountability to beneﬁciaries through ensuring a transparent explanation to affected populations about the objectives of the security incidents that pose threat on their routes frequently used and overall incidents happen in their areas.
