Project Summary

Gender-based violence (GBV) and other forms of violence have been proven to be at an increase during conflict situations in many communities around the globe. The situation is not different with the current Anglophone crisis and the sudden outbreak of the COVID19 pandemic in and beyond the Southwest Region Cameroon. This is highly caused by the government restrictive measures such as restricted movements and social isolation measure as many women, girls and children have now become more exposed to high incidences of violence. Most women, girls and children are at home with their abusers and perpetrators and as such are limited from daily support services.
Our intervention shall focus on building a community level agenda to mitigate incidences of GBV cases during this COVID 19 pandemic in our targeted communities, through sensitization and awareness raising with focus on building referral pathways and linking service providers to respond to GBV cases within these communities. To respond to remote case management, we shall work with our community focal points to also provide psychosocial support and other relevant services to survivors and those at risk. Our means of intervention shall be through phone calls for local communities and in case there is a complete shortdown.
