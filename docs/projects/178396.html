Project Summary

The project aims to enhance the logistics capacities of the humanitarian community to improve the access of beneficiaries to the assistance. It also aims at reinforcing the coordination of logistics partners through information sharing concerning logistics risks and mitigation across Burundi, mainly in case of border closure due to pandemics with various movements restrictions impacting the supply chain.
Another objective of this project is to reinforce storage capacity across Burundi. It was one of the core needs expressed by the members of the Logistics Sector, indicating that a shared logistics hub would be useful, especially to pre-position closer to implementation localities.
Setting a forward logistics hub closer to strategic locations would overall mitigate constraints due to the rainy season and potential restriction of movements leading to supply chain disruptions.
