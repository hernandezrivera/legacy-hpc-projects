Project Summary

WARDI will provide an integrated package of basic life-saving nutrition services to highly vulnerable IDP, host- community, and hard-to-reach populations in Dharkeynley, HamarJajab,Kaxda,Beletweyne,Mataban, Bulo burte, Jalalaqsi and Wanlaweyn designed to enhance access to prevention,promotion and management of acute malnutrition in children between 6-59 months and pregnant and lactating women. Services will be be provided through eleven(11) fixed sites and eight (8) mobile outreaches to expand coverage and enhance equity. In total, the project will target a catchment population of 273276 persons among them 54655 children between 6-59 months(27874 girls and 26781 boys) and 24595 pregnant and lactating women. The project will treat 7724 children 6-59 months for Severe Acute Malnutrition.other than treatment the project will undertake intensive community engagement and sensitization to initiate behaviour change. Services will be integrated with the existing primary healthcare and MAM treatment supported by WFP.

Exclusion and marginalization:The following measures will be undertaken to avert the risk of exclusion;a)undertake an initial assessment to identify the existence of marginalised and minority groups with aim of understanding their current situation and the various challenges they face in accessing services b)In prioritising locations for projects such as villages to be visited by Mobile units,ensure villages predominantly inhabited by these groups are given consideration and that the services meet their need and contribute to the reduction of such discrimination. c)Enhance community participation in the project making sure the various groups including these groups are well represented in consultation meetings and their views are well taken care of.d)Ensure services are provided on a need basis and everyone in need of healthcare can access regardless of their background. e)Provide relevant messages that address prejudices against these groups and disseminated such messages in multiple forums including; through CHWs, in community meetings and at health facilities by health workers. e) Continuously monitor factors limiting access for the group and review inclusion strategies applied by the project.2b The main focus of activities is on women of reproductive age and children U5 Dignity and privacy will be promoted; health facilities are accessible to persons with disabilities. In consultation with the various actors, WARDI continues to engage men as major decision makers in Somali culture. In rural areas where women are often responsible for household chores and parenting, family finances are largely controlled by the male gender indicating decision-making power. Here, women are also tending the family’s livestock. This activity keeps them away and makes self-care and care for children very difficult. Consequently, women and children are at a greater risk for disease and malnutrition. To ensure privacy and confidentiality at the supported health facilities, work has been done on partitioning for privacy during patient screening and examination and also confidentiality in handling and storage of the patient’s information records. WARDI’s approach is influenced by cultural observations, such as barriers for acceptation of family planning activities that are rooted in customs and traditions. Staffing in HF balances male and female clinicians. In the network of Care Group Volunteers, the majority are women, selected from within and by the communities where they live and work. They are trusted by their community members and can broach sensitive topics of conversation, such as family planning and other health topics as cultural insiders. They are also able to reach influential mothers-in-law, who impact on the decisions of fathers about health care seeking behavior and the use of family finances for health. Care Group Volunteers also help identify households of persons with disabilities and special needs through household visits. Volunteers are trained to include these in health, nutrition, and hygiene promotion and to assess for any specific health needs
