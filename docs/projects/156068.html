Project Summary

since the beginning of the conflict in 2011 in Syria and the length of the conflict , there were increasing in the number of wounded , injured and disabled , especially victims of explosions of mines and missiles and explosives, Permanent and serious disabilities.
where The greater the number of people with disabilities, the greater the need for medical tools and the needs of rehabilitation centers and their support in providing assistance.
Due to this urgent need in the region especially after the increasing waves of IDPs from Damascus and Homs countryside to the area and the absence of a specialized medical treatment centers serve the large segment of patients. , INSAN CHARITY started two projects in maaret Annuman and saraqeb . each center provides physiotherapy services (more than 1000 sessions monthly) .
The centers have a group of specialists work under the supervision of a professional PT to follow up the patients and provide physiotherapy treatment.
The staff in each center consists of :
1 physiotherapist who is supervisor , 3 rehab workers in men section , 3 rehab workers in women section.
The center has a special section for women and another for men, in addition to a warehouse containing medical supplies required for physiotherapy - assisstive devices) which are distributed free of charge to patients. free transportation through ambulance available for cases referred from hospitals and other centers and for disabled who couldn't come to the center .
catchment population : 190,000

Needs

Hundreds of thousands of people have been killed over the course of the conflict,with some estimates suggestions that 470,000 people including 55,000 children , have been killed (Human Rights Watch ,2017 ). It is estimated that over 1 million people have been injured as a direct consequence of the war , with approximately 30,000 people suffering conflict - related trauma injuries each month (OCHA , 2016) . According to assessment in june 2018 initally there are 33 percent of people in Aleppo and Idlib are disabled or in need of physical therapy , which lead us to have clear idea about the available huge needs in Syria right now . however we have more than 3 millions people with disability are in need of rehab services in the north of syria. the health system struggles to respond to trauma and rehabilitation needs. High numbers of trauma-related cases continue to be reported across Syria, particularly in north-western and north-eastern Syria. An estimated 10.2 million Syrians are at risk of explosive hazards, further increasing vulnerability of people to traumatic injury. Further, an assessment among adults in western Aleppo, Idleb and Ar-Raqqa governorates indicated that an average of 45 per cent of people surveyed had sustained hostility-related injuries are expected to sustain permanent physical impairment. In addition, in 2019, an escalation of hostilities is anticipated in a number of areas in north-eastern and north-western Syria, necessitating further support to trauma centers and referral systems. At least 15 per cent of the population of Syria is disabled . Forty-five per cent of people injured by the conflict in Syria are likely to face a permanent impairment and require specialist support which will far outlast the hostilities .

Protection Risk Analysis

risks 1- risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (separated sections for men and women and children . female staff for female section -train all health facility staff on issues of gender, GBV, women's /human rights, PSEA and PFA .2-risk of travelling to the center related to culture issues or other reasons to receive treatment .(provision of home based programme -outreach rehab mobile unit travel via mobile van to the camps/ communities patient settlement etc , to provide rehab services .3- difficulties to access to center (center is physically accessible with ramps ,handrails , adapted toilets and medical equipment such as stretchers .

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- A lack of qualified healthcare workforce, ( provide regular training to increase staff capacity)- lack of adequate or timely health care ( effective compliance mechanism )- The absence of, or deterioration of, assistive devices such as mobility aids or specific items , which can make a disability more severe and create a barrier to accessing services (coordination with donors and partners for offering assistive devices).-The lack of information about available basic and specialized care in physical rehabilitation. ( cooperation with local and national authorities to inform the host community and IDPS of the services provided and its locations )- The high psychological distress preventing family members and caregivers of the injured and vulnerable to cope with the situation.( provision of psycho - social support and referral system.)
