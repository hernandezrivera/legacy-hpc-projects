Project Summary

*- described the program:
The program is aimed at young children of the first years (3-8) years, suffering from developmental delays and various disabilities, as well as their families. Due to the great importance of early intervention to teach the child new skills and overcome the problems he faces due to developmental delays, which increases his chances of academic success and success in life.
Program objectives:
1. Targeting 1,600 children inside four centers (- Tel al-samn camp-Sabkha-Aldbsay_Maadan)
2. Developing the skills of children with developmental delays, the most important target skills are as follows:
Communication skills such as speaking, understanding, using gestures.
O cognitive skills: including thinking and problem solving.
O learn social skills like playing and making new friends.
O adaptive or motor skills such as eating, brushing teeth and dressing.
O physical and sensory skills such as Vision, observation and hearing.
3. Develop the skills of families of children with developmental delays and enable them to obtain educational practices appropriate to the needs of their children.
4. Empower the facilitators working in the program through the educational strategies needed by children with developmental delays.
5. Providing a safe learning environment for children
6. Protect young children from academic and social failures.
7. Provide health care , including periodic medical examinations, necessary treatment and psychological support to help them cope with health challenges
Strategies:
1. Training programs for facilitators, facilitators and parents.
2. Visits to families of children with developmental delays.
3. Group and individual therapy sessions for children with developmental delays.
4. Social media platforms.
The project will be monitored and monitored by facilitators working for the office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs and a third partner, and reports will be submitted according to agreement with the partners, so that these reports will be periodic either daily, weekly, monthly or spring, as they include the number of beneficiaries and the extent of their progress through these programs so that these reports will be a database for future use.Al Ihsan will work to benefit women, men, girls and boys (and non-disadvantaged) from this program and activities that have been adapted based on different needs, interests and the level of vulnerability among them.
The association will draw up an action plan that does not cause any harm to the safety, dignity and integrity of women, men, girls and boys receiving the program's activities and is presented in ways that respect their rights and do not reinforce stereotypes of gender injustice, emphasizes the topic of sexual exploitation and zero tolerance on this topic and puts notes on the existence of a hotline.
To receive opinions and complaints and allow beneficiaries to ask questions through a direct interview with the beneficiary or through the complaint box that opens every Thursday with a note next to the box the box will be opened every Thursday or through the hotline that will be circulated throughout the Centers
