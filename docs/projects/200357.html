Project Summary

The project will ensure a full dedicated coordinator at interagency level will support the RH/HC and the Humanitarian Country team and UNCT on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. The position will be administrated by UNFPA but serve at interagency level to ensure collective accountability to affected population with protection from sexual misconduct. The project will also fund part of the activities of the 2023 action plan of the PSEA network to ensure advancement of protective environment. Agencies as have been allocating budget to support other actions (like assessment and training) in the past year but a complementary 15.000 will ensure we can scale up training and print the new IEC material to be distributed in particular to CBOs that might not have budget for it

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

A robust inter-agency approach to prevention and response to SEA should be sustainable with buy-in, engagement, and contribution of resources and expertise of all involved. Continued leadership by the RC/HC is essential to ensure PSEA is mainstreamed across LCRP, 3RFs, and ERP sectors, inter-sectors, and within organizations, including sector-lead organizations, and is not as a standalone agenda. Inter-Agency PSEA Coordinator will lead the in country efforts on PSEA and report network Workplan progress quarterly to the UNCT and HCT to ensure continued engagement. This project will contribute to the sector objective by 5 key actions in line with the 5 years PSEA strategy adopted by HCT/UNCT. The project will 12 months of funding for the Inter-agency PSEA Coordinator position and a limited package of capacity development activities and production of IEC materials to be delivered through the PSEA Network with a focus on local NGOs.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Victim right assistant is crucial on PSEA. The PSEA network works closely to the GBV working group to ensure services are provided in a compassionate matters to survivors.GBV and PSEA mainstreaming interventions are carried out together when it comes to minimize risks of distribution across other sectors with agreed interventions like separated line by sex

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The overall objective of the project is to improve accountability to affected populations by:- distributing PSEA flyers on information on how to report sexual misconduct by humanitarian workers. Noting that flyers have been designed in consultation with communities- improving capacity on humanitarian workers to act as PSEA focal points- improving the PSEA network coordination- supporting investigation for CBOs and NGOs
