Project Summary

- Provide vocational training to equip the targeted population with skills and experience needed to bring back some type of balance to the individuals who are filled with despair and hopelessness.
- Create new job and/or income generating opportunities in a town and the areas besides ,that has suffered tremendously over the seven years of the crisis.
- Stimulate financial independency among the targeted population and encourage them to become active in the process.
- Reconstruct 180 houses which damaged between 35% to 65% , with priority given to the houses of families most vulnerable as per the criteria set forth and agreed on with the partner.
-The project Target to train and graduate 1400 trainees with (females and males ) to help them use the fundamentals qualifications and skills to rehabilitate their houses ,besides to help them find their own way to work and increase the livelihood recovery circumstances.
- Encourage the residents to abandon negative coping mechanisms they have used to adapt to the crisis.
- Part of the plan is ensuring the sustainability of the project to target more houses in need of rehabilitation even after the project ends. This will be conducted by adapting a marketing strategy in the area and its surroundings to sell the manufactured blocks with competitive prices. The profit will be used to fund the rehabilitation of other damaged houses in the area, and to cover the wages for the workers in the workshop. Other families will also benefit from the competitive prices which will help them rehabilitate their own homes.
- Mobadrah Ahl al-Sham will be responsible on following up with the sustainability of the Blocks workshop, and will provide any addition guidance and monitoring as needed.
- As a result, some negative social traits have emerged including restraining from education and the resorting to undesirable psychological practices with one another.

Needs

- The total number of local residents is nearly 300.000, in addition to 8000 displaced families coming from various areas, with an average size of 5 individuals for each family. - 85% of the families suffer from poverty and the lack of job opportunities which are direct result of the damage inflicted on the shops, factories, and other entities in the area. - The destruction in the areas is major with a 35– 80 % damage to the infrastructure and main facilities. Most families have lost their businesses and therefore their income and their ability to purchase anything but their basic needs of food and other items from street vendors. They have also been relying on in-kind assistance given from charities and local NGOs or other UN agencies.-The Data has been collected by several sources, such as international organizations, local government and civil society organizations, and the visits of the Mobadrah Ahl al- sham Needs Monitoring .

Implementation of activities

- Divide the trainees to work in 5 workshops (Cementing Walls, Construction, Electricity and Sewage, Building Blocks, and Wooden Column Shuttering).-Graduate 1400 trainees with tool kit ,in order to help them in livelihood. - Establish a Vocational Training School/Center to train those unemployed and of special needs. - Equip the workshops with all necessary tools for the training. - Advocate for the workshops in the town and reach out to as many individuals in the town as possible. - Contract with professional trainers and/or experienced volunteers; preferably from the town to ensure easy access and facilitation and to avoid any delays that might occur; before starting the training. - Interview candidates by a committee established jointly with Mobadrah Ahl al -Sham. - The committee will ensure the eligibility of the trainees and the qualifications for the trainers. Males and females trainees participating in the project will be chosen based on a vulnerability criteria agreed upon with OCHA and ,Mobadrah Ahl al -Sham. Priorities will be given to women headed of household, persons with special needs who are able to endure such training, and men from both the displaced families and the local residents as well. - Trainers will be chosen based on both their experience and vulnerability, too. - Work with the procurement team at Mobadrah Ahl al -Sham to conduct all procurement in adherence to the PQP. - Arrange schedules for the workshops to accommodate the trainees and the trainers, and to ensure the workshops are progressing according to the schedule.- Make sure the trainers and the trainees follow safety procedures and take precautions to ensure the safety of all participants. - Establish a time line for the project .- Share periodic reports reflecting the progress of the project, and list any challenges that might cause delays in the implementation. - Administer a type of test at the end of each workshop to make sure the trainees are eligible to graduate and are ready to pursue such work. This step is taken in line with the sensitivity of the nature of these workshops considering the safety measures of this type of construction work for both the workers, and the families residing in the houses to be rehabilitated and /or constructed. - All graduates will be tested before given the tool kits, which will be distributed among them according to their performance, attendance, and commitment during the workshops. -The trainees will attends a special courses (inductions to workplace health and safety ).
