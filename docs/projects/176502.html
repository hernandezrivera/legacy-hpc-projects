1 - Needs

According to South Sudan HNO 2021, estimated 5.9 million people, out of which 310,000 are refugees and asylum seekers in South Sudan, will have inadequate or no access to clean water and improved sanitation and hygiene practices in 2021. This is expected to negatively impact their living standards and wellbeing. The limitations in access to WASH services render all segments of the population including IDPs, host community members, returnees from internal displacement and refugee returnees. In terms of access to water, in 2020, only 36% of households reported having access to an improved water source in under 30 minutes without facing any protection concerns. The lack of access to water is critical in the current COVID-19 context. Access to sanitation is also reportedly very low with 77% of the households having no access to latrines. IDPs in (former) PoC sites remain particularly vulnerable as they do not have sufficient access to hygiene and sanitation facilities and are at risk of disease outbreaks in the congested conditions. WASH needs are also high among IDPs and returnees in non-camp settings and among their already stretched host communities. While host communities are in need of WASH items and facilities, increasing needs are expected with recently returned refugees and IDP groups, or those settling in new locations. Under its interventions conducted in 2020, ACTED found critical needs pertaining to WASH. Severe flooding witnessed across South Sudan since July 2020 has significantly exacerbated WASH needs with an estimated 1,042,000 people affected; 450,000 within Jonglei State alone. Flooding has caused extensive damage to existing water infrastructure and facilities; with latrines and boreholes destroyed and/or rendered non-functional, compounding vulnerabilities as households are left without access to the most basic services. Therefore, ACTED will continue implementing WASH interventions in 2021 in line with the following cluster objectives:CO2: Health and WASH - Reducing prevalence of WASH-related diseases and risk of outbreak by transmission contexts upon epidemiological investigationCO3: Access to basic services in emergenciesCO6: Improve access to safe water and promote safe hygiene practices in communities showing high prevalence of GAM and high WASH vulnerabilities

2 - Project activities

ACTED will implement the following activities to increase vulnerable populations’ access to WASH services:- Rehabilitation of non-functional boreholes and solar upgrading- Rehabilitation/repair of handpumps- Rehabilitation of water points (quick impact projects)- Emergency latrine construction and decommissioning- Distribution of core WASH items- Construction of haffirs for water storage- Hygiene promotion sessions/campaigns including COVID-19 awareness- Formation and training of COVID-19 committees- Conducting safety audit and risk mitigation assessments to identify the key WASH resources, facilities, needs and service provision gaps- Installation of handwashing points- Establishing COVID-19 tracking system and service monitoring mechanism

3 - Project indicators

ACTED will utilize the following WASH cluster indicators to monitor the progress of the project:- Number of beneficiaries at risk of WASH-related diseases reached with safe access to semi- or permanent water- Number of beneficiaries at risk of WASH-related diseases reached with hygiene promotion- Number of beneficiaries reached with safe access to temporary or semi-permanent water- Number of beneficiaries reached with safe access to temporary or semi-permanent sanitation- Number of beneficiaries reached with hygiene promotion- Number of beneficiaries in communities showing high prevalence of GAM and high WASH vulnerabilities reached with safe access to (semi-)permanent water- Number of beneficiaries in communities showing high prevalence of GAM and high WASH vulnerabilities reached with hygiene promotion

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

During sensitization of beneficiaries on CRM mechanism, ACTED informs beneficiaries about PSEA and provides clear instructions on how to report such cases utilizing CRM present at the site of implementation. ACTED explains how such sensitive cases are handled to encourage reporting.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

As per its standard operating procedures for CRM, throughout the project, ACTED will ensure that multiple channels of communication (e.g.: help desk, staff registering complaints, hotline etc,) are available for ACTED to receive feedback from communities including the most vulnerable members such as individuals living with disabilities and individuals who are not literate. Furthermore, ACTED will have at least one women staff member in help desks to encourage women to register their feedback to ACTED.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

During the implementation project activities, beneficiaries will be sensitized on the complaints and response mechanism in camps, and on how to submit concerns or complaints to ACTED. Moreover, help desks at project sites will be installed at central locations and equipped with visibility materials.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

ACTED’s independent AMEU will continuously register the complaints and feedback of the target population and insert them into ACTED South Sudan’s learning database. The findings will be discussed at least on a monthly basis with the project implementation and project development teams to ensure that the feedback is incorporated into the ongoing project as well as for the design of upcoming projects.

8.11. Mitigation Measures

ACTED counts on a robust internal mechanism to combat and prevent child safeguarding and PSEA. All ACTED staff are required to sign a Code of Conduct which includes ACTED’s Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy and Child Protection Policy. The staff will receive refresher trainings on these policies on a regular basis. ACTED will take the necessary measures to ensure that the communities receiving WASH items will have safe access to distribution sites by selecting an accessible location. Vulnerable and/or marginalized groups will be included in community consultations to ensure they are included in decision-making processes and receive the assistance provided by ACTED. ACTED will conduct inception meetings with external stakeholders including local authorities to ensure that the scope of the project and any selection criteria is widely understood.
