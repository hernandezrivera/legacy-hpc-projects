Project Summary

The project aims at providing critical lifesaving and life sustaining support and further increasing resilience of drought, flood and conflict affected IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable host communities in Bay, Bakool and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia by providing them with essential livelihood assistance, restoring and protecting their livelihood conditions and increasing their income sources. The project targeted beneficiaries are worst affected by a combination of climate, socio-economic and health related shocks attributed to very serious Gu 2020 flooding, impacts of locust invasion that affected crop production and insecurity and related trade disruptions. Their plight is further exacerbated by impacts of Covid-19 containment measures that significantly eroded their purchasing powers and further weakened their fragile food insecurity situation (July/August 2020 post-Gu assessment). The project targets 450 farming and pastoral families/27000 persons (women 11232, men10368, boys 2700 and girls 2700) with the following activities: Provision of food vouchers for most vulnerable and displaced women and children headed households and those with chronic illnesses and the disabled, rehabilitation of water catchments, farming lands/rangelands, canals and feeder roads on CFW scheme, Provision of hay/fodder to the most vulnerable and drought and flood affected, pastoral families, and distribution of farm inputs (seed package) to farming households in the project targeted locations in Bay, Bakool and Lower Shabelle Regions.

Project Activities

1. Rehabilitation of 8 water catchments and 6 feeder roads in the targeted locations in Bay and Bakool. and Lower Shabelle Regions on cash for work scheme to benefit 1500 vulnerable and displaced persons 2. Provision of food vouchers to 1500 most vulnerable and displaced women headed households and those with disability and health problems in the targeted villages in Bay, Bakool and Lower Shabelle Regions 3. Provision of unconditional and conditional cash transfers for the most vulnerable and displaced 1500 women headed households in Bay,Bakool and Lower Shabelle Regions 4. Provision of fodder procured locally for animals to 850 pastoral families in Bay, Bakool and Lower Shabelle Regions. 5. Training of 600 farmers and agro pastoralists on good agricultural practices, post harvest,integrated pest management (IPM), trans boundary pest and disease in Bay, Bakool and Lower Shabelle Regions
