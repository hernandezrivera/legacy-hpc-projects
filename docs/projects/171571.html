Project Summary


The operation in Somalia remains underfunded, which has hampered the delivery of vital services and the restoration of facilities which were destroyed by flooding and drought coupled with global pandemic of COVID-19 since March,2020. Major needs still persist with increases in displacement figures in lower Shabelle region there has been a build-up of pressure on existing resources. Heavy rainfalls affected more than 289,400people in August,2020. WASH infrastructures suffered from important damages in many part of Galgadud and Mudug region target districts. Most of these infrastructures have not been repaired yet. Because of the floods and the chronical lack of investment in WASH infrastructure, Somalia continued to suffer from AWD/Cholera in 2020 and risk factors for an outbreak persist in several hotspots Galgadud and Mudug region Unreliable access to a temporary water source and/or unprotected water is still an important feature of the WASH humanitarian landscape in Somalia. Nationwide, access to an improved water source remains below 40%, with large variations from one region to another. In drought affected regions, water scarcity and poor quality are leading causes of population displacement and conflict, particularly in south central regions, respectively 69 and 35% of household need more than 30 minutes to reach their main drinking water points. More than half of the population lack of access to adequate sanitation. The situation is particularly critical in Lower Shabelle (below 25% each) and in IDP settlements. Latrine safety index is worryingly high in all parts of Shabele region district in Somalia, resulting in all categories of users being at high risk of violence when using facilities. Hand washing with soap at critical moment is practiced by only a third of the population as access to soap and availability to a functional handwashing point is limited this couple with high needs of hand washing due to COVID-19 pandemic that have also put at risk to many population safety. This percentage drops to below 25% in Mudug and Galgadud region, despite that these two states were badly affected by drought and AWD/Cholera recently, DEH therefore intends to provide enhanced water, sanitation and hygiene promotion to these affected populations including disable people in the two region target district hence DEH will ensure enhanced access to appropriate WASH lifesaving and life-sustaining services while also endeavors in ensuring the community resilience and capacity is strengthen as well as improve consultation and inclusion of disable population in project design and implementation period

Project Activities

1.1Rehabilitation and upgrading of 10 strategic boreholes in 10 droughts, flood and AWD prone areas through replacement of electro-mechanical systems into installation of hybrid solar systems (PV+Generator), construction of elevated tanks, extension of distribution system, construction of new water kiosks hence reaching a total of 8000 vulnerable population in the target district 1.2 Construction and extension of 12 communal water points including water kiosks in IDPs in xaradheere,Hobyo,Dhusamareeb and Cadado and 18 rainwater harvesting systems with storage facilities at health/nutrition sites and schools raching 8000 men,women,boys,girls and people with disability 1.3 Rehabilitate 20 community water points including roof water catchments in schools, CFS and health/nutrition facilities in both rural and IDPs. 1.4 Create/reactivate and strengthen the capacity of 140 water point management committees and 40 community based hygiene promotion committees in target communities including IDPs, with a focus on gender parity and disable population 2.1 Construction of 150 HH emergency shared latrines(10% for PLWD) in IDPs, AWD/Cholera hotspot areas and drought affected villages with portable hand washing facilities.2.2 Rehabilitate/Desludge 100 flood affected latrines (10% for PLWD) in Dhusamareeb,Hobyo,Cadado and xaradheere district 2.3 Construction of 100 gender segregated latrine blocs with hand washing stations in health/nutrition facilities and public schools with attention to ensuring proper sanitary facilities for girls, in line with SPHERE standards2.3. Construction of 30 medical incinerators at health centers for proper treatment of medical waste3.1 Conduct community hygiene promotion sessions to promote good hygiene and sanitation practices in targeted communities, health/nutrition facilities and schools 3.2. Conduct woman to woman sessions on hygiene promotion to focusing on menstrual hygiene management (MHM) and safe drinking water within the family.

Mitigation Measures

The project is meant to build community resilience by strengthening local water infrastructure and thus provide timely action, maintaining momentum of the response, and expanding reach in underserved areas promoting equity. Based on the cluster objectives, the response strategy includes the need to deliver a core package of integrated services, where priority will be given to the delivery of equitable access to basic Wash service services, while simultaneously addressing underlying causes of exposure to the risk to the vulnerable IDPs and host communities .To increase community uptake of provided services, community level demand creation will be strengthened. DEH will strengthen capacity of WASH committee by training them on life-skill, Wash service and technical project staff as well as community Wash service . Communities will be involved in project designing and will be part of the committees supporting . Working with other actors will ensure leverage amongst themselves and providing maximum support to target communities strengthening their capacities in various aspects, For sustainable and equitable access of the donor funded project DEH will ensure community inclusion in the initial decision making of the project design and structure in this case the WASH component will be prioritize the needs of community and in such will ensure to capacitate the existing community wash committee as well as the staff that will ensure the carryover/sustainability of the project after the expiry of the donor funds. DEH will ensure to provide more responsive services to the affected population under the strict supervision and coordination with government and other humanitarian actors within its area of operation. The WASH activities will consider the most vulnerable and disadvantage groups hence build the community resilience to prevent recurrent and protracted emergency.DEH will provide sufficient internal control through the dedicated monitoring and accountability officer who will ensure the project activities are implemented in accordance with set DEH internal monitoring criteria as well as include the donor and community level contribution hence this will minimize the project financial and quality risk while it also enhance the accountability to the affected population DEH will ensure to capacity build its staff and community focal point on prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) organization policy as well as its code of conduct hence ensure proper understanding of the PSEA policies and procedure hence reduces the risk related with PSEA,
