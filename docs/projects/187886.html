Project Summary

Project proposal aims to improve safer and more dignified living conditions for 100000 beneficiaries (men 20000– women 20000– boys 30000– girls 30000) in 50 sites in Dana, and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts in Idleb with a set of integrated interventions in CCCM sector; supporting establishment of accountable, and competent management structures to promote the capacities of IDPs in camps to conduct care and maintenance for infrastructure and fire mitigation and response within these camps.
In order to enhancing the capacities of the displaced in the camps to prevent and respond to fires SAED will establish 100 fire points( 1point / 250 families) , providing them with equipment recommended by the Fire Working Group, and training 1333 displaced men and women on the use of fire extinguishers and first aid by professional trainers (1 traines/75 of the IDP population). These activities will be implemented during the first four months of the project in 2022.
SAED will continue to provide technical support to members of the fire committees during the duration of the project, maintenance of equipment and refilling of fire extinguishers as necessary. These activities will continue to be implemented for 20 months (8 months in 2022 and 12 months in 2023).
In order to enhance the capacity of the displaced to maintain infrastructure, carry out repairs and maintain facilities and services in the targeted camps Saed will form 50 repair and maintenance committees in the targeted camps, each committee consisting of 10 individuals, and training these committees on maintenance work and light repairs, and providing these committees with basic maintenance tool., These activities will be implemented during the first four months of the project in 2022.
And SAED will continue during the project period to provide technical and limited financial support to these committees.These activities will continue to be implemented for 20 months (8 months in 2022 and 12 months in 2023).
As part of this project, SAED will inform the camps cluster of fire incidents, share target camp data and gaps, and submit 4W reports monthly.

4 - Needs

To understand the real needs for these camps' population and figuring out the uncovered gaps and problems , In the second half of 2021 SCA conducted a need assessment in 100 camps under Dana mareet mesrin sub-district using many ways of collecting data in coordination with stakeholders (IDPs, local authorities and clusters), SCA conducted the following: - Rapid survey. - Conducting 13 FGDs that covered different age and sex categories of IDPs - Meeting with camp managers to specify current interventions by humanitarian partners The summary: - The reasons of fire in camps maybe classified: 1- related to beneficiary awareness towards fire and its causes, daily practices which is mostly categorized by neglect and lack of knowledge.2- Reasons related to camp structure which contribute enlarging fire when it occurs, these reasons include: Inappropriate design of camps in terms of distancing and high population density, and lack of consideration to fire separation lines during establishment. - there are no fire points near targeted camps in this proposal, a big difficulty to respond to fire inside these camps because of its nature and high density. - All targeted camps did not get any previous interventions in terms of fire, and there is no equipment or tools to fight fire.- The IDPs suffer from the negative consequences of the destruction and malfunctions that occur in the camps, and they do not have the ability to carry out repairs and maintenance without support.

5 - Implementation of activities

The specific objectives will be achieved through the following activities:1- fire mitigation: - Establishment of 50 concrete-made fire units (fire points) in targeted camps , this activity will implement by contractors throwgh 3 months- Equipping the fire units with necessary equipment and tools according to Fire mitigation TWS Guidelines.-Forming, training and qualifying of fire fighting committees from IDPs in camps with average of one person among 75 IDPs in camp with coordinating with camp management to chose impulsive persons with including women in these committees.Periodic Follow up that includes:- Periodic follow up with fire committees and checking their preparedness and alertness and providing them with any new instructions.- Checking the preparedness and safety of all materials and tools in fire points especially fire extinguishers and replacing the damaged ones if necessary.-Follow up with fire incidents reported in camps and evaluate the competency of equipment and Safety volunteers units and share information with CCCM Cluster .2-Maintenance and light repairs:-Forming, training and qualifying of maintenance committees from IDPs in camps with average of 10 person in each camp with coordinating with camp management to chose impulsive persons.- Providing maintenance committees with basic repair tools.-Providing financial support for maintenance and light repairs.-Follow up the work of the committees and share reports from stakeholders.
