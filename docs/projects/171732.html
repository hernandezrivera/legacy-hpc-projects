Project Summary

This project aims to retain and provide learning opportunity in safe and protective learning environment for 18,000 affected children (49% F:51% M) in 50 primary schools in 9 regions- 4 in Puntland and 5 in South-central. Of the 18000 children to be supported, 10,716 children (51.5%) are displaced and 7,284 (40.5%) are non-displaced children. Besides, 370 teachers (20% F) and 350 CEC members (43% F) will be supported. The proposed areas for project implementation are districts and cities with highest number of displaced in need such as Banadir, Baidoa, Bosaso and Galkacayo. In these areas, schools were closed due to the COVID 19 Pandemic thus disrupting learning and consequently cutting off school meals which many children relied on. Lack of educational materials to support learning and lack of adequate latrines and/or gender segregated latrines as well as access to safe drinking water and hand washing facilities in schools are constant needs.
The project will adopt an integrated approach by promoting safe, inclusive and quality learning opportunities for the children affected by crisis and provide essential support for students and schools including construction and rehabilitation of temporary learning spaces, distribution of learning materials, construction of water barkets, gender-segregated latrines; provision of drinking water; hygiene promotion services including Covid-19 awareness and provision of live-saving services such as provision of school meals. Community mobilization including awareness on traditional harmful practices and back to school to school campaign will be regularly conducted to reduce dropouts. The project will also give special emphasis on the protection and the well-being of children by training teachers on psychosocial support, and Community Education Committees on child protection and school safety as well developing community-based child protection mechanism to promote the safety and protection of students at schools, make school safe and child-friendly zone and identify and refer children who need psychosocial support. The project will also strengthen the capacity of the Education authorities and education partners on Emergency preparedness, response and coordination.
Gender will be mainstreamed into the project during the design of activities and gender will be cross-cutting. The project will support 1,800 children with disabilities (40% F) which is 10% of the 18,000 target students. NRC acknowledges that children with disability are disproportionately affected by crisis and experience additional challenges in accessing education. Attitudinal, physical, structural, environmental and communication barriers impede their participation in the education and as a result large number of children with disability are out-of-school. Females with disability have more vulnerabilities including inability to use public WASH facilities. The project will address these barriers and support children with less complex disability through community mobilization, improving their identification and data collection, training teachers on inclusive education making school latrines & classrooms more accessible, establishing disability clubs, developing a buddy scheme and adding parent of a disabled child to the school CECs and ensuring disabled children can access extra-curricular activities and sports. On quality of learning, the project will enhance use of locally made learning aids. NRC would work with Health actors on the referral and support of these children including provision of assistive devices and medical support. Children having complex impairment will be referred to the special schools. On monitoring, the project will capture information about access by persons with disabilities or the accessibility of services to them. The project will be directly implemented by NRC in collaboration with federal, state Ministries of Education and Somalia Education clusters.

Mitigation Measures

NRC is aware of potential risk threats faced by the beneficiaries. Therefore, following measures will be put in place to mitigate these threats.1. Information provision to population at risk & affected population2. Community participation in beneficiary selection through consultative process and rigorous pre-determined selection criteria3. Beneficiaries selection and verification committees to have equal members of male and female4. Recruitment of more female teachers and increase of CEC female members to at least 43%5. Training of teachers, head teachers and CEC on Sexual Exploitation and Abuse6. Enhance project’s PSEA prevention and management capabilities by training staff on it7. Data protection training for staff and school management8. Establishment of Child clubs 9. Establishment school protection committees consisting of students, teachers and CECs10. Regular Joint supervision to schools by NRC and MOEs to identify protection issues11. Referral of affected children to services providers and protection agencies12. Construction of school facilities in land in public land with tenure13. Advocacy for permanent land for protracted IDPs and lease for temporary IDP settlements. 14. Increasing community awareness on explosive and hazardous material and working with authorities and demining agencies in its safe removal15. Making separate arrangements for vulnerable individuals and groups to access information and services when and where required16. Promotion of participation and empowerment of these communities.17. Awareness raising to beneficiaries to report concerns through complaint and feedback mechanisms18. Ensuring accountability to affected population Gender Girls enrollment is low in schools due to poverty, lack of appropriate learning spaces, distance to schools and cultural factors including traditional female roles and parent not giving priority to girls’ education. Early marriage is also another problem for girls. To address these barriers, the project will provide incentive to teachers so that girls can learn freely. Girls will also receive learning materials. Besides, NRC in collaboration with the education authorities and CECs will conduct community mobilizations to increase girls’ education and sensitize parents on the importance of female education. The project will recruit more female teachers to the target schools so as to increase female enrollment. On school environment, the project will construct and rehabilitate classrooms, latrines and barkets to make the school environment safe and protective. Besides, teachers will be trained on inclusive education. The beneficiaries’ selection and verification committees will have equal number of male and female so as to ensure fair selection of beneficiaries. Girls clubs will be established in all schools to empower and enhance female participation.
