Project Summary

About eight years of continuous armed conflicts in Syria were enough to weaken the Syrian communities. The war has left one of the worst humanitarian crises of all time. Millions of people have been killed or forced to flee their homes. Families are struggling to survive inside Syria, or make a new home in neighboring countries.
The economic and social situation in Syria continues to decline and severely affect everybody especially those who have been displaced from or returned to their homes. The humanitarian needs in Syria are expected to remain high in 2019 as several factors still worsen the economy situation that make people in front of a big daily challenge to secure their basic food needs. The Economic pressures are key drivers of need, with large groups stating that the most widespread challenges are inability to afford needed food items and following negative coping strategies.
The purpose of this project is to provide food parcel to most vulnerable Syrian people in Rural Damascus, Der ez-Zor, Dar'a, Al Quneitra and Al Hasakeh; as according to HNO 2018, an estimated of 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity.
Our contribution to this objective is to reduce the vulnerability of affected population through the provision of essential food items in targeted areas of this project by distributing food parcels, with a purpose of assisting the very vulnerable population in these areas
As an outcome for this project The well being of 12,000 vulnerable families (around 60,000 individuals) in need who will receive 12,000 essential food items.

Needs

The need of food is the core need to people in Syria. According to HNO 2018, an estimated of 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity. Compared to HNO 2017, there is 4 percent decrease in proportion of PIN, however, the need of food is still high in 2019. An estimated 40 percent of Syrians spend more than 65 percent of their expenditure on food, 57.2 percent of food insecure households reported to have outstanding debt and 65 percent of food insecure people are adopting crisis or emergency negative coping strategies. In general, provinces of Der ez-zor, Dar’a, Rural Damascus,Al Hasakeh and Al Quneitra were recorded as the most severity areas. These governorates currently host about 6 million people with an estimation of more than 2 million PIN. Based on HNO, Food and still remain a high priority to the displaced and affected families as well as returnees.And based on the GOPA Needs assessment mechanism, which is based on discussion within focused groups,78% Of communities assessed reported some types of food too expensive, 48% Some food items unavailable on market, 42%Lack of resources to bay available food, 21% lack of availability of cooking fuel, 20% Local food production has decreased ".The purpose of this project is to provide direct assistance to people living in targeted areas.So our contribution to this objective is to reduce the vulnerability of affected population through the provision of essential food items

Implementation of activities

The project aims to provide total of 36,000 food parcels to the most vulnerable people in Rural Damascus, Der ez-Zor, Al Hasakeh, Dar’a and Al Quneitra.Based on GOPA Previous intervention in the targeted areas, which were classified HTR Areas before by providing basic foodstuffs and non-food items, GOPA Has a database in those areas showing the continued needs of foodstuffs in these areas Due to the continued poor economic situation and low purchasing value of local currency, and that is matching with HNO 2019.Therefore, through the implementation of this project by providing basic food for those families we can ensure the food security for them, in addition to the provision of such assistance will have a significant positive impact in reducing the economic burden for those families.The distribution will go through clear steps; each family will receive three food parcels during the project life with giving the priority to female-headed household, pregnant or lactating women, persons with disabilities and families of more than 5 members.The distribution process s will go through five different phases as following: Preparation: GOPA contacted with the targeted groups to check their needs, opinions and suggestions in order to proceed the implementation. Selection: This phase includes coordination with other NGOs working in the same area to avoid duplication of beneficiaries. Bidding: Tender is announced at this phase after all needed documents are prepared, offers from different suppliers will be received, a specialized committee will study all offers and then GOPA will contract with the best technically and financially offer. Implementation: All Fi kits will be delivered to the targeted area and will be distributed according to a planned schedule. Monitoring actions will be taken to make sure of the best implementation in terms of beneficiaries’ satisfactions and quality of the distributed kits. Evaluation: An evaluation process will follow the implementation to identify the program weak and strong points and to set a plan for other projects.
