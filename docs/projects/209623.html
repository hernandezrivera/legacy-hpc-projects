Project Summary

Shifting its focus to none emergency, the Logistics Cluster will focus on providing coordination services to all sectors working in Syria to promote cost effectiveness through overlap and cost avoidance. Benefiting of the good logistics setup, the LC would be able to position itself in the response of emergencies through the provision of common services in storage and transport. Maintain regional WoS inter-agency logistics Coordination and Information Management to support the humanitarian response. Continue to facilitate transshipment services for UN supplies through the cross border under the Consent based Agreement. Continue the United Nations Humanitarian Air Service to provide safe, reliable and sustainable air access to beneficiaries for the humanitarian community, medical and security evacuations and facilitating the movement of light cargo as required.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The rationale of the Logistics Cluster strategy is focused on the partners needs towards effective and efficient humanitarian response. Coordination and Information Management will be the centre of focus of the cluster as main services offered to partners. In due course, Common Services will be based on actual needs of partners. However, the Logistics Cluster will maintain a pool of services providers lists ready to be engaged should the need arise during the period. It is also in line with the humanitarian community strategy and goals.Transfer the focus from emergency to non-emergency activities, identify and support nexus initiatives• Strengthen intra-sectoral coordination network & intersectoral coordination (ERL)• Enhance partnerships with stakeholders, government and private sector• Optimise the use of agency resources to serve the bigger community• Raise partners awareness on reliable local source of information• Identify logistics long lasting solutions to meet partners’ needs (local market, pooling of resources)• Address identified challenges through technical working group (fuel, pooling of resources, access constraints)• Advocate for sectoral concerns• Building partners technical capacity when and where needed• Building partners physical capacity by donating and erecting mobile storage units (MSU) where and when it is needed• Support the definition and the implementation of preparedness activities• Share leadership and localize the response• Develop and implement a transition and exit strategy• Increase the focus to North East Syria (NES) and Deir Ezzour area by conducting regular meetings focused on them
