Project Summary

In 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3
million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced, including over 2 million who live in last resort sites.
After 12 years of hostilities and a persistent economic crisis, people in Syria saw their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit
Northern Syria and Türkiye on 6 and 20 February 2023.
On September 2023, the MEAL department at SECD conducted a needs assessment in the self-settled and planned camps in Jisr Ash-shughur, Idleb- Maaret Tamsrin, and in Jebel Saman districts and Atareb) sub-districts to assess the most challenges in terms of camp management and fire point plans’ services and determine gaps and the main needs of IDPs living in the camps. The questionnaire has been deployed on KOBO. Then data were sent to the MEAL department in Turkey The assessment is at the KIIs level. The KEY FINDINGS 1. According to the data analysis, 61 of the assessed camps have no contingency plans. Also, according to the KIIs. 2. Regarding camp committees, analyzed data show that 49% of camps have no committees at all however 11 % of assessed camps have committees, and 51% of the assessed sites reported that they do not have any fire points.
This project hopes to improve the lives of more than 121612 IDPs (Men: 22111, Women: 22996, Boys: 37015, Girls: 39400) who stayed in 61 camps in the northwest of Syria, which poses an additional burden on the area. The targeted 87 camps in Aleppo and Idlib governorates are either an extension for pre-established camps or new
. SECD is aiming to support the donors to put an end to the threats to 121612 IDPs by implementing complementary activities to improve the targeted camps' infrastructure, ensuring protect the women, children, and elderly while focusing on the inclusion mainstreaming for people with disabilities through the following key activities :
-Establishing camp management and training them on the necessary knowledge for
targeted camps
-Prepare a fire response plan in the targeted camps by establishing 113 fire point extinguishing points and training for a fire safety plan.
- Provision of comprehensive support to existing or establishing fire mitigation and response systems in IDP sites.
-Developing fire warden systems, and creating/supporting fire committees, which will be responsible for providing awareness sessions for IDPs on fire hazards and how to respond to incidents
-Provision of relevant vocational training and tools to camp management committees to participate in the infrastructure upgrade/repairs in the sites.
-- Provision of ¦re response resources (such as extinguishers, fire blankets, fire extinguisher tools, and first aid kits) about 22111fire banket will be distributed
Protection Risk Analysis :
SECD is committed to mitigating the following:
Activity: Strengthening emergency preparedness and (fire) response in IDP sites.
Protection Risk/Threat: Lack of fire prevention and response would be critical for the
safety of the IDPs living in already precarious conditions and environments. Likelihood: High, Impact: high Mitigating Measure: 1. Strengthening Fire Mitigation and Response Measures in IDP Sites in NWS through purposeful, related activities. 2. Maintain the CCCM and SNFI Cluster technical guidance. 3. Reporting and tracking through Incident reporting and referral tools. 4. Providing emergency support through Site Monitoring Teams. 5. Ensure Contingency Plans are in place.
Activity: Promote participatory management structures Likelihood: Medium,
Impact: Medium Mitigating Measure: 1. Implementing partners should ensure that any
management structure is representative of the entire community with strong selection criteria.
