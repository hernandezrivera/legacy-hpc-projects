Project Summary


Humanitarian WASH Assistance for scaling up health and well-being project is designed 23010 individuals in light of COVID-19 perspective i.e. Provision of access to safe and clean drinking water, adequate sanitation facilities at household and institutional level, provision of life saving Equipments, PPEs and building the capacity of front line health workers and target population through hygiene sessions, awareness campaign, dissemination of IEC materials, infection prevention and control trainings.
Improved access to safe and clean drinking water at community and institutional level will be ensured through rehabilitation, construction and installation of communal water systems i.e. Hand pumps, solar pumps etc.
Adequate sanitation facilities will be provided through construction and rehabilitation of sanitation facilities in both institutions and at household level. Adequate sanitation facilities will be ensured through component sharing and participatory approaches. Components i.e. CGI sheets,doors,WC,pipe,cement etc will be provided by the organization, whereas the target household will be mobilized for provision of ,local available materials ,skilled and unskilled labor.
COVID-19 exposed the WASH facilities in rural and urban areas; however it also realized the importance and significance of water sanitation and hygiene in light of this pandemic. Group hand wash at institutions and hand wash facility will install along with provision of soap in public and community points. In order to protect the target community from viral diseases, the practice of good hygiene in terms of washing hands with soap will be encouraged especially among men, women and children in COVID-19 affected area.
The capacity of front line health workers at tehsil and district level would be increased through conducting training of trainers on Infection Prevention and Control (IPC).The participants will trickle down the training component to their concern staff members in their concern health units. Lifesaving health Equipments will be provided to THQ and DHQ. Hospitals and public offices will be disinfected through chlorine powder and designated spray.
Added to this a one week COVID-19 Awareness campaign will be launched and executed for the target beneficiaries. Furthermore IEC materials will be provided and disseminated in the target area of intervention. The target community will be equipped through conducting hygiene session, with special focus on COVID-19 perspectives along with provision of Hygiene kits and PPEs.
The proposed intervention aims to ensure access to and provision of safe drinking water, appropriate sanitation, and promotion on safe hygiene practices including improved knowledge and practice of IPC for the covid-19 affected people and staff at healthcare, quarntine/ isolation centres in district D I Khan, which will further reduce the chances of viral transmission, pandemic, associated with waterborne diseases.
Overall improved knowledge, attitude and practice of healthy habits, access to safe and clean drinking water, adequate sanitation facilities, Healthcare facilities attending to COVID-19 cases with missing/ less than required WASH facilities, capacity building of front line health workers and provision of lifesaving Equipments into health units will improve the health and well-being of 23010 target population and thus will reduce the chances of viral transmission, pandemic and other waterborne diseases in district D I Khan.
The proposed activities will be held with close liaison of concern stakeholders and a strong complaint redressal mechanism will be established for accountability purpose to 3540 target families and other concern stakeholders. The tangible items will be hand over to concern stakeholders at community and institutional level.
