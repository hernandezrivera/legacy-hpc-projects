Project Summary

The UN Women Multi Country Office- Caribbean, joined the wider UN system Rapid Assessment Team (RNAT) and emergency response, led by the affected governments through their National Disaster Management Agencies and in close collaboration with the Caribbean Disaster Emergency Management Agency (CDEMA), to assess the scope of destruction, needs on the ground, and to manage the coordination of immediate assistance needs and strategically plan for longer term aid. What has emerged from initial assessments, observations from staff as well as requests from the affected States is that in addition to key essential needs around food, shelter, WASH and health are measures that offer protection of the affected population that caters to their specific needs, to the existing and often exacerbated inequalities and the often-disproportionate impacts felt by already vulnerable groups. Project Activities

• 1. Supporting psychosocial and self-care needs to address trauma, stress and anxiety being experienced post-disaster by the population, in collaboration with other agencies in the Health sub-sector on PSS, by:, • 1.1 Rolling out of the UN Women Self Care Toolkit through a communication campaign for instance (flyers, posters, radio and tv ads provision of printed materials, portable PA system) to shelters and local communities, and training of trainers., • 1.2 Contributing to the mobilization of an additional counsellors(varying PSS Staff) needed to support the mental health and psychosocial needs of the people in shelters, communities, GBV referral pathways and other specialized spaces (e.g elderly homes, etc). And hire of 2 psychologists to serve as managers and where more complex cases will be referred and to which the PSS staff (30 counsellors will report)., • 1.3 Establishing a ‘Caring for Carers’ program to provide psychosocial support mechanisms to psychosocial workers and first responders (shelter managers, PSS staff, ministry staff, directors, medical staff , GBV responders , case workers , social workers), in collaboration with PAHO., • 1.4 Provision of psychosocial and recreational items such as sensory toys, art supplies, musical instruments and other trauma-reducing tools., • 1.5 Hosting football activities in partnership with the Caribbean Football Union and the local football association., • 2. Providing (relief) supplies and services (primarily cash) that cater for the needs of women, men, boys and girls, in collaboration with others delivering NFIs and cash transfers, by:, • 2.1 Facilitating access to non-food items and other needs to key parts of the affected population through establishing relationships with civil society organizations (CSOs) and providing them with financial resources to on-grant to local CSOs and community-based organizations that can understand the needs and help to translate that into targeted assistance., • 2.2 Supporting key assessments and targeting of caseloads for multi-purpose cash, as well as utilizing other CBT instruments for supporting women-lead MSMEs, agri-businesses, CBOs, care services operators, as well as small business based in tourism and creative and cultural industries., • 2.3 Procuring and distributing non-food items identified as key needs for women, men, boys and girls particularly in various emergency and non-emergency shelter situations through UN Women’s ‘Share the Care Packs’., • 3. Ensuring that shelters are safe spaces for all, in collaboration with IOM and other Shelter sector participating agencies, by:, • 3.1 Providing training for shelter managers and coordinators particularly on protection measures particulalry measures to prevent and respond to GBV, within shelters., • 3.2 Supporting persons in public and private shelters with options for care services to allow them to engage in recovery activities., • 3.3 Contributing self-care and wellness tools and activities (including sports) to a comprehensive package of approaches to support sheltering families manage their mental state and be able to engage in recovery., • 3.4 Provide the National Gender Machinery with electronic devices (tablets) to conduct assessments and other related activities associated with recovery efforts and resilience building., • 3.5 Creation of community notice boards (Mayreau, Union Island, Canouan, Bequia) to support the dissemination of critical information, MHPSS information etc..
