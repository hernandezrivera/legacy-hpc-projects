Project Summary

According to the national level, the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) has remained Serious (10– 14.9%) over the past three seasons: 2021 Gu (11.1%), 2020 Deyr (11.8%), and 2020 Gu (11.8%). The FSNAU findings in Bay and Bakool confirms a global acute malnutrition (GAM) prevalence of 11.9 % (8.7 - 16.0 95% C.I.) while the prevalence of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) in the survey was 1.2 % (0.5 - 2.7 95% C.I.). The Somalia Health cluster confirms 20,869 cumulative confirmed cases of COVID-19 in Somalia as of 2nd October 2021, including 767 associated deaths. From the start of Covid 19 March 2020 to 2nd October 1,133 deaths reported with a CFR of 5.4%. There is high AWD/Cholera. The District lacks access to primary healthcare.
We focus on families with Persons with Disabilities, Minority clans, child-headed Households, the poorest, IDPs,Returnees and those affected by floods and droughts with child-bearing persons.
Main interventions include :
1. - Projects summary
I. Setting and supporting mobile 6 Health clinics to increase access to basic health services.
II. Ensure access to primary and referral health care ( maternal and child health, (BEmONC) and (CEmONC), expanded Immunization.
III. Advocacy calling for access to basic health services as a human right.
IV. Availing curative and preventive lifesaving health services (ANC/PNC and maternity services to 8000 pregnant and lactating women and Girls, including 1000 PWDs and under five 6,500 Girls and 4,500 Boys including 1000 Girls and 1000 Boys living with Disabilities).
V. Monitor and identify, screen and lead the target households to undergo Health examination, referrals and treatment.
VI. Training on EENC, KMC, BEmONC, Clinical management of rape, GBV core concepts, safe and ethics.
VII. identify , treat, record , treat and refer SGBV , lead anti-SGBV advocacies
VIII. COVID-19 mitigations including sensitisations and distribution of COVID 19 TOOLKITS to the beneficiaries
IX. Monitor,evaluate and report project activities
X. Setting up Humanitarian health accountability, complaints and complementary mechanisms
XI. Reporting on access to basic health among the Persons living with Disabilities.
