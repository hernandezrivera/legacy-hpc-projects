Project Summary

This intervention seeks to provide lifesaving access to water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) solutions for displaced and returnee populations in the Ancuabe and Macomia districts of Cabo Delgado, Mozambique. Years of armed conflict have severely damaged infrastructure in these areas. The primary beneficiaries are families returning to their places of origin, who face dire living conditions with no access to basic WASH services. These communities are at heightened risk of waterborne disease outbreaks due to the absence of latrines, water supply networks, and safe sanitation practices.
Key activities include constructing and rehabilitating sanitation systems, such as community and household latrines in high-risk areas. The intervention will also deliver hygiene awareness campaigns focusing on critical behaviors like handwashing, using treated water, and safe water storage. Additionally, community leaders and local authorities will receive training to ensure the long-term sustainability of these efforts.
The intervention prioritizes the most vulnerable groups—women, children, and individuals with special needs—who face disproportionate challenges in displacement and return scenarios. Beyond addressing immediate health risks, the initiative aims to promote a sustainable and inclusive recovery, empowering communities to independently manage their WASH resources and build resilience. Guided by humanitarian principles, the response integrates urgent needs with efforts to restore dignity and ensure the safety of affected populations.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project is a lifesaving humanitarian response aimed at addressing the critical water, sanitation and hygiene needs of displaced and returnee populations in Ancuabe and Macomia districts, Cabo Delgado. These communities, severely impacted by years of armed conflict, face acute vulnerabilities, with many living in precarious conditions lacking safe drinking water, adequate sanitation, and essential hygiene facilities. According to the Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM), these districts experience high rates of forced displacement and return, leaving families in dire need of support. Returnees face destroyed infrastructure, unsanitary environments, and limited access to services, significantly increasing the risk of waterborne disease outbreaks such as cholera and diarrhea. The intervention prioritizes lifesaving actions, including the construction of emergency latrines, installation of hand pumps for safe water access, and urgent hygiene awareness campaigns to mitigate health risks. This response focuses on the most vulnerable populations, including women, children, and people with reduced mobility, who are disproportionately affected by these conditions. By addressing critical immediate needs and reducing the risk of disease, this intervention will save lives while also laying the groundwork for longer-term recovery. It aligns with humanitarian principles of urgency, equity, and inclusion, ensuring that lifesaving assistance reaches those most in need.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The emergency WASH intervention in Ancuabe and Macomia districts focuses on improving the health and sanitation conditions of displaced and returnee populations, with targeted actions in specific high-need centers. The intervention prioritizes Nanona, Milamba, and Teule in Ancuabe, and Machova, Serração, and Nacate in Macomia, chosen for their high return rates, high vulnerability and inadequate WASH infrastructure. Below is a summary of priority activities for each location: ANCUABE District** **Nanona, Teule, Milamba Center** -- Support communities in the construction of family latrines using the emergency adapted -SANTOLIC approach. Construct emergency communal latrines- Rapid rehabilitate or drill wells, and install waste management systems to ensure access to potable water and reduce environmental contamination.- Provide critical hygiene education and awareness campaigns, rapid distribute emergency hygiene kits, and promote safe practices like handwashing and water treatment.- Train community leaders and women’s groups to manage water, sanitation, and waste systems, ensuring sustainability in emergency contexts. MACOMIA DISTRICT **Machova, Serração , Nacate Centers** - Facilitate the construction of family and community latrines, including drainage systems, with approaches adapted for emergency and high-risk areas.- Provide emergency potable water systems through well rehabilitation, new wells, cisterns, storage, and distribution points, with integrated water treatment solutions.- Conduct hygiene education and awareness campaigns, emphasizing cholera and diarrhea prevention, safe water use, and community engagement through schools, women’s groups, and local leaders.- Train community leaders and groups to manage and maintain water and sanitation systems for long-term sustainability. CROSS-CUTTING ACTIVITIES - Distribute lifesaving family hygiene kits with essential WASH supplies, including soap, buckets, water purification tablets, and educational materials. - Continuously monitor community water and sanitation conditions, tracking health improvements and reductions in waterborne diseases. - Train community committees to independently manage and maintain water and sanitation infrastructure.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project proposal emphasizes equitable access to assistance while promoting the participation and empowerment of women and the elderly through inclusive and needs-sensitive approaches. From the planning stage, priority will be given to actively consulting women and the elderly to ensure their voices shape decisions regarding the location and design of water and sanitation infrastructure. Workshops and awareness sessions will be tailored specifically for these groups, focusing on hygiene practices and the safe use of water. Efforts will be made to ensure that information is presented in an accessible and relevant manner. Gender-segregated latrines and water points will be installed to enhance privacy and security for women. Additionally, facilities will be designed to accommodate the needs of the elderly, ensuring accessibility and ease of use. To sustain empowerment, women and the elderly will receive training to manage and maintain water and sanitation systems within their communities. Local management committees will be established to actively involve these groups, promoting shared responsibility and leadership. These actions not only ensure equitable access to resources but also enhance the autonomy and leadership of women and the elderly, enabling them to play key roles in managing and sustaining water and sanitation systems. This approach fosters a safer, more inclusive environment for all.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The environmental impact of the project will be carefully monitored, as activities related to the construction of latrines and water supply may affect the environment if not managed sustainably. Potential impacts include contamination of water sources, deforestation for infrastructure construction, and improper use of water resources. To mitigate these risks, the project will adopt various environmental management measures.First, detailed environmental assessments will be conducted before the start of activities at each site to identify and minimize potential negative impacts. These assessments will focus on preserving water sources, selecting appropriate locations for the construction of sanitation infrastructure, and ensuring the sustainable use of natural resources. The project will also prioritize the use of local and sustainable construction materials, avoiding deforestation and promoting construction practices that respect ecological balance.Environmental awareness programs will be conducted with the communities, addressing practices such as proper solid waste management, conscious water use, and the preservation of local ecosystems. To ensure long-term sustainability, the project will include ongoing training on the maintenance of water and sanitation infrastructure, enabling the communities to preserve the environment while managing their own resources responsibly.
