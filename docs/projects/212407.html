Project Summary

According to HNO 2024, JENA June 2023 reports, the context in NWS can be summarized as follow: After 12 years of conflict, the country has the highest PIN since the beginning of the crisis. There has been a 4% increase in the PiN, compared to 2023.This emphasizes an urgent need for emergency education services to support 1,862,203 children 5-17 years (46% girls, 54% boys) and 62,073 education personnel, with (48% F and 52% M). PiN consist of 10% of CwD, highlighting the need for specialized education services. Idleb has the highest percentages of OoSC (69%). Over 15% are at risk of dropping out. Financial hardship causes the family to pull their children out of school and resort to negative coping strategies. The situation is exacerbated by the EQ and amid rising inflation, 85% of HHs are unable to meet their basic needs. OoS boys are at a higher risk of being recruited by armed groups and exposed to child labor exploitation, girls are at a higher risk of early forced marriage, sexual and domestic violence. Barriers also include overcrowded, damaged and distant schools, lack of school furniture and school supplies, lack of textbooks and learning materials, lack of TVET, winterization issues, protection issues like physical punishment and violence. Schools lack electricity, heating, and lighting. Schools' infrastructure was already insufficient prior to the EQ.1,054 schools in NWS need rehabilitation. Teachers need (MHPSS), adequate remuneration and capacity building.
WATAN findings at 10 schools-7 sub-districts-8 communities(4 sub-districts are 5 and three sub-districts are 4 on PiN severity scale) are aligned with HNO2024.
Project Interventions:
-Rehabilitation of 94 classrooms and 20 wash blocks at 10 schools with consideration of CwD, gender related issues to provide protective learning spaces and increase the most vulnerable children access to learning
-Construction of 18 TLS/2 in each of the 9 targeted schools.
-Formal Education at 9 schools to 5084 pupils (2471 b/ 2613 g) 454 CwD
-9 BTL campaigns at 8 communities to encourage OoSC reenrolling & retaining children at risk of dropping out
-NF education at the same previous 9 schools for 437(226 b/ 211g)
-TVET at 1 formal school in Idleb city for 234 pupils (180 b/ 54g) 23 CwD
-Providing the same 9 schools with ECCE & 1-month cycle of ECD sessions according to IRC curriculum.1508 CH (795 b & 713 g). New CH will be targeted every month.
-Protection & safety materials, protocols and emergency evacuation plan with needed trainings to mitigate emergency risks
-Schools furniture for 10 schools to complete already existing furniture and for the established TLS
-Solar power systems to 10 schools.
-Maintenance and running cost including cleaning material, stationery and fuel for heating for 10 schools.
-School supplies besides school bag to 7263 CH (3672 B/ 3591 G) in FE and NFE settings to decrease HHs financial burdens and improve CH access to schooling
-Textbook to 5755(2877 B/ 2878 G) 511PwD in FE/NFE/ TVET at 10 schools
-Learning materials to 1945 (1021 B &924 G) in remedial, catch up, and ECCE & ECD children at 9 schools
-Supplementary printed and online materials such as ECD, educational games, health and hygiene brochures to 7263 CH (3672 B/3591 G) at 10 FE & NFE
-Providing 7263 CH and 331 teachers (3672 B/ 3591 G /143 M/ 188 F) with PSS activities in FE & NFE setting.
-Cash transfers for 234 (180B /54G) pupils 14-17 years at 1 school to ensure their schooling continuation
-School feeding program for 1508 CH 3-5 years (795B/ 713G) at ECCE program at 9 schools
-Monthly monetary incentives to 331 teachers and Edu personnel (143M / 188F) in FE &NFE settings.
-Teachers capacity building trainings related to professional development, soft skills, MHPSS, CP and safeguarding, classroom management, SE learning, pedagogical skills and positive discipline. 303 teachers(126M/177F).
-Teaching materials, resources and guides for 273 teachers (110 M &163F) in FE & NFE settings.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Protection Risk Analys:1.Risk:Teachers participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect pupils. Mitigation: Teachers will be trained and monitored on CH safeguarding, reporting of violence & physical punishment and positive discipline practices.2.Risk: Discrimination against PWD, IDPs & girls at schools. Mitigation: WATAN will enhance an inclusive education approach in coordination with local actors and PTA. Child safeguarding policy will be in place and monitored by project staff. CM services will be provided when needed. Teachers soft skill competencies & pedagogical skills will be built and coached.3.Risk:schools don’t protect children from flood & rain. Mitigating: Rehabilitation of classrooms, wash facilities and infrastructure, besides mainstreaming &training on emergency evacuation plans.4.Risk: PwD, girls& female teachers may face SEA due to insecure wash facilities. Mitigation: Sex and age Segregated toilets & provision of door locks. Wash facilities will be accessible by PWDs through provision of ramps, special seats for PWD. Training of BNFs on PSEA reporting mechanism.5.Risk: Female teachers & girls at schools may face harassment and abuse. Mitigation: Train staff on Do No Harm, (PSEA), and child safeguarding. sign and monitor adherence to related codes of conduct. CM and referral to needed specialized services will be provided. PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy:SEAH against women and girls. Mitigating Measure: Mainstreaming PESA in all the phases of the project When hiring the project staff & teachers through signing the PSEA policy and code of conduct. Conducting PSEA training & provision of reporting mechanism and clear reporting channels (lines, Posters, email, Focal points)that are accessible by all BNFs. MEAL officers will monitor schools and ensure PSEA policy commitment. beneficiaries will be able to follow up their complaints. Confidential investigations, if any, will be conducted with no harm policy commitment. CM and referral to specialized services will be provided. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures:1-Air attacks: Likelihood: Low Impact: High. Mitigating measures: In emergency cases, attacks will be reported to the relevant coordinator & EC through Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism. safety and security materials will be provided besides informed emergency plan among BNFs and CGs.2-Intervention by local authorities. Likelihood: Middle Impact: High. Mitigating measures: The project will be coordinated with local authorities. WATAN will sign MoU with ED to clarify targeted schools, planned activities and take recommendations to achieve project target and community benefits.3-Cholera/Covid/health outbreaks spread. Likelihood: low. Impact: High. Mitigation measures: WASH facilities will be sanitized, disinfected periodically according to Health & Hygiene TWG measures. Hygiene and health awareness sessions will be implemented with CH.
