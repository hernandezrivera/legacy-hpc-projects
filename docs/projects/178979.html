Project Summary

Food Basket: The overall goal of the project is increase access to monthly emergency
food provisions for recently displaced households, thereby contributing to improving the
food security status of food insecure people in Idleb Governorate
Specifically, the project aims to distribute emergency monthly food baskets (FBs) for
2,695 for displaced households and vulnerable host community members, and Ready-ToEat Rations (RTERs) for 1,000 newly displaced households (including women, children,
people with disabilities, and elderly people), through conducting rapid distribution of
Ready-To-Eat Rations (RTERs) after registration and verification of beneficiaries, in order
to provide a rapid response to newly displaced households; that will happen in an
emergency context. as well as MWL field team will conduct door-to-door monthly
distribution of Food Baskets to the selected families (verified and approved by IOM
Turkey) over a period of eight months.
Agriculture intervention: The project aims to take advantage of what is currently available
in the form of local resources and services, and focus on dealing with the major
challenges facing the olive sector in the target communities, specifically in relation to
costs at all stages of production. In addition, the project aimed to overcome the gaps of
knowledge about the best agricultural practices for the olive sector through Integrated
Olive Management.
The project is designed to contribute to enhancing stabilization and rebuilding olive value
chain through support 700 HHs from IDPs and Host Communities from mid-scale olive
farmers who own 4-10 dunums of olive orchards in Aleppo Governorate (Afrin district)
and support seasonal workers by select 1050 workers from (HHS IDPs and Host
Communities) for manual harvesting, and pruning by cash for work activities.
Livestock intervention: MWL aims to improve the food security and livelihood situation of
vulnerable conflict-affected people and enhance their resilience in Idleb and Aleppo, five
district will be targeted (Afrin, Ariha, Harim, Idleb and Jabal samaan), through the fodder
distribution for the vulnerable herders in the locations, targeted the livestock by the
animals treatment for the 600 HH, and to raise the capacity and skills of veterinary
service providers, MWL proposed project aim to t raise the capacity and skills of
veterinary service providers and the herders on the Livestock topics for both of males and

4 - Needs

Due to the severity displacement’s movement happened in the last three months wherethe number of IDPs reached to 100,000 based on the CCCM Cluster and with a huge needto the basic aids. Based on HNO 2021,Needs related to agricultural livelihoods therefore remain considerable. Unilateral coercive measures have contributed to a reduction in the availability of quality agricultural inputs and equipment,.Farmers also suffer limited access to market.Based on RNA conducted by MWL, the main problems are as following: •The high cost of compost, pesticides, pruning, plowing, and milling services, leading to an increase in production costs. •The spread of insects (olive fruit fly,olive moth, and olive psyllid), diseases (olive wilt, and Olive leaf spot (peacock eye spot)).•Lack of skilled workers for conducting services of pruning and pest management.•Marketing difficulties. All of factos have caused a deterioration in the olive sector,olive oil unavailability, olive oil low quality, this has led to low farmer incomes, low marketmovement, and food insecurity accompanied by weakened livelihoods. MWL team targeted (Afrin, DaretAzza, Ariha, Dana, Maaret Tamsrin) with assessment. MWL has made sure that project istargeting most vulnerable people MWl select the sub-districts that witnessed thedisplacement movement and have a big number of IDPs and PIN,The assessmentanalysis results showed a huge need for the interventions. 54% of respondentsmentioned that one of the challenges on the livestock field is no availability of fodder andpastures, and 47 % of them see that supporting the herders by fodder will mitigate thechallenges where 46 % mentioned that securing the medicine will contribute to face thechallenges.
