Project Summary

UNICEF intends to support the Coordination in the WASH sector, essential to strategize and optimize the intervention of all actors in WASH.
After reviewing different assessments, keeping in view the situations in East, needs, capacity and comparative advantage, UNICEF is planning to respond the needs of 0.8 million individuals in the two conflict affected areas of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts (GCA and NGCA) and Ivano-Frankivsk, Chernivtsi and Zakarpattia Oblasts in western Ukraine, with provision of safe drinking water, improving access to safe sanitation and hygiene supplies, dissemination of critical water and hygiene related messages on communities located on both side of contact line in the two oblasts of Eastern Ukraine, and to respond actively to the COVID-19 emergency.
This will be implemented through the water utility companies (centralized network of primary and secondary water suppliers) and decentralized structures (in NGCA). A particular focus will be made on WASH in social institutions (education/health facilities) on both GCA and NGCA.
The different activities take into account the gender, age and disabilities specificities and promote equality, especially on gender. People reached with more than one activity are only considered once to avoid duplication in counting beneficiaries.
The COVID-19 pandemic has furthered hampered the situation in Eastern Ukraine, for the conflict-affected population, suffering from the restriction of movement, the resulting economic crisis and being more and more directly affected by the disease. Institutions such as Health centers, Social institutions, educational facilities, are deeply impacted, depleting the level and quality of services too be provided to the population.
Also, Western Ukraine was part of the regions where people were among the first affected by the pandemic; Roma populations, often amid difficult conditions, are at particular risk; UNICEF proposes to support their HHs directly and through the public services the Roma frequent (health centers, social institutions, educational facilities…), and insist on hygiene messages (COVID-related, but also, menstrual hygiene etc.).
The total amount for the project is 6,245,044 USD, to provide assistance to 814,506 people.

1 - Needs

After 7 years of armed conflict in Eastern Ukraine, the local population continues to be affected by on-going hostilities & consequences with issues related to WASH as ones of the most acute. HNO indicates that 1.9 mln people (out of 3.2) require direct WASH primary assistance (GCA/NGCA). Also, critical public water infrastructure located close to the line of contact (LoC) is still at risk daily of being targeted: 57 incidents (shelling, shooting…) were reported over the past 9 months. Water companies continue to struggle financially, requiring further support in the procurement of essential chemicals to treat water, of equipment/ material to make critical repairs. The stoppage of water would also impact the heating systems, with the potential for widespread population suffering (-20C in winter).Poor condition of WASH infra. both on the institutional & community levels or deterioration of access to water & water quality in conflict-affected areas could result in applying negative coping strategies among people (7% HH store water in bathtub or buckets*), with a potential for microbiological contamination and an immediate impact on public health.On isolated settlements, 35% HHs have been experiencing water shortages occasionally, 11% on a weekly basis. This creates additional costs for purchasing & transporting water and affects people’s ability to maintain hygiene & livelihoods. 31% HHs on both sides of the LoC require help with water supply. This need is even more acute for people living <20 km LoC or in rural areas, and for HHs with disabled members. Due to the lack of support to the main water suppliers in NGCA, the access to piped drinking water dropped from 64% (<2014) to 53% by 2017 (REACH). A deterioration of the water situation since 2014 is reported by the population*.According to the HNO 2021, 84%/771 education facilities in Donbas GCA required some form of repair like WASH facilities & heating systems.Towns/villages near the frontline report problems with garbage. village councils can no longer afford to pay for trucks to remove it. The same applies to sewage in remote areas along the contact line, where people also need to empty HH level septic tanks; 26% of all HHs in GCA/20% in NGCA need help with sanitation*. Lastly, the access to hygiene materials remain challenging with some being not available in all locations in Donbas. According to ACCESS/NRC Rapid Market Assessment Apr 2020 ,25% people report that hygiene items were not fully available locally in April, compared to only 7% in Feb 20; the prices of hygiene items rose by nearly 7% from Feb to April.Since the COVID-19 pandemic was declared, a huge impact has been detected especially among the most vulnerable population (poorest HHs, elderly, GBV survivors, single-headed families, minorities…). The paradoxical situation that 95% of people [UNICEF, C-19 Public Opinion Survey 2020] is aware of the need to wash hand regularly to prevent contamination while soap & running water are often unavailable represents a major burden to fight against the pandemic. According to REACH [KAP for GCA Sep 20], 77% of people protect themselves by washing their hands & 86% of people know to wash their hand for 20 seconds or more. Hence, more effort must be done to prevent COVID-19 spread.The health system has been struggling to respond to this pandemic, but the lack of disinfection products represents another burden. According to REACH [Rapid Assess. Health Facilities Apr 20], in 473 P, S & THC in Donbas GCA, 60% do not have proper medical waste disposal in place (incinerator), 72% have only limited availability of Hand Sanitizer and 47% PHC rely on trucked water/wells for drinking: 29% experienced problems with water for cleaning.Vulnerable groups are of particular focus:Spending on hygiene items is the highest (<300UAH) for 24 households with children < 2 (diapers)17% HH with people with special needs in HCA experienced problems tap water availability* WASH Cl Asst 19

2 - Activities & Outcomes

utput 1: Coordination and advocacy – Live-savingWASH coordination mechanism continued for effective, efficient and accountable humanitarian response for affected people (Female, Male & children) in Eastern Ukraine.There is a need for further coordination on activities implemented in the WASH sector, identification of gaps, avoidance of duplication, standard setting, assessments & response strategies, technical guidance. As well as an advocacy work around 1) the targeting of water infrastructure by all parties to the conflict; 2) increased funding for water utility companies (international donors, government); 3) facilitated & safe movement of utility workers and essential materials to complete timely repairs. Output 2: Support to service providers – Critical to Live-savingMain Water Service providers supported and strengthened, in coordination with WASH actors, to ensure continued water supply and sanitation services to the affected people in Donetsk and Luhansk, GCA and NGCAUNICEF intends to support VD & PVK directly (GCA), as a pre-identified gap already coordinated with ICRC/SDC with liquefied chlorine gas & chemicals for water treatment. A support to repairing critical big water/wastewater systems and smaller vodokanals in NGCA, with the provision of spare parts, materials & equipment, is planned. Wastewater systems, pipelines & treatment plants, also impacted, will also be supported. Output 3: Support to institutions – Live-savingBoys and girls and their families in different social institutions including education and health facilities have access to improved and sustainable WASH services Assessment and repairs to damaged water, sanitation and heating systems in health facilities, schools and kindergartens is essential to keep the essential services operational. A particular focus on gender and dignity will be made on toilet related activities.Provision of hygiene kits/e-voucher-based assistance.Implementation of Hygiene Promotion activities to children, parents and teachers. In partnership with CSOs (cf. ‘Implementing Partners’) Output 4: Service provision to most affected people, including women and children – Live-savingAffected population has access to the efficient WASH services through provision of WASH supplies, emergency repair of WASH communal infrastructure/facilities.Short-term water trucking of potable & technical water (for cleaning) will be initiated only when critical needs arise, complemented by bottled water distribution, in respect with SPHERE Stds. In parallel, the most vulnerable HHs will be supported with water storage (water tanks or jerricans) or small-scale water systems (filtering devices);Repair of water structures of decentralized water networks is planned, with empowerment of women in village councils, in NGCA.Desludging of septic tanks & solid waste removal activities are required especially in areas close to LoC where access has been limited due to the conflict, and where costs of removal are now high. WASH activities supported by integration with livelihoods activities.Given the harsh situation at wintertime, some winterization activities will be realizedImproved availability of essential hygiene items, through an e-voucher programme in GCA, and distribution of hygiene kits in NGCA or in hard to reach areas in GCA. Programmes are designed to answer to the specific needs: women, elderly, people with disabilities... An appropriate HP programme will be implemented: sessions on reproductive health & MHM to youth; for pregnant women; for elderly people. Output 5: Emergency response to COVID-19 pandemic – Live-savingHealth facilities & vulnerable population have improved capacities to prevent COVID-19 contamination.Provision of essential hygiene and disinfection items/e-vouchers for health facilities and the most vulnerable HHs. Provision of PPEs for health workers to prevent contamination.Raise awareness campaigns on prevention and hygiene to fight against the pandemic.
