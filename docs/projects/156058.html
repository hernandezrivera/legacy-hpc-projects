Project Summary

GVC will intervene in Deir-Ez-Zor and Aleppo Governorates restoring basic water schools’ and community services. In both areas, the Action will target Hard to Reach (HTR) areas where the NGO is present with running programs in WASH and EiE sectors.
The action aims to provide sufficient and safe water to around 137 000 people living in Deir-Ez-Zor and Rural Aleppo areas. These projects will ensure access to water to inhabitants and will increase the capacity to cope with the increase number of returnees. Water networks and pumping stations are damaged and overloaded, unable to guarantee the quantity and quality needed to the inhabitants and obliging them to recur to other sources, exposing them to waterborne disease and depleting their already stretched resources. The rehabilitation of WASH infrastructures in 12 schools will guarantee the access to gender and disabled oriented facilities for all the students favoring school attendance. The HP campaign with the distribution of WASh items will allow the inhabitants of the selected areas to adopt adequate hygiene practices by reducing the incidence of waterborne diseases.

Needs

A technical assessment was carried out in March 2018 and Nov-Dec 2018 by GVC staff and SARC WASH team in coordination with MoWR,Water Board and Sewage Company and Wash Sub Sector Group in Aleppo Governorate(main focus of assessment:Jabel Saman,Al Bab,Tadaf,Dayr Hafir,Membji,Al – Khafsa, Maskana sub-districts)and Deir-Ez-Zor city.Areas were selected considering:sub-district severity scale(from 3 to 4 as per HNO 2019),returnees’ flows;GVC long-lasting presence in the field.The following information were considered:historical information on WASH system;analysis of secondary data(Water Board);analysis of qualitative data by other agencies(Reach, INSO)and Sector(WoS Wash Assessment 2018);infrastructure analysis on site by GVC Engineers;Staff field visits,data collection in the field.GVC has identified lack of/limited access to quality water related to the urgent needs to:1)Repair water infrastructures and restore functionality of alternative water sources;2)Increase quality and quantity of water;3)Raise maintenance skills;4)Raise affordability of WASH services;5)Increase storage capacity on HH level;6)Promote proper hygiene strategies.In particular Water supply in Deir- Ez-Zor Governorate is a major concern in terms of quantity and quality.In June-July 2018,the percentage of water trucking reached 60.01%; in Deir ez Zor city,pumping and treatment facilities were damaged by armed groups.Only 2 out of 6 are currently functional and,due to overloading,ensure low pressure and low quantity of water.Public network is not functional in many areas and the share of the delivered water per capita varies across city.Only 3 neighbourhoods rely only to the network while 16 rely on multiple sources.Controlled Water trucking service is active in part of 10 neighbourhoods while in the rest of the city people buy water from private suppliers and pull from private wells or directly from Euphrate.Quantity available per capita is hard to determine.Crosschecking data from different sources we estimate an average of 40 l/p/d with lower peaks of less than 20 litres in hard to reach neighbourhoods.To cope with the intermittent water delivery,HHs store water in unsafe recipients(drums and jerrycans).Key Informants (KIs) report that water have bad smell,strange colour and turbid in many neighbourhoods.This is due to low depuration,probable infiltration of the network and uncontrolled water sources.KIs report(where the network is not functional)waterborne diseases such as diarrhoeas(proxy indicator of contamination of water).Considering the size of the city and population trends,infrastructural rehabilitation are urgently needed to ensure safe access to water,namely to neighbourhoods currently not reached by the central water system.In Aleppo governorate,water trucking was 29,7% of all water sources in June-July 2018.In Hader area,due to disruption of Water network,HHs obtain water from wells located in the mosques and private houses.The water is distributed through pipes and private water trucking and quantity depend on the area.The WB confirms rehabilitation of main line is planned.In Khafsa area,the most compelling problems are the non-functioning of Tal Alshaer water tower and the structural damage of the pipeline.This creates a scattered water distribution.For instance,Tal Aswad and Khafsa are served 7 days/week while in Al Mamoura,network distributes water only 1 day/week. In Kherbet Elkhathraf, the pump is currently out of service and water comes from Al Assad lake by trucks(without control)3 times/ week.Different needs are found in the eastern suburbs of Aleppo,where water pump of Jibrin was damaged during the fighting for Aleppo Airport.The pumping station served 2 main villages and several facilities(including the only Mental Health hospital in Aleppo Governorate)and currently around 7000 HHs are experiencing a shortage of water.WB connected the two villages and the hospital through emergency lines but this cannot pump needed water to the villages.

Implementation of activities

1.1 Repair and rehabilitation of water systems: - Rehabilitation of 2 Water Pumping Station. In Deir-Ez-Zor, rehabilitation of 17 April Water Station Typical water station managed by Deir-Ez-Zor Water Supply & Sewerage Authority (DAZ-WSSA) located on the bank of the river Euphrates and, which is the main source of drinking water for the province. The intervention includes the pumping suction, sterilization process and boostering to the main distribution line serving 37,000 people. In Aleppo, rehabilitation of Jibrin Pumping station. The priority works are: Remove debris and rehabilitate/reconstruct buildings, Supply, installation and operation of 1 horizontal pumps, hydraulic accessories for the pumping house, connect electrical cables to operate the water plant, liquid Hypochloride injection devices, electric transformer, connect lighting and water safety systems, Diesel Power Generating Group 80 KW, Small rehabilitation works on distribution system to connect to the main city network. (60,000 people reached)- Rehabilitation of decentralized water network in Deir-Ez-Zor and Rural Aleppo. GVC will launch a procurement process in order to sign a framework agreement with a local contractor to provide the needed items in a timely manner. The supplier services will be mobilized by GVC in accordance with the Water Board following the needs identified. The foreseen interventions are pipes substitution and small repair to the network, in accordance of the need of Water Board.(20,000 people reached)1.2 Support to water quality assurance and operation and maintenance of water supply systems:- Establish of Water Safety Plan (WSP) and the Operational and Maintenance Plan (O & MP). In the particular situation of Deir-Ez-Zor and Jibrin, the system management activities will be performed by the Deir Al-Zour Water Supply & Sewerage Authority (DAZ-WSSA) and the Aleppo Water Board (AWB). For WSP: Create a map of the distribution system, Identify hazards, Develop and implement an incremental improvement plan, Monitor control measures and verify the effectiveness of the WSP, Monitoring the compliance of the water safety plan (WSP Manager, WB and NGO task). For O & MP: It is a combination of all the activities undertaken to restore or do a device, machine, building, etc., be in an acceptable condition (60,000 people reached)2.1 Distribution of essential WASH NFIs & Hygiene promotion- Purchase and Distribution of 10,000 Hygiene Kits (1 HK per HH) and 20,000 jerry cans 10l distributed (2 per HH): The kits will be distributed during the Hygiene Promotion Campaign (HPC) in order to provide a concrete support to these families in adopting proper hygienic practices. The kits will contain items for the entire family with a focus on the needs of women and children. The kits will be compliant with the standards adopted by Wash cluster. The distribution will target more vulnerable and displaced people in communities, especially the new IDP’s from the areas of Idleb, Afrin and Al Raqqa (50.000 people reached)- Implementation of the HPC. The HPC will be conducted by SARC volunteers in coordination with the GVC Staff. The campaign will be community-based and will provide both theoretical and practical actions through the use information materials that will be tailor-made. The main topic of the HPC will be the promotion of hygiene best practices in order to increase the awareness of the target population about diseases such as cholera, diarrhea and other waterborne diseases, emphasizing the importance of washing hands properly in order to reduce the spread of diseases. (50.000 people reached)2.2 Improved access to lifesaving/ emergency WASH facilities and services- Rehabilitation of WASH facilities in 12 schools in Rural Aleppo and Deir-Ez-Zor City (7,000 people reached)- Purchase and Distribution of Hygiene supplies for 12 schools in Rural Aleppo and Deir-Ez-Zor City (7,000 people reached)

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

ASSUMPTIONS a) Security context in Deir-Ez-Zor and Rural Aleppo targeted area remains stable b) Access to target areas is safe for staff and enterprises to conduct the foreseen rehabilitation and activities c) Access to infrastructure to repair is guaranteed by the presence of the Water Board d) The Ministry of Water Resources and the WB maintain the planned priorities in the areas and the emergency strategy adopted e) Internal return of people sensibly increases because of improved political and safety situation (mostly in Deir-Ez-Zor) f) Collaboration of the Authorities to set up criteria for the selection of the beneficiaries and to monitor the regular distribution of the Hygiene Kits and Jerry Cans. g) Local communities and other agencies/actors on the zones of intervention will be positive in relation to the services now available.RISKS: 1. Security situation deteriorates to the point to prevent the implementation of some of the activities; 2. Shrinking of the humanitarian and operational space in some intervention area due to military operation 3. Major change in demographic in the area of intervention due to population movements requires modification in the strategy 4. Major change in attitude of authorities and local partners toward the planned interventions. RISK MITIGATION STRATEGY:1. GVC ensures a continuous coordination with local partners (SARC) and international specialized agencies (UNDSS) in order to monitor the situation. GVC adopted a security plan and SOP in case of any major accident.2. Policy environment is monitored through the attendance to Cluster, Working Groups and Coordination Mechanisms.3. GVC monitors the demography in the area and liaise with other agency in the area.4. GVC ensures good relationship with authorities in the area, informing them of the planned activities. It ensures a transparent and accountable way of working. Plans of intervention (rehabilitation) are shared and discussed in order to increase acceptance.5. GVC ensures directly the monitoring and evaluation of the activitiesCONTINGENCY MEASURES1. Security alert system in place prevents risk and different routes or timing are adopted to assure the service.2. If it is impossible to access the area, it will be studied the remote management through SARC or the change of the area 3. Any unpredictable decision of MoWR, WB, on priorities to assure WASH services in the target areas will be discussed and solutions found according to the final objective of the project and to the improvement of the conditions of the beneficiaries
