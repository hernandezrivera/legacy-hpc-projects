Project Summary

The Anglophone crisis that have persisted for more than six years today has led to a humanitarian situation characterized by loss of lives, Abuses against civilians, physical attacks and forced displacements. Ordinary people are paying an unacceptable price, as parties to the conflict continue to violate international laws, attack hospitals, schools and critical WASH infrastructures, and commit rampant human rights violations, including gender-based violence against women and girls, boys and men.
In the North-West and South-West regions, the unstable security situation and persistent violence are exacerbating humanitarian needs. According to the United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) in June 2023, it was estimated that 9,418 person in these regions are displaced as a result of the violence, which led to the deaths of over 25 people, the destruction of homes, the closure of businesses and the cessation of various forms of livelihood activities.The displacement has further weakened the social structures necessary for providing a protective environment for children, adolescents, youth and vulnerable women. The displaced populations have no basic social services and lack critical supplies among which WASH needs were identified as priority in the humanitarian needs overview. The situation is a breeding ground for public health epidemics, and human rights abuses.
With the recent dynamic escalation of the conflict between government forces and non-state armed groups in the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon, The complexity of the conflict increases the vulnerability of the civilian population with disastrous humanitarian consequences which impact access to basic water and sanitation services and social cohesion. The massive displacement of the population, continuous attacks on property, limited access to basic services, and human rights violations are common experiences in the NW/SW regions. In quest to have access to WASH facilities, women, girls and children are particularly at risk of incidents of sexual and gender-based violence as well as abuse and exploitation. In parallel, national and community systems as well as social support networks have been weakened and disrupted. The humanitarian WASH needs is further compounded with frequent occurrences of natural disaster such flash floods, and outbreaks of epidemics such as cholera particular in the South West region. As of 27 September 2023, the South-West region had reported 83 cases with 1 death and in October 23, 2023, Tombel Health District has a total of 28 cases, and 5 deaths (CFR, 14.3). It is therefore imperative for a good WASH system to be developed in the communities with little or inadequate WASH services, so as to reduce the possibility of cholera outbreak and provide equitable and inclusive access to water, hygiene and sanitation. The humanitarian needs overview for 2024, spots out WASH needs as one of the first three major needs for crisis affected population which include access to portable drinking water, safe and dignify sanitation facilities, menstrual hygiene and WASH kits, capacity strengthening of community structures to ensure sustainability.
The response will seeks to address among others, long-term solution for cholera control which lies in universal access to safe drinking water and adequate sanitation. Actions will target environmental conditions which will include the implementation of adapted long-term sustainable WASH solutions to ensure use of safe water, basic sanitation and good hygiene practices in previous cholera hotspots, this intervention will equally prevent a wide range of other water-borne illnesses.The project in its response will prioritize the safety and dignity of the targeted women and girls, and will reduce the risk and exposure to gender based violence and other violence related to accessing WASH facilities. The project will be implemented in the South West and North West region.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The North West and South West regions have been experiencing serious ongoing socio political crisis that have stirred up humanitarian needs. The crisis has had serious negative effects on water, sanitation and hygiene with mostly women and girls highly affected, as they are the ones who access WASH facilities often. The breakdown of the WASH systems in the crisis affected regions therefore plays negatively on their access to portable drinking water, better hygiene and sanitation, affects their dignity. According to OCHA SITREP as of June, 2023, escalating violence in these two regions displaced over 9,418 people. The humanitarian WASH needs is further compounded with the health crisis of cholera outbreak which have hit the already crisis affected communities in the North West and South West regions. The SiTREP on cholera as of 27 September 2023, the South-West region had reported 83 cases with 1 death and in October 23, 2023, Tombel Health District has a total of 28 cases, and 5 deaths (CFR, 14.3%).. The influx of IDPs into relatively safe community has led to a significant increase in the scramble of life support services especially water resources which are not sufficient for the host population therefore placing these IDPs at a disadvantage in accessing basic critical services for their survival. Returnees equally find it difficult to have access to safe and dignified WASH services as they return to face the realities of starting all over in community completely destroyed water supply systems. This precarious situation exposes the IDPs and returnees to possible public health emergencies such as cholera . The major barrier measures to cholera require regular good hygiene practices, with emphasis on hand hygiene, clean environment and addressing predisposing cholera risk factors such as limited access to safe drinking water and dignified sanitation facility. It is imperative to have a good WASH system to enhance population resilience to the crisis, reduce the spread of cholera and other water-borne diseases as well as provide equitable and inclusive access to water, hygiene and sanitation services to the crisis affected population. This will be achieve through the construction/rehabilitation of gender sensitive and inclusive latrines, water points, hygiene promotion, life TV and radio awareness programs, door-to-door sensitization, WASH in schools campaigns, community-led total sanitation (CLTS) program, distribution of critical menstrual hygiene and WASH kits, training of local artisans and water management committee for sustainability, baseline, midterm and end line survey in accordance to a designed M&E plan for the project. WASH needs remains urgent as these two regions are in dire need of humanitarian supports because of the ongoing crisis that has damage most exiting water supply structure.
