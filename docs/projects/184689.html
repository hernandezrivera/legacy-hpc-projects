Project Summary

The objective of the project is to provide lifesaving emergency water supply, sanitation and hygiene promotion services for conflict, drought, flood and Covid-19 affected people, vulnerable host communities and people in disease prone areas, mainly women and children including strengthening of communities’ resilience to cope with the impact of emergencies and recurrent shocks. During the proposed emergency WASH project design and implementations women and girls will be consulted and WASH facilities will be designed and located to ensure the specific needs like privacy, safety and dignity of women and girls. In addition, the project is also targeting to reduce the risk of violence against women, children and vulnerable groups while accessing water and sanitation facilities by providing gender segregated and disability friendly WASH facilities and establishing water supply facilities within 500 meters’ distance of the settlements and sanitation facilities within 50 meters. The emergency WASH response will also be implemented by considering the needs of people with disabilities. People with disabilities will be represented in the community consultation process, beneficiary identification and on design and location of WASH facilities. In addition, a certain percentage of emergency WASH facilities will be constructed with disability friendly components like ramps and support facilities to cater for people with disabilities.

1. - Projects activities

Output 1: 1,252,028 emergency affected people (262,927 women, 225,366 men, 388,127 Boys, 375,608 Girls) have temporary access to adequate and safe water. Activity 1.1: Provision of water for 736,487 people affected by emergencies (AWD/Cholera, drought, flood and conflict) through water trucking/vouchers, O&M services (15L/person/day for 45 days) Activity 1.2. Provision of water for 515,541 people affected by emergencies (AWD/Cholera, drought, flood and conflict) through water source chlorination/disinfection and HHWTs. Output 2: 168,888 emergency affected people (35,467 women, 30,400 men, 52,355 Boys, 50,666 Girls) provided with access to appropriate sanitation facilities (single sex and equipped with hand washing facilities). Activity 2.1 Construction of 3,300 gender segregated emergency latrines with handwashing facilities for 96,507 people. Activity.2.2 Desludging and repair of an estimated 2,400 latrines / cesspits /septic tanks in institutions and densely populated IDP settlements, to benefit 72,381 people. Activity 2.3: Support clean up campaigns and/or cash for work solid waste management to improve the sanitary conditions in IDP settlements and marginalized communities. Output 3: 1,196,513 emergency affected people and vulnerable host communities (251,261 women, 215,374 men; 370,918 Boys and 358,953 Girls) provided with hygiene and sanitation promotion messages and emergency WASH supplies including Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) kits. Activity 3.1 Procurement, prepositioning and distribution of critical emergency WASH supplies and equipment including hygiene kits to meet WASH emergency needs for water point disinfection and household level water treatment to benefit at least 1,196,513 people. Activity 3.2: Training and deployment of 1,197 community hygiene promoters for hygiene promotion and sanitary surveillance at Households and health and nutrition facilities. Output 4: 178,683 people (37,523 women, 32,164 men, 55,391 Boys, 53,605 Girls) provided with reliable and sustained access to basic water services through rehabilitation and construction of new strategic water sources. Activity 4.1: construction of 12 new strategic water supply systems. Activity 4.2: Rehabilitation, upgrading and/or development of existing 48 water supply systems and water points. Activity 4.3: construction/upgrading of 72 shallow wells with hand pumps. Activity 4.4: Train/support community based and public private partnership management structures for 72 water supply schemes for sustainable water supply management Output 5: 40 schools and 80 health facilities provided with WASH Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) activities including provision of WASH facilities and hygiene promotion activities benefiting 176,000 people (36,960 women, 31,680 men, 54,560 Boys, 52,800 Girls) Activity 5.1: Construction of new or rehabilitation and upgrading of existing water and gender segregated sanitation facilities in 40 schools Activity 5.2: Construction of new or rehabilitation and upgrading of existing water and gender segregated sanitation facilities in 80 health and nutrition facilities Activity 5.3: Hygiene promotion and distribution WASH IPC supplies in 120 institutions (40 schools and 80 health and nutrition facilities) Output 6: Efficiency of Emergency WASH response National, Zonal and/or regional authorities, as well as partners, in targeted high risk areas/districts are prepared to deliver emergency response. Activity 6.1 Support WASH cluster team coordination and information management activities Activity 6.2. Support WASH cluster assessments at national, zonal, regional and district level with ensuring government involvement at all levels. Activity 6.3: Training of WASH cluster partners and government counterparts to improve knowledge and ability for emergency preparedness and response.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Risk 1- Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and services - Provide WASH assistance including water trucking and hygiene kits distribution in the affected populations settlements - Monitor climatic conditions and provide anticipatory WASH interventions in chronically affected areas to reduced displacement - Prepositioning of supplies close to target beneficiaries. Risk 2- Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. - Provide WASH facilities within accessible areas in the affected population settlements - Provide gender segregated sanitation facilities especially in IDP camps, schools and health facilities - Equal representation and consultation of women and girls in all WASH committees and selection of locations for WASH facilities, training of implementing partners staff on PSEA.
