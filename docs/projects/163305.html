Project Summary

The Ministry of National Education and Vocational Training convened the Group of Education Partners to share information and to organize an informal rapid assessment of the damages. A total of 32 schools – 18 in Djibouti City, 5 in Arta, 5 in Obock and 4 Tadjourah - suffered flooding of classrooms, sanitation facilities, playground and access. Working with partners, UNICEF will help to government to implement a multi-phased strategy to improve the levels of preparedness and response and to mainstream disaster risk-reduction approaches at policy, school and community levels. The immediate response is designed around procurement and distribution of supplies and services to restore access to quality and education in a safe and protective environment as soon as possible. This will include replacing damaged school kits for 9, 806 children and school uniforms for 8,050. The response will aslo replace damaged equipment and teaching materials.
