1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The aim of the proposed Activity, to be implemented through a consortium led by the IRC together with two partners (CADEPI and ALDEPA), to provide immediate humanitarian assistance to 10,572 displaced people and 5,464 host community members in Logone et Chari, Mayo Sava, and Mayo Tsanaga départements through an integrated multisectoral package of protection, multipurpose cash, and economic recovery assistance, while strengthening communities’ resilience and ownership of services. A key focus of the Activity will be a progressive handover of key Protection services to the IRC’s local partners and community-based organizations and networks, to strengthen communities’ resilience and ability to maintain service continuity for vulnerable people in the face of continued conflict, natural disaster, and displacement. The Activity will coordinate its approach with government authorities, other international and local humanitarian and development actors, and community development plans. Safe programming, the “Do No Harm” principle, and participative, community-based approaches will be mainstreamed across all project activities. In the Northwest and Southwest regions particularly in Momo and Manyu divisions respectively, the proposed MPCA activities are geared towards meeting the basic needs of newly displaced populations due to the crisis. The IRC will provide immediate humanitarian assistance via MPCA to a maximum of 677 displaced households (4,062 individuals (the IRC will support 4,962 individuals in the FN region)). The IRC will work with all actors involved in humanitarian and development sectors in these chosen locations and will monitor other areas and be ready with donor validation to intervene in other areas presented
