Project Summary

Intensive education centre to accommodate 200 students dropped out in Kafar takharim,.
The centre will contain 8 classrooms, 25 students for each classroom. Students will be provided with weekly activities such as citizenship - life skills in a safe area for children and provide weekly awareness raising sessions for parents during the project period.
There will be an ACER assessment test at first sorting students by their level and students will be sorted into two levels as follows:
• A Therapeutic Reading course which is developed and full adopted through Chemonics Foundation - Manahel Program.
• The UNICEF curriculum B as an approved curriculum for students who are dropped out of school.
The work will be carried out to bridge the educational gaps of the students and help them to restore the educational gabs and to enable them to enroll in formal education schools like their peers. School bags with stationery will be distributed to student during the project period.

4 - Needs

Because of the ongoing war and heavy shelling in the Syrian north by the Syrian regime, around 400,000 Syrians fled to the north. 5 - Implementation of activities

 MIDAD will run a fully equipped educational centre in Kafar Takharim. The centre will include 8 classrooms (200 students) to accommodate 25 students for each classroom. The duration of the project will be 8 months. M&E Mechanism : The monitoring and evaluation team depends on monitoring all activities from the start of implementation until the closure of the project and measuring the quality of the implementation of the activities and the satisfaction of the beneficiaries.So, our field team (monitoring and evaluation team) in Syria will provide us through the following reports and documents:• Photos and videos periodic (Monthly Media Report).• Monthly report on project developments.• A monthly financial report explaining the amounts issued with all the financial documents that have been disbursed. • Attendance timesheets for teachers, administrators and students with their mothers, (Beneficiaries).• Full financial and administrative documentation. The centre time will be five days like: (Saturday - Sunday - Monday - Tuesday - Wednesday). Students will be provided with a weekly session includes supportive activities about life skills and the importance of citizenship with educational follow-up by an educational expert for each classroom. 25 Mothers and 25 fathers will be provided with a monthly session of awareness raising and the importance of caring for children and integrating with society. There will be an ACER assessment test at first to sort all students by their level as follows: 1) A Therapeutic Reading course which was developed by the Chemonics Foundation - Manahel Program will be provided to the very low-level students to qualify them for three months, then to enrol them with their colleagues to have UNICEF curriculum B.2) The UNICEF curriculum B as an approved curriculum will be provided to middle-level students.A completion certificate at the end of the project will be provided to students who have passed the final exam by MIDAD.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

In fact, we had a current experience for the same project, where the issue of community participation in the region was taken into consideration by holding evening and morning community meetings for men in our office in Kafar Takharim,. Where they were listened to and shared their views on the issue of students who dropped out of education. The center is also equipped with facilities to assist students with special needs.Also, the issue of (the attendance of the elderly parents to the awareness raising sessions) was taken into consideration, in which a young man and a girl from our field team were assigned to help the parents and organize them to attend the monthly sessions (awareness raising sessions - focused discussion sessions).

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The challenge:1) Too large numbers due to unprecedented sudden displacement.2) Very heavy shelling targeting the project site. Level: 1(Middle.2) High. Mechanism of Solution:1)Seeking large place that accommodates large numbers above the target number in advance.2) Make the place of the project in a basement or inside caves.
