Project Summary

In the Cox’s Bazar area, there are about 14,000 humanitarian workers, operating in over 1,000 facilities, supporting over 810,000 refugees, spread over 34 camps.
Because of the limited availability of reliable commercial services, UN agencies, government representatives, NGOs and the entire ecosystem supporting refugees from within and around the camps struggles with even basic communication and security telecommunications services.
In 2020, the ETS will work with local and international partners to focus on the connectivity and communication needs of all entities involved in the operation, including Government, humanitarian workers, health centres, and the commercial network utilised for entitlements’ distribution (retailers).

Key project/performance indicators

The infrastructure will be depending heavily on self-standing towers erected in strategic locations in coordination among the humanitarian actors on the ground to avoid waste of resources by erecting two towers in the same location. It will start at the ModhuChara Logistics Hub with self-standing tower as the base of the infrastructure and then will have self-standing towers erected in various locations as CIC offices, distribution centres, common humanitarian space, ISCG hubs, key medical facilities and other locations deemed in need of data to be connected by the operational team on ground.
