Project Summary


The project aims to contribute to continuity of essential life-saving health services tonsure equitable access of people in need and improving quality of services through supporting convergent primary health care activities through activation of the pending pre-existed health facilities in Al-Bab district. and emphasis focused on five key vulnerable groups: displaced persons, children under five years (U5), women of reproductive age WRA (15-49), older persons (60+) and people with disabilities affected by the crisis in northern Syria. Amal Organization will continue to contribute to the improvement of the health status of pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and children under 5 years by strengthening lifesaving program through integrated services in the proposed PHC Centre. The project consists of a comprehensive primary health care center providing services though policlinic and specialty clinic as the following:1-Genecology and reproductive health clinic, through this clinical department there will be a gynecologist and midwife who will provide reproductive health care including pre-natal care, post-natal care, prevention and appropriate treatment of infertility, treatment of reproductive tract infections, and family planning counselling. 2-Pediatrics and Nutrition clinic3-General and internal clinic 4- ENT clinic 5-Ophthalmology clinic 6-NCD package services 7- Psychological Support services 8- providing essential and therapeutic medicines. Amal will coordinate with WHO cluster and local health partner (Health directorates and other Health actor) beside to other stakeholders (local authority and beneficiaries through planning implementation and evaluation of this project to ensure better health services responses and strengthen the health network at the targeted area. Moreover, dealing with covid-19 cases in terms of Coordination, planning, and monitoring, on the other hand Infection prevention and control, protection of the health workforce, and therapeutics and coordination of vaccination campaign, moreover coordination with Cholera taskforce for the needed actions points regarding awareness sessions and other related activities. Amal will coordinate with WHO cluster and SRH technical group to designing the project according to who criteria in terms of outputs activities and indicators. Moreover, Amal will coordinate with nutrition sector related of assessment and management of management of moderate acute malnutrition beside to management of severe acute malnutrition. Amal will continue cooperation with GBV sub cluster and MPHSS technical work group to coordinate of psychosocial support PSS activities designated at the beginning of the project. Amal is committed to robust MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning) and set the Indicators Performance Tracking Table (IPTT) which will be developed and tracked using the internal M&E Workplan developed at project start-up). The main database to insert and track the beneficiaries will be designed as well on Access Microsoft with ensuring clear gender markers, sex, age, disability, and displacement disaggregation will be considered). Amal will identify the timelines for data collection support information follow and analysis of progress against targets. MEAL personnel will conduct regular spot visits to the operation area on weekly basis. Also, during the monitoring field visits, MEAL staff will use a standard center checklist to be filled on monthly basis to identify any challenge/gap in the center. Moreover, beneficiary satisfaction and accountability surveys will be collected, and the findings will be shared monthly. More qualitative data will be collected by conducting Focused Group Discussions with the community. The MEAL team will share a follow-up report with the program on the same day of the visit to follow up with the monitoring observation. Also, will observe COVID-19 precautions

3 - Needs

Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply and irrigation systems. According to HNO 2022, The fragile health system in Syria continues to face concurrent emergencies and chronic challenges which affect the availability and quality of health services across Syria, as well as the physical and mental wellbeing of the population. With more than 164,000 confirmed cases and close to 5,800 deaths. Moreover, shortages in resources risk contraction of health access at time when the COVID-19 pandemic is on-going, other communicable diseases are on the rise, treatment access barriers persist, household level vulnerability is increasing. Essential inclusive health services–such as sexual and reproductive health and safe delivery, child health; care for communicable and non-communicable diseases; mental health and psychosocial support; must be maintained at every level of the health system and ensured to avoid negative outcomes in population mortality and morbidity. Amal organization will respond to the needs of PHC center in Al-Bab district the city in which at least 190541 most affected persons will benefit. Amal coordinated with the health cluster to support one PHC center.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risk (Health): There is a risk of attacks from armed groups during the implementation of project’s activities may lead to suspending health services. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: 1- Amal will choose place facilities away from possible armed forces 2-Amal will consult the community and potential beneficiaries about their preferences 3- coordinating with appropriate policingside. Protection risk (Health): There is a risk of the infrastructures of the facility is not adapted for vulnerable groups, so individuals and groups can't access and use them in safety and dignity. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: 1- Amal will make infrastructure adaptations such as ramps and railings to health facilities and latrines. Protection risk (Health): increased risk of covid-19 infection and transmission between health work personnel and beneficiaries, so may lead to high incidence of locally acquired, widely dispersed cases and imply high risk of infection for the general population. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: Ventilation of facility naturally, Physical distancing: Introduce measures to keep a safe physical distance, Hand hygiene promoting, Cleaning and disinfection of environmental surfaces on daily basis and using Personal protective equipment (PPE)
