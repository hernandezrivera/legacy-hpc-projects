Project Summary

The project seeks to contribute towards diversifying livelihood opportunities and improving the food and nutrition security situation of at least 42,500 people (22,100F; 20,400M) vulnerable urban and rural households disaggregated as follows Buhera rural 15,000 people, Chipinge urban 12,500 people and Chipinge rural 17,500 people. Zimbabwe has been going through economic challenges for over two decades, ranging from unemployment, poor economic performance, financial crisis and the liquidity crunch, which exacerbates the vulnerable situation thus reducing its capacity to invest in water and sanitation infrastructure. The COVID-19 pandemic, has further put a strain on the ailing economy making the population in both urban and rural areas more vulnerable. People living in poverty with or without access to water, sanitation and healthcare services face the greatest risk from the COVID-19 pandemic as they may not be able to observe prevention guidelines due to the limited resources and their need to continue with livelihood activities for survival despite the imminent threat. The combination of a market system approach to addressing food security, underpinned by a deep knowledge of the context and informed by resilience thinking is unique and allows Mercy Corps to ensure programming is meeting the needs of households today while building their ability to survive future shocks and stresses. Mercy Corps proposes to implement in the selected districts in order to leverage on the current work being implemented in Chipinge and Buhera districts funded by the EC. The proposed districts are at high risk of food insecurity, COVID-19 and other WASH related diseases. The EC Value project is working with smallholder goat producers promoting productivity and enhancing market access opportunities. This project will leverage on the current work by Mercy Corps on WASH and the EC Value which will phase out in 2023. The proposed project will support quarantine and isolation centres used in the target districts with food for the affected populations. The proposed project recognizes the roles that women, men, boys and girls play in families and society and activities have been tailored to accommodate these needs. Mercy Corps will build capacity of program staff as well as government and private sector partners in gender analysis tools, gender perception, and their influence during the implementation of this project the target districts.
