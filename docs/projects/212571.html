Project Summary

BRD will providing lending services for both M and W on favorable terms.
This project aims at firmly establishing work culture and principle of self-reliance in addition to providing job opportunities for people in need.
This project is directed towards providing an opportunity for those who have the skills, capabilities and willing to establish a private business.
BRD offer them liquid cash required to accelerate and promote their activities. This will help in creating new jobs and increase beneficiaries’ income.
BRD providing financial inclusion and lending services for 200 persons.
Returned installments that are paid by those persons will be given to other ones.
BRD aim to number of BNFs will reach 120 different from the 200 persons/borrower who supported before by the end of the year, using favorable loans system to be the total 320.
The persons who got the financial support will again this persons support other people by supporting them to reach total number of 320 people.
More than 76% of the Syrian citizens suffer from extreme poverty, 35% of them suffer from extreme poverty.
On other hand, number of evacuees from south of Idleb have reached more than 520K (CCCM cluster) evacuee from MAR2021 to DEC 2022
Most of those evacuees are settled in NWS in Idlib, Afrin and Euphrates shield.
This project is offering means and tools of revenue generating. Moreover, those projects will emerge people from poverty, improving the quality of living and enhancing resilience.
Total number of BNFs 1500 Individual
M 700, young youth male individual 50, W 700, young youth female individual 50. 50% IDPs and 50% HC, 10 PwDs
In implementation phase: During implementation of project, partners will ensure that project is introduced to local community and LA including goals, activities, duration, location, and the criteria of selecting BNFs. To ensure well introduction process, it will include: consultation sessions with LA, seminars with the local community, sessions with the BNFs, flyers, social media posts, road posters. Several project activities, for example: community assessment and community initiatives, are designed to be BNFs an essential part of outputs and implementation.
In addition to - Micro-grant committee is involved in PMA (Output 2.1) to avoid supporting projects that can make poor much poorer, PMA contributes to Do No Harm principle because it define real gaps in market and avoid projects that have no real potential in the market, it studies the competition in market, and bottle-neack in value chain that can cause lack of resources or create more supplies than demand in the market. PVCA, PRA, and PMA are going to guide the secio-economic intervention to avoid the projects that can harm the whole value chain.
- Creating referral system with clear selection criteria based on priorities of the community to avoid creating competition among workers by studying wages scale that can not disturb the wages in future.
Grant programing is taking in consideration social cohesion among IDPs and HCs to avoid creating any perception of unjust distribution of grants, giving fund to a certain group (e.g. IDPs, as they lost their assets after displacement) this can cause more harmful reactions from HCs who has the perception that IDPs are taking their opportunities.
functioning mechanism (BRD will contract with agency to deliver micro-credit which will ensure accountability and transparency infront of BNFs and Donor which will be those micro-credit ad-hoc entity, this entity will be as 3rd party in between BRD and BNFs and BRD will supervise about the delivering), those loans will be circulated after BNFs establish their works to be converted to other BNFs.
BRD will target at least 15% from the PwDs to get this support, to establish their own work, as well as they will be asked during the project life through a surveys, questionnaires and satisfaction surveys, as well as atleast 35% of BNFs will be women to empower them.
