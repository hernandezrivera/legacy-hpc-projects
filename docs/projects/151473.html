Project Summary

The project will provide life-saving and life-sustaining integrated multi-sectoral assistance to vulnerable communities of Awdal , Bakool, Banadir, Bari, Bay, Galgaduud, Hiraan,Lower Juba, Mudug, Nugaal, Sanaag, Sool and aims to reach a total of 550,453 IDPs and host communities contributing to 40% of the cluster target in the targeted geographies. Its objective is to improve equitable access to quality emergency and essential lifesaving health services for crisis affected aimed at reducing avoidable morbidity and mortality and Mitigate impact and respond to shocks to the affected population as a result of conflict and public health emergencies. This will be achieved by supporting the Somalia governments and communities to be able to delivery essential package of health free services to communities with acute humanitarian needs using developments programs as a platform for immediate responses. The required budget for the delivery of the project is USD 3 million.

Needs

The people of Somalia face significant hazards and conditions that result in overall poor health and wellbeing of individuals. In recent years an already weak health sector was further overburdened in 2017 due to the impacts of the drought, driving increased levels of malnutrition across the country. Poor environmental conditions and limited access to water and sufficient sanitation facilities were worsened by flooding and Cyclone Sagar in 2018 leading to outbreaks. People’s health and access to healthcare continues is worsened due to widespread violence, especially affecting the displaced and marginalized groups who face barriers to health care and exposure to disease with limited reserves. Many of the 2.6 million IDPs in the country face inequity in access to healthcare. Over 1.5 million people are extremely food insecure; particularly in northern parts of the country, and as such they are more at risk of serious illness.. The health system remains fragmented and under-resourced and is ill-equipped to provide the life-saving and preventative services needed. The household assessment survey conducted by REACH in the targeted regions shows high prevalence of Pneumonia, diarrheas, measles and injuries with reported to have difficulty in accessing health services with significantly increase in sending on health. The survey also showed that IDP households that have been displaced for protracted periods appeared to be more vulnerable, compared to those recently displaced, suggesting that humanitarian response to these displaced groups has been sporadic, with a higher proportion of recently displaced IDPs being targeted.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

IDPs are prone to exclusion because of host communities’ reluctance to integrate them and also constant eviction in the case of Mogadishu in particular but there is no significant obstacle in the full participation of IDPs, including from marginalized communities in the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the projects.• Though the assistance targets vulnerable IDP communities but does not discriminate of exclude host communities and hence the assistance or services provided will not aggravate the tensions between IDPs (including those from marginalized groups) and host communities or trigger violence against IDPs.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Undeniably there is a risk of possible exclusion based on gender, age and disability. The project will identify such risk groups at the beginning of the project and deliberately priories services to these vulnerable groups.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Beneficiaries and their communities face particular risks relating to armed conflicts and violence and as such this contributes significantly to internal displacements. We will uphold the humanitarian principle of neutrality and impartiality and will take the service as close to the affected communities as possible but the project will not be expected to aggravate the exposure of targeted communities to insecurity and other impacts of armed conflicts.
