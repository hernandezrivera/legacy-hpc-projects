Project Summary

The current crisis in the targeted areas of returnees is due to the exposure fo service facilities and institutions , including schools and PHCCs , to damage during military operations ; and because of the dense number of school students and PHCCs frequenters , poor services and inadequate sanitary units supposed to cover the needs . In the view of depletion of resources at governmental and local levels and the weak potential of the State in the education and health sectors to meet the needs of families and students to provide a safe , healthy environment , intervention in constructing and rehabilitating sanitary facilities for schools and PHCCs get more and more necessary and urgent . The project will support 16,274 returnee students (male and female) with the teaching staffs and health centers frequenters to obtain safe , sound WASH services by high standard quality . The project proposed by JF will rehabilitate sanitary units , add new sanitary units , rehabilitate and reconstruct septic tanks and provide safe drinking water sources through desalination stations and small electric filter set devices for schools and PHCCs .
The project will also provide water coolers and hand-washing points along with dissemination of acquainting messages and messages of health awareness and menstrual hygiene . It will establish health awareness and hand-washing workshops , distribute hygiene kits in primary schools and secondary schools for girls . The project will be carried out in the province of Salah al-Din (districts and villages : Samarra , Balad , Baiji , Dour ) by (30) schools for boys and girls (some co-educational)and (6) PHCCs .
The JF proposed project is designed to be flexible to address gaps and to meet expected needs . It will focus on primary school students , girls of secondary schools and PHCCs in Salah al-Din districts .
First Line Response
Rehabilitation of sanitary facilities : - in Salah al-Din areas : Baiji , Balad (Ishaqi and Dhuluiya) , and Samarra (Dijla, Qal'a, Mu'tasim) . JF team will contract with local builders , plumbers , painters , and transport workers for rehabilitation activities -under the supervision of engineers – which will last for (4) months targeting schools and medical centers lacking environmental services through the rehabilitation of available latrines , sewage system and septic tanks .
Installation of mobile latrines : in addition , JF will install mobile latrines in schools and PHCCs - where there are no toilets – with septic tanks , water tanks and plumping installations .
Second Line Response
Health promotion activities : JF will build community mobilization networks to conduct hygiene promotion initiatives and support the operation and maintenance of water and sanitation facilities . This activity will be implemented in primary and secondary schools in targeted locations . Hygiene improvement sessions will enhance hygienic conditions nd practices , especially those related to waterborne diseases .
One of the project's objectives is to provide cash for the affected families of the returnees by working on employing target groups within the targeted geographical area , which helps to reduce the level of unemployment which is considered as one of the factors that help to overcome the challenges .

Needs

Due to the dynamic counter-movement and bad , difficult conditions of the liberated areas inhabitants , it is necessary to deliver water and sanitation support to the returnees . JF assessments confirm the need for safe drinking water , rehabilitation for sanitation facilities and hygiene practices for schools students , high school girls , medical centers frequenters , and workshops to enhance personal hygiene of both students and staffs . JF will target primary school boys and adolescent girls of secondary schools and medical centers in Salah al-Din districts namely Balad , Baiji , Dour and Samarra where there are large numbers of returnees who do not have access to water and sanitation services . So far , there is only a few active parties to provide adequate water and sanitation support . The project will address the gaps related to water and sanitation services through : • Rehabilitation of sanitary units ,• Providing safe drinking water for students ,• Providing a safe and healthy environment for students ,• Providing a safe and healthy washing area ,• Separation of genders in the joint units ,• Dissemination of health awareness messages ,• Awareness of menstrual hygiene ,• Distribution of hygiene kits ,• Establishment of health clubs ,By the expected funding , JF will provide an intervention to respond to the immediate WASH demands of these returnees . JF will implement the proposed project in the targeted locations to provide the most direct improvement packages for comprehensive water , sanitation and hygiene assistance without any duplication or overlapping of activities .

Activities

As in the strategic objectives of Water and Sanitation Group , the second strategic objective regarding returnee families is the first target for the provision of integrated water and sanitation interventions (WASH in schools and health centers) to increase impact and coverage by rehabilitation of sanitary facilities , sewage tanks , installation of plumbing and water tanks , providing water coolers and electric water filter set , providing and installing mobile latrines in schools and health center where no toilets available . According to the proposed project target , the number of targeted schools is (30) and (6) health centers . Rehabilitation of sanitary facilities and construction of mobile toilets , installation of water filter sets and water coolers.As in the strategic objectives of Water and Sanitation Group , the strategic third goal for returnee families , article 3 related to the following objective is to promote practices of the Province progress by community manner , and to keep water conservation through appropriate approaches and community structures .Hygiene promotion activities will take place in primary and secondary schools : hand washing , water conservation and economization , method for cholera prevention through the formation of health clubs , appointing cleanness hygiene promoters among the teaching staff and the selection of women in secondary schools for the purpose of enabling them to manage clean menstruation .

Mitigation Measures

During JF's work in the education sector , it had rehabilitated sanitary units in (87) primary and secondary schools and provided safe water through its work with UNICEF and HIF overcoming obstacles and challenges . 1- It has high , good coordination at governmental and local level and with the education and health sectors ,2- Good coordination with security forces for the purpose of freedom of movement and access to targeted sites during work '3- Working after the end of working hours and in holidays ,4- Provide mobile sanitary units as alternatives for the site prepared for rehabilitation ,5- In co-educational schools , it works on gender separation in regard of using the sanitary units and hand washing points ,6- Provide integrated raw materials in all work sites through processing and transporting to sites ,7- Coordinate with the administration of schools , local councils and community representatives to mobilize efforts for the purpose of participating in the rehabilitation of the project through awareness and guidance and the benefits of their children to provide safe , healthy environment and to protect them from the danger of the spread of waterborne diseases ,8- The Organization has trained cadres in the field of health awareness and trained competent of both genders . The gender aspect of the work will be considered to be more acceptable in the delivery of awareness and guidance messages .9- Various gender vulnerabilities and needs will be addressed to ensure that humanitarian standards are met and will ensure gender-sensitive programming . For example , promotion messages will be appropriately targeted for both sexes , recognizing the differences between male messages and female ones to ensure that users feel safe and comfortable .

How is your project accountable to affected population

The entire project has been developed mainly according to the needs of the affected population through previous WASH programming in Iraq . JF has developed a robust set of standard monitoring tools to ensure effective implementation of the proposed program activities . Regular activity reports , attendance and registration databases and surveys will be completed by JF staff to document trends , results and satisfaction of JF program beneficiaries . Monitoring and evaluation team also provides weekly monitoring reports to their immediate managers indicating their visits and observations and action points in coordination with the program team .JF practices downward accountability and thus involve beneficiaries at all levels of the project cycle ranging from needs assessment and project design to project end . It is within JF's strategic framework to enhance beneficiary participation in any decision-making process that affects their lives . During the implementation of the project , a mechanism called participatory review will be developed where beneficiaries explain their level of satisfaction in providing services and participation in joint monitoring activities , allowing their inputs to be integrated to improve the quality of project implementation . The beneficiaries of the current project will participate in the needs assessment . Qualitative and quantitative information will be collected from the population of the selected area . During the implementation of the project , the voices of the participants , students , teaching staffs and government authorities will be heard directly if there is any guidance from participants and people of the region . There will be a survey to get feedback from participants and residents . There will be a note box for students and teachers if they have any supplement or note about the project activities . All project activities have been developed and will be implemented in accordance with the principles of non-harm . The Organization is well aware of the principles of non-harm .
