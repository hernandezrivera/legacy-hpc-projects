Project Summary

The project will support the affected population in communities within Demsa and Numan LGAs with Basic hygiene and Dignity packs, such as water storage, soap, bedding, sanitary towels, sanitary pad, potty, kettle, construction/rehabilitation of water point, hygiene sensitization and distribution of menstrual hygiene kits to adolescent girls and women.
The beneficiaries of the project include the returnees, IDPs in host communities, vulnerable groups, women and youths in the communities.
The project seeks to also integrate and link the beneficiaries with other opportunities outside the project scope.
