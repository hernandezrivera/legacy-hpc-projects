Project Summary

Our response will focus mainly on WASH where we seek through this response to reduce the risks of the Covid 19 pandemic among the already affected people. Our strategy depends on innovative intervention to confront the impacts of Covid 19 and limit its spread and its effects on the vulnerable people, where the water, sanitation and hygiene package is designed to limit the spread of the Covid 19 and change the wrong and unhealthy hygienic practices and provide the needed personal items that will protect people and enhance their personal hygiene.
The project aime to re-provision and expansion of the already existing integrated, life-saving WASH programming of Silk Road in the targeted locations and broaden to other communities. Silk Road seeks to implement strategies that increase the availability and accessibility of water for women, girls and other at-risk groups (Follow Sphere standards) as well as promote the active participation of women, girls, and other at-risk groups in all WASH assessment processes (Especially assessments focusing on the satisfaction and accountability to the beneficiaries).
The approach that we will follow in implementing WASH intervention to achieve the desired goals will be based on providing the needed safe drinking water to people as well as support and manage waste collection systems to maintain a safe and clean environment in addition to distributing hygiene personal supplies (Soap -750 gr per famil per month) that helps reduce the spread of the Covid 19 epidemic. These activities will be accompanied by awareness-raising activities that will be provided by the hygiene promoters who will be assigned in each targeted communities(Two in each one). During this period of support (Operation of the water systems), a cost recovery system will be established to ensure continuous cash flow required for running the systems during and after the project (Only the first two months of the project will be supported 100% while the others will be partly supported), this will involve a study on a suitable tariff rate that takes into account the dwindling financial capacity of the benefiting population and will be based on focus group discussions with a representative group of the community including members of local councils of targeted communities.
Efforts will be made to especially improve the quality of WASH services will be provided in this project, to reach international standards on both qualities of services, and address reported GBV issues and scale up assistance in terms of emergency response or receiving more IDPs to the project areas.
Hygiene activities will include social and behaviour change communication in target to reinforce positive hygiene behaviours among the target communities and within the household to reduce the spread of COVID 19 where these activities will be supported with the distribution of soap. Hygiene promotion activities will centre on COVID 19 awareness massages, the safe water chain such as water storage and household water treatment, hand washing and personal and prevent water and vector-borne diseases.
Applying the cost recovery system and partial support system will increase the efficiency and effectiveness of the fund.

4 - Needs

The economic crises and the negative results of the spread of the Covid-19 pandemic, the disruption of business in many sectors, the depreciation of the Syrian pound against the USD, in addition to the burdens of the winter season besides the lack of humanitarian support, all these factors prove the urgent needs to support local communities, enhance their resilience, and help them to continue in providing basic services such as water and waste management, especially with the limited fund and the difficulties that they are facing to apply the cost recovery system in light of very difficult economic conditions.The most common problems and challenges in terms of Water supplyDifficulty in securing running costsDifficulty in collecting fees from vulnerable families, especially in light of the very poor economic situationIncrease the prices of fuel in the last two months.Silk Road still follow up on the situation in these areas to get more results and information about applying the cost recovery system there. However, the information shows that the displaced and vulnerable families did not recover sufficiently to be able to contribute to the collection system, especially with the high cost of fuel, which makes the proposed amount for each family not less than 3.5 USD per month, and yet it is not adequate to secure 100 % of costs (Best estimation to cover 80 % of the total costs assuming that All families will pay.In general, hygiene materials are available in the local shops in most of the areas and it is available to the population but weak purchasing power may contribute to the limited access of many families to these materials.

5 - Implementation of activities

The operational period of the water stations will be 6 months as mentioned and once we supply the needed consumable materials ( Fuel + Hypochlorite sodium +Engine oil and spare parts etc we will start pumping water in January 2021 which will include Conducting the regular water quality testing at the household level in all targeted locations).We will provide all needed fuel in the first and second month of operation ( 100 % of support), while we will provide 75 % of needed fuel in the third and fourth months of operation, 50 % of needed fuel in the fifth and sixth months of operation.During this period of support, a cost recovery system will be established to ensure continuous cash flow required for running the systems, this will involve a study on a suitable tariff rate that takes into account the dwindling financial capacity of the benefiting population. The cost recovery system that will be established will be utilized to cover the rest. To operate the water stations in the best picture, Silk Rod will support the wages of 21 Operators and technicians for 6 months. Distribution of Hygiene items for one-off during the project that aims to increase access to hygiene kits and contributing to decreasing risks of Covid 19, diseases related to drinking water and poor hygiene practices in the targeted locations.Delivering hygiene promotion activities through 4 hygiene promoters ( Two female ) will be recruited for 6 months through a door to door visits. Those promoters will be guided by :Support the municipality and waste management system in the targeted locations for 6 months through the provision of regular rehabilitation and maintenance of the 10 tractors and provide needed diesel fuel and daily workers.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion; Timing and process of distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV (Likelihood high- Impact high). Mitigating Measure:Promote Establishment of the adequate management plan; Promote involvement /engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback from different groups on the package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among partner staff.2- Distribution of expired unhealthy items (Likelihood medium - Impact high).Mitigating Measure:Establish and monitor expiry date of supplies prepositioned in the warehouse, ensure relevant storage provision and carry out timely disposal of damaged/expired items. Strengthen end-user monitoring.3-Quality and/or quantity not adequate as per humanitarian standards (Likelihood high- Impact high).Mitigating Measure:Ensure provision of safe water as per country Water Quality standards where possible and establish regular water quality monitoring including water source, water trucks and end-user samples; Collect water from safe sources certified by water authorities or local councils.Ensure and comply with agreed context /SPHERE standards (minimum humanitarian standard at 15 litres/person/day) and establish water quantity tracking & monitoring system involving beneficiaries;4- Health risk associated with garbage removal (Likelihood high- Impact low).Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.
