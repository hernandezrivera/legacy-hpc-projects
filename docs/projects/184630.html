Project Summary

The project seeks to improve well being and reduce threats to health and lives of 34,600 vulnerable persons including agro-pastoralists, IDPs and people living with disabilities in the most affected villages that have been hit by recurrent severe droughts in Awdaal, Woqooyi Galbeed and Togdheer Regions. With the aim of reducing vulnerability to life threatening AWD associated with water scarcity and poor hygiene practices. The project will rehabilitate high yielding strategic groundwater sources such as boreholes and shallow-wells. The exercise will include protection of water sources from possible contaminations, installation of solar pumping system to cushion vulnerable populations from generator fuel costs. The project will also extend pipeline to targeted villages to reduce walking distances in a bid to reduce vulnerability of women to protection issues. The project will also conduct regular water quality monitoring and treatment in a bid to increase access to safe and sustainable water. In areas without permanent water sources, emergency water trucking will be conducted as a temporary measure as permanent solution is being sort.
The project will construct low cost communal latrines in IDP camps and targeted villages to increase the current low latrine coverage ratio to reduce open defecation. In collaboration with community leaders and ministry of health, the project will jointly select community based hygiene promoters, train them and deploy them to disseminate good hygiene practices messages in the targeted villages. To strengthen the adoption of good hygiene practices, the project will distribute hygiene kits in targeted IDP camps and selected villages where poverty does not allow for any purchase of hygiene items.
For water provision, water quality hygiene and sanitation, the SPHERE standards are the ultimate benchmark. For mainstreaming protection ,inclusion and gender, the project will refer to specific HRP guidelines. Environmental impact, beneficiaries selections, employment and other issues will strictly follow the government regulatory framework. In particular the cooperation with the Ministry of Water Resources Development and NADFOR will be constructive and close.
Welthungerhilfe is basing the proposed action on twenty years of water provision linked to all other elements of WASH. This linking refers both to technical excellence as well as to sensitivity to community needs and dynamics for sustainability of the installed technical solutions and learnt content.
Emergency WASH projects have always and will be linked to long-term development engagement. Under the umbrella of "Sustainable WASH Services" (SWS) all humanitarian WASH activities will be part of a long term system, by which communities, government authorities and private sector complement each others' strengths for sustainability of WASH services.
Under the proposed interventions, rehabilitation and construction of strategic water sources will benefit 26,000 persons, emergency water trucking will benefit 1,500 persons, sanitation interventions will benefit 8,500 persons, hygiene promotions will benefit 29,000 persons while distribution of hygiene kits will benefit the same 29,000 persons targeted above.

The project will ensure trekking distance to the nearest water point is reduce through pipeline extensions to reduce vulnerability of women and girls.The project will register all targeted beneficiaries prior to implementation to ensure that all targeted beneficiaries are reached and receives equal share during implementation.
