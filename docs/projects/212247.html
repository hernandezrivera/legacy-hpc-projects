Project Summary

•Needs.
At HNO 2024 FAS PIN is 15.4 million people. Agricultural production, still does not meet the full national need, the drivers of low agriculture production include a continuous deteriorating economic situation, a decline in both the exchange rate and purchasing power of most people, high prices of agricultural production inputs and low quality of available seeds, weather anomalies, water scarcity, destruction of irrigation systems and canals, scarcity of energy supplies, especially fuel, and electricity for various activities, which impacts on supply chains and transportation costs, increasing the production costs. The water scarcity, damaged irrigation infrastructure has forced most farmers, especially in Rural Damascus and Rural Aleppo, to rely on surface wells to restore their production thus their income source. This will negatively impact groundwater decline and depletion, leading to high pumping costs.
• Intervention, Implementation of activities.
Output 1: Small-scale irrigation infrastructures in the targeted governorates are assessed and light rehabilitation is undertaken, through:
1) select locations and target people based on vulnerability, food security need, level of irrigation damage and agroclimatic conditions
2) Assess the irrigation infrastructure damage and needs, as well as water resource availability.
3) Priority irrigation infrastructure rehabilitation:
- Rehabilitation of the main and subsidiary irrigation channels.
- Rehabilitation of some collective irrigation wells
- Promote with certain control measures the use of alternative source of energy
- Maintenance of some closed and pressurized irrigation networks.
- Maintenance of selected drainage canals
- Maintenance and rehabilitation of irrigation canals siphons
- Pilot fields for modern irrigation.
4) M&E the project activities.
Output 2: Capacity of technical staff and stakeholders including farmers strengthened to increase water use efficiency and establishment of Water Users Associations WUAs, through:
1) Training selected beneficiaries on, water use efficiency and water–crop requirements, installation and operation of drip irrigation systems and solar panels.
2) Establish the WUAs.
3) Training on operation and management of water schemes/infrastructure and water crop requirement
•Protection Risk Analysis
1) Risk: Beneficiaries being targeted due being selected for project/Risk of community tension arising from beneficiary selection. Mitigation: different categories of affected population based their specific needs and clear selection criteria agreed upon by local committees.
2) Risk: Inclusion and exclusion errors. Mitigation: Establishing a local beneficiary selection committee with equitable representation of vulnerable groups; putting in place clear and precise guidance on beneficiary selection criteria and process; ensuring effective communication for guidance in local language.
3) Risk of diversion of inputs/assistance. Mitigation: Establish a community-based beneficiary selection and community-based monitoring.
