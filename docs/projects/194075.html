1 - Needs

The humanitarian situation in Sudan after one year from military coupe remaining complicated addition to that the conflict between Sudanese Army Forces(SAF) and Rapid Support Forces(RSF) the result, increased number of death, injures as well as economic crisis already been problem for people, add to that tribal conflict, deterioration of general services, increased human right violation and most people in conflict zone become living in hight risk. West Darfur state considering one of Darfur state witnessed inter communal conflict and the result many people moved from their villages and become living in temporary gathering side in El-Geneina town as displacement people most of them is women, children and adolescent and they are depending on their needs from humanitarian organizations and other UN agencies. according Humanitarian Need Overview(2023) indicated that, the most four significant issue identified are conflicts, disaster associated with natural hazard, disease outbreak and economic deterioration. HNO estimated that, a total number of Sudan population is 49.7 million people include 0.93 are refugee, the people in need are 18.1 million and it segregated by sector, age & gender, the people who in needs for protection services remain increased and marked hight figure 13 million people. in wast Darfur OCHA reported that aboutthe capital of West Darfur. All 86 gathering sites for displaced people throughout Ag Geneina have reportedly been burnt to the ground, forcing over 85,000 people into secondary displacement in neighbourhoods in the northern parts of the town. Many are sheltering with relatives and friends, while others are taking refuge in public buildings or staying in the street. according to Monitoring Report in June(2022) the needs of child protection affected by conflict continue is become common with worrying accident being killing, maiming and sexual against children particular in west Darfur, and child who in needs estimated about 3.2 million. The deterioration of security situation in west Darfur increased the risk of GBV for women and girls and other vulnerable people whose movement and access livelihood, health and other services has been restricted, lack of privacy space for displaced women and girls socialize and interact is a concern and additional safe spaces in needed for them. Dispute the risk of GBV is increased there is increased reluctance to report GBV cases. Engaging and enhancing the role of CBPNs, which are vital entry points for the referral of survivors to available services and access to GBV-related information. The GBV services remains much needed in West Darfur due inter-communal conflict which took placed different time ago as well as militia attacks and those conflict made gap in GBV needed to be addressing such as Clinic Management of Rape(CMR), Psycho-Social Support (PSS), and Cause Management. Also the number of functional Community Based Protection Network(CBPN) and Women and Girls Safe Spaces (WGSS) offering GBV services are limited. APDO will work with GBV sub- sector working group to strength GBV services and establish a new CBPNs in west Darfur.
