Project Summary

Selection of the project participants for all project activities will be done by the formed project committees whom will be trained (committee in each targeted sites comprised of 20 each) and in-consultation with other project secondary partners (local authorities, government technical bodies, community leaders, NGOs operating in the locality). An onsite orientation technical guidance and demonstration will be done by skilled project specialist provided by PORD in every location within the operation areas. A concurrent monitoring and periodic data collection with regard to the set indicators will be conducted, and depending on the indicators, frequency of data collection will vary from daily to monthly. The primary sources of data collection will be versus project indicators and their targeted figures, within emphasis regarding the required project quality such as formed based project committees or trainees, training reports, mass session attendants list. Objectively field observations, project monthly reports, and continued dialogue with communities all which will be undertaken systematically by project staff. This data once collected will form the basis for periodic assessments of the project on a monthly basis, thereby allowing production of accurate and consistent reports as required by project stakeholders. The project sources of evaluation will be through the information that will be conducted from project assessments and evaluation that will be conducted during project execution and at the end of the project using a format similar to the project secondary stakeholders, but more targeted to the current issues. The survey will measure a limited range of indicators that relate to this project impact; these will be conducted in collaboration with project partners. During the project period PORD team will continually work and coordinate with the project related state Ministries, local authorities, community leaders and INGOs project related partners for consultation and share project information. The program will be evaluated according to outcomes and impact. The assessment/survey takes around one month in planning and execution, post assessment period prior to final report release can be two weeks to allow for data entry, and initial drafts to be produced and edited. for Additional conflict Effected in Jabal Moon Locality villages are WASH and Environment PORD Will Work 1. 6 - Protection mainstreaming
6.1 - Does the project incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity for the beneficiaries?

4=Fully in place

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

PORD strategized to manage any risk during the project implementation through provision of intention on the project to be set on the exposure to the uncertain situations. The proposed project associated with various opportunities and threats at the same time. For instance water and sanitation project will bring water availability and a health relief to the community which will be perceived as a positive impact, however the uncertainties encountered and the pending risks will be counted as threats to the community and dangerous to human health. The provision of good water and sanitation to the community leads to poverty alleviation and improve productivity. Community can decide on corporate social responsibility that help to build the social dimension by demonstrating corporate action in order to meet social and environmental expectations of the stakeholders to maintain future social corporation. This also increases public participation in the community-based project and supports the decision making on what suits the community. Therefore PORD will provide concurrent monitoring to and an overview over the whole project, to identify problems which are causing damage to the water and sanitation project. Provision of effective communication between project PORD personnel to the successful implementation of project. Project manager will tract the internal communication to ensure project deliverables to make ends meet.
