Project Summary

The project seeks to ensure adequate access to legal remedies through provision of legal assistance and advice to communities, capacity building of Law enforcement and law implementing agencies, as well as services for Person with Specific needs (PWSN) and strengthen the communities through community based structures and self-reliance systems within urban settlements through community volunteers and to develop referral pathways with stakeholders and to raise awareness about Sexual and Gender based violence and Child Protection.

1- Project Activities

SHARP will focus on the below mentioned prioritized activities of HRP through its three currently operational offices in Mardan, Kohat & Peshawar and will establish two Satellite Protection Units in district Swat and Dera Ismail Khan. These units will serve as a community safe places where protection services are available under one roof. These units will enhance the capacity of the organization to reach out to the far flung areas to provide first hand protection assistance to the vulnerable families in need. General Protection:1. Comprehensive case management and coherent referrals and helplines- awareness raising on available services including referrals to service providers for provision of targeted and specialized protection services; 2.Legal advice/counseling/interventions/sensitization and capacity building;3. Protection Monitoring and Protection Assessments; 4. CBP preparedness and protection risk mitigation; 5. Protection awareness raising and prevention messaging; GBV: 1. Provision of psycho-social support services to GBV survivors and at-risk groups (in person or tele-psychosocial support services); 2. Prevent and protect SGBV issues through community-based protection mechanism; 3. Establish GBV multi sectoral coordination and referral pathways and case management services; Child Protection:1. Provision of Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) Services to the most vulnerable children (girls and boys); 2. Establish and support the operations of a case management and referral mechanism to ensure access of vulnerable children to basic services; 3. Disseminate information and promote dialogue among families and communities on stigma and Violence Against Children (VAC) and GBV prevention.
