Project Summary

UNHCR will continue pursuing a protection and solutions strategy that builds on the incremental gains of previous years. This will be achieved through enhanced partnerships and collaborations with government and Non-Governmental Organisations. In the context of the Comprehensive Refugee Response Framework (CRRF), the operation will continue to pursue partnerships with traditional and non-traditional donors, private sector players and financial institutions. UNHCR Zimbabwe strategy for 2020/21 will follow the implementation of the Global Compact on Refugees (GCR) to “ease pressure on host communities”.

1 - Activities

Fair Protection Processes and Documentation Registration is conducted jointly by UNHCR and the Government of Zimbabwe (GoZ). GoZ conducts the initial registration of asylum seekers upon arrival in Tongogara Refugee Camp. Individual protection documentation is availed to each refugee/asylum seeker receiving an ID card, Temporary permit, refugee status (if granted by Zimbabwe Refugee Committee (ZRC) and birth certificates for children born in Zimbabwe. Refugees who qualify apply for the Machine Readable United Nations Convention Travel Documents in accordance with Guidelines agreed with the Commissioner’s Office. UNHCR will support eh Government of Zimbabwe in the above activities. Population has optimal access to reproductive health and HIV services The WASH sector remains an integral part of Tongogara Refugee Camp programming as it is the gateway for preventative health measures. There is need to continuously upgrade , maintain and improve the available water, sanitation and hygiene services to ensure alignment to the current increased populationServices for persons with specific needs strengthened Persons with specific needs (PSN) face unique challenges such as stigma and discrimination, abandonment, neglect and low self-esteem. This affects their participation in community activities. There is also a limited access to services due to limited disability friendly infrastructure. PSNs have however received a wide range of support which included assistive devices, occupational therapy, counselling, cash assistance and material support. UNHCR will support the procurement of assistive devises for PSNs. Food security improved UNHCR partners with World Food Programme (WFP) to ensure adequate food security for refugees and asylum seekers. UNHCR / WFP will ensure adequate food security to ensure that SDG 2 on zero hunger is achieved. World Food Program (WFP) provides cash based interventions and in-kind food assistance to refugees and asylum seekers in Tongogara Refugee camp (TRC). The need for humanitarian aid remains relevant due to the peculiar Zimbabwe policies on refugees which has reservations on employment and freedom of movement (encampment policy). Livelihood opportunities are also limited due to the harsh weather elements in the Lowveld region 5 where Tongogara is located. UNHCR with the support of WFP will ensure that refugees and asylum seekers receive at least 2,100 kCAL per person per day. Population has optimal access to education By end of 2018, school enrolments at Tongogara Refugee Camp were at a combined 3,652 students for the early childhood development, primary and secondary schools. Enrolment of pupils at the early childhood development centre, primary and secondary schools stood at 21 per cent, 75 per cent and 49 per cent respectively. However, despite the efforts to improve access to education services, many children especially at early childhood (79%) and secondary schools (51%) lack access to school facilities and remain out of school. UNHCR will ensure construction of more school at Tongogara refugee camp to ensure safe learning environment of children in TongogaraShelter and infrastructure established, improved and maintained Shelter in the camp remains inadequate and in high demand against a background of a growing population. Unavailability of shelter exposes persons of concern to protection risks such as SGBV as a result of living in overcrowded shelters and exposure to harsh environmental conditions
