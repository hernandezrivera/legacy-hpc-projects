Project Summary

Maram has designed this proposal to provide Shelter and NFI assistance for the most affected people in NW Syria to upgrade their living conditions through:
- Distribution of essential NFI items 380 NFI kits.
- Installation and construction of 380 dignified Shelters/temporary transitional shelters (target: 1,900 individuals/ 380 HHs - 380 Shelter units)
- Improving the Site Infrastructure/ HH level improvements implemented (target: 1,900 individuals /380 HHs) through land preparation, drainage system, water & Sanitation networks, and lighting poles in addition to all relevant infrastructure needed.
Maram has designed this project to be in line with the priorities identified in the 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan, the proposed project intends to enhance dignified living and address acute needs for 380 HHs/ 1,900 individuals living in Idleb with different ages and vulnerable cases PWN (23 % men, 25 % women, 26 % Boys, and 27% girls ) taking into consideration Gender and age diversity through integrated Shelter and NFI interventions
Maram Foundation has full access to the mentioned area and is implementing several Emergency Response Projects.
The Project aims at upgrading the living conditions of the targeted IDP site by improving their infrastructure, this will be achieved by implementing the proposed activities to improve th eliving condition for 1900 IDPs in IDP site.
All activities within the projects will be according to the Shelter cluster objectives and will be designed according to the SPHERE standards.
Furthermore, the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially “Do no harm” throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls.
During this project, Maram will consult with the community and individual IDPs taking into account the speciﬁc needs of each group. Maram Foundation also will involve the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during the community outreach plan.
Summary, this project aims to reach 1,900 individuals targeting most vulnerable men, women, boys and girls and prioritize family members with high vulnerabilities according to the beneficiary selection criteria (female/single/child-headed households, IDP status, no source of income and support, people with disabilities, elderly,...etc.).
Maram always seeks to high-level coordination with all parties involved in any project (SNFI cluster, Donors, local authorities, other NGOs, and other related parties) to avoid any duplication. So for the activities included in this project and during the designing phase, Maram will coordinate with all the relevant parties and select the locations according to the gaps, which means that there will not be any duplication during implementing the proposed project activities.

3 - Needs

Based on the HRP 2022 report "It is estimated that of the 6.92 million people displaced, 3.73 million reside in substandard, damaged, and/ or inadequate shelter conditions, primarily being driven to move to such locations due to hostilities, security concerns, and economic deterioration. Of those displaced, 2.02 million people live in sites of last resort mainly within the Northwest and Northeast of Syria.Shelter adequacy issues stem from a range of root causes."Rapid and immediate lifesaving services remain a critical priority for many IDPs residing in the camps, encouraged Maram team to conduct a Rapid Needs Assessment to identify the Shelter and NFI needs.The reported percentages in the RNA conducted by Maram reflect the poorly available services of shelter as well as the huge gaps in the infrastructure in the IDP sites, particularly in NWs Syria.MF seeks to save lives, provide dignified life, uphold rights and promote access to safe and more privacy shelter through the provision of SNFI assistance in particular in 2023, including rehabilitation of the IDP Site / improve the site infrastructure, providing and installing dignified shelter units instead of the tents, and distribution NFI kits for 1900 individuals/380 HHs living in one IDP site in Harim community.
