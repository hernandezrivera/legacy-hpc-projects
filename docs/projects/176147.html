Provision of timely child protection and access to psychosocial support including MHPSS services in Pibor County, Jonglei State, Nasir County, Upper Nile State and Rumbek North County, Lakes State.

Basic Info

Project Name

Provision of timely child protection and access to psychosocial support including MHPSS services in Pibor County, Jonglei State, Nasir County, Upper Nile State and Rumbek North County, Lakes State.

Start Date

01/01/2021

End Date

31/12/2021

Project Summary

CHADO will provide Child Protection Case Management including identification, support and referral of children with protection concerns for appropriate support to extremely vulnerable 120 boys and 80 girls, Child protection outreach and awareness raising through community Based Protection Mechanisms targeting 11,266 boys, 10,864 girls, 10,059 women and 8,048 men. CHADO will establish 5 child friendly feedback, reporting/complaint mechanisms to enhance accountability to children and their caregivers.
CHADO will also provide Child Protection awareness through outreach activities targeting 500 students, 100 teachers, 50 Parents Teachers Association (PTA) members to challenge cultural practices, behaviors and social norms that constitute Child Protection issues or GBV and in addition provide capacity building trainings on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and Psychological First Aid (PFA) and establish mechanisms to monitor and report PSEA in the school environment to 50 teachers and NGO workers. CHADO will provide Focused and non-focused Psychosocial Support interventions and preventive messaging to children and their caregivers as well as preventive messaging and further provide Psychosocial Support (PSS) and Life Skills interventions to extremely vulnerable 20,000 children and their caregivers, CHADO will also strengthen 5 community-based structures/networks and outreach activities.
Pibor County has an estimated population of 222,297, the PIN are 73,319 people and Child Protection Sub cluster targets 51,323 people. CHADO will be targeting 25% of the Child Protection target which is 12,830 people (3,207 women, 2,567 men, 3,464 girls and 3,592 boys). Among them are an estimated 15% (1,540) of people living with disabilities. Similarly, Nasir County, with an estimated population of 286,628 people has 64,308 PIN and Child Protection Sub Cluster targets 32,154 people. CHADO will be targeting 35% of the Child Protection Sub Cluster target which is 11,253 people (2,813 women, 2251 men, 3038 girls and 3153 boys). Among them are estimated 1,688 people living with disabilities. Likewise, Rumbek North has an estimated population of 70,949 people, and the PIN are 35,177 and Child Protection Sub Cluster targets 64,624 people. CHADO will be targeting 15% of the Child Protection Sub cluster target which is 9,694 people (2,424 women, 1,939 men, 2,617 girls and 2,714 boys). Among them are an estimated 1,454 people living with disabilities. CHADO is currently on ground in Pibor, Nasir and Rumbek North implementing GBV and CP in Pibor and Nasir and Communications for Development in Rumbek North which has child protection components.

1 - Needs

Years of conflicts coupled with flooding for 2 consecutive years and the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic have made worst the humanitarian situation in South Sudan. As the newly independent nation continues to struggle with the implementation of the revitalized peace agreement, its delay continues to impede on the efforts of the humanitarian response as the country still remains a protection crisis in 2020. According to the 2021 HNO, about 1.6 million people remain internally displaced and another 2.2 million people as refugees in the region (HNO, 2021). According to the Integrated Phase Classification (IPC) of Acute Food Insecurity, in October 2020, it projected that Pibor County is currently at IPC Phase - 5, Nasir County is IPC Phase - 4 and Rumbek North is at IPC Phase 3. IPC also projected that between April and July 2021, Pibor County will still be in IPC Phase -5, while both Rumbek North and Nasir Counties will both be at IPC Phase 4. Children continue to be disproportionately affected by crisis and shocks, exposing them to multiple protection risk.Furthermore, outbreak of COVID-19, localized conflicts, including inter-communal violence, cattle raiding, revenge killings and child abduction, wide spread flooding in 2 consecutive years, across Pibor, Nasir and Rumbek Counties, is severely impacting on household’s ability to access food and livelihood sources. People’s coping mechanisms has been weakened as a consequence of the cumulative shocks, leading families to adopt negative practices and children continue to be expose on psycho-social distress, family separation, child marriages, abuses, neglect, exploitation, sexual exploitation, forced labor, child abduction, child re/recruitment, malnutrition, communicable diseases and physical violence. CHADO conducted a quick assessment (desk review, FGD and KII) prior to submission of this proposal found visible cases of people with special needs (PSN) such as extremely vulnerable children, orphans, elderly and persons with disabilities in the targeted counties, including high levels of stress due to loss of friends and family members are also taking a toll on mental health and emotional development of children.In Pibor County, CHADO will be targeting 25% of the Child Protection target which is 12,830 people (3,207 women, 2,567 men, 3,464 girls and 3,592 boys) and in Nasir, CHADO will be targeting 35% of the Child Protection Sub Cluster target which is 11,253 people (2,813 women, 2,251 men, 3,038 girls and 3,153 boys). Lastly, CHADO is targeting 25% of the Child Protection Sub cluster target which is 16,156 people (4,039 women, 3,231 men, 4,362 girls and 4,524 boys) in Rumbek North.Proposed activities are in line with the Child Protection Sub-Cluster (CPSC) and Protection Cluster priorities, which include identification, reunification and provision of interim cares to separated and unaccompanied children; reintegration of children associated with armed forces and groups as well as prevention of family separation, children recruitment, as well as land mines and sexual violence and abuses prevention's. In addition CHADO will establish and strengthen community based child protection networks in order to monitor, report and referee protection concerns. In order to address emotional problems and provide to children, CHADO will establish and strengthen existing child friendly spaces (CFS) for psycho-social activities including community-based Mental Health and Psycho-social Support (MHPSS). As the Protection Response Plan includes capacity building as an approach, CHADO will strengthen the capacity communities to prevent (re)recruitment of children into armed forces. As communities can play a crucial role in protecting children, CHADO will set up and/or strengthen community-based structures to prevent separation, recruitment and/or other threats to children’s protection or well-being and use referral pathways to respond to child protection cases and concerns.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse lie at the core of CHADO strategy to combat Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. As such, CHADO has a strict PSEA policy that is signed by all staff and contractors before engaging in any business with CHADO. It’s implementation and adherence are undertaken in a comprehensive and proactive manner in collaboration with all project beneficiaries and CHADO partner agencies. CHADO screens and vets all hired staff for any misconduct in any former humanitarian organization, when a person is found to be in violations, this person is not hired by CHADO. Any person that is found to have committed an act of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse; such a person is immediately terminated. CHADO will conduct training to staff and partner agencies on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. This training is mandatory before a staff starts to work with CHADO and will continue regularly on refresher sessions. CHADO will also conduct awareness and outreach activities on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse including how to report, where to report and why it’s important to report. CHADO will regularly conduct risk assessment on PSEA including safety audits and taking all measures in preventing Sexual Exploitation and Abuse

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

The project will use complaint and feedback mechanisms at child friendly spaces, schools, CHADO offices and other locations which community members can come and use. The project involves strengthening community-based child protection networks which offer an opportunity for the community to report their concerns to the committees to report these concerns. Focus group discussions, one on one interviews and monitoring visits will also provide opportunities for people to give feedback. Feedback will be equally collected from girls, women, boys and men. CHADO will use a conflict sensitive approach in collecting feedback including translating questionnaires to the local dialects, ensure that individuals living with disabilities are fully involved by contextualizing each questionnaire to each groups specific needs and disability. This will include making feedback questionnaires and easily readable, questionnaires will be read to those who can't read and translated to all who can't read

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

CHADO will establish feedback and complaint mechanisms that will be easily and equally accessible to women, girls, boys and men. This will include one on one real time feedback and complaint collection point that will be opened to all persons of concerns, regular Focus Group Discussions (FGDs). CHADO will also put feedback boxes at meeting points such as Child Friendly Spaces, schools and CHADO offices. CHADO staff on ground will conduct a one day orientation to project beneficiaries targeting women, girls, boys and men including PWDs and elderly. Complaint and Feedback Mechanism will be available and accessible to women, men, boys and girls including PWDs and elderly throughout the entire project life cycle

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

Beneficiaries' feedback will greatly influence on project management, implementation, monitoring throughout the project cycle. CHADO will conduct Focus Group Discussions, Individual interviews and community meetings to collect real time feedback that will influence planning, implementation and monitoring.

12.1.4. Quality, timely and inclusive protection services (including medical care, legal support, safety and mental health psychosocial services to children, women at risk and GBV survivors

CHADO will provide child protection services including case management, PSS, awareness raising and challenge harmful cultural practices in this project targeting women, girls, boys and men including PWDs and elderly. CHADO will ensure services are inclusive such as safety and MHPSS, women at risk and GBV survivors. CHADO will work and collaborate with medical and legal partners in ensuring that services are adequately and safely referred according to needs of women, girls, boys and men including PWDs and elderly.

12.1.2. Decreased proportion of populations facing IPC 3,4 and 5

The project will generally reduce the vulnerability of the affected population and with a resultant reduction in severity of needs and decreasing populations facing IPC 3,4 and 5. The project will promote sustainable solutions to the food security challenge that will improve household income and dietary diversiﬁcation especially for children. The project will utilize local initiatives, such as enhancing traditional livestock-crop systems, including supporting the key role that women play in improving poultry and crop production integration.

12.1.3. Equitable access to cross-sectoral basic services to vulnerable populations (including women, children, the elderly, persons with disability and older persons)

All vulnerable population will have safe and equitable access to CHADO services including women, children, the elderly, persons with disability and older persons. CHADO will adopt systematic approaches to ensure the inclusion of older people and people with disabilities. Both (overlapping) groups are affected by many of the same or very similar barriers to access and participation. CHADO will ensure that women, children, the elderly, persons with disability and older persons are recognized to ensure they receive assistance that is appropriate and relevant to their needs and have access to the health services they need. CHADO will also ensure that they are not negatively affected, and are more prepared, resilient and less at-risk as a result of CHADO action in the provision of health services including ensuring that they have access to information, and participate in decisions that affect them on an equal basis with others. CHADO will also ensure that they receive assistance from competent and well-managed staff and volunteers who are skilled and equipped to include them in humanitarian responses, and they have equal opportunities for employment and volunteering in humanitarian organizations.

12.1.1. Decreased prevalence of global acute malnutrition among children under age 5 in severity levels 3 and 4

CHADO will coordinate with Health and Nutrition cluster partners in the micronutrient supplementation for pregnant women, nutrition education during ANC, promotion of breastfeeding and appropriate complementary feeding, child growth monitoring and promotion (GMP), management of severe acute malnutrition (SAM), nutrition education within integrated management of childhood illnesses (IMCI) packages, vitamin A supplementation for children; and treatment of diarrhoea with zinc. Will also help to identify sick and malnourished children and refer them to Health and Nutrition partners.
