Project Summary

The Emergency Telecommunications Sector (ETS) implements two main activities, to provide and maintain a data connectivity network for the entire humanitarian community working in and around Cox’s Bazar region, and provide and maintain a security telecommunications network for VHF radio use across the operation to support the safety of UN staff. In 2023, the ETS will provide additional activities such as an Internet of Things pilot programme, Services for Communities assessment, and ICT capacity building activities.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

There are 136 humanitarian organizations (UN, INGO, NGO) working in the Cox's Bazar refugee response in the camps. ETS provides telecommunications and data connectivity services available to the entire humanitarian community as needed. It has been assessed that there is a lack of mobile network in most areas of the camps, and that there is a need for internet connectivity and a radio network for emergency communications.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

The ETS provides services to several sectors, including Food Security, Health, Logistics, SMSD, and others that are in need of connectivity, as well as all UN agencies and several NGO partners where there are no other services provided.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

ETS implements new green technology with the installation of solar power solutions across all sites, both to save energy and environmental efficiency, as well as to mitigate network outages due to power issues. The ETS also implements preventative measures such as cyclone resistant equipment and installations.
