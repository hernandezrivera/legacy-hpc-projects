Project Summary

The project aims to safely access formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected school-age girls and boys (5-17 years).
By providing intensive education to 840 students who have dropped out in order to enter the school stage appropriate for their age.
The project is to set up tents in the camps
It is supported by the block and contains the necessary school equipment (a blackboard, chairs, a table and chair for the teacher, in addition to stationery materials)
Which received new numbers of displaced people in the recent period in northern Syria and prepared it to receive children (3-17 years, girls / boys) in the following areas: (Tom- Afrin)
and support this educational process as required,
Based on the recent recommendations of the Humanitarian Needs Review in Syria 2022,
After ten years of conflict, it is critical that formal and non-formal education services be treated as a long-term investment not only by children and their families but by aid actors.
via
Back to Learning (BTL) campaigns and curriculum delivery through (temporary government curriculum)
So that the student can join his class at the nearest public school after the student completes the intensive curriculum within 3 months
Or 6 months, meaning the delay that occurred to him as a result of displacement and displacement
Note: The numbers of the tents were determined according to the statistics conducted by our team in the mentioned areas during the third month of this year.
4 tents are set up in Afrin, two for the primary stage, two for the secondary stage (males - females), comprising 120 students in one stage,

And that is during two successive periods, ie 120 * 2 = 240 within 3 months
3 tents are set up in Tom camp, one primary, and two secondary (male - female), 90 students in one stage, and during two successive periods ie 90
* 2 = 180 in 3 months.
One class includes 15 students in one lesson, and there is a rotation between males and females, so that the total number of students is 30 students per period within the same class.
Recommendations to avoid infection with the Coronavirus will be taken into account by raising awareness of leaflets, maintaining social distancing between children, and training teachers and administrators in these techniques.
The curriculum for this process will be a scientific curriculum that shortens the teaching period for the academic year to three months
will be contracted with
33 teachers (male and female) will be appointed for the secondary stage, and 8 teachers (male and female) for the primary stage.
5 (male - female) mentors.
4 (male - female) administrators. notice:
All teachers and administrators from the same camps and targeted areas, our organization is working to secure funding for this project through an ongoing
We have previous experience in recent years through the rapid education project in Kafr Naha, before launching military campaigns against it. The curriculum that has been adopted is (the curriculum of the Interim Government of the Nation) that has been adopted by the Abrar Organization since 2017.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Violence against education facilities and education provider Mitigating Measures 1-learning facilities be deconflicted.2-Learning facilities will have contingency safety plans and safety protocols in place.Through basic firefighting equipment, basic first aid supplies,exit signs.3-for all will be provided psychosocial support.4will be a referral system to specialized protection services. Protection Risk Violence, physical punishment or bullying in schools Mitigating Measures: Learning facilities will be enforced code of conduct.The code of conduct will cover the conduct of teachers and other education personnel that enables learning spaces to being welcoming,inclusive safe,violence-free and a protective environment. headmasters,teachers and caregivers will make aware of positive discipline methods.school personnel will be monitored as part of ongoing professional development and support.Teachers and school personnel will be trained on prevention and reporting of violence,physical punishment and bullying.The learning by teachers, school personnel will be monitored as part of ongoing professional development and support.Students,their caregivers, teachers and school personnel will be encouraged to report issues and have safe access to a trusted person they can report instances of violence, physical punishment or bullying or other threats that cause fear.will rely the PRA annex shared from the Ed cluster and focus on the risks related to the rights, safety of beneficiaries.
