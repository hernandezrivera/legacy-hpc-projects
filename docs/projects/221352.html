Project Summary


This intervention aims to strengthen child protection systems in conflict-affected areas of the North-West Region of Cameroon. Its purpose is to safeguard children from risks such as family separation, recruitment by armed groups, sexual violence, early marriage, and limited access to education. The project will target vulnerable children and their families in highly affected communities, focusing on capacity building, community engagement, access to essential services, and advocacy. By empowering stakeholders and fostering community-based protection mechanisms, the intervention seeks to create a safer and more resilient environment for children, ensuring their rights and well-being are prioritized.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project to strengthen child protection systems in the North-West region of Cameroon will address the following assessed needs through its activities: 1. Protection from Violence, Abuse, and ExploitationAssessed Need: Children in the North-West region are at high risk of physical, emotional, and sexual abuse, as well as exploitation due to conflict and displacement.Activities to Address This Need:Establishment of safe spaces for children, such as Child-Friendly Spaces (CFS).Training and deployment of social workers and child protection case managers to provide timely support to affected children.Community awareness campaigns to identify and mitigate risks of abuse and exploitation.2. Psychosocial Support and Mental Health ServicesAssessed Need: Children affected by conflict and displacement face significant trauma, stress, and emotional distress.Activities to Address This Need:Provision of psychosocial support through counseling services in schools and community centers.Capacity-building for teachers and community leaders on Psychological First Aid (PFA) and stress management.Setting up peer support groups for children to foster healing and resilience.3. Access to Education in Safe EnvironmentsAssessed Need: Many children have dropped out of school due to insecurity, displacement, or destruction of infrastructure.Activities to Address This Need:Rehabilitation of schools damaged by the conflict and equipping them with adequate learning materials.Supporting community-based learning initiatives where formal education structures are inaccessible.Training teachers and providing them with conflict-sensitive education methodologies.4. Strengthening Local Child Protection SystemsAssessed Need: Weak child protection systems and lack of coordination hinder timely and effective response to child protection issues.Activities to Address This Need:Formation and training of community-based child protection committees to monitor and respond to cases.Development of referral pathways for child protection cases, ensuring children receive appropriate support.Advocacy and training for local authorities and stakeholders on child protection laws and practices.5. Family Tracing and ReunificationAssessed Need: Conflict and displacement have separated children from their families, leaving them vulnerable.Activities to Address This Need:Collaboration with partners to trace families of separated and unaccompanied children.Reunification of children with their families or placement in alternative care arrangements where reunification is not possible.6. Support for Livelihoods and Basic NeedsAssessed Need: Economic hardship among families leads to neglect and exploitation, such as child labor or forced early marriages.Activities to Address This Need:Livelihood support programs for vulnerable families to enhance economic stability.Provision of essential items like food, clothing, and hygiene kits to families in crisis.7. Advocacy and Awareness on Child RightsAssessed Need: Many communities lack awareness of children’s rights and the long-term impact of violations.Activities to Address This Need:Educational campaigns targeting parents, caregivers, and community leaders about children’s rights and protection needs.Use of local media (radio programs, posters, and drama performances) to spread awareness.8. Monitoring and Data ManagementAssessed Need: A lack of data on child protection issues hampers informed decision-making and effective programming.Activities to Address This Need:Development of a child protection information management system to collect, store, and analyze data.Regular surveys and needs assessments to identify emerging trends and adjust programming accordingly.By addressing these critical needs, the project aims to create a protective environment for children in the North-West region, mitigating the impact of conflict on their lives and fostering their overall well-being.
