Project Summary

Since 2016, The North West and South West Regions of Cameroon have been grappling with a protracted crisis, which has disrupted normalcy in educational institutions, with schools becoming vulnerable environments for children due to increased security threats and potential disasters and also adversely impacting the safety and well-being of children within the community. According to the Cameroon education cluster 2023-2025 strategy plan, an estimated 1033,000 school age children have been affected by the crisis. Furthermore, the prevailing crisis has heightened the risk of natural disasters and emergencies, making it crucial for community members to be well-prepared and capable of responding effectively to such situations. According to PAID-WA, September, 2023, Preparation of students/pupils in schools of the North West and South West (NWSW) Regions of Cameroon to response to emergency have been minimal and that most schools (77%) do not have an emergency plan, do not have the capacity to develop it and also, Schools efforts towards teaching students/pupils on emergency related topics have been less encouraging as revealed by 75% of school authorities and students especially teachers in lay private schools and confessional schools. Household survey results with heads of houses and members of PTAS confirmed that 78% of respondents are unaware of topics taught at school that help learners deal with the current situation of emergency and do not know the basic steps to take in safeguarding their children during an emergency and disaster risk situation.
There is an urgent need to address the lack of capacity and preparedness among members of Parent-Teacher Associations (PTAs) and facilitators of Community Learning Centers (CLCs)/community learning Spaces (CLSs) in these regions regarding school safety, child safeguarding, and disaster risk reduction. This knowledge gap increases the vulnerability of children in crisis situations, necessitating a comprehensive training program to mitigate the impact of the crisis on education.
Because of the conflict and the fact of being part of it, the government capacity to contribute to improving the resilience of the education system by strengthening the ability of children and teachers to adapt to the new realities of life brought about by the conflict is limited.
In light of the above, The Pan African Institute for Development West Africa, (PAID-WA) Buea, Cameroon seeks to strengthening the capacity of 300 beneficiaries which will comprise of members of PTAs, teachers and CLCs/CLSs facilitators from 20 schools and 20 CLSs, 10 in the North West and 10 in the south West region of Cameroon through 12 workshops (6 per NW, 6 per SW) training approach that not only educates participants on the principles of safety and safeguarding but also equips them with practical tools and strategies to mitigate the impact of crises on the education sector and safeguarding the future of the children in these regions.
