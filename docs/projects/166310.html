Project Summary

The project aims to increase the capacity of the most vulnerable communities to limit transmission of COVID19 in key locations through provision of preventative WaSH and Health initiatives. More specifically through the provision of information to high risk communities to improve knowledge of ways in which to prevent, identify symptoms, and mitigate transmission of COVID19. The project shall focus on community volunteers, CBOs, CSOs, engaging village and religious leaders from targeted communities to provide key messages on COVID19 prevention and addressing misinformation. Hygiene promotion activities will help buttress prevention by utilizing mass media campaigns and Information, Education and Communication (IEC) materials to provide messaging on proper handwashing, and social distancing. Crucial WaSH activities to increase access to a potable water supply, establish hand washing stations and distribute hygiene kits will combine to arm at risk communities with provisions needed to combat the spread of the virus.

1 - Needs

As of the 28th April 2020, the country has recorded 1273 cases, 239 people have been discharges and 40 deaths recorded from 33 states. The project aims to ensure the most vulnerable communities in NE and North Central Nigeria are able to withstand the negative impacts of a COVID-19 outbreak. Conflict affected and vulnerable communities will be equipped to prevent and mitigate risks presented by COVID19. All project activities will be crafted through a protection and gender sensitive lens to ensure the communities we serve experience optimal benefits from the intervention. The first objective aims to increase the capacity of the most vulnerable communities to limit transmission of COVID19 in key locations through provision of preventative WaSH initiatives. Informal settlements in urban and peri-urban areas along with camp and host communities in the NE have limited ability to mitigate the spread of COVID19. With high levels of congestion, these environments rarely afford inhabitants the privilege of following physical distancing measures. Living in makeshift shelters, having limited WaSH services, poor access to health facilities, irregular access to food. Conditions mimicking those of the NE can be found in urban slums around FCT. Without immediate, informed and well-resourced preventative and containment efforts in these areas, the virus will spread quickly and unchecked among conflict-affected and food-insecure populations – with higher death rates a near-inevitable consequence among affected populations that reduce the body’s capacity to fight infection. To reduce the risk of transmission of Covid 19, the project is applying a 3 pronged strategy: 1) increase awareness, manage rumors and engage with the population; 2) Develop readiness and response plans including community surveillance 3) Adapt and supply infrastructure to enable positive behaviors (hand washing and physical distancing) in camps and host communities.

2 - Activities

The COVID19 response will contribute to the reduction of human to human transmission of the virus, reduce the risk to health workers, and improve the prevention measures in the targeted beneficiaries in IDP camps, health facilities and host communities. high risk communities to improve knowledge of ways in which to prevent, identify symptoms, and mitigate transmission of COVID19. The project shall focus on community volunteers, CBOs, CSOs, engaging village and religious leaders from targeted communities to provide key messages on COVID19 prevention and addressing misinformation. Hygiene promotion activities will help buttress prevention by utilizing mass media campaigns and Information, Education and Communication (IEC) materials to provide messaging on proper handwashing, and social distancing. Crucial WaSH activities to increase access to a potable water supply, establish hand washing stations and distribute hygiene NFIs will combine to arm at risk communities with provisions needed to combat the spread of the virus. Activities include:1) Hygiene promotion to ensure sustainable adoption of key hygiene best practices such as handwashing and behaviour change communication (BCC) to reduce spread of COVID19.2) Ensure hygiene promotion to limit the transpission of COVID 19 through hygiene awareness campaigns and construction of 250 block communal latrines, the Installation of hand washing stations in public places (Markets, Distribution points , worship places, entrance to camps and markets) and distribution of 6500 hygiene kits focused on COVID 18 prevention among affected population in host comunities and IDP camps in Borno and Yobe state.3)Water trucking, Water points reticulation, water point rehabilitation, to increase the quantity of water required for hand washing and basic needs.4) Train healthcare providers and Community volunteers/CNM on risk communication, community surveillance, infection prevention and control.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will use the existing structures to set up a feedback and complaints mechanism and will adopt the existing mechanism to the project context. The team will emphasize mechanisms that are low cost and beneficiaries are familiar with them in their day to day life like Face to Face discussions. The beneficiaries will be given opportunity to provide feedback during face to face interviews, Focus group discussions and during accountability field visits and distribution of project inputs. The mission also has a dedicated CRM toll free line with dedicated trained personnel who understand the local languages/culture and the IASC’s principles and commitments for accountability to affected populations which will be used by beneficiaries to contact AAH team in case they want to report or inquire about project operations and inputs. There will also be dedicated days where by feedback desks will be set up in health facilities, nutrition sites, rehabilitated/constructed water points, and distribution sites to collect beneficiaries’ views on the quality and quantity of implementation. Furthermore, complaints/feedback boxes will be kept in specific locations were beneficiaries and other project stakeholders can lay their concern regularly.Action Contre La Faim will ensure that all formal and informal communications from persons of concern, both positive and negative, inform protection, assistance and solutions to programming, and that corrective action is taken as appropriate. Finally ACF will collaborate with partners in feedback referral and responses where appropriate, especially to ensure counseling and referral of GBV and PSEA victims.
