Project Summary

Humanitarian needs in Sudan are driven by several factors including displacement, climatic shocks and hazards, North Darfur state is one of the most hosting returnees and IDPs` population in spite of lacks economic access to food due to limited livelihoods and income sources, limited availability of household food stocks, suffers from reduced dietary diversity and high rates of malnutrition. As Sudan continues to face economic challenges, the food insecurity of IDPs, residents, refugees and returnees is expected to worsen. The intervention project is designed to cover the immediate food insecurity and livelihoods diversification skills, lack of knowledge of improved agriculture practice and wide spread of livestock diseases and management of natural resources. This intervention will target 4995 HHs (2250 in Alabas2 , 2745 in Abu Shok) extremely vulnerable displaced persons and host community in Alfashir , Meliat localities.

1 - Needs

The intervention project is designed to complete SHF project 2019 (in Abu Shok) and to cover the immediate food insecurity and livelihoods diversification skills in two localities in North Darfur (Elfashir , Meliat) by distributing seads before rainy season and winter season to adapt the insecurity food needs , lack of knowledge of improved agriculture practice and to increase the improve the family income source by vocational training skills and management of natural resources. This intervention will target 4995 HHs 1) 2250 HHs (extremely vulnerable displaced and returnees persons and host community in Alabasb camp, Melliet locality in North Darfur State. The project implementation includes improvement of food production and productivity of 1600 HHs ( distribution of crop and vegetables seeds (for both summer and winter seasons) agricultural tools )FAO standard seeds package – core pipeline; capacity building training through information sessions on sustainable agricultural practices, distribution of livestock of small ruminants for 300 vulnerable households (3 each), Agro processing training for 50 women on drying food and vermicelli making, training of 100 women in efficient stoves , training of 30 women on tailoring, provision of Soap making training for 3o women ,provision of vegetable tables for 20 women , mobile maintenance training for 20 youth, training on production stabilized soil blocks (SSB) for 100 youth . 2) 2745 HHs (extremely vulnerable displaced persons and host community in Abu Skok camp, Elfashir locality in North Darfur State) 1600 HHs ( distribution of crop and vegetables seeds and agricultural tools ) FAO standard seeds package – core pipeline; capacity building training through information sessions on sustainable agricultural practices, distribution of livestock small small ruminants for 500 vulnerable households (5 each), Agro processing training for 100 women on drying food and miserlia making, training of 100 women in efficient stoves , training of 50 women on tailoring, provision of vegetable tables for 100 beneficiaries , training on mobile maintenance for 20 youth, training on stabilized soil blocks (SSB) for 100 youth and 75 women in soap making.
