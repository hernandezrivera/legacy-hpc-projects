Project Summary

Mozambique is facing multiple humanitarian situations due to COVID-19, armed attacks, and cyclones consequences. Stigmatization and discrimination of children with disabilities (CWD) remain widespread in communities and within families. The majority of CWD live in rural areas where access to basic services is more difficult and where stigmatization is stronger.
Within this context, AIFO and relevant partners will carry out an integrated action Cabo Delgado Province in order to promote inclusive child protection, to encourage community disability mainstreaming and to endorse the rights of IDPs with disabilities. The action will respond through service providers’ capacity building and through services delivering to 2.000 CWD (from 2 to 18 years; 60% female and 40% male) and their families. AIFO will strengthen community protection mechanisms already put in place since 2021 in the Districts of Montepuez, Balama, Pemba and Metuge through Community Child Protection Committees (CCPCs) and Social Action Technicians capacity building on child protection and case management, specifically on CWD. The project will create linkages and referral paths between community structures and relevant institutions, optimizing lesson learnt from previous experiences. It will also expand the coverage areas in other relevant districts, where inclusive protection mechanisms are still far behind expectations.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activities will take place in all project districts involved and are: ASSESSMENT- Rapid assessment to determine existence and accessibility of basic assistance services CAPACITY BUILDING- Local Authorities and leaders’ (community leaders, religious leaders, traditional healers, traditional midwives) training on disability and inclusion, specifically targeting children with disabilities (CwD) and their protection and participation in community life.- Protection Actors’ (Government and non-government) capacity building on disability, inclusion and protection, disability national legal framework, principle and standards.- Social Action worker’s capacity building on Case Management of Children with Disabilities- CCPCs (Community Child Protection Committees) members’ training on disability and inclusion, Child protection, identification and support to children with disabilities.- Strengthening/establishing protection and communication networks between service providers (government and non-government) and community structures.- Community sensitizations sessions carried out by trained CCPCs members CASE MANAGEMENT- Case Management of Children with disabilities- Attribution of Assistive devices to children with disabilities

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The action aims at creating an enabling an inclusive protective environment in the communities of intervention.Before starting all trainings and case management activities, AIFO will conduct a rapid assessment, involving DPOs, community members, Civil Society Organizations and local authorities, focused on services auditing to determine their accessibility and propose jointly agreed and simple solution when they are not fully accessible. Through this exercise, potential risks of exclusion from protection mechanisms and of harm will be identified and duly addressed. Community sensitizations sessions carried out by trained CCPCs members will encourage meaningful access to assistance. The active partnership with CSOs and DPOS will guarantee the active participation of people with disabilities in the action.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Following the Community Based Inclusive Development approach, AIFO works within and with communities and its staff is on a continuing auscultation and dialogue process with them. Community members (in particular CCPCs members) will be the main actors of the action as they will be equipped with tools and knowledge in order to establish an inclusive child protection environment, taking into consideration cultural setting and within this context, acting for pro-active behavioral change. The design of the project also benefited from the longstanding partnership between AIFO and FAMOD, the national Forum of the Association of People with Disabilities in Mozambique and from the dialogue with the Disabled People Organizations (DPOs), who are the spokespersons of people with disabilities. Feed-back mechanism: Focus Groups and periodic meetings with community members involved will be carried out; sponsor the use of Linha Verde, Linha Fala a Crianca and a specific Hot line established by AIFO for referral of CwD within communities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Target beneficiaries of this action are precisely CwD, who are particularly vulnerable among communities, living ostracized and excluded from social life. They will be identified by CCPCs members and community leaders duly trained and using the Washington Group Short Set of questions (re-adapted to community understanding) and in particular WG/UNICEF Child Functioning Module adapted for 2-4 and 5-14 years old. CwD, in particular girls, are often subject to sexual and physical abuse, exploitation and neglect. They are often excluded from education and not provided with the support to help them develop to their full capacity. The most vulnerable and discriminated are children with albinism, psychosocial and intellectual disability (myths and superstitions have generated the common belief that persons with albinism have magical aptitudes, and their body parts have become instruments for witchcraft practices). The exclusion of CwD is exacerbated when they belong to another disadvantaged group, such for examples the IDPs. The action will contribute to identify (rapid assessment) and remove (capacity building) attitudinal and social-cultural barriers which prevent the full development and participation of CwD, providing local authorities and service providers with specific knowledge and means to guarantee assistance and support to CwD.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in the respect of AIFO Environmental and Sustainability Policy, included in AIFO Manual of Operational Policies- Code of Ethics and Conduct 2020. According to the essential points of this Policy, an environmental assessment will take place and will include representatives of people with disabilities, strengthening environmental measures that increase accessibility and break down existing barriers.This action does not have a negative impact on the environment while mitigation and protection risks within communities, as part of the Disaster Risk Reduction framework, will tackle community preparedness to natural disasters – as the geographic area is exposed to this catastrophic natural events. The action will Include representatives of vulnerable people with disabilities in the preparation and implementation of disaster risk reduction plans to limit their impact on the most vulnerable.
