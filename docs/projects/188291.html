Project Summary

Mansoura sub-district is located southwest of Raqqa governorate and has a population of about 72,000. Mansura is one of the most vulnerable and disadvantaged areas in NES and it has been marked as a class 5 severity rate. The region has various challenges that are affecting the lives of residents in different aspects. First of all, the city has very limited access to health services as the only health facility serving more than 72,000 inhabitants is a PHC providing limited and basic services 5 days a week. Furthermore, there is a huge WASH issue in the city caused by the non-functioning of the wastewater treatment facility and causing the emergence of swamps of non-treated water which became a habitat to mosquitos and different types of insects and resulting in the emergence of various waterborne diseases such as leishmania and typhoid fever. In addition to that, the northern part of the city is not linked to the wastewater treatment plant and the sewage water is dumped directly in the Euphrates River which results in polluting the water that is pumped to the residents’ households without any kind of treatment and thus resulting in diseases and diarrhea. Furthermore, the water network in the city is mainly damaged and non-functional which is depriving the residents of water access, and even the Households that have access to water are suffering from low quality and always complain that it is undrinkable due to the absence of chlorination and treatment. Additionally, the city is surrounded by various informal camps, the biggest among them is the Harrakat camp hosting almost 300 households. These families have no access neither to water nor to latrines. Open defecation is the regular process in this camp and no handwashing facilities are available which constitutes a huge source for potential infections and spread of diseases.
AFH is planning to conduct an intervention with the aim of finding a sustainable solution to the waterborne disease spread issue, removing the sources of infections, and providing clean drinkable water to the inhabitants. The intervention consists of the following activities.
In Mansura city:
- Rehabilitation of the sewage treatment plant and providing it with all accessories needed to be functioning again (a backfilling process of the old estuary, constructing a new estuary at a distance of 7 km from the place of the settlement, and connecting the station to the new estuary with sewage pipes and a new pump).
- Installing a tank to collect the sewage water in the northern part of Mansura (Al Wadi neighborhood) and transport it with pipes to the sewage treatment plant to be disposed of in the newly established estuary.
- Ensure rehabilitation and maintenance of water network in the city with the aim to increase the water pressure which will result in increasing the access of residents to drinkable water.
- Ensure the provision of chlorination machines in the water networks to make sure that the targeted population has access to clean drinkable water.
In Harrakat informal Camp: AFH will conduct the following activities:
- Installing segregated SPHERE standard-compliant latrines.
- Installing Washing and bathing facilities.
- Ensure the provision of water to the camp throughout signing and MoU with the water committee in which AFH will provide two water trucking vehicles and the Committee will ensure the sustainable provision of water to the IDPs.
- AFH will as well conduct hygiene promotion sessions and distribute Hygiene kits and brochures to improve the hygienic practices in the camp.
AFH believes that the proposed intervention will contribute to increasing the good hygienic practices in the area and improving WASH infrastructure which will eventually lead to a reduction of waterborne diseases in the targeted region.

5 - Implementation of activities

the project will be implemented through the following activities:- Rehabilitation of wastewater treatment facility in Mansura sub-district and ensure it resumes its work. this will be conducted in coordination with water committee who agreed to provide electricity to the facility should AFH grant fund to rehabilitate it and provide necessary equipment-Eliminate wastewater swamps in the outskirts of Mansura by back filling them and change the estuary outside of the city- Create a tank for wastewater in the northern part of Mansura and link it with the treatment plant to avoid dumping wastewater in the river and thus prevent water pollution.- Ensure rehabilitation and maintenance of water network in the city of Mansura and equip the water station in Hunaida village with the aim to increase the water pressure which will result in increasing the access of residents to drinkable water- Ensure the provision of chlorination machines in the water networks to make sure that the targeted population have access to clean drinkable water-Installation of segregated latrines, washing and bathing facilities for 300 HHs in Harrakat camp in the outskirts of Mansura-Ensure hygiene promotions and distribution of hygiene kits to improve the hygiene practices of the IDPs in Harrakat camp-Ensure sustainable water trucking to the camp

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The main protection Risks that might encounter the project are the following: Threat: Quality and/or quantity not adequate as per humanitarian standards, Likelihood: High, Impact: High, Mitigating Measure: Ensure provision of safe water as per WaSH WG Water Quality standards and establish regular water quality monitoring including water source, water trucks, and end-user samples; Collect water from safe sources certified by water authorities or local councils - Threat: Men refusing that humanitarian actors can contact women during the awareness-raising sessions and hygiene promotion sessions, Likelihood: High, Impact: Medium, Mitigating Measure: AfH will make sure to hire women to conduct these sessions with females in the camp - Threat: Risk of abuse that women or children may face when accessing toilets at night, Likelihood: High, Impact: High, Mitigating Measure: AFH will ensure to provide lights through the installation of solar panels on each set of latrines to prevent any cases of security risks (Sexual abuse, harassment, child abuse), in addition to that, each 2-3 families will have a dedicated toilet - Threat: Increase risks of GBV if latrines are not separated or there are no locks on the doors, Likelihood: High, Impact: High, Mitigating Measure: Consulting with women and girls on the location of latrines, ensuring locks on the door.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Protection risks: There is a risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls, Likelihood: medium, Effect: high, Mitigation measures: 1) Establish a confidential complaint mechanism; Post-monitoring / client feedback forms are used also - 2) Train staff on code of conduct and Include PSEA topic in regular supervision sessions


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

the main risks that might affect the projects' implementation and sustainability are the following:1- availability and sustainability of electricity to ensure the functionality of the Wastewater treatment plant after the rehabilitation. in this regard, AFH has discussed with the Water Committee and agreed that both parties will sign an MOU that clarifies the role of AFH in the rehabilitation of the plant and the commitment and engagement of Water Committee to provide electricity to the facility as well as to ensure its regular maintenance and functioning.2- Lack of water in Harrakat camp which will affect the hygiene of the residents as well as the functionality of the installed latrines. to cope with this risk, AFH will sign an MOU with the Water department in which AFH will commit to provide two water trucking vehicles to the committee and the committee engages to continue the regular provision of water to the camp and the installed latrines and washing facilities which will ensure the sustainability of the intervention3- the third risk would be the non usage of the latrines or the lack of cleanliness of the facilities. In this regard, AFH will ensure to conduct many Hygiene promotion and awareness raising sessions, engage the camp leaders to guarantee the regular cleaning of the latrines and ensure that the water committee conducts regular monitoring visits to reinforce the engagement of the IDP towards the cleanliness of their latrines.
