1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will consist of promoting health, hygiene and sanitation. In the health component, the project will focus on guaranteeing the consumption of drinking water for all displaced people and hosting families, it will also focus on the spread of COVID-19 prevention messages. In the hygiene and sanitation component, the project will consist of the distribution of hygiene and sanitation material, promotion and dissemination of messages on construction and proper use of latrines by all project beneficiaries.The project is aimed at 10,000 displaced people and host families, and will be implemented in the districts of Montepuez. The project will be implemented for the period of six months (March to October 2022).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

 Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level. Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accommodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees. Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households) Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media. The project is aimed at 10,000 displaced people. All of these activities will take place in the pre-established districts of Montepuez.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The identification of risks will be done through involvement and dialogue with local leaders,it is known that the target group belongs to several ethnic groups. To seek to know, togetherwith the leaders, what are the main priorities and immediate needs for the beneficiaries. Alltarget groups will be included to avoid possible confusion between them.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Caritas as the humanitarian arm of the church, serves all the needy without anydiscrimination. If there are people qualified to support the distribution of the NFIs anddissemination of messages, Caritas involves them in their work and in return, they gainexperience in the work of assisting the needy, which they can use in the future.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be one of the priorities during the distribution, reason whywithin the community consultation, the field officers will be trained to understand themodality of targeting for the most vulnerable beneficiaries such as people with disabilitieswith leadership character and ability to do some sort of work, they will be included in theteam. Distribution points will be conveniently located ensuring ease of access by communitymembers particularly people with disabilities and women.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is health, hygiene and sanitation, so the field officers will be trainedto explain to the local target communities how to keep the environment clean, avoidingthrowing garbage in inappropriate places, promoting the use of latrines to avoid watercontamination, obviously respecting the environment, avoiding opening large holes, throwinggarbage anywhere and promoting hygiene debates. Some individual and collective hygienesessions will be carried out by the activists to ensure the welfare of the whole society.
