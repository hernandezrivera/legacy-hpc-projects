Project Summary

The dependency of the camp population on humanitarian assistance especially on food due to their inaccessible state of income or livelihood opportunities. Therefore, the Rohingya Refugees are solely reliant on humanitarian assistances given by Govt., UN and other humanitarian aid agencies. The scale of the current influx has placed intense pressure on host communities, stretching public services and increasing prices of food and other daily essentials services. Moreover, Refugees have now occupied the land for habitation those previously used by host communities for livelihood purpose and day-to-day living. As a result, the crops production in this locality is dramatically reduced which basically contributed to increase in the food price in the local markets. Simultaneously, host community people have lost work opportunities to cheaper refugee labour market. The increasing impact on host communities' livelihoods is contributing to social tensions and resentment between the local population and refugees, particularly over natural resources such as land, water, fish, firewood and labour. The need to support local communities' livelihoods under the guidance of Bangladesh authorities is paramount and will have a key role in mitigating tensions.
This project, therefore, highly emphasised on the priority needs for both host and Rohingya communities in terms of in-kind and cash-based programming. The total target of this project to support 15,296 living in the host and camps.
Firstly, planned to ensure direct cash & in-kind support to 3,600 Host community HHs (18,000 individual) for livelihood diversification including IGA covering Baharchora union of Teknaf where -
2,000 individuals will receive conditional cash support for homestead gardening or small scale agro production including the costs for vegetable seeds/ sapling, fencing, organic fertilisers etc to ensure their HH level supplementary food and nutrition sources. Also, 1,185 individuals will receive conditional cash support to start small scale backyard poultry farms including the cost for chicks/ducklings, chicken feeds for 6 months, shed construction and vaccination. This activity specifically will target elderly men and women to create home-based income opportunity for these targeted individuals. Furthermore, special cash provision has planned for 200 individual women for goat / sheep rearing (including shed, feed for 6 months and vaccination); 200 individual women for organic fertilizer /Verme compose plant set up in the community; 5 individuals will receive cash support for poultry hatchery business/ enterprise development; 10 individuals (5 men & 5 women) developed as paravet, vaccinator with seed/ feed seller with equipment and market linkage with producer company and also 04 FFBS will be developed for the community (construction and operations).
Secondly, total 148 community level capacity building sessions/ orientation and follow up sessions have been planned for the host community as handholding support to generate their livelihood opportunities.
Thirdly, this project has planned to provide in-kind micro gardening support (sapling, bamboo, organic fertilisers) to 11,696 Rohingya families of camp 14 & 15 including training on plantation, use of organic fertilisers, fencing etc (4,853 persons of Camp-15 and 6,843 persons of Camp-14 for Micro gardening, from the same number of HHs).
