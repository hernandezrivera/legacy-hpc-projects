Project Summary

The goal of the project is to provide a comprehensive package of water and sanitation services, which includes the distribution of hygiene products, the implementation of garbage collection activities, the provision of drinking water, and the provision of sanitation services, in order to meet the needs of the most vulnerable among the conflict-affected IDP and host community girls, boys, women, and men in NWS. The project intends to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, environmental hygiene solutions, and the provision of appropriate core hygiene items and good personal hygiene practices for both host community populations in the targeted locations as well as IDPs. It also aims to improve health through access to sanitation and a healthy and dignified environment. Additionally, by implementing planned activities aimed at bridging the gaps and reducing needs between the displaced and the new, as well as lowering the risk of disease through improved awareness and knowledge of health risks, Silk Road hopes to improve the general condition of the majority of those affected, with a special focus on the newly arrived and existing IDPs in the targeted communities. Restoring a cost recovery system will be the means by which sustainability will be achieved. The proposed activities include: providing pumps, generators, pipes, and electrical panelboards; rehabilitation of water supply systems; equipping wells with galvanized pipes and submersible pumps; rehabilitation of concrete bases and housing for the pumps and generators; and providing solar energy systems for water stations. After rehabilitation work is done, the systems will be provided with operations and maintenance support to ensure the sustainability of the provided assistance. During this period of support, a cost recovery (tariff) system will be established to ensure the continuous cash flow required for running the systems. This will involve a study on a suitable tariff rate that takes into account the dwindling financial capacity of the benefiting population and will be based on focus group discussions with a representative group of the community. In addition to the development of the water unit in the civil councils, ensure that they have adequate operations and maintenance teams. These teams will be responsible for ensuring sustainable water services by activating a tariff system. In addition to hygiene promotion activities and hygiene kit distribution to improve hygiene knowledge and access to hygiene materials, which coincide with the water activities, Proposed activities will be the best solution for the current situation, and in this context, they can allow newly formed IDPs and already existing IDPs to have access to safe water through restored water systems to reduce vulnerability to preventable illnesses and improve resilience, while IDPs and other vulnerable groups (disabled, elderly) have access to life-saving assistance to reduce the risk of WASH-related morbidity and mortality. Furthermore, the proposed WASH interventions in this proposal will ensure that they do not create additional risks for the affected population (especially IDP women and children and the newly IDPs) but will provide the best solutions to the suffering population in the targeted areas. Silk Road will ensure that the health impact of this emergency WASH intervention will reduce disease risk and transmission risk while adhering to internationally recognized standards and best practices, ensuring that services are provided in line with Do No Harm, WASH cluster standards, and humanitarian principles, and that services are of the highest quality.
