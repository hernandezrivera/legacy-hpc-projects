Project Summary

Years of prolonged conflict, spanning over 13 years, coupled with various natural disasters such as earthquakes, floods, and droughts, have exacerbated the suffering of the population in NW Syria. This is due to the catastrophic damage inflicted upon the infrastructure, including buildings, roads, and markets, as well as the scarcity or absence of job opportunities and means of livelihood resulting from the deteriorating economic situation accompanying the conflict. Additionally, inflation and price hikes have affected all markets in the surrounding countries in the region, leaving vulnerable families in desperate need of assistance and unable to rely on themselves to improve their situation.
To alleviate the suffering of the population and to find solutions that enable vulnerable families to become self-reliant, Amal plans to work on two separate interventions in this project as the following:
The first intervention is to establish the infrastructure that contributes to early recovery and facilitates access to markets and services, which involves repairing the main roads that connect population centers to nearby markets or link towns together, thus facilitating commercial activity and the movement of workers, professionals, or employees to their workplaces and earning their livelihood. In the communities of Kafr Lusin and Hazano, there are damaged roads where crossing a distance of 3 km takes 3 hours, hindering the movement of residents to their workplaces (under normal conditions, crossing takes less than 10 minutes). Amal will work on paving the connecting roads between Hazano and Kafr Lusin for a distance of 3 km and a width of 8 m. As a result, 22,627 ind (13,693 in Hazano, and 8,934 in Kelly) will benefit from this component, creating an enabling environment for prosperity in the region.
The second intervention aims to support 3 safe spaces with improved access to create a supportive environment for vulnerable groups including women, children and PWDs in Idleb and Aleppo through the provision of ER intervention in-line with HRP goals, as, the program objectives and activities explained in detail in this proposal have been designed through practical practice in NW Syria and direct communication and dealing with the women, girls, elderly, PWDs.
This intervention will be implemented in the WGSS, CFS of Amal in Idleb and Aleppo - which targets the IDPs and the host community.
Amal organization will seek to support ERL sector with 24-month project.
Activities enrolled in this proposal include;
1. Small business grants
2. Apprenticeship. Provision of vocational training and income-generating activities (Providing special training courses to enable women and caregivers to work in the humanitarian organizations: project management, etc)
3. Creating joint small projects
4. Empowerment of adolescents and youth including the payment of transportation and the distribution of small equipment to these beneficiaries after the end of the training
A. Photoshop and graphic design and Digital security
B. Media: principles of media work, text editing, preparing press reports
