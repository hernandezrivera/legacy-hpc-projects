Project Summary

This project targets 6,000 children and adolescents over the period January – December 2021, of which 6,000 (55%) are female. H4BF seeks funding to initiate the implementation of the project by focusing on reaching girls and boys in Wouri and Nkam in Littoral region and, Mezam and Donga Mantung in North West region Cameroon. In total, over 6000 girls and boys will access learning opportunities, including some 500 girls and boys in early learning programmes, 3000 at primary level and some 2500 at the secondary level, in both formal and non-formal education settings.
• 6,000 girls and boys will access learning opportunities, including
• 500 girls and boys in early learning programmes,
• 3000 at primary level and
• 2500 at the secondary level
These children will be reached through both formal and non-formal education settings.
H4BF will focus on over 60% of the children, adolescents targeted, and the programme will ensure at least 10% of beneficiaries are children and adolescents with disabilities. 10% support Early Childhood Education for the youngest learners. Over the three-year period, implementation will contribute to achievement the overall EiE Strategic Plan.
This will be achieved through following outcome interventions;
Outcome 1 Increased access to quality, inclusive, holistic education for crisis-affected girls and boys (including adolescents)
Outcome 2-Increased continuity of education for crisis -affected girls and boys (including adolescents) is through improved transitions to formal education from alternative provision
Outcome 3 Existing learning environments are child-friendly, gender-responsive, equitable, disability-friendly and inclusive
Outcome 4 Improved quality of learning for crisis-affected girls and boys, including adolescents
Outcome 5 Safe and protective learning environments for crisis-affected girls and boys are established
Outcome 6 Improved coordination mechanisms to respond to education in emergencies
Outcome 7 Improved rates of enrolment and learning outcomes for girls, including adolescent girls
Outcome 8 Sufficient resources are available to implement all H4BF strategies and enable the handover of the beneficiary caseload to development and government partners

1 - Besoins / Needs

The conflict in the NWSW has had repercussions for the education. The whole system has failed in providing access to inclusive learning. An estimated 1,033,000 school aged children have been affected by the crisis. While girls and women are particularly vulnerable due to the combined effect of gender discrimination and socio-economic vulnerability caused by the crisis, leading to specific threats against them (in particular gender-based violence), boys and men are more exposed to arbitrary arrests, forced recruitment and physical violence. Both genders therefore live with psychosocial trauma related to violence, including sexual violence and rape. These multiple crises have a common denominator: children are in distress and teachers lack knowledge and competence to deal with crises affected students. Children are highly affected by conflict, exposed to violence, abuse and exploitation, family separation issues and have significant psychosocial needs. As a result these children easily drop out of school, face early and forced marriage, engage in petty commerce, child labour and other negative coping mechanisms for survival. With the COVID-19 crisis, teachers are being forces to adapt IT skills and learn to work in a remote way, while children continue with home schooling, which adds additional burden on the education system in these areas where structural challenges were already inherent, particularly in the Far North . Conclusively, the Far North continues to face educationally challenges that include i) Access to the Protective Learning Environment, ii)Quality and relevant education services provided by teachers and education personnel with the knowledge and skills to deal with formal and non-formal approached to learning iii) Provision of Teaching and learning materials and iv) Capacity building of teachers on Pedagogy, EiE, PSS and Protection.
