Project Summary

The proposed project seeks to protect children and relieve psychological pressure from them through a friendly space in Sarmada District in Idlib Governorate, as it provides full protection services, including: Psychosocial support
Provide case management and referral services for children at risk
Provide systematic awareness campaigns to protect children
Educating children about the dangers of explosives and mines
Training humanitarian workers on child protection issues
The project will be implemented in the Cham Humanitarian Center in Sarmada District, where the Al-Haramain Foundation can reach the area easily through the implementation of several projects in that region, and it will also be implemented through 6 mobile teams.
The project takes into account the inclusion of children
Persons with disabilities in society through specialized activities including psychological rehabilitation, provision of PSS activities, life skills and awareness. And provide sessions on their rights within the community and work will be done to improve their access to the services provided by the project by providing their transportation to ensure their safety
The project was designed to be based on gender equality in the number of jobs and the number of beneficiaries and to determine the priorities for employing women who are most able to interact with children's issues that were addressed in the project, as the project achieved the proportion of girls benefiting more than the proportion of male children
The project provided services to the displaced and the host community as all activities support equality and make the push for equality one of its most important goals
Priority has been given to the principle of not harming the project by working to empower the community
The project will consider stakeholder accountability and focus more on girls within the accountability standards
AHF will make accountability an integrated system where there is an accountability employee
The most important tools that we use to track accountability issues are the complaints offices, the complaints box and the complaints phone number, the complaint email address
The Director of Monitoring and Evaluation will issue frequent reports: • Weekly and monthly reports, which will provide a comprehensive view of the progress made against the specified indicators • To take advantage of lessons learned and better follow-up project activities as any problem that occurs during the implementation of activities and project indicators will be documented to measure the progress of the project at the level of Activities, outputs, and recipients according to gender

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

AssumptionsCommunity-based child protection for girls and boys is available in targeted locationsRisksRisk of inter or intra-community tension due to service delivery locations or types of services offered if one community is served and a neighbouring community not served. Protection risk faced by girls and boys may persist if community members do not play an active role in the delivery of protection services. Risk of existing traditional and religious practices increasing due to limited community engagement or community acknowledgement of certain traditional and religious practices as a protection concern. Risk of girls and boys exposure to protection risk due to the limited initiatives to enhance children's participation in their own protection. Lack of a sustainable protection intervention due to limited involvement of national stakeholder and targeted community members in child protection service delivery. Mitigating measures1. Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed.2. Ensure consultation with communities and neighbouring communities related to location of services to understand perspectives of communities are all understood and addressed. Including access barriers for those with injuries/disabilities and communication mechanisms for persons with visual/hearing impairments.3. Liaise with "neutral" parties as much as possible and triangulate all information from all sources prior to decision making.4. Utilize sector agreed key messages to ensure consistency and inform sector of discrepancies or if new messaging is required.5. Ensure safety audits for access are conducted for each fixed site or mobile service location. 6. Linking protection intervention with existing services within targeted communities to enhance the system approach.7. Support national actors and community based groups to ensure sustainability of protection intervention.
