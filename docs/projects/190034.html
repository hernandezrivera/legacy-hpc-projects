Project Summary

1. To reduce physical and social economic vulnerabilities of women and girls to sexual and gender-based violence in camps
2. To enhance life sustaining initiatives for women, girls and people living with disabilities in the camps and host communities through cash transfers.
3. To enhance safe spaces for (protection against Sexual Exploitation and abuse) feedback and reporting on Sexual and Gender Based Violence through camp structures and community systems
