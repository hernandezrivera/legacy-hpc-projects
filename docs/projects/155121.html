Needs

With this programming, IRC will seek to meet WASH needs in the host community, in alignment with sector standards and demonstrated need. Initial assessments demonstrate that these needs include as a top priority access to clean water. The influx has lead to depletion and contamination of wells. Properly installed, deeper wells, as well as significantly increased clean surface water collection points will need to be established to meet water needs in host communities. Proper community consultations, including consultations with women and girls, will be needed in order to properly site this infrastructure. Given the newly increased density of the population,water borne disease including AWD is an ongoing threat. Infrastructure alone cannot address this; culturally adapted in-depth hygiene promotion will be needed to properly understand current hygiene practices and effectively address risks. IRC will work across health, GBV, CP, Protection and education programming, and would work in a highly integrated manner to deliver wash programming. IRC will also draw on global expertise in WASH and WASH-protection integrated programming to deliver in Bangladesh.

Activities or outputs

"IRC is new to the WASH sector and aims to start up small WASH activities in the coming year in the host community. This funding will be integrated into other sector programming, including protection, GBV and education, and would also include a complaints response mechanism (covered with secured funding). IRC also aims to begin working with partners and will be ramping up capacity in order to properly manage and evaluate potential partners in the coming year. Potential activities include several IRC curriculums that are in use world wide>Water infrastructure + water management committees/community maintenance structures. Depending on what is demanded by the community, selected in consultation. Committees would commit to the maintenance of the infrastructure and help manage its construction. This could include community infrastructure as well as WASH infrastructure in school buildings. > Hygiene awareness (health clubs, linked to health awareness activities under other outcomes). This will include hygiene messaging to start, including with youth and ideally though youth programming funded through other sectors. Later in the year IRC will assess to begin CLTS programming. "
