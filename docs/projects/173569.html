Project Summary

In line with the 2020 allocation paper, the objective of the project is to dramatically improve WASH and protection services of IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities totaling 48,776 people in Khartoum and Geneina localities in Sudan. ECDO proposes innovative WASH and Protection interventions in the localities mentioned above.
These interventions are aimed at providing assistance with lifesaving and an early recovery approach, coupled with strengthening the targeted communities’ resilience, sustainability and protecting and improving their existing livelihood assets to pave the way forward for the early recovery of these communities
ECDO on the WASH sector focuses on improving community access to sustainable WASH facilities and ensure proper management on project progress related issues
SHF funding will be used for core life- life-saving social assistance and service through Establish/support protection referral pathways including medical, psychosocial and legal support and livelihood or IGAs activities, Create / support community-based protection structures/networks, Procure and distribute Dignity Kits/Personal Hygiene Kits (PHKs), Establish/ support women center as a safe space for women through provision of psycho-social services, legal aid services and awareness activities, Strengthen capacity of services providers and authorities, including training and development of protection related reference documents/tools, , Conduct protection awareness raising campaigns among communities (including MRE), Provide direct support to vulnerable people (PSS, counseling, mobility equipment, cash grants, etc), .This intervention seeks to address some of the most urgent protection needs of the most vulnerable individuals and families selected beneficiaries through a community based approach including direct lifesaving assistance and Psychosocial support. The action will contribute to support beneficiaries to attain safety and dignity by improving their protective environment taking into account both external threats to the target population as well as the coping strategies adopted to counteract the vulnerabilities. ECDO will use its already existing programs to approach and strengthen coordination to leverage referral pathways to ensure the needs of the survivors are addressed and considered with respect and confidentiality. This approach includes prevention through awareness raising and community-based education and information campaigns, capacity development for partners, monitoring and reporting, and provision of services for the survivors by, Conducting protection assessments for communities, targeting Persons with Specific Needs (PSNs), CBCPMs are strengthened and incorporated to CPCs CAPs through the development of risk reduction action and monitoring plans by CP focal points,ECDO has a strong partnership and linkages with local communities and authorities that facilitate access and operations in the targeted areas. The project pave the way for resilience through supporting the capacity of the community structures and local authorities in response to protection, general social needs and GBV concerns. The community will be closely involved in the project activities. The local authorities, including the State Ministry of Social Welfare, State Ministry of Education, and the National CCW will be also consulted and engaged in the project design and implementation.Smooth exit strategy will be elaborated to ensure the sustainability of the activities. The project has thoroughly investigated and focused on the “DO NO Harm” principle during the consultations with the community to avoid any harm that might be brought about by the intervention. In addition and during the selection of beneficiaries, the project will ensure participation of all groups, including women, people with disabilities, minorities, and others. The project will use the vulnerability criteria applied by the protection sector.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection is a major component of this project. ECDO will ensure protection and GBV is well mainstreamed in each sector and some community level activities related to protection is done. In addition, each sector will ensure that all activities implemented improve the wellbeing of women and children and contribute towards improving women's access to and control over assets created by the project or resources provided by each sector. In Sudan, women and girls are overburdened in domestic labor including preparation of food, fetching water, collecting fire wood, agricultural labor during planting and harvesting, marketing domestic supplies, looking after livestock, preparing flour mills and serving as the sole responsible for child care in the household. Also, women have almost no right to make decisions on the utilization and transfer of the major assets. This project has analyzed all the constraints that affect women and girls and developed strategies that ensure their active participation and benefits. The water supply infrastructure will be put in safe and accessible areas to make sure most women and girls fetch water from nearby areas within a distance of <1km, which minimizes loss of time and energy and protects against violence. Also, water points will be closed around 6 pm to minimize the SGBV risks and will be fenced to minimize the risk of women or girls sustaining injuries while trying to draw water. Women will constitute 50% of the number of (WASH) committees, and task forces and they will be empowered to have active voice in the decision making process during the planning, targeting, and implementation and monitoring of this project. The project indirectly contributes to improving girls’ participation in school by making appropriate gender-specific latrines and affordable or locally prepared reusable menstrual hygiene supplies. The organization recognizes the importance of the human rights of host communities, returnees, IDPs and especially the most vulnerable groups such women, girls and minority groups. While implementing this project, ECDO will make conscious and intentional efforts to avoid all situations that expose beneficiaries to risk, threat, violence and abuse of their rights, reaching out to populations who are unable to access the static facilities, thus ensuring that individuals afraid or unable to move to the static facilities are not denied assistance or services. This is particularly important for elderly and disabled beneficiaries who may be unable to walk the required distances to project sites.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Besides the strategic choice of service locations, strategic choice of staff can also strengthen protection of beneficiaries. ECDO will also be sure to promote peace sensitivity by ensuring that all community groups including IDPs, host community members, returnees, pastoralists, and varying tribal and ethnic groups have equal access to project services by selecting project sites in areas accessible to the different groups and hiring diverse staff. This minimizes the risk of conflict due to competition over social services. The possibility of discrimination by locally hired staff against beneficiaries who do not hold the same tribal or political affiliations mitigated by ensuring that program teams that consist of combinations of locally hired national staff representing all ethnic groups, national staff hired from outside the programmatic area and international staff. ECDO have been implementing lifesaving and resilience building actions in Protection and WASH sectors in Khartoum and geneina locality, leveraging resources from donors and addressing a coverage area of around 50,000 households. Synergies are identified at three levels. There is synergy with other projects in the proposed sectors as well as the different projects currently being implemented by agency members.Overall, the proposed actions will fill significant gaps in services existing in the target communities. Maximum precautions are taken to avoid duplication of efforts. Protection and WASH activities will be implemented within the same villages as protection activities funded elsewhere to enhance the impact of all actions. There are other INGOs and NNGO is operating in the same localities, but have agreed to work in different villages or catchment areas to avoid duplication and enhance overall humanitarian service coverage. ECDO has been actively involved in the sector coordination led by the respective UN agencies. They will continue its coordination with the line ministries and the clusters to ensure that NGOs are not duplicating resources, effort and geographic areas and to avoid competition over resources. The members participate in the inter-agency joint assessments, monitoring, and evaluation of projects and programs in Khartoum and West Darfur. ECDO is a member of the NGO steering committee in West Darfur, which comprises. The Steering committee has a monthly meeting to discuss the security situation, humanitarian access, project agreements and some joint actions for acute emergency responses. ECDO has a ‘one program’ approach which aims to consolidate the efforts of all sectors to bring synergy in achieving a broader goal. Additionally, ECDO will effectively coordinates multi-sector programs with Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) at state and federal level to facilitate signing project agreements, facilitating stay and travel permits to international staff and visitors.
