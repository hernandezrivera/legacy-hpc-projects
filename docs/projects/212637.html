Project Summary

The project is designed to integrate protection services (GP,GBV,CP) in 6 targeted sub-districts Afrin/Albab /Dana /maartTamsrin/Bennsh/ salqin and nearby camps for 1 year.
The project targets 76400 (M:4575 / W:4575 / B:14000/ G:24650) including 7000PWDs and will be implemented throughout 3 sectors interventions as the following:
GP intervention:aim at supporting vulnerable children, women & girls at-risk groups, PwD, and affected populations to easily access available services within the targeted areas for 8100 BNFs( 4300W,14300 M).
Through providing quality protection services, with a focus on community-based approaches, including awareness-raising documentation, and providing legal counseling to 3000 W&M to offer accessible legal advice & provide cash support to 100 W&M. Establishing 2 protection help desks as entry points, activating hotlines, and facilitating transport for at-risk families during emergencies. Creating 8 Protection Committees to share knowledge, and address challenges. Providing training for committee members and educational personnel on child protection minimum standards. Lastly, identification and referrals.
CP intervention:MWL is proposing integrated child protection, MHPSS, focused on children (girls and boys) and women of IDPs and host communities, in addition to men and women caregivers including PwDs within camps and communities through 5 functional and equipped CP centers which were established by MWL previous projects within 5 sub-districts (Albab, Afrin, Dana, Marrat Tamsrin, Salqin ) and 5 mobile teams in the previously mentioned sub-districts in addition to Bennsh sub-district.Targeting 35150 (M:4575 / W:4575 / B:13000/ G:13000)
- MWL will provide sustained structured PSS within 5 centers, that will enhance well-being of 6000 G&B and semi-structured PSS within camps including CwDs. In addition, short-term PSS as recreational activities will be conducted in camps, and Psychological First Aid (PFA) in case of emergencies for 12000 B&G.
- Mental health and psychological consulting services will be provided to children and their caregivers by specialized and trained staff in 5 sub-districts.
- MWL aims to empower caregivers and parents by providing parenting skills sessions and fostering their awareness through informative sessions for 3000 W&M.
- MWL will offer specialized child protection services (case management, Emergency case fund (ECF), and identification and referral services) for 2000 G&B.
Conduct child protection awareness sessions, utilizing both group and mobile formats, aiming to transform community knowledge, attitudes, and practices regarding child protection and psychosocial support.
- Frontline workers will receive comprehensive training on MHPSS and Psychosocial Support, PSEAH, safeguarding, and accountability measures, ensuring a holistic and impactful implementation of the project.
GBV interventions :
GBV interventions will be in the following paragraph in /Project Summary/ :
- MWL will support women and girls in 5 sub-districts through centers and mobile teams to provide case management services for women and girls at risk or those who have experienced GBV in line with enhanced existing referral pathways, incorporating psychosocial, health, and legal services for those at risk or survivors of GBV.
- MWL will offer life skills activities as a structured program in 5 subdistricts. Also, MWL will provide group psychosocial support (PSS) as recreational activities through mobile services.
