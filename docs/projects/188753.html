Project Summary

Through this project, SI proposes a multisectoral integrated response that will address humanitarian consequences of the crisis and assessed needs for affected most vulnerable Venezuelans. SI will develop a new component aiming to reinforce food security for most vulnerable households through the development of urban agriculture activities at domestic level. The activity is intended to alleviate nutritional insufficiencies (obtaining more diversified diet) at beneficiary HH level whilst also enabling the socio-economically vulnerable HH to obtain small incomes through commercialization of vegetables. The project will contribute to its programmatic approach to improve access to basic WASH services for the most vulnerable Venezuelans in communities and schools through distribution of water storage equipment, domestic water filters and hygiene kits to most vulnerable households, the promotion of good hygiene practices and COVID-19 prevention measures, and WASH rehabilitations in health facilities and schools, supply of hygiene and IPC consumables to health facilities and schools.
To this end, SI will focus on the following axes:
I. Improved food security and diversity for food insecure households through the development of urban micro-gardening;
II. Improved WASH conditions in the targeted communities of vulnerable urban and rural areas.
