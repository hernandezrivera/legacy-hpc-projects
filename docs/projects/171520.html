Project Summary

The overall objective of this action is to protect and promote the WASH rights of the most vulnerable communities in West bank and Gaza. It will do so by achieving two specific objectives (1) strengthen the capacity of 44 grassroots WASH human rights defenders (community advocacy groups) to advocate their access rights to WASH, and (2) advocate the access rights to WASH in West Bank and Gaza, through assessment, documenting and highlighting the violations by producing different advocacy materials and organizing advocacy events with national and international allies. The action builds on PENGON’s experience to protect and promote the rights of EHRDs and will achieve the following results to better anticipate threats and respond more effectively to protect the human rights of WASH activists working at the frontline in area C in West Bank, and the most suffering communities by Israeli Blockade in Gaza;
44 WASH human rights defenders (community advocacy groups) have increased in-house knowledge and awareness of (gender specific) risks, human rights, protection methods and mechanisms, managing risks, monitoring and documenting human rights violations, specifically WASH violations, access emergency response mechanisms, use legal mechanisms, and develop effective advocacy strategies. Through participatory trainings and workshop, the project will build the technical knowledge and capacities of key activists within WASH human rights defenders on (gender specific) risk analysis and management, human rights, protection methods and mechanisms as well as on the monitoring and documentation of WASH human rights abuses and advocacy strategizing. WASH human rights defenders (community advocacy groups) will be able to carefully assess the risks they are facing and identify appropriate measures to protect themselves in their work. They will also have a better understanding of the legal and policy frameworks at the national level that guarantee human rights and the corresponding legal or support mechanism. WASH human rights defenders (community advocacy groups) will gain the essential documenting knowledge and advocacy skills in order to be an active part in advocacy wash campaign. the action will enable the set up a rapid response and Alert system that allows for the quick communication of cases in which settler violence against access rights to WASH from local to global.
Multi-stakeholder WASH assessment and advocacy strategizing will be the second part of this action. This part will include technical assessment report on access restrictions to WASH in area C in West Bank and the same in Gaza. Awareness products on access restrictions to WASH; 2 fact sheets that highlights WASH and Gender needs in Gaza under 15 years of blockade, one public advocacy event in Gaza, international radio interviews, Testimony Film raising women voices in Gaza about WASH rights, preparing legal case focuses on access wash restrictions from H.R and legal perspective, and organizing blog buses for media to highlight different cases of settler violence against access rights to WASH.

1 - Needs

The project demonstrated the existing urgent support needs of communities that are vulnerable to settler violence against access rights to WASH in area C in West Bank and the urgent need for advocating the WASH rights in Gaza while it passes the 15 years of blockade, as well as the need to enhance the structural protection and promotion of their rights. On the basis our learning, PENGON has incorporated the protection and advocating the rights of EHRDs into its organization’s structures and programming. Based on the lobbying strategy to resist Israeli violations that was prepared by PENGON and its allies in 2018, it has also shown the need to continue advocating for access rights to WASH. We seek to build strong local capacities in area C (13 communities) in West Bank and the most marginalized communities in Gaza ( 5 communities) to be able to connect with allies, human rights, legal and WASH organizations to reduce the risks they face and advocate their access rights to WASH. In the West Bank, continuing Israeli settlement expansion and the destruction of homes and livelihoods, alongside other human rights violations, remain major sources of conflict, and have led to a heightened risk of forcible transfer for many Palestinians in Area C. Access to essential services including WASH, healthcare, education, energy and housing is severely restricted for Palestinians mainly in these areas. Settler violence primarily takes place inside Area C, where Israeli settlers are usually protected by the military. 2018 saw a continuing rise in attacks by settlers against Palestinians, with 265 incidents recorded by OCHA in which Israeli settlers killed or injured Palestinians or damaged Palestinian property. This was a 69% increase from the year before.For Gaza, it has experienced continuous WASH deterioration since the Israeli blockade and closure was imposed in 2007. It has also had serious economic, social and humanitarian consequences for most of its 2 million inhabitants, which have been exacerbated by three wars and continued Israeli aggression since the blockade. The two million Palestinians in Gaza are living in an open-air prison under Israeli occupation and siege. Based on a technical paper that was published by PENGON in 2020, the impact of environmental pollutions on health that was assessed in the technical paper alerts the essential need for urgent advocacy interventions to save the catastrophic WASH sector in Gaza.There is an urgent need for the WASH human rights defenders (community advocacy groups) to build alliances with support organizations at national level or with organizations working at the regional or international level to raise and expose the settler violence against access rights to WASH in West Bank and advocating the WASH rights in Gaza. It will break the isolation of WASH human rights defenders (community advocacy groups) advocating for the rights of their communities as they can leverage a wide support network for accessing justice and protection. PENGON has an established a documentation system for environmental human rights violations in the Hebron governorate. However, many cases go unnoticed because local activists do not have the information, skills or access to communicate. It is therefore paramount to create a system that allows local community groups to communicate and access support from national to international networks.The immediate support and urgent response mechanism in the cases of violations against access rights to WASH is essential to empower the affected communities and defending the rights of these communities. The creation of rapid response system will enhance their access to justice. It is essentially needed to assess and highlight the current situation in relation to the access restrictions to WASH in order to be a tool for advocacy strategizing in the high risk areas in West Bank and Gaza. It should be worked on an innovative advocacy methodology to protect and promote the access rights to WASH, the project will allow to produce advocacy materials for the local/national level to highlight WASH rights in the targeted areas, focused on gender need. This action will bring Palestinian women to the centre of a discussion about the gendered dimensions of Israel’s violence. Through this actions we will highlight the hotspot cases for violations among women, children, and elder. This action will highlight how the violations for the access rights to WASH brings the gender and occupation to the fore.

2 - Activities

1. Capacity building- 44 WASH human right defenders (13 community advocacy groups in area C, 5 in Gaza)Through participatory trainings and workshop, the project will build the technical knowledge and capacities of key activists within WASH human right defenders (community advocacy groups) on (gender specific) risk analysis and management, human rights, protection methods and mechanisms as well as on the monitoring and documentation of WASH rights abuses and advocacy strategizing. WASH human right defenders (community advocacy groups) will be able to carefully assess the risks they are facing and identify appropriate measures to protect themselves in their work. They will also have a better understanding of the legal and policy frameworks at the national, and international level that guarantee human rights and the corresponding legal or support mechanisms. The following criteria have been developed for people who will be in the community advocacy groups and who will participate in the trainings: - women and men who have already been facing settler violence or are exposed to risks; - women and men who hold leadership and active within the community; - women and men who show an interest and willingness to learn and engage on WASH-human rights issues; - young women and men who are keen to work with their communities.2. Creating Rapid Response System: The objective of this work stream is to set up an interconnected system from local to global to be able to monitor settler violence against access rights to WASH and to respond effectively and quickly to provide short-term remedy, as well as to mobilize national and international solidarity and action in specific cases. WASH human right defenders (community advocacy groups) will set up structures and processes to apply their technical knowledge to manage identified risks, monitor, document and respond to WASH violations. They will take appropriate security measures on the basis of their own risk assessment. And together with the national partners, the action will enable the set up a WASH Monitoring and Alert system that allows for the quick communication of cases in which WASH rights are at imminent risk. They will set up simple systems to register and document WASH violations and quickly and safely communicate cases to access emergency or legal support. Furthermore, they will have developed advocacy strategies for their work that take into account the risks. They will therefore be better equipped to work together with national human rights and legal support networks, and engage with media, policy makers and other stakeholders.3. Cluster needs assessments conducted on access restrictions to WASH: technical assessment report will be prepared on access restrictions to WASH in area C in West Bank and in Gaza. The aim of this assessment report is to increase the access to information for WASH cluster and allow them to plan an effective advocacy interventions and strategy reduces the settler violence against the access rights to WASH and saving the WASH rights in Gaza.4. Organizing advocacy campaign for access rights to WASH: This activity has four interrelated elements to advocating for access rights to WASH, 1. developing awareness products on access restrictions to WASH:1.1 Preparing fact sheet: “Women's access to water and sanitation in Gaza during COVID 19”1.2 Preparing fact sheet “Gaza Blockade and WASH”1.3 Testimony Film: raise women voices in Gaza about their WASH rights.1.4 Radio interviews: producing 2 interviews in international radios to highlight the impact of Gaza blockade on WASH rights at international level.1.5 photo exhibition Gaza- 15 years of Blockade, demands to international community will be raised using photographic, different violations against WASH rights will be raised through photographs. A local exhibition will be organized in addition to sharing these photographs internationally within our allies. 2. Organizing public advocacy event in Gaza to raise their WASH demands and sending messages to international community and the main actors at local and international level. This advocacy event demanding " the end of the siege on Gaza", will be coordinated with PENGON international allies.3. Legal report: preparing legal study focuses on access wash restrictions from H.R and legal perspective. This legal report will take pilot cases in area c.4. Organizing 2 blog buses for media specialists to 4 sites in Area C in West Bank. The participants will write media articles on different cases that will be used in different media tools.
