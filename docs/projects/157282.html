Project Summary

Based on Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2019, Sudan host large number of vulnerable populations including those affected by natural disasters, armed conflict and drought, some two millions are still displaced of which 75% are women and children. Education is one of the gap services for these affected population mainly in, North Darfur(ND) in Dar El Salam, and Blue Nile (BN)(Tadamon & Damazine) ,these localities considered the most accessible areas, the need for quality education is increasing across these areas with humanitarian needs. Children of displaced communities and refugees are adding pressure to existing education facilities which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources. Education remains challenges where enrollment for basic education stands at 40 % average. Moreover, as macro-economic challenges impact Sudanese people’s lives nowadays, following the decrease in purchasing power let many households unable to cope with schooling costs. As a result, children are at risk in term of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor. This project aim to increase access to quality education services for the most disadvantaged and vulnerable children .The intervention aim to build the capacity of education stakeholders (Parent committees s, teachers, headmasters, government officials and local CBOs ) to mitigate the impact of conflict on education service delivery by target a total of 2800 students , 99 % will be children and youth (age 6- 13), 1% will be adults including education personnel. Both the assessment needs and the severity ranking based on vulnerability will be used to identify needs and priorities to determine targets at a locality level. The following activities will be conducted; the establishment of learning spaces (rehabilitation/ construction classroom semi -permanent materials);capacity-building of teachers and line government officials; provision of school learning supplies; and promoting community and children’ participation. The indicators were; children aged between 6 and 13 years have access to basic education or Accelerated Learning Programmed ,)the % of nonfunctional or closed schools; and number /percentage of children with access to teaching and learning materials, the quality of safe access to education for children in these locations. The selection criteria will be bases on vulnerability. The project will coordinate closely with the Ministry of Education to improve the quality of life skills training for girls and boys and youth , including the disable, to strengthen social integration and protection through formation of strong mechanism such as steering committees, community leaders, parents association, social workers this guarantee local community ownership which in turn will ensure project effectiveness, efficiency and sustainability . Furthermore, the implementation will be in partnership between Al Rohama and Gayat for peace and development with clear role and responsibilities determine their commitment and quality assurance , both organizations have offices will equipped with professional staff in leadership and commitment to del0verly timely and high quality performance , Al have more than ten years experiences in provision of school facilities , while Gayat in addition to unique experiences in school programs has experienced in e-learning specially with droup out school children in South Kordofan and Darusalam Jabell Awlia which will guarantees the safe implementation of the project

Activities

activity 1 : the provision of teaching, learning and recreational materials;activity 2 :the construction/rehabilitation furnishing of semi-permanent classrooms; activity 3 :-the rehabilitation of permanent classrooms and premises, including green spaces; -the provision of gender sensitive WASH facilities in schools/learning spaces; -capacity-building for teachers and other education personnel; the mobilization and participation of communities and children in school-based activities. Activity 4 : support community engagement and interaction with schools though parent teacher association (PTA) meetings, school management communities meetings --establishment of complaints and feedback mechanisms; to support activities that contribute to child protection and gender-based violence (GBV) prevention including psychosocial support, Construction of new class rooms using environmentally friendly materials –Activity 5: -Provide seedling trees for schools to enhance the environment - Rehabilitation and construction of school latrines Activity 6 : Provide solar energy for school lighting - Provide gender sensitive WASH services for schools include hygiene promotion – Activity 7: Establish child clubs focusing on psycho – social support - Conduct sessions on role of children participating in school hygiene campaigns Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR), and referral pathways; and to support with school grants to cover school running costs, school feeding and awareness on nutrition and establishment of referral systems for multiple functions including nutrition, GBV .Activity 8: Coordination meetings with State and local authorities include HAC and line ministries to get permissions to access the project sites and consultations - Community mobilization and sensitization through sessions and meeting with stakeholders to inform about the project –Activity 9 : Conduct further Rapid Needs Assessment to identify gabs on education to be shared with concerned bodies.
