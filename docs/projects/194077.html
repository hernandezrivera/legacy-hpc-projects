1 - Needs

According to the recent study (Voices from Sudan) carried out by the United NationsPopulation Fund and the Unit to Combat Violence against Women, we have designed ourprogram according to the results of the study. We will target (areas affected by violence)by providing legal aid to survivors of violence based on social awareness, in addition toraising awareness on Gender-based violence focusing on vulnerable people such aschildren, domestic workers, people with disabilities According to a study of Voices fromSudan, the most areas that we will target are: (Darfur and Khartoum states, River Nilestate, Red Sea, Gezira, Gedaref, Blue Nile, South and West Kordofan). There are higherrates of domestic violence, sexual violence, forced marriage, and circumcision in thesestates. female

2 - Activities/Outputs

Our activities will be as follows: Providing legal services to gender survivorsViolence in Khartoum State, River Nile, Blue Nile, Northern State This service will be provided byLawyers from our organization Target number = 500 survivors- Awareness sessions targeting all segments of society in (Khartoum states, River Nile state,Al Jazeera and Blue Nile (on the causes of gender-based violence and how to address it, target number = 5000 beneficiaries - Holding training workshops for activists and community leaders on gender-based violence in the targeted states Target number = 500 The target number of the project is 6000 beneficiaries

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Accountability to the affected population is core in the mutawinat response and will bestrengthened through continuous consultation with the affected population on theresponse modalities as well as their satisfaction with services provided. In addition,mutawinat is an active member of the national PSEA technical and coordination teamand leads the survivor support task team and together is working with the teammembers to ensure that GBV referral mechanisms are accessible to survivors of SEA.mutawinat will equip implementing partners and targeted communities withinformation regarding the SEA, and how to report and access services including health,legal and psychosocial support services. mutawinat partners and beneficiaries play anactive role in the decision-making process in regard to the planning, implementing, andevaluating of activities.
