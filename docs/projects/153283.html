Project Summary

The outbreak of conflict in South Sudan in 2013 and renewed violence in 2016 resulted into internal displacements of persons (IDPs) in South Sudan. Several South Sudanese took refuge in the neighbouring countries. Some IDPs end up in protection of civilian sites/camps (POC) under United Nations protection while others like those in Lake state immersed within the host community.
South Sudanese (IDPs & refugee) gave several reasons for displacements or fleeing the country. The armed conflict is the main while others includes; economic hardship, Vandalization of public and individual homes and assets, looting of individual properties and Human rights violations/abuses. Several reports indicate that the Human rights violations and abuses against people fleeing the conflict were perpetrated by government SPLA-soldiers and armed groups. Some of the violations or abuses include; torture, enforced disappearance, arbitrary detention, prejudicial killing and abduction.
The IDPs and the host communities faced and are facing similar hardships, violations or abuses mentioned above mostly on their way to safe protected places or as they search for a living.
AMA intends to intervene and respond to the above mentioned violations, abuses and hardships in Lakes state (Yirol East, Yirol West, Maper and Amongpiny). The primary target group in this project include; persons who suffered displacement or whose rights were violated.
The purpose of the project is to develop mechanisms to respond to violations/abuses, general protection concerns, legal aid and to people and their properties/asset in the mentioned project locations. Additionally, the project aim to form and enhance capacity of community focal points persons (CFPs) and Local government Authorities (LGA) to be able to respond to general protection concerns, the violations/ abuses of the rights of all persons in the project locations.

Needs

The armed conflict in South Sudan caused displacement, Human rights violations/abuses or other forms of violence, economic hardship, Vandalization of public and individual homes and assets and looting of individual properties. There is need to document the above violations. Perpetrators need to be held to account for their action to deter others from committing similar violations or end impunity in South Sudan. The Human rights violations and abuses against people fleeing the conflict were reported to have been perpetrated by government SPLA-soldiers and armed groups in the mentioned project locations. Some of the violations or abuses includes; torture, enforced disappearance, arbitrary detention, prejudicial killing, abduction and vandalization of properties or assets. Direct and indirect victims and the general public need to see justice being done. The IDPs or Returnees in the host communities faced and are facing similar hardships, violations or abuses mentioned above mostly on their way to search for a living. IDPs/returnees need to be empowered. The host communities need to be educated on the rights of IDPs and returnees. It is base on this assessed information and needs that AMA is developing this project. AMA with the fund from this project will attempt to address these numerous needs, general protection concerns, violations or abuses in the implementation phase of the project.

Activities

AMA will carry out the following activities to address the needs identified above under this project. 1. Monitoring the movement of IDPs, documenting and reporting violations and abuses of human rights in Yirol East, Yirol West, Maper and Amongpiny Counties. With the help of the county local authorities, AMA will identified community based focal point persons (CFPs), train the CFPs on basic human rights laws and form the CFPs in pairs to make the CFPs monitor, document and report human rights violations and abuses to legal Aid Officers to intervene or respond. 2. Providing general protection in Coordination with protection clusters. AMA will identify partners' organization that are members of general protection cluster and will coordinate in providing necessary protection to people in need.3. Establishment of four legal aid clinics (litigation) in the four counties (Yirol East, Yirol West, Maper & Amongpiny). AMA will establish the four legal aid clinics and employ four Legal Aid Officers that will be base in the project locations with the aim of providing legal aid (pro Bono) to victims of grave human rights violations, abuses and violations of rights to land, housing and properties. 4. Advocacy for the respect and general protection of the rights of people in the mentioned project locations. AMA will advocate for the respect, general protection and promotion of human rights at the local level of government. AMA will advocate for the enactment of laws/polices that shall provide general protection to people at the local level and at the national level.5. Carrying out Awareness raising (36 Radio talk shows) on general protection, human rights and legal service that will be provided at the legal Aid Clinics. AMA will use the available radio stations to educate the public on general protection, human rights and legal service that will be provided at the legal Aid Clinics. There will be conducted nine (9) talks shows in each location or nearby town in the first nine month of the implementation phase of the project. Questions and feed backs from the talk shows shall be use for advocacy. 6. Capacity building of local authorities. AMA will train at least 160 members of LGA, forty in each of the project locations. The LGA will be supported to enact and implement laws/policies that will support general protection, respect and promote human rights. 7. Training of community focal points persons (CFP). AMA will identify, select, train and form a group to be referred to as (CFPs). CFPs will be able to monitor, document and report general protection concerns, the movement of IDPs or their needs and all forms of human rights violations or abuses that people in the project locations are subjected into. The documented cases or concerns will inform humanitarian respond and legal intervention.

Briefly describe how

Many South Sudanese and people in South Sudan became victims of torture, enforced disappearance, arbitrary detention, prejudicial killing and abduction.
