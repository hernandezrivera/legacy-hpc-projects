Project Summary

Description of intervention: - This project will provide 100% in-kind ES/NFI support to new IDPs, people with new vulnerabilities and protracted IDPs, returnees and HC who are not able to support themselves as effect of covid19 pandemic. The total target is 8,400 individuals (1,680 HHs). Through gift-in-kind (GIK) distribution, the project intends to support 3,360 individuals (672 HHs) with emergency shelter materials and 5,040 individuals (1008 HHs) with life saving non food items (NFIs). The items will be sourced from pipeline through signing of MoU with cluster and raising pipeline request to cluster for approval upon approval of verification/registration reports in order to access the items for distributions. The items will be transported for distribution by log cluster.
- CHAS will conduct joint in depth needs analysis and protection risk assessment with the involvement of protection staff and the participation of the targeted populations through consultative community meetings, key informant interviews, focus group discussions and household level and individual interviews with men, women, boys and girls .
- During assessment, CHAS will use the SNFI Toolkit as a guide to review conflict sensitivity, HLP issues, GBV referral pathways, and safety audit findings. CHAS will work with HLP Partners - NRC to promote security of tenure for IDPs and returnees. CHAS do in-depth HLP assessments before provision of emergency shelter material to the affected population.
- CHAS will use advantage of its static presence in Uror to respond rapidly to any S/NFI emergency and engage more with communities within the county at cost effective manner. CHAS will coordinate its activities with Jonglei SFP and other partners (ADA, Nile Hope,HDC,Reach-Africa&Tearfund) in order to share resource and avoid duplication. Since CHAS is statically present, CHAS employ coordinated response with other partners in its area of operations by providing relevant information about the area and planning joint responses. CHAS will act as a SiFP.
-CHAS will employ integrated approach through coordination of activities with SFPs and SiFPs other clusters –FSL, WASH, Health, Education, Protection and CCCM and including partners out of cluster system in the targeted location through newly proposed humanitarian hub. CHAS will use the hub for coordination of joint missions with other S/NFI and non S/NFI partners, especially the mobile partners from Juba and static partners on the ground and for sourcing items for quick response to emergencies in hard-to-reach areas.
- CHAS will establish complaint and feedback mechanisms in its intervention locations through establishment of community led complaint and feedback committees/ relief committees and engage them throughout the project cycle. CHAS will provide all categories of affected population (men, women, boys, girls,) with accurate and updated information about the intervention through preferred community communication channels and will clearly explain any feedback to the community and ensure that informationn interventions is provided in formats and appropriate languages reaching everyone. CHAS will as share feedback from post distribution monitoring with the community.
- CHAS will conduct covid19 infection prevention awareness campaign alongside other project activities through announcements and use of posters and banners.
- Joint Post-distribution monitoring (PDM) will be conducted 1 month after the intervention by the gender balanced M&E team, RRC leaders beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries to measure the impact of the projects and to determine if the assistance reached the most vulnerable groups. Community committees will be formed for complaint/feedback mechanism and to insure community participation and empowerment at all phases of project cycle. Whereas continuous rapid monitoring will be conducted during distribution days. Feedback on the findings will be shared with affected population and with SNFI Cluster which will feed into ICWG.
PRP Pillar:
Geographical coverage: Uror
Activity: 1- Needs analysis of target covid beneficiaries while observing covid19 infection prevention measures. 2- Registration of target covid beneficiaries. 3- Distribution of emergency shelter materials (wooden, poles, bamboo, plastic sheets an rubber ropes) for shelter construction or shelter upgrades in PoCs and Collective Centres to improve living spaces for vulnerable populations, and the provision of basic NFIs (blankets, plastic sheeting, mosquito nets, and kitchen sets etc.) 4- Covid19 infection prevention awareness campaign in target intervention area. 5- Establish hand washing facilities during distributions. 6- Providing emergency shelters for isolation in order to support government effort in combating covid19 pendamic., Total estimated beneficiaries = 5300
