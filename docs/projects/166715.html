Project Summary

1. Recruit and train Case Managers/GBV responders on prevention, response and remote reporting of gender-based violence. Due to COVID-19 adolescent girls experience increased risks of like premature marriages, sexual exploitation and abuse. GCR will focus mainly adolescent boys and girls including CWD between 10-17 years old.
2. Conduct community sensitization/awareness and campaigns using mass media, community radio and digital media to provide children, including adolescents, and their communities with access to information and communication tools for children on hygienic and prevention measures on COVID-19 and to increase knowledge on the available GBV services and the importance of timely reporting, care and treatment. GCR will intensify dissemination of Child Help Line (116/2) and case management
3. Disseminate safe, trusted and confidential channels for children, including adolescents, to report violence, exploitation and abuse like the CHL
4. Conduct mapping of available GBV services based the type of GBV cases and Hold provincial and district meetings with DPGCAS to strengthen Multi-sectorial coordination of the GBV Technical Working Groups at both levels and improve referral pathways to ensure and facilitate safe referral of allegations to the GBV service providers at district and provincial level.
5. Roll out GBV screening to identify children mostly girls who may have experienced GBV, and to improve rates of early detection and referral to health, psychosocial, and protection services using a child survivor centered approach of confidentiality, efficiency, and effectiveness. GCR will conduct screening based only on the types of violence where quality referral options are available and accessible for both clinical care and GBV case management support.
6. Hold provincial and district meetings with DPGCAS to strengthen Multi-sectorial coordination of the GBV Technical Working Groups at both levels and improve referral pathways to ensure and facilitate safe referral of allegations to the GBV service providers at district and provincial level.
7. Conduct remote and mobile GBV Case management for Children who have experienced GBV / survivors in close collaboration with CCPCs. GCR will provided bicycles and some cell phone and PPE kits to some CCPP members in Manica and Sofala to strengthen CP mechanisms and case management and psychosocial support through technological mechanism at community level
8. Train GCR - Linha Fala Crianca councilors and Staff on CP during COVID-19, PSEA and remote case management
9. Provide case workers and most vulnerable childrens including CWD and caregivers with necessary PPE for protection and hygienic measures
10. Support the referral of SEA and GBV survivors to assistance services and referral of allegations to the agency/organization of concern for investigation and action.
11. Procure and distribute dignity kits in COVID-19 to IDPs and women and girls in hard-to-reach areas have access to essential items.
12. Provide integrated SRH and GBV mobile services, in parallel with strengthening of hotline services (LFC - COVID-19) to include provision of counseling services, psychosocial support, safe referral, case management and information services.
