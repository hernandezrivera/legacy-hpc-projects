Project Summary

Based on community participation, the project is designed based on the following: Increasing the food security and enhancing the livelihoods of IDPs and host communities
(50% IDPs, 50 % host communities) in Idlib and Aleppo Governorates through the following:
The first stage: (support value chains for strategic crops such as wheat and olive crops).
This intervention will seek to support these households to begin to restore this production, as main sources of livelihoods and to enhance their resilience by enabling them to have more food availability through personal production of the crop products in addition to improving the crops value chain by addressing the gaps during the chain (Production, processing, marketing).
The number of beneficiaries from agriculture interventions will be 15500 HHs.
Key interventions will include:
• Provision vouchers to buy agricultural inputs, accompanied by related extension services.
• Capacity building targeting farmers and local community members.
• Support olive oil marketing and wheat marketing by purchasing olive oil and wheat from beneficiaries to help farmers to market their products.
The M&E department will conduct an assessment of gaps and bottlenecks within the value chains of strategic crops and ATAA will design an intervention that addresses these gaps along the chain stages.
The second stage:(Support livestock sector).
This intervention will include:
1- Supporting 5000 livestock breeders with veterinary services (vaccines, antiparasite, artificial insemination, and treatments).
2- Supporting 1500 livestock breeders by distributing 1000 MT fodder.
3- Support 1500 HHs with poultry (9 Layer hens and 1 rooster, feed, and chicken coop).
4- Establishing a veterinary laboratory to support animal breeders and veterinarians by providing appropriate diagnosis and effective treatment of antimicrobials quickly and effectively.
ATAA also plans to increase the awareness of the host community and the IDPs related to the Coronavirus risk and the need to take preventive measures in addition.
 ATAA will identify, verify, and profile target beneficiaries through local councils according to the eligibility selection criteria:
• The shortlist will be identified depending on the vulnerability criteria
• Women headed households (widows, divorced, GBV survivors).
• Large families (more than 5).
• Families with disabled members.
• Families with low income (less than 50 USD)
• Families with elderly (men-women).
 ATAA will remain AAP through open dialogue, that accurate and timely information (including goals, outcomes, and any eligibility criteria to participate) will share with them.

4 - Needs

• According to Food and Agricultural Organization (FAO), high cost and limited availability of quality crop and livestock production inputs, damaged irrigation infrastructure across the country, degradation of livelihoods and income earning opportunities, coupled with the direct and knock-on effects of Covid-19 and climate-induced shocks continue to drive more people into food insecurity (HNO 2021).ATAA conducted RNA by M&E and our technical team in order to get more clarity and information on current situation in NWS.50 interviews were conducted with farmers, livelihoods KIIs and households from the local community and authority. The assessment covered 39 communities in 14 sub-districts in 7 different districts (Jarablus, A'zaz, Ariha, Harim, Idleb, Jisr-Ash-Shugur) in Aleppo and Idleb governorate. Based on assessment results the Key Findings was as following: 1- There are many areas that mainly depend on agriculture production (strategic crops) and contain irrigation sources. High prices of agricultural inputs (fertilizers, fuel, and pesticides) led to the reluctance of farmers to cultivate their lands. 2- There are many gaps within the value chains of strategic crops in terms of agricultural inputs and stages of processing in addition to marketing.3- Lack of modern techniques and laboratory service for the diagnosing infectious animal diseases increase the period and price of treatment, decreases the productivity of animal and the daily income of breeders.4- Epidemic and infectious diseases, e.g. PPR, FMD, LSD, smallpox lead to increase the mortality of animals. 5-Weak livestock support services and low numbers of well-trained field veterinarians which lower livestock productivity.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Risk: Female beneficiaries/Project disabled beneficiaries experience sexual exploitation or abuse by the project trainers/other staff, which endangers lives of the perpetrator and victims. ATAA will lose the project funding accordingly. ATAA Office, assets and other field staff will get under risk, as well.Mitigation:ATAA have developed PSEA, Code of Conduct, and Child Safeguarding policies and mechanisms in place to ensure every effort is made to protect all Project BNFs. ATAA PSEA Mechanism is designed around four core components: Awareness Raising (for staff regarding power dynamics, risk and vulnerabilities etc.), Prevention (awareness regarding the existing controls which are in place and how PSEA is prevented), Reporting (how participants can report and what are the specific steps which need to be taken regarding SEA cases) and Response (how and what ATAA will do when cases of SEA are reported). A similar mechanism is available for CSG where the staff are familiar with how to approach and deal with CSG cases of child abuse, exploitation or violence. All Project staff will sign ATAA CSG and PSEA Policies to ensure a safe environment for all Project beneficiaries. A dedicated channel for CSG and SEA related complaints will be available through dedicated WA number to ensure such complaints go to PSEA and CSG Focal Point in Turkey office directly.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Markets are functioning and all the inputs are available. The crops varieties are stapled and adapted to the environment. There is social acceptance for the project and the people are prepared to interact with it. ATAA will contract with many local markets to avoid fraud Where risk has been classified into protection, environmental and security risks. Environmental risks: 1-Plant diseases will be addressed through the distribution of pesticides in a timely manner in addition to strict observation by the competent agronomists through continuous tours of farmers' fields to investigate any diseases and advise farmers accordingly. 2- Fire during the harvest season: there is a high risk during the harvest due to factors such as high temperature or neglect, in addition to the Deliberate shelling during harvest. Raising the awareness of the local community about the dangers of fire and methods to mitigate it by: -Removing the weeds on the roadside -Work the lines of fire (firebreaks)between farmers' lands to prevent the spread of fire from one field to another. -Coordination with the Civil Defense -Distribute awareness-raising brochures to the community about the importance of cooperation to avoid fires. -Security risk: This will be processed with strict follow-up by the S&S Department. for livestock intervention the risks will be addressed as follows: A.In the case of difficulty in securing the quantity of required sheep from one location: We will purchase from more than one supplier according to the approved procurement policies and alternative supply plans. B.beneficiaries may negligee sheep and the occurrence of some acute diseases that cause mortality or poor production: This risk will be addressed based on the beneficiary
