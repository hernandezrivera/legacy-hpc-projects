Project Summary

The proposed project will facilitate rehabilitation of 20 broken down boreholes (5000 people) in 8 wards of Buhera South district. Water point Commitees for all the established 20 boreholes and 20 Village pump mechanics shall be strengthened .To make WPC sustainable the project will promote Savings and Internal Lending (SILC).

1 - Activities

This project seeks to increase access to clean water to drought and Cyclone Idai affected households in 8 wards of Buhera South district. A technical assessment shall jointly be done with the community to identify boreholes for rehabilitation and establish bill of quantities. Two boreholes that have broken down for a long time shall be flushed before pump installation. WPC and VPM will be strengthened and Savings Internal Lending Communities (SILC) will be promoted to strengthen sustainability of WPC.PAWR II project will be implemented in 8 wards of Buhera South. The project will work closely with local stakeholders in identifying broken down boreholes and determining bill of quantities. Boreholes that have been broken down for a long time will be flushed before rehabilitation. Establishment and training of WPC and VPM will be jointly done with local stakeholders. Head-works and fencing of rehabilitated boreholes will be done as well as water quality testing. SILC will be promoted to increase sustainability of WPC.
