Project Summary

Supply of school furniture to 50 classrooms constructed; FH already built classrooms using fund from ECW and KOICA to construct the 30 classrooms in 7 schools.
WASH in schools; construction of latrines in schools to meet the standard latrine pupils to latrine ratio of 1:50, train children to make tippy taps, hand washing behaviors and practices, installation of hand washing facilities in schools.
Teacher and students trainings on disaster risk reduction (prevention, mitigation, coping strategies, preparedness).
Advocacy and campaigns on key risks, resiliency, build back better among the teachers, students and community.
Children protection campaigns: formation of children protection clubs in schools, child protection committees in the communities and sensitizing the communities on child protection issues.
Rehabilitation of 20 classrooms which were destroyed by the cyclone within Dondo district.
Construction of 15 new cyclone resistant classrooms in 3 schools to be identified and used as models.
Strengthen school councils and children clubs on mapping and drawing DRR plans.
Promotion of reusable sanitary pads in schools (training female teachers and adolescent girls on making reusable pads) and distribution of the sanitary pads in schools.
Value for education campaigns within the communities for children and parents.
