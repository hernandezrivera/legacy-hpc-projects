Project Summary

The Syrian conflict will enter its twelfth year, however, the need for life-saving assistance is still present in many areas and at the same time some opportunities for long-term interventions are emerging.
The proposed activities were built based on the real needs that exist on the ground with link with HRP 2022 for same areas .
Deep understanding of the reality and need, and more than Eight years of experience in WASH services and intervention in NWS ,
Therefore, the proposed activities will directly support the implementation of the 2022 Humanitarian
Response Plan (HRP) for WASH sector . The permanent water, sanitation and hygiene services for the
conflict-affected population in the Idleb ,Aleppo , Al-Hasakeh and Ar-Raqqa regions ranked high in the severity of the water, sanitation and hygiene needs.
The proposed intervention is a comprehensive and integrated intervention for water supply, solid waste management, hygiene practices, and sewage system. In this plan, some long-term and sustainable interventions will be added, such as a solar energy system for water pumping and a sewage treatment system to become suitable for irrigation to support the agricultural sector in North Western Syria and to mitigate the effects of climate change ,
The proposed areas were selected based on People in need and severity scale in the draft HNO 2022 / WASH sector ,
All of the above activities will benefit approx. 196,750 beneficiaries ( 45,525 Women , 43,285 Men , 55,090 Girls and 53,123 Boys ) , including around 117,000 IDPs , and around 12,500 Returnees ,
This will be achieved through delivery of an integrated and comprehensive response to
potable water, Solid Waste Management (SWM) and sanitation needs of approx. 184,535
individuals of which an estimated 76,083 IDP. Areas have been selected based on People
in Need (PIN) and Severity of Need as presented in the draft Humanitarian Needs
Overview (HNO) for 2021.
As we mentioned above , the proposed activities were build in line with the strategic objectives of WASH cluster in HRP 2022,
Part of the proposed activities includes some sustainability solutions through the
application of a hybrid energy system in some water stations or linking water stations to
the electricity network in the areas that have electricity access, Connecting the camps to the nearest available water source to find an alternative solution to the water supply process through water truckıng in addition to starting the application of a cost recovery system Fair and built on community engagement.
Hygiene promotion activities will continue to be offered in clustering areas, with a special
focus on preventing and responding to COVID-19 ,
Moreover, one of the proposed interventions is waste water treatment plants to convert the black water to become irrigable water to alleviate the suffering of farmers in finding clean water for irrigation and to mitigate the negative effects of sewage in open areas and at the end to mitigate the effects of climate change

4 - Needs

Several concurrent assessment sources emphasize the need to address water access and water quality provision in the proposed area of operation.Comprehensive WASH assessments were conducted in mid-2021 to inform the HNO 2022 These assessments included a representative, individual-level household survey that focused specially in IDP camps and informal settlements in NWS and NES .The results indicate a slight increase in the overall water share received from the water distribution networks in 2021 compared to previous years. However, the results also indicate that the water chlorination level remains largely insouciant, and that many people are still forced to complement the water they receive from the network with other sources or to solely rely on these alternative sources. ,However,unregulated water operators often offer poor quality water,thereby seriously increasing the risk for their consumers of contracting waterborne disease. Results also show that limitations in regular operations, as well as delays in maintenance and repairs have had a significant impact on the regularity of water supply . According to HNAP assessment of PIN’s WASH activities in the area in Dec 2021, the PIN rate in Afrin and A'zaz 100% of total population , While in Idleb Gov PIN rate is around 90 % , 53 % in Tell Abiad in Al Raqqa and 100 % in Ras Al Ain , some of these areas has even reached level 4 out 6 which is considered extreme. (Sources :ACU platform , HNO draft WAS cluster , HH survey HNO22 )

5 - Implementation of activities

Planned proposed activities will improve public health and mitigate against the risk of COVID-19 through ensuring adequate access to improved WASH services. Sustainability of WASH service provision is built into program design through the provision of solar system with the necessary technical support to establish cost recovery mechanisms already started with the proposed intervention through support and rehabilitation 3water stations in Afrin and 5 in Idleb with supporting more than 57 camps in NWS ,in addition to 3 water stations in Tell Abiad , At same time ,SR will support the IDPs sites with life saving activities ( SWM , WT , Des and HP ) , with connect the camps to water network if applicable .The suitability solution will be in Sanitation field also , through connect the IDP sites to main sewer line if applicable , at community level the proposed intervention will help us to improve the infrastructure including sewage and water pipes , the climate changes will be considering in our intervention through construct water water treatment plant at communities and IDP sites level to convert the black water to irrigation water , and that will be with full coordination with Agriculture sector ,Complementary hygiene promotion and sensitization activities will increase awareness and understanding of COVID-19 prevention and response, in addition to other positive hygiene practices.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

In line with WASH cluster , SR will ensure " Don no harm "principles are applied to identify and mitigate against potentialprotection risks that may arise from the implementation of the WASH sector strategy andrelated activities. We will ensure that SPHEREstandards are strictly followed and adaptedto the context during each part of project implementation, especially during emergencysanitation infrastructure implementation phase when the risk of GBV is higher. Measureswill also be taken to minimize exposure of the most vulnerable groups (women,adolescent girls, children, people with disabilities, female‑headed households) to greaterprotection risks, and particularly to GBV. WASH staff will be trained on key GBV conceptsand referral pathways to properly support survivors. Women, adolescent girls and peoplewith disabilities will be consulted during the design, implementation and monitoringphase of each project, with a need to put the necessary mechanisms in place to facilitatefeedback from users. We design our projects with an emphasis on meaningful access,safety, dignity and non‑discrimination so that all potential beneficiaries will bene􀂦t frominclusive service provision to the greatest extent possible ,

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

SR has security staff at Turkey and Syria level and they are able to identify the risk and improved risk mitigation ,SR will create a risk register, in which it will identify relevant risks, conduct their analysis and their evaluation by identifying mitigation measures. This will allow to have understanding of the risks and agree on the internal controls to be applied and monitored.Overview of the risks and internal controls:The risk of fraud and misappropriation ofassets and supplies due to inadequate financial controls,the lack of segregation of dutiesand personal integrity. This will be mitigated by having robust internal procedures with theadoption of checks and balances allowing the prevention and detection of fraud.monitoring teams from SR is present in the field to report irregularities by monitoring anddocumenting the delivery of activities and conduct post-distribution monitoring.thorough stock management process is established with a field capacity to spot-checkwarehouses and conduct stock counts. whistleblower hotline is available to all projectstakeholders and they are aware of it early in the project to report any suspected act offraud or corruption. An emphasis will be put on the risk of mishandling PersonallyIdentifiable Information (PII) related to project beneficiaries, staff,contractors, vendors,To prevent the spread of COVID-19, staff are trained onCOVID-19 protocols issued by Health, Protection and WASH clusters.
