Project Summary

There is a growing need for the amelioration of the educational sector in the north west especially as most schools have been affected by the ongoing crisis in the North west and South West Regions. ITAKA Cameroon has as purpose is to ensure that children have the best education and are safe, in school and learning. ITAKA Cameroon specializes in developing solutions for education and protection provision in Cameroon especially in emergencies, such as in highly impoverished regions, as the protracted conflicts. In complex contexts such as the ongoing armed conflict in the NWSW regions, where education has been attacked by parties to the conflict, protection and psychosocial support remains top on the list of needs. Specifically, most of our schools in the catholic mission has been destroyed by fire, benches and books lost to fire, and our children affected psychological by the ongoing armed conflict. More so, many teachers do not have knowledge on psychosocial support to better handle the affected children in the schools. Therefore, Foundation ITAKA hopes to address this situation through provision of learning materials (Books, benches, tables, chairs computers) to 10 Catholic Schools affected by the crisis, Train Teachers on psychosocial support and grant learning materials to underprivileged children.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

From the education cluster strategy for 2024,, there is an increasing need to strengthen education in the North West and South West Regions as many schools are still not functional like it use to be. Specifically in the North West, and in Mezam Division in particular many of our schools have witness a considerable loss of property (learning materials and Infrastructure) as a result of the increasing attacks on education. On the other hand, leaners too are face with challenge of accessing quality education due to lack of adequate means to acquire basic leaning materials, as well as been challenge by the general context of the crisis and as such affected psychologically, while others simply lack the means to attend schools as the crisis has hit the parents badly that they cant afford to send the children to school due to lack of money. Our proposed integrated education and child protection project has been designed based on Multi Sectorial Needs Assessment of education 2023 and child protection needs amongst IDP and affected children and our organizational need assessment. Our assessment found that only 53% of our schools has suffered a hug loss of school materials, IDP children are attending school regularly but face psychosocial support challenges, to effectively ITAKA Cameroon will identify 10 beneficiary school in Mezam Divion provide highly affected by the crisis in the north west region.
