Project Summary

Camp 8 West for Rohingya Refugees & Ratna Palang Union is situated in Ukhiya Sub-district of Cox's Bazar District, Bangladesh. As of 31 October 2021, 31,818 (51.35% F) Rohingya Refugees live in 06 Blocks (JMSNA). As CwC JPR 22 Target, 26,885 Rohingya (PIN) lives in camp 8W out (51.79% F). As J-MSNA, 74% HH were unable to get enough clear info on types of assistance available (NFI 59,% Livelihood 42 % Mgt 33,% Shelter 31, remote education 29% protection 21%).100% Camp, 97% host community HH received info on Covid 19, 97 % camp, 83% host HH get info on cyclone. Still there are gaps of access to issue & as practice of information. 9% HH faced challenges providing feedback or complain.
85% consulted HH feels aid providers took opinion into account (69% aid, 6% modality & only 7% both). Still have gaps 6% not consulted & 9% opinion not accounted. Repeated Covid outbreak caused negative impacts on Humanitarian Response. CwC agencies massive interventions reduced Covid impact significantly by information providing, vaccination & others. Yet, long term impact prevailing both in camps & host community. To address impact, HHs requires information related to changed coping strategies due to covid-19 pandemic, fire incidences like tragic one of March 2021, cyclone, outbreak, dengue, diarrhea etc. are also challenge for Humanitarian Response. To minimize these, the HHs need continued access to information as, adequate information does not reelect in their behaviors. So, access to information provision has to be continued as it requires time to bring necessary behavioral change by adoption & sustainable. To materialize info into practice & address gaps in needs, require continuation of info provision by Community Resources, Community Engagement, Information & feedback service center & capacity development. The project will bring the information provision bringing Information
Centers at Blocks of targeted camps and wards in Union of Ratna Palang. Learning gaps will be minimized by using audio visual instrument& interactive tools like Street Drama. Community
Engagement includes Block management Committee, Community leaders, Imams, Majhees, Women, Youths and Persons with disabilities and be sensitized to oversee the BCC among the community. They can play significant role in Prevention and Response to COVID-19 and other
Humanitarian concerns utilizing their influence and networks. Considering their potentials. The project will enhance their capacity through capacity building initiative.
The project will also be implemented in the host community of Ratna Palang Union of Ukhiya Upazila where 26,197 people out of which 49.22% Female lives at risk as they are not practice the learning of Information like wearing mask. Maintaining social distance and lack of adequate information center or hubs. To address the problem, we have been planning to implement this one year Project. Purpose of the Project is to protect 39,984 people’s in needs lives in Camp & Host community (In 8W (26,885 PIN, and Ratna Palang Union (11,251, 50% PIN of Ratna Palang Union) from Fire incidence, next wave of COVID -19 and other, Humanitarian concerns.
Target beneficiaries will be proportionate to Sex (51.08%F) and age, 10% will be PWDs and 10% will be ethnic minority Activities to be Performed under 3 different objectives include Community Engagement, Awareness raising, Capacity Enhancement and Setup and Functioning of Block Level and UP Ward level Information Hubs. Manpower’s like Team Leader. Trainer, Facilitator and 06 Community Volunteer in Each of the 06 Blocks will be assigned in the Project. Numbers of beneficiaries and budget will be proportionate to Sex. Similarly age will be Considering in targeting beneficiaries. Organization Management,
Gender, Child Protection Policy and Financial Policies will be followed in implementation of the Project. Considering DRR, the Project will have provision of 5% contingency on operational costs.

1 - Identified Needs

Influx of Rohingya refugees brought massive consequence to the life and livelihood of both the refugee and host community in Bangladesh that is reinforced by outbreak of corona virus in March 2020 and its next waves as well as repeated fire incidences. To address the crisis humanitarian actors have been implementing massive response program in Camps as well as (recently) in host community where CwC agencies have been playing significant roles providing lifesaving messages. It is evident from Joint Multi Sector Needs Assessment (J-MSNA) that since COVID 19, households reported to get enough information on the issues related to COVID 19, (100 % of camp, 97 % of host) and Cyclone (97% of camp, 83 %). Lack of access to information still a barrier for getting available services, as JMNSA shows, 74 % of Camp, and 99 % of host households could not have access to information on the types of available Service. On another side, among those, received the adequate information does not reelect in our behaviors. Also repeated fire incidences in camps are a concern for protection. Here, to be mentioned that, World humanity was knocked on the apparition of that massive fire incident which was happened in last 22nd March 2021. Around 7500 family were continuously faced the miserable condition as they had no roof on top. A lot of service facility was burnt including all supporting materials. Humanitarian organizations ware working hardly to cover the gaps as 3 Camps are severely affected like: Camp 8W, Camp 8E and Camp 9.Other massive issue of protection includes GBV, Trafficking. Violent Extremism. Reamer etc. So, we should continue providing the information provision to bring necessary change in behaviors of both the refugees and host communities that need times to for adoption and to raise awareness. Otherwise result of CwC efforts will be deemed keeping the target audience at risk. For that numbers of information centre has to be setup at door step, be increased in numbers and quality. It is recommended to setup information center in block levels of Camps and in wards of the concerned Union Parishad so that people can get continuous information from door steps. Multi-level community like religious leaders, youths, women, village police, UP leaders, Majhees can play significant role. So efforts to engage and capacity enhancement of the community are crucial as they act as key informants and massive influence on the households.AAP: There are still significant gaps in Accountability to Affected Population and Priority needs, particularly in Shelter Materials, Remote learning, Food Assistance, Site Management and Nutrition (%) reporting assistance / services that did not well: (25-33% since COVID 19, 11-17% before Covid 19). Decentralized Information Provision and Community engagement could minimize the gaps.Information provision in host community needs further improvement as host community is far behind the camp households on different indicators (Access to information, CFM).
