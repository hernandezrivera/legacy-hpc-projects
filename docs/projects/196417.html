Project Summary

Project will contribute to SO3 “Increase the resilience of affected communities by improving access to livelihood opportunities and basic
services,especially among the most vulnerable HHs and communities,
reaching in total 2200 people vocational trainings, IGAs and rehabilitation of productive assets.afocus will be kept on woman
headed HHs,vulnerable youth,HHs with PwD and high number of children, IDPs and Host in order to facilitate the cohesion and the
relationships with the host communities.Though information sharing and consultation with the communities, the main community’s needs will be
identified and SCA will give the support to implement the actions proposed directly by the participants to the Community Led Initiatives.
SCA will adopt an integrated approach among its projects taking place in the same communities, in order to avoid overlapping and promoting
synergies.SCA will coordinate with all the relevant Sectors,but also with national and international actors active in the same areas in order to
implement a harmonized response.SCA through this Project will strengthen social cohesion by empowering communities to lead early recovery processes together will be supported by
engaging in pilot participatory context analysis and capacity building workshops for community representatives to advocate for early recovery needs
and to identify solutions to their needs.achieve self-reliance through own-food production and/or income generation.To facilitate the
transition of displacement affected populations from humanitarian assistance to self-reliance,SCA will support economic recovery of households so
they can restore their ability to produce food,The Project Will include training in food production techniques and Basic business and soft skills training.in addition to the project will inclusion of persons with disabilities mainly women with disabilities will be ensured through using twin track approach,the logic of intervention will combine a package of activities aimed at empowering persons with disabilities and building more inclusive and accessible working environment at community level.Activities will be based on assessment of barriers faced by persons with disabilities. While the organization will conduct several actions to overcome barriers such as awareness raising on persons with disabilities rights,tailored case management support,and intensive coaching support.Participation of women will be supported through awareness and advocacy with their families,provision of female trainers and skills training for home-based businesses.this approach will allow to move from dependence on humanitarian aid to more sustainable food security for the HHs.the project will also contribute to the wellbeing of the families and communities, supporting the reinforcement of income generating activities (IGAs).Vocational trainings will be organized to enable the beneficiaries to gain needed skills and knowledge to start their own production and to maximize the resources provided through FSL activities(e.g.dairy products,vegetables).Project will subsidize the costs of training for all beneficiaries in order to guarantee their participation. In vocational trainings on transformation and conservation of vegetables, the topics will also focus on basic economic elements functional to the marketing of the products and to the good management of the resulting revenues, while vocational training for the production of dairy products(eg: cheese, yogurt, etc.)will focus on the physical and chemical characteristics of milk,heat treatments,production methods of dairy products (yogurt, cream, cheese, butter, etc.)SCA will purchase the equipment (eg: jars, gas stoves, kettles, etc.) necessary for the production and storage of vegetables
in order to have a lasting supply of food even after the harvest period and basic equipment for the production of dairy products for the start of a
small income-generating business linked to the sale of products on the LM.

Furthermore, vocational trainings about diary products production andpreservation will take place, including marketing and business management, in order to enable the participants to start their own activity. activities willbe addressed to selected vulnerable beneficiaries, focusing on woman headed households and youngs. This activity aims furthermore to purchasethe equipment necessary for the production and storage of vegetables and dairy products in order to have for the start of a small income-. 8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Participant safeguarding is central to SCA’ values, and we uphold a zerotolerance tolerance approach towards any form of sexual exploitation and abuse. Inorder to prevent safeguarding violations of any kind, all staff, casual labor andexternal contractors receive orientation and training, and are required to sign the Codeof Conduct. The safeguarding training is comprehensive and is delivered to all SCAand partner staff. Where SCA and partner staff have higher levels ofcontact with participants, they will receive tailored capacity strengthening onProtection Against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and will participate inquarterly events, which address PSEA concerns in target communities, and facilitatethe roll-out of PSEA tools and preventative actions. This process is led by our Safeguarding Manager and is supported by a network of focal points within SCA. Partner staff will have the opportunity to risk assess their own areas in terms ofsafeguarding and create proactive action plans. Community participants will beinformed on what safeguarding is and how they can report any concerns throughsensitisation work across programmes. In addition, external contractors and staff suchas suppliers or Third Party Monitors (TPMs) are regularly trained by department focalpoints who have completed tailored Training of Trainer (ToT) safeguarding training.
