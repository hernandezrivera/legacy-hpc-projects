Project Summary

This project aims at providing a disability inclusive humanitarian response in Cabo Delgado. People with disabilities are in urgent need of MHPSS and rehabilitation services. Training of mainstream humanitarian actors and government institutions in ‘disability inclusive humanitarian action’ will further contribute to this objective. In addition, inclusive data collection in the area will be conducted with a new innovative tool paired with training of relevant actors and showcasing.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The crisis in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique, has rapidly escalated in 2021. Attacks by non-state armed groups (NSAGs) expanded geographically and increased in intensity over the last years, significantly heightening protection risks, especially for people with disabilities. People with disabilities as a target group have been severely underserved in the crises response in Cabo Delgado, especially around technical support in disability mainstreaming and Disability Inclusive Community Development (DICD). The specific gap our proposed project will address is the inclusion of persons with disabilities across all the priority areas we have identified. Data collection and thus reliable data about how many girls, boys, women and men with disabilities have been affected by the conflict and how they are being reached by humanitarian response is still insufficient. According to our assessment the main identified needs for people with disabilities are: mental health and psychosocial support services, rehabilitation services and training of mainstream humanitarian actors and government institutions in ‘disability inclusive humanitarian action’ to ensure a disability inclusive humanitarian response. In addition, the project will have a focus on improving data collection of people with disabilities. 5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Overall, we will apply a ‘Do No Harm’ (DNH) approach across all our project interventions in order to understand and mitigate any risks of harm for our target groups. Light for the World has also conducted a risk assessment to identify potential risks and develop mitigation measures accordingly. The main focus of this project is meaningful access to assistance for particularly vulnerable groups. For the limited scope of this project one of the most vulnerable groups, people with disabilities, have been identified as the main target. The project supports them in accessing different forms of assistance and empowers them to have their voices heard in clusters and also works with humanitarian and government actors to design their humanitarian response in a disability inclusive way.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The focus of the project is to support and work with people with disabilities, this includes older people as well as girls and women. Light for the World works directly with Organisations of People with disabilities and follows a community-centred approach that focuses on direct engagement with target groups. Through our community workers, we have identified people in need of assistance and their families. They are receiving customized support based on their needs in terms of service provision, food security assistance, assistive devices and community empowerment activities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The main objective of the project is to support girls, women, boys and men with disabilities within the humanitarian response. People with disabilities will be identified through our work on Disability Inclusive Community Development, applying a community-based approach of identifying people with disabilities according to the Washington Group Questions and in addition provide further assessment to determine appropriate rehabilitation plans for their individual needs. People with disabilities will further receive food assistance, MHPSS and assistive devices. The project will further provide training and awareness raising to other humanitarian and government actors to achieve a broader disability inclusive humanitarian response.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Light for the World has an environmental policy, which will be taken into consideration throughout the project. No major negative environmental effects are foreseen based on the planned project activities. Nevertheless, we will take into consideration reduced transport and green procurement to reduce unnecessary waste. We are also planning to conduct a participatory risk and mitigation analysis. Furthermore, Light for the World is working already in Sofala province with INGD on disability-inclusive Disaster Risk Management Committees, which we aim to expand to Cabo Delgado.
