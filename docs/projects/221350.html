Project Summary

The proposed intervention aims to strengthen protection services for urban refugees residing in the Center and Littoral Regions of Cameroon, focusing on enhancing their safety, resilience, and access to essential services. This project targets vulnerable refugees, including women, children, persons with disabilities, and those at heightened risk of exploitation, abuse, and neglect.
Key activities will include legal assistance and case management, psychosocial support, capacity building for refugee-led groups, and advocacy to improve their integration into national systems. The project will also address immediate protection needs through the identification of at-risk individuals, provision of livelihood support, and training for local service providers to deliver inclusive and sustainable solutions.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project addresses critical protection gaps faced by urban refugees in the Center and Littoral Regions of Cameroon, exacerbated by their precarious living conditions, lack of access to essential services, and exposure to heightened risks such as exploitation, abuse, and social exclusion. Needs assessments conducted with refugee communities and stakeholders highlight the following priorities: Access to Protection ServicesRefugees face legal barriers, including challenges in obtaining documentation and navigating complex administrative processes. The project will address this through legal assistance, support for documentation, and case management services to ensure refugees can exercise their rights and access justice. Psychosocial Support and Mental Health ServicesRefugees, particularly women, children, and survivors of violence, report significant psychosocial distress due to their displacement and challenging living conditions. The project will provide psychosocial support and mental health services through counseling sessions, referral mechanisms, and community-based support groups. Livelihood SupportEconomic vulnerability remains a major issue for urban refugees who lack sustainable income opportunities. The project will support skills development, vocational training, and income-generating activities to enhance self-reliance and reduce dependency on external aid. Capacity Building and AdvocacyRefugee-led groups and local service providers require enhanced capacity to address community needs effectively. The project will organize training sessions to strengthen the capacities of these groups and advocate for the inclusion of refugees in local and national development plans. Activities and ImplementationThe intervention will implement the following activities: Legal assistance and advocacy for the issuance of documentation.Case management for at-risk individuals and survivors of violence.Provision of psychosocial support and mental health services.Skills development and livelihood support initiatives.Training and capacity building for refugee-led organizations and local stakeholders.These activities will be carried out in collaboration with key partners, including UNHCR, local authorities, and community leaders, to ensure sustainability and alignment with existing national frameworks. Expected Results Improved access to protection services for at least 3,000 refugees, ensuring their rights and dignity.Enhanced psychosocial well-being for individuals and families through tailored support services.Increased self-reliance for 1,500 refugees through livelihood interventions.Strengthened capacities of 10 refugee-led groups and local service providers to deliver inclusive solutions.Through this intervention, the project seeks to address urgent protection needs while laying a foundation for longer-term resilience and integration.
