Project Summary

To improve, advocate and mobilize for better mechanism and policies that enhance the protection of Women and Girls. Enhancement of protection laws and justice system to provide better environment in which at risk population groups live. Decrease and eradication of GBV during and after famine, in IDPs and in hosted communities. .Awareness raising campaigns, and promotion of gender-equality. Offering community resilience programs, educating and empowering the community in holistic way. Empowering women/Girls to speak against violence . Training community members to provide psycho-social counseling to victims. GBV survivors in line with the essential needs of women, men, girls and boys for those affected conflicts and natural disaster Gender Based Violence through multi sectoral response.

Needs

Somalia’s 1.3 million IDPs and an increasing returning refugee population are in dire need of durable solution that gives them a way out from the associated protection risks, so far IDPs are far more vulnerable to mass scale, unrelenting and brutal forced evictions, to pervasive gender based violence trends. IDP children are the most vulnerable group and in emergencies they are exposed to far greater rights violations, civilians on the move are witnessing unprecedented impediments to their freedom of movement, the decades long proliferation of explosive hazards coupled with conflicts have forced those fleeing to use unconventional routes, avoid main supply routes and are exposing themselves to greater risks. In this dire protection environment Somalia is witnessing its first mass refugee return since the late1990s, refugees are forfeiting their basic rights to return to Somalia in their masses. The political processes in 2016 have led to outbursts of violence, mass displacement and a myriad of protection violations, coupled with mass refugee returns 2017 is anticipated to continue in the trajectory set by political process and military operations set in 2017 unaddressed these trends will result in a deterioration of the humanitarian situation when the caseload of needs is substantially higher.PROBLEM STATEMENT:The twenty six years of conflict in Somalia has led to the increase of human rights abuse, particularly the rights of children. During the war, most of the communities were sheltered the Internally Displaced People experienced the worst situation of rape, sexual abuse, economic exploitation, torture, etc. Women and children rights were abused on a daily basis without concrete actions taken as a deterrent to this inhumane act.Cognizant of the growing needs of these highly vulnerable group,SOYVGA has been working with the local authorities, the business communities and local Chamber of Commerce in south central of somalia in implementing skills training

Activities

SOYVGA Will Provide Survivor based support such as (medical, legal, psychological support, and safety) and case management GBV Survivors in Lower Shabelle Region of South-West State of Somalia-.Provision of material assistance (dignity kits, fuel for cooking and Solar lanterns) persons from the most vulnerable IDPs who are at risk of GBV in Lower Shabelle Region-Provision of Social economic reintegration and livelihood skills, employment promotion that empower GBV Survivors and vulnerable women and girls in Lower Shabelle of South-West State of Somalia.-.Organize and perform celebration for international day for the elimination of violence against women in in Lower Shabelle Region of South-West State of Somalia.-Conduct 12 Workshops Community based GBV/gender mechanisms (prevention, mitigation, identification of individuals in need for community members including elders, youth, disabled, IDPs, Returnees and Host community in Lower Shabelle of South-West State of Somalia.-.Hold GBV training for duty bearers, Health employees including nurses, doctors and medical staff, Local authority including Ministry of Justice and Constitutional Affairs, Law-Enforcement officials, Aid-Workers.- Facilitate Clinical Management Training for first res-ponders to Rape victims. Psycho social support and community resilience training and proper case management in Lower Shabelle of South-West State of Somalia.-SOYVGA will involve and mobilize and train IDPs-camp gate-keepers to honor, pledge support and become in-house support to the first respondents , counsels, and advocates of gender equality and empowerment.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Service providers, volunteers and law-enforcement officials will receive legal aid ad proper case management trainings. Empowering Victims and mobilize community members in prevention of GBV and speaking out against it by holding continues campaigns and holding celebrations of the international day for elimination of violence against women and girlsParticipation of beneficiaries will also ensure that the proposed intervention is appropriate and effective. The safety and dignity of the beneficiaries will be prioritized by ensuring confidentiality, prioritizing the best interests of the child and respecting the wishes of parents/caregivers, where they do not endanger the child further. All measures to respect and adhere to the Do No Harm approach will be taken during the process of beneficiary selection, service provision and data collection and management. Specialized services and useful information about service providers will be provided to all beneficiaries that meet the vulnerability criteria, including both IDP and host communities. Hence, services will be provided purely on the basis of need, in line with humanitarian principles.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The local authorities grant permission to access the target areas. - The necessary funds are timely disbursed. Risks: The volatile security and political situation might limit or even preclude access to the target areas and regular implementation of the project activities. A shortage of adequately skilled staff especially around CMR and response to child survivors, capable to positively interact with extremely vulnerable people, as well as the lack of specialized services in the target areas, might affect the quality and the impact of the project activities. Mitigations: - Continuously assess and closely monitor the evolvement of the security and political situation in the target areas, and have the flexibility to quickly adapt the implementation methodology as needed in order to guarantee the achievement of the project objective and the most effective impact. In this regard, SOYVGA will maintain regular information flow and constantly consult with the cluster leads and donor. On the staff skills continuously train, coach and follow up the frontline workers to ensure timely and quality services is provided to beneficiaries.Providing Livelihood and Socio-economic support to the victims of GBV Survivors and employment promotion and self marketing strategies after graduation from the programs. Special attention will be given during the program literacy and enumeration classes with tailoring, beauty salon and technical skills such as computer usage knowledge.
