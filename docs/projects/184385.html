Project Summary

Alight health project will be implemented in close collaboration with the MOH nationally and in each region as well as WHO, UNICEF, UNFPA, other health cluster partners and stakeholders in each area. ALIGHT plans to respond in Banadir, Hirshabelle and Jubaland regions of South- Central Somalia and Sool, and Waqooyi Galbeed of Somaliland, where Alight established presence and with critical humanitarian need and excess morbidity and mortality due disease outbreak driven by climatic shocks, displacements and conflict. The intervention will support 13 existing government health centers with adequate health care workers including two clinicians (under five and over 5 years), in each site providing safe and quality primary and reproductive healthcare services to 151,382 individuals including People living with disabilities (PLWD) as direct beneficiaries in an equitable and dignified manner while reaching to estimated 471,000 individuals as indirect beneficiaries. Access to essential health services remains a priority for the conflict affected vulnerable populations in proposed project target locations largely affected by the COVID-19 outbreak and displacements due to flash flood. ALIGHT will undertake WASH interventions across all facilities like latrines, drinking water, hand-wash installation to improve patient safety and prevent COVID-19 transmission and poor health outcomes.
ALIGHT health project transform gender role and relation while provide emergency and basic primary and reproductive health care services that include treatment of common illnesses, maternal and child health care, health education, provision of essential medicines, control of communicable diseases within the community, identification and management of gender-based violence, mental health services that includes psychosocial counselling to the distressed individual, PLWDs and referral of severe cases for secondary healthcare. Protection will be mainstreamed into all project activities, through a comprehensive package of training of Health Facility staff on identification and referral of severe cases of S/GBV. The intervention will reduce morbidity and mortality among the displaced vulnerable population including PLWDs by primarily focusing on ensuring that target facilities provide an integrated essential package of primary health services (EPHS) with emergency referrals, disease surveillance and the inclusion of reproductive and mental healthcare services. The project will improve access to quality and essential lifesaving health services in an equitable, safe and dignified manner to contribute to improvement in the physical and mental wellbeing of the displaced and vulnerable population including PLWDs and sexual assault survivors to reduce excess morbidity and mortality due to disease outbreak, climatic shocks and conflicts. ALIGHT will closely coordinate with the MOH and respond in locations where significant gaps in health service delivery have been identified. ALIGHT will ensure the participation of the local communities in decision making throughout the life cycle of the project to further strengthen community acceptance and ownership. ALIGHT will invest in local health capacity, strengthening the existing physical structures and human resources, ensuring that staff incentives are in line with the MOH scale paving the way for the transition to locally and nationally owned and managed facilities. Through the lifetime of the project, ALIGHT will work with the MOH to pave the way for a proper handover and discuss the transfer of responsibility slowly or in phases to D/RMOs where and when possible.

1. - Projects activities

Provision of lifesaving primary and reproductive healthcare services considering gender inequality, PLWDs and age through fixed and mobile outreach services in line with Somalia EPHs standards; Identification, treatment, detection or case management for epidemic-prone diseases for crisis-affected vulnerable populations aimed at reducing morbidity and mortality by providing emergency health care services including emergency or trauma case, NCD treatment, STD/HIV treatment, mental health for the inaccessible communities targeting number of outpatient consultations per person per year (Target > = 1 new visit/person /year). ALIGHT coordinates closely with the MOH and UNICEF in providing child health and ensure all children below 5 years have been immunised to ensure a great proportion of children under 1 year received Penta3 and measles vaccines at ALIGHT supported facilities further screening all beneficiaries for COVID-19 and providing specialised reproductive health care for pregnant and lactating women that includes skilled deliveries to increase proportion of skilled birth attendance. ALIGHT continues upgrading national facilities that need rehabilitation, ALIGHT will support MOH facilities and will facilitate training on EWARN, disseminate weekly EWARN reports, and send alert for notable and outbreak-prone diseases. 4.6 - Mitigation Measures

ALIGHT is dedicated to safeguard that beneficiaries safely access services across all gender and PLWDs, to promote meaningful access, accountability, and participation in the provision of health assistance considering risks and barriers to access faced by persons with disabilities, maximising the positive impacts of health programs on people’s safety and dignity and support affected populations access and enjoy their rights. Establish proper information sharing protocol for the disabled in a manner they can understand and survivors of abuse to avoid exposing them to further trauma and efforts are made to ensure confidentiality and wishes or comfort of the beneficiaries. ALIGHT’s needs assessment tools and frameworks will be adapted to strengthen understanding of disability inclusion ensuring that persons with disabilities are involved in any key informant interviews and focus group discussion. A varied sample of persons with disabilities will be part of the focus group discussions including men and women, boys, and girls, of all ages and impairment types so that a diverse insight into the risks and barriers can be understood ALIGHT will keep beneficiaries and personal data records confidential and safe from unintended use for effective data protection measures. Clearly marked separate toilet and bathing facilities for males and females accounting for persons with disabilities and children will be provided. For impartial and equal access of the basic services for all, ALIGHT will ensure proper documentation, collect and use data disaggregated by sex, age, disability and diversity to effectively inform programming and ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable. ALIGHT requires all partners and contractors, to sign Sexual Harassment, Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) policy at the time of agreement, and all staff, suppliers and consultants at the time of offer letter so that the contracts are not valid without a signed code of conduct. To strengthen participation and empowerment, ALIGHT will involve all categories, age, or gender group, disabled and layers of the affected population in identifying and responding to needs. For purposes of transparency, ALIGHT will set up accessible, confidential, and well-understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment, and assist in detecting misconduct by creating complaint boxes at health sites, phone lines, setting up mechanisms for submitting feedback that does not require the beneficiary exposing themselves ALIGHT teams will respond to complaints, immediately documenting lessons learnt, organize awareness-raising sessions so that people know how it works. ALIGHT will utilize the real time beneficiary feedback system, Kuja Kuja as a tool that captures the ‘pulse’ of customer satisfaction with services and to allow service teams, technical teams, and support functions both at the local and global levels to understand real-time issues being faced, see opportunities for immediate and strategic improvement to service delivery. Feedback mechanisms will be accessible to all persons including the disabled with support from the Kuj Kuja team members who are all times standby to provide necessary guidance Through the adoption of Do No Harm approaches, efforts will be made to ensure relationships with the project targeted population including P local leaders are enhanced and programs implemented to improve relationships, reduce tension, and promote peaceful pathways out of conflict. ALIGHT believes that understanding the context in which it operates is paramount to ensuring that interventions are successful and avoid negative consequences. ALIGHT will assess the context and conflict dynamics, especially the role of dividers and connectors to link the conflict analysis with the program cycle helping ALIGHT to re-plan, implement and monitor the interventions.
