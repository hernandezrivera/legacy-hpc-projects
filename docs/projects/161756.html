Project Summary

This program seeks to enhance quality teaching and learning among teachers and conflict affected children in schools through teaching at the right Level (TaRL) in MMC, Jere, Magumeri and Mafa LGAs of Borno state within the period of 12 months. The program shall identify and build capacity of 210 teachers, to engage 10,000 school children ages 3-17years with after school lessons to improve their learning capacities, 40% boys and 60% girls including children with special needs across areas of intervention.
GOALPrime through this program will train 210 teachers and Volunteers as instructors to engage school children with accelerated learning. The school children will be assessed and grouped according to their learning need basis to provide them with required learning support to enhance their learning abilities.
The main investment to this program would be in building capacities of education human resource through training and mentoring of teachers and training of universal basic education board monitoring team. 210 teachers will be trained on Teaching at Right Level (TaRL) innovative approach who will serve as instructors to engage school children with TaRL program activities. GOALPrime shall collaborate with State universal basic education board (SUBEB) to build the capacity of 15 state government education monitoring team representatives to provide quality monitoring, assessment and evaluation to guide quality implementation of TaRL approach.
Through this program GOALPrime will train the engaged teachers on psychosocial support and positive discipline to enable teachers to engage the grouped school children according to their level of understanding which will further enhance leaning outcome for conflict affected school children. GOALPrime shall strengthen school clubs (spelling bee and mathematics) to promote literacy and numeracy learning capacity of conflict affected school children to compliment teaching at the right level outcome among the children.
This project shall mainstream protection and GBV in every component of the program by providing training on GBV prevention, child protection principles and protection and prevention of sexual exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) to implementing staff, teachers and field-level volunteers to be able to guide the children on how to identify and report any suspicion of any case of PSEA or protection to prevent it from occurring. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall be coordinating with other partners to sustain advocacy to the government and community engagement to promote sustainability, ownership and accountability to the affected population. Also GOALPrime shall make available free toll lines to the beneficiaries of the project for complaints and feedback from the project implementation process and will commit to address concerns raised by the beneficiaries within the capacity of the organization and coordinate with education sector and other partners to resolve any concern that GOALPrime organization cannot address within her capacity.

1 - Needs

Since the onset of the crisis, according to 2019-2021 Humanitarian Response Plan Strategy “NSAGs have deliberately and systematically targeted learning institutions and abducted, killed and maimed learners and teachers. Some 2.16 million people, 986,000 girls, 731,000 boys and 43,000 education personnel, are in need of emergency education support. This represents a decrease of 24 per cent from 2.9 million in need in 2018, mainly as a result of increased accessibility. However, the number of children with chronic needs in education remains high across the BAY states and the crisis has put additional pressure on an education system that was already fragile. Despite a decrease in the number of security incidents targeting education structures since 2017, the risk of violent attacks, abduction and kidnappings associated with education remains a real threat as illustrated by the high-profile abduction of at least 110 girls from their school in Dapchi, Yobe State, in February 2018. A greater concern is the risk of child recruitment into armed groups (especially boys), use of children in suicide attacks, and enslavement of Children, which are all grave violations against children”.The Education Sector through 2020 HRP guidelines has estimated that there are over 1 million children (3-17 years) that are affected by the humanitarian conflict that need quality basic education services, while about 17,598 teachers are also estimated to need basic Psychosocial Training. This brings the estimated total people in need to be 1,077,727 which is 50% less than the 2.2 million people in need estimated for 2019. The people in need breakdown consists of 33% internally displaced persons, 20% returnee and 47% Host community. As of September 2019, the sector had achieved 30% of its 1.5 million targets and hopes to improve on that in the coming year. In Borno state, the total persons in need of education services according to the Humanitarian Needs Assessment 2019 are about 749,387 while the number targeted is 563,898. The education activities in MMC and Jere has been stable for some time now due to the security presence in the LGAs, the number of children accessing formal education in these LGAs is high as a result of the influx of IDPs in the late 2018 and early 2019 therefore, there is need for more learning spaces and class rooms to accommodate this conflict affected children to access quality and safe education. The long effect of the insurgent crises has caused a lot of families to be displaced to MMC and Jere where there is stability. This make the schools and learning spaces to be filled with children who are in need of basic education services, the classrooms are overcrowded with majority of the children accessing education in open space and some under the shade of trees. With this circumstance, nearly half of all children who have completed primary school cannot read nor do basic calculations effectively. The poor learning outcomes are caused by low level of teacher’s competence to handle such large classes, lack of instructional materials, poor student attendance and lack of safe, inclusive classrooms, lack of psychosocial support services to put teachers in the right frame of mind to discharge their responsibilities in the context of crises environment. Overall, children who complete basic education do not acquire critical skills that would enable them to function effectively or earn a decent livelihood. To bridge this gaps GOALPrime organization Nigeria seek to enhance quality teaching and learning among teachers and conflict affected children in schools through teaching at the right Level (TaRL) where low learners will be assessed, grouped and engaged according to their learning abilities across 30 schools in MMC, Jere, Magumeri and Mafa LGAs.
