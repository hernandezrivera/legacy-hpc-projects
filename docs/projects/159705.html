Project Summary

The project aims at reducing enhancing resilience of drought and conflict affected IDPs and most vulnerable communities in Bay and Bakool Regions, Somalia that are in sever acute food insecurity by protecting and restoring their livelihood conditions and increasing their income sources through provision of appropriate livelihood inputs and technical support in rural and peri-urban settings and support household and community resilience to shocks, through rehabilitation of community assets. The project targets 433 farming and pastoral families/26000 persons (women 10816, men 9984, boys 2600 and girls 2600) whose plight is exacerbated eroded purchasing power and very fragile food security situation that has been impacted by impacts of successive seasons of below-average rains experienced across most parts of Somalia during the 2018 Deyr (October-December), followed by harsh weather conditions during the dry 2019 Jilaal (January–March) season and the delayed and poor performance of the 2019 Gu (April-June). The situation has been exacerbated sustained impacts of the devastating 2016/2017 drought.

Project Activities

1. Rehabilitation of 8 water catchments and 6 feeder roads in the targeted locations in bay and Bakool Regions on cash for work scheme to benefit 1200 vulnerable and displaced persons 2. Provision of food vouchers to 1500 most vulnerable and displaced women headed households and those those with disability, those with chronic illnesses and those with health problems to increase their food consumption and increase their Household Dietary Diversity in the targeted villages in Bay and Bakool Regions 3. Provision of unconditional and conditional cash transfers for the most vulnerable and displaced 1500 women headed households particularly those with disability, those with chronic illnesses and those with health problems to increase their food consumption and increase their Household Dietary Diversity in Bay and Bakool Regions 4. Provision of fodder for animals to 800 pastoral families in Bay and Bakool Regions. Fodder will be sourced locally in Somalia through a completive bidding process where the bidder with the best prices and quality fodder will be selected5. Training of 400 farmers and agro pastoralists on good agricultural practices, post harvest, integrated pest management (IPM), trans boundary pest and disease in Bay and Bakool Regions 6. Provision of tractor hours to 500 farmers in Bay and Bakool Regions
