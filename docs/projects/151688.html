Needs

From the first days of the armed conflict in the east of Ukraine, one of the main problems for residents of the Donetsk region in the field of housing remains the satisfaction of needs related to extreme winter conditions. In the context of a local economic crisis and a sharp decline in the incomes of the majority of the population in the region, it becomes more difficult to provide their homes with warmth and with winter clothes and shoes. Particular difficulties in extreme winter conditions are experienced by elderly people, people with poor health, children and residents of the region who have lost their jobs.To meet the needs of low-income residents of the region with warm and winter clothes, it is proposed to implement a project to create a stock of equipment for heating, winter clothes and shoes, sleeping bags and blankets, as well as to provide the most needy residents of the region with technical devices for heat and warm clothes. Within the framework of the proposed project, it is planned to create a warehouse on the basis of the Donbass Development Center, equipped for storing metal stoves of the bourgeois type, oil electric heaters, as well as winter clothes, shoes, sleeping bags and blankets .To quickly respond to requests from residents of the region and streamline the system for providing appropriate assistance, it is planned to create a computer base for registering relevant requests from citizens and forming a volunteer team capable of delivering devices that provide heat and warm clothes to problem areas. In addition, in order to provide technical assistance to residents whose homes have been hit by shelling, it is proposed to create a base-rental of basic construction tools and equipment for the repair of residential premises, including for the purpose of insulating them.During the project implementation period and after its completion, the necessary devices for heating residential premises and warm clothes will be distributed to residents suffering in the cold winter.

Activities & outcomes

1. Create and put into trial operation a computer database for registering residents' requests for assistance in supplying heat and / or purchasing winter clothes and shoes.2. Complete and commission a warehouse for storing devices for heating residential premises (stoves and heaters), winter clothing and footwear, sleeping bags and blankets, as well as opening the rental of construction tools and equipment.3. To form a team of volunteers capable of delivering devices that provide heat, as well as warm clothes to problem areas.4. Identify the most in need of heat, winter clothing and shoes for residents of the region and deliver these items and equipment to them.
