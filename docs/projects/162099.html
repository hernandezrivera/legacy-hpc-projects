Project Summary

The project aims to ensure the safety and dignity of the refugees through transitional shelter assistance (TSA) to 5,000 households (also 750 extremely vulnerable individuals (EVIs)) in Camp 13, including the construction of 50 model shelters. Community sessions on sustaining safe shelter, and training for carpenters (for better understanding of constrcution tools and best practices of it) will be organized as part of the sustainability of the project, and of creating community ownership. A lessons learned workshop will also be held to capture ideas for developing better programming in the future.

Needs

Despite previous shelter interventions, the settlement area still needs significant shelter repair/upgradation. As of 31 July 2019, the Shelter Sector Phase 3 Response is facing a gap of 66.9% of total households in the camp. Major needs identified are: Ensuring shelters with minimum performance standards: All shelters are needed to be safe, repaired/upgraded with materials that are resistant to short term deterioration and can withstand during adverse weather. In Camp 13, only 1,132 households have been provided with TSA - I by Shelter actors out of total 9,823, while there is need of 8,991 households (ISCG/IOM). Provision of support for EVIs during the repairs/upgradation of shelters is required. Gender considerations: Assesing needs from different gender and age groups to incorporate those needs in the shelter intervention process is highly required. Treated bamboo for the longevity of the structures: To enhance the better performance of these shelters, treated bamboo is needed to enhance the duration of a robust structural approach. Capacity building of the beneficiaries: Shelter requires a thorough maintenance to sustain its quality for a certain period of time. Otherwise, the quality and strength of the shelter deteriorates. The communities are needed to be aware of the use of construction tools and materials, and the knowledge of maintaining safe shelter. As such, there is great need of rolling out training and orientations of safe shelter maintenance.The beneficiaries need to be aware about DRR, environmnt and sustainable living. Hence the beneficiaries also needs to have sessions for them to introduce and aware them about DRR, environmental issues and sustainable living. Site improvement works: Due to the geographic difficulties, camps are always at risk of natural disasters like heavy rainfall, floods, landslides and cyclones. Considering these, a set of site improvement works along with community awareness sessions are required to improve living conditions of the refugees with protection and safety. Site improvemnt activity at household level is required to keep the households safe from any sort of natural disasters.

Activities or outputs

a) Distribute TSA - I materials to the selected beneficiaries in line with selection criteria, provide treated bamboo in the TSA-I materials (untreated bamboo will be exchanged for treated bamboo from IOM Bamboo Treatement Plant). Take feedback from the community thorugh FGD to identify their needs in shelter and implement them with rational effort (different gender and age group will be part of FGD).Female and child participation will be highly encouraged during this phase. At the distribution points, there will be help desk, where the beneficiary can explain their needs and community mobilizers will be present there to help them identify and recognize their needs. Based on previous experience in implementing TSA-I in differents camps, the estimate has been prepeared having an amount of 458 USD.b) Conduct maintenance awareness sessions during distribution of materials. The messaging and materials to support these sessions will be developed to address coping mechanisms, emergency repairs, and will support vulnerable groups in the transition towards safe shelter. Session will conducted for raising awareness about DRR, sustainable living and environmental issue. A brief session will be given to them regarding tree plantation, based on household level.c) Monitor the distributed materials, by the community mobilizers, in the distributed areas to ensure that distributed materials are safe and not sold to the local markets d) Provide technical assistance to the beneficiaries on how to repair and upgrade their own safe shelter e) Provide assistance in transportation, repair and upgrade of shelter for EVIs. A complete package of support will be provided from distribution to completion of shelter. EVI's will be choosen under these criteria: 1) Household composition and social protection 2) Physical shelter characteristics 3) Capacity to lead shelter upgrading/repairf) Create labour pools of women, who will support the improvements to their own shelters and neighbouring households. After this support, it is expected that the women will have the capabilities of working in any settlement works. g) Conduct training sessions for carpenters on tools and techniques of construction material h) Organize lessons learning workshop to share the findings and successes As the project will be only implemented in refugee camps, as a part of social cohesion, community mobilizers, volunteers and required technical person to support "on field" activities will be hired from the host community. The project will assess need from the community through FGD where different gender and age group will participate. Child participation and female participation will be highly encouraged, while addressing female members needs in terms of their privacy during menstruation, recommemdation for cooking facilities, special features in the shelter for infants and elderly person. To break the stereotype of technical issues, in the training sessions female presense will be highly encourage to help them gain knowledge about the use of construction tools and materials so that they can take good care of their shelter on their own rather depending on someone else. Total project management will be highly influcned by the feedback of different gender and age group by rationally addressing their needs and building their capacities through these sessions. As a part of ecofeminist approach, while mostly the female members run their household activities, through these courtyard session they will be encouraged to provide a sustainable and environment friendly living with household level tree plantation including kitchen garden. Participation of children is also encouraged in tree plantation under their parents assistance, which will aware them about environmental issues and their role towards it and grow a certain level of accountability to their surrounding environment.
