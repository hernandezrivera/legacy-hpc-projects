Project Summary

SC project targeting vulnerable communities, IDPs and returnees under localities identified by the nutrition sector with severity ranking 4 (extreme need) and 5 (catastrophic need) . These localities have a GAM and SAM rate ranking of either very high or severe. Nutrition intervention will cover treatment of Acute Malnutrition and preventive services of IYCF, provision of nutrition supplies, training of volunteers, case identification and follow up, strengthen the community awareness of causes, danger signs and treatment of acute malnutrition, promoting positive health and nutrition behavior change. SC integrated services delivery based on need and is inclusive and non-discriminatory. Nutrition staff will reflect the gender, ethnic, and linguistic balance of the beneficiary population. Enforced policies prevent and respond to any breach of SC's Child Safeguarding Policy

2 - Activities / Outputs

The project will respond to the basic needs of IDPs, returnees and host communities while increasing their self-reliance and improving their nutrition status and resilience. Provision of lifesaving nutrition service for acute malnourished children (boys and girls 6-59 months of age) and PLWs . SC will continue run Outpatient Therapeutic Program (OTP) for children 6-59 months. Trained healthcare worker will determine if a child 6-59 months is suffering from SAM according to the admission criteria. The child will receive routine medication according to the national treatment protocol as well as a take-home ration of RUTF equivalent to about 200 (kcal/kg) of body weight per day to last until the next health visit. Treatment will be managed at the health centre, and continued at home. The child returns to the health facility point for follow-up of the health and nutrition progress and for replenishing RUTF supplies until full recovery. Children with SAM having medical complications or edema +++ will be referred to in-patient care at one of the stabilization centers, usually in the locality hospital. Infants <6 months with SAM will be referred to in-patient care centers (in hospitals) as per the national guidelines where they will receive support on re-establishing effective breast feeding. SC will support management of moderate acute manultrion through TSFP services . Will address the gender and age speciﬁc concerns as a crosscutting approach throughout the design ,implementation and capacity building and resilience mechanisms -SC will provide training for staff on scope of gender analysis, gender aspects and how women, girls, boys, and men are affected differently in different situations. Awareness sessions on gender aspects and increase the capacity to prevent abuse. The environmental issues will be considered through monitoring of water sources, waste management and sanitation as the main environmental issues affecting health and nutrition status of target communities.SC will coordinate and work closely with MOH, UN agencies, and partners.
