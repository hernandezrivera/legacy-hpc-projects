Project Summary

Since the break out of the Syrian conflict more than seven years, Syria has become a battleground for many world and local powers. Armed groups were emerged and the war has left what can be described as one of the worst humanitarian crises of all time.
Millions of people have been killed or forced to flee their homes. Families are struggling to survive inside Syria, or make a new home in neighboring countries.
Eight years of conflicts were enough to weak the Syrian citizens and create execrable humanitarian situation. The degree of resilience and positive coping mechanism of the affected population have reduced due to the protracted nature of the conflict. The economic and social situation in Syria continues to decline and severely affect everybody especially those who have been displaced from or returned to their homes. Statistics reveal that Two-thirds of Syrians are living in extreme poverty, unable to meet basic food and non-food needs. The main reasons for poverty are loss of property and jobs, loss of access to public services, including health and clean water, and rising food prices.
According to HNO 2019, over 75 per cent of communities reported that clothing had been unaffordable or unavailable over the last 3 months. Unavailability and unaffordability of individual items are linked, indicating that limited supplies may be driving higher prices.
In spite of the fact that number of PIN in 2018 has decrease comparing to 2019 one, people in Syria are resorting to harmful coping strategies such as depletion of assets and increasing debt in order to meet their needs.
It was reported that 4,403,329 people are in need for NFIs assistances in Syria,
The purpose of this project is to provide winterization items to people living in the most severity areas as GOPA has access to Dar’a, Aleppo, Rural Damascus, Der ez-Zor and Al Quneitra.
This project will target 50,000 Individual beneficiaries included 20,000 child will receive winter clothes and shoes

Needs

The United Nations (UN) estimates last year that 6.1 million people are internally displaced and a total of 13.1 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance in general; children have been particularly hard-hit. According to HNO 2019, 4.4 million people require NFI assistance, a 6 per cent decline from the 2018 HNO, and the second year in a row that the total number of people in need of NFI assistance has reduced. However, NFIs is still the most essential and life-saving needs for population. Economic pressures are key drivers of need, with large groups stating that the most widespread challenges are inability to afford needed non-food items. The changeable security situation in many Syrian governorate resulted unstable movement of IDPs and returnees. In general, governorate of Der ez-zor, Dar’a, Rural Damascus, Rural of Aleppo and Al Quneitra were recorded as the most severity areas with an estimated number of over than 300,000 people are in sever need According to the database collected by GOPA through the implementation of programs in those areas during 2018.

Implementation of activities

The project aims to distribute winterization kits including clothing kits, shoes and blanket kits (each kit includes:five single blankes size 220*160,weight 1,100 gram) to the most vulnerable people. Each family will receive one blanket kit. As for children, each child will receive one clothes kit and one pair of shoes. To ensure target the most vulnerable families, each family will receive one blanket kit, three clothes kits and three shoes kits as maximum. The distribution process of (clothing, shoes and blanket kits) will go through five different phases as following:Preparation: GOPA contacted with the targeted groups to check their needs, opinions and suggestions in order to proceed the implementation.Selection: This phase includes coordination with other NGOs working in the same area to avoid duplication of beneficiaries.Bidding: Tender is announced at this phase after all needed documents are prepared, offers from different suppliers will be received, a specialized committee will study all offers and then GOPA will contract with the best technically and financially offer.Implementation: All NFI kits will be delivered to the targeted area and will be distributed according to a planned schedule. Monitoring actions will be taken to make sure of the best implementation in terms of beneficiaries’ satisfactions and quality of the distributed kits.Post distribution monitoring: An evaluation process will follow the implementation to identify the program weak and strong points and to set a plan for other projects. Vulnerability criteria which GOPA usually follow in such project are as following:- female-headed households(In this case, the woman is either divorced or widowed and has children to whom she is responsible,So the priority will giving to such cases)- households with a large number of family members (i.e. 7+).In this case the family has a large number of children or there is more than one wife in the family. Consequently, there is a priority in targeting them from this project- Families without any income or limited income.(Families without financial resources to cover basic needs)
