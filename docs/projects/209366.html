Project Summary

HA-UK is proposing to deliver comprehensive child protection and GBV services targeting vulnerable IDPs and host communities in South Kordofan, Blue Nile, and Sinnar. This response aims to reduce the suffering of boys, girls, women, and men including children with disabilities who are at risk of experiencing violence, abuse, exploitation, and neglect through the provision of specialized/lifesaving child protection and multi-sectoral services.
HA will use a multi-sectoral approach and strengthen coordination to leverage referral pathways to ensure the needs of the survivors are addressed and considered with respect and confidentiality. This approach includes prevention through awareness raising and community-based education and information campaigns, capacity development for partners, monitoring and reporting, and provision of services for the survivors by Conducting protection assessments for communities, targeting Persons with Specific Needs (PSNs), CBCPMs are strengthened and incorporated to CPCs CAPs through the development of risk reduction action and monitoring plans by protection focal points.
GBV:
• Establishment/ activate, train, and equip 15 women and girls groups, 5 groups per state, (15) members per group, and train them on protection, GBV reporting, monitoring, and response to local GBV/PSEA cases.
• Conduct 25 awareness-raising sessions on GBV and PSEA including 16 days of activities to prevent GBV and increase knowledge of GBV services, and reporting.
• Conduct locality-level advocacy through 3 meetings in each state targeting 35 Stakeholders staff and community leaders on the impact of GBV on girls, Female genital mutilation/Cutting FGM/C, and child early and forced marriage – CEFM.
• Provision of quality case management services for girls and women GBV survivors, targeting 750 women and girls, including referral to specialized services (legal, medical. PSS, cash, etc..) through services providers (referrals)
• Train 250 service providers including social workers on GBV, PSEA, and GBV case management services for survivors of GBV including clinical management of rape and referral pathway based on GBV Minimum standards.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

HA adheres to the five Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) defined by the IASC Task Force As part of its strong commitment to AAP, HA is engaging refugees and other persons of concern in the programme cycle through formation, training and strengthening of community-based structures and networks who take leading role in project implementation. HA has distinctive community based and AGD approaches that promotes the active participation of targeted population particularly girls, women, disability and minority and ensures engagement of all people of concern across the interventions levels. The people of concerns and other stakeholders are also able to share their opinions and complaints through feedback mechanism. This ensures greater accountability to people of concern and support the project sustainability. The opinions and feedback are regularly collected, analyzed and processed to inform project adjustment and improvement. This has largely reflected in building trust with the people of concerns and host communities as well as led to client satisfaction. Through its presence and capacity support to counterparts, HA has strengthened partnership with the local authorities and line ministries including HAC and COR. This helps for smooth and timely delivery of services to the targeted people of concerns in the hard to reach areas. HA has open access to all areas where refugees are hosted and able to deliver uninterrupted services

The feedback received from the communities will be processed as follows:- Collecting Feedback: Through robust complaint and feedback mechanism established for the project, all the complaints and feedback will be received in line with the SoPs and shared with the designated staff for analysis. - Analyzing Feedback: The designated staff will analyze the feedback receive and share with analysis with the project team and the management. - Applying Feedback: If the management deems feedback productive and acting on the feedback may increase efficiency, effectiveness and impact of the project, it will be applied. Where complaints are concerned, all the complaints will be resolved and redressal provided. - Communicating Feedback: The person providing feedback/registering complaint will be communicated back informing the feedback was received fixed.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

HA-UK has established multi-sector Community Feedback and Response Mechanisms (CFRM) in all its operational sites including in the proposed project location South Kordofan, Blue Nile and Sinnar states) through which the beneficiaries will be informed on their rights to launch complaints or give other forms of feedback in regard to the implementation process and the quality of services they are receiving from the HA-UK. The existing complaint and feedback mechanism will be used during the project period with beneficiaries being sensitized on beneficiary accountability standards. Communities will also be informed about the focal point in the HA-UK to handle any compliant report and feedback. The reporting procedures will be displayed in a visible place in the project location and complains and feedback boxes will be placed at WGSS and project site offices for those who are literate to use. Views and feedback will be collected from women, girls and community beneficiaries including those living with different disabilities and the minority, authorities from the start of the project to the intervention and monthly thereafter. Feedback and complaints will generally be collected using FGD’s with groups of men, women, boys, and girls; during community sensitization and awareness sessions; and through the use of suggestion boxes. HA’s community feedback mechanism allows for a range of complaints to be received and managed, including a mechanism for confidentially fast-tracking concerns of a serious nature such as Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. Through this discussion, the information and the feedback obtained from the beneficiaries of the HA-UK will explain its procedures, decisions and allow women and girls to hold HA-UK accountable. Awareness raising on existing PSEA Networks or Focal Point(s) will be included to ensure that beneficiaries are well informed of existing reporting mechanisms.
