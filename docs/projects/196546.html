Project Summary

The project design was according to SPHERE standards and WASH guidance priorities where all the proposed activities are based on the needs that were identified as per the needs assessment conducted by ONSUR's team.
The planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults.
ONSUR will implement the following activities under this project:
1- Expansion of water distribution networks and sewerage systems in informal settlements and camps through connection with existing water and sewerage main pipelines. Where needed, a three-month water trucking may be included and Rehabilitation/reactivation of WASH facilities, using hybrid power supply solutions (including renewable, environmentally friendly supply) and exploring mixed approaches (user fees and cash assistance) for mobilization of local resources for management/maintenance. Onsur has addressed this point through:
- Establishment of water system within the camp (Water network, Borehole equipment, water tanks). The water points will be accessible for the people with special needs where applicable.
- Control of water borne diseases (WBDs) through hygiene promotion and chlorination of water through Supporting the system with HTH, fuel and oil.
- Water trucking for three months
2- Construction and rehabilitation of latrines, showers, and regular emptying of septic tanks. Onsur has addressed this point through:
- Provision and installation of latrines blocks
- Maintenance for the existing latrines blocks
- Provision and installation of latrines blocks for people with disabilities
3- Hygiene promotion and hygiene kits (in kind and through the CVA modality). Onsur has addressed this point through:
- HKs distribution – Voucher modality, where Onsur has experience in implementing the voucher modality in food items through BHA fund
- Hygiene promotion sessions about general hygiene and COVID-19 precautionary measures
4- Regular solid waste management. ONSUR has addressed this point through solid waste management under different project with a need of Covering the existing comps.
The Project also designed to provide disabilities, elderly, and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups needs into consideration during the design phase of these infrastructures (such as providing the PWD and elderly with ramps for easy access to the toilets blocks), with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘PWD’.

3 - Needs

Syria faces a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies. According to (HNO, 2023) 12.1M people are estimated to be food insecure. Over 2M people live in informal settlements . More recently, the accelerating economic deterioration and impacts of climate change have increasingly become additional key drivers of needs, compounding vulnerabilities even further. 2.8 million IDPs live in last-resort sites in Aleppo and Idleb Governorates in Northwest Syria, of which 77 percent are women and children.With the continuation of war operations, the humanitarian situation in Northwest Syria continues to deteriorate, impacting all life aspects of the Syrian families and further increasing their struggles to meet their basic needs.Currently, the first cholera case has been confirmed in northwest Syria and the number of cases is still increasing. Furthermore, the hygiene situation, in general, became further worsened the situation let to a huge number of water-borne and vector-borne diseases in the project's sub districts in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. So far, in 2022, about 321,849 water-borne diseases, 22,374 cases of leishmaniasis, and cholera diseases have been reported in the project's sub-districts.According to 2022 HNO, the proposed project sub districts rank 4 on a final severity scale.Also, 24.4% of functioning water stations in NWS were working without Chlorination according to ACU dashboard in August 2022.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Movements of water trucks in congested, camp like settings Mitigating Measure:Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding location of distribution points, delivery routes and its schedule; Avoid location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establish clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Conduct safety training with truck drivers/operatorsRisk:Toilets present GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7;Movements of desludging trucks in congested, camp like settings;Reliability of services.Mitigating Measure: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned.Risk: Bathing spaces are installed inappropriately and culturally insensitively Mitigating Measure: Consult/engage community regarding desludging services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Establish clear routes and schedule for desludging trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Conduct safety training (include operational safety/PPE) with truck drivers


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk : SEA by humanitarian workers male dominated, gate keepers and potential other beneciariesMitigation measures: 1. Establish a feedback mechanism 2. Recruitfemale staff and train them.3. Train staff on code of conduct 4. All staff contracts toinclude PSEA policy signed 5. Community committee with equitable representation of women 6. Train staff on the Inter-Agency Coordination in Community-Based ComplaintMechanismsRisk :Exploitation while receiving assistanceMitigation measures: Ensure that facilities (and distributions as required) are held in locations with safe access, convenient for recipients, and that all participants are aware of and follow protection guidelines. Also ensure that complaint and response mechanisms are well known and function well for a positive experience by users.
