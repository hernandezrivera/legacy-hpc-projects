Project Summary

Project summary:
The crisis has cast a heavy shadow on all towns in As-Suwayda Governorate, especially in recent years, and the economic situation has worsened. There are many existing agricultural projects that require large amounts of fertilizer.
The project will produce concentrated liquid fertilizer necessary for agricultural production and reduce or end dependence on chemical fertilizers. The project may contribute in the future to the transition to organic agriculture.
The region is also characterized by the spread of cattle, goat and sheep farms
Establishing a unit to produce concentrated liquid fertilizer from animal waste, based on an (existing and effective) model that the organization’s employees contributed to building.
This project is based on establishing this unit among a group of farmers who raise cows in the village of Al-Qurayya (there is a prepared study for this place, but it can be done any other places with a small changes) in order to:
1. Establishing a unit to produce concentrated liquid fertilizer with a capacity of 100 m3, and selling the production to farmers wishing to improve their products. There were scientific experiments conducted in the village on fertilizer and it proved to be a resounding success. (You can see the technical appendix attached at the end of the presentation.)
2. Producing the necessary gas to invest in the pasteurization process for fertilizer, and in home cooking for the families benefiting from the project.
3. Employing a number of workers in the facility from the most needy groups.
4. Investing the profits resulting from the project in establishing similar projects in preparation for creating a larger entity that can contribute seriously and effectively to the development process, both professional and educational.
Expected results from project implementation:
1. Strengthening the capabilities of local communities to enable them to withstand crises and recover from their effects, by helping the affected and poor recover early and improve their quality of life.
2. Encouraging the expansion of the use of clean energies at home and in the factory in small and very small projects.
3. Contributing to creating job opportunities, reducing poverty and stimulating economic growth.
4. Developing the capabilities of women in particular, actively involving them in early community recovery processes, supporting female-headed families, and empowering women and youth to carry out local income-generating activities.
5. Supporting agriculture, livestock, various rural activities, rural industries, and supporting businesses.
6. Supporting the establishment of similar projects by allocating a portion of the projects’ profits for this purpose.
7. Supporting the professional educational programs carried out by the institution.
8. Supporting the social needs of beneficiaries, such as health, housing, education, and people with special needs.
Beneficiaries:
1- Owners of small cow farms spread in the targeted area.
2- Farmers in the targeted area.
3- The local community.
The project justification:
General project justification:
- The current crisis in Syria: in terms of the increase in the number of people in need of humanitarian assistance and the worsening levels of poverty and unemployment, especially in the most affected areas, all Syrian governorates.
- The need for projects that are based on clean energies: by establishing and encouraging projects that produce and invest in clean energies.
- Producing specific fertilizers that help improve agricultural production: especially in light of the current conditions that limit the production and import of fertilizers. It can even help at a later stage in the spread of organic farming.
- Contributing to the completion of similar projects in other regions based on the profits generated from the project.
