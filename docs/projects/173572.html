Project Summary

This project will contribute to the strategic objectives set by the HRP by providing protection services ( Child Protection , Gender Base Violence , General Protection) and Shelter/ NFIs for bot SouthSudanese and Non- South Sudanese .In the states where South Sudanese Khartoum ( Jabal Aulia) , White Nile ( Kosti , As-Salam, Aj Jabalian) ,
West Kordofan ( Keilak , Al-Odia , Al-Meriem , Al-Nouhoud , Al-Salam , Al-logawa , Al-debab , Al-Ghubaish) , South Kordofan ( Leri , Kadugli , Taloudi) , South Darfur ( Beilil , Burem , Elsalam), North Darfur ( Allait) , East Darfur (Elferdous & Adil & Bahr Al-Arab & Abukarinka & Assalaya & Yassin & Abujabrah) , and as For Non-South Sudanese South Darfur ( Umdafouq , Dimso) , Kassala ( Khashim Al-Girba , Wad Al-Helway) , Garedif ( Al-fashaga).
The intervention in general protection will be through enhancing the essential welfare activities to provide people sufficient services to decrease the trauma of conflict and crises they have been through , and its effect on their psychological status with raising their dignity by delivering direct livelihood activities
with to PSN , WAR , PWD , EAR . Through diverse training and livelihood activities to increase they ability to have dignified services. through providing communities with small scale projects to give them the ability to enhance their areas with live saving activities
They will be activities for women empowerment to unable women to receive more trainings to support the families and children and decrease the number of WAR and CAR , and give them the ability to stand alone. Providing the knowledge to vulnerable people to know their right , assistance , and raising their awareness through out psychological , psychosocial , referrals pathways , and sessions to have an over all outcome in changing their behaviors to decrease the violence that have been or might be facing them. With enhancing their capacities for sustained resilience, involvement and community participation CBPN , and Women Committees . Strengthening referral and working with other community based structures.
The project will improve access to CP emergency and prevention services to reduce the incidents and effect of violence, exploitation and abuse. Vulnerable children will be provided with adequate psycho-social support at constructed/rehabilitated CFSs at the community level. GAH will also enhance the protection capacity of the of communities to identify, report , respond and prevent different protection risks and concerns by sensitization and training of CBCPNs to act and respond better to CP issues.
For refugees living in camps, over-crowding and congestion remains a serious concern, with all camps currently hosting populations beyond initial capacity. It remains difficult to ensure that adequate space and basic services are available to absorb new arrivals while sustaining service provision to the existing caseloads. This is particularly problematic in camps hosting South Sudanese in White Nile and East Darfur with the added challenge of complex land rights of private landowners
