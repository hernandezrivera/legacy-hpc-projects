Needs

Based on its past and ongoing work, an effective reintegration programme for children formerly associated with armed forces or armed groups, Prevention and response to GBV are the priority areas for WINN intervention in the state. Under a previous UNICEF-funded 12 months project, and 6months NHF funded project, WINN identified and trained 115 MRM Volunteers from 6 LGAs of Bayo, Kwaya Kusar, Konduga, Mafa, Gwoza and Dikwa to document profiles of CAAFAG, unaccompanied and separated children (UASC)and other vulnerable groups including orphans. Despite the progress made in previous projects, some gaps and needs remained due to high influx of people on daily basis from the remote villages after military clearance and operations. There is an urgent need to strengthen the system of community-based system of identification and acceptance of CAAFAG and GBV survivors, and children released and escaped from JAS, and provide them with psycho-social support and sustainable Socio-economic reintegration services linked to their individual needs and services availability, aimed at reducing their vulnerability to other grave violations of their rights. Also, to address violations that already occurred to them. WINN will work in collaboration with existing/established child protection partners/committees in project locations. WINN will use CPIMS tools developed by CPSWG currently being used by other partners. This will ensure standardized approach to data collection and information management for CAAFAG, GBV and Otherwise vulnerable children including child at risk etc. Identification needs to be strengthened by ensuring that case workers/MRM Interviewers apply the definitions CAAFAG and GBV within the local context and culture and account for varying levels of vulnerability. This understanding includes the formal and informal association of children with armed groups including the CJTF and local vigilante groups and also those rescued, released and escaped from armed groups.

Activities

Through this project, WINN with the support of Donors and other stakeholders proposes to implement a broad-based programme using seven main strategies. These include: i)Building the capacity of MRM Informant/Interviews to strengthen the monitoring, verification, prevention and reporting of incidents of SGBV and grave violations against children; ii)Building the capacities of community actors on SGBV, iii)Building the capacities of community actors on MRM and CAAFAG programming; iv)Putting in place community-based mechanisms to prevent SGBV and the recruitment and use of children by armed groups; v)Providing Psycho-social support and short-term reinsertion packages to Both SGBV survivors, Boys and Girls and Caregivers released, escaped, surrendered from armed groups.vi)Providing Reinsertion kits to children and caregivers released from administrative custody/military detention; vii)Providing economic reintegration based on age and gender sensitivities for GBV survivors, CAAFAG and other highly vulnerable children including children in CJTF as well as the caregivers. In order to actualize these strategies, WINN will ensure an effective coordination and partnerships arrangement are in place with all relevant State actors on GBV, MRM, CAAFAG and Child protection programmes.
