Project Summary

SEMA plans to build on its previous project, in order to proceed in providing CP services in Azaz, Afrin, and Idleb city. The project will provide comprehensive child protection services including 1-Structured PSS to the children(B&G) and youth (M&F) through using "Child Resilience" and “Youth Resilience” curriculums for children and youth to develop their self-resilience and social skills such as cultural and recreation skills. 2-Document and provide services for child survivals and at-risk cases including children with disabilities Supervision mechanism will be in place to support caseworkers to provide safe, ethical and competent case management services. 3-Providing emergency cases with IPA to enable immediate response when it is needed. 4-Provision of awareness-raising campaigns that will be covering CP issues (Child labour, family separation, dropping out of schools and dealing with CWDs) through 3 outreach teams. 5-Supporting 3 Community Child Protection Networks (CPNs) in order to respond to violations affecting children. CPNs will be composed of youth and adults (M&F) including people with disabilities volunteering to protect children in their communities. Moreover, SEMA will mobilize their Health, Nutrition, Physiotherapy, Specialized Mental Health services to build internal referral pathway in order to contribute to family strengthening approach.
IN CASE OF EMERGENCY
SEMA will use one harmonized training kit developed by the protection cluster for the emergency response to make use of existing capacity and reduce duplication. Mobile teams will target areas with high numbers of new IDPs and adapt locations based on the dynamic needs. Activities will include prevention and response services, which will be mainly (PFA, and dissemination of key messages on (GBV, CP, MA), referral to protection or other services, high-risk cases provided with direct support service, provide specialized services and conduct rapid protection needs assessment. Coordination will take place with actors in Syria and in Turkey at the district level. building on our capacity to provide integrated protection services. In addition, SEMA will coordinate within its respective districts with non-protection actors to facilitate referrals, share information about protection services, and engage in joint activities such as distributions. SEMA will have dedicated focal points in Syria and Turkey.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020: More than 5.7 million children reported sever need and child protection issue in all 14 governorates of Syria putting children at risk of exploitation, abuse or neglect. Events leading to family separation indicate how related protection and child protection issues increase vulnerability of affected boys and girls, with death or disappearance of caregivers, separation due to displacement or conflict, child marriage and child recruitment among cited reasons for family separations. Respondents in those assessed communities where discrimination in the delivery of humanitarian assistance emerged, identified unaccompanied girls as the most likely group to face such discrimination. The conflict and its impact on children and families have negative coping mechanisms such as child labor, early marriage and child recruitment, putting adolescent boys and girls at particular risk, while straining the capacities of families and communities to protect children.According to child-protection-situation-monitoring-dashboard Dec 2020 conducted by the CPMT, interviewing 41 key information KIs providers in those three communities of Aleppo and Idlib: 73.17 % of KIs reported children in need for focused non-specialized psychosocial services.70.73% of KIs who reported children engaged in type of work that prevents them from going to school. 60.98% of KIs that reported children living in households with persons with specific needs (PWSN) engaged in child labour. 24.39% of KIs who reported children being separated from their usual caregivers.Events leading to family separation indicate that diversity of vulnerability factors with death (24 per cent for boys and 22 per cent for girls), economic need (20 per cent for boys), child marriage (22 per cent for girls) and child recruitment (14 per cent for boys) among top cited reasons for family separations.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be Implemented through three structured and outreach teams (M&F) in Azaz, Sharran, and Idleb city. Also, PSS workers will provide structured PSS services. The structured teams will be in a static facility supported by SEMA respectively: Moaz Abu Mehdi Hospital in Azaz - Sharan WGSS centre in Sharran Idlib Surgical Hospital in Idlib city and the outreach team will take each facility as a starting point for their activities in the surrounding communities. In each location, there will be a CP case management supervisor, a case specialist, and three outreach team each one consist of 4 outreach worker who will Identify and refer the cases to the centres and provide semi-structured PSS and awareness-raising sessions to (B, G, M, F) on CP issues. SEMA team will be responsible for the management of the CP teams through the Project Manager, as well as the M&E team.SEMA’s field teams will be in close coordination and consultation with local stakeholders. The project will be managed by SEMA Project Manager who will follow-up the detailed implementation plan and closely oversee the field coordinator and provide him/her with clear tasks and roles so as to ensure that the work plan is met, and target outputs are achieved. SEMA will ensure the coordination with the local councils, and the technical team will follow-up the regular procedures until the end of the project. SEMA will ensure that the good access of female youth through provision transportation. SEMA MEAL team will conduct different activities to monitor the work progress in each facility including the following tools:Monitoring visits-Bimonthly.Facility assessment-Quarterly.FGDs-Satisfaction surveys-indicator tracker.Furthermore, SEMA team applies SAFE mechanism in order to improve accountability. The system facilitates receiving complaints and suggestions through a hotline, WhatsApp, email, and complaints box.

Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed; 2. In multi-family tents , lack of doors and partitions for sleeping and changing clothes can increase exposure to sexual harassment and assault, early marriage is also increased Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: a) advocate for appropriate and adequate assistance to be provided. B) sensitization of women, girls, boy and men will be conducted. c) SEMA will hire localized and gender balanced staff, the staff will be trained on GBV core concepts and referral mechanism to refer any GBV survivor upon disclosure.The project will enhance awareness among CP networks of CP issues related to protection by avoiding exposing children to further physical and psychological harm, assist them in referring them to appropriate help and support which is available.
