Project Summary

Through this project, GOALPrime will improve the lives of 300,000 conflict-affected individuals (30% female, 20% male, 30% girls, and 20% boys) through the provision of WASH services to sustain safe and quality water, clean latrines, and sanitation facilities across 10 selected LGAs in BAY States. 300,000 individuals (60% women/girls and 40% men/boys), including 50,000 household heads, will benefit from hygiene promotion via door-to-door and will also receive hygiene kits to support proper hygiene practice. 50,000 vulnerable women in camps and adolescent girls in schools and camps will be sensitized on menstrual hygiene management, promotion, and education, after which they will receive reusable sanitary pads (RSP) and laundry soap for daily maintenance of the RSP. 5% of the total target will be people living with disabilities. Handwashing will be established as a component to achieve proper hand hygiene and a range of health and development objectives. Through this project, GOALPrime will establish 200 handwashing facilities, which will be positioned across all the latrines provided and in some other strategic locations across the camps and host community and will be managed and maintained by the WASHCOM team. Constant water supply to the handwashing stations with a mild chlorine content of 0.5% chlorine to ensure decontamination of jams and other hand-transmitted diseases. Also, through this project, GOALPrime will rehabilitate 180 boreholes to provide safe and clean water at a minimum of 15 L/P/D to 300,000 individuals and continue to maintain the rehabilitated boreholes via the services of community borehole operators (local artisans), who will be trained by the GOALPrime WASH technical team, who will continue overseeing the O&M activities of the community-based borehole operators or local artisans and WASHCOM. This approach is to ensure that there is no disruption of water supply across the target LGAs. This project will support water point chlorination (WPC) and monitoring the free residual chlorine (FRC) in line with best practices and standards as recommended by the Water Technical Group (WTG) through an engaged community water point chlorinator. An online chlorination method will be primarily used by using an automated Dosatron on the rehabilitated boreholes. 50 dosatrons will be installed and calibrated according to the sector standard. Through this project, GOALPrime Water Testing Lab will continue the water quality testing and monitoring of the chemical parameters and bacteriological contamination of water samples across the targeted locations.
Through the implementation of this project, GOALPrime aims to undertake the rehabilitation of 200 latrine blocks, the construction of 30 new blocks adhering to sector standards, and the systematic dislodgment of 230 filled-up latrine blocks. Additionally, the project entails the procurement and distribution of 1,000 sets of sanitation kits to targeted end users across the LGAs involved. This strategic deployment of resources is intended to support daily routine environmental maintenance and elevate standards in latrine management within the designated regions.

1 - Needs

According to the HNO 2024, 5 million people have been identified in need of humanitarian WASH assistance across the BAY States with a significant deterioration of the severity compared to last year. Women and girls accounting for 52% of the population in need, often are tasked with fetching water far away, potentially exposing them to protection issues. Children accounting for 60% of the population in need are highly impacted making them susceptible to WASH-related diseases and malnutrition. Persons with disability require adaptive WASH infrastructures, water supply, and sanitation, through which their dignity and hygiene are upheld. (HNO-2024) Access to safe and sufficient quantity of water remains limited in nearly all of the BAY states affecting the population groups in the IDP/informal settlements, returnees, and host communities, with 17% of assessed households reported having less than SPHERE standard of 15l/p/d with 5% of assessed households having to walk for more than 1 hour round trip to access water. GOALPrime via this project provides access to safe and clean water to 1,736,819 in need via rehabilitation, upgrades, and reticulation of water points, Water quality analysis, Waterpoint chlorination, and provision of common pipelines services for partners with the need to ascertain the quality of the dear beneficiaries water quality for consumption through the WASH Sector. These limitations with access to water within SPHERE standards especially impact women and girls who primarily are tasked with the responsibility of fetching water for the family exposing them to protection concerns, especially in locations where the water collection point is far away from the community. Within the camps, overcrowding coupled with groundwater challenges has resulted in water supply needs remaining unmet. Overall Borno has 9 LGAs with critical/severe water needs (Severity between 3 and 5), while Adamawa has 12 LGAs with critical/severe water needs (severity between 3 and 5) Yobe has 4 LGAs with critical/severe water needs (Severity between 3 and 5), total 25, LGAs with critical/sever water needs (severity b/w 3 and 5) in three BAY states (Borno, Adamawa & Yobe). (HNO-2024) According to the WASH sector gap analysis (NE_NGA_2024_Response_Framework_WASH), there are 1,778,020 people in need across 10 LGAs selected by GOALPrime in Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe States affected by shortages of clean water, and poor sanitation and hygiene practices in 2023. The WASH Sector’s latest PiN and HRP 2024, show that 5,044,433 people in the BAY States need WASH services. GOALPrime via this project selected 10 LGAs. In Girei with 45765 people in need, Yola North 29731 people in need, Yola South has 41891 people in need, Dikwa has 93,406 people in need, Jere 338156 people in need, Kala/Balge 33943 people in need, Mafa 61516 people in need, Maiduguri 146362 people in need, Geidam 112154 people in need, and Jakusko 50929 people in need.Within the 10 selected LGAs, a total of 789,673 people need the Provision of safe drinking water at the community and/or household level, through new construction and rehabilitation of water supply systems including handpumps, boreholes rehabilitation, or construction, water systems, and networks rehabilitation. 551,997 people need access to safe water for drinking, cooking, and personal hygiene through maintenance/repair and replacement of water system parts (including Handpumps, Solar Systems, Generator Systems, and Electric pumps), operation and maintenance of existing water systems, batch chlorination, bucket chlorination at the points of use, water treatment tablets and provision of fuel for water supply. 300,000 people need the provision of gender-segregated sanitation facilities and services

2 - Activities

Activity. 1: Map out borehole gaps in target LGAs in coordination with community stakeholders and other complementary service providers. Through this activity, GOALPrime will map out borehole gaps in specified LGAs, collaborating with community stakeholders and complementary service providers. This initiative will identify key boreholes for potential rehabilitation, assess their impact on the surrounding population, and determine the extent of damage in those slated for repair.Activity. 2: Rehabilitate and upgrade selected non-functional boreholes in target locations Through this activity, GOALPrime aims to rehabilitate and upgrade non-functional boreholes in the targeted areas, addressing water scarcity issues and enhancing access to clean and safe water for the affected population.Activity. 3: Carry out water point chlorination and free residual chlorination (FRC) monitoring. Through this activity. Through this activity, GOALPrime will enhance the safety of drinking water by chlorinating rehabilitated and government-owned boreholes in the camps and host communities and monitoring free residual chlorine levels. Automated chlorine pumps will be installed for efficient online chlorination, automatically injecting chlorine into rawwater, ensuring proper contact time before beneficiaries access clean water.Activity. 4: Identify, coach, and engage community-based borehole operators (CBBO), Water points chlorinator (WPC), Community Hygiene Promoters, and Local Area Mechanics (LAM) for the continuous operation of the boreholes, chlorination, and hygiene promotion sensitizations activities across all the project targets. Through this activity, these are community based staff (CBS) which will be responsible for carrying out Door to Door Hygiene Promotion (D2DHP), Borehole operation for daily opening and closure of the water facilities and systems troubleshooting to report to the Local Area Mechanics (LAM) that have been trained by the GOALPrime Borehole Engineers, which will be carrying out minor Operation and Maintenance (O&M) of the water system and GOALPrime will provide them with Borehole Operation and Maintenance repairing tools and borehole parts to ensure uninterrupted water supply across all the targeted locations.Activity. 5: Construction of reticulation lines in the camps, schools, health facilities and communities Through this activity, GOALPrime will construct water reticulation networking from the rehabilitated borehole a good yield to other locations where members of the community cover longer distance before accessing for water. GOALPrime would ensure sector standard on waiting time and distance to covering before accessing water. Schools with with few water fetching point will be consider to during this project implementation and schools with no borehole or damage will be construct or rehabilitate to make the learns and student more comfort to learning and be free from sanitation challenges.Activity. 6: Rehabilitation, Dislodgement and upgrade of non-functional and filled-up latrines in the locations targeted by this project in the camps, schools, health facilities and child friendly spaces. Through this activity, GOALPrime will provide sanitation facilities according to sectors standard to ensure the people in need live with dignity and to ensure reduction of Open Defecation. Through the CLTs triggering under this activity GOALPrime will coordinate with other existing partners in the location to End Open Defecation (ODF) across all the locations.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

By incorporating protection principles into every strategy, GOALPrime aims to integrate the safeguarding of beneficiaries and stakeholders throughout the project execution, emphasizing the prevention of gender-based violence (GBV) and ensuring a Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) framework. Our commitment to prioritizing the safety, dignity, and well-being of both beneficiaries and project participants is unwavering. Equitable access to services, devoid of obstacles and biases, will be a cornerstone of our approach, tailored to the specific needs of the target population. GOALPrime is dedicated to addressing the needs of particularly vulnerable individuals and groups, including those with disabilities and survivors of GBV. We will implement GBV Risk Mitigation measures to minimize harm and ensure compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. Adhering to the DO NO HARM Protection Principle, our methodologies will focus on avoiding additional risks for beneficiaries. To maintain an environment free of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA), all individuals involved in the project—staff, volunteers, contractors, and vendors—will undergo mandatory PSEA orientation. They must affirm their commitment by signing GOALPrime's Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. Reporting protocols for PSEA incidents will be established, with utmost confidentiality maintained for the identity of reporters. Anonymous reporting will be encouraged whenever possible. In alignment with the Inter-Agency mechanism for reporting SEA, GOALPrime will engage systematically with affected populations, implementing confidential complaints and investigations procedures at both systemic and individual agency levels. Following recommendations from the IASC Task Force on PSEA, GOALPrime will actively cooperate with other agencies in creating and maintaining community-based complaints mechanisms (CBCMs) to prevent and respond to SEA. Awareness campaigns will emphasize that humanitarian assistance is unconditional and not contingent on sexual favors. Project beneficiaries will be informed about how to access appropriate complaints mechanisms, and support will be provided to establish community-based mechanisms if SEA occurs. GOALPrime is committed to ensuring the effectiveness of established CBCMs and promoting inter-agency coordination to facilitate broad and straightforward access for potential complainants. Recognizing the importance of environmental sustainability, GOALPrime will ensure that project approaches and tools do not contribute to environmental degradation. Additionally, climate change integration and routine sensitization on climate change prevention will be incorporated into project activities to foster community awareness and resilience.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Throughout the project, GOALPrime is committed to a robust monitoring and feedback system. Monthly monitoring visits will be conducted to project locations to oversee progress. Quarterly Focused Group Discussions (FGDs) in intervention communities will gather comprehensive feedback from diverse groups, including males, females, youth, elders, stakeholders, and people with disabilities. Feedback collected from these processes will be carefully analyzed and utilized for continuous learning for better implementation of the project. To ensure project adherence and effectiveness, routine internal spot checks will be performed, and reviews will be organized periodically. Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will be carried out after each distribution, with a particular emphasis on feedback from vulnerable communities. This feedback will inform ongoing efforts to enhance service delivery on a monthly basis. Recognizing the importance of open communication, GOALPrime will establish a 24/7 beneficiaries communication and feedback mechanism accessible across all project locations. This platform will be dedicated to addressing queries and complaints promptly, fostering a responsive and accountable project environment.
