Project Summary

Inter-agency, toll free hotline for the Idai and Kenneth cyclone responses. A call centre based in Maputo receives and logs cases from the affected population, providing immediate responses to standardised queries and where necessary referring cases on to humanitarian focal points for follow up and action before calling the complainant back with an update on the action taken or clarification.
