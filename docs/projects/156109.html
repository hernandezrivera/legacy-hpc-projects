Project Summary

Considering the high numbers of newly arriving people , there is a significant need for the provision of non- formal education support to minimize the gap in education provision for vulnerable IDP populations. Displacement is likely to have caused significant disruption, both to IDP and hosting communities. IDP children and youth put a significant strain on the capacity of host communities to accommodate increased education needs. Whilst there is willingness from host communities to support education needs of IDP children, school capacities are in many cases far overstretched. Overwhelming schools’ capacity to meet education needs through expecting them to accommodate new influxes of IDP children poses the risk of both undermining quality of teaching and learning for all school going children, as well as potentially ‘doing harm’ through raising tensions between vulnerable IDP and host populations. Considering the prolonged and violent nature of the conflict in northern Syria, moreover, there is a high likelihood that populations, both host communities and IDPs, have experienced significant war related stress/duress.

Protection Risk Analysis

The most expected risks that beneficiaries or humanitarian workers may be exposed to through the implementation of the project’s activities are:1. Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target areas, affects the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in project. This includes the risk of targeted attacks and bombing of schools/ communities and the risks of mines, airstrikes and cross fire.2. Child safety incidents in school due to bullying and overburdened teachers.3. Risk of not having enough WASH facilities for boys and girls. This might cause lack of privacy to the children.4. Risk of long distance and young children walking alone. Children may get lost, be attacked or lose interest in learning. 1- the related mitigating measures that will be adopted:Closely monitor the security situation in coordination with partners and relevant stakeholders and conduct a security assessment. They will ensure that staff members and teachers are trained in safety and security best practices, including mine risk education.2- If and when the local security situation deteriorates: a) Immediately inform internal emergency focal point on the situation; b) Coordinate with partners, cluster and hibernate/suspend the operations if necessary; c) Examine the possibilities of adopting different modalities to implement the project. All important documents will be saved as soft copies, so that they will not get lost due to an attack. Decisions are taken in advance3- Work in collaboration with WASH section who will establish WASH facilities in schools.4- Training of partners and local teams on Child safety, development of school’s specific child safety policies; establishment of complaint mechanisms in school.
