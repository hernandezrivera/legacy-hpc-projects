Project Summary


This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls,women,and men,with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities with attention to new IDPs and people wirth special needs,targeting 247500 beneficiares during the project periode(85000 women, 69000 men, 50000 girls,43500 boys) including all age groups;children, adolescences, and older adults.9000 of them PWDs.
This will be done through specific interventions focusing on:

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk related to GBV on CHWs when visiting the homes, also risks of GBV on benefciaries and health staff in health facilities Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High, Mitigation measure: each two CHWs will work together, training of health staff on providing first-line support, identification of GBV cases,Identification of safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBVcases ,Risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers of women and girls, Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High, Mitigation measure: Establish a confidential complaint mechanism, including victim assistance, train staff on the code of conduct, including the code of conduct of all staff contracts and address any SEA complaints, SDI is a member of PSEA network. Risk of discrimination or if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others. Likelihood: Low, Impact:High Mitigation : the project design encompasses accessibility of services by vulnerable groups (physical accessbility..etc) to ensure from equal access for all beneficires including PWDs through doing rehabilitation work, which includes installation of ramps, handrails and WCs special for PWDs to increas thier access to receive the health service. Risk: Increase outbreak of COVID19 and Cholera ,Mitigation :SDI will strict apply precautions measures to protect the staff and patients in accordance to IPC guideline and Integrate RCCE to enhance applying all prevention measures


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Beneficiaries are at risk on being exposed to any sexual misconduct by the humanitarian workers and they might not report the misconduct due to the following reason: 1. lack of knowledge of reporting mechanisms, especially for people with disabilities and women living in far away areas. 2. feeling afraid of retaliation. 3. fear of stigma. 4. fear of deprivation of assistance. 5. being unable to understand written banners for being illiterate and/or for having a visual disability. 6. for children, language might be complicated To mitigate these risks, SDI will : 1. aim at clearly announcing the hotline number and email specialized for PSEA of SDI and UN numbers. through many means, like, banners, social media, and messages to beneficiaries phones through telegram channel to ensure reaching to the biggest number of beneficiaries including women living in far away areas and people with disabilities. 2. Conduct PSEA training for all staff members and contractors/suppliers dealing with beneficiaries and make them read and sign CoC that state Zero Tolerance towards SEA violations. 3. Conduct awareness raising sessions on PSEA for beneficiaries to encourage them to report any violations and stress on the confidentiality and on eliminating any other fears that they might have. 4. Conduct awareness raising sessions for children in an appropriate language. 5. complaint boxes 6. Using PSEA field and organizational checklists tools


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: All beneficiaries especially those who may have physical limitations are able to receive services. Risk: Physical accessibility difficulties Mitigation: SDI planned activities to meet the specific needs to enable access for all disabilities to services, SDI had modified the health facility including ramp and entrance with handles for disabled. Assumpion:There is no change in the covid19 and Cholera context nor an increase in the infected cases and the staff of the project is fully workingRisk: Increase outbreak of COVID19 and Cholera and health facility staff infected.Mitigation :SDI will strict apply precautions measures to protect the staff and patients in accordance to IPC guideline, Integrate RCCE to enhance applying all prevention measuresAssumption:Access to targeted areas remains possible and safe. Risk:Access to targeted areas becomes hindered or insecure, Increased checkpoints and road closures Mitigation:Monitor the security instances in the targeted location and the routes leading to the area to ensure staff access.If the security situation changes in a certain area,we can wait or postpone activities for a few days or change targeted areas to another accessible area.Assumption:No more deteriorating security situation,Risk:Increase in influxes of IDPs result from more escalation in South Idlib, Mitigation:SDI always increase the operational capacity and provide more medical drugs through a private fund to respond to the extra need for most vulnerable
