Project Summary

Objectives

Improve hygiene and sanitation environment through Fecal Sludge construction, bathing cubicle and hygiene promotion training to refugees. Also improve water environment for bangladeshi host community.

Needs

In the target location, there is a gap for access water and latrine. Also they are not received hygiene kit.

Activities or outputs

Component 1. Construct 7 Fecal Sludge Management Site and Hygiene Training, Hygiene kit distribution in Kutupalong Camp19/ Approximately 17,500 people can sustainably access toilet through whole year and improve refugee’s consciousness about Hygiene and promote proper action.Component 2. Construct 90 bathing cubicle and Hygiene Training, Hygiene kit distribution in Kutupalong Camp19/ Approximately 3,600 people can access bathing cubicle through whole year and improve refugee’s consciousness about Hygiene and promote proper action.Component 3. Construct 20 Deep Tube Well and Hygiene Training, Hygiene kit distribution for Bangladeshi in Host community/ Approximately 2,500ppl can access safe water through whole year and improve Host community’s consciousness about Hygiene and promote proper action.
