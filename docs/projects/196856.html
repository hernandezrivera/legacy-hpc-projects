Project Summary

POINT has a prolonged professional technical CCCM experience and had successful interventions in 185 IDP sites through a strong strategic partnership with CCCM Cluster,
SCHF, IOM, ACTED as POINT has implemented more than 10 CCCM projects in Northwest.
supporting IDP sites to provide CCCM professional activities and support the IDPs in 40 sites based on the extreme and essential needs of the displaced people inside these sites
and mitigate the negative coping mechanisms at the community level.
POINT will start the project by ensuring that all project human resources are in place, and this includes all the direct and support staff and completing all related processes like
hiring or renewing the contract of the project staff. POINT CCCM team will start the interventions by conducting advocacy activities and the targeted 40 sites to acquaint the
community and site, residents, with the project's activities and objectives and gain all needed support and endorsement and ensure a high level of participation in the project's
activities for all the population segments in the sites. POINT CCCM team will conduct a detailed gap assessment in the targeted sites to specify the contextual status and generate needs analyses regarding all sectors including CCCM, WASH, shelter, education, health, and more, especially the CCCM sector, to design an appropriate intervention plan to ensure the project achievement and its objectives will be met at the end of our intervention and the end results to be delivered as planned. The planned project core activities will be in place and are being carried out in the middle of the first month of the
project as per the project work plan by starting the establishment of the site management committees (SMCs) and it will be followed by providing the physical CCCM training for the committees' members and they will sign an official ToR after the CCCM training to start their roles and activities in their sites accordingly. The 1st objective: Building camps management and camps coordination capacity (CCCM capacity) for 40 IDP sites. Conducting advocacy and
community sensitization through public sessions, and disseminating advocacy messages such as leaflets, and posters to introduce the project activities to the targeted
communities. Coordination with local authorities, site managers, and relevant focal points to ensure their endorsement and facilitation. Conducting a detailed assessment of management capacity in the targeted (40) IDP sites. Conducting CCCM and SPHERE training for the site management committees Conducting physical fire safety
practical training (maneuver) for the site management committees. Forming subcommittees in each IDP site. Mentoring and coaching for committees and site management. Empowering the SMCs on protection monitoring aspects related to safety audit and service monitoring inside the IDP sites.
The 2nd objective: Improving living conditions and fire safety in 40 IDP sites' Community care and maintenance component. Establishment of 280 fire points in the targeted and maintaining approximately 216 old fire extinguishers in In previously targeted sites ( depending on the previous reports of the committees upon the use of the fire extinguishers ). The 3rd objective: conducting awareness sessions camping on the means of protection against the cholera pandemic including the distribution of awareness brochures and posters inside the IDP sites, in addition, the POINT team will conduct a periodic protection service mapping and referrals pathway inside IDP sites and link the referrals cases with the suitable nearby referrals centers and following up the cases with the relevant actors.
The 4th objective: Increasing the well-being of camps residents through conducting recreational activities and social events that will be initiated by the established SMCs and the sub-committees in each IDP site

3 - Needs

The assessment aims to determine the situation in 40 sites in case of the availability of the site management committees who are responsible for managing the camps' affair andtake the lead in the coordination between camps residents and the local authorities to deliver people needs and voices to the management representatives, also to define the most in-demand needs within the camps and what are the most rehabilitation and restoration activities which can raise the living conditions inside the camps and give more suitability atmosphere within the camps. Most of the sites in Idlib governate need support with first aid tools and fire extinguishers, also establishing trained teams on first aid and fire suppression, in most the camps about 95 % of the total number of camps there are no fire extinguishing or first aid tools even there are some people about 33% of the total number of targeted camps who attended training and have experience on fire extinguishing as many of them are working with the civil defense organization, but they need to receive technical training about fire extinguishing and first aid and need to be formed as an official committee inside the camps with clear terms of reference and supported with the needed tools to respond when a fire happens in the site or any emergency. It is recommended to establish fire points inside the camps supported with fire extinguishers and first aid kits and activate the protection services inside the IDP sites

4 - Implementation of activities

After coordinating with the CCCM cluster to identify the project core activities will be the establishment of the site management committees (SMCs) will be followed by providing the CCCM training for the committees' members and they will sign an official ToR after the CCCM training to start their roles and activities in their sites accordingly. for the established site management committees (SMCs) as planned. coordination was held with the CCCM cluster to identify the camps in need to fire mitigation and care andmaintenance activities and IDP sites are shared with the cluster. The project is divided into 4 main components, first: Building camps management and camps coordination capacity (CCCM capacity) for 40 IDP sites. The second component: Improving living conditions and fire safety in 40 IDP sites Community care and maintenance by establishing fire points in the targeted and maintaining old fire extinguishers in previously targeted sites.The third component: Raising communities' health awareness to mitigate the risk of the cholera pandemic in 40, in addition, to activating the protection services and referral pathways inside the IDP sites . The fourth component: Increasing the well-being of camp residents through conducting recreational activities and social events that will be initiated by the established SMCs and the sub-committees in each IDP site.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

All POINT staff and contractors sign or will sign on POINT gender and code of conductto follow our policies related to gender and protection. POINT organization iscommitted to gender balance within its team and always seeks to have a balancebetween the number of female and male team members and during any activityimplemented the team should include a member from both males and females inorder to ensure the best way of response for beneficiaries that do not cause anyembarrassment atmosphere especially for women beneficiaries, furthermore, POINTteam is trained on the protection principles and have a good experience dealing withpeople from special needs as well. Gender is mainstreamed in the design of thethe proposed project, ensuring that the needs of women are considered and properlyaddressed. Duringbeneficiaries’ selection phase, specific emphasis will be put onwomen and women-headed households to ensure that women have equal access tothe provided services. POINT will closely monitor protection risks throughout theimplementation period and will ensure coordination with Protection Cluster partners.POINT has developed mitigation measures set in place to ensure that protection ismainstreamed in all project activities and GBV risk is minimized to a minimum levelIndividual residents may over-rely on any civil response/DRR committee forresponding to a natural disaster and may shirk individual responsibility.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

POINT is strongly committed to protecting children and women from abuse, a commitment that is reflected in their Child Protection and PSEA Policies and is a member of the PSEA network and follows the guidelines. During the beneficiaries’ selection phase, specific emphasis will be put on women-headed households and their specific vulnerability to protection concerns and reduced access to employment opportunities due to their gender, and ensure to set-up feedback and complaints mechanism to receive and investigate requests and grievances regarding CCCM interventions, facilities and services at the displacement sites, as well allegations of intimidation, coercion, violence and sexual exploitation and abuse experienced by women, girls, boys and men in receiving assistance. Respond to all complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place. Ensuring that protection principles are incorporated throughout all project activities, including the promotion of meaningful access, and ensuring the safety and dignity of beneficiaries. POINT trains its field staff to safely identify and refer protection concerns to the correct unit(s) and external actors to reduce risks and support safe and dignified access for all affected populations. Ensuring that staff members and all relevant stakeholders are trained on the following do not harm principles in interventions and signed a code of conduct, receive PSEA training, and have a reporting system.
