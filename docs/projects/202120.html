Project Summary

Given the rapidly declining economic situation and compound crises in Lebanon including Cholera, several thousands of Lebanese children are missing out on education or dropping out of school. To subsidize the cost of accessing education, UNICEF will support these children, public schools and education centers they attend with funding toward hygene material, retention support programmes and other.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Project activities include ensuring students affected by the economic and health crisis are provided with the necessary health and hygene supplies in public schools or with education opportunities, recreational activities or retention support.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

All MEHE public school under the DGE and DOPS are iteratively implementing the child protection policy in schools. This engages a complex system of DOPS councilors, CP hotlines and community based interventions. Through these projects, UNICEF intends to reduce children’s risks from sexual exploitation.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

UNICEF will work with the MEHE central and regional offices, and school directors in continuous loops to receive feedback through the process. In parallel, UNICEF partners will work at school level and community level. UNICEF field officers will also undertake monitoring visits to schools as part of the HACT assurance activities. UNICEF established call centers for beneficiaries to provide feedbakc and raise complaints.
