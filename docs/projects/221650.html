1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project focuses on improving the living conditions and rights of women and children in the CD region. Through psychological support and the creation of safe spaces, the initiative aims to enhance social skills, emotional well-being, and positive coping mechanisms. It seeks to reduce the risks of forced recruitment, sexual violence, forced labor, and trafficking, prioritizing vulnerable individuals, including those at risk of recruitment, abuse, unaccompanied minors, and people with disabilities.Additionally, the project will focus on Protection Coordination, Advocacy, and Information sharing to ensure affected populations are aware of essential services. Information sessions will help them understand available services and how to access them. The project will also strengthen community conflict mitigation and resolution mechanisms. Working with local leaders, particularly in areas with both host and displaced communities, the project will foster peaceful coexistence and reduce tensions. Community leaders will help create self-protection strategies in 28 communities, promoting conflict resolution, positive coping mechanisms, and collective resilience to ensure sustainable peace, protection, and equal access to services.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project focuses on improving general protection, child protection and addressing gender-based violence (GBV) across four districts: Macomia, Muidumbe, Nangade, and Mocimboa da Praia. Protection: the project will support the referral process to protection services and information on services, it will also enhance community-based protection (support to community mechanisms and structures, empowerment activities, awareness on protection risks) and provide capacity strengthening, training and advocacy sessions for local and district authorities and other relevant and interested stakeholders. Child Protection: The project will establish four safe spaces for children to play and learn. These spaces will offer recreational and socialization activities, providing boys and girls with a safe environment to develop skills, socialize, and receive psychological support as needed. Protection assistants and social workers will identify children and adolescents at risk, referring them to specialized services following official pathways. Additionally, the project will strengthen community-based child protection systems in 24 communities by training local focal points to link communities with service providers and support structures. Prevention of and Response to GBV: The project will also create safe spaces for women and girls at risk of GBV or survivors of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV). These spaces will offer immediate safety, psychosocial support, and relevant services. Information sessions will raise awareness on GBV rights, prevention, and available services. The project will identify those in need of specialized GBV services and refer them to appropriate providers. Furthermore, community-based protection systems will be strengthened through sessions in 24 communities, focusing on self-protection mechanisms, reducing harmful practices such as survivor sex and forced marriage, and improving coordination with service providers to address and prevent GBV risks. By empowering communities and providing critical services, the project aims to create safer, more supportive environments for women and children at risk.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project identifies potential risks for women, girls, boys, and men by conducting a thorough risk assessment, consulting with affected communities, analyzing vulnerabilities and capacities, identifying exposure to protection risks such as gender-based violence, exclusion, and exploitation. The project provides a relevant response to reduce and mitigate the risks, enhancing capacities and reducing vulnerabilities, prioritizing a "do no harm" approach,. Safeguarding mechanisms, including confidential reporting systems, safe spaces, and PSEA (Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) training for staff, are in place to protect all participants from harm.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

To ensure meaningful access, the project provides age-appropriate, gender-sensitive, and disability-inclusive interventions that are easily accessible to all. We actively promote the participation of women, children, and marginalized groups in decision-making processes, ensuring that their voices are heard at every stage.Empowerment is central to the project’s design. We engage affected communities in leadership roles, support capacity-building initiatives, and encourage local ownership of the project’s activities. By involving people in decisions that impact their lives, we foster empowerment and ensure that they contribute to the solutions, leading to more sustainable and effective outcomes.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will identify people with disabilities of different sexes and ages through community-based outreach, partnering with local disability organizations, healthcare providers, and community leaders. We will ensure that both visible and invisible disabilities are recognized, taking into account the specific needs of women, girls, boys, and men with disabilities. To remove barriers to access, we will adapt our services to be inclusive and accessible. This includes providing physically accessible locations with ramps and disability-friendly facilities, as well as offering materials in accessible formats such as braille, large print, or audio. We will also provide sign language interpreters for those with hearing impairments and offer personalized support for individuals with cognitive or intellectual disabilities to navigate services. Additionally, we will establish confidential reporting channels specifically for people with disabilities to voice their concerns and access assistance. Trained facilitators will work with individuals to ensure they understand the available services and can access them without fear of discrimination or neglect. By addressing physical, informational, and social barriers, the project ensures that people with disabilities, regardless of sex or age, can fully participate in and benefit from the services, promoting their inclusion and empowerment.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed project is committed to minimizing its environmental impact through careful planning and proactive mitigation measures (implement effective waste management systems, ensuring proper disposal and recycling to minimize pollution, etc). Furthermore, the project will engage local communities in environmental education and awareness programs, encouraging sustainable practices and fostering a culture of conservation. Regular environmental monitoring will be conducted to assess the effectiveness of these measures and make any necessary adjustments, ensuring continuous environmental sustainability throughout the project’s lifecycle. In this way, the project aims to balance its development objectives with the need to protect and preserve the environment for future generations, creating a positive and lasting impact on both the community and the ecosystem.
