Project Summary

Considering the economic challenges nowadays in Northeast Nigeria in the targeted areas mention above the assessment reveal that there is food insecurity facing IDPs, residents, refugees and returnees it is expected to be more worsen, in addition to above mentioning factors, women and girls experienced (GBV), including rape .sexual assault, intimate partner violence, sexual harassment, trafficking, and sexual exploitation and abuse of girls, early married, FGM this specific considering violence is socially ascribed intended to control female body and live them in subordinate position , moreover, women and girls are economically and socially vulnerable and have a higher incidence of poverty, they lack job-based skills and means of production which increases their vulnerability to GBV; returnees, and Host communities in Kala Balge still have challenges in accessing services of SGBV Survivors of S/GBV are not support properly because they were traumatized. There is a need to offer more support to these vulnerable targeted communities and m the social workers and services provider need more training including Psychological First Aid (PFA) and Psycho-social support (PSS) counselling, there is also demand for formation of SGBV protection mechanism. Taimako-CDI will implement this project in close coordination with key stakeholders like local community and locality leaders in particular the administrative leaders , popular committees and other effective key persons and line miniseries. This project will use a community based approach to reach the protection needs of the most vulnerable population especially women, and girls. The activity foster to enhance protection services to most vulnerable women in the targeted areas through establishment/ rehabilitate of women centers with local materials to serve multi- functional purposes such as strength protection committees , provision of in –kind supports for women , strengthening the capacity of the services provider, social worker and local CBOs, provision of protection services and motivate the community.

The protracted nature of the conflict continues to reinforce pre-existing gender inequalities (with women having lesser opportunities) across the BAY states. 54 per cent of the displaced populationare female and half of these are of reproductive age and in need of access to reproductive and health services.According to GBV 2020 Need overview, Kala Balge is one of most vulnerable LGA in borno with m more than 100 thousand people in need of GBV assistance. The numbers of displaced populations continue to create vulnerabilities, with the majority being children and women. respect for the rights of individuals. Needs extend beyond material assistance, and require the provision of specific counseling and humanitarian protection activities. For example, training of social workers, construction or rehabilitation of women centers for multi -function will be deployed to ensure that community-based protection mechanisms extend its protection responsibilities the project intervention will include , establishment of women centre by local materials which will served for multi- functional services for women and female empowerment , for family tracing and reunification, for reintegration of children from state and non-state armed forces and armed groups, for referral pathway for improving the monitoring and reporting mechanism on violations among conflict affected children and prevent and respond to GBV against women and female

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

For all projects, Taimako-CDI foresees an interim monitoring system, which includes verification on the achievements and on the status of the activities’ implementation every three months. Keeping these deadlines in mind, the accountability time frame must allow Taimako-CDI staff to collect and analyze information, and to disclose it to beneficiaries respecting the principle of “taking into account”. Another key point for a functional accountability system is the awareness of Taimako-CDI staff of the fact that the monitoring process is a continuum: since the planning phase, through all the implementation process, the gathering of information and feedback has to be continuous and carried out both in structured, specific moments, and in more informal contexts that can be found through the projects’ development. Regarding the opportunities and right times for collecting and sharing feedback, information and changes, Taimako-CDI staff have to be aware that the moments for doing this are not only formal and agreed (meetings, reunions with representatives, interviews, etc.), but can happen during other activities, even those that are not foreseen or implemented by the organization, but of which it has to be made the most.
