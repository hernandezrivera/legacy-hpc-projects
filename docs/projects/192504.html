Project Summary

Throughout this project, PUI will support vulnerable individuals through health tailored activities designed to strengthen the resilience of households with vulnerable pregnant/lactating women. This project was designed based on PUI’s technical expertise and long-term presence and acceptance in the area of intervention.
Monitoring activities will be carried out throughout the life of the project in order to guarantee the compliance of the activities with agreed objectives and targets. A detailed monitoring and evaluation plan (M&E) will be developed at the beginning of the project by PUI’s Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) unit together with the technical teams and programme staff, in line with the proposed indicators defined in the intervention logical framework. Tools and monitoring activities detailed in the M&E plan will provide regular feedback on the progress towards the proposed results and allow for swift adjustment if needed. A dedicated MEAL Activities Schedule will also be prepared and followed up by the MEAL Manager.
Data collection and storage tools will grant the highest level of data protection and confidentiality, allowing a systematic disaggregation per sex, age and disability and providing automatic reporting features. While PUI MEAL team will ensure the design, development and maintenance of internal Indicator Tracking Tables and Databases, a wider integration with the MoPH Health Information System (Phenix) will also be pursued, allowing an additional layer of data triangulation between different sources and thus enhancing the overall data quality and accuracy.

Programme monitoring will combine different methodologies, based on qualitative and quantitative approaches, including field visits and surveys occurring on a regular basis. Their roll out will be ensured under the supervision of the MEAL Manger, which will be in charge of the design of the tools, the management of the MEAL Officers in the bases, and of the data analysis and report writing and dissemination. In particular:
Patient Satisfaction and Post Intervention Surveys will be conducted at least twice during the project implementation period in order to gather feedback from pregnant women on their perceptions about the quality and the outcomes of the services received, their feeling about topics such safety and accessibility as well as the patient centred approach supported by PUI. While based on a quantitative approach, these surveys might also include qualitative data collection methodologies such as Focus Groups Discussion and in-depth interviews, depending on the context.
Post Distribution Monitoring exercises will be regularly carried out in order to monitor cash and NFIs distribution, focusing on outcome and process related research questions, including direct feedback from beneficiaries about the items distributed and, potentially, complaints to be addressed.
In specific circumstances preventing direct access to the project areas, such as COVID 19 related movements restrictions decided by the GOL or civil unrest, measures will be taken to shift these activities to remote modalities (phone calls, reduced sample sizes, online FGDs, etc.)

Dedicated capitalisation activities, to be conducted at least every six months, will include Lessons Learnt workshops and/or project reviews, to be facilitated by the MEAL unit and involving technical and field teams. Such activities will be carried out against specific ToRs and will focus on detailed learning agendas and evaluation questions; best practices, implementation challenges and ways forward will be identified and discussed; capitalisation reports will be issued and disseminated to all relevant stakeholders.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

PUI is proposing this project in response to ERP's Health Sector Objectives as follows:Objective 1: Ensure access to life-saving and life-sustaining health services for those most vulnerable and in need in the context of worsening socio-economic conditions.The linked Activities are the following ones:1-Identification of pregnant and lactating women who will be enrolled in SBCI program in Akkar. 2-Individual health and nutrition counseling for the pregnant and lactating women will be provided by the midwives during the enrollment in the SBCI program.3-Maternal/child consultations will be provided to the identified pregnant/lactating women and their babies born during the project timeline4-Provision of ANC, PNC consultations for women eligible to access the assistance for Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care or Post-abortion Care5-Provision of counselling and health promotion sessions for eligible beneficiaries who will be benefit from the program in the areas of intervention Objective 4: Provide enabling, cross-cutting support to coordinated, safe, timely and quality health responses with an emphasis on vulnerable communities.The linked Activities are the following ones:1-Mobilization and training for 6 community health and nutrition volunteers (CHNV) on health, nutrition integrated curricula.2-Establishment of community health and nutrition clubs (CHNC) as part of mother-to-mother support as group support.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

A PRA was conducted at project design phase and mitigation measures were identified. Mitigations include: provision of capacity building for health staff on safeguarding policies and FCRM in place to mitigate risks for PoC of abuse and exploitation while receiving services; provision of SHR services by female staff to ensure female benfs’ access to services and respect of social and cultural norms; provision of services to all groups/nationalities and clear communication with PoC on criteria of assistance to avoid increase of inter-communal tensions; provision of services at community level to reduce risks for PoC to be exposed to violence and abuse while approaching PHCC; respect of all relevant prevention protocols to ensure that service provision does not increase the risk of transmission of COVID-19. Consultations with community members will take place during the entire project cycle to identify possible risks and mitigation measures, and adapt service provision accordingly.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

To ensure that projects implemented by PUI in Lebanon are responsive to the population’s needs within targeted areas, PUI involves the affected populations in all stages of the project cycle. During implementation, PUI will follow the recently revised FCRM Standard operating Procedures to gather feedback from beneficiaries, using multiple channels. All beneficiaries’ feedback/complaints are addressed and used to improve program implementation, provide timely response, and enhance transparency. Informed consents will be taken from any person of concern and strict confidentiality rules will be followed. The programming is tailored based on primary data directly collected from communities and continuous consultation which allow PUI to design and adapt the intervention to best suit each HH’s needs. Beneficiaries are informed of all available feedback mechanisms throughout project implementation.
