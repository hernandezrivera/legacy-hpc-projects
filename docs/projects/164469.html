Project Summary

- Provide vocational training to equip the targeted population with skills and experience
needed to bring back some type of balance to the individuals who are filled with despair
and hopelessness.
- Create new job and/or income generating opportunities in a town and the areas besides ,that has suffered tremendously over the seven years of the crisis.
- Stimulate financial independency among the targeted population and encourage them to become active in the process.
- Reconstruct 180 houses which damaged between 35% to 65% , with priority given to the
houses of families most vulnerable as per the criteria set forth and agreed on with the
partner.
-The project Target train and graduate 1400 trainees with (females and males ) to help
them use the fundamentals qualifications and skills to rehabilitate their houses ,besides
to help them find their own way to work and increase the livelihood recovery
circumstances.
- Encourage the residents to abandon negative coping mechanisms they have used to adapt to the crisis
- Part of the plan is ensuring the sustainability of the project to target more houses in
need
of rehabilitation even after the project ends. This will be conducted by adapting a
marketing strategy in the area and its surrounding to sell the manufactured Aluminum + kitchens and traditional carpentry furniture with
Competitive prices. The profit will be used to fund the rehabilitation of other damaged
houses in the area, and to cover the wages for the workers in the workshop. Other

will also benefit from the competitive prices which will help them rehabilitate their own

4 - Needs

- The total number of local residents is nearly 420.000, in addition to 8000 displaced families coming from various areas, with an average size of 5 individuals for each family. - 85% of the families suffer from poverty and the lack of job opportunities which are direct result of the damage inflicted on the shops, factories, and other entities in the area. - The destruction in the areas is major with a 35- 80 % damage to the infrastructure and main facilities. Most families have lost their businesses and therefore their income and their ability to purchase anything but their basic needs of food and other items from street vendors. They have also been relying on in-kind assistance given from charities and local NGOs or other UN agencies. -The Data has been collected by several sources, such as international organizations, local government and civil society organizations, and the visits of the Mobadrah Ahl al- sham Needs Monitoring . DPP needs assessment annual report 20019 describes the details numbers and percentages in the targeted location through the monitoring and needs assessment team ,depended on (focus groups, surveys and interviews). Data analysis: * More to 87% of people need an urgent aids ( food baskets, nutriment supplements especially for pregnant women). * Nearly 270.000 men are jobless and need to increase their income to help their own families. * Men between age of 21 and 60 are 48% of local people. * 60% of houses need to rehabilitate , because of the destruction during the war . * Nearly 50000 women are able to engage with a new criteria in order to help in rehabilitation , and build the community

5 - Implementation of activities

Divide the trainees to work in 5 workshops (carpentry furniture(woodworking), Aluminum kitchens workshop, Electricity , Sewage, and Paint). -Graduate 1400 trainees with tool kit ,in order to help them in livelihood. - Establish a Vocational Training School/Center to train those unemployed and of special needs. - Equip the workshops with all necessary tools for the training. - Advocate for the workshops in the town and reach out to as many individuals in the town as possible. - Contract with professional trainers and/or experienced volunteers; preferably from the town to ensure easy access and facilitation and to avoid any delays that might occur; before starting the training. -Interview candidates by a committee established jointly with DPP. -The committee will ensure the eligibility of the trainees and the qualifications for the trainers. Males and females trainees participating in the project will be chosen based on a vulnerability criteria agreed upon with OCHA and ,DPP. Priorities will be given to women headed of household, persons with special needs who are able to endure such training, and men from both the displaced families and the local residents as well. Trainers will be chosen based on both their experience and vulnerability, too. - Work with the procurement team at DPP to conduct all procurement in adherence to the PQP. - Arrange schedules for the workshops to accommodate the trainees and the trainers, and to ensure the workshops are progressing according to the schedule. - Make sure the trainers and the trainees follow safety procedures and take precautions to ensure the safety of all participants. - Establish a time line for the project . - Share periodic reports reflecting the progress of the project, and list any challenges that might cause delays in the implementation. - Administer a type of test at the end of each workshop to make sure the trainees are eligible to graduate and are ready to pursue such work.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Protection: the beneficiaries (Females, children ) might be at risk of harassment when being in isolated places while training process inside workshops . Impact :High The Measure: The workshops will be completely evacuated , while the training process in order to avoid any kind of abuse The Odds: low The organization working within the project will adhere to international agreements and will specially provide complete protection for the beneficiaries and the working staff of the project. The organization will also adopt its own regulations and resort to laws and the governing rules of the Syria Arab Republic in case of harassment, abuse, or assault 2- Protection: beneficiaries (Females ) could be subjected to sexual or physical abuse. The Odds: average The Impact : High The Measure: Workshops and training are equipped with surveillance. There are also confidential boxes for suggestions and complaints. * The Master chief project has no danger or harm on the beneficiaries. - Mobadrah Ahl-Sham has created a system to ensure the trainees' safety at all times ,and to avoid any mistakes which could pose harm to the beneficiaries /trainees . * The systems which mentioned before, were studied carefully and organized by experts in safety and health, by adopting the international standers in safety and health and reflecting the knowledge and experiences to the types of training process by : 1- All the target beneficiaries will attend a special session (inductions to health and safety)> By name the head of safety and health analyst. 2-The department of safety and heath in Mobadrah Ahl-Sham, will join the sessions in order to share the qualifications with lecturer and head of section (safety and Health ). 3-Responsibilities of Mobadrah Ahl-Sham will be to * manage health and safety in the workshops; * Set up a system of regular communication throughout the training about health and safety

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

* Financial: Fluctuations in the currency exchange rates in the market, which could affect procurement of the materials, supplies and toolkits. Odds: High / Impact: Average. At the beginning of the project, a contract will be signed with traders who met the procurements criteria. This can be avoided by contracting on a Fixed exchange rate with the traders. Conducting market assessment and prices, to learn about the daily, weekly, and monthly exchange rates, and maybe making reliable predictions. .The procurement committee of the project will adopt a procurement mechanism in cooperation with the project management in which the prices are not affected by the fluctuations of the market prices. Acceptable. * Logistics :Risks The Training center has been carefully chosen to Ensure easy access of the workers and the beneficiaries from the catchment area, and to cover the surrounding area as well. The logistic risks management are the responsibility of the project management and the center management.
