Project Summary

The project will scale up ongoing protection activities recently concluded by AVSI in the Jamame and Badhadhe districts of Lower Juba region in order to develop and strengthen a community response mechanisms and respond to violations through case management for survivors of Gender-based violence (GBV), together with supporting vulnerable people (elderly and people with disabilities) affected by forced displacement and evictions. The main focus of the project will be to provide quality and timely assistance in hard-to-reach areas of Lower Juba, such as the Jamaame and Badhaade districs, which see a very limited presence of humanitarian partners intervening in the areas. The project will target all members of the community, including women, men, girls, and boys, to improve their understanding of GBV and protection risks and increase community engagement in the prevention and mitigation of those risks. This will happen through the provision of an integrated response that will ensure access to support services to survivors, including direct life-saving support, legal support, and case management, awareness sessions for the communities, and provision of in-kind support for the improvement of life condition and reduction of exposure to threats. The inclusion of the whole community will be a key aspect of this program, as it will provide them with lifesaving information designed to prevent acts of violence against women and to empower them to respond to incidents in a way that takes into account the prejudice suffered by victims.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The distribution of cash instead of direct in-kind support is justified by the experience and knowledge of the context gathered so far in the targeted areas. AVSI has recorded improvement in beneficiary monthly income, expenditure on food, reduced debt which are both indications of improvement in the general wellbeing of the beneficiaries. The crisis affecting the targeted households is layered and multifaceted, with specific needs for each household. The provision of MPCA will allow each family to independently provide to their immediate needs.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

The project design kept in consideration the indications provided by the National Cash Working Group, adapting the amount to also the additional strain put on households by the current crisis situation. The design was also based on the evaluation of previous projects with a cash component, which provided guidance on best practices.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

As a mitigation measure, the criteria for the provision of material and cash assistance is based on analysis of risk with the target communities, and transparency for selection with IDPs camp leaders and village leaders. AVSI's approach of giving minimal and targeted assistance for those most at risk is based on do-no-harm principles. Additionally, AVSI will take advantage of the experience and knowledge of the field context to ensure that discretion and safety for all the activities is maintained, which will reduce exposure to threats and risks for both beneficiaries and project staff, particularly in hard-to-reach areas where AVSI worked already successfully delivering assistance to communities.
