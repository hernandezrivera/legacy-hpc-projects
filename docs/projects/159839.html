Project Summary

The project intends to provide life-saving and preventive nutrition services for 3440 children (1720 boys and 1720 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 1052 Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) through selective feeding and nutrition treatment and further increase evidence based BNSP services linked to Nutrition, Health, WASH, Food security and protection in the targeted locations in Galkayo South and Hobyo Districts Mudug Region Somalia. Malnutrition rates among children aged under 5 and PLWs is very high in Galkayo IDP camps with GAM rates at 20.2% (FSNAU/FEWSNET post Gu assessment, 2019 and Joint Multi-Cluster Needs Assessment, 2019). The situation is attributed to poor breastfeeding pattern among lactating women and poor diet diversity and complementary feeding as well as limited access to nutrition and health services. The above plight calls for emergency Nutrition services through treatment of acute and moderate malnutrition and preventive therapeutic nutrition services in the project targeted locations Galkayo South and Hobyo Districts. Mudug Region Somalia.

Project Activities

1. Screening of 3440 children (1720 boys and 1720 girls) aged 6-59 months and 1052 PLWs and treated through OTP/SFP program 2. Promotion of optimum targeting 1052 PLWs in project locations in Galkayo South and Hobyo District3. Prevention and management of common diseases (anemia, diarrhea, malaria etc) through Vitamin A supplementation, deworming,multiple micro nutrients, supplements, iron/folate supplements and dissemination of hygiene promotion messages and practices to the targeted 3440 children (1720 boys and 1720 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 1052 Pregnant Lactating Women 4. Training of 40 OTP/SFP staff (20 men and 20 women) as well as volunteers in case detection through MUAC measurements and weight measures 5. Training of 60 Community health workers (30 female and 30 male) on nutrition education and to enhance their knowledge in Malnutrition to mitigate increasing malnutrition cases among children aged 6-59 months and PLW 6. Conducting 12 Community education sessions for 60 community nutrition volunteers (60 female and 60 male) to increase awareness on malnutrition
