Project Summary

-12-month project
-Goal: to improve IDPs, returnees and host communities’ socio-economic conditions and resilience in the Mayo Tsanaga division.
-Objectives: (1) participants have improved capacity for agricultural production and (2) participants have restored and protected their livelihoods.
-Target: 29,800 people in 4,000 HHs, including 25% vulnerable host community members, in the four most affected municipalities in the Mayo Tsanaga Division - Koza, Mokolo, Mogode, and Mozogo.
-4,000 new HHs (29,800 individuals) to receive cash, vouchers, agricultural training and financial literacy training to support their livelihoods

Needs

-Boko Haram forced more than 236,000 Cameroonians to abandon their homes, villages, and means of subsistence to settle in safer areas.-IDPs’ livelihoods have transitioned from small-scale trade, agriculture and livestock prior to displacement to unsustainable activities such as collecting firewood and daily and temporary labor.-Many displaced people lack agricultural inputs, like certified seeds and farming tools.-Food security is low among IDPs, due to the disruption of the livelihood and agricultural activities.
