Project Summary

Sudan is one of the biggest countries in Africa with 18 State, and it contains many wealth such as minerals, agricultural, and animal wealth, but the country faces major contests in her quest for maintainable peace and development. Sudan has been through tough times since the armed conflicts that occurred in Blue Nile, South Kordofan, West Kordofan, Darfur region, …etc. and there were many peace agreements has been signed by Sudan government, but still there were conflicts going on in the country, and the last conflict was The outbreak of heavy fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) on Saturday April 15 in Khartoum and in other locations throughout the country, in particular in North and South Darfur comes during a proposed transition to civilian rule. As of April 21, over 400 people have been killed, and more than 3,700 injured, according to the World Health Organization. The ongoing fighting is exacerbating an already difficult humanitarian situation as well the already existing discrimination and violence against women and girls. Incidences of sexual and gender-based violence are already being reported. The UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) estimates over 15.8 million people in need of humanitarian assistance. The humanitarian activities have been interrupted in many states due to widespread insecurity and looting. In South Darfur in particular the armed conflict resulted in destruction of public facilities, looting of proprieties, losing of lives and exposed to violence, also the lack of access to life saving services such as healthcare, GBV services, daily living requirements for families like groceries and essential needs. Across Sudan, GBV continues to be a life-threatening concern, worsened by a variety of factors including armed conflict, tribal clashes, forced displacement, and periodic disasters such as floods, therefore The project aims to ensure that vulnerable women and girls, especially those survived from the armed conflict and have free and safe access to life-saving services during and after the conflicts, this will be achieved through provision of emergency gender-based violence humanitarian response services and activities targeting 15,000 people.
Rural Development Organization will contribute to the three GBV Sub -Sector objectives:
Provide life- saving specialized quality GBV services, including CMR, case management and psychosocial support, and reinforce referral pathways. (With a focus on women and girls with disabilities and adolescent girls) (Linked to HRP Strategic Objective 1), through the provision of different services to vulnerable women & girls including GBV survivors (Case management, PSS, referrals), assessments, community awareness-raising, and programming, provision of dignity kits, and capacity building of GBV frontline actors.
To enhance strategies for community resilience through empowerment of women and girls and strengthened community-based interventions (Linked to HRP Strategic Objective 2), by supporting women centers, community protection networks, and training of women & girls on vocational skills
To promote GBV risk mitigation into all aspects of the humanitarian response (Linked to HRP Strategic Objective 3) by training on GBV for Non GBV actors and to strengthen GBV coordination mechanism at national and sub-national level and strengthen data collection and information management

1 - Needs

Rural Development Organization as one of the NGOs working in humanitarian field with its huge experience and knowledge in gender-based violence responses will seek to address the gaps and needs in GBV at South Darfur State, especially after and during the armed conflict between RSF and SAF which resulted in the situation evolving as reports of shootings and clashes are amplified across South Darfur daily. The Humanitarian spaces and government premises, hospitals, universities, Organizations (UN agencies, INGOs, NGOs), and private businesses have been subjected to looting, severely impacting the delivery of life-saving humanitarian aid to vulnerable people. Thousands of families have been displaced thus far, and the situation remains unpredictable, for resilience. Whereas the situation is still unclear, reports from the ground show that from the onset of the fighting, women & girls are the most affected people in the ongoing clashes, and they have been subjected to different types of violence. Such as sexual violence and trauma created through war, the assessments that done by some individuals NGOs Organizations and groups of NGOs in south Darfur showed the urgent needs of GBV to be provide for GBV survivors and vulnerable women and girls, moreover the destruction of public facilities and necessary service systems resulted in lack of access to life saving services such as healthcare, GBV services, daily living requirements for families like groceries and essential needs.According to the assessments and field visits that conducted, by Rural Development Organization to some areas there was an observation that there were key GBV findings, The most affected are children and women (separation, trauma, social destruction, abuse, exploitation, violations, disabilities), in addition to deterioration of psychological health of women and girl. Also the closure of health facilities and private hospitals caused some risks for women and girls those have chronic illness, moreover those whom deliver babies, they faced a challenge to have access to health facilities, in addition to that there was internal displacement for some families from Hai Elwadi Area to Karrari area and other localities, the most challengeable factors that face the humanitarian actors to deliver services during the clashes, was the looting of their stocks , offices beside the lack of resources caused an enormous gap in GBV, therefore RDO is seeking through this project proposal to provide some important services, in order to reduce and mitigate the GBV Gap in South Darfur State. In addition to the conflict Sudan has one of the highest prevalence in harmful practices globally. Adolescent and young girls are particularly affected by early marriage and other forms of harmful practices. Among girls aged 20-24 years, 60.2 % were first married or in union before the age of 18, and 87% of women aged 15–49 years have been subjected to Female Genital Mutilation (FGM) (UNICEF, S3MⅡ 2018), Violence against men and boys goes underreported, key barriers include the belief of it being a sign of weakness and lack of masculinity, all these factors and more made RDO to take the responsibility of proposing a Project to reduce Violence against women and girls.

The project will support the progress of self-protection capacities and aim at improving beneficiaries' daily lives through improved formal and informal protection mechanisms & SOPs for GBV and protection. This is considered as the most effective way of tackling gender inequality as the root cause of GBV.
