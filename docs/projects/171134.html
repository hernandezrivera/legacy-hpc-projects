Project Summary

HINNA women organization is proposing to provide protection of quality gender-based violence (GBV) through the provision of quality gender-based violence and prevention mechanism of Multi-sectoral response services which will bring positive impact to the GBV survivors in Daynile, Kahda districts of Benadir region and Afgoi and Merka of L/Shabelle region, Baidoa districts Bay region, This project intends to strengthen the protection of GBV services in the two regions especially where gender violations are rising and the response of quality GBV services is limited.
Through loyalty targeting to the right survivors in the project targeting areas, HINNA has set a number of targeting GBV survivors of 23,336 individuals estimated to be 3,890 HHs recorded from the Protection Cluster total GBV PiN, included persons, Afgoi, Merka districts of lower Shabelle and Baidoa district of Bay regions, out of the 23,336 targeting persons 15% - 3,500 are women, 15% - 3,500 are men 66% are children among them (33% (7,701) of the children are girls and the rest of the 33% (7701) are boys), while 4% are elderly persons (933), overall these individuals, they compose of 60% of are IDPs (14,002), 40% (9334) are non IDPs. Out of those total targeting households, 3500 are disabled persons, as this project is mainly considering the disability inclusion during all the project phases from the assessment, designing, planning, implementation, and monitoring stages as to keep transparency and accountability to the disabled people.
The overall objective of this project is to strengthening access to quality GBV services to the GBV survivors and poor vulnerable households as well as decrease Gender-Based Violence harassments and protection risks through community education, empowerment and access to legal and justice services, medical aid, and improving human dignity as well as community basic services through increasing the knowledge of GBV Mainstreaming in Banadir and L/Shabelle region.
According to HINNA’s implementation capacity and knowledge of GBV services in that last 7 years with SHF and other UN agencies including UNFPA, HINNA will strengthen its work in improving the
