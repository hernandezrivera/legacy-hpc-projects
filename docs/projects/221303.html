Project Summary

Following the concentration of children in schools within the divisional headquarters of the Northwest Region (National Institute of Statistics 2022 edition) as a result of the ongoing crisis in the Northwest Region, there has been a lot of open defecation around school premises due to the poor state of latrines in most of these schools.
Furthermore, with the ongoing needs assessment exercise in schools, health facilities and communities of the Northwest Region that is being carried out by some members of the WASH cluster, there is an urgent need to ameliorate the sanitation situation in most of these schools because the conditions of some of these school latrines are very deplorable.
As a result of this situation, many streams from where the local inhabitants fetch drinking water are becoming contaminated due to runoffs from rainwater.
In the light of the health dangers that are posed by this situation, CAFEDO through this project seeks to construct 10 gender friendly latrines of 4 cubicles each (5 in Mbengwi and 5 in Bafut subdivisions). Secondly, the project will construct/rehabilitates 3 water sources and extend water supply from the communities to these schools so that these school children will be protected from stomach and water borne related diseases (typhoid, diarrhea, and dysentery / cholera outbreak)

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Objective 1: To ameliorate the hygiene and sanitation condition of children through their educational establishments. It is believed that with the construction of these gender sensitive latrines fitted with hand washing facilities and access ramps for children with disabilities and with the extension of safe drinking water from communities to these educational institutions, school attendance will obviously be regular while enrollments will certainly be on a steady increasing trend. This is because the number of children who will stay away from school due to ill health will be reduced since a majority of this stomach and water related disease will also be reduced. Furthermore, this will also encourage parents who have children with disabilities to enroll them into schools hence increasing the literacy rate for children which are their right to education. Objective 2: To establish and train 10 latrine management committees. These latrine management committees will be created in all the 10 schools where these latrines will be constructed. They will be provided with a maintenance toolbox each and some basic maintenance accessories. These committees will have dual functions because they will also serve as channels for complains and feedback in their various communities who will constantly communicate with the organization on the welfare of these latrine. It should be noted that this committees will comprise of 5 persons (3 women and 2 men) from each school administration and their local communities. The project seeks to empower and incorporate more women because men are usually targeted more than women in a crisis situation and so even if the men are displaced, these women should still be able to maintain and keep these latrines functional.
