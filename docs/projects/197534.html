Project Summary

Cameroon has about 2.9 million people who are food insecured and North West and South West Region alone has more than 950,000 crisis-affected people suffering from food insecurity. 775, 696 people in the North West Region are in the third level of food insecurity (March 2022 cadre harmonise). The deteriorating food access and incomes has exposed poor households, IDPs and returnees to Crisis (IPCPhase3). This has equally affected the nutrition status of the families. Malnutrition is a critical risk in the North West Region and access to safe and nutritive food remains a challenge for vulnerable household, pregnant / lactating women and children.
INTERVENTION
- The project intends to improve on the livelihood of 500 vulnerable women IDPs and vulnerable host families in Mezam, Menchum and Ngohketunjia in the Northwest regions through training and support with farm inputs and startup materials (crop seeds, manure, vegetable seeds, goats or chicks)
A rapid needs assessment conducted by SHUMAS in these localities indicates that the priority needs are farm inputs, rearing of goats and fowls , which has a good market with the increase in prices of cow meat. If each farmer is provided with farm inputs such as maize, beans and vegetable seeds and 25 chicks or a female mature goat, each of them will make an average income of 50,000frs from livestock and 35000 from vegetable gardening
THE MAIN OBJECTIVE
Improve the resilience of 500 vulnerable crisis affected persons (70% women) in Menchum, Ngoketunjia and Mezam in the North West Region
Specific objectives
a) Enhance the productive capacity of 500 vulnerable IDPs and host families (300 women and 200 men) on poultry or goat farming, backyard gardening, crop farming, basic management and marketing skills.
b) Improve the mental health and self esteem of the 500 vulnerable IDPs to enable them full focus on their farms
c) Support them with promotional materials consisting of 25 three weeks chicks or a female goat - 3 bags of feed, - 3 sachets of vegetables seeds, crop seeds (maize and beans ) 2 feeder, 2 drinkers, 1 bag of manure, a hoe and a wheelbarrow.
ACTIVITIES
1) Organize a preplanning meeting with all project stakeholders to ensure everyone is on the same page
2) Carry out vulnerability registration of IDPs
3) Purchasing of training materials
4) Organize and run two days training session in a safe an easy to access location especially for those with disabilities
5) Purchase promotional materials - Distribute 25 three weeks chicks or goat - 3 bags of feed, - 3 sachets of vegetables seeds, 2 feeder,2 drinkers, 1 bag of manure, a hoe, a wheelbarrow, maize seeds , and beans to each farmer
6) Distribute the materials
7) Provide psychosocial support to 500 affected IDPs especially GBV survivors for a period of 12 months in 4 phases
8) Put in place strong outcome tracking system and backfilling when and where necessary - Carryout monthly follow-up trips to ensure all the beneficiaries are successfully implementing their activities
9) Write progress reports monthly and final reports We expect that this project will provide the following results; Outcome The beneficiaries improve their level of economic sufficiency reducing their vulnerability and enabling them to meet their daily needs The 1000 beneficiaries become more confident and feel protected and safe to carry on with daily activities and integrate themselves in the society Improvement in health and nutrition.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The persistent socio-political crisis in the North West and South West Region of Cameroon that started in 2016, has led to dreadful consequences. 2.2 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance, 598000people internally displaced, 417,500 returnees and 79,500 seeking refuge in neighboring Nigeria (OCHA sitrep of September 2022). With the heightened insecurity, property has been destroyed, livelihood lost, social structures collapsed, markets are non-functional, farm to market roads dilapidated, potable water systems collapsed, etc. A greater percentage of the labour force is currently unemployed in the restive areas as the population is often forced multiple times to leave their homes. Humanitarian needs, such as protection, shelter, Water Hygiene and Sanitation (WASH), Education, Food and Non-Food items are on the increase. Food security has deteriorated in the North-West and South-West regions under the weight of the recurring violence, insecurity, frequent lockdowns and ghost towns. Farmers have fled from their villages into bushes, forest and a large number made it to nearby cities. Those who return or stayed back have limited or no access to their farms as some bridges have been cut off, roads dilapidated due to no maintenance. The ongoing war in Ukraine has made the situation worst as prices of main food commodities such as oil, wheat flour, and rice has drastically increased. A litre of oil that was sold at 1000frs is now 2000frs etc. Transportation cost in the NWSW increased with the crisis due to multiple check points . Prices of farm inputs have tripled in less than a year and worst still the crop seeds used have lost it’s vitality. This has placed crisis affected persons in a precarious situation.
