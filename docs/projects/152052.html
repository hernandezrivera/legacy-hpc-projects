Needs

In North East Nigeria, precisely in Magumeri and Nganzai LGAs in Borno State, The impact of the conflict in these communities has been enormous with lost lives and destroyed physical, social, government infrastructure and civil authority which has led to a drastic increases in gender base violence incidences including rape and other form of abuse as well as problem livelihood opportunities for survivor of SGBV. Previously marginalized groups have become even more vulnerable due severer damage to their Establishments including Hospital and shelter which makes women and children to be more prone to GBV and SGBV as a result of the arm conflict by the Boko Harm group which is adding problem to the already experienced under-development in these LGAs. According to GBV HNO 2019 severity ranking in which Magumeri and Nganzai LGA Ranks 7 and 11 respectively as this indicate that an urgent GBV prevention and response measures should be put place at such location to prevent secondary and future displacement. According to the same source, it is estimated that 72,905 people are in need of GBV protection services in the said LGAs. As relief activities start to scale back to the areas, the focus must now be on providing the most vulnerable stigmatized survice of SGBV and GBV with short-term livelihood to boost income so survivor no longer need to rely on emergency aid in a way that supports longer-term intervention. This 12 months project seek in bridge the gap in the prevention and response to violence against survivor to sensitization, awareness and capacity building of traditional and religious rulers as well as interrogated case management for the survivor of SGBV and GBV in this context for a proper well coordinated response and prevention.
