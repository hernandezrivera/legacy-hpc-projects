Project Summary

Need Assesment:IhsanRD conducted a needs assessment to identify the protection concern The assessment aims to understand demographic dynamics, highlight special needs and gender nuances,, , analyze community engagement, and examine children's and other protection challenges. The following outlines the interconnected findings and the critical gaps that demand intervention.
- Child Well-being and Protection:
Child well-being and protection present significant challenges, with instances of separation from parents, unaccompanied living situations, child abuse, exploitation, violence, and neglect. Education and child labor intertwine, with 20% of children experiencing school dropout and 10% facing health problems, indicating a pressing need for improved healthcare services.
GBV: GBV has been a pervasive feature of the conflict in NWS since it began in 2011. Sexual violence, including conflict-related sexual violence, has been regularly reported. Other forms of GBV in NWS include targeted physical, psychological, and emotional abuse, family and intimate partner violence, child and forced marriages, femicide, and “honor” crimes.
The project aims to provide protection, HLP, legal, child protection and GBV prevention and response services to the most vulnerable MWGB through community-based interventions and protection emergency response mobile teams in Northern Syria (North Aleppo) and Northwest Syria The project will cover WGSSs, CFSs, widows camps, support WLOs ,community centers ,Mobile teams, legal clinics (most of those teams are emergency response protection mobile teams to response for the new displacement movement and to conduct the assessment needed to respond for those IDPs).
IhsanRD's Protection team aims to enhance social cohesion, peaceful coexistence, and the rule of law. The project demonstrates a continuation of the Community Center and outreach services, including legal, HLP, transitional justice services, and awareness sessions on HLP safeguarding. The intervention includes developing and delivering curriculums on social cohesion and peaceful coexistence. Surveys and assessments will identify contaminated areas with war remnants in communities.
Community Protection Networks will be established, , playing a pivotal role in implementing initiatives for peaceful coexistence, ensuring the inclusion of returnees and Persons with Disabilities (PwDs). Capacity building and in-kind support will extend to Community-Led Organizations (CLOs), including WLO and YLOs.
Legal protection interventions, involving training on the rules of law and transitional justice. Awareness sessions on the importance of Housing, Land, and Property documentation safeguarding, counselling sessions, and the establishment of legal support networks (LSNs).
Specialized child protection services will be provided, focusing on child survivors of trafficking, child marriage, child labor, (UASC) children and other forms of abuse and exploitation. IhsanRD will support capacity building on child-friendly procedures and processes in judicial systems.
IhsanRD will continue to enhance and broaden its services by upholding existing WGSS in NWS. the services will encompass comprehensive GBV case management, PSS, empowerment activities, and recreational services alongside the AMAL initiative,and IGA. These will include tailored support groups and specialized PSS and life skills training designed for girls, addressing their unique needs and challenges
To ensure accessibility and inclusivity, IhsanRD will enhance its referral system for women and girls. Outreach initiatives will specifically target communities less likely to access services directly at the WGSS, aiming to extend our reach and impact.
MA: IhsanRD aim to target EORE implementation and reaching the target of 5000 people in EORE with coordination with MA SC starting from June 2024 by provide capacity building to the staff

0 - Project Summary (extended)

In order to combat sexual exploitation and abuse by humanitarian workers, Ihsan Organization has implemented a stringent strategy. This strategy emphasizes the absolute prohibition of any form of sexual activity with children, Furthermore, the organization strictly prohibits the exchange of money, employment, goods, or services for sexual favors, as well as any sexual relationships that involve improper use of rank or position. Ihsan Organization is committed to maintaining an environment that prevents sexual exploitation and abuse, and it actively promotes the implementation of its code of conduct to uphold the integrity and credibility of humanitarian aid work.Risk: When referring to a case, the survivor may be exposed to a new higher risk, which may be related to the referred service location, inept staff, or service providers' failure to handle the survivor. Mitigation: IhsanRD team will ensure to keep a high level of coordination with the service providers before referring the cases, along with raising the provider's awareness on how to deal with survivors' cases.Moreover, secure transportation means accompanying the referred case to the location of the service provided.- Communities are not encouraged to participate in community-based protection activities due to the social sensitivity for sharing in these activities.Mitigation: Ensuring the strong integration of the local council and CPNs in their support to encourage community members to participate in community-based protection activities. .- Lack of experienced specialists in the field of social cohesion, peace coexistence in the targeted sub-districts.Mitigation: Job vacancy advertisements will be shared with the local councils and the surrounding areas. - Women are not encouraged to participate in community-based protection activities due to the social sensitivity for sharing the women in these activities.Mitigation: Ensuring the strong integration of the local council in their support to encourage women to participate in the community-based protection activities. Also, IhsanRD will ensure that the protection staff will include female to conduct activities for the women participating in this intervention. The group activity will be implemented by separating men and women during sessions and delivered by female staff.- Local authorities Non-cooperation: Presuming local authorities may be resistant or unwilling to collaborate in community-based protection activities, showing reluctance in reporting cases and hesitancy to support community-based protection activities.Mitigation: Implementing strategies to foster local authorities' cooperation in protection interventions, such as building trust through transparent communication and involving community leaders. A vital role for the CPNs and LSNs in fostering trust and establishing channels of open communication with the local authorities
