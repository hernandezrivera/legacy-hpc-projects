Project Summary

The project aims to provide agricultural assistance to displaced populations living in 3 relocation centers,. in Ancuabe district in the villages of Nanjua A and B, and Nangume for 8,000 beneficiaries, and Chiure district in the village of Meculane for 4,500 people, totaling 12,500 direct beneficiaries, through the distribution of agricultural instruments and inputs, with the objective of revitalizing their agricultural production ensuring food self-sufficient for consumption; thus improving the food security situation.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project proposes to support displaced families in providing livelihoods that are found in the district of Ancuabe, in the villages of Nanjua A and B, in the administrative post of Mesa; and in Nangume at the Ancuabe administrative post, and in the Chiure district in the Meculane village at the Chiure headquarters administrative post, where families seek refuge because they are considered safe.Through humanitarian intervention, we intend to reduce the vulnerability of displaced families with actions and activities aimed at immediate support, with the allocation of agricultural fields for the production of essential crops and income generation. KULIMA will distribute agricultural seeds (Orange-fleshed sweet potato seed; corn; rice; cowpea and pigeon peas; millet, sorghum; and cassava cuttings), and agricultural production instruments (Hoes, Knives, Katana, watering cans , and rake) and the respective organic fertilizers for soil enrichment respecting the lines of Conservation Agriculture. The target group of the project includes all displaced families, from Mocímboa da Praia, Macomia, Quissanga, Ibo, Meluco, Muidumbe, Moeda, Palma and Nangade, which are being installed in the villages of Ancuabe and Chiure district, mainly elderly people, people with disabilities , women and girls considered more vulnerable, single mothers and unaccompanied girls and heads of household, totaling approximately 12500 people.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

To achieve good results, KULIMA will develop the following activities: -Distribution of agricultural inputs (seeds for 1 season (dry season) and field materials):This process will be carried out periodically throughout the year, in collaboration with the responsible leaders of the two districts, where the beneficiaries will be selected based on the extreme needing, and through this scale they will be supported for at least two daily meals and provide necessary goods such as seeds, fillets, material for production fields, thus allowing food stability for their families. The beneficiaries left their agricultural land. So in coordination with the district economic activity services (SDAE) and district planning and infrastructure services (SDPI) at the district level of Ancuabe and Chiure and as local authorities will be defined the land to be cultivated for the benefit of the Displaced. -Training beneficiaries in techniques for:(i) Management of agricultural crops;(ii) (ii) Use of attractive plants;(iii) (iii) Consortium with other species, among other techniques.(iv) Pest management, post-harvest management, storage and processing of agricultural products. -Rehabilitation of infrastructure related to agriculture, including irrigation system (pump, piping and sprinklers), post-harvest structures and markets.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

For a suffering population, that left everything and seeks shelter, that has even witnessed the brutality of those closest to it, the project will seek to identify specific cases that require more personalized intervention and will follow up them. Mechanisms will be created for participants to live their lives in the future without third party intervention or assistance. The project will focus on providing food assistance to displaced populations. The target group will be involved through a participatory and even approach that will make it possible to establish direct contact with the community.The identification of the target group will be done through a baseline study, that will facilitate their involvement, which will have a very important role in the design of assessment instruments for the participation mechanism, with a view to achieving significant community participation in preparation activities, reducing the risk of being excluded and neglected in the humanitarian response and consequently being disproportionately affected by the impacts of the crises. Training of personnel in matters of prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse, code of ethics and professional conduct in the field in order to avoid attitudes

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The success of any activity requires the involvement of the main beneficiaries. Thus, starting from the consultation with possible beneficiaries, their needs were identified and possible solutions were studied. Interest groups (focal points) will be formed to connect with the project's technical team. These groups will be responsible for collecting the information if they are good or not about the project intervention. In addition to the mechanisms mentioned above, complaint boxes will be created in the project areas. It is known that there are complaints boxes in state institutions, but they are rarely, if not, never opened. In the case of this project, there will be a team with the responsibility to regularly watch over these instruments. The project will always seek, directly or indirectly, to collect the level of satisfaction of the beneficiaries.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

This target group will be involved through a participatory and even approach that will make it possible to establish direct contact with people with disabilities through focal points that will be identified in the target group, made through a baseline study that will facilitate their involvement, with a community representative.These focal points will play an important role in the design of instruments for evaluating the participation mechanism, with a view to achieving a meaningful participation of people with disabilities in the activities of distributing agricultural inputs and agricultural production instruments, as well as training, reducing the risk of exclusion in the country. the implementation of the project.Inclusion of people with disabilities will improve results for a diverse group of people and homogenize groups and overcome barriers to participation: flow of adhesion of the target audience to the proposed program, Attitudinal Barriers ignorance (ignoring the potential of people with disabilities) Environmental Barriers: physical obstacles in the natural environment and inaccessible communication systems.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The issue of respect for the environment is the issue currently being discussed. In the intervention of this project we will try to respect this principle by raising awareness of the correct use of natural resources that avoid damaging the environment. In agricultural activities, we will encourage the use of methods that are not harmful to the environment by introducing organic means of production to enrich agricultural soil. We will encourage respect for the sanitation of the environment, because in addition to preserving the environment it also protects the contamination of various diseases such as diarrhea, cholera and others
