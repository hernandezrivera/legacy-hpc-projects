Project Summary

Ensuring access to basic WASH services to the affected population in Beirut, including a comprehensive rehabilitation and upgrade of the affected water system in Beirut from sources to beneficiaries, reconnection of almost 500 buildings from the lightly, moderately & severely affected buildings and institutions the to WASH services and networks. The intervention will include support to 1600 HHs through a Cash for WASH program to rehabilitate the plumbing systems and toilets inside the affected buildings. All activities will be associated with a Community Mobilization & capacity building components focusing on
Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) and IPC to key stakeholders, local associations, Government counterparts, and key community influencers to support the displaced and vulnerable population while focusing on strengthening the trust between affected customers and the Water Establishment of Beirut Mount Lebanon.

1 - Needs

On the 4th of August, Beirut, Lebanese Capital, witnessed a large blast at the city's port caused by the explosion of ammonium nitrate stored for more than 6 years.The blast had damaged the entire Beirut city. Buildings have been destroyed, hospitals were overwhelmed and around 200 people were killed, more than 5,000 injures and 300,000 displaced. The explosion comes at a very sensitive time for Lebanon, with a socio-economic crisis and COVID 19 outbreak. The explosion impact area reached 6km from the blast epicenter with what can be categorized as ‘severe damage’, 10km with ‘moderate’ damage, and up to 24km with ‘light’ damage. The preliminary assessment is done by the Water Establishment of Beirut Mount Lebanon with technical support from the WASH cluster focusing on the potential damages on the water network starting from the sources to beneficiaries showed that, despite the network being operational, the structural part of the network has suffered from the blast and the underground shakes from the explosions have affected the main reservoirs in Ashrafieh, Talet el Khayat & Bourj Abi Haidar. This comes at a very sensitive time where the water establishment is facing serious financial issues affecting the operation and maintenance in the pumping stations serving this area including the Hospitals, schools, and health care facilities. In-depth investigations on the network damages have started, and some significant damages have already been identified on the water pipelines close to the blast. In addition to the above, the urgent priority remains to ensure that all buildings still occupied and safe are provided with access to basic WASH services. So far UNICEF and the WASH Sector have assessed almost 3,500 buildings that have been affected and identified 125 occupied but without a water point and 135 disconnected from the wastewater network. More than 800 are unoccupied or the victims didn't accept to be visited. Some Hospitals and PHCs have been totally damaged by the blast, the assessment is being conducted by the health sector and MoPH in coordination with the World Health Organization. Under the leadership of the Health sector, UNICEF would respond to WaSH needs inside the operational hospitals and PHCs if needed, as already done in Karantina hospital. The Shelter cluster also needs WASH contribution to support the implementing cost of plumbing systems and toilets rehabilitation. All the above needs are during the COVID 19 outbreak with more than 600 daily cases, which add an additional burden on affected people without living in poor hygienic conditions, and health institutions that have reduced capacities

2 - Activities

UNICEF response activities will be divided into four levels: 1- Testing of the network and assessment of NRW includimg quick repair 2- Reconnection of 500 affected buildings to WASH basic services.UNICEF will ensure the provision of basic WASH needs where applicable in light, moderate, and severely affected buildings and institutions including DRM centers, Hospitals, PHCs & schools where services including water, sanitation, and electricity are not or maybe only partly accessible.The WaSH response activities will mainly include the reconnection of the Households to the network: • Supply, installation and rehabilitation of ground and roof water tanks per household with all necessary valves, accessories & fittings• Fixing of the connection to the network including but not limited to: pumps, risers, valves, ...• Fixing building connections to wastewater and stormwater networks• Water trucking & Desludging• Distribution of IPC/Disinfection kit, Hygiene kits, Elderly and Baby kits to vulnerable families. 3-Community Mobilization:•Include timely and consistent AAP mechanisms (Accountability to affected population) to ensure that the programme responds to people's needs.
