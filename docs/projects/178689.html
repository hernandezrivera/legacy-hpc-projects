Project Summary

The project aims at providing a comprehensive protection services to the vulnerable people in Damascus and 15 areas in Rural Damascus, project activities are tailored to support vulnerable groups (men, women, boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community) taking into consideration the inclusion of people with disabilities and variations in needs among all groups.
Project will be implemented in Damascus and Rural Damascus to provide services related to GBV prevention, Child protection, general protection and risk education
Project aims at establishing 12 new community centers in Rural Damascus, and supporting one existing center and 2 new centers in Damascus, and 3 existing centers in Rural Damascus. Activities in the existing centers will provide an added value and expansion to the services that are being implemented due to the high need and demand for protection services especially with the COVID-19 pandemic and the harsh living conditions, taking into consideration duplication avoidance. AlTamayoz has the capacity to train the needed staff to run the project in all selected locations ) currently AlTamayoz has 30% of required human recourses, other 30% are available but require trainings, and the 40% to be selected once project is launched, in addition, AlTamayoz has collected data from beneficiaries registered in AlTamayoz and are living in the selected locations who expressed the need for proposed services (child marriage reached 60 % of girls 14-17 years of age, 40% of children aged from 12-18 are engaged in child laborm about 60% of women are exposed to GBV.

5 - Implementation of activities

Activities in Damascus will be implemented in 3areas AlMidan in Southern Damascus where a density of IDPs and overburdened host community exist, Rukeneldin to support IDPs in Northern Damascus, and Dummar where a density of IDPs exists. This project will support the already exist community center in Almidan in order to expand its activities due to the heavy load of beneﬁciaries seeking for its services. And to establish 2 community centers in Dummar and Rukeneldin.In Rural Damascus, proposed project aims at establishing community centers (including women and girls safe spaces) in 12 locations (Ein Elfijeh, Madaya, Darayya, Sahnaya, Ghizlaniyyeh, Nashabiyeh, Arbin, Babella, Kafr Batna, Kisweh, Maliha, and Qudsiya) where severity scale 3,4 and 5, and to expand services in 4 existing centers in(Harasta, Duma, and Zabadani). Centers in all locations will act as a space where affected people especially women and girls will be able to express and exchange concerns and experiences and receive appropriate services, and where children can interact with peers and where specific needs of children are met Activities will be implemented in all mentioned areas with consideration of coordination with other humanitarian actors in order to provide a complementary services and avoid duplication. The proposed project mainstreams protection through considering the principles of Do No Harm during activities implementation. GBV issues will be taken into consideration through conducting awareness raising sessions to mainstream GBV aspects and GBV prevention.All staff members have to sign a code of conduct that includes GBVandPSEA related aspects. AlTamayoz has a well organized database system in each center for monitoring purpose

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: Financial risks related to increase in materials and services prices Likelihood: medium. impact: high this will be mitigated by considering this increase while preparing the proposal budget.Risk: lack of fuel needed for transportation and for generator, and the increase of its price.Likelihood: high. impact: highMitigating measure: provide centers with invertor and lid lamps, try to cooperate with local authorities to support our organization with fuelRisk: COVID-19 related restrictions and lockdowns Likelihood: High. impact: highMitigating measure: Applying the social distancing, providing centers with hygiene materials and supporting the staff with protection equipment (masks, hygiene hand gel, gloves,..), conducting activities through on line modality, and to enhance initiatives that support COVID prevention measuresRisk: lack of well qualiﬁed staff in the area of implementation due to the lack of education opportunities during the past years Likelihood: high. impact: low Mitigating measure: capacity building for some people from the implementation area.Risk: staff turn over Likelihood: high. impact: high Mitigating measure: capacity building for more than the required project staff to maintain a continuity of the core activities, Use technical resources to continuously train staff.Risk: lack of electricity and water in the Rural areas that might affect the implementation eﬃciency Likelihood: high. impact: medium Mitigating measure:support centers with generator or led lamp, support centers with tank for waterRisk of staff refusal to work in far areas from their homes Likelihood: high. impact: high Mitigating measure: support transportation for staff (rent a bus for staff )
