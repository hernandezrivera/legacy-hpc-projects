Project Summary

The project aims at improving the protection and reintegration of women survivors of violence, refugee
women and girls(RWG) female heads of households, and women and girls with disabilities(WGD) into society through improved access to essential multisectoral services Including psychosocial support, legal support, referral and GBV case management in alignment with international Human Rights standards.
The project geographical area will focus on Gaza Governorate particularly the refugee camps, east part of Gaza and North Gaza. The project will be implemented directly by CWLRCP. The project will work to achieve the key result: R1: Improved access of vulnerable women and girls to essential multisectoral services including GBV prevention and response ( The project will target 300 women of them 160 middle age,125 youth, 15 elderly age (from vulnerable background with severe poverty and social marginalization, Women survivors of violence (Hayat and CWLRCP beneficiaries)and Households' women victims of violence.)by providing multi sectorial services ( psychosocial support, legal assistance, sheltering services and economic
support ),raising awareness sessions will be provided for communities ( 1000 women and 250 men)on GBV, GBV prevention, and on the importance of seeking the protection services ,The Hayat centre will provide child visitation services for at least 20 families (divorced couples) and their children which entails biweekly visits to the centre to enable the parent
(can be the mother or the father) who does not have custody over the children to see them in a supportive child-friendly environment. The visitation services are coupled with counseling for parents on how to deal with the children to minimize the impact of divorce on the wellbeing and psychosocial status of the children. It also avails regular support
(mainly psychosocial and legal) by the social workers and lawyers to the divorced
spouses and their children.

Needs

GBV remains a significant problem in Gaza given the deterioration of the humanitarian situation in the last decade. In Addition, access to quality protection services has been affected by the blockade. CWLRCP witnessed an increase of the number of women and girls requesting the center's services by 60% during the first 6 month of 2018, the majority of women are asking for multi sectorial services (PSS, legal assistance, sheltering services, economic aid). The number has increased to 50 women per month comparing to 30 last year. The Increased demand for services as a result of current (electricity crisis, salary cuts, and Great Return of March) in addition to the closure/blockade on the Gaza Strip, resulted cases with physical, sexual and emotional and psychological violence such as domestic violence; sexual abuse. From 1/1/2018 to 30/8/218, CWLRCP and Hayat Project provided multi sectorial services ( psychosocial support and legal assistance) for 360 vulnerable women (Survivors of Violence) of them 213 youth, 138 middle age, 7 elderly age and 2 children, child visitation services support for 16 families.In addition to that 6 women provided with sheltering services .Whereas in 21/6/2018 CWLRCP succeeded in getting the final _ approval from the related authorities to open Hayat Center as the only NGOs multipurpose shelter in the Gaza Strip. CWLRCP conducted an accessibility audit as well as some accessibility work over the last 6 months.A minimum of 20 to 30 women and girls are reached by the centre monthly, however, the number of women and girls who are in need to be included is expected to be more than this. This action is part of the CWLRCP strategy in Gaza, expanding the zones of intervention. The action has been designed to accumulate on our previous experience and complementary expertise to address rights of women, girls and people with disabilities working with the target groups of CBOs, women, girls and activists etc. new trends of domestic violence cases were reported during the period of women who were physically abused by their addicted siblings,. An alarming issue of violence against women and young girls in Gaza was clearly highlighted following the awareness sessions implemented by CWLRCP about the Electronic violence. The awareness resulted in an unexpected number of complaints from young girls and women (below 25 years old) who were abused. It reached 20 cases in April & May while the awareness was running. Counseling services including social, psychological and legal for women and girls in camps is still needed as well as fighting stigma associated to it. As for the legal counseling, the demand is more as a result of the work of several legal clinics in the Gaza Strip and the increased number of awareness campaigns carried out by several actors, legal representation and reconciliation, couples counseling are demanded more by the community. CWLRCP has a 17 referral agreements with 17 different CSOs, professional multipurpose protection services that cannot be delivered by CWLRCP are referred to these CSOs whiles ensures the coherence of the project activities with the on going efforts of other key stakeholders engaged in addressing different levels of GBV prevention and response and promoting inclusion and justice for all.According to above, CWLRCP conducted Rapid needs assessment in the vulnerable area in the Gaza Strip from 07 to 10 August 2018 through 15 focus groups (3 FG/5 governorates) with complete participation of women, girls and men to identify their needs. the recommendation of needs assessment is to provide multi sectorial services The project will ensure safety and dignity of target groups, they will be empowered to fully participate and take the lead of parts of the project, it will ensure do no harm approach; more equitable to greater access to protection services, prevention of violence and greater inclusive participation, it will supports self-protection and assist people claim their rights.

Activities

Activity :1.1: Providing multi sectorial services ( psychosocial support, legal assistance) target 300 women including 160 middle age,125 youth, 15 elderly age (from vulnerable background with severe poverty and social marginalization and ignorance, refugee women and girls, and WGD, women headed household, WGBV. Of them 150 women will be provided psychosocial support and 150 women will be provided legal representation before the courts (resp.)-The case manager at Hayat center will conduct arapid case assessment to assess the needs of each case -The case manager will hold acase conference consisting of psychologist, social worker, legal advisor, etc.-The case conference will come up with the intervention plan for each case. The intervention will be carried out at the following levels: -Social intervention (social counseling, home visits, family interventions, and child visitation services) -Psychological intervention(psychological counseling, and individual and group psychological support(entertainmentopen days, psychodrama support, etc-Legal Intervention (legal consultations, legal mediation, and civil and Sharia court representation).-Progress of each case will be evaluated by virtue of a pre and post questionnaire of each case .Activity 1.2. CWLRCP will refer cases (at least 70) in need of professional multipurpose protection services as a result of case intervention plans to other service providers such as health, mental health,and child protection actors,etc Identified from among the community outreach targetgroups or who access at Hayat and CWLRCP headquarters (Respo.) Since CWLRCP has itSOPs and referral system and maintains 17 referral agreements with 17 CSOs,professional multipurpose protection services that cannot be delivered by CWLRCP are referred to these CSOs while CWLRCP will pays for service provision for each case.Activity 1.3: Provide sheltering services for 12 women (whose life at risk) and offer to them Rehabilitation services to integrate them into the community and recover from violence by conducting Craft training & other rehabilitation intervention plans.(Respo)Activity 1.4: Provide training and economic support to 20 women from vulnerable backgrounds to empower them economically (remedial) as following:-Conduct 50 training hours for 20 women of successful cases from vulnerable backgrounds on the following subjects: Gender, GBV, and women's rights. -Providing small project management training, and then they prepared their own business plans. (Remed.)-Provision of income generating opportunities (also through the business food incubator), including establishing and/or supporting existing small businesses for 8 WSV. Selection criteria for candidates:. (WVV) with severe poverty and social marginalization and Ignorance.WVV have leadership skills, self confidence and ability overcome the Challenges they may face. Having feasible proposed businesses or having innovative Business in harmony with market needs in marginalized areas.Motivated women with good skills.Activity 1.5: provide dignity kit for project's beneficiaries who benefited from multi sectorial services totally 100 women (from vulnerable background with severe poverty and social marginalization and Ignorance.(Remed)Activity 1.6: The Hayat center will provide child visitation services for at least 20 families (divorced couples) and their children which entails biweekly visits to the center to enable the parent (can be the mother or the father) who does not have custody over the children to see them in a supportive child-friendly environment. The visitation services are coupled with counseling for parents on how to deal with the children to minimize the impact of divorce on the wellbeing and psychosocial status of the children.Activity 1.7 Carry out 50 awareness-raising workshops for 1000 RWG, women impacted GMR and WGD. and 250 men .The workshops aim at increasing the awareness on VAWG,the importance of seeking protection services
