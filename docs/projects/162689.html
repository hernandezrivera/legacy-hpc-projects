Project Summary

The project of multi-sector assistance for Chadian refugees in Central Darfur State, where the duration of project will cover 1 year. The objective of the project is to assist the newly and old Chadian refugees who’s shelters need replenishment. The project will target chadian refugees in the Azum and Zalingi localities in Central Darfur State. SORR will assist the refugees mainly chadian with multI-sector project focusing on livelihood & resilience, shelter/NFI, energy & environment and Protection. The multi-sector project will train the Chadian refugees on self-resilience & income generating activities, protection & maintenance of environment & energy usage, rehabilitation & constructions of shelter & NFI distribution and protection services that includes GBV, child protection and general protection. SORR will coordinate with community leaders, COR, UNHCR, Line ministry and other stakeholders for the project start up. Currently there are limited or only few humanitarian organization working with Chadian refugees in Azum and Zalingi. SORR will coordinate the implementation of the multi-sector project.

1 - Needs

SORR will target mostly newly and old refugees who’s shelters need replenishment, further more host communities who have been identified lately as returnees in umshalaya village,SORR will only intervene in targeted refugee sites where other sector partners have been unable to respond, in particular as a result of insufficient funding.This project is tending to target refugees in umshalaya especially the new arrivals as indicated for the last verification on September 2019-2020 RRP. SORR will prioritize refugees who need replenishment for the old shelters and provide new shelters for the new arrivals to ensure an inclusive and adequate shelter is put in place. Furthermore the refugees and host communities will be given Livelihood activities like skills in vocational training and be provided with business startup kits, meanwhile the host communities will be provided with enough tools and Agricultural inputs since they are back in their home lands in additional skills on how to maintain and upgrade there inputs for sustainability and improvement of conditions, this will eventually avoid Idleness which may lead or mitigate any risks

2 - Activities / Outputs

SORR will conduct the following activities: 1-Training of Community Based Protection Net Work (CBPNW) committee on how to facilitate Sessions on protection related issues. Under this activity there will information sharing among the actors on protection related activities and conduct 4 awareness sessions on mapped basic services on CMR, PSS, general protection, GBV and child protection among and beyond the beneficiaries. 2-Creation/reactivation of community base women groups comprising of through FGD with community leaders, women, adolescent girls and minorities where applicable to look into women concern and training of the community base women groups on community-based protection, sexual gender based violence since umshalaya village comprises of host communities . 3-Community capacity building To enhance community resilience and allow people to develop their activities, this activity plans to create saving groups in the targeted area. A saving group is a self-managed group composed of 15 to 25 members implementing different kinds of business who save money together. The money saved by the group can be used either to grant loan to one or several members, lent as credit to earn additional income, saved in case of emergency situations or invested to develop the members’ businesses. 4- A special training will be provided to each group to allow the members to use small personal incomes to finance more important purchase and to allow the group to grant a loan to one of the members. This training will be conducted by an external consultant with expertise on the matter. Saving groups members will be trained on: - group organization - funds management - credit, loan and repayment - accountability These are the main training topics, However the detailed modalities of the training will be elaborated by the selected consultant. SORR will implement a close and regular follow-up of the saving groups after the training in order to identify any obstacle or difficulty saving groups members’ could face and to help them finding appropriate solutions. The members will be encouraged to organize regular meetings and discuss the management of the funds, potential loans and investments.. 5- stablishing 10 Women and Youth Livelihoods Centers for income Generating Activities and vocational skills development, trainings including provision of startup kits for 150 women and 150 youth. Since there are no vocational training centers in the camp, lecturers from Zalingei will be engaged to train youths and women on these skills. Soon after training they will be given start-up kits .6- Conduct IGA training on grass mats and fuel efficient stoves making for 320 vulnerable women (widows, poor lactating mothers, unaccompanied old women,headed household)). The participants are part of of 2000 targeted beneficiary for NFI kit distribution. The training will be for 10 days for both grass mat and fuel efficient stoves making. Each participant will receive start-up kits, so that they can immediately produce grass mats and stove after completed the training. The participants will be encouraged to conduct the same training to other women. SORR will establish a training center with local material (5X8 m) to help making the training more convenience for the participants. The centre will be handed over to the women groups once the project is completed hence there will be training on ASCAs( accumulating savings and credit Assossiation groups).7- The objective of this activity is to provide critical emergency shelter and NFI assistance to 320 recently new arrivals HHs. All 320 HHs 1600 person will receive emergency shelters which include plastic sheets (1 per HH), jerry-cans (1 per HH) kitchen sets (1 per HH), blankets (2 per HH), 800 sleeping mats (2 per HH), 8-Targeted beneficiaries will be trained on how to construct or assemble the emergency shelter.
