1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Strengthening crisis response in the fragile province of Cabo Delgado by providing transitional life-sustaining assistance and stabilizing structures to ensure sustainable livelihoods by improving food security, resilience, and equity among 7.000 IDPs and host communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Climate compatible cultivation methods / agricultural inputs in Montepuez and Metuge;2. Small livestock in Montepuez and Metuge;3. Four community-based tilapia aquaculture ponds in Metuge;4. Improved skills for access to economic activities in Montepuez and Metuge;5. Local early warning mechanism linked to state systems in Montepuez and Metuge;6. Resilience against food insecurity in Montepuez and Metuge;7. Reforestation and natural resource management in Montepuez and Metuge;8. Land Use and Land Title in Montepuez and Metuge;9. Reducing the prevalence of gender-based violence in Montepuez and Metuge;10. Mediation Committees in Montepuez and Metuge;11. Dialog between religious and ethnic groups in Montepuez and Metuge.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project has identified different risks (high, medium and low) that can affect the implementation as well as the dignity of the beneficiaries, and one of the greatest risks is the issue of Gender-Based Violence and Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. In order to mitigate this risk, the project has 3 Protection Officers and 10 locally trained activists who will help the population to disseminate protection messages.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The main aim of the project is to train internally displaced families and host communities in agricultural and other techniques, so that they have independence and are able to work independently.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The Project will regist all the beneficiares including people with desabilities. The project will work with the local government and community leaders in order to identify people with desabilities in communities to be registed in the project, specialy in the inconditional assistence. The project will also contract fiel protection officers to guarantee that all the rights of this group of people are considered.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project did an environmental study in the begining in order to identify potencial impacts that will have to the environment and were noted that all the activities that the project will implement will not damage the environment; on the contrary, some are aimed at improving it, such as reforestation activities, efficient fires and other activities.
