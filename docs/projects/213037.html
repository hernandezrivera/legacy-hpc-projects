Project Summary

The project aims to provide a multi-sectoral support dignified solution to 443 HH (4.647 individuals) the most vulnerable IDPs in Idleb sub-district in the following camps. The targeted areas were identified by selecting from the regions identified as priority areas by the SNFI Cluster.
1. Hawaa Al- Janouby = 32 HH (CP CODE: CP000920)
2. Al Zeyara (Eman Billah) =288 HH (CP CODE: CP000694)
3. AlRabiee-Almaqlaa =123 HH (CP CODE: CO002158)
TRC's response plan showed innovative environmentally friendly and dignify solutions. The provision of physical safety and security for the displaced communities, while maintaining the accessibility and inclusion of IDPs and vulnerable groups, would improve measures and light humanitarian infrastructure at the IDP site. The TRC team will be led by pertinent cluster priorities and safeguarding and protection principles, including the Do No Harm approach, across all phases of the project cycle as it aspires to continue providing these services and all activities to 443 HH and 4.686 people in 3 camps. The IFRC/ICRC, SPHERE standards, and humanitarian ideals are fully embraced by TRC.
TRC is plan to enhancing and strengthening effective and accountable humanitarian WASH coordination will result in timely, predictable and high-quality WASH outcomes that are inclusive and equitable, for the people most affected by and vulnerable to crises. With this project TRC will provide to ensuring effective and responsible humanitarian WASH coordination and systematically supporting, monitoring and evaluating to achieve timely, predictable and high-quality WASH outcomes. TRC aim to contribute to effective and accountable humanitarian WASH coordination and the need for active participation, meaningful representation, and decisive leadership by local and national actors. TRC will focus on resistance programming with limited life-saving intervention. To focus on the rehabilitation and repair of WASH infrastructure, camps in the Dignified Shelter and wide restoration of WASH service provision systems. Most vulnerable people of IDPs including PWD in underserved and high-needs areas will dignified and safer living conditions through improved access to WASH services integrated with Shelter-NFI and CCCM Services.
Within the scope of this project, TRC will complete the Sewerage infrastructure and Clean Water Infrastructure System of the re-located area as a package and monitore of water quality to ensure safe daily access to safe water.The water will meet Sphere quality standards. TRC will carry out regular maintenance-repair and leak detection activities. This activity will be carried out 3 times in total during the entire project period. TRC will provide to solarisation of water sources and construction of high elevated tanks to distribute water via gravity, or operational support to water.
