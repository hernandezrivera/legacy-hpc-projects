Project Summary

Project aims at strengthening services through protection monitoring, reporting, coordination, as well as mitigate the risk of and address acute protection needs stemming from Gender Based violence among IDPs, returnees and marginalized women and girls in Wanlawayne, Kurtunwarey, Afgoye and Barawe Districts of Lower Shabelle Region, Somalia by: conduct monitoring, profiling, quantitative and qualitative analyses, and reporting of protection violations and displacement trends, provide immediate protection specific responses to incidences of abuse through both static and mobile outreach service delivery and material assistance alleviating their worst effects or preventing their occurrence through: Survivor centered multi sectoral support (psycho-social support, medical assistance, legal aid, and physical security) and case management addressing needs of survivors of GBV in acute displacement situations, referrals of individual cases requiring immediate life-saving health and protection interventions, ensuring coordination among key stakeholders for effective and principled responses to survivors of GBV. The project will further ensure training of duty bearers of on GBVIM and case management to improve provision of case management support as well as conducting awareness of GBV in the project targeted locations to enlighten the communities on their rights as provided in the law of the county as well as need for reporting and provision of necessary support by duty bearers in addition to enhancing coordination with other clusters and provide information to displaced communities on procedures and available services, while providing feedback to the broader humanitarian community on needs and the protection environment. The project targets 25,000 persons (10,000 female,12,100 male and 5000 children aged under 18)

Needs

A combination of drought, conflict and forced evictions are the main factors that contribute to displacement in Somalia while IDP women and girls are prone for various form of Sexual Gender based violence when they travel long distance in search of water and firewood and other social amenities/services within the community. The situation is exacerbated by the fact that a majority of women and girls are still ignorant of their rights while weak judicial systems are controlled by majority clans, religious leaders as well as incompetent local government structures that are easily influenced by the majority clans that perpetrates such violations. Gender based violence affecting women and girls remains under reported but widespread, with IDPs remaining extra vulnerable (96 per cent of GBV survivors who accessed services in 2016 were female; 76% IDPs).UNFPA & UNHCR, GBVIMS Data of 2017 indicated that GBV occurs while in transit to IDP sites, while exposure is especially high in IDP sites, for example during water collection after dark or in shelters that offer little physical protection. Furthermore, IDPs from minority clans are reported to be more frequently affected, allegedly due to outsiders intruding into IDP sites while GBV cases related to intimate partner violence, especially domestic violence and deprivation of resources, continue to be the most reported type of GBV. However, huge gaps exist in basic service provision for survivors, including post rape care, psycho-social support, and comprehensive survivor centered care, including case management, hence need for timely and appropriate intervention. The project targeted locations are Wanlawayne,Kurtunwarey, Afgoye and Barawe Districts, Lower Shabelle, Region, Somalia.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Risk of deliberate exclusion of marginalized and minority groups during selection for provision of humanitarian support such as food distribution. Mitigation measure: Having a representation of all groups selection and distribution teams

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Risk of Sexual Gender based violence meted on displaced /returnees as well as vulnerable women and girls from host community. Mitigation: Increasing security of at the community level as wells as ensuring that displaced /returnees as well as vulnerable women and girls are accompanied whenever they are away from homes/IDP camps

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Risk of IDPs, returnees and Vulnerable host community members getting caught up in the armed conflictparticularly when accessing protection services.Mitigation measures: GBV treatment and psycho social sites are located away from conflict areas
