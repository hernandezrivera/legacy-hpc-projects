Project Summary

As indicated on Somalia 2019 Post Gu FSNAU FEWS-NET technical release together with FEWS-NET-FSNAU-Somalia food security outlook report, we found that approximately 2.2 million people will experience crisis (IPC3) or emergency (IPC4) outcomes in september 2019 in Somalia. these people are reportedly in urgent need of humanitarian food assistance and interventions to prevent high levels of acute malnutrition. These reports indecated that in Waqooyi galbeed region 159000 people will be in stressed (IPC2), 142000 people will be in Crisis (IPC3) and other 31000 people will be in emergency (IPC4).
To address the critical food insecurity faced by the drought and cyclone affected IDPs and host communities living in and around Hargeisa, Gebiley and Berbera Districts in Waqooyi galbeed region, Disability Protection Association (DPA) is prepared to improve access to basic food needs, enhanced economic opportunity and restored livelihood assets for vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Waqooyi galbeed region. This project was designed to target 5,600 drought and cyclone affected households comprising 33,000 individuals (Men: 13,200, Women: 19,800, Boys:5,280, Girls: 11,880) who are in critical need of humanitarian assistance.
In the project period, the project will achieve the following activities and outputs:
Provision of emergency unconditional cash transfer and safety nets to 1,500 (1200 IDPs and 300 HC) vulnerable IDPs and host community households (at least 50% of beneficiaries registered must be women).
Vulnerable households benefiting from unconditional cash transfers are sensitized on nutrition and hygiene best practices for food preparation.
Engage 40 women UCT beneficiaries in voluntary women self-help groups and provide them with mentoring and couching on savings.
Enterpreunual and market-based skills training for 200 (160 IDPs and 40 HC) vulnerable women and youth.
Provision of business support grants to trained 200 vulnerable women and youth.
Treatment and vaccination of animals for 800 (2,400 individuals) rural households’ targeting up-to 150,000 livestock
Rehabilitation/construction of key community infrastructure through Cash for Work (CFW) approach, enabling 300 (80% HC and 20% IDPs) households to rehabilitate markets, feeder roads connecting key trading centers, or key water points through the (CFW) approach.
This project is expected to contribute to the strategic objective 1 (Lifesaving) and objective 3 (resilience building) of the HRP for Somalia.
