Project Summary

The project contributes to increased general protection for vulnerable IDPs in Mueda District through setting up a protection monitoring system (including at community base), support to civil documentation for IDPs, case management and referral, and by adopting a coordinated approach to mainstream protection across sectors.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Mueda is a transit district and an area of refuge for at least 94,000 IDPs (according to IOM/DTM) at the time of submission of this proposal. In Negomano border point, there is a large presence of refouled individuals with family separation, single female headed households, pregnant women with no support. Based on comprehensive monitoring of protection issues affecting the IDP population that Helpcode has carried out in 5 sites (Eduardo Mondlane, Lyanda, Namatil, Negomano and Naschitenje) during the final quarter of 2021, the projects aims to address general protection needs and to contribute to improve the protection environment for the targeted population; while ensuring protection mainstreaming through coordination with different sectors, including those (WASH; Education) where Helpcode is already operational. The project focuses on (i) strengthening community structures that supports or implement risk reduction activities; (ii) iproved access to individual protection assistance; (iii) Improved access of POCs to legal/civil documentation; (iv) displaced persons benefiting from community-based risk mitigation, preparedness, and resilience programming; (v) local authorities and civil society actors to be sensitized or trained on protection legal frameworks, principles, and standards, including on durable solutions, Human Rights, Women's rights and Humanitarian law and (vi) improved access of civilians to structures and mechanisms for protection and safety. Target groups are the IDPs population in 5 sites: Eduardo Mondlane, Lyanda, Namatil, Negomano and Naschitenje.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims at improving the protection environment for IDPs through combining community-based approached (aimed at ensuring awareness and participation) and improved access to protection through existing, strengthened services. It also aims at contributing to protection mainstreaming across sectors, by coordinating with institutional and humanitarian partners through the incorporation of protection principles into aid delivery. The project is built on the (still preliminary) results of protection monitoring activities carried out by Helpcode in the final quarter of 2021 in partnership with UNHCR, which aims at identifying specific protection needs and avoid potential risks of exclusion.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Helpcode AAP’s strategy abides to IASC 5 commitments, according to the following: Leadership:- Promote a culture among staff of acceptance of failure and negative feedback from affected people. - Ensure that relevant staff and managers collect feedback from affected populations in appropriate age groups (sensitive to gender & age); respond to this feedback; and report back. - Work with local and national partners, so that there is a coordinated plan and capacity which is sensitive to gender, age, disability and ethnicity. Transparency:- Provide accessible and timely information on organizational procedures, structures and processes that may impact communities in order to support informed decisions, and engage communities in a dialogue as part of information provision Participation:- Work with relevant local and national partners and actors to support and influence effective design, assessment, implementation and monitoring of the response.- Engage all parts of a community, including those who are vulnerable or at risk to draft robust, contextual vulnerability and capacity analyses. Inclusivity is ensured through a thorough analysis of local social dynamics, mobility capacity of beneficiaries, differences in the use of time by women and men, identification of “role models” among community stakeholders that can promote social change. Feedback and complaints:- Provide information to all in affected communities about the organisation, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave (including commitments made on PSEA), the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver. - Communicate in languages, formats and media that are easily understood, respectful and culturally appropriate for different members of the community, especially vulnerable and marginalised groups.- Establish and maintain effective feedback systems, using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non-sensitive feedback. The feedback is disaggregated by age and sex at minimum. Design, monitoring and evaluation:- Assess the implementation of the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and other collective standards including the IASC AAP commitments through assessments of organizational performance on accountability to measure and improve accountability to all persons of concern.- Include persons of concern as partners throughout the operation's management cycle and report the results of assessments and follow up actions to them.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will work with the District Services of Health and in coordination with the humanitarian partners who have developed guidelines and tools for ensuring disability-inclusive protection, in order to go beyond the minimum standards for disability inclusion in humanitarian action.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Among its internal procedure, Helpcode has developed an environmental policy aiming to avoid, minimize and mitigate the environmental impact of its activities in the field, in line with Sphere Humanitarian Standards and global environmental standard, guidelines and tools. In this respect, each project is assessed since design in terms of environmental impact. In this case, planned activities are expected to have a minimal impact on the environment, being activities that do not require distribution of materials / infrastructure construction / changes in land use / water management. Environmental assessment will be carried out again at project kick off and at the end.However, the overall implementation context is affected by severe vulnerability to climate change and extreme climate events. In terms of mitigation, the project foresees the realization of community training son disaster management.
