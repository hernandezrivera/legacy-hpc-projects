Project Summary

In line with the Shelter and NFI cluster guidance, SECD is planning to sustain and save the dignity and lives of the targeted people by implementing a timely, needs-based and effective interventions
the people in north-west Syria are living in a very diﬃcult situation, the area experienced one of the biggest displacement waves, more than 700,000 people left their houses in search for a safer place to save the lives of the women, children, elderly, PWD and other families members
through these interventions, SECD is planning to support18300 People ( 4039 Men, 4379 Women, 4750 Boy and 5132 Girl) based on in- depth need assessment, with focusing on responding to the critical needs of people with considering the access, the functionality of the markets and the community engagement approach by consulting the people in need about the proposed interventions
According to HNO 2020 "In the north-west, there are critical levels of overcrowding putting signiﬁcant pressure on overburdened communities, with few if any remaining shelter options available." the IDPs cannot ﬁnd a shelter to live in, and if it is available HNO 2020 mentioned that "Up to one million IDPs are residing in last-resort sites in the north-west and north-east, a signiﬁcant proportion of whom are in improvised shelters which will need replacement/ repair in the next year.9 In addition, some communities in 23 sub-districts in Aleppo, Hassakeh, , Idleb and Rural Damascus OR Al Tal, Tal Rafat, Dana, Sabe Byar and al Hol and Sabe Byar10 are overburdened as they host more IDPs than resident populations with limited shelter and hosting capacity,"
SECD is planning to build its response plan based on the following interventions:
1- Undertake the rehabilitation of 5 damaged, unﬁnished buildings, collective centers, and 800 houses
2- Establishment of two planned camps (one in Merimen and the other will be identified based on the available lands) in the safe areas as the last resort for the IDPs.
the shelter intervention will be based on the following aspects:
- Ensuring proper HLP situation for the targeted shelters
- Prioritize the cost-effective interventions
- Ensuring the participation of the beneﬁciaries in all the intervention stages

Moreover, the plan is considering the obvious need to support the IDPs with the NFI assistance which is still a big gap in the humanitarian response, HNO 2020 indicated that " many communities suffer from high prices, unpredictable inﬂation, and low wages, while populations routinely report need for items outside the core NFI packages.5 There is still ongoing displacement, with roughly one million people expected to be displaced in 20206 while return movements have increased to some areas and an estimated 64 per cent of communities are underserved7. This is exacerbated by the deterioration of purchasing power resulting in families not able to meet their needs during harsh weather with fuel and power sources remaining in high demand"
SECD is committed to supporting the people in need especially the new IDPs by distributing 1000 core NFI kits 500 winterization kits including stoves, fuel, and blankets in addition to the distribution of 180000 litter of fuel in the winter

4 - Needs

Since 2011, waves of conflict, political and economic instability in Syria caused severe deterioration in the standard of living on the community and individual levels and the escalation of this conflict since 2019 has worsened these conditions. Damage to housing and infrastructure has been observed across NWS, mostly on the communities and areas which are classified as safer area. According to HNO 2020 "Up to one million IDPs are residing in last-resort sites in the north-west and north-east, a significant proportion of whom are in improvised shelters which will need replacement/ repair in the next year". Lack of appropriate shelter and NFIs including clothing, bedding, and hygiene items negatively affects all population groups regardless of their age (children, young adults, vulnerable adults and elderly) and gender (men, women, boys, and girls) worsening their wellbeing, with negative impacts on health, protection, and access to basic services. The dire economic situation, limited access to livelihood opportunities increases the difficulty of making rent payments and can consequently increase the risk of eviction. Shelter represents 50% of domestic expenditures for most households. According to SECD multi-sectorial need assessment, The IDPs face a combination of liquidity constraints and a shortage of housing, which has led to very high rental costs. Those living in public and informal settings, experience lack of access to basic services, poor water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) conditions, overcrowding, and a lack of privacy. Informal settlements are often places of physical insecurity due to crime and other forms of violence. In displacement areas, thousands of people without adequate shelter are living in damaged and/or unfinished buildings and houses, or in collective centers. These living conditions make people more vulnerable and increase the need for appropriate shelter responses.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will focus on providing basic and essential NFI and shelter assistant in addition to the establishment of four planned camps to receive the people who are not having a proper shelter or are living in the open areas and random camps SECD program is planning to provide NFI and winterization to the people in need. The items are designed for emergency response. Distributions are based on needs and must be supported by needs assessments and appropriate verificationsSECD will maintain minimum standards for distribution to ensure transparency the community will be informed (sensitized) regarding the following: vulnerability and selection criteria will be used to select the beneficiaries, the roles of the stakeholders will be explained, moreover all groups including women and youth will be involved across all the process. SECD will conduct assessment/registration using agreed selection criteria of targeting of the most vulnerable, distribution report will be prepared and shared with the SNFI cluster to coordinate. SECD with attends the regular sector coordination meetings to discussing coverage of beneficiaries and future plans to meet the needs of targeted beneficiaries, best practices, and sector standards. Establishment of the planned camps: The overarching objective of this activity is to save lives, provide protection from harsh weather and climate, and restore a sense of dignity by the timely provision of the assistant. The establishment will consider providing a durable solution with focusing on the cost-effective approachSECD will develop a comprehensive (MEAL) tools to systematically track project progress against results. The Do No Harm and the gender-sensitive approaches will reflect the principle that beneficiary needs differ based on culture, vulnerability, gender, and age. Women, men, girls, and boys are all affected and especially women and girls' safety, protection and dignity are critical to this Action.
