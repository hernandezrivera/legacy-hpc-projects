Project Summary

The project aims to improve the land relationships between IDPs and host communities in the target areas by assessing the impact on local land use and land rights due to the mass influx of IDPs and identifying adequate solutions to secure the tenure of all parties.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The mass influx of IDPs to the districts of Metuge, Mecufi, Ibo, Ancuabe and city of Pemba in the past several years due to cyclones and armed conflict has transformed the land use and land tenure systems in host communities. Specifically, both formal and informal camps to settle IDPs have been established, and the government has encouraged the allocation of lands to IDPs for agricultural purposes. These responses have not yet taken into account questions of tenure which are essential to ensure peaceful co-existence between host communities and IDPs and to mitigate the risks of environmental degradation associated with changes in tenure and land-use patterns.This project will assess the changes to land tenure systems, and, through the use of information sessions, training, and community facilitation, identify locally appropriate mechanisms to secure the tenure of both host communities and IDPs for the medium-long term. Specific attention will be given to ensuring that women’s rights to land are recognized and protected through these processes. Target groups:- IDPs and host communities whose land rights have been affected in the target area, with particular attention to women from both communities.- Local authorities of the target area. Project indicators: - Number of districts targeted by the protection monitoring activities, including HLP: 5- Number of people benefiting from protection interventions in Housing, Land, and Property (HLP): 14.255- Number of local authorities and civil society actors trained on HLP legal frameworks, principles and standards: 200

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

- Assessment to identify changes in land use and tenure to the arrival of IDPs in the districts of Metuge, Mecufi, Ibo, Ancuabe and city of Pemba. - Information sessions for IDP and host communities on assessment goals and basic issues related to exercise of land rights - Training local authorities on assessment findings, as well as on importance of HLP-based protection in emergency contexts - Training of women leaders from IDPs and host communities to ensure their voices are adequately represented in the dialogues. - Facilitating inclusive dialogues between IDP, host community, and local leaders to identify locally appropriate mechanisms to secure tenure of all parties for the medium term (5-10 years). 18 training sessions involving 255 total beneficiaries (half for women leaders, half for men). 175 information services involving 80 people each. Total of 14.255 distinct beneficiaries.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of local institutional stakeholders (Serviço Distrital de Actividades Economicas – SDAE) and target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation.The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorising traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation.The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to improve relationships over land . All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The initial assessment will enable to identify the most in need beneficiaries, that will be assisted along all the project implementation. Furthermore, other aspects will be ensured:● Involvement of the community leaders on the diffusion of the messages. In fact, they have significant potential to influence the cultural norms.● Holding single-sex, age segmented focus discussion group sessions on constrains to their behaviour change.The action follows a participatory approach for the design and implementation of activities. The action will ensure meaningful access to assistance through activities such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection.The project will empower the women to act as the first agent of change in their own communities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The Serviço Distrital de Actividade Economicas - SDAE will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities. All the activities have been structured with the aim of guaranteeing access to people living with disabilities, such as the selection of the location for the training and the project staff will guarantee all the support to allow them to participate in the actions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The assessment will look at the impacts on land and nearby natural resources (trees, water) caused by the influx of IDPs, which will have changed land and natural resource use patterns, and likely has exacerbated environmental stress in host areas. Once these risks are identified, Oikos and NRC can work with local communities to identify solutions to mitigate and potentially reverse these negative impacts, using a land-tenure perspective.
