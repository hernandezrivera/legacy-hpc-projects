Project Summary

South Sudan literate levels remains very low. According to UNICEF only 23% of the toral population in the country have had formal education and this has affected the access to education to millions of young children. Its even worse for the children with disabilities who have no access to education services and schools are not accessible.
The capacity of teachers to teach in schools is very low and the availability of disability inclusive learning materials for learners with disabilities is also quite limited. This has left learners with disabilities huge learning gaps for disability inclusive learning materials and role plays.
In line with the education cluster strategic objectives, light for the world will Improve equitable access to safe and protective disability inclusive education services to 2000 children with disabilities displaced in IDPs camps, returnees, spontaneous refugees, and host communities.
This project is intended to support disability inclusive education in emergency for children with disabilities through identification and registration of children with disabilities in the IDP camps and in the host communities to support them to access disability inclusive education.
Light for the Word will establish disability inclusive WASH facilities including hand washing will provide a safe environment from water-borne diseases and improve sanitation standards in all the schools that will be supported by the project.
Integrated capacity building of teachers in pedagogy, disability inclusion, PSS, protection/GBV would help them to give lifesaving skills support to learners so that they can be able to protect themselves and improve resilience by the youths. This will include ensuring that teachers are able to do classroom management for learners with and without disabilities.
Light for the world intents to support 1980 children with disabilities in Tonj South, Tonj North, Pibor and Tambura Counties. This will be done through community-based rehabilitation programme where children with disabilities in the communities are supported by CBR field workers will offer rehabilitation, train them on sign language, community awareness on the need of persons with disabilities to access education services. Distribution of assistive devices that will enable children with disabilities to access education services will also be done.
Light for the world will also work closely with OPDs and train them on advocacy for inclusive education in emergency this will ensure that children with disabilities are able to access education services and that education institutions are disability inclusive.
The project will establish disability inclusive temporary learning spaces (TLS) and classrooms, establish disability inclusive gender-segregated latrines with water points. Light for the world will give schools supplies, hygiene kits, and recreational materials as well as building the capacity of teachers and PTAs to offer disability inclusive education.
Light for the world has vast experience in disability inclusion and supported the ministry of education in drafting the disability inclusive education policy. This project will also ensure that the disability inclusive education policy is disseminated to the policy to key stakeholders in effort to realize disability inclusive education in South Sudan.
Light for the world has physical presence in the target counties and has vast experience in implementation of disability inclusive education programmes in South Sudan.

1. Needs

The need to support disability inclusive education in South Sudan has increased and the urgency to ensure that children with and without disabilities have access to quality education has further increased. This is informed by the number of out of school children with over 70% of the children in South Sudan still out of school calling for efforts to improve access to quality education and especially for children with disabilities.The countries literacy levels are one of the lowest in the country with UNICEF estimating that only 23% of the population are literate and this is even worse for persons with disabilities that have limited access to education services due to lack of disability inclusive learning environment and materials.The country is experiencing some challenging climate change related issues like flooding that has already affected more than 1.4 million people in 22 counties. Tonj East has over 18000 people that have been displaces and Tonj South has over 16000 people also affected by flooding. The flooding have affected learning infrastructure with classes socked in water and destruction of learning materials (Flood snapshot Nov 2024).The country still struggles with acute food insecurity and this is a key contributing factor for many children to be out of school. Over 6.3 million people are facing acute food insecurity and the target counties are among those severely affected. This affects the enrollment of children to learning institutions and especially the children with disabilities.Lack of disability inclusive teaching materials for teachers and disability inclusive learning materials as well as lack of disability inclusive learning infrastructure have negatively impacted the enrollment of children with disabilities in learning institutions. This has led to low enrollment of children with disabilities in learning institutions.Lack of payment of salaries and delayed incentives to the teachers have led to low turnout and motivation of teachers to attend to learners. This has had detrimental effect on learning of children and even worse children with disabilities.Lack of segregated disability inclusive WASH facilities, menstrual hygiene management kits and hand washing facilities in the schools have also led to girls absenteeism in schools. This is even more for girls with disabilities who are often neglected. Early and forced marriages among young girls have had a huge impact on education services for young girls that has led to relatively low number of girl with and without disabilities enrollment in schools.This therefore calls for urgent intervention to ensure that children including those with disabilities have access to quality disability inclusive education that will be key in improving literacy levels in South Sudan as well as empowering the most vulnerable in the community.Light for the world address the needs by offering disability inclusive quality education to ensure that children with and without disabilities have the right to education and teachers have access to disability inclusive learning and training materials in the target counties.

2. Project activities

• Enrollment of 1980 Children with disabilities in Primary schools in Pibor County of Jonglei state and Tonj East and Tonj South County of Warrap state and Tambura county in Western Equatoria State. • Establishment of 16 disability inclusive temporary learning spaces (TLS) and classrooms in the four target counties.• Establishment of 8 gender segregated disability inclusive latrines with water points and in Tonj East, Tonj South, Pibor and Tambura Counties.• Procure and supply Sanitation and Hygiene materials (hand washing soap, sanitizers, masks, etc.) to students and teachers • Provide assistive and mobility devices for children with disabilities to support access to education institutions.• Provision of quality disability inclusive education recreational materials to the conflict and crisis affected children and youth in the two target counties.• Training of 160 teachers in disability inclusive Education in Emergency pedagogical skills and disability inclusive Child-centered methodologies to improve disability inclusive teaching and learning processes.• Train teachers and education personnel on disability inclusive referral pathways on protection/GBV, and health, and nutrition. • Training of 100 SMCs/PTAs and education authorities on disability inclusive school management and governance, psycho social support (PSS), and Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) • Conduct back to learning campaigns 4 per county in Pibor County, Tonj East, Tonj South and Tambura Counties.• Identification of children with disabilities in the communities and supporting them to join learning institutions. • Strengthening referral pathways for children with disabilities to have access to rehabilitation services and enable them to school without difficulties.• Conduct Home based rehabilitation like therapy, mobility and orientation training , sign language and more to able children with disabilities fit in school• Awareness raising on disability education in emergency.• Recruitment and train 16 Community Based Rehabilitation field workers to work home to home• Recruit and train 8 youth with disabilities as disability inclusion facilitators.• Train mainstream actors on disability inclusion• Train OPDs to advocate for an inclusive education in emergency.
