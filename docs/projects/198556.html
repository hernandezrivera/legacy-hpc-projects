Project Summary

The Alternative Teaching and Learning Support (ATLS) for 20420 Out of School children living in IDPs Camps and host communities across 6 LGAs in Yobe, Adamawa and Borno states.
The Project seeks to complement three key objectives which are to help reduce morbidity and mortality due to conflict among school learners, mitigate the negative impact school closure on children’s learning and wellbeing, and ensure effective, inclusive and safe return to quality learning for learners after the conflict outbreak.
The initiative shall be roll-out through the direct involvement of Education and Protection Stakeholders at community level and sector levels

1 - Needs

The North East of Nigeria has suffered from a decade-long conflict of the popular West African Terrorist Group “Boko Haram”. A crisis which has lived hundreds of thousands of civilian population in IDPs camps and weakened the already weak education system-pushing more children out of school through the systematic destruction of educational infrastructures, displacement of the population etc. Through scourges of violent attacks and killings mainly in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe States in the North East. The region has recorded over 5.3 million displaced population and an estimated 2.2 million conflict affected children in need of education.In addition, this region is also characterised by abject poverty as the population is mostly nomadic and highly illiterate. Pastoral and nomadic farming is largely considered as the major economic activity in this arid region though subsistence but with the frequent attacks of the insurgent groups, farmers no longer feel safe to farm with the bulk of the rural population now living in Internally Displaced Persons Camps across safer areas of Borno state.
