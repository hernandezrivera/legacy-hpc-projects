4 - Needs

Based on March 2021 data (Community Surveillance report conducted by PAC )) that included Idlib district in northwest Syria, the overall proportion of GAM rate was 3 MAM among children under 5 2.9%, and MAM among PLWs 11.6%. Also, the data showed that: - 17.19 % of the mothers in Jis-Ash-Shugur area are malnourished with GAM 4.22% among children under 5 - 22.92 % of the mothers in Harim District are malnourished with 3.35 % GAM among children under 5- 9.01% of mothers in Ariha sub-district are malnourished with 1.27 GAM among children under 5 in addition to that, the facility nutrition surveillance report conducted in March 2021, data showed a prominent level of moderate acute malnutrition among children, especially between children 6-23 months, that is also associated with a high prevalence of irreversible stunting between children 48-59 months. The data shows a link between lack of exclusive breastfeeding and lack of dietary diversity or minimum acceptable diets to these nutrition concerns. Additionally, the malnutrition proportions among PLW were also high at 28.35 % in Idleb. The PLW data on breastfeeding and acceptable diet almost all show that less than 50% of PLW population are within standard acceptable minimum safe and healthy practices. Mercy-USA has conducted its own internal monitoring and data collection exercises (TPM) that have shown unfortunate findings relating to food security, nutrition, and access to livelihoods. During the month of October 2020, Mercy-USA TPM found that Aleppo and Idleb governorate residents are highly food insecure.

5 - Implementation of activities

Nurses will screen the children under 5 and refer the ones with dangers signs and medical problems to the available health facilities. Community Health Workers (CHWs) will assess and screen nutritional status among the children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them where needed. Also, the infant and young child feeding practices in emergencies (IYCF-E) services will be provided to prevent any case of malnutrition by: - Counseling the PLWs and caregivers of children under 24 months of age and providing messages on IYCF-E recommended practices to mothers who have malnourished children. - Distributing micronutrients and nutrition supplies for the PLWs and children.- Reporting to the cluster any distribution of breast milk substitute. Midwives will screen pregnant and lactating women and refer the ones with any danger signs or medical problems to the nearest facilities. Also, a pre and rapid coordination combined with referrals will be in place if the team notices or identifies any sign of abuse or violence among children under 5 and or mothers. All cases of violence against women and children will be referred to a pre-identified center. This response will adopt the recommendation of the cluster in COVID-19 context to minimize the risk of infection for staff and beneficiaries Also, this project will involve empowering mothers and HH members through training to screen their own children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes and check for Odema.
