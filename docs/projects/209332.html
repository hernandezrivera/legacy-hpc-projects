Project Summary

Executive Summary:
This project aims to contribute to reducing violence against women and girls during the current armed conflict in Sudan, and in order to provide protection and appropriate response to women and girls at risk. This means protection of women and girls from all forms of violence , reducing the effects of violence through the axis of prevention and create a change in attitudes and behaviors of local Communities related to combating violence and enhancing the capacity of women and girls protection networks, stakeholders and all actors through participatory approaches to protecting women and girls from violence.
The project’s activities are to provide self-protection and life and social skills for girls and to ensure the participation of women and girls in protection activities through: training of food and tea sellers Women in the markets in prevention and response to gender-based violence (basic concepts, referral, psychological support, sexually transmitted diseases, voluntary examination) in - Nyala south locality- markets of (Thebes, the Nile, Aljabel, the Al borsa, Al Mawaser, Al-Aluba, and Souq Sittah) and forming a joint protection network for the purpose of contributing to raising Awareness among women and girls working in markets with the possibility of monitoring and following up on suspected cases of PSEA. The network also represents a link between the community and the relevant authorities (referral and hotline). Also conduct training for girls working in food and tea selling shops in GBV response and prevention, Community awareness sessions will be organized targeting women, girls, youth and men, psychological support sessions for women and girls will also be organized by a social worker, with distribution of 500 dignity kits for vulnerable women and Finally, organize training targeting service providers (health/education/Law), community committees and civil society members in GBV.
The project targets 2,632 people (food and tea sellers, 100 women, age group 25-35 years), (joint protection network, 16 women, age group 25-35 years and 9 girls, age group 14-17), (girls working in food And tea shops, 100 girls, age group 14-17 years), (community outreach on GBV, 350 women and girls, youth and men, 150, age group over 18 years), (psychological support sessions, 500 women and girls, age group over 15), (Distribution of dignity kits 500 women and girls, age group 14-35 years) and (training on GBV for service providers, community committees, and civil society members, 25 women and 25 men, age group over 25 years) Provide life skills and recreational activities on CP & MRE for ( 580 children)
( in Nyala South locality
Activities/outputs
Output 1: The knowledge and skills of women and girls have improved and the level of participation of women and girls in protection activities has increased.
1- Training of 100 women who sell food and tea, in GBV (basic concepts, referral, psychological support, sexually transmitted diseases) for a period of 3 days.
2- Formation and training of a joint women network (16 women and 9 girls) in the targeted markets for 4 days.
3- Conduct 10 meetings for the Joint Women’s Protection Network during the life of the project.
4- . Organizing 25 psychological support sessions targeting 500 women and girls
5- Distribution of 500 dignity kits.
6- Training of 100 girls working in food and tea shops in the field of prevention and response to GBV for 3 days.
7. Provide life skills and recreational activities on CP & MRE for ( 580 children/500 girl-80 boy – age group 13-17 year)
The second output: Strengthening and increasing positive practices at the local community level.
1- Organizing 25 community outreach sessions in GBV targeting 500 women, girls, and youth at the project site.
2- Training workshops for 50 service providers, community committees and civil society for 3 days.
3- Organizing 5 mobilization and advocacy meetings with stakeholders and the local community.
