Project Summary

(1) To contribute towards the provision of quality essential GBV and health services during the Cholera Response that retains dignity and safety of community members especially women and girls;
(2) To reduce infection rate of Cholera through Social and Behavioral Change Communication initiatives in targeted communities integrated with GBV
(3)To reduce the vulnerability to abuse, violence and exploitation of vulnerable groups including women and girls during the Cholera Outbreak
