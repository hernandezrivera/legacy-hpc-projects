Project Summary

The project will aim to improve the multisectoral response for the refugees and asylum seekers in Cameroon, and the implementation of sustainable solutions for their benefit.
UNHCR commits to support the Cameroonian Government in implementing the Global Refugee Forum pledges in health, education, protection, employment, and socio-economic opportunities and will work with development actors and the private sector to increase refugee inclusion in national services, development plans and activities. This will enable a transition from humanitarian assistance to more sustainable development, reduce refugees’ dependence on humanitarian assistance, and strengthen their resilience. By becoming self-reliant, refugees will also be able to contribute to the country’s economy.
This will be done through improving the protection environment, access to complementary solutions and pathways for refugees and asylum seekers regarding registration activities, documentation, protection of childhood, prevention, response to sexual and gender-based violence, access to basic social services (health, education, and water), access to decent shelter and goods essential domestic needs as well as building resilience. To achieve this UNHCR Cameroon will have to reinforce Government capacity building and strengthen non-transitional partnerships and inclusion of refugees in the programs of other funded actors, as well as repatriation.
The interventions of UNHCR and its partners will be aligned with the priorities defined by the government (national development strategy 2020-2030). They are also part of its contribution to achieving the SDGs, the UNSDCF and the principle of leaving no one behind. Emphasis will be placed on the continued inclusion of refugees and asylum seekers in government programs, as well as humanitarian and development actors such as the World Bank.
For the year 2024 the project will target 472,806 refugees including 354,078 CAR, of which 187,585 children (<18 years), 153,535 adults (18-59 years), 12,958 older people (>60 years); and 118,728 Nigerians, of which 73,658 children (<18 years), 40,379 adults (18-59 years), 4,691 older people (>60 years); as well as 15% of vulnerable host populations.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Priorities will be: Maintain and enhance the protection space in the four countries under the UNHCR Cameroon multi-Country Office, and capitalizing on key partnership with other UN agencies, development actors, regional organizations, NGOs, academic institutions, and civil society. Identify venues to ensure that protection policies are sustainable and well-aligned with the regional protection principles and regulations. Gradual phasing out from some key sectors such as registration, documentation and refugee status determination in urban areas and effectively transfer responsibilities to the Government. Promote the economic integration of refugees.  Strengthen data and information management to support evidence-based decision-making. Discuss with the Government on alternative to the Minawao camp, hosting Nigerian refugees. This will be done through improving the protection environment, access to complementary solutions and pathways for refugees and asylum seekers regarding registration activities, documentation, protection of childhood, prevention, response to sexual and gender-based violence, access to basic social services (health, education, and water), access to decent shelter and goods essential domestic needs as well as building resilience. To enable refugees and asylum seekers to rebuild their lives by returning to their respective countries of origin, or in the country of asylum, or third countries, UNHCR will facilitate the implementation of durable solutions:- Facilitate the return and reintegration of 1,000 Cameroonian refugee returnees from Nigeria- Integration of camp-based refugees into the local socio-economic fabric.- Voluntary repatriation of 2,500 Central African refugees - Voluntary repatriation of 10,000 Nigerian refugees - Capitalize on indirect funding/non-transactional partnership and partnership with the private sector - Strengthening UNHCR supervisory/coordination function. UNHCR will continue to strengthen its information management system and support the inter- agency information management systems established by the Government and by other partners such as the Education Management Information System), the Gender-Based Violence Information Management System to ensure robust and up-to-date data collection disaggregated by age, sex and specific needs. This will ensure inclusive and effective targeting and programming.
