Project Summary

Since 2016, political and social unrest simmered in Cameroon’s Anglophone South-West (SW) and North-West (NW) Regions, home to roughly 4 million people.1 In late 2017 the conflict escalated culminating in violence and hostilities between non-state armed groups (NSAGs) and state defense and security forces, triggering significant internal displacement and humanitarian needs. Fear of violence, destroyed roadways, burning of villages, and government and NSAG imposed lockdowns have limited people’s ability to circulate and conduct livelihood activities.
Anglophone Crisis Emergency Response(ACER) is a 12-month project from March, 2020 to March, 2021 to meet the immediate food and NFI needs to the affected households in Bamenda I, II, III and Santa subdivisions, Mezam Division – Northwest, Cameroun.
The project implemented by Catholic Relief Services and its partner, the Archdiocese of Bamenda is providing 27,756 participants in 3,917 households six rounds of seasonal food assistance. i.e. 6.38 USD per person per month covering 50% of the monthly calorie needs based on WFP Food basket., using e-vouchers and one round of NFI to IDP’s and Host households. The voucher value for NFI is based on the cost of sleeping mats, blankets, and kitchen sets in the NFI cluster-recommended kit.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Since 2016, political and social unrest has simmered in Cameroon’s Anglophone Southwest (SW) and Northwest (NW) Regions. In 2017, the conflict escalated, culminating in violence and hostilities between non-state armed groups (NSAGs) and Cameroonian state defense and security forces, triggering significant internal displacement and humanitarian needs. The OCHA NW/SW Situation Report from August 2020 estimates that the crisis has affected approximately 3 million people, including 679,000 internally displaced persons (IDPs), 204,000 returnees, and 59,000 refugees. The widespread conflict, displacement, and unrest have deepened the vulnerability of host communities (HCs), refugees, IDPs. These populations continue to face multiple short and long-term challenges including food insecurity and interrupted livelihoods. As of August 2020, 1,473,000 million people were food insecure according to the Food Security Cluster dashboard, while only 234,449 people received food assistance that month, so massive needs remain. Insecurity in the NW/SW has also resulted in the interruption or loss of livelihoods as families fled their homes and now have limited access to farms. The weekly Monday ghost town days, when movement and market opening are banned, frequent lock down days, recurrent banning of the use of motorcycles; and border closures as a result of COVID-19 curtailed economic activities, reducing families’ purchasing power and ability to cover their basic needs. Food Assistance: CRS ACER project will provide 27,756 participants in approximately 3,917 households six rounds of seasonal food assistance using e-vouchers. CRS will cover 50% of the caloric needs, aligned with the Food MEB for Northwest. NFI Assistance: CRS will target approximately 3,917 households for a one-time NFI e-voucher. The voucher value is based on the cost of sleeping mats, blankets, and kitchen sets in the NFI cluster-recommended kit. Tiered voucher values are based on household size – households of 8 or more will receive a voucher for 60% of the kit, and those with 7 or fewer will receive 50%. The NFI assistance has already been distributed in July, 2020. Activities to be implemented are as follows: • ACER Start-up, CAT set-up, training, procurement • Participant registration and baseline survey.• Vendor selection and orientation.• Sensitization and card distribution.• Food e-voucher transfers • NFI e-voucher transfers• Monitoring surveys, Post Distribution and Onsite Monitoring(s), Feedback and Response Mechanism• Vendor paymentsAll the above will contributing in the overall goal of alleviating suffering of displaced and host households in Mezam through ensuring conflict-affected households have NFIs and improved food security, Vulnerable households access sufficient food in the local market and Vulnerable households receive Food and NFI vouchers.
