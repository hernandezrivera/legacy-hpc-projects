Project Summary

To increase access to life-saving sustained safe drinking Water, Gender sensitive sanitation facilities and promotion of good hygiene practices to support 6,700 people including PWDs in Galkacyo, Burtinle, Garowe, Galdogob, Jariiban IDPs and Host Communities in crisis and/or emergency affected Women, Girls, Boys, and Men. These groups shall be involved at the various project stages from planning to actual implementation to ensure their views and needs are fully captured. The project's cluster objective 1 will require 67% financial requirements while the Cluster objective 2 will require 33% project finances for efficient and successful implementation.

Project Activities

The proposed project seeks to sustain the lives of 4 million people requiring humanitarian assistance, including 2.4 million non-IDPs, 1.6 million IDPs and people with disability across 74 districts, by ensuring safe, equitable and dignified access to livelihoods and essential services by the end of 2021. Pmwdo proposes to achieve this through:-(i) - Delivery of life-saving WASH assistance to reduce acute needs among most vulnerable settlements and communities (including men, women, boys and girls) in a dignified manner.(ii) - Continuing to restore access to safe and equitable basic WASH services in targeted locations (including non-IDP).(iii) - Establishing of WASH Club peer groups in identified schools in both Mudug and Nugaal. Pmwdo will fully engage all targeted groups i.e. women, youth, girls, boys, IDPs, PWDs, etc through consultations and participation in all project phases to ensure that their views and needs are captured and represented.

Project Indicators

1 - Improved equitable access to quality emergency and essential lifesaving WASH services for crisis affected aimed at reducing avoidable cases. 2 - Mitigate impact and respond to reported and identified cases from the affected targeted population3 - Number of Non-IDPs (including men, women, boys and girls) improved and accessing safe water and adequate sanitation supported by hygiene promotion interventions by end of 2021, to prevent public health risks and minimize disease transmission.4 - Number of IDPs (including men, women, boys and girls) improved and accessing basic WASH services by end of 2021, to prevent public health risks and minimize disease transmission.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash transfers work well because the private sector is functioning despite the ongoing conflict. This is largely because of the country's dependence on food imports, which ensures there are relatively consistent markets to buy food and other items.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

The project design includes everything from who is responsible for completing the project to a description of the project, its goals, outcomes and objectives. It describes when these goals, outcomes and objectives will be reached, and the major deliverables, products or features that will be completed. It will help in supporting the ‘Market assessment and risk identification’ and 'Market situation monitoring' processes of cash-based assistance operations. It will also help PMWDO make informed transfer modality decisions and detect changes in market functionality over time. The design also provided the rationale for the Market Functionality Index and describes the key dimensions that a market assessment should tackle:- i) Assortment of essential goods, ii) Availability, iii) Price, iv) Resilience of supply chains, v) Market competition, vi) Infrastructure, vii) Services, viii) Food quality, and ix) Access and protection. The design also describes the procedure to calculate the index.
