Project Summary

This project intends to Strengthen provision of life- saving specialized quality GBV services, for survivors of GBV and vulnerable women and girls. (with a focus on women and girls with disabilities and adolescent girl in addition to enhance strategies for community resilience through empowerment of women and girls and strengthened community based interventions.
The project will focus on most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host communities vulnerable to GBV and other protection concerns in South Kordofan state, Specifically, Dilling and Talawdi localities by providing them with access to services, information about GBV prevention and response, and specific material support and capacity building to reduce GBV vulnerability.
Key interventions will include:
1- Establishment of a Women Development Center in Talawdi locality.
2- Establish and support 5 community protection networks (CPNs) in 2 localities (2 Dilling and 3 Talawdi).
3- Conduct (12) regular meetings with the members of the Community Protection Networks.
4- Conduct (10) training workshops targeting community leaders, religious leaders, NGOs/CBOs, government on GBV prevention.
5- Organize awareness raising campaigns with local authorities and communities commemorating international days such as 16 Days of Activism and Human Rights .
6- Produce and disseminate IEC materials on GBV prevention.
7- Conduct Training in the use of fuel efficient stoves for 200 women.
8- Provide fuel-efficient stoves to 200 most vulnerable women.
9- Provide training to women and girls on vocational skills and support them with startup capital for IGAs target 20 women's
With a position in South Kordofan, Turath has a long history of providing humanitarian support and protection assitanses including GVB.
Turath Have two field offices in South Kordofan ( Dilling and Abujubaiha) will full capacity operation and staff. in addition to small coordination office in Kadugli.

1 - Needs

The Dilling and Talwdi localities in South Kordufan as high priority areas in need of humanitarian assistance. Turath is already implementing projects in these localities and has detailed information about the needs of the target populations based on a baseline survey it conducted, monitoring reports of earlier interventions supported in the localities and other reports such as provided by OCHA and UNFPA. Turath has also conducted a needs assessment in the proposed project areas which included focus group discussions with the community to assess protection concerns. This assessment highlights that GBV incidents against children are not adequately reported due to stigma and cultural issues, while various reports confirm GBV/SV incidences and prevalence among girls and women in South Kordofan and West Kordofan. The current humanitarian situation in these localities is already characterized by extreme vulnerability and the protracted crisis and displacement further exacerbates the situation in Dilling and Reif Ashargi localities resulting in a high risk of violence, abuse and exploitation especially among the most vulnerable group being children and women. Prevention of and response to GBV including provision of psychosocial support services, are priority areas of the protection sector. Turath, as a GBV Sub-sector member, actively contributes to prevention, response and psychosocial support services for GBV survivors. Other urgent identified concerns include the need to respond to psychosocial trauma, separation of children from parents/guardians and safe access to basic services. Gaps in protection monitoring and population movement tracking also lead to untimely and inadequate assistance. The aim is to increase response capacities of government and local communities to prevent and respond to GBV (legal, medical, psychosocial support and counseling) with a special emphasis on health response as the first entry point for GBV survivors This will be achieved through multi-pronged interventions including capacity building of service providers, advocacy among stakeholders and propositioning GBV emergency supplies (such as personal hygiene kits and RH kits). Advocacy for multi-sector GBV prevention; enhancing utilization of SOPs and effective referral and protection mechanisms creating awareness on children and women’s rights among communities, propositioning of GBV emergency supplies, capacity building of workers from governments, civil society and CBOs to deliver prevention, response and psychosocial support services, are all crucial aspects of Turath focus. Therefore identifying and training GBV Emergency response teams to provide rapid interventions, including rapid assessment, is paramount.

2 - Activities/Outputs

1- Establishment of a Women Development Center in Talawdi locality. The Center will cater for the needs of recently settled IDPs and host communities and will provide a number of services and host training and awareness raising events including community based psycho social support service training and awareness raising on GBV and gender issues, reproductive health, HIV AIDS. 2- Establish and support 5 community protection networks (CPNs) in 2 localities.3- Conduct (12) regular meetings with the members of the Community Protection Networks to mitigate GBV risks, response to incidences and eliminate stigma of survivors. 4- Conduct (10) training workshops targeting community leaders, religious leaders, NGOs/CBOs, government on GBV prevention and response with focus on GBV concepts and consequences, referral pathways, psycho social support and legal and medical support to the GBV survivors 5- Organize awareness raising campaigns with local authorities and communities commemorating international days such as 16 Days of Activism and Human Rights Day through workshops, seminars and symposium, mobile theater, informative coverage, cultural and sports festivals targeting 5000 community members including, religious leaders, women, youth and local authorities from both localities. Turath will develop visibility materials (T-shirts, caps,) with the campaign theme to be used in the campaign activities. 6- Produce and disseminate IEC materials on GBV prevention. 7- Conduct Training in the use of fuel efficient stoves for 200 women. Women are often faced with increase risks of sexual harassment and GBV as they venture far from outside their homes in search of firewood. In addition, if they need to buy firewood, it puts an economic strain on the household that is already resources constrained. Access to fuel efficient stoves reduces the exposure to GBV risks and also has an impact on availability of household cash further mitigating GBV vulnerability. 8- Provide fuel-efficient stoves to 200 most vulnerable women. These stoves are made from empty tin cans and ceramic giving thermal power (2.8 kW) and an average efficiency of 36.8%. Generally, the appliance consists of a solid metal (usually cast iron or steel) closed fire chamber, a ceramic and an adjustable air control. The appliance will be connected by ventilating stove pipes to a suitable chimney or flue, which will fill with hot combustion gases once the fuel is ignited. The chimney or flue gases must be hotter than the outside temperature to ensure combustion gases are drawn out of the fire chamber and up the chimney. 9- Provide training to women and girls on vocational skills and support them with startup capital for IGAs target 20 women's

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

According to protection and GBV principles Turath will ensure protection is mainstreamed with the project by different ways among the targeted beneficiaries. Turath will construct shelters that will be used in the proposed intervention as safe spaces for women to meet together to discuss their own related issues, and participate in the different activities such as ; awareness raising , and fuel efficient stove . The women center space will be friendly alocated in the same area and close to the participants’ homes thus reducing the need to walk far distances. The activities design such as Fuel efficient stove is friendly with the environment, and contributes to the reduction the risk of GBV. Furthermore, The awareness activities will focus on changes of the attitudes and behaviors towards violence against women to ensure women and girls rights are respected and not exposed to risks and assault. Additionally, through train services providers Turath will contribute to strengthen of the referral pathway for the survivors and improve the quality of services through train the services providers of the guiding principles and maintain the confidentiality and safety and security of the survivors while receiving the different services.
