Project Name

Start Date

30/12/2019

End Date

29/12/2020

Project Summary

This proposed project will focus to strengthening of the resilience of 5000 vulnerable Adolescent boys and girls through provision of life-skill and Psychosocial support services, and support the livelihood capacity of 100 caregivers, and Adolescent headed household, Provide Child focused Sensitization massages to educate 50,000 Boys and girls among children, adolescents, and adult men and women on COVID 19 response and prevention to reduce or mitigate transmission and to spread of coronavirus among Children and Child protection massages in IDP Camps and communities across MMC, Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Dambao LGA in Borno state.
GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall build the capacity of 250 community volunteers engaged under the Borno state Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development (BSMoWASD) to deliver structured: age appropriate psycho-social support, recreational activities, life skill and carry out child targeted messages on COVID 19 risk and prevention through Mass Sensitizations and distribution of COVID 19 prevention IEC Materials across targeted IDP camps and Communities.
Through this project GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall identify 100 caregivers and Adolescent headed households and give them livelihood skill training and support to improve their livelihood in IDP Camps and communities across MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Damboa LGAs.
Through this project GPON shall strengthen the capacities of existing Community Based Child Protection Committee (CBCPC) to be informed on how they can respond to support children in their respective communities to manage the COVID 19 emerging situations and create more if necessary; and build their capacities to be able to identify child protection and GBV concern and respond accordingly and/or report such cases to the child protection monitoring desk.
GOALPrime shall also in collaboration with government stakeholders and the child protection sub-sector work towards strengthening the referral pathway for the COVID 19 response across the targeted location. Through the community based child protection monitoring and advocacy desk that will be established in the LGAs, child protection and GBV concerns shall be identified and GOALPrime shall be intentional about addressing the identified concerns directly and through revitalized partnership with all relevant community, humanitarian and government stakeholders operational in MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Damboa LGAs.

1 - Needs

Emergencies such as we have in Northeast Nigeria has severe consequences on children and their families as well as affect their ability to build back better. According to Nigeria’s HRS 2019/2021 https://reliefweb.int/report/nigeria/nigeria-2019-2021, 7.1 million people (2.3 million girls, 1.9 million boys, 1.6 million women and 1.3 million men) are in need of humanitarian assistance in north-east Nigeria as a result of a crisis that is now in its tenth year. In the midst of the ongoing humanitarian activities in North-East Nigeria, a new threat has emerged in the form of a global pandemic of COVID-19, a coronavirus disease caused by SARS-CoV-2. The number of infections in Borno and other parts of Nigeria and surrounding countries continues to climb. As an initiative deployed in many vulnerable and crisis-affected countries, humanitarian community is deeply concerned by the devastating impact the COVID-19 pandemic may have on the millions of affected people. The projections of the Child Protection Subsector in the 2019 Nigeria HRP estimates that 2.7 million young children and adolescents (1.5 million girls and 1.2 million boys) in the BAY States are in need of protection and cross-sectoral services. Threats and risks to the well-being of girls and boys include family separation, explosive remnants of war, gender-based violence including child marriage and conflict-related sexual violence, recruitment and use of children, abduction and other grave child rights violations. According to the Child Protection Guidance Tool for the 2019 HRP, it is projected that at least 6,000 children (more than 50 per cent girls) are estimated to have experienced sexual violence by the Non-State Armed Groups. The crisis, evidently has caused severe psychological distress amongst over 2 million young children and adolescents; Borno State being the epicenter. The conflict has not only disrupted the social fabric but has also negatively affected the ability of parents and communities to appropriately care for their children. Also, according to the 2019 Humanitarian Response Plan, At least 150,000 caregivers of vulnerable children have suffered from severe psychosocial distress. The social relations disruption, the anxiety and fears that accompany the event before, during and after, and uncertainty for the future have rendered young children, adolescents and their families helpless thus preventing them from taking care of themselves and their environment. As a result, they are evidently losing hope for the future hence limiting their active participation in the realization of national priorities. If the situation is left unaddressed, and this proposed programme is not implemented timely, the psycho-social effects of the conflict may lead to continuous suffering, indifference, depression, frustration, negative coping mechanism, violent behavior and other antisocial dispositions, thereby increasing social tension, prolonging the recovery process and weakening the resilience of children in the long-term. It is therefore top priority that we provide these conflict affected young children, adolescents as well as their caregivers’ continuous access to psychosocial support services by strengthening the capacity of the Borno State MoWASD Community Volunteers to deliver quality Life-skills and Art Therapy to enhance beneficiaries’ reintegration and resilience, provide child focused Covid 19 risk and prevention messaging to children and their caregivers to mitigate the spread of the virus.

2 - Activities

1. Preparatory visit and consultation with the community stakeholders and social welfare workers in the LGAs as well as community entry meetings in MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Damboa LGAs2. Identify and train community volunteers in MMC, Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and on delivery of quality community-based PSS and community level child protection monitoring, Covid 19 risk and preventive messaging for children and their caregivers.3. Based on agreed selection criteria, identify, select and profile 2,000 adolescents and younger children to be reached by the trained community volunteers with PSS and Life-skills and GBV Adolescent safety and wellbeing in MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Damboa LGAs4. Development of child focused messages on COVID-19 response and prevention with the support of the child protection sub sector.5. Printing and distribution of the developed COVID 19 response and prevention child focused massages6. Begin three sessions per week engagement with girls and boys with on structured local context PSS and life-skills using UNICEF adolescent kits for Expression and Innovation in emergencies, Community Art based Psycho-social Support and the iDEAL Modules adapted from WAR Child Holland and the GBV adolescent safety and wellbeing.7. Trained community volunteers provide sensitizations to children and caregivers and the community on Covid 19 preventive and risk.8. Carry out weekly Child focused Sensitization on COVID19 response and prevention across the targetd IDP camps and communities8. Engage children on Covid 19 round discussions at least once weekly.9. Provide monthly field level technical support to the trained community volunteers to enhance continuous quality delivery of community-based PSS, Child Protection Monitoring, and awareness campaign around child protection concerns, GBV Risk, and Human Rights.10. Sensitizing the community on PSEA and mandatory reporting in MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Damboa LGAs.11. Provide leadership or support to leadership of LGA level CPSS coordination via convening monthly meetings of both the CP Actors, Camp Management and all relevant community stakeholders in MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge and Damboa. 12. Facilitate the update of referral pathways in the LGAs and community level through the Child Protection Subsector.13. Consultation with and advocacy to relevant community stakeholders on the relevance of the establishment of community-based child protection monitoring and advocacy mechanisms in the LGAs14. Establishment of community-based child protection monitoring and advocacy mechanism (desk and groups) in camps and communities in MMC Jere, Konduga, Kala/Balge, and Damboa LGAs
