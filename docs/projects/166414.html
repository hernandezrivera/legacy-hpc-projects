Project Summary

IOM will respond to the COVID-19 crisis in both the Rohingya camps and the host communities within Cox's Bazar District through a community-led engagement approach that places affected populations at the forefront of the response. Firstly, IOM will conduct large scale consultations across the affected populations to ensure different perspectives and needs of men, women, boys and girls are reflected within all sectors of the response. IOM will also launch an interactive-voice-response (IVR) hotline to disseminate information and collect feedback from the affected populations. IOM will support this and other awareness raising work with content that is developed through engagement and participation from the affected population. Finally, IOM will support local community-led initiatives to respond to the COVID-19 crisis at all stages of the response.

Needs

COVID-19 poses a major threat to existing means of conducting community engagement and outreach work. Traditional means of communication and engagement with communities will need to be significantly reworked to ensure the distribution and provision of COVID-19 related information and messages. Under utilized means of communication, such as phone calls, need to be incorporated into the response if communications campaigns are to be effective at safely reaching everyone. There is also a large need to better understand the population's perceptions of healthcare and the overall COVID-19 response in order to ensure accountability to affected populations. Many different groups of Rohingya and host communities will struggle to access information and may have specific cultural needs related to the COVID-19 response that need to be consulted and engaged with. There are also community groups in need of COVID-19 support that need basic materials and information in order to improve their own response efforts.

Activities or outputs

Communication with Communities & Risk Communications will form a core part of IOM’s COVID-19 response. As long as possible, IOM will continue to conduct routine consultations with affected populations in host and Rohingya communities to better understand their perceptions of COVID-19, needs related to operational changes, concerns and requirements of health facilities, and other impacts of the COVID-19 health response. This will ensure that the voices of affected populations are heard at all stages of the response. Their perspectives will be provided to all operational actors and the wider Inter-Sector Coordination Group to help mainstream community engagement through the response. This work will also directly inform the development of content and engagement strategies by IOM’s CwC team itself. IOM will also continue to conduct community engagement and awareness raising activities in accordance with COVID-19 guidelines, limiting group size and maintaining social distancing within these activities. Awareness raising efforts will also include IOM produced Information Education Communication (IEC) content on rumours, beliefs and perceptions related to COVID-19 and key information disseminated by the response to prevent transmission, respond to outbreaks and ensure the protection of the most vulnerable. Additionally, IOM CwC will support Rohingya-organized groups within the camp to conduct their own outreach and COVID-19 related projects across the response and hold routine engagements with different groups to ensure coordination and information exchange between them and other humanitarian actors in the response. CwC ensure the dissemination of lifesaving information and response related information by establishing an interactive-voice-response (IVR) platform that will disseminate information to both Rohingya and host communities across the response. These messages will be recorded in local languages and provide a two-way communication capability, allowing affected people to record their feedback, questions and concerns. The IVR platform will be supported by enumerators from IOM’s Needs and Population Monitoring unit, who will help transcribe messages and respond to phone queries provided by affected populations. The platform will be used to support different communication needs of IOM’s programs and sectors across the response and be equipped to act as a cyclone-early warning system and to support protection activities aimed at addressing the needs of the most vulnerable populations.
