Project Summary

In this project, Sadad aims to continue providing GBV services in the WGSSs established in Idleb (Darkoush) and in Aleppo (Afrin) and establishing new community centers in Aleppo ( Sheikh El-Hadid) and in Idleb (Sarmin). The community centers aim to provide a space where men, women, boys, and girls can socialize and strengthen previously fractured social networks to ensure that a greater number of people have access to the information related to GBV, CP and GP services in their communities. The planned number of centers is (4) where women and girls can socialize and gain access to lifesaving GBV services, including case management, PSS for survivors focusing on vulnerable groups such as divorced, widows, females with disabilities, as well as targeting male survivors of sexual violence. Also, they will gain access to other GBV activities such Life Skills, Free spaces for women and girls, vocational Training, Awareness raising by using (GBV-SC toolkit), training of Humanitarian actors (non-specialist) on GBV risk mitigation (Sadad will take advantage of the inter-agency capacity development initiatives) In addition to providing direct support service and referrals to other services according to the referral paths that are shared by the sub-clusters and creating a service mapping through coordination with actors in the area, continuously updated.
Sadad will also provide Community-based protection services to ensure that IDPs and host community are effectively involved in the project lifecycle from design to implementation to feedback through the already established protected feedback channels to help them report suggestions, complaints, and PSEA concerns (Sadad is member in PSEA Network). Enhance equitable access to quality Child Protection services including parental skills, structured PSS, PSS, capacity building, awareness raising, Case management & Referrals for children survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect, child labor, early marriage, children exposed to severe/extreme psychosocial distress (8350 beneficiaries including (4250 IDPs and 4100 HC) will be targeted with the project’s activities including 4,500 Females, 3,850 Males (600 Elderly, 2,800 Adults, 4,100 Children, 300 Young Children, and 550 PwD). Legal services to increase legal awareness among affected population on how to access CSD and HLP documentation including provision of individual legal assistance. Protection monitoring service through conducting service mapping and establishing referral pathways and reporting on protection concerns and issues.
The proposed intervention will be implemented with particular emphasis on quality services to People with Disabilities (PwDs), PwDs will be targeted through center and community AR program, specialized services, and supported by transportation from and to the WGSSs which will be rehabilitated to be inclusive for PwDs.inaddtion to elderly women and men.
Sadad will consult with the community members at the beginning of the project to introduce the project activities and have them involved in the selection of the community center's locations, as well as Sadad will establish community committees from the same communities and train them on protection topics such as; referral and communication skills to enable them identify and refer people in need to the case management team.
The activities will emphasize equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social specificities of targeted populations.
the project will consider the environmental situation from the perspective of children/youth with disabilities and the individual needs for all of them ,The communities centers will be provided with disabilities facilitation;“e.g. ramps, toilets.
Sadad will use the M&E tool kit to monitor the GBV activities, Sadad will monitor the quality of the project also, through KAP surveys and semi-annual FGDs.

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2021, around 13.4 million people are estimated to require some form of humanitarian and protection assistance, including 6 million in acute need, 25% of them have disabilities and 75% of them are women and children. According to Sadad need assessment that conducted, childing dropout of schools and child marriage are the most common violations occurring. 91.5% of the KIs in Aleppo and Idleb reported that child labour prevents children to attend schools, also, more than 23% of Aleppo KIs confirmed that B&G experiences psychological distress. According to the Voices 2021, 62% of communities confirmed that cases of early marriage affected adolescent girls between the ages of 12 and 17, and 62% of communities surveyed agreed that adolescent girls between the ages of 15-17 drop out of school in order to work, while that number jumped to 84% in relation to adolescent boys of the same age. As for MSNA Key Informant Protection Needs Assessment level for HNO 2021, in Aleppo 45.1% of the KIS reported Lack/Loss of civil documentation while 73.4% in Idleb reported Lack/Loss of civil documentation.According to CP Situation Monitoring Annual Report 2020, 79.90% of KIs reported children are in need for focused non-specialized services, 44.37% reported for the need of the focused specialized services, 85.64% reported that children are engaged in some types of work that prevents them from going to school. According to the Voices 2021, Economic deprivation, lack of livelihood opportunities, discrimination, exclusion, and socio-cultural norms have led to the continued normalization of GBV and severely impacted the psychosocial wellbeing and continued erosion of women’s and girls’ rights, including those with disabilities.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Violate PSEA or CSG, CoC principles. Mitigation: - Signing PSEA and child safeguarding Sadad’s policy by all staff. - getting training on PSEA for all staff. - PSEA awareness through brochures, videos. - Assigning complaint mechanism for SEA issues. policies of Code of conduct, PSEA, child safeguarding is mandatory for all staff and vendors working with Sadad. Risk: Lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. Lack of quality services also lead to fewer disclosures and lack of trust in services Likelihood: High. Impact: High Mitigating measures: 1. Service mapping including only those that can provide survivor centred services2. Promotion of SOPs roll out for all staff of organizations involved in the referral Risk: Persons with disability have difficult accessing protection services Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: Ensure all CCs staff are properly trained on how to identify persons with disability in need of treatment for a timely referral to relevant services, Provide transportation support for persons with disability. Include persons with disability in all conducted assessments at the level of CCs and/or community to understand their needs and gaps in their access to services.

Copies of the policy will be made available and accessible to all stakeholders, both in hard copy and electronically in order to ensure that the policy commitments, principles, and practices are publicized and that everyone is clear about Sadad position in relation to PSEA and safeguarding children.
