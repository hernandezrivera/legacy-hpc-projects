Project Summary

It is estimated that there are 2.9 million internally displaced persons in Somalia in over 2,400 sub-standard and unplanned IDP sites in urban, semi-urban and rural areas across the country. Conflict, drought, COVID-19 and flooding are some of the main shocks that drive displacement and largely affect resulted in an enduring humanitarian crisis in Somalia that demands urgent lifesaving interventions. Concomitantly, the protracted nature of this crisis requires a strategy that sees beyond the immediate needs and incorporates durable solutions throughout the response. With this project CESDO will provide camp management and camp coordination services to men, women, boys and girls in displacement sites in the districts of Baidoa, Hudur, Wajid, Dinsoor, Barawe, Marka, Afgooye, Beletweyne, Jowhar, Dhuusamareeb and Mogadishu to ensure that the most urgent needs of the IDPs are met, while also setting the foundation for the achievement of durable solutions.
The project will focus on Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) activities undertaken in planned or unplanned settlements across South-Central Somalia. Through this project, CESDO will reach a total of 15,000 HHs (95,000 individuals - 50% females) with necessary services through; CESDO will Strengthen safe access to multi sectorial services at site level through improved site management and coordination, Improve living conditions of displaced people through site development, care and maintenance and decongestion initiatives, Strengthen community self-management and access to information for displaced populations and Support opportunities for displaced people to achieve durable solutions.
CESDO will ensure disability inclusion at all stages of the project, Gender will be mainstreamed into the project through the specific consideration of the different needs of women, men, boys and girls during the design of activities. The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis-segregated data.

1. - Projects activities

CESDO will continue CCCM activities in Baidoa/Berdale, Jowhar and Beletweyne and start new Camp Coordination & Camp Management in Hudur, Wajid, Dinsoor, Barawe, Marka, Afgooye, Dhuusmareeb and Mogadishu to set up CCCM coordination structures in collaboration with appropriate government counterparts, improve the coordination of service providers, conducting site monitoring and service mapping to identify gaps, Conduct site verifications on bi-annual basis, conducting community-led site maintenance activities to ensure the upkeep of sites (cash for work, site maintenance committees, distribution of tools), supporting governance structures to ensure community participation, and self-management of sites, provide core trainings (CMC Training Package) to community governance structures and information management at a site level, site improvement activities to minimize protection risks and ensure safety in sites and safety audits to identify site risks. Establish and maintain inter-sector complaints feedback mechanisms (CFMs) at the IDP site-level and conduct site decongestion activities to minimize protection risks at target sites.CESDO will also conduct mapping exercises on existing community structures. Settlement planning training will be conducted to improve accessibility in site and community led site maintenance activities will be supported to ensure upkeep of sites (cash for work, site maintenance committees) through cash for work to improve emergency sites to minimize protection risks and ensure safety in sites (flood mitigation, fire prevention).

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Risk that marginalized groups do not participate in consultations: a. CESDO will gather data (age, sex, diversity) of communities and beneficiaries for effective and informative programming. This will help identify vulnerable and marginalized persons for planning, inclusion purposes and engagement b. A systematic outreach process will be done to pre-register marginalized/minority vulnerable groups to avoid exclusion c. Efforts will be made to ensure that information regarding provision Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) services will be disseminated to all members of communities (including women, girls, boys, men) through appropriate communication channels such as an inclusive feedback mechanism that will include all beneficiaries will be put in place which will help to evaluate selection criteria and overall services gaps. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance or violence during/after registration or distribution. Services at IDP sites will be put in central, visible places and less than 500 meters away from settlements while the locations will be decided following consultations with women and girls as this will help to protect them from risks of all forms of GBV when accessing services. In close consultations with partners, solar light installations will be done near community centres and services to deter any risk of violence against women and girls and distributions will be done during day light to allow beneficiaries who are often women and girls reach their homes on time.CESDO will support the establishment of inclusive community governance structures where there are no governance structures and will work with HLP partners to ensure that the sites have their eviction status mapped and relevant advocacy with government authorities are carried out. The community leaders and respective committees will be fully involved in project design, implementation, monitoring and representation and that the community committees are inclusive and that segments of the communities are well represented at all levels such as age and gender diversity.
