Project Summary

The overall security situation in Tribal Districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has markedly improved. Over 1.6 million people who were initially displaced in KP and other provinces have returned to FATA. Access to health care in the return areas of FATA is poor. Preliminary findings from (Ex) FATA Vulnerability Assessment exercise conducted in August/September 2017 revealed that 64% of the population in FATA Agency and FR Areas faced difficulties in accessing health care facilities. The mean distance travelled by the population to the nearest health facility in FATA Agency is 7 km and in Frontier Region (FR) Areas is 19.5 km. Out of the women who had a child in the last three years, 24% reported that they didn’t seek any medical help or services during their pregnancy. A significant proportion (40%) reported delivery at home, mostly with the assistance of traditional midwife (Dai). Around 20% of the households from FATA and 15% from FR areas reported that their eligible children were not vaccinated during routine EPI. Around half of the respondents (51% in FATA and 46% in FR areas) reported that during the last three months, they were unable to seek medical help even it was needed. Key reasons include the unavailability of relevant staff, service and financial constraint. Objectivesto: Improve access to essential health care services
