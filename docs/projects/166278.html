Project Summary

This project is aimed at providing an all inclusive basic quality education for 6000 out of school children due to COVID-19 in Monguno LGA in Borno State using alternative home learning remedies.
The outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic in Nigeria has affected the entire education system forcing children to stay home with the closure of schools by the government in an attempt to control the spread of the virus.
The goal of this project therefore seek to complement the Nigeria Education response which aimed at increasing awareness against the spread of COVID-19 among school learners, teachers and schools stakeholder in North East Nigeria, providing alternative learning programs for affected children to mitigate the school closure's negative impact on children’s learning and teacher well being and to support mechanisms that ensure effective, inclusive and safe return to quality learning for learners,parents/caregivers, teachers and SBMCs after COVID-19.
The approach shall contribute to State level radio teaching programmes(SUBEB) and provide digital learning Packages through recorded radio teaching Podcasts( including COVID-19 key IPC messages) to 6000 learners, develop and distribute learning materials for learning at home, distribute 1000 digital/solar radios to 1000 vulnerable households, provide Cash incentives and Voucher Assistance(CVA) to 1000 vulnerable Households to support food and Non- Food items, provide livelihoods support (ie. Incentive, Food, NFI or Cash.) to 200 volunteer and Quranic teachers in Monguno LGA, contribute in developing a toll free mobile platform that support bulk-SMS for the dissemination of messages on COVID-19 and general information on education to Teachers, Learners,SBMCS and community members.
The intervention shall also support back to school sensitisation activities and provide Wash, Learning materials and school uniforms, support agency coordination and capacity building on IPC, Teaching methodologies and MHPSS and General Protection for Teachers, SBMCs and education authorities.

1 - Needs

The outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic and it rapid spread in Nigeria has affected the entire education system forcing children to stop schooling and to stay home with the closure of schools by the government in an attempt to control the spread of the virus.The closure of schools affect close to 46 million students throughout the country, 4.2 million students in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe States in North East Nigeria. With the existing complexities of children accessing education in the northeast, about 400,000 IDP children attending some form of learning in the camps and host communities have already been affected by the closure of schools. Prior to the outbreak,The decade-long conflict in Northeast Nigeria weakened the already weak education system as the crisis pushed more children out of school via the systematic destruction of the education system. More children have been exposed to horrific forms of violence,abuse and exploitation which has negative effects on their psychosocial well being. Our effort in complementing the goal of Nigeria Education In Emergencies working Group remains preparedness and response plan seek to reduce morbidity and mortality due to COVID-19 among school learners, teachers and schools stakeholder in North East Nigeria, mitigate the school closure negative impact on children learning and teacher well being and and ensure effective, inclusive and safe return to quality learning for learners, teachers and SBMCs after the outbreak.The project shall target 6000 community children living in IDPs and host communities in Monguno LGA who were forced out of school due to COVID-19 as a way of providing them with alternative learning opportunities that will enable them to continue normal schooling after the outbreak. Our focus shall contribute to provide incentives for State level radio teaching programmes(SUBEB) and provide digital learning Packages through recorded radio teaching Podcasts( including COVID-19 key IPC messages) to 6000 learners, develop and distribute learning materials for learning at home, distribute 1000 digital/solar radios to 1000 vulnerable households, provide Cash incentives and Voucher Assistance(CVA) to 1000 vulnerable Households to support food and Non- Food items, provide livelihoods support (ie. Incentive, Food, NFI or Cash.) to 200 volunteer and Quranic teachers in Monguno LGA, contribute in developing a toll free mobile platform that support bulk-SMS for the dissemination of messages on COVID-19 and general information on education to Teachers, Learners,SBMCS and community members.The intervention shall also support back to school sensitization activities and provide Wash, Learning materials and school uniforms, support agency coordination and capacity building on IPC, Teaching methodologies and MHPSS and General Protection for Teachers, SBMCs and education authorities in Monguno LGA in Borno State, North East Nigeria.
