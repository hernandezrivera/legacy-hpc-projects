Project Summary

The project's main goal is to provide and increase access to quality, integrated essential, lifesaving health services through supporting 21 Health facilities in different locations with essential running costs and supplies along with increasing the capacity of staff in order to raise the sustainability level.
It is worth mentioning that the integration of health services is crucial in providing Mental Health, Nutrition, and Community awareness along with GBV case management.
Through 14 supported Primary Health Care centers, 5 of them Basic Emergency Obstetric Care centers, in addition to 4 Secondary Health Care facilities (Comprehensive Emergency Obstetric Care centers), 1 TB center, and 2 Hemodialysis centers, SRD will provide support to all beneficiaries in need for health services with the support of one Referral desk and an ambulatory system consisting of 24 ambulances and 14 EPI teams.
Furthermore, SRD is committed to applying the Infection Prevention and Control protocols and measures to be able to respond to any outbreaks. This will be through supporting the IPC team in addition to an IPC laboratory and 2 incinerators in order to ensure have proper management of medical wastes.
Social inclusion for People with Disability is mandatory in all supported activities by ensuring that all facilities are well prepared and the staff are trained on Social Inclusion training.
