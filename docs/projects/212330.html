Project Summary

Intervention in the Deir Ezzor countryside in the areas of livelihoods, food security, community awareness support, and youth capacity building is considered a revival and support for the region’s sustainable economic and social recovery through:
• Empowering rural families, especially women who are breadwinners for their families, professionally and economically by building their capacities in the field of food industries.
• Empowering youth in the professional field through vocational courses.
• Building the capacities of youth in computer use.
• Holding pioneering courses that help improve the efficiency of beneficiaries in developing project ideas, implementing them, and managing them.
• Supporting pioneering projects that fully rely on the use of renewable energy.
• Supporting community initiatives that have a tangible impact on the residents of the targeted villages.
• Raising the level of agricultural awareness through training on modern farming methods.
• Raising the level of community awareness through awareness workshops.
Takatof shababeAssociation suggests implementing a project that supports livelihoods, food security, and activates the role of women in rural communities in the villages of the western countryside in Deir Ezzor in the following areas.
• Food industries: Food industry training (dairy and cheese - sweets - popular foods - food manufacturing and preservation) is offered
• Vocational training: Vocational training (carpentry - blacksmithing - home electricity - solar energy - painting) .
• Agricultural training: A package of training that supports value chains such as (beekeeping - compost manufacturing - fodder manufacturing) and supports modern agricultural methods to adapt to climate changes is offered
• Capacity building: A package of training such as (basics of computer - entrepreneurship - life skills - designing community initiatives - Office advanced)
• Awareness workshops: A package of workshops that contribute to raising awareness in rural communities such as (social gender - gender-based violence - prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse - climate changes - recycling) is offered.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The expected results from implementing the project:• Increase opportunities to obtain sustainable income sources, especially for families with disabled members.• Enhance the ability of communities, especially vulnerable groups, to withstand challenges resulting from climate change on the one hand and the crisis on the other.• Raise the level of local output in the targeted areas by supporting the manufacture of products with a competitive advantage in the market.• Improve the level of services provided in the labor market associated with vocational training.• Build the capacities of youth in the targeted area through training courses.• Generate new ideas in the labor market and create added value (value chain).• Enhance the access of small producers to the labor market and research in investing technological development to enhance their access to markets and enhance their returns.• Raise the level of social awareness of issues of violence, climate change, and benefit from agricultural waste.• Food industries: Food industry training (dairy and cheese - sweets - popular foods - food manufacturing and preservation) is offered and the training aims to:o Support food security and easy access to food.o Provide temporary job opportunities through cash for work.• Vocational training: Vocational training (carpentry - blacksmithing - home electricity - solar energy - painting) is held to:o Develop their professional capacities and integrate them into the labor market.o Enhance job opportunities and fruitful participation in local economic recovery.• Agricultural training: A package of training that supports value chains such as (beekeeping - compost manufacturing - fodder manufacturing) and supports modern agricultural methods to adapt to climate changes is offered and the training aims to:o Improve the level of agricultural production.o Improve the standard of living in rural areas and increase job opportunities and improve food security.o Improve the quality of agricultural products and increase their marketing value.o Increase farmers’ income and improve the economy of the region in general.o Support the optimal exploitation of resources (agricultural and animal waste) and water resources.• Capacity building: A package of training such as (basics of computer - entrepreneurship - life skills - designing community initiatives - Office advanced) is offered where:o Pioneering projects with economic and social feasibility will be supported.o Initiatives with a tangible community impact will be supported in light of the project’s goals.• Awareness workshops: A package of workshops that contribute to raising awareness in rural communities such as (social gender - gender-based violence - prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse - climate changes - recycling) is offered.
