Project Summary

UOSSM France strategic plan adheres to Health Cluster objective 1# provides lifesaving and life-sustaining services to affected people in NWS, by supporting Primary and Secondary Health care services in three primary health care centers, 2 mobile clinics for nine months (March to December 2021), and Arrahma Hospital for four months (September-December 2022) being supported now by OCHA in partnership with Space of Peace NGO. UOSSM France has coordinated with the health cluster and Idleb technical Health Directorate of the Syrian Interim Government to identify the prioritized services and locations to be supported. The health cluster has performed a prioritization exercise based on a request from UOSSM France to confirm the priority of Primary and Secondary Health care services at Barisha, Azmarin, Ein-Shib PHCs, and 2 Mobile clinics in Jish-AshShugur and Ariha districts in addition to Ar Rahma Hospital in Darkosh/Jisr-Ash-Shugur district.
Through this project, UOSSM France will set the following outputs under the overall and specific objective:
• Primary (integrated with MHPSS) and Secondary Health care services are operated and functional for four to nine months through skilled medical staff.
The quality of services delivered by UOSSM France through this project is enhanced as per WHO, UNFPA, health cluster, UNICEF, Primary and Secondary Healthcare cluster, and UOSSM France’s policies and guidelines under the re-visited ESHP. To achieve this output, UOSSM France will coordinate with all partners and stakeholders to deliver training sessions on the relevant policies and guidelines, including Re-visited EHSP of WHO, Minimum Initial Service Package MISP of Reproductive Health RH in emergency settings of UNFPA, Infant and Young Children Feeding IYCF, Primary and Secondary Health care CMAM, and UOSSM France/DHO supply line guidelines. UOSSM France will coordinate with the health cluster to deliver a training session on the Health Information System HIS of the health cluster to ensure the precise and accurate reporting mechanisms to the donors and stakeholders.
Concerning Ar Rahma Hospital, UOSSM France ensures service provision continuity at Ar Rahma (Darkoush) Hospital: HeRAMS code: “G0704003!Arrahma Surgical Hospital”. The hospital is located in Darkosh community/subdistrict, Jisr-Ash-Shughur District, Idleb governorate. The hospital is one of the leading referral hospitals (besides Bab Al Hawa Hospital) in Idleb governorate.
UOSSM France has communicated with the hospital's management to coordinate a future plan, including capacity and needs assessment and fund obtaining. UOSSM France has signed an authorization letter with the hospital to get better access to donors, partners, and stakeholders.
Through this project UOSSM France (DHO) will support Al Rahma Hospital to continue the provision of surgical procedures and interventions of the following specialties:
Surgical procedures including ENT, General Surgery, including caesarian, Neurosurgery, Thoracic Surgery, Orthopedic Surgery, Uro surgery, in addition to the internal medicine department: internal Thoracic, cardiology, and gastrologic services.
UOSSM France will provide essential primary healthcare services, including Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI), Non-Communicable Diseases (NCD), Reproductive Health (RH), and nutrition services. Health services are provided according to the re-visited EHSP 2019 endorsed by WHO and the health cluster. Furthermore, through these facilities, UOSSM France will provide Clinical Management of Rape (CMR) linked to the GBV-Health referral network and the PHC is equipped well, Communicable Diseases (CD), Infant and Young Children Feeding (IYCF), Community-based Management of Malnutrition CMAM, Lab, medicines distribution, referral services integrated with MHGAP/MHPSS services in three PHCs and Arrahma hospital.

4 - Needs

Compared to 2020, the health sector People in Need (PiN) has increased three percent with the largest absolute change seen in Idleb Governorate where the PiN increased by more than 200,000 people (HNO 2021). UOSSM France aims to provide Primary and Secondary Health care services at three PHCs (Barisha PHC, Ein-Shibb and Azmarin PHCs), in addition to 2 MCs in Jisr-Ashshugur and Ariha in addition to Ar Rahma Hospital in Darkosh/Jisr-Ash-Shugur. By November 2021, there were no primary health services provided in Barisha Community, so UOSSM France partially supported Barisha PHC and one MC in two locations (MC paths). During the last three months, UOSSM France provided 7875 consultations through partial functioning of Barisha PHC and MC. There is a health facility in Barisha community (with a catchment population as of 39113 of whom 18537 are IDPs), but it is partially functional. In addition to providing services according to the re-visited EHSP/WHO standards. Meanwhile, there are still some people cut-off services in scattered communities (MC 1 and MC 2 paths) with a total population up to 21,806 including 4,746 IDPs. Thus, MC1 will provide services to 5183 population in six communities in Jabal Alzawiyah (Ariha district). While MC2 will target five communities in Jabal Westani (Jisr-As-Shugur district) with a total population up to 16623.Al Rahma (Darkoush>jisr-Ash-Shugur district>Idleb) Hospital provides health care services for a catchment population of around 300,726.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented throughout three phases: i. Preparation phase: Within the first week of the project, the first order for medicines will be prepared and submitted to be performed; to save time, efforts and maximize the value of money taking into account the quality of items and reliability of available providers duly. A recruitment plan will be set up within the first week of the project and will be performed within one month. Additionally, a training plan will be set in the first month to ensure all medical and paramedical staff received the packages of trainings according to the revisited EHSP, UNFPA, UNICEF and UOSSM France guidelines, each staff will receive the package of training relevant to their specialties. ii. Implementation phase: Through this phase, the facility will continue functioning to provide primary health care and nutrition services to the beneficiaries, and distributing medicines, pharmaceuticals and nutritional complements. iii. Exit phase: starting the last quarter of the project UOSSM FRANCE will communicate closely with health cluster, its partners, and Idleb Health Directorate to explore the available opportunities to secure the fund for the facility and/or set up a proper exit plan. All data will be collected using tools designed by the health information management department

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The following risks were identified based on UOSSM France joint experience working in NWS since:1. Risk: Limited/ interrupted access to intervention areas due increased insecurity and/ or loss of access due to change in control. Mitigating actions: 1) Close monitoring and participation to info-sharing mechanisms and meetings 2) Security related SOPs in place; 3) Remote monitoring mechanisms in place; 4) Maintain certain level of flexibility to suspend the work partially in the hospital when possible; 5) Ensure accurate inventory of assets within the hospital and establish plans to relocate assets as necessary; assess security of area and staff and take action to cease or continue support based on risks; 8) Ensure the maintenance of the humanitarian standards and the neutral positioning.. Risk: COVID-19 spread among staff and beneficiaries. Mitigating action: 1) Reduce number of staff at facilities/clinics or work on a rotation plan; 2) Provide adequate PPE supplies and enhance IPC measures at clinics; 3) Implement social distancing measures in waiting areas and entry point screening; 4) Suspend activities requiring gathering of beneficiaries or break the beneficiary groups to smaller ones. Improve relationships with all stakeholders through seminars and training processes that may have targeted them. Solving outstanding problems early and not ignoring them to prevent their negative development.
