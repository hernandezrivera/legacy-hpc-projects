Project Summary

1 - Needs

Over the course of two years ago all Darfur states and people going to live in stability and security situation is safe , the cause is all operation armed stop from all parties, in the meantime political change has been made and a new transitional government established . although the economic situation and inflation still in high rate ,according to the Humanitarian Need overview 2019 , people in need is 5.7 million and this base on highest figure , people targeted is 4.4 million , in center Darfur about 388,371 people in need and south Darfur 537,02. Accordingly to the general situation Darfur and all conflict areas will witnessing stability over upcoming years and these reason will allow for Child protection actors to access and provide quality services for peoples on effected target .despite the situation going to the better particularly on conflict zone but the IDPs they are facing violence and attack , there are many accidents regularly happen in Jebal Marra especially in Nertiti and east jebel Marra and south Darfur some time ago witnessed an accidents took place in different locationHRP and HNO 2019 , 5.7 million people in humanitarian need including children 2.6 million require humanitarian assistance ,services and protection support in Sudan as well as people targeted is 4.4 million . all those figure include (protection, child protection and GBV & SGBV) these figures categorized by 70% female and desegregated by age 70% children(< 18 years), 30% adult (18-59 years) , elderly (>59 year). The target group of the protection project disturbed two state ,south Darfur ( East Jebal Marra, Murshing and Beleil) and central Darfur (Nertiti and Zalingei localities ).this project aim to mobilizing community in different localities in two states in order reduce child protection issue and combat GBV throughout strengthening the mechanism of community mobilizing and advocacy .All need assessment carried out in recent years in center Darfur and South Darfur indicated that, child protection and GBV issue needed to be address, on period between 10 -15 September 2017 up to 25-29 April, 2018 there are different need assessment conducted in Nertiti and East Jebal Marra and those resulted , no Community Based Protection Network( CBPNs). Child labor , birth registration issue , child marriage and child leading house hold also GBV reported

2 - Activities / Outputs

Activities: 1- Organize 12 TOT training of 5 days on child protection and GBV, women rights, child rights for a total of 300 participants from the following groups: ( women leaders, community leaders, PTA, religious leaders, teachers, youth associations leaders ,midwives , artistes and media personnel ).2- Conduct 60 awareness raising sessions on child protection and GBV issue for a total of 5000 students in secondary and primary schools in 5 localities in South Darfur and Center Darfur .3- Organize 60 coffee meetings with a total of 1500 women in7 localities to discuss child protection topic and GBV issues among their communities.4- Conduct 50 drama show to educate and send acknowledge & information for community to combat GBV and address child protection in 5 localities in South Darfur and Center Darfur. 5- Publish 50 Radio episodes by using Arabic and local languages to contribute of reducing child protection and preventing GBV and SGBV.6- Conduct vulnerability assessment to identify the person with especial needs and their needs in targets areas. Indicator :Indicator - A number of workshop on child protection and GBV, women rights, child rights for a total of 300 participants from the following groups: ( women leaders, community leaders, PTA, religious leaders, teachers, youth associations leaders ,midwives , artistes and media personnel will be conducted - A number of raising awareness session on child protection and GBV issue for a total of 8000 students in secondary and primary schools in 7 localities in South Darfur and Center Darfur will be conducted - A number of coffee meeting with women with a total of 1500 women in7 localities to discuss child protection topic and GBV issues among their communities will be conducted . - A number of 50 drama show to educate and send acknowledge & information for community to combat GBV and address child protection in 7 localities in South Darfur and Center Darfur will be conducted - Publish 50 Radio episodes by using Arabic and local languages to contribute of reducing child protection and preventing GBV( domestic violence, and abuse , rape and SGBV will be conducted - A number of vulnerability assessment to identify the person with especial needs and their needs in targets areas will be conducted
