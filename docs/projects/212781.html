Project Summary

The countryside of northeastern Syria has suffered from climate change during the last five years, which has affected livestock farming in these countrysides, as it was the means of securing food and livelihood, in addition to being the only and last source for the region’s residents. These rural areas live and secure their daily food. Accordingly, we suggest distributing the sheep. In each of the countrysides of Raqqa - Deir ez-Zor - Al-Hasakah, families of people with disabilities and women with breadwinners will be targeted as a priority in this project, as rural women are considered pioneers in these works and are currently their only source of income and hope for securing their livelihood in light of the surrounding circumstances. The idea of the project is to target 300 families, distributing 3 sheep to each family in addition to sufficient fodder for these sheep for a period of 3 months, with veterinary follow-up by specialists for the necessary vaccination operations and providing advice and support to the beneficiaries to exploit the share according to the goal and purpose of its distribution, in addition to carrying out awareness campaigns about livestock in addition to To lectures on the role of women in society and urging them to play their role and fully integrate into society, as they have become more than half of Syrian society, and to enhance their self-confidence.
First, the employees in the project will be selected according to the approved policies and the required certificates and will be given to allocating a role for women in the project staff. Then an electronic form will be designed for the process of registering the beneficiaries, after which selection will be made according to the project’s specific criteria in conjunction with the qualification and training of the staff especially on the protection policy in the event that there are no number of beneficiaries within the project Standards in the areas: Proposing new and nearby areas will be discussed in accordance with the context, as the need assessment that we carried out shows the extent of the need for the proposed areas and the areas close to them as they were all subjected to the same damage. As for the approvals there is no objection from the local authorities as they discussed the need and proposed solutions, and they did not If there is any objection to the project or the targeting areas regarding the project’s objectives, a form will be attached to the beneficiary obligating him to exploit the share according to the goal that was determined. In addition, the teams will continue their field visits to teach the beneficiaries about modern sustainability methods, which will make the beneficiaries see with their own eyes the methods of dealing with livestock and explain to them the production mechanism. Making good use of the distributed share, in addition to making them aware of the necessity of supporting the reproduction of livestock to achieve greater benefit.
The Authority supports any discussion or amendments to the project that are appropriate to the context

Through the support provided, activities related to the livelihoods of beneficiaries of both sexes will be restored and will support all age groups to earn a decent life that they were forced to leave due to the crisis and climate fluctuations. The idea of the project also enriches rural women with the work they grew up doing and doing since their childhood and does not expose them to any kind of harassment. Or exploitation, as she will secure her livelihood in a sound way and will be given priority during the evaluation and support processes, in addition to analyzing her suffering with the aim of proposing subsequent solutions, as the suffering in the countryside is greater than the suffering of women in the rest of the regions.All contracts concluded with the Authority include an attached copy of the beneficiary protection behavior, response and reporting protocols (physical abuse - violence - gender-based violence - exploitation - discrimination - any violation of the beneficiary protection policy and specifications that puts the beneficiary in direct danger - the employee also has the right to Protection - PSEA) All new and old employees also receive training courses on beneficiary protection and the policy and code of conduct and encourage them to be treated equally regardless of gender, nationality, race, political or religious beliefs, especially with regard to women, protecting them and supporting their rights and people with special needs.
