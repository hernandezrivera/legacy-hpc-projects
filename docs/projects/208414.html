Project Summary

The intervention aims at strengthening the general protection of the crisis-affected through strengthening local protection system including building capacities of local actors to respond to protection concerns, strengthening humanitarian protection actors to deliver quality protection interventions including standard protection standards, provision of specialized protection services including PSS to persons with protection concerns, enhance access to case management services ( identification, registration, referral ) protection monitoring, supply of protection core materials to beneficiaries to persons with protection concerns. In addition, local protection actors capacities shall also be strengthened on protection principles. Furthermore, women and girls access to Gender based violence prevention and response services shall be carried out to end gender based violence against crisis-affected girls and women. Through right-based, user-centered approach, the program shall adopt digital to expand rights holders (women and girls) access to gender-based violence prevention information and response services. however, women and girls at risk/survivors of gender-based violence access to justice shall be enhanced through empowerment activities including improved knowledge and small grants to fulfil their potentials, end gender-based violence including forceful early marriages, female genital mutilation and cultural harmful practices.

1 - Needs

The consultation with all the relevant beneficiaries young men, men, women and girls across diversity via mixed methodologies ( qualitative and quantitative approaches) reported crisis-affected face grave rights violation issues, crisis-affected women and girls of poor households are at rights of gender-based violence ( sexual exploitation and abuse), adolescent girls are exposed to negative livelihoods. Crisis-affected men, women and girls needs core protection materials, affected communities needs need protection mechanisms, local community heads need improved knowledge on protection standard principles, right-holders ( women and girls) need information on GBV service delivery, improved knowledge on GBV prevention and response services to end GBV, There is need to utilize media such as local radios to reach right holders and duties bearers with GBV awareness prevention messages to end harmful practices including forceful early marriages , female genital mutilation. However, women and girls at risk/survivors of gender based violence reported need to access to justice including access to socioeconomic. Furthermore there is also need for policy reforms including advocacy for review of laws, policies as well as implementation of violence against persons prohibition act to end violence against women and girls.

2 - Activities

The key activities under the general protection are as follows: capacity building of local protection actors including local governance structures heads on protection principles and standards, strengthening capacities of humanitarian protection actors on core protection standards including a do no harm, protection monitoring of humanitarian actors through focus groups discussions, interviews during field visits etc. case management including identification, registration, referral as well as building as well as provision of protection specialized services including PSS to persons with protection concerns, registration and referral to service providers; provision of protection core materials to persons with protection concerns. in addition, capacity of community protection structures shall be built on protection standard principles, provision of protection core materials. lastly the program under general protection shall carry protection monitoring by staff during field visits through focus groups discussions etc. Under the Gender based violence, the program will use digital (SMS) to expand young women access to information, participation and leadership to gender-based violence prevention and responses services in established women and girls’ safe spaces (WGSP) In addition, girls and women knowledge shall be improved on gender-based violence prevention to end harmful practices, forceful early marriages and female genital mutilation. However, awareness raising messages through utilization of media such as local radios, television broadcast and community engagement activities, dialogues shall be carried involving women and girls, youths and community heads as agents of change to end violence against girls and women. However, series of advocacies shall be conducted in partnership with national women rights/ led organizations on review of laws, policies as well as implementation of violence against persons prohibition Act. lastly women and girls at risk/survivors shall access justice through provision of livelihoods opportunities including small grants to set up small businesses such local production of dignity kits, re-usable sanitary materials etc to strengthen their participation to the socioeconomic and social reintegration.
