Project Summary

This project aims at addressing the acute winterization needs of the conflict-affected population in Donetska Oblast, both GCA and NGCA. The intervention is in line with the objective defined by the Shelter/NFI Cluster for 2021:
- address critical gaps in the essential shelter and NFI needs of the most vulnerable IDPs and conflict-affected population.
This will be achieved through the implementation of the following activity:
- distribution of solid fuel to the most vulnerable private HHs.

1 - Needs

After six years of the humanitarian response in eastern Ukraine, the conflict still affects thousands of internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host communities. The crisis has prevented the majority of conflict-affected people from recovering their livelihoods, and therefore people are not able to prepare adequately for winter season. Winterisation remains a life-saving need for vulnerable residents on both sides of the LoC, especially in isolated settlements. During the cold winter months, many such HHs depend on coal, wood or other fuel for heating, which are often unavailable or costly. With harsh winter temperatures, heating shortages have a significant impact on the health and well-being of the people. Available evidence suggests that 11 percent of the households reported heating shortages. This rate was particularly high in rural areas at 37 percent. Fuel shortages contribute to nearly a third of households (29 percent) reporting insufficient indoor temperature. At the same time, the planned intervention will pursue to complement the assistance provided by state agencies in GCA and de-facto authorities in NGCA. This winterization assistance will be delivered ahead of the start of the cold season, to allow target groups to plan and prepare.
