4 - Needs

Children are bearing the brunt of the 11-year-old conflict in the Syrian Arab Republic. The scale of children in need of humanitarian assistance is on the rise, increasing 27 percent from 2021 to 2022, with 6.1 million children now affected.Overall, 13.4 million people require humanitarian assistance, up from 11 million in 2020. This includes 3 million people with disabilities and 7 million internally displaced persons (IDPs). This escalation is being driven by a severe economic crisis, mass displacement, a widely devastating public service infrastructure, and the COVID-19 pandemicThe qualitative protection sources are a crucial source of information to indicate how different protection issues are interlinked and should, therefore, be addressed in a holistic manner. The current crisis has interrupted community routing and destroyed many services hence creating a huge humanitarian need. These conditions are re§ecting the suffering and psychological trauma of the IDPs in these areas, especially children aged from(6-14) The Rescue & Relief Team collected information from the residents through our direct contact with them where we found that the IDPs in Dier Ez-Zor are facing harsh conditions and nothing available to protect them from cold and heat inundation to the lack of the minimum basic requirements like protection, privacy, and other non-food items and the children are facing severe psychological stress.
