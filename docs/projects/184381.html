Project Summary

The school closures during the pandemic significantly disrupted children's education in Somalia. Some 1.9 million children currently require access to school. Declining enrollment could lead to a serious deterioration in education outcomes and impede children's learning over the long-term. Post Gu’ FSNAU report released in mid Aug 2021, show over three million people are in need of humanitarian assistance. Shocks related to floods, COVID-19 pandemic and infestation of Desert locust in parts of the country worsened the situation for vulnerable communities who have not fully recovered. Women and children (especially girls) are the most affected population in Somalia. The national Education Cluster, puts over 1.4 million school age children in of support. The nation-wide closure of schools due to COVID-19 has led the Education Cluster and its partners to alter their response. This included reducing face-to-face trainings and suspending transitional learning spaces (TLS ) construction. However, many activities are similar to the usual EiE response but delivered through different modalities. Significant changes have included the development of distance learning programs (Radio, online, TV), remote PSS through head teachers’ network, increased focus on WASH rehabilitation and less focus on general TLS construction.
SOYDA aims to undertake education in emergency intervention for 3,000 children (of whom 1500 will be girls, and 1500 boys) with 15% of the beneficiaries anticipated to be CWD. The project will facilitate access and continuity learning for crises affected children in 2 districts of Benadir (Daynile and Kahda) and 2 districts of Lower shabelle (Merka and Afgooye) within protective environment. SOYDA will also document and report learning facilities damaged and/or attacked to cluster for action.
The project will enhanced school feeding to the 3,000 learners (1500 girls and 1500 boys consisting of up to 450 CWD) to improved nutritional and health status. The project will ensure basic school WASH facility through construction of twin (male /female) latrines for the purpose of providing/enabling safe learning environment to both girls and boys. Clear label and privacy for better protective learning environment. The project will also provide dignity kits(sanitary towels) to the vulnerable adolescent girls to contribute to retention in the learning centers. The project will sustain the current operation of the 10 SOYDA run schools in Daynile, Kahda, Merka and Afgooye districts and also add 12 additional Temporary learning space (TLS) in the event teachers to student ration exceed the recommended 1:40 students per INEE standard (with planning for 1.5 meter spacing protocols). In time for school reopening, SOYDA will coordinate identification of needs specific to boys, girls and CWD to ensuring better protective environment to all learners. To increase enrollment of boys and girls living with disabilities SOYDA will address existing barriers faced by CWD and awareness will also focus on inclusive education.
Through the project, SOYDA will Provide clean water for learners as well as build the capacity of 40 teachers on pedagogy, child rights and protection, then 60 CEC’s, school management, and education stakeholders on EiE to equip them with knowledge and skills to ensure coordinated support to sustain of learning during crises (targeting 50% female) . SOYDA will coordinated with CP AoR, GBV AoR and protection cluster to ensure General/Child protection and gender principles are mainstreamed during implementation including access to MHPSS where applicable per integrated Education-CP framework. For transparency, sustainability and community participation in the project, SOYDA will work closely with the Ministry of Education (MoE), camp leadership and school management in all levels of implementation.
The proposed project will complement our ongoing Education, WASH, Health, Nutrition and Protection projects in the target districts.

1. - Projects activities

1. Support and sustain the existing ongoing 10 schools in Daynile, Kahda, Merka and Afgooye districts 2. The project will construct a total of 12 new TLS or rehabilitate existing classrooms in IDP settlements with priority for Kahda and Merka district with complete with child-friendly and inclusive (incl. children with disabilities) WASH facilities (COVID-19 mitigation)3. SOYDA through this project will provide school feeding program to a total of 3,000 (1500 girls, 1500 boys including 15% those with disability) vulnerable children from most affected IDPs and host communities in Merka, Afgooye, Daynile and Kaxda districts. The school feeding is intended improving the learner’s attendance, retention and nutritional health status. The school management committee will be solely responsible on school feeding program. 4. The project will install 10,000-liter capacity plastic water in each of the target IDPs and host community schools, this will ensure a total of 3,000 (1500 girls, 1500 boys including 15% CWD) children benefits from clean drinking water within the learning environment. 5. The project will provide emergency incentive at rate of $100 per month to 40 teachers (50% female) while 15 school administrators will be paid a total incentive of $150 per months for 12 school months. The incentive will ensure the schools to exempt school fee from the poor students whose parents cannot afford to pay the fee. It will also help teacher motivation, retention and the overall improvement of the school operation including minimizing the risk of school closure due to the drought impact. It will also increase teacher’s improved services delivery within the learning environment. 6. The project will provide 5 days capacity building training on education in emergency for 40 teachers (50% female) in 4 target districts in order to improve their skills and knowledge in student- centered teaching methodologies and supporting students during crisis. Federal government MOE supervisors will provide follow-up support and mentoring the trained teachers in order to help them adopt the acquired skills and knowledge 7. SOYDA will conduct regular community mobilization and back to school campaigns that will be conducted in all the target IDPs and host community schools throughout the project lifespan, this will ensure to improve the school enrollment, retention. The community education committee will be responsible on the community mobilization activities. SOYDA will also employ 10 volunteers (5 men and 5 women) from the IDPs to sensitize and mobilize the IDP parents to enroll their children particularly girls to the schools through visiting house-to-house, community gathering squares and mosque. Awareness will also pay special attention to needs of children with disabilities (CWD) and inclusive education.8. SOYDA will provide Hygiene promotion sessions In order to minimize the risk of cholera/AWD outbreak in the target areas, the project will increase community knowledge and awareness on hygiene issues through regular hygiene promotion campaigns (including COVID-19 risk mitigation). 9. SOYDA will train selected 60 (30 male, 30 female) CEC’s of the both public and private schools on their roles and responsibilities in school management. This training is critical for them to be able to effectively support the head teachers and teachers for effective running of the schools. 10. Support Child to Child protection activities in targeted schools through the Integrated Education-Child Protection Response Framework11. SOYDA will support mechanisms for reporting facilities damaged and /or attacked within areas of operation 12. Provide Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) and Kits to 1000 adolescent girls.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

SOYDA is currently working in Merka district, Afgooye and Afgooye corridor and Mogadishu IDPs in different location providing life-saving emergency service, however, the following are the main risk issues being faced by the beneficiary on daily basis and their mitigation measure, that is recruitment of teachers and student into militia group: There is high likelihood that school going children being entice with worldly materials and cash to attract them into militia group, this have hindered the country security over a long period hence SOYDA intend to make sure such issues are dealt with through active community engagement/consultation and social mobilization/awareness on the negative impact of such verses Conflict of interest among CEC and school management risk factor– The management and committee might divert the schools food stuff and equipment for personnel gain and as well mishandle the schools infrastructure design in construction thus poor quality services to the vulnerable boys and girls in the target location, however, SOYDA will ensure affective and strict monitoring and reporting as well as proper management of the schools infrastructure. Distance and location of school is also risk based on exclusion of disable and marginalized communities to minimize such disparities SOYDA will construct temporary learning space within the settlement. The following risk of exclusion based on gender or age such factors are limited capacity among the trained female teachers, within the education sector might hampered the inclusion of women in the school staying as well as management role hence the might provide room for men dominant. To mitigate this SOYDA shall ensure to capacitate the female teachers and provide them equal chance on both management, community education committee as well as ensure enrollment of more girl child within the target learning centers in Afgooye and Mogadishu IDPs and host communities. Information gap, discrimination, gender disparities and infrastructural design of learning environment further contribute the exclusion risk factors facing the target population. To mitigate this SOYDA will embark on social mobilization and information dissemination through public Barraza radio show for larger coverage on community education enlighten, hence this will provide better avenue for both enrollment and increasing the value for girl child education SOYDA will also ensure to identify the most needy group and provide more responsive intervention by providing the material needs for both girls, boys, children with disabilities, minority communities, children belonging to disadvantaged or marginalized groups and work with children, teachers, community leaders and parents to support equal and safe access to education without discrimination.it will provide adequate community sensitization and monitoring and coordinating with stakeholders that will improve both retention and enrollment, SOYDA however, intends to provide friendly schools toilet as well as easy adaptation of schools facilities by disable and physically impaired persons hence provide better learning material that shall enable to provide adequate access and safe learning facility.
