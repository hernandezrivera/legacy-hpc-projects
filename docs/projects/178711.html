Project Summary

the project aims to improve the livelihoods of the most vulnerable in NWS through the implementation of the following activities:
1. creating temporary employment opportunities through teh implementation of CfW to rehabilitate and restore the community infrastructure.
2. Youth capacity building through the provision of Vocational training to enable them to start their own businesses
3. provision of cash grants to allow the vocational trainees to start their own businesses
Moreover, Violet aims through this project to improve the access of the farmers to farmlands through road rehabilitation (Gravelling) in order to support the farmers in marketing crops transportation and marketing their products.
during the project implementation, Violet will prioritize women-headed households, PWD, youth, and other vulnerable groups to participate in the program. Moreover, the project will be designed and implemented in a way to ensure the inclusion of PWD in the program implementation mainly the vocational training and Business grants. To this end, Violet will ensure the training halls are PWD-friendly spaces and easy to access.

4 - Needs

Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply, and irrigation systems. 13.4 M people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance – a 21 percent increase compared to 2020 – with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline The continued conflict and the economic downturn have deteriorated the living conditions of people who are already in need of humanitarian assistance. On top of that, the massively reduced purchasing power and financial unaffordability for food and other goods have pushed further parts of the population in high need of assistance. According to the needs and response summary report, 12.4M people are food insecure, of whom 1.3M severely food insecure. The food basket average price was increased by 236% between Dec 2019 and Dec 2020. 51% of the HH income spent on food in Sep 2020 leaves little room to meet other basic needs. 28% of HHs adopt emergency strategies to cope with increased food prices including child labor and early marriage, selling property, migrating due to lack of food. According to the response and needs summary, More than 90 percent of the Syria population is now estimated to live below the poverty line and Food security remain the first priority need for 71% of the population, and Livelihood assistance is the priority of 50% of the population

5 - Implementation of activities

Under the ERL sector, Violet is planning to implement the following activities: 1. 250 beneficiaries (mainly from the youth age segment, where 10% are with disabilities and considering the female with at least 30% participation) are obtained a two months vocational training ( 200 training hours) in addition to the basics of business management. the VTs topics will focus on the renewable energy the vocations with high demand in the market 2. 75 beneficiaries (female mainly from the youth age-segment) out of those who accomplished the vocational training program are obtained Bussiness-Cash-Grants and the required support to start up their small-scale businesses so most of them (75% out of them) are successful in obtaining a sustained income source. payment to BNFs will be made in 2 rounds, the 2nd round will be conditional to taking serious steps to start a business by BNFs. 3. Rehabilitation of 10 KM of roads to the farmlands in Harem to improve the access of 1200 farmers to farmlands through road excavation, leveling, and graveling. the roads will support farmers in marketing crops transportation and marketing. Violet will ensure access by coordinating with authorities on the usage of roads and ensuring no fees are taken for road usage. 4. Sustainable livelihoods opportunities for households and communities provided for 1100 householders (short-term work for three months) as a result of their participation in rehabilitating the basic infrastructures of their communities (derbies and garbage removal, sewage system rehab), so their resilience is improved against the negative coping strategies and the basic services of the host communities are improved by operating-or-rehabilitating the main services/infrastructure.
