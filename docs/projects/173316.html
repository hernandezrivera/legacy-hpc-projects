Project Summary

With the fund and technical assistance of AAR Japan, and implemented by NGO Forum for Public Health, two Women Friendly Spaces (WFS) in Alikhali, camp 25 and Jadimura, camp 27, support total number of around 400 women and adolescent girls. WFSs offer protection service including MHPSS, counseling services, PFA, referral support, GBV Case Management, awareness sessions on human rights and protection risks, work therapy as well as community outreach to raise awareness of GBV issues. Community engagement will be promoted through WFS committee where various actors including CiC, Majhis, women representatives and adolescent representatives join and share their opinions on protection environment in the community and activities in WFSs. 40 specific disable person will be benefitted from the project with at most 50 dollars as medical cost or basic need items and other specific need support. This Projects will consider DRR and environmental aspects in the design and programming, and the COVID-19 response will be mainstreamed including mental health services through MHPSS team. This project is also advocating with GBVIMS for being a data gathering organization of the GBVIMS.AAR Japan and NGO Forum for Public Health are committed to oblige the principle of "do no harm," PSEA policy and COVID19 safety security guidelines and all other government and sector protocols in implementing activities prioritizing the Safety and dignity of beneficiaries.. AAR Japan and NGO Forum ensure adequate Complain Response Mechanism through hotline, complain box, and verbal complaint following policy and procedures to provide quality services and ensure accountability.

1 - Identified Needs

Bangladesh hosts about 860,697 (Ref. UNHCR population factsheet as of 31 August 2020) individual Rohingyas from Myanmar who are living 32 congested camps in Cox’s Bazar district. Among them 7,604 and 14,970 individual Rohingyas are living in the camps 25 and 27 in Teknaf upazila respectively. In Teknaf upazila, Rohingyas refugees are living amongst the host community. Rohingya refugees have limited access to formal justice mechanisms, livelihoods, movements and education, which continue to impact negatively on the protection environment, contributing to harmful coping mechanisms, including GBV. For instance, only 20% of survivors in camps are willing to accept legal services after having experienced GBV ("The Shadow Pandemic", IRC, June 2020). To tackle this situation, increasing GBV survivors' access to various services in a secure and confidential manners are priorities. Due to the impact of COVID19, pre-existing gendered risk has been exacerbated. GBV is increasing, including IPV and polygamy, while access to support is more restricted ("In the Shadows of the Pandemic", ISCG et al., October 2020). Especially the majority of GBV in Cox's Bazar, 81%, is perpetrated by intimate partners which have been likely to be increased under COVID19 lockdown ("The Shadow Pandemic", IRC, June 2020). Therefore, raising awareness and community-based protection involving religious and community leaders and refugee volunteers are urgent priorities.In Camps 25 and 27, there are not enough places for Adolescent girl and women to spend daytime safely. Furthermore, there are few opportunities for skill development and recreation activities which contribute to their physical and mental wellbeing. Specifically, in Camp 27, our GBV Case management service will be the only one offered in the camp, so it is critical to identify GBV survivors and each of their needs, and prompt response.

2 - Activities or outputs

1. Awareness Sessions - To increase the knowledge of women's rights and gender, and to avoid protection risk such as human trafficking, IPV and early marriage, awareness sessions will be conducted in WFS with small number of people at one time with COVID-19 preventive measures taken. Awareness sessions will be altered and improved by reflecting beneficiaries’ feedback and request.2. Work therapy: Work therapy for skill development, recreation, and stress alleviation will be conducted under the supervision of PSS Counselor and skilled trainers depend on monthly plan and beneficiaries’ needs and interest. This activity covers small scale sewing activities such as mask making, handicraft, and makeup.3. GBV Case Management - The project following the Interagency GBV case management guidelines, GBV case management service will be provided with survivor centered approach. Based on the assessment, Special Needs Fund will be utilized for the survivors who require support for medical cost, basic needs items, transportation for referral or any other emergency needs.4. MHPSS - MHPSS Support will be provided by PSS Counselors, Clinical psychologist, and skilled volunteers under the supervision of Clinical psychologist through group and individual counseling including psychosocial first aid, trauma Identification, stress management, anger management, and meditation. This service follows the Sector's relevant principles and procedures.5. Referral - Referral support will be provided to meet beneficiaries’ actual needs, which are mainly medical support. Vehicle support for the referral cases and follow-up will be taken to maintain the confidentiality of the concerned survivor.6. Capacity Building - Capacity building of staff and volunteers on protection related issues will be conducted to ensure quality services through training, workshop, on job the coaching and experience sharing etc.7. WFS Committee - Monthly WFS committee including CiC will be held to maintain close liaison with the concerned government authorities, information sharing on camp situations, WFS services and issues and advocacy the protection mainstreaming.8. WFS Committee - Monthly WFS committee including CiC will be held to maintain close liaison with the concerned government authorities, information sharing on camp situations, WFS services and issues and advocacy the protection mainstreaming.9. Host Community and Social Cohesion- The project will run some socisal cohesion activities in host communities. There will be provision of awareness sessions for host community's adolescent girls and women and PSS support will be ensured through phone calls. 10. Disabilty Inclusion- The Centers will have ramps for the smooth entrance of Person with disabilty and will also offer disable friendly WASH facilties in center. The PWD beneficiaries will be referred to the proper service provider with the support of vehicles. Also Internal referral support will be provided for Special Need Fund for the PWD beneficiaries maintaing the SNF Criterias. The project will ensure the impartial access to assistance and services for all beneficiaries. 11. Coordinate with Sector, Government and intersectoral linkage: Coordination with GBV sub-sector, Child protection sub-sector, protection working group, district and Upozila administration as well as RRRC office and in the camp level coordination with CiC, other child protection intervention organizations, site management, GoB, protection actors and other stakeholders are maintained with the aim to smoothly achieving the common goal with other actors by maintaining all govt. and sectorial protocols , ensuring proper referral support to the beneficiaries, for sharing lesson learnt with each other’s and advocacy the protection mainstreaming (CP & GBV) in different sectors.
