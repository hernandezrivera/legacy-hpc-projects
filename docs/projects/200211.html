Project Summary

The Action aims at securing migrants’ access to humanitarian aid and multisectoral assistance (sector overall objective). It will take a two-pronged approach to implementation, both of which will focus on the BML area. Indeed, BML is identified as a priority area for the migrant sector as set out in the 2023 Sector Response Plan due to the high density of migrant communities.
1. Grassroots humanitarian NGOs are often first responders, including to migrants’ urgent needs. However, they are too often left out of the institutional international funding streams and not given the opportunity to develop a more accountable, evidence-based and inclusive response through tailored capacity sharing. ACTED Lebanon has a long track record of CSO support and is a key member of the Localisation Task Force. 84% of our projects were designed & implemented with national CSOs in 2022. With an increasing focus on the emergency response, ACTED has been building the capacity of local frontliners to improve the quality, accountability and impact of emergency aid delivery in the current crisis, with support from LHF. Furthermore, ACTED’s standards for AAP are recognised at global level by the CHS Alliance, with ACTED’s system scoring overall higher than other organizations, and ACTED representatives having presented the mechanism during global learning events. ACTED also has a dedicated MEAL unit, separate from the project implementation team and reporting directly to the Country Director, which further informs project design & implementation according to global standards of quality, accountability, relevance and impact. Through the proposed project, ACTED will further share this capacity with grassroot CSOs.
This part of the Action will take an innovative approach to inclusive localization, benefiting 4 grassroot organizations. To this end, ACTED will partner a well-established national NGO (“the Mentor”) specialized on protection issues as they affect migrants in BML. The Mentor will be selected based on its sector of expertise and will take a “bigger brother approach” to technical coaching on protection for the 4 grassroots organisations. They will receive operational capacity strengthening, informed by ACTED’s Grassroots Organisational Capacity Assesment (GOCA). They will then be provided with subgrants to improve migrants’ access to and knowledge of protection services (as per the Sector Outcome 1), with ACTED guidance on operations and grant management.
2. In line with Outcome 2 of the Sector Response Plan, vulnerable migrants will have improved access to multi-sectoral humanitarian assistance through the provision of MPCA for 600 migrant households (1,800 individuals) in BML, where ACTED has been implementing projects since 2007 and has high community acceptance.
This will build on ACTED’s experience implementing unconditional cash distribution projects globally, including currently to 325 vulnerable migrant households (975 individuals) in Bourj Hammoud.
To select the vulnerable migrant households, ACTED in collaboration with the 4 grassroots organisations and the Mentor will outreach widely in the targeted location. Eligibility will be assessed and prioritization based on pre-defined criteria developed with the expertise of the MEAL unit (gender of the head of HH, food consumption score, level of income, etc.), and coordinate with relevant partners for de-duplication. 2022 consultations showed there is no existing system for de-duplication of assistance, nor any harmonized guidelines for the targeting of migrants through cash assistance. ACTED will thus continue to coordinate de-duplication efforts directly with actors present in BML, the mentor and Grassroots Orgs and the Sector.
ACTED will provide the selected households with 100 USD per household for 10 months, through the money transfer agency it has a contract with. Recognizing that many migrants do not have proper IDs, so prepaid cards have been issued to the beneficiaries.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Output 1: The role and contribution of local CSOs to an accountable, inclusive and coordinated humanitarian response is strengthenedActivity 1.1 Selection & capacity strengthening of humanitarian grassroots organizations targeting migrants: Through an open CfP, 4 humanitarian CSOs providing emergency assistance to migrants will be selected by ACTED and its protection partner. Their capacities will be assessed utilising ACTED’s dedicated tool to inform tailored capacity strengthening plans, including on protection, MEAL & AAP, to enhance their capacities to efficiently & effectively respond to urgent needs. Activity 1.2 Sub-granting to 4 Grasssroots humanitarian NGOss to address the emergency needs of migrant populations. The sub-granting projects will aim at addressing the sector primary needs (access to and knowledge of protection services), further enhancing the quality of the assistance delivered against sector standards. The CSOs will also be supported hands-on on sub-grant management by ACTED. Output 2 Emergency cash needs of migrant populations in the targeted areas are metActivity 2.1 Beneficiary outreach, identification and selection Activity 2.2 Distribution of monthly MPCA assistance to 600 vulnerable HHs (1,800 indiv): ACTED will provide the selected Hhs with 100 USD per HH for 10 months, with funds transferred using a MTA with which ACTED has a contract. A one-off code will issue from BOB finance to the beneficiary in their name overcoming the absence of ID

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

ACTED a strong commitment to incorporate protection principles in humanitarian response by ensuring meaningful, dignified and safe access to aid for affected communities. ACTED will ensure to target the most vulnerable women and girls, boys and men, regardless of nationality, ethnic, religious affiliation or sexual orientation who struggle the most to meet their basic needs. Under this Action, ACTED will work with the relevant stakeholders to ensure that protection and gender is mainstreamed throughout the intervention by following existing capacities and procedures to ensure equal access to aid for crisis affected populations. The Action focuses on integrating PSEA messages and information on access to services and shares them with the targeted stakeholders. The Action will also strengthen the capacities of local actors to better respond to the needs of the vulnerable migrant population. The Action will ensure a do-no-harm approach to the targeted migrant population.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Accountability to beneficiaries and communities will be firstly ensured through the Complaints Response Mechanism. A range of communication modalities will be made available to targeted beneficiaries and local NGOs to ensure the mechanism is easily accessible, taking into account the specificities of the local context and culture, as well as project resources and beneficiary vulnerabilities and preferences.To ensure that ACTED adopts the most effective and preferred means; during M&E surveys conducted in the target communities, questions to review accessibility of communication means used and re-assess beneficiaries’ preferences for both information and feedback mechanisms will be integrated at relevant frequency. ACTED Policies and Code of Conduct will also be attached to the agreement with partner organizations.Throughout project implementation, the project team and Accountability team will ensure that the CRM hotline is widely communicated to beneficiaries.
