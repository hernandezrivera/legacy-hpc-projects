Project Summary

The direct beneficiaries of this project will be people in need of WaSH services. people with limited access to drinkable and clean water, and people with water-prone diseases will directly benefit from this project.
1-WaSH: The WaSH services will target the 8 communities with the following activities.
Set up 1000 water container of a capacity of 1000 liters with a water filtration system for each container for 2685 households within selected communities.
2-2- Support the areas with waste management and disposal services through the distribution of garbage containers of a capacity of 770 L (one per 500 population. The total number of targeted populations is 16111. Thus, 33 garbage containers will be procured and distributed), and waste collection and transfer from the target communities to dumps that are located at least 10 km far from the target location.
The beneficiaries will be registered and disaggregated according to age, gender, and residential status. Every person will have the right to access available services. The priority in terms of access and support will be given to the most vulnerable groups, which are:
- Pregnant and Lactating Women PLW.
- Under-five children.
- Elderly persons
- Chronically-ill patients.
- Women-headed households.
- Malnourished children and women.
- Reproductive-aged women
The catchment population of the target communities will be considered as indirect beneficiaries because the access of these communities to WaSH services will be increased. As a result, the overall socio-economic status of the population will be enhanced. As a result, the contribution of these communities to the economic condition and sources of income will be increased
The logistics, procurement, and recruitment activities related to this project will be carried out according to WHOSD policies and Standard Operating Procedures SOPs. WHOSD will announce, through websites, WHOSD Facebook page, WHOSD website, and other means of communication about its request to contract suppliers to procure/rent and install if necessary: pre-fabricated containers according to a specific design, two ambulance vehicles, medical supplies and equipment, furniture, water containers and filtration systems, IEC materials, and waste management and disposal tools. WHOSD will prepare technical specifications and designs for each of the mentioned items.
WHOSD will announce the recruitment of the health and WaSH staff through the mentioned means of communication. The service delivery phase will start after the initial delivery and will last for four months. WHOSD will directly implement the project activities. However, for the quality of data and results measurement, WHOSD will contract a third-party entity to carry out the baseline and endline data collection to measure the impact of the project on the target population and the changes in attitude and knowledge in relation to the hygiene promotion topics. Additionally, WHOSD will develop its Health Information Management System HIMS and will deliver the required training to the engaged staff.
WHOSD will deliver the services using the facility-based and community-based approaches. approaches

4 - Needs

The area was suffering from collapsed infrastructures and significant humanitarian needs. WHOSD has conducted several needs assessments throughout 2020 to conclude the prioritized needs of the affected population, especially vulnerable groups. The prioritized clusters were: health, WaSH, and safe water, shelter,and protection The WaSH needs were related to the provision of safe drinkingwater, especially that most of the containers were destroyed due to the military operations. Access to drinking water is a challenge to the people of Deir-ez-Zor, especially those living in temporary shelters and rural areas. Additionally, there is a need for waste management and disposal. In general, the level of awareness in the target areas was loweven before the crisis in Syria. Thus, people are in need of raising awareness campaigns

5 - Implementation of activities

WaSH: WHOSD, through this component, will install 2685 1000 L metal water tanks with metal beas with all necessary of for 2685 HHs, and will support the area with waste management and disposal services.WaSH Activities:1- Install 1000 water containers of a capacity of 1000 L per each, with a purifying system for 1000 households. It is a purification device consisting of h stages and it is available within the local markets and aims to treat water turbidity and purify it from impurities. There are filters within the device that are replaced every year, and the filter does not require any maintenance2- Deliver general waste management within the target communities by installing garbage containers and deploy cleaners and waste collectors with Heavy machinery and equipment. Garbage will be collected by a contracted supplier, garbage collection workers will be employed from the local communities, and staff will be trained by the local council on the mechanism of garbage collection and removal. At the end of the project, the community will be able to collect and move the garbage, where all equipment and machinery will be handed over to the local council.3- Deliver medical waste management within the supported 2 PHCs

Medium: High.Mitigating Measure:Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the reasoning behind the sale/exchange of assistance and solicit their feedback with regards to solutions. 2-Risk: Movements of water trucks in congested, camp like settings Likelihood: High. Impact: Low.Mitigating Measure:Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding location of distribution points, delivery routes and its schedule; Avoid location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establish clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Conduct safety training with truck drivers/operatorEnsure agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Impact: Low.Mitigating MeasureEnsure staff/partners/contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.
