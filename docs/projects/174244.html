Project Summary

The project will be implemented with the participatory approach. FRD will conduct household assessments in consultation with the village development committees formed for project implementation.
The project will contribute to the overall objectives of the sector through an integrated approach
Objective 1: Improve food security by providing targeted cash, food and nutrition support to most vulnerable people affected by multiple shocks
Objective 2: Improve coordination and harmonization of humanitarian response across all levels
Objective 3: Preserve the ability/resilience of people most vulnerable to multiple shocks to meet their food, livelihood and other basic needs, through the provision of timely livelihood support and humanitarian assistance
FRD will carry out Cash feasibility assessments comprising security risk assessments, market functionality, and ICT assessments to assess food and cash feasibility depending upon the Market Functionality and access to the market. The assistance will be provided to the most vulnerable community at the household level. Women headed, child-headed, disabled, and elderly headed households will be the primary prioritized for assistance. Assistance will also cover the Vulnerable farming households with pregnant or lactating women and children in their first 1,000 days. Priority will be given to women-headed households, families with disabilities, and children under five years of age whose subsistence agriculture/food security has been affected. Besides, vulnerable households whose crops were damaged or lost because of shocks will also be facilitated.
The project will be implemented with an integrated approach with a mix of activities covering various aspects of community livelihoods and diversifying livelihood opportunities through targeted interventions.
Preference will be given to Vulnerable farming/livestock rearing households who previously received limited or no assistance in livestock managent training and involving the female community in specific activities. Additionally, Vulnerable families who rely on livestock livelihoods will also be included to prevent the loss of their productive assets.
The proposed project will be based on the cash-based transfer modality. Cash-based transfers (CBTs) will be used where markets are stable and sufficient supplies are present.
FRD will consult with the district administration to finalize the tehsil and villages, which might include validation of the district administration's needs. The targeted beneficiaries will consist of the most vulnerable families who fulfill the criteria for assistance mentioned above. FRD will facilitate the selection or the identification process but carried out by the village development committees (VDCs) to make them accountable and responsible to avoid any risk of leaving out any deserving community member. These families will be the direct beneficiaries of the project. While the community as a whole who will benefit from the interventions like the DRR measures, Rehabilitation of irrigation channels, Rehabilitation of access roads, consumers of the services after the cash for training activities for the trained persons will constitute the indirect beneficiaries of the project. To include the female population, FRD will form women VDCs to assist the female population upon the recommendation of women VDCs. FRD intends to have almost 50 % of female beneficiaries in the assistance. Already formulated beneficiary criteria will be used to select beneficiaries. The participants under CBT activities will receive cash from the financial Service Provider through electronic transfer. For which, FRD will establish cash disbursement points at the central location to view the ease of access by the beneficiaries to the point. The cash will be provided to the selected beneficiaries upon presenting the original CNIC at the disbursement point. On average, each beneficiary will be engaged in conditional cash for three months.

1- Project Activities

1. Coordination Due to its ground presence in all the districts since 2012, while working within Food Security and Shelter with WFP, UNHCR, and UNOCHA, FRD enjoys good acceptance in the districts and has developed robust linkages and coordination mechanism at the field level. FRD, before the new intervention, will hold coordination meetings with the relevant stakeholders for smooth implementation of the activities. 2. Staff Hiring, orientation and deploymentFRD has already developed a pool of experienced staff on standby for deployment on concise notice and were previously engaged in relevant interventions. For a new recruitment process, proper written tests and interviews will be conducted to assess candidates' suitability for the position in the project. Reference checks for new employees will be carried out according to FRD’s HR policy. Once selected, the team will be provided with a detailed orientation on the implementation of the project.3. Validation of the villages and profiling.To finalize the villages' list, FRD will consult with the funding agency and the district administration before the village selection shortlisting will be carried out through market assessment.4. Village Development Committees formation.In line with the community-based participatory planning approach, FRD mobilization staff will form village development committees that will primarily be responsible for facilitation in identifying needs, ensuring beneficiary’s identification & endorsement, and monitoring the cash for work activities and facilitation of the community sessions and meetings. Female VDCs will be formed along with the male VDCs to include women's voices in need identification and planning and the rest of the functions. 5. Needs Assessment Revalidation and prioritization: FRD will carry out need assessment in the selected villages to identify the scope of work. The VDCs will play a facilitating role in the need identification process. The process of needs prioritization would be fully documented by FRD and will be a community-driven exercise. FRD will use the required tools as agreed with the donor for collecting information/data and compilation of the results and submit a report in an agreed format6. Activity Planning & designing: After the profiling of the village, the VDCs will facilitate FRD staff in preparing the activity plan. The activities will be in line with the community's needs. The previous experience might include clearing and rehabilitation of irrigation channels, Protection of critical points of irrigation channels, reclamation of agricultural land, and rehabilitation of access roads and Farm to Market roads. After the activities' finalization, FRD staff will Visit the activity sites prioritized by the community and prepare the final list of proposed activities. Emphasis should be laid on the identification of women-focused activities that could be implemented by women. Training and capacity-building activities could also be identified to enhance capacities to increase family incomes and improve livelihoods. 7. Community Infrastructure and Disaster Risk Reduction measuresBased on the findings and recommendations of the VDCs, the activities will be implemented under the supervision of the technical staff; these activities will contribute to improved food security for the locality and can contribute to enhanced food production or resilience against disasters will identify participants for each activity. the activities of Irrigation Channels: Flood Protection structures and DRR activities: Rehabilitation of Access Roads, Restoration /establishment of Water Ponds, Water Shed Stabilization.8. Cash For Training Component: FRD will provide training on improved agriculture practices, skill development, especially for the women community, to have women participate in the activities without compromising their privacy
