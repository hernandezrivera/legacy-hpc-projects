Project Summary

This project aims to increase access to life-saving and essential assistance to vulnerable people and will focus on supporting the creation of safe and more secure environments for children by supporting them and their mothers with vital nutrition services at northwest Syria
This project will cover 120,000 people as disaggregated as follows:
- 30,000PLW ( 15,000Host community – 15,000IDPs)
- ( 90,000CU 5) Children under 5 (50,000 girl /25,000 Host community – 25,000 IDPs/ 40,000 boys / 20,000Host community – 20,000IDPs/ )
One OTP center will be established and 16 rapid response teams that will be established and will work alongside the OTP center to enhance and optimize the services.
Each Rapid response team (RRT) will work in specific communities and will cover approximately 7,500(pregnant and lactatıng women in addition to children under 5 )
These Rapid response teams will work in parallel and in coordination with the available health facilities in the target areas.
The RRTs will provide integrated health, nutrition, child protection, and hygiene promotion.
A pre and rapid coordination combined with referrals will be in place if the team notices or identifies any sign of abuse or violence among children under 5 and or mothers.
All cases of violence against women and children will be referred to a pre-identified center.
These integrations between services will help create and provide safe and more secure environments for children and their families.

4 - Needs

Since 1st of December in just two months, more than 520,000 people have been displaced from their homes, the vast majority of them being women and children.this continuous displacement will limit access to a diversified diet during the critical period of pregnancy and breastfeeding and may have negative effects on the health of both mother and child. Therefore, supporting pregnant and lactating women is a priority to help minimize negative health implications that might result from low micronutrient intakes due to the lack of food diversity- Nutrition survey (SMART survey, May 2019) has shown that at least one out of each three children currently living in North West Syria is stunted. According to survey results, more than 130,000 children under the age of five years in Idlib and Rural Aleppo will likely not reach their full educational and intellectual potential because of stunting; half of these stunted children are also affected by displacements. - Data indicate that one out of each mother and children are anemic. -According to RNA across 33 IDPs camps in northwest Syria, acute malnutrition remains high among IDPS as well as population affected by the conflict Also, Nutrition surveillance system is showing that 20 % of mothers feeding their children properly and according to facts this is not because just food insecurity, suboptimal infant feeding practices is one of the main causes behind increasing acute and chronic malnutrition. Also, data indicates that malnutrition among mothers and child mothers could explain the increased prevalence of poor and stunting

5 - Implementation of activities

Each Rapid response team (RRT) will work in specific communities and will cover approximately 75,00 (pregnant and lactatıng women in addition to children under 5 ) All project staff will be trained on on IYCF-EThese Rapid response teams will work in parallel and in coordination with the available health facilities in the target areas The RRTs will provide integrated health, nutrition, child protection, and hygiene promotion through: Nurses: will screen the children under 5, and refer the ones with dangers signs and medical problems to the available health facilities. Community Health Workers (CHWs): - They will assess and screen nutritional status among the children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them where needed.- Also, the infant and young child feeding practices in emergencies (IYCF-E) services will be provided to prevent any case of malnutrition by:o Community-based 1-1 counseling on WHO-recommended Infant and Young Child Feeding Practices to mothers who have malnourished children.o Providing messages on IYCF-E recommended practices. o Distribute micronutrients and nutrition supplies for the PLWs and children. o Reporting to the cluster any distribution of breast milk substitute. o Referring cases of any visual signs of abuse /violence among children and PLWs. Midwives: They will screen pregnant and lactating women and refer the ones with any dangers signs or medical problems to the nearest facilities (eclampsia - preeclampsia ........)Mercy-USA in coordination with Shelter/NFI cluster received funding during the last reserve allocation to provide fuel and stoves for IDPs in the above-mentioned camps during this winter season Also, a pre and rapid coordination combined with referrals will be in place if the team notices or identifies any sign of abuse or violence among children under 5 and or mothers. All cases of violence against women and children will be referred to a pre-identified facility

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Monitoring: Incidence Report Resources Required: security officer Protection /Risk/Threat: Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: -Mercy-USA maintains a ZERO TOLERANCE policy towards SEA in accordance with internationally-established protocols and standards for SEA protection. Mercy-USA staff, interns and volunteers are trained in SEA protection, child safeguarding with clear protocols on reporting potential SEA and child protection concerns. Mercy-USA will directly contact GBV and PSEA networks for referral of any case can be met - Effective recruitment and performance management of all humanitarian staff. (Each employee should sign code of conduct document) Monitoring: Case Management / Reports / Complaint Resources Required: Train the staff Code of conduct document

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: - Cross border access from Turkey is available. - Easy and smooth Logistical access. - Cooperation from local councils - Facilitation and acceptance from major regional stakeholders/actors (Turkish Government, OAG groups, etc.) - Timely fund dispersal - Targeting of most vulnerable beneficiaries - Recruitment of female staff within teams will be considered. Risks: - General daily security threats including robbery and looting - Macro-level conflict-related threats including aerial bombardment, advancement of hostile actors, shifting alliances, border closure, and chemical attack. - Delays in fund dispersal -Fire hazard and/or conflict-related activities happen during the ongoing implementation. Risk Mitigation: - Following Mercy-USA safety and security guidelines and established safety protocols. - In the event of major macro-level conflict-related threats, Mercy-USA will take appropriate action that is commensurate with the scale of the threat. This may include: - Suspending operations temporarily -Shifting resources and operations to more secure locations - Clear steps will be taken to mitigate risks between staff and beneficiaries including gender-based violence. - Project staff may be dismissed at any time in the event of endangering beneficiaries or other staff, and this will be clearly outlined in each employment contract. - Contingency stock will be in place for shipments to avoid any impact if Bab Alhawa borders become closed (for items purchased from Turkey) - Gender balance and equal opportunities will be taken into consideration when hiring staff for a different position. -All workers will be given the proper needed instructions ahead of any deliveries. -All necessary precautions will be taken to prevent a fire incident moreover, unarmed guard(s) will be available in the facilities at all times, in order to avoid any conflicting activity might happen.
