Project Summary

The project has 3 main objectives:
1- To reduce the risk posed by explosive hazards and the likelihood of exposure to them through implementing the EORE pillar of Mine Action Sector and raising the awareness and knowledge of civilians and humanitarian workers through implementing tailored EORE programming in 2 subdistricts: Afrin, Jandairis, and Ma’batli-Afrin district.
This objective will be achieved through conducting EORE sessions in the mentioned locations, targeting both children and adults in schools, Bahar's community centres, camps, and public spaces; as well as, conducting training for community focal points and humanitarian workers, as an indirect approach to disseminate the knowledge of EORE, providing victims assistance, and conducting contamination impact surveys in 4 communities, 2 in each of Afrin and Jandairis sub districts (Afrin, Marata, Jandairis, and Jalma).
2- Enhance equitable access to quality Child Protection services including (1) community-based PSS and AR, and (2) quality specialized CP services, mainstreaming children with disabilities, in Afrin and Jandairis sub-districts.
The CP objective entails CFS-based and outreach-based approaches; where CFS-based activities include:
- Establishment of CFS which will be tailored to be inclusive for CwDs i.e. constructing ramps and rehabilitating the wash facilities to be CwDs-friendly, and providing transportation.
- Structured and sustained PSS program
- Parenting program
- Life skills for adolescents >13 years
- Case management & Referrals for children survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect, child labor, early marriage, children exposed to severe/extreme psychosocial distress, cases of CAAFAG, UASC.
While for outreach-based activities:
- AR for children and adults and IEC campaigns
- Semi-structured PSS
- Establishing 2 CPNs.
- Referrals of children to the CFS and services in other sectors for a comprehensive response.
- Implementation of emergency response activities and coordinating the protection emergency response amongst Protection organizations in Afrin district, given that Bahar is the Protection emergency response FP for the district.
Note that there will be 2 outreach teams covering Afrin and Jandairis SDs and implementing these activities and targeting schools, community and IDPs centers, and public spaces.
3- The GBV component aims to provide access for MWBGs survivors to life-saving specialized GBV response services provided at 1 WGSS in Afrin SD
The activities will be:
At the WGSS: AR program using the updated AR toolkit for W&G, and my safety- my wellbeing curriculum for adolescent girls, focused PSS for W&G survivors and at-risk of GBV, focused PSS and specialized case management supported by case fund, CMR, and referrals to other humanitarian services, in addition to VT and educational courses for the most vulnerable women and adolescent girls.
Through the outreach teams in 4 communities in Afrin SD: Awareness raising program targeting men, women, boys, and girls (4 sessions at least per group), information sharing, PFA, referrals of W&G survivors of GBV to the WGSS.
Moreover, transportation for women and girls with disabilities to the WGSS to be provided with the available services given that the WGSS will be rehabilitated to be inclusive for women and girls.
Also, a training will be provided to the GBV staff on GBV basics and CM, and they, in turn, will provide training to non-GBV health staff on CMR, PFA and referrals, and GBV basics.
To ensure effective coordination is in place, Bahar will continue to attend CP SC monthly coordination meetings and will submit 4Ws reports.

4 - Needs

MA:Explosive accidents cause serious injury and death, leading to long-term physical and psychological trauma for survivors and their families and severely impacting their lives and livelihoods. Men and boys are more frequently exposed, comprising more than 80% of victims of reported EO accidents in the past five years. Therefore, Bahar will provide MRE sessions, training for CFPs, victim assistance, and referrals of victims to PSS services, and will ensure that the teams are capacitated to provide MRE services.CP:According to the CP dashboard as of December 2021, 90% of the KIs in Afrin and Jandairis reported children engaged in type of work that prevents them from going to school, 69% of KIs reported children in need of focused services. Therefore, Bahar will be providing case management and focused PSS to children facing CP violations, structured PSS, parenting programs, and community-based awareness-raising, taking CwDs into consideration.GBV:Afrin is at 4th severity scale according to the HNO 2022. According to Bahar’s needs assessment conducted in October 2021, IPV against women, early marriage against girls, emotional abuse against PwDs are the most common GBV types in Afrin; This matches with HNO 2022 stating that W&G are subjected to increased denial of economic resources and education, forced and child marriage. Thus, Bahar will raise communities’ awareness about GBV, provide CM, and CB of the humanitarian workers to ensure quality intervention and do-no-harm.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Bahar has a Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA policy that usuallyis attached to each employee’s job contract clarifying all the related procedures.Bahar will ensure the field teams attend PSEA\safeguarding trainings andrefreshing sessions provided by Bahar internally or externally. Bahar’s PSEA focalpoints responsibility is to ensure trainings are delivered to the project staff,informative materials (either soft copies in case of online or in-place approach ofimplementation) are distributed to both staff and beneficiaries, and updating anddevelop the PSEA policies including the complaints pathway/reporting mechanismregularly. The MEAL team will monitor if any danger is threatening the safety of thebeneficiaries and will report findings in order to take the appropriate measures.Bahar team will also inform the community, particularly men and youth, about thethe objective of the project and that its aim in order to mitigate exposing women andgirls to any potential harm or violence.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: COVID-19 pandemic may impose lack of BNFs’ access and participation inservices due to curfew restrictions, home lockdown, isolation, or people’s fear ofphysical interaction, as well as, the in-place approach might expose the participant to therisk of getting infected. Mitigation Measure 1: Bahar will provide sanitizers and PersonalProtection Equipment PPE to BNFs accessing the facilities and also during theoutreach activities and will ensure applying other prevention measures including socialdistancing, minimizing the groups sessions participants to 5-7 max. BNFs will beinformed also about measures taken to encourage them to access the services. Baharwas also a member of the GBV SC COVID-19 Task Force and will implement theassistance according to the guidance provided for GBV COVID-19 guidance and the otherSub sectors’ guidance and instructions as well when feasible. Finally, COVID-19 ARmessages will be mainstreamed and disseminated along the course of the project. Risk 2:Breaching Bahar’s CoC, safeguarding, and PSEA policies by the project staff against theBNFs in an intended or unintended way. Mitigation Measure 2: Bahar includesthese policies in the employees’ contracts and they are being delivered with orientationsessions by the HR department or the FP. In addition, Bahar has assigned 1 FP to conducttrainings on those policies, receive the allegations and act accordingly, ensure timelyresponse and investigate the allegations to take proper action.
