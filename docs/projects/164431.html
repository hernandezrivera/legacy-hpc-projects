Project Summary

The project aims to provide both preventive ( raising awareness, empowerment activities, recreational activities , safety audit..) and responsive (CM including CMR, PFA, MHPSS, referrals) services for( W,M,B,G), and to enhance the integration between GBV and RH in both of GBV and health delivering points, using 2 types of modalities (static ,mobile) for this purpose in all locations. SEMA will establish 3 WGSS (Sharran, TalAbiad, Sejo), and will provide integrated GBV services in health facilities (Azaz, AlBab, Idlib, Atmeh, Kafirtakharim, Tal abiad, Suluq) as well as in mobile clinics at camps (Azaz, Atmeh, and Aldana). Key strategies will be taken into consideration during the implementation: 1- Adding male staff trained to provide preventive and responsive services for men and boys ,2- special focus of adolescent programming .3- All static facilities will be rehabilitated to be friendly for PWD, to ensure the accessibility and inclusion of PWS and elderly .4-Localisation: SEMA tries to build the capacity of local organizations to expand their GBV technical knowledge as well as supporting their organizational capacity. 5- Train humanitarian front workers on GBV core concept, and safe referral .6- Providing Individual case fund using the GBVSC SOP’s. 6- SEMA as the lead of M&E consortium in the GBV SC, in collaboration with the SC and the assigned consultant will Facilitate the process of developing the standardized M&E toolkit, which will be rolled out and used by the GBVSC members at the second quarter of this year as scheduled.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020,13.6 M are in need to GBV services , with more than third of locations recognized as sever on the severity scale , more than half of those affected people are adults , and one third of them are children (5-18).Referring to Voices 2020, various types of GBV continue to pervade the lives of women and girls, particularly adolescent girls. Physical violence, emotional and psychological violence, sexual violence, exploitation and harassment, domestic violence, family violence against women and girls and early/forced marriage are types of GBV identified over the years. Adolescent girls are forced into marriage by parents for several reasons, such as easing financial burdens, as well as to protect them against sexual violence, abuse and exploitation, In north-west, Syria reported incidents of rape are on the increase, which doesn't necessarily mean an increase in prevalence but signifies an improvement in the quality of services only few participants identified sexual violence perpetrated against men and boys, GBV experts noted the increase in males, especially boys, disclosing and seeking care through GBV specialized services. Also, of note, sexual violence of both males and females is still perceived to happen within detention facilities, Women, girls, adolescent girls, particularly if they are widowed, divorced and/or displaced, as well as women with disabilities and older women, were identified as affected populations most at risk of GBV. Some consequences of violence identified were shame and stigma, psychosocial distress, health problems, loss of childhood, and fear. The increase in reported suicides and suicide attempts by GBV survivors is also an alarming emerging trend. We conducted RNA in Talabiad:83% of interviews claim lack GBV services in the area, 68% talked about restriction of movement for women and girls due to security reasons, early marriage, sexual harassment, domestic violence, denial of resources are the main GBV concerns.

5 - Implementation of activities

All activities will be conducted to achieve the Provision of life-saving quality specialized GBV services including case management, psycho-social support and, women empowerment strategies. SEMA team will Provide specialized GBV services to survivors, inside health/RH facilities with a robust internal referral system between well-trained health team (on GBV, CMR) and GBV services, cash assistance will be provided for high-risk cases. WGSS will secure safe and non-stigmatizing GBV response. provision of GBV prevention activities using GBV SC toolkit awareness-raising through well-trained outreach staff members from both genders. male staff will be trained (GBV, CM, Toolkit) to target Men and boys in schools’ mosques, cafe’s with responsive and preventive services, Outreach teams will be always ready to provide emergency response for the new IDP’s. Provision of recreational activities, vocational training, life skills activities, with a special focus on adolescent girls in WGSS. safety audits will be conducted in nearby camps. To ensure the inclusion of PWD and elderly by the rehabilitation of the facilities to be friendly with PWD, staff will conduct GBV SC online training on disability inclusion. SEMA will ensure the quality of services through capacitating the team with set of training on core concepts of GBV, communication skills, CM, GBV SC toolkit. Close supervision in the field through GBV supervisor will take place to ensure the quality of the specialized service. SEMA will Mainstream GBV through train health and nutrition actors on GBV core concept, and safe referral. CBO led by women will be supported and technically capacitated by SEMA GBV technical team inside Syria. SEMA Meal department developed tools like (satisfaction survey, pre-posttest etc.) to ensure the quality of services provided. SEMA with the M&E consortium will be able to finalize the standardized M&E toolkit in collaboration with the GBVSC and the assigned consultant.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- Lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reappraisal and further trauma.Likelihood: High.Impact: High. Mitigating Measure:-Case management with survivors to be conducted by trained personnel only.2. Low participation of girls because of family and community resistance.Likelihood: Medium.Impact: High.Mitigating Measure:-Targeting adolescent girls with socially accepted activities like life skills as safe entry point.Engage with the community and local authorities and clear communication on project objectives.3. Focus on quantity of service providers trained instead of on quality of training, relevance and impact on knowledge and improved service provision and high turn over and loss of capacity.Likelihood: High.Impact: High.Mitigating Measure:-Use of SOPs and other guidelines and resources from GBV AoR in line with global standards.4. Livelihood opportunities may increase risks of GBV and Expectations of income may be unrealistic and might create dependency.Likelihood: High.Impact: High. Mitigating Measure:-Consult women and men, boys and girls in the design of the program.-Ensure beneficiaries know services where to disclose if violence occurs.5. Exploitation by service provider or by other actor in the community.Likelihood: High.Impact: High.Mitigating Measure:-Raise awareness on PSEA and other types of exploitation and where to report.-SEMA as a member of PSEA network had a very robust PSEA policy for all departments. -Standardized Code of Conduct of the Turkish hub need to be signed by all employees.-strong reporting mechanism is in place ,with the ability to conduct investigation through trained staff.
