Project Summary

The project will support vulnerable groups IDPS and hosting communities in both river Nile and Dabah and Dongla after the war in 15th April many group migrate from Khartoum to other place Dongla and Dabahh receive a number of the IDPs and recently the number start to increase due to the recent development in the boarder with Egypt , women and kids are accommodate in public places or with families there is efforts by local community to accommodated them but of course there is shortage due to the capcity of the community it self, shortage in kits for women and girls shortage in medicine an open day was organized by volunteer from the community but medicine was a big problem. The number that increase rapidly make shelter and PSS support as well as yesterday 27 families arrived from Khartoum to Dabbah in Avery bad condition due to the harassments and problems they faced in the trip
This project seeks to civic-educate girls and women in areas of displacement on the outskirts of Mahmia - , Dongla and Dabah on their rights, and how to protect themselves against GBV.
the project will give space for women to share with each other and get support by each other as well as women from the communities who would also share their stories. Women and girls, long subjected to violations of their rights and lacking sufficient awareness, especially as they were affected by the war, need to build a solid platform of awareness of their rights and how to support these rights and how to report abuse. the project will support them with shelters, dignity kit, tent urgent need

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

CfPS adopts a rights-based approach to conflict prevention. this approach sees poverty as a major driver of conflict. the way people in rural areas pursue farming and animal husbandry is backward. clearing land for farming and overgrazing by herders both negatively impact the natural environment. the environment, natural resources and climate change have been included in the geographical and humanitarian context analysis, as well as in project design, proposed activities and budgets (including mitigation measures to minimize impacts and enhance benefits)

7.1.1 - Please specify other means to collect/share feedback or information if there is any

CfPS will ensure that staff, volunteers and IP comply with PSEA standards, highlighted in the national GBV SOPs ass well as safety and security controls. CfPS will also support stronger and smoother communication channels with both state and non-state ac

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

accountability to the affected population is core in the CfPS responses and will be strengthened through continuous consultations with affected population on the response modalities as well their satisfaction with services provided. in addition, CfPS is an active members of PSEA technical and coordination team, and supports the lead the survival support task team and is working with team members to ensure that GBV referral mechanisms are accessible to survivors of SEA. CfPS will equip IP and target communities with info regarding the SEA, and how to report and access services including health, legal and psychological-support services. CfPS partners and beneficiaries play an active role in decision-making process in regards with planning, implementing and evaluation of activities. this is done by conducting FGDs with women and girls in women centers. the project will support the development of self protection capacities, and aim at improving beneficiaries daily lives through improved formal and informal protection mechanisms. this is considered the most effective way of tackling gender inequality as root causes of GBV.
