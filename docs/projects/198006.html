Project Summary

Since 2009, the Boko Haram insurgency has crippled northeastern Nigeria, thousands of people have lost their lives either through gun shots, abduction, bomb blast etc. women and girls are the population that have been particularly most affected AOGs also continue to attack LGAs which led to increase food insecurity and hardship.
According to the 2022 Humanitarian response Plan (HRP) 8.4 Million people are estimated to be in urgent need of humanitarian assistance with only 5.5m targeted. 22 percent of this population are women, 62% are children while 7 % are persons with disability. Looking at this data women and children are the worst hit by this crisis.
Between June 1 and August 31 2022, IOM’s DTM programme carried out assessments to ascertain the level of damage sustained in IDP and host community sites due to heavy windstorms, rainfall and flood. Overall, 72 host community locations, 11 camps and nine collective settlements were assessed. The worst-hit of the assessed sites was Banki, a host community location in Buduwa/Bula Chirabe ward of Bama LGA, where heavy rainfall alongside the opening of the dam gates in Cameroon led to the flooding of the community aﬀecting an estimated 7,389 individuals. In total, 4,989 shelters/houses were damaged by the flood, leaving a total of 8,181 households (4,645 in Borno State, 962 in Adamawa State and 2,574 in Yobe State) in immediate need of shelter. Three hundred and thirty-four casualties were reported as a result of rainstorms and floods during the period. A total of 37,475 individuals (16,393 in Borno State, 5,979 in Adamawa State and 15,042 in Yobe State) have been aﬀected by heavy rainfalls that have led to flooding since the inception of the rainy season. Of the total individuals, Borno State accounts for 44%, Yobe State 40% and Adamawa State 16%. This has led to increase vulnerability of women and girls to various forms of Gender based violence against women and girls and if urgent intervention is not rendered it might lead to loss of lives.
Violations and sexual abuse against women and children are widespread both in and out of camp settings. According to the 2022 HRP, an estimated 1.3 million people are identified to be in need of GBV prevention and response services across the BAY states. 46% IDPs, 23% returnees and 31% host communities many of whom are heads of households living in crowded, culturally inappropriate conditions. Women and girls are often targeted while performing basic tasks such as travelling to water points, collecting firewood to cook food, accessing farmlands to supplement food rations during the farming season and going to communal latrines. GBV threatens the health of women and girls through injuries, sexually transmitted infections (STIs), including HIV, unwanted pregnancies, and obstetric fistula caused by sexual violence. Women and girls who experience GBV are also prone to mental health issues resulting from stigmatization, discrimination, isolation, exclusion and other social impacts.
A substantial and coordinated effort is required to address this needs. FRAD currently operates integrated health, nutrition, protection, gender based violence (GBV), WASH and accountability to affected population and Localization programs in three hard to reach areas in Bono, Adamawa, and Yobe states. In addition, FRAD has integrated GBV, education and early recovery and livelihood activities in Konduga, Mobbar and Monguno. this support will ensure these life-saving programs can scale up and be sustained to meet urgent needs for this crisis-affected population in Northeast Nigeria.

2 - Activities

Gender Based Violence Support for Self-employment or Wage-employment for women and Adolescent Following the completion of SAFE Curriculum by adolescents girls, FRAD will support the adolescent through small grants to acquire the resources needed to set up their businesses according to their business plans. Self-employment may include support to set up their own businesses either on their own or collectively in groups of up 3 specialized case management of GBV survivorsFRAD will work along side coordination with the government to provide specialize GBV case management services. FRAD will target both adolescents and women for these service. response will be In-line with global guidance in the Interagency GBV Case Management guidelines using survivor centered approach Dignity Kits Distribution FRAD will provide provide dignity kits to 1,500 beneficiaries. FRAD will focus majorly on camps and community of return where beneficiaries are returning without any possessions and are in desperate need of basic items for their safety and wellbeing. Psychosocial support for women and girlsIn collaboration with community structures, FRAD will provide basic psychosocial services for all women and girls accessing safe space. FRAD will work with trained personnel to implement psychosocial support (PSS) activities with wider range of women and girls. Digital Skills TrainingFRAD Foundation will train women and girls on digitals skills to reduce the risk of Gender based violence, the activity will bridge the digital divide for women and girls in terms of access to information and technology. life Skills TrainingLow source of income or poverty are mostly other contributors to gender base violence, the FRAD Foundation will train 900 beneficiaries on various skills of their choice to increase individual and household income. Community GBV PreventionFRAD will implement community GBV prevention activities using participatory approach to allow communities come up with initiatives to end GBV in their communities. this approach will include setting up men GBV action group that will be ambassadors to end GBV in their communities. General Protection Community Accountability Forum FRAD will establish community accountability forum in all the wards of intervention, the forum will serve as a group that will demand accountability of intervention by government and NGOs in their community. Establishment of Protection Action GroupThis group will be comprised of women and men to form the action group, the groups will identify human right violation and any lost of dignity and call for redress. Early Recovery and livelihood Community Dialogue sessions FRAD will organize various dialogue sessions in community to identify conflicts triggers and develop mitigation plans with the community. Rehabilitation of Community Structures or point of services.In consultation with the community, FRAD will identify in consultation with the community a critical infrastructure to the community and rehabilitate it to function maximally. Small business empowerment supportto increase household income, FRAD through this activity will work with small business to bust their business through business support and mentorship to increase business income and sustainability. Train government staff on good governanceFRAD under the project will train local government staff on good governance in other to increase output at the LGA level.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The Project will ensure protection mainstreaming at all phases of the project Ranging from inception down to close out of the project. FRAD would include Protection mainstreaming in the training of its staff during onboarding training, this training will ensure staff are aware of the protection terms and prevent violation of the right of beneficiaries as a result of our action.FRAD will give more consideration to disability inclusion during the implementation and targeting of beneficiaries. All facility sites and activity sites will be constructed in such a way that everyone can have access without any limitation especially for people with special needs. Although the project Target mostly women and girls, attention will be paid to ensure representation of all gender in community programming and staffing of the Project, doing so will ensure their is Gender balance. Protection Monitoring will be conducted by FRAD M&E unit on quarterly basis to ensure that all facility safe space and sites are mainstreaming protection and address all concerns reported in field monitoring Report by the M&E unit.FRAD Foundation will from time to time collect feedback from communities on their view to know if they field protection have been mainstream, if the result turns to be negative, FRAD will work towards addressing the Gab immediately.
