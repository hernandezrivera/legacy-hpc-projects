Project Summary

As a result of the crisis affecting Syria for over ten years now, the conflicts have left several areas around the country with considerable damages in the infrastructures, housing, and services.. causing a large number of the population to flee their areas of residence & seek refuge in neighboring areas. & after the military actions have ceased, IDPs are eager to return to their houses in their original areas of residence.
The Syrian Society for Social Development is planning to provide basic necessary life-saving services, through rehabilitation of Damaged houses for Returnees & Host Communities in various locations in the governorates of Aleppo, Hama, Idleb & Lattakia.
SSSD has previously implemented a similar project in the area of the suggested locations, rehabilitating Damaged houses, Schools, and sewerage systems serving the residential neighborhoods, in addition to community centers providing services to beneficiaries of the host community & returning population, & seeking to obtain official approvals for the assessed locations.

Mitigation measure: No security risks in project locations / (ERW) risks to be mitigated in coordination with competent authorities by SSSD team trained by UNMAS /Personnel is managed through SSSD offices & community centers in the proposed areas / Approvals to be obtained ahead of the start of the project. Risk: Beneficiaries subject to harm or other form of violence /exploitation Mitigation measure: 1. SSSD staff, volunteers & contractors (of both genders) sign a Code of Conduct. 2. a well-established & functional Code of Conduct Reporting Mechanism (CoCRM) & a clear pathway to report and deal with suspected violations including allegations of sexual exploitation and abuse. 3. SSSD adopts mechanisms to collect Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA)-related complaints focusing on confidentiality (mainly periodical visits of SSSD staff) complaints are directed to M & E team (referred to as case numbers & no names) to follow-up and handle with SSSD policy. Risk: Gender equality violations Mitigation measure: Selection criteria prioritizes vulnerability and needs of beneficiaries, mainly women or children headed households and households with elderly, disabilities. and all beneficiaries including women and girls are consulted during conducting the needs assessment, and at the design phase of the project.
