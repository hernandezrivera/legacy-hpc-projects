Project Summary

Project Summary
The North West and Southwest region of Cameroon has suffered from a serious crisis for the past five years leaving close to 2 million people in need of humanitarian assistance. The deteriorating security situation, killing of civilians, and burning down of homes and property has resulted in displacements of over 11,718 people forced to flee their homes with an amount of 10,000 most vulnerable displaced to bushes and villages. The NW region has been greatly affected with over 226,708 displaced people (MSNA August 2021)
The shelter and NFI general outlook for SW and NW regions is worrying, with a high percentage of the displaced population self-settled in the bush in rural areas and suffering from strong shelter needs.
In May 2018, the humanitarian community launched an Emergency Response Plan. Shelter and NFI was identified as the key sectorial priority after food, and follow by health, WASH, education, and protection. Local partners have flagged repeatedly that shelter needs are critical and needs to be prioritized.
This project intends to provide shelter kits and nonfood items to 1000 IDPS from 200HH in Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions living in bushes and farm huts and returnees whose homes were burnt
The main objective of this project is to improve the shelter condition of 1000IDPs households living in bush/forest settlements, returnees whose houses have been destroyed in the North West Region and vulnerable IDPs, through the provision of complete emergency shelter kits and NFI in Bui ( Jakiri, Kumbo,Mbven and Nkum sub division) and Donga Matung Division (New, Ako Ndu and Misaje).
And this project has as specific objectives to
- To improve shelter and living condition of extremely vulnerable households living in Bush/forest, and hard to reach areas, through the provision of emergency shelter and Nonfood items. This will be done incorporation with GBV by ensuring that men and women, people living with disabilities, pregnant and lactating women are given priority.
- Provision of emergency shelter including prevention of Covid 19 by carrying out temperature checks at entry and hand washing facilities at each distribution points, accountability to the affected population by having complaint and feedback mechanisms in the communities to 1000IDP household in Bui and Donga Matung within a period of six months
To achieve the above objectives, the following interventions and target have been put in place
- Training of all recruited focal persons on methodology of verification and confirmation
- Training and equipping of focal persons in all map out areas for targeting, distribution and the construction of 1000 emergency shelter.
- Distribution of shelter kits of tarpaulin, nails, wood, mattresses, blankets, households utensils like pots, spoons, stainless plates, mats, buckets, cups rechargeable torch, ropes and face mask to 1000 IDP households.
The shelter project is targeting vulnerable IDPs and returnees whose houses have been burnt specifically in hard to reach areas in bush/forest settlements in Bui and Donga Matung Division in the North West who have been severely affected by the socio political crisis.

1 - Besoins / Needs

With the escalation of tension in the NWSW regions of Cameroon, Bui and Donga Matung Division have been hard hit and many have lose property due to burning, others who are displaced from their original homes lack means of survival and are barely being supported by friends and well-wishers. Victims live in deplorable conditions with large families and have little or no access to household material that can improve their ability to living conditions. Also continuous displacement of the affected population due to frequent gun battles between warring factions is major cause for concern. Providing victims with shelter kits that can be used at safer sites is of utmost importance. This will serve as emergency packages that is easy to move along with should in case there is displacement to bushes. Shelter is one of the basic needs of every human person and lack of proper shelter necessitates attention. People who have been victims are already traumatized with negative revenge tendencies further aggravates the situation we are presently suffering from. It is important to assist these groups of persons, once their security and shelter are guaranteed; it contributes to building self-esteem and gives room for positive responsive attitudes towards the perpetrators of such acts. Knowing fully well that the victims are supported and thought of enables they have a sense of belonging. Provision of shelter kits contributes to part of the transformation process and change. The following activities will enable us realize the needs for this project. To improve shelter and living condition of extremely vulnerable households living in Bush/forest, and hard to reach areas, through the provision of emergency shelter and Nonfood items. This will be done incorporation with GBV by ensuring that men and women, people living with disabilities, pregnant and lactating women are given priority. - We have to recruit project staff both at office and field level ( focal points, Project managers, Shelter supervisor and M & E officer) - Carry out a needs assessment and verification and the size of the beneficiary population - Establishment of distribution site distribution related processes - Establishment of committees with adequate representation of women - Crowd controllers at the level of distribution site - Ensure equitable distribution of relief commodities using the appropriate distribution system - Make sure there is inclusion, safety, and accountability in the distribution process - On site monitoring of the distribution process - Do report on quality, quantity and impact of commodity distributions. Provision of emergency shelter including prevention of Covid 19 by carrying out temperature checks at entry and hand washing facilities at each distribution points, accountability to the affected population by having complaint and feedback mechanisms in the communities to 1000IDP household in Bui and Donga Matung within a period of one year - Identification and creation of initial beneficiary list with awareness of special needs to vulnerable people - Inform local authorities and community leaders about the expected date of distribution - Agree with community leaders on a storage area before delivery of items - Ensure security and safe spacious space for distribution to prevent Covid - Inform beneficiaries about the schedule of the distribution - Training of feedback committees to receive and register complaints - Movement of stocks to the field for distribution - Setting up the distribution point by putting up posters, sensitization and ensuring social distancing and wearing of face mask. - Coordination of the distribution and support for additional capacity if needed - Guidance on technical issues where appropriate for example protection referrals - Establish a complaint and feedback mechanisms for beneficiaries and authorities - Writing of distribution reports.
