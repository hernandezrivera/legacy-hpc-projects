Project Summary

The socio-political crisis in the North west and South west regions of Cameroon have been ongoing since 2016. Schools have unfortunately been one of the main targets during this crisis. During the 2021-2022 academic year, 66% of schools in both regions were still closed and for the 2022-2023 school year, 54% of schools still remain closed, causing the displacement of about 200,000 school aged children most of who remain out of school. According to the Cameroon Education Cluster Strategy of 2023-2025 an estimated 1,033,000 school-aged children have been affected by the crisis in the North West region. They have been denied the opportunity to learn and develop themselves, failing in achieving Sustainable Development Goal (SDG), specifically, the goal 4: ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. The current system is leaving more than 955,700 children behind. The impact of the crisis has deprived thousands of older children and youth of education. Displacement being in a child-headed household, being an ex-child soldier or being disabled compounds many of the losses suffered by those deprived of an education. For many of these children and youth, the experience of the crisis has made their participation in education a challenge with the disruption of education due to insecurity and attacks on schools. It is worthy of note that all school aged boys and girls affected by the Anglophone crisis be provided with access to inclusive, safe and quality education.
The Accelerated Education Programs are flexible, age appropriate programs that promote access to education in an accelerated time frame for such disadvantaged groups specifically, for out of school, over age children and youth excluded from education or who had their education interrupted due to the conflict. In this context, the AEP will fill a critical gap in the provision of essential educational services to the conflict affected populations and ensure learners receive an appropriate and relevant education responsive to their life circumstances.
OBJECTIVES:
GENERAL OBJECTIVE: To ensure crisis affected over aged boys and girls have access to alternative and continuous learning through the accelerated education program (AEP)
SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
1) To sensitize the community and education stakeholders on the Accelerated Education Program
2) To design and structure an Accelerated Education Program curriculum
3) Train teachers on the attributes of Accelerated learning methodologies
4) Identification and selection of direct beneficiaries
5) To provide teaching and learning materials
