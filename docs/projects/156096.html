Project Summary

In Mir Ali and Miran Shah Tehsil of North Waziristan, the education sector presents an alarming scenario.As per EMIS report (2016-17), in North Waziristan Agency (NWA), the female literacy rate was less than 2 per cent whilst it was 10 per cent for FATA. Half of the 4-9-year-old children population was out of school.( EMIS report (2015-16)Women Empowerment Organization (referred to as WEO) proposed project will aim at ensuring the provision of quality education services to returning children in 9 schools of Mir Ali, and Miran Shah area of North Waziristan Agency. The overall objective of the project is to provide safe, inclusive and quality education services to 2400 returning children (1200 Girls and 1200 Boys) by creating an enabling environment in the returning area.
WEO proposed project strategy includes the establishment of Twelve (12) Prefabricated schools (6 Girls and 6 Boys) in Mir Ali and Miran Shah Tehsil of North Waziristan Agency. According to response plan 2017, PHP funds providing an opportunity for a shift from Temporary Learning Centers to establishing pre-fabricated structures at fully damaged school sites. Beside this 4 Non functional schools will be rehabilitated (2 Girls and 2 boys Schools). WEO also proposed to install Solar Panels in 16 targeted schools. Report of Inter Agency Mission to NWA (3-6 Jan 2017 and Need Prioritization Mission (14-18 March 2017) stressed on the needs solar panels for schools as there is no electricity . WEO recently conducted a rapid need assessment (25 to 28 April 2017) in North Wazirstan Agency and on the basis of that assessment and the list of fully damage and non functional school shared by education cluster, WEO is targeting Sixteen (16) non-functional schools in Mir Ali and Miran shah for its education interventions.
In addition WEO will provide teaching and learning material (school-in-box, learning kits (school bag with stationery), recreational kits, white boards, and furniture, teachers' chairs/tables cupboards and students chairs ) in 16 targeted schools, and on the other hand it will form or reactivate Taleemi Islahi Jirga (TIJs) in 16 targeted schools, comprising of 8 Girls and 8 Boys Primary Schools. 8 Female Para Teachers will be hired on need basis and all teachers will be given teacher-training on child friendly teaching skills.
Building on the momentum of ongoing enrollment campaign across FATA,
WEO will organize 32 ‘Enrollment Campaigns’ at village and Agency level to create public awareness on the importance of education, with special focus on increasing the girls’ enrollment.
At the beginning of the project, need assessment will be carried out, which will help in profiling the school data related to enrollment, and the nature and extent of damages to educational institutions.
The project activities will support provision of education services to 1854 children (56% Girls and 44% Boys). Under the capacity building component, 5 Female Para-Teachers will be provided training on child-friendly teaching skills, and English as a medium of instruction. Furthermore community meetings,awareness-raising sessions, agency level seminars, walks will be arranged to increase the enrollment of both boys and girls in the targeted schools. WEO plan to enroll 600 new students in 16 schools out of which 50% will be girls.The teachers and government officials will be trained on the Disaster Risk Reduction. 2000 sets of IEC material containing messages on importance of Education, especially for girl’s education will be disseminated. Beside this local media campaigns will be carried out.WEO will publish messages in local newspapers on on importance of boys and girls education at agency level.

Needs

Since March 2015, the returns to North Waziristan are underway. Around 70% of the registered displaced families have returned while the remaining will return by March 2017. In Mir Ali and Miran Shah Tehsil of North Waziristan , the education sector presents an alarming scenario. As per EMIS report 2016 in North Waziristan, the Female literacy rate is less than 2 per cent whilst it is 10 per cent for FATA. Half of the 4-9-year-old children population was out of school as per EMIS report 2016. Women Empowerment Organization conducted a rapid need assessment (25-28 April 2017) in Mir Ali and Miran Shah area of North Waziristan . Mostly the schools were damaged either fully or partially. The schools visited in North Waziristan were in need of rehabilitation, basic teaching-learning material including text books. Based on discussion with the communities, civil administration, teachers, and visit to different locations, repair, rehabilitation and reconstruction and rehabilitation of schools(Girls and Boys), provision of water and restoration of WASH facilities emerged as a prime need in schools During an inter-agency mission from 3-6 January, 2017, most of the educational institutions visited were found to be either partially or fully damaged. The children were seen sitting on bare floors. The schools lacked basic teaching and learning material. As Per education authorities, 32 primary schools are fully damaged in Mir Ali and Miran Shah Tehsils of the North Waziristan. As per multi cluster Assessment, The education sector was badly affected due to heavy damage of the infrastructure, around 70 per cent of girls and 30 per cent of boys were not enrolled in primary schools. Key issues were the low priority of education for parents (15 per cent of HHs), long distances to schools (12 per cent of HHs), child labour (10 per cent of HHs) and protection/security concerns (9 per cent of HHs). For instance, protection issues and security concerns were mostly highlighted by female-headed HHs (15 per cent of female-headed HHs versus 2. 8 per cent of male-headed HHs). Similarly, child labour was more prevalent among boys (10 per cent versus 3 per cent). As per Inter - Agency Mission to North Waziristan January 03 – 06, 2017 and WEO rapid need assessment the following are the most urgent needs. Immediate needs:Need of emergency education package like teaching and learning material (school in a box kits, recreation kits, school bags, stationery, pens/pencils, school mats, furniture, text books, black/white boards, chalks/markers, dusters, class room charts, laboratory equipment, teachers’ tables and chairs, and staff room furniture and WASH facilities. Reconstruction of fully damaged schools or as an immediate alternate installation of pre‐fabricated structures, ensuring deployment and attendance of teachers; particularly female teachers, and improve teacher‐student ratio in high enrollment schools. Recovery and Rehabilitation needs:Repairs/rehabilitation of school buildings including restoration of water supplies and provisionof water/rehabilitation of WASH facilities.Installing solar panels at schools for provision of electricity to schools, water pumping, laboratory.Female hostel facility for female teachers coming from neighboring districts.Launch an ‘Every Child in School’ campaign to support reactivation and strengthening of TIJs Based on above assessment and findings of Multi-Cluster assessment, inter cluster mission report, need prioritization mission report, WEO assessment, Women Empowerment Organization is proposing a project aimed at ensuring the provision of quality education services to returning children in 16 schools of Mir Ali and Miran Shah area of North Waziristan . The overall objective of the project is to provide safe, inclusive and quality education services to 1,850 returning children (44% Boys and 56% Girls) by creating an enabling environment in the returning area.

Activities

WEO proposed project activities includes the establishment of Twelve (12) pre-fabricated school structures and rehabilitation of 4 damage schools in Mir Ali and Miran Shah, All the 16 targeted schools are non functional. WEO physically assessed the schools during rapid need assessment (25-28 April 2017) and targeted these schools. WEO plans to enroll 600 new student in targeted 16 schools out of 50% will be girls students. WEO will provide conducive learning environment to 600 newly enrolled 2400 already students through construction and rehabilitation of targeted schools. WEO has also proposed to to install solar panels at 12 Pre-fabricated schools and 4 rehabilitated schools. Report of Inter Agency Mission to NWA stressed on the needs of solar panels.WEO will provide teaching and learning material [school-in-box, learning kits (school bag with stationery), recreational kits, white boards, teachers' chairs/tables student chairs] in 16 targeted schools. WEO will form/reactivate TIJs in targeted 16 schools, out of which 8 TIJs will be in Girls Primary Schools. Capacity of TIJs will be enhanced through teachers training. WEO will hire 5 Female Para Teachers as there is a shortage of Female teachers in targeted schools.WEO will organize 32 Enrollment Campaigns at village and at Agency level for Public awareness on importance of education with special focus on girls to increase their enrollment. The project activities will support provision of education services to 3000 children, including 1500 girls and 1500 Boys. Under the capacity building component, 8 female para teachers will be provided training on Child friendly teaching skills.24 community meetings and 24 community awareness sessions will be organized, 10 teachers and 10 government officials will be trained on DRR out of which 50% will be females.. WEO will develop social mobilization and exit plans for this project. The project will be executed as planned and plans will be translated into actions in compliance with all agreed standards and procedures. Measures will be carried out to ensure cost efficiency, mitigation of risks and effective management of project implementation to meet objectives and time schedule.
