Project Summary

QC will increase access to safe drinking water sources for 7500 beneficiaries including IDPs and host communities in Rashad locality in South Kordofan State. QC will drill and install 15 hand pumps as well as construction of 2 units of common latrines in one school in the same locality. Each latrine unit consists of 4 units.
Also, QC will install one water yard in Abuhugar area in North Kordofan. The water yard consists of 2 connected well, 10,000-gallon capacity tank, animal trough, loading card as well as water stand taps. This yard will serve 5 villages where it can serve more than 10000 people as well as animals according to its capacity. Solar system will be used to operate the water yard as also there will be Electric Generator for emergency use if needed.
The aim of “universal” access to drinking water, sanitation and hygiene “for all” in Targets 6.1 and 6.2 implicitly highlight the need for expanding WASH monitoring from the household to non-household settings, such as schools, as we progress from the MDG to the SDG era. To contribute to this goal QC will construct 89 natural water coolers in Gezira state which will cover 89 schools. About 31,150 pupils will access safe drinking water using the installed water coolers.
In River Nile state, QC will install Water Treatment Plant in Barbar locality, where three villages of about 6000 population will drink treated water instead of raw water.
QC select targeted areas and beneficiaries depending on multifactorial aspects including need assessment, Fund availability and donor interest as well as accessibility and geological formation.

1 - Needs

In Sudan a total of 12.1 million people, including refugees, are in need WASH assistance. This population is at risk of contracting a growing number of WASH-related diseases such as acute watery diarrhea (AWD), cholera, diarrhea, dysentery, hepatitis E, typhoid, acute respiratory infections, polio, and malnutrition.As a result of conflict and factional fighting in 2021 & 2022, thousands of IDPs, returnees, and resident communities were displaced in different hot states including South Kordofan state. The 2022 HRP identifies a total of 39 localities as priority one. Of these, 7 are located in South Kordofan. About 0.9 million people are in need base on Sudan HNO 2022 in South Kordofan. The Joint Education Needs Assessment (JENA, 2021) found that there is a significant need to invest in school infrastructure, including water and sanitation facilities. About 46 per cent of schools have no access to clean water for drinking or handwashing, and across the schools assessed, the average user to latrine ratio was 132:1. QC will contribute to address this problem in South Kordofan by installing 2 latrine units in one school of total of 4 latrines for each as well as provision of 89 natural water cooler in 89 schools in Gezira state. In September 2022, Barbar locality recorded the highest amount of houses destructions in River Nile state floods particularly in the west and East administrative units. During late July 2022 and up to Mid- August 2022 above-normal levels of flooding have been occurring in the area caused massive damage to House Holds, public institutions (schools, health facilities), and loss of crops and livestock. According to the Federal Ministry of Health emergency unit report, more than three thousand HHS were affected by the recent floods. As per Save the Children Rapid Need Assessment conducted on 18th Aug 2022, around 2633 totally damaged, 746 partially damaged. Families have lost their clean Water sources, which increases the risk of water-based diseases. QC will increase access to safe drinking water sources for Brbar West communities including people affected by floods by Construction of Water Treatment Plant which will serve 6000 individuals including about 2000 Flood affected people. This will help people to drink treated water instead of raw water.
