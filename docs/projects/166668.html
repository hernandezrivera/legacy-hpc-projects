Project Summary

1. Engage communities, including CHWs, activists, female leaders and other community leaders into COVID19 community-based surveillance in Sofala; This will include identification and training of CLGRC members in matters of COVID19, GBV and case references to PRM, SDMAS 2.Establish COVID-19 prevention points (hygiene, disinfection,..) at transit places frequented by women and girls (markets, public transport); This will include the distribution of protective masks to women, girls, more vulnerable groups and the creation of a prevention committee to ensure that measures are followed; the committee will also ensure that people with symptoms of COVID 19 do not stay in these places and prevent people in transit from being harassed without protection. 3. Case management and psychosocial support to GBV survivors ; This will include training in GBV case management and psychosocial care. Members of the GBV sub-cluster in the province and districts will be part of this training. Complementarily, a call center will be established to guarantee long-distance assistance since people will not be able to leave home 4. Develop awareness raising campaigns through radio, TV, and social media to disseminate messages about to prevent and mitigate risks associated with increased GBV and SRH cases during the COVID-19 crisis and inform audiences about available SRH GBV services; These campaigns will also be important for Dissemination of messages and information related to GBV and ensure the response to COVID-19 does not reproduce or perpetuate harmful gender norms or discriminatory practice. Pamphlets will also be produced to support the campaign because people cannot leave the house or receive visits from activists
