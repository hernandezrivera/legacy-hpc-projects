Project Summary

The project targets 200,000 individuals affected in the target areas of Kukawa, Nganzai, Marte, and Kala-Balge LGAs of Borno state. Monclub will ensure that the most pressing needs of IDPs, host communities and returnees in the LGAs are met.
Mon Club International aims to minimise the undermining effects of breastfeeding practices and to improve knowledge of optimal infant and young child feeding practices among affected IDP camps and host communities in Nganzai, Kala-Balga, Marte and Kukawa LGAs of Borno state. The targeted beneficiaries are caregivers and their children 0-23 months. This requires specific expertise and technical support, that MonClub will provide with her experienced staff and consultants. MonClub is well positioned and already on ground in these areas and will leverage on that to provide response to the affected communities.
Monclub will focus intervention on locations with the highest needs, mostly location of returns and areas most humanitarian organizations consider inaccessible.
Monclub will prioritize working with other actors on the ground to avoid possible duplications. With sex and age disaggregated data, this project will target all affected persons including IDPs and returnees. Monclub will pay specific attention to the people with special needs such as pregnant andlactating women, women headed households, elderly and disabled, people with disabilities, and chronic diseases.
Inclusive WASH services like disability-inclusive collection points (for water facilities), and inclusion of people living with disabilities to WASH committees would be prioritized. Monclub will ensure that WASH facilities are physically and sex-disaggregated, clearly marked, lockable from the inside, and well lit. Monclub will ensure that gender is respected and that community hygiene promoters are mostly women from targeted locations.
Facilities for disposal of menstrual waste and the specific needs of children and persons with disabilities would be considered. Full participation and involvement of community leaders on protection awareness activities directed to improve children's welfare (Gils and boys) would be ensured. In mainstreaming protection, our team will coordinate with other stakeholders to enhance the reunion of separated children and prevent gender-based violence among the IDPs and surrounding communities, while ensuring that all GBV cases are reported and referred for support.

2 - Activities

1. MonClub will conduct mass MUAC screening for all children 6-59 months for identification of children with severe acute malnutrition, providing treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) among children 0-59 month without complications through our Outpatient therapeutic Programme (OTP). 2. There will also be provision of treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) with medical complications in Nutrition Stabilization Centres (SCs) while referral/transfer children with severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and medical complication who require advanced treatment to higher level medical facility. We will ensure the promotion of appropriate Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) practices in emergencies through counselling of primary care givers of infants 0-23 months and pregnant women. There will be provision of micronutrients tablets (IFA) to pregnant and lactating mothers and distribution of MNP to children 6-23 months for enriching family foods to prevent micronutrients deficiencies, where partners are not implementing BSFP. 3. Provide Vitamin A supplementation, deworming and prophylaxis to children 6-59 months. Finally, we will carry out capacity building activities for the SPHCDA/Health workers in Nutrition in Emergencies interventions.4. Formation and implementation of IYCF through mother to mother support groups in the implementing locations. 5. MonClub will repair all available water sources and introduce batch / bucket chlorination to ensure Household (HH) drinking water is safe. We will distribute WASH NFIs to cover 80% of the affected population with emphasis on the affected HH, while ensuring immediate chlorination of all drinking water sources, monitoring FRC and promoting HH water treatment with aquatabs. There will be provision for safe sanitation access to displaced population through construction of emergency latrine and rehabilitation of existing facilities. 6. We will conduct hygiene promotion including hand hygiene in any population with suspected disease outbreak.7. MonClub will also see that the capacity of the beneficiaries built to operate and maintain the WASH facilities that have been provided. Monclub will collaborate with relevant WASH actors that are core to the project communities in these locations. Government representatives such as the Ministry of Water Resources, RUWASA, Borno state Water Corporation would be informed of any planned activity within their community.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

When targeting households, gender issues will be implicitly tackled, as the household entity is often shared by men and women. The project will be so designed to include both males and females as beneficiaries. The project takes into consideration gender differences by coming up with designed interventions which are specific to female beneficiaries. The project will create synergies between both males and females through an approach which provides the opportunity to both genders to interact and participate jointly in project activities. The project is all inclusive as volunteers provide community outreach services which can be easily accessed by people with disabilities. As part of project monitoring, data will be gathered and segregated by both gender and disability type. Monclub will also work closely with the Nutrition sector at LGA and state levels. We will also coordinate with all organizations that work in the Nutrition sector to be sure we avoid duplication of functions and apply best practices in implementation. Lastly, we will ensure that we carry out community engagement sessions with the implementation communities so that we can have their buy-in and also ensure that they are a major stakeholder in all aspects of our project. This will help to strengthen sustainability and resilience. Monclub will implement and hold itself accountable to her Minimum Standards for Gender Equality and Women’s Rights in Emergencies, safeguarding of children and vulnerable adult’s policy, and Do No Harm policy.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

In all the locations, Mon Club will conduct community mobilization and sensitization exercises prior to and throughout the implementation period to ensure that beneficiary and non-beneficiary communities are well informed about the project. Field staff will explain the key components and the approach, as well as soliciting regular and ongoing feedback on the implementation. Project Management Committees (PMCs) will be formed, trained and empowered in-line with best practices.Active FCM desks will be set up to receive and respond to community members.
