Project Summary

WFP plans to provide food assistance (conditional and unconditional) to IDPs, returnees and non-displaced in cooperation with the local crisis committees, the Ministry of IDPs and cooperating partners. WFP will be responsive to population movements and will identify and include vulnerable Libyan populations as and when needs arise.
Following a country-specific agreement with the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), and in line with the UNHCR-WFP global memorandum of understanding, WFP will support the most vulnerable refugees registered by the UNHCR. Refugees will be assisted at the community development centres and the gathering and departure facility (GDF).
In coordination with other actors and based on assessed needs, WFP could consider the distribution of in-kind assistance to the most vulnerable migrants who are able to access the medical centers, clinics and/or who have been approached by organizations doing outreach.

Needs

According to the HNO 2019, 297,818 people are in need of food (180,664 Libyans and 117,154 non-Libyans). WFP will focus its targeting exercise on the mantiquas deemed to be the more food insecure in the HNO 2019 without excluding the mantiquas where the food security level is moderate or acceptable as the huge use of negative coping strategies all over Libya might have a direct impact on the food security level of the populations that are not considered food insecure if the situation continues to deteriorate. During the implementation phase, quick needs assessments will be conducted prior to the enrollment of the beneficiaries in the programme to assess their food security level.

Activities

Food assistance to displaced Libyans, returnees, Host-communities, refugees and potentially migrants through conditional and unconditional programmes.
