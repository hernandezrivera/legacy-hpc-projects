Project Summary

Orange Organization is looking forward to implementing a project to enhance access to income generating opportunities for vulnerable people and support the vocational skills acquisition.
The project aims to create a Technical vocational and educational center to provide knowledge and technical skills that enable people to access job opportunities.
- Map the market to enable skilled workers internship opportunities and encourage business to acquire them
- Offer Micro grants for people who demonstrate abilities in starting new businesses and entrepreneurships.
- Offer on the job training stipend of beneficiaries able to join the labor market though covering internship costs.
The project will consider coordination with Protection and FSL sectors and partners to establishing prioritization criterion for the respective activities, support social cohesion between IDPs and residents, contribute to a gender balance in livelihood opportunities in a culturally appropriate atmosphere

4 - Needs

The OCHA situation 7th, Report of 29th, Jan.2020 shows that the number of IDPs exceeded 390k since the security deterioration in Marret Alnouman in the beginning of DEC19. The Syrian pound according to the same report had an unprecedented devaluation being around 1100-1200 Syrian pound per US dollar in January 2020 compared to 640 pounds per US dollar in mid-October 2019 causing the value chain in the market to deteriorate and large portions on the communities unable to afford basic goods.A Labor market assessment (LMA) conducted by Orange in Azaz subdistrict of Aleppo on Sep.2019 to inform the design and scope of future projects in the area. The objective was to collect robust quantitative and qualitative data about the state of the economy, the hiring preferences and practices of employers in the region, and the skills necessary for young people seeking employment. The LMA used a combination of surveys, interviews, and focus group discussions. While security and cost of equipment and materials are key challenges in the business environment, the lack of skills and access to financing where main challenge for around 40% of the participants. In employment preferences, gender seems to be an issue where 55% of employers preferred to employ men and another 27% has no preference. Only 20% of employers evaluate their employees’ technical skills to be good.The assessment key findings are used to inform the design of this project, identify the training topics, and help inform the mapping of job opportunities.The assessment concluded the following recommendations:- Communication and technical skills are on the highest demand in the Labor market- Professionalism and work ethics are of great importance- Job financing or availably of equipment and materials are on of the biggest obstacles for starting businessesMost job opportunities are relying on personal connections.

5 - Implementation of activities

- During the initial stages of the project, Orange will be conducting a through market needs assessment which will inform decisions about the market needs as well as the type of the technical, vocational, and educational trainings needs, livelihoods obstacles, training needs, etc. - TVET Technical and Vocational Education and Training intervention aims to provide Skill Development Trainings to vulnerable people while directly equipping them to enter the current job market/start their own business endeavours. All courses and training programs are designed to improve skills, knowledge, competencies and capabilities of young people towards a sustainable career opportunity and according to the local and cultural needs of the area. Specific training activities will be tailored for women to be included in the training and later eligible to reception of micro-grants and internships. - Micro-grants will be given to beneficiaries from the TVET and job readiness center to be provided with in-kind equipment, materials and support for their entrepreneurship projects, along with running cost support for the starting phase of the project to be managed by Orange.The candidates will receive MSME training to have the necessary tools to manage their fresh projects. And couches will be provided to support beneficiaries to plan and start their projects. This will tackle the issues obstacles related to financing and availability of job equipment and materials. - Internship: Orange, will create networks with business activities, local community and partners to refer beneficiaries of the TVET and job readiness center to relevant job opportunities according to their skills and abilities, and cover their stipends in the probationary period to encourage employers to acquire, creating a connection and an incentive for both parties and opening an employment channel with employers.
