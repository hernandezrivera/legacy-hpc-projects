Project Summary

The activities aim to positively impact three main areas: respond immediately to nutritional needs of U-5 children and PLW fleeing from conflicts and sheltered in host communities and accommodation sites, acute malnutrition cases identification at the community level and treatment, and IYCF promotion.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The proposed intervention unfolds on two main layers. On the one hand, it aims to strengthen the community-based actors’ (APEs, activists, local leaders, primary school teachers, etc.) capacity to single out acute malnutrition cases among U-5 children and PLWs to be addressed at the community, outpatient (without clinical complications) and inpatient care level (with clinical complications), as well as to disseminate IYCF key messages. On the other hand, the intervention sets out to reinforce and support local institutions, namely the local SDSMASs and DPS of Cabo Delgado, so that to improve the overall activities coordination among partners, information-sharing and data collection on AM. The reference policies for the implementation of the project will be the PRN and the PIN (Pacote de Intervenção Nutricional) and activities will take place both in newly established accommodation centres and communities hosting IDPs.As to key activities, the capacity building aimed at community-based actors to detect and follow up U-5 children and PLWs AM cases will be carried out in close coordination local health authorities and according to MISAU guidelines. Similarly, the active case finding, screening and surveillance of U-5 and PLW will be realized in collaboration with local SDSMAS. The coordination with SDSMAS, Nutrition cluster and DPS will be paramount in each proposed target area in order to avoid duplications and overlapping with existing partners working in nutrition.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1.1: Restore and expand capacity for life-saving nutrition interventions through health facilities and outreach activities in communities with high influx of IDPs (including host communities) and accommodation centers/ resettlement site1.2 Procurement and delivery of specialized nutritious foods, nutrition supplies for therapeutic feeding and other supplements as well as nutrition equipment, for treatment of acute malnutrition in children 6-59 months and PLWs, including those with HIV1.3 Support the supply chain logistics for nutrition1.4 Support Human Resource surge to address higher needs at the health system to support the IMB and other outreach interventions1.5 Strengthen the CHWs (APEs and ACS) in treatment of acute malnutrition at community level1.6 Undertake nutrition assessments and SMART surveys for assessing the nutritional status of the populations at risk2.1 Implement active case finding and referral of malnourished children and monitor the nutrition situation through MUAC screenings2.2 Strengthen the CHWs (APEs and ACS) in delivering micronutrient supplementation at community level3.1 Support in the IYCF corners in both the accommodation centers/ resettlement sites3.2 Promote optimal breastfeeding and complementary feeding practices, and overall Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) best practices messages for caregivers of children under two either though awareness sessions and cooking demonstration in the communities with high influx of IDPs (including host families)3.3 Training of CHWs (APEs and ACS) on topical areas related to IYCF and HIV to provide one-on-one sessions at household level

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential harms and risks may arise, for instance, either when the list of beneficiaries selected for the activity of distributions (1.1 and 1.2) are not double-checked and then the products ends up in the hands of wrong beneficiaries; or when the intended beneficiaries sell the products received from the distributions or give them to a third party/person in exchange for personal favors not related to improving the nutritional status of the target population. To avoid this, the list of beneficiaries will be verified by AVSI staff before the distributions to ensure that the beneficiaries meets the vulnerability. Moreover, the consumption of product will be monitored through post-distribution assessment.The access to assistance that does not depend directly from the implementing partner is also promoted at the SDSMAS and DPS level. By coordinating closely with the local authority, AVSI will advocate for increasing the assistance to people in need whenever deemed necessary.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project itself is based on the strong participation of the beneficiaries for the activities implementation. Before the beginning of the project, focus group activities will be planned to understand which are the beneficiaries expectations concerning project impact. These meetings will be organized and held in close coordination with local leaders and the existing health structure at the community level created through national policies (APEs, health committee members, and, where in place, activists). Firstly, community’s opinion on how to adapt the general aspects of activities implementation and coordination according to local culture, customs, beliefs and capacity, will be taken into account and incorporated into the work methodology. Secondly, it will be created a specific hotline for feedback and complaints related to the project and it will be promoted the Linha Verde. It is worthy to note that AVSI has adopted an internal policy on accountability to Affected People on the March 23th, 2020 based on the IASC Commitments on Accountability to Affected Populations (CAAP).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and nutritional assessment have been included as key activities. During their implementation, the detection of U-5 children and PLW with disabilities will be reported and given special attention. This translates into prioritizing the abovementioned target population as to the assistance for both moderate and severe acute malnutrition especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support both for the distribution of nutritional supplements, infant formula and emergency food ratios and medical check-ups in health facilities. Moreover, it is worthy to note that very often people with disabilities are discriminated within their own communities. This can be a compounding factor when people with disabilities arrived in a host community, as in the case of IDPs. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Attention will be given to the disposal of solid residues used throughout the implementation of all the activities. Notably, the packages of RUFTP and emergency food ratios reaching the beneficiaries will be disposed so that to not impact negatively the target population environment. Moreover, given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy efficiency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal. Moreover, ASVI adopted an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
