Project Summary

The project aims to secure the safety and dignity of the vulnerable internally displaced households in informal camps by maintaining the available and essential services, providing light rehabilitation for the infrastructure, and deploying firefighting points in the site.
The intervention seeks as well to build an accountability-based community in sites through establishing well trained having-capacity site committees, which are responsible to transmit the gained knowledge to the IDPs and reflect the governance environment in these sites. The site committees will be accountable for the provided services, reporting the gaps and needs and coordinating with the respective organization, that provide services. The intervention will focus on the care and maintenance of infrastructure and facilities to ensure safe and dignified living conditions and to prevent and mitigate the impact of incidents in IDP sites.
Therefore, this project seeks to provide support to IDP sites in Salqin and Qurqeena to build the resilience of the communities and promote the safe environment and dignity of life-saving services at 50 camps located in NWS.
By pursuing the referred main objective, the project aims to achieve the following outcomes:
1. Improving the management quality in and accountability of IDP sites
2. Provision of streamlined humanitarian life-saving, multi-sectoral assistance in IDPs sites
For this purpose, a set of comprehensive and interlinked activities will be conducted in coordination with CCCM, WASH, Shelter and Education Clusters. In particular, the project's activities include:
• Establishment Camp management committees as well as Site Management committees, that are going to engage the IDPs and build the governance and accountability mechanisms of the site.
• Building the Communities’ capacity by establishing rapid deployed team, which will conduct diverse training and capacity building sessions for the established committees.
• Enhancing the Engagement and accountability of Community members through FGDs, establishing sub-communities and feedback mechanisms.
• facilitation IDP sites with Fire Safety Points and Fire response items
• Provision of camp care and camp maintenance through conduct regular repair activities to infrastructure and public facilities
Furthermore, the project foresees the identification of vulnerable HHs with a specific focus on severe vulnerability, including female-headed households and PwDs. Moreover, the project is specifically designed to foster social cohesion among IDPs themselves through the establishment of a Community Committees for advising the design and implementation of the project activities, fostering local ownership, being accountable and ensuring sustainability of the achievements of the project upon its completion.
The project foresees the participation of all relevant stakeholders at all stages of the operations management cycle.
During camp management committees' trainings, TAS will focus on the importance of preserving the environment, where these committees will spread knowledge and raise the camp residents' awareness of the best ways to help preserve the environment and the most key factors that lead to damage to it.

3 - Needs

As the conflict in Syria continues, the number of IDPs increased significantly, with many IDPs forced to seek shelter to live. IDP sites are a last resort option that hosts vulnerable IDPs, providing minimal services and temporary solutions.
