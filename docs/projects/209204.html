Project Summary

World Vision aims to provide support for vulnerable people affected recently by the current Sudan conflict which left most of them in urgent need for protection services as the conflict is prolonged and expanded. World Vision intervention will provide comprehensive GBV and Child protection services as well will deliver general protection services for IDPs, Returnees and vulnerable host communities in Blue, South Darfur, South Kordofan and East Darfur.
World Vision intervention will through Strengthening individual protection assistance to response to protection needs, ensure Persons with specific needs referred to specialized services , as well provide legal assistance and provide case management, MHPSS for PSN, in addition will conduct protection monitoring for vulnerable people, and support PSN with cash assistance and supply in kinds, also World Vision will support Multi-Purpose Community Centers.
As part from Child protection intervention, World Vision will support Un accompanied and Separated children, provide MHPSS and case management, will raise community awareness and advocate to end child violence, build capacity of social workers, case workers on case management and PSS, Strengthen community structures and community engagement on child protection, as well support child friendly safe spaces.
World Vision will support GBV programming through provide support for GBV survivors by providing case management and PSS for affected girls and women, and support women centers, also WV will strengthen referral pathway services including Clinical Management of Rape-CMR, in addition WV will support GBV survivors with dignity kits, build capacity of services providers by providing structured training on case management, PSS, CMR. as well will strengthen capacity of community member to response to GBV survivors through GBV trainings, conduct awareness raising on GBV, SEA, and provide start up capitals for GBV survivors to mitigate risk.
