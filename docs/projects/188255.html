Project Summary

SKT organization is proposing an integrated project of Non-food items and Camp Coordination and Camp Management activities in 17 IDPs sites of the Harim cluster for 7 months.
The project aims to address critical and urgent life-saving needs for winterization and protection of the most vulnerable internally displaced persons who reside in 17 IDPs sites. The project proposes providing NFI assistance of stoves and bio-mass fuel for most vulnerable IDPs families and supports IDPs sites with Fire Response System through awareness-raising and training sessions including fire response resources for fire incidents in IDPs sites.
The project targets17 IDPs sites listed under Harim Cluster, located in Harim, Kafr Hum, and Besnaya – Bseineh and Set Aatekacommunities. Targeting 1200 IDPs families / 6000 IDPs individuals.
As in the current global pandemic of COVID-19, SKT has developed the distribution procedures in the pre-distribution phase, distribution phase, and after the distribution phase. 15 steps in the sequence are detailed in the activity of distribution.
SKT Procurement and Logistics Department have taken pre-measurements on the procurement process of NFI items to foster the distribution process of NFIs and provide flexibility to the programs department with optimized resources.
SKT non-food item response will be based on the SNFI cluster guideline for the distribution of stoves and heating fuel in 14 IDPs sites.
1200 families / 6000 individuals will receive 250 KG per month for 3 months (total 750 KG per family) of olive-pit biomass fuel for heating. Where the total amount of fuel to be distributed is 2035.5 tons. The first distribution will be commenced in the third week of December/2020. Each distribution will take 13-14 days. Responsible staff on distribution day will follow the new procedures of the SKT distribution process.
1,663, of the families who will receive heating fuel, will also receive heating and cooking stoves of good quality and applicable to the heating fuel type that will be distributed across the 14 IDPs sites.
