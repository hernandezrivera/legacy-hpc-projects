Project Summary

The proposed Hope 360 initiative for peace project is aimed at scaling up timely and equitable access to quality basic and vocational educational empowerment, through community mobilization, sensitization and advocacy, and capacity building. The project is targeting to reach 5000 IDP, out of school children which are in the age (4-17 years) in MMC, Hawul and Jere Local government area of Borno state and 2000 children within the age bracket 4-7 years will be targeted for sensitized on basic education while 3000 children within the age bracket 8-17 years will be targeted on sensitization on both basic and vocation education childrenliving with disability will be mainstreamed into both. The proposed project is schedule to run for one year successful.
Overall specific objective of the project: Increase Regular and Timely Equitable Access to quality education services to 5000 vulnerable Boys and Girls (IDPs and Host Community) in Hawul, MMC and Jere LGA.
The project objective are.
1. To increase vulnerable Boys and Girls access to quality basic and vocation educational through orienting education and help the children to developed knowledge, skills values and behavior needed for sustainable development to the local government Hawul, MMC and Jere local government Borno state
2. To increase awareness, sensitized and advocacy on importance of quality basic and vocational education to the child through Back to School Campaign to the targetted local government to benefit of quality basic and vocation education.
3. To increase the safe and equitable access to sufficiency quality of education empowerment and equipped the children on various vocation through quality vocation educational capacity training.
Project Justification
For the past 7 years it is estimated that over Two HundredThousand (200,000) children in Hawul, MMC and Jere Local government area of Borno state are out of school children and have no access to basic and vocation education with Jere and MMC constituting over 80 percent thereby living the children moving round the street and area hence increase the number of violence within LGA’s. this is due to fact that the children within this locality feel abandoned and neglected by caregivers and community, inability of the parent or caregiver to provide quality education for this children and no emergence response have been taking to solved the issue or meet to their need.
Considering the increasing number or nature of the out of school children and will still be increasing if emergence response of Hawul, MMC and Jere are not met. Gearing towards the HRP and Sector objectives of the project Hope 360 Initiative for Peace with the HRP Fund Grant will focused on providing timely educational emergence response services to Hawul, MMC and Jere LGA in Borno state which are of high risk of increasing number of out of school children in other to restore peaceful co-existing and promote sustainable development in target location.
