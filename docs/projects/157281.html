Project Summary

the project aims to Improve access to Primary health care services including Medical referral services to the people affected by emergencies and conflicts in Bau locality in Blue Nile state, tow localities in South Kordofan (Abu Jubaiha and El Abassya) and West Kordofan (Wad Banda locality ) through continue support the local MOH to operate non-function health facilities by provision of basic curative and preventive health care. and provision of quality maternal and child health services, as well as the project will support and strengthen the capacity of local communities to better preparedness and response of health threads and diseases outbreak.

Needs

SRCS is the one the humanitarian actors with a presence in the field, having access to all areas affected by emergencies , providing humanitarian support to effected population including IDPs & Returnees also their hosting communities, SRCS interventions target the most vulnerable people , SRCS had humanitarian intervention in Bau locality during 2016-2018 Supported by SHF by operated 2 clinics), based on the SRCS reports submitted to MOH 45% of medical consultations were children under five years of age and 37% were females. Malaria, Acute respiratory infection and diarrheal diseases were reported as the main causes of morbidity 22,318 male and 43,159 females (about 16% of the total consultations), the common diseases reported included Diarrhea, Acute Respiratory and Malaria. These are expected due to limited access to clean water, poor sanitation and inadequate shelter conditions, poor infrastructure, lack of health services and lack of trained human resources. The situation is expected to worsen with continues new displacement due to insecurity situation in surrounding areas. It was noted high morbidity rate among women (more than 55%) due to lack of health services particularly maternal and child health services, women do not go to seek medical support during a challenging pregnancy since they often do not have transportation means to the near health facilities in addition to There are no or limited midwifery and ante-natal services in most targeted areas Based on that assessment had been conducted by SRCS in collaboration with SMOH in Kadugli locality in July 2017 the 9th and Rashad Locality in July the 12th 2017, malaria, acute respiratory infection and diarrheal diseases were reported as the main causes of morbidity. Also it was noted that high morbidity rate among women due to lack of health and behavior change awareness including family planning ,FGM as well as the community culture which Impedes women to be a part of decision at family and community level.
