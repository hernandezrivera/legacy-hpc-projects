Project Summary

Conflict affected population in Borno are experiencing unimaginable nightmares, with the beginning of
the rains which is usually accompanied with heavy/strong sandstorms, winds and the rains. In the past
weeks the heavy rains/winds have already rendered many homeless as their emergency shelters and
NFIs have been destroyed, coupled with already over congested camps.
This project proposed by GREENCODE titled ’Scaling-up Emergency Shelters and Improved NFIs for
IDPs (Old and New Arrivals) in camps and Host Communities in MMC and Jere LGAs of Borno State is
a 9 month designed to contribute to closing some of the emergency and NFI gaps as assessed by our
team, CCCM partners and compiled by the CCCM and Shelter/NFI sector.
The sector shelter/NFI gap analysis shows that Maiduguri alone has shelter gap of 17,498 and 5,378
households are without NFIs kits. Similarly, Jere LGA that is hosting majority of the conflict affected
population need 22,751 shelters and 5,822 households need NFI kits. This figure is mostly likely to
increase with the rains as most shelter will be destroyed by both rains/heavy wind-storm and floods.
The project will be implemented in partnership with Local Community Development Initiative (LOCDIN)
who is also an active member of CCCM/Shelter/NFI partner and has experience in camp management
and shelter issues, so in this project LOCDIN will be leading in Land Advocacy, training of Enumerators
for Shelter Assessment and join GREENCODE in weekly and monthly monitoring of both shelter and
NFI distribution and other project activities

1 - Needs

GREENCODE team and implementing partners have conducted on-the-spot assessment and have confirm that the need for emergency shelters and NFIs are on the increase as a result of new arrivals, fire outbreaks in some camps and recently the rain/windstorms.According to Shelter/NFI Sector Gap Analysis Report for May, 2020, the report has is that Maiduguri alone has shelter gap of 17,498+ and 5,378 households are without NFIs kits. Similarly, Jere LGA that is hosting majority of the conflict affected population needs over 22,751 shelters and 5,822+ households need NFI kits. This figure is mostly likely to increase with the rains as most shelter will be destroyed by both rains/heavy windstorm and floods.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

GREENCODE and Partner will continue to ensure that protection is mainstreamed into all its activities as noted in the previous sections. The mandate of our humanitarian intervention is rooted in the 'Do NO HARM’ Principle, also GREENCODE approach to protection is governed by the SPHERE Standards and Protection Principles, the Core Humanitarian Standards. Protection concerns are considered in the planning of all Shelter/NFIs activities in order to ensure that any risk of harm, abuse and Sexual exploitation faced by the target population is mitigated. Furthermore, GREENCODE and Partner has protection officer that will be working alongside other project officer in monitoring the gender and protection markers as well as handle all Protection related activities. As part of achieving this protection mainstreaming, we have planned for community outreach using FGDs, IEC and large gathering of community member to talk about protection, GREENCODE and Partner will closely with the Protection sector and other partners to closely monitor the identification of protection cases in order to adequately serve groups at risk, such as youth, female¬ headed households, people living with disability and the most vulnerable. Through protection referral systems, GREENCODE and Partner will continue to conduct special NFI distributions for especially vulnerable people. On days of distributions provision for shades, mate, biscuits of local snacks and drinking water will be provided to women and children. In addition, protection/mine awareness sessions will be organized for large numbers of community members on a monthly basis, while the about 5% of the project budget will go into protection activities from the start of the project to the end
