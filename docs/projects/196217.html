Project Summary

Secured fund
Supporting wheat value chain by providing the assistance to 800 farmers (males and females),800 hectares in A’zaz and Afrin, 800 farmers will be supported in 2023, where the main activities are:, providing farmers with theoretical and practical extension training sessions, supporting them with the harvesting process, marketing their crops, and establishing units for extinguishing the fires that may occur prior to the wheat harvest, additionally, TAS will support the market power by purchasing part of the wheat crops of the supported farmers to allow them to encourage them to cultivate wheat in the next season. Project’s locations were identified based on clusters reports, HNAP reports. In addition to support livestock sector by providing assistance to 366 livestock (sheep and goats) keepers (males and females), about 1500 sheep and goats in Harem, Azaz and Al Bab districts. The main activities are providing 80 tons of bran (kind of fodder for livestock). Project’s locations were identified based on clusters reports, HNAP reports, HRP, and detailed need assessments conducted by the TAS team in August 2022.
The project will ensure equal access for both genders with priority for women, children headed household, PWD, sick and elderly in addition, the project staff of female and male members are trained on the protection and gender equality, PSEA and have been oriented on the Code of Conduct as well as ensuring the Core Humanitarian Standards are upheld. TAS will ensure that the principle of Do No Harm is applied in all project's activities through several visits to the markets, distribution monitoring and exit surveys.
TAS already secured the fund needed for implementing this project in 2023 from BHA

4 - Implementation of activities

Supporting Wheat Value Chain by providing assistance to 800 farmers, 800 hectares in A’zaz and Afrin, where the main activities are:• Theoretical and Practical Extension Services.• Spot Check Visit and Providing Consultancy Services.• Removing Undesired Weeds and Dealing with Diseases.• Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR)• Harvesting and Crops Marketing.TAS will support the same farmers by purchasing part of their wheat crops, with total of 400 tons of wheat crops will be collected from the supported farmers, in average TAS will purchase around 500 kilo gram from each farmer, the aim of this activity is encouraging the farmers for cultivating their land with wheat again in the next season, the procurement process of this intervention will be supervised by a committee consists of (MEAL, Program, Procurement and community representative, the price of the 1 ton of wheat will be fixed through consulting with the FSL cluster after conducting market assessment, where the farmer will sign on the invoice and receipt forms of TAS, on the other hand, the wheat that was collected (350 tons) will be filtered and processed through a contracted mill, in order to produce 320 tons of wheat flour in addition to the production of 80 tons of bran (kind of fodder for livestock), the cost of the wheat processing will be around 80$ per ton, to cover the costs of transportation for collecting the wheat from the farmers locations in addition to the costs of the filtering, milling and packaging.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: The vulnerability of children and women in conflict circumstances highly increases and places them at higher risk of being exposed to exploitation, abuse, and rights violationsMitigation strategies: 1- Mandatory PSEA training will be conducted for all project staff before doing any task.2- Staff personnel will sign the code of conduct and safeguarding policy. 3- PSEA focal points will provide awareness sessions to the beneficiaries about PSEA.4- The team will publish clear and accessible complaint mechanisms.5- TAS will recruit female staff to deal with female beneficiaries. 6- Sensitize affected communities on PSEA and who/where to report.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures Assumption 1: The Information that the project built on it is true and neutrality. Risk: there is a risk of giving incorrect data to our team. Probability: Low Impact: High Mitigation strategies (MS): Our team will take in consideration IDPs tracking tables from CCCM and after that will crosscheck the data from local relief offices in the targeted location in addition to site visits to samples of supposed beneficiaries. Assumption 2: Work environment is safe and no risk threats workers in work locations. Risk: workers may be subjected to risks threats their life in work location. Probability: Low. Impact: High MS: Workers will receive training on safety and security procedures, in addition, our security team will monitor the situation and report if there is any potential risk. Assumption 3: The activities are done according to planed timeline. Risk: Because of weather in winter may causes a delay in some activities. Probability: Medium Impact: Medium Mitigation strategies (MS): when designing work plan and the timeline we will consider the weather and its effect. Assumption 4: All roads are open and there is a full safe access to targeted location\s Risk: Road closures and lack of access to targeted locations. Probability: low Impact: High Mitigation strategies (MS): Delay the distribution for several days pending the calm situation - develop an alternative plan to deliver assistance to the beneficiaries in the new situation
