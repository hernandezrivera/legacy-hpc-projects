Project Summary

This project comprises the Common Service for Community Engagement and Accountability - which will continue to provide a wide range of capacity strengthening support, communication materials, feedback analysis and knowledge products to agencies and sectors across the response - with more focused community engagement activities targeting gender, violence, rights and social cohesion.

Objectives

1 - Reinforce coordination, advocacy and technical support across different sectors and stakeholders for mainstreaming CwC and AAP principles enabling affected peoples to have access to life-saving information and knowledge on rights and services.2 - Further improve access to information – with a focus on currently underserved areas and groups – through rationalised, community-centric and evidence-based communication and community engagement approaches. 3 - Improve participation of and accountability to affected people through adoption of minimum standards for the referral and resolution of community feedback; and through increased use collective data analysis.

Needs

Activities led by BBC Media Action in the response to date – including the common service – have already contributed to substantial successes in terms of refugees' and host communities' access to information and the ability of affected people to have their voices heard and complaints resolved. There are still gaps - both geographically and in terms of certain more vulnerable groups (particularly women and older people) who still do not have access to relevant and accessible information and who are less able to access and use feedback, complaints and participation opportunities. There are also a number of more complex and long-term topics - such as gender-based violence - where communication and community engagement efforts so far have not been sufficient; and a need to improve efforts to address issues which require long-held beliefs, attitudes and values to be addressed. While there has been much improvement, language gaps - particularly for women - also remain a challenge when addressing sensitive and complex topics. There are also emerging and escalating needs in terms of community cohesion, mutual understanding and tolerance - both between the refugee and host communities and within each community independently. And there is an ongoing need for the response to continue to understand the evolving perceptions and attitudes of affected people; and to ensure that the views and concerns of all refugees and host communities - especially women, who are currently under-represented in terms of participation and feedback - play an even more central role when decisions are being made by humanitarian agencies and policy makers.

Activities or outputs

BBC Media Action and partners will continue to deliver the Common Service for Community Engagement and Accountability:• Content/material/message developed - Content creation for cross-response use as reactive support.• Content/material/message developed - Production of range of proactive communication tools, including professional development products aimed at practitioners as well as multi-media content for use with refugees and host communities, including target material for women, children and older people.• Technical support to the humanitarian/media agencies/sectors/WGs - Further strengthening of feedback mechanisms, in consultation with the CwC working groups with the aim of operationalising the CwC Accountability Manifesto and ensuring equal access for women and other underserved groups.• Capacity development Training organized for community members - Support to community leaders, including mahjis, as key intermediaries for information provision and feedback collection. And, a particular strand of support for female community leaders. • Capacity development Training organized for agencies/ sectors/ WGs (Staff and Volunteer) and Audio/Podcast/Narrowcasting program developed and disseminated - Increased number of listening groups, with training and mentoring for facilitators, together with the production of communication materials for the listening groups to use.• Content translated - Common, high-quality service for the translation, editing and simplification of community-facing documents for all agencies within the response.• Capacity development Training organized for agencies/ sectors/ WGs (Staff and Volunteer) - Deliver coordinated package of training, mentoring and support to improve two-way communication practice between field staff, volunteers and affected communities (including host communities); and help agencies to streamline and roll-out longer-term, more strategic CwC and accountability actions, with a particular focus on strengthening activities which help to fill gaps for women, older people and other groups who currently have less access to information and feedback provision.• Dissemination/ advocacy sessions with agencies/ sectors/ WGs - Comprehensive dissemination of communication tools to all levels of humanitarian staff.• Technical support to the humanitarian/media agencies/sectors/WGs - Collective feedback analysis service. More sector-specific products or rapid summaries on key, time-bound issues will be explored.• Technical support to the humanitarian/media agencies/sectors/WGs - On-demand support (training, mentoring, consultancy, technical advice) for agencies and sectors across all CwC activities.• Other Surveys/assessments conducted - Regular and in-depth research on community perceptions, attitudes, language and culture, to support the CwC working group and a range of other actors across the response.• Formative/endline / evaluation study conducted - Learning activities which capture, synthesise and disseminate evidence from the common service and other elements of the CwC, to improve into regional- and global-level networks and structures. Beyond the Common Service BBC Media Action will directly implement additional activities including:• Content/Material/Message developed - Audio and video drama and face-to-face community engagement activities to address sector-based needs, with an initial focus on GBV and other protection-related issues.• Capacity development Training organized for community members - Participatory media activities within and between host and refugee communities, focused on working with young people to improve community cohesion and peaceful coexistence outcomes.• Content/Material/Message developed - Provision of accurate, timely, relevant and appropriate information for refugee communities related to rights and entitlements; and activities which allow refugee voices (especially women) to be more clearly heard in national and international-level policy forums
