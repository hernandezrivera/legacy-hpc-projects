Project Summary

The project will aim to improve the access and availability of improved water, sanitation, and hygiene service to IDP, and the most vulnerable host community in El-Fasher, al-Malha, Al-Tewisha, Kebkebiya, Kalemando, Mellit, Saraf Omera, Umkadada, Tawila, Sortony localities in North Darfur; Abu Jabra, Elferdous, Bahr Al Arab localities in East Darfur, and Bau, Geisen, Roseries, and Damazine localities in Blue Nile States. To address the challenge of extreme water scarcity in these areas, the WASH intervention under this action will focus on expanding access and availability of WASH services through water yards rehabilitation, handpump rehabilitation, upgrading of handpumps to mini-water yards, integrated hygiene promotion; the construction of household and institutional latrines, and essential WASH upgrades to health facilities; water quality and groundwater level monitoring; installation of handwashing facilities and the distribution of hygiene kits. The proposed WASH activities focus on communities in El-Fasher, al-Malha, Al-Tewisha, Kebkebiya, Kalemando, Mellit, Saraf Omera, Umkadada, Tawila, Sortony localities in North Darfur; Abu Jabra, Elferdous, Bahr Al Arab localities in East Darfur, and, Bau, Geisen, Roseries, Damazine localities in Blue Nile. This project will reach a total of 161,936 individuals without double counting.

1 - Needs

Based on 2020 and 2021 assessment the gap in WASH services is big. According to OCHA a total of 6.1million people (including 1.9million Women) over all Sudan are in need of humanitarian assistance of which 2.55 million are internally displaced. A significant number of returnees are also in need of support. The majority of these people in need of humanitarian assistance fall in the North Darfur, and Blue Nile states of Sudan. These areas have been facing armed conflict (According to OCHA, the incidence of inter-communal violence reported in Darfur doubled during the second half 2020 compared to the same period in 2019) and displacement at various magnitude since 17years. In addition to the conflict related protracted humanitarian crisis, this area has witnessed different emergencies like the flooding and the occasional intercommunal clashes, which has led to death(250people resulted in Darfur only including to the three humanitarian workers) and displacement of more than 100,000 people . Despite the already precarious situation of WASH due to low coverage/existing poor infrastructures (limited new investments on WASH facilities), growing water scarcity (continued use of unprotected water sources) and ground water seasonal fluctuation, high non- functionality, the new displacement of peoples and flooding have further worsened the provision of WASH services. Sudan has also continued facing the health and humanitarian consequences of COVID19, experiencing high COVID19 cases (24,118 people) and 1,707 C19 related deaths in 2020. As a result, this Covid-19 crisis increases the need for water sanitation and hygiene (WASH) infrastructure.The gap in WASH services is not just for the host communities but it also affects the IDPs, refugees and returnee populations. RI is operating at the second biggest IDP camp in Darfur, Zamzam IDP camp which still lacks a lot of WASH needs due to high IDP population, existence of non-functional water points and risk of water related diseases. Zamzam IDP camp was located in arid area with no other alternative for ground water supply and inadequate awareness raising work on the link between aquifer recharge and controlled use of the ground water resources. Also regular water quality monitoring and supporting of Operation and maintenance is needed for this IDP camp. Apart from issues with quantity and quality of water supply, Sanitation and hygiene infrastructure, supplies and awareness is a major concern for the targeted areas of this action. A number of HHs in the IDP camps have no access to latrines, some use shared latrines and others forced to practice Open defecation bringing further risks of contaminations. RI is currently supporting North Darfur (Zamzam IDP camp, Alsayah, Almalha ) and Blue nile ( Ed Damazine, and Ar Rusayris) states with water supply, sanitation and hygiene including waste management support at Health facility and community level. In 2022, RI would like to continue to support this locations adding other locations and states which are in dire need. As a result, in 2022, RI has a plan to provide life saving WASH services for refugees in Khartoum and Bluenile states in addition to other localities within its existing operation area.

2 - Activities/Outputs

In the targeted areas, IDPs, returnees and host community members are combatting extreme water scarcity. The project’s contributions to safer water access revolve around rehabilitation and upgrading of water points as well as water quality testing and groundwater monitoring. In total, RI will rehabilitate 92 water points (18 water yard rehabilitation, 22 water yard solarization, and 52 Handpump rehabilitations) and upgrade 1 water point to mini-water yard in the localities of Al fasher , Al tawisha, Sarafomera, Mellit, Altina, Umkadadah, Kalemando, Kebkabiya, Tawila, Sortony, Almalha, in North Darfur, Ed damazine, Ar Rusaris, Baw, and Gisan in Bluenile and Abujabra, Bahralarabil, and Elferdos in East Darfur. Additionally, to improve the water availability in RI supported facilities, RI will carry out facility water tracking in El-Fasher, Mellit, Al-malha , Al Tawisha localities in North Darfur; and Damazine and Roseries in Blue Nile; overall a total of 161,936 individuals will benefit from the water supply service provision. To improve the access to sanitation services, RI will also intervene in the same localities with the construction of household and facility latrines. Accordingly, RI will undertake the construction of 1,603 household latrines reaching 15,054 people at localities of Al fasher and sortony in ND and Ed Damazine and Ar Rusaris in BLNS. these localities. RI will construct 30 facility latrine at Al fasher, Al Tawisha, Tawila, Saraf umera, Altina, Sortony, and Um kadida in ND and Ed Damazine, Ar Rusayris, Baw and Geisan in BLNS benefiting a total of 31,200 people. Under the hygiene promotion intervention in target localities, RI will undertake the Installation/ construction of 475 foot press hand washing facilities in communal set ups (communal toilets in camps, schools, markets, public places and health facilities) in Alfasher, Almalha, Mellit, Al Tawisha, Tawila, Saraf umera, Altina, Sortony and Um kadida localities in North Darfur reaching 7,600 people;; 46 facilities in Damazine to reach estimated 736 people. As part of the hygiene promotion intervention, RI will conduct 392campaigns in Al-fasher Almalha, Mellit, Al Tawisha, Tawila, Saraf umera, Altina, Sortonyand Um Kadida localities in ND reaching 73,110 people; ; and Damazine 90 reaching 22,700 people in BLNS. Integrated with the hygiene promotion messaging, RI will distribute WASH Supplies for Alfasher, Almalha, Sarafomera, Altina, Mellit and Sortony to 4,020 Households reaching 24,120 individuals in ND; and in BLNS of Damazin will distribute 1,250 supplies to 7,500 people.

6 - Protection mainstreaming
6.1 - Does the project incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity for the beneficiaries?

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

RI will mainstream minimum protection standards to ensure equitable and safe access to WASH programs and facilities. In addition to setting locations of community water points based on hydro-geologic conditions consideration, socio-political considerations will be factored in to ensure the location is open to the full community and that the WUC is representative. Safety and dignity are key considerations for sanitation activities, and a household will be involved in the construction of their IHHL to account for the unique needs of its members, and the hygiene kits will incorporate MHM items. The hygiene promoters, CHWs and CNVs will receive hygiene promotion training that considers how to tailor messaging to diverse groups.RI will ensure that protection is mainstreamed throughout the project’s WASH activities. Priority will be given to the elderly, the disabled, female-headed households, and beneficiaries with cases of acute malnutrition. When selecting beneficiaries for latrine construction, beneficiary households meeting the vulnerability criteria will be prioritized. In the selection of sites for the rehabilitation of water yards and hand pumps, priority will be given to locations with high percentages of vulnerable households. In the training of WUCs, RI will ensure that there is representation from vulnerable community groups and that the WUCs develop processes to ensure that vulnerable community members are able to access water easily. Throughout the project planning and implementation phases, RI will continue to monitor the communities to ensure that vulnerable households are benefiting from WASH programming.
