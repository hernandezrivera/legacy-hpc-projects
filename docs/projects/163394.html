Project Summary

Inefficient Municipal Solid and liquid waste Management is endemic in Bamenda identified by persistent uncontrolled littering of solid waste (plastics, old tyres, cloths, tins cans etc.), on streets, illegal dumps in streams and canals clogging it path way. Also uncontrolled liquid waste disposal from bath rooms, kitchen, outside clothes washing sinks etc. from homes either channel to the road to constitute standing pools of sewage or to neighbors’ homes or horrible uncover gutters in the neighborhood. Industrial waste (garages) is disposed in an uncontrolled manner also. All these result to; aesthetic insults, unclean streets, environmental degradation, water pollution, standing pulls of sewage acting as breeding grounds for disease vectors (mosquitoes, flies, rodents etc. deteriorating the population’s health.
Also crowded IDP host homes, women/girls inclusive have limited access to Hygiene and Sanitation tools (WASH and MHM kits) and poor disposal of the used sanitary clothes. Besides, the sanitary products they used are unhygienic torn pieces of dresses which are unhealthy. These IDP host homes generate a lot of waste water which is also poorly disposed increasing the incidences of disease infections among them and the entire community.
Root causes include; ignorance of the population on solid and liquid waste disposal practices. Limited access to WASH and MHM kits poorly managed and disposed. Poor implementation of policies on waste disposal practices. This proposal will introduces a multi-stakeholder approach where platforms will be created for each sector in the community to define a clear road map on how to disposed waste generated in their sectors
The purpose of this proposal is to improve the environmental hygiene and sanitation of IDP host communities to reduce the incidences of diseases (malaria, typhoid fever, etc) as well as protecting urban water and land resources. This will be achieved through;
Improve IDPs and host population attitudes and behaviors in disposing solid and liquid waste in the public, home and the communities contributing to clean, safe and green environment
Increasing access to WASH and MHM kits by IDP women and girls to aid them improve their hygienic conditions.
Improve the implementation of policies, rules and regulation in solid and sewage waste disposal.
Targets;
IDPs particularly in crowded homes need to be educated in hygiene and sanitation and support them with basic WASH and MHM kits
Land lords have to ensure solid waste and sewage is well managed in their compounds
Trader leaders (Tailors, barbers/hair dressers, garages etc.) will have to designed a strategy to collect their waste and disposed it in the friendliest environmentally accepted norm.
Quarter heads (custodians of the communities) have to put in place a structural plan to ensure their entire quarters are clean through the creation of functional Hygiene and Sanitation committees.
Religious leaders have the platforms to continuously educate and inform the population of a safe, clean, green homes and environment.
Activities and strategies include;
 Working sessions with different trade groups and stakeholders on proper waste management and WASH themes.
 Sector based (networks) monitoring of stake-holder activities in promoting proper hygiene and sanitation in their different sectors and communities.
 Sensitization campaigns in the communities including awards for the cleanest quarters/homes.
 Supporting construction of waste pits and provision of waste bins to vulnerable target for proper management of liquid and solid waste from homes and industrial work places.
 Engaging stakeholders in policy formulation and implementation to reduce defaulters and sanction perpetrators
 Creation of Functional Hygiene and Sanitation Committees including water.
 Education on menstrual hygiene management to target population.
 Provision of menstrual hygiene and management (MHM) and WASH kits to target population.

Besoins / Needs

The endemic poor waste disposal practices both solid and liquid waste in the environment is reflected in the presence of waste (nasty plastics, cans, bottles etc.) in every available space in the community. Households/workplaces dump solid waste in canals, gutters, streams and land, while sewage from bathrooms, kitchen sinks are directed into streets forming sewage pools in the neighborhoods and sewage directed into streams pollutes waters making the animals inside toxic for consumption. In addition waste dump in streams contaminate it and exploited by farmers in irrigating vegetables that are sold to the population for consumption, besides homes close to streams channel toilets into streams and wells are dug close to these contaminated streams because of water table accessibility and the wells become contaminated. These contaminated well waters are used in washing home utensils, vegetables and other consumables. The contaminated streams are exploited in washing many other products that are commercialized and consumed instantly by buyers particularly fruits. Sector traders (hair dressers/barbers, tailors, garages, bars etc.) poorly dispose solid and liquid waste into the communities and streams. Some of these waste which is purely chemical in nature and non-biodegradable increase the toxic level of compound both on farm land or in streams that affect land or aquatic organisms. The presence of IDPs and IDP crowded homes has further worsened the poor waste disposal practices as much waste is generated than can be managed. IDPs Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) is very poor as most of them use torn dresses as sanitary pads which are more of a medium for disease causing and degrading to them than providing a solution to a problem they are experiencing. The effect of the above challenges has put the health of the entire population in danger either from disease vector breeding grounds, usage of contaminated water in crop irrigation, home usage or consumption of toxic aquatic animals. Ecosystems both terrestrial and aquatic are also highly threatened as well as lots of conflicts among neighbors due to misdirected sewage into homes by other neighbors. The poor waste disposal and its devastating consequences are due to;The populations are having limited knowledge in proper waste disposal practices, basic home hygiene and sanitation management. Many homes do not have waste pits and bins to properly dispose sewage and solid waste generated hence sewage and solid waste streams out in an uncontrolled manner and form waste heaps and standing pools of sewage in the neighborhood constituting breeding ground for disease vectors. IDPs in crowded host homes have limited knowledge and resources on Menstrual and Hygiene management products. Limited or complete absence of policies that promote proper waste disposal (both solid and liquid) and sanction to defaulters. This has kept the population in the poor attitude and behaviors of waste disposal.
