Project Summary

The IRC will provide WASH support to 60000 people in communities within the 3 districts who are facing environmental sanitation problems especially open defecation and have no access to water sources of sufficient quality. The water supply intervention will be focused across the 3 districts where the average percentage of households of households whose water supply situation is classified as severe is 22%. The environmental sanitation interventions will however be targeted to Beitbridge, Chiredzi and Chipinge Districts. In view of the on-going COVID -19 pandemic, the IRC will target all the 3 districts with risk awareness and community engagement activities (RCCE).
The IRC will target whole communities with water supply infrastructure prioritizing communities whose access to safe clean water is low. Sanitation interventions will directly benefit vulnerable households particularly households with people living with disabilities, the elderly, child headed and headed by women. These will be used as demonstration sites for latrine construction whilst encouraging other households to replicate. Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) will be implemented through Community Health Clubs (CHC). Target communities will be selected through district and community level coordination structures to ensure that the right beneficiaries are targeted.

1 - Activities

The project will provide a full package across all sectors overall, however there will be variations from one district to the other based on needs. The water supply interventions that include rehabilitation of water points including boreholes, deep wells and springs will be implemented across all the 3 districts. Where high yielding boreholes exceed with sufficient water demand, the IRC will consider solarizing such boreholes to improve efficiency and reduce the waiting time largely borne by women and girls. In areas without alternative water sources for livestock the IRC will rehabilitate and construct requisite infrastructure to enable communities to provide water for their livestock. The environmental sanitation interventions will be implemented only in Beitbridge and Chiredzi where the IRC will construct latrines for vulnerable households as demonstration units that the broader communities will be encouraged to replicate. The demonstration latrines will be 100% friendly to people living with disabilities and local builders will be trained to enable them to construct these latrine units. In view of the COVID – 19 pandemic, the above interventions will be complimented by hygiene promotion activities especially risk communication and community engagement activities across the 3 districts with a strong focus on protecting communities from the COVID -19 disease along with other WASH related diseases. The IRC will work through community health clubs and distribute WASH related NFIs that will help enhance hygiene behaviours. Health facilities will also be supported with requisite IPC materials and PPE.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

The IRC is working with Disaster Risk Communities in Beitbridge District whilst in Chiredzi and Chipinge Districts, the IRC has been working ward development committees and these structures are responsible for informing the project design process as they provide the critical link with the broader communities. In addition, the IRC also participates in district level coordination forums especially the District Water and Sanitation Sub-Committee (DWSSC) in each district that coordinate WASH interventions in the respective district. The IRC has a client responsiveness mechanism (CRM) that enables project beneficiaries to provide feedback to the IRC on project implementation. The IRC client responsiveness mechanism enables beneficiaries to raise complaints through texts, phone, WhatsApp and email. IRC ensures that each complain raised is recorded and is only closed once an IRC staff member reached back to the beneficiary who raised the issue and satisfactorily addresses their complaint.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will not cause any damage to the environment as rehabilitations will only be conducted on existing infrastructure. Local materials such as sand will be collected from rivers without compromising the environment. Where communities need firewood for brick molding the IRC will work with local traditional leadership and the Environmental Management Agency (EMA) at district level to identify sources of firewood that can be utilized without compromising the environment. The IRC will work with local environmental health technicians to site latrines appropriately such that they do not pollute water sources. Appropriate environmental education will also be incorporated in the curricula for community health clubs to enhance their awareness.
