Project Summary

The proposal aims at ensuring access to essential life-saving health services to targeted IDPs and vulnerable host communities which is in line with the Health Cluster objectives. This project is developed from the needs, justification and findings of need assessments and reports that explains the level of health risks and the needs of vulnerable groups in these areas. The project also aims protection risk groups (Rape survivors, victims of physical and psychological trauma) which contributes to the strategic objective of the cluster. The target locations are in line to the Health cluster priority locations for this call of proposal.
IRS plans to respond to the needs of displaced people and poor host communities in order to deliver lifesaving health services including maternal and child health through existing facilities and mobile teams in the affected IDPs and poor hosts in Daynile and Balcad districts. As there is no treatment for COVID19 diseases, the project will adapt activities to prevent COVID-19, as prevention is better than cure. Our aims is to raise general awareness among young people, student, adolescent, IDPs and staff in reducing the spread of COVID-19 in the communities. With a focus on maternal and child health, this project will respond directly to significant gender-related issues that exist in the country, most notably in relation to access to health care. Issues constraining females accessing health services include: lack of awareness of services required or where to go, cost of treatment, the distance to the health facility, having to take transport, not wanting to go alone, and concern that there may not be a female health provider present. Addressing these gender issues is crucial for project success. IRS will respond training female (lady) community health workers which will promote transfer of health knowledge to their female clients.
The health centres will be equipped with essential medicines, PPEs and medical equipment and staff will be trained to provide quality services. The project will be emphasis on Maternal and Child Health, including Basic Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (BEmONC) and Expanded Programme on Immunization to IDPs and most vulnerable communities. The activities of the project will focus on provision of both preventive and curative health services integrated with protection components.
IRW will also integrate surveillance and emergency preparedness ensuring adequate stocks for epidemic outbreaks of AWD/Cholera are prepositioned. The project will focus risk reduction due to covid-19 through proper screening of patients and risk mitigation activities both health facilities and mobile teams in the communities.
IRS will support: • Through two static health facilities, IRS plans to conduct 31,200 (18720 female & 12400 male) OPD consultations (50 per day per facility). • Establish two Mobile clinics for 12,480 (7,488 female & 4992 male) IDPs in Daynile & Balcad 26 clinics per month per district. •provide clinical services, ANC, PNC, normal deliveries, BEmONC, FP and new borncare for 7,200 women.
•IRW will procure essential drugs including emergency surgical and obstetric kits (including trauma kits), drugs and medical supplies; basic medical equipment and laboratory supplies. IRW, together with the Ministry of Health (MOH) and the District Health Authority plans to strengthen emergency preparedness and response capacity at all supported health facilities and affected communities. •To support communicable disease prevention and control, IRW will also raise community awareness, boost immunization coverage through regular vaccination campaigns, and promote improved hygiene practices. Moreover, IRW will engage in case finding, treatment and health awareness-raising for the prevention of common infectious diseases. Participation: Consult persons. Health facilities and mobile clinics wil be designed easy access to people with disability and diasability friendly environment.

Please provide further explanation

The project is aimed to build the capacity of local staff, improve the infrastructure of health facilities and Islamic Relief Somalia will put in place mechanisms of accommodating persons with disabilities into the projects in terms of physical accessibility, information, and communication accessibility. All the health infrastructures will be designed for the people with disability have ease accessing the facilities. Meaningful participation of persons with disabilities in the project design, this is to protect disable people from discrimination and disadvantage when seeking health services. Islamic Relief frontline health staff were trained on protection and disability mainstreaming training in order to accommodate persons with disability into their services. The staff are trained on how to deal with and provide services all kinds of people with disability. The disable persons are able to receive services they required without prior appointment or without putting them into waiting line. They will receive as soon as they reach the facility. The CHWs give priority status those suffering with disability as soon as they reach the facility and their needs immediately communicated to the appropriate office, who are able respond with additional, targeted assistance. The mobile team also take the services to the homes of persons with disability during mobile outreaches. - Project management committee will also be established that will oversee the project and ensure that the project is completed successful with appropriate impact
