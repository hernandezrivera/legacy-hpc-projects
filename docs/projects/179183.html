Project Summary

SEMA plans to build on the previous project in order to proceed in providing preventive and responsive CP services in 4 CFSs in Azaz, Afrin,Albab and Jisr alshoughur sub districts including:1-Structured PSS to the children(B&G) and youth (M&F) through using "Child Resilience " and “Youth Resilience” curriculums for children and youth to develop their self-resilience and social skills such as cultural and recreation skills 2-Document and provide services for child survivals and at risk cases including children with disabilities Supervision mechanism will be in place to support caseworkers to provide safe, ethical and competent case management services.3-Providing emergency ECF to enable immediate response when it is needed 4-Provision awareness raising campaigns that will covering CP issues (Child labor, Family separation, dropping out of schools and dealing with CWDs and the prevention and response to COVID-19. through 6 outreach teams 5-Supporting 4 Community Child Protection Networks (CPNs) in order to respond to violations affecting children. CPNs will be composed of youth and adults (M&F) including people with disabilities volunteering to protect children in their communities.
Moreover, SEMA will mobilize their Health, Nutrition, Physiotherapy, Specialized Mental Health services to build internal referral pathway in order to contribute for family strengthening approach.
Activities will be adapted according to COVID-19 consequent restriction of movements, and in line with (Guidance Note on GBV and CP Service Provision during the time of COVID-19) and (Ethical Closure Matrix), COVID-19 precautionary measures in line with WHO standards will be applied in face-to-face activities.
IN CASE OF EMERGENCY
SEMA will use one harmonized training kit developed by the protection cluster for the emergency response to make use of existing capacity and reduce duplication.
Each organization will implement an agreed mobile team structure. Mobile teams will target areas with high numbers of new IDPs and adapt locations based on the changing needs. Activities will include prevention and response services, which will be mainly (PFA, and dissemination of key messages on (CP), referral to protection or other services, high risk cases provided with direct support service, provide specialized services, conduct rapid protection needs assessment. Coordination will take place with actors in Turkey and Syria at district level. building on our capacity to provide integrated protection services. Each organization will work within their respective districts to provide coordination with other protection actors to ensure sufficient coverage of priority areas with high numbers of new arrivals. In addition, we will coordinate within our respective districts with non-protection actors to facilitate referrals, share information about protection services, and engage in joint activities such as distributions. SEMA will have dedicated focal points in Syria and Turkey.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be Implemented through 4CFS and outreach teams in Albab, Azaz, Sharran, and Aljanoudeyh. PSS animators will provide structured PSS services and parenting skills. The 4 CFS will be static outreach team will take each facility as a starting point for their activities in the surrounding communities. In each location, there will be 1 CP case management supervisor, 3 case specialist, and 2 outreach team each one consists of 4 outreach worker who will Identify and refer the cases to the CFS and provide semi structured PSS and awareness-raising sessions on CP issues and the prevention of Covid-19. the Project Manager will be responsible for the management of the CP teams, as well as the M&E team. The Project Manager will follow-up the detailed implementation plan and closely oversee the field coordinator and ensure that the work plan is met, and target outputs are achieved. SEMA will ensure the coordination with the local councils, and the technical team will follow-up the regular procedures.SEMA will ensure that the good access of female youth through provision transportation. SEMA MEAL team will conduct different activities to monitor the work progress through the following tools: Bimonthly Monitoring visits. Facility assessment Quarterly. FGDs-Satisfaction surveys-indicator tracker. Furthermore, SEMA team applies SAFE mechanism in order to improve accountability. The system facilitates receiving complaints and suggestions through hotline, WhatsApp, email, and complaints box. Supervision will be conducted by CM Supervisors to ensure the quality of Case management service, ensure that all emergency Case Fund document are inline with CP Sub Cluster guideline.
