Project Summary

The FSC will aim at pre-stocking the basic resources for 1 week food assistance in accommodation centres and a return kit for families to go back to their houses after the shock, while also providing some basic agricultural support for a 2nd planting season and horticultural seeds for the winter in coordination with FAO. This will be in response to a cyclone or flooding, but the FSC will be on alert to respond to the drought that is already affecting the interior and southern part of the country. Finally, activities to strengthen anticipatory action system-building to support partners to mitigate of negative impacts of floods, cyclones and droughts before they occurr are considered. Specifically, this includes availability of early warning and climate trigger information to all partners as well as the formulation of local anticipatory action plans through a harmonized participatory approach. It also includes activities to strengthen anticipatory action system-building for the mitigation of negative impacts of floods, cyclones and droughts before they occurr, focussing on the availability of early warning and climate trigger information as well as the formulation of local anticipatory action plans..

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project employs a comprehensive risk identification and mitigation strategy to ensure the prevention of harm. Firstly, a thorough risk assessment is conducted, identifying potential hazards and vulnerabilities. This involves engaging stakeholders. Proactive measures are then implemented to minimize identified risks, incorporating safety protocols, contingency plans, and community engagement. Regular monitoring and feedback mechanisms are established to adapt to evolving circumstances.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project prioritizes inclusive access to assistance, particularly for women and the elderly. It employs targeted outreach, tailored communication, and specialized training to actively involve these groups in decision-making processes. By considering linguistic and cultural nuances, the project ensures comprehension and meaningful participation. Capacity-building initiatives empower women and the elderly with skills relevant to project objectives. Feedback mechanisms amplify their voices, allowing them to contribute to planning and implementation. This approach not only guarantees meaningful access to assistance but also promotes empowerment and inclusiveness, aligning with principles of community participation and social equity.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project ensures inclusivity for people with disabilities through straightforward measures. Firstly, there will be targeted outreach using accessible communication methods to identify individuals with disabilities. Specialized surveys and engagement with disability organizations will provide insights into specific needs. Collaborations with disability advocacy groups will inform and tailor assistance to address unique challenges.

7 - Environmental considerations
