Project Summary

The project “Emergency Assistance to Conflict Affected Populations in Eastern Ukraine” (February 14, 2018 - June 13, 2019) aims at addressing the most urgent hygiene needs of the conflict affected populations residing in the government-controlled areas of Luhansk and Donetsk Oblasts, particularly in communities within 5 km of the Line of Contact. It was a part of a wider programme which included Food Security and Winterization cash grants. Since markets are operational, the main project modality is cash assistance (unconditional grants to cover basic food, hygiene and winterization needs). However, bed-bound beneficiaries and beneficiaries with limited mobility are provided with in-kind assistance.
Until 13 June 2019, the project was implemented by ASB in consortium with ACTED and SSU and was funded by the Federal Foreign Office of the Federal Republic of Germany. The follow-up project “Hygiene, Nutrition and Winterization as Cash as well as Psychosocial Support for Conflict-Affected People in need along the Contact Line, Eastern Ukraine” (14 June 2019-13 June 2021), funded by the Federal Foreign Office of the Federal Republic of Germany, will be implemented by ASB Country Office Ukraine (food, hygiene and winterization components) and Samaritan Union of Ukraine (psychosocial support component).

Needs

The conflict in Ukraine continues to have a negative impact on the socioeconomic situation in Ukraine, particularly in the Eastern oblasts of the country (“Analysis of Impact of Conflict on Socio-Economic Situation in Eastern Ukraine” (FSLC report, September 2017). According to WASH Cluster’s inputs to the 2019 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), the total population in need of WASH-related assistance in the GCA 20-km area is 0,5 m. Because of continuous shelling and decrepit infrastructure, conflict-affected populations frequently experience stoppages of water. Therefore, conflict-affected people need access to clean water and adequate sanitation. If the situation with water, sanitation and hygiene does not improve, the health risks are eminent, especially for children and older people (WASH HNO summary). According to the ACTED-led Joint Market Monitoring study, undertaken in February 2018 by ACCESS Partners, NRC and Save the Children International, hygiene prices increased by 10% between September 2017 and February 2018. As per report, hygiene items are fully available in market sub-centers and semi-peripheral localities; in some periphery locations, however, hygiene items are either partially available (Lobacheve, Peredilske, Troitske) or unavailable (Bolotene). As demonstrated by all three rounds of market study, the least available commodity are diapers (with adult and infant diapers being unavailable in 53% and 29% of monitored localities, respectively). Within the framework of the project “Emergency Assistance to Conflict Affected Populations in Eastern Ukraine” (14 February 2018-13 June 2019), which was implemented by ASB in consortium with Agency for Technical Cooperation and Development (ACTED) and Samaritan Union of Ukraine (SSU) in the 5-km zone (GCA), over 3500 beneficiaries received cash and in-kind assistance to cover their basic food and hygiene needs during January-June 2019. Considering the significant strain on households’ incomes that food and heating costs represent, non-vital supplies such as hygiene items are often neglected by households. Therefore, beneficiaries who are provided with food aid also receive assistance to cover their essential hygiene needs. Whereas over 3000 beneficiaries received cash assistance, more than 400 beneficiaries, mostly those with limited mobility, were provided with in-kind assistance. The consortium consisting of ASB, ACTED and SSU was present in the target locations of 5-kilometer zone along the contact line implementing itsemergency assistance project. The population profile of 3500 beneficiaries who received cash or in-kind assistance in 2018 within the framework of the project “Emergency Assistance to Conflict Affected Populations in Eastern Ukraine” (2018-2019), demonstrated significant vulnerability: • Older persons represented 38 % of the caseload;• Women represented 63 % of the caseload; • Children represented 18% of the caseload; • People with disabilities represented 16% of the caseload;• The average household size was 1,9.The project was implemented in 25 localities along the line of contact (Donetsk and Luhansk GCA), with the localities subject to adjustment if needed after the next round of assessment. Newly added proposed project activities contribute to the WASH Objective 2: “Provision of critical WASH-related supplies and information for the prevention of water- and sanitation-related diseases.” In addition to providing cash and in-kind assistance to cover basic hygiene needs (soap, washing powder, toothpaste, etc.), the consortium will seek to provide diapers to bed-bound adults in the localities where these hygiene items are not available.

Activities & outcomes

Purpose: To meet hygiene needs of vulnerable conflict-affected populations in Eastern Ukraine.Output: 6500 beneficiaries receive cash and/or in-kind assistance to cover their hygiene needs.Outcome: target beneficiaries improve their hygiene situation.Beneficiary identification Since food insecure households usually prioritize their food needs over other expenses, the beneficiaries identified for food aid will automatically receive WASH-related assistance. Simultaneously, specific WASH-related needs of older persons (e.g. diapers) will be taken into consideration. Beneficiary selection will be undertaken in coordination with WASH Cluster, local authorities and humanitarian agencies operating in the area. Local populations will be consulted in the various assessment phases, as ASB will undertake community mobilization in the target areas to seek referrals and identify vulnerable population groups/individuals in target communities. ASB will ensure that beneficiaries meet vulnerability criteria identified by HRP 2019-2020 (i.e. older persons, people with disabilities, female-headed households, IDPs).Beneficiary assessment will take place in summer 2019 to identify caseload for July 2019-February 2020).A hotline feedback mechanism will be available for community members and assessed/selected individuals to ask questions, request assistance, provide feedback and lodge complaints. At all project locations, contact details will be displayed. All complaints will be recorded in the Complaints Registration Database for follow-up. All complainants will be treated respectfully, whether it is felt the complaint is justified or not. Communities will be provided with information on how their feedback was addressed. Post Distribution Monitoring will be undertaken to measure the utilization of the aid provided. All assessments, including PDM, are designed to allow age and gender disaggregation.Distribution of assistanceIn 2018, the consortium distributed UAH 70-75/person/month for hygiene to the target beneficiaries. For the first half of 2019, this amount was increased to UAH 90/person/month. The latter estimate was based on the ACTED-led Market Monitoring study, which noted the increase of prices on essential hygiene items by 10% between November 2017 and February 2018. However, starting with July 2019, ASB will follow the revised recommendations of the WASH Cluster (UAH 300/person/month). Hygiene kits for persons with special needs will be provided in-kind (budgeted at 1750 per person per month).Cash is the most appropriate aid modality as markets are functioning. The consortium will also consider in-kind distribution of hygiene supplies in cases of extreme isolation/disability for beneficiaries who are not able to purchase commodities or reside in localities where these commodities (e.g. diapers) are unavailable.For the population in the 5-km zone near the line of contact (GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts), the most preferred modes of delivery for cash assistance are bank account (48%) and post office (32%) (REACH Humanitarian Trend Analysis 2018). Thus, the modes of cash disbursement used by ASB (Oshchadbank and UkrPoshta’s cash home delivery system) are in line with the preferences of the affected populations. In order to prevent and/or mitigate water, sanitation and hygiene-related diseases, flyers on hygiene promotion will be handed out, including during the in-kind distributions.

Indicators & targets

The main indicators and targets are the following:• Overall 6500 people are provided with critical WASH-related supplies and/or awareness (63% female, 38% older people, 18% children, 10% homebound and/or persons with disabilities)• 3000 people benefiting from cash or vouchers for hygiene supplies (to June 2019)• 400 people (m,w,b,g) benefiting from the provision of hygiene items during January-June 2019• 3000 people benefiting from cash or vouchers for hygiene supplies (after June 2019)• 540 persons with disabilities receive additional hygiene items (hygiene kits for persons with special needs) after June 2019• 85 % of beneficiaries are satisfied with the aid provided
