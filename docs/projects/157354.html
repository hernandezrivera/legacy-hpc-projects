Project Summary

The project is intended to provide livelihood and WASH support for South Sudan refugees and host community in El Kharasana to enhance their resilience and self-reliance through diversifying their livelihoods options, improving access to employment opportunities and income generating activities as well as improving their access to water and WASH facilities. The project targets 9,600 individuals (8,000 refugees and 1,600 host community).
Normally livelihood assets availability in regards to the agricultural land and livestock is mostly owned by host community, but the South Sudan refugees are also playing essential role in accessing these livelihoods assets through renting of agricultural lands and/or working in these lands. Supporting these two components will enhance the host community livelihood assets that also lead to increasing the opportunity of getting employment for refugees and improving food security for both the refugees and the host community.
Beside livelihood interventions the project will deliver a comprehensive package of WASH services (construction and rehabilitation of water facilities, training packages for the targeted population, and distribution of hygiene kits).
Through these package of interventions, the project will ensure improve WASH services, improved livelihood conditions and self-reliance.

Needs

Sudan hosts the largest population of South Sudanese refugees in the region, with more than 850,000 individuals reported across the country as of December 2018. Among these, nearly 385,000 fled to Sudan since the outbreak of conflict in South Sudan in 2013, and have been registered or recorded at reception areas by UNHCR and the Government’s Commissioner of Refugees (COR). West Kordofan State is hosting 67,893 refugees scattered in different towns causing tension and pressure to vulnerable infrastructures and sharing with host community the limited resources. The refugees in West Kordofan have particular vulnerabilities due to fact that WK is their first reception area to Sudan before moving to other parts. They received international protection from the Government of Sudan, however they do not have the same rights as Sudanese citizens, and often have specific barriers to accessing services on par with nationals. As a result, refugees are largely reliant on humanitarian assistance to meet their basic needs, a situation that is compounded by limited access to livelihoods. Additionally, refugees are situated in some of the most remote and economically disadvantaged areas of the State (El Mairam and Kharasana), which places additional strains on limited host community resources. In El Kharasana of West Kordofan, the access to sanitation facilities, hygiene promotion activities and to adequate levels of safe water is very low. Water and sanitation infrastructures need to be improved, or maintained. There is a need for a concentrated efforts to sensitize/raise awareness around best hygiene practices for refugees and host communities and to expand for improving sanitation facilities. The livelihood conditions of refugees as well as host community, also needs urgent interventions and support through livelihood activities to ensure access to adequate food and income generating opportunities. The humanitarian partners in Sudan has contributed for responding to the needs of South Sudanese refugees, despite critical funding gaps and current ongoing economic crisis, as well as fuel and cash shortages. Nevertheless, significant gaps still remain, in terms of WASH and access to livelihood interventions.

Activities

Responding to situation needs, the project intends to provide integrated livelihood and WASH activities to achieve self-reliance for targeted 9,600people (4,608 male and 4,992 female) groups, the project activities are categorized under two outputs: Under output 1: Improved Self-reliance and livelihoods opportunities for food insecure groups among refugees and host communities in El Kharasana, the proposed activities are: Conduct market assessment to identify business opportunities for target beneficiaries, Conduct vocational trainings for 100 youth from SS refugees & host community, Conduct handy crafts trainings for 100 women heading household both refugees and host community Conduct IGA trainings for 100 refugees women in food processing, oil pressing, and cash crop production, Conduct agricultural extension training for 60 agents, Rent of agricultural land for 100 refugees to cultivate during 2019 coming rainy season, Provide agricultural inputs, seeds and tools for 600 farmers (450 refugees and 150 from the host community), Conduct animal health worker training for 60 Pra-vet, Provide goats restocking for 100 HHs, Conduct 12 awareness sessions on GBV, and CP for targeted population. Under output 2: Increased access to safe, sufficient water supply and hygiene services for targeted population in El Kharasana, the Key activities are: construct three water lines with distribution points in refugees settlement, Rehabilitate and replace existing broken water line, Train 20 WASH committee members on water resources management, train 20 hygiene promoters to facilitate hygiene promotion activities, provide and distribute 50,000 pieces of soap, provide 1000 hygiene kits including sanitary kits for women, provide 60 sets of cleaning tools, mobilize refugees and host community to conduct 60 cleaning campaigns, Conduct 60 awareness rising sessions, and provide and distribute six hand washing facilities.
