Project Summary

TAS has designed this proposal to provide Shelter and NFI assistance for the most affected people in NWS to improve their living conditions.
The project areas were selected to be within a severity scale of 3 and the highest PIN according to the WoS list.
The project will benefit these high priority areas by strengthening and enhancing the dignified living conditions in both in/ and off-camp locations. TAS will consider Do No harm principles, Cluster guidelines, matching with the context and ensuring the adhering to HLP rights to prevent any harm.
As the beginning of the project coordination communications with the SNFI cluster will be done in order to ensure that no overlapping occurs.
TAS will seek to achieve the following deliverables:
- Provision of Cash for Winter assistance (180 USD per HHs) for provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs such as of heating fuel, stoves, winter clothing kits and winter NFI kits. This activity will be through voucher modality (target: 25,000 individuals /5,000 HHs - $180 USD per HHs).
- Installation and construction of 520 dignified Shelters/temporary transitional shelters (target: 2,600 individuals/ 520 HHs - 520 Shelter units)
- Improving the Site Infrastructure/ HH level improvements implemented (target: 16,460 individuals /3292 HHs) through land preparation, drainage system, water & Sanitation networks, and lighting poles in addition to all relevant infrastructure needed
- Rehabilitation of minor repairs to damaged and affected houses -600 houses (target: 3000 individuals/ 600 HHs - 600 Shelters).
- Rehabilitation of of damaged or unfinished housing (Moderate and Severe) -600 houses (target: 3000 individuals/ 600 HHs - 600 Shelters).
The total estimated beneficiaries of the proposed project are as follows:
- Shelter Component: 22,460 individuals / 4492 HHs
- NFI Component: 25,000 individuals / 5,000 HHS.
Following the priorities identified in the Humanitarian Response Plan 2023, TAS has designed this project to enhance dignified living and address acute needs for 9,492 HHs/ 47,460 individuals living in Idleb and Aleppo with different ages and vulnerable cases (23 % men, 25 % women, 26 % Boys, and 27% girls ) taking into consideration Gender and age diversity through integrated Shelter and NFI interventions.
All activities within the proposed project will be according to the Shelter cluster objectives and will be designed according to the SPHERE standards.
In addition, the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially “Do no harm” throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls. During this project, TAS will consult with the community and individual IDPs taking into account the specific need of each group, TAS also will involve the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during the community outreach plan.
The main objective of the proposed project is to reach 47,460individuals targeting most vulnerable men, women, boys and girls and prioritize family members with high vulnerabilities according to the beneficiaries selection criteria (female/single/child-headed households, IDP status, no source of income and support, people with disabilities, elderly,...etc.).
TAS will ensure high-level coordination with all parties involved in the project (SNFI cluster, OCHA Clusters, Donors, local authorities, other NGOs, and other related parties) to implement the project in good quality and avoid any duplication.
