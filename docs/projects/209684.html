Project Summary

Primary health services
The project consists of four medical points and a mobile team distributed in the following areas (a fixed medical point in the city of Al-Hasakah - a fixed medical point in Al-Hawl camp (sector seven), a fixed point in Al-Arisha camp (expansion), a fixed medical point in the center of Al-Shaddadi city, and a mobile team in the countryside of Al-Shaddadi city. .
The mobile team consists of the following medical personnel (an internist, a pediatrician, a gynecologist, two nurses, a midwife, a paramedic, a pharmacist, a health educator, and a data entry person), in addition to a coordinator and a cleaner. . And a guard.
The mobile team consists of a general practitioner, a nurse, a midwife, a health educator, a pharmacist, a data entry and a coordinator.
Primary health care services are provided at medical points after equipping the points with the necessary medical equipment and supplies for work.
-The project includes the provision of secondary health care services, where a referral system is adopted, whereby beneficiaries in need of secondary health care services are approved through medical points in the city of Al-Hasakah, Al-Shaddadi city, Al-Hawl camp, and Al-Arisha. The beneficiary is also required to submit papers proving the patient’s need for the required procedure, where the Submitting (medical report + transfer). From the specialist doctor) explaining the medical condition. A statement from the hospital or imaging center stating the financial value of the required procedure. Contracts are made with private hospitals in the city of Al-Hasakah to perform surgical operations, including (Al-Hikma Hospital - Issam Al-Baghdi Hospital - Al-Raja Hospital), and contracts are also made with radiology centers. (Al-Hekma Center and Al-Rajaa Hospital Center) Secondary health care services include providing the following services:
-CT scan referral
-M.R.I. referral
-Support for cesarean section
-Supporting natural births
-Referring general surgical operations
-Referral of gynecological surgeries
-Conversion eye surgeries (cataract,...)
-Orthopedic surgery referral
-Referral of neurosurgical surgeries
- Referral of urinary surgeries
-Refer babysitting services for infants
Regarding primary mental health, it is provided
Awareness sessions on mental health and psychosocial support (group sessions) Mental health and psychosocial support services will be provided by team members with experience and training in the field of mental health for people who suffer from psychological distress and are encouraged to live purposefully despite adversity, and those who have been exposed to direct violence, Especially women who have experienced intimate partner violence using… other evidence-based guidelines, tools, and protocols. Noting that this will enhance the capacity of health and community care providers to enable them to provide culturally appropriate evidence and community services through the planned training courses. “Awareness sessions on topics of gender-based violence. The MH team consists of 8 community psychosocial support workers and a team leader divided into two teams (a team in Al-Hawl camp + a team in Al-Arisha camp). Workers in Al-Hawl camp - Al-Arisha camp will provide the following:
• Community initiatives to improve the situation of the host community
• Organizing mental health campaigns at the community level linked to the agenda
-Providing individual psychological interventions
Through the implementation of all activities in the project, emphasis will be placed on gender equality, including project staff and beneficiaries, and the following measures will be taken:1- Ensure fair participation of all beneficiaries of both genders in all activities, according to gender and age group.2- Ensure the availability of appropriate facilities for persons with disabilities and access to them.3- Focus on implementing community initiatives that send messages to prevent gender-based violence and promote gender

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project supports the provision of primary, secondary and primary health care services, as there is a great need to provide primary health care services in the targeted areas, especially in the camps. There is an urgent need to provide secondary health care and referral services in the camps as well as in the city of Al-Shaddadi, due to the great need for health services in general due to the fact that most health facilities are out of service.Through the project, fixed points are being prepared in all targeted areas to provide primary health services, and their number is 5 fixed medical points (one point in the center of Al-Hassakah city, one point in the city of Al-and a mobile team in the countryside of Al-Shaddadi city., A point in Al-Hawl camp (Sector Seven) And a point in Al-Arisha camp-Inspection services are provided through clinics (pediatrics - internal medicine - women's), as well as providing first aid services and providing free medicine. Primary mental health services are also provided by teams of psychological and social specialists.As for secondary health services, they will be provided by adopting a referral system, where reliance will be placed on the medical points affiliated with the project, especially in the camps, where beneficiaries who need operations will be referred to hospitals in the city that will be contracted with in the future, as well as in the city of Al-Hassakah and the city of Al-Shaddadi, so it will be approved. Referrals from the project’s medical points, as well as external referrals from doctors, and the beneficiary’s health and social condition are studied within the specified priority criteria and in agreement with WHO.-Secondary health care services include the following: performing surgical operations of all kinds - as well as performing radiography - and providing medical analysis services.Contracts will be made with a number of hospitals in the city of Al-Hasakah (Al-Raja Hospital - Issam Baghdi Hospital - Shabo Hospital), as well as contracts with a number of radiography centers and medical analysis laboratories within the city of Al-Hasakah.
