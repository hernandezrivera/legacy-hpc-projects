Project Summary

The case of displacement due to the ongoing war in Darfur, since early 2003 up to date is created a kind of violence against women and child, it’s destroyed the social relation, livelihood, basic service and development opportunity in Darfur Region. In this project AOSCD is planning in South and East Darfur stats through Protection sector to save lives and reduce vulnerabilities, especially of women and children by increasing gendered power to reducing Violence Against Women, Girls VAW/G and boys by providing community awareness and training for the Women, Girls, and Boys.
. AOSCD will establish community systems to strengthen protection and referral services, and build community ability to recognize and understand protection risks and stop harmful practices, such as child labor, FGM, GBV, etc.
Violence against women and girls has reportedly intensified due to the fragile economic and social structures. Girls are married off while extremely young for dowry to cope with low incomes; men unemployment results in frustration which often catalyzes violent behaviors. In addition, it is reported that men are often power holder of the household, which leads to their increased control over women reproductive health. All of this is coupled by a spread fear amongst survivors and the community more in general of stigmatization over reporting of violence, acceptance of violence especially within family structures, low socio-economic status of women and lack of awareness amongst community members. Overall, various needs assessment conducted formally and informally by AOSCD and other relevant partners. So the need for GBV service providers to implement specialized GBV services in the areas of intervention is very high, AOSCD has conducted informal interviews with women and girls residing in the health facilities of the IDPs camps where it works, revealing that there are significant barriers for survivors seeking support due to lack of awareness of SGBV consequences and available services; fear of retaliation by perpetrators; blaming of survivors and lack of confidentiality in the community and service providers.
As part of protection intervention, AOSCD also provide life skills and livelihood opportunities to ensure the evidence of AAP , also youth and women who are least protected when they do not have sustained relatively safe income. AOSCD will provide venerable children In schools of target areas with recreational materials as cross cutting activities of education but it serves as protection for children in addition to that we will contract two class rooms with semi permanent in each target areas. .

Needs

The Humanitarian Bulletin Sudan Issue 19 | 5 – 25 November 2018 reported in East Darfur’s Al Nimir camp, the enrollment of refugee IDPs and Host community children at primary schools increased by 11 per cent in October, with 75 per cent of primary-school aged children in the camp now enrolled in school, UNEPA in their latest report appointed that South Darfur and according to WHO there are 584,237 people in need of humanitarian assistance and support including 328,942 children. There are 367,398 protracted IDPs (60% children) in need of assistance. The ongoing insecurity and economic situation has impacted on lives of affected communities, where some families are suffering too much to sustain access for their children to basic services, with increasing needs for direct support. Child Protection Sub-sector identified critical gaps need and most is gaps of capacity among the affected communities and systems for ensuring resilience and takes over responsibilities. In South Darfur Nyala ( Kalma , Bilal, Outash, Dreege IDPs camps, are among the highly ranked areas in need of support for 2019 mostly for strengthening capacities and systems to access sustainable services; according to Plan monthly updates; Humanitarian agencies are steadily shifting towards early recovery programming after almost 15 years of lifesaving services deliveries. In East Darfur , Khor Omer and Alnim IDPs camps and Al Nimir children are in need of protection and care from parents and community based structures and systems. In Kalam IDPs camp AOSCD has transformed 2 Child Friendly Spaces (CFS) to the community. Violence against women and girls has reportedly intensified due to the fragile economic and social structures. Girls are married off while extremely young for dowry to cope with low incomes; men unemployment results in frustration which often catalyzes violent behaviors. Community Based Child Protection Network (CBCPN) is also in need of capacity on organizational techniques. Child labour is very dominant in all local markets, which lead to abuse and exploitation and school dropped out. Girls were exposed to violence, abuse and harassment represented on increased teen age pregnancy, some lead to unsafe abortions some or death, girls also exposed to female genital mutilation/cutting (FGM/C), absence of knowledge and awareness about sexual and reproductive health and life skills among boy and girls has a major contribution to unwanted teen age pregnancies and social stigma. While adolescent boys and youth are also involved in sexual harassment of girls, drugs, sexual abuse, rape and recruitment. The project will strengthen local capacity to undertake documentation programmes in order to generate credible knowledge and data on the commission of gender-based sexual violence in armed conflicts in order to ensure domestic and international accountability of these crimes.The project will also provide assistance to IDPs in need of medical or healthy care and Hygiene kids
