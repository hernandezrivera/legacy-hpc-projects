Project Summary

Supporting the economic cycle and the resilience of societies in Maaret Tamsrin, Idleb, and Al-Bab communities as locations that have a high need to support economic and livelihood opportunities through; establishing and building the capacity of Community Representative Committee (CRC) in each location to represent the local community in all activities, rehabilitating three markets and roads along with supporting 75 CFWs, and 175 (50% male, 50% female, 15%PwDs)male and female existing business owners will be granted with $1,200 to expand their project to be more sustainable and capable of employing 350 beneficiaries(50% male, 50% female, 15%PwDs) who will be trained through apprenticeship modality (on-job training) for four months. Moreover, 150 males and females (50% male, 50% female, 15%PwDs)will be granted $1500 to start their own businesses to support themselves and fill the gap existing in the targeted communities.
-A Community Representative Committee in each location will be formed to represent the local community in all interventions.
-Increasing the capacity of the three markets in the targeted locations by working on restoring sidewalks and sewage, securing containers, operating lighting, and equipping toilets... This will be carried out by supporting 30 workers in the form of CFW for 2 months
-Improving the main roads that reach the target markets, this will be carried out by supporting 45 workers for 2 months in the form of CFW.
- Providing a grant of 1200$ to 175 owners of existing small businesses in order to expand, to be more capable and resilient in the face of economic fluctuations and a Committee will be formed to select projects after field visits to see its ability to expand and to verify their information. The committee then will: review the applications and visit each business. Thus, The most competent and feasible projects will be selected
A 5-day training will be provided to the beneficiaries on business development, E-marketing, market, …, and accounting.
An amount of $1200 will be granted to each beneficiary in 2 payments to ensure the business owner’s seriousness and continue as Business owners will receive trainees as a condition for obtaining a grant to expand their business.
- Qualifying 350 men and women to enter the labor market through Apprenticeship (on-the-job training in the workplace) after a 5-day capacity building training in entering the labor market, Internet, communication skills, ..and accounting.
Then shop owners (175) who were selected and granted 1200$ to expand their businesses in the previous activity will be contracted to train 350 in the workplace for practical, hands-on experience. for 4 months, while each trainee receives $80 per month. That will let trainees gain hands-on experience and the trust of Business owners which in turn will encourage them to employ the trainees.
-150 males and females will be selected in the three targeted areas to receive 5-days Business development training and a $1500 grant to start up their new business
An amount of $1500 will be granted to each beneficiary in 2 payments to ensure the business owner’s seriousness and commitment to their plans.
The professions which will be granted will be selected according to the TAS need assessment in the targeted areas.
This project integrates several activities to comprehensively support the economic cycle by strengthening the infrastructure, markets, and roads, enhancing the qualified workforce, supporting projects and businesses, and improving the marketing capabilities of the beneficiaries.
