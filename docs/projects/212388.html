0 - Project Summary (extended)

In response to the FSA SO2, and SO3 our proposed project aims to address the urgent needs of returnees & vulnerable population as identified in the FSA Sector HNO 2024 This project focus on the Rehab of bakeries, irrigation systems & the distribution of essential inputs. Our project design stems from thorough assessments conducted in collaboration with the DoA & relevant stakeholders. These assessments identified the critical needs for enhancing community resilience & HHs livelihood sustainability & via securing subsidized bread, particularly among the most vulnerable populations. The project will be implemented across 3 governs, prioritizing subdistricts based on severity scales 3&4. 1. Light Rehab of bakeries &provision of new production lines, ZARBAH PiN 2,847, DB 20,000 in 25 adjacent towns, RDam-At Tall PiN 123,421, DB 35,000, DEZ-Muhasan PiN 10,088 DB 15,000. 2. Rehab of irrigation systems, including canals in Aleppo-Maskana PIN 36,031, Rehab of olive nurseries plant in Daraa Izraa PiN 48,434, DB 1750. 3. Distribution of agricultural inputs such as seeds, fertilizer, pesticides, & equipment in Maskaneh, Quneitra & DEZ, DB 7500. Our proposed activities encompass a range of activities aimed at 1. Coordination with relevant ministries & departments. 2. Procurement procedures & contract management. 3. Site supervision & monitoring of materials & works. 4. M&E including field visits & beneficiary feedback mechanisms. To mitigate potential risks associated with the project, various measures have been identified: 1. Health risks associated with construction works will be addressed through the enforcement of appropriate safety measures & the provision of PPEs. 2. Discrimination in beneficiary selection will be minimized through needs-based distribution, pre-consultations with HHs & the strengthening of complaint & feedback mechanisms. 3. GBV & Protection from PSEA concerns will be mainstreamed into the project, with the establishment of a call center & CRM. Operation R including miscommunication, resource limitations, & approval delays, have been anticipated & addressed through robust coordination mechanisms, stakeholder engagement & contingency planning. In conclusion, our proposed project seeks to address the urgent needs of vulnerable population thru targeted interventions with bakery Rehab, irrigation system improvement & agricultural input distribution. Thru careful planning, coordination, & risk mitigation strategies, we aim to contribute to the resilience & livelihood sustainability of the most vulnerable communities.
