Project Summary

Enlargement of Project # 160045: 1. Scale up access to diagnosis and treatment and necessary follow-up of COVID-19, in
women, men, boys and girls form both IDP and host populations. 2. Provide training and ongoing supportive supervision to
male and female healthcare workers at facility and community level on COVID-19 infection prevention and control and MHPSS.
years and below. 3. Deploy additional male and female CHWs and FHWs to facilitate health
education/promotion on COVID-19 at health facilities and FHWs at community level while observing infection prevention protocols such as social distancing, using megaphones. 4. Form representative (gender, clan, IDP and host balanced)
community health committees/health centre committees to provide oversight of COVID-19
implementation activities. 5. Scale up surveillance and response to COVID-19 outbreaks through stand-by emergency
rapid response teams and mobilise community to respond to mitigate impact of disease
outbreaks., Total estimated beneficiaries = 51400
