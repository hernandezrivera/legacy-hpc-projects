Project Summary

One of the main sector plans is the expansion and rehabilitation of protective and safe learning environments including gender-sensitive WASH facilities in school and school inclusiveness for children with disabilities. In addition to provision of school furniture, supplies, stationary, heaters and water tanks.
The protracted crisis in Syria has resulted over 5.8 million school-aged children and over 120,000 teachers to be in need of education assistance inside Syria, 61 per cent of whom are in acute and immediate need.
Children who are out of school face increased protection risks including child marriage and the worst forms of child labour.
The project main goal is to provide support to vulnerable persons by addressing the most critical humanitarian needs. This project will support people in need, especially school-aged children via improved access to safe and protective spaces through small-scale rehabilitation of schools in the targeted cities through rehabilitation, equipping and furnishing of partially damaged existing schools in, Dar’a, Al Quneitra, and rural Damascus to improve the learning spaces through implementing small-scale rehabilitation, including gender-sensitive/inclusive WASH facilities and ensure receiving safe and adequate access to safe educational environment.
The project is planned to target 15 schools with estimated number of 6000 students to be targeted.
GOPA-DERD will coordinate with Ministry of Education and Directorates of Education in target areas during the project. During the life of this project, GOPA-DERD will coordinate with the community leaders, other humanitarian actors providing assistance in this area, Education sector, through the sector meetings. As well GOPA-DERD will l coordinate with suppliers, local authorities and school to make a plan of works for each school.
Since one of the sector’s priorities in school rehabilitation works is the concern for health facilities, therefore coordination will be made with the Wash sector to be compliance with the standards approved by them in this issue.
GOPA will engages the community and schools teachers in work plan and project objective and review of the project as their suggestions will be consider.
During the meeting, GOPA team will explain to community and teachers project work plan, project objective, complaints mechanism and encourage families to send their drop out children to school in order to join schools in the next academic year.
Before completing the rehabilitation works and equipping of the schools, back to learning campaign will be prepare in coordination with MoE and local communities in order to encourage the communities to send their children to school in coming. The campaign will include distributing Posters, Brochures, and leaflets to the communities.

4 - Needs

Due to a shortage of functional classrooms, classes are overcrowded and over one million children required attending double-shift schools and sometimes to walk for long time to reach the nearest schools. Teaching capacity remains overstretched. Children who are out of school face increased protection risks including child marriage and the worst forms of child labor. GOPA-DERD depends mainly on HNO 2022 as well as GOPA-DERD team has conducted a needs assessment aiming to provide an overview of the situation in the areas to address these gaps of providing the assistance. The needs assessment visits have a clear idea of the population dynamics and need. As well, through needs assessment GOPA-DERD was able to identify the most schools in need to be targeted through this project. One of the main sector plans is the expansion and rehabilitation of protective and safe learning environments including gender-sensitive WASH facilities in school and school inclusiveness for children with disabilities. In addition to provision of school furniture, supplies, stationary, heaters and water tanks.

5 - Implementation of activities

Field visits will be conducted to each school to prepare all needed documents including BOQs.The works will be carried out according to the tendering method followed by GOPA through a dedicated contractor. -The execution of the work will be by contractors who are experienced in the field of architectural and civil projects. -After all planned works are being completed, a special committee is formed to take over the executed works and to ascertain the quality in the specified time. The work will then be officially handed over to the concerned authorities (Directorate of Education and communities leaders on the schools areas). The rehabilitation will include implementing minor maintenance works of the schools including wooden works, aluminum works, electric works in addition to rehabilitation of WASH facilities taking into consideration gender-segregated and special needs of disabilities students. Also the project will provide Equipping & Furnishing for the targeted schools with the following items: -Class desk seats - Class tables - Class chairs - Whiteboard - Stationery (one per school / Yearly), supplies, heaters and water tanks will be provided to each school.The schools will be chosen upon the field visits to the planned targeted areas in coordination with MoE and Education Sector. The implementation will include rehabilitate about 225 new classrooms in 15 schools as estimated number of 9000 of children (3-17 years, girls/boys) will benefit from classrooms rehabilitated.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

At the hiring phase of new volunteers, and within the scope of GOPA-DERD’s Recruitment policy and whilst developing the capacities of existing volunteers, GOPA-DERD makes sure that all new volunteers sign the PSEA, Code of Conduct and Whistleblowing policies after conducting training on it. All our activities use a rights-based approach to provide a protective environment that promotes equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social specification of targeted populations.Also, all our GOPA-DERD volunteers have already received a package of training including Do-No-Harm: psychological first aid, non-violent communication, protection induction, basics of SGBV, first aid, PSEA and risk management in case of any emergency, as well as other basic information that enables them to act towards any person in need with total respect and most importantly to deliver assistance while protecting the dignity of the person.GOPA-DERD will ensure the “Do-No-Harm” principle by carrying out a Protection Risk Analysis as part of its strategy development.
