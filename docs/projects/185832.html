Project Summary

"Based on the needs as per JMSNA-2021, our community consultation and different need assessment, For the JRP-2022, total 24,500 families (123,453 individuals) have been targeted for site development and improvement program. The camps where the activities are planned include camp 4 (2,500 HHs), camp 4 ext. (800 HHs), camp 5 (2,000 HHs), camp 13 (3,500 HHs), camp 17 (1,700 HHs), camp 19 (2,000 HHs). Conversely, the targeted areas in the host community include Haldiapalong union (4,000 HHs), Jaliapalong union (4,000 HHs) and Rajapalong union(4,000 HHs). The targeted areas have been selected based on their vulnerability to natural disasters and lack of interventions. Caritas Bangladesh (CB) will provide wide range of interventions with the community-based approach for both the Rohingya and host community. Inclusion of different groups including women, men, adolescent girls, boys, elderly aged people, and persons with disability have been ensured all through the program cycle like design phase, Assessment, implementation & Monitoring phase. In the cash for work (CFW) activities, women, elderly aged peoples and persons with disability will be included in light weight work or works according to their preference. The joint needs assessment will be conducted through community consultations with community people along with relevant partners and the identified areas will be mapped with GIS. Community people will also participate implementation & monitoring process of the project. The overall program will include COVID-19 safety considerations, Do no harm approach, natural disasters included heavy rainfall, PSEA, child protection, safeguarding, effective complain & feedback mechanism etc. Enhancing Protection & gender Mainstreaming for all site development activities is one of the key objectives for CB in 2022. CRS will provide all kinds of technical support to Caritas Bangladesh throughout the project. Ramp with handrail will be ensured for the peoples with disability for any shelter interventions.
Regular and effective coordination will be ensured with government authorities such as CiC, CMO, RRRC, UNO, and DC Office, camp wise Site Management, SMSD Sector, UNHCR and IOM. In 2022, CB has targeted Construction/Repair of different types pedestrian Bridge (400 Meter);Drainage with Discharge Point (3000 Meter);pedestrian access with Bamboo handrail (2000 Meter); pedestrian access (3000 Meter) ; Masonary (HBB) Vehicular road (450 Meter); retaining wall & slope protection (5000 Meter); Repair/Reconstruction of earthern road through CFW activities – Host community (8100 Meter); engagement of site development daily laborers such as women/persons with disabilities, Elderly aged people (3%) to cover targeted camps & host community areas. CB will construct/repair mostly sustainable low-cost brick/masonry infrastructures for site development/improvement. and some bamboo infrastructures will also be replaced based on need and their damage level and bamboo related infrastructure will also construct as need basis. The primary focus for CB for this aspect will be to ensure safe access to CiC offices, health centers, distribution centers, law enforcement centers, WFS, CFS, learning centers, other community gathering places etc. Furthermore, CB will conduct capacity building and orientation to the community on the community-based approach, DRR, repair & maintenance and ownership of these structures for . All these activities will contribute to improving social cohesion and strengthening resilience of the targeted households.""

1 - Identified Needs

Cox’s Bazar district has always been prone to natural disasters being a coastal and hilly area in Bangladesh. Small to large scale weather events cause moderate to severe damage to site development infrastructures both in camps and host communities on regular intervals. Untreated bamboo is used in most of the structures which deteriorate quickly. Households that are situated in landslide prone areas face additional risk of shelter damage and injuries due to heavy rain, strong wind, soil erosion etc. The Joint MSNA published in October 2021, indicated that 22% of the households faced challenges to walk on the pathways that are blocked, damaged or slippery. The targeted areas of Caritas Bangladesh (CB) are mostly in hilly areas which require additional stepping, retention walls, slope protection, pedestrian access, and drainage. In 2021, COVID-19 restrictions stopped all kinds of site improvement and development interventions for a significant amount of time. Within this lockdown, safe access to the key community facilities like CiC offices, health centers, distribution centers, law enforcement centers, WFS, CFS, learning centers etc. could not be repaired. Furthermore, this year’s monsoon was quite severe compared to last year and caused massive floods which damaged the site improvement structures like pathways, bridges, retention walls, drains etc. Household survey conducted in the camps by CB identified that the people in the targeted camps faced injury while moving in slippery slopes due to lack of slope protection and retention walls. Moreover, due to lack of effective drainage system, the rainwater could not flow downwards properly and households which are in the downstream faced significant water logging. The bamboo structures faced a significantly more rapid deterioration due to these natural disasters. On the other hand, the concerns of protection have a serious impact on the physical and psychological well-being of the people in both Rohingya and host community. Due to lack of or low community consultations, the site improvement/development interventions usually fail to address the most urgent needs of the community. More focused should be put on persons with disabilities as they find it difficult to access to different areas of the camps and they feel unsafe within their own communities. The local community is still largely male dominated which makes addressing gender & Protection related issues very important. Moreover, There is a huge gap of community participatory approach/equity all through the camps/Host community.
