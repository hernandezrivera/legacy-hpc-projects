Project Summary

The project aims to address and mitigate protection risks affecting children, with a focus on various challenges such as family separation, violence, neglect, and abuse. It involves placing children affected by these risks into alternative care arrangements. Additionally, the project emphasizes comprehensive multi-sectorial case management support for children at risk, encompassing issues like abuse, neglect, exploitation, child labor, and child marriage. The initiative pays particular attention to children living with disabilities who are susceptible to protection risks, ensuring they receive specialized child protection services. Moreover, the project seeks to empower adolescent girls and boys by providing life skills education, contributing to their personal development and resilience against potential risks. The success of the project is likely measured by the quantifiable outcomes in terms of the number of children placed in alternative care, those receiving comprehensive support, and the specific impact on girls, boys, and adolescents with disabilities.

2 - Activities

Adjust placements as needed based on evaluations and feedback.Multi-Sectorial Case Management Support:System Establishment: Set up a comprehensive case management system involving various sectors such as health, education, social services, and legal assistance to address the diverse needs of children at risk.Training: Provide specialized training to social workers and case managers to handle cases involving abuse, neglect, exploitation, child labor, and child marriage.
