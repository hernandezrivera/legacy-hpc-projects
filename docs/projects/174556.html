Project Summary

The 2020 Cameroon Humanitarian response report presented statistics of about 3.2million children who are in urgent need of humanitarian needs. The report englobes the crisis situation of children in the Far North Region who have been affected by violent through separation from their families , leaving them vulnerable to all sorts of abuse and exploitation, with little or no access to basic services such as health. Moreover, many of these children who have been separated from their families end up in quranic schools, yet, they still remain vulnerable and unprotected from radicalisation, recruitment and all forms of violence, abuse and exploitation. Our Assessment from engaging with children displaced by violence and separated from their families shows that over 60% of these children are in dire need of psychosocial support due to loss of their proper childhood and traumatic experiences from violence. Host communities in which these children get integrated equally do not respond fully to their needs to be protected socially and economically, because the communities themselves are faced with poverty and insecurity. There is therefore a great need to strengthen communities with the capacity to protect children, taking into consideration the four guiding principles of the Convention on the Rights of Children (survival and development, non-discrimination, child interests and child participation). To provide a strong responds to all this, an initiative that seeks to respond to the deep concerns of security and protection of children within contexts of violence. It aims at protecting children (aged 03-17) from radicalization, recruitment and all forms of violence, abuse and exploitation in the Far North Region by providing them with psychosocial support through recreational activities, empowering families and local communities on child protection mechanisms and reconnecting children with their families and social safety service.
Local Youth Corner Cameroon will amplify her response to child protection in the context of the Far North Region by targeting wives of quranic teachers, families in conflict and homeless children that is boys, girls, and disable between the ages of 03-17 and provide them with psychosocial support through direct health assistance, recreational activities and counselling, set up early warning and response platforms to respond to and prevent violence against children and their recruitment/radicalization, build local family capacities on preventing separation during crisis and reconnect children with their families or social welfare services through identification and situational analysis. Also, loyoc has a great experience in working with young people especially adolescents to prevent them from being recruited by armed groups that have been active in the recruitment and abduction of children and adolescents in recent months according to UNDSS reports
