Project Summary

The outbreak of fighting in Sudan on 15 April 2023 has caused an influx of people fleeing the country to the crisis affected South Sudan. More than 259,451 individuals have fled from Sudan into South Sudan. Nine out of ten of those arriving are South Sudanese; of those fleeing, 50 % of the new arrivals are women and girls, nearly half of the Women and girls fleeing are below the age of 18 and 4% of the Women affected by the Sudan crisis are women over 60 years (UNHCR 2023). The primary cause of movement of people, particularly women and girls, from Sudan to South Sudan is to find Safety/protection. On their journey to south Sudan they face multiple safety risks and challenges, inadequate shelter, insufficient NFIs and GBV. The situation on their return locations are not sufficiently safe either. This is due to recurrent sub-national and localized violence, and clashes between State and non-State armed forces and community-based armed militias groups continue to displace civilians. The multiple displacements usually inconvenience whole communities; men, boys, Women, girls, and children. This is because not only do displacements endanger the lives of IDPs, but predispose the IDPs to a range of insecurities. Displacements in Unity and Upper Nile particular have been worsened for the last four years by widespread flooding, recurrent violence and localized dry spells. The most food insecure states between December 2023 and March 2024 where more than 50% of their populations are likely to face Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity are Unity State (61%), Jonglei State (59%), Upper Nile State (57%) (IPC September 2023). In order to ease the predicament of the displaced, returnees and most vulnerable host community and food insecure communities, Humane aid for Community Organization (HACO) proposes S/NFI response in Unity-Koch and Upper Nile States-Panyikang and Manyo; S/NFI Distribution, Emergency Shelter Upgrades, CVA, shelter rehabilitation and construction, awareness sessions on disaster risk reduction, setting up CFM/Communication and Community Engagement, Reaching community members through awareness sessions on disaster risk reduction, Forming and training Disaster Management Committees and developing Climate Change Adaptation Plans and training Partners on HLP (Due diligence, and access rights and challenges). HACO intends to reach 22,222 individuals taking into account Gender, Diversity and Inclusion (50% IDPs, 30% Returnees and 20% most vulnerable host community) with the S/NFI response in Unity and Upper Nile States.

2. Project activities

Like in Previous Experience in S/NFI response activities in Unity State; Mayom and Mankien that helped to improve the protection environment for the displaced, HACO hopes to reduce the exposure of Vulnerable people to protection threats and incidents, and those who are exposed have access to tailored, timely, appropriate services through integrated and inclusive humanitarian action by providing shelter and non-food items for displaced people in situations of emergency and transition in an accessible, gender-responsive manner. HACO Also intends to reduce Vulnerable people's exposure to protection threats and incidents, and those who are exposed have access to tailored, timely, appropriate services and Promote collective action as a Centrality of Protection and ensure the population groups targeted to receive assistance are consulted throughout the entire cycle of the response and their needs are taken into account in decision-making by HACO project team and Vulnerable people withstand and recover from shocks, have their resilience to shocks and stresses built, and seek solutions that respect their rights by Enhancing their resilience capacity especially of returnees and host communities in Panyikang, Manyo and Koch Counties. The Proposed project activities are linked to the S/NFI strategy of Emergency due to natural or humane induced disasters with focus on survival kits: NFI Distribution, Emergency Shelter Upgrades, Cash Voucher Assistance, shelter rehabilitation and construction, setting up CFM/Communication and Community Engagement fora and Transition strategy focusing on program design; Capacity building, Advocacy, Coordination and partnerships- awareness sessions on disaster risk reduction, setting up Disaster Management Committees, training Disaster Management Committees, developing Climate Change Adaptation Plans and training Partners on HLP (Due diligence, and access rights and challenges). Monitoring and Evaluation; Distribution and post Distribution monitoring.
