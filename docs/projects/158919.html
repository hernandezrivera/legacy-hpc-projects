Provide Catch-Up classes for children with Kidney failure, chronic, and normal diseases inside main hospitals in Gaza Strip

Basic Info

Project Name

Provide Catch-Up classes for children with Kidney failure, chronic, and normal diseases inside main hospitals in Gaza Strip

Start Date

01/01/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

Overall Goal : Ensure all sick children get the right to learn in Gaza Strip
Objectives:
1- Establish Catch-Up Classes (School Class) for children with kidney failure and other chronic diseases inside main hospitals in Gaza Strip.
2. Provide psycho-social support to vulnerable children inside the hospitals
3. Provide compensate classes for temporary illness children inside the hospitals.
Target group:
More than 600 sick children with chronic diseases (specially kidney failure) and their ages from 6 to 18 years, and 30 teachers for different subjects.

After that assessment, the MPCimplement a pilot project with partnership with MOHE & MOH which aim to establishSchool Class As self-assessment with cooperation with MOHE, MOH, and UNRWA onDecember 2017. Then this project is turn to a regular project on MPC and was implementing during 2018 and 2019 scholastic years, the project retched about 202 child (under 15) (40% girls) with kidney failure takes the classes in the Rintisi hospital only ( it’s treats about 45 child form all Gaza as reported in MoH), and more than 200 other children (Under 15) with blood diseases, cancer , chronic diseases, and other diseases. 2 - Activities

The idea of the project is to provide catch up classes for 600 illness children ( 45% girls)(kidney failure as essential ) those children resident on the hospital for treatment for morethan 15 days in each month, so that, most of them dropout from schools and they can'tActivities in details Activity 1-Establish Catch-Up Class (School Class) inside 3 maingovernmental hospitals in Gaza Strip Those classes will provide 4 basic educationalsubjects and exams as realistic school class and contains two classroom, for the childrenstay for longtime inside the hospitals, whose full or partial dropout of school because oftheir illness such as kidney failure , cancer, blood diseases, and gastrointestinal diseases.This Class is certified , special books contents, and full supervision by MOHE and allstudents gets accredited certification from MOHE to complete educational stages withcooperation with UNRWA and MOHE. So, every child resident in the hospital for a longtimewill take this classes by especial teachers , and will get psychological support, alsosupport child mother from the specialist, this will help the child and his/her family to beacceptance for child situation and the child be able to complete the his educationalstages as in real school and gets the needed certificates. This classes will provide all theneeded tools, books, and equipment's for teaching, and customize especial room insidethe hospitals. More than 90 child (40% girls ) will enroll in this classes and takes lesionsas School Class. This is a response activity. Activity 2- Provide compensate classes in thechosen hospitals. The compensative class will server children whose stay inside thehospital for short time (or have emergency disease), and they already enrollment inschools. So that, this class will help them to continue their lessons as their peers in theirschools and provide psychological support. Those children will take the needed subjectsas their schools study schedule in different hospital departments or in the catch-up class.The compensate class will server 510 children (55% girls) whose stay inside the hospitalfor short time ( about 40 child (55% girls) each month) , and they already enrollment inschools. So that, this class will help them to continue their school's subjects as their peersin the school. Those children take the lessons in different hospital departments or in thecatch-up class. This will help children to be able to complete the studding in their schoolsas normal and didn't lose more time in understand the lost lessons. This is a remedialactivity. Activity 3: Training and capacity building for 30 teachers (70% females) toconduct the catch-up classes with the children inside hospitals Classes. The training willbe on (New teachers course) and (methodology and planning the lessons courses) withcooperate with the Ministry of Education and UNRWA . This training will improve teachersskills in dealing with illness children, and learn new methodologies in teaching. This is aremedial activity. Activity 4: provide more than 3 psychological support session for 200children (50% girls) ( 85% ages from 6 to 15 year) who are suffering from kidney failure,cancer, and other chronic diseases this will helps them to dealing with their illness asnormal, and coexistence with it. Most of children frustrated, hopeless, and unwilling tolearn, after the sessions, they will accept to return to studding through the Catch-upclasses in the hospital and be able to communicate with their peers, teachers as normal.This will helps child mother to support, and provide assistant for her child. She will believeon her child abilities to continue the education as other children, and helps her child toachieving his/her ambitions. This is a remedial activity.
