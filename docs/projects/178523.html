Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls, women, and men, with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities with attention to new IDPs and people wirth special needs, targeting (67000 women, 62000 men, 58000 girls, 34000 boys) including all age groups; children, adolescences, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults.5600 of them people with disabilities.
This will be done through specific interventions focusing on:
• Provision of essential and primary health care services to cover the health care needs of vulnerable populations in targeted communities, including RH services, Child Health care as well as CD and NCD services. these services will be provided through:
1-One PHC center L3 in Afrin, (B0203052),Community:Afrin
(7500 women, 6800 men, 5500 girls, 4000 boys)
2-One PHC center L2 in Atareb (G0703109),community:Kafr Naseh
(6500 women, 6000 men, 4500 girls, 4200 boys)
3-One mobile clinic in Harim (SDI Mobile clinic 3),Kafr Mu,Kafr Hum,Bsnia
(4500women,4300 men, 4000 girls, 2250 boys)
4-One mobile clinic in Al-Atareb(Temp code),Al Atareb subdistrict
(5500women,5000 men, 4500 girls, 3800 boys)
5-One mobile clinic in Al-Dana and mareet Tamisrin(G0700151)
(6500 women, 6500 men, 5500 girls, 4000 boys)
• Ensuring comprehensive general health services including :ICU ,outpatient clinics ,hospitalization,laboratory services , and referral pathways through one General Hospital located in Harim city –Idlib.
(G0705021).(16000 women, 18100 men, 15000 girls, 9750 boys)
• Providing gynecological and obstetrics services, address emergency obstetric and newborn care cases (B&CEmONC), post-surgical care for mothers and other women, and
providing incubator services for newborn, also outpatient clinics and referral pathway
These services will be provided through one maternal and child care hospital located in Jeser Al Shogour city-(19500 women, 15000 men, 18000 girls, 6000 boys)
• Support and operate Swasia center (B0203062) for prostheses, orthosis&physical rehabilitation in Afrin city(Manufacturing and installation of artificial limbs and splints, Providing physiotherapy services, with include educating the beneficiaries and their caregivers about the beneficiaries’ physical disabilities and impairments, tailored facility based as well as home exercises, also donate of assistive devices and mobility aids, provide psychosocial support services and counseling to patients to suit their needs.(500 women, 300 men, 500 girls, 250 boys)
• Support and operate Tell Abyad center for physical rehabilitation located in Al Raqqa, Tell Abyad city,providing emergency response to basic and specific needs of crisis-affected population inside Syria targeting all people with physical or functional impairment who could benefit from physical rehabilitation services.
(500 women, 300 men, 500 girls, 250 boys)
• Expanding access to specialized services for hostility-affected populations, including Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) services through:
Integrate MHPSS services including MHgap intervention and focused - non-specialized services by PSWs within SDI health facilities, also at the community levels in Atareb, Afrin, Salqin, Marret Tamsrin, Harim subdistricts
•Providing blood products that are very important to save patients' lives who have major surgeries, acute haemolytic anaemia, thalassemia, etc, and thalassemia services including chelating therapy and medications interventions ,these services will be provided in each of Afrin and Harim areas within Harim general hospital and Afrin blood bank.
•Disseminate and promote health-seeking and basic hygienic behaviors focusing on COVID-19 and prevention measures, 20 CHWs will provide services as an activities provided at the community level .
All activities will be implemented considering COVID-19 protective measures.

SDI will implement this health project for people in need in 8 sub districts: Afrin,Atareb,ِAlDana,Salqin,Marret Tamsrin,Jeser Al shogour,Harim and tell Abyad through the following:Two PHC, 1 PHC center level 3 in Afrin and1 PHC center level 2 in Abin Saman in Atareb sub district, three mobile clinics, 1 in Atareb subdistric,1 in Harim subdistric and 1 in Al-Dana and MaretTanisrin subdistricts in addition to 2 hospitals ,General hospital in Harim sub distric, and maternal and child care hosital in Jeser Al shougour area,and 2 specialized center in P&O and rehabilitation in Afrin and Tell Abyad areas.The main activities will be: provision of essential primary, secondary and tertiary services. Essential services comprisesurgical and trauma services, reproductive health, childhealth ,mental health, diagnostic services, and specialized care for people with disabilities in addition to prevention and treatment of communicable and non-communicable diseases, .Also Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies, including personal protective equipment (PPE)and Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services.Field M&E team will conduct feild visits to ensure the quality of services, in addition to verifying the raw data before preparing HIS report, conducting beneficiaries’ satisfaction surveys,Observational checklists/ spot checks (once-per-month) - following complaints,The reporting will be according to guidelines agreed bythe technical group SRH,MHPSS, NCD,CHW of the health cluster (4Ws, HeRAMS,and other reports)

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Beneficiaries are at risk on being exposed to any sexual misconduct by the humanitarian workers and they might not report the misconduct due to the following reason: 1. lack of knowledge of reporting mechanisms, especially for people with disabilities and women living in far away areas. 2. feeling afraid of retaliation. 3. fear of stigma. 4. fear of deprivation of assistance. 5. being unable to understand written banners for being illiterate and/or for having a visual disability. 6. for children, language might be complicated To mitigate these risks, SDI will :1. aim at clearly announcing the hotline number and email specialized for PSEA of SDI and UN numbers. through many means, like, banners, social media, and messages to beneficiaries phones through telegram channel to ensure reaching to the biggest number of beneficiaries including women living in far away areas and people with disabilities. 2. Conduct PSEA training for all staff members and contractors/suppliers dealing with beneficiaries and make them read and sign CoC that state Zero Tolerance towards SEA violations. 3. Conduct awareness raising sessions on PSEA for beneficiaries to encourage them to report any violations and stress on the confidentiality and on eliminating any other fears that they might have.4. Conduct awareness raising sessions for children in an appropriate language. 5. complaint boxes6. Using PSEA field and organizational checklists tools.
