Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in Lakes State. WAV Gender Based Violence project plan will directly target 5,200 vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Rumbek Centre and Cueibet counties with a total of 2,000 females, 1000 males,1000 boys and 1200 girls 5% of the target population will include people living with disability, female headed households, pregnant and lactating women and the elderly .The project aims at providing quality GBV services to GBV survivors and reduce suffering of women, girls, men and boys by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs and promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks. WAV proposes to implement comprehensive, lifesaving SGBV prevention and response activities by providing quality and timely, age appropriate, case management and psychosocial support to GBV survivors.
The prioritized objectives to achieve the overall goal will include:
Cluster Objective 1:Reduce the suffering of GBV survivors including women, men, girls, boys and ability-challenged persons through the provision of multi-sectoral specialized GBV response services.
Cluster Objective 2: Vulnerable women, men, girls, and boys including ability-challenged persons have improved access to the provision of GBV prevention and risk mitigation interventions. Reduce the suffering of GBV survivors including women, men, girls, boys and ability-challenged persons through the provision of multi-sectoral specialized GBV response services.
Cluster Objective 3: Prevent and mitigate GBV risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience.
This projects is designed to improve responses to cases of GBV and enhance the ability of
survivors to recover from GBV incidences.
The main activities to be carried out to realize these objectives and achieve the overall goal will include :
1. Provision of survivor-centered psychosocial support focused on healing, empowerment and recovery
2. Provision of appropriate GBV case management services including coordinated care and support
3. Establish and / or strengthen GBV referral systems and linking GBV survivors to available response services and support
4.Implement Women and Girls Friendly Space (WGFS) programming to provide services, information and activities that promote healing, well-being and empowerment
5.Conduct dignity kit distribution and programming targeting vulnerable women and girls as part of GBV risk mitigation
6. Conduct community awareness raising activities on GBV risk factors, mitigation measures and on how survivors can access support and services in line with the “do no harm” principle.

1. Needs

Lakes State has experienced a great deal of conflict and flooding in recent years that has caused degenerated and rampant instability. The current projected population of Lakes State is estimated at 1,310,142 with Cueibet and Rumbek Centre Counties having an estimated population of between 360000. In May 25-28th 2023 an IRNA was conducted by the Rumbek ICCG in collaboration with the Lakes State RRC Office for verification assessment in the affected locations with a total of 7 clusters represented with a focus on the 3 payams(Pagor,Citcok and Duony) of Cueibet and 2 payams(Matangai and Amongpiny) of Rumbek Centre counties of which 12460 (men 3492,women 6230,boys 1246 and Girls 1492)people had been displaced in Cueibet and 9885 (men 2977,women 4943,boys 989 and girls 977) in Rumbek Centre,the total number of IDPs who were repatriated to Rumbek north is 2,832 mainly from Pagarau, MacharAchiek and Jier IDP camps in Nyang, of which 1248 are women, the elderly this was both direct and indirect beneficiaries. Furthermore Rumbek Centre and Cueibet counties have been subjected to numerous displacements with IDPs being integrated into the host community .The displacements are caused by floods ,conflicts of inter and intra-communal violence ,revenge killing, farmers pastoralists conflicts, land disputes, clan fighting and many more making women, children, elderly and PSNs victims of circumstances. The immediate impacts of flooding included damage to property, several houses and latrines have collapsed in the villages posing danger of open defecation in bushes which increases risk of GBV to women and girls, many people are displaced from their homes and have moved to higher grounds and are sleeping outside exposing them to GBV risks . Damage of bore holes has caused congestion at the few functioning bore holes which are quite far exposing women and girls to sexual gender based violence . The 2 counties of Rumbek Centre has WAV as the GBV service provider however Cueibet lacks GBV service providers and trained health service providers there is also unavailability of PEP kit in the health facilities in all the locations visited however due to lack of awareness on GBV services women and girls were reluctant to open up on GBV issues due to fear of criticism from community members and families for discussing such issues . Sexual violence and other forms of GBV against women and girls are becoming more wide spread in Rumbek Centre, and Cueibet counties due to movement of the populations because of flooding and conflicts. Through WAV GBV programming and peace building and governance projects in Rumbek Centre and Cueibet WAV has noticed increasing and targeted attack with existing and growing levels of sexual violence perpetrated. In Rumbek Centre and Cueibet counties adolescent girls and women are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information and experience growing levels of sexual violence, abductions ,sexual harassment ,early and forced marriages, sexual exploitation and abuse and also other forms of sexual violence being perpetrated in the Rumbek Centre and Cueibet counties . In addition, fear of retaliation through revenge killings was mentioned to be a major barrier in accessing the available services by survivors. Across the two counties the GBV situation has further been exacerbated by the insecurity situation, flooding and COVID-pandemic. Through this plan at least 70% targeted beneficiaries will be supported through PSS and life skills. Responding to the new crisis requires a targeted emergency response outside the current WAV program sites with a total of 10000 beneficiaries targeted for this intervention with 5% being people with disability .WAV will utilize existing internal capacity to respond quickly and effectively to the emerging crisis and will provide emergency GBV services in Rumbek Centre and Cueibet counties.
