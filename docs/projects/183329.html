Project Summary


Project Name: SCEC (Supporting communities to ensure education)
The following project is done by delta association in order to address the unmet educational needs of the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant school aged children who are in school and out of school. Due to the socio-economic crisis in Lebanon, many parents were obliged to stop their children’s education in order to save money to satisfy the minimum standard and basic needs of each family (food, medication).
At the beginning of this project, a needs assessment will be done in order to identify the students that left their schools in districts of, Aaley, and Chouf. An assessment will be prepared by our M&E covering all needed info for households in several topics, educational, economical, and food security. All public school directors in districts of Aaley and Chouf will be contacted. Delta will ask for a list of all registered students in school year 2019-2020, to compare it with registered students of 2020-2021. Students who left their schools will be contacted, and reasons for their dropouts will be discussed with their caregivers. After knowing the reasons of all the caregivers, households will be divided into clusters, each cluster having its own reason that caused their children’s dropouts. Delta will perform online meetings with all these clusters separately (according to their problem), in order to help them and persuade them to return their students to the school. In addition, certain vulnerable students that remain in school, will be given awareness sessions to retain them in school.
The PSS team in Delta will perform a certain study to know the students suffering from psychosocial problems, due to COVID, and Beirut’s blast. Delta will perform multiple awareness sessions through certain qualified protection staff discussing different topics such as harassment, risky behaviors, violence, and bullying. In addition, Delta will raise students’ awareness regarding SEA (Sexual Exploitation and Abuse). Prevention of Sexual Abuse and all types of abuse is a must. In case of any kind of abuse, Delta has referral forms that can be sent to the competent references. Children and even their caregivers should be lectured on SGBV risk. Delta will also raise students’ and caregivers’ awareness on online safety, by explaining the dangers of using the internet, hacks, and untrusted sites.
Reports will be prepared by M&E officer during the duration of the project, regarding the lessons learned and will be sent to the donors to guarantee the success of the project. Four reports will be done, one report every two months, in order to follow up with students’ records and improvements.
Delta will be informed about the number of students with certain disabilities in order to provide them with assistive technologies. Delta has a partnership with NRDC (National Rehabilitation and Developmental Center) which will give Delta all the needed information regarding people with disabilities.
Households of students that dropped out from education will be given a certain amount of cash to help students return back to school. In addition, households of certain vulnerable students will be aided with an amount of cash, to help with their educational expenses. Delta, using its financial department, will prepare a plan about how the procedure will take place and will work by a partnership with OMT to send C4E to each household directly. A report of the households that received cash assistance will be sent to the donor. In addition, the finance team in Delta is preparing the yearly financial report to be sent to the external audit (Delta has audit reports of Years 2018-2019-2020). Delta will perform its procurement global policy in order to insure low cost solutions (low tech and high tech) and food packaging.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

A1: Conduct Needs Assessment to identify out of education Lebanese and migrant children, including dropouts from impact of socio-economic crisis.A2: Outreach, awareness raising & referrals on Out of Education Children (OOEC) to basic services and to bring them back to education.A3: Provision of assistive technologies for most vulnerable children with disabilities to enable their access to and retention in education.A4: Cash for Education support targeting the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant families to enable retention in education.A5.1 Homeschooling support including transferrable skills, responsive caregiving and basic and digital literacy to parents /caregivers.A5.2 PSS-SEL programs integrated in the remote learning support for the most vulnerable students, parents/caregivers.A5.3 Promoting and applying SGBV risk reduction in adapted learning programmes including through raising awareness amongst children and caregivers on violence, bullying, cyber bullying, online safety.A5.4 Supporting with low-cost solutions (both low-tech and high-tech) for inclusive remote learning in the customized learning support programs for the most vulnerable children and youth, adapted to age groups (ECE, Primary, Secondary) and location.A5.5 Procurement of food packages for children and youth enrolled in learning support programs.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Our protection department will make a plan to ensure safety and protection to all children who are in and out of schools. This plan will consider the role of caregivers and school directors and teachers as well, who will be given awareness and training sessions to ensure no SEA incidents will occur. In addition, the protection team will be available on a daily basis in all remote learning groups online, and will treat any referrals confidentially.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

There are many key elements of accountability, which includes participation and inclusion, where boys and girls of diverse backgrounds engage meaningfully, and avoiding any kind of discrimination against new students who came from private schools or any refugee student. Another key element is communication and transparency, where communication is facilitated between partners and people of concern. Sharing information and media, as well as financial reports that will be audited externally.
