Project Summary

The project aims to provide Wet Food Assistance and Fuel Efficient Stove to 14,000 beneficiaies among the most vulnerable in the target LGAs of Borno namely: Nganzai, Kale Balge and Gwoza
Wet food Assistance:
A total of 10,000 beneficiaries are being targeted for the wet food intervention. It is planned for the New Arrivals IDPs and IDPs in overcrowded camps to receive wet food for three days (Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner). The major deliverables include:
Provision of wet feeding support to 10,000 most vulnerable IDPs with no means of preparing the food items. The support will cover breakfast, lunch and dinner for three days for each target beneficiaries
• Breakfast – Pap (Kunu geda), Beans cake and bread or tea, bread and beans cake
• Lunch – Rice, beans, protein or tuwo shinkafa and protein
• Dinner - Rice, beans, protein or tuwo shinkafa and protein
Fuel Efficient Stove:
Provision of fuel-efficient stoves to 2,000 household. This will involve carrying out a baseline survey of energy utilization, training on usage of the Fuel – Efficient Stove (FES) and distribution of FES. A post distribution survey will also be carried out to evaluate the impact of the intervention.
The goal of the project is to provide emergency support to new arrivals IDPs at identified entry point in partnership with Nigerian Government, IOM and other related stakeholder to addresses food related emergencies faced by the IDPs. The food and fuel-efficient stove are critical to ensure that the affected populations can effectively and safely cook food, maximizing nutritional intake, and minimize protection vulnerabilities associated with fetching firewood

Needs

Now in its tenth year, the crisis in north-east Nigeria remains one of the most severe in the world. In recent months, thousands of new arrivals have been recorded, largely coming from hard-to-reach areas for reasons related to insecurity and military operations. This displaced population are highly food insecure and with disruption in commercial activities in the region in general, it is difficult for the New Arrivals to have any meaningful means of survival other than assistance from the humanitarian actors. In view of this the project aims to address the following needs:1. Lack of food for immediate consumption by New Arrivals IDPs and Returnees in some reception points in the target LGAs. 2. Some of the New Arrivals and Returnees have limited to no access to balanced and nutrient fortified food3. Some of the displaced population have no source of safe fuel or energy to prepare food items for there households. 4. Some of the IDPs have no means of livelihood after being displaced from their farms and place of residence.

Activities

The activities that will be carried out to address the identified needs include:For Wet Food Assistance to 10,000 beneficiaries:1. New Arrivals IDPs are documented at reception points2. Rapid Assessment Survey is carried out to analyse the IDPs.3. Provide per-distribution sensitization on entitlement, feedback mechanism and protection issues4. Procurement and distribution of cooked foodBreakfast – Pap (Kunu geda), Beans cake and bread or tea, bread and beans cakeLunch – Rice, beans, protein or tuwo shinkafa and proteinDinner - Rice, beans, protein or tuwo shinkafa and protein5. Execution of Post Distribution Monitoring to ensure the distribution is addressing the defined needs6. In collaboration with other humanitarian actors, KABHUDA will ensure distribution point are secure for distribution of food items For Fuel-Efficient Stove:1. Carryout baseline survey to ascertain the fuel utilization of target population2. Provide per-distribution sensitization on entitlement, feedback mechanism, protection issue, etc.2. Procure FES and distribute to 4,000 beneficiaries3. Train the 4,000 beneficiaries of usage and refueling of the FES4. In collaboration wit FAO & SAFE Working Group, KABHUDA will train 60 IDPs (20 each from the three LGAs) on manufacturing of Local FES with about 30% fuel efficiency5. Carryout outcome monitoring of beneficiaries of FES.6. There will be zero tolerance for Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) or any form of exploitation of vendors, project memebers or security personnel
