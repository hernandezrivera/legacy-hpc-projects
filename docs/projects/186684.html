Project Summary

To Provide a simplified integrated nutrition, hygiene promotion and sanitation messages to the local communities, strengthen hygiene promotion and sanitation among PLWs in Yobe State.
Due to poor sanitation and hygiene, Nigeria annually incurs about US$3 billion or 594 billion
Naira economic loss (December 2015 exchange rate). This sum is equivalent of 1.3% of the
national GDP. Out of US$ 3 billion economic loss, US$2.5 billion is lost each year only due to
premature deaths, mainly children under five years of age in Nigeria this is according to National Hygiene promotion strategy.
Various global studies suggest that merely washing hands with soap after defecation and
before eating, can reduce over 42-47% diarrhoeal cases among young children.
Diarrhea is the second leading cause of mortality among Nigerian children under five years of
age, after pneumonia. It is frequently related to the consumption of contaminated water and to
unhygienic practices in food preparation and disposal of faeces
In connection to hand washing, this generally means washing hands with soap after defecation,
handling children's faeces or cleaning their bottoms or disposing faeces, and before eating,
feeding/breast feeding children, cooking and handling food or water.
The need for focused and strategic intervention is therefore urgent and compelling. To that effect Monclub intends to support the whole intervention by providing timely, and simplified information to the local communities, targeting pregnant and lactating women to reduce the rate of lack of hygiene and clean water preservation or used link mortality. the conflict in the Northeast has left thousands of people away from their homes, women and children included thereby making them susceptible to the risk and danger of diseases associated to the consumption of unclean water and malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women.
This is a women focused program, which will have a direct bearing and impact on the household sanitation and hygiene practices, the impact in reducing diseases and improving household hygiene and nutrition.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Inclusive WASH services like disability-inclusive collection points (for water facilities), and inclusion of people living with disabilities to WASH committees would be prioritized. Monclub will ensure that WASH facilities are physically and sex-disaggregated, clearly marked, lockable from the inside, and well lit. Monclub will ensure that gender is respected and that community hygiene promoters are mostly women from targeted locations. Protection staff will be hired throughout the project circle to ensure protection mainstreaming is effective.
