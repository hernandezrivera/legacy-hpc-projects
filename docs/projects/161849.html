Project Summary

This project proposes to provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to address critical needs of returnees and IDPs with emergency WASH services through rehabilitation and maintenance of 300 emergency latrines and showers, distribution of sanitary materials for menstrual hygiene management to young women and girls, and emergency hygiene promotion to reach 30,000 men, boys and girls of different age groups, returnees and Internally displaced people in IDP camps and communities of Monguno, Konduga and Gwoza LGAs within the period of 12 months.
The project shall maintain latrines and showers in target IDP camps, to consolidate partners effort to achieving the international standard of a maximum of 50 people per latrine and 100 people per shower, through rehabilitation and maintenance of 300 latrines and showers in IDP Camps of Monguno, Konduga and Gwoza LGAs during emergencies with the aim of improving access to WASH facilities and good hygiene practices for IDPs and returnees in Monguno, Konduga and Gwoza LGAs.
GOALPrime shall carryout hygiene promotion via radio jingles advertisement, leaflets and posters, Bugs Bunny Mobile Hygiene Promotion Tool throughout the period of the project in IDP camps of Monguno, Konduga and Gwoza LGAs, to improve hygiene practices of 20,000 conflict affected women, men, boys and girls. The Bugs Bunny Mobile Hygiene Promotion Tool is a strategy adopted by GOALPrime to use cartoon characters to carryout context-specific, gender and age appropriate hygiene promotion. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria, will establish community-based WASH committees that will take lead in ensuring proper maintenance of WASH facilities in the IDP camps and host communities of target LGAs.
Through this project, GOALPrime shall reach identified 3000 young women and adolescent girls with menstrual hygiene promotion, education and support them with appropriate sanitary materials for menstrual hygiene management.
GOALPrime will also engage members of the community and IDP camps to carry out monthly environmental sanitation. GOALPrime shall coordinate with BOSEPA to set up community sanitation structures to be led by the established community-based WASH committee for effective environmental sanitation to reach 7000 women and men, boys and girls residing in IDP camps and host communities in Monguno, Konduga and Gwoza LGAs.
GOALPrime shall train community volunteers and community-based WASH committees on sexual exploitations and abuse (PSEA) and Gender Based Violence risk mitigation measures and shall be intentional about sensitizing the community on SEA and its prevention and reporting mechanism. community-based WASH committees shall work closely with the ministry of Water resources to monitor and report SEA related concerns.
GOALPrime shall establish functioning complaints and feedback mechanisms to receive feedback from the community members and the beneficiaries on the impact of this project on the beneficiaries and their community. Complaints boxes shall be kept at strategic positions within the project environment for beneficiaries and the community to drop their complaints. GOALPrime shall also hold bi-weekly focus group discussions with target beneficiaries and other community stakeholders to receive feedback on the project impact. GOALPrime shall provide feedback of complaints to the beneficiaries and relevant community stakeholders on weekly basis. GOALPrime shall also receive direct feedback from beneficiaries through one on one interactions with the affected people. We shall also conduct focus group discussions with the affected people to receive direct feedback, and evaluate the impact of the project. Effective and timely actions shall be taken to address concerns received through our established feedback mechanism. We shall also provide complaints and feedback hotlines in the LGAs with the available communications network providers.

1 - Needs

The ongoing and protracted crises has created displacement and secondary displacement resulting to severe humanitarian WASH needs in Northeast Nigeria placing additional pressure on already fragile WASH services and facilities, especially in 43 LGAs across Borno and Adamawa states. With congested populations in displacement sites, notably in 15 LGAs in Borno State, water-borne diseases can spread rapidly. Spiraling cholera outbreaks have taken hold each year. In 2018, the cholera outbreak affected 18 LGAs with a total of 4,250 cases, in addition to 1.5 million people who are considered at risk and require cholera prevention interventions. According to 2019-2021 Humanitarian Needs Overview, Flooding, fierce winds and sand storms have caused substantial damage of WASH infrastructure with at least 20 per cent and 28 per cent of IDPs living in camps in Borno and Adamawa states respectively seriously affected. New arrivals from inaccessible areas, especially women and children, are also in urgent need of WASH support. This population include at least 100,000 children under five and 30,000 pregnant and lactating women across nine LGAs in Borno State and two LGAs in Yobe State. The WASH Sector has prioritized 3.2 million people, including 1.1 million IDPs, 400,000 in host communities, 900,000 returnees and 800,000 people in inaccessible areas. According to the Multi-sectoral site tracker bi-weekly service monitoring and gap analysis tool, October, 2019. There is a signiﬁcant gap in WASH where 4,008 latrines are currently non-functional hence need to be repaired, 2,655 latrines are ﬁlled and need to be dislodged while 1,986 showers need to be repaired.The limited number of latrines in camps is a key restriction to improving good hygiene behavior. GOALPrime organization Nigeria seek to address these critical needs of returnees, IDPs and Host communities with emergency WASH services through rehabilitation and maintenance of 300 emergency latrines and showers, to mitigate the risk of disease outbreak among the affected population, distribute hygiene kit to reach 30,000 returnees and Internally displaced people in IDP camps and host communities,provide context specific emergency hygiene promotion to sensitize IDPs, host Communities and returnees on the proper use of the hygiene kit, proper use of the latrines and showers, and carryout over-all hygiene promotion in Monguno, Konduga and Gwoza LGAs within the period of 12 months.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime’s accountability to the affected populations spans through the Program Cycle from the Rapid Specific Vulnerability Assessment through to design, through to implementation, and through to monitoring. The proposed project prioritizes constant creating of opportunities for review and feedback from beneficiaries, community leaders, partners present in location and donors. This integration of feedback and input from communities closely aligns with both the SPHERE Standards; Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Minimum Standard, and guidelines set forth by the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC). The pre-design assessment on the feasibility of this project in target locations collected information from local beneficiary populations, IDPs camp coordinators, host community Bulamas and a review of what other partners are doing in target LGAs via the WASH sector ReportHub partners update from target LGAs. As a result, the proposed project uses direct contributions in its proposed structure, scope, and proposed activities to allow a more community informed implementation process. Throughout the implementation of this project, GOALPrime shall engage participatory Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) and establish a platform which will allow participation and feedback from the community on project goals. GOALPrime shall conduct regular feedback surveys among the beneficiaries to gauge levels of satisfaction and solicit suggestions for project implementation and improvement.
