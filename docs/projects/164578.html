Project Summary

The project is targeting 33 camps to address the WASH needs of 110000 people within Idleb, the following interventions are planned:
• Supply safe and adequate water either through trucking This intervention will meet the daily needs (consumption and personal hygiene) of 110000 IDPs living between the 33 camps and 5 communities in Afrin, Azaz, Jarablus, Harim, and Salqin, Districts throughout the entire project duration. Each individual will receive 25 to 40 liters per day over the duration
• Water quality monitoring through testing at water sources, storage, and household to ensure water is according to standards Water testing: Random and regular water samples testing will be undertaken throughout the project period. They will be split as follows monthly
• Installation of water tanks: IYD will procure and install 500new galvanized water tanks between 33 sites to address a current shortage. Each tank is equipped with multiple taps to serve more than one person simultaneously.
• Construction of 40 new toilets in 33 sites. Latrines will be built in blocks of two, and each block will have a handwashing basin and water tank on the roof. Inside each latrine, there will be a water tap and a hose, and ceramic tiles to a height of 60 cm to make cleaning easier and make the latrines more durable. All latrines will be provided with solar lighting and have inside locks.
• Desludging the greywater Each month approximately greywater will be discharged from the septic tanks in all sites from the latrines and showers which connected to those septic tanks.
• Solid waste removal will be organized from each of the 33 camps and five communities in Afrin, Azaz, Jarablus, Harim, and Salqin, districts. All waste will be disposed into a communal landfill after the consent of the respective local council
• distribution of essential hygiene materials; IYD will distribute 10500 hygiene kits to each household in each of the 33 sites/settlements during a once-off distribution.
• Hygiene promotion and awareness. practices.
• Construction of 20 new latrines which is specifically designed for PwD.
• Rehabilitation work of five water systems will be cried out in the five targeted communities this work includes providing the required equipment of the water stations such as submersible pumps, horizontal pumps, control panels, and generator sets to make the water station fully functional, providing required work to prepare and extend the existing water network through providing the required pipes,
• 22 schools in the same locations of the targeted camps will get light rehabilitation of WASH facilities in the schools

5 - Implementation of activities

IYD’s response to the Syria crisis is led in Turkey and Syria by the Program Manager (PM) who is responsible for the operational and strategic management of the organization’s programmed in both countries. The Syria Project Officers are responsible for programs’ quality and provide support and technical advice for the implementation and management of the actions. The Quality and Program Development Coordinator leads IYD’s MEAL, CRM, program development and information management teams. The HR officer oversees the support functions of HR, logistics and administration. Finances are overseen by the Finance Manager (FM), assisted by Finance Officers and Assistants. 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

IYD has a security and safety team that conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils and civil society activists, as well as by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released by the international community. IYD has a good reputation as a nonprofit organization and works side by side with the community to empower and encourage the community ownership of facilities. Accordingly. For emergency relief projects, when a security situation arises that threatens the safety of staff, the staff is redirected to a nearby, safer location until the area is safe enough to conduct the aid or distribution project. For non-relief projects, an assessment of the security situation is conducted in order to determine the location of the project. While very few locations in Syria are completely safe, IYD takes time to advise its staff and partners of ways to mitigate against security threats – such as avoiding specific routes, avoiding certain groups, or selecting locations or building types that are lesser targets.IYD will coordinate together in the implementing process and both of the teams will work together to ensure the safety and security of the team in the field. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

the border between Turkey and Syria closes sometimes which might affect the delivery of some required items into the proposed locations especially the capital equipment such as water pumps and electric generator sets and to mitigate this access restriction, IYD has increased local procurement of project supplies inside Syria, recruitment of staff from inside Syria further access controls on sending supplies to the project area would make it extremely difficult to implement the project activities. Therefore, this proposal presumes some kind of continued access to the project area or the availabil of supplies and equipment in the local market. Sudden clashes or air attacks which probably happen during the project period. To mitigate these risks, IYD staff have received mine awareness training which will be repeated on a regular basis during the project period. IYD cross border security group provides a forum for IYD staff at different levels in the organization structure to analyze security risks and to update the security management plan. Security groups are used for tracking of staff movements within Syria and for dissemination of information about security incidents to all staff.This proposal presumes that the local authorities in the targeted area will continue to allow NGOs to implement projects according to the humanitarian principles without undue interference in the selection of beneficiaries and staff.IYD will not pay fees or taxes to armed parties to the conflict inside Syria or to ISIL or other sanctioned groups as per the Joint Operating Principles.
