Project Summary

Overall objective: is to contribute to resilient and equitable agricultural growth from which
olive small scale women and men producers will benefit in the targeted area. The specific
objective is to increase the well-being and income of women and men engaged in small-
scale agriculture in vulnerable communities through improved agricultural and access to
land and market. The project support 4231 person (2158 M, 2073 F) of the communities
and families to build their capacity to absorb shocks, through the road rehabilitation and
retaining walls, access to good quality agriculture extensions, these are the starting points
to for appropriate engagement in resilience. The project will operate at two levels: one, at
the sub-sector and community level, to facilitate the overall access to the agricultural land,
and two, at the farm level to address the opportunities and constraints in the targeted
areas and in the olive trees mainly. These two inter-connected layers will be approached
from the small producers’ perspective, with clear benefits to women farmers. Outcome 1:
Small farming system is more productive and pro table, with more access to agricultural
land. The project will contribute to the country S.O. and to the FFS objective by increasing
Palestinian resilience to the Israeli occupation practices as well as to natural crises,
including basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through
the provision of essential services, in accordance with the rights of protected persons
under IHL. The project will enhance farmers’ accessibility to their agricultural lands where
no one did so. The targeted farmers are highly vulnerable and lack access to basic
services, including access to land, market, agricultural extension, and finance. AAA will
facilitate these essential services in close coordination with the ministry of agriculture and its branch in the district, to ensure the right mitigation's steps are in place to
decrease threats to life, food insecurity, and unstable income. More precisely, this will
contribute to: 1- Support farmers’ resilience equity in the targeted locations against the
climatic changes and the occupation practices on their land's productivity.2- Improve
farmers' livelihood sources in the targeted villages.3- Prevent farms and trees from wild
pigs, 4- Reward returns for women and men small farmers.

Needs

AAA presence in the proposed targeted area is permanent, hence, our team are in the field all the time, and keep monitor the situation on the ground in close coordination with the MoA teams and other local authorities including the musicality. AAA have done a comprehensive needs assessment in Salfait area in August 2018, the needs assessment confirmed that farmers are facing various levels of drops in agricultural productivity, and profitability, furthermore, farmers in this area are facing other challenges like access restrictions to their lands and waters, Israeli illegal settler’s violence against farmers and their farms, limited services like extension, damaged roads, wild animals like pigs, and continues flash floods. Road rehabilitation has proven to be an effective means of reducing the time and expense needed to transport inputs and products between the farms and the markets, thereby enhancing market readiness. This will be complemented by facilitating the access of farmers in targeted sites to relevant technical assistance on promotion and adoption of water conservation practices, in order to advance more efficient water management, water efficiency practices and technologies, AAA proposes to work on the most badly eroded 10 km of agricultural roads. Access to farm and market, and basic services are the main issues that farmers are currently facing. Which jeopardize farmers from obtaining the optimal use of their main source of income that contributes significantly to their families’ food security. In Salfit area, over 38,500 donums are planted with horticultural crops, with almost 98% being Olive trees. The assessment showed that around 25% of these lands need light rehabilitation and maintain/build damaged retaining walls to prevent soil erosion, increase water holding capacity, and destruction of olive trees’ productivity. These walls need to be high to the level that prevents wild animal enter the land and damage the trees and in-between plantation like vegetables to make sure that farms will be fully protected. Over than 90% of the people at the targeted locations work in agriculture as the main source of livelihoods. The assessment reported sharp increase in the unemployment rate in those villages mainly among farmers and especially young people and women. It is noticed lately that the Israeli’s attacks have increased on farmers’ land, that includes expansion of master plans for 10 Israeli settlements in Yaouf, Iskaka, others, this expansion is on the Palestinians fertile agricultural land, it is also reported several times that settlers bring wild pigs and release them in the area to attack farmers and their land, which become an extra risk for farmers mainly women when attending their lands. Illegal settlement expansion leads to more fertile land grabbing and trees uprooting, moreover, this lead to closing ve agriculture roads in the targeted area and prevent farmers from accessing their lands. With the aim that farmers will not attend their land and thus become easy to be taken by the Israelis. Additionally, man-made damage to agricultural land in the targeted areas, like settlements wastewater flows into the agriculture land in Salfit and nearby villages, wild pigs, and dogs, settlers shepherd that has extremely harmed existing farming. Despite the bad situation in the targeted areas, limited responses actions to the need were taking place, however, the need is still great in the proposed targeted areas and need immediate action. AAA will implement this project in close coordination with the MoA and other actors in the area to avoid any duplication and overlapping. The assessment which included focus group discussion, simi structure interviews, and survey, revealed that women are more vulnerable to the limited access to land and low income, it is worth to note that over than 65% of the workload in agriculture is made by women, while women presence in the is about 15% only.

Activities

Prior to the project implementation, the organization will strictly coordinate the final definition and selection of localities and beneficiaries with line ministries offices, and with any other organization working in the same area and sector of activities. AAA will be targeting farmers in all the communities mentioned with main activities including (rehabilitation of agricultural roads, rehabilitation of agricultural lands through maintaining/build retaining walls). Through agricultural roads and lands rehabilitation includes retaining walls has proven to be an effective means of reducing the time and expense needed to transport inputs and products between the farms and the markets, thereby enhancing market readiness. Capitalizing upon its technical expertise, AAA will manage the rehabilitation of 10 km of agricultural roads, facilitating the access of farming communities to 2500 dunums, directly benefiting 1778 farmers.Road rehabilitation generally consist of widening and connecting of existing narrow roads and is based on following labor intensive activities, generally implemented through cash for work schemes, including cutting and removal of rocks or earth; adding of base course layer; construction of culvert in certain places for water drainage; construction of stony retaining walls for road protection; and construction of longitudinal drainage trench. The principle of obligatory community contribution will be applied and Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) will be signed with local councils or community structures, ensuring sustainability through maintenance of the roads. Farmers will perform farming practices more effectively and easily and secure, this will increase productivity and quality of the produce and easy access to marketing of high quality of agricultural products. Furthermore, through the retaining walls, the agricultural soil will be protected from erosion as well as increase the soil capacity to retain enough moisture for the dry summer season, more importantly, trees and human including women and children will be safer from wild animals’ attacks. AAA in coordination with local CBOs, related ministries, and village councils, will formulate a steering committee in each community, which will create clear beneficiary selection criteria with clear marks that will help in the selection of the poorest farmers. Activity:1: Rehabilitation of 9.75 km of agricultural roads that serve around 2438 dunums of agricultural lands owned by over 1778 people in total. The activity will provide 44 workers with over 400 working days. Output 1: Enhanced access to 2438 dunums of agricultural lands as a result to the rehabilitated 9.75 km of agricultural roads. 2: Installation of 15000 m2 of retaining walls that serve around 600 dunums of agricultural lands owned by 720 vulnerable people. The activity will provide 317 workers with over 3750 working days. Output 2: Increased productivity and quality of 600 dunums of agricultural land through rebuilding 15000 m2 of retaining walls.

Indicators

1) It is estimated that each 1 km of roads serves 250 dunums. The expected objective verifiable indicators and target from the project are: • At least 4231 people are directly benefited from the project activates, and more than 3675 persons indirectly benefited from the project. • At least 3038 dunums of agricultural lands became protected, accessible and more productive. • At least 4189 working days for 361 unemployed workers created. The monitoring & evaluation will be done as follow: an M&I Action Plan will be developed which will include (i) the identified and appropriate indicators to monitor the outputs and results of the various sub-projects and their impact; (ii) establish a baseline for key indicators; (iii) set change targets for each indicator that expected to result from each sub-project activity; and (iv) set out a detailed plan for collecting and analyzing data to monitor and measure performance; more details; • The M&E according to AAA financial system and with full participation of the local committee. • The monitoring process is designed to continuously assess the appropriateness of the project interventions, propose corrective measures and to support learning and evaluation. • There will be continues of a regular field visit to ensure that all things are ongoing according to the AAA and the project specifications and standards. • Forming technical committee from the project coordinator, local committee and representative of MoA to confirm implemented the activates. • A final evaluation will be conducted at the end of the project. The criteria should include technical standards, household status, and income, 15% extra marks will be given to each household headed by women, number of family members, disability members in the family, chronic diseases in the family, having a university student in the family, the benefited land space, the type of crop, where we will target crops with higher viable economically, and land location if its near settlements and bypass roads, military camps... etc. AAA. will discuss the applications with the participation of the selected committee to each location with the implementation of field visits to verify the information written in the applications specifically (the technical standards, the economic situation) AAA will make sure to enhance women role through participation in the local committees and will give priority to women-headed households and youth. Rehabilitation roads will facilitate farmers mainly women access to cultivated lands, in addition, to transfer the product or production materials to the cultivated land. The agricultural road will ease the access to tools and machines to the agriculture land, which decreases women workload, noting that 65% of the agricultural work is on the shoulders of women (Oxfam’s olive value chain report). AAA will make sure to women representative on the selection committee.
