Project Summary

Generally speaking, this grant will be in line with health cluster response plan and strategic objectives matching with the critical health needs on the ground. This project will support the required services for one of the most vulnerable group who are elderly including female and male through supporting four health facilities.
The first health facility is the internal medicine hospital (G0703014). The second one is Barish primary health care center.
This project will serve the areas populated with IDPs and affected population and covering variety of urgencies and dire health needs giving extra attention for those with disabilities.
By time being: Idleb city has 200340 population and Harem sub district has 70000 population.
As such, this project will support the urgent health services at two levels. The first one is through specialized internal medicine hospital which is located in Idleb city. The second one is through adding RH, NCD and CD services to Barisha PHC located in Harem district, Barisha community.
Practically speaking, the internal medicine hospital (G0700033) is serving more than 250 thousand population in Idleb city and surrounded. It is the only specialized internal medicine hospital in NWS. It is providing 8500 medical consultations per month. This vital hospital is providing the advanced internal medicine services included but not limited to: gastroenterology, nephrology, neurology, endocrinology, pulmonary, cardiology, ICU, laboratory, radiology and emergency internal medicine services along with outpatient care services serving 200340
The second health facility is Abi Ther (G0700034)PHC which is located in the porest nighbrhood in Idleb city. And serving more than 36000 population. The monthly cosultations are 4000 per month more than 70% of them are boys and girls and women.
The third health facility is Mechmchan ( G0704014) PHC which is located in difficult to reach areas in Jisr Al shogour district. Western Marj Alkhader community. This vital PHC is serving more than 9000 population. It is providing 3500 consultations per month.
Final health facility called Barisha PHC, it is located in Area heavily populated IDPs.
Barisha PHC provides (G0703043) the outpatient services serving 20000 population

5 - Implementation of activities

SIMRO will support the only specialized internal medicine hospital in NWS to provide the advanced internal medicine services including the outpatients services, emergency services, gastroenterology, nephrology, neurology, endocrinology, pulmonary, cardiology, ICU, laboratory, radiology services. These activities will be implemented from the beginning of the April. On the other hand the project will support the primary health care center level three in Barisha located in Idleb governorate, Qurqaneea Sub-district. This PHC is providing the agreed primary health care services in line with the essential health services package agreed by WHO and cluster partners. SIMRO will support three critical primary health care centers located in areas heavily populated with IDPs.These PHC are as follow: Abi Ther PHC which is located in idleb city.The second one is located in Harem district, Barisha community.The third one is located in Jisr Al Shogour district, Barisha community. These PHCs will provide the high quality primary health care services for needy boys, girls, women, and men. SIMRO will procure the required Pharma and medical supplies for those crucial health facilities to ensure that all the agreed services will be provided in integral way. SIMRO will coordinate with Sarmada MCH to refer the complicated cases from Barisha to this hospital.SIMRO will coordinate with Avicenna MCH to send the complicated cases from Abe Ther PHC.Finall SIMRO will coordinate with Local health partner to receive the complicated internal medicine cases. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

As a matter of fact, the first step we have carefully analyzed was to determine the various types of potential risks that may arise from the implementation of the project’s activities. To start with the security aspects, the situation on the ground along with the overlapping dynamics make it a must for us to take safety always on top of our priorities. So that, it is important to anticipate the potential risks in order to be able to tackle them and eventually to mitigate or avoid them from the very beginning. Practically speaking, the golden role throughout our entire work is that once there is potential offensive and or airstrikes we immediately suspend the work and evacuate the staff simultaneously such incident will immediately be reported in due process to OCHA and cluster. Furthermore, if the hostilities either carry on or get closer to our working areas, we instantly coordinate with cluster and partners in order to relocate our project filling other crucial gap on the ground. Plan B: we will coordinate with local health partner on the ground to relocate this health facility to more safe and secure place in areas with IDPs waves. The operational risk comes in second as, there are many instances where the fuel prices increase insanely affecting the project implementation. Plan B: As for our response to mitigate these huge obstacles, fuel will be procured from the very beginning at a fix price then it shall be safely stored in our warehouses. Additionally, the financial aspects has always been a major problematic issue in the conducting of the activities throughout the entire phases of the project. For example, sometimes due to uncontrollable conditions the disbursements might be delayed. This will hugely affect the entire chain of the activities.Plan B: Therefore, we have sought from the very beginning to secure alternative payments from SIMRO side to cover the potential shortages in the financing.
