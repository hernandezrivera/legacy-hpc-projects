Project Summary

The proposed project aims to improve food security and the livelihood of drought affected vulnerable population in Lasqoray district, Sanaag region. The project aims at improving access to nutritious and diverse food and as well improve the resilience of the target population through the implementation of complementary FSL projects suited to address effectively the general food insecurity and context specific challenges undermining livelihood activities of the community. This is one year period project covering objective one, two and three of the food security cluster as it involves improving households’ immediate access to food through provision of conditional and unconditional assistance, protecting and restoring livelihoods, related food and income sources, through provision of seasonally appropriate livelihood inputs and technical support in rural and peri-urban settings.
The first phase of the project (Jan-April) targets 475 households (2,850 beneficiaries) most affected HHs through unconditional cash transfer. This MPC will enable the vulnerable target HHs to have access to nutritious food and other basic needs in their local markets. The cash gives the affected population the power to prioritize their needs and as well purchase the most pressing food commodities. ADEC will use inclusive community based targeting approach with the support of village elders and local authority to ensure inclusivity and transparency in all the activities. The selection criteria used will include female headed pastoral drop out, HHs with malnourished children under the age of five, disable and elderly people with no income. These beneficiaries will receive unconditional cash transfer for 3 month with a monthly transfer value of $110 based on Somalia Cash and Market Quarterly Dashboard (June-June 2019)
On the other hand, ADEC will select 275 households (1,650 beneficiaries) for conditional assistance. These beneficiaries will engage in the rehabilitation and protection community assets like water catchment, feeder roads, canal rehabilitation and market rehabilitation. Able bodied members of the target HHs will carry out physical and manual activities. The rehabilitation of the community owned assets help the community to have resilience against possible shocks. In addition to that, ADEC will provide fodder to 3000 livestock (300 HHs) comprised of goats and sheep whereby every household among the target HHs will get fodder allocation for 10 heads of goat/sheep. The livestock input (fodder) will be given to pastoral households who cannot afford to purchase fodder from the local market for their livestock. This is expected to have impact on the livestock productivity i.e. milk production during the lean season hence strengthens household’s resilience against food security shocks. The CFW participants will be mainly semi-skilled and works for 15days/month at the rate of $5 per day.
ADEC ensures beneficiaries of its project to be vulnerable groups including women headed pastoral drop out household, pregnant and lactating women, Malnourished infants and young children under 5, disable and elderly with no external support. In addition, thorough consideration is given to cross-cutting themes i.e. gender, inclusion of disable people, community consultation etc.

Project Activities

Activity1: Unconditional Cash TransferActivity 1.1: Identification of 475 most vulnerable households in Lasqoray with a priority given to those with Pregnant/Lactating Women (PLW) and children <5 years - Verification of beneficiaries, sensitization on project modalities. The initial community mobilization exercise will include creation of awareness on aims and objectives of the project and those targeted to receive the support being provided. The criteria will include gender disaggregated needs targeting. Beneficiaries will be registered and profiled disaggregated by sex and age as well as asset ownership. Selection will prioritize most food insecure, poor women or men headed households, aged and physically challenged, IDPs and returnees. The action will endeavor to attain 70% of the beneficiaries’ households to be female headed. The selection will be done with full participation of village leaders and other stakeholders. During the registration, ADEC ensures all beneficiaries have mobile account matching with their name where they can easily receive their money without travelling. Activity 1.2: Identify and signing of framework agreements with Local Service Provider (Xawaala) to transfer the cash to the beneficiaries. A pre-qualification exercise will be conducted for all potential service providers that have linkage with the targeted locations. Activity 1.3: Provide 475 vulnerable households in humanitarian crisis with cash through cash transfer system for 3 months. They will received MPC for 3 months whereby they can easily access food and other basic needs from their local market. The current recommended transfer value for multipurpose cash is $110 for Sanaag region, therefore each target household will received $110 per month. The seasonal calendar will dictate the period three months that the food vouchers will be distributed to avoid impacting negatively on productive activities and in order to promote self-dependency. A post distribution monitoring will be conducted after every cycle of voucher distribution.ACTIVITY 2: Conditional Cash Transfer (CFW)Activity 2.1: Identification and assessment of productive infrastructure to be rehabilitated. The identification will start with community leaders and community committee providing information on the infrastructure they prefer to be rehabilitated. Activity 2.2: Engage 275 HH in rehabilitation of productive infrastructure over a period of 6 months through CFW approach: Beneficiaries will work 15 days/month for 6 months, earning USD 75 per month. The timing for the CFW will be determined by the seasonal calendar to avoid disruption of livelihood activities. Women headed households will be equally enrolled in this activity as long as the work does not conflict with other household priorities. Activity 2.3: Conduct post activity survey after cash distribution and infrastructure rehabilitation: ADEC will collect information to assess the added value the rehabilitated infrastructures have brought to the supported community. Activity 2.4: Signing agreements with Local Service Provider (Xawaala) to transfer the cash to the beneficiaries. Activity 2.5: Cash distribution: Activity 2.6: Cash receipt verification: After the distribution of the cash ADEC field staffs will verify the receipt of the cash and its quantity while cross-checking with the list from the service provider data base. Activity 2.7: Post distribution MonitoringACTIVITY 3: Provision of livestock inputsActivity 3.1: Selection and registration of 300 vulnerable pastoral households. The identification will be done through community elders, community committee and local authority to ensure the most vulnerable who are not in a position to buy fodder for their livestock. Vulnerable Female headed pastoral households will be given priority.Activity 3.2: Procurement of the fodder: ADEC will procure 300 units fodder and deliver at beneficiaries’ location. Activity 3.3: Distribution of the fodder.
