Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to continuity of essential life-saving health services tonsure equitable access of people in need and improving quality of services through supporting convergent primary health care activities through activation of the pending pre-existed health facilities. and emphasis focused on five key vulnerable groups: displaced persons, children under five years (U5), women of reproductive age WRA (15-49), older persons (60+) and people with disabilities affected by the crisis in northern Syria. Amal Organization will continue to contribute to the improvement of the health status of pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and children under 5 years by strengthening lifesaving program through integrated services in the proposed PHC Centre. The project consists of a comprehensive primary health care center providing services though policlinic and specialty clinic as the following:1-Genecology and reproductive health clinic, through this clinical department there will be a gynecologist and midwife who will provide reproductive health care including; pre-natal care, post-natal care, prevention and appropriate treatment of infertility, treatment of reproductive tract infections, and family planning counselling. 2- provide the service of early detection of cancer through (Mammogram, Ultrasound, and the chemical Lab)3-Pediatrics and Nutrition clinic4-General and internal clinic 5-Endocrinology clinic 6- ENT clinic 7- Ophthalmology clinic 8-NCD package services 9- Psychological Support services 9- providing essential and therapeutic medicines beside to laboratory services. Amal will coordinate with WHO cluster and local health partner (Health directorates and other Health actor) beside to other stakeholders (local authority and beneficiaries through planning implementation and evaluation of this project so as to insure better health services responses and strengthen the health network at the targeted area. Moreover, dealing with covid-19 cases in terms of Coordination, planning, and monitoring, also through laboratories and diagnosis, on the other hand Infection prevention and control, protection of the health workforce, and therapeutics and coordination of vaccination campaign. Amal will coordinate with WHO cluster and SRH technical group so as to designing the project according to who criteria in terms of outputs activities and indicators. Moreover, Amal will coordinate with nutrition sector related of assessment and management of management of moderate acute malnutrition beside to management of severe acute malnutrition. Amal will continue cooperation with GBV sub cluster and MPHSS technical work group in order to coordinate of psychosocial support PSS activities designated at the beginning of the project. Amal is committed to robust MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning) and set the Indicators Performance Tracking Table (IPTT) which will be developed and tracked using the internal M&E Workplan developed at project start-up). The main database to insert and track the beneficiaries will be designed as well on Access Microsoft with ensuring clear gender markers, sex, age, disability, and displacement disaggregation will be considered). Amal will identify the timelines for data collection support information follow and analysis of progress against targets. MEAL personnel will conduct regular spot visits to the operation area on weekly basis. Also, during the monitoring field visits, MEAL staff will use a standard center checklist to be filled on monthly basis to identify any challenge/gap in the center. Moreover, beneficiary satisfaction and accountability surveys will be collected and the findings will be shared on a monthly basis. More qualitative data will be collected by conducting Focused Group Discussions with the community. MEAL team will share a follow-up report with the program on the same day of the visit to follow up with the monitoring observation. Also will observe COVID 19 precautions

4 - Needs

Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply and irrigation systems. According to HNO 2021, the health system remains heavily disrupted. As the region suffers from a lack of medical centers and needs special medicines and quality medical equipment where whole community and patients in particular, suffer from:1- The consequences of covid_19, which made patients unable to travel easily in the region, unable to have treatment in Turkey or elsewhere because of the strict measures taken to limit the pandemic and closing of the borders 2- Lack of pharmaceutical supplies and medical equipment devices for diseases in general and incurable diseases, as well as the scarcity of human specialized resources in the region 3-Lack of medical centers capable of providing quality medical services and the limited availability of quality medicines, which forces the patients to travel long distances (if they can) and the need for huge sums of money for treatment. To this end, Amal organization will respond to the needs of PHC center in Akhtarin the city in which at least 90,000 most affected persons will benefit. Amal coordinated with the health cluster to support one PHC center.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risk (Health): There is a risk of attacks from armed groups during the implementation of project’s activities may lead to suspending health services. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: 1- Amal will choose place facilities away from possible armed forces 2-Amal will consult the community and potential beneficiaries about their preferences 3- coordinating with appropriate policing side. Protection risk (Health): There is a risk of the Infrastructures of the facility is not adapted for vulnerable groups, so individuals and groups can't access and use them in safety and dignity. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: 1- Amal will make infrastructure adaptations such as ramps and railings to health facilities and latrines. Protection risk (Health): increased risk of covid-19 infection and transmission between health work personnel and beneficiaries, so may lead to high incidence of locally acquired, widely dispersed cases and imply high risk of infection for the general population. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: Ventilation of facility naturally, Physical distancing: Introduce measures to keep a safe physical distance, Hand hygiene promoting, Cleaning and disinfection of environmental surfaces on daily basis and using Personal protective equipment (PPE).


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Protection risk (Health)There is a risk of absence of guidelines and mechanisms for monitoring and reporting instances of abuse and exploitation, so the beneficiaries maybe more susceptible for sexual exploitation. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: Amal will ensure all staff sign and adhere to a code of conduct that includes a “whistle blower” policy- Establish an accessible and well understood mechanism for complaints- Ensure staff understand and sign the code of conduct - do meetings on the code of conduct to remind everyone of their obligations. Protection risk (Health): There is a risk of community tensions between displaced and host community. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: Amal will maintain equitable access to healthcare which lead to no tension or conflict within the community and with other surrounding communities-In camp settings, will consider also providing services to the local/host community. Protection risk (Health)There is arisk of absence of mechanisms for suggestions and complaints, or well understood. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigations measures: Amal will make sure that there are other possibilities for submitting complaints that do not require the beneficiary exposing themselves to project staff- respond to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place-put complaints mechanisms in line with with Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abusive systems.
