Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in Lakes State. WAV Gender Based Violence project plan will directly target 10000 vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Rumbek East county with a total of 5000 females, 1000 males,1000 boys and 3000 girls 5% of the target population will include people with disability, female headed households, pregnant and lactating women and the elderly .The project aims at providing quality GBV services to GBV survivors and reduce suffering of women, girls, men and boys by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs and promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks.
The prioritized objectives to achieve the overall goal will include:
Cluster Objective 1: Ensure the provision of critical protection related assistance and specialized services through an integrated approach to address the priority needs of targeted people among vulnerable women, men, girls and boys in hard-to-reach and priority geographical areas – linked to intersectoral SO1 and SO2
Cluster Objective 2: Prevent and mitigate protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience - linked to intersectoral SO2
The main activities to be carried out to realize these objectives and achieve the overall goal will include :
1. Provision of survivor-centered psychosocial support focused on healing, empowerment and recovery
2. Provision of appropriate GBV case management services including coordinated care and support
3. Establish and / or strengthen GBV referral systems and linking GBV survivors to available response services and support
4. Implement Women and Girls Friendly Space (WGFS) programming to provide services, information and activities that promote healing, well-being and empowerment
5. Implement community engagement and awareness raising activities on GBV risk factors, mitigation measures and on how survivors can access support and services in line with the “do no harm” principle.

1 - Needs

Lakes State has experienced a great deal of conflict in recent years that has caused degenerated and rampant instability issues. Due to the constant upheaval, the capacity of the local social and economic infrastructure is one of the lowest in the country. Furthermore Rumbek East county has been subjected to numerous displacements with IDPs being integrated into the host community .The displacements are caused by floods ,conflicts of inter and intra-communal violence ,revenge killing, farmers pastoralists conflicts, land disputes, clan fighting and many more making women, children, elderly and PSNs victims of circumstances. The Inter-Cluster Working Group (ICWG) conducted an Initial Rapid Needs Assessment (IRNA) in Rumbek East from 14th -17th December 2020 to assess the humanitarian situation of the flood and intra tribal conflict affected populations. The team consisted of UN Agencies, INGO and NNGO, RRC, Government officials, with participants from various humanitarian organizations, representing all clusters (Education, FSL, Health, NFI&ES, Nutrition, Protection, and WASH).The County of Rumbek East is located in Lakes state and inhibited by more than 134120 people, majority of them are Dinka. Key informant interviews were conducted to get in-depth technical knowledge on the onset of flooding through its diverse impacts and local mitigation measures. The number of Households affected by the floods were estimated 4244 HHs, 560 HHs in Aduel, 640 HHs in Gok Akon, 320 HHs in cueicok, 165 HHs in Pacong, 576 HHs in Paloc and 674 in Ayen Mayar in Rumbek East. The immediate impacts of flooding included damage to property, several houses and latrines have collapsed in the villages posing danger of open defecation in bushes which increases risk of GVB to women and girls, many people are displaced from their homes and have moved to high grounds and are sleeping outside exposing them to GBV risks . Damage of bore holes has caused congestion at the few functioning bore holes which are quite far exposing women and girls to sexual violence due to competition for water resource. The 4 payams of Pacong,Paloc,Aduel and Cuiecok lack GBV service providers and trained health service providers there is also unavailability of PEP kit in the health facilities in all the locations visited however due to lack of awareness on GBV services women and girls were reluctant to open up on GBV issues due to fear of criticism from community members and families for discussing issues perceived as sensitive . Secondly the recent inter clan conflict among the Akot community and Aluakluak due to cattle raiding displaced over 456 people. Sexual violence and other forms of GBV against women and girls are becoming more wide spread in Rumbek East due to movement of the populations because of conflicts. Through GBV programming and peace building and governance projects in Rumbek East WAV has noticed increasing and targeted attack with existing and growing levels of sexual violence perpetrated. In Rumbek East county adolescent girls and women are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information and experience growing levels of sexual violence, abductions ,sexual harassment ,early and forced marriages, sexual exploitation and abuse. Through this plan at least 70% targeted beneficiaries will be supported through PSS and life skills. Responding to the new crisis requires a targeted emergency response outside the current WAV program sites .WAV will utilize existing internal capacity to respond quickly and effectively to the emerging crisis and will provide emergency GBV services in Rumbek East county.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

Addressing sexual exploitation and abuse in Rumbek East County requires robust measures to promote and protect women’s and girl’s rights, response mechanisms when those rights are breached, the services and programs to meet the needs of women and girls who have suffered violence are in place. WAV will ensure the component on PSEA is part of the Meetings and training activities to ensure the information is disseminated to the community also at the women and girls friendly spaces they will be IEC materials on PSEA translated in local language so that women and girls can be able to read and understand.WAV also has a PSEA policy and the staffs have been trained this will be utilized to manage the conduct of WAV staff when interacting with beneficiaries in the field.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

Prior to this application participatory assessment through focused group discussion (FGD) and key informant interviews (KII) was conducted in Rumbek East with 30 participants involved drawn from different ages ,gender and diversities to ensure there is commitment in placing accountability to people affected by disaster and conflict at the core of its emergency policy and practice, from preparedness and the onset of an emergency throughout the phases of the programme cycle. The current GBV concerns being faced by the vulnerable marginalized groups (women headed HHS ,people living with disability, elderly women, pregnant and lactating women) patterns and and GBV risk factors have been identified through beneficiary feedback mechanism. WAV will continue to ensure that this project utilizes a people-centered approach, sensitive to the dignity of all human beings, the varying needs of different segments within the community, and the importance of ensuring that women, men, girls and boys can equally access and benefit from GBV services provided. During the selection of the beneficiaries there will be community consultative meetings to identify the vulnerable beneficiaries to be considered for support with GBV services.WAV will follow participatory assessments to identify, verify and profile targeted beneficiaries. The criteria will prioritize vulnerable people, including IDPs, host communities and female headed HHs.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

The project life cycle will commence with the project inception where participatory assessment will be conducted in Rumbek East County to assess the current GBV concerns being faced by the vulnerable marginalized groups patterns and the risk factors for violence and abuse. WAV will strengthen the existing community-based protection structures and women groups in Rumbek East county to support programming and activity implementation through trainings to enhance long term sustainability. The intervention is designed to provide lifesaving GBV support to vulnerable IDPs and host communities. In order to strengthen clinical management services .WAV will work closely with health care workers at the PHCC to provide CCSAS to expand the health care service contribution to GBV survivors. To strengthen the existing GBV referral pathway mechanisms survivor centered approaches, GBV basic concepts and the key principles in dealing with the GBV survivor will be engraved within the case management interventions. Regular consultations will be provided within the women and girls friendly spaces (WGFS) with women and girls meeting quarterly to discuss key interventions implemented within the period, achievements, challenges, recommendations ,gaps and opportunities within the given intervention to ensure responses are informed by their voices, recommendations and experiences. WAV has existing tools to capture beneficiary feedback on key programming interventions to ensure quality service provision is adapted for this project. WAV will continue to work closely with other partners to harmonize this tools and strengthen responses to ensure integrated programming. The key stakeholders will inform programming and contribute significantly to the responses and adhere to “DO NO HARM”.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

WAV feedback mechanism will be used for this project to guide in service provision to the beneficiaries .A complaint mechanism desk will be set up for information collection at the women and girls friendly space where different opinions will be collected and solutions generated.
