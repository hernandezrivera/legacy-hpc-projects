Project Summary


This project aims to increase the food security and enhancing the livelihoods of 1500 HHs of IDPs and host communities in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb Governorate through following activities:
1- wheat value chain activities:
ONSUR will provide agricultural inputs for 10 donum of wheat for 600 HHS of crop farmers in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb according to specific selection criteria (The eligibility and vulnerability criteria).
The scheme is to provide 250 kg of Wheat seeds, 25 kg of seeds/dunum, 200 Superphosphate fertilizer,200 kg Azote Fertilizer (Urea 46%), three types of pesticides (insecticide, fungicide, and herbicide) covering 10 donum for each HH, Furthermore, one fuel cash voucher (75 USD) for supplementary irrigation and one harvest cash voucher (50 USD) accompanied by related extension services. Additionally, ONSUR field team (ONSUR will hire two agronomist) to follow the growth and life cycle of the wheat crop, best agricultural practice and giving tips and recommendations to farmers about diseases and ways to prevent them through regularly filed visiting for wheat farmers in their fields.
ONSUR will support wheat marketing by purchasing 1 ton of wheat from each supported farmers ( in total, 600 ton of wheat crop)
ONSUR will contract with mill to grind 450 ton of wheat the purchased wheat.
Also, ONSUR will contract with bakeries to produce daily bread bundles.
- ONSUR will distribute free bread bundles to IDPs inside camps.
- ONSUR will contract with sifter to sift and sterilize the rest of purchased wheat seed (150 ton ) to produce135 ton of clean-good seeds to distributed for other 600 wheat farmers in same location in next season.
2-Support olives value chain activities:
- ONSUR will distribute agricultural olive inputs in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb for 600 of olives farmers in Idleb according to specific selection criteria (The eligibility and vulnerability criteria) (4 dunums/farmer) includes ( manure of animals for natural fertilizer, pesticide vouchers (insecticide, and fungicide), in addition to 2 fuel vouchers for supplementary irrigation.
Additionally, ONSUR will contract with seasonal workers from IDPs by cash for work activities to harvest and prune olives for 600 HHs.
ONSUR will contract with presses in project area to extract the oil of olive of HHs.
ONSUR will purchase 2 cans (32 kg of olive oil) from each supported farmer
3- support vegetables value chain farmers:
The project will target 300 vulnerable Vegetables farmers in Western rural of Aleppo and Idleb governorate according to specific selection criteria (The eligibility and vulnerability criteria).
ONSUR will provide agricultural inputs (seedling, fertilizer, and pesticide) vouchers in right time for 300 HHs for cultivation 1 dunum Vegetables. Furthermore, ONSUR will hire two agronomist to follow-up the stages of the Vegetables production cycle in beneficiaries’ field
ONSUR will buy 500 Kg of Vegetables crops from targeted farmers to support their marketing .
Also, ONSUR will create Small women vegetables processing group:
30 women beneficiaries will be selected based on the vulnerability criteria by beneficiaries scoring system.
- Each group will receive a set of necessary tools and 10 ton of Vegetables (that will be purchased from targeted farmers)for each women group, to encourage them to start their own project.
-Each women group will be trained for Vegetables Processing, Packaging, branding and marketing.

3 - Needs

Syria faces a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies. According to (HNO, 2023) 12.1M people are estimated to be food insecure. Over 2M people live in informal settlements and planned camps, a 5% increase since 2021. Moreover, 85% of households are unable to meet basic food needs in 2022. • Based on the 2022 Food Security Assessment (FSA) and 2022 Food Security and Livelihoods Assessment (FSLA) findings, WFP’s mobile vulnerability analysis and mapping (mVAM) and food prices data, an estimated 13.5 million people need some form of food and agriculture-based livelihoods assistance. • It is important to note that both agriculture and non-agriculture-based livelihoods have been affected by the prevailing economic challenges. However, the constraints in agriculture-based livelihoods will have negative consequences on both incomes for households and national food production. Taking these challenges into consideration, this project seeks to provide agriculture-based livelihoods opportunities for the extremely vulnerable host communities and IDPs living in NWS.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risks will be analyzed throughout the project life-cycle. According to theproject activities, the following protection risks and mitigating measures are presented:1)- Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access toservices Mitigating measures: Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available . Provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA. 2)- Risk of doing harm orperpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys Mitigating measures: 1. Ensuretraining packages are in line child protection minimum standards 2. Establish consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ONSUR strongly considers gender aspects at every step of its project design andimplementation. A gender analysis was included in this project’s needs assessment. Atevery stage of the program, men and women will be included engaged, withconsideration to the “do no harm approach”, especially those that are infl uenced oraffected by project performance. Furthermore, ONSUR proposes by our project toengage women in decision making, particularly regarding the location of projectactivities.ONSUR will ensure rigorous follow up through monitoring and evaluation of projects. In addition, Rigorous M&E frameworks will be implemented to record gender differences in project implementation and results. Information will be disaggregated according to gender and results will be shared at the cluster and community level to assist in decision-making. ONSUR will never tolerate any abuse by anyone towards a woman or a girl.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:- ONSUR has good experience in distributing agricultural input - Based on a market assessment by ONSUR showed that the markets are functioning grand, agricultural input is available in the local market.-ONSUR will hire two agronomists (engineers/ experts), who will follow up the stages of the crop production cycle and accompanied by related guidelines and conduct technical training.There is social acceptance for the project activities Risks and mitigations: -Risk: The inability to meet the needs of all people in the project area;Mitigation: Transparency with local authorities and community and inform them very clearly of the selection criteria.- Risk: Corruption and fraud by the traders and providers; Mitigation: -Clear criteria to select vendors- Beneficiary will sign a list containing the redemption of voucher according to the specific cations mentioned in terms of quantity and quality. - Risk: Beneficiaries sell the agricultural inputs Mitigation: awareness sessions to the beneficiaries that these inputs to increase their production of their crops- Risk: Drought. Mitigation: ONSUR will distribute fuel for supplementary irrigation.
