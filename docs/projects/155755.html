Project Summary

The project will be implemented in security cleared areas of North Waziristan and target 9,463 individuals (Women: 27%; men: 26%; girls: 24% and boys: 23%), addressing humanitarian needs of returning TDPs. SDO will undertake WASH response that will target returning IDPs and maximising beneﬁts for the communities by providing water to 7,570 individuals, sanitation facilities to 5,679 individuals and hygiene items/awareness to 9,463 . Under Water component, the project enable increased access to drinking water through installation of new, repair and rehabilitation of water systems linking Cash for Work (CFW), adding Conditional and Unconditional Cash Grants (CCGs/UCGs). Each beneﬁciary will be provided a monthly grant of PKR 7200 for 3 months. Under the sanitation, SDO will rehabilitate sanitation infrastructure in communities and schools. In addition, SDO will conduct gender sensitive hygiene promotion and distribute hygiene kits.

Needs

Since 2008, more than 5 million people have been displaced from Tribal Areas of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP). These displaced women, men, girls and boys ﬂed their homes to escape insecurity and sectarian violence and/ or were requested to leave by the Government ahead of security operations. Of these IDPs, around 70% are women and children. According to the UNHCR data, 15% of the returning IDPs are female headed household. Now as the return is almost complete and people have returned to their native areas, there are still critical gaps in meeting minimum humanitarian needs, despite of continued humanitarian support by partners and donors during their return to enable reintegration efforts with return. Total affected population of NW District are 100,977 that need WASH assistance urgently. According the WASH DNA 2017 by OCHA, only 26% returnees have access to piped water for drinking, while 61% are using surface water or open spring while 41% using unprotected hand pumps and dug well. Only 23% get water within 500 meters limit while 77% have to travel more than that including 36% to travel more than 1 KM to collect water. Water cost 150PKR per day for 14% returnees. Only 19% returnees have access to latrine out of which 97% have been constructed by themselves. 73% latrines are shared by 2-4 families while 74% says they cannot construct latrine being expensive which require behavior change. 45% families through garbage anywhere which creates sanitation issues. 31% diarrhea and 25% skin disease are note.
