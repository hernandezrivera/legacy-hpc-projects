4 - Needs

According to the 2021 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance - a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020 - with needs being exacerbated by economic decline. An estimated 3.5 million people are in need in areas controlled by Non-State Armed groups (NSAG) and Turkish Armed Forces in the north-west and northern Syria, an increase of 13 per cent. Similarly, since early 2020, the proportion of people in severe and northern catastrophic need has further risen in north-west/ Syria (91 per cent of PiN in severe and catastrophic need) and SDF-controlled areas (72 per cent). People in Syria require humanitarian assistance, some of which are in acute need due to a convergence of vulnerabilities resulting from displacement, exposure to hostilities and limited access to basic goods and services.The framework for the protection and assistance provided to IDPs, returnees and affected populations is the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP), are aimed at supporting the Government's efforts to reduce vulnerabilities, enhance protection and strengthen resilience through a wide range of community-based interventions, with attention to the most vulnerable groups.While new displacements continue to occur in some parts of the country, self-organized IDPs and refugees from returns of countries continue to areas where the level of hostilities has declined or ceased, and which have become more accessible.
