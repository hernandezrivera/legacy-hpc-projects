Project Summary

The project aims at increasing the resilience and livelihood opportunities for most vulnerable male and female farmers and livestock breeders in order to revitalize of agricultural and livestock sectors in the target locations, where various farming and livestock breeding were major pillars of providing employment and livelihood opportunities. The project will target 3000 farmers (2000 olive farmers, 1000 summer vegetables farmers), 3000 livestock breeders (2000 sheep breeders own 1-10 sheep/or goats and 1000 cow breeders who own one cow). Furthermore, this program aims at creating short-term job opportunities (one month during olive harvesting season) of most vulnerable women (1000 women over 18 years, of them 500 women of the host community and 500 women from IDPs) through CFW intervention for the harvesting of olive orchards owned by the target olive farmers. The beneficiaries will be prioritized according to a clear vulnerability and eligibility criteria. The target farmers and livestock breeders will be supported with agricultural and livestock inputs needed to help then to return to their income generation activities (details are illustrated in project activities below). Provision of livestock and agricultural support will be synchronized with technical support on the best agriculture and livestock practices that will maximize the impact of project interventions.

4 - Needs

According to HNO report 2020, the agriculture sector has had significant losses since the beginning of the crisis in 2011 affecting both crop and livestock production with a noted decline of 49 per cent and 51 per cent respectively. Widespread damage to irrigation systems and shortages of labor and quality inputs such as seeds, fertilizers and fuel due to imposed sanctions on Syria has affected the country’s production.. It is estimated that during the first three years of the crisis sheep numbers fell by 45 percent, goat numbers by 30 percent and cattle numbers by 40 percent. In addition, slight reduction in sheep and cattle were reported during 2018/19, as compared to the previous year. The constraints for livestock production include prevalence of common epizootic disease, slow success rate of artificial insemination livestock feed prices and destruction of veterinary services. The overall impact is high prices of livestock and their products that generally show an upward trend compared to previous years. youth, PWDs and members of local councils), people in the targeted location was depend on income generation activities-whether agricultural/livestock activities or different income generating activities associated with agriculture and livestock value chains. Because of the conflict, several barriers have been identified which have left a negative impact on the local communities and livelihood opportunities which led to a high suffering because of the lack access to income for families. Women-headed households and PWDs remains to be one of the most vulnerable populations within the target locations, as they are restricted by both movement and access to the labor market. Income generating small businesses had been crippled totally or partially as a result of socioeconomic situation, spending of savings and the accumulation of debt that financial inability to continue the income generation businesses

5 - Implementation of activities

The project includes the following main livestock and agricultural activities:1. Support 3000 Livestock breeders (2000 sheep breeders and 1000 cow breeders) who own 1 cow or maximum 10 sheep/goats with compound fodders. Each sheep/or goats will be supported with 0.75 kg of compound fodder and each cow will be supported with 10 kg compound fodder daily sufficient for 4 months.2. Establishment of Mobile veterinary clinic and provision of veterinary services and vaccinations campaigns (Enterotoxemia, Smallpox, and Foot-and-mouth)3. Delivery of training program on the best practices for livestock breeding.4. Support 1000 male and female summer vegetables sufficient w agricultural sufficient to plant 4 dunums (1 dunum Tomato, 1 dunum cucumber, 1 dunum eggplant and 1dunum pepper). Each targeted farmer will receive 8 gram of tomato hybrid seeds, 200 gram of cucumber hybrid seeds, 20 gram of eggplant local seeds, and 20 gram of pepper local seeds. Each dunum of summer vegetables will be supported with 20 kg of NPK and 20 Kg of Urea fertilizer 46 % and 50 liter of fuel for supplementary irrigation. 5. Provision training program based on Good Agricultural Practices “GAPs” for summer vegetables farmers 6. Support 2000 male and female Small and Mid-sized olive framers (owned 3 to 10 dunum) with agricultural inputs, agricultural services and harvesting support sufficient for 3 dunum. More specifically, each olive farmer will be supported with 100 kg compound NPK, 2 liters pesticides, 1 Scissor, and 1 Hand Saw,12 tins (capacity of 16 liters).7. Delivery of training program on the best practices for olive farming 8. Implementing of olive harvesting (3 dunums ) through CFW to be conducted by 1000 women. Each woman will receive 6 USD per day in order to perform the harvesting activities for 30 days, followed by exchanging of cash vouchers by Hawala agent. 9. Meal Activities (verification, base line,PDM, end-line and final evaluation of the project )
