Project Summary

Brac Protection team is implementing and operate community based protection that facilitate and support community group structure, formed groups and completed activities according to community people need. Besides BRAC is implementing info points, outreach program, and community center interaction, provide multiple points where community members can raise concerns, complaints, and ideas about camp services. BRAC use and will use in future in different types of information gathering methodologies and monitoring tools. Project undertake the initiatives on identifying trends, assessing protection risks and providing relevant Information to different stakeholders, as well as initiating various types of consultations and advocay sessions to strengthen the safety environment. Protection monitoring teams is identify persons with specific needs through field visits, household visits, and interaction with community leaders, youth and other relevant stakeholders. Team ensure appropriate records are kept of all identified cases and referred through the inter-agency referral pathway or to specific service provider depending on capacities and when appropriate.There is an ongoing need to ensure that the community is well informed about the services available and where and how to access these, as well as a need to continue to increase the level of social empowerment and self-management. CBP provides a vital role in both these areas with services including information points, community centres, community outreach and self-defined community groups giving the community the opportunity to lead in the provision of protection services. Along with the government (RRRC, CiC, Police, etc.) and civil society (Chairman, member, CBOs, etc.) Protection Monitoring and Community Based Protection Unit will work for creating social cohesion between the host and refugee communities.

Needs

More than 730,000 Rohingya Refugees entered into Bangladesh in 2017, along with the existing vulnerability of the population in the area and there are now more than 1.4 (approx. ) million people in need of assistance in the Cox’s Bazar area . In order to support the improving of living standards of the refugee and host communities in Cox’s Bazar, BRAC work to promote community based protection (CBP), reduce the risk of Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV), provision of protection in the form of Legal Assistance , contribution to improving reception conditions of refugees through monitoring of the protection environment, in order to support the improving of living standards of the refugee and host communities in the Cox’s Bazar. Local community also facing various socio-economical vulnerabilities and sufferings in their life due to sudden overcrowded population in Ukhya and Teknaf Upazilas. Women and girls of this areas are also living under the risks of Sexual and Gender Based Violence such as child marriage, polygamy, domestic violence, limited access to livelihood and income generating activities. In current camp setup, there is a need of a project which will aim at identifying vulnerable people and people with specific needs, especially elderly people, children and women who are unable to access services on their own. It has been seen in BRAC working area through FGD and General meeting, community engagement is highlighted as a gap with efforts needing to be scaled up, including through the rapid establishment of inclusive camp governance structures, the prioritization of community centers, recreational and protection spaces as critical infrastructures in the camps.
