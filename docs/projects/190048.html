1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Comprehensive package of WASH services in partnership with government, private sector, INGOs, and LNGOs to deliver WASH services, items, and facilities to vulnerable displaced populations in formalized areas and hosting communities and critical WASH services for prevention of disease transmission. As the lead UN agency for the WASH Cluster UNICEF supports the government at the national and provincial level in coordination and serves as a lead agency for provision of WASH for the most vulnerable groups.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

WASH Cluster coordination; Water supply to accommodation centres / transit camps through centralized distribution systems, including water trucking (temporary / first response for shortest possible term), management and operation of water distribution points and monitoring of water quality; Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level; Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accomodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees; Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas; Expansion of piped networks to affected communities, resettlement sites, and hosting communities; Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites (first phase); Solid waste management (including collection, removal, disposal and drainage) in accommodation centres / transit camps or resettlement sites; Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households); Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media; Procurement and distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, with focus on most vulnerable families / households; Increasing access to WASH in isolation wards and health facilities; Increasing access to WASH in vulnerable spaces and vulnerable and highly impacted communities; Messaging on COVID-19 on hygiene, prevention, and access to services; Emergency support for the safe, continous operation of centralized water supply systems

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Water committees are central to community engagement for the promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices. They are also an entry point for conflict prevention and community dialogue. The prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse and the principle of ‘do no harm’ is at the core of UNICEF’s mandate. For UNICEF staff and non-staff personnel, sexual exploitation and sexual abuse constitute acts of serious misconduct and are therefore grounds for disciplinary measures, including summary dismissal. For entities working with UNICEF that fail to address sexual exploitation and abuse through appropriate prevention measures, investigation and corrective measures, such failure shall constitute grounds for cessation of the cooperative arrangement with UNICEF. To ensure adequate safeguards and appropriate action related to sexual exploitation and abuse, UNICEF’s procedure involves screening, informing, monitoring, and training of partners. Using the 2010 United Nations (UN) human rights council endorsement that access to WASH is a human right, UNICEF will work with partners to ensure that all people have equal access to safe WASH services. To achieve this, UNICEF and partners will work to ensure that communal latrines are gender separated, culturally appropriate with MHM and disability design components and that WASH service delivery points are well lit and accessible.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process. UNICEF will coordinate the Linha Verde complaint mechanism responses for all WASH partners in addition to promoting the call center numbers in all programs.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs. Water points will be accessible to individuals with mobility issues.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

UNICEF's concern for children extends through the totality of a child's environment, including all the factors that influence a child's survival and development. UNICEF is required to ensure that negative environmental impact is prevented and minimized in its supported programmes and strives to improve the immediate environment of children (e.g., home, school and community) wherever possible and within UNICEF's technical competence. As a member of the UN System, UNICEF has committed itself to support the implementation of the various action plans adopted at all the major UN conferences of the 1990s. These action plans, especially Agenda 21, emphasize the need for integrating social, economic and environmental factors at the policy, planning and management levels.
