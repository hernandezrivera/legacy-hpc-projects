Project Summary

Strengthening the capacity of 500 mobile and road side cocked food vendors in combating COVID-19Mobile and road side cocked food vendors in Bamenda interact with a considerable number of people but they are the least empowered to combat community transmission of COVID-19. They touch coins, food etc but practice very limited personal hygiene. Their customers too are mechanic, apprentices who are also exposed to contaimination etc who some of the times are reluctant to protect themselves. These women have also lost income due to both COVID – 19 and the crisis in the North West. Strengthening the capacity of 500 mobile and road side cocked food vendors in combating COVID-19 proposes to train 500 cocked food vendors in the production of hand sanitizers and provide a micro grant as startup capital to diversify their source of income. It shall also help them to develop home protocols and distribute hand washing kits.
