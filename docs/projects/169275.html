Project Summary

Following the Beirut explosion that took place on August 4, 2020, many individuals residing in Beirut and its suburbs have been affected by the blast. Over 300,000 individuals have had their houses damaged, and are thus thought to be homeless. Official reports indicate that the death toll is over 180 casualties, with more than 6,000 people injured, and dozens of missing people. 
The aftermath is not only detrimental to people's homes and livelihoods, but has also affected the mental health and protection of many individuals, especially the LGBTIQ+ individuals.
Based on our database, many LGBTIQ+ individuals reside in the neighbourhoods of Achrafieh, Gemayzeh, Mar Mikhael, Bourj Hammoud, Geitaoui, Quarantina, and many others, which were severely hit by the explosion. This tragedy comes to add up to a massive economic, financial, political and health crisis in Lebanon.

The current economic, financial and political situation in the country, had a major impact on the LGBTIQ+ community. The latter has been facing a lot of challenges to sustain the basic needs for daily life. Many LGBTIQ+ individuals are in constant need for a safe shelter. This issue has been exacerbated within the COVID-19 pandemic. LGBTIQ+ individuals are usually at higher risk of struggling with economic difficulties in Lebanon, given the lack of employment opportunities - due to stigma and discrimination, especially when gender expressions are not conforming. 
In this proposed project, MOSAIC aims to respond to the protection and mental health needs of the LGBTIQ+ individuals affected by the explosion, by provision of direct SGBV services that address their protection, livelihoods and coping mechanisms caused by this emergency, such as case management, PFA, MHPSS, hygiene kits, and cash-based assistance.

1 - Needs

The next day following the explosion, MOSAIC conducted a needs’ assessment to check for our beneficiaries’ pressing needs, and assess their shelter status, and their overall situation. The identified needs include food, MHPSS, sanitary kits and cash-based assistance. All of those who have been contacted so far, and who reside in the areas affected by the explosion, have expressed their need for cash to be able to restore their damaged homes. This includes glazing, kitchenware and appliances, doors, and furniture.Additionally, some have completely lost their homes, and require immediate relocation to a new safe shelter. Also, many people do not have any food security and are in urgent need of food items. Others require immediate counselling, psychological first aid and mental health services due to the trauma caused by the explosion. In addition, MOSAIC identified the need for COVID-19 hygiene kits among most of the beneficiaries affected by the blast. Access to sanitary kits from other Health/WaSH actors might jeopardize the safety and security of the people of concern, especially that during this emergency response, there is a lack of capacity building/development to service providers - on sexual orientation, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics issues. In order to mitigate this risk, MOSAIC prefers to provide the sanitary kits directly to people of concern. This needs’ assessment activity was carried out through phone calls, as well as door-to-door visits - especially in Karantina, where Trans* individuals have limited mobility.During this assessment, psychological first aid and COVID-19 awareness session were provided, and beneficiaries were requested to suggest contacts that have also been affected by the blast. MOSAIC thus aims to expand its response, through direct protection services, to all LGBTIQ+ individuals affected by the blast, and who present pressing needs.

2 - Activities

- Provision of case management to 60 individuals referred to MOSAIC, or identified through the conducted needs assessment. - Provision of psychological first aid to 100 beneficiaries and referred LGBTIQ+ individuals affected by the blast. - Provision of 120 hygiene kits; each kit will include face masks, sanitizer, disinfectant, and personal hygiene products.- Provision of cash-based assistance to 300 LGBTIQ+ individuals affected by the explosion to ensure their safety, well-being and protection- Conducting door-to-door visits to provide COVID-19 awareness session and distribution of kits and cash-assistance, especially to LGBTIQ+ individuals with limited mobility due to their sexual orientation, gender identity and gender expressions.- PSEA training sessions to volunteers that will conduct door-to-door visits. MOSAIC will will make sure that beneficiaries and people of concern are aware of the existence of our office's landline to report on any act of sexual exploitation or abuse, during the emergency response and service provision. MOSAIC is a member organization within the SGBV TF, and will be closely coordinating with the task force accordingly.- Mapping provided services by other NGOs and local initiatives, and thus referring beneficiaries to other comprehensive services such as health-related services (e.g. medicines)
