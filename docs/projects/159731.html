Project Summary

Somalia is experiencing protracted emergency leading towards a deteriorating humanitarian due to long term conﬂict, drought and other natural hazards, 3 million people in drought/AWD/cholera affected areas are in need of health services. In 2019, Over 68,000 of Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD) cases were reported and 480 of these died with case fatality of 0.5%. 58% of the cases were children under 5 years of age. In southern regions of Somalia in particular Gedo, more than 587 Acute Watery Diarrhea AWD/cholera cases have been reported (Health Cluster Bulletin, 2019). According to the Somali Health Cluster, Somalia's health sector remains in critical condition with one of the worst health indicators in the world and the 7th largest internally displaced people. The under ﬁve mortality rate is 137 per 1,000 live births while approximately 732 women per 100,000 live births die from pregnancy or childbirth related complications. The country has signiﬁcant high maternal mortality 700 per 100,000 live births, high newborn mortality 94 per 1000 live births. Key challenges facing the Somali health sector include persistently high burden of disease, limited institutional capacity and stewardship role of ministries of health, inadequate, unpredictable and unsustainable level of ﬁnancing, with a high share of out of pocket spending on health, absence of balanced, motivated, well distributed and well managed health workforce with the appropriate skills mix; limited and unequal access to essential health services, and poor quality and safety of services across all levels of care, weak births and deaths registration, limited operational research and disease surveillance, lack of synergy of humanitarian response to health; and inadequate action on social determinants of health, therefore, this project will address that gap.
The project will be implemented directly HIRDA organization in Bardhera, Garbaharey and Belet Hawa district in Gedo region Somalia. Through this project, HIRDA will be able to prove emergency primary health care services both MCH/HC facility as well as the mobile clinic, HIRDA will ensure provide quality treatment on communicable and non-communicable diseases as well as immunization of children and mothers as well as provision of vaccine-preventable disease, Through this project HIRDA will ensure capacity development of staff on integrated management of childhoods illness, community awareness and EPI services.HIRDA will ensure to coordinate with MOH Jubaland regional state as well as MOH federal government as well health cluster and other humanitarian actors. However, this project will contribute towards the cluster strategic objective to ensure equal and integrated lifesaving assistance to children and people living in humanitarian emergency and crisis to reduce mortality and destitution. The project seeks to strengthen and provide support the vulnerable children, women and men to enable ensure all gender inclusivity within the program. The program shall also coordinate with other humanitarian actors in the region and more so the Garbaharey and Bardhera district in which HIRDA remain commitant in delivering coordinated health, nutrition and livelihood response. The project will ensure coordination with other clusters and agencies with similar activities and also ensure gender is mainstreamed throughout the implementation
