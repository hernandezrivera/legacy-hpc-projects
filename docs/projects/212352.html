Project Summary

Throughout 2024, VO plans to implement comprehensive FS interventions by applying a variety of modalities (In-kind, Cash, and vouchers) to address the needs of the different groups with proper modalities. The proposed project will effectively improve the food security status of the assessed food-insecure people through life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance by improving food consumption and dietary diversity, avoiding negative coping strategies. The planned activities to be implemented during 2024 are the following activities:
1. Emergency Food assistance distribution (such as ready-to-eat rations ) for HHs of crisis-affected IDPs through proper modality (Cash - In Kind) in coordination with the FSL cluster and other operating agencies to avoid any possible overlap in the targeting, also to fill the gaps in the need for such type of assistance.
2. Regular Food assistance per modality (in-kind, Cash, Voucher) for crisis-affected HHs (IDPs and HC)
3. Provision of free and subsidized bread for IDP individuals and host communities.
4- Provision of cooked meals for the crisis-affected households during the emergency (extreme weather conditions, such as floods, snowstorms, and emergency displacement).
Moreover, in terms of the implementation activities, Violet is planning for cash and voucher modalities whenever possible for regular food assistance to ensure market revival in the targeted location, considering market functionality/proximity/capacity and the functionality of financial service providers in the targeted location. The value of the voucher will be according to FSL and CWG
recommendations for food assistance based on their last updated versions. In the meantime, VO will consider an in-kind modality for regular food assistance where there are low-functionality markets in the targeted areas to increase food availability in the targeted locations; the weight of the in-kind food basket (when applied) will be based on the WFP recommended food basket composition and FSL cluster response packages.
Also, VO will consider the food security sector guidance on hot meals to distribute cooked meals for the planning. Designing and distribution.
Regarding beneficiary targeting, referring to the FSL cluster guidelines on the beneficiary targeting approach, VO will target the severely food insecure first for IDPs in the camps, also food distribution off-camps through applying fair eligibility and vulnerability criteria, taking into consideration the gender balance and targeting the most vulnerable groups such as women-headed households, child-headed households, elderlies-headed households, families with big size numbers and people with special needs and chronically ill members, as well as households with pregnant and lactating women; moreover, during the design and implementation of projects Violet will ensure the design of different activities to better inclusion of PWDs and female beneficiaries in the project activities.
Based on the situation/context analysis, VO will ensure considering the best suitable modality for all of the food assistance; then, if voucher, Violet will ensure proper and enough voucher distribution and redemption period, and if in-kind, then the distribution will be through setting distribution points in the targeted location based on recently updated VO distribution SOPs during COVID-19 context.
Despite the fact that no budget was secured for this project, 50% of it is in the pipeline.
With regard to the quality control and monitoring mechanisms for the cooked meals, this will be assured through full adherence to the FSL cluster guidelines for the Cooked meal distributions in emergencies – practical guidance also the same applied to the bread assistance; Violet will consider the BBTWG guidelines for the bread production quality control in terms of ingredients and other conditions, as well as the monitoring process for the bread production and distribution monitoring process including BNFs selection

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Throughout this project, Violet aims to respond to emergency needs by implementing different activities with an appropriate modality based on the needs, BNFs preference availability, and functionality of the markers. Before the implementation, Violet will engage the BNFs and project stakeholders in the project design and implementation to ensure the local ownership of the project. Thus, Violet will sign MOUs with the LCs, demonstrating their roles and responsibilities. Moreover, Violet will ensure close coordination with the FSL cluster and humanitarian actors in the field to avoid potential overlapping. During the implementation, Violet will ensure the BNFs' protection and do No Harm principle by consulting the BNFs on the distribution location, Timing and arranging the priority ques for elderlies, women, and PWD, but also through training the staff on the Organization code of conduct and PSEA policy. To avoid the tension between the communities and different groups, Violet will include a percentage of the most vulnerable host community members in the project. It will also announce the project criteria and make sure it's clear to the BNFs and activate the complaints response mechansims (CRM) mechanism. For the CVA modality, Violet will ensure the market is functioning before the distribution to avoid the harm that may occur to the non-BNFs
