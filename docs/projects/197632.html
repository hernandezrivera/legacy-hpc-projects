Project Summary

The project will support the educational process in Syria by providing Education, P, and WASH services in high severity locations namely, Aleppo (Eastern Kwaires,Rasm al abood-Shekh Ahmad),Idleb (Ma'arrat An Nu'man,Khan Shaykun,Tamanah),&Daraa (Masmiyyeh, Izra', Ghabagheb).
The project will help children pass their classes successfully and complete their studies to reach their potential, thus will reduce the risk of dropping out of school by providing remedial classes, and encouraging drops outs to go back to school by providing BTL campaign, providing non-formal education, build the capacity of teachers and head teachers by training them on the active learning methods, this will have a positive impact on the entire educational process in Syria. The project will provide CVA for children to enable to continue their education, and providing and installing Solar Power for schools. As for WASH, the project will provide HP campaign for the students in the schools and HK, especially under Cholera outbreak, and raise awareness of girls on MHM. As for CP, the project aims to improving mental health of children and reduce protection risks by providing psychological support for students, and case management, and awareness sessions to caregivers and students.
The main project activities:
-HK distribution and HP and MHM: The kits items will be according to UNHCR guide and HP &MHM will be according WASH sector and HP group guidelines.
-BTL campaign
The campaign seeks to extend the benefit of education to all drop out through a wide campaign to promote and inform about the importance of curriculum B for children who have dropped out, to enable them to continue their education, inform them of the required identification papers required for registering children in school. This activity will take place through door-to-door visit, flyers, information desks.
-Provide solar power to schools: To provide proper lighting for schools
-Provide schools furniture according to the need of each school according to the team visits
-Provide materials and stationery to schools: including papers, white boards and markers, pens, workbooks, teaching aid.
-NFE (Non-formal education) for out-of-school children: In coordination with MOE, run 2 cycles of NFE to target dropouts.
-Stationery distribution for the students in the schools: Provide the students in the targeted schools with stationery kits.
-Active learning training for teachers and head teachers in the schools: In coordination with MOE, provide the teachers and head teachers with active learning training to obtain new educational skills and methods, and help the to overcome the educational challenges they face with students.
-Provide PSS activities for the students in the rehabilitated schools: PSS aims to improve the mental health of children, PSS workers will do an assessment in each targeted area with the children to identify their needs, then they will design the PSS program based on these needs, and then they will work with these children and provide these sessions to them.
-Provide protection awareness sessions for caregivers and students: PSS workers will do an assessment in each targeted area with caregivers to identify the protection problems and need, then they will design the protection awareness sessions based on these needs, and then provide these sessions to them.
-Case management: This service will be provided individually based on the needs of each child, also the case managers in each targeted area will design services map for the referral pathways.
-Provide CVA for the children: The activity will help children and their caregivers by supporting them with cash assistance to help children continue their education, and the project will support the most needed children based on specific criteria.

3 - Needs

Education needs of Syrian children and adolescents will be further hindered by the protracted crisis and economic distress. These factors will continue to limit the education system’s ability while limiting the parents’ ability to afford their children’s education, leading to an increase in out of school children and dropouts, exposing children to a myriad of protection risks. Unaddressed needs will limit the ability of children to reach their potential, weaken resilience and undermine early recovery.Learning environments will continue to be unconducive for children, further contributing to low learning outcomes as schools will continue to unduly promote teachers to teach using “one-size fit for all” approach, without being able to pay attention to individual learning levels, while preventing the teachers from teaching meaningfully in multiple shifts without adequate lighting. Over-crowded schools will increase risk of spreading communicable diseases to communities due to insufficient WASH facilities.Learning will continue to be disrupted by insufficient support to attract and retain skilled teachers. A lack of teacher remuneration will further reduce retention and a lack of transport support will reduce ability to reach schools, particularly those in remote areas.Limited investments in strengthening multiple learning pathways, including both formal and non-formal TVET, will continue to limit the quality of education services thus creating great risk of “lost generation".

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

EPDC will mainstream protection principles in all stages of the project (from design to implementation and monitoring/evaluation), and gender-specific needs are taken into account and addressed throughout the activities. EPDC will apply protection and gender mainstreaming as follows: prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm to the targeted communities. The proposed activities will follow the principle of ‘Do no harm’, encouraging inclusive participation of women and men, and ensuring relevance across population in activities by taking into consideration differing needs e.g. HP will include MHM for girls separately.Throughout the implementation of all activities, EPDC will ensure that strong mitigation measures are put in place to prevent risks of GBV and SEA by rehabilitating separate gender latrines and installing locks on the doors and also conduct the WIS activities during the summer break to ensure the safety of the children.EPDC MEAL team will also conduct monitoring on the conducted rehabilitation works to ensure it is conducted in a safe and timely manner. The team will also ensure at the end of the works delivery that beneficiaries are satisfied with provided works and the behavior of EPDC staff and contractors, enabling EPDC to take corrective action in a timely manner. EPDC will recognize PWD needs in the targeting. At the same time, EPDC utilize CRM channels that are clear to BNFs including children, caregivers, and teachers, and referral pathways.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:1.Security situation is stable and no sudden displacement movement occurs2.Market is stable and materials are available3.The weather conditions are good so they do not disrupt conducting the activities4.The precautionary measures against C-19 and AWD/Cholera are in place and the project does not risk their spreadRisks and Mitigation:1.The security situation escalates and movement is restricted accordingly. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High. Mitigation: Coordinate with the local community, official authorities, and relevant sector. Plan mitigation measures to ensure safety of beneficiaries, EPDC staff, and contractors. If the situation keeps escalating, negotiate scale-up in other project locations that are safer.2. Market is unstable and materials are no longer available or their prices increase to a point disrupting the works. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High. Mitigation: Communicate any challenges in the availability and prices of materials and negotiate alternatives similar in quality.3. The weather conditions are bad causing delay in implementation of the activities. Likelihood: Low. Impact: Medium. Mitigation: Adhere to workplan and keep updated with weather forecast. The WIS will take place during summer break so that the schools become ready for the students.4.The project increase risk of C-19 and AWD/Cholera spread. Likelihood: Low. Impact: High. Mitigation: EPDC team will ensure the use of PPEs on site including gloves, facemasks,and hand sanitizers.
