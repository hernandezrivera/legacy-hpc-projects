Project Summary

Food Security and Livelihoods (FSL) project is targeting natural disaster/flood and conflict (compounded by inter-clan, inter-tribal violence and cattle raiding) affected most vulnerable IDPs, and Returnees and host communities who have been affected over the past one year. LWF plans to reach 4500 HHs (27000 Individuals )and additional 690 Individuals (total of 27690 Individuals) from IDPs, and Returnees communities in Twic East, Duk and Bor South counties through supporting emergency food production and building capacities of people and households’ level to build resilience to better respond to shocks. This project will contribute to strategic objective 1 (Specific objective 1.1) and Strategic Objective 3(specific objective 3.1) of the South Sudan and Humanitarian Needs Response Plan (HRP 2022). The project intends to reduce the food insecurity and malnutrition through enhanced emergency food production via distribution of seeds, agricultural tools, fishing kits and poultry for the most vulnerable people but also to build resilience slowly reducing the dependency on food and agricultural inputs through training & capacity building on-farm production, with demonstration plots and kitchen garden created also in proximity of schools and health facilities to be of example for the community; awareness on nutritious food consumption and food waste management, capacity building on saving and income generation.
The project will ensure the sustainability of its outcome through working together with customary institution, line departments, women groups, youth groups, and local administration from beginning of the project.
The project activities will be implemented in consideration of preventive measure to prevent the spread and transmission of COVID-19.

1 - Needs

Jonglei state is among the states affected by conflict between warring parties since 2013. Conflict in Jonglei continues to negatively affect humanitarian needs among the population throughout 2021. Clashes between armed groups and pervasive insecurity, particularly in central Jonglei. Secondly, the inter tribe conflict is a common phenomenon manifest by cattle raiding, child and women abduction . In the process of raiding there have been massive attacks of villages, leading to significant causalities. In addition, Jonglei State is among the top worst floods affected States of South Sudan, following unprecedented devastating floods incidences in 2019 and 2020/2021. Approximately, 400,000 people were displaced from their villages of origin to the high grounds within Jonglei State. Some people moved to neighbouring Counties and States between June 2020 and February 2021 (https://fscluster.org/south-sudan-rep/document/ipc-south-sudan-october-2020-july-2021). Twic East County has been experiencing the worst impacts of displacement, loss of livelihoods, infrastructure and almost 100% inaccessibility due to blocked roads since July 2020 to date due to unprecedented high levels of flooding (IRNA Twic East October 21,221). The major cause of the flood disaster was due to torrential rains across the upper Nile basin that increased the water level in the river. In Twic East the primary dyke got broken, and the river water flooded Pakeer, Ajuong, Nyuak, Lith and Kongor Payams, all the way to Duk county through the Jonglei canal. 80-85% of Twic East County is underwater, basically this is change in landscape. At present, there are approximately 48,000 people in islands/upper grounds (living drylands in flooded areas and river islands). Of these populations, 90% are Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) congested in narrow islands (LWF Commissioned climate Risk analysis and community action plan in Twic East five payams, July 2021). Similarly, Bor south county was affected by flood since in 2020 and 2021, where communities in Jalle payam and Baidit were affect. Jalle communities were displaced and settled with in county as IDPs. For example, from August to October 2021 over 5,000 families, including women, children and the elderly, have been displaced by severe flooding in Bor South county including the state capital of Jonglei, Bor. In Duk County has been affected by flood from July to October 2020 and then similar period of 2021. As a result of the flood in Area of Padiet, Donchack, Payuel, Ageer significant number of house/Tukuls were submerged and collapsed, people affected by flood faced displacement and loss of livelihood and livestock., 50% of planted cereal crops was damaged at early growth stage in June and at maturity stage in September. Significant number of domestic animals were died. Pit latrines submerged in water collapsed. Due to flood and inter clan conflicts in Duk over 50,000 individuals verse I in sever condition (RRC Inter Agency Rapid Assessment from 2020-2021). According to Integrated Phase Classification (IPC) report projection for period April-July 2021, Twic East, Duk and Bor South counties are under acute food insecurity /Emergency, IPC 4 (https://fscluster.org/south-sudan-rep/document/ipc-south-sudan-october-2020-july-2021). The humanitarian assistance for food access does not adequately cover the existing needs in the counties. The humanitarian needs of the Twic East County, Duk and Bor South counties are complex, in Twic 43,200, In Duk 34000, in Bor South 104,000 people including IDPs and locals need food assistance, they mainly depend on humanitarian assistance, besides the fish they catch from the flood waters and the river Nile. However, the Food Assistance different partner did not fill all gaps across year. Hence this project will enhance emergency food production and build capacity of communities and household for resilience. In addition, it will mainstream floods risk reduction/mitigation

2 - Project activities

Specific Objective 1: Support emergency food production through crops, vegetable and fishing production while considering COVID prevention measures • Provide 2500 most vulnerable HHs with agriculture kits (cereal crops and vegetable kits) for emergency food production • Provide 2000 most vulnerable Fishing folks with fishing kits to catch fish for HH consumption • Provide poultry birds for 350 most vulnerable Women headed (3 hens and one cock each) • Provision of early warning and food security information at payam level Specific Objective 2: Enhance capacity of people and families for resilience to absorb shock while considering COVID -19 prevention measures • Establish 12 FFS (Farmers Field School), each 20 members (240members)• Train 12 FFS facilitators on FFS approach and facilitation and support to facilitators with incentive• Establish 12 demonstration sites,• Support FFS groups with crop and vegetable kits - 240 member and 12 FFS groups• Training of farming households on nutritious food consumption and food waste management • Organize and support (in training) most vulnerable women into 5 Village Saving and Credit Associations (VSLAs)-each 20 members (100 women)• Provide small grant (seed capital) to five VSLAs The project will mainstream flood risk reduction/mitigation through empowering and reinforcing community based flood taskforces and based community action plan developed in 2021 in all payams od Twic East, five payams of Duk and one payam of Bor South county, while expanding efforts to other payams
