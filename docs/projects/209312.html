Project Summary

After the eruption of clashes between the SAF and RSF in Algazira locality, on Dec 15th, 2024, many people are forced to leave the state, which was considered as a hub for IDPs and seek shelter in many safe areas such as Kassala, Elgadarief and Port Sudan, as well some have crossed the border to neighboring countries. This incident has directly affected the other state like White Nile, Gadarief and Kassala, due to the massive movement of IDPs who have created additional burden and sharing the limited resources available in these areas, as well the cut off of supplies that was delivered from Algazira state has maximized the gap and the need for humanitarian services. NEF has been working in Algazira and White Nile, and currently moved its office to Kassala, from which a rapid assessment has been made including collecting basic information from the local authorities in the three states, the information revealed critical need for WASH services, basically access to potable water, Sanitation and Hygiene in the three states. In white Nile the spread of water born disease is recorded to be high, while the spread of Cholera has been recorded during Dec in some northern localities such as Alduim and Algotaina. As well the existing IDPs gathering were living in very severe conditions. In Kassala the rapid assessment conducted, indicated a serious increase on number of IDPs due to the clashes in Medani, over 70,000 people are currently living in 120 gathering points, lacking basic WASH services. In Algazira, still the situation is very severe within the gathering points, where some IDPs still are living estimated to 60% of the previous record those who hasn’t managed to relocate from Algazira. NEF is proposing this intervention to cover the WASH gap in the three state, White Nile ( Alduim and Algabalain localities), Algazira ( Medani Alkobra, Almanagil, and Ruffa localities) and Kassal ( in side Kassala town), for estimated 150,000 IDPs, living within the gathering points. NEF will leverage and coordinate this intervention with already existing RRF project through IOM, to seek lessons learned and approach of implementation specially working with the existing community plat forms, such as trained Water & Sanitation committees who are able to proceed with the implementation in the inaccessible areas. The intervention will focus mainly to upgrade, rehabilitate existing water sources, provision of hygiene kits, awareness, and massive sanitation interventions, such as rehabilitation of existing latrines, provision of sanitation tools, as well as massive awareness through the WASH committees, the project as well will focus on protections mainstreaming and dignity through the existing forums and the community plat form.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

NEF guiding principles. NEF mainstreams protection through training and enforcement of its Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct. Through these principles, and its adherence to Protection Principles as per the Humanitarian Charter and Minimum Standards of Child Protection in Humanitarian Action (CPMS), NEF resolutely ensures the protection of children and at-risk adults. NEF further ensures the safety and dignity of all beneficiaries through its strict adherence to the principles of Do No Harm (DNH). NEF actively looks for ways to minimize any threats and vulnerabilities of every group of the affected population. Protection is mainstreamed throughout project design and implementation by NEF’s promotion of meaningful, safe, and dignified access. Contractors and project partners need also to adhere to NEF’s guiding principles. NEF applies DNH and Sphere principles to its selection of participants, guaranteeing fair representation, regardless of their ethnicity, religion, or gender. NEF further reflects Sphere’s belief that all individuals have a right to assistance in our commitment to working with marginalized and vulnerable populations, including female head-of-households, survivors of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), youth, older people, and persons with disabilities. These considerations guide all of NEF to prevent exposing vulnerable and marginalized people to further harm. Consultation, feedback, and response. Through confidential and secure feedback mechanisms, NEF ensures that all beneficiaries are able to raise concerns and complaints. NEF responds to beneficiary feedback by adopting the DNH principles of constantly learning and improving. In addition to responding to and reducing risk, NEF assists affected populations to claim their rights and access appropriate remedies. As appropriate, NEF will establish community mechanisms (example, WASH committees) that are diverse and representative of the community. Women and girls are at disproportionate risk of GBV and SGBV. NEF strives to mitigate these risks through consultation in project design and implementation. NEF has a formal gender policy as well as a dedicated gender officer and it seeks to mainstream the participation of women in all activities. Throughout the project, NEF will give special attention to gender-based constraints and opportunities to ensure that activities and decision-making processes are accessible to women and girls and that they promote appropriate access to and safe use of NEF services.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

NEF projects are designed, implemented and monitored with the participation of the beneficiary communities, government line ministries, HAC, donors and local partners. NEF ensures that affected populations are engaged at all levels of the project cycle. To help operationalize its commitment to actively seek, listen to, consider, engage, protect and act on feedback from the communities and individuals that are the direct recipients of assistance, NEF maintains an organization-wide Accountability to Affected Populations Framework linked to its organizational policies and practices and is built upon the 5 Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations. NEF expands upon this framework to include principles of protection, do-no-harm, appropriateness, and coordination. Leadership & Governance. NEF works in partnership with local NGOs and CBOs throughout the lifecycle of our projects. Where there is need and demand, NEF devotes resources to capacity building of local entities, aligning projects with relevant policies and priorities. All project staff, regardless of organizational affiliation, will work together as a single team. As the lead, NEF takes full responsibility for project management and implementation, for coordination among partners at an organizational level, for delivering results and for reporting. Transparency. Outreach events include information about the organizational stakeholders, criteria and processes for beneficiary recruitment, participation, and access to project benefits, project approach and objectives, and project sponsors, all in local languages, displayed in public, accessible locations. Community-based project association meetings and trainings will use culturally and linguistically appropriate communications. Feedback and Complaints. Beneficiary feedback mechanisms will be incorporated into program implementation, particularly through monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEAL) processes. NEF will transparently communicate the purpose of all MEAL activities with beneficiaries, and seek their feedback. NEF will consult communities to establish an appropriate feedback and response mechanism. All methods of feedback collection will involve sensitization of staff, partners, volunteers, and beneficiaries on the purpose of methods used and will communicate clear roles, responsibilities, and processes. Feedback will be collected legally, ethically, and with due regard to the welfare of those affected by the results. In addition, the feedback system will uphold confidentiality and non-retaliation principles as per NEF’s Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct. Participation. To verify needs, gauge acceptance, and ensure the target population understands the project, community consultation and orientation meetings will be held at the beginning of the project. These meetings will raise awareness of the communities’ participation roles and responsibilities and of the program partners’ responsibilities toward them.
