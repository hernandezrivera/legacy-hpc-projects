Project Summary

The project addresses the problem related to Zimbabwean women and children consumption of limited and undiversified diet. Through Social Behaviour Change and Communication (SBCC) activities, the project aims to positively influence behaviours related to women dietary consumption and young children feeding practices. The project will be implemented in the districts of Buhera, Chipinge, Kariba and Tsholotsho. The project will be implemented through four distinct phases namely: formative, development, programming and monitoring phases. The summary of the steps included in the four phases is as follows:
• Conduct formative work to gather context-specific information
• Develop SBCC objectives to define communications purpose
• Draft creative briefs from formative work to develop SBCC material
• Pre-test SBCC materials among target audience segments
• Train staff for effective implementation of SBCC campaign
• Implement SBCC campaign within WFP programme with partners
• Monitor SBCC campaign to identify areas for improvement
• Improve SBCC strategy based on monitoring efforts
