Project Summary

Sahil will use its years of hands on experience, technical capacity and community outreach for prevention, response and rehabilitation of effected children.
Under this project, Sahil will work directly with communities, children, government and civil society organizations to strengthen the mechanisms for child protection.
Sahil will work on awareness raising and capacity building of community members including parents, teachers, elders and children through training sessions.
Linkages will be developed with service providers to establish effective and functional referral mechanisms.
Sahil will implement this project in close collaboration with local Social Welfare departments and local civil society organizations.
Sahil will arrange training sessions for representatives from all Union Councils of 4 districts of Islamabad, Peshawar, Sukkur and Jaffarabad. The training sessions will include child rights, abuse and its effects, child protection, gender based violence effects on children and parenting skills. Case management procedures will also be shared with the UC representatives including a contact list of service providers. Posters of relevant information will be given to each participant.
Training events for community members will be arranged with a maximum of two groups of 15 participants each (male and female) in each Union Council, so that SOPs of COVID 19 are observed.
Community members sessions will be on child rights, abuse and its effects, child protection messages, gender based violence effects on children, and parenting skills. Posters on each topic will be provided to each participant for recall and sharing with others.
Sahil directly provides protection information to the children through puppet shows. Puppet shows for children will be delivered in every Union Council of these 4 districts for boys and girls. Maximum participation in each session will be 30 children in open spaces.
Child protection awareness sessions will be arranged for stakeholders including departments of Social Welfare, Education, Health, Shelters for children, Baitul Maal, Police, and local civil society organizations.
A district level referral system for case management will be shared through an exchange of contacts between service providers and UC representatives.
To disseminate child protection messages for a larger public, an awareness campaign through electronic media (cable and radio) will be conducted in 4 districts.
Sahil will provide free legal services to victims of child sexual abuse, early marriages and women victims of violence. For this service Sahil already has 5 legal officers in its offices, and the provision of 172 referral lawyers to deal with child sexual abuse cases across the country.
Sahil has been dealing with legal cases through its collection of data of newspaper reported cases published yearly as “Cruel Numbers”. The analysis of this data is the only available statistics in Pakistan. It is quoted by government, media, donors, and civil society as evidence, and reference.
Counseling facilities will be available through Sahil’s 5 offices, which provides free counseling through in-person, telephone, and email for any kind of psychological issue and especially for victims and survivors of violence. Sahil team of 5 clinical psychologists is available for addressing the issues.
Sahil has a toll free helpline for contact.
