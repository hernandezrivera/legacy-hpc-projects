Project Summary

The deep humanitarian crisis resulting from the long-running conflict in Eastern Ukraine continues to have a negative impact on the population and infrastructure of the Donetska NGCA territory .
The health system, which is already overloaded due to the fighting, is most affected by the outbreak of the COVID-19 virus. CO "ICF "MIRA" conducted monitoring of the situation and needs of medical institutions that are directly identified for receiving COVID-19 patients on the territory of the Donetsk NGCA. As of 29.10.2020, the number of COVID-19 cases is 6,433, but the actual figure is probably much higher. According to local ministries, cases of infection and spread of COVID-19 directly in health care institutions have been identified. Based on information obtained from communication with medical professionals and from letters of request received from health care institutions to the organization, CO "ICF "MIRA" it concluded that there was a shortage of personnel, insufficient provision of personal protective equipment, and outdated medical equipment that was failing.
Many medical institutions do not have the necessary medical equipment to save the lives of people who have a severe new coronavirus infection. Treatment of these patients is impossible without oxygen concentrators. In addition, there is a catastrophic shortage of disinfection chambers and steam sterilizers, which significantly increases the risk of spreading infectious diseases among medical personnel and patients, including COVID-19,due to the inability to disinfect soft hospital equipment: work clothes, bed linen, blankets, pillows, mattresses, towels and medical instruments. To reduce the risk of COVID-19 spreading in healthcare settings, save the lives of critically ill patients, and protect healthcare professionals, CO "ICF "MIRA" it plans to provide assistance in the form of medical equipment, disinfection chambers, oxygen concentrators, medical gurneys and personal protective equipment for medical personnel. These humanitarian response measures will help to minimize the risks of COVID-19 spread, as well as allow to cure seriously ill patients in the shortest possible time, which will reduce the mortality rate among medical personnel and patients.
This project will be implemented within 6 months and will cover 10 health facilities that are intended for patients with COVID-19.
These types of assistance do not include gender and age differences between beneficiaries. CO "ICF "MIRA" he has experience in cooperation with medical institutions and in providing humanitarian and targeted assistance. The organization has a clear understanding of the requirements of international partners, both for the implementation of international projects, as well as for gender equality, financial discipline of the project, prevention of corruption and financing of terrorism.
The organization has clear standards and internal policies that meet the UN international standards for humanitarian activities on the territory of NGCA, developed in accordance with the requirements of international partners. Project programs strictly follow the recommendations of clusters. Thanks to the successful implementation of socially significant projects, CO "ICF "MIRA" gained sufficient experience, formed a team of professionals and plans to continue its activities in the humanitarian sphere.
