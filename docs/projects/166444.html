Project Summary

EYN through the support of UNICEF has a network of over 2,000 Community Nutrition Mobilizers (CNMs) who have been undertaking community based screening and referral for treatment for children aged 0-59 months having SAM. Through close collaboration with the local authorities and nutrition partners working in similar Local Government Areas (LGA), EYN has been able to reach and work in the most hard to reach areas in Southern Borno where International Non-Governmental Organization have had challenges accessing due to security reasons. Between the period of January to November 2019, EYN has been able to screen 527,418 children aged 6- 59 months in MMC, Jere, Konduga, Baga, Mobbar, Ngala, Gwoza, Dikwa, Rann, Mafa and Bama LGAs at community level.
The COVID 19 pandemic has posited a threat to the nutritional status of young children and mothers. Actions by the gov’t in an effort to contain the spread of the virus and reduce mortality, currently affects the project implementation in the communities. State lock-downs has led to suspension of almost all activities. The economic effects which presently is leading to a disruption of food supply in turn leading to increase in food prices and exacerbating food insecurity. If this trend continues it would increase or even worsen malnourished cases amongst children.
It is estimated that the levels of acute malnutrition expected to increase by 30% due to the COVID-19 social-economic impact.
EYN seeks to align fully with the NUTRITION SECTOR COVID-19 PREPAREDNESS AND RESPONSE which has as its objectives to: Reduce morbidity and mortality due to COVID-19 among beneficiaries and communities, and nutrition service providers, in North East Nigeria, Mitigate the negative impact on the nutritional status of children, pregnant and lactating women and nutrition service providers wellbeing, Ensure the continuation of safe delivery of nutrition interventions.The sector has outlined the following key considerations for any nutrition intervention program aimed at protecting the nutritional status of vulnerable populations:
• Ensure continuity of Programme Criticality (lifesaving) nutrition interventions, such as treatment of acute malnutrition (MAM/SAM).
• Ensure recommended Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) measures in all nutrition activities, and reinforce hygiene and sanitation measures at all service provision sites;
• Strengthen integration of nutrition response with Health, Food Security and WASH
• Strengthen nutrition supply chain system, and coordinate with UNICEF/WFP and other partners to ensure rationalization of available resources;
• Adapt all nutrition activities to ensure adequate social distancing measures;
• Implement mother MUAC screening as soon as possible, and ICCM approaches;
• Distribution of messages and materials to promote breastfeeding and other IYCF practices in the COVID-9 context.
In response to this, EYN seeks to carry out Nutrition COVID 19 Response in Borno State. The purpose of the project is to Contain the spread of the COVID-19 epidemic and decrease morbidity and mortality through:
1.Screening of children 0-59 months with acute malnutrition through the adoption of Mother/Family MUAC approach.
2.Referral for Treatment of children 0-59 months with severe acute malnutrition. The levels of acute malnutrition expected to increase by 30% due to the COVID-19 social-economic impact.
3. Referral for Treatment of children 0-59 months with moderate acute malnutrition.
4. Carrying out training on Infection, prevention and control (IPC) while conducting nutrition awareness, screening of malnutrition and referrals at the community level.
5. Provision of Personal Protective Equipment(PPE) As a way of Infection, prevention and control (IPC) while conducting nutrition awareness, screening of malnutrition and referrals at the community level.
The project targets children from the age of 0-59 months screened monthly, women/caretakers in the communities.
