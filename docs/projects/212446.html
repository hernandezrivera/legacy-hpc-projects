Project Summary

The project aims to provide the most urgent life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance through integrated intervention, including winterization interventions and NFIs, House rehabilitation, and better-dignified shelter solutions in NW Syria for the most vulnerable people in NW Syria.
Mercy-USA for Aid and Development in this project will support the most vulnerable people in NW Syria in many components in Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Darkosh, Salqine and Jandairis sub-districts:
1-Provision of better-dignified shelter solutions according to the last version of SNFI_Dignified_Shelter_Guidlines_2023
Implementing dignified shelter units with two designs (Two rooms and three rooms) will provide dignified living conditions. The implementation of the dignified shelters also comes associated with basic infrastructure improvement (including road graveling, construction of drainage and sewage networks, water network and installation of solar streetlights. (The target is 480 households, comprising 2,450 individuals (513 men, 567 women, 658 boys, and 712 girls))
2-Cash for winter (180 USD) for each HH one-off during November and December 2024.
(The target beneficiaries for this cash assistance program are 5,000 households, comprising 25,431 individuals (5,008 men, 5,375 women, 6,482 boys, and 8,566 girls)
3- Implementation of minor repairs to damaged or unfinished housing (The target is 200 households, comprising 1,020 individuals (224 men, 243 women, 267 boys, and 286 girls)
4- Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing (Moderate and Severe), (The target is 200 households, comprising 1,025 individuals (230 men, 243 women, 270 boys, and 282 girls))
Total number of beneficiaries who will be targeted in 2024 is 5,880 HH (29,926 individuals)
