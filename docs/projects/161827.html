Project Summary

The Supporting WASH for Conflict Affected Populations in Borno and Yobe States project proposed by GREENCODE is as result of WASH gaps currently experienced and observed in IDPs and host communities in Jere, Konduga, Nganzai and Mongonu LGAs of Borno state and host communities in Gaidem, Damaturu LGAs of Yobe state
Currently, GREENCODE assignment in in Kaga, Konduga, Jere and Mongonu LGAs of Borno state shows gaps in sanitation facilities (inadequate latrines and dysfunctional ones), open defecations, inadequate access to water supply as well as menstrual hygiene kits.
The proposed GREENCODE project is aimed at improving ongoing WASH services in LGAs in Yobe and Borno state with critical WASH Needs. In this project GREENCODE in a timely manner as well as grant equitable access to safe water through water trucking where necessary as temporary measures for some days, repairs of water points (hand pumps and motorised solar powered borehole), promote hygiene and sanitation services, undertake partial and full repairs of dysfunctional latrines and showers, public health promotion activities and WASH NFIs, with a specialized provision hygiene kits that include menstrual hygiene products, particularly re-usable sanitary pads.
The project is targeting to reach 17,000 persons with 15 liters of water daily and 2,500 women and girls of reproductive age with girls will be targeted specially for menstrual hygiene management. People living with disability will be mainstreamed into both targets.
The project objectives are;
i). To increase safe and equitable access to enough quantity of water for drinking, cooking and for personal/domestic hygiene purposes for both formal and informal camps hosting new arrivals and host communities/returnees in Yobe and Borno states.
ii). To promote good hygiene practice (user friendly and gender appropriate kits) among the new arrivals in both formal and informal camps in the project locations/LGAs.
In achieving this GREENCODE with donor grant will be trucking water daily to IDP locations (new arrivals and host communities with water crisis) in LGAs in Borno and Yobe states for 90 days, drilling of new boreholes, rehabilitation of both hand pumps and solar motorized borehole, train and facilitate proper hygiene /health education activities using the two major language (Hausa and Kanuri), for proper understanding aimed at improved knowledge, attitude and practice of the people, GREENCODE will adopt the use of cartoons/pictorial messaging during the hygiene promotion sessions as tool for in promoting lifesaving behavioural change. Also, the project will provide specialised hygiene kits that include menstrual hygiene products particularly re-usable sanitary pads for women and girls of reproductive age.
In line with WASH Sector priority, GREENCODE will continue to assess and undertake repairs of sub and super structure of latrines and showers in LGAs for both states.
GREENCODE in the past 5 months has been responding WASH needs in Borno state, but the needs are overwhelming and need more actors and resources. Through the provision of life saving WASH activities, GREENCODE will contribute to mitigate the risk of cholera outbreak as the two states has in recent past experienced outbreak of Cholera and this intervention will help checkmate future occurrence.
Protection interventions will be mainstreamed into the project and maintain in all the project cycle. GREENCODE will adopt all-inclusive gender ratio of 60:30: 10 (women, men and youths) in the following activities, WASH committee, training, community Hygiene promoters, WASH kits distribution. Also, SGBV and child protection will be part of the training during WASH Committee and Hygiene promoters’ training. The hygiene/health promotion will be designed taking into account disposal of used menstrual hygiene products, accessible to children and the physically disabled, sex dis-aggregated. 9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Women, men, girls, and boys, the aged and persons with Physical disability within target communities will be directly involved at all stages of the program cycle in order to encourage ownership and participation in project activities. Community structures, networks, and support groups are fundamental to building resilience and are at the heart of the GREENCODE’s programme implementation strategy. GREENCODE recognizes that communities are not homogenous, and the gender analysis will enable us to jointly design an appropriate intervention, with the community, looking at the needs of sectors, including all other WASH, Gender/protection, health, FSL and NFI. It is particularly important to consult with the community on their specific needs. Men, women, boys, girls, youths, the aged and disabled will be consulted on design and location of WASH facilities. They will be included in any training and awareness raising on HIV AIDS and SGBV. Women and girls will be consulted specifically on menstrual hygiene kits. GREENCODE is fundamentally committed to ensuring accountability to communities and installing a rigorous beneficiaries and other stakeholders feedback mechanism, which is part of the program collected through Focus Group Discussion and also linking with other clusters who have set up desks in the biggest sites. GREENCODE has places high value and premium on humanitarian accountability. The proposed project is highly gender and age¬ sensitive, considering gender equality for effective programming with assurance that all affected members of the target population have equal access to services and the targeted actions is based on gender and age analysis. The project takes into account presence of men and women in the area of community mobilization and community screening of under five years old children and will ensure that women are represented in higher ratios. Community involvement specially women in the community mobilization.
