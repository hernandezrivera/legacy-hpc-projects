Project Summary

Implementation of a response-wide multi-sector needs assessment and support to assessment coordination and analysis through the Information Management and Assessment Working Group, in order to inform strategic planning at sector and intersectoral level in line with the 2021 JRP planning process.

Activities or outputs

Activity 1: Implementation of Joint Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment1. Secondary data review and review of 2019 lessons learned2. Develop partnership framework and define stakeholder group for 2020 MSNA. 3. Develop terms of reference (ToR) for the MSNA process, outlining objectives, analytical framework, population of interest, research design, roles and responsibilities, dissemination strategy, and timeline. 4. Implement one round of quantitative and qualitative data collection in refugee and host communities, disaggregated by population and geographic area, in line with JRP 2021 planning timeline.5. Conduct data analysis and facilitate sector and intersectoral joint analysis of MSNA and secondary data.6. Restitution and feedback of data to affected populations7. Output production.Activity 2: Allocation of analysis capacity support to ISCG1. Provide in-depth secondary analysis support to ISCG in response to information gaps specified by ISCG and in line with the objectives of the IMAWG/MSNA process. All activities will be coordinated through the IMAWG and carried out in full collaboration with sector coordination teams and ISCG.
