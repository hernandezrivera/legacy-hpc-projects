Project Summary

The proposed project aims to increase the resilience of affected communities in NWS through integrating agriculture activities and food security by supporting wheat value chain alongside bread distribution
- The project will work on producing up to 625 tons of locally high-quality wheat seeds by providing high-quality agricultural inputs to 250 farmers, in addition to providing technical support to carry out the best agricultural practices to increase wheat production in 5 communities in al-rouj valley which are :
1- Idleb - Harim-Armanaz-Milis
2- Idleb - Harim-Armanaz-Sheikh Yousef
3- Idleb - Idleb-Idleb-Kafruhin
4- Idleb - Idleb-Idleb-Bshmaron
5- Idleb - Idleb-Ma’aret Tmasreen-Murin
selection criteria will be set based on need and vulnerability with prioritizing displaced farmers who cultivated small invested lands ( about 5 dunums ) with local wheat seeds. beneficiaries' names and vulnerabilities will be identified through registration and verification procedures.
In addition to that, The project will seek to purchase 631.5 tons of wheat crops from the local market or farmers during harvest season in 2022, Out of 631.5 tons, 31.5 tons of soft wheat will be cleaned, sterilized, and packed to be distributed to 250 farmers ( 125 kilos for each farmer) for wheat cultivation in next season 2023, while 600 tons of the purchased wheat will be supplied to mills to produce 450 tons of flour which will be mixed with 400 tons of flour ( donation from IHH) to baking 850 tons of flour to produce 1,190,000 bread bundles. Each ton of flour will produce 1400 bread bundles.
- 1,190,000 quality bread bundles will be distributed to 5700 Displaced families in 40 camps in Maaret-tamasrin subdistrict, 6 days a week for a period of 8 months
- technical committee from (IHH) will be formed to determine the quality of 631.5 MT of wheat
seeds that will be purchased from local markets or farmers.
Bread distribution will be based on the following criteria :
HH with 1 - 4 individuals will receive one bread bundle.
HH with 5 - 8 individuals will receive two bread bundles.
HH with 9 and above will receive three bread bundles.

4 - Needs

Ten years into the Syria crisis, humanitarian needs are deepening. Ongoing insecurity and the compounded impact of displacement,combined with worsening socio-economic conditions characterized by sharp currency depreciation, record-level price increases, and cuts insubsidized goods have prompted an almost 20 percent increase in the number of people in need compared to early 2020 - with adisproportionate impact on women and children. As of January 2021, around 13.4 million people are estimated to require some form ofhumanitarian and protection assistance, including 6 million in acute need, due to a convergence of factors arising from a sharp reduction inpurchasing power, the loss of essential livelihoods and income, mounting food insecurity, limited access to basic services such as health,WASH, education, nutrition, and critical protection services, inadequate shelter conditions as well as the immediate and longer-term effectsof the COVID-19 pandemic.The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agricultureassistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity of PiN has risen by 45 percent in the last year.• At least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. 5 - Implementation of activities

1- Advertise for the project, make meetings with local councils and coordinate with stakeholders.2- Hire the project team, ensuring a gender-balanced approach.3- Prepare the beneficiaries list for 250 farmers in 5 villages.4- Advertise for the tender and receive a quotation for fertilizers, herbicide……etc and receive, store all agriculture inputs received from the suppliers in a secure warehouse.5- Distribute agriculture inputs to the selected farmers.6- Purchasing 663 tons of wheat seeds from farmers or local markets.7- Clean, sterilize, bag, and store 63 tons of wheat seeds in warehouses in proper conditions.8- Distribute 63 tons of wheat seeds to 250 farmers ( 250 kilos) for each farmer.9- Contracting mill to grind 600 tons of wheat seeds to produce flour with extract percentage 75% and then transport the produced flour to IHH warehouse10- Contract bakery to bake 4100 Kg of flour daily to produce good quality pita bread and deliver them daily to distribution centers at selected camps, Each ton of flour produces 1400 bread bundles weighing 850 grams.11- 5700 displaced families in 40 camps will benefit from bread distribution for 8 months.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

• The project will contribute to alleviating the suffering of 25788 IDPS (4706 men 5787 women 7527boys 7768 girls) by supplying them with life-saving services through offering bread which is an essential food item.• IHH will take into consideration the norms of the society regarding gender in each step of implementation, where bread will bedistributed in two queues one for men and one for women. MEAL team of IHH will include female staff to alleviate the sensitivity for families screened, to support society customs (where men are not allowed to enter tents of women), and to receive feedback from the women about the services provided.• Female distributor workers will be hired to make distribution more comfortable and easier for females and to prevent any case of exploitation or abuse that may be done by Male staff.• All project documents and data will be disaggregated by age and gender.• Training sessions about GBV and PSEA will be conducted to IHH before the beginning of the project to ensure full compliance with PSEA and GBV guidelines.• All the staff and suppliers will sign on Code of conduct adopted by IHH.• M&E team will monitor the behavior of workers on-site, and make sure they are abiding by the humanitarian principles and code of conduct.• CRM mechanism includes suggestion and complaints boxes, complaints phone numbers, and emails of IHH will be available on bread cards, banners, posters in the distribution centers for raising concerns regarding SEA or GBV


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Wheat is available, not affected by crisis, drought, or insects.2- Wheat price is at an average rate3- Stable of fuel price4- Mill is working smoothly5- Timely disbursement of fundsRisks :1- unavailability of wheat because it is affected by the crisis, drought, or insects.2- Increase wheat price.3- Increase fuel price4- Technical problems in the mill5- The delay in funds will have an adverse effect on the start of activities-Mitigation Measures:1- IHH will purchase flour from Turkey and transport it to Syria.2- Decrease the procured quantities of wheat, while IHH will increase the donation the flour quantity3- IHH will contract with the selected vendor/s with a fixed price with USD to avoid any price increase4- It will be easy for IHH to agree with a new mill, with these connections and power in the region, in case of technical problems that the millmay experience5- Implementing low-cost activities
