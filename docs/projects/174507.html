Project Summary

The HNO 2021 Protection Analysis Summary report of 09 November 2020 states that as of September 2020, an estimated 101000 girls and 99,000 boys were displaced within the NWSW, and IDP children below the age of 18 for the NW was estimated at 126828. It is projected that an estimated 217051 children in need of child protection assistance in 2021 in the 2 Regions. The 2021 HNO and Cameroon Operation provide a detailed context and need analyses that have been taken into consideration in this project proposal. The five main areas highlighted are; Weakening or destruction of the community, social and family social supports in which children live, Protection needs related to the risks of all forms of exploitation of children as the circles of protection around them have been eroded, the direct impact of armed and physical violence, including sexual violence especially against women and girls, Overall adoption of negative coping mechanisms by children including child labor, child marriages, survival sex, and exploitation, and lack/loss of civil documentation as a triggering cause to severe protection risks
COMINSUD has selected target activities from the CMR_HRP21 CLUSTER FRAMEWORK PROTECTION SECTOR to implement in the NW. The HNO identified family separation as an important need to address with a target to sensitize 55175 caregivers/community leaders in the NW about key measures to prevent family separations during the conflict. COMINSUD plans to identify and sensitize 2500 caregivers/community leaders (1500 female and 1000 men). The HNO also identifies addressing the need and growing challenge of unaccompanied and separated children (UASC) children with a target of 7650 UASC in the NW. We plan to identify 150 (80 girls and 70 boys) of the regional target of UASC.
To provide support to families to better protect their children, 55156 caregivers to be sensitized on positive parenting in the NW. We aim to target 1500 caregivers (800 female and 700 men). To build the capacity of Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms, the regional target of community-based child protection mechanism functional is 734 to provide case management services support to 28484 children facing protection risks. COMINSUD plans to ensure that 20 Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms are functional and to build the capacities of existing ones to provide appropriate case management services to 2500 children (1300 girls and 1200 boys) out of the 22846 regional target.
675 conflict-affected children are targeted to receive birth certificates as well as 37476 persons to be sensitized on birth registration in the NW. We plan to facilitate the establishment of 200 birth certificates (110 for girls and 90 for boys) and sensitize 2500 persons (1500 female and 100 men). To Provide interventions for the prevention and mitigation of risks and response to GBV affecting children the regional target for the number of children accessing GBV risk mitigation, prevention, or response interventions is 55156. We shall target 1200 children (650 girls and 550 boys).
To provide support to identified at-risk families to protect children from child labor, through prevention support, the target for the number of families to identify is 2054. We plan to target 150 families and free 150 (80 females and 70 males). To provide children already engaged in child labor, with minimum tailored child protection case management; multi-sectoral services (psychosocial support, education, and family economic empowerment); and pathways to decent work for children of legal working age, the regional target for the number of children identified in child labor provided with or referred to appropriate services is 15196. The target for the number of children identified in child labor who are removed from it is 974. We will identify 800 children (450 female and 350 males) in child labor and remove 150 children (80 girls and 70 boys) from it.

1 - Besoins / Needs

To address the above needs, COMINSUD has picked out some activities to be implemented from the Humanitarian Response Plan. The following key activities and corresponding indicators have been selected and they will be realized through the following implementation strategy. 1-Family Separation: identify and sensitize 2500 caregivers and community leaders amongst whom shall be 1500 female and 1000 men to take measures to prevent family separations during the conflict. Organizing a 1 day reflection workshop with 10 key stakeholders who will identify the causes and demerits of family separation, and proposed various measures that will be taken to prevent family separations. 2- Addressing the need and growing challenge of unaccompanied and separated children (UASC): Identify 150 separated and unaccompanied children (80 girls and 70 boys). 3-Adequate alternative care arrangements and reunification for UASC: Ensure that one 800 children (450 girls and 350 boys) are placed under adequate alternative care arrangements and 150 children (84 girls and 66 boys) are reunified or in contact with their family in the North West Region. 4- Support to families to better protect their children: Provide support to 2500 caregivers (1500 female and 1000 men) on positive parenting to better protect their children. This activity aims to identify and implementing measures to prevent family separations. Organize a 1 day reflection workshop with 10 key stakeholders will integrate content for families to protect their children. Production of sensitization material and outreach by the 20 Community Child Protection Agents shall ensure that 2500 caregivers receive support to protect their children. 5- Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms: Ensure that 80 Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms are functional, build the capacities of existing ones, and ensure that they provide appropriate case management services to 2500children (1500 girls and 1000 boys). 6- Birth certificates to conflict-affected children: Facilitate the establishment of 200 birth certificates to conflict-affected children (110 for girls and 90 for boys) and sensitize 2500 persons, (1500 female and 1000 men) on birth registration. 7- Prevention and mitigation of risks and response to GBV affecting children: Ensure 1200 children (650 girls and 550 boys) have access to GBV risk mitigation, prevention, or response interventions. Establish guidelines for conducting GBV safety audit, risks, and vulnerability mapping will be developed for use in 40 localities by with the members of the Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms amongst other community members. 20 Community Child Protection Agents provided with orientation for 1 day on how to conduct GBV safety audits, risks, and vulnerability mapping, and carry out interventions (prevention and mitigation of risks and response) on GBV facing children. 8- Family protection of children from child labor: To address this need and free children caught in the net of exploitation and child labor, COMINSUD to cover a target of 150 families and free 150 children (80 females and 70 males) from child labor. 9- Children involved in labor, including worst forms of child labor: COMINSUD to ensure that 1500 children (800 female and 700 males) already engaged in child labor, including worst forms of child labor receive a minimum tailored child protection case management services; multi-sectoral services support. Ensure pathways to decent work for children of legal working age, and to remove 150 children (80 girls and 70 boys) identified in child labor.
