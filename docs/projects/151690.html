Project Summary

ARDI organization will provide outpatient therapeutic feeding program through 3 mobile/outreach services as well as two static OTP in Kismayo north ,where there were limited nutrition intervention due to access and population being marginalized communities,ARDI will ensure proper community screening , referral and treatment are done in humane manner, it will also conduct caregivers counselling on IYCF and NHHP as well as mother MUAC led activities to ensure proper project community intervention.It will also provide basic nutrition packages including immunization against measles,hence this will further contribute to the nutrition cluster strategic intervention

Needs

Malnutrition is one of the most persistent humanitarian challenges in Somalia despite concerted efforts to address it. Acute malnutrition in children between 6-59 months is a direct outcome indicator of recent changes in nutritional status which could be attributed to changes in food security, WASH and health situations among others. The significant deterioration of malnutrition situation seen among the IDPs can be attributed to high morbidity (disease incidence e.g. AWD, measles), low humanitarian support, poor child feeding and caring practices. Similarly, partners on the ground generally felt that food insecurity, limited health service availability, like poor EPI coverage, and increased morbidity, poor health seeking behavior, and difficulty of accessing clean water supply are driving factors for the current situation. Malnutrition is an outcome indicator highlighting needs a number of sectors, and the vulnerability of communities. In Somalia, the key drivers of malnutrition such as protection concern, insufficient health services, climate hazard and displacement of population out of rural areas due to conflict The high morbidity reported two weeks prior to assessment with the second highest prevalence reported in Kismayo urban (2.4 %).As a result, nearly 954,007 children are expected to be acutely malnourished, including 173,594 who are expected to be severely malnourished during the next one year period (Sep 2018-Sep 2019). The surveys indicate Critical levels of GAM (15-29.9%) in 12 out of 33 populations surveyed (7 IDP and 5 Rural) while Critical levels of SAM prevalence (≥4-5.6) were only seen among IDPs in Mogadishu and Guban pastoral. Activities

•Provided therapeutic treatment to severely acute malnourished <5 children (3000 boys, 3000 girls) as well as moderately malnourished pregnant and lactating women (2000) in Kismayo district Lower Juba region.•Conduct 12 community nutrition, health and hygiene promotion through the community health workers for effective NHHP message delivery to the target communities in Kismayo district. Provide referral services through linking with health and WASH as well as available livelihood program to both severely and malnourished boys, girls and PLWs in the target region.•80% of eligible pregnant and lactating women and children under five boys, girls will receive multiple micro nutrients, De-worming and vitamin A supplementation in the target project sites.•Provision 24 session of health and nutrition education to beneficiaries and care giver•Project staff, community health workers and volunteers (20 male and 20 female) provided with IMAM, IYCF, NHHP, adult learning techniques and sphere training, with supportive supervision.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Limited information on the target beneficiaries may hinder the actual and immediate intervention,however, ARDI will ensure to conduct rapid nutrition assessment to enable understand the extend of severity and prioritize the location based on the needs identified.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Women and marginalized communities in these are been subjected on discrimination in terms of employment in the larger kismayo district and thus exclusion of services and other important community program, ARDI will ensure these vulnerable mothers,girls and marginalized mothers were provided with adequate and improved services Poor coordination and mis-prioritizing the needs ARDI organization will ensure proper coordination at the field level as well as local actors to ensure the beneficiaries received the necessary services

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

ARDI staff will be capacity build on the best humanitarian practices and principal as well as code of conduct thus ensuring more cohesiveness and understanding the right of beneficiaries.
