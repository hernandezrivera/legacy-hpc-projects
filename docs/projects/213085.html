Project Summary

Due to the crisis, the protection needs of most vulnerable people women, girls, PWD, and children are highly increased and critical to be delivered, adding more lack of income resources for those vulnerable people, especially under the COVID-19 epidemic. The percentage of dropping out of schools increased due to the crisis, child labor, forced early marriage, domestic violence, and SGBV concerns in the community.
Women and young girls are the most vulnerable groups in Syria. Their access to services is minimal, as they always need to be with a trusted male to have access to any service like education, training, community center, work, etc. Girls and women are less considered to reach education, vocational training, etc., as most of the services provided don’t take into consideration the difficulties of those groups to reach it. but at the same time the communities there believe that if safe access to services is provided, they are very supportive of having those vulnerable groups reach their needs.
This project aims to support women socially and economically and help them become active and productive in society and make their lives better socially and financially.
