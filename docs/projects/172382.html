Project Summary

The project overall objective is to improve food security and livelihood of vulnerable household host community in Aj Jabalain locality White Nile state. The project will target 3500 host community –(17,500 individuals) in White Nile state in four areas/ villages namely, Alalagaya, Dabat Bosin, Alwarad and Gadh Aj Jabalain locality hosts south Sudanese refugee.The livelihood of the host community depends mainly on farming and non-farming activities such as fishing and petty trading. Their livelihoods are over stretched by the huge refugee community in the locality. Besides, the current country economic situation, climate changes, flooding in July -Sept 2020 and COVID-19 pandemic have exacerbated the situation in Aj Jabalain.
The project seeks to address food security and livelihood needs of the most vulnerable households in the locality, in addition, the project will be focused on strengthening self-reliance if the Host community. The selection of individual beneficiaries/households for the project will be based on the level the needs in terms of food (access, availability and affordability), level of socio-economic impact of COVID-19 on food security to households and focus on female head households. The selection will be done through community consultations forums (overserving COVID -19 prevention protocols) and through the community leadership structures.
The project will support 3500 HH (17500 individuals). The project intervention includes: Support 1,500 HH- HC with agriculture inputs and services in 4 project areas. 1,000 HH will be supported with veterinary services and livestock treatment and 1,000 HH will be supported with small business start-up kits (using voucher approached). There will be skills training and 12 saving internal and lending community groups formation for the 1000 HHs selected for the small and medium business start-ups. In addition, 100 households will be trained on construction of energy saving stoves in which they will make use of the skills to start community focused small business of setting up energy saving stoves for a fee at household level to promote environmental conservation.
A rapid assessment on livelihoods and food security coupled with assessment on the impact of COVID-19 on food security and livelihood will be conducted in Aj Jabalain at the onset of the project. This will form foundation of beneficiary identification process. During the project implementation, in coordination with Ministry of Production and Natural resources in White Nile state the project will conduct one study/ assessment on mid- seasonal crop assessment to assess the impact of the agricultural inputs’ delivery of the project.
The following are the project outcomes:
Outcome 1: Improved crop production to enhanced food security among the vulnerable farmers in Alalagaya, Dabat Bosin Gadha – Aj Jabalain locality.
Outcome 2: Improved veterancy services and animal husbandry.
Outcome 3: Increased capacity to save for improved income and credit access to women and poor households.
Outcome 4: Improved skill on business management and environmental conservation.
Project partner staff and community leaders will be trained in Save, Accessible, Dignify and inclusive program- SADI to support community in developing community complaint mechanism- and feedback.

1 - Needs

According to FSL HNO/HRP 2020-2021, hunger has increased across Sudan and over 7.1 million people are severely food insecure at the peak of the lean season (October to December). The figure, the highest ever recorded in the history of the IPC analysis in the country, represents an increase of 22 per cent if compared with the same period last year. At least 1.3 million people are facing emergency levels of acute food insecurity (IPC 4), while around 5.8million people are facing crisis levels of acute food insecurity (IPC 3). Another 17.4million people are estimated to be under stress (IPC 2) and any additional shock could push them to severe levels of hunger. Based on group of indicators and approach (IPC, Food Expenditure Share, Household Dietary Diversity, etc) which reflects the food security situation and most vulnerable people in each locality. For 2021, food security sector PiN will be 7.2M with around 6.2 M will be targeted. Sudan FSL HRP / HNO- 2021, stated the Food insecurity and humanitarian situation in Sudan has deteriorated rapidly in the first half of the year 2020. The devastating combination of recurrent climate shocks, conflict, economic downturn, disease outbreaks, exacerbated by the consequences of the COVID-19 pandemic and floods, has driven rising food insecurity levels across the country. In Aj Jabalain locality, White Nile stat, total people analysed are 349,077, PiN are 87,269, people targeted are 87,269.The project will target 3,500 in 4 areas of Aj Jabalain locality to sustain food security and livelihood for the affected vulnerable community in White Nile State . The continues influx of SSR to White Nile state make pressure to the host communities by sharing the limited resources and fragile economic opportunities. The project focuses on economic empowerment, i.e. increasing people’s access to and control over economic resources and opportunities including financial services, property and other productive assets, as well as skills development and market information. This approach will reduce the poverty and economic vulnerability of the target population.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project will support 3500 HH (17500 individuals) in Aj Jabalain locality , in 4 araes/villages ,Alalagaya, Dabat Bosin, Alwarad and Gadh . The project aims to strengthen self-reliance of HC through improved access to livelihoods opportunities. The livelihood of the host community depends mainly on farming and non-farming activities (fishing and small business). The community from the selected areas are vulnerable and are hard hit by current country economic situation, climate change, recent flooding July -Sept 2020 and COVI-19 pandemic. The project intervention includes, support 1,500 HC with agriculture inputs and services in 4 project areas, 1000 HH will be supported with small business kits and livestock kits (either in cash or voucher, after skill training and establishing Saving Internal and Lending Community (SILC) groups. 1000 HH will be supported with veterinary services and livestock treatment. In addition, 100 women be trained and equipped with skills on energy savings stoves constructions, in which they will use the skills to start community focused small business of energy saving stove construction at the community level to support environmental conservation.To achieve this the project proposes deliver the following activities/outputs: Output 1:1500 (crop farmer) Households’ farmers increase production and income through access to agriculture input and service . Activities for output 1: Procurement and distribution of agriculture seeds and tools, organize extension training workshops based on agriculture activities. Output 2: 25,000 animals belonging to 1000 families have access to veterinary services and treatment. Activity for output 2: Mobilize the targeted communities and to prepare the vaccination fences, Launch the vaccination campaign, Provision of veterinary services, this will include; refresher training to 12 CAHWs, vaccination and treatment to 25,000 heads of livestock belonging to 1000 herders ( through the linkages of Ministry of production and natural resources and CAHWs). Output 3: 1000HH Increased capacity to save for improved income and credit access to women and poor households and IGA opportunities. Activity for output 3: Mobilise the community to establish Internal Saving and lending Community SILC groups, Train community field agent on SILC modality , with the assistants of the trained field agents 12 saving and loan groups in 4 villages (Alalagaya, Dabat Bosin, Alwarad and Gadh) will be selected and trained on the saving and loans fund management. Small business investment projects (IGAs) will be provided to each of the trained saving and loans group. Output 4: 100 women have improved skills for business management and environmental conservation. Activities for output 4: 100 selected women will be trained and equipped with skills to construction energy saving stoves and provide them with start-up capital stove to start small business of energy saving stove construction at the community level to support environmental conservation. Project partner staff and community leaders will be trained in Save, Accessible, Dignify and inclusive program- SADI to support community in developing community complaint mechanism- and feedback.
