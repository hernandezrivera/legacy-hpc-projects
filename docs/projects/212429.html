Project Summary

Al-Khazna village in Al-Hasakah Governorate (36.993893, 41.400684) is located in the middle of 12 villages with a population of more than 13 thousand people (Medina - Kado - AlSiha - Tato - AlKhazna - Umm Jarn - AlHusseiniyah - Khuwaitla - AlQadah Msheirfa - SudanMsheirfa- AlDayeh - AlNajjar), as there is a dug well and there is no submersible group in it. It is necessary to install a submersible group with solar energy, a protection fence, and a tank with a capacity of 10 cubic meters at a height of no less than 4 meters, with a faucet manhole to fill water tanks and a car manhole according to the quantities studied by the engineering team. After rehabilitating the well, the area will be revived and a large burden will be relieved on the people. From the expense of purchasing water on a daily basis, as the residents of these areas will benefit from filling water from the well, as these villages do not have a main source of water, and therefore this manhole will be a local solution for accessing water. Water scarcity is considered one of the most important causes of human insecurity. As a result of the climate changes taking place in one of the most important Syrian regions exposed to drought, the establishment of water stations, especially those related to hygiene, enhances human security, disease resistance, and protection of the environment, humans, and society. The project targets all segments of society in addition to coordination with the competent water authority. In the region, the project falls within the continuity of work and does not stop at a stage after the end of the project period. The project is considered one of the local solutions to prevent crises at once and with the available resources, which is one of the goals of sustainable development and recovery from crises. The participation of public sector stakeholders strengthens community cohesion and contributes to the implementation of integrated climate security solutions. Coherent relationships between water availability (the most important climate factor), disease recovery, and inclusive growth promote peace in the region. The project will also be accompanied by training workshops for workers in this station and awareness campaigns for all segments of society to promote the idea of resisting climate change and creating local ideas for resisting it, water use efficiency, and field environmental awareness campaigns.
There is no source of water in these villages except from water from unreliable tanks, which is transported from far away places and therefore has a high cost of filling. Rehabilitating Khazna well will facilitate access to water and reduce the cost of filling water for the beneficiaries, in addition to the possibility of direct transportation from the well, especially for the people of Khazna village.
