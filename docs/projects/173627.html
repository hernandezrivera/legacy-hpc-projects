Project Summary

The north east region has been faced with the Boko Haram insurgency for over a decade. With huge displacement and high mortality of the citizens, this conflict has dragged for so long and the coping mechanisms of the affected population mostly in camps and host communities have reached breaking points. Over 1 million people still remain displaced in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states. Research has revealed the need for alternative sources of cooking energy as the search for firewood is risky and life threatening in rural areas. Charcoal is too expensive for their level of living standard and well-being, and other alternatives such as plastic are not healthy. Several cases of abduction and killing have been reported to protection actors in recent times, women, girls and boys are most predisposed due to the societal norms which saddled them with responsibility of sourcing for cooking energy. In view of this, Aid initiative to support vulnerable and idle people , have introduce innovation that will bring durable solution, biomass briquette training that made of degradable waste at our environment that will be a substitute to charcoal and fire wood. INTERVENTION DESIGN/APPROCH
Our approach to implementation will rely heavily on evidence and data.We will proceed through the steps below: Startup
1. Community entry, advocacy and sensitization during which we will conduct community mapping to locate the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host community members.2.We will also hold meetings with community gate keepers in particular traditional and religious leaders from each community so that they understand the intervention and the roles they can play. 3. Beneficiary identification, screening and selection- once communities have been selected and mapped, we will proceed with identification of the vulnerable groups. 4.These include most vulnerable girls, women, girls and men who will be screened to find out those who meet the selection criteria.The targeted(1,001 HH)beneficiaries will then be selected who best meet the criteria. 5.Assessments - we will conduct assessments in selected communities. The assessment will enable us to identify viable source of cooking energy use by the communities and its effectiveness & durability This will guide us to train them on its substitute.6. Once beneficiary have been identified, we will engage the beneficiaries on the theoretical and practical part of the training (Biomass briquette production). 7. The selected beneficiaries will be grouped 20 per training class before training will begin, 8. Sensitization on COVID 19 pandemic protection measures for all selected beneficiaries to maintain during and after the training, this is a key activity of the intervention 9.Training will be provided for (7days)per class and after training, Other training such as financial literacy and business management will be given to the beneficiaries should they want to make the biomass briquette production a source of their livelihood. All these activities would be monitored, successes and learning documented so that the project impact can be evaluated at the end.10.The beneficiaries will be guided to form a cooperative group of 20 members, subsequently to select their leaders by nomination and voting for at least 3 leaders to run the affairs of their group. Leadership position will be Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer.
11.Graduation will take place at the end and the beneficiaries will be provided with empowerment/start-up kits. Only the beneficiaries who successfully complete the training and attend the post training apprenticeship would be empowered.
