1 - Needs

According to visits conducted in coordination between PBA and MoG (Ministry of Guidance) during 14th and 19th of October 2019, South Darfur State, EJM had been established as a locality composed of Deribat, Jawa, Admin units. The area has been broadly affected by insecurity and rotation of authorities since the beginning of the Darfur conflict 2003/2004; and witnessed movements of population to the internally displaced camps at Mershing, Nyala, Kass, Elfasher and Manawashei. Thus during 2010 to 2015; the ongoing tensions and fighting between the Movements forced a large number of people to flee their homes seeking for settlement in different villages such as Dira, Kiba, Krony, Soro, Korbal, Baimadina, Sawani, Leiba and Jawa at the Jebel Marra mountains. The socio- economic structures of EJM had been broadly affected by the conflict as the entire displaced population sought refuge in satellite villages within JM mountains and some sought refuge in South and North Darfur IDPs camps, and therefore the social services are disrupted while the destruction of the scarce income generating sources devastated the general economic situation. In the view of general situation, population could be categorized into three groups; host- community, internally protracted displaced persons and returnees who returned home due to the improvement of the security situation within the territory. According to the above assessment, Deribat Admin Unit has not being accessible for the UN agencies during the last seven years; worth noting that the State authorities have committed to facilitate humanitarian access so that affected and displaced civilians who should receive humanitarian assistance they urgently need. Um Baru Locality located far West North (240 Km) from Al Fasher Town. Surrounded by Meilet and Malha from the East, Kutum from the South, the Great Desert from the North, Chad and Altina from the Eastern direction. The Locality witnessed a recent voluntary returns from in and out of the Country. This information confirmed by Mr. Hussein Adam Abdellah the X- Education Unit Director at Um Buru Locality till 2014 and who live now at the Locality. Blue Nile State has received recently an above average heavy rainy season resulting into flooding and related diseases including a cholera outbreak affecting 5 out of 7 localities which affected the whole State system including the education sector. According to the FMoE assessment (92) basic schools at Blue Nile State have been affected by the heavy rains and flash floods (Emergency Committee statistics survey on September 2019). OCHA, WHO, UNICEF and the Government led joint efforts to assess and find out the humanitarian needs caused by flash floods and the Cholera outbreak. At the State (364,200) from the total population have been identified as affected,PBA Sub- Offices team conducted a FGDs with key informants from the area and assessed the education needs of the people there and named some of the most needy schools to address their urgent assistance we could provide. The project will address the urgent needs of construction or rehabilitation of classrooms, provision of clean drinking water and hand- washing points, latrines construction, EiE and recreational materials, training for the teachers on core and EiE, issues such as PSS and life saving skills, and management issues for PTAs members, awareness raising on the importance of education and enrollment, hygiene promotion campaigns and spreading a culture of peace among the target groups.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

PBA adopted Core Humanitarian Standards (CHS), Humanitarian Accountability Partnership (HAP) guidelines and Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Task Force on Accountability to affected population (AAP) in setting standards and building capacity, communication, participation in programs, monitoring and reporting on compliance, addressing complaints and development of strategy to improve quality of partnership in principles of accountability. PBA is mainstreaming approach focusing on assessing and addressing the needs of all groups within communities where we work including men, women, boys and girls specially the most vulnerable groups such as groups with disabilities, minorities and all project’ stakeholders to ensure inclusive community consultation and participation, access to service by all, specific needs of extremely vulnerable groups and the safety of all project participants. To ensure that community engagement is maintained through various stages of implementation, and that key information is shared and collected by and with the integration of all these groups, PBA undertook the following:- Formulate Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) signed by all the project stakeholders to establish feedback and compliant and response mechanisms. - Sign with the aproject staff a contract with clear code of conduct steps to prevent all forms of abuse and sexual exploitation on affected population- Draft AAP guidelines for data collection and reporting. - Monitoring the project by all staff to ensure excellent and on- track implementation of the activities. - Organize community consultation meetings to promote participation and involvement of beneficiaries in the formation of community committees and planning the implementation schedule.- Community meetings held at the beginning of the project to orient the target groups and all stakeholders to ensure their understanding and acceptance as well as check the needs, guaranty the engagement and support of all groups and involvement of the target groups at all stages. . - Community Committees such as PTAs, CBCPNs oriented to organize and mobilize the beneficiaries. The Community Committee composed of 15 elected members. They help a lot at all assigned tasks during the implementation process such as following on construction and rehabilitation of classrooms, development of selection criterion of the training participants, transport and distribute the EiE, educational and seating materials, organization of the campaigns…. etc.- Appointed Community Mobilizers as feedback, complaint and response mechanisms for the targeted community. - PBA involved affected population at the Education Committees that composed of 10 members/ each school) which aims to participate on project implementation process e.g re-assessing the real gabs and priorities of education in schools at targeted communities at all Localities will conduct regular meetings and group discussion for follow up project performance.
