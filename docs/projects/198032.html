Project Summary

EMERGENCY WASH ASSISTANCE FOR THE NORTHWEST AND SOUTHWEST REGIONS OF CAMEROON
This project named the Emergency WASH assistance for the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon was developed based on the following identified problem: The ongoing sociopolitical crisis in the North West and South West regions of Cameroon continues to cause a lot of queues, trauma as well as displacement of persons from their homes. The sicio-economic fabric of many areas have been destroyed, this has led to congestion in semi urban and urban areas of the Northwest Region of Cameroon as many of these people move to the urban and semi urban areas and are received by host communities in these areas, making the number of persons per household large. This makes it very difficult especially for women to manage their personal hygiene, most especially their menstrual hygiene. The population in schools keeps increasing as many children from the rural areas move to town together with their parents to pursue their education, this has lead to the continues increase in the needs of the population in the domain of WASH among many other needs.
Project Objectives
The main objective of this project is to reach out to forcibly displaced persons and vulnerable host communities affected by the crisis with assistance in the area of WASH. Specific objectives include provision of portable drinking water in most efficient ways (by borehole drilling, rehabilitation of existing boreholes, construction and rehabilitation of catchment areas, based on the context), provision of sanitation facilities including provision of community emergency latrines based on context, hygiene promotion based on situational analysis and distribution of hygiene kits as well as menstrual hygiene management materials for young girls and women, also provision of waste management material.
Project Activities.
To achieve the expected result, the following inputs will be put in place.
• Provision of safe drinking water in most efficient way by borehole drilling in communities to serve the community inhabitants.
• Construction of latrines in community spaces and safe space for children to improve on their sanitation and prevent open defecation.
• Waste management in communities and safe spaces through provision of refuse trash cans to some communities and safe spaces
• Provision of hand washing facilities in communities
• Hygiene promotion activities based on the situational needs in communities and safe spaces (playful hygiene promotion sessions in child friendly spaces in some communities). Focus is on most critical hygiene behaviors that could have adverse impact on health.
• Provision of hygiene kits to some communities.
• Provision of stock of soap to children in safe spaces (IDPs).
Project target/Activities
This project targets internally displaced persons and vulnerable host communities affected by the ongoing Anglophone crisis in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon. The project areas of execution include both host communities, child friendly spaces and other institutions. At the moment, Caritas Bamenda is providing Asistance in 5 divisions; Mezam (Bamenda II and III), Momo (Njikwa and Batibo), and Menchum (Wum and Weh), NgoketunJia, Boyo. In these areas, 12 boreholes will be constructed, 8 latrines and distribution of personal hygiene kits to 200 beneficiaries as well as menstrual hygiene management materials to 100 young girls and women and hygiene kits to some institutions as well as water storage containers to 450 households.
the number of persons targeted for this project include a total of 28,664.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Based on the results of the need analysis conducted in communities, it is evident that most of these communities do not have access to portable sources of water and people often have below the recommended water need required in an emergency setting. In addition, a substantial number of areas are exposed to the risk of water and feaco-oral transmitted diseases due to compromised personal and environmental hygiene that is directly related to poor provision or substandard basic hygiene services like hand washing facilities/stations in strategic areas of the community, appropriate toilets and urinals, efficient waste management systems and limited access to portable water. In this light, inhabitants often spend longer time to fetch water from other sources like streams, rivers or water brought whose supply are regularly interrupted and not sufficient for effective washing, mopping, drinking and other activities. Therefore, emergency WASH services in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon currently affected by the crisis is a great need which could ameliorate the hygiene and status of these communities. This rapid response shall help limit the spread of diseases especially in the North West Region such as the current reported cholera outbreak in the South West Region.
