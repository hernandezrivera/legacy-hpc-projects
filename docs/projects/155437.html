Project Summary

Due to the anticipated drought children with disabilities are more likely to be neglected as parents and caregivers focus on food. As a result their educational needs are likely to suffer. There is therefore need to strengthen coordination and relationships with other sectors such as nutrition, protection and WASH for provision of disability friendly services in schools.

Needs

Need to safeguard meaningful access to education for children with disabilities in drought affected areas as caregivers are more likely to prioritise other issues and CWD more likely to drop out or having educational needs neglected. There is need to strengthen the coordination and relationship with other sectors such as nutrition, protection and WASH for provision of disability sensitive services during the emergency. Need for disability sensitive infrastructure like accessible classrooms and toilet facilities. Need for disability sensitive learning material.

Activities

Improving Meaningful Access to Education (Facilitating new enrolments of CWD in schools, Development of promoter IEC material and awareness raising to educate caregivers, service providers and communities on the importance of creating meaningful access to education and inclusivity in schools. Rehabilitating exclusive schools-disability sensitive toilets, access ramps). Strengthening coordination with other sectors, (disability service provision coordination meetings at District Level plus @ most affected area/wards. Disability learning material (customising ECD play centres to be inclusive, provision of disability friendly learning material).
