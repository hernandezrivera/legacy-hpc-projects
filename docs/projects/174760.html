Project Summary

Cameroon is witnessing armed conflicts including the Anglophone crisis that began in late 2016. In late 2017 the crisis turned in armed conflict, triggering a complex humanitarian crisis in the NW and SW region. In September 2020 some 409,173 people were reported displaced within these Regions with 360,547 considered as returnees. Female population count for 48% of the total displaced population, children 44% (51% girls and 49% boys), and older people for 12%. This has caused women and girls to have limited access by survivors and vulnerable persons to GBV lifesaving and humanitarian assistance services due to security and operational constraints in many localities especially rural areas. Many family face insufficient means of subsistence, likelihood and welfare resources as they are forced displaced far away from their home and from available services with women and girls face the risk of denial of resources.Due to a decrease in economic and destruction of the social safety nets, women, girls are adopting negative coping mechanism such as sexual exploitation, survival sex, child labor early marriages..There is limited protection framework against SGBV leading to rape, sexual assault and exploitation with poor response systems for survivors of SGBV. Sexual violence is committed by armed parties and also by relatives within host communities during forced displacement . There is limited access to holistic GBV management services due to the a weak referral pathway
The purpose of this project is to contribute in life saving interventions and restoring human dignity for at least 50,000 people affected by crisis and alleviating hardship and suffering by strengthen resilience of at least 5,000 people especially women and girls.
The project will provide holistic support Psychological Support Services to people at risk of GBV or GBV survivors. COMINSUD plan to provide support to 3,500 persons (3,000 women and 500 men) of the 4,250 people targeted in the North West Region. Strengthen legal and medical assistance for GBV survivors in conflict affected areas with referred to legal assistance and received medical support including Post Rape Kit. COMINSUD plan to cover 500 (450 women and 50 men) of 620 GBV survivors targeted for legal assistance and 450 GBV (400 women and 50 men) of 575 GBV survivor targeted for medical assistance in the Region Conduct safety audits, risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV to reduce risk and vulnerability to the occurrence of some GBV incidences and enhance mitigation measures. COMINSUD will conduct 20 of the targeted 28 safety audits, risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV.
Strengthen GBV prevention activities at community-level including sensitization of conflict-affected communities, local authorities, traditional leaders and religious leaders. COMINSUD plan to reach out to 90,000 persons (55,000 women and girls and 35,000 men and boys) of the 110,000 persons targeted for in the North West Region. Provide interventions for the prevention, mitigation of risks and response to GBV affecting girls and women. Reach out to 15, 000 persons (10, 000 women and girls and 5,000 men and boys) of the 19, 700 persons targeted for the Region. Provision of basic vocational training, economic support and livelihood income generating activities to women and girls who are victims of sexual exploitation, provide 200 of them with start up support for income generating activities, place 100 in vocational training centers and as well as and carry out mentoring to all 300 beneficiaries.
The realisation of this project will benefits to over 50,000 persons in need of psycho social support services, prevent over 25000 women and girls including those living with a disablty from the risk of GBV, and provide response support to over 3500 women and girls survivor of GBV and and inviolved in other negative coping machanisms

1 - Besoins / Needs

Over 50,000 persons in need of psycho social support services, with a need to prevent over 25000 women and girls including those living with a disablty from exposure to GBV, as well as provide response support to over 3500 women and girls survivor of GBV and those inviolved in other negative coping machanisms. To respond to the above targets the following needs have to be addressed this project is thus designed to contribute in providing :Adequate access by survivors and vulnerable persons to GBV lifesaving and humanitarian assistance services there is still a significant number of Sufficient subsistence, likelihood and welfare resources for displaced families as well as host community members to enable them access basic needs for entire households and reduce their vulnerability. Support for women, girls, PLWD and families so that they adopt healthy and positive coping mechanism in the face of the crisis. The decrease in economic activities and destruction of the social safety nets has pushed and will continue to put many women and girls in the web of sexual exploitation, survival sex, forceful marriages and child labor. Prolonged closure of schools has numerous protection risks for children and teenagers with consequences such as pregnancies, unsafe abortions and early marriages. Strengthening the protection framework against SGBV and enhancement of response systems for survivors of SGBV. Holistic GBV management services with strong referral pathway with assistance for medical, legal, shelter and economic empowerment support to GBV survivors. Project strategyIt will develop and provide 100 copies of a manual on Psychological Support Services and train 20 case workers on case Management respecting GBV Minimum Standards. It will create and provide support the functioning of 5 safe spaces for women and girls and 3 adolescent and youth friendly spaces and carry out community outreach to identify and connect people in need to service points. It will procure and distribute 1,000 dignity kits to vulnerable women and girls and provide support for shelter, and other basic protection needs to at least 1,500 persons.Further more,the project will produce and distribute in collaboration with 30 actors involved in GBV service delivery 10,000 copies of the referral pathways, and support the functioning of a good referral system to benefit 1,000 GBV survivor for legal and medical support including Post Rape.20 GBV Focal Point trained and supported to conduct safety audits, risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV in 20 sub divisionsThe project will carry out identification of vulnerable women and girls, organize trainings on managing income generating activities, provide 200 of them with start up support for income generating activities, place 100 in vocational training centers and as well as and carry out mentoring to all 300 beneficiaries
