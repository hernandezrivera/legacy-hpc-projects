Project Summary

It has been three years children have been at home without access to education due to the NWSW crises in Cameroon. Education has been on hold by non-state armed groups and attacks, over 80 per cent of schools have been closed, affecting more than 600,000 children. At least 74 schools have been destroyed, while students, teachers and school personnel have been exposed to violence, abduction and intimidation. Since 2018, more than 300 students and teachers have been kidnapped. After traumatic experiences, they were all subsequently released. The targeting of education is putting the future of an entire generation of children at risk, children who with the right support and opportunities can build a more stable and prosperous future. Schools and classrooms must provide safe spaces for children to learn, to be with their friends and to restore a sense of normalcy in their lives.
Another problem in addition to the anglophone crisis which also affected children’s learning access was the outbreak of Covid-19. Increased lockdown of schools in the entire Country made it a dual issue to both NWSW regions. Parents are facing difficulties to afford basic school needs of their children due to little or no income activities. Miracle Charity Foundation (MICHAFOUND)’s data collected from 7 primary schools in Mutengene t a total of 985 children exposed and at same time confirmed the currently problem facing by children. 73.4% were made of IDPs meanwhile 68.5% from total data of 985 had have no birth certificate. All 985 children had incomplete exercise books summing up to 7256 with an average of 7 books lacking per child. Majority cannot afford other needs such as uniforms, bags, pens and above all food.
In an attempt to mitigate the above issues, we intend to distribute learning materials to 3200 children in different primary schools within Fako, Kupe-muanenguba and Meme division, that is 2000, 200 and 1000 respectively. Covid-19 preventive measures shall be put in place such as distribution of face masks and sanitizers to all impacted children. We shall visit 20 primary schools with approximately 160 children per school who shall receive complete learning materials such as pens, pencils, pencil sharpeners, erasers, books, text books, rulers, bags while some shall have extra materials for specific classes such as markers and calculators. Total budget is 91,070USDinclusive of direct cost, staff cost, operational, logistics, monitoring, evaluation and communication cost.
