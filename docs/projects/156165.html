Project Summary

Silk Road Organization will rehabilitate three water stations and associated water networks and will also rehabilitate sewerage networks as well as conduct clean up and hygiene promotion campaigns for two target communities in Ariha sub-district. These activities will be conducted through a combination of direct implementation and also through sub-contractors. Local councils in this area remained intact over the past years, and this investment in the rehabilitation of the hardware will be accompanied with training to the individuals whom Silk Road plans to handover to after the project. Silk Road will promote ownership by the local community (local leaders, and Local Councils) in an effort to enhance the sustainability of the project. Moreover , women, men, boys, and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to improve health through access to sanitation and a healthy and dignified environment and increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions and provision of appropriate, core hygiene items, and good personal hygiene practice for IDPs ,and host community populations in Ariha and Motaram communities, in addition, to reducing the risk of disease through access to essential hygiene items and improved awareness and knowledge of health risks . Once the rehabilitation of the water stations and the networks is complete, Silk Road will provide diesel fuel, engine oil, and spare parts (diesel filters, oil filters, air filters for generator) for the operation and maintenance of the three water stations for the remaining period of the project. These inputs will also include the provision of Sodium Hypochlorite, or liquid chlorine, which qualified staff will administer to the water supply via the chlorine dosing pumps.
Silk Road expects that these stations can run for 22 days a month, and provide water to over 40199 individuals who live among the target communities. A two-person team of Station Manager and a Technician will operate each station and corresponding network. They will be based at the station on a daily basis. As part of the sustainability of this project, Silk Road will work with community leaders to develop a cost-recovery plan that will enable these individuals to be hired and paid (from user fees) by the local councils after Silk Road’s project ends. Silk Road’s field-based Chemist will conduct regular water testing at the wells and at various points along the networks, including random checks at households. At the well, they will check the daily consumption of Sodium Hypochlorite. At the stations and at various points of the network (ensuring at the end of the network at household level where the chlorine residual is most likely to have been exhausted) they will test will test the Free Chlorine Residual (FRC) and pH using portable chlorine testers, as well as microbiological analysis of source water using microbiological water analysis kits.
Silk Road also aim through implementing this project to improve the overall condition of most affected people with a special focus on women and girls through the delivery of Hygiene kits for two times during the project period, hygiene promotion campaigns, conduct waste collection campaigns and rehabilitate sewage networks. The hygiene promotion campaigns will likely focus on awareness of water-borne diseases, basic guidance for personal hygiene, environmental cleanliness and the specific activities and inputs of the overall project. Waste collection campaigns will cover entire neighbourhoods and will last for two consecutive days in each location.
Silk Road will provide capacity building for local councils and water committee to operate and maintain their WASH structures, contributing to the rehabilitation of sustainable WASH solutions, thereby improving the resilience of the population.

Needs

Idleb like much of the country continues to experience significant population displacement, reduction of livelihood opportunities, lack of services such as WASH and decreased market access, all of which place disproportionate pressures on women. However, Idleb’s geopolitical location mean that the combination of characteristics and challenges in the governorate are unique compared to other parts of the country. Idleb hosts a constant influx of internally displaced persons (IDPs), predominately from Aleppo, Hama, and elsewhere in Idleb, as well as smaller proportions from Homs. 43% of the population in Idleb is an IDP , and these IDPs are more likely to have been displaced multiple times . Acording to HNO 2019, Idleb Governorate, as well as surrounding NSAG controlled-areas, have become extremely crowded amidst the significant displacement to and within those areas. Increased pressure on already overstretched WASH services has resulted in an increased dependency on alternative and often unsafe water sources that significantly increase the risk of waterborne diseases, also it was mentioed in Dara’a and Idleb where only around 13 per cent of the population is served exclusively via piped water, as well as , Reports for Idleb indicate that in the first half of 2018, there was a 100 per cent increase in the number of cases in comparison with the number of reported cases last year. The high proportion of water-borne diseases in some communities, including across Idleb or last year outbreak of acute bloody diarrhea in some areas of Deir-ez-Zor, are examples of some of these risks.According to the need assessment conducted by Silk Road team in Ariha and Motaram communities which are remote and rural, and as a result, vulnerable segments of the population lack essential services such as WASH. Most of people depend on water traking with high costs to secure their water needs which are unsafe and unclean. Power outages and irregular sources of power (electricity/fuel) for generators to operate water systems is a major constraint for water supply, as is slight damage to water infrastructure and lack of spare parts, technical expertise and other resources for much needed repairs and maintenance. Sanitation systems also need rehabilitation that can seriously affect these communities because they do not work properly and have many failures. The current situation in the targeted communities has reduced the quantity and quality of water available for consumption, personal hygiene practices, and domestic cleaning. In addition, limited access to essential hygiene products such as soap, sanitary towels, and diapers combined with strained economic capacity inhibited good regular practices amongst the affected population, where conditions exacerbate the risk of diarrhoea and other communicable diseases. WASH service quality needs to be increased assurance, rehabilitating and equipping the water wells and water systems in the two targeted communities with, Submersible pumps, water pipes, cables for submersible pumps and electrical panel boards, electronic chlorine dosing pump, repairing centrifugal pumps, install valves, electrical Control panels, rehabilitating water networks, supply and install all needed parts for rehabilitation of the two water wells, Supply and install Centrifugal Pumps and operate the two water wells by providing diesel fuel, engine oil, spare parts, and Sodium Hypochlorite.

Implementation of activities

Silk Road will finalize necessary job descriptions and complete recruitment in the first month of the project. They will organize on-boarding orientation and training for new staff to ensure that the project team and senior management are clear on the project goals and work plans. The baseline assessment will be a basic KAP survey aimed at bench marking all WASH indicators for this project and informing the design of the hygiene promotion campaigns. Silk Road’s Gaziantep based M&E Coordinator will provide remote training and support to the Field M&E Assistants, who will be responsible for data collection. Silk Road will hire a contractor to rehabilitate three wells and their corresponding networks. As well as we will hire a contractor to rehabilitate sewage network. The works require new submersible pumps, improvements to network cables, replacement of galvanized water pipes, PVC pipes, and installation of electrical panel boards and generators. The electrical panels and generators will be enclosed in cement rooms, which will be equipped with theft prevention steel doors and locks. Silk Road’s field-based engineers will oversee the contractors through daily monitoring visits, and along with the M&E assistants, they will approve the contractors’ completion of work in accordance with the contract. Once the network is rehabilitated.Once the rehabilitation of the water station and the network is complete, Silk Road will provide diesel fuel, engine oil, and spare parts (diesel filters, oil filters, air filters for generator) for the operation and maintenance of the three water stations in Ariha for the remaining period of the project. These inputs will also include the provision of Sodium Hypochlorite, or liquid chlorine, which qualified staff will administer to the water supply via the chlorine dosing pumps. Silk Road’s field-based engineers will be responsible for monitoring the consumption of fuel, and engine oil, and will also conduct periodic maintenance of the generators. Silk Road monitoring will ensure there is no fraud related to the fuel deliveries. Also and during the operation period, Silk Road’s field-based Chemist will conduct regular water testing at the well and at various points along the network, including random checks at households. Local councils and water committee will have the capacity building to operate and maintain their WASH structures, contributing to the rehabilitation of sustainable WASH solutions, thereby improving the resilience of the population and development of plans for their sustainability. Regarding hygiene promotion and waste collection campaigns, Silk Road will hire, train, and equip 40 temporary workers who will conduct the campaigns (20 workers per campaign) whereas hygiene promotion campaign will likely focus on awareness of water-borne diseases, basic guidance for personal hygiene, environmental cleanliness and the specific activities and inputs of the overall project. The M and E team will remain present throughout the various activities of the project. Silk road organization will be responsible for the direct implementation on the ground, procurement of the materials for the project (per Silk road organization procurement standards), the hiring of project staff (based off of criteria agreed upon with Silk road organization), installation of the equipment, rehabilitation of the water systems, hand over to the local council as well as supervision of the O and M.The cost – recovery plan will initially be managed by Silk Road, but the program design entails handing this over to the local council. Together, Silk Road and local councils will broadly disseminate the cost-recovery plan details to promote community ownership through contribution to these critical WASH systems, and to create a sense of transparency around the criteria scale.

Protection Risk/Threat 4: Risk of theft of chemicals such as chlorine/Sodium Hypochlorite which may be attractive to armed groups.Mitigating Measure: Silk Road provide feedback and accountability mechanisms to users;Work closely with all partners on the ground;Promote distribution stock between multiple warehouses in the target regions (for large quantities of stock).Promote distribution stock between multiple warehouses in the target regions (for large quantities of stock).Protection Risk/Threat 5: Movements of garbage trucks in congested, camp-like settings; Reliability of services.Mitigating Measure: Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes, and schedule; Provide household level solid waste containers/plastic bags to ease disposal of waste from H/H to the public SW collection points. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks1: Corruption in procurement by employees, suppliers or contractors or change in required specificationsMitigation Measures:1- Application of procurement policies and procedures2- Continuous follow-up of procurement3 -Check before receiving4- Establishment of a Procurement Committee5- Using Goods/services received notes 6 -Ensure a thorough quality check of items received vs sample agreed uponRisks 2: Target areas are inaccessible or insecure because of the advance of armed groups and/or aerial shelling.Mitigation Measures:1. Ensure there is a process established that allows consulting the communities on how to proceed with the proposed response in a deteriorating security environment.2- Monitoring of the security situation; extensive acceptance measures to sustain good relations with communities for a continuous understanding of local context and to facilitate unimpeded access to target areas.3- Temporarily suspend operations until the situation becomes clear.Risks 3: Increased armed conflict due to increased tensions between different groups. This could delay, pause, or suspend programming. Mitigation Measures:If this reaches Silk Road’s project towns, they will pause activities and wait until the situation on the ground becomes safer. Risks 4: Inflation, especially the cost of fuel and construction materials, may result in higher than planned prices. If prices spike too high, then SRO may not have the budget to complete the project.Mitigation Measures:Silk Road will monitor inflation and prices and include this risk in its contracts with service providers. If inflation arises, they will work with the donor to re-align the budget and program accordingly. Risks 5: Market System Disruption (due to changes in conflict dynamics, border closures interrupting supply chains, etc.). This could cause a delay in the implementation of some activities of the project and/or inability of Silk Road to complete the project.Mitigation Measures:Include ability to ensure procurement as a criteria in the contractor's bid analysis. Consider using fixed prices and placing this risk in the contractor's contracts.Conduct a strong and constant market monitoring before starting any local procurement to have early warning of potential lack of supplies, and also to map out alternative supply. To mitigate against lack of contractors/quotations, Silk Road should hire a qualified engineering team who can implement in case contractors are unavailable.
