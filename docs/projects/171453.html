Project Summary

Somalia has been prone to severe flooding, armed conflict and violence, protracted displacement, inequality and marginalization pushing many to poverty across the country. This situation has been worsened by the COVID19 pandemics and climatic shocks leading to severe flooding and less than minimal rains in most parts. Lack of safe and clean drinking water has led to disease outbreaks such as Cholera/AWD, Polio among others increasing the already worse health indices. sections of the communities in Somalia including elderly and persons with disabilities face additional challenges and barriers including environmental barriers, distance, unfriendly infrastructure among others which increases their vulnerability.
The targeted populations has 265,413 people in need of WASH services and out of this, Active in Development Aid plans to target 29,182 vulnerable populations in Gedo and lower Jubba as well as Galgaduud regions of Somalia. The projects key activities will include provision of emergency water services through water tracking, rehabilitation of existing water sources, rehabilitation and construction of household/communal and institutional latrines as well as hygiene promotion. each location will have water committees who will be chosen by he target communities following the WASH guidelines and will be trained on proper water management. The project will be implemented to ensure all members of the community including persons with disabilities have equal access to WASH services. ADA is similarly submitting HRP proposals in health, nutrition, livelihood, education and protection in the same target areas hence the project will be integrated and have synergy with these other thematic areas. ADA will engage with the community through FGDs for the different sexes and ages to ensure accountability and that their needs are catered for and able to benefit from the project. To ensure people with disabilities benefit equally from the project, ADA will design disability friendly infrastructure such as ramps, safer latrines, hand washing facilities among others. Data on usage of WASH services by the people with disabilities will be collected and shared with cluster/donor.

Mitigation Measures

Our mitigation strategies will include community engagement of all groups of people especially marginalized groups to ensure a joint transparent process in the response mobilization phase. Focused group discussions will be conducted with gender/age segregation so that people of different ages and sex are freely able to provide their inputs. ADA will seek to strengthen of gender inclusive community-based protection mechanisms. Information campaigns will be boosted to ensure impartial and timely access to WASH services, especially in situations of displacement. ADA will as much as possible with community inputs try to ensure water sources are away from military installations as well as reduce the distance between the water sources and human dwellings to ensure protection for women and girls. ADA will use evidence-based advocacy, especially with authorities responsible for the implementation of durable solutions for displacement affected communities. ADA will build trust in the efficiency of our Complain and feedback mechanism to identify any situation of exclusion, and to condition implementation to be an equal and ensure direct access to all communities. We will ensure that complaints are followed up immediately and corrective measures are implemented on time. In site planning by provision of solar lights around water and sanitation facilities will reduce risk. ADA will ensure to always follow the AAP and always prioritize the ‘do no harm’ principle. The organization will train all its employees and contractors on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse to ensure no beneficiary is exploited.
