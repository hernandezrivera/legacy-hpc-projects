Project Summary

This project is to add value to improvement of education environment for children
effected by conflict and disaster affected boys and girls in South, East Darfur and
Khartoum specially in return areas, by ensuring schools availability and capacity in term
of classroom availability and quality of the existed classroom, and improve access to
inclusive high quality of basic and secondary education, and improving and applying
child protection measures.
The project will make sure the standard of Ministry of Education met in all activities
implemented, the involvement of the MoE in each stage of the implementation.
The project will work also to emphasize WASH issues and make sure target groups are
aware of COVID19 prevention and apply them.
