Project Summary

The project aims to improve dietary diversity at household level and particularly targeting those households with pregnant and lactating women and children under 2 years, older people over 60 years, people with disabilities and female-headed households, in Rohingya Refugees and host communities through home gardening and provision of poultry. Households with children under two and PLW are targeted because these population groups have increased nutrition needs. In addition households with limited livelihood options such as the elderly, women headed households and people living with disabilities will be targeted. Supporting home gardens and small livestock will ensure that households have improved access to diversified nutritious diets.
The project has a SBCC component to increase knowledge on nutrition and improved MIYCF practices. The project will support awareness sessions with pregnant and lactating women, husbands and mother in laws of Mother Care Group members to assist them for IYCF activities and practices. Furthermore, caregivers (mothers and fathers) who specific challenges with IYCF will be identified and referred for individual one to one counselling on IYCF.
In the project areas, Concern will institute a strong system to accountability to affected population (AAP), during the startup phase Concern will give information to project beneficiaries, provide channels for beneficiaries to provide feedback on project activities. During implementation, participatory methodologies will be used including focus group discussions with the different populations groups.
To ensure that communities are resilience sustainable activities such as gardening, livestock rearing will ensure that communities are prepared for any shocks. Key information on monsoon will be shared with the communities for them to be ready and prepared. This project will be implemented within the sector guidelines and reports and program updates will be shared with the nutrition sector to ensure there will be no duplication of activities with other partners. Concern Worldwide have a contingency plan for each of the project activities. When any disaster happen Concern will continue support to the beneficiaries.
