Project Summary

The project intends to deliver life saving static and mobile services to the most vulnerable men women, boys and girls affected by HLP issues attributed to conflict , displacement , economic decline and COVID 19. This comprises of returnees, Host community and internally displaced population living in Jonglei state ( Pibor county),Warrap ( Tonj East counties) and Upper Nile ( Longochuk and Maiwut counties ) The total target population is 11,351 most vulnerable individuals ( 7000 women and 3,351 men,500boys and 500 girls). Out of this 1351 are people living with disability ( 520 Men,480 men ,200girls,151boys).The primary purpose of the project is to address compounding effects of converging factors related to land and property ownership i.e loss of livelihoods/land, displacements, protection threats and lack of services which contributes and aggravates HLP issues.This project will focus on both preventive and responsive aspects of . The project activities focus on establishment of legal clinics, strengthening Land dispute resolution mechanisms, legal awareness and counselling, case management , Cash based Intervention and integrated sectoral activities which contribute to mitigation of HLP issues In addressing such issues and other Housing, Land and Property matters, CIDO will establish accountability to affected population Committees in all project locations, who will conduct meetings with Local authorities, chiefs and Protection Partners to map existing and potential HLP issues in the proposed project locations followed by HLP messaging. CIDO will conduct rapid HLP assessments with focus on the immediate shelter and protection needs of affected people and the potential risks they face, including their access to different shelter types, security of tenure, including risk of forced eviction. The assessment report will inform ways of engaging the local authority and raising awareness on HLP rights among crisis-affected populations, including the services available to support claiming such rights (e.g., legal assistance, dispute resolution and shelter assistance) and local rules and regulations on HLP matters (e.g., rental/lease agreements). .CIDO will work closely with GBV , FSL ,S/NFI WASH and Education partners to ensure an integrated approach for a holistic service delivery that entrenches resilience and sustainability of the services rendered to the affected population. The intervention will be guided by the IASC guidelines for HLP ,national SOPs and cluster strategy. The response is strategic to the attainment of Cluster objectives of ensuring host communities, IDPs and Returnees, including women and persons with disabilities, have increased knowledge of and ability to exercise and enjoy housing, land and property (HLP) rights. In addition ,the activities align to the objective of the HRP 2022 .CIDO will be guided by the cluster safety audit while mainstreaming conflict sensitivity and AAP. CIDO will ensure protection mainstreaming through adhering to protection principles to capture needs of vulnerable people like the old and the people living with disability. Considerations will be made to ensure the DO No HARM principle is exercised.

1 - Needs

The HNO of 2022 has highlighted housing , land and property (HLP) issues affecting communities in South Sudan. The major HLP issues facing returnees , IDPS and Host community of people living in Pibor, Tonj East, Maiwut and Longechuk are derived from conflict, natural calamities and lack of implementation of legal frameworks on land hosing and property. According to the 2021 report produced by the shelter and NFI cluster ,Urban and peri-urban areas of South Sudan are witnessing an often systematic take-over of land and properties belonging to displaced populations by actors to the conflict. This also includes the unlawful transfer and sale of confiscated properties for profit. Likewise, many IDPs and returnees, for lack of other shelter options, are now occupying Houses left behind by those displaced. 80% of these occupants reported to be involved in land disputes. There is need to establish Dispute resolution mechanism and train local authority and community leaders on HLP and alternative dispute resolution mechanisms . Pibor county has witnessed multiple shocks including the recurring seasonal floods which has damaged property and housing. Similarily, Majority of houses and property in Maiwut and Longochuk counties have been affected by the severe 2021 floods. According to REACH Humanitarian Situation Monitoring in Warrap (January-March 2021), in Tonj East, KIs in 50% of assessed settlements where the presence of IDPs was reported, reported that more than half of IDPs were living in the open without shelter attributed to the 2021 severe floods and inter-communal conflict. Majority of population still do not understand their housing land and property rights and do not know where to seek redress in case of violation. There is need for CIDO to conduct outreach and awareness raising campaign on HLP .Most vulnerable households cannot afford to take cases of land to court or even pay for a service of a legal counsel. Host communities have also seen a lot of unprocedural encouragement on their land by IDPS and also government . There is need for CIDO to support communities with legal aid and legal counselling. While evidence points to increasing acceptance of women’s rights to property, December 2021 gender analysis research by CIDO and DRC shows that there has been little change with regards to their ability to independently obtain and exert tenure rights, both in rural and urban areas for widowed women. The majority of women continue to access holdings exclusively through a male relative as required by customary law. CIDO will ensure inclusion of women and promote protection mainstreaming through adhering to protection principles to capture needs of vulnerable people like the old and the people living with disability. Considerations will be made to ensure the DO No HARM principle is exercised. CIDO is keen to work with HLP TWG to strengthen governance structures on HLP rights including on the development of progressive and nuanced national-level legislation and continuous advocacy for the adoption and review of HLP related legal frameworks.

3 - Project indicators

The following key indicators will be used to measure the project : # of dispute resolution mechanisms strengthened or established. # of local authorities and relevant institution's staff trained on HLP, dispute resolution and ADR# of people supported with legal aid# of people supported with legal counselling# of people of reached through HLP awareness raising and outreach activitiesCIDO's M&E officer will lead the development of a monitoring plan for this project guided by the logical framework of the project .This project will be managed by project officers in each location who will monitor the project progress on a daily basis . The success of the project will be measured through various monitoring activities which will include site visits and where applicable monitor from the baseline surveys. The M&E department will use various tools like use of Questionnaires, Key informant interviews ,observation and focus group discussions to assess the progress of each activity .The M&E tools are tailored to capture information from people with disability, women, men, boys, girls and the elderly on how the project has impacted their lives. CIDO will be keen to capture progress from means of verification (MOV) . Data analysis will focus on receiving both qualitative and quantitative data and project feedback from all people of both gender, all ages and people living with disability in a participatory manner. The M&E team will compile reports and share with state focal points, national cluster and other stakeholders .The report recommendations will be used to adjust the project where applicable and inform future programming.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

CIDO will establish a Complaint and Feedback mechanism and establish Helpdesk during distributions. There will be Community communication andengagement (CCE) before the project begins to harmonise the expectations ofthe community in regards to the project activities vs the available services.During CCE , consultations will be made with communities to establishcommittees which will oversee the distribution, GBV issues, and AAP issues.The committee on AAP will ensure that voices of the most vulnerable are heardincluding people with disability who may be faced with access challenges. Thecommittee will ensure that location of static services like legal clinics does notbar access to PWDs .The team will be careful to provide opportunity to peopleliving with disability, women and girls to air their views through separate FGDsand ensure that they are included in various leadership roles like being part ofthe committees. Feedback from People with Disability will be used to tailorservices to meet their most critical needs.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

During AAP sessions , CIDO team and committee on AAP and GBV will provideinformation on PSEA to all beneficiaries including how to identify and reportPSEA cases. The beneficiaries will be made aware that vendors of CVI, CIDOcontractors, CIDO staff and volunteers are all required to sign codes of conductand commit to protect beneficiaries against sexual exploitation and abuse. IECmaterials on PSEA will be sourced from the Lead (UNFPA) and translated anddistributed in project locations.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

Community Initiative for Development Organization (CIDO) is committed toensuring that frontline staff engage in timely and proper CommunityCommunication and Engagement (CCE) while being guided by theAccountability to the Affected Population (AAP) guidelines all through theproject implementation. CIDO will conduct entry and exit meetings prior to theend of the project implementation to have discussions with not only communityleaders but also directly with all targeted beneficiaries who are women, mengirls and boys to capture their interests and recommendations and asses thevulnerability. In addition, CIDO will conduct awareness sessions on AAP rightsand how to use the established Complaint and feedback mechanisms (CFMs).The AAP committee composed of community members representing men,women, elderly, youth , people with disability will be established to ensurecommunities rights and dignity is respected by all stakeholders. There will be anopen-door policy, complaints, response, and feedback mechanism (CRFM)boxes, hotline and CRFM desks set up at each distribution site wherebeneficiaries will be given opportunity to air their feedback to the project teamthrough using both leaders and open-door policy. The hotline will be linkeddirectly to the management. This will facilitate Communication, transparency,and Community full engagement.
