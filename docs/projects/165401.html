Project Summary

establish an agriculture lab to micropropagation of potatoes in order to produce high-quality immunity and free from fungal or viral diseased seedlings in lower costs and providing the essential agricultural guidance for farmers during the potato cycle seasons and encouraging farmers to give their best care to get the best results

4 - Needs

According to the CCCM cluster the number of populations of the Syrian in north is 5 M divided into 30% male and 70 % female, and 70% of them are displacedAs in the Sphere report, an adult carbohydrate daily requirement is estimated to be 250g of carbohydrate which potatoes is one of main the sources according to the Syrian culture. The needs of potato seed per year in NWS is 13000 -15000 tons.Cultivated areas 10000 hectaresThe number of farmers is about 2500-3000 farmers Sources: Gossum

5 - Implementation of activities

Producing plantlets of potatoes using tissue culture and micropropagation techniques.Provide GOSSOM foundation with free disease plantlet to allow them to proceed in producing seedlings tubersCommunity outreach activitiesProvide support services and monitoring results from fields

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The main risk is child labor in farms, so if we watch any child of the beneficiary family we will send it to Self-learning projects SLP or Child Protection in our organization or other related org

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

*Security situation, Bombardments, stopping the work, security changes of which will be mitigated through:-Evacuation plan/ ready-Daily security update-Good location of the center as consulted to best avoiding possible risks-safety and security officer dedicated to follow up with the buses and assure their daily trips are safe *Financial risks: Materials, services prices increase, transfer fees increase, we have a well-established financial control, procurement and anti-fraud policy to ensure the duties are sufficiently segregated for the benefit of transparency, control and avoidance of any frauds. In terms of disruption to our operation due to shortage of required items, we estimate the risk to be low in that the required food items and kitchen tools are available in the market. In addition, the risk is minimized by having more than one supplier to work with OB.*Case referrals system to be not well active in the area-already communicated the protection cluster to make sure of finding the service providers
