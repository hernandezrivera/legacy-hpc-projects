Project Summary

The fragility of Somalia over the past 30 years has resulted in weakening of the public sector, its systems and personnel, with a subsequent focus on emergency response interventions to recurrent crises. Lower Shabelle is the most AWD/Cholera affected region among others in Somali regions. Further, people who lack access to basic WASH services continue to use contaminated water with poor hygiene and sanitation services in SWSS.
The burden of disease is heavily dominated by waterborne diseases coverage is considerably low e.g. Acute Watery Diarrhea/cholera, Malaria, Bilharzia in riverine villages in the different districts of Lower Shabelle region including in IDP settlements IDPs in Marka, Qoryooley, Wanlaweyn, Barawe and Kurtunwarey.
The Project will strengthen behavior change and risk mitigations of aforementioned diseases including AWD/Cholera and COVID-19 through commitment of hygiene promoter’s effort by collaborating with MOEWRS,WASH, religious leaders, Women groups, youth, to emphasize on best hygiene practices.
The project will enhance access to water supply through voucher method to promote local economy and market systems, construction of new and upgrading of existing water supply systems. in addition to constructing sanitation facilities, hygiene promotion, hygiene kits distributions and chlorination/disinfection of water both at the source and household levels.
The Lack of adequate and Sustainable clean water remain to be the major indicator of environmental health conditions such as AWD/Cholera that threatens many lives and particularly children under five. Most IDPs often settled in areas with no access to water or far away from the main source of water and they lack both communal and household water storage facility. As such, this programme will enhance water storage capacity through provision of cluster approved jerry cans for household water storage and chlorination at the source. Most water sources have been depleted and access to clean water is not a guarantee in most parts of the districts. To address this, the proposed intervention aims to construct 1 solar powered/hybrid Strategic Shallow wells with elevated water tanks in Lower Shabelle region districts to provide clean and sustainable water.
In consultations with the relevant stakeholders, this project will initiate a piping system from the main strategic Shallow well to the IDPs camps. Shared water kiosks will be constructed and will be managed by trained WES committee who will report to the management of the main well. An alarming water shortage and pre-drought conditions that precipitate large scale displacement is Confirmed in districts of Lower Shabelle region South State Somalia and particularly Marka, Qoryooley, Wanlaweyn, Barawe and Kurtunwarey remains one of the worst affected districts in terms of water shortages due to poor Deyr rain in 2021.
The intervention will provide comprehensive WASH services in line with the Essential Package of WASH Services (EPWS) and build the capacity of technical WASH workers to ensure clean water service delivery that meet the standards. WASH workers will conduct preventive outreach campaign aimed at behavior change including hygiene and sanitation Practices that contributes to prevention of morbidity and mortality caused AWD/Cholera and reducing other outbreaks of preventable diseases including COVID19.
