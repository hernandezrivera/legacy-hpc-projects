Project Summary

South Sudan continues to face multiple compounding shocks and stressors. The interrelated hardships continue to adversely impact the weak health care systems. This is evidenced by some of the health-related indicators, Maternal mortality rate (MMR) is still among the highest in the world at 1223 per 100,000 live births with death occurring within 42 days during pregnancy (MMEIG, 2022). Under-five and neonatal mortality rates estimated to be 98 and 40 per 1,000 live births, respectively, reflecting a heavy disease burden for South Sudanese children (UNIGME, 2022), also utilization of maternal-child health services is wanting.
The country generally has low EPI coverage for the last couple of years as number of zero dose children at national level 7,817 while DPT3 stands at 73% and Measles at 72% below the recommended threshold of 85% by end of December 2022 (WHO Immunization Portal report 2022). Cold chain availability and Limited health facility functionality, capacity, and poor coverage and transmission of infections prevented by immunization like measles remain very high. ISNA 2023, indicates that the main barriers to access and utilization of care services were long distance to health facility and lack of transport (62.2%), long waiting time for services (36%) and insecurity or security concerns (6%) (floods, and high cost of healthcare (mentioned by host communities, IDPs, and returnees).
The 12- month project proposed by Medicair will align her responses with Health Cluster strategic objectives 1). Improve equitable access to life-saving essential quality health care services including maternal, child, and adolescent health, sexual and reproductive health, treatment of common illnesses, disability, MHPSS, and GBV health services to crisis-affected populations. 2) Reduce excess morbidity and mortality by timely detection and coordinated response to epidemic prone diseases. 3 Enhance resilience and promote humanitarian-development linkages to strengthen health system recovery and coping mechanisms to vulnerable population of Akobo, Uror and Panyijiar County.
Medicair will collaborate and work closely with State Ministry of Health and County Health Departments to deploy static and mobile teams to provide health care services in the targeted locations (1 PHCUs and 2 mobile teams in Akobo, 1 PHCU and 2 Mobile teams in Uror and 1 PHCU and 2 mobile teams in Panyijiar County) to improve accessibility of hard-to-reach areas.
The target Population will include 50,224 in total men 10,547 (21%), women 12,556 (25%), boys 13,058 (26%) and girls 14.063 (28%) while persons with disability being 7,534 (15%) M, 1,582. W. 1,884. Boys. 1,959. Girls. 2,110. (52%) 26,116 of the population will be women of the targeted population.
The activities include i) carry out disease surveillance, prevention and control including outbreak detection prevention and response, ii) OPD consultation, iii) case management of common illnesses, iv) immunization against vaccine preventable disease, v)Basic Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (BEmONC)(ANC,PNC and skilled deliveries), vi) Clinical Management of Rape, vii) Mental Health and Psychosocial Support, viii) Capacity Building, ix) infection prevention and control, x) community health service linkage, mobilization and consultative meetings

1. Needs

Humanitarian assistance is needed to support an estimated population of about 8.9 million people in need. According to Public Health Situation Analysis report (PHSA, Aug 2023), 82% of health facilities are functional, with 10% providing the full package of BPHNS and 41% moderately functional, with more than 1 BPHNS service available and 12% are non-functional with no BPHNS service available to some communities in many parts of the country. 58 percent of the Bomas in the country are still not having Boma health teams (Community Health system). South Sudan Maternal mortality rate (MMR) is still among the highest in the world at 1223 per 100,000 live births with death occurring within 42 days during pregnancy (MMEIG, 2022). Under-five and neonatal mortality rates estimated to be 98 and 40 per 1,000 live births, respectively, reflecting a heavy disease burden for South Sudanese children (UNIGME, 2022). At least 18.5% households reported losing a relative (HNO,2023). Jonglei state is particularly affected by the same challenges and one of the states with the highest health access gaps, this is evident with Out-patient Consultation slightly above a third (35%) of the population while MNC services particularly ANC standing at 44% (DHIS 2, 2022). Akobo county is engulfed in the already week healthcare system poor access to Maternal and child health services in the county with only 38% (383/1018) of women having access to skilled Birth Attendant in Akobo, 33% of pregnant women attended ANC services. (HMIS/DHIS2, 2022). Immunization reports indicates that measles vaccine for the years 2021 and 2022 remain at 50% below the standard threshold of 85% coverage while 12% DPT 3 dropout rate in the county. The health service delivery, especially the immunization programme has been put under further strain by the Covid-19 and episodes of Measles outbreaking the recent past where the same scarce human resource capacity is involved in COVID-19 and measles vaccination campaigns, further compromising the routine immunization service delivery capacity. It is concerning that in Uror counties, community-based healthcare coverage is inadequate and needs to be improved. The District Health Management Information System 2 data reveals that around 55% of the community health system is not functional, while approximately 58% of the population lives more than 5km away from health facilities. Furthermore, only 50% of the population can access health services within an hour, hindering access to health care services. Therefore, there is a pressing need to increase coverage and availability of community-based health service provision to address this issue.In Panyijar county, 80% of the total population is likely to experience Crisis (IPC phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity (April-July,2023 IPC) due to localized conflicts, revenge killings, cattle raiding and intercommunal conflicts disrupt normal livelihood. Unity state is among the worst affected with an estimated 480,000 were displaced while in Panyijar county 5,791 HH (Nyal 3,577 and Ganyliel 2,214) people were affected. Poor utilization of health care services due to cultural believes, leading low skilled birth attendance coverage at 7% as resuscitation, trauma, obstetric emergencies, and childhood pneumonia is limited.Lack of WASH facilities, and High GAM prevalence above emergency threshold of 15% in these targeted counties compound the already precarious health situation, and therefore integrated health service delivery will be key in reducing maternal mortality rates. This will ensure, protection needs, nutrition needs, WASH interventions in health facilities, and provision of quality health services to reduce the mortality and morbidity rates in all the target counties.
