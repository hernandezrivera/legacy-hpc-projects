Project Summary

The prolong violence attacks in northeast region of Nigeria by the deadly Boko Haram has displaced millions and also push millions into poverty and untold hardship.
The violence conflict which started 10 years ago has pushed hundreds of thousands to flee from their homes into neighbouring states and countries, similarly, it has disrupted community conflict management mechanism and the impact is quite enormous on the community and individuals.
Also, both social and economic infrastructures, livelihoods have been destroyed, women, children, elderly and persons with disability has been affected the most and in protecting lives and properties.
Although the displacement has witnessed some level of life saving interventions by United Nations Agencies and international/national NGOs. Despite all the interventions very few actors have considered and paid attention to conflict management and women empowerment, whereas the present situation need urgent and consistent conflict management initiative that will help stem down the tide of violence conflict, counter terrorism, reconciliation, reintegration and peace building.
The social cohesion has weakened due to mistrust and lack of respect for each other or communities’ religious beliefs and political convictions, thus threatening people’s effective socio-economic participation and their overall human development.
The targeted community leaders have little or in most cases lack knowledge and skills to steer successful peace and reconciliation efforts.
In view of the above, there is and there will always be a greater need to develop local capacities in northern Borno state communities for purposes of propagating Peacebuilding, Reconciliation initiatives among the people, Similar there is an urgent need for women empowerment, if a lasting peace, security and development are to be sustained with is in line with ERL Sector and HRP 2020 of conflict management and supporting initiatives aimed at promoting the involvement of women in peace-building and addressing the impact of violence on women and girls.
The objectives in which GREENCODE is initiating this project are
Obj 1: To promote the involvement of women in peace-building and addressing the impact of violence on women and girls in Nganzai and Mongonu LGAs of Borno state by 2020.
Obj 2: To promote the restoration and strengthening of community level conflict management mechanisms and peacebuilding through Early Warning and Early response system at community levels in Nganzai and Mongonu LGAs of Borno state.
The project activities concieved by GREENCODE are;
In order to achieve these objectives, the project through GREENCODE will undertake the following activities;
i). Community and social mobilisation of project communities and stakeholders in Nganzai, Mongonu and Maiduguri LGAs of Borno.
2). Consultation and Advocacy meeting with community leaders, Peace actors among the INGOs/NNGOs and Security Agencies in MMC, Nganzai and Mongonu LGAs of Borno state.
3). 10 project communities mobilised and their support obtained for the project by March, 2020.
4). Training of 10 Female police officers in Nganzai and Mongonu Trained on Family Support Unit Services to female gender violations by Feb, 2020.
5). Conduct training of 80 Community leaders and stakeholders trained on Conflict management, mediation, peacebuilding and EWER in the two project locations by Feb, 2020.
6). Identify and conduct training of 100 women and girls/boys trained on conflict management, peacebuilding and EWER April, 2020.
7). Identify 100 women, girls and boys that are victims of violence that are willing to undertake training for livelihood empowerment.
8). Conduct training for victims of violence on livelihood options with the aim of the empowering and empowered with seed grant for kick-off of small business by May, 2020. The trainers will be grouped based on their different livelihood skills of their wish.
9). Organise 2 Dialogue sessions held with security agencies

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GREENCODE takes monitoring and evaluation as a priority for project delivery.In the implementation of this project at first instance a dedicated and experience M/E officer in GREENCODE will be assigned to on this project. The M/E officer in collaboration with the project team will develop a details Monitoring, Evaluation and Reporting plan and data collection tools for both qualitative and quantitative data gathering.Some of the tool for monitoring this project will include;- Every activities against the project objectives in both LGAs of Borno. state.- Feedback sheet for entering feedback from both the direct and indirect target beneficiaries).- Attendance sheet for monitoring of Peace clubs, Security meeting and attendance at referral centres etc. -The standard ERL sector 5Ws.-Number of EWER Reports collected platformMonitoring of the project will be done daily for some project activities and every month . Also, a joint participatory monitoring will be conducted together with project stakeholders (GREENCODE, donor and community leaders) once or twice in the life of this project.In addition to the above mentioned tools for monitoring, we will adopt and use the agreed ERL Sector monitoring and evaluation strategy for monitoring and evaluating the project. Also a complaint mechanism will be put in place to collect feedback from project beneficiaries and from external stakeholders.In summary, GREENCODE is committed to regular monitoring and evaluation (M&E) to ensure effective project management in line with the set objectives and expected outcomes of the project.Also, a dedicated phone line will be pasted across the communities for target beneficiaries to call and lay complain and share feedback. Simially, Opinion boxes will be places at all the project locations for feedback
