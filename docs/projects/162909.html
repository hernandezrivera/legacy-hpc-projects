Project Summary

An estimated 5.7 million people in Sudan need some form of humanitarian assistance. This includes over 1 million refugees and asylum seekers, the majority of whom are largely dependent humanitarian assistance. Further the situation is exacerbated by the ongoing macro-economic challenges resulting in high inflation - a three-fold increase compared to one year ago. This has led to shortages of fuel and cash, increase in prices of key commodities and medicines and subsequently reduced household purchasing power
In Children, women and girls, people at risk of landmines, and host communities remain exposed to protection risks. According Mission in Darfur (UNAMID) report of October 2018. People living in protracted displacement remain exposed to risks of harassment, rape, or violence in and around camps. As UNAMID reduces its presence in Darfur, protection is one of the key areas of concern, which will require close monitoring during 2019.
Through this project Almassar with their partners (MOE) will respond to an urgent and critical emergency for people affected by the conflict in South Kordofan state and North Darfur, we targeting vulnerable, IDPs, undeserved children, teachers, PTA's, and host affected community.
About 2.5 million children under age five suffer from wasting annually, out of whom close to 700,000 suffer from severe acute malnutrition (SAM). The Government has scaled up response in line with the National Nutrition Strategic Plan 2014-2025, and the number of children who have access to treatment of SAM has doubled over the past five years.

1 - Needs

An estimated 6.3 million people across Sudan were experiencing Crisis (IPC 3) or worse levels of acute food insecurity from June through August, according to a September IPC analysis. Civil unrest and poor economic conditions—including cash shortages, elevated food and fuel prices, and high inflation—are factors cited as contributing to the high prevalence of acute food insecurity. The Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) indicates that IDPs and poor households in Jebel Marra—a mountainous region encompassing parts of Central Darfur, North Darfur, and South Darfur—and in parts of South Kordofan are experiencing Emergency (IPC 4) outcomes during the ongoing lean season. Prices for key staple foods continued to increase sharply in August to approximately double the prices from August 2018 and three times higher than the five-year average, according to FEWS NET. While the start of the harvest in October may improve food security outcomes in some areas, Crisis conditions are likely to persist in parts of Blue Nile, Kassala, North Darfur, North Kordofan, Red Sea, and South Kordofan states, as well as in Jebel Marra through at least January.According to the national program of the Federal Ministry of Health to reduce malnutrition diseases especially among under five years, where there were many interventions, studies, and surveys to reduce malnutrition diseases and to include nutrition services to primary health services. Almassar organization was one of the organizations who included nutrition services to her health centers at Zawia Gara and Mustariha in Kutum and Serif localities. Since the necessity to include nutrition services to those centers because the areas were considered as areas of high priority according to HNO 2019.An elevated number of Sudanese are facing poor nutritional outcomes, due in part to the increasing cost of the basic food basket. Around 2.4 million children ages five years and younger are acutely malnourished, with approximately 700,000 suffering from severe acute malnutrition, according to the UN.The beneficiaries of this project included IDPs and Refugee are the most severely affected people who live in the high priority areas as described according to HNO for 2019. The targeted areas include localities (Kutum and Kabkabia) which has been classified as highly needed and they lack the basic services in many sectors. Therefore, Al Massar ORG is targeting Kabkabia locality, Al Salam IDPs Camp, returnees and host communities in Jawrai & Thanbiel villages.The target population is in urgent need of health, nutrition, and caring support. The target area suffering from the prolonged conflict and currently both chronic & acute malnutrition increased amongst the population. This increase has resulted in reaching the international threshold in wasting 15%.The beak of malnutrition in previous years has to be around the rainy season (May-August) & thus could hunt at increasing malnutrition during the 2019 dry & rainy season above 15%. Al Massar response strategy to address the target population needs will include implementation of active case finding, screening and referrals of SAM/MAM& SAM, IYCF and complementary feeding covers the overall PLW population, this in addition to the provision of treatment services.Also Through this project Almassar with their partners (MOE) will respond to an urgent and critical emergency for people affected by the conflict in South Kordofan and North Darfur, we targeting vulnerable, IDPs, undeserved children, teachers, PTA's, and host affected community. This project also ensure that school age children affected by emergencies (including conflict, natural hazards), particularly children with disabilities, continue to get access to safe and quality formal and non-formal education with targeted interventions. The 2020 HNO has identified 1,461,032 people in need of access to education.
