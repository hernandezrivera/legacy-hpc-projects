Project Summary

The project aims at improving health service provision in areas where health services are limited or overstretched, as well on the prevention of disease outbreaks in high risk areas and dwellings, through strengthening existing national capacities, improving access to necessary health items and improving health behavior of affected populations.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project aims at improving health service provision in areas with low health service coverage vs number of residents, as well as improving the overall health conditions through targeted prevention campaigns for cholera outbreaks. This especially applies to the top IDP receiving districts and localities, where health services have become extremely overstretched through the drastic population increase and where high population density with poor hygiene conditions causes recurrent cholera and malaria outbreaks.Under this project, JIA will support the creation of increased emergency health response capacities of the local health authorities and technical personnel through training and complementary response by JIAs own EMTs (Emergency Medical Teams). EMTs are WHO-certified medical teams usually trained in emergency health care. This is a multidistrict project that will have a duration of 12 months.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Training of Health professionals on district level through JIA Emergency Medical Teams (EMT) (Metuge, Quissanga, Ancuabe, Montepuez)JIA is among the biggest health care providers within Germany and has complete WHO approved EMTs available for on point support in emergency settings. These EMT have previously been deployed to support after cyclone Idai. Now, they will complement obile brigades, and provide theoretical and on the ground technical medical trainings to national mobile medical teams. (Fully implemented by JIA)2. Logistic and technical support to Mobile Brigades (Metuge, Quissanga, Ancuabe, Montepuez)Logistic support will be provided I the forms of vehicles, fuel and equipment, as well as necessary payments for staff. (Fully implemented by JIA)3. Health Promotion Community health promotion will be provided through through APs who will receive trainings by partner WWA alongside local authorities, as well as through innovative theatre and community plays carried out by partner Fundacao Quero (Metuge, Quissanga, Ancuabe, Montepuez) (Carried out by Fundacao Wiwanana and Fundacao Quero)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A conflict-sensitivity assessment will be carried out previously to the implementation phase in order to avoid inflicting harm through the action. In order to decrease ground for tensions and conflicts, both, host and IDP community will be participating in the project activites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Meaningful access is assured on the basis of a vulnerability assessment among the IDP population, which will be carried out prior to the implementation phase and which will inform the beneficiary selection. Targeting will thus be need-based, not status based and the project will consider the specific needs that arise from different vulnerabilities

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Effective disability inclusion will be assured through identification of persons with disabilities including different disability status in an exhaustive vulnerability assessment and using the fundamental tools (Washington Group Questions). Persons with disabilities will be prioritized as especially vulnerable group.All project elements will adehere to the standards for physical accessibility. Furthermore, FGD wil we bonducted with PWD or with their families in order to understand specific needs and barriers in accessing health and hygiene services.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project aims too reduce the environmental footprint wherever possible. As far as quality permits, Health items are locally purchased in order to reduce transport and the related emissions and plastic is avoided wherever possible.
