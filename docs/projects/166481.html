Project Summary

This Project is targeted at mitigating risk and containing the spread of the COVID-19 virus in other to decrease morbidity and mortality in Northeast Nigeria, with an emphasis on preparedness and response for the coronavirus pandemic using various means of awareness-raising to Educate and Communicate COVID 19 response and prevention key massages to 100, 000 women, men, Boys and girls in Communities and IDP camps across Maiduguri Metropolitan Council , Jere, Konduga, KalaBalge and Damboa LGA of Borno state within six months.
GOALPrime Organization shall coordinate with the health sector to develop and translate and print tailored key massages on COVID 19 response and prevention and distribute across IDP camps and communities of the targeted LGAs. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall carry out household sensitization with the printed IEC material to educate each house hold in IDP camp and community across the targeted LGAs, Use social media platform to engage with the youths across Borno state on the Corona virus response and prevention with the project implementation period. Develop mass media coronavirus response and prevention jingle for radio communication all through the project implementation time.
GOALPrime will also aim to scale up psychosocial care/support for 5000 boys and girls in the IDP camps communities of the targeted LGAs through community volunteers for the period of six month. The organization staffs designated to this various location will ensure that perceptions, rumors and feedback from camp residents and host communities would be monitored and responded to through trusted communication channels, especially to address negative behaviors and social stigma associated with the outbreak. Preventive community and individual health and hygiene practices, in line with the national public health containment recommendations will also be communicated to the people.
GOALPrime will work in close collaboration with the community leaders, religious leaders, youth and women leaders, health workers, community volunteers, women’s groups, youth groups, traditional healers, camp management teams, camp/site committees and/or community leaders to carry out consultations on risk assessment, identification of high-risk population group, existing trusted communication channels (formal and informal), and setting up of surveillance focal points, as well as community task teams. GOALPrime will through the media team distribute small radios and batteries for locations with or without power supply, educate them on how to use it, this is to ensure that the health information communicated through the media throughout the duration of this project is heard across the target locations.

1 - Needs

In a multi-sectoral and multi-agency joint assessment conducted by the humanitarian community and the government regarding the health status in the north-east. The conflict has resulted in 7.1 million individuals in need of humanitarian assistance, the crisis has had a major impact on the health care system; there are 375 (50%) non- functioning (of total 755 assessed health facilities), 292 (39%) fully damaged, 205 (27%) partially damaged, 253 (34%) not damaged. Two thirds of health facilities in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states have been damaged while those that are functioning are short of staff, lacks safe water, basic drugs and equipment. Eighty percent of internally displaced persons (IDPs) are located in Borno State only, with a majority living in urban host communities. In addition to this humanitarian landscape in accessible areas, most recently the humanitarian community has identified around 1,000,000 individuals staying in hard-to-reach areas with little hope to be reached by humanitarian assistance. In the midst of the ongoing humanitarian activities in North-East Nigeria, a new threat has emerged in the form of a global pandemic of COVID-19, a coronavirus disease caused by SARS-CoV-2, Since the pandemic the humanitarian situation is expected to worsen due to COVID 19, particularly due to the present stressors on the health system including disrupted health system in many hard-to-reach LGAs, non-functional health facilities, lack of health care staffs, overwhelmed response capacity of international humanitarian agencies and donor fatigue.In response to this pandemic, certain measures were put in place to curb its spread, one of which is the restriction of movement to reduce person to person transmission of the virus, However tightening of pre-existing movement restrictions has resulted to economic deterioration coupled with strained public services which will further increase humanitarian needs and compound existing operational challenges. Among the critical factors that might contribute for this rapid spread are; the weak health system, high population concentration in certain urban centers like the city of Maiduguri, inadequate awareness on preventive measures, and some traditional and cultural practices. The impact caused by imported COVID-19 cases in Borno State is estimated to be high due to lack of previous experience in managing the disease and the large numbers of travelers using Maiduguri airport also truck and cargo with neighboring countries. Although the country has the capacity to confirm COVID-19, event-based surveillance and community surveillance are not fully established in BAY State and Laboratory confirmation must be undertaking in Abuja. The fact that the State still have major gaps in its preparedness underlines the need for rapid action and support.
