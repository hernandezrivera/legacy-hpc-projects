Project Summary

The project plans to assist the most vulnerable population displaced by violence in Cabo Delgado province through actions aimed at ensuring the provision of basic primary health care, emergency care and psychosocial services in the districts of Metuge, Pemba, Montepuez and Ancuabe, Chiure, Balama and Namuno.
The planned activities will guarantee access to basic health services to the population displaced by violence in the districts of Pemba city, Metuge, Montepuez and Ancuabe, facilitating health care for the displaced through participation in the mobile brigades organized by the province's health authorities, joining in health consults and vaccination and immunization campaigns according to the established schedule.
special attention will be focused on the prevention of diseases such as cholera, AWD and malaria, being implemented vector control measures, especially mosquitos.
It will be special emphasis health promotion through hygiene promotion campaigns aiming to prevent some diseases, including covid19, focusing in hand washing and maintaining hygiene measures, food handling and water management, including correct use of latrines. For these activities will be very important to create health committees in each of the locations where work, that will be the nexus for the relationship between the population and the project health promoters. In addition will be rehabilitated 6 waiting mother houses (1 per district), to support and strengthen the institutional birthwork program.
Psychosocial health will be taken in special consideration in these districts and especially in various neighborhoods in the city of Pemba, which at this time are hosting a large number of IDP’s within the city living mostly with their families, and yet they need to have access to these services.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The joint and coordinated work between local authorities, Cluster Leading international Agencies (UN) and the beneficiaries themselves is essential for a successful, flexible and continuously developing risk analysis and management and screening it.For this reason, will be carried out a previous identification of potential risks , which will also include a risk and management analysis , through the implementation of a collaborative coordinated work system between the beneficiaries, district and provincial health authorities and with the different clusters involved in the project (CCCM, Health Cluster (WHO)). These results will be contrasted with the previous risk analyses identified by the Mozambican authorities in charge for the assistance to the displaced people (INGC, DPS, Gender Social Assistance and Gender Directorate), as well as the district administrators and the corresponding DDSMAS). The empowerment of people will be one of the crosscutting goals that the project aims through individual and familiar health promotion activities, and involvement of people within the activities themselves, making them part of the decision making process, and not just recipients of the activities, making people the protagonists of their own well-being. For this process will be highly involved the health committees created in each sites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The base of this project is the care of people's health through a combination of active participation and service provision, so it is vital to encourage and empower people to be the engine of their own health care, and taking the control of the activities filling the porject as own. Taking this into account from the beginning and maintaining it throughout the project. This is the reason for the importance of the formation of the APE's, and above all it is the reason for the formation, training and supervision of the health committees in each of the locations and their articulation with the district and provincial health authorities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be located and incorporated into the activities as priority beneficiaries through the implementation of a baseline census of the beneficiaries at the beginnig of the project conducted among health promoters , community leaders, and the collaboration of other national and international partners directly related to the work with vulnerable people. will be placed an special emphasis on the active incorporation of these persons in the activities, being prioritized when receiving health care, and in the development of health promotion and psychosocial care activities. At the same time, will be developed a map of existing barriers to identify and eliminate these, or at least to manage it correctly to allow a full and fair access to health services for all. Finally, will be carried out a permanent screening of people with disabilities and special vulnerability during the visits of the health promoters and with the collaboration of community leaders and health committees.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Respect for the environment will be ensured throughout the project, avoiding any type of environmental pollution, such as forest degradation, and the contamination of aquifers that could lead to a deterioration in the quality of life of the people who will benefit from the activities of this project. The collection and adequate treatment of medical waste materials will be ensured, following the international regulations dictated by the Mozambican Ministry of Health and WHO in the matter of adequate treatment of medical waste. Likewise, the development of activities that promote an adequate environmental sanitation of the sites camps and homes that they host will be encouraged, aiming to guarantee a healthy, clean and hygienic environment. It will be included in all health promotion activities as a cross-cutting element of environmental conservation and environmental health, highlighting the impact that environmental degradation has on people, and why it is so important to avoid deforestation, preserve water sources and what measures can be taken to respect in daily life.
