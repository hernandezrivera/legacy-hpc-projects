The main goal of ‘Education for a Better Future’ ensure that crisis-affected children and youth have access to quality education in a nurturing learning-environment supports and encourages individual growth and the development of vital skills and values.
During times of war, education systems and therefore children and youth, are often on the frontline of violence. The Syrian conflict has had a devastating impact on the educational sector. According to statistics, prior to the outbreak of conflict in Syria, school enrolment was high and “primary grade enrolment was described as almost universal.” However, the first five years of conflict saw the internal displacement of more than 2.8 million children, the destruction of infrastructure and massive disruptions to Syria’s education system. The consequences for children and youth is severe, with an estimate of one in three of all Syrian children – up to 1.7million children - out of school.
In Northern rural areas of Hama and Idlib, there is a broad range of barriers to education. According to a survey of 24 communities in Hama, the most commonly reported barriers to education were a lack of teaching staff (81 percent), Destruction of facilities (71 percent) and a lack of school supplies (19 percent). In terms of the communities, financial insecurity is a major obstacle to access and learning. Limited vocational and educational opportunities, high unemployment rates and rising poverty levels, particularly within the IDP context exacerbates financial strain. A lack of income leads to increased reliance on negative coping strategies; the same survey found that child labor was the most common strategy for alternative income generation .
According to the plan we will provide the education, training and PSS services,
as such Maram’s education initiatives seek to overcome the obstacles to education and ensure 450 IDP,1600 Host-communities, Female 70, Male 95, children(Boys and Girls) 2980 between 5 to 17 years old-(2600 Formal)(380 Non-Formal) and 74 persons with disabilities have equitable and safe access to inclusive, quality education within a protective and enabling environment. Maram’s approach can be understood as encompassing three S’s. Strengthen, Structure and Support, the goals of which are in line with the three fundamental education objectives outlined in the latest HRP for the WoS report. The initiative aims to Strengthen the capacities of nine schools to deliver improved and effective formal and non-formal education to children between the ages of 5-17 Boys and Girls in two ways. Firstly, strengthening teaching capacity through incentivized programming to ensure a balanced teacher-student ratio and; comprehensive trainings for teachers and staff on pedagogical protection, active learning, and flexible teaching methodologies.
Structure refers to ensuring students have access to safe, gender-sensitive and inclusive learning environments, prioritizing accessibility for children with disabilities. Structural activities will focus on the rehabilitation and expansion of protective school infrastructure and the distribution of key materials and school supplies.
It is vital that all children regardless of socio-economic status and particularly out-of-school children have equitable access to learning opportunities. Therefore, Maram’s educational programs offer Support and are designed to be flexible and adaptable to the changing needs of children and youth. Priority will be placed on catch-up classes during the summer and winter breaks, remedial education and back-to-learning advocacy campaigns .

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 2:Weak Education services and process in classrooms- Reasons: Incompetent teachers – Percentage: Poor probability – Solutions: New teacher according to the targets and results required with protection principals training - M&E team will be monitoring the study process in daily basis. Risk 3:Lack of heaters or other heating system during the winter – Reasons: Rehabilitation and maintenance needs –Percentage: High probability because of the old shelling and bombing or long time without using – Solutions: Technical assessment and check for all materials such as stoves, heaters.
