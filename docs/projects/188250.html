Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining essential health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls, women, and men, with attention to PwDs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities through 77 PSWs, 6 MHPSS supervisors, and 2 mhGAP doctors to provide integrated MHPSS services including individual consultations such biopsychosocial assessment, PM+, PFA, identification and referral, psycho-education, follow up, and group MHPSS awareness raising sessions on MHPSS topics, through 33 MHPSS teams (23 HFs and 15 linked mobile units). Selection of these facilities will be in close coordination of health cluster and MHPSS TWG, locations of static HFs include (Kherbet Eljoz !G0704038, Kafr Takharim G0703021, Hamama G0704036, Idleb G0703021, Qah G0703077, Atma G0703071, Atma camp !G0703063, Sandara b0204075), and locations of mobile units include (Kherbet Eljoz !G0704038, Sarmada 2!G0703105, Janudiyeh 1!G0704039, Janudiyeh 2!G0704040, Sijer Mobile clinic 1!G0700147, Sijer Mobile clinic 2!G0700148, Arshaf 2!B0204102, Aghtrin 1!B0204101).
MHPSS activities include Biopsychosocial assessment, PM+, PFA, identification and referral, diagnosis, psychotropics by mhGAP doctors, psycho-education sessions, follow up and group MHPSS awareness sessions such as (depression, epilepsy, psycho-behavioral problems etc.). Most vulnerable groups (such as PwD, pregnant women, and elderly) will be targeted through outreach visits. In addition to the MHPSS trainings that will include training of 120 health staff from different health facilities in Idleb and Aleppo governorates on different MHPSS topics in coordination with Health cluster to select those trainees.
Besides HRO will train 8 CHWs on different health topics according to adopted standards manual developed by WHO to provide services. As for health services, HRO is planning to expand the MHPSS services with adding health (2 PHCs and 2 Mobile units) in the following locations: Armanaz, Darkosh. Health care activities will be provided by 6 doctors, 4 midwives, 8 nurses and 8 CHWs, activities include diagnosis and treatment, Child health services, Prevention and control of communicable diseases, Prevention and treatment of non-communicable diseases, Reproductive health services including ANC, PNC and family planning, nurse activities such as minor surgery and follow up of related cases, prevention services including health education and raising awareness on different topics such as IYCF, NCD, RH, and child health, in addition to the COVID-19 pandemic through conducting household visits by CHWs. All the health staff will be trained on the basic principles of GBV including identification and referral to specialized GBV services to enable the health facility accommodate survivors. HRO through running 2 PHCs, training 120 health actors, and conducting raising awareness sessions for the community members will contribute to improving the resilience of the overburdened health systems during COVID-19 outbreak. All activities will be implemented considering COVID-19 protective measures, besides the health staff will receive a training on COVID-19 IPC.
During the two years (2022-2023), 193,488 beneficiaries (135,442 IDPS and 58,046 HC) will be targeted with the project’s activities including 116,092 Females and 77,396 Males (13,544 Elderly, 77,396 Adults, 44,502 Children, 58,046 Young Children), of them 7,740 are PwD. HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the health NGO partners, local councils, and camp mangers throughout the project period. Community members will be involved in implementing the proposed activities, and multiple channels will be in place to help them report suggestions, complaints, and PSEA concerns.

4 - Needs

Based on 4 need assessments conducted by HRO in NWS 2021: There is still a huge need for MHPSS services as 84% of the respondents mentioned that there are people who still have psychological problems. 15% expressed they themselves have psychological problems. The main problems were depression, adaptation problems, Epilepsy, behavioral problems at children. Most of KIIs emphasized the need for MHPSS awareness, 67% fear of COVID-19. Respondents mentioned the lack of specialized MHPSS inpatient centers, MHPSS clinics and services in PHCs, but no addiction treatment centers in NWS, in addition to the absence of research bodies. Most of them confirmed the prevalence of cultural practices and treatments implemented by religious figures especially when dealing with suicide cases besides blaming and rebuking and hiding the facts and justifying it as unintended accidents. This was associated with the absence of suicide response programs at the level of ministries and health directorates. Recommendations are to provide mhGAP and psychotropics, MHPSS interventions especially for behavioral problems at children, MHPSS specialized interventions through the MHPSS projects such as psychotherapy. In the last 2 years HWs experienced increased stress, placing additional demand on MH services. Non-COVID-19 outbreak risk remained present due to cumulative effects including fear of exposure to COVID-19, cost of health services, over-crowding and long wait times affect beneficiaries.
