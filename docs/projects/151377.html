Project Summary

The project intends to prevent and response to GBV issues through Prevention mechanism and Multisectoral response. HINNA will implement this project in Banadir regions Particularly Daynile District, the main objectives are to prevent GBV incidents, provide capacity building and to provide GBV service through multisectoral response, HINNA has one GBV stop centers in Banadir in Daynile district called HINNA Weydoow Center and currently implementing Gender Based violence services in Daynile district in partnership with SHF.
HINNA will implement the strengthening protection of Gender Based Violence through Prevention and multisectoral Response and increasing the knowledge of GBV Mainstreaming in Banadir region, the project location will be Banadir region particularly Daynile district the direct beneficiaries of the project in Mogadishu will be beneficiaries, consisting of 816 men, 2,104 women 450 boy and 850 girls. the targeted beneficiaries in Mogadishu will be 4,220 beneficiaries including IDPs/drought affected persons, host Communities, GBV survivors, vulnerable women and girls and GBV service providers in Mogadishu.
HINNA will scale up one GBV stop centers in Banadir especially in Daynile district, the GBV survivors will be supported service accessibility and livelihood project, through multisectoral response for 320 GBV survivors will benefit. to prevent and reduce gender-based violence incidents in targeted area HINNA will utilize behavior change strategies through community sensitization and Engagement with Religious Leaders, Focus Group Discussion and awareness rising in Mogadishu for 360 participants, HINNA will Procure and distribute of 1000 solar lanterns and 1500 dignity kit, HINNA will conduct forum debate to prevent Gender Based Violence incidents for 425 community members from IDPs, during the project 300 beneficiaries including GBV survivors and vulnerable women and girls will benefit tailoring skills training also will conduct capacity building training about gender based violence for 75 police in order to highlight the key role police have to play as duty bearers in both the prevention of, and response to Gender Based Violence. HINNA will commemorate 16 days’ activism on 25 November 2019 for 60 participants from stakeholders, as well as HINNA will conduct GBV Mainstreaming to the other Clusters for 180 participants.

Needs

Somalia experienced emergency situation including floods, drought and conflict which caused Gender Based Violence to become highly prevalent and multifaceted problem in particular sexual violence is a serious life threatening protection issue, which hinders women and girls’ personal development and active participation in the public arena and hugely contributes to the low status of women in society.Gender-Based Violence (GBV) remains a serious concern, mostly for women and girls in Somalia. The protective environment remains weak for the Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) and civilians affected by the clan conflicts, regions where the military offensives by the AMISOM and Somali National Army against the Al Shabaab took place, regions affected by the floods, forced evictions and where life-saving services are either limited or facing closure. Gender Based Violence Information Management System (GBVIMS) continue to show high prevalence of sexual violence, physical assault, and other forms of GBV among women and girls, particularly those living in the IDP settlements.According to the GBV sub Cluster Bulletin The rainfall in South Central regions following prolonged drought led to flash floods that affected an estimated 795,000 people including 230,000 displaced and 21 deaths.UNHCR-led Protection and Return Monitoring Network (PRMN) Somalia recorded 407,366 displacements during the reporting period, with April recording the highest number in 2018 at 247,920 displacements possibly due to the floods and the conflict. This displacement situation increases the vulnerability of women and girls to GBV.Daynile district arrived huge new arrivals from conflict area, this vulnerable people are the most susceptible people some of them faced sexual violence in conflict during on the leakage before they reached in the district and require urgent need.

Activities

1.HINNA will scale up one GBV stop centers in Banadir, the GBV survivors will be supported through service accessibility and livelihood project, the project will provide lifesaving to GBV survivors through multisectoral response including Clinical Management of Rape (CMR), Psychosocial Support (PSS), Legal aid assistance/Justice, case management services and material assistance for 320 GBV survivors will benefit.2.Procurement and distribution of 1000 solar lanterns and 1500 dignity kits for those who are at risk of GBV from vulnerable IDPs and GBV survivors in Daynile District.3.Conduct 5 practical GBV prevention and mitigation forum debates on community sensitization for 425 people in total project communities - each session 85 participants will be attending in Mogadishu. the forum debate will highlight GBV risks, consequences, mitigation, prevention mechanism, referral network and service available for GBV survivors.4.The project will commemorate of 16 days Activism for 60 participants from community members and local authority civil society religious leaders on 25 November 2019 in Banadir Region.5.The project will procure 300 sewing machines to provide tailoring skills training for 300 vulnerable women and girls including GBV survivors and drought affected populations in Mogadishu.6.To prevent and reduce gender-based violence incidents in targeted area HINNA will utilize behavior change strategies through community sensitization and Engagement with Religious Leaders, Focus Group Discussion and awareness rising in Mogadishu for 360 participants in Daynile district.7.HINNA will conduct six (6) days capacity building training about gender based violence for 75 police in order to highlight the key role police have to play as duty bearers in both the prevention of, and response to Gender Based Violence in Daynile district.8.HINNA will Conduct GBV Mainstreaming Training to the members of the other Clusters cluster for 180 Participants in Banadir region.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Risk: the GBV survivors may not seek the service available or they are not reporting the incidents due to fear of stigma within the community.The community elders and religious leaders might reject the project due to the negative result and misunderstanding of the project.Mitigation Measure: HINNA will prepare gender-sensitive assessments and risk mitigation measures are put in place before the starting of the project.HINNA has a good collaboration and coordination about the local authority and the community members, the community members including elders, religious leaders and local authorities will take part the consultation of the project.The Coordination results in effective action to protect women and girls, boys and men, mitigate and prevent gender-based violence, and promote survivors’ access to multi-sector services

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The project will benefit all members of the community levels regardless of their age, gender or other differences. In particular, the project will address the issue of Gender Based Violence and SGBV.HINNA will utilize Behavior change strategies of the existing social values, norms and practice which contribute to the perpetuation of GBV and gender inequality in the community especially women and girls are the most vulnerable.The main risk fronting of the project beneficiaries it can be result negative social outcome during the project implementation due to the Gender based violence is a complicated and sensitive issues. Reporting on gender-based violence means discussing issues that are often considered “taboo”. Additionally, project staff in the field may be at risk of GBV themselves in the course of site.HINNA Organization has a good relation to the local authority and community leaders, HINNA will engage to the community leader with their consultation with the implementation of the project, during project design and implementation HINNA will utilize DO NO HARM principle to the staff of the project and beneficiaries of the project and also guiding principles will be consistent while responding to GBV survivors HINNA will apply survivor centered approach and using the existing referral pathway in order to get the project service access with equal to the gender and age group.HINNA will make every effort to strengthen the already started efforts in order to ensure equal participation and benefit based on gender and age.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

HINNA works closely with community structures including community elders and religious leaders as well as government (local authorities) in the project areas, as to mitigate and measure any unexpected conflicts and violence. the community will highly involved in the project implementation and they will come up with solutions for any conflicts including armed conflicts and any other violence.
