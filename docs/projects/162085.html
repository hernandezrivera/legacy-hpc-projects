Project Summary

The programme will provide basic nutrition services to children under five and pregnant and lactating women. It is expected that the programme will contribute to the reduction of acute malnutrition (severe and moderate) rates and underweight prevalence among children under-five years, improved nutritional status in pregnant and lactating women, increased knowledge on health and nutrition matters by beneficiary households

Objectives

Reduce the risk of excess mortality and morbidity among boys and girls under 5 years old, PLW and other vulnerable groups through provision of life-saving interventions to treat and prevent malnutrition

Needs

According to the preliminary results of the fourth round of the SMART survey, GAM rates are still 10.9% almost similar to last year. In addition, anaemia rates are still quite high. Results from food security assessments still show that there is lack of dietary diversity. Currently an outbreak of acute watery diarrhoea has been reported in the camps. It means that efforts to prevent and treat and malnutrition should be sustained

Activities or outputs

Activities:-1. Screening & referral, community sensitisation and discussions, and follow up of under-fives and PLW (absentees, defaulters, poor growth and MAM cases)2. Treatment and counselling for moderately acutely malnourished under five children and PLW3. Malnutrition preventive blanket supplementary feeding programme for refugee children and PLW4. IYCF counselling5. Nutrition surveillance through growth monitoring6. Centre-based and community-based BCC sessions for all beneficiaries and family members7. Conduct surveysOutputs:-1. Number of children and PLW screened in the community2. Number of PLW and caregivers provided with counselling(IYCF included) in the WFP funded Nutrition centres3. Number of PLW and CU% enrolled and provided with BSFP4. Number of children participating in GMP5. Number of BCC sessions held6. No. of surveys held
