Project Summary

the project will provide a comprehensive WASH service for people in four districts. the project will focus on the rehabilitation of the damage WASH facilities and the infrastructure due to the absence of the regular maintenance and unorganized extend especially in main cities due to the displacement movements. so during the project, SHAFAK will rehabilitate four water pumping stations The work will include :
equipping the stations with all electrical devices and;
rehabilitating the management and control rooms and;
extending the water network and maintain the damaged parts and;
water quality assurance and test by provide the chlorine for water stations and measure the percentage of free chlorine in the pumped water.
training the workers about the water pumping station management and general protection
establishing with the stations’ workers the cost recovery system
the project will cover the services related to the sewage systems; SHAFAK will conduct a maintain for the main lines in the sewage networks and extend the networks in un-serviced neighbourhoods. in addition to this SHAFAK will provide the service related to the waste management by supporting the local councils to collect the garbage and install garbage containers. Under this project, SHAFAK will work with local councils, health directorate, and education directorate to conduct hygiene campaigns for children (boys and girls)

Needs

Depending on HRP 2019 for WASH services the results indicate that despite some improvements by comparison withlast year (2018), the results showed that many of those systems are only capable of operating at a fraction of theiroriginal design capacity and that many others remain (partly) non-operational, and many of households are compelledto turn to costly, alternative water sources to meet their minimum water supply needs like water truckers and privatewells to meet their needs of water.This way caused more financial burdens regarding buying water truckers, further than the unsafe quality of waterregarding the absence of water quality assurance mechanisms.Moreover, the results indicate that many stations are out of service because of the broken pumping equipment because of the shelling or stealing actions. Furthermore, the increase in fuel prices played an important role to stop pumping where the local council couldn’t afford to purchase the fuel needed to operate the stations, which caused to stop the stations for many days or weeks till the support comes. Also, the results referred that more than 90% from used. drinking water is not treated.- stop the pumping: the results indicated that many stations are out of service because of the broken pumping equipment due to the shelling or stealing actions. Moreover, the increase in fuel prices played an important role to stop pumping where the local council couldn’t afford to purchase the fuel needed to operate the stations, which caused stopping the stations for many days or weeks till the support comes.The need of potable water still exists, and the proper way to meet these needs is by enhancing the water infrastructure and activating the control quality system.the deterioration of sanitation services:Many reports refer that many heath care centers recorded the many reasons that cause water borne diseases because of polluted water, and mixing the potable water with sewage water because of the destruction in the sewage systems.The lack of solid waste transport services in the cities has led to an increase in the spread of insects that transmit diseases, as well as the suffering of the population from the smells resulting from the collection of waste on roads. The results also indicated that the methods used to treat solid waste are randomly taken into unequipped landfills, causing damage to groundwater.

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk (1): Sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), including harassment, against women and girls by distributors and male beneficiaries while queuing and before/after distributionsRisk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure:- recruit male and female volunteers who are respected in the community and independent of armed groups. No children to be employed (under 18 years old)- make sure staff are already trained on Do No Harm, protection from SEA (PSEA), and child safeguarding; and sign and adhere to code of conduct that respects and uphold the rights of beneficiaries and PSEA- set up communication and complaints mechanisms or desks that are clearly marked; face interviews with beneficiaries, including women and children Risk (2):Tension between host community and IDPs (who were evacuated)Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure:- clearly communicate selection criteria to the community, community leaders and beneficiaries prior to the announcement of the activity.- harmonize the assistance delivered as per Cluster instructions and guidelines.- coordinate with local council in the targeted area as well as being active in field coordination Risk (3): Difficult access for women, unaccompanied children, elderly, people with disabilities (PWD)Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure:- Register all adult women, unaccompanied children, elderly, people with disabilities as main recipients of Kits distributed- During post -distribution follow-up, understand how women, children, and other groups accessed the distributed goods and their feedback on the process and materials

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk (1)Health and safety of the community during implementation or rehabilitation of the water networks.Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure:- ensure through regularly monitoring of the rehabilitation site; - awareness-raising meetings with the familiesregarding the ongoing work; - well-planned interventions (phased intervention) and adherence to strong guidelines for safety and protection. Risk (2)Theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (duringdelivery/storage/ after installation) by groups and used in conflict activities.Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure:- MoUs with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities; -promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. Risk (3) the risk of not having enough WASH facilities for boys and girls. This might cause lack of privacy to the children.Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure:- Work in collaboration with WASH section who will establish WASH facilities in schools.
