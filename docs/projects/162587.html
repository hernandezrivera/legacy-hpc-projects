Project Summary

This project intervention will be implemented in Allait, El Fasher, El Kuma, Malha, Mellit and Um Kaddada localities (North Darfur State) and it will consist of the following activities:
- creation of Community Asset (Cash for Work methodology);
- provision of Agriculture inputs (seeds and tools);
- training on farmers in post-harvest management (provision of storage facilities);
- provision of Livestock Assets;
- Vocational training (women target);
- Refresher training and provision of equipment to CAHWS;
- Support to VSLAs;
- support to small-scale irrigation for dry season vegetable production (solar power energy)

1 - Needs

In 2018, over 980,000 people in North Darfur are classified in IPC phase 3 and 4. While the 2018 seasonal rainfall across Sudan is reported to be on average (2018 Seasonal Monitor & JJAS Forecast), increasing prices of seeds and livelihood assets as well as long dry mid-season spells are exacerbating Crisis (IPC Phase 3) and Emergency (IPC Phase 4) food security outcomes, requiring urgent humanitarian interventions to protect livelihood assets and strengthen self-reliance of vulnerable HHs. Compared to the previous year, cereal crop productivity is significantly reduced across all North Darfur (2018 State Ministry of Agriculture’s post-harvest assessment report).The current economic crises in Sudan (limited foreign currency, fuel shortages, high inflation rate) is projected to persist and will continue to reduce household’s purchasing power and limit their access to food. WFP Monthly Market Updates (January - August 2018) reports that average prices of sorghum, goats and groundnuts are steadily increasing, all prices are over 130% compared to 2017. Livestock diseases including PPR, Sheep Pox and HS are affecting gravely the livelihood of the purely pastoral and agro-pastoral communities (COOPI - livestock vaccination campaign report). Reduction in the animal production contributes to the deterioration of food security. Water for production is scarce with few functional structures, often shared between humans and livestock.

2 - Activities / Outputs

This project will seek to improve Food Security and Livelihood of vulnerable IDPs, Host and residents through:- creation of Community Asset (Cash for Work methodology): 2 hafirs and 6 rock check dams targeting 2.100 HHs;- provision of Agriculture inputs (seeds and tools) targeting 3.000 HHs, including training for 900HHs and post-distribution monitoring:- training (frontal lessons and practical demonstration) for farmers in post-harvest management targeting 2.000 farmers that are going to receive silos and hermetic bags (storage facilities);- provision of Livestock Assets targeting 200HHs (3 heads of cattle for women HH);- Vocational training on tailoring machines and accessories targeting 100 women; - Refresher training and provision of equipment to 72 CAHWS;- Support to 12 VSLAs;- support to small-scale irrigation for dry season vegetable production (solar power energy) targeting 60 women.
