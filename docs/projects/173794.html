Project Summary

HELP IN NEED (HIN) is proposing safe, accessible WASH services along with Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) comprehensive package for two disaster prone districts (SWAT & Shangla) of KPK. The project will reach in total 55000 individuals directly through a combined approach of hard (WASH infrastructure) and soft (IPC) comprehensive package of services to augment government efforts to provide immediatedisease prevention and control support to the affected population that may leads to lifesaving and life sustaining assistance in response to disasters.
As quality of safe drinking water usually worsen during the disasters and accessibility to take water for drinking and hygiene purposes always challenging for the vulnerable groups and they are depending on unreliable and unsafe water sources. Moreover, the situation forcing communities to travel long distances to access water, women and young girls wait for the darkness for defecation as sanitation facilities abandoned due to emergency situation. The scarcity/ unavailability of water has also increased the limitation for Older people and children to practice basic WASH services in such type of disasters; so “HIN” has planned to rehabilitate 15 DWSS and construction of 50 hand washing stations along with 15 hand washing stations in public institutions/ quarantine centers. One DWSS WL ensure services fr 1330 individual nd 200 HH on average. Under this project “HIN” will also construct/ rehabilitate 200 sanitation facilities in public institutions along with disinfection supplies and comprehensive package of IPC for the affected population. HIN has planned to build the capacity of front-line workers/ public and local govt. servants so they response may be scale up in the targeted districts.
Under this project total 55,000 beneficiaries will be outreached including 33% boys (18150), 32% girls (17600), 18% men (9900) and 17% women (9350). Moreover, the WASH response will ensure the mainstreaming of gender and DRR aspects across the interventions scheme, as appropriate to the cultural context, and that the special needs of vulnerable groups are prioritized. HIN will not tolerate its employees, volunteers, consultants, partners or any other representatives associated with the delivery of its work carrying out any form of sexual harassment, sexual abuse or sexual exploitation. “HIN” will use behavioral change model and IPC modalities for immediate disease prevention and lifesaving. Community based groups of women and men will be established to support the project interventions and monitoring of hard and soft component. Community and local stakeholders’ engagement will also help HIN to maintain accountability and sustainability that may leads to lasting resilience for current and future emergencies as well.
