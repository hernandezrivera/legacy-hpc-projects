Project Summary

This funding will support Burundian returnees from the United Republic of Tanzania through reintegration projects including WASH, Housing Land and Property (HLP) and shelter interventions. A community-based approach, including cash-based interventions (CBI), will shape the methodology to support returnees in Cankuzo, Makamba, Muyinga, Rutana and KirundoMuyinga and/or Ruyigi provinces, based on needs assessments. To ensure that social cohesion is reinforced during the reintegration process, the returnees and host community members in the local communities of return in Burundi will be provided with critical WASH, HLP support and shelter solutions, contributing to reintegrate returnees and improving social and economic conditions.
Protection outcomes are integrated within the objectives of shelter/NFI, HLP and WASH interventions and trainings, to ensure that the responses are provided in a way that avoids any unintended negative effect (according to the “do no harm” principles) and are in line with the needs, prioritizing safety and dignity. IOM will also ensure that interventions are grounded on participation – especially of women – and empowerment of local communities. Finally, specialized protection activities will also comprise 1) the improvement of referral pathways relevant to the response and prevention of GBV, as well as different forms of violence, abuse and exploitation, 2) updating the mapping of services that respond to GBV, as well as different forms of violence, abuse and exploitation in the areas of intervention, 3) case management in exceptional circumstances and 4) financial and technical support to local actors to deliver targeted assistance.
