Project Summary

The project aims at enhancing the trauma pathway by providing essential blood units in satisfying Gaza Strip's hospitals needs freely, especially those work in emergency situation or those located in vulnerable areas in southern governorates by the means increasing the blood donations campaigns ,raising the awareness of the blood donation among people to attract more voluntary donors, Pursuant to the statistics of ministry of health report regarding laboratories in 2017 percentage of blood donation shows that 2.3% and the rate of voluntary donors in Gaza strip that 29% of total blood donors
It will target non-governmental institutions, universities and mosques where blood donation campaigns will be launched and implemented. the campaign includes a numerous of activities , such as holding orientation meetings about the process of blood donations and its benefits to the health of individual , its urgent needs in the current situation ,and the eligibility requirements for donating , examining drawn blood samples of the eligible donors and then drawing blood units and examining it in the laboratories of society branches according to the standards of ministry of health and the standards of WHO in the field of blood banks.

Needs

Gaza strip is a tiny area of land with almost two million people packed in here. Despite the 2005 Israeli disengagement from Gaza, the United Nations international human rights organizations, and the majority of governments and legal commentators consider the territory to be still occupied by Israeli. Israeli maintains direct external control over Gaza and indirect control over life within Gaza: it controls Gaza’s air and maritime space, and six of Gaza’s seven land crossings.For eleven years, more than two million Palestinians who are living in Gaza facing the blockade imposed over the strip from Israel and Egypt. In recent years, Israel has launched a number of military operations on Gaza strip, where the latest was on 2014 and lasted for 51 days. Every time the Gaza strip is exposed to military operation, there are hundreds of causalities ,on the other hand the number of causalities has doubled to reach more than 20833 since Great March of Return day –events which begin from 30th March 2017, moreover the situation threatens to go from bad to worse after the deterioration of political situation where the internal split went more deep and the continuation of the Great Return of March day events which are extended now to the sea shores beside border areas. Because the population of the Gaza strip is living in a state of instability and the possibility of any military escalation at any time, the national blood supply must cover every day needs and unexpected escalations.Actually, CBBS support the hospitals with their needed of blood units for people who receive the most blood according to CBBS technical reports of 2018 those:• Around 3218 blood units for casualties resulted from Great march of return .• Around 1700 blood units for Cesarean surgeries. • Around 2500 blood units for Cancer and Thalassemia cases • Around 2700 blood units for surgeries Moreover, and because the CBBS is the only center in the Gaza Strip that meet the hospitals needs of free blood units we need not to sustain only service, but to increase and develop the capabilities of (CBBS) sustainable by using instruments and testing kits with highly trained technical team to increase the supporting for governmental and nongovernmental hospitals with safe blood units freely. The CBBS supports the governmental hospital with 70.1% of total distributed units. According these situations and the limited resources of governmental hospitals.• The CBBS is the only source of blood units for nongovernmental hospitals (around 13 hospitals) which take lesser percent than the governmental hospitals up to 26% of the CBBS total units.• 1 Providing the two branches in Khan Younis and Rafah Govern orates with solar energy in order to keep the units inside the refrigerators of the centers instead of keeping them in the governmental hospital because of long hours of electricity cuts.• 2- Also, providing the three branches with Equipment and medical supplies with trained technicians. Hence, they are the important source of blood units for the hospitals. They cover about 28 % of total units per year. • 3 Providing the CBBS with mobile blood bank unit. Which will contribute in increasing the number of collected units by performing outer blood drives among cities, camps, and universities. The blood donation campaigns participate of 45% of total collected blood units.

Activities

- Carrying out 120 outer blood donation campaigns to obtaining around 5,500 units - Carrying out 120 health awareness campaigns parallel with outer blood donation campaigns- Determining and coordinating with institutes and NGOs which are will contributing in outer blood drive- Collecting around 5,500 through Headquarters of CBBS- Viruses testing for around 6600 blood units in main center- Diagnose Hb. test for 20,000 donors- Distributing the 1500 FFP units for hospitals- Sharing episode about blood donation benefits on social media

Indicators

According to the cases of casualties and patients in hospitals ,the 11000 blood unit freely will distributing as need of each of one of the below group • The blood units distributing for men around 5610 (casualties, cancer, thalassemia , accidents , kidney failure and surgeries) • The blood units distributing for women around 2580(Cesarean surgeries ,cancer, surgeries, thalassemia, accidents , kidney failure) • The blood units distributing for girls around 1390 (Cesarean surgeries ,cancer, surgeries, thalassemia, accidents , kidney failure) • The blood units distributing for boys around 1420(casualties, cancer, thalassemia , accidents , kidney failure and surgeries)
