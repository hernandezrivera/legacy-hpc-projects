Project Summary

The proposed intervention under ECHO HIP 2025 aims to enhance conflict preparedness and protection (CPP) efforts and raise awareness on explosive ordnance risks (EORE) in the conflict-affected districts of Cabo Delgado, Mozambique. Through collaboration with local partners, including Wiwanana, and other consortium members, NPA will build on existing CPP activities to strengthen community resilience, increase local capacity for protection, and educate displaced populations and host communities on the risks associated with improvised explosive devices (IEDs). The intervention will target up to 261,200 beneficiaries across Macomia, Quissanga,Nangade and Meluco districts, with a focus on empowering communities, local leaders, and government stakeholders to better respond to conflict risks and mitigate harm.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project under ECHO HIP 2025 focuses on enhancing Conflict Preparedness and Protection (CPP) and Explosive Ordnance Risk Education (EORE) in the conflict-affected districts of Macomia, Quissanga, and Meluco in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique. The intervention aims to build the capacity of local communities, leaders, and government authorities to better prepare for and respond to conflict-related risks. Key target groups include displaced populations (IDPs), host communities, local leaders, community activists, government staff, and other stakeholders.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Conflict Preparedness and Protection (CPP)Activity 1: Regular community meetings (monthly) between INGD/ SDPI, community leaders and the IDPs/ Host communities: These will serve as a channel to communicate on any risks/ and for disseminating information about protection risks, protocols and mitigation measures to increase community resilience. Activity 2: Mass communication campaigns: These will provide carefully packaged information on risks, and response options, using local radio, local theatre, SMS campaigns and social media. Will seek to reach up to 250,000 persons with mass media campaigns in the course of the proposed action.Activity 3: CPP Training Workshops to strengthen local capacity to implement CPP, increase community protection: Using the NPA Mozambique already developed curriculum which will be tailored to the community needs and context, NPA will conduct CPP trainings/ capacity building workshop in the various communities; 45 persons to be trained in each target district as leaders and facilitators.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project identifies potential risks through a comprehensive contextual analysis and needs assessments to understand the protection threats faced by communities in conflict-affected areas of Macomia, Quissanga, and Meluco. This process includes engaging with local stakeholders, including displaced persons, host communities, and local authorities, to assess vulnerabilities and understand the specific protection risks, such as gender-based violence, recruitment by non-state armed groups, and exposure to explosive ordnance. Through risk mapping, the project identifies how these risks can be exacerbated by the intervention and ensures that the activities do not contribute to further harm. Regular consultations with affected communities help adjust the project design to address emerging risks and prioritize the needs of marginalized groups like women, children, and persons with disabilities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project ensures meaningful access to assistance and promotes the participation and empowerment of women and elderly persons by prioritizing their inclusion in all stages of the intervention. Women and elderly individuals are actively engaged in capacity-building workshops and trainings, which enhances their role in community decision-making and conflict preparedness. The project also utilizes accessible communication channels, such as local radio, SMS campaigns, and community theatre, to ensure that information on protection risks and services reaches these groups effectively.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The proposed project will involve local disability advocates to help ensure that people with disabilities can actively participate in training, workshops, and other CPP\EORE activities, thus ensuring equal access to the assistance provided.
