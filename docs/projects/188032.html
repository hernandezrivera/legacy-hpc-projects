Project Summary

The project is based on supporting 752 male and female from IDPs and HHS and enhancing their economic capacity by securing income-generating opportunities for them, by providing grants to support projects for 440 male and female with previous experiences or whose projects have been damaged by Covid 19 in addition to providing grants to support projects for 60 people with disabilities (PWD) that suits their disability and re-integrates them into their society, and also by providing vocational training for a 216 male and female and 36 of PWD, followed by a paid employment and internship in the labor market for three months. taking into account their reintegration into society.
Additionally 4 markets will be established, 120 CFW workers will work in the markets establishment and 480 beneficiaries SBGs will be trained and 120 grants will be givin in the 4 markets 30 grants in each market.
4 CRCs will be established to follow up and monitor the working environment.
the project will be in the three locations of Azaz, Dana and Maarat Misrin
Additionally, Takaful Alsham conducted a market needs assessment in Dana and Idleb sub-district by meeting Key Informants in February 2021, it was figured out that the difficulties that residents face in accessing markets are: high cost to reach markets, lack of transportation, and the rough roads to reach the market, in addition to, the long distance to markets, where the nearest market for residents was found to be far 13 Km, so they suffer from the long distance.
Also, it was found out that the main source of all the residents is humanitarian assistances. KIs mentioned that none of the residents has received training for capacity building before and that they need it. KIs suggested stores that are needed to residents and community which are clothing selling, foodstuff stores, vegetable and fruit shops, pharmacies, meat shops, and shops for selling detergents.
By this intervention, TAS is going to contribute to the needed response to these big counts of IDPs and youth and provide to them early recovery and livelihood opportunities.

4 - Needs

ERL needs remain considerable in scale, severity, and complexity across Syria, with an estimated 11.6 million people needing dignifying early recovery and livelihood support. The poor state of basic infrastructure also represents a major impediment to the return of displaced persons to their places of origin, pushing them towards harmful coping strategies, in addition to increasing dependency on humanitarian assistance. In this regard, 74% of the available basic infrastructure’s facilities are reported damaged, with 53% of them reporting significant damage [HNO, ER, 2021].Takaful Al-Sham carried out an ERL NA in August 2021 in three Sub-district” Dana, Azaz, Maaret tamsarin) and it was found that most of the markets in those areas are able to serve the population in these areas, 46% of the respondents confirmed that their communities have a hardship in ensuring an expert manpower, while the other 54% reported that their communities have a sufficiency of expert manpower, also, 54% of the respondents confirmed that the disabled persons with in their communities are not working.TAS will follow the recommendation and the procedures of the Cash for Work Minimum Recommended Actions Guidance for NWS and will ensure that no harm was done in the area

5 - Implementation of activities

1st: support projects for 440 (M, F) who have previous experience or have projects affected by COVID-19, where Takaful (TAS) will form 3 Community Representation Committees (CRC) which will receive training to study, evaluate and development of Small-Micro business.2nd: Providing Small-Micro Businesses to support 60 PWD (M&F) who have the experience to set up a small income-generating project which fit with their special situation.3rd: Providing vocational training (VT) for 216 males and females for three months and then provide internships to them in the market for the other three months supported by the project. TAS will update the market needs and needed professions. 4th: Vocational Training for PwDs for 3 months followed by other 3-months paid internship. 120 CFW workers will be selected to work in the market establishment. 4 market will be established using CFW modality. Following the verification activity and analyzing the findings. Then, the selection of the 480 initial beneficiaries who will receive the training on "Business Plans Development" and submit their proposals after the training. These practices will ensure accurate selection and then ultimate success of the supported projects, and 120 will be selected for granting them 1000$ grant each market 30 grants. 4 CRCs groups will be established to supervise the work in the markets each group contains 3 members.TAS will coordinate with the other humanitarian actors in the area to avoid doing harm in these areas

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Several meetings with the beneficiary's representative will be conducted before and during the implementation of the project, where this can help to avoid harm and unintended bad effects.During the implementation of the project, we will ensure that the principle of Do No Harm is applied in all project's activities through several visits to the markets. The project will be routinely monitored to ensure that women and men benefit equally from the assistance including a gender balance and obstacles to equal access and benefit will be promptly addressed. In designing the project, the assessment has been conducted to identify protectionWe will have teams monitor all project activities and respond quickly and fairly to safety concerns including coercion, cheating, violence, or exploitation, including actions perpetrated by project staff.Project staff will sign a code of conduct agreement for sexual exploitation and abuse and child protection. A confidential complaint and protection reporting mechanism system is in place and the beneficiaries know where to refer or report incidents of rights violations in addition to WhatsApp numbers through the MEAL team (male and female). the project manager will regularly review complaints and ensure immediate response and investigations to avoid further abuse. Awareness of GBV issues will be factored and beneficiaries' satisfaction will be verified on service provided.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk 1: The vulnerability of children and women in conflict circumstances highly increases and places them in higher risk of being exposed to exploitation, abuse and rights violations.Mitigation strategy: 1- Staff personnel will sign code of conduct and child protection policy.2- Most of the management and field staff had attended workshops to increase the awareness of PSEA cases.3- the compliance mechanism will be defined and shared with beneficiaries.Risk 2: There are not enough humanitarian staff (both male and female) at the distribution site monitoring the distribution process Mitigation strategy: 1- Ensure oversight of distributions by aid workers who are trained on PSEA, Codes of Conduct and how/where to report misconduct.2- Ensure field level PSEA Focal Points in distribution teams and/or that staff involved in distributions know how to report or refer any PSEA allegations received.3- Deploy gender balanced teams to support projects for disaster-affected communities.4- Consider wearing clearly marked organization logos on shirts/jackets/caps for ease of identity by beneficiaries.Risk 3 : when challenges and issues arose during distribution, they are not handled well, and there is no female employee if the beneficiary was a female1. Train field workers on PSEA2. Ensure female aid workers are present and involved in the resolution of the challenges3. Sensitize affected communities on PSEA and who/where to report
