Project Summary

The water of the Euphrates River in Deir ez-Zor in general and in the eastern countryside is largely polluted and unsuitable for drinking, because of the sewage that flows into the river in all the villages located on the bed of the Euphrates River, and the significant drop in the river water level has exacerbated this problem. , especially the areas located in the far eastern countryside of Deir Ezzor, especially Hajeen district and its affiliated villages, which number up to 20 villages The population in those villages depends on purchasing filtered drinking water from private filtered water stations, which is only available in some areas, and the production capacity of these stations does not cover the needs of the population on the one hand, in addition to the high costs of purchasing this water and the inability of the majority of the population to buy it, especially families poor.
The population's dependence on river water for drinking causes many intestinal diseases, urinary tract infections and kidney diseases. The organization will install filtered water plants (operating with reverse osmosis technology) and operate using solar energy systems, the production capacity of each plant is 3000 liters per hour, and a filtered water plant will be installed in each working water plant in the targeted villages, where 20 filtered water plants will be installed with a production capacity 3000 liters per hour powered by solar energy, and a manhal will be established to distribute filtered water next to each station to make the residents’ access to the filtered water easy, and the filtered water will be available to the residents at all times.
The filtered water plant consists of:
1. 5 hp . water pump
2. 7 hp . water pump
3. 3 reverse osmosis membranes, each reverse osmosis device producing 1000 liters per hour
4. Sand filter
5. Charcoal filter to remove unpleasant odors from water
6. Solar panels with a capacity of 400 watts, 35 solar panels
7. Converter from solar energy to alternating current, 2 (one for each pump)
8. Manhal filtered water distribution
9. Filtered water collection tanks
10. Pipes and fittings.
The organization will train the workers who will work on operating these stations, so that they can operate them in the correct manner after handing them over to the local authorities at the end of the project.
Each filtered water station needs $150 per month as consumables that the station needs during the work period.
After handing over the station to the local authorities, these authorities will provide and secure these consumables, and the local authorities will also pay the salaries and wages of the workers that the organization has trained to operate the filtered water stations.
The water source for the filtered water plants is the Euphrates River

4 - Needs

The eastern region extending from Abu Hardoub to al-Baghouz, which is administratively affiliated to Hajin district, is considered one of the most difficult areas in Deir ez-Zor to obtain drinking water, as the waters of the Euphrates River are highly polluted and unfit for drinking, and this water is the only source of drinking water in the region. Because of this, stomach and urinary tract infections and bladder diseases have spread in the region, where 60 percent of the population of the region suffer from these diseases, according to the data of the Health Committee in Hajin district.There are special filtered water stations in the area that work using reverse osmosis technology, but this water is very expensive and the majority of the residents of the area cannot buy it, as the cost of water per person per day reaches approximately $1, and this cost is not available to the majority of the population.Each filtered water station will produce 3000 liters per hour, and assuming that the station operates 9 hours per day on solar energy, the productivity will be 3000 * 9 = 27000 liters per day, According to the humanitarian sphere standards , the per capita consumption of safe water is between 7.5 liters to 15 liters, and therefore the number of beneficiaries is 3000 * 9 * 20/7.5 = 72000 people
