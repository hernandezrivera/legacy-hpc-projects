Project Summary

The project aims to serve 7680 populations (1600HHs) in three blocks (A, B & C) of camp 27, complementary to the shelter maintenance activities including shelter upgradation and maintenance (SUM), transitional shelter assistance, household level site improvement and regular maintenance of existing Shelter focal point and other shelter actors. During the project implementation World Vision will maintain the effective and representative coordination through active participation in monthly coordination meeting with all respective partners/actors specifically Shelter/NFI Sector, Shelter Focal point and respective AoR.
As per the consultation with shelter sector, needs for shelter assistance in Teknaf (specially in camp 27) is higher than Ukhiya camps. WV will focus on shelter reinforcement/upgradation based on the technical assessment and need identified by Shelter/NFI Sector, Shelter Focal point and respective AoRA. Materials support will be provided to the rohingya for the regular maintenance of shelter. In case of extremely vulnerable households (EVHHs) World vision will engage skilled labors in supporting upgradation of shelters and porter assistance in terms of materials delivery. One designated camp engineer will be assigned to lead the task and monitor the team. To ensure meaningful participation of Rohingya in every stages of decision making community will be involved through community consultation at block level and committee formation. The project will follow all sorts of Government, sectoral, ISCG guidance’s and standards in terms of implementation.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

As per result from multi sectoral need assessment (MSNA) conducted in 2020, 63% refuges considered shelter assistance as priority overtaking food sector which has increased to 74% in 2021 MSNA. This happened due to covid restriction in 2020 and 2021 for shelter maintenance activities. In addition to that, fire broke out in 2021 in three camps affected funding and shelter maintenance has been delayed. Since the beginning of the Rohingya response, the GoB has restricted shelter assistance to bamboo and tarpaulin, which require annual replacement because of their limited lifespan and exposure to flood, landslide, fire and cyclone risks. For the above shelter maintenance needs, World Vision is proposing this project to compliment the shelter maintenance activities of existing partner in camp 27.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

World Vision will ensure project implementation with no duplication of shelter maintenance activities and optimal use of funding. In order to avoid duplication, WV will follow existing coordination channel both in camp and sector. Additionally, World Vision will maintain regular reporting to the sector and camp in charge (CIC) of their implemented activities as per reporting requirement within designated timeline. WV will also actively participate in camp coordination meeting, sector meeting, working group and contribute as per organizational capacity. Also, WV is an active member of PSEA network and the organization has their own safeguarding policy. All the activities will be implemented in according to sectoral design. The project timeline will be initially 6 months with possibility of extension. In addition to that, World Vision has their strategy to operate response in Shelter sector till 2025.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

By ensuring people’s safety, dignity and supporting affected populations to access and enjoy their rights, beneficiaries will safely access shelter services, without causing harm, have meaningful access, participation and accountability. The project will reinforce regular Protection monitoring, establishing mechanisms such as, consultations, referrals, feedback gathering, and training staff to strengthen participation and empowerment.

3.2 - Gender

The project is designed to involve and reach gender-responsive demography such as male, female, people with disabilities, old age, persons with incontinence and LGBTQ. This will be informed by a gender analysis to identify their specific needs and involvement in decisions that impact their rights to access service. Inclusive consultations in gender and age-disaggregated groups will address issues around accessibility, appropriateness, and safety, encourage maximum participation, respect cultural norms, and change the discriminatory norms and service policies that deprive their rights.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

World Vision will ensure following mainstreaming guidance in this project; ● Include female enumerators for assessments and PDMs. Provide information to HHs on the purpose of the assessment and how it will be used. ● Do not distribute to children (under 18 years). Child-headed HHs fall under the SNFI Sector’s EV HH criteria and should receive porter & labor assistance and should be referred directly to CP actors for case management. ● Establish safe location and time for distributions so that beneficiaries can return to their shelters safely. ● Establish distribution points that are safe and accessible to beneficiaries, including elderly or individuals carrying children and person with a disability.

3.4 - Diversity

World Vision will ensure following mainstreaming guidance in this project; ● WV will conduct consultations with the community to understand their shelter needs for regular repair and maintenance. Consultations will include groups considering age, disability and gender-segregated. ● WV will prioritize the meaningful participation of refugees of all genders, ages and abilities and special measures will be taken to facilitate the inclusion of marginalized groups such as persons with disabilities, LGBTQ, youth and women, pregnant, women headed household in material delivery. ● WV will consider budget for providing porter support and construction labour support to extremely vulnerable households (EVHH).

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

World Vision (WV) recognizes that the essence of accountability is to respect the needs, concerns, capacities and disposition of those with whom we work and to answer for our actions and decisions. WV works with the most vulnerable to identify critical needs and seeks to ensure their right to receive assistance and protection based on their informed consent. WV use participatory approach based monitoring tools and techniques- survey questionnaires, onsite Distribution Monitoring (ODM) checklist, key informant interviews, focus group discussions to understand satisfaction of adolescents, women, older persons, and vulnerable individuals in the HH. The MEAL staff will use mobile phone based digital monitoring tools namely KOBO for quality project monitoring. Based on monitoring visits, MEAL officer will produce monthly monitoring reports in the prescribed template with recommendations, which will be shared with project manager for taking corrective measures against the gaps identified.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

The project activities include; regular maintenance of shelters, shelter upgradation and maintenance, and site improvement will directly contribute to disaster resilience. Household-level site improvement will consider risk reduction during a disaster. World Vision will use treated bamboo to reduce environmental degradation.
