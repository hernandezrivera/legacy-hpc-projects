Project Summary

Guarantee access to basic water and sanitation services for the displaced, victims of terrorism in the districts of Macomia and Chiure. (Cabo Delgado Province) In the GBV Component, the project aims to contribute to a dignified life for women victims of GBV for an informed and active participation in the processes of governance, empowerment and establishment of advocacy partnerships.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will guarantee access to basic water and sanitation services to the population displaced by terrorism in the districts of Macomia, and Chiure, by implementing two kind of actions:The first type are those activities to offer a quality and safe service of a) sanitation, through the construction of latrines in adequate places avoid contaminating the aquifers, and at the same time guarantee the safety especially of women and children and considering gender issues, and b) water supply through the rehabilitation of some existing sources and the construction of some new sources in some communities, IDP's site camps, and health centers, whenever possible, including the formation and supervision of water committees where necessary.The second kind of actions are aimed at strengthening the capacities of the people targeted, and improving their hygiene and health conditions including management of water for consumption, maintenance of personal hygiene, and adoption of healthy and safe behaviors, with the NFI WASH related distribution (ex. chlorine, hygiene kits) and mass media WASH message diffusion, and finally, hygiene promotion campaigns aiming to prevent some diseases, including covid19, focusing on hand washing and hygiene measures adoption, water management, and correct use of latrines.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials (Certeza©), including training and monitoring on proper use at household level. • Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accommodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees. • Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas • Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites (first phase) • Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households) • Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media • Procurement and distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, with focus on most vulnerable families / households: ex. Hygiene kits • handwashing promotion and covid19 prevention measures regular campaigns • promotion campaigns at waterborne diseases prevention level: Cholera and AWD outbreaks prevention, malaria prevention, including water management at household level. 5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The joint and coordinated work between local authorities, Cluster Leading international Agencies (UN) and the beneficiaries themselves is essential for a successful, flexible and continuously developing risk analysis and management and screening it. For this reason, will be carried out a previous identification of potential risks , which will also include a risk and management analysis , through the implementation of a collaborative coordinated work system between the beneficiaries, district and provincial authorities and with the different clusters involved in the project (CCCM, WASH Cluster, Protection Cluster (UNICEF)). These results will be contrasted with the previous risk analyses identified by the Mozambican authorities in charge for the assistance to the displaced people (INGC), as well as the district administrators. Above all, it will be taken maximum precautions to ensure the safety of the women and prevent any kind of harassment or assault. The empowerment of people will be one of the crosscutting goals that the project aims through individual and familiar WASH hygiene related promotion activities, and involvement of people within the activities themselves, making them part of the decision making process, and not just recipients of the activities, making people the protagonists of their own well-being

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through XXX mechanism. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responded to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Respect for the environment will be ensured throughout the project, avoiding any type of environmental pollution, such as forest degradation, and the contamination of aquifers specially regarding to latrine use and water management, and avoiding public health problems as open-air defecation that could lead to a deterioration in the quality of life of the people who will benefit from the activities of this project. The collection and adequate treatment of home/familiar waste materials will be ensured, following the public health most elemental regulations. Likewise, the development of activities that promote an adequate environmental sanitation of the sites camps and homes that they host will be encouraged, aiming to guarantee a healthy, clean, and hygienic environment. That is the ultimate goal of all promotion activities related with encouraging families to build and manage properly their own latrines and waste pits. It will be included in all health promotion activities as a cross-cutting element of environmental conservation and environmental health, highlighting the impact that environmental degradation has on people, and why it is so important to avoid deforestation, preserve water sources and what measures can be taken to respect in daily life.
