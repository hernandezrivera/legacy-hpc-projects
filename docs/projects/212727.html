Improving the quality of education for students with disabilities in care institutes (visual, auditory, motor, and cerebral palsy) in Damascus and rural damascus

Basic Info

Project Name

Improving the quality of education for students with disabilities in care institutes (visual, auditory, motor, and cerebral palsy) in Damascus and rural damascus

Start Date

01/01/2024

End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

Based on the importance of children with disabilities (males and females) obtaining their rights, especially the right to learn like other children through approved educational curricula that refine the abilities and skills of the child with disabilities and help him in the future to obtain a job opportunity that achieves financial and moral independence for him instead of being a recipient. For social and economic aid.
With the increase in the number of cases of children with disabilities in Syrian society as a result of terrorist acts that targeted all segments of Syrian society (males and females).
The Athar Development Foundation team carried out field visits and meetings with focus groups and stakeholders in educational institutes affiliated with the Ministry of Social Affairs and Labor designated for students with disabilities aged (6-18) years (Institute for the Hearing Impaired, Institute for Rehabilitation of the Blind, Al Amal Institute for the Motor Disabled, Cerebral Palsy Institute For children, Cerebral Palsy Institute for adults) and the importance of implementing a project targeting 3605 in rural damascus and damascus in budget 289300 $ that includes the following activities:
- Compensating the educational loss of students with disabilities due to the dropout of a large number of them, about 300 students, as a result of (the large costs incurred in transportation, especially in remote and rural areas, a number of institutes that served as shelter centers during the previous period, and the closure of institutes during the Covid-19 pandemic).
- Providing support in educational means for each disability separately in preparation for the exams of the second semester of the academic year 2023/2024 and preparing the educational environment.
- Psychosocial support for students with disabilities, especially those who drop out of institutes.
- Rehabilitation of safe and recreational spaces within the institutes.
Especially since institutes for students with disabilities provide quality education (vocational - regular).

Several indicators will be adopted to implement monitoring and evaluation:
- Number of students benefiting from the project.
- Number of beneficiary care institutes.
- Percentage of students who dropped out after implementing the project.
- - The number of students for whom educational losses were compensated compared to the number of students who dropped out
- The quality of educational methods and achieving their purpose
- Number of students benefiting from psychological and social support services
- The extent of rehabilitation of safe and recreational spaces within institutes.
