Project Summary

The main aim of this project is to provide lifesaving WASH services to most vulnerable and underserved communities living in floods affected areas in Daynile, Kahda districts in Mogadishu and Merka, Wanlaweyn and Afgooye district of Lower Shebelle region. A total of 18,700 flood affected men, women and children including the 15% disabilities, marginalized communities and IDPs will benefit safe drinking water and will have access equally throughout the project period. SOYDA in collaboration with the local authority, partnership with main water vendors in rural villages in target districts, will provide emergency water voucher to the 7,440 (1808 men, 1,536 women, 2,048 boys and 2,048 girls) targeted beneficiaries including people with disabilities and marginalized communities in the targeted IDP camps, which they can redeem for water at the specified water vendors (water trucks). The action also includes to hire water truck of a capacity of 6,000 liters of safe water to the targeted locations. Each voucher can be used to redeem specified quantity of safe water of 7.5 liters per person per day for a period of 30 days. The project will build the capacity of 10 WASH Committees (composing 4 men and 3 women per Committee) shared equally men and women on operation and maintenance of developed water infrastructures shall be throughout the project implementation period, to ensure the sustainability of the developed water facilities after the project is faced out. SOYDA will make sure that men, women, girls and boys including people with disabilities and marginalized community have equal access to the rehabilitated/constructed WASH services. SOYDA will upgrade/rehabilitate two boreholes equipped with hybrid to improve its water quality and reduce its running cost, in order to receive the community their water needs at an affordable cost. SOYDA will replace the engine powered boreholes in to hybrid system and construct 8 water kiosks nearer to the IDP settlements supplied water through piping network to avoid the risk of rape against women and girls in target districts. The project will provide reliable and sustainable access to environmental sanitation through construction of 200 IDP latrines in target districts. The latrines will be gender separated and flood proof with lockable doors and hand washing basins. construction of latrines will be implemented through market-based programming.
SOYDA will distribute 200 sanitation sets to 20 IDP camps, in order to improve the environmental sanitation of the targeted locations. The aim of the sanitation sets distribution is to carry out clean-up campaigns, in order to reduce the spread of AWD/cholera diseases and to improve the environmental sanitation of the target locations. SOYDA will conduct comprehensive hygiene promotion campaigns focusing hand washing with soap practices to 18,700 men, women, boys and girls through visiting house to house, health facilities, and nutrition centers. 2500 HP kits will be distributed to the most vulnerable and underserved communities in the target locations.
The project will ensure the participation of people with disability in all decision-making processes and ensure WASH- related information is in formats that people with different types of disabilities can understand and as well will consider disability groups in the design, monitoring and evaluation of development and emergency WASH interventions through disability mainstreaming in the programme cycle is to improve the understanding of WASH staff on the barriers and opportunities for children and adults with disabilities, identify where and why disability inclusion is not being addressed, and develop creative and scalable disability inclusive strategies to influence policy and programmes and contribute to knowledge generation.
SOYDA proposed WASH project would develop synergies with other ongoing health, protection and nutrition projects targeting the same areas in order to maximize the impacts.

Mitigation Measures

Domestic violence within the IDPs camps families that led many children unsupported. This is a rampant within the displaced person in Mogadishu and Afgooye area where, SOYDA is currently presence with active program, however, in this proposed program, SOYDA will ensure these unaccompanied or separated children were provided with adequate dignity kits as well as material assistance that will ensure to deter any form of abuse. We will ensure to provide hotline/call centers within the community WASH committees who will in turn report to the project team for quick feedback and attention these vulnerable children and mothers. SOYDA will ensure awareness creation to reduce the occurrence of domestic violence by conducting joint community and other stakeholders meeting within the project area Conflict a raising from water point, SOYDA will mitigate this menace through building the capacity of WASH committee as well as improving proper coordination with government line ministry for sustainable water services. The WASH committee will be selected among the communities with the project location as well as provide them with infrastructural equipment that will enable them to perform the minor breakdown within the water sources. Resettlement of the IDP – This is mostly evident in Mogadishu IDPs where after the construction of community water points and training of the WASH committee the IDPs are relocated to far areas that will risk vulnerable girls and mothers to walk to distance and sometime susceptible for rape, however, we will ensure on timely community information sharing and dignified relocation of the IDPs as well as proper handling of rape cases by providing psychosocial services integrated within the WASH activities hence this will further reduce the protection risk related to the poor vulnerable girl and women in the target location. Lack of information and coordination- project staff will ensure every step is being coordinated with the community as well as FGS. SOYDA will ensure more responsive response to the needs of the target population through the following to avoid the risk of gender disparities that is: Equal employment and services provisionopportunity – The minority and disable people as well as these elder people might be left out for either employment and services provision such the long queues for the elderly or disable as well as pregnant and lactating women, to avoid mishandling of such group SOYDA will ensure to provide mobile registration during the project initiation and documents people with such needs hence given priority during the implementation period, SOYDA will also ensure to employ both sex as well as have no discrimination policy on age, race and religion in its services provision Project WASH committee representative- Lack of women, disable and minority group might create conflict hence exclude one of the special group mention, to ensure inclusivity, SOYDA will provide equal opportunity for both men, women, boys, girls, disable and minority group when constituting its WASH committee as well as its staff or community hygiene promoters hence this will drastically reduce the exclusion of either group. Lack of beneficiary information might also hinder the inclusion of girls child needs however, SOYDA will ensure include sanitary pads to cater for girls better hygiene and promote conducive learning environment as well as reduce the risk of discrimination against girl child in social gathering. SOYDA organization intends to implement the above proposed WASH activities in Afgooye corridor and Mogadishu IDPs thus due to the nature of the areas inhabitant by IDPs it might bring the following risk however, SOYDA will mitigate such risk as indicated below. Conflict zone - in lower Shabelle areas where there high number of displace person and sometimes fighting between government force and the militia group might escalate to the nearby settlement.

The project will complements the already ongoing reliance activities that have been implemented by SOYDA organization and will ensure timely and visible access to clean water services to the beneficiaries, in doing so it will ensure the wash facilities are within a less distance with properly lightening system, however, SOYDA will ensure to coordinates with NFS sectors by ensuring these vulnerable women, girls and boys are provided with alternative lightening material such as lamps to enable their security during the night time. The project will be closely coordinated with the ministry of water under the federal republic of Somalia as well as Banadir and south west regional state (Afgooye) for community and government ownership in the long term objective as well as in building a better resilience structure within the ministry of water. For sustainable and equitable access of the donor funded project SOYDA will ensure community inclusion in the initial decision making of the project design and structure in this case the WASH component will be prioritize the needs of community and in such will ensure to capacitate the existing community wash committee as well as the staff that will ensure the carryover/sustainability of the project after the expiry of the donor funds. SOYDA will ensure to provide more responsive services to the affected population under the strict supervision and coordination with government and other humanitarian actors within its area of operation. The WASH activities will considered the most vulnerable and disadvantage groups hence build the community resilience to prevent recurrent and protracted emergency.
