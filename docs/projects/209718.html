Project Summary

The proposed project is intended to support the scale-up of humanitarian operations in Sudan and
enable the programme delivery of UN agencies, funds and programmes and implementing partners (under the Saving Lives Together (SLT) framework) in response to the rapidly evolving and complex humanitarian situation. Security is an essential common service. Without it, it will be impossible for the UN and its SLT partner organizations to provide humanitarian assistance to those in need and operate closest to the points of impact regardless of shifts in conflict dynamics. UNDSS response will be grounded in the imperative to stay and deliver. The project will deploy professional security personnel and equipment to the new humanitarian hubs and other locations with security gaps and will rebuild the security structure in the field, conduct rapid security risk management analysis in areas identified as high priorities for humanitarian operations, provide increased analytical and operational capability, enhance security awareness, coordination with the SLT, forward-planning, crisis readiness and preparedness, and full operational support for the scaled-up humanitarian operations across Sudan. The project will significantly contribute to facilitation of safe passage of humanitarian personnel and humanitarian aid to the affected areas in Sudan, including through cross-border operations.
