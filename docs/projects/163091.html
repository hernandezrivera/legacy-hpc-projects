Project Summary

Zimbabwe is prone to a number of rapid and slow onset disasters which have generated acute humanitarian needs, aggravated by the current economic crisis. The delivery of a targeted and efficient humanitarian response relies on the capacity of humanitarian actors to collect accurate and regular information on the locations and needs of population of concerns (IDPs, returnees, host communities). In order to support advocacy and inform operational planning and response, a data collection system is already operational in the country. This system includes various data collection tools, elaborated in close consultation with humanitarian partners and which are currently used to collect information on populations affected by the Cyclone Idai.
The continuation of the data collection system is crucial to ensure an efficient and targeted humanitarian response. IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) has being systematically deployed in medium to large-scale humanitarian response operations, since objective analysis of primary data is key to effective advocacy and a well-informed humanitarian response. IOM DTM will serve as a coordination and key accountability tool to regularly inform analysis by other humanitarian and policy actors.
IOM DTM assessments will highlight persistent gaps and serve to inform relevant actors about where assistance is most needed and how the interventions are impacting in the population in need.
IOM DTM will enhance disaster preparedness and migration crisis response through better integration of systems and actors, strengthening national and local capacities on displacement tracking, and enhancing predictive analytics, ethical data collection and accountable data use promoting accountability towards the affected populations.
