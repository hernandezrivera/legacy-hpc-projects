Project Summary

To enhance the safety and security of humanitarians through the coordination and implementation of security telecommunications in Tripoli and Benghazi the emergency telecommunications sector will enable the close coordination between all UN agencies within Libya, UNSMIL owned and maintained Tetra radio network is the primary security communications network, and the ETS plans to establish a backup VHF radio network in Benghazi which will ensure the safety and security of staff in that area. ETS and UNSMIL are working closely together with the authorities to facilitate this approach. The ETS will also identify an alternative satellite-based Push-To-Talk solution which does not rely on the same frequency licensing and radio or the same towers and infrastructure. The ETS will explore capacity building opportunities with the Ministry of Telecommunications and Information Technology to ensure the sustainability of services.

1 - Needs

While the evacuation status in Tripoli was lifted in February 2018, UN agencies still do not have a functioning security telecommunications network in Benghazi, and the coverage in Tripoli remains limited. UN agencies do not have licenses to important and use essential security communications equipment, including radios and satellite phones with the UN Support Mission in Libya (UNSMIL) being the only UN organization currently licensed to use radios across Libya. The licensing and provision of security telecommunications is an essential part of the Minimum Operations Safety and Security (CMOSS) standards and a requirement for the UN to operate in Libya. Vehicle tracking also ensures safety of staff and assett movements across Libya. Engagement with the Ministry of Telecommunications is critical to ensuring the safety and security of humanitarian aid workers and humanitarian assistance.

2 - Activities

Provide support to emergency telecommunications through the provision of technology and staffing to support project implementation, coordination and monitoring across Libya. Provide capacity development to the ministry of telecommunications within Libya.
