Project Summary

This project aims to contribute to food security for vulnerable households (HHs) in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates. It has 2 components: HH Food Security and Bakery Support. NGO X will target an average of 31,492 HH/148,755 individuals per month with CVA. Bakery Support provides subsidized bread to 16 supported bakeries' catchment area at a reduced and stabilized price. NGO X will implement the proposed project in partnership with 3 NGOs and submissions have been coordinated to ensure no duplication. NGO X has permission to operate in all proposed locations.
Needs: People in NWS are suffering from the effects of 12 years of conflict compounded by the 2023 earthquakes, intermittent violence, and harsh winter weather conditions. Women and girls, particularly IDPs, are more vulnerable to mobility limitations, discrimination, exploitation, and safety risks, and female headed HHs are more likely to be displaced. People with disabilities face more challenges accessing adequate accommodation and humanitarian assistance. In NWS, 4.1 out of the 4.5 million population need humanitarian assistance, and 2.9 million are internally displaced.
NGO X 's MSNA highlighted the urgent need for cash-based assistance, with 98% of interviewed individuals preferring cash. Just under half (49.74%) are food insecure of which 45.91% are moderately insecure, and 3.82% severely. Lack of money, cooking materials, high prices and distance to markets were the main difficulties. Food insecurity is more prevalent among female headed HHs and HHs headed by people with disabilities. As household size increases, so does the likelihood of food insecurity, contributing to rising rates of malnutrition and increased humanitarian needs. Intervention, Implementation of activities: NGO X will respond to the most vulnerable HHs in line with the gap analysis and severity of needs shared by the FSL Cluster and prioritize locations based on NGO X MSNA and HNO 2024. In line with FSL guidance, NGO X will distribute 8 rounds of Cash for Food with a sliding scale of $13 per HH member and a maximum transfer value of US$65 per HH. This represents 70% of the food SMEB (1,550 kcal/person/day). Any changes in FSL Cluster guidance will be adopted in implementation. NGO X will use Cash for Food as the default modality with flexibility to alternate delivery mechanisms in accordance with changes in context and the most appropriate option.
The bakery support component will improve HH access to diverse foods and community access to bread at affordable prices. NGO X will provide 40% of flour and yeast inputs to 16 bakeries to ensure high-quality bread is available to the catchment population at an agreed affordable price (up to 35% less than market price). Routine monitoring will be conducted for all distribution-based activities. For the bakery component, bakery, vendor, and beneficiary feedback and satisfaction surveys will be conducted.
Protection Risk Analysis: NGO X emphasizes the safety and well-being of participants with robust measures integrating comprehensive PSEAH (Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse, and Harassment) strategies into all activities, including prevention, awareness, responsive reporting, and risk mitigation, reinforced by training for staff, partners, and leaders to enhance incident management.
Specific protection risks for this project include Cash distributions put beneficiaries at risk of robbery, exploitation, or extortion.
Mitigation: select safe and accessible distribution points in consultation with local authorities, organize queues for men and women, distributions conducted during the day raise awareness of NGO X’s CFM, arrange home delivery for those who need it.
Child headed HHs experience abuse from guardians, NGO X, or partner staff.
Mitigation: NGO X and partner staff sign and adhere to PSEAH policies, protection team regularly check children’s status, raise awareness on how to report such cases included child headed HH in consultations
