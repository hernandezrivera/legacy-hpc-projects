Project Summary

The proposed project will target 12,500 direct beneficiaries (Men 3000, Women 5500, Boys 2000 and Girls 2000) while overall population of the target areas will be the indirect beneficiaries especially regarding to the sensitization and awareness.
The project will be contributing to the overall objectives of the cluster
Strategic Objective 1: Save lives by ensuring those affected by shocks have access to integrated services, ensuring that the needs of women, girls, elderly and people with disabilities and other vulnerable groups are addressed, following a rights-based approach.
Strategic Objective 2: Vulnerable people are supported to sustain their livelihoods and improve their living conditions and resilience.
The incidence of violence against women and children, sexual and physical abuse will be minimized through increased awareness and sensitization among the target groups through integrated efforts. Existing support services of Government like Child Helpline (1121) and Women Complaint Cell/ Development Centers will be strengthened through effective engagement of stakeholders.
Existing social infrastructure in shape of Men, Women, Adolescent and Children groups formed in both target districts in communities and schools will be leveraged through sensitization, capacity building and campaigning for improved access to psychosocial as well as counselling services. Likewise, community based support mechanism and structure will be established and strengthened for sustainability of the project interventions after the completion.
Staff of most relevant departments (Social Welfare, Women Development and Child Protection) will be trained and skills will be enhanced on case management, documentation and legal obligations for providing support to survivors as well as to improve the utilization of existing support service structures. Organizations working and providing free counselling and psychosocial support to women, girls and boys will be taken onboard and engaged with the relevant authorities and communities for sustaining the interventions and continuity of professional services with privacy and dignity after project is completed.
REEDS is already implementing project titled "Growth and Innovations Funds" with the financial and technical support of Better Cotton Initiative (BCI) in district Dadu while project titled "Primark Sustainable Lives" with the financial and technical support of Cotton Connect in district Ghotki for improving the socio-economic status of communities through integrated approach. REEDS has project outreach in 15 UCs of district Dadu and 38 UCs of district Ghotki and strong linkages with the district administration. The long-term relationships with the targeted communities and farmer groups will support and facilitate in the implementation of proposed project.

1- Project Activities

1. Project inception workshop with stakeholders for common understanding and clarity on role & responsibilities 2. Project introduction and orientation meetings with community groups, adolescent groups and children groups 3. Sensitization and awareness raising sessions with groups on International conventions, covenants and treaties related to GBV and Child Protection 4. Sensitization and awareness raising sessions with groups on legal and fundamental rights of adolescent girls and women in the constitution of Pakistan 5. Sensitization and awareness raising sessions with groups on legal and fundamental rights of children in the constitution of Pakistan 6. Sensitization and awareness raising sessions with groups on various forms of violence, support mechanism and available resources 7. Capacity building trainings of support groups on GBV and Child Protection Mechanisms 8. Capacity building trainings of government officials (Women Development Department, Social Welfare Department and Sindh Child Protection Authority) on GBV, Support Mechanisms, UNCRC, Case Management & Reporting 9. Celebration of International Days for mass awareness raising among the target beneficiaries and general public 10. Printing of IEC/BCC material and recorded messages for mass campaigning through community meetings, theatre performances, airing messages on local TV cable, radio and other forums 11. Provision of support and facilitation for birth registration of children, CNIC/voter registration and renewal of CNICs to women 12. Provision of counselling services with the engagement of staff and dedicated service providers through mutual collaborations 13. Support and facilitation to the GBV/CP survivors through engagement and liaison of legal experts and practitioners 14. Provision of small grants to identified women micro entrepreneurs for establishing and improving their livelihoods 15. Establishment of referral mechanism with relevant authorities for psychosocial support and access to different social protection programs of Government 16. Coordination and support mechanism established at district level with the engagement of relevant government departments 17. Quarterly progress review and planning meetings for effective program management 18. Lessons learnt workshop with key stakeholders for documenting the learnings, best practices and key successes
