Project Summary

In this project, SCS will implement an intervention aiming to enhance safe and equitable access to education. This will be achieved through rehabilitation of 5 schools: 1 in Hama city (Al Fayhaa (SS:4)) and 4 in its rural in As-Saan (SS:3), Tiba Elemam (SS:3), Suran (SS:3) and Murak (SS:3). The project will increase access to education for 3000 children (50% female) of all school grades (6-17 years). Also, 150 teachers and education personnel (70% female and 25% are youth) will have job opportunities and improve their livelihood. The total estimated budget of the project is 362,000 USD.
SCS will apply the two-year strategy/planning. In 2022, SCS will rehabilitate 2 schools of Suran and As-Saan. The estimated budget for this duration is 147,370 USD, and the number of beneficiaries is 950 students and 60 teachers and education personnel. In 2023, SCS will rehabilitate the other 3 schools of Hama city (Al Fayhaa), Tiba Elemam, and Murak. The estimated budget is 214,630 USD, and the number of beneficiaries is 2,050 students and 90 teachers and education personnel.
In 2022, SCS is implementing 6 projects in cooperation with UNHCR, UNICEF, SHF and WFP in many sectors including Shelter, Non-Food Items and Cash Assistance. These projects aim to respond to the displaced, returnees and residents in most of Hama city and rural.
SCS is one of the leader NNGOs in Hama that has practiced humanitarian work in providing Shelter, Non-food Items, Protection, WASH, Health, Education, Food and Livelihoods services since 2013.

4 - Needs

According to priority needs outlined in Syria 2022 HNO “The formal system, is unable to absorb and retain all school age children. Many students continue to learn in unconducive and/or unsafe learning environments. At the national level there is an average of one operational classroom for every 54 school aged child.” “Existing services are unable to physically accommodate or meet the different learning needs of pre-primary children, adolescents, IDPs, returnees and children with specific needs. Many children who are in school continue to have reduced learning time due to multiple shifts and school suspension due to Covid-19 and security issues.” On the other hand, “In areas where schools are available, learning environments will continue to be unconducive due to damage/destruction of schools, overcrowding, lack of school furniture/school supplies, insufficient heating and WASH facilities.”In this vein, SCS is submitting this proposal based on the explicit recommendation from the Directorate of Education (DoE) in Hama. According to DoE, the rehabilitation of the targeted 5 schools, their severity scales are between 3-4, will mitigate overcrowding because these schools will receive the students in their neighborhoods and districts who were forced to join other schools and caused overcrowding. Also, these schools will provide jobs for 150 teachers and education personnel (70% F). (Please see Needs' comment for more details)

5 - Implementation of activities

This project is in line with the Education Sector’s objective1 “Availability & Access ".Proposed activities are rehabilitation of schools/classrooms to increase access to protected, safe and friendly learning spaces and to ease overcrowding and encourage smooth return to schools; improvement and rehabilitation of gender-sensitive WASH facilities: 1. Rehabilitation of schools/classrooms: all rehabilitation activities will be carried out by experienced contractors, selected through SCS tender procedures. SCS staff will supervise, monitor and prepare the needed reports of the rehabilitation. 2. Improvement and rehabilitation gender-sensitive WASH facilities: carried out by an experienced contractor following WASH SPHERE standards for WASH in schools.3. SCS will ask contractors to involve the people in the targeted locations in the rehabilitation works which will be a short job opportunity.4. After all planned works are being completed, a special committee is formed to take over the executed works and to ascertain the quality in the specified time. The work will then be officially handed over to the concerned authorities (DoE).SCS will verify project outputs and indicators indicated in the project Logical Framework by using Monitoring and Evaluation tools and methods to ensure activities happen in a timely manner, and with the agreed quality and quantity.
