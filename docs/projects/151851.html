Project Summary

The Logistics Sector will aim to provide common logistics coordination, information management and services to all humanitarian organisations operating across Libya, with a focus on main hubs such as Tripoli, Benghazi and Sebha, to address operational gaps and bottlenecks.

Needs

Based on a WFP logistics assessment conducted in April 2018 and consultations with humanitarian organisations, the major gaps and needs identified are:- Centralized logistics support platform that can collect, analyse and share timely and reliable logistics information in order to coordinate organisations' interventions and avoid duplication of efforts;- Mapping and rehabilitation of critical entry points to maximize the use of resources available in country;- Common storage facilities in strategic locations to serve as preposition hubs for emergency and contingency stocks;- Sustained and well-maintained access to fuel to ensure a constant availability of fuel where gaps exist.

Activities

The Logistics Sector activities will include: - Maintaining and strengthening the coordination and information sharing role to facilitate a more coordinated, pragmatic, and cost-effective operational approach; - Scaling up logistics services provision to hard-to-reach areas to enhance the ability of the humanitarian community to deliver relief items where most needed; - Mapping and assessment of key logistics infrastructure and entry points, to clarify processes, mitigate access constraints and bureaucratic impediments for cargo movements, thus increasing logistics options to assist the affected people across the country. These activities will benefit the humanitarian organisations across all sectors of intervention, and will reflect inter-sectoral strategies and complementarity. The scale and geographical coverage will be adjusted according to the needs and requests from humanitarian actors.
