Project Summary

"The proposed project aims to address the humanitarian needs in relation to GBV services integrating the WASH services in light of protection needs for the targeted Rohingya refugee women and adolescents. The project will maintain existing two Safe Space for Women and Girls (SSWG) in camp 25 and 27 for Rohingya refugee community. The GBV services will cover a range of activities including awareness raising, recreational activities, psychosocial support, case management, referral support, skill development, community engagement including male and adolescent, capacity building etc. Integration of WASH services to reduce the GBV risk is another major area of the project intervention which will include the hygiene awareness issues, community involvement in maintenance and repairment of WASH facilities, establish linkage between community and the WASH service providers to meet the priority needs, management of small scale water distribution system managed by the community etc. Monitoring will be a continuous process to assess whether the project is being implemented as planned; progresses are made; if any problems are faced and how the problems are being resolved. Facilities and target activities will be monitored on sample basis. Though, all facilities will be covered under the monitoring tools and techniques during the project period. Both qualitative and quantitative methods will be used in collecting data as required. The techniques will be followed such as i.e. observation, interviewing, spot visit, FGD, KII etc. In order to address the challenges of humanitarian response in the long term, sustaining uninterrupted humanitarian services coordination and liaison will be maintained with RRRC, key sector stakeholders and actors, the respective CICs and with the relevant sector and sub-sector committees. The project activities will be context specific to their surrounding environment, and includes local voluntaries including the gender issues, sector recommendations and standards, local authorities, concerned development partner, coordination bodies for better understanding the situation, effective implementation of actions and sustainability of the intervention.
The purpose of the project: To ensure safety, well-being and dignity of crisis affected Rohingya refugee community by mitigating, preventing and protecting against pervasive Gender Based Violence.
Around 9,259 Rohingya population will be benefited by this intervention."

Needs

"Bangladesh hosts about 913,988 (Ref. UNHCR population factsheet as of 30 September 2019) individual Rohingyas from Myanmar who are living 32 congested camps in Cox’s Bazar district. Among them 9,497 and 14,269 individual Rohingyas are living in the camps 25 and 27 in Teknaf upazila. In Teknaf upazila, Rohingyas refugees are living amongst the host community. The lack of formal status attributed to refugees, inadequate access to livelihoods and education, as well as movement restrictions, access to civil documentation, and formal justice mechanisms continue to impact negatively on the protection environment, contributing to harmful coping mechanisms, including gender-based violence issues. An unstable security environment in and around camps in 2019 impacted negatively on the physical and psychological well-being of both refugees and host communities in particular for women, limiting access to public spaces and thus hindering effective access to basic services. It is within this context that social cohesion has deteriorated, exposing communities to safety and security risks. One third of the population exhibiting particular vulnerabilities, tailored community-based and individualized protection responses are required to ensure access to basic services for persons with specific needs. Further more, the WASH response capacity is challenged by factors such as limited access of space, hilly topography leading partners to consider rented lands to ensure the provision of quality services, lack of water sources etc. "

Activities or outputs

Two existing SSWG located in camp 25 & 27 will be operated continuously, supporting total number of 400 registered refugee women & girls. SSWGs provide Psychosocial Support so that the women and girls can go to SSWG at any time to feel safer and empowered and have access to information, recreational activities, support and services which mainly associated with Psychological First Aid (PFA), referral support and establish linked the case and concerned service center. Psychosocial support will be ensured through group and individual Counseling Sessions which includes Trauma Identification, Normalization, Stress management, Anger management, Relaxation, Breathing, Safety planning with Women & Girls under the supervision and guidance of Clinical Psychologist. SSWGs are operated as per operational manual titled ‘Standard Operating Procedure’. To engage community and ensure participation of beneficiaries, SSWG Committee will be held monthly maintain close liaison with the concerned government authorities and instruction. CRM will play a potential role for keeping functional of complain response feedback mechanism which will be played a vital role to attain the quality of services and accountability of project staff towards the deserving community. Different policy and procedures of NGO Forum like Code of Ethics, Child protection Policy, Gender Policy, Safeguarding Policy, Whistleblower Policy, Human Resources Policies & Procedures complying with the sector standard for managing PSEA and conflicting issues to be encountered during implementation of project activities.One of the major psychosocial support activities is designed in the form of Different Skill Development Activities for Women and Adolescents as Work Therapy Session which will be conducted in SSWG. The aim is to provide the participants with positive mind-set to overcome the challenges faced and look forward towards re-building their lives through positive engagement in productive works. The work therapy will mostly cover small scale Sewing activities, Makeup and may include any other issues found demanding through the assessment. Awareness Sessions will be held on different issues related to protection and GBV for the purpose of creating more opportunity for women and adolescent children to increase awareness of gender inequality, rights, protection risk prevention etc. Anticipated prolonged situation of Rohingya refugees in Bangladesh, the project aims to develop mechanisms to reduce, prevent and mitigate GBV and related risks and to strengthen community-based protection mechanisms and practices. To provide dedicated case management service and referral for GBV, and additional support according to the need of case survivors with a strong focus on “Do No Harm” approach. Each of the SSWG roof also use as catchment for establishing the Rainwater Harvesting System (RWHS) to meet the water need in the center and additional amount of water will be distributed within the community. The community groups also review the immediate WASH needs, prioritize the needs and communicate with the respective service provider for getting the services. WASH facilities maintenance groups will be formed, and training will be provided for maintain the WASH facilities hygienically. For OnM of RWHS groups will be formed and trained. Apart from the skill development on tailoring and work therapy, community group orientation etc entering the beneficiary the project also focusses on capacity building of the project staff. Relevant training, coaching or mentoring will be conducted by in-house capacity, haring the expert from the sector and participating in different training programme organized by sector actors like UNHCR, IOM, UNICEF and other competent organization.In case of emergency response, the project will maintain an emergency stock for WASH related services like WPT, ORS and materials for repairing of WASH facilities along with the provision of fund for repairing of the SSWG.
