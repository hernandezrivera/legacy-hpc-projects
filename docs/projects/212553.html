Project Summary

The project aims at supporting households affected by the war through rehabilitating partially damaged houses and distributing NFI kits to displaced and returnees in Afrin and Azaz in Aleppo.
In total, 1000 HHs will benefit from the activities of this project (3150 females –2980 males) - (Men:1300, Women:1550Boys:1680, Girls:1600), Total 6130 (year 2024)
- The rehabilitation will target 200 houses that are partially damaged houses that people are living in or have returned to
So the Main activities will be as following:
NFI distribution: 800 HHs (in 2024), which the kit content (emergency kits (Plastic Sheeting, Jerry Can, Solar Lamps, Mattresses, Blankets, Hasira in summer and Carpet in winter as per cluster technical guidance)
- The rehabilitation will target 200 houses that are partially damaged houses that people are living in or have returned to
BRD will conduct segregated awareness sessions while distribution to keep the IDPs aware about the social distance while distribution, risk of COVID-19 and prevention measures as well as BRD will distribute soaps to ensure the IDPs prevention measure against COVID-19 in addition to provide the masks, gloves and sanitizers during the distribution process for the beneficiaries

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project will compromise Rehabilitation of 200 partially damaged houses and distributing 800 core NFI kits in addition to Rehabilitation of damaged houses BRD will receive applications from bene ciaries who are in need of house rehabilitation, the application will include the ID of the applicants in addition to property ownership documents that will be verived by the community. In case of rent, there must be a contract for at least six months from the completion of the repair Two members of the staff (one male and one female) who are trained on HLP will be focal points responsible for any HLP issues confronted during implementation of the project. A technical needs assessment of every house will be conducted before starting the implementation of the rehabilitation works. The house should be impartially damaged, and the cost of all rehabilitation works will not exceed $1000 for each house The selection of bene ciaries for the Shelter rehabilitation activities will be based on the following criteria: -HHs who have returned to their damaged houses. - Female headed households (both from host communities and IDPs) -HHs with chronically ill members or with disabilities (both from host communities and IDPs) - People from HC who still live in their damaged houses After identifying the needed works, BRD will announce a tender to contract with a competent local contractor to provide some job opportunities for the youths in the targeted area.
