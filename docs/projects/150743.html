Needs

A protracted humanitarian crisis continues in Somalia, which remains largely attributable to natural climatic disasters, clan conflicts and militant activity. As a result, significant humanitarian and protection challenges abound, as described in the 2019 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO). Across Somalia, an estimated 3.4 million Somalis are in need of some form of humanitarian assistance, including around 2.6 million internally displaced persons. Against the unique contextual backdrop of Somalia, there is a continued need for coordinated humanitarian response and advocacy. There is a high demand within the local humanitarian community, as well as in the broader international community, for support to develop a robust, detailed, evidence based common situational analysis; for the coordination of advocacy and operational humanitarian responses; for the management and monitoring of country based pooled funds; and for the development and monitoring of strategic needs analyses and response plans. OCHA’s unique role in facilitating and coordinating these streams of humanitarian work continues therefore to be of critical importance. The specific context of Somalia warrants that the international community explore efficiencies in the ways in which to respond, including through multi-cluster humanitarian planning and a strengthening of the humanitarian-development linkages, where appropriate, to identify response activities that can be carried over to development sectors. This has led the humanitarian community to produce a HNO for 2019 that updated the People in Need (PiN) figures for 2019; the 2019 HRP will revalidate cluster strategies, key activities, indicators and targets; and conceptualize objectives into four categories that correlate to: (1) lifesaving, (2) protection, (3) resilience-building, and (4) nutrition.

Activities

- Coordination mechanisms, strengthened at national and decentralized level- Collection of disaggregated data by gender and age, to improve and influence both responses and advocacy- Support and increase resource mobilization efforts- Promote the commitment of all partners in all stages and aspects in the preparation and implementation of the 2019 response plan

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Monitoring and responding to the needs of the displaced, advocating for equal access to basic services for all and for a reduction in the frequency of forced evictions, by coordinating the provision of public land for IDPs from the government of Somalia.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Monitoring and responding to the needs of women, girls, men and boys, in whichever way necessary. Advocating for the cultural rejection of FGM and early child marriage, coordinating reintegration mechanisms for former child soldiers, introducing community-based activities which will motivate and mobilize the country's youth.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Monitoring and responding to the needs of those caught up in armed conflict, including those vulnerable populations living in rural, hard-to-reach areas. Advocating for the education and employment of those returning for war; strengthening the capacity of local and federal institutions to dis-encourage clan conflicts and move towards the rule of law.
