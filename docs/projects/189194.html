Project Summary

Flood and conflict have forced hundreds of thousands of people to stay in displacement sites across the Jonglei, Warrap, Unity and Upper Nile which were heavily affected by floods. The situation remain fluid. the above state continues to experience natural disaster-driven and conflict displacing communities in informal site,camp like-setting and hard-to-reach area's. The project will ensure equal access and needs based assistance to improve the quality of integrated services for populations affected by displacement, well-coordinated and integrated humanitarian intervention to 40 000 ( 15 000 Male and 25 000 Female) IDPs four state of Unity, Jonglei, Upper Nile and Warrap to improve their well-being, their resilience and participation in decision-making processes and improve living condition of IDPs by facilitating safe, equitable and dignified access to cross-sectoral humanitarian services, including CCCM. HRSS will respond to emergency needs by empowering community and strengthening the capacities of local actors to have access to both essentials camp management service through mobile strategy approach. Using CCCM cluster strategy , HRSS will coordinate and enhance the protective environment, respond to vulnerable population with priority on addressing protection gaps and strengthen the predictability and effectiveness of multi selector interventions at site level. HRSS will expand it activities to camp-like settings and hard to reach area's to strengthen accountable, community-led governance structures and site management mechanisms and community base led initiative, In conjunction with humanitarian and development actors, HRSS will effectively support vulnerable IDPs to recover from the crisis by strengthening community self-management, build resilience to promote solutions for displaced people in sites.

1 - Needs

Flood and conflict have forced hundreds of thousands of people to stay in displacement sites across the Jonglei, Warrap, Unity and Upper Nile which were heavily affected by flood.The Humanitarian community in South Sudan estimates that more than two-thirds of South Sudan's population, 8.9 million people, are in need of humanitarian assistance. More people are likely to experience severe food insecurity in 2022 (according to HNO -2022). As per (HNO-2022) An estimated 1.7 million people are in need of CCCM and this number is expected to increased at the peak of the 2022 lean season (May-July) as water still increase in Unity,Warrap,Upper Nile and Jonglei. The counties of most concern are in the states of Jonglei, Unity and Warrap (specifically Greater Tonj), with the most extreme case being Fangak County in Jonglei State, where the situation is comparable to Pibor County in 2021. People have largely exhausted their emergency coping strategies. In 2022, the precarious situation is expected to be further exacerbated by the country’s current economic crisis which is impacting people’s ability to access markets, while conflict, flooding and seasonal effects are limiting the flow of both commercial and humanitarian goods and service. A continuation and increase in sub-national violence reported in many states across the country, including Jonglei, Lakes, Unity, Upper Nile, Warrap (particularly Greater Tonj) and Western Equatoria (notably Tambura), will likely continue to disrupt livelihoods and trade and contribute to an increase in humanitarian needs in 2022.Due to compounding shocks, both in areas of displacement and return, populations have been forced to keep displacing time and again. These stressors present a burden for displaced individuals and families and negatively affects their mental health, psycho social well-being and daily functionality. All collective site, camp like settings need some form of management to create safe and secure living environment in order to meet the minimum human rights standards for the displaced population. Increasing diversity in modalities of settlement requires the HRSS to preposition herself with robust approaches and skills to meet the diverse needs of the displaced persons. Unless these gaps are addressed, the protective environment of camp and camp-like settings will continue to deteriorate resulting in aggravated risks and abuse. Displaced persons in camps and camp-like settings including out of camp are constrained with challenges over options for durable solutions. People in informal and collective sites lack access to information about their rights, freedom of movement and available services. The existence of settlements without any coordination and management structures have led to sporadic or a complete lack of aid provision to vulnerable people living in these informal settlements. Strengthening a well coordinated service delivery and community coping mechanisms geared towards lasting solutions, local ownership, self-governance and self-reliance through mobile respond in Unity,Upper Nile,Jonglei and Warrap.

2 - Project activities

Hope Restoration will focus on the following cluster objectives and activities in Jonglei, Unity state,Upper Nile and Warrap.Sectoral Objective 1 : Improve living condition of IDPs by facilitating safe, equitable and dignified access to cross-sectoral humanitarian services, including CCCM. HRSS will conduct site maintenance activities with specific focus on People with Specific Needs (PSN) and improving water passage at the displace site. Conducting care and maintenance activity , Set-up Community Based Complaint Mechanism (CBCM) for informal settlements. Conduct communication with community forums at the site ,Introduce Cash-based initiatives that will be community led initiative, Conducting service mapping and Set up site coordination meeting with minute share with relevant partners, conduct site level monitoring and developed site profile and sit rep. Sectoral Objective 2: Enhance the protective environment, respond to vulnerable population with priority on addressing protection gaps and strengthen the predictability and effectiveness of multi sectorial interventions at site level . HRSS will conduct safety site audits, strengthening and provide site path referral in coordination with partners at the site, Conduct care and maintenance, Carry out multi-sectoral needs assessments at the site. Conduct accountability and PSEA training community leaders, Advocate for the needs of GBV survivors to Protection partners at the site and state focal point. Conducting communication Conduct beneficiary satisfaction/intention/disability access surveys in the site. Sectoral Objective 3: Support vulnerable IDPs to recover from the crisis by strengthening community self-management, build resilience to promote solutions for displaced people in sites. HRSS will established inclusive site community structure committee, conduct capacity building training to CCCM staffs and community leaders on introduction to CCCM, accountable to affected people and ect, Conducting partners training on CCCM,Supporting site community structure committee with in kind goods of chairs, tables, and etc. Support youth through promotion of sports activity at the site, Support community led initiative through cash for work, Establish coordination mechanisms with all sector partners at the site and carry out service monitoring exercises at the site through service mapping.

HRSS will survey the market at each site by observing and conducting focus group discussion with trader union to understand the available of goods at the market place. HRSS will used monthly CCCM rate when conducting Community Base Initiative to avoid economic pull factor at the site.
