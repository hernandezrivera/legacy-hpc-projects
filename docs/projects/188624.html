Project Summary

Through this activity, our Deirna aims to bring 42 kilometers of drainage channels to a fully operational state. The restoration of this distance from the canals and their full operation will provide approximately 42 thousand dunums of irrigated agricultural land in 7 agricultural associations (Al-Jaabi, Al-Hawama, Wasat Hawama, Al-Labadiya, Al-Hajiya, Abu Al-Hassan, Abu Al-Khater) belonging to the city of Hajin in the eastern countryside of Deir Ezzor. It benefits the population of the area / direct beneficiaries of an estimated 1,977 farmers and indirect benefits to 20,000 individuals/beneficiaries.
Viable options for agriculture-based livelihoods and economic growth will be promoted. In addition, the activity will help support the revival of the agricultural sector in the region. This in turn will have a significant positive impact on the stability of the relevant communities and facilitate the reintegration of returnees and the IDP with the community through this activity.
This project works to enhance social cohesion and reintegration of vulnerable social groups within the local communities in the targeted villages, in addition to reducing the level of heavy rains and shallow suspended surface water, which in turn will reduce the risks of increased soil salinization, and return large areas of usable agricultural land and become more Suitability for agricultural purposes, which reduces the process of competition and civil conflicts in the area.
Deirna cleans clogged drain channels due to excessive accumulation of mud, dirt, and rocks as a result of vandalism and excessive growth of vegetation growing in the channel. Road ferries along the canal may need to be replaced with cement pipes and covered. Repair and/or installation of waterway crossing points will allow pedestrians/livestock and vehicles to cross the canal at normal locations. Transfer sites are essential and have been created and used by some local citizens. Crossing points help residents to move easily through canals. During the process of isolation and cleaning, light and heavy mechanisms are used to remove the reed plant, which is easy to grow in swamps and stagnant ponds.10 workers are contracted (to cover the work area, to be replaced according to the region) for a month and a half as a kind of cash-for-work service to carry out the process of sorting the reeds that were removed during the cleaning process, and to distribute the good ones to the people of the area, specifically the families headed by women, to benefit from them In the process of cooking, heating and repairing the roofs of some homes and barns.To compensate farmers for the damage caused to their lands over the past years, and to ensure the success of the drainage channels, we support farmers with a crop (corn) that helps re-desalinate the soil during the fall season of July, according to specific standards. We work to support farmers with agricultural requirements for an area of ​​1.5 acres to grow yellow corn Which many of the people of the region depend on in their manufacture of bread and the rest of the pastries, especially with the loss of flour and the increase in its prices in the markets, in addition to its high nutritional value because it contains many vitamins that help reduce the risk of undernourishment, especially in children and pregnant women and the use of waste As natural feed for animals. Farmers are supported with basic necessities, in addition to preparing the land for cultivation. It is estimated that each dunum needs 2 kg of seeds in addition to 25 kg of superphosphate fertilizer and 25 kg of urea fertilizer in two batches before germination and after germination, 25 kg of humic fertilizer is added after germination, thus ensuring the return of most of the lands to their vitality and reducing the high percentage For suspended groundwater in addition to maintaining the moderate salinity rate in the soil.

4 - Needs

The canal is existentially important to most farmers trying to operate in this area. Farmers cannot provide for their farms or families without adequate access to irrigation water. Compounding the competition for irrigation water is the fact that farmers/displaced people from the Al-Shamiya area are renting large hectares of land in most areas. This in turn puts IDPs in direct competition with local farmers, which in turn degrades local farmers' often desperate desire to welcome IDPs or other returnees. An already stressed irrigation system will continue to deteriorate over time if it is not improved. The high levels of groundwater in the channel corresponding to a high level of soil salinity. Poorly managed water flows contribute to and undermine swamps unsuitable for agriculture, according to an assessment by the Water and Sanitation Commission. Clearing and improving drainage canals, opening new canals, and repairing irrigation gates will help reduce the risk of over salinization, improve agricultural production, reduce tensions between local communities, and directly reduce tensions between local and displaced/non-local farmers. In this case, easing tensions that increase already desperate and hostile competition for limited irrigation resources and lead to increased social division and reduced community acceptance of IDPs or returnees.

5 - Implementation of activities

Our Deirna is working on cleaning the canal, which is about 50 kilometers long, as a first stage of the project, using light machinery to open the road and heavy machinery to remove reeds, weeds, harmful algae, shallow silt, and the remains of dumped waste in it.It is the use of 10 workers for each village to sort the reeds that are suitable for use in the process of cooking, heating, baking, and repairing the roofs of some houses, animal stables, and poultry houses. As a type of (cash-for-work).The sides of the canal are settled after the isolation process is completed, thus eliminating the flood irrigation process, which causes a high percentage of surface water, and ensuring that no more lands go out of service and limit the spread of the areas of water bodies, which are a fertile environment for the reproduction of insects, mosquitoes, and leishmaniasis.In the last phase of the project, our Deirna is working on hygiene promotion campaigns for people against COVID-19 and other infectious diseases by holding some seminars, hanging up posters, and distributing brochures. In addition to conducting agricultural activities to support the cultivation of the yellow corn crop

9 - Protection Risk Analysis
