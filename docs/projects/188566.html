Project Summary

- A majority of AR-Raqqa areas are considered of low economic status with little access to work opportunities.
Rescue & Relief Team food basket distribution will be targeting areas considered the most in need. Many of these areas are ¦lled with newly arriving IDPs that have not been reached.
- A number of vulnerable host & IDP families in AR-Raqqa city heavily rely on the monthly Food Rations in order to survive the month and the worsening war conditions have only made work opportunities scarcer. According to HRP 2022, 4 out of 5 Syrians live under the poverty line unable to sustain themselves. in the AR-Raqqa city: there are 35.6 % of the population IDPs.
- The rescue & Relief Team, through this project will support vulnerable host & IDP families in AR-Raqqa city in AR-Raqqa with a monthly food basket for 8 months increasing their food security condition and supporting their survival.
1325 Food baskets will be distributed in AR-Raqqa city for 8 Months each month covering the needs of 1325 most vulnerable IDPs & host families starting from March till Dec 2022.
