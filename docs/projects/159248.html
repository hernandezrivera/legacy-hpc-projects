Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the immediate & protracted protection crisis resulted from conflict-related violence in GS, which particularly associated with the GMR demonstrations & have also resulted in high levels of psychosocial distress among the Gazans population. Clashes and GMR demonstrations exacerbate existing child protection and MHPSS needs.
The project will contribute in supporting the recovery and strengthen the resilience of acutely affected& injured boys, girls& adolescents including children with chronic illness & their caregivers in the face of chronic exposure to conflict-related violence by ensuring their access to the MHPSS &protection services through implementing a set of structured, specialized, and comprehensive MHPSS services by taking in consideration the different needs &interests of gender sensitivity ,illness, diversity &aggregation by targeting 1020 of acutely affected &injured boys, girls &adolescents aged from 11-18yrs. and their caregivers(1000 mothers& 100 fathers)& targeting 58 of child protection workers and service providers (F&M) working in CBOs &NGOs. On the other hand, children with chronic disease (boys& girls) will be included in MHPSS services to enhance their participation and inclusion in the project activities.
The project will focus on providing emergency child protection services, including case management, MHPSS& programs aimed at supporting vulnerable children and families who have been affected by conflict-related violence including the following activities:
-Group-based psychosocial support &life skills sessions for 1020 acutely affected &injured boys, girls, adolescents including children with chronic illness within (CBI) community-based intervention evidence-based module to increase their resilience in coping with their psychological &health status.
-Group counseling deep sessions for 200 affected &injured boys, girls & adolescents who have weaknesses in their self-confidence by using mind-body module
-Joint sessions for 1020 children &1100 their mothers to enhance the relationship between children and their mothers and asserting the duties & rights for the two parties.
-Outdoors activity (Camping days) for targeted children to enhance their wellbeing& develop their abilities & potentials
- Community-based initiatives to enhance social participation to deliver messages about their rights.
-Child protection case management and referral to specialized child protection, individual counseling, mental health, legal, social &educational services through accessing appropriate safe services in accordance to the national referral pathway in coordination with child protection network.
-Group-based caregivers support for 1100 parents of targeted children to manage their stress, improve emotional availability for parenting, strengthen their social networks and support, build their coping methods for dealing with stress and how to deal with children's psychological and behavioral problems
-Family counseling sessions for 20 families of the most acutely injured& affected children in need of family counseling services.
-Furnishing premises of 8 CBOs /NGOs with basic supplies as first aid kit, chairs, tables, and materials needed to implement the activities
And Implementing capacity building training for Child protection workers on the guidelines on mental health & PSS to develop their capacities
-Helping the Helpers sessions for the project's technical team and our partners' staff within using ICRC a recognized- evidence-based protocol to enhance and motivate the helpers' job performance, minimize their work stressors may impact on them, also to give them self-care methods to provide better care for themselves & the target beneficiaries
The project will be lasted for 12 months and will be implemented by a qualified and seasoned team
Coordination with respective partners of CBOs in the targeted location &related clusters will take place in the project implementation, M&E.

1 - Needs

Children in GS live in a nightmare that seems endless to them, they have grown up surrounded by conflict and violence practices. The continued violent events in GS & the dramatic situation that Gaza has witnessed since 30, Mach2018 has increased the prevalence of Mental health disorders and damaged child protection system in Gazan families and community as well as created a great need for MHPSS interventions and healing among children and their families. The violence experienced at the fence whether through being injured directly, witnessing an injury or death or knowing someone injured or killed–can significantly impact the mental health & psychosocial well-being of a person both immediately as well as over the long term. MHPSS is essential to prevent the manifestation of longer-term mental illness. As issued by OACHA in the last of July 2019: Since the mass demonstrations by Palestinians began in Gaza on 30 March 2018 until the end of July 2019 about 34.185 injured by Israel forces including (7104 boys, 549 girls, 24524 men, and 2008 women)According to the WHO mental ill-health represents one of the most significant public health challenges in the oPt. In the Gaza Strip, in particular, over half of the conflict-affected children may be affected by post-traumatic stress disorder, while an estimated 210,000 people are acutely vulnerable & suffer from severe or moderate mental health disorders. As reported in HNO 2019 the violence in Gaza has also generated widespread mental health & psychosocial (MHPSS consequences with over 50,000 people, half of them children in need of MHPSS responses, despite this high relevance of mental health disorders there is a gap between the mental health needs particularly of the acutely vulnerable and the provision of MHPSS.A 2019 CTCCM project evaluation showed that about 94% of boys and 86 % of girls had SDQ scores in (very high) distress categoryA rapid need assessment has been conducted by CTCCM's counselors for families who have injured children & affected by the recent violent events in GS which started since 30 March 2018 and the action report shows that children (boys& girls) have an increased risk of psychosocial & behavioral problems and mental health disorders. 350 of surveys have been conducted in Gaza, North of Gaza, the Middle area and khan Younis for 350 of affected children (boys& girls) including (25 boys &4 girls aged from 6-12 yrs.) and (301 boys & 20 girls aged from 13-18 yrs.) The action report shows that the affected boys suffer from specific psychosocial and behavioral problems as frustration, fear, tension, anxiety, excessive nervousness, sever aggression behavior, negative outlooks for society, hyperactivity. Intense excitation. getting angry. nightmares & bad dreams, introversion, while girls have difficulty in speech as (Stuttering), bedwetting, fear, tension, anxiety, excessive nervousness, nightmares, and bad dreams. Both of them have extra problems as Sense of disability, sense of loneliness, low concentration, verbal and physical violence. Those problems reflect adversely on the major tasks of childhood for achieving healthy development &functioning in the physical, cognitive, emotional and psychosocial domainsTo address this critical situation the project will focus on working on the protection mainstreaming and providing MHPSS services to strengthen the resilience of children (boys, girls & adolescents) and their caregivers (mothers &fathers) as well as communities in the face of chronic exposure to violence by providing community-based MHPSS to affected children & caregivers by using gender and age-sensitive resilience-building approaches, special attention will be payed to individuals & groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance & services. Supporting parents, service providers working in CBOs with psychosocial intervention and capacity building to better take care of and respond to the needs of children in distress.

2 - Activities

1- Provision of emergency child protection responses, including case management, psychosocial support for vulnerable children & families who have been affected by violence as:-1.1 Group-based psychosocial support &life skills sessions for 1020 of affected children (11-18 Yrs) (180 girls & 840 boys) using CBI-classroom/community/camp-based intervention evidence-based module to increase their resilience in coping with their psychological &health status, minimize and manage their psychological stressors as a result of the traumatic events (60 cycles, each 17 participants / 10 sessions 2 hrs. per a session)-2.1 Group counseling deep sessions for 200 of affected children 11-18 Yrs (170 boys & 30girls) who have weaknesses in their self-confidence, they will be provided with self-care techniques by using mind-body module (20 cycles/each 10 participants / 5 sessions)-3.1 Joint sessions for 1020 targeted children(180 girls & 840 boys (11-18 Yrs)) & 1000 their mothers aged from 25-59 Yrs to enhance the relationship between children & their mothers and asserting the duties and rights for the two parties (126 joint sessions, each targeting 17 of children & her mother (one session/child & her mother)-4.1 Outdoors activity (Camping days) for targeted children to enhance their wellbeing& develop their abilities & potentials-5.1 Implement community-based initiatives to enhance the social participation to deliver messages about their rights (the right of the play, education and live in security)-6.1 Child protection case management and referrals for 100 of affected children(20 girls, 80 boys) (11-18 Yrs.) to specialized child protection, Individual counselling, mental health, legal, social & educational services through accessing appropriate safe services in accordance to the national referral pathway in coordination with child protection network & the social development Ministry. CTCCM has online access system about cases in need protection & advanced of MHPSS services /referral to other related centers CTCCM psychologists will provide individual counseling sessions for affected children who in need. CTCCM's specialists have participated in case management & referral system training & coaching through UNICEF, NRC, GCMHP & War Child. CTCCM also has strong capacity in case management supervision and referred many cases to GCMHP and CPN -7.1 Family counseling sessions using family counseling IRPAL module for 20 families of the most affected children (provide each family with 6 sessions /2 hrs. per session)-8.1 Furnishing premises of 8 CBOs /NGOs in GS with basic supplies as first aid kit, chairs, tables &materials needed to implement the activities2-Provision of MHPSS for 1100 for adults(parents) (25 -59 yrs.)as follow:1.2 Group-based caregivers support for 1100 parents (1000 women & 100 men) of affected children within CSI- caregivers support intervention (WHO module) to manage their stress, emotional strengthen their social networks and support, build their coping mechanisms and how to deal with children's psychological & behavioral problems (65 cycles /each 17 women/men/6 sessions).3-Capacity building training for 58 of Child protection workers (22-45 Yrs) on the guidelines on MHPSS including:-1.3 Helping the Helpers sessions for the project's technical team and our partners' staff (M&F) within using ICRC a recognized-evidence-based protocol to enhance & motivate the helpers' job performance,supporting their psychological status &minimize their work stressors may impact negatively on their psychological status, also to give them self-care methods to provide better care for themselves and the target beneficiaries (2 helping the helpers activity)-2.3 MHPSS & protection training for 18 of project's technical team to develop their capacities on CBI, CSI & family counseling modules and referrals protocol-3.3 Child PSS &protection needs training for 40 of child protection workers (20F, 20 M) of CBOs to develop their capacities

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

CTCCM believes that each one of the target beneficiaries must have equal opportunity in terms of access to the project services according to his/her needs and benefits from the project activities regardless of gender, religion, culture.Our intervention is based on conducting an analysis of the protection context according to the gender-sensitive in safety and integrity way and understanding that women, girls, boys, and men have distinct needs, priorities, roles, responsibilities, limitations and protection needs to address their needs accordingly. The MHPSS services contribute in respecting and protecting the target groups' rights and diversity also CTCCM avoid causing harm by ensuring the decreasing of human rights violations and works within the child protection minimum standards and CHILD protection policies and procedures that mitigate any potential protection risks for the target groups to increase their access to the project MHPSS services.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The project intervention will focus on targeting the most vulnerable groups of boys, girls, adolescents, women, men who affected directly or indirectly by the conflict-related violence in Gaza Strip by ensuring that all the vulnerable groups and individuals are able to access MHPSS services equally and without any discrimination. As well as CTCCM has a wide network with the near local CBOs /NGOs and child protection working group/cluster throughout Gaza Strip governorates to ensure the safety and security procedures for better access to the MHPSS and protection services CTCCM will provide the target groups with safe transportation to the centers that implementing the project's activities, Transportation will be provided to families with economic or health problems and may hinder their access to places of implementation of activities. Furthermore, CTCCM will make a particular effort to identify community vulnerabilities and include groups less accessible or those facing additional risk or challenge.
