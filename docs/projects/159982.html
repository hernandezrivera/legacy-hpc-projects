Project Summary

This project aims to improve the provision of Gender Based Violence services to 7,250 girls and women through specialized healthcare responses including psychological care, case management and community based psychosocial support in Women and Girls Safe Spaces. The total number of beneficiaries targeted is 7,250, including 3,950 women and girls from IDP communities and another 3,300 from the host communities.
The 12 months project will be implemented in 4 districts of Gedo; Dollow, Luuq, Garbaharey and Belet Hawa. In Gedo region the protracted humanitarian situation and particularly displacement has eroded protective environment for women and girls increasing GBV risks. GBV survivors have limited access to GBV services majorly due to lack of adequate services and the stigma associated with seeking GBV services (Trocaire Gender and Human Rights Analysis, September 2019).
The Protection 2020 Humanitarian Needs Overview draft mentions findings from the recent JMCNA that indicates that IDP women and girls (as reported by 31% of interviewed households) and host communities (18%) still have considerable concerns about GBV. Women and girls among IDPs (63% of the interviewed households) and host community (72%) were aware of where and how to seek services for GBV incidents. However, only a small percentage of the IDPs (14% of the interviewed households) and host community (13%) members use the referral pathways. The low utilization of the referral pathways could be attributed from prevailing stigmatization of GBV survivors and lack of information. The project is aligned to the Protection cluster specific objectives one and three. Trocaire will respond to highlighted needs by strengthening specialized clinical management of rape services and psychological care. Case management will be provided in health facilities and psychosocial support through women and girl’s safe spaces. The services will be provided through static and mobile structures with a strong referral system developed between mobile structures and health facilities.

Project Activities

Emergencies affect men, women, boys and girls differently, the preexisting social norms particularly inequalities are exacerbated during emergencies resulting in higher vulnerability based on gender, identity group (clan), disability and displacement. Trocaire’s intervention will seek to address stigma associated with GBV services by primarily building the capacity of health staff to respond compassionately to survivors of GBV. Additionally mobile services will be provided within IDP camps to bring services closer to the community. A strong referral network will be developed to facilitate referral of cases requiring specialized interventions.• Survivor-centered multi-sectoral support (medical, legal, pss, and safety) and case management (GBV). Trocaire will provide specialized medical responses to GBV including clinical management of rape, wound care and management of medical complications relating to other forms of GBV. 4 health facilities in Dollow, Luuq , Belet Hawa and Garbaharey will offer medical responses to GBV. Case management will be offered within health facilities and through women and girls safe spaces (WGSS) attached to facilities and some located in IDP camps to respond to needs of women and girls. Psychosocial support will be provided through WGSS that will offer structured activities, educative sessions and skills building.• Training of duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff (GBV). Capacity building of health workers on compassionate care and appropriate response to survivors of gender based violence will be undertaken in all 4 districts. All health workers in the facilities offering medical response will be trained on psychological first aid and appropriate attitudes and ways to support survivors .• Skills training for girls and women in safe spaces. They will receive life skills that will enable them get access to income in the long term.• Conduct participatory safety audits at health facilities and other service delivery points to point out barriers to equitable access of services. Recommendations from the safety audits will be used as advocacy tools by Trocaire in collaboration with women and girls, and other local organizations to remove access barriers for women and girls.• Material and in-kind support will be provided to survivors of GBV to implement safety plans.• Construction and equipment of safe spaces for women and girls from IDP and host communities. These will be located in central locations that are easily accessible and where oversight of the facilities will be done by qualifies health staff.• Production and airing of radio shows on protection issues and behavior change advocacy to contribute to positive behavior change by the community in reducing GBV and reducing stigma that survivors of GBV face.• Production of IEC material to communicate safe practices and positive social norms that create a conducive, non-violent environment for women and girls.

Mitigation Measures

1a Protection of CiviliansMitigation measures:- Risk monitoring and safety audits in project sites to ensure project sites are not located within areas of potential attacks- The service will have mobile outreach to ensure services are taken closer to address access barrier and that people are not leaving their locations to access services. - A strong referral system will be put in place to refer cases requiring advanced services;- Providing mobile outreach services to bring aid closer to beneficiaries;- Linking beneficiaries will service providers closer to their homes.2a Exclusion and marginalization of marginalized communitiesMitigation measures:- All communities systematically represented in governance structures of projects conducted- Project volunteers will systematically include members of marginalized communities- Phone number for feedback and complaints disseminated among all communities in areas targeted- Permanent contact with marginalized communities maintained by teams on the ground, for protection monitoring, advocacy, and inclusion;- Outreach programmes conducted to attrack more vulnerable beneficiaries.2b Exclusion and marginalization of women and girls in consultationsMitigation measures:- • A detailed analysis has been conducted to exclusion and marginalization risks• Staff who speak language of minority groups will be hired to provide services • Number of female staff will be increased to facilitate better participation, access and utilization • Information sharing on services will be provided in language of minority groups, feedback and complaints mechanisms have also been translated into the two main dialects of Somali including Afmaay widely spoken by minority groups• Platforms for women and girls participation will be enhanced, requiring gender representation in committees, involving women and girls at all stages of the project (female specific forums to enable women raise their concerns)

Please provide further explanation

Trocaire Protection activities are integrated within all other sectors including Health, WASH, and Education. The involvement of the community is emphasized through consultations and outreach programmes that seek to challenge detrimetal social norms and increase acceptance of women and girls' health rights as human rights. They also seek to reduce gender based violence through trusted community structures such as the traditional midwives who are well versed with the community norms and traditions- they serve a critical role of changing mindsets in order to improve the general well-being of women and girls.
