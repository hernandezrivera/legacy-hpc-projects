Project Summary

According to HNO 2024 the emphasizes the urgent need for emergency education
services to support up to 7.1 million school aged children and teachers, including CwD.
There are more than 2.45 million Out of School Children (OoSC) (46% girls, 54% boys) and
over a million who are at risk of dropping out. Learning environments are unconducive due to overcrowding, lack of school
furniture/school supplies, and operational WASH facilities. Lack of electricity is affecting
education supply, as schools lack heating, cooling, and lighting to operate in multiple
shifts. There is a decrease in the availability of qualified teachers, especially in rural
areas. The education infrastructure was already insufficient prior to the earthquake. An
estimated 3,700 schools in Syria in need of rehabilitation/repairs.
GOPA-DERD will target 2000 children in risk of drop-out (6-18 years) in: Jaramana (Rural Damascus), Severity scale is 4, Qteilbiyyeh (Lattakia- Jablah), SS is 4, Muhradah (Hama- Muhradah), SS is 3, Khan Shaykun (Idleb- Al Ma’ra), SS is 3
GOPA-DERD will conduct trainings for 200 teachers about education in emergencies, life skills topics, first aid and risk and disaster management. Rehabilitation of 8 schools with furniture and equipping with solar power system for 11 schools.
School kits which will be provided, include a back bag and stationery, with light meals for children during the sessions, teaching tools will be provided for teachers. In coordination with MoE, trainings for teachers will be to improve their communication skills and to apply active learning methods with food vouchers for them. For rehabilitation of schools the needed works will be conducted through contracting with good contractors as per GOPA-DERD procurement policies.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

GOPA-DERD will implement the activities as following: 2000 students will be selected (500 students per location). As per the MoU between GOPA-DERD and the MoE, coordination will take place with the departments of education in the targeted governorates to select schools that meet the school’s selection criteria of safety and accessibility. After the school’s selection, FGDs will be conducted with the schools’ teachers, admins, and the students' parents to assess the educational and trainings needs. Pre-tests will be applied to the registered students to evaluate their educational level and to select the subjects they need, then the students will be divided into classes based on their educational level (20 students per class). Moreover, to provide quality education, qualified teachers who have experience, apply active learning methods, and have good communication skills will be selected through in-person interviews. Regarding the sessions’ target: grades from (1 to 12) students, 240 sessions. The students' and parents' feedback will be taken into account regarding the attendance hours, during the weekend, or after the official school hours; taking into consideration the sunrise and sunset. School kits will include a back bag and stationery as per the global UNICEF criteria. The stationery will be distributed in two batches. Students will be provided with light meals (cake, biscuits, juice, milk) during the sessions. Stationery as well as teaching tools will be provided for teachers. In coordination with MoE, training will be conducted for teachers participating in the NFE courses to improve their communication skills, and encourage them to apply active learning methods, as well as raise their awareness about dealing with potential risks. The training will include pre-and post-tests, and the trainer will be selected from experienced technical specialists. Post-exams will be applied at the end of the course for all students to measure the NFE sessions’ impact. • The teachers’ training beneficiaries will be provided with a food voucher • The first step of Rehabilitation works and providing solar power system will be by nominating schools based on MoE recommendations, then GOPA-DERD engineering team will select the schools based on field visits to ensure damages and needed maintenance to enhance the learning environment and keep into account for solar power system especially for schools with two shifts, full technical study for each school will be prepared. Regarding the rehabilitation for WASH facilities the implementation will be ensure separate toilets considering the privacy and safety for females and males of students and teachers, the number of these facilities according to the number of teachers and students, After completing the implementation works the schools will be handed over to related educational directorates.
