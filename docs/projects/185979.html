Project Summary

This project is aimed at providing food assistance and livelihood to IDPs and vulnerable groups heavily affected by the crisis within vulnerable communities in the three sub-division of Kupe-Muanenguba Division. This project targets IDPs who have been displaced from North West, Lebialem and other communities in Nguti Sub-division who are heavily hit and now settle in Bangem, Tombel and some few areas of Nguti Sub-division where there is relative calmness. These IDPs have no livelihood and very little food available for their families, therefore, most children stand the chances of developing malnutrition. IDPs living with disabilities equally face greater challenges in term getting a source of livelihood and food. This food assistance will target IDPs especially women from the ages of 18-59 paying attention to widows, unmarried IDPs, girls displaced from the ages of 10-17, displaced facing severe food shortages/imbalance, people living with disabilities (male and female), elderly people, families hosting large number of IDPs with little food to eat, families who lost their bread winners within the course of the crisis, and families with little or no farm land in IDP sites to carry out small scale agriculture. The food items will help and sustain most women provide food to their children and avoid malnutrition. Also, focus will be on children who lost their parents during the crisis or whose parents have been displaced leaving the children behind with no related family member as a main source of providing food.
Since the year 2016, the North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions of Cameroon, have been experiencing social instability erupting into violence displacing thousands of people and disrupting livelihoods and agricultural activities of various communities. In recent months, Bangem and Tombel has received a large number of IDPs due to rising insecurity tension in the North West region.
As the crisis persists more households have been pushed into food insecurity, shortages specially IDPs and farming populations. This project thus targets vulnerable women/homes who in most cases the children look at for food on daily basis. This project is equally needed because IDPs livelihood and nutritional condition have been affected by COVID-19. Some might be interested to carry out small sustaining agriculture, business in localities like Tombel and Bangem but have little access to land, capital in a strange environment.
This project is also aimed to fortify foods for children and mothers to respond and prevent severe acute malnutrition levels. This will include diets to children to curb malnutrition and ensure appropriate growth. Its should be noted that since the beginning of the crisis in the NWSW, 'NO INTERVENTION' has been done in the domain of food security in Kupe-Muanenguba division. Reason while AFRINET have this year following favourable access/security condition to design a plan of action for intervention in food security. Therefore, the project will pay critical attention to alleviate the poor nutrition condition of children from the ages of 1-8 years in Kupe-Muanenguba Division. In addition, our nutrition team will design nutrition combination and through workshops with target population trained these beneficiaries/familie on how to maintain good nutrition even at moderate cost.
Despite the slight increase in humanitarian assistance especially in WASH from july 2020 to November 2020, overall needs remain high in terms of food assistance and livelihood to IDPs and host community in Kupe-Muanenguba Division. The volatile security conditions, coupled with limited access to the most affected rural areas within the division is now positive, and the acceptance by local communities( chiefs, NSAG, population in general) towards humanitarian intervention is actually favourable following our recently intervention in WASH. Also, larger scale food distribution will be done and livelihood(empowerment) to target population.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Activity 1: Provide unconditional food, IDPs and local vulnerable population. This activity will involve distribution of food items to IDPs in all the 3 subdivisions of Kupe-Muanenguba division. First, identification and registering of IDPs will be carried out by trained field staff and volunteers with criteria set. Project planning and mobilisation of resources and all related stakeholders starts as funding agreements are to be signed by AFRINET and the donor. This will be instrumental in the sense that implementation starts immediately funds are received. The hygiene and and sanitation unit of AFRINET will make sure that effective hygiene and sanitation condition are available in various distribution sites. This will equally consider COVID-19 prevention measures from WHO and other administrative stakeholders in Cameroon. This hygiene and sanitation condition at distribution sites will also reduce the threats of a recent Cholera outbreak recorded in other divisions of the South West Region of Cameroon.Also, registration of IDPs and other beneficiaries will be planned and effected to make sure criteria for selection relay capture the most needy and most vulnerable. While this registration is being conducted, the list will submitted to the donor or review and approval. Distribution sites will consider key factors such as the Beneficiary Centered Approach; safety, equitable access, and transparency at all levels. Mainstreaming gender will be a key component of the project with distribution messages. Monthly reports will be sent to the donor and effective monitoring and evaluation will be done ensure that resources are put in the right use at the right time. Needs: Food items, human resource, volunteers, financeExpected Results: availability of food for IDPs, improvement in the nutritional values of IDPs and a reduction in hunger rate.Activity 2: Conduct food security assessments (CH, mVAM, FSMS, JAM, Multisector,ENSAN) to support and reinforce humanitarian planning the Nesus Humanitarian-Development and peace. This will involve field work by trained staff and volunteers on a practical approach to field assessment on food security. This activity will be designed and evaluated by a nutrition specialist (Consultant) with AFRICA DEVELOPMENT BANK (Mr Ngande Njabe; son of Kupe-Muanenguba, a very important development partner of AFRINET, willing and available at all times be it virtually to support our programmes that alleviate the nutritional challenges of vulnerable groups in Kupe-Muanenguba ). This consultant has already given the assurance, willingness to support AFRINET in any nutritional activity or plan of action at his best in Cameroon. Considering his practical experience in nutrition, he and the other AFRINTE team staff will design this assessment to know the food security situation of Kupemuanenguba, which is one of the pillars of food supply in Cameroon. Key attention will be based on factors that most prominent of causing food insecurity paying attention as well to perishables, fruits ( that are consumed directly without passing through a process like cooking),. Aspects of food storage will also be a key concern in this assessment. Needs: Assessment questionnaire, training of staff, finance, field materials, technical supportExpected Results: Assessment results achievedWith livelihood, registration of beneficiaries will be done alongside beneficiaries of food distribution. Criteria will be set by the team such that the beneficiaries decide the type of livelihood and empowerment they will prefer and where. It should be noted that key empowerment staff will ensure proper follow up on timely basis defined ahead of time. During this follow up, beneficiaries will be sensitized on the value of money and ho t improve the worth of their current value.
