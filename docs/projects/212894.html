Project Summary

This intervention aims to enhance the warmth for the IDPs NW Syria camps to alleviate their suffering of harsh winter which led to increase the winter diseases. The proposed will target the most vulnerable people who meet the vulnerability and eligibility criteria as (PWD, elderly, headed by child or woman, poor) with heating, NFIs and clothes kits. Around 4290 HHs / 22740 individuals will be benefited from this intervention through 2 modalities in-kind and CVA as follow:
1- Heating materials: the proposed in total value 163,350 USD will target 3290 families who contains 5 members (HHs, wife, 3 children) with heating kits (pomace olive) 200kg will be allocated to each family for one round as in-kind assistance. As expected 200kg of pomace olive sufficient for a family in one month.
2- Non-Food Items: the proposed in total value 45,000 USD will target 500 HHs / 2500 individuals with non-completed NFI kits each family, each family will be benefited with a kit contains (1 plastic sheet, 2 blankets, 2 mattresses, 1 carpet) to rehabilitate their old tent's items and protect them from the harsh winter especially for the heavy rains and floods. This assistance will be in-kind.
3- Winter children's clothes vouchers: this project in total value 37,700 USD will target 500 HHs / 2500 individuals with vouchers for children clothes protect them and enhance the warmth for the children in the tents during the winter. The targeted group of age will be in years 4-14 boys and girls. The plan to transfer the families from their camps to the clothes shops to redeem their vouchers in value (60 USD per family), it gets around 12 pieces of clothes as (jacket, pajama, trousers, blouse, boot, socks).
In all implementation, HA team considering the data collection and need assessment from the field, after that verify from the beneficiaries. The post monitoring process is one of the most important activities which conduct by MEAL staff after the implementation has been done. HA programmes team try to apply the clusters guidelines in all projects.
