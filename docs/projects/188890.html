Project Summary

The armed wars that took place on the land of the city of Raqqa and its countryside, and the resulting negative variables, we find today many facts and influences that directly or indirectly contributed to the development of society in the city of Raqqa for new concepts that give rise to the concept of citizenship, seeking to provide humanitarian community services , such as contributing to solving social problems.
Because of the wars, new residents came to the city of Raqqa, and as a result of these developments, a new social force grew in the society of the Syrian city of Raqqa, but it needs to reconsider the equality between men and women.
The field of women’s economic empowerment is one of the main entry points for establishing equality between women and men, and this field derives its importance from the requirements of the current stage, which has given great importance to empowering women economically, socially and politically as a basic determinant of supporting society in Raqqa, and to achieve the goals of the Weqaya Association’s strategy, and the Weqaya Association program that Pay special attention to the advancement and protection of women through a set of necessities:
1- Promoting women's rights and activating the principle of equality.
2- Combating all forms of discrimination.
3- Combating violence against women.
4- Strengthening the economic integration and empowerment program for women.
The problem: the inability of women (the displaced and the host) to develop themselves in the social, political, economic and cultural fields, due to the lack of support and the lack of sufficient funding to meet their needs.
Where the rate of women’s participation in the labor market is weak, and the rate of women’s participation in public life is weak, and since women want the participation of women in the labor market, the illiteracy rate in education and vocational training for women has increased, and the percentage of abused women has increased.
- The main objective of the project: to contribute to improving the economic situation of 200 women (breadwinners - widows - divorced - disabled - single) affected by the war, their ages (20 to 45 years) for a period of two years. This is done by creating multidisciplinary centers, enhancing access to safe spaces, building women’s capacities, providing psychological support and vocational training for those interested, and providing the necessary funding for small projects, in five population areas where displaced women and hostesses are present in the targeted areas of Raqqa (the locations are mentioned). in activities).
The project will also strengthen protection procedures and mechanisms, with the full participation of the community, to prevent any violations or violence, by forming protection committees, training them and activating their role to ensure that cases that need protection services are referred to the service providers department on a daily basis.
- the expected results :
1- Promoting equal access to decent work.
2- Providing opportunities for professional advancement for women.
3- Improving the economic status of women and empowering them with the means of production.
4- Enhancing the gains and reducing the differences between women and men.
5- Building the capacities of the various women participating in the field of economic empowerment.
6- Ensuring equality and equal opportunities at all levels.
7- Reducing the gender gap in all fields.
• Expected impact indicators:
1- The percentage of women entering the market.
2- The percentage of women’s representation in decision-making in the public and private sectors.
3- The number of mothers benefiting from the support provided within the framework of the project.
4- The rate of women's employment.
5- The rate of employment of disabled women.
6- Percentage of skilled women in the field of vocational rehabilitation by specialization.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Before the program begins, or in its early stages, we reach out to parents, partners, and other important guardians of the participating women's lives to explain the benefits of entrepreneurship, and to share successful examples of women entrepreneurs. • We will include problem-solving, communication, and negotiation skills in training to prepare women to negotiate this resistance as it arises. • Encouraging participating women to learn about work readiness - interview skills - work engagement skills • We will develop training materials and methodologies for training accordingly. • We avoid technical terms and specialized audio vocabulary, and use realistic examples that are familiar to the participants. • We encourage participating women to draw on their own experiences (if not professional, then personal), and offer frequent opportunities for hands-on practice in a structured environment. • Addressing financial literacy at any level that is appropriate and required. • Weaving simple arithmetic skills, especially basic functions such as counting and measuring, throughout the training period to increase participants' sense of mastery and their understanding of the integrated nature of these skills in daily life, especially in business. • Precautions for COVID 19 .
