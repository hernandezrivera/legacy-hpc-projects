1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the life conditions and access to food of 22.000 (13.200 F /8.800 M) members of IDPs families and vulnerable host community members in Balama, Montepuez and Namuno districts renforcing and giving continuity to the ongoing AVSI multisector interventions on CCCM, Protection, Wash, Food Security, Education.The program foresees Food-for-asset (FFA) to build infrastructure for agriculture and accommodation/resettlement sites targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities; reinforcing resilience of the communities throught Training on sustainable agriculture techniques targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

O.S.2.1.1 Food-for-asset (FFA) to build infrastructure for agriculture and accommodation/resettlement sites targeting IDPs living in sites and host communitiesThis activity entails the creation of community seedling nurseries for horticultural and fruit produce, irrigation canals from main water sources and set-up of plots suitable for irrigation, planting of trees (windbreaker, repellent, and fruit varieties) in resettlement camps, and general maintenance works in the resettlement sites whereas needed.1.2 Training sessions on sustainable agriculture techniques targeting IDPs living in sites and host communitiesTraining sessions will be based mainly on conservation agriculture techniques, and some agroforestry techniques aimed at ensuring that the soils cultivated do not undergo depletion of nutrients and erosion due to practices based on monoculture, use of chemicals, and heavy reains1.3 Seeds and agriculture tools distributions targeting IDPs living in sites and host communities.Distribution of seeds and tools will privilege the IDPs community and will be mainly based on crops suitable for the rainy season considering the high prevalence of rain-fed plots allocated to IDPs.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, selection of individual interventions will be done considering vulnerability criteria and not the status. Criteria and Selection process will be explained and widespread to local communities, institutions, in order to ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality and at the same time guartantee that specific need of U 5 years old Protection monitoring will be garuatee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY (DIGE 11/2020) on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. All staff are always oriented and re-oriented on the need to seek the views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. AVSI Foundation will carry out household surveys and ad hoc studies to shed lights on the condition of special and vulnerable groups. The results will be presented and validated with all communities in the camp and host communities, local government, as a means to establish a productive fact-based conversation with affected populations.In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account, but, more importantly, solutions in line with the specific contexts and people capable to support such initiatives are identified. Example of these practices could be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/c891c32a-7c60-4aa9-bf6d-a8b48a84730ahttps://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/multi-sector-household-survey-rumbek-north-county-lakes-state-june-2021The capillary and continuous presence that AVSI has in the communities targeted by the intervention (AVSI has more than 80% of field based staff) ensures that such fruitful cooperation with project beneficiaries continues throughout the project’s implementation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention and priority to inclusion as beneficiaires.AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. Examples of such work in similar context can be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/e3f17126-d1f5-4a78-80a4-99dea7c46e69.AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy effciency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal.In addition of that, it will be prioritazed the use of local seeds/foods and facilitate local agricultural production in order to adopt an environment-friendly and a more sustained approach.For Assets provision, AVSI will use local and natural materials.In order to guarantee protection Environment, AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
