Project Summary

Objective: This project aims to enhance maternal and child nutrition in Venezuela, with a particular focus on improving the early detection and treatment of wasting and other life-threatening forms of acute malnutrition in early childhood. Additionally, it seeks to prevent malnutrition, micronutrient deficiencies, and overweight conditions during childhood, adolescence, pregnancy, and breastfeeding.
Project Description: In alignment with UNICEF's global strategy and commitment to safeguarding and promoting diets, services, and practices that support optimal nutrition, growth, and development for all children, adolescents, and women, this initiative aims to provide lifesaving interventions while strengthen public health and nutrition services. The strengthening initiatives will encompass community health, primary healthcare centers, and referral centers while ensuring comprehensive approach with teams focused on water, sanitation, and basic hygiene promotion. Additionally, will provide treatment supplies and anthropometric equipment to facilitate the delivery of maternal and child healthcare services and effectively manage malnutrition and its associated complications.
A significant component of the project is focused on capacity building for healthcare workers and community members. This will be executed in collaboration with implementing partners, academic institutions, the Ministry of Popular Power for Health (MPPH), and the National Institute of Nutrition (NIN). This partnership will facilitate the early identification of wasted and at-risk children and undernourished PLW.
Strategies and Implementation:
UNICEF's strategy will be based eight pillars detailed in below:
1. ENHANCING CHILD NUTRITION
Growth monitoring, breastfeeding, complementary and child feeding counselling and supplementation with MNP, adolescent nutrition counselling and supplementation, deworming, management of MAM, SAM and Complicated SAM cases, prevention with LNS for the most vulnerable children
2. ENHANCING MATERNAL NUTRITION
Counselling, Supplementation with MMS, deworming, LNS-PLW for PLW at nutritional risk
3. STRENTHEN SUPPLY CHAIN 
4. STRENTHEN INFORMATION SYSTEM
5. IMPROVEMENT OF CAPACITY BUILDING OF FRONTLINE HEALTH WORKFORCE
6. REINFORCEMENT OF COMMUNITY PLATFORMS
7. PROMOTING HEALTHY FOOD ENVIRONMENTS
8. INTEGRATING LIFESAVING WASH SERVICES (WATER ACCESS, SANITATION, SBC)
The project will prioritize community engagement by collaborating with implementing partners and local health and nutrition authorities to develop sustainable nutrition practices. Through close partnerships with local communities, the intervention will be tailored to align with cultural dietary practices effectively. UNICEF efforts will emphasize knowledge sharing and resource optimization, while promoting empowering communities and establishing robust local support networks.
Monitoring and Evaluation: To ensure the effectiveness of the project, we will implement a robust monitoring and evaluation system. This will involve high frequency monitoring of nutritional and health status, competency assessments of health professionals after training, and feedback through third party monitoring to assess the impact of programs interventions. It will also include monitoring the distribution and use of supplies, as well as evaluating the quality of case management based on standardized treatments.
Prioritized areas: the project will mainly focus the full interventions on 54 municipalities, in Amazonas, Apure, Bolívar, Delta Amacuro, Distrito Capital, La Guaira, Miranda, Sucre, Táchira, and Zulia states, however this also includes the support to national programs such as deworming program and support to Nutritional and Educational Recovery Services at nationwide, especially for management of cases with acute malnutrition and complications.
UNICEF will promote the Community-based Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM) and simplified approach to address response of SAM & MAM cases.
