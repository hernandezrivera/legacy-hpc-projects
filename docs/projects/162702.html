Project Summary

This project will target returnees at Um Baru Locality, ND State with multi- sectorial activities (education.WASH, The target will be children at basic education and their teachers and PTAs from the most vulnerable and needy groups, namely the project will target the following schools:
- Um Baru Basic school for Girls with 470 total students
- Um Baru Basic school for Boys with 460 total students
- Shangari Mixed Basic school with 643 total students (M 420, F 223)
- Abu Laiha Mixed Basic school with 760 total students (M 500, F 260)
- Arkuri Mixed Basic school with 830 total students (M 610, F 220),
All these schools need classrooms construction or rehabilitation of TLS, WASH and Sanitation activities, training for teachers and PTAs, EiE materials for students and teachers, recreational materials and CFS activities.

1 - Needs

Um Baru Locality located far West North (240 Km) from Al Fasher Town. Surrounded by Meilet and Malha from the East, Kutum from the South, the Great Desert from the North, Chad and Altina from the Eastern direction. The Locality witnessed a recent voluntary returns from in and out of the Country. PBA Sub- Office team conducted a FGDs with key informants from the area and assessed the education needs of the people there and named some of the most needy schools to address their urgent assistance we could provide. This information confirmed by Mr. Hussein Adam Abdellah the X- Education Unit Director at Um Baru Locality till 2014 and who live now at the Locality. The project will address the urgent need of construction or rehabilitation of classrooms, provision of clean drinking water and hand washing points, latrines construction, EiE and recreational materials, training for the teachers and PTAs on core and EiE subjects, awareness raising on the importance of education and enrollment, hygiene promotion campaigns and spreading a culture of peace among the returnees.

2 - Activities / Outputs

- Rehabilitation and construction of new and damaged classrooms and latrines at the most needy (5) basic targeted schools is the most important intervention right now. - Provision of the EiE and recreational materials for students and teachers is also a priority. - Training for teachers and PTAs on EiE, PSS, Core subjects is essential especially now. - Health, education and hygiene promotion campaigns at schools and at the community is very important as well as HRs, CC and law.- Establish complaints mechanisms and orient the community about the humanitarian assistance laws and policies and how they could make use of them at their level. - Provide rapid response and dignity hygienic kits to meet protection needs among students girls and family visits by social workers to the families to raise their awareness on GBV and other social harms around the targeted schools.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will avoid using harmfully the natural resources on its activities such as wood from trees or mis- use of water sources, digging latrines to access the groundwater. PBA will organize environmental awareness and health promotion campaigns as part of the activities, planting trees making use of the surplus water.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project will provide safe learning spaces for children, will fence all the schools, employee social workers to provide PSS support to the students and families, consider gender at constructions, provide dignity kits for the girls at the targeted schools from grade 6 and 7, the project will target the teachers, PTAs and social workers with PSS training to support the students and their families and PBA will consider orientation to the all target groups on HRs, CRC and PSEA and establish mechanisms to report cases among them.
