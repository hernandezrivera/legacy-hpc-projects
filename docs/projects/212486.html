Project Summary

Ain Tarma is a Syrian town located on the northern bank of the Barada River, administratively affiliated with
Arbin District - Rural Damascus Governorate.
Because of the difficult circumstances that the region went through, which created a very poor local community in need of care and support, suffered from a low level of public services, in addition to the low level of health services, which made people visit clinics and medical centers continuously due to the inability to go to private hospitals.
Hence, the idea of providing diagnostic and therapeutic services by establishing a medical center equipped.
to receive, examine and treat patients within the following departments:
- Gynecology
- Pediatrics
- Internal
- Psychiatry
- Medication
The goal is to provide basic health services in an integrated manner with a high degree of efficiency, so that they are available to everyone and easy to access, based on fair distribution and understanding of the
demographic features of the region.
With the importance of supportive, technical, organized and continuous supervision and striving to raise the awareness of society’s concepts and culture towards health center services as places to develop the health of society and prevent the occurrence of disease for its members and provide the best methods of provision of primary health care.
Taking into account the keenness to understand the patient’s needs, providing the service on time, being accurate in medical test, diagnosis and treatment, maintaining files, responding immediately to inquiries and complaints, in addition to provide adequate training to local stuff (that will be supplied from the local Area) on the proper procedure on dealing with beneficiaries such as (age and gender).
