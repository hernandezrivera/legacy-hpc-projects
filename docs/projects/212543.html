Project Summary

EPDC has many necessary medical equipment which can be used in Nashabiyeh – Eastern Ghouta – Rural Damascus (3 Eco – ECG – X-Ray) in addition to the offices and clinics equipment. Also, EPDC has a community center in Nashabiyeh – Eastern Ghouta – Rural Damascus Providing Protection services (GBV). EPDC plans to open the PHC center with the following clinics, cardiac, gynecology, internal, and pediatric clinics and a pharmacy to provide the patients with the prescribed medicines. This project will include PHC supplying with medical and non-medical items, PHC maintenance, medical devices maintenance, and generator maintenance if needed, buying fuel. The services which will be provided are as the following:
- Consultation (according to the diverse clinics).
- Provide patients with the prescribed medicines according to the medical prescriptions.
- Compensation of some contracted laboratories with the fees of number of lab tests.
- X-Rays depending on the need.
- Health Awareness sessions.
All needed people will be received to have the medical consultations and awareness sessions, but the other services’ beneficiaries will be determined according to a suitable selection criterion.
EPDC’s Center which contains a community center that is specified to raise the awareness about the Gender Based Violence, has a clear advantage on all similar health centers due to the great harmony between the health services and the protection services, which may let the beneficiaries notice the relation between these two essential centers indirectly.
The main risk which may be faced by EPDC is the same matter which enhance the necessary of the intervention, the distance from the city which make it difficult to recruit medical staff, but as EPDC has an experience there, the solution is by saving the transportation. Anyway, this risk is the same necessary matter that must stay in mind, the distance from the city makes the people in an essential need to obtain the services that cannot be obtained in the same region.
