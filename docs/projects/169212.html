Project Summary

High need of Water and sanitation materials were rapidly increased after the explosion in the seaport of Beirut, hundreds of thousands of people were affected, wash facilities were damaged, and a higher need to COVID.19 disinfectant materials was reported.
Through this project, Islamic Relief Worldwide will support to repair the WASH facilities in the affected areas, targeting more than 500 families, as well as will provide hygiene kits (including COVID.19 disinfectant materials, aligned with the WASH sector guidance on the kit composition, as well as menstrual hygiene management materials) for more than 8,000 families.
This intervention will be implemented in a full coordination with the WASH sector to avoid duplication or having any gaps, as it allows the affected families to recover earlier and enhance their protection against the pandemic of COVID.19.
IRW has already granted the fund (partially) for this intervention and the other part is almost granted (SLA under signature).

1 - Needs

Based on initial assessments, the explosions have compounded the fragility of the water and sanitationinfrastructure in the greater Beirut area.Significant number of water tanks and plumbing systems in buildings near the blast were damaged.An estimated 25 per cent of the buildings in the affected area have been disconnected from the mainwater network.In the absence of reliable water and sanitation services, vulnerable populations will need WASH kitsand other forms of in-kind assistance. where water and hygiene materials are vital to protect for COVID.19.

2 - Activities

1. Distribution of more than 4700 Hygiene kit (including protection materials from COVID.19)2. Conduct a need assessment in Karantina for installation of small water treatment plant serving the vulnerable community.
