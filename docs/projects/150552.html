Project Summary

The intervention seeks to provide water accessibility instruments in significance of efficient and effective water utilization and support the bedouin community’s resilience portrayed in protection against settlers abuse, better animal productivity and enhanced economic situation. In this context, the project targets the inhabitants of Fera'a community comprised of 13 households with a total of 104 individuals mainly considered as herders in addition to targeting their livestock through providing water inputs. The activities proposed are summarized as follows (The increased investment per individual is due to the fact that the beneficiaries are a small bedouin community that faces Israeli violations):
• Water quality testing of six samples of the main water source of Ain Feraa and the provision of a filter
• Spring rehabilitation including cleaning and maintenance, fencing against settlers, construction of retaining walls, closing of water cement pipes, reparation of the road leading to the spring through stairs construction
• Supplying water tanks and the installation of transmission lines.
• Conducting training on water management and transmission lines maintenance and Filter Maintenance.
• Providing drinkers and semiautomatic tanks.
The methodology of selecting the beneficiaries is derived from a field visit to the community, it was examined through sight, observation, interviews and questionnaires in terms of accessibility to basic means of life and it was chosen on the basis of being the most vulnerable community compared to nearby areas.
UAWC centralizes its projects within Area C and strives at promoting the steadfastness and sustainability of small farmers’ livelihoods and protecting and defending the farmers’ national and democratic rights, not to mention that one of the strategic objectives within its strategic plan 2018-2020, UAWC seeks at responding to emergencies, different variables and instability, having worked extensively with the most marginalized and vulnerable areas in Palestine and being established in correspondence to the continuous Israeli abuses in Palestine.

Needs

The community of Fera’a is an extremely vulnerable community. The settlers of the surrounding settlements keep abusing the community members and exploiting the natural spring of Ein Fera’a; which is the only water source for the community, for their bathing rituals. Access to the spring is females' and children's responsibility and herding. The community drinks from Feraa Spring and use it to water their herds. The community HH's with an average monthly income of NIS 1,670 below the national poverty line. This is considered a great challenge for these families due to the difficulty of transporting fodder and water, and the possibility of transporting them to farmers in this area due to the lack of water lines or tankers, more than 300 cubic meters of water are required for these herds monthly (ie, 10 cubic meters per day for 1,200 heads of sheep raised in this area). Plus another 310 cubic meters of water required for HH's needs. Lack of water will have significant impact on the Health of the people, their Hygiene and will weaken the physiological capacity of their herds thus affecting their livelihood. The lack of clean water also limits the consumption of water for humans and sheep. In addition to that the region is one of the areas with high temperatures. In numbers, and as per UAWC assessment, 59% of households rely on livestock only and 41% rely on agricultural and livestock. Activities

The intervention’s proposed activities follow the following components: Activity 1. Water Quality Testing: extracting 6 samples of water and carry out lab testing of the following: pH, Electrical Conductivity (EC) & Total Dissolved solids, Chloride (CL-), Ammonium (NH4+), Nitrate (NO3-), Total Coliform, Fecal ColiformActivity 2. Providing 3 filters: at each distribution point a physical filter will be installed to purify water and make it available for HH use.Activity 3. Spring Rehabilitation and Protection: Cleaning and Maintenance, Fencing, Construction of retaining walls, Closing of Water Cement Pipes, Reparation of the road lead to the spring through Stairs Construction. Activity 4. Supplying water tanks and filters: Installation of 3 Water Tanks with the capacity of (5 Cubic Meters) near the gatherings and have it filtered from general Impurities, for HH use. Activity 5. Installation of a Solar Water Pump: One Unit of water pump and one valve. Activity 6. Supplying and Installation of Transmission Lines: 2 Pipes of 1,200 Meters, to supply water for the bedouin community who has no water connectionActivity 7. Conduct Trainings in water management and the transmission line maintenance and Filter maintenance in order to ensure sustainability of the Action. The training will include means to reduce water losses with information about the amount of water supply from the spring, and to acquire the minimum knowledge of maintenance in case of emergencies (clogging, faults in the pipe, or how to maintain the filter)Activity 8. the provision of tanks and semi – automatick drinkers and plastic tanks for each farmer in his area and linked to water networkActivity 9. Installation of a Biogas unit utilizing the animal waste that is available in large quantities and which can be used to generate electricity through a reactor or for provide the houses with gas for cooking in addition to be exploited to make organic fertilizer for agriculture.Activity 10. Providing legal support to the owner of the land to defend against the settlers continuous harassment (violations against citizens, swimming in and polluting the water spring).
