1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Cabo Delgado province had been experiencing severe consequences of the actions of non-state armed groups/insurgents particularly in the North, since 2017. This protracted crisis escalated when in the beginning of 2021 a violent attack to Palma district, on 23 of March triggered a severe disruption of services and a massive displacement of people, either fleeing from the conflict areas directly affected, such as Palma, Quissanga, Muidumbe, Macomia and Mocímboa da Praia and other areas and moving to safer districts in the South of the province. Currently, the estimation is that nearly 740.000 people from Cabo Delgado are displaced in different districts, including in Nampula and Niassa provinces.On one hand, by the time the school year official reopening in April 2021, after one year disruption due to COVID-19 pandemic, all schools in Palma and Moçimba da Praia and also most schools (more than 50%) of 7 other northern districts (including Ibo) remained closed. On the other hand, in the district considered safe or safer, the big challenge was to ensure a proper response to the huge demand caused by the sudden influx of IDP in host community schools. In the second half of 2021, with an increased number of national security forces in the affected districts and the arrival of international military forces slowly some normality is being restored. Reconstruction and development plans led by the government are in place supporting the framework for recovery.UNICEF has very actively supportedthe education local authorities and implementing partners to respond the current EiE needs in its cluster coordination role. Within this current proposal UNICEF will be adapting the response to ensure medium to longer term impact on the education sector through an integrated approach, for example: Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS) through a resilient approach, including WASH in schools, distribution of supplies – educational and hygiene materials, teacher training on Psychosocial Support (PSS), including referrals mechanism in collaboration with Child Protection colleagues and on Teachers in Crisis Context (TiCC) but also on Violence Against Children (VAC), Gender Based Violence (GBV) and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) supporting prevention of violence. At the same time, UNICEF response will be focusing on accelerated learning to help teachers and children with the learning loss and to support better learning outcomes and skills development, fostering engagement and active participation in communities, and labour market. Target groups: 75833 children and teachers 74,740 children from 0 to 15 (18,670 female IDP; and 18,700 male IDP) 1093 teachers (577 female and 516 male)

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

To address education needs in the targeted districts, UNICEF plans to provide:1. Teachers training on PSS, TiCC frameworks and cross cutting topics such as PSEA, GBV and VAC. Host and IPD- teachers will be benefitting from learning and capacity building opportunities to support them to address the learning, social and health challenges students might be facing due to the current situation. Raising awareness and give tools to teachers to refer students to services and support is a priority. Fostering peer support in also included in the TiCC framework.2. Accelerated and Alternative Learning. On one hand this proposal has a strong focus on boosting learning outcomes, particularly after school disruption due to COVID-19 and insecurity. UNICEF will be supporting in school children in catching up through Accelerated learning. On the other hand, as a way to prevent violence and recruitment Out of School Children and Adolescents (OOSCA) as also targeted to benefit from alternative learning opportunities such as life skills and Non Formal (NFE) initiatives3. Support to the ongoing improvement of education facilities. The TLS settled so far have been highly appreciated by local authorities and communities not only to respond to COVID-19 restrictions and safety measures in schools, but also to the demand caused by an increase of IDP students. Education Provincial Directorate (DPE) and emergency response partners in Cabo Delgado worked together, at the Provincial Education Cluster level to develop a new model for TLS, more sustainable, environmentally friendly, durable and easier to maintain with the resources existing in most of the communities. Along with the model for TLS also a new model for temporary latrines has been developed, also with local materials. 4. Basic educational and hygiene supplies (school in a box, recreation kits, learners’ and specific Early Childhood Education (ECE) kits) to support teachers and students in their teaching and learning processes. Many children are more vulnerable now due to the effects of conflict and insecurity. Forced displacement and migration has been increasing the level of vulnerability of families, not being able to provide the basics for their children to attend schools. Providing appropriate education and hygiene supplies cannot only ensure physical and emotional protection to children, but also attract parents, caregivers and children to school, ensure their enrollment and prevent dropout. 5. Monitoring- the monitoring process will be jointly done with DPE and partners to ensure quality implementation, participation and accountability on the outcomes planned.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A risk assessment process will be carried out respecting the origin of all communities. UNICEF aims to reduce the risks caused by hazards and anything else like social or cultural aspects that can harm children. Because many children and families are hosted by their families in the targeted districts, in our activities we will also involve these members of their families. In the places for children that are identified and permitted by the authorities to play and to do other activities, UNICEF will adhere to and promote all regulations set by the government and MINEDH in preventing COVID 19. The proposal envisions an integrated EiE & CP approach.As co-lead of the WASH and Education clusters and CP Area of Responsibility, and as active member of the ICCG, UNICEF is very well positioned to ensure a coordinated response to all protection risks working together with other agencies and organizations, mobilizing stakeholders, resources, avoiding overlaps and leveraging the impact of the intervention.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This proposal has a focus on Child Participation, namely under indicator 3 set of targets. Consultations and Complain mechanism will be in place so that beneficiaries can influence the project design and implementation. Necessarily the proposal will engage teachers, parents and caregivers, throughout Parents and Teacher Associations and School Management Committee engagement, holding UNICEF accountable for the outcomes committed with.In the monitoring of the process, UNICEF ensures that children are comfortable and engaged listened to, so the activities firstly aim to address their stress and traumas rather than learning and studying. In this monitoring, the communities, parents or caretakers of these children will be as much as involved as possible through creation of reference groups, discussion groups of different sex and age and also using the complaint boxes that will help us improve our implementation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNICEF will work with NGOs and staff experienced in delivery specific services and support for children with disabilities (CWD) so that they can fully participate in the activities. Therefore as explained above, these activities are organized in good collaboration with the protection, WASH and health cluster and their selected NGOs in order to provide an intersectoral service for all these children. Parents, caregivers and local authorities and community will be fully involved in the promotion of inclusive actitivies and education. In the long run, facing and overcoming challenges and reducing the prejudices from the general public and increasing their chances to actively participate in society will help them to grow stronger and more resilient

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The selected location of education facilities will be preceded by an evaluation of the location in collaboration with the local authorities, so that the location can be considered safe with materials that are safe for children to use, free of natural hazards (floods, storms) and clean with basic WASH conditions, and with confirmation on responsibilities for maintaining security, WASH and protection. At the same time the activities will aim to raise environmental awareness and protection of natural resources and avoiding erosion for instance by planting fruit and shade trees, creating living fences, grass planting and directing and harvesting rainwater etc.
