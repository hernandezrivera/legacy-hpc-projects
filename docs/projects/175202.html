1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

Logistics services are essential to facilitate the humanitarian response in Chad through transport of actors and humanitarian assistance. United Nations Humanitarian Air Services (UNHAS) provides this service to organizations involved in the humanitarian response in Chad.In Chad, road infrastructures are inadequate and often in poor conditions, and long distances between the capital and the intervention zones combine with insecurity to make access to many humanitarian intervention sites difficult. Herein, the need for continued humanitarian air service remains high as regular and safe access to implementation sites is a challenge for most humanitarian organizations operating in Chad.In addition, many of these areas, particularly in the east of the country, become completely inaccessible by road during the rainy season (between mid-May to September) due to floods and the closure of many dirt roads.In these conditions, air transport is the safest and most reliable way for humanitarian workers to travel safely from N'Djamena, Abéché, Goz Beida to more than fifteen localities in the South, Center, East and West to deliver humanitarian assistance. The United Nations Humanitarian Air Service (UNHAS), operated by WFP, is designed to support the immense humanitarian effort in Chad, through the provision of safe, reliable, efficient and cost-effective air transport to the humanitarian community, donors and government counterparts in areas where surface transport is limited or unavailable. In addition to providing scheduled passenger and light cargo transportation services, UNHAS facilitates critical lifesaving medical and security evacuations, upon user requests along with undertaking rehabilitation of airstrips to provide cost effective and direct access to deep-field implementation sites.Since 2016, UNHAS has opened regular lines to new destinations in the countries of the Lake Chad Basin, such as Maroua (Cameroon) following the demands and needs of the humanitarian community. UNHAS also facilitates high-level visits that are critical to supporting the humanitarian community in its advocacy efforts. The weekly flight schedule contains 22 destinations (19 in Chad and 04 in Cameroon) on a regular basis.The use of services divided by organizations is 46 % for NGOs, 50% for UN agencies, 2% for government counterparts, and 2% the diplomatic corps and the donor community.UNHAS operates a fleet of one medium size inter-regional aircraft Emb135 (jet) used for long distance destinations and one Beechcraft 1900D (19 seats) and two small size aircraft Caravan C208B (12 seats) that serve the deep field locations with rough runways only accessible by short takeoff and landing (STOL) aircraft. A 'hubs and spokes' network structure was established whereby the medium size aircraft perform longer range connections between hubs and provide loads to the smaller aircraft shuttling to smaller, challenging airfields. Two aircraft are strategically based in the East of the country bordering Sudan - in Abeche and Goz-Beida, one other aircraft is based in N'Djamena, providing connections between the West and the East of the country, the Southern region bordering Central African Republic and the Western region, i.e. the Lake Chad area, at the border with Nigeria, Niger and Cameroon and the forth aircraft based in Yaounde -Cameroon connecting the two countries - Chad and Cameroon.UNHAS expects to transport a total of 12,000 passengers in 2020, subject to user demand. Between January and November 2020, the service transported 8,943 passengers and 78.99 MT of essential light cargo accessing 23 destinations. It also carried out 67 life-saving medical evacuations, of which 05 were COVID-19 able-bodied medevac passengers. In 2019, UNHAS transported 15,176 passengers, 85 MT of cargo, and 87 medical evacuations across 22 destinations. The reduction in the figures in 2020 compared to 2019 is mainly due to reduced user demand as per the impact of the pandemic. UNHAS will continue to provide critical access and increasing frequencies to the implementation sites in 2021 to the emergency areas of the Lake region and to the South region, including access to airstrips on the basis of ad-hoc flights, upon user requests. For 2021, UNHAS will continue to rely on security networks to obtain information to mitigate threats to the security of the operation. UNHAS Chad will continue to provide air services to NGOs, UN agencies, donor representatives, the diplomatic community and implementing humanitarian partners, with the necessary access to rapidly reach remote or inaccessible beneficiaries.For 2021, UNHAS will maintain its current weekly schedule and operational model, based upon user demand. Furthermore, Tchadia Airlines has been approved for UN staff travel by UNDSS to facilitate connection between main cities within the country. Herein, UNHAS will continue the discussions for a harmonization of schedules to serve the hubs, whilst UNHAS will continue to serve the deep field locations. UNHAS considered all the main risks associated with the project and presented mitigation measures for each possible risk. In addition, building the capacity of international and national staff, NGOs and key authorities is a major component of UNHAS's footprint at the local level. As part of the broader efforts of the aid sector to develop a better aviation architecture in UNHAS 'areas of work, the service will continue to build the capacity of national and international staff, NGOs and authorities as a major component of the local level. UNHAS will continue to implement activities aimed at advancing the transfer of knowledge from aviation-related core disciplines and transferring skills towards strengthening national authority. UNHAS will continue to seek funds towards improving key airfields whereby rehabilitation works are planned for Bol, Guereda, Goz Beida, Mongo, Moussoro and Gore.
