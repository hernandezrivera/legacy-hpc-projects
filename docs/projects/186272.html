Project Summary

Building on GOALPrime Organization Nigeria’s expertise in designing and supporting the delivery of art based psychosocial support programs will target to reach 30066 People (Women, Men, Girls, and boys) across Monguno, KalaBalge, Damboa, and Maiduguri with 12 months of 2022, with the following service
I. Provision of structured recreational and creative services ( PSS) to vulnerable boys and girls including those living with Disability and caregivers with PSS, Counselling, and Positive parenting session.
II. Provide life skills education for adolescent girls and boys including those living with disability across Monguno, KalaBalge, Damboa, and Maiduguri.
III. Re-integrate and provide community-based reintegration assistance to girls, boys, and young people formerly associated with armed groups (CAAFAG)
IV. Target boys and girls living with disability with Child protection support services.
V. Carry out awareness-raising sessions, on child protection issues, positive parenting, and existing referral pathways to improve camps and community child protection structure.
VI. Provide training to the existing and functional Community-Based Child Protection Committee Members.
VII. Provide training on child protection and related areas to MWASD community volunteers to provide local context child protection services.
Conducting psychosocial support, recreational and non-formal education activities requires a diverse skillset spanning across the fields of child development, social work, physical and health educational, and education (teaching). While community volunteers currently receive training to introduce them to the basics of facilitating psychosocial support to children through the Child-Friendly Space (CFS) model, community engagement, and child protection, the current training does not provide direct coaching and mentoring to volunteers to enable them to properly organize and conduct activities with children. Feedback from monitoring visits shows that volunteers often lack the skills to create, organize, and lead structured activities that facilitate learning and skill development in children. In brief, while children are playing at Child-Friendly Spaces, they are missing a prime opportunity to acquire valuable life skills that will reduce psychosocial distress, increase their resilience, and prepare them for success in formal education and beyond.
In order to build the capacity of community volunteers so that they can run creative, recreational, and non-formal education activities within a psychosocial support framework that impacts and improves the lives of children, Goalprime Nigeria will develop a hands-on mentorship program for community volunteers which will be rolled out in collaboration with the Borno State MWASD. The mentorship programme will be hinged on creative art and life skills interventions for providing psychosocial support to children and include skills and methodologies for creating and maintaining structure when working with children in CFS, facilitating learning and acquisition of skills, as well as upholding rules and redirecting negative behaviors.
Currently, psychosocial support services for children in Borno are mainly being provided through CFS. Although this is reaching a substantial number of children, the integration of psychosocial support services within education provides a great opportunity to reach more children.
GPON will strive to use a combination of house structures within schools to build the capacity of housemasters/mistresses and prefects to organize PSS.

1 - Needs

Emergencies such as we have in Northeast Nigeria has severe consequences on children and their families as well as affect their ability to build back better. According to Nigeria’s HRP Needs Overview on https://www.humanitarianresponse.info/sites/www.humanitarianresponse.info/files/documents/files/ocha_nga_humanitarian_needs_overview_march2021.pdf.pdf' Children under the age of 18 years comprise more than 61% of the IDP, returnee and other populations affected by the humanitarian crisis in northeast Nigeria; with 55% girls. The projections of the Child Protection Subsector in the 2021 Nigeria HRP estimates that A total of 1,001,000 children (53% girls, 47% boys)and caregivers will be targeted with child protection services to prevent and respond to abuse, exploitation, neglect, violence and discrimination in north-east Nigeria. Forty-three per cent of the targeted population for the child protection response is in Borno state, 33% in Adamawa and 24% in Yobe. Among the LGAs in Borno, Maiduguri has the highest number of targeted children and caregivers. At least 6,000 children (more than 50 percent of girls) are estimated to have been violated by the Non-State Armed Groups. The crisis evidently has caused severe psychological distress amongst over 2 million young children and adolescents; Borno State being the epicenter. The conflict has not only disrupted the social fabric but has also negatively affected the ability of parents and communities to appropriately care for their children. At least 150,000 caregivers of vulnerable children have suffered from severe psychosocial distress. The social relations disruption, the anxiety and fears that accompany the event before, during, and after, and uncertainty for the future have rendered young children, adolescents, and their families helpless thus preventing them from taking care of themselves and their environment. As a result, they are evidently losing hope for the future hence limiting their active participation in the realization of national priorities. If the situation is left unaddressed, and this proposed program is not implemented timely, the psychosocial effects of the conflict may lead to continuous suffering, indifference, depression, frustration, a negative coping mechanism, violent behavior, and other antisocial dispositions, thereby increasing social tension, prolonging the recovery process and weakening the resilience of children in the long-term. In response to this situation, GPON has been working with UNICEF and the Borno State Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development (MWASD) to deliver critical child protection in emergency services including case management services to unaccompanied and separated children and other children with protection concerns, support for socio-economic reintegration of children associated with fighting forces and armed groups, and basic psychosocial support services to children. The Ministry has a huge network of trained community volunteers through whom it works to deliver basic psychosocial support services, but program quality has remained a gap. And is missing the opportunity to reach more children through integration with other sectors, mainly education and nutrition. The partnership with GPON will focus on training of CV’s currently engaged to deliver MHPSS services for Children, as part of the partnership no new CV’s will be trained. The proposed action is to improve the quality of services as the capacity of CVs is low The PSS assessment recommends that there is a need for tailored training are conducted especially for CV's on remote programming and other skills. The current CV's capacity will be strengthened to improve the quality of services as the capacity of CVs is low. Goalprime organization of Nigeria is an organization dedicated to using interactive arts for psychosocial support, community interaction, enhancement, development, and capacity building GPON approach to the provision of PSS.
