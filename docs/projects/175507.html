Project Summary

The project, designed in line with the centrality of protection and community participatory and age gender and diversity approaches, is two-fold: 1) shelter support, including in collective shelters and for SGBV survivors and families hosting IDPs and 2) distribution of non-food items to persons with specific needs.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Improvement of shelter infrastructure in collectives sites, shelter support to families hosting IDPs and distribution of non-food items to persons with specific needs.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In coordination with the Shelter Cluster, UNHCR will undertake the following activities: 1) Improvement of shelter infrastructure and living conditions in the shelters for around 5,000 households living in collective sites and GBV survivors living in safe houses. This will be done in coordination with the Shelter Cluster and following its standards and guidelines. It may include the following types of support: improvement of existing buildings and infrastructure, building temporary roofs or extensions, repair and refurbishment of collective shelters. The selection of collective shelters and safe houses will be done in close coordination with Shelter Cluster, local authorities, communities and other clusters (Protection, CCCM, WASH). UNHCR will undertake detailed assessments of the collective shelters and safe houses to identify interventions needed. If needed, it will also conduct assessment of the risk of eviction and alternatives. 2) Improvement of shelter infrastructure and living conditions in the shelters for around 5,000 households hosting IDPs. This will be done in coordination with the Shelter Cluster and following its standards and guidelines. It may include the following types of support: repair of roofs, walls or other components of the house, refurbishment of rooms to be used be hosted families and improve access to services. The selection of hosting families will be done in close coordination with Shelter Cluster, local authorities, communities and other clusters ((Protection, CCCM, WASH). UNHCR will conduct detailed assessments of the shelters of the hosting families to identify interventions needed and will apply due diligence on Housing Land and Property (HLP) issues. UNHCR may also undertake repairs and construction. 3) Distributions of non-food items for around 40,000 households with persons with specific needs. This will be done in coordination with the Shelter Cluster and following its standards and guidelines. UNHCR will conduct assessments and selection of beneficiaries in close coordination with Shelter Cluster, local authorities, communities and other clusters (Protection, CCCM, WASH). UNHCR will also undertake post-distribution monitoring. In coordination with the Shelter Cluster, UNHCR will also assess relocation sites using a holistic settlement planning approach and aligning it with the Government villagization strategy. Cabo Delgado: Pemba, Metuge, Montepuez, Balama, Chiure, Ancuabe, Mueda), Nampula and Niassa.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNHCR staff and its partners are trained on humanitarian principles and the key elements of the centrality of protection and these principles are integrated in its programmes. In line with the centrality of protection approach, UNHCR shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will focus on saving lives, guaranteeing the rights to safety and physical integrity of affected populations, do not harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, accountability of affected populations and community participation and empowerment. In order to guarantee the dignity of people, it is planned to provide timely and quality services taking into account the background of the individuals, the respect for their culture and religion and the survivor-centre approach, respecting their freedom of choice. In addition, UNHCR has adopted the Protection cluster vulnerability targeting criteria based on the specific needs of individuals. The shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will take into account the vulnerability targeting criteria for targeting/prioritizing its beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

In coordination with ICCG, UNHCR will conduct frequent consultations with the communities to involve them in the design, implementation and monitoring of community interventions, including shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items. Furthermore, in 2021, UNHCR will set up community complaints and feedback mechanism to allow people affected by the crisis to have access to information and participate in decision-making on projects developed by UNHCR and its partners.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNHCR shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will take into account the targeting vulnerability criteria adopted in the Protection cluster, in which the disability is one of the criteria. UNHCR also conducts specific consultations with persons with disabilities in order to assess their specific protection issues and provide personalized assistance and material support, including shelter support and distributions of non-food items. In shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items, UNHCR will ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities, in particular in the rehabilitation of collective shelters and safe houses for GBV survivors. During distributions of non-food items, UNHCR will consider separate/fast track queues for persons with disabilities. When feasible, UNHCR will organise door-to-door distribution for persons with disabilities, persons with mobility problems (in particular older persons) and injured persons.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

UNHCR take environmental impact into account in all its operations, given that the environmental damage left by displaced populations could be a major concern. As part of its community-based protection strategy, UNHCR ensures that the communities are sensitized and engaged on the protection of the environment, emphasizing their responsibility for the local environment where they settle. As part of the community initiatives that will be supported in 2021, UNHCR will consider joint eco-friendly projects with IDPs and host communities.
