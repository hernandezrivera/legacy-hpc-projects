Project Summary

Unconditional and unrestricted cash transfers to improve access to immediate food needs for most vulnerable and marginalized households. The project will target regions and districts in IPC 3-5 (mostly in crisis and emergency). It will target 200,000 people (about 13,300 households). Initially awareness will be created to community about their rights and responsibilities to the project. Community based targeting approach will be an inclusive and equally represented Village Relief Committees (VRC) using set targeting criteria. Targeting will ensure inclusion of most vulnerable and marginalized groups by considering age, gender, disabilities, households’ location, IDPs, returnees, minority clans, etc. Community will be made aware of how to channel arising complaints and any feedback in regards to the project. Response to arising complaints will be provided. The project will ensure protection risks to beneficiaries are prevented through prioritising their safety and dignity, ensuring equitable and impartial access, accountability to all population and ensuring community participation and empowerment. Unconditional and unrestricted cash transfers will be delivered to targeted households through mobile money. This will also minimize any protection risks as payment can be done through mobile phones without collection of liquid cash. Baseline assessments of the targeted households will be undertaken before start of the cash transfers. Post-distribution monitoring will be undertaken about 2 weeks after each monthly transfer. Third party monitoring will be undertaken by random calling beneficiaries and asking questions about targeting process, cash received, how it was utilized and any complaints or feedback on the whole process. An independent end line evaluation will be undertaken. Lessons learned and good practices will be documented.

Needs

Latest (post-Gu rains) FSNAU Technical Release indicates that between September and December 2018, over 1.5 million people will face Crisis or worse (IPC Phases 3 and above) food security situation. IDPs constitute about 57% of this population. Within the same period, an estimate of 294, 200 children will likely face acute malnutrition, including 55 200 who are likely to be severely malnourished. Areas classified in IPC 4 (emergency) are Guban (including Awdal) and Bosaso IDPs. Sool and Sanaag is a vast contiguous region that falls with IPC 3 (crisis). Most of the 13 main IDP settlements are classified as Crisis (IPC Phase 3). Despite average to above average Gu rains and similar levels forecasted for Deyr rains, acute food insecurity is prevalent among populations who lost most of their animals and who became destitute during the 2016-2017 severe drought, those affected by flooding in April and May 2018 and others who were affected by large-scale and protracted displacement due to a combination of conflict and natural hazards. These population groups are expected to face large food consumption gaps and need humanitarian assistance to prevent further deterioration of their food security and nutrition situation.

Activities

1. Unconditional and unrestricted cash transfers. This activity will target most 200,000 vulnerable and marginalized people to enable them meet their immediate food needs. Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The project will deliberately prevent risk of exclusion of most vulnerable and marginalized groups based on displacement and other societal factors by ensuring their representation into the VRC which lead and make decisions on target groups. Save the Children will create awareness about the target group and facilitate community to clearly define targeting criteria which will deliberately ensure inclusion of the most vulnerable and marginalized groups.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The project will deliberately prevent risk of exclusion of most vulnerable and marginalized groups (based on gender, age, minority clans, IDPs, returnees, persons with disabilities, etc) by taking the following:• Project relieve committee: The project will undertake sensitization and awareness raising after which, the community will be asked to establish a project committee. To do this, communities will be asked to establish them in public and endorse the committee in public. The committee will represent all different groups and age brackets (excluding children but youth), and any other groups including women and minority. SC will sign an MoU with the committee. The MoU will detail the role of committee and beneficiaries selection criteria. • Setting selection criteria: SC will, in consultation of local authorities where possible, and by the endorsement of the community establish a selection criteria which includes but not limited to: Very poor HHs with limited income, few assets, and no external support. (this is the required criteria while others are optional, one should meet at least one of the other criteria);IDPs and newly displaced or evicted households, high number of malnourished children under 5, Child headed HHs (for example, orphans), Female headed HHs, elderly-headed household (i.e. elderly person primarily responsible for income), disabled HH member, especially when disabled person is head of household, HH includes pregnant and/or lactating women among others. this will help us ensure minimize age based inclusion and exclusion. • House to house and public verification: Community committees will do the selection. Once the list is submitted by the committee. SC, committee and local leaders will take a sample (10% -12%) from the list and verify them physically, after which a public verification will be conducted.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Save the Children will mitigate the impacts of conflict and violence by targeting IDPs and returnees including the ones evicted from urban areas. The organization will take a context assessment where it will identify the hot spots of new arrivals in the target vicinity, and new evicted communities. • Awareness raising and sensitization: Save the children will start sensitizing stakeholders including community leaders and local authorities on the objective, processes and targets of the project. The organization will than meet people in target community in a public place where we will ensure all those who can attend come to the meeting. In these meetings, information about the project including objective, entitlements, duration, targeting criteria, targeting approach will be shared. • Setting community response mechanism: The action will establish community response and feedback mechanism. A hotline will be provided and time will be given before registration. This will help that any omissions or errors in targeting arise. During verification and mobilization, SC MEAL team will provide a help desk.
