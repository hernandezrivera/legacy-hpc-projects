Project Summary

Through this project, HDPO is planning to intervene in North and South Darfur States (Gereida, Nyala Janoub, Kas, Elfasher ,Saraf Omra and Umbarru) focusing mainly on addressing the most critical protection needs and gaps for IDPs, returnees, host communities, women, men, boys and girls, PWSNs, elderly and disabilities exposed to risks of violence and exploitation. Due to ongoing violence the population is at risk, lacks basic needs and PWSNs require specialized services. Protection intervention will enable tracking of incidents and reduce effects of violence, exploitations, abuse that have escalated due to the outbreak of war in Sudan on 15th April,2023 through GBV, General protection and CP activities.
The three project components are: Gender Based Violence (GBV), General Protection (GP) and Child Protection (CP). The GBV activities are conducting mass public awareness raising campaigns on GBV, Community Based awareness raising sessions on GBV, provision of women dignity kits by prioritizing vulnerable women and girls in reproductive age. HDPO will provide capacity building on GBV response services focusing on comprehensive GBV prevention interventions and response services for survivors, sexual violence and addressing multiple forms of GBV within the population. HDPO will conduct a GBV assessment targeting women, girls, children and people with disabilities, and their caregivers in their communities, potential barriers to accessing response services and participating in programs and activities, and their suggestions for improving GBV programs. The second component is General Protection which includes referral of people with specific needs to specialized services. In line with General protection, HDPO will establish and support General protection, Community-Based Structures and protection monitoring will be conducted. Furthermore, site management briefings for government and partners and capacity development including government officials and other duty bearers, community representatives and humanitarian staff, will be train in protection issues and safe and inclusive service delivery and conduct awareness raising and community outreach activities on general protection, conduct protection assessment at community level and provide legal aid & civil documentation assistance .The third component is child protection where girls and boys are provided with specialized child protection response services, including CP monitoring & assessment, child survivors of sexual violence, child labor, child recruitment, UASCs and children in need for alternative care. Reaching the public with education & awareness on preventive measures to address the root causes of child protection risks among the population to reduce the likelihood of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or violence against children, including child labor, child recruitment, separation, sexual violence, child marriage, FGM, are identified and address specific sources of threat and/or vulnerabilities of children as being at particularly high risk of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or violence. The project will also maintain child friendly spaces and equip them, procure and distribute dignity kits & recreation PSS for emergency response. Additionally, provide vulnerable and at-risk children with gender & age-appropriate mental health support. It will also include supporting protection committees to ensure community active participation to participate and respond to CP needs. HDPO will also use social media, and radio to reach various segments of the community. It is expected to reach at least 19000 persons through this activity directly and indirectly. HDPO is planning to target in this project approximately 29,400 persons (men/6000, women/7900, girls/ 8500 and boys/ 7000) including IDPs and host communities, newly affected persons, people with special needs, most vulnerable women, elderly persons and other conflicted affected people within the areas of our responsibility

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this project, HDPO is focusing on promoting effective participation of targeted communities across gender, age, and vulnerability throughout the project cycle to ensure GBV survivors and those at risk are not exposed to additional harm. In addition, the project will take into the account mainstreaming of meaningful access, safety, and dignity when implementing activities.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.
4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The project will use the collected information to track project progress and to determine the level of community satisfaction with project activities and deliverables. The obtained information will be continually analysed and where necessary informed decisions and corrective action taken for continuous improvement of the project.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

HDPO and implementing partners will employ an integrated approach by exploiting our expertise and potential. A holistic approach that covers safety, protection, feeding, inclusivity, psychosocial support, education shall be implemented to ensure that beneficiaries are provided with all the basics of survival in order to be safe. Implementing partners will be given responsibility to handle provision of specific services according to their expertise, capacity and programmes. Accountability is central especially to the project stakeholders more especially the beneficiaries in creating a favorable working relationship for better project implementation. For that purpose, a beneficiaries committee will be established. Local leaders, youth, women, girls, CWD, civil society organizations (CSO) will be included in all project engagements to provide feedback to project management for consideration in decision making.
