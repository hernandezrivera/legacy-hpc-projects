Project Summary

Following the request from local authorities, the Head of municipalities and Moukhtar and Religious leaders, in coordination with the Inter-Agency, since 5 August, AVSI has been assessing: Achrafieh (Geitawi and Rmeil); Mar Mikhael; Gemmayze; Furn Chebbak; Burj Hammoud; Medawar. AVSI deployed a team composed of technical and logistic staff, social workers, education, protection, MHPPS staff; supported by a team tasked with management, coordination, monitoring.
The Rapid assessment of the structural and physical damages, of the MHPSS and vulnerability level of the population aimed at providing immediate relief, basic assistance, support/referral, and shelter. AVSI reached 94 households (209 members); 95% Lebanese, 5% Syrian, PRL. 78% of them did not receive assistance: of those who did: 81% got food, 12% health; 6% financial assistance.
Out of the HHs (70%F, 19% <14yo, 9%>14<18 yo, 28 % < 18yo; the majority aged >40): 7% PwDs (20% physical, 20% hearing, 60% intellectual impairments); 24% with chronic illness; 6% with life-threatening conditions.
20% of the HHs were injured by the explosion: 79% mild, 21% moderate/severe injuries. 53% of the HHs are unemployed; 27% are studying. 8% have a university degree, 48% only completed the primary education; 7 HHHs lost their shops and the income.
Thanks to its Lebanon Emergency #LOVEBEIRUT, campaign, AVSI has been providing emergency shelter and livelihood and MHPSS to these families already.
Although not part of the immediate response, AVSI assessed the education in the area: evidence shows it has been affected severely. There are 410 schools in the area (CERD), including public, semi-private and private; the number of Non-Formal Education Centers is unknown. The IA assessment identified 20 public TVET centers damaged, attended by. 8000 youths. 120 public and 67 private (12 with heavy damages) schools reported damages, including buses, school equipment and material. This affects 55,000 children. Loss of education can lead to permanent drop-out, increase of child labor and long-term decrease of income earning opportunities.
Many families and schools have lost also IT assets that would allow implementing remote/blended education, likely to happen from September onwards due to the high level COVID infection (Lebanon recorded the highest rates in August).
NFE centers and private schools, that often provide free education to the most vulnerable children are on their own at the moment. While supporting the Sector with its technical capacity (100+ schools rehabilitated, equipped, 100,000+ children supported with FE, NFE and CP/PSS), AVSI propose:
• To assess to private and semi-private schools, and NFE offering free/affordable education to vulnerable OOSC and school children
• Rehabilitation 10 schools, and 3 NFE centers including equipment, WASH and COVID infection prevention measures,
• provision IT equipment, including data bundle to schools/centers/families to undertake the blended/ distant education until education premises are available. AVSI will use I Learn From Home and Can’t Wait to learn for NFE ECE, BLN, Retention. The systems are already in place and tested during the COVID response.
Interventions will be in line with the ESP (Effective School Profiles), GOL, MEHE, PMU, Sector standards.
The Project will support approx. 5,000 students and 900 OOSC attending NFE, 1180 families, 250 schools/learning centers staffs. Direct beneficiaries 7330 (80% children, 79% Leb, 21% non-Leb, 51% Females, 4% PWD). Activities mainstream Protection and CP, gender and environment. AVSI adopt a Child-centered and Participatory Approach. Schools/Learning centers will meet safety and safeguarding standards, be child friendly and inclusive.
Staff will increase knowledge and skills on CP, enabled to safely identify and refer children at risk of abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Caregivers and community will be engaged in preventing/addressing CP concerns improving the child wellbeing

2 - Activities

AVSI support the Sector capacity:100+ schools rehabilitated,equipped and 100,000+ children supported with FE, NFE and CP/PSS over the years) and will support approx. 5000 students (FE); 900 OOSC, 1180 families; 250 schools/learning centers staffs. Direct beneficiaries: 7330 (80% <18, 79% Leb, 21% non-Leb, 51%F, 4% PWD). Activities will mainstream Protection, CP, gender, environment. AVSI adopts a Child-centred and Participatory Approach. Schools/centers will meet safety and safeguarding standards, be child friendly and inclusive. Indirect beneficieires are 36550.AVSI will be in charge of the rehabilitation, equipment, Capacity building and training on PSS and Remote learning, while B@Z will be implementing the NFE in the learning centres1. Educational institutions assessment and minor/major rehabilitation of schools/centers offering free/ affordable education to vulnerable students or OOSC1.1. Assessment of private and semi-private schools, needs for upgrading.The team will perform an comprehensive, multisectoral assessment of 10 schools and 3 Non-Formal Education/learning centers affected by the blast. The assessment will consider the pre-existing condition and gaps and post explosion situation; it will include site visits, FDGs and Key Informant interviews (Covid mitigation are included); and will focus on - Facilities: Physical, structural and infrastructural, energy/environment level and sustainability - Equipment, furniture, IT, comms, Labs, etc.- Educational (school capacity, composition, performance, programs, teachers, disability and inclusive approaches)- CP linked to facilitates, services, and risks1.2. Validation of interventions involving community, directors/staff/families and students/pupils, and approval from the relevant authority; and execution of Rehabilitation 10 schools and 3 centers (WASH and COVID prevention)2. Education supplies for children enrolled in affected educational institutions2.1. Based on the school assessment, schools/learning centers will receive the school supply for the upcoming school year2.2. Schools/centers will be provided with IT/comm equipment for the activity 4.1.3. PSS training for teachers/education personnel, as well social emotional learning (SEL) activities in close coordination with the Child Protection partners3.1. AVSI will invest in capacity building CBOS/staff on education and CP integration subjects, pedagogy, classroom management, inclusion and differentiated approach, teaching methodologiesChild protection:package of core methodologies will be delivered to all Education staff/volunteers, enabling them to identify and address the CP needs of children: PFA, First Aid; Mapping; identification and referral; GBV; Child development and communication; Inclusive and conflict sensitive approaches. Safe identification and referral: staff will be trained on identification and referral of children at risk, with special needs to specialized services (speech therapy, MHPSS, etc.). AVSI tracking system is linked to RIMs. CP focal point will follow up, receive referrals and continue coaching. The Staff will be supported with the education also and PSS support as they are also part of the affected community.4. Remote learning support to educational institutions not ready to fully function due to explosion damages and rehabilitation works.4.1. Implementation of Distance/Blended NFE for CB-ECE, BLN and Retention (HWS, catch up) for OOSC and school children. AVSI will adopt I Learn From Home (http://seenaryoplaykit.org/i-learn-from-home/ and Can’t Wait to learn for NFE ECE, BLN, Retention. The systems are already in place and tested during the COVID response and in line with the national education Sector and MEHE-PMU standards 4.2. Provision of data bundle to schools/centers/families to undertake the blended/distant education until education premises are available. 4.3. Technical support to optimize use of digital/online platform by the school staff/ students.
