This project will support the educational activities through establishing 5 TLSs considering the access of children with special needs besides the cultural appropriateness of the context in order to ensure that girls have safe and equitable access providing children with the chance to continue their studies and providing access to quality formal and non-formal education is ensured by providing incentives and capacity building for teachers and education personnel.
The project will focus on out-of-school children getting them back to education and ensuring their continued education. The project will provide unconditional cash aid in exchange for a commitment to education within schools, whereby each student with an attendance rate of more than 80% will receive a monthly financial aid estimated at $5
The project contributes to the Education Sector’s HRB objectives through:
1- Project will scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education in Harim, Al-Dana, Jisr-Alsahogour districts – Idleb governate and Al-Atareb districts - Aleppo governate age (6- 17) by establishing and operating 5 temporary Education Centers.
2- Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth (aged 6-17 years) within a protective environment and raise the capacity of teachers and education personnel through several needed training and coordination with the education directorate.
The project targeted the camps area within Harim, Jisr-Alsahogour, and Al-Dana districts in Idleb and Al-Atareb districts - Aleppo governate through the following activities:
1- 5 TLSs will be established and supported by providing supplies and equipment (2022)
2- the project will provide staff salaries, running costs,s, and heating fuel (2022 - 2023)
3- 1050 students will receive formal education as well as receiving monthly cash aid based on their attendance (2022 - 2023)
4- 900 students will receive non-formal education (catch up & remedial classes) "SiG curriculum (textbooks, activity books, and supplementary materials) will be used for remedial non-formal programs; and Ummah curriculum for a catch up and literacy and numeracy programs" (2022 - 2023)
5- 93 school staff will be provided with incentive and capacity building
The project will increase the boys’ and girls’ beneficiaries by providing the activities of formal and non-formal education and both will access these activities for children and youth (6 – 14) (2022 - 2023)
The project will increase the boys’ and girls’ beneficiaries with disabilities by providing the TLSs with the necessary tools and equipment’s to be appropriate with disabilities

4 - Needs

According to HNO report 2021, "There are 6.9 million children in Syria who need education, 2.45 million of whom are out of school, in addition to another 1.6 million children who are at risk of dropping out of school, due to the cumulative effect of the conflict, the closure of learning facilities, and/or reduced learning hours due to COVID-19, worsening economic conditions, increased population numbers and displacement, the numbers of children out of school, and children who are at risk of dropping out, is almost certain to increase"The project is based on the needs of the targeted community, to increase access to education and provision of formal and non-formal education. Conflicts cause several negative impacts and it is mostly affecting children in many aspects. Children, who are out of school for a long period are unlikely to re-attend or enrolled in the schools and subject to the lost generation War creates chaos. From a child psychology perspective, it is important that all children have some routine in their lives. If children living in a warzone cannot go to school, the routine disappears, and they will have trouble recovering from the crisis. SDI's intervention contribution provides chances to children and youth to have their routine back, in a safe environment.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Persons with reduced mobility and CwDs have no access to education infrastructure and services Likelihood: low Impact: High Mitigation measure: SDI will train teachers on how to teach children with disabilities. Teachers should at the very least be aware of any. SDI will Install seats in latrines for children with disabilities. Adjusting educational centers to be friendly to people with special needs, such as preparing ramps and handrails..etc SPANISH Source text Girls have no access to education Likelihood: low Impact: High Mitigation measure: SDI will sensitize the community about the value and benefits of girls’ education Include proactive activities to encourage equal registration of boys and girls and maintain retention rates Ensure there are separate, secure, hygienic, and private washroom facilities for boys and girls Children are deprived of their right to education and forced to leave school and work to help their families. Mitigation measures: - SDI through BTL campaigns will advocate to child right to education through visits, explaining NFE mechanisms like catch-up which is flexible to meet the best interest of the child. - SDI will target the children with cash for education, where children should achieve %80 attendance every month. SDI will commit to the do no harm principle by committing to the Education cluster salary scale for Education personnel. This Project adopts a protection mainstreaming approach in project design and implementation.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Beneficiaries are at risk on being exposed to any sexual misconduct by the humanitarian workers and they might not report the misconduct due to the following reason: 1. lack of knowledge of reporting mechanisms, especially for people with disabilities and women living in far away areas. 2. feeling afraid of retaliation. 3. fear of stigma. 4. fear of deprivation of assistance. 5. being unable to understand written banners for being illiterate and/or for having a visual disability. 6. for children, language might be complicated To mitigate these risks, SDI will : 1. aim at clearly announcing the hotline number and email specialized for PSEA of SDI and UN numbers. through many means, like, banners, social media, and messages to beneficiaries phones through telegram channel to ensure reaching to the biggest number of beneficiaries including women living in far away areas and people with disabilities. 2. Conduct PSEA training for all staff members and contractors/suppliers dealing with beneficiaries and make them read and sign CoC that state Zero Tolerance towards SEA violations. 3. Conduct awareness raising sessions on PSEA for beneficiaries to encourage them to report any violations and stress on the confidentiality and on eliminating any other fears that they might have. 4. Conduct awareness raising sessions for children in an appropriate language. 5. complaint boxes 6. Using PSEA field and organizational checklists tools.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: The spread of the COVID-19 19 virus within the project camps Likelihood: Medium - Impact: Low Risk management strategy: All preventive measures will be taken during the period of the project, but in the event that the virus spreads significantly, physical education will be suspended and the transition to distance education, as SDI has good experience in managing distance education during the last period Risk 2: Bombardment/bombing on schools in targeted areas Likelihood: Low - Impact: Medium Risk management strategy: The areas of the project were selected to be far from frontline and near borderline. Moreover, the teachers and student will be trained on evacuation and other action during emergencies. Moreover, if the project areas become unsafe, we will relocate to other safe areas.Risk3: Suspension of cross-border assistance from Turkey to NWS.Mitigation Measures: To work in coordination with AFAD and Turkish Red Crescent to deliver aid to Syria.- To agree with the suppliers to provide all the goods to inside of Syria through border gates as a commercial materials. - Participate in the different coordination bodies to organize assistance and avoid duplication to NWS.
