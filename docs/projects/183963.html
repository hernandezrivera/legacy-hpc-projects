Project Summary

This project will focus on the provision of timely protection and lifesaving assistance, and equitable access to improved basic protection, WASH, food security, and energy services and facilities, and opportunities to build self-reliance for Ethiopian refugees living in camps situated in Gedaref State, Sudan. The project will focus on the following:
General Protection:
Provide legal counselling and assistance to the refugee population
Raise awareness on general protection topics
GBV:
Capacity building on GBV topics
Community Awareness raising
Direct Material Support e.g. dignity kit
Energy and Environment:
Providing solar lighting for public spaces
Provide trainings for to use clean energy sources
Provide HH by clean and sustainable energy solutions such as solar cooker fuel efficient stove
FSL:
Provide cash for work activities
Provide support activities for youth
WASH:
Construction of communal latrines
Rehabilitate household latrines
Do vector control (spraying, draining standing water)
Provision of soap

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will seek to address environmental sanitation through construction of latrines in suitable sites; weekly solid waste campaigns will seek to improve practices on waste (household) disposal and management; proper disposal of defecation. Hygiene promotors will undertake environmental conservation awareness activities with info on effects tree-cutting. Use of bamboo tree (has high regeneration rate) will be promoted in the construction of super-structure parts of latrines. Use of alternative sources of energy will also promote conservation of trees.
