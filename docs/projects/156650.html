Project Summary

The Project aims to increase the production of summer vegetables crops (Pepper, tomatoes and eggplant), in addition to contribute towards the food security for about 20000 (direct and indirect) families in the targeted communities to overcome the high increase of prices.
According to the latest FSL report on priorities and responding to the need assessment that MWL has conducted in rural Idleb in coordination with the local councils, this project will assist the targeted poor and vulnerable IDPs & Host community through reducing the financial burden by supporting 400 local farmers who own lands but they are in need for agricultural inputs through support them to plant 0.5 donum as homestead garden with seeds, fertilizer, agriculture tools (shovel, Axe, garden Hose 25 m, Farming Rake) to plant summer vegetables (Pepper, tomatoes, and eggplant ).
Plant 0.5 donum with summer vegetable crops will produce nearly 0.5 ton vegetable crops that will improve the basic need from vegetable.
Moreover, will hold training sessions on new agriculture methods by using new network irrigation.
Therefore, the beneficiaries who are the 400 targeted families will be able to learn practically from the agronomist who will deliver his experience to the beneficiaries.
The other part of this project depends on choosing 125 vulnerable families who own 1 cow with fodder support for 6 months to provide the protein, energy, and menials in the fodder ration, veterinary serves and follow up reproductive health through insemination the cows to increase the number of cows and increase the production of milk after birthing a, after three months of fodder distributing stage, each family will benefit from fodder and veterinary service will give 2.5 liter of milk as free to vulnerable family led by a woman and have children under five years age for three months.
Moreover, insure job opportunities for 400 vulnerable families specially families headed by women.
Furthermore, supporting the local market with summer vegetable crops and milk in order to decrease the prices in the targeted communities, and support 125 vulnerable families headed by women and have children under five years old with 2.5 L milk daily as free will improve child nutrition and enhancing social cohesion in the targeted area.
MWL proposed project aims to:
1- Generating income for 400 farmers through provide agricultural inputs to plant 0.5 donum and increasing the number of planted lands by planting 150 donum.
2- Increasing the availability of dairy products with acceptable price which improving child nutrition and contributing to improve the situation and number of livestock.
3- Increasing job opportunities in the targeted area and activating income generating activities from agriculture & livestock.

Needs

As of early September 2018, Idleb governorate and the surrounding areas of western Aleppo and northwestern Hama are one of the last remaining opposition-held territories in Syria.1,2 Since the outbreakof the conflict in 2011, the predominantly rural region has seen its population swell following continuousinfluxes of internally displaced persons (IDPs) from across the country.3 More recently, from late 2017, theregion has witnessed further large-scale IDP arrivals, following escalations of conflict and displacementsfrom formerly opposition-held areas in south-eastern Idleb, Rural Damascus, Homs, Hama, Dar’a andQuneitra governorates. With widespread concern over an imminent military offensive in the region –potentially displacing up to 800,000 people, according to OCHA5 According to multi-sectoral needs assessment covering 362 out of approximately 700 opposition-held communities in the regionthrough community-level Key Informant Interviews (KIIs). This assessment aims to address criticalinformation gaps and to serve as a baseline informing humanitarian actors of the needs and conditions ofpopulations in the region. This situation overview provides an outline of findings from this assessment. Populations in assessed communities of Idleb governorate and surrounding areas were reported tohave faced multiple challenges in accessing food markets, attaining sufficient livelihoods,and meeting their basic food needs in the month prior to data collection. In 104 (29%) assessedcommunities, KIs reported humanitarian assistance as one of the main sources of livelihoods forIDPs. In parallel, the use of negative coping strategies for lack of food was reportedly widespread,with KIs in almost two thirds (63%) of assessed communities reporting that households were usingstrategies such as purchasing food on credit, reducing meal sizes, selling productive assetsand skipping meals, in order to cope with a lack of food in the month prior to data collection.[REACH]

Protection Risk Analysis

MLW proposed project aims to secure a decent and stable life in terms of providing livelihood means for beneficiaries and by this either giving them the choice to control the produced materials from the project whether that was benefiting from it or selling it to the market at affordable prices in order to help other families. In this strategy, women and teenagers will be safe from disturbing and the families will be able to secure their needs such as medical treatment and education; specifically, the children won’t be obliged to work and they have the chance to learn and go to schools and children’s education is one of MWL mission. Moreover, the farms are near beneficiaries’ houses so the beneficiaries won’t move to other far communities so they will be protected from air strikes and snipers.Field team is adhering to help and provide the available assistant not only to the beneficiaries but also to the whole communities.In addition support 100 vulnerable families, which headed by women and have children under five year’s age with 2.5 L milk daily at no cost from families which benefit from fodder and veterinary service for three months will improving child nutrition for these families.The project's activities will be monitored by M&E staff, which will provide the necessary counseling when needed targeted for families.After procuring the necessary supplies for the project will storing it special equipped warehouses away from danger of artillery, and isolating it in order to conservative it away from insects, and store the vaccine in refrigerator and our staff will ensure the distribution of project supplies on beneficiaries equally.Through holding lectures, we will encourage the idea of women work, as it is not a familiar phenomenon in targeted society in Ar-Rastan and thus encourage on work women and men in all activities of the project which will support the stability and increase in the income of targeted families.
