Project Summary

The project builds the capabilities of women, introduces them to their rights, and opens the way for them to break out of the ignorance and societal marginalization, and raise their scientific, cultural and social level, which qualifies them to exercise their constructive role in the development of society.
This project targets Syrian families in (Toum - Safa -Alsad - Bsratun - Albab ) which are camps for displaced families in northern Syria.
This will be done through a set of educational and professional programs and courses in politic, human rights, media, awareness sessions and qualification training workshops conducted by a group of professors, trainers and specialized lecturers by creating technical and technical training and qualification centers for informational sciences and simple works for the breadwinner and those in their equivalent, which includes On:
The Institute of Sewing and Embroidery
Computer and foreign language training courses
- A training workshop for recycling
- A training workshop on possible small and domestic industries
Training in women's shaving and cosmetology.
Encouraging the first in the center through financial support to start their project, in each session
Where the necessary equipment and tools will be distributed to the first five in the sewing and embroidery department and in the computer department as well as in women's recycling and shaving work. As for the small industries section, the first five will be supported with a financial amount that helps them in the first step of their project.
The project will be carried out in two phases, each one with 200 trainees distributed over five centers (A total of 400 trainees) In addition to five trainers, one assistant, and technical in each center
It should be noted that most of Abrar's management and field staff (males and females) had attended workshops to increase their awareness of PSEA cases.
Abrar has trained a field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to follow up on the reported case and take the required measures.
- Ensuring that all volunteers who work with Abrar, service providers, and local entities that take part in implementing Abrar's activities have signed specific Abrar's PSEA code of conduct.
Note:
The project had started in the city of Idlib six months ago and was scheduled to continue in Taum camp, but it had been halted due to the recent displacement situation.
The project is funded with a value of 10 percent.
