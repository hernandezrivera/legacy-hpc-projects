Project Summary

Muzun presents this proposal with the aim of preserving lives, providing life-saving and sustaining services and enhancing safety in Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) sites. This will be accomplished by implementing Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) activities in 14 camps located in Aleppo and 8 in Idleb. This initiative takes into account the diverse needs of men, women, boys, girls, and individuals with disabilities in all project-related activities.
This proposal target 79,051 Ind, including 16,140 households in camps (Sheikh Bahir , Kafr Jales ,Old Bab Al Salame ,Mseibin ,Anadan ,Albunyan ,Al Khayrat2 , Kafr Karmin ,Tajamuaa Ataal Alsakani Althalith , Al Tareek Al Azrak , Ekkdeh , Masa Village , Al Bourj , Ard Alamal 2 , Kaeebeh and Qarah Kupri, Al Layth,Al-Firin,Qaryet Alkuweyt.Kafr Jales,Bhora,Haranbush,Sheikh Yousef,Toum)
Muzun will implement many of main activities :
Activiy1: Develop a Capacity Building and Fire warden systems.
Muzun field team will train and implement capacity building sessions for camp committees’ representatives in the targeted 14 IDP camps.
Muzun will depend on CCCM Cluster guidance related to Fire prevention camp committee training where Muzun field team will conduct training two sessions in each camp (one session every six months) during the project period as follow for each session :
First day session :
- Ensuring there are camp committees’ representatives able to receive the Fire prevention and Fire mitigation training and ensure the participation of women and people with disabilities in the training sessions - Forming new camp committees for the Fire prevention and Fire mitigation training in case there are no exist committees in the targeted camps and/or the exist committees have received the training previously taking into consideration the active participation of women and people with disabilities
Second day session:
- First Aid kit materials and how to use them
- Elaboration about Fire mitigation materials (first aid kits ,Anti-electrostatic ,gloves, Powder extinguishers, Foam fire extinguishers ,Shovel ,Metal ,stick ,Scissors for Iron cutting ,Water transport container , Fire ,blankets ,PPT , Light).
- practical training on the Fire scenario and train the committee how to deal with Fire incidents.
Activity 2.: Fire Point Establishment
Muzun will establish 66 new Fire Response Points taking into account the follow criteria:
- Muzun will establish 1 Fire point for each 250 families as per CCCM Cluster Guidance and considering the needs of fire points to mitigate and prevent the risk of fire incidents
- Muzun will identify the Fire response points location in coordination with camps committees and the most vulnerable groups (PwDs , women , widows and Older adults ) to foster their feeling Safe
Activity .3: Muzun will Provide 66 fire response resources for the established Fire points including
first aid kits -Anti-electrostatic gloves- Powder extinguishers -Foam fire extinguishers -Shovel -Metal stick -Scissors for Iron cutting -Water transport container -Fire blankets -PPT -Light
Activity 4: Muzun will Care and maintenance to IDP sites through light infrastructure
repairs of lighting poles, roads , water taps, latriness on monthly basis.
The regular maintenance will assure the immediate response of the incidents , particularly the floods , snowstorm and other incidents ,which will secure the uninterrupted life-saving services access to the targeted camps
Activity5 : Conducting Vocational Training for 500 Beneficiaries.This initiative will enhance the skills and employability of young men and women, as well as Persons with Disabilities (PwDs), aged 18 to 45. The training sessions will encompass various vocational areas, delivered by qualified trainers. These areas include:
Mobile maintenance-Barbering-Motorcycle repair-Plumbing-Hairdressing-Knitting wool-Sewing. The diverse range of skills will empower beneficiaries for future employment opportunities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

contitue : Activity 6: Muzun will form camp committees representatives in the targeted reception centers by selecting the qualified members in an equal and fair process taking into account the active participation of community leaders, women , people with disabilities , youth and various group of the IDPs. Muzun field team in close coordination with IDPs and camp management will develop a selection criteria to include and not limited on : - The committee representatives should to be from the targeted camps residents - Able to dedicate time for meeting , training and awareness sessions - Able to solve the camp issues and problem - Able to communicate with NGOs/INGOs in the camps to raise the camp residents complains and improve the services quality - Has a good relation and reputation with camp residents Muzun will follow up with the camps committee the daily tasks and responsibilities to enable all targeted camps residents to participate in the camps management Implement site management activities in the targeted locations.Muzun's field team will provide CCCM site management services within the camps , as follow :- IDPs reception and introducing the locations facilities and services.- IDPs registration and HHs profilingMuzun will work from the first day to register all IDPs families in the targeted reception centers and provide code for each family to facilitate the distribution process, services provision and special needs and vulnerability of family’s identification Furthermore, Muzun will hire officers in each locations to register the new arrival and update them regarding the camp management mechanism- Data collection and updating Arrange regular meetings between the camps management, camp committees , Active NGOs\INGOs in the targeted camps and CCCM project staff on monthly basis to discuss the gaps and needs in the camps and the appropriate way to solve them Muzun will focus to Improve service monitoring tools (AAP) and ensure members' monitoring capacity, and will accountable feedback mechanism and compliance mechanism in the targeted locations . Muzun through the field team (female and male) will reach out to all IDPs in the targeted RCs to conduct awareness sessions and community engagement on a weekly basis during the project duration, in addition to brochures distribution. The awareness sessions and brochures will focus on: - PSEA -Accountability to Affected population -service mapping -Feedback and complaints mechanism Moreover, Muzun will establish feedback and complaints mechanism in the targeted IDP sites through installing the complaint boxes and posters in the camps , in addition to activate the hotline for SEA complaints
