Project Summary

The project will be implemented in the targeted communities of Banteng, Bahne, Dih and Gomevu villages in Wabane subdivision of the Lebialem Division, South West Region of Cameroon. Lebialem Division occupies an area of 617km² with an overall population of 113,736 as of 2005. Lebialem has 3 administrative subdivisions made up of Alou, Fontem and Wabane and it is located on the mountainous north eastern part of the south West Region and it is dominated by two main tribal groups. The Nweh and the Mundani people.
Lebialem is one of the major strongholds of the separatist fighters since the outbreak of the Anglophone Crisis in 2016 which has 3 generals spread across the 3 subdivisions. Fontem subdivision controlled by the Field Mashal, wabane subdivision controlled by General Die Man and Alou controlled by General Ayeke who is of late. Lebialem is surrounded by 3 military camps which till date; the military have never been able to take control of it. The division is now a complete shadow of itself as inhabitants have been cut off from the rest of the country as they have neither network nor electricity. Life is really hard in Lebialem and access into the division is restricted.
The livelihoods option of the Lebialem people is farming but unfortunately, since the outbreak of the Anglophone crisis in 2016 inhabitants of Lebialem can no longer go to the farms because of insecurity in these affected communities. The rate of killings, criminality, hardship, poverty and prostitution has increased why because people are unable to take care of themselves, feed the family and sponsor their children to go back to school.
WARDA is requesting for an opportunity for the young people in this community to be empowered, sincerely it will go a long way in impacting and changing lives, thereby reducing crime rate in the Wabane community.
The proposed project by WARDA is aimed at promoting Livelihood Support through gender mainstreaming. This will be done through the promotion of women/Youth participation through capacity building and skills trainings.
GOAL
The organization goal is to ‘Create access to social and economic opportunities towards empowering Women and youth. To achieve this goal, WARDA works by mobilizing and recruiting women/youth into self-help groups through which the women/youth engage in various socio-economic activities.. The proposed project by WARDA is aimed at empowering women/youth by building their capacities and competencies. This will be achieved by conducting a six-month period skills and entrepreneurship trainings which will equip them with the necessary skills and experiences to manage their own businesses. The project will empower them to be able to position themselves in strategic decision-making processes that take place at varying levels in social, economic and political issues of the region and country at large.

1 - Besoins / Needs

For over 5 years, violent clashes in Cameroon between the military and armed separatists have driven thousands of Cameroonians in internally displaced and some moved to the boarder of Nigeria. The displaced, most of whom are women and children face deplorable humanitarian situation in both countries. Having fled with very little belongings, their presences in the impoverished host communities is lack of food security and already limited health, education, and wash facilities in these areas.According to the Cameroon Humanitarian Dashboard Report December 31, 2020, almost 2 million people were displaced within Cameroon, either as internally displaced persons, refugees or returnees including 706,000 IDP’s and 361,000 returnees. People continue to move within the 2 regions to other regions. Temporal displacements continue to be recorded as civilians flee for safety. They are living in deplorable conditions and unable to feed themselves and families. It is contended that a large proportion of these IDP’s are marginalized and the worst affected groups include youths with disabilities, school dropouts, the unemployed, drug addicts, women/youth living with HIV/AIDS, Victims of sexual abuse, pregnant, nursing and teenage mothers.Unemployment is the highest among the IDP’s forcing many to involve in prostitution and alcoholism. The project focuses on capacity building of the IDP’s and host community women/youth that are socially and economically disadvantaged and giving them start-up capital to be able to run small businesses on their own. The infighting in the two English speaking regions of Cameroon has created gross poverty and unemployment levels among the women/youth in the project communities. These challenges among other issues of underdevelopment have precipitated a vicious cycle of poverty and hopelessness. WARDA project seeks to assist these women/youth and other vulnerable Cameroonians to improve their livelihood through offering tailor-made/hair – dressing, petty trading vocational trainings opportunities and enhancement of local opportunities for holistic development. WARDA proposes to work with women/youth members to economically empower them by building their competencies and capacities to participate constructively in development and policy processes through provision of vocational training courses and startup capital. The project targets 80 women/youth that will be recruited at different levels of intervention depending on prevailing needs and role in the community. Objectively, the project prioritizes the most vulnerable women/youth (the organization targets poor illiterate, marginalized and displaced members of the society. These are determined through recruitment evaluation procedures with the assistance of local authorities and religious leaders. WARDA will empower the selected women/youth (15 -50 years) through skills and entrepreneurship training to economically empower them through capacity building. We seek to offer vocational trainings within the communities of Wabane, Lebialem Division. GENDEROut of the 80 selected beneficiaries of the project, 70% (56) will be female while 30% (24) will be male. The project’s specific objectives are: - (i) To Increase the financial abilities of women/youth to enable them purchase food for their families, educate their children, afford better health, clothing and shelter for their children and reduce poverty and attain improved standards of living. (ii) Advance gender equality, equity and the empowerment of women, ensuring elimination of all kinds of violence against women and children through Full participation in social, economic and political spectra at all levels and bringing women into the mainstream of development. Targeted primary beneficiaries to this project shall include 80 women/youth (15-50 years) who shall be mobilized from 4 different villages within Wabane sub-division, Lebialem Division, South West Region of Cameroon. Duration 6 months.
