Project Summary

The main goal of this project is to strengthen the preparedness and effective response capacities of health facilities in targeted geographical areas. The project equips selective secondary and tertiary facilities with the supplementation of basic and supplementary kits and basic and advance life support training. Furthermore, the project will support expansion of trauma care and life support through primary health care facilities to ensure maximum coverage of vulnerable people including migrants, displaced families, returnees and people with special needs.

Needs

The Libyan public health system has been long challenged by very limited capacities, gross ineptitude and lack of transparency and accountability. Given the on-going crises, more than one million of the Libyan population, including women, men, girls, boys and old adults are in need of health assistance. Hundreds of thousands of people are left without access to life-saving health care and essential medicines. Internally Displaced People (IDP), refugees and migrants are identified as having the most severe needs. Returnees and non-displaced Libyans in the worst affected areas are also in need of humanitarian assistance. The main hospitals lack basic life support skills and equipment; in far-flung areas there are no adequate skilled health workforce. The country is currently experiencing an outbreak of measles, a vaccine preventable contagious disease; in addition cases of Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD) are being reported on weekly basis. The readiness and operational capacities of major health facilities to respond to these emergencies is limited.Despite the anticipation of unexpected onset of incidents that include disease outbreaks and flare-up and spread of armed clashes which could trigger major displacements, capacity of Ministry of Health to provide a rapid and time-bound response is limited.

Activities

The following activities will be implemented in order to achieve the overall objective of the project 1. Capacity strengtheninga. Train 600 health workers on basic and advanced life support with the use of standard toolsb. Deploy 10 Emergency Medical Teams (EMT) to secondary health facilities in remote areas for the provision of standard trauma care and life support. 2. Provision of medical equipmenta. Procure and distribute 300 basic and 30 supplementary interagency health emergency kitsb. Procure and distribute 20 Italian Trauma A and B kits c. Procure and distribute 200 blood bags3. Support an effective and functional referral systema. Support the Ambulance department and wounded directorate of Ministry of Health with fuel and per diem to run round the clock ambulance service for the medical evacuation and referral of cases during emergencies.b. Procure and distribute three ambulances to health facilities in remote areas 4. Contingency stocka. Procure and per-position emergency medical supplies.b. Procure laboratory reagents and consumables as well as rapid diagnosis tests.

Access, safety and dignity of beneficiaries

During the community consultation and needs assessment activities, the project identified the gender implications and gendered health determinants influencing health inequities between men, women, boys and girls. Vulnerabilities and strengths of men, women, girls and boys; older persons; persons with disabilities; persons belonging to minority groups and persons with mental disorders were also identified through joint assessments. The project will improve access of affected people to services by making services readily available round the clock. Gender equality in health service provision will be taken into consideration in order to warrant equity in health access and promote the Universal Health Coverage.WHO will organize a full orientation session on the accountability to affected people and protection for the service providers. A complaint mechanism will be established to receive and address concerns of beneficiaries.
