Project Summary

The proposed activities under this project will stress on UNHCR's outreach and response to vulnerable groups affected by the conflict, which will contribute to strengthening their resilience, mitigating protection risks and boosting their coping mechanisms. This will be done through the provision of primary health care and emergency response to address urgent specific needs of vulnerable refugees and asylum seekers in addition to people with Disabilities, Injuries and psychological distress as well as providing medicine for certain chronic illnesses.
Furthermore, UNHCR will conduct awareness trainings on life skills and family planning targeting the most vulnerable of persons of concerns, single mothers, big families, minors and women at risk.
The project will target the POCs in detention centers, disembarkation points and urban setting such as at the community day centers and shelters managed by UNHCR and partners.

Needs

The majority of the refugees and asylum seekers intercepted or rescued at sea are in urgent need of medical assistance as well as those in the urban context. The local health services are severely under-resourced and unable to attend to their own nationals let alone POCs. Health care is rarely provided to person of concerns through local health services for a variety of different reasons, including that most local public health facilities insist that POCs should have a valid ID and not all places recognize the UNHCR certificate. Private healthcare remains an expensive option and is hence not accessible to many POCs. Furthermore, access to services is often discriminatory, and particularly for women and girls.

Activities

Under this project the following activities will be implemented:UNHCR will work with partners to ensure access to POCs to primary health care and emergency response. UNHCR will support providing medicine for certain chronic illnesses. Epileptic cases/ blood pressure/ diabetes, etc… and at the same time providing emergency intervention and response to the cases in need for life saving intervention. UNHCR through partners will provide PFA, PSS and psychiatric services to the increasing number of cases in need. UNHCR plans to strengthen support and enhance relations with at least two Public Health Care centers, Al Jalaa (Children Hospital) and/or Al Razi (Mental Hospital) and Bou Sitta hospitals (Chest disease and Surgery). UNHCR plans with the help of WHO to increase cooperation and support in relevance to medication and vaccination. Furthermore, UNHCR will conduct awareness trainings on life skills and family planning targeting the most vulnerable of persons of concerns, single mothers, big families, minors and women at risk.
