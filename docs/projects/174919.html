Project Summary

The project will work under the NDC Consortium and will focus to provide immediate humanitarian assistance to affected population and improving the WASH facilities in school and communities to ensure the access to safe drinking water, appropriate sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion to cope with better hygiene practices.
The project will focus to rehabilitate and improve the 90 schools and health units WASH facilities to ensure the access to safe drinking water availability, improved sanitation and waste collections, which will include the rehabilitation and restoration of water supply system, inclusive latrines, solid waste proper collection and installation of hand washing station with liquid soap and provision of WMC kit for the operation and maintenance and disinfection.
240 number of community-based water points rehabilitation and installation of hand pumps will be done at accessible locations and inclusion facilities. The rehabilitation of these water points will improve access of 12000 households (individuals 75600) to safe drinking water. Each village level community-based water management committee formation, training and operation and maintenance (WMC kit) will provide for the sustainable operation and maintenance of the water facilities. Water quality testing will conducted for all the improved water facilities to ensure the improve quality of water.
Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, these communities and schools will also be reached with training and provision of disinfection kits. The disinfection spray will be regularly carried via trained school and health facilities staff in targeted areas to avoid the spread of COVID-19 virus.
To ensure the beneficiaries better hygiene practices at household, 8000 household’s beneficiaries will be provided with 12 hand washing soap bars and 2 Jerry Cans per households along with COVID-19 mitigation measures flyers and orientation on best hygiene practices of hand washing and COVID-19 precautionary measures.
The project will also be reached to adolescent and deprived women in schools and communities with distribution of 6000 mini MHM kits and sensitization on the improvement of menstrual hygiene practices.

1- Project Activities

Output: Ensure access to and provision of safe drinking water, appropriate sanitation, and promotion on safe hygiene practices for the disaster affected communities.Activity 1.1: Rehabilitation of 90 WASH facilities in Schools and Healthcare centersACTED intends to support provision of safe drinking water and appropriate inclusive sanitation facilities in schools and healthcare centers. The rehabilitation and restoration of WASH facilities in schools and healthcare centers will provide access to 13500 individuals to safe drinking water, appropriate sanitation facilities and improve hygienic environment.Activity 1.2: Rehabilitation of 240 community-based water pointsACTED intends to support provision of safe drinking water facilities in targeted affected communities. The rehabilitation and installation of 240 water points i.e. hand pumps, dug wells will provide access to 12000 households (individuals 75600) to safe and potable drinking water. All these rehabilitated and installed water points will be tested through PCRWR laboratory with basic physio-chemical and bacteriological parameters to ensure the provision of safe drinking water to communities.Activity 1.3: Water Management Committee (WMC) training and provision of Toolkit ACTED intends to improve the capacity of school, healthcare units and community-based water management committee members for regularly operate and maintenance of the rehabilitated water points. These WMC members will provide training on the operation and maintenance and WMC toolkit. Activity 1.4: Provision of Disinfection Kit for COVID-19 ACTED intends to improve the capacity of school and healthcare units to cope with COVID-19 pandemic environmental cleaning. Thus ACTED intend to provide one day training to cleaner/sanitary workers on the environmental surface cleaning and disinfection with provision of Disinfection kit which will include the chlorine liquid, spray machine and other safety items. The provision of the 330 disinfection kit will benefit 49500 beneficiaries those visiting healthcare centers and school children. Activity 1.5: Distribution of Soap and Jerry Can and Hygiene promotion sessionsTo ensure the beneficiaries better hygiene practices at household, 8000 household’s beneficiaries will be provided with 1 hand washing soap bars, 2 Jerry Cans each with capacity of 10 liters and IEC materials per households along with COVID-19 mitigation measures flyers and orientation on best hygiene practices of hand washing and COVID-19 precautionary measures. The hygiene improvement will benefit 8000 households (individuals 50400) with better critical hygiene practices of hand washing with soap, using of face masks, latrine cleaning, water cleaning and treatment.Activity 1.6: Women inclusive mini Menstrual Hygiene Kit (MHM)The project will also be reached to adolescent and deprived women in schools and communities with distribution of 6000 mini MHM kits and sensitization on the improvement of menstrual hygiene practices
