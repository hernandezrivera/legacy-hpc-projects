Project Summary

More than 9 million people are affected by the prevalent poor WASH conditions in Somalia. Among the 2020 People in Need (PIN), 1.7M are children, 0.4M are women and 0.4M are men. Among those groups, the cluster also identified 0.1M elderly and 0.4M people living with a disability who will require specific attention.
A large proportion of the People in Need of WASH assistance is located in South Central Somalia with Baidoa bearing the highest number of these people with 149,000 people in need, with a large proportion (49%) of them being IDPs (WASH HNO October 2019).
In response to this, READO intends to provide access to safe clean water, sanitation, and hygiene for vulnerable populations in Baidoa, Bay region. This will be done through emergency latrine construction, providing temporary access to water, hygiene promotion and kits distribution, and rehabilitation of water points.

Project Activities

Objectives: Deliver life-saving WASH assistance to reduce acute needs among the most vulnerablesettlements and communities in Baidoa. Activity 1.1.1: Support provision of temporary access to safe water for 1,500HHs (9000 people) of affected communities in the emergencies (IDP's) through water trucking/vouchers, O&M services and household water treatment HHWTs. This will also involve the installation of emergency water tanks (water distribution points) in the Southwest state of Somalia. The water trucking will last for two months Activity 1.1.2 Support provision of Emergency VIP latrine construction for 200 latrines and desludging for IDPs communities in Baidoa Southwest State Somalia. Considering the scale of internal displacements, and to contain the AWD/cholera outbreak, additional emergency latrines, and garbage disposal will be pertinent. Activity1.1.3; READO will distribute key emergency WASH supplies and equipment including hygiene kits (including Jerricans, aqua tabs, soap) to meet WASH emergency needs of 1,500HHs vulnerable communities in Baidoa especially IDPs. Activity 1.1.4; READO will train CHWs and volunteers who will in turn conduct community sensitization and hygiene promotion around drinking water collection and storage; hand washing, hygienic sanitation. Objective 2: Provide or restore sustainable access to safe water services in targeted settlements and communities. Activity 2.1.1 READO will assess key community water sources of shallow wells, boreholes and other water sources and rehabilitate 3 strategic water points in Baidoa that will beneﬁt 7,000 people. Also, READO will establish Water Management Committees (WMCs) at the community/IDP level to manage WASH facilities and conduct hygiene promotion campaigns. The WMCs will also comprise of people with disability as a focal point for that group. WMCs will be trained on the operation and maintenance of the rehabilitated water points.In Baidoa, READO will consult the WASH Cluster focal point to ensure that all activities plannedare integrated into the WASH master plan, endorsed by the Federal Authorities. In regards to people with disabilities, READO will ensure participation and active engagement with people with disabilities in all stages of the program to ensure that WASH services and facilities are tailored to needs and preferences. Furthermore, READO will raise awareness amongst WASH staff on the need for and approaches to data collection on children with disabilities. Moreover, during needs assessments and PDMs, READO will disaggregate all data collected by disability, age and gender and present findings with theseconsiderations.

Mitigation Measures

Protection of civilians & Protection Mainstreaming: In regards to insecurity risk in locations and routes to WASH facilities, READO will identify central locations and avoid areas near military installations or threats such as attacks, stray bullets, crossﬁre or violence. READO will also consider installing lights near communal facilities. If lighting is not possible, consider coordinating with the NFI sector for the provision of solar lamps/torches for each household. Furthermore, READO will locate water sources in visible, central locations and not more than 500 meters from the settlements and accessible to people with disabilities. In regards to the distribution of hygiene kits, READO will identify central locations for distribution points so that the beneﬁciaries do not have to traverse long distances or pass through conﬂict zones to access distribution points. As far as GBV is concerned, READO will sensitize women to accompany each other in groups especially when going to fetch water to reduce threats to the safety and dignity of women and girls. In addition, READO will build separate toilets for males and females. READO will ensure they are marked in pictorial form for illiterate users and work with the community to ensure they are used by the indicated sex. For privacy, READO will provide secondary enclosures around facilities or put locks on the doors to latrines. This will be discussed with beneficiaries to consider their preferences. In regards to safety risks to children, READO will consider the size of the drop hole; provide smaller jerry cans for children to collect water to avoid potential injury and consider their physical capacity in designing water pumps.In regards to Accountability to Affected Communities, READO will establish a complaint response mechanism that will be accessible, conﬁdential and well-understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneﬁciary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. This will be done through the use hotline number where beneﬁciaries will be able to call or text this number to register their complaint. Exclusion and Marginalization: During the distribution of hygiene kits, READO will establish mechanisms to give priority to those who cannot stand in line for long periods, such as older persons, persons with a disability, unaccompanied children, pregnant and lactating women, so that they wait for less time. For more gender inclusion, READO will consider the gender balance of distribution teams and committees to ensure that there are enough women available as a contact point for women in the community. Also, respond to the speciﬁc hygiene needs of women, girls, older persons - include sanitary pads and adult diapers in hygiene kits and provide additional personal hygiene items for persons with incontinence or severe diarrhea. Furthermore, READO will involve and consult all categories and layers of the affected population in identifying and responding to WASH needs. READO will consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, chronically ill, older persons, pregnant and lactating women and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their speciﬁc needs and preferences for WASH target locations, design and services. Moreover, READO will engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, to promote a sense of ownership, build their self-esteem and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response.READO will ensure that the lack of documentation does not exclude individuals from accessing services. Besides, READO will consult both displaced and host communities about WASH need and assess whether WASH interventions could cause tension, harassment or conﬂict between the two communities through employing the holistic DO NO HARM approach.
