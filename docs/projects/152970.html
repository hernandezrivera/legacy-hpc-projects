Project Summary

Former Northern Bahr el Ghaza State is among other states in the country whose populations are projected to face extreme levels of food insecurity in 2019 with Aweil East and Aweil West Counties rated Emergency while Aweil South, Aweil Centre and Aweil North at Crisis levels (IPC September, 2018).
Advance Africa Initiative, a women led national NGO seeks to provide FSL interventions that will target vulnerable 4, 000 beneficiaries (1500 boys, 1500 girls, 1000 men and 1000 women) through crop, vegetable and fishing kits emergency distribution, animal health – livestock treatment and vaccinations, responding and addressing to the FSL Custer objective - 2:food production for the most vulnerable households: Increase vulnerable household food production to protect the erosion of livelihoods through agricultural seeds and fishing inputs. Inputs will be prepositioned through FAO/Cluster pipeline.
Proposed activities will ensure mainstreaming of Accountability to affected populations, protection of vulnerable groups, gender equity, environment conservation, GBV, HIV/AIDS control especially among youth.
ADAFIN will ensure linkages and coordination with other actors in WASH, Education, Health, Nutrition, Protection which will ensure delivery of an integrated package of activities.

Needs

Former Northern Bahr el Ghaza State is among other states in the country whose populations are projected to face extreme levels of food insecurity in 2019 with Aweil East and Aweil West Counties rated Emergency while Aweil South, Aweil Centre and Aweil North at Crisis levels (IPC September, 2018). September IPC update indicates at 5.2 million people projected to be extremely food insecure in Jan-March 2019, with 54.6% of these in former Northern Bahr el Ghazal facing crisis, emergency and catastrophe outcomes. High food insecurity levels have been due to an array of factors, which include a protracted conflict in the country, hyperinflation and high commodity prices especially in market dependent regions like Aweils, reduced incomes due to limited livelihood options, crops and livestock diseases which continue to erode key assets and livelihoods among others. Possible returns from within and outside of the country attributed to recently signed peace deal may cause further pressure on already scarce resources in the state, likely to fuel further conflict in the state (IPC, 2018; Food Security Outlook, 2018). Annual Arab migrants traversing the region often elicit tensions and fighting, associated with livestock related fighting as they traverse towards Aweils, with incoming herds which may often untroduce livestock diseases among the host population cattle, further fuelling conflict (FAO Livestock Situattion, 2017/18). Projected high malnutrition levels are driven by poor access to public health, poor access to and availability of food due to dysfunctional markets and reduced farm production, and poor caregiving practices on food utilization. This further compounded by annual effects of dry spells and floods further exacerbate their vulnerability and consequently forcing people to engage in negative coping mechanisms (United Nations Environment Programme and South Sudan Ministry of Environment, South Sudan First State of Environment and Outlook Report, 2018). Advance Africa Initiative, a women led national NGO seeks to provide FSL interventions that will target vulnerable 4, 000 beneficiaries (1500 boys, 1500 girls, 1000 men and 1000 women) through crop, vegetable and fishing kits emergency distribution, animal health – livestock treatment and vaccinations, responding and addressing to the FSL Custer objective - 2:food production for the most vulnerable households: Increase vulnerable household food production to protect the erosion of livelihoods through agricultural seeds and fishing inputs. Inputs will be prepositioned through FAO/Cluster pipeline. Proposed activities will ensure mainstreaming of Accountability to affected populations, protection of vulnerable groups, gender equity, environment conservation, GBV, HIV/AIDS control especially among youth. ADAFIN will ensure linkages and coordination with other actors in WASH, Education, Health, Nutrition, Protection which will ensure delivery of an integrated package of activities.
