Project Summary

The Food Assistance for Assets (FFA) program offers an approach by which food assistance is conditionally provided in exchange for labor in disaster risk reduction and assets creation activities that contribute to rehabilitation and reconstruction post-shock. The intervention will be implemented in the Southern and Central drought-affected areas which mainly include Gaza and Tete provinces of Mozambique, reaching 250,000 drought-affected people in IPC Phase 3 and higher in the drought-affected provinces, throughout the lean season from October 2019 to March 2020. Households with labor constraints will be receiving unconditional food assistance, representing 10% of the overall planned numbers.
