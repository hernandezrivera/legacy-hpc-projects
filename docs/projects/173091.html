Project Summary

Because of protracted conflicts affected many state across Sudan many of international actors engaged in providing humanitarian support a targeting wide spectrum range of communities who were severely affected by long protracted conflict, World vision international is a child focus organization engaged in supporting most needy communities in Sudan focusing on certain geographical coverage (Blue Nile, South Darfur, East Darfur and South Kordofan), as a result of protracted conflict children’s are severely affected due to by losing their opportunity to access to education as a result of consequences of conflict which lead to poverty, insecurity, social and economic implications further to several protection issues such as absence of parents and security in the families, Family separation and sexually exploited girls; as a result children are left without care and support, moreover the limitation of financial resources allocated for education whether it comes from national of local government has contributed in worsen the situation lead to left behind thousands of children’s out of school who engaged in child labor, and participating in anti-social, criminal activities. Therefore, we want to mainstream these children in the nation building by providing education, care, support and protection.
As most of targeted communities are IDPs, families facing lack of financial resources to support for school uniforms, stationery, and pay the applicable fees (e.g., examination fees) for their children. Most adult household members work as daily wage-earners, some migrant and seasonal farm workers.
Current situation
- most of students are studying in open space, or locally constructed schools with very poor condition of permeant available permeant classes which is constructed many years ago without renovation
- Most teachers of the School are untrained and hence are unskilled, thus not able to deliver quality service. Due to the use of traditional teaching methods it affecting the quality of education.
- Schools have remained without being properly monitored by the government, moreover PTAs members who engaged in supporting educational efforts at local level, but they suffer of lack relevant of management capacity/skills.
- Lack of effective learning environment (untrained teachers, lack of sanitation, physical
- facilities –play-grounds, toilets for female teachers and girls’ students, drinking water taps, library, laboratory, etc.), physical infrastructure (roof, classroom, floor), stagnant technology, etc.
- Lack of awareness on the part of parents about the importance of education, and educating their children and Incidence of dropouts due to various cultural factors (early/child marriage, need for taking care of young ones, etc.) in additional financial; reasons
Therefore, improving education facilities at public schools and strengthening the local capacity (PTAs and school improvement committees (SIPc), and monitoring the accessibility and quality education would be an ideal approach to minimize the problems being faced by the marginalized communities and local education authorities and institutions. Hence, the proposed action is intended to address the above-mentioned problems by supporting the children of Disadvantage group's families, strengthening the governance capacity of the SIPc and management capacity of the schools, contribute to improving education facilities of the neediest of basic schools, and bringing them to a common platform to address the underlying causes of the problems mentioned above, moreover supporting the alternative learning approaches and mainstreaming strategic solutions support teachers training through supporting and strengthening the available mechanisms working on building the capacity of teachers and educational workers.
