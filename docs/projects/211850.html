Project Summary

Syria is one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies laded with ongoing
hostilities, resulting in widespread destruction of civilian infrastructure, including health
and agricultural facilities leading to food, nutrition and related insecurity.
The proposed action seeks to protect and enhance food security and livelihoods of
conflict‑affected highly vulnerable IDPs and host communities in North West Syria (NWS)
(Aleppo). In addition, the targeting strategy will ensure assistance reaches the most
vulnerable IDP and host HHs at greatest risk of protection concerns, specifically female,
child and elderly-headed households, PWD and PLW.
This will be achieved through an integrated program of assistance including repeated
mixed‑modality life‑sustaining food assistance (monthly assistance to the most
vulnerable households) and emergency lifesaving food security assistance in addition to
bakery and livelihoods support. The complementary package of livelihoods support will
increase household resilience through the promotion of entrepreneurship and the
development of small businesses along with the improvement of agricultural production
and increasing the ability of targeted households to meet food needs. The bakery
intervention will assist bakeries in filling the gaps in supply chain for flour and yeast.
Further the bakeries intervention will also assist un-employed youth is setting up small
bakery outlets to support income and will contribute to developing entrepreneurial skills.

The BNFs Selection Criteria:
Female/Widow headed households
Households headed by someone with a disability, and with two or more of PWDs
Elderly-headed households
Household with more than 5 children
Families with PLW
Modality: The planned activities will have a repeated support for a period 10 months
through our food kits, food vouchers, micro grants and direct contracting.
Data Collection and Monitoring-The monitoring will be through our well defined M&E
systems. Monitoring will be diligently executed starting from Verification, Distribution
Monitoring, Post Distribution Monitoring and Satisfaction Surveys. The data will be
collected online, primarily using tablets and Kobo tools. We deploy several tools to
include Excel and other tools to analyze and report. A robust system is in place for complaints
receipt and redressal, with dedicated teams available for handling and resolving
complaints. A robust system of lodging complaints is already in place through
communications tools such as WhatsApp, SMS, email, and complainant boxes.
Reporting: We will ensure prompt reporting on all of our projects through regular filing of
donor and 5W reports and ad hoc reporting requirements.
CVA Support Systems: A fully automated and online Cash Voucher System developed
internally and utilized for last 8 years will be deployed. The system is flexible to use paper
vouchers or cards and can be of single or multiple use. To complement our voucher
programs, we have framework agreements with Fund Service Providers that provide cash
distributions through formal arrangements. Due to this system, our response time is
short.
