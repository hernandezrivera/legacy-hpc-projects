Project Summary

The social centre of Zahret Al Madan (ZAM), partner of the initiative, was created with the specific task of assisting families in which there are children and adolescents with physical and / or cognitive disabilities. The central activity of the association is the creation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for minors, to which have been added those on which the impact of the conflict was particularly dramatic, both from a psychological and physical point of view (e.g. minors with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder - PTSD). The strong reduction for families of the possibility of accessing social and health assistance services, both due to the lack of structures in the suburbs and the lack of economic resources, makes it necessary to strengthen the services offered - free of charge - by the ZAM Centre, through strengthening the technical skills of operators and volunteers and increasing the relationship between ZAM and other realities (local NGOs and specialized treatment centres, such as the Centre for Prosthetics and Rehabilitation of the Ibn Al Nafis Hospital).
The main protection programme goals of the projects are: a) implementation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for children with disabilities, and favouring their social inclusion through a wide range of activities; b) enhance the social and economic conditions of their families through the delivery of vocational training for their economic empowerment; c) promote awareness campaigns among the communities of Damascus and Rural Damascus for the social inclusion of children with disabilities; d) raising awareness about explosive ordnance risk.
Furthermore, the lack of resources of the families of coming from Hajar Al Aswad, Yarmouk and Midan (host community) does not allow them in access quality services for their relatives. The reinforcement of the technical skills of volunteers and workers of the ZAM association, as well as of the other associations involved in the activities, is the first answer to the increasing needs of the population of Damascus and Rural Damascus. The project foresees the strengthening of capacity of ZAM volunteers and workers in the field of assistance to minors with disabilities, as well as the training of other 4 associations of operating in Damascus and Rural Damascus in the field of assistance and care of children and youngsters with disability.
The project is integrated with activities designed under the Health and Food Security sectors’ umbrella.

4 - Needs

As outlined in several analysis a decade of conflict trapped civilians in a pervasive crisis. In areas that are more secure, people require support for recovery and resilience and rehabilitation or re-establishment of basic services. At the same time, it can be expected that without emergency assistance to the most vulnerable in the immediate term a further deterioration of their life conditions should be expected. The needs hare highlighted, are collected in coordination with MoSA and MoH, local associations and beneficiaries targeted by the programme 2022-23. In the areas of intervention, people are suffering of many negative factors: disintegration of community, hindered social nets, high levels of trauma and psychological distress. Initial conditions of poverty and scarce access to basic services have been further aggravated by the extended crisis. 91% the targeted families see the presence of one or more minors with disability, and their lack of resources aggravate the physical and cognitive status of them. According to the analysis realized by the I-NGO, there is also continuous need to increase the capacity of the social and health workers, as well as the number of volunteers and workers in order to match the increased quantity of beneficiaries of social services in the area. Furthermore, the consequences of the COVID-19 mitigation measures have exacerbated many already difficult situations, claiming an urgent response in terms of psycho-social and economic support.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented in 2 locations: 1) Midan, ZAM centre, and 2) in Jaramane, where a similar centre should be opened in 2022. Since more than 10 years, Armadilla and ZAM working in activities addressing minors with disability, in collaboration with the MoSA and the MoH. Project activities will be implemented by Armadilla and ZAM staff, involving local associations and volunteers of Jaramane. M&E activities will be done consistently to good practices and quality standards, involving primary stakeholders (beneficiaries, local partners) as per AAP policy of the Organisation. Key beneficiaries are involved in all the phases of the project, from the development to the implementation, and evaluation.Activities to be implemented:1) Therapeutic pathways for children with disabilities, by health and social workers. In Midan the centre works 6 days a week, for around 7 hours a day. In Jaramane, the activities will start on twice-a-week basis.2)Psychosocial activities addressing families of children with disabilities.3)Training for volunteers and workers of ZAM and of Jaramane.4)Recreational days for children with disabilities.5)Supply of assistive aids and educational toys for children with disabilities to the associations.6) Awareness community sessions on inclusion for children with disabilities7)Awareness campaigns about explosive ordnance risk at communities’ level, involving the schools of the target communities and at the CPR of Hospital Ibn Al Nafis

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Sexual exploitation and abuse are a violation of fundamental human rights. It can also be a criminal act. Armadilla, as international I-NGO, owns its procedures and guidelines in order to mitigate any risks related to SEA. Armadilla is committed to acting ethically and with integrity in all our business dealings and relationships and to implementing and enforcing effective systems and controls to ensure the exploitation and abuse of adults is not taking place anywhere in our own business or in any of our supply chains or partnerships. Armadilla is committed to ensuring our approach is consistent with our national and international disclosure obligations, and shall comply with all applicable laws, statutes, regulations.Considering the peculiar environment in which activities will be developed, no risks of SEA have been detected; activities with children and women will be implemented in safe spaces, and delivered by trained professionals. Moreover:1. Professionals working in the project belong to the same community of beneficiaries, having hence also a role of rights’ promoters among the families of the community, and the capacity to assess any possible SEA which will be immediately reported;2. Activities such as community awareness campaigns, do not only provide information regarding SEA, but help workers, volunteer and beneficiaries in detecting risks and reporting to specialised staff of Armadilla-ZAM in order to tackle any issue.
