Project Summary

An educational and humanitarian project that aims to provide a free and safe opportunity for children and displaced people in the informal settlement in the western countryside of Raqqa Al-Sukhna Camp & Al-Salam Camp It aims at non-formal education in eradicating illiteracy behind their educational level, which contributes to their request for education and accelerated compensatory education activities for students in schools for those who They live in the nearby settlement AlSukhna Camp, which increase at their educational level and achieves gains for them, compensates for the lessons they missed, and opens their horizon. The project targets 900 "males and females children aged (6-17) who are outside the school,The organization’s team worked to visit the camps office in the Raqqa Civil Council and took the necessary information and then conducted field visits to the camp and held meeting and discussion with community leaders and people.The camp was never targeted for education
These activities are targeted educational activities which includes: Literacy, entertainment, psychological activities, social emotional learning activities, artistic and sports activities, and useful activities for camp residents. It targets only 270 children from (6-17) years old in Sukhna camp with supplementary education through multiple lessons in schools and through a self-learning program and psychological entertainment support activities, targeting (150) parents who do not have the ability to read through literacy activities in addition to awareness sessions for care provider. It will provide a mobile library containing 250 books, stories, educational materials,Through this project, children with special needs will be referred to effective organizations (which have referral system with Solidarity Organization ) and the vulnerable women will be referred to the Solidarity Organization’s Protection Department within the Protection Program, and women who wants for profession training will be referred to the livelihood department also in Solidarity Organization, noting that the organization has professional trainers in this side, The Solidarity Organization will provide an educational center which will participate in illiteracy for children in informal settlements of 2 groups, so the Solidarity Organization will contribute the solving main problem faced by children in the informal settlements in Raqqa countryside is “the spread of illiteracy and dropout from education among displaced children in the informal settlements in Raqqa, and their inability to reach education centers. The reasons of this was "lack of schools or educational centers" in the camps, the inability of families to register their children in schools in nearby areas, and the general goal: enhancing the right of children who are out of education in the informal settlements in Raqqa to obtain qualitative, comprehensive, and equitable education opportunities.
Special goals:
increasing availability of education. Informal education and the possibility of obtaining it safely at the level of reading and arithmetic by 70% from their current level, and increasing opportunities to obtain education, through remedial education, for 1,170 male and female children in informal camps.
Eradicate illiteracy for the families of targeted children, improve the quality of education, and educate caregivers of both genders in the informal settlements in Raqqa on proper practices in dealing with children
*Enhancing children’s ability, reducing school dropout, and developing the life, communication, cooperation, and thinking skills of targeted children, at a rate of 70% from their current level, and alleviating the effects of trauma and psychological and social pressures on 1,170 children in scholls
Targeted Category: Children from 6 to 17 years old, 50% of both genders, and caregivers for children in informal settlements. Children who are enrolled in schools near the informal settlements in Sukhna
