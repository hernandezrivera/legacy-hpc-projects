Project Summary

This project aims to reduce the mortality and the morbidity due to malnutrition by offering adequate treatment for 15,111 children suffering from severe acute malnutrition among displacements people in The Lake province due to attacks by non-state armed groups and military operations in borders with Nigeria and Niger.

1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

Needs Chad continues to face multiple humanitarian crises. According to the 2020 SMART Survey carried out countrywide, the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) is 10% with 2.1% of severe acute malnutrition (SAM). In addition to the persistence of the nutritional crisis and food insecurity, the province of Lake is directly or indirectly affected by the continuing crisis in Nigeria. The insecurity has forced several thousand people to flee attacks from non-state armed groups in the Lake Chad basin, mainly in the areas of KaigaKindjiria and Ngouboua. As to date, there are more than 330,000 internally displaced people and 30,000 returnees from Nigeria and 15,892 Nigerian refugees in the country relocated to the Dar Essalam camps. Those displacements people exert an increased pressure on existing structures in a context of low access to essential basic services (health, education, water). There are facing many challenges to meet their essentials needs and more than 15,000 children will require treatment for severe acute malnutrition. Assistance will be tailored based on the needs of specific groups whithout discrimination and taking into account the evolution the COVID-19 pandemic. Activities1. Procurement and distribution of 15,111 RUTF boxes and others nutrition supplies, materials and equipment to health facilities 2. Ensure training and refresher training for 50 community health workers on on the integrated nutrition community package including nutrition essential actions, protection and accountability for beneficiaries3.) Raise awareness of 1,250 mothers on IYCF practices4.) Conduct monthly monitoring of nutrition status for 20,673 children using Mothers MUAC strategy for early detection of SAM cases 5. Ensure regular Nutrition sub cluster coordination meeting 6. Conduct quarterly joint field monitoring visits and joint supervision to health facilities Results 1.) 15,111 children treated for SAM, disaggregated by sex and age 2.) 50 community health worker trained on the integrated nutrition community package including nutrition essential actions, protection and accountability for beneficiaries disaggregated by sex and age 3.)
