Project Summary

The project is proposed to support the effected families for food security through on farm and off farm support of COVID-19, Locust & flood effected families in district Badin/Sindh. Each targeted family will be provided concentrated animal feed sufficient to protect their core livestock assets and livestock-based livelihood for at least 6 months. Additionally, where there is extreme loses of livestock. Vaccination for livestock would also be provided based on need to protect them from fatal disease and parasites. Vaccination will be done through the recommended relevant government department.
Similarly on the other hand most vulnerable families will be provided crops support where they can be given trainings on kitchen gardening and they will be given seeds of enriched vegetables and fruits to cover the nutritional food values especially females and children. Seed packages will be distributed among the trained individuals including both males and females.
The proposed project will benefit 17528 individuals in selected areas of district Badin. A total of 2504 households would be direct beneficiaries with ratio of 45% females, 55% males and 10% adolescent girls, boys and young children. Proposed interventions will include provision of food parcels to 2504 households meeting the criteria of nutritional values especially for females and adolescent girls. Concentrated animal fodder packs will be distributed to 300 households. The animal feed will be provided in sufficient amount at least for three months to protect their core livestock assets. The same identified 300 families will be given fast growing, multicut, nutritious and acclimatized fodder seeds of sorghum, millet, guar and rhodes grass etc. to replenish the fodder which was deteriorating due to drought affects. 750 farmer families will be provided with crops packages to protect their agriculture assets. They will be given short cycle cash crop seeds of good quality for different crops including pulses etc to enable them to cultivate their lands immediately to ensure food security with special focus on nutritional needs of women and adolescent girls. The project will also integrate women specific needs and will provide vegetable seed packages for 750 families for kitchen gardening in order to support household economy and balanced diet for each household.
200 trainings one in each village will be given for water reservoirs that can store rainwater for agriculture and livestock usage. The water resources rehabilitation will be done through distribution of food packs mentioned above. Around 72 sessions on livestock handling and kitchen gardening will arranged for target community in District Badin in order to optimize the impact of assistance and to make the action more sustainable.

1- Project Activities

Activity 1: Affected families will receive food & livelihood support to protect and enhance their livestock productivity1.1: Formation of 25 Village Committees in selected 25 villages of district Sujjawal. Each committee will consist on 10-15 members with an equal representation males and females.1.2: Identification of target households for distribution of animal feed1.3: Distribution of concentrated animal feed packages sufficient to protect their core livestock assets among 300 households identified based on their livestock rearing activities in which 2100 individuals will be benefitted1.4: provision of fast growing, multicut, nutritious and acclimatized fodder seed packages(i.e. sorghum, millet, guar and Rhodes Grass etc.) among 300 families.1.5: Trainings of farmers (males & females) on animal feed production and livestock rearing for 300 households1.6: Vaccination of livestock based on need will be provided to protect them from fatal diseases and parasites for 300 households. In total 5000 crops seed package and vaccination will be distributed. Activity 2: Establish kitchen gardening along with provision of kitchen gardening toolkits for disaggregated people (women) from community to practice food security measures at HH level2.1:200 crops packages to improve variety crop / fodder and pulses seeds to enable affected farmers to cultivate their lands 2.2: 750 families provided with highly nutritious assorted vegetables seeds packages for kitchen gardening to support household economy and a more balanced household nutritional diet.2.3: 72 trainings on kitchen gardening provided to 750 households. From each household one female and one male participation will be ensured and each training will include 20 participants with an equal ratio of males and females.2.4: 725 tool kits for kitchen gardening will be distributed among identified farmer families.2.5: 150 disaggregated drought affected households will be trained to manage poultry, livestock . Activity 3 : Irrigation infrastructure improved for water conservation3.1: 200 trainings will be conducted over rehabilitation and building of water reservoirs for 2504 target households in which 02 participants will be selected from each household (one male and one female). In this way total 5008 individuals will be benefitted.3.2: 2504 Food packages will be distributed among identified vulnerable families to give them incentive for rehabilitation of their water storage systems. In this way total target population of 17528 individuals will be benefitted including males, females from all age groups.
