Project Summary

The project aims are providing critical life-saving primary health care services to IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities to reduce morbidity and mortality among vulnerable crisis- affected women and children in Badhadhe and Jamame through provision of essential primary health care services. The project targets the most vulnerable 5092 Children under 5, 1018 children under 1, 5856 WCBA and 1018 Mothers in Badhadhe and Jamame Districts, Lower Juba Region, Somalia through the following activities: Training of MCH staff and TBAs, provision of medical supplies including essential drugs , provision of essential health services including pre-natal and neo-natal for new mothers and their children at the health facility, Provision of quality and affordable curative and preventive health services at the facility, conducting immunization on common disease outbreak including EPI, Conducting awareness on utilization of health services at the health facilities in Badhadhe and Jamame District, Lower Juba Region, Somaia through mobilization and sensitization meetings and forums.

Needs

The core health problem in Somalia particularly in Lower Juba is limited access to preventative and curative health services (in terms of quality, quantity and distribution), resulting in high levels of morbidity and mortality. The core problem can be attributed to two root causes, which are: shortage of essential resources (technical, financial, material and human) and limited capacity of the state's health support systems. These two problems are synergistic and aggravate each other thus weakening health situation. In addition these causes are further exacerbated by the scattered population, long distances to health facilities, outdated socio-cultural practices which are not conducive with good health, poor environmental conditions, insecurity, high levels of poverty experienced in the region and limited interventions undertaken by humanitarian agencies in the area owing to insecurity and corresponding access challenges. The situation is exacerbated by acute food insecurity and sustained malnutrition rates among children aged under 5 years and pregnant lactating women. Furthermore, diseases outbreaks re-occur due to poor health infrastructure and a lack of clean and safe water, limited sanitation facilities and poor hygiene conditions and practices. Huge Health gaps in Badhadhe and Jamame are compounded by neglect of existing health facilities after the collapse of the central Government. (FSNAU post Gu technical report of 2 September 2018) 70% acutely malnourished including and severely malnourished and face increased risk of morbidity and death, are in South Central regions of Somalia including the project locations of Badhadhe and Jamame with, GAM prevalence of above critical threshold (15%) hence need for an urgent and integrated health, Nutrition, WASH, Food Security.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Risk of deliberate exclusion of marginalized and minority groups during selection ofthe targeted beneficiaries. Mitigation measure: Having a representation of all groupsselection and distribution teams

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Risk of Sexual Gender based violence among women and young girls when covering long distance toaccess nutrition services. Mitigation: Accompanying the targeted women and young girls to healthcenters or organizing for them to go in groups

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Risk of women, men, boys and girls getting caught up in the armed conflictparticularly when accessing health services at the health centers locations. Mitigation: Ensuringhealth centers/sites are away from conflict areas
