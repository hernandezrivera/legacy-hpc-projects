Project Summary

For Palma and Mocimboa districts: out-of-camp displaced and returnees populations are supported through a dedicated gender responsive approach enhancing coordination, communication, information sharing and accountability in both districts.
For Nangade district: i) Enhance self-governance through meaningful participation and engagement with displaced persons.
II) Enhance the protective environment in site and site-like settings for the displaced populations and the host community.
III) Coordinate inclusive and adapted access to services and provision of information in meaningful way to improve the quality of integrated services for displaced populations in site and site-like setting.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

SI will adopt mechanisms that promote and support collective and participatory approaches that inform and listen to communities in their diversity. Continuously, while monitoring the project activities, the MEAL team will also collect the opinion of the community to assess their level of satisfaction. Opinion will be collected taking into consideration the views of the different groups within the communities. A reporting mechanism will be put in place to adapt activities as necessary. An inclusive and adapted complaint collection and response mechanism and a fraud prevention policy will be developed to ensure that beneficiaries, employees and other stakeholders are able to voice their concerns and claims about the activities that it implements and receive timely feedback in a confidential and adapted manner. The various complaints received will be recorded and processed according to a well-defined process, depending on their level of seriousness and subject matter.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

SI is committed in accountability to affected populations at all stages of the project, from the immediate response to the reconstruction and transition stages. Placing women and men in their diversity from the communities at the center of programs contributes to the overall improvement of the quality of SI's projects and limits the risk of abuse by SI employees. Accountability means that SI is committed to listen to the differentiated needs , concerns and opinions of all stakeholders of women and men, particularly in the communities. More generally, SI strives to improve the quality, learning and effectiveness of its programs by putting partners and people from the communities at the center of its work.Beneficiaries womena d men will be equaliy involved at every step of the project. Beneficiaries were involved at the assessment stage through focus group discussions (gender and age segregated) and key informant interviews. When the project was designed, recommendations of interviewed IDPs were taken into consideration to define the activities as well the methodology, so as to best answer needs and to respect local habits As much as possible, SI will work with staff familiar with the local communities’ socio-cultural background. A complaint mechanism will allow the community to give its opinion about the activities, in particular distributions, and the way they are implemented. Moreover, data protection and confidentiality will be guaranteed throughout the treatment process of complaints through various mechanisms (protected and anonymized documents and databases, limited number of staff having access, strict transmission channels, etc.).Collection of voices of women and girls will be ensure to adapt the response to tehir specific neds and capacities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

SI intends to conduct a Community Safety Mapping exercise to identify physical, security, protection and health risks through regular monitoring of the site, site observations and consultations with different groups, including women and men with disabilities. Sensitization activities with IDPs on CCCM concepts and best practices will be conducted as well, and Information Education and Communication (IEC) materials distributed. These IEC materials (posters, leaflets, brochures, flip-overs, billboards etc) will includes (but are not limited to):- Assistance and services available in the relocation sites;- Prevention of PSEA, fraud, and corruption;- Meaningful inclusion of women, children, the elderly, and persons with specific needs into decision-making processes;- How to access linha verde and complaints response mechanisms;

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

SI will use the “Framework for Responding, Assessing, Monitoring and Evaluating the environment in refugee-related operations (FRAME) toolkit, developed by UNHCR for refugee and return situations, to ensure that environmental assessment, monitoring of programmes and evaluations are conducted in a systematic manner that improves environmental management and livelihood security of displaced persons and others affected by the presence of humanitarian operations. For the community centers, SI will use local materials and source all materials from local markets when and where available. For this project, it includes bamboo, wood poles, and purlins. In addition, SI will develop community engagement campaigns on:• Energy efficient cooking techniques• Sustainable constructions and risks of deforestation• Fire hazards and mitigation strategies
