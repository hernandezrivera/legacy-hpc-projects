Project Summary

Under the sector Objective 1, activity 1.1.3 Community-based protection services as well as CBI/CVA.
The project has capitalized on evidence-based best practice for the development of social protection systems, Protection CVA is a targeted support designed specifically to address protection incidents and vulnerability because cash assistance has an indirect impact on mitigating structural drivers of protection issues, aim to help prevent and respond to protection concerns such as violence, exploitation, deprivation or discrimination and to support beneficiaries to enjoy their rights.
. When financial burden is reduced, cash assistance can render women and girls (caregivers) less vulnerable to sexual exploitation and negative coping strategies such as quit education to serve a person with disability or an elderly,
The duration of the cash intervention was found to have favorable protection impact, with longer-term grants correlating with a decreased number of protection incidents.
Paper cash restricted voucher with SYP 100,000 per month for one year to the most vulnerable HHs who have an elderly or PWDs. The amount has been calculated to support HHs with the diapers cost to reduce the HH economy deficit. CVA will be distributed on monthly basis to 1200 affected persons in Shaar at Rescate office. The selection was done based on vulnerability score analysis.
The project activities will be conducted in coordination with the protection sector. Rescate will, under Activity 1.1. Provide quality and integrated protection services, with a focus on community-based approaches, including awareness-raising, PSS assistance, targeted support to persons with specific protection needs, through outreach mechanisms. Trainings will include: caregivers raise awareness, address their own attitudes and build skills to design and deliver age-oriented programmes, Identify and manage chronic and non-communicable diseases among older people, and to provide accessible information on healthy ageing and self-management, paying attention to the different health needs of older women and older men with and without disabilities. Support volunteers and caregivers with information, advice, and appropriate equipment, for example, use of gloves or masks when changing diapers assistive products, check lists for assessing needs and progress, and supervise their activities. Prevent elder abuse specially those who are in need: Emotional abuse – inflicting psychological pain, anguish, or distress on an older person by means of verbal or non-verbal acts that are humiliating, intimidating or threatening, Abandonment – desertion of an older person by anyone who has assumed the responsibility for care or custody of that person. Prevention measures: Consistently raise awareness in communities and families and among older persons about what constitutes elder abuse, sexual abuse and GBV against older women and men and other forms of violence. Train staff and community volunteers 50 on the warning signs of elder abuse and how to respond (through established procedures of referrals to others services that RESCATE does not provide.) and respecting the wishes of the survivor. Work with GBV survivor support services to respond to older persons who seek their services. Include elder abuse, including GBV, within protection strategies and pay attention to the protection of widows. This project will impact positively on the wellbeing of elderlies, their families and caregivers. It will enhance their resilience, dignity and positive relation with themselves and community. This project will impact positively on the wellbeing of 1,200 elderlies and PWDs the families and caregivers. It will enhance their resilience, dignity and positive relation with themselves and community.

4 - Needs

Rescate team collect and data, where relevant, on needs, priorities and capabilities relating to cash assistance. Rescate applied assessment, targeting, design, implementation, monitoring and accountability, to ensure that people with specific needs and protection risks are identified. In the areas of intervention in Shaar Severity 3 HNO 2022, Rescate engaged a sample of community members, with an AGD lens, in ensuring that mitigation strategies are incorporated into design based on risks and benefits identified in assessments. Data proven that 16% HHs have persons in need of CVA support to cover diapers costs to alleviate burdens from women and girls who are mostly caregivers. Protection sector MSNA covered psychological distress of elderlies and PWDs. Also tackled women and girls who are caregivers to elderlies and PWDs and are in many cases subjected to increased denial of economic resources and education, movement restrictions, exploitation, psychological, emotional, and social distress due to this livelong task. The assessment provided for Rescate design tailored approach, particularly for CVA against NFI items outside of the standardized kits and thus diversify needed inputs. Under the current escalated socio- economic crisis, record unemployment and a Covid-19 pandemic captivating community who are trying to recuperate from a decade of conflict, thus elderlies & PWDs, in addition to women and girls will be protected from health risks, distress and violence.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Occurrence of gender-based violence, sexual exploitation & abuse: Rescate staff, volunteers and partners are child protection gender and GBV aware; GBV is strongly mainstreamed in this project; Safeguarding accountability and reporting mechanisms are in place in Rescate and partners. Women may be harassed to get access to the services, hence; Rescate will conduct awareness sessions on GBV and the role of women in their communities, besides to the extent possible Rescate will ensure women participation in the activities to ensure their support when beneficiaries are females• Rescate always prioritizes community engagement to ensure a two-way communication and strengthen AAP systems. Rescate will conduct FGDs, assessments, analysis of complaints and response mechanism (CRM) and post distribution monitoring (PDM) reports, including the quality and appropriateness of ongoing assistance or risks related to PSEA & GBV. An AAP system including beneficiaries’ participation & appropriate information, complaints & feedback mechanism to be used under this intervention to ensure that communities have access to a mechanism that they can use to express their grievances. Risk of CVA misuse, a staff will verify eligible beneficiaries and will conduct home visits to ensure CVA is used for the ultimate purpose. Build the capacity of GBV specialist and non-specialist to respond, prevent and mitigate GBV & ensure appropriate sensitive communication techniques with a do no harm approach.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk of sexual exploitation: Gender-based violence and sexual exploitation and abuse Likelihood: Low – Impact: High Mitigation measure: All Rescate staff and partners, and contractors, are required to sign the Code of Conduct which directly refers to the Protection of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and to Gender Based Violence (GBV). As part of on-going efforts to improve accountability, Rescate is committed to implement a beneficiary Complaints and Response Mechanisms (CRM) in all of its project to allow beneficiaries to raise concerns, make complaints and give feedback on Rescate work, as well as the contractors’ work, and to rely any arising issue, including potential PSEA or GBV issues. All PSEA and/or GBV issues will be addressed first by the Project coordinator, who will share the concern with the Head of Mission based in Damascus, in order to establish a solution or way to proceed to enhance the protection of the PSEA/GBV survivor. When needed, the Head of Mission will scale the issue to ONG Rescate Headquarters in Spain, where the Director, in consultation with the gender specialists’ team, will look for potential solutions and/or an appropriate way to proceed.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

CVA for protection: Risk that aid provided will be diverted or cause unintended harmful consequences will be mitigated as follows: Targeted risk assessments and ongoing monitoring and adjustment of programming accordingly should be conducted. Modalities will be selected based on a thorough assessment of risks and benefits. Joint work between cash and protection colleagues will be fostered to strengthen technical aspects throughout the programme life cycle.Coordination with other sectors, particularly health, remains critical to overcome challenges amongst direct beneficiaries, caregivers, and volunteers.Assumption that Covid-19 will not disrupt the project. It is difficult to assess beforehand the probability for this to happen and its consequences. Rescate will take prevention measures since the project launch to avoid contagion: PPE will be distributed for the staff & volunteer. Assumption is that there will be stability of prices for the project implementation, there is a high risk that this will not be the case, and could have grave consequences. Mitigation will be to pay down-payment to the local contractor to purchase stock for the project. In terms of support, the assumption is that stakeholders, Sector, and local authorities maintain a strong collaboration with Rescate. There is a low risk this would not be the case, as previous projects have been successfully implemented under this line.
