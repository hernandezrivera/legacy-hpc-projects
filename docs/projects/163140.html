Project Summary

This project is in line with the Basic Education program between UNICEF and MINEDUB. The project aims at building an active social and learning environment that permits children to enroll, learn and complete the school cycle in 30 schools in 03 subdivision. This project is part of the ‘Connect my school’ program that is presently being implemented in the East region. UNICEF goal is to connect 3 sub division in the East region with internet services precisely Ngoura, Garoua boulai and Bertoua2. A total of 642 schools are in these divisions, which will benefit 49,489 pupils.
Presently the Spanish Natcom for UNICEF is financing the connection for 8 schools in these sub divisions with internet services. A field visit was conducted to visualize how the children are using the tablets with both online and offline content. The children showed a mastery of the usage and this will go a long way to improve on learning and ICT capacity of the underprivileged children in these schools. The tablets with both online and offline content are improving the learning of children. This project will provide teachers and learners not only with learning ICT, but also learning through ICT, it will also aid actors of the educational supervision chain to ensure a better supervision by relying on tools existing through ICTs. This will contribute in improving the quality of education. It will then be a question of revitalizing/making functional the internal and external participatory management structures in the schools so that they can mobilize and permanently raise awareness to the parents on the importance of education for children in general and most especially girls and other vulnerable children. Thus, it will consequently improve the school retention in the locality of the project
The project will consist of connecting these 30 schools with internet using internet fly box and a solar panel to provide electricity. A small classroom will be constructed to serve as a multimedia center where the children will easily have access to the devices that will be handed to the school. Materials such as desktops, tablets, fly box…. etc. will be handed to the school. The school administrators in collaboration with the community will make sure the materials are well kept and manage. The teachers will be trained and on the use of ICT in schools, on the usage of these tools to improve learning. They will also be trained on how to manage these materials and on the sustainability side of the project.
The project will be monitored by UNICEF in collaboration with all the other stakeholders such as the government, computer start-ups and the community. A donor field visit will be organized to evaluate the project.
At the end of the project, it is expected that in each school:
- Students will demonstrate learning improvements. We will train the regional inspector to make a simple learning outcome evaluation per year to ensure that children improve knowledge.
- Learning ICT based platforms will be ameliorated. The ICT platforms will be put in place by start-up to help teachers not only to do research, but also to exchange experience and discus on pedagogic approach.
- Teachers will use ICT to improve their teaching (i.e. to plan lessons). After the project the need of utilization of ICT will be identify with teachers and pedagogic inspectors. Then it will be evident for users to improve their teaching.
- School enrollment and retention will be improved. ICT is more attractive for children, and it will reinforce the school attending for children.
- Teachers retention will be improving. Teachers in rural area like to be move to urban zone, then ICT and internet will permit them to be connect with the world in the rural zone.

Besoins / Needs

The target population consists of school-age children, school children, teachers, parents, especially members of the school's participatory structures and community structures. The project supports the Ministry of Basic Education through decentralized structures such as the Regional Delegation of the East, the Divisional Delegation for Basic Education of Lom and Djerem and the Sub-Divisional Inspectorate for Ngoura, Garoua Boulai and Bertoua II. In school and out of school children:Our project aims to educate children of school going age from 30 communities in the Sub-division of Ngoura, Garoua-Boulai and BertouaII in the East Region of Cameroon. 45,000 in-school children will be targeted. 200 schools are targeted for this project.Teachers:180 teachers will be directly targeted within the framework of training courses on the Pedagogic Practice, learning and teaching through of ICT.Parents of the Pupils:Parents of the pupils will be mobilized through awareness raising campaigns on the importance of Education, enrolment and retaining of children in schools. Among these populations are members of the school's participatory structures (SMC, PTA, APM, GC). We will be targeting 3000 households of about 6000 parents.Regional Delegation of Basic Education (RDBE)The project is in support of the RDBE, whose mission is to ensure inclusive and quality basic education for all children in the region. During the Project implementation, UNICEF will advocate for the recruitment of qualified teachers and to ensure an equitable distribution in schools within the region. The training and the pedagogic supervision of the teachers will be ensured by the technical staff of the RDBE. Divisional Delegation of the Basic Education (DDBE) of “Lom et Djerem”The DDBE consists of a Delegate and four pedagogical advisors. It is the supervisory structure of the practicing schools in the sub-division. It ensures regular implementation of the pedagogic supervision in the schools of the area. The Delegate and his four pedagogic advisors will be responsible for the implementation of the technological innovation involved in the activity of pedagogic supervision and the follow-up of attendance in schools. Sub-divisional Inspectorate of the Basic Education (IAEB) of Ngoura, Garoua-Boulai and BertouaIIThe IAEB consists of an inspector and two pedagogical animators. It is the supervisory structure of proximity to the schools of the sub-division. It ensures regular implementation of the pedagogic supervision in the schools of the area. The inspector and his two pedagogic animators will be responsible for the implementation of the technological innovation involved in the activity of pedagogic supervision and the follow-up of attendance in schools.
