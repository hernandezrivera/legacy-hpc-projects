Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to enhance access to life saving reproductive health care services including basic and emergency obstetric and neonatal care services, referral of obstetric complications and clinical management of rape survivors to 239233 women of whom 113810 are refugees and IDPs and 125424 are women from host communities. In general 93817 women are in reproductive health age and 140726 are youth and adolescent (boys and girls).
Summary:
This project aims to provide reproductive health services to save the lives of women and girls of reproductive age for both displaced (refugees and IDPs) and host communities. On the other hand, the context of a protracted crisis with a tendency towards calm, the project aims to strengthen the health system thus responding to the approach nexus humanitarian - development. The main interventions can be summarized as (i)ordering and distribution of equipment and SR kits to health facilities, (ii)the deployment of mobile clinics with a primary health care package, (iii) ensuring evacuations for obstetric complications, (iv) the clinical management of rapes, (v) the deployment of midwives in health facilities, (vi) strengthening the capacity of health workers on certain key themes for the survival of women of reproductive age and (vii) sensitization of young people and adolescents on HIV and gender-based violence.
The total cost of the project is estimated at 2217000 USD.
ACTIVITIES AND OUTPUTS
OUTPUT 1: The life of 233239 women and girls (including pregnant and lactating women) residing in Diffa region is saved:
• Activity 1.1: Procure and deliver maternal health medical equipment, supplies and Emergency Reproductive Health Kits (ERHK) to 20 of targeted facilities;
• Activity 1.2: Procure lifesaving medicines, Voluntary Counselling Testing (VCT), Prevention of mother to child transmission of HIV from an HIV positive mother (PMTCT) materials and testing kits as well as PEP kits;
• Activity 1.3: Procure and deliver 30,000 Dignity Kits for pregnant women and vulnerable adolescent girls.
• Activity 1.4: Conduct two orientation sessions to strengthen capacity of 40 health service providers including medical doctors, midwives and nurses, to provide quality services on Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal Care
• Activity 1.5: Conduct 3 orientation sessions on clinical management of rape for 60 health service providers
• Activity 1.6: Conduct 4 orientation sessions on Minimum Initial Service Package for sexual and reproductive health (MISP) for 80 health service providers and partners;
• Activity 1.7: Procure one ambulance to facilitate referral of complicated obstetrical and GBV cases from CSI to CSME and referral hospitals in Diffa Region;
• Activity 1.8: Rehabilitate 15 maternity according to the nexus humanitarian/development
OUTPUT 2: The access to SRH information and services to vulnerable people in hard to reach zones is improved
• Activity 2.1: Undertake three emergency response mobile clinic missions per quarter in the 5 most affected health district (60 missions) to scale up SRH integrated package services
• Activity 2.2: Deploy 10 midwives in the 10 most affected health facilities in Diffa
• Activity 2.3: Ensure the referencing of 400 obstetric complications to mother-child health centers and district hospitals;
• Activity 2.4: Ensure the exemption of the expenses related to the 6000 assisted deliveries;
• Activity 2.5: Conduct community mobilization on SRH integrated package including HIV/AIDS transmission to affected people and refugees in Diffa Region;
• Activity 2.6: Produce IEC/BCC materials to be used during sensitization activities and distributed to affected people and refugees in Diffa Region;
• Activity 2.7: Ensure the distribution of 5,000 hygienic delivery kits to visibly pregnant women during mobile clinical missions and refugee camps

Needs

Niger is currently facing complex and multifaceted crises such as terrorism attacks from Boko Haram Jihadists group inter-community conflicts resulting in serious humanitarian needs in the areas of reproductive health. Most of time, sexual and reproductive health (SRH) is overlooked with dire consequences. Pregnant women risk life-threatening complications, women and girls become more vulnerable to sexual violence and HIVThis project will target 220198 host communities living five departments of Diffa (Diffa, Bosso, Nguigmi, Mainé and Goudoumaria) and 118868 refugees living, in Sayam camps as well as in different localities of Diffa Region and 104288 are IDPs, from this affected people 93817 are women in reproductive health age. Among them, 18763 are expected to give birth in twelve month period, while 2815 women would experience obstetric complications at delivery and need referral to a comprehensive emergency obstetric care center for delivery or risk losing their lives; or 1876 are at risk of sexual violence which, in the absence of adequate medical care, will probably lose their lives. Building on the activities that were successfully supported and implemented in 2018 to save lives of women refugees and women from host community in Diffa, UNFPA is committed to provide (i) lifesaving medicines, (ii)medical equipment, (iii) supplies and emergency reproductive health kits to affected health facilities needed to offer quality basic and essential RH services including safe deliveries, emergency obstetric and newborn care, (iv) Family Planning, and (v) clinical management of sexual violence. In addition, UNFPA will support the capacity building of health providers to the above services and enable health facilities to improve referral mechanism in place and deploy integrated mobile clinics for populations without access to health primary care.
