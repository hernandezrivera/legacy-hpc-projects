Project Summary

As per current Recovery Need Assessment conducted by Natural Disaster Consortium; Consumption of water from Hand pumps decreased from 72% to 66% while increased from 26% to 33% for surface water in District Sanghar. 95% HH in Sanghar are reported their drinking water source affected by floods. Most of the population is getting muddy, foul tasting and bed smelling water for their water sources. Very less population has been reported for using water treatment methods before consumption of water.
For 89% population in Sangahr the use of basic hygiene items is not common in assessed areas. Diarrhea Malaria and Skin disease are most common reported disease increasing day by day after floods. 42% population is not using soaps for hand washing in District Only 22% population in Sanghar is reported washing their hands before cooking and nearly 16% women in Sanghar wash their hands before feeling the children.
As per coordination with the partners, data collected under an assessment. Out of 64 surveyed schools 38% were reported fully damaged, while 36% schools are partially damaged. 85% water systems have been reported damaged. Out of which 55% are fully damaged. People have to look for alternate sources to gain water, through either water trucking or fetching water from distant sources, which is also not reported as completely safe for purpose of drinking. Most of the population is not using any method for water purification. Practice related to hygiene are not satisfactory Diarrhea, Malaria, Cough and Cold are the majorly reported disease in District Sujawal.
The purpose of the intervention is to ensure access to WASH services to 60,000 disaster affected people in Sindh (Including women children, men, elderly people and people with disability). To accomplish this, extensive series of activities will be undertaken to improve the living conditions of people. Project intervention will be contributing to Sustainable Development Goals-SDGs, goal 6 “Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all”. HANDS overall aim is to provide humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable individuals, while maximizing the efficiency and effectiveness of intervention. considering the vulnerability of the selected UCs in the selected districts namely Saman Shah and Loon Khan from District Sanghar and Jhok Sharif and Ali Akber Shah from district Sujawal.
Assistance will be provided to beneficiaries selected through defined scoring criteria for flood affected families, prioritizing vulnerable families and households including female-headed, child-headed, elderly-headed, and households headed by disabled persons or chronically ill. This arrangement will help in reducing the risk of exclusion, will help in avoiding duplication and minimize relief disparity at the community level.
HANDS is already implementing Flood Response program in both districts but different UC’s. The UC’s selected in coordination with District Administration in each district. HANDS is actively engaged in coordination with District Administration, DDMA and other stakeholder, as there is HANDS Disaster Management Center (DRMC) in each district. HANDS DRMC’s play a role or bridge between communities and Government line department, DDMA and other stakeholders through coordination and linkages development with existing structures and community institutions.

1- Project Activities

1. Rehabilitation of Community Water Points (Hand pump, dug wells etc.) To meet immediate need of safe drinking water HANDS will identify and rehabilitate communal hand pumps, damaged by the floods. This will included preparing new platform, repair or boreholes and machines. Platform built for the hand pumps will be based on DRR as well as inclusive approaches to ensure access of Persons with Disability to water. 200 Community Water Points will be rehabilitated after identification to cover 18900 population including women and children, this will meet the need of safe drinking water in the communities. 1.6 Water quality testing (Pre & Post)HANDS will undertake water quality test of water sources to determine their suitability for the supply of drinking water to affected communities. A key aspect of this assessment is to determine the microbiological quality of the water in order to determine the water treatment requirements. A total of 260 water quality tests will be conducted before and after rehabilitation of water points in community and also in schools.2. Hygiene Promotion CampaignHANDS will ensure adoption of safe and healthy hygiene practices throughout the duration of WASH responses. This will include key messages about regular hygiene and demonstrate handwashing with soap as well. With an aim to change affected populations’ hygiene habits, key messages will reach to 9,000 HH (56,700 people). To ensure the safe hygiene practices by the communities, hygiene promotion campaigns will also include;2.1 Provision of Women MHM/Dignity KitsHANDS will provide the Women MHM/Dignity Kits in 9000 Flood affected families of district Sanghar and Sujawal. Kits comprise a minimum package including the Cotton Cloth piece, laundry powder detergent etc.2.2 Provision of Soaps and Jerry Cans Package of Hand Washing Soaps and Jerry Cans will be provided to 9000 flood affected families in district Sanghar and Sujawal. A packet of soaps will be comprising of total 12 soaps and two Jerry Cans for each family. This will benefit the communities especially women & girls as Jerry cans will contribute to storage of drinking water and soaps for personal hygiene. 2.3 Provision of disinfection kits to Health and Schools facilities Disinfection kits will be provided to Health and School facilities for ensuring the hygienic environment. This package will be comprising on Disinfection Spray Machine, Chlorine Disinfectant for surface cleaning, PPE Kit and Industrial Safety Long Rubber Rain Boots. This will benefit the approximately 100 health centers and 100 School facilities in flood affected areas in district Sanghar and Sujawal.3. Rehabilitation of School WASH Facilities Under this Activity HANDS will assess Schools for rehabilitation of WASH Facilities in the Flood affected areas of District Sanghar and Sujawal. 60 School WASH Facilities will be rehabilitated. After the rehabilitation of School WASH facilities approximately 6,000 School children will be benefited with WASH Services. It will also be ensured that there should be mechanism in place to ensure functionality of the sanitation system even after the end of the project period.
