Project Summary

This 12 months project seeks to support youth engagement in Human Rights Development while ensuring meaningful ownership in assisting the humanitarian drive in the process of implementing humanitarian needs and reporting incidents in the Southwest.

This will be done by youths at community levels; creating awareness through civic education for citizens to understand their constitutional rights and obligations and become more responsible. The project will help build the capacity of youth leaders to organise outreach activities. The youth leaders will then serve as agents4change in mobilizing and supporting their communities to address specific issues on protection, human rights and social justice in rebuilding institutions of governance and promoting peace in the southwest and northwest region of Cameroon.
PEP Africa office is a multi-purpose centre that provides empowerment, recreational, educational activities and opportunities for youths through partnerships with, colleges, NGO’s, government agencies, UN systems, INGOs and stakeholders in youth development.
The services and activities include but not limited to; training , job preparation orientation, counselling, life-skill lessons, inclusive education, sexual reproductive health education (SRHE) , internship,, arts and craft, film production, competition, volunteerism etc. It also offers opportunities through which youths acquire cognitive, social, and emotional skills and abilities required to navigate life into their adulthoods. The centre offers a variety of programmes and activities for youth allies all in a safe and supportive space, as well as education services to organizations through innovative and engaging workshops and trainings.PEP Africa engages with youths as equal partners by providing opportunities for them to participate in public life and in development activities.
With an initial financial support from UNHCR/INTERSOS, PEP Africa will spur a number of initiatives both at the level of civil society actors and the media. The Restless Peace Coalition of which PEP Africa is a member will play a significant role to support the project and, will provide the needed training tools and resource persons.
The project will make a difference in the quality of lives of approximately 50000 youth leaders living in conflict areas in the following way: 1) will engage in strategic partnerships to address ethnicity, illegal arrest, and social values amongst youths. 2) Contribute to address, monitor and report issues of human rights abuse and violation. 3) Improve youth’s capacity to understand protection issues and opportunity to respond.
Finally, the project will focus on creating bridges of collaboration with youth synergies and media. As part of the Joint Action Plan for Media Support (JAPMS), one of the axes “communications for behaviour change. This will include the production of TV/Radio programs, SMS Platform, online news papers, mop clips, posters, flyers on protection and awareness education. Reaching over 100,000 Cameroonians with information and messages relevant to protection. Strong contacts already woven the collaboration with PEP Africa.
