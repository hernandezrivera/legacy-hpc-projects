Project Summary

Education is a fundamental and enabling human right, and it must be realized for all children in order to disrupt the inter generational transmission of exclusion, poverty, violence, inequality and family breakdown. Education is key for a life of dignity and respect and for advancing inclusive and fair human development. This 12 Months Project seek to bridge the gap of education in emergency to provide normal formal child friendly learning centers for 2000 internally displace children and other highly vulnerable children including vocational training for Adolescent children within safe learning environment in IDP camps and host communities in Borno State (Magumeri and Nganzai LGAs) North East Nigeria as related to strategy sector objective 1 of Education in Emergency in North East Nigeria.

Needs

An estimated 1.8 million people are displaced in North East Nigeria states of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe and around 40 percent (0.8 million) of that population is comprised of school aged children between three and seventeen years displaced across the three states. While the number of displaced persons and returnees increased compared to the previous year, the total number of children and education personnel in need decreased from 2.9 million for 2018 to 2.2 million for 2019 based on education sector estimates (EiEWG HNO, 2019).The HRP 2019 indicate that there is high severe need for education in Magumeri and Nganzai LGA among the affected by the conflict over 80 per cent were already living below the poverty line. Nearly 2000 homes and Government schools were damaged or destroyed and 75,000 people were internally displaced (NEMA 2016 report) in these two LGAs. The education of the children is severely affected which were classified as vulnerable before the insurgency. There is an urgent need for Education in eermgency interventions in these areas, including restoring livelihoods of adolescent children and access to protective temporary learning centers, to strengthen the self-reliance of affected populations especially to girls and youth.

Activities

This 12 months project plan seek to support 1500 children to access temporary learning centers and 500 Adolescents for livelihood activities in Magumeri and Nganzai LGAs in Borno State of North East Nigeria through the following activities: 1. Create awareness and sensitization on important of education for all age group both under emergency and development to access the learning centers2. Advocate with community leaders and local government authority on integration of formal education system Quranic Education including accelerated learning Programs 3. Construction of Temporary learning centers (TLS) and support in rehabilitation of destroy Class rooms and reopening of schools
