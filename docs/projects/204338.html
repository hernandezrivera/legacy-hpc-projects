1 - Needs

During the Fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has lasted Sudan Humanitarian Response has been increasing special the main needs due to the soaring needs spurred by the current crisis in the country And as the fighting continues between the SAF and the RSF has killed hundreds of people, forced more than 218,000 to flee across borders and displaced over 730,000 people inside the country in the first four weeks of the conflictalso has affected numerous hospitals,12 further compromising Sudan’s already fragile health sector. Since the start of the conflict, the World Health Organization (WHO) has verified 30 attacks on health care facilities, resulting in eight people killed and another 18 injured. A maternity hospital was attacked in Khartoum on 4 May. Power shortages, limited medical supplies, and infrastructure damage are severely hampering the delivery of essential health care. Critical medical supplies, including for managing pregnancy complications, are running dangerously low. In Khartoum alone, live-saving reproductive health for an estimated 219,000 pregnant women is threatened. Urgent assistance is required to restore critical emergency care.And with the increase needs, Sondos Voluntary Organisation implement this project to provide urgent relief that saves people's lives in WASH and health services, urgent medical clinics and medical aid

2 - Activities/Outputs

The strategic goal 1(Water):2-Provision of safe drinking water to the vulnerable communities in khartoum through drilling and upgrading of 5 hand pumps to motorized solar system , extension and expansion of the water sources to the unreachable areas. 3-Conduct environmental sanitation campaigns for proper garbage management and disposal in and the removal of post-war waste from the street neighborhoods of the city of Khartoum. 2-Conduct community training's on water resources management, needs assessment, effective leadership, and cholera/ malaria prevention. 4-Conduct regular hygiene campaigns at community, public areas and school level. 5- Distribution of soap and other Personal hygiene Kits to the most venerable communities also Distribution of jericans most vulnerable households in omdrman Umbadah. 5- Provision and Re-maintenance of sanitation facilities such as hand washing facilities, to health centers, and schools was destroyed by the war.
