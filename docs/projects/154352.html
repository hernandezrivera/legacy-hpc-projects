Needs

The project responds to the needs identified by the WASH Cluster for a target population of 20.000 people by providing access to a package of wash interventions for newly displaced, returned and host populations in the lake area. Vulnerable people, especially women and children, are the most affected and will receive special attention. The design as well as the location of the works will take into consideration the gender aspect and cross-protection, the beneficiaries play important roles in all stages of the project.

Activities or outputs

The project will contribute to improve health and living conditions of the returnees, IDPs and host communities through a provision of safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene promotion in the regions affected by the Boko Haram in Lake crisis. Women and under five children represent more than half of this population,The main activities of the project are:1-access to drinking water and sanitation in communities and institutions2-Promotion of hygienic behaviours in communities and institutions
