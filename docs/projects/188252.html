Project Summary

Recent reports and HRP prioritize the need for scaling-up interventions to contain the economic deterioration affecting livelihoods, agriculture sector and food security as well as the need to improve and establish protection services especially among the IDPs in camps and informal settlements. AHF has conducted a rapid needs assessment in 7 sub-districts in Idleb and has reported the need for strengthening farmers’ livelihood, providing life-saving assistance, and supporting self-reliance of affected IDPs especially those in the camps that have been affected by the floods during Jan to Mar 2021. To that end, AHF is designing a 10-months project in FSL three stages:
1) Supporting wheat value chain in Maaret Tamsrin.
2) Purchasing farmers' wheat production(600ton)in Maaret Tamsrin.
3) Produce bread and distribute it to the 18 target IDPs camps in Dana sub-district(attached list of the camps 01)
AHF is already implementing bread distribution in the same camps for two years and currently seeking for new funding to continue providing life-saving assistance to the PiN through sustaining the bread distribution activity. the selected beneficiaries from the target IDPs who will receive food assistance(bread) a
Support wheat value chain with share in the cost of fertilizer irrigation harvest and purchasing wheat production from 300 farmers (2 tons from each farmer at a price of $400 per ton) in Maaret Tamsrin.
Distribution of agricultural inputs (wheat seeds) to farmers for the coming season (200 Kg/farmer) with total beneficiaries’ of 1,000 farmers in Maaret Tamsrin.
Bread production at AHF existing bakery & distribution to 18,500 individuals (250g per person of daily bread for 78 days) in the 18 target camps in Dana.
Cash-for-work to 28 individuals for 3 months to support wheat value chain at stages 1&2.
Cash-for-work to 31 individuals for 3 months through baking and distribution activities at stage 3.

4 - Needs

specific needs assessment for this project was conducted during the Dec of 2021 in 3 sub-districts including 7 communities and Dana camps from the most affected by the previous floods. Please see attached (03). Key Findings:There are 390650 individuals in the assessed towns.▪ There are 70470 HHs in the assessed towns,45.1% of them are IDPs (31750 HHs), 54.7% of them are host (38520 HHs), 0.3% of them are returnees (200 HHs).▪ 11.4% of the HHs (8060) are headed by women.▪ There are 26329 persons with disabilities in the target towns.▪ The majority of the respondents said that the majority of the population (more than75%) live in difficult conditions and earn less than 150.000 SP.▪ Most of the respondents said that the majority of the population (more than 75%) are families headed by people with special needs and live in weak economic conditions and their income is less than 150,000 SP.▪ The majority of the respondents said that the percentage of the population that are able to meet their basic needs such as (food, shelter, and health) is little (less than25%).▪ All the respondents said that the most important basic needs for families are bread and food items.▪ The majority of the respondents said that farmers face challenges regarding irrigation fuel costs.▪ All the respondents said that there are young people who are unemployed.▪ The majority of the respondents said that there are NOT humanitarian organizations that support wheat cultivation projects.
