Project Summary

This project consists of two components:
1- Distribution of monthly food parcels to the most vulnerable 1300 HH Lebanese and migrants in North and Akkar for six months
2- Distribution of monthly food parcels for two months to vulnerable positive cases of COVID-19 who are isolating/quarantining in their homes
In total this project will assist 7000 individuals (Men 2319, Women 2512, Boys 1041, Girls 1128) from the most vulnerable households/individuals (including female headed households and caregivers for children under 24 months and elderly dependents, families with children at risk, women and young girls, PLWs, the elderly, and disabled).
Mercy-USA is proposing to target 90% Lebanese and 10% Migrants vulnerable individuals. Mercy-USA will coordinate with IOM and multiply its outreach efforts for migrants. The Migrant Presence Monitoring exercise being finalized now by IOM will be shared with Mercy-USA which will give a good understanding about migrants’ geographical distribution. Moreover, Mercy-USA will implement a referrals mechanism with IOM for migrants. Dissemination and language barriers will be taken into consideration, and Mercy-USA will use IOM's network of Outreach Volunteers and liaise with active partners on the migrant workers coordination group, consequently, the percentage of migrants supported under this project may increase. Coordination with other NGOs working in the area will also be ensured to avoid duplication of services and to ensure a wider coverage of vulnerable communities.
Each food basket is 65.85 kg and contains 1543 Kcal per person per day enough for 5 people for one month which is 73% of the total need 2100 Kcal/person/day, and each household will receive one food basket monthly for six months (from September 2021 until February 2021). This is in line with Food Security Sector recommendations for food baskets.
Each food parcel contains a variety of food items designed to cover all basic nutritional needs tailored to the Lebanese culture and aligned with the Survival Minimum Expenditure Basket (SMEB).

Mercy-USA is proposing to purchase food parcels locally from Lebanon to be transferred to warehouses in the North, then to be distributed regularly on a monthly basis to the selected beneficiaries in the targeted locations following a vulnerability assessment conducted (with previous endorsement from the sector).
Accountability to affected populations will be given a high priority via transparent beneficiary selection criteria, as well as Complaints and Feedback mechanism which will be put in place and accessible to all people through different channels.
Mercy-USA did not consider cash as top up to in-kind food because the respective markets are not fully functioning due to:
1- Limited number of large scale retailers in the respective locations especially in Wadi Khaled
2- Limited access for people to markets especially in far villages due to road closures/blockages
3- Extreme shortage of fuel affects people's access to markets and food transportation to the retailers
4- Most food items are exported from outside Lebanon such as Canada, Turkey and China with limited local production
5- Exchange rate fluctuations (USD/LBP), while all retailers consider higher rates than the black market exchange rates to avoid losses in selling
6- It has been noted recently the quality of available food items is lower than before with few alternatives
7- Availability of the food items with limited quantities for the staple items which can be an issue at any moment

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

The project will contribute towards improving food security status of food-insecure populations affected by the crisis including Lebanese and Migrants communities, as well as households which are COVID-19 positive and isolating in the North (Tripoli) and Akkar (Wadi Khaled) by accessing life-saving in-kind nutritionally balanced food assistance. This project enhances Mercy-USA's capacity to provide lifesaving regular nutritionally balanced monthly food baskets for 6 months through the provision of monthly food baskets. This project aims to provide life-saving food security to those most in need Lebanese and Migrants including female headed households, women and young girls, PLWs, elderly households, and disabled individuals in North and Akkar. 1300 of the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrants households in North (Tripoli) and Akkar (Wadi Khaled) will receive up to 6 in-kind monthly food parcels with minimum 1543 Kcal per person per day (73% of the full requirements of 2100 Kcal/person/day). 500 individuals which are COVID-19 positive and isolating/in quarantine at home or at great risk of contracting COVID-19 in North and Akkar will receive up to in-kind food parcels with minimum 1543 Kcal per person per day (73% of the full requirements of 2100 Kcal/person/day) paired with standardized hygiene Kits.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Mercy-USA will ensure gender mainstreaming protection across all project stages. Female staff will be hired for distribution & MEAL to ensure equal access for all beneficiary categories (females headed HH, girls & widowed). Beneficiaries will be clearly informed that distributed food baskets are free of charge & options for home delivery will be ensured for highly vulnerable persons (e.g. persons with disabilities, elderlies, etc.). A confidential complaints & protection reporting mechanism will be designed & beneficiaries will be clearly informed where to refer or report incidents or violations. The day, time & location of the distribution will be discussed with beneficiaries and area leaders to determine the safest and most accessible location to allow all individuals to access the service with dignity and without exposure to harm. All staff and vendors will sign a code of conduct for sexual exploitation and abuse. All project and IP staff will be trained on PSEA & GBV prevention.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

AAP will be given key role in all project stages. The parcel is designed based on extensive experience with the Lebanese context & culturally accepted food in light of the current economic situation. Mercy-USA will carry out consultations with community members including but not limited to women, disabled, elderly and PLWs throughout varying project stages through FGD to ensure they are at the center of the response & contribute to the decision making process. Mercy-USA will prioritize the hiring of persons from AP in order to maximize community input, encourage transparency. A fixed/Permanent complaints boxes will be put in place at each distribution along with a MEAL team. Complaints will remain strictly confidential. Field staff will coordinate with local authorities & stakeholders to ensure accountability. Considerations will be taken to use door to door modality to deliver food assistance directly to beneficiaries with limited mobility (disabled and elderly).
