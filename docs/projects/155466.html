Project Summary

Restoring, improving and upgrading water supply; Rehabilitation of 110 Boreholes Solar mechanization of 11 boreholes, water quality testing 110 boreholes, Rehabilitation of 3 piped water schemes, including rehabilitation of five ZINWA water supply stations) Hygiene Promotion with a focus on safe hygiene and sanitation practices, participatory health and hygiene education and water conservation (Training of EHTs/Extension workers on PHHE lifesaving messages, Training of VHWs on PHHE lifesaving messages, Dissemination of critical hygiene messages, IEC material development & distribution, Dissemination of messages focused on sanitation improvement, household water treatment and water conservation, Monitoring diarrheal diseases, PHHE sessions in institutions(schools and clinics) Distribution of NFIs to 4,871 Households, including logistics and monitoring. Need to restore and improve the availability of water in areas that have been adversively affected by the drought. There is need to strengthen the relation/link betweween WASH, health, livelihoods and resilince by harnessing water sources that have proven to be high yielding perenial sources to serve more people. Need for water sources which are inclusive i.e accessible to people with disability, elderly and most vulnerable. The distribution of hygiene enabling NFIs and hygiene education pertaining to safe and hygienic child care and feeding practices to prevent occurence of diarrhoreal & other water related diseases. Need for triggering sustainable self provison latrine construction. Need to strengthen community based structures to ensure sustainability.

Activities

To restore and improve access to safe and sufficient water and promote safe hygiene and sanitation practices among vulnerable communities in 11 targeted districts and rural service centers. Priority Actions Based on the above triggers, Christian Care will carry out the following interventions to achieve the three Sector Objectives:• Rehabilitation/upgrading/ construction of strategic water points with a focus of maximizing use of perennial water sources to cover needs of institutions (schools, health centres) and vulnerable communities• Conducting Participatory Health and Hygiene Education and awareness in institutions (health facilities, schools) and communities with high malnutrition rates. This will include training of Environmental Health Technicians (EHTs), Village Health Workers (VHWs) and Community Health Workers (CHWs) supporting community based counselling of caregivers on infant and young child feeding practices on critical lifesaving WASH messages / hygiene practices.• Distribution of WASH hygiene kits targeting care givers of severe and moderate acute malnourished children• Strengthening coordination and surveillance mechanisms at national and sub-national levelsMonitoring Indicators and targets• Number of people (disaggregated M/F, children) having access to at least 7.5 – 15l of safe water per day• Number of people (disaggregated M/F, children) reached with key health and hygiene messages• Number of caregivers receiving WASH hygiene kitsCoordination arrangementsThe overall coordination at the national level will be done through the WASH Sector Coordination and Information Forum (WSCIF) and its Emergency Strategic Advisory Group (E-SAG). At the sub-national level, interventions will be coordinated through the regular coordination structures: Provincial and District Water Supply and Sanitation Committees (PWSSC and DWSSC). The inclusion of specific activities to monitor interventions including feedback from beneficiaries / communities will be promoted among the WASH partners as part of the implementation of this sector strategy.
