Project Summary

According to 2019 Sudan (HNO) and the gaps which were identified by nutrition sector, insufficient access to safe water, poor sanitation facilities, poor hygiene practices, there is no supplementary feeding program for the Modern Acute Malnutrition cases, no stabilization center at the hospital, only one Out Patient Therapeutic (OPT) center at the hospital to manage acute malnutrition in the whole of Central Jebel Mara locality. There is shortage in RUTF for the treatment of children with malnutrition also the high rates of malnutrition in the targeted localities. SORC nutrition project designed to focuses on prevention of life saving and preventive activities to provides quality lifesaving nutrition services for acutely malnourished children (boys and girls 6-59 months of age) and pregnant and lactating women ( PLW) among highly variable community, capacity building for nitration staff and volunteers by train them on CMAM Protocol and conduct immediate screening and referral system, Provide health education sessions to program beneficiaries and caretakers at nutrition centers, Conduct case finding of malnourished children during routine screening at health clinics and community screening and refer them to TSFP/ OTP, IYCF counseling at community level through 10 mother support groups. The project targeted a total of 3,880 benefices from the targeted localities who will be reached through nutrition services including OTP, and IYCF. A Total of 776 PLW, 776 children (under 5), 776 girls, and 776 boys.

Needs

According to 2019 Sudan (HNO) and (FMOH) 2.47 million children suffer from global acute malnutrition annually and some 694,000 children suffer from severe acute malnutrition. In the context of humanitarian crisis in Sudan, malnutrition mostly affects children under the age of five, pregnant and lactating women while the prevalence of malnutrition among the vulnerable groups is informed by the severity mapping. Across all of Sudan, in 2018 around 1.5 million children did not receive malnutrition management services due to lack of resources and capacity of the humanitarian partners. This left them vulnerable to morbidity and further exacerbating the situation. The most vulnerable people live in areas with levels of severity four and five regarding nutrition specific and sensitive indicators in access to food, sanitation, clean drinking water and low coverage of social protection programs. Localized conflict has also contributed to high malnutrition rates. From 17-25 January 2017 survey carried out in Central Jebel Mara locality, GAM and SAM rates were as high as 17.5 per cent and 4.2 per cent respectively. About 2.8 million children suffer from wasting annually, out of whom 693,924 suffer from SAM and 523,243 pregnant and lactating women are in need for counselling on optimal infant feeding and care practices. Maternal nutrition is a concern, with some 277,855 pregnant and lactating women (PLW) undernourished every year. In Um Dukun town the situation is also alarming with inadequate nutritional intake during pregnancy, insufficient access to safe water, poor sanitation facilities, poor hygiene practices, there is no supplementary feeding program for the Modern Acute Malnutrition cases, no stabilization center at the hospital, only one Out Patient Therapeutic (OPT) center at the hospital to manage acute malnutrition in the whole of Central Jebel Mara locality. There is shortage in RUTF for the treatment of children with malnutrition also the high rates of malnutrition in the targeted area Um Dukun, Nertiti, and Golo indicate that malnutrition situation is alarming and need continuous support for the treatment services to children U5 to reduce the malnutrition in Central Darfur. The continued tribal clashes have led to newly displaced persons so the total population in need was estimated in Um Dukun locality is 182,707. 62,901of them IDPs, 31,629 returnee, and 38,941 vulnerable residence. In Golo the total number of population in need is 151,193. 52,675 are returnee, 66,606 vulnerable residence, 4,502 IDPs. Also in Nertiti the total population in need was 197,472. IDPs 58,844, returnee 18,052, and 49,869 are vulnerable residence.

Activities

SORC will implement this project in close collaboration and partnership with key stakeholders like the community, HAC, locality leaders, partners on the ground like CRS, TGH, IMC, WFP, UNICEF, and field coordinator this is to ensure there is no overlap/ duplication of programs. SORC staffs shall directly lead implementation of all activities. SORC has excellent access at the targeted project location since, SORC has field office in the locality. Also has national local staffs with local knowledge supported by senior staff in Um Dukun main office and Khartoum HQ who maintain good working relationships in targeted location with local authorities. This helps SORC reach direct beneficiaries of the project in close coordination with partners on the ground.•Outcome 2:• Conduct 10 assessment at Nutritional centers in Golo, Nirtiti, and UmDukun locality in Central Darfur.• Training of 100 medical and nutrition staff on nutrition in CMAM protocol, acute malnutrition management (severe and moderate) and infant young child feeding.• Training of 24 community volunteers and equip them as rapid response team to conduct MUAC screening.• Sources supplies from WFP for MAM prevention and UNICEF for SAM treatment at OTP.• Establishment of 2 breastfeeding center supported with IYCF awareness and counseling in Golo and Um Dukhun localities.• Conduct regular households MUAC screening and SAM referred to OTP and MAM to SFP while SAM with medical complication to Stabilization center.• Organize 72 awareness sessions on IYCF, optimal breastfeeding, hygiene and sanitation.
