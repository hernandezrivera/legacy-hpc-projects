Project Summary

The project will target the potential beneficiaries in both settings in-camps and off-camps to provide the most lifesaving WASH Services to ensure the puplic health along with enviromrntal hygeine for those which are effected from continious conflict over 10 years and also the implecations of COVID-19 pandemic. the project will provide the assistance as following:
1. In-Camp:
-Deliver safe and chlorinated drinking water through water trucking or network 35l/person/day along with distribution for communal water tanks. (85,000 beneficiaries are already covered)
-Establish Latrines in with rate 20-25 person/cell. Taking into consideration PwD and gender segregation. (10,000 beneficiaries are already covered)
-Transfer and proper disposal the solid waste along with distribution for communal waste containers.
-Desludging the septic tanks and also rain water drainage along with establish proper infrastructure inside the camp. (85,000 beneficiaries are already covered)
-Distribution for Hygiene Kits either through in-kind modality or cash voucher. (28,285 beneficiaries is covered already)
-Conduct hygiene promotion sessions. (85,000 beneficiary are covered already)
-Establish/rehabilitate water systems inside these camp with involving the green energy such as solar power systems.
-Implement vector control sessions to combating the flyers, rodents and reptiles.
2.Off-Camp setting:
- Rehabilitation for water systems in order to deliver drinking water through water network including maintenance and/or expansion for the existing water network with involving the green energy such as solar power systems.
- Rehabilitation for sewages network in order to ensure proper disposal for the waste water including maintenance and/or expansion for the existing network taking into consideration the wastewater treatment and re-cycling.
- Re-activate the SWM mechanism along with distribution for communal waste containers.
- Re-activate the Tariff system to ensure the community ownership and sustainability.
- Distribution for Hygiene Kits either through in-kind modality or cash voucher.
- Conduct hygiene promotion sessions.
- Implement vector control sessions to combating the flyers, rodents and reptiles.
-Ensure that WASH Services are reaching the other facilities such as educational, health and protection facilities.
The activities which are related to Victor Control Specially which prevention for spreading the sand fly which is responsible for Leishmaniosis diseases will be coordinated with MENTOR Initiative in order to avoid overlapping along with best impact for the intervention.

4 - Needs

Over ten years of conflict, more than 2 million people are living in more than 1100 IDP's site in NWS. The multi movement and displacement increased suffering for those peoples to WASH services in addition to other services related to SNFI, FSL, and health humanitarian sectors. in another hand, part of the IDP's are living inside the communities which caused more pressure on the existing infrastructure and caused some times maul-functioning for those systems which increased the sufferings as well.The project will cover those needs through implementing sustainable oriented activities to ensure that the affected populations have the proper access for the WASH services in both settings such as treated drinking water.Violet have worked on assessing the WASH needs in communities and IDP's sites in the proposed targeted locations and the main findings were:- Lack for sustainable access to safe, adequate and clean drinking water in both IDP's Camps and Communities (500,000 Beneficiaries in Communities and 200,000 beneficiaries in Camps)- Lack for proper waste water disposal and treatment. (200,000 Beneficiaries in Communities)- Lack in accessing the Sanitation facilities in IDP's camps. (200,000 beneficiaries in Camps)- Massive need for SWM and vector control to avoid spreading out the outbreaks and diseases (200,000 beneficiaries in Camps)- Respondents also reported that they have an issues in accessing the hygiene related supplies. (500,000 beneficiaries).The assessment were covering around 80 communities and 250 camps in the proposed targeted area.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be started with mobilization for the resources along with signing the required MoU's between Violet and Local Authorities such as Local Councils and Camps Manager which showing the responsibilities for all involved parties.The services will cover the life-saving WASH services in IDP's camps to deliver comprehensive WASH Package for the targeted IDP's along with implementing sustainable solution to access the drinking water and also proper disposal for the wastewater along with rainwater drainage to avoid the flood. In addition deliver hygiene supplies either through in-kind or restricted conditional cash voucher to be distributed for the targeted beneficiaries. Moreover, implementing SWM and vector control related activities to enhance public health and environmental hygiene.on the other hand, rehabilitation for water and sanitation systems inside the targeted communities in order to ensure access for safe drinking water and proper disposal for wastewater along with rainwater. SWM and Cost Recovery Mechanism will be implemented too in the targeted communities in order to ensure sustainable access for the beneficiaries. Ensuring the connectivity of the other facilities inside the targeted communities to the WASH services will be considered in the project implementation.Moreover, the activities will be implemented taking into consideration the WASH Cluster Guidelines and recommendation in order to ensure the impacts and appropriateness of the intervention.The vector control activity which will take place in both setting, will be coordinated with MENTOR initiative in order to avoid overlap and also to ensure the impact for the intervention specially in prevention of sand fly.

Assumptions:Main assumptions are that security situation will be overall stable in targeted areas and there won’t be any deterioration in security environment which will prevent implementation and water management groups will remain committed and provide support throughout the life of the project.Risks:Main risks are related to the security and access which might affect the transportation of materials and movement of project staff in the operations locations such as:Delays in equipment delivery due to restrictions in borders: Likelihood: Medium. Impact: MediumViolet has wide out reach for local suppliers who can provide the required equipment's and works inside Syria locally. also Violet has wide network of pre-qualified local contractors by implementing several projects in such relation.Armed groups trying to get influence in the project: Likelihood: Medium. Impact: HighViolet maintain solid engagement with local councils and communities in addition of sign MoU from the beginning of project specify the roles and responsibilities of Violet and local authorities and make clear that humanitarian activities have to be protected from any non-humanitarian usage. Violet employs the use of local, low profile vehicles and employs staff from the targeted location of all programs related activities in project sites.Airstrikes and bumping: Likelihood: Medium. Impact: HighViolet avoid keeping equipment's in stores for a long time and avoid gathering around the project location for people who are not related to the site. Keep contact with security officers in OCHA and INSO to get the latest updates about the working area. The team based in Gaziantep regularly communicates with community leaders to assess security risk
