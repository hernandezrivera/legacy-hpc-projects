Project Summary

This project aims to provide quality protection to children through prevention and response mechanisms to cases of abuse, exploitation, violence and neglect. It is also a Project with a GBV component which, in view of the cultural and social realities of the Far North region, plans to carry out constant advocacy to promote children’s rights, especially those of young girls, who are mostly confronted to GBV and need to be supported with quality prevention and response services.
Therefore, project targets both children (0-17years) and young people (18-24 years) whether they are refugees, internally displaced persons, returnees or belonging to the vulnerable host community.
With the main objective of improving the protection of girls, boys, young women and increasing access to prevention and response services against Violence, abuse, exploitation and neglect, this project aims to reach a total of 10,000 people: 4800 children (0-17years) including 2400 boys and 2400 girls, 3200 young people (18-24 years); including 1600 girls and 1600 boys and 2000 caregivers (1000 men, 1000 women). The revitalization of community child protection mechanisms will be guaranteed through this project, within the three-target divisions in this region (Mayo Tsanaga, Mayo Sava and Logone et Chari). This will be achieved in close collaboration with community-based groups, traditional, religious, and state sector leaders in order to strengthen the capacities of the latter and ultimately improve the functioning of the child protection system in this region, that has also been facing the social and economic consequences of the covid-19 pandemic .
In addition, through the distribution of cash to beneficiaries and the guarantee of significant access to the various services available, especially in this Covid-19 pandemic context. In this regard, this project aims to ensure transversal protection and strengthen through the contextualization and implementation of child protection standards, the resilience of target populations and children, where applicable.
Finally, the project will make a significant contribution to ensuring the recognition and enhancement of children's rights, in particular those of young girls, so that they can achieve their full potential.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In 2020, Cameroon remained the most affected country by the conflict in the Lake Chad Basin, after Nigeria. The Far North region has seen a sharp upsurge in violence. Ongoing hostilities have forcibly displaced a total of 559,699 individuals (114,496 Nigerian refugees, 321,886 internally displaced people, and 123,489 IDP returnees) and continue to push more people to flee their villages. Among the forcibly displaced population, 64% are children calling for a dedicated protection response. The number of IDPs increased from 297,000 at the end 2019 to 321,000 as of September 2020.The affected population is regularly subjected to armed attacks, kidnappings, including kidnapping of children, looting and destruction of property and infrastructure. From January to October 2019, a total of 2,622 GBV cases were reported to GBVIMS. The data analyzed revealed significant gaps in the provision of essential GBV services. Globally, children are mostly exposed to violence, abuse, exploitations, neglects and other kinds of security. Among them are many vulnerable children in need of specific humanitarian assistance.In order to better address these different protection needs and to reached project main results, specific activities will be carried out, based on the indicators identified below. Activities will thus be implemented with the aim of reducing the impact of the crisis and of displacement on the population and children in particular, but also to help the communities to be more resilient for the future. All project activities will be focused on prevention and response to cases of abuse, exploitation, violence, neglect identified in the different communities and will be implemented so as to ensure the mainstreaming protection and thus guarantee significant access to the various services available to all people. In full compliance with the barrier measures to fight the COVID-19, prevention activities will revolve around:Door to door awareness raising, educational talks, life skills sessions, positive parenting education sessions, on child protection and GBV issues affecting children and young people. These will aim to inform the community about the rights of children and the various risks to which they are exposed within their communities. If parents and children become aware of harmful cultural practices, of their role and responsibility and above all of the place that each occupies within the family or the community, then the risks of abuse, of violence, exploitation and neglect will be reduced. Awareness-raising on issues relating to child labor and the risk associated with the remands of explosives of war will be carried out, and ad hoc support services will be made available to communities for cases of child victims of the worst form of child labor. revitalizing protection mechanisms within different communities, Training of RECOPE, protection committees, community-based groups, to work in a participatory manner so that community mechanisms play the roles of prevention and response to protection risks. To this end, functional alert systems will be set up within the various communities in order to be able to prevent the protection risks to which children are exposed, through community mechanisms.Advocacies with state institutions,Capacity building.As a response A case management system will be set up for a holistic management of cases of children victims of violence, abuse and exploitation child marriage on children (Identify, document, place in host families and refer UA/SC if necessary)Train the feedback and backup focal points to ensure the collection a of community feedback. Listening and support units will be set up, for GBV cases and victims of trauma.Recreational KITS,(development, dignity, NFI/FI) will be provided to children and families with urgent need. For the coordination between CP and GBV sub sectors, data related to GBV will be provided to the GBV sub cluster, in order to avoid duplication with the child protection.
