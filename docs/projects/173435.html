Project Summary

Camp No 5 and Camp 4 Extension for Rohingya Refugees is situated in Ukhiya Sub-district of Cox's Bazar District, Bangladesh. As of 31 October 2020, 24463 Rohingya Refugees live in 05 Blocks of Camp-5, out of which 51.54% are female (Ref from MSNA). As per CwC JPR 21 Targeted, 20, 9566 Rohingya (PIN) lives in camp 4 Extension out of which 52.70% are female. As the outcome of CwC agencies massive interventions, It is evident from Joint Multi Sector Needs Assessment (J-MSNA) that since COVID 19, households reported to get high level of information on the issues related to COVID 19, (99 % of households received information on precautionary message, 95% on points of contact and 94 % about symptoms / vulnerable groups). Scenario on cyclone is also almost similar. But, despite of receiving the adequate information does not reelect in our behaviors. But, this access to information provision has to be continued as access to information requires time to bring necessary behavioral change through adoption and sustainable. To materialize the information into practice and address the gaps in needs, still there is need for continuation of information provision through Community Resources, Community Engagement, Information and feedback service center and capacity development.
The project will bring the information provision bringing Information Centers at Blocks of targeted camps and wards in Union of Ratna Palang. These learning gaps will be minimized through using audio visual instrument and interactive tools like Street Drama (using both local language and dialect as to address AAP). Community engagement will include Block management Committee, Community leaders, Imams, Majhees, Women, Youths and Persons with disabilities (considering ‘gender & other’ needs, role & power dynamics as well as Affected People’s influence) and be sensitized to oversee the BCC among the community. They can play significant role in Prevention and Response to COVID-19 and other humanitarian concerns utilizing their influence and networks. Considering their potentials. The project will enhance their capacity through capacity building initiative.
The project will also be implemented in the host community of Ratna Palang Union of Ukhiya Upazila where 22524 people out of which 50.42% female lives at risk as they are not practice the learning of information like wearing mask. Maintaining social distance and lack of adequate information center or hubs. To address the problem. We have been planning to implement this one year Project. Purpose of the Project is to protection of 39,092 people’s in needs ( 19,911 persons, 100% of Camp -5 , 5,760 persons , 100% of Camp 4 Extension, and 13,421 persons, 33.33% ; irrespective of age and sex, of Ratna Palang Union) from second wave of COVID -19 and other humanitarian concerns in an environment-friendly way. Considering (gender & other) needs, role & power dynamics, Target beneficiaries including key actors of Community Engagement initiatives; will be proportionate to Sex (50 %+) and age, 10% will be PWDs and 10% will be ethnic minority. Activities to be performed under 3 different objectives include Community Engagement, Awareness raising, Capacity Enhancement and Setup and Functioning of Block Level and UP Ward level Information Hubs. Required Manpower’s like Team Leader as well as Community Volunteer in Each of the 05 Blocks as well as 3 Wards of Ratna Palang Union will be assigned in the Project. Numbers of beneficiaries and budget will be proportionate to Sex. Similarly age will be considered in targeting beneficiaries. Organization Management policy, Gender policy (that address gender needs), Child Protection Policy (that protects children irrespective of age, sex & disability) and Financial Policies will be followed in implementation of the Project. Considering DRR, the project will have provision of 5% contingency on operational costs.

1 - Identified Needs

Influx 2017 of Rohingya refugees brought massive consequence to the life and livelihood of both the refugee and host community in Bangladesh that is reinforced by outbreak of corona virus in March 2020. To address the crisis humanitarian actors have been implementing massive response program in Camps as well as (recently) in host community where CwC agencies have been playing significant roles providing lifesaving messages. It is evident from Joint Multi Sector Needs Assessment (J-MSNA) that since COVID 19, households reported to get high level of information on the issues related to COVID 19, (99 % of households received information on precautionary message, 95% on points of contact and 94 % about symptoms / vulnerable groups). Scenario on cyclone is also almost similar. But, despite of receiving the adequate information does not reelect in our behaviors. We should continue providing the information provision to bring necessary change in behaviors of both the refugees and host communities that need times to for adoption. Otherwise result of CwCs efforts will be deemed keeping the target audience at risk. For that numbers of information Centre has to be setup at door step, be increased in numbers and quality. It is recommended to setup information center in block levels of Camps and in wards of the concerned Union so that people can get continuous information from door steps. Multi-level community : religious leaders, youths, women, village police, UP leaders, Majhees- most trusted actor (RGA) needed to be engaged and their capacity should be enhancement who will act as they act as key informants and massive influence on the households. J-MSNA identified some findings as outlined below that has to be addressed:In Refugee Community,• Language is barrier for refugee that can be mitigated by engaging community leaders.• Poor mobile connection prevent people from receiving information as well as reporting problem (J-MSNA) that could be mitigated through community engagement and awareness like street drama.• Illiterate household faces problems filing written Complain to CIC where Information Centre at Block could minimize the gaps. In host community, Peoples having no mobile or elderly people have problems receiving information that could be addressed engaging household heads, community leaders Ward and Union Based Information Centre engaging UP members, Village police, IPC by volunteers.AAP : There is still significant gaps in Accountability to Affected Population and Priority needs., particularly in Shelter Materials, Remote learning, Food Assistance , Site Management and Nutrition (% reporting assistance / services that did not well : (25-33% since COVID 19, 11-17% before Covid 19). Decentralized Information Provision and Community engagement could minimize the gaps. Information provision in host community needs further improvement. Information on Remote Education (2%), Nutrition (6%) and Protection (7%) remains greatest concern. Considering comparatively less funding and presence in host community, there is a need for exploring and using some special avenues for information sharing that are context appropriate and cost effective. Use of Satellite Cable Network, local elected bodies for mass awareness raising should be extended. Localization is highly needed by increasingly engaging local actors, developing capacity of government officials and national humanitarian staff. Research, increasing information provision particularly at ward level of Union Parishad and dispelling rumors are crucial for ensuring communal cohesion. The feedback system in host community needs to be boosted up as 7% people found difficulty to give feedback and 69% didn’t give any feedback. Contextualization of communication tools, CwC and AAP mechanisms needs to be continued.
