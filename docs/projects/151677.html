Project Summary

The objective of this project is to provide a humanitarian assistance for immigrants, IDPs and host-communities.
This project aims to improve sanitation in a number of detention centers and 10 schools in Misurata and 5 schools in Sirte .
The number of beneficiaries is 5,000 people, including 1000 irregular migration and 4,000 students
DETENTION CENTER / estimeted women 400 and 600 ( boys &girls )
SCHOOLS / estimeted 2,500 girls and 1,500 boys.

Needs

For decent living in detention centers, sanitation services must be provided (toilets, washing water, drinking water, and personal hygiene items for women and children).In schools, in order for the student to continue the study, the appropriate bathrooms must be kept that protect the child's safety and dignity. Also, there is a need for washing water and drinking water.Target number 5000 person , children 70% from total , 15% wamen and 15 % Men, Children Ages 05-14 years boys and girls

Activities

Activities in this project focus on the maintenance and rehabilitation of the bathrooms, the connection of the washing water network and the provision of hygiene kits, especially for children.This project seeks to preserve and protect the dignity of beneficiaries without discrimination (not on the basis of sex or color).The project will include a health awareness campaign.Follow-up will be through frequent visits and reports
