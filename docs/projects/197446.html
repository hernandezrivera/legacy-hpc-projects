Project Summary

This project is a continuation of existing education and protection activities by NDYD organization as well as scaling up education and protection activities in additional schools and locations. the project will contribute to HRP/ASO 1. (Helping to create a safer and more secure environment for children). This initiative will focus on children under the age of 18 in and out of school by providing an integrated set of services that fulfill the needs of the most vulnerable group of beneficiaries in the education sector while ensuring protection services. The project will support (3) schools and a total of 5412 beneficiaries, 1365 men, 1427women, and 2620 (5/17) children. 51% are women and girls, and 2,620 are children. 48 children have disabilities or special needs. 52% of the beneficiaries are adults: teachers, parents, and the community.
The project's main goal is to integrate protection into education to provide a safer environment for children and youth.
NDYD organization will implement the project activities in the following target schools in Idleb /Jisr Ash- Shughur district/Badama sub-district include:
1) Khirbet Al Jouz school 1+2 in Khirbet AlJouz village
2) Al Shatouria school 1+2 in Alshatouria Village.
3) Mazeen school 1+2 in Aljabal camp, Khirbet AlJouz random camps.
This project seeks to scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education for school-aged girls and boys between ages 5-17 in a safe environment by providing safe and secure access for girls to the school, with the rehabilitation of 61 classes in 3 schools. The project ensures safe, child-friendly, and accessibility for children of all needs and with disabilities.
The recruitment for the remaining school will be through face-to-face interviews and the certificates will be verified with the education directorate.
Formal education: The project activities are to support 1920 (5-17) children in formal education (951 boys/ 969 girls) in the education classes from 1 to 9 level. Moreover, it aims to provide those children with education supplies and learning materials in the classes and provide WASH services in the schools to prevent the infection of Cholera among the students by supplying the schools with clean water and providing hygiene kits to the schools.
Nonformal education: Moreover, the project aims to enable 700 school-age children (7/18) (390 girls/ 310 boys) to return back to school by supporting nonformal education for those children as the following:
(80 girls/ 70 boys) ALP activities.
(250 girls/ 200 boys) Remedial education.
(60 Girls/ 40 boys) Catch up on classes.
Training: Providing training for 86 educational staff who will be trained on protection and education activities (such as accelerated learning programs (ALP), those recommended by (INEE), nonviolent communication, etc.). NDYD organization will also educate parents and the community on CP concerns at parent meetings at school.
Providing incentives and running costs: This project will cover the incentives for teachers at all 3 schools. The project will distribute teacher and student kits, with all needed stationery and teaching tools, and specified by age and gender. Children with low literacy and numeracy will be provided with catch-up classes in the summer.
NDYD organization will perform rehabilitation projects for classes according to assessments
Raising awareness: Psychosocial support (PSS) will be provided for children and teachers, run by case management with referrals to other relevant services.
Back to school, (BTS) campaigns will be conducted for one month in the summer to encourage the value of education and children to school.
Moreover, N.D.Y.D will conduct Cholera raising awareness sessions for the children and in the parents' meetings to prevent cholera infection among the children and will provide posters and learning materials that were distributed by WHO in the three schools.

3 - Needs

According to the HUMANITARIAN NEEDS OVERVIEW 2023 issued by For 2023, the following are planned Joint Education Needs Assessments (JENA) to be conducted by Education across the three hubs. 6.9M People in Need of education services, 1.060.750 out of them are in Idleb Governorate NDYD organization conducted need assessment that covers Badama subdistrict/ Khirbet AlJoz,and Al Shatouria village to identify the education needs assessment adopted a purposive sampling method and consulted selected community leaders/representatives, school principals, as well as teachers. For these schools, NDYD conducted a baseline assessment using the following methods: FGDs, HHs survey, KII interviews. the result of the need assessment is as the following: there are 49.000 IDPs &Host lives in Khirbet AlJoz and Shatouria. There are 21.000 school-age children ( 12000 girls/ 9000 boys). According to the need assessment results, not all of the children are going to school where about 12.000 ( 6500 girls/ 5500 boys) are out of school. Most of the out-of-school children are children who go to work due to the following reasons: 1- 30% said that because of economic needs where boys work to help their families.2- 38% of families don't have the ability to send their boys to school3- 20 of the girls didn’t go to school because the schools are so far for them.4- 14% because there are no girls' schools for the high school level in the area so girls can't complete their education.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

N.D.Y.D is taking the responsibility to respond to any case of SEA from any volunteer that works with Nimat Relief towards any of the vulnerable people in the project locations. Moreover, Nimat Relief is focusing on the principle (do no harm) in the affected community and takes into consideration community needs and raising awareness about SEA cases by providing access to complaints about any SEA cases by providing hotline and compliant boxes in clear places as well as compliant training for the beneficiaries. Moreover, Nimat Relief in all the raising awareness sessions and meeting with the beneficiaries emphasizes that all the services provided by Nimat Relief is free and encourages the people to inform about any money or SEA cases that the beneficiaries might notice. the risk of SEA ( Education): the risk of SEA might take place in the schools by any of the education personnel.Probability: law Impact: High Mitigation measures:1- Providing training on PSEA for the educational personnel and teacher.2- provide brochures and posters about the compliance mechanism in schools in easy-seeing places. 3- NDYD also will provide its staff with uniform clothing such as vests and IDs that will show that they are working for NDYD organization. 4-NDYD is working to be a member of the PSEA network so when it comes to sensitive complaints, they are referred to PSEA focal point persons, which are the HR manager and M&E manager, therefore they are treated in very high confidentiality.
