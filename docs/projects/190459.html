Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to prevent and respond to CP/GBV by providing capacity building training for service providers, strengthen community based structure/government structure on CP/GBV who are working in to ensure the communities can forestall new and increased child protection risks and GBV cases through different activities.
The aim is thus, increase the awareness of the community members and children to identify, report and respond on child protection and GBV risks/issues , to strength Community-based mechanisms to address risk mitigation, prevention, and response intervention to reduce CP/GBV cases, strengthening case management system to create timely access to community-based and multisector services through referral systems for unaccompanied, separated and other vulnerable children at-risk of abuse, neglect, exploitation, and violence, provide psychosocial support to victims and have access to age, gender and culturally appropriate psychosocial support services linked to multi-sectoral services, Children, girls, and women who are at risk and victims of GBV supported with prevention and response services and Children and adults have got access to a safe and accessible channel to report sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA).
The planned protection activities will be contributing to ensuring provision of safe, accessible and inclusive protection services for crises affected people, especially vulnerable groups such as women, children and people with disability/ reduced mobility are identified, referred, and addressed with functional complaints and feedback mechanisms in place. ASDEPO is identify existing protection risks and protection monitoring, GBV/CP identification and case management, Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS), GBV risk mitigation and Conduct raising awareness activities on CP/GBV and PSEA in the targeted woredas.
In order to strengthen humanitarian actors on protection mainstreaming a capacity building training will be provided on basic protection principles and the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) the CP/GBV case management training for different service providers will be given and strengthening referral service for those who needs multi-sectoral needs. This project planned to be implement in three different regions (Amhara- Werebabu- Ambasel & Teweldhure), Tigray- (Adwa & Rama) and Afar ( Gelalu, Haruka and Dulecha woreda).
This proposed project will be benefited 60,000 women, men, girls, pregnant women of IDP's and Host communities. ASDEPO has a very good reputation with the local government as well as strong presence in all three regions in general and in the targeted Zones in particular. Currently, ASDEPO is implementing CP/GBV projects in this specific woredas.

1. - Projects activities

The project aim to improve the availability of access to and quality of protection and response services for children at risk of, or survivors of violence including harmful practices, exploitation, family separation, in need of case management and psychosocial support services. The activities ensuring the provision of safe, accessible and inclusive protection services for crises affected people, especially vulnerable groups such as women, children and people with disability/ reduced mobility are identified and referred/addressed. The project will going to implement this activities;-  GBV/CP identification and case management;  Provide positive parenting skills training for selected parents/caregivers  FTR and family-based care arrangement  Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS);  CP/GBV risk mitigation and awareness;  GBV-Dignity kits distribution;  Establishing key community based structures such as Child Protection Committees (CPC) and school and community Child rights clubs (CRC) to identify and address protection risks of children including UASC  In order to strengthen humanitarian actors on protection mainstreaming a capacity building training will be provided on basic protection principles and the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) the service mapping will be conducted for the referral service for those who needs multi-sectoral needs. The recruited social workers will focus on participate in case identification and management, educating IDPs and host communities on GBV/CP concern mitigation, prevention and response  CP/GBV awareness-raising and risk mitigation activities

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

Communities are relevant to both GBV and child protection, both as sources of threats but also as having a role in finding solutions to protection issues. To further ensure accountability of the project and project staff, a feedback mechanism will be put in place (suggestion box, suggestion registration book and hotline calls, as well as case, can report anonymously. This will primarily consist of community meetings, segregated by sex and age and with an emphasis on persons with specific vulnerabilities such as persons with disabilities, in which beneficiaries are encouraged to give feedback or voice complaints/in person at the field office. This method is proposed given the low level of literacy which rules out written feedback. Moreover, ASDEPO M&E staff, not involved in project delivery, will also be involved, so beneficiaries feel confident to report problems. Feedback collected from these meetings will be reviewed by program staff, who will adjust interventions accordingly, where appropriate. Notes will be taken, and actions taken reported back to the next community meeting.
