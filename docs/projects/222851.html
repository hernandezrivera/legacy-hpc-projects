Project Summary

Boys and girls continue to face various risks such as violence, exploitation, abuse, and neglect and are in acute need of child protection, though sustainable community based protection networks and accountable justice systems. Protracted conflict compounded by current flooding extremes which has affected at least 1.4 million people across 43 counties and influx of people displaced by Sudan crisis; coupled with prevalent cyclical waves of inter/ intra communal violence, revenge killings, children and women abduction including forceful recruitments and cattle related fighting continue to impact vulnerable populations, particularly among girls and boys who have witnessed various atrocities and at risk of post-traumatic stress disorder, depression and anxiety disorders. This has been coupled with loss of friends and family members further affecting mental health and emotional development of children.
Recent IPC November 2024 project at least 7.69 million people including 2.1 million children will suffer from acute malnutrition through June 2025.This coupled with deteriorating macro-economic conditions and high cost of food and other essential commodities has further contributed to negative coping strategies including child labour and early and forced marriages (UNICEF,2024; IPC November 2024) in addition to increasing cases of both forceful and voluntary recruitment of children into armed groups (UNMISS, 2024). Most vulnerable and displaced populations’ needs are further worsened by protection concerns including GBV and sexual exploitation and abuse, predominantly among girls and women with an uptick incases of GBV cases; which are often under-reported due to stigma and lack of legal assistance.
Women and girls continue to face increased risks of harassment, assault and sexual violence, early marriages with estimated that 52 percent of girls are married between the age of 15 and 18 (Child Rights Coalition, 2016), poor rule of law which continues to prevent perpetrators from being held accountable for violations. Proposed project which will target 7,700 persons/ beneficiaries comprised of 350 men, 350 women, 3500 boys and 3500 girls including people with special needs/ disabilities. Proposed initiatives, which in line key Child Protection Area of Responsibility objectives that will ensure vulnerable and at risk girls, boys, men and women including children with disabilities have improved and sustainable access to quality lifesaving child protection; with reduced suffering of boys , girls ,women and men including children with disabilities who are at risk or experience violence ,abuse, exploitation and neglect including GBV through provision services.
Proposed action will respond to the child protection needs via child rights and child protection awareness, including strengthened psychosocial support, case management and strengthening community based child protection networks including families to prevent and respond to violence against children and other protection concerns including recruitment and use of children in armed forces and groups, family tracing and re-unification, child protection response monitoring, support and referrals among others.. The project will ensure quality programming, inter-cluster synergies, Accountability to Affected Populations, regular context analysis and advocacy on environmental conservation, ensuring conflict sensitive programming by upholding Do No Harm ethos.

2. Project activities

Comprehensive case management targeting 500 boys and girls including material and cash support. Conduct mapping and establishment of functional / existing referral mechanisms among various multi sectoral platforms at County level. Provide focused and non-focused Mental Health and Psycho-social support and other related services targeting 3500 girls and 3500 boys including those with disabilities. Carry out awareness raising sessions on child protection and psychosocial support including those with disabilities among targeted 7,700. Monthly protection monitoring, assessment, information sharing / reporting, awareness raising & mainstreaming in the targeted counties. Capacity building of 100 women and men (community leaders or humanitarian and government social welfare staff) on Child Protection in Emergencies (CPiE) and Gender Based Violence.
