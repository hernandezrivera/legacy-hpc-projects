Project Summary

This project aimed to improve access to livelihood services targeted directly 400 beneficiaries ( 60% IDPs- 40% Returnees ) in need, by promoting their livelihood options through increase accsess to agricultural production, facilitate accsess to business and provision of vocational Training/Technical skills .
Project foccsing :
Agriculture Crops Production: Traditional and Rain Fed targeted (240 of IDPs)the main types of crops proposed include Sorghum , Sesaame, Vegetables and Watermelons ,areas of support include agricultural extention and assets to increase productivity.
-Small Businesses and Vocational Training: provision of employment-oriented skills trainings ,targeted (160 of Returnees) the opportunities include Welding ,Motorbike, Carpentry, Handcrafts ,Donkey cards, Green Mills ,Food processing ,Coffee Shops , Bakeries etc

Needs

Due to conflicts South Kordofan state has been affected by , IDPs, huge pool of unemployment of youth with conflict carring capacity , female headed households who suffer from acute poverty since their husbands have either been killed in war or have migrated to urban areas in search of work, in addition there is influx of refugees from South Sudan due to internal conflicts there , also influx of returnees from SPLM/N areas due to drought and lack of food , further more the conflicts between Sudan and South Sudan affected nomadic routes thus putting additional pressure on natural resources, all this circumstances in the lights of the state limited economic and livelihood opportunities , will further aggravates the situation. Based on the experiences of previous and ongoing projects implemented by IPDO in South Kordofan, plus Project evaluation assessment, there is a need to strengthen the returnees and IDPs’ livelihoods opportunities for the promotion of self-reliance and self-employment. The evaluation used qualitative methods triangulated by using focus group discussions (FGD), in-depth interviews (IDI), consultative meetings, review of literature, monitoring data, baselines and end lines and a short questionnaire. The assessments indicated that food security situation in this state is not good in terms of food availability and accessibility as the old case load have to share the food rations among them rendering both foods insecure. Food accessibility is a challenge for all IDPs and returnees due to lack of income generating activities and due to their poverty and vulnerability resulting from fleeing with no assets, food, money or livestock. Also the assessment indicates high unemployment among the affected population with majority of them relying on humanitarian assistance.IPDO has an ongoing agriculture project in Abu karshola-alabasiya, targeted returnees, and IDPs. During The course of project the project team conducted an assessment and home visits for community mobilization and sensitization which indicates that the livelihood opportunities remines big concerns for the targeted groups due to limitted resoures and population infulation. In addition IPDo's project is not cover the big number of people in needs, so that why IPDO look at this area as a complementray for it's ongoing intervention. In the other hand IPDO implementing Water, sanitation and hygiene activities in Gadir and Lerri localities where implemented at family level through home visits for IDPs and Returnees sites where the livelihood gaps are obviously noticed, as most of IDPs do not have their own lands as their reported and they have an expectation in humanitarian actors to help them to fullfill their needs.
