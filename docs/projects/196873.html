Project Summary

This project aim to increase access to quality education services for the most disadvantaged and vulnerable children and adolescents .The intervention aim to mitigate the impact of conflict on education service delivery by target a total of 7692 students , 99 % will be children (age 6- 13), 1% will be adolescents . Both the assessment needs and the severity ranking based on vulnerability will be used to identify needs and priorities to determine targets at a locality level. The following activities will be conducted; activity 1 : establishment of learning spaces (rehabilitation 5 schools 3 in ND(Kabkabyia, Al Fasher , Al Salam ) and 2 in BN (Damazine and Al Tadamon ) activity 2 construction 5 classroom one per each area by semi -permanent materials) ; activity 3: provide recreational for school children formation of 5 students club) ; activity 4: provision of 7692 school learning supplies for targeted schools based on the need assessment ; activity 4 : promoting community and children’ participation in CP issues (events) activity5: provision of dignity kits for 7692 students, activity 6: construction of 5 latrines per each school , provision of 7692 students` school uniform, activity 7 : provision of 5 drinking fresh water harvest/tank one per each targeted , activity 8 provision of 4 e-learning centres one per each school . The indicators were; children aged between 6 and 13 years have access to basic education or Accelerated Learning Programmed ,)the % of non functional or closed schools; and number /percentage of children with access to teaching and learning materials, the quality of safe access to education for children in these locations. The selection criteria will be bases on vulnerability. The project will coordinate closely with the Ministry of Education to improve the quality of life skills training for girls and boys , including the disable, to strengthen social integration and protection through formation of strong mechanism such as steering committees, community leaders, parents association, social workers this guarantee local community ownership which in turn will ensure project effectiveness, efficiency and sustainability . Furthermore, the implementation will be in partnership between Al Rohama and Gayat for peace and development with clear role and responsibilities determine their commitment and quality assurance , both organizations have offices will equipped with professional staff in leadership and commitment to del0verly timely and high quality performance , Al have more than ten years experiences in provision of school facilities , while Gayat in addition to unique experiences in school programs has experienced in e-learning specially with droup out school children in South Kordofan and Darusalam Jabell Awlia which will guarantees the safe implementation of the project.

1 - Needs

Based on Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2022, Sudan host large number of vulnerable populations including those affected by natural disasters, armed conflict and drought, some two millions are still displaced of which 75% are women and children. Education is one of the gap services for these affected population mainly in, North Darfur(ND) in Dar El Salam, and Blue Nile (BN)(Tadamon & Damazine) ,these localities considered the most accessible areas, the need for quality education is increasing across these areas with humanitarian needs. Children of displaced communities and refugees are adding pressure to existing education facilities which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources. Education remains challenges where enrollment for basic education stands at 40 % average. Moreover, as macro-economic challenges impact Sudanese people’s lives nowadays, following the decrease in purchasing power let many households unable to cope with schooling costs. As a result, children are at risk in term of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor.
