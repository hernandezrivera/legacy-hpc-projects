Project Summary

The proposed project will focus on construction/ rehabilitation of Clean Drinking Water Supply schemes, Sanitation and Health Hygiene Education in 12 villages of Tehsil Ladha SWA. The interventions are also planned to be implemented in 1 selected school identified through cluster.
Two (2) damaged government motorized water supply infrastructure will be rehabilitated where borehole are already available and pumping system will be needed to rehabilitate, due to non-availability of electricity after the conflict DC motors with solar power energy generating systems will be installed where the BOQs and specifications are developed as per well depths/water table and required discharge, two partially damaged tanks and one fully damaged tank will be repaired/reconstructed and damaged parts of supply line will be rehabilitated.
(3) Community owned gravity based water supply schemes will be rehabilitated/constructed that included rehabilitation intake chamber, distribution tank, valve chamber and damaged parts of main and distribution lines. After completion of Water supply schemes PVDP will provide operation and maintenance kits and will train Village WASH committees and School teachers focal persons on operation and Maintenance of Water Supply schemes. 2 Trainings will be conducted while 4 O&M kits will be provided. Unavailability of proper water storage facility is the major issue of Household PVDP will provide two, (20 litre) jerry can to extremely vulnerable household. In this way 20% Household (500) will be benefited. 10 Pre/10 Post water quality tests will be carried out to know the level of contamination in water.
PVDP will construct construct/rehabilitate disability inclusive twins latrine structure separately for male & female with hand washing facility. In addition, 1 twin Latrine structure along with hand washing facilities will be constructed in 1 cluster preferred school .Disability chairs will also be provided in school based on the real time need assessment. Solid Waste Management is also an important component of Sanitation. PVDP will provide 2 solid waste wheeler school for disposal of solid waste easily and after construction/rehabilitation of latrines at institutes, PVDP will further provide 2 latrine cleaning kits for operation and maintenance of latrines. PVDP will use strong mobilization strategies of Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) and Triggering techniques in order to ensure the construction of latrines through self-help basis under community led models. In this way PVDP will ensure construction of 1000 latrines through community using component sharing modalities after identification of 20% extremely vulnerable households. They will further be facilitated by providing them sanitation kits while construction.
80 sessions on improved hygiene practice are proposed in the project. Field teams will ensure the participation of 20-25 participants (on average) in each session. Separate sessions for men and women will be organised owing to cultural values. Each participant will be provided with IEC material and light refreshment. Pre/Post-KAP survey will be carried out in second week of the project start date. This will gauge the knowledge and practices of the local communities at the start of the project activities. Similarly, towards the end of the project completion a Post-KAP survey will also be carried out. The beneficiaries will be assessed on the same parameters as were used during Pre-KAP. Questions related to (developed indicators) will be evaluated to see if the project was successful as compared to the baseline findings.

Needs

PVDP has carried out rapid WASH needs assessment in Tehsil Ladha using its local staff was previously on board with PVDP in PHPF funded project in 2016. The assessment team has also included female in order to capture the specific needs of women and girls. The detail assessment report is uploaded under document section for your reference. Assessment methodology was mainly based on validation of secondary information being taken from UN Mission and other stakeholders assessment reports through Key Informant Interviews(KIIs) and Focus Groups discussion (FGDs) separately with men/women, school teachers, Maliks, Religious and faith leaders. The scope of the study engulfs the information and data collection related to returnee basic humanitarian needs and issues faced by them especially women and children in return communities of Tehsil Ladha. Major findings of KIIs and FGDs related to clean drinking water, Sanitation and HHE are as follow; 1. Clean Drinking Water: - 59% population of the area is mostly dependent on unhygienic and unprotected water sources (canals, rivers, open wells, ponds, springs), which is not sufficient and result in water borne diseases (Diarrhea, Malaria, Typhoid, Eye infection, and hepatitis)- 95% populations don’t have access to sufficient water.- Women and Children are mostly responsible to fetch water from the source with an average distance of 800-900 meters.- No proper water storage facility is in place, 64% population is using jerry cans, 36% are using plastic buckets.- 60% population use water purification methods to treat water. According to the FGDs and semi-structure interview findings sources of water particularly springs are not protected properly and needs to be protected from animal and human excreta and other dust particles. Due to utilization of contaminated water of the springs, diarrhea and malaria is very common among the local community particularly in children and women. Besides cement water storage tanks should be constructed with distribution lines. While pipelines are also damaged from place to place and the supply of water is not proper to the user points. 2. Sanitation:- 61 % population don’t have access to latrines facility, while among 36% population 23% population ,who have access to latrine facility at their HHs level but still practicing open defecation due to lack of awareness and social norms. - 53 % population both male and female use to go for open defection away from their houses to the open fields, while covering the distance with an average of 80 meter due to which female segment of the society are at risk to abuse, violation, Exploitation, Harassment ,wild animals and other violent crimes. - 32% population uses pour flush with open drains and traditional pit latrines According to the data analysis only 39% have access to latrine facility, 36% have composting latrine, 29% have pit latrine with slabs. 3% latrine draining to canals/creek/river and only 20% Households have pour flush latrine. 97% households have constructed their latrine on self-basis while only 3% say that their latrines have been constructed through NGOs. In addition, 70% of the community members still use to go for open defecation early in the morning or late night to the open fields, rocks, bushes, rivers and streams; due to which particularly female and children are at risk to harassment, exploitation and kidnaping, killing and wild animals like dogs, pigs and snakes. 3. Health Hygiene Education: 60% populations don’t have knowledge about critical timing of hand washing and proper hand washing steps and don’t use soap due to lack of awareness and weak financial status. 87% respondents say that they haven’t heard any hygiene message in last 3 months.
