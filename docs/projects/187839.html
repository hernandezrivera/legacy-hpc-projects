Project Summary

The project aims to ensure the survival and to improve the living conditions of IDPs and vulnerable host communities in rural areas in Idlib and Aleppo, Syria
the planned activities are intended to enable internally displaced persons and host communities in the Idlib region, as well as internally displaced persons in informal camps, to survive in dignity and contribute to self-determination. By providing free bread ,and food parcels,

4 - Needs

The current rapidly deteriorating economic situation in Syria, which is particularly suffering from the Covid 19 pandemic, is reflected above all in the strong devaluation of the Syrian pound (SYP). As a result, the purchasing power of people in north-west Syria is also falling rapidly. According to a study from the end of 2020, the cost of living increased by 67% in the period from May to June 2020 alone. Comparable analyzes of the situation in communities in the North West concluded that 71% of households do not have sufficient income to meet their basic needs. This corresponds to an increase of around 25% in the period under study. Around 70% of the households surveyed indicated that food accounted for more than 65% of their spending. About 37% of food-insecure households reported taking on debt, and 57% of food-insecure individuals had adopted negative coping strategies. Nationally, an estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure, and another 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. The situation was further aggravated by floods in the second half of January and the first days of February 2021. These have harmed more than 140,000 IDPs living in camps. Natural disasters and fire disasters continue to impact displaced populations.

5 - Implementation of activities

Activity Distribution of a total of 19,350 food packagesContents: Rice, bulgur, red and brown lintel, sunflower cooking oil, margarine, baking peas, shairya, sugar, zaatar, canned tuna, halwa, tomato paste, tea.Recipients: needy households in 19 communities/informal settlements Period: Beneficiaries will receive a food parcel once a month for a period of 3 months. After this period, new beneficiaries will be selected. A total of three such 3-month cycles are planned per year. In the interest of the enormous needs, the distribution period was chosen to be short, in order to reach as many people in need as possible and to provide some relief.Activity Establishment of a traditional bread oven, production and distribution of at least 500 loaves of bread per day, 6 days a week (pilot project)Recipients: 400 IDP families daily in 2-3 small informal settlements Description:1st phase: construction of a room with a traditional oven for conventional bread-making. Providing all the materials needed for bread making (flour + fuel + yeast + water + salt + salary) for 30 days and distributing the bread packs among the residents of the informal IDP settlement.2nd phase: Reduction of the materials needed for bread production by half. The other half of the ingredients are self-funded by the committee of the informal IDP settlement for 30 days.3rd phase: conclusion of a supply contract with the committee responsible for the bread production for the selected beneficiaries
