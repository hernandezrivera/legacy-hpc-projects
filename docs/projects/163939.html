Project Summary

Considering the high numbers of newly displaced people, there is a significant need for the provision of WASH services to the people who are living in Camps which they do not have these services.
The proposed action will fill the gap of needs to displaced people from south of Idleb to improve safe access to water of sufficient quality and quantity; sanitation; hygiene practices for 12750 (6418 Male and 6332 Female) 2800 HHs conflict-affected people in mentioned camps below in Dana and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts of Idleb governorate as following:
-Idleb, Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Haranbush, (Alsawamii camp)
-Idleb, Harim, Dana, Burj Elnumra, (Alsalam camp)
-Idleb, Harim, Dana, Sarmada, (Omar5 camp)
-Idleb, Harim, Dana, Deir Hassan - Darhashan, (Aljehad camp)
The proposed action will ensure safe and chlorinated water delivery to 12750 IDPs, and improving sanitation and Hygiene promotion services for 4camps, reaching 12750 people who will also benefit from increased access to safe water.
This will be achieved through implementation of essential support for water trucking services in addition to providing, installing, maintenance, cleaning a Prefabricated latrines with desludging a septic as well as hygiene kits distribution to enhance personal hygiene, in addition to Removal of solid waste from 4 camps and self settlement and disposal to the communal landfills.
BRD will ensure an integrated approach covering Water, Sanitation and Hygiene's (WASH) needs and protecting the health of targeted camps.
In addition to mainstreaming the protection principles in all project phases. BRD will provide equitable access for men/women and boys/girls to the same amount of water during the project period, latrines, HKs and promotion activities.
BRD will train some M/F from affected population in those camps as hygiene promoters to take the lead of raising the hygiene awareness of the beneficiaries in those camps.

4 - Needs

Due to the security situation in south of Idleb governorates which more than 520K displaced since beginning of DEC 2019 to JAN 2020.BRD completed a rapid technical and need assessments (from male, female, girls and boys) in (Alsawamii, Omar5, Alsalam and Aljehad)'s Camps in the northern of Idleb (Data collected by BRD from Camps' managers and Survey (need assessment) which the number of population as following:12750(Men:2801, women:2912, boys:3617, Girls:3420)These confirm that, the targeted camps meaning conflict-affected people are forced to resort to unsafe water sources, which are often not safe and costly. Unregulated water trucking is currently the only source of water for the camps in areas targeted by this action. These often-supplying poor quality, untreated water and thereby increasing the risk of waterborne diseases (WBD). Camps not served through water networks are also severely strained by the cost of water, which impacts needs across other sectors. According to Basma’s assessments, families who have no option but to purchase water from commercial water trucks spend on average 25% and in some cases as much as 40% of their income on water.Basma also completed a rapid sanitation assessment in targeted camps and identified a critical need for rehabilitation and installing of latrines systems in all of the targeted camps in addition to desludging a septics in order to improve system efficiency and reduce health and safety risks, which the need assessment showed some camps has latrines but those latrines out of work and the septic tanks are full and no bodies working in those locations, most of those camps have no latrines block.IDPs, especially children in those locations, are forced to walk long distances to bring the water.A large number of IDPs interviewed by the BRD team reported water-related illnesses.Within the overall targeted camps, an urgent need for complementary hygiene practices support was also identified in all camps.

5 - Implementation of activities

Day to day management by BRD team and department, firstly, procurement will be announced through bids.BRD will implement the project in 4 camps for 12 months, at beginning will conduct detailed technical assessment to assess the most current situation because the situation is changing daily (new IDPs)Field staff will train voluntaries to conduct hygiene sessions promotion activities under supervision of BRD staff, BRD will conduct Hygiene awareness raising campaign with distributing the brochures and invite members of camps management, Provide presentations, Drama and street theatre to explain the importance of hygiene.BRD will construct 64 latrines' caravans and dig 64 septic tanks, each caravan has 5 latrines (4 normal and 1 for older and disabilities) with ramp and lights, doors, windows and 3washbasins..etc)BRD will rehabilitate 60 latrines in those camps as needed and water trucking as well.After conducting the hygiene promotion activities BRD will distribute 2800 (2 times) Hygiene kits for IDPs in those camps (Razor, Sanitary pads, Shampoo...etc)BRD will distribute 380 water tanks with 50cm base and 2 taps with installation, BRD will deliver the chlorinated water (70 ML chlorine will be added to each 1000 L) Regular testing will be conducting for water in tanks and trucks..25 L per capita will be delivered on daily basis (25 L*12750=319M3 per day) for drinking and 12750*2l per day for latrines caravans=26M3, BRD will conduct PDM survey to measure the satisfaction and Verification of selected beneficiaries who received mentioned services.BRD will desludging the septic tanks which BRD going to dig (64 septic tanks), 2 time for each septic tanks per month.BRD will Remove of solid waste from 4 camps and self settlement and disposal to the communal landfills in daily basis and cleaning the latrines caravans in daily basis.
