Project Summary

The project will provide integrated emergency assistance and resilience building initiatives through provision of services related to Livelihood and Resilience, WASH, Health & Nutrition, Emergency Shelter and NFIs and Education for Refugees from South Sudan in South Darfur and East Darfur States.
This project is also going to provide The multi-sector Education, protection and WASH assistance targeting CAR refugees. The project proposed will target 14910 refugees from central Africa in South Darfur inside and outside Nyala including Ottach and Derage camp and also Mramoso and Krakada refugee camps in Dimso locality who arrived due to prolong political conflict in the Republic of Central Africa, the situation deteriorated in last year few years , that lead to massive fled of the Sudanese cattlemen’s used to migrate there in most of dry seasons from Oct to June to Republic of Central Africa, Chad, Zaire and Cameroon for raring and return back to Sudan in July up to September each year ,some of central African refugees are in camps living together with IDPs seeking for services as the situation is seem the same the project is seeking to solve three main problems live saving through distribution of IGA items for the most vulnerable and skilful Persons with specific need and accessing education by distribution of school fees and creation of suitable learning environments and improvement of Hygiene & sanitation for refugee in south Darfur .

Needs

The civil conflict in South Sudan coupled with famine has displaced 2.1M within South Sudan and caused 1.8M others to flee into neighboring countries. Refugees from South Sudan are fleeing insecurity and famine as well as people from Republic of Central Africa who arrived due to prolong political conflict in the CAR Republic. East Darfur State (EDS) is home to registered and unregistered refugees.According to UNHCR, the number of South Sudanese Refugees is 844,262 (which is the sum of UNHCR/COR registered population and IPP registered & unregistered figures). Additional sources estimate a total of 1.3 million South Sudanese refugees in Sudan; with total arrivals in 2019 estimated at 6,253, and 3,142 of them arrived only in Feb 2019 however, note that data requires verification. The CAR refugees are estimated at 8,194 (Sudan: Population Dashboard - Refugees and Asylum-seekers, Feb 2019). The majority of the CAR refugees are located at South Darfur and few are at Central Darfur.South Darfur only has 34,655 refugees and asylum-seekers, 26,610 of them are south Sudanese part of them are UNHCR/COR registered, and part of them IPP Registered & unregistered.From IPC classification, food insecurity in Al Buram, Umm Durein, and Heiban localities has alternated between crisis and famine over the last five years. Apart from basic needs such as health and food, refugee children are in need of protection in their families, communities and when interacting with humanitarian services. Children make up over 56% of the refugee population. As refugees struggle to meet their basic needs in an increasingly difficult economic environment, children are at heightened risk of harassment, exploitation, neglect and abuse. Unaccompanied and separated children (UASC) are particularly vulnerable in this regard, as they lack the support structure and protection of their parents. Additionally, refugee youth suffer from lack of educational opportunities or vocational skills training, making it difficult for them to find a livelihood when they become adults. In addition there is a limited amount of recreational or social activities targeting adolescents and youth, having a serious negative their development and psycho social well being. These refugees are coming States with already high health needs. Men and women play different roles in the assessment of the health and nutrition, Education needs, water use in a community, etc, it is important to consult both men and women, seeing that they have different roles in the society. For example Men might have a better knowledge of the use of water for livestock and women often have better knowledge of the amount of water used on a household level. The combined information will give more accurate information on the total need for water for a community. Equally, assessing the need for wood women often have better information on cooking practices, whilst men might have better knowledge of wood used for construction.

Activities

The project activities were designed to achieved different benefits for the different gender and age group based on the different roles they play. Activities for SSR:Health and Nutrition1. Improve health status of the SS Refugees 2. Improve Nutritional well-being of the SS RefugeesWASH1. Improve access to safe and sufficient water supply for SS refugees2. Improve access to safe and dignified sanitation and clean environment free from open defecation for SS refugees3. Improve access to hygiene servicesLivelihoods 1. Strengthen self-reliance through improved access to livelihoods opportunities(Provide protective assets and provide support in kind)Protection 2. Protection of children strengthened • Provide appropriate interim or long term alternative care to UASC• Manage individual case of refugee childrenEducation1. Mainstreaming of refugee Education into national systems (Using the ESSP 2018-2022 as a guiding document) leading to improved access, quality and enrollment of SS refugees.Activities for CAR:The project will also target people from CAR and they will be supported with Education, protection and WASH assistance: 1- To empower CAR refugees through skills training for IGA and self-employment in and around the camps to the extent that they will be able to support themselves. The project will address the needs of the 200 HH most vulnerable refugees through providing various social wellbeing services especially women and youth in South Darfur state.2- Improve access, quality and enrolment of refugee children in the existing schools through supporting them with school fees, text books and improvement of learning environment for 1000 CAR refugee children in South Darfur state.

Impact on environment/natural resources

The project is going to address the environmental issues by considering the local environmental context of the targeted area, and a continuous assessment for potential environmental impacts will be carried throughout the project life. The project is also going to do mitigation measures by compensating for negative impacts (if any), and enhance environmental benefits in the project by practicing some measurements, the followings are part of it: • Replace the use of fired bricks with Soil-Stabilized-Blocks (SSB) or Cement-Stabilized-Blocks (CSB).• Locally available material is recommended provided that it is not impacting negatively on the environment.• Include environmental awareness sessions in projects, and support solid waste management schemes • Reduce packaging material and discourage use of plastic bags to reduce the amount of waste generated. • Promote alternatives such as Fuel-Efficient Stoves to reduce use of wood, solar panels for lighting and pumps. Include fuel efficient cooking techniques (e.g. pre-soaking beans, sheltering cooking fires, etc.) in trainings.• Raise awareness on environmental protection and conservation• Reduce wood consumption by supplying iron pipes and using environmentally friendly material for constructions and rehabilitation; and promoting alternatives such as Fuel Efficient Stoves, and fuel efficient cooking techniques. Consider also alternative energy sources such as solar panels for lighting and pumps.
