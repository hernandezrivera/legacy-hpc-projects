Project Summary

Since 2020, CARE has been supporting internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host community families in Cabo Delgado, through various interventions in WASH Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), shelter, livelihoods, and gender and protection sectors. Currently, in Ancuabe district, CARE is implementing a project funded by the Austrian Development Agency (ADA) focuses on Livelihoods, WASH and Gender and Protection. The project aims to strengthen the resilience of women, men, girls and boys affected by conflict, displacement and natural disasters in Mozambique.
Despite these efforts, reports from the local government and Food Security Cluster data as of October 2024 reveal that over 60,000 individuals remain at risk of food insecurity due to insufficient assistance. This include the vulnerable populations across Ancuabe, Chiure, Montepuez and Metuge districts.
To address this urgent need, CARE is designing a new initiative to support the most vulnerable households in Cabo Delgado affected by conflicts and natural disasters. This initiative will:
1) Provide access to seeds for the main and fresh agricultural season;
2) Provide knowledge and training on climate-smart techniques to improve agricultural production;
3) Support Village Loan Saving Associations (VSLA) with distributing seed grants for savings groups to develop income-generating activities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

CARE has conducted an in-depth Gender Analysis in July 2024 under the ADA project to further understand the gender dynamics in the target communities. The analysis focused on the distinct needs, relationships, roles and responsibilities of all – as well as the protection risks (including GBV) and power dynamics between women, men, boys and girls. CARE will use the findings of this research to plan how best to increase female participation ensuring no harm is caused to the beneficiaries and community at large.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

CARE will facilitate community awareness sessions on gender and protection to promote gender equitable practices and norms, understanding on protection risks within the target communities as a whole. These sessions aim at educating and empowering community members, encouraging participation and promoting social behavior change. These sessions will target women, elders and people with disability.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

CARE recognises the importance of implementing sustainable practices across project implementation to minimise the risks of doing no environmental harm. CARE procedures comply with the INGD Code of Conduct on the environment safeguards for humanitarian action. INGD has provided an orientation session for CARE and 7 local partners to ensure the activities are in line with their regulation. Under the agriculture component, while training the farmers in smart climate agriculture techniques, CARE will include topics related to the production of botanical pesticides to control agricultural pests and preparation of natural fertilizers to fertilize the soil, as to avoid contamination of soil, water and air and, consequently, damaging ecological biodiversity. During the distribution of seeds, CARE will also conduct raising awareness sessions on how to manage the seed packs when treated with chemical pesticides, recommending the beneficiaries to incinerate the packs in a place far from water sources, and when they are untreated packs, CARE will encourage the recycling of packs using traditional techniques and/or reuse them for example in the production of seedlings for transplant crops or ornamental plants.
