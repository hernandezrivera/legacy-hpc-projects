Plan fields

1 - Needs

This project will provide language services, pictorials, and audio recordings, as needed by the Water and Sanitation sector to carry out their activities.

2 - Activities

CLEAR Global/Translators without borders aims to bridge the language gap and address one of the Humanitarian Sector's most prominent blind spots by ensuring no one is left behind because of the language they speak, read or understand. We provide language services, as well as pictorials and audio recordings to ensure people get access to vital information in a language and format they understand.
