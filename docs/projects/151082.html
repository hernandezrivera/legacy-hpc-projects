Project Summary

Specialists of LLC “Demining Solutions” has been developed strategy of the project which will provide for an integrated approach to informing children and young people about the risks from mines and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) based on international standards of IMAS. It should be noticed that the project has already tested in schools of Donetsk oblast during 2015-2017, giving a positive result in raising the safety of children. The program were taken into account and were made appropriate corrections. also. The aim of the project is to help reduce the risk from mines and Explosive Remnants of War to a level where people, especially children and youth, can live safely in the presence of ERW hazards.
All our activities will be coordinate with Mine Action Sub-CLuster (UNDP), Ministry of Defense of Ukraine, regional state administration (Donetsk and Luhansk regions).

Needs

Mine and UXO contamination affects the lives of two million people in the eastern part of Ukraine, putting vital civilian infrastructure facilities at risk, inhibiting freedom of movement, access to livelihoods and limiting recreational activities for children.Ukraine ranks amongst the most severely affected places in the world for casualties as a result of landmines and other explosive remnants of war (ERW). According to the annual report Landmine Monitor 2017, Ukraine placed 5th in the world for a number of casualties behind Afghanistan, Libya, Yemen and Syria.On a monthly basis, the number of civilians injured or killed by these hazards regularly outstrips those as a direct result of an active conflict. Landmines, ERWs and unexploded ordinances (UXOs) were the leading cause of child casualties in 2017-2018, accounting for about two-thirds of all recorded deaths and injuries that left many children with lifelong disabilities.A recent report published by the Geneva International Centre for Humanitarian Demining (GICHD) placed Ukraine as number one in the world, ahead of both Afghanistan and Syria, for anti-vehicle mine casualties.These statistics come as an immediate result of the ongoing conflict in eastern Ukraine, which has seen significant areas of land contaminated by landmines and UXO in both government-controlled (GCA) and non-government controlled areas (NGCA). Concerted efforts have been mounted in GCA to tackle the issue of explosive hazard contamination, both by the Government of Ukraine and the international community, but critical challenges remain. In NGCA, little to no coordinated action has been taken, whilst the situation is understood to be acute.For information:Total (adults and children) civilian casualties resulting from mine-related incidents in the period from 1 Jan until 30 Sept 2018 - killed - 15, injured - 16, total - 31.Total (adults and children) civilian casualties resulting from ERW mishandling in the period from 1 Jan until 30 Sept 2018 - killed -14, injured - 56, total - 70.Civilian casualties among children resulting from mine-related incidents in the period from 1 Jan until 30 Sept 2018 - killed - 4, injured - 1, total - 5.Civilian casualties among children resulting from ERW mishandling in the period from 1 Jan until 30 Sept 2018 - killed - 1, injured - 16, total - 17.

Activities & outcomes

Conduct informational training events in educational institutions on the territory of the project implementation in accordance with international standards IMAS and SOP-9.Provide training of mine danger instructors among teachers, NGO members and volunteers.Development, printing and distribution of information posters, booklets, flyers, etc. printed products in educational institutions, community centers and the common public.Provide roundtables, conferences on this issue in the region.Conduct a broad information company in the media in accordance with the developed PR-strategy.
