Project Summary

The protracted multi-faceted armed conflict, recurrent natural disasters, and a resulting upsurge in internal displacement have led to a highly complex crisis of protection in Somalia. The protection needs are very critical given the overstretched nature of limited resources due to the inﬂux of IDPs, the emergence of the COVID-19 Pandemic. People with disabilities face additional vulnerabilities. The purposes of this project is to fill the existing critical gaps in protection, GBV and CP through intervention to reduce the risks which are currently faced by the targeted children boys and girls in these areas, the incidents of SGBV, promote awareness on prevention and response to GBV and provide child protection services targeting 34,800 people (13,920 women 10,440girls, 8,700boys and 1,740men) including 3,480 people with disability in Gedo and Banadir regions.
The project will conduct wide-ranging rights based public outreach and awareness raising; Provision of appropriate assistance to children and adolescent victims of SE; includes safety, medical care, PSS, referral to legal assistance; Provision of MHPSS, child protection, and GBV victim’s assistance services in the target areas; This project will provide protection activities of psychosocial support including specialized counseling sessions, group therapy, support groups, Medical referrals, legal aid counseling services, distribution of emergency social assistance (dignity kits), solar lanterns to survivors, awareness and sensitization on GBV prevention and response, capacity building for community structures on GBV prevention and response and comprehensive case management services at the women and children wellness centers and Safe houses. Also the project will seek to increase the capacity of target communities to identify, report, and refer children with urgent child protection concerns as well as to provide psychosocial support services in the long term. Establishment of community-based child protection mechanisms training on raising awareness about existing referral pathways; Establishment of Child-friendly spaces, facilitation of psychosocial support for children including play therapy, art work , drawing, recreational activities and basic literary and numeracy. Distribution of 40 wheelchairs and 60 support sticks for persons living with disabilities. There will be number of training and capacity building events (PSS and CP), targeting religion elders, women groups, youth, children, school teachers, police, and community based protection committees; Adequate training for teachers and child protection staff in how to incorporate children living with disabilities into classroom learning and social service.

Mitigation Measures

1) Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.) Mitigation: CoDHNet Community protection and child protection workers especially Female workers and Community Liaison focal persons will engage community stakeholders on its available protection and child protection services and as well enhance community education, mobilization and awareness on available Protection services in its areas of operation. CoDHNet protection staff will ensure referral pathways are available for protection survivors. 2) Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys, and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities. Mitigation measures: CoDHNet will ensure that the marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys, and girls, participate in consultations or decision-making processes during the project design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation. CoDHNet will put robust measures to address barriers that hinder access to services or prevent protection-seeking behaviors especially among youth, disabled, illiterate and poor, GBV and protection affected beneficiaries. CoDHNet will coordinate its activities and identify disadvantaged and vulnerable groups (IDPs, minority clans, other marginalized) that may face unequal access to assistances, preventing unintended harm working in close collaboration with other community resource persons. CoDHNet will give priority vulnerable and marginalized people including people with disabilities. 3) Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms Mitigation measures: Mitigation measures: CODHNet will develop elaborate beneficiary complaint and feedback tool, project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be given a 1- day workshop on how to use the complaint /feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. CoDHNet will ensure that the project implementation process is accountable and transparent to the community and to the stakeholders including women, people with disabilities, and underserved communities. Community Feedback and Response Mechanism (CFRM) will also be used to make sure that the beneficiaries are satisfied with services provided and complaints can be channeled in the right direction. One of the ways of achieving this is through the establishment of a feedback and complaint mechanism to record and act on expression of satisfaction or dissatisfaction about the quality of the project services.4) Risk of explosive hazard contamination in the project target area. Mitigation: Learning will take place during the day and end in the evening so as to give time for learners to reach home safely. CoDHNet will carry out security measures training to all target area beneficiaries’.We will also work closely with community leaders to advise and notify us on areas that might be insecure so as to take precautions
