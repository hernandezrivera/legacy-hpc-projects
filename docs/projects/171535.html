Project Summary

ACTED is implementing a market-based project in six largely underserved and high-WASH risk IDP sites in Bardera, including Bulo Ceesan, Bulo Garass, Liban, Xabalcadey, Horseed and Kulmiya IDP sites, and is serving approximately 44,280 individuals. To ensure the sustainability of this action, ACTED is collaborating with the private sector to extend existing water networks to these communities and where necessary construct new water assets. The activities aim to meet both the acute and long-term needs of the vulnerable IDP communities, whilst establishing relationships with the private sector for continued maintenance and operation of these assets.
ACTED throughout project implementation will ensure all gender, persons of disability are included during consultation and in WASH management committees. In selecting beneficiaries, ACTED will give priority to female headed households, households led by persons with disability and households with lactating or pregnant women.

Project Activities

Activity 1.1.1 Water point construction or rehabilitationACTED will collaborate with the Jubba Building and Construction Company (JBCC) to expand JBCC’s existing water network to these sites. The water network expansion will bring safe and sufficient water to 2034 households across five IDP settlements. Activity 1.1.2 Water point construction or rehabilitationACTED will construct an infiltration well and establish a water network to serve the Horseed IDP site. The infiltration well and water network will meet the water needs of 600 households (3600 people) in Horseed. ACTED will also identify and rehabilitate seven shallow wells (six in Kismayo and one in Baidoa) reaching 3,500 individuals (583 households). Additionally, ACTED will rehabilitate five boreholes (four in Sool and Sanaag and one in Baidoa). The rehabilitated boreholes will serve 25,000 individuals (5,000 per borehole) as per SPHERE standards. Activity 2.1.1 Latrine construction or rehabilitationACTED will construct 290 2-door flood-resistant latrine blocks which will be served by the expanded water networks and will rehabilitate 40 latrines. A total of 25,440 people will benefit from access to the sanitation infrastructure. ACTED ensures accessibility of women and children and increases the protection-measures by installing solar lights and locks. ACTED will also ensure that 10% of the latrines will be accessible to persons with disability through installation of ramps and hand rails. Activity 3.1.1. Community Hygiene promotionDoor to door hygiene promotion sessions will be conducted by trained CHP reaching approximately 44,280 individuals Activity 3.1.2 Hygiene kit distribution (complete kits of hygiene items)ACTED will distribute hygiene kit commodity vouchers to 7,380 households Activity 3.1.3 Capacity building (water committees and WASH training)ACTED will provide capacity building to community members in order to ensure the sustainability of community water management systems. ACTED will select/revive and train six water management committees (WMC) in the six targeted IDP sites with each WMCs composed of 3 members each.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

ACTED and implementing partners will prioritize cash assistance to provide the beneficiaries with the much needed purchasing power, enabling them to respond to their basic needs while offering flexibility and diversity of procurement at household level. Cash preserves the dignity of the households and reserves their right to purchase items according to their needs.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

In the designing of the project, ACTED carried out a market analysis in the project implementation areas. Prior to project kick-off, all markets were warranted as functional and the local inflation and market dynamics analysed in order to provide beneficiaries with adequate amounts according to the prevailing conditions. In the unlikely event of an nation-wide inflation, ACTED would revise the amount accordingly after due consultation with the donor.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1- Throughout ACTED’s activities for this action gender and protection will be mainstreamed, with a special focus on targeting and supporting vulnerable groups including women, girls, people with disabilities and the elderly. ACTED will undertake focus group discussions with women to identify locations which are safe. To further embed protection mainstreaming, Water Management Committeee (WMC) members and Hygiene Promoters will be 50% women so that women feel comfortable to share their views with these representatives. In addition, ACTED will install solar lights at latrines and water kiosks so that women and children can safely access these facilitates.Risk 2-For this project, ACTED will ensure that the latrines are protected and are gender sensitive by equipping each block of latrines with a solar panel powered lamp on a 3m high mast to provide light to the all block. In each latrine, bulbs will be also fitted and connected to the battery. Risk 3- In order to ensure that the needs of all vulnerable groups are addressed, both beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries, will be made aware of ACTED’s Complaint and Response Mechanism (CRM) including the hotline during project sensitization, beneficiary selection, registration and verification to ensure that women can raise any protection or GBV related concerns. Users of the CRM are provided with the option to speak to a female member of ACTED’s accountability team. ACTED will ensure that persons with disabilities are made aware of the multiple CRM pathways, including the hot line, contact with the VRCs and contact with ACTED staff. ACTED will sensitize community leaders to engage with these members to ensure their voices are heard. To ensure participation in the project, hygiene promoters will undertake a door to door hygiene promotion so that messages are delivered to all beneficiaries, that 10 per cent of latrines are disability accessible and that water assets include ramps for access. ACTED estimates that 1580 persons of disability will be targeted under this action. Persons with disabilities face barriers in terms of access to WASH assets. Therefore, ACTED will ensure that 10 percent of latrines are accessible through ramps and railings and that water points have similar characteristics.
