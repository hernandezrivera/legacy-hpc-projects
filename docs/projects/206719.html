Project Summary

COHESODEC’ response activities regarding community health related interventions in 2024 will be targeting over 19,555 conflict affected populations in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. These targeted communities include the following health districts: Fundong, Benakuma, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Mamfe, Kumba North & Kumba South Health Districts!
The project seeks to ensure the availability of mental health care and psychosocial support involving 300 beneficiaries including the deployment of 3 human resources.
Furthermore, the project seeks to support 90% of epidemic preparedness and response ensuring that 1 kit is prepositioned in a high risk area. Moreover, this project shall be enhancing community engagement and adherence to vaccination in affected areas by ensuring that 150 community health workers are trained and/or supported for epidemic surveillance as well as ensure that 80% of vaccination coverage of Measles/Rubella is effectively carried out.
In addition, the project shall ensure the availability of essential healthcare services in areas affected by the crisis especially regarding outpatient consultations involving 12,955 beneficiaries for main morbidity and mortality causes through 1 health facility.
Also, the project shall be providing health care in hard to reach areas through the use of 1 mobile clinics ensuring that 1,000 affected populations do benefit from these free services. It will also be providing safe pregnancy/delivery and antenatal care involving some 2,000 women while also ensuring that 70% of births are assisted by skilled attendants.
Finally, this project shall be providing referrals to other sectors by ensuring that at least 150 cases are referred and are effectively received at other facilities.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will need the services of both specialized and non-specialized healthcare workers such as: Medical Doctors, Surgeons (health specialists with surgery capacity), mental health specialists, reproductive health specialists, Community Health Workers, Community mobilizers, community stakeholders and skilled project staff. Medical Doctors in affected health areas will be engaged to support health care in non-functional health facilities through the use of Mobile clinics in hard-to-reach areas in the NWSW regions. Moreover, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will require Mobile tablets for the collection and transmission of epidemic alert investigations as well as epidemic response kits, trauma kits, dignity kits, mobile clinic kit, experienced nurses and psycho-social Support specialists.
