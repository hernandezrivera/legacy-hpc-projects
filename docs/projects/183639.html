Project Summary

The project aims to address and respond to unmet needs as well as improving access to integrated and accessible healthcare services for people with disabilities living in marginalized communities . The proposed project is in line with the strategic objective #2 and which address the needs of the vulnerable Palestinians and respond to those needs through the provision of quality basic services and improved access to resources, in accordance with the rights of protected persons. The project will also contribute to and enhance the main objective of the health cluster towards ensuring the availability, accessibility, acceptability and quality of essential lifesaving health services to vulnerable communities in Gaza and West Bank/EJ, including through health system strengthening, preparedness and community resilience.
The current project is built on the needs assessment outcomes and recommendation of the health cluster reports and needs highlighted in 2021 for multidisciplinary rehabilitation services and the need for outreach services for people with disabilities (PwD). The project activities and approach will focus on an integrated multidisciplinary rehabilitation services for PwD which will include (outreach rehabilitation services and visits, assistive devices, MHPSS, follow up on associated non communicable diseases, COVID-19 response, addressing and responding to GBV, and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse whenever there is a need discovered; and finally first aid training and awareness for families of PwD in particular and local communities in general). By implementing the delineated interventions, HWC will contribute to a more equitable response for people in need (PwD) for basic and comprehensive health services to leave no one behind.
Among the targeted areas, the project will target H2 Area in Hebron City, Area C of South Hebron, Tubas governorate with special focus on the northern Jordan Valley and some location in Nablus City (Old city of Nablus, Balata and Askar Refugee Camps. Palestinian living in H2 Area of Hebron City are facing barriers to access specialized health services in particular for people with disability due to the imposed movement and access restrictions by the Israeli army. HWC will benefit from the presence of its health care center (Ishraqa) in the old city of Hebron where it will be used as a main hub service center to provide the specialized and comprehensive health services for the people with disabilities as well as the marginalized and vulnerable groups living in the Israeli controlled H2 Area. This project will contribute to strengthen the capacity of the Ishraqa center in H2 Area to provide affordable, quality and accessible services for people with disabilities in particular and indirectly benefiting the people in need for other health services (e.g response to GBV, women health, chronic diseases and sexual & reproductive health services and finally providing health awareness and services to respond for COVID-19). Palestinians living in Old City of Nablus are also lacking the health services for people with disabilities which forms another layer to the high poverty and unemployment rates which resulted in deterioration the socioeconomic status in the area. This had led to inability of the families, especially the families of people with disabilities to afford health services for their vulnerable family members.
Moreover, the project will target Palestinians who live in Area C of Tubas (Northern JV) and S. Hebron, where access to basic health services are of crucial concern and burden on the families. These areas will be served with outreach rehabilitation services.
The project will be implemented in full coordination and cooperation with the health actors in the targeted areas (mainly MoH and UNRWA and other health actors in each location) to avoid duplication and ensure complementarity of services provided and to maximize the effectiveness of the project activities.

1 - Needs

The Palestinian health system in the West Bank is gambled and burdened to respond to the demanding and growing needs of the Palestinian population in particular those who live in Area Area C and H2 due to various access restriction and lack of control over these areas. The people living in these areas (including people with disabilities and vulnerable groups) continued to suffer from lack/difficulties of access to basic health services; resulted in deteriorated in health determinants and at the end the general humanitarian situation is severely affected.Moreover, the health system in the West Bank faces significant challenges in extending quality services into refugee camps where thousands of Palestinian refugees are living with poor infrastructure and sanitation, high poverty and unemployment rates. Two refugee camps namely Balata and Askar in Nablus city will be also targeted in this project where the needs of the people with disability is prevailing and significant. In addition to the Old City of Nablus which suffers from lack of specialized health and rehabilitation services with deteriorated social determinants of health. Achieving a provision of affordable and accessible comprehensive healthcare services is still away from being realistic, this is due to inability of the Palestinian health system to provide services in some particular areas like H2, Area C and other isolated areas where Israeli is controlling the access and movement to these areas, in addition to the political and economic corrosion which led to recurrent financial crisis for the PA and its inability to cover and respond to the various needs of the population in particular the PwD, women and children as well as elderly. Recent reports indicate that more than 160,000 Palestinians living in 150 localities in Area C and H2 are lacking access to health services. The figures on disability in Palestine is not well calculated, but according to the latest PCBS survey in 2017, the prevalence of disability in Palestine is estimated on 5.8% (255224 people), among them 127262 in the West Bank. These figures still remain under debate and argument, and some researchers are estimating the figured higher beyond these findings. The proposed project aims at improving access to comprehensive healthcare and rehabilitation services for people with disabilities in particular and tackling the associated consequences of the disabilities, and indirectly serving other vulnerable groups (women, children and elderly) through provision of other healthcare interventions e.g women health including sexual and reproductive health, chronic diseases and referral to specialized health services, COVID-19 response and MHPSS. People with disabilities (PWD) remains the most disadvantaged and vulnerable among other groups in the targeted areas as they need more integrated healthcare and specialized rehabilitation services. PWD are not fronting only because of their disabilities but also because of the associated social barriers, negative attitude and stigma imposed by the community, and sue to the barrier in the surrounding physical environment and infrastructure. According to a recent report (PNGO-2019), more than two third of the people with disabilities do not receive appropriate care and are denied access to and participation in rehabilitation and healthcare plans, education, employment, and social participation. According to the GUPWD, most people with disability and their families struggle to meet the basic healthcare needs as assistive devices, medical disposables and medications.Among determinants to have disability is Palestine is lack or insufficient Mother & Child Healthcare services (MCH), where poor antenatal healthcare and child healthcare may lead to disability. Henceforward, a provision of women health services including SRH is very crucial to prevent disability.Disability per se does not constitute alone for struggling and suffering, but there are much more consequences associated with the disability; disability may result in increased GBV and sexual exploitation and abuse especially for women and girls. The proposed project adopts the provision of complementarity and inclusiveness social and healthcare approach based on human rights and protection modalities, in addition to raising awareness on the rights of PWD and philosophy of leaving no one behind. Having a disability, without availability of the proper resources to acquire treatment and rehabilitation services can greatly increase the risk of having other health problems and social consequences including GBV & SEA, poverty, unemployment. MHPS problems, lacking access to education and finally affects the social fabric and cohesion. The presence of disability, especially an insufficiently and inappropriately well treated disability, prevents individuals from living with dignity in an inclusive community. This project addresses needs of PWD in an integrative and holistic approach. It fills gaps in service provision by developing and adopting the human rights based and protection approach not only for the disabled individuals but also tackling the associated possible significances in the surrounding social and physical environment. TRIGON team (Treatment, Rehabilitation, Inclusion, Gender oriented, Social worker, Nurse) will be recruited to support the HWC center (Ishraqa) in delivering the intended activities of the project and to conduct outreach visits and support programs to PWD. The gaps, needs and interventions explained and proposed under this project are in line with recommendations of the health cluster to work more towards providing outreach rehabilitation programs and services. This will contribute to reduce and alleviate the suffering of the people with disabilities and their families while seeking healthcare services.

2 - Activities

The project was designed and conceptualized in close consultations and coordination with health actors in the targeted areas as well as after discussion with beneficiaries in our two Ishraqa centers in both Tubas and Hebron governorates where the local community members including women and disabled people highlighted the importance and vitality of such projects and integrated interventions for not only providing healthcare services but also taking into consideration other neglected social aspects associated with disabilities.The implementation of the project activities will be mainly through our centers. Ishraqa centers were established four years ago, and they are community based centers with a vision to develop and enhance the community participation with special focus on women and girls and vulnerable groups as well. The centers considered and viewed to be safe places for women and girls aiming to provide healthcare services and working towards the welfare of the local communities from an integrated and comprehensive perspective and human rights based approaches. Various activities are being implemented in the centers including health awareness sessions for local communities with special focus on women, debriefing sessions and MHPSS services. GBV and PSEA are also well considered and integrated in our services for women. Our centers are viewed by the local communities also as a new unconventional space which designs and implements programs and activities based on the needs of the women perspectives, and believes in the power of the women in achieving the desired change in healthcare approaches and rehabilitation of the PWD. HWC approach for rehabilitation focuses on responding to the needs with effective participation from the local communities in planning, implementing and monitoring the success of such programs. HWC sustains an excellent relationship and coordination with partners and active health players on the ground and will ensure to consult and collaborate them in implementing the project activities where applicable to reach maximum numbers of beneficiaries and increase the effectiveness of the project. Therefore, we will be working to achieve the following results/ activities: Result N1: Improve access of people with disabilities in the targeted marginalized communities to affordable quality rehabilitation and healthcare services.Activity N1:1: Provision of Comprehensive and participatory inclusive rehabilitation and healthcare services with special focus on outreach rehabilitation, programed field visits to reach PWD and family assessments to diagnose and identify needs for treatment and rehabilitation and follow up plans, physiotherapy, speech and occupational therapy, MHPSS, MCH and SRHS (in-house and outreach) as well as treatment of associated health problems and chronic diseases. Result N2: Homes of 60 PWD (men, women, boys and girls) have been adapted to improve their living conditions. Activity N2:1: Improve the physical environment (rehabilitation) of the 60 homes for PWD to enhance and improve performance in daily living activities. Activity N2:2: Endowment of assistive devices and other living aids (hygiene kits) to improve and ease the access and movement of 90 PWD (men, women, boys and girls) and to improve their quality of life. Result N3: Improved Responsiveness to GBV & SEA.Activity N3:1: Conducting 60 awareness sessions on GBV & PSEAActivity N3:2: Steering 60 counselling and MHPSS sessions for victimized women and girlsResult N4: Improved women health and MCHActivity N4:1: Providing antenatal and postnatal healthcare services including SRH for 1200 women in the targeted locations.Activity N4:2: Conducting 60 health awareness and education sessions on women health and MCH (COVID-19 incorporated). Result N5: Improved access of PDW (women) to Ishraqa centers in the Old City of Hebron and Tubas Activity N5: Two women safe centers (Ishraqa centers) are adapted and well modified for CWD (Hebron and Tubas).

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

One of the main motives behind the project idea is to make the healthcare service as close as possible and affordable in or close to the place of residence of people with disabilities in order to protect them from any risk and vulnerability while seeking healthcare services in other far away areas. The wellbeing of the target groups is of high concern and is a priority in this project. The project will consider and ensure a non-discriminatory service amongst all groups of the community, the gender is mainstreamed in all activities of the project. HWC is working with strict standards for data protection and confidentiality. HWC is dedicated, loyal and accountable to serve and protect all people served. Activities will be implemented regardless sex and age of beneficiaries. Monitoring and evaluation of the project will be very well articulated and designed to include segregation of data collected. Confidentiality will be ensured to safely secure the data of served people. Interests and demand of the people will be always well tackled and considered. The project team will be proactively trained on PSEA before starting the implementation of the project.
