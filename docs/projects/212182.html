Project Summary

Reference to HNO report 2023, Aleppo Governorate hosts the highest number of people in health need 2.7 M, what made the situation even worse the recent catastrophic and destructive Mw 7.8 earthquake that struck on the 6th of February 2023.
In line with the health sector’s first priority in terms of responding to the health impact of the earthquake that affected and overstretched the already overloaded public health system, AL-TA'ALOUF Association as an active member of the national and health sector coordination group aims to increasing access to life-saving and life-sustaining, equitable health services for those most vulnerable and in need, as securing access to health care services is a key priority in Aleppo governorate especially Rural areas
Therefore, we propose to implement a project "Supporting the provision of basic health care services to affected populations in rural Aleppo
The project consists of four mobile teams covering the following areas: Hretan - Zerbeh - Bannan - Hajeb - Maskanah - Hadher - Tall Ed-Daman, The MMTs will consist of a general doctor, internist, gynecologist, pediatrician, pharmacist, Health Educator, Midwife, and 2 nurses. Moreover, The Medical Mobile Teams shall operate 6 days a week / 8 hours daily from 8:00 am to 4 PM providing medical consultations and medication for medical consultations and medications for Child health, gynecology, reproductive health, communicable and non-communicable health care, elderly health care, and adolescent care.
Implementation of activities:
1.Preparatory work (announcements for recruitments, vehicle contracting, inventory list preparation, community needs reassessment/ awareness of new services
2.Running 4 Medical Mobile teams
3.Provision of specialized medical consultation.
4.Procurement and provision of medication
5.Supporting advanced surgical treatment under Secondary care on referral basis.
6.Supporting Trauma Care management services on referral basis
7.Provision of assistive devices.
8.Performing of MHPss services
9.Monitoring and evaluation
Protection Risk Analysis:
1- Protection for safety and security: the project aims to ensure the safety of PoCs during project activities implementation, where decisions are made to prevent any possible security threats and ensure the safety of PoCs and staff working in the project.
2- Protection of privacy and dignity: PoCs are treated with dignity and their rights are respected, where their personal information is confidential.
3- Protection against discrimination: PoCs are treated without discrimination or preferentiality based on their gender, race, religion or any other personal preferences
4- Protection for children and women: special procedures are taken to protect children and women from any form of violence or exploitation, proper support and counselling is provided for affected women and children.
PSEA strategy:
AL-TA'ALOUF Association internal PSEA policy includes clear definition, reporting and feedback mechanism, investigation confidentiality. Moreover, AL-TA'ALOUF Association internal PSEA policy states that every new staff member in the NGO should receive PSEA training by authorized trainer in the NGO by UNHCR.
Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures:
Risk 1. Exchange rate fluctuation, which may lead to an unexpected rise in the price of materials.
Mitigation measures: procuring the necessary materials at the beginning of the project to avoid procuring them at a higher price later.
Risk 2: Inefficiency of the employees:
Mitigating measures: AL-TA’ALOUF is able to establish a group of competent medical professionals to handle all pertinent referrals.
Risk 3: Local Approvals
Mitigation measures: AL-TA’ALOUF will be responsible for obtaining the necessary approvals, and ensuring effective coordination with the local authorities prior to the commencement of medical assistance activities.
