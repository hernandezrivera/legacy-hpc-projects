Project Summary

The project will cover Medical waste services in West Mosul in Ninewa and Fallujah in Anbar
Pekawa organization is already providing medical waste services through its partnership with WHO in Hamdaniya, Hamam Aleel, East Mosul, and 5 districts in Salah Aldin (Tikrit, Aldor Alalam, Beiji, Sameraa); which will also be covered for the first 6 month in 2019.
However, some gaps remain in West Mosul, and Anbar (Fallujah and Ramadi) where there are no services for medical waste and a lack in non-incineration medical waste, transport vehicle and service.
Project implantation Method:
Supply and Set-up of two medical waste treatment centres to serve healthcare facilites that provide health services for returnees, IDPS and host community, one in West Mosul city and one in Fallujah
Each centre will consist of a caravan, medical waste machines, and a generator. Where The main activity of each centre will consist of collecting waste from healthcare centres operated by local NGOs, INGOs & DOH
Locations:
1. West Mosul targeted Healthcare centre:
Targeting 19 PHCs serving 24088 beneficiaries of which 12868 are female; in addition, to AL Mosul general hospital which has an incinerator that is not fully functioning.
2. Fallujah targeted healthcare centres
Fallujah has two functioning hospitals, the general Fallujah hospital and the birth hospital. The estimated targeted PHCs in are 12 spread across the district.
Exit Strategy:
Three months before the end of the project, Pekawa would request from DOH to establish a team to overtake the operations of the centres. The team for each centre will consist of at least two technicians and 2 operators, they will be trained and work along with the existing team, so that they gain enough experience to run the facility on thier own.

Activities

Activities:1. Setup of the medical waste centres1.1 Set up the medical treatment centre according to its pre-set standard specifications based on WHO “safe management of waste from health-care activities” book. The facility consists of a caravan built using of sandwich panels. In discussion with DOH Anbar and Ninewa the facility will be installed in the back yard a designate PHC or a Hospital.1.2 Pekawa will Purchase medical autoclave waste treatment units for each centre. 1.3 Pekawa will Lease one medical waste transport vehicles for each centre 1.4 Pekawa will organize training program for its operating staff; the machine operation training will be conducted through the machine supplier technician in the centre itself. 2. Operation of the medical waste service2.1 Pekawa will develop, in collaboration with WHO and DOH, a pick-up schedule and rout for health-care waste with coordination of partners. 2.2 Monitoring and follow up on complaints.Waste collection and treatment of medical waste will be monitored on daily bases, using a data entry website. During the operation period Pekawa will submit monthly operations report that include detailed information about the performance of medical waste management service.2.3 Maintenance of facility and equipmentPekawa will maintain the following items in good working condition capable of performing their intended function:• Caravan and other structures including, and not limited to, perimeter fencing, gates, paved surfaces, unpaved surfaces, drainage structures and yard piping, wash-down water collection and storage facilities, utilities, and truck scales.• Stationary equipment including, and not limited to, waste treatment equipment and waste.• Mobile equipment including collection vehicles, transport trucks, etc.• Medical waste and treated medical waste residue storage and transport containers
