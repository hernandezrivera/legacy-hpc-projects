1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project is based on water, sanitation and hygiene activities for the response, the activities are being implemented in host communities, resettlement centers and communities of origin of the IDPs, in order to alleviate the suffering of the populations in the northern region due to conflicts and insecurity in Cabo Delgado, due to frequent displacements of populations.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Main activities (WASH):Rehabilitation and construction of water sources,Hygiene promotion, distribution of slabs for latrines,Construction of latrine blocks andDistribution of hand washing station,Distribution of hygiene kits and WASH survival kits.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project, for its evolution and in order to avoid risks and damages, works in collaboration with NGO and government partners, as well as the WASH Cluster, in order to create joint planning and monitoring in relation to the risks foreseen in the implementation of the project.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Respects social inclusion and joint decision-making with partners, local leaders and the government.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the process of collecting baseline information/assessment in places where the activities are implemented, barriers are identified and with that, in the process of designing and implementing field activities, techniques are included, and/or tools that adapt and/or guarantee social inclusion, be it in water infrastructures as well as insanitation infrastructures of the environment.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

In all planned infrastructures or activities, the environmental impact conditions are respected in order to preserve the environmental conservation procedures.
