Project Summary

The project will target GBV survivors while considering gender equality and social/disability inclusion in leaving no one behind (LNOB), with provision of lifesaving livelihood support. The project will ensure that marginalized groups are accessed by specifically reaching out to them in community outreach activities, such as adolescent girls, women from minorities and transgenders in Sherani, Musakhail, Sibi, Kachi and Harnai districts of Balochistan. These districts have been selected keeping in view the ACLED ranking, fatalities’ severities, affected population and catastrophic considerations.
The proposed project will address a total population of 394,876 including men, women, youth, adolescents, PWDs and transgender. The project will be steered with the competent team of BRSP that include a well experienced Coordinator in the area of GBV, Psychologists and social mobilisation teams at districts’ levels. The overall supervision of the project will be carried out by Head of Department Health, Nutrition, GBV and Disabilities. BRSP has strong presence in the proposed districts and will operate through its independent offices and create synergies to address GBV affected population, gender equality and social/disability.
BRSP is well experienced in the area of GBV/SRH and implementing a project with the support of UNFPA in 07 districts of Balochistan. The project will address the catchment population of at least 20 Basic Health Units (BHUs). Community Resource Persons (CRPs) will be selected from the targeted communities who will be trained on GBV sensitization and will work as gate keepers. These CRPs will be supervised and supported by a team of social organizers. The CRPs will work as gate keepers for social mobilisation teams and will arrange awareness sessions in the communities.
The CRPs will also identify GBV affected women/men, adolescent girls/boys and PWDs for counselling purpose and they will be encouraged to contact the nearby BHUs and Women Friendly Health Spaces.
The psychologists will provide psychosocial support and create referral pathways for need based protection services. Dignity kits will be provided to women and girls for the maintenance of their personal hygiene. Media campaigns will also be launched for mass awareness among the districts’ populations. Recreational activities will also be organised for youth/adolescents girls and boys to promote mental health and awareness raising on GBV and its prevention. Beside all, livelihood activities will be promoted among GBV survivors to stabilize their families and create confidence to participate in economic uplift of their families and children.
