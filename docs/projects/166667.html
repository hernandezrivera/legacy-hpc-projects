Project Summary

A.1.1 - Distribution life-saving items, such as soap, water containers and reusable textile protective masks (as per MISAU guidelines), to vulnerable groups as to single mothers, persons with disabilities, elderly, families living in slums and IDPs in camp A.2.1 -Training on COVID-19 for councilors of Linha Fala Crianca to provide adequate support to children. A.3.1 - Coordinate with other partners for the correct application of Guidelines to protect and create alternative care for children who have parents need in quarantined for COVID 19. A.4.1 - Awareness campaign on existing hotlines (including Child Helpline- 116) that children and other community members can use to report cases related to violence against children in the context COVID-19 interventions as well as to provide PSS. A.5.1 - Provide case workers with necessary PPE and technology solutions to enable them to do their work. A.6.1 - Provide of MHPSS support to children and caregivers in the context COVID-19 through outreach team and Community Comities for Child Ptotection in coordination with Ministry of Health, Gender and Education.
