Project Summary

Main objective of this project is to improve WaSH services in host communities of Ukhiya and Teknaf, Cox's Bazaar district. SI designed its intervention with a combined approach. 1) improvement of wash situation through the construction of water supply network and sanitation (including solid waste management) at both HH and community level (i.e. - schools, market and health centre. 2) providing access to technical service for other wash actor with technical support on diagnosis of bore hole, water quality monitoring and capacity building of NGOs.
SI yearn to scale up its intervention in solid waste management system in HC by designing long term and sustainable action which will enable community people and local authority to take more ownership in action. Also SI will continue its intervention on community engagement and awareness raising on WASH issue including SWM in institution, public place and community level. In all level of SI intervention, protection, gender, inclusion and social cohesion will be mainstreamed and most vulnerable community will be targeted as priority.
Finally, SI will continue to follow the WASH sector strategy and get involved in long term solutions to enhance understanding on the hydrogeologic context of Teknaf and Ukhiya Upazila. SI intends develop its research on surface and groundwater availability, quality, watershed etc. in both Ukhiya and Teknaf Upazila in order to assess the feasibility of sustainable water supply infrastructure in HC.
In its intervention SI is committed to managing its equipment and its waste in a responsible manner. The organization will conduct assessments on environmental impact prior to implementing eco-significant activities in order to choose those options least disturbing the local environment. Notably, the WASH infrastructure energy supply will be through solar energy system.

1 - Identified Needs

According to last JMSNA findings, access to safe and functional latrines, clean drinking water and hygiene items remain the top 3 priorities need in HCs. In Ukhiya and Teknaf HCs, the majority of households (88%) access water from tube wells/hand pumps, but only 77% reported having enough water for their domestic needs and 74% in Teknaf where groundwater is particularly scarce. Over the past years, this figure has significantly increased (from 63% in 2019) due to the drilling of 2,840 boreholes in Ukhiya and Teknaf highly impacting ressource management. Additionally, there is a major challenge in terms of equity of the water access over the area.JMSNA indicates that 20% of population reported using an unimproved sanitation facilities where in 2020, only 63% of households had at least basic access to sanitation. This shows a significant improvement, however community participation, community lead total sanitation, and functionality of WATSAN committee remain critical for sustainable impact. Moreover, waste management remains a key need for HC as only 28% HH reported having 1 bin at HH level and 69% HH reported throwing waste in open fields. Appropriate hygiene practices remain low despite the progresses observed partly due to fear from Covid-19 and focus on hand-washing promotion. As a result, 92% HH reported having access to soap. Those improvements need to be confirmed to ensure that this access is ensured on a long-term basis.
