Project Summary

The situation in Lebanon continues to rapidly deteriorate due to the compounding factors of economic decline, political stalemate, protracted refugee crises, the COVID-19 pandemic, the Beirut Port Blast in 2020, and growing fear and uncertainty for the future. Since the political crisis began in October 2019, unemployment rates of migrants in Lebanon sky-rocketed while living conditions have significantly deteriorated. The Kafala System in Lebanon further exacerbates vulnerabilities and opens the door to employer abuse, and despite reforms, there is still a huge number of protection cases associated with migrant workers. In order to address the situation faced by vulnerable migrants, this project will take a holistic approach to meet the needs of critically vulnerable female migrants, often the survivors of abuse and trafficking.
Under this project, Tabitha will support a total of 200 women inside of a shelter providing not only a safe place to stay, but also food, non-food items, access to healthcare, psychosocial support, referrals to services such as legal assistance and voluntary repatriation, and most importantly the feeling of security and dignity in a situation where so many women are left without any social safety net.
Tabitha will prioritize cases of trafficking and/or GBV in a shelter with 40 beds. Women will be able to reside in the shelter for up to 6 months before finding a durable solution, including the possibility of voluntary repatriation to their home country. Women will be referred to the shelter from partner organizations, consulates and embassies, or through Tabitha's outreach network in Aley, Batroun, and Achrafieh through its community centers; Tabitha will utilize its reputation as a trusted protection-based organization with community outreach workers to ensure that women are aware of the service and able to get support. While this project is focused in the locations where Tabitha has its community centers, Tabitha is open to referrals from across the totality of Lebanon, and can also reach women through its mobile protection units in the Bekaa Valley to accept more referrals.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

This project falls in line with the migrant sector objective 3, "To enhance timely, unhindered, and equitable access to protection assistance for migrants." The project will achieve this objective by providing multi-layered services, including protection services, for women staying at the Tabitha run shelters. As previously mentioned, the purpose of the shelter is not only to provide a place for homeless migrant women to stay and feel secure, but the shelters will also run various humanitarian services, like healthcare and psychosocial support, and provision of lifesaving and life-sustaining humanitarian goods, like food and hygiene items. Tabitha's social workers will link those staying in the shelter with services run by partners to access legal and other services, and will support women as they seek sustainable shelter solutions.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Tabitha mainstreams protection in all its activities which allows maximization of the protective impact of the project through ensuring that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Social cohesion activities are designed to bring together individuals of different backgrounds. Activities create a space for discussion and peer mentoring which allows different groups to reflect on their similarities rather than differences. Project activities are designed to include any critically vulnerable individual within the population, regardless of their nationality, gender identity, disability, race, religion, or socioeconomic background. Staff will be sensitized to gender & diversity and should recognize that vulnerabilities differ from one person to another.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Tabitha creates opportunities for beneficiary involvement throughout the program cycle. This project’s activities have been designed based upon the feedback given by beneficiaries and stakeholder engagement, where possible. Tabitha has a robust two-way feedback mechanism with multiple channels like complaint boxes, email address, hotline, and SMS, and engages with stakeholders such as consulates and embassies of concerned vulnerable migrants. Within this project, Tabitha will have a complaint & feedback mechanism located inside of the shelter with weekly meetings to ensure issues are addressed as quickly as possible. For those who do not speak Arabic, French, or English, Tabitha will locate community volunteers who can communicate with them to ensure the opinions of all residing in the shelter are heard.
