Project Summary

GOALPrime Organization Nigeria (GPON) is proposing to carry out a 12 month project that focused on community-based approaches to enhance GBV preventive mechanism and support effective delivery of urgent lifesaving protection services in three targeted LGAs with focus to address the need of new arrivals. The project is targeting to reach 9,000 beneficiaries including IDPs and affected host community members. Adopting a community base approach in providing services to vulnerable beneficiaries in the community is to include community input in building of a system of care. This approach will address both the short term (lifesaving) and long term (Humanitarian nexus) protection needs of boys, girls, men and women eventually.
GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will specifically:
Strengthen gender and age sensitivity community based protection mechanism. GPON shall work with community and government structure to develop context specific evidence base capacity building strategy that will build upon the existing formal and informal community based protection mechanism and link them to formal government structure. This approach will reinforce the participation of the affected population in needs identification, strengthen accountability throughout the project cycle.
Build psycho-social resilience of GBV survivors and individuals at risk of GBV. The project will strengthen the capacity of psychosocial workers to provide safe and confidential services in line with the minimum standards and guild line with emphasis on managing child survivors. Also the project will engage community-based case workers to provide follow up supports to the survivors. Sexual reproductive health right information and referrals will be integrated in the GBV case management services provided at the women and girls safe spaces by the project. Survivors including women and adolescent girls will receive culturally appropriate safety and dignity kits including integrated livelihood and or income generation support.
The project will indirectly reach 32310 individuals with the assumption that every beneficiaries reach by the project directly, a minimum of 3 individuals from that household will be reached indirectly, individuals will be reach by the social workers through outreach and focus group discussion are all considered as indirect beneficiaries.

Needs

Going to the Tenth year of the crisis, over 7.7 million people are still in need of life saving assistance and protection, and 1.83 million people are internally displaced across the Northeast (DTM, August 2018). Despite some areas becoming more stable and accessible in 2018, thousands of new arrivals have been recorded in recent months, largely coming from hard to reach areas as a result of insecurity and military operations. Recent displacement to already-crowded areas has a compound effect on severely stretched services. Currently 14 formal sites across 12 LGAs in Borno are classed as in a ‘high congestion’ status, with 285,000 individuals above camp capacity (OCHA Situation Update, September 2018). In this context, protection needs remain severe, especially for vulnerable groups, children, and adolescents with protection concerns. More, for the 2019 Humanitarian Response Plan, the total population of people in need of GBV services have been projected to be 2,964939 across the three state of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states. Borno state is have 65% of the projected people in need with 2,040234 Adamawa have 25% (625258) and Yobe is 10% (299574) of the people in need. GBV sub-sector working group target to reach 1,281314 in 2019 humanitarian plan; borno 940382, adamawa 215465 and Yobe 125467. Affected population in 14 LGAs of Borno state are faced with life-threatening conditions causing high level of suffering and irreversible damages to health status, which can result in deaths if no external humanitarian assistance is provided immediately. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria is proposing to reach people in Maiduguri, Munguno and Konduga LGA which are part of LGA that are high in the priority ranking of needs of people in LGAs with establishing targeted assistance that effectively respond to prevent sexual and gender base violence.

Activities

Activity 1Conduct GBV safety audit in project communities and support prevention actions. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will conduct quarterly safety audit to identify GBV risks for vulnerable women and girls in the project communities. This safety audit outcome will be sheared with relevant partners and community based structures to inform the design of services and increase safety for women and girls. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will work with community to identify ways of addressing the risk identified during the safety audits.Activity 2Develop IEC/BCC materials: GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will engage with the community to develop key massages for different audience. The BCC material development will follow a working session with project staff and community members especially adolescent during the working season, key knowledge, attitude and practice around GBV will be identified and massages developed. GOALPrime will develop massages/jingle including posters and brochures.Activity 3Conduct community awareness: GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will develop culturally appropriate IEC/BCC Materials that will be used for community awareness. Community based dialogue seasons will be conducted targeting 5000 individual with key massages on GBV. GOALPrime will target teachers, community leaders and religious leaders with target key massage, Mass awareness campaign will be held especially at reception centers to provide individuals with massages on available GBV services, risk and consequences.Activity 4Train social workers and community volunteers including community leaders on GBV principles and psychosocial first aid. GOALPrime Organization will Mobilize 100 individuals including military personnel, social workers, community leaders and community structures. The training will focus on community base support for prevention and respond to GBV risk as well as referral pathway.Activity 5 Provide psychosocial support to 3000 children and adolescents in CFS. The CFS will provide psychosocial support children and adolescents. GOALPrime will recruit and train community base CFS facilitators who will lead children as well as outdoor games like puzzle games, storytelling, traditional songs that support the cognitive development of children. At the CFS centers, case managers will be prepared to provide psychosocial counseling support and facilitate referrals when needed to other specialized services. Adolescent boys and girls will taught life skills that will help improve their self-confidence, decision-making and negotiation skill as well as prevent violence abuse and exploitationActivity 6 Provide Psychosocial and resilience building support to survivors of GBV and individual at risk of GBV: the psychosocial support activities include recreational activities, social networking opportunities and information shearing seasons. The safe space for women and girls will provide hub for women and girls to receive information on a range of issues, including the availability of GBV and other Services in their communities. Also 3000 women and girls will receive numeracy and literacy skills including sessions on life skills.Activity 7Provide dignity kits and safety material to 1500 women: GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will facilitate quarterly distribution of dignity kits to 1500 highly vulnerable women and girls in project communities. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will liaise with GBV sub-sector working group for the standardize package and content of the dignity kit.Activity 8Provide case management support to survivors of GBV: GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will identify and provide survivor centered case management support to GBV survivors. Activity 9 Organize regular case management meeting and case conferences to identify and address immediate needs of survivors.
