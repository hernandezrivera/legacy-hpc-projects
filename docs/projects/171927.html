Project Summary

The aim of the project “Multi-Purpose Cash, Winterization Assistance and Psychosocial Support for Conflict-Affected People along the ‘contact line’ in Donetska and Luhanska GCA” is to cover basic needs (food, health, hygiene, winterization, PSS) of conflict-affected populations in the 5-km zone along the 'contact line' in Eastern Ukraine (GCA of Luhanska and Donetska oblasts).
Whereas the project’s MPC and winterization components will be implemented by ASB Country Office Ukraine, the psychosocial support (PSS) will be provided by ASB’s local partner, Samaritan Union of Ukraine (SSU).
The winterization component of the project aims at addressing the most urgent winterization needs of the conflict-affected populations residing in the 5-km zone along the ‘contact line’ in Donetska and Luhanska GCA by providing winterization cash grants to 500 households, solid fuel to additional 500 households and house insulation (glazing) to 100 households for the winter season of 2021-2022.
By providing winterization cash grants, solid fuel and house insulation, the proposed project will contribute to Shelter/NFI Cluster Objective 1 “Address gaps in essential shelter and NFI needs of the most vulnerable IDPs and conflict affected population through monetised/in-kind assistance and contingency” and Cluster Objective 2 “Contribute to adequate transitional solutions (monetised or in-kind) related to shelter and NFI needs meeting minimal international and national shelter standards”.

1 - Needs

The conflict in Eastern Ukraine continues to have a negative impact on the socioeconomic situation in Ukraine, particularly in the areas adjacent to the ‘contact line’. As demonstrated by the WASH Cluster Study 2019, the 20-km zone has a significantly higher proportion of households in the poorest category. For the 20-km zone, food is the main household expenditure, with the costs for heating and utilities being the second largest category of expenses (REACH Economic Security Assessment. Government-controlled areas of Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts within 20 km of the contact line, March 2019).Household vulnerability increases in winter, when high heating costs negatively affect food security of particularly vulnerable households (FSLC’s Guidance Note on Winterisation (2017-2018) in Food Security). As identified by the Shelter/NFI Cluster, winterization assistance is needed by approximately 174,000 people in both GCA and NGCA. Out of them, 46,749 people reside in in the 5-km zone along the ‘contact line’ in Donetska and Luhanska GCA. In the areas along the ‘contact line’, the needs are the most severe (severity scale 3 or 4).In the urban areas, 38% of households in the 20-km zone (GCA) rely primarily on mains heating (REACH Economic Security Assessment, March 2019). Therefore, increased heating costs during the winter months pose a serious challenge to vulnerable households. Apart from cash for winterization assistance, people in rural and isolates settlements need support with solid fuel. As identified by the Shelter Cluster, one of the lessons learnt from the Winterization campaign 2019/2020 is the need to provide in-kind assistance instead of cash assistance to beneficiaries in isolated settlements, as they have limited access to markets. Unavailability or only partial availability of solid fuel in many locations along the ‘contact line’ has been also demonstrated by the market analysis done by the Cash Working Group. According to the last round of market analysis (February 2020), coal is not available in 50% of assessed locations in Luhanska oblast. There are small localities close to the ‘contact line’, with no gas or central heating and limited or no possibility to purchase solid fuel and have it delivered to their locality. Moreover, many suppliers are reluctant to go to the areas close to the ‘contact line,’ so inhabitants of these villages heavily rely on winterization assistance from humanitarian agencies.ASB’s rapid needs assessment in the 5-km zone along the ‘contact line’ (GCA), conducted as a part of PDM on in-kind assistance in summer 2020, showed that 61,4% of all HHs using solid fuel reported that they do not have fuel reserves for heating this winter, 32,9% of respondents have a small supply of fuel, which is partly enough to heat their homes during 2020/21 winter season, and the remaining 5,7% noted that they have absolutely no stocks. Therefore, ASB considers provision of in-kind winterization assistance to people in need of solid fuel. Moreover, based on the frequent requests to ASB hotline to provide house insulation, ASB will assist up to 100 households with glazing.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

Purpose: to meet winterization needs of vulnerable conflict-affected populations in Eastern UkraineOutput: vulnerable households along the ‘contact line’ received winterization assistance. Outcome: target households improve their preparedness to winter.Activity 1. Household identification for winterization assistance The beneficiary assessment will be conducted in August-September 2021 to identify 1100 particularly vulnerable households (approximately 2000 beneficiaries) in the 5-km zone near the ‘contact line’ (Donetsk and Luhansk GCA) for winterization assistance. Whereas 500 HHs, predominantly in rural areas of Luhanska GCA, will receive in-kind assistance (either coal or briquettes), another 500 HHs, mainly in urban areas of Donetska GCA, will receive cash for winterization. Also, based on need, up to 100 HHs will be selected for house insulation.Beneficiary selection will be undertaken in coordination with Shelter/NFI Cluster, local authorities and humanitarian agencies operating in the area. ASB will target primarily beneficiaries who meet vulnerability criteria (i.e. older persons, people with disabilities, female-headed households, IDPs) or, in case of small localities, provide blanket coverage to avoid community tensions. Activity 2. Distribution of winterization cash grants to cover critical winterization needsThe winterization cash grants will be disbursed in December 2021. ASB will follow current recommendation of the Shelter/NFI Cluster. Thus, 500 vulnerable households will be provided with winterization cash grants to cover the costs for central/gas heating for the period of 3 months (340 USD per household). Due to the exchange rate fluctuations, ASB may reduce number of beneficiaries and/or revise cash value. For the population in the 5-km zone near the line of contact (GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts), the most preferred modes of delivery for cash assistance are bank account (48%) and post office (32%) (REACH Humanitarian Trend Analysis 2018). Thus, the modes of cash disbursement used by ASB (Oshchadbank and UkrPoshta) are in line with the preferences of the affected populations. Due to the home delivery of cash by Ukrposhta, older people and persons with disabilities would not need to travel to bank to withdraw their cash grants. The PDM on cash for winterization showed high satisfaction of beneficiaries with the ASB winterization programme and modality of assistance. The top three expenditures of ASB winterization cash assistance were central heating (87,6%), other utility bills (57,6%) and food (33,2%), so the cash grants were used for an intended aim.Activity 3. Distribution of solid fuelASB’s needs assessment and consultations with local authorities in the areas along the ‘contact line’ have demonstrated the need to provide in-kind assistance instead of cash assistance to beneficiaries in rural areas along the ‘contact line’.ASB will thus provide in-kind winterization assistance (coal or briquettes) to 500 households in need of solid fuel. The amount is calculated in line with the recommendation of the Shelter/NFI Cluster, with the delivery costs added.Activity 3. Winterization insulation ASB hotline registered a number of requests for provision of house insulation solutions.Therefore, ASB plans to assist up to 100 households with glazing. The amount is calculated as 278 USD per household, in line with the recommendation of the Shelter/NFI Cluster. Post Distribution Monitoring will be undertaken to measure the utilization of the aid provided. A hotline feedback mechanism will be available throughout the project to ask questions, request assistance, provide feedback and lodge complaints. All complaints will be recorded in the Complaints Registration Database for follow-up. All assessments, including PDM, are designed to allow age, gender and disability disaggregation.

3 - Indicators & targets

The main indicators and targets are the following:• 85% of beneficiaries are satisfied with the aid provided;• 85% of beneficiaries report that the assistance was easily accessible;• 70% of beneficiaries report that they are aware of the complaint mechanisms;• 500 HHs receiving winterization cash grants;• 500 HHs receiving solid fuel and heater support for winter;• 100 HHs supported with shelter insulation solutions• at least 60% of program beneficiaries are female;• at least 30% of beneficiaries are older people;• at least 10% of beneficiaries are persons with disabilities and/or homebound.The overall target number of households will be up to 1100 (approximately 2000 beneficiaries).Given the demographic and socioeconomic profile of the vulnerable population in the 5-km zone along the ‘contact line,’ women will particularly benefit from the project activities. Thus, out of 1000 beneficiaries who received ASB winterization cash grants in December 2019, 670 were women, including 148 girls, 287 women aged 18-59 and 235 older women (60+).In-kind modality of winterization assistance will also take into consideration specific needs of girls and women. Female-headed households are generally more vulnerable, particularly if women live alone. The residents of localities along the ‘contact line’ sometimes collect wood in the nearby forests and, if they purchase wood, need to chop it for winter. This is particularly problematic for women, older people, and persons with disabilities. Therefore, provision of solid fuel in these localities will allow vulnerable groups to prepare for winter without resorting to negative coping strategies and undergoing protection risks.
