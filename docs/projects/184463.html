Project Summary

To ensure sustainability of delivering essential Primary Health Services (EPHS) with integrated of Covid-19 indicators and WASH related hygiene promotion practices on AWD/Cholera and COVID-19 prevention BTSC will implement this project in Burhakaba district of Bay region to improve health status. The prevention component of the Health will focus on delivering routine immunization services to 0-12 month’s old infants including undertaking Measles and Polio vaccination campaigns to U5 children, ANC services for pregnant women, health education of expected women on danger signs during pregnancy, health education sessions on prevention of other communicable diseases including COVID-19. For immunizations BTSC will implement monthly outreach programs where they advocate for immunizations door to door for children who are not brought to the health facility. BTSC will commit to embark in mass awareness creation to ensure that people are aware of the services offered under this project hence improving uptake of health services. BTSC has been in a close relationship and collaboration with the other stakeholders and Line Ministries (SMoH, SMoE, and SMOEWRS).
BTSC will ensure to strengthen inclusion of marginalised/Minority communities and people with disabilities in humanitarian response by including a question on clan affiliation/membership in marginalised communities and ensure the meaningful access of people with disabilities to humanitarian assistance through training and capacity building support to clusters on disability inclusion and strengthened data collection on people with disabilities reached, in line with IASC guidelines.
Key beneficiaries those will be served under this project include Vulnerable Children and Women, Marginalized and Minority Groups, and People with Disabilities. BTSC will ensure and contribute and Increased minority involvement to allow persons from minority groups to witness representation of diversity from the population served; to see different cultural backgrounds from staff; and bring appreciation and respect for, and understanding, of organization’s work.This holistic consideration of gender aspects will foster assistance that considers the sensitivities of the different needs and capacities of women, girls, boys, men and those with disabilities by ensuring they participate constructively and benefit from interventions that address their specific concerns. (BTSC) do have a zero tolerance and it is not the mandate to accept any racialism including discrimination or ethical exclusions to the minority people. BTSC believes that all the people have the equal right, the equal dignity and mutual respects on the areas of BTSC humanitarian interventions.
Prevention and Control of AWD/Cholera and Covid-19 are key component for BTSC’s objectives in delivering the project to the neediest community to ensure improved preventive measures in the district.

1. - Projects activities

To ensure the continuation of delivering health activities like primary health care services, and integrating related hygiene promotion practices on AWD/Cholera and COVID-19 prevention BTSC will implement this health project in Burhakaba Town and Remote Rural Villages in the district. Following population figures will be directly targeted:1. 40,000 beneficiaries (including; vulnerable women and children, marginalized and minority groups and disabled people) those are drought and crisis affected will have access to emergency health interventions through 2 HC and 2 Mobile Health Clinics in Burhakabadistrict. 2. Mobile Health Clinics will have a key contribution in supporting the people living in the hard-to-reach areas to address the needs of health services in those remote areas.3. 8000 children U5 (gender-disaggregated) reached with emergency life-saving health services in High-risk areas, Measles vaccination (disaggregated by age and Sex)4. A child-focused Health and Nutrition behavior change and social mobilization activities will be implemented to strengthen community engagement of the health care services.5. During the project course, AWD/Cholera case fatality rate to <1% when need arise6. Reduce Covid-19 impacts in the community The health prevention component will focus on delivering routine immunization services to 0-12 month’s old infants including undertaking Measles and Polio vaccination campaigns to U5 children, ANC services for pregnant women, health education of expected women on danger signs during pregnancy, health education sessions on prevention of other communicable diseases including COVID-19. BTSC will offer these services in health centres as well as one outreach Mobile Clinics. For immunizations BTSC will implement monthly outreach programs where they advocate for immunizations door to door for children who are not brought to the health facility. BTSC will also embark in mass awareness creation to ensure that people are aware of the services offered under this project hence improving uptake of health services. BTSC will collaborate with other partners to ensure that uptake of health services is improved. To prevent cholera and AWD outbreaks that are common in Burhakaba, BTSC will conduct hygiene promotion and water chlorination. At the health facility BTSC will treat moderate cases of cholera. and conduct contact tracing for disinfection, hygiene promotion and water treatment at Household level.
