1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Mueda is a transit district and an area of refuge for at least 94,000 IDPs (IOM/DTM) at the time of submission of this proposal. The overall number of CIN for the WASH sector reached 110,922 and currently, stress level for Mueda is “catastrophic”. This proposal builds on Helpcode ongoing operational presence in the Mueda, with interventions in the WASH and protection sector and on its capacity to work in H2R areas to propose a coordinated intervention with other humanitarian partners in order to ensure increasing protected and reliable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and a safe and healthy environment for IDPs and host population. The project is intended as a follow-up to the support activities carried out across 2021 and includes: access to water through water trucking included as a contingency mechanism, rehabilitation of water treatment plant and network and construction of new water points including training of recipient families on the appropriate use of treatment products / materials. Access to hygiene and sanitation and waste management with promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in long-term resettlement and returning areas.Key target group for the projecy is the IDPs population both living in sites and with assigned plots outside the camps.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activities to be implemented in the target areas include: - Construction and rehabilitation of water points- Distribution of point-of-use water treatment and purification materials / products- Provide sanitation and hygiene facilities, bathing facilities including management of solid waste in accommodation centres, resettlement sites, and communities- Training of local community to provide HH hygiene facilities- Sensibilization with community activists to reconstructing HH latrines in returning / resettlement areas- Sensibilization with community activists and media to provide sanitation and hygiene messages to prevent spread of WASH related diseases, including cholera and COVID-19 prevention

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims at mainstreaming protection for IDPs through combining community-based approaches (aimed at ensuring awareness and participation), and ad hoc training to be carried out under planned Protection interventions. Protection mainstreaming is also ensured through coordination with institutional and humanitarian partners and through the incorporation of protection principles into aid delivery. The project is built on the (still preliminary) results of protection monitoring activities carried out by Helpcode in the final quarter of 2021 in partnership with UNHCR (Mueda), which aims at identifying specific protection needs and avoid potential risks of exclusion.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Helpcode AAP’s strategy abides to IASC 5 commitments, according to the following: Leadership:- Promote a culture among staff of acceptance of failure and negative feedback from affected people. - Ensure that relevant staff and managers collect feedback from affected populations in appropriate age groups (sensitive to gender & age); respond to this feedback; and report back. - Work with local and national partners, so that there is a coordinated plan and capacity which is sensitive to gender, age, disability and ethnicity. Transparency:- Provide accessible and timely information on organizational procedures, structures and processes that may impact communities in order to support informed decisions, and engage communities in a dialogue as part of information provision Participation:- Work with relevant local and national partners and actors to support and influence effective design, assessment, implementation and monitoring of the response.- Engage all parts of a community, including those who are vulnerable or at risk to draft robust, contextual vulnerability and capacity analyses. Inclusivity is ensured through a thorough analysis of local social dynamics, mobility capacity of beneficiaries, differences in the use of time by women and men, identification of “role models” among community stakeholders that can promote social change. Feedback and complaints:- Provide information to all in affected communities about the organisation, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave (including commitments made on PSEA), the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver. - Communicate in languages, formats and media that are easily understood, respectful and culturally appropriate for different members of the community, especially vulnerable and marginalised groups.- Establish and maintain effective feedback systems, using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non-sensitive feedback. The feedback is disaggregated by age and sex at minimum. Design, monitoring and evaluation:- Assess the implementation of the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and other collective standards including the IASC AAP commitments through assessments of organizational performance on accountability to measure and improve accountability to all persons of concern.- Include persons of concern as partners throughout the operation's management cycle and report the results of assessments and follow up actions to them.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will work with the District Services of Health and in coordination with the humanitarian partners who have developed guidelines and tools for ensuring disability-inclusive protection in WASH, in order to ensure the minimum standards for disability inclusion in humanitarian action.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Among its internal procedures, Helpcode has developed an environmental policy aiming to avoid, minimize and mitigate the environmental impact of its activities in the field, in line with Sphere Humanitarian Standards and global environmental standard, guidelines and tools. In this respect, each project is assessed since design in terms of environmental impact. In this case, environmental concerns will be addressed by:- Limiting and avoiding whenever possible the use of plastic wrapping during distribution of NFI;- Ensure adequate disposal in order to avoid environmental damages.Environmental assessment will be carried out again at project kick off and at the end.It is also critical to highlight that the overall implementation context is affected by severe vulnerability to climate change and extreme climate events. In terms of mitigation / preparation, the project foresees to provide ad hoc information during training /awareness activities.
