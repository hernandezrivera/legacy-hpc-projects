Project Summary

The proposed project aims to improve the livelihoods and hygiene standards of 30,000 individuals (i.e:5000 HHs) including 8640 men, 12960 women, 4200 boys, and 4200 girls in both Luuq and Bardere districts of Gedo. This will be through improving agriculture production of 5, 0000 small-scale farmers (2500 IDPs, 2500HCs) as well as promoting healthy hygiene practices. The proposed project is based on scaling up the outcomes of HA’s previous FSL project and it will contribute to the FSL cluster objectives SO1 and SO2, as we are aiming to achieve the following:
- To improve agriculture production and self-reliance of the targeted farmers through providing assorted seeds, pesticides and fertilizers, farming tools, land preparation support, as well as capacity building on Good Agricultural Practices and business risk management.
- To promote healthy hygiene practices amongst the local communities to contain the transmission and exposure to COVID-19 through provision of hygiene kits and COVID 19 awareness.
The project will be implemented in 12 months and will achieve its objectives through the following activities:
1. 5000 small-scale farmers benefit from the distribution of agriculture tools including insecticides and knapsack sprayers, irrigation pumps, climate resilience seeds and harvesting tools.
2. 1000 (500 in Luuq & 500 Bardere) small-scale farmers benefit from land preparation support using modern technology to increase the production efficiency.
3. 28 training sessions conducted to build the capacity of 2000 farmers on the good agriculture practices.
4. 20 training sessions conducted to build the capacity of 2500 farmers on business risk management.
5. 2000 farmers will receive ToT in 20 training sessions to be able to deliver the training to additional 3000 farmers in both the communities.
6. 100 of harvesting storage facilities provided to the targeted farmers to help them store their products while preparing them for selling in the market.
7. 5000 hygiene kits will be distributed to 5000 HHs in both targeted locations.
8. 400 Communal washing points will be installed in targeted farms.
9. CVOD-19 awareness sessions will be organized reaching 5000 HHs.
The project will target 5000 farmers from the most vulnerable, and small-scale cooperative farmers who will be expected to cultivate specific farms. The project maintains a strong focus on gender mainstreaming where 74 % of beneficiaries being women & girls. Priority will be given to the most vulnerable segments of community and of supported, 40% HHs will be women or children headed; while 10% HHs will include PWD.
Farmers will be selected from the most vulnerable and most affected by the climatic hazards and the ones who don’t have the ability to purchase machineries such as the irrigation pumps or to prevent pests to improve on their farming. The vulnerability criteria will be further identified through engagement of the local communities and the concerned stakeholders. The project will also benefit, indirectly, community members who will be hired to assist farmers and also those who will benefit from the availability of agricultural food production at the end of the planting cycle.
This project will contribute to the vast unemployment in the region through providing employment opportunities and it will sufficiently increase the efficiency of agriculture production, through using modern technology that farmers can’t usually afford. The project will also help farmers and businesses that struggled to continue working through the spread of COVID 19 due to lack of safety, knowledge and tools, to carry on with their work. This is through providing them with business risk management training, hygiene kits distribution as well as awareness campaigns. Finally, the project will incorporate all the lessons HA learned from the previous similar projects, where for example we will be providing harvesting storage facilities for farmers to store their products

Project Activities

-Community mobilization and sensitization: HA will identify agriculture committees in the targeted communities. All program activities will be carried out in collaboration with agriculture committees as well as local authorities and relevant stakeholders (i.e: District commissioners, FSL clusters, Agriculture personnel’s in the region, local committees, the Jubaland federal state Administration). This will promote ownership which will have a positive impact on the sustainability of the project.-Conduct Baseline and endline surveys at the start and end of the project. -Provide land preparation support for 1000 farmers: once the targeted farmers are identified, HA-Somalia will coordinate with local vendors to prepare the lands using modern technologies that will reduce time and cost as well as increasing the production. The land size to be prepared is 1200 hectare and will be done in two phases.-Provision of insecticides and professional operators to spray 1000 farms: HA is aware of the possible harm that may be caused by the inconsiderate use of pesticides. Therefore, HA will ensure reducing the accompanied risks through following the ecologically-based approach, promoted by FAO and WHO. HA will hire professional pesticide operators who will work with farmers to spray their crops. This will protect farmers from contacting or handling the knapsack sprayers as well as the environment from inconsiderate use. HA will procure 2000L of pesticides (2L for each farm) and it will be used in two phases. -Provision of harvesting tools to 2000 farmers: toolset includes (Hoe, shovel, kawawa).-Provision 100 irrigation pumps: HA will ensure grouping farmers, who do not have access to irrigation pumps, into cooperatives so they can all share the benefits from each pump. Farmers will be grouped according to the village and location they live in. This will ensure effective use of resources. -Provision of assorted seeds to 5000 farmers: HA-Somalia will contract local vendors to supply the assorted seeds to the farmers. We will ensure engaging the farmers in choosing the type of seeds they need according to the region and agriculture production. -Provision of the following trainings: •Good Agricultural Practices (28 sessions, 2000 farmers): this will contribute to reducing the risk of harmful contamination that may cause harm to consumers. • Business risk management (20 sessions, 2500 farmers): it will help farmers to manage any risks they might face in the future including pandemics, floods, or any other risks. The need for this training occurred after many businesses struggled to mitigate the risk of COVID 19 and floods on their businesses.•ToT (20 sessions, 2000 farmers): this is to increase the efficiency of the provided training. Each trained farmer will train another 1 or 2 farmers to ensure COVID 19 safety measurements are in place. HA will ensure that COVID19 precautions will be taken into consideration in all the trainings. -Conduct multiple COVID-19 awareness session reaching 5000 people: Given the spread of COVID-19 in Somalia (according to Somalia COVID-19 impact update 3,864 cases confirmed including 60 cases in Gedo region), many farmers were struggling to continue with their cultivation work, incorporating safe measurements and some of them stopped their business for the same reason. Therefore, the project is proposing to conduct awareness sessions to mitigate the risk of COVID 19 exposure. This will help farmers to continue their work by applying safe measurements. -Distribution of 5000 hygiene kits and communal washing points: targeted HHs will receive a one-time household hygiene kit that is in line with the WASH Cluster COVID-19 Technical Guidance Hygiene kits will include (Soap bars, Hand wash gel, Disposable face masks & gloves and hand washing tanks).Each hygiene kit will include the instruction sheet in the local language. HA will install washing points for communal use at the farms to increase effectiveness

HA will ensure to promote the dignity of these groups so they can participate in the assessment, design, and implementation of the project and also mainstreaming gender equality.• As part of AAP mechanisms, HA-Somalia will prioritize the concerns of persons with disabilities, to ensure that the response is inclusive and appropriate.
