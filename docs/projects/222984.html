Project Summary

Considering the current situation (volatile security situation, limited access to the islands, need for security, increasing number of IDPs, and raining season approaching), Istituto Oikos plans to support about 9,620 people of Ibo District by providing emergency shelter kit, trainings to skilled and unskilled manpower and technical support for safe shelter construction; emergency rehabilitation of community infrastructure and shelter reconstruction.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project has the following objective: 1) Provide adequate and contextualized shelter and non food items life saving assistance responding to the immediate needs of most vulnerable crisis affected population; 2) Provide sustainable shelter and essential non-food items support promoting selfrecovery and access to livelihoods for most vulnerable crisis affected population; 3) Strengthen the Shelter and NFIs assessment, analysis and implementation capacity through community participatory approaches, to support quality emergency and early recovery response, and reinforce the Accountability to Affected Population Beneficiaries: Based on the guidance from protection cluster, vulnerability criteria will be adopted for the selection of beneficiaries. Totally the project will target 9620 inhabitants.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The following activities are foreseen: 1- Selection of beneficiaries: The selection will be done based on the following criteria - number of people in the family, number of children under 5, existence of family members with physical or mental disability, number of elderly people and single mother as head of household with children. Based on the above, with KOBO, Istituto Oikos field team will carry out an assessment of all the IDPs and their level of vulnerability and produce priority lists from the collected data.2- Provision of Emergency Shelters Kits: The project will take care of the transport and distribution of kit to be delivered to the identified beneficiaries for a total of 600 people 3- Training and technical support for safe shelter construction: Qualified project staff will provide theoretical and practical classes to 600 people, where basic Resilient Construction Techniques will be explained and demonstrated. At the end of the training a test will be taken to check the level of learning. Assistance will be guaranteed throughout the project to all beneficiaries trained and involved in the construction activities4- Technical Trainings for 70 local artisans: A dedicated training course will be conducted for skilled local artisans to improve their knowledge and local skills. Artisans will be then contracted for the implementation of the construction activities5- Emergency rehabilitation of community infrastructure: the project will rehabilitate 5 existing community infrastructure in Ibo distric which is used for a broad range of services designed to protect or promote mental health and provide support to individuals who are experiencing psychological distress or social difficulties6- Early recovery/Temporary/Transitional shelters: in order to meet the need of shelter for the most vulnerable not only offering temporary refuges but potential homes for the future, restoring dignity, security, and hope to the displaced families, The project will build 600 ES Type B. Key steps:Adopt the Owner Driven Approach methodology which promotes the participation of the beneficiaries in the construction of their own shelters, providing a construction training with a self-help concept. Distribute a construction tool kit for each HH teaching them how to use each tool in the shelter construction process. Promote traditional construction techniques using only four types of material Introduce a shelter design which is easy to build, resistant and cool enough thanks to the ventilation provided by the design, allowing also possible evolution/additions by the beneficiariesPromote the dissemination of improved know-how on resilient & BBB safer construction and maintenance techniques 7- Shelter Reconstruction (retrofitting, improvements repairs and upgrades): additional reconstruction activities will be delivered as follow: Shelter Reconstruction / Retrofitting focuses on making the existing shelter more resilient to future hazards (e.g., cyclone, strong winds). It targets structural and non-structural components critical to the shelter’s ability to withstand specific disasters and involves technical modifications or additions to an existing structure to improve its safety performance. IO team will carry out an assessment to verify the status of the existing ES in Ibo and technical team will intervene in strengthening walls, roofs, or adding bracing systems or anchors for about 620 shelterShelter Reconstruction / Improvements, repairs, and upgrades focuses on restoring, enhancing, shelter’s usability and functionality. It includes functional, or minor structural repairs that improve living conditions. This involves maintenance or minor enhancements, replacing deteriorated materials. Based on the shelter assessment, priority will be given to shelters in need of improvement that belong to the most vulnerable families for a total of 590 shelters

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The provincial and districtal infrastructures services DPOPHRH and SDPI in the selected districts for implementation of the project activities will work with assistance from the shelter cluster and active partners in Cabo Delgado and utilize existing feedback mechanisms such as Linha Verde and Linha Fala Criança for which specific pre-formulated questions and replies already do exist. Therefore, the DPOPHRH, SDPI and partners will be aware and can follow up about any comment on the distribution or availability and quality of the services and products provided, and of specific requests for information.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus gender groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adoption.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Local Institutional stakeholders will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities. The technical support offered to the vulnerable families will take into account the special needs of people with disabilities, conceiving the shelter structures accordingly to the evaluation of the specific situation.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will take into account some environmental considerations that can come up during the NFI distribution (such as the production of waste) or linked to the construction material provenience. The project identified the following solutions: ● During distributions activities, the population and other beneficiaries will be taught the correct way to dispose the waste produced (such as plastic bags and packaging) in a way that would not be prejudicial to the environment together with technics of how use this waste in the resilient house construction taking into advance the material for construction works; ● All the construction material will be provided assuring its sustainable provenience and avoiding to encourage the illegal traffic of wood, in order to prevent the exploitation of the resources of the QNP (Quirimbas National Park) ● The project will avoid to promote material coming from non-renewable resources and it will ensure a minimal use of non-re-used material to reduce mass waste production in IDPs accommodation sites and host communities.
