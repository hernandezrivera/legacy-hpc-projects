Project Summary

akaful has designed this program in order to establish a network of competitive and innovative agricultural value chains in the targeted locations, (Afrin, Sheikh El-Hadid, Sharan, Atareb, Daret Azzaand Dana, so that will contribute to Support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets, restoring and creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives, also Improve communities' capacity to sustain households' livelihoods by improving linkages with value chain through the rehabilitation/building of productive infrastructure this will achieved through three main interventions:
First: supporting 880 farmers (males and females), 4400 individuals (1012 M, 1056 W, 1320 B, 1012 G) who are the most vulnerable and have already a cultivated land with wheat at least 1 hectare by providing them with the needed agricultural supplies for 1 hectare (fertilizers, pesticides, supplementary irrigation support) using restricted vouchers, as well as, income generation through the marketing of all harvests of the project's beneficiaries in fair price and prevent the dealers' monopoly of wheat, where We have signed an MOU with Syrian public establishment for Grain (SPEG) which obliges SPEG to buy all harvests of the project's beneficiaries with a fair price. Further, we will conduct the related training programs "the best practices in agriculture, irrigation and marketing" as well as providing technical support for farmers who cultivated wheat throughout regular field visits. Second: The rehabilitation for food community asset "set of silo" which will complete one value chain in the project areas and enhance food security and increasing resilience of the community, the silos consider the only a safe place to keep grain away from tampering with birds, insects and erosion factors and the percentage of loss to zero. Silos also apply the rules and health requirements that make the stock suitable for human use after the storage period, so that SPEG can buy and store the farmers' harvest. the project includes also the light rehabilitation of major artery, access and feeder roads to the silos, where the difficulty of transporting goods especially the wheat continues to jeopardize the food security of the affected people in the project area. The Afrin district's Wheat farmers and their families, 71656 individuals out of 229667 "as 60% of Afrin HHs works in agriculture and 52% of them grow wheat" (16481 M, 17197 W, 21497 B, 16481 G) will benefit from rehab of silos set.
Third: Supporting the most vulnerable IDPs and HCs rebuild their livelihoods and restore the food security-related community assets to service to gain access to food production and lives saving of population through Cash For Work component, which will target 300 males and females of skilled and unskilled laborer (including 5 persons with disabilities) who lost their livelihoods, where we will provide 20 work opportunities for skilled labor who will work in the rehabilitation (silos, road) for 2 months, and 40 work opportunities for unskilled labor who will work in the SPEG (silos, mills, Bakeries- PWD will work in frame of office works) for three paid months, where SPEG will commit hiring those workers for at least other 3 months after closing the project. To ensure local community support, KIs including local actors and IDPs representatives in each targeted location were interviewed to take their suggestions and will be informed about the project's goals and activities at the beginning of the project. We will coordinate with FSL and clusters in all implementation areas. So, in case we were not able to run project activities in the mentioned areas, we will share our updates, and the needs assessment with clusters and donor to choose another location.

4 - Needs

An estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. Some 70 per cent of the assessed households reported that food accounted for more than 65 per cent of their expenditure. More than 37 per cent of food insecure households reported having outstanding debt and 57 per cent of food insecure people had adopted negative coping strategies. (HNO 2020). Takaful Alsham Assessment team conducted agriculture RNA in the targeted sub-districts Afrin, Sheikh El-Hadid, Sharan. The cultivated areas in the targeted locations depend on 31% irrigation and 69% on rain-fed. The highest irrigated percentage was 40% in Janderis inAfrin District and the lowest 15% in Daret Azza subdistrict. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measuresAssumption 1: The Information that the project built on it is true and neutrality. Risk: there is a risk of giving incorrect data to our team. Probability: Low Impact: High Mitigation strategies (MS): Our team will take in consideration IDPs tracking tables from CCCM and after that will crosscheck the data from local relief offices in the targeted location in addition to site visits to samples of supposed beneficiaries. Assumption 2: Work environment is safe and no risk threats workers in work locations. Risk: workers may be subjected to risks threats their life in work location. Probability: Low. Impact: High MS: Workers will receive training on safety and security procedures, in addition, our security team will monitor the situation and report if there is any potential risk. Assumption 3: The activities are done according to planed timeline. Risk: Because of weather in winter may causes a delay in some activities. Probability: Medium Impact: Medium Mitigation strategies (MS): when designing work plan and the timeline we will consider the weather and its effect. Assumption 4: All roads are open and there is a full safe access to targeted location\s Risk: Road closures and lack of access to targeted locations. Probability: low Impact: High Mitigation strategies (MS): Delay the distribution for several days pending the calm situation - develop an alternative plan to deliver assistance to the beneficiaries in the new situation - ask suppliers to secure the material long enough.
