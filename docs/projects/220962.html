Project Summary

Africa Development Aid intends to provide WASH response e integrating it with nutrition and health in Fangak, Ayod,Nasir,Canal/Pgi,Malakal,Renk,Mayendit and Panyikang Counties In 2025, Africa Development Aid will focus in 8 counties of priority one and the project will contribute to reducing WASH related morbidity and mortality in the target location with improved access to sanitation and hygiene services integrating with nutrition programing. The project will enable access to safe water through rehabilitation/construction of 78 water points with resilient flood designs if require at nutrition and at community level(40 existing boreholes,20 new boreholes,8 emergency SWATs and 10 motorized water yards. A total of 1304 SAM/children/caretakers and PLWs will be provided with WASH NFI kits paired with hygiene promotion targeting 43 nutrition facilities will have improved access to safe water sources. Provision of 55 latrines stances will be rehabilitated/constructed with flood resilient designs if require at nutrition and community level. The project will improve access to 42004 people with access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene in the targeted counties by year 2025 and funding require for this response is USD 1,680,000, Under this response total of 42004 people will be reached with hygiene key messages in the targeted locations.

1. Needs

. Based on the several assessments conducted in some counties mentioned in this project revealed that significant number of sanitation facilities have been destroyed unusable due to flooding, and this have increased in open defecation practices especially around the constructed dikes and this could lead to contamination of water bodies. Flooding disrupted routine hygiene promotion coupled with lack of soaps. If nothing is done this could lead to outbreak of diseases such as cholera and diarrhea (IRNA Fangak County September 2024).According to the IPC AMN (October 2024 to March 2025), a total of 38 counties are classified in IPC Phase 4, 15 counties are in Phase 3, 19 counties are in Phase 2, and 8 counties are in Phase 1. 1 - Acceptable 2 - Alert 3 - Serious 4 - Critical 5 level. The IPC, AMN Classification Phase remains similar in 76 out of the 80 counties .among the counties classified in Phase 4, 24 of the 38 counties are located in Greater Upper Nile re which include Jonglei state,Upper Nile and Unity State where ADA is intends to respond in six Counties namely;(Ayod,Fangak and Pigi/canal),in Upper Nile (Nasir,Renk,Malakal and Panyikang and Unity (Mayendit respectively.A significant portion of the population (41 percent) lacks access to improved water sources, and 37 percent need to travel over 30 minutes to reach their primary water source, posing particular hardships for persons with disabilities and making women and girls more vulnerable to GBV. Access to sanitation is even more limited with prevalent open defecation, as 88 percent of the population lacks access to improved latrines, according to the 2023 FSNMS data. The compounding challenges of displacement, insecurity, flooding, economic difficulties, and the influx of returnees from Sudan further strain the already limited resources.According to the report the contributing factors of acute malnutrition include high prevalence of diseases with 49.4% of children reported to have been ill two weeks prior to the FSNMS Round 30 assessment and 44out of 80 counties reported illness among 50% or more children. Diarrhea ,fever and cough were leading symptoms reported at 18.3,38,and 20.3% .Poor access to sanitation and drinking water compromises hygiene and increases the risk of high incidence of illness leading to acute malnutrition .The report further said only 9.9% of the population reported having access to improved sanitation facilities and 74.3% reported using open defecation .Overall,63% of the population reported having access to improved sources of water leaving 37% of the population depending on unsafe water sources. Africa Deveopment Aid, WASH will conduct safety audit to address WASH related GBV ensuring that there is disability inclusion that people with disability to have access to WASH services

• Provide 1304 WASH NFI kits to SA/children/caretakers paired with hygiene promotion targeting nutrition facilities• Construct/rehabilitate 78 water points at nutrition facilities (28) and 50 at community level (20 new boreholes,40 existing boreholes ,10 motorized solar water yards and 8 Emergency SWATS targeting crowded areas with flood resilient designs.• Construct/rehabilitate 55 latrines stances segregated by gender and sex at nutrition facilities and at community level with flood resilient designs if require.• A total of 2575 Households will be reached through distribution of WASH NFI kits during emergency responses.
