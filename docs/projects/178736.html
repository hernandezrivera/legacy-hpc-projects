Project Summary

The planned project will support 2 participatory irrigation projects in Aleppo and Idleb
(i.e. existing systems) in vulnerable communities by rehabilitating irrigation systems through providing and maintaining water distribution pipes, providing water pumps, electricity generator sets, repairing electricity network and providing electrical panels to irrigate 200 hectares. In addition to provide and install one
additional participatory irrigation project solar panels with required electricity equipment as an energy source for water pumps to irrigate 50 hectares in Jarablus.
The project will rehabilitate a bakery in a high severity community managed by the local council, the rehabilitation work will include the bakery premises, electricity network, and about 3,000 meters of access road connecting the major mill-bakery-main route, in addition to repair, provide and install bread baking equipment to increase bakery productivity 8 MT of flour per day.
The project will recover the community capacity throughout rehabilitating basic service and infrastructure of a war affected community.
The project will assist and contribute to the protection of the most vulnerable groups of women, men, girls and boys in all age groups and disabled people in the targeted communities, moreover beneficiaries have been involved in project designing and implementation through a close coordination with local council, elected WUA management board members and community representatives, also beneficiaries will be
engaged in evaluation through satisfaction survey and complaint mechanism.

4 - Needs

5 - Implementation of activities

The project intervention will be throughout: - Providing and installing water pumps, control panels, elec. cables, water pipelines and electrical generators to operate and rehabilitate 2 participatory irrigation projects. - Providing and installing solar panels to operate one participatory irrigation project. - Rehabilitating one bakery and providing and installing bread producing equipment.. - Rehabilitating 3 Km of roads connecting bakery with mills and main routes. Project implementation cycle will start from coordination with the communities representatives and local councils, performing market assessments. locations selection will be built according to the communities severity. Qatar Red crescent society will implement the project by Syrian expertise through an infield team with wide knowledge of the Syrian context and belong to the targeted communities, all members have high awareness and long experience in the humanitarian service providing and had been trained on GBV, PSEA, doing no harm, protection and security aspects. Trained M&E team will be assigned/hired locally on this project who will be charged with collecting information regarding beneficiaries and data analysis and will also closely monitor and verify the services and renovations rendered to beneficiaries to ensure correctness, timeliness and efficiency.An end impact phase will also be undertaken to obtain beneficiary feedback on quality, quantity and further needs for the services/goods received. Monitoring will involve sporadic and regular visits to the projectsites, interviewing staff and beneficiaries regarding the activities and continuously feeding back observations to the project manager in order to promptly take mitigation or corrective action
