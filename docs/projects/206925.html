Project Summary

The North West and South West Regions of Cameroon have been in an armed conflict since 2017. There are multitudes of Non State Armed Groups (NSAGs) fighting with state security officers within the North West Regions of Cameroon. Within the armed conflict state security officers separated from their families have been assigned to confront the growing influence of NSAGs in localities without prior military presence. It is unclear how many armed men (both NSAGs and state security) are actively engaged in hostilities within the North West Region, but they can be estimated in their thousands.
There have been multiple cases of (1) members of non-state armed groups holding girls/young women in captivity for domestic servitude and sexual exploitation; (2) state security agents using legal control operations to engage children, girls and young women in coercive sexual exploitation; and (3) belligerent of the conflict victimizing any girl/woman suspected of being in a sexual relationship with a rival combatant group, considering her as the enemy whereas she is actually a victim of conflict related sexual violence (CRSV).
The armed conflict has engendered a growing wave of conflict-related pregnancies resulting in a growing number of children fathered by belligerents of the conflict. Survivors of conflict related sexual violence including children born as a result live in constant fear of further violence and prolonged sexual exploitation, slavery, and suffer considerable neglect/stigmatization based on the circumstances of their pregnancies and births. Survivors do not self-identify as victims and are not motivated to seek support. Their social support network is largely limited to the combatants. this association with the armed groups further makes the cycle of their sexual exploitation to expand once the abuser dies or is transferred to another location.
Without a source of income and lacking social/emotional support from their surroundings, some survivors regard the children as "dream killers". A situation which seriously jeopardize the mother – child bond
In 2022, GLOWA started a program to promote the rights of children born from conflict related sexual violence, promoting the healing and empowerment of the survivors single mothers through vocational training and livelihood activities. The execution of this initiative has identified the establishment of a child safe space within the vocational training center as a vital component of the healing and empowerment process of survivors of prolonged conflict related sexual exploitation that bear children for combatants. The distraction from toddlers hanging on the backs and laps of program participants during individual and/or group empowerment activities has equally been enormous. Similarly, the movement of these toddlers around the vocational training environment equally possesses safety and security risk for them. In respond, GLOWA is proposing a closed child safe space within the premises open only to the children born of conflict related sexual violence whose mothers participate in our healing and empowerment program.
The closed child safe space project shall facilitate the recovery and livelihood development of the participants at GLOWAHaus by reducing the distractions and anxieties created by crying and/or straying babies. It shall equally facilitate the development of vital pre-school skills for children 0-4 years whose moms participate in our recovery program. It is estimated that the children shall stay in the program during the time their mothers are actively participating in it.
The presence of the child safe space shall serve as additional incentive to program participants to enroll and participant in the program to its logical end. This will address the current rate of absenteeism that is related to fending for themselves and childcare at home.
