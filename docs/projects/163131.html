Project Summary

Africa Millennium Development Network has her Head Office in Bangem, adjacent former divisional delegation of basic education for Kupe Muanenguba. For the purpose of effective coordination, each of the sub divisions of Kupe Muanenguba has has an office. Kupe Muanenguba Division is made up of Nguti, Tombel and Bangem sub division. Africa Millennium Development Network (AFRINET) was created in 2008 with its headquarters in Bangem Sub-Division of Kupe Muanenguba. Before the outbreak of the crisis, AFRINET worked mainly with funding from International organizations like Water Collective, Dig Deepwater Global Fund for Women and community elites to carry out advocacy, sensitization and education on Gender Based Violence and Sexual and Reproductive Health, and WASH.
With the arrival of the crisis, AFRINET gained support from the UN and other international partners like UNFPA, UNICEF and Doctors without Borders in terms of capacity building to operate as a humanitarian partner. The organization currently participates in the GBV, SRH, WASH, CP AoR sub-cluster and the humanitarian working group.
AFRINET has received lots of trainings on Protection on humanitarian work notable in the GBV Sub Cluster as follows:
Case Management on GBV, BeMONC on SRH, Elaboration and validation of SOP, HNO and GBVIMS. With the partnership with Doctors without Borders, AFRINET through her (03 midwives) medical staff is able to reach IDPs in hard-to-reach places in the enclave Muanenguba Division notably covering Tombel, Nguti and Bangem Sub Divisions through a monthly consignment of drugs to fight STIs and promote Sexual Reproductive Health. Child protection activities ar not left out during interventions
To date, AFRINET is able to work in the context of humanitarian development nexus. Presently, we are the only organizations working on Humanitarian Response in Kupe Muanenguba Division
AFRINET has a staff of 25 workers, excluding volunteers. As a membership organization AFRINET has a membership of 467 members from all works of life who willingly contribute to the development of the organization sparing their time to the benefit of those in need of their services
Consequently, the ongoing humanitarian response project is interwoven as follows:
During GBV interventions, children are mostly victims of circumstances thereby calling for Child protection. In all IDP hideouts we have been in Kupe Muanenguba, water, sanitation and hygiene are the promoters of ill health. These people live under trunks of trees, caves consuming water from very doubtful sources. The closure of health facilities encouraged our partnership with Doctors without Borders to assist through our medical staff a consignment of drugs each month to treat cases of rape, sexual harassment etc. That’s the reason our interventions are tailored to these three components notably GBV, SRH, WASH and CP AoR
Even as most communities have been set ablaze, we encourage IDPs to return to their home-villages because the forest is not their place.
Our field staffs in the three Sub Divisions know all hideouts of IDPs in the forests. Our project targets 12.000 people
