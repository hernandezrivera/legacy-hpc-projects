Project Summary

The project is intended to provide treatment and prevention services to children under5 and PLW in Magumeri, Bama, Dikwa and Ngala LGAs, through the establishment and running of various OTP centers where children with severe acute malnutrition will be treated with RUTF. These OTP sites will also be sites where children with moderate acute malnutrition will be supplemented with micro-nutrient powder. In addition, INTERSOS has established a stabilization center in Magumeri LGA for the treatment of children with complicated Severe Acute Malnutrition. The project intends to mainstream gender-based violence risk mitigation strategies in all of its different parts.

1 - Needs

The humanitarian situation in the North-East of Nigeria has remained critical since the onset of the conflict between Armed Opposition Groups and Security Forces about eight years ago. This has resulted in the displacement of more than 1 million individuals to date in the BAY states of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe. INTERSOS has been responding to the needs of these individuals since 2016 in various locations in Magumeri, Bama, Dikwa and Ngala and has nutrition activities specifically in Magumeri and Bama who have been noted to be food insecure, Despite all of INTERSOS activities, rates of malnutrition have remained precarious in all locations. In Magumeri LGA, the most recent mass MUAC screening in August 2019 identi ed children aged from 6-59 month ,1,336 SAM cases were detected by MUAC, 476 with bilateral edema and 3,960 MAM cases. BAMA had the highest rate of edema, 1.8% of children (n=255), Ngala, 0.9% (n=114), Magumeri 0.6%(n=76) and Dikwa 0.5%(n=31). The malnutrition problem has been been associated with a number of causal determinants, from the lack of access to food and health interventions for the beneficiaries,also due to the persistent insecurity and lack of humanitarian access,social determinants such as poverty, lack of education and various sociocultural factors which encourage patriarchy and place men as the sole decision makers in most family settings. Referral for stabilization center care from Magumeri LGA has also been a huge challenge as the predominant sociocultural factors that place men as sole decision makers result in a lot of referral requests refused by the husbands of most care-givers. In Bama and Magumeri recent SMART survey conducted by INTERSOS,The study estimated the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in Bama (WHZ-9.9%, MUAC-10.3%) and Magumeri (WHZ-10.3%, MUAC-10.5%). Prevalence of underweight is estimated as 25.7% and 38.4% in Bama and Magumeri respectively, ),both of which are classified in Serious nutrition situation. Dikwa and Ngala LGAs are also hotspots for migration according to the IOM’s displacement tracking matrix and INTERSOS has also continued to intervene in health and nutrition in these areas through the provision of primary healthcare and facility-based counselling sessions for Infant and Young Child Feeding as well as through Mother-Support Groups.

2 - Activities

INTERSOS is continuing its nutrition activities in both LGAs through the provision of a global package of CMAM. This is through the maintenance of the thirteen OTP sites in Magumeri LGA and 1 OTP in Bama LGA, a stabilization center located in the Magumeri General Hospital supported by the HMB of the Borno SMoH and USAID/OFDA as well as continuing standard IYCF Practices through supporting various Mother Support Groups (MSGs) as well as ensuring MNP supplementation at the various OTP sites through the continuing support of OFDA. In Bama, INTERSOS will continue to provide outpatient care to children with SAM in the host community as well as the camp IDPs. INTERSOS will also continue facility based IYCF in Magumeri stabilization center as well as its clinic in the IDP camp. In Dikwa and Ngala and link the identied MAM cases with the FSL actors in the respective locations for case mangment. INTERSOS will also continue its facility-based one-on-one IYCF counselling at the health facilities supported by the organization. All OTP services as well as MNP supplementation will continue to be supported in-kind by UNICEF supported by USAID/OFDA. INTERSOS proposed intervention will be targeting primarily children under five with treatment services for malnutrition with the support of WHO through the provision of SAM kits and IMAM training of staffs. The proposed action would contribute to mitigating access constraints to both health and nutrition services especially for children under-five. As this project is also linked to INTERSOS other health, WaSH and protection interventions, it will be a multi-sectoral intervention that will aim to tackle all of the gender-induced constraints mentioned above. Through this project, INTERSOS will also mainstream services for survivors of GBV. These services will act to link some survivors of GBV into the criteria for admission into support structures for mothers of children with severe acute malnutrition for income generating activities and short-term skill training activities. The project will also engage in community health and hygiene promotion as well as mass muac screening through volunteers who are selected locally from the community and this will function to improve its community acceptance and engagement and ensure dissemination of IYCF and GBV messages to all individuals.INTERSOS will also engage with the community through focus group discussion with men and women,through community leaders workshop,through men agent of change and mother to mothers support group.INTERSOS will also engage in house -house hygiene promotion and health messages.INTERSOS will engage in various survey which will help to measure the impact and needs met,the survey would also involve objectively measuring rates of malnutrition in different project locations,which will show the impact and effectiveness of the Nutrition activities.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The nature of the services offered is an opportunity to receive community feedback/complaints and actively seek the views of the populations to improve where necessary, and at the same time ensure that feedback mechanisms for affected population are appropriate, streamline, and robust enough to deal with complaints and dissatisfaction. INTERSOS will conduct a KAP survey and small-scale household surveys to monitor the impact of the intervention, giving to the community space to share their thoughts.The goals and objectives of the program will be designed, monitored, and evaluated with the involvement of the affected population by feeding lessons back into the organization on an ongoing basis and at the same time reporting on the results of the process. Furthermore, accountability towards beneficiaries is guaranteed through the application of internal tools, such as the PSEA Policy, Child Protection Policy, Code of Conduct and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs): as for the latter, INTERSOS SOPs on Data Protection and SOPs on Complaint Mechanism will be particularly relevant for the project. These tools will also ensure vulnerable groups that equal and impartial access to the services were provided. Beneficiaries will be provided with the contact details of focal points and informed about the complaint mechanisms available.
