Project Summary

The intervention is aligned with HRP 2022 Strategic Objectives 1, 2, 3 is intended to complement nutrition sector efforts in Idleb and Hama.The project aims to implement ad support life-saving services for the treatment, preventive and awareness activities targeting children under five years and PLWs in Hama(Suran -As-Suqaylabiyah-Karnaz-Muhrada) &Idleb(Khan Shaykun)for two years (2022-2023) to reach for 79396 beneficiaries in 2022 and 79318 beneficiaries estimated for 2023. In addition to address some of the key determinants of malnutrition by promoting improved IYCF practices and healthy behaviors associated with unnecessary deaths among vulnerable populations in the above areas. By integrating the CMAM program with the implementing partner's existing PHC clinics in the targeted areas, which will be supported by an outreach team to screen and identify cases of malnutrition among boys, girls, and women among IDPs and the host community.CMAM clinic will also be established in the target areas supported by a team of trained volunteers from the project implementing partner to address the unmet needs of acutely malnourished boys, girls and women among vulnerable returnees, refugees and IDPs in the target areas. With coordination with the nutrition sector ,TdH will focus on equitable and inclusive access to essential, life-saving humanitarian assistance based on vulnerability targeting. As well TdH will focus on identification of malnutrition cases through screening with appropriate referral.The targeted areas do not have CVA interventions , for that reason TdH will provide CVA to ensure social protection with cash availability for vulnerable households. The focus will be on improving availability of diverse nutritious food for 105 PLWs with MAM cases and who at risk to be MAM in 2022 and 104 PLWs in 2023 to improve maternal dietary diversity .The project will provide focused training for local partner and Ministry of Health volunteers to strengthen their capacity to provide structured CMAM and IYCF services and activities to those in need, case management and referrals, in addition to the capacity building activities for local partners through training of volunteers and community members, and support to facilities and communities for 1-counseling on infant and young child feeding practices recommended by WHO. be divided into urban and rural areas, with coordination with existing partners (NGOs) and local partners to identify and divide sectors for outreach activities. In terms of treatment, the local partners' centers are located in the targeted areas and are accessible to beneficiaries according to Sphere standards. Also, the distribution of PPE (personal protective equipment) to volunteers, staff and beneficiaries as a protective measure for work in the event of an outbreak of the disease COVID. All activities will be coordinated with the nutrition sector and working groups to avoid overlap or duplication as TdH is an active member of the nutrition sector.Routine data will continue to be monitored using the revised 4Ws tool to measure progress towards planned targets. All the activities will be supervised on-site by a TdH staff and will be carried out in close coordination with local partner in order to guarantee a continuous and fruitful collaboration.TdH Project Manager and M&E Officer will conduct field visits on a regular basis and dealing with process monitoring basically, the methods that will be used to ensure implementing partners and donors know whether the beneficiaries have been targeted properly and whether they are receiving the correct services in a timely manner through monitoring the efficiency, effectiveness and accountability of the project.
Track program indicators according to work plan and logical framework, report progress and highlight areas of concerns. AAP participatory process will continue to fully engage communities and provide qualitative information to complement the quantitative data.

4 - Needs

In Syria, poor nutrition situation is largely attributed to the continued exposure of children and mothers to unsafe living conditions, diseases including COVID-19, suboptimal IYCF practices, high maternal malnutrition, economic downturn, low purchasing power, poverty, food insecurity, family separations and poor care practices, early marriages , and destruction of infrastructure causing limited access to health services and depletion of skilled human resources, and inadequate sanitation and water supply (Nutrition Sector,2022-23 HRP Sector Chapter Template (DRAFT). In 2021 the nutrition sector observed an increasing trend in malnutrition compared to 2019. According to the SMART survey report, July, 2021) the prevalence of acute wasting MUAC< 125 mm and/or bilateral pitting oedema) increased from 0.9% to 4.7% since the2019 SMART survey results.As per HNO 2022- Nutrition Sector Severity, the total population in the project two targeted locations: Hama (Suran, As-Suqaylabiyah, Karnaz and Muhradah) and Idleb (Khan Shaykun) is 126,474 people including 1,167 children suffer from acute malnutrition and 1,897 PLWs suffer from MAM. Therefore, TdH will provide nutrition services to vulnerable mothers and children<5 in the mentioned areas based on severity of needs 4 and 5. As well, the targeted areas do not have CVA interventions, for that reason TdH will provide CVA to ensure social protection with cash availability for vulnerable households that have PLWs with MAM cases.

Activities to be implemented in the project includes:Support facility- and community-based screening for acute malnutrition, Support treatment of acutely malnourished children with MAM, Support treatment of acutely malnourished children with SAMSupport treatment of acutely malnourished children. - Support treatment of acutely malnourished PLWsCounselling on WHO recommended Infant and Young Child Feeding practicePLW being reached with IYCF in child protection sessions like mother-toddler and positive parenting, as well as community outreachProvide micronutrient supplementation to children for prevention of micronutrient deficiencies.Provide micronutrient supplementation to women for prevention of micronutrient deficienciesTrain and support center staff as needed for IYCF and psychosocial care practices on two separated trainingsM&E activities include field visits on a regular basis and dealing with process monitoring basically, the methods that will be used to ensure implementing partners and donors know whether the beneficiaries have been targeted properly and whether they are receiving the correct services in a timely manner through monitoring the efficiency, effectiveness and accountability of the project. Along with 4Ws, quarterly reports and spot checks.the project will be linked to other TdH interventions in food and Health mainly in addition to the distribution of PPEs that is linked to WASH in areas of implementation to have more impact and sustainability.

M1: TdH will consider thispoint upon the selection of the center location and specifications in addition to theinvolvement of women and girls in the activities timing and center shifts to ensure theiraccessibility during the day. PR2: The volunteers may be not understand the culturalcontext . M2: TdH will ensure female-to female communication, and male to malecommunication between staff and beneficiaries to respect the cultural background of thearea of intervention and improving access to services, majority of the volunteers in IYCF &CMAM are females for the sensitivity of the topics , in addition TdH will ensure thatmajority of staff and volunteers will be selected from the same area of intervention tohave community acceptance, and culture background. PR3: COVID outbreak during the activities M3: TdH will provide PPEs to staff and beneficiaries during the activities , and will ensure dividing the beneficiaries into different shifts and groups to avoid any potential gathering PR4: abuse from staff to beneficiary during the project implementation. M3: TDH has different banking accounts in Syria with different pipelines to receive the fund from different sources. - R4: new quarantine by the local authorities due to COVID outbreak . M4: TdH has on-line policy for approvals and working from home, also, the PHCs will follow COVID measurement protocols , in addition, TdH health Advisor will conduct training to TdH staffand local partner volunteers for COVID measurements during humanitarian response and will distribute PPEs to both beneficiaries and staff. , Fraud Usage of cash assistance by households.
