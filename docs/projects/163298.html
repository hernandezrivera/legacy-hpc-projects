Project Summary

The Project will Contribute to the immediate life-saving needs and development of sustainable services for the reduction of morbidity and mortality among IDPs, returnee and affected host community men, women, boys and girls in East Darfur, South Darfur, South Kordufan, and Kassala, the Nutriton program will be integrated with other multi sector project, Health, WASH and FSL.

1 - Needs

Humanitarian need remains critical for 3.66 million people in Sudan, due to ongoing conﬂict between government armed forces and rebel groups; inter tribal ﬁghting and the recent unanticipated inﬂux of refugees from South Sudan 1.2 Millions of south Sudanese refugees are currently taking refugee’s settlement in Sudan. The displaced live in camps, among host communities, and in rural areas leaving most reliant on humanitarian assistance and placing an additional burden on the existing resources within host communities. On the other hand the conﬂicted affected people in South kurdufan are in high need to health services. In the four CIS operation areas (South Darfur, East Darfur, South Kordufan, and Kassala states) the health and Nutrition facilities are not suﬃcient compare with the population, and the health and nutrition facilities which are running are lacking drugs, staﬃng, and operation cost. CIS Nutrition project will contributed effectively in the live saving through provision of quality Nutrition services to IDPs, returnees and host community, CIS approach to provide integrated nutrition services with other sectors, Health, WASH, and livelihood activities Malnutrition is chronic in Sudan due recurrent internal conﬂict and unrest, drought, limited access to health care and lack of safe drinking water. The crisis and related displacement has also had serious implications on the nutrition of women, boys and girls. The malnutrition rates among displaced populations in East Darfur’s, South Darfur, and South Kurdufan, and Kassala are extremely high. The new IDPs and South Sudan refugee inﬂux in East Darfur and and South Kurdufan have exacerbated the problem and malnutrition status has deteriorated. Those affected with SAM and MAM in these vulnerable communities do not have access to critical nutrition services following the sudden departure of the organization responsible for nutrition in this area. CARE is well placed to ﬁll this critical gap and provide a comprehensive response to help meet the nutritional needs of children under ﬁve and pregnant and lactating women. CIS is planning to provide quality CMAM and IYCF services to response to the nutrition need in high priority localities in South Darfur, East Darfur, and South Kurdufan. this include IDPs camps, returnees areas, and Host community, in South Darfur [ Kass locality: IDPs & Host community, Shataia locality: IDPs/ returnees , Beilal locality: DPs & Host community , Shargalgabal locality: IDPs/ returnees and Host community, Buram locality:, and Alsalam, Kalama IDPs camps in Nyala locality]. East Darfur state [ Eddein locality IDPs and Host community, Abukarynka Locality, Assalyaia Locality, Baharalarab Locality], South Kurdufan state: Kadugli Locality, Alabasia Locality, Rashad locality, Alliri Locality, and Abujebaha Locality], Kassala (Aroma, Khashmalgirba)

2 - Activities / Outputs

CIS is planning to run full package of CMAM & IYCF services in 20 CMAM sites the activities include the following: Provision of CMAM SAM services to treat sever acute malnutrition to 4247 SAM children, (3861 in OTPs, and 386 in SCs) Provision of CMAM MAM services to treat Moderate acute malnutrition to (15444 children 6 59 Months, and 4680 Pregnant, Lactating women). established 20 MSGs to provision of IYCF services to 78000 People, men and women. provide basic construction/ rehabilitation to 20 CMAM centers. conduct quarterly nutrition assessment/ MUAC screening in 20 location. training of community volunteers and established 20 community health and nutrition committees (CHNC)

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will be will contribute in environmental and natural resource protection; CARE ensures that 100% of supported health facilities, are implementing environmental protection measures; reliable sanitation services, safe waste disposal Standard Operating Procedures, and maintaining functioning incinerators. CARE will ensure all the relevant staff (especially nurses and cleaners) are trained in infection control and waste management, using the safety boxes to transport waste to the incinerators. in facilities where there is no incinerators, CIS will construct quality incinerator to ensure proper medical waste management. also CIS is planning to plant trees in all health facilities,
