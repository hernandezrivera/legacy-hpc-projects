Project Summary

The population of the North West and South West regions of Cameroon for close to 6 years running and have been under a serious arm conflict between government forces and separatist non state armed groups. The armed conflict has led to the dead of over 3000 people leaving hundreds of thousands of internally displaced person. According of OCHA situation report No 25, 9,418 persons displaced in June in the North-West (NW) and South-West (SW) regions (NWSW) due to violence giving average of roughly 1%. Once people are displaced, their lives cannot be the same given that their sources of livelihood have been abandoned in their original habitats. Food insecurity is main fallout of loss of livelihood, and such vulnerable people needs serious humanitarian response to alleviate the suffering populations. As a palliative humanitarian organizations have been supportive via direct food aid to IDPs, returnees as well as host communities. Nevertheless, providing assistance to the routine livelihood activities of these vulnerable populations could be a more sustainable approach in tackling the issue of food security. 80 % of the crisis victims in the North West are originally farmers depending exclusively on agro-pastoral activities for their livelihood. Therefore through provision of agropastoral inputs to the entire vulnerable population will appear to be a perfect approach in alleviating the food security situation of the armed conflict victims in Mezam and Menchum divisions. Cultivation of short cycle crops and poultry farming are examples of activities that can improve on the food security situation of these people within a short notice.
INTERVENTION
The project intends to improve on the livelihood of 550 vulnerable women IDPs, physically challenged and vulnerable host families in Mezam, and Bui divisions of the Northwest regions through training and support with agro-pastoral inputs and startup materials (crop seeds, manure, vegetable seeds, chicks)
A rapid needs assessment conducted by SHUMAS in these localities indicates that the priority needs are farm inputs and rearing of table birds. If each farmer is provided with farm inputs such as improved huckleberry seeds, maize and beans and 25 chicks, each of them will make an average income of 75 000 frs from broiler table birds and 100 000 frs from crop production
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVES
1.1. Main Objective
Improve the resilience of 550 vulnerable crisis affected persons (60 % women, 10 % physically challeged) in Bui and Mezam in the North West Region
1.2. Specific Objectives
• To improve crop yield through provision of farm inputs (improved seeds of huckleberry, corn, and beans, as well as fertilizers, pesticides and farm tools) to 250 households heads including 100 men and 150 women in 8 months
• To increase protein needs by distributing 25 three weeks old table birds and 3 bags of feed, 2 feeders and 2 waterers to 300 households heads in 6 months
2. EXPECTED RESULTS
The following results were expected on completion of the project execution.
• 250 households heads including 100 men and 150 women in the project zone will improve on yields of selected crops by 50 %.
• 300 households will improve on their protein intake and get some income by selling of table bird.
