Project Summary

The project aims at providing protection services displaced and vulnerable and marginalized women, men, boys and girls as well as the elderly and those with disability in Banadir/Mogadishu ( Dharkenley, Garasbaley and Deynile Districts ) as well as Afgoye, Marka and Barawe Districts in Lower Shabelle and Jowhar and Balcad Districts in Middle Shabelle Region that have been experienced increased protection issues owing to insecurity and huge displacement occasioned by conflict and devastating drought conditions.
The armed conflict between government forces backed by their allies alshabab insurgent group, as well as clan-based violence has compromised the physical safety of the targeted vulnerable and marginalized communities and IDPs that expose them to serious risks. A total of 1,005 civilians were reported as casualties as of 31 July 2019 (384 civilians killed, and 621 injured), an increase by 32 percent compared to the same period last year (HRPG). There are reported cases of child recruitment in militia groups as fighters by both parties to conflict. It worth noting that, IDPs and marginalized residents face serious risks when moving out of their settlements in search of livelihoods, water and firewood and basic social services such as health and nutrition services. Women and girls are exposed to GBV while men and boys face serious risk of arbitrary arrest owing to perceived affiliation with armed groups. Of concern is the fact that a number of violations happens to the targeted displaced and vulnerable host community member that go un reported for fear of retaliation and lack of information reparding protection services hence serious health and psychological suffering. Their plight is exacerbated by weak judicial system and over reliance traditional resolution of mechanisms that are never fair to the victims. In response to this plight, the project targets a total of 38036 persons (18257 women and girls as well as 19779 men) while 64% of the project overall population are children

Project Activities

1. Conducting Community based protection by settling up protection community structures for the displaced communities to identify protection concerns, and work with communities on protection strategies to address these concerns in the project targeted locations2. Conducting advocacy through monthly meetings with local authorities in the project targeted locations to raise protection concerns and advocate for equal access to rights/services. In addition, protection workers will ensure that displaced communities from marginalised groups have meaningful access to humanitarian assistance, through advocacy with service providers, humanitarian assistance providers, authorities in the project targeted locations3. Awareness-raising and outreach through meetings with community members to enlighten them of protection concerns, reporting and provision of protection based services/ medical, psycho- social support, legal aid aid4. Capacity strengthening of non-institutional service providers training of community protection committeees on key protection competencies (human rights, psychosocial first aid, gender, participation). These committees will be trained on referral for protection cases to specialise services5. Identification, referral and case management of individuals with specific needs or at heightened risk6. Support for legal aid through formal legal structure as well as community dispute resolution mechanisms7. Protection monitoring of displacement and protection violations where protection workers will ensure protection monitoring in line and with the support of the Protection Monitoring System of the Protection Cluster
