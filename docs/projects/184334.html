Project Summary

The proposed project is emergency in nature and is going to deliver immediate humanitarian food assistance to address the food insecurity gap of the severely drought affected IDP populations living across Benadir region. The project will address the acute food insecurity among the targeted households with particular focus on recently displaced families who are at the borderline and have no means of survival, people with disability and vulnerable groups including women who are unable to engage livelihood means for earning and will support 6,740 H.H under IPC#4 phase living in Benadir IDP camps for a period of 4 months with unconditional food assistant through voucher system. The interventions are expected to enhance the vulnerable household’s food consumption score and improve nutrition status among children and also reduce the negative coping mechanism they apply when such nature of crisis strike.
The project will give an early relief to the crisis affected people enabling them to quickly access food and improve food consumption score among children, women and elderly people living in the IDP camps. At the end, food security status of 6,740 vulnerable H.H living in Benadir will be improved through monthly food voucher assistants thus contributing to the overall cluster strategy of minimizing the number of people in crises (IPC4 and above). QC in collaboration with the target recipient groups, QC will identify food vendors/suppliers in the target zone with particular attention given to the protection concerns in the process of food collection. People will be given the chance to demonstrate their willingness and choice regarding vendor selection process. Once vendor suppliers identified, Beneficiaries will be given voucher to enable visit the vendor and collect the food ration on monthly basis. QC MEAL staff will conduct continues monitoring exercise on the process of distribution and they will be present at the distribution points to ensure process are smooth and people are receiving their entitlement accordingly. In addition to this, food quality will be inspected before and during distribution exercise to ensure food quality.
Community based targeting will be used for beneficiary selection process. Gender will be given special role as project will work to improve gender equity and participation in all project planning and implementation processes. Local authorities and camp relief committees will be engaged during the selection process. Community will take the process ownership and identify beneficiary based on vulnerability criteria set. Camp relief committees will be trained on selection process especially vulnerability criteria. Post selection verification will be conducted by QC team to verify the lists with the participation of the whole community. The final and verified list will be jointly approved by the camp relief committee, local authorities and QC.
Project will engage broader stakeholders in the ground including government officials, local authorities, members from local community and other humanitarian partners for coordination and collaboration reasons. Further on this, QC will work to build exist strategy through linking the supported beneficiaries with other resilience programs going on in the areas. This will be achieved through continued networking and engagement with all including federal ministries officials, local authorities and partners that work resilience programming.

1. - Projects activities

1. Engagement. Project will coordinate with all stakeholders including local authority, target beneficiary groups, camp relief committees, other partners on ground to rollout assistances and agree on implementation processes. Separate engagement meetings will be organized with all stakeholders including targeted beneficiaries to discuss and agree on selection process, vulnerability criteria, implementation schedule. Special consideration will be given to women and girls groups during this process to ensure gender equity in project implementation and also protection concerns of these groups is addressed. 2. Baseline survey will be made to assess the food security outlook during the beginning of the project implementation. The outcome of this exercise will be used to benchmark the progresses that will be made in each and every month. 3. Identification and selection of beneficiaries. Once engagements are done, project team will work with both local authorities and camp relief committee from the IDP community to conduct community-based selection approach. Target communities will be mobilized on project objectives, target deliverable, timing and selection criteria and inclusion of gender. Camp relief committees including women representatives will conduct profiling exercise in consultation with community members. 6,740 IDP H.H will be identified. 4. Food distribution. 6,740 H.H will receive monthly food ration through unconditional voucher in a period of 4 months. Monthly family Food ration consists of 82kgs of assorted food comprising of 25kg of rice, 25kg of wheat flour, 25kg of sugar, 4 liters of oil, 2kgs of dates and 900 grams of powder milk. Voucher system will be used in which beneficiaries collect food from pre-identified suppliers. Consultation will be made with the community regarding selection of suppliers to enhance and mainstream protection and wellbeing of the target groups specially women and girls. 5. Monthly PDM exercise will be conducted to verify how beneficiaries received the food ration. Also, consumption patterns will be addressed in the evaluation. At the end of the project, end-line evaluation will be made to examine the food consumption outlook among the targeted H.H.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Qatar charity will apply “do no harm policy” as guiding principle of the proposed interventions to safeguard the safety and enhance the protection of the target communities. QC will organize beneficiary consultation meetings including all community structures such as marginalized groups and children to familiarize with project objectives, location, duration and entitlements to increase participation and promote the sense of ownership among the recipients. Qatar Charity will be accountable to the project beneficiaries and will strengthen its communication channels with the beneficiaries to ensure the assistance is delivered in a dignified way and the protection and safety of the recipients is well safeguarded according to humanitarian standards. QC strongly believes in localization and supports local economy and selection of local vendors will not only help to boost the economy however it will also ensure easy accessibility for the beneficiaries. QC will engage with local supplier to facilitate food distribution through distributed vouchers and before any selection is made, QC will give a first priority for the location of the suppliers as well as accessibility, distance and safety in order to prevent and minimize any unintended negative effects and beneficiaries will be included in the consultations and decisions making process to enhance dignity. Additionally, protection and APP related training will be given to the project staff to ensure the safety and protection of the targeted population are preserved and project activities are delivered in a meaningful way. Qatar Charity will use community-based targeting where the camp relief committee identifies the vulnerable H.H for inclusion but before that to happen, QC will mobilize the CRC and agree on the vulnerability criteria. Marginalized groups and those facing challenges in accessing humanitarian assistance will be given special consideration particularly women, people with disability and minority groups. Minority groups will be part of the decision-making process and the project will ensure effective participation of the wider community in the project-related decision to increase project impact. Any discrimination against minority groups will be addressed immediately and the project team will oversee the beneficiary identification process to make sure the registration is inclusive and registered beneficiaries are under the vulnerability criteria agreed upon. QC feedback mechanism structure which is already in place such as PTM reports, regular field visits and hotline number intended the complains to have directed will be employed as an additional tool to enhance project accountability and promote inclusion of minority groups and adjust any complaints received during the project implementation cycles timely to further increase project impact.
