Project Summary

WFP is providing food assistance to approximately 3.7 million people in 60 districts in Zimbabwe with over 1,700 distribution points. This proposal is for funds to support the implementation of the additional health and mitigation measures, including PPE and hygiene equipment for staff, cooperating partners and the beneficiaries required to contain the spread of the disease, and to enable WFP to continue to provide timely and safe food assistance in the context of this pandemic.
