Project Summary

The organization was registered in 2017, during this period 6 advocacy projects and 1 infrastructure project have implemented. These projects were directed by affected population of Donetsk region. Project's donors were: UNDP, ICF "Ukrainian Women's Fund", "People in Need" and NGO "Slavonic Cultural Center" Zadirkal ".

Since 2014, all members of the organization and experts have been working as volunteers in the territories from 0-20, and 20+ from the contact line, implementing humanitarian and infrastructure projects, and then many partners were appeared during this work.
In implementing advocacy projects, our colleagues saw that it is very important, to satisfy basic needs so that the next help will be important for people. Based on this, the warm schools and kindergartens (repair of the heating system of 25 institutions) and the provision of necessary basic equipment by the schools are very important for people in hazardous conditions.
In parallel, it is important for children to see the examples of stable adults, parents and teachers. For this purpose it is planned to conduct trainings for 1500 persons (parents and teachers) in 50 educational institutions. (NGO has experience in conducting adult education projects)
