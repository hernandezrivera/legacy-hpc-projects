Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to reduce morbidity and mortality rates through timely provision of comprehensive, equitable and quality health care services.by easy access to and provision of primary health care services including diagnosis investigation, outpatient consultation, and medication and raising awareness on COVID-19 and other PHC strengthened subjects. Moreover, Increasing the access to coordinated, equitable humanitarian health services for those most vulnerable and in need.
In addition, the project will provide advanced medical treatment under Secondary health care, surgeries, trauma care Services -rehabilitation services -assistive devices and MHPSS services.
The project target is 66,678 persons benefiting from the health services provided during the project implementation period including relief and obtain better life style.

3 - Needs

Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities, which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply and irrigation systems. Today, 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance - a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020 -with needs increasingly been exacerbated by economic decline as indicated in HNO report 2021.WHO estimating a 22% prevalence of mental health disorders in conflict settings globally.In Syria, almost one in two households (46%) is worried about conflict-related insecurity; 29% are anxious about safety and home security issues.These numbers only provide a glimpse of the multiple factors, which continue to cause significant stress, and, in many cases, explicit psychological trauma amongst the population.Latakia is classified as 4 on health severity scale with an estimated number of people in health need of 755,982 out of the total population number estimated of 1,274,433 (Average percentage of PWDs is 24%) due to limited number of specialized doctors, limited or even non-existing medicines in many of the centers especially NCDs for elders, lack of electricity and alternative power supply in the centers that caused the working hours to be very limited.

4 - Implementation of activities

One primary health center (PHC) in Alramel alJanoubi area with five clinics rooms, pharmacy, and mental health sessions.Tow mobile teams (MMT) in Haffah and Qurdaha rural areas provids medical consultation services and PSS team plus medicins in each.Services include (medical cunsltation, medicines, MHPSS services)Mosaic Association for Human Relief and Development will monitor day-to-day implementation of the activities and report back on bi-weekly and monthly basisMoreover, one monitoring and evaluation officer will be recruited and responsible for monitoring and evaluating the project implementation where reports showing the progress towards achieved results will submit on monthly basis. In addition to conducting surveys using questionnaires about the beneficiaries' satisfaction, on a quarterly basis, and benefiting from their observations. Furthermore, installing a box for complaints.
