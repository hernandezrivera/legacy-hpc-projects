Project Summary

This is a twelve-month project that will seek to address water, sanitation and hygiene gaps/needs of about 54,000 vulnerable host community, returnees and IDPs in El Salam (Alrawat, Wad Alcod, Alakaf, Halayb villages - severity level of the locality is 4) and El Jabalian (Joda Aljadida, Joda Algadeema villages – returnees areas; severity level of the locality is 4) localities, White Nile state; and Om Durman locality in (Naivasha settlements/open area; severity level 3) in Khartoum State.
Delivery of the project will be through construction of new water systems, rehabilitation of existing water points, delivering capacity building activities, to ensure sustainable management of WASH facilities, provision of O&M tools, assisting in solid waste management and community raising awareness, through provision of personal, household and community sanitation and hygiene services; and capacity strengthening of sanitation and hygiene structures in the targeted areas.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Limited access to safe, sufficient and sustainable water, hygiene kits and low awareness of safe hygiene practices and behaviour portent great health risks to the people. The population’ capacities remain low to accessing personal, household and environmental hygiene kits; there are also low capacities in the targeted villages for proper solid waste management. Limited services for solid waste management puts the vulnerable populations at high risk of solid waste environmental hazards such as breeding grounds for rodents, mosquito, which are vectors that can lead to outbreak of diseases, and eventually human mortality
