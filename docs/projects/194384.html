Project Summary

The emergency support project is a 1 year response plan to assist vulnerable IDPs and host community comprising of 2579 households and 15477 population in Gengeng,Abang and Yirol payams of Yirol West county of Lakes state .The intervention aims to ensure that lives are saved and human suffering is reduced through provision of emergency dry and main season vegetable, crops and fishing kits for vulnerable households and livestock interventions.
The prioritized objectives to achieve the overall goal will include:
Cluster Objective 1:Provide food assistance to prevent famine and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies for vulnerable populations (in IPC 5, 4 & some 3)
Cluster Objective 2: Enhance emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop seeds and fishing and livestock support (in IPC 3, 4 & 5)
Cluster Objective 3:Reduce dependency on food and agricultural inputs to support and strengthen households’ ability to absorb shocks (implemented across all 78 counties)
The main activities to be carried out to realize this objectives and achieve the overall goal will include:
CO1: Food distribution through in kind and cash voucher assistance.
CO2: livelihood support: crop & vegetable seed kits, tools, fish kits and livestock vaccination and treatment in-kind except nutrition vouchers (cash + poultry + goat) and seed trade fairs (vouchers);
CO3: local procurement of seeds & cereals (aggregation centers); value chain addition and training: improved agronomic practices, post harvest losses.
WAV needs assessments, as well as Post Distribution Monitoring from ongoing programs, have identified significant unmet needs for both IDPs and host families who have lost productive crop agriculture or fishing assets due to ongoing conflict or displacement and as a result has reduced purchasing power and incomes that has hindered food security. It is critical to support these households to begin to restore lost crop agriculture or fishing livelihoods, thus enabling them to have increased food availability at personal production level, as well as small income for increased food access. The project will distribute fishing kits and then train fishermen on the usage of the new fishing equipment. Early distribution of fishing kits will support fishermen to take advantage of the fishing season. Similarly the project will distribute vegetable seeds and tools to beneficiaries (farmers) during early February for planting in the same month. In addition, the project will do post planting monitoring and provide periodic technical advice and follow up as necessary. During April and May, WAV will distribute staple crop seeds and tools to farmers. The beneficiaries of staple crops and tools will take advantage of planting season and will do the planting between May and ­June, 2023. The majority of IDPs in Yirol West traditionally men take care of the livestock and they are also involved in the fishery business, while women do most of the agricultural work. Due to these cultural and traditional approaches, the majority of agriculture beneficiaries will be female headed HHs.

1 - Needs

Recurrence of violence Intra and inter communal conflicts and floods in parts of Lakes state has had detrimental effects on the food security and livelihood situation affecting mainly women and children. 5.5m people in South Sudan are currently food insecure (IPC October 2022-July 2023) a trend expected to rise due to seasonal forecast in locations prior to multiple crises, already below thresholds in food security, WASH, Health, Education and Nutrition . The county targeted in Lakes is Yirol West in Gengeng,Yirol and Abang payams currently in IPC 4 and jointly have a populations of 32000 people with 5% of the population currently IDPs. (IOM DTM, ’18). These County host the IDPs integrated into the host community without access to work, with food distribution as the main source of livelihood (WFP,NPA and CRS 2021 ). FSNMS round surveys indicate that at least two thirds of respondents in Yirol West County reported having received some humanitarian assistance. Multiple displaced IDPs have significantly higher levels of food insecurity at households (43%) than residents (35%). Female headed households also has higher prevalence of food insecurity (45% versus 33% for male headed households), a possible indication of gender based vulnerabilities. Significant decrease in the amount of households that receive income from casual (skilled and unskilled) labor passed from 20% to 30% to merely 6% to 8% in Yirol West county as a result of multiple displacements. Populations are now engaged in alternative coping livelihoods options such as sale of crops, livestock and unsustainable natural resources (poles, grass, firewood, charcoal) and/or begging. National production is expected to be below the five-year average, which will lead to low domestic supply in 2023 and a continuation of extremely high food prices. At the household level, food availability is now expected to be lower than previously projected due to crop damage from Fall Armyworm infestation. Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) prevalence rates are above emergency levels malnutrition thresholds (GAM >15%) in the targeted payams of Yirol West county. Disease outbreaks such as cholera and other AWDs have had significant effects on food security. Populations have resorted to less preferred dietary alternatives due to loss of livelihoods to both natural and conflict fueled causes, crop failure and multiple displacements. Prices of commodities have presently increased by over 200% due to local currency depreciation. Inflation currently stands at over 600% (SSNBS, 21). With over 61000 people said to be flood prone and inter tribal/intra tribal conflict in the targeted county (52% Female) alone, this has affected road accessibility within the aforementioned county causing food prices to skyrocket in the local markets. Livestock conditions have also been impacted negatively due to changed migration patterns to cattle raid conflict, insecurity, and concentration of animals in small areas has raised concerns about disease outbreaks, reduced access to resources and veterinary extension services, as well as increased tensions between herders and farmers. Importantly, the terms of trade between livestock and cereals have drastically declined across all markets, undermining the purchasing power of aggro-pastoralists in Yirol West county

2 - Project activities

• Food distribution in the 3 payams of Gengeng,Yirol and Abang of Yirol West. • Livelihood support: crop & vegetable seed kits, tools,fish kits and livestock vaccination and treatment in-kind except nutrition vouchers (cash + poultry + goat) and seed trade fairs (vouchers); • Local procurement of seeds & cereals (aggregation centers); value chain addition and training: improved agronomic practices and post harvest losses. • Training of farmers on improved vegetable and crop production technologies and management • Conduct distribution of vegetable ,crop and fishing kits in the 3 distribution sites. • Training fisher folks on fish production and fish preservation methods in the 3 distribution sites. • Conduct post distribution monitoring for fish, crop and vegetable kits in the distribution sites

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

Prior to this application participatory assessment through focused group discussion (FGD) and key informant interviews (KII) was conducted Yirol West county with 30 participants involved drawn from different ages ,gender and diversities to ensure there is commitment in placing accountability to people affected by disaster and conflict at the core of its emergency policy and practice, from preparedness and the onset of an emergency throughout the phases of the programme cycle. The current FSL concerns being faced by the vulnerable marginalized groups (women headed HHS,people living with disability, elderly women, pregnant and lactating women) patterns and the risk factors of food insecurity have been identified through beneficiary feedback mechanism. WAV will continue to ensure that this project utilizes a people-centered approach, sensitive to the dignity of all human beings, the varying needs of different segments within the community, and the importance of ensuring that women, men, girls and boys from the vulnerable groups equally access and benefit from FSL services provided. WAV will strengthen the existing community volunteer structures of farmers and fish folks groups by ensuring that the vulnerable groups are well represented in these structures in Yirol West county to support programming and activity implementation through trainings to enhance long term sustainability. During the selection of the beneficiaries there will be community consultative meetings to identify the vulnerable beneficiaries to be considered for support with the vegetable and fishing kits. Furthermore, WAV will build up this project based on the already existing knowledge of farmers and fishermen, and provide them with training's on improved vegetable production technologies and use of the new fishing kits. WAV will follow participatory assessments to identify, verify and profile targeted beneficiaries. The criteria will prioritize vulnerable people, including IDPs, host communities and female headed HHs.

8.11. Mitigation Measures.

The project will incorporate community involvement and participation as a key ingredient in the delivery of good planning outcomes in the Yirol West county. Age, gender and diversity will be considered throughout the project cycle. This will assist in the identification of local needs, inform policy-making, and provide feedback on FSL service delivery while at the same time fostering a sense of local ownership towards the project .The project will ensure that this issues are mitigated through community participation. During implementation phase the community members will be involved and they will participate in the activities within the work plan to achieve the desired results and monitor progress.
