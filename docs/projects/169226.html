Basic Info

Project Name

Health emergency assistance to children and women affected by Beirut blast, having access to essential MNCAH, including mental health and immunization, amidst COVID-19 pandemic

Start Date

03/08/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

For every child and woman affected by the Beirut blast, to have access to the continuum
of care (Maternal newborn child adolescent health and immunization including Mental
health) with quality services amidst COVID 19 pandemic. UNICEF will reconstruct,
rehabilitate and equip damaged health facilities including dispensaries, PHCCs, UNRWA
clinics, Pediatric/ Newborn unit and Maternal Obstetric ward and the Central Warehouse in Quarantina, in line with the national Primary health care network appeal, and in coordination with the National Mental Health Programme. The project will encompass procurement of essential supplies,
PPE and IPC materials, cold chain management, immunization supply chain strengthening and waste management of all health supplies in primary health care network.

Plan fields

2 - Activities

- Reconstruction and rehabilitation of (i) 10 damaged dispensaries (ii) 3 PHCCS in Greater Beirut in coordination with the National PHC network appeal, (iii) the immediate support to the re-start of the Pediatric and Newborn unit, as well as the Maternity Obstetric ward, in Quarantina hospital in coordination with the MoPH and other actors, (iv) the cold chain, dry room and electricity in the central warehouse in Quarantina in close coordination with WHO and the MoPH - Procure quality essential medical supplies, considering 25% increase demand in the PHC in blasted area, including obstetric kits, necessary medicines, medical equipment and consumables for the newborn and pediatric unit and maternity obstetric wards, health kits, essential drugs, vaccines, vaccination equipment, cold chain, oncology pediatric drugs and equipment, PPEs, non-medical PPE and IPC material for COVID 19 prevention to damaged facilities and to Palestinian health centers, in complementarity to WHO and other actors - Support waste management and ensure the development of different specific appropriate waste streams for the proper disposal of waste (Asbestos, Organic trash, Glasses, Debris, Plastic, Electronic waste, Medical waste, PPE, IPC material etc) in the PHC facilities in the blast area *EDIT NOVEMBER 2020*: This activity will not be implemented during the project period. -Staffing support and capacity building of health workforce in PHC network, as well as in central warehouse and in Pediatric/ Newborn unit and Maternity Obstetric ward, and frontline workers and municipalities on all related MNCAH services and practices including mental health through life cycle approach - Technical support and contribution to the PHC re-start strategy, through Package of subsidization for medical consultations focusing on MNCAH, immunization and Mental Health services in the PHC facilities supported by UNICEF in the blast area. - Increase community knowledge, awareness and engagement to raise the demand for quality MNCAH including mental health as well as COVID 19 prevention and care through (i) outreach and referral to services in most vulnerable municipalities (ii) conducting health education for prevention, home-based care and care-seeking (iii) innovative and tailored sessions targeting children at schools, medical and non-medical staff at hospitals and medical centers, private corporations and institutions; (v) Capacity building of healthcare providers - Secure access to quality treatment in the affected health centers (hospitals, CCCL and CHANCE) for 500 cancerous children, including psycho-social support to these children to strive and overcome the trauma (psychosocial support, wellbeing program).
