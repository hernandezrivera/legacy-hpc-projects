Project Summary

The project will aim to support the primary health services in NWS, through this project, the following health facilities will be supported:
- Orient medical complex (PHC L3 + physical rehabilitation center)-Idleb-Idleb-Idleb. Code: G0700050.
- Orient MRI center- Idleb-Idleb-Idleb. Code: G0700099.
- Orient mobile clinic-Idleb-Idleb-Idleb. Code: G0705057.
- Orient PHC L3 Idleb-Harim-Salqin. No code.
The project will aim to Sustain and increase access to essential health services in the implementation areas. including routine reproductive, maternal, neonatal and child health services, non-communicable diseases, infectious diseases,and advanced radiology services, prioritizing the most vulnerable groups: children under 5; women of reproductive age (15-49); the elderly (starting at age 50 in relation to the burden of NCDs); IDPs and returnees, and people living with disabilities, as identified by the health cluster.

4 - Needs

In the sense of the current military escalation and hostilities in southern and south-eastern rural Idleb which is causing casualties and important displacement among civilians, including among health workers. Bombardments are also resulting in destruction of vital health care infrastructure further weakening the health system and overwhelming the remaining health facilities.OCHA has estimated about 700,000 displacements that would take place in the coming soon future if the military escalation continued. Most IDPs are moving to the central and northern parts of Idleb Governorate, around the existing informal IDP sites.Health facilities are entry points: humanitarian life-saving and life-sustaining essential health services at primary level – including first-line support for gender-based violence, and coordinated referrals to secondary care, trauma services and specialized care, are vital to addressing population health needs and preventing further harm.Where health structures are damaged or none exist, mobile teams are needed to fill gaps while repairs are addressed.

5 - Implementation of activities

Orient will start the implementation by communicating with the staff, local health authorities and other partners to inform them about the project and the changes that will take place on the targeted facilities.A Kick-off meeting will take place in Orient office to inform the departments about their tasks and duties through this project.Orient will renew the contracts with the health staff of the ongoing facilities, and start the recruitment process to hire the staff of the new ones.The logistics department of Orient will conduct the procurement process for the items mentioned in the budget according to the Standards Operating Procedures SOPs of the logistics and Finance departments.Orient will conduct an Information, Education, and Communication IEC campaign in the target communities to inform them about the health services provided through the targeted facilitiesOrient will communicate with WHO to allocate pharmaceutical kits to these centers according to the number of beneficiaries and type of services.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

-Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers: Orient will report any attack on health facility, and support any efforts to highlight protections challenges facing health workers across Syria. -Gender Based Violence GBV: Hiring of female staff to increase access to health services for women and girls, training gynecologists, midwifes and nurses on the clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals, and link the health facilities with the social services to ensure safe referral pathways between both of them.-Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Orient will conduct orientation session on PSEA on all health facilities to ensure all the staff are aware about PSEA, all the recruitment process will include PSEA in the criteria of selection, and all the staff should sign Orient code of conduct, and PSEA policy.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

-Cooperation and coordination with authorities: Orient have good coordination with the local health authorities in Idleb, in addition to good relation and coordination with the local councils and all the active partners on the targeted areas.-Access to implementation areas: Orient has good experience in implementing projects in the targeted areas, and to decrease the risk of the lack of accessibility, all the project materials will be procured as a on shot.-Changing front lines, changes in the parties controlling the implementation areas and significant population movements as they flee shifting conflict areas: Orient has an evacuation plan and can ensure the security and the evacuation of the staff in case of emergency.-Available, qualified staff: Orient has a good experience in implementing health projects, and have many high qualified staff, also, we will build the capacity of the staff to increase the quality of services.
