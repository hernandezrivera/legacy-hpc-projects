Project Summary

This project’s main goal is to provide NFI support to the conﬂict affected population. The consequences of the conﬂict have been particularly severe and have converted many parts of the different governorate into extremely hard-to-reach areas, where people suffer from extreme needs, due to the enormous destruction and being cut off from access to markets. Displaced people, returnees and the overburdened host communities face tremendous struggles while trying to provide basic products for their families and ensure a digniﬁed survival. With this NFI support, the living conditions of 169.000 people in areas with high rates of displacement and large-scale destruction will be improved and the basics of a digniﬁed life will provided through the distribution of essential NFI kits.
The number of kits, kit components and targeted locations have been determined after assessing the overall situation in the regions, needs of the population and timing of the intervention.
The project is in line with the NFI sector strategic objectives and priorities of this allocation, with a primary goal of delivering urgent assistance to people in need, prioritizing vulnerable groups, according to the identiﬁed needs in the areas of intervention.

4 - Needs

Almost nine years of armed conﬂict in Syria has caused major devastation in many regions of the country, has destroyed large parts of the basic infrastructure and has led to extreme suffering. The needs among the 11,5 million people requiring humanitarian assistance remain massive. Of these, almost3.42 million people according to HNO 2020 need NFI assistance, especially in areas with a constantly high displacement rate and volatile situations, with different parties involved and frequent changes of control. Major safety, security and accessibility issues in those regions currently hinder the humanitarian community from reaching wide parts of the affected population and from providing aid and support to those in need. The hardship and struggles in many governorates across the country are overwhelming, especially in Aleppo, Deir-ez-zor, Hama, Daraa, Raqqa and Rural Damascus where people have suffered from the negative outcomes of tensions and ﬂuid situation including besiegement, large displacements, damages to services and infrastructure, inability to access local markets and engage in economic activities. Many of the displaced families have minimal belongings and state that one of the most widespread challenges during their daily struggle is the inability to afford rent and non- food items. This project will serve to address the critical need for lifesaving and life- sustaining Non-Food Items, to support vulnerable families and ensure a digniﬁed life. The needs of affected people in conﬂict affected areas are crucial in every NFI support, as the primary goal is to accommodate such responses with respect to the local context and the local communities' background and circumstances. An estimated need conducted assessment resulted in stating the need of 10000 hygiene kits, 5000 shelter kits (mattresses and blankets) 15000 of seasonal kits in the targeted areas.

5 - Implementation of activities

RSRP's activities are in line with the NFI sector’s response strategy adhering to the needs-based approach, identification of PIN within the framework of 2020HNOto sub-district level and the following strategic objectives which have been set in coordination with local authorities as well as the communities and NFI cluster. RSRP will be focusing on:Distribution of Seasonal items of winter kits and summer kits(for high temperature areas;underwear kits,mosquito nets,jerry cans,etc.) core essential items such as mattresses,hygiene kits and solar lamps, will also be distributed to sustain lives and reduce the impact of exposure to extreme conditions.Training will be conducted for the stakeholders mentioning resilience, NFI skills and capacities. Partnership with STD facilitates access to areas STD is present and active in. Relying on STD to provide the approvals within the local authorities,cooperation with the national and the local government allow field staff to move freely to all selected locations.This intervention will be implemented and monitored in coordination with key stakeholders,including the local community, local authorities and NFI cluster to avoid any duplications with other implementing partners in the area. RSRP identifies the most in need groups through a clear beneficiary selection criteria based on the prioritized vulnerable groups taking into consideration age,gender,displacement status,health issues and other considered aspects based on the components of every kit and the contextual characteristics of every location,hence the selection criteria is adapted according to the kit components.The coordination with the local community will serve to identify needs and determine appropriate implementation plan with respect to the local cultural norms and traditions.The accountability to the affected population will be guaranteed throughout the entire project cycle through the establishment of a complaint mechanism and the regular evaluation of feedback.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk 1: Tension from excluded beneﬁciaries might lead to conﬂict between target groups and complaints against the humanitarian partner. Mitigating measures: - Clear and comprehensible communication of the selection criteria to the community, community leaders and beneﬁciaries - Complete impartiality in informing people within an affected community. Protection Risk 2: Tension within the community itself due to variation in assistance. Mitigating measures: -Coordination with sectors to align kit components of every distribution with minimum standards and prioritized content. Protection Risk 3: Inability to access registration or distribution sites by vulnerable groups in the community (the elderly, disabled people and people with diseases and chronic illnesses). Mitigating measures: -Collaboration with local community to determine suitable distribution sites for all targeted groups. -Well established distribution sites to ensure an eﬃcient distribution process. Protection Risk 4: Sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers.Mitigating measures: -Inform and train humanitarian workers on all aspects related to protection. -Obligate humanitarian workers to adhere to a code of conduct. Establish an eﬃcient and effective complaints mechanism.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: Safety and Security Issues leading to inability of achieving the registration of beneﬁciaries. Likelihood: Low Mitigating Measures: -Constant coordination with locals and local authority in the areas of intervention.-Transparency in the implemented activities and the desired outcome of the emergency response. Risk 2: Major increase in the population in the targeted areas leading to insuﬃcient supplies (newly opened areas where returnee inﬂux is high) Likelihood: High Mitigating Measures: -Frequent coordination with all the humanitarian actors to encourage capacity mobilization and response in the areas where the needs are extensive. -Experienced program and project staff with experience in risk management and able to adapt the overall response, and human and ﬁnancial resources. Risk 4: Safety and security issues preventing the implementation of the emergency response. Likelihood: High Mitigating Measures:- Constant overview of situation reports, and latest updates related to the Syrian crisis which allows RSRP to address the needs of alternate vulnerable beneﬁciaries in other areas of intervention, should access to the targeted area not be feasible. Risk 5: Delayed, approvals which prevent delivering the assistance in a timely manner or prevent implementation of the project. Likelihood: High Mitigating Measures: -Transparency with local authorities in the areas of intervention, in addition to strong communication and collaboration prior to project implementation with local partner. Risk 6: Major Fluctuation in prices. Likelihood: Medium Mitigating Measures: -An experienced ﬁnancial department able to stay updated on the economic situation in the area, make the correct ﬁnancial assumptions and generate quality ﬁnancial reports for donor.
