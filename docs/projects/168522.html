Project Summary

Enlargement of Project # 163178: Job creation, cash assistance and social counseling on petty trading 100 PIN affected by Covid-19. We also intend to work with 5 radio stations for a period of 6 months being for vocational trainings. Based on our COVID-19 assessment feedback from field, gotten from 31 communities within 8 sub-divisions, education during covid is at 3% only. Intensive vocational training through local radio will actually improve literacy level within some communities involve. Average of 650USD per beneficiary for businesses and 6000USD for training through local radio and admin cost
