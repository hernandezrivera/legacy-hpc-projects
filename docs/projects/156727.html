Project Summary

In line with the 3rd objective adopted by the health cluster/ Gaziantep hub “Strengthen health system capacity to support continuity of care, strengthen community resilience, and respond to IDP movements and shifts in lines of control”, HIHFAD seeks to enhance the quality and availability of the healthcare services provided throughout northwest Syria. That is planned to take place through the establishment of a midwifery institution in Atmeh town, Dana sub district, Harim district, Idleb governorate.
The intervention entails the followings:
• 40 female students able to complete the second year in midwifery school including practical training in Maternity hospitals.
• 40 Female Students able to complete the first year in Midwifery school.

Needs

loss of dedicated, qualified maternal health care providers, in particular those with Midwifery competencies, there is a huge need of a Midwifery capacity building strategy which will contribute to the rapid availability of health based human resources. In areas of Syria reachable though cross-border operations from Turkey, women and girls experience exacerbated restrictions on freedom of movement, due to lack of security but also increasingly conservative gender norms, coupled with early marriage and lack of access to family planning. All of these inter-related factors impact on the women’s reproductive health needs, health seeking behaviour and access to health services, ultimately negatively impacting maternal and neonatal health. At the same time, the health system is seriously challenged and unsuited to the situation of on-going conflict whereby caesarean sections have become commonplace due to ‘safety and rapid execution of surgical techniques.

Implementation of activities

Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services: HIHFAD will support the capacity building of 80 trainees in terms of midwifery and in accordance with the UNFPA guidelines. The goal of the intervention is the empowerment of those trainees in order to safely practice basic midwifery skills.
