Project Summary

This project will directly target 3874 beneficiaries [1898 girls, 1976 boys affected by drought and displacement in Gedo region, Somalia. The project intervention is suitable significantly to address and respond to the education cluster and drought response plan (January-December 2020) strategies: the project gives priority to educational support to children that live in crisis affected districts of Gedo region. As well it provides educational opportunities to IDPs children in places where they moved to guarantee they can maintain their learning process. Education is the entry point to all other interventions during emergencies like drought, floods and conflicts. It protects children from risks and protection related concerns. Protective learning spaces will be established for all School-aged children who are living in Gedo region in order to ensure lifesaving educational interventions, overcoming barriers preventing attending schools and mitigate risks of school drop out for protection concerns. Equally, school-aged children who are displaced by the crisis will be reached out to their places to ensure they have access to quality education and protection services in locations they moved to. The proposed project will be targeting 3874 school aged children affected by the crisis in 28 schools in Gedo Districts such as Belet Hawa (5 schools), Bardhere (5 schools), Luuq (4 schools), Garbaharey-Buurdhubo (4 schools) and Dollow (10 schools). The project will target 3874 school-aged drought affected children in Gedo 1976 boys and 1898 girls). Education specific activities will include the provision of teaching learning materials: As per the education minimum standard on teaching and learning materials, learners and teachers will be provided learning materials in accordance to set standards. Each grade 1-3 student will receive learning kit consisting of 7 books (6 single ruled and 1 single Squared 100 sheets), 4 pencils, 4 rubbers and 2 sharpeners while students between Grade 4 and 8 will receive 7 books ((6 single ruled and 1 single Squared 100 sheets), 2 pens,2 rubbers , 1 sharpener and one student mathematics set. Bags will be given to girls to carry sanitary kits in schooling days in order to reduce absence during menstrual period, emergency school feeding that promotes retention of learners, emergency teacher incentives for 168 teachers (30 female) to motivate teachers, training of teachers and CECs on education and child protection, and sanitary kits for adolescent girls to encourage their regular attendance at school. WASH Specific activities will include water trucking, Installation water system to the schools and construction of water storages (Barkets), the construction of gender segregated school latrines with hand-washing facilities. To ensure sustained solutions, the project will also focus on establishing/strengthening existing community structures including child welfare committees and child rights clubs, schools based protection focal points, community education committees build their capacity to prevent and respond to child protection violations

Mitigation Measures

1.Conflict of interest among CEC and school management risk factor–The management and committee might divert the school’s food stuff and equipment for personnel gain and as well mishandle the school’s infrastructure design in construction thus poor quality services to the vulnerable boys and girls in the target location, however, HIRDA will ensure affective and strict monitoring and reporting as well as proper management of the schools infrastructure.2.Distance and location of school is also risk based on exclusion of disable and marginalized communities to minimize such disparities HIRDA will construct temporary learning space within the settlement 3.The risk of exclusion based on gender or age such factors are limited capacity among the trained female teachers, within the education sector might hampered the inclusion of women in the school staffing as well as management role hence the might provide room for men dominant. To mitigate this HIRDA shall ensure to capacitate the female teachers and provide them equal chance on both management, community education committee as well as ensure enrollment of more girl child within the target learning centers in Bardhere,Dollow,Luuq,Belet Hawa and Garbaharey IDps and host communities4.Information gap, discrimination, gender disparities and infrastructural design of learning environment further contribute the exclusion risk factors facing the target population. To mitigate this HIRDA will embark on social mobilization and information dissemination through public Barraza radio show for larger coverage on community education enlighten, hence this will provide better avenue for both enrollment and increasing the value for girl child education5.HIRDA will also ensure to identify the most needy group and provide more responsive intervention by providing the material needs for both girls, boys, children with disabilities, minority communities, children belonging to disadvantaged or marginalized groups and work with children, teachers, community leaders and parents to support equal and safe access to education without discrimination.it will provide adequate community sensitization and monitoring and coordinating with stakeholders that will improve both retention and enrollment, HIRDA however, intends to provide friendly schools toilet as well as easy adaptation of schools facilities by disable and physically impaired persons hence provide better learning material that shall enable to provide adequate access and safe learning facility.6. Landmine and explosive remnant of war - school children are susceptible for the risk related to landmines since children are found of handling all object in their exposal, however, Somalia having many sites of such explosive due to its porous security situation HIRDA will ensure schools going children safety and dignity is prioritized and provided with all necessary prevention of such landmine by ensure it works closely with mine action agency in closer coordination with community leaders in mapping out the areas proposed for construction of temporary learning space(TLS) in doing so HIRDA will prevent such risk occurrence within the learning institution.

Please provide further explanation

HIRDA organization will adapt a community based management approach that will embraces community participation and encourages the community and their leaders to ensure the sustainability of the benefit expected for the project. And this will guarantees some level of project sustainability.Formation of community education committee (CECS) will be an important step towards local ownership and sustainability of the benefits made by the project beyond the project funding periods. Moreover HIRDA will work hand in hand with the local administration and the ministry of education both jubaland state and the federal government of Somalia and all other education stakeholders in the region to sustain the education project. Somalia scores the highest on the INFORM Global Risk Index due to long-standing political instability, armed conflict, environmental stress and weak governance structures. Despite political progress and ongoing efforts to strengthen government institutions, the Ministry of Education (MoE) continues to lack the capacity to deliver basic education services for IDPs, children living in areas with ongoing conflict and other groups living under the most challenging circumstances. This results in 1.37 million crisis affected children (691,295 girls) being in need of education assistance during 2020. The crisis in Somalia has two primary drivers, conflict and natural disasters. According to the Protection Return Monitoring Network (PRMN), 270,000 people were displaced in Somalia between January and August 2019, of which 141,000 were displaced due to conflict and 112,000 due to drought of the people displaced this year, an estimated 108.00 (40%) are school-aged children. However, according to Joint Multi-Cluster Needs Assessment (JMCNA) assessment conducted across Somalia a total of 4.5 M school age children were enrolled in schools in which 35% of girls and 41% of boys, Accordingly, education cluster regional educational disparities in term of needs, Gedo region is among the regions with highest number of school-aged children in need of humanitarian assistance this is due to that fact that 68% of school-aged children in Garbaharey,61% in Ceel Waaq, and 59% in Bardhera are in humanitarian assistance.The main barriers of accessing basic education in Gedo region and across the country is due to recurrent drought that affected the communities’ livelihood, lack of teacher’s incentives, teaching and learning materials, lack of capacity by the community and education stakeholders coupled with lack of hygiene and sanitation as well as lack of clean drinking water that affected both the enrollment and retention of learners within the learning environment hence increased risk of girl child abseentism within the learning environment, therefore, HIRDA intends to respond to the education in emergency by providing adequate access to quality education to7438 affected children in Garbaharey,Bardhere,Luuq and Belet Hawa and Dollow district in Gedo region through provision of adequate school infrastructure, school, feeding, teachers incentives, improved learning materials as well as enhanced capacity to both teachers, school CECs and regional education representatives from Jubaland regional government while also coordinating the response to both concern ministries, sub cluster and regional cluster coordination as well as the local community at the target districts.
