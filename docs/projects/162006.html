Project Summary

Provision of transitional shelter assistance - complemented with technical assistance, household level NFI distribution and tailored HLP support - for Rohingya refugees in camp 25 Teknaf and neighbouring host communities.
The proposed intervention builds on shelter activities (TSA 1) undertaken in 2019 and proposes an integrated shelter, NFI and HLP approache aimed at ensuring a holistic approach to the shelter needs of Rohingya households, as well as HLP support in neighbouring host communities. "

Needs

Camps and sites remain congested seriously impacting physical and psychological well-being, especially of children, women, and persons with disabilities. The round 12 Needs and Population Monitoring (NPM) report indicates an average area of 18.76 m2 per person, signiicantly lower than the Sphere standards. Moreover, the lack of adequate lighting in shelters and public spaces was reported as a top safety concern followed by danger or violence when collecting firewood. Female key informants highlighted that the recent monsoon season had weakened the shelters. Bamboo has also deteriorated due to beetle infestation, resulting in a situation in which shelters can easily be broken into. Several such cases resulting in thefts were reported. Additionally, there is a gap of approximately 50% of household NFI needs. Following on from surveys conducted in late 2018 about tenancy practices across the camps, NRC ICLA will support existing community mediation structures to address conflicts, particularly those pertaining to land.

Activities or outputs

"TSA II (Transitional Shelter Assistance 2nd Phase): building on TSA I undertaken in 2018/2019, complementatry shelter activities and technical training to improve the standards of shelters, using treated bamboo through IOM common supply pipeline. Interventions will include training and ensure a gender-sensitive approach, including eg training to women on safe cooking practices.Provison of NFIs: targetted distributions, using the common NFI pipeline, addressing household level gaps in NFIs.HLP Training: Drawing on NRC's global HLP expertise, provision of training and counselling on HLP also to ensure that male family members understand that women have HLP rights to the shelter. Tenure and land conflict analysis conducted prior to shelter assistance.Alternative dispute resolution: HLP cases addressed through conflict mediation and alternative dispute resolution, including individual sessions with both men and women.Emergency response: NRC staff made available to inter-agency emergency response (through Humanitarian Coordination Cell), as required to support needs assessments, information dissemination and family reunification "
