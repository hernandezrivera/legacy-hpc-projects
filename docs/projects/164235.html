Project Summary

This project aims to establish and improve 10 informal IDP settlements infrastructure in
Northwest Syria, particularly in northern-west Idleb in Harim district. It targets the IDP sites that reported to be impacted by recent floods in winter 2019-2020 and had a miserable condition with partially/completely damaged tents and restricted of physical access to their site. The improvements and upgrades that will take place by MHD will contribute to the winterization plan of 2020-2021. Targeted IDP sites will be selected based on specific criteria that ensure which informal settlements are recommended for basic infrastructure improvement and upgrade. Speaking of which, the criteria of site selection will be based on the recommendations from a topographical information, HLP due diligence, secondary data form CCCM Cluster in addition to CCCM Cluster guidelines. Interventions are based on the main needs reported by CCCM during the recent flood response, Gravelling, tent leveling, tent inner and outer insulation as well as establishing drainages to steer the rainwater from the site. The proposed activities will ensure the resilience and safety of IDPs in future flood and deteriorated weather. Additionally, awareness activity will be in place where Muzun will provide camp management and IDP committees brochures that include first actions to be taken in expected weather deterioration.
Moreover, MHD will be keen to establish proper site management in the site that include 1 IDP representative committee and 2 sub-committees of 1-Fire Safety and Security and 2- Infrastructure maintenance considering women, youth and disability participation.

4 - Needs

IDPs sites remain highly exposed to weather conditions. more than 130,000 IDPs were directly affected by floods that hit several areas in northern Syria in 2019. More than 18,000 tents were destroyed partially or completely requiring replacement and further compounding needs of IDPs in these sites. (Syria HNO 2020)MHD has been assessing IDP sites affected by various incidents in winter 2019-2020, 98 incidents occurred in IDP sites in northwest Syria, 69 of them are flood related incidents in 51 unique spontaneously IDP sites in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Most of the incidents resulted in completely and partially damage of tents, however, the common reason of these flood incidents was the absence of proper infrastructure and the spontaneous establishment by IDPs or unaccountable actor. Drainage system, gravelling of roads, wash facilities and other related infrastructure activities are necessary to avoid such incidents in winter 2020-2021.The increase of spontaneously established IDP sites in northwest Syria in 2019 is showed an increase in IDP severity in many areas. Based on the IDP Site Integrated Monitoring Matrix (ISIMM), around 263 IDP sites were listed on ISIMM during 2019 in northwest Syria only. Currently, 920,747 IDPs are residing in 588 IDP sites and most of these sites has no sustainable infrastructure support to avoid the IDPs from potential floods in next winter. Vulnerability of women, girls, boys and men in the last resort IDPs sites has become protracted over time with no long term shelter solutions in sight. Due to their temporary nature, IDP sites are designed to remain temporary and equipped with minimum services. IDPs often resort to these sites after exhausting other options such as securing other types of shelter and/or being hosted by friends, relatives or by host families. Women constitute 56 per cent while children represent 39 per cent of the overall last resort IDP site population (Syria HNO 2020)

5 - Implementation of activities

This project will be implemented in parallel with IDP committees in location, 10 IDP committees should include males and females with various ages toensure good and effective participation in project design, implementation of activities. Asto commence with, the project will be implemented in 10 Informal settlements in Harimdistrict northern Idleb Governorate where high concentration of IDP sites are located. Theselection of the IDP sites will be based on criteria that includes: recommendations from atopographical information (land slope, soil condition…etc), HLP (land ownership easyverification), secondary data (CCCM online coordination tool for flood response) andspace in m2. All applicable informal settlements that meet the criteria will be considered. improving the physical conditions in these already existed Informal settlements is the main outcome of these interventions and increase theresilience for those IDPs through establishing and improving infrastructure in informalIDP sites to meet the minimum standards. In order to meet the needs and protect the IDPs form potential floods in winter 2019-2020, several components considered to be cost effective that will prevent the IDP site from flooding,- Gravelling for main roads site entrance.- Tent leveling (ground insulation).- Tent inner and outer insulation. - Establish drains for water to pass (based on topographic engineer).- Awareness campaign for IDPs on how to protect themselves before and during floods. MHD will provide direct support the targeted camp’s managements through providing Camp Coordination and Camp management basic trainings including CCCM reporting tools.MHD will be also be keen to target the site with awareness brochures for fire safety and security for the IDP residents through the IDP camp representatives. In terms of coordination and monitoring, MHD will be the focal point of the targeted IDP sites in reporting to CCCM cluster, this will include ISIMM/Plus and incident reporting.
