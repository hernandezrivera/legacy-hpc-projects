Project Summary

The project objective will be :
1- Meeting the needs of the most vulnerable groups in the city of Aleppo and its countryside by providing primary and secondary health care services in the city of Aleppo and its countryside.
2- Physical and occupational therapy services and providing their needs.
3- Building the capabilities of the association’s cadres and the local community, especially enhancing the capacity of young people in the field of development work
4- Strengthening citizenship in the city of Aleppo and its countryside
5- Preventing school dropouts through a year-round campaign that includes providing all of the school needs of the most vulnerable children in the city of Aleppo and its countryside, in addition to providing medical and mobility aids for age groups from 7 to 15 years old.
6- Supporting community initiatives
7- Enhancing the access of the underserved communities to clean enviroment by providing household water tanks, providing other hygiene supplies (children, menstrual hygiene management, elderly, etc.) and providing hygiene awareness courses.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project aims to provide primary and secondary health care in addition to some Assistive devices to provide occupational and physical therapy services, in addition to psychological support services, support community initiatives, and meet humanitarian needs in the intervention areas, which are summarized as follows:1- Primary Health Center (PHC) , in addition to 4 mobile medical teams, which includes a medical staff in addition to a staff specialized in psychological support and medical awareness.2- A referral system, which aims to provide primary and secondary health care services to the most vulnerable groups, which include (laboratory tests, x-rays, Echo , dialysis sessions, eye operations, orthopedic operations, general surgery, cardiac, urinary, neurological, and surgical operations , Normal Deleviries and cesarean sections) 3- Providing medical mobility aids to the most vulnerable groups, as the project will specialize in targeting age groups from 6 to 15 years old. The project will target school-age children to prevent school dropout by providing their medical and mobility needs, in addition to providing school needs when the school season opens.4- Occupational and physical therapy at the fixed center affiliated with the Al-Ihsan Charity Association, which will provide services and the needs accompanying that service such as (speech improvement sessions, treatment of autism problems, fractures, scoliosis, trauma-related injuries (brain or spinal cord), autism learning problems/disorders). Diffuse growths, rheumatoid arthritis, mental health problems or behavioral problems, broken bones or other bone injuries.5- Community initiatives that aim to involve the community in solving its problems, especially young people who are able to provide a creative solution to the problems that the community suffers from.6- Solve some water-related problems, especially in rural areas, by providing water filters.7- Supporting community capabilities through training courses that will target the association’s cadres and the local community to enhance the capabilities of young people in the field of development work.8- Supporting small projects for people with disabilities who have lost their ability as a result of their illness to work in their previous tasks. Thus, the project will aim to integrate them into society again according to their abilities and abilities.
