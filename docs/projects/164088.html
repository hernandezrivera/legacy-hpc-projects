Project Summary

the project aims to Support and increase livelihood opportunities on both households and community levels through creating temporary income-generating activities like CfW, building capacities of youth through providing Vocational training and enabling them to start their own businesses by providing Business cash grants. Additionally, Violet aims through this project to improve the access of the farmers to farmlands through road excavation, leveling, and graveling in Salqin subdistrict, Idleb governorate, in order to support the farmers in marketing crops transportation and marketing their products.

4 - Needs

As a consequence of the years-long crisis in Syria, the number of people in need and vulnerable communities has reached 11.7 million people in need. Needs are broad across the sectors, leaving host communities and displaced people independence for assistance. A large number of families are food insecure (6.5 million) or at risk of insecurity (2.5 million). Insecurity is further challenged through decreased income, overstretched capacities, unemployment or displacement. Limited livelihood opportunities lead to food insecurity due to limited income, higher protection risks especially for women and children as well as increased negative coping mechanisms of the communities such as crime, child labor or early marriage. Due to the crisis, women often have become head of household without work experience or income opportunities. Thus, vocational training can support women, who are in need of sustaining their families but without opportunities/knowledge/skills to create income. There is a need to tailor activities to their needs and providing childcare to enable their participation. Further, PWDs are in need of opportunities that are accessible and create independence.due to the years-long crisis in Syria, a lot of community infrastructures like public facilities, markets, and roads have been affected partially or fully (destroyed), that affect directly or indirectly on the livelihoods on the community and reduced livelihoods opportunities and the access to it.

5 - Implementation of activities

1. 250 beneficiaries (mainly from the youth age-segment, where 10% are with disabilities and considering the female with at least 30% participation) are obtained a two months vocational training ( 200 training hours) in addition to the basics of business management. 2. 75 beneficiaries (female mainly from the youth age-segment) out of those accomplished the vocational training program are obtained Bussiness-Cash-Grants and the required support to start up their small-scale businesses so most of them (75% out of them) are successful in obtaining a sustained income source. 3. Rehabilitation of 10,550 m of roads to the farmlands in Salqin to improve the access of 1190 farmers to farmlands through road excavation, leveling, and graveling. the roads will support farmers in marketing crops transportation and marketing. Violet will ensure access by coordinating with authorities on the usage of roads and ensuring no fees are taken for road usage. (secured and funded under SCHF)4. Sustainable livelihoods opportunities for households and communities provided for 350 householders (short-term work for one month) as a result of their participation in rehabilitating the basic infrastructures of their communities, so their resilience is improved against the negative coping strategies and the basic services of the host communities are improved by operating-or-rehabilitating the main services/infrastructure.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. A key risk is a gender-based violence and sexual exploitation and abuse through humanitarian workers. As Violet’s staff will interact with the most vulnerable groups of the community, the power dynamics between staff and beneficiary can be exploited. Violet trains all staff on PSEA, raises awareness of beneficiaries and informs on available complaints mechanism. The M&E team will include post-monitoring and PSEA topics in supervision sessions to identify any misconduct. Any complaints, allegations or rumors will be elevated on the country director level and investigated immediately.2. For community activities, there are child protection risks and GBV risks for participants e.g. due to the time of activities or their location. Violet’s team will mitigate those by assessing appropriate times for PSS activities and choosing a location that allows safe travel. Distances will be minimal and locations will provide sufficient shelter. 3. During the activity implementation, Violet will ensure avoiding any child labor by signing acknowledgment respect the code of conduct and Child protection policies with all contracted vendors and service providers after explaining it to them. Violet will not register or accept any request form to join the CFW activities from the applicants under 18. 4. During evacuations, all community members are exposed to protection risks due to their dependency on those conducting the evacuation. Violet will ensure both awareness of staff on code of conduct and PSEA, but also include monitoring questions for PSEA and satisfaction with the evacuation. 5. During the evacuation, people may be exposed to intensive shilling while they are fleeing. Thus, Violet will ensure continuous coordination with OCHA to de-conflict the roads and areas utilized to allocate people to safe areas.
