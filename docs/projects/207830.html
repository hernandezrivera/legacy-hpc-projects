Project Summary

Waterborne and hygiene-related diseases pose a severe threat in Bui Division, Cameroon, exacerbated by the Anglophone Crisis. Over 80% of hospital admissions are WASH-related, with inhabitants relying on contaminated water sources. ACOHOF, in collaboration with Rural Doctors and local Health Centers, seeks to address this crisis by improving Health WASH services for over 80,000 people across 22 rural communities.
Key Components:
Comprehensive Needs Assessment:
Conducting a thorough assessments in selected villages to identify unique WASH requirements and challenges, tailoring interventions accordingly.
Empowerment and Education:
Empowering communities with knowledge on WASH-related diseases, implementing cost-effective water treatment techniques, and initiating school-based WASH programs.
Revitalization of Unsafe Water Points:
Identifying and renovating unsafe water sources, upgrading infrastructure, and training community members to maintain these points.
Establishment of New Water Points:
Installing new water sources in designated communities, ensuring safety, quality, and community-led management and maintenance plans.
Effective Waste Management:
Establishing waste collection and disposal systems, providing training on proper waste management practices, and promoting recycling.
Distribution of WASH Kits:
Distributing WASH kits containing essential items such as soap, waste collection bins, and solar disinfection stands to improve health and living conditions.
Community-Centered Approach:
Holistic approach focusing on community education, empowerment, and active involvement in water and sanitation resource management.
Targeting enclaves and remote communities that have not received assistance since the crisis began, prioritizing highly populated schools and areas with limited access to clean water.
Implementing a criteria-based selection process, considering factors such as the severity of impact, high population density, and areas without previous assistance.
Special attention to addressing the water needs of minority groups within selected communities.
ACOHOF aims to not only provide access to clean water but also to build resilient communities capable of managing their water and sanitation resources effectively.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project aims to address several assessed needs in the Bui Division through its activities. These needs are identified through a need assessment in selected villages, and the project's activities are tailored to address these specific challenges. Here are the assessed needs the project will address:Waterborne Diseases:Prevalence of waterborne diseases in the Bui Division, with over 80% of hospital admissions being WASH-related.High mortality ratesHigh mortality rates, particularly among children, due to poor water hygiene and sanitation.Inadequate WASH Infrastructure:Shortage of safe and reliable drinking water sources for the population. Reliance on contaminated water sources such as wells, streams, and ponds of stagnant water used for drinking, both by humans and animals leading to health risks.Impact of the Anglophone Crisis:The Anglophone Crisis exacerbating existing challenges, with the Bui Division being one of the bloodiest theaters of the conflict.Slow government response to the WASH crisis, further aggravated by recent displacements and resource scarcity due to conflicts.Resource Scarcity and Conflict Risks:Concerns about increased death rates and potential conflicts due to resource scarcity, especially with internally displaced persons (IDPs) and returnees competing for limited resources.Lack of Assistance in Remote Communities:Identification and targeting of enclaves and remote communities together with schools that have not received any form of assistance since the crisis began.Community-Specific Challenges:Varied WASH requirements and challenges in different communities, necessitating tailored interventions.Identification and targeting of highly populated schools and villages with limited access to clean water.Minority Groups' Water Needs:Special attention to addressing the water needs of minority groups within the selected communities. By understanding and addressing these assessed needs, the project aims to implement a comprehensive and targeted response to improve the overall health and living conditions of the population in the Bui Division. The activities outlined, such as education, infrastructure revitalization, establishment of new water points, waste management, and distribution of WASH kits, are designed to directly mitigate the identified challenges and promote sustainable solutions. The community-centered approach ensures that interventions are not only effective but also culturally sensitive and inclusive.
