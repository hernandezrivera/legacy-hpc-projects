Project Summary

The project aim is to increase access to basic nutrition services among PLW and children between the age 6 - 59 months in Garbahare district, in Gedo region, Somalia. To accomplish this feat, it will conduct screening and provision of life saving and preventive nutritional supplements to the children and the PLW. Furthermore facility and community based (BNSP) program will cover both curative and preventative interventions targeting children U5 years (boys and girls) and pregnant and lactating women.
The project will facilitate identification, referral and treatment of cases of SAM at facility-based as well as mobile OTP teams. All the while, the project will refer complicated cases of SAM to SC and MAM to TSFP. The project will also include routine deworming and immunization services. The organization runs MCHN and provides drugs to PLW at their facility in Garbaharey run by a trained and qualified pharmacist. As such provision of these aforementioned nutritional services will also be overlaid by the strengthened health services at the MCHN which has also been strengthened to cover mild and moderate health complications for children and PLW. Furthermore, the organization has provided access to drugs at the MCHN chemist and entred into agreement with several suppliers to ensure adequate requisite support is available at all points in time. This will provide a full package of community and facility-based nutrition, health and WASH services.
To emphasize on the resilience aspect, provision of sustainable skills in WASH & hygiene to support the prevention of infection and/or fatalities related to poor hygiene especially in PLW will overlay. Women will be capacitated on breastfeeding and Infant Young Feeding Program (IYFP), which are key sustainability and resilince approach as well as basic hygiene programs.
The program will be overlaid with adherence to the Covid-19 protocals through insisting on washing of hands on all visits at the facility level, social distancing, provision of face masks, public notice board and publicly displays of protocal on notice boards. The orgainzation will also provide water tanks and soaps at all its facilities. Furthermore it will continue its essential awareness knowledge and skills related sharing mobiled-based messages to profiled beneficiries on proper hygiene.
The organization will also work in sysnergy and cordination with other actors at the cluster and field level to ensure referals, information sharing, feedback and cordination provide effective managenet of nutrition within the district. The organization will continue its partner-level attendance of meetings and sharing inportant data.
Beneficiaries selections will be targeted including the vulnerabe communities. The focus will also be made on women-headed households as well as vulnerable families in IDPs. The beneficiaries' selection process will be inclusive and conducted after extensive social mobilizations and communications about the project. Special focus will be made on the disabled with provision of enablers including transport to the facility where applicable or visiting the beneficiaries in their homes or settlement. For other forms of visibilities the organization will make intentional effort to identify people with special needs in the community and prioritise their nutritional and health needs during the social mobilzation, screening and profiling the beneficiaries.
Screening will be conducted to identify the qualifying and those that meet the creteria profiled and selected for the project. The focus of the screening will be identified villages and communities mosty affected. Mothers will be trained on MUAC-led household level screening to strengthen them to acquire adequate resilient skills. This will enable vulnerable households to be sufficiently skilled to conduct the necessary screening and bring the very at-risk HHs to access quality nutrition, sanitation and health services.

Project Activities

The following will be the core project implementataion activities; 1. Identification, referral and treatment of children suffering from severe acute malnutrition through the community, facility-based approaches and OTP Mobile teams. Complicated cases of SAM will be referred to SC and MAM to TSFP centres. Mother-led MUAC approach will be used to identifying and diagnosing malnutrition by mothers. Mothers will use MUAC tapes to measure the Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC). Mother led MUAC training will be conducted to support mother’s to screen, detect oedema and self-referral of their children to nutrition sites. Efforts will be made to ensure integration of OTP and TSFP sites where possible. To ensure the sustainability of IMAM interventions technical local capacity will be strengthened for integration of IMAM into the Primary Health Care system through the functional MCH facilities. Additionally, the capacity of nutrition staff will be strengthened for effective service delivery at facility and community level. In coordination with the GBV-Subsection and health, survivors of gender-based violence (GBV) identified at the nutrition sites and catchment areas, will be referred to multi-sectoral services. 2. Prevention services will include deworming for children 12-59 months and pregnant women in the 2nd and 3rd trimester, counselling and promotion on infant and young child feeding. Community mobilization and screening as well as micronutrient supplementation for PLW (multiple micronutrients and Iron folate) and children 6-59 months with Vitamin A supplements. The program will promote home-based fortification to enrich the diets of young children 6-23 months through the distribution of micronutrient Powders. 3. Provision of routine immunization services to children and pregnant women in this project. 4. The community resilience approach will be overlaid with basic service provision of key health, WASH and nutrition basic services for prevention, promotion and referral and will be done by trained community workers. 5. Provison of awareness, sanitation and prevention materials against Covid-19

Project Indicators

The project will be underpinned by the following indicators; (i) The number of boys/girls (6-59 months) with severe acute malnutrition admitted and treated. (ii) The number of boys/girls (6-59 months) with moderate acute malnutrition admitted, successfully treated and discharged. (iii) The number of PLW with moderate acute malnutrition treated. (iv) The number of boys/girls under the age of two and PLW provided with BSFP. (v) The number of children successfully immunized. (vi) The number of sessions on basic training on WASH, sanitation and basic health skills. (vii) The number of PLW with moderate acute malnutrition treated. (viii) The number of face masks distributed, water tanks provided at all the facilities, sanitizers availed and awareness materials printed and pinned on every facility.

Mitigation Measures

GEWDO's programming, and aid activities, are meant to promote safety, dignity and integrity of the beneficiaries. The organization has placed the beneficiaries' involvement at all levels of decision-making process including addressing the prevalent needs to include their opinion in daily programming. Inclusion of all the beneficiaries including the people with disabilities and the minorities are structured under a targeting approach that harness the input and opinion of all the stakeholders. Inclusive and targeted beneficiries' selection will ensure only needy persons are equitably, and collectively, selected, harness community support and eliminate any social tension. Safe access is also a fundamental pillar in GEWDO programming. Planning for the screening and faclity-based activities will be structured such that most of the activities will be conducted in the morning hours to reduce exposure to protection risks. Additionally, the project will support efforts towards ensuring that beneficiaries are not exposed to high risk through moving the distribution activities closer to the community. The facilities will have safe planning including separate washroom and saintation facilities. GEWDO will work with other community stakeholdersto ensure the project brings about unity and progress rather than any form of division; clear communications and transparent engagaement will be a qualitative approach at all levels of project planning. The organization's management will be a strictler to its own policies including PSEA, Child Protection, Code of conduct and HRM which will support quality maintenance and cover protection needs around project management. WHile protection risk can adopt different dynamics, the organization will fully engage with the community, the local administration and make efficient feedbcak mechanism in the project prioritization. The organization will share a public feedbcak number and notices in local language for Complaints and Feedback.
