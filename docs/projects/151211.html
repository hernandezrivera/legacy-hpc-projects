Needs

Many international organizations express the extreme concern about the situation prevailing in eastern Ukraine as a result of the ongoing armed conflict. In particular, the attention of the world community is focused on the observance of the norms of international humanitarian right and human rights in the region.According to leading experts, the population of the region is becoming more and more vulnerable as the armed conflict progresses. Tensions in the society, high unemployment, low social benefits and pensions, loss or damage of own property are all factors have negative affect on the psychophysical condition of the inhabitants of the region. Depression, anxious and obsessive states cover an increasing number of residents who suffer daily from shelling, administrative restrictions, and hardships of wartime. The project is implemented taking into account gender balance, in case of its violation targeted measures are expected to be adjusted in favor of the missing target group. In general, the situation with gender balance in the region is favorable, however, in the conditions of war, there are certain disputes related to the fact that women and girls are in a more vulnerable positionAmong the population of the Donetsk region, the elderly are especially vulnerable (men and women over 60) and people with chronic diseases who live near the contact line. Often, elderly people and people suffering from chronic diseases are forced to solve their current problems on their own, as their younger relatives and friends left the territory of Donbass.We especially note that many residents of the region are not sufficiently informed about their rights and about how they can work together in order to solve problems that arise. The fragmentation of citizens, attempts to survive in extreme conditions alone, as a rule, lead to the aggravation of the socio-economic situation.Considering the above, there is a need in psychological and social assistance and legal assistance to the residents of the Donetsk region.

Activities & outcomes

1.To conduct a primary analysis of the psychological and socio-economic state of the most vulnerable inhabitants of the region, as well as to identify their main legal problems.2. To equip the premises for work with conflict victims in the region (5 locations).3. To provide adequate psychological and social assistance to the most vulnerable residents of the region, by providing individual counseling of psychologist, targeted assistance in the purchase of clothes, footwear, household goods and medical supplies.4. Provide to the victims of the conflict to the residents of the region the comprehensive legal assistance, in the form of consultations, on issues of compliance with and protection of their rights, as well as provide legal support for their actions to solve urgent problems.5. Conduct seminars and training for the subjects of social enterprises, municipal self-government, dedicated to the methods of solving the urgent tasks of the community and the issues of consolidation of citizens.Monitoring the quality of implementation of planned activities and feedback to residents of the region on their satisfaction with the results of the assistance provided will meet the requirements of the regulatory document “Suggested Actions for Cluster Coordination Groups to Strengthen Accountability to Affected Populations and Protection in the Humanitarian Programme Cycle (September 2016).

Indicators & targets

Objective1: improving the socio-economic situation and maintaining the psychophysical condition of Donbas residents suffering from conflict at the proper level # of people receiving psychological assistance: at least 100 people, including 55 women and 45 men. # of people receiving social assistance: at least 150 people, including at least 75 men and 75 women. Objective 2: reducing the number of legal problems of residents of the region # of people receiving legal consultation: 50 people, 25 men and 25 women. Objective 3: consolidation of Donbas communities for the solving urgent problems of livelihood. # of trainings: 5 (2 seminars and 3 trainings) # of people trained: at least 70 participants, including 30 men and 40 women The ratio of the number of women and men who will receive appropriate assistance under this project, was adopted based on the analysis of our practical activities.
