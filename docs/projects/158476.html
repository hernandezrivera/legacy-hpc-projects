Project Summary

In response to the Letters from the Manhush Raion Administration and Talakivka Village Administration addressed to the NGO “Ukrainian Deminers Association” (hereafter also – Association) experts of the Association has been developed the project that will provide for an integrated approach to humanitarian demining on the territory of Manhush Raion and Talakivka Village Administrations of the Donetsk Oblast. The project developed in accordance with new Law on Mine Action in Ukraine and based on international standards of mine action (IMAS).
This project includes:
Non-Technical Survey (NTS), Mine Risk Education (MRE), Marking Potential Dangerous Zone, Technical Survey (TS) and Battle Area Clearance (BAC) of the territory in the area of the settlement Talakivka (south-eastern part) about 100 hectares;
NTS, MRE and Marking Potential Dangerous Zone of the territory in Manhush Raion.
Mine risk education with local population based on IMAS 12.10:2013 and marking of potential dangerous zones based on IMAS 08.40:2013 and the New Ukrainian Regulation on marking mines.
Works of humanitarian demining will finish by External Quality Control inspection of the Demining Centre of MoD (Kamianets-Podilskyi). All tasks of this project will be carried out taking into account the needs of the local administrations of the region, which are indicated in letters to the NGO “Ukrainian Deminers Association” (letter attached).
The aim of MRE (as a part of the NTS complex approach) is to help reduce the risk from mines and ERW to a level where people can live safely in the presence of ERW hazards.
The strategic directions of MRE:
 More direct sessions in at risk communities better targeting the groups adults male and female and adolescents;
 Focus on school children;
 Adaptation MRE to school curricula;
 Coordination with other MRE actors. Also possible joint our MRE with ICRC training
Generally, the project has two main aims:
to help reduce the risk from mines and explosive remnants of war (ERW) to a level for the local people in the territory of people Manhush Raion and Talakivka Village Administrations including strategic area near the Entry-Exit Checkpoint (EECP) Gnutovo;
to adapt to new Law on Mine Action in Ukraine and New Ukrainian Regulations on Mine Action.

Needs

Mine and unexploded ordnance (UXOs) contamination affects the lives of two million people in the eastern part of Ukraine, putting vital civilian infrastructure facilities at risk, inhibiting freedom of movement, access to livelihoods and limiting recreational activities for children. Ukraine ranks amongst the most severely affected places in the world for casualties; as a result, of landmines and other ERW. According to the annual report Landmine Monitor 2018 published by the Geneva International Centre for Humanitarian Demining (GICHD) placed, Ukraine placed 3d in the world for a number of casualties. On a monthly basis, the number of civilians injured or killed by these hazards regularly outstrips those as a direct result of an active conflict. Landmines, ERWs and UXOs were the leading cause of child casualties in 2017-2018, accounting for about two-thirds of all recorded deaths and injuries that left many children with lifelong disabilities. A recent GICHD’s report Ukraine as number one in the world, ahead of both Afghanistan and Syria, for anti-vehicle mine casualties. These statistics come as an immediate result of the ongoing conflict in eastern Ukraine, which has seen significant areas of land contaminated by landmines and UXO in both government-controlled (GCA) and non-government controlled areas (NGCA). Concerted efforts have been mounted in GCA to tackle the issue of explosive hazard contamination; both by the Government of Ukraine and the international community, but critical challenges remain. In NGCA, little to no coordinated action has been taken, whilst the situation is understood to be acute.Accordingly to the “Plan activities of arrangement for humanitarian demining release territory of Donetsk and Lugansk regions on 2019” issued by Ministry of Defence” the Manhush Raion and territory of Talakivka Village Administration of the Donetsk Oblast are high priority regions for humanitarian demining.The territories of the Talakivka Village Administrations is strategic area for Ukraine due to presence there the Entry-Exit Checkpoint Gnutovo. Every day EECP Gnutovo crossed by a thousand people who, in turn, are faced by the danger of mines and ERW.As a result of hostilities in the territory of the Talakivka Village Administration, there was an urgent need for humanitarian demining activities. In the period from 2015 to 2018, 4 accidents occurred on the territory of the Talakivka Village Administration caused by EOD, which resulted in victimization among the local population, farm animals, damage to equipment. The implementation of these measures will significantly increase security in the area and preserve the lives of the local population including peoples who cross EECP Gnutovo, as well as contribute to the economic development of the Manhush Raion and Talakivka Village Administrations.
