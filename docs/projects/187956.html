Project Summary

Supporting wheat value chain by providing the assistance to 1400 farmers (males and females),1400 hectares in A’zaz and Afrin, 700 farmers will be supported in 2022 and another 700 farmers will be supported in 2023, where the main activities are:, providing farmers with theoretical and practical extension training sessions, supporting them with the harvesting process, marketing their crops, and establishing units for extinguishing the fires that may occur prior to the wheat harvest, additionally, TAS will support the market power by purchasing part of the wheat crops of the supported farmers to allow them to encourage them to cultivate wheat in the next season. Project’s locations were identified based on clusters reports, HNAP reports, HRP, and detailed need assessments conducted by the TAS team in Mar 2021.
TAS already secured the fund needed for implementing this project in 2022.

5 - Implementation of activities

Supporting Wheat Value Chain by providing assistance to 1400 farmers, 1400 hectares in A’zaz and Afrin, where the main activities are:• Theoretical and Practical Extension Services.• Spot Check Visit and Providing Consultancy Services.• Removing Undesired Weeds and Dealing with Diseases.• Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR)• Harvesting and Crops Marketing.• Market Power Support: by purchasing part of farmers' wheat crops (500 kg from each farmer), totaling of 700 ton which will be filtered and processed through a contracted mill, in order to produce 560 tons of wheat flour (which will be baked and distributed as free bread) in addition to the production of 140 tons of bran (kind of fodder for livestock) (which will be distributed for 466 livestock breeder).
