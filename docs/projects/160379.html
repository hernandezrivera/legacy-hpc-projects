Project Summary

Nutrition situation in the country remain at a grips of serious humanitarian interventions throughout 2019 attributed by combination of factors like poor harvest, past conflicts, displacement and lack of basic needs. 2019 January - July IPC analysis informs that food security is expected to worsen with projection of estimated 63% of total population at IPC 3 food insecurity an increase of 11% from the 57% population affected in 2018.2018/19 Crop and Food security assessment mission report indicates that the estimate net cereal production is at 15.5% below the 5 year average & 2.5% less compared to the 2017/18 production indicating food security will continue to deteriorate. Malnutrition situation in former Unity state Mayendit, leer and Panyijar counties and former Lakes state Yirol west and Cueibet counties continue to be a major concern showing localized pockets of humanitarian needs attributed to severe food insecurity poor access to health nutrition services , extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene . According to August 2019 – April 2020 IPC analysis projects that IPC 3 food insecurity is expected in Leer and Mayendit while January – June IPC analysis cites panyijar, Yiro west, Cueibet at IPC 4 with population at IPC 5. Thus in this project UNIDOR seeks Continue providing lifesaving services to the vulnerable population with keen focus to children 6-59 months, pregnant and lactating women and people with disabilities through provision of integrated nutrition services. Treatment and rehabilitation of children suffering from severe acute malnutrition (SAM) 1098 leer, 1243 panyijar 1380 mayedit 1460 Yirol west and 1410 in Cueibet and Moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) in 1217 leer, 2617 panyijar 3917mayedit 1610 yirol west and 1640 Cueibet and treatment of 120 boys and 130 girls with severe acute malnourished children with medical complications in the 2sc in mayendit and treatment of 11,085 PLW in the respective counties and provide linkages and referral within other sectors like WASH, FSL,HEALTH protection/GBV, Education through provision of key interventions like OTP,TSFP, SC. UNIDOR will reinforce the component of GMP in all the nutrition sites and ensure integration of Early childhood development to ensure there friendly spaces for stimulation for the children U5. Surveillance through continuous program monitoring including the SMART surveys and Mass MUAC screening will be reinforced. Routine community mobilization and sensitization, active case finding, referrals and follow ups at the household level will be strengthened in all the components with key MIYCN practices, nutrition, hygiene and promotion messages. The main objective of this 2020 project is to ensure; 1, Timely, qualitative and quantitative lifesaving management of acute malnutrition for the most vulnerable and at risk children U5, girls and boys , pregnant and lactating women reaching at least 80% of SAM, 65% MAM and 55% PLW in mayendit , leer,panyijar,yirol west and cueibet counties. Increased access to maternal and infant young children nutrition preventing under nutrition to the vulnerable and at risk communities, Increased integration of Nutrition with WASH,FSL,HEALTH ,GBV/CP and Education activities in the 5 counties in line with the set cluster strategic objective two, continuous nutrition situation monitoring, analysis and utilization of the early warning information for timely coordination response and decision making and ensure we report to the cluster and taking part in assessments like FSNMS and surveys as per sector objective 4. UNIDOR will ensure continued nutrition activities through Community screening, treatment of MAM/SAM children U5 and PLW, capacity building the nutrition and health workers by conducting CMAM,MIYCN,GMP,ECD&RDT trainings &reaching mothers/caregivers with MIYCN messages, Malaria testing for SAM children Vitamin A supplementation for children 6-59 months twice a year and Deworming of children 12-59 months.
