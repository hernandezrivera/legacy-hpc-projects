Project Summary

Increased concerns of exposure to gender-based violence (GBV) are recorded at points of entry, as a result of the increasing aﬄux of returnees and limited availability of protection sensitive quarantine facilities to host them. Furthermore, as a result of increased “border jumping” and smuggling in persons, exacerbation of exposure to Sexual exploitation and abuse is expected on the increase. Women and girls remain among the most vulnerable to the risk of GBV, while men, boys, women and girls returnees are exposed to increased stigmatization and community neglect due to the fear of COVID infections. Priority needs at quarantine facilities include availing NFIs that ensure dignity of the most vulnerable, psychosocial support as well as dissemination of critical information on available GBV multi-sectoral services. The needs for sensitization of quarantine centres personnel on the establishment of GBV complaints mechanisms, psychologic ﬁrst aid and GBV referral pathways remains critical. In order to respond to the heightened GBV concerns for returning migrants in and around quarantine facilities, UNFPA with support from
international and local NGOs will scale up GBV mitigation and response interventions
targeting a total of 5,000 returnees across 27 districts. The proposed interventions aim at:
1) Enhancing community-based GBV surveillance, through the recruitment, training and deployment of Behavior Change Facilitators (BCFs), to enhance monitoring of GBV risk exacerbation at border posts, within and nearby quarantine facilities, safety audits, establishment of GBV and SEA complaints mechanisms/ committees/help desks, provision of PSS through W/G safe spaces, referrals to GBV essential services, including through direct link with remote and mobile services – Hotlines, distribution of Dignity kits and MHM supplies
2) Strengthening the availability and accessibility of life saving multi-sectoral survivor-centered GBV services, through the set up of mobile OSCs nearby QFs and at points of entry
3) Improving the capacity of Quarantine facilities personnel through trainings of QFs multi-sectoral staff on COVID19 and GBV impact, PFA, the survivor-centred approach, referral pathways and interaction with specialized GBV service providers, PSEA, in line with IASC standards.
