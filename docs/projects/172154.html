Project Summary

he El-Nino induced drought phenomenon has adversely affected the provision of safe water,increasing the risk of WASH related diseases among the vulnerable communities. According to RWIMS, access to safe water in rural areas remains a challenge with only 30 per cent of the 55,709 water sources providing water from a protected source.
The ZIMVAC Assessment report 2020, showed that 17% of households are travelling more than 1km to fetch water from the nearest main water source while 53% are travelling more than 500m. This travel distance will increase as more water sources dry up thereby increasing the burden of fetching water on children, women and girls who are forced to travel longer distances in search of safe water. The scarcity of water exposing women and girls to the risk of sexual exploitation and abuse and is forcing communities to compromise on safe sanitation and hygiene thereby increasing the risk of WASH related diseases.
The project will restore access to sufficient water of appropriate quality and quantity to fulfil the basic needs of children and women and alleviate suffering among vulnerable communities. High yielding water sources that have the potential of serving more people will be targeted. The project is targeting 22902 beneficiaries (Chipinge 8147 & Centenary/Muzarabani 14755) with access to safe water of appropriate quantity to fulfill basic needs.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

During the designing of the project, a consultative methodology was used through:• A feasibility study which carried out first to establish the priorities, capacities and recommendations of the prospective community beneficiaries. • The community will formulate committees responsible for every day running of the project including among others facilitation of distribution of inputs in close collaboration with implementing partners and resolving disputes as they arise with the support of community leaders. In order to get feedback from the community we propose to conduct evaluations complemented by hotline, helpdesk and suggestions box.
