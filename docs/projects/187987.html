Project Summary

Mercy-USA through its field team will target 9500 HH with USD$100 one-off emergency cash per HH (MPC) to cover the survival needs of the affected people. This intervention is aligned with FSL cluster and CWG to ensure the value covers the agreed SMEB value of 100USD.
The fund for this project is partially secured (60%) to be implemented during 2022
Mercy-USA will respond to any sudden/onset crises born from natural hazards (flooding, fire, snowstorm) or man-made (displacement) across NW Syria with coordination through FSL, SNF, WASH clusters and Cash Working Group to avoid any overlap.
Mercy-USA will conduct vulnerability assessment and verification before the cash distribution and will ensure the response to be implemented within 7 days from the emergency to ensure effective response is in place
Mercy-USA will have contract with Financial Service Provider (FSP) to cover all targeted locations for timely response to any emergency, so the FSP has the capacity for cash distribution with huge amount.
Locations will cover broad area in NW Syria at the district level: Ariha, Harim, Idleb, Jisr Ash-Shugur, Afrin, Jebel Saman where Mercy-USA has full access, even though we will focus on four sub-districts: Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Jandairis, and Darkosh
The project will be implemented directly by Mercy-USA without IPs
PTT will be possible option for the MPC response in Afrin by transferring to the FSP who will be distributed to beneficiaries under supervision of Mercy-USA team

4 - Needs

Based on the HNO 2021, In North-west Syria, the number of people living in last-resort IDPs sites reached 1.5 million individuals by November 2020, up from 868,000 individuals in November 2019. s, as of November 2020, CCCM monitored 1,214 IDP sites in the north-west, of which 1,113 were self-settled, 83 were planned sites, 12 were collective centers and six were reception centers. In HNAP Reports: in NW Syria 4.4 million people from them 2.7 million IDPs. Additionally, according to REACH ENT weekly bulletins between 3-9 January 2022: Multi-purpose cash grants are a first priority need for 73% of the assessed communities •In 45 communities (92%), KIs reported that the preferred modality of assistance for newly arrived IDPs was cash assistance. Based on the RNA that has been conducted immediately following the snowstorm and flood in 2nd half of January 2022, In Maaret Tamsrin, Afrin and Jaindaris, Mercy-USA just reported 23 affected IDPs sites due to flood and snowstorm, the sites are self-settled and they are most vulnerable to bad weather conditions during the winter season. The most need was heating fuel, shelter, food and safe water. In The NW Syria Market Monitoring Exercise 1st-8th Nov 2022 REACH: MEB November value: 135 USD- increase of4% (compared to October) and 17% compared to MayMercy-USA Rapid Market assessment shows: 70% of the assessed retailers have the ability and capacity to restock the food items on daily basis, 5%on weekly basis, 25% several times a week

5 - Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA conducts rapid cash feasibility assessment in area for newly displaced (market accessibility, functionality, risk analysis), while engaging with local authority, host community; and coordinating with other humanitarian organizations planning responses in same area to avoid overlap and ensure complementarity. . Coordination will be with related stakeholders including local authority, camp management, IDPs representatives and other organization as well as with FSL, SNFI, and Wash clusters and CWG, Following beneficiary verification and determination that MPC is most timely and appropriate modality, Mercy-USA distributes one-off emergency MPCA based on the agreed and harmonized value of SMEB 100 USD in cash per HH along with pre-vetted contracted Financial Service Provider (FSP) in USD currency as the FSP have the capacity, in very limited cases may TRY currency can be used for future based on availability of USD, •Targeting: -Eligibility: Newly displaced HHs (30 days or less) from new emergencies (weather-related, fire, attacks)-Vulnerability: blanket approach as the targeting will be for vulnerable affected sites and the needs is common between all of the affected populationMOU will be signed with LCsCFM channels: WhatsApp, hotline, email, complaints box helpdesk and in person.Within one month following the distribution, Mercy-USA M&E will conduct the PDM taking into consideration the minimum requirement harmonized by REACH with support from SNFI cluster and CWG.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks:1.Decreased availability of cash in project locations, meaning that FSPs are unable to cash out or deliver cash assistance2.The closure of borders and/or key markets creates a problem of supply for other markets and industries,resulting in price increases and product shortages3.Beneficiaries are pressured into paying ‘taxes or fees in relation to CVA’ out of their aid transfers4.COVID-19 outbreaks lead to reduce FSP activity5.Heightened visibility for beneficiaries/exposure 6.Cash not being equally shared/distributed within the HH7.Abuse of Authority by distribution actors (GBV,SEA, discrimination) Mitigation measures1.Mercy-USA will consider a shift in modalities, or to use a combination of modalities (cash, vouchers, and in-kind)2.Mercy-USD will conduct regular market monitoring to assess price and availability, and programming should be adjusted as needed3.Mercy-USA will conduct awareness to the beneficiaries that the assistance is free of charge and they are not required to pay ‘fees’ or ‘taxes’ out of it, this information will be printed out on the banner in each distribution site, complaint feedback mechanism will be in place4.Mercy-USA will arrange to longer distribution period with limited number of beneficiaries per day, as well as door-to-door distribution for most vulnerable household exposed to COVID-195.Engage with relevant authorities as appropriate. 6.Vary the payment days 7.Ensure close monitoring by female aid workers4.Sign COC by staff


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

SEA Risks: • Overcrowded during the cash distribution which may cause SEA • Mixed distribution lines for males and females • All distribution staff are males • The cash distribution is not accessible to all gender groups • No shared clear information about timing and amount of cash • No clear procedure regarding loss of card • Taxation on the MPC assistance • No complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) in place Risk mitigation: • Ensure extend distribution days to avoid overcrowding • Separate lines for males and females in all distribution process • Ensure gender balance in the hiring process, ideally to be 50% of the team are female • The cash distribution sites will be chosen to be accessible to all gender groups especially female HH and disables • Clear information about timing, cash amount, and procedures about loss of card of the distribution will be shared with all beneficiaries beforehand and will be existed in the banners in the distribution sites • Sensitization about the scope of the project to all communities with highlighting “This assistance is Free” and this statement will be printed on the beneficiary cards. • Different CFM channels will be in place such as WhatsApp, hotline, email, phone as well as physically by MEAL team. • PSEA awareness materials (such as posters and pamphlets) and reporting hotline will be visible to beneficiaries at the distribution site


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks: -Markets are not functioning which may affect the use of MPC -General daily security threats -Danger to staff and beneficiaries including conflict between beneficiaries. - Delays in fund dispersal - Exchange rate fluctuations between USD and TRY and SYP -Uncontrolled Spread of COVID-19 pandemic in NW Syria which may cause complete lockdown -FSPs offering over-the-counter services do not have sufficient capacity to meet higher caseload -FSPs forced to reduce staff and office hours, limiting over-the-counter service -Limited trading; market shutdown -Critical items unavailable due to market closures, increased demand and hoarding Mitigation: -Regular price monitoring and markets assessment to ensure this intervention will not cause harm to the local markets -Currency is USD and in case of huge currency fluctuation, Mercy-USA might increase the transfer value in coordination with CWG or change the modality-Consider market-based programming, supporting critical markets/traders (actors across the supply chain, including small retail shops) traders directly, by providing them capital to augment their stock of essential goods FSP vetting will be done in coordination with donors to exclude any FSP listed in the sectioned list of OFAC for US treasury, SAM, UNSC.Local Authority interference in cash distribution, mitigation: by signing MOU with LC and camp management to clarify roles and responsibilities of each party without interference with Mercy-USA team to be independent.
