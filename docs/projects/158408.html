Project Summary

The Cyclone Idai weather system swept through five provinces – Sofala, Manica, Tete, Zambezia and Inhambane – decimating livelihoods and leaving a wake of death, destruction and damage. The Cyclone damaged nearly 500,000 hectares of crops, along with other livelihoods, right before the annual harvest. This number is expected to rise as the full extent of the damage becomes known. Extensive livestock losses are expected, as well as widespread damage to fisher folks’ assets. Many assets and structures, including markets in the port city of Beira, have been destroyed. Food insecurity is therefore expected to rise significantly in the months ahead as a result of the destruction and damage. Women in Mozambique play a critical role in agricultural production, providing labour to support the production of food crops and also supporting the production of cash crops. At the same time, women remain responsible for the majority of caregiving. With the caregiving burden likely to increase in the aftermath of the cyclone and floods – as illness and injury have risen – women may have to decrease their agricultural production, further heightening food insecurity.
Goal: Resilience of Cyclone Idai Affected Communities strengthened though general food distribution, own food production, income generating activities and disaster risk reduction.
Lifesaving interventions: Increased access to nutritious food
Activities:
• Food distribution
• Food for asset
• Where feasible Cash for work
• Pilot targeted cash transfers.
• Promote vegetable growing through kitchen gardens to increase dietary diversity
Early Recovery: Objective 2: Increased food production
Activities:
• Agriculture support activities- access to agricultural inputs (drought resistant/ locally adaptable seeds and tools).
• Livestock support interventions, restocking birds (poultry), small ruminants (sheep and goats), rabbitry, piggery.
• Vaccination campaigns
• Community seed multiplication initiatives linked to markets
• Promote and support good agronomic practices that enhance quality production using techniques that are friendly to the environment
• Promote fertility enhancement and management intervention using organic and compost manure.
• Promote soil and water management structures to control flood and soil erosion.
• Rehabilitation of key livelihood infrastructure such as roads and water facilities (check dams, water catchments and reservoirs)
• Promote and scaling up water use efficient methodologies and technologies such as conservation agriculture;
• Support agriculture value chains (value addition)- support to develop businesses, promote SME
Objective 3: Increased income to promote self-reliance
Activities
• Establish and promote saving and loan schemes using Saving for Transformation model
• Business facilitation interventions to enable small businesses and other income generating activities to grow and thrive.
• Entrepreneurship and life skill trainings that enhance their personal competence
• Promote and strengthen Disaster Risk Reduction and mitigation activities.
