Project Summary

During 2022, under the Joint Reponses Plan (JRP) for the Health Sector in Ukhiya, Cox's Bazar, Prantic Unnayan Society has planned to implement Health Program in the Rohingya Camp and in the Host Community to complement to achieve the overall goal of the Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh to uplift the health status of the forcefully displaced Rohingya Community of Myanmar and disadvantaged host community. Overall focus of the intervention would be to limit the crises of health issues of the population in the camp and in the host community. In order to fulfill the above requirement, specific actions to address the need of health care and services will include but not limited to – (1) Establish household-based health status monitoring system through assigning CHWs to identify new patients to refer to PRANTIC Clinic and follow-up old patients, (2) Curative treatment for Outpatients including IMCI, NCD, ORC and diagnostic services through primary health facilities both in RC and HC, (3) Preventive Services, (4) Provided Mental Health and Psychosocial Support according to WHO’s Mental Health Gap Action Program both in RC and HC, (5) Provide SRHR including services to survivors of Sexual and GBV both in RC and HC, (6) Provide community outreach services including community-based surveillance, health education and follow up both in RC and HC, (7) Provide Referral services by establishing a referral chain with stretchers and ambulances to tertiary health facilities both in RC and HC, (8) Maintain regular communication with the health sector, information management following EWARS and reporting through HIS both in RC and HC, (9) Provide Dental service to the targeted patient both in RC and HC, (10) Provide emergency medical service through Mobile Medical Team to meet emergency health requirements both in RC and HC in collaboration with MMT WG (11) Ensure infection prevention and control within the health post by a trained IPC team, (12) Provide Ophthalmology service to the targeted patient both in RC and HC, (13) Construction or Re construction of a Primary Health Care Center including procuring medical instrument and others. (14) Formation of a team of volunteers from Rohingya and Host community who will perform some sort of popular theater / role play/ street drama regarding health related awareness issues as well as cross cutting issues as mention in JRP Guideline . (15) Observation of related international days, such as: World Humanitarian day, World Women's day, World Health Day, Human Rights Day, etc. jointly with the Rohingya and the Host Community. In addition to provide vaccination services and EPI, the project will collaborate with the Government of Bangladesh Health Directorate because the Directorate already runs a program for Refugees vaccination. Prantic believes this collaboration and supporting each other will help our government to reach the national goal of vaccination. It is to be mentioned that although the project will include community and house based health monitoring system and community outreach services to ensure accountability and transparency of the services delivered but the project will use monitoring tools as followed by the Community Health Worker Working Group (CHWG) for monitoring project activities in the field.
Prantic Unnayan Society has been conducting a health post (UID # 040) in Camp 04 of Kutupalong, Ukhiya since 2018 with the funding support of the OBAT Helpers Inc. USA. OBAT is collecting funds from individuals and other fund raising organizations like OBAT Canada, Med Global, Refugee Crisis Foundation (RCF), Human Concern International (HCI), Bee Humble and others. At present OBAT is facing constraint to provide required funds to support all activities of the existing Health Post. So, we are looking forward to other interested donor/s to support us to implement the proposed project.

1 - Identified Needs

Prantic Unnayan Society (PRANTIC) has been implementing all projects successfully in Camp-4 and in the Host Community with due diligence to the need of the beneficiary as well as to the direction of the local administration. The plan for JRP 2022 for the ongoing Health Post has been done in consultation with the beneficiary and the local administration and related stakeholders working in the Rohingya Camp and in the Host Community.The Rohingya refugees do not have access to good housing, safe drinking water, and good sanitation systems, which in turn, increase their vulnerability to a wide range of infectious diseases. Besides, Rohingya people are exposed to war-related traumatic events, loss of family members, witnessing extreme violence, and injury or loss of property. These events have the potential to make Rohingya refugees suffer from different psychological distress. PRANTIC is, therefore, assessed the need of implementing Health Program in the Rohingya Camp as well as in the Host Community to complement to achieve the overall goal of the Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh to uplift the health status of the forcefully displaced Rohingya Community of Myanmar as well as of the disadvantaged host community. So specific needs of the project will include (a) Provide health services and medical treatment to Rohingya and disadvantaged Host Community, (b) Provide dental treatment through the dental unit, (c) proper diagnosis of diseases through Quality laboratory Services, (g) Provide Mental and Sexual Health & Rights, (g) Extend medical services for Gender Based Violence (h) Running a full pledged Mobile Medical Team to meet emergency health supports, (i) Running a well-trained infection prevention team to control nosocomial infection, etc. In order to fulfill the above needs, specific actions to
