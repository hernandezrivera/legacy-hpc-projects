Project Summary

The project will augment protection interventions in Gedo and Lower Juba regions including hard-to-reach areas addressing protection issues worsened by drought and conflicts with a special focus on displacement, family separation, psychological distress, child soldiering, GBV and other distressed coping strategies including child labor, force/child marriage among others. Acknowledging the robust expertise and capacity of drought-hit communities, community-based protection mechanisms will be designed jointly, to increase prevention of and response to GBV and child rights violation.
Protection oriented direct and urgent assistance to survivors affected by GBV, family separation and other child protection issues will be scaled-up; referral pathways will be strengthened. Mobile outreach activities including out-door CFS, awareness raising on Child rights and harmful practices including FGM and child marriage will be scaled up to reach children and their caregivers directly
Aimed at cushioning the impact of the humanitarian emergencies on children and their families, SEDHURO will focus on strengthening community-based child protection mechanism and provision of critical child protection services including IDTR to unaccompanied and separated children, case management and referrals, provision of age and gender appropriate psychosocial support services for children and their caregivers, awareness raising to mitigate risk and capacity building of duty bearers to effectively respond to the protection needs in line with Minimum Standards for CPMS..
This project is informed by good practice from 2019 and will establish linkages with and draw lessons from the existing SEDHURO child protection and GBV activities. Community-based protection mechanisms are already established by SEDHURO and are in place for quick scale-up and sustained response thanks to UNICEF, SHF, UNFPA, NCA, DRC and NRC. These include existing Child Protection Committees (CPCs) in Gedo, Kismayo and Afmadow, Community-Based Child Protection Mechanism (CBCPM) in Beledhawo, Community Discussion Leaders (CDL) in Beledhawo and Luq, Children Clubs in Luq and Bardera, child friendly facilities in Dollow, Luq and Bardera.
SEDHURO has learnt child clubs established in schools is an important avenue for encouraging child protection education in schools, disseminate information on child rights, raise awareness on child abuse and a pathway for reporting incidents of family separation, GBV and child abuse in and out of school. The children clubs, which put children at the center of child protection actions with their views heard and acted upon, will be extended beyond to other schools in the project zone.
Deliberate efforts will be made to ensure meaningful participations by the marginalized groups, such as minority clans, people with disabilities, single headed households, women and girls, among others that might be identified in the field.
Women and girls at risk of GBV will be provided with dignity kits. Clinical Management of Rape, psychosocial support (PSS) and socio-economic reintegration will be provided to GBV survivors through a survivor-centered approach ensuring confidentiality, safety, respect and non-discrimination. GBV survivors, UASC and vulnerable returnees will be empowered through VT and conditional cash transfer to be self-reliant and resilient.
Child Friendly Spaces will provide children with protected environments in which they participate in organized activities to play, socialize, learn, and express themselves as they rebuild their live. The center will provide a child-focused and child-friendly environment in which children continue their cognitive development and will further give them opportunities to learn Literacy and numerical skills as well as live skills appropriate for their development and growth all the while finding ways to respond to particular threats to all children including those with particular vulnerabilities.

Project Activities

1. 0 Survivor-centered multi-sectoral support (medical, legal, pss, and safety) and case management (GBV) in L/Juba and Gedo. Provide PSS to 500 GBV survivors to overcome trauma & assume positive roles in communities through coordinated assistances. This will be provided by professional PSS officers with different levels of training on mental health and psychosocial well-being. 200 survivors from inaccessible areas will be provided with transport assistance to enable them access protection services. Emergency & Live-saving medical care including PEPs will be provided to 80 survivors within 72 hours to ensure survivors are safe from STDs and unwanted pregnancy. The project will provide legal aid including information on rights, availability of services and advocacy will be provided to 50 survivors in Gedo and L/Juba.S EDHURO will coordinate with Jubaland Ministry of Gender, Family Affairs and Human Rights, JRA & other relevant community stakeholders in providing representatives & legal aid. 2 safe houses, providing full board and accommodation, will be established in the regions for 40 at-risk survivors to ensure safety and/or uninterrupted service delivery. 1.1 Provision of material assistance (dignity kits; solar lanterns). 2 Safe spaces will be established for 1000 women and girls where they will feel safe physically, emotionally and where they will be able to express themselves without fear of being harmed or judged. In the WGSS, women and girls will socialize and build their social network, acquire Formal and non-formal education, receive safe and non-stigmatizing multi-sectorial GBV services (legal, medicine & psychosocial).400 GBV survivors in all locations will be provided with age-sensitive dignity kits whereas 300 others will be provided with solar lanterns. The Solar lamps will help to improve security, education for children and health.2.0 Child protection case management in Gedo and L/Juba.Identify & document 600 UASC. Trained CP officers will be stationed in all the locations & will coordinate with agencies involved in way-stations management (IOM, ARC, MERCICOPS, DRC), IDP camp management, food distribution points, madarasa/school, medical facilities. Established referral pathways will be used to refer children to service providers. 2.2 Reunify 550 children successfully with their families after successful tracing & verification. 80 children, awaiting reunification, will be placed under foster care arrangement. 30 Foster mothers trained by SEDHURO/UNICEF and who will further receive refresher training will be involved. Counseling sessions will be undertaken for children & foster parents & weekly follow-up will be made. Material support, medical assistances, nutrition & psychosocial support will be provided. 2.3 Strengthen Child Friendly space established by SEDHURO in Kismayo, Luq, Dollow and Bardera to support 4000 children through provision of materials and assignment of Child Friendly space facilitators. The facilities are already in place thanks to UNICEF-and-SHF-funded projects. The project will maintain the facilities through provision of recreational materials and provision of formal and non-formal education. 3.0 Social-economic reintegration and empowerment (livelihood; cash-based assistance) Equip 120 children GBV survivors & other vulnerable groups including minority groups, PLWLD and returnees with marketable skills and market driven VT skills identified through market/demand assessment. SEDHURO will provide 6-months comprehensive VT & basic literacy & numeracy sessions for 120 girls & boys from vulnerable IDPs & host communities. Two weeks of Entrepreneurship & business management training will also be provided. 50 boys & 50 girls will be supported with cash to jump-start business. Place 10 girls & 10 boys in gainful employment related to skills gained through engagement with business communities to solicit support in provision of attachment, mentoring & employment.
