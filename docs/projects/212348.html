Project Summary

After over a decade of conflict repeated displacements and economic decline the humanitarian crisis in NS affects every sector area and population Needs remain high and widespread with almost three-quarters of households reporting they were unable to secure basic necessities
people in Syria saw their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit northern Syria and Türkiye on 6 and 20 Feb 23
In 2024, 16.7M people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria
The project aims to respond to urgent winter needs and provide urgent shelter needs through upgrading sites and providing innovative shelter solutions to ensure better-dignified shelter and measures to limit flood damages and address current emergency needs:
1- Provide winterization assistance:
A-GAN aim through this activity to provide life-saving winterization needs for 2000 HH, 180$ will be allocated per family as agreed with SNFI and CWG
B -Provide heating means items (firewood, coal, diesel, and Perrin) with a 180$ value for 1000 HH where the markets are not functional.
2- Better dignified shelter with associated infrastructure and cooking facilities:
The project is tailored to provide a new durable shelter unit, which is a prefabricated unit (sandwich panel), to be set up as a substitution for the normal tent. The project aims to target 93 HHs of IDPs
3- Infrastructure improvements for 500 HH shelters to mitigate the risk and prevent flood damages considering the HLP through:
A- Graveling of the roads and raising the tents’ grounds.
B- Rain drainage system and channels.
C- Provide and install solar-based points
Protection Risk Analysis:
-Protection risk SEA by humanitarian workers male-dominated, and potential other beneficiaries L: H in terms of reputation, trust I: H, M: Establish a feedback mechanism Recruit female staff and train them. Train staff on code of conduct
-A key Do No Harm principle is to avoid exacerbating additional tensions and conflicts in communities
-Theft and looting; extortion for beneficiaries: a- ensure that complaints and feedback mechanisms are in place for beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries, two-way feedback mechanisms b-Whistleblowing mechanisms and swift agency response to reports of fraud or corruption GAN try to build the capacity of individuals to protect themselves and recover from acts of GBV by encouraging and promoting equal participation of men and women in all community activities and through providing equal opportunities of employment
GAN has developed a policy stating standards of conduct, including acts of SEA, GAN will ensure all staff members sign and adhere to a CoC policy, PSEA and will support the members to access relevant training (PSEA, GBV), ensuring that non-humanitarian stakeholders who will be in touch with beneficiaries such as suppliers will be informed and adhere to the humanitarian principles and CoC
Assumption:
The MTA using the e-voucher modality are available and functioning - Markets are functioning properly - Access to Syria remains possible and safe through the neighboring countries
Risk:
1:the risk of that one MTA service is responsible for transferring the cash: L: low, I: high. GAN has a prior fundamental condition for MTA: the payment is conducted after distribution
2:There is a risk that markets are not functioning properly due to lack of access L: low, I: high. Mitigation strategy: The situation of the market will be monitored regularly by the program team
3:HLP and propriety conflicts:
-Ensure validation of the documents
-Communicate clearly with all the stakeholders
-Involved the camp management committees, local councils, Key Informants in the process
4:Method and location of cash distribution can put vulnerable beneficiaries at risk of robbery or extortion L:H, I:M
-Distribution methodology with clear protection risks analyzed and mitigated
-Door-to-Door distribution

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Needs:At least one-fifth of the IDP population reportedly lived in unfinished or abandoned residential buildings, at least one-fifth of the IDP population reportedly lived in tents. Shelter and repair materials being too expensive was the most commonly reported challenge for HHs to repair their shelter.As winter hit NWS, winterization assistance was among the most frequently reported needs, especially for IDP HHs. However, among the assessed communities in which KIs indicated that winterisation was a top priority need for IDP HHs, 58% reportedly did not receive such type of assistanceActivity 1-A:since cash modality gives beneficiaries flexibility in choosing heating items they need and gives the project the ability to meet all changes in beneficiary needs as all targeted areas have access to the markets and heating items according to the assessment, so blanket distribution will be applied in camps and the prioritized criteria will be applied out-off camps as follows: Female and/or child-headed HHs, disabled members, Elderly-headed HHs, HHs with more than 5 children, Families without a breadwinner, Families with pregnant and lactating womenActivity 3:These sites exposed/could be exposed to floods to mitigate and protect tents from the driftage by water rain or from being partially/totally destroyed during rainstorms, in addition, road graveling /compacted layers of graded gravel for internal roads so this will improve the access to /within camps and prevent camps disconnecting with its surroundings during the heavy rainstorms and facilitating the living conditions and the access of humanitarian aids to population and vulnerable peopleProtection Risk Analysis:this project will have female staff members to deal with sensitive cases, the communication through WhatsApp between our female beneficiaries will be conducted through the female staff and the distribution points will be safe and accessible places, availability of electricity and ventilation and toilets for males separated from femalesPSEA:GAN is a member of PSEA network, attending a monthly meeting and following the guidelines, so to mainstream the protection from sexual exploitation and abuse in the project.PSEA posters will be available at distribution pointsGAN recognizes the rights of all stakeholders particularly the vulnerable to access humanitarian programs without the risk of sexual exploitation or abuse. GAN intends to prevent abuse by implementing procedures to protect beneficiaries through best recruitment practices, staff induction, creating an open and aware culture and assigning clear management responsibilities, ensuring that victims are supported, and perpetrators held to account. Information on the referral pathway will be circulated through posters and banners at the distribution points. The distribution points will be inside the camps and safeguard that there is appropriate segregation for women to create a culturally sensitive and safe operational environment
