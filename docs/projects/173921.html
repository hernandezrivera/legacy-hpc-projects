Project Summary

This project is aimed at providing food and cash cash transfer assitance to IDPs heavily affected by the crisis in the three sub-division of Kupe-Muanenguba Division. This food and cash transfer assistance will target IDPs especially women from the ages of 18-59 paying attention to widows, unmarried IDPs, girls displaced from the ages of 10-17 dispalced from their original locations and facing severe food shortages, people living with disabilities (male and female), elderly people, families hosting large number of IDPs with little food to eat, families who lost their bread winners within the course of the crisis, and families with little or no farm land to carry out small scale agriculture. The food items will help and sustain most women provide food to their children and avoid malnutrition. Also, focus will be on children who lost their parents during the crisis or whose parents have been displaced leaving the children behind with no body as a main source of providing food.
Cash transfers will help women and men (families) get some additional income to either acquired more nutritional foodstuff or carry out a small income generating activity to sustain the family. This will target women, people living with disabilities, youths, men to make families have that ability to avoid hunger through involvement in income generating activities.
Since the year 2016, the North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions of Cameroon, have been experiencing social instability erupting into violence displacing thousands of people and disrupting livelihoods and agricultural activities of various communties. Over 8000 people have been disiplaced in Kupe-Muanenguba Division with Nguti Subdivision heavily hit, removing people from their comfort zones, leaving many persons with little to make-life-keep going under threat of insecurity. Also, host communities too have been affected because the insecurity posed severe threads to local farmers and thus affecting the next year(s) harvest.
As the crisis persists more households have been pushed into food insecurity, shortages specially internally displaced people (IDPs) and farming populations, who are impacted by the disruption of their livelihoods. This project thus targets vulnerable women/homes who in most cases the children look at for food on daily basis. This project is equally needed because IDPs spent much time at home (worsened by COVID-19) with little to eat. Some might be interested to carry out small sustaining agriculture in localities like Tombel and Bangem but have little access to land in a stange environment.
This project is also aimed to fortify foods for children and mothers to respond to high severe acute malnutrition levels, and multi-purpose cash assistance. This will enable some of these vulnerable women get a livilihood to keep the family. This will include diets to children to curb malnutrition and ensure appropriate growth. Its should be noted that since the begining pf the crisis in the NWSW, no intervention has been done in the domain of food security in Kupe-Muanenguba division. Reason while AFRINET have this year following favourable access condition to design a plan of action for intervention in food security. Therefore, the project will pay critical attention to alleviate the nutrition condition of children from the ages of 1-8 years in Kupe-Muanenguba Division. In addition, our nutrition team will design nutrition combination and through workshops with target population trained these beneficiaries on how to maintain good nutrition even at moderate cost.
Despite the slight increase in humanitarian assistance especially in WASH from july 2020 to November 2020, the overall needs remain high in terms of food assistance and livelihood to IDPs and host community in Kupe-Muanenguba Division.
