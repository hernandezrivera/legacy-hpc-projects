Project Summary

We, for the Art (as Swiss association) & Astharté (a Lebanese NGO) have created an art therapy center in Achrafieh for all survivors of the Beirut explosions and people affected by it. The center is located in Karm el Zeitoun ( Al saydi street, maria Building). It is composed of one big area that will be used for art therapies and 4 regular rooms that will be used for both art therapies and individual assessments with social workers.

1 - Needs

With the recent colossal Beirut explosion, a big need to take care of the affected people psychologically has arisen. The damage is not just material, it is now multifaceted and the psychological component is not to be ignored. The centre’s first objectives were limited to the emergency response to the trauma that has befallen us, and to limit the psychological damages of those children affected by working on managing their stress, anxiety and preventing potential PTSD and other issues that might arise. This window of work was limited to 3 to 90 days post the traumatic event.Our team will outreach to children surrounding the center as well as to Nabaa and Bourj Hamoud. We will as well coordinate with other NGOs to respond to their needs.In this time frame, in order to reach the maximum number and be effective trauma prevention wise, we will need to see each affected survivor (whether in individual sessions or group sessions) three times. The social worker will organize transportation when needed and will make the follow-up in order to ensure children will come to the three sessions. Additionally, the social worker would work on referral pathways in case they identify a child or a family who need further support. Starting 2021, our objectives are sustainable practices using various art-therapy forms. We also help individuals cope with emotional and relational concerns, recover a sense of well-being and pursue personal growth.

2 - Activities

We are offering group art therapy at the centre in Karm el Zeitoun and at other NGO's premises The duration is of one hour to one hour and 30 minutes. Group art-therapy are a great way to help those affected move away from the trauma and concentrate on themselves and their resources to help heal and prevent psychological issues related to the trauma. Art-therapies will allow them to express in various forms and give them new perspectives when their world seems to be just about one issue. We will be using various art-therapy forms: musico-therapy, movement therapy, expressive arts therapy etc.. Our team is composed of licensed therapy practitioners who will be supervised and following regular training sessions to allow them to give the best help possible. Due to Covid 19 restrictions and guidelines, our groups will be limited in number.
