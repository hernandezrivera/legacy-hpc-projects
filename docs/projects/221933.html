Project Summary

Cabo Delgado faces complex challenges such as armed conflict, poverty and gender inequality, which directly impact access to education, especially for girls. Female school dropout is a worrying reality, with menstruation, lack of literacy and the conflict context exacerbating the situation.
This project aims to combat female school dropout and promote gender equality in education in Cabo Delgado, through an integrated approach that combines literacy, distribution of sanitary towels and accelerated learning.
Project pillars:
1) Literacy: Offer literacy classes in Portuguese and local languages, with teaching materials suited to the reality of the students and a safe and stimulating learning environment. Literacy will be a foundation for the girls' empowerment and for building a better future.
2) Distribution of sanitary towels: Guarantee free access to sanitary towels and promote menstrual education, fighting taboos and stigma. Menstrual poverty must not be an obstacle to education.
3) Accelerated Learning: Implement accelerated learning programs for girls who have lost years of schooling due to the conflict, allowing them to make up for lost time and re-enter the education system.
With the implementation of these activities we expect to: Increase in the female literacy rate in Cabo Delgado; Reduce school drop-out and increased retention of girls in schools; Improve school performance and future opportunities for girls; Empower women and promote gender equality; and contribute to peace-building and development in Cabo Delgado. Call to Action: This project requires financial support and strategic partnerships to ensure its implementation and sustainability. By investing in this project, you are investing in the future of Cabo Delgado and in building a more just and equal society.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This Response Plan aims to implement the project and ensure that girls in Pemba City and Metuge have access to education and a better future.This project aims to combat female school drop-outs in Pemba City and Metuge through an approach that combines:- Literacy: Classes in Portuguese and local languages, with teaching materials suited to the local reality.- Distribution of sanitary towels: Free access to sanitary towels and menstrual education.- Accelerated learning: Programs for girls who have missed years of schooling due to the conflict.Main target groups:- Adolescent girls who have not had access to formal education or have dropped out, directly benefiting internally displaced persons.- Girls and women affected by the conflict in Pemba City and Metuge District, including the host community.The project seeks to empower girls through education, contributing to gender equality and development in Cabo Delgado.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will prioritize the following activities to combat female school dropouts and promote gender equality in education in Cabo Delgado:1. Literacy:- Literacy classes: Offer literacy classes in Portuguese and local languages (Macua, Kimwani), focusing on reading, writing and basic mathematics. Classes will be held at different times and places, including schools, community centers and shelters, to meet the needs of the students.- Teaching materials: Develop teaching materials appropriate to the students' reality, with content that is relevant to everyday life and that promotes a culture of peace and gender equality. The materials will include books, posters, educational games and audiovisual resources.- Learning environment: Create a safe, welcoming and stimulating learning environment that promotes self-esteem, confidence and participation among female students. Classrooms will be equipped with suitable furniture and teaching materials.2. Distribution of sanitary towels:- Free access: Provide free sanitary towels and other menstrual hygiene materials (such as soap and underwear) to schoolgirls, ensuring that menstruation is not an obstacle to school attendance. Distribution will be discreet and regular, through schools and other access points.- Menstrual education: Promoting menstrual education, breaking down taboos and stigmas around the subject, with special attention to the needs of displaced and conflict-affected girls. Menstrual education sessions will cover topics such as the physiology of menstruation, personal hygiene, reproductive health and female empowerment.- Hygiene conditions: Create or rehabilitate adequate hygiene and sanitation conditions in schools, with access to drinking water and toilets with privacy, considering the infrastructure difficulties in some areas.3. Accelerated learning:- Accelerated learning programs: Implement accelerated learning programs for girls who have lost years of schooling due to the conflict, allowing them to make up for lost time and re-enter the education system. The programs will be flexible and adapted to the individual needs of the students, with a focus on developing essential skills.- Individualized support: Offering individualized support to the students, with pedagogical and psychosocial accompaniment, so that they can overcome the learning difficulties and traumas related to the conflict.- School reintegration: Facilitate the reintegration of students into the formal education system, through partnerships with schools and support in the transition to mainstream education.The project will focus on one of the areas affected by the conflict: Pemba and Metuge DistrictNote: Activities will be adapted to the specific needs of each geographical area, taking into account local challenges and opportunities. The project will work closely with communities, schools and local authorities to ensure its effectiveness and sustainability.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Identifying and mitigating risks are crucial elements in ensuring the safety and success of the project. To this end, we will adopt a proactive approach that includes the following measures:1. Risk assessment:- Context: Analyze the specific context of Cabo Delgado, including the armed conflict, population displacement, gender inequalities and socio-economic vulnerabilities.- Potential Risks: Identify the potential risks associated with the project, such as: o Security: Risks related to the armed conflict, such as violence, forced recruitment and limited access to some areas.o Protection: Risks of abuse, sexual exploitation and gender-based violence, especially for girls and women in vulnerable situations.o Health: Risks related to the physical and mental health of the participants, including psychological trauma, illness and lack of access to health services.o Social and Cultural: Risks of stigmatization and discrimination, especially in relation to girls' education and menstruation.o Logistics and Operational: Risks related to access to materials, transportation, communication and infrastructure.o Sustainability: Risks related to the long-term continuity of the project, including dependence on external funding and local capacity.2. Mitigation Measures:- Security: o Collaborate with local authorities and security organizations to ensure the safety of project participants and teams.o Implement safety protocols, including evacuation plans and communication mechanisms in the event of an emergency.o Prioritize project implementation in safe areas with guaranteed access.- Protection: o Establish mechanisms for reporting and responding to cases of abuse, exploitation and violence.o Train project teams in child protection and gender-based violence prevention.o Create safe and welcoming spaces for girls and women, with access to psychosocial support.o Implement the project in collaboration with child protection and women's support organizations.- Health: o Promote the physical and mental health of the participants, with health education activities, access to health services and psychosocial support.o Integrate menstrual education into the curriculum, promoting girls' health and well-being.o Ensure that schools have adequate hygiene and sanitation conditions.- Social and Cultural: o Raise awareness in communities about the importance of girls' education and combat stigma and discrimination.o Involve community and religious leaders in promoting the project and changing social norms.o Develop culturally sensitive teaching materials that promote gender equality.- Logistics and Operations: o Establish contingency plans to deal with logistical and operational challenges.o Diversify the sources of supply of materials and guarantee their quality.o Strengthen local capacities to ensure efficient project management.- Sustainability: o Develop exit and transition strategies to ensure the long-term continuity of the project.o Strengthen local capacities so that communities and schools can take over project management.o Seek diversified sources of funding and promote financial sustainability.3. Monitoring and Evaluation:- Continuously monitor risks and the implementation of mitigation measures.- Carry out regular assessments to identify new threats and adapt prevention strategies.- Collect data on the impact of the project on the safety, security and well-being of the participants.- Document lessons learned and good practices to improve risk management.By adopting this proactive and comprehensive approach, the project seeks to minimize risks and avoid causing harm, while ensuring the safety, security and well-being of the participating girls and women. Community participation and constant monitoring will be key to ensuring the effectiveness of the mitigation measures and the sustainability of the project.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The inclusion of people with disabilities is essential to guarantee equal access to education and the success of the project in Cabo Delgado. To achieve this, we will implement the following strategies:1. Identification:Partnerships: Establish partnerships with organizations of people with disabilities (OPDs), inclusive schools and other institutions that work with people with disabilities in Cabo Delgado. These partnerships will be essential to identify and reach girls and women with disabilities who can benefit from the project.Community Mobilization: Raise awareness in communities about the importance of inclusion and encourage the participation of girls and women with disabilities in the project. We will use different communication channels, such as community meetings, religious leaders and local media outlets, to reach families and ensure they are informed about the project and its benefits.Questionnaires and Interviews: Include specific questions about disability in questionnaires and interviews conducted during the research and evaluation phase of the project. This will help identify the specific needs of girls and women with disabilities and develop strategies to ensure their inclusion.Note: During visits to communities and schools, project teams will be attentive to identify girls and women with disabilities who may be excluded from the educational system.2. Barrier Removal:Physical: Ensure that the places where project activities will be carried out (schools, community centers, etc.) are accessible to people with different types of disabilities. This includes access ramps, adapted bathrooms, adequate signage and ample spaces for wheelchair users.Communication: Use different communication methods to ensure that project information and teaching materials are accessible to all participants. This includes materials in Braille, sign language, audio description and assistive technologies.Pedagogical: Adapt teaching methodologies and teaching materials to meet the specific needs of students with disabilities. Teachers will receive specific training to work with students with disabilities, using individualized and inclusive teaching methods.Individualized Support: Offer individualized support for students with disabilities, including pedagogical support, psychosocial support and guidance on accessing health and rehabilitation services.Assign tutors or mentors to support students with disabilities, helping them overcome learning difficulties and integrate into the group.Resources and Materials:Provide adapted resources and materials, such as Braille books, audio materials, educational software and assistive technologies, to ensure that students with disabilities have access to information and can fully participate in project activities.Awareness:Promote awareness about the inclusion of people with disabilities among project teams, teachers, community leaders and the community at large. Raising awareness will help combat stigma and discrimination and create a more inclusive environment for all participants.Monitoring:Monitor the participation of girls and women with disabilities in the project and evaluate the effectiveness of inclusion strategies.Collect data on the barriers faced by students with disabilities and adapt strategies to ensure access and participation for all.Carry out periodic assessments to identify challenges and areas for improvement in the inclusion of people with disabilities.By implementing these measures, the project seeks to ensure that girls and women with disabilities in Cabo Delgado have access to education and can fully participate in activities, contributing to their empowerment and the construction of a fairer and more inclusive society.
