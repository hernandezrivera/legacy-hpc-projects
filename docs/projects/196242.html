Project Summary

Considering the risk level in the area, WATAN through this project intends to provide both preventive activities through, raising awareness, empowerment activities and safety audit. and responsive ones through CM including, PFA, MHPSS and referrals to other specialized service providers. The targeted groups by these services are women, men, girls and boys including the PWDs.
WATAN is going to make integration between GBV and MHPSS in health centers.
WATAN will support establishing two WGSS in Harem area and Efrin, and will provide integrated GBV services in health facilities (Idlib, Efrin, and Harem)
Key strategies will be taken into consideration during the implementation:
1- Provide specialized GBV services to survivors with supervision, all cases will be uploaded to GBVIMS + Providing Individual case fund using the GBVSC SOPs,
2- Provide activities empowering women and girls and GBV prevention activities include Awareness-raising toolkit, life skills activities, and vocational training, small grants will be provided for empowerment purposes, WATAN will use the guidance note (Sops of Cash Income Generating Activities -IGAs) developed by UNFPA during the implementation of this activity.
3- Providing and enhancing specialized adolescent girls programming, including life skills programs.
4- Support women and girls with disabilities to reach service delivery points.
5- train humanitarian actors (non-specialist) to reduce risks of GBV and refer to specialized services.
6- Conduct regular safety audit in the formal camps.

3 - Needs

Women and girls reported higher levels of intimate partner violence in 2022 predominantly associated with men spending longer periods of time at home either due to COVID-19 restrictions or unemployment. These forms of violence can cause unwanted pregnancies, permanent disabilities, and increased levels of psychological distress which can sometimes result in suicide. Social stigmatization continues to affect GBV survivors of detention and marginalized women and girls more broadly women and girls are facing physical injury and health complications due to GBV including serious and life-long sexual and psychological issues. There are increased reports of early and forced marriage as well as sexual harassment and exploitation. Cases of femicide are becoming a growing concern in some areas. Lastly, technology facilitated GBV, such as harassment and sexual exploitation committed online has also been reported more frequently. According to Voices2021, women and girls still experienced violence from partners or husbands They also experienced violence from family like brothers and fathers and strangers each day.In certain places like camps they were more likely to experience violence. Those who had a disability were divorced, whose husbands had died were pushed out or treated badly by their family, teenage girls Forced puberty is thus a risk for early marriage, sexual abuse and negatively affects girls physical and mental health through increasing chances of complications in development

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- The lack of access GBV survivors to GBV services due to the COVID 19 pandemic and their exposure to further harm Likelihood: High Impact: High Mitigating WATAN will provide all GBV services remotely and will allow everyone to benefit from this service 2- Community and family stigmatization for participating in programming, family resistance to girl’s mobility. Low participation of girls because of family and community resistance Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating •Targeting adolescent girls with socially accepted activities like life skills as safe entry point •Engage with local authorities and clear communication on project objectives •Secure transportation for safe mobility 3-Lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. Lack of quality services also lead to fewer disclosures and lack of trust in services Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating •Service mapping including only those that can provide survivor cent red services •Promotion of SOPs roll out for all staff of organizations involved in the referral •Generalization of the strategy issued by GBVSC on how to deal with GBV cases for health workers 4- GBV male survival are very discreet to disclose especially for those being detained Likelihood: High Impact: High Mitigating •Deploy male case workers to conduct awareness sessions using Toolkit •Provide them with the knowledge and skills to identify and manage such cases
