Project Summary

Cisda is proposing an Emergency and Resilience light support interventions in three major hot spots food insecure former States of Unity, Warrap and Western Bahr- El Ghazal . The proposed project runs from January 2020 to December, 2020. .
The key objective of the project are to increase Vulnerable household access to food through support to increased food production and to reduce the level of vulnerability amongst malnourished children through linkage of nutrition related activities to Food security activities. This linkage will involve supporting mothers of malnourished children with training and vegetable seeds for their own production; whilst at the same time linking the same households with resilience intervention such as goat, chicken restocking though another implementing partner. The project is line with HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2 and FSL cluster objective 4
The project will target mainly IDPs and Vulnerable host population in these areas. A total of 60,000 people will be targeted in the three States. The program will be delivered through direct distributions of inputs, resilience training in improved vegetable production and vaccination with treatment of livestocks. These activities are mainly agricultural and livestock related and they will be targeted
Women , children, girls that are at high protection risks throughout the implementation of the project . Additionally the project has major consideration for the accountability to affected population, as it will work through Accountability to affected community AAP committees. Feedback mechanisms will be established in the community so that beneficiaries are well informed about the project actions
CISDA has over four years strong experience in implementing similar Emergency food security and livelihood projects in the same geographical context-.there activities has been funded by diverse donors such as FAO , SSHF and IOM. The project thus will leverage on the experiences gained during the past implementations inorder to adequately address the food insecurity problem.
The project will integrate or work closely with both the protection cluster and the nutrition cluster. With protection cluster, the project will support beneficiaries identified by the protection partner that are high protection risk. They will be mapped out and supported during implementation. With the nutrition project, the project will set up demonstration sites at OTP centers and train households with malnourished children. These households will also be supported with high nutritious vegetables which they will produce to address the nutritional needs of their malnourished children.

1 - Needs

The conflict in South Sudan that started in 2013 which have led to over 2 million of people displaced in the Country also have deteoriating effect on the food security situation of the Country . Latest IPC report In August 2019, an estimated 6.35 million people (54% of the population) are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, among whom an estimated 1.7 million people are facing Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity and 10,000 people are in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5). Compared to the same period in 2018, there is a slight reduction in the proportion of people facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity by an estimated 5%. However, high levels of acute food insecurity still persist in the country. The main drivers of this conflict and insecurity, displacement and economic decline in these areas. The IPC report specifically projects that by April, 2020, the food insecurity is expected to deteoriate further with the depletion of the current harvest. In Wau and Jur River the current Food security situation outlook is in phase 3 but expected to deteoriate due the current conflict that have caused displacement in Jur River of 250,000 people into Wau and neighboring payams in Jur River; looting of livelihood assets, poor access to food due to restricted movement to productive farmland, limited access to natural food sources, Seasonally poor feeder road conditions causing low market supply and irregular salary payment to the civil servants and outbreak of fall army worm which extensively destroyed most of the crops By April, 2020, The nutrition situation is further expected to deteoriate generally in the Country of which more than 5.50 million people are expected to be in crisis (IPC Phase3) or worse from January to April 2020. In Jur River and Wau Counties the same trend will of having malnourished children will happen. Generally in these two Counties of former Bahr-el ghazal, a total of 293,150 Households are expected to face food insecurity by April, 2020. In Twic county, Warrap state , which is very much similar to Western Bahr El Ghazal situation , 139, 870 are expected to face high food insecurity. The driving factors in this area are as a result of poor harvest this year due to heavy flooding which affected every households in the community. Malnutrition rate is expected to be phase 3. In Rubkona County, Unity State , a Country that has hosted huge number of displaced population, is expected to have an estimated number of 171,200 people expected by April, 2020 to be faced with high food insecurity. The driving factor in this area is mainly as a result of the conflict, high displacement, poor harvest this year due to extensive flooding and loss of livestocks.To address the expected high food insecurity in the three states, there is need for Emergency livelihood support intervention which will address both long term and mid term food needs for the expected high food insecure population.

Formation and training of Accountability to affected Population committee. Vaccination of livestock Treatment of Livestock.These activities are seasonally realistic and fit the context of the three States.
