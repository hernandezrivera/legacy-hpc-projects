Project Summary

The intervention is aimed to intervene in the protracted health needs by enhancing the access to basic health services, readiness and timely response to emergencies, and enhancing the capacities of health systems to increase the resilience of vulnerable groups in order for them to recover from the shocks associated with vulnerability and displacement in the 4 states. The Intervention targets up to 956,896 displaced individuals and up to 1 million host community members. The intervention will target Pregnant and lactating mothers for this 2021 year amounting to 136,983 and children under five years of 391,379.
The Intervention of aligned to the three strategic objectives of the Health cluster and shall provide interventions to ensure that the 3 outcomes are achieved in the 4 localities. Alignment to the SO1: Increase equitable access to humanitarian lifesaving and life-sustaining basic health services for those residing in high risk areas and those most vulnerable and in need. This will include promotion of access to basic health care implementing the minimum health care package through inpatient and outpatient care. For clients fare from the Health units, mobile teams shall be instituted for integrated community outreaches. World Vision manages a number of Health units in the 35 localities, where a capacity needs assessment in key Healthcare models including EMNOC, management of child hood illnesses and customer care will be done to ensure the system is stronger to offer services to the most vulnerable children. World Vision shall work with CHWs to support health education, behavioral change and community mobilization for services at the household level. World Vision will also suggest supporting a number of isolation centers for COVID-19, support the interventions around the messaging on the prevention of COVID-19, integrate WASH principles and commodities in IDPs to prevent COVID-19 and accelerate referral for the sick. World Vision will strengthen the provision of COVID-19 Personal protection equipment for all frontline workers, and work to ensure there is a transmission reduction in health workers catching the disease. World Vision will work to ensure there is zero stock outs of the vital essential medical supplies for OPD, but also include the 13 lifesaving commodities for Sexual and reproductive health.
For the Second SO2: Strengthen health sector capacity to prepare and deliver timely response to affected population by conflict, natural emergencies, and disease outbreaks. World Vision Sudan will focus on an integrated approach with the WASH sector on encouraging hygiene and sanitation at home and around the home to avoid vector breeding. In the health facilities the Infections prevention and control (IPC) to ensure no infectious diseases are prevented. World Vision suggests to accelerate the strengthening of the expanded program on immunization. This will also facilitate the prevention and control of any immunize able diseases outbreaks. As a treatment strategy World Vision will implement the integrated community case management of childhood illnesses targeting malaria, diarrhea and pneumonia and malnutrition working with CHWs and building a structure of referral to the health facilities. World Vision will focus on fighting malaria transmission by providing mosquito nets and contribute to diagnosis and control of Tuberculosis among the IDP and refugee communities.
For the third SO3: Strengthen health system capacity to enhance vulnerable groups and communities (including IDPs, refugees, and people living with disability) resilience to absorb and respond to shocks. World Vision will focus on prevention and control of gender based violence. The Clinical management of rape and sexual assault shall be implemented. World Vision proposes to also support post exposure prophylaxis (PEP) for any girls who come out to the Health facilities for support. 1 - Needs

About 12.7 million people (28 per cent of the population) including 7.4 million women and girls, will need humanitarian assistance in 2021. There is a general disruption in the health services, including the availability of medicines with only 15 per cent of essential drugs available in the country. Large protracted displacement remains, with limited returns. Pockets of violence reported in Darfur region, and South Kordofan states continue to lead to displacements, tensions and increased humanitarian needs. Sudan remains prone to various hazards, including localized conflict, floods, and cyclical disease outbreaks, which drive humanitarian needs and affect thousands each year. In 2020, almost 900,000 people were affected by floods. More than 79,442 houses and 7,708 feddans of agricultural land were destroyed in what was one of the worst flooding in the last 100 years.The worsening economy of Sudan and protracted health emergencies resulted in increase of number of people who can’t access basic health services and decrease in the health system capacity to deliver the required the needed essential healthcare to the population. This has been worsened by the effects of the global COVID-19 pandemic that further strained the capacity of healthcare providers and affected access to essential health services. By November, total COVID-19 confirmed cases reached 14,090 including 1,115 associated deaths. The high case fatality ratio CFR of 7.9% reflected the prevalent low capacity in case management, and availability of intensive care cadre and equipment.There is low access to safe drinking water, low vaccination coverage, and the effects of seasonal floods are contributing to the resurgence of vaccine preventable, water-borne, and vector borne disease outbreaks. During the first half of 2020 Penta 3 and measles vaccination coverage declines by a 10% from the previous year leaving over 100,000 children in need for immunization catch-up campaigns. Attention to services for GBV survivors, and outreach services for remote communities and displaced population need to be further strengthened.

2 - Activities/Outputs

SO1: Increase equitable access to humanitarian lifesaving and life-sustaining basic health services for those residing in high risk areas and those most vulnerable and in need. Output: Health system strengthened to offer equitable access to services: through services to promote access to basic health care implementing the minimum health care package through inpatient and outpatient care, Mobile teams instituted for integrated community outreaches. Capacity building and technical support supervision in Health units in the 35 localities, in key Healthcare models including EMNOC, management of child hood illnesses and customer care will be done. Work with CHWs to support health education, behavioral change and community mobilization for services at the household level. World Vision will work to ensure there is zero stock outs of the vital essential medical supplies for OPD, but also include the 13 lifesaving commodities for Sexual and reproductive health. Output: Preventive approaches implemented to reduce the spread of COVID-19: Supporting a number of isolation centers for COVID-19, support the interventions around the messaging on the prevention of COVID-19, integrate WASH principles and commodities in IDPs to prevent COVID-19 and accelerate referral for the sick. Strengthen the provision of COVID-19 Personal protection equipment for all frontline workers, and work to ensure there is a transmission reduction in health workers catching the disease. SO2: Strengthen health sector capacity to prepare and deliver timely response to affected population by conflict, natural emergencies, and disease outbreaks. Output: Community Health structures strengthened to prevent infectious diseases: Focus on an integrated approach with the WASH sector on encouraging hygiene and sanitation at home and around the home to avoid vector breeding. In the health facilities the Infections prevention and control (IPC) to ensure no infectious diseases are prevented. As a treatment strategy World Vision will implement the integrated community case management of childhood illnesses targeting malaria, diarrhea and pneumonia and malnutrition working with CHWs and building a structure of referral to the health facilities. World Vision will focus on fighting malaria transmission by providing mosquito nets and contribute to diagnosis and control of Tuberculosis among the IDP and refugee communities. Output: Vaccination services accelerated to improve child survival: The strengthening of the expanded program on immunization by integrating this in outreaches, mobile teams, and static vaccination. Support the district health teams to micro plan for vaccination. This will also facilitate the prevention and control of any immunize able diseases outbreaks. Support operationalization of the EWARN teams and promote surveillance to report any suspected cases at the household level. For the third SO3: Strengthen health system capacity to enhance vulnerable groups and communities (including IDPs, refugees, and people living with disability) resilience to absorb and respond to shocks. Output: Communities mobilized against gender based violence: World Vision will focus on prevention and control of gender based violence. The Clinical management of rape and sexual assault shall be implemented. World Vision proposes to also support post exposure prophylaxis (PEP) for any girls who come out to the Health facilities for support. World Vision health department shall link up with the Safeguarding department to implement a community based GBV preventive program to ensure human protection at the community level.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

World Vision for the last quarter as an organization has spearheading the safe guarding to protect the beneficiaries from any form of exploitations. Our child protection and adult safe guarding principles have been streamlined in our filed level programming. The program staff in their daily duties are tasked to keep reminding lower level staff and community groups on the relevancy of reporting any protection issue. World Vision works closely with local authorities to ensure all accelerated issues are dealt with. World Vision has a reporting mechanism internally if the protection issue involves our own staff at the field level.
