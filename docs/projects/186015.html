Peace and Resilience Building: with this action plan, aims to establish a holistic and systematic approach to building resilience in crisis and risk-prone contexts or area, notably by supporting the populations at risk to withstand, cope with, adapt and quickly recover from stresses and shocks without compromising long-term development prospects, with a focus on efficient interventions having a lasting impact. In times of economic crisis it is more important than ever to ensure that aid is effective, efficient and is focused on results. 8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Creating an environment safe from SEA – Core Principle, establish and provide oversight for the implementation of adequate and relevant policies. Policies should incorporate clear definition of SEAH, expected behavior and sanctions; offer protection to those who report and should be made aware to all personnel. Documented procedures should clearly outline roles and responsibilities, processes for reporting and responding to allegations. Monitor implementation, document and share progress and lessons learnt. PSEA policy: Standard of conduct; mandatory PSEA training; whistle-blower policy, Effective recruitment performance management measures. Training of personnel: including but not limited to PSEAH concept; Reporting procedure; Victim assistance.Appropriate safeguards & procedures to take action while working with IPs, contractors, vendors.
