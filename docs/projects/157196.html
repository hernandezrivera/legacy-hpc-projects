Project Summary

This project aims to support basic health packages and proper healthy nutrition for IDPs and host communities in the states South and West Kordofan with special focus on women, girls and children under 5.
Regarding health, this project seeks to focus on providing health activities that aim to increase access and quality to life saving through integrated primary and reproductive healthcare services including community-based health promotion activities.
It also intends to carry out mobile clinics that include medical doctors, medical assistants, lab technicians, mid wives and trained nurses to deliver medical services for individuals who have little access to the HFs and It will initiate and strengthen the existing referral system for complicated cases for further treatments in nearby towns with superior quality services. The referral system is to include providing of referral cost, transportation and medical cost for the targeted vulnerable communities during the lifetime of the project at 100%.
This project will also organize many awareness rising sessions to increase utilization of life saving health interventions. The sessions will be also organized for target communities during the mobile clinics and outreach immunization campaigns. The projects also plans on training community health promoters and volunteers on health promotion in addition to awareness and ANC sessions related to STIs, HIV/ AIDS
As for immunization, this project plans to train vaccinators and community volunteers on basic immunization training, as well as give support to organize the National Immunization Days and EPI campaigns.
For nutrition services, the project aims to implement CMAM & IYCF activities in order to improve the nutritional status of vulnerable members of the communities especially children under five years of age and PLW in accordance with the guidelines of MoH.
Also aims to support the referral system of SAM children with complications, as well as the referral of pregnant, post partum and lactating women to micronutrient clinics and it will conduct awareness rising sessions for IYCF as well as supporting organizing of breastfeeding week.
This project intends to promote routine screening and MUAC screening for the early detection of malnutrition and providing essential food supplements to prevent the deterioration of the health statuses of those affected.

Needs

According to the 2018 Inter-Agency Assessments conducted in South Kordofan State: [Talodi, South Kordofan] [Oct. 2018], [Rashad, South Kordofan State] [12-26 October 2018] and the Inter- Agency Rapid Assessment Report: Al Abbassiya Locality, South Kordofan State- 4-8 February 2018, it was found that Alabbassiya locality hosts 11,796 IDPS residing in 16 villages west of the locality. While 22,324 IDPs are in Rashad locality and in Talodi the total population of Talodi is 48,862 people, including IDPs. Kadogli locality also suffers the protracted influx of IPDs due to its location and relevant stability. These large numbers of IDPs adds more strain on the already limited services available to the host communities, namely food and health services. It is estimated that approximately (according to 2006 census) 1.32M people are living in West Kordofan. According to the South and West Kordofan States Inter-Agency AGDM Participatory Assessment, conducted by UNHCR and partners (November 2017) the community is hosting thousands of IDPs (150,474) and is expected to have the highest child mortality data; this is due to the continued conflicts, refugee influx and the frequent outbreaks of AWD. According to these assessments there’s a big gap in health facilities in terms of the number of running and functional HFs and in terms of the number of medical staff running them, which leads to the halting of the little services available. The health system is un-able to provide adequate support to all the population in the community, and although there are many organizations supporting health facilities, still the community suffers the irregularity supply of routine medicines and weakness in terms of referral system in addition to low EPI coverage. For RH more trained midwives are needed and clean delivery kits as well as provision of HIV/AIDS services, newborn health care should be established. There’s a shortage in labs for diagnosis, the spread of communicable diseases: malaria, RTIs and diarrhea was also reported. Nomadic communities exist in the state and routine immunization campaigns don’t always reach them, this necessitates mobile immunization campaigns. There’s a need for more hand washing facilities and incinerators for medical waste. As for nutrition, per these assessments, poor feeding practices were found to be prevalent in the assessed communities; RUSF supply isn’t stable as was reported by several centers, as well as the shortages of MUAC screening in IDPs areas and the low coverage of CMAM activities. IYCF activities are also not available, there’s no sufficient active community outreach for case finding and referral for treatment and no PHC services providing micro nutrients to pregnant women to avoid deficiency diseases such as anemia, these communities also suffer weakness in terms of referral system,furthermore most of nutrition staff are volunteers working without pay.

Activities

GAH has worked in the past years in South and West Kordofan states, with the support from MoH in technical issues as well as provision of medical cadres, WFP and UNICEF in West Kordofan, CONCERN and WFP in South Kordofan were they supported with funding and supplies. Health and nutrition are complementary sectors, in emergency to provide lifesaving activities conduct intensified immunization service and to routine service later, procure health commodities (ORS, essential medicines and mosquito nets) at Abuzabad and Lagawa at W.K and talodi and aldaling at S.K , also we need to expand more outpatient therapeutic centers along with the extensive screening of MUAC of active case finding and to support the referral system from Almariam to Almujlad.Public awareness should be made through local media of different kinds to eases the activity in the districts, Additional support except previous is urgently required to provide hygiene kits, family planning and procure therapeutic food for severely malnourished childrenThis is beside delivering the minimum basic package of health care services with well-trained health professionals and support the referral system,supplying of health facilities and CMAM sites with medical and non-medical supplies, equipment and essential medicine for communicable and non-communicable diseases to Talodi and Rashad. Support STIs and HIV/AIDS testing and treatment and psychosocial support. Activities will be held inside PHCs while maintaining dignity and safety of all beneficiaries. Rehabilitation and maintenance of health facilities with incinerators for medical waste at Almeriam.Conduct training sessions for all involved parties by establishing, training and supervising Nutrition Care Groups and mother group ,for (IYCF) promotion, Organizing new and refresher training for SMoH staffs on CMAM program, family planning and to give them incentives . Also to conduct standard nutrition and health need assessment at Abaseya and Kadugli
