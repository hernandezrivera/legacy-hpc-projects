Project Summary

The project aims to promote improved hygiene and sanitation through renovation and rehabilitation of WASH facilities and availability of clean water to the disaster affected communities especially in floods. Through awareness building around infection prevention and COVID-19 preventive measures, communities will be made aware of infection prevention practices and to prevent COVID-19 in the communities.
