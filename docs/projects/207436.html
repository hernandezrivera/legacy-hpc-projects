Project Summary

This project proposal aims to address the pressing issue of gender-based violence (GBV) within the returnees community in the South West region of Cameroon. It recognizes the vulnerability of returnees who often face significant challenges in terms of social integration, economic stability, and access to basic services, making them more susceptible to GBV. By implementing comprehensive interventions, this project seeks to raise awareness, provide support, and empower returnees to eradicate GBV within their communities.

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Activitiesa) Conduct a baseline survey to understand the prevalence and types of GBV within the returnees community.b) Establish and train a team of qualified social workers to provide psycho-social support to GBV survivors.c) Conduct community sensitization campaigns on the causes, consequences, and prevention of GBV.d) Organize workshops and training sessions to equip returnees with skills for advocating against GBV and reporting incidents.e) Establish safe spaces and support groups for GBV survivors to heal, share experiences, and access crucial services.f) Provide vocational training and livelihood support to enhance the economic independence of returnee women.g) Assist in the creation of community-led committees to address GBV, enhancing their overall response system.h) Collaborate with local and national NGOs, government bodies, and stakeholders to maximize project impact and sustainability.This project proposal seeks to address gender-based violence within the returnees community in the South West region of Cameroon. By raising awareness, providing support, and promoting economic empowerment, this project aims to create a safe and inclusive environment for returnees, reducing their vulnerability to GBV and fostering a society free from violence and discrimination.
