1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

NRC will provide life-saving shelter and NFI assistance to newly displaced IDPs. Leveraging the local populations’ strong community resilience and technical construction know-how, NRC will support households in relocation sites affected by protracted displacement through a contribution for construction of shelters and ‘build back better’ technical support. In the event returns intensify, the response may target returnee and remainee families through emergency rehabilitation of existing dwellings. Depending on restrictions and potential constraints on access to markets/financial providers, NRC will decide on a case-by-case basis which modality (cash-based vs in-kind) to opt for to maximize efficiency and minimize response time.Targeting strategy will adjust to the response phase in which it is intervening. In emergency settings, priority will be given to displaced populations with assessed shelter/nfi needs in hard-to-reach areas, while in early recovery settings, priority will be given to families experiencing protracted displacement. In both settings, NRC will also target HC supporting and sharing resources with IDPs. In return areas, NRC will not only target returnees, but also remainees, as their decision to stay is often associated with extreme vulnerability and lack of resources to flee. Estimated 55% female – 45% male (all ages).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The first key objective for the Shelter and NFI response will be to save and sustain lives through the provision of timely, targeted and appropriate shelter assistance and relief items. This will include the provision, distribution of core relief items and contribution for emergency shelter kits or materials complemented with technical trainings to displaced people in temporary and reallocation sites, mainly in Mueda and Nangade. It will also include the rehabilitation, repair or upgrade of existing shelters that are below minimum standards in potential return locations such as Palma, Mocimba da Praia and Quissanga.In-kind assistance will be prioritized in the forms of family NFI packages during emergency responses and also for long-term displaced populations in need of support. Seasonal items (such as warm clothing and thermal blankets in winter, summer clothing in summer), will also be distributed to sustain lives and reduce the impact of exposure to extreme conditions and will be one of the main focuses of the NFI response.A combination of in-kind and cash-based assistance will be promoted for people that have been assessed as socio-economically vulnerable through a close linkage with actors providing multi purpose cash to ensure that scarce resources are directed to those most in need.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

As part of a Do-No-Harm approach, NRC will work with both IDPs and HC not to exacerbate inter-communal tensions. The provision of shelters to IDPs will alleviate the burden on local communities and will contribute to regain access to communal infrastructures. On the other side the direct support in retrofitting and up-grading existing HC’ dwellings will support a sustainable integration process and improve poor living conditions of most vulnerable host families. The Building-back-safer approach will benefit IDP, as much as the HC. This could have a potential positive spill-over effect in the overall community, where improved living conditions in targeted HH could lead to increased acceptance of the continuously growing IDPs population.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Protection mainstreaming will ensure the inclusion of groups at risks in all different activities and will monitor and manage the potential abuse of participation and power through corruption, nepotism, peer pressure and the pursuit of self-interest.PDM is planned for direct input and feedback on assessing the impact of the activities. Complaint and feedback response mechanisms (CFRM) will be complementing the Linha Verde mechanism to provide a unified and centralised platform for accountability and transparency towards affected populations and local stakeholders.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In addition of priority and preference during any kind of distributions, People with Special Needs PSN’s committees will be included or ad-hoc focus groups will be consulted for the revision of shelters design, adapting sectorial shelter designs to their specific needs. This may include, among others, shelter access adaptation, privacy screens, kitchen units dimensioning, wheel-chair drainage friendly design, lightening, ramps, handrails, etc... to be determined based on PSN’s community consultation. To reach out to a representative sample of PSN unable to attend consultation sessions in the community, NRC team will take care to include households visits at their dwellings during assessment, planning, implementation, and M&E activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The intervention will strive to limit environmental impact through the selection of appropriate construction materials and local solutions, reducing at the maximum extent plastic in packaging and ensuring non-biodegradable waste will be safely disposed after distributions. NRC will work in consultation with host/IDPs communities to support relocation with consideration for existing community, future shelters/infrastructure, fire/flood mitigation as well as environmental impact, under the overarching NRC “building communities” approach. The environmental aspects of the intervention, looking at sourcing of natural material, as much as overstretched water resources and cooking fuel, have also a crosscutting protection relevance on potential conflicts that may arise in the community due to competition for natural resources. The response will therefore take into consideration the environmental aspect in close connection with the relevant protection considerations.
