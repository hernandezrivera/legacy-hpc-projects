Project Summary

Humanitarian actors are supporting the Government of Bangladesh’s efforts to ensure a timely and efficient response, unimpeded humanitarian access, a common analysis of needs, and a voice for Rohingya refugees, women, men, boys, and girls, and affected host communities. In 2022, UNHCR will continue to lead and coordinate actors engaged in the humanitarian response. This includes ongoing advocacy for the protection of refugees regardless of their legal status and location through active participation in and staffing support to the ISCG based in Cox’s Bazar. In addition, in line with the recommendations of the coordination review and consultation with the sectors and working groups, UNHCR’s engagement with the Government and humanitarian actors will further increase, in a way that fosters an integrated approach across all sectors, including protection, field, and technical and operational support. Management and sector linkages will be reinforced, ensuring a timely, coordinated, and adequate response to the humanitarian situation. UNHCR in collaboration with the relevant Agencies will streamline and improve the efficiency of the humanitarian response through effective coordination and leadership. In 2022, UNHCR will support the cost-sharing of technical support, staffing and administrative operational costs of the ISCG as well as the implementation of the joint MSNA (Multi-Sector Needs Assessment) for refugees with UNHCR partner(s). UNHCR will ensure the centrality of protection and protection and gender mainstreaming through inclusive coordination and leadership of the overall humanitarian response, with a key focus on PSEA. Through the coordination efforts, UNHCR will bolster inclusion through support to host communities, addressing the broader challenges that undermine protection space while promoting peaceful coexistence and social cohesion. UNHCR will empower Rohingya refugees by placing them at the centre of the response, building community capacities for leadership, self-management, and engagement with the Government and the humanitarian community.
UNHCR will continue to actively contribute to strengthen the coordination of the humanitarian response in Bangladesh, in order to build, systematise, and scale up solid mechanisms, processes, procedures and capacities to address the protection and basic human needs of refugees.
Support is currently provided by the ISCG Secretariat to the Strategic Executive Group, the District HoSO along with the Sector Coordinators to ensure inter-sector interaction with close synergies among all these diverse actors, and with the core objective of timely, needs-based, and evidence-driven humanitarian assistance, efficient use of resources and avoiding duplication.

Associated response plan

Rohingya Humanitarian Crisis Joint Response Plan 2022

Plan fields

1 - Identified Needs

Support SEG and HoSO efforts to take decisions on key policy, security and operational challenges, informed by protection considerations. Manage the response programme cycle: needs overviews and analysis, strategic planning and appeals (including contingencies), and response and needs monitoring. Monitor and analyse access constraints, and engage relevant stakeholders to address them. Manage data and information in support of humanitarian decision-making, advocacy and public information, resulting in regular information products. Work with Sectors and humanitarian partners to strengthen accountability and understanding of community capacities and preferences. Progress on a roadmap to localisation, including building the capacity of national partners to ensure sustainability of the response. Galvanize agency and Sector capacity in gender and protection to ensure mainstreaming across the response during all stages of the programme cycle. Support the development of a Centrality of Protection Action Plan in collaboration with the Protection Sector/actors and ensure it is regularly monitored. Ensure adequate cyclone preparedness and contingency planning with Sectors and humanitarian partners, including SIMEX prior to the cyclone seasons. Liaise with key stakeholders inside and outside Bangladesh, including humanitarian partners, donors, authorities and representatives of neighbouring countries, with a view to mobilize resources, promote humanitarian access, and advocate respect for international law. Reinforce and operationalise decentralised coordination mechanisms through the continued management of the humanitarian hub in Ukhiya, and support to Upazila level coordination.

2 - Activities or outputs

Conduct camp-level coordination meetings with the participation of government authorities, Sector focal points, and partners; Develop information products, including camp and union profiles and 4Ws shared with the Government; Conduct consultative forums bringing together the Government and the humanitarian community in Cox’s Bazar on operational priorities; Develop information, education and communication materials developed to raise awareness amongst refugee and host communities about protection from sexual exploitation and abuse; Facilitate donor missions together with partners in the Rohingya refugee response; Number of key operational policy issues discussed strategically through Sector/WG Coordinators, HOSOG and Dhaka-level forums; Develop reporting products, including JRP implementation updates, funding updates, and critical incidents reports on the Rohingya refugee response.
