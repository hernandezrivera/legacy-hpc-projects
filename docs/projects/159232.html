Project Summary

In the framework of this project proposal, Emmanuel aims at addressing immediate shelter, health care and food security needs of people with disabilities (PwD) living in isolated settlements of GCA (Krasnohorivka, Maryinka and Pivdenne) making sure that the distinct needs of women and men are met in order to ensure equal opportunities and access to assistance.
Shelter Cluster. Activity 1. Distribution of solid fuel to 200 vulnerable households of PwD in Krasnohorivka and Maryinka (3,4 tons of solid fuel briquettes per HH, according to the winterization recommendations of Shelter Cluster). Activity 2. Distribution and installation of solid fuel heaters in 70 vulnerable households of PwD in Krasnohorivka and Maryinka where the gas supply has been interrupted. Emmanuel will recruit local brigades of construction teams to make sure that the solid fuel heaters are properly installed and ready to operate. Response modality: in-kind provision of solid fuel and solid fuel heaters where Emmanuel will be able to use wholesale prices and make sure that the items are delivered to the household, unloaded and properly installed.
Health Cluster. Activity 1. Primary health care services will be provided to 200 PwD living in Maryinka and Krasnohorivka through three 2-day mobile clinics (servicing 30-35 patients per day). Emmanuel’s “Medical Mobile Clinic” will provide its resources (skilled medical staff, mobile equipment, express lab and supplies) to enhance the ability of the local health care providers to assist people with disabilities. The suggested packages of medical services to be provided by the mobile clinics to PwD are tailored to meet specific health care needs of men and women (they include but are not limited to consultations, instrumental and lab diagnostics (Ultra Sound scanning of abdominal organs, retroperitoneal space and thyroid , ECG, blood tests)). The local medical representatives will be included into the mobile medical teams and will be able to advise Emmanuel’s medical team on the health care services needed for each specific patient. Locations of medical outreaches will be identified by the local administrations taking into consideration the current security situation in the town (risk of shelling, heating in the premises, accessibility, etc). Bedridden patients with disabilities will be attended at home. Patients who are able to walk but have troubles coming to the mobile clinic location will be transported to the location. Activity 2. Distribution of 200 cash grants for healthcare expenses. The patients attending Emmanuel’s mobile clinics or other PwD in need of medication will be able to purchase prescribed medicines with the help of cash grants amounting to 1000 UAH (based on Emmanuel’s experience in providing health care services since 2003, average cost of basic medicines per one course of treatment varies between 800 and 1000 UAH). For the patients who cannot buy the medications by themselves due to disability or absence of pharmacy in the area, Emmanuel will purchase the needed medicines according to the prescription. Activity 3. Facilitation of lifesaving surgeries of 5 disabled patients in a critical condition. The patients in need of lifesaving surgeries will be identified in course of mobile clinics and referred to the relevant hospitals, with their transportation, cost of surgery, medical supplies and other related expenses fully covered. Emmanuel will provide full facilitation of the surgery in coordination with the doctors of its “Medical Mobile Clinic” and local health care providers. Response modality: mixed modality where the primary and lifesaving healthcare services will be provided in-kind and the medications are procured by the beneficiary through cash grants.
Food Security Cluster. Activity 1. Distribution of cash grants for food of 2475 UAH (based on recommendations by Food Security Cluster) to 65 PwD in Pivdenne. Response modality: assistance will be provided in form of cash transfers.

1 - Needs

Five years of the protracted conflict in eastern Ukraine continues to affect an estimated 5,2 millions of people of whom 3.5 million people need humanitarian assistance and protection. The most vulnerable groups in need of humanitarian assistance are the elderly, single-headed households with low income, people with disabilities, and children. Of the 3.5 million in need, 30 per cent are above the age of 60. These men and women face severe hardships when accessing essential services because they experience higher rates of disability and immobility and are often separated from their families. They are also more susceptible to abuse and neglect, have specific health and nutritional needs, and are highly prone to economic insecurity. The lives of millions of people living in Donetska and Luhanska oblasts have had to be re-organised, placing additional burdens on already over-stretched services, such as health, education, water, electricity and heating. Most vulnerable people are increasingly forced to buy food on credit, cut health expenditures. Loss of income and lack of livelihood opportunities are the second main concern among conflict-affected communities, which is closely correlated with increased alcohol and drug use, particularly among young people. This has contributed to the increase in domestic violence. One in five elderly people living along the ‘contact line’ reported experiencing at least one type of violence and abuse, 76 per cent being women (HelpAge. Humanitarian Needs of Older Women and Men in GCA). WINTERIZATION NEEDS. Harsh winters in Ukraine continue to pose a threat to life and well-being of people living along both sides of the “contact line”. Almost 30,000 vulnerable households in both GCA and NGCA are struggling to adequately prepare for the coming winter and were identified as a priority for receiving winterization assistance by Shelter/NFI Cluster. For the 2019-2020 winter period, in GCA there is a gap of 5,531 vulnerable households (or 13,385 people) in need of winterization assistance, according to Shelter Cluster. Many such households depend on coal, wood or other types of fuel for heating, which are often unavailable or costly, especially in isolated settlements. HEALTH CARE NEEDS. According to the data of Health cluster, 1.3 million people within 20-km of the 'contact line' in need of life-saving and essential health services. People with disabilities, families with young children and the elderly, are among the most vulnerable. With two-thirds of health facilities in areas closest to the ‘contact line’ damaged, 38 per cent of households report lacking access to health-care services. Four in 10 civilians in rural areas of GCA experience difficulties accessing critical health care (Humanitarian Trend Analysis in GCA 2018. REACH). The elderly suffer from chronic health conditions that require regular life-saving medicines. Irregular supply of medicines and equipment deprive those highly dependent upon the availability of life-saving care FOOD SECURITY NEEDS. Socio-economic conditions of the conflict-affected region continue to deteriorate, threatening livelihoods and wellbeing of the population. Accessing food is also a challenge due to active hostilities, especially for 16 per cent of households closest to the ‘contact line’ in GCA, who are at poor and borderline food consumption scores (Humanitarian Trend Analysis in GCA 2018. REACH). Based on this information, experience and available resources, Emmanuel plans to address the immediate shelter, health care and food security needs of most vulnerable PwD living in isolated settlements of GCA aiming at meeting the distinct needs of women and men in order to ensure equal opportunities and access to assistance.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Overall project objective: The project aims at addressing immediate shelter and health care needs of most vulnerable people with disabilities living in isolated settlements of GCA through distribution of solid fuel, installation of solid fuel heaters, providing primary health care services through mobile clinics, facilitation of life-saving surgeries, distribution of cash assistance for health and food, taking into consideration the distinct needs of women, and men in order to ensure equal opportunities and access to assistance. SHELTER CLUSTER: Cluster objective: Address essential shelter and NFI needs of the most vulnerable IDPs and conflict affected population through monetised/in-kind assistance and contingency SRP objective: CO3. Affected people have consistent access to critical civilian structures; Contribution to Cluster/Sector Objectives : 270 Shelter and NFI needs of most vulnerable conflict-affected population living in isolated settlements will be addressed through in-kind assistance in form of solid fuel (200) and installation of solid-fuel heaters (70); Activity 1: Distribution of solid fuel; Outcome 1: 200 Vulnerable households of PwD living in isolated settlements will receive solid fuel support which will help them survive the winter. Activity 2: Replacement of gas-heaters for solid-fuel heaters; Outcome 2: 70 vulnerable households of PwD living in isolated settlements will benefit replacement of gas-heaters for solid-fuel heaters. HEALTH CLUSTER. Cluster objective: Conflict-affected population access quality life-saving and essential health care services, including psychosocial and mental health support. SRP Objective: CO2. Affected people have improved access to quality and affordable services. Contribution to Cluster/Sector Objectives: 405 most vulnerable PwD living in isolated settlements in GCA will receive access to quality life-saving and essential health-care services. Activity 1: Provision of primary health care services through mobile clinics (consultations, instrumental diagnostics, lab diagnostics); Outcome 1: 200 PwD living in Maryinka and Krasnohorivka will benefit from primary health care services provided through mobile clinics Activity 2: Distribution of cash assistance for purchase of prescribed medicines; Outcome 2: 200 PwD living in Maryinka and Krasnohorivka will benefit from cash grants for purchase of prescribed medicines. Activity 3: Full facilitation of life-saving surgeries; Outcome 3: 5 PwD in a critical condition will have life-saving surgeries FOOD SECURITY CLUSTER: Cluster Objective: Most vulnerable conflict-affected groups have immediate access to food. SPR Objective: CO2. Affected people have improved access to quality and affordable services. Contribution to Cluster/Sector Objectives: 65 PwD living in isolated settlements in GCA will have immediate access to food through cash assistance. Activity 1: Provision of cash transfers for food; Outcome 1: 65 most vulnerable PwD living in Pivdenne will have immediate access to food through cash assistance.Each project activity is tailored to meet the gender-and-age specific needs of these elderly men and women with disabilities to ensure an integrated approach to the provision of assistance to the disabled. Namely, solid fuel will be delivered to people’s homes and unloaded in the storage areas; the solid fuel heaters will be installed into the heating systems and ready to operate. All health care activities will be tailored to the specific health needs of men and women of this age group, taking into consideration their disabilities. Emmanuel’s humanitarian relief teams include female personnel and all of the workers have underwent “Prevention of GBV” training.
