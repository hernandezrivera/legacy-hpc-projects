Project Summary

The project supports the most vulnerable populations in Idleb and Mahmbal subdistricts. It aims to enhance the resilience of affected people through integrated early recovery interventions. 2550 HHs (3875women, 3775 men, 3800 boys and 3820 girls) will benefit from the activities of this project. This aim will be achieved by supporting the value chain of wheat in Sahel Al rouge area via light rehabilitation of new 3 wells including connected irrigation channels in the area in addition to rehabilitation of the roads to cultivable lands and farms in addition to rehabilitating the roads around the bakeries. Capacity of farmers will be also enhanced by training on strategic thinking, business management and marketing, cash for work scheme may enhance a feeling of inequality and discrimination among different segments of the target communities. In order to avoid an escalation of any tensions, the project will clearly inform the selection criteria to the target communities, and let the communities understand that the vulnerable households are supported through the project, not only land owners. In order to enhance the social cohesion in the targeted areas, the project will ensure outreaching IDPs, including IDPs who are renting a land for and who are able to participate in cash for work or families what have persons with functional difficulties. Women headed households (whether IDP or from the host community) as priority in the selection criteria as they tend to face limited income generating opportunities, compared with their male peers. Community will be engaged throughout the project cycle and HIHFAD will build on the already existing beneficiary committee in Sahel Al Rouge and will make sure that it includes people from the host community and the IDPs in addition to women in order to reach all groups in the targeted area.
HIHFAD will coordinate with both Education Cluster,Field Education Directorate and Local authorities to select the list of schools which is already running and needs for rehabilitation and then HIHFAD will select 15 schools, where HIHFAD already did same activities in 2019, where HIHFAD rehabilitated 5 schools in Afrin Area in collaboration with Education cluster, Field Education Directorate and Local authorities, HIHFAD will use a field contractors and will enhance the cash for work for IDPs to complete the rehabilitation of the Schools.

4 - Needs

The ongoing crisis in Syria has severely affected the lives of people, leading to major displacement, loss of livelihood opportunities, made people (host community and displaced people) depend on negative coping mechanism. According to HNO 2019 more than 2.7 million are in need of humanitarian assistance. The impact of hostilities in the last five months is particularly severe on women and children who constitute 76% of the population in north west Syria, of which over 51% are children. REACH intercluster Needs Assessment in July 2019 reported that the high number of IDP arrivals to assessed communities in north-west Syria within recent months has posed several challenges to available services and livelihood opportunities within the region. Likely due to strains on stable livelihoods, certain protection issues have become more prevalent. Child labour was reported by KIs in 51% of assessed communities, through which many children are likely exposed to various forms of abuse and exploitation. Many people have no or insufficient incomes to cover their basic needs in Northwest Syria. Moreover, 10% suffer from the physical difficulty of accessing possible jobs due to factors such as damaged roads or a lack of transportation,(REACH Initative July 2019). HIHFAD also conducted a needs assessment in Al Rouge plain in November in order to identify the exact needs of the people and their difficulties. Interviews showed that farmers face various types of challenges such as the high harvesting and sowing costs in all assessed villages with 70% out of all interviewed farmers, followed by high fertilizer prices with 65% and high seeds prices with 65%. Concerning access to markets, 40 % of the farmers said that they have difficulties reaching the markets. The most important constraints of selling wheat are selling in low prices with 32% of the total percentages of problems, followed by the lack of markets with 29%.

5 - Implementation of activities

HIHFAD filed team will conduct a light rehabilitation of 22 KM of roads to cultivable lands and farms in addition to rehabilitating 3 KM of the roads around the bakeries using a casual labors from both IDPs and locals form the same community to provide a short term job opportunities.HIHFAD will conduct a package of training for farmers on strategic thinking, small business management and marketing, also HIHFAD will re-contact the local education authorities and Education Cluster to re-update the list of the schools in northern part of Aleppo which need for rehabilitation in addition to keep coordination with UN-Education Cluster, then HIHFAD field team will revisit the 15 schools to put the final study for the rehabilitation before tendering "also this activities will be conducted through local suppliers and will use cash for work for the IDPs and locals".

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

In order to support the vulnerable households, the project ensures 10% of the beneficiaries to be female-headed households. Field labour and frequent contacts with farmers and the suppliers may increase concerns over harassment and GBV.HIHFAD code of conduct underlines the importance of the humanitarian core standards as prerequisites for working with the organization, where humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence must always be applied while delivering any humanitarian assistance. HIHFAD provides all staff with an induction training on the protection principles so they become oriented enough to provide the services in a dignified and safe way for the beneficiaries. In addition, all of the services under proposed project will be provided on a free-of-charge basis for all those in need regardless of their individual gender groupingsParticularly vulnerable households such as women headed households, families with person with disabilities will be prioritized. The project will pay attentions during community mobilization, not to increase child labour and out of school children due to the project.HIHFAD field prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) assistant will ensure the participating beneficiaries are informed PSEA approaches as well as aware of anonymous reporting mechanism. When required, HIHFAD phycologists can provide counselling sessions remotely from Turkey.HIHFAD has a gender balanced MEAL team, enabling to both male and female beneficiaries. The MEAL team is well-trained on gender and protection sensitive interview and communication skills.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

General overview of Alrouge plain and Albab Area from the Security from the security perspective:In general, Alrouge plain is Stable from the security point comparing to the other parts of Idleb Governorate, as for Albab it's safe since 2018 ,these areas has not seen any major security incidents within the last years, since it is located far from the frontlines with the Syrian Regime.Regarding the personnel Risk Assessment for the field Team and the Management and the Resources for the Risk:1-IEDs and explosive Devices: this Risk is common all around Idleb and Aleppo Governorate, targeting vehicles and military personnel significantly.Mitigation Measures :-Avoid crowded places.-Avoid peak times for movements.-Avoid military points as possible.-Keep 200 meter as a safety distance from any military cars and suspected cars.-Using the main roads only and after check with HIHFAD security team.-Improved internal information management.-Maps of the safe roads (HIHFAD security Team provides them on Daily Bases).-SMP / SOP / CP in place and permanently updated.2-Problems with /among the Beneficiaries: the probability of this Risk is low , but it could happen at any time.Mitigation Measures :-HIHFAD had a good experience in this kind of projects ( HIHFAD had a previous one before in the same area).-HIHFAD explained the project very well to all beneficiaries in this project.-HIHFAD has well-trained Team on the ground.-people in the area are familiar with kind of projects.-Good relationship with the people on the ground.3-Robbery of the Staff : according to the security incidents data in the area , the probability of this Risky is very low.Mitigation Measures :-Activities may be split into several small ones.-Guards to be assigned.-Good communication means.-Avoid moving in the dark.-Thorough check of security situation before departure to the field.-Personal Security training for HIHFAD staff.
