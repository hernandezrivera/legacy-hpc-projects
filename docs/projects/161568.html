Project Summary

This program is designed to raise the level of menstrual education among vulnerable adolescence girls and young women who do not understand the changes that occur in their as they approach adulthood. this program will enable the beneficiaries understand the changes that occurs in their bodies and how to manage themselves and the best menstrual hygiene practices. the program is designed to reach Twenty-thousand (20,000) adolescence girls and young women within the ages of 12-35 years in different Local communities and at the end, they will be provided with menstrual hygiene kits.

1 - Needs

The lack of menstrual education among vulnerable adolescence girls and young girls and poor menstrual hygiene practices, has resulted to several reproductive health implication among the target group and as such has become a great concern. Lack of access to sanitary wares as cost implications on the social, psychological and physical health of vulnerable adolescence girls and young women in the Northeast.There are over 400,000 girls and women in need of sanitary pad. Lack of sanitary pad is a critical issue of sanitation, hygiene, mobility and dignity for all girls and young women in the northeast. Many girls miss and average of 4 days of school which is over a month in a year meaning some fall behind class or even drop out of school.Some would not be able to carry out their normal daily activities because of their menstrual periods.Some girls/ young women use leaves, cotton wool, clothes, Rags, chicken feathers among others during their menstrual periods.Menstruation is treated with silent and a taboo topic, where Men are not taught about the menstrual hygiene to be able to support their girl child and many young women and adolescent do not have access to relevant and important information about their bodies and this is an issue that has been identified by disadvantaged women and girls as a driver of gender inequality and non-empowerment in the Northeast and this is an urgent and huge need for this intervention.

2 - Activities

• Identification of vulnerable adolescents girls and young women to be supported• Identification of community volunteers as stakeholder and active hygiene promoter within the various communities.• Training beneficiaries about menstrual hygiene management• Promotion, sensitization and advocacy meeting with Stakeholders• Capacity building for community representatives and leaders.• Distributions of menstrual hygiene kits to the beneficiaries.
