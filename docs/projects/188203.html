Project Summary

Through this integrated intervention, Silk Road has a plan in the field of food production to finding solutions supporting the agricultural and livestock products where 151500 direct beneficiaries will be tragted through our FS interventions.
In terms of the livestock support projects, cow, and sheep breeders will be supported to create a dairy value chain that starts from the milk production till the stage of marketing for the final products in the local market. the cow and sheep breeders will be technically supported. Fodders will also be purchased to be distributed to the cow and sheep breeders. There will be another support for dairy product manufacturing, by providing small grants with vocational training to the women who produce yogurt and cheese. The last step of the dairy value chain support is providing plans and facilities to market the final product by establishing local selling points in the targeted communities. The products will be freely distributed through the selling points, to pave the way to move to the selling phase. In terms of agricultural support. We seek to support vegetables, lentil, barley, wheat, and olives farmers in Idleb, and Aleppo country sides with agricultural inputs.
These inputs are seeds, fertilizers, land ploughing, pesticides, and yield mechanical harvesting. We have a plan to activate the wheat and olive value chains. As the wheat value chain starts by purchasing the wheats from the farmers directly to benefit from the added value. Technical support will be provided also through the farmer's school establishment, besides that field visits will be conducted by our technical agricultural expert team to follow up the case of the crop with the farmers step by step. Agricultural brochures also will be distributed to the farmers to raise awareness and guide them to the correct scientific methods which must be applied. Projects will support livelihoods in the targeted areas, by providing cash for work activities.
Furthermore, the IDPs in the targeted locations will receive a ration of the final production
by taking a part of the total production from the targeted farmers according to agreement signed with them, those amounts of agricultural products will be distributed for the most affected people in the communities and camps as well.

4 - Needs

As came in HNO 2022 report, 14.6 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68.6 per cent of the total population. Based on a countrywide assessment of 36,484 households and technical reviews and consultations, at least 14.6 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. The agriculture sector, which was once a cornerstone of the Syrian economy, has also been severely impacted. Silk Road had conducted Needs Assessment in 33 communities many people are facing shortages in food items in the assessed locations due to lack of money to buy food and the high price of food when available. The available food in these locations come either from markets or lending food to IDPs from markets or host communities. In case of food access difficulties, IDPs can cope this challenge by reducing the numbers of meals eaten in a day, borrowing food or limiting the portion size at meal time. The main sources of income for families are Agriculture and livestock breeding, and for the displaced families is daily working and Aid. Where people declared that agriculture consider main source of income in this area. Regarding theAgriculture situation the most important crops grown in the covered communities are 44% wheat, 15% potato, 10% barley, 8% winter vegetables, 6% summer vegetables, 5% bean, 4% beas, and 3% chickpeas and lentils.

5 - Implementation of activities

Regarding livestock project - Purchase and distribute the fodder to the cow and sheep breeders, - Purchase and distribute the group of vitamins, medicine required for the cattle health in parallel with the technical support - Providing small cash grants for the women and establish community association for producing the dairy products. - Support marketing the dairy production produced by the targeted women by establishing celling points and creating a special brand for the dairy products. Starting by distributing those products freely to the most vulnerable people in the first stage to secure smooth transferring for the selling stage later. - Building the capacity of the local council in the targeted community: this will be achieved by technical training courses in the targeted location. As the agricultural projects will be implemented as the following. - Purchase and distribute of seeds for the farmers high quality Hybrid seeds will be distributed to farmers by in-kind distribution modality for seeds. - Distribution the pesticides (insecticides- fungicides) and agricultural fertilizers (Organic fertilizers-urea and compound fertilizers - amino acids). - Farmers will be supported also by lands ploughing for two times. - Farmers will be supported with yields harvesting services by hiring daily workers. Specific vulnerability criteria will be applied to choose workers. contract will be signed with the farmers to deliver a portion of the crops they produce to the IDPs Benifit.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

We assume that the security situation is stable in the targeted areas but the measures will be taken in case of risks as the following: 1- Prevalence of infection with COVID 19 virus in the targeted areas: 1-1 Provide the field teams with the safety equipment (Masks, gloves, personal hand sterilizers) 1-2 Door to door distribution of the agricultural inputs. 1- 3 in case of activities needs people gatherings like field days we will following measures: - Emphasis will be placed on maintaining social distance with a constant awareness of it. - Providing personal safety equipment such as masks and rubber gloves. 2: Corruption in procurement by employees, suppliers or contractors or change in required specifications.Mitigation Measures: 2-1 Application of procurement policies and procedures 2-2 Continuous follow-up of procurement 2-3 Check before receiving 2-4 Establishment of a Procurement Committee 2-5 Using Goods/services received notes 3- Increased armed conflict due to increased tensions between different groups. This could delay, pause, or suspend programming. Mitigation Measures: If this reaches Silk Road’s project towns, they will pause activities and wait until the situation on the ground becomes safer. 4: Target areas are inaccessible or insecure because of the advance of armed groups and/or aerial shelling. Mitigation Measures: 4-1 Ensure there is a process established that allows consulting the communities on how to proceed with the proposed response.
