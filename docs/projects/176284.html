Project Summary

Provision of Life-saving emergency food parcels to IDPS in Nicuapa and Mapupulo Resettlement sites, Montepuez district. The project will distribute food parcels to 1,500 IDPS comprising of Cereals, pulses and vegetable oil to each targeted household and CSB to families with children under 5 years of age

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Currently there are over 36,000 IDPs living in MontePuez District. Approximately 12,700 are being moved to Resettlement sites. IDPs arriving in paquite in Pemba district are heading towards resettlement site in Montepuez and Metuge, therefore the number of IDPs in montepuez district is expected to rise. The project aims to provide emergency food parcels to IDPs in the resettlement sites as well as safeguard children under 5 years from deterioration in their nutrition status by providing CSB+

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Provide emergency Food parcels to IDPs in Nicuapa and Mapupulo sede settlement sites. The Food parcels composition will be determined after conducting assessment of the affected population and local market for food availability enough to provide consistent and easy supply. The food basket will meet the Kcal requirements of a household of approximately 6 people and will consist of Cereals, Pulses and Vegetable oil. The project will identify food stores/Super markets or milling companies with the capacity to supply the food parcels to the IDPs in resettlement sites for a period adequate enough for the government to allocate cultivation land and start of cultivation. Currently Government has not approved the express use of Cash transfer in Cabo Delgado, until mechanisms are in place to ensure cash transferred does not get to the wrong hands.2. Provide protective CSB plus rations to children under 5 to safeguard deterioration of their of their nutritional intake. The CSB Plus will be sourced from JAM factory in Sofala currently used for school feeding in Sofala and Inhambane provinces. Each Child under 5 will get 100 gms of CSB per day

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection activities will be integrated into all the proposed intervention areas. The project will identify barriers in accessing assistance and services for vulnerable groups and take practical actions to maximize access for beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Community beneficiary feedback mechanisms to gauge satisfaction anddissatisfaction levels on project delivery will be undertaken periodically and will also be used to make minor adjustments where necessary. Feedback and complaints: A suggestion box, face to face and help desk will be put in accessible places at every distribution point. The MEAL team aims to review every complaint as well as collect feedback from beneficiaries in order to measure their satisfaction, collect suggestions, complaints, recommendations etc. Most of the direct complaints will be solved in the moment and others will be discussed at field officer level with PM/key staff and feedback will be provided in the subsequent distributions or through community leaders.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The response strategy will ensure that persons with disabilities and the elderly are involved in decision making by encouraging their participation in assessments. The project will prioritise people with disability.
