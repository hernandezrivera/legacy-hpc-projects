Project Summary

The proposed project builds upon NEF’s continuous presence in South Kordofan State(SKS) since 2011, including ongoing USAID/OFDA-funded humanitarian support for IDPs and vulnerable populations in Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), its work throughout Sudan since 1981, and its century of global experience.The Near East Foundation (NEF) proposes Humanitarian Support for IDPs and Vulnerable Populations in Water, Sanitation and Hygiene in South Kordofan State. This project aims to provide life-saving activities to improve health and well-being among conflict-affected people (IDPs and vulnerable host populations) by improving their access to potable water and adequate sanitation and hygiene. The project will assist people in 4 localities of SKS: El-Abassiya, Gadeer, Rashad, and Al-Liri.
However, for central Darfur. the proposed project will focus on communities with pressing humanitarian needs. It (a) capitalizes on NEF’s continuous presence, experience, and networks, (b) maximizes available local capacities (e.g., local community-based organizations and WSCs) and community assets (e.g., underground water sources, local construction materials), (c) builds local resources and capacities to ensure improved WASH conditions and minimizes dependence on external assistance during emergencies and impeded access to the region, and (d) mobilizes available resources (e.g., land, community contributions, local water supply and construction materials, local trainers and hygiene promoters) to immediately respond to help those affected by the conflict – giving priority to time-sensitive activities such as hardware-based interventions (e.g., latrines and provision of safe water) and integrating software-based interventions (e.g., behavior change promotion and community resource management) – to improve WASH access while improving transition strategies and sustainability of project results
The overall environmental health, hygiene and sanitation situation is particularly poor in target communities. Average water consumption level is 4.53 l/p/d – far lower that the Sphere and WASH Sector-recommended 7.5-15 l/p/d; 63% of the population has no access to latrines and hand-washing facilities. More than 75% of displaced populations are women, children, persons with disability, or elderly. Communities experience increased incidence of waterborne and water-related diseases (schistosomiasis, malaria, diarrhea), widespread open defecation, and decreased health and increased environmental contamination associated with poor sanitation and hygiene. Without this intervention, more than 109480 conflict-affected persons (indirectly 258062 persons) will have no access to clean sources of water, adequate sanitation, or awareness of proper hygienic practices. Their risk of morbidity and mortality will rise, waterborne and water-related diseases will become more frequent, and environmental contamination will increase. the project aiming at that, conflict-affected people (IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host populations) in 7 localities (North Jabel Marra, West Jabel Marra, Central Jabel Marra, Wadi Salih, Mukjar, Azoom and Um Dukhun Localities ) will reduce their risk of morbidity and mortality through improved access to clean water, adequate sanitation, and good hygiene. Total number of beneficiaries expected to be targeted is 109,480 directly [258,062 indirectly].

Needs

The proposed project builds upon NEF’s continuous presence in South Kordofan State (SKS) since 2011, including ongoing USAID/OFDA-funded humanitarian support for IDPs and vulnerable populations in Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), its work throughout Sudan since 1981, and its century of global experience. The Near East Foundation (NEF) proposes Humanitarian Support for IDPs and Vulnerable Populations in Water, Sanitation and Hygiene in South Kordofan State. The project aims to provide life-saving activities to improve health and well-being among conflict-affected people (IDPs and vulnerable host populations) by improving their access to potable water and adequate sanitation and hygiene. Since 2011, SKS has been plagued by violence stemming from clashes between the Government of Sudan (GOS) and SPLM-N. Efforts at a peace agreement in November 2015 failed, leading to renewed violence and new displacements, with many displaced from SPLM-N-controlled areas to GOS-controlled areas. For the case of central Darfur, the influx of returnees at Um Dukhun, Mukjar , Garsilla, Azum, North and West Jebal Marra has put pressure on existing WASH services. The overall food security, environmental health, hygiene and sanitation situation in target areas is very poor. Host communities and returnees have identified food security, water and sanitation-related issues as a priority, and there is a lack of basic sanitation and water supply hardware facilities.

Activities

This project is still in the planning phase, though NEF will be focusing on rehabilitating the broken Hand pumps as priority and upgrading to scale up the water consumption per person per day, creation of water care takers will be the must, however, in relation to hygiene promotion, NEF will do the same, provision of PHAST training and CLTS based on the needs and context, NEF has quite good experience on both methods of training, based on the experience gained from the OFDA funded projects in the areas (Central Darfur and South Kordofan). In regards to sanitation, NEF will help the affected communities to dig their own latrines and encourage them to use traditional means to cover their pits, moreover, CLTS can be the led methodology in this regards. For the emergency setting, NEF will ensure PHAS methods is followed and that concrete or plastic slabs to be distributed to the more vulnerable households. Clean up campaigns will be organized to elevate the hygiene situation and to reduce the open defecation. The proposed activities : (1) to increase access to drinking water through upgrading of water points (2) form /activate and train local water and sanitation committees to raise awareness about personal and domestic hygiene, the importance of using latrines, community and household hygiene, and overall community environmental health; (3) provide local committees with support to expand services and implement priority sanitation interventions as identified by them per the context in each village and (4) promote the participation of affected population in rehabilitating and maintaining pit latrines. Digging tools and materials to support these goals will be provided.
