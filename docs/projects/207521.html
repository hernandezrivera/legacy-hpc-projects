Project Summary

The North West and Southwest region of Cameroon has suffered from a serious crisis for the past six years leaving more than 2 million people in need of humanitarian assistance. The deteriorating security situation, killing of civilians, abductions and burning down of homes and property has resulted in displacements of over 225,516 people forced to flee their homes with an amount of 10,000 most vulnerable displaced to bushes and villages. The NW region has been greatly affected with over 226,708 displaced people (MSNA August 2021)
The shelter and NFI general outlook for SW and NW regions is worrying, with a high percentage of the displaced population self-settled in the bush in rural areas and suffering from strong shelter needs.
In May 2018, the humanitarian community launched an Emergency Response Plan. Shelter and NFI was identified as the key sectorial priority after food, and follow by health, WASH, education, and protection. Local partners have flagged repeatedly that shelter needs are critical and needs to be prioritized.
This project intends to provide shelter kits, non food items to 1500 IDPS households in Donga Mantung Divisions and Menchum living in bushes and farm huts and returnees and rental assistance to 250 vulnerable IDP students and hoseholds in Mezam whose homes were burnt
The main objective of this project is to improve the shelter condition of 1750IDPs households living in bush/forest settlements, returnees whose houses have been destroyed in the North West Region and vulnerable IDPs, through the provision of complete emergency shelter kits and NFI in Donga Mantung ( Misaje, Ako,Nwa and Nkambe sub division) and Menchum Division (Menchum valley, Wum, Fungom) and rental assistance to vulnerable students and households in Mezam.
And this project has as specific objectives to
- To improve shelter and living condition of extremely vulnerable households living in Bush/forest, and hard to reach areas, through the provision of emergency shelter and Non food items and rental assistance. This will be done incorporation with GBV by ensuring that men (40) and women (60) in every stage of implementation, people living with disabilities, pregnant and lactating women are given priority.
- Provision of emergency shelter including accountability to the affected population by having complaint and feedback mechanisms in the communities to 1500IDP household in Menchum and Donga Matung within a period of nine months
To achieve the above objectives, the following interventions and target have been put in place
- Training of all recruited focal persons on methodology of verification and confirmation
- Training and equipping of focal persons in all map out areas for targeting, distribution and the construction of 1500 emergency shelter/NFI.
- Distribution of shelter kits of tarpaulin, nails, wood, mattresses, blankets, households utensils like pots, spoons, stainless plates, mats, buckets, cups rechargeable torch, ropes and face mask to 1500 IDP households.
The shelter project is targeting vulnerable IDPs and returnees whose houses have been burnt specifically in hard to reach areas in bush/forest settlements in Menchum and Donga Matung Division in the North West who have been extremely affected by the socio political crisis.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The tension in Cameroon's NWSW regions has caused significant damage. Many people have lost their homes to fires, and those who are displaced lack the means to survive. They receive minimal support from friends and well-wishers. The victims, often large families, live in terrible conditions and lack basic household items to improve their living conditions. The continuous displacement due to frequent gun battles between factions is a major concern.Providing shelter kits to the victims is crucial and rental assistance to vulnerable internally displaced persons and households in Mezam. These kits would be emergency packages that can be easily moved to safer locations, including the bushes if necessary. Shelter is a fundamental human need, and the lack of proper housing requires attention. The victims are already traumatized, and their negative feelings of revenge worsen the situation. Assisting these individuals by guaranteeing their security and providing shelter not only meets their basic needs but also helps rebuild their self-esteem. It also creates room for positive attitudes towards the perpetrators.We aim to improve the shelter and living conditions of vulnerable households in remote and hard-to-reach areas, particularly those living in the bush or forest. Our project will focus on providing emergency shelter essential non-food items and rental assistance to vulnerable IDP students and households in Mezam. We will work in collaboration with GBV (Gender-Based Violence) initiatives to prioritize the needs of men, women, people with disabilities, pregnant women, and lactating women. By doing so, we hope to bring about positive transformation and change in their lives.Our project involves several key activities to provide assistance to the affected population in Donga Mantung, Menchum and Mezam: Recruitment of project staff (office and field level) including focal points, project managers, shelter supervisors, and M&E officers.Conducting a needs assessment and verification to determine the size of the beneficiary population.Establishing distribution sites and related processes.Forming committees with adequate representation of women 60 women to 40 men.Deploying crowd controllers at distribution sites to ensure order and safety.Ensuring equitable distribution of relief commodities using appropriate systems.Promoting inclusion, safety, and accountability throughout the distribution process.Monitoring the distribution process on-site.Reporting on the quality, quantity, and impact of commodity received.Providing emergency shelter and being accountable to the affected population through complaint and feedback mechanisms within a nine-month period.Identifying and creating an initial beneficiary list with a focus on vulnerable individuals and their specific needs.Informing local authorities and community leaders about the distribution schedule.Securing a storage area agreed upon with community leaders before delivering items.Informing beneficiaries about the distribution schedule.Training feedback committees to receive and register complaints.Transporting stocks to the field for distribution.Setting up distribution points with posters, sensitization on waste management, and peace building.Coordinating the distribution process and providing additional capacity if required.Providing guidance on technical issues, such as protection referrals, when necessary.Provision of rental assistance to vulnerable IDP students and households in Mezam.Writing distribution reports.Overall, our project aims to provide 1500 emergency shelter kits and non-food items to assist the affected population in the targeted regions and provision of rental assistance to 500 vulnerable IDP students and households in Mezam.
