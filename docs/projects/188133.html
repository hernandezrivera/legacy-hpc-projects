Project Summary

The project aims to rehabilitate the physical access to ensure that IDPs and host communities in Northwest Syria continue to receive an uninterrupted supply of humanitarian assistance transported through Turkey cross-border (Bab Al-Hawa) near Sarmada to the IDPs camps and other cities. This project proposes a multisectoral strategy that aims to address multiple Early Recovery sector objectives in (Azzaz, and Afrin) sub-district in Aleppo governorate, which is to provide critically needed rehabilitation - upgrades- to the main access road, and to support both economic growth and IDPs resilience, where the road is considered the lifeline for IDPs in camps and the neighbor areas.
The project will promote the following:
i. Improve the technical situation for 19,7 KM of the road network and reduce the damage in winter by upgrading/rehabilitation of the main roads in need of rehabilitation (asphalt/graveling), excavating the open channels (rain drainage systems), and filling the pits. The link below shows the road:
https://goo.gl/maps/sNCVKNSdxAXhYLi7A
ii. Expansion/cleaning of sanitation networks, the drainage system in need of expansion, and installation of sewer pipes.
iii. Increase the resilience of IDPs by generating new employment for 60 skilled and unskilled IDPs through the rehabilitation and maintenance of road infrastructure, the CFW activity design will take into consideration the “Cash for Work Minimum Recommended Actions Guidance for NW Syria”, this intervention will be coordinated with all humanitarian partners working in the area to avoid any service overlap, the local community will be engaged in the selection criteria, wages, work scope and working hours of all cash for work labors.
iv. Establishing 2 rescue teams and providing them with training and equipment in order to assist in responding to the flood’s risks.
v. The project was designed to restore sections of the road network which is in poor condition, and at the same time to strengthen the planning, implementation, and management capacity of the local authorities, moreover, to develop further the emerging local road rehabilitation and maintenance industry, using mainly labor-based methods and generating job opportunities to assist with poverty alleviation.
In the CFW activity, social cohesion will be fostered by engaging all community representatives in the selection criteria, the scope of work related to cash for work activities to be implemented in the targeted area, many community engagement meetings will be conducted by IMAAR in the design, implementation phases of the project to ensure community engagement fully in this intervention.

5 - Implementation of activities

Implementation will include the following:Community engagement: IMAAR will conduct discussion sessions with the main stakeholders/authorities and community representatives to discuss the project scope, work plan, and handover process and collect detailed information about specific locations of the targeted roads. Design of roads rehabilitation/maintenance plan: The present activity will be guided by the good practice guide for road rehabilitation shared by ERL cluster in November 2021 and the Basic Guiding Requirements guidance to Reduce Flood Incidents in IDP Sites and Communities ‎in NWS shared by the ERL and other clusters in April 2021 and consists of designing a detailed, technical rehabilitation plan, developing ‎the detailed bill of quantities, technical specifications, detailed engineering drawings and layouts, and scope of works through conducting technical assessment ‎and topographic survey of the targeted roads via specialized engineering and design services team.Procurement process: IMAAR will identify experienced companies/contractors able to deliver effective and efficient outputs - Formalize the contracts with all required annexes through framework agreements - Implementation of road rehabilitation - Supervision and monitoring of roads rehabilitation. Handover of the rehabilitated roads to the local councils/authorities - Provision of road maintenance services by the contractor as per defects-liability clause of the contract - Forming maintenance committees.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:- Security situation allows access to the project location- Project scope and design are accepted by the community and key stakeholders, including landowners-Needed equipment, goods, supplies, and contracting services are available in the local market.Risks: - Roads are not rehabilitated to specific specifications. We will overcome this risk by performing daily monitoring with a mobile-based remote monitoring system that updates Imaar teams on a real-time basis, thereby allowing us to make quick and informed decisions. - Housing land property (HLP) issues between landowners and camp population, we will prepare HLP including checklists with taking into consideration the Guidance of Due Diligence of Shelter; in addition to, the recommendation of HLP TWG and protection guidance. We will take into consideration the contractual status between stakeholders and beneficiaries, and the legal procedures after the end of the period project - Design changes are required due to acceptance issues with the local community landowners - The border closure has a negative impact on the availability of needed equipment, goods and supplies in the local market - Local authorities, community, landowners and other key stakeholders do not agree with public ownership plan-Risk: HLP risks, interference by local actors, engagement with contractors. Probability: M/Impact: H- MS: Coordination with local community representatives and LA will be done during thedesign and implementation stages.
