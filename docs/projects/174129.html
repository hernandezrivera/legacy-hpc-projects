Project Summary

IDEA is registered as Not for Profit Organization having valid MoU with Economic Affair Division Government of Pakistan till December 31, 2021 Beside this IDEA also having the Allow to work status from the Ministry of SAFRON and Tax exemption certificate from Federal Board of Revenue Government of Pakistan.
Currently IDEA is operational in 12 Districts (Peshawar, Swat, Nowshera, Mansehra, Torghar, Charssadda, Mardan, Kohat, Hangu, Lakki Marwat, Bannu and DI Khan) in WASH, Education, Health, Livelihood, COVID-19 emergency response and DRR in partnership with WHH, START Network, British Council, CONCERN-Rapid Fund, PPAF, UNHCR, UNESCO and Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa for the covid emergency response.
The project is designed in line with the WHO technical guidelines and Government National Action Plan in order to complement the efforts of relevant government authorities and other stakeholders to mitigate the widespread transmission of COVID-19. The proposed project is submitted in the frame of the refugee support of the Humanitarian Response Plan. It will participate to the third strategic objectives by responding to Healthcare facilities attending to COVID-19 cases with missing/ less required WASH facilities, It will implement a capacity building program for front line workers and installation of public hand washing stations. It will provide carpet coverage for the Afghan refugee communities using interpersonal communication, social and mass media, public service messages and billboards. The main focus will on the curtailing the spread and strengthening institution through WASH with supporting stakeholders efforts toward breaking chain of COVID-19. IDEA will support the refugees village administration and health and educational institutions through short term, effective and quick solution by providing ready to use 50 Hand Washing Stations with soap in each camp, covering schools and health facilities, mosques and community centers. A total of 300 hand washing points in 06 RVs will be installed with running water installation of 100+gl water tanks having base of steel for each tank with 2 taps point and soap holder. IDEA will Repair/Rehabilitate/ install 5 water supply systems including hand pumps and pressure pumps in each Camp, a total of 30 water supply systems will be rehabilitated in health and education facilities. IDEA will Repair/Rehabilitate/ install 6 water supply systems including hand pumps, pressure pumps and dug wells in each camp, a total of 30 water supply systems. IDEA plans to repair/ rehabilitate damaged latrines at 06 health facilities (total latrines 36) and 06 formal schools (total latrines 36) at each camp.
IDEA designed the response strategy to target Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE), Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) and secondary impact of the COVID-19 response specifically Health, WASH, Livelihood and Education. IDEA will utilize the local influential like Shura Members, elders, and other key stakeholders in undertaking risk communication and community engagement promotion activities. As a component of RCCE, awareness raising messages will be disseminated through means available in the lockdown scenario e.g. radio messages, load speakers in the mosque and other modes of communication. Information, Education, Communication (IEC) materials: IDEA will print and fix 400 banners IEC materials with the relevant messages. IDEA will procure 20,000 mask and 20,000 pairs of disposable gloves and would be handed over to health and education facilities in the presence of RV village administrator for ensuring accountability. IDEA will install 20 waste bins for the waste management in five targeted RVs.
IDEA will provide water, hygiene and sanitation assistance in the RV Lalma, Shana Wuna in Nowshera while in District Peshawar will target Naguman , Khazana and Khurasan.
