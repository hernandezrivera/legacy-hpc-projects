4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021, 58 % of hospitals and 53 % of Primary Healthcare Centers (PHC) fully functional, the hospital of Shamarin is the only facility in north rural Aleppo and Afrin with capacity to provide these specialized services (major orthopedics surgery and cardiac care unit). Immunization (Routine & Campaigns) is one of the building blocks of primary health care the project aims to support the EPI (Expanded Programme Immunization) program through extending supporting 4 cold rooms and transportation of the vaccines to all 93 PHCs which providing EPI services. To have a sustainable mechanism to store and distribute effective vaccines and to ensure high immunization coverage in NWS. The Dialysis centers in Syria have been struggling since the beginning of the conflict to keep delivering of services to most vulnerable categories “Patients with End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD)”. In north-west Syria, there are 17 hemodialysis centers providing hemodialysis services and the number of patients is 702 with the total number of dialysis sessions conducted is 6058 per month, this project is designed to cover the gap in the hemodialysis services at Jarabulus district. According to the disability report of HNAP 2021, in northwest Syria 20% of people live mobility disabilities, the Prosthetics center in Azaz will provide equitable qualified service to most vulnerable group with mobility disability in the area.according to Epi reports shared by health cluster the NWS COVID-19 update as of Epi Week 16-2021 the total confirmed cases were 21,630, 11,679 are in Idleb, and 9,951 are in Aleppo, and the total confirmed death cases related to COVID-19 in NWS is 641, CCTC Zayadiyeh will contribute to COVID-19 response in Azaz area

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although the security situation across Syria is generally unsafe, this project does not expose beneficiaries or staff to additional physical hazards, violence, rights abuses and or instability. • This project provides all target beneficiaries, equal access to project integrated activities with no discrimination.• Persons with disabilities may face particular barriers to accessing health services due to physical access obstacles or inaccessible information PAC will be sure that all Persons with disabilities have access to its health facilities through mainstreaming of disability access in the health facilities.• Health care services provided by PAC respect principles of dignity, confidentiality, with the free and informed consent of patients.• This project is designed to have monitoring teams in place to assess whether service delivery locations are adequate and are being used in a dignified way.• A confidential complaints system and protection reporting mechanism will be designed and all beneficiaries will be informed about this mechanism and how to refer or report incidents or violations. • Measures are in place to ensure that beneficiaries understand that it is their right to receive free equitable and safe assistance including people with specific needs or vulnerabilities. • PAC has different channels for information dissemination including, but not limited to, WhatsApp groups, IEC materials, fixed posters and banners • All project staff trained on GBV prevention and response in emergencies and PSEA, in order to ensure full adherence with the Do No Harm principle with strict dealing with (SEA) and GBV issues. • All staff and vendors will sign a code of conduct for sexual exploitation and abuse.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Delays in fund dispersal: Likelihood: Low, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: PAC may use a portion of its private funds in order to avoid distribution or implementation delays.2- Closure of Bab Alhawa and Bab AL Salama Cross-Border gates between Tukey and Syria: Likelihood: Low, Impact: High Mitigation: Contingency stock will be available in Syria to avoid any impact from border closure 3- Spread of COVID-19 in NW Syria Likelihood: High, Impact: highMitigating measures: PAC is applying recommended IPC measures at the health facility, Screen and isolate all beneficiaries with suspected COVID-19 at the entrance of the facility, social distance, patients with chronic diseases to have their medications for two months in advance in order to minimize visits to the health facility. 4- Macro-level conflict-related threats including aerial bombardment, advancement of hostile actors, shifting alliances, border closure, and Chemical attack. Likelihood: High, Impact: HighMitigation: Following PAC safety and security guidelines and established safety protocols. In the event of a major macro level conflict related threat, PAC will take appropriate action commensurate with the scale of the threat. This may include: Suspending operations temporarily - shifting resources and operations to more secure locations5- Fraud, aid diversion and mishandling of supplies, Likelihood: Low Impact: HighMitigating Measure: PAC maintains a ZERO TOLERANCE policy towards Fraud, aid diversion and a strong beneficiaries complaint mechanism in place (Hotline, complain boxes, staff members, field office, etc.). Disciplinary action up to and including immediate dismissal of personnel found to have engaged in fraud.
