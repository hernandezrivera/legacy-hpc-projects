Project Summary

According to FSNAU, the humanitarian situation in Somalia is still critical as per September 2019. Nearly up to 4.8 million are facing food insecurity while closely 2.1 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps, high acute malnutrition, or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse outcomes through December 2019. Moreover, current estimates indicate that 1 in 10 children under the age of five are acutely malnourished with the median National GAM of 13.8%, translating to 1.08 million children; of which 178,000 are affected by life threatening severely malnutrition while 830,000 are moderately malnourished. From FSNAU technical release, Pockets of GAM above the emergency threshold of 15% were reported in 10 out of the 33 areas surveyed in the 2019 assessment; with the majority (51.7%) of malnourished children cases concentrated in the six regions of Banadir (14.8%) Shabelle (11%), Bay (8.8 %), Hiraan (6.1%), Woqooyi Galbeed (5.6 %) and Gedo (5.4 %). These regions also realized below average rainfall and the expected food harvest is 68% lower than the long-term average since 1995.
Hudur and Bakool region, the GAM rate is 15.1% and a SAM rate is 3.8%, with the rural and urban people are approaching serious and critical GAM levels. In September FEWS NET technical release, it is indicated that 1,000 IDPs and 25,000 people from rural areas are in Emergency (IPC4) while 31,000 people are in Crisis (IPC3) in Food Insecurity.
Afgoye and Wanlwayn areas in Lower Shabelle, the GAM rate is 15.8% and SAM rate is 17.1%. While 36,000 people are in an Emergency level (IPC4) and 108,000 people are at a Crisis stage (IPC3). Failure of crop production and poor harvest with a protracted climate shocks in Lower shabelle resulted food insecurity and malnutrition.
The efforts will be focused on maximizing benefits and avoiding harms for the most deprived and vulnerable groups including children and women within the targeted regions. The project will be aimed focus on the ''Do No Harm'' principle to protect the beneficiaries from exploitation at any given level. The project will implement activities aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of all individuals in accordance with international law. There will be continuous sensitization both internally and externally on child protection to ensure that the rights of the children within the target communities are not violated at any given point. The proposed action will be implemented to promote meaningful access safety and dignity of the target populations to the project services.
People with disabilities often face exclusion, increased vulnerability and barriers in accessing their fundamental human rights. The project will ensure inclusion of people with disabilities, preventing stereo typing, discrimination and enhancing their access to the project intervention irrespective of the disabilities. HIJRA will make deliberate effort to ensure the project sites are closer to the community to enhance access to the services by the disabled and vulnerable population thus reducing walking distances and exposure to harm.
HIJRA Will scale basic nutrition services in priority to IDPs and their host communities and other socially marginalized groups in Hudur, Tayeeglow, Afgooye, Marka and wanlaweyn.Community (men and women and youth) will be involved and will be fully participate in all project cycle and decision making processes. HIJRA is planning to reach and treat acute malnutrition, a total 39131 children 6months to 59 months boys and girls, 12643 pregnant and lactating women as well as promoting IYCF practices to care givers through static OTP and mobile OTP in target areas.

Project Activities

Activity 1: Community screening for malnutrition and referral for ChildrenThe proposed intervention will be to undertake community screening for malnutrition and strengthen referral systems for 23631 children (113471boys and 12294 girls) of 6-59 months old through community nutrition diagnosis and early detection of acute malnutrition in, Lower Shabelle and Bakool Region. Admitting of severe acute malnutrition and referral of 8000 (3840 boys and 4160girls) to the nearest supplementary feeding program for moderate malnourished children after providing basic nutrition counseling on infant and young child feeding. Activity 2: Treatment of Severe Acute Malnutrition in children 6-59months.The project will provide treatment of severe acute malnutrition for children who are 6-59months old. Specifically, admit and treat 8000 children (3840 boys and 4160 girls) with severe acute malnutrition without medical complication in outpatient therapeutic program. Through scaling up of 8 fixed nutrition centers and six mobile teams in Lower Shabelle and Bakool Region. Activity 3: Provide multiple micronutrients supplementation for pregnant and lactating womenIn accordance with the project objective, we intend provide multiple micronutrients supplementation for pregnant and lactating women in the targeted regions. This will be achieved by providing micro nutrient supplementation, Vitamin A and Iron folic supplementation to 6745 of eligible pregnant and lactating women and 15641(7500boys and 8134 girls) children from 6-59 months in the target districts and .80% of the PLWs in the project areas will be targeted to prevent and treat anemia which is the leading cause of morbidity infant and young child feeding promotion Activity 4 Provide Infant and young child feeding promotion and Counselling We intend to Conduct IYCF Counseling (both individual and group counseling) for mothers/ caregivers visiting the nutrition centers. 4045 mothers and 200 fathers will be targeted during the project period.The project intends feed Infant and young child and also provide counseling by Organizing 20 sessions on IYCF-E at community level targeting 800 elder mothers and 100 fathers. The IYCF officer will prepare the relevant IYCF topics and appropriate IEC materials. The IYCF officer will prepare the relevant IYCF topics and appropriate IEC materials. Activity 5: Empowering mothers through educating MUAC usage at community levelThe project intends to provide training to 540 mothers and 60 traditional birth attendants of using MUAC for nutrition diagnosis at household level, through supervising of nutrition officer and educating of CHNWs/ CNV. Activity 6: Capacity building of Nutrition StaffThe project intends to Conduct 5 days of integrated management of acute malnutrition to Nutrition staff in the mentioned Districts in Lower Shabelle, and Bakool Regions. Activity 7: Constructing child friendly spaces Constructing three child friendly spaces of public fixed MCH in order to promote mental wellbeing of children and to enhance leisure of children in, Lower Shabelle, and Bakool.
