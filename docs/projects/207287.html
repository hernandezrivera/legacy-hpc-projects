Project Summary

The project is targeting 17.000 households from IDPs, and host populations affected by conflict in Montepuez, Mocimboa da {raia and Palma districts through enhanced access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services. The project activities will include drilling boreholes, rehabilitation of water points, water committee trainings, latrines construction and hygiene promotion.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project is targeting IDPs, and host populations affected by conflict in Montepuez and Meluco districts. The project seeks to enhance access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services to these vulnerable populations.ForAfrika will target IDPs living in the resettlement sites, accommodation centres and host communities affected by the conflict in Cabo Delgado with the proposed services. ForAfrika intends to drill boreholes and rehabilitate water points in locations with high populations and lacking essential services, including schools. Water committees will also be established and trained for the maintenance and repair of the rehabilitated boreholes. Their role will promote the sustainability of the WASH facilities beyond the project period.Households will be provided with concrete slabs to construct latrines. The project will construct latrines for people living with disabilities (PLWD) and female-headed households. ForAfrika will further construct emergency communal latrines in transit centres and new resettlement sites.Hygiene promotion activities will be carried out to help the affected population avoid and limit hygiene risks associated with poor sanitation. The hygiene promotion will also help reduce the spread of diseases.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) Drilling of boreholes : Establishment of water points through boreholes with handpumps and solar powered systems will be prioritized to ensure that vulnerable populations have access to sufficient quantity and quality of water.2) Borehole/water point maintenance/rehabilitation:Rehabilitation of boreholes will also be undertaken; vulnerable families will also be supported with point of use water treatment and purification materials/products.3) Water committee establishment/training/re-training: Water committees will also be established for new water points and trained for the maintenance and repair of the rehabilitated boreholes. Existing water committees will also be targeted based on assessed training needs. 4) Sanitation: Access to appropriate, safe, clean and reliable sanitation facilities especially for conflict affected populations will be prioritized. This will achieved mainly through households will being provided with concrete slabs to construct latrines. People living with disabilities (PLWD) and female-headed households will also be assisted to construct latrines. Emergency communal latrines in transit centres and new resettlement sites.5) Hygiene promotion: Hygiene promotion supporting information dissemination, behavior change and actions to reduce the risk of preventable disease will be undertaken. The targeted population will be reached with sanitation messages which will be delivered by staff on one-to-one basis through use of megaphones and community leaders. Hygiene kits will also be distributed to households – with women and girls prioritized with products that support their dignity and wellbeing.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will conduct security risk assessments to identify potential risks that may harm project personnel. Social and Environmental Responsibility assessment will be contacted to assess any possible negative impact of the project. Through, community consultations, possible sources of conflict and risk will be identified.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Identification of potential beneficiary and the selection of vulnerable people will be conduct in consultation with community leadership. This will be conducted to ensure that everyone in need is properly identified and included. The project will conduct vulnerability assessment with the use of the Households vulnerability assessment tool (HVAT). The toll will enable project to identify different household vulnerability situation and determine those in need to be targeted.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disability inclusion allows for people with disabilities to take advantage of the benefits of the same health promotion and prevention activities experienced by people who do not have a disability. Inclusion should lead to increased participation in socially expected life roles and activities, such as being a student, worker, friend, community member, patient, spouse, partner, or parent.There will be disability measurement in household surveys with the use of Model Disability Survey to collect functioning and disability data at the population level. The tool will help to identify people with disabilities in consultation with affected population and community leadership.
