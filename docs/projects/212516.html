Project Summary

Considering the high numbers of newly displaced people, there is a significant need for the provision of WASH services to the people who are living in Camps which they do not have these services.
The proposed action will fill the gap of needs to displaced people from south of Idleb to improve safe access to water of sufficient quality and quantity; sanitation; hygiene practices for 12750 (6418 Male and 6332 Female) (Men:2560, Women:3540, Boys:3858, Girls:2792) 2800 HHs conflict-affected people in mentioned camps below in Dana and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts of Idleb governorate as following:
-Idleb, Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Haranbush, (Alsawamii camp)
-Idleb, Harim, Dana, Burj Elnumra, (Alsalam camp)
-Idleb, Harim, Dana, Sarmada, (Omar5 camp)
-Idleb, Harim, Dana, Deir Hassan - Darhashan, (Aljehad camp)
Camps can not be connected to nearly water source, due to no near source to those all camps as well as due to HLP issues as well, so not allowing BRD to do it,
The proposed action will ensure safe and chlorinated water delivery to 12750 IDPs, and improving sanitation and Hygiene promotion services for 4 camps, reaching 12750 people who will also benefit from increased access to safe water.
This will be achieved through implementation of essential support for water trucking services (35 L per day per person) in addition to providing, installing, maintenance, cleaning a Prefabricated latrines (25 persons per cell of latrines caravans) with desludging a septic as well as hygiene kits distribution to enhance personal hygiene, in addition to Removal of solid waste from 4 camps and self settlement and disposal to the communal landfills.
BRD (Basma for relief and development) will ensure an integrated approach covering Water, Sanitation and Hygiene's (WASH) needs and protecting the health of targeted camps.
In addition to mainstreaming the protection principles in all project phases. BRD will provide equitable access for men/women and boys/girls to the same amount of water during the project period, latrines, HKs (hygiene kits) and promotion activities.
BRD will train some M/F from affected population in those camps as hygiene promoters to take the lead of raising the hygiene awareness of the beneficiaries in those camps.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

BRD will implement the project in 4 camps, at beginning will conduct detailed TA to assess the most current situation because the situation is changing daily (new IDPs) Field staff will train voluntaries to conduct hygiene sessions promotion activities under supervision of BRD staff, BRD will conduct hygiene awareness raising campaign with distributing the brochures and invite members of camps management, Provide presentations, to explain importance of hygiene. BRD will construct 70 latrines' caravans and dig 70 septic tanks, each caravan has 5 latrines (4 normal and 1 for older and disabilities) with ramp and lights, doors, windows and washbasins..etc) BRD will rehabilitate 60 latrines and water trucking as well. After conducting the hygiene promotion activities BRD will distribute 2800 (2 times) HK for IDPs in those camps (Razor, Sanitary pads,etc) BRD will distribute 380 water tanks, BRD will deliver chlorinated water (70 ML chlorine will be added to each 1M) Regular testing will be conducting for water in tanks and trucks. 35 L per capita will be delivered daily, BRD will take into consideration safety of water through Water safety plan (35 L*12750=319M3 per day) for drinking and 12750*2l per day per person for latrines, BRD will conduct PDM to measure satisfaction and Verification of selected BNFs who received services. BRD will desludging septic tanks which BRD going to dig (64 septic tanks), 2 time each septic tanks per month. BRD will Remove solid waste from 4 camps
