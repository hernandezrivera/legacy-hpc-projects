Project Summary

As an endeavor to support the relatively fragmented healthcare system in Afrin Area, HIHFAD plans to support the equipping and the operational costs of three ambulances so they can be used to promote the referral capacities of the health system in Afrin area.

Needs

Increasing access to humanitarian life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for those most vulnerable and in need is the first objective of the health cluster.Afrin is among the areas mostly affected with the ongoing conflict. Among the consequences is the fragmented healthcare system in the area. According to the latest data communicated by the health cluster/ Gaziantep hub, Afrin district hosts some 280.000 individuals and the healthcare severity levels across the district ranges from 3 to 4 out of 6. The area suffers shortages particularly on the referral networks between primary and secondary or tertiary healthcare facilities not to mention the shortages suffered in terms of the provision of the services at the available facilities. Given that, HIHFAD seeks to support the healthcare provision for those in need in the area of Afrin through operating three ambulances that will potentially support the referral system as a part of the whole health system in Afrin area.

Implementation of activities

- Provide essential primary and secondary health care services, including trauma care, EmONC and referral to specialist services: HIHFAD will ensure that three ambulances are made available to respond to any case that requires referral from a facility to another if Afrin area where the whole intervention will be coordinated with the other partners operating in the area as well as the WHO and Hatay health directorate.• Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies: HIHFAD will ensure that the ambulances consistently have adequate quantities of the logistical and medical supplies that are required for the implementation of the planned activities, • Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services: HIHFAD will make sure that all of the paramedics assigned to operate the ambulances are well trained according to the applicable modules communicated by the WHO and Hatay health directorate so those paramedics will be on the same page of capacities with the other healthcare providers providing similar services in the area.• Increase access to health services by establishing functional health facilities and mobile medical units and supporting referral: The basis of the intervention is operating ambulances to support the referral system in Afrin area.

Beneficiaries are affected by conflict related incidences while being served.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: The intervention will take place in an area considered relatively safe and distant from any potential conflict hot zones. Coordinate with the local authorities (Civilian LCs and HD) to support the exclusion of the health teams from any possible clashes. Technical support by HIHFAD safety and security team. Training of staff on contingency plans.2. Patients are subject to harm due to low-quality healthcare services.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: HIHFAD follows strict HR recruitment procedures in terms of the qualifications and expertise required for medical personal. Also, HIHFAD consistently evaluate any gap of the technical capacities for its medical staff. Feedback mechanisms to be made available for the beneficiaries to ensure their participation in identifying any gap in the staff technical capacities that need to be addressed. In the same context, HIHFAD consistently seeks to ensure the standardization of the services provided in terms of medical equipment, medical supplies and pharmaceuticals. 3. While being served, Beneficiaries are abused, harassed, undergo compromised privacy or dignity.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Child safeguarding training provided for all staff. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. Ensure individuals of vulnerable groups (children, women, elderly and people with disabilities) are accompanied while served. Ensure adequate female staff representation.4. Confidentiality of the information provided by the Beneficiaries is compromised.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: staff of the Facility adapt HIHFAD code of conduct. Tags about confidentiality of patient records and information to be made available for the beneficiaries. Maintaining patient information through well secured and strictly monitored modalities inaccessible to public. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. 5. Communities are affected by medical wastes produced as a result of the intervention.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Securing the medical wastes prior to disposal. Application of medical waste management pathways for the purpose of proper disposal of them.
