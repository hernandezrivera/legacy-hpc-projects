Project Summary

The project aims at providing access to protective learning routines for 201.000 conflict and extremism affected children and adolescents through support to operational formal schools (primary and secondary) in Far-north region. The project intends through the blend of interventions proposed (distribution of school inputs, teachers training, studies) to prevent attacks on education and ensure education in emergencies delivery in conflict and high insecurity context.

Besoins / Needs

The target population consists of pupils and teachers from seven subdivisions in the Far-north region. The intervention zones are the subdivisions hosting the highest numbers of enrolled children affected by the Lac Chad Basin crisis, which are Logone and Chari Division (Fotokol, Makary, Blangoua), Mayo Sava Division (Kolofata) and Mayo Tsanaga Division (Mokolo, Koza and Mayo Moskota)The project benefit 201,000 children aged 4 to 17 in school and in NFE. It will build the capacities of 500 teachers from primary and secondary schools on PSS and C/DRR to improve the protective learning environment in and around schools. Teachers will learn to conduct a risk analysis based on the fears of children, and will use the umbrella methodology to prioritize gender specific risks to be addressed through mitigation measures. In order to generate evidence on the perception of safety by school users (parents, students, and teachers) and to strongly advocate for the protection of education against any form of attacks, the project will also conduct a Protective Learning Environment study on selected schools (non-representative sample).
