Project Summary

The complex emergency and conflict in the North West and South west started in late 2016 and has resulted in the displacement of over 230,000 (UNOCHA, situaton report No 11).
H4BF is proposing the continuation of its current provision of WaSH services through a one-year WaSH project that will address critical gaps in WASH activities for a highly vulnerable population among the People in Need in host communities in Mifi, Bamboutos, Haut-Nkam, Menoua, Ndé, Noun of the West region and Mbere, Mayo-Banyo, Mbéré and Vina in Adamawa region of Cameroon.
Due to ongoing conflict and security operation in West and Adamawa regions remain “hard to reach” communities with the least access to health services, permanent shelters, safe water, sanitation facilities and the highest rate of malnutrition and protection issues (REACH - November 2018 - April 2019). Some sites in those 10 division’s still have access to less than 7L/pers/day of safe water and a huge gap noted in terms of sanitation referenced to the WASH Sector Gap matrix in the“hard to reach” communities do not comply with the SPHERE standard of 50 pers/functional latrines.
H4BF is thus proposing to intervene in Mifi, Bamboutos, Haut-Nkam, Menoua, Ndé, Noun of the West regions and Mbere, Mayo-Banyo, Mbéré and Vina in Adamawa under this project so as to achieve its specific objective which is “to improve to sustainable Safe Water, Sanitary facilities n and Hygiene Services to the affected population in West and Adamawa regions through:
• Training of 2 WASH committees at Mayo Banyo and Noun Division
• Construction of 30 households latrines in Mayo Banyo, Bamboutos, Menoua, Mifi, Nde and Noun division
• Construction of 3 boreholes in Noun Division
• Construction of 2 taps in Mayo Banyo
• Distribution of Menstrual Hygiene management Kit in Menoua, Mifi, Nde and Noun division
• Carryout WASH need assessment in Noun Division
