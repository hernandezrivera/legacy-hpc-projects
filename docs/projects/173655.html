Project Summary

The program will ensure access to life-saving quality messages and services for the management of severe and moderate acute malnutrition of children between ages 6-59 months (Disaggregated to boys, girls and disabled children), prevent under-nutrition among vulnerable groups by disseminating key messages of infant feeding among pregnant and lactating mothers, and the advantages of nutritious supplements, and locate out-patient therapeutic points and health facilities in the community in close collaboration with SPHCDA and UNICEF.
The program will reach 120 children referred for Micro-Nutrient Powder supplements, 1500 new mothers reached with key messages of IYCF, and caregivers will be reached with hygiene promotion messages and COVID-19 prevention protocols as well as referred for birth registration.

1 - Needs

The BAY (Borno, Adamawa and Yobe) states have been prone to malnutrition due to inadequate food production, intake, uneven distribution, poor preservation techniques, improper preparation, restrictions, taboo and other inequality issues , the situation worsened due to the conflicts infiltrated in the region by the Non state armed opposition group (NSAG). Since 2009 which resulted to loss of livelihoods especially farming/gardening activities . This crisis has led to deteriorating health and nutritious conditions of vulnerable people especially children under the age of 5 years, resulting to severe food insecurity and acute malnutrition problem. About 1.1 million people are in need of nutrition assistance . The BAY states have remained a great concern with issues of malnutrition, in the year 2020, this concerns have been greater than before due to the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, poor hygiene conditions, less access to good food due to inflation rates and the lean season The information available has shown that the Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) rate mostly affects children between ages 6 to 59 months particularly in North east Nigeria couple with the protracted attacks by the NSAG and the recent COVID-19 pandemic has gruesomely increase malnutrition rate in Borno state precisely in the southern part, the rainy/lean season also pose as a bottleneck as food items are scarce and expensive due to inflation rates across the country as a result of the lockdown and border closures as most families cannot afford to carter for their wards and communities due to the economic hardship experienced . The Global novel COVID-19 pandemic has shifted national priorities as high mortality rates are registered this year than the previous years , the restrictions placed on movements and accessing some vital services, precisely mass MUAC screening which has been suspended to contain the spread of the corona virus especially among the most vulnerable internally displaced persons has caused a threat on the lives of vulnerable Acutely malnourished children Scaling up and strengthening service provision in southern Borno to address the issues of SAM and MAM through CMAM has played a vital role in curing SAM and MAM cases as relevant IYCF key messages are disseminated in the communities to caregivers of children under the age of 5 years and community based health facilities are strengthen to treat and respond to acute malnutrition cases. Community Mobilization, which is one of the components of CMAM has proven effective overtime as it has proven results of timeliness, referral and follow-up and tracing of defaulters in the community of implementation.

2 - Activities

Strengthening community mobilization in confronting sensitive acute malnutrition is one approach to be used for this program while integration with other sectors such as WASH, Food Security and Livelihoods and Child’s protection will be used as another approach in implementing the project.For community Mobilization, Community based Nutrition Mobilizers will be engaged to carry out Mass MUAC Screening for children (girls and boys) in the community between the ages of 6-59 months with MUAC tapes, under close supervision, they will carry out sensitization messages and key IYCF messages across the communities, targeting caregivers, pregnant mothers, and fathers.Through the established Mother and Father support groups that serve as the gateway, the community Mobilizers will conduct meetings and disseminate IYCF key messages and hygiene promotion messages, and COVID-19 prevention key messages, using the multi-sectoral approach, referring vulnerable children with severe acute malnutrition cases without complication to out-patient therapeutic posts while SAM cases with complications will be referred to stabilization centers, upon discharge, they will be referred to food assistance by other actors, vulnerable children with moderately acute malnutrition will be administered Micro-Nutrient Powder to enhance recovery.As an integral part of the project innovation, caregivers will be trained on how to locally make tom-brown powder, which is highly nutritious and locally available in southern Borno which include: Corn, Millet, and Groundnuts powder that can easily be prepared with hot water and administered to the child, this will also compliment the micro-nutrient powder and can be administered to both severely and moderately acute malnourished cases.For accountability and effectiveness of the project implementation, Nutrition Supervisors will be engaged to carry out supportive supervision and work hand in hand with the community-based nutrition mobilizers.
