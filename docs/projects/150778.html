Project Summary

The intervention will be aligned with the third Strategic Objective of the 2018 2020 humanitarian response plan. As it is mention on this Strategic objective, Palestinians are vulnerable to rights violations, especially in relation to their livelihood, with which they struggle due to the prolonged crisis of the OPT. One of the main concerns of the Palestinian population is the impact of protection threats and violations on livelihoods, due to the fact that these communities rely heavily on agriculture and livestock as their main source of income. The lack basic agricultural needs and resources to improve the infrastructures are causing many lost to the farmers on the production in terms of quality and quantity.
Also, it will address the Food Security Sector objectives: “the resilience, food security and the productive capacity of vulnerable households are restored/enhanced and livelihoods protected”.

Needs

In relation to the coordination of the project, during the first phase of identification, PARC was involved in the coordination mechanism promoted by the FSS. In addition, PARC organized workshops with the communities to coordinate and to focus on their main needs. Initially, based on the organization experience and information, in addition to the information given by the MoA about the situation of the different governorates, Tubas governorate was selected due to the high number of greenhouses (GH) that are in need of rehabilitation because of the weather conditions , Qalqilyia, for carrier lines., the focus will be on area C and surrounded by settlements, areas were women and youth have an important role, availability of water resources to expand irrigated agricultural lands and agricultural lands located inside or outside the separation wall depending on the activity. After this, PARC met with the different village councils and Ministries in the Governorates, in order to coordinate the action with them. The field supervisor conducted different workshops on the different locations. For the GH, there were two workshops conducted on the 9th of september 2018: first one with representatives of Pioneer farmers, Farmers committee and Cooperatives. In relation to the water carrier lines, a workshop was held on 2 Oct 2018 with the local councils, farmer’s committees and the cooperatives in order to verify and update the needs that they have. The most urgent needs for the farmers: the lack of material resources necessary for the rehabilitation and expansion of the water carrier lines, the farmers' access to their land behind the separation wall without suffering any kind of restriction or harassment, the high loss of water due to the old networks and high production costs. Also, this is making the distribution of water in irrigated areas unorganized. in addition to the previous situation, the damage of infrastructures like the greenhouses are making the production much more expensive for the farmers because the wide spread of insects and diseases. In addition, one of the common needs was the high unemployment rate that these areas are facing. PARC could identify specific needs for women for this intervention: one of them is the ventilation system on the GH. As mainly is women who work on them, they lack of good conditions and hygiene which resulted in different diseases and problems. Due to the context, the lack of incomes will generate a worst situation for women and girls which will be the ones that will suffer the lack of money before: girls will be the ones who will not be able to go to the school and, in terms of special needs for women for hygiene, they will not be able to get them.Finally, a workshop was conducted with key stakeholders and actors in order to validate the available information and to coordinate with the different NGOs. Protection Mainstreaming: all the data stated in this document regarding the needs was collected from the targeted beneficiaries, who clearly identify their needs through their participation in the organized workshops. Accountability, all the materials that will be provided for the beneficiaries will be in a good quality, meeting the money cost efficient, that the beneficiaries will be enrolled on the material selection process. Rehabilitating the GH condition, and carrier lines will help in upgrading the safety and production of the farmers and upgrade their allowing them to keep having their livelihood.A Validation for this needs happened by many field visits to these proposed locations, recommendation of the MOA and FSS meetings, Direct contact with the local village councils and CBO’s.

Activities

Finally; the need assessment will be conducted in order to start implementing the different activities. This will allow PARC put all the efforts in the more specific and urgent needs, choosing which activities will be implemented to each farmer. Beneficiaries will be selected in a transparent and participatory way based on clear criteria. In order to target the most vulnerable farmers, application forms will be filled up by the farmers including different data such as average income, land location, owneland area, exposed to natural/man made disaster or water prices for irrigation. Prior to any project implementation, the organization will strictly coordinate the final definition and selection of localities and beneficiaries with line ministries offices, and with any other organization working in the same area and sector of activities. Activity 1: Rehabilitation of 100 dunums of Greenhouses benefiting 100 families. The GH will be selected according to the following selection criteria to reach the most marginalized group. The project has a specific questionnaire that should be filled through the beneficiaries. This will be through giving priority for those families who have the less income salaries comparing to the number of the family members, headed by women, high number of family members, if they have students in the university, if they have disable people in the family, giving the priority for lands located in area C since the majority of the project is implemented in area C, and giving less priority for families that received similar services from other institutions. The high priority will be given for people who are suffering from natural disasters, and their sole and main income is from these GH only. In addition, a special consideration will be given to those families who are not able to rehabilitate their greenhouses due to difficulties in their economic situation rather than families who could rehabilitate their houses from their own income resources. This rehabilitation will include different activities depending on the needs of each farmer: Tubas Governorate• Plastic roof and sides ‘for GH.• Side protection of greenhouses.• Replacement of the deteriorated irrigation systems with new regulated irrigation networks • Rehabilitation of wood with good specifications for greenhouses.• Rehabilitation of ponds beside the greenhouses to benefit from the harvesting of water from green houses in some cases. The rehabilitation of these greenhouses will increase the protection of the livelihood of the farmers, reducing also their vulnerability and food insecurity. Activity 2: Expansion and Rehabilitation of water carrier lines: Qalqiliya Governorate 10 km of water carrier lines will be expanded and rehabilitated, benefiting 200 families. The first step will be to select the beneficiaries and to identify the specific needs of each of them priority will be given to the lands located in Area C, Surrounded or behind the wall, high percentage of leaking water, after that, the tender will be prepared and announced then the water carrier lines will be rehabilitated. After the implementation of these activities in the open irrigated lands; the farmers will be able to increase the productivity of the land and to decrease the amount of water lost with the old irrigation system. Activity 3: Rehabilitation of 2 water reservoir: 2 metallic water reservoirs will be rehabilitated, these 2 reservoirs located in Qalqiliya and in Alnabi Eliyas which considered area B , but it provides water to lands inside area C, this activity will organize the access of water to farmers increase the number of the irrigated areas, and protect land from the confiscation risks.
