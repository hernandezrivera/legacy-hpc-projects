Project Summary

JMCO will support and implement multi-sectoral humanitarian response (education and child protection) to affected population in Central Darfur state, targeting 5 localities. JMCO planned to cover about 81,075 individuals direct and indirectly, ( education 35,375 , child protection 45,700 ). JMCO will continue implementing integrated education and child protection components in Central Darfur state. the intervention is focus on integrated inclusive access to quality education and child protection specially for children at primary school age, the project designed to implement and improve school environment, through expanding access and improving the quality of basic education and construction of class rooms, building capacity of teachers on different aspects includes interventions at system and school levels, targeting disadvantaged schools through three components:
Strengthening the education provider’s capacity, as well as supporting a state learning assessment.
JMCO has ongoing education child protection programme in three localities of Jabal Marra (North Jabal Marra, Central Jabal Marra and West Jabal Marra) including Um Dukhun and has active offices at locality level which is accounted as one of JMCO Strength and increase success of project activities implementation.
From the report Displacement Tracking Matrix DTM About 8,079 cases of additional vulnerabilities in need of further support and assistance were identified through the protection indicator. Lactating and pregnant women made up 42 and 29 per cent respectively, followed by physical disabilities (9%), chronic illnesses (7%), single parents (5%), unaccompanied elders (4%), mental illnesses (3%) and unaccompanied minors (less than 1%) indicated just in Jabal Marra localities reported by IOM on August 2020 as well as the drop out of school lead to risk of sexual abuse and harassment for children, protection part designed to prevent and protect people at risk children (girl and boys) including vulnerable adolescent at risk , JMCO will focus on enhanced coping skills through various psychosocial activities will contribute in strengthening resilience of children and communities ,service providers at Central Darfur States have strengthened capacities and deliver quality specialized child protection services with appropriate referral mechanisms in place as well communities in Central Darfur State have strengthened mechanisms to protect children from violence, abuse, and exploitation including FGM/C and child marriage , Construction / rehabilitation of center for integrated legal and social services, mapping of the social workforce at state level , establishment of preventive child protection services through family support, community based alternative care and case management program including training , establishment of child protection community-based networks and skilled social work volunteers and legal aid volunteer teams including training , Construction of child and adolescent safe spaces

1 - Needs

In the past years including this year JMCO has conducted two education and child protection surveys using SMART methodology during (22nd January 2019 & 28th February 2020) dropout of school and lack of quality education environment prevalence were estimated very low including child protection issues and still there’s ongoing displacement as reported by IOM registers over 12,300 displacements and returnees between July and September 2020 that Central Darfur, conflict between factions of the Sudan Liberation Army resulted displacement of 8,846 , however still well above the critical threshold. Although the results suggested an improvement of the education and child protection required, the localities mentioned still have huge need to establish a better education and child protection environment for the displaced living in camps and the returnees through the following strategies below. Central Darfur state one of Sudan states indicated in HRP priority areas and HRP focus on Providing timely multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to crisis affected people to reduce mortality and morbidity among population including Contribute to building resilience to recurrent shocks and improving vulnerable people’s access to basic services as well as Enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs through quality and principled humanitarian action also education indicated that a number 1.46M in need and 0.74M targeted as well protection sector indicated 1.84M people in need and 0.74M targeted . JMCO will support improvement of education and child protection through campaigns to ensure participation of Community leaders and Parents-Teachers Association (PTAs) members will work with the community to mobilize Out of School Children (OOSC) and ensure they are enrolled in primary and Accelerated Education Programme (AEP) schools , rehabilitation and construction of class rooms , training teachers & PTAs on learner centered teaching methodologies, school management and roll out the unlock literacy program, including advocacy on recruitment of Teachers with state actors at sub national level including School improvement plans are drafted and shared to improve teaching and learning through enhanced literacy skills with links to longer term education options including Literacy Boost in Emergencies (LBiE) training as well School Code of Conduct, Teacher Code of Conduct, Inclusive Pedagogy , support campaign on Girls’ education strategy and advocate for the implementation of strategy at all level with participant of Girls themselves , establishment of child protection community-based networks and skilled social work volunteers and legal aid volunteer teams including training , construction of child friend space , construction of family and child unit for legal support , provision of psycho-social support as the foundations to social cohesion within the communities for vulnerable adolescents at risk, including UASC, children on the move, at risk of violence and exploitation , Support participation of children/students in the development of code of conducts for teachers and students, structured extra school activities/ program , establishment of system for prevention and response to school related gender-based violence (SRGBV) , Conduct KABP surveys; design and implement programmatic interventions in the selected localities to ensure education and child protection data are updated to help sector for planning and intervention .

2 - Activities/Outputs

Output 1.1: More than 19,050 out-of- school children, especially girls, and those in the most vulnerable situations in central Darfur States, ( Bindis , Golo , Rokero and Um dukhun ) are enabled to access quality pre-primary, primary and alternative learning opportunitiesOutput 2: Education providers, parents and communities in central Darfur States have improved knowledge and skills to contribute to quality learning in inclusive safe and protective school environmentsOutput 3: Service providers at Central Darfur States have strengthened capacities and deliver quality specialized child protection services with appropriate referral mechanisms in place.Output 4: 48% of communities in Central Darfur State have strengthened mechanisms to protect children from violence, abuse, and exploitation including FGM/C and child marriage.
