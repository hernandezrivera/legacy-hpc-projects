Project Summary

The project aims to strengthen the capacity of Health Department Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to respond to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic by assisting them in training of the healthcare staff, ensuring the availability of the appropriate personal protection equipment for the frontline health workers and development of Risk Communication and Community Engagement material to appraise the communities on the prevention and management of COVID-19 and dispel the false information and rumors.

1- Project Activities

International Medical Corps propose to support the Health Department, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK) province, where we have long standing presence and close working relations with the Health Department, PDMA and District heath authorities. Duration of the interventions will be 6 months. International Medical Corps propose to support the Health Department KPK through the following interventions: a) Training of frontline healthcare workers:As the understanding of the COVID19 transmission patterns and impacts are rapidly changing, there is an urgent need to keep the healthcare staff updated on the latest information through training and refreshers. International Medical Corps will provide capacity building trainings to the healthcare providers from the Health Department Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) on various topics specific to their areas of expertise and areas of interventions. The proposed topics of capacity building trainings are COVID-19 case detection, investigation, management, infection prevention, and control, and proper use (donning and doffing) and disposal of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).b) Developing, printing and displaying of IEC material:Although it has been more than ten months that first case of COVID-19 was reported in Pakistan, but despite hundreds and thousands of infections and deaths, there are still misconceptions, myths and false information circulating regarding COVID-19. The lethality of COCID-19 is observed to be intensified in the second wave of the coronavirus in Pakistan. At the same time people are seen to be much more careless than the first wave Therefore, there is urgent need of ongoing risk communication and community engagement, to dispel the myths and ensure accurate information on the diseases, prevention and management reaches the community. International Medical Corps will work closely with the Health Department KP to develop and contextualize standardized messages. These messages will be translated into local languages and disseminated through electronic (local Radio and Television channels) and social media. Provision of PPE: As the second wave of infections is gaining strength all over the country, the ongoing need for Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) has also increased. As more patients are being admitted to hospitals, increasing number of healthcare staff is encountering the positive patients and are at risk of contracting infection. In order to ensure safety of Health Department, KP staff, International Medical Corps will procure surgical masks, face shields, goggles, bio hazard bags, N-95 masks, gowns, examination gloves, heavy duty gloves, gum boots, disposable plastic aprons, scrubs, hand sanitizers and sharp containers and will donate the PPEs to the health Department, KP to be used in their health facilities. The provision of PPEs will enable the government healthcare staff to continue the essential health services for the vulnerable population during this pandemic and at the same time protect themselves from the virus.
