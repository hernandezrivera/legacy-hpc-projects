Project Summary

According to the International Organization for Migration Displacement Tracking Matrix (IOM DTM) Sudan Weekly Displacement Snapshot (16), more than 6 million people have been displaced within Sudan, increased by an estimated 611,000 people being the new displacements from Aj Jazirah during December 2023. The 6 million internally displaced persons (IDPs) are sheltering in 6,282 locations across all of Sudan’s 18 States, including North and West Kordofan States. Almost nine months after the fighting between the two parties that erupted on 15 April 2023 in the capital Khartoum, and gradually expanded to other States causing people fled their homes and properties, with children representing about half of the people displaced. Sudan is now considered the country with the largest number of displaced people and the largest child displacement crisis in the world according to the IOM report.
The impact of displacement on war affected populations and hosting communities where these IDPs were occupied is the limited access to basic services including WASH, Health and Education and access to market in situation of non-availability of cash on hand.
Various challenges including insecurity, looting, bureaucratic impediments, poor network and phone connectivity, lack of cash, and limited technical and humanitarian staff on the ground, have been affecting the delivery of humanitarian assistance in many parts of the country. Fuel shortages also affecting the movement of humanitarian staff and supplies and the generation of power needed for operations (maintaining cold chain storage, supplying water, etc.). Despite all these challenges, humanitarian partners continue to provide life-saving assistance to the vulnerable people they can reach.
Islamic Relief Worldwide is aiming to contribute in addressing the urgent needs for 5,000 families,1,000 in North Kordofan comprising of 7,000 individuals and 4,000 HHs in West Kordofan (28,000 individuals) comprising in total of 3,500 war affected IDPs and 1,500 vulnerable resident HHs in both North and West Kordofan States, through provision of immediate lifesaving humanitarian assistance including multi-purpose cash value equivalent to 120,000 SDG (per HH. The cash assistance aimed to benefit target beneficiaries in covering their basic need of food and other lifesaving items. The intervention will be implemented in El Fulla and El Nuhud Localities of West Kordofan State and in Shiekan Locality of North Kordofan State, where large number of IDPs were occupied according to State HAC and IOM separate assessments that conducted recently in both States. HAC assessment in West Kordofan is indicating total number of IDPs in the State are 28,548 HHs comprising 154,386 individuals scattered in all 14 localities of the State, where in North Kordofan, the assessment in indicating around 34,000 families. The largest numbers of IDPs in West Kordofan are in El Fulla (4,161 HHs) and El Nuhud (4,005 HHs), whereas in North Kordofan, the majority are in Shiekan Locality. Accordingly the project will target 4,000 HHS from the two indicated localities and support them with cash assistance and in North Kordofan will target 1,000 HHs, because there are other agencies providing similar interventions. In West Kordofan, no any partners currently delivering this type of intervention.
The proposed intervention will also include registrations, verification for the targeted beneficiaries in addition to awareness rising campaigns and sessions to affected populations on personal hygiene, socio psychology and protection issues to support the IDPs cope with the new environment.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

IRW has in place a robust complaint response mechanism (CRM) strategy that is fully implemented during project life-cycle and are culturally appropriate, accessible and effective in line with CHS 5. These allows for timely response and feedback to the affected population. Three levels of monitoring beneficiaries feedbacks will followed for the purpose of quality delivery and continuous improvement of services. These are the establishment of complaint boxes, to enable affected populations to report their complaints and provide feedback, The CRM number and email address is printed on each activity implementation banners in local language to allow beneficiaries to report and CRM mobile number is also provided which can be used by majority of the affected populations. These three levels of obtaining community feedbacks is crucial aspect ensured by IRW staff to manage and respond to feedbacks for improvement purposes.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Complaints management is a critical and pivotal aspect of program accountability and continuous improvement of any service delivery program. Islamic Relief worldwide recognizes the need for a standardized approach to establish and strengthen complaints handling mechanism and related processes to be able to demonstrate effectiveness and impact. Special CRSM- Complaint Response Mechanism System for all planned activities will be developed to ensure quality services are delivered to communities in positive manner and feedbacks were collected for improvement purposes on programme delivery. Global guiding document has been developed through a consultative process at local and global levels. Islamic Relief continues to stand firm on zero tolerance for (sexual) misconduct. Therefore, through all delivered interventions IRW, will make sure to create and maintain an environment that prevents sexual exploitation and sexual abuse. IRW staff at all levels have a particular responsibility to support and develop systems that maintain this environment which includes placing and having direct contacts with the target population to manage and deal with such cases if happened. IRW used to share mobile number where beneficiaries can provide feedback, suggestions or complaints on SEA activities or miss appropriateness.
