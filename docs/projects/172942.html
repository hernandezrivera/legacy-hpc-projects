Project Summary

During the recent period, the security situation has changed which reduces the armed clashes and the inter tribal conflict in most areas within North Darfur, but still there are some elements indicating that, the need for shelter and NFI support especially in the areas hosting Refugees, Returnees, and IDPs, mainly due to limited cooping mechanism and deterioration of the humanitarian needs particularly for the most vulnerable families, New comers, Women, Men, Girls, Boys, Children, Elderly, and PWSNs among the community. although some families were affected by natural disaster Flood / Fire with no support provided at the time when the incident took place.
Therefore the the Project is aiming to assist new arrivals, newly accessed area, refugees, returnees. Pregnant and lactating women, chronically ill people, elderly, unaccompanied minors, and physically disabled are particularly vulnerable amongst all affected populations with minimum protection needs, in addition to durable Shelter and NFI needs, especially most vulnerable without shelter and sufficient NFI items to protect them from the elements, this will lead them to remains highly exposed to further health risks.
The Project will target the (Tina, Karnoi and Um baru) due to deteriorated and un secure situation despite the ongoing economic situation as well as the spread of COVID 19 and the lack of preventive drugs, under these circumstances the assessment showed that the need for Shelter and NFIs increased for the said beneficiaries (ES/NFI) to reduce mortality and morbidity as well as to address the protection concerns, and to mitigate the health threats and harsh weather. in addition to flood, fire and natural affected families who were not supported with basic items to have some sort of protection means, with special consideration to PSN, Children, Women and vulnerable families, these are the most category affected by the lack of access to basic services.
All the signs showed that, the need for environmental friendly transitional shelters, this require training for the beneficiaries on the construction techniques to ensure long lasting duration and better secure condition, automatically this operation will create job opportunities on livelihood and in the long term the beneficiaries will be able to undertake the construction role and not relaying on other technical expertise on such activities.
NOHS will arrange training session for the youth and women to be familiarize with technical site of the construction, the training will be coordinated with the community leaders to ensure participation of all beneficiaries located in different part of the camp / site,

1 - Needs

Based on the current humanitarian situation the needs for life saving intervention which become a pulling factors in most areas hosting Refugees, Returnees, IDPs and other vulnerable is catastrophic particularly due to the socio-economic crisis and limited cooping mechanism, despite the ongoing economic crisis continues to be a key driver of deteriorating humanitarian needs, which have been exacerbated by COVID-19. this situation have direct impact to Women, Men, children's, people with disabilities, elderly, refugees, IDPs, returnees and other vulnerable families. NOHS primarily role is the implementation of the activities in coordination with Coopi, and IOM in three (03) localities Tina Karnoi and Um baru, NOHS usually stocked small quantities of small items such as water cups, plates, Ebrigs, clothes for small children on the age of 1 to 5 years, these items will be provided to fire / flood affected, new arrivals or vulnerable families subject for an assessment and verification, as explained this small stock is provided by NOHS own funding for distribution to the said group who are minority among the population in need for support,The role of NOHS is to implement the seasonal distribution (Rainy / Winter) and ongoing distributions to new comers fire or flood affected, all activities will be coordinated with the distribution team which involve Women, men, boys, girls, children, and community leaders to sensitize the targeted group, who were selected during the assessments on the schedule plans for service provision, then the assessment exercise strategy (house to house visit) which is normally be done early in the morning before people goes out to work or collecting fire wood, for the team to observe the shelter condition and the NFI items then recommend for replenishment, in most cases the need is for only plastic sheet which does not last long as they use it throughout the year and the sun is very hot during the summer which decrease the lifetime of the item.Winter distribution: Similar exercise will be undertaken in coordination with the team the schedule for this exercise start in late October / November and most of the item required are Blankets and Plastic Jerry cans, mainly for PWSN, Vulnerable families, however, sometime we coordinate with Wash colleagues for jerry can distribution as they included on the distribute to improve the hygiene promotion.Basically, NOHS team support the distribution for newly comers Refugees/ Returnees, Protracted and disaster / fire affected throughout the year, therefore NOHS have permanent presence in these areas for coordination with stakeholders and timely response to the assessed need. For new comers, they will be hosted in the reception center to be registered then provided with some support and they will be allocated plot to construct their shelter, with assistance from the team on the new building techniques,

2 - Activities/Outputs

NOHS staff are located in the camps 24/7 and they keep track of population movement in coordination with Coopi, IOM, RRR and the sector lead in order to have accurate information on the population figures. for baseline data and future activities related to the sector. NOHS team will coordinate with the community members with some support from the Sector leads to introduce construction techniques thus includes brick making woven mats etc, to mitigate hazards and the impact of natural disasters, Coordinate with the sector lead to maintain small stocks of NFIs and shelter materials (Plastic sheet only) for timely response to people in need to coup with the situation, one of the major activities the team will carry out market survey in a regular basis to indicate the availability of the items and accordingly alert the sector leads on the findings to review the strategy based on the findings. Also NOHS through awareness raising will address some steps related to the distribution exercise such as, concretization of beneficiaries, on the distribution plans (Where when and who are the target) thus includes identification of the distribution sites, planned date and timing, though as NOHS recommend all the distributions related to Shelter and NFIs regardless the beneficiary type to undertake the distributions early morning until breakfast time and continue late afternoon, to allow the community to do their routine home work, and to avoid the hot sun during the day particularly for people with specific needs and other vulnerable. Prior for distribution arrangements the team members will be oriented on their roles during the process. in term of security of the distribution site, who will do what in term of items and quantities per family size, however NOHS will prepare distribution list form includes Camp location, Name of Ben, Omda / Sheik, Block No, family size, item quantity and signature / Finger stamp of the receiving person. Information sharing is the vital part of the process for transparent and work quality, therefore the targeted group will be sensitized in advance, approximately tow weeks before the distribution and they will be given NFI distribution tokens, the token contains information similar to the distribution list. this information will be cross checked against the distribution list for verification purposes prior for issuing the items, nevertheless, the beneficiaries will be aware of what they are going to receive in term of distribution sites, Items name, QTY date and timing. NOHS field supervisor in each location will will carryout on site distribution monitoring (On Site) during the distribution then after 4 to 6 weeks from the distribution, to ensure the beneficiaries received the allocated items and quantity, and observe the availability and utilization of the items and advice the beneficiaries on the best use in order to ensure items kept in good condition and expected to last according to the lifespan. the second monitoring exercise will be planned after three month from the distribution, however, all activities will be coordinated with Coopi the sector lead, IOM, RRR, service providers on ground and local authorities on ground, and Multi Sectorial assessment which will provide much information on the needs and how best they can be addressed, though for coordination purposes and to avoid duplication when it comes for future service provision.NOHS team will support the community are ready to join Inter agency assessment or sector specific assessments, as long as properly coordinated on ground.Monitoring is
