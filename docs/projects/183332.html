Project Summary

Provision of Basic Food Needs for the most vulnerable 350 Lebanese Households and 130 migrant workers Sheltering in grey areas ( Palestinian camps adjacent areas) , in the context of the socio-economic situation is designed is designed for a duration of 12 months starting from September 2021. 480 HHs will be supported with six rounds of food parcels sufficient for a family of 5 individuals. The identification of the most vulnerable 480 HHs is based on the Naba’a database and matching the targeted vulnerabilities/ 2021 first standards Allocation strategy paper, where;
110 families including pregnant and lactating women
173 headed families by a single elderly with one or more children
120 single female-headed households
77 Households with four or more dependents (children at risk)
2160 individuals will be receiving 6 rounds of in-kind food assistance monthly through food parcels, disaggregated by gender as follows; 368 men, 455 women, 605 Girls, and 732 boys. (The objective of distributing food parcels in kind is linked to price changes from one day to another, which makes it more difficult to purchase the planned quantity if the food voucher is approved. The other reason is due to the huge amount of expired materials in the market, so we decided that the best interest of the family requires giving them six food parcels over the course of the six months). Food parcels will be sanitized and then handled door-to-door to the eligible 480 families in order to reduce spreading COVID-19 among the refugee community in case a distribution point is set. the
200 HHs ( 100 Lebanese HHs + 100 Migrants ) are already included within the currently approved LHF project which started on the 15th of august and will be receiving the same type of food kits for 6 rounds.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Naba’a team is well oriented and trained to recognize and detect warning signs of people at risk of violence or victimization, including signs of abuse, neglect, domestic violence etc. The team prepared to address problems and challenges that are faced might create an abusive environment. In case of suspected abuse, Naba'a’ is under full obligation to report to authorities. In addition, the beneficiaries will be well oriented to Naba'a complaint mechanism in order to facilitate receiving it and act accordingly. The estimated percentage of females benefiting from the intervention are approximately equal to male, The distribution method will be based on a door-to-door system to avoid exposing people to transmitting the virus if collected at a distribution point , and this will help us to avoid exposing people to additional risks through this emergency intervention.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

beneficiaries and affected populations have been involved in the needs assessment during March 2021 titled “The Implications of the Economic Crisis in Lebanon on Food Security” outlined through conducting focus group discussions and questionnaire as well. currently the project team are working on the formation of a steering committee composed of; Popular committees, and UNRWA ( due to the fact that the current approved LHF intervention is located within the Palestinian camps , while this part which is complementarity will be beyond the borders of the camps and the steering committee will be enlarged through adding other active members from local community and municipalities. This will facilitate the communication with different stakeholders without doing long process of communication, while INGOs whom are actively working in all locations will be kept updated and promoted to activate referrals.
