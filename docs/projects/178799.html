Project Summary

The proposed project aims to support the wheat value chain in Syria through purchasing wheat crops
from local farmers and use the purchased wheat in bread production.
- 1000 tons of wheat crop (750 soft wheat and 250 durum wheat) will be purchased from the most
vulnerable farmers in Alrouj valley and Maaret tamasrin in order to support their livelihood and hence their resilience.
- IHH&INSAN will coordinate with local councils and local authorities for selecting farmers based on the
following selection criteria:
• Household headed by females up to 30 % of total beneficiaries.
• Households who have people with disabilities in their families.
• Large household with 6 or more individuals in the family.
• Households who have access to farming land from 10 to 15 dunums.
• Households don't receive any agricultural support from other NGOs for this year.
Beneficiaries' names, vulnerability and ownership of the lands will be verified before registration.
committee consists of IHH &INSAN team members in addition to agricultural experts will check the
quality of the wheat before purchasing it in order to detect its Free of cracking, humidity, Mite insect,
fungal diseases and impurities.
The 1000 tons of purchased wheat will be sent to the mill to be grinded to produce 750 tons of local flour which will be sent to the bakery to be baked and produce about1645 bread bundles for
each ton of flour, the weight of the bread bundle is 700 grams.
1,233,750 free bread bundles will be distributed to 4570 Households in 37 camps in Maaret Tamasrin
subdistrict for 26 days / monthly up to 6 months from 1/7/2021 to 31/12/2021 in the following
communities:
- Kaftin 3 camps
- Kafr – Kafrehmul 14 camps
- Kelly 17 camps
- Maaret Elekhwan 3 camps
Distribution criteria will be:
HH with 1 - 4 individuals will receive one bread bundle.
HH with 5 - 8 individuals will receive two bread bundles.
HH with 9 and above will receive three bread bundles.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 20201 , Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply and irrigation systems. Today, 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance - a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020 -with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline.The economy has experienced irreparable harm since the crisis began, with the gross domestic product having declined by60 per cent and the government increasingly unable to raise sufficient revenue to subsidize essential commodities such as fuel and bread on which the most vulnerable families rely. The Syrian pound is in virtual freefall having lost 78 per cent of its value since October 2019, while price increases for staple goods are at an all-time high. More than 90 per cent of the population is now estimated to live below the poverty line. INSAN CHARITY has conducted rapid need assessment on 11/ April 2021 to a Random Sample of 1021 H.H of IDPs in the proposed Camps . The need assessment was able to highlight the most pressing needs among IDPs where primary top concerns for them was free bread with percentage of 94 % -Food baskets 5.5 % -shelter 0.3 % -medical services 0.1% education 0%.-the average range of income for most families (14%) on monthly basis is 300 TL while the average range of expenditure level of most families (14%) on monthly basis is 500 TL. Displacement information:100% of the families displaced one year ago

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Risks of SEA / GBV Likelihood: Medium Impact: HighMitigation Measures: - HH and IC will ensure all staff and vendors sign the code of conduct about SEA/ GBV to deal with these cases strictly and this will mentioned and explained clearly in all contracts -IHH and IC will conduct PSEA and GBV training as refresher to staff before the beginning of the project to Ensure full compliance with PSEA network and GBV sub -cluster guidelines.-choosing safe and secure distribution centers in the targeted camps to be accessible for all beneficiaries especially disables with two distribution lines (one for males and one for women) as well one line for disables and people who have movement difficulties -Distribution will be done at the daytime to avoid any possibility to GBV/SEA cases .- SEA/GBV complaints will be dealt with urgency and confidential manner with referral cases to the focal point assigned by GBV sub-cluster / PSEA network in the targeted area, and the beneficiaries information will not be shared with any external party.-Female distributor workers will be hired to make distribution more comfortable and easier for females and to prevent any case of exploitation or abuse may be done by Male staff.COVID-19 Risk: Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigation Measures: all staff will use the Personal Protection Equipment including masks and gloves and using sanitizer to beneficiaries before entering to the distribution center, moreover, maintain social distance between beneficiaries during distribution.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Security Risks:1-Shelling and aerial bombardment,Monitor the security situation closely by security and safety officer by IHH and IC to take actions accordingly with contingency plan for different scenarios 2- Tensions between local elements in the regionThere will be a flow of information together with IHH's relationship with the local authorities in the tension that may be experienced between local elements and the necessary initiative will be taken to end this tension.Operational Risks:1-Increase wheat price -IHH will the contract with the selected vendor/s with fixed price with USD to avoid any price increase 2-Low quality of breadIHH had dedicated M&E staff in the bakery sites to monitor the bread quality in terms of raw materials and added materials needed such as yeast with random and regular weighing the bread bundle weight to ensure the weigh is 700 gram as agreed upon 3- Technical problems in warehousesWith the coordinated work of IHH and IC, regular controls and reports will be made in order to eliminate the problems that may occur in warehouses.4- Technical problems in the mill It will be easy for IHH to agree with a new mill, with its connections and power in the region, in case of technical problems that the mill may experience.5- Technical problems that may occur in bakeriesIn order to eliminate the technical problems that may arise in the furnaces and to continue the process, lists of other furnaces in the region will be kept and if necessary, it will be ensured that the process is not interrupted by contacting other furnaces. In addition, it will be regularly checked and reported in order to eliminate any technical problems that may arise in existing furnace.
