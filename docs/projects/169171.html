Project Summary

OHCHR has a pre-existing presence in Lebanon, in the form of the Regional Office for the Middle East and North Africa (ROMENA) which is based in Beirut. ROMENA is a member of the HCT/UNCT and the protection sector.
Working closely with the HCT/UNCT, OHCHR will support the mainstreaming of the protection of human rights in the humanitarian response. Activities will focus on the identification of emerging and evolving trends and gaps related to human rights, including individuals and groups at risk of being left behind/experiencing discrimination; analysis and documentation of cases of human rights abuses; support for individuals seeking redress for human rights violations related to the Beirut blast; and advice and advocacy based on international human rights standards to facilitate the humanitarian relief effort.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

(1) identification of emerging and evolving trends and gaps related to human rights including groups at risk of being left behind and those groups in the most vulnerable situation, to inform the humanitarian relief effort. (2) Support for individuals whose human rights have been affected by the Beirut blast. (3) Advice and advocacy to facilitate humanitarian relief through engagement with local authorities.

2 - Activities

(1) In collaboration with local partners, gather and analyse information on protection of human rights with a view to monitoring and reporting on the situation of human rights in Beirut in the aftermath of the blast. (2) Documenting and following up on individual cases, facilitating referrals to Protection Sector parners for humanitarian assistance, and facilitating access to redress mechanism for victims of human rights violations. (3) Support local partners (NHRI, CSOs) with technical assistance and advice on international human rights standards to enhance their capacity to document human rights violations and provide legal aid for individual victims. (4) Support existing mechanism for the provision of legal aid to victims affected by the Beirut blast. (5) Engage with national authorities and security forces with regard to their conduct in facilitating the humanitarian relief effort. OHCHR is coordinating and collaborating with UNDP on support provided for legal aid services (technical support/advice on international human rights standards), dissemination of human rights-related information, and technical assistance to the National Human Rights Commission.
