Project Summary

Mercy-USA in this project will provide the most vulnerable host communities and IDPs in Afrin district Aleppo governorate with different support:
1. Home Garden: through providing CVA assistance for eligible households who have land size about 1 dunum for vegetable production to access agricultural inputs (seed, fertilizer, tools, and irrigation) as well as they will receive training through practical sessions, field days, and FFS. Targeted beneficiaries 300 HH (1500 individuals) can be broken down as follows (Men 412, Women 413, Boys 338, and Girls 337)
2. Livestock Value chain: by Supporting livestock holders to strengthen the productivity through improving their access to fodders by linking them with the local market. They receive CVA to access the fodder from the fodder suppliers. Also, they will receive training through practical sessions, field days and FFS.
Total of 300 livestock breeders, 1500 individuals (750 female and 750male) with average of 15 animals per HH (approximately 4500 sheep) will be supported through E-voucher for animal feed (fodder) three times. The E-voucher animal feed will be distributed at the beginning of the project to the targeted BNFs, each BNFs will receive one E-voucher with the PIN code, the distribution will be per site with close coordination with the local councils. Information about the redemption process, CFM, participated fodder suppliers will be shared with the BNFs during the distribution. The number of rounds is three and each round will cover the needs for two months per round starting from January 2024 to December 2024.
The animal feed will be pellet feed and quality will be based on the livestock technical working group (LTWG) under the FSL Cluster support. The training will cover different topics such as good agricultural practices, Integrated Pest Management, Climate-Smart Agriculture.
**Locations and beneficiaries:
 Aleppo governorate - Afrin district- Jandairis sub-district, 300 HH (Men 412, Women 413, Boys 338, and Girls 337)
 Idlib governorate (Idlib district – Idlib and Maaret Tamsrin sub-district) 300 HH (Men 412, Women 413, Boys 338, and Girls 337).
 Targeted beneficiaries will be both IDPs and host households with 50% each.
**Eligibility Criteria will be:
1) Livestock holders with a maximum of 20 sheep or 1 caw, for livestock;
2) Average of 1 dunum for home garden.
3) No or a low source of income,
4) Do not receive a similar type of assistance,
5) Households Lacking Employable Adults Aged 18-59
**Vulnerability criteria will give for:
1) Women-headed households,
2) Elderly headed households,
3) PLWs,
4) PWDs,
5) IDPs,
6) Household size.
