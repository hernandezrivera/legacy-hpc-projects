Project Summary

The COVID-19 pandemic has increased the need of distance education equipment for children living in NGCA of Donetsk oblast. As part of the response to the threat of the spread of COVID-19, students twice went on extended holidays and on a distance learning format for the period of March-May 2020 and from October 2020.
In the context of distance education, the realization of children's basic rights to receive education becomes limited by the material well-being of their parents — the ability to purchase a tablet / computer to participate in online lessons, to perform online tests and to send homework to teachers for verification.
In addition, there is a need to create an aggregator of online courses that will allow students and teachers to navigate the already freely available distance education tools more effectively in order to optimize the learning process and the process of self-education.
Another aspect is to ensure that children living in NGCA of Donetsk oblast have access to distance education tools designed specifically for their integration into the state educational system. These tools were created to help children studied in NGCA schools to enroll and to continue their education at GCA. Unfortunately, the effectiveness of these models was reduced, both due to insufficient popularization of the tool, and the lack of technical equipment for the population affected by the conflict to use the tools.
An additional effect is to increase the potential of children with special needs. Providing them with tablets (with a protective case) that can perform applications for people with special needs (audio playback, screen magnifier, dictated-to-printed text converter, etc.) will increase their potential in learning and communication.
Another effective tool is the creation of a coworking center that provides computer equipment, Internet access, printing, photocopying and scanning services. The coworking center will temporarily provide the population, both schoolchildren and students, as well as adults who want to get additional education, with public tools for distance learning. The coworking center as a zone of attraction for students will be a convenient platform for providing advice to young people on transition from schools to the job market, vocational or higher education.
In order to help solve these problems, it is proposed to provide children from low-income families (including children with disabilities, children from large families, children from families with one guardian) with tablet computers with a protective cover, to develop an aggregator of existing online courses and to create an educational coworking center based on the organization's office in Donetsk.

1 - Needs

According to information received during cooperation in the framework of humanitarian activities with schools located on the NGCA of Donetsk oblast, from 20% to 40% of students are unable to use the distance learning format due to the lack of technical means (including smartphones that are not adapted to such activities). The respondents were rural and village schools both near the demarcation line and outside the 20 km zone. According to information from open sources, in 2018-2019, the number of children who received school education at NGCA of Donetsk oblast reached 143,409 children. There is no accurate information about children who need technical means to provide access to distance education, but the organization's experience in the field suggests that this need is relevant for about 20,000 school-age children, including:Children from rural areasChildren living near the contact lineChildren from large familiesChildren with special needsChildren with serious illnessesChildren with one caregiver Providing accurate statistics is currently impossible due to restrictions on collecting information at the NGCA, but speaking of individual cases, the organization has repeatedly recorded the needs of the population in such technical means. At the same time, this type of assistance is currently a non-priority area of existing humanitarian projects, which makes it difficult to meet the needs and leads to the actual exclusion of children from low-income families from the educational process in the context of the spread of COVID-19. Also, school institutions have a need (and in connection with distance education, this need has increased) for IT equipment for conducting distance learning sessions and operating schools. The need includes personal computers, printers, Internet modems, and tablets. The need to create an aggregator of existing online courses is due to the need to assist students and teachers who often have insufficient skills to search for information on the Internet due to age. The aggregator should be a platform that will contain links to existing educational materials and manuals, classified by grades and academic subjects. An aggregator of publicly available tools for distance education will improve the effectiveness of lessons and / or self-learning. It also helps to increase the inclusiveness of education, in particular in the case of popularization of popular science podcasts, it will facilitate the education of children with vision problems. The need for a co-working center equipped with computer and office equipment is due to the presence of high school students, secondary and higher professional educational institutions who need such equipment for their own professional orientation, search for educational grants and scholarships, and develop projects for participation in competitions. According to information from open sources, in 2019, 8120 people graduated from the 11th grade at NGCA in Donetsk oblast. Due to the difficult economic situation, some of them did not have access to specialists in vocational guidance and computer technology to independently plan their own path in the labor market and / or continue their education.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

As part of the project, the following stages are planned: 1) Identification the most in need of IT equipment schools2) Development of an aggregator of existing distance education tools, informing the local population of its existence, including during further distribution of tablets to beneficiaries. 3) Purchase and distribution of IT equipment (PC, printers, Internet modems, tablets etc) to 15 schools. 4) Organization of a co-working center (with the purchase of 10 computers and the involvement of a consultant in the field of professional orientation) based on the organization's oﬃce.

3 - Indicators & targets

Cluster objectives within HRP: 1) Ensure access for conflict-affected boys and girls to safe, inclusive, enabling learning environment as a basic essential service. Activity: Procurement and distribution of equipment to conflict-affected education facilities including equipment to support distance learning — will be achieved by distributing IT equipment to the most in need of it schools.2) Promote non-discriminatory opportunities for continuation of education for conflict affected boys and girls. Activity: Provide consultations for boys and girls from NGCA on transition from schools to the job market, vocational or higher education — will be achieved by creating a coworking center that provides computer and office equipment, as well as professional orientation services.Indicators:1. #3000 of girls and boys benefitting from provision of equipment to education facilities2. #300 of male and female children and youth provided with consultations Indirect beneficiaries of the project will be parents of children who have received access to distance education. The project also plans to create an aggregator of already developed and publicly available distance education tools. Within the framework of the aggregator's existence, it is planned to inform 10,000 people about these tools.
