Project Summary

With an increase of more than half a million people classified as IPC phase 3 and above , the food insecurity situation in Sudan continues to worsen in 2019 due to continued economic decline and natural shocks, resulting in the increased vulnerability of people. In 2020, it is estimated that 6.2 million individuals, representing 14.2 percent of the total population, will need urgent and timely lifesaving food and livelihoods security support. The highest prevalence of food insecurity is in the 6 states that for the first time have been classified as IPC phase 3 and above, including South Kordofan (40 percent prevalence), Red Sea (28 percent), North Darfur (24 percent), Central Darfur (22 percent), South and West Darfur (21 percent).
North Darfur, the target State of the project is one of the six State that classified as IPC 3 and above which are the highest prevalence of food insecurity.
The purpose of this project is to Improve and Sustain livelihoods and create income generation activities for most vulnerable returnees and host communities in three localities: Kutum, Mellit and Kalimendo – North Darfur.
According to FSL sector PiN based on IPC phase 3 and above. For all localities IPC June - August 2019 analysis was used included the above target project localities.
1 - The FSL targets for Kutum locality is 47,300 individual out of which the project targets 5,500 individual.
2 - The FSL targets for Mellit locality is 29,200 out of which the project targets 4,500 individual
3 - The FSL targets for Kalimendo locality is 13,200 out of which the project targets 4,500 individual.
The total targets of the project as direct beneficiaries = 14,500 individual (men 3,500, women 4,000 boys 3,500 and girls 3,500).
Sustainable Action Group (SAG) organization is a front line of humanitarian responder on the geographical area of North Darfur since 2005 through which the organization acquired good relationship and expert on coordination and collaboration with all humanitarian actors on the area.
In order to achieve the purpose mentioned SAG will:
a - Coordinate with State Ministry of Animal Resources to vaccinate and treat 100,000 head of livestock owned by 2,000 households benefit 10,000 individual.
b - SAG will intervene through cash based approach using conditional cash transfer to rehabilitate three Hafirs (water reservoir for dry season) by excavation and removal of soil and gravels from bottom of these hafirs result in increase carrying capacity.

1 - Needs

Based on the Comprehensive Food Security Assessment (CFSA) by WFP in November 2018, more than half of households from resident communities spend 74 percent of their expenditure on food, out of which more than half of that expense is done on cereal, meat and sugar. This is an indication of households’ vulnerability, leaving limited resources to other basic needs and services or to invest in livelihoods assets. Overall, in 2018 there was a 117 percent increase in households’ expenditure across all states compared to 2017, with the highest rate reported in East Darfur (265 percent). Approximately 58 percent of households cannot afford a local food basket due to limited purchasing power resulting from inflation and high food prices. The highest rates are reported in the Darfur States, which means that households’ have financial difficulties in accessing food, therefore likely resulting in poor food consumption. To cope with the inability to acquire enough food, around one third of resident households adopt food based coping strategies. This includes borrowing money to buy food, limiting portion sizes, reducing the number of meals and reducing adult consumption favoring smaller children, and eating less preferred food. The overall highest rates of negative coping strategies are reported in Blue Nile state (67 percent). The highest rates of severe coping mechanisms are adopted in Kassala (29 percent). Households’ resilience and future capacities remains a major concern. On average, some 54 percent of the households’ resort to spending their savings on food, cutting down on medical expenses and selling last remaining female animals causing an irreversible loss of livelihoods, with highest rates reported in Blue Nile, while Red Sea state reported highest proportion of households adopting emergency coping mechanisms. Women headed households are more vulnerable to food insecurity, with 41 percent of female-headed households being food insecure compared to 29 percent of male-headed householdsSAG and Plan Sudan conducted Rapid Needs Assessment on November 2018 covered three localities in North Darfur aimed to:1 - identify the areas and population affected2 - To assess the areas where people return3 - To identify the urgent and the future needs for the affected population4 - To assess the rehabilitation requirements for community assets. Sources of information:1 - WFP Market Monitor – August 2019 2 - Sudan Comprehensive Food Security Assessment (CFSA) – WFP November 2018 and Food Security Monitoring Systems (FSMS) – WFP November 2018 3 - Crop and Food Supply Assessment Mission to the Sudan – FAO March 2019 4 - Rapid Needs Assessment Conducted by SAG and Plan Sudan November 2018 covered three localities in North Darfur.From the needs assessments sources of information mentioned,the situation in North Darfur especially the three target localities requires an urgent innovative interventions through which the project will contribute to improve the situation.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Outcome of the project: Most vulnerable returnees and host communities in Kutum, Mellit and Kalimendo localities - North Darfur, their livelihoods assets improved and sustained and their ability to buy food increased.Output 1: 100,000 head of livestock owned by 2,000 households in Kutum, Mellit and Kalimendo localities - North Darfur vaccinated against infectious diseases and treated of parasitic worms. Activity 1.1: Conduct coordination meetings with Humanitarian Affairs Commissioner (HAC) to get permission of access to project areas.Activity 1.2: Conduct coordination meetings with State Ministry of Animal Resources for consultation and collaboration to prepare the vaccination campaigns.Activity 1.3: Conduct coordination meetings with State Ministry of Animal Resources to calculate the cost of 200,000 doses of vaccines and pay for the Ministry of Animal Resources to bring and preserve the vaccines.Activity 1.4: Collaborate with State Ministry of Animal Resources to prepare 6 vaccination and treatment campaigns.Activity 1.5: Procure from the private sector drugs for treatment of parasitic worms. Activity 1.6: Conduct the vaccination and treatment campaigns.Output 2: Most vulnerable 900 households of returnees and host communities in Kutum, Mellit and Kalimendo localities received cash and being able to buy food and other basic needsActivity 2.1: Conduct coordination meetings with Humanitarian Affairs Commissioner (HAC) to get permission of access to project areasActivity 2.2: Conduct coordination meetings with Ministry of Infrastructure for consultation and supervisionActivity 2.3: Conduct Community mobilization and meetings with Community leaders in the three localities to identify sites of three hafirs for rehabilitationActivity 2.4: Select 900 most vulnerable households, 300 households from each community using selection criteria stated by SAG in collaboration with community leaders.Activity 2.5: Prepare beneficiary performance list (format) to records the beneficiaries performance (excavation and removal of soil from bottom of hafirs).The measure unit of beneficiary performance is one cubic meter of soil excavated and removed from bottom of hafirs.The project will pay 5 dollars for each cubic meter of soil excavated and removed.The project aim to excavate 30,000 cubic meters of soil from three hafirs, from each hafir will excavate and remove 10,000 cubic meters accordingly we calculate the performance.Activity 2.6: Procure the digging equipment for excavation Activity 2.7: In collaboration with beneficiaries, assign beneficiary representatives to ensure the accurate records and calculation of beneficiary performance.Activity 2.8: Start excavation and removal of soil from the three hafirs at one time under close follow up and day to records of beneficiary performance using the beneficiary list (format).
