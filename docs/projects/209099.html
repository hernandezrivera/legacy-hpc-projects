Project Summary

The project aims to minimize the impacts of humanitarian emergencies in WASH sector resulted from the armed conflict in targeted localities for affected population both IDPs and host communities through improve access to quality WASH services to people in need specifically vulnerable one like PWD, IDPs, and Host communities in White Nile, North Kurdufan, Gadaref and Kassala. The project will be strengthening provision of quality WASH services across the 4 targeted programme areas: White Nile (Ad Diwaim, Aj Jabalain, As Salam / Ar Rawat, Guli, Kosti, Rabak, Tendalti, Um Rimta), North Kurdufan (Sheikan), Kassala (Madeinat Kassala, Reifi Aroma, Reifi Gharb Kassala, Halfa Aj Jadeedah, Reifi Nahr Atbara, Reifi Shamal Ad Delta, Reifi Wad Elhilaiw, Gedaref (Al Mafaza, Al Galabat Al Gharbyah - Kassab, Gala'a Al Nahal, Madeinat Al Gedaref, Al Qureisha).
The project link to Humanitarian Response Plan 2024 Strategic Objective 1: Provide safe, timely, principled and gender-responsive life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable groups to reduce mortality and morbidity in areas with high severity of need or at risk of a further deterioration of critical humanitarian needs. And to Strategic objective 3: Provide safe, equitable, dignified and unhindered access to critical basic services and livelihoods opportunities for the most vulnerable groups to prevent further erosion of their coping abilities. This project is contributing to all three sector objectives for crisis-affected population to access basic water services, adequate, protected, and gender-sensitive sanitation services, and to hygiene promotion and other environmental health interventions.
The selected activities aim at improve the access of crisis-affected population to gender and climate change-sensitive basic water services, adequate provision of gender-sensitive sanitation services and to ensure that affected people have access to gender-sensitive hygiene services, risk communication and community engagement (RCCE), menstrual hygiene management (MHM), vector control, and waste management.
Life-saving interventions will be scaled up in the immediate term, water trucking will be provided in the areas where the water supply is disrupted including health facilities , and establishment of distribution points, including gathering and transit points. Essential WASH supplies and NFI emergency sanitation and handwashing facilities will be provided in hotspot locations. Hygiene promotion and household water treatment practices will be promoted as an immediate alternative, especially for those reliant on surface/river water. Plan will integrate water, sanitation and hygiene interventions in this project, with education, nutrition and health activities to contribute to reducing malnutrition and epidemics.
The project team will also ensure coordination with other interventions. The project will deliver these activities through community services adopting the sector objectives and approaches, besides integrating some of the WASH interventions into health and nutrition centers to ensure the minimum IPC is applied.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project intends to ensure equal and safe participation of target beneficiaries,particularly girls and women, who are most at risk of being exposed to protectionviolations. Plan International will ensure this through: - Involving women, men, boysand girls in design, identification of latrine/water points location, hygiene kit contents,etc. - Involving women in setting priorities and making decisions about water supplyprograms - Consulting women on how water is collected, and ensure water supply isalso accessible to women with limited mobility - Providing containers for watercollection and storage - Providing separate latrines facilities for girls, boys, and women- Ensuring facilities are positioned in safe places to ensure the privacy and security ofwomen and girls, as identified by beneficiaries - Ensuring women’s leadership andparticipation e.g. in WASH committees for successful programming
