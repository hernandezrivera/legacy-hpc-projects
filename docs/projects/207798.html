Project Summary

Through an integrated protection approach implemented in the 4 divisions of the Southwest region: Fako, Meme, Ndian and Kupe-Manenguba, DRC is looking to respond to and reduce the 3 main protection risks identified through protection analysis: arbitrary arrests, child labor, and Gender-Based Violence.
The project will work with a local partner to implement activities in the Southwest region across the sectors of protection including child protection and GBV, shelter/NFI and Multi-purpose cash assistance.
A coordination component will also support: the co-facilitation of the protection cluster and provide funding and capacity building to the platform coordinating local civil society organizations (CHOI)
