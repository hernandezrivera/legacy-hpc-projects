Project Summary

The project will be accountable to the affected population , conflict and gender sensitive protocols by observing all aspects of the project programming . In this case, 23% adult male 17% adult female , 60 % of school going age children including children with disability who are above 10 years. The old age persons will also be considered in the recruitment during construction of Temporary learning spaces as being the primary beneficiaries fulfilling the requirement for the planned project targets for each location People with great vulnerability for example children with disability shall be considered in the selection process .Returnees, IDPs and host communities shall be given priority in recruitment process in the project . Children boys and girls from marginalized groups shall be given priority to receive learning opportunities. The project shall identify the project beneficiaries based on gender age and disability consideration to serve a positive role model for women, men , girls and boys in the communities selected to ensure that even the staff recruited will understand how to articulate the gender transformative goals of the project . In this respect, the staff recruitment positions shall be identified to recruit the right staff that have the capacity to deliver based on the procedures of the project activities. This will be key factor to undertake the first quarter of the project. Channels of hope gender and social analysis and action (SAA) are key models on which the project shall be built on , and in this case it requires face to face interactions to ensure that the facilitation skills are obtained from the recruited project staff. There is vast majority of needs driven by the underlying education system where many challenges are faced by , women men, girls and boys in the area where the project targets and are based on the immediate education needs prescribed in this project .There is also a need to enhance the understanding of the underlying needs and cross cutting protection challenges by seeking durable solutions provided to beneficiaries. All project staff shall receive training or refresher training on protocols for disclosure and referral mechanism before the commencement of the project. The project will connect children to learning environment , parent, teachers and persons with disability who will be connected to livelihood interventions through cash- basis to provide social support to improve schools through renovation and repair of damaged learning environments to increase their resilience by improving support systems for children to access quality education . The project shall cater for education needs of the refugees, returnees, internally displaced persons especially training teachers on pedagogy and code of conduct. This shall involve vulnerable and marginalized host communities to have coherent humanitarian education needs addressed which really continues to put young population in the country in a dire situation. As the influx of returnees continues to increase due to relevant peace acquired in the Country, the complexity and breadth of response requires a more nuanced and rigorous intervention approach to better understand the strategy to be used to resolve the huge educations needs in the project locations selected. WAO has a field office in Wau and shall monitor the activities in Tonj South -Warrap state through making field visits but in the long run establish the office with recruited staff that shall be able to collect 5Ws information regarding the project. The information regarding 5Ws shall be incorporated with the 5Ws information for both Wau and Jur River Counties and submitted to Education cluster for further analysis.

1 - Needs

Since the conflict, at least 1.7 million Children, adolescents and those with dis abilities are in need of emergency education, including 2.8 million children boys and girls including those with disabilities have dropped out of School. Many of them are unable to access learning due to displacement caused by the conflict. Children and adolescents youth in Wau, Jur river and Tonj South Counties remain outside of education are susceptible to dangerous labor practices , recruitment into armed groups and other negative coping mechanisms such as joining gangs and crime, substance abuse and gender based violence. Additionally, gross enrolment decreased by approximately 7 per cent between 2020 (2,727,209) and 2021 (2,523,204) boys and girls with or without disabilities due to COVID 19 outbreak In Wau, Jur river and Tonj South Counties. The limited provision of education opportunities due to insecurity and flooding phenomena have led to increased cost of operation in Schools. In Tonj South County this has led to early and forced marriages and other likely early pregnancies widely reported as reasons for drop-out especially in Wau, Jur River and Tonj South Counties. During school closures, evidence suggests that, daughters were married off to alleviate household economic stress through a “bride price” payment. Hygiene and Sanitation has been compromised in schools , when the Schools reopened, Adolescent girls recorded absenteeism due experienced menstrual periods happening in the absence of dignity kits and lack of Sanitary pads for menstrual hygiene management managing (MHM) in the project locations .Most schools in Tonj South, Jur river and Wau counties lack assistive or mobility devices for children with Disabilities, and inadequate nutrition leading to poor academic performance but instead . Wau County has been relatively haven from violence that engulfed much of the rest of the Country in the past years of the war .Wau in general has been severely impacted by the conflict the effect of it continue to be felt among the civilians. War has inflamed the preexisting ethnic tensions between Wau’s three groups , the Dinka, the Feritit and the Luo weakening the prospects for cosmopolitanism and peaceful coexistence that the area may have once provided. Due to ethnic conflict many schools have been destroyed and damaged some occupied by armed groups . Distance to school, inadequate learning spaces, lack of school supplies and untrained teachers are other barriers to learners accessing quality education in Tonj South, Jur River and Wau . (WAO) will in this case target boys and girls of school going age including pre-school children , adolescents and young people, as well as parents , teachers and other relevant community members in Tonj-South ,Wau and Jur River Counties in Western Bahrel Ghazal and Warrap States . Through this project intervention, will contribute to all three objectives of the strategic response plan1.Improve equitable access to safe and protective education services of IDPs returnees , spontaneous refugees and host community children including those with disabilities and youth boys and girls. 2Improve the quality of gender and disability inclusive education service and teacher capacity to protect the wellbeing of all children and prevent the negative impact of emergencies on learning environment 3Strengthen the education system response capacity of school, community and national / local education actors to mitigate the impact of existing emergencies on learners, teachers and the community.

The community need cash for providing their services during construction of the temporary learning spaces TLS since the cash shall help them procure essential needs to complement their living eg procurement of food and other non-food items . 5.1.2. Explain how the project design considered market functionality

Through this project causal analysis shall be developed for the communities in each county to identify the market behavior and their functionality . The markets in all counties have both demand and price information of the essential commodities including the food items. Farmers and other groups of people have market requirement and have viable information and right communication channels establishing to create contractual relations at different market spheres. In all the target counties, there are customers and suppliers who share demand forecast of varied commodities and market prices with strong production plans. In all the project locations, there will be established and improved advanced market sales systems established to boost their functionality Materials for constructing temporary learning environment I spaces shall be procured from the market established in the selected locations and such they shall be enhancing the market functionality in the project locations. However, the prices of the required commodities shall vary from market to market due to remoteness and distance for transporting such items those items required to improve the learning conditions of the children affected by conflict.

The project will provide psychosocial support training (PSS) to teachers , parents and students to mitigate trauma , stress and fatigue . The education cluster shall work jointly with child protection Sub cluster collaboration with GBV sub cluster to ensure safe learning environment is achieved in the project locations mentioned . Cases of gender based violence shall be handled collectively by the local authorities , school management committees and the GBV humanitarian actors to reduce GBV, child abuse and other forms of discrimination . Safety measures shall be put in place through collaborative efforts to ensure that GBV-survivors and juvenile survivors are effectively coordinated to get required services through referral mechanism established in the project locations . Advocacy networks shall be consulted from time to time to scale up their advocacy strategies of reducing GBV and enhancement of gender equality and equity in the communities this project will target. Case management will be handled in the correlated manner with reference to established case management networks and advocacy groups . By conducting routine Safety Perception mapping in the project locations , women , men and male and female youth shall identify areas where and when they feel safe or at risk and potential flashpoints which may include conflict and GBV. The project will ensure that protection is mainstreamed, based on the results of the Safety Perception mapping. The project will also build on several years of previous Do No Harm analysis, peacebuilding and activities to provide alternatives to violent issues durable solutions to disagreements. The school environment will also contribute to mediation efforts led by local leaders and school management committees where feasible and appropriate. The baseline assessment community gender analysis ( CGA) and feedback from participants shall confirms that children and youth, in particular young men and women , are often pulled into localized conflict. The project will work with existing and new youth groups promoting gender equality and peaceful coexistence in resolving conflict issues . Youth will also be supported to coordinate and facilitate activities to build and promote peace, including art, sports and cultural events, and radio programming during inter school competitions in the project locations

12.1.3. Collective Outcome 3 - Flood related displacement is 30% lower in 2025 than in 2021.

The community , school and other learning institutions in collaboration with the project staff shall conduct training on disaster risk reduction and management . In Tonj South the terrain is flat where the dyke construction will be ideal to leverage the efforts of the disaster risk reduction committees formed to respond to flood phenomenon . Dyke construction is therefore, one of the community activity that will take most of the time educating them how to control the floods and to have early warning signs of flooding so that community members are tuned to emergency preparedness mood. This project shall also emphasize Climate Smart agriculture to be practiced in the community being part of the action point to improving the environmental. School gardening including vegetable cultivation both in rural and urban settings shall be encouraged , efficient stoves as a source of energy shall be encouraged to be used by the Schools implementing the school feeding programme in the locations where this project shall be implemented supported by WFP so that firewood is less used which involves massive clearing and felling of trees and bushes thus making the environment flood prone with thick soil erosion . The above activities if implemented well shall reduce flood related population displacement in the project located areas. The project will incorporate practices to help female and male farmers sustainably manage natural resources. For example, they will test and learn about the use of organic farming practiced in urban settings . After mapping degraded areas, the project shall determine suitable locations where durable solutions shall be tested , members of farmers groups will learn how to use climate-smart agricultural practices, such as farmer Managed Natural Regeneration, to improve production, restore soil fertility, create fodder for cattle, create income opportunities for women through the restoration of fruit trees and other material, all while maximizing sustainability of South Sudan’s valuable natural resources. The school children, farmers and the community at large will extend existing early warning systems, increase accessibility specifically for women farmers, and will invite schools to contribute their own information using simple meteorological tools to disseminate when and how to prepare for adverse events. In consultation with County Agricultural Departments and Meteorological Department, the specific make of tools (thermometers, barometers, rain gauges, etc.) shall be identified for procurement. Agriculture extension workers and Community-based Disaster Risk Management Committee members will be trained to use the tools and share the information with the coordination mechanism team and farmers. Female and male extension workers will be the key agents of information dissemination of meteorological ideas to Schools and farmers face-to-face during field support and training sessions.
