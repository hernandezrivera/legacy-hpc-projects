1 - Besoins / Needs

The project will benefit nearly 5,000 people by providing them with:- Basic food for a healthy and balanced diet,- Arable land and space for agriculture and animal husbandry,- Agricultural equipment (inputs, seeds, hoes, machetes, etc.),- suitable drinking water points,- Medical consultations and medicines for people suffering from pathologist,- A listening and guidance center.Advocacy will also be carried out with community leaders, traditional, religious and administrative authorities for better supervision / monitoring / support of these fragile people so that they will not longer just be spectators but, become real actors of community development, inclusive and participatory. The expected results at the end of this project are:- Full empowerment of beneficiaries,- Emotional and psychological equilibrium,- Better integration within the host population,- The establishment of a health system adapted to needs. This will include making the beneficiaries development actors ready to face the future with confidence, regardless of their current or future place of residence.
