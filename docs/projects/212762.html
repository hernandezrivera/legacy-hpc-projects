Project Summary

An area in northern Hama governorate, Syria (Muhradah countryside) has been selected for the establishment of a project to set up a vocational training center and distribute vocational kits, due to the lack of qualified centers in the region and the need of its residents for such projects. The project aims to provide training and qualification opportunities for youth and job seekers (500 beneficiaries ) in various fields, including manufacturing, construction, agriculture, and services. The center will include training rooms equipped with the latest technologies and devices, as well as a library containing books and educational resources in all fields. Short-term training courses will be offered under the supervision of experts and professionals in each field, with the goal of enhancing trainees' skills and increasing their chances of obtaining better jobs. Vocational kits containing necessary tools for each field will be distributed to ensure that they have everything they need to start their professional lives. Periodic recruitment events will also be held for local residents by project partners.
The project aims to enhance social cohesion and improve relationships between villages and regions that have experienced previous conflicts , especially after the return of displaced populations.
It will also contribute to revitalizing market activity and enhancing livelihood opportunity for various social groups.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The northern rural area of Hama (including the city of Maharda, Halfaya, Karnaz, Kafr Zita, Tremseh, and surrounding villages) is an area that heavily relies on agriculture and agricultural industries. These areas have suffered greatly from the effects of war (repeated displacement, infrastructure destruction, significant losses in private property, loss of a whole generation of youth either as war victims or migrants, a significant increase in the number of disabled individuals, many women becoming breadwinners, a large number of children dropping out of school due to the need to work, and the absence of essential professions). Additionally, the region has been affected by the aftermath of earthquakes causing significant damage to properties. The economic and living conditions in the country as a whole are also challenging with poverty, high cost of living, and shortages in electricity and fuel. The Maharda Charitable Organization (MCO) is a developmental organization capable to access all parts of the region with volunteer teams in various cities. After conducting a needs assessment study (The study was conducted in November 2023 and documented with the Relief Commission in Hama Governorate) and collaborating with relevant sectors (livelihoods), social affairs department in Hama , Crisis Management Unit in Hama Governorate And the local community, revealed that there are no specialized vocational training centers or technical institutes in the entire region. Therefore, the Maharda Charitable Organization (MCO)is proposing this project: "Establishing a training center with vocational workshops and distributing professional kits to some needy individuals who meet targeting criteria." The project will focus on female-oriented professions needed in the job market as well as skills relevant to youth employment. Training will also include marketing and advertising skills to enable trainees to enter the job market confidently. The ultimate impact will be supporting the local market with skilled women and youth workforce, Creating job opportunities, while reducing youth migration and improving living conditions for needy families. This initiative aligns closely with Syria's financing strategy (third item) to increase resilience and access to services.The project will also work on enhancing social cohesion through two community initiatives carried out by the trainees within the center.
