Project Summary

The project aims at improving the living conditions of displacement affected communities through the implementation of a full package of camp management services to ensure access to assistance and protection. The project will be implemented in close coordination with the displaced and host communities, camp administration and the CCCM Cluster.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

ACTED will ensure that a full package of camp management services is in place in the targeted sites/urban out of camps displacement areas to improve access to service and protection for conflict affected communities. All the activities proposed will be conducted in close coordination with the displaced and host communities, camp administration and other local authorities, the CCCM cluster and relevant clusters and humanitarian and development partners working in the target areas. Sites targeting in the prioritized districts will be defined in coordination with the CCCM Cluster in order to prioritized the most in needs sites and maximize coordination. Overall, this project will benefit an estimated population of 63,224 conflict affected individuals (estimated 11481 Adult males; 18746 adult females; 32997 children) currently living in the targeted sites/areas or to be relocated in coordination with Camp Administration authorities, the CCCM Cluster and UNHCR. This program will also fill critical information gaps for actors working across the districts prioritized.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

ACTED will implement a comprehensive package of camp management activities: mapping, setting up community participation and communication with communities mechanisms, including complaints and feedback mechanisms; supporting intra and inter sites coordination though service monitoring, information management activities and strong ties with the CCCM cluster, contributing to avoid duplications, report gaps and increase efficiency of the existing resources; prioritizing small-scale site improvement works focusing on rainy seasons preparedness and response and with the primary focus to improve safety, security and access to services for all the residents. These activities will be complemented by capacity development activities that will target Local Government and partners in the displacement response, contributing to strengthen coordination, clarify roles and responsibilities, increase accountability and support a locally driven response. The Camp Management Teams will also support relocation movements between sites, coordinating with all relevant stakeholders involved in the process and ensuring that relocations happen in a dignified, informed and participatory manner and in line with the provision of the Camp Management Minimum Standards. ACTED CCCM support will be implemented through a mix of methodologies including:• traditional (or “static”) camp management in the biggest/most populated displacement sites • mobile camp management through mobile camp management teams that will be responsible to implement activities through a roving approach in small-medium size displacement sites• Out of Camp/Udoc approach for out of camp displaced populations living out of camps-dispersed in host communities. Activities will be implemented through Community Coordination Centres, complemented by outreach interventions.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection principles and gender will be mainstreamed in all the project activities and throughout the project management cycle and special attention will be given to guarantee: • Prioritizing safety, dignity and avoiding causing harm thanks through regular safety audits that will assess specific risks and vulnerabilities existing in the sites as well as gender dynamics. The Safte Audits will provide practical actions to be taken to reduce safety and security risks, including gender based violence related risks. Consulting women, girls, men and boys will ensure that all groups will have a voice in determining their living spaces and the services provided to them, leading to reduction of stigma, acceptance and respect of all people in the camp and therefore increasing social cohesion. •Meaningful access to services in proportion with vulnerabilities and needs, throughinclusive service monitoring and information management activities will incorporate an age, gender and diversity lens in data collection. Self-reliance will be promoted ensuring that all groups have equal and appropriate opportunities to building their resilience and agency for recovery. •Accountability to affected population through communication with communities activities and complaints and feedbacks collection and referrals that will reflect different preferences of communication channels as expressed by all age and gender groups•Participation and community empowerment through the set up of inclusive community participation mechanisms able to represent marginalized and vulnerable portion of the population. Promoting participation of both women and men as leaders in service provision will strengthen ownership and challenge gender inequality

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As per the latest DTM Baseline Assessment the number of IDPs living with disabilities is 808 individuals. However it should be noted due to the baseline methodology, where data are collected primarily through Key Informant Interviews, the number of persons with disability might have been underestimated at site pr area level. For this reason, ACTED will incorporate the Washington Group Short Set Questions in every household level data collection exercise that will be implemented in order to provide a more reliable number of people living with disabilities and their needs. Moreover, through strong community participation mechansims, ACTED will esure adequate representation of people living with disabilities into camp governancre structures and accountability toward affected population mechanisms. Specific attention will be given to guarantee meaningful access to services for people living with disabilities through monitoring of service provision and implementation of specific site maintenance and improvements activities that will make the site environment more safe and accessible for everyone. In its action ACTED will be guided by the Disability Inclusion Checklist included in the Camp Management Standards.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Whenever possible, ACTED will implement the NEAT+ tool in the site targeted by the intervention (either at site selection stage or at the start of the intervention) ensuring that environmental risk are properly assessed and that mitigation actions are referred and coordinated with other camp actors and key stakeholders as needed. Additionally, ACTED will include some specific environmental indicators in its site monitoring tool, thus mainstreaming environmental considerations throughout the camp life cycle. When needed, based on specific environmental risks, ACTED will work with other camp actors and the affected communities to develop a Community-based Environmental Management Plan. In mainstreaming environmental considerations, ACTED will refer to the checklist in the environment chapter of the CCCM Toolkit.
