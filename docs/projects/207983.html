Project Summary

This project will complement ongoing efforts by IOM’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) to support partners with data for Central and Southern Mozambique Humanitarian, Development and Peacebuilding (HDP) approaches aimed at monitoring displacement, reducing overall vulnerabilities, root causes of fragility, strengthen risk management capacities and respond to critical needs.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

OM proposes to collect primary data from Host Communities, IDP and Returnee populations on mobility trends, livelihoods, access to services, stability as well as social cohesion to statistically analyse and report on Displacement and Resiliency in Central and Southern Mozambique

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. All disaster affected districts of Nampula, Niassa, Tete, Zambezia, Manica, Sofala, Gaza and Inhambane -Participatory review and training of DTM methodology and tools monitoring displacement, perceptions and solutions over time 2. All disaster affected districts of Nampula, Niassa, Tete, Zambezia, Manica, Sofala, Gaza and Inhambane - Deployment of Area-based DTM Tools (Displacement and Solutions) 3. All disaster affected districts of Nampula, Niassa, Tete, Zambezia, Manica, Sofala, Gaza and Inhambane- Dissemination of Displacement Trends, Humanitarian Needs, Recommendations on conditions for Resiliency

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Training of all DTM Field Teams on data collection techniques, methodologies, introducing IOM’s data protection, general protection, disability, and inclusion as well as PSEA standards in data collection efforts for local authorities, partners and IOM staff.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Direct data collection and data management that is gender responsive and is inclusive of sex and age disaggregated data, population needs vulnerabilities

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disaggregated data collection and analysis that includes identification of persons with disabilities in process of monitoring conditions and needs of affected populations
