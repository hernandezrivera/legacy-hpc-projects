Project Summary

This project is designed to provide integrated intervention in education and protection and
increase safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and nonformal education within two years, 2022 Binaa aims to target (7200) crisis-affected children and youth (3593 girls, 3607 boys including 258 PwDs ) in 4 schools in Ma'arrat Tamasrin and Aghtrin community) in addition to 6 schools in 8 targeted camps including 4 new established camps as follow: (2 camps in Haranbush,1 camp in Alshekh Bahr/Ma'arrat Tamasrin),(1 camp in bhorta in Aghtrin) and (1 camp in Bazagha) addition to Yasin camp and Omran Camp which were identified by Binaa rapid need assessment as the nearby targeted camp in Harnabush with no access to education services which can be provided with education services through the school in Harbabush camp, as well as two camps Besnaya and Alqima camps which Binaa has 2 established schools there but they have no secured support for the ongoing academic year.
Through support the provision of formal education to 4359 children, and
Provide 2841 children with non-formal education programs including remedial classes, Accelerated Learning Programs (ALP), catching up classes, literacy and numeracy classes. Conduct 4 Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns in targeted communities.
Disinfection of 10 schools and learning spaces, where the schools will be held in two shifts , taking into account surfaces disinfection between shifts as Covid-19 mitigation measures, in addition, that Providing personal protective equipment (PEE) to (students,
teachers and education personnel)to mitigate the risk of Covid-19.
Establishment, and rehabilitation of 100 classrooms. Providing 5 schools with school furniture, including desks, chairs, blackboards. Improve and construct gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive WASH facilities for 10 schools to provide an adequate environment for
7200 children. Providing 10 schools with safety and security equipment and systems, Providing maintenance and running costs for 10 schools. Providing 153 teachers with teaching materials in formal and non-formal settings. Providing 600 children with school transportation support (for students who are in nearby camps and CwDs).
Provide 350 children with school-based psychosocial support and 300 children with life skills programs
The project aims to enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth through providing professional development to 181 teaching staff and education
personnel, including psychosocial support, subject knowledge, pedagogical skills, classroom management, positive discipline, life skills, technical and vocational training, life skills and citizenship education, remote teaching pedagogy, education in emergencies (EiE), national/INEE (Inter-Agency Network for Education in Emergencies) minimum standards, Covid-19 related knowledge, codes of conduct and anti-bullying, mine risk education,
safety/security protocols. Provide teachers and education personnel with incentives and teaching resources.
Binaa will take into consideration all precautions and procedures related to the protection
from COVID-19 epidemic following the clusters guidelines and instructions.
In 2023, Binaa aims in its strategy to increase the number of targeted schools to reach 10,000 children and youth.

4 - Needs

Over the con§ict years in Syria, an extremely adverse impact affected the health, social, service, and education of the whole society’s aspects. According to the HNO 2022estimated that there are over 6,609,775 million people in need of education support,18per cent (3-17 years old) are out of school, and half of the children are not in school (30per cent), Idleb (28per cent), Aleppo (26per cent). Due to the cumulative effect of the conflict, the closure of learning facilities, displaced communities remain significantly underserved, with most IDP camps having insufficient or non-existent education services, so as an expected result, children are dropping out of school. Since the academic year started recently and to attain the noble goal of providing children in Syria with equal opportunity to children around the world for their right to get an education, our assistance is must. The intervention model aims to enhance and ensure access to formal education in 6 schools in camps and 4 schools in hostcommunities and improve access to education in 8 camps. During the design of this project, BINAA conducted a rapid needs assessment 10 schools in targeted communities are not supported for the 2022-2023 academic year. As per assessment results, there are7200 students(3607 boys,3593girl) there is a huge gap insupport for the education sector as (no education facilities in the camps, lack of improvement of WASH facilities to be more gender-sensitive and more prepared againstCOVID 19.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk-01: Teachers or education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. the likelihood is medium and the impact is high, Mitigating Measure: Binaa will ensure an enforced code of conduct, and train the teachers and school personnel on prevention and reporting of violence, physical punishment, and bullying. sensitize the students and their caregivers on the Code of Conduct and related prevention, identification, reporting systems.Risk-02: GBV, sexual abuse or facilities are not disability-sensitive in the rehabilitated schools’ facilities. the likelihood is low and the impact is high. This risk will be monitored basically by Binaa M&E team. Referral path system and Feedback and complaint mechanism are adopted to contribute to solving the issues.Risk-03:Violence against education facilities and education providers Protection: the likelihood is medium and the impact is high. Mitigating Measure:the risk will be monitored by Binaa field staff in addition to the report received from the security department. all works will be stalled in the low-security locations and being prepared to apply either the contingency or exit plan,reporting Mechanism (MRM).Risk-04:Insuficiently safe learning facilities, and unable maintain Covid-19 safety measures. the likelihood is low and the impact is high. Mitigating Measure: Binaa will provide the learning facilities with Covid-19 safety protocols (prevention measures, awareness raising, referral).
