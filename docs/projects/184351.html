Project Summary

The project aims providing critical life-saving WASH services, enhance access to sustainable WASH services and infrastructures and further strengthen resilience of the most vulnerable 12500 persons (5200 women, 4800 men,1250 boys and 1250 girls) in South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts, Mudug Region, Somalia by supporting immediate as well as long term WASH needs for women, men, boys and girls as well as those with disabilities and the elderly from rural villages and IDP settlements . The targeted communities and IDPs are yet to recover from impacts of conflict and insecurity as well as cumulative impacts of past seasonal flooding and below average rains Deyr 2020, Gu 2021 and past successive drought seasons that significantly compromised existing limited WASH infrastructures and services and exposed the vulnerable populations to dangers of outbreak of AWD/cholera and other sanitation and hygiene related diseases. Furthermore, some of the existing Water sources and sanitation facilities are dilapidated huge gaps and needs by the affected communities.
Current weather forecast shows likelihood of below average Deyr 2021 rains and greater risk of severe drought till mid 2022. (FSNAU-FEWS NET 2021 Post Gu Technical Release of September 9, 2021). The above plight calls for urgent need for implementation of emergency Water services to save lives of the affected communities and IDPs while putting in place sustainable WASH services and infrastructure in the targeted project locations in South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts, Mudug Region, Somalia. The project will be implemented in an integrated approach with health, Nutrition, education and protection partners in with comprehensive support from Galmudug Administration.

1. - Projects activities

1. Rehabilitation of 4 boreholes in Wisil, Jilable Hayre, Hilmo and Toorage 2. Upgrade of 5 shallow wells in Xerodhagaxley Village 3. Training of 50 water committee members (men and women) 4. Water Distribution by Voucher for 5 weeks in 15 drought affected remote rural villages in Hobyo andSouth Galkayo District 5. Construction of 50 gender sensitive(for men and women) new latrines fitted with hand washing facilities and lockable doors and are lighted at night in vulnerable IDP camps in South Galkayo. 6. Conducting CLTS in remote rural villages South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts with very limited sanitation facilities 7. Distribution of 1000 Hygiene kits in South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts to villages with cases ofAWD/Cholera and other water borne diseases 8. Conducting comprehensive hygiene promotion in targeted remote rural villages prone to AWD/Cholera outbreak and other water borne diseases as well as in schools, nutrition and health centers in the project locations in villages and IDP camps through house house visits, demonstration, media as well usingIEC materials translated in Somali Language for the targeted beneficiaries 9. 4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Supporting and ensuring comprehensive inclusion of minority clans/ethnic groups in the community consultations right from the commencement of the project at the needs identification stage and assessments and ensuring they are part of key decision made regarding the project and that their needs are incorporated in to the project2. Ensuring protection of women and girls from sexual violence by positioning WASH facilities at acceptable distance in line with WASH minimum standards to limit exposing them to cover very long distance beyond the safety of their settlements that exposing them to Sexual violence as well as spacing the WASH infrastructure to limit time they spend there in order to avoid reaching their homes under the cover of darkness. In addition, designing latrines as per WASH minimum standards to protect them from risk of Sexual violence with good structures that supports their privacy with lockable doors and lighted at night besides having separate latrines from women and men. Secondly, putting into practice the use of PSEA policy and ensuring women and girls are fully aware of the project and their rightful entitlements as per Hygiene kits distribution and other WASH services to mitigate sexual exploitation and abuse. In addition, ensuring that complaints and feedback mechanisms are rightfully in place such as suggestion boxes and toll free hotlines.
