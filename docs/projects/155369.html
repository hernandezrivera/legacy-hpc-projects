Needs

Need to restore and improve the availability of water in areas that are most affected by El Nino. There is need to strengthen the relationship /link between WASH, health, livelihoods and resilience by harnessing water sources that have proven to be high yielding to serve more people as well as drilling additional water points.. Need for water sources which are inclusive i.e accessible to people with disability, elderly and most vulnerable. Promote household point of use water treatment. The distribution of hygiene enabling NFIs and hygiene education pertaining to safe and hygienic child care and feeding practices to prevent occurrence and recurrence of diarrhea & other water related diseases. Need to strengthen community based structures to ensure sustainability.

Activities

Restoring ,improving and upgrading water supply (Drilling and equipping of 20 boreholes, Rehabilitation of 150 Boreholes Solar mechanization of 15 highly productive boreholes, water quality testing 170 boreholes, Rehabilitation of 5 pumps at the ZINWA water supply station in Gokwe South urban and support the District Hospital to have a stand alone sustainable water supply and storage facility. Support 1000 HH with point of use water treatment technologies. Hygiene Promotion with a focus on safe hygiene and sanitation practices, participatory health and hygiene education and water conservation (Training of EHTs/Extension workers on PHHE life saving messages, Training of VHWs on PHHE life saving messages, Dissemination of critical hygiene messages, IEC material development & distribution, Dissemination of messages focused on sanitation improvement, household water treatment and water conservation, Monitoring diarrheal diseases, PHHE sessions in institutions (schools and clinics) Distribution of NFIs to 20,000 Households, including logistics and monitoring.
