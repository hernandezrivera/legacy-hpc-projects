Project Summary

World Vision in Lebanon will target the most vulnerable Lebanese, Palestinians and Migrants girls and boys and their caregivers under ERP with essential Child Protection (CP) and psychosocial interventions including Gender Based Violence (GBV) awareness raising. The current situation in Lebanon has further exacerbated the compounding CP needs and risks of violence, abuse, neglect and exploitation, child labor, family separation and unsafe migration, GBV and Mental Health Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) concerns among vulnerable populations. Moreover, the socio-economic crisis, uncertainty regarding the future and breakdown of essential basic services in the country are taking a toll on people living in Lebanon, especially persons with specific needs including children and adults living with a disability. WVL will leverage its presence in the areas and its long-standing partnerships in the communities and utilize previously implemented outreach strategies that have proven effective in reaching the Most Vulnerable children and youth among targeted population through local partners, door-to-door, media including social media and phone calls. The selection criteria for children will be based on vulnerability/risk factors studied and set by WVL’s CP technical team as highlighted hereafter:
- Children identified through local community assessments with trained local partners.
- Experiencing abuse in any form (physical, emotional, sexual, exploitation, neglect and other forms of violence)
- Children living or working on the street
- Children involved in labor affecting their school attendance and / or health and moral development
- Trafficked children
- Children in early marriage
- Children living in child-headed households
- Children whose household is designated below poverty line
- Children deprived of parental care, because of their parents’ destructive behavior (alcoholism or drug dependency etc.)
- Children separated from parents or regular caregiver due to abandonment, imprisonment, trafficking or migration
- Children living with parent or caregiver with chronic illness in a household designated as below poverty line or in extreme poverty
- Children living with older adults or caregivers
- Children living in contested / conflict prone areas
- Children left out of school because of disability
- Children living in isolated locations far from neighbors
- Children with disabilities living in deprived or underprivileged communities
- Out-of-school children / school drop-outs
- Children living with parents from marginalized groups (imprisoned, HIV-positive, ethnic minorities, sex workers…)
- Children with no ID

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

WVL and partners will provide FPSS services to 300 children in the intervention areas with a CP sector curriculum consisting of 15 sessions either face-to-face or remote through an online FPSS platform as a mitigation measure. Partners will be trained, supervised and coached by the technical team throughout project cycle phases to ensure quality. WVL and partners will strengthen CP community-based networks through the provision of CBPSS, Caregivers Support Program and Caregivers FPSS services. As such, 2000 children under CBPSS programming will cover topics on personal and interpersonal skills, CP and risks prevention, Child Rights, Story reading from a WVL developed comic book called “Gazing Ahead” including recreational activities. In addition, 500 Caregivers under WVL’s Responsive Caregivers Toolkit will cover sessions on managing stress, anger and other challenges, mitigating familial conflict, child and adolescent development, positive parenting, CP GBV and risk prevention and arts to enhance self-expression and psychosocial well-being. Moreover, 100 caregivers showing medium to high risk levels of psychosocial distress will be registered in the sector based FPSS program, covering 8 topics where they learn new skills to be resilient in the face of new shocks. Finally, WVL and partners will support the implementation of 10 community-led CP initiatives on mitigation of CP risks where the community will design, plan, implement and evaluate the initiatives.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

WVL will use a participatory method to ensure equal access for all and will ensure gender-responsive learning environments and training materials. The project will take into consideration identified barriers that create obstacles for boys, girls, men and women when planning to participate and benefit from the sessions. Furthermore, male and female facilitators will be conducting sessions while gender disaggregated sessions will be conducted as needed. Gender-responsive questions will be incorporated into the routine project monitoring and evaluation disaggregated and analyzed by sex and age to understand the benefits of the services and remaining challenges for both genders.WVL supports the inclusion of children with disabilities in its programs. The inclusion process focuses on lessening the barriers to disability by identifying CWDs using WGQ, raising awareness on the community level to create more understanding and acceptance while tailoring activities whenever needed.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

World Vision Lebanon will guarantee the presence of a functional and accessible Accountability system to enhance beneficiary and community participation as well as ownership of project design and implementation. Targeted beneficiaries will have access to information packages including information about World Vision, the project and the community response mechanism via SMS, flyers, helplines and/or information sessions held at field level. The Accountability team will ensure that a help desk is available during major activities. Feedback and consultation activities will be conducted during the project implementation to obtain input on the beneficiaries’ preferences and needs to inform programmatic decisions. Complaints will be confidentially handled and closed within the timeframe after proper actions and measures are taken to solve it. Finally, monthly reports will be drafted to provide updates on finalized activities, pending and solved complaints.
