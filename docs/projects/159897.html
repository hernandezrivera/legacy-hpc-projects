Project Summary

This WASH program targets a population of 10,000 people (1700 women, 1300 Men,
3200 boys and 3800 girls) who are mainly IDPs and vulnerable host community families
in 3 districts of Merka, Qoryoley and Kurtunwaarey of Lower shabelle region. AYUUB
proposes immediate WASH interventions to reduce the number of AWD/Cholera cases
reported in these areas. The activities to be conducted include, construction of pit latrines
with lockable doors and lighted at night, distribution of hygiene promotion kits, provision
of safe water through construction and rehabilitation of shallow wells as well as
chlorination and hygiene promotion training in villages, schools and OTP centers i.e hand
washing with soap at five most critical times. All these activities will enhance access to
adequate sanitation facilities especially to women and girls who are at risk of being
violated due to lack of WASH facilities. AYUUB expects the Project response will meet with
the beneficiaries needs and save lives of the targeted group as planned, the organization,
by applying all experience gained from the previous projects, the project will achieve all
set goals and objectives respectively.

Project Activities

1. Construct 1,000 IDPs pit latrines lockable with Hand washing facilities and lighted at night for 34 IDP camps and communities in Lower Shabelle region. These latrines will be built separately for males and females and marked clearly according to sex for illiterate users. 2. Door-door distribution of hygiene kits to 3,000 persons (1,700 female and 1,300 male) in the IDPs camps of Merka, Qoryooley and Kurtunwarey districts 3. Train 250 community hygiene promoters (125 female and 125 male) on hygiene promotion including hand washing, water storage and collection, safe solid waste disposal to improve hygiene and sanitation in the IDP camps and the surrounding villages. 4. Community hygiene promotion based on cultural and religious practices through hygiene promoters to mobilize 10,000 people (1,700 women, 1,300 Men, 3,200 boys and 3,800 girls) of vulnerable people in the IDP camps. 5. Chlorination of 100 water points to provide safe water to 10,000 people (1,700 women, 1,300 Men, 3,200 boys and 3,800 girls)6. Provision of safe water to 10,000 people (1,700 women, 1,300 Men, 3,200 boys and 3,800 girls) through Rehabilitation and construction of 40 shallow wells and 40 water tanks, in Lower Shabelle region.
