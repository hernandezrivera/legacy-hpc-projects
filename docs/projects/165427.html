Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the needs of the most vulnerable among conflict-affected IDP and host community girls, boys, women and men in Idlib governorate and Tell Abiad district, to the protracted needs of the existing IDPs and the hosting community by providing safe, timely and equitable access to water for drinking and domestic use for men, women, boys and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to improve health through access to sanitation and a healthy and dignified environment and increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, environmental hygiene solutions and provision of appropriate, core hygiene items, and good personal hygiene practice for IDPs, also, host community populations in the targeted locations. Morover, Silk Road aims to improve the overall condition of most affected people with a special focus on the newly IDPs and the already existed IDPs in the targeted communities through implementing of planned activities aimed at filling the gaps and reducing the needs between the displaced and the newly in order to reducing the risk of disease through access to essential hygiene items, improved awareness and knowledge of health risks. Sustainability will be aimed through the re-establishment of a cost recovery system. The quantity of water that will be provided to each person in the targeted communities is 40 L per person a day.
The proposed activities include; Providing pumps, generators, pipes and electrical panelboards, rehabilitation of water supply system, equipping wells with galvanized pipes, submersible pumps, rehabilitation of concrete bases and housing for the pumps and generators. After rehabilitation, the system will be provided with an operations and maintenance support to ensure the sustainability of the provided assistance. The assistance will cover the costs of fuel to operate the standby generators, disinfection chemicals (Chlorine), stipends for operators/technicians and tools/spare parts for a period of 6 months.
During this period of support, a cost recovery (Tariff) system will be established to ensure continuous cash flow required for running the systems, this will involve a study on a suitable tariff rate that takes into account the dwindling financial capacity of the benefiting population and will be based on focus group discussions with a representative group of the community including members of local councils of targeted communities. In addition to the development of the water unit in the civil councils so as to ensure that they have adequate operations and maintenance teams. These teams will be responsible for ensuring sustainable water services by activating a tariff system. In addition to hygiene promotion activities and hygiene kits distribution to improve hygiene knowledge and access to hygiene materials, which coincide with the water activities.
By implementing these main WASH activities, that will be the best solution for the current
situation and in this context that can allow newly IDPs and the already existed IDPs have access to safe water through restored water systems to reduce vulnerability to preventable illnesses and improve resilience and IDPs and other vulnerable groups (disabled, elderly) have access to life-saving assistance to reduce the risk of WASHrelated morbidity and mortality. Furthermore, the proposed WASH interventions in this proposal will ensure that they do not create additional risks for the affected population (especially IDPs women and children and the newly IDPs) but will provide the best solutions to the suffering population in the targeted areas, also Silk Road will ensure that the health impact in this emergency WASH intervention will reduced disease risk and reduced transmission risk and it adhering to internationally recognized standards and best practices, ensuring that services are provided in line with the Do No Harm framework, Sphere standards, and humanitarian principles, and that services are of the highest quality.

4 - Needs

WASH services and supplies to at least 500,000 IDPs accommodated within host communities – are needed. According to OCHA, from 1 December 2019 to 2 February 2020, some 586,000 people fled from their homes in northwest Syria as a result of ongoing hostilities. According to the need assessment conducted by Silk Road, people in the targeted communities live now on unsafe water trucking or closed wells to provide their water needs which are unsafe, unclean and is not regulated as well. Chlorine is not added to the tanks before sold to people and this can cause diseases, especially for kids. Many families, especially Newly IDPs, have to face difficulties in reaching the needed water where there is one private source with a high price and not available at all times. This situation seems difficult for people like widows, divorcees and people with special needs because all people depend on this source (water trucking). In the targeted locations the Displaced people, especially the newly displaced, are dispersed in abandoned houses or in rented houses and in scattered tents around the town. (They have to face difficulties in reaching the needed water and this situation seems difficult for people like widows, divorcees and people with special needs). The waste management sector in the targeted towns needs support in order to reduce the prevalence of epidemics and diseases due to the suspension of the garbage removing beside the limited number of waste metal bins and workers available.Tell Abiad district is suffering from a massive lack of drinking water supply, currently, it receives water in a limited time and during specific days of the week as this kind of services is not adequate to all families need and even it doesn’t reach faraway houses who had to buy water from tanks owners with a 1000-1500 SYP for each barrel 220 litres. NES Populated areas are essentially a series of small towns and villages, with limited infrastructure.

5 - Implementation of activities

The main activities of the project as follows: Activity 1 Project preparation key activities including announcing the project for the targeted community. Activity 2 Rehabilitate of the water networks and water systems in the targeted communities (This include water pipes and high water tanks for 7 water systems) Activity 3 Distribution of the 750 Water tanks for the 750 newly IDPs family. Activity 4 Supply diesel fuel to operate the generators in the targeted locations for six months ( 10 water stations). Activity 5 Supply engine oil and spare parts for periodic maintenance of the generators in the targeted locations for six months. Activity 6 Support water stations and water systems with needed Sodium Hypochlorite for disinfecting water for six months. Activity 7 Conducting regular water quality testing and FRC at the household level. Activity 8 Develop the cost-recovery mechanisms in the targeted communities in preparation for applying after the handing over of the project. Activity 9 Twelve workshops each one for 2 days, technical, management, operational and Re-establish a cost recovery system to support sustainability in the targeted communities. Conducting 10 hygiene promotion campaigns in the targeted community ( Two campaigns in each location). Activity 10 Distribution of 2000 Hygiene kits for one-off during the project. Activity 11 Support the municipality and waste management system in the targeted locations for 6 months through providing needed diesel fuel and daily workers and maintenance of dumpsites. Activity 12 Distribute of 250 new metal waste bins for the targeted location according to the need in order to ensure support the municipality and waste management systems. Activity 13 Field monitoring visits by the field team. Its purpose is to monitor progress and the works to ensure that all activities are under continuous monitoring and they are a part of the Silk Road monitoring system.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/materials such as generators and constructionmaterials and chemicals such as chlorine (during delivery/storage/ after installation) byarmed groups and used in conflict activities. Mitigating Measure: -MoUs to be agreedwith local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials andrehabilitated/new facilities. Promote cost recovery systems for longer-term maintenanceand ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. Work closely with allpartners on the ground; Promote distribution stock between multiple warehouses in thetarget regions (for large quantities of stock). 2- Improper storage of hazardous supplies(chlorine and other) materials) - Possibility of the explosion, human casualty and assetdestruction. Mitigating Measure: - Ensure proper storage of supplies with the clearprovision of care and safety consideration. Make sure that clear instruction for use isavailable for chlorine products primarily for household use. 3- Movements of garbagetrucks in congested and Reliability of services. (Its probability is low and its impact ismedium) Mitigating Measure: - Consult community (separately women and girls)regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Providehousehold level solid waste containers/plastic bags to ease the disposal of waste fromH/H to the public SW collection points. Establish clear routes and schedule for garbagetrucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travelto/from school; - Potential exposure to the explosive hazard in case of support tomunicipal solid waste removal; 4- Market system disruption. - Silk Road will monitorinflation and prices and include this risk in its contracts with service providers. If inflationrises, they will work with the donor to re-align the budget and program accordingly. - Wewill Consider using fixed prices and placing this risk in the contractor's contracts.
