Project Summary

The project aims to support the resilience of affected populations in Idleb and Aleppo through provision of integrated protection activities focusing on CP, Protection, and GBV prevention and response services based on the structure of static facilities and mobile teams will operate protection intervention in 2022 and 2023.
According to the needs and severity scale of 2022, ATAA will target the most severe locations in Idleb including (Dana, Maaret Tamisrin, Idleb) sub-districts, and Aleppo including (Atareb, Daret Azza, and Al bab) sub-districts by the following structure:
Protection community centers in Kelly - Maaret Tamisrin, Qah – Dana, Atareb – Atareb providing integrated protection and child protection intervention with a total of 42 qualified protection staff members with gender balance.
Continue providing activities in IDPs sites as education – WASH – FSL – Protection integrated intervention through five mobile teams in Daret Azza, Al Bab, Sarmada, Kafr Yehmul, and Maaret Al Naasan in a total of 20 qualified protection members.
Community based protection is a main approach of intervention through supporting community mechanisms by emphasizing the role of community and child protection committees to support the protection mainstreaming, referrals pathways, and community awareness raising.
Child Protection:
ATAA will provide child protection prevention activities through the protection centers and mobile teams including:
Awareness raising about CP concerns.
CPSM surveys
Recreational PSS
Identification and referrals
Capacity building for humanitarian workers on CSG, PFA, SIR, and CPiE.
Conduct structured programs (PSS and parenting skills).
On the other hand, ATAA will deliver specialized CPCM training for CP staff to provide response CP services including case management and emergency case fund provision to the most vulnerable cases identified in the project locations.
All activities will be reported to CPSC through 4Ws, and CPCM report in addition
GBV:
ATAA will apply a solid approach to build the capacity of specialized GBV staff starting from hiring qualified staff, implement specialized capacity building plan including provision of GBV case management and case management supervision training programs targeting GBV CM staff, and close coordination with GBV SC to target the GBV project staff with capacity building initiatives and submit monthly 4Ws reports and participate in GBV CM submission for the supported cases. On the other hand, ATAA will establish comprehensive service mapping including the available services in the project locations to support the vulnerable GBV survivors through specialized GBV intervention in the established protection centers by providing comprehensive case management for survivors including PFA, PSS, ECF, counseling and referrals.
The delivered service quality will be measured by GBV M&E toolkit and satisfaction surveys for closed cases with subject to the information sharing protocols.
Beside the specialized services, ATAA will provide prevention activities including (awareness raising through awareness raising toolkit, PSS, life skills, vocational training) for female women and adolescent girls in the project locations.
Protection:
ATAA will interfere in protection services provision including protection monitoring surveys, awareness raising about protection concerns, PSEA, COVID-19 and provision of legal CSD, and HLP awareness raising and counselling for male and female BNFs both HC and IDPs.
Mine Action:
ATAA will provide EORE sessions through outreach mobile teams targeting IDPs sites and affected communities through the awareness raising messages developed by MASC.
All the planned activities will be subject to COVID-19 prevention measures and accompanied with multi-sectoral services (education, FSL, WASH) to provide integrated and comprehensive service to the people in need.

4 - Needs

According Voicec from Syria 2022, women and girls across all governorates of Syria were subjected to physical violence, psychological and emotional, sexual,and social violence, as well as children of all ages face violence and protection concerns in many life situations, children comprise almost half of the affected population. Adolescence brings exposure to new forms of violence due to harmful coping mechanisms within the family. Adolescent boys are more likely to be killed and injured, detained and recruited or to be involved in child labor, while adolescent girls are particularly at risk of child marriage.Lack/loss of civil documentation impacts all population groups with specific vulnerabilities ,HLP issues affecting women were reported as occurring in 42% of communities consulted during the MSNA in 2021.Approximately one in two people are at risk from explosive contamination, and a third of the communities are potentially contaminated, yet certain population groups are at greateror different risk depending on gender, age and disability with gender and age that increase the need for community protection centers and committees which will help in emphasizing the role of community and child protection committees to support the protection mainstreaming, referrals pathways, and community awareness raising in both NES and NWS. All above-mentioned concerns and gaps had been confirmed and highlighted by many protections needs assessment conducted by ATAA during 2021.

5 - Implementation of activities

Generally, there will be static facilities within relatively densely populated locations. Mobile units will be attached to those fixed centers to reach out to smaller populations including IDPs sites and camps. ATAA will coordinate with local authorities to ensure access and community acceptance of the proposed activities. Besides, ATAA plan to deliver the proposed services as integrated services with Education/FSL services to make them more appealing to targeted communities.The proposed activities will be delivered through adapted modalities to reduce COVID-19 outbreak, in addition to safety and security procedures to ensure the safety of BNFs and staff.Online platforms and radio broadcasting will be alternative means for awareness raising sessions provision, while all training and physical awareness raising sessions will be delivered to a maximum of 8-10 person per session with using PPE.Case management will be delivered in a mixed manner with considering COVID-19 precautious measures during physical follow-up sessions. All project staff and community committees will be trained on CP, and GBV core concept in addition to specialized training set for specialized services including case management, along with PSEA and CSG training to support the protective environment and do no harm principle during the implementation.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ATAA PSEA policy developed in December 2021 and shared with donors, and set a dedicated PSEA committee including (PSEA focal point, HR focal point, MEAL focal point, Grants and partnership focal point).ATAA adopted a core concept in all intervention to make PSEA and CSG as mandatory in all projects and activities, in this regard, all ATAA staff, volunteers and contractors will got orientation sessions about PSEA and CSG policies and sign both PSEA and CSG code of conducts.All project locations will include banners and complaint boxes with delivering messages from accountability teams regarding the complaints and feedback channels in addition to stand alone system regarding reporting and response of any PSEA case starting from receiving the report, to investigation process, and provide aid and support to the survivor till closure of the case after achieving the objectives of investigation.ATAA is PSEA network member participate in all regular network meetings, in addition to submit monthly reports to the network, in addition to conduct training for all staff about PSEA and target humanitarian workers in the intervention location to support the role of PSEA network.Gender balance will be a core point in the project within all teams (programs, media, MEAL) and locations will be accessed by women and girls safely.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Loss of resources in the local market (L: M I: M): the resources will be bought from the Turkish market.2. Escalation in armed conflicts and displacement of civilians (L: M I: H): this will require high coordination with the cluster groups to map the services for the displaced population and refer them through service mapping and provide emergency response package.3. COVID outbreak leads to delays or suspension of activities. (L: H I: H)All activities will become very context-dependent and will be adjusted accordingly in close coordination with all stakeholders. In addition to shifting to an online module for activities using WhatsApp groups for adults and children to maintain the BNFs' maximum reach during any restrictions.4. suspension of cross boarder SC resolution. (L:M,I:H)Coordinate closely with donors to continue provide the service through handed over grants and search for alternative sources of funding. ATAA has contingency plans that are set to ensure adequate planning and preparation for a variety of potential emergency situations of deterioration of security conditions in the area of ATAA presence, and provide instructions and guidance to ATAA staff involved in the mitigation planning. As well, ATAA has a risk management policy in place which forms part of the ATAA governance framework and by which ATAA processes the whole set of activities carried out to identify, assess, manage, and monitor any risks to which ATAA may be exposed.
