Project Summary

Zimbabwe is experiencing one of its worst food security crises in recent years with atypically high humanitarian food assistance needs. Despite ongoing humanitarian food assistance, large-scale assistance needs persist and are expected to continue through at least the start of the main harvest in April/May 2021.
The persisting poor macroeconomic environment continues to impact livelihoods and remains a key driver to the near-record levels of rural and urban food insecurity. The high parallel market exchange rates for foreign currency still largely influence the pricing of most goods and services. This is impacting a large proportion of the population whose earnings in local currency are vastly eroded.
Mwenezi district in Masvingo province is expected to be among the very few districts in IPC 4 (emergency) for the current period just like last year. CARE Zimbabwe will be targeting Mwenezi based on the high level food insecurity, the organization’s capacity to implement in these areas and limited coverage by other players. The organisation implemented similar intervention the same period last year through ECHO funding.
CARE proposed project will benefit 25 600 vulnerable individuals (approximately 5 120 households) through unconditional cash transfers at US$12/individual for 4 months. Food security outcomes will be enhanced through-out complementary activities that include gender and protection trainings, nutrition and hygiene messages.

1 - Activities

District Level Launches with Stakeholders - This is where the program will be launched and stakeholders, including local government authorities, are made aware of the program implementation process and roles and responsibilities are also agreed.2. Community consultations, targeting, registration and verification of beneficiaries - Community stakeholder meetings will be conducted to discuss the cash transfer approach and modalities. These will lead to community sensitization, targeting and verification. In addition, beneficiary registration will be conducted using the selection criteria identified during the targeting sessions. This activity will also present an opportunity to raise awareness on Child safeguarding and Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA).3. Establishment of Community Feedback/Accountability Mechanisms at District Level - The channels include suggestion boxes, a help desk, the Deloitte toll free hotline andengagement with community gender and accountability focal person.4. Cash Transfers to the most vulnerable households – Type of agency to be used will be decided in consultation with communities5. Support monthly active screening for acute malnutrition and referrals - The project will engage with district level and health center-based mentors on a monthly basis to support the mentoring of VHWs. The VHWs in the targeted areas will conduct the activity and the consortium will leverage and use the already planned community mobilization sessions for the cash transfer activities to conduct these activities.6. Awareness raising messages (SMS) on acute malnutrition, IYCF-E and hygiene promotion - The message will be sent to the targeted recipients through mobile phones. The activity seeks to ensure that appropriate and optimal IYCF and WASH practices are promoted to prevent and reduce the prevalence of malnutrition and WASH-related diseases. The messages will focus on practical behavior change that will be disseminated through SMS as well through village health worker outreach activities.7. Awareness raising on protection and gender and child safeguarding - Protection messages will be disseminated using multi-media platforms (bulk SMSs), distribution of fliers in schools and at Help Desks, and banners will be displayed at project sites with protection-related content.8. Monthly post distribution monitoring

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

CARE International’s Humanitarian Mandate is to meet immediate needs of disaster-affected populations in the poorest communities. Our mandate calls on CARE staff to demonstrate the highest standards of quality and accountability. CARE‘s accountability to affected populations will be based on CARE’s Humanitarian Accountability Framework (HAF) a commitment to accountability at all stages of emergency preparedness and response. The framework draws together the existing internal and inter agency standards and codes for humanitarian quality and accountability from Inter Agency standing Committee (IASC) and Accountability to Affected Population (APP) commitments. Staff are oriented on CARE’s core values, Humanitarian Principles, IASC Guidelines, and CARE Code of Conduct, Complaints and Reporting System among other topics. This is followed up with regular staff workshops and meetings which are used to reinforce CARE’s commitments to beneficiaries it serves. All staff contracts with CARE also reflect on the organization’s desire to being accountable to beneficiaries. All CARE staff will be responsible for implementing work in accordance with this accountability framework, including when we are working with and through partners. CARE managers have a specific responsibility to ensure that roles and responsibilities are clear within and between teams. CARE will involve the disaster affected communities in all stages of the project. The organization will facilitate the selection process of community leaders with proven skills to lead community members where such selection is necessary, and trainings will be facilitated to equip them with the required skills so that they are better able solve conflicts and grievance handling. Women will be actively encouraged to be considered for critical decision-making positions to assure equality. CARE will put formal mechanisms in place in consistent with our Core Values of Integrity. We care about eliminating all forms of fraud, discrimination, exploitation, misappropriations, sexual harassment and other wrongful conduct. A clear reporting for wrongful conduct mechanism will be in place in form of community help desk, anonymous tip off, toll free dial numbers will be shared with the community. In consistent with our CORE values communities are publicly informed that reports will be kept confidential to the greatest extent possible and can be submitted anonymously.
