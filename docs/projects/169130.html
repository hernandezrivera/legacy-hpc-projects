Project Summary

Following the Beirut Port explosion that occurred on August 04, 2020, different areas of Beirut were severely affected by this blast. These areas host a wide demography of people ranging from youngsters to elderly. According to the Humanitarian Response Info, 160000 vulnerable people residing in the catchment area of the 22 moderate to serious damages medical facilities who rely on them for medications and basic health care, were affected.
With reference to HelpAge International, many of older people have difficulty to access the healthcare they urgently need and have lost their medications with their destroyed homes.
According to a new study conducted by ESCWA, 55% of Lebanon is in Poverty as of May 2020 before Beirut Blast.
Many elderly who are living on their end-of-service allowances are in precarious situation, while others are already destitute, having no one to care about them and staying days without eating.
In addition, many elderly who used to receive financial support from their family are finding themselves without any income, since their children are no longer able to meet their needs.
The present Project aims to provide home care services to elderly and disabled Lebanese and all refugees resident of the affected areas more specifically:
- Geitawi / Rmeil
- Mar Michael / Gemmayze
- Karantina / Bourj Hammoud
- Sassine / Karm el Zeytoun / Saydeh.
Following the preliminary assessment already conducted by different actors, two weeks of outreach are scheduled to be conducted with two Outreach Officers and two Social Workers ahead of the operation in order to target the beneficiaries. A map will be established to indicate all addresses of the targeted population.
The home care free of charge services consists of providing:
- General medical consultations
- Acute and chronic medical drugs
- Psychosocial support for patients and families
- Taking blood samples for analysis (analysis to be produced by PHC laboratories)
- Hygiene products: Diapers.
These services will be delivered by five teams composed of:
- General team composed of four nurses, two Social Workers and four drivers with the support of four rented vehicles
- Specialized team composed of one Psychologist with the support of one rented vehicle in addition to two General Physicians who will be on hold for specific home visits .
The medical team will be accessible from 8:00 am to 17:00 pm to receive calls for light follow-up and medical advices.
A capacity building program will be organized to train the outreach officers as well as the teams.
Around 1200 acts will be delivered on a monthly basis to 400-500 patients.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Our assessment is based on the needs' assessment already conducted by different actors (Lebanese Red Cross, World Bank,....).Our main target is to provide medical services and psychosocial support to the vulnerable population mainly the elderly and the disabled who are not capable of accessing primary healthcare services due to their harsh financial and economic situation as well as their difficulty of mobility.During the first two weeks of assessment, the outreach Officers and the Social Workers will identify the most important needs in order to adequately respond and create the response map.The assessment will target Lebanese and refugees mainly the elderly patients, the disabled (children and adults) and bedridden persons with chronic diseases, in need of medical drugs, medical follow-up, laboratory tests, psycho social support, and diapers.This assessment will help organising the sustainability of the home care services above this Project period. The Order of Malta Lebanon is currently submitting a proposal to BMZ to Home Care Services which should start end of 2020 beginning 2021. These services will be further completed by this new Project.The proposed activities will be launched through our Primary Health Care Center in Ain el Remmaneh which is located at 3 to 5 km from Greater Beirut, in addition we will be in close cooperation with Dar el Fatwa medical Center and Howard Karagheusian Commemorative Corporation PHC.

2 - Activities

Following the preliminary assessment already conducted by different actors, two weeks of outreach are scheduled to be conducted with two Outreach Officers and two Social Workers ahead of the operation in order to target the beneficiaries. A map will be established to indicate all addresses of the targeted population. The home care free of charge services consists of providing:- General medical consultations- Acute and chronic medical drugs: An assessment related to the needs will be conducted before purchasing the medical drugs. In order to reduce the cost of living of the vulnerable patients due to the actual economic crisis, the Project will focus on the chronic medical drugs according to the list authorized by the Lebanese Ministry of Public Health for Primary Healthcare Centers. The Order of Malta Lebanon has a long experience in managing its ten Primary Healthcare Centers since 1957 and six Mobile Medical Units since 2006. All purchases are centralized in the Central Pharmaceutical Warehouse of the Order of Malta. All lists are checked and approved by the Medical Committee of the Order of Malta. - Psycho-social support for patients and families: Since the inception of the mental health program established within four key centers of the Order of Malta Lebanon, we have been in talks with the National Mental Health program directors with whom we’ve met several times to discuss the Order’s participation in the program and further collaboration through time. As members of the MHPSS taskforce created in response to the Beirut blast, we have been attending the online meetings and have adapted our own response to the needs identified through this action.The project activities will be instigated from our PHC in Ain el Remmaneh, where the operation’s HUB will be located. The process’ logistic will be entwined with the PHCs. - Taking blood samples for analysis (analysis to be produced by PHC laboratories): a refrigerator is budgeted to be purchased and will be dedicated to preserve the blood during its transportation according to bio-safety and cold-chain measures. The field team will be communicating the Blood tests' results to the patients and in some cases a visit from a general physician will be scheduled.- Hygiene products: Diapers.These services will be delivered by five teams composed of:- General team composed of four nurses, two Social Workers and four drivers with the support of four rented vehicles.- Specialized team composed of one Psychologist with the support of one rented vehicle, in addition to two General Physicians who will be on hold for specific home visits.All teams will be equipped by adequate PPE to face the COVID-19 pandemic and to protect the beneficiaries. Some masks will be distributed to the patients for free.The medical team will be accessible from 8:00 am to 17:00 pm to receive calls for light follow-up and medical advice.A capacity building program will be organized to train the outreach officers as well as the teams.Around 1200 acts will be delivered on a monthly basis to 400-500 patients. Every patient will be visited on average three times per month for the Project period.
