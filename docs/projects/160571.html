Project Summary

This intervention is intended to mitigate and reduce the risk of gender based violence among women, girls, boys and men in Ayod County through provision of quality services that include preventive measures against GBV in the community. The targets for this intervention is to reach 12000 individuals with timely access to life saving activities and will cost about 250,000 USD The scope of activities will include the following, establishment of WGFS,cash based assistance, Conduct vulnerability assessment, awareness raising, train women ,girls, boys and men on fuel Efficient stoves and strengthen staff and communities on protection issues, distribute dignity kits to women and adolescent girls after the assessment of suitability of kits ,provide preventive initiatives, provide counselling and legal aid, that provide space and entry point for women and girls for GBV case management.
WAO will also focus on risk mitigation .WAO will work closely with actors proving services in Ayod county that include CAO to avoid service duplication and resource wasted and will be done through coordination meeting and service mapping that will include 5Ws.WAO will work closely with FSL cluster members to coordinate the cash based assistance modalities /methodologies..WAO team will conduct service mapping on available GBV services to ensure that the GBV referral pathway is established / strengthened, updated and functional before providing training for frontline service providers and do information session on available services. As to contribute toward cluster overall objective, WAO will join other partners in developing and disseminate the GBV standard operating procedures. WAO will ensure that safety audit is conducted as part of GBV risk mitigation measure.
