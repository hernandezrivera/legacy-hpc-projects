Start Date

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to contribute to addressing the winterization needs of the affected communities in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (NGCA).
The action will be implemented through a comprehensive set of winterization support activities, including distribution of coal for 8,000 households, winterization kits for 4,000 households, electric heaters for 1,000 beneficiaries, distribution of winterization kits among 30 social institutions benefiting 2,400 individuals and conduction of winterization rehabilitation works in 45 social institutions beneficiating 13,500 patient/visitors.

1 - Needs

• During the post distribution monitoring (PDM), March 2019, on coal distribution among the beneficiaries of the current project, the overwhelming majority of the respondents (95%) had essential need for coal at the time of distribution. The PDM results showed that vulnerable households were not able to purchase coal for housing heating and they depend a lot on humanitarian support; • As per field visits conducted in frames of coal distributions in 2018-2019 by IOM project staff, most of the interviewed beneficiaries confirmed that after they received solid fuel the average temperature in the house is above 18°C. Since the amount of monthly social payments for vulnerable categories is about UAH 1,600 (USD 55) per month, thus, families belonging to the socially unprotected categories are not able to purchase coal for housing heating independently as USD 83 was spent to purchase one tone of coal. Regarding the alternative types of heating the 88% of respondents don’t use anything besides coal; • According to the conducted surveys the relevance of assisting vulnerable households with non-food items (NFIs) such as winterization kits is more than obvious, beneficiaries of this type of humanitarian assistance reported that social benefits and pensions remain at a critically low level (below the subsistence minimum), however, prices for general consumer goods are rising very rapidly. Families are not able to fully meet their basic needs; • According to IOM's post distribution monitoring (PDM) on hygiene kits distribution activities carried out in February 2019, infrastructure rehabilitation in Donetsk NGCA remains one of the top priorities. This is predominantly the case for public infrastructure and social institutions that have been directly affected by the hostilities and those that no longer receive public support; and• Population with specific vulnerabilities (physical disabilities or individuals made vulnerable due to gender such as young boys or women) are considered and targeted with assistance to meet specific needs, served using methodologies that ensure access and prevent harassment or theft of any properties provided. In line with the latest data published in the IOM’s NMS report, June 2019, the share of women residing in NGCA was 58%, among which share of aged over 60 years old was 53%. Being pensioners with the income that hardly allows to procure three tons of coal to address heating needs during the harshest period of time, elderly people are prioritized for the winterization support. Based on IOM's previous humanitarian interventions in the NGCA, the elderly, persons with disabilities and single-headed households (in most cases female-headed) remain particularly vulnerable and will be prioritized for NFI distribution. The coal and NFIs will be distributed directly to the homes of beneficiary families, ensuring tailored assistance for particularly vulnerable households.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Outcome 1. Vulnerable conflict affected persons in NGCA have increased access to essential commodities. Output 1: Vulnerable households are enabled to meet their basic heating needs for winter. Activities for output 1: - Conduct verification and selection of beneficiary households in coordination with local counterparts; - Launch Call for Partners and select the coal supplier; - Through open tender to procure winterization kits, cast-iron stoves and heaters; - Carry out distribution of winterization kits and coal to the targeted households; - Carry out distribution of the cast-iron stoves to the selected households; - Conduct post-distribution monitoring on the cast-iron stoves and heaters, winterization kits and coal distributions. Output 2: Critical infrastructure in key community sites and social institutions is functional and meets the needs of individuals seeking social services. Activities for output 2: - Assess and select sites for rehabilitation and winterization kits distribution in coordination relevant de facto authorities in Luhansk NGCA, and with the IP’s involvement in Donetsk NGCA; - Prepare the Bill of Quantities for rehabilitation works either by directly contracted construction company, or by selected IP, launch the tender and select the construction company; - Carry out the rehabilitation of targeted sites in Luhansk and Donetsk NGCA; - Through open tender to procure winterization kits to be distributed among the selected social institutions; - Carry out distribution of the procured winterization kits among the key social sites; - Conduct post-distribution monitoring of rehabilitation and winterization kits distribution of the targeted sites in Luhansk and Donetsk NGCA. Beneficiary selection: The beneficiaries of winterization NFIs will be identified based on agreed upon vulnerability criteria such as: single headed households, presence of elderly or persons with disabilities within the household and large families with three and more children. Public structures eligible for proposed winterization rehabilitations and repair works will be selected by prioritizing areas close to the contact line, those damaged in the course of hostilities, and those of the greatest importance for the local communities, accommodating the most vulnerable groups of population. Project design and planning phase: Beneficiaries have informed the project design and planning phase of proposed activities through engagement in focus group discussions implemented under IOM’s ‘National Monitoring System on the Situation of Internally Displaced Persons’, highlighting their most urgent needs. Moreover, through IOMs experience implementing similar actions previously in the NGCA, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) reports have confirmed recipient’s urgent need for winterization and hygiene support.Project implementation phase: Within the present action, IOM has tailored implementation modalities to ensure access and eligibility of the most vulnerable households. The capabilities, as well as age and gender, were mainstreamed into processes for distributions to ensure the safety and dignity of recipients. Whenever necessary, distributions will be made directly to the homes of beneficiaries. Project monitoring: PDM as well as focus group discussions carried out within the framework of project activities provide beneficiaries the platform to share their opinions on the assistance received, including quality and quantity, relevance, implementation modality and usefulness to allow IOM to take urgent actions to address any issues and inform future programme planning.

Outcome 1. Output 2: Critical infrastructure in key community sites and social institutions is functional and meets the needs of individuals seeking social services. - # of social institutions and key community sites supported with essential rehabilitation (Target: 45); - # of individuals served by the social institutions and key community sites rehabilitated (Target: 13,500 individuals, including 8,100 female and 5,400 male, out of whom 1,350 children, 4,050 adults and 8,100 elderly); - # of winterization kits distributed among the additionally selected social institutions (Target: 2,400 kits); - # of individuals supported from the winterization kits distribution among the additionally selected social institutions (Target: 2,400 individuals, including 1,440 female and 960 male, out of whom 240 children, 720 adults and 1,440 elderly).
