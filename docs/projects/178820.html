Project Summary

The project will support the Objective-1 of education sector: Availably & Access
Increased safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children and youth. As the help to address the education community needs for 9780 child in 4 camps in Sarmada (Alhamoud, Jabil alhous, Alsarout, Jabil Alzaoieh , Shahshabo, Alrahel, Abu Nawas, and Alyousuf Camps) and in Azaz SD in addition to Maree in Northern rural of Aleppo.
In Sarmad camps, the project aims to:
1. Reach out to 2000 children OOS with BTL campaign in the two areas and conducting placement test during the campaign to identify children students levels and grades.
2. Raising community awareness of importance of education process and participating in declining child labour by encouraging parents to resume sending their children to schools.
3. Establishment of 4 non-formal education centers in 4 Samrada camps and in addition to scale up for 3 exciting centers
4. Provide 1840 children (900 boys and 940 girls) (5-17 yeas) in eight camps of Sarmada with the below activities:
a. Provision of remedial classes and catch up classes to support the children in restoring missing knowledge and level up with their peers. The learning curriculum to be used for the remedial classes is Short version of Syrian Interim Government SIG curriculum Or UNICEF.
b. Enlighten children students with "I DEAL" courses as supportive materials to provide them with practical and discovery interest.
5. Providing 90 teachers and education personnel with incentives, and providing them with teaching resources, kits and guides.
6. Building the capacity of the teachers , education personnel in the schools ,in addition to 70 education actors from the local authorities and humanitarian workers on in crisis- sensitive data collection and analysis and dissemination, relevant standards, Education in Emergencies (EiE) and INEE Minimum Standards, and/or advocacy on the needs and rights of children in crisis contexts.
6. Equip the learning centres with Child Protection specialists to provide needed services and referrals.
7. Establishment of gender-sensitive school wash facilities which are taking into consideration the people with disabilities as well.
And in Azaz and Maree after the coordination with Kilis education directorate we are planning to rehabilitees around 348 classes to cover 29 schools to support 7680 child.

5 - Implementation of activities

POINT is going to conduct the following activities in Sarmada CAMPS in Idlib and in Azaz and Maree in Aleppo Governorate.In Sarmada camps POINT will -Provide children and youth with non-formal education programs (including catch-up classes, self-learning program (SLP), remedial education, literacy and numeracy classes) -Conduct Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns, and establish Parent Teacher Associations. -Provide professional development to teaching staff and education personnel (child-centred and protective pedagogy, active learning, self-learning, life skills, Curriculum B, ECCE, New Curriculum, psychosocial support and referral mechanisms, Education in Emergencies (EiE) and national/INEE minimum standards (MS). -Provide teachers and education personnel with incentives. - Provide teachers and education personnel with teaching resources, kits and guides in formal and non-formal settings. -Provide children with supplementary learning materials for children attending formal and non-formal education. -Build the capacity of humanitarian actors in crisis-sensitive data collection and analysis and dissemination, relevant standards, Education in Emergencies (EiE) and INEE Minimum Standards, and/or advocacy on the needs and rights of children in crisis contexts. - Support or establish school-based governance and accountability mechanisms to support school operations. - Establishment of gender-sensitive school wash facilities which are taking into consideration the people with disabilities as well in the 7 schools.In Azaz and Maree based on the needs and coordination with Kilis Education Directorate to rehabilitate 142 classes in Azaz and in Maree 206 classes
