Project Summary

To strengthen leadership of the GBV Sub-Cluster and address identified GBV protection concerns in the occupied Palestinian territory, through more effective coordination of GBV prevention, mitigation and response interventions as well as inter and intra cluster mainstreaming of GBV.
The program seeks to respond to the prolonged humanitarian crisis in the occupied Palestinian territories affecting the Palestinian population under occupation, by contributing to the protection of their rights
and dignity in accordance with IHL and IHRL, and with a cross-cutting focus with other sectors such as health, education, child protection.
The project is aligned with the the strategic objectives of the Protection Cluster as it i) seeks to increase the respect for the IHL and IHRL and accountability for violations as well as ii) to put in place protection
mechanisms to prevent and mitigate the effects of the occupation and conflict related violence

Needs

GBV is a key protection concern in OPT. This project would support the implementation of the Humanitarian Response Plan Strategic Objective 1 and 2 as well as the GBV Sub Cluster Strategic Plan 2018-2020. Through the GBV Sub Cluster Strategy as well as Needs Overview, the Sub Cluster has identified the following key issues • Impact of the occupation on GBV resulting in more than 294,000 every married women in need of GBV services. • Lack of available, acceptable, accessible and quality GBV services including referral in Gaza Strip, Area C, East Jerusalem and H2 area. • Lack of awareness raising on GBV issues and services We can additionally in our analysis see that certain groups have a much higher risk of GBV. These groups includes Bedouin women, internally displaced women, women and girls living in refugee camps, women with disabilities, women and girls impacted by the Great March of Return and women and girls from poor households in Area C. Against this background, the GBV Sub Cluster is working towards three key results in 2016 • Provision of multisectoral services for GBV survivors• Raise awareness on GBV and available and accessible GBV services UNFPA, as GBV Sub Cluster lead has overall responsibility for ensuring coordinated GBV prevention, mitigation and responses. The GBV Sub Cluster meets in Ramallah and Gaza City. UNFPA is responsible for ensuring that all GBV protection responses are coordinated taking into account gender and diversity, and the differing protection needs of various groups within the population. Effective coordination between Sub Cluster partners are essential. Gender considerations are an integral part of the operational response coordinated by UNFPA. The humanitarian work of the GBV Sub-Cluster focuses on Gaza Strip, Area C of the West Bank, H2 as well as East Jerusalem. In WB, GBV Sub-Cluster partners are also providing services in Area A and B and GBV survivors residing in Area C are therefore also referred to specialized services provided both by Government and NGOs in Area A and B. The Sub-Cluster uses the ‘GBV Minimum Standards in Emergencies’ as the guiding document for provision of services in the humanitarian setting and the ‘Essential Services Package for Women and Girls Subject to Violence’ for the development sector. However, linkages are needed between the development and humanitarian sectors e.g. when it comes to forensic medicine, which is seen as part of the development sector. The Sub-Cluster is working with the PNA to ensure that these are part of the essential package thereby supporting GBV survivors in Area C to hold perpetrators accountable for their crimes. UNFPA as lead of the GBV Sub Cluster has since then strengthen the coordination of interventions by mapping gaps in services and advocating for filling the gaps, reached more than 32,000 survivors with at least one type of GBV services in 2017, strengthened GBV mainstreaming in other clusters such as Health Cluster, Protection Cluster, Legal Task Force and Child Protection and MHPSS Working Group, which has led to GBV being included into referral pathways and prevention and mitigation mechanisms are put in place. The GBV Sub Cluster has additionally, established a GBV service directory, conducted mapping of services as well as established a GBV information management system with GBV incident data which NGOs, government institutions and UN agencies are reporting into. However, there is still gaps and needs to strengthen GBV prevention, mitigation and response. Additionally, support is needed for UNFPA to continue being Sub Cluster lead.

Activities

Given the large membership (approximately 45 organizations) of the GBV Sub Cluster and the multisectoral service provision for GBV survivors coordinated under the Sub-Cluster, sufficient human resources are essential to ensure leadership and coordination. During the first half of 2018, the GBV Sub-Cluster held around 12 meetings tackling different issues such as GBV information management, mapping of services and development of an emergency and preparedness plan. This project includes budget for 1 international officer to ensure overall coordination of the GBV Sub Cluster in the oPt (Sub Cluster Coordinator) and contribution to a national associate in GS. Working with the Protection Cluster, other clusters and with the members of the GBV Sub Cluster, the coordination team will do the following. 1. Provide vision and leadership for the GBV Sub Cluster, based on consultations with members and stakeholders;2. Undertake day to day coordination of the GBV Sub Cluster, including convening meetings, information sharing and ensuring GBV Sub Cluster input to humanitarian processes (e.g. HPC); 3. Support the implementation of the GBV Sub Cluster Strategy 2018-20204. Strengthen the GBV information management system of the Sub Cluster by harmonizing categories, indicators etc. as well as capacity building of partners ensuring ethical and safe data collection and strengthen linkages with the national VAW observatory . 5. Ensure coordination with the work of Protection Cluster including working groups, namely the Child Protection and MHPSS Working Group and Legal Task Force including to ensure equal access to and gender sensitive protection responses. As part of this, the GBV Sub-Cluster is planning to continue trainings on the do’s and don’ts when detecting and referring GBV survivors to the partners of the GBV Sub-Cluster as well as non-GBV specialist, as there is currently no formalized referral mechanisms on GBV cases inter and intra cluster, but only within the GBV Sub-Cluster. Health Cluster, Protection Cluster, Legal Task Force and the Child Protection Working Group has requested the support in how to deal with GBV survivors. The training and dissemination of the referral pathways and standard operating procedures will take place for within each governorate thereby ensuring that field staff are equipped with skills and knowledge on how to deal and refer GBV survivors to GBV specialists. This will be based on the pilot which already took place in Hebron in 2018. Additionally, the GBV Sub-Cluster will see how GBV cases detected in other clusters and sub-clusters/working groups can be reflected in the GBV incident data system (established with the suppport by OCHA) as well as how to avoid duplication of cases when reporting.6. Ensure coordination with National Coalition to combat VAW (al Muntada) and the National Committee for combating VAW under the PA. 7. Identify and propose action to address gaps, including in information gathering, management, analysis and coordinated response to priority GBV protection concerns, with a focus on increased analysis on gender specific vulnerabilities and gender specific impact of ongoing concerns; 8. Coordinate joint advocacy efforts under relevant priorities of the GBV Sub Cluster Strategy 9. Support partners with resource mobilisation 10. Develop policy papers on top priority GBV needs and gaps11. Work with the other clusters and sectors to mainstream GBV and gender sensitive humanitarian responses in the overall humanitarian efforts, including through the provision of guidelines, checklists and training, and coordination of operational responses to protection issues both in Gaza Strip and West Bank. 12. Support cluster members to strengthen their accountability to affected populations.13. Support introducing the GBV minimum standers for prevention and response in emergenc

Indicators

All GBV Sub-Cluster partners are required to follow the GBV Survivor Centered Approach thereby prioritizing safety, and dignity, do no harm, provide meaning full access, ensuring accountability mechanism as well as participation and empowerment of GBV survivors.
