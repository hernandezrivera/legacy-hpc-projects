1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Due to the country's precarious position in humanitarian crises, including disease outbreaks, natural disasters, and conflict, emergency health assistance will focus on providing accessible, life-saving, and life-sustaining essential health services to targeted populations, directed at the main causes of morbidity and sexual and reproductive health. Health response will aim for maximum integration of response across different sectors, reducing mortality and morbidity among the most vulnerable and improving population standards of living. It will provide quality care for people with physical injuries, disabilities, and mental health needs. The project will target preparing for, detecting, and responding to epidemic-prone disease outbreaks. All these are in line with the three core HRP objectives. These interventions will be provided in partnership with SPS, DPS, SDSMAS, and other health partners. The services will be delivered through fixed health facilities and mobile clinics or mobile health brigades. This project will target all age groups composed of girls, boys, women, men, adolescents, the elderly, child and female-headed households, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups identified during the periodic assessments. It will be implemented in the three provinces (Cabo Delgado, Niassa, and Nampula).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities will be conducted to cover all 17 districts of Cabo Delgado Province, 20 districts in Nampula Province, and 16 districts in Niassa Province. The activities will be to provide leadership to Health Cluster strategic planning and decision-making to ensure health response needs are appropriately reflected in sectoral and multi-sectoral discussions; Conduct advocacy for resource mobilization for all health partners, ensuring a people-centered approach, including MHPSS; Facilitate monitoring of health cluster response through the collection, validation, analysis and dissemination of reliable information for effective decision-making ; Provide regular updates on the health services functionality through the Health Resources and Services Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS) platform; Provide of essential medicines and supplies based on emergency response to bridge gaps observed in regular drug supply ; Support assessment and monitoring of e-IDSR performance, support gaps and needs, and enhance e-IDSR training to improve timely detection and response to disease outbreaks and health conditions (e.g., cholera, AWD, measles, malaria, malnutrition, MHPSS); Guide the SPS/DPS in the implementation of strategic emergency preparedness and minimum actions (at the institutional and community-based level); Support COVID-19, cholera, polio, and measles vaccination campaigns and reach the targeted vulnerable groups ; Scale up laboratory testing capacity and rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs) (COVID-19 and cholera); Conduct in-depth situation and needs assessment of laboratory infrastructure status and technical equipment to control and prevent infections at the health unit level; Support partners’ service delivery by working to identify and respond to priority needs and gaps, including participation in joint assessments and revitalization of district coordination forums; reinforce the integrated mobile brigade approach for easy reaching of the IDP population and continuous provision of a comprehensive set of health services; and strengthen MHPSS services at the health facility and community level.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The identification of protection concerns will be conducted by a desk review, which will analyze available reports to assess potential hazards to protection. The project team will consistently interact with staff members, technical advisers, and program managers in order to gather and analyze the existing data on protection risks. The data utilized in this study may be sourced from secondary sources, if they are accessible, or derived from internal evaluations pertaining to various aspects such as gender and sectoral demands. These assessments may also encompass data related to protection risks and threats. The consultation of key informants, including protection cluster leads and relevant government departments, among others, would be conducted periodically in order to identify potential risks. The process of identifying protection risks for women, girls, boys, and men will entail holding targeted group discussions tailored to the needs and characteristics of each group. Participants will be divided into groups during the discussion to identify and count security threats collaboratively. Following that, each risk will be assessed and classified according to its priority level, which is determined by considering both the likelihood of occurrence and the magnitude of potential consequences. Following the AAP's recommendations, collaborating organizations will conduct all collaborative mission assessments. The World Health Organization (WHO), in collaboration with the health cluster, will establish a close working relationship with the protection cluster in order to gain a comprehensive understanding of the protection concerns identified during their regular field visits. The results will be utilized to facilitate the inclusion of all individuals in the execution of the project. The personnel assigned to all sites will get training on the World Health Organization's non-discriminatory policy and the fundamental values of humanitarianism.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project will be executed in accordance with the fundamental values of equity, transparency, fairness, and accountability as outlined by the United Nations. Furthermore, the project will prioritize the involvement and engagement of all stakeholders throughout the implementation process. The field officers responsible for executing this project will undergo training and awareness programs on the principles of Accountability of Affected Persons (AAP) and gender-based violence (GBV). They will get guidance to guarantee that there is no discrimination against any individual during the provision of training or vital health services at all project sites. The project will endeavor to gather perspectives from individuals across various age groups by means of focus group talks in order to ensure that the needs and requirements of all age cohorts are duly taken into account. The CCM findings will be used to build future assessments of people's perspectives and opinions across age groups. As a result, any flaws found will be addressed using the most appropriate recommended action.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Individuals with disabilities will be identified through a process of screening that occurs concurrently with the provision of health care. The individuals tasked with responding will receive training on the process of assessing individuals with disabilities, including the identification of their age and gender. They will then proceed to record this information in the designated registers. Community-based APEs will focus on the identification and assessment of individuals with disabilities in order to determine their eligibility for participation in specific health initiatives, such as mass vaccination campaigns and the provision of focused healthcare services. All data capture technologies incorporate indicators that have been designed to accommodate those with disabilities. The question pertains to the identification of the entity responsible for advocating for inclusive and accommodating environments inside healthcare institutions, with a specific focus on individuals with disabilities. This advocacy seeks to ensure that the unique needs of these group are considered and addressed during the delivery of healthcare services.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The potential environmental consequences of the initiative encompass the production of injectable waste during the process of conducting outreach consultations and administering vaccinations for diseases such as measles, polio, and cholera. These activities are accompanied by well-defined mitigation measures or policies regarding waste management, which involve the utilization of safety boxes and incineration processes. This project's activities will be carried out in accordance with environmental regulations intended to protect against hazardous waste disposal. It will also follow established protocols mandated by government agencies as well as global health standards.
