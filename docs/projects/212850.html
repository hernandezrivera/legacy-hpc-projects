Project Summary

WATAN Foundation will undertake interventions to support communities in the countryside of Aleppo and Idleb by providing agricultural resources and food items through various projects aimed at sustainability and assistance for both farmers and vulnerable populations. These initiatives focus on enhancing productive assets in agriculture and livestock sectors while ensuring food security.
One such project involves targeting a livestock to increase dairy and meat production, thereby expanding the cow and sheep population. This endeavor aims to establish a dairy value chain, starting from milk production to the marketing offinal products in local markets. A specialized veterinarians employed to oversee daily health monitoring of the cows and sheeps. Qualified personnel will be hired to do a vaccination and artificial incrimination under veterinary supervision, in addition to providing the needed reproductivity health care. Additionally, a facility for dairy product manufacturing will be set up to process milk into yogurt and cheese, which will be distributed in subsidized price to vulnerable communities. This intervention will start by 15.04.2024 and continue till 31-12-2024. targeting 1000 cow breeders and 1200 sheep breeders providing them with the veterinary care and 6 months of fodder support in line with the FSL cluster recommendation.
Upon completion of the project, marketing and selling of the final products will commence. WATAN Foundation will continue to participate in asset management alongside other stakeholders, ensuring quality assurance measures are upheld.
In terms of agricultural support, the foundation will assist potato, lentil, barley, vegetable, and olive farmers in the region by providing essential inputs necessary for cultivation. The intervention will start by the end of March 2024 and continue till the end of 2024. 1276 farmers will be targeted using a proper modality utilizing the FSL cluster standards in terms of quantities rand rates used, to ensure optimal investment for the available resources and applying best practices related to environment conservation. This support is crucial due to the challenges faced by farmers, including high input costs and market price imbalances, which have resulted in many abandoning their lands. By supplying seeds, fertilizers, land preparation services, pesticides, and mechanical harvesting equipment, the foundation seeks to optimize agricultural output.
Technical assistance will be provided through the establishment of farmers' schools and field visits by agricultural experts to address crop-related issues such as breeding and pest control. Educational materials will be distributed to raise awareness and guide farmers on best practices.
Furthermore, the projects will create employment opportunities for locals, particularly vulnerable individuals, by providing labor for harvesting. This approach aims to improve livelihoods and alleviate financial hardships for families in the targeted areas.
Lastly, internally displaced persons (IDPs) in the region will benefit from the project by receiving a portion of the agricultural produce through agreements with local farmers.
This distribution will help address food insecurity among the most affected populations in both communities and camps.
On other side, WATAN Foundation will provide the irreplaceable food items needed by distributing the food vouchers, which will contribute to supply these items for the IDPs and host in the targeted locations.
2000 HH will be targeted for 8 months as per the recommendation of the FSL cluster. This will aim to help the affected people of crisis cope with the urgent need for food due to the crisis and lack of access to food and high food prices through increasing access to needed basic food items and as well as to improve local market accessibility through Food voucher program led to strengthening the resilience of vulnerable IDPs and host communities in the targeted communities.
