Project Summary

in line with WASH Cluster objectives, 2023HRP strategic objectives, and AKDN’s Global and in-country frameworks, the response addresses important humanitarian needs with the principal objective of increasing the resilience of the affected and at-risk communities by improving access to safe drinking water and improved solid waste management services in rural and peri-urban areas of Salamiyeh District. (80% living on less than $2/day) The specific areas include Eastern Bari, As- Saan, Aqareb, Jaddouah, Tal Eltut al Altot, and Theil Elejel, . The proposed interventions seek sustainable and long-term solutions to increase access to safe and adequate water and improve safe WASH and SWM practices among the target vulnerable populations.
Following are the two project outcomes:
Outcome 1: Improved level of solid waste management services, and hygiene practices of both genders in local communities. This will be achieved by promoting safe WASH and waste disposal practices and hygienic behaviors among different age groups and genders. .
Outcome2: Increased access of local communities to safe drinking water through a sustainable and uninterrupted water supply. This longer-term solution will help severely water-insecure communities to have access to clean water. This will also support the ongoing AKAH water interventions by solarization of dormant water pumps and ongoing maintenance of the existing water pumps.

3 - Needs

Most households in Salamiyeh district rely on the weekly supply of water through expensive WT.The requirement is around 30,000m3 whereas it gets only around 10,000m3.In Salamiyah City,water supply is irregularly available only every6days on average.In rural,200 wells have run dry while many others have unstable water quality and quantity.Salamiyah’s essential services,natural resources and critical infrastructure are stressed by both climate and conflict-related pressures.Lack of sufficient fuel to run the generators has worsened the situation with only1-2hours of availability of electricity to the households, which impacts water access and disrupts farm and off-farm activities.SWM service efficiency is relatively low,due to shortage in the number of workers for the daily cleaning and collecting,insufficient quantity of waste containers and poor condition of vehicles.The private sector is absent ,also the local communities are not fully adhering to the municipalities’instructions on drop-off timing/location.According to AKAH’s desk review in corporation with the SWM department in Hama Governorate,around200 tons of municipal waste and 1-2tons of medical waste are generated in the area on a daily basis.There are no controlled waste sorting processes or treatment process.The collected waste is transferred to Burgan landfill11KMto the northeast of the city,the daily received quantity is around 100ton.The transferring process is1-2 times per week in countryside and daily in city.

4 - Implementation of activities

1.Formation of community-level water management committees to participate in the planning, management, operationa and maintenance related to water in 9 villages.2.Solarize13 critical boreholes in 9 villages in Salamiyeh district with solar systems to ensure electricity for the required pumping hours.3.Awareness raising on the risk of using plastic bags and how to minimize the consumption to the minimum in Salamiyeh city. AKAH will identify local NGO partners active in environmental issues to implement the campaign, tapping into their existing networks and community outreach channels.4.Form and train women groups on reusable-bags manufacturing.In cooperation with the Local NGOs in Salamiyeh who are interested in the environment, groups of women will be formed and trained to create their own business by producing reusable bags to replace the common plastic ones in Salamiyeh market. The group consists of 6-8 women who will be selected by the LNGO according to the following criteria:- Women-headed family - Low income - Physical ability to work Women will receive training on producing eco-friendly bags by using the available resources in the local market. Then selling them to the vendors in the local market for a fee. Basic business planning training and preparing feasibility study will be provided to the selected women as well.
