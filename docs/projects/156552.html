Project Summary

Coverage the need for a family of basic foodstuffs for the end of 2019.
1- Food insurance for 2,000 families for a period of 10 months as the family made up of 5 people on average.
2- Reduce unemployment by employing a number of people.
3- Reduce child labor and bring them back to school .
4- Relieve the pressure of life on children so that they do not become fertile environments for extremism .

Needs

1. The number of forcibly displaced persons has increased to the north of Syria.2 - Counting more than 10,000 displaced families and 40,000 families of the people of the North almost lost their dependents (the census was done by local councils).3 - There is 95% below the poverty line of the (50,000) aforementioned families .4 - Low wage ratio compared with prices of basic materials .5 - The prevalence of child labor resulting from poverty .6 - Lack of job opportunities available in the Syrian north for several reasons, including lack of capital and few projects .

Implementation of activities

Admission Requirements:The family should be a displaced person or a poor person who has no income.All family identification papers (if any) should be brought.**The estimated time period starts from the first day of implementation of the project , And will continue throughout the project ** Preparation phase:1- Ensure that the information provided by families is correct. ( Continuous )2- Collect all the data for each family, according to the forms approved models by the institution. ( Continuous )3- Appointing a supervisor for the distribution center capable of organizing the work. ( Two weeks before the start of the project )4- Appoint 3 employees to pack food bags. ( Two weeks before the start of the project )5- Appoint two persons in the field of information to document the work . ( Two weeks before the start of the project )6- Appointment of three administrative staff. ( Two weeks before the start of the project )7- Appoint an employee to monitor and evaluate the work . ( Two weeks before the start of the project )8- Hire a large place to be a warehouse and distribution center. ( Two weeks before the start of the project ) Implementation phase:1. Divide the families into four sections. (Each week a distribution is made to a division of families)2. The officer will follow up on each step and find a solution to each problem the team faces.3. The Monitoring Officer shall follow up the work at every moment and record his daily observations and submit them to the Department.
