Project Summary

The Emergency Telecommunications Sector aims at fullfilling identified needs for reliable security communications and Internet connectivity services in common operational areas, as well as to enhance the current capacity. The ET Sector also aims at supporting the humanitarian community, in collaboration with inter-sector initiatives, to improve affected population’ access to vital information and communication services

Needs

Results from the ICT assessment mission and a needs assessment survey in May 2018 showed the need for reliable security communications and Internet connectivity in at least one common operational hub, starting with Benghazi and Sebha. Findings also highlighted the need to enhance current ICT services in Tripoli for humanitarian organizations currently operating in Libya and for those who plan to return

Activities

The Emergency Telecommunications Sector priority activities are to provide Internet connectivity, ICT helpdesk and security telecommunications services to humanitarians in common operational areas as required and to maintain critical coordination and information management activities. Through the inter-sector coordination mechanism, the Emergency Telecommunications Sector will identify Services for Communities (S4C) gaps and needs in order to collaborate with integrated initiatives to communicate key information to and from the affected communities by providing advice to humanitarian organisations and implementing technical solutions.
