Project Summary

The objectives of the project are:
1. To build capacities for improved and well-coordinated SRH/GBV response including the implementation of Minimum Initial Service Package for Reproductive Health in Crisis Situations (MISP)
2. To enhance access to life saving reproductive health care services including basic and emergency obstetric and neonatal care services, referral of obstetric complications, clinical management of Rape including psychological support.
ACTIVITIES OR OUTPUTS
OUTPUT 1: Access to and utilization of SRH services with emphasis to adolescent/young people
• Activity 1.1: Procure and deliver maternal and neonatal health equipment, medical and non-medical supplies and Emergency Reproductive Health Kits to 44 of targeted facilities;
• Activity 1.2: Procure and deliver materials and testing kits Prevention of mother to child transmission of HIV from an HIV positive mother (PMTCT) as well as PEP kits;
• Activity 1.3: Conduct 3 training sessions to strengthen capacity of 90 health service providers including medical doctors, midwives and nurses, to provide quality services on Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal Care
• Activity 1.4: Conduct 4 training sessions on clinical management of rape for 100 health service providers
• Activity 1.5: Conduct 6 training sessions on Minimum Initial Service Package for sexual and reproductive health (MISP) for 100 health service providers and partners;
• Activity : Establish a referral system for obstetrical emergencies including transportation (ambulances)
OUTPUT 2: Improved access to SRH information and services to vulnerable people in hard to reach zones
• Activity 2.1: Undertake three emergency response mobile clinic per quarter in the 2 most affected health district (60 missions) to scale up SRH integrated package services
• Activity 2.2: Deploy 10 midwives in the 10 most affected health facilities
• Activity 2.3: Ensure the referencing of 1583 obstetric complications to mother-child health centers and district hospitals;
• Activity 2.4: Ensure the exemption of the expenses related to the assisted deliveries;
• Activity 2.5: Conduct community mobilization on SRH integrated package including HIV/AIDS transmission to affected people in North West and South West Region;
• Activity 2.6: Produce IEC/BCC materials to be used during sensitization activities and distributed to affected people and refugees in in North West and South West Region;
• Activity 2.7: Ensure the distribution of 3,000 hygienic delivery kits to visibly pregnant women during mobile clinical missions.

Needs

The conflict in the South West and North West regions of Cameroon goes into the third year with escalating violence and destruction of social services affecting the lives of displaced people and host communities. 959,000 people area affected by the crisis while an estimated 594,000 people are in need of life saving health and other humanitarian services in the region. The largest proportion of the People in Need (PIN) are IDPs.In the South West region alone, 40% of the health facilities are non-functional with another 30% partially functional according to the Health cluster in NW&SW regions. 5 out of 18 districts in the region currently have no functional health facility yet access to the population in these areas by humanitarian actors remains a challenge due to insecurity. Abducted women and girls have experienced physical, sexual and emotional abuse. Access to services for survivors of GBV is limited in sites of displaced persons and host communities.Based on the MISP calculator, 117,250 women of child bearing age will need lifesaving services in the four regions above. A total of 14,070 live births are expected over the next one year, 1583 pregnancies will result into life threatening complications while 528 will require caesarian sections. 2,345 women are at risk of sexual violence in the regions affected by the conflict.
