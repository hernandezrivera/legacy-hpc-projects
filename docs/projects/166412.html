Project Summary

During the COVID19, UNDP will be supporting the WASH sector through the dissemination of key messages, and distribution of hygiene items and cash for food to market owners, HH, and scrap dealers and feriwallas in Host Communities. This also includes the installation of handwashing points, and outreach activities by door-to-door, miking and radioshows. Besides promoting behaviour focused on COVID19, UNDP will continue implementing its critical activities on solid waste collection and cleaning in camps and host communities to prevent/stop the spread of the virus. However, knowing that the coronavirus can remain in surfaces such as plastics and cardboards for days, and that the use of masks and gloves by WASH workers and beneficiaries may increase considerably during the crisis, UNDP will put special attention to the waste in host communities where there are no SWM systems in place yet. UNDP will establish a basic system of safe collection and transportation of the PPEs generated by community workers, market owners, households, local government, etc. in 14 market area locations in 5 UPs in Ukhia and Teknaf Upazila, including Teknaf Municipality. Aligned with the WASH sector COVID19 Response plan, PPEs will be transported to the closest COVID19 health facility for proper storing/incineration. To avoid the burden of waste in health centers, UNDP together with WHO will support the Health sector on its capacity to deal with the medical waste generated in the COVID19 health centers by constructing/procuring an incinerator for one of the assigned health centers.

Objectives

Obj.3) Ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through hygiene promotion and distribution of hygiene items with strong focus on highly contagious diseases, for all refugees, affected or vulnerable communities of the district.Obj.2) Ensure women, girls, men and boy refugees living in camps, host communities or affected by Covid-19 have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times.

Needs

3.1) Provide access to COVID19 key information and hygiene items to the main market areas, surrounding HH and recycling stakeholders in host communities for prevention against the virus.2.1). Prevent the spread of the disease and potential social tensions caused by PPEs generated in host communities (non in health centers) and dumped in drains and open public spaces. 2.2) Increased the capacity of COVID19 health facilities to incinerate/storage PPEs generated in host communities and other COVID 19 related waste.

Activities or outputs

The activities will be implemented in 14 market areas in Nhilla, Whykong, Teknaf Municipality, Raja Palong and Palong Khali, in coordination with WASH sector and HC coordination platforms in Ukhia and Teknaf:1) Distribution of Hygiene items to 3,317 shop owners, 3,475 households surrounding the markets and 360 recycling sector workers in Host communities. The hygiene package also includes cash for food to 2,280 HH and 360 recycling sector families (in coordination with Food Security sector).2) Operationalisation of 30 hand-washing stations in 14 market areas in Teknaf and Ukhia host communities.3) Outreaching strategy to strengthen hygiene promotion focusing on Covid-19, including door-to-door, miking, radio shows and development of IEC materials.4) Establishment of a safe collection and transportation system of PPEs (masks and gloves) generated by households, market owners, WASH workers, etc. in Host communities in Tenaf and Ukhia Upazilas. Distribution of 150 yellow bins in market areas (pharmacies), local government buildings and other frequented areas, and regular collection and transportation to Ukhia and Teknaf Health Complex (ratio 1 bin for 1,614 HH). Initial consultation to the local government and market bazar, followed by an awareness strategy to engage market owners, housheholds and WASH partners workers, and monitoring system will be operationalized by the NGO partner and community volunteers in each Union and Teknaf Municipality. 5) Joint initiative UNDP-WHO: Procurement and installation of a high.tech Incinerator (I8-M250 – 150kgs per hour / Capacity 2.4m3) to support the COVID19 health facilities and isolation centers.
