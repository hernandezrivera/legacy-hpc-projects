Project Summary

RPN's Response will provide safe, protective, and inclusive and age appropriate learning children aged 3-14 years and adolescent aged 15-24 years focusing on basic education, life skills and vocational training for ensuring refugee children have optimal access to education with psychosocial support in order to improve their learning capacity and minimize their traumatization and develop life skills and vocational skills. Provide awareness education to parents with competencies to enhance positive attitude to child parent interaction and reduce violence within the home. The proposed project will ensure headways or progress among children and youth (aged 3-24) of Rohingya through enhanced basic education and life skills and professional skills training, in cooperation with Humanity Rises and Islamic Help. Safe and equipped learning centers with gender-sensitive WASH facilities will construct. Necessary project staffs i.e Psychologist, Teachers, ECD Caregivers and Teaching & Training Facilitators and administrative staffs will be recruited for smooth operation of the project. Education core materials and training materials will collect for conducting training course. Two shift classes will operate. First shift will start at 09.00 am and end at 12.00 am and second shift will start at 13.00 pm and end at 16.00 pm. First shift for children aged 3-14 will provide non formal education including life skills and 2nd shift for adolescent will provide vocational skills and life skills. Life skill will include different skills like self-esteem, problem solving, creativity, child rights, gender, reproductive health, child marriage, eve teasing, dowry, neglect and exploitation, trafficking, drug addiction and abuse and professional skill will include tailoring and handicraft course. Per course duration will be 156 hours and participant will be 20 adolescent. We will also provide ECD care for refugee children aged 4-5 years.Teaching Facilitator will provide non formal education and Training Facilitator will provide vocational skill training. The Clinical Psychologist will assess individuals to diagnose their mental, emotional and behavioral disorders and then treat them using a variety of techniques, including cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and Social Psychologist will focus on social & education-related issues. H/She will assess in how individuals are impacted by other people, social settings, and social environments and how on devising solutions to social challenges.
Presently RPN has been conducting two center at camp 8E and willing to expand it with 2nd shift with starting vocation training for Rohingya adolescent aged 15-24 years old. In addition we want to establish more than 9 new centers for Rohingya eged 3-14 years old in different parts of camp 8, 9 and 10 and willing to provide basic education, life skill and psychosocial support.

Needs

69% Rohingya children of the camps’ population and 89% of adolescents have been termed by the UN as a “lost generation” for having no access to education including vocational training. Lack of available learning centers and distance to existing learning centers has been identified as key barrier to education by the Education Cluster in Cox’s Bazar. Also existing learning centers are running at full-capacity and are unable to enroll additional school-aged children. There are still a significant number of children and adolescents (boys and girls) not accessing any education. The provision of learning and skills opportunities for adolescent’s boys and girls requires urgent need. Adolescent boys and girls equally need positive engagement into education activities so as to mitigate the risk of early marriage for adolescent girls, human trafficking, and child labor for adolescent boys and adoption or engagement in other negative coping practices.On the other hand, there are huge gap of psychosocial service for Rohingya kids and adolescent boys and girls. To filling the gap, psychosocial support is needed to help them adjust and cope with the social, personal effects of displacement and hopeless. RPN has planned to provide learning and psychosocial service to kids and adolescent boys and girls. RPN also has planned to provide vocational training to adolescent.

Activities or outputs

To achieve the target the following activities will include- Ensure access to equitable learning opportunity of the targeted Rohingya refugee children and adolescent (3-24). Ensure accountability and transparency by developing and practicing various monitoring tool. Construction of safe and equipped learning centers with gender-sensitive WASH facilities. Recruiting of project staffs. Equipping learning centers, learners and teachers with materials. Training teachers, teaching facilitators and caregivers on child safeguarding. Provide psychosocial service to targeted children and adolescent of Rohingya. professional training in tailoring and handicraft for Rohingya Adolescent (15-24) .Formation of School Management Committee (SMC) and sensitized them about protection concern/system. Awareness raising of parents (parenting meeting, session etc),Engaging parents and community in learning outcome assessment and participatory monitoring.
