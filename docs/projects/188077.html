4 - Needs

In 2021, the number of people requiring shelter assistance remained substantial, with 5.88 million people in need. This is attributed to the loss of capital, destruction of housing infrastructure and the limited response. The systematic targeting of social infrastructure including civilian homes has been a major driving force behind mass internal displacement. Families deprived of adequate shelter provision suffer from a wide spectrum of fundamental issues that erode their quality of life, continue to deprive them of essential resources, diminish their physical and mental protection and reduce their overall resilience/ability to respond to con ict-related events. ongoing economic limitations will negatively impact household resilience across all aspects of basic living standards, further reducing families’ ability to pay rent, address shelter issues, meet their basic needs – potentially causing households to resort to harmful coping mechanisms HNO2021 In addition to poor sanitation, exposure to hazardous environmental conditions and lack of safety, families endure psychological instability that arises from lack of privacy within degraded homes, HF had conducted a needs assessment for most of the sub-districts in targeted locations, where we received many appeals from LCs, our team headed to the location and accomplish the rapid assessment where some ndings of the surveys were: It showed a great and urgent need for: - rehabilitation for houses for the IDPs and host community i
