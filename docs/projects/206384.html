Improving access and conditions of basic services in schools for 1,077 direct beneficiaries and 5,772 indirect beneficiaries, this project aims to support access to education through various sectors: (1) improvement of hygiene and sanitation practices in schools to reduce health risks, (2) distribution of educational and recreational materials.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection mainstreaming is an integral part of AeA’s design strategy, this alongside the participatory methods that AeA use to collect information help greatly to identify the risks posed to each demographic. Staff working in the field are aware of the do no harm principle. AeA prioritises preserving safety and dignity in the design of any installation, infrastructure or the planning of any event. Ensuring that there is equitable access for all and encouraging participation in order to empower those we are helping.A multi-sector risk analysis for each component of the project will be made to ensure that all risks are considered and mitigated. As an example early marriages, which are a frequent practice on the sites. In these cases a previous analysis will be carried out and provide extra support for those large families and/or having children under this risk.In addition, AeA will closely coordinate with protection actors in the field in order to attend referrals about GVB or other child protection cases to assure the security, dignity and comfort. In addition, a risk assessment and safety walks will be carried out prior to the intervention in order to identify the safe areas and potential and current risk in the community for the different population groups in order to adapt the design of the action.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

AeA ensures that, during the design phase of its projects, information is gathered on the specific needs of each gender and various vulnerable groups. To achieve this, AeA conducts a socio-spatial analysis to identify the requirements of each demographic (considering age, ethnicity, gender, disability, etc.). This involves exploratory walks, interviews with key informants, focus groups, and direct observation to pinpoint the challenges and risks, including access to public spaces and services, associated with each specific group.AeA then analyzes this data to target groups where it can have the greatest impact, actively coordinating with relevant stakeholders to avoid duplication of efforts. Subsequently, the activities are customized to address the actual needs observed in the field. AeA also monitors these activities, placing a strong emphasis on using a gender-aware lens throughout the process.For this project, major focus will be on children (boys and girls), therefore the empowerment of women is considered crucial.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Throughout the project implementation, households with PwD will be prioritized for assistance, receiving additional support, if needed. Considerations such as functional diversity and other special needs will guide the adaptation of tailored activities. In the context of Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) activities, specific training will be provided with a focus on addressing the needs of PwD. This training will incorporate elements that highlight the unique requirements of individuals with disabilities, preparing both teachers and students to respond effectively in the event of a natural disaster.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Prior to the initiation of development projects, a comprehensive diagnosis of the territory, inclusive of an environmental risk assessment tailored to our actions, is conducted by AeA. The guiding principles of our initiatives revolve around risk management, adaptation, and mitigation. To delineate the scope of our work, we emphasize specific actions such focusing specially on the WASH component.
