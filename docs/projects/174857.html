Project Summary

The crisis that has started in the North West and South West Anglophone regions of Cameroon in October 2013, has caused waves of violence which provoked population movements in the two regions of North West and South West. Thousands of people are victims of Kidnapping, physical assaults, extortion of money, gender-based violence including rape and sexual assaults, destruction of properties, arbitrary arrests and detention, indiscriminate killings mainly of male youth are on the rise since the onset of the crisis. Public and private infrastructures are destroyed limiting access to basic services in both regions. Insecurity, displacement of teachers, threats and attacks on education infrastructure and personnel have left 850,000 school-aged children out of school; 90% of public primary schools and 77% of public secondary schools remain closed.
UNHCR and partners have been assisting to provide protection and assistance to people affected by the crisis. To continue with the response to the crisis is UNHCR is initiating the current projet to alleviate the suffering of at least 300,000 internally displaced persons in the two (02) affected regions in cluding 13 divisions (Nord West – Divisions of Mezam, Mechum, Momo, Bui, Boyo, Donga-Mantung and Ngo Ketunjia; South West – Divisions of Fako, Lebialem, Mémé, Ndian, Lebialem, Koupe Manengouba and Manyu).
To this effect, UNHCR well serve the persons of concern by responding to needs the needs following three strategic objectives;
• Strategic Objective1 - Emergency Preparedness and Response: Set-up of an agile and speedy emergency preparedness and response process, reinforcement of UNHCR’s leadership in protection, shelter/NFIs and CCCM Clusters management and coordination for IDP situations
• Strategic Objective 2 - Response to Critical Protection Needs: Improve access to PoCs and civilians, scale-up of coordinated protection monitoring based on evidence, enhance support to education and livelihood and socio-economic inclusion, prevent and address SGBV, reinforce social cohesion and peaceful coexistence and improve CIMCOORD
• Strategic Objective 3 - Search for Solutions: Facilitate voluntary return, promote reintegration (where possible) for IDPs, enhance resettlement and complementary pathway for refugees, reinforce resilience, livelihoods and socio-economic inclusion.
In this regard, UNHCR intends through the project to pprovide monitoring and registration activities to report on the human rights situation in target regions to humanitarian aid workers and donors for planning and response.
Consequently, activities are planned to be either directly implemented or through partners which are as follows:
 Provision of Legal Assistance
 Expansion and strengthening of protection Monitoring;
 Cash Interventions for Persons with Specifics Needs;
 Support to local authorities for the provision of Civil documentation to about half of the number of internally displaced persons who will have requested UNHCR’s assistance for having lost or never had documents;
 Organization of protection cluster meetings Capacity-building sessions on human-rights law principles;
 Advocacy interventions conducted on behalf of the Protection Sector/Cluster;
 Training of local authorities and traditional leaders to effectively addressed the issues of Humanitarian Law principles (HLP);
 Provision of material, psychosocial support to survivors of BGV and support to community-based structures for the prevention and response to GBV Persons of concern receiving cash for business start-up Persons of Concern receive cash for agriculture/livestock purposes
 Purchases of goods and services, maintenance and repair of vehicles, fuel and lubricants, warehouse management and transport

1 - Besoins / Needs

Protection: The project aims at providi daily protection monitoring on human rights situation in the North West and South West. At the same time reporting and recorded data will contribute to humanitarians and donors planning and response. UNHCR will continue advocacy with different stakeholders to facilitate access to people in need for protection, issuance of civil documents for about 50% beneficiaries who are in need. In this regard, trainings will be organized to build the capacities of local authorities and other stakeholders on: Humanitarian Law Principles, centrality of protection and the guiding principles relating to the protection of internally displaced persons. Similarly, UNHCR will ensure direct interventions in prevention and response to SGBV and strengthen the community-based approach by focusing on the protection of survivors, especially women and children. To this end UNHCR will:  Strengthen the coordination of protection activities through the protection cluster by ensuring regular attendance and reporting of protection activities of at least 60 protection cluster members; Organize at least 10 capacity building sessions on human rights law;  Conduct 24 advocacy activities on behalf of the protection cluster, strengthen local NGOs, CBOs and FBOs in Providing first response to protection needs; Provide legal assistance to 4,000 PoCs in need of legal assistance;  Support 1,200 persons with specific needs through the CBI modality for multipurpose; Support local authorities for provision of 6,000 civil documentation and;  Strengthen its protection monitoring and protection assessments to inform partners on needs. The daily protection monitoring activities will contribute to record and report on the human rights situation in the North West and South West to inform the humanitarians and donors for planning and response. Due to the lack of access and security challenges, remote and mobile monitoring is currently organized in nine out of thirteen divisions. Provide material, psychosocial support to 1,500 survivors of SGBV and support 26 community-based structures for the prevention and response to SGBV. UNHCR will implement direct interventions in prevention and response to GBV and strengthen the community-based approach by focusing on the protection of survivors, especially women and children.  Provide cash grants for business startups to the benefit of 1,000 PoCs for agriculture and livestock purposes as part of its protection response. Peaceful cohabitation: Advocacy and awareness-raising will be carried out in the 13 divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions to mitigate pressure on host communities and promote acceptance of IDPs by their hosts. Peaceful coexistence, and non-violence messages will be communicated. Group discussions and trainings will be also organized on consolidation of peace in the NWSW (“Ambassadors of Peace” in communities; establishment of local social dialogue platforms, etc...) Community mobilization: IDPs and their hosts will be consulted will be involved in programming process from needs assessment to monitoring & evaluation of the protection response. These consultations will be carried out at the stage of identification of protection needs and throughout the implementation to know their priorities and capacities by ensuring their level of satisfaction. Emphasis will be placed on survivors of sexual abuse and exploitationCoordination and management of operations: The efficiency and effectiveness of protection response largely depends on UNHCR's ability to support and guide partners in the implementation and protection delivery.
