Project Name

Support strategic water interventions for increased access to water for Livestock and domestic use and food and nutrition security to pastoralists in dry season livestock grazing areas in arid and semi arid counties affected by severe drought in Kenya.

Start Date

01/01/2023

Project Summary

To strengthen food, nutrition and livelihood security in (agro)pastoral through peaceful co-existence of communities in dry-season grazing areas due to improved availability of water resources, livestock based interventions for enhanced livestock productivity
