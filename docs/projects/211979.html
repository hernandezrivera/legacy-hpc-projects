Project Summary

The overall objectives of livelihood interventions include fostering sustainable reintegration of IDP and refugee returnees who have spontaneously returned to their respective communities, limiting the adoption of harmful coping strategies including strategies that undermine the sustainability of the natural resource base and achieving more coherent and coordinated actions between UNHCR and other mandated stakeholders. This is to be achieved by providing livelihoods support to IDPs and returnees as well as selected members of host communities across Syria. The project contributes to the strategic objectives of the Early Recovery and Livelihoods (ERL) as well as Food security and Agriculture sectors outcomes as follows:
Sustainable livelihoods for households and communities provided through business training, small startup business projects and provision of agriculture inputs, livestock farming.
Access to basic social infrastructure established and increased.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

To improve livelihood opportunities and stimulate local economic activity, UNHCR seeks to provide a comprehensive and coordinated response particularly for the most vulnerable IDPs and IDP/refugee returnees identified through UNHCR community centres in severe locations and who are increasingly compelled to resort to harmful coping mechanisms such as child marriage, child labour, survival sex and temporary marriage. In accordance with UNHCR’s age, gender and diversity (AGD) approach, priority shall be given to individuals from one or more the following population groups single females, women heading households, elderly who increasing becoming the sole or main bread winners, persons with disabilities, GBV survivors and parents whose children have dropped out of school in order to contribute to the family income. Support will be based on the business projects/feasibility for the establishment of a range of businesses/agriculture support as identified by people with support and or consultation provided by partner or directly UNHCR. Support to small start-up business projects will be explored though cash modality to mitigate the risks related to delays and inflation on beneficiaries.
