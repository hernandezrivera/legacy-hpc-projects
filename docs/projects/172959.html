Project Summary

This project responds to critical needs aimed at enhancing protection of civilians through various programmatic responses and in diverse locations. The overall objective of the proposal is to enhance protection of civilians through enhancing community security and protection by strengthening community policing, unarmed civilian protection and strengthening of grassroot committees for protection of civilians. The project will also seek to strengthen the observance of human rights and promote community-based reconciliation approaches.

1 - Needs

Sudan is currently going through a transition phase with the ouster of the former regime of Omar el Bashir. A transitional government is now in place and this has ushered in a semblance of peace in the country leading to reduced security incidents country wide. Although some armed movements have not yet signed the Juba Peace Agreement, there are ongoing efforts by various stakeholders to ensure the success and implementation of the Peace Agreement and this has, in part led to the eventual drawdown and exit of UNAMID in Darfur. In the light of imminent UNAMID departure and as committed, the primary responsibility for Protection of Civilians lies with the Government of Sudan (TGoS). Though under the peace agreements, the TGoS and parties to these agreements committed to form 12,000 joint security forces and deploy for protection of civilians, returnees, IDPs, and host communities in Darfur that are affiliated to SLA/AW and who are not party to the peace agreements will not feel safe and be left without adequate protection and subject to potential security risks. Furthermore, with the conclusion of the peace agreement in Juba, it is envisaged that many IDPs and refugees will start to return to areas of origin. However, besides the obvious security risks that might emanate from UNAMID exit, there is a likelihood of remnants of unexploded ordinances and various munitions lying uncollected in return areas where fighting took place. Similar situations of returnees with a need to protection is expected in South Kordofan, Blue Nile and the East. Therefore, there will be need for establishment of grassroot committees for protection of civilians, at least in several localities in Darfur and the Two Areas, besides training and elevating the capacity of community protection networks. Such an intervention is vital for guaranteeing protection of civilians through building trustful relationships between IDPs/ host communities, governmental and grassroots committee members as well as through construction of physical facilities for grassroots committees, where the volunteers (GCV) i. This approach helps instill public confidence in security forces as well as providing community level protection to citizens. In the context of SCR2524, Unarmed Civilian Protection is assuming increasing importance, especially in the context that is defined by the Juba Peace Agreement and the approach of UCP will help in reducing the negative impacts of the exit of UNAMID Peacekeepers.

Enhanced community security and protection by strengthening community policing, Unarmed Civilian Protection and community dispute settlement mechanisms; 1.1 Promote community policing activities through recruitment of community policing volunteers, identification and training of CPV.1.2 Promotion of unarmed civilian protection through deployment of international protection monitors embedded and working with local youth in 12 locations;1.3. Undertake sensitization workshops and advisory services on issues associated with demining and removal of exploded ordinances at state and locality levels; 1.5 Crop protection through farm protection committees; Output 2.1 Awareness raising of the targeted beneficiaries; Output 3. 1 Enhanced protection of human rights and strengthened local conflict resolution mechanisms, including on land issues; 3.2 Awareness raising of the targeted beneficiaries.
