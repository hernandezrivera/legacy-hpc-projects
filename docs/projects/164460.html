Project Summary

This project aims to improve the management quality in and accountability of 32 IDP sites in Kelly cluster, and better advocate for needs in these sites. this objective correlates to the following CCCM objectives: CCCM Objective 2: Improve the management quality and accountability in IDP sites CCCM Objective 4: Disseminate operational information on IDP figures and movements on a timely basis
Establishment Camp Management system. Furthermore, Mercy-USA will implement the following activities in the same camps:
1. CCCM training, including on the job for the purpose of exit strategy on long term
2. Contingency planning (fire fighting system, evacuation plan, etc)
3. Coordination and monitoring services provided

4 - Needs

Based on IDPs Site Integrated Monitoring Matrix(ISIMM) December 2019: there are 588 IDP sites listed in the CCCM database, which host a total of 920,747 IDPs/ 169,269 families out of them 3,288 IDPs with special needs, 80 % out of the population are women and children. out of them 32 IDPs sites in kelly cluster which were recently established after huge waves of displacement due to intensification of violence in south Idleb.Those IDPs sites are self established due to limited available lands, so they need urgently for establishing participatory management committees for better management and coordination between all NGOs as well as better information dissemination.Due to the informal nature and the way these communities have sprung up in pockets of perceived safety, there is often a complete lack of management, and those communities which are managed either by landowners, local councils or other stakeholder who may be even less inclined to adhere to humanitarian standards. Often these communities are vulnerable to being taken advantage of for labor. Women and young girls are particularly vulnerable in informal settlements. Risks include, marginalization, sexual violence and exploitation due to the imbalance of power. There are dire needs to help these community members organize themselves by forming participatory management structures and providing them with the tools and guidance they need in order to represent and advocate for themselves. This is also very important because it helps humanitarian actors establish needs in these locations and arrange for effective and targeted humanitarian interventions and support. Another huge challenge in this communities is keeping track of the population and trends of displacement across Syria. Often when the conflict escalates elsewhere, we see huge waves of IDPs, and it is challenging to track where they are going to end up.Therefore,it is important to establish systems and provide communities with tools to track movement

5 - Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA will held meeting with each IDPs site to nominate representatives, then all IDPs representatives will have a training about participatory management approach and the output of this training will be establishing of participatory management committees for the whole kelly cluster. This committees will be responsible for timely sharing IDPs movement from and to kelly cluster, as well as coordination with all NGOs to ensure provision humanitarian assistance based on need and to avoid overal might happen.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk1: Refusion of the community for women participation in the committees Mitigation: Conducting of Awareness sessions for the community and good communication with local councils to avoid any issues in advance. Monitoring: Mercy-USA field staff will oversee and monitor the achievement of the workplan on daily basis-- Risk2: Difficulties in the implementation due to huge distance between camps and even between the tents in the same camp- Mitigation : dedication of two field staff in each camp to maintain proper implementation- Monitoring: Project manager will make field visits to the different camps on regular basis and feedback-complaints mechanism in place.-- Risk3: Sexual Exploitation and Abuse- Mitigation: Training for all staff about PSEA - Monitoring: Mercy-USA will have complaints and feedback mechanism in place and the SEA complaints will be treated with high confidentiality and referral system will be in place for such cases to PSEA focal in the area .

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: The management structures could provide legitimacy to actors who are not truly representative of the IDP communitiesAssumption 1: The management structures will be representative and inclusive.Mitigation Risk 1: Mercy-USA will ensure that any management structure is representative of the entire community with strong selection criteria.Risk 2: Individual residents may over-rely on any civil response committee for responding to a natural disaster and may shirk individual responsibility.Assumption: local efforts has the capacity to respond to any natural disaster Mitigation 2: 1.) Any dedicated DRR response (such as a training of first responders) incorporates general awareness raising focused on the individuals capacities of men, women, and children Ensure that training indicatives include sessions on DNH and protection mainstreaming.
