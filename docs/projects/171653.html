Project Summary

According to post Gu’ FSNAU report released in September 2020, over three million people are in need of humanitarian assistance. Shocks related to floods, outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic and infestation of Desert locust in parts of the country worsened the situation for vulnerable communities who have not fully recovered from the past shocks. Women and children are the most affected population in Somalia. According to the national Education Cluster, over 1.4 million school age children are in need of humanitarian support. The nation-wide closure of schools due to COVID-19 has led the Education Cluster and its partners to completely alter their response. This included reducing face-to-face trainings and suspending transitional learning spaces (TLS ) construction. However, many activities are similar to the usual EiE response but delivered through different modalities. Significant changes have included the development of distance learning programs (Radio, online, TV), remote PSS through head teachers’ network, increased focus on WASH rehabilitation and less focus on general TLS construction.
SOYDA aims to undertake a key emergency intervention targeting 3,000 (1500 girls, 1500 boys including 15% those with disability) to assure access and continuity of education to crises affected children in 2 districts of Benadir (Daynile and Kahda) and 2 Districts of Lower shabelle region (Merka and Afgooye).
The project will provide enhanced school feeding program to the 3,000 (1500 girls, 1500 boys including 15% those with disability) vulnerable children to enable improved their nutritional and health status as well as ensure basic school WASH facility through construction of twin latrines for the purpose of providing enabling learning environment to both girls and boys as well as ensuring better protective learning environment, the project will also provide dignity kits(sanitary towels) to the adolescent girls that will provided retention of these vulnerable girls within the learning centers. The project will sustain the current operation of the 10 SOYDA running schools in Daynile, Kahda, Merka and Afgooye districts and also SOYDA will expand to add 12 more additional Temporary learning space (TLS) in the event teachers to student ration exceed the recommended 1:40 students per INEE standard (with planning for 1.5 meter spacing protocols) to avoid degradation of existing facilities in time for school reopening will strictly follow the needs of both boys, girls and disable group among the target children hence ensuring better protective environment to all learners. The project will also focus on the enrolment of boys and girls living with disabilities. Communities’ and school managements’ awareness on importance of sending children living with disabilities to schools.
Through this project, SOYDA will Provide clean water for enrolled learners as well as build the capacity of 40 teachers on pedagogy, child rights and protection, 60 CEC’s on school management, and education stakeholders on the other hand will be trained on Education in Emergencies to equip them with knowledge and skills to ensure coordinated support and sustenance of learning during crises Child protection and gender principles will be mainstreamed across all activities during implementation. To ensure transparency, sustainability and community participation in the project, SOYDA will work closely with the Ministry of Education (MoE), camp leadership and school management by involving them at all levels of activity implementation.
The proposed project will serve to complement our ongoing Education, WASH, Health, Nutrition and Protection projects in the target districts and also enhance gains so far made and as well build up implementation strategies based on prior experience in what has worked and what has not worked on as continuation of education interventions and, scaled up for more vulnerable localities identified during the life of the project.

Project Activities

1. Support and sustain the existing ongoing 10 schools in Daynile, Kahda, Merka and Afgooye districts2. The project will construct a total of 12 new TLS or rehabilitate existing classrooms in IDP settlements with priority in IDP settlements in Kahda and Merka district with complete with child-friendly and inclusive (incl. children with disabilities) WASH (including COVID-19) facilities3. SOYDA through this project will provide school feeding program to a total of 3,000 (1500 girls, 1500 boys including 15% those with disability) vulnerable children from most affected IDPs and host communities in Merka, Afgooye, Daynile and Kaxda districts. The school feeding is intended improving the learner’s attendance, retention and nutrition status. The school management committee will be solely responsible on school feeding program.4. The project will install 10,000-liter capacity plastic water in each of the five target IDPs and host community schools, this will ensure a total of 3,000 (1500 girls, 1500 boys including 15% those with disability) children benefits from clean drinking water within the learning environment. 5. The project will provide emergency incentive at rate of $100 per month to 40 teachers while 15 school administrators will be paid a total incentive of $150 per months for 15 school months. The incentive will ensure the schools to exempt school fee from the poor students whose parents cannot afford to pay the fee. It will also help teacher motivation, retention and the overall improvement of the school operation including minimizing the risk of school closure due to the drought impact. It will also increase teacher’s improved services delivery within the learning environment. 6. The project will provide 5 days capacity building training on education in emergency for 40 teachers in 4 target districts in order to improve their skills and knowledge in student- centered teaching methodologies and supporting students during crisis. Federal government MOE supervisors will provide follow-up support and mentoring the trained teachers in order to help them adopt the acquired skills and knowledge 7. SOYDA will conduct regular community mobilization and back to school campaigns that will be conducted in all the target IDPs and host community schools throughout the project lifespan, this will ensure to improve the school enrollment, retention. The community education committee will be responsible on the community mobilization activities. SOYDA will also employ 10 volunteers (5 men and 5 women) from the IDPs to sensitize and mobilize the IDP parents to enroll their children particularly girls to the schools through visiting house-to-house, community gathering squares and mosque8. SOYDA will provide Hygiene promotion sessions In order to minimize the risk of cholera/AWD outbreak in the target areas, the project will increase community knowledge and awareness on hygiene issues through regular hygiene promotion campaigns. 9. SOYDA will train selected 60 (30 male, 30 female) CEC’s of the both public and private schools on their roles and responsibilities in school management. This training is critical for them to be able to effectively support the head teachers and teachers for effective running of the schools. 10. Support Child to Child protection activities in targeted schools 11. Provide Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) and Kits to 1000 adolescent girls.

Mitigation Measures

SOYDA is currently working in Merka district, Afgooye and Afgooye corridor and Mogadishu IDPs in different location providing life-saving emergency service, however, the following are the main risk issues being faced by the beneficiary on daily basis and their mitigation measure, that is recruitment of teachers and student into militia group: There is high likelihood that school going children being entice with worldly materials and cash to attract them into militia group, this have hindered the country security over a long period hence SOYDA intend to make sure such issues are dealt with through active community engagement/consultation and social mobilization/awareness on the negative impact of such verses Conflict of interest among CEC and school management risk factor– The management and committee might divert the schools food stuff and equipment for personnel gain and as well mishandle the schools infrastructure design in construction thus poor quality services to the vulnerable boys and girls in the target location, however, SOYDA will ensure affective and strict monitoring and reporting as well as proper management of the schools infrastructure. Distance and location of school is also risk based on exclusion of disable and marginalized communities to minimize such disparities SOYDA will construct temporary learning space within the settlement. The following risk of exclusion based on gender or age such factors are limited capacity among the trained female teachers, within the education sector might hampered the inclusion of women in the school staffing as well as management role hence the might provide room for men dominant. To mitigate this SOYDA shall ensure to capacitate the female teachers and provide them equal chance on both management, community education committee as well as ensure enrollment of more girl child within the target learning centers in Afgooye and Mogadishu IDPs and host communities. Information gap, discrimination, gender disparities and infrastructural design of learning environment further contribute the exclusion risk factors facing the target population. To mitigate this SOYDA will embark on social mobilization and information dissemination through public Barraza radio show for larger coverage on community education enlighten, hence this will provide better avenue for both enrollment and increasing the value for girl child education SOYDA will also ensure to identify the most needy group and provide more responsive intervention by providing the material needs for both girls, boys, children with disabilities, minority communities, children belonging to disadvantaged or marginalized groups and work with children, teachers, community leaders and parents to support equal and safe access to education without discrimination.it will provide adequate community sensitization and monitoring and coordinating with stakeholders that will improve both retention and enrollment, SOYDA however, intends to provide friendly schools toilet as well as easy adaptation of schools facilities by disable and physically impaired persons hence provide better learning material that shall enable to provide adequate access and safe learning facility.
