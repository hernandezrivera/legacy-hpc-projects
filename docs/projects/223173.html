Project Summary

Project Summary will be one per organization (including GP, GBV and CP)
HI will contribute to increase the self-reliance and coping mechanisms for vulnerable persons, including persons with disabilities in Mozambique, by providing access to a comprehensive package of services (physical rehabilitation and mental health) while crosscutting a disability and inclusion mainstreaming approach in the 1 province where HI is intervening in the scope of the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP), in Cabo Delgado.
The aim of the project is
i) to work with IDPs (including people with disabilities) host communities to; promote access to quality, improved referral and coordination mechanism across all sectors of Humanitarian intervention;
ii) to work with Humanitarian Actors to Increase their capacity to mainstream disability and collect relevant data to measure disability inclusion within their activities and address the immediate effects of armed violence through conflict sensitivity and risk education.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The aim is to work with IDPs (including people with disabilities), returnees, host communities and families exposed to violence and adversity to access to increase the self-reliance and coping mechanisms for vulnerable persons, including persons with disabilities in Mozambique, by providing access to a comprehensive package of services (physical rehabilitation and mental health) and by improving the referral pathway and coordination with partners for a meaningful access to services adressing the specific needs of persons with disabilities and the most vulnerable. As long as working with Humanitarian Actors (or Partners) to Increase their capacity to mainstream disability inclusion and ( EORE) risk education for behavior change in their activities and collect relevant data to advocate and provide the specific services required for persons with disabilities mainly children as an active service can be lifesaving and prevent for further cognitive or motor impact, and elders, as they are the less targeted and covered by the humanitarian actors.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection is mainstreamed throughout all the activities. HI due to its missionally, is very sensitive to identify protection risks, and set a referral pathway to address them. The active participation at the protection clusters allows to identify risks and coordinate with other partners for an effective and meaningful access to services for the most vulnerable. Furthermore, HI conducts a risk assessment , to identify potential risks ( a. Breach of Safeguarding policies and Code of Conduct; b. Sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment ; c. Breach of Child protection policy; etc) For each risk identified HI set’s up a package of mitigation measures, these include: All staff will receive refresher training on the Code of Conduct and Safeguarding policies, including PSEAH and child protection, and will sign revised documents in their personal files.; community members will be consulted on the design, adaptation and implementation of the complaints mechanism, where appropriate, and will be consulted on the handling of complaints including by conference call sessions; The recruitment and selection of staff to work directly with children will include other child protection elements throughout the process (e.g. child protection mentioned in the work description, reference checks, interview questions, etc.)

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Activity implementation will be operationalized under a people based approach. The dialogue with targeted groups and community members throughout the implementation is crucial to ensure the understanding of overall and specific needs and also the awareness raising on key factor for inclusion. Community engagement and participation is one of those key factors.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

HI’s mandate is to prioritize persons with disabilities as the main target population throughout all the activities and ensure meaningful participation. HI identified, assess, provide direct service and refer for those services not covered by HI, to ensure that persons with disabilities will receive adequate assistance to respond to their specific needs. A referral pathway is very needed to ensure a comprehensive response. stakeholders A follow-up system will be set up to receive feedback from beneficiaries referred and confirm if they received the adequate assistance. Beneficiaries will be informed about their right to equitable and safe services and assistance as well as on the selection criteria. HI will consider the factors and barriers limiting the participation of beneficiaries to make sure all have equal opportunities true social cohesion activities that included communities' active participation.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project activities are planned to have a limited environmental impact. For the teacher training activities, the use of disposable materials will be avoid and the team will use participatory methodologies instead. Additionally, the selection of the supplies to be provided to children and adolescents will take into account the environmental practices from supplies and the criteria of the use of durable materials, quality and long-lasting devices will be prioritized.
