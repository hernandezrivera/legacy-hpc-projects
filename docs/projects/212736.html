Project Summary

The ongoing crisis and Feb 2023 Turkey and Syria earthquake have resulted in a significant number of IDPs in need of safe and dignified shelters. In response to this pressing need, Human Appeal Turkey propose initiative to create the Al-Yasamin IDPs Site project. This project involves the direct implementation by Human Appeal to build 1,600 dignified shelter in Maarrat Misrin sub-district, specifically in the Kafr Jales community.
First Phase: This phase focuses on the construction of 400 shelter blocks, with each block comprising four dignified shelters. A total of 1,600 shelters will be built, complete with a sewage system and main drinking water network.
Each shelter measures 40 square meters, containing two main rooms, a kitchen with a table, a lavatory, and a foyer. A 6 square meter green area will be present in front of each shelter entrance. The design of these shelters is intended to provide a comfortable living space for the IDPs while prioritizing their privacy and dignity.
Second Phase: The aim of the second phase is to improve the site through road leveling, gravelling, and lighting, as well as implementing a rainwater drainage system. These enhancements will ensure that the IDPs have easy access to their shelters and that the site remains functional during different weather conditions. Human Appeal is currently seeking funding for this phase, and if secured, work will begin immediately, running concurrently with the first phase. This will enable the Al-Yasamin IDPs site to receive beneficiaries as soon as possible.
Third Phase: The final phase of Al - Yasamin IDPs site project will focus on constructing service buildings for the dignifies shelters, which may include school, primary health care center, and a marketplace. The goal is to create a self-sufficient community that caters to the essential needs of the IDPs, enabling them to lead dignified lives and have access to vital services.
At present, Human Appeal has secured funding for the first phase 1,500 shelters of 1,600, which covers the shelter units, sewage system, and drinking water network and water source.
