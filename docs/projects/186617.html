Project Summary

Through the project WFP will provided unconditional food support to 115,000 Nigerian refugees, IDPs in the Far North region including 5,000 contingency caseloads. In line with WFP Cameroon Country Strategic Plan (CSP 2022-2026), life-saving assistance will be provided Nigerian refugees in Minawao camp and IDPs that are located in Mayo Sava, Mayo Tsanaga and Logone and Chari Divisions. Food assistance through in-kind and cash-based transfers will be provided to 73,000 Nigerian refugees, and 27,000 IDPs. The displaced that moved from their villages located Nigeria that were established in Minawao camp for the refugees IDPs that fled from the border villages with Nigeria and that sought safer places within Mayo Sava, Mayo Tsanaga and Logone and Chari Divisions. A 10-month food assistance to the beneficiaries will be delivered through monthly unconditional in-kind food transfer to 100,000 beneficiaries. The full ration of the in-kind distributions will provide 2,100 kcal energy per day per person for the beneficiaries who entirely rely on humanitarian food assistance. Daily in-kind ration are 370g for cereals. 120g for pulses, 40g for vegetable oil and 5 g for iodized salt.
Cash transfers will be provided to 15,000 IDPs with a monthly ration of 8,500 XAF per beneficiary to support them to access adequate and nutritious food. A 10-month full ration assistance will contribute to prevent the IDPs from adopting negative coping strategies during the farming period.
For the conception of projects, beneficiaries’ opinions have been considered through focus group discussions, gender and protection evaluation field missions, monitoring exercises. These gave beneficiaries opportunities to state their preferences and transfer modalities and opinions on targeting criteria. The preparatory activities are inclusive, WFP ensures that women, men, girls and boys are involved in various stages such as vulnerability targeting process, for in-kind and cash transfers and monitoring. WFP in collaboration with local authorities and partners, conduct assessments to identify IDPs to be considered for assistance based on WFP vulnerability criteria. These criteria included among others household size, presence of under-5 children, the elderly and handicapped, women-headed households, assets.
After distributions monitoring will be conducted through Cooperating Partners and attention will placed on the role played by men, women, girls and boys including decision-making on food consumption and food security in the household.
Through the established complaints and feedback mechanisms, beneficiary IDPs will freely express their views and concerns on the project implementation. Their views will be considered for project adjustment. Through sensitization, WFP will promote women and men equal participation in decision-making and social cohesion between host and displaced communities.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The humanitarian situation in the Far North Region has been of a concern mostly due to attacks by Boko Haram armed groups that prompted the displacements in Nigeria and Cameroon. Nigerian refugees are established in Minawao camp while IDPs are settled in several locations in the Fra North regions. IDPs affected by the crisis are struggling to survive in difficult conditions after abandoning all their properties and livelihood assets. They are living with host families in various safer locations in villages or towns, putting enormous pressure on already scarce community resources and the local economy. The most affected divisions of Logone-et-Chari, Mayo Tsanaga and Mayo Sava are the most food insecure areas of the far north region. The presence of over 400,000 displaced people mainly IDPs (341,535), returnees (23%) and out of camp Refugees (11%) has put more pressure on the natural resources and other economic assets of these areas. The arrival of IDPs in food-insecure areas has severely affected host communities. Boko Haram attacks have disrupted the economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in the Far North region, causing a depletion of livelihoods and household assets. Therefore, the existing chronic vulnerability and structural food insecurity have been aggravated. The region is predominantly arid and regularly hit by drought and seasonal floods. The most recent inter community clashes in Logone and Chari division has led to over 12,000 IDP . The October 2021 Cadre Harmonisé highlighted that to over 900,000 people projected to be food insecure from June to August 2022 in the Far North. Out of this number, 380,143 people are severely food insecure, which means that they have limited or no access to sufficient, nutritious food required to live a healthy life. The Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) reports that vulnerable families in the conflict-affected areas of the Far North Region are experiencing ‘Stress’ level of acute food insecurity due to flood and pest-related crop damage, and also attacks by Boko Haram. As food insecurity affects differently men, women, girls and boys of various ages, food assistance through cash-based transfers will prioritize the most affected IDPs. In addition, access to land, resources, income-generating activities; credit, seeds have an impact on the economic situation of the household and on food security. Special attention will be paid on households with people specific needs and necessary support will be provided taking into consideration of the situation of the head of the household (whether male or female or child); the presence of a person with specific needs (pregnant woman, elderly person, disabled person etc.)
