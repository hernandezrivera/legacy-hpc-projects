Project Summary

The project has the objective to improve the malnutrition situation of IDPs and the vulnerable resident population of the target areas and provide basic health and hygiene services, through training and awareness raising activities on the importance of a balanced diet and the provision of basic material to health facilities and WASH services.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will be implemented in the districts of Mossuril, Mecufi and Pemba and has the objective to improve the quality of life in terms of nutrition and deliverance of basic services for the IDPs and resident population of the target areas. Malnutrition in the districts is rampant, specifically among children under 5 years of age and vulnerable groups. Among the problems is the lack of knowledge and awareness of the mothers and the population in general on proper nutrition habits, and on access and correct use of supplementary food for SAM and MAM children. The situation of the IDPs is even more precarious being displaced from their homes and suffering the lack of livelihood means. The precarious health system often does not have the necessary means to serve the population and IDPs in an adequate way and provide adequate cures and/or treatments for the problems presented by users and tackle the malnutrition situation. The project will elaborate a strategy in close collaboration with the Direção Provincial de Saúde – DPS and the Serviço Provincial de Saúde - SPS, in order to identify and address specific needs at District level and support the local authorities to improve the nutrition state of the target population. Target groups: - children of IDP families and local population of target area affected by malnutrition that will be assisted and adequately treated; - women, specifically mothers that will develop knowledge and awareness on the problems affecting their children and will learn how to and the importance of providing a balanced diet; - health personnel that will participate on capacity development and increase their technical knowledge - brigadas móveis that will be provided with basic material for the treatment and cure of population's ordinary needs and children affected of malnutrition; resident population and IDPs of target areas that will receive nutritional messages and will be provided adequate and improved health services

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Implement active case finding and referral of malnourished children (>5) and monitor the nutrition situation through MUAC screenings (Mecufi, Mossuril, Pemba); Undertake nutrition assessments and SMART surveys for assessing the nutritional status of the populations at risk; diagnostic and treatment of SAM and MAM cases in collaboration with districts SDSMASs and DPS; ; Strengthen the CHWs (APEs and ACS) in treatment of acute malnutrition at community level; Promote optimal breastfeeding and complementary feeding practices, and overall Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) best practices messages for caregivers of children under two either though awareness sessions and cooking demonstration in the communities with high influx of IDPs (including host families); Training of activists / volunteer mothers for home monitoring of children with malnutrition; Trainings to key women and activists on nutritious good practices including implementation of cooking demonstrations events, food preparation and food conservation; Nutritional messages disseminations related to IYCF and HIV through community radio, novellas, banners, etc.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The main risk is the “Possible distrust and difficult acceptance from the part of the beneficiaries of the adoption of new nutritional behaviors”. The project identified the following solutions: The introduction of new nutritional practices will be made after consultations with the principal stakeholders and community leaders ensuring a better acceptance from everyone Involvement of the community leaders on the diffusion of the messages. In fact, they have significant potential to influence the cultural norms. Holding single-sex, age segmented focus discussion group sessions on constrains to their behavior change. Cooking demonstrations and promotion of healthy dietary practices valorizing traditional knowledge and local food culture and keeping in consideration food taboos, preferences and consumption patterns. The action follows a participatory approach for the design and implementation of activities. The action will ensure meaningful access to assistance through activities such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection. The project will empower the women to act as the first agent of change in their own communities. Specifically, the intervention methodology aims at increasing the capacities of the women to interconnect and transfer knowledge and experience by building their awareness on the nutritional issue and by creating examples of good nutritional practices in the communities themselves.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of local institutional stakeholders (Serviço Distrital de Saúde Mulher e Acção Social - SDSMAS, Direcção Distrital de Saude - DPS) and target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better nutrition conditions. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The Serviço Distrital de Saúde will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities. All the activities have been structured with the aim of guaranteeing access to disable people, such as the selection of the location for the training and the project staff will guarantee all the support to allow them to participate in the actions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project does not to produce any negative environmental impact, using reusable plates and glasses, and not distributing disposable materials/packaging.
