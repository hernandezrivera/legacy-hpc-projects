Project Summary

The insurgency affecting North East of Nigeria has left many communities wanting as families have recorded loss of lives and means of livelihoods. One of the most significant attributes of the insurgency which has ravaged Nigeria’s North East region since 2009, is the widespread destruction of infrastructure and basic services, with some entire communities completely broken down. According to IOM DTM (Data Tracking Matrix) Round 26, a total number of 1,325,776 crisis affected persons (IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities) have been displaced. In which Adamawa accounts for 153,229, Yobe, 156,681 and Borno, 1, 015,865. Of this number, adolescent girls and women are made up of 597,184 and 446,686 respectively. This alarming situation has placed many households especially adolescent girls and single female headed household at risk of protection concerns such as sexual exploitation and Abuse, rape, and trafficking. several Boys, girls, men and women are abducted, enrolled into non-state armed group (NSAG) to be used as suicide bombers while others have been killed. This situation has interchanged gender roles whereby women are single female headed household with several children under their custody. Thus, are single female, adolescent girls are exposed to undignified and harmful practices. The insufficiency in livelihood opportunities for women leads to underproduction, scarcities of stapled foodstuff in the existing local market coupled with the fluctuations of prices hence under minding the purchasing power of vulnerable persons affected by the insurgency. This is the cause of malnutrition cases as the nutrition sector reports the GAM rate at 11.2, 12.4 in some localities. Furthermore, the sporadic attacks of the insurgence and military operations have limited movement of persons with restriction of farming lands to 5 kilometers around. This drastically affects the accessibility to WASH services and congestions of existing infrastructures with incidence on hygiene promotion, open defecation and reported cases of rape in insufficient sanitation. The limitation in accessing basic infrastructure and services due attacks, flood and fierce stormy winds, have affected shelters and expose women, girls and adolescent girls to risks of rape as they lack dignified access to assistance.
In order to rehearse dignity of vulnerable women, girls, boys, men, improve access to Quality GBV services and strengthen their resilience, AHA shall provide standardized dignity kits, access to quality PSS to women, girls, boys, men of reproductive age as well as strengthening their resilience through livelihood support activities.

AHA shall also collect from the communities their perception regarding the program implementation through single and collective reporting.
