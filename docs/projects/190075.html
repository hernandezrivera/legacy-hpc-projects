Project Summary

Since its beginning, in November 2020, the ongoing armed conflict in Tigray region in the northern part of Ethiopia has spilled over to the neighboring regions of Amhara and Afar and resulted in severe humanitarian needs of conflict-affected and displaced population in all three regions. The conflict has seen several stages of expansion and withdrawal, fighting taking place also in Amhara and Afar and forcing people to flee their homes. Most recently (January 2022), the conflict has been mostly confined to Tigray region, opening Amhara and Afar for humanitarian interventions and support. There are estimated 400,000 Internally Displaced
Persons (IDPs) in Amhara region only. The operation proposed by FRC will be jointly implemented with Action for the Needy Ethiopia (ANE), a well-known national NGO, and will target four woredas in South Wollo, that hosts IDPs as well as receives a constantly growing number of returnees. Basic humanitarian needs of at least 30,000 people will be addressed; IDPs still present in the area and people now returning to their homes in the project area will be equally targeted. The intervention takes a multi-sector approach, combining 1) cash for
rent for improving access to housing for 320 households (HHs), 2) improved shelter and dignified living through distribution of Emergency Shelter (ES) and Non-Food Items (NFI) relief Kits for 700 HHs, 3) provision of safe water for the population in the target area (for at least 25,000 beneficiaries) 4) best hygiene practice awareness raising and promotion, reaching approximately 15,000 people, and 5) Gender Based Violence response, which includes dignity kit distribution (for 320 women and girls of reproductive age), strengthening of the coordination
of referral services for GBV survivors, and GBV awareness raising targeting both men and women (at least 7,500 people).

1. - Projects activities

Provision of Water treatment chemicals include: Assess water quality of targeted Woreda water systems, Procure required water treatment chemicals, Train Woreda hygiene and water experts on Infection, Prevention and Control (IPC) & chlorine solution preparation & disinfection, Distribute water treatments to technical bureau of target woredas & Monitor regularly water quality in cooperation with woreda technical bureau. Hygiene promotion include: Select and train community hygiene promoters, Prepare, print and disseminate IEC and Behavior Change communication materials on best hygiene and sanitation practices, Organize community awareness raising events on hygiene and sanitation best practices & Carry out Hygiene Behavior Change activities through community hygiene promoters .

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

ANE will work in consultation and collaboration with all relevant actors and stakeholders at both national and sub-national levels in the design and implementation of this program. ANE attends coordination at the national and district level and provides updates on its activities and responses, specifically with the WASH at Regional coordination cluster and Federal government level. This ensures that programming follows the government’s standards and guidelines and ANE activities are also reported in the 4Ws&5Ws reports. Target areas are discussed with the cluster at all level to avoid any duplication amongst agencies as well as referring potential gaps.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?
