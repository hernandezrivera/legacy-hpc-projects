Project Summary

The project aims to provide WASH services including expand the water network to conclude the three camps in the area (Alkarama camp – Alduaa camp – Alrahma camp) beside making upgrade and rehabilitation to both water station and water network in the seven villages ( Shiekh Bahr - Bir Altaib - Tel Doir - Kharab Khais - Khassarat - Alatah - Kafr-Taanor ) that provided with drinking water from two water stations located in Sheikh Bahr community, supply of drinking water, solid waste removal, desludging, hygiene kits distribution, hygiene promotion to 34312 beneficiaries of Sheikh Bahr villages and camps in Idleb governorate. SAED will be committed to the humanitarian standards and criteria of SPHERE and the Syrian code in the implementation in such projects.

4 - Needs

Saed charity conducted a rapid assessment in those locations to verify the number of population and to check on the available services to ensure no overlapped activities are included in this project. Methodology:Two assessment were conducted as follow:- Technical assessment: This assessment was based on the direct observation of technical staff as a result of a field visit to each of the targeted 10 locations.Key findings:> 32 functional public water tanks with different capacities are noticed in 3 camps > 7 normal latrines are available in 3 camps which need some minor repairs.> 5 villages have functional water system.> 7 septic tanks are available in 3 camps with different capacities - Household-level assessment: This assessment is based on WASH cluster questionnaire for villages and camps and informal settlements. A total of 93 household visits were conducted with one male and one female interviewers in 10 targeted locations (camps and villages).* Key findings:> 93% of interviewed families in the camps responded that their main water source is water trucks while 7% responded that they are receiving water from public tap stands.> 81% of interviewed families in the villages responded that their main water source is the water network while 19% responded that they are receiving water from water trucking.> 51% of interviewed families are not differentiating between drinking water and water for personal hygiene. > 17% of interviewed families are not receiving enough water. > 18% of interviewed families in the camps did not receive water in the past two days. > 86% from interviews families stated that the garbage is left in public areas while 14% stated that the garbage is disposed of by the family to a dumping location.

5 - Implementation of activities

- Coordination with main stakeholder in the project and taking opinions of beneficiaries by conducting FGDs for women and men separately taking into consideration the people with disabilities and elderly. - Conducting field needs assessments to the water network in the targeted locations and determine type and size of required intervention to make list of quantities.- Contracting with experienced contractors according to SCA policies and putting the technical specifications and ensure safety and security measures especially GBV ones.- Implementing the rehabilitation and maintenance operations on 3 levels: 1. Making rehabilitation to water network already exists in the in the targeted village where the network just required.2. Establish new water network in the parts of villages where are no water networks exist.3. Establish water network to cover the three targeted camps to ensure providing sustained drinking water to the IDPs.- For the camps, in the first 3 months of the project where the water network is under construction, Saed will provide 7612 IDPs in the targeted camps with drinking water through trucks with amount of 25 Liter/person/day to ensure drinking water supplies will not affected.- Saed will construct 76 blocks of latrines (70 normal and 6 special) (2 cells per block for normal latrines and one cell block for disabled IDPs) in the targeted camps.Saed will remove solid waste from both targeted villages and camps and remove the waste to formal landfills; this is a crucial step. Saed will implement desludging the waste water from septic tanks in the targeted camps Saed will distribute trash bins in both targeted villages and camps to prevent littering. Important hygiene promotion will take place in gender-segregated groups, accompanied by the distribution of 11000 hygiene kits for two rounds.- Monitoring and Evaluation: it includes the monitoring and evaluation of daily activities of maintenance and rehabilitation activities.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: The activities are done according to the planned timeline. Risk: Because of the weather in winter it may causes a delay in some activities.Mitigation: when designing work plan and the timeline we will consider the weather and its effect. Assumption: All roads are open and there is full safe access to targeted location\s Risk: Road closures and lack of access to targeted locations.Mitigation: Delay the distribution for several days pending the calm situation – develop an alternative plan to deliver assistance to the beneficiaries in the new situation – ask suppliers to secure the material long enough.Assumption: Authorities approved taken. Risk: Not giving the permission to implement the project from Local council and / or the forces on the ground.Mitigation: coordination with local council from the first beginning of the project, also they are included in the project design phase. Assumption: the cost of materials in the project is not changing.Risk: Raise at prices of materials in local markets. Mitigation: Procurement shall be done for one time whether from Syria or Turkey. Assumption: contractors have finished their assigned works as the contract.Risk: The contractor cannot fulfill his/her commitments. Mitigation: there shall be a contract to implement activities with more than one contractor at the same time.Assumption: Good collaboration with the IDPs.Risk: - Major increase in fuel price and exchange rates.- Increase in the IDPs numbers due to huge displacement from fighting areas.Mitigation: - Close monitoring of the fuel prices in the implementation area, contracts with suppliers will be fixed in USD to ensure less deviation when fuel prices increase.- Close monitor to the number of the IDPs in each site. Once happened, SAED will report back to WASH cluster any IDPs movement which may affect the targeted sites.
