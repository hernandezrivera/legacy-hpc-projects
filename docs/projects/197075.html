1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to establish a system for local maintenance and management of the constructed/repaired water supply facilities, contributing to the optimization of the number of users per facility by constructing new water supply facilities and providing maintenance for existing water supply facilities in resettlement centers or its host communities, Chiure District. For sustainable access to water, water management committees will be trained on how to maintain water supply facilities, and the project aims water management committee to perform maintenance independently.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

This project prioritized to establish the system which water management committee will conduct appropriate maintenance and management, and community directly reports uncontrollable breakdowns to the local government. It can prevent more breakdowns of water supply facilities and eventually strengthen communities' response capacities. This project targets communities hosting IDPs in Chiure district, Cabo Delgado province.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will identify the potential risk by collecting appropriate information with frequent site visits and listening directly to the voices of the beneficiaries. All project staff will receive the code of conduct training by the organization. A hotline system called “Linha verde”, which communities can express their opinions on assistance, will be share to the community and at the same time an opinion box will be set up in the community so that beneficiaries can express their requests and complaints. We will manage the collected information and protect privacy, and take due care not to expose the beneficiaries, communities and residents to danger.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

We established the field office in the district and despatch the staff in the district, also in the project site we will assigne community promoters in order to facilitate the communication with communities and respond their needs promptly .Staffs will be assigned with consideration for gender balance so that women can feel free to participate in the project activities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

At the beginning of the project, assessment will be conducted to identify the people need special support. Then the community group will be established to support to construct sanitation items with household level for people with disabilities. In terms of water supply facilities construction, ramp will be installed at the platform. And the project tries to design latrine slabs to match the needs of people with disabilities as much as it can.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

During the water supply facilities construction, the distance from household latrine will be considered strictly to avoid the water contamination. And after every activities conducted in the site, we will bring back garbage which was made by our project activities to leave the site clean as before.
