Needs

Since 2014 Far north region of Cameroon experience a deterioration of security situation due combined effect of direct attacks by Boko haram and influx of Nigerian refugee. Up to now , the the security situation remains unstable.Amnesty international reported on last September 05th that in Cameroon and Nigeria there has been a resurgence in Boko haram attacks and suicide bombings that has left at least 381 civilians dead in the five months since the start of April 2017, with casualties more than double the previous five. 235,913 IDPs reported by DTM in july 2017 aigainst 228,109 Individus last year in september. 49% of women and 51 of men. The whole IDP population comprised 61% of minors. IDP are mainly located mainly in following division per higher number of IDP Logone and Chari, Mayo Sava and Mayo tSANANGA Needs to target: family separation, psychosocial support distress , persons of concerns among youths especially vulnerable girls, GBV , prevention of child recruitment in army groups remain tremendous and worrisome months.
