Project Summary

HRO aims to continue providing CP services for IDPs and HC in our already existed community centres in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur, Darkosh, and Kafr Jales) & in Aleppo (Afrin) & through outreach visits to the widows’ camps (1 in Janudiyeh and 2 in Darkosh) to target the female widows & children with different CP activities, also HRO will target those children outside the camps who were separated from their mothers due to exceeding the age specified for them to stay in these camps, as they will be targeted with CP CM, including emergency case fund, CVA, & IPA to reduce the cases of child labor, CAAFAG, exploitation & other issues that they may be exposed to, considering that HRO has its own previous experience in providing CP services in emergency situations & is the focal point of emergency response in Jisr Ash Shugur.
HRO will train employees on (principles of CP, CP CM, FTR, Structured PSS, Inclusion of PWD), HRO will provide services to children, their families, caregivers, & local community, including IDPs, HC & the most vulnerable groups such as young children, UASC children & children with disabilities & those who are at risk of protection. Awareness-raising activities for community & caregivers on CP will be provided including CP services in crisis, parenting skills, early marriage, child labor, importance of education, & any other suggested topics by beneficiaries considering cultural context. Also, women & men will be engaged in parenting programs. PSS structured & semi-structured for children to enhance flexibility & adaptation skills for them, with several structured programs, including resilience Program for Children, which is provided to children & parents together, the instant challenge program. CM for children in line with SOPs, including CM of UASC based on FTR standard, & UNICEF for response, reunification, or alternative care support.
4 community committees for children & adults will be established (10 members each) will be trained on basic CP and identification & referral. Also, humanitarian actors will be trained on CP minimum standards.
M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
HRO will provide transportation & adapt the activities & centers to fit the needs of PwD & elderly through installing the needed tools such as ramps, toilet rails, extra lighting in the corridors & WCs for those with poor vision, etc, in addition to targeting them in all project activities including (IPA) & case fund.
16,913 beneficiaries including (12,702 IDPs and 4,211 HC) will be targeted with the project’s activities including 10,830 Females, 6,088 Males (124 Young Children, 5,435 Children, 9,816 Adults, 1,538 Elderly) among them are 818 PwD, through 3 community centers including 14 protection staff (3 outreach, 4 case managers, and 7 facilitators).
There will be 1 CP supervisor in each governorate to supervise case management teams ensuring the quality performance, staff wellbeing, & the regular monitoring & improvement of project’s progress.
Also, HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the protection NGO partners local councils, & stakeholders throughout the project period, in addition to considering the WHO standard COVID-19 preventive measures throughout the implementation period.
It is worth mentioning that HRO is a member of the protection cluster and is committed to updating the protection 4WS on a monthly basis.

4 - Needs

According to CP MSNA Findings in NW Syria, the scale of children in need of humanitarian assistance is on the rise, increasing 27% from 2020 to 2021, with 6.1 million children now affected. 75% of people in need for protection services are women & children, where children with & without disabilities comprise 44% of the people in need across Syria. As of October 2021, approximately 2.8 million IDPs were living in 1,389 camps or informal sites, the Cluster tracked over 273,000 individual displacements, which include multiple displacements. Also, as top reason, 21.6% of HHs reported that children between 3 & 17 years of age do not go to school because they are working to support their families. In Idleb Governorate, 25.6% of HHs confirmed this statement, while in Aleppo 26.4% of HHs confirmed it.According to HRO Protection need assessment conducted in October 2021 in Jisr Ash Shugur, Janudiyeh, Badama, 99% reported that there is child labour. However, according to KIIs in CP MSNA findings, Child labor represents a problem for 42.9% of adolescent girls preventing them from attending schools in NW Syria, whereas 77.2% of adolescent boys reported the same problem. KIIs reported “Child marriage” as one of the reasons for the separation of girls from their families in Idleb at 78.3% & at 86% in Aleppo, while for boys was financial reasons in Idleb at 41.7% and 72.5% in Aleppo.HRO conducted a Protection Need Assessment in Mar & Apr 2021, where 53% reported school dropout in Afrin.

5 - Implementation of activities

After ensuring the preparedness of the project logistics & recruiting procedures, staff will be trained on CP basics, CP Case Management, PFA, PSS, inclusion of PwDs. Afterwards, we will conduct awareness raising on CP-related topics, CAAFAG, & including children with disabilities in center & outreach activities, PSS & structured PSS activities for children in centres & schools, conducting CP monitoring assessments to identify CP needs/risks in the target communities, training widows’ camp managers & security & safety officials on CP prevention, & referral system, besides training women committees in widows’ camps on CP prevention, PSS, referral system, & suicide prevention.Key messages & IEC materials on COVID-19 will be disseminated at the end of each session. Also, in case of severe COVID-19 situations awareness-raising sessions will be conducted online.M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
