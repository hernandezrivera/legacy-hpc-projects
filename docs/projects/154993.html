Project Summary

This T-ICSP in targeted areas aims to contribute all year-round to: Crisis-affected populations to meet their basic food and nutrition needs during and after the crises; Food insecure school-age children, to access adequate and nutritious food; Vulnerable groups (children, pregnant women and adolescent girls) to have enhanced nutritional status; Food insecure populations and communities to have more resilient livelihoods and sustainable food systems; Capacity strengthening of National institutions; Humanitarian and development partners to access common services.

Needs

The security situation in the Diffa region since 2015, caused by the conflict in the Lake Chad region, has aggravated the already precarious living conditions of vulnerable populations in the region.• In june 2018, an estimated 249,813 people (figures DREC) were displaced (refugees, returbees and IDPs) due to the conflict, whose 118 868 refugees.• In 2018, in areas where the security situation remains volatile, WFP plans to continue to provide food assistance and targeted support to vulnerable displaced and host populations.• Moreover, in areas where early recovery activities can be considered, targeted assistance will be provided to support early recovery of affected populations.• Based on the same logic adopted for the national level, which aimed to use the “Cadre Harmonisé” analyzes of the last years in the absence of analyzes for 2019, the number of people in need for 2019 in Diffa Region has been estimated at 290,000 people.
