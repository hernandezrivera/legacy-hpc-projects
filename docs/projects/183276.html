Project Summary

Through the proposed intervention, Caritas will ensure the delivery of food packages with a prevention and response approaches, encompassing meeting the immediate food needs of the population and provide vulnerable families with multipurpose cash assistance (to other households) to alleviate some of their concerns and help them meet their most basic requirements.
As CL is already implementing two WFP projects in 2 different areas and seeing the huge gaps reflected by the field team, it will compliment the intervention and target the same North and Mount Lebanon in order to expand the provided services. CL will ensure complementary approaches with the existing WFP projects in the areas of intervention and will adapt its modality of delivery to the specific needs of the targeted population by ensuring both, safe and accessible distributions. Based on the identified needs, the project participants will be referred to other services either provided by CL or other actors and a proper follow-up will be done.
The intervention will target an addition of 2,640 Lebanese beneficiaries or around 660 households (4 per household), with food parcels for 6 consecutive months, while 600 migrants beneficiaries or 200 households (3 per household) residing around the Ashrafiyeh social center will be provided with food parcel for 2 consecutive months. In parallel, all beneficiaries receiving the food kits will be supported with Multipurpose cash assistance for 3 months to cover their remaining costs.
In order to mainstream protection and safeguard them from additional abuse, priority will be given to female-headed households, families with disabled members, and unaccompanied elders. Accountability to affected people will be ensured at all stages of the project through consultation, information sharing and feedback mechanisms.
Caritas will outreach, identify and select Lebanese beneficiaries; whose criteria fit with the socio-economic vulnerability principles, and who are not benefitting from regular NPTP e-cards assistance and/or are not covered under LCRP and have approached the WFP team during distributions to receive assistance. Likewise, CL will also outreach migrants who are being mistreated by their employers and have requested assistance through the social center. To eliminate duplication of households and ensure more individuals are reached, the household will get either food kits or multipurpose cash support, depending on their level of vulnerability.
For both interventions, CL ensured that the services provided are in line with the FSS WG guidelines. Caritas team, the porters and the distribution personnel will conduct small distributions on sites selected based on its Standards Operating Procedures (SOPs), which include several criteria such as beneficiary safety and accessibility, as well as hygiene standards, and which could be on municipal premises or its own selected sites, depending on the evolution of COVID-19 contamination and the context in each area of intervention. Caritas Lebanon staff and volunteers are trained to ensure the highest standards of accountability to the affected population and know how to manage informal complaints or feedback that can be addressed during the distribution or during any contact they might have with beneficiaries. For the cash assistance, the service will be provided to address an emergency situation that exposes households to serious harm. Caritas Lebanon social workers will assess the households based a vulnerability assessment and select families that receive the highest vulnerability score. Caritas already works on distributing cash assistance without using the cash in hand system and by resorting to a cooperation with BOB Finance. A door-to-door service and a different payment modality will be considered for vulnerable beneficiaries who cannot leave their homes and/or do not have the means to come to the distribution site due to disabilities or other risks (elderly, pregnancy).

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under Obj 1. Act. 1.1 – Outreach and selection: Caritas Lebanon staff and volunteers will explain to the community the activities that will be implemented, the selection criteria, the different feedback mechanism and all information they need to ensure they can make informed decisions and choices. CL will select households that will receive both food kits and cash assistance.Act 1.2 – Procurement of Food parcels and storage: The food kits procured will be in line with the items recommended as per the SPHERE Guidelines, as well as FSS WG and will be stored in safe warehouses. Act 1.3 - Distribution of food parcels - Caritas team, the porters and the distribution personnel will conduct small distributions on sites. A door to door option will be provided to vulnerable families.Act 1.4 – Provision of cash assistance to families – The information about the vulnerable households will be sent to the finance department by a CL social worker. The financial department will work with BoB Finance to send a TN number, which will allow family members to get monetary assistance. Act 1.5 – PDM - CL will develop the projects’ monitoring tools including the food security PDM’s and cash PDM and FGD questionnaire ensuring accountability is mainstreamed throughout the implementation.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Caritas Lebanon staffs have been trained by partners on GAM and GMS ensuring appropriate integration of protection, gender and GBV issues within the design, delivery, and review of the project. Gender is mainstreamed in the design of the project which will ensure at least equal access to the provided services to females, males, girls and boys. Eventually, it is worth mentioning, Caritas Lebanon is a strong actor in the Protection sector where it has been co-leading the sector and is significantly involved in all decisions related to protection issues. Moreover, protection is central to all CL interventions, both as a standalone programme area, but also mainstreamed across all sectors, alongside safeguarding best practices. The proposed project has protection and disability inclusion integrated, with the explicit aim of ensuring prevention and mitigation of protection risks to the most vulnerable and marginalized including women, girls, the elderly, and persons with disabilities.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Caritas Lebanon will actively engage communities, local authorities, and other stakeholders within the frame of the project. Through this engagement, CL is ensuring the project is designed and implemented based on the priorities. Caritas Lebanon has operationalized the organizational PSEA and safeguarding mechanism as well as a Feedback and Complaint Handling Mechanism which provides staff, communities and others, with multiple channels to raise concerns and provide feedback including through the complaint boxes and hotlines, and through the MEAL process which include accountability questions and satisfaction surveys. Caritas Lebanon will fully incorporate Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) modalities into all stages of this project while strengthening the approaches to reflect the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and protection mainstreaming.
