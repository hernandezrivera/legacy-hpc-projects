Project Summary

The project presupposes the repair of preschool institutions (restoration of window and door blocks, restoration of roofs, restoration of WASH infrastructure, etc.). It also provides for the provision of these institutions with equipment, furniture, soft equipment and toys to improve the quality of services provided to children.

1 - Needs

During the ongoing fighting in Eastern Ukraine, NGCA have been gripped by a deep economic crisis. The consequences of the active phase of hostilities were significant destruction of social infrastructure and lack of opportunities for its full recovery. Kindergartens were significantly affected, not only the buildings themselves, which were partially or completely destroyed during the fighting, but also the fact that equipment, furniture, and inventory were damaged by the blast waves. As of 2020, there are 496 pre-school institutions and 49,300 pre-school children in the Donetsk region (NGCA), and there is a tendency to increase the coverage of children and the occupancy rate of institutions. For 2020, the occupancy rate of preschool institutions is marked at the level of 92%. In the course of our activities, we note serious problems faced by pre-school institutions: - first of all, it is the destruction both from the fighting during the active phase and from the operating period of the buildings of pre-school institutions (mainly the glazing, roof suffered from shelling, heating and water supply systems were often damaged);- sometimes it is the risk of children from NGCA being exposed to open areas due to mine contamination and the possibility of shelling;- lack of funding for the restoration of pre-school buildings and the purchase of necessary equipment and inventory for educational and developmental activities in a comfortable and safe environment, arrangement of safe playgrounds; In view of this, it is planned to identify the 20 most affected pre-school institutions (NGCA), restore them (restore glazing, roof, heating, arrangement or repair of lavatories and laundry facilities, etc.), as well as provide inventory and equipment (provision of bedding, as during monitoring visits the need for them was identified as one of the necessary ones, provision of washing machines and kitchen equipment, children's furniture, sets for training and games).

2 - Activities & Outcomes

The proposed project includes the following activities:1. Selection of 20 pre-school institutions most in need of restoration, verification of needs;2. Departure of specialists to make estimates and work plans;3. Procurement of materials and equipment;4. Delivery and transfer of materials and equipment.5. Carrying out restoration and repair work in 20 selected pre-school institutions;6. Provision of equipment and inventory to 20 selected preschool institutions;
