Project Summary

The project will target the Rohingya and the host communities in Cox's Bazar through WASH service provision with more emphasis on community engagement while sustaining the access to basic life-saving output but also the expanding and improving the quality of the infrastructures with social inclusiveness. The project will target the beneficiaries and the caseloads that are not covered by the funds provided through UN agencies. In host communities, the project will support the efforts of social cohesion and attempts to integrate the services between the camps and the host community program. Additionally, this project will address the concurrent pandemic COVID-19 issue regarding the WASH facilities in the camp area and host community as well through strengthening the existing effort, particularly in awareness-raising, hand washing practicing, social distancing. This project will use the WASH sector strategies and the guidelines in all the stages of the implementation.

Needs

As stated in the WASH sector 2020 JRP comprehensive document, the access to the basic WASH service in the camp still not at a satisfactory level in all the WASH components. BRAC as a Key partner and a front line responder organization has also identified gaps within its operation area, Water facilities are still under up-gradation from the hand pump to the water pipe network, though the space limitation and lack of experience vendors to drill and equip the borehole is the critical issue, water quality at household still showing significant contamination. The majority of Latrines and bathing facilities remain communal and different feedback from the communities where the voice of women loudly demanded more privacy. At the same time, the functionality rate needs improvement besides the rationalization of quality and quantity.Hygiene promotion: it is essential to shift the hygiene promotion from message dropping to such kind of interactive approaches with communities thus the newly adopted strategies ( RANAS, CCC ...etc.) used to improve and change the behaviors showed good results while the MHM remains a crucial area to continue to target women in reproductive health and adolescent girls.The hygiene kits and Aquatab distribution and monitoring the efficiency of utilization of these items is one of the gaps which need to be focused and addressed. The community WASH committee and their active involvement in the WASH program, also one of the critical areas, need to be continued with close consultation with the government and other stakeholders to adopt the participation and increase the participation level as well.The integration of the WASH program with other lifesaving sectors is crucial and necessary to quickly get the outputs(AWD, Safeguarding, WASH in schools and social inclusiveness...etc.) that support the humanitarian response. Therefore health/nutrition, protection, Education, and site management will be the critical sector WASH will work with to ensure the joint efforts are combined during the response and at different project cycle phases.The Targeted area used to face several types of emergencies such as cyclone, monsoon, and AWD, and other water-related outbreaks that need excellent preparedness and response plans. On the contrary, the first case of COVID-19 was identified in Bangladesh about three months after the JRP finalized for this year in March. Even worldwide, COVID-19 took its pandemic size in March 2020. So, as the situation in the Rohingya settlement is quite congested, and the risk of infection could be high unless special attention with critical interventions put in place. To limit the spreading of this disease, WASH partners intervened actively with measures that contribute in the prevention of this virus spreading such as social distancing, hand washing, and improvement of general hygiene and awareness campaigns using house to house messaging, while soap distribution to the entire community to support the hand washing initiatives remain a crucial intervention.

Activities or outputs

To respond to the COVID19 outbreak, BRAC will implement the WASH COVID19 strategy through the continuation of the lifesaving activities within the targeted areas. In Sanitation: BRAC will provide the infrastructure remain functional, clean, and used by the communities; this will be achieved through the Operation and maintenance of the facilities, including the dislodge activities and where possible user groups will be mobilized to clean the latrines and bathing facilities a. All the existing handwashing stations will remain functional while new ones will be installed to strengthen and promote the handwashing practice, especially during COVID 19 and AWD outbreak times. Fecal Sludge Management will remain the critical activity, and all sanitation infrastructures should remain functional and used by the population in hygienic condition hence BRAC will operate and maintain the existing FSMs while constructing new FSM plants with a network that allow the smooth flow of the effluent from the transfer and intermediate stations to the FSM, BRAC also will allocate trained human resources to manage the system with clear follow up and care while expert consultants will be engaged to develop the system and its operational guidelines.Solid waste management will be the sanitation element that BRAC will work for in 2020, and this will include waste production mapping , collection, segregation, and disposal with appropriate technologies that the WASH sector recommends with more emphasis on recycling and involvement of private sector and the communities in the whole process. The emphasis will put on strengthening the awareness campaigns, teaching them how to wash hands, and keep the social distance while ensuring the availability of water and soap always at the handwashing stations. Door to door soap distribution to the entire households associated with proper COVID19 preventive messaging will be maintained as the WASH sector strategy and hygiene promotion technical working group guidelines. COVID19 adopted MHM kits distribution will be part of the hygiene promotion program during this period ensuring the women in reproductive age and the girls have access to their MHM needs.The people with disabilities, the elderly, and children will be supported in all the activities mentioned above. At the same time, they will be included in hygiene and dignity kits, together with individual interactive awareness sessions. Health clinics and the isolation centers will be equipped with WASH infrastructures, the WHO and WASH sector standards will be followed in the implementation and operation stages.The Key Activities during COVID19 Operation and maintenance of 200 handpumps and five water networks, including repair of the breakdowns.Three hundred samples for water quality testing targeting from households and sources.700 latrines, FSMs, and bathing facilities remain functional through Operation and maintenance. One hundred ninety-six thousand six hundred fifty-one persons in camps and host communities reached through hygiene promotion activities, including MHM, hygiene Kits, soap distribution, and awareness campaigns focused on COVID 19 prevention and mitigation.30 community cleaning campaigns and solid waste management before the rainy season startsWASH facilities construction and operation maintenance in 3 health centers Disinfection and cleaning of WASH facilitiesemergency water trucking in areas with water scarcity.Hand washing facilities installation and Operation and maintenance.Provision of PPE materials for the staff and the volunteers and in the projects.
