Project Summary

The goal of this project is to reduce the existing gaps through installation and maintenance of accessible and gender-segregated WASH facilities (water facilities, functional latrines etc.) in the Rohingya settlement camps, with some targeted interventions to address Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) issues.

Objectives

Ensure effective, sufficient and continuous water and sanitation facilities and to encourage Rohingya and Host communities to adopt health seeking behaviors for mitigating public health risks.

Needs

According to latest report circulated from WASH sector, we have found in still 21% gap on water facilities, 26% gap on functional latrine facilities and 6% for hygiene kits. WASH facilities in settlements are still under strain with most facilities lacking basic protection measures (including gender segregation, MHM facilities) and are in locations not easily accessible for women. The risk and fear of GBV for women when accessing latrines at night have also been reported.

Activities or outputs

(1.1) Installation of 40 deep tube-well, 120 Latrine, 120 bathing spaces and 80 hand-washing facilities;(1.2) Establishing of 1 water testing laboratory;(1.3) Installation of supporting infrastructure i.e. garbage pit and construction/ upgradation of drainage facilities;(1.4) Repairing and maintenance of new and existing WASH facilities;(1.5) Desludging of 333 latrines;(1.6) Construction of 2 new fecal sludge management units and repairing of 2 existing fecal sludge management units;(1.7) Construction and maintenance of incinerators for 222 female toilets for disposing MHM materials;(1.8) Installation of household-level water supply facilities;(2.1) Developing community volunteers for disseminating hygiene awareness messages (including MHM);(2.2) Organize capacity building training for staff and 142 Volunteers;(2.3) Develop caretakers for DTW repair and maintenance;(3.1) Hygiene kit distribution among 20,000 families.
