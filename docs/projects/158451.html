Project Summary

Objective: Ensure strong and effective leadership and coordination of the Food Security response in Mozambique, including support to coordinated food security situation and response framework analysis, information management and monitoring systems.
Key Activities
- Develop FSC sectoral plans, objectives and indicators for overall response
- Assess needs and harmonize assessment methodologies and tools
- Develop mechanisms to minimize duplication of interventions in different areas
- Establish and maintain appropriate humanitarian coordination mechanisms at all levels
- Periodic monitoring reporting on activities and needs to illustrate progress against results
- Develop contingency plans/scenarios to ensure emergency preparedness in a timely manner
- Coordinate with the national authorities and partners (INGC, MOSA, others)
- Ensure inclusion of cross-cutting themes and application of minimum standards in the FS cluster interventions
- Train and build capacity of members on assessments, tools, and FS concepts
- Ensure inter sectoral meetings/coordination at national (Maputo) and hub level (Beira, Chimoio, Pemba) for effective and efficient delivery of sectoral assistance
- Advocate for resources mobilization for FS interventions
