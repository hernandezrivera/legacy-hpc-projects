Project Summary

The overall objective of the 2023 Humanitarian program is to provide emergency response and develop resilience of affected communities, including children and women, by improving their access to quality water services as well as their capacities to withstand water borne diseases through proper sanitation and good hygiene practices.

2 - BESOINS, ACTIVITÉS ET RÉSULTATS ATTENDUS
2.1. Décrivez les besoins, activités et résultats attendus

An estimated 5.5 million people require humanitarian assistance in the country, including 3.2 million children. Of these, more than 3 million people need adequate WASH services, including 1.8 million lacking access to safe water.The program targets for 2023 are the following: - 755,000 people accessing a sufficient quantity and quality of water for drinking and domestic needs - 800,000 people accessing appropriate sanitation services - 950,000 people reached with hand-washing and behaviour change programmes
