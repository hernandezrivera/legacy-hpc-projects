Project Summary

This project started in January 2021. As part of this intervention, AAH provides technical and organizational support to 12 health centers of the 6 health districts of KOZA, MOZOGO, MORA, MAGA, VELE; GUERA, regularly affected by shocks in order to better equip them to respond to health / nutrition emergencies and to address psychological distress.
The project will also support health facilities in deploying the phase 1 of the national strategy of universal health coverage through a model of performance-based financing to the supported health facilities in maternal, neonatal and infant health services. In addition, donations of medicines and medical consumables, monitoring and supervision of drugs’ management, joint field supervisions workshops on preparedness and response to epidemics and training on epidemiological surveillance will be implemented.
Patients who present signs of severity during the management by community health workers (ICCM+) will be referred to the Health Centre (children under 5) and those who present signs of severity during the management by the mobile clinics will be referred to the District Hospital. WIN kits will be delivered to SAM children and their caregivers. **80 SAM cases for U5 will be treated with the support of the project**. Health workers and community volunteers participated in trainings on health / nutrition / mental health and care practices so they can better address the different needs and facilitate referral to activities provided by AAH and other actors in the region. AAH sets up mobile clinics (MC) to provide emergency assistance in nutrition / health / mental health for affected and isolated vulnerable populations in the Mayo Sava (MS) & Mayo Tsanaga (MT) divisions. The package of services and care is delivered free of charge. It targets children under five (U5), pregnant and lactating women (PLW), but also disabled people and the elderly among the newly displaced. With regard to the MHPSS (mental health Psychosocial support) component, the activities targets children over six and adults through emotional stabilization and psychological resources strengthening , which will be administered in groups for children above 6 years old (girls and boys) and adults (men and women). Indeed, MHPSS activities aim to strength men and women, in different age categories (adults and children above 6 years old) including girls and boys with disabilities, survivors of GBV and victims of abuse). This is also the case for women (pregnant women, breastfeeding women, including women with disabilities and survivors of gender-based violence). The latter will receive psychosocial support (for the prevention of perinatal distress and the reinforcement of good care practices, the prevention of risky behaviours of adolescents and children. The teams will prevent cases of GBV orchestrated by men through activities that promote good anger and emotion management with men and advocacy. The schedules/Days of implementation of the activities are fixed taking into account the daily tasks of the women and men in the community
