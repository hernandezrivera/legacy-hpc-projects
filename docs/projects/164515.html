Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

The project installs latrines for camps (Safa - Alsad - Bsartun - Toum), 2 in each one
According to the need with the required accessories and
Equipment for use by target families.
Each toilet block will consist of four separate male and female bathrooms.
In addition, each toilet will be equipped with one shower and one toilet for persons with disabilities and two sinks.
During the year awareness sessions will be held to promote hygiene:
Abrar will conduct 4 awareness sessions to promote hygiene and provide printed awareness brochures on hygiene, how to use hygiene materials, water conservation, and rational consumption.
Abrar will also distribute hygiene kits in 4 rounds during the life of the project, 3 months between each round.
, Each group will contain the essential hygiene items required according to the standards of the WASH group.
Part of the project has been completed in Taum camp.
And the project is secured Partially 20%.
