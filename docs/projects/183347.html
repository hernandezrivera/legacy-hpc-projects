Project Summary

Lebanon is facing an unprecedented situation. A series of multiple and consecutive shocks has
worsened the country’s socio-economic, political, and health situation. A highly volatile currency, closely
tied to political developments, further fueled inflation. This in turn severely hampers the ability of both
Lebanese and refugee populations to access basic goods and services. The CPI witnessed inflation of
121.66% in April 2021 compared to April 2020 (CPI, CAS), while the Food Price Index (FPI) registered
inflation of 423%. This has been exacerbated by the decreasing of the value of the Lebanese pound by
80% in the last year. An anticipated removal of food subsidies without an implementation of appropriate
social safety nets programming, will severely impact access to adequate food and likely further increase
the depth and breadth of food and nutrition insecurity in Lebanon.
The main target of this project is persons with disabilities and older persons who are facing exacerbated
vulnerability due to the Lebanese economic crisis and the COVID-19 outbreak. The latter has increased
their isolation, further impacting their capacity to cope and reducing their access to community support
(LCRP Situation Update, December 2020). According to the same report, persons with disabilities and
elderly people are facing severe lack of basic food items, chronic medicines shortages and the inability
to cover health care costs, including mobility aids and home care supplies. The "Assessing the Impact
of the Economic and COVID-19 Crises in Lebanon" report (WFP, 2020) indicated that families with at
least one older person and with at least one member with disability have reported to be compromising
their food consumption and apply severe coping strategies more often.
In order to mitigate the above mentioned situation, and to address the emerging challenges and needs
of Lebanese and refugees food insecure HHs this project will provide 1,440 WASH kits (1,140 Hygiene kits, 150 Baby kits, 150 Elderly kits) for 480 HHs for a duration of 6 months.
In alignment with the FSS priorities and LHF 2021 first standard allocation strategy paper, the targeted
vulnerabilities of this project will be identified using a Validation Assessment prepared by LHF:
- Persons with Disabilities (Men with disabilities, women with disabilities and children with disabilities).
- Older persons (Older women and older men)
- Households including at least one person with disability.
- Households including at least one older person.
The following distribution modalities are adopted to address the accessibility challenges faced by our
targeted groups:
- Center-based distribution for the beneficiaries who are capable of safely accessing FoH centers
without compromising their health situation.
- Home-based distribution done by the social workers for beneficiaries facing accessibility challenges.
- Local level distribution.
Given that North Lebanon and Akkar are the most affected regions by the economic crisis and the
COVID-19 pandemic. North Lebanon and Akkar have respectively 92% of HH and 91% of HH living
under the Survival Minimum Expenditure Basket (LCRP Situation Update, December 2020). Moreover,
FoH owns 3 centers in these 2 governorates which will ease the implementation of the project activities.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

- To identify 400 Lebanese households who meet the vulnerability criteria of the project, with priority given to households that include women with disabilities and older women. - Validation of beneficiaries through the updated Validation Assessment. The assessment attributes a certain weight per question that allows to generate a final score, based on which FoH team will be able to prioritize the most vulnerable beneficiaries.- Procurement of 1,200 WASH kits with respect to the composition of kits provided by the WASH sector, according to the following:* 948 hygiene kits for 316 Lebanese HHs with at least one adult person with disability aged between 18 and 60 years old (on bi-monthly basis)* 126 Baby kits for 42 Lebanese HHs with at least one child with physical/mental disability aged between 0 months and 18 years old (on bi-monthly basis).* 126 Elderly kits for 42 Lebanese HHs with at least one older person aged 60 and above (on bi-monthly basis).- The distribution of 1,200 WASH kits. The distribution methodology will be based on the following modalities: Center-based distribution, Home-based distribution, and Local Level distribution.- Cross Cutting Messaging: FoH team will be disseminating multi-sectoral messaging to the keypopulation (WASH Messaging). - Post-distribution monitoring: following up with the beneficiaries via phone calls in order to collect data such as complaints, feedback and recommendations.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

- The project team will put in place adequate means to ease and facilitate the participation of girls with disabilities, women with disabilities and older women. This includes accessibletransportation, key messages disseminated in different and adaptive communication channels, individual meets through households visits, food parcels/WASH kits home-distribution.-Identify and reach the most vulnerable and isolated within the affected population.-Avoid creating an additional risk of harm through the project intervention.-Detect and identify protection concerns and incidents such as violence, abuse and exploitation that come to the team's attention in the course of work, and take all the necessary measures to address these protection concerns.-Provide safe and dignified access to project services.-Work towards removing the barriers and negative social norms, through advocacy and building the local community capacity and understanding about PwDs and older persons rights.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

-Conducting meetings with marginalized groups in particular persons with disabilities and older persons with the aim of collecting relative information, challenges, and specific requirements that will feed into the project's initial assessments and design.-Providing the affected population with information about the project eligibility criteria in a clear manner with the aim of increasing transparency, and ensuring that they are well informed of their entitlements and rights.-Informing the affected population on the way to make a complaint about a staff member or about the organization.-Post distribution follow-up surveys, telephone hotline, complaints box, household visits, FGDs are the mechanisms that will be put in place to enable the provision of feedback and complaints.- Establishing two-ways communication channels to keep the affected population informed in all phases of the project life cycle.- Conducting meetings with community stakeholders to inform them about the project
