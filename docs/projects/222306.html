Project Summary

This project aims to provide timely, effective, and quality lifesaving protection responses, Gender Based Violence services, Child Protection Services and provisions to promote enjoyment of Land, and Property rights. The project will target returnees, IDPs, and vulnerable host community members in South Sudan through preventive and remedial mechanisms. To achieve the project objective, HDC will focus on; 1.) General Protection; 2) Housing, Land and Property; 3) Gender- Based Violence; 4) Child Protection.
General Protection will be through awareness raising, Protection Monitoring and Protection Case Management. Persons with specific protection risks will receive individualized assistance, including psychosocial support and material assistance. HDC will work with volunteers in affected communities to ensure the community participates and is involved in the project implementation. Community-based protection structures will be established or enhanced and related activities implemented. HDC will continue to assess the protective environment for shifts in risks and negative coping mechanisms and adjust awareness-raising messages to better suit the needs of targeted populations. The messages and activities might shift from responding to acute protection needs to mitigating new risks and establishing some resiliency at the community level.
To promote HLP rights and prevent HLP rights violation, HDC will strengthen community dispute resolution mechanisms, Provision of HLP information sharing and awareness, Provision of legal counselling and assistance and capacity building through training of the HLP stakeholders. Legal assistance will range from restitution of lands and property and acquiring civil documents and land titles to ensure land tenure security. Capacity building of the HLP stakeholders will focus on existing HLP legal frameworks, functions of the HLP administrative systems, Alternative dispute resolution as well as litigation among other topics that may be of interest. HDC will ensure gender sensitivity and prioritize female clients with HLP rights violations and discriminated in accessing equitable justice. HDC will work closely with the community in identification of beneficiaries to ensure inclusivity, accountability and ensure the intervention is community driven
For GBV and Child Protection, this project aims to provide accessible GBV and Child Protection services to provide prevention, risk mitigation and scale up response service in addressing harmful social norms and systemic gender inequality including child survivors and linkage to multisectoral services to benefit women, men and children; and to upscale CP-services to benefit children, adolescents and caregivers. Key protection risks to be addressed are UASC, school dropout, early/forced marriage and GBV, child labor, family separation and neglect, prevention of recruitments, distress, support for persons living with disability, dangers, and injuries, sexual health, physical harm, mental health and Stigma, access to justice and access to multi sectoral services as per Protection cluster objective. Beneficiaries will benefit from comprehensive case management and psychosocial support services, service mapping, establishing, and training community-based structures on GBV and CP, provision of awareness sessions to prevent and respond to GBV and CP risks occurring within the communities.
To ensure centrality of protection, this project will address protection risks through strengthened focus group support, community safety audits, protection risks assessments and monitoring etc. HDC’s staff will adhere to the code of conduct to ensure safety, dignity and avoid causing harm during interventions. Complaint and feedback mechanisms will be availed in the community, PSEA messages on the banners, risks reporting hotline number etc, as a means for Accountability

1. Needs

Protection needs in South Sudan remain critical with 5.34 m people in need of protection services. This has been due to the ongoing conflict in Sudan that has caused an influx of South Sudanese returning and refugees fleeing into the country, internal displacement by conflict and environmental crises and the hard economic crises. These are factors that have led to increasing protection needs. The population mainly affected are women and children. Levels of violence, exploitation, and abuse are notably high, including conflict-related sexual violence, GBV, and growing child protection concerns, especially in Unity, Jonglei, Upper Nile, Warrap, and Eastern Equatoria. Economic hardship has led to increased theft and cultural stigmas further marginalize survivors, limiting available support. The lack of livelihood opportunities, shelter and services also place PSNs, particularly elderly people, women, unaccompanied minors, at high risk of exploitation, sexual and physical abuse.Displacement has affected over 2.3 million people, heightening risks of theft, eviction, and property destruction in conflict-affected areas like Upper Nile, Unity, and Jonglei. This has led to an increase in HLP rights violation. Minority groups, people with disabilities, and displaced individuals often face discrimination, lacking essential resources and documentation, which restricts service access and increase vulnerabilities. Family separation remains a critical issue, with nearly 20,000 children reported as separated, unaccompanied, or missing, and high child abduction rates in Greater Jonglei. These issues continue to jeopardize the safety of children and destabilize impacted communities.To address these needs, HDC will target the following locations; Pibor, Duk, Twic East, Bor South, Ayod, Fangak, Canal Pigi, Uror, Manyo, Panyikang, Akobo, Malakal, Renk, Baliet, Maban, Rubkona and Juba. These are locations affected by floods, conflict or have the highest number of returnees from Sudan. Additionally, these are locations that HDC have had interventions before and based on past experienced coupled with the Protection PRMS report and the HNRP severity classification and prioritization.

2. Project activities

HDC will carry out protection monitoring through static and mobile response. This will be done in areas of return employing participatory methodologies such as mini-surveys, FGDs, spot checks and protection help desks established in each location. Protection awareness campaign and information sharing during the project implementation. Monitor men and women upon arrival into south Soudan at the entry point. Identification of new/existing referral pathways to services for vulnerable boys, girls and GBV survivors and Provide appropriate case identification, referral and follow up services for boys, girls and GBV survivors. Establish or enhance community based protection structures to support community protection activities. HDC will conduct 3 trainings on general protection for community-based protection frontline actors (Community leaders, women group, CBPN, WASH Committee and local authority). The training will cover general protection aspects including MHPSS, referral of protection concern cases and principles of Community based protection for community structures, stakeholders and partners staff for better support and response. The community-based structures will support community mobilization and awareness on different protection issues; Child protection activities will include; Comprehensive Case Management for UASC, Mental Health and Psychosocial Support, capacity building to community-based Child protection networks, teachers/PTAs and members of community-based child protection networks on PSS/PFA/CPiE risks, child rights and child protection related needs. Establish foster family bank and identify foster parents to provide the alternative care to Unaccompanied children and other children with similar needs. Conduct awareness raising activities on Child Protection issues. GBV Activities will include; Provision of individual case management and psychosocial support for GBV survivors. HDC will provide legal aid information/legal education targeting GBV survivors at WGFS as well as through legal aid clinics Provide structured psychosocial support to GBV survivors and persons at risk of GBV. HDC will provide dignity kits to SGBV survivors and at-risk women and adolescent girls. The provision of these dignity kits will significantly contribute to safeguarding the sexual and reproductive health and rights of women and girls, mitigate the risk of gender-based violence, and protect the dignity of women and girls adversely affected by the protracted crisis in the target locations.: Carry out community outreach awareness campaigns on GBV prevention and risk mitigation using holistic community engagement tools and approaches such as EMAP and SASA. Integration of risk mitigation and provision of fuel-efficient stoves and income generating activities to GBV Survivors and persons at risk of GBV. Updating Referral Pathways and IEC materials. Training on GBV prevention, PSEA, combine with environmental risk mitigation and multi-hazard early warning system for CBPNs targeting 50% females of the target in each intervention sites.To promote HLP rights; HDC will conduct training on HLP legal framework, land administration and management, tenure security, HLP Due Diligence, women rights to HLP among other relevant topics. The training will target government officials, community leaders, local authorities in land administration offices and representatives from the target groups. Provision of Legal counseling and assistance. Legal assistance will range from restitution of lost lands and property and acquiring civil documents and land titles to ensure tenure security of the target group. Awareness raising and HLP information sharing to increase knowledge on HLP. HDC will strengthen existing community dispute resolution mechanisms. HDC will identify existing dispute resolution mechanisms in the target location, identify knowledge gaps in these structures and respond to these by providing technical support and where needed provide material support
