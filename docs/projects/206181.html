Project Summary

The recurrent conflict in Sudan, and 2013/2016 of South Sudan worsen by flooding have enormously contributed to the humanitarian crisis of influx to refugees and returnees of South Sudanese from Sudan into Rubkona and Pariang; so caused poverty and suffering to children and their community since the available resources are limited to meet their basic needs. This has resulted to family separation while many children going unaccompanied into places strange to them in search of safety and survival. This project therefore seeks to provide Lifesaving Childcare Services and Psychosocial Assistance for Girls and boys affected by disaster and armed conflict in Rubkona County, targeting Rotriak Payam, and Pariang County of Ruweng Administrative Area; who on a daily basis are at risk and indeed exposed to child labor, negligence, early and forced marriages. Worse to it, these children have limited recreational centers/facilities. Therefore, GUNO’s already existing child protection field team, our tested community child protection networks are to aid identification and documentation of unaccompanied and separated children as well as, identify befitting foster care families that are in effect to provide a durable solution to the UASC. All activities are to embrace messaging for peacebuilding and peaceful coexistence.
The project will provide life-saving child protection prevention and response services through the provision of Comprehensive case management services and referrals for children with critical protection concerns; Psychosocial Support (PSS) activities; establishment of 8 child-friendly spaces( 4 for each County) to provide psychosocial support and recreational services, Child protection awareness raisings and Strengthening of community-based child protection mechanisms to prevent and respond to child protection concerns targeting 12,000 most vulnerable children and caregivers for the two counties ( 6800 girls, 5000 boys, and 200 caregivers both male and females). The activity will target 6000 beneficiaries per each county of Rubkona and Pariang. The target group includes unaccompanied and separated children (UASC), children associated with armed forces and armed groups, children victims of or at the risk of GBV, children against with law, vulnerable children in and out of school, domestic violence, physical and sexual abused, neglected, and emotionally damaged. There is a timely need for protection services in Rubkona of Unity State and Pariang Ruweng Administrative Area.
GUNO’s current program in Rubkona and Pariang identifies child abuse, exploitation, inadequate child friendly spaces and UASC, and there is still consistent need to proceed with further identification, registration and reunification with their families. And this can be fully accomplished through proposed activities such as; establishment of child friendly spaces, unification of Unaccompanied and Separated children with their families through family tracing, Psychosocial Support for Children who are affected by the Conflict of Sudan and South Sudan. There is need for Mine Risk Education, Cash based Individual Protection Assistance to the most vulnerable school children. The community will undergo through GBV/UXO’s Prevention Awareness and attending to Case Management, Peace Building engagements among affected children and community. Provision of training on child protection in emergencies (CPiE) including Case management and PSS to community stakeholders especially PTA members in schools. GUNO will work closely with other child protection partners to avoid overlapping and duplication of efforts. GUNO will also coordinate with CP partners through referrals of CP cases. This project will ensure close collaboration with the Child Protection and GBV Sub clusters and will be realized in a period of 12 months in Rubkona and Pariang Counties. Grave violations of child rights, Stigma and traumatization will be eliminated to a greater percentage.

1. Needs

Rubkona and Pariang County in Unity State during the past conflict has experienced high returnees and refugees from Sudan instability, destroyed infrastructures of livelihood, community social norms and services; leaving the children, adolescence, youth and care-giver particularly women with trauma and poor attitudes towards one another. More so it has destroyed the social communications of various institutions for response of service delivery in the sector of health, nutrition, education, protection and WASH. And now have received 19,648 returnees, of whom 6,877 are women. This calls for engagement of women and Youth in planning, developing, and implementing social and behavior change interventions that will be achieved through the promotion of integrated, cross-sectoral approach and establishment of community-based partnerships in Rubkona county. Grave violations of child rights, Stigma and traumatization are also widespread; cases of UASC, CAAFAG, sexual violence, rape, child labor, early and forced marriages. Children, adolescence, women and youth is the most common forms of underlying issues at hand. Social behavior change specifically will support women, young people, families, and communities to create an enabling environment; and reinforce attitudes, behaviors, and social norms conducive to social cohesion and resilience against conflict through meaningful community engagement process by ensuring 1) Improved information access as well as inter-personal and communication skills for social, economic empowerment of youth and women mobilized through village saving and credit loans for cash assistance to groups, 2) Improved self-confidence, motivation, and skills to make informed decisions and take appropriate action promoted at community, household, and individual level for making income generating activities, 3) Empowered communities, adolescents, and children, especially the most marginalized, to take part in collective situation analysis, decision-making and advocacy to improve their wellbeing, 4) Increased awareness, preparedness, capacities, resilience, and direct engagement of affected populations in humanitarian Action, and behavior change 5) Improved data quality and use for decision-making at County and State levels.The target group includes unaccompanied and separated children (UASC), children associated with armed forces and armed groups, children victims of or at risk of GBV, and domestic violence. There is a timely need for protection services in Koch and Pariang. GUNO’s current program in Koch and Pariang identifies of child abuse and exploitation and UASC have been identified and there is still consistent need to proceed with further identification, registration and reunification with their families and this is to be full accomplished through proposed activities such as; Unaccompanied and Separated girls and boys identified, documented, and receiving family tracing and reunification services (UASC) ; Psychosocial Support for Children who are affected by the Conflict; Mine Risk Education; Education for special need for disability Children and Adult; Cash-based Individual Protection Assistance; GBV Prevention Awareness and Case Management; UXOs/Mine Risk Awareness community campaigns; Peace Building engagements among affected children and adults and training on child protection in emergencies (CPiE) including Case management and PSS.

2. Project activities

With our already existing child protection field team, our tested community child protection networks are to aid identification and documentation of unaccompanied and separated children as well as, identify befitting foster care families that are in effect to provide a durable solution to the UASC. All activities are to embrace messaging for peace building and peaceful coexistence, avoidable of Gender-based Violence and awareness of the dangers of UXOs. The project will provide life-saving child protection prevention and response services through the provision of comprehensive case management services and referrals for children with critical protection concerns; Activity (1) Establishment of 8 temporary child-friendly spaces with modern facilities ( 4 in each of the two locations in Rubkona and Pariang targeting (2,000 girls and 2,000 boys each location) Activity (2) - Unaccompanied and Separated girls and boys identified, documented, and receiving family tracing and reunification services (UASC) targeting 300 (70 girls and 80 boys) in Koch and Pariang (80 girls and 80 boys). Activity (3) - Psychosocial Support for Children who are affected by the Conflict targeting 10,000 (3,000 girls and 3,000 boys) in Koch (2,000 girls and 2,000 boys) in Pariang. Activity (4) - Mine Risk Education among 12,500 targets (2,000 women, 2,000 girls and 1,000 men and 2,000 boys) in Rubkona and (1,500 girls, 1,500 boys, 1,500 women, 1,000 men) in Pariang. Activity (5) - Cash-based Individual Protection Assistance to girls and boys supported with as part of Case management and referrals activity estimated to directly reach 100 persons (40 girls and 20 boys) in Rubkona and (25 girls, 15 boys) in Pariang. Activity (6) GBV Prevention Awareness and Case Management activity estimated to directly reach 3000 targets (500 women, 1000 girls, and 500 men and 1000 boys) in Koch and (50 women, 100 girls,50 men, 50 boys) in Pariang. Activity (7) Training on Child protection in emergencies (CPiE) including Case management and PSS estimated to directly reach 200 persons (200 women, 100 girls, and 300 men, and 100 boys). The activity will target 200 persons in Rubkona and 500 persons in Pariang. Activity (8) Training to care givers in order to give PSS services to UASC and CAFAAG in the Rubkona and Pariang targeting 300(200 women and 100 men). Activity (9) formation and establishment 10 Girls forum (5 in each location and targeting 4000 children; 2000 boys and 200 girls). Here the beneficiaries of these project /poor students/ shall meet once annually to discuss their issues. Support to the poor boys and girls. Beneficiaries will be the poorest of poor children. Support to poor girls the selected poor girls will be supported through education. During their education there are some provisions that need to be fulfilled. These are stationary materials, clothing, and medical expense. Establishing and strengthening girls’ clubs in different schools. Empowering the poor girls supporting the girls through provisions is not a sustainable activity. To sustainably support them their families, have to be empowered economically. To this end, there is a plan to provide them with small ruminants. Then their families shall breed the animals to support themselves and their children in the future.
