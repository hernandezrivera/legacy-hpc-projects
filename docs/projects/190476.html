Project Summary

Ongoing hostilities in Benishangul Gumuz Region in western Ethiopia continue to affect the lives and livelihood of communities and increase humanitarian needs. The conflict was contained in Metekel Zone since mid-2020, but it has spread to Kamashi and Assosa zones, as well as to Mau Komo Special Woreda in recent months. Hundreds of thousands of people have fled looking for safety and assistance, with the majority displaced inside Kamashi and Metekel Zone, while some have fled to neighboring Amhara and Oromia regions. The ongoing armed conflict has displaced a total of 410,000 people (Multi-Sectoral Rapid assessment Report 2-3 February 2022). The emergency shelter and non-food items Kit for the IDPs and host communities project is planned to be implemented in Mizyiga, Zayn, Sedal and Bulen woredas in Kemashi and Metekel Zones of Benishangul Gumuz Regional State. Due to the conflict and high needs of IDPs and host communities, Mizyiga, Sedal, Zayi and Bulen woredas are prioritized by the ES/NFI cluster for HRP 2022. The woredas are extreme and severe. The overall goal of the project is aligned to CLSHL/CO1 and CLSHL/CO2; ensure contextualized access to lifesaving shelters and NFIs for displacement affected populations with a focus on the most vulnerable and at-risk population group to safeguard their health security, privacy, and dignity and Contribute to improving protection outcomes by ensuring the conflict/displacement affected people have adequate safety, dignity, well-being, and equitable access to shelter solutions. To achieve the project goal, ASDEPO will propose the following activities; Provision of Non-food items to reduce the likelihood of health and protection consequences(4360HHs), Provision of NFI Kits to the persons with disabilities, older people and persons with chronic illness(769HHs), Provision of Emergency Shelter and NFI that consider the needs of women, children, people with disabilities and improve the beneficiaries safety and security (4361), Provision of ESNFIs to the persons with disabilities, older people and persons(770HHs ) , provide PSEA training for its frontline staff as well mainstreaming gender(20 frontline staff of ASDEPO and 30 staff from stakeholders, Provide awareness on COVID-19. ASDEPO will put measures in place to prevent the spread of COVID-19 as well as teach the HHs during the distribution of ESNFIs kit, conducting PDM. During the selection of beneficiaries, women, girls, and people with disabilities will be given a priority. The required budget for this proposes project is 1,670,497 USD for the consecutive six months. 70% of the project will be IDPs and 15% for host communities as well as 15% will be pwds’ affected by conflict. ASDEPO will reach 56,436 people in four woredas.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

ASDEPO will work closely with partners – INGOs, NNGOs, clusters, the government who have also long experience in the sector implementations and have grounded and proven expertise in implementing humanitarian response to reinforce the long-term impact. There are currently limited organizations providing emergency shelter and non-food items kit responses for IDPs and host communities in the area. When developing ASDEPO live-saving activities, prioritization of activities was done to ensure the complementarity of emergency shelter and non-food items kit activities with other operating partners and ensure geographical complementarity. The deployment of the humanitarian personnel will enhance the coordination forums both at the zonal and woreda level for better response services to IDPs and host communities are found in the targeted woreda. The emergency ES/NFI Cluster Strategy Plan tracks the ongoing and planned responses of cluster members and is specifically designed to support complementarity and coordination. Throughout this project, ASDEPO will take into account the continued and active involvement and participation of the community, particularly women, and stakeholders. ASDEPO will advocate at the zone level to link efforts by organizing monthly cluster coordination meetings to evaluate the sector activity implementations and humanitarian situation of the zone with other partners and UN agencies. ASDEPO will actively participate in national and sub-national ES/NFI clusters and HLP WG.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

. To further ensure accountability of the project and project staff, a feedback mechanism will be put in place (suggestion box, suggestion registration book and hotline calls, as well as case, can report anonymously. This will primarily consist of community meetings, segregated by sex and age and with an emphasis on persons with specific vulnerabilities such as persons with disabilities, in which beneficiaries are encouraged to give feedback or voice complaints/in person at the field office. This method is proposed given the low level of literacy which rules out written feedback. Moreover, ASDEPO M&E staff, not involved in project delivery, will also be involved, so beneficiaries feel confident to report problems. Feedback collected from these meetings will be reviewed by program staff, who will adjust interventions accordingly, where appropriate. Notes will be taken, and actions taken reported back to the next community meeting.
