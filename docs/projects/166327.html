Project Summary

This project will undertake food assistance and provision of access COVID 19 prevention campaigns to affected persons in Jere, MMC, Ngala and Monguno in Borno State which will contribute significantly to the healthy, physical and mental well-being of IDPs. There are however going to be challenges and changes in relation to the covid 19 pandemic.
We acknowledge that many project promoters and partners are in a challenging situation given the current covid 19 threat and may need to postpone or cancel activities, but for us, the project and activities thereof are still feasible and will go ahead but with little changes/challenges in the implementation strategies which will justify the increase in the funding requirements. These changes/Challenges will only be communicated before project kick-off and during project revisions. The Newly increased funding requirement by USD200,000 will go towards funding the increased costs related to the previously budgeted non-covid project activities, activities of Preparedness and Response Plan towards the covid 19 threat as follows: Buying and shipping essential supplies such as masks, gloves and other protective wears/gears for frontline workers, Producing guidance for the POCs and for particular groups on measures to take to prevent the spread and prevent themselves and others, Producing evidence based guidelines and advice, make sure health workers and responders get the information and training to detect and refer affected patients for the treatment and also cater for the outrageous rise in the cost of project activities as budgeted previously due to the COVID19 pandemic. SHO also anticipates the creation of isolation centres in relation to the COVID19 pandemic, which will increase the number of POCs, hence the justification for the increase of target beneficiaries from 40,000 to 50,000, i.e. additional 10,000.

2 - Activities

Preparedness and Response Plan towards the covid 19 threat will require SHO to carry out activities as follows: Buying and shipping essential supplies such as masks, gloves and other protective wears/gears for front-line workers and POCs, Producing trainings and sensitizations for the POCs and for particular groups on measures to take to prevent the spread and prevent themselves and others, Producing evidence based guidelines and advice, making sure health workers and responders get the right information and training to detect and refer affected patients for treatment, advocacy visits towards human decongestion, hygiene requirements both at the households and community levels, fending for children whose parents are held in isolation centers and also catering for the outrageous rise in the cost of project activities as budgeted previously due to the covid 19 pandemic. The anticipated creation of isolation centres in relation to the covid 19 pandemic in some locations that may include those intended to be managed by SHO will increase the number of POCs, hence the justification for the increase of target beneficiaries from 40,000 to 50,000, i.e. additional 10,000 which will come as a result of the additional wet ration feeding needs of the isolated IDPs with the COVID 19 virus.SHO will also carry out construction of communal kitchens which would also serve as nutrition centres in targeted locations to compliment anticipated isolation centres for providing COVID19 infected patients with the required quantity and quality of wet-feeding ration of food, since the dry-ration of food would be of no use to them at the critical situation, these patients would include new arrivals and other POCs with COVID19 infection. These procedures will apply standard precautions according to WHO recommendations (such as respiratory and hand hygiene measures) for all patients as well as implementing additional precautions (including contact and droplet precautions and airborne precautions for aerosol-generating procedures) for any cases where COVID19 infection is suspected.SHO will also cater for new arrivals who have lost their means of Livelihood as a result of the COVID19 pandemic through the provision of wet-food rations with diversified diets at the doorsteps of the most vulnerable of this category of POCs.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

SHO shall collect feedback from the vulnerable members of the community through FGDs (Focus Group Discussions) and installation of suggestion boxes and help-desks, securely placed in camps and host communities for reporting. To enhance relevance and quality of responses, it will further maximize the impact of the intervention, enhance the targeting of beneﬁciaries and avoid the harm potentially generated by the programme and increase the safety of the staff and beneﬁciaries during the intervention. Our complaints and feed back mechanism will be accessible, effective, conﬁdential and well understood with a view to improving programming, assisting in understanding beneﬁciary and community perceptions, promoting beneﬁciary empowerment and assisting in the early detection of problems such as targeting, misconduct including sexual exploitation and abuse, food/covid 19 supplies diversion and fraud. It will respond to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can immediately be put in place.
