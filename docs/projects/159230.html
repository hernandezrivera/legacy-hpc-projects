Project Summary

Droughts and regular conflicts in have increased protection concerns especially for displaced populations on transit routes, in areas of displacement and newly accessible areas. Analysis of SOYDA registrations of separated, unaccompanied and missing children peaked during conflict and flooding displacement periods throughout 2018 and 2019; this puts boys at higher risk of recruitment into armed groups, as well as potentially perpetrators of sexual violence. Furthermore, the South West Sate Child Protection Working Group (CPWG) analysis indicates that the mostly common coping strategy adopted for Unaccompanied and separated children (UASC) is through the kafala/traditional foster care system; this is especially true for girls who are often "hidden UASC" and hence at risk of exploitation and other forms of abuse. Most of the people perceive that sexual violence had increased since the last offensive and that girls are most commonly stressed by this risk. Analysis of South Central Gender based violence information management systems (GBVIMS) trend analysis (Jan 2018-Sep 2019 ) shows that 42% of cases were rape and sexual assault and 44% physical assault with the majority of survivors being IDPs (83%) and female
The objective of this intervention is to strengthen case management through direct service provision to unaccompanied and separated children (UASC), children at risk of protection violations and referral services for survivors of gender-based violence (GBV). This includes providing training and mentoring to the CP and GBV staffs of SOYDA including CPiE especially targeting in Daynile, Dharkenley, Kahda districts of Mogadishu, and Afgoye and Marka district of lower shabelle IDP camps. The project aims to provide immediate lifesaving assistance to vulnerable populations. Beneficiaries will receive comprehensive case management and psycho-social support (PSS), including emergency care, referral to SOYDA medical teams trained in clinical management of rape (CMR), access tailored material assistance, basic emotional support, and follow up on the services. Capacity building of CP community structures and committees to strengthen and improve identification of cases. The key focus will be to scale up prevention and response to separation of children from their families through family tracing and reunification activities and gender based violence survivors.
The project will establish referral system in schools and support community-based Child Protection Mechanisms for quick and efficient referrals of children with protection needs through service mapping within the community and in the schools (community child protection committees and School Management Committees). This project will strengthen service mapping to facilitate appropriate referrals, coordination and linkages in service delivery to ensure quality comprehensive services to unaccompanied and separated children and gender based violence (GBV) survivors
In addition, in Daynile, Dharkenley, Kahda districts of Mogadishu, and Afgoye and Marka district of lower shabelle. Region, SOYDA will conduct and strengthen protection monitoring in areas of settlement of returnees and IDPs through community based protection monitors working with CSOs in order to ensure that quality evidence based reports about protection issues and human rights violations and systematic collection of protection related data are produced to inform better response and advocacy.

Project Activities

1. Safe and confidential identification and case management process of 400 GBV survivors and children survivors of violence, referral and follow-up. With the support of community sensitization on available services & referral pathways, its expected, that more survivors will seek services. The survivors themselves will also be requested to keep the identification visit to them and not share with people they do not trust. Referral will be through phone calls to make sure that only those working in the case have the information. Follow ups will also be done through support lines and confidential visit2. Case management and psychosocial support for 200 GBV survivors (10 Men, 120 women and 70 girls) in Daynile, Dharkenley, Kahda districts of Mogadishu, and Afgoye. And Marka district of lower shabelle. Region, provided by 12 GBV/IDTR case workers, 18 Community mobilizers and 6 community engagement officer specifically focusing on child sexual abuse. Strengthening one GBV stop centers within the existing health facilities in Marka district districts (the support includes Staffing, psychological support and referral to the existing legal entities for the GBV survivors).3. Conduct distribution of dignity kits to 3000 female headed household, who are at risk of GBV or have been exposed to GBV. Vulnerable women especially, women with disabilities and female headed households will be given consideration for receiving dignity kits. 4. Conduct community-led GBV and CP awareness campaign to improve access to response services will be supported by the community mobilizer and community engagement officer. This will include initial identification and training on GBV & CP core concepts, safe referral, and basic emotional support and community mobilization methods for 30 community volunteer focal points in the target locations5. Distribute portable solar lamps to 3000 most vulnerable persons in the community and the host communities. Women and girls facing protection risks will be issued with solar lamp that lightens their homestead and the environment they live in in order to to reduce risks of abduction and rape and other protection risks.6. GBV Case management and PSS first aid training for 10 SOYDA Project staff, this includes and Project officers to strengthen their capacity in case management for seven days before going to the field. Also incorporating referral training to ensure safe and ethical referral of survivors this will help us ensure that we have a survivor centered approach7. Conduct identification, documentation, tracing and reunification (IDTR) for 500 UASC (50% boys and 50% girls) provided by trained and experienced IDTR/CP case workers in Marka. IDTR process is based on agreed standards with UNICEF adapted from the Inter-Agency Guidelines for UASC.8. SOYDA will facilitate community based psychosocial and recreational activities through individual and group therapy sessions, and other psychosocial interventions for children and their caregivers. Caregivers support sessions will be organized and Training of Teachers and Child Friendly Space facilitators/volunteers in the IDP camps. 9. Conduct training and mentoring for CPWG members on IDTR, case management, basic emotional support and CPIMS for UASC. Training will be conducted (total of 6 days each for 40 persons - 50% Male/Female) based on CPWG AoR Case Management Training Manual and IA Guidelines on UASC.10. Contribute to the protection monitoring system through monthly Key Informant Interviews. 11. SOYDA will identify and train 60 community-based child protection structures such as community child protection committees, children’ clubs, young people’ groups, School Management Committees in Daynile, Dharkenley, Kahda districts of Mogadishu, and Afgoye. and Marka district of lower shabelle. Mitigation Measures

The following are the main protection risk related to exclusion of the vulnerable population upon displacement during nature adversity includes increased sexual gender based violence towards women and vulnerable girls, separation and lack of foster care among the children hence discrimination,abuse,child labor, rape,marginalization,diseases,ealier marriage by teenage girls and insufficient or/an undocumented vulnerable people needs and poor information sharing, lack of coordination as well as increase sexual exploitation among the girls and women in the IDP camp. SOYDA will be mitigating this through establishments of strong referral pathways during displacement as well as ensure boys, girls and women are given first priority in the emergency intervention, SOYDA will also provide integrated protection component within its intervention to ensure quick supply of prophylaxis to enable reduce the risk related spread of infection as well as liase with local authority in more coordinated version to enable bring the culprit of rape to the books and thus either be referred to the local authority for punishment. On mitigation of separated or unaccompanied children SOYDA will undertake reunification process by using the social media and electronic media as well as radio and TVs and this will ensure wider information sharing hence ease the reunification process for these affected families SOYDA will strictly respect the labor law of the country and will not engage in any activities that are going to facilitates abuse of children right, it will however, conduct wider community campaign and awareness to enable the community respect the children right and stop any form of child labor. It will also strive to provide equal opportunities for both girls and boys as well as marginalized, minorities and disable children hence improve the disparities among these in needs.SOYDA will also ensure proper information sharing and timing of session with target communities. The following are some of the preventing risk associated with exclusion on gender and age such are, lack of consultation among the adolescent girls in the various camps about their sexual reproduction, safety and dignity hence this will create an avenue for oppressed girl child at the age of adolescent, also such exclusion as special group in decision making process in the camp might hinder proper advocacy and awareness among the disable, minority and marginalized population in the IDPs and host communities hence poor diversity and resilience building. For more effective and efficient mitigation SOYDA shall propose to have equal men, women, boys and girls in both services centered activities as well as employment within the target/operational areas in Afgooye and Mogadishu IDPs, Also on the issue of decision making. We shall route for proper representation of all interested group within the society such as disable, minority and marginalized as well as elderly and single mothers in these targeted IDPs and host communities. SOYDA will be will to cater the needs of both sex and age and shall strive to ensure all are equally served whether on employment opportunity or service provision with the target areas, it will also provide proper coordination mechanism that will enable the protection focal point within the community and project team work in harmoniously for preservation of beneficiary data without any alteration of their information or sharing with an external party to avoid any risk of discrimination among the beneficiaries. In the instances for the distribution of dignity kits, material assistance as well as non-foods items SOYDA will ensure equal share are provided by all the target vulnerable children boys, girls, separated, unaccompanied children & women within the target IDPs and host communities, will also ensure to advocate within the community in consultation with stakeholders within the project area that these children to prevent child soldiering
