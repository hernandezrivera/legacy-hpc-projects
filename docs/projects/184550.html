Project Summary

According to Somalia Humanitarian needs overview, a total of 6.4M people are in need of WASH services of these 4.1 M are children ,1 M women ,965,700 people with disability while 254,500 are elderly.The high humanitarian WASH needs in Somalia are driven by seasonal floods, severe drought conditions, acute water diarrhea/ cholera outbreaks and protracted crises, exacerbated by COVID-19 outbreak. Drought and protracted conflicts are the main drivers of WASH needs in Somalia leading to displacement and communal conflict over water points. From February to May 2021, 50 of the 74 districts in Somalia faced severe water shortages and water prices increased by 66% in Gedo, Galgadud, Mudug, Puntland, and Somaliland communities in drought hotspot districts rely on temporary water trucking and unprotected shallow wells that dry up in the dry seasons. In addition, flash floods contribute to the severity of needs among riverine communities, in middle, lower Shabelle, and Hiraan regions seasonal floods damaged 82 per cent of water and sanitation infrastructure triggering spikes in AWD/ cholera cases.430 The resulting lack of adequate WASH services to the population is a main cause of spread of Acute Diarrheal Diseases (ADD), leading to malnutrition and contributing to the existing stunting rate of 25.3% in children below 5 years of age.
As per the findings of HNO and JMNAC,2021 the majority of the IDPs settlements in these districts are heavily congested and have also proportionally received the largest number of new arrivals and is faced with a huge task to provide basic social services to population. The increasing new arrivals and returnees are either hosted by other IDP households or staying in makeshift shelter structures. Hosted IDPs are putting pressure on the already inadequate resources for protracted IDPs while those staying in makeshift shelters are exposed to natural whether elements such as cold at night, domestic violence, rains and hot sun during the day. Water is the greatest need in the community and has remained a serious challenge in the target district. Water is mainly supplied by the private individuals, who currently lack the capacity to meet the ever-increasing demand due to booming population with the daily influx of IDPs. IDPs are mostly affected by the water problem and the assessed IDPs cannot also afford the water since they don’t have an income to sustain this. The sanitation situation in the area is 1 latrine per 500 persons. Many people are living with disabilities inflicted from conflict and are unable to access any water consumption points. Protection concerns among women and people with disability are also at an alarming rate where GBV cases are reported daily among the IDP settlements.
Against the backdrops, therefore, PDA intends to provide enhanced water, sanitation and hygiene promotion as well hygiene kits and sanitation too to the affected population including disable group in Middle Shabele, Hiran and Mudug region target district.
PDA will ensure inclusion of disable person in the target location through enhance representation and participation of disable group in project employment, beneficiaries hence improving their representation and decision making within the community, PDA will also ensure accountability to the disadvantage group as well as enhance prevention on sexual exploitation and abuse among its beneficiaries by ensuring its staff are well aware of organization policy on PSEA and code of conduct before the initiation of the program

1. - Projects activities

1.1 Provide safe drinking water to 3000 men,women,boys,girls including disable drought, floods and conflict displacement affected population through rehabilitation/construction of 6 strategic boreholes in 6 droughts, flood and AWD prone areas through replacement of electro-mechanical systems into installation of hybrid solar systems construction of elevated tanks, extension of distribution system, construction of new water kiosks in the three target district hence providing life-saving clean water, PDA will ensure the rehabilitated water point is friendly for disable and elderly people 1.2 Construction/rehabilitation 10 communal water points including extension of water point by constructing water kiosks in IDPs for protection purpose in the project target location, PDA will ensure 10% of the extended water point is exclusive for disable population 1.3 Rehabilitate 20 community water points including roof water catchments in hard to reach rural and IDPs hence reaching 10,000 vulnerable boys,girls, women and disable group in the target district 1.4 Build the capacity of 60 water point management committees and 60 community based hygiene promotion committees in target communities including IDPs with a focus on gender parity. 1.5 Train pump technicians and preposition pump maintenance kits for 32 water points including 15 strategic boreholes, of these 5% will be disable people 1.6 Construction of 80 HH emergency shared latrines in IDPs, AWD/Cholera hotspot areas and drought affected villages reaching 4,000 vulnerable women, boys, girls and disable persons 1.7 Rehabilitate/Desludge 100 flood affected latrines (10% for PLWD) in the three region target district 1.8 Construction of 100 gender segregated latrine blocs (10% PWD) with hand washing stations in health/nutrition facilities and public schools with attention to ensuring proper sanitary facilities for girls, in line with SPHERE standards, PDA will also ensure disable person are also consulted on the design, location and safety of the area thus ensuring proper consultation and participation of the program activity 1.9 Distribution of 50 sanitation tools to drought affected communities, schools and health/nutrition facilities i.e. wheel borrow, rakes, spades, pick axes for debris clean up 1.10 Conduct 24 comprehensive community hygiene promotion sessions to promote good hygiene and sanitation practices in targeted communities, health/nutrition facilities and schools, PDA will distribute hygiene kits to vulnerable women including disable person emphasis will be female headed household and families with malnourished children hence targeting to reach 13,200 people 1.11 Conduct community meetings to sensitize people on women inclusion in the management of community WASH committees 1.12 Conduct woman to woman sessions on hygiene promotion to focusing on menstrual hygiene management (MHM) and safe drinking water within the family
