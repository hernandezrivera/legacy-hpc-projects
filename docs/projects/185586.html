Project Summary

GFO is proposing this project to expand lifesaving GBV and child protection in response to the HNO developed to identified protection vulnerability of women and child in the 3 States of Darfur and Khartoum State.
The economic crisis, including inflation, the increase of prices including foods exacerbated by COVID-19 all over Sudan and the political and Inter-communal conflicts around in Darfur, beside the low economic status with no or little livelihood opportunities, lack of community awareness on women’s rights and GBV due to traditional and social norms all these factors contributed to limited access to emergency and lifesaving GBV, child protection.
The GBV intervention target women, men, girls and boys in Shattaya and Al Salam localities in SD, 6000 ( 2700 women, 1200 men, 1400 girls and 700 boys )will be reached by GBV awareness, 25 social\ case worker (15 F, 10 M) will be training on GBV Case management, 100 GBV survivors will received direct support, 50 frontline service providers (25F,25M) from law enforcement, police, health service workers will train on basic GBV concepts, PSEA, PFA , 600 women and girls through CM services, 50 Non GBV actors (25 F, 25M) through GBV Mainstreaming training, 30 CBPN members committee consist of 15 members(female represent 85%), 600 women targeted on life and vocational skills training, 600 women accessing women and girl safe spaces per month, 1200 women and 800 girls received dignity kits two WGSS will be established, in addition to establish 2 GBV confidential Corners
The CP intervention will target overall 4,000 minors from Beliel and Al Salam localities in SD. The majority of children in Beliel and Al Salam are all internally displaced by continuous violence and show high level of psychosocial distress and various other protection concerns. Children targeted by the FTR component of the proposed project will be approximately 160 UASC and 200 OVC; while children targeted by CBPSS activities are anticipated to be at least 4,000 attending GFO CFS on a weekly basis and 100 children of 10 years old and above targeted with specific resilience building workshops.
Moreover, approximately 300 adults will directly benefit from GFO CP programme in SD from the entire community. GFO is currently employing only IDPs as staff members in both locations, of which 32 community members were trained as FTR workers and 34 as PSS facilitators
The ES/NFI intervention will target 2000 HH, 10,150 individuals (45%women, 27%men, 10%boys, 18%girls) in El Salam, Shattaya and Beliel localities in SD through Distribution of life-saving non-food items to crisis affected 1600 HH, Distribution of life-saving emergency shelter to crisis affected 800 HH, Distribution transitional shelter to vulnerable protracted 400 HH, Training 150 ( 95F, 55 M) on shelter material construction as a source of income generating activity
Beneficiaries are reached with CP and GBV prevention and response ES/NFIs service packages through a community centered approach with engagement of relevant sectors. Activities and interventions were designed in close consultation with beneficiaries.

The project will implement through close coordination with ES/NFI WG, CPWG and GBVWG at state level and national level in addition to the line ministries to ensure that all stakeholder involve during all project phases, planning, implementation, M&E

1 - Needs

GBV , CP remains significant problem in Sudan according to the 2021 HNO a total of 4.6 million people in need of protection out of them 2.6M in need of the CP interventions, 2.3M in need of the GBV interventions in Sudan. violent clashes between an armed militia and the Fur tribe in and around Jabel Marrah, Clashes erupted on 23 April 2021 across multiple villages to the west of Nyala town, namely, Gussa Jammat, Mansoura, Sersera, Bilili, Miro Kabar, Habanyia and Dambo Dambo. violent clashes that erupted between an SLA/AW faction and the Bani Hussein tribe on 5 June 2021 and inter-communal conflict between Falata and Taesha tribes on 6 June 2021 in Mandowa village, Um Dafoug Most of these villages have been burned. Many people displaced to different localities such as Al Salam, Beilel, Mersheng, and some fled to Kalma IDP camp adding to the inter-communal conflict, The economic crisis, including inflation, and a surge in food prices, exacerbated by COVID-19, resulted in record-high numbers of food-insecure people. The COVID-19 measures significantly affected commodity movement, market functionality and cross-border trade, and compromised livelihoods and daily labour opportunities. This reduced households’ purchasing power and access to food for the most vulnerable people, in addition, to the poor quality of available services heath, PSS, all these factors contributed to limited access to emergency and lifesaving GBV, child protection and ES/NFI services. In May 2021 GFO conducted GBV, Child protection and ES/NFI need assessment in in Central Darfur: Mukjar, Bendisi and Umdukun localities and also East Darfur in Shaeriya, Eddaen and Adila localities. The assessment aimed to have an understanding of the vulnerabilities faced by IDPs, in particular women and girls, as well as to identify root causes of GBV , gaps in GBV services and factors limiting access to available services. In South Darfur, the assessment covered Mersheng, Edel Fursan, Kas and Bileil localities. in Shattaya localities, in Beliel Town. In June 2021 GFO conducted an assessment in Khartoum Jabel Awlia, Umbada and Umdurman localities. The assessment teams looked at the health facilities and determine their capacity to deliver basic primary and reproductive health services. In additional the teams looked at Police station to determine their capacity to deliver safety and security for the GBV survivors. The teams used qualitative methods triangulated by using focus group discussions (FGD), in-depth interviews (IDI), consultative meetings,and a short questionnaire. FGDs were conducted among CBPN members, women, community leaders and youth. In-depth interviews were among primary beneficiaries. CP and GBV concerns have been identified in three localities . Specifically, IDPs children are not attending school and considering the high presence of underage armed actors, they represent an easy target for armed groups' recruitment. There is a high potential of conflict between host community and IDPs because of the increased pressure on limited resources. There are no protection services in the area: the need of a GBV prevention and response has been highlighted as an urgent priority as well as the CP and GBV mainstreaming into other sectors. In fact, in the latter, from the FGDs held with IDP community, GBV concerns were highlighted by the women, specifically early and force marriage and domestic violence. the main findings from the FGDs held by GFO assessment team for protection are as follows: GBV cases, forced and early marriages as source of income, and high level of domestic violence. There are not any service available in order to guarantee PSS to the GBV survivors. General identified needs: Risk of GBV, including domestic violence, physical and sexual abuse: from the FGDs held with the community, GBV concerns were highlighted by women and girls, specifically domestic violence and GBV as well as early and force marriage (as source of income)

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The proposed project aims at reinforcing GBV and child protection response in the areas of intervention, thus protection mainstreaming is a core component of each project activity. This includes strengthening referral pathways for provision of FTR and CBPSS support from other organizations and referral of CP and GBV issues and concerns to other service providers including general protection, education, NFIs, FSL, health services. This will ensure that protection and child protection and GBV mainstreaming is ensured at all levels of the intervention. in addition, GFO will conduct GBV Mainstreaming training targeting Health, Nutrition, Livelihood and WASH actors target 50 Non GBV actors.
