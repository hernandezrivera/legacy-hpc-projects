1 - Needs

New arrivals at the Pulka Transit camp dont have emergency shelter and Non- Food Items. Kanem Borno Human Development Association(KABHUDA) conducted a needs assessment on new arrivals in 16-17 October 2019 to assess the shelter needs of new arrivals particularly those who come to the Pulka transit camp. The needs assessment shows that the new arrivals may not get spaces in the existing temporary shelters in the Pulka existing IDP camps Thus this proposed project intends to provide shelter and NFI kits to new returnees and thus giving them a dignified place for temporary shelter and basic items for living

2 - Activities

This project would provide emergency shelter and NFI kits to new arrivals at the Pulka transit camp. News arrivals after been screened by IOM KABHUDA would select head of a household whether it is headed by a male, female or an adolescent person to distribute the shelter and NFI kits. The NFI distribution would be have initial, mid term and final distributions to ensure that the selected beneficiaries receive adequate NFI kits through out the project duration Post distribution surveys would be conducted after each round of distribution to ascertain the impact
