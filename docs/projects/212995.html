Project Summary

Through this project, Violet is planning to distribute regular and Emergency MPCs for the most vulnerable groups in and out of camps. through cash distribution, Violet aims to provide humanitarian assistance in a flexible and dignified approach. before the cash distribution, Violet will conduct the Rapid needs and Market assessment to verify the feasibility of cash distribution, and beneficiaries' preferences, but also to avoid the harm that may occur as a result of the project actions.
Violet is planning to conduct the following activities:
- One-off emergency distribution of MPC (150 USD) for 10,000 households affected by the displacements and forced evacuations or any natural disaster.
- Distribution of regular MPC (100 USD) for 8 distribution rounds to 4000 most vulnerable HHs with restricted se; selection criteria
So far, Violet has about 10% of the total appealed fund for 2024 only.

Needs:
Communities in north-west Syria have faced an onslaught of crises in 2023 – from earthquakes and ongoing food shortages to the most significant escalation of hostilities in four years. Now, the total population in NWS is 4.5 million, 4.1 out of them are in need for humanitarian assistance according to HNO 2023, considering The February 2023 earthquakes in north Syria and Turkey have added agony to an already. catastrophic situation, increasing the strain on services, causing displacement, and inflicting widespread damage. The earthquakes resulted in almost 6,000 deaths and more than 12,800 people injured in Syria, many families lost their main breadwinner due to death or injury, at a time when the economic situation was already dire, resulting in millions of people unable to meet their basic needs. Recovery from the earthquakes will necessitate long-term funding and improved emergency preparedness.
through this project, Violet is planning to distribute regular and Emergency MPCs for the most vulnerable groups in and out of camps. through cash and voucher distribution, Violet aims to provide humanitarian assistance in a flexible and dignified approach. prior to the cash or voucher distribution, Violet will conduct the Rapid needs and Market assessment to verify the feasibility of cash/ voucher distribution, and beneficiaries' preferences, but also to avoid the harm that may occur as a result of the project actions.
Implemenation:
Violet is planning to conduct the following activities:
- One-off emergency distribution of MPC (150 USD) for 10,000 households affected by the displacements and forced evacuations.
- Distribution of regular MPC (100 USD) for 8 distribution rounds to 4000 most vulnerable HHs with very restricted criteria
So far, Violet has about 10% secured fund out of the total money appeal for 2024.Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigating Measures
1- inability to respond to the IDPs within the first phase of their displacement: VO has an IDPs tracking initiative by setting communication channels with all LCs in the whole community which usually hosts IDPs, then once an emergency occurs, the LC will inform VO focal points to respond immediately.
2 - Cash liquidity: Violet has three active framework agreement with three vetted financial service providers
3- aid diversion and fee collection in return for Humanitarian assistance: VO will widely announce that humanitarian assistance is free of charge. additionally, VO will activate the CRM mechanism so the BNFs can report complaints regarding fee collection, and coordinate with OCHA and CWG if needed.
4- Exposure to sensitive data that could lead to various forms of harm for affected people and/or
Humanitarian staff.
• Breaches in data protection, data privacy, and data security.
• Potential misuse of data for non-humanitarian purposes by third parties.
• Loss of trust between affected people and humanitarian organizations
The actions that Violet is taking to ensure data protection are:
1- Ensure all cash staff are well-trained in data protection and privacy.
2- Map all related stakeholders who are dealing with the data (Organization staff, local authorities, financial service providers) to identify the data gap and possible duplication, and ensure there is a signed protocol or agreement with the related stakeholders which will protect the data of beneficiaries.
4- PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy
Violet targets PSEA risk through a) training on PSEA for staff, BNFs, and vendors; b) integrating PSEA in AAP with safe and accessible complaints mechanism; c) appointment of PSEA focal points in each location to handle reports of misconduct, d) support to survivors through case management, e) regular information of affected community on their entitlements.
Targeting: Violet has separated selection criteria for MPCA one -off and the repeated MPCA focusing on income, people at risk, gender, and nutrition, considering the selection criteria matrix is harmonized with CWG and other actors and will be consulted with the affected communities based on the needs, situation and updates on the filed.
Harmonization with CWG: Violet will coordinate with CWG to ensure using the harmonized tools for Needs assessment, market monitoring ( by coordinating with REACH ) baseline, and online for repeated MPCA as well as PDM.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Implementation: Through this project Violet aims to respond to the most vulnerable groups by distributing MPCs. the MPC is identified to be a flexible and dignified approach. prior to the MPC distribution, Violet will ensure the markets are functioning and the necessary items are available to avoid the harm that may occur because of the project actions. this will be done through conducting rapid needs and Market assessments in the targeted areas to check the feasibility of the modality considering the locations with high needs for MPCA. Moreover, Violet will ensure effective CRM mechanism to provide equal access to the different groups to raise feedback and suggestion. further, Violet will activate the referral mechanism and refer special cases internally between the Organization's projects and externally with other organizations To ensure the coordinated response, Violet will coordinate its activities in both Turkey (through the cluster and CWG) and Syria level with the humanitarian actors to avoid the duplication Violet will ensure the readiness to immediately respond following the shocks by signing a frame work agreement with a Hawala agents. After the cash distribution, Violet will conduct the PDM to measure the beneficiary’s satisfaction on the modality.
