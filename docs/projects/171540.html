Project Summary

WRRS will set up in the targeted settlement mobile site management teams for identification, selection, implementation, coordination and for monitoring purposes. Capacity building of the key players in the CCCM on the guiding principles, which will later lead to the formation of a special committee with representation from all the sectors. This will entail Basic training on internationally recognized CCCM training modules and complementary training/workshops on other relevant themes filling gaps identified through assessments Provision of the solar light lamps/ touch to the 3000 beneficiaries identified and registered in the targeted regions. Through the provision of multi-purpose cash transfer address basic site repair and improvements that respond to safety and physical risk concerns. Through cash-for-work, pre-identified skilled and unskilled IDPs are able to repair and maintain basic infrastructures that underpin a safe and risk-free environment. Where possible the organization will embark on settlement decongestion.Provide technical assistance and guidance in establishing or reinforcing existing community representative/committee structures as necessary (including women’s representation) Support and help facilitate IDP governance meetings. Support and help facilitate site coordination meetings with relevant partner’s/service providers. HLP awareness raising interventions to 3000 beneficiaries on duty bearers on HLP, this will be achieved with the assistance of the formed committee groups.

Project Activities

The most vulnerable settlements in Berdale,Kismayo and Afmadow Districts with large numbers of needy IDPs, including those with recent arrivals who lack services, will be selected. 9 Settlement planning trainings will be conducted for 300 participants and will target community leaders, elders and local authority leaders and equip them with skills that will facilitate participatory management and maintenance of public spaces and fire breaks in the future. The site plan will be developed for the targeted settlements to provide spaces for essential/basic infrastructure services, public/communal spaces, fire breaks, main streets, lighting system and housing. This will be done in close consultation with the CCCM cluster, benefiting women, girls, boys and men, local and central authorities, religious and traditional leaders.In addition, WRRS will conduct 3 coordination meetings during the entire project period, which will include partners working in the same area. Each meeting will consist of 15 participants from different organization. The purpose of these coordination meetings is to avoid any duplication, sharing information and referral mechanisms.Mapping exercise will be conducted to find out the existing resources/infrastructures in the targeted settlements to identify gaps and inform responses in the target 4 districts, which will be shared with CCCM cluster and will be useful in creating master list, which will then be posted online and will be used by different humanitarian partners for data collection and their response analysis. This will enhance the strategic thinking of all stakeholders in terms of needs and gaps in particular settlement. Through this mapping exercises, it will be easy for all stakeholders to plan their activities related to particular settlement. In total, 3 mapping exercises will be conducted in Kismayo, Afmadow, Berdale and Baidoa to evaluate the existing capacity of resources.Community committees will be formed at settlement level to invigorate the existing community structures. Through these committees, coordination at field level will improve and access to information will be easy. These community committee member will be trained on information gathering and sharing. There will be 4W matrix, which will be shared by respective cluster coordinators, which will mainstream information sharing at all levels. Standard Operation Procedures will be adopted for each settlement in terms of information and coordination. Settlement leaders will have information on the figures of in and out, how many new IDPs have come to specific settlement and how many have left the settlement.Capacity Building training will be provided to existing settlement leaders to have improve access to information and accountability to affected populations. Through this training, settlement leaders (community committee members) will have understanding on counting IDPs in and out figures per settlement. Member from local authorities will also be benefited through participating in these training for information sharing (like 4W matrix) and coordination at settlement level.6 Community centers will be established/constructed at settlement level, which will be utilized for community meetings, training etc.These centers will also be used for focus group discussions and will have multipurpose outputs. The center will improve communication by creating a safe space where the community can meet and discuss their day to day affairs, resolve disputes, and share ideas. Furthermore, the center will also be utilized to strengthen referral path ways (HLP/GBV/CP), relay messages to the community through structured information sessions on HLP, and counsel HLP violation cases. WRRS will also establish a feedback and complaint mechanism that will be accessible to all the community members.

Mitigation Measures

Somalia has over the years reported cases of violence during distribution of aid/relief to mitigate this risk WRRS will ensure that the distribution sites for NFIs and ESKs are located in a well secured area and that security is provided to avert any incidences of Violence or GBV within the areas of distribution. The location will be selected carefully to ensure that beneficiaries are not exposed to any form of insecurity. The distribution centers will also be located near the IDP and Host community sites where there is an open space and that the security is not a threat to any beneficiary.The organization is well versed with the risks associated with SEA and therefore will work closely with all the stakeholders to ensure that the beneficiaries are not exposed to any form of sexual exploitation and especially during the delivery of project activities. Awareness raising on how to report the incidences of SEA will be done to all the organization stakeholders that is the staff, contractors and other stakeholders involved with the activities of the organization. A reporting mechanism for PSEA will be established to look into any issues related to Sexual exploitation during and even after the end of the project.WRRS will work with other partners in the area of operation where necessary to ensure that there is a proper referral of beneficiaries to services and especially the victims of Gender based violence and reported cases of child abuse.WRRS will ensure that all groups of beneficiaries are involved from the onset of projects, normally in Somalia people from marginalized clans and women have been left out in key decision making process and mostly they are not consulted. This is not going to happen for this project as WRRS will ensure that all the minority and marginalized persons will be involved in major decision making of the project. Women will be given priority and especially those from the marginalized clans. Persons living with disability will also be included in the project activities as in many instances they have been left out. The elderly will also be part of the key decision making of this project.WRRS will establish a complaint and feedback mechanism that will be accessible to all the beneficiaries in the project target area. WRRS will create awareness on the same to ensure that all the beneficiaries are well informed of the availability and use of the complaint feedback mechanism. A hot line will be established that will be accessible for all, a help desk will also be put in place for beneficiaries who have no access to a mobile phone can access the help desk. Confidentiality will be maintained in the CFM for beneficiary’s protection in instances of sensitive matters.WRRS will work closely with the land owners and the local government in instances where there will be land disputes as Somalia is well known of this kind of disputes. The organization will work with the authorities and more especially in areas where construction is anticipated to secure land tenure.In areas where WRRS work cases of explosives are rare although measures will be put in place to prevent the risk of explosive hazards in the target area.

Please provide further explanation

IDPs have limited options for durable solutions to their displacement. Accordingto JMCNA findings indicate that the majority of IDPs prefer local integration.This however has so far remained a challenge due to lack of land availability,repeated evictions and minimal livelihoods options. Returns to the area of originare extremely limited, as most IDPs have lost livelihoods and are reliant onhumanitarian assistance. WRRS will make use of the CCCM coordinationplatforms to advocate Housing, Land and Property rights for the displaced.Durable solutions are one of the key pillars of WRRS Somalia programming,and continuous efforts are directed towards promoting durable solution andself-sufficiency of the displaced and vulnerable people across Somalia.However, WRRS recognizes the complexity and long milestones required toreach this vision. This project will contribute towards supporting opportunitiesfor displaced people to achieve durable solutions given people canteredresponse, capacity building for both the government and IDP communities,linking community priorities with national government plans and as well asimplementing community-based initiatives that can promote communityrecovery. WRRS with the UNHCR post distribution monitoring team will worktowards ensuring accountability and proper registration of the beneficiariesverses the entitlements they have been issued with. The project will contributeto durable solutions efforts and especially in the targeted areas where thegovernment together with UNHABITAT are working on site planning andsettlement management in the existing IDP settlements. The support by thegovernment offers an environment that if well utilized will see many durablesolutions achieved. In line with the CCCM Cluster strategy, the proposed projectwill aim to provide specialized assistance for the most vulnerable andundeserved temporary settlements, enabling a livable environment for IDPs inwhich they receive timely and effective comprehensive response to their needs,in accordance with international humanitarian standards and aligned with thespecific needs of women, men, girls and boys. People with disability will befactored in in our intervention so that they can be in a position to benefit withthe humanitarian assistance being offered to the rest of the beneficiaries.
