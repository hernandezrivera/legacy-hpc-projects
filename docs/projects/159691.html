Project Summary

Two successive rain seasons, that is Deyr rains (October- November) 2018 and Gu rains (April-May) 2019 have registered below average amounts (FSNAU Port Gu report 2019). Leading to water deficit and poor pasture conditions (FSNAU Technical Release September 2, 2019) for pastoralist livelihood groups in Central Somalia. This has resulted in Galgadud Region being among areas classified as being in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) while other Mudug Region’s rural region areas falls in the category of areas classified as being in Stressed.
CPD proposes to provide 32,500 women, girls, boys and men with sustainable WASH services in Galmudug, Hirshabelle and Banadir regions of South Central Somalia. This will be done in line with the WASH Cluster objectives targeting areas with the highest PIN. CPD will construct 2 new strategic boreholes to serve 10,000 people, rehabilitate 3 others to serve 15,000 people, construct 15 berkads and 20 Shallow wells to serve 12,000 and 10,000 people respectively. 400 sanitation facilities will be constructed to serve 20,000 people and hygiene awareness carried out for all the target beneficiaries.

Project Activities

Water1. Construction of 2 boreholes and rehabilitation of 3 others, 20 shallow wells and 15 berkads. The boreholes will be equipped with generating sets, gen set rooms, caretaker's room, water tank , animal trough, water kiosks, pipes and provided with fuel subsidies. The shallow wells will be protected, provided with water tanks, animal troughs and water kiosks. This will be done in Galmudug, South West State, Hirshabelle and Banadir regions. It is expected that 32,500 women, girls, boys, men and alternative gender will be involved in the design, implementation and eventual management of these projects in order to have sustainable access to potable water.2. Distribution of 10 water bladders (each with a capacity of 10m³) to increase water access at strategic water points that lack adequate storage facilities. This will enable the beneficiaries get access to water in the four target regions. Vulnerable members of the community including women, girls, the elderly and the sick will have easy access to water within reasonable distance.3. Carry out 22 Water Management Committee trainings for 43 WASH committees (for 8 Strategic Boreholes, 20 Strategic shallow wells and 15 berkads). Gender balance of at least 50% for each gender will be a requirement for the establishment of the committees. The trainings will focus on water structure management, chlorination and safe water handling at field and house hold level.4. Establish emergency WASH systems like water trucking, bulk chlorination, household/point of use water treatment and safe storage and cash based programming. Sanitation5. Construction of 400 lockable gender segregated Emergency/communal latrines with hand washing facilities for IDPs and host communities in Galmudug, South West State, Hirshabelle and Banadir regions. This will include construction of 60 gender segregated twin latrines for 15 schools in the target areas. Facilities designs will be done in consultation with Camp Management Committees to take care of protection issues. Hygiene6. Carry out 5 no. 3-day hygiene promotion trainings for a total of 120 hygiene volunteers (both women and men) selected from the IDPs and host communities in the targeted areas. Thereafter, disseminate health and hygiene promotion messages to the target populations by the 120 trained hygiene volunteers using IEC messages, radio programmes, personal hygiene awareness, latrine use and food handling.7. Distribute 6,000 hygiene kits (comprising of Washing Soaps, Aqua tabs, water collection and storage jerry cans, sanitary towels for women and girls, and ORS sachets) to the most vulnerable IDPs and host communities in the targeted areas.8. Construction of a garbage pits in every IDP camp in the targeted 40 camps. 9. Liaise with Camp Management committees to incorporate protection measures in use of WASH facilities.10. Establish a robust Complains and Feedback Mechanism in all the project areas.11. CPD will draft a detailed work plan and implement through close monitoring of project activities on regular basis to ensure that all activities are being undertaken in accordance with agreed outputs. CPD will also ensure that beneficiary feedback is taken into account and feeds into the project learning to improve implementation efficiency. This will be done through established beneficiary feedback and complaints mechanism established. High quality photos with GPS and time stamps depicting various activities planned will be provided as documentary evidence of implementation. Monitoring visits to the sites will be organized for stakeholders. Final evaluations will be carried out after every completion of the projects.
