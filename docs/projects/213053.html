Project Summary

In 2024, 16.7 million people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced.
After 12 years of hostilities and persistent economic crisis, people in
Syria their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit northern Syria. As Syrians grappled with these shocks, economic indicators
took a further downturn, increases in commodity prices continued to
drive humanitarian needs in Syria. This resulted in an increase
in poverty and reliance on humanitarian assistance (HNO, 2024)
ONSUR is strategically oriented to use vocational skills training as a means of economic reconstruction in post-conflict areas. Through this project, ONSUR will conduct skills training for 500 from IDPs and host communities male and female, beneficiaries Of the 500 beneficiaries undergoing vocational skills training, 250 individuals will be male and 250 of them will be female. Based on the labor market survey conducted during the design of this project, men and women in Azaz have different training needs. Therefore, the topics of skills training will be different for male and female beneficiaries. ONSUR seeks to provide male beneficiaries with vocational sills trainings focused on the following topics
basics of mobile communication,
renewable energy and solar photovoltaic (PV) system
hairdressing and beauty therapy,
food and confectionary industry,
Women tailoring
Training timeline and duration: vocational skills training will be started from the fifth month of project implementation and are going to continue for a period of 4 months. Target beneficiaries are required to attend their relevant training sites at least 5 hours a day and 5 days a week.
Start-up toolkits: At the end of their four-month training, target beneficiaries are going to take exams and upon successful graduation, they will receive a start-up tool kit, valued at 500 USD. For the purpose of this project, a start-up toolkit is a set of the most important devices, equipment or instruments that are needed by the skill learners to start their own business. According to this, each skill learner will be provided with a toolkit suitable for the topic in which s/he is trained.
The project will give considerations for protection from GBV through the following :
ONSUR will assign a focal point for accountability, another one for PSEA and CSG (Child Safeguarding) who are accessible through direct phone contact numbers.
By the project beginning, all ONSUR Staff will attend the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA)
ONSUR has zero tolerance for any kind of abuse and exploitation against women, girls, and children
 The project will make sure that all the training center has well-functioning AAP/PSEA reporting channels as per UN Guidelines. the beneficiaries will be sensitized about the reporting channels in order to use them effectively and confidently .
ONSUR will coordinate with the GBV sub-cluster and GBV-specialized organizations in the region to enhance the capacity of field staff in dealing with PSEA and GBV cases
location of the training center will be decided in consideration of the women's preferences.
Also, the project will give equal opportunities to men and women to access the vocational training.
Time of training will be during daytime; the provision of the training will include women and other at-risk groups, 
Transportation support to and from training sites also will be provided by ONSUR.
To ensure disability inclusion. ONSUR will identify and assess the needs of persons with disabilities, from the start of the registration process in this emergency response project and throughout.
ONSUR will prioritize providing training through this project and other protection and assistance which ONSUR does in the project area to persons with disabilities on an equal footing with other persons of concern.
