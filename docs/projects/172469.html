Project Summary

Project title improve food, livelihood Assistance and protection services for South Sudanese and Central Africans refugees in Khartoum and Central Darfur States that will start from 1/1/2021 to 31/12/2021.
This project shall target Khartoum State localities of Shariq el Neil, Jebel Awlyia, Umdurman and UmBadda where as in Central Darfur with Central African refugees in Um Dukhun locality whom also needs humanitarian assistance in form food and livelihood and protection services. The project will be targeting 6000 refugees' household (5000 South Sudanese and 1000 Central African refugees) among them vulnerable women and youth for capacity building. The project will address agriculture, food security and livelihood, reduce protection risks, construction of women, child and multi purpose centers, conduct awareness raising and capacity enhancing of community members structure.
This project will target South Sudanese and Central African refugees women, men, boys, girls and elders including people with disability or people with special needs during the design and implementation. The project will design livelihood activities for targeted beneficiaries who are at protection risks and risks of exposure.
This project will have a positive impact that have mitigation measures to reduce negative impacts and improve benefits. This includes 70% of women will be targeted to improve fuel that includes FES, bio fuel and bio gas using agricultural and livestock waste that reduces forest destruction in Um dukhun and expose to risk of firewood collection and give chance for forest regeneration, maintenance of natural resources can be mitigated by involving women on manufacturing improved fuel efficiency stove which reduces cutting of trees for meal preparation and use of solar power for water pump by small scale winter season vegetable production which is environmentally friendly.
SORR will involve the community leaders from the start of the project starting from need assessment, design, and implementation and monitoring of the project. SORR together with community leaders shall establish a sustainable channel of communication on monitoring and taking decisions on better practices of the project implementation. Further SORR will create suggestion box or ledger book to file all complaints whether abuse or project is taking different path. SORR field and head office will have hotline cell phone designated for complaints to person who will be focal person.

1 - Needs

Khartoum rapid needs assessment conducted in September 2020 in Khartoum, Omdurman and Sharig el neil indicated the flood affected over 500,000 people across the country. The most affected were South Sudanese refugees who lives or settled in open areas camp like with temporary shelter which expose them protect risks. It further more affected their livelihood where they most depends on daily labour income where reduces the daily meal consumption.HNO 2020, indicated that food insecurity continued to worsen in 2019 due to continued economic decline and natural shock with half a million people were classified as IPC 3 food insecurity or worsen. In 2020, it is estimated that 2.6 million individuals - 14.2 % of the total population will need urgent and timely life-saving food security & livelihood support as well protection assistance.Late 2016 influx of South Sudanese refugees from various conflict localities to various states n Sudan including Khartoum State. In Khartoum the South Sudanese refugees were situated in open areas camp like settlement and in urban setup. The current economic decline and natural shock had affected the South Sudanese refugees both Urban and open areas where there are limited access to food, protection services. Therefore the South Sudanese refugees estimated 729,580 individuals where most of them living in open areas or camp like settlement needs humanitarian aids and protection services while South Sudanese refugees in Urban or cities needs protection assistance.This project shall target Khartoum State localities of Shariq el Neil, Jebel Awlyia, Umdurman and UmBadda where as in Central Darfur with Central African refugees in Um Dukhun locality whom also needs humanitarian assistance in form food and livelihood and protection services. The project will be targeting 6000 refugees household (5000 South Sudanese and 1000 Central African refugees) among them vulnerable women and youth for capacity building. The project will address agriculture, food security, livelihood, reduce protection risks, construction of women, child and multi purpose centers, conduct awareness raising and capacity enhancing of community members structure. This project will have a positive impact that have mitigation measures to reduce negative impacts and improve benefits. This includes 70% of women will be targeted to improve fuel that includes FES, bio fuel and bio gas using agricultural and livestock waste that reduces forest destruction and expose to risk of firewood collection and give chance for forest regeneration, maintenance of natural resources can be mitigated by involving women on manufacturing improved fuel efficiency stove which reduces cutting of trees for meal preparation and use of solar power for water pump by small scale winter season vegetable production which is environmentally friendly.
