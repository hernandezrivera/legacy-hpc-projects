Project Summary

Light rehabilitation for 25 Public/Private educational institutions; where the selection of the schools will be in coordination with MEHE and Education Sector lead/UNICEF. The Activities time frame is 3 months (September - November 2020) with an average cost of $22,000 per Public/Private school.

1 - Needs

25 Public/Private educational institutions/facilities (estimated 7500 students) in need for light rehabilitation within 3 months.

2 - Activities

Light rehabilitation for 25 Public/Private educational institutions; where the selection of the schools will be in coordination with MEHE and Education Sector lead/UNICEF. The Activities time frame is 3 months (September - November 2020) with an average cost of $22,000 per Public/Private school. Past Experience: Based on experience we had with USAID under LEAD and D-RASATI programs (previous job with CHF International -US based NGO; also known as Global communities). CHF International lead the rehabilitation for 300 public schools across Lebanon (other partners were Hariri Foundation for Sustainable Development and IOCC) and provided the system and checked the schools at risk (30 schools allover Lebanon) and their testing and recommendation for reinforcement and rehabilitation.
