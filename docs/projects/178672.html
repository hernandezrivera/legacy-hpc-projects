Project Summary

This project will aim to contribute to the preparedness and response plan PRP of WHO task force for COVID-19 in NWS under the pillars 2 and 7; Risk Communication and Community Engagement RCCE, and Infection Prevention and Control IPC, respectively. Through this project, WHOSD will deliver WaSH services to the Zoghara camp in Jarablus sub-district and Jarablus city. The activities will be implemented under one output, i) deliver humanitarian WaSH supplies, services, and improve hygienic behaviors and practices of IDPs in Zoghara camps. In Jarablus city and Zoghara camo the project will target a total of 23111 individuals in 4890 HHs (3000 in Jarablus city and 1890 in Zoghara camp) through raising awareness activities about COVID-19 public health prevention measures, relevant knowledge, and referral pathways. Additionally, each of the 1890 HHs will receive a WaSH NFI kit for COVID-19 preparedness and response.Furthermore, WHOSD will contract suppliers to install latrines, water tanks, and garbage containers in the camp, and to perform waste management and disposal, taking into account vulnerable groups.
Through this project, WHOSD will contribute to WHO Preparedness and Response Plan PRP for NWS by improving risk communication and community education. The intervention of WHOSD will be through the delivering of awareness messages to both host communities and IDPs. Raising-awareness will be conducted along with distribution of WaSH NFI kits among the target population to increase their resilience against the anticipated COVID-19 outbreak and reduce the expected incidence rate of the disease in the region, especially among IDPs in camps who are considered to be very vulnerable to the outbreak.
According to recent assessment conducted by IDA in the region and secondary sources of information, there are significant gaps and needs related to WaSH services in the area, especially within camps. The preparation to respond to the anticipated COVID-19 outbreak in the area are still limited. Furthermore, the response level among people is very poor in relation to the COVID-19 preventive measures due to the lack of income, lack of prevention means, and lack of awareness. It is highly expected that the occurrence of COVID-19 outbreak in the area will overwhelm the infrastructure services, including health and WaSH. Therefore, WHOSD will address the existing needs in both camps and non-camps settings as well as WaSH needs in health centers to contribute to the preparedness and response plan of WHO and UNICEF in NWS. The awareness activities will be carried out by two teams of hygiene promoters (2 females and one male of each) in camp and city. COVID-19-related preventive measures will be taken into account during the delivery of awareness sessions, including masks, alcohol swabs, and social distance. The awareness sessions will be for individuals rather than groups.

4 - Needs

Through previous PD with UNICEF, WH has targeted the old Zoghara camp, which consists of 10 sectors, the neediest sectors were targeted in the mentioned PD were 100% reliance on trucked water for the first 4 sectors were the expense is covered through the sectors residents with rate of 8 L/p/d, as for other 3 sectors, water reached them through network (once every 3 days) at a rate of 10l/P/d. Regarding latrines, percentage of in targeted sectors for latrines is 1:75. For SWM and garbage deportation, it was through LC once a week and most of population resorted to burning waste to get rid of it because of accumulation, especially in summer. As for hygiene supplies, all sectors were not targeted, and no kits were distributed to familiesAfter the completion of the implementation of project 31/12/2020, per capita daily share of water in the 7 targeted sectors increased, and proportion became 1:25, and the garbage were deported once every two days, and hygiene and prevention supplies from COVID 19 were distributedThrough this project services will be continued provided to previously targeted 7 sectors and to the additional families which they moved from informal camps by supplying water, collecting garbage, dislodging septic tanks, distributing HKs and cleaning latrines, in addition to targeting other 3 sectors, as water reaches them through network once every two days at a rate of 12L/p/d and through this project percentage will increase to 25 L individual’s share, also we will increase number of latrines to match recommendations of WASH sector through COVID 19 pandemic, percentage of latrines will become 1.25 water tanks and garbage continuers will be provided in addition to providing daily services and distributing HKs
