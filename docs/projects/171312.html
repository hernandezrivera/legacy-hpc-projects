Project Summary

This project will focus on GBV response in humanitarian contexts. The main focus will be
on service provision in line with SOPs and GBV guiding principles. The program will offer
immediate lifesaving services to survivors of GBV including comprehensive case
management, facilitating access to clinical management of rape services, dissemination
of key messages on GBV, referrals and capacity building of relevant actors to ensure
services are quality and responsive to the protection needs of women and girls. The
project will seek to enhance access to GBV services. IRC will provide Group and Individual PSS services at the safe spaces where women and girls (including survivors of GBV) will get psychosocial and
economic skills including counselling services, skills building, listening sessions and building
social networks. The safe spaces will be a hub for women and girls to build agency and
those that are survivors to learn strategies on how to protect themselves as well help
them get reintegrated in the community as they undergo the healing process. The IRC will
work with various relevant stakeholders to and will establish protection networks for women and girls and ensure women and girls are protected from GBV risks. Some of the stakeholders include the local administration, the police, women leadership at community level, and community and religious leaders.
Community action plans and protection mechanisms will be developed and implemented to increase reduction of protection risks.
GBV key messages will be disseminated to increase awareness on GBV and the available
services including when and where to access them - emphasis will be put on accessing
services within 72 hours of the incident. Dignity kits will also be provided to vulnerable
women and girls of reproductive age to restore dignity. Safety audits will be conducted to assess protection risks for women and girls and design appropriate responses to address the identified risks. IRC will continue participating in GBV Sub-cluster coordination meetings and co-chair at Galmudug level and provide capacity building for GBV Actors on relevant GBV topics. IRC will also continue working with GBV Actors in order to strengthen referrals and enhance access to GBV services.

Mitigation Measures

1. - Train IRC Staff and GBV Actors on SEA - All staff to sign Code of Conduct - Increase awareness among beneficiaries on SEA- Create Awareness on Complaint And Feedback Mechanism and act on all complaints received.2. -Create awareness on available GBV services . - Establish , strengthen and disseminate referral mechanisms in target locations. -Increase provision of assisted referrals - Strengthen coordination for effective referrals.3. - Improved targeting to ensure inclusion of the marginalized . - All needs assessments and consultations to include members from the marginalized groups as informants -Ensure disaggregated data by age , disability ,sex and any other special categorization. - Ensure the selection criteria for beneficiaries is inclusive.4. - Consult with beneficiaries on the most appropriate feedback mechanism . - Create awareness on the feedback mechanism - Give feedback to beneficiaries regarding the reports they made through the CFM . - Ensure that complaints are followed up on immediately and corrective measures.
