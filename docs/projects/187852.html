Project Summary

This project will target 4 camps in Idleb ( Dana-Maaret Tamsrin as sub-district) which are classified among the areas most of the need according to the Food Security Sector Report.
This project aims at responding to reach assessed food insecure people with a harmonized selection criteria to meet their monthly food needs at least 8 rounds in a year through distributing 671 in kind food baskets monthly (each food basket is sufficient for one family of 5 individuals) .This will cover a minimum of 1550 kcal person/day (70% of daily recommended standard) as the rest of the 550 kcal per person/day is expected to be covered by other partners/or their own sources (such as bread from bakeries) .The project will support total number of individuals disaggregated
approximately 929 men, 1032 women, 804 boys, 1054 girls and 89 person with a disability
in these camps( Qorress Palace -Al Waleed(Kafr Deryan)-Al Radwan (Kfr Arouq) and Al_Moho)
In addition, this intervention approach will contribute in ensuring vacancies for youth through hiring a new staff for the project includes Ms and Fs.
The project will ensure females participation and inclusion as MHD will hire a female (one at least) in each distribution team, and as FHH is a selection criteria. Moreover, PWDs will be given proper attention through the beneficiaries selection process and has been prioritized as a selection criteria, and Muzun will assign a truck during the distribution only for FHH, PWD and elderly people to carry and transfer food basket to their living place (door-to-door) since the elderly beneficiaries considered the most at risk during the COVID-19 pandemic in NWS.
MHD will deliver FBs through the tendering/contracting with the qualified contractor to purchase the materials in Turkey and ship them to Syria. Also, the FBs procurement and supply works will be implemented through contractors, and the tenders will be done in accordance with MHD’s procurement policies and procedures.
MHD will also conduct monitoring and evaluation processes throughout the project, as well as awareness sessions for COVID-19 and providing water tanks, sterilizers to protect against COVID-19 in the distribution locations taking into account the social distancing during all activities implementations. Also MHD will share information regularly with the concerned groups and raising awareness about the accountability mechanism to the affected population (consistent reporting).

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Muzun during implementing the activities of the project may face these risks according to the project's activities. Protection risk 1: Community tension arising from the beneficiary selection. Likelihood: high / Impact: High. Mitigation: Sadad will prioritize the most vulnerable female-headed or males headed with PSNs HHs, considering the children's situations and difculties within these HHs (priorities to HHs including children with disabilities or chronic diseases), to receive services during this project. Muzun has partnered with local leaders (to make them aware of the whole project’s activities and interventions) in designing the project to minimize harm and support local capacities to build peace. Protection risk 2: Risk of harassment of women and girls by the humanitarian workers. Likelihood: medium, Impact: high. Mitigation: establishing a con dential complaint mechanism and enhancing accountability mechanisms besides training the staff to deal with bene ciaries in addition to providing workers with training on humanitarian standards and signing code of conduct.For mitigating risk security and safety on Beneficiaries and MHD staff, we will: Organize the distribution at the door of the home to avoid overcrowding, taking into consideration people with specific needs and women.
