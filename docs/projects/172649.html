Project Summary

The project will build community resilience to water and sanitation related challenges in high denstity districts of Bulawayo Metroplotan province and Plumtree Urban District of Matebeleland South Province. We will work with local communites, local authorites and government to enhance WASH mitigation and rapid response mechanism.

1 - Activities

The project will:• Establish Community and School Health clubs to enhance social cohesion and behaviors modification around WASH in both Bulawayo and Plumtree Urban• Improve access to adequate safe water through Water Quality Monitoring training and support to the local authorities• Install 2 piped water scheme in Plumtree Urban• Install 2 water kiosks in Bulawayo • Drill 4 new boreholes in each of the districts.• Promote City of Bulawayo and Plumtree staff urgent attention to burst pipes to reduce contamination and lost due to loss of treated water.• Erect strategic storage facilities to cater for breakdown times• Retooling of water maintenance teams.• Train water point user committees• Improve sustained subnational WASH coordination mechanisms (PWSSCs-DWSSCs WWSSC-WPUC)• Train stakeholders on rapid response to WASH challenges• Run road shows and clean up campaigns in Bulawayo and Plumtree• Promote U-reporting in Bulawayo and Plumtree• Involve youths in WASH • Develop business linkages for WASH adopting the 3Rs model (reduce, recycle and reuse)• Inline Chlorinators installation at water points

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

“AAP is an active commitment to use power responsibly by taking account of, giving account to, and being held to account by the people humanitarian organizations seek to assist, involving putting communities and people at the centre of humanitarian action and promoting respect for their fundamental human rights, underpinned by the right to life with dignity, and the right to protection and security as set forth in international law.” (IASC 2017) The project will be accountable to the affected population by clearly explaining the program package to the community at inception and during the program. Activities to engage stakeholders will include consultative meetings, workshops, focus group discussions, and project sensitization meetings at provincial, ward levels, phone /email, one-on-one interviews, distribution of pamphlets/newsletters/IEC materials and project briefs and media. Community participation will be promoted so that the communities own the program processes themselves. Deliberate efforts will be carried out to seek out the traditionally marginalized members of the community like the disabled among other marginalized minorities. The project will define channels to share information about services provided by the programme, ensure that representatives of community groups are engaged in developing criteria for the selection of beneficiaries and make the criteria for selection open and transparent and agree on a cross-sectoral community engagement. A feedback and complaint mechanism for the programme with an accounting offer will be established

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The Project will comply with the World Bank Environmental and Social Standards (ESS) and the UNICEF generated general Environmental and Social Management Plan (ESMP). Africa AHEAD will adapt the general ESMP and ensure area specificity through the formulation of the ESMP. The ESMP will be submitted to the fund managers for review. Upon approval, Africa AHEAD will implement and report on the ESMP while DWASHC and the fund managers monitor for compliance. Area specific assessments on the potential impact of the project on various aspects like the socio-cultural ways of the community like displacement of populations, conflicts with heritage sites, impact of the project on biodiversity, land degradation, natural resources and risk of environmental pollution will be done. Mitigating measures, budgets and responsible members or groups will be tabulated onto the ESMP.
