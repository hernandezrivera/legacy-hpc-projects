Project Summary

Pakistan ranks as the sixth most risky country in the world for women , with rampant cases of sexual and domestic violence. The situation calls for a gender-responsive emergency measures to mitigate the harmful impacts of COVID-19 on protection of women. As the pandemic deepens economic and social stress, violence against women intensifies, with serious consequences for their mental health.Evidence shows that national emergencies and global crisis situations exacerbate cases of violence against women, particularly domestic violence and sexual abuse.
This proposed project is an effort to integrat mental health into the COVID-19 pandemic response and called for a significant increase in investment in this field to avoid an increase in linked cases of mental illness.
The proposed project will target the female proportion of two districts in KP i.e. Mardan and Charsadda. A total of four government’s Basic Health Units (BHUs) in each district would be selected in tehsils where the effects of COVID-19 are enormous. Selection of BHUs would be made with the consultation of the district health department. Total 16,000 individuals (4,000 in each BHU) are to be directly impacted and a staggering 35,000 family/ community members in each district; indirectly with this proposed intervention, which includes women aged 18 – 45years, and girls aged below 18years who are vulnerable to exploitation and hence needs to be given special attention to ensure their protection; health, legal, psychosocial and gender (GBV) wise. Inclusion of men and boys can have a positive transformatory affect for the lives of women and girls, therefore, awareness rising sessions for men and boys have been included to promote gender equality and to prevent and reduce gender based violence. Besides, women and girls cannot be accessed without going through the men due to the local culture.
Following are proposed activities.
1. Women Friendly Health Spaces (WFHS) would be established in each BHU to increase access of women to protective services
2. Awareness sessions for women on protection, and COVID-19
3. Awareness sessions for men on protection, and COVID-19
4. Establishing referral pathways
5. Referral to relevant service providers on GBV case management
Further, to disseminate the good messages at broader level, IEC materials will be distributed at community as well as at sites/WFHS. Radio messages will be developed in local context and also be aired for creating awareness regarding women rights, GBVs (Women empowerment), education, health (COVID-19) and information of the WFHS and the services that are made available at these spaces. The project will not only work at the communal level, but somehow at the policy level as well, the activity “building the capacity of stakeholders” is part of the proposed initiative to create awareness and importance of the project’s interventions and gain their support achieving the overall objective. WFHS will provide a series of services including awareness sessions, , psychosocial support, referral mechanism and as well as distribution of Women dignity kits.
SRSP has a successful experience to execute the similar nature of projects with UNFPA in Bannu and South Waziristan, Kohat, and Peshawar during 2014 to 2016 and then in 2020. This partnership helped over 60,000 women and adolescent girls in GBV related series of interventions. SRSP in this regard has already developed its roots and linkages with the stakeholders in proposed districts, hence there is likelihood to take advantage of that existing outreach within communities and good relationship with stakeholders on ground.
