0 - Project Summary (extended)

In line with WATAN S strategy, our responsibilities include direct implementation of this.project in target locations, coordinating closely with health authorities in Northwest Syria.We will leverage their extensive network to facilitate access. Local communities willactively participate in our capacity development efforts through appropriate traininginitiatives. WATAN will ensure adherence to quality and accountability standards, providing.necessary training for staff and fulfilling contractual obligations. Additionally, we willmanage all program and financial reporting to the donor. Our coordination efforts willextend to relevant clusters and coordination bodies in Turkey. This program will progress.through the following phases: Procurement of essential equipment, devices, drugs,consumables, and conducting the required training. Operate the health facilities andlaunch of outpatient services, comprehensive reproductive health services, chronicdisease treatment and supporting blood bank services and thalassemia services. services, RCCE and community health services and primary health care services inalignment with health cluster and Technical Working Group (TWG) guidelines, particularlyfocusing on Essential Health Service Packages (EHSP), Non-Communicable Diseases(NCD), and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS). Regular reporting, dataanalysis, monitoring, and evaluation. This will involve the development of the indicators.Performance Tracking Table (IPTT), Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) work plan, and adisaggregated database. The reporting will be according to guidelines agreed by thetechnical group of the health cluster (4Ws, HeRAMS), and to dHIS 2 as well.
