Project Summary

In Syria, women have become more isolated as a result of the Syrian crisis and war over the course of 11 years. Therefore, the project is a special center for women and girls that provides a safe space for them to socialize, rebuild their social networks, acquire skills, access services, and improve prevention and response to gender-based violence. Their age is from Ages 17 years to 60 years. The center is considered safe and provides safety and protection for women and girls. They can resort to it during the day to be a refuge for them from harassment or assault, and to support them at all levels: psychological, intellectual, cultural, and professional, and to provide prevention and the needs they may need.
The center will be established in the Muliha area of Eastern Ghouta , will provide services:
Legal services: Given that the region was exposed to destruction after the war, most women do not have identification papers or proof of their identity. Therefore, the center will provide legal services through legal consultations and address legal issues through awareness sessions on provisions and laws.
We will provide legal services in cooperation and coordination with the Syrian Trust located in the Muliha area.
Health services: This is through coordination and networking with a clinic within the region and doctors within the neighborhood or hospitals in the city for comprehensive health treatment for women, as well as providing the necessary medical equipment (blood pressure device, insulin, diabetes device, glasses and other medical tools for women).
Psychological services: through psychological support, psychological counseling, as well as psychological medical referrals.
Awareness sessions: covering everything women need to prevent harm and violence, with various topics (early marriage, adolescence, etc.).
A simple professional course: learn sewing, hooking, embroidery, and wool in order to empower women and develop their skills and abilities.
Providing a complete food basket with the necessary needs to improve her health and secure what she and her family need.
Educational courses for girls: those who dropped out of school due to war, ignorance, and customs that prevent girls from leaving the home, and courses for the secondary stage. We will also link girls to education by helping them submit the required papers to return them to school.
Literacy course: It is one of the most important courses that must be undertaken because women in the region have not learned to read and write, and the illiteracy rate in the region is 70% of the population, and this makes women more vulnerable to exploitation, harassment, and violence.
All of this will help reduce risks, prevent further harm, improve prevention of all forms of violence, and work towards equality with men and non-discrimination.
