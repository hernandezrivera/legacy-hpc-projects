Project Summary

This project will aim at providing practical training and support to Internally Diaplaced Persons and hosts in the domain of mushroom production, Traditional Poultry and market gardening. this will involve carrying out a survey and identifying IDPs in the South West Region, identify their various needs and train them on the various skills needed and later providing support for them to start their activities. This project will target 1,500 IDPs and 2,500 hosts in the South West Region who will be trained and supported with Materials to start their activiteis.
The purpose of this project is to empower IDPs with training and support on mushroom production, traditional poultry and market gardening activities where they can generate income and become economically independent from the hosts.
The targeted population is IDPs and Host Communities in the South West Region of Cameroon in towns like Bangem, Tiko, Buea, Limbe, Idenau, Mundemba and Alou.
