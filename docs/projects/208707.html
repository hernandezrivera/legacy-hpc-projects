Project Summary

The project will target host communities and IDPs/Returnees in host communities, including households with adolescents with special needs.
The project will use household vulnerability assessment matrix, to identify vulnerable households within communities, through identification of vulnerable adolescent boys and girls, who are out of school, hawks the street and engage in all forms of labour that has negative impact on attaining their full potentials.
The project will further identify and strengthen existing and/or creates community-based social structures and networks, whose responsibility is to identify, respond and refer cases of child labour and other child protection concerns within local communities.
In collaboration with identified and/or established community-based social structures, the project will identify caregivers of vulnerable adolescents, engage them in behavioural change sessions, towards positive parenting, identifying risks adolescents are exposed to, and to proffer sustainable solutions within their community context, to end child labour and enhance school enrolment, retention and participation of adolescents in age and sex-appropriate vocational skills towards their development.
In collaboration with identified community-based social structures and networks, the project will further carryout massive community sensitization on child labour and other child protection concerns, towards risks mitigation.
The project will also engage adolescents within local communities in life skill sessions to develop their abilities and motivate them to use information to develop meaningful life choices.
Following behavioural change session with caregivers, the project will provide small grants for caregivers to boost existing and/or establish new income generating activities.
