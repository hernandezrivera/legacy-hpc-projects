1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Ayuda en Accion, through this intervention, is planning to assist around 1,600 households (8,060 people) to improve their livelihoods and food security by running agricultural production and sustainable businesses to strengthen their capacities. The proposed project will directly address the problems of lack of food, economic exclusion andthe risk of deprivation of rights of a growing population of internallydisplaced persons that requires an effort of social and economicinclusion through economic empowerment to take off its capabilities.The project seeks to introduce vectors for the empowerment of women in the economic sphere, as well as to contribute to the occupation of young people, so as not to expose them to the risk of aligning themselves with the insurgency and contribute to the construction of peace from a triple Nexus perspective: emergency- peace-development. The intervention will cover around 8,060 IDPs selected from both districts Metuge and Ancuabe centers. The number of IDPs in Metuge by September 2021 (IOM, 2021) increased to 127.646 ( 11% male, 25% female and 64% children), and in Ancuabe by November 2021 (IOM, 2021), increased to around 50,283 ( 16% male, 22% female and 62% children).The final decision on implementing sites will depend on coordination with Food Security Cluster to avoid overlap.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection mainstreaming is an integral part of AeA’s design strategy,this alongside the participatory methods that AeA use to collectinformation help greatly to identify the risks posed to each demographic.Staff working in the field are aware of the do no harm, a principle which assists in making the most suitable decisions for the populations in question. We prioritise preserving safety and dignity in the design of field activities and in the planning of any event. Ensuring that there is equitable access for all and encouraging participation in order to empower those we are helping.Special attention is given to individuals more vulnerable.Protection risks are identified jointly with the community and followed up on by the team. Some risks and challenges are already identified and will be addressed during the project implementation, such as the following:1. the complexity of working with young people, due to emotional volatility and life projects;2. the cultural aspects that limit the participation of women in economic activities;3. the instability of the region;4. the lack of safe land for agriculture for the displaced;5. the residential instability of the target group.Information derived from FGDs, community gatherings and committees will be processed and forwarded to the relevant clusters. In order to be able to face these challenges, the intervention that is proposed has a sensitive nature, seeking to better know the target group, their needs, capacities, assets and expectations, in the context of the local culture and local conditions, as well as looking for solutions that are adapted to their conditions.Trainings and the fostering of community structures allow forimproved and sustainable agency of the displaced community, with special focus on those who are more vulnerable, in all community relevant aspects.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

As part of Ayuda en Accion long term strategic interventions protection of Girls and Women against sexual abuse and exploitation is a key priority, to contribute to the overall strengthening of protection systems at community, district and national level.We promote the social acknowledgement of girls, boys and women rights to avoid violence and sexual exploitation. All trainings are accompanied by a PSEA training of all activists/artisans/committee members. On top of that protection of women and girls against gender discrimination and violence is key to promote human rights and equitable access to opportunities for both women and men.AeA has a code of conduct, a policy for the treatment of minors and other provisions that must be known and complied with by our staff, as well as a complaints line that is set up and disseminated among the communities where we operate. These instruments are alsomandatory for our partners.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities are considered more vulnerable than others and will be involved in the project from the beginning of the project implementation. The two local partners (Wiwanana and Kampos) are experienced in leading interventions with displaced communities and will work with Ayuda en Accion staff to identify and include people with disabilities in all activities, according to their respective locations. Households containing people with disabilities will have priority in the distribution of start-up kits for home garden, as well as raising chickens. The selection of nutrition training locations forcooking demonstrations, will be held considering the distances to the households with people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

It is expected to create 2 associations / MPMES for waste management in the resettled communities of Metuge district: The objective is the creation of 2 associations / micro-enterprises for waste management in the Naminawe and Nicavako camps. These local entities will allow, on the one hand, to manage the waste, as well as the separation for its eventual reuse in tasks such as composting or reusing them. The question of composting will be essential when providing compost for the creation of home gardens. Associations / micro-enterprises will be able to provide services for such waste management and obtain returns that allow them to be made sustainable in the future, through donor contributions, sale of products from waste, etc. Opening of wells, horticulture as well as use of production of composting will be implemented according to environmental recommendations to avoid contamination of watersources, and handled with care considering the most vulnerable as children and elders.
