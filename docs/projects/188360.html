Project Summary


The WoS population continues to suffer extreme physical and psychological harm facing simultaneous multiple emergencies, ongoing displacement and economic hardship compounded by the COVID-19 pandemic. The major objectives of mental health programs are in alignment with the proposed HRP 2022 strategic objectives and the mhGAP extended action plan 2013-2030, is to strengthen effective leadership and the advocacy for mental health; providing comprehensive, integrated, and responsive mental health services in health facilities and at community-based platforms; develop strategies for promotion and prevention in mental health and strengthen information systems for mental health. potential coverage of mental health service, including accessibility-availability- and service acceptance, and psychosocial support services at the level of functioning PHCs across the country reached up to 35%, wherein, 490 functional public health centers providing the integrated services
as 68,569 consultations by trained and supervised Drs on mhGAP program, and 58,185 basic psychological interventions by health workers,
At the community level, WHO supported 16 key implementing partners by delivering a total of 180,538 MH consultations/services in community wellbeing centers and mobile teams reaching 268 subdistricts, through community workers trained on different MHPSS programmes in response to the people in need (IDPs, host communities in NWS, Southern Syria (SS) and in NES) on the other hands at public health centers and through 5630 health and community workers to enhance the provision of integrated and appropriate services at health and community levels . WHO also procured and distributed 280,848 treatment courses to more than 50 health partners across the country, including in NES, to support the provision of pharmaceutical therapeutic interventions for persons suffering from mental health conditions, In context of COVID19 response and building on 2020 campaigns achievement and lesson learned, series of community campaigns under RCCE pillar with the focus on MHPSS key messages, coping with stress, awareness raising session targeting children, elder adults, and caregivers, combating stigma, up to 287,700 unique services delivered in Rural Damascus, Aleppo, Homs, Hama, Deir eZZour through more than 18000 different sessions and activities, including remotely MHPSS consultations
In these challenging circumstances WHO continue to provide support to all 141 existing facilities in NWS with MHPSS services, providing technical support and regular supplies of medication in 69 communities and 29 sub-districts. It is planned as well to continue supporting 2 non-specialized MHPSS facilities that are providing quality non-specialized MHPSS care and are helping to fill- in the gap on MHPSS services, including supporting MHPSS Helplines and the 24/7 Suicide Prevention
Hotline for NWS, while ensuring creation of MHPSS messages to conduct awareness raising sessions focusing on reduction of stigma towards mental health patients.
Two main referral pathways for MHPSS in NWS will be secured.
1. The online referral in case of a need for upper-level care and support to the helpline caller.
2. The physical referral for the more serious and severe cases to receive further help from the psychiatrist upon need.
Herein, all the teams of PSWs will be provided with a service mapping that includes all of the available MHPSS humanitarian actors operating in the covered areas.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Vulnerability: The mental health service user can be at risk of or exposed to damage or harm through personal or external factors or other resource or capability issues. Likelihood: medium Impact: high, Mitigation procedures:-Mainstreaming of the risk management through MH care facilities and through mhGAP program -Effective care requires that mental health service providers to consider service users not as passive recipients of service but as actively involved, core contributors to the risk and care process-Risk can be minimized by ensuring that there is good communication, sufficient attention to staff and patient safety, and appropriate training and support for staff and service users. -Best Practice Principles summarize the key principles for effective risk assessment and management. -Risk is safely identified if all parameters (Historical, Clinical, Dispositional and Contextual) and features of individual functioning are examined Stigma and discrimination for people suffering from MNS conditions. Exclusion from accessing different services, at emergency response sites, Likelihood: medium Impact: high, Mitigation’s procedures: Mainstream MHPSS IASC core principles for services providers, enhance stigma combating at community level and in the hosting communities. Orient mental health focal point and project managers in implementing partners about M&E proper tool for participatory approach to deliver dignified services
