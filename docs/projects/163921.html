Project Summary

With this project FCA proposes to contribute to the well-being of children in Syria by the provision of equitable education interventions, Most vulnerable children and underserved areas in Dara'a governorate will be reached through comprehensive approach project and based evidence-based needs identify the gaps.
Proposing 1) professional teacher development 2) Remedial classes for students at risk dropping out 3) Catch up classes for OOSC 4) stationary distribution to support the families in enrolling the children in schools 5) Schools Rehabilitation to improve the access along with education services there will be provision of protection components to protect the children in& outside the schools such as; Awareness sessions and PSS activities to create integrated communities as well as preventing the dropping out, early marriage, child labour and recruitment.

4 - Needs

In Syria more than one in three schools are damaged or destroyed while others are used for purposes not related to education, such as shelter for displaced persons (Education Sector HNO 2020). Complementary to the HNO, as a part of an ECHO project, FCA has conducted assessment in five areas of Dara’a Governorate (Daraa Al-Balad, Khirbet Ghazaleh, Sheikh Miskin, Al-Harak, Dael) in December 2019 and found that the number of out of school children in the 5 targeted communities is about 1539 (Source: Statistics of the Ministry of Education and Mukhtar Municipalities). The assessment also included the conditions of ﬁve schools in the respective areas that witnessed a large displacement movement.Based on the information from the local authorities in the targeted locations, the large number of families did not enrol their children in schools, because children are working to support the families and depend on negative coping mechanism such as early marriage. The project will take place in 5 schools of the areas mentioned above, with an estimated number of students 1589. The 5 schools are: • Munther Shehadeh / Dael• Sheikh Miskin Al-Oula / Sheikh Miskin• Eighth school / AlHarak• Adnan Al-Masalmeh / Daraa Al-Balad• Katiba / Khirbet GhazalehThe five schools are in a very poor condition: schools are not equipped with doors or windows, and in addition to the presence of light destructions in the walls, children are not protected from the weather conditions that compel them to be absent from school in the severe winter months. Such condition of the schools can be considered as the main root-cause of dropping out and significant decrease in their academic achievement (source: school principals). In Syria, due to the large displacement movement witnessed in some areas, there is a basic problem, which is the lack of teachers who have university degrees to teach in schools. 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk1: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment or bullying of students. Mitigating Measure:Teachers and school personnel will be trained on prevention and reporting of, and issues related to, physical punishment and bullying. Risk2 : Students participate in bullying of students. Mitigating Measure: Students and their caregivers will be sensitized on the prevention, and issues related to, physical punishment and bullying and the systems in place to present and response to it. Risk3:Children are over-exposed to the risk of, or experience, harm by teachers, school personnel, organization staff or related entities. Mitigating Measure: Students, teachers and other education personnel will be provided regular psychosocial support services. Those services will have the capacity, ability and resources to address basic needs. For needs that are beyond what should be reasonably expected from learning services there will be a referral system to specialized protection services. Risk4: Learning facilities or their surroundings are structurally unsafe or do not provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, dust, etc.). Mitigating Measure:Learning facilities will be improved to ensure that buildings and grounds are safe and protected. This may include stairs, walls, roofs, flooring, electric, water, etc. School grounds should have fencing. All works should be supervised and monitored by technical personnel. The organization should ensure Child Safeguarding measures are in place before work start. Risk 5 : Members of the community or local authorities try to over-influence or take actions that prevent, or try to change, the type of support provided to education services or who will benefit from the support provided. Mitigating Measure: The community will be consulted and listened to through the life of the project (assessments, design, monitoring, corrective actions, evaluation, etc.).
