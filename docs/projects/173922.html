Project Summary

The project seek to build the capacities of youth and youth platforms including NGOs, youth in government and other community members to increase their active and meaningful participation in humanitarian systems as well as local governance. The project will work with a range of stakeholders including local government officials, community youth groups in formal and informal platforms. The project will seek to also increase the visibility and participation of young women.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

The project will support the early recovery efforts of the Borno State Government by providing needed training and other capacity building/mentoring actions for youth in community in government to increase their functionality and participation in humanitarian systems. The project will also seek to support life skill training as well as support for small businesses for most vulnerable young people in selected communities. The project will deploy a community targeting process in the selections of beneficiaries.

2 - Activities

Activities on the project will include1. Formal training2. Step down trainings3. Advocacy intervention targeted at key structures such as the HCT, Government and other actors4. Budget monitoring and influencing to increase government and donor investments in early recovery and livelihood interventions.5. Citizens consciousness building actions6. Community targeting and beneficiary selection7. Livelihood support to vulnerable young people.
