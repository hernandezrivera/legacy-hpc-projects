Project Summary

The action will contribute to create an accessible response to GBV survivors and enabling a disability inclusive protection environment to prevent and mitigate GBV risks within most vulnerable crisis-affected population in Cabo Delgado. The action will enhance resilience capacity, involving DPOs, women with disabilities, community members and local government authorities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In Mozambique there is a strong need for empowering communities, CSOs and local authorities to deal with the GBV, in order to produce an inclusive and accessible response for all.The action covers some of the districts most affected by the displacement of people in Cabo Delgado Province. It aims at enhancing community, civil society and governement actors’ capacity on GBV prevention and response, focusing on women and girls with disabilities. This will contribute to create an enabling and inclusive GBV prevention and response environment, promoting access to information, reporting and basic services. The action will identify and remove barriers, advocating for universal access to life-saving and basic services and supporting stakeholders’ implementation of inclusive and accessible approaches for GBV prevention and assistance. The main target groups will be women with disabilities (of any age), who are the most exposed to GBV risks and experience multiple barriers to access life-saving services. To empower them with financial autonomy, GBV survivors will be provided with financial and technical support to start up small IGA.The project will benefit from lessons learned and synergies with other AIFO projects in the same area.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

● Carry out accessibility assessments on existing GBV prevention and reporting mechanism;● Develop/integrate disability-specific referral pathways for women and girls with disabilities; ● Provide inclusive and accessible GBV case management and referral services for women and girls with disabilities;● Conduct GBV awareness raising sessions for people with disabilities at community level, thereby ensuring proper engagement and support for women and girls with disabilities as well as awareness about reporting mechanisms.● Provide cash grants for IGA implementation to GBV survivors, especially for women with disability● Establish/integrate inclusive and accessible women friendly spaces within communities

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

As a preliminary action, AIFO will conduct an accessibility assessment, involving DPOs, community members, Civil Society Organizations and local authorities, focused on assessing accessibility of information, reporting systems and assistance services related to GBV and support in establishing practical solution when they are not fully accessible. During the assessment, potential risks will be identified and mitigations solutions found.The project aims at establishing inclusive and accessible mechanisms related to GBV information, risk prevention, reporting and response, creating synergies with main specific protection and assistance providers, including CSOs and local institutions. The development of disability-specific referral pathways for women and girls with disabilities will encourage meaningful access to assistance among one of the most marginalized and vulnerable group. The partnership with CSOs and DPOS, in particular DPOs of women with disabilities, will guarantee their active participation in the action.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project has been designed according to the Community Based Inclusive Development approach, favoring the dialogue with local partners/stakeholders and in particular with the Disabled People Organisations (DPOs). AIFO works within and with communities, always in strict collaboration with local partners, taking into consideration the specific needs of the different population groups, with a particular focus on women and girls with disabilities. Consultations with them was guaranteed by the longstanding partnership between AIFO and the local DPOs in the project area.Community members and beneficiaries of the project will be provided with assistive tools and information in order to establish an inclusive GBV prevention and assistance mechanism, taking into consideration the “Twin-track Approach” (combining disability mainstreaming with disability specific support) and age-specific considerations. The feed-back mechanism will be based on Focus Groups and regular meetings with community members and beneficiaries, including peer-to-peer dialogue with women and girls with disabilities. In addition, facilitation and communication activities will promote the use of Linha Verde, Linha Fala a Criança.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities (PWDs) are at risk of being most excluded from protection mechanisms because of multiple barriers: physical/architectural, attitudinal/socio-cultural, economic, communication/information, organizational and others. Despite the existence of specific disability laws and policies, line Ministries – at central, provincial and district level – are not fully aware of PWDs rights and they don’t have the tools to apply policies. This affects program design which are not inclusive, such as GBV prevention and assistance interventions.The project is designed specifically to guarantee the access to GBV information and services by men and women with disabilities on equal basis with others. PwDs will be identify with the collaboration of local DPOs and the activities will be designed together with DPOs representatives (Nothing about us, without us). AIFO has a longstanding experience in the field of disability in Mozambique, especially in Cabo Delgado. AIFO is member of the Disability Working Group at national and provincial level and acted as implementing partners of disability oriented projects with UNICFEF, UNDP, PSEA. During 2021, AIFO has been leading numerous capacity building activities in Cabo Delgado, involving local government (district and provincial level), civil society actors and community structures on inclusion of PWDs and Inclusive Planning in different sectors.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in the respect of AIFO Environmental and Sustainability Policy, included in AIFO Manual of Operational Policies- Code of Ethics and Conduct 2020. According to the essential points of AIFO Environmental Policy, an environmental assessment will take place and it will consider also gender aspects: women living in rural areas are often more vulnerable to environmental degradation due to inequality in access to land.The project will tackle community preparedness to natural disasters, as the geographic area is exposed to catastrophic natural events, as part of the Disaster Risk Reduction framework. Within this framework, an environment assessment will be conducted with SDAE (District Services of Economic Affairs) for the IGAs that should have minimum environmental impact, guaranteed through the inclusion of this specific criterion for determining grant awards.
