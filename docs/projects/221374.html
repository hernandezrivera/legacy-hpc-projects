Project Summary

With the on-going political situation in the North West Region coupled the negative effects of climate change, small holder farmers has suffered enormous setbacks ranging poor yields to post harvest losses. In the light of the above, the project is aimed at enhancing food security and addressing the challenges related to food availability, access, and utilization. The project also intends to implement various strategies and interventions to improve agricultural practices, strengthen local food production systems, and empower local communities to achieve sustainable food security through trainings, provision of farm inputs and enhancing marketing avenues.
