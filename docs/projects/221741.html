Project Summary

FADM is proposing to implement a WASH project in Baliet County. Baliet county is among the WASH cluster priority two counties. In order to mitigate the severe WASH gaps and to improve the living standards of people living in this county, FADM will carry out borehole rehabilitation both at the nutrition centers and the community, construct latrine at selected nutrition facilities, carry out hygiene promotion activities both at the nutrition centers and the community and distribute WASH and menstrual hygiene kits to caregivers at targeted nutrition centers targeting to directly reach a total of 15,000 people.
FADM will target five payams in Baliet with borehole rehabilitation, construction of latrine at nutrition sites and hygiene promotion at nutrition centers and the community. FADM will rehabilitate 4 boreholes in each of the targeted payam thus the project will rehabilitate 20 boreholes providing 10,000 people with access to clean and safe drinking water and helping to reduce time that women and girls spend in searching for water. FADM will also construct 3 latrines at selected nutrition centers without latrines providing access to safe and dignified sanitation facilities to the people using the targeted nutrition facilities. FADM will additionally initiate hygiene promotion activities in all the five payams that will be targeted by the project. The hygiene promotion activities will target to reach 15,000 people thus creating increased access to messages on good hygiene practices.
Lastly, FADM will procure and distribute WASH kits and menstrual hygiene kits to 400 caregivers with children admitted at nutrition centers. This will aim at helping the targeted mothers to put into practice the good hygiene practices being shared with them thus reducing hygiene related diseases. With these initiated activities FADM is aiming to contribute to WASH cluster objective of reducing WASH- related morbidity and mortality in priority counties through WASH integrated programming with Nutrition.

1. Needs

2024 HNRP indicates that approximately 5.6 million people require WASH assistance in 2024 in South Sudan. The data also indicates that 28 counties, Baliet being among them, have extreme WASH conditions often coinciding with high malnutrition rates. A significant portion of the South Sudan population (41%) lacks access to improved water sources and 37% need to travel 30 minutes to reach their primary water source, posing particular hardship to persons with disabilities and making women and girls more vulnerable to GBV. Access to sanitation is even more limited with prevalent open defecation, 88 per cent of the population lacks access to improved latrines, according to FSNMS 2023. The compounding challenges of displacement, insecurity, flooding, economic difficulties and influx of returnees from Sudan further strain the already limited resources. According to the 2023 FSNMS and SMART surveys' findings, the national Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) levels in South Sudan remain above the WHO emergency threshold of 15%. Baliet county is expected to experience critical nutrition IPC classification from the month of October 2023 to June 2025 indicative of the dire malnutrition situation in this county whose effect is expected to be compounded by the poor WASH situation. A rapid assessment carried out by FADM in Baliet county indicated high prevalence of open defecation with more than 90 per cent of the population lacking latrines and are thus practicing open defecation. The assessment also revealed low knowledge on key hygiene practices such as the five critical times of handwashing among the people interviewed. FADM is therefore proposing to carry out a WASH project in Baliet county that will involve rehabilitation of boreholes to provide increased access to clean and safe drinking water resulting into reduced time spent in search for water thus improving the lives of women, girls and people living with disability. The project will also construct latrines at nutrition centers thus increasing access to safe and dignified sanitation facilities in those nutrition centers and contribute to reduction in open defecation particularly within and around the targeted nutrition centers. The project will also initiate hygiene promotion activities to increase access to messages on good hygiene practices. The project will therefore aim to reduce prevalence of water borne diseases and malnutrition among the targeted communities.

2. Project activities

To address the issue of lack of clean and safe drinking water at the community and nutrition centers, FADM will rehabilitate broken down boreholes both at the community and at nutrition centers. FADM targets to rehabilitate 4 boreholes in each of the five Payams targeted in Baliet. FADM will first carry out boreholes assessment to identify the parts that will need to be replaced and the type of rehabilitation that will need to be carried out. FADM will ensure that it incorporates flood resilient model to the boreholes that will be rehabilitated. This will provide increased access to clean and safe drinking water to 10,000 people.FADM will also construct a total 3 latrines in selected nutrition centers that lack latrines. The latrines will be temporary unless in nutrition centers that are run at health facilities where a permanent latrine will be constructed. This will ensure that there is increase access to safe and dignified sanitation facilities to the the communities that will be targeted by the project.To ensure that there is increased access to messages on good hygiene practices and that the targeted communities practice good hygiene practices to reduce both waterborne diseases and diseases related to poor hygiene practices, FADM will carry out hygiene promotion activities. FADM will recruit and train 20 hygiene promoters in each of the targeted payam. The trained hygiene promoters will then be deployed to carryout hygiene promotion activities both at the community and the nutrition centers. The hygiene promotion exercise will involve house to house visits to demonstrate good hygiene practices at the household level, hygiene promotion through mother-to-mother support groups, hygiene promotion at the nutrition and health facilities, hygiene promotion at public places such as markets, schools, worship places and at water points. The hygiene promotion activities initiated by this project will target to reach 15,000 people. In order to equip the communities that will be targeted with hygiene promotion to put into practice the good hygiene practices that they will be taught, FADM will procure and distribute WASH and menstrual hygiene kits to 400 caregivers. The menstrual hygiene kits will target school going girls and women caregivers at the nutrition centers.FADM will also carry out safety audits both at the water points and the latrines that will be targeted to ensure that safety concerns are addressed and that the rehabilitated boreholes and latrines are safe for use especially for women and girls. FADM will equally ensure that all the project activities take into consideration safety of the individuals who will be targeted by the project.
