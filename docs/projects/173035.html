Project Summary

Since the arrival of the Rohingya refugees in Cox's Bazar District in 2017, Teknaf and Ukhia Upazilas have suffered the challenges of massive amounts of waste generated in an area where had no funcional systems of waste collection. While in the refugee camps, Solid Waste Management (SWM) systems have immediately been set up by WASH partners of the international humanitarian response, host communities have suffered the major consequences of waste threateaning the health of people and the environment. In 2019 UNDP initiated a project to establish basic systems of waste collection in the most affected Unions of Teknaf and Ukhia Upazilas (Raja Palong and Palong Khayli, Whykong, Nhilla and Baharchara and Teknaf Municipality). Based on the lessons learned of that ongoing initiative, this project aims at approaching with the same solution the remaining Union Parishads of Teknaf and Ukhia Upazilas (Haldia Palong, Jalia Palong and Ratna Palong, Sabrang, Teknaf, as well as Ramu Municipality. In summary, the project aims at:
1) Supporting the local government to establish basic systems of waste collection and safe disposal and build their administrative, financial and governance capacity to take over the overall management of waste collection, recovery and generation of green job opportunities.
2) Supporting the District level authorities to operate and maintain the large SWM facility serving both Upazilas and camps for waste recovery and final waste disposal.
3) Developing a community engagement and behaviour change intervention to ensure no littering and proper segregation of solid waste.
4) COVID19 and monson contingengy plan for Solid Waste.
5) Improving the SWM systems in key areas of Ramu Municipality.
6) Supporting the camps in waste transportation: dumpable waste to the sanitary landfill to ensure proper and safe disposal of waste, and recyclables to the recycling sector in host communities.

Plan fields

1 - Identified Needs

1) To collect and properly manage solid waste generated daily in the targeted Unions with 260,470 people - 48% of Teknaf and Ukhia Upazilas population. Out of the total, 60% of organic material to be proceeded and sold as organic fertilizer; 10% of recycling material to be sold to the recycling dealers; 30% of the remaining waste to be safely disposed. 2) To build a network of SW facilities in the above-mentioned locations to properly process the SW, generate incomes and protect the health of the population and the environment. 3) In overall Teknaf and Ukhia Upazilas, behaviour change and waste segregation at source to be promoted among HH and market areas. Local governement of these locations require to be increased their technical and financial capacity to ensure the sustainability of the SWM systems in place.3) To transport non-recyclable waste from all Unions of Teknaf and Ukhia Upazillas and Teknaf Camps and safely dispose of and manage a large SW Facility where a second level of waste segregation and build value of low grade plastics will minimize the final waste to be landfilled.4) To ensure rapid capacity to prevent and respond a new wave of COVID19 and mitigate the effects caused by unmanged waste during monsoon season.5) To optimize the SWM system in Ramu Municipality, along with the Ramu compost plant. 6) To connect the Ukhia camps with the Sanitary landfill in Camp 20Ext, and transportation of recyclables for recyling.

2 - Activities or outputs

Activity 1. Establishment of basic SWM systems in Haldia Palong, Jalia Palong and Ratna Palong UPs (Ukhia Upazila), and Sabrang, Teknaf and Saint Martin Island UPs (Teknaf Upazila). This includes the engagement of community voluntneers (33,264 labour/day; 22 in each UP) for an initial quick solid waste cleaning campaign and regular waste collection, compost production, sensibilization and waste disposal for a year in the main market areas and HH surrounding of each Union. Waste workers require to be fully equipped with working tools and PPEs. Volunteers will be selected from the community, prioritizing vulnerable people and women engagement. Activity 2. SW infrastructure and facilities to collect, transport, process and dispose solid waste in Haldia Palong, Jalia Palong and Ratna Palong UPs (Ukhia Upazila), and Sabrang, Teknaf and Saint Martin Island UPs (Teknaf Upazila). This includes the construction of Material Recovery Facility (MRF: compost unit, recyclables storage chamber and transfer station) and secondary collection points for market areas; and large and small set of 2 bins for stores and HH. Activity 3. Development the Local Government capacity to gradually take over the management of the SWM systems in place by the end of the year. This includes capacity building on monitoring, management of second party (private sector, NGOs, etc.) as service providers, and governance system for SWM at the different administrative level Union, Upazilla and District. Activity 4. Community engagement and behaviour change for SW intervention. Initial and continuous engagement of the targeted community for information sharing and active participation to stop littering waste into drains and waste segregation at source. This includes an initial mapping of HH and market stores, community consultation, participatory design of the system and monitoring. IEC materials to support the messages and a public communication strategy accross the UPs adjusting the methods to the local practises and believes and gender mainstreaming. Activity 5. O&M Large-scale SWM Facility in Teknaf and Ukhia Upazilas. A new large SWM facility - material recovery unit, compost unit and sanitary landfill cells will be constructed this year to serve Ukhia and Teknaf host communities and Teknaf Camps. Workers to manage the facility, drivers to operate the trucks for waste transportation across the UP, and other mantainance costs will be necessary to cover for one year until the local government takes fully responsibility of the operation. Activity 6.COVID19 and monsoon contingency plan for SWM. Awareness, best practises and assets such as, gloves, masks, handwashing facilities for workers to prevent the community, workers, volunteers and local goverment from COVID19. Additional measures are included in this activity to face emergency clearance of solid waste in hot spot areas to prevent aggravated effects of the monsoon season and other potential WASH and HEALTH related emergencies. Activity 7. Reinforcing the SWM system in Ramu Municipalitiy. This includes an initial assessment of the status of the SWM system, the compost plant in Ramu, distribution of waste large bins, awareness raising and optimization of the collection system by the Municipality. Activity 8.Transportation of waste to the sanitary landfill in Ukhia camps and recyclable waste to local recycling sector in host communities. To ensure transportation capacity of dumpable waste (only, 30% of the total waste) to the sanitary landfill in camp 20 Ext. for safe disposal, as well as recyclables to the recycling sector in the host communities. This is a joint coordination with IOM, UNICEF and UNHCR camp WASH/SWM focal person and respective partners to follow the SOPs on the use of the landfill and SWM guidelines and improve the current sanitary conditions of the camps.
