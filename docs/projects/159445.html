Project Summary

The proposed lifesaving and life sustaining interventions will include provision of Multipurpose cash transfer, conditional cash transfer and launching of safety net program. Whereas recovery interventions will focus on engaging youth in skill development program for livelihoods diversification, establishing small enterprises, improved agricultural production and livelihoods assets building & protection through cash for work in the long term. These initiatives will not only help beneficiaries to deal with the negative coping strategies on immediate basis rather will also help communities to recover from shocks through improved asset based resilience, enhanced capacities and strengthened linkages. Livelihoods and Food Security will continue to engage with other actors including humanitarian agencies working in the areas, public / private sector institutions and local authorities to promote cash programming for synergized support. NRC has already in place signed framework agreements with the Telecom companies in each of the operational areas. E transfer using telecom services are swift, safe and ensure upholding dignity of the beneficiaries.
Vocational skills development will help in creating market sensitive job opportunities for unemployed women and youth. Under the safety net programs; NRC will conduct research to identify poorest of the poor households and will register these households for safety net program. Registered poorest HHs will be provided with monthly safety net support as per CMWG recommended transfer values. NRC has adopted localization approach in program operational areas and the complementing integrated programs funded by various donors are being implemented in clustered villages. So identified and registered HHs Where appropriate will benefit from a companion program for awareness raising around good nutrition sensitive intervention, BCC, safe food preparation, access to diverse food, improved hygiene practices and diversified livelihoods options. These companion programs will ensure that poorest HHs have improved capacities and access to food and income sources. Likewise, Small farmers will be provided with agricultural inputs including (Training, cereal / pulses / horticultural crops seeds and Tractor tillage hours) for improved agricultural production and access to nutritious horticultural crops. NRC will make sure to implement cash transfer programs (CTPs) considering seasonality, agricultural calendar and fluctuating market prices. Resilience building initiative will help beneﬁciaries to sustain shock, protecting and conserving natural resources on which they bank and making informed decision. NRC will support targeted populations especially communities in hard to reach areas to meet their basic food and non-food. In the long run this will help in peace building.

Project Activities

Activity 1: 11,000 households (3,000 HH representing 18,000 individuals from Somaliland), (2,000 HH representing 12,000 individuals from Juba land), (2,000 HH representing 12,000 individuals from Puntland) and (4,000 HH representing 24,000 individuals from South central) are registered and released MPC transfers. Activity 2: 2,000 households (600 HHs representing 3,600 individuals from Somaliland), 400 HH representing 2,400 individuals from Juba land), (400 HH representing 2,400 individuals from Puntland) and (600 HH representing 3,600 individuals from South central) are registered and engaged in cash for work initiatives for asset building. Activity 5: 1,500 small farmers (9,000 individuals) will be provided with agriculture inputs (Seeds, Extension training and tractor tillage hours), these Farmers will also be trained in improved agriculture techniques.

Mitigation Measures

The project will deliberately prevent risk of exclusion of most vulnerable and marginalized groups (based on gender, age, minority clans, IDPs, returnees, persons with disabilities, etc) by ensuring their representation into the village committees for identiﬁcation and selection of the vulnerable households. As we understand that women and children suffer the most in any of the disaster, therefore a cognizant effort will be made to make sure that the vulnerable segment particularly women are included in the program decision making processes and there is no compromise on beneﬁciaries selection criteria. NRC will make sure that targeting criteria for including the most vulnerable and marginalized groups is understood and followed by the village committees. As a standard practice, NRC engages with all stake holders including local authorities right at the time of the project launch. This practice helps NRC to gain conﬁdence of the key stake holders in very beginning and also helps to address any duplication of resources. Communities sensitization will also be done by disseminating sensitization posters during communities’ meetings, displaying pan ﬂexes with beneﬁciaries selection criteria and complaint response mechanism number (CRM) at visible sites in the villages. Communities will be encouraged to call and register their concern on the given toll free numbers. Community consultation and sensitization and drawing on the community based targeting helps NRC to identify and reach the neediest households.

Please provide further explanation

Project will help to reach displacement affected communities in hard to reach areas through a set of life saving and livelihoods diversification interventions. Life saving intervention will help the benefiting households in mitigating negative coping strategies, protection of their productive livelihoods assets and will help them to get improved production through assets building. Likewise, Safety net program will help the extremely poor group of society to bridge a gape in accessing food. These safety net programs will be linked and or supported with a companion program focusing on knowledge transfer and self reliance initiatives. Over the period of time, NRC will keep monitoring these extremely vulnerable households enrolled in safety net and the companion programs. These multi sector approach will support the poorest and most vulnerable households to achieve sustained income and move out of extreme poverty within a certain period. Furthermore, Skills development, entrepreneurship and business support grant will help youth and unemployed women to establish a decent livelihoods source in their nearby market. In addition to the regular source of income and the enabling livelihoods environment, this will also contribute in the peace building as well.
