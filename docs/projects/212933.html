Project Summary

Mercy-USA aims to target 7,074 HH (36,125 individuals) with food assistance in Aleppo governorate (Afrin district- Jandairis sub-district), Idlib governorate (Idlib district: Maaret Tamsrin and Idlib sub-districts, Jisr-Ash-Shugur district: Darkosh sub-district), Al-Hasakah governorate: Ras Al Ain, Ar-Raqqa governorate: Tell Abiad. By using e-voucher modalities and the value of the e-voucher will be based 65USD for 8 months.
The project is for 12 months: Total number of rounds is 8 as per FSL and CWG guidelines
The assistance will be ongoing until their food security status is maintained without deterioration or to have graduation approach towards livelihoods.
From the same targeted beneficiaries 3600 HH will receive the top up value on the E-voucher (5 USD for 8 months) to be able to access dry and fresh food items from the participated shops in the targeted area.
Mercy-USA will contract with financial service provider (FSP) to provide three services:
Technical service: the E-voucher system who meet Mercy-USA requirements.
Logistic services: contract with shopkeepers in the targeted area. Mercy-USA market team will conduct market assessment and regular market monitoring. eligible shopkeepers (willingness, capacity, reputation, background from previous experience)
Financial service: to reimburse the shopkeepers after each redemption round.
The markets in the assessed locations based on the participants feedback is regularly open and accessible and the basic food items such as wheat flour, rice, bulgur, lentils, tomato paste, oil, sugar, chicken is available as well, however, the price of the basic food items sharply increased mainly due to the Russian invasion of Ukraine, high fluctuation of Turkish lira. The Fuel price highly affected by the Russian invasion of Ukraine, this increasing reflecting increase of other items in the market, as well as the wheat and vegetable oil as the main source of importing these items is from Ukraine which affected by the on-going invasion.
Transfer money mechanism through bank transaction to FSPs.
The project will promote gender equality by having selection criteria to empower women and most vulnerable people by giving some categories higher priority for inclusion such
as:
•Households with malnutrition cases or PLWs with children under 2.
Female/ Child/ disability/ Elderly headed households
•Households with two or more members with a disability (mental and/or physical)
•Household with more than 5 children
• Families without a breadwinner.
•Families with pregnant and lactating women.
