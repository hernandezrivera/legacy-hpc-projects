Project Summary

The project will to provide hygienic kits, promote individual, household and mass level awareness,
disinfect public & mass gathering places, repair & maintain safe water supply options, establish
handwashing points at public & mass gathering places and conduct Psychosocial activities to prevent
COVID 19 pandemic for the vulnerable Host and Rohingya people.

Objectives

1. Ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for Rohingya refugee women, men, boys and girls living in camps, and affected host communities, to safe water for drinking and domestic needs and medical purpose.2. Ensure women, girls, men and boys living in camps and affected host communities or affected by covid-19 have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times.3. Ensure the change of potentially risk behaviors through hygiene promotion and distribution of hygiene items with strong focus on highly contagious diseases, for all refugees, affected and vulnerable communities of the district.

Needs

Access to hygiene materials to promote hygiene practice and behavior Access to safe water supply facilities to reduce waterborne disease Access to individual-level psychosocial support to keep up mental health Access to individual-level health awareness to increase hygiene practice

Activities or outputs

Distribute hygiene kitsConduct mass, household and individual level awareness activities Disinfect public & mass gathering placesRepair & maintain safe water supply options Establish hand washing points at public & mass gathering placesConduct Psychosocial activities
