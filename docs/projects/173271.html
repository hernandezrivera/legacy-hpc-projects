Project Summary

Tdh aims to increase access to quality WASH services for Forcibly Displaced Myanmar Nationals (FDMN) in camps 26 and 27 and host communities, strengthening inclusiveness of facilities through community empowerment and awareness raising. The project will aim to ensure WASH facilities in these areas are functioning, maintained and used. Tdh will continue to operate and maintain the FSM that is already constructed in Camp 26. Given the needs and lack of operational FSMs in the area, the Tdh-operated FSM will continue to receive and treat the sludge from camp 27, the two PHCs and the host communities, as well as the new MPCC. Additionally, Tdh will construct 72 new latrines and rehabilitate the existing ones, as well as constructing 36 bathing cubicles to allow safe and dignified use for women and girls. Throughout, consideration will be given to specific vulnerabilities and needs (e.g. women, youth, children, elderly, people with disabilities, etc.) in order to improve the quality of access to WASH facilities. The project will also focus on community-based hygiene promotion activities, with a concentration on COVID-19 preparedness and response, as well as gender and Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM). These outreach activities will be combined with distributions of proper hygiene kits and dignity hygiene kits.

1 - Identified Needs

In camps 26 and 27 lack of access to proper waste disposal services is a major challenge. Most households reported arranging their own desludging. Less than half of the respondents reported a designated waste pit as the main source of waste disposal, close to a quarter referred to disposing of waste in a water source while another quarter cited undesignated pits. The density of the population results in the latrines quickly filling up, and inadequate desludging services can lead to pits overflowing and contaminating surrounding water sources. In the host communities, though majority of the respondents reported using latrines, 57% of these were reported to be dirty or in poor condition and 34% as out of order. 8% reported relying on open defecation. 20% of households in Camp 26 and 50% of households in Camp 27 reported not having soap in their shelters. In the host communities, 66% reported not having access to soap and water after utilizing latrines.

2 - Activities or outputs

Activity 1: Operations and maintenance of FSM plant, including desludging and dry sludge management. Activity 2: Latrine construction, maintenance, rehabilitation and decommissioning, through consultations with beneficiaries and consideration of gender and disability needs. Activity 3: Construction of separate bathing cubicles to improve comfort, privacy and safety of women and girls within the implementation area. Activity 4: Implementation of hygiene promotion activities with attention to COVID-19 preparedness and response. Activity 5: Implementation of outreach activities that raise awareness of gender and Menstrual Hygiene management (MHM), accompanied by the distribution of dignity hygiene kits. Activity 6: Distribution of hygiene kits four times a year. Activity 7: Participation in the monthly Camp Cleaning Campaign, an activity connected to solid waste management that aims to reduce the volume of waste in the camp by promoting composting and recycling. Activity 8: Borehole installation Activity 9: Tap stand platform construction Activity 10: Bibcock changing Activity 11: Soak pit installation for the waste water management Activity 12: Chlorination Activity 13: Aquatabs distribution regular basis Activity 14: Water Quality Testing Activity 15: IEC material development Activity 16: Water kit distribution
