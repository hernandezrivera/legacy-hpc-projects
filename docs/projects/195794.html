Project Summary

The proposed project will contribute to achieve the SO1 through improved access to humanitarian lifesaving emergency WASH services, and with improved hygienic behaviour and practices of vulnerable conflict-affected households, with stress on prevention measures of COVID 19 and cholera.
In light of the high population density in displacement areas and camps, and the dependence of civilians on alternative sources such as water trucking, which are absolutely unsafe, new concerns arise from the spread of diseases due to the lack of water, especially among children. therefore, there is a strong need to provide personal hygiene items.
The project intends to provide personal hygiene items for about 30000 IDPs (6000 family) in camps of NWS that hosting IDPs.
IAC has good experience in distributing in-kind assistance due to its previous experience in distributing food baskets and non-food baskets since 2018 in several areas within Syria.
Project also will provide containers for solid waste, communal garbage bins and small garbage bins for each tent, also trucks will periodically collect garbage and transport it to designated places to be processed by sorting the contents so as not to damage the environment and surroundings.
Project also includes conducting hygiene promotion sessions to raising awareness about personal hygiene, COVID-19 and cholera prevention and IEC, taking into account prevention measures, social distancing and sterilization. The campaign will be done by trained team on HP principles and presentation skills. Throughout session team will distribute materials printouts and stickers in public spaces in targeted camps, in addition to distributing three rounds of hygiene kit for every family in targeted camps(Kits ‘contents will be according to cluster guidelines).

3 - Needs

According to HNO 2023 the people in need will reach to 13.5M, a 2.6% increase over 2022,they require WASH assistance, more than 2M living in IDPs sites that require comprehensive and continued WASH assistance to survive. In addition to need to access to safe water, and adequate sanitation, the solid waste management and hygiene supplies remain a challenge for IDPs, in particular those in informal or overcrowded sites. On the other hand, on September 2022, the Ministry of Health announced a cholera outbreak in Aleppo governorate with 15 laboratory confirmed cases reported between August 25 and September 9, 2022, and this number is constantly increasing. It is estimated that about 5M of 8M need WASH assistance to control and prevent further spread of the outbreak.IAC has conducted RNA in many camps in 2 locations of severity 4 according severity scale of HNO 20231.Daret Azza: 7000 IDPs 1400 HHs, 4000 Female, 3000 Male, 1200 young children, 2300 children, 2500 Adult, 1000 older people, 380 PWD.2.Atareb: 2300 IDPs 4600 HHs, 12000 Female, 11000 Male, 3800 young children, 8700 children, 7500 Adult, 3000 older people, 600 PWD. 80% have previously received WASH assistance.60% were not fully satisfied with received assistance.50% said the quantity was insufficient.60% said the quality was not good.70% said assistance was distributed at once or a few times in irregular periods.60% said they do not have the ability to permanently and adequately secure personal hygiene supplies.

4 - Implementation of activities

- At the 1st month, IAC will conduct a meeting with the local actors and IDPs representatives (males and females) in each targeted location to explain the project and its goals, take any suggestions which help in the success of the project and ensure their support. - Distributions of hygiene kits or each family on three rounds according to WASH cluster guidelines.- Installation of solid waste containers (100 liter capacity), provision of communal garbage bins (100 liter capacity) and periodically collect garbage and carry it to a designated place. (one container for every 10 families).As well as will conduct Hygiene promotion campaign through trained team of male and female hygiene promoters, the main topics will cover the self-hygiene as per the COVID-19 and cholera related recommendations and water conservation and rational consumption.the team will use the recommended IEC materials by WASH cluster.In addition, they will install wall panels contains messages on protection and awareness of the COVID-19, and cholera directed and easy to understand for children.The project is considered as a planned and will target two locations (Daret Azza and Atareb) in Jebel Saman district of Aleppo governorate.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

To prevent any PSEA issue from occurring during the implementation period of the project activities, especially the activities in which the project staff are in direct contact with the beneficiaries, such as the distribution team. All field activities have been reviewed and assessed for risk factors for PSEA with the use of the PSEA Network Field and Organizational Mitigation and Prevention Checklists and the following prevention and mitigation measures will be taken: 1- IAC will make sure that All staff, and volunteers understand and sign the Code of Conduct. 2- Field staff with direct contact with beneficiaries will receive PSEA training 3- IAC will review internal reporting mechanisms and ensure all staff know about reporting mechanisms 4- female staff will always be present at the distribution site and involved in the distribution process 5-Benefeciaries will be given clear information on timing and content of distribution 6- Beneficiaries will be given information that aid is free7- PSEA messaging and awareness raising will be done though pasting PSEA posters and placing PSEA banners at the distribution site or other service place.
