Project Summary

Mushroom farming and production unit is located at Idlib Governorate-Harem district-Armanaz city, This area has been selected for its proximity to the project raw materials and expected discharge markets of project outputs, in addition to the availability of a network of good roads linking it with other regions, with the availability of adequate infrastructure of water, transportation, telephone and sanitation, in addition to the remote area by the targeted areas of aviation bombing, in addition to the presence of a large proportion of IDPs and residents in the area due to the quiet of the security situation of the region, which is considered better than other areas.
The BNFs of project activities will be from Al-Wastani & Al-Zawia mountain villages (target areas) which considered as the poorest regions in Idlib governorate, and suffered from neglect and marginalization before and during Syrian conflict.
Mushroom farming were spread in 25 villages of all geographical areas of Al-Wastani & Al-Zawia mountains(which consider as more profit places from the constituents of mushroom farming) before the starting of Syrian conflict, .
Villages of Al-Wastani & Al-Zawia mountains population is about 500,000 people and under the current situation, the number raised to more than 700,000 people because of many IDPs were registered in the area where the presence of many of the shelters and housing centers for the IDPs.

Needs

Villages of Al-Wastani & Al-Zawia mountains were one of the most importance production centers for mushroom, in addition that this activity was essential solution for rocky lands problem which spread in this area.In 2011 the number of mushroom farmers in targeted areas reached to 2500 farmer at 25 villages, which produced large quantities of mushroom placed in the local markets and covered population needs from that substance (this activities were running by rural development projects which related to regime), Currently 2016 the constituents of mushroom farming were losted and the main reason of loosing is the absence of supporting and adminstrative body. Inspite of the availability of manpower in targeted areas, but agricultural ownership are small and unsufficient to meet local population needs, this leds to large migration to neighboring cities or other countries looking for a sufficient source of income.This situation reflected negatively on the food security and livelihoods of the local population in terms of the scarcity of these products in the local markets and prices increase as follow (125 Sp in 2011 to 1200 Sp in 2016), except for economic loss resulting from the stopping of mushroom production projects.Some local population start thinking about income sources farness from land need, and originally including mushroom farming, which actually required experience and production requirements.
