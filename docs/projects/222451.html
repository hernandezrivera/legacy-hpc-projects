Project Summary

This project aims to enhance community health systems by strengthening the delivery of essential health, nutrition, and protection services for vulnerable and underserved populations especially in crises-affected and remotes areas. This can be done through Boma Health Initiatives (BHI). Humanitarian and Development Consortium will empower Boma Health Team (BHT) and deploy mobile health units to provide critical services, including maternal and child health, communicable diseases prevention, nutritional interventions, mental health support and Gender Based Violence (GBV) response. The project will also address disability rehabilitation, safe deliveries and ensure access to essential medicines and medical supplies furthermore, the initiatives will provide individual protection assistance (IPA) to empower vulnerable households to access necessary health and nutrition services while nurturing resilience and sustainability within the community.

1. Needs

Communities in Crises affected regions face significant barriers to accessing quality healthcare due to inadequate health infrastructure, limited medical personnel, and ongoing displacement challenges. Vulnerable persons like women, children, person with disabilities and survivors of GBV are particularly at risks often suffering from preventable illness, malnutrition, and lack of reproductive and psychosocial care. High morbidity and mortality rates from communicable diseases such as malaria, diarrhea, and acute respiratory infections further worsen the situation. Nutritional vulnerabilities, especially among children and pregnant women highlight the critical need for sustained health interventions and community engagement. Moreover, individual with disabilities face stigma and limited access to rehabilitative services, assistive devices and vocational training, which hampers their inclusion and dignity. Displaced populations and returnees require comprehensive support to address physical emotional and social challenges.

2. Project activities

Improve equitable access to life-saving essential comprehensive quality health care services including maternal, child and adolescent health, sexual and reproductive health, treatment common illnesses, disability, MHPSS and GBV health services to crisis affected populationsActivities: 1.1.: Strengthening Community Health Structure (Boma Health Initiative)In line with the Boma Health Initiative (BHI), Humanitarian &Development (HDC) will form and lead a team Boma Health Team (BHT) composed of trained HDC Community Mobilizers and Boma health Worker to provide quality health care services at community level according to the needs, the services will include management of un-complicated medical cases and referral of the critical cases for further level of management, ANC and postnatal follow-up, referral of pregnant mother to PHCU/PHCC for safe deliveries; Provision of Provision of sexual and reproductive health (family planning) awareness and information. Child survival interventions like vaccination campaigns, Vitamin A supplementation, deworming, health education promotion and counseling services will be provided both at community and health facility level. Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) services will be strengthen by mobilizing children in the community and linking them with health facilities for immunizations. The BHT will carry out Nutrition Surveillance and Health Promotion through monitoring nutrition, particularly in vulnerable populations where children are at risk of acute malnutrition. This includes: regular community-based screening; referral of malnourished children to existing therapeutic feeding programs, follow-up and defaulter tracing to ensure continuous care.Activity: 1.2.: Outreach/mobile health, EPI services delivery and home visitHDC will form and deploy 2 mobile health teams in each, each team will be compost of 4 health staff (a clinical officer, a nurse, a midwife and nutrition assistant). The Mobile health team will conduct at least once a month scheduled outreach health service to each village far from the health facility under the project catchment and in areas where there is a displaced population. The activity will ensure access to each component of essential health services for crisis-affected population. The outreach service will include treatment of common illness, delivering preventive and promotional health messages, nutritional screening and treatment, EPI services, follow-up ANC, and distribution of LLINs, child health, newborn health, adolescent care, maternal and sexual reproductive health, non-communicable disease, injury and first-aid, mental health and psychosocial support. The BHT will support the mobile health team by mobilizing the community. Triage, curative consultation, and referral: The mobile health team will provide OPD treatment service targeting children and adults during outreach. Rapid laboratory test services including rapid malaria test, dipstick urine test, syphilis test, etc will be available. Critical cases will be resuscitated on emergency basis and referred to the nearest highest facility.Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) services will be provided by linking with existing cold chain facility in the CHD and by incentivizing the CHD EPI vaccinators joining HDC mobile health team, Community engagement will be promoted by the strengthening BHT.The key intervention for Mobile health team will include:Communicable disease, Child health, Maternal and reproductive health, no disease, Injury and first-aid, Mental Health and Psychosocial Support. Activity 1.3.: Safe deliveries assisted with qualified staff: Based on the needs, HDC will deploy a qualified midwife to the communities in the county to run and facilitate the implementation Activity 1.4: Provision of Clinical Management of GBV/Rape cases: As part of its integrated response, HDC will strengthen the capacities of existing health facilities to provide CMR to GBV survivors.
