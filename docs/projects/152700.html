Project Summary

The Purpose of this emergency nutrition response is to provide lifesaving emergency nutrition services to the most vulnerable population living at hard to reach areas in Yerol West and Yerol East counties of the Eastern Lakes Republic of South Sudan.
The nutrition project will be implemented in Yirol East and Yirol West counties of former Lakes State. UNOCHA estimated the mid-2018 population at 131970 and 168679 for the two counties respectively.
September IPC report predicted Population will be in critical emergency phase if not catastrophe phase in Yirol West, Yirol East and other former counties in South Sudan.
According to UNOCHA based on the survey conducted in 2017 and published in 2018 the below table shows nutrition status findings.
County GAM SAM – U5 MAM – U5 MAM - PLW
Yirol East 21.7 5.9 11.1 31.4
Yirol West 17.7 2.5 15.2 31.4

The Nutrition project will include prevention and treatment of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) and Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) using OTP, and TSFP, modalities to treat or prevent Moderate (MAM) and severe acute malnutrition (SAM) among children under five years and PLWs.
Grant total budget for LiveWell’s blended emergency nutrition response for 2019 is US$ 250,000 (two hundred and fifty thousand only)

Needs

The effect of conflict in South Sudan led to high inflation, increased incident andprevalence of communicable disease, food insecurity, and destruction of the existing health infrastructures and social services for the general population, which attributed to worsening nutrition situation. It also has a profound effect on the existence and functionality of health facilities to provide lifesaving nutrition services to the most vulnerable groups among IDPs and host communities due to looting, destruction, and killing of health workers (SSA 2017/18). Recent IPC report shows that 25,000 people in Yirol East, and Yirol West counties may go into a catastrophe (IPC 5) if there are no measures to improve post-harvest in October and December. Also, a critical GAM rate (15%-29.9%) level of acute malnutrition in the Lakes State. nutrition services and other cross-cutting issues like HIV/AIDS, gender mainstreaming, TB, Mental health, disabilities and NCDs by the current donors supporting LiveWell to provide lifesaving services. Protection Risk Analysis?

Briefly describe how

The project will focus on gender disparity and sensitivity to ensure that all the vulnerable populations enjoy the same positive essential life-saving health services. Women bear the economic responsibilities of their families, are not in a position to make family and personal choices including reproductive issues.The project will ensure that women and girls are empowered proportionally to be given equal opportunity to be employed and serve their community. Women and girls must receive equal treatment in accessing essential lifesaving clinical health and protection services. Special effort will be made to ensure that theneeds of adolescent youth (Boys/Girls) will be provided with equal opportunities. However; based on the Nutrition cluster strategies, LiveWell’s activities will be designed to target GBV mitigation in collaboration with the protection cluster and through gendered risk assessments. Staff will be trained on Protection and GBV guidelines and communities consulted (disaggregated by sex/age) before designing a program/project. LiveWell will strengthen advocacy for high protection standards to rollout nutrition interventions in the areas of implementation.
