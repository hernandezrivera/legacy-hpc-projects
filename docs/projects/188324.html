Project Summary

This project is to responds to the need for humanitarian life-saving shelter and NFI assistance to IDPs and host community HHs in sub-districts in Aleppo and Idleb governorates targeting 22,000 HHs (110,000 individuals)
Through:
*Rehabilitation for 250 unfinished buildings (4 rooms in each, for 4 HHs) with value 3000$ (750$ for each room), totaling, they will be hosted by 1000 IDP HHs (5000 individuals).
*Provide NFI assistance (Winter Labelled CASH) with the value of 150$, in one go in Dec, as SNFI Cluster Guidance to 8000HHs (40000 individuals).
*Flood Response (activities include tents winterization, insulation, leveling, graveling pathways, and culverts construction) (over 150 camps), targeting 10000HHs (50000 Individuals) to ensure safety, flood protection, and dignified living conditions
*Provide shelter repair valued at the average of $600 for 3000 HHs (15000 individuals)
Assistance is determined as technical assessment of each house/unfinished building, and camp needs, and BINAA will follow SNFI Cluster Guidance to ensure the scope of work that ensures the standards, dignity, privacy, and protection
The project will be in Idlib and Aleppo covering sub-districts where most vulnerable persons are sheltered in damaged/unfished buildings, IDP sites, and individual houses.
The project is 24 months, from Jan-22 to Dec-23
Shelter repair beneficiaries will be IDP and HC HHs living in an inadequate shelter that does not meet minimum SPHERE minimum standards for square meters of covered living space per person and has inadequate privacy or protection from the elements, settlement planning, and WASH facilities. Targeting houses that have light to medium damage but remain structurally sound. Works include doors and windows repair or replacement, partition installation, sealing cracks and holes in walls, and WASH facilities repairs. The works will ensure increased privacy between males and females, host and hosted families that enhance gender-sensitive response.
Furthermore, for HHs with PwDs, BINAA will follow Washington Tool, additional works will be included to respond to PwDs' needs including disability-friendly entrance and toilet facilities (e.g., a ramp with a handrail for using wheelchairs at the building entrance and seating provisions for toilets)
The rehabilitation varies as the number of rooms in each unfinished building, installation of masonry partitions, repair of WASH facilities within the building. To enhance gender-sensitive response, necessary adaptations that particularly affect women and girls will include appropriate lighting, separate toilets and showers for men and women, accessible facilities for people with disabilities (disability friendly entrance and toilet facilities), areas for bathing, washing of clothing, and partitions to create spaces for different households and additional partitions within households to create privacy for men and women
The minimum standards are derived from Sphere Project standards and indicators, agreed by operational Shelter Actors specifically for the programmatic context in NW Syria so that beneficiaries live in safety with dignity. Using these standards will enable BINAA to achieve the agreed project objective consistently, accountable, and equitably and help to avoid diversion and duplication of aid
BINAA will follow the Cluster guidelines on Shelter repair and rehabilitation
BINAA staff have strong communication skills and a clear understanding of the humanitarian principles to enable a dialogue with communities and avoid potential tension and conflict and will work on the beneficiary selection. BINAA will ensure that these staff had received PSEA training and work closely with the Protection team to identify possible protection concerns
BINAA team is well experienced in delivering both shelter rehabilitation and cash assistance with solid program management. The experience is gained, and lessons are learned to improve the implementation of future programs.

4 - Needs

As per HNO-21, only 40% of IDPs in Idleb and Aleppo live in finished houses or apartments. An estimated 770,000 IDPs live in tents, 483,000 in unfinished houses or apartments, 305,000 in concrete block shelters, and 77,000 in makeshift shelters' all inadequate shelter types’ and therefore have particular high needs for continuous shelter assistance/repair and NFIsMost IDPs in urban and rural areas live in poor host communities that need support, where the IDPs are facing shelter/accommodation issues, In light of high population density in the relatively safe displacement areas, which is accompanied by insufficient number of equipped housing, the cost associated with meeting the IDPs’ housing needs was $600/year, this forced IDPs to live in places that don't meet the minimum standards of housing requirements or preserve either security or dignity of family members, such as schools, shops, destroyed and unfinished buildings in which shelter and WASH facilities are sub-standardFurther, BINAA conducted need assessment to understand their ever-changing needs more in-depth. Given the continually growing humanitarian needs, the need assessment revealed that shelter security is one of the most urgent needs. The participants also stated that MPG including cash for rent or cash grant to support shelter reconstruction/rehabilitation is most priorities interventions to protect IDPs who live in damaged or unfinished shelter units from climate effects and secure their privacy and safety

5 - Implementation of activities

BINAA will task expert engineers to do:Setup and planning.Community Sensitization.HLP Due diligence.Vulnerability Assessment.E-voucher service provider.Redemption.Tendering and Contracting for rehab.M&E throughout all steps including Price Monitoring and Market Assessment.Quality control and daily supervision: the contractor will be requested to submit weekly reports on work progress, challenges, time plan deviations, updated time plan. BINAA technicals will be daily following up on work progress, reviewing, and approving the weekly reports before submission for ensuring information accuracy.Quality Control tools will be used for ensuring the quality of work and materials provided.Safety and security: The contractors must provide all work sites with warning signs and isolate them during the work for avoiding any injuries. BINAA will assign safety and security qualified staff for ensuring safety and security practices and they can intervene when needed by using stored materials in BINAA warehouse (warning signs, tapes, etc.).Time control: BINAA team will be providing daily updates on progress, implemented works (quality and quantity), and this information will be reflected on the designed time plan, therefore, the time plan will be tracked, and the needed corrective actions be taken.COVID19: BINAA staff will be provided with masks and sanitizers and requested to keep social distance and take all measures. In addition, the contractors must do the same practices.
