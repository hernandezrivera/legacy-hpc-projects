Project Summary

AVSI will set up 5 Multipurpose Spaces, which serves as space for protection and psychosocial support services and activities for children, adolescent, women, caregivers and communities.
Within the facilities, a group of 4 animators in collaboration with the identified community volunteers, will assist families and children through psychosocial support and recreational activities. The group of animators will receive training sessions on CP Approach, PSS and Child Marriage Prevention to be ready also for the implementation of awareness sessions in the communities. In the same spaces, community awareness sessions will be organized targeting women, caregivers, and families and by strengthening bonding between parents and children, community-based PSS can be enhanced. A particular attention will be given to the prevention of early child marriage, which is a known risk in the area for adolescent girls.
In order to encourage the sustainability of the initiative and to foster the appropriation of the essential mechanisms of protection by the target communities, 5 people for each Multipurpose Space will be selected during the awareness-raising sessions held in the communities by AVSI staff in order to become community based volunteers. The community volunteers will be identified mainly among youth (of 18 to 25 years of age), taking into consideration a gender balance, in order to involve them directly as protagonist and agents for a positive change in all the interventions put in place in the project.
The animators and the Community Based Volunteers will be responsible of the implementation of the psychosocial activities for the main age groups: Children from 6 to 10 yrs. Recreational and PSS activities will be organized on a daily basis for groups of children,aimed to promote psychomotor skills, communication skills, self-esteem, sharing and learning and creativity; Children from 10 to 14 yrs. For this age group activities will be organised using the methodology of focus group discussions and cultural events. It will be also promoted the creation of youth club for social activities inside the communities. Previous experiences have shown the importance of involving adolescents and youth in promoting the participation of community members and increasing community awareness toward protection mechanisms. Adolescents from 14 to 18 yrs. Debates, focus groups and youth clubs will be organised also for this age group, focusing mainly on GBV and early marriage prevention. On a voluntary basis, adolescents will be involved in implementing the PSS and recreational activities for children in the multipurpose spaces, to increase their self confidence, sense of belonging and responsibility towards their community.
The project will budget also 5 months salary for an average of 2 Case Workers per multipurpose space for a total of 10 caseworkers (5 men and 5 woman) and will also provide necessary materials and resources, such as bag, stationary, and T-shirt. Case Management training will be based on the DPGCAS existing case management forms and training packages, which have been adapted with agreement of the government for the emergency context. In addition, Case workers will receive a more in-depth training on child protection issues, PSS, GBV, early marriage prevention, referral pathways and coordination mechanisms.
Faced with the need to strengthen the capacities of local operators in managing the most fragile cases and in order to better structure the existing referral mechanisms, the project provides for transversal activities of capacity building of local authority member and organizations.
Beyond the Child Protection Committees, AVSI will support other existing structures to ensure that they are aware of protection issues and means of referral. At government level, AVSI could organize training and refresher training on PSS for government Social Workers.
