Project Summary

The present GBVS project targets to support community members in Askira Uba LGA with sanitation facilities for proper disposal of waste and solid fecal matter. The project will also ensure protection of communal water sources through hygiene support and water chlorination, as well as creation of WASHCOMs among community members in Askira Uba LGA of Borno state. The project will also support the provision of real-time data on the mapping and functionality of water and sanitation and water facilities in the LGA of Askira Uba. Through this, the project will facilitate the provision of real-time and verifiable data to aid decisions for the GBVS and other Sector partners including IMC, who serves as cluster lead in Askira Uba. Finally, the project shall work towards ODF certification for the LGA.

1 - Needs

The present GBVS project aims to address various needs related to clean water supply, sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion towards KAP transformation in Askira Uba LGA of Borno State. The specific identified needs that this WASH project will address issues that include: 1. Provision of Access to Clean Water: Ensure a sustainable and safe supply of drinking water. Improve access to water sources, especially in areas with water scarcity. 2. Provide Sustainable Sanitation Facilities: Provide access to proper sanitation facilities, including toilets and latrines. Promote the construction of sanitary infrastructure to prevent open defecation. Ensure the safe disposal of human waste to prevent contamination of water sources. 3. Support KAP through Hygiene Promotion: Educate communities on good hygiene practices. Promote handwashing with soap and water to prevent the spread of diseases. Raise awareness about the importance of personal and environmental hygiene. 4. Support Disease Prevention in Askira Uba LGA: Reduce waterborne diseases by improving water quality and sanitation. Implement measures to prevent the spread of water-related diseases like cholera, diarrhea, and dysentery. 5. Foster Community Empowerment: Involve communities in the planning and implementation of WASH projects. Build local capacity for the sustainable management of water and sanitation facilities. Promote community ownership and responsibility for maintaining infrastructure. 6. Foster Gender and Social Inclusion: Address the specific needs of vulnerable groups, such as women, children, and people with disabilities. Ensure that sanitation facilities are designed to be inclusive and accessible for all. 7. Sustainability: Ensure that gains achieved by the project are sustained beyond its life cycle.

2 - Activities

Overall project activities include: 1. Water Targets: Increase access to safe and reliable drinking water sources. Modalities and Technologies: Drilling and installation of boreholes or wells. Rehabilitation of existing water sources. Construction of water supply systems, including hand pumps, motorized pumps, and gravity-fed systems. Installation of water purification and treatment systems. 2. Sanitation Targets: Targets: Ensure access to improved sanitation facilities and promote proper waste disposal. Modalities and Technologies: Construction of latrines and toilets, including ventilated improved pit latrines (VIPs) and pour-flush toilets. Promotion of community-led total sanitation (CLTS) approaches to end open defecation. Implementation of fecal sludge management systems. Construction of sewage systems in urban areas. 3. Hygiene Promotion Targets: Targets: Improve hygiene practices within communities. Modalities and Technologies: Community-based hygiene education and awareness campaigns. Distribution of hygiene kits containing soap, toothbrushes, and other essentials. Establishment of handwashing stations with soap and water. Training on proper food handling and storage.
