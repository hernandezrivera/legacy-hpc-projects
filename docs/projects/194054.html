Project Summary

HOPE is planning to work on recovering access to WASH services for the 495,880 vulnerable affected population in localities of Jebel Awlia & Khartoum in KH, Zalingei & Rokero in CD, Assalaya in ED, Ed Al Fursan & Beliel in SD, Geneina & Habila in WD, Al Idia & An Nuhud in WK, and Al Fao & Al Butanah in Gadaref, that will contribute to 3 HRP WASH sector objectives for 2023.
In this project, HOPE will (1) provision access to lifesaving water services include (i) operationalization of water treatment plants in Khartoum, (ii) water distribution (water trucking) to health facilities and gathering points, (2) provision of essential WASH supplies and NFIs to support emergency sanitation services and compliment hygiene promotion activities – particularly in flood prone areas affected people and (3) Rehabilitation and operation and maintenance (O&M) water supply schemes includes supply pipeline support and technical and management capacity for sustainable WASH services.
The main activities will be delivered by HOPE in this project are: Rehabilitate of Mini Water Yard (MWY); Drilling and construction of Hand Pump wells (HP); Rehabilitate hand pumps driven water points; Upgrade high yielding hand pump system to Mini Water Yard; Support operation and maintenance of motorized mini water yard; Establish and train water quality volunteers on surveillance, treatment, and reporting; Conduct chlorination treatment at the household, and water point; Train Water Management Committee (WMCs) members on the O&M, and sustainability; Conduct locality wide CLTS approach; Construction of gender sensitive latrines in schools; Train and support women on PHAST and hygiene promoters; and Train and support Solid Waste Management Committees and hygiene worker to conduct clean-up campaign at weekly basis.
HOPE will consider accountability to affected people through full community participation, strengthen the establish / refresh the community mechanism/ committee such as WASH committees and hygiene promotors as well as do follow up for all benefit targeted separately to assess the project, identify challenges and provide recommendations to improve the project outcomes.

1 - Needs

Clashes between SAF and RSF have been ongoing since 15 April, particularly in and around residential areas in Khartoum, Darfur stats except East Darfur as well as a north part of West Kordofan state and capital city of North Kordofan. As of 13 May, the Federal Ministry of Health reports that at least 700 people have been killed and over 5,200 injured since the onset of the fighting. The number of casualties is likely to be higher, as many individuals were reportedly unable to reach hospitals, services facilities and markets due to movement constraints. Several cities have faced heavy bombardments and clashes, and associated unexploded ordnance, causing hundreds of civilian deaths and casualties, as well as damage to critical water, health care and power infrastructure. Looting and destruction of health facilities, markets and government institutions was the main feature in the Khartoum and Darfur states. Ongoing violence has caused extensive damage to infrastructure, leading to water shortages, blackouts, communication disruptions and incidents of looting. Large sections of the capital, including factories, banks, shops and markets, have been looted, damaged, or targeted by rocket attacks.Based on WASH gabs and findings collected by HOPE in the targeted localities in Sep-Oct 2022 and updated in mid of May 2023, in these target localities, compared to a gap, the WASH interventions are very weak including a shortage of clean water drinking, deterioration of sanitation & hygiene; no latrines in most villages; open defecation is widely practiced increasing, drinking water is suspected to be contaminated meanly in the rainy season, no vector control was in place; increasing of flies and mosquitos was reported. Based on PiN – May 2023 by WASH sector, The WASH severity for 12 localities it is 4 or 5, the total People In Need (PIN) for 12 localities are 5,869,114 disaggregated to 495,503 old IDPs, 439,785 Displacement due to clashes by place of ORIGIN after 15 April to 5 May 2023, while the PiN for WASH sector is 2,174,772 and targeted beneficiaries is 948,779. The PIN/ target disaggregated to 19.6% of men 20.4% of women 29.4% of boys 30.6% of girls 12% people with disabilities, also disaggregated to 41% male, 59% female, 8% elderly, 63% children and 29 adults.The main communicable diseases and outbreaks that related to the deterioration of WASH services of vector control, solid waste management, and contamination of drinking water in the target locality are Malaria, AWD, Fever, and Schistosomiasis due to the bad health system, lack of communities’ practices in term of handwashing.In Darfur, the inter-communal, inter-tribal conflicts, and violence that started in 15 April which are affected the safety and security of people exacerbating the risk of sexual violence especially when displaced. Women and girls continue to face the risk of GBV including sexual violence when they leave the villages or during attack by looters. Fear of reprisals from attackers often prevents rape survivors from seeking medical help. An Nuhud and Al Idia locality are also suffering from poor WASH services, in last years, registered the highest cases of Malaria and AWD due to a deterioration of WASH services, lack of sanitation, and hygiene practices by community people. In April 2023, RSF attacked the city, some WASH services are affected.El Fao and Al Butanah localities of Gadaref is suffering from poor WASH services and a deterioration in services due to the floods that happened this year. Also, in October 2020 the arrival of Ethiopian refugees fleeing the fighting in Ethiopia began, as well as Gadaref state hosted more than 66,779 refugees (UNHCR record – Oct 2022) and expected increasing the new refugee's arrival, so there is an urgent need to improve the WASH services of the host communities. Due to the above situation, HOPE looks for improving access to WASH services.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

During design, HOPE assessed the safety, security and risk for target areas as well as types of GBV that facing women. During implementation, HOPE will support the capacities of committee members and assist to know their rights to WASH. All essential drugs at the pharmacy will be handled with the utmost care and storage - warehouses will be clean to avoid contamination. This project will include psycho-social stimulation activities for infants and young children will also be provided with psychosocial support to help mothers deal with their own distress and improve care for their children. Child protection messages on prevention and response as well as referral mechanisms will be included in activities related to WASH outreach and awareness-raising. All WASH volunteers will be trained on referral procedures and mechanisms linking patients directly to experts from other agencies and the government is already supporting the WASH sector. Children and women will be given priorities. Privacy for HH shared latrines will be considering. Awareness-raising during community mobilization on WASH, education a key component that HOPE has put in place for better understanding among the beneficiaries for self-protection on various programmatic matters. HOPE also will develop a system to collect feedback from beneficiaries to measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints.
