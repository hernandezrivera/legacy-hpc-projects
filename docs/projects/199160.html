Project Summary

The project supports the most vulnerable populations in Idleb and Mahmbal subdistricts.
It aims to enhance the resilience of affected people through integrated early recovery interventions. 11,000 HHs (8174 women, 7986 men, 8704 boys and 9536 girls) will benefit from the activities of this project.
the proposed project keep focusing on increasing resilience of people with disabilities (PwDs) and IDPs through community and beneficiary participation through utilizing outreach modality which will focus on improving PwD resilience through set of prevention and response activities that mitigate protection risks, where all of the project activities will include the consideratiojn of PWD in all of the intervention stage, also the proposed interventions will be implemented in coordination and collaboration with other HIHFAD projects of psychosocial supportting for people with disabilities and their family members, structured PSS program, problem management (PM+) which affect positively the beneficiary's social network and strengthening the positive lifestyle during their rehabilitation journey.
This proposed project aim will be achieved by supporting the value chain of wheat in Al rouge area- Idlib Governorate and Tell Abiad subdistrict - Ar-Raqqa via light rehabilitation of new 3 wells including connected irrigation channels in the area in addition to rehabilitation of the roads to cultivable lands and farms in addition to rehabilitating the roads around the bakeries. Capacity of farmers will be also enhanced by training on strategic thinking, business management and marketing, cash for work scheme may enhance a feeling of inequality and discrimination among different segments of the target communities. In order to avoid an escalation of any tensions, the project will clearly inform the selection criteria to the target communities, and let the communities understand that the vulnerable households are supported through the project, not only landowners. In order to enhance the social cohesion in the targeted areas, the project will ensure outreaching IDPs, including IDPs who are renting a land for and who are able to participate in cash for work or families what have persons with functional difficulties. Women headed households (whether IDP or from the host community) as priority in the selection criteria as they tend to face limited income generating opportunities, compared with their male peers. Community will be engaged throughout the project cycle and HIHFAD will build on the already existing beneficiary committee in Al Rouge plain and will make sure that it includes people from the host community and the IDPs in addition to women in order to reach all groups in the targeted area.
HIHFAD will coordinate with both Education Cluster, Field Education Directorate and Local authorities to select the list of schools which is already running and needs for rehabilitation and then HIHFAD will select 15 schools, where HIHFAD already did same activities in 2021, where HIHFAD rehabilitated 10 schools in Afrin and Idleb Area in collaboration with Education cluster, Field Education Directorate and Local authorities, HIHFAD will use a field contractors and will enhance the cash for work for IDPs to complete the rehabilitation of the Schools.

3 - Needs

HIHFAD conducted a needs assessment Recently in Afrin district in Aleppo governorate to identify needs, the study surveyed a sample of 86 respondents, 91% were males. The vast majority of the respondents (94%) were married, nearly 5% were single, and 1% were widows. Study found that out of 872 individuals in the selected sample, there were equality of both males and females’ percentages with 50%. The surveyed households have on average 5 people comprised of %48 adults (male and female), and 52% children.Overall, 8% of total individuals were elderly people, 8% having chronic diseases, and 2% with disabilities.(75%) indicated that they derive their income from agriculture activities. Few percentages of respondents work on a regular basis and some of them depend on livestock production, casual labor and the transfer of money from relatives abroad. On other hand, respondents were asked to report the contribution of each source of income in their total income, the households stated during their interviews that 98% of them work in agriculture, with an income participation rate of up to 83%.The main source of irrigation for agricultural lands in the areas of Afrin district is rainwater with the rate of 80%, and some villages reported using the water of the nearest river with mentioning the bad situation of the canals.KIs expressed concerns related to the roads in Afrin area that need rehabilitation to allow farmers to sell their products. 4 - Implementation of activities

HIHFAD field team will conduct a light rehabilitation of 40 KM of roads to cultivable lands and farms, light rehabilitation of new 3 wells including connected irrigation channels in the area, in addition to rehabilitating 5 KM of the roads around the bakeries using a casual labors from both IDPs and locals form the same community to provide a short term job opportunities, all of the suitable jobs for PWD will be dedicated only to PWD during the implementation of this stage. HIHFAD will conduct a package of training for farmers on strategic thinking, small business management and marketing, also HIHFAD will re-contact the local education authorities and Education Cluster to re-update the list of the schools in northern part of Aleppo which need for rehabilitation in addition to keep coordination with UN-Education Cluster, then HIHFAD field team will revisit the 15 schools to put the final study taking in consideration PWD needs in all of these schools (suitable entrance and toilets) for the rehabilitation before tendering "also this activities will be conducted through local suppliers and will use cash for work for the IDPs and locals", where all of the cash for work activities will be follwoing Cash for Work Minimum Recommended Actions Guidance for NWS and all of the selection will be done in collaboration with the community and will ensure thtat the wages will not over or belwo the local market level.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

In order to support the vulnerable households, the project ensures 10% of the beneficiaries to be female-headed households, via all of the project's contracting phases and will announce for that and share the feedback mechanism in the communities to ensure the non-discriminations, also HIHFAD MEAL team will encourage the beneficiaries to use the feedback mechanism and to keep claiming for their rights and feel free to complain on our serveries and on others as well.Considering the high vulnerability of beneficiaries, HIHFAD will provide all the hired staff an induction training on the protection principles, and safeguarding training including (PSEA, CP and gender equality) to ensure that the project services will be provided in dignified, confidential, maintaining privacy, accessible and free-of-charge basis for all those in need regardless of their individual gender groupings.Beside training, reporting channels will be distributed to the beneficiaries in all project locations including an email address and hotline, this number is held with the female and male focal pointA monthly accountability report is prepared accompanied by statistics that express the numbers of beneficiaries that have been reached, disaggregated by gender.HIHFAD provides all staff with an induction training on the protection principles, so they become oriented enough to provide the services in a dignified and safe way for the beneficiaries.The project will pay attention not to increase child labor.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Persons with disabilities are at higher risks of neglect and exclusion from the families and the service providers. Women and girls with disabilities are two times at higher risk of neglect and exclusion from the families and the service providers, to compare with their peers without disabilities all of this makes the persons with disabilities in higher risks of SEA. Females with disabilities remain most invisible groups in the Syrian society, they may not leave their houses, and not participating in the social events in the communities hugely isolated. Thus, the project will ensure outreaching house-bounded females by ensuring appropriate gender balance within the team, so ensure our access to this group. HIHFAD provides all the hired staff an induction training on the protection principles, PSEA and GBV so they become oriented enough to provide the services in a dignified and safe way for the beneficiaries. A focal point for PSEA and GBV would be appointed per area. HIHFAD has a pre-established feedback system and safeguarding reporting channel that supports the ability of the targeted populations to submit their complaints, comments, and suggestions in a beneficiary-friendly and easy-to-access way. The system provides diverse options for the convenience of the beneficiaries to deliver their feedback including WhatsApp hotlines, feedback boxes presented at all HIHFAD supported facilities in addition to the direct feedback.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1-IEDs and explosive Devices: this Risk is common. Mitigation Measures: -Avoid crowded places. -Avoid peak times for movements. -Avoid military points as possible. -Keep 200 meter as a safety distance from any military cars and suspected cars. -Using the main roads only and after check with HIHFAD security team. -Maps of the safe roads (HIHFAD security team provides them on Daily Bases). -SMP / SOP / CP in place and permanently updated.2-Risk of market volatility: the probability of this Risk is low, but it could happen. Mitigation Measures: -HIHFAD had a good experience in this kind of projects. -HIHFAD has a good and qualified logistcis team among all of NW Syria areas and will balance the procurement from different markets to ensure the stability of the markets. -also importing goods from Turkey will stay as a contengecy plan to not affect the markets for the local populations.3-Robbery of the Staff: according to the security incidents data in the area, the probability of this Risky is very low. Mitigation Measures: -Activities may be split into several small ones.4- the road or canal is passing a private property Risk is low, but it could happen at any time. Mitigation Measures: take an official approval from the official owner to conduct the rehabilitation on his property.- take an official documents from the aothuroties that the roads and canals are public property and HIHFAD allowed to conduct the rehabiliutation. 5
