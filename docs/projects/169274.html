Project Summary

PU-AMI will aim to Strengthen infection prevention and control measures through provision of PPEs will be donate to 5 supported or will be supported by PU-AMI in order to prevent sharp increase in COVID cases.
PU-AMI will ensure the good quality of PPEs as per WHO recomendations for health facilities such as alcohol handrub, none sterile gloves , medical face masks, Soap, Mask N95,Goggles, face shield, apron, heavy duty gloves, and bleach. PU-AMI will procure those PPEs and distribute according to the distribution plan. The quantities of PPEs were estimated based on the average number of consultation and the previous consumption.
Furthermore, PU-AMI will ensure to provide through PU-AMI Quality advisor and /or Pharmacist the proper use of those PPEs.A donation letter will be signed by the PHCCs once the PPEs are delivered to the 5 PHCCs (Bolorgogian, Zarif, Binet hoda, Khatam Anbiaa, Hariri).
PU-AMI will also be supporting the 5 PHCCs (funded under other grants) with a comprehensive packages such as subsidized consultations and 100% coverage for Laboratory and diagnostic tests, provision of free acute medication except for LHF PHCCs( Binet Hoda PHCC) but PU-AMI will be donating medications under another fund as well medical supplies, HR support if needed through direct support or Performance based financing.
The PHCCs were selected based on MOPH recommendations and UNHCR I PU-AMI conducted PHCC assessments based on different criteria such as Vulnerability, accessibility, affordability, Presence of HR, Phenics and as well PHCCs within the network recommended by MOPH initially and coordinated by UNHCR.
Prior the assessment, PUI Health team based their prioritization on different elements such as the vulnerability localities through 251 Cadaster Mapping as well location of those PHCCs surroundings Beirut blast.
PU-AMI will continue the coordination with MOPH PHCC department and as well build the capacity of the PHCCs to register PPEs stock and consumption on Phenics.
