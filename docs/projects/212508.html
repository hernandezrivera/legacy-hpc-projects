Project Summary

In response to the most urgent needs in the most deprived areas of Syria, several cities and towns in Idlib Governorate will be targeted with WASH assistance in light maintenance and rehabilitation of water and sanitation supplies and solid waste collection and disposal.
The targeted governorates suffer, by their nature, from a severe weakness in providing all basic services related to water, sanitation, and solid waste management. As the crisis continues, asset depletion has hampered local authorities from responding to the immediate and high humanitarian needs in this region.
The proposed project's WASH interventions seek to improve the humanitarian situation of war-affected populations by restoring water and sanitation systems that will be able to respond to increasing demand in communities overburdened by the ongoing arrival of tens of thousands of people forcibly displaced by the war. This action will be integrated with emergency water, sanitation and hygiene response activities for affected populations in Idlib while 61,031 people, elderly and young (women, men, and people with special needs) will directly benefit.
Through this project, Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation will produce the following outputs:
Six water stations will be rehabilitated and operated in Idlib Governorate in the targeted locations, where AHF will rehabilitate the WS and Making it able to serve the targeted beneficiaries.

Additionally, the project team will conduct regular visits to the beneficiaries to provide support and monitor the progress of their projects.
