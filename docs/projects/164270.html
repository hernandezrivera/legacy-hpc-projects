Project Summary

The project will include integrated nutrition, child protection, health, and hygiene promoting services focused on life saving nutritional needs of children U5 and PLWs for both IDPs and Host communities in targeted areas in Aleppo/ Daret Ezza sub -district and Idleb / Dana sub-district through establishing 8 Rapid Response Teams (RRTs)which providing the following services:
1- MUAC screening for children (Boys and Girls) under the age of 5 and for pregnant and lactating women in Dair Hassan/ Idleb and Daret Ezza/Aleppo camps.
2- referrals of malnourished cases, Children U5 with dangers signs and Medical problems, PLWs with Medical problems, disabled beneficiaries, Identified visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs to the respective treatment centers.
3- IYCF-E with recommended feeding practices awareness session, Hand Washing awareness session, in addition to one-to-one IYCF-E counselling, nutrition communication and social mobilization messages.
4- Distribute micro-nutrients/nutrition supplements for preventing anemia and malnutrition among for children (Boys and Girls) under the age of 5 and for pregnant and lactating women in Dana/Idleb and Daret Ezza/Aleppo camps.
Given to the current huge IDPs influx to Dana sub district (Camps area) SRD is going to establish additional 5 RRTs as of March 2020.

4 - Needs

Continued hostilities in the north-west and north-east with large scale displacement has resulted in populations experiencing poor nutrition conditions in 2019. around 1,179,869 pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and 3,443,752 children under 59 months of age need life-saving nutrition services.By Jan 2020, the ongoing military operation escalated significantly as the government forces of the Syrian army advanced towards Idleb. Further escalation of military operations and hostilities in northwest Syria displaced more than 90k people, uprooting lives and further concentrating the need for humanitarian support to both IDPs and host communities in ever-shrinking areas of northwest Idleb within districts borderline Turkey and northern Aleppo. Based on the population assessment done by HNAP assessment in NW Syria on 1st Mar 2020 since 1 Dec 2019, the New Displaced Persons’ number reached to 961,286 out of them 226,098 displaced to Dana sub district and 4,265 to Daret Azza with 21% living in Camps and 14% individual tents and 18% in unfinished buildings, 73% of the total new IDPs need food.Based on SMART Survey 2019 results, almost 500,000 children suffering from stunting of physical and cognitive development as a result of poor infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices; lack of psychosocial stimulation, repeated illnesses; and low birth weight. Additionally it was revealed that Malnourished children with complications have increased two fold in some sub-districts. On the second hand, almost one in three pregnant women are anemic while maternal malnutrition hasincreased five-fold compared to last year. Based on these facts, it became proven the necessity to apply immediate, comprehensive, and multi-sectoral services to fully address the nutritional needs of pregnant and lactating women and children in Syria.RRT approach was developed to ensure securing of immediate, multi sectorial (health,nutrition, WASH and and Protection) and full coverage intervention

5 - Implementation of activities

SRD will provide integrated essential nutrition services for children under 5 & all pregnant and lactating women through the respective basic field services. The RRT will apply CMAM and IYCF-E, protocols for managing the respective groups of population. It is also expecting that pregnant women who are malnourished will received life-saving kit (personal hygiene items, high energy preventive nutritious food..etc) enough for at least 5 days until the final settling and referral. All children under 5 will be specifically screened for malnutrition, if SAM condition is revealed than a treatment is assigned, however, if SAM child has medical complications, he/she to be referred to hospital treatment immediately. All caregivers of children under five will receive both one-to-one IYCF-E counselling sessions during the medical examination and nutrition communication and social mobilisation messages during campaigns or group sessions.RRT will provide MUAC screening for children under 5 and for pregnant and lactating women in Dana/ Idleb and Aleppo camps. The RRT will apply CMAM protocols for managing the respective groups of population. It is also expecting that pregnant women who are malnourished will received the same services. If SAM condition is revealed then a treatment will be assigned, however, if SAM child has medical complications, he/she to be referred to treatment hospital immediately. The project will include secure referrals of malnourished cases, Children U5 with dangers signs and Medical problems, PLWs with Medical problems, disabled beneficiaries, Identified visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs to the respective treatment centers. The project will include also provision of IYCF-E, in addition to one-to-one IYCF-E counselling, nutrition communication and social mobilization messages, distribute micro-nutrients/nutrition supplements for preventing anemia and malnutrition among for children under 5 and for pregnant and lactating women

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The population displaced and specifically IDPs women might face challenges in accessing nutrition services due to multiple barriers on where/why to access, for that the mobilization activities will take place to share information with the community focusing on IDPs women.Referrals from and to other services might be disrupted due to the security situation. For that regular meetings are conducted with other services providers to facilitate referral pathways and project staff are regularly updates on the up-to-date service providers of the surroundings communities. Also, the nutrition staff are trained on the mechanism referral system.Overlapping with other nutrition services might occur, for that coordination with cluster in addition regular communication with the community members and service providers will reduce the risk of duplication. Security situation and continuous air strikes can hinder the work of RRTs. The RRTs’ team trained on emergency response to respond rapidly to new displacements.Disabilities might face challenges in accessing to nutrition services. Designated car will be provided to facilitate the access.Confidentiality/privacy of mobile nutrition services might not always be guaranteed in certain locations and this might not be a conducive environment for PLW to share their concerns around nutritional issues. In consultation with female community members ensure mobile tents or any other safe space that will mitigate the risks of violating confidentiality and privacy. In Syria context PLW may have critical concerns to share nutritional and IYCF issues with male staff, for that female staff will be delegated to deal with PLW’s nutritional issues.SRD has clear MEAL plan implemented in all its project to monitor the activities implementation and achieving objectives along with beneficiaries feedback, complaints and satisfaction.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Security situation such as airstrikes, shelling, explosions, gunshots, theft, kidnapping can affect the beneficiaries and RRTs’ workers in addition to hinder the work of RRTs, for that RRTs will be trained to better response in case of incidents. Feeding of women and girls and GBV survivors in addition to equitable access to nutritional services and supplies might not always be guaranteed in certain locations because of social and physical limitations, for that the RRTs will target vulnerable groups including PLWs and children under five, and ensure to have private spaces for breastfeeding mothers and help decrease the risk of harassment or violence against female participants, in addition to train all IYCF counselors on how to engage with GBV survivors and provide information in an ethical, safe and confidential manner as well as ensuring that the referral mechanisms to specialized GBV support, health care, MHPSS, and child protection are in place. The program also will make sure that the locations, times and methods of nutrition services are safe and accessible for women and other at-risk groups in addition to provide nutritional support to GBV survivors.Additionally, Children receiving nutrition services are over-exposed to the risk of harm by nutrition staff in addition to volunteers, visitors, for that SRD has Child Safeguarding polices and systems in place cover all people who may interact with children. RRTs will be trained on child safeguarding, and orientated on signs of child abuse.RRTs members may use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving. For that SRD has Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies in place which includes safe and effective and response reporting systems in addition to survivor centered approach that ensures the safety and confidentiality of the affected person.RRTs’ staff may not be able to make informed decisions. SRD has clear Duty of Care policies for staff
