Project Summary

this project aims to provide all inclusive WASH services to the rohingya population including the local communities in camp 27 and camp 24, this includes sanitation, desludging, hygiene promotion , solid waste management, water provision etc.

Objectives

1. To ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities to sufficient and safe water for drinking and domestic needs. 2. To ensure all refugees living in camps and affected Host Communities have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation (including household waste) facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times.3. To ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviours through access and exposure to innovative hygiene promotion (including hygiene items) for all refugees living in camps and affected host communities.

Needs

Need of water provision, collection of waste, management of solid waste management site. Repair and maintenance of WASH infrastructure. Ensure access for all, filling the gaps relating to latrines and bathing cubicles. Improvement of domestic and personal hygiene through hygiene.

Activities or outputs

M&O of all WASH infrastructure in allocated area. Where needed improve access to WASH infrastructure by installing lights, upgrade latrines and bathing cubicles and filling the gaps by means of construction of facilities. Construction of handwash facilities. Provision of drinking water, M&O of water supply systems. Upgrading fecal sludge management, only mechanical emptying, construction of infrastruction for this at camp level. . Hygiene promotion activities focusing on issues identified, and working to a solution by involving the beneficiaries in maintaining and operating the facilities. Operation of solid waste management site. Collection and separation of waste. Distribution of Menstrual hygiene packages, as well as hygiene kits.

Key project/performance indicators

Proportion of target population having access to potable water (accessable for all, gender)Proportion of target population with access to adequate latrines and bathing facilities (safety, privacy, gender, access for all)proportion of target population maintaining and covered with appropriate (recycling, segregated collection) solid waste management. proportion of target population having knowledge about and practise domestic and personal hygiene.
