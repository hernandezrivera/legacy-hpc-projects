Project Summary

The project aims to improve the academic performance for 1000-targeted vulnerable children students (Injuries, Children with disabilities and children in labor) from the Gaza Strip through providing them with necessary remedial classes by well-designed programs and high trained teachers in addition to facilitate for recreational activities. The project extends to 10 months and intended to be implemented in coordination with known, registered and reputable educational centers.

Needs

Frequent wars, continuous conflict, siege & closures causing death & destruction left the high densely populated the GS in a state of severe vulnerability. The current isolation has taken humanitarian situation to an unprecedented level, with exhausted coping mechanisms, widespread absolute poverty & an inability of civil society organizations & formal authorities to meet even the basic needs of the population. The collapse of the economy has left little money to buy food & little food in the local market to be bought; that make the education less priority for the families, which led that families to pay less attention to follow up the school achievement for their children. World bank, 2017 The proportion of children amounting to 42.6% of the total population (PCBS,2017), which indicates a majority of children in Palestinian society & these results show that Palestinian society is a young society. According to IRW study, 2017 indicated that 42.3% of children in the Gaza Strip live in poverty, of which 23.9% live in extreme poverty because of the current political & economic situation which Gaza Strip experiencing. Additionally, men typically head the household & are the primary breadwinners. Still, the percentage of female headed households in Palestine is 9.4%, 16.3% out of them are in the Gaza Strip. Widows often find themselves falling victim to cultural discrimination & economic & social marginalization. It is exceptionally difficult, in the Gaza Strip, for a woman to live alone; therefore, widows are forced either to return to their own family home or to re-marry. Both alternatives present difficulties as a woman tries to recover from the psycho-social distress of offense, rebuild her life & her children’s lives. According to the recent statistics, more than 6.4% widows in Palestine need immediate support especially under difficult conditions that they suffer. PCBS, 2017In other words, Gaza environment where children & female headed households live are suffering from variant problems that left dire impact upon them. Therefore, services should be provided for them in order to promote bright & prosperous future.Since 30 March 2018, the GS has witnessed an enormous increase in Palestinian casualties in the context of mass demonstrations taking place along Israel’s perimeter fence with Gaza. The demonstrations have occurred as part of the ‘Great March of Return’, a series of mass protests. The large number of casualties among Palestinian demonstrators, including a high percentage of demonstrators hit by live ammunition, has raised concerns about excessive use of force by Israeli troops. A # of 1191 of children has been injured during the demonstration. OCHA, June 2018 Humanitarian snapshot According to regular meetings with education cluster members in Gaza shown that the remedial classes for children in Gaza strip will be the most needed for children after the most horrible offensive on Gaza, According to Education for All 2017 National Review report shown that, overcrowded schools, limited hours of instruction and poorly trained teachers, learning outcomes and completion rates for students keep dropping, and the student performance in Gaza is falling behind. Based on HNO sheet shared by Education Cluster under Cluster objective # 2, strategic objective # 3, Activity: Provision of remedial education to vulnerable school aged children. Indicator: # of students benefiting from remedial education services. the total number of children in need for catch up classes is 24,389 (Gaza Strip), 12,121 (49.6) out of the total number are females & 12,268 (50.4) are males. The remedial classes provide good opportunity for children students (injury, children in labor, CwDs and children at risk of dropout) to improve their levels & to ensure delivering adequate learning for them.

Activities

- Conduct inclusive training program 50hrs for 80 (60 females) graduates/teachers as 20/each group on key topics linked to vulnerable school aged children (injury, children in labor, CwDs and children at risk of dropout) teaching approaches and methodologies. The training will be braked into two groups as the following:1. Interpersonal Skills (Soft skills): 20 training hours on (leadership, time management, communication skills, debate, arts in dealing with human patterns) this will enable the teachers acting properly with the students as they from various mentalities and environments.2. Specialized training: 30 training hours participants will be introduced on new techniques in training methodologies such: informal education, education by doing, recreational education) as the targeted vulnerable students won’t show high commitment when it comes to traditional education due to the living conditions for them.The previous training program is a crucial response to female graduates needs and based on previous learning and experience with such category as teachers expressed difficulties in which how to increase the level of commitment and interest for the vulnerable students through responsive methodologies. Provide children students with remedial classes in different subjects according to their real needs in order to raise their level of academic achievement. This will be implemented in cooperation with local organizations and educational centers (not profit) in order to build the capacities of CBOs that give remedial classes to poor school achievers as each student will benefit from 2 subjects according to his/her needs for 6 months over the year. This component will target 1000 students (70% females); SWG will make best coordination with supervision department at MoEHE in order to ensure the validity of targeted students in addition to make sure of the appropriateness of proposed selected CBOs in order to provide the remedial classes. - Educational tool kits and books for students, to reduce the financial burden on students and their families, SWG will support students with assistance which will distribute school bags and stationery for the 1000-targeted students (70% females). A comprehensive monitoring & evaluation practices will be applied during and after the implementation of the project’s activities to ensure smoothness implementation of goals as follows: Focus group discussions (FDGs) with children students and project stakeholder to be conducted on a monthly basis;- Bi-weekly meetings with project staff, teachers and service providers to ensure appropriateness of the type of intervention to the beneficiaries needs and priorities, field visits and reporting with facilitators, centers coordinators, trainers and experts Management information Online system will gather, measure, update and evaluate the project activities and beneficiaries in each center, and will provide a detailed statistic about each child and his progress. Inception, interim and end (technical and financial reports).
