Project Summary

Nkafamiya Rescue Mission (NRM) through this funding will address critical water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) needs of Guyuk and Shelleng LGAs, Adamawa state and Shani in Borno state. According to the Multi-Sector Needs Assessment (MSNA) between 2021 and 2022, the BAY states experienced an increased number from 3M to 5M people in need of WASH humanitarian assistance, across internally displaced persons (+29%), returnees (+ 113%) and hosting communities (+95%). In 2022, access to safe water, dignified sanitation and hygiene awareness was impacted by heavy flooding, camp closures, and the movement of IDPs and returnees. This has led to negative coping mechanisms including drinking water from unsafe sources, increased open defecation and poor personal and household-level hygiene, resulting namely in an unprecedented cholera outbreak. Further displacement due to conflict has led to poor access to safe water, and congestion in camps has resulted in limited access to safe sanitation with partners resorting to manual dislodging facing heavy flooding, moreover with some challenging access to the dumping sites. Hygiene awareness and personal hygiene at the household level have been impacted by the high cost of living which has resulted in a lack of soap at the household level, leaving vulnerable populations, especially the elderly and children under 5 at high risk of diseases.
In line with the WASH sector strategy, NRM will integrate multi-sectoral approach in the implementation of this project through risk mitigation and case area targeted interventions, such as before the rainy season in the flood-prone areas and cholera hotspots.
NRM will target the following activities throughout the project cycle:
a. Provision of clean water at health and nutrition centers to prevent shortage. NRM will ensure constant monitoring and chlorination of the water sources.
b. Rehabilitation and construction of gender segregated toilets/latrines at public places especially health and nutrition facilities ensuring that people with special needs are considered in the design of the facilities.
c. Vector control campaigns towards the beginning of rainy seasons ensuring that preparedness plans are put in place.
d. Airing of hygiene promotion messages on radio with the support of Translators without borders with translation services.
e. Printing of hygiene promotion messages on fliers, posters, banners in local languages and during campaigns and celebrations.
f. Distribution of WASH NFIs to households with SAM and or cholera children across project locations.
g. Provision of menstrual hygiene items to women and girls
h. Activate and lead in LGA coordination to ensure that monthly coordination meeting takes place in the LGAs of implementation.
i. Build and strengthen the capacities of WASH community volunteers and WASH committees
j. Conduct quarterly GBV safety audit to ensure that water points are safe for all genders at all the time.
k. Strengthen the capacity of staff through trainings for preparedness against emergency.
l. Support the WASH sector common pipeline with soaps, kettles, tooth pastes, dislodgement of latrines/toilets at community and IDPs.
The project will target IDPs in host communities, host communities and returnees across 3 LGAs (Guyuk & Shelleng in Adamawa and Shani in Borno). The project will target 6000 individuals (20% boys, 30% girls, 35% women and 15% men) across all locations for a period of 11 months.
The total amount requested for this project is Two Hundred and Twenty One Thousand, Nine Hundred and Ninety Four US Dollars ($221,994).

2 - Activities

NRM will target the following activities throughout the project cycle:a. Provision of clean water at health and nutrition centers to prevent shortage. NRM will ensure constant monitoring and chlorination of the water sources. This activity was chosen because most health and nutrition centers lack access to clean water and hand washing facilities. By regular washing of hands, it prevents diseases.b. Rehabilitation and construction of gender segregated toilets/latrines at public places especially health and nutrition facilities ensuring that people with special needs are considered in the design of the facilities. This will help in improving sanitation both at the community and household level ensuring an open defecation free community. People with special needs will be put into consideration where ramp will be constructed at each facility for easy access.c. Vector control campaigns towards the beginning of rainy seasons ensuring that preparedness plans are put in place. This activity is aimed at breaking the transmission cycle of communicable diseases (especially cholera) which will improve food intake and health of children especially those under 5, significantly contributing to health and nutrition objectives.d. Airing of hygiene promotion messages on radio with the support of Translators without borders with translation services. e. Printing of hygiene promotion messages on fliers, posters, banners in local languages and during campaigns and celebrations.f. Distribution of WASH NFIs to households with SAM and or cholera children across project locations.g. Provision of menstrual hygiene items to women and girls including capacity building on production of locally made menstrual hygiene items.h. Activate and lead in LGA coordination to ensure that monthly coordination meeting takes place in the LGAs of implementation. The purpose of the coordination meeting is to help partners present in streamlining their activities and to know where there gaps and advocate for more resources. It will also help the local community to take ownership of the project if they are involved in chairing the coordination and have their capacities strengthened.i. Build and strengthen the capacities of WASH community volunteers and WASH committees for ownership and sustainability purposes.j. Conduct quarterly GBV safety audit to ensure that water points are safe for all genders at all the time.k. Strengthen the capacity of staff through trainings for preparedness against emergency.l. Support the WASH sector common pipeline with soaps, kettles, tooth pastes, dislodgement of latrines/toilets at community and IDPs.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Nkafamiya Rescue Mission will continue to mainstream protection and mitigate gender-based violence (GBV) to ensure that access to WASH services is framed after systematic consultations with women and girls from accountability for affected population (AAP) perspective at the top level beyond the feedback mechanisms, in particular women and children can do so in a safe manner. NRM will ensure that it has put in place an effective feedback mechanism in all project locations and also liaise with the WASH sector through available guidelines to continue to engage communities on issues of access, reliability and safety in the design and implementation of WASH facilities. NRM will work closely with the AAP working group to roll out various feedback mechanisms across the project locations while encouraging real-time feedback to beneficiaries. Further, any feedback received from CCCM, and other sectors will be addressed with relevant partners in different locations.
