Project Summary

RI aims to provide prevention and response GBV services as well as empowerment activities through both a standalone as well as integrated approach that promotes access to health services provided by RI in the targeted area. RI will target the most vulnerable women and girls including: people with disability , widowed women living in widows camps , women and girls headed household , adolescent girls and older women among others. RI will continue to support and establish the functioning 2 WGSS plus 5 MPUs in the following subdistricts: Kisreh and Basira in DeZ and Hassakeh Center, Twina and Al Hol in Hassakeh. In addition, following the integrated approach with its health services, RI will deploy GBV teams in two RI health facilities to provide prevention and response services for women and girls visiting the health facilities in Basira and Kisreh PHC’s. RI is working on strengthening the referral for women and girls at risk between RI health and GBV services in the targeted area either by establishing a WGSS near to RI health facility or through deploying GBV team in the health facility to provide prevention and response services In line with the GBV Minimum Standards in Emergencies, the services provided within RI WGSS under the present proposal are case management following the GBV case management SOPs developed by GBV SC, community based and focus PSS services including psychoeducation sessions , psychological first aid , individual and group counselling through trained and supervised PSS team as well as awareness raising and empowerment activities including skills development and life skills. Moreover, in order to strengthen the community approach RI will build on community protective mechanisms already in place. In addition RI will conduct safety audits to services and facilities, risk mapping, contextualize participatory GBV key messages, integrated awareness raising and psychoeducation sessions for men and boys as well as psychosocial workshops to address behavioral problems such as anger, violence to enhance their engagement in the GBV program. RI will provide integration approach within its supported health facilities by providing awareness raising and information sessions for women , men girls and boys on GBV topics in addition to providing focus on strengthening the referral pathway for GBV survivors and women and girls visiting RI health facilities , in addition to provide case management through social assistants based at PHC’s. All activities will be implemented while adhering to strict COVID 19 prevention measures such as reduced numbers of participants in group activities, social distancing, and provision of PPEs for both staff and participants at the centers activities. Additionally, Hotline services will be used to provide online information sharing, Psychological first aid , individual counselling and case management for women and girls who don’t have access to the center. Moreover, RI is currently conducting a research project on Mental Health with a focus on Trauma and people living in conflict affected areas, to identify recommendations and best practices for MH programming.
Within the overall services available at RI WGSS, RI will also integrate Emergency Cash in Case Management, and survivors and women and girls at risk will be able when relevant and integrated in the Case Action Plan to benefit from cash Support to meet and respond to emerging needs. Cash support will be available to all survivors and women and girls at risk in all the static services or upon referral from social workers and assistants in mobile units or health facilities, as well as from external referrals.

5 - Implementation of activities

RI will continue to promote a gender sensitive and inclusive approach throughout the program cycle including in the MEAL mechanisms setup. RI data is collected and analyzed under an AGD approach (Age, Gender and diversity) as well as participation of hard to reach groups is fostered in consultation processes by ensuring groups assessed are representative of social dynamics to promote a better understanding on needs, risks, gaps and barriers. Moreover, through the establishment of a risk matrix MEAL can monitored the impact of adopted mitigation measures and how barriers are addressed and access is being enhanced. Among the key measures adopted by RI in the program cycles one of the key access barriers for persons with disabilities was transportation and mobility, for that purpose RI has adapted the vehicles supporting the WGSS activities in order to suit the mobility needs of women and girls with disabilities, moreover RI has adapted the center facilities to ensure dignified access to women and girls with disabilities as well as provided a training on Disability & Inclusion to all the frontline service providers. During the next program cycle RI will continue to enhance the inclusiveness of services and gradually address identified barriers, for example the project will target among over all beneficiaries at least 506 Persons with disabilities.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

The project aims to provide life skills and skills development to women and adolescent girls in addition to linking them when available to vocational training and livelihood opportunities. However Livelihood opportunities may increase risks of exploitation by service provider or by other actor in the community and RI will work on mitigation measures through the following : 1. Consult women and men, boys and girls in the design of the programme. 2. Ensure participants know services where to disclose if violence occurs 3. Raise awareness on PSEA and other types of exploitation and where to report 4. Establish good relation with local authorities, clarify humanitarian principles and create internal and external monitoring mechanisms to measure compliance to them .5.ensure the compliant and feedback mechanisms accessible for all women and girls 6. Mainstream PSEA across key program activities and ensure awareness on SEA is a key component of all the services provided at the WGSS and in MPU’s

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The WGSSs and MPUs are located in relatively safe areas. Risk: Armed Clashes, arrest and detention, shelling/artillery, VBIEDs/IEDs. mitigation: staff to contact the security focal point and alert all staff to be prepared for immediate evacuation/hibernation if possible. Activate an emergency plan, previously designed and explained to all staff members. Being neutral and Avoid political action Suspend/Hibernate and/or relocate as per the severity of the risk and based on a contingency plan and in line with “Protection Cluster Guidance: Protection Principles and Minimum Standards for Emergency Relocation Support in Northwest Syria, May 2020”. Staff to Stay away from convoys of military exercises during the movement Following the steps for ethical closure of the program and ensure continuous updating of the plan to close the program as needed.
