Project Summary

Medair will continue to implement multi sectoral interventions in its proposed catchment areas of Leer, Pibor and multistate under the Emergency Response Team (ERT). Medair will provide nutrition services that are integrated with health, WASH, MHPSS in all its static and emergency response project sites. The total targets for 2025 based on Medair’s mapped catchment populations with at least 60% coverage in all areas will be 3503 SAM admissions (Pibor 385, Leer 1714 and ERT 1404), 8,258 MAM Under 5 admissions (Leer 4,664 and ERT 3,594) and 4,580 PLWs (Leer 3,633 and ERT 947). The planned community screening will be 44,914 under-fives screened (Pibor 11,046 Leer 21,556 and ERT 12,312) and 22,325 PLWs screened (Pibor 4,940, Leer 12,201, ERT 5,184), while 24,148 primary caregivers of children aged 0-23 months will be reached with MIYCN messaging (Pibor 7,765, Leer 12,236 and ERT 4,147). Medair will continue to integrate MIYCN and IYCF at both community and facility levels and support VASD in Medair’s catchment areas when no other actors are conducting. The proposed intervention in the plan aligns with the nutrition cluster strategic and sector objectives 2025 and contribute to increase equitable access and utilization of quality preventative, lifesaving, early detection and treatment of malnutrition and program monitoring, analysis and utilization of nutrition information.
Leer
The recent SMART Survey (July 2024) reported a GAM rate of 17.2 percent (SAM 4.3 percent) among children 6-59 months in Leer County with aggravating factors such as poor maternal, infant, and young child feeding nutrition (MIYCN), poor water supply and sanitation infrastructure, poor health behaviors, food insecurity and high rates of disease (morbidities). According to the SMART survey report, 15.2 percent of the PBW were found to be at risk of acute malnutrition as their Mid Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) measurements were less than 23 cm. In Leer, Medair provides multi-sectoral services such as health, WASH, MHPSS and integrated nutrition services. Medair is the lead nutrition partner and currently provides nutrition services in 18 nutrition sites and 1 Stabilization Centre (SC). In 2025, Medair proposes to continue providing quality lifesaving nutrition services in up to the 18 nutrition sites and SC. Medair will also support a SMART survey to measure GAM rates this year.
Pibor
The recently conducted SMART Survey 2024 in Pibor revealed a GAM rate of 25.4 percent (SAM of 7.5 percent) among children 6-59 months. Over half (52.1 percent) of the PBW were found to be at risk of acute malnutrition as their MUAC measurements were less than 23 cm . The report also indicated high levels of morbidity and mortality in children U5, low vaccination coverage, poor MIYCN and health seeking behaviors. In Pibor, Medair provides multi-sectoral services such as health, WASH, MHPSS and integrated nutrition services. Medair is running the SC for SAM patients with medical complications and refers discharged children to another nutrition partner, ForAfrika. In 2025, Medair proposes to continue providing quality lifesaving nutrition services in the SC and continue strengthening referral system of discharged patients to continue OTP treatment.
ERT will continue to respond to emergency levels of acute malnutrition by establishing CMAM services to address the most lifesaving needs, in areas with high GAM and low program coverage. The RRTF in collaboration with the NC gives input on priority areas and intervention modalities in scenarios characterized by agreed triggers where other actors are absent or unable to fully intervene. The team will work with the partner on the ground to identify areas for capacity building for quality service delivery. The team will provide nutrition emergency services usually for up to 3 months and thereafter handover services to an identified partner before exiting.

1. Needs

According to the IPC 2024 report, in 49 counties the acute malnutrition rates exceeded the emergency threshold of 15%. Additionally, the report indicated that the nutrition situation has deteriorated with an estimated 2.1 million children aged 6-59 months and about 1.1 million PBW suffering or expected to suffer elevated levels of acute malnutrition between July 2024 and June 2025. An estimated 67% of the acute malnutrition burden is concentrated in the five states of Jonglei, Northern Bahr el Ghazal, Upper Nile, Unity and Warrap. Medair’s static intervention sites are in two of these states, that is Greater Jonglei and Unity State. Furthermore, these sites are in counties (Pibor and Leer) which have been classified as priority 1 in OCHA’s intersectoral priority listing and high priority areas by the nutrition cluster with emergency levels of GAM. The populations of Leer and Pibor have both experienced multiple emergency humanitarian situations due to floods and intercommunal violence for the past few years with significant impact on health and nutrition services.LeerThe SMART Survey (July 2024) reported a GAM rate of 17.2% (SAM 4.3%) among children 6-59 months in Leer County with aggravating factors such as poor maternal, infant, and young child feeding nutrition (MIYCN), poor water supply and sanitation infrastructure, poor health behaviors, food insecurity and high rates of morbidities. The SMART survey report also indicated 15.2% of the PBW were found to be at risk of acute malnutrition as their MUAC measurements were <23cm. 2. Project activities

Infant Young Child Feeding and Behaviour ChangeMIYCN messaging will be integrated into the SC, OTP and TSFP services to provide sufficient opportunities for knowledge acquisition and to promote and support positive behavior change among PLWs and mothers/caregivers of children 0-23 months. Medair will repeatedly promote key messages related to breastfeeding and complementary feeding in MIYCN-E through health and nutrition staff. Child friendly spaces will be used as an opportunity to support mothers, particularly in the violence-affected areas of Leer, and Pibor.Given the poor MIYCN practices (low rates of exclusive breastfeeding and early initiation of breastfeeding) among infants 0-6 months, Medair proposes to continue implementing the MAMI program in Pibor County in 3 sites (Pibor County Hospital, Gumuruk PHCU and Lekuangole PHCU) and in Dindin, a Medair supported health facility in Leer. This is on a small scale to ensure that these infants and their mothers receive integrated maternal and child health services at each point of contact including EPI, GMP, OPD, ANC, delivery, PNC and TSFP PLW. At the community level, MIYCN messaging will be conducted through the existing community network (CNVs/ICCM) and care groups. Medair will use Care groups to disseminate the MIYCN messages by educating mothers and caregivers of children under five years on optimal complementary feeding practices through food demonstrations and kitchen gardens. The Care groups also disseminate key messages on health, WASH and PSS ensuring a multi-sectoral approach at community level.Capacity building on MIYCN for facility and community staff will be done through trainings, supportive supervision, mentoring and on-the-job training. Management of Acute MalnutritionMedair will treat children under 5 years for SAM and MAM and PLWs with acute malnutrition, through the provision of CMAM that contributes to improved nutrition status and reduction of morbidity and mortality among these groups. SC, OTP and TSFP will be integrated within existing health care services, WASH and PSS, ensuring a comprehensive approach to addressing poor nutrition outcomes in communities. Nutrition coverage will be strengthened through community mobilization by CNVs and iCCM CBDs. Care Groups will continue to reinforce the training of mothers to measure MUACs of their own children under five and seek care as directed. Passive case finding will be done within the health facilities in OPD, EPI, and ANC/PNC clinics. The “Family MUAC” initiative will also increase caregiver knowledge and skills to monitor their children’s nutrition status and promote early health seeking.Capacity building on CMAM for facility and community staff will be done through trainings, supportive supervision, mentoring and on-the-job training. Medair will monitor nutrition activities through the Sphere standard performance indicators and all nutrition data will be analyzed at least monthly, and trends and quality performance will be monitored.LeerMedair, as the lead nutrition partner of the Leer County will run up to 19 nutrition sites implementing SC, OTP and TSFP for children under 5 and PLWs located in the neediest areas of the county. PiborMedair will continue to run the SC located in the Pibor County Hospital and covers a large area serving the counties of Gumuruk, Lekuangole, Verteth, and Pibor. Medair will work closely with ForAfrika, the lead nutrition partner in Pibor for a functional referral mechanism of complicated SAM through active case finding of SAM cases from community and OTP and TSFP under the umbrella of sub-cluster and CHD. The Emergency Response Team (ERT) will aim to provide the full CMAM package in at least 4 proposed interventions as needed. The actual response will depend on the needs and gaps identified in the Needs Assessment. Emergency nutrition interventions will be rolled out in the following cluster rapid response phased model depending on security and accessibility.
