1 - Needs

Since 04th of April 2019 in South Tripoli, more than 8,385 families (approximately 41,925 individuals) have been displaced from their homes, due to armed conflict, this only in the second quarter of the 2019. Due to the prolonged stay of these IDPs most of the WASH services are in dire need of rehabilitation. Both quantity and quality of water has been observed not fit for drinking. In terms of sanitation facilities, these are either very old or overburdened due to the high number of people relying on these facilities. Likewise, key hygiene items in particular soap is lacking in the localities either due to lack of purchasing power or appropriate hygiene awareness. Therefore, urgent emergency action is required to improve the water and sanitation facilities to prevent disease and diarrhea outbreak.Since the start of new clashes, surely, There gaps is been in migrant’s detention, IDPs collective centres and some schools. Since the start of new clashes in April, UNICEF is closely monitoring the situation and have responded in some of the locations through RRM. Additional requirement of activities will be undertaken through this project agreement.Moreover, During April 2019, site visits were undertaken by LS team to some of families returning to Tawergha city. The objective of the visit was to assess and identify the needs of the returning home of Tawergha people, and schoolchildren regarding access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities. The team identified gaps in many things, such as cleaning campaign, fumigating campaign, a school needs include provision of new water pump, Repair of water pump, and pipes. As well as electrical cable, PVC tank for potable water with Poly Propylene (PPR) pipes, also needs maintenance of toilets, windows, doors, installation of lighting, garbage collection and garbage boxes.Most vulnerable people affected by protracted crisis are needed with improved access to water, sanitation and hygiene services. Most Vulnerable children and families are needed with essential hygiene items and information
