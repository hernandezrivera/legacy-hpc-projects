Project Summary

RMF has a stong presence in Pibor and Ayod counties of Jongolei state implementing an integrated nutrition program in areas.
The conflict-related displacement of over 200,000 people from northern, central, and eastern former Jonglei has severely disrupted livelihoods and access to social services, thus severely undermining food security in the State. The situation was exacerbated further by last year’s poor harvests as well as the economic crisis that has eroded households’ purchasing power.
For the past two months the security situation has worsened in and around Boma areas due to increased increased inter tribal clashes between the Murle and Jie tribes leading to mass displacements and restricted movement.
Displacement in the area led to disruption of nutrition and health services which made it impossible to implement a coordinated and integrated programming in the areas.
Furthermore with the onset of the raining seasons the population is always expected to be highly vulnerable to malaria and other related illnesses which could in turn worsen the nutrition situation for under five children as well as PLWs.
A recent SMART survey conducted by IMC had Pibor GAM rates at 20.7% and SAM rates of 5% well above the W.H.O recommended threshold .This has placed Pibor County at IPC phase 4 classifications and at risk of humanitarian catastrophe.
RMF will work in close collaboration with WASH,health and FSL partners in order to implement minimum package currently recommended as an intervention package for partners working in Pibor and Ayod counties
This intervention will lead to improved management of SAM cases in both under 5 and PLWs .Over all 4490 children 6-59 months are expected to be treated for SAM while while 6313 children 6-59 months are expected to treated for MAM.7616 PLWs will be treated for MAM.The intervention is also expected to champion preventive aspect of the nutrition program through an elaborate MYCN activities .Our presence on the ground will allow us to work together with other stake holders to continue to monitor, analyze and share the nutrition situation on a regular basis.

Needs

The humanitarian situation in South Sudan remains critical, with an estimated 7 million people in need of humanitarian assistance in the second half of 2018. The situation is compounded by ongoing violence, displacement, disease, seasonal floods, and economic decline, continuing to erode communities’ capacity to mitigate risks to life, livelihood, and well being. Some 4.6 million people have been uprooted since the crisis began in 2013, including around 2 million displaced internally. Due to armed conflict, over 200,000 people from northern, central, and eastern Jonglei State have been displaced, severely disrupting communities’ livelihoods, access to social services, and food security. Disruptions of nutrition and health services have made it very difficult to implement a coordinated, integrated program in most communities of Pibor and Ayod. With the rainy season, the population is also expected to be highly vulnerable to malaria and other related illnesses, in turn worsening the nutrition situation for under-5 children and PLW. Young women who become mothers as early as age 15 are particularly vulnerable. Due to limited support and the disruption of extended family units, they do not have the knowledge and ability to meet their own nutritional needs, let alone those of their children. RMF understands the challenges that young mothers face, and seeks to boost local capacities, especially at the household level and through mother-to-mother support groups. A recent SMART survey by IMC had Pibor GAM rates at 20.7% and SAM rates at 5%; well above the WHO recommended threshold and placing Pibor County in IPC phase 4 classification. In Ayod, the last SMART survey revealed the GAM rate was 16.2% and SAM 6.3%, also above critical as classified by WHO. RMF will work in close collaboration with WASH, health, and FSL partners to implement the intervention package recommended for partners working in these areas. Also ECD activities will be implemented as part of a holistic approach that guarantees both child survival and development .

Activities

Under nutrition prevention strategies through health and nutrition education, maternal nutrition, infant and young child feeding practices, micronutrient supplementation and early detection and treatment of acute malnutrition are among the major national priority strategies used to combat causes of morbidity and mortality among children in the first 1000 days of life. The problems of under nutrition is a general burden to the health sector, economic situation of the country and as well a factor of low productivity. The national ministry of health and other line ministries such as agriculture have guidelines and policy documents in place that enforce the need to address the under nutrition problems in the country through the various avenues in different sectors. This programme seeks to support host population, IDPs and returnees in both Ayod and Pibor counties by providing lifesaving nutrition interventions, preventive health/nutrition measures closure to the community as possible. The programme will strive to address the problem of under nutrition identified in the 2 counties (Ayod and Pibor ) during the 1 year .
