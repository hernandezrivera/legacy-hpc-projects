Project Summary

Overtime READO has realized that because of the widespread and severity of GBV cases in Baidoa, it is important for actors to lay the ground for offering or addressing the post practice using a strong foundation while efforts are directed at preventive and curative measures.
READO has health facilities and referral centers in Baidoa especially where statistics show is still hugely affected by the GBV practice and women rights abuse. It also operates in most IDP settlements surrounding Baidoa town. It has developed network with medical practitioners, legal aid, religious support system, local actors, IDP leaders and local administration to establish a solid network of support to strongly approach issues dealing with GBV and blatant violation of the rights of women, men, boys and girls.
READO will develop unique approach based on each area of target of the project. For counseling services of the GBV affected victims the organization will establish the current data base of the possible victims based on the available estimates. This is because GBV cases will be reported daily and counseling services offered as well as referrals in cases that require higher level of medical attention especially GBV and mental cases or other related cases. To do this the organization will need rough estimates based on the flow of similar statistics for the past 3 months which will enable the organization to set up the required outlets for the counseling services and referrals and assigning the right number of staff for the activity within the required time period. Awareness activities shall be held monthly and during national events. During these times, the local Somali professional especially medical practitioners will be used to showcase the dangers of the harmful practices. The messages shall also be posted in popular Somali websites and local May-May language radio stations to increase the reach of the message. These events shall be carried out in coordination with the local authority, local actors and the community structures within the project areas. The events shall also be done with the support and participation of GBV working groups who might want to participate in the events to maximize impact.
Planned activities also include distribution of emergency NFIs through in-kind distribution, Distribution of dignity kits through in-kind distribution and Training of READO humanitarian staff interacting with populations on Psycho-social First Aid and principled referrals besides awareness campaigns.

Please provide further explanation

Throughout the project implementation, the community will be involved in the project activities. This is so that they can come up with homegrown solutions to such perennial problems that greatly affect them. By the end of the project, it is envisaged that each of the targeted IDPs shall have a patron group that shall consist of local business leaders, religious and local community leaders to oversee the continuity of protection services. The patrons will work on economically viable and socially friendly solutions to fund infrastructure development while providing necessary supplies for continuity of child protection programs. Community together with school management will be working on contingency plans for drought and floods for Baidoa and Diinsor in collaboration with READO and will soon be doing a mapping of services and organizations/companies that can be approached to support child protection programs like Dahabshiil banks through EVC contributions by well-wishers, corporate companies like Hormuud and others to help lure in well-wishers to support schools. On the CP activities, it is envisaged that by the end of the project cycle, all UASC will have been identified, documented and reunified with their parents/guardians/foster parents. READO is also coordinating with agencies working on CP programs in Baidoa like INTERSOS, UNICEF and SCI among others to handle any pending cases by the close of the project. These will include referrals and PSS. In addition, the trained teachers, CECs, CPCs and CtC club members will continue to provide comprehensive case management support to learners for as long as they are still serving in their different capacities. Moreover, the child to child clubs have also been sensitized on maintenance of school facilities including hand washing facilities, latrines and water points. This will ensure that users of such facilities are able to understand their importance and thus maintain them for a longer period after the project duration. In regards to the sustainability of the latrines, the size of the pit have been carefully calculated to ensure the sustainable lifespan of the vault. This will ensure the durability, the improved privacy, it is easier to dislodge, therefore a longer lifespan.
