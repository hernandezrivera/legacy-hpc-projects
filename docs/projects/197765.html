Project Summary

The project aim at improving the protection and respect of fundamental rights for persons affected by crises in the NW region of Cameroon. The project will also make provision for psychosocial support and cash/livelihood assistance to vulnerable IDPs and affected population. The activities include: Conducting protection monitoring in crisis-affected areas; strengthening the civil documentation to conflict-affected people; providing targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (including livelihood); Sensitization on peaceful coexistence between host and IDPs communities and non-discrimination of IDPs; Build capacity of monitors, social and psychosocial workers to monitor human rights violation in standardized, rigorous way for credible, compatible documentation and supports advocacy and direct action effectively; Identify protection issues, monitor events, trend and changes; Collect, gather and analyze information related to physical, material and legal safety, vulnerabilities encountered by displaced and other affected population; Inform humanitarian programing through identification of needs, gaps and trends; Generate a responds from relevant stakeholders through referral and/ or advocacy actions; Identify individual protection cases for referral to specialized agencies (internal or external referrals based on services provided in the different locations); Disseminate information on available services to the displaced and host communities; The project location is in the Northwest region (Bui, Ngo ketunjia, Donga mantung, Momo, and Boyo divisions), and South west region (Ndian Division) of Cameroon. The project targets 28000 affected people from targeted communities

1 - Besoins / Needs

As of August 2022, Cameroon is responsible for 2,023,233 persons of concern including 490,322 refugees 8,865 asylum seekers, 975,786 internally displaced persons (IDPs) and 548,206 returnees.Specifically, in the Northwest and Southwest regions (NWSW) where a violent crisis broke out in 2016, the conflict has produced some 532,290 IDPs (August 2022 statistics) as well as 417,516 returnees while exacerbated the protection environment. As a result, the protection situation is marked by systematic violence inflicted on civilians by the parts in conflict (State forces and non-State armed groups (NSAGs) and increased gender-based violence, attacks on property, illegal or arbitrary arrests, recruitment of children into armed forces and groups, attacks on life, physical integrity are among the most recurrent violations reported in the regions. From January to August 2022, 3252 protection incidents were reported through the protection monitoring while in the same period 7594 GBV incidents were recorded (31% Emotional abuse cases, 22% Denial of resources/opportunities; Physical assault 21%; Sexual assault12%; Rape 8%).The neighboring regions of NWSW in particular the West Region and the Littoral have seen an increase of IDPs presence (more than 90,000 for each region) especially in the semi urban areas where living conditions are particularly precarious and IDPs are exposed to regular abuses especially women and girls Without access to decent jobs and effective support to pursue their studies, many unemployed women and young girls who have not attended school have turned to survival sex to meet their basic needs. Many of them are victims of exploitation and physical violence. Some of them have lost their civil documents, including birth certificates and identity cards. This limits their access to services and the enjoyment of their rights.
