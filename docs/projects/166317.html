Project Summary

The protracted crisis in the Northeast of Nigeria has brought untold misery to vulnerable men, women, girls and boys who are now in dire need of protection services. A lot of efforts have been put in place to mitigate the effect of the crisis. However, the outbreak of the world health pandemic, COVID-19 has increased the vulnerabilities of IDPs in the North East, especially women and girls. In Nigeria, as of April 25th 2020, 1,182 cases have been registered with fatalities across the country. Of this number, Maiduguri has 30 positive cases with 1 death, Adamawa one case while no case has been recorded in Yobe state. This has resulted into imposition of restrictive measures by the government authorities with intention to contain the widespread of the disease. The closure of schools will increase women and girls’ roles as caregivers and exposure to COVID-19 infection as they would be responsible in caring for infested relatives. The lockdown situation has increased prices of commodities which will make things even more difficult to low income households and will eventually bring economic hardship on women with negative impact on management of menstrual Hygiene. Experiences have demonstrated that where women are primarily responsible for procuring and cooking food for the family, increasing food insecurity as a result of the crises may place them at heightened risks of intimate partner and other forms of domestic violence due to increased tensions in the household. According to GBVIMS Annual Report, 2019, 73% of reported incidents of GBV in the BAY States occurred in the context of intimate partner violence, meaning that, of the married/cohabitating survivors who reported an incident of GBV, in at least 98 per cent of the incidents, the perpetrator was their partner (former or current). With current situation of COVID-19 spreading rapidly, the number of cases keeps increasing every day, this means more stringent measures to flatten the curve of the pandemic in the region and this will increase the hardship on women and girls.
AHA shall mitigate the spread of corona virus in IDPs camps and protect vulnerable IDPs specially women and girls at risk of Covid-19 infection in the context of lockdown and quarantine in the North East of Nigeria through provision of dignity kits and covid-19 preventive materials.

1 - Needs

The declaration of corona virus by the WHO director as a world health pandemic was followed by a contextualized series of lock down in each country as a preventive measure to curb the spread of the virus. As of Nigeria, especially , the North East, the lock down has been negatively impacting low income households among IDPs and daily laborers, characterized by negative coping mechanisms among women especially single female headed households in feeding their families. In addition, the closure of schools by the FME ( federal ministry of education ) has given more burden to girls in households, in caring either for the infested or sick relative or burden them with domestic chores, thus exposing them to both infection or sexual exploitation and abuse. Furthermore, the lock dwon has been hindering the free movements of women and girls to their daily earning activities, thus impacting negatively the management of their menstrual hygiene. In addition, insufficiency of knowledge in GBV principles coupled with core preventive measures against COVID-19 place women and girls in the North East at high risk of infections and GBV (Gender-based violence incidents).

2 - Activities

- Sensitization of women, girls, boys, men on corona virus: means of prevention, transmission, signs/symptoms of infection and age groups at risk.-promotion of hygiene among women, men, girls and boys to curb Covid-19 among IDPs in camps and host communities.-distribution of core Gender-based violence protection materials to women, girls ,boys in the context of quarantine and lock down.-livelihoods opportunities to women and girls for: assembling, sourcing, procurement and distribution of dignity kits.-awareness raising among vulnerable IDPs, especially women and girls,boys on self-protective measures against covid-19.-distribution of self-protection and prevention kits against COVID-19 to IDPs in camps and host communities.-empowerment of women, girls/boys in locally produced materials for protection and prevention of COVID-19.
