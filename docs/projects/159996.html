Project Summary

In reference to the education cluster PiN 2020, the school-age population in need of support, meaning lacking completely any humanitarian assistance in education is as follows: 13,250 children in Qansax Dheere District , 14621 in Baidoa district, Ceel Barde District has got 275, Diinsoor 35049 while 10699 children in Wahjid district.
INTERSOS aims to undertake a key emergency intervention targeting 4,892 (2,448 girls, 2,444 boys) to assure access and continuity of education to crises affected children in 5 districts – Ceel Barde, Qansax Dheere, Waajid, Baidoa & Diin Soor. The action will be conducted in line with the Somalia Education Cluster framework and objectives.
In Bay region; INTERSOS anticipates supporting 4 public schools in Qansax Dheere district with a student target population of 900 (450 girls, 450 boys). Schools in the districts are Qansax Dheere Primary school 435 (236 male, 199 female), Fathu-Rahman Primary school 221 (125 male, 96 female), Tawakal Primary school 255 (125 boys, 112 girls), Al-Furqan primary school 157 (106 boys 51 girls). INTERSOS also proposes to support 1,828 (915 girls, 913 boys) children in 5 IDP schools in Baidoa District in the area of Wadajir and Horseed thus promoting complementarities with the UNHCR education funded project. Additionally, Diinsoor Districts with a total school in need population of 35,049 (cluster education cluster PiN 2020) also remains in the plans for support targeting 1000 (500 girls, 500 boys) children in 2 schools (howl wadaag and Waberi) and 3 IDP camps- Raama dhabay IDP, Saandala IDP and Tuniida IDP.
Bakool Region; INTERSOS plans to support Waajid District targeting 705 (353 girls, 352 boys) children in schools that are in a gap including 5 schools in with a total student population of 3,190 (1,304 girls); Bilal 670 (230 girls) Amir 530 (174 girls), Kaboos 650 (350 girls) Fatirow 670 (210 girls), and 670 (340 girls). These schools were previously supported by INTERSOS in the 2018 through Education Cannot Wait grant and they are now lacking any support. Ceel Barde with a total school in need population of 2757 (cluster education cluster PiN 2020); INTERSOS plans to target 459 (229 girls, 230 boys) children in the following IDP camps; Banaaney IDP and Qoraxey IDP camps.
The project envisages to provide the immediate needs of the school going children in the form of scholastic materials including learning kits, curriculum books, school furniture, and recreational materials. Children with special needs and protection concerns including 80 (40 male; 40 female) children living with disabilities will be supported through strengthening referral mechanisms and pathways to ensure their inclusivity via specialized services. The project will offer gender mainstreaming, and equity as 500 girls will be supported with dignity kits and sanitary pads.
The project will also provide emergency school feeding, water and the construction of gender segregated wash facilities to improve hygiene and sanitation in the schools. Using reflective circles approach; INTERSOS will train 160 (80 female) teachers, CECs and the students on Education in Emergency contents and life skills development as sixteen (24) TLS to be constructed and 20 classrooms rehabilitated in the 5 regions equally. The project will use the wheels of the MoE to instigate new partnerships between public schools and IDP schools for proper reintegration and transition of IDP children into the public schools. Additionally, INTERSOS will consider 200 (100 female) for accelerated learning program and transition to formal education.

Mitigation Measures

To reduce the risk of exclusion of marginalized community’s/ minority groups from accessing humanitarian services/assistance; INTERSOS will adhere to the humanitarian principle of non-discriminatory with respect to neutrality and will ensure to promote right based targeting linked to criteria-based selection to access services. INTERSOS will also give special consideration to people living with disabilities and other minority groups as they have special cases for assistance unlike others. INTERSOS will advocate for more resources by giving adequate data and information of people in need so that humanitarian and development actors can synergize for sustainable solution to the overall problem of displacement. INTERSOS will engage with all community members including the marginalized, the disable, women, men, children, boys and girls to ascertain their needs, risks, protection concerns, preference and as well understand their coping mechanisms. This information will be collected, analyzed and monitored to ensure holistic discovery of the situation hence reduce the risk of exclusion of marginalized communities to access humanitarian services. INTERSOS will also build the capacity of the marginalized/minority groups to adopt to situations in emergencies, disaster mitigation, and build their resilience portfolio. Secondly, to reduce the risk of women, girls and boys excluded from consultations and decision-making processes; INTERSOS will endeavor to support to build the individual self-protection capacity through training so as to contribute to high self-esteem among girls and women. Adolescent girls in schools will be trained on life skills development, decision making and protection to build their capacity to develop positive coping mechanism. INTERSOS will have a spectrum of meetings and community forums to have an active role of women and girls in decision making and bringing solution to their problems. This will also enhance the relevance of the project as well induces sustainability. Furthermore, INTERSOS will continuous build rapport by having an appropriate, relevant and suitable feedback and complaint mechanism in place. Besides complaint and feedback box which will be placed in convenient locations within the community to improve communication and contribute to decision making, INTERSOS will provide beneficiaries with a hotline and complaints number thus ensuring inclusion of the largest possible segment of beneficiaries. INTERSOS will commit to promote internal whistle blowing/reporting of SGBV cases and any reported case of SGBV incident will be kept with utmost confidentially and proper proceeding followed to ensure justice is prevailed to the victim. INTERSOS will form women committees will also be used to address women issues including access of girls to education. Thirdly, to address protection concerns stemming from displacement; INTERSOS will conduct community sensitization forums to allow local acceptance and reduce community hostilities during crises and displacement as communities are asked to consider fundamental human rights with respect to gender, age and diversity. Moreover, during crises, family composition changes as children and other family members are traced nowhere, in this respect, INTERSOS will employ the expertise of the protection department to allow families who don’t know the whereabouts of their loved extended assistance. INTERSOS will also build the capacity of the displaced communities to cope with impacts of the displacement as asset mapping and services referrals are maintained to ensure steady positive coping mechanisms. Staff from INTERSOS will ensure to have distribution locations that are easily reachable to all with specific attention to women and children with protection concerns receive specialized treatment and services. INTERSOS will ensure to have supplies in time so as to induce speedy access of humanitarian services to avoid further damage.

Please provide further explanation

This project proposes to support IDP's children within the Inter Agency Standing Committee (IASC) framework and principles on durable solutions for internally displaced populations, which recommends that IDP's should enjoy without discrimination, a minimum access to basic education among other rights, also as part of building their resilience in the face of the challenges they face. In this regard, this project aims at supporting IDP children not only access school, but also transit to public schools that are in the host communities to bene􀂡t from quality education within the national education system. In the search to find localized and durable solutions to the education problem, INTERSOS has for years worked on promoting the “school Cluster approach” where children from IDP schools who attained higher than Grade 3 are transitioned to a nearby public school to continue learning. The transitioned children will equally be supported with transport fare, fees, learning kits, and other educational materials to achieve uninterrupted access of learning. Most importantly, the project activities are aligned to the delivery of the national education outcomes, strategies, approaches and systems in place for the country as outlined in the National Development Plan. Explicitly, INTERSOS will promote the use of the approved curriculum books of the Federal Government of Somalia. This will ensure that the IDP children are not marginalized from the mainstream education system and are able to easily transit into the public-school system. Incorporation of WASH, gender, child rights and protection, safe hygiene and sanitation practices like washing hands after toilet use, among other lifesaving information and life skills education will enable the learners and communities to build their resilience during disease outbreak and/or crises.As interventions are intended to begin to shift to formal learning, the project will also respond to the educational needs of school dropouts from the IDP camps and the host communities through accelerated learning program to overcome challenges of interrupted schooling linked to floods, poverty, and over-age or disadvantaged and vulnerability. INTERSOS considers reaching out to 200 (100 female) for the accelerated learning program with learners transitioning to the mainstream formal education. In this project; INTERSOS seeks to promote complementarities with other ongoing projects and therefore the VTC center in Baidoa currently funded by UNICEF will be used to enrollover age children, youth and adolescents to access marketable professional skills. Professional courses such as tailoring, tie and die, electrical skills and computer will be provided at the center. During activities implementation, particularly trainings, INTERSOS will use the reflective circle approach to inspire community-based solutions to barriers to education in the 5 targeted districts, thus ultimately contributing to a more sustainable and lasting impact.
