Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to save lives, increase access to safe water, and minimize risks of water borne diseases. The project aims to achieve this purpose through rehabilitation of broken-down boreholes, and ensure all-inclusive access, and community participation through training of Water Management Committees (WMC) and sensitization of individuals on basic hygiene and sanitation practices to ensure sustainability of the project. The intervention is expected to benefit a total of benefit 21,600 individuals in the targeted project intervention areas.

1 - Needs

Analysis of IPC data for August 2019, estimates that 6.35 million people country wide (54% of the population) are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, among whom an estimated 1.7 million people are facing Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity and 10,000 people are in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5), and of these, 267,610 people are in need from the project targeted Counties of Aweil North, Aweil South and Aweil West Counties of Northern Bahr el Ghazal State. Given areas have an extreme lack of food, the population is basically malnourished and the Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) prevalence, as measured by weight-for-height z-score (WHZ), exceeds 30 percent, and morality, as measured by the Crude Death Rate (CDR), is greater than 2 per 10,000 per day. Food and nutrition insecurity in proposed intervention counties are further worsened by limited access to safe and sustainable access to water, and adequate sanitation facilities resulting in chronic nature of waterborne diseases, low use of latrines, poor personal hygiene and living environments, and limited access to soap for hand washing. Access to health services is also poor and this leads to high incidences of diseases that not only affect the health of the population, but also negatively affects availability of labor and leads to reduced income at household level. WASH needs for the mentioned Counties are therefore high and require significant investment to address them. To further create synergies with other Welthungerhilfe’s ongoing projects in the area, and address the high incidence of malnutrition, sensitization on Infant and Young Child Feeding practices (IYCF), nutritional needs of Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW), and hygiene will be rolled out.

2 - Activities

Rehabilitation of boreholes: To provide the target group with sustainable access to safe water, Welthungerhilfe will rehabilitate identified non-functional boreholes in the target area. To ensure sustainability and efficiency of the boreholes, Welthungerhilfe will equip the boreholes with simple India Mark II hand pumps that are common and spares part available in local markets. Training of water Management Committee members: To ensure the sustainable operation and maintenance of the boreholes, Welthungerhilfe will set up a Water Committee for each borehole whose members will be trained on key hygiene and sanitation messages practices. The committee will also be trained on basic safety issues, bookkeeping skills, roles and responsibilities and water points hygiene. The committee will be supported to set up a user fee system to raise funds for routine maintenance of the boreholes in case it breaks down. To ensure sustainability of the boreholes, Welthungerhilfe will train boreholes hand pump technics from the project intervention areas to ensure routine maintenance of the boreholes. The boreholes hand pump mechanics will be equipped with a complete set of India Mark II hand pump repair kits. Hygiene promotion messages: To minimize the of risk of waterborne diseases such as cholera and diarrhea, and promote health of the families, households and or individuals will be targeted with key hygiene and sanitation promotion messages focusing topics such as safe water chain, safe food chain, personal hygiene, proper use of sanitation facilities , good hand washing practices, and environmental hygiene. This will be reinforced with key promotional strategies such as the use of media, special events, IEC materials, mass campaigns etc.
