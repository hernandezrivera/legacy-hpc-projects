Project Summary

Through this project, CARE seeks to build Syrians’ resilience by capitalizing on their positive capacities and reducing their reliance on negative coping strategies, while also finding ways to deal with the larger contextual challenges which continue to weaken people’s resilience.
The proposed project will serve the affected population, who do not have a reliable income to improve access to markets and income generation opportunities and boost their ability to meet their basic needs themselves. Further, the project will establish Community Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) that provide disadvantaged communities with a safe place to save and obtain loans to start a business or access productive assets to better able to deal with impacts of the conflict and reduced dependence on humanitarian assistance. Rebuilding infrastructures, and maintenance of electricity stations will strengthen equitable access to essential services, livelihood opportunities and social cohesion.
The wheat VC offers a critical entry point to build economic sustainability and food security in NWS. Through supporting the functionality of wheat value chain specifically the wheat-to-bread segment CARE will ensure that market systems are strengthened and sustainable livelihoods and access to productive assets are enhanced. Farmers will continue cultivation of arable lands this season after receiving cash for cultivation and marketing their wheat production to maintain a sufficient amount of bread produced at a subsidized cost.

3 - Needs

In 2023estimated 15M people will need food & Ag assistance, representing 68% of total population, the result shows a 7% increase PIN from 2022. 12.1M people are estimated to be food insecure, including all 2M living in camp. The consequent displacement impacted businesses, job creation,only 43% of the population worked in the last three months.Decreased purchasing power led to gap between incomes and expenditures on essential needs especially for women and PWDs.Further,the lack of electricity and fuel undermined the agricultural sector and negatively impacted private businesses forcing owners to reduce the number of employees. Price increases for food commodities and the adoption of negative coping strategies led households to buying less preferred foods.The wheat value chain has been disrupted due to a combination of drought,deteriorating economic conditions, which contributed to increase in bread prices.The conflict dismantled wheat-to-bread market system and damaged bakeries,mills irrigation channels and Ag roads.The situation is made worse by the water scarcity and climatic change.The crisis in Syria the contamination of displacement has negatively impacted social capital and the relationships that transcend traditional bonds.Regions with high IDP to resident ratios face increased tension between the two communities.The conflict and economic decline impacted the rates violence against women,impedes their movement,access to employment and increase their exposure to abuse.

4 - Implementation of activities

CARE’s area-based resilience approach is designed to deliver integrated packages of FSA&ERL assistance to individuals, households, and communities to maximize the impact and boost resilience. CARE will deliver 750 market-relevant skills training followed by 480 start-up ‘kits’ (cash or in-kind) for individuals to learn a viable vocation that can be leveraged for more consistent income. in addition to providing 800 inclusive temporary job opportunities that contribute to the restoration of infra. (Maintenance of electrical networks/roads rehab1,250 Ind). VSLAs module will be expanded in targeted locations (170 groups & approx1700 participants). CARE wish to establish community committees to foster social cohesion, facilitate community-level dialogue around equitable decision-making & resource control. CARE will purchase harvested wheat from 3,000 local farmers to expand the production of subsidized bread. Also, Cash support for wheat cultivation to be provided to 1,900 farmers combined with Climate-Smart agriculture sessions. CARE will undertake 20 Infrastructure Rehabilitation projects to support the Ag sector, including irrigation canals and Ag roads, Bakeries and mills; and shifting to solar energy for the public facilities. CARE’s1,500 CfW opportunities will focus on Firefighting (DRR) for wheat crops; as well as activities targeting women such as Public nurseries services, and seasonal activities linked to Ag VCs. Project budget for proposed activities is not secured.

Protection priorities are integrated, whilst program staff are trained on Code of Conduct & Safeguarding Policy.Conflict sensitivity and risk assessment tools such as Do No Harm will be used to identify potential harmful impacts that are likely to be caused by the proposed activities.CARE following the Global Protection Cluster guidance and committed to ensuring the safety and dignity of BNFs and especially female, mitigating the effects of physical and psychological threats faced affected populations,(Select safe appropriate activities and implementation location. provide the project participants with safety training and equipment, especially in CFW& infra rehab), and facilitating their access to assistance and services. Support the development of self-protection capacities and assist people to claim their rights.Markets in NWS in general are maledominated. provision of employment for women increase women’s empowerment and decision-making but change power dynamics which may increase GBV risks. GBV mitigation measures will be considered in the implementation of the intervention in line with the IASC Guidelines for integrating GBV in humanitarian action. The risk of supply chain interruption and procurement delays will be overcome by Securing several LTA with vendors to avoid suspensions, for the inflation risks CARE will ensure to use US dollars as main currency and monitor markets to purchase the project items at reasonable price
