Project Summary

The planned intervention will target both children and adults girls and boys with urgent protection and psychosocial needs caregivers, community members and service providers.specific activities of the intervention will include psycho-social support and face barriers of accessing child protection services such as structured and non-structured PSS activities to enhance their resilience, positive coping mechanisms and children's wellbeing and development , Children with protection concerns will be provided with Case Management services including referrals. Activities on prevention and response will focus on structured and non structured PSS using evidence based curriculum such as Families Makes the Differences (6-17) on positive parenting to enhance caregivers skills on nurturing children, in addition to ECD components that will target caregivers of children aged 0-8.
Recreational activities will be provided in Child Friendly spaces to enhance social and emotional skills for children. Awareness session will target adults and children in enhancing the role of community in protecting children.
Community systems and structures will be enhanced through capacity building activities focusing on various topics including safe identification and referral, safeguarding to enhance the role of community in protecting children.
