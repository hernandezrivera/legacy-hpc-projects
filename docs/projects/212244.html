Project Summary

The poor nutrition situation is still a major challenge for public health in NWS, mainly affecting infants, U5, and PLW, causing different forms of malnutrition, including wasting, SAM, MAM, chronic malnutrition or stunting, and micronutrient deficiency, especially iron deficiency anemia. According to the nutrition smart survey that was conducted in NWS in August 2023, the prevalence of GAM WHZ increased from 3.3% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2023, and the prevalence of MAM also increased from 2.4% to 3.4%, while the prevalence of SAM has been decreased slightly from 0.9% to 0.6%. The Prevalence of combined GAM has increased from 5 % in 2022 to 5.6% in 2023, and the prevalence of underweight has increased from 8.5 % in 2022 to 10.4% in 2023. Compared with 2022, The prevalence of stunting rate remains at the same level at 22.4% with a very slight increase compared with last year at 22.3%, but it is still over 20% which is considered Critical/ Extreme according to the GNC severity scale. Close to 2022, The prevalence of child anemia remains at the same level at 37% with a slight increase compared with last year at 36%, but it is still over 20% which is considered Serious/ Severe according to the severity scale.
This project aims to improve access to high-quality preventive and curative nutrition services for all vulnerable people in the implementation area, by ensuring that nutrition services are fully available and integrated into the health services in the health facilities supported by Al-Ameen, to enhance community engagement and strengthen the linkages with the health care facilities, this will support the early recovery efforts and contribute to building resilient primary health care systems in north-west Syria. the integration will be through establishing an OTP in each health facility, the OTP staff will work at the health facility, in addition to conducting home visits in the community to provide nutrition services, provided services include screening, CMAM, IYCF, FMA, and TSFP, in addition to awareness messages about handwashing practices, food processing, COVID-19 and cholera signs, symptoms, means of infection, and IPC measures.

Monitoring and Evaluation Plan:Al-Ameen Organization has an integrated system (policies, procedures, and tools) for the MEAL Department that is applied to all projects and centers supported by the organization by a trained and qualified team in all of the AL-Ameen’s workplaces and offices.Before starting any project implementation, the M&E plan should be prepared to guide us on how we can monitor the project implementation. MEAL field employee makes a regular visit to the centers to evaluate the work, and directly meet beneficiaries and other stakeholders, he will collect the required data regularly as per a monitoring plan, and use a checklist to check including:1- Readiness of the centers’ infrastructure (electricity - water - building condition - access - waiting room - security and safety)2-Readiness of the organizational structure (signs - records and forms - complaints and feedback mechanism - staff commitment – center hygiene)3-Services (reception and clinics - infection control procedures - pharmacy - laboratory - equipment)then he feeds it back to the project manager, this will help to triangulate the findings as well as to gather impact information from them.The data is evaluated and analyzed every month and is analyzed statistically for proper results and early detection of any outliers. MEAL Officer, make sure that the monitoring plan is moving systematically as it should by measuring indicators and activities versus time and budget.About the data collected from the different sites, reports are generated periodically in a unified format, including statistical reports and operational reports for the different sites.Also, the generated statistical data from all centers that ALameen supports is available to everyone, the generated data will have a role in disease surveillance and could contribute to predicting and detecting disease and epidemic outbreaks.The monitoring and Evaluation process is implemented by using the following documentation1.
