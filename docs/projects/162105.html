Project Summary

This project covers approximately 9,000 beneficiaries in the targeted camps providing various WASH services including construction of latrine, bathing spaces, tubewells/water networks, repairing and maintenance of latrine, bathing spaces and tubewells, desludging of latrine, solid waste management and hygiene promotion. Greater emphasis will be given in community ownership and capacity building so that they can take initiatives to repair their WASH facilities and maintain appropriate hygiene practices to prevent any kind of water-borne disease and reduce cost of treatment. Gender and protection will also be mainstreamed in the programming.

Objectives

"1. To ensure refugess living in camps have access to sufficiently safe, secure and dignified sanitation facilities that allow for easy and convenient usage at all times. 2. To ensure refugess living in camps have access to continuous, equitable and dignified access to sufficient and safe water for drinking and domestic usage. 3. To ensure refugess living in camps harness behaviors and good practices of hygiene through access and exposure to innovative hygiene promotion and provision of hygiene kits"

Needs

There is substantial need for repairing and maintenance of latrines, bathing spaces and tubewells in the targeted camp area as mentioned in the NPM R15 report that the HHs do not have WASH facilities nearby and the long distance reduces their accessibility. The report also suggests that there is not sufficient functioning WASH facilities in the camps. There is also significant need of solid waste management within the camp in order to prevent water-borne diseases and sustain clean water for regular usage. Communities still have room for improvement in their regular hygiene practices which minimize their costs and keep them safe from ailments in all seasons. Furthermore, WASH facilities need to be more accessible to PwDs, women, children, elderly people and other vulnerable groups. Protection and DRR factors need to be mainstreamed in the facilities and services so that the targeted refugee community can be more resilient to natural and human-made crises.
