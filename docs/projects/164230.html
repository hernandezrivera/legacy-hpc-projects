Project Summary

The project primarily aims to restore water supply and sewerage services in Tall Abyad to minimum acceptable standards and assist local councils/ authorities to establish an appropriate mechanism for sustainable service delivery.
the project is mainly targeting Tall Abyad District in North-East Syria including three main locations; Tall Abyad city, Sluk, and Ain Issa. the area becomes in late 2019 considerably safe after the end of hostilities in that region. the estimated number of people living there is 200,000 according to the local council of Tall Abyad. majority of them are returnees due to the cessation of hostilities. clean drinking water emerges as the first priority followed by the sewage system according to the local council.
Minber Al-sham intervention will focus on rehabilitating the water network in the targeted locations in order to deliver safe drinking water, in addition to rehabilitating the sewage systems.

4 - Needs

Despite the overall slight improvement of WASH conditions in Syria, vast WASH-related needs continue to exist in many parts of Syria, affecting various population groups. Access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations, IDPs living in IDP sites, as well as other most vulnerable households with limited livelihood opportunities and access to basic WASH services. After nine years of conflict, the WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria require significant investments, repair and operational support. WASH systems have suffered from damages owing to years of functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, destructions, displacement of technical staff and effects of climate change and poor water resources management. 42 per cent of the population is relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs. Communities not served with public water networks are in general at higher risk of unsafe water consumption, use less water than people well served via free/low-cost water networks and spend significantly more of their incomes on purchasing water. At least 80 per cent of sewage is discharged untreated while at least half of the sewerage systems are not functional. Source: WAHS Cluster 2019 (Turkey Hub)

5 - Implementation of activities

the project will implement mainly three activities as the following: 1. rehabilitation of the water system2. Rehabilitation of the sewage system 3. Solid waste disposal.The project will commence by doing an in-depth assessment of the current situation of the water and sewage network and what are the required rehabilitation steps. in this regard, Minber Al-sham will rely on its technical staff expertise of engineers who have past experience of implementing WASH Projects. the second step would be contracting with experienced vendors to conduct the required rehabilitation activities. simultaneously, Minber Al-sham will be procuring trash bins according to the needs and will contract a company to conduct the solid waste disposal into an official waste landfill. throughout the activities' implementation, Minber Al-Sham will be monitoring the intervention closely to make sure that all activities are implemented according to the humanitarian standards and sphere standards.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk/Threat #1: Movements of garbage trucks in congested, camp like settings; Reliability of servicesLikelihood: highImpact: lowMitigating Measure: Consult/engage community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Provide household level solid waste containers/plastic bags to ease the disposal of waste from H/H to the public SW collection points. Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Protection Risk/Threat #2: Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities.Likelihood: low Impact: highMitigating Measure: MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities.Promote cost recovery systems for longer-term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. Protection Risk/Threat #3: Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases.Likelihood: high Impact: medium Mitigating Measure: Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.
