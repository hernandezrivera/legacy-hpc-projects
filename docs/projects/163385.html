Project Summary

Among difficulties faced by the target population, JRS finds a lack of access to formal education and professional training, especially for girls, who have a high drop-out rate. Young boys of Gado are leaving the education system because of the lack of visible opportunities in the job market. The lack of prospects for their future may become a cause of frustration, from which youth can easily be recruited by armed groups. Plan International Cameroon has also remarked an increase in the practice of sex for survival amongst young girls, especially in Garoua Boulai, as a negative coping mechanism. Power imbalance between male and females is also materialized in access to education, health (especially reproductive health and family planning), and means of production. Under the law, women have the same access to land as men. However, while 80% of food produce is done by women, women only have ownership of 2% of the land. Also, among those who have access to education, there is a high drop-out rate, sometimes associated with the burden of housework. The Ministry of Social Affairs (MINAS) has noted the lack of existence in the region of an intervention for girls at the level of their ministry. To gain access to vocational training and work, young women depend greatly on their parents' permission. Even when they access employment, their wages are lower than those of men, and maternity leave is not available.
GOAL
To improve access to personal development for all ages via increasing the economic and social integration of marginalized refugee and host community groups in La Kadey and Lom et Djerem departments
OBJECTIVES
Y2: Objective 1: To promote local professional, social and economic integration for young refugees and the host community by providing access to vocational training in Bertoua, Gado, Manjou and Garoua Boulai
Y2: Objective 2: To improve access to child protection by developing community-based preschool centers and community training on psychosocial support in Kette and Manjou municipalities
Y2: Objective 3: To improve social cohesion and community links through women empowerment and agro-pastoral environmental improvement in La Kadey and Lom et Djerem Departments

Besoins / Needs

Analyse des besoins fait en Aout 2017 a démontré le besoin pour un appui aux jeunes qui manquait un niveau d'éducation importante ainsi que des opportunités de moyens de subsistance pour qu'ils se prennent en charge (camerounais et centrafricains). En lien, des problèmes avec la cohésion sociale entres les différentes communautés qui empêchaient le bon déroulement des activités de moyens de subsistance pour les réfugiés a été noté. Le manque quasi-total d'opportunités d'éducation préscolaire diminuait la probabilité de réussite en école primaire aux enfants ainsi que leur bienêtre en générale; cela empêchait également aux mères des enfants de faire leur activités génératrices de revenue parce qu'il fallait rester pour s'occuper des enfants. Le renforcement des infrastructures préscolaires vise à combler les deux problèmes. OBJECTIFSY2: Objectif 1: Promouvoir l'intégration local au niveau professionnel, social et économique aux jeunes réfugiés et à la communauté hôte à travers la provision de la formation professionnelle à Bertoua, Gado, Mandjou et Garoua BoulaiY2: Objectif 2: Améliorer l'accès à la protection de l'enfance à travers le devéloppement des centres préscolaires à base communautaire et la formation de la communauté sur la provision de l'appui psychosociale à Kette et à Mandjou.Y2: Objectif 3: Améliorer la cohésion sociale et les liens communautaires à travers le renforcement de la femme et l'amélioration de la cohésion sociale entre les agriculteurs et éleveurs dans les départements de La Kadey et à Lom et Djerem.
