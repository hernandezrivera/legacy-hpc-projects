Project Summary

Due to targeting a lot of health facilities in western Aleppo and Idleb, also due to withdrawing a lot of donors from the area because of changing of controlling area, the health system in the area maybe facing challenges and all health system may be collapsing.
IDA going to support secondary health center in western Aleppo to cover main needs in the area. Population in the area more than 500,000, also may be facing new movement of IDPs to the area from east and south Idleb.
the project will be targeting Aleppo Jebel Saman district and Al-Atareb countryside. the propsoed center is located in Kafer-Karmeen community - Al-Atareb sub-district- JabelSaman district- Aleppo governorate.

Needs

the proposed project aims to cover a attachment population of 50,000 including IDPs and host community accommodating in the hard to reach areas of Aleppo governors ( western countryside) with secondery health care services. This facility will respond to IDPs movements from southern Idlib, as well as play a key role in refer cases to the main refferal hospital ( Bab Al-Hawa hospital). This facility was rehabilitated and run for 2 years by several NGOs, however, it is out of any contracted funding since 3 months. IDA going to support facility to covering gap. Most of staff already required and the infrastructure in good condition. IDA has good relationships with Health Directorate and the facility put in their heath map for 2019

Protection Risk Analysis

This project proposal has been designed on the basis of needs assessments consisting ofinterviews with key informants within the target population. The project staff will be hiredwhenever possible from the target area itself, ensuring intimate knowledge of the contextand good acceptability of the staff by the community. IDA has an incorporatedbeneficiaries feedback into their existing feedback system, through suggestion boxes ateach PHC, which are collected and reviewed by the M&E field officer on monthly basis.The M&E Officer will obtain direct patient feedback during the monitoring process. Allpatients feedback will be registered in complaints tracking sheet, the tracking sheet willeasily show the statues for each complaint (such as category, time cycle, decision, extra),the beneficiary will get the result of his/her complaint if possible( since the anonymouscomplaints are acceptable). IDA team are trained on service provision according tohumanitarian principles, as well as, IDA will implement meetings with campsmanagement, Local Council and community leaders as well as with Health Directorate toknow the affected population suggestions and needs to improve the services provided.Prohibition of Sexual Exploitation and Sexual Abuse (PSEA) IDA continues to stand firm onzero tolerance for (sexual) misconduct. Therefore, IDA and the contracted sub-partnersare obliged to create and maintain an environment that prevents sexual exploitation andsexual abuse. Managers at all levels have a particular responsibility to support anddevelop systems that maintain this environment. The partner should ensure that systemsare in place to collect and effectively manage feedback from communities Assumptions:- The border crossing between Turkey and Syria remains open for humanitarian assistance and staff- Target areas districts remain free of armed conflict- The situation is stable and IDPs movement fit with project objective. Risks:- Intensification of the conflict resulting drastic changes of the context, including further population movements into or out of the area- Theft of project equipment by warring parties following the change of front lines in the project area- An exodus of professional staff- The outbreak of an epidemic due to poor hygienic conditions and overcrowding
