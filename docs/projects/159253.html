Project Summary

About 55% of the 600,000 IDPs are concentrated in two of Mogadishu’s peripheral districts. It continues to draw displaced populations from Lower and Middle Shabelle Regions with the situation expected to worsen as the Somalia National Army (SNA) and AMISOM continue to intensify their onslaught against the non-state actors occupying parts of the two regions. Many of those already in Camps especially along the Afgooye Corridor in Mogadishu face the risk of eviction from private developers and claimants to the land they occupy. The combined drought and conflict is displacing the populations who opt to migrate to Mogadishu in search of humanitarian support. Deynille and Kahda Districts where CPD has been implementing integrated programming for the past 3 years with the support of UNOCHA and Save the Children, have the bulk of the IDPs among Mogadishu’s 17 Districts. There is need to work with the local administration and CCCM partners in Mogadishu to proactively identify public sites and support the IDPs relocate to secure and improved sites with land tenure.
On the other hand many of the displaced IDPs from drought and conflict in Galmudug are currently hosted in temporary camps in Adado (2,409 IDPsHH), Abduwak (15,709IDP HHs) and Galkayo South Districts (5,805 IDP HHs). South Gaalkacyo has 48 IDP sites, hosting 9,554 households or 54,573 individuals (48% male and 52% female).
The majority of IDPs settle in informal and unplanned settlements in the target Regions have poor management which has been appropriated by the ‘gatekeepers’ (informal managers), with living conditions being poor, observed high rates of Gender Based violence (CPD Managed Garasbarey Health Clinic Returns in Daynille District). There are few rights for the IDPs including on the land they settle (lack of land tenure) with forced evictions being a regular feature in the IDP Camps. Many of the IDPs especially in Mogadishu City’s Deynille and Kahda Districts have been and continue to be at risk of being victims of multiple displacements.
IDPs are often marginalized and discriminated against because they belong to minorities or are separated from the protection of their clans. Protection needs are acute especially for women and girls who are more vulnerable. The responsibilities of the gate keepers and their accountability is limited as the gatekeeping role is a self-appointed one. There is need to provide IDPs with alternative livelihood means by offering skills training and labor opportunities through Cash-for-Work (CfW) opportunities in undertaking site improvement works (roads and drainage) targeting mainly women and youth. The project also plans to work jointly work with Durable Solution Unit(s) and/or other similarly designated entities to initiate actions to address access to secure housing and land tenure, shelter upgrading materials and shared household gender friendly latrines for the IDPs in the 4 target regions.
Protection of women and girls and also children remains a paramount need in the IDP camps to ensure security from sexual and gender based violence. Safe access for the IDPs to basic services and also to and from their camps is a very important need for the displaced in all the indicated three targeted regions. The project will work with the CCCM Cluster and the GBV sub-cluster to conduct awareness workshop for the camp leaders and the local administration on GBV and its role in ensuring protection for the women, girls and children. The project plans to support the provision of CCCM services in order to reduce barriers to assistance, ensure equitable delivery of services, enhance the accountability to affected populations, and strengthen community engagement, participation and empowerment towards self-determination.

Please provide further explanation

The project is supportive of the Somalia IDP Solutions Initiative led by the Federal Government of Somalia and also the local regional authorities in the targeted regions of Banadir and Galmudug, South West and Hirshabelle States. The project activities as designed will contribute to substantially improve living conditions of IDPs as per CCCM Cluster Objective 4, through the establishment of governance systems, increased Housing, Land and Property Rights and social, economic and political inclusion. The integrated activities will contribute to the strategic, targeted and sustainable approach, tailored for the respective regions that takes into account specific displacement and socio-economic context (which varies from region to region) to ensure better governance, basic services access and accountability in supporting the IDPs. The strengthening of the local governance structures within the local authorities and IDP Camps will enhance accountability mechanisms in order to make relevant offices and institutions accessible to IDPs and enable authorities to assume their primary responsibility to gradually enable durable solutions processes in Mogadishu, Galmudug, South West and Hirshabelle States. In addition the project will contribute to employment generation in support of improved access to affordable housing and protective and basic service delivery to IDPs and returnees.The project will work jointly with Banadir Durable Solution Unit and/or other designated entities in Galmudug, South West and Hirshabelle States to initiate actions to secure housing and land tenure, shelter upgrading materials and shared household gender friendly latrines and employment for the IDPs in the 4 target regions. The actions detailed will integrate relevant components that create durable solution systems capable of addressing IDP’s needs and facilitating access for IDPs and refugee returnees to an increasingly productive, safe and dignified life. The integrated interventions are novel, replicable and have a potential for scalability to become effective durable solution systems that can be linked to local governance and integrate in longer-term urban development over time. Significantly the proposed actions will also address exclusivity of all IDP including the Women, Youth, minorities and disabled to ensure they are included in the proposed durable solutions.
