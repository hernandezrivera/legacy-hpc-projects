Project Summary

Considering the economic challenges nowadays in Sudan ,especially in SD and SK and based on the need assessment done by Gayat and El Ruhama as consortium field staff in the targeted areas ,it reveals that there is food insecurity facing IDPs, residents, refugees and returnees it is expected to be more worsen, women and girls experienced (GBV) Food insecurity remains alarmingly high in Sudan with increased and protracted displacement, economic decline and inflation, and high food price hikes exacerbated by the impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic and the high inflation rates. The lockdown measures to prevent the spread of COVID-19 significantly decreased commodity movement, market function and cross-border trade, and compromised livelihoods and daily labor opportunities, reducing household purchasing power and food access of the vulnerable population. An estimated 7.1 million people face crisis or worse levels of food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 and above) and need urgent action to save lives. This represents a 22 % increase from 5.8M compared to the same period last year (IPC analysis 2019). Around one million of the 7.2 million food insecure people are facing emergency levels of acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 4) and around 6 million people are facing crisis levels of acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3), including rape .sexual assault, intimate partner violence, sexual harassment, trafficking, and sexual exploitation and abuse of girls, early married, FGM this specific considering violence is socially ascribed intended to control female body and live them in subordinate position , moreover, women and girls are economically and socially vulnerable and have a higher incidence of poverty, they lack job-based skills and means of production which increases their vulnerability to GBV; Elruhama and Gayat as consortium partner and both are women-led organization recognized that returnees, still have challenges in accessing services of SGBV survivors, Survivors of S/GBV are not support properly . There is a need to offer more support to these vulnerable communities and the social workers and services provider need more training, there is demand for formation of SGBV protection mechanism .this project targeted 12900 persons 95% will be female , the project activities will be drawn from the two organizations experiences ;GAYAT’s experiences are focusing on Reducing Violence against Women and Girls, Extending hands to the Women and girls in Darfur region this imitative funded by FAR from its DCPSF funded project the project aim in Improving women access to sexual and reproductive health services, Empowering women economically towards realization of economic, social and cultural rights (ESCR) and Building inclusive women’s movements. Another intervention with Reducing Violence against Women and Girls with women street vendors project in Khartoum , funded by NCA , the project focuses on addressing the deeply rooted subordination of women, their alienation and marginalization based on an intersection between patriarchal culture all combined with current conflicts and cultural/ tribal disintegration, and the gradual escalation of sexual violence; GAYAT project “Non-violence spaces” funded by Switzerland embassy in Khartoum is aimed at increasing the capacity and raising the awareness of youth on issues of gender, religion, race, ethnicity, and peaceful coexistence. These youth members moved on to be mediators and facilitate a dialogue with other youth from their communities in fighting the GBV the outcome of these intervention resulting in the formation of two mechanisms the paralegal group and men against violence against women group(MaVAWG) their roles are to participate on in SGBV prevention and response interventions enabled and sustained ,timely provision of material assistance provided to survivors , psychosocial counseling, they also deal with issues of rape, domestic violence, FGM .Gayat has 12 well professional staff in all field .

1 - Needs

The inter-agency assessments conducted in areas such as SD and SK reveal uncovered massive humanitarian needs in protection especially with GBV , some of the concerns related to ability to respond to humanitarian needs. The status quo of sporadic fighting during 2018 also meant that the numbers of displaced populations continue to create vulnerabilities, with the majority being children and women. respect for the rights of individuals. Needs extend beyond material assistance, to address the socioeconomic impact of COVID-19 specially with issues related to GBV and there is need for the provision of specific counseling and humanitarian protection activities. For example, training of social workers, construction or rehabilitation of women centers for multi -function will be deployed to ensure that community-based protection mechanisms extend its protection responsibilities the project intervention will include , establishment of women centre by local materials which will served for multi- functional services for women and female empowerment , for family tracing and reunification, for reintegration of children from state and non-state armed forces and armed groups, for referral pathway for improving the monitoring and reporting mechanism on violations among conflict affected children and prevent and respond to GBV against women and female

2 - Activities/Outputs

Activity 1 : conduct 4 base line survey for the socioeconomic impact of COVID-19 on women and girl and the level of GBV occur due to the restriction and lockdown as many of the targeted women are in the informal sector these activity will targeted locality (Kadoguli and Abu Korshola in South Kordofan), Beliel and Demso in South Darfur Activity 2: Establishment / rehabilitation ofsix women centers four in SK and two in SD from local materials to serve as multi -functional purpose enters ,such as referral pathway, services provision, market for women small business, information and learning center …etc . the targeted direct beneficiary will reached 2000 at SK and 1059 at SD Activity 3: 4 capacity workshops targeted 100 persons in SK and 100 in SD (gender sensitive )for services provider, social workers, line minsters, community leaders …etc to provide services properly Activity 5: formation of three protection mechanisms /committees / networks targeted 200 at SK and 100 at SD (100 Beliel in SD and Kadogoli and Abu Koreshola in SK 100 person per each (gender sensitive))to overseas services inside the center , guarantees the quality of service delivery and bear other responsibility related the GBV services and for project sustainability Activity 6: conduct 6 awareness session (possible 16 days of activism ) for combatting the GBV and stop violence against women and female specially with incidents that related to FGM, early marriage , domestic violence , trafficking and rape. the events will targeted 8000 persons , including the dissemination of radio broadcasting messages and TV. Activity 7: provision of SRHS kits targeting 2000 female (1000 per each targeted state(SD,SK) , 500 per each locality(Beleil, Demso in SD and Abu Kershola and Kadogoli in SK ) ) Activity 8 : conducting four workshop for food processing and market –based skills targeting 60 female (15 per each locality) to be handle at the centers with possible advance TOT training for the best to run the activity within the centers and to oversee the women economic groups that will be formed by the end of the workshop. Activity 9 : provision of start –up kits for 60 female as evolving fund and for resilience . Activity 10 : conduct 8 sessions targeting 200 persons including media, ministry of social welfare, stakeholders , the session will be about women rights, gender, GBV, VAW, reproductive health rights , HIV/AIDs and STD (sexual transmitted disease ),FGM
