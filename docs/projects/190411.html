Project Summary

This objective of this UN Women multi-country programme is to provide survivors of GBV and those at risk of GBV with livelihoods
and agency to facilitate their access to lifesaving humanitarian services and facilitate their voices in promoting GBV accountability
in humanitarian contexts. The impact of the programme will be to more effectively mitigate, prevent and respond to GBV amongst
crisis affected communities. UN Women interventions focus on three outcomes, two of them to be jointly achieved with UNFPA.

The three key outcomes of the program are outlined below and they cover aspects of social norm programming that will contribute
to protection and empowerment of crisis affected women and girls in priority countries; scaling up partnerships with local
WLOs/WROs and strengthening their capacities to participate in humanitarian decision making; increasing access and improving
quality of services provided to crisis affected women and girls (refugees, IDPs and in host communities) both in relation to
protection and livelihoods/income generation (including cash for women). The program will also contribute to the protection and
address the needs and priorities of at risk and marginalized groups including LGBTIQ+ populations, women and girls with
disabilities. The first two outcomes will be delivered in close partnership with UNFPA in the 6 priority countries (common outcomesOutcome 1 and 2).
Common Outcome 1: Targeted people, through programme interventions, report changes in perceptions and attitudes towards
social norms and practices that perpetuate gender-based violence and gender inequalities.
Common Outcome 2: WLOs and WROs are empowered to increasingly engage in decision making and leadership in GBV
response, mitigation and prevention.
Outcome 3 (Agency specific): Women and girls who have experienced/are experiencing GBV or are at risk of GBV benefit from
provision of and access to quality, multi-sectoral services, including legal aid.
Outcome 4 (Agency specific): Reduced risk of GBV through provision of livelihoods opportunities, cash transfers including cash
for work.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 25.1 Conduct awareness-raising of communities, including women and girls, men and boys in IDPs and host communities on GBV prevention, mitigation and associated risks, including promotion of non-violent, non-discriminatory and gender equitable norms.Activity 26.1 Conduct refresher training sessions for leaders and representatives of WLOs/WROs on humanitarian architecture (HCT, cluster system, inter-cluster system, humanitarian program cycle process, GAM) Activity 26.2 Organize dialogues among WLOs/WROs and members of humanitarian clusters on gender related priorities and gaps in cluster planning and response and how to address them through the leadership of WLOs/WROs.Activity 27.1 Provide GBV and SEA-related survivor support and services through risk assessments, service mapping, CBCM-community based complaint mechanism establishment, community engagement and information campaigns.Activity 27.2 Provide shelter-based comprehensive services for survivors of violence through food provision, accommodation, medical service and psychological support).Activity 28.1 Organize community dialogues and media campaigns on prevention and protection of women and girls from all forms of violence.Activity 29.1 Conduct rapid assessment on institutional customary judicial mechanisms in the two regions.Activity 29.2 Conduct training for community-based mechanisms, including those led by women-led committees and women’s groups to enhance protection, prevention and response to GBV.Activity 29.3 Establish CBCM procedures with inputs from community members/ partners and design user friendly guides/procedures on CBCM.Activity 29.4 Conduct training for CBCM focal points and community members on the functionality and process of SEA and GBV referral and reporting.Activity 30.1 Organize trainings for WLOs/WROs to identify GBV risks and engage in/lead GBV mitigation strategies, referral pathways and community level protection mechanisms. Activity 30.2 Orient and train WLOs/WROs in developing locally relevant tools to monitor GBV prevention and mitigation strategies and engage in participatory safety audits at community level.Activity 31.1 Provide refresher trainings for leaders and representatives of WLOs/WROs and women’s committees on gender in humanitarian action, leadership, and advocacy skills.Activity 32.1 Hold community-based consultations with IDPs, host communities and key partners to assess existing procedures for handling SEA and GBV reports and complaints.Activity 32.2 Conduct gender analysis of the GBV situation, including risks, trends, mitigation, response and prevention.Activity 32.3 Support quick orientation for service providers on psychological first aid for SEA and GBV survivors.Activity 32.4 Conduct refresher training for customary courts, police force, and other security units on GBV and SEA.Activity 32.5 Conduct refresher training targeting for key actors and organizations in the local justice system on survivor-centered support. Activity 32.6 Establish two legal aid centers by EWLA to expand women access to justice, with focus on survivors of GBV.Activity 32.7 Provide legal counselling and legal aid support services, including through facilitation of access to official documentation, mobile aid clinics, referrals, case management and court assistance.Activity 33.1 Conduct skills/vocational trainings for GBV survivors and at-risk women in IDPs and host communities.Activity 33.2 Establish self-help saving groups for GBV survivors and women at risk, including IDPs and in host communities. Activity 33.3 Facilitate livelihood opportunities for GBV survivors and at risk women through cash and in-kind support.
