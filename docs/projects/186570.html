Project Summary

The intervention is to build resilience of the target local government areas (LGA) of Bama (banki), Konduga and Jere of in Borno state, Nigeria. Beneficiaries will perform task in the communities of the 3 LGA rehabilitating communal assets and get payment after the work each month, beneficiaries with existing small-scale business or newly established will be supported with cash to boost their business. The beneficiaries will be attached with master Trainers for skills training and be supported with in-kind start-up tools, 84 VSLA will be established covering all the 1,740 people. To strengthen the local government system, 25 staff each of the 3 LGAs will be trained to improve on their capacity in the areas of management, accountability, efficiency and planning, this will also include topics on GBV , PSHEA and fraud prevention. To enhance the functionality of the LGA, the project will support each LGA with 50 chairs, 50 tables, 10 computers set, 1 projector, 3 printers, 250KVA generator, 3 projector screen, 10 pair of air conditioners and 3 mobile communication sets. This project will be an integrated one, the different sections of the project will be running at the same time. It will be for 12 months, the cash for work and small scale business project beneficiaries will be mobilized to join the Village Savings Loan Association (VSLA), this will help them to have access to saving and loan facilities. The groups of beneficiaries in the VSLA will have access to training on leadership, business development and financial management. The project target majorly the adult for direct support with a special focus on 70% female due to their high vulnerability. Households headed by women, widow, children and people living with disability will be given priority in participants’ selection. Household with pregnant and lactating women will be given preference in targeting. Plan has strong mechanism for Accountability to Affected Population (AAP), Plan International will work with community structure in the project location, this community structure will be carried along during project planning, implementation and evaluation. Responses from stakeholders will be received through a dedicated phone line and response will be given while issues will be resolved as soon as possible, issues that may need referral will be referred to other partners for support. Plan International has zero tolerance to GBV , there will be a Gender and Inclusion staff who will ensure prevention of gender discrimination in all interventions and monitor the prevention of gender based violence that may come up in the project. All stakeholders will pass through PSEA training, sign the organization PSEA policy. All contract will have PSEA policy as part of the document to sign. All cases of SEA are to be reported immediately. All training sessions will have a short PSEA session as part of the agenda. This project is part of our Plan Emergency Response Plan strategy, all the interventions will be continued for long impact even after the end of the project period.

1 - Needs

Livelihood support through effective vocational skills development at the community level is an initiative design by PRIDE to mitigate the challenges facing by implementing partners at the filed (LSTVS) is a community base vocational training and capacity development using local available vocational skills and trainers to boost beneficiaries capacity before venturing into business, PRIDE comes out with this idea to support persons of concerns PoCs to develop new skills apart from their old skills before displacement (farming) so that they can adopt the new livelihood skills which are available at their areas of displacement, also preparing them for available partners that wish to support aid. This project is a community base approach and it was developed from the past experiences and some lessons that learn by implementing partners through post monitoring distribution (PDM) from various field location in borno state, where key challenges was transformed into methodologies of success. Over the years of displacement in the North-East Nigeria, especially in the BAY states (Borno, Adamawa and Yobe) about 500,000 Household lost their means for livelihood, of which 90 percent are farmers, as a result of insurgency operation by (non-state actions) which block their access to farmland and loss of wealth, these has created a lot of gaps and increases their vulnerability.Government, Development partners, Donors, Private Sectors and the Humanitarian Actors have spent millions of dollars in mitigating risk associated with livelihood to the affected persons but the issues need holistic approach for effective implementation. Due to the lack of dedicated locally developed vocational skills most of the design livelihood face a challenge after successful implementation the beneficiaries end up selling all the support given to them because they lack skills make use of the items(aid’s), some keep it their homes because they don’t know of its importance (they don’t know how to make use of the items) while continue making live very difficult to themselves.All stakeholders need to come together in addressing these key protection concern that affecting the livelihood beneficiaries through adopting these new idea and method toward providing sustainable solutions.

2 - Activities

The objective is to build the capacity of the community though the existing locally (vocational skills) livelihoods available that are existing in the communities and also, to coordinate with livelihood partners to ensure that beneficiaries are undergo 4-month livelihood skills training of their choices and also to ensure that they are well train and acquire needed skills before issuing them grants or livelihood (aid’s) items. The main objectives are as follows;• To build the capacity of the local community with the available vocational skills • To mobilize grant and loans to support their business ideas • To sensitize the community on the importance of developing new skills for their sustainability • To issue certificate of completion.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

he project will follow protection guidelines, interventions will be carried out in safe spaces chosen by the community, all activities will take place during the day time to prevent attack that usually occur in the dark of the day. Activities locations will close to the beneficiaries houses to prevent long distance travelling. There will constant awareness creation on protection, prevention of sexual abuse, exploitation and abuse during all activities. There will constant feedback mechanism put in place to address grievances and risks that may come up during project implementations. All travelling will be approved by security manager before take off. All categories of people including women, people living with disabilities, marginalized section of the community will involved in project planning, implementation and monitoring. All stakeholders will pass through Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) training, sign the organization PSEA policy. All contract will have PSEA policy as part of the document to sign. All cases of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse are to be reported immediately. All training session will have a short PSEA session as part of the agenda.
