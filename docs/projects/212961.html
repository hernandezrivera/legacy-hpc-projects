Project Summary

The continued syrian conflict has huge and deep negative impact on social, economic and political sides in Hasakeh Community.
Conflict and instability cause significant setbacks or even reverse decades of progress in the area that are key to a community’s future, like economic development, public health, and access to nutritious food. Hasakeh community experiencing conflict have higher rates of poverty in comparison to more stable communities.
Sharp economic downtowns, increasing inequality, and rising authoritarianism — to name a few — are not only contributing to the uptick in violence but are also eroding the very foundations that make communities strong and resilient. The effects of climate change and the COVID‑19 pandemic are only compounding these challenges.
The project will work to promote peace and defuse conflict, and achieving sustaining progress. By focusing on youth, helping them access the knowledge and resources they need to thrive psychologically, socially, economically, and politically so they are able to find peaceful solutions to their challenges. By educating youth the civic engagement , how to advocate for their rights will support to reduce violence.
Through the project SALAM Will work with communities to manage conflicts before they escalate. Building a cornerstone of peacebuilding work which is strengthening relationships and building trust within and across communities, identity groups, and authorities so that they are able to work together to achieve common goals. The project also provide training in interest-based negotiation and mediation so conflicts can be resolved locally and peacefully.
Through the project SALAM will work to transform the norms, attitudes, and institutions that enable conflict. This includes a sharp focus on strengthening good governance practices. The project will achieve thus by helping local governments and communities work together to tackle some of the biggest drivers of conflict, like shrinking resources from climate change and economic inequality, and develop long-term solutions.
the project will work with the community and stakeholders to ensure that the Community members collectively create change by taking ownership of identifying needs, developing interventions, and challenging power dynamics. the project will increase the ability of the local partner to bring together diverse stakeholders in a common process and identify the stakeholder's needs and how to manage their expectations and needs. by building trust between community members, the community will work together to select their needs and accept each other. the focusing on the community voice and skills, Diverse community members will be better equipped, and working with local leaders to foster peaceful, community-led change.
Project Goal: Community members collectively create change by taking ownership of local development processes (common challenges), expanding inclusion of marginalized groups, enhancing gender-just, nonviolence,and inclusive peace led, and fostering the development of stronger social relationships and non-traditional leadership.
The project outcomes
#Men and women, girls and boys in the community have increased capacity and knowledge to challenge local power dynamics, improve local governance and social structures
#Enhanced leadership by young women and men in the delivery of inclusive, gender-responsive peacebuilding, violence reduction, and social cohesion initiatives.
#Local markets, services and communities have an enhanced ability to cope with shocks, meet long term needs.

Policy and Code of Conduct: Salam Organization will develop and implement a clear PSEA policy and code of conduct that explicitly outlines expected behavior, zero-tolerance for any form of sexual exploitation and abuse, and the consequences for violations. All staff, volunteers, and partners involved in the project will be required to adhere to this policy.2. Training and Capacity Building: Comprehensive training on PSEA will be provided to all project staff, mobile unit staff, and volunteers. This training will focus on raising awareness about sexual exploitation and abuse, identifying and responding to potential risks, and understanding their role in preventing and addressing such incidents.3. Reporting Mechanisms: A robust reporting mechanism will be established to ensure that individuals can safely and confidentially report any concerns or incidents of sexual exploitation and abuse. Clear channels of communication, such as hotlines or designated focal points, will be established to receive and respond to reports promptly.4. Complaints Mechanism: Salam Organization will establish a complaints mechanism that allows individuals, including beneficiaries and community members, to report incidents of sexual exploitation and abuse. The complaints mechanism will ensure that all reports are taken seriously, thoroughly investigated, and appropriate action is taken to address the situation and provide support to survivors.5. Community Engagement: Engaging with the community is essential for effective PSEA. Salam Organization will actively involve community leaders, women's groups, and local organizations in raising awareness about sexual exploitation and abuse, promoting community ownership of prevention efforts, and fostering a safe and supportive environment for women and girls.6. Monitoring and Oversight: Regular monitoring and oversight will be conducted to ensure the implementation of the PSEA strategy. This will involve periodic assessments, internal audits, and external evaluations to assess compliance, identify gaps, and make necessary adjustments to strengthen the PSEA measures.
