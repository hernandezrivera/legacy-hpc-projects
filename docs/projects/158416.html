Project Summary

Cyclone Kenneth made landfall on 25 April in Cabo Delgado Province. Almost 250,000 people were reported affected by the impact of new cyclone. In total, according to DPEDH, 147 schools for a total of 480 classrooms, of which 189 destroyed, 897 teachers and 41.694 students are affected. Children have lost their homes, been displaced, and in some cases lost family members, friends, classmates and teachers. Three month after the disaster, the effects of the cyclone continue to limit the access to education increasing the risk of school drop-out. In the project’s targeted area, due to the lack of accessible safe schools, the education activities are carried out in 3 shifts or more. The main needs identified are:
1. Insufficient safe and protective space for educational activities: original spaces of the school buildings are not enough to accommodate safely all the students in the areas.
2. Lack of teaching and learning materials and furniture: because of the cyclone in the schools there is scarcity or lack of adequate furniture and didactic material. Schools need to be equipped with teaching materials, while the vulnerable families don't succeed in facing the purchase of the didactic material.
3. Poor quality of education: teachers have not the proper skills to deal with children who have been exposed to stress and trauma. There is high need to improve skills and update didactical approaches to allow them to serve vulnerable children.
