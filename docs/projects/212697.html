Project Summary

Project Overview
The project aims to repair 480 desks in three vocational schools in Rural Damascus. It serves as a dual-purpose initiative: providing practical training and skill-building for vocational school students and contributing to the rehabilitation of educational infrastructure in the region.
Justification
1. Enhancing Educational Infrastructure
• Need for Functional Facilities: Many schools in conflict-affected areas like Rural Damascus suffer from damaged or insufficient infrastructure. Repairing desks directly contributes to creating a more conducive learning environment.
• Impact on Learning: Functional and comfortable classroom furniture is essential for an effective learning environment. Improved facilities can lead to better educational outcomes.
2. Capacity Building for Students
• Practical Skill Development: Engaging vocational school students in the desk repair process allows them to apply their skills in a real-world context, bridging the gap between theoretical learning and practical application.
• Job Readiness: This project equips students with hands-on experience, enhancing their employability and job readiness in a region where opportunities for practical training might be limited.
3. Community Engagement and Empowerment
• Student Involvement: Involving students in the rehabilitation of their own schools fosters a sense of ownership, responsibility, and pride in their contributions.
• Community Impact: The project also serves as a model for community engagement and empowerment, demonstrating how local resources and talents can be mobilized for community betterment.
4. Sustainability and Resource Optimization
• Resource Efficiency: Utilizing the skills of vocational students for desk repairs is a cost-effective approach, maximizing the use of local resources and reducing the need for external funding and materials.
• Sustainable Practices: The project encourages sustainability by repairing and reusing existing furniture rather than discarding and replacing it, aligning with environmentally responsible practices.
5. Supporting Education in Conflict-Affected Areas
• Resilience in Adverse Conditions: By focusing on school rehabilitation in a conflict-affected area, the project contributes to the resilience and continuity of education, a critical factor in long-term recovery and development.
• Alignment with Educational Goals: The project aligns with broader educational goals in Syria, addressing the urgent need for improved educational facilities and practical vocational training.
6. Multiplier Effect
• Skill Transfer: Students involved in the project can transfer the skills learned to their communities, potentially leading to further repair and construction projects, thus amplifying the project's impact.
Conclusion
This project is not only a practical initiative to improve school infrastructure but also a strategic investment in the skills and empowerment of vocational students in Rural Damascus. It embodies a holistic approach to educational and community development, addressing immediate needs while building capacity and resilience for the future.
Gender, Equity and Sustainability - Gender, Equity:
KHA will ensure the equitable participation of youth and adolescents, both girls and boys in all activities, as the activities will take into consideration the different gender and age groups.
Furthermore, during the selection of young facilitators, both men and women will be included.
The targeted groups are both IDPs and host communities’ youth and adolescents. The beneficiary identification process will ensure gender equity and representation

KHA promotes risk protection through:1- Protection Risk Analysis: Cultivate a safety-oriented ethos by integrating safety protocols into the repair procedures for school desks. Students will be instructed on adhering to established safety guidelines, utilizing appropriate protective gear, and fostering open communication with team members to prevent accidents and injuries. Encouragement will be given for students to take personal responsibility for their safety and that of their peers, promoting a proactive stance towards risk management.Establish a first aid response protocol outlining procedures for reporting injuries, accessing first aid supplies, and seeking medical assistance when necessary. Students will be trained on the proper use of first aid kits and emergency response equipment, ensuring that they can quickly address minor injuries or provide initial care before professional medical help arrives. Regular drills and simulations will reinforce students' readiness to handle medical emergencies in a timely and organized manner.2- PSEA: KHA has its own PSEA politics which is implemented in all KHA projects.
