Project Summary

This project is aims to contribute to addressing the needs of the most vulnerable people affected in White Nile state in addition to affected IDPs by the conflicts in Blue Nile, due to OCHA more than 90,000 displaced to White Nile localities in 2022 during the bloody tribal events erupted recently, in a context of increasing inter-communal violence, as illustrated by the massive clashes . 60% of the IDPs HH stated in Kosti and Rabak Localities in addition of most vulnerable host communities in these localities and Tandalti.
The project intended to address the Food in-security and livelihood, Protection and provision of lifesaving services for IDPs in Kosti, Rabak, and Elduiem localities . Women empowerment is critical for their protection being left exposed to risk of sexual and gender based violence, harassment, and discrimination. Enhancing protection environment for women, children( targeting 17605 to have safe access to education),provision of life skills intervention for children, youth, and disables, reinforce PSEA through strengthening community based protection mechanisms awareness and engagement with key groups such as adolescent girls, persons with disabilities, to strengthen safe and confidential entry points to disclose SEA and request assistance, through Protection Women/Child Friendly safe space multipurpose, livelihood activities trainings, Psychosocial support and counselling BPWVO will provide protection services to decrease risks of sexual violence, and uphold the safety and dignity of affected people, to protect the most-at-risk including victims/survivors of SEA.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this intervention, BPWO is committed safeguard Right holders from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. BPWO will implement the project in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance during assessment, design of project and delivery of services. The project team will ensure all elements of accountability to affected population to be captured through extensive engagement and consultation with the community. Project is designed to inform all displaced about the availability and location of services. The project ensures safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of services. The intervention will also carefully apply Do No Harm approach to avoid any negative impact that it might bring to the area or the targeted communities. Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. The assistance and protection measures will not undermine local capacities for self-protection as well as support the efforts of the affected population and local communities to find security and restore dignity. Project intends to respond to the root causes of the protection concerns in the area. To achieve this, BPWO embraces community based protection approach, for building capacity of local communities, service providers and government officials. Their capacity will be enhanced in analyzing & finding solutions to protection problems, inclusive of reducing threats& risks, reducing vulnerability of affected population and increasing their capacity to cope with protection problems following exposure to shock and disaster.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

BPWO ensures community participation through formation of protection networks which includes community representatives from men, women, and youth groups. They are continuously consulted during design and implementation of interventions on a monthly basis. Key methods used are focus group discussions and individuals consultations. BPWO has adequate experience in involving beneficiaries in implementation Right holders of the project. Feedback and Complaint Mechanism: BPWO will consult communities on establishing a viable complaint mechanism. Complaints will be validated through initial screening processes; an investigation will take place in case of valid and serious cases. BPWO has well trained staff in complaints handling. BPWO has developed a comprehensive CRM policy with clear guidance, SoPs and formats to ensure better accountability and transparency. Targeted communities and CBPNs members will be informed and trained on complain format.BPWO has well trained staff in complaints handling. BPWO has developed a comprehensive Complain and Response Mechanism and Physical and Sexual Abuse and Exploitation Policies with clear guidance, Standard operations procedures and formats to ensure better accountability and transparency. Targeted communities and community Based Protection Networks members will be informed and trained on complain format. The committee will be assigned as focal points for conveying/communicating complaints and feedback regarding the project from the community to structures of project staff. A specific tool (Beneficiary Satisfaction Form) has been designed for this purpose. Term of Reference for these mechanisms/ was developed , process of receiving and responding to complaints and feedback will be developed as a simple written procedure, this will be coupled with a complaint box to enable submission of private, confidential or sensitive complaints.
