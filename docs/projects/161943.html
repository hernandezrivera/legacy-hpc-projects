Project Summary

To Continue LPG refill distribution among the 2500 Rohingya and 650 host communities HHs in 2020 and to increase knowledge on safe cooking practice improve health & reduce fire risk, improving Household living condition as well as to reduce environmental impact through distribution of LPG refill ,consultation with women's and youth groups, principally through training, workshops and learning events.

Needs

Already 100% of households at camp 23, Shamlapur has used cook with LPG. Now their need is to ensure continuous refills. Need monitoring they are use LPG gas easily or not for cooking purpose. Need to follow up safety and security measure door to door at targeted Rohingya and host community household.

Activities or outputs

Already have provided training on proper use of LPG cooking set for ensuring safety and security during distribution of LPG cooking set at first time for 2500 Rohingya women and 650 host community women. For increasing knowledge on safe cooking practice and improving health & reduce fire risk we have plan to arrange refreshers training and consultation with women’s and youth groups, training, workshops and learning events.Considering old aged,child,single women, different able,lactating & pregnant women, providing cylinder carrying service from distribution center to a safe require place of beneficiaries.
