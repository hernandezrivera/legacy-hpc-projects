Project Summary

WASH needs, mainly access to clean safe water for drinking, hygiene and sanitation have continue to increase in scale and severity across IDPs, Returnees and vulnerable host communities North-East Nigeria. The complexity of needs due to the COVID-9 outbreak continues to impact on needs for hygiene promotion and awareness. Access to safe water has been constrained as a result of new and protracted displacement as well as returns (self-organized and government-led) resulting in host communities registering a reduced resilience due to high pressure on the existing and already overburdened water points and sanitation facilities. Water supply intervention will focus on 1) maintenance and operation/upgrading of exiting WASH facilities including hybrid boreholes and hand pumps in protracted congested IDP camps and host communities, 2) extension or construction of new facilities in support to IDPs camps, returned communities and vulnerable host communities. Access to sanitation facilities continues to be diminished due to wear and tear of emergency type construction, continuous displacements and seasonal flooding leading to high open defecation and reduced handwashing practices. Sanitation facilities needs for persons with special needs, women and children to ensure safety and equitable access are in critical gap as the sector which requires additional resources as other issues of proximity and design of the facilities are considered.
WASH and Health continue to work together on disease early warning and cholera prevention campaigns coupled with environmental hygiene to prevent high AWD cases and mitigate cholera outbreaks. The WASH sector has further rationalized partners operations to ensure more accountability to beneficiaries and better coverage of needs with reduced funding, which has improved the quality of the response.
The closure of IDP camps in Maiduguri and Yola metropolitans is resulting in high need for WASH services amongst IDPs and returnees this year and which will continue to mount pressure on the existing WASH facilities, mainly due to continuous/secondary displacement and the high number of expected returns.

1 - Needs

1 - Needs The WASH, shelter and settlement needs vary from location, including status – IDP, returnee or host community. The main needs across the IDP camps are; water, sanitation and hygiene promotion, NFI kits and shelter repair kits remained closely similar to returnees and some areas with vulnerable host communities. In summary; 1. Access to clean safe water: The level of services and gains need to be maintained for the operation and maintenance of the WASH facilities. Frequent breakdown of facilities requires round the clock maintenance for improved functionality, in Bama water supply has continue to improve but there still water stress as the result of returnees and new arrivals of IDPs to the Bama IDP camp, as a result of long queue, working distance as well lack of adequate water sources. In Michika and Madagali LGAs water stress (high number of returnees communities) results in seasonal cholera outbreak. 2. Access to Sanitation: The overstretching of WASH facilities in the camps and returnee's communities, resulting in high sanitation needs are persistent, with existing ratios of above 20 persons per 1 latrine still largely exceeded. The adverse weather, wear and tear of emergency design of facilities and high dislodging have forced vulnerable persons to resort to open defecation, crowding and associated protection issues around facilities. New arrivals increase pressure on existing facilities while a lack of policy shift and investment to improve designs have left thousands of sanitation facilities not functional. 3. Hygiene Promotion and WASH NFIs: Knowledge of disease prevention and practices associated with improved hygiene has increase, however the lack of resources and low-income households has continued to hinder implementation at household level. Hygiene awareness remains a gap in locations without WASH partner coverage. Besides, the constant need for NFIs due to factors of availability and capacity of IDPs to provide for self in the settlements come with negative coping mechanisms. Women and girls face critical shortages in MHM kits (Menstrual Hygiene Management) including soap for personal hygiene. The WASH core pipeline has further supported access to lifesaving NFIs but gaps remain in areas with high needs. WASH sector continues to work with all partners to ensure that IEC materials for environmental and personal hygiene awareness are available to the population. 4. Shelter and settlement: Emerging needs of shelter and settlements reported by CCCM and shelter sector due to various factors, such as new arrivals, shelter decongestion, fire out-break and old caseload to receive appropriate NFI assistance Affected people IDPs, returnees and host communities without adequate shelter solutions to receive appropriate assistance.

2 - Activities

The project will continue to support protracted IDPs in congested camps, returnees communities and most vulnerable host communities in Borno and Adamawa with assistance in water supply, sanitation and Hygiene, Shelter and NFIs Activities will include: 1) Improve access to long-term safe water facilities and services as per sector’s standards through new construction and rehabilitation of water supply systems WASH: 2) Provide Assistance to water systems’ functionality by sustained operation and maintenance services (e.g. boreholes, pumps, solar panels, overhead tanks, piped networks, taps, chlorination including monitoring of free residual chlorine) 3) Support IDPs in camps having access to gender-segregated sanitation facilities and services (latrines, showers, handwashing stations) as per sector’s standards 4) provide to community-informed hygiene messages promoting hygienic attitudes, behaviors and practices, including, Menstrual Hygiene Management 5) Provide basic hygiene items including regular top-ups to IDP and vulnerable host communities. Shelter & NFIs: 1) Provide Emergency Shelter Assistance to conflict affected households 2) Provide assistance in Transitional shelter to conflict affected households 3) Provide settlement NFI assistance conflict affected households.
