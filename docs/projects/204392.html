Project Summary

The GBV response will focus on hotspot areas, at risk areas and new IDP hosting sites in Sudan / South Darfur State. Additional capacity building and new partnerships are required to set up multispectral comprehensive services aligned with the GBV Minimum Standards. The response will focus on the most vulnerable 4 million women, the adolescent girls and vulnerable groups, including persons living with disabilities and survivors of SEA, among displaced people, returnee and host community settings in the prioritized geographic locations of conflict-affected Sudan.
Priority interventions include GBV case manage-ment, specialized psychosocial support and counseling, distribution of dignity kits, including menstrual supplies, and using women centres,including temporary or semi-permanent structures, as service delivery points, referrals and empower-ment opportunities.
The GBV Area of Responsibility will prioritize devel- opting/updating referral pathways in coordination with local actors including women-led organizations and community networks by mainstreaming GBV risk mitigation measures into their activities.
The GBV in emergencies response will focus on service provision to survivors of all forms of GB
GBV is one of the most important sectors in humanitarian field and by provision of protection services will open the path for other humanitarian services, Sudan is one of the countries, that facing a big problems in its security situation which caused different obstacles in providing humanitarian assistance for vulnerable populations, since the last conflict and clashes between RSF and SAF on 15th of April 2023 in Khartoum and different States that resulted in a widespread destruction of public facilities, properties, losing of lives and deterioration on Security situation. Also, the life cycle is stopped due to lack of the basic services,
South Darfur and Central Darfur State was one of the affected States that suffered from the armed conflict and this affection resulted in looting of properties, Women’s Violation, killing of civilians and thousands wounded, among them (women, children, men, youth, and elderly people, PSN). Since then, almost two months and there is no change in the situation therefore the assumption of high risk is remained because of escalation of the two conflicting parties. The project interventions will empower the GBV services in SD/CD Darfur state; Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) is proposing to improve the provision life-saving activities and access to safe environment for people, thus increasing accessibility of affected population to live with dignity. This in turn will ensure a smooth and continuous flow, while keeping people away from the danger caused by conflict and expose to explosive bodies. The project proposed interventions will enable Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) to obtain the key objectives of protection services in emergency situation through integrated interventions targeting vulnerable communities in Kass Locality and Central Darfur State / Nitrite locality ,ECORD believes that tackling general protection issues through strengthening the capacity development of existing structures while increasing awareness among communities, and enhancing monitoring and linkages and integration to service provision for individual, households and community levels.
Through this proposed sector interventions, ECORD is hoping that by using a combined approach to solve the challenges faced by communities in Kass in South Darfur State and Central Darfur State / Nitrite unit , ECORD will provide much needed lifesaving services, and set a stable basis for their sustainability through community outreach, appointment and finally adoption of key lessons for better self-reliance. 1 - Needs

according to Humanitarian Response Plan that Vulnerable women, girls and at-risk groups in Sudan face heightened threats of GBV driven bythe conflict and subsequent displacement, as well as loss of income, non-availability of cash, skyrocketing prices for basic commodities, and limitedaccess to basic services and support.Displaced and conflict-affected women and girls face increased risk of GBV, including sexualviolence during displacement and in temporary accommodations, exacerbated by the increasedpresence of armed actors. Domestic violence risks have increased with limitations on mobility,increased stressors and decreased access to basic needs, as well as vulnerability to sexual and other forms of exploitation.Women, adolescent girls and at-risk groups on the move and crossing borders are exposed toincreased risks of GBV and exploitation. Unaccompanied adolescent girls and youth may be unable to cross the border due to a lack of documentation.New states hosting displaced people require urgent GBV response capacity. Additional needsare emerging in areas with pre-existing humanitarian crises and now hosting a significant numberof displaced persons.The elderly, persons with disabilities, survivors of sexual violence as well as sexual exploitationand abuse, and at-risk boys and men, may face additional challenges in accessing life-savingGBV services .Widespread gaps in access to life-saving essential services for GBV survivors have been severelyexacerbated in conflict-affected states. There is a critical shortage of supplies across Sudan,including Rape Treatment Kits and Dignity Kits including menstrual hygiene, due to ongoing hostilities in Sudan’s main procurement hub, Khartoum.To overcome access challenges, especially in conflict-affected areas, assistance will be provided in different formats, including through an e-platforms (remote case management and Psychological Support Services), mobile and static service delivery points, mobile teams for hard-to-reach communities and state level hotlines when feasible.The GBV Area of Responsibility will focus on accel-erated capacity building and refresher training. Base on HRP at least 700 civilians have been killed and over 5,500 have been injured because of the conflict between SAF and RSF, as of 13 May. Active fighting is taking place in densely populated areas violating International Humanitarian Law principles of discrimination and proportionality without due consideration to the safety of the civilian population. There have been widespread indiscriminate attacks targeting civilian infrastructure including markets and hospitals, as well as looting of homes, belongings, and humanitarian offices/assets in different parts of Sudan. Criminality is reportedly increasing. This portrays the breakdown of the rule of law and protection of civilian mechanisms. Similarly as the situation is terrible in Khartoum and may States, also South /Central Darfur states is affected in the ongoing armed conflict and clashes and as a result of the fighting many civilians properties have been looted, Organization’s Offices and stocks were totally looted, governmental institutions were destroyed, which made the situation of humanitarian actors very difficult to provide services to vulnerable and most affected population, moreover there were civilians displacement from their locations to other locations West Jabal Marra locality in central Darfur state , the displacement spread to include some SD/ CD Darfur localities such as Kass locality and other areas (West jabal marra in CD), which caused the population to be in IDPs camp and other left their homes to other safety places. Also the clashes resulted in spreading of reckless shootings that killed many civilians and other were seriously injured, in addition to houses damages and burning of properties, all these factors added the load on the economic situation which resulted in deterioration of the most vulnerable and affected .

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project aim is to enhance the GBV of population affected by the crisis through community-based and individually targeted Health interventions; The project aims at strengthen the delivery of GBV services and interventions for women’s and men’s with a focus on those most at risk of violence, exploitation and abuse in conflict affected locations, Increasing access to safe community spaces to empower women and children; Mental health and psycho-social service provision for Conflict-Affected person’s ; improving Psycho social resilience through Social Capital for IDP's and host communities, ECORD will provide the following activities in this project cycle:1- Support women & girls with startup capital for Income Generating Activities and vocational skills2- Conduct trainings on GBV for specialized GBV service providers3- Provide dignity kits4- Capacity Building- Conduct trainings for community members involved in GBV prevention and response5- Conduct community based awareness raising sessions6- Capacity Building - Conduct trainings on GBV for Non-GBV service providers
