7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although all Syrian population has been heavily affected by the current crisis, due to traditional local norms, women are exposed to additional protection related risks. All types of violence are cited as occurring in opposition areas of Syria, with domestic violence, sexual exploitation and child marriage as top three. While sexual exploitation is somehow new, child marriage and domestic violence are often normalized and ingrained in the culture. Women and girls are exposed to structural and societal risks as the lack of safe spaces and public transportation, limited freedom of movement, poverty, unemployment, displacement, stress associated to war, gender role change, lack of documents. All these poses enormous pressure, men feeling they failed in protecting and securing a good life for their families. With men perceiving women as a threat to their control due to gender roles change, domestic violence has increased in Syria. Whereas men actions are explained as a result of stress, women are often blamed for provoking the violence or allowing it to happen. Parental violence against children has also been reported as increasing. Displacement and living in camps are noted as being particularly unsafe environments for women/girls due to the lack of privacy (in tents or in latrines), overcrowding and mixing of people, poverty, financial desperation, and lawlessness. The WoS Protection analysis reveals that 17 subdistricts in Idleb and 23 In Aleppo are facing severe.
