Project Summary

The project will provide nutrition services for 8,400 PLWs including 2000 adolescents, and 18000 children under 5 years (including 1800 PWDs). Through this project, MWL is going to have three interventions:
1st : ** Scaling up integrated nutrition response support through the Rapid Response teams which will be dispatched to the areas of high IDP flow: ( Afrin sub-district has 188,058 population, Al-Bab sub-district has 201, 298 population in Aleppo governorate, (Dana and Salqin sub-districts has about 718,813 populations in Idleb governorate), to provide the following activities:
-Scaling up integrated nutrition response support through the Rapid Response teams which will conduct MUAC screening for 18000 children 6-59months, and 8400 for pregnant and lactating women in (Afrin, AL Bab sub-districts in Aleppo governorate, (Dana and Salqin sub-districts in Idleb governorate), referral and follow up who are malnourished.
-Disseminating messages on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, for caregivers including PLWs.
- Distributing micronutrients for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and PLWs.
-Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutrition screening.
2nd: ** Continue functioning of the OTP-SFP services in Afrin and Salqin sub-districts through 15 of the RRTS that are existed in Salqin and Afrin, where OTP-SFP services for cases suffering from severe acute malnutrition (SAM) or moderate acute malnutrition (MAM). In addition to MUAC measurement, odema measurements and appetite test. etc.
3rd: Capacity building of 50 frontline nutrition services providers on IYCF-E and CMAM programs in the context of COVID-19.
As well as, MWL will train the caregivers on MUAC and edema screening, referral, recognize signs of deterioration.
4th: Cash voucher assistance for Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs) and care-takers of children under 2.
MWL will coordinate closely with the cluster to ensure no overlapping with other partners in the same area through regular reporting, attending the cluster meetings, and emails.
MWL MEAL staff will follow-up regularly on project activities .MEAL staff will set an M&E plan as per to project log-frame. Regarding the reporting, the M&E plan would develop outlines data collection methods (interviews, Focus Groups Discussion and observation, data sources, timeframe, responsibilities, and dissemination procedure).
MWL is very aware about the accountability to affected people, where the design phase will include focused- group discussions with vulnerable women, PWDs, and Adolescents to determine their needs and highlight their gaps to included it within the project. Also, during the implementation until closing, MWL will make sure their full access to complaints and feedback through adapted mechanism such: Email, hot line, and meetings directly with them through MEAL staff.

3 - Needs

Based on HNO 2023, the depreciation of the SYP and rising costs of goods, combined with the widening gap between incomes and expenditure, have a negative impact on the real and perceived food security of populations across the country, and on reported needs for food and nutrition assistance. Approximately 5.9 million people including 3.75 million children (2.2 million girls and 1.55 million boys) and 2.1 million women are in dire need of nutritional assistance in Syria, an increase from 5.5 million people in 2022. The PIN figures have shown an increasing trend since 2016 indicative of a fast-deteriorating nutrition situation.Syria is faced with multiple layers of malnutrition: rising levels of chronic malnutrition evidenced by a high prevalence of stunting; increasing levels of acute malnutrition; and emergency levels of anaemia among children and women of reproductive age. An estimated 609,979 children below five years of age are stunted in Syria, with stunting prevalence ranging from 25 per cent to 28 per cent across different geographical areas. Approximately 544,752 Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) suffer from MAM, including 460,124 PLW suffering from anaemia. Similarly, an approximate 25 per cent (721,388).

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

PSEA Risk: Violate PSEA or CSG, CoC principles. mitigating measures: - Signing PSEA and child safeguarding MWL’s policy by all staff. - getting training on PSEA for all staff. - PSEA awareness through brochures, videos. - Assigning complaint mechanism for SEA issues. - policies of Code of conduct, PSEA, child safeguarding is mandatory for all staff and vendors working with MWL. Accountability Officer in the field will ensure all beneficiaries are aware of MWL complaints and feedback mechanism, which will be in place. The mechanism will include fixed and mobile complaint boxes, WhatsApp number and hotline that will be handled by Accountability Officer. The officer will compile all complaints/suggestions or feedback and share them with the related Project Manager to be addressed. Urgent complaints will be shared with PM in 24 hours. CSG and PSEA complaints will have a different channel as there are two dedicated emails through which the CSG and PSEA Focal Points in Turkey Office can be reached directly. In addition, the Accountability Officer will introduce the project activities to local communities so that they will be aware of Project implementation time, locations, types of Project services and selection criteria.
