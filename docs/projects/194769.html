Project Summary

RCDO is a National NGO is proposing in 2023 Humanitarian appeal an emergency livelihood support intervention in Western Equatoria state. The proposed project runs from January 2023 to December 2023 and plans to support 20,000 Households (140,000 Beneficiaries) The key objective of the project is to reduce vulnerability and increase resilience amongst Vulnerable household access through support to increased food production and to reduce the level of vulnerability amongst malnourished children through linkage of nutrition related activities to Food security activities.
The project is line with HRP strategic objective 2 (SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2. The programmatic delivery approach will be direct distribution targeting mainly IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host community in Tambura. A total of 140,000 people will be targeted Livelihood input distribution, target women, girls, men & boys, elderly, disabled.
In addition, the project has major consideration for the accountability to the affected population, as it will work through Accountability to affected community AAP committees. Feedback mechanisms will be established in the community so that beneficiaries are well informed about the project's actions the project will integrate or work closely with both the protection cluster and the nutrition cluster. With the nutrition project, the project will set up demonstration sites at OTP centers and train households with malnourished children. These households will also be supported with high nutritious vegetables which they will produce to address the nutritional needs of their malnourished children.

1 - Needs

With 8.9 million people in need which include 8.54 million South Sudanese women, men, girls and boys . Two million people are internally displaced within the country; of these 1.4 million require urgent support, and 1.8 million are returnees, of these 1.2 million have urgent needs (UNOCHA 2022), the Humanitarian need in South Sudan is overtly higher than the preceding years. High Food insecurity needs has exacerbated to this high threshold of the Humanitarian situation. The high food insecurity are covariate climatic, conflict and economic shocks (such as floods and dry spells, inflation and rising food prices an the continued disruption of livelihoods due to violence or fear of violence and household level idiosyncratic stressors (such as death of bread winner, gender -based violence, morbidity and mortality due to poor hygiene and lack of access to essential services) which are greatly amplified by the continuous effect of shocks . According to Integrated Food Security phase report, food consumption gaps are extreme and people have largely exhausted Emergency coping strategies. In Tambura Country, the high food insecurity was exacerbated by the tribal conflict which left more than 80,000 people displaced in Tambura. This specific displaced number of people missed all the period of cultivation in 2021 and had to rely on desperate Food assistance from humanitarian workers. In Tambura, the high number of IDPs and returnees to the area of origin have put much more stress on the community that have led to exhaustion of the coping strategies. The most affected are children, people with disabilities, older persons and Women headed households that are identified as the most vulnerable to increase in Food insecurity. According to WFP, 19 percent of the 3.1 million caseloads receiving Emergency food assistance from January to October, 2021 are internally displaced people (IDP’s analysis, the main factors affecting food security are food availability, access, utilization, stability and seasonality. These are influenced by other sectors such as nutrition, water and sanitation and protection.

2 - Project activities

The key activities for this project include: Formation and training of Accountability to affected Population committee, distribution of main agricultural seeds crops, vegetables, fishing and tools kits. Promotion of resilience light vegetables e.g., setting up the demo plots and fishing kits training; provision of timely food.Provision of key information and key messages that will help reduce the impact of COVID-19 awareness and other risks diseases.
