Project Summary

Persistent and overlapping organised violence throughout 2021 increased the scale, scope, and complexity of civilian needs in greater Tonj, resulting in hundreds of casualties, the displacement of tens of thousands of people, widespread loss of livestock, disruption to agricultural and pastoral livelihoods, and the destruction of civilian property and humanitarian facilities and assets. By August 2021, communities in greater Tonj were hit by a second round of unprecedented and devastating floods. The cumulative effects of conflict and flooding left Tonj North, South, and East facing Emergency Phase 4 levels of food insecurity according to the Integrated Phase Classification (IPC) with 5%-10% of the population projected to face Catastrophic Phase 5 levels of food insecurity until July 2022. The dry season saw increased raids by armed cattle keepers from Unity and Lakes,raising the level of insecurity in the greater Tonj and mostly in Tonj East where needs are more . The purpose of this intervention is to provide life saving services to conflict and flood affect communities of Warrap State through provision of safe drinking water hygiene and sanitation service integrated WASH programming with nutrition.This response aim at reaching out to 32,462( 8,440 Male ,5,519 female ,9,414 boys and 9,089 girls ) in hard to reach areas of Tonj East through drilling of permanent water points, rehabilitation of permanent water points ,capacity build of water users committee ,construction of sanitation facilities in health/OTPs Centers , training of Nutrition volunteers at health facilities and as well as hygiene promoters . This program will reach out to pregnant lactating women at OTPs through distribution of WASH NFIs. Water for South Sudan Foundation implement this project in collaboration with the State ,County authority ,local communities and as well as WASH actors on ground . Water For South Sudan Foundation has responsibility to ensure that this program generate meaningful and relevant results and outcomes for girls, boys, women and men, in accordance with their specific needs, priorities and preferences. Water For South Sudan Foundation will involves working in ways to protect, facilitate and enable them to exercise their rights, including the right to safe, fair, equitable access to quality services and accurate, reliable and relevant information, the right to share their views and opinions about the quality and effectiveness of program, and participate in decisions that affect them. Water For South Sudan Foundation will build relationships of trust between Water For South Sudan Foundation Staffs and vulnerable people and communities, based on mutual respect, transparency and two-way communication and engagement. Water For South Sudan Foundation will also include people with disability in it program by ensuring that their needs are capture during the planing ,implementation and during monitoring and evaluation in order to make sure that they are factors in the design of the project components

1 - Needs

A deterioration of people’s physical and mental wellbeing, living standards and coping mechanisms is expected in 2022. Findings from the various data review processes indicate that 8.9 million people are estimated to be in need of humanitarian assistance in 2022, compared to 8.3 million in 2021. This increase is largely driven by compounded shocks triggered by continued conflict, widespread flooding, deepening food insecurity, inflation, high food prices, and lack of access to basic services. More people are likely to experience severe food insecurity in 2022. According to humanitarian need overview (HNO) ,about 8.3 million people, including refugees, are expected to experience severe food insecurity at the peak of the 2022 lean season May-July. This represents 7% increase from the 7.7 million in 2021 and the most counties affected are in the states of Jonglei, Lakes, Unity and Warrap mostly Greater Tonj . In 2022, the precarious situation is expected to be further exacerbated by the country’s increasing humanitarian caseload, the expected increase in the duration of needs in the most affected areas and the projected further reduction in humanitarian assistance across the country. Food assistance is expected to decrease by 10 - 20% from the already reduced levels in 2021. Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) prevalence above the emergency threshold of 15% was reported in people in twenty-six counties in six states which Warrap state is among the states that are affected . According to the humanitarian needs overview (HNO) shared by the WASH Cluster, about 140,149.6 people are going to be affected particularly in Tonj east in 2022 ,where 29% ,28% 26% and 17% respectively are boys ,girls men and women from the age of 5-60 years old . A continuation and increase in sub-national violence reported in Tonj East County , will likely continue to disrupt livelihoods and trade which contribute to an increase in humanitarian needs in 2022 and will affect people carrying out their routine activities, which further exacerbated due to flooding that limits livelihood opportunities in Tonj East. Throughout 2021, insecurity and flooding have simultaneously driven large-scale displacement and prevented vulnerable populations, particularly in the Greater Luachjang region of Tonj East, from undertaking displacement journeys to access food and services. Greater Luachjang communities have reportedly were unable to moved to the north, east or south due to tensions with communities in the neighbouring counties of Tonj North, Mayendit Unity State, and Rumbek North in Lakes State. Vulnerable groups also reportedly face substantial challenges displacing to the west, as this involves travelling through insecure areas where internally displaced persons (IDPs) have reportedly been robbed or killed.According to need assessments conducted by the partners in October 2021 in Tonj east , about 80% of the water points were not functioning ,20% functioning boreholes were congested with long queues due high number of people accessing the water points ,sanitation cover was low as 17% as witness by the the assessment team . Base on the record at OTPs about 80% of the children were malnourish and are in dire needs of nutrition intervention .Water For South Sudan Foundation with financial support from UNICEF ,NCA ,The Carter Center and Water For South Sudan international reached out over 45,350 individual with WASH services in schools ,health centre and in the communities through provision of safe drinking integrated with Nutrition .This propose intervention aim complimenting the gap by reducing the morbidity and mortality rate of water related disease and malnutrition rate of the needs through provision of safe drinking water in the community and health facilities ,increase access to safe sanitation through construction of latrines in OTPs and community ,increases level of awareness session in the community and at OTPs sites .

2 - Project activities

In response to the articulated needs analysis , Water For South Sudan foundation will response to such need with aim of reducing the morbidity and mortality rate of water related diseases and malnutrition children through WASH programming with Nutrition . This will be done in conjunction with nutrition program in order to curbs the growing needs of malnutritions and chronic WASH needs in the County . Water for South Sudan foundation with present in greater Warrap state will provide safe drinking water in OTPs through drilling of permanent water sources rehabilitation of a permanent water source in Nutrition facilities and in the community ,Construction of a semi-permanent sanitation source in nutrition site and in the community ,Construction of waste management structures in nutrition facility and at public places ,Distribution of water treatment product PUR, aquatab, filters and hygiene promotion sessions participatory interaction and Installation of hand washing facility with water and soap , provisions of the hygiene kits to SAM children . As part of the Sustainability plan, water For South Sudan Foundation will trained water management committee ,hand pump mechanic ,community base hygiene promoters and as well as nutrition volunteers on PHAST ,CHAST and etc.

Also water for South Sudan Foundation is child safeguarding policy will provide base for handling any PSEA or SEA related case .
