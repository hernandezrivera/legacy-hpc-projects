4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021, regarding Food security at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure population as 1.7 million people living in camps are deemed to be 100 percent food insecure, and 1.8 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity. SKT conducted a rapid need assessment (RNA) in 41 camps in Harim and Salqin locations. The methodology of data collecting included key informant interviews (KIIs) with camps managers, members of the administrative committees, sector experts, and representatives of local authorities (LA). The selection of key informants was based on the nature of each section in the assessment and took into consideration that the local authorities, community leaders and camps managers are commonly trusted resources for this information. As per the findings: 1- Almost all people in surveyed camps faced difficulties accessing enough food due to: - No resources/income to purchase food. - High bread prices. 2- A majority of people purchased on credit or borrowed to obtain bread. 3- Looking for income opportunities, sharing items, borrowing items, and taking credit / going into debt were the most common things people do when they could not meet their basic food needs. 4- When asked about their needs and priorities, the majority has defined bread bundles as their priority need. Based on the above, SKT will provide life-sustaining food assistance (GFA) to 8691 HHs

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- Risk: crowding of women during the distribution of bread.Likelihood: Low.Mitigation: SKT will hire females for the distribution process.2- Risk: Risk of harassment of women and girls by the humanitarian workers.Likelihood: medium.Impact: high. Mitigation: establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and enhancing accountability mechanisms besides training the staff to deal with beneficiaries in addition to providing workers with training on humanitarian standards and signing a code of conduct. 3- Risk: in the distribution area, we may face a risk of harassment for female beneficiaries by male beneficiaries.Likelihood: High .Impact: High. Mitigation: we will conduct separate distribution timed for males and females.4- Risk: Change in flour prices before starting or during project implementation.Likelihood: LowImpact: high.Mitigation Purchasing the full quantity of flour before the start of the project and storing it in a warehouse equipped with all supplies to ensure and guarantee the preservation of the quality of the flour.
