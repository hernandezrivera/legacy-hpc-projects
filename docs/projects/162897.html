Project Summary

This project will seek to support vulnerable host communities (HCs) and out of camp refugees in 8 (Kilikees, Shandi Foog, Um Alguara - Kanbo 4, kanbo Allafa, Alshate, Aldbaiba, Almagabi and Almakhalif) villages in El Jabalain locality by providing them with basic sanitation and hygiene services. The project will also target schools (provision of sanitation and hygiene facilities). White Nile state is host to over 247,555 (UNHCR South Sudanese Refugees dashboard of October 2019) South Sudanese Refugees (SSRs) and due to congestion, available WASH services are over stretched.
Project activities include will include:
- Provide and enable crisis affected and vulnerable people access to limited sanitation services through conducting hygiene and sanitation awareness campaigns, CLTS activities to discourage open defecation in the target 8 villages; construction of school latrines and support on school-based hygiene promotion activities.
- Provide and enable crisis affected and vulnerable people access to hygiene services Hygiene promotion through building capacity of the community and provision of hygiene kits and IEC materials, the community will be trained to undertake daily awareness raising among their community through different methods.

1 - Needs

ore than 8.5 2019 million people required food, nutrition, protection or other assistance to survive and millions of others struggled to make ends meet. These numbers are projected to be more in 2020. (UNOCHA Sudan situation report Nov 2019). Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview indicates that in 2020 there will be 6,187,285 people who will need humanitarian assistance. While conflict is still a major driver of humanitarian needs in Sudan, incidents have significantly reduced in recent years. The deepening economic crisis, the climate crisis and pockets of violence also continue to drive humanitarian needs. White Nile hosts over 247,555 SSRs (UNHCR SSR dashboard Oct 2019), refugee population who have limited livelihood opportunities; limited access to essential services such as water, sanitation, medicine and education. Increase in the cost of living due to high inflation has undermined HCs’ capacities and refugees, thereby exacerbating humanitarian situation of vulnerable SSRs and HCs in El Jabalain Locality. The situation has in addition made the vulnerable population to be dependent on support for basic needs such as food, water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and services. This inability to cover basic needs has also been accompanied by several knock-on effects, including increased tensions between SSRs and HCs, school drop-out of children, prevalence of child labour, early marriage, and sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) risks.According to a multi-agency (UNHCR, UNICEF, WFP and MoH) survey conducted in December 2017 on out-of-camp SSRs in White Nile State, refugee families were estimated at 11,003 and individuals calculated to 88,024 (average of 8 per family). This high number constrains accessibility to limited basic services to both the SSRs and HCs. The report indicates that sanitation coverage was low and open defecation commonly practiced. Use of latrines was reported in less than 30% of the communities with over 75% practicing open defecation. Poor hygiene was observed in most of the communities. People generally wash their hands but handwashing with soap was reported in less than half of the communities. There needs provides a compelling and urgent need to respond and provide WASH service to the vulnerable population.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Triggering of 8 communities (a total 160 people will be mobilised and sensitised on hygiene and sanitation); Triggering of 8 communities on CLTS processes and actions (target people will be mobilised and sensitised on hygiene, sanitation and CLTS processes); Training of 240 community volunteers (30 from each of the target village) on hygiene promotion; Establishment of 20 Sanitation Action Groups (SAGs) in the target villages to support in undertaking of the CLTS processes and actions interventions; Undertake 176 follow-up visits (22 per target village) by state and locality officials will provide additional monitoring support to the CLTS activities in the target villages; Construction of gender segregated latrines in 8 schools one block have four pits in each school; Establishment of 8 school-based hygiene and sanitation club ; Provision of Hygiene Kits to 10 school clubs; Undertake hygiene promotion massaging in 8 villages; Undertake community-based waste management (community mobilisation, solid waste collection, safe solid waste disposal; Production, dissemination of IEC materials with hygiene and sanitation massages; Support school with hand washing kit (Ibrig) to promote hand washing among school students; Conduct commemoration of global hand washing day (GHWD) celebration for the year 2020 in two selected villages.
