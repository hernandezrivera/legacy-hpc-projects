Project Summary

The project targets 2,500 (1,250 men and 1,250 women) vulnerable people (farmers, agro-pastoralists and pastoralists) in Al Qoz locality, South Kordofan. The project interventions will be conducted in in four villages; Alsundogya, Albshma, Albuglti and Algmez. The project will be focusing on agriculture in Albshma, Albuglti and Algmez and supporting pastoralists in Alsundogya.
This project aims at assisting the vulnerable households of IDPs, and poor host communities with agricultural inputs and livestock services to enable them to restore their farming activities and produce their own food during the coming season and protect their livestock against major epidemic diseases. The project interventions will also focus on increasing women income through diversified livelihood by provision of Income Generation kits and support women to form saving and loan groups. These interventions are expected to reduce hunger and sufferings as well as contributing to lessening external dependency on food aid and protect livelihoods. In addition, the capacities of the community will be strengthened to minimize risk/hazard to shocks, through DRR training as part of the project interventions.

Needs

Due to the displacement as the result of the conflict in South Kordfan, the targeted communities in Al Qoz locality are unable to maintain secure livelihoods as a result of the disruption of traditional livelihoods patterns. This has resulted to; loss of assets, disruption of social services, collapse of social networks, lack of access to economic opportunities, limited access to safe water and health services, and few schools with poor infrastructure. The locality has acute levels of poverty with women mostly affected particularly those heading households.Due to weather variability, farming households are unable to produce adequate food to sustain themselves and their families because poor rainfall reliability. In addition, the households have limited access to tools, seeds and skills for improved crop production. Pastoralist households are also unable to maintain healthy livestock to sustain themselves and their families because of livestock losses due to; preventable diseases, limited access to veterinary services and poor animal feeds.
