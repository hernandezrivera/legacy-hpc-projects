Project Summary

This project ensures the bridging between humanitarian response and development phases by addressing issues of PSEA/GBV with enriching ownership of the project by beneficiaries/community members as well as developing local capacity. With ultimate goal of the sustained protective environment for children and women against all form of violence, abuse and exploitation by the community members, the project will inform, train, advocate and involve various type of community people to be able to take their own role in their community to protect their own children and women (Nexus). This project targets a total of 37,142 people (2,232 girls; 1,440 boys; 25,267 women; 8,203 men)
Output 1: Survivors of GBV/PSEA have access to quality services for holistic assistance.
Output 2: Humanitarian and development actors in Mopti have increased knowledge on the principles for establishing appropriate PSEA reporting mechanism and coordination with the PSEA in-country network.
Output 3: Community awareness, referral networks and linkages to care for GBV/PSEA survivors are strengthened.
Output 4: The needs of beneficiaries and access to referral systems are well assessed, understood, and monitored with a participatory approach, and the quality of the project is monitored and evaluated with the feedback of beneficiaries.
