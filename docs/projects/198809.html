Project Summary

This project is designed to provide integrated health, nutrition, food security, and hygiene promotion services in NWS in Idlib and Aleppo for 2023.
The proposed project will reach 175,000 people (75.000 PLWS out of them 2000 PWD) and 100,000 CU5 (52,000 G, 48,000 B) out of them 5000 CWD) through supporting 6 Nutrition centers, and 23 nutrition teams / RRTs in addition to establishing 20 mother-baby areas. The nutrition centers and RRTs will provide IYCF and CMAM services through different modalities and will conduct the following center-based/outreach activities:
IYCF; Breastfeeding and lactation counseling, complementary feeding counseling will be provided for mothers and caregivers of children under two years, also a Provision of USD$20 top-up cash voucher for identified households to ensure they have access to recommended diet and nutrition for a period of 6 months.
CMAM; Screening and treating malnutrition cases (SAM and MAM) for PLWs and children under 5 that do not show signs of any medical complications, those with medical conditions are referred for support to the appropriate field partners, also distribute micronutrients for PLWS and children to address the micronutrient deficiencies among the PLWs and children. Distribute fortified blended foods to prevent stunting among the children in the targeted areas.
Mercy-USA will support Two stabilization centers to provide treatment for children with severe acute malnutrition with medical complications that would be referred from our RRTs and other nutrition partners.
Moreover,20 mother-feeding spaces will be established in 20 different camps in Maaret Tamsrin and Harim where the lowest proportion of Exclusive Breastfeeding is found that provides a safe space for lactating mothers to breastfeed their children. Each space will have 1 nutritionist and 2 CHWs who will be responsible for providing IYCF sessions and MUAC screening.
Also, 5,000 PLW will be provided with a $50 soft-conditional CVA for six months; this intervention will be linked to food security, health, and nutrition services to increase access to nutritious food, PNC, ANC, SAM treatment, and micronutrients tablet. Changing behavior activities will be conducted alongside cash distribution, one-to-one counseling, mother-to-mother support groups, and MIYCN messages.
This project will empower mothers and HH members through training to screen their children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes and check for Odema.
Nutrition supplies:
Mercy-USA will undertake nutrition supply distribution in NWS for the cluster partners; this activity will be coordinated closely with the UNICEF and cluster team.

3 - Needs

According to the preliminary 2022 SMART Survey report for Northwest Syria (NWS), 20% of children between 6-59 months are stunted (categorized as Serious/Severe on the global severity scale). The Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) rate by weight-for-height z-scores (WHZ) increased from 2.5% in 2021 to 3.3% in 2022, the Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) rate increased 0.8% to 1.3%. 11.8% of women of reproductive age (15 –49 years) and 12.95% of pregnant and lactating women and girls (PLWs) suffer from wasting (Alert/Stress categorization). Maternal malnutrition compounds the vicious inter-generational cycle of malnutrition and this impacts upon increased number of low-birth weight babies and higher prevalence of SAM amongst children between 6-59 months. 52.37% of women in reproductive age (WRA) and 36.18% of children 6-59 months suffer from iron deficiency anaemia. Blanket supplementary feeding programs for prevention of SAM and management of MAM cases in NWS are limited and this has contributed significantly to increasing number of SAM cases and relapse cases. In our targeted areas and according to surveillance data Sept 2022, Only 11.2% of children aged 6-23 months consume the minimum acceptable diet (MAD), and only 20.7% of children 6-23 months were reported as consuming diet from 5 or more food groups making them minimum dietary diverse (MDD) (Extremely Critical/Catastrophic scale).
