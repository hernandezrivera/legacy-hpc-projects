Project Summary

Created in June 2019, the Coordination of Humanitarian International NGO (CHINGO) sets out to promote coordination, information sharing, advocacy, and government/donor/UN engagement on relevant issues pertaining to humanitarian and early recovery needs in Cameroon. This is achieved through the fulfilment of the following objectives:
1. Foster cooperation, coalition-building and collaboration of INGOs within the Coordination group and outside, ensuring information sharing, lessons learned and best practice to effect principled and quality programming responses and operations in Cameroon.
2. Promote and protect principled and effective humanitarian action through consistent and high-level representation of INGOs in decision-making concerning humanitarian and early recovery interventions in Cameroon.
3. Promote a strong “representation” of INGOs in different fora and champion advocacy initiatives to enhance humanitarian response in support to policy that enhance compliance to both national and international instruments.
4. Strengthen linkages with national humanitarian NGO platforms and the INGO Forum.
In other to facilitate the work of its members to meet the humanitarian needs of the people of Cameroon in 2025, CHINGO as a coordination body will remain focused on a three folded approach: Internal coordination including information and experience sharing among members and observers External representation of INGOs-and Joint advocacy.
For the 2025 response plan, CHINGO intends to consolidate its position within the humanitarian coordination architecture. To this end, the forum will focus on strengthening coordination between its members and observers, through information sharing and more effective coordination at all stages of programming the response to needs in the three crisis hubs.
As part of its contribution to meeting the humanitarian needs of 2025, CHINGO will provide information-sharing, coordination, advocacy and capacity-building services targeting its members, their local implementing partners, government authorities and others. These services will aim to:
 Support the coordination of the response to New Emergencies at both national and field level,
 Contribute to the provision of data to facilitate humanitarian programming through joint data collection initiatives,
 Contribute to the improvement of humanitarian access through regular and adhoc consultations with government authorities, donors and other relevant actors depending on the situation (consultation frameworks, roundtables, conferences, workshops, etc.).
 Support the consideration of cross-cutting issues (gender, accountability, inclusion) to enhance the effectiveness of humanitarian programming.
 Support localization issues by building the capacity of local players in areas such as governance and coordination.
 Support the dissemination of the NEXUS HDP approach through a coordinated approach and the mapping of actors in target areas.
