Project Summary

The project aims at enhancing sustainable access to critical WASH services and strengthening resilience of IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host Communities in Somaliland for both urgent and long term WASH needs owing to the fact that the project targeted locations are yet to recover from impacts of successive seasons of below normal rains experienced across most parts of Somalia during the 2018 Deyr (October-December) season that was followed by harsh weather conditions during the dry 2019 Jilaal (January–March) season exacerbated by delayed and erratic 2019 Gu (April-June) coupled by limited recovery from the prolonged impacts of devastating 2016/2017 drought . Of concern is the fact that a number of water sources/boreholes and shallow wells are in dilapidated state and needs urgent rehabilitation while some permanent water sources/boreholes are experiencing over use and risk exhaustion and breakdown. The situation is compounded by huge populations of drought and conflict displaced that have settled in IDP camps that overstretch existing sanitation facilities thus compromising already precarious sanitation conditions and poor hygiene practices hence risk of AWD/cholera and other water borne diseases outbreak. The project targets a total of 18,000 beneficiaries (7488 women, 6912 men, 1800 boys and 1800 girls) across Somaliland.
