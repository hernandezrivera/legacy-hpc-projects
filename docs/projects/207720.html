Project Summary

The Live Safe aims to ensure that internally displaced persons in Cabo Delgado are safe and safely.
The project's target audience is internally displaced persons in the district of Mocimboa da Praia, Muidumbe and Pemba city.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The conflict in Cabo Delgado is increasing the risk of individuals being exposed to sexual and gender-based violence, child abuse, child labor, early and forced marriage and, consequently, recruitment into the armed forces and exploitation.One of the most worrying aspects is child labor and recruitment into the armed forces, with formal work considered very normal among children and adults in all the target districts and the latter identified as a risk measure by parents for children leaving school, including all at very young ages. At same the same time the host communities are facing numerous challenges such as insufficient resources overcrowded services and also the impact on the household level as most displaced people as same relatives and friends.The poverty is also another factor that contributes for driving recruitments and radicalization in the selected districts. Since most children and adolescents still faces serious problems in their lives, such as, lack of financial conditions, lack of food, decent houses etc.AMAMO intends to carry out the Live Safe project, the general aim of which is to ensure that internally displaced people in the districts of Nangade, Pemba, Mocimboa da Praia, Macomia and Muidumbe live safely.The specific objectives are to provide psychosocial support to displaced people in post-trauma situations; to provide individual protection assistance; to provide legal assistance; to provide assistance to child survivors of the conflict and to manage cases of gender-based violence.The target group is women, children and men, i.e., internally displaced people living in the above-mentioned districts.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Individual protection assistance - psychosocial services / MHPSS - districts of Miudumbe, Mocimboa da Praia, Nangade, City of Pemba and Macomia;Individual protection assistance - case management - City of Pemba, Nangade, Muidumbe and Mocimboa da Praia;Community based protection - risk reduction - districts of Miudumbe, Nangade and City of Pemba;Capacity building and mainstreaming for protection - districts of Muidumbe, Mocimboa da Praia, Nangade, Macomia and City of Pemba;Protection monitoring (equivalent system)/Community based protection monitoring - districts of Muidumbe, Mocimboa da Praia, Nangade, Macomia and City of Pemba;Legal assistance and counseling / access to justice - districts of Mocimboa da Praia, Muidumbe, Nangade, City of Pemba and Macomia;Law and policy including on Cluster - related matters - districts of Mocimboa da Praia, Muidumbe, Nangade, city of Pemba and Macomia;Law and policy including on Cluster - related matters - districts of Mocimboa da Praia, Muidumbe, Nangade, city of Pemba and Macomia;Individual protection assistance - In-kind and cash for protection - districts of Mocimboa da Praia, Muidumbe, Nangade, city of Pemba and Macomia.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will identify risks through: Analyzing the level of vulnerability of the target public. In order to prevent these risks from causing damage, the project will develop training/ capacity building for vulnerable communities in terms of self-sufficiency; providing assistance and referring their problems to the right people.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Involve communities in training for life skills;The project will encourage meaningful access to assistance and participation and empowerment by removing barriers to access and assistance.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

- Direct contact;- The project will identify people with disabilities through their particularities and specificities;- The project will develop specific skills training for the removal of barriers, empowering communities in life skills.

7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

- The project aims to preserve the environment;- It inhibits actions that pollute the environment;- The project develops actions aimed at empowering communities to recycle waste, such as recycling food and other unused materials;- The project provides for environmental mitigation and assessment measures.
