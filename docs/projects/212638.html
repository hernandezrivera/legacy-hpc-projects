Project Summary

The proposal aims to address the urgent and sustainable Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) needs in North West Syria for the year 2024.
Mercy-USA, in this project, will support the most vulnerable people in NW Syria including dignified shelter camps implemented by Mercy-USA in many components in Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Darkosh, Salqine, Ariha and Jandairis sub-districts:
Targeted beneficiaries in 10 sites for all activities: 10,868 (Male 4,630, Females 6,238)
Key components of the proposal include:
- Enhancing access to safe water and sanitation facilities for IDPs through immediate humanitarian response measures.
- Implementing emergency solid-waste management strategies to promote public health and environmental protection among IDPs.
- Providing essential WASH Non-food items (NFIs) on an emergency basis to meet the urgent needs of IDPs and the host population.
- Conducting comprehensive hygiene promotion campaigns and activities targeting both IDPs and the host community to raise awareness and promote hygienic practices.
Enhancing WASH facilities and services within healthcare facilities managed by Mercy-USA and utilized by IDPs and the host population, with close collaboration with the Health sector to ensure effective coordination.

- Implementing hygiene promotion, risk communication, and community engagement initiatives specifically aimed at populations affected by outbreaks, with a focus on improving WASH facilities and services within healthcare facilities utilized by these populations.
