Project Summary

CW focuses on enhancing access to WASH facilities through sustainable water supply systems and sanitation facilities. The programme aims to improve sanitation and hygiene management through hygiene promotion and capacity building to reduce waterborne diseases and integrating DRR across all the WASH initiatives. The programme is also introducing inclusive WASH interventions in menstrual hygiene management, hygiene promotion for children, and bio composting to address intestinal parasite.
