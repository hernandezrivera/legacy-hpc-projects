Project Summary

MICHAFOUND aims at reducing the problem of Education within some IDPs in the SWR of Cameroon. Targeted population is at 2500. These IDPs are as a result from the current Anglophone crisis ongoing in Cameroon for the past three years.
This project aims at providing assistance both on vocational trainings and onsite campus education thereby providing monthly fees as well as school (Books and others).
IDPs related to education as a problem based on data collected shall be called to secured venues, sensitization on various fields of studies as well as vocational training programs shall be put in place. This will be for both male and female for age groups 3-50 years.
Project execution will be done chronologically that is from one location to another for proper monitoring. Towns involve are Kumba, Buea, Dibanda, Tiko, Mutengene and Limbe.
Feedback will be collected in-house through short paper surveys for general reporting. This shall increase literacy level, enhance development and play positively in the minds of the vulnerable
Total budget is at 118,621 USD beginning 01/01/2020 – 31/12/2020
