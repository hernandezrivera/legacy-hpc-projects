Project Summary

The title of the project is 'Enlightening Hearts through the Basic Education and Life Skills'. The name of the appealing and implementing partner is the SAWAB (Social Agency for Welfare and Advancement in Bangladesh). The project will focus on early childhood development as well as sector guided education system i.e., grade one and two also ALP one and two for FDMN children. To facilitate these teaching and learning activities at least five schools will be set up in five camps in Ukhiya Upazila. Targetted students will be 130 in camp 7, 50 in camp 8East, 180 in camp 9, 180 in camp 18, and 50 in camp 19. Total students 590. About 40 teachers will be recruited and trained on pedagogy as well as early childhood development. Not only the students but also the community people especially the parents of the children will also be included under our learning intervention. The major components of the project will be schooling, extra-curricular activities and parenting. This project will span from January 2023 to December 2023. The intended results of the project will be as follows: a good number of students will be provided basic non formal educations as well as life skill educations. The teachers will be trained on quality education. The parents will be trained on parenting. The major activities of the project will be setting up schools, hiring and training up teachers, preparing school curriculum and extra curriculum. The entire project will focus on cohesion between host community and Rohingya community by arranging different platform of work and sharing learning.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

Rohingya children are one of the most vulnerable children in the world. They are deprived of fundamental opportunities e.g., food, education, health, safety and security and dignity of life. Among them deprivation from education is becoming more detrimental than any other deprivation. Because it helps to prolong their vulnerability. On the other hand proper education might be the most helpful for their emancipation. Only education could help them to raise their voice and ultimately making them able to fight back to return to their homeland. And this education should be qualitative and effective for ensuring a dignified life for the FDMNs.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

The project will fully align to the rationalization principles by adopting an integrated approach as well as ensuring predictability and continuity. About integrated approach we will report to the RRRC office and CiC office about updating project approval, implementation, reporting and attending every meeting arranged by govt. and non govt. coordination agencies. 3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

The beneficiaries and members of implementing agency will be protected of any kind of violation of their basic rights. The project will take initiative to ensure the safety and security of all stakeholders having followed the four principles of protection including prioritizing safety and dignity and avoiding causing harm, meaningful access to assistance and services, accountability mechanism and participation and empowerment. Flyers and leaflet will be distributed among the rohingya including project stakeholders on protection issues.

3.2 - Gender

Gender sensitivity and inclusiveness will be considered as an indispensable part in our entire project implementation strategy. To do this will provide awareness training and campaign to our beneficiaries and all stakeholders.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

People with all ages having stake in our project including child safeguarding will be considered strongly under this project. All stakeholders will be aware on child rights by training and counseling. Child protection principles including no discrimination, right to development, best interest of children and views of children will be mainstreamed properly.

3.4 - Diversity

People with gender based diversity won’t be victimized any way by any of our project intervention. People will be guided and motivated not to be violent against gender based identity. All diversified group will be treated respectfully especially LGBT group.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

The project will remain fully accountable to the affected people having included their voices in our project. Information, communication and transparency is a key to ensure accountability to affected people. We also make sure their participation, engagement and inclusion in decision making and implementing process. There should be a complaint and feedback response mechanism. Learning and adaptation will be another platform for ensuring accountability to affected people. We will arrange periodic review meeting with concerned affected community. To make sure that the voice of affected people is heard some regular meeting including FGD and courtyard meeting will be arranged.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

As it is an education project and we have some school buildings so these facilities could easily be used as shelter house during disaster time. The school building will be designed in such a way where facilities of disaster center will be available.
