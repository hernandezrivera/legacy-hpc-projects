1 - Needs

The latest report by UNOCHA reveals that the crisis in the North East Nigeria remains one of the most severe in the world. IOM (DTM ) Round 28, August- 2019 assessment report reveals that The most-affected State of Borno continues to host the highest number of IDPs at 1,483,566 recording an increase of over one per cent or 15,658 as against the number assessed during the last Round 27 DTM assessment. Lack of infrastructure and resources, damaged buildings, overcrowding, and misuse of education facilities have negatively impacted the quality and safety of educational environments. The crisis in Northeast Nigeria has further weakened an already fragile education system, resulting in increasing the number of out of school children. In Borno, Adamawa and Yobe around 40 percent of the displaced population is comprised of school aged children between three and seventeen years with Borno having high population of girls out of School, this was reported in (MICS 2016-17 Survey) as having the highest number of girls out of school in Nigeria at 71% with Yobe recording one of the highest rates of children out of school at 42.2% compared with 27.2% of the estimated children out of school in the whole of Nigeria. Education sector faces an upward task of infrastructural rehabilitation and expansion, education management stretching and the need to address emerging education needs such as skills acquisition and protection of education against attacks, across the three states. Children and teachers in all three affected states are in dire need of psycho-social support to cope with the negative effects of the conflict. There is an urgent need to engage the local communities in supporting education as a longer-term strategy for recovery and peacebuilding. Between October and November 2017, education sector partners conducted an education needs assessment across the three states. The findings indicated that poverty was identified by the respondents as the greatest barrier to access to education expressed as inability of parents to meet financial costs demanded in schools. Lack of infrastructure such as classrooms, school furniture and WASH facilities was also recorded as a critical need. Lack of teaching and learning materials was a major factor contributing to poor attendance and retention of learners in schools.

2 - Activities

Activities to be implemented to achieve the objectives and accomplish the goal are as follows: i) Conduct community sensitization fora on girls’ and conflict affected children non-school attendance in communities. They will provide the opportunity for the community people to be enlightened on government education policies and improve their knowledge on plight of the girl child and conflict affected children without basic education. ii) Organise meetings with PTAs and SBMCs in communities on identifying families with conflict affected girls and Children missing out of school. The aim is to work with the community education stakeholders to identify and profile families with conflict affected children missing out of school in the communities. iii) Hold dialogue meetings with families with girls not attending school. Through this intervention, the affected children in need of support will be identified in collaboration with their respective families. iv) Organise interaction forum with school management ( PTAs and SBMCs) to prepare for enrolment and integration of girls. This will offer an opportunity to know the number of schools and vacancies available for their enrolment. v) Provision of support and counselling to girls out of school to start or continue schooling. Number of girls supported to start or go back to school will be known, thereby facilitating appraisal of the project. vi) Establishment of temporary class rooms with appropriate WASH facilities vii) Enrollment of Boys and girls learners into schools viii) Provision of classroom equipment and teaching Materials ix) Emergency provision of learning supplies, degnity and hygiene kits, x) implementation of Early Childhood Development xi) Distribution of self learning materials for Boys and girls who are unable to access school xii)
