Project Summary

In line with cluster guidance, the objective of this response is to save and sustain lives by providing timely, targeted, and appropriate shelter assistance and relief items. PIN plans to provide different shelter service to meet the priorities and different needs in North-West Syria (NWS), this service contribute to the SNFI sector objectives of reinforcing and enabling a protective environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure through repairing the damaged housing and related community/public infrastructure, with dignified shelter and site upgrading.
PIN aims to ensure safe, dignified housing to affected populations, protecting them from environmental and protection risks and allowing them to cover basic needs and restore livelihoods. PIN will continue activities started in previous years, where needs are still observed in host and IDP communities, and will remain flexible to the needs presented through 2023-24.
PIN aims to support more sustainable housing solutions through improving housing and community infrastructure. PIN plans to conduct the following activities in NWS selected beneficiaries will be supported with minor and moderate damage (grade 1,2 and 3) rehabilitation to their houses in a continuation of private shelter rehabilitation, providing a durable solution to the chronic shelter needs of the population and reducing dependency on shelters of last resort after the EQ. The work is done through local contractors.
Also, PIN plans to target Idlib city with dignified shelter.
According to HNO 2024, 6.8M people need shelter and 6.2M people need NFI assistance. 42% of the Syrian population is living in substandard and damaged shelters. In 2024, the deterioration in economic conditions, depreciating Syrian pound and volatile security environment has contributed to the increase of NFI PIN to 5.7M, 9% from 2023. 3.2M people are living in last resorts cites across Northern Syria in substandard, damaged or inadequate housing.
