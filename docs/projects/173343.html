Project Summary

This project will address the educational needs of Rohingya Refugees Children in Camps 13, Cox`s Bazar. The aim of the project to provide basic education to 525 Rohingya refugee children aged 3-14 at Camp-13 in Cox`s Bazar (boys 260 and girls 265) through Temporary Learning Centers (TLCs), with capacity development initiatives for their teachers and facilitators to deliver high quality teaching and learning to the children. Community participation will also be ensured through Temporary Learning center Management Committees, which will include representatives from the Rohingya Community. Center Management Committee (CMC) will meet monthly basis. Community awareness raising session will also be conducted during parents meeting in every TLC on rights to education, equal rights and opportunities for all children, CRC, personal health and hygiene, Sexual and reproductive health, prevention of child marriage, trafficking, Violation against women etc. so that they can support in their children`s education.

1 - Identified Needs

Currently 515,052 children and youth (3-24 years) from the Rohingya refugee community at Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh are in need of education and 75.90% (390,923) of them have been targeted under JRP 2021. Similarly, 24,909 children aged 3-24 are in need of education in Camp-13, of which 76% (18960 Children) have been targeted under JRP 2021. Besides, 6576 children aged 3-24 of JRP-2020 are still out of education in Camp-13. [Source: EduSector_PIN_Target (By Camp)] ISDE will address these critical gaps in education in the Rohingya camp-13 by renovating and maintaining existing 5 safe and equipped learning centers with wash facilities for 265 female children and 260 male children. To ensure continuous and high-quality educational opportunities for Rohingya children, a pool of skilled and trained teachers is needed along with the necessary infrastructure. To enhance the skills of teachers, ISDE currently trains their teachers through pre-service and in-service training using the necessary training materials and methods. In addition, parents and caregiver need training so that they can cooperate in their children's education. Rohingya parents and caregivers will also be made aware through issue-based discussions at parent meetings under the project. This project will address the needs of children and parents/caregivers in camps by incorporating participatory approaches, beneﬁciary feedback mechanisms and alternative delivery methods tailored towards persons with speciﬁc needs into programme design and implementation (as per Inter-Agency Network for Education in Emergencies (INEE) standards. An additional needs assessment will establish the exact needs of the different communities. This will be followed by regular assessments that assess whether the programme is meeting the population’s needs and promoting accountability to the affected populations. This will be complemented with regular monitoring of enrollment rates, retention, teacher`s attendance and improvements in learning outcomes.ISDE has been working with the host communities of Ukhiya and Teknaf for the last 15 years in developing health, education, food securities, nutrition, climate change, disaster risk reduction, water supply and sanitation, gender and women’s political empowerment & women`s rights. ISDE is on the ground provided cooked food distribution, safe drinking water, Child happiness center and medical assistance to help the victims after Rohingya influx. ISDE has been implementing Early childhood and non-formal education for displaced Rohingya children project including raising awareness among parents and caregivers CRC, GBV, early marriage prevention, personal health and hygiene, SRH through establishing 10 temporary learning Center in Camp-13 and 16, Ukhiya, Cox`s Bazar with support from DoPeace USA and Muslim Aid UK since January 2018. Now ISDE operating 5 Learning center for 525 Rohingya Children at Camp-13, Ukhiya, Cox`s Bazar with support of DoPeace USA. ISDE has wide experience on NFE program. ISDE has been implementing NFPE program in Chittagong, Cox`s Bazar district since 1994 with support from BRAC, GOB/BNFE, UNICEF, DoPeace USA.

2 - Activities or outputs

- Refurbishment and Maintenance of Learning Center: ISDE will repair/upgrade the existing learning center in the camp-13 under the project. - Provision of high quality learning and educational materials for Rohingya children through learning Center: ISDE will provide high quality learning and educational in the learning center for the Rohingya refugees children.- Collection and distribution of learning materials to Rohingya children: ISDE will develop teaching learning materials (like bag, Khata/note book, T-shirt, etc.) for learners and also collect some martials (like Books) from Education Sector for distribution among the learners.- Learning Center Management Committees: ISDE will form or rearrange the learning center management committee with inclusion of women in every learning center for community engagement activities. LC management committee will meet monthly basis to review the progress of learning center. Every LCMC member will oversee the progress of learning center as a representative of the community. - Foundation and Monthly refresher training for Teachers and Safeguarding training for staffs and LCMCs:ISDE will arrange 7 day long foundation training for all teachers and staff. Monthly day long refresher training also conducted in every month for skill development of teachers. ISDE also arranged a day long safeguarding training for teachers, staff, member of Learning center management committee and parents under this project.- Bi-Monthly Parents meeting: ISDE will arrange parents meeting in every learning center on bi-monthly basis for ensuring community participation. Awareness session for parents and caregivers on rights to education, equal rights and opportunities for all children, CRC, personal health and hygiene, sanitation, sexual and reproductive health, child marriage, trafficking, etc. will be also conducted under the project, so that they can support in their children`s education. - Coordination with Sector and GoB department: ISDE will coordinate with Camp-in-Charge (CiC), Upazilla and District administration, education sector and other stakeholder regularly.
