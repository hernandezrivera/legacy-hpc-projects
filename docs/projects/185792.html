Project Summary

To enhance livelihoods and resilience of vulnerable host communities in cooperation with the Government of Bangladesh SI will provide support and skill development training to home-gardening and demo farmers, Livestock owners and Fishermen. Moreover, IGA practice will ensure the diversified and climate resilient trades while implementation of small mitigation scheme work will generate income directly for the CFW and indirectly the community will become more resilient and access to amenities will increase. SI proposed activities are based on the communities’ feedbacks received during previous project implementation from the targeted communities and local authorities, and will be accountable to them for the decisions made during the project implementation. The intervention was designed to ensure long-term positive impact on the food security of the targeted beneficiaries, but also to increase market accessibility of the neighbouring households and communities not directly targeted through the project. This will be ensured through business trainings of the beneficiaries and the provision of organizational support to enhance the linkages between the beneficiaries and neighbouring markets through notably the creation of groups or collections points. This community based approach and market linkage assistance will ensure the acceptance and sustainability of SI project.
Beneficiary selection will be based on vulnerability criteria (i.e. Female headed HH, presence of disabled person in the HH, elderly headed HH, HH affected by disaster recurrence, level of poverty…) for IGA, livestock, and agricultural activities except for demonstration farmers who will be selected based on technical experience, motivation and recognition at the community level. Females will be favoured for the home garden, livestock and IGA activities as they face more barriers to access trainings and livelihood opportunities.
SI has a well-established Complaint Response Mechanism (CRM) and whistleblowing policy in Teknaf to receive beneficiary feedback regarding the assistance provided. The MEAL team ensures that the appropriate mechanisms are in place, through which the affected populations can measure the adequacy of the intervention and address their concerns. In case of protection needs, the case will be referred to relevant protection actors working in the area.

1 - Identified Needs

From the joint Multi Sector Need Assessment (MSNA) it was identified that Sabrang, Hnila and Bahachora are the most vulnerable Unions in Teknaf. Findings from SI MSNA held in the 5 unions of Teknaf in September 2021 revealed that climate Vulnerability, insufficient livelihood support, absence of multi-dimensional income source, lack of source to receive training on skill development, scarcity of water for both drinking and irrigation are common problem of Teknaf host community people. This makes this population highly vulnerable to climate change and natural disaster impacts.
