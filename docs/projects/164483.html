Project Summary

This project has been designed to responds to the needs for WASH services in the Sub Districts which received the IDP's moved from Southen Rural of Idleb and Western Rural of Aleppo.
The locations are prioritized as per the HNO 2020 data "draft" and according to prioritization of BINAA to the location as per the field teams communication with the local councils and their field visits to some villages.
The project will target the IDP’s and Host Community in the targeted locations, (IDP’s 611,577 ; Host Community 485,228; Women 250855 ; Men 238618 ; Girls 317,323 ; Boys 305,643) in order to improve the safe, adequate and sustainable access for these service along with the improvement for the public health and environment to avoid any outbreaks caused by contamination of the drinking water and lack in the SWM which might cause an outbreak in leishmaniasis either cholera. In this project the rehabilitation for water system, sanitation system and restoration for the SWM system. Moreover, rehabilitation for water system will target increasing the efficiency for the water station and network by conduct the needed maintenance, replacement and extension for the existing infrastructure and water station as well along with establishment for water flow meters in order to re-activate the tariff system. Rehabilitation for the sanitation system will target maintenance for the sewage network to repair the leakage points to avoid the mixture between drinking water and waste water along with conduct needed extension for the network in order to ensure that all shelters and settlements are connected to the sewerage network to ensure the best practice for waste water disposal along with install for rain drainage in the targeted communities and collective centers to avoid floods which caused by heavy rain . The Solid Waste Management restoration will cover distribution for the containers in the targeted areas along with providing the needed equipment’s, trucks and tools to the local council/ authorities to collect and transfer the solid waste to the related landfill which will also be rehabilitated. Distribution for Hygiene kits (WASH NFI supplies) will be considered to the newly come IDP’s in Camps and communities for two times in the year (30,000 beneficiary) will receive a hygiene kit (WASH NFI's) based on the need along with consideration for age and gender in each family as per the assessment.
In addition, with collaboration with MENTOR initiative prevention training and capacity building sessions for the vectors which causing leishmaniasis will take place in order to improve the capacity for preventing the leishmaniasis.

4 - Needs

Based on the HNO 2020 for Syria and the WASH assessments there are an estimated 10.7 million people who have a lack in the WASH services access and require a WASH assistance while they are facing a difficulty in accessing the safe drinking water, lack in transfer the solid waste while 10% of the garbage is left in the public areas, and mule functioning sewerage system. Based on Binaa Field Surveys the needs can be summarized as following:- Enhance the access for safe and adequate water through rehabilitation for the existing water systems along connect to it those system the surrounding camps.-Enhance access for the Sanitation and SWM mechanisms in Community.- Improve the hygiene practices through hygiene promotion sessions in Schools, Health Facilities and camps.- Improve the safety of the water through implement water safety plans which reduce the number of WBD's.- Conduct prevention sessions for Vectors which are responsible on Leishminsos.- Improve the access for the WASH facilities in the formal and informal settlements.- Increase access for WASH Supplies (WASH NFI's Supplies)

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented with taking consideration of participating for the women, youth and children’s in order to ensure the best impact and practices for the beneficiaries by conducting several FGD with the management of protection centers, health facilities, schools and local authorities in order to understand their needs and respond to them as well. Using the WIS tool which are developed by UNICEF and the technical tools for sanitation system in order to define the technical needs to restore these systems and put them in duty. During restoration process and conducting the needed works another FGD will be conducted in order to discuss and design the sustainability mechanism through the cost recovery and build the capacity for the Services Unit which will be established from the available human resources into the community/ Collective Centers by conducting several technical training on cost recovery system along with Operation and Maintenance for the recovered systems. In the beginning of the project and after the FGD in the design stage, base line need assessment on HH level will be conducted and the results will be used also in order to define the gaps and needs for different groups of beneficiaries to be considered in the implementation phase. . The project will target around 60 WASH systems and will benefit the communities along the surrounding camps while the approach of the project to maintain the sustainable WASH services for the communities along with the settlements.In addition, water safety plans will be conducted and implemented into the projects in order to ensure the safety of the water reaching the beneficiaries.for the lifesaving activities in camps and collective centers, the provision for the water trucking and WASH NFI's supplies along with establishment for the public latrines in order to ensure the access for the most critical lifesaving WASH component during the implementation for the project.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Risk: Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors. The likelihood of this risk is medium and the impact is also high. The mitigation action is Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organize joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care. - Risk: Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organisation, its partners or contractors. The likelihood of this risk is high and the impact is high too. The mitigation action will be Ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ laborers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labor in the contracts; check and register civil documentation. - Risk: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks). The likelihood of this risk is medium and the impact is high. The mitigation action will be Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming. - Risk: Movements of garbage trucks in congested, camp like settings; Reliability of services. e likelihood of this risk is high and the impact is low. the mitigation risk will be Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Provide household level solid waste containers/plastic bags to ease disposal of waste from H/H to the public SW collection points. Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school.
