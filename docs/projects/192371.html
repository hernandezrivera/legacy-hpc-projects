Project Summary

Lebanon was hit by unprecedented ascending multiple crises at a time, which has negatively been impacting the lives of boys and girls living on its soil. More of 80% of the Lebanese, PRL and migrant populations are under the poverty line, leading to exposing children to child labor in its worst forms as schools are closing, or became inaccessible due to the financial crisis. Lebanese children are more and more in need of an adequate response as other response plans failed to compensate the emerging need, since targeted areas were linked to the presence of Syrian refugees, leaving the host and other cohorts in other areas underserved.
All proposed activities are long awaited by the affected populations, given the fact that the needs are exponentially increasing since the onset of the financial crisis. himaya first suggests implementing an integrated package of community based and focused psychosocial activities, addressed to both children and parents as well as case management services in Tripoli, Akkar, Central, west and North Bekaa, Saida, Marjaayoun, Beirut and Mount Lebanon. himaya will also provide trainings on safe identification and referral and other CP related topics to other CBOs, other sector partners as well as municipal police. Finally, the judicial protection of children remains an important component as himaya is mandated to cover this service by the ministry of justice in both Bekaa and North.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

In line with the sector objective and with its first output, himaya will provide specializedcase management entailing of an intervention of both a social worker and a psychologistto protect the children at medium to high risk while working with their parents and theenvironment to decrease the risk on the child and ensure a sustainable care plan. Thisactivity will target 462 children during a period of 5 months, and 300 high risk children in both Bekaa and North who will be judicially protected over a period of 9 months, not to forget providing allchildren in imminent danger with alternative care solutions and referral to adequateservice providers. 638 children will also benefit from focused psychosocial interventionespecially under certain thematic such as violence and child labor to address the risk.Himaya will continue adopting its community based approach under objective 2 throughstrengthening the child protection networks in the area starting with building the capacity of the stakeholders, outreaching, then providing community based PSS to children (1820) and parenting for caregivers (508) who will benefit from life skills to overcome the hardship and its impact on the protection risks. Himaya's wellestablished community groups will also detect and refer cases in need of more focused orspecialized intervention. 8 initiatives will be led and 80 partners and local authority member will be trained on CP.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Being an active PSEA network member, all himaya services address PSEA by providing information/awareness to beneficiaries(children, caregivers, community groups, trainees and volunteers) and reportingmechanisms/ pathways. All himaya curricula and care plans consist directly ofresponding to protection needs through informing, responding, detecting and referringchildren and caregivers to service providers with whom himaya will collaborate with on aneed based approach. All himaya content, data segregation, intervention strategies andprogramming will be designed in respect to gender, disability and social inclusion

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

AAP is mainstreamed throughout himaya's strategy and programs and will be reflected inthe project deliverables: himaya ensures feedback and complaint mechanisms to all of itsbeneficiaries (through complaint boxes and visuals present in all of its service deliverylocations in addition to other pathways such as social media, and hotline). Feedback aretracked, taken seriously and a proper action is taken. All beneficiaries are consulted beforeany care plan is set or any service is delivered so that they decide on their need andactively participate in the process. Localization is prioritized via the capacity building thatis ensured to the CBOs, the adapted material and language in respect to the locals, andthe exit strategy that ensures sustainability of the action beyond the project. Monitoringand Evaluation tools will accompany the project via data management, analysis,accountability and learning. All resources will be optimize to reach the most vulnerable
