Project Summary

The project aims at reducing the adoption of negative coping strategies for Lebanese and Migrants in order to access food. The main objective of the implementation of the project will include the provision of diversified food that will enable beneficiaries to neither compromise on their essential dietary diversity nor worry about finding the ingredients they need in the market or fuel for cooking.
Adopting the modality of distributing cooked meals through community kitchens across the country will not only contribute to a short-time impact but will be fully in line with the Nexus Approach humanitarian principles since the involvement of targeted population and other local organizations with a focus on building the resilience of the community as well as context-specific concerns have been taken into consideration.
By identifying and operating community kitchens, beneficiaries will have access to a dignified assistance through receiving food at their own premises without the hassle of queuing in order to make food available for their families. This modality will also ensure income-generating activities to workers and cooks of the community kitchens and will enhance market linkages by creating an economic cycle when purchasing ingredients from local markets/farmers.
The distribution of meals according to a pre-set menu, will ensure an improvement of consumption of the targeted families as meals will be nutritionally balanced, diversified and also include bread as well as fresh fruits/vegetables that will be cleaned and washed with safe water which also became hard to access at HH level.
In addition to operating community kitchens, IOCC plans to pilot the modality of a free supermarket along with local NGOs. This specific modality will be based on a voucher approach that enables identified vulnerable families to access these supermarkets and purchase their relief needs according to a voucher worth 400,000 LBP per household per month. In order to also tackle other challenges encountered in these crises, the modality of the free supermarket will be indirectly tackling the solid waste management by providing extra reward points to families that bring back the empty detergents plastic containers for example, earlier purchased with the voucher. Identified households will benefit from the basic assistance vouchers for a period of 2 months.
The basic assistance intervention will be implemented through local partners that are non-profit Lebanese organizations. Basic Needs items will be procured and evaluated by IOCC and partners' representatives ensuring a standardization across all shops. This will enable beneficiaries to have access to the same quality of the needed items and ensures better monitoring for the families' expenditures. IOCC will ensure to cover the running costs of the voucher-based supermarkets which will be also sustaining the partners' work by reducing their direct cost.
Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the project's implementation by providing PSS support to beneficiaries, PSEA trainings to IOCC and partners' staff and having all stakeholders sign on a zero tolerance paper in what relates to gender harassments and child safeguarding.
By implementing the above mentioned modalities, IOCC will be building the capacity of the partnering local organizations as well as the beneficiaries which will enhance the sustainability and resilience concepts.
In general, the project's main sector of intervention will be Food Security that is linked to components of WASH, income-generation and protection.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Food Security: 1- IOCC will operate 5 community kitchens in the country to serve vulnerable people in rural and urban settings. The community kitchens will be used to cater cooked hot meals based on a set menu that is diversified and nutritionally balanced. The menu also takes into consideration the inclusion of fresh ingredients that are no longer affordable for families such as meat, chicken and fish.Eligible families for the assistance are those that fall under the below criteria:-female headed households-families with disabled individuals-families with Pregnant and lactating women-families with children under 5-families with no income-families with elderly-families adopting more than 2 coping mechanisms to acquire foodidentified families will have access to cooked meals 3 times per week. Each received meal will be accompanied by 2 bread parcels for a family size of 4. Families will also receive fresh fruits/vegetables twice per week. Meals are adjusted according to family size in order to reduce food waste to the maximum. Basic Needs:1-Through the free supermarket modality, IOCC will adopt providing vouchers to families that they can use in these free supermarkets as per their specific needs. this will put the beneficiaries in a state that they have a purchasing power and are decision makers in the selection of their needed items.This modality will be implemented with local non profit NGOs where available items will be standardized across all facilities.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

IOCC will provide PSEA trainings to all staff and implementing partners who will in turn sign as annexes to their contracts a zero tolerance paper for PSEA and child safeguarding.IOCC will also provide its staff with Psychological First Aid Training that includes safe identification and referral component.PSS support will be provided to all project's beneficiaries.As part of the nexus approach, the project takes into account short- and long-term needs of the affected population, based on a needs and vulnerability assessment, making no distinction of nationality, race, gender, religious beliefs, class or political opinion.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Beneficiaries will take part of the design of the menu during the vulnerability assessment. Once selected, they will be informed about the modality of implementation and will receive a copy of the monthly menu. Monitoring systems are in place to regularly and continuously conform the kitchen's registry with what the beneficiaries have received. The same will apply for the free supermarket modality where beneficiaries can access their basic needs with the provided vouchers.Additionally, IOCC will conduct FGDs and distribute flyers to raise the awareness of the beneficiaries about IOCC's safe line where they can register and address any complaint or feedback in a total anonymous way.
