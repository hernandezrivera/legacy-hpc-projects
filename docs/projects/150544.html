Needs

The Ministry of Agriculture (MoA) reported that more than 20,000 dunums of vegetable’s lands all over Gaza Strip have been severely affected and damaged due to the Israeli attack in 2014, and only less than 50% of them have been rehabilitated (FSS damages and response report, March 2017). In April 2017, the Gaza farmers faced one of the worst energy crisis in its history having an average of only 2 hours power on and 22 hours power cut. Farmers were considered of those whom have been harshly affected since they depend on electricity power to irrigate their lands.The sharp decrease in electricity resulted I losing approximately 60% of irrigation water needed for vegetables, the damages were doubled considering the planting seasonality and the hot summer. This led to (according to MoA report, May 2017) about 60% losses (about 90 million USD) in production especially in the Deir Al Balah, Khan Yunis,Rafah governorates; the Gaza Strip vegetables basket.ACAD field visits show that majority of agricultural lands in Gaza and Deir Al Balah, Khan Yunis,Rafah governorates weren't rehabilitated. According to MoA needs in September 2017 there is still more than17,000 dunums in need for agricultural interventions in four governorates.This doubled crisis led to significant increase of inputs prices (mainly irrigation water) on the farmer’s burden leading him to huge losses and lead also to some farmers destroying their crops due to their inability to complete the season.Consequently, the owners of these lands became vulnerable.The protracted crisis of blockade into Gaza with very limited to resources and inputs is also an obstacle facing farmers causing additional burden with the increase prices of inputs followed by limitations on exports. - Recently and in March 2017 ACAD conducted six focus groups in Gaza governorate and in Khan Yunis with the affected farmers (30 women and 30 men) and 4 interviews with the planning directorate of MoA and related MoA directorates to assess main needs of the affected farmers. The recommendation we got was to achieve kind of complementary of activities. Water wells rehabilitation, rehabilitation and installation of agricultural water carrier lines and vegetable lands rehabilitation. As a result, ACAD plan is to conduct the following activities in the four governorates:1. Rehabilitation of agricultural water wells2. Rehabilitation/installation of agricultural water carrier lines3. Rehabilitation of vegetables open lands (provision of irrigation networks, seeds and seedlings, organic fertilizers,4. Provision of cash for work support to number of skilled and unskilled labors.- Center Implemented 3 meetings with agricultural sector organization to monitor farmers needs and women farmers- Four focus groups were Implemented in Gaza,Dair El.Balh, Khan Younis and Rafah in September 2018ACAD will closely coordinate activities with MoA, FSS and other organizations working in the targeted governorates, naming PUI, CARE, UAWC, MAAN and PARC, as well as other actors

Activities

Project is designed to address the needs of vulnerable farmers in Gaza,Deir Al Balah,Khan Yunis and South governorates according to the size of damages and MoA priorities. Social and technical criteria will be adopted to select project beneficiaries. The farming practice is the main profession source of income of selecting beneficiaries. Other social criteria like size of income, HH size,female headed households, elderly people,PWDs and other people with special needs.Technical criteria include experience in agriculture, land ownership,etc. ACAD and local committees in communities will select beneficiaries based on these criteria and in close coordination with MoA and FSS.ActivitiesForming acommunity committeeThe committee will consist of members from entrepreneur farmers, municipalities and ACAD project staff as well as MoA representatives. The aim of committee is to determine selection criteria of the beneficiaries, review list of the applicant farmers, identify and finalize of beneficiaries list, conduct field visits to the final list.This community will also cork on conflict resolution if occurred in community, it will also assist ACAD team to provide high sense of accountability towards the communities through a well established feedback and complaint mechanism.Through strong coordination with FSS,MoA and other NGOs working in the area ACAD will ensure no duplication in providing service nor beneficiaries. ACAD will ensure fair presence of female in the committee hence can ensure female farmers are well represented in the project committee. Project committees in targeted communities will ensure effective communication with them.Tendering,Purchasing and distributing the agricultural inputsACAD will purchase the inputs for the damaged open lands rehabilitation including:irrigation nets,organic fertilizers,seasonal seeds or seedlings.ACAD will contract with local suppliers according to proper agricultural standards. 150 farmers (2 dunums each) will be provided with the required rehabilitation inputs according to their needs and under supervision of community committee and project staff.For carrier lines rehabilitation, ACAD will select certain and the most in need lands that serve the larger number of agricultural dunums to link affected lands with proper source of irrigation water. Works will be completed through cash for work. 132 workers (12 skilled and 120 unskilled workers) will work for 7,920 of days implement related excavation and backfilling works.The skilled workers will coach the unskilled ones during process of rehabilitation in order to acquire them new skills of design and implement works on the land.This will open new job opportunities for 40 unemployed workers based on cash for work shceme.For and water wells rehabilitation ACAD will contract a specialized company to deliver these works under the complete supervision of MoA. MoA will provide ACAD with list of potential damaged wells in the targeted communities and project staff will conduct verification visits with MoA technical team.OutputsOutput_1:Formation of project committees (steering committees) in the targeted communities(15 female and 25 male members)Output_2:Rehabilitation of 1210 dunums of agricultural lands.Output_3:Rehabilitation of5 water wellsOutput_4:Rehabilitation and installation of 20 k.m of agricultural water carrier lines(1,200 dunums to be productive).ACAD is going to provide carrier lines to complete the supply chain and improve the service provided to farmers beneficiaries .carrier lines will be installed to link rehabilitated water wells with rehabilitated agricultural lands in order to maxim benefit of farmers .NO carrier will be installed as stand alone activity.Output_5:Create temporary job creation opportunities for132 number of workers(7920 days)Output_6:Maintenance and agricultural materials distributed in sufficient quantity &quality for targeted farmersOutput_7:The agricultural cycle activated and operated.

Indicators

Throughout the project life span, ACAD management team will initiate effective monitoring and evaluation and capitalization in order to ensure timely implementation of activities according to scheduled plan and to ensure the achievement of outputs and outcomes through daily follow up and monitoring. The project committee will ensure the engagement of the community in all steps of project implementation.ACAD will establish complaint mechanism in the communities to ensure transparency and accountability with the community. A complaint box will be set in the four committees and inside ACAD place as well. Then, a ‘complaint day’ will be conducted in each community to listen to those who have any concerns.IndicatorsIndicator 1: Number of damaged vegetable lands rehabilitated: 1210 dunumsIndicator 2: Number of farmers (male and female) received agricultural inputs: 11,685 Indicator 3: Kilometers of agricultural water lines rehabilitated/installed: 20Indicator 4: Number of agricultural water wells rehabilitated: 5 water wellsIndicator 5: Number of dunums made productive: 1210 dunumsIndicator 6: Number of workers received temporary job opportunities: 132Indicator 7: Number of working days created: 7,920 Targets11,685 vulnerable farmers (5,595 5,959 and 5,726 female) including female headed households (widows, divorced, single …etc.) and their families12 skilled workers and 120 unskilled workers and their families Monitoring and reportingThe project team is expected to monitor and report on work progress and the team will prepare monitoring plan based on the measurable indicators and targets. A number of tasks fall under the preview of the project team along with the community committee:? Meet regularly with the farmers to assess progress through field visits and to get feedback to improve the implementation of activities,? Prepare reports (progress and final) on the achievements of indicators to bring lesson learned and good practices? Prepare success stories to highlight the impact of the project on the livelihood of the farmers,? Supervise the maintenance process by the skilled and unskilled workers through attendance sheets? Written and camera documentation and filming EvaluationThe final term evaluation is expected to conduct an assessment of the relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, impact, sustainability of the project to date and provide ACADteam with an independent review of the status, performance of the project as compared to the project document and indicators. The evaluation results are envisaged to identify and describe the lessons learned, through measurements of the changes in the set indicators, summarize the experiences gained, technically and managerial. The evaluation should provide ACADwith useful information, analysis and recommendations for future ideas. ACAD will evaluate this project with the following tools:? Monthly meetings with the farmers will provide direct feedback on the usefulness and impact of the project's activities on their livelihood.? Focus groups and interviews randomly selected participants in the project activities to provide additional feedback on the content and utility.
