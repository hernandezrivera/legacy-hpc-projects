Project Summary

Concern will provide hygiene kits to 1200 households and conduct hygiene promotion activities reaching 15000 in the refugee transit site in the border town of Adré, Ouaddaï province. As UNHCR launches the resettlement process, to relocate refugees from the transit site to new or existing refugee camps, Concern will maintain flexibility over where the response takes place and respond where the needs are greatest, in coordination with the UNCHR and other actors.
To facilitate the response in Adré, Concern will establish an office in Adré to serve as base of operation; this office also acts as a base for Concern’s cross-border team who focus on responding to needs on the Sudan side of the border. 1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

On 15 April 2023, conflict broke out between the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and the Sudan Military Forces (SAF) that quickly escalated. The conflict is devastating West Darfur where existing tribal conflict has triggered fighting between RSF and SAF and has led to mass killing and displacement of the population to neighbouring countries. Since the onset of the conflict, 418,126 displaced people (about 124, 874 households) have been identified by the UNHCR across Chad’s three border regions: Ouaddaï (319,545 individuals), Sila (63,808) and Wadi Fira (34,773 people). The consistent influx of refugees, combined with the absence of any potential exit from the current crisis in Sudan, leads authorities to expect up to 600,000 people to arrive in Chad by the end of 2023. Adré, a small border town, is currently hosting approximately 226,848 displaced people and has insufficient infrastructure and services to adequately support them. The needs of those displaced are immense and a recent protection monitoring survey conducted by Project 21 , identified the need for basic essential services: WASH, health and nutrition, food, and shelter. As the rainy season is now under way, WHO has indicated that there are increased rates of dengue fever and cases of acute watery diarrhoea. UNHCR is coordinating the humanitarian response and has identified WASH as a key gap – the agency considers an additional 11,700 latrine-showers are needed in camps and host towns, while hygiene promotion activities have received about 20% of the total funding required. As such, safeguarding the sanitation conditions for refugees and host communities is critical at this time of the year to prevent the transmission of potentially fatal diseases. Despite the glaring needs, there is a significant gap in the response capacity among the actors currently operating in the area. Funding of the response is also insufficient, out of the USD226 million requested for Chad as part of the Sudan situation Regional Refugee Response Plan, only USD75.7 million (34%) has been raised.

Output 1.1: 1,200 households receive an essential hygiene kit: Concern will provide 1,200 households (about 5,000 individuals) with hygiene kits to help improve their WASH practices. The composition of the hygiene kits comply with the Sphere and national standards, are harmonised with other actors and consider the specific contextual needs. The hygiene kit composition (per household) will include: 12 bars of soap, 4 jerry cans (20L), 2 plastic buckets (20L), 4 ablution jugs and 4 bottles of bleach. All the items in the kit will be packaged together in one large bag to facilitate transport of the items by the programme participants. Concern will conduct a PDM survey after the distribution to assess beneficiary satisfaction, quality and quantity of items received. Output 1.2: Improved awareness on good WASH practices: To reduce occurrence of water related diseases, Concern will conduct awareness raising activities with an estimated 15,000 people to promote improved WASH practices including key hand washing moments, water treatment and storage, and consequences of open-air defecation. Awareness raising on the use of bleach for water disinfection will be done before its distribution and will be closely monitored by the community volunteers to ensure correct usage. 30 trained community volunteers under the technical guidance of Concern staff will conduct the hygiene promotion activities aided using the image boxes for easy comprehension of key messages.
