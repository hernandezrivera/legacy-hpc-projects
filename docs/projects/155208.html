Activities or outputs

"ACAPS is an independent information provider, as a non-profit, non-governmental project with the aim of providing independent, ground-breaking humanitarian analysis to help humanitarian workers, influencers, fundraisers, and donors make better decisions. ACAPS has been collaborating with IOM’s Needs and Population Monitoring (NPM) project since December 2017, and has established an Analysis Hub in Cox’s Bazar. The aim of the Analysis Hub is to support decision-making through the creation of products which contribute to a shared understanding of the Rohingya crisis. Activities: Through the partnership between ACAPS and NPM, the Analysis Hub supports operational actors in providing analytical briefs, by looking at primary data – including NPM data –, conducting secondary data review, and discussing with key stakeholders on different themes. The Hub provides stakeholders with an overview of the situation through regular updates and specialized thematic products including scenario building. Regular updates come in the form of an ACAPS-designed overview product and through other products that will be a combination of stakeholder requests and initiations of the Hub. ACAPS is specialized in needs assessment and analysis and within those bounds, provides targeted and specific technical reviews of information and management processes. During its work with NPM, ACAPS supports revisions of the questionnaire in line with sector specifications and aligns it with best practices in assessments."
