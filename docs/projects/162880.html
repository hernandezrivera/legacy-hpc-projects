Project Summary

This project aiming at improve the access to quality protective education services for more vulnerable IDPs and returnees children in EJM at (4) basic schools that includes: One school at Derbat Admin Unit namely King Mixed School with total of 513 students (376 M, 217 F), the other 3 schools in Deribat Admin Unit namely Dara Mixed School with total of 1,255 students (649 M, 606 F), Korny Mixed school with total of 970 (M513 F457) Abu hrira 524(m213 F237) The total beneficiaries is3,268 children (1,751 M, 1,517 F). Through this project PBA intervenes directly to provide teaching, learning and recreational materials for 2524 children and provide seating (benches/ desk) for is3,268 students, construction of 28 semi-permanent classrooms for 480 student (60 students/ classroom), rehabilitation of 4 existed classrooms for 432 students, conduct 4 training sessions for 120 teachers (50% M, 50% F), 60 participants/ training for 7 days based on SMoE guidelines) on core subjects and another one for 5 Days on EiE including Psycho- social support and child protection, Conduct One training for 90 Parent Teacher Associations (PTAs, 60% M, 40% F) on roles and responsibilities of the committee, school co-management, Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) and advocacy and networking methods techniques, construction of 6 gender sensitive latrines in schools (6 holes/ unit- 30 girls, 60 boys/ unit) 1080 boys and 540 girls, the total beneficiaries 1620 students, provision of 6 water points (water connection+ 2 tanks 1000 Liters) and 6 hands- washing units that benefiting 2092 students, conduct 6 campaigns on hygiene promotion at the schools targeting 2400 children (each campaign will targeting 400 child/ school through- out the academic year), establishing (3) child clubs focusing on life saving issues, PSS, fine arts, drama & games, peace building & protection, hygiene promotion and leadership skills targeting 60 child/ club totaling 180 children, conduct 6 enrollment campaigns at communities around the targeted schools to sensitize them on the importance of education especially for girls and encourage them to register their children at school age (each campaign will target 400 individuals). All the project activities will be conducted with technical support from line Ministries at State level

1 - Needs

According to visits conducted in coordination between to PBA and MoG (Ministry of Guidance) during 14th and 19th of October 2019, South Darfur State, Sharq Jebel Marra had been established as a locality composed of Deribat, Jawa, Admin units. The area has been broadly affected by insecurity and rotation of authorities since the beginning of the Darfur conflict 2003/2004; and witnessed movements of population to the internally displaced camps in Mershing, Nyala, Kass, Elfasher and Manawashei. Thus during 2010 to 2015; the ongoing tensions and fighting between the Government Forces and the Sudan Liberation Movement – Abdul Wahid (SLA-AW), forced a large number of people flee their homes seeking for settlement in different villages such as Dira, Kiba, Krony, Soro, Korbal, Baimadina, Sawani, Leiba and Jawa at the Jebel Marra mountains. In 2016 the State Government has dominantly controlled wider areas within Jebel Marra including surroundings of Deribat, Belle Elsereif, Jawa and Kidineer clusters. The socio-economic structures of Sharq Jebel Marra had been broadly affected by the conflict as the entire displaced population sought refuge in satellite villages within Jebel Marra mountains and some sought refuge in South and North Darfur IDPs camps, and therefore the social services are disrupted while the destruction of the scarce income generating sources devastated the general economic situation. In the view of general situation, population could be categorized into three groups; host- community, internally protracted displaced persons and returnees who returned home due to the improvement of the security situation within the territory. According to the above assessment, Deribat Admin Unit has not being accessible for the UN agencies during the last seven years; worth noting that the State authorities have committed to facilitate humanitarian access so that affected and displaced civilians who should receive humanitarian assistance they urgently need. Thus Deribat town situated 165 kms NNW of Nyala Admin Unit contains (46) inhabitant satellite villages, while Kidineer Admin Unit contains (24) satellite village. The mission team interviewed the local authorities and cross- checked the information with representatives from the villages and estimated (105,000 persons-17,510 HHs) distributed at (46) villages are inhabitant of Deribat Unit, these population are 42,000 internally protracted IDPs, 1,000 returnees (inside Deribat town) and 62,000 host- communities. The most population is women and children. As highlighted above, 100% of the IDPs, Returnees and host- communities in Deribat and Kidineer Admin Units their most urgent needs are of basic humanitarian services including health, nutrition, protection, WASH and education urgently and emergency food for the most vulnerable affected populations.Activities 1.
