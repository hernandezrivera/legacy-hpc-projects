Project Summary

GBV is rooted in patriarchal norms and values coupled with a vast gap between the rich and the poor. The marginalized groups are prevented from enjoying existing rights because of the prevalence of negative customary practices and attitudes. It is imperative to recognize the fact that GBV is multi-faceted and has many structures and categories. The major impact of GBV is witnessed on women and that’s why this is very commonly also used for violence against women. There are many underlying causes such as: early marriages and consequent psychological and mental abuse of women including ignorance about Rights and responsibilities, minimal access to justice and redressal mechanisms, expensive and prolonged justice, generally poor implementation of the law and state support services for victims of violence.
The GBV during emergencies always remains at a higher side. The proposal is drafted against the call for proposal to address the issue of GBV in selected districts of Sindh. Women Health & Welfare Organization (WHWO) is having over 5 years of experience on working on similar issues and have a dedicated team to implement such project.
The project covers case management, awareness raising & capacity building as the key components. WHWO will targeted 04 districts including Larkana, Qambar Shahdadkot, Noushero Feroz & Sukkur. The organization will establish a helpline to aware people about the GBV (SRH). The helpline will mainly target women while giving priority to LGBT community. The helpline will also be utilize for case management. The survivors of GBV can report the case via helpline. The helpline will be linked with all the relevant referral services to facilitate such cases. The mapping of the different service providers including legal, health, shelter, protection etc will be carried out and partnerships will be strengthened to facilitate such cases. The helpline is one of a great resource to facilitate GBV cases during corona.
The 2nd hey element will be raising awareness among community on GBV and its prevention. The organization will use different awareness tools including TV channels, FM radio, local cable, newspapers to spread awareness and introduce helpline services. Additionally, the social mobilizers will aware women by visiting their houses on GBV and will arrange sessions once corona situation becomes better to organize such activities.
The team will consist a Project lead along with 2 psychologists, 1 case worker & 2 social workers in each district. The psychologists will be mainly responsible for telephonic counseling and referral. The case worker will manage the reported case in each district whereas social mobilizers will be carryingout community based mobilization. 1 M&E officer based in Larkana will carry out the monitoring across the districts. The M&E officer will be also responsible for project accountability and will ensure the feedback from marginalized groups taken into care in project implementation and design changes. He will also look after the overall complaint management for the project.
The IEC material (flyers, posters) on GBV in local language will be developed and disseminated during the project. The capacity building trainings will be carried out for the frontline workers on GBV protocols along with inclusion of how to work effectively during COVID. The training manual will be developed and disseminated. The PPEs will be also distributed among the frontline workers.
Finally, the sustainability will be ensured by building the effective referral network and strengthening the relevant stakeholders’ capacity to manage such cases in future. The women developmemt department, Social Welfare Dept, CBOs working on GBV will be also invited in capacity building trainings and meetings for referrals.
