Project Name

Start Date

01/05/2024

End Date

01/05/2025

Project Summary

This project aims at responding to the critical and basic needs of 6124 Internally displaced families as 30610 individuals in Salqin cluster (Salqin sub-district), with a total number of individuals disaggregated approximately 6,511 men, 6,623 women, 8,624 boys, 8,852 girls, and 185 Person with disability living in Salqin Cluster informal IDP sites as well as camp management structure support for 20 IDP sites in the cluster prioritizing the most vulnerable and highly populated locations through providing furniture and establishment of camps management committee that includes male and females for enhance the gender equality base on an election that hire camps management.
SKT aims to provide the hard component, care, and maintenance of wash and shelter services, supporting the management structure in each IDP site through training and establishment of IDP committees, social units’ volunteers, office facilities, and equipment.
The project includes the care & maintenance of latrine blocks, waste holes, toilets lightning, internal roads, a drainage system, and water tanks)and provision of the camp management office building, facilities, and equipment, and provision of capacity building trainings and awareness-raising sessions for the SUVs, IDP committees, and Camp managers on Camp Management toolkit, awareness on COVID-19 risks, fire safety and security, care, and maintenance and provision of fire extinguishers 6 kg for every 20 tents in informal IDP sites and provision of site management for existing 20 informal IDP sites in Salqin cluster. The PSEA activities will be taken into consideration throughout the project to secure the strategy.
This will include registration of newly arrived IDPs, data collection tent to tent on IDP needs and vulnerability mapping, regular information sharing with respective clusters, awareness-raising on accountability to affected population mechanism (compliant reporting) as well as full coordination in the multi-sectoral assistance through other sectors partners (food, health services, protection, shelter and non-food items, education, and WASH) and provision of dedicated awareness sessions on COVID-19 risks, prevention, and response measures through the SUVs across all IDP sites to reach 4,111 IDP families in camps in Salqin.
