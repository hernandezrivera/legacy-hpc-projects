Project Summary

This WASH in school intervention shall strengthen access of crisis-affected boys and girls , teachers and schools stakeholders in Monguno to safe drinking water through new construction, water treatment and capacity building of school based management committee(SBMC) on water chlorination, repairs of solar panel powered boreholes. in addition, the response will sustain access to sex segregated latrines in schools through new construction, supply of sanitation kits for repairs and cleaning and capacity building of SBMC on repairs and maintenance. Furthermore, the response will improve access of crisis-affected boys and girls, teachers, SBMC to good hygiene practices and supply through awareness campaigns and cash transfer; while enforcing protection of crisis-affected boys and girls, teachers through awareness campaigns on covid-19 prevention and distribution of PPE materials as well as pre-positioning of hand washing stations with running water and soaps.

1 - Needs

The rapid Wash Assessment carried out by AHA in 30 schools in Monguno in late November 2020 through direct observation, focus groups discussion with school stakeholders showed severe gaps in water, sanitation and hygiene. in monguno, there is a gap of 10 water points as compared of the number of teachers, school stakeholders children enrolled in the schools. the existing water points have not been chlorinated for the past three months, there need of sustained chlorination of water points in schools in monguno. 10 existing latrines in Monguno are without doors, lock inside , lights. adolescent girls are exposed to rape. poor hygiene practices are high, with risk of cholera outbreak. opened defecation was observed in 67% of assessed schools as a result of limited access to safe sex segregated latrines as per standards. learners lack sustained access to safe sanitation as per standards; there is need of 10 new sex segregated latrines. in addition, 20 latrines assessed were poorly maintained. schools stakeholders reported lack of sanitation kits to maintain existing latrines; there is need to supply 20 primary schools in monguno with sanitation kits. the focus groups discussions held with school stakeholders showed that, 35% of assessed schools had limited funds to procure pre-positioning sanitation kits ( hand washing points with safe running water, hand sanitizers, soaps) at schools premises to contain spread of corona virus.
