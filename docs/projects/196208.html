Project Summary

This project delivers a strategic and integrated set of interventions in 7 camps in the Idleb governorate and the communities around it in sub-districts Idleb, Ma'arat Tamsrin and the communities of Arshani, Idleb, Kaftin, Kafr - Kafrehmul and Haranbush. in the aftermath of protracted conflict further aggravated by the impact of the C-19 pandemic and deteriorated economic situation. The project formulation has been based on needs as identified in multi-cluster RNA/REACH/HNAP data and MHD's market assessment. Besides, firsthand consultations, conducted specifically for this project with local stakeholders, have also informed the design of this proposal. By pursuing the referred main objective, the project aims to achieve the following outcomes:
Targeting 1,653 IDPs and Host communities (8,265 individuals 1,477M / 1,653 F : 2,362 boys: 2,773 girls).
1- Infrastructure improvements that consider the seasonality of the intervention which will include the following:
a) Graveling and of the main and sub-roads in IDP sites/camps;
b) Tents leveling;
c) Rain drainage system and channels

3 - Needs

A needs assessment was conducted in October 2022, show in the severity of need in the proposed locations,so we will intervention of Infrastructure improvements that consider the seasonality: the graveling of the main and sub-roads in IDP sites/camps; Tents leveling; Rain drainage system and channels.The need assessment looked at 28 camps, where Muzun looked at 6 different category levels of need, from the highest need to no need. Some of the camps did not have any graveling at all and we measured them at 0%, some had 1-25% graveling done previously, and others had between 26-50% graveling, 51-75%, 76-99% and the rest had zero need for graveling and were 100% graveled.Muzun will target 7 camps and will target the 0-25% camps that have the highest need for graveling in the main and sub-roads. Along with the graveling, these camps also need tent leveling and drainage systems rehabilitation.-Total area of these 7 camps is about 917,947 m2; The 7 camps house 1,653 families (8,265 individuals 1,477 male: 1,653 female: 2,362 boys: 2,773 girls).The length of the roads that need to be graveled is 6687.3 m.-4 of these camps do not have drainage channels, one with closed drainage channels covered with grill manholes, one has open drainage channels.-All these camps are registered in CCCM cluster.-4 are private property, 2 are public property, camp are mixed property;All property rights are clear (HLP)-All camps were flooded through the last three years-568 tents need leveling

4 - Implementation of activities

The contractors should implement all the works according to the signed BoQs and technical condition books. -Conducting Ground insulation for 568 tents in the targeted camps (Al Hussein, Al Khair , Al Manhal, Al Nasr, Al Shuhadaa, Sabron except Al Thanaweya camp-The insulation works will be the implementation of concrete casting for the tents basement (7.5 cm thick layer) to be poured over the surface of the coarse gravel with a suitable gradation from (0.3 to 1.2 cm), including fees of transportation, loading, offloading, grading, humidification, compacting with a vibration plate, and fees of labors and machines to complete the work. When raising the level of tents with gravel, a ramp (1:12 – 1:20 m slope) from the raised tent to the ground out of it shall be installed so PWDs can be able to get out and back to the tent. The length of the ramp will be 1m.-Conducting of the rainwater drainage system of HDPE 100: This type will be a closed drainage network and the pipes are made of HDPE (300 MM).The total of length of channels is 6455.75 m (different between each camp). The levels of drainage pipes will be studied in a precise engineering manner and suitable and the depth of the excavation will take into account adequate coverage of the pipes so that they are not susceptible to fracture. -Gaveling roads: The length of the roads that need to be graveled is 6687.3 m (different between each camp). The field team will coordinate with the IDPs and camp management committees.
