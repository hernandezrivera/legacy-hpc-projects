Project Summary

The social centre co-managed by Armadilla and the Syrian association Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) was created with the specific task of assisting families with presence of children and youngsters with physical, intellectual and/or cognitive disabilities. The main activity of the Association is the creation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for minors, including those which conditions have been impacted by the consequences of the conflict, both from a psychological and physical point of view (e.g. minors with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder - PTSD). The strong reduction for families of the possibility of accessing social and health assistance services, both due to the lack of structures in the suburbs and the lack of economic resources, makes it necessary to strengthen the services offered by the ZAM Centre in the area, through strengthening the technical skills of operators and volunteers and increasing the capacity of local actors in working in a network..
The main goals of the projects developed by Armadilla in the field of Protection sector are: a) implementation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for children with disabilities, and favouring their social inclusion; b) enhance the social and economic conditions of their families through the delivery of vocational training for their economic empowerment; c) promote awareness campaigns among the communities of Damascus and Rural Damascus for the social inclusion of children with disabilities; d) raising awareness about explosive ordnance risk; e) provide service and protection pathways for women.
The project is integrated with activities designed under the Health sectors’ umbrella, and may be enriched with Early Livelihood and Agricultural activities to be developed in the course of the year by the Organisation.

3 - Needs

The needs of the targeted communities (southern suburbs of Damascus and Rural Damascus) are generally aligned with the findings at Country level, as highlighted by the NHO 2023 and the HRP 2022. Disability prevalence is higher than the global average, estimated at 24 per cent for individuals two years and older. Children with disabilities are very likely not to attend or to drop out of school, due also to the impossibility of teachers to deal with the specific needs of children with disabilities, lack of skills and equipped spaces, and Gender-based violence (GBV) continues to increase in the community, affecting women and girls’ coping strategies. People require support for recovery and resilience and support in the re-establishment of basic services. At the same time, without emergency assistance to the most vulnerable in the immediate term a further deterioration of their life conditions should be expected. In the areas of intervention, the initial conditions of poverty and scarce access to basic services have been further aggravated by the extended crisis. 91% the targeted families see the presence of one or more minors with disability, and the lack of resources aggravate the status of children and women in needs. There is specifically continuous need to increase the capacity of the social and health workers of local NGOs, as well as the number of volunteers and workers in order to match the increased quantity of beneficiaries of social services in the area.

4 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented using as focal points 2 locations: the centre in Midan, and in the area of Jaramane, where spaces for specific activities will be rented. Activities will be implemented by Armadilla and ZAM staff. M&E activities will be done consistently to good practices and quality standards, involving primary stakeholders (beneficiaries, local partners) as per AAP policy of the Organisation. Key beneficiaries are involved in all the phases of the project, from the development to the implementation, and evaluation. Main activities to be implemented comprise:1) Therapeutic pathways for children with disabilities, by health and social workers. In Midan the centre works 6 days a week, for around 7 hours a day. In Jaramane, the activities will be set on twice-a-week basis.2)Psychosocial activities addressing families of children with disabilities (especially women).3)Training for volunteers, and local associations’ workers in the field of PwD assistance.4)Recreational days for children with disabilities.5)Supply of assistive aids and educational toys for children with disabilities to the associations.6) Awareness community sessions on the rights of PwD and the social inclusion of PwD7) Awareness community sessions on women protection topics8) PSA for women (groups and individual sessions). Legal assistance for women.9) Awareness campaigns about explosive ordnance risk at communities’ level, involving the schools of the target communities.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Sexual exploitation and abuse are a violation of fundamental human rights. It can also be a criminal act. Armadilla, as international I-NGO, owns its procedures and guidelines in order to mitigate any risks related to SEA. Armadilla is committed to acting ethically and with integrity in all our business dealings and relationships and to implementing and enforcing effective systems and controls to ensure the exploitation and abuse of adults is not taking place anywhere in our own business or in any of our supply chains or partnerships. Armadilla is committed to ensuring our approach is consistent with our national and international disclosure obligations, and shall comply with all applicable laws, statutes, regulations.Considering the peculiar environment in which activities will be developed, no risks of SEA have been detected; activities with children and women will be implemented in safe spaces, and delivered by trained professionals. Moreover:1. Professionals working in the project belong to the same community of beneficiaries, having hence also a role of rights’ promoters among the families of the community, and the capacity to assess any possible SEA which will be immediately reported;2. Activities such as community awareness campaigns, do not only provide information regarding SEA, but help workers, volunteer and beneficiaries in detecting risks and reporting to specialised staff of Armadilla-ZAM in order to tackle any issue.
