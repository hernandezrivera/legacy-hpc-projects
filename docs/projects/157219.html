Project Summary

UMCOR will ensure that the proposed interventions are aligned with its own as well as WASH standard commitments to gender and diversity in emergency programming. The SDG targets on WASH pay special attention to women and girls especially in vulnerable situations and therefore the gender and age perspectives highlighted in this project is an important step towards the fulfillment of the SDGs. UMCOR will target women headed households, pregnant or lactating women, and men and children made vulnerable by the disaster. Beneficiaries will be children >5 , children 5-17 years of age , adults 18-59 years & adults > 60 years . The objective of this project is to contribute towards ensuring adequate and sustainable water services and, sanitation / hygiene promotion interventions, for 100,464 mixed population composed of IDPs, returnees and vulnerable residents in four localities of East Darfur State ( Daein, El Ferdous, Sheria & Yassin )Women represent 40% and Children 30% of the total beneficiaries. The project will adopt a bottom up participatory approach to ensure community ownership towards promotion of community self-reliance. This approach also guarantees the principles of accountability to the affected population. The project will give particular attention to strengthen the linkage between provision of WASH services and its impact on child malnutrition. Different gender and age groups needs will be integrated in the course of the project implementation. In addition, the project will consider the likelihoods of negative impact on environment and put in place the necessary mitigation strategies. The project monitoring, evaluation and learning system will ensure not only the project beneficiaries’ needs are fulfilled, but also the intervention is of relevance, cost effective and provided through an efficient service delivery mechanism. The project will document and share the learning’s with WASH Sector at national and state levels. More over the project will achieve this through rehabilitate of water yards with installation of generators , training of WASH committees & hand pumps repair teams , Conduct CATS/CLTS facilitation training to community leaders to facilitate implementation of CATs/CLTS for promotion of latrines construction , Construction of VIP latrines in schools , Distribution of soaps to vulnerable HH for 12 month , cleaning campaign , Jeri can cleaning campaign & distribution of Jeri can to HH , and training & support of hygiene promoters.

Needs

According to the HRP planning exercise 2019 , there are 68,361 IDPs , 29,277 returnees and 120, 121 vulnerable residents in need of services and assistance in East Darfur state. The targeted localities host a total of 135,354 individuals (54,373 IDPs, 23,856 returnees and 57,125 vulnerable residents). The government of Sudan reports that only 41.2% of H/Hs in East Darfur State have access to clean water. The WASH Sector and field data reveal a dire need of a range of WASH infrastructure, sanitation and hygiene promotion services in East Darfur State in general and in the 4 localities of Daein, El Ferdous , Sheria and Yassin in particular .The WASH needs have been further exacerbated by the influx of the South Sudanese refugees into State Darfur State since beginning of 2016 due to war and famine in South Sudan.. The access to clean water is limited in part because water infrastructure and efficiency is compromised, with pumps, wells, and generators in general disrepair due to improper usage and management. This circumstance has exacerbated the general infrastructure shortage, compelling populations to access unsafe water sources and purport parasitic infection & acute watery diarrhea . The sanitation and hygiene situation is also inadequate. UMCOR will target rehabilitation of 4 borehole in Daein, El Ferdous, Yassin & Sheria & 15 hand pumps in Sheria & Yassin localities. Moreover, UMCOR will provide sanitation facilities to IDPs and most vulnerable residents & returnees. This is in addition to the hygiene promotion activities for all the target population.

Impact on environment/natural resources

To mitigate of the environmental degradation resulting from cutting of trees, UMCOR will use cement blocks as opposed to fired/burnt bricks for fencing water yards. This will reduce desertification resulting from tree cutting The platform stand will also be constructed from cement block. The Environmental Impact of Spillage and Mud formation around water yards , UMCOR will •UMCOR in its design of water yard has incorporated separation of watering points for both animal and human points .This will minimize effects of flies from urine and wastes of animals contaminating drinking water•UMCOR will construct soak away pits at watering points to infiltrate any spillage into the ground.This will prevent mud formation resulting from hooves from animals while watering at the water yard- Use of solar pumping system for the boreholes and the generator will be used only when the solar system is either broken down or under maintenance - Include environmental messages to beneficiaries to raise awareness & also to support sustainable use and maintenance of WASH facilities- Together with water committees monitor water facilities & their impact on the environment & set quick mitigation measure.- Ensure WASH facilities in the school are adapted to the child age to guarantee proper use of facilities and reduce environmental risk- Support school ,communities & camps to implement a system for solid waste management , reduce packaging materials & discourage use of plastic bags- Reuse of grey water from hand washing facilities for extra water needs ( gardening )- Provide water committees & schools with cleaning kits- Include environmental issues in school hygiene clubs- Ensure that the constructed school in the schools are far away from water sources
