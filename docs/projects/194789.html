Project Summary

Across Sudan, GBV continues to be a life-threatening concern, exacerbated by a variety of factors including conflicts and recurring disasters. According to the first nation-wide, qualitative GBV assessment, “Voices from Sudan 2021”, physical and sexual violence are prevalent both inside and outside the home. Sexual violence is more pervasive in rural and conflict-affected communities including refugee camps,especially in SD and SK and based on the need assessment done by Gayat field staff in the targeted areas ,it reveals that there is food insecurity facing IDPs, host residents affording them into violenc, This project` intervention aim to address the current root causes of GVB , the root causes of displacement in conflict-affected areas, such as Darfur and South Kordofan areas . The interventions will focus on the most vulnerable populations (including refugees, internally displaced populations and host communities) through promoting resilience in security and secure livelihoods through interventions programmed based on : food security, livelihood and protection. Also, through local initiatives to manage migration more effectively ,to support rights-based and protection-sensitive efforts to combat and prevent violation such as GBV. Indeed, the latter threatens to undermine development gains and future opportunities, as it increases the vulnerability of households, ethnic and resource-based conflict, and consequent dispossession and forced displacement. The targeted areas of the project The intervention will be handle through two interventions targeting 8800 person (gender sensitive)by improving SFL as sources of violation and ensuring access to Protection assistance for community base protection services through community base structure. The targeted groups will be women , youth , adolescents(gender sensitive) with more consideration given to women HHs and disable , the project will last for 12 months it will involve innovation, good practices and lesson learns and will be share and exchanged regularly in the coordination's meeting , reporting to the donor. Gayat will utilized its experiences, potential personnel and volunteers towards achieving the best results of this project.
