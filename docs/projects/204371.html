Project Summary

This project is planned to provide an integrated emergency shelter, non-food items and environmentally friendly transitional shelters for conflict affected people. The project will target 370,000 people 74,000 households (male, female, elderly, boys and girls) as follows returnees, IDPs and resident communities, in five Darfur states (50%), Jazeera (25%), East Sudan (10% and South Kordofan (15%). The project will ensure that the target communities will have adequate ESNFIs items to ensure their most basic human needs as it relates to the maintenance of health, privacy and dignity and it’s easily within their reach. SORR will undertake periodic joint inter-agency assessments with special consideration to female headed households to inform the project on the new and emerging situations in the targeted areas. Post-distribution surveys will be an integral part of the project undertaken with ESNFI project or other partners of the sector in the targeted locations to evaluate the effectiveness of the distributions. SORR is a partner in the ESNFI sector and has previous and ongoing experience in distributing NFIs and constructing ES and transitional shelters as well as cash based interventions.
The 370,000 targeted people will receive NFIs and Essential shelter materials for 74,000 households (44,400 female and 29,600 male) including cash based interventions (CBI).
SORR will conduct needs assessments and verification of beneficiaries of ES/NFI to ensure priority assistance is given to people with specific needs (PSNs), EVIS and the most vulnerable in line with agreed sector guidelines. Assessments and distributions will be done in collaboration with stakeholders, community, women’s associations, on the ground. fire and flood awareness and environmental protection will be undertaken to ensure capacity is built with special consideration to role of gender, skills built and environment is protected. SORR will coordinate with stake holders like UNHCR, UNOCHA and partners on the ground.

1 - Needs

Fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has lasted for four consecutive weeks. Over 1,000,000 people have been newly displaced by the conflict since 15 April (OCHA flash update), including 736,200 people displaced internally and 200,000 who have crossed into neighboring countries. Due to the increasing number of displacements and most vulnerable Protracted displaced people across Sudan, SORR will coordinate with stake holders like UNHCR, UNOCHA and partners on the ground to provide 370,000 targeted people will receive NFIs and Essential shelter materials as well as CBI interventions for 74,000 households (44,400 female and 29,600 male), and training of IDPs and returnees vulnerable households in transitional shelter and fuel eﬃciency environmentally friendly manner and further provide renewal distribution in the targeted location. SORR further plans to provide NFIs support to most vulnerable women headed households of both Protracted IDPs in need and returnees utilizing the common pipeline. This shall be done in collaboration with stake holders, community, women’s associations, and other UN agencies on the ground. SORR will conduct assessments and verifications of beneficiaries of ES/NFI to ensure priority assistance is given to people with specific needs (PWSNs) and the most vulnerable in line with agreed sector guidelines. However disaster risk reduction and environmental conservation will be undertaken to ensure resilience is built with consideration to role of gender, skills built and environment is protected. Affected populations will continue to need life-saving NFI Kits, these items help restore a minimal sense of dignity and protection against exposure to the elements and mitigate health risks. IDPs returning to their places of origin, resettling in stable areas or integrating within host communities are also in need of Shelter/NFI assistance to facilitate and stabilize. Hence SORR is operating in multiple states across Sudan, there is a wide gap for protracted idps and returnees who will be verified targeting the most vulnerable and the most affected households by flooding and need replenishment due to the upcoming rainy season.
