Background
According to UNICEF, every child has the right to quality education which includes; access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) services while in school. Students spend a significant portion of their day at school, where WASH services have impacted on their learning, health and dignity, particularly for girls. The inclusion of WASH in schools is the Sustainable Development Goals (targets 4.a, 6.1, 6.2). However, many reports have documented lack of water and sanitation facilities in schools.
As a result of this, there is a remarkable rise in academic setbacks for pupils/students and teachers in school institutions. For example lack of water and sanitation facilities in schools may facilitates the spread of diseases such as water-borne diseases (cholera) and even restricts pupils/students from using the restrooms. These may render learning environments very unfriendly.
Many WASH facilities due to the “Anglophone crisis” have been disabled or rendered non-functional which is a call for concerns as schools are resuming in many communities. Thus there is a need for WASH assessments and implementations in school institutions. Accessing the WASH facilities in schools institutions in these communities will promote health/well-being and enhance educational outcomes by creating a safer and healthier learning environment,
Objectives
• Access to safe drinking water
• Improve sanitation and hygiene facilities
• Promote health and educational developments
• Capacity building on wash services
Targets
The project will be implemented at Government Primary School (G.S) Great Soppo Group one, located in the Fako Division of Cameroon’s South West Region, around 40 9’ N 90 17’E. The primary beneficiaries are the pupils and teachers made up of 500 pupils, 10 teachers, and 250 IDPs. This indicates that the school has a high population of vulnerable pupils with sensitive hygiene needs and concerns hence the need to implement WASH services in the school premises.
Methodology
• Need assessments: During our back-to-school intervention in Government Primary School Great Soppo, Group One, where we provided school accessories (books, pens, pencils, rulers, erasers) to the vulnerable pupils, we identified the lack of WASH facilities (no water availability and incomplete toilet structure) in the school. As a result, a pre-assessment survey was conducted to assess the impacts of lack of WASH services on campus.
• Stake holders engagement, awareness and sensitization campaigns: Mobilization of available stakeholders and awareness raising campaigns sensitizing the pupils and teachers on the needs and benefits of WASH availabilities on campus.
• Procurement of building materials and equipments. Procurement of all the materials needed for the water connection and toilets constructions.
• Water connection: Establishment of sustainable and clean water tapped from CAMWATER supply.
• Toilet rehabilitation: Renovation of the uncompleted toilet structure.
• Capacity Building workshops: To train teachers and pupils on capacity building and maintenance of WASH facilities within the school institution .
• Monitoring and Evaluation: Post assessment surveys will be conducted to assess the progress, effectiveness, and impacts of the WASH services adoption and use among pupils and teachers.
Timeline
This project will follow a detailed timeline from needs assessment (October 2024) through implementation to report writing (August 2025).
Conclusion
This project aims at providing WASH services to foster an environment conducive to learning by improving health and educational outcomes to pupils and teachers.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

NeedsThe main objective of this project is to provide clean water and sanitation facilities to Government Primary School Great Soppo, Group One. The specific objective includes the following:• Provide safe drinking water• Improve sanitation and hygiene facilities• Promote health and educational developments • Capacity building on wash services Activities• Need assessments: During our back-to-school intervention in Government Primary School Great Soppo, Group One, where we provided school accessories (books, pens, pencils, rulers, erasers) to the vulnerable pupils, we identified the lack of WASH facilities (no water availability and incomplete toilet structure) in the school institutions. As a result, a pre-assessment survey was conducted to assess the impacts of lack of WASH services on campus.• Stake holders engagement, awareness and sensitization campaigns: After the need assessment survey implementation, we mobilized the available stakeholders (Divisional Delegate of Basic and Secondary Education, community focal points, local experts for construction work, pupils and staffs, Camp water Manager, and HSFAN staffs), and did an awareness raising campaigns highlighting and sensitizing the pupils and teachers on the needs and benefits of WASH availabilities (toilets and clean water facilities) on campus. The pupils and teachers were sensitized and educated on proper hygiene practices such as handwashing with soap, menstrual hygiene management and sanitation facilities. • Procurement of building materials and equipments. Procurement of all the materials needed for the water connection and toilets constructions.• Water connection: A sustainable source of clean water will be establish within the school institution connected from a water supply company (CAMWATER) with a capacity to meet the daily water needs of the school population (500 students, 10 teachers, 200 IDPs).• Toilet rehabilitation: Renovation of the uncompleted toilet structures to ensure proper hygiene and privacy needs of the pupils/student and teachers.• Capacity Building workshops: Capacity building workshops will be done to train teachers and pupils on maintaining water and sanitation facilities within the school institution . The aim is to increase the capacity of school personnel to ensure the long-term functionality of the water and sanitation facilities and to reduce risk of breakdowns and disruptions in WASH services.• Monitoring and Evaluation: Monitoring and evaluation will be done systematically throughout this project to assess the progress, effectiveness, and impact of the WASH services at Government Primary School Great Soppo, Group One. Post assessment survey will be conducted to measure adoption and changes in knowledge and attitudes towards WASH services among pupils and teachers. Both quantitative and qualitative data on pupils attendance rate, health indicators, educational performance matrices etc will be collected and analyzed with the use of kobo collect tools to assess project’s impacts and effectiveness. • Project report: A comprehensive report will be prepared upon project completion, including achievement of objectives, impact on WASH access and hygiene practices, sustainability plan, lessons learned, and possible recommendations. TargetsThe project will be implemented at Government Primary School (G.S) Great Soppo Group one. G.S Great soppo is located in the Fako Division of Cameroon’s South West Region, around 40 9’ N 90 17’E. The primary beneficiaries are the pupils and teachers of G.S Great Soppo, Group one, including a 250 internally displaced persons (IDPs) aged 5 to 18 years. The total School population is made up of (500 pupils, 10 teachers, 250 IDPs). This indicates that the school has a high population of vulnerable pupils with sensitive hygiene needs and concerns hence the need to implement WASH services in the school premises.
