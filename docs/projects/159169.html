Project Summary

READO proposition aims to ensuring that 2915 girls and boys (1465 boys, 1450 girls) affected by the crisis have uninterrupted access to education in 7 identified schools in 2 Districts of South West State, particularly in 5 schools in Baidoa (Barwaaqo Primary School, Yaiyow Kooyow IDP School, Jaraale Primary School, Barare IDP School and Awdinle Primary School) and 2 schools in Dinsoor (Holwadaq Primary School and Waaberi Primary School).
The project envisages 2 Outcomes and intends to improve the educational attainment, safety of Somali girls and boys affected by crisis, contributing to resilience building within increasingly peaceful, secure & democratic environment.
Outcome 1: Equitable access to education for emergency-affected children and youth is increased
Community Outreach Initiatives (COI), distribution of supplementary food, construction/rehabilitation of 7 learning spaces, distribution of teaching and learning material, learners’ kits, safe transport for children will be implemented. Moreover, READO will provide the schools with tanks to store water in order to promote sustained access to water while constructing latrines and hand washing facilities in the target schools.
Outcome 2: Learning outcomes are improved for emergency-affected children and teachers should be in the position to act as role models for the children, to give them the strength to imagine for themselves a better future and to thrive for it.
In Somalia, teachers are often working on volunteer basis or underpaid, therefore READO will provide capacity building for 85 school personnel [52 teachers (21 female) and 33 CECs (16 female) and teachers’ incentives over a period of 12 months.
Protective learning environments are established and strengthened for emergency-affected children at school level: READO will conduct trainings on psycho-social care of the children and child protection for schools’ personnel, promoting awareness and coordination on child protection.
Communities are more resilient to shocks and prioritize education for children
Communities will enhance their capacities to cope with shocks, particularly drought and conflict. Emergency preparedness committees will be formed in each partner schools and tasked to monitor, evaluate and report to inform appropriate action for both prevention and response on child protection issues as a child safe-guarding mechanism.

Project Activities

Objective: To improve the educational attainment, safety of Somali girls and boys affected by crisis, contributing to resilience building within increasingly peaceful environment.Outcome 1: Increased equitable access to education for crises affected boys, girls and adolescences (including children with disabilities) in Baidoa and DiinsoorOutput 1.1: 2915 (1450 girls) crises affected children including (children with disabilities) access uninterrupted learning in safe and protective environment fostering retention and increased enrollment in 7 schools in Baidoa and Diinsor.Activity 1.1.1 Community Outreach Initiatives led by the CECs and CP focal points to sensitize the community on the importance of education and promotion of child rights.Activity 1.1.2 Construction of 6 Temporary Learning Spaces in Baidoa and 4 in Diinsor giving a total of 10 TLS erected according to the SPHERE standards for Education considering context specificity to each location and school and in line with the guidelines for minimum standards of the Somalia Education Cluster.Activity 1.1.3 10 provide teaching & learning materials (desks, teachers’ chairs, blackboards) to 2915 (1450 girls) crises affected children including learning kits in the form of books, pens, ruler, pencils and a school bag. Each kit will have Four (4) A4 exercise books, 4 pens, 4 pencils, 2 colored pencils, 2 Erasers and 1 sharpener and school uniforms.Activity 1.1.4 READO will provide a monthly dry food ratio corresponding to 2kg of rice, 2kg of flour, 2kg of sugar and 2.5 liters of cooking oil per child for a period of 9 months to enable 2915 (1450 girls) learners in 7 partner schools.Outcome 2 Learning outcomes are improved for 2915 (1450 girls) crises affected children and youth in Baidoa and DiinsoorOutput 2.1The learning outcome of 2915 (1450 girls) crises affected children are improved by providing quality relevant trainings and resources to Community Education Committees and Teachers within Baidoa and Diinsoor.Activity 2.1.1 Train 33 (17 males, 16 female) CEC’s from the 7 schools on the knowledge to manage schools, clarify their roles and responsibilities in EIE management, and entice community engagement towards access of education by crises affected children. Activity 2.1.2 Train 52 (31 males, 21 female) IDP school teachers on Pedagogy and Child Protection, EiE, and school’s management. Additionally, the trainings will cover also minimum standards of education in emergencies. Activity 2.1.3 Support 52 (31 males, 21 female) IDP school teachers and head teachers for a duration of 9 months through teachers’ incentives corresponding to the amount of 100 USD per month for teachers and $150 for head teachers.Activity 2.1.4 Support the production of IEC materials, meeting transport costs, per diems and refreshments during meetings with CECs and teachers.Activity 2.1.5 Identify and train 14 selected teachers (7 female) on psycho-social care of the children and child protection for schools’ personnel, promoting awareness and coordination on child protection.Activity 2.1.6 Provide support for 14 focal point teachers to report, document and refer child protection cases.Activity 2.1.7 Establish Emergency preparedness committee in each partner schools and task them to monitor, evaluate and report cases.Output 2.2 READO will Improve access to sustained WASH facilities by providing learners with sustained access to safe drinking water.Activity 2.2.1 Provide the 7 partner schools with a 10,000-liter water tank for storing water and a further distribution of 10,000 liters of water twice a week in each school providing some 2915 learners with 140,000 liters of safe drinkable water (7 liters per child per day).Activity 2.2.2 Construct 7 additional gender appropriate and culturally acceptable latrines for 2915 learners (1450 girls) including hand washing facilities in 7 schools in Baidoa and Diinsor to continue learning without skipping lessons.

Mitigation Measures

READO will implement a training regarding the information needed to identify hazards, evaluate benefits and costs, make risk-informed decisions and implement preventive and mitigation measures to reduce risk exposure. The training will be conducted for scholastic personnel, parents of the children and relevant local authorities. After the training a committee/group of adults will be identified and tasked to develop an Action Plan which will serve the school and the surrounding community to identify and mitigate the impact of possible risks and to put preventative and responsive measures in place.
