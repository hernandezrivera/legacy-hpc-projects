Project Summary

The project responds to child protection by implementing all standards to ensure the rehabilitation of children associated with armed groups and forces (CAAFAG), provision of different services to children survivors of any type of abuse, children with disabilities and children separated from their families through case management, provision of individualized and group psychosocial support through multipurpose spaces, positive parenting, prevention and response to EAS and GBV cases. Project wil target the returns, IDP and Host communities and project pretend to target 23443 children's including CWD and 2345 adults.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Currently there are reports indicating an increasing number of the population returning to the areas of origin particularly in the districts of Palma, Nangade and Mocímboa da Praia, as a result of the stabilization and improved security. Population returning to their original areas are in need of humanitarian assistance and protection services, particularly services for separated and unaccompanied children (UASC), namely family tracing and reunification services, the monitoring and community reintegration of children associated with armed groups and forces (CAAFAG), mental health and psychosocial support, case management, health, protection, legal support involving birth registration, education, water, and sanitation.It is in this context that the Foundation for Community Development (FDC) and the United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF) have established the partnership with the objective of providing support to the government in strengthening their capacity to manage cases for children’s access to critical child protection services for girls, boys, adolescents, and their families affected by the humanitarian crisis in Cabo Delgado Province, while fostering key child protection interventions in the field. Through this partnership, technical support will be provided at various levels, thus contributing to the reinforcement of actions in the child protection sector with a view to providing children and their families with the support and guidance necessary to improve access to basic and complementary protection services.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will consist of technical and training support to technicians from the District Services for Health, Women and Social Action in establishing an effective and functional case management system at institutional and community level through Community Child Protection Committees (CCPCs). The main objective of the project is to contribute to strengthening a functional and comprehensive case management system for the protection of vulnerable children, separated and unaccompanied children (UASC) and Children associated in with armed conflict (CAAC), protection of children from all forms of violence, including prevention and protection from VBG and PSEA, and provision of mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), complemented by birth registration services and social protection programmes. Through this partnership, technical support will be provided at various levels, thus contributing to the strengthening of the social protection sector with a view to provide vulnerable families with the support and guidance necessary for them to access protection, services. Through the Multipurpose Centers (MPCs), the project will help accelerate the implementation of a new concept for provision of integrated services to children, adolescents and caregivers across the districts of Mueda, Nangade and Mocimboa da Praia

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Through door-to-door campaigns, dissemination of key messages, security audits, establishment of risk reporting and response mechanisms. Leadership, other actors and local government will be involved to ensure the efficient and effective implementation of the project. Therefore, district officers, case workers, activists and facilitators will be recruited and trained, who in turn will follow up on identified cases and are also responsible for identifying possible risks and developing actions to mitigate them.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

From the MPC project, there will be developed a professional skills and competencies can be achieved through the integration of the carpentry, cutting and sewing skills, the awarding of scholarships to girls and boys with visible vulnerabilities and the support for families/caregivers of children to develop activities that aim to achieve educational recovery and encomonic empowerment. The main objective is to have children's caregivers (elderly, mothers, uncles, brothers, etc.), members of CCPCs, members of host families benefit from these opportunities

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Through case workers, facilitators and activists working at the community level will identify children with disabilities and develop an action plan for providing means of redress and referral to service providers. The multipurpose spaces that must be established must accommodate children with disabilities without distinction in order to guarantee inclusion.The project should also allocate means of compensation to children with disabilities and ensure that messages about preserving their rights and duties are considered.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project always preserves the environment, ensuring that where multipurpose spaces will be established there is no destruction of vegetation but rather the planting of more trees and preserving the green environment.
