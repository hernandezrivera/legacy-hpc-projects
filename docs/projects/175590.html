1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to provide emergency response to the war displaced populations of Cabo Delgado resettled in Corrane Meconta District in Nampula with a view to assisting all displaced children in host families or accommodation and relocation centers to continue their education at home and in living spaces. temporary apprenticeships with the participation of the local and administrative authorities of the district and the provincial government. A series of activities will be carried out, namely: to raise awareness among parents, caregivers of displaced children, heads of family and the community in general about the importance of education, particularly girls' education.The new neighborhood was identified, to accommodate the displaced people who are in the whole province of Nampula, more children are of school age, hence the need to carry out an assistance project.The target group:It will be essential to work closely with community authorities and other locally accepted key social authorities to deepen understanding about child education, DPEDH, SDEJT, SDSMAS and other local structures that work to defend the rights of children. Gaining their support is essential to overcome resistance to the promotion of gender equality and the rights of the child.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Assist the school integration of displaced children in host families to continue their education at home and in temporary learning spaces.2. Survey affected or vulnerable children in the region where the project is being implemented, disagreeing by gender, age and people with disabilities needing special assistance;3. Sensitize parents, caregivers of displaced children and family heads and the community in general about the importance of education, particularly girls' education.4. Identification of temporary physical learning spaces close to children's homes;5. Identification and training of facilitators to work with children who cannot return to school immediately;6. Refer and facilitate the integration and reintegration of children into the national education system at the local level and other essential basic services;7. Facilitate the acquisition of school supplies for children, through the identification of potential partners that have these components.8. Facilitate liaison and communication between parents, school councils and school management through visits to monitor children's educational performance;9. Identify, refer and facilitate access to birth registration documents, attesting poverty to children and their families, according to their needs.10. Conduct GBV sessions, premature marriages, early pregnancies, enrollment and retaining the child at school,11. Sensitize and involve community leaders in the facilitation process for the registration and legalization of documents, including poverty attestation.12. Monitoring and evaluation of project activities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The following appear as risks in the implementation of this project:• Insufficient management capacity for this project, especially for controlling the flow of funds and the budget, constitutes a risk;• Poor response from the people involved to provide important data for the project;• Inadequate coordination between key project actors, in particular the various service providers operating at district level (public, NGO's and private entities), as well as with the activities of district administrations;• Women and girls are at greater risk of gender-based violence, facing barriers in accessing the police, justice and sexual abuse in the resettlement zone;• Due to the conditions in which inadequate food is exposed, they are at risk of being malnourished, and contagious diseases including COVID-19, especially children, women and the elderly, will be exposed;To encourage access to assistance, participation and empowerment of people, it will be necessary to involve the community itself to be a key player in the implementation of actions through training to support, when required, the activation of the health cluster and other specialized work groups, which activated within the framework of Humanitarian Reform.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

People will be involved from the beginning, through presentation meetings that include local community and administrative structures, parents and guardians will receive training.Also in the project, some parallel activities will be implemented, such as Functional Literacy, promotion of Savings and Credit Groups.Temporary learning locations for children's learning will be prepared by the children's parents. The project facilitators will be elected from among the community members through an acceptance starting profile.In literacy, parents and guardians as they participate in literacy and adult education will learn the contents related to governance, democratic participation to improve reading and writing skills, thus increasing the level of awareness about monitoring education. of your children. While in the Savings and Credit Groups, people will have the opportunity to understand the basics of financial literacy where they can carry out income-generating activities.With these approaches, people will be involved and encouraged to participate actively in all phases and, consequently, will be empowered, so that after the financing is finished, activities will continue.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disabled girls face double discrimination due to gender and disability. People with disabilities are seen as a burden on society because they need support and often find it difficult to find a job. This is even more true for girls who, due to gender norms, tend to be restricted to home, rather than carefully studying economic or educational opportunities. Understanding the interrelated nature of these essential barriers to girls 'education, UATAF-AFC will apply an integrated, multisectoral, intersectoral and gender-transforming approach to effectively support girls' enrollment and retention in school, as well as learning outcomes positive for girls, including young mothers and girls with disabilities.UATAF-AFC will engage key stakeholders at the home, community, school and provincial and district levels to mitigate the risks of reinforcing inequalities between women, men, girls and boys, or even creating unwanted results that can further exacerbate existing gender inequalities.Girls in particularly vulnerable situations (that is, survivors of SGBV or pregnant teenagers / mothers / girls with disabilities) will participate in a mentoring process to strengthen their skills, self-confidence and decision - making education about their future.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The activities planned for this project have no environmental impact that may be necessary for its assessment. In the case of a resettlement site for displaced persons, there will be additional pressure on the same environment, but for this purpose the project may envisage sensitizing families and communities to clean and eliminate water pools in the backyards and the surrounding areas, referring children to health facilities in cases of having malnourished children due to vulnerability.
