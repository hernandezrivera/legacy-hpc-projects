Project Summary

The emergency health response aims to save lives, contribute towards improved physical and mental wellbeing of 389,988 individuals and their families. This will be realized through provision of sustainable access to quality and comprehensive health care services .The interventions will support 35 Mother and Child Clinics, 50 Primary health units/Health posts and 7 mobile teams located in 15 districts spread across 6 regions in Somalia. The interventions are aimed at contributing towards a reduction in morbidity and mortality among children, women , families and people living with mental and physical disabilities .Measures are aimed at disease prevention such as vaccination services to prevent vaccine preventable diseases, Clinical diagnosis and management of common illnesses ,disease outbreak surveillance , reporting, investigation and response, communicable disease management , comprehensive basic reproductive health care services aligned to Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) , health care worker capacity building to enhance quality of care provided to beneficiaries, referral provisions, medical supply chain management processes to minimize stock outs of essential medical supplies, community and stakeholder engagement for coordination , accountability and feedback response mechanisms. COVID-19 case detection, diagnosis and management, Gender Based Violence and protection incident management as well as psychological First aid will be integrated in the project interventions .Timed and targeted counselling (ttC) aimed at enhancing demand and health seeking behavior will be adopted across the target project locations.
WVI will enable PLWDs access health care services through the disability friendly health facility set up including latrines provisions .The project will utilize CHWs who will do home visits and follow up on PLWD HHs , facilitate their referrals and movement to the health facility to seek health care services. Special emphasis on health promotion and home follows up will be done targeting pregnant women and HHs with children living with disabilities. Moreover, WV through continuous engagement with community leaders, disability associations and the protection unit will sensitize communities to raise awareness on health rights of PLWDs to mitigate stigma. In each community engagement, WV will ensure and facilitate representation with 15 % of participants being PLWD during all community engagement forums. Boys and girls, men and women living with disabilities attended to will be closely followed up to ensure continuity in care thus transforming their health and well-being.

Mitigation Measures

WV will put robust measures to address barriers that hinder access to health services or prevent health-seeking behaviors especially among youth, disabled, illiterate and poor, GBV and protection affected beneficiaries. WV will coordinate its activities and identify disadvantaged and vulnerable groups (IDPs, minority clans, other marginalized) that may face unequal access to health, preventing unintended harm working in close collaboration with other community resource persons. Their feedback will influence realignment of response to continuously adapt and cater to health needs of all groups including GBV and protection survivors’ .In consultation with community members and with MOH, WV will be responsible for selection of beneficiaries. WV will promote active and representative participation of different groups in communities to influence decisions that affect their lives. To ensure transparency, communities will be sensitized on available services, health priorities, their rights and what is expected of community members and the complaints and feedback mechanisms in place. WV will further work together with MOH to strengthen coordination mechanisms, which will ensure effective service provision and prevent duplication of efforts while reallocating and prioritizing resources to underserved areas. WV working with MOH will provide timely information to target population regarding project objective, interventions and timeline to make informed decisions and provide relevant comments. Inception meetings will be held both at regional and community level to share information on all aspects of interventions including support for SGBV, protection and evictions, Accountability and Feedback System: The project will take into consideration Inter-Agency Standing Committee commitment to affected populations. Functional feedback system will be established for continuous collection of feedback from community. The project will leverage on existing WV Community Response Map platform that facilitates online recording, tracking, compilation and visual mapping and feedback received from community members daily. A 3 digit Toll free hot line number already in use in the target locations and will be strengthened and integrated with Interactive Voice Response system, suggestion boxes and community help desk teams to facilitate timely feedback.WVI will enable PLWDs access health care services through the disability friendly health facility set up including latrines provisions .The project will utilize CHWs who will do home visits and follow up on PLWD HHs , facilitate their referrals and movement to the health facility to seek health care services. Please provide further explanation

Implementation of the proposed interventions will be done in collaboration with respective MOH leadership ,health cluster , local administration and LNGOS. Health care worker capacity building , health service demand creation through awareness and subsequent influence on health seeking behavior , medical supply management systems ,DHIS-2 systems support are part of the health systems strengthening measures geared towards building longer term durable solutions .The integrated approach inclusive of outreaches will enable hard to reach and IDP beneficiaries access basic health care services. Moreover, the community engagements , community dialogue, accountability and feedback mechanisms will inform future health development plans ,resource mobilization and allocation in the targeted locations based on multi-stakeholder needs identification and prioritization processes .The disease surveillance and health information generated will be utilized to inform health sector development plans both at district and regional levels including service planning for displaced and returnee populations, Over the years WV working closely with community resource groups have established and continuously build capacity of Community integrated early warning early action committees, these committees comprise of multi-sector representation and addresses all pertinent issues arising within the community regardless of sector involved , providing dispute resolutions for peaceful co-existence .They are recognised by administration in the respective locations and provide a legal framework of engagement .
