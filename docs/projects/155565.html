Project Summary

The project focuses on the urgent needs of women and girls affected by the Socio-political crisis in Northwest and Southwest regions, particularly in the areas of protection and livelihood. Overall goal of the initiative is to ensure that women and girls lead, participate in, and are empowered by and benefit from humanitarian response and recovery efforts by meeting specific needs of women and girls as well as mainstreaming gender in humanitarian actions. It builds on the success of UN Women’s humanitarian work since 2014 in response to the refugee crisis from Central African Republic (CAR) in the East and the Boko Haram crisis in the Far North, much of which was supported by the Government of Japan (GoJ). These consecutive initiatives have improved the lives of thousands of women and girls by providing an integrated service package at women cohesion spaces created in the refugee camps.

This project consists of four outcome areas:
• Outcome 1: Women and girls in affected areas have increased protection against gender-based violence (GBV)
• Outcome 2: Women and girls have new sources of income and economic opportunities
• Outcome 3: Women’s leadership and engagement is increased in community mobilization, social cohesion and decision making
• Outcome 4: Gender analysis and assessments are reflected in multi-sectoral humanitarian crisis response planning and programming
The project is in line with the Cameroon Humanitarian Response Plan 2018, and Emergency Response Plan for Northwest and Southwest regions.
