Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to strengthen Accountability to Affected Populations and CCCM services for the populations affected by conflicts in Cabo Delgado, in line with the commitment to AAP by humanitarian partners. In an effort to strengthen AAP and to ensure that the needs of the returnee populations are met, the project also aims to improve Camp Coordinition Camp Management services, access to key information on available services and assistance for individuals and strengthen two-way communication channels to engage affected communities regularly. This will improve access to information for returnee populations who are facing acute humanitarian needs and enable speedy response to their needs through community feedback mechanisms established under this project. While seeking to improve living standards and the responsible provision of services, the intervention will seek to strengthen community engagement and participation through focus group discussions, committees of different social groups (women, people with disabilities, boys and girls), information dissemination and door-to-door sensitization.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

An estimated 446K displaced persons including returnees and people affected by displacement are in need of Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) services in 2025. This includes 164K IDPs in protracted displacement who are unable to return home due to ongoing conflict in some potential areas of return and those whose housing, land and property have either been destroyed or occupied. There is no significant decrease from the 600K people who needed assistance in 2024. It is therefore necessary to ensure that affected people have equitable access to humanitarian assistance services through an effective coordination, community engagement and needs-based approach while seeking durable solutions. With this project, ASMOG will contribute to respond the HRP response plan for 2025, in the CCCM sector and meet the following objectives: CO1: Ensure equal access and needs based assistance to improve the quality of integrated services for populations affected by displacement through Coordination and monitoringCO2: Respond to vulnerable population by addressing protection gaps and strengthen the predictability and effectiveness of multi sectorial interventions through robust community engagement and participationCO3: Strengthen inclusive community participation to ensure local ownership, self-governance and self-reliance

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

In order to ensure none of the CCCM project activities and modalities contribute to further discrimination of marginalized groups, an assessment will be conducted to determine the dynamics that exist in the community including factors exacerbating marginalization. The ASMOG CCCM team will promote representation of marginalized communities in decision-making and community governance structures (i.e.Community Disabled Committee; Proportional Representation among Block Leaders; bilateral consultation with leaders of newly arrived communities).Camp Management will ensure that marginalized groups are reached with all public awareness messages, information about project activities and concerns regarding access to services. With an accessible complaints and feedback mechanism, most marginalized groups will be able to address issues of exclusion/lack of information and lack of representation. Prior to any CCCM intervention in new sites, Camp Management teams will regularly consult with different actors including community leaders, state and local authorities, OCHA and partners regarding camp administration and land usage to ensure that the displaced population will not be forcibly evicted from the site. Where returns are affected by housing, land and property (HLP) issues, Camp Management will link them with HLP actors for assistance. CCCM will participate in inter-agency safety audits to identify gender-based violence (GBV) risks and address related issues. In addition, Camp Management will collaborate with protection and security actors for the development of community based protection measures that enhance the safety of women and girls reducing their exposure to sexual violence including proper site planning, infrastructure, and site care and maintenance interventions to reduce GBV risk (i.e. Solar lighting, elimination of dead-end corridors, better designed public spaces) and engaging with women’s governance structures, representatives, and IDPs directly to identify and address safety concerns. Through the GBV referral pathways Camp Management team will be able to refer GBV cases to GBV actors for assistance

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

ASMOG will use the CCCM Cluster tools to carry out rapid assessments with the target population to ensure that the project is in line with their needs before and during the implementation of activities. Through the creation of target group committees, ASMOG will ensure that vulnerable groups including elderly women and people with disabilities have a specific forum where they can present their concerns and complaints regarding restrictions on access to humanitarian services.The project will train these groups in PSEA and gender-based violence and provide them with information leaflets to raise awareness of the issue. In addition, all the channels for referring PSEA cases will be disseminated and promoted. Women and elderly empowerment activities, promoting a community-involved approach to preventing and mitigating Gender-Based Violence (GBV) and strengthening community knowledge.ASMOG will regularly make consultation sessions in all phase of the project development, with affected communities and key stakeholders to ensure that the programme is designed in a way that responds to the needs and preferences of the affected population, including during the project implementation and phase out to ensure project owenship and community empowerment. The programme design is intended to meet immediate life-saving needs. Post-distribution monitoring (PDM) and on-site distribution monitoring tools will be used to track beneficiary satisfaction with different aspects of the intervention. Monitoring data will be consolidated and analysed, improving ongoing programming and informing future response plans.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Through collaboration with organizations that work directly with people with disabilities and local leaders, ASMOG will map people with disabilities and identify their needs and challenges. As a way of ensuring the active participation of people with disabilities in the decision-making process, inclusion committees will be set up and focus group discussions conducted to identify barriers and adapt humanitarian services to their needs.The project will seek to minimize risks related to access barriers by prioritizing inclusive communication. ASMOG will also prioritize raising awareness among communities about the rights and inclusion of people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Site planning is crucial to reduce the risk of creating a negative impact on the environment and the practices of the communities which can be addressed through community engagement. thorough assessment of the area prior to implementation, the site planner will be able to adjust for environmental degradation of resources such as water sources and trees. In addition, the interventions will use locally sourced materials wherever possible to reduce the transportation requirements and to ensure that materials are appropriate for the context. Labour based interventions will be prioritized reducing use of fuel and increasing community participation in the sites. Community engagement throughout the project will ensure community buy-in to environmentally friendly practices through community committees. Community committees responsible for site maintenance will equipped with disaster risk reduction as well as environmentally sustainability knowledge and skills. Decommissioning of infrastructure particularly in temporary sites will take into account any potential negative effects on the environment. Environmental reclamation will be carried out and promoted. Sustainable bamboo exploitation and deforestation efforts will be supported.
