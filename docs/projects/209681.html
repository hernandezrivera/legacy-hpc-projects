Project Summary

Starting with the needs, HNO2024 underscores the imperative for urgent humanitarian assistance in Syria, where 16.7 million people, including 8.4 million females and 8.3 million males, require support—a significant increase from 15.3 million in 2023. This dire situation stems from a protracted economic crisis exacerbated by a series of earthquakes in February 2023, which uprooted hundreds of thousands of families, caused nearly 5,900 deaths, injured over 12,800 people, and severely damaged already fragile infrastructures. The economic downturn, coupled with high inflation, currency depreciation, and rising commodity prices, led to increased poverty and heightened reliance on humanitarian aid, creating continuous cycles of vulnerability.
[*] Bonyan Organization designed its 2024 ERL intervention based on the analysis of MSNA data in NW Syrian, need severity for ERL assistance, and key messages of HNO 2024, as well as, in line with SOs of HRP and ERL Cluster.
The project aims to strengthen access to livelihoods, improve access to basic services and infrastructure, and support efforts of local economy-recovery in addition to support social cohesion for the affected-population, for 21,900 INDVs (11,075 M/ 10,875 F - 75% are children and youth) in Aleppo and Idleb.
The project activities fall into three main categories as follows:
[1] Support the Maintenance & Improve Equitable Access to Basic Services targeting 13,050 INDVs.
[2] Increase Access to Livelihood and Job Opportunities targeting 2,850 INDVs.
[3] Strengthen Social Cohesion by Empowering Communities to Lead Early Recovery Processes Together targeting 6,000 INDVs.
The details of the activities included in the extended summary paragraph.
[[**]] As of 2025 Bonyan expect to expand its ERL activities by 30% reaching 28,000 INDVs with a total budget of 1.6M USD
[*] Brief on PSEAH prevention measures
To fortify against sexual exploitation and abuse, Bonyan Organization institutes a robust Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) strategy.
PSEA Focal Persons are appointed to address allegations promptly, ensuring investigation and reporting follow standard procedures. Persons with disabilities receive PSEA awareness and direct communication access. Call center numbers and PSEA-related materials are promoted in project areas. Survivor-centered approaches guarantee information access and immediate assistance.
Risk: SEA Violate from one of the staff, Mitigation includes staff training, community feedback, and safe community centers for domestic violence. Mandatory PSEA training, CoC, and complaint mechanisms.
[*] Key Risks and Mitigations:
(1) Risk: Security risks may hinder the safe implementation of activities. - Mitigation: Collaborating with local authorities and humanitarian agencies, conducting thorough security assessments, and maintaining flexibility in project timelines to adapt to changing security situations.
(2) Risk: Vulnerable groups may be overlooked - Mitigation: Regular review and updating of vulnerability criteria, extensive training for community committees, and the establishment of a robust feedback mechanism to ensure continual refinement and inclusivity in beneficiary selection.
(3) Risk: Inadequate coordination may lead to duplication of efforts or gaps in service delivery. -Mitigation: Establishing clear communication channels, active participation in cluster meetings, and regular sharing of project updates to ensure effective collaboration with relevant stakeholders, minimizing the risk of duplication or service delivery gaps.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Bonyan Organization will coordinate with relevant stakeholders to conduct MSNA to collect most recent data about ERL needs of IDPs, HCs and RTRNs (with keeping in mind the specific needs of different age and gender groups), in addition to collect any other necessary data Bonyan will assess the market system’s functionality and capacity to absorb increased demand for goods and services, and potential impact on markets to apply the "DO NO HARM" aspects Then, BO will select beneficiaries following transparent processes based on clear vulnerability and eligibility criteria to ensure provision of support to the most vulnerable. All activity will be documented, lessons learnt will be collected to measure impact of the activities and the community initiatives. On community level, the project will rehabilitate 15 classrooms for different schools as well as provide 5 schools with support to rehabilitate its WASH facilities and provide the required furniture, accounting for 2,000 children with access to safe learning environment as direct beneficiaries for the project.Also 2 km of roads to be repaired and another 2 km of sewage network, along with removing solid waste benefiting more than 11,000 INDVs who are settled in the surrounding communities for these infrastructures. Another aspect of the project is to Increase Access to Livelihood and Job Opportunities where several activities will be conducted to benefit 310 HHs and 1,200 INDVs to have stable income and access the job market reaching in total 2,850 INDVs. Activities ranging from supporting business entrepreneurship startup, provision of MSME grants, training programs on soft skills, technical business and TVET, initiating apprenticeship and On-Job Training programs, and lastly the Creation of short-term jobs through cash for work modality. That last part of the project will include a pilot activity where a community will be identified with the coordination of the cluster to conduct socio-economic context analysis followed with supporting 6 initiatives promoting social cohesion MEAL team of Bonyan will conduct ongoing monitoring of project activities to verify achievement of indicators and to correct any deviation. The PwD will be involved in all activities as direct beneficiaries in CfW or rehabilitation of infrastructures … etc. Workers with disabilities will be chosen so that they have a mild / moderate degree of disability, so that they can complete tasks that do not require much muscular effort. For example, these people can conduct periodic cleaning campaigns, wiring works for the lighting systems, data collection survey, or sensitization sessions for the community members. On the other hand, PwDs will benefit directly from the improved services including sewage and drainage systems, solar lighting, and rehabilitation of roads. Also through the selection criteria Women, and PwD will be fully participated in the activities that support generating income such as ( MSMEs, VT, ..etc)
