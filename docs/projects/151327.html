Project Summary

Despite above average Gu 2018 rains coupled with improved harvests and increased humanitarian support in Galgadud, Bakool, Bay and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia, Food Security situation is still fragile owing to impacts of past devastating drought that nearly culminated into famine exacerbated successive seasons of below normal rains has significantly impacted to sustained food insecurity, limited access to safe water and sustained critical malnutrition rates among children and pregnant lactating women in Galgadud, Bakool, Bay and Lower Shabelle Region, Somalia (FSNAU Post Gu Technical report of September 2018).
The above plight calls for timely life-saving Food security intervention to enable the worst drought , displaced, returnees and vulnerable host communities' men, women boys and girls in the project targeted locations that are yet to recover from impacts of past devastating drought to meet their immediate food food security needs through conditional and non conditional safety nets as well as earn income in form of cash for work to sustain their family food needs in order to mitigate already precarious food insecurity conditions and further restore their livelihoods and strengthen their resilience with suitable food security support programmes to cope with the current and future shocks.The project targets 28800 persons (14,861 females 13,939, male, 2880 boys and 2880 girls)

Needs

Despite increased Humanitarian support and good Gu 2018 rains that significantly improved food security status, impacts of past devastating drought that nearly culminated into famine are still prevalent owing to sustained food insecurity, limited access to safe water and persistent critical malnutrition rates among children aged under 5 and PLWs and families that were badly affected in Galgadud, Bakool, Bay and Lower shabelle Region, Somalia with GAM ≥15.0% classified as stressed and crisis IPC 2-3 (FSNAU Post Gu Technical of 2nd September 2018).The situation is compounded by trade disruptions in parts of South and central regions of Somalia due to military action and related insecurity as well as currency depreciation that has impacted to high food prices in the local markets hence exorbitant food prices that is affordable to the vulnerable host communities, IDPs, returnees (men, women, boys and girls) whose purchasing powers is completely eroded having lost their livelihood assets to flooding, past severe drought and successive seasons of below normal rains. The affected vulnerable host, IDPs, returnees families are living precariously under very poor conditions and rely on marginal unreliable livelihood strategies under diminishing coping mechanism. There are affected families that moved to the IDP camps in the neighboring regions seeking humanitarian support while some men and women have moved to the market centres in search of manual jobs. The situations calls for timely life-saving Food security intervention to enable the worst drought affected families in the project targeted locations in Wanlawayne, Barawe, Dinsor, Qansaxdhere,Wajid, Ceelbarde, Cadado and Dhusamareeb to receive safety nets and earn some income in form of cash for work to sustain their family food needs in order to mitigate already deteriorated food insecurity conditions and further restore their livelihoods and strengthen their resilience to cope with the current and future shocks.

Activities

1. Provision of food vouchers, conditional and unconditional cash transfers as well as e transfers to 1800 families most vulnerable and displaced women and children headed households and those with chronic illnesses and the disabled in Qansaxdhere, Dinsoor, Wajid and Ceelbarde Districts, Bay and Bakool region, Somalia 2. Rehabilitation of 5 water catchments, farming lands/rangelands, canals and 3 feeder roads in the targeted locations in Caadado, Dhusamareeb, Wanlaweyne and Barawe Districts, Galgadud and Lower Shabelle Regions on cash for work scheme to benefit 3600 vulnerable and displaced families3. Provision of hay/fodder to the most vulnerable and drought affected 2500 pastoral families in Qansaxdhere, Dinsor, Ceelbarde and Wajid Districts, Bay and Bakool Regions, Somalia 4. Provision of emergency veterinary services (animal vaccination and treatment) to 3200 most vulnerable and displaced pastoral families in the targeted villages in Cadado, Dhusamareeb, Qansaxdhere, Dinsor, Ceelbarde and Wajid Districts, Galgadud, Bay and Bakool Regions, Somalia 5. Distribution of farm inputs (seed package) to the 2800 most vulnerable and displaced farming households in Wanlawayne, Barawe, Ceelbarde, Wajid Qansaxdhere and Dinsor Districts Lower Shabelle, Bay and Bakool Regions6. Provision of tractor hours to 1800 farmers in Wanlawayne, Barawe, Ceelbardeand Wajid Districts, Lower Shabelle and Bakool Regions

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

1. Risk of exclusion of a number of some of the most vulnerable and more deserving IDPs getting from the project as beneficiaries of conditional and unconditional cash vouchers, cash for work, farm inputs, hay distribution and veterinary services.Mitigation measure:Conducting a comprehensive mobilization and sensitization campaign with the project staff, elders, religious leaders IDPs committee member, women heads of households and regional government representatives to outreach the targeted communities and ensure their awareness of the project and identification and selection of beneficiaries in a very transparent manner through a committee consisting of the mentioned stakeholders to ensure that actual targeted beneficiaries are selected for the project.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

1. Risk of exclusion of female, elderly and children/children headed households that are very vulnerable and more deserving from the project as beneficiaries of conditional and unconditional cash vouchers, cash for work, farm inputs, hay distribution and veterinary services.Mitigation measure:Conducting a comprehensive mobilization and sensitization campaign with the project staff, elders, religious leaders IDPs committee member, women heads of households and regional government representatives to outreach the targeted communities and ensure their awareness of the project and identification and selection of beneficiaries in a very transparent manner through a committee consisting of the mentioned stakeholders to ensure that actual targeted beneficiaries are selected for the project. Furthermore the selection committee should also have a representative of the targeted groups for selection (female, elderly, children/children headed households)

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Risk of the targeted beneficiaries getting caught in conflict while seeking their entitlements of the project; conditional and unconditional cash vouchers, cash for work, farm inputs, hay distribution and veterinary services: Mitigation: Ensuring project locations are in safe places away from the armed conflict and violence with potential risk on the targeted beneficiaries besides affecting the project implementation.
