Start Date

01/01/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

An emergency socio-ecological response to child protection issues which develops and implements community based child protection approaches to promote and strengthen the capacities of families and communities to address child protection issues.
The program aims to mitigate the immediate and long term effects of the current disasters in Zimbabwe for example drought, cyclone Idai, cholera and floods order to achieve overall child well-being in Chipinge Chimanimani and Rushinga districts.

1 - Activities

Objective 2: Strengthen the resilience of most vulnerable women and girls, men and boys, to mitigate their risk of exposure to protection risks  Child Rights advocacy OutreachesCommunity and schools outreaches will be conducted in the targeted districts to raise awareness on child rights through strategic engagements of local leaders, influencers and minority religious groups. GBV Child labor etc risks harmful traditional practices including child early and forced marriage.  Child Friendly SpacesEstablishment of safe spaces for boys and girls between the ages of 3 and 17 in heavily affected communities like Chimanimani and Chipinge. The community will take part in the selection of sites and contribute local building materials and labor for the SFS. They will be highly inclusive, non discriminatory and will be led by community volunteers and facilitators, who would have undergone child protection in emergency training and PSS. Kids will be sensitized on child rights and protection issues through inter-schools edu-tainment activities like drama competitions and sport matches.  Child protection in emergency training for community leadersTeachers, Case Care workers, Community facilitators, traditional leaders and women support groups will be trained on their role of caring and protection of children in schools and encouraged to recognize and report abuse and neglect cases. To strengthen community mechanisms and coordination on Child Protection in emergencies, there will be monthly feedback meetings for the trained people to discuss issues on Child Protection and share experiences.  Psycho-social Support Training in emergenciesMale and female teachers, Case Care workers, Community facilitators, traditional leaders and women support groups will be trained on emergency sensitive approaches and support to survivors of child labor, neglect, abuse and child focused gender based violence in their communities.  Suggestion Boxes Secured suggestion boxes to be placed in Child friendly spaces and schools so that suspicious cases can reported anonymously and dealt with by the appropriate authorities. They will be managed by teachers, case care workers, community facilitators, community volunteers and leaders who will have been trained on Child protection, PSS and proper handling and reporting of cases in emergencies.  Documentation and reporting of child protection violationsThe community facilitators and Volunteers will document the cases that would have been reported through different channels including the suggestion boxes and the responses from the community. They will record the process that a case goes through in order to understand the different emergency justice systems in the communities.
