Project Summary

Considering the rapidly evolving context and the diversity of shocks to which vulnerable populations are exposed, SI proposes to rapidly respond to disease outbreak, sudden-onset disasters, drought/floods or spikes in conflict/displacement. SI will provide emergency WASH activities (emergency chlorination at water points) and emergency raining season preparedness and response activities in hotspots areas of Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD)/Cholera and COVID-19 in the Borno, Adamawa and Yobe (BAY) states considering 2021 Cholera outbreak in Nigeria as one of the worst breaks that have occurred.
SI as the lead of the wash sector infectious diseases working group will also continue to coordinate with other partners and other relevant stakeholders ( health and government agencies) on the preparedness and response ( in case of an outbreak) activities of partners in the BAY states through meetings, workshops, trainings etc.

2 - Activities

-Hygiene Promotion : The population of the BAY states are vulnerable to infectious diseases outbreaks, in particular Cholera outbreak during the rainy season. In order to scale up the Covid, Cholera outbreak preparedness and response for epidemics entitle focusing on Hygiene Promotion activities aimed at increasing the knowledge of the affected population on safe hygiene practices, key messages on safe water chain, waterborne diseases (cause, symptoms and prevention), fecal-oral route and key handwashing moments, through recruitment and training of CHPs for messaging. -Water point chlorination : Capacity to chlorinate 40 water points during 2 months as preventive safe water provision during raining season in hotspots areas of water borne disease in the BAY states where WASH partners have limited capacity to cover the gaps. -HHs disinfection : Following a declaration of cholera outbreak in any of the BAY states, in coordination with the health sector partners managing a CTC/CTU, SI will deploy its rapid response team using CATI approach to undertake targeted household disinfection -Water trucking/Rehabilitation of water points : SI Rapid Response Team will ensure water trucking for three months and including installation and setting up of distribution points. This activity will be supervised by a staff in order to ensure the monitoring of the quality of the water distributed. This activity could be conducted in parallel with rehabilitation of water point work. Indeed, capacity for water trucking could be ensured to provide an alternative source of water to beneficiaries before the completion of the activity and to ensure continuity of service. However, this activity could also be deployed in function of needs in BAY States and run for a period of 3 months (25m3/day) to cover the needs of individuals. -Rehabilitation/Construction and desludgment of Sanitation facilities : SI will rehabilitate/ construct sanitation facilities depending on the need in hotspots areas to prevent waterborne disease outbreaks. The rehabilitation work will include repair of the superstructure to ensure privacy and safety of the users and improve access and inclusion whenever needed. The latrines will be handed over to the sanitation committee with latrines maintenance kits that will be used for regular cleaning of the units by the Beneficiaries.SI will have the capacity to deslugde filled up latrines sanitation facilities to be implemented following new influx of IDPs or any other relevant situations (natural disaster – flooding, disease outbreak). From SI’s past experience, the lack of desludging is a major concern in the BAY states and most of its areas of intervention. Indeed, the fact that several latrines (most of them built by humanitarian actors) are full, is forcing the communities to defecate in the open area. Beyond the protection issues linked to this practice, it represents a high hygiene risk. The trigger for this activity will be in case of epidemics outbreak, or important needs identified by SI or others actors, when they lack response capacity requiring intervention to increase the availability of usable and safe sanitation facilities. Sanitation kits will be distributed to already existing committees. -Support to CTC/CTU ( Through provision of IPC materials, provision of water, sanitation facilities -Infectious diseases coordination with partners : SI as the lead of the wash sector infectious disease technical working group will continue to coordinate with wash partners and other relevant stakeholders ( Health partners, Government agencies etc ) on the preparedness and response activities implemented in the BAY states and also on the capacity building of partners through refresher training, workshops etc..Cholera, Covid and Hygiene kits distribution : This kits will be distributed to suspected or confirmed cholera/covid patients and for the hygiene kits they will be distributed to vunlerable HHs that are affected by shocks.
