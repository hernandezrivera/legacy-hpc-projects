Project Summary

In this project, NWO will improve the access and utilization of Emergency integrated Nutrition services for women and children living in the most affected areas in Lower Shabelle region using both static and mobile sites. The static nutrition sites will be strengthened by adding WASH and health Services, provision of nutrition supplies and the improvement of staff capacity. Remote villages will be reached through mobile clinics; community mobilizers and CHWs will be recruited and trained. Among the activities that NWO will be providing include: Immunization to under 5 children and Women of child bearing age (WCBA); Treatment of uncomplicated malnourished cases i.e SAM; Promote the adoption of good health and hygiene practices to the affected communities; Breast feeding promotion and support for infant and young child feeding; Identify and assist in the train of community volunteers on nutrition promotion and education; MUAC screening and identification of malnourished children; Distribution of standard IEC materials for social mobilization; MUAC screening for all children 6-59 months and pregnant and lactating women (PLW); Treatment of SAM cases without medical complications; Referral of SAM cases with medical complications and failed appetite test. Under this project, NWO will scale up its intervention through fixed integrated Nutrition sites and mobile services targeting 68,717 drought affected people IDPs and Host communities.

Mitigation Measures

NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS is an independent, neutral organization ensuring humanitarian protection and assistance for victims of armed conflict and other situations of violence, natural disasters such as drought and famine. It takes action in response to emergencies and at the same time promotes respect for international humanitarian law and its implementation in national law. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS is guided by a code of conduct to ensure that displaced people are treated with the humanitarian principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence. As such the highest regard for the protection of human life and health will be provided to all people needing and seeking help with clear policies and procedures in place for NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS staff and partners to endure that not protection risk is incurred. In the event of any exposure to protection risk, NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will follow its laid down procedures to mitigate those risks to ensure protection is provided at all times to all in need. Displaced populations, where by natural or human-made disasters are at a high risk to be excluded from accessing the humanitarian support they need the most due to multifactorial issues some of which include segregation of minority groups, stigma, exclusion due to lack of knowledge or information sharing. Taking cognizance of these risks of exclusion that displaced populations face, NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will actively seek an all-inclusive policy in order to reach all those in need especially in the areas NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS is working. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will recruit some of its project staff from displaced populations especially for positions such as community workers and Community Resource Persons in order to ensure IDPs get inclusion in accessing and benefiting from all our projects and will also ask for their participation in project design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Populations displaced by conflict and violence face a lot of problems especially humanitarian need and lack of life-saving health services. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work to ensure the provision of access to basic life-saving health to all populations in need in our areas of interventions without discrimination. In this regard, NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will also work within the humanitarian context and coordinate with other actors to ensure the severe impacts of armed conflict and violence is reduced. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS works very closely with communities and local authorities, ensuring that local laws, customs and culture are adhered to, and avoiding action and advocacy that may be perceived as offensive. Rigorously apply ‘do no harm’ principles. Programme informed by regular conflict analysis and understanding of politics at different levels.

Please provide further explanation

NWO will work with the Government in order to build a resilient and responsive health system infrastructure. NWO will focus both on the everyday routine challenges that front line staff face on a regular basis as well as focus on sudden shocks such as disease outbreaks. NWO will work to build the leadership capacity of district and regional state health systems and in this regard will target Government key health managers, health facility staff, community members as well as patients accessing services so as to empower them to solve problems at their level and build relationships and social networks. NWO will focus on strengthening existing social networks to provide access to multiple resources available in the community and forms of value such as community knowledge and reputation that can build and restore resilience to health systems. NWO will use the model of everyday resilience building in the health system of Government through a combination of absorptive, adaptive and transformative strategies that health authorities, staff and community members can use to adopt the health system in responding to strains and shocks. This will help in producing a strengthened and more resourceful resilient health system infrastructure. NWO will train the Health Authorities, health facility staffs, patients and community members on how to deal with sudden shocks to the health system. All the different strata of the health system will be taught how to manage such disease outbreaks at different levels starting from the community level to the outpatient therapeutic feeding centres to the stabilization centres to the referral facilities so as not to overwhelm only one level of the health system but to build a resilient and responsive health infrastructure into every level. NWO will use the funding from donors as well as its own funds to ensure adequate resources and strong health system infrastructure is built to contribute to generating a resilient and responsive health system in Lower Shabelle region.
