Project Summary

The project is a 2024 intervention to mitigate humanitarian crises in Ayod and Uror Counties (Jongei State). The aim of the project is to ensure provision of needs-based emergency aid to prevent or decrease morbidities, mortality and acute malnutrition especially for vulnerable people in acute humanitarian crises triggered by fight and displacement, droughts, floods and anticipated periods of hunger. This project will contribute to the overall strategic objectives of the 2024 HRP and the 4 sectoral Nutrition cluster objectives to assist vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host community comprising of 5,293 households in Ayod and Uror Counties of Jonglei state by #1; increasing equitable access and utilization of quality nutrition services to prevent malnutrition and build resilience, #2; increasing equitable access and utilization of quality lifesaving nutrition services for early detection and treatment of wasting, #3; Enhancing nutrition situation and program monitoring, analysis, and utilization of nutrition information, and #4; Strengthening coordination & integrated programming for equitable access to services that address key drivers of malnutrition among vulnerable children and women. The 2024 IPC report states that climate change continues to impact different sectors, particularly agriculture, which is one of the most climate-sensitive economic sectors. Above normal rainfall for the fourth consecutive year in 2024 led to prolonged floooding, which impacted areas that had not ooded in previous years. Other key drivers for malnutrition include low agriculture production and localized conflicts that continues to disrupt livelihoods and forcing displacement of people in Ayod and Uror counties.
Due to vastness of Ayod and Uror Counties, CMD will maintain establish OTP/TSFP sites that includes those within MoH structures, standalone and outreach sites/teams including further decentralisation in Ayod,Uror counties (islands, canal and cattle camps). Reduction of morbidity and mortality rate for the IPC 4 populations in line with CMAM Protocol through detection and treatment of children under 5 reaching at least 4,532 children U5 with SAM, 8,085 children U5 with MAM and 7,796 MAM- PLW in Ayod and CH<5 with SAM 7,480,CH<5 with MAM 11,512 and MAM PLW 10,428. SAM Children with medical complication will be referred to existing stabilisation centres. At the community level, Community Nutrition Volunteers will conduct active case finding to strengthen early detection and timely referral of malnourished children and PLW, both through routine community screening and family MUAC Approach.
The programme will support nutrition specific, and nutrition sensitive sectors as follows:- Apply in-country (MoH) CMAM and MIYCN technical guidelines in the implementation of the program, Strengthening care practices and services for maternal, infant and young child nutrition (MIYCN) by improving management of peer groups (MTMSG, FTFSG); actualizing of Positive Deviance Study (PD) enquiry finding, scaling-up SBCC; promotion of Exclusive breast feed, diet diversity, of Acute Malnutrition in Infants (MAMI) through counselling; implementation of Baby Friendly Hospital Initiative (BFHI) in Ayod Hospital and Strengthen Family MUAC, growth monitoring and promotion, VASD for children under 5 years. Project will link with food security activities e.g Promotion Model, IGA, kitchen and increase community vegetable gardens to care givers and peer groups. Continuous improvement on ECD activities in the nutrition sites; promotion of nutrition,supplementations of older children, adolescents through collaboration with PTA, School clubs by ensuring BCC and supplementation for prevention of micronutrient deficiencies, training Youths and school heads on how to take MUAC.CMD will work to improve Nutrition information data quality check, and documentation of lessons that will generate knowledge.

1. Needs

Ayod,Uror counties of Jonglei state has experienced diverse effects of conflict and displacement, with majority populations multiply internally displaced. Chronic food insecurity and unavailability of basic services have further exacerbated the already dire situation. With a population of over 212,650 persons (NBS projections) .54% female, 14% <5s, the county faces interrelated challenges in delivering adequate and appropriate essential services with malnutrition remaining high, disease outbreak burden,climate related shocks, inter-communal violence,economy contribute to acute food insecurity and a deteriorating exacerbating the situation. The proximity of Ayod along the Nile / Phow rivers flood plains makes the susceptible to flooding, especially during the rainy seasons resulting in an increase in preventable diseases. The recent FSNMS R-29 report shows Ayod GAM rate of 23.7%, Acute malnutrition prevalence of (U5 Children) MAM 18.3 % and PLW prevalence of 41.9%. The current IPC report, places Ayod in critically situation (IPC phase 4). 62.5% of children reported to have been ill due to diarrhoea, fever, cough, pneumonia, malaria among other diseases. 30.2 % of HHs are reported to have poor food consumption and household dietary diversity score falls at 22.5% of HHs experiencing severe hunger. The key drivers for malnutrition and high GAM rate are multifaceted, including severe acute food security, sub-optimal feeding practices, high prevalence of disease, coupled with inability to get early treatment. Under 5, PLW and adolescent girls remain the worst affected by malnutrition. Barriers include overall poor access to health and nutrition services, high prevalence of diseases and poor food intake. Critical gaps in WASH contribute to high levels of acute malnutrition and overall high morbidity and mortality rates.The nutrition situation has further been worsened by public health challenges such as poor water and hygiene sanitation, inadequate to access clean water, direct and indirect effects to Vulnerable U5 children and PLW, high GBV emergency cases due to Insecurity caused by intercommunal revenge fights which have a severe impact on people’s livelihoods, hampers access to basic services. HMIS data indicates the lowest access to children Under 5 and PLW, patients to nutrition and health services in Ayod – less than 0.3; mainly due to the concentration on CU5 & PLW, the bulge in IDP figures, the vastness of the location, limited nutrition supplies and current WHO guidelines on management of wasting. Huge malnutrition cases reported in Ayod, mainly attributed to the agro–pastoral nature of populations that tend to migrate to the marshes in the quest for water, food and security in crowded the villages. There is huge burden of disease including malaria, diarrhea, pneumonia and malnutrition contributing to the high morbidity and mortality rates to Women and children.CMD proposes support of OTP/TSF through an integrated nutrition programme focusing on prevention, early detection, and treatment of acute malnutrition. Project seek to build on 2023-2024 successes;to deliver an integrated and expanded package of nutrition services that build on # Provision of timely and quality lifesaving management of acute malnutrition through a full CMAM Package of services reaching at least to SAM CH<5 4,532,MAM CH<5 11,550 and MAM PLW 11,137 in Ayod and SAM CH<5 7,480,MAM CH<5 11,512 and MAM PLW 10,428 # SBCC reaching 21,449 in Ayod and in Uror 20,877 MAM PLW, new mother, caregivers and community members to improve nutrition and health-related knowledge and practices that prevent malnutrition. This project will be linked to (BHI) and contribute to the country’s Health Policy 2016-2026 Basic Package of Health and Nutrition Service (BPHNS) 2011 which commits to a minimum service package. The project will build knowledge of CHD,FSL,Health,WASH to ensure nutrition sensitive integration and address barriers contribution to severe wasting.

2. Project activities

Project Start-up (Inception Phase); Enter MoU with State/National MOH. Start-up Meeting with local coordinating partners. PSEA/ Code of Conduct signing by all the project staff and 3rd parties. Project Implementation: Establish 12 OTP sites that includes standalone sites within the island and facilitate 4 outreach teams including further decentralisation in health facilities within Ayod and Uror Counties. Support nutrition specific, and nutrition sensitive sectors as follows: Nutrition-specific actions: Strengthened care practices and services for maternal, infant and young child nutrition by improving management of peer groups (MTMSG, FTFSG); actualizing of Positive Deviance Study enquiry finding, scaling-up SBCC; promotion of Exclusive breast feed, diet diversity, Management of Acute Malnutrition in Infants through counselling; implementation of child Friendly space Initiative and Strengthen Family MUAC, growth monitoring and promotion, VASD for children under 5 years. Screening,referral,defaulter tracing and Management of Acute Malnutrition through provision of services in 6 Static sites , 4 outreach OTP/TSFP within the county and oversee the implementation of nutrition component , establishment of 1 Stabilisation Centre; while also strengthening the nutrition supplies i.e. RUSF management. Strengthening of the nutrition integration with sensitive sectors: -Health and WASH - 80 CNV,HBI,HP,Care groups will be trained on integrated CMAM/MIYCN and facilitated with supplies to provide treatment of common ailments i.e Malaria, Acute Respiratory Tract Infection, and diarrhoea in line with the BHI approach; maintain screening and treatment of malaria to SAM & MAM cases, sensitization of women of reproductive age to enhance optimal behaviours practices for improved nutrition and referral of pregnant mothers for antenatal care. WASH integration and adoption of positive hygiene and sanitation behaviour change communication. Link project with food security activities- IGA, kitchen and increase community vegetable gardens to care givers and peer groups (MTMSG Members). Education -Continuous improvement on ECD activities in the nutrition sites; promotion of nutrition and supplementations of older children, adolescents through collaboration with local education ministry, PTA, School clubs, leadership by ensuring BCC and supplementation for prevention of micronutrient deficiencies. Improve on Nutrition information - improving on data quality check, inclusion of nutrition data to County DHIS 2, conduct surveys, documentation of lessons that will generate knowledge and new ideas for action.CMD will use (MoH) CMAM/MIYCN technical guidelines in the implementation of the program. SPHERE standards for Emergency response will also be used as reference for nutrition indicators. On the other hand, CMD will use IASC/AAP as reference for implementation of protection-GBV interventions including referrals. The project implementation approach will comply with CHD Principles to ensure accountability of Affected population. PSEA training within the first month of starting the partnership. Monitoring, supervision and support: Routine joint monitoring/supervision for activity tracking/field visits, Conduct evaluations and assessments, Establish accountability systems/ conduct feedback sessions, setting up complaints handling system, Training of staff on MEAL.
