Project Summary

The recent Monsoon rain caused flooding in Balochistan, mostly the access within province was badly affected roads and bridges were destroyed, vehicles were stuck in the rains. In the rains and flash floods district Kachhi and Jhal Magsi of Balochistan province were badly affected. The basic infrastructures of both districts badly affected included link roads, Schools, WASH and Health facilities etc. The Houses of poor and vulnerable population were also damaged badly, the houses were damaged partially and fully. Both district Kachhi and Jhal Magsi affected by floods 2020. In the Floods affected Union Councils about 20 % households are fully 80% households are partially affected. Due to passage of long time of flood some households have rehabilitated their houses on self-help basis. Due to the poverty and lack of resources remaining vulnerable or most poor families are living without proper shelter facility. Population of both districts consist on small poor farmers and tenants. The affected families’ income solely depends on agriculture and Livestock that mostly damaged due to floods 2020. They are facing the problems and hurdles due to the lack of proper shelter facilities in this harsh and hard weather. Women, children and old age persons are mostly facing trouble in this season without proper shelter facility. The both districts were also affected by other disasters like drought and current COVID-19 emergency situation.
According to the need of community BSDSB team has proposed the provision Shelter repairing Kits and NFIs Kits to 1600 most floods and disasters affected families of districts Kachhi and Jhal Magsi (800 in each district). BSDSB will provide Shelter Kits consists of per family including 2 Girders, 15 Bamboos, 1 Chick (hatch) for roof, 1 Iron Door, 2 Iron Windows, 2 Iron Ventilators, 1 Polythin sheet (Plastic sheet), Wheat Straw 80 Kgs and NFIs Kit per family consist of including 1 Floor Mat (Slandered size), 1 Claw Hamer, 1 Wooden Saw, 1 Shovel, 1 Pick Axe, 1 Spade and Binding Wire 2 Kgs.
In the proposed project Gender will be cross cutting theme of the project, preference will be given to the most floods and disasters affected vulnerable families, women and children house headed, widows, elderly house headed, minorities and other vulnerable communities of the target area.
