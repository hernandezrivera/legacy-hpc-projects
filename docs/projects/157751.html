Project Summary

1. Qualified technical support to Shelter Cluster and its Members in urban areas to harmonize and implement adequate, affordable and resilient shelter provision within urban settlements: 1.1 Technical advice, guidance and monitoring of Central Government (INGC, GACOR and MOPHRH-DNUH), Local Authorities, NGOs, shelter cluster members, communities in provisioning and building shelters including safer recovery of housing partially destroyed. 1.2 Deploying staff at central and local level to interact with the cluster structure, assist in coordination as well as in prepare training and monitoring activities on shelter and housing reconstruction; 1.3 Blueprints and technical guidelines of intervention for set-up emergency shelter, temporary shelter and permanent shelter under rapid safer construction approach using available materials in affected areas (conventional and no-conventional materials), shelter and tools kits, architectural and engineering models, and technical measures to improve resilient to natural hazards. 1.4 On the job training involving all stakeholders, affected households, rapid reconstruction core teams and technicians from NGOs working in shelter and camp establishment. e. Setting-up rapid rehabilitation core teams per districts and/communities composed for local master builders and support owner-driven reconstruction. 2. Rapid Participatory Spatial Planning based on neighborhood readjustment, macro/micro resettlement accommodation supported by georeferenced information and technical local and global standards: 2.1. Site analysis and identification, based on close interaction with local authorities and cluster partners, including participatory session with people involved in the peration; 2.2 Preparation and elaboration of “Planos de Cadastro”, following local regulations and standards. 3. Field damage assessment on housing and basic service infrastructure, as well as self-recovery qualitative evaluation for building back better in the affected urban areas of Sofala and Manica: 3.1. Identify typology of houses affected, main elements vulnerable or damage, cost for temporary and permanent recovery per typology, typology and needs of materials construction. 3.2. Survey or collection of technical information on the beneficiary schools’ and health infrastructures including WASH that complement shelter and camp establishment
