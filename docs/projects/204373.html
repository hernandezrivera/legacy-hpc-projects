Project Summary

IRC proposes to delivering food support to persons affected by the current crisis in Sudan states. IRC team will register all affected persons who are food insecure and whose livelihoods have been affected. The project will be essentially life saving interventions by distributing food in-kind or vouchers. The target will be drawn the five states where IRC has a strong operational presence and it will be easy to quickly respond to the emergency needs of affected people. IRC and its partners will work in these states to distribute the most need food to reduce negative coping strategies of affected women, girls and men. In recent responses IRC works with SUDO and CAFA who are strategic partners as they are well versed with the country and the locations of people affected by the conflicts. The partners also have experience of livelihoods and food assistance programs over time. IRC has already secured technical agreements of working in the states mentioned and in new states IRC is working with HAC to submit relevant papers for submission to legalize operations in Sudan. IRC has a strong team that also has capacity to mentor local partners in technical aspects of response. Affected persons in IPC3+ will be reached with aid. Those that are in agriculture will be reached by supporting them with tools, seeds and fertilizer so that they can plant their own crops and recover. The purpose will ideally be life saving according to strategic objectives SO1 and SO2. Women recipients will be mostly encouraged to register to receive aid as it is a common norm that women are more responsible with HH chores compared men counterparts who may end up selling the aid before reaching home. In most crisis IRC will target HHs who are acutely food insecure and in this response IRC team wants to reach 23 500, and will be flexible to accommodate new arrivals as the situation and context resolves. The target will be 15 500 women and 8 000 men. The current situation in Sudan will keep pushing populations to move to IRC operational states and IRC will use evidence on ground to strengthen its response plan and to implement.

1 - Needs

The project will address food security needs through provision of food or cash to affected persons. The country is faced by serious food shortages. Men, Boys ,Girls Women now need food or cash for survival since their livelihoods were affected by the crisis. Recent assessments show the food needs and CVA needs of the affected persons rural or urban. The markets have been disrupted and the income projects are at a stand still. Women who normally do petty trading in cities have been affected and they cant do any small livelihoods projects and as a result they need cash for day to day running. Most persons are in IPC3+ and they are food insecure. Some HH who depend on labor for income are becoming food insecure as a result of disruptions caused by the conflicts. There are so many food needs of rural and urban folks as the situation on ground is persistently pushing hundreds of thousands of person to I{PC3+ most markets are not functional and banks are closed this then renders more people to be most vulnerable.

2 - Activities/Outputs

IRC will consult with women on the food assistance and cash modalities, ensuring their safety while accessing assistance. This will be done by the cash team and protection team.Where possible, provide in-kind and multipurpose cash assistance (MPCA) for affected population and IDPs so that they can meet their food related and other basic needs, this needs to be particularly prioritized for relatively stable areas such as River Nile, White Nile, Ai-Jazirah, and Port Sudan. As people are moving in a very dynamic way, there is a need to provide them with flexible means to access what they need.Assess the situation of current prepositioned supplies and estimate the needs of populations, as well as restocking strategiesProvide food support that is enough to meet 3 meals to affected persons as persons with special needs have to survive and eat dietary diversified food. Where the situation is calm provide them with multi-purpose cash transfers to meet basic needs. IRC will in this Action engage in high level advocacy to open safe roots for humanitarian assistant as hundreds of thousands around the assessed areas are currently left without shelter, food, and drinking water due to inaccessibility for humanitarian assistant and mainly lack of food due to a combination of factors.IRC's cash and livelihoods team will embark on provision of seeds, tools for communities to be able to provide local food for themselves.The identified food gaps will be addressed by a combination of modalities in Kind or cash. IRC will be flexible depending on context to distribute food.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Under Food Security activities, a comprehensive community engagement will be conducted to understand the implementation barriers and seek improved ways to reach the most vulnerable HHs. During community engagement sessions, the IRC will ensure the participation of women and encourage women leaders to take part in the working committees to advocate the needs of women at grassroot level. A specific selection criterion will be included for disabled people and unaccompanied children to be included in the MPCA. At the end of each project, the Lesson Learned discussions will be conducted to capture the views of the local community and align program strategy with learningsinvolvement of women , girls , boys and men. The project will will ensure women are protected from harassments and segregation at all times. Youths, (boys and girls) will be benefitting from the project to be vibrant in society and to gain foods that are of a dietary diversity. Women will be the recipients of aid at all times and we will ensure that they participate in committees and other leadership and decision making roles. At design women, girls , boys and men will be involved in meetings and at inception meetings. will make sure we hear their voices and contributions to inputs and provide what they know best in regards to food, cash and lessons learnt. At implementation the whole gender groups will be protected from harm, abuse and any form of exploitation. IRC will work with the protection department to ensure that the safety of all groups is in place and that no-one is left out in any situation. The Do no Harm approach will be employed in the project where the safety of all persons will be key to all stakeholders. The safety of project beneficiaries will be assessed and incoporated in the mainstreaming and women and girls and boys and men will not be subjected to harm either by the armed forces or aid workers. At implementation monitoring of the security situation during and after to see/ check if all persons are safe from the exercise and women will be consulted at the most on preferences to aid modality. Project beneficiaries will be refrained from harm at all ways possible as they are at the center of the programming. People in need should be protected from harm by thieves, their assets need protection too. their livelihoods need protection and ultimately their dignity and lives need protection. Gender based violence issues will be addressed by taking affected persons for support, health and the FSL team will support with food/ cash. IRC's protection team with the help of livelihoods team will work in collaboration to see if the affected and survivors of GBV are well taken care of strategically.
