Project Summary

The project will support Infection Prevention and Control, case management and logistics pillars. The initiative will support IPC trainings which will be carried out nationwide. This will also ensure sexual and reproductive health (SRH) lCOVID19 infection prevention control to most vulnerable pregnant women Procurement and distribution of personal protective equipment ,disinfectants , waste management and Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal Care ( EmONC )commodities will also be carried out to enhance IPC ,delivery and post-delivery care . Printing IPC Covid 19 Guidelines and IEC materials will also be done to create awareness amongst the health workers and affected population. For case Management ,procurement of essential equipment and commodities required for treatment of severe COVID-19 cases will be done. Support will also be given to Case management training on COVID-19 in partnership with Ministry of Health and Child Care and other partners. Continuity of essential SRH services to avert morbidly and mortality from obstetric complications will be supported The project will also strengthens strengthen referral system by supporting ambulance services. Maternity Waiting Homes will be assisted to decongest and to improve the IPC measures including provision of hand hygiene commodities.
