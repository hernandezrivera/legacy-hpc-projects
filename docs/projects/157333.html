Project Summary

HOPE Sudan is planning to work in Increasing safe access to education and protection services for vulnerable, internally displaced and returnee boys and girls in Central Darfur State that will contributes to the three HRP education and CP strategic outcomes. This project allocated 25% of the resources to contribute to the first outcome which is Populations in areas affected by natural or man-made disaster receive timely assistance during and in the aftermath of a shock. HOPE Sudan is focusing on the second outcome allocating about 75% of the resource. The second outcome is displaced populations, refugees, returnees and host communities meet their basic needs and/or access basic services while increasing their self-reliance. In this project HOPE Sudan will conduct, in Education: (1) Construct of 4 classrooms, provide 6 Semi-permanent, (2) Provide gender sensitive WASH facilities, including hygiene promotion, latrines (in line with INEE and SPHERE minimum standards), (3) Provision of education in emergency materials including school textbooks, exercise books, etc.. for (1000) children (500 boys and 500 girls), (4) Provide with appropriate seating for (200) children, (5) Conduct one training workshop on EiE and child protection, (6) Conduct one training workshop on core subjects and teaching methods, (7) Conduct one training workshop on psychosocial support services, (8) Establish of 2 child clubs focusing on lifesaving psychosocial support, fine arts, drama & games, peace & protection, hygiene promotion and leadership activities. In Child Protection HOPE Sudan will conduct: (9) Provide timely and coordinate emergency psychosocial support to 2600 out of school children (1320 boys and 1280 girls), with specific needs and exposed to violence, exploitation and neglect, (10) Identify /register /assess/document/ and reunify the 500 out of school children, who are have already been registered, to the school and provide them with education and NFIs materials, (11) Provision of support for alternative care, case management, foster care of UASC with protection needs and provision of education and NFIs materials for UASC reunified to 150 children (80 boys and 70 girls), (12) Identify /register /assess/document/ and reunify the 130 UASC with their care givers and 20 with their families (80 boys and 70 girls), (13) Conduct training of 30 CBCPN members representing on psychosocial support, CRC/CP, GBV, civil registry, prevention of recruitment, and Sudan Child Act 2010, (10 female at least), (14) Conduct training for 30 CBCPN members on positive discipline, code of conduct and child safeguarding policy and Revise/strengthen/update referral system pathways, (15) Conduct 3 community campaigns awareness on issues of CRC/CP, GBV, and importance of education. HOPE Sudan will consider accountability to affected people (AAP) through community participation, strengthen the current community mechanism/ committee as well as do follow up for all benefit targeted separately to assess project, identify challenges and provide give recommendations to improve the project outcomes.

Needs

After the long time and ongoing conflict and prolonged humanitarian crisis, the emergency situation in Darfur raised intensively and the conflict between government and armed groups and then between tribes as well, has negatively affected children especially in Jebel Marra in addition to continuous displacement. The targeted sites in Rokoro include 49,992 IDPs (HNO 2018) with 41% school age children. The last displaced happened in Apr 2018 when 11,425 individuals fled to Rokoro from surrounding areas due inter-tribal conflict (OCHA April 2018). The school children are 7571 (3711 girls, 3860 boys) and dropout are 8.5 for girls and 7.3% for boys as well as the 57.05% from school age children out of schools (SMoE 2016),In Rokoro locality, the access and quality of education is very low, based on the following: - The number of pupils/children per classroom is more than 90 pupils, in addition to that it is not at adequate level even lack of learning environment.- Lack of WASH facilities including latrines, water points and hygiene promotion, the 66.6% of schools in need, even the 60% schools have access to WASH facilities, there are partial facilities of it.- There are more than 40% school age children are out of schools.- Lack of child seating.- Lack of textbooks, exercise books etc..Also, a huge gap for CP services for below reasons:- Ongoing conflicts has been and newly displaced resulted in severe negative impacts on people in CD; casualties, trauma among children, female and other groups.- Child, women and young female have been at risk and live within very destructive environment; do not enjoy their rights, possible incidents of GBV, poor health services, and many other issues affecting their life.- Lack of protection services especially services to address Sexual & Domestic Violence and Family tracing & reunification, and referral system.- Lack awareness of f Unaccompanied and Separated Children (UASC). - Poor capacities local mechanisms of CBCPN for effective protection issues and concerns resolution and absence of follow up for action taken.HOPE Sudan, through its partnership with SRCS, Majalas Alkhair and SMoE in Central Darfur, will support education and CP in Rokoro as well as will fully coordinate with DRC, Sekear and UNICEF. While no concrete data is available, increased fighting, inter-communal and inter-tribal conflicts have affected the safety and security of people exacerbating the risk of sexual violence especially when displaced.Due to above situation HOPE Sudan looking for improve the access to Education and CP services through improving of schools environment, increase children enrolment and reduce drop-out, develop capacity of schools teacher, promote support community PTA to school, as well as healing the children from trauma by providing Psychosocial Support Services, reunify out of school children to school, reunify of UASC with their care givers, strengthen of CBCPN, strengthen referral system pathways.
