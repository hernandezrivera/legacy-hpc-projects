Project Summary

ACF is proposing to deliver cash-based assistance, support for income-generating activities it includes cash for livelihood, seeds, agriculture inputs, forestry tree nurseries and transformation from of skills for food and livelihoods to households in Yobe & Borno State of Nigeria. The provision of a social safety net in the form of cash/food voucher transfers aim to support IDPs and conflict-affected households to meet their food basket needs or the cost of maintaining a nutritious diet. Complimentary sessions focused on good nutrition and hygiene practices, as well as training on income-generating activities, good agricultural practices aim to strengthen affected populations' food and nutrition security to support the establishment of their livelihoods and build their resilience to prevent the use of negative coping mechanisms.

1 - Needs

The protracted conflict in Northeast Nigeria continues to cause mass displacement and loss of human lives; affecting livelihood activities. The Cadre Harmonize analysis in June 2019 revealed that 3 million individuals are in a combined critical food insecurity situation of Crisis and Emergency (CH phase 3 and 4) for June-August 2019.Although many resumed farming activities, the food security situation worsened due to increased attacks between military forces, Non-state armed groups, and recent floods that affected people’s ability to plant and harvest from their farms. Heavy rains and flooding in August affected an estimated 924 hectares of farmlands across several LGAs, while many households lost food items and livestock and Borno State is the most affected (UNOCHA, Sep. 2019).In Borno, the epicenter of the crises, there are 1,483,566 IDP and 45.1% (674,406) of IDPs are in host communities while 54.5% (809,160) are in camps. In Yobe state, there are 131,597 IDPs; 90.2% of the IDPs are in host communities while 9.7% are in camps (DTM IOM Round 28). IDPs struggle to meet their daily needs; food remains the immediate need for 73% of IDPs. Those in camps are among the poorest with limited access to land and income-generating opportunities. Host communities also face vulnerabilities being in a situation of poverty prior to conflict and facing poor access to resources for their food production In Borno planting is limited to a 1-2km periphery area) and markets are disrupted and the conflict causes inflation (resulting from restricted cross-border trade, movement curfews and attacks by NASAG).Some 54% of IDPs as female and 18% as children under five years of age; they are further excluded. Women’s access to land is dependent on the male members of the household and they have limited formal education, engagement in the formal economy and value addition activities. They also face cultural systems of patriarchy that restrict their ability to influence key decision-making processes in the household (ACF, HEA analysis, 2018). In Yobe, livelihood and resilience-building interventions aim to stimulate the economic recovery of IDPs, host communities and returnees to build their livelihood who are under stress. Continued food assistance in remote locations who are experiences low food consumption. During the upcoming dry season farming, ACF is among the sector partners that will be targeting up to 37,800 households.

2 - Activities

ACF is proposing cash/food voucher assistance, income-generating activities (off-farm & on-farm) and social protection activities to support the most affected communities of Gujba, Nangere, Yunusari, Geidum of Yobe State and MMC, Jere, Monguno, Nganzai, Mobbar and Kaga LGAs of Borno State of Nigeria. The assistance would also be focused on predicted crises periods e.g. the lean season, during the period of conflict and disease outbreak. The objective of the intervention are directly related to the humanitarian situations in MMC, Jere, Monguno, Nganzai, Magumeri, Mobbar (Borno) and Gujba (Yobe) where some 36,391 households will be supported through monthly cash and food vouchers based on the food basket. Secondly, the interventions will focus on social protection and wellbeing of 11,552 PLWs of Mongunoin MMC/Jere beneficiaries would be receiving monthly NGN 18,000 50% cash and 50% food vouchers for 10 months. In Monguno, Magumeri, and Nganzai, beneficiaries will receive NGN22,000 through food vouchers for 10 months while in Gujba Beneficiaries would receive monthly cash vouchers of NGN18,000 for 10 months. In Kaga, Nangere, Yunusari LGAs to dignify their food and other priority needs. lastly, Project will also assist to some 9,000 HHs for on-farm and off-farm activities including agriculture inputs, income-generating activities, tree nurseries, and other forestry activities. In social Protection, beneficiaries would receive monthly NGN5,000 through cash/food vouchers in Monguno, Kaga, Nganzai and Nangare LGAs in Yobe and Borno. The targeting groups are pregnant and lactating women in targeted communities. Action Against Hunger will also coordinate with existing partners in targeted LGAs to avoid the duplications of efforts in term of assistance and harmonization of beneficiaries criterion. To ensure accountability to communities ACF will establish a Complaints Response Mechanism to ensure transparency and accountability. Feedback Mechanism established, monitored and issues resolved ACF actively engage and coordinate with other partners through the Food security working group. All the stakeholders, communities and partners would be involved from planning, implementing, monitoring and evaluation of the programs so that they make informed decision. ACF will also put efforts in the Engagement of local traders and market actors, Sensitize stakeholders relevant Ministries, State, and LGA authorities as well as beneficiaries on social protection. Women, girls and child-headed households will be given priority to the distribution sites. AAH will also ensure the access of people with disability and older people in the program and proper arrangement will be done in accordance to their needs so the segment of the group have access to food and cash.All the assistance will be provide based on the needs of targeted groups with no any discrimination. Women and child-headed households will be given priority. This intervention will be monitored and evaluated by the ACF team in close collaboration with the local authorities and public institutions and shall make use of conflict-sensitive monitoring and evaluation framework for this purpose. Data collection will be carried out using a wide range of data gathering tools such as Monthly Activity Progress Reports, field visit by technical coordinators and monitoring visit by relevant stakeholders, Baseline and end-line survey, PDM and external evaluation. Promotion of gender equality will be systematically integrated into technical approaches, including implementation mechanisms and participatory evaluation of the project especially involvement of women. ACF will adjust the project activities if needed based upon the gender analysis findings. The program also considers the protection risks that particularly affect women and children and will ensure activities do no harm and support specific needs, with the ultimate objective to empower women.
