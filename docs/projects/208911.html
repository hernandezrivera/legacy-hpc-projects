Project Summary

Sudan's ongoing food insecurity endangers people's lives, means of subsistence, and stability. A large portion of Sudan's population is at risk of food insecurity as a result of the protracted conflict, environmental degradation, and other disasters such as drought and floods, in 2023, there would be an estimated 18.7 million individuals in need of FSL assistance in Sudan.
This project is designed to improve the food security and livelihood status of 30,000 (17000 Female, 13,000 male) vulnerable agro/pastoral individuals directly, as part of the project activities, 19,000 individuals (3,000 males, 16,000 females) will receive agricultural assistance, 2,000 individuals (4,00 males, 6,00 females) will receive livestock assistance, (500 males, 1500 females) providing emergency Livelihood and social support through cash and in kind transfers processing ,fuel efficient stove-micro on in come generation will receive cash or vouchers to support their livelihoods 1,000 , as well as their families indirectly. The action is expected to reach up to 10,000 people including children, youth, adolescent girls, people with
disabilities, women-headed households, IDPs, and Returnees in in four states in Darfur East Central,, South and West Darfur . The project interventions will allow households to grow their own reliable food for family consumptions while earning some money from the sale of the surplus. This will be conducted through improving farmers and pastoralists awareness on good agricultural practices, provision of improved seeds and essential agricultural hand tools, distribution of milking goats to enhance the availability of milk to reduce Malnutrition among the most IDPs and Returnees whom are most in needs as well as the distribution goats will hep IDPs and Returnees to have meat for home consumption and conducting a livestock vaccination campaign to maintain the households' animal assets in the targeted communities and vouchers for goods or services will be provided directly to affected people to meet one or more of their basic humanitarian needs and save their life providing emergency veterinary (Vaccination ,deworming/ treatment for animals diseases surveillance for control of zoonotic and trans-boundary animals diseases targeting 150,000 in the four states East, Central , South and west Darfur

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

African Relief Committee (ARCO) will prioritize safety and dignity to ensure no harm is caused and will seek to identify, prevent and mitigate any unintended negative effects or risks during the intervention to girls, boys, children, young people, and project participants. ARCO will target the most vulnerable without discrimination and ensure that all participants are aware of and has access to necessary and relevant services. ARCO implements ‘Do no Harm’ measures to ensure the safety and dignity of children and young people that interact with its staff, associates or volunteers. It has zero tolerance against all forms of abuse and exploitation as articulated in its policy on safeguarding for children and young people. ARCO will continue to engage with the local authorities to ensure that activities are context appropriate and will orient all third parties working with and for ARCO and keep signed acknowledgements of the safeguarding policy thereafter. Activities will be underpinned by human rights principles and will strictly adhere to the core humanitarian standards and maintain the principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence. ARCO maintains high standards of safeguarding and preventing sexual harassment, exploitation, and abuse PSHEA, especially in emergency situations. ARCO’s Safeguarding Policy and procedures are implemented during the design and implementation of project interventions with vigilance. Safeguarding and PSHEA risk analyses and assessments of project interventions will be conducted with mitigation measures/contingency plans identified for each risk. ARCO will work to ensure staff, partners, project participants and communities are aware of the safeguarding and PSEA policies, its minimum codes of behavior, and requirements to uphold high standards of professional behavior. Project participants, especially children and young people will be respected, and their safety and protection will not be compromised when obtaining and using information on and images of them. ARCO will identify groups at risk of unsafe livelihoods practices and priorities provision of alternatives. ARCO will identify the power dynamics (who can access assets and inputs and who can influence decisions) and will monitor and respond to potential exploitative practices or abuses. Information will be communicated through various means to reach the wider community and account for different literacy levels. All layers of the affected population, especially those with intersecting vulnerabilities, will be consulted in identifying and responding to FSL needs to collect accurate information about specific requirements and preferences. ARCO will encourage communities to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes to promote a sense of ownership, build self-esteem and improve the sustainability of the response. Collective livelihood projects will be promoted contribute to community protection and resilience.
