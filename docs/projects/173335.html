Project Summary

Pakistan has transitioned from the first and second stages of COVID-19 in which case types change from those who have travelled from abroad to local transmissions. However, the province of Sindh in particular, appears to be at stage three of the cycle - the community transmission stage, where those who have not been exposed to infected persons or those with a travel history to affected countries are testing positive. At present, especially while the Federal Government imposed lock down persists and as Pakistan continues to tether over more disaster with the fast spread of COVID-19, the access of survivors and victims of Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV) to support and services like policing, shelter, legal aid, counseling remains adversely impacted.
Pakistan ranks as the sixth most risky country in the world for women, with rampant cases of sexual and domestic violence. The situation calls for a gender-responsive emergency measures to mitigate the harmful impacts of COVID-19 on protection of women. Evidence shows that national emergencies and global crisis situations exacerbate cases of violence against women, particularly domestic violence and sexual abuse. Hence, it is imperative for the state to develop and implement time-sensitive mitigation measures to prevent and control VAW, particularly against vulnerable groups of women and girls.
CDF suggests strengthening a coordination, early warning, and response for women, children, religious minorities, transgender and persons with disability by strengthening protection service established in district Jacobabad Sindh. These protection facilities established by the government departments and are Safe home, Women center, women complain center at police headquarter, child protection unit, human rights cell. Due to limited resources, lack of capacities and present situation of pandemic COVID protection services for survivors become limited to reach the survivors and response timely. On the other side general community and survivors do not know about the protection services or have limited access to reach the services providers for protection purpose. Due to reason above GBV survivors, children victim of violence and abuse and person with disability are deprived of right to legal, Psycho social , medical and police support timely and efficiently
The project aims to establish a strong coordination and referral mechanism of government, civil society and protection service providers in district Jacobabad by strengthening and building capacity of service providers, relevant departments and civil society. Aware general public and survivors about the essential services of protection and support mechanism available in the district. Establish and activate the referral mechanism by capacity building of civil society organization including NGOs, CBOs, Media, human rights defenders.
The project target is to increase access of women, girls, children, transgender, persons with disability, old age to the protection services, through the support of government line departments, civil society organizations including NGO/CBOs, Bar Association and Media.
The specific objectives of the project are:
strengthen government protection services for effective and timely response on violence and abuse cases
Establish an effective coordination and referral mechanism for survivors of violence and abuse.
Aware general community as well as survivors of violence and abuse about the protection services and rights .
The proposed project aspires to achieve following results in the project duration:
Protection service providers are responding survivors of violence and abuse timely and efficiently.
Survivors of violence and abuse are able to get services of legal aid, psycho social , health and police support in the situation of violence
An effective and efficient coordination and referral mechanism established in district Jacobabad for survivors of violence and abuse.

1- Project Activities

1. Establishment of coordination and referral mechanism: A coordination mechanism of protection service providers including Women center of women development department, Safe home established by social welfare and women development department, women police complain cell, Human rights cell, child protection center, child protection units in health and education departments, civil society organization of the district, Media and BAR association of district Jacobabad. Fortnightly meetings of the forum will be conducted for review and planning on the situation of violence and response mechanism 2. Preparing and publishing Referral directory: A referral directory will be published consisting on all details of protection services, protection departments, civil society and other support for survivors. It would widely circulate through media and social media.3. Legal aid to GBV Survivors: Legal aid would be provided through the free legal aid penal of district BAR association, District legal empowerment committee and available legal officer of women center4. Psycho Social support to survivors: A psycho social expert will be available to provide service at women center for online support and as needed at any Protection service centers.5. Awareness of Protection services through FM radio and IEC material: Awareness campaign will be run thought the project duration through FM radio, Social Media and IEC material in the communities for GBV service providers are available even in the time of pandemic and through online access 6. Technological Support to Protection service providers: Technological support like mobile phone and internet facility for GBV support centers including women center, safe home, and woman complain cell, human rights cell and CPU for online access and early warning of protection cases.7. Training of GBV service providers: Training would be conducted for GBV service providers on standers of care during the COVID for service providers.8. Data management support: Technological support like software will be provided to protection service providers for effective data management 9. Public Awareness: A public awareness campaign will be run to increase the knowledge of general public through legal literacy sessions, theater performance, interactive sessions, roundtables and conferences.10. Training of Human Rights defenders: A series of training will be conducted for Human rights defenders on Human Rights, GBV laws, early warning on human rights violation, Case reporting and case study making for the strengthening early warning and referral of protection cases
