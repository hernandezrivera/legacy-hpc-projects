Project Summary

The project seeks to improve the livelihoods of IDPs and their host population in the North West and South West regions of Cameroon, who have been affected by armed conflict in these 2 regions.
There are 2 components to this project.
- support in poultry production ( broilers and layers)
- support in cereal and leguminous crop production during the main cropping season
- The project targets 1000 beneficiaries in Livestock production and 3 000 beneficiaries in cereal and leguminous crop production. For leguminous crops, beneficiaries will choose between soybean and bean production.
For the livestock component, FAO will assist beneficiaries in setting up livestock units in either broilers or layers
For the crop production component, FAO will support beneficiary households through the distribution of seeds and fertilizers and follow up the beneficiaries through out the production process.
