Project Summary

FRAD Foundation seeks to provide urgently needed GBV humanitarian services to communities of return, IDP camps and host communities in Borno state where the Boko Haram insurgency has uprooted millions from their homes and lead to more increase in GBV cases and vulnerabilities over the last decades. millions of people have been displaced with women and girls suffering the most of the impact of the ongoing crisis. FRAD Foundation will be strategically scaling-up integrated GBV services in areas of return and IDP camps to address the needs of women and girls in the state. the project will be leveraging on digital skills technologies to address the needs of this population of people, we believe when the woman and the girl child have access to the right tools and resources, their protective factors will increase thereby have the ability to build resilience and take control of their life.
FRAD Foundation proposed project targets areas of greatest need in hard to reach local government areas (LGAs) in Borno targeting 3 LGA seeing the increase in the return of IDP back to their home town after decades of staying away from home, their may be increase in vulnerability which call for response that will ensure women and girls return safely with dignity and receive support to continue their life with decent means of livelihood.
Through this approach FRAD will increase its lifesaving activity and as well mainstream other sectors like wash and ERL to meet to the complex needs of the most vulnerable people affected by the debilitating conflict. Throughout this intervention FRAD will ensure protection mainstreaming, inclusive program design for persons with specific needs, and attention to gender sensitivity. Moreover, FRAD will leverage its renown digital skills in addressing the needs of women and girls in these response.

1 - Needs

GBV concerns have been on the increase over the years, violations of the right of women and girls receive little or no attention for redress by key stakeholders. the 2021 HRP shows that 1.5 million individuals are in need of GBV service response with 57% girls and 24%. Violations and sexual abuse against women and children are widespread both in and out of camp settings and communities affected by crisis in the state. it was identified that more than a million people are in need of GBV prevention and response services across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe. Women and girls are exposed to various risk on daily basis and are often targeted while they are performing basic tasks such as travelling to water points, collecting firewood to cook food, accessing farmlands, going to market, attending schools and sometimes places of worship. all these various insecurity affecting women and girls have long time threats to their health which often lead to sexually transmitted infections (STIs), including HIV, unwanted pregnancies, and obstetric fistula caused by sexual violence, lost of self esteem and dignity. Women and girls who experience GBV are also prone to mental health issues resulting from stigmatization, discrimination, isolation, exclusion and other social impacts. FRAD Foundation see all these issues as life threatening to women and girls thereby design this project to to ensure that women and girls have access to appropriate and safe GBV services, and to strengthen the capacity of community structures and social groups to support GBV survivors and those at risk of GBV.

2 - Activities

Support for Self-employment or Wage-employment for Adolescent Following the completion of SAFE Curriculum by adolescents girls, FRAD will support the adolescent through small grants to acquire the resources needed to set up their businesses according to their business plans. Self-employment may include support to set up their own businesses either on their own or collectively in groups of up 3 specialized case management of GBV survivorsFRAD will work along side coordination with the government to provide specialize GBV case management services. FRAD will target both adolescents and women for these service. response will be In-line with global guidance in the Interagency GBV Case Management guidelines using survivor centered approach. Dignity Kits Distribution FRAD will provide provide dignity kits to 1,500 beneficiaries. FRAD will focus majorly on camps and community of return where beneficiaries are returning without any possessions and are in desperate need of basic items for their safety and wellbeing. Psychosocial support for women and girlsIn collaboration with community structures, FRAD will provide basic psychosocial services for all women and girls accessing safe space. FRAD will work with trained personnel to implement psychosocial support (PSS) activities with wider range of women and girls. Digital Skills TrainingFRAD Foundation will train women and girls on digitals skills to reduce the risk of Gender based violence, the activity will bridge the digital divide for women and girls in terms of access to information and technology.Community GBV PreventionFRAD will implement community GBV prevention activities using participatory approach to allow communities come up with initiatives to end GBV in their communities. this approach will include setting up men GBV action group that will be ambassadors to end GBV in their communities. Life Skills TrainingLow source of income or poverty are mostly other contributors to gender base violence, the FRAD Foundation will train 900 beneficiaries on various skills of their choice to increase individual and household income.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The Project will ensure protection mainstreaming at all phases of the project Ranging from inception down to close out of the project. FRAD would include Protection mainstreaming in the training of its staff during onboarding training, this training will ensure staff are aware of the protection terms and prevent violation of the right of beneficiaries as a result of our action.FRAD will give more consideration to disability inclusion during the implementation and targeting of beneficiaries. All facility sites and activity sites will be constructed in such a way that everyone can have access without any limitation especially for people with special needs. Although the project Target mostly women and girls, attention will be paid to ensure representation of all gender in community programming and staffing of the Project, doing so will ensure their is Gender balance. Protection Monitoring will be conducted by FRAD M&E unit on quarterly basis to ensure that all facility safe space and sites are mainstreaming protection and address all concerns reported in field monitoring Report by the M&E unit.FRAD Foundation will from time to time collect feedback from communities on their view to know if they field protection have been mainstream, if the result turns to be negative, FRAD will work towards addressing the Gab immediately.
