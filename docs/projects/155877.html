Project Summary

Bousla will engage to train lead instructors, Instructors, lead teachers, teachers through provision professional development to teaching staff (Active learning, self-learning, life-skills and national/INEE minimum standards). in addition, Bousla will provide a training to serve as coaches for peer instructors in the education assemblies in two provinces across Syria. Serving as coaches for the peer instructors and teachers will ensure longer term coaching and supervision that will result in better outcomes than standalone interventions. Following the training, Bousla will manage periodic follow up and coaching meetings to ensure that instructors and teachers provide quality coaching over the course of the fall 2019 semester.
Grant objectives:
• Provide professional development to teaching staff (Active learning, self-learning, life-skills and national/INEE minimum standards)
• Lead Instructors and lead teachers are trained on coaching and supervision;
• Lead Instructors and lead teachers are trained on lesson appraisal;
• Instructors and teachers are trained on lesson appraisal by Lead Instructors and lead teachers.
• Number of teachers and lead teachers trained in literacy and numeracy.
• Number of coaching plans developed by lead teachers and instructors.
• School staff have the necessary knowledge and skills to provide numeracy and literacy instructions.
• Number of school staff trained, and coached by trained safeguarding officers in non-violent classroom management, psychosocial first aid, and staff care.

Protection Risk Analysis

The number one risk is security, as education facilities have been one of the main targets in the conflict. Although the situation has slightly improved due to the cessation of hostilities, it is unknown how long it will last. The security situation is not one that BOUSLA can play a major role in mitigating, besides perhaps providing additional safety and security training for teachers to use with their students in the case that security concerns arise. Other mitigation will be the pausing of activities in the case of major conflict escalation including rehabilitation and training (as was previously done in past Bousla programs). Also, we will equip the Safeguarding officers to implement safeguarding policies, Conducting safeguarding training will lead to positive change in staff behavior. Training and supporting safeguarding officers to conduct child protection community-based service mapping, through school committees, will result in improving access for marginalized children.School staff specifically teachers are willing to sign and enforce code of conduct, which will result in maintaining child protection policies.Finally, BOUSLA has chosen a location that is relatively safer and has historically been a location where IDPs flee to, not where people are fleeing from. As the context in Syria is one of war, there are often priorities that families list, and most often they focus on food and health. The secondary is usually education and PSS support. In order to mitigate this risk, the BOUSLA team will have to conduct strong advocacy and outreach during the project set up phase of the project when meeting with stakeholders. Furthermore, the rules and mandate of the committee will be formed by the community itself, so as to encourage ownership and interest in the topic at hand.
