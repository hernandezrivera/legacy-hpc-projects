Project Summary

This project will provide protection assistance in regions of Dana including widows camps, through GBV with integratation ERL and CP services( GBV 50%, CP 30% and ERL 20%) in women and girl safe spaces (WGSSs) and CFSs targeting (total beneficiaries are (33600), Women: 13200, Girls: 9200, Boy: 8000, Men: 3200). There huge need for protection services, especially GBV and CP in the targeted areas and till now a big gap in the coverage of the WGSS and CFS due to many reasons related to funds and lack of capacities and most of the established centres are suffering from the cutting in the fund. Therefore, MF will reactivate an already established centre (belongs to MF) in Atma and expand in Aqrabat to ensure continuing of GBV service and reach more vulnerable women and girls and those with special needs in target areas, and continuing providing GBV services in widows camps (MF already working and has access in targeted areas) and expand to provide CP services through mobile teams and CFSs.
• Expected outcome: Reduce harmful practices against women and girls as a result of continuing instability through providing lifesaving, specialized, quality interventions for GBV prevention, mitigation, and response. Strengthened, Enhanced quality of CP services and mitigate recruitment and family separation. proposed activities are:
•Provide specialized GBV services to survivors, including case management and individual PSS.
•Provide GBV empowerment and prevention activities related to the needs of women and girls, computer, English course, and cooking, each training lasts 3 months. 4 vocational training for each centre. The choice of vocational topics is decided on a consultative and participatory basis involving the women and girls in deciding and choosing topics. As well as recreational activities, including coffee meetings, weekly group support meetings, art groups, fun beautifying activities
Provide GBV service for adolescent girls for life skills using the guides dedicated to this category
•Provision of GBV and reproductive health services linked to family planning by midwives. Raising awareness of family planning issues in addition to providing individual consultations for women in a safe environment and referral to medical services in case of need for specialized medical services.
•Boys, men, people with special needs, and persons unable to reach will be targeted through mobile teams by providing awareness-raising awareness raising toolkit, PSS activities
•The community will be targeted through capacity-building activities, including humanitarian workers within different sectors to ensure integration of protection considerations and better overall quality and integration of services provided within safe areas
•Establish friendly spaces for boys and girls taking into consideration gender needs and capacities
•Conducting training for MF field staff who are working in this project on (PFA), CP, referrals pathways and case management
•Securing safe spaces for interviews, intake and case management for extremely vulnerable boys and girls in targeted camps
•Enhanced awareness and Organize recreational activities in friendly spaces, ensuring that they are age-appropriate and accessible for beneficiaries
•parents will learn how to deal with children during crisis through a parenting program
•Enhance women and girls’ economic conditions through integration with livelihood program (vocational training and microfinance services) according to the selection criteria (BNFs selection criteria will be created according to ERL Cluster guidelines in coordination with protection program). Then the selected BNFs will be provided with technical support via coaching and followed up to ensure the effectiveness and sustainability of the program, taking into consideration all protection guidelines in coordination with the ERL program
•All project activities will be adapted to match gender guidelines including (PWD, Men, women, girls, boy and elders)

4 - Needs

According to MF needs assessment (Violence_against_Women and Girls Mar 2021) for a total of 650 families were surveyed for this study (350 families in Idlib) using the FGD methodology and/or the questionnaire. Within these 650 families, (490 women/girls in Idlib), Ages: 90% ages of 18 and 64 years, 4% 65 years or older 6% girls (i.e under the age of 18 years). In the Al Dana Region specifically, there are multiple informal camps and settlements dedicated only to widows and female-headed households. These camps are largely off the radar of humanitarian interventions and are in dire need of support. Women in other camps and settlements also have ongoing needs for GBV prevention, mitigation, and response MF Assessments have identified the following specific needs for women and girls in the Al Dana region: Displaced girls and women in camps need more privacy to maintain their security, dignity, health and personal cleanliness. According to the need assessment and 26 FGDs in 13 widows camps(for 60 women ages between 30 and 50 and 60 children ages between 10 and 13) conduct by MF in Mar 2021 (see Annex 1), the most important CP issues that need attention are child marriage 23%, child labour 18%, neglect 15%, domestic violence 13%, financial exploitation 12%, family separation 8% and Children Associated with Armed Forces and Armed Groups (CAAFAG) 8% and Unaccompanied children 3%. These issues are a result of poverty, lack of awareness about CP risks and general information about CP, male children being forced to leave camps at the age of 14, and lack of schools and compulsory education

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented through WGSS, CFSs and mobile teams at Al-Dana region, focusing on the provision of CP, GBV, and integration with reproductive health services linked to family planning by midwives. The program YMC (Young Mother Club) targets pregnant young mothers (adolescents girls) to mitigate risks related to early marriage, provide information and services related to sexual and reproductive health in a smooth way. Raising awareness of family planning issues and individual consultations for girls and women in a safe environment. the team of YMC will be a legal midwife, psychologist, case manager, and GBV facilitators. Women, girls, boys, and men will be targeted within awareness-raising through a range of activities tailored to the age group and context to support the delivery of specialized services and facilitate access to survivors. the project will help increase the capacity and skills of women and girls. people with special needs and persons unable to reach through mobile teams will be targeted to provide awareness-raising, PSS support, and referrals and through providing transportation from and to centres and mobile teams. The proposed project intends to incorporate protection principles by supporting vulnerable people including women girls, boys, and men through CP, raising awareness, PSS, and GBV sessions. MF will prioritize the training of GBV and CP prevention, principles, and resources through the engagement of mobile teams. In addition to focusing on targeting males and young males from the main objectives of the project through awareness-raising sessions. MF will ensure service quality using the M&E plan, GBV SC M&E Toolkit, CPSC guidelines and inter-agency guidelines and regular follow up

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Preventing women, girls and boys from reaching centres to receive services within the centre Possibility: Medium Impact: high Mitigation Strategy: Mitigation Measures: Ensuring the provision of services for males and family members of the families and couples during the implementation of the project activities and in particular, awareness activities in order to mitigate and reduce the risk of preventing the access of beneﬁciaries to services Risk: Lack of acceptance of society and the forces controlling the content of the activities provided they contain highly sensitive topics Possibility: High Impact: High Mitigation Strategy: The beneﬁciaries and local staff will be consulted about the possibility of implementing the sessions of the program and work on structuring the sessions in a contextual and speciﬁc manner when there is a need to address the topics indirectly and depends on the resources within the group and supports activities. Risk: Category of beneﬁciaries Risk: Disability of persons with disabilities, older persons and areas away from centers. Possibility: high. Impact: high. Mitigation: Mobile teams will be allocated to reach beneﬁciaries who cannot access the centers to participate in activities for people with severe disabilities and elderly people with mobility diﬃculties in remote areas. Beneﬁciaries will be facilitated by providing transportation as needed to ensure their effective participation. Risk: further harm to beneﬁciaries during service delivery Possibility: Low Impact: High Mitigation Strategy: staff will be trained to provide specialized services to survivors on concepts and procedures to provide the service in a manner that ensures the safety of the BNFs

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The project areas have been relatively safe and far from clashes, therefore they are a destination for many IDPs. MF safety and security field team are already and will continue to be monitoring the security situation project areas on a daily basis. In addition, MF teams in Syria are aware of safety controls during emergencies and familiar with project areas, as we implemented former projects in the Dana sub-district and our community acceptance is very good in the Dana sub-district.MF will take all precautionary measures to avoid exposing our teams and project beneficiaries to danger. Key roles involved in this include the Safety and Security Manager (SSM) who is responsible for approval of the security plan, leads and participates in the risk management process, and takes ownership of risk mitigation/contingency planning and execution. The SSM is ultimately responsible for the final decision on risk actions, in coordination with the project team. Project team members participate in the risk identification process and discuss risk monitoring and mitigation activities at team meetings. Project stakeholders assist in monitoring risk action effectiveness and participate in risk management, as necessary. Regarding the security within the targeted camps, MF staff has to send their movement plan in advance, which will be approved by the safety and security departmentIn case of any event that triggers the contingency plan, particularly the evacuation and/ or relocation of the project, MF identified Plan B locations for this project. Firstly, project activities will be shifted to camps in close distance, but if the security is not guaranteed there, MF will change the location to Azaz, where MF has projected as well.
