Project Name

Providing high impact critical nutrition interventions for children under five years pregnant and lactating women with a focus on IMAM, IYCF-E and MNPs in El Nino affected areas of Zimbabwe

Start Date

01/01/2019

End Date

30/06/2019

Project Summary

• To protect the nutrition status of vulnerable children under the age of five years; pregnant and lactating women from deterioration due to the impact of the drought.
• To provide life-saving nutrition treatment to all children affected by acute malnutrition.
• To provide community level Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies (IYCF-e) support to parents and caregivers of children below the age of two years.
• To improve decision making and effective nutrition response through strengthened coordination and information systems.

Activities

1.1.1 Training of health facilities staff on Outpatient Therapeutic Management of Acute Malnutrition / OTP, inpatient facility based Management of Severe Acute Malnutrition /IPF 1.1.2 Support bi-monthly mentorship visits to health facilities by district staff1.1.3 Build capacity of community health volunteers on key caring practice and counselling through integrated community IYCF-e training1.1.4 Establish/strengthen mothers support group for Maternal, Infant and Young Child Feeding and Capacity building / training of Village health workers (VHW) / lead mothers and fathers on active screening at community level1.1.5 Capacity building for EHTs and Nurses for supervision of village level active screening and community-based IYCF (cIYCF) activities1.1.6 Conduct monthly social mobilization and active screening of U5 children, pregnant and lactating women (PLW) for malnutrition at community level and in hard to reach areas1.1.7 Distribution of RUTF, F75, F100, MNPs and anthropometric materials and distribution (including other essential supplies and IEC materials) too difficult to reach areas1.1.8 Support monthly review and reporting meetings at health facility level for VHWs1.1.9 Support bi-monthly review and reporting meetings at district level1.1.10 Provision of psychosocial support to women and children through community-based interventions in the locations where drought food assistance (cash based/direct food), health and nutrition support is being provided1.1.11 Support ward, district and provincial level stakeholder meetings through capacity building and mentorship of office bearers.
