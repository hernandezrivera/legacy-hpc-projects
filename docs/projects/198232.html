Project Summary

Within the course of the Anglophone crisis since 2016, the WASH conditions for IDPs and vulnerable population within rural communities in Kupe-Muanenguba is a call for concern. With the increasing threads posed by the global health crisis ( COVID-19), and potential cholera outbreak, it is very important for much attention be given towards improving the WASH situation of IDPs and host communities to ensure resilience to shocks. Strategically, this project intends to reduce vulnerability and build resilience of IDPs and crisis affected communities in Kupe-Muanenguba. Also, considering the WASH cluster framework for 2022, a total of 1225 (males) and (1275) females people are in need. This the target for this project and IDPs will be priortized and secondly highly affected host communities.
The project fully promotes and supports the WASH Cluster specific objectives in the following ways;
 Improves inclusive access to basic sanitation kits and facilities, safe drinking water to vulnerable people in Kupe-Muanenguba
 Reduces the risk of poor hygiene-related diseases on IDPs and affected population in Kupe-Muanenguba
 Reinforces capacities and increase mobilization, engagement and participation of local actors and communities themselves to improve WASH services and resilience of affected populations.
This project is intending to improve the WASH condition of IDPs, crisis affected communities in Kupe-Muanenguba. This will be achieved through; promotion of good hygiene and sanitation practices (intensive sensitization and awareness raising - including training of hygiene and sanitation promotion agents as focal point for each target community, reinforcement of water user committee, construction of gender-sensitive latrines, and also distribution of WASH kits especially soap and detergents to IDPs and vulnerable people.
Made up of hard-to-reach communities/ areas, humanitarian response is still needed in the WASH domain especially targeting women and girls, elderly population and people living with disabilities.
The lack of good tiolets, pushes most people in these areas to practice open defication. Worse about this is the fact that the few latrines which are manageable are not gender sensitive and nclusive. This is quite risky especially to preganant women (IDPs), children and because such a conditions can lead to cholera outbreak. In this case, IDPs sites with situations like this will be assisted with latrines to improve their hygiene condition.
Also, sensitisation will be key in target communities and IDP sits on appropriate use of latrines respecting the best hygiene and sanitation conditions which will prevent outbreak and spread of diseases.
Looking at the economic hardship and persistent increases in prices of basic items most women IDPs cannot afford hygiene kits ; soap, sanitary pads. Therefore, menstrual hygiene management for women and girls of child bearing age is being compromised and needs urgent action. This will involve awarness raisinh, workshops, seminars, talks and focus group discussions targeting these women and girls (IDPs).
This project is also directly inline with UN SDG No-6 :- clean and accessible water for communities especially crisis affected. Therefore, this project will be improving quality of water available by providing water purification tablets (aquatabs) to IDPs and to IDP sites. Also, water points management committees in IDP locations, will be reinforced to better management community water under good hygiene and sanitation conditions.
WASK kits will have as content : buckets, soap, jericans, aquatabs and most importantly sanitary pads. This will help many IDPs to access basic WASH kits considering the high inflation for items in doemstic markets in Cameroon. Cameroon going by the Human Development Index (UNDP, 2019) is ranked 150 out of 189, with over 39% of total population lives below poverty line. Providing WASH kits to IDPs helps them maintain dignity especially for women.
