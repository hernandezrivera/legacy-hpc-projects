1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Goal: Conflict-affected households in Cabo Delgado province, Mozambique live with dignity and rebuild their lives with access to basic needs. The following strategic objectives (SOs) support this overall goal:• SO1: By the end of the project conflict-affected families will have received assets, tools and support to improve their shelter.• SO2: By the end of the project conflict-affected families will have access to safe WASH infrastructure and practice appropriate hygiene.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Shelter Assistance Package 1: The Shelter Assistance Package 1 targets IDP HHs to be resettled in Government-appointed sites and are in need of a full shelter. The recommended shelter model is in line with the Shelter Assistance Package 1 design submitted with the original proposal, with only minor adjustments. The assistance comprises in-kind materials valued at US$490. The BoQ and design are attached, for reference. CRS will target 820 participants with the full package (1a), and CRS and IOM will contribute to the full package for an additional 500 households (1b). Under package 1b, IOM will provide CGI sheets and roofing nails (valued at $200) and CRS will provide in-kind materials for the remaining items, valued at approximately US$290, to comprise the full package. Is the full shelter assistance package still split between 1a and 1b as described here? From the budget, it looks like it is all grouped under one. Please confirm.• Shelter Assistance Package 2: The Shelter Assistance Package 2 targets IDP HHs resettled in Government-appointed sites, who are already building their temporary shelters but unable to complete it due to lack of materials. CRS will provide targeted households with in-kind assistance totaling US$130, allowing for participants to complete the roof structure and strengthen the walls, including tools to perform such activities based on HH preferences. This is complemented by in-kind distribution of 1-2 tarps from CRS’ stock donated by USAID. The attached BoQ provides more details for an indicative list of materials under this assistance package for improving shelters that have already been constructed. • Toolkits: As originally proposed, CRS will provide toolkits to groups of five households that are also receiving support under Shelter Assistance Package 1, to support IDP households in building the temporary shelters. The number of toolkits to be provided has increased to 264 (from 76 originally planned), since the number of participants receiving Shelter Assistance Package 1, to build temporary shelter, has increased. Under the WASH sector, CRS plans to work with families to construct latrines and will provide hygiene items and also hygiene promotion materials.
