Project Summary

This project delivers a strategic emergency-based intervention to overcome the access-based obstruction to IDPs' sites in the severe risky sub-districts in Aleppo Governate ( AZAZ, Jarablous, Afrin, Al-Bab, Jabal Samaan) and the IDPs communities in Aleppo countryside in NW of Syria. Its main objective is to ensure uninterrupted physical access to IDP sites during winter and as when emergency situations arise. Maram Foundation MF will undertake strategic planning with response functions to ensure the delivery of the humanitarian goods to those in need, at the right time, in the right place.. In order to create scientific-based response and well-planned intervention during the disaster, MF has designed communication plan and risk analysis in order to prognose the risks and engage the stakeholders in planned response, and MARAM will continue developing the risk plan and the intervention plan, beside raising awarness of the stakeholders and involve more representatives of the women, PWDs and youth to have their inputs in the plan and risks effects on increasing the PSEA and GBV cases. Based on the outcomes of the committee, MARAM will continue targeting the highest risky locations with awarness signs and signals to draw attention of those acess roads' users to possible risk and guide them for the safer acess/exit. MF Rapid Response Teams will conduct site visits to the locations to have an accurate overview about the site and access roads and to identify whether there are humanitarian organizations acting on the ground for a better response and notification coordination.
Additionally, as per the outcome of this committee, MF will design a map to identify the risky location and accrdingly locate the procured service providers’ machineries nearby them in the call up time. MF is intending to procure service provider/s in each sub-district in order to cover the majority of Aleppo governate principally in the risky locations. Urgent information and description of issue will be delivered.Accordingly, MF will assign its RRTs and the procured contractor/s to response within 24-48 hours of the Crisis Committee, that the clusters will establish, approval. The project will have a positive impact on all beneficiaries in the selected areas, where the total number of the targeted persons approximately: 141985 IDPs (32656 men, 39755 women, 45435 Boys, 24139 Girls) from different age groups (Young children, Children, Adolescents, Young, Middle-aged adults and elderly). This includes about 7100 Persons with Disabilities; in 2022 and 124000 IDPs (30000 men, 35000 women, 35000 Boys, 24000 Girls) from different age groups (Young children, Children, Adolescents, Young, Middle-aged adults and elderly). This includes about 6000 Persons with Disabilities in 2023.

4 - Needs

the project is designed to intervene in the IDPs sites that their roads is inaccessable due to the floods and winter storms in Aleppo governarate countryside. The IDPs sites are located in Aleppo districts ( Jarablous, Al-Bab, Azaz, Afrin, Jabel Samaan) and according to CCCM cluster updated list in Decemeber there are 330 camp sites beside the random camps that are located in those districts.MF is intending to target through this proposal Aleppo governate with focusing on A'zaz, Jarablus, Atareb and Afrin sub-districts as they are characterizing a high risk of floods according to the prioritized list of sites.The targeted locations were prioritized based on their severity and number of people in need (<= 100,000), according to 2021 SYRIA HNO ERL SECTOR CHAPTER data. Moreover, the assessments conducted in these locations by Maram Foundation on November 2021, and others by monitoring institutions, have shown the urgent need rescue people from floods and ensure the assistance to reach them, providing that there are restrictions on targeting new sites not listed in the ground agreement with the donors where an urgent and quick intervention .

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

For visibility during the project, MF will display banners in the targeted communities (after obtaining consent from the local councils). MF staff will wear uniform clothing such as vests and IDs that will show that they are working for the MF organization. MF will implement training on PSEA to be mandatory for all personnel. Periodically, with the support of Field Managers, Focal Points will organize a training session for recruited staff, ideally within three months of recruitment. MF will incorporate an ‘attitudes and beliefs’ session in the training as many respondents highlighted this as the key obstacle to reporting.

After a decade of crisis in Syria, humanitarian needs remain extensive and multifaceted across the country. Avoid targeted locations. 2. Do not stay long at checkpoints. 3. Avoid driving close to other armed groups convoys/ vehicles or any vehicles raising any other armed group sign or flag. 4. Staff should not travel to or be in areas they are not familiar with. 5. Vehicles should be equipped with first aid kits.
