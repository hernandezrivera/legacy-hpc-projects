Project Summary

The action will provide urgent humanitarian support to people affected by conflict and climate change focusing on IDPs, returnees and host communities. The action focuses on delivering MPCA and Protection services (GBV and CP) with a strong integration and both internal and external referrals. The implementation targets Ulang, Fashoda, Pibor, Baliet, Panyikang, and Pigi Counties, which are all in IPC 4 (except Baliet, which is in IPC 3) and are all priority 1 and 2 Counties for the HNRP 2025. The action targets 42,600 people, of which 15% will be people with disabilities. 46,800 will be targeted with MPCA (7800 HHs), and 5800 with Protection.
MPCA will be given in three rounds each targeting 70% of the location specific MSSMEB. The protection component consist of prevention and response within both GBV and Child Protection focusing on community-led prevention work utilising community structures to enhance sustainability, and conflict sensitivity. The response component will take place as case management and MHPSS in both WGFS and CFS.
The implementation will ensure conflict sensitivity, and protection mainstreaming across all activities. The action will be implemented with NNGO Nile Hope across the locations.

1. Needs

All the six proposed Counties are experiencing high levels of food insecurity with all Counties being in IPC 4, except Baliet, which is in IPC 3. Furthermore, the communities are grappling with a high influx of returnees, as a result of the war in Sudan, high levels of IDPs as well as inter- and intracommunal violence. Additionally, the climatic changes, that South Sudan has been witnessing over the recent years are manifesting as above-average flooding, seasonal droughts, and changes in seasonal patterns, which is affecting harvest negatively, but also more acutely impacting access to vital infrastructure in many hard-to-reach communities and causing displacement. Both conflict and climate change affects women and girls disproportionally, and this coupled with strong gender roles and cultural harmful practices, women and girls are facing extremely high protection risks.

2. Project activities

1. MPCA - unconditional multi-purpose cash distributions to 46,800 individuals (7800 HHs) in three rounds constituting 70% of the context-specific MSSMEB. 2. Protection - GBV prevention through awareness and women protection groups3. Protection - GBV response through case management and MHPSS in WGFS4. Protection - Child Protection Prevention through awareness raising5. Protection - Child Protection response through case management and MHPSS in CFP6. Due to the volatile situation in South Sudan, the action will include an Emergency Response Mechanism in order to rapidly respond through MPCA to any sudden onset shocks.
