Project Summary

The humanitarian crisis in the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon is hugely characterized by protection concerns, with children greatly affected by the crisis and has given rise to child protection concerns. As a result, the rights of these populations especially children and young adolescents are not respected, facing impediments to their freedom of movements, their right to be protected from violence and harm. With forced recruitment into armed groups, they are at risk of ill-treatment, abductions, illegal detention, resulting to many cases of unaccompanied and separated children, children without documentation such as birth certificates. Children's rights to live in a family is affected for the numerous unaccompanied and separated children; their right to be protection from violence, abuse and exploitation is put at stake in an armed conflict environment. BETA within the framework of the project. The Child Protection Area Of Responsibility within the mission of the protection cluster has identified child protection as an urgent concern and need. BETA in response to the urgent protection needs, plans to provide conflict-affected children with psychosocial support and protection from violence, exploitation and abuse in the South West Region. To achieve this milestone BETA will Empower and accompany/support community based structures and child friendly families/documented individuals to Provide psychosocial support to conflict-affected children in new displacement locations, in collaboration with the empowered community based structures and child friendly families/documented individuals, Identify and document unaccompanied and separated children, monitor and report violations against children’s rights and providing support to survivors of sexual and gender-based violence.
These actions will be complemented with an effective project coordination and monitoring by the specialized skilled Coordination and M&E Department

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The protection issues in the project targeted communities are really alarming, and so needs community based structures to respond to these protection needs, but these structures are weak and some absent. BETA will carry out situation assessment missions to the new displacement sites to identify, document and recruit 60 volunteer families, community based institutions and individuals into a community-based rapid child protection and psychosocial support systems. With the understanding that these community based structures are weak and lack capacity, BETA will Conduct a quick two days training on basic child protection and psychosocial support for 60 selected community-based child protection and psychosocial volunteer members to provide first line child protection, psychosocial support for conflict affected children as well as documentation and referral for Unaccompanied and Separated Children (UASC) victims of Sexual Gender Based Violence (SGBV) to BETA and other implementing partners case managers or to other services providers
