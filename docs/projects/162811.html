Project Summary

Given the current situation in Sudan the refugee population and other migrants and asylum seeker in different states are at risk of GBV and Sexual and reproductive health SRH problems and complications due to insecurity, limited access to health care services, low economic status and lack of livelihood opportunities, lack of community awareness, combined with negative coping strategies. UNFPA in 2020 will support integrated GBV and SRH life-saving prevention and response services for South Sudanese refugees, refugees and asylum seekers in Khartoum, Ethiopian, Eritrean asylum seekers in the east and CAR refugees in South Darfur, with focus on women and girls, men and boys.
Khartoum presents a diverse and complex migration profile of Eritrean, Ethiopian, Congolese, Syrian and Yemeni. UNFPA objective in this project is to address GBV prevention and response needs including the SRH consequences among the urban refugees in the prioritized localities of (Jabal Aulia, Khartoum, Sharq El Nile, Um Durman and Umm Badda as well as the Open Area) through the planned activities: Support immediate GBV/SGBV prevention and response services, community-based protection networks, Community outreach activities, Host community sensitization, and trafficking prevention and response services, and supporting provision of clinical management of rape CMR services to survivors. So far the GBV referral pathways builds on the availability of existing services including medical treatment of survivors (CMR). Vulnerable host communities living near to the targeted population will be considered in the response.
In East Sudan UNFPA objective is to address GBV prevention and response and SRH needs among the Eritrean and Ethiopian refugees in the prioritized localities El Fashaga (Um Gargour camp), Khashm Ghirba (Kilo 26 and Girba camps),Wad Sherifey camp; Wad El Helew (Shagarab I, II and III camps ). Host community sensitization, Capacity building of local authorities and institutions as well as Minimum initial service package for RH - MISP for RH services will be provided through procurement and distribution of interagency reproductive health kits IARHK serving safe delivery, preventing complications, and providing CMR, mobile clinics, and capacity building of health care providers HCP on safe motherhood and CMR will be provided.
For CAR refugees UNFPA will respond to the SRH and GBV needs in the prioritized localities of ( Dimsu, Um dafug, Elradom, and Nyala) through procurement and distribution of IARHK, in addition to mobile clinics, and capacity building of health care providers HCP on safe motherhood and CMR. The response will address GBV intervention based on identified needs such as community-based protection services, community outreach, information dissemination, SGBV awareness and service access.
In South kordoafan UNFPA will support GBV and SRH services in Abu Jebaiha, Talodi, Sirajiya and El Leri , and West Kordofan the intervention will cover, El Meiram and Kharasana. In while Nile UNFPA will support GBV and SRH services in the refugees’ camps in AlSalam and El Jablain localities as well Kosti town. The project will link SRH and GBV interventions in so far as the GBV referral pathways builds on the availability of existing services including CMR.
The project consists of a SRH (40%) and GBV (60%). The current situation affected both the refugees and the host community therefore 10% of the host community will be benefited from the project services. Implementation of activities will be through NGOs and Gov partners with a strong technical capacity and field presence.

1 - Needs

UNFPA has gathered information from its monitoring missions as well information from partners during 2019 indicated that still South Sudanese refuges, Ethiopians, and Eritreans asylum seekers and refugees, particularly women and girls are vulnerable, under-served with regard to basic services and also lack behind with regard to exercising their sexual and reproductive health rights SRHR. As well based on the identified needs provided by the 2020 Refugee Response Plan needs overview indicated that urban refugees and asylum seekers in khartoum, refugees in Kassala, and CAR in South Darfur facing different of protection concerns. Physical protection needs are very high among refugees living in Khartoum, many other refugees face the challenges of limited or no access to legal aid and other protection assistance, high risk of trafficking and exposure SGBV additional physical protection issues linked with host community tensions, inequitable access to public services, early marriage in Syrian and Ethiopian communities, all require to be address through targeted community-based protection intervention. Pregnant women and girls face gaps in sexual and reproductive health service SRH, including limited or no access primary health services at public health facilities. In East (Kassala), identified needs in the RRP2020 need overview such as risk of SGBV due to smuggling and trafficking, kidnapping and abduction, SGBV lack of awareness and service access, limited access primary health services including SRH gaps. Reference made the refugees target for health, and using MISP calculator for RH needs, it is estimated that 615,800 women and girls in reproductive age will require assistance from the refugees, among whom 81,760 pregnant women are estimated and in need of access to safe birth services. 12,264 of the estimated pregnant women are expected to experience complication, and 12,264 will require caesarean section. For all identified localities, it is expected that 12,316 survivors of SGBV will seek medical care and required to be served.
