Project Summary

This project came as an urgent response to enhance the resilience of 109 vulnerable families located at area C of the West Bank from natural and manmade hazards. The targeted families include 150 adult males, 146 adult females, 115 males less than 18, and 112 females less than 18.
The project aims at improving, rehabilitating, and winterizing of vulnerable and inadequate shelters in Nablus and Qalqilya area C. This will reduce the exposure of targeted beneficiaries to both man-made and natural disasters, weather conditions and risk of flooding in which threat the existence of these shelters and protect them from the harsh practice of settler violence. This will be by enabling the targeted families to live in adequate standards with taking in consideration of giving priority for families headed by women.
The proposed project addressed directly to the second and third cluster strategic objective of the 2019 humanitarian response plan (part of 2108-2020 HRP). Where “vulnerable household exposed to harsh weather and protection concerns are supported to meet their basic needs and enhancing their coping capacity”, and at the same time it will contribute to third objective “Capacity to respond to natural or manmade hazards in a timely, efficient and effective manner is increase”. The planned interventions are directly response to the need of improving the access for basic services by vulnerable Palestinians, which is in this project is shelter (renovation, upgrading, and at the same time to raise awareness and risk mitigation at household level. The project was developed in a participatory approach at community levels (October 2017 and updated assessment April 2018 coordinated by the shelter thematic group, and lastly during September 2018 by PARC staff), in addition of coordination and consultation with key stakeholders at institutional level (working NGOs and related governmental ministries). The targeted areas are characterized as some very remote areas with weak services (inadequate water and sanitation services and electricity). HHs at the targeted communities suffer from Israeli occupation and become less able to cope with sudden manmade shock (demolitions, evacuation, limited area for construction and no possibility for natural expansion). Natural or environmental hazards (winter storms, hot wave, water scarcity ...etc) as a result of the longer term effects of climate change and the environmental impact. Additionally, the project will tackle the agreed shelter cluster long-term objective; enhance/improve the living conditions according to shelter minimum standards focusing on privacy and dignity, safety and protection. In addition to the second objective, which is protection from immediate harm.
The planned interventions are responsive in nature. It will fill gaps resulting from emerging or long-lasting need for protection vulnerable HH from natural or manmade disasters, and aimed at alleviating its immediate effects through mitigate and eliminate the negative impact of harsh weather conditions/summarization/winterization, health and hygiene) or manmade disasters (Israeli measures, limited construction area and natural expansion for Palestinian, forcible transfer or settlers’ violence). Also the action has a remedial nature with activities beyond immediate response towards addressing time-sensitive or high impact underlying issues, advancing the top priority resilience needs, additional priority/time sensitive activities that support affected people or prevent secondary risks. The planned activities will adequate functionality of the main and essential shelter elements like roof and walls leakage treatment, windows and doors, suitable floor, enough space, insulation and privacy.

Needs

HH need assessment carried at three stages with fully participatory approach at community level, LGU, CBOs, key stakeholders and cluster lead; 1) October 2017 where PARC through its field supervisors (almost 10 days) had conducted a survey to collect and analysis an in-depth information about targeted locations at four governorates :Jordan valley, Hebron, Qalqilya and Nablus. 2) April 2018 where shelter assessment launched and coordinated by the cluster lead where also Salfeet governorate included, and 3)the last assessment, during September 2018, where PARC’s staff validate these needs and better coordination with other NGOs (MAAN, ACH,UWAC...etc) and grass-root organizations, in addition of two central workshops were conducted with participation of community representatives including (60 male, and 25females) in the Qalqilya workshop, 40 mal and 33 females at Nablus workshop. The last assessment aimed to needs validation and final selection of five targeted vulnerable communities distributed at 2 governorates as follows:10 HH at Khirbet Twayel, 14 HH at Lifjim Froush Beit Dajan including; 8 HH at Basaliya, and 8 HH at Abu Kbash- mixed and herder communities, knowing that the previously mentioned communities located at Nablus governorate. The other community located at qalqilya governorate where 19 HH were selected at Arab AL- Ramadin al Janubi-bedouin ). The result of the assessment shows that there is a huge need for rehabilitating shelters for 109 families where they are very close to winter season. Displacement becomes an increasing hazard, with many communities in area C specifically among the most vulnerable Bedouins and herders. The natural disasters due to heavy rain and storms in the winter time and high temperature in the summer time have increased the vulnerability of those communities in addition to the Israeli policies which formed multiple and accumulative Hazardous. On other hand the nature of the shelters (tents, caves, ironic shelter, concrete, Mud, and old stones) which are getting worst by time and inadequate for housing to Women, children and elders. The main obstacles identified at the proposed areas are: Poor hygiene and health living conditions, which is also in line and a reflection for the SDG3 (Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages). And also SDG 6 (Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all -WASH), due to the bad living condition, and ventilation -lack of proper bathroom, kitchen, water network, and broken or lack of doors and windows in which reduce the privacy of women and effect their health condition and prevent them from practicing their life in adequate living standards. People are subjected to more diseases and risks due to the direct exposal for environment. Some of the families are living in the same place that where they raise their animals which expose them for diseases. Crowding and high number of family members living in the same room, which make them infected from each other viruses if occurred. Some of the families have members with special needs in which they need special arrangements in the shelter.
