Project Summary

As a result of the military operation in the last months, GOS took control of several cities and towns in southern Idlib, and according to the United Nations, nearly 700,000 civilians were displaced towards the Syrian-Turkish border and 2000 civilians were killed.
In the project areas and as a result of the relatively stable security situation due to its proximity to the Turkish border, the number of IDPs increased significantly, which increased the economic and social burdens on the region, especially with the decrease the humanitarian aids and the suspension of a number of humanitarian agencies for their work in northwestern Syria.
As a result of the increase in the number of IDPs, the number of informal camps in the region increased, which left large burdens on the host community and the IDPs, especially with the lack of natural resources in the region, especially water resources and weak the infrastructure in the region.
As a result of the increase in the number of IDPs, disturbances have emerged between IDPs and new IDPs in order to benefit from the services provided by humanitarian organizations, which threatens community cohesion in the region.
Through this project, Ataa will be reaching 125,011 beneficiaries (54%female-46%male)- (56% under18-44% above18) in:
1- Idleb, Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Kelly, Kelly camps, and Zardana Mashehad.
2- Idleb, Harim, Dana, Tal Elkaramej.
3- Aleppo, A'zaz, A'zaz, Kafr Kalbein.
The targeted communities and camps population size are about 25,559 HHs, 47% Host communities and 53% IDPs.
The targeted communities and camps are in need such project due to the increase in the number of IDPs, the number of informal camps in the region increased, therefore it is very essential for the target communities to have affordable, long-lasting water source as well as to have a sewage system, solid waste management, and hygiene promotion, all of that will greatly help in preventing many diseases to beneficiaries.
60% of HHs, will be able to use water through the water
network and directly from their houses or directly from water taps in the camps.
60% of HHs, their houses or camps will be able to connect to the sewage system instead of sewage pits.
100% of HHs will receive hygiene along with hygiene promotion activities.
70% of HHs will help with SWM activities.
Specific monitoring activities include: rehabilitate water networks and chlorination in the communities and camps, confirming the distribution of hygiene items through onsite monitoring, monitoring report, confirming beneficiary verification for their access to cleaned and chlorinated water, feedback, and satisfaction according to beneficiary lists.
Ataa will work on the rehabilitation of the water stations and provide equipment, in addition to equipping wells with operation by alternative energy (solar energy) to reducing operating costs.
Ataa will activate the cost recovery mechanism by collecting monthly fees from every HHs in the targeted communities and camps, and the local council in each community will be responsible for collecting the fees from HHs. MoU will be signed with the local councils.
It is worth to mention during the implementation of the project, if the targeted locations receive more IDPs, ATAA will target the newly IDPs with all project activities, and the project activities will help to decrease the disturbances that emerged between host and IDPs.
Ataa will conduct FGDs with targeted communities to inform them about the goal, outcomes, and, activities of the project. Brochures and banners will be used to share information with the communities.
Ataa will set a community-based mechanism to make sure that community members can submit their complaints and feedback. feedback tracker will be prepared on a monthly basis.
Local councils and other stakeholders in the targeted communities and camps will be consulted to oversee all aspects of the project.

4 - Needs

According to OCHA“The humanitarian situation in northwest Syria continues to deteriorate at an alarming rate. From 1st Dec/2019 to 4th Feb/2020, over half a million people displaced in northwest Syria."WASH cluster stated "in both formal and informal camps there is a huge need to increase the water supply and manage drinking water safety. There is an increasing need for desludging, as the septic tanks and pit latrines are flooding causing contamination to nearby water sources. As the displacement is ongoing and displaced population are prioritizing communities to settle in, there is a need to increase the operational support to existing piped water systems."ATAA between 1st-6th Feb/2020 conducted a primary need assessment in Idleb and Aleppo governorates, Kelly and its surrounding camps, Zardana Mashehad, Tal Elkaramej and Kafr Kalbein with 2 KIIs in each community and camps by 8 enumerators.There are 9 water stations in the targeted location fed by 12 wells, which need for minor rehabilitation, their approximate production capacity is 1200 m3/day and have 5 generators their capacities are ranged between 550 and 150 KVA those stations need for minor rehabilitation and adding another pumps and generators to be able to deliver water to whole residents.60% out of whole residents in both communities and camps don’t have access to both sewage and water system using instead sewage pits, public latrines and water trucks coming for private wells and the existence networks need for minor rehabilitation which forced the displaced people to settle in newly established camps which are lack of all life essential requirement.Due to the high price of hygiene items’ and water (2.5SYR/L), whole residents can’t afford to buy their basic needs of water and there are need for distributing hygiene kits and conducting hygiene promotion.Waste collectors should be recruited, bins should be provided and solid waste trucks should be assigned and ensure there is daily waste transport

5 - Implementation of activities

Ataa will conduct an infrastructure assessment for the water network and prepare estimated costs and quantities. Ataa will extend the water networks, and support visits to the working areas will be carried out on a daily basis in addition to final inspection for the networks and start pumping the water through the water networks.Ataa will rehabilitation and install a 110mm polyethylene pipes for 8000m as the main pipe and connect it with the existing main pipe and will connect a 75mm polyethylene pipes for 6000m as a sub-network. (This network will be in the communities and will connect the camps with the communities water network to provide safe and drinkable water on a daily basis and to dispense with the purchase of water trucks).Ataa will rehabilitation and install sewage systems network, the process of drilling and filling of 9,000m will be implemented and installing a 1.5m length and diameter of 0.4m for 3,300m and 1.5m length and diameter of 0.3m for 4,500m as a sub-network.Ataa will cover the sewage pits with reinforced concrete and provide an iron top cover to the sewage pits, provide and install asphalt for the top covers. (This network will be in the communities and will connect the camps with the communities sewage network to cancel the septic pits in the camps and prevent the spread of diseases).Ataa will provide pre-fabricated public toilets to the project camps.Ataa will equip the 9 W.S with all the necessary equipment, in addition to equipping the wells with operation by alternative energy (solar energy) to reducing operating costs, and provide monthly running costs to cover some of the operation costs.Ataa will conduct hygiene awareness sessions in the targeted locations and distribution of 20,000 Hygiene kits, and conducting the PSEA/GBV awareness-raising sessions to the beneficiaries.Ataa will work on re-activate the SWM in the targeted location throughout distribution garbage bins and collecting garbage on a daily basis.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project was designed in a manner that benefits all the categories of the target community, including women, children, people with disabilities and the most vulnerable category.As well as the project has designed to achieve fair gender equality, without focusing on a specific gender without the other. Equality will also be achieved in the recruitment process and personnel selection.A- Sexual exploitation and abuse including workplace harassment and child abuse by ATAA personnel, its partner, services providers/suppliers toward the beneficiaries especially women and girls. The mitigation:- Mainstreaming PSEA/ workplace harassment policy, in addition to CSG policy including training ATAA personnel.- requiring ATAA`s personnel, partner, services providers/suppliers to sign a commitment of the policy.- Activate the complaint mechanism and penalty procedures.- Awareness raising sessions to the beneficiaries about sexual exploitation and abuse and their rights, and aware them of complaints mechanism.- Assign a protection officer to monitor all protection standards during the implementation of all of the project activities- Activating the mechanism of referring survivors of SEA/GBV to the specialist servicesB- The risk of sexual abuse/harassment within toilets unitsThe mitigation: • The units will be separated, Special units for females and special units for males• Assign A female clean worker for the toilet units of females, and a male clean worker for the toilet units of males, This clean workers will be trained in standards for protection from sexual exploitation and abuse, to be responsible for monitoring the security situation, and reporting the sexual violations.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school.R5: Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitationL.H:LM.M: MoUs to be agreed with local councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities and promote cost recovery systems.
