Project Summary

An integrated approach responding to the needs of conflict affected children (boys and girls needs) and their families, targeting 400,000 persons with a focus on adolescents, caregivers, young mothers, social workforce and community based groups including children with disabilities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The programme has key monitoring functions throughout the programme cycle to identify child protection risks and assess the appropriate response. A needs assessment is used to identify child protection risks for boys and girls, safety audits are conducted on a monthly basis throughout the programme with boys, girls and caregivers and communities to identify risks for different ages and genders of under 18s. These risks are then prevented and mitigated through community action and also support through advocacy, legislation and insitutionalisation. To ensure access the programme will conduct community outreach to reach the target groups, will ensure accessibility through design of time and location of programmes and advocacy with community leaders and will conduct a barriers to services analysis to inform changes to programming to improve services. The programme will be based on the use of the appropriate minimum standards (CPMS) to ensure that key principles such as best interest of the child are implemented. Through the case management programme, children will be enabled to participate in decisions on their cases based on their age and maturity.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

A barriers analysis will be conducted with adolescent boys and girls to assess barriers to services. Services will be provided at community level to ensure that services are adapted based on consultations. Monthly consultations will be conducted with the key stakeholders in the programme to ensure monitoring and adjustments throughout the programme. UNICEF uses child rights framework in the planning and design of programmes. This approach places children at the centre of a response and is based on principles relating to the best interest of the child. This involves a process of designing programmes based on assessment of needs, child participation and triangulation with communities and caregivers. MHPSS programmes are designed in consultation with the persons targeted for the programming including time, location and consultation on activities. The programmes have feedback sessions with the participants at the end of each session to ensure amendments can be made throughout the cycle of programming. Child protection monitoring is completed in consultation with communities and action plans are developed with communities to implement protection strategies, these are reviewed with the community on a monthly basis through community groups that are accessible to men, women, youth and children with disability. All UNICEF partners have access to training on how to work with persons with disabilities and in the use of inclusive approaches. Programmes will be tailored for age and gender and inclusive of abilities considering the use of language, literacy levels and will be adjusted accordingly. The programme will target at risk groups through community outreach and by engaging youth, community leaders who are from different backgrounds and gender.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNICEF parnters with AIFO and Light of the World to support training to implementing partners on disability inclusion and also to link referral for assistance for specialized support. Structures, facilities and recreation kits and other forms of kit are designed with consultation of disability agencies to ensure the design is accessible for all and items can be used by all.Data is also disaggregated by age, gender and disability. Awareness raising is implemented at the level of the community to ensure that communities are aware of disability inclusion and support members of their community to participate in activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The programme will use an approach of resilience building of communities to ensure that communities can build on their own resources and strengths to limit the need of outside materials and support. The design of the multipurpose centres will be based on local natural materials. The programme aims to empower communities to participate in the building of the structures. Implementing staff will be recruited locally to provide sustainable approaches to knowledge development and resilience. Where possible, kits will be locally procured to put back into community business and use materials that are familiar and used by communities.Emergency preparedness measures will be supported at district and provincial level to support local response capacity and regional responses to reduce need and use of flights in case of further crisis or natural disaster.
