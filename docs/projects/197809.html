Project Summary

COMINSUD has selected target activities from the CMR_HRP21 CLUSTER FRAMEWORK PROTECTION SECTOR to implement in the NW. The HNO identified the need to address the issue of loss/lack of Civil Documentation by providing support to issue civil documentation to conflict-affected individuals and the host population in affected localities, COMINSUD’s target is 15000 persons of both sexes (7600 females and 7400 men) out of the NW target of 94620
Awareness campaigns on the importance of civil documentation is another need. We aim to organize 8 awareness-raising sessions on civil documentation. To advocate with national & regional authorities for better adequacy of civil documentation procedures, we aim to organize 6 advocacy interventions.
Violation of human rights law: COMINSUD shall 3 organize advocacy and training on violation of human rights law with all parties in the crisis. We shall organize 2 capacity-building sessions on human-rights law principles. Protection monitoring activities: To organize protection-monitoring activities, COMINSUD will target 30,000 individuals of both sexes (17000 females and 13000 men). We shall also record 150 cases (80 females and 70 males) of arbitrary arrest and unlawful detentions. Legal aid provision: COMINSUD will provide legal aid to 250 persons (153 females and 147 males) arbitrarily arrested/unlawfully detained.
To address the need for protection assistance to the most vulnerable people: We aim to identify and target 750 persons of both sexes (383 females and 367 males) who are the most vulnerable persons to provide targeted protection assistance. Profile 2000 vulnerable people and determine their needs. Strengthening capacity of the affected population on community-based structures: We aim to strengthen the capacity of 20 community-based structures to respond to the identified protection needs of persons affected by the conflict in the North West Region. Livelihood support: COMINSUD aims to target 160 (78 men and 82 women) most vulnerable persons to benefit from livelihood support.

1 - Besoins / Needs

From the HNO COMINSUD has picked out some important needs to address. The project will address the following key assessed needs through its activities: Loss/Lack of Civil Documentation: to provide support to issue civil documentation to conflict-affected individuals and host population in affected localities, COMINSUD’s will target to achieve this is 15000 persons of both sexes (7600 females and 7400 men). Engage and provide orientation to 100 community volunteers to facilitate processes for the establishment of civil documentation for needed persons, Identify and profile 20,000 persons in need of civil documentation, Support the process for the establishment of 15,000 civil identification documents to 15,000 people. Awareness campaigns on the importance of civil documentation is another need that will be addressed. For this activity, we shall organize 8 awareness-raising sessions on civil documentation. Organized one-day working sessions with 20 Stakeholders (Councils, Courts, and BUNEC) to elaborate on the importance of having civil status documentation, what is needed to have which service document, and the steps to follow to have which status document, Conceived, produced, and distribute awareness sensitization raising materials (1000 Posters, 500 Flyers, 10 Banners, 10 Roll up, 4 Backdrops), Engaged 10 local community radios stations and 100 community volunteers to run awareness-raising campaigns on the importance of having civil documentations Advocate with national & regional authorities for better adequacy of civil documentation procedures. To address this issue, COMINSUD aims to organize 6 advocacy interventions with regional authorities on civil documentation related-matters. Violation of human rights law by all parties to the crisis: COMINSUD shall conduct 3 advocacy interventions on behalf of the Protection Sector. We shall also organize 2 capacity-building sessions on human-rights law principles. Research and extract elements related to human rights laws such as the Geneva Convention, protection of civilians involved in armed conflict, and the general framework for human rights protection. Produced a simplified document that highlights human rights laws that must be respected especially in a conflict context. Protection monitoring activities: COMINSUD will target 30,000 individuals of both sexes (17000 females and 13000 men) covered by protection monitoring activities. Also, we shall record the 150 cases (80 females and 70 males) of arbitrary arrest and unlawful detentions. Determine venerability risk protection, and security needs for which the population needs to be protected and develop protection monitoring tools (Guideline for referencing and conducting protection monitoring exist), Legal aid provision: COMINSUD will provide legal aid to 250 persons (153 females and 147 males) arbitrarily arrested/unlawfully detained. Set up legal interventions or teams to intervene for 250 persons in detention, engage a process to free persons involved in undue detained, Create hotline to report cases of undue detention Protection Assistance to the most vulnerable people: We aim to identify and target 750 persons of both sexes (383 females and 367 males) who are the most vulnerable persons to provide targeted protection assistance, define criteria for selection of vulnerable persons left behind after family separation, Strengthening the capacity of the affected population on community-based structures: We aim to strengthen the capacity of 10 community-based structures to respond to the identified protection needs of persons affected by the conflict in the North West Region. Create 10 community-base structure to offer support to the affected population in communities where non-exit, livelihood support: COMINSUD COMINSUD aims to target 160 (78 men and 82 women) most vulnerable persons to benefit from livelihood support.
