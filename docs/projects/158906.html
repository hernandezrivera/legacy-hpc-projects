Project Summary

The Project will be implemented over a period of 12 months and targets around 7015 individuals residing in vulnerable and marginalized communities at Area C communities of both Jenin and Salfit Governorates. The project mainly targets 3 communities (A'ba and As Saideh Communities in Jenin and Haris in Salfit). It aims at improving the health situation of a total of 7015 individuals in those three mentioned communities through ensuring access to safe and adequate water and hygiene education. The inhabitants of those communities are currently suffering from chronic health problems due to lack of access to clean and adequate water and hygiene education that in turn resulted in poor economic status. The project will address these key needs which form the basis of all development through participatory, inclusive and effective means.
The Union of Agriculture Work Committee will implement the project with the Municipality Council and the target beneficiaries and CBOs. The project will be implemented by integrating water supply, facilities, and mass hygiene education as a package, community participation, and Capacity development of CBOs. The project main activities are 1. Construction and installation of main pipes and valves 2", 2. Construction and installation of main pipes and valves 3", 3. Complete Supply and installation of House Connections with whole requirement fitting excavation in all types of soil, cast iron hand hole, and cover, backfilling and reinstatement, and all required types of fitting, 4. Conduct a training course (capacity building) for the municipality working team in target locations regarding the best practices and technical issues in water. the course will be for 30 hours for 15 people, 5. Conduct awareness sessions for the local community including printed material, 6. Conduct a training course regarding best practices for water consumption and use for the people in the area

1 - Needs

Palestinians in the West Bank are subject to a complex system of control, including physical (the Barrier, checkpoints, roadblocks) and bureaucratic barriers (permits, closure of areas) which restrict their right to freedom of movement. The expansion of Settlements, restrictions on access to land and natural resources and ongoing displacement due to demolitions in particular, are ongoing. Israeli policies curtail the ability of Palestinians in Area C and East Jerusalem to plan their communities and build homes and infrastructure. The result is further fragmentation of the West Bank. Ongoing violent incidents throughout the West Bank pose risks to life, liberty and security, and – security considerations notwithstanding – concerns exist over reports of excessive use of force by Israeli forces. Referring to the targeted communities within the project, 3 communities (A'aba and As Saaideh in Jenin Governorate and Hares in Salfit Governorate) at the North of the West Bank suffer from the lack of access to domestic water and sanitation and hygiene services due to their vulnerability status for being located at Area C. In general, based on the Vulnerability profile of OCHA, most of the 54 communities in Jenin in Area C are considered highly vulnerable to Water, Hygiene and Sanitation and where 24 communities suffer from irregular supply of water or no supply at all and the majority suffer from a high price of water tanker reaching over 25 NIS per cubic meter. In salfit governorate there are 10 communities within Area C and are considered highly vunerable to Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Services with irregular supply of water or no supply at all and where the price of a tanker is higher than 25 NIS per cubic meter. As for the targeted communities within the project, Northern A'aba community with a population of at Jenin Governorate for instance lacks a domestic water connection network directly to the households where residents are required to get their supply from water tanks where there's only one water tank with the size of 4 Cubic Meters to supply the residents of the area while they get in shortage in Summer having to wait several days until they can get a new supply of water that most most of the times they fill only galons of 18 litres for drinking and postponing other necessary house activities accordingly. The majority of the community depends on farming and herding and from 2000 till 2019 around 250 individuals left the community due to insufficient water supply. As Saideh is located between four villages A'nin,T'inik taybeh and Seelat Al-harethia Residents in the Area are not connected to Water Supply and they rather get their supply from buying from Water tanks. The residents of the Area also suffered from demolition orders issued by the Israeli Occupation and they don't even have access to medical centers or schools. Many of the residents get diarrhea due to polluted water supply as well and many as well disregard washing their hands before eating or after going to the bathroom due to limited supply. As for Haris in Salfit, the area is at risk of forced displacement due to demolition and confiscation orders, Um Al-Khouf within this area is populated with 500 individuals with no water connection and irregular supply of water by water tanks leading to social conflict between the residents and diseases from polluted water accordingly.
