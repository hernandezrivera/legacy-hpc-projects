Project Summary

Timely provision of quality lifesaving and life-sustaining nutrition assistance to vulnerable populations in Cabo Delgado with particular focus to children, pregnant and breastfeeding women and girls and people living with HIV/AIDS who have the highest risk of undernutrition and mortality. The response package aims to prevent and reduce child wasting and micronutrient deficiencies while protecting growth and development during the critical period of pregnancy and the first years of age. Households with children 6-23 months of age and pregnant and breastfeeding women/girls (PBWG) severely food insecurity will receive food assistance topped up with specialized nutritious foods to ensure that essential nutrient requirements are met. Children with moderate wasting and undernourished pregnant and breastfeeding women/girls will be identified and enrolled in the Nutrition Rehabilitation Programme to receive dietary support and clinical care according to MoH protocols. In target districts, isolated communities will benefit from regular mobile brigades that will conduct massive nutritional screening and referral, and provide other health services such as immunization, micronutrients supplementation, deworming, growth monitoring, sexual and reproductive services and treatment of infection diseases. WFP will integrate in its food and nutrition assistance, including climate adaptive livelihoods support, counselling on maternal, infant and young child nutrition through a comprehensive community-based approach. The collaboration with government, UNICEF and other members of the nutrition cluster will continue to identify districts at higher risk of increased child wasting and overall malnutrition through the investment in a nutrition surveillance system, nutrition assessments and IPC acute malnutrition analysis.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) Targeted Supplementary Feeding for Prevention: Through vulnerable-based targeting exercises, will identify which vulnerable households have children under two years of age and/or pregnant and breastfeeding women and girls (PBWG). Those households will receive an additional protective measure to ensure that children 6-23 months of age (CU2) and PBWG meet most of their nutrient requirements by complementing WFP food ration with individual support for these specific groups. As such, each CU2 will receive Super Cereal Plus and each PBWG will receive Super Cereal aligned with the food assistance cycles. This represents 100 percent of daily kcal and nutrient requirements of a child 6-23 months of age and 74 percent of the daily kcal and 69 percent of micronutrient requirements of a pregnant women. This complementary food supplementation will be prioritized in the four districts classified as most in need (severity 4) according to the intersectoral data analysis, starting with Macomia and Quissanga, classified in emergency phase of food insecurity (IPC AFI 4). 2) Targeted Supplementary Feeding for Management of Wasting: In the 15 priority districts of Cabo Delgado, WFP will support health authorities to implement Targeted Supplementary Feeding Programme (TSFP) for children under 5 years of age (CU5) with moderate wasting and for pregnant and breastfeeding women/adolescent girls (PBWAG) with moderate and severe undernutrition. The TSFP is an integral part of the National Nutrition Rehabilitation Programme (PRN). Both moderate and severe wasting cases will be identified and enrolled in PRN services through health facilities and integrated health mobile brigades. WFP will procure and supply health facilities with specialized nutritious foods (RUSF and CSB+) to supplement CU5 with moderate wasting and undernourished PBWAG, including those living with HIV and Tuberculosis (TB). WFP will also supply health facilities with non-food items (e.g. screening tapes, job-aids, registration books) and ensure health facility staff is trained to provide quality PRN services. 3) Active-case finding of wasting cases and referral to treatment services: To ensure early identification and increase access to and coverage of PRN services, WFP will support regular active case finding through nutritional screening and referral of wasting cases to PRN services in isolated communities. Mobile health brigades will be implemented by health professionals from nearby government-led health facilities with involvement of community health workers or by trained health staff from NGOs. These mobile services will also provide treatment and management of wasting and other health services in communities with limited access to health facilities. WFP will provide technical and financial support to the district health authorities or NGOs in the 14 districts to improve microplanning and logistics to implementing these mobile brigades on a regular basis. 4) Social Behaviour Change (SBC) to increase demand for, and consumption of safe and nutritious foods, including adoption of hygiene practices: SBC activities will be implemented through the "Say YES to Nutrition" (NutriSIM) campaign that focus on healthy eating behaviors and nutritious recipes and incorporates radio listening sessions and a cooking school as its core components. NutriSIM is guided by a formative assessment conducted in Cabo Delgado in 2024. These activities will be integrated alongside the provision of food and nutrition assistance and climate adaptive livelihoods programmes to facilitate the adoption of desired behaviors. 5) Generating evidence to guide the nutrition response: WFP is supporting the government to establish a nutrition monitoring system to anticipate nutrition needs and inform responses to prevent and address malnutrition in humanitarian crises. This includes the implementation of nutrition assessments in the most shock-affected areas, followed by IPC analysis.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

WFP is committed to taking action to facilitate safe inclusion in food and nutrition assistance to victims of SEA and GBV (where targeting criteria are met) with cases referred through the PSEA network and GBV actors. WFP is furthermore working closely with the recently established social and environmental safeguarding division of INGD to ensure synergies between WFP and the Government on integration of protection, AAP and PSEA in the humanitarian responses in Mozambique, including supporting strengthened commitment and capacity to address abuses of power in the humanitarian response recognizing that the most visible perpetrators are local leaders acting on behalf of the Government. Recognising the identified risks above, WFP Mozambique is committed to ensuring protection-centered and principled interventions across Mozambique and in this regard maintains a targeted protection and accountability structure, with a particular focus on the Northern region. The protection and AAP team works with senior management, programme, and supply chain colleagues as well as cooperating partners (CPs) to incorporate mitigation and prevention measures for the identified protection risks associated with the food assistance and operational context. Furthermore, they actively engage with the affected population and implementing partners to handle individual cases such as abuses of power, sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), exclusion/ inclusion errors, food quality concerns among others reported through the available community feedback mechanisms. As a preventive measure to protection risks and to best ensure clarity on beneficiary rights and intervention objectives WFP works with CPs to ensure accessible community engagement which is supported by the provision of accessible communications tools and regularly updated communication-with-communities documents containing key messages regarding the intervention, which are disseminated to WFP and CP staff, CFM teams including the tollfree inter-agency hotline, as well as the broader humanitarian community and government where possible. Furthermore, recognizing the challenges faced by people living with disabilities (PWD) to access assistance, WFP is taking targeted action to address this through several methods. For instance, by ensuring inclusion and participation of PWD in community committees, by engaging the disability working group to directly refer PWD and their families to food assistance, and by working with the Trinity College of Dublin to develop communications supports with the dual goal of tackling both communication barriers caused by disability and language generally due to high illiteracy and mixed language needs of the displaced population. As an active member of the protection cluster, PSEA network (co-chair at national level), WFP And CPs are enabled to access protection actors and Government referral pathways for GBV and child protection, for example, enabling a strengthened rights-based approach and access to vital services beyond food assistance.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project targets specifically children under five and pregnant and lactating women and girls. SBC activities are tailored to empower women as the main caregiver of the family and children to be able to provide proper nutritional care for herself and her children.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Where there is overlap between beneficiaries that are disabled and those that are acutely malnourished and the same quality of services will be provided regardless of disability status. Additionally, the targeted supplementary feeding programme for prevention is blanket for all children 6-23 months, including those children with disabilities. Active screening and treatment through regular mobile brigades occur in isolated or hard-to-reach communities to ensure services are closer to those unable to reach health and nutrition services.
