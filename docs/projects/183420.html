Project Summary

The project aims at providing access to primary health care and providing COVID 19 homecare to individuals affected by the crises in Lebanon. The intervention will be rolled out through primary health care centers and mobile care teams operated by Amel. It will target 9000 individuals over a duration of 12 months through facilities (centers and mobile) that were not supported by other response plans prior to the launch of the ERP. Consequently, the project will respond to the additional demands for healthcare, in particular the ones expressed by the vulnerable communities including migrants and Lebanese, as per the PHC package defined by the health sector / MOPH. It will also contribute to the COVID-19 plan through homecare mobile unit support. The approach towards targeting will be balanced and contribute to the vision of ensuring universal access to health for all the populations affected by crises in Lebanon, with a clear focus on Lebanese and migrants, as per the ERP rationale.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

(Objective 2) The project will support health facilities to maintain operations during the ongoing emergency context and will provide the PHC package to vulnerable individuals including migrants and Lebanese: specialized and general consultations, lab tests, drugs, awareness sessions, other relevant exams as per the package. (Objective 4) The project will include 3 roving teams who will provide homecare support to vulnerable individuals. Home care may be considered for adults or children with confirmed or suspected COVID-19 when inpatient care is unavailable or unsafe for example when capacity is insufficient to meet the demand for health-care services. Such patients who have been discharged from hospital may also be cared for at home, if necessary.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

PSEA: Amel will implement its PSEA policy (consistent with the PSEA network Lebanon guidance) in order to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse. The intervention will be gender balanced and mainstream gender in its design / implementation / follow up. Social inclusion will be ensured through the universal and non discriminatory approach adopted by Amel.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Amel, as an HAP partner, will be rolling out its AAP processes, within the framework of this project: hotline, complaint boxes, focus group discussions / centers' committees, post evaluation, etc.
