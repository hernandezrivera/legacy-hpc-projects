Project Summary

The project aims to provide immediate support to communities in Grenada most impacted by Hurricane Beryl. This will be achieved through a two-pronged approach: Cash assistance which will provide relief to individuals and families in order to meet their basic needs and recover from the immediate impact of the crisis. Technical Assistance by collaborating with Government agencies and other actors to design and implement effective cash assistance programme under the CCRIF that address the specific needs of impacted communities, ensuring sustainable recovery and resilience in the long run. Main Objectives: 1. Provide cash assistance to vulnerable populations, ensuring access to food, and other essential goods and services. 2. Enhance the Government of Grenada's ability to assist with the delivery of the assistance in the timely and efficient manner. 3. Provide logistics services in partnership with national and regional entities in order to meet the needs of affected people
