Project Summary

Kumbo located about 110kms away from Bamenda, is the second largest town in the northwest west region after Bamenda town and has a population of about 283,079 inhabitants
Agriculture plays a vital role in the lives of the inhabitants here. Due to the hard working nature of the people the town can boast of the production of Arabica coffee, beans and irish potatoes etc. These crops are exported to Douala, Gabon, the Central African Republic and the Equatorial Guinea.
Kumbo as a town in the Anglophone region of Cameroon have since late 2016 witnessed a sociopolitical crisis which has worsen overtime and led to violent clashes between armed forces, loss of lives and property, internal displacement and a growing climate of fear and incertitude. Most people left in kumbo have lost their source of livelihood.
The Reconciliation Development Association (RADA) whose mission is to support community development by building bridges to sustainable solutions, carried out survey in this crises affected town in June 2021 to access the needs of the inhabitants in Kumbo in the main quarters of the town as far as Household Food Security is concerned.
The data was administered in an unbiased manner. We had total number of 151 respondents of which 109 were women and 42 men. 55.6 % of these respondents are family heads. Majority (50.2 %) of the respondents fell within the ages of 30 and 35 years. 27.6 percent of the household had six number of persons per household and mostly 1 person (48.0% of the respondents) in each household who have a monthly salary. 81 % of the respondents had 5 years of schooling (primary), meaning a greater percentage were not much educated. Most of the respondents were owners of their houses (56.6%), 113 respondents said they have not receiving support in terms of cash from anyone out of their households. This is to show that the respondents are decision makers in their homes and because they have houses of their own, they can always create space to do other things.
For food consumption, majority of the respondents eats rice, and other carbohydrates and only few of them consume meat, fish and chicken. Reason being that they do not have sufficient income to afford the animal protein available. Also, due to the crisis, many persons have lost their businesses and have nothing to do. Chicken rearing from our needs assessment to supplement their carbohydrate consumption is the idea activity for most of our respondents. From the data carried out, it shows that in the past 3 months, 144 respondents a percentage of 94.7% indicated that they have not eaten chicken.
Objectives
1. To empower 50 households with 15 chicks each.
2. To educated 50 persons on chicken rearing skills.
3. To complement the consumption of carbohydrates with proteins (chicken).
4. To improve the standard of living in the community of Kumbo.
Activities
1. Contact beneficiaries and trainers to prepare their minds.
2. Send out bids for suppliers of chicks, feed and selection.
3. Construction of mini rearing cages in homes
4. Training of beneficiaries.
5. Distribution of chicks, feed and other materials.
6. Follow up of beneficiaries on their rearing.
7. Consumption and or commercialization of chicks.
8. Report writing
Timeline

1 - Besoins / Needs

Due to the crisis in the Northwest and Southwest of Cameroon, so many people have been internally displaced and properties and businesses destroyed. So many business owners are now sitting at home because their source of livelihood had been destroyed. Through the questionnaire administered in the subdivision of Kumbo as far as food security is concerned, the needs of the population are numerous with poor nutrition being a key component. RADA seeks to come in as a community actor to mitigate this by providing some selected 50 households who have met some set criteria for selection to assist them with 15 chicks per household to rear for consumption and commercial purposes. The consumption purpose is to help in food supplementation as the population will have some proteins complementing their gross consumption of carbohydrates. This is the main purpose of this project. The commercialization purpose is just a minor one where the beneficiaries can sell some of their chickens after maturity to be able to have some money in order to buy oil and other food classes sustainably. We shall also engage well-trained professionals to train these beneficiaries who have little or no idea about chicken rearing for these purposes. They will also provide vaccines for the chickens on a timely basis. Activities for this project will include;1. Contacting of beneficiaries and trainers to prepare their minds.2. Send out bids for suppliers of chicks, feed and selection and contracting. 3. Construction of mini rearing cages in homes. 4. Training of beneficiaries.5. Distribution of chicks, feed and other materials.6. Follow up of beneficiaries on their rearing.7. Consumption and or commercialization of chicks.8. Report writing and follow-ups. The sustainability of this project is on the fact the beneficiaries will still have their well-constructed rearing houses and the rearing equipment still left so after some sales of the mature chickens, they can buy some chicks on their own and start rearing. Their knowledge obtained through the intensive training will still remain with them and they can apply it to the continuous rearing after the project. Last but not the least, since most households indulge in farming activities, the dunk from these chickens will be used as organic manure to grow their farms and gardens.
