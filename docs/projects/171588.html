Project Summary

FSNAU and partners survey conducted between September 2020 indicate the persistence of widespread acute malnutrition in Somalia due to triple effect of COVID-19,locust infestation and flooding with combination of other factors, including high morbidity, low immunization and vitamin-A supplementation, and poor care practices. At the national level, the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) has remained Serious (10–14.9%)for the past four consecutive seasons (11.8 in Gu 2020, 13.1% in 2019/20 Deyr, 13.8% in 2019 Gu and 12.6 % in 2018/19 Deyr). High levels of acute malnutrition continue to persist driven by several factors, including high morbidity, below sphere immunization and Vitamin-A supplementation, poor childcare practices, inadequate access to clean/safe drinking water and acute food insecurity. Morbidity among children was high (≥20%) in 15 out 33 population groups surveyed. However, Crude Death Rate and Under-Five Death Rate were relatively low in most areas. Urgent treatment and nutrition support is required for an estimated 1.4M children under the age of five years (total acute malnutrition burden), who will likely face acute malnutrition through June 2021, including 190,366 children who are likely to be severely malnourished. Integrated interventions should be provided to support recovery and prevent deterioration in the nutrition situation. The proposed activities will improve equitable access to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services through systemic identification, referral, and treatment of acute malnourished boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women among vulnerable & marginalized community and families with malnourished children. It will also provide holistic life-saving and life sustaining nutrition intervention, SRC has been an active nutrition cluster where the organization have been implementing nutrition intervention in Awdhegle district in lower Shabele through the support from UNICEF Somalia, therefore SRC intends to continue its nutrition intervention in Awdhegle district in lower Shabelle region through outpatient therapeutic program sites hence provide active community cascading, screening, admission and treatment of severe acute malnutrition to boys and girl. SRC shall enhance referral services to the effective and most complicated cases to the nearest stabilization center to enable provide adequate attention to the severely malnourished boys, girls among the internally displaced person, With the organization has strong experience working with most vulnerable community and marginalized community, SRC shall also provide routine immunization, deworming and vitamin A supplementation services to most vulnerable community. SRC has strong coordination with organizations operational in Lower Shabelle region and will continually coordinate for better integrated services to vulnerable community looped in poverty and difficult.
SRC with therefore, ensure closer coordination with nutrition national and regional cluster as well as south west Ministry of health, SRC shall also ensure greater coordination with community while it endeavors inclusion of disable and gender appropriate approach in the project implementation
