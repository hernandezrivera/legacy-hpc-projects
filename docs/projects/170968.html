Project Summary

1: To provide immediate household access to food and increase purchasing power to the most vulnerable populations in emergency throughout the year and to people in crisis during the livelihood lean season by providing monthly food vouchers and unconditional cash relief/transfers
2: To restore community productive assets through provision of livelihoods inputs and develop alternative livelihood strategies for vulnerable population, realized via provision of farm inputs, vocational training, temporary job creation and daily stipends for maintainers of productive assets.
3: To Improve food access to school going children in vulnerable communities by providing them with daily meals to prevent further food insecurity and malnutrition

Project Activities

Output 1.1: Facilitated humanitarian food access to 1800hh ( 10800 beneﬁciaries) (806 men headed households and 994 women headed households), for 3 months for communities in Hudur Bakol region. Activity 1.1.1: Provision of food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil and pulses) to 1300 hh (7800 beneﬁciaries) in emergency (586 men headed households and 714 women headed households) each household receiving a voucher worth 71 USD per month which total amount of food provision is 60 mt of Maize, 8 mt sugar,6 mt Oil and 9 mt Pulses. Activity 1.1.2: Provision of food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil and pulses) to 500 hh(3000 beneﬁciaries) in IDP camps (220 men headed households and 280 women headed households) each household receiving a voucher worth 71USD per month. Total amount of food provision is 20 mt of Maize, 3 mt sugar,2 mt Oil and 3 mt Pulses Output 1.2: Increased minimum household basket for 800hh(4800 beneﬁciaries) (352 men headed households and 448 women households) who are extremely vulnerable through cash relief worth 150 USD for 2 Months per year. Activity 1.2.1: Provision of cash relief worth 150 USD per month for 2 months to 800 hh (352 men headed households and 448 women headed households) in communities in crisis focusing more on the most vulnerable individuals both men and women Output 2.1: Enhanced food production and assets for 3370 households(20220 beneﬁciaries) for 1 year (1443 men headed households and 1928 women headed households) through provision of farm inputs (seeds, farm tools and land preparation) for one season per year Activity 2.1.1: Provision of seeds (maize seeds, sorghum seeds, cow peas, mung beans, onion ,tomatoes, lettuce) farm tools and land preparation to 3150 hh( 18900 beneﬁciaries) (1390 men headed households and 1760 women headed households);
