Project Summary

This emergency WASH response is to support conflict affected and natural disaster affected populations in 4 counties 3 of which are part of the 15 counties of the South Sudan WASH Severity Classification 4 (WSC 4) for 2022. The project aims to provide support for basic WASH services to households, healthcare centres and nutrition therapeutic centres, including COVID 19 prevention and mitigation support. This is a 9-month project that will have a strong component to support and mainstream GBV, AAP and disability inclusion into the project. The project is focused on supporting communities that have been affected by floods, intercommunal violence including displaced and host communities whose access to safe water has been constrained resulting in the high prevalence for poor WASH related diseases and high rates of malnutrition which is compounded by poor WASH services.
The project will mostly focus on the improvement of access to safe water through (1) rehabilitation of existing hand pumped boreholes (2) surface water treatment (3) household water treatment and provision of water handling and storage containers (4) construction of flood proof institutional latrine construction (5) hygiene promotion
The project will work to achieve its objectives through WASH Cluster Sectoral objective 1, to achieve the HRP Strategic Objective 1. The project will reach a total of 355,969 people with a budget of 640,000 USDs. NCA will be directly responsible for the implement this project working in partnership with two national partners this approach will ensure the localisation of aid as well as capacity building of local entities.
NCA being an international ecumenical actor has commitments under the grand bargain to support local partners to deliver humanitarian assistance to a high quality and degree and this project will also ensure resilience of local communities and CBOs in WASH service provision by having components that will promote resilience through training water committees, supporting local government actors and line ministries in WASH service provision. NCA has a static presence in Budi, Kapoeta South, Gogrial East, Tonj North and Gumuruk in Pibor. NCA is also implementing activities in Kapoeta East, Tonj East and Aweil South counties.

1. Needs

Assessments carried out in Tonj East and Kapoeta East in February 2023 indicate a high need for Emergency WASH support services in all these counties, all of which have intersecting multisectoral needs due to a combination of factors. As a result, these counties are also among the 15 counties on the high priority 2023 HRP.In Tonj East, for instance, assessment findings carried out by NCA in September 2023 show that many villages have a large number of internally displaced people following recent intercommunal conflicts, which also consequently places WASH services under severe strain.In Kapoeta East, following a poor harvest due to massive flooding in 2022, many households have been displaced to neighboring areas, such as Kapoeta North County, Boma, and Ethiopia. In addition, the low number of operational water points coupled with low capacity for maintaining the existing facilities means overcrowding, which further exacerbates the challenges of maintaining access to water, basic sanitation and hygiene needs, which are severely constrained. As a result, children are particularly affected, with an increased number of children suffering from diarrheal diseases increasing their vulnerability to the compounding effect of under-nutrition.Lack of access to safe water is a contributor to under 5 mortality and morbidity, which needs to be urgently addressed in Pibor and Grater Kubal area. Flooding and intercommunal violence in mid-2021 caused a large displacement of populations towards Pibor Town. This, coupled with the worst flooding in 30 years in the area, has also disrupted farming and destroyed crops. The fighting between communities from Bor, Upper Nile and Pibor area also resulted in WASH infrastructure to be targeted and destroyed. Over 75% of the functioning water facilities in the area were destroyed during the invasion of greater Kubal. The only functioning water facility in Gumuruk that was developed and run by NCA was vandalized and the communities in the area now have no access to the only source of water that previously served over 20,000 people in the area.

2. Project activities

NCA and partners will provide timely and equitable and access to safe water through repair and rehabilitation of existing ones and installation of water treatment unit in areas where IDPs are settled in order to increase the number of water points and thus improve ease of access to safe water. We will also ensure access to clean water and sanitation facilities to PLW and children with SAM and GAM cases in the nutrition centers through connecting them to the water supply facilities and constructing sanitation facilities and handwashing facilities to facilitate nutrition programming and reduce the risk of Hospital Acquired Infections (HAIs) transmission.We will also create communities to demand improved sanitation practice through contextually tailored hygiene promotion activities and messaging. The Hyegine promotion activity also will target to raise the community awareness on safe water handling at household level coupled with provision of household water treatment kits and Hygiene Kits, specially targeted households with SAM children and PLW will get the treatment chemicals for three months in order to reduce their vulnerability to diseases from contaminated water intake. NCA and partners will use CASH and Voucher approach to distribute NFIs where the market are functional while mitigating all the risks associated with the use of CASH with that specific context of the intervention area. NCA will provide the best modality of the CASH component in order to ensure inclusion of the service to all segment of the community in need.Our WASH programmes have gender and AAP mainstreaming This will be achieved by carrying out a variety of activities not limited to the following; - • Rehabilitation of existing hand pumped boreholes • Surface water treatment Unit near to IDP sites• Distribution of household water treatment products and provision of water handling and storage containers.• Construction of flood proof institutional latrines and washrooms/bathing shelters at nutrition centers• Hygiene promotion focusing on safe food and water handling, including risk communication, community engagement and infodemic management on Covid-19. • Distribution of hygiene kits. • Consultation with stakeholders on WASH needs.• Setting up Complaints response systems for community feedback.• Training of hygiene promoters• Identify hygiene promotion volunteers.• Monitor sanitation improvement. • Identify people with special needs and provide targeted support. • Carrying out Knowledge awareness and practices Surveys • Water point inventories to identify water points needing repairs. • Carrying out water committee’s formation • Designing and Printing Vouchers• Identification of vulnerable populations and target groups • Carrying out monitoring of progress of implementation• Participating in Coordination mechanisms• Carrying out assessments of needs• Providing information and getting feedback from right holders and attending to complaints• Carrying out Safety Audit
