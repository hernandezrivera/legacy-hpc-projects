Project Summary

The project is establishing two CP CFS centers in Al-Atareb city and in Azaz city and outreach team for each centre to conduct outdoor child protection awareness activities.
The project will aim to response to protection needs related to child protection in the communities that suffer from the lack of the prevention and responsive services, and will address the lack of access to sustained protection interventions in addition to the lack of access to community where service is available by establishing two centres in the communities most in needI

Needs

Depending on HNO 2019. Children throughout Syria continue to face pervasive violence and a range of protection risks. Grave child rights violations remain a significant concern, including in areas where hostilities have declined, with children at risk of killing and injury, recruitment and use in hostilities, torture, detention, abduction, sexual violence, attacks on schools and hospitals and denial of humanitarian access by parties to the conflict. During the first 9 months of 2018, the UN verified 2,354 such violations against children, in 13 of 14 Governorate of Syria , illustrating persistent trends of violence though not the full scope of conflict-related violence affecting children . This included more than 1,300 verified incident of killing or injuring of children . Respondents in assessed communities reporting explosive hazard contamination in Syria identify adolescent boys and pre-adolescent boys as the groups most at risk of death or injury from accidents, with incidents reported while children were playing and going to school .TThe ongoing or recurrent conflict environment experienced by many boys and girls over the past seven years has profoundly affected their psychosocial well-being – weakening their sense of security, purpose, control and significance, or exercising social agency, and causing profound distress. Many are without civil documentation to prove their identity, access basic services and safeguard their rights. It is estimated that more than 2.6 million children remain displaced and separation from caregivers is a reported child protection issue in all 14 governorates of Syria putting children at risk of exploitation, abuse or neglect . Events leading to family separation indicate how related protection and child protection issues increase vulnerability of affected boys and girls, with death or disappearance of caregivers, disappearance of children, separation due to displacement or conflict, child marriage and child recruitment among cited reasons for family separations . Including access barriers for those with injuries/disabilities and communication mechanisms for persons with visual/hearing impairments.- Liaise with "neutral" parties as much as possible and triangulate all information from all sources prior to decision making.- by utilising sector agreed key messages the the project will ensure consistency and inform sector of discrepancies or if new messaging is required.-the project will Ensure safety audits for access are conducted for each fixed site or mobile service location. -Considering a conflict patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing fixed site or mobile service provision.- Ensure safety audit is conducted to ensure safe access (route and location) by boys, girls, women and men, elderly and populations with decreased mobility (due to physical, visual/hearing deficits, psychological issues).- Engage with local authorities on safety and security in the community.- Engage with diverse members of community on safety and security in the community (to ensure diverse perspective and perceptions are considered).- Ensure consultation and mobilisation of key persons in the community.
