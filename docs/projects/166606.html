Project Summary

Using a health systems strengthening approach and through direct support to Risk Communication and Community Engagment, Infection Prevention and Control and Case Management, support the MOHCC to minimize morbidity and mortality resulting from COVID-19 and secondary effects of impeded access to health services.

Plan fields

1 - Activities

Risk Communication and Community Engagement• Support the Government and partners to develop and broadcast messaging and information on COVID-19 through radio and TV programmes, press statements, social media, digital platforms • Production and printing of general public information materials • Development of official government WhatsApp information hub (Bot)• Accelerate and expand Village Health Worker (VHW) and other community cadres trainings to integrate COVID-19 prevention and preparedness, as well as community surveillance• Develop IEC kit for VHW and other population groups • Training on COVID-19 of HIV community workers, support groups and other lay HIV workers (to be integrated and accelerated/expanded with existing plans on Cholera, Typhoid) for integrated community HIV awareness campaign, reduce stigma and follow up of children, adolescents and pregnant/BF mothers living with HIV on ARV medications Infection Prevention and Control• IPC training in health facilities nationwide as well as at key points of entry and in districts where isolation facilities are situated• Support scaling of integrated disease surveillance and response training • Procurement of Personal protection equipment Case Management• Procurement of essential equipment and commodities required for treatment of severe COVID-19 cases (oxygen equipment)• Support to front line health worker hazard pay/top-up• Case management training on COVID-19 at lower levels of the health system • Expand Emergency Triage and Treatment trainings to include COVID-19 for provincial hospitals • Ensure continuity of routine services to avert morbidly and mortality from other illnesses.
