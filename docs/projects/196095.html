Project Summary

HARECOD through this project aims to ensure there is access to emergency integrated lifesaving mobile healthcare services to under-served Flood and conflict affected IDPs, returnees and host populations in the hard-to-reach payams in Cueibet county, of Lakes state.
We aim to achieve Health cluster strategic objective 1, and specific objective 1.3 under sectoral objective 2 as well as specific objective 1.4 under sectoral objective 1.
Out of the county’s population In Need estimated at 94,461 people, 75,569 are considered target population for provision of essential health services. HARECOD will target 20% of the people equivalent to 15,271 people in need disaggregated as follows: 3,716 children less than five years (1,917 males and 1,799 females), 6,334 children aged more than 5 years but less than 18 years (3,177 males and 3,157 females), 4,746 adults aged 18-60 years (2,320 males and 2,426 females) and 475 elderly people (60 years and above) including 235 males plus 240 females.
The project is also expected to reach at least 2,225 people with disabilities representing 15 percent of the project target population.
The intervention aims to establish and support 3 community-based mobile clinics at Cueibet center,Pagor, and Achol Malek payams where response activities shall be coordinated in close collaboration with county Health department, CUAAM, and Women Aid Vision(WAV) including Medair.
HARECOD shall focus on provision of integrated essential life-saving primary healthcare services based on the basic package of health and nutrition services (BPHNS), the Boma Health Initiative Model, epidemiological profile of disease outbreaks, seasonality and trauma related emergency requirements. Prevention, preparedness, and responsive activities to the people in need paying considerable attention to the needs of vulnerable groups (women, children and persons Living with disabilities) shall be greatly prioritized.
Through the 3 supported facilities, HARECOD will provide basic healthcare services including: OPD curative consultations, antenatal care (ANC) and postnatal care (PNC), conducting clean, safe and hygienic deliveries by skilled birth attendants, Routine mass vaccination (EPI services) of U5 children, Screening, treatment of MAM and referral of SAM cases, conduct surveillance on epidemic prone diseases (integrated disease surveillance & response/early warning alert response system- IDSR/EWARS), health education and hygiene promotion, preparedness, response and control of epidemic prone disease outbreaks focusing on, malaria, pneumonia, cholera, measles and COVID-19, management & referral of SGBV cases; clinical management of rape cases (CMR) and basic psycho-social support (counselling), Management of war wounded (injuries), provision of basic & minimal initial HIV/AIDS/TB services, RDT Screening services for most endemic illness on children and pregnant women, distribution of LLITNs and micro nutrient supplementation and deworming tablets, growth monitoring and nutrition assessment to under 5 children and PLW.
The proposed target population is 15,271 direct beneficiaries at a cost of 300,000 US dollars.

1 - Needs

Lakes state particularly Cueibet county has one of the lowest Health indicators according to District Health Information Survey 2(DHIS2 2021): such as ANC4 30% (ANC1 85%), curative consultation merely reaching 1. Preventable diseases such as Malaria, Acute watery Diarrhea, and preventive respiratory infections such as Pneumonia are the main Under five diseases exacerbated by very low provision of critical water, hygiene, sanitation services coupled with practice of open defecation, lack of hand washing soap, drinking water treatment tablets, and low knowledge on water treatment methods. These conditions have led to common disease outbreaks if no countywide improvement of both prevention and surveillance sectors are not strengthened. This situation is expected to worsen as the only local HRP partner on the ground gets overwhelmed with severe needs.Over 93,193 people have been severely affected by Flooding alone in Lakes state according to Latest South Sudan: Flooding Situation Report No. 1 As of 31 October 2022(https://reliefweb.int) out of which 75,569 are targeted by the Health cluster partners for provision of emergency health services out of 373,788 targeted vulnerable persons in Lakes state. Cueibet county has the highest number of people targeted for health services representing 20 percent of the total target population in Lakes state.HARECOD and some partners on the ground conducted a recent Initial Rapids Needs Assessment(IRNA) in Cueibet county on 31 August 2022(https://www.humanitarianresponse.info) and found that 11,498 households have been severely destroyed by Floods representing 68,988 vulnerable persons(6 persons per Household, Cluster standard). Payams of Cueibet center, Pagor, and Achol Malek have the highest number of destroyed households representing 33,984 persons.The recent IRNA assessment found prevalence of common illnesses like Malaria, diarrhoea, acute respiratory infections, and the assessment team recommended emergency set up of Mobile health clinics, provision of treated long lasting mosquito nets, and intensifying health education.Provision of life-saving services remains critical, including immunization, emergency Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH) and gender-based violence (GBV) health services through outreach and accessible health facilities according to the latest Flood Situation Report No. 2 as of 31st October 2022(https://reliefweb.int).There is only one HRP partner local organization(Women Aid Vision, WAV) providing emergency health services(Health Cluster south Sudan HRP Partners Map 2022) to less than 20,000 vulnerable persons in Cueibet county out of the Health cluster target of 75,569 persons(Analysis of People In Need(PIN), and Targets for Health cluster, 14 November 2022) leaving a gap of over 50,000 vulnerable persons to be reached with essential health services.

2 - Project activities

Under Strategic Objective 1, HARECOD will provide Health education sessions during the mobile health clinics to ensure that the communities get to know the importance of health seeking behaviors and COVID 19 Prevention messages. • Outpatient consultations in the mobile health clinics • Vaccination services will be offered to children to ensure that there is a control in the vaccine preventable infections. • ANC services will be given to the pregnant mothers • Vitamin A supplementation to the children under five years • Folic Acid supplementation for the pregnant mothers • Skilled Birth attendant Deliveries to be conducted in the mobile health units and in the community and referral of complicated cases to the health facilities. Disease surveillance through EWARS and IDSR reporting and 5W monthly reports • Training of health workers on IDSR, CMR, IPC and other capacity building sessions • Coordination with other partners on ground and the County Health Department as well as the health cluster. • COVID 19 awareness on prevention measures in the community and in the health facilities • EPI services in the mobile units and at the static facilitiesImplement WASH in health facilities as HARECOD is a WASH HRP partner including building the capacities of health workers.Conduct rapid needs assessment and AAP to get the view on communities on healthcare providers in all the targeted payams.• Conduct monitoring visits, and support supervision to ensure quality provision in health facilities. •Conduct health education for all patients and households living near a health facility •Implement integrated community case management of diseases including boma health initiatives.
