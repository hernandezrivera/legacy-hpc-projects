Project Summary

The project aims to Increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for those most vulnerable and in need in Idelb, Governorates, dleb / Ariha / Ma'arrat An Nu'man

a. tow primary health care center
b. One primary Health care unit
c. One mobile clinic
2- Provision of medical supplies (these supplies are intended to support the delivery of essential PHC services, RH service, essential NCD services, CD, mental health and child health.
Mercy-USA is seeking to ensure the continued operation of these health facilities through sustained staffing, provision of medical supplies, data management, and reporting, finance, governance, training, monitoring, and oversight of programme quality, and infrastructure maintenance support.
These facilities will provide obstetric and gynecological services, treatment for diarrhea, respiratory tract infections and skin conditions for boys and girls under five, treatment for chronic conditions including hypertension, asthma, cardiac ailments and psychosocial
support (PSS). The project, therefore, aims to continue to increase access to appropriate and impactful health services to address these urgent needs.

Needs

- The number of people who are in need of health assistance in Syria is estimated to be 13.8 million. Females make up 72 percent of the PiN. Vulnerable groups include 5.2 million women of reproductive age, 2.2 million children under 5 years old, and 2.3 million people aged over 50 who are vulnerable to no communicable diseases. Syria is home to an estimated 3 to 4 million disabled people and 7 million IDPs.(OCHA)- Needs assessment for essential primary health care services in rural Idleb was conducted by Mercy-USA in coordination with local health authorities. The assessment indicated the gaps and the shortages of essential primary health care services in Idleb / Ariha - Ma'arrat An Nu'man ( severity score is 3 for these locations according to health cluster )- The project aims to support (44,164 / 21,032 Female -23,132 Male) out of them (20,440 IDPs10,752 Male 9,688 Female) - 3533 Pregnant and lactating women -7,508 Boys and Girls need immunization and special care -3,065 disabled populations,1,596 Male/1,469 Female. Provision of essential primary health care services was identified as a priority Through closed consultation with the affected community. WHO estimates that approximately 12 percent of the population have diabetes and 20 percent hypertension. Untreated non-communicable diseases lead to serious complications such as renal failure and disability due to strokeand amputations ( http://www.who.int/nmh/countries/syr_en.pdf?ua=1 ; data for 2014/2015) - Mental health needs are extensive, with WHO estimating that at least 15 percent of the adult population is in need of treatment for moderate to severe mental health needs. A survey conducted in June 2018 highlighted that on average 14 percent ofthe population in Idleb, Aleppo and Ar-Raqqa governorates report a high level of anxiety or depression on a daily basis, with 73 percent of these labeling the crisis as the cause (Physical Rehabilitation & Disability Working Group ‘Disability and Access to Healthcare:Western Aleppo, Idleb & Ar-Raqqa’ 2018) The WoS Health sector assessment indicated that 28 percent of health workers reported the non-availability of organized family planning services in their community. Free contraceptives were reportedly available in 192 out of 271 surveyed sub-districts. Both health providers and community representatives highlighted the limited availability of clinical management of rape (CMR) as a services gap across Syria. (MSNA 2018 Health Sector Questionnaire)

Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA will be responsible for implementing the program in coordination with the health cluster and stakeholders. Mercy-USA will apply the Essential health package to effectively and efficiently deliver comprehensive quality health services that are equitable, accessible and sustainable. In addition, and ensuring the timely delivery of cost-effective, integrated and standardized health services tailored to meet the priority health issues faced by the majority of the population. The quality and accountability will be monitoring by Mercy-USA to ensure meet the standards furthermore Mercy-USA will be responsible for all program and relevant financial report to the donor and to the cluster ( 4Ws ) and other related reports. All health staff will be trained on different health topics.The program will be monitored and managed by the program coordinator who will work directly from the Turkey office with the field’s staff to ensure the program is being correctly implemented.The selected health facilities will start when the fund is received. The health facilities will be divided between the health officers, each medical officer will supervise two health facilities providing administration and technical supervision. Mercy-USA supply chain department in coordination with our team in Syria and turkey will ensure all needed medical equipment and operational supplies are delivered on time. the Information Management officer will work with data entries and our health team to produce monthly information on the progress of the program which includes caseload information desegregated by cases/age, sex, gender, and disability. We provide services regardless of race, color, religion, creed, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, ancestry, age, and disability

Monitoring: Incidence Report Resources Required: security officer. Protection /Risk/Threat:Gender-Based Violence GBV Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: - MinimumInitial Service Package (MISP) as part of the essential health package. - Trained specialized health staff on providing first-line support, clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals. - Safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBV cases connecting health services and clinical management with social services, mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), in a manner guarantees safety, confidentiality, and privacy of the survivor. - Recruit female staff in health facilities will increase access of women and girls to health services. Monitoring: Case Management / Reports Resources Required: Train the specialized health staff Protection /Risk/Threat: Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA:Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: -Mercy-USA maintains a ZERO TOLERANCE policy towards SEA in accordance with internationally-established protocols and standards for SEA protection. Mercy-USA will directly contact GBV and PSEA networks for referral of any case can be met - Effective recruitment and performance management of all humanitarian staff. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: - Cross border access from Turkey is available. - Easy and smoothLogistical access. - Cooperation from local councils - Facilitation and acceptance from major regional stakeholders/actors (Turkish Government, OAG groups, etc.) - Timely fund dispersal - Targeting of most vulnerable beneficiaries - Recruitment of female staff within distribution teams will be considered. Risks: - General daily security threats includingrobbery and looting - Macro-level conflict-related threats including aerial bombardment,advancement of hostile actors, shifting alliances, border closure, and chemical attack. - Danger to staff and beneficiaries including the conflict between beneficiaries. - Delays in fund dispersal -Fire hazard and/or conflict-related activities happen during the ongoing implementation. Risk Mitigation: - Following Mercy-USA safety and security guidelines and established safety protocols. - In the event of major macro-level conflict-relatedthreats, Mercy-USA will take appropriate action that is commensurate with the scale of the threat. This may include: - Suspending operations temporarily -Shifting resources and operations to more secure locations - Clear steps will be taken to mitigate risks between staff and beneficiaries including gender based violence. - Project staff may be dismissed at any time in the event of endangering beneficiaries or other staff, and this will be clearly outlined in each employment contract. - Contingency stock will be in place for shipmentsto avoid any impact if Bab Alhawa borders become closed (for items purchased from Turkey) - Gender balance and equal opportunities will be taken into consideration when hiring staff for a different position. -All workers will be given the proper needed instructions ahead of any deliveries. -All necessary precautions will be taken to prevent a fire incident at the health facilities. The health facilities will be equipped with the propertype and number of extinguishers. moreover, unarmed guard(s) will be available in the health facilities at all times, in order to avoid any conflicting activity might happen.
