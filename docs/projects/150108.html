Project Summary

This project aims to provide integrated medical rehabilitation services to 680 patients, injured clients
and persons with physical disabilities it will also offer education to 1,360 members of their families about disabilities .
Services will be provided within 12 months with full coordination of health providers in the governmental and non-governmental sectors.
This project’s services are divided into two parts according to the patient’s needs and place of service:
First is out reach program for 300 patients, elderly and persons with disabilities, whose names will be nominated by Ministry of Health in addition to 600 family members who will receive education, (three home visits per week per patient).
This project will result in a total of 54,00.0 home visits throughout the project duration.
The services included are medical and evaluation visits, study of patient’s needs to adapt their environment, medical examination and follow-up, physical and occupational therapy sessions, psychological and social support, and nursing care.
If a patient needs to stay in the hospital, they will be transferred to El-Wafa Hospital to complete the treatment plan and rehabilitation (expected number of admissions 100 client for a length of stay for average 3 days per patient)
Second, 380 patients and persons with disabilities along with 760 of their families will receive medical and guidance services at the hospital mainly in the out-patient clinic and education hall
The project will give 10 appointments per case per month. This will result to a total number of 4630 hospital visits throughout the project duration.
Health and Awareness services will be provided along with physical and occupational therapy, nursing care during their stay in the hospital.
In addition, they will be given healthy, nutritious meals that are high in protein and calcium to help heal wounds and treat fractures of bones for the wounded.
OBJECTIVES :
Promoting a community based response to basic health and rehabilitation needs of vulnerable people with disabilities and elderly in emergency situations.
Enhancing provision of lifesaving healthcare services for more vulnerable people with disability in the Gaza strip
The project is designed to provide us with:
1 - Medicines and medical supplies needed to heal wounds and assist in the rehabilitation process.
2 - Medical aids and assistive devises
3 - Fuel to operate the generator to operate the needed medical devices.
4 - Emergency medical equipment.
5- Healthy foods high in protein and calcium.
6- Specialized and experienced medical and technical cadres (4 doctors,6 physiotherapists, 8 nurses, 6 occupational therapists, 2 psychologists and social workers, 1 Pharmacist and 2 laboratory tests).

Needs

The 2020 forecast for the Gaza Strip warns that challenges including poverty and health insecurity will only intensify. The Gaza Strip has been under a health blockade since June 2006. Its health services is severely crippled in the longer term under restrictions of movement and access to resources.Before the Great March of Return, there had been 44,200.0 persons with physical disability in Gaza. Since the beginning of the march till 10th of October 2018 the number of PWD increased to become 46,100 in our hospital, the beds are full since the beginning of April 2018. We have 150 PWD in a waiting list fir medical rehabilitation. our limited capacity limited our capability to extend our hands to more PWD. this proposal will enable us to reach 300 clients in their home as we will hire more staff and procure more medical supplies and assistive devices through which we will expand our entire services. We have an experience of 22 years of implementing similar programs in all areas of Gaza strip from Rafah to Beit Hanoun city. We will coordinate through the health providers and MOH not to duplicate any service. we strongly believe that through this proposal we will reduce the pain and get 680 PWD back to hope and prosperity InjuresSince the mass demonstrations began until the 30th September 1200 cases of injured people have been identified as in need of limb reconstruction, and will require extensive rehabilitation and treatment for up to 2 years (According to Medical Aid for Palestinians (MAP-UK 2018)Non-trauma patients are being affected by the ongoing violence, not because they are being directly injured in large numbers, but because their treatment and healthcare for other urgent conditions is being interrupted as the wider health system responds to the casualties from the fence. People with disability(PwD`s)According to the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics and using the conservative description of disability at least 2.7% (2.9% males and 2.5% females) of the Palestinians had one form of disability most common of which is the physical disability (47.20%).People with disability(PwD`s)who live in poor socioeconomic situations in general and buffered marginalized areas (ARA) from different age groups has different health needs.Those people in need of massive medical care at home including assistive devices, drugs ,disposable and non disposable medical supplies and supporting personal skills of care givers through training their family members about proper health practices ,In emergency situations, persons with disabilities and the elderly are in even more need for specialized services as they face extreme vulnerability compared to their other peers however rehabilitation and persons with disabilities themselves are incapacitated with regard to how much they can prepare for and respond to their needs in times of crisis.The project will significantly contribute to gender equality, including across age groups.The above mentioned needs was discussed with focus group of PwD`s ,elderly and injures include males and females during regular work of ElWafa Hospital as feedback revision to urgent and essential needs of PwD`s affect their health status.This project was based on a study of a survey which evaluated the needs of the wounded. It was done in coordination with the Ministry of Health, civil society organizations, a network of organizations representing people with disabilities and a team of experienced specialist from El Wafa hospital. For 22 years, El Wafa hospital implemented dozens of rehabilitation projects, where social diversity, age diversity and geographical distribution were taken into account, particularly those in need in remote and marginalized areas.Interviews and visits were conducted with a group of beneficiaries to identify their needs and meet their requests.

This will foster collaboration and ownership over the rehabilitation goals set. Besides, the project will use a community based approach which will allow for community engagement in the project implementation. Transparent and direct communication with the target communities will be pursued to ensure beneficiaries can provide feedback about quality of service provision. This will be facilitated by quarterly community meetings within referral networks and beneficiaries in the five governorates of the Gaza strip and all the relevant stakeholders, partners and representative of the beneficiaries .. A special focus will be place in supporting women’s involvement to ensure they are more active and “visible”. Feedback mechanisms exist through a Complaints System that was developed by Elwafa during previous projects and beneficiaries can freely access it and voice their concerns verbally or otherwise while ensuring confidentiality.
