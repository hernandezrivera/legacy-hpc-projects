Project Summary

Due to the drought, families and caregivers are more likely to focus on food searching and children are most likely to be neglected especially so for children with disabilities. Disability needs are not going to be taken into consideration. Some children will be left in the care of elderly grandparents as parents try to find food for the family. As children with special needs left in the care of elderly people their vulnerability increases also increasing chances of them facing abuse and or violation of their rights.
The overall goal of the project is to have well protected and safe children with disabilities in participating districts despite the drought and its accompanying risks. Apart from reacting to the anticipated drought the project also aim to build resilience of communities especially with regards to making sure that children with disabilities are not neglected, are safe and when endangered the community is able to identify the danger and refer appropriately. The project will achieve this by training stakeholders of the Victim Friendly Unit (VFUs) and Community Case Care Workers (CCWs) in Participating districts. (70% of VFS stakeholders and 80% of CCWs to effectively mainstream disability issues and be able to identify children with disabilities needing case management services and refer appropriately. The objective is to create a disability and child protection sensitive workforce in the participating districts. This will increase case intake and the project is targeting 3000 children. The project will raise awareness on prevention of abuse, neglect and community linkages put in place for efficient and faster case identification and reporting. The objective is to have safe communities where caregivers and community members work together in ensuring that children with disabilities are safe and in the event of abuse, neglect and or critical need the community is at the forefront in identifying and reporting. The target is 5000 caregivers and community members per each participating district. the Project also will facilitate access to disability specialist services (not limited to: physiotherapy, assistive devices, critical welfare concerns etc.) for critical cases. This targets 40% of the identified children as it targets only the critical cases. The objective being to make sure that all children with disabilities continuously access critical services like rehabilitation and assistive devices despite the vulnerabilities brought to their families and communities by the drought.
By the end of the project a system of active community participation in child protection in general will be in place. Specific for children with disabilities, communities will be able to identify different types of disabilities, appreciate the vulnerabilities that comes with disabilities especially in emergency situations. This is part of the project`s building resilience of the communities such that in the event of other emergencies the communities will have the knowhow of supporting children with disabilities and refer appropriately within the existing government programmes like the Case Management System.

Needs

Due to the drought families and primary caregivers will mainly priorities food for the family. This will likely increase the vulnerability of children and especially so for children with disabilities. Children with disabilities are more likely to be neglected as caregivers focus on food and may be away from home for days. They are also likely to be abandoned in the care of elderly grandparents as parents move around searching for food. Disability specific needs (physiotherapy/assistive devices) are also likely to be ignored/neglected as parents divert the little income they have to acquiring food.

Activities

• Training in disability sensitive humanitarian response to humanitarian staff including VFS stakeholders and community cadres (CCWS). • Identifying children who are at risk and or have been violated and create linkages to the National Case Management System providing support for case follow ups.• Awareness on Prevention of abuse, exploitation, neglect and discrimination of children with disabilities • Facilitate access to disability friendly services including supporting access to physiotherapy, counselling, assistive devices, mobility devices, outreach services etc., for critical cases that may lead to increased vulnerability for children.
