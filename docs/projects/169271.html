1 - Needs

The Beirut Port blast had a significant effect on the education sector damaging approximately 178 public and private schools and leaving over 85,000 children across primary, secondary and 7,300 youth in TVET without a place to learn. The immediate need is to rehabilitate schools with light to moderate damage and provide necessary equipment and supplies including furniture for classroom equipment for TVET students. At least 14,000 students will require tablets and Internet bundles to access distance learning platforms and ensure continuity of learning over the next three months.

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Under Sector Objective 1 (Support rehabilitation of schools)UNICEF will support the rehabilitation of 20 primary schools and 4 TVET compounds including 20 TVET schools that suffered light to moderate damage in Beirut and Mount Lebanon as a result of the Beirut Port explosion. The rehabilitation process will be preceded by a thorough and documented assessment to generate appropriate Bills of Quantity for timely commencement of work at each rehabilitation site. All firms contracted to complete the works will go through recognized and transparent public procurement processes. A quality assurance team of technical consultants will work with the UNICEF and MEHE construction units to ensure all works are completed in a timely manner and meet all MEHE standards of quality and safety. Activity 2: Under Strategic Objective 2 (Increase access to quality formal and non-formal learning opportunities for affected children) UNICEF will procure and distribute furniture and equipment damaged or destroyed across the 4 TVET compounds, including 20 TVET schools, affected by the blast. This will ensure that up to 7,300 TVET students in the affected areas can have ready access to the hands-on training offered by their respective institutions. All equipment provided will MEHE-TVET specifications and will be procured through the UNICEF Supply unit. This will provide adherence to recognized procedures of public procurement and ensure efficiency, value, and timely delivery of all goods. Activity 3: Activity 2: Under Strategic Objective 2 (Increase access to quality formal and non-formal learning opportunities for affected children) UNICEF will provide 7,000 school children with Android tablets (32 GB tablets w/10.1” screen, Sim card ready) and 20 GB/three-month Internet bundles to ensure access to distance learning. These intuitive devices will ensure that with the imminent start of the school year children will not miss out on learning during the rehabilitation of their respective schools. The UNICEF Supply unit will ensure timely procure and delivery with distribution to primary level students based on needs-based criteria agreed in collaboration with MEHE and sector partners.
