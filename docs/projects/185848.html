Project Summary

The aim of the Analysis Hub is to support informed humanitarian and development decision-making through research and analysis, contributing to a shared understanding of the Rohingya crisis and improved accountability to the affected populations. The Analysis Hub will support response actors in designing and accessing thorough analysis of the complex drivers of needs and the inter-sectoral relationships between needs, which would better inform response priorities and take into account the perspectives of the affected population. The Analysis hub will continue its support to different sectors and partners with customized technical support for data collection and analysis based on requests. This includes the ongoing support to the Joint Needs Assessment and J-MSNA frameworks. Specifically, NPM will carry out Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) flights covering all Rohingya refugee settlements. The UAV imagery will be used to produce maps and digital elevation models for operational actors, to identify natural hazard risks and monitor changes in site conditions and development.

1 - Identified Needs

Building on its experience in Cox’s Bazar since 2017, the ACAPS-NPM Analysis hub will conduct and support a range of joint assessments, spatial analyses and joint analysis projects to support informed humanitarian planning, prioritization and delivery of assistance. The Analysis Hub will seek to meet the response's need for common and impartial analysis to support programmatic and operational decision making. The Hub will also build on its established relationship with the Gender Hub and seek to ensure all analysis incorporates a gender lens, producing targeted products to fill current gender analysis gaps.

2 - Activities or outputs

1- ACAPS/NPM Analysis Hub: Support to J-MSNA process, including qualitative research design and secondary data analysis, and technical contributions to the technical working group. 2- ACAPS/NPM Analysis Hub: Produce joint analysis products with other members of the JRP utilising both primary (qualitative and quantitative) and secondary data to elevate the voices of the Rohingya refugees and affected host community.3- ACAPS/NPM Analysis Hub: Support JRP partners to improve their research, analysis, and design capacities and frameworks by providing ad hoc support to local, national, and international NGOs and UN agencies who request methodological and/or analytical support.4- NPM: Support to J-MSNA process, including data collection in host community and technical contributions to the technical working group.5- NPM: Continued support to the ISCG through the Joint Needs Assessment (JNA) framework.6- NPM: Provision of updated imagery of displacement sites through unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) flights and footage.
