Project Summary

Emergency WASH program (E-WASH) is a Latter-Day Saint Charities (LDSC) funded project designed to provide Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) resources to reduce malnutrition and WASH related disease in vulnerable households of Awerial County, Lakes State. . The E-WASH project is aimed at reducing the incidence of malnutrition and WASH-related diseases in the targeted counties by increasing the availability of safe water and sanitation services and promote safe hygiene and sanitation practices. Children under five (CU5) and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) among newly arriving IDPs and protracted IDPs are the main target, as these groups are some of the most at-risk for adverse health outcomes resulting from poor WASH access and practices. The project’s target area is Awerial county in Eastern Lakes State, which has experienced severe flooding that has resulted in mass displacement which has stressed already limited WASH infrastructures in the region. CRS will collaborate with the County’ water department and County Relief and Rehabilitation Commission office, local administrations at Payam and Boma levels, WFP, and other CRS programming in the area to ensure appropriate relevant targeting and safe programming. Interventions will include distribution of soap, distribution and training of Menstrual Hygiene kits, and HH WASH sensitization visits carried out by Community Hygiene Promoters. The project will also construct HH latrines and school latrines, as well as engage in borehole rehabilitation.

1 - Needs

The project aims to address the lack of access to WASH services among PLWs and Children Under 5, with a particular focus on PLW and Children under 5 among the IDP community.

2 - Project activities

1. WASH Sensitization home visits - visiting vulnerable HHs and explaining healthy and appropriate WASH practices2. Distribution of Soap to Vulnerable Households.3. Distribution of Menstrual Hygiene Kits - Distributing Menstrual Hygiene Kits to vulnerable HHs and training them on their use4. HH Latrine Construction - Construction of HH Latrines using plastic slabs for vulnerable HHs5. School latrine Construction - Construction of School latrines to be used by students and wider community where there is an identified need.6. Borehole Rehabilitation - Rehabilitating boreholes used by vulnerable communities that have fallen into disrepair.7. Formation and Training of Water Use Committees for each rehabilitated borehole

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

The project will already target individuals assessed as the most vulnerable (PLWs, people that meet other vulnerability criteria, and the feedback mechanism will include multiple ways to register complaints to accommodate persons with disabilities or illiterate individuals.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

The project will inform beneficiaries about the purpose of the project and what they can expect to receive in regards to NFIs and services. The project will also inform beneficiaries on how to access the project's feedback and response mechanisms

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

The project will use feedback from the community to modify the project on an ongoing basis to ensure we are targeting the most vulnerable individuals with our programming.
