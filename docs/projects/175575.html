Project Summary

UN-Habitat look at this response as a opportunity to support the physical, social, environmental and economic conditions to create a more resilient community. The purpose is to increase the number of safe learning enviroments to hoste the arrival of IDPs safeguarding the access of education of host community and support the communities and govement to maintain this balance in sustainable and participative manner.
Accordingly, UN-Habitat will ensure that the quality of infrastructure be better and all concerned people including, IDPs, school related staff, students, children with disabilities and communities to be more equipped with the skills/knowledge regarding disaster risk reduction and prevention which is specific to their own context

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UN-Habitat look at this response as a opportunity to support the physical, social, environmental and economic conditions to create a more resilient community. The purpose is to increase the number of safe learning enviroments to hoste the arrival of IDPs safeguarding the access of education of host community and support the communities and govement to maintain this balance in sustainable and participative manner. Accordingly, UN-Habitat will ensure that the quality of infrastructure be better and all concerned people including, IDPs, school related staff, students, children with disabilities and communities to be more equipped with the skills/knowledge regarding disaster risk reduction and prevention which is specific to their own context

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

- Acknowledging that the country experiences high rates of girls’ dropout from school (there are 1.2 million children out of school in Mozambique and 55 per cent are girls) which is motivated by existing harmful cultural practices in the country (child marriage, initiation rites, early pregnancy), the programme will use “circulos de interresse” a platform created for extracurricular activities in schools to target girls and empower them with knowledge and life skills. Such activities will also count with the participation of boys, with the aim of creating awareness about issues of gender, the impact of gender discrimination on girls’ education and the importance for respecting the rights of girls.- Project encourages the leadership and promotes the community ownership, influencing the process through a large-scale form of participation in the implementation that will infuse better construction practices in the communities – abilities that can be transferred to shelter construction and others, that will help to guarantee that the strategies are accepted and appropriate to the communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

- UN-Habitat promotes a community-based approach, with a view to having a commitment and ownership by local communities. Therefore, IDPs and local communities (since they are the primary beneficiaries) will be involved throughout the implementation process, from assessing and surveying the current situation, implementing activities and monitoring the process. This approach include: - (i) Empowerment of IDp’s and host communities in the process; (ii) Utilization of local knowledge about natural existing threats in their zone and how these threats can damage the school structures and houses. Each school and community will have direct access to UN-Habitat technicians by phone to guarantee claim mechanism

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

- Trainings on Basic school emergency plan take into consideration the needs of all, especially children with disabilities and girls. To make schools more inclusive, the project will look into what are the barriers to gender and disability inclusion (Social-Cultural and Physical) in the selected places through the collection of gender-sensitive disability data.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

UN-Habitat mandate is to promote environmentally sustainable, socially equitable and inclusive human settlements that can provide adequate housing for all. UN-Habitat subprograms are designed to support communities, local governments and decision makers in developing policies to find pragmatic and long term solutions to build more resilient and secure human settlements.All the related questions with the environmental safeguards will be taken in consideration during all the process of planning and execution of the activities. Therefore, mitigation and management of potential environmental and social impacts resulting from the DRR training activities and installation of tarpa tens will be included within the project.
