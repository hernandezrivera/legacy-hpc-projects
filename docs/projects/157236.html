Project Summary

The Overall Goal of the proposed programme is to enhance the institutional capacity and coordination of Syrian Civil Society, towards an effective and efficient role in serving and advocating for the most disadvantaged populations in Syrian. The programme will achieve intended results for what can be initiated in 2019, knowing that some results will be fully sustained and functioning through follow up and support during 2020.
The programme intends to achieve the four following Outputs/Results:
 Output 1: Coordination among CSO networks is improved within a common platform
 Output 2: CSOs organizational and institutional capacity is developed
 Output 3: Collective actions, joint programming and implementation, and sharing of resources are enhanced in the CSO networks
 Output 4: Improved dialogue and corporation among Whole of Syria CSOs
The programme is about strengthening the capacities of national bodies to manage and better serve their crises and affected population, therefore, it falls under strong resilience marker.

Needs

International organizations and donors had to rely, especially for cross-borders support provision, on the Syrian civil society organizations to provide humanitarian assistance to the population. An estimated 700 Syrian CSOs were created of which 15 to 20% are estimated to be really active inside Syria and operating with a minimal organizational structure and an expressed commitment to humanitarian principles. While the CSOs started working on relief and humanitarian activities, a shift was observed recently as some started supporting following early recovery approaches as well as good governance and transitional justiceThough international organizations and donors supported capacity-building of CSOs in various areas, most CSOs and networks suffer from weak organizational capacity despite the fact that the CSOs are often composed of highly qualified professionals (such as engineers, medical doctors, lawyers, academics etc…). They have in fact little experience in operating within the framework of a civil society organization. Their fast growth since 2011 to respond to the urgent and emergency needs of the Syrian people prevented them to consolidate their operations in a more structured way and build a culture of organizational behaviour. Most CSOs require therefore support in enhancing their organizational capacity that would allow them to adapt to changing context in Syria, diversify their operations to include early recovery and livelihood activities and become sustainable by lessening their reliance on donors’ fundingUNDP conducted an organizational capacity assessment of Syrian CSOs during November 2018 – January 2019. The main objective of UNDP Initiative is to maintain and develop the capacity of the CSOs at the institutional level, that will strengthen their role in representing civil society and improve their execution of projects, through enhanced Coordination Platforms. For this purpose, the assessment included:• An analysis of the provided capacity building, and its impact on the institutional capacities of CSOs.• An analysis of the current networks and platforms of CSOs and formulation of related recommendations• An assessment of the organizational capacity of CSOs and identification of the current capacity gaps• Formulation of capacity development responses to improve CSOs’ project execution of Humanitarian Response and strengthen their institutional capacitiesThe assessment’s main conclusions and recommendations were validated by the stakeholders, through a special validation workshop, which demonstrate the credibility and ownership of such assessment.The organizational capacity assessment of Syrian CSOs revealed gaps in the capacity of CSO networks as well as of individual CSOs.Please refer to the conducted UNDP's "ORGANIZATIONAL AND CAPACITY DEVELOPMENT ASSESSMENT OF SYRIAN CIVIL SOCIETY ORGANIZATIONS" report.

Implementation of activities

Based on the conducted Assessment Report, the programme intends to make an implementation phase responding to the conclusions and recommendations, aiming to achieve the four following Outputs/Results: Output 1: Coordination among CSO networks is improved within a common platform(1) A coordinated strategic framework among all CSO networks is developed(2) A governance structure of the platform is initiated (together with the restructuring of the League of CSOs).(3) An executive management body to coordinate the operations of the platform is initiated, to be fully functioning in 2020.(4) Working groups of CSOs covering various areas of interventions are established, to be developed gradually to the creation of specialized networks.(5) Automated management and support systems  Output 2: CSOs organizational and institutional capacity is developed(1) An integrated and comprehensive capacity development strategy to improve CSOs performance is designed(2) Through a core unit on capacity-building, provide specialized ToT training on specific areas based on assessed gaps. (3) Capacitating the core unit to enable the coordination of capacity building for all CSOs, to be functioning in 2020.  Output 3: Collective actions, joint programming and implementation, and sharing of resources are enhanced in the CSO networks(1) Mapping of CSOs based on field of expertise towards specialized networking(2) A framework/approach for local community mobilization to improve service delivery and ensure local ownership is developed.(3) Create system for sharing collective actions among CSOs in each of the networks.(4) A strategic framework (including advocacy and funding strategy) of each of the CSO networks is prepared(5) Initiate participatory approach platforms for assessments and prioritization  Output 4: Improved dialogue and corporation with CSOs in Whole of Syria(1) Conduct bridging & dialogues workshops(2) Initiate platform for legal and procedural cooperation is created.(3) Initiate platform for lessons learned and exchange of experiences is established.

Protection Risk Analysis

The programme is designed to support the role and capacity of civil society organizations, who are one of the front lines for dealing with protection issues. The programme includes several support and capacity building on adapting protection principles, human rights, and humanitarian principles that will allow CSOs to tackle and design responsive interventions for vulnerable groups. The programme will include providing technical assistance for establishing executive units that will adapt protection principles in the strategic planning, implementation of activities, and to be part of the criteria for joining. Protection principles will be supported by establishing core expertise under the Syrian Network League that can support CSOs to consider while implementing their activities. The programme considers also gender and youth as cross cutting issues that will be considered through the different components and stages of the programme.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Political Factors and Uncertainty:• Major changes in political environment that will change the priorities for CSOs• Ongoing uncertainty in the roles of civil society organizations at the national and local levels.Mitigation:• The programme offers added capacity to civil society organizations and networking, where it will be perceived as an added value regardless of political environment. UNDP will continue consultations with stakeholders on political changes with flexability for adaptation.• The programme offers creating dialogue platforms among all CSOs that legal and policy changes can be discussed and reflected at the national level. Policy Factors:• Opposing the suggested governance reforms by Syrian Networks League and other stakeholders from CSOs• Weak commitment to the changing process and following the new governance guidance.Mitigation:• UNDP made comprehensive consultations with stakeholders before the design of this programme. Agreement with Syria Networks League and other stakeholders will be made at the beginning fo the programme to set the main broadlines for the programme before starting which will eliminate future opposing to the change process.• The programme follows strong participatory approaches during the implementation to strengthen ownership; and considers adequate monitoring and support period after delivering the technical assistance, to ensure commitment and sustainability of achieved results. Capacity Factors:• Considerable capacity limitation within Syrian Networks League and other stakeholders to comprehend and adopt proposed changes.Mitigation:• Programme proposes “learning by doing approaches” where technical experts will be stationed and working directly with stakeholders. Intended results are designed to be achieved in a participatory approach and not prepared by UNDP for adopting.
