Project Summary

WVSR's WASH program continues to support IDPs in NWS/NCS by providing life-saving, comprehensive WASH services. Water supply through emergency trucking, desludging, SWM, and hygiene supplies/cash/voucher distribution will be provided in the subdistricts of Azaz, Idleb, Dana, Maaret Tamsrin, Teftnaz, Ras Al Ain and Tell Abıad (RAATA), and future sites targeting 150,000 beneficiaries in IDPs sites. In some of these locations, WVSR plans to install water networks and pipelines to connect the IDPs camps and communities to support/rehabilitated water stations to support phasing out water trucking activities to more sustainable water supply services through supported systems to target another 150,000 beneficiaries alongside the beneficiaries of 15 water stations that will be supported by WV and its partners in subdistricts Azaz (6), Sarmin(1), Taftenaz(2), Maaret Tamsrin(1), Atarep(5), and another 10 stations in Toum, Kfraya, Kaftin, Qmenas, Shallakh, Hazano, Jabal Harim, and West Ariha, through the operation (staffing, chlorine and fuel supply), and maintenance. WVSR, directly and through its partners, will provide and install solar energy systems in some of these stations to support hybrid energy systems to decrease the electricity costs and overall running costs of the water systems. Beyond O&M support, we'll also provide training workshops and feedback meetings for local councils, water station managers, and community leaders on cost recovery, O&M, and reporting. Desludging activities are proposed in IDP sites in Azaz (Al-Ryan, Talil sham, and Al Zayton), and Dana subdistrict. Sewer network rehabilitation and expansions are proposed in Azaz, Dana, and Ras Al Ain subdistricts to target around 50,000 beneficiaries. In 2018, WVSR broke ground with the first wastewater treatment plant of its kind in Sejjo camp, funded by private funds, where the treated water is suitable for agriculture. In the institutional setting, WV plans to rehabilitate and construct WASH facilities in 30 schools in Maaret Tamsrin, Dana, Atareb, Harem, Bennesh, Tell Abiad, and Ras-AlEin, targeting around 5,000 students during the year, and support WASH needs for persons with disability in 3 camps in Azaz. Hygiene promotion and RCCE campaigns will use diverse formats (school messaging, street posters, water tanks, waste vehicles, and social media) with targeted messages for different settings and age groups. WV uses “nudges and cues” as subtle reminders, such as painted steps to handwashing stations and distancing markers at water points to encourage healthy habits. Leveraging its partnership with Sesame Workshop, WV will apply the Sesame St. curriculum to targeted children at several informal learning centers in Azaz IDP camps. Empowering children to have healthy and hygienic habits through the promotion of behavioral changes in sanitation and hygiene through children's play-based learning materials and multimedia content programs developed through the Gates Foundation. At health facilities level, WVSR and its partners will support water supply to serve around 2,000 patients and additional 500 during outbreaks. In addition to distributing standard hygiene kits to the targeted population and customized ones targeting PwD and elderlies needs. In summary, these proposed interventions are lifesaving and resilience activities, enhancing access to essential WASH services, reducing harmful coping strategies that have adverse cascading effects on individuals and communities in terms of public health, environment, inequality, and poverty, and providing a foundation for the prevention of Cholera outbreak and reduce transmission of other water-borne illnesses and ultimately save lives.
