Project Summary

The aim of this project is to sustain continues supply of Insulin and Adult Heamodialysis kits to avoid preventable mortality among diabetics and renal failure patients.
UNDP has been procuring those two life saving items for Sudan National Medical Supplies Fund since 2017 through funds provided by the government. Since war erupted in Sudan, funding from the government was irregular resulting in interrupted supply and frequent stock outs of the two items. This was associated with plenty of preventable mortality.
NMSF annual expenditure on insulin is around USD5.01 million and Adult dialysis kits is USD13.1Million. Both items are subsidized by the government to patients.
Through this project, UNDP request partial funding to support procuring Insulin and Adult Heamodialysis kits so as to assure continues supply and prevent deaths related to stock out.
