Project Summary

This project aiming to contribute to achieving main objective that is:
Support livelihoods of affected communities and households by increasing agricultural production, protection of productive assets, and restoring or creating income-generating activities to prevent negative or irreversible coping mechanisms for 1800 HHs in Aleppo and Idleb governorates.
Main targets:
1- Improved the socio-economic status in targeted areas for 1800 HHs in 2021 season.
2- Increased sustainable livelihood opportunities in Aleppo and Idleb areas for 1800 HHs during 2021 season.
This project by the two implementation methodologies will contribute in creating work opportunities, Ahl Horan Foundation (HF) can summary the opportunities as follows:
1-The first methodology depends on cultivating one dunum with summer vegetables so each farmer will get profits by selling their products in the market at the end of the harvest season which allowed them to have a source of income to plant their lands for the next season and HF will select 1000 local farmers whom their lands are near their houses with no affordability to agricultural supplies, financial resources or guidance in cultivating their lands.
2- The other methodology is to distribute 4 sheep with fodder enough for seven months, also veterinarian’s support will be available along seven months.
HF will select 600 internally displaced people who fled previously from their houses towards northern Syria. HF will increase skills and knowledge of these beneficiaries by conduct practical and theory trainings.

4 - Needs

Based on the HNO-2021,82% of households indicate decreased ability to meet basic needs since mid-2019, Growing income gap: average household income now 20% below expenses, Increasing unaffordability: households spend 51% on food, leaving little room to meet other basic need, Lack of access to basic services due to non or severely reduced functionality of critical service, infrastructure in almost half of all sub-districts and increasingly aggravated by reduced affordability. The conflict had killed more than 500,000 people, including hundreds of thousands of civilians, and caused waves of displacement and wide-ranging concerns about the protection of the population is the highest on a large scale within Syrian territory. The estimated number of people living below the poverty line with 85% of the total population has passed more than six years of war, and GDP shrank more than two-thirds. The unemployment rate of 57%. More than 50% of households are buying food, and at a higher rate for the displaced and internally displaced persons. Besieged areas have the highest percentage of households that are used to buy food and other negative coping strategies. Livestock numbers declined sharply since 2011: the number of cattle has dropped by 30% and of sheep by 40% and poultry by 50%. Animal feed prices have risen by 168%. based on UN official statistics. The proposed locations in Afrin and Jandairis subdistricts are one of the locations considered as level 4of severity as by HNO severity scale. As per our need assessment currently being conducted in which showed that more than 34% of the population are IDPs with more than 50% of people in need of food and livelihood assistance.

5 - Implementation of activities

1. Distribution of agricultural inputs; and related training. 2. Support to asset building and asset protection (small livestock and animal feed distribution) including winterization activities; and related training. HF will divide the activities as flowing: Agriculture activities - Hold training session -Coordinating with the local council to determine the benefited farmers -Registering the targeted farmers according to agreed criteria -Signing of agreements with farmers. -Choosing a contractor according to procurement policy of HF. -Preparing irrigation network and distributing fuel. -Distributing agriculture inputs families to receive the agricultural summer kit from awarded suppliers -Irrigation process. -Providing the necessary agriculture service. -Holding Practical training in the chosen seven typical fields. -Harvesting the crops.Livestock activities - Hold training session -Coordinating with the local council to determine the benefited IDPs. -Registering the targeted IDPs according to agreed criteria - Signing of agreements with beneficiaries. -Rehabilitating suitable place for raising sheep -Procuring 2400 sheep and fodder for them.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption the security situation remains steady the time of implementing the project. risk the situation deteriorate in the area mitigation: since the targeted location is in Afrin which is controlled by the Turkish government it is unlikely for the security to deteriorate in this area. Assumption 2 – Access to Syria remains possible and safe from the neighboring countries. Risks: A change of government policy/attitude in Turkey could lead to a closure of the borders or and/or negatively impact humanitarian access to Syria Mitigation strategy: As a registered NGO in Turkey, HF is expecting that this legal framework will, at best, give the leeway to operate. If the general attitude towards cross-border humanitarian assistance should change. Assumption 3 – The activities can be completed without delay Risk: As the activities will be implemented during the rainy season, HF might face difficulties accessing the area. Mitigation strategy: HF has anticipated such a situation could happen and as a consequence, including the risk of not being able to access the communities due to heavy rain or flooding by designing the work plan of the activities. Assumption 4 – Markets are functioning properly in the area identified Risk: Due to the unpredictable situation, there is risk markets are not functioning properly due to lack of access and/ or supplies. Mitigation strategy: HF teams have confirmed the market is functioning and the security situation allows the implementation of the project. The situation of the market will be monitored on regular basis by the program team.Risk of physical harm at distribution points (security, safety), medium, high, Well organized distribution to avoid overcrowding with COVID-19 mitigation measures
