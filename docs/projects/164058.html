Project Summary

A project is a training center through which we will provide training for 100 beneficiaries, including internally displaced persons and returnees, and we will work to teach beneficiaries to connect household electricity, and install solar energy, plumbing and construction.
At each stage, 25 trainees are trained by an expert trainee, and at the end of the training, a basket will be provided in which the equipment needed to start the work will be provided. Each stage and craft will be trained per month, and each stage will have 25 trainees, i.e. 25 beneficiary families. This project aims to provide a livelihood project for beneficiary families so that they can get money and economic support.
The duration of the project is 4 months. At the end of the project, 100 families benefiting from the project will be self-sufficient.
Objectives:
Contributing to support the livelihoods sector in Idlib Governorate - Jisr Al-Shughour
Empowering 100 families to achieve self-sufficiency and achieve economic support
Integration of IDPs and the host community
Training 100 beneficiaries on a profession in which he can earn money
