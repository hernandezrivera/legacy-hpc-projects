Project Summary

According to reports by FSNAU and FEWSNET, combined effects of widespread and severe flooding, Desert Locust infestation, socioeconomic impacts of COVID-19 and the cumulative impacts of previous shocks, up to 2.1 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps or depletion of or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse outcomes through December 2020 in the absence of humanitarian assistance. In addition, 849 900 children under the age of five are likely to be acutely malnourished through August 2021.Desert Locust continues to pose a serious risk of damage to both pasture and crops at least until the end of 2020. The 2020 Deyr (October-December) rainfall season is likely to be below average to average across the country, which could lead to drought and trigger food insecurity if the 2021 Gu (April-June) season rainfall is also delayed or performs poorly. The 2020 Gu seasonal flooding (river flooding) contributed to loss and damage to property, housing, farmland and infrastructure. The riverine areas of Hiran, Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle, Gedo, Middle Juba and Lower Juba were some of the worst-affected areas.
Agricultural and livestock production in Jubaland state riverine, rain-fed, agro-pastoral and pastoral areas in Somalia have been affected by various shocks. From October to December 2020, food insecurity is expected to deteriorate among poor households with limited livestock or low capacity to cope with harvest losses.The COVID-19 pandemic and associated socio-economic impacts also contributed to the food insecurity situation Gedo region .
Its against this backdrop that the proposed project seeks to provide emergency and sustainable response to food security and livelihood crisis among IDPS and host communities in Jubaland state, Somalia specifically Gedo region .This will be achieved by distribution of food vouchers to 1080 vulnerable households especially those with malnourished children; Rehabilitation of 20 water catchments destroyed by floods through Cash for work approach directly benefitting 3000 men, women, boys and girls in Gedo ; 500 vulnerable pastoralist households (especially those headed by women and disabled persons) supported with 5 goats to help them recover their lost livelihood; Provision of seasonal inputs (seeds, tools, fertilizers, water pumps and fisheries equipment etc) for 700 households . The proposed project will ensure that marginalized groups are given apriority during project implementation, A project implementing committee (PIC) will be established comprising of community structures (women and youth), local authorities, community leaders and organizations representing PWDs. The PIC will be involved in selection of beneficiaries to ensure that the most vulnerable people (PWDs, women and youth ) receive immediate assistance. The project will also address barriers that hinder women and PWD access assistance services

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash programming has contributed to greater level of accountability through electronic reporting, local ownership engagement, building local coping mechanism and support cohesion and transition to development. Food security will use cash transfers as a delivery mechanism using local service providers and will support local supply of financial services. The CTP will provide immediate access to food for vulnerable drought affected communities and support to household and community livelihoods assets (water catchments, canals. Do No Harm approach will be applied to ensure that the interventions do not cause or fuel conflict. The mobile money transfer ,currently used to administer Cash transfers as well as E- vouchers will also be used for this project to administer payments to target beneficiariesThe approach will ensure beneficiaries restore their dignity since they will be working for their income instead of getting in-kind hand-outs. The cash transfer will also offer them freedom of choice on how to spend their income. The decision to use the approach was identified following discussion with the community.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

The project was designed in a way that will strengthen the local markets and boost the demand and supply chain in the target locations .The project will engage local business to supply food items to beneficiaries for 3 months using food vouchers, this will in turn increase the supply of food in the target location and facilitate the recovery of the local economy , Cash based modality for work in rehabilitation of WASH facilities (water catchment systems) will strengthen livelihoods at household level, giving families an income through which they can be able to meet other basic needs including water access

Mitigation Measures

The project takes cognizant of the fact that the crises has different impacts men, women boys and girls differently which can exacerbate unequal power relations, discriminate affected populations on basis of disability, gender and age. recognition of this differences will enable the program to reducing existing inequalities by ensuring that services are available to all persons including people with disability. Just like men and boys are engaged, women, girls and people with disability will also be empowered to participate actively in program activities and take up leadership positions in project committees for enhanced development as they enjoy equal opportunities. Protecting human rights of people with disability and promoting gender equality in this regard will remain central to this response. Gender will be mainstreamed and integrated throughout the project cycle to ensure that adequate and efficient services are provided. Cash based interventions will accommodate both male and female beneficiaries and people with disability in affected locations. The will be need to strengthen and improve safe programming based on ‘Do No Harm’ principle ensuring that basic protection principles are weaved into the humanitarian response across thematic priorities. To enhance protection of target groups , the project will uphold standards and guidelines that enhance service delivery. The SPHERE project and the Somalia operational Framework ( SOF) by the food security cluster Somalia will be referenced to ensure protection for women and girls and distribution sites are accessible and within acceptable distanced to reduce risk of abuse. DF will maintain up to date financial management systems including books of accounts and payment vouchers to eliminate the risk on mismanagement of funds. Payments and accounting will be segregated where roles will be assigned to different persons. Proper tendering procedure will be followed for purchase of tools and materials. Proper lists of trainees and participants shall be provided after each training. The lists shall contain the names and telephone contact details of the participants. All the lists shall be properly signed by the attendees and participants.
