Project Summary

It is economic, social and developmental project depends on micro financing.
Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation established this project, offering lending services for both men and women on favorable terms. This project aims at firmly establishing work culture and principle of self-reliance in addition to providing job opportunities for the Syrian people.
This project is directed towards providing an opportunity for those who have the skills, capabilities and willing to establish a private business. The foundation offer them liquid cash required to accelerate and promote their activities. This will help in creating new jobs and increase beneficiaries’ income.
This project will offer financial inclusion and lending services for 165 customer. Returned installments that are paid by those customers will be given to other ones. The Foundation expects that the number of beneficiaries will reach 230 customer/borrower by the end of the year, using favorable loans system.
Project’s background:
69% of the Syrian citizens suffer from extreme poverty, 35% of them suffer from extreme poverty. This means per capita income ranging from 0 to 1.9 dollar daily as per definition of income line issued by world bank.
On the other hand, number of evacuees inside Syria have reached more than 920 thousand evacuee in 2018. Most of those evacuees are settled in north of Syria in Idlib, western and northern countryside of Aleppo.
Micro financing project of Al-Sham Foundation is considered a part of humanitarian Response Plan of early recovery cluster. This project is offering means and tools of revenue generating. Moreover, those projects will emerge people from poverty, improving the quality of living and enhancing resilience.
Also, micro financing project in its new version is considered an extension and development for Good livelihood projects that was implemented in 2015 – 2016. It has a positive effect on the living situation of the beneficiaries and the surrounding society.
Note: this project has been emerged with Soft skills for business and economic empowerment project.

Needs

As per humanitarian needs over view (HNO) 2019, many Syrian citizens strive to stay alive after their whole life was destroyed due to the conflict for nearly six years. Millions of people are exhausted from adaptation strategy. Nearly 69% of citizens suffer from poverty and nearly 35% suffer from extreme poverty characterized by severe deprivation from foodstuff required for survival. The potential of having essential services stopped almost completely, including medical care, clean water and education. Some families resort to unsustainable and unsafe means to survive, including compulsory and/or early marriage, child labor and child soldiering to survive.On the other hand, increase of prices and decrease of currency value in 2016 obliged Syrian families to struggle to earn their living. Earn living opportunities decreased more than before; also loss of income affected millions of dependents.Nearly 85% of the Syrian citizens are below poverty line, while 69% of them suffer from poverty as 35% of them lives in extreme poverty. Those who suffer from extreme poverty are not able to afford essential needs for their families. It is likely that, family income may confront more pressure especially after the rise of fuel prices. This reality has created the need for operational solutions instead of temporary rescue solutions. Those solutions proved to be less effective over the long run, in addition to its negative psychological effects on the affected people desires to work and secure their life.
