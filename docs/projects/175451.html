1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The Project will be realized in the districts of Pemba and Mecufi, targeting 8 schools in Pemba (Primary schools of: Paquetequete, Ingonane, Chuiba, Wimbi, Mahate, Eduardo Mondlane, Natite, Samora machel) and 4 schools in Mecufi District (Muitibua, Murripa, Sicura, 3 de Fevereiro) and 2 schools in Ancuabe (Primary schools of: Macaia, Sunate).The objective of the project is to guarantee a safe return back to school to the students (host community and IDPs) of the three districts. The project aims on supporting the affected and displaced children, providing teaching and learning material and orientation to their parents on how to support the learning process at home, school or in temporary accommodations sites. The project will also disseminate hygiene, health and sanitation best practices, in order to prevent the COVID 19 transmission.Clearly the most vulnerable and poor children, children with disabilities, living in these accommodation centres or within their own families have hardly a chance to access the messages or lessons, radio and TV messages. Staying at home or in the temporary accommodation sites increase the risk for being exploited for domestic tasks, for being confronted with domestic violence and/or sexual abuse, in particular girls. Target groups: - Students of 8 schools of Pemba District, 4 schools of Mecufi District and 2 schools in Ancuabe District;- Professors that will be involved.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will support schools through:- Providing hygiene and WASH related to Covid-19 material to schools (Pemba, Mecufi)- Awareness and capacitation messages disseminations through palestras and theatre shows (Pemba, Mecufi, Ancuabe)- Installation of hand-washing points with soap to ensure that the practice take place in every school (Pemba, Mecufi)- Assisting the education services in producing and provisioning of teaching and learning materials, in particular by focusing on displaced children and families (Pemba, Mecufi, Ancuabe)- Conduct trainings to teachers, local authorities, community leaders and IDPs camp sites representatives/responsible persons, to promote best practices of hygiene and sanitation to prevent COVID 19 at school (Pemba, Mecufi)- Develop, produce and promote non-presential communication material with guidelines on hygiene, sanitation, nutrition and environment guidelines in the context of COVID 19, to be used for distance education and sensibilization, targeting young students and adults (both local and people living in IDPs camps). The communication material will include posters, brochures, comics, weekly radio drama and debates (in partner with local Community radio) (Pemba, Mecufi, Ancuabe)- Door-to-door or small groups open awareness sessions for students and adults, including people leaving on IDPs camps, to disseminate the guidelines of hygiene, sanitation, nutritional and environmental best practices. The session will be facilitated by the trained teachers, community leaders and technicians. Participants will be divided into 2-3 groups of 12 students per event, according to the guidelines against the Covid-19 pandemic. (Pemba, Mecufi, Ancuabe)- Door to – door or small groups for demonstrations activities (keyhole techniques, school clean up, construction of innovative waste bins with locally recycled materials) to disseminate among the students and communities agricultural, nutritional and environmental best practices. (Pemba, Mecufi, Ancuabe)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The compliance of all COVID 19 protocols will be ensured, in accordance with the health guidelines to avoid possible contagion or infection between the main stakeholders and beneficiaries of the project. Also, the identification of beneficiaries of the project will be based on the criteria of priorities established at the level of local government, counted with involvement of teachers, community leaders under the monitoring of the local entities, district government, provincial directorate of Education. Therefore, this approach will ensure the ownership and empowerment of local people in relation to the initiatives and interventions to be developed during the project.The main risk is the “Possible distrust and difficult acceptance from the part of the beneficiaries of the adoption of Covid-19 risk mitigation measures”.The project identified the following solutions:● Involvement of the community leaders on the diffusion of the messages and on how to better explain the necessity of following those hygienic measures ensuring a better acceptance from everyone. In fact, they have significant potential to influence the cultural norms. ● Holding single-sex, age segmented focus discussion group sessions on constrains to their behavior change, always taking into consideration anti-Covid norms.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos and ECO Moçambique in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.The provincial and district education services DPEDH and SDEJT) in the selected districts for implementation of the project activities will work with assistance from the education cluster and active partners in Cabo Delgado and utilize existing feedback mechanisms such as Linha Verde and Linha Fala Criança for which specific pre-formulated questions and replies already do exist. Therefore, the DPEDH, selected SDEJTs and partners will be aware and can follow up about any comment on the distribution or availability and quality of the services, products or equipment provided, and of specific requests for information.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Clearly poor children or/and with disabilities, living in accommodation centers or within their own families have hardly faced less chance to access education during this situation. Furthermore, the longer it takes to resume classes or not being involved in some basic learning, the higher the risk for many of the poorest children and girls of never returning to school. Appropriated and adequate approach to ensure inclusion of this target group to access education and lifestyle in the context of COVID 19 pandemic, will be appropriately developed.Local Institutional stakeholders will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities.All the activities have been structured with the aim of guaranteeing access to disable people, such as the selection of the location for the training and the project staff will guarantee all the support to allow them to participate in the actions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Some environmental considerations that can come up is waste disposal and how to dispose of those safety equipment (such as individual masks for example) in a way that would not be prejudicial to the environment. The project identified the following solutions:● During communication and awareness activities, the population and other beneficiaries will be taught the correct way to dispose of the individual safety materials distributed by the project, so that this correct disposal will contribute to prevent excess waste that could bring great harm to the environment. ● We ensure a minimal use of non-re-used material to reduce mass waste production in refugee accommodation sites, schools and host communities.
