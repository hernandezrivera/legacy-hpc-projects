Project Summary

The objective of this project is to contribute to prevention and reduction of excess morbidities and mortalities amongst children 0-5 years and PLWs in the crisis affected communities in the SW region.
To achieve this goal, AAH set up mobile clinics that will operate in the divisions of Fako, Meme and Manyu Division. these mobile clinics will deliver a care package including: the treatment of the main diseases of children under 5 including the iCCM approach in localities with difficult access (malaria, acute respiratory infection, diarrhea, SAM without complications) l ANC, PNC, supplementation with VITA, deworming of children from 1 to 5 years, vaccination, etc.
This project will also integrate support to the SC of the Kumba hospital and a psychosocial support component for populations affected by the crisis.
Rehabilitation of water points will also be carried out and distribution of WASH kits to the vulnerable in order to increase the acceptance of AAH in the region.

Besoins / Needs

As a result of the internal conflict in the northwest and southwest regions of Cameroon, the existing health infrastructure was destroyed and abandoned, and most of the medical personnel were moved to other cities because their lives was threatened . This conflict has led to the displacement of several people who sometimes stay in the bush without access to any humanitarian assistance, particularly in the health / nutrition / WASH sector. Women and children under 5 are the most affected by this crisis.In response to this humanitarian crisis, AAH has implemented since May 2019, 2 mobile clinics to directly offer a health care /nutrition package to relieve PLW and children under 5 years; two more mobile clinics have been set up since october 2019. This intervention also integrated the rehabilitation and distribution of WASH kits to vulnerable populations in order to improve their hygiene / sanitation conditions.Specific objective of this project: To reinforce access to basic healthcare, PFA and WaSH services in conflict affected areas in southwest region.
