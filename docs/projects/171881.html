Project Summary

The multicomponent project, designed for protection and advocacy of rights and freedoms of the population affected by the conflict in Eastern Ukraine and consists of 3 components:
Component 1. Operating Hotline for the conflict-affected population on wide range issues.including consultations on a wide range of issues related to COVID-19
Component 2. Legal support for the conflict-affected population, especially in the context of legislative changes related to COVID-19
Component 3. Expert support to the governmental bodies and Advocacy of rights and freedoms of the conflict-affected population
