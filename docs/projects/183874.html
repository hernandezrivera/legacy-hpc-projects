Project Summary

This project is intended to inform the CRP 2022 chapter of the HRP 2022. The proposed interventions are designed based on the CRP 2022 Sector Strategies, PiN and Severity of Needs HNO 2022, and the Inter-Agency Refugee Response Plan - Sudan Ethiopian Influx (Revision May 2021). The proposed interventions are intended to address the urgent WASH needs of the Ethiopian refugees in Al Fashaga Locality (CRP priority 1) of Gedaref State in East Sudan over a 12-month response. The target population of this proposal is approximately 16,000 individual refugee in Umgargour refugee camp and village 8 transit site. Throughout the proposed interventions, contribution to safe access, dignified and segregated sanitation facilities is improved and hygiene services are improved as well. To address this, 1,000 households will be provided with monthly soap distribution to promote handwashing practices. 1,000 woman/girl will be provided with monthly personal hygiene materials distribution to promote personal hygiene for women and girls of reproductive age. 200 new household shared/non-shared latrines will be installed based on available space and priorities. 200 new shower blocks/bathing facilities will be constructed and segregated for women and men to promote personal hygiene.

1 - Needs

Based on the Basic Needs and Vulnerability Assessment (BaNVA Sep 2021) for Refugees in Sudan, high WASH vulnerability is an issue for both refugees and host communities across all surveyed states, namely Gedaref, Sennar,Khartoum, North Darfur, Central Darfur and North Kordofan. Except for White Nile, refugees have higher WASH vulnerability compared to their host communities. Most refugees experienced high and severe WASH vulnerability in all states. For the new Ethiopain refugee influx water distribution networks in Um Rakuba, Tunaydbah, Village 8 and Hamdayet need to be expanded to phase out from water trucking and accommodate the needs of both refugees and host communities. Across all assessed states, more than half of the refugee population reported having unusable latrines or no latrines, meaning that they are high to severely vulnerable as well as access to shower blocks. In all assessed states, more than half of refugees reported having no access to soap and/or handwashing facilities.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The proposed WASH interventions are composed of four main activities as follow:1. Construct/install (200) new household/s latrines (shared/non-shared between two households) in high need and/or prioritized locations. The 200 new latrines will improve access to 4,000 individuals (20 prn/latrine). 2. Construction of (200) shower blocks/bathing facilities in high need and/or prioritized locations. The 200 new latrines will improve access to 10,000 individuals (50 prn/shower block). 3. Provision of Soap for (1,000) households on a monthly-based distribution to promote handwashing practices.4. Provision of personal hygiene materials for (1,000) woman & girl of reproductive health on a monthly-based distribution who will receive personal sanitary materials. The above activities will be directly implemented by JASMAR staff through its Sub-Office in Gedaref State.
