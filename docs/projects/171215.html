Project Summary

The proposed project is designed respond to child protection needs by addressing multiple protection risks faced by girls and boys through a comprehensive set of activities in well-coordinated and gender-sensitive approach by supporting children at risk and survivors including unaccompanied or separated children through case management services that meets their unique needs, as well as preparedness measures for speedy family tracing and reunification (including psycho-social support).
The project will also work with volunteers to prevent and mitigate risks to children by building their capacity on rights approach of whom support identification and basic support to vulnerable children (including psycho-social first aid - PFA). SOYDA will also work with caregivers on positive parenting as well as provide psycho-social support to vulnerable caregivers affected by floods and made worst by the pre-existing emergencies related to conflict in Somalia as well as COVID-19. Additionally, the project will engage with the community on protection awareness raising on prevention messages on recruitment/or use of children by armed actors, gender-based violence, harmful practice, family separation and risk of dangers in the community (including in IDP camps or the local community) while coordinating with the CP AoR in service mapping operation of the standardized thematic tools and procedures.
The project will establish referral system in schools and support community-based Child Protection Mechanisms for quick and efficient referrals of children with protection needs through service mapping within the community and in the schools (community child protection committees and School Management Committees). This project will strengthen service mapping to facilitate appropriate referrals, coordination and linkages in service delivery to ensure quality comprehensive services to unaccompanied and separated children.
In addition, in Daynile, Dharkenley, Kahda districts of Mogadishu, and Afgoye and Marka district of lower shabelle. Region, SOYDA will conduct and strengthen protection monitoring in areas of settlement of returnees and IDPs through community-based protection monitors working with CSOs in order to ensure that quality evidence-based reports about protection issues and human rights violations and systematic collection of protection related data are produced to inform better response and advocacy.
SOYDA will involve staff to promote the welfare of children and protect them from harm by ensuring child’s family, cultural background is respected while activities and process are in a manner that does not put the child at risk of harm. Participation of boys and girls, will be facilitated in a responsible and ethical manner. SOYDA will disseminate guidelines on prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and reporting mechanism. In the long run protecting children from maltreatment, preventing risks contributing to impairment of children’s health or development, so as to grow up in circumstances consistent with safe, effective care with action to enable children have the best outcomes.
SOYDA will actively support the rights of all boys and girls to participate and express their views in all matters affecting them in accordance with their gender, age, maturity, and capacity. Different participatory methodologies will be applied for different ages (i.e. younger children, adolescents). Effective participation recognizes children and adolescents as rights-holders, it builds their capacity and resilience, and allows them to protect themselves and their peers.
Through non-discrimination principle will ensure procedures wich support and advocate for the access all, displaced and including children to protection systems, regardless of their age, gender, ethnicity, religion, nationality, or disability. Approach ensures that all persons of concern, including boys and girls children and adolescents, enjoy equal rights, recognizing diversity.

Mitigation Measures

The following are the main protection risk related to exclusion of the vulnerable population upon displacement during nature adversity includes increased sexual gender based violence towards women and vulnerable girls, separation and lack of foster care among the children hence discrimination,abuse,child labor, rape,marginalization,diseases,ealier marriage by teenage girls and insufficient or/an undocumented vulnerable people needs and poor information sharing, lack of coordination as well as increase sexual exploitation among the girls and women in the IDP camp. SOYDA will be mitigating this through establishments of strong referral pathways during displacement as well as ensure boys, girls and women are given first priority in the emergency intervention, SOYDA will also provide integrated protection component within its intervention to ensure quick supply of prophylaxis to enable reduce the risk related spread of infection as well as liase with local authority in more coordinated version to enable bring the culprit of rape to the books and thus either be referred to the local authority for punishment. On mitigation of separated or unaccompanied children SOYDA will undertake reunification process by using the social media and electronic media as well as radio and TVs and this will ensure wider information sharing hence ease the reunification process for these affected families SOYDA will strictly respect the labor law of the country and will not engage in any activities that are going to facilitates abuse of children right, it will however, conduct wider community campaign and awareness to enable the community respect the children right and stop any form of child labor. It will also strive to provide equal opportunities for both girls and boys as well as marginalized, minorities and disable children hence improve the disparities among these in needs.SOYDA will also ensure proper information sharing and timing of session with target communities. The following are some of the preventing risk associated with exclusion on gender and age such are, lack of consultation among the adolescent girls in the various camps about their sexual reproduction, safety and dignity hence this will create an avenue for oppressed girl child at the age of adolescent, also such exclusion as special group in decision making process in the camp might hinder proper advocacy and awareness among the disable, minority and marginalized population in the IDPs and host communities hence poor diversity and resilience building. For more effective and efficient mitigation SOYDA shall propose to have equal men, women, boys and girls in both services centered activities as well as employment within the target/operational areas in Afgooye and Mogadishu IDPs, Also on the issue of decision making. We shall route for proper representation of all interested group within the society such as disable, minority and marginalized as well as elderly and single mothers in these targeted IDPs and host communities. SOYDA will be will to cater the needs of both sex and age and shall strive to ensure all are equally served whether on employment opportunity or service provision with the target areas, it will also provide proper coordination mechanism that will enable the protection focal point within the community and project team work in harmoniously for preservation of beneficiary data without any alteration of their information or sharing with an external party to avoid any risk of discrimination among the beneficiaries. In the instances for the distribution of dignity kits, material assistance as well as non-foods items SOYDA will ensure equal share are provided by all the target vulnerable children boys, girls, separated, unaccompanied children & women within the target IDPs and host communities, will also ensure to advocate within the community in consultation with stakeholders within the project area that these children to prevent child soldiering
