Project Summary

The project aims to bring vulnerable and underprivileged IDPs children in the south west, north west, littoral and west regions back to education. This will be both, through ensuring enhanced access to basic education for out-of-school vulnerable and underprivileged IDPs children, as well as by providing remedial support for children to be able to catch up on lost learning of the previous school years and help avoid that they drop out of education. The project seeks to build their basic literacy and numeracy skills and preparing them to transition to formal basic education or vocational skills development opportunities. Initially a total of 1800 children (boys and girls) shall benefit directly from the program. The age group of 4 to 12 years will be considered.
