1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Besides the measures already in place, Mozambique is still struggling to keep pace with preparedness for a timely and effective response vulnerable to Armed conflicts in Cabo Delgado. According to Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment (MSNA), approximately 47,000 internally displaced from Cabo Delgado Province, have relocated to Nampula Province. Many of these people lack water and are exposed to malaria, while remaining in unhygienic and overcrowded conditions, thus increasing the risk of an outbreak of diarrhea. To address these issues, WV in partnership with UNICEF proposes to improve access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities (as per WASH cluster standards), disseminate information on safe water, sanitation and hygiene as well as distribute non-food items. With these, World Vision expects to prevent the spread of acute diarrhea, cholera and mitigate its impacts on the most vulnerable people, including the Internally Displaced Persons living in resettlement sites through improved sanitation and hygiene practices

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Meconta, Monapo, Erati and Memba : • Install handwashing stations in public spaces and vulnerable and highly impacted communities/ barrios, including provision of soap; • Install and manage temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centers and resettlement sites; • Facilitate solid waste management (including construction of communal pits as well as collection, removal, disposal and drainage) in accommodation centres and resettlement sites; • Promote self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in long-term resettlement areas (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households); • Promote hygiene in accommodation centres and resettlement sites and; • Procure and distribute WASH related Non-Food items, with focus in most vulnerable families / households; • Disseminate WASH related messages through mass communication media.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Children at Risk of Vulnerability will be taken priority specially those who live in a: elderly headed households, child headed household, single parent headed household. These groups will be the top priority of this project and to ensure protection/Safeguarding issues are also taken into account whenever anyone comes across them, formal and informal protection structures and stakeholders are going to be consulted on the way vulnerable people and Protection issues are handled in the community. Reporting and referral mechanisms and systems will be also accessed and strengthened to ensure people know how to identify, report and follow-up on Protection/Safeguarding insures properly. Protection /safeguarding will be important in all WASH committees to ensure they have all necessary to act as a safeguarding/ protection structure and ensure vulnerable people are safe to participate a make part of the project activities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

World Vision will regularly consult affected communities and key stakeholders to ensure that program is designed in a way that is responsive to the needs and preferences of the affect population. The program design is intended to meet immediate life-saving needs. Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) and on-site distribution monitoring tools will be used to track beneficiariesʼ satisfaction with different aspects of the intervention. Monitoring data will be consolidated and analyzed, improving ongoing programming and informing future response plans. A report of the post-distribution monitoring of NFIs will be shared with UNICEF. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations using World Visionʼs Programme Accountability Framework (PAF) that is primarily focused on WVʼs own accountability to the communities we work with. The programme accountability covers how World Vision uses power responsibly and creates mechanisms that empower children and communities to hold us to account. It includes four practices, sometimes referred to as ‘pillarsʼ (providing information, consulting with communities, promoting participation and collecting and acting on feedback and complaints), that we commit to whenever we directly engage children and communities across all types of programming.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

For vulnerable households such as those that include individuals with disabilities, elderly and child-headed households, fully subsidized household level latrines will be provided. The remaining households will provide a labor contribution for their pits and superstructures. World Vision will work with the local authorities and leaders to identify individuals with disabilities and conduct individual consultations with these individuals to define their specific needs for adaptations of latrines to allow them to safely access and utilize their latrines. For these households World Vision will provide a fully subsidized latrine (sub-structure, slab, superstructure, and adapted features as applicable) if the households cannot construct their own latrine. An estimated 10% of latrines will include adaptations for individuals living with a disability.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will do promotion of self-construction of household latrines and facilitation of solid waste management (including construction of communal pits as well as collection, removal, disposal and drainage) in accommodation centres and long-term resettlement sites will including appropriately sitting of latrines and garbage pits and in an adequate distance from any surface or groundwater source to avoid contamination.
