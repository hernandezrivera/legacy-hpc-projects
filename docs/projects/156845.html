Project Summary

Since March 2011, Syria has faced protracted conflict causing loss of human lives, significant displacement, and migration, weakened social services, destruction of basic productive infrastructure, disruption of livelihoods, and deterioration of rule of law. Combined, this has undermined social cohesion and stability and contributed to an overall decrease in the ability to meet basic needs and access services and opportunities within Syria. In Idleb, nearly half of the population are IDPs (44%) and one of the main drivers of food insecurity in Idleb is high concentration of IDPs and their increasing vulnerability.
SKT is seeking support to improve the food security of 2,382 households within Idleb region living in camps through food basket distribution. SKT will distribute 2,382 food baskets to the beneficiaries in the following camps Omahat Al Moumenien, Al Azraq, Al Aman, Al Madenh Monarh, Dalama, Jabal Harim, Abna`a Al Ghab, Tajmae Aleiza, Kafr Hum, Alsafsafh, Al Taakhi, Alzeraah, and Ayidoun in Idleb governorate. The proposed project will ensure a response to time-critical requirements of the vulnerable families, including women headed households. Therefore, this project will contribute to other ongoing and planned food security and livelihood interventions within Idleb to person, 10-12% of which come from protein and 17% from fat accurate and sustain gains in line with cluster sector objectives and plans. SKT food kit will provide 2,100 kcal according to WHO/FAO guidelines (1791.4 Kcal provided by the food basket, complemented by 200-400 Kcal by the bread bags). In addition, SKT has an ongoing bread distribution project within the same selected geographical location which will supplement the food kits distribution. Hence, families' nutritional status will be increased and therefore will lessen the burden of buying bread as each HHs will get a free bread bag on a daily basis. The food basket takes into account beneficiary preference in commodity type and quantity, and will be delivered on a daily basis within a period of three months.
SKT will coordinate its activities with other organizations providing food security support in the same local areas to ensure broadest coverage of persons of concern not otherwise receiving food kits, and to avoid duplication and enhance complementarity where feasible. This cooperation will be through field meetings, e-mails, Skype, including essential participants, such as, camp managers, local councils and community leaders, in addition to active and regular coordination with the FSL cluster. This practice is to avoid any potential overlaps and improve knowledge of good practices and lessons learned. SKT has a good communication with Turkish Red crescent may be used to mitigate any risk may face exporting Flour and Sugar to Syria. SKT has active humanitarian registration and maintain good relation with Turkish border authorities.
At the end of the project, the targeted beneficiaries will have their nutritional state improved by 60% based on the assessment led by SKT and the comparison of the initial and final HHs dietary diversity situation. The different monitoring tools implemented by SKT in order to measure the improvement of nutritional state of targeted beneficiaries will be selected in accordance with FSL Cluster indicators.

Needs

SKT conducted a rapid need assessment (RNA) in 25 camps in Harim and Salqin locations. The methodology of data collecting included key informant interviews (KIIs) with camps managers, members of the administrative committees, sector experts and representative of local authorities (LA). Selection of key informants was based on the nature of each section in the assessment and took into consideration that the local authorities, community leaders and camps managers are commonly trusted resources for this information. As per the findings: 1- Almost all people in surveyed camps faced difficulties accessing enough food due to:- No resources/income to purchase food.- High food prices.2- A majority of people purchased on credit or borrowed to obtain food and bread.3- Looking for income opportunities, sharing items, borrowing items and taking credit / going into debt were the most common things people do when they could not meet their basic food needs.4- When asked about their needs and priorities, the majority has defined food basket as their priority need. Based on the above, SKT will provide life-sustaining food assistance (GFA) to 2,382 HHs in need of critical and sustained food assistance in 13 camps through GFA. The GFA will be supplemented with bread bag distribution for each family for a period of three months. Regarding the targeted beneficiaries, SKT is putting a particular attention to target both newly arrived IDPs as well as previously displaced people settled in the targeted camps. This letters have been remaining in these areas since September 2017 and have not received any food assistance since their arrival. SKT will assure that required service providers (transportation services, warehouse workers for loading and unloading goods), as well as warehouse rentals, are contracted on time and in line with standards and best practices defined in SKT Logistics Manual. All our service providers are verified against sections lists (anti-terrorist checks) and are subject to ongoing quality monitoring by SKT field staff. Therefore, this project will contribute to other ongoing and planned food security and livelihood interventions within Idleb to leverage and sustain gains in line with cluster sector objectives and plans. SKT food kit will provide 2,100 kcal per person, 10-12% of which come from protein and 17% from fat (according to WHO/FAO guidelines). The food basket takes into account beneficiary preference in commodity type and quantity. Recipients will not be made to walk long distances to collect their food kit (no longer than 1.5 km in distance1), and distributions will be scheduled at convenient times to minimize disruption to everyday activities. Affected communities’ leaders and local councils will be involved in deciding where to locate distribution centers/points. The final decision will be based on wide consultation and on non-discrimination for all potential beneficiaries. Waiting areas and potable water will be provided at distribution centers/points. On top of that, to ensure access for females at distribution centers/points, separate channels are provided for males and females and the appropriate ratio of male to female staff are allocated during distribution and monitoring.A major percentage of them are women and girls, living in poor settlements, being at risk of facing gender-based violence and meeting with high protection vulnerabilities, particularly for Pregnant and Lactating women. This vulnerability dimension will be particularly assessed and addressed through adequate activities in order to ensure that women and girls’ rights and living conditions will be properly secured. Implementation of activities

The distribution activity will be fulfilled by SKT (self-implementing) without any mediators.SKT will communicate in advance the selection criteria and the outreach plan with all stakeholders including local councils, community leaders and camp management to make sure that the selection process and activities are transparent and clear.SKT always works in close coordination with communities, LCs, and relevant stakeholders to ensure fair selection of the beneficiaries using selection criteria and therefore, maintaining a good relationship with a range of concerned stakeholders and strengthening transparency.The food kits will be unloaded at SKT’s warehouse in Bab Al-Hawa border area. The logistics team represented by warehouse is responsible for the receiving process of the food kits. The warehouse staff will conduct a physical inspection on the receivables and checking the food kits conditions. There will be a committee which will take a random sample of the food kits and check the quality of the items provided. As well as, the logistics team in Turkey will follow the food kits regarding quality and quantity provided by the supplier in Turkey through the transportation process from Turkey to Syria. The food kits will be purchased from Turkey. All this information will be filled in (GRN) template and will be prepared and approved by at least 2 persons (1 Log and 1 PM) and submitted to the Logistics Manager for further actions. All food kits received during the project will be filled in a GRN form with all information and the conditions of the assets at the arrival time. Each food item received to SKT’s warehouse will have a separate tracking document which a stock card form to be used with all necessary information. The Stock Card will indicate the available balance of each item and this information will be passed by the warehouse staff to the Log Manager on periodical basis to act accordingly. Based on internal needs for the department, the warehouse staff will document the issuance process of the commodities or items by filling a Handover (HO) form and obtain all necessary signatories for authorizing the issuance of the commodities during the project cycle. SKT will distribute 2,382 food baskets to the beneficiaries in the following camps Omahat Al Moumenien, Al Azraq, Al Aman, Al Madenh Monarh, Dalama, Jabal Harim, Abna`a Al Ghab, Tajmae Aleiza, Kafr Hum, Alsafsafh, Al Taakhi, Alzeraah, and Ayidoun in Idlib governorate. Crowd management of beneficiaries:● In case of big number of BNFs, SKT usually establishes more than one distribution centers (distribution points close to the beneficiaries and located in such a way as to minimize the numbers of people who attend any distribution point at any time) to manage the distribution process by informing families about the schedule of upcoming rounds of distribution.● Also, SKT uses the strategy to avoid big congregations by breaking down the distribution for two or three days for one location or/and per day depending on the situation.● In coordination with LC, the information about locations, villages and exact timing of distribution are clearly communicated to beneficiaries. In addition, distribution cards are printed and handed out to beneficiaries two days prior to the distribution in order to arrange specific timing for selected beneficiaries into the group (each group might contain 50-60 HHs). Usually, 300-400 HHs per day, per single location. ● Each HH will come forward with a distribution card, carrying his/her ID for identification. She/he will sign to acknowledge the receipt of the quantity of the commodity on the assistant receipt form (ARF).● Another element, important for crowd control, is that SKT always makes sure that the food kits are unloaded in a room with locked doors at the distribution center, away from the crowd of beneficiaries to make sure that any person without authorization has no access to the food kits.

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk1: SKT during implementing the activities of the project may face these risks according to the project's activities. Protection risk 1: Community tension arising from the beneficiary selection. Likelihood: high / Impact: High. Mitigation: SKT will priorities the most vulnerable females-headed or males headed with PSNs HHs, considering the children situations and difficulties within these HHs (priorities to HHs including children with disabilities or chronic diseases), to receive services during this project.Risk 2: Risk of harassment of women and girls by the humanitarian workers. Likelihood: medium, Impact: high. Mitigation: establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and enhancing accountability mechanisms besides training the staff to deal with beneficiaries in addition to providing workers with training on humanitarian standards and signing code of conduct.Risk 3: in distribution area we may face a risk of harassment for female beneficiaries by males beneficiaries.Likelihood: High . Impact: High . Mitigation: we will conduct separated distribution points for males and females.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: BNF selection process disrupted for security or political reasons. Mitigation: - The distribution of food baskets may be suspended for a short period of time due to the bad security situation where the distribution can be resumed thereafter as the distribution is one-time monthly and there will be no significant impact on the distribution.Risk 2: BNFs sell or re-distribute food materials. Mitigation: - Food basket materials are selected carefully to be suitable for people who are food insecure and according to the minimum standards of food baskets so, the beneficiaries will not try to sell the food basket materials. Risk3: Large groups forming around distribution center/camp could lead to mounting tensions and progress to physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion. there will be more than one distribution points in each area, there will be also special distribution points for famales and ones for males. There will be also different times of distribution. Risk: 4 Banding exporting or supplying flour and sugar towards Syria. Mitigation: SKT has good coordination with Turkish Red Crescent as well as active humanitarian registration allow to mitigate any risk. - SKT has been prepared data and conducted awareness sessions regarding the beneficiaries list and clarified that all people in need will receive the food basket
