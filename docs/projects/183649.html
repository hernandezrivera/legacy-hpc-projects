Project Summary

The project’s overall objective is contribute in Ensuring the availability of acceptable and quality essential healthcare services to vulnerable communities in Gaza City.
The project work to avoid over-crowded waiting lists without age/gender-based separation ,the targeted patients are selected from those ones inserted in the MoH waiting lists; where around 12,000 elective surgeries were postponed leading to complications and morbidity according to the Humanitarian Needs Overview OPT Dec.2020.
. The surgical services will be provided through 6 well-equipped operation theater which are led by highly qualified consultants; multidisciplinary team in all specialized medical areas.
To respond to the identified needs and to contribute to the HRP, and health cluster objectives, PAS general director communicated with Director of Financial and Administrative Affairs in the General Administration of Hospitals, Director of the Coordination Unit with NGOs - MoH to verify and prioritize the list and types of elective surgeries. And PAS will implement this project through:
- Conducting 1380 (236 Men, 198 Women, 617 Boys, and 329 Girls) elective surgeries that are referred from MoH as:
1. Pediatric Surgery (270 Minor, 50 Moderate and 100 Major)
2. E.N.T surgeries (270 Minor, 50 Moderate and 30 Major)
3. Orthopedic and tendon repair surgeries (30 Moderate and 10 Major)
4. General Surgery (30 Moderate and 40 Major)
5. Urology (20 Moderate and 30 Major)
6. Eye surgery (100 Minor, 320 Moderate and 30 Major)
- Procurement of medical supplies and disposables of surgeries, medications, PPEs, Hygiene and infection control materials for about 150,000 beneficiary (38,387 Men, 29,113 Women, 46,918 Boys, and 35,582 Girls)
The proposed intervention is expected to last for the duration of the project (11 months) and to benefit a total of 1380 direct beneficiaries. Moreover, its impact will last beyond the project's period as it will ensure avoiding deterioration in health status of people targeted in a safe manner and will minimize the load on MoH hospitals.

1 - Needs

Gaza is experiencing a third wave of COVID-19; According to the Ministry of Health more than 1,000 were recorded in Gaza the last 24 hours; as the harsh economic situation, extreme poverty and bad health conditions, exacerbated by years of blockade and siege Gaza is considered one of the most areas affected negatively. This is likely to get worse with all hospitals in Gaza have postponed elective surgeries and outpatient services. More than 12,000 elective surgeries have been postponed due to preparedness measures for possible management of COVID-19 cases. Additionally, about 1,484 medical cases cannot be referred to hospitals outside Gaza due to crossing closure the highest number of permit applications since February 2020. (Monthly Report WHO sep.2021). Delays of necessary surgical interventions have a grave impact on decreased health status and affects the psychological and social life of the patient. In some cases, this may lead to more medical complications.According to the HNO 2021, Patients who need Access to specialized services within Gaza are considered as a priority and stated that patients in need of elective surgeries and in the waiting list are the most vulnerable group in need of health intervention. Despite implementing such interventions by other international and local institutions, the need still exists & the number of patients increases steadily especially in light of the fact that MoH allocated a large part of its facilities to deal with emergency and COVID-19 cases. The proposed activates will complement the role played by other service providers who cover patients in other geographic locations (e.g. Northern and Southern governorates) and other specialties (e.g. Eye surgeries, Gynecology and Obstetrics, Minimal Invasive Surgeries …etc.). The proposed project responds to the Health Cluster’s Objective no.1: “Ensure the availability of acceptable and quality essential healthcare services to vulnerable communities in oPt” this related to Strategic Objective no. 2 “The basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through the provision of quality basic services and improved access to resources, in accordance with the rights of protected persons under international humanitarian law”.

2 - Activities

1. Conducting 1380 elective surgeries for patients (women, men, girls, boys, elderly, PwDs) including: Pediatric Surgery (270 Minor, 50 Moderate and 100 Major), E.N.T surgeries (270 Minor, 50 Moderate and 30 Major), Orthopedic and tendon repair surgeries (30 Moderate and 10 Major), General Surgery (30 Moderate and 40 Major), Urology (20 Moderate and 30 Major), and Ophthalmic surgery (100 Minor, 320 Moderate and 30 Major) 2. Procurement of medical supplies for surgeries, disposables, medications and PPEs for the staff and Hygiene and infection control materials.

9.1c - How will feedback be used?

PAS allocates a special email as complaint box to receive complaints from beneficiaries. The beneficiaries are to be informed about complaint mechanism during the project’s period and will be encouraged to submit complaints freely. Field visits by project’s staff and also interviews with beneficiaries will take place periodically, to measure the beneficiaries’ satisfaction, and to ensure that the services are delivered in an accepted way. PAS will adopt complaints and feedback system, where data are to be collected, analyzed and correction measures will be taken ensuring beneficiaries satisfaction. Moreover, beneficiaries’ suggestions will be taken into consideration for ongoing intervention and future similar interventions. Complaints will be handled in confidentiality. A wide range of project groups, local authorities, local partners, project staff, and beneficiaries should have the chance to provide their feedback in relation to any project activity they are taking part in.Number of patients provided with services from PAS will be reported to MoH so they can re-prioritize their services.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

The project activities is implemented at PAS, where safe environment with no discrimination in function and access for all, regardless of age, sex or condition, without obstacles, with dignity and respect regardless of any ethnic, gender, social, religious and/or political considerations. The health services are to be provided through a specialized and vaccinated team for COVID 19 who follow a practical and a comprehensive mechanisms in order to maintain patients’ good health status with minimum risk of getting COVID-19 transmitted to them as well as facilitate the provision of high quality health services in a diligent, professional and proper manner through a series of flexible procedures in a computerized system for keeping the consultations, documentations and records private and confidential.The project is going to provide health services for about 1380 patients (236 Men, 198 Women, 617 Boys, 329 Girls)who will receive equitable services without discrimination taking into account each patient individual characteristics and special needs, especially individuals with disabilities, children and women as the most vulnerable in the society and who need special attention and protection depending on Human dignity, confidentiality and non-discrimination. Consent form will be used giving the patients the rights to decide to get or reject receiving the services. Patient consultations, documentations and records will be saved to be private and confidential. To ensure Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, Project related contracts will include standard clause on PSEA in accordance with IASC principles and guidance and also Project’s staff will directly and in directly engage in the HCT oPt PSEA network.

11. - Equal and impartial access to aid
11.1 - Equal and impartial access to aid

Project is designed to be implemented at a well trusted hospital located in an accessible and safe location (Center of Gaza City) targeting in need vulnerable individuals and communities without partiality or discrimination. Moreover, the project's design takes into consideration the principles of protection stipulated in the humanitarian charter including the right to live with dignity, the right to receive assistance and the right to protection. The hospital provides a high quality comfortable environment for the all patients especially vulnerable one that is conducive to healing, supported by friendly and encouraging qualified teams in addition to facilities that enhance patients’ feelings of comfort and well-being through ensuring a number of measures including:Wheelchair ramps, elevators, solar energy system, generators, comfortable rooms, clean wards, comfortable temperatures, proper ventilation, clean bathrooms Clean bedding, , healthy meals and entertainment.
