Project Summary

In its eighth year, the conflict inside Syria shows all the signs of a complex protracted humanitarian crisis. Takaful Al-Sham is aiming to support IDPs and part of vulnerable HCs with SNFI assistance in Aleppo and Idleb governorates according to the following sub-districts:

NFI Interventions:
The total 6000 HHs will be targeted with various fuel types (Kerosene - Diesel - Firewood and Olive pits) provision, stoves, winter clothing kits and NFI winter kits according to family needs where priority will be given to most vulnerable families to face severe and harsh winter. Vendors will be selected through a committee according to specific criteria focus on accessibility, trust in community…etc.
Distribution of vouchers will be conducted in four phases for the same beneficiaries

whereas distributions of in-kind winter NFI kits will be one off distribution.
Shelter repair 250 HHs, collective center rehabilitation 250 HHs.
Takaful's shelter interventions will take place as follows:
- Installation of tents with associated Infrastructure: this will take place In the response of emergency displacement, re accidents, and floods, where tents will be Installed with the graveled ground according to recommended specifications. targeting 1100 HHs.
- Upgrading of unfinished buildings: Targeting IDPs residing in collective/ transitional centers that doesn't match minimum standards and doesn't respond to protection and privacy needs. targeting 55 buildings
- Installation of transitional shelter: replacing old tents with new RHUs to provide better living conditions to protracted IDPs, targeting 500 HHs.
- Shelter repair for housing: Targeting IDPs (tenant), and host community members (owners), with focus on returnees to seek sustainability by laying the essential foundations for long-term recovery. This will target the most vulnerable categories of society.
- Infrastructure Rehabilitation: rehabilitating community Infrastructure such as water / sewage networks, roads …etc.
At the beginning of the project Takaful will conduct a meeting with local community and local actors to ensure their participation in the project, explain the project goals and take their suggestions and notes to develop the project activities and implementation.
Takaful will coordinate with SNFI cluster and with other organization who works in the
same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication.

4 - Needs

The shelter situation has continued to deteriorate given the unavailability of adequate and affordable shelter, the limited response for longer-term solutions, the new challenges for communal shelters with COVID-19 and the displacement trends in NWS. An estimated 31 per cent of the overall population continue to live in inadequate shelter conditions, with returnees and IDPs being the worst affected, as the Shelter PiN increased to 5.88 million in 2021 which is a 4 per cent increase in PiN from 2020 (5.7 million). The NFI PiN increased from 3.4 million by nearly one million in mid-2020 due to developments in NWS and the price fluctuations. The NFI PiN rose to 4.69 million in 2021, which is a 9 per cent increase from the revised mid-year 2020 PiN (4.33 million) and a 6.5 per cent increase from 2019 (4.4 million). 5 - Implementation of activities

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

- In case the complaint was related to SEA classification we share it with our trained field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to investigate of a case and take the required action with the employee according to SEA Code of conduct and refer the case to GBV sub-cluster to follow up with the survivor as well as we report the case to PSEA Network. We classify under the sensitive complaints about sexual exploitation or abuse (SEA), Takaful has zero-tolerance for sexual harassment, exploitation, or abuse by any of its staff members, any report or allegation of verbal or physical sexual exploitation or sexual abuse by organization staff or providers against child or women or disable groups of beneficiaries or people in needs to get humanitarian services, such cases should be reported, where the beneficiaries of Takaful services have the right to report SEA, and TAS staff are required to report SEA by another staff member. - We applied some steps to avoid SEA cases as the following: Most of Takaful management and field staff (males and females) had attended workshops to increase their awareness of PSEA cases.- TAKAFUL has trained a field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to follow up on the reported case and take the required measures.- Ensuring that all volunteers who work with TAS signed PSEA CoC

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Safety and security:locations are far from front-lines so change control is not expected.Risk: Changes in the security situation or authorities/groups in control of project areas may make the projects activities less appropriate to the new situation.Likelihood: highImpact: HighMitigation:-In coordination with the Access Working Group, the security officer will help mitigate the risks through collecting information on security updates from field staff, the network of local councils and civil society activists.-Ensure that staff members are trained in safety and security.-All critical documents will be saved as soft copies, so that they will not get lost due to an attack.-If an emergency arises that may threaten the safety of staff and/or beneficiaries, staff is provided with directives to move to a safer location until the area is safe enough to continue with project activities or to ask for location change.- Preparing plan B, where if one of the targeted locations affected by the military operations, it will be replaced with another locations.Programmatic:Risk: Some of targeted locations may be supported from other donor, which lead in duplication of services.Likelihood: LowImpact: MediumMS: -At the beginning of project, we will re-check with related clusters and other networks to make sure that there isn’t any organization implementing similar programs in the targeted location.-If it appears that any organization was responded in one or more of the targeted locations, we will coordinate with cluster to change the location.
