Project Summary

The project focuses on ensuring the emergency GBV service provision to SGBV survivors and women and girls at risk, with special attention to vulnerable populations (adolescent girls, heads of household, people with disabilities, migrant workers, older people, LGBTQI+ people) in affected areas by the Beirut explosion. The project will provide women and girls with psychosocial support services including GBV case management, and refer them to specialized legal, health, mental health and other required services through static and mobile facilities. The project also aims at addressing vulnerability of women and girls by distribution of Dignity Kits as well as information materials on GBV risk mitigation and available services as well as on COVID-19 specific mitigation measures. Building on the findings of study on Integrating for cash and voucher assistance (CVA) into GBV program conducted by UNFPA Lebanon (August 2020), UNFPA with its partners will ensure the provision of CVA targeting women and girls at high risk of GBV or GBV survivors with an aim to reduce household tensions by contributing to household needs, to pay for safe alternative accommodation and for GBV survivors to meet urgent needs. Outreach activities will be conducted to facilitate access to the GBV services for women and girls as well as to increase awareness of men and boys on GBV risks and mitigation to have them as allies based on "do no harm" approach. UNFPA will support its partners to establish and scale up existing hotlines for provision of remote specialized services. Support to existing Women and Girls Safe Spaces (WGSS) managed by UNFPA partners will be further strengthened and linkages with sexual and reproductive services will be reinforced. UNFPA and its partners will continue to conduct GBV safety audits to ensure that programmes are accountable to the needs of people in a way that promotes their safe and dignified participation and engagement in decisions affecting them. UNFPA IPs will conduct site-/location-based advocacy on GBV risk mitigation with other sectors, namely shelter, health, and protection; Specific focus will be given to GBV risk mitigation within the newly established shelters through the schools.
UNFPA will enhance partner capacity on Prevention from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). Based on the PSEA protocol developed for Lebanon, feedback and complaints mechanisms are in place and UNFPA will ensure response to unethical behavior by humanitarian workers as per agreed code of conduct. UNFPA will ensure that staff and partners are aware of PSEA standards and will keep raising awareness on SEA risks. The social workers and mobile team members at the WGSS will be deployed only after going through training and having relevant/required skills on survivor-centered approach for GBV case management and emergency psychosocial support. Mobile teams are also oriented on Psychological First Aid (PFA), community outreach, and both have critical tenets of ‘do no harm’ approach. All partners and staff working on the projects, including frontline workers, will be further capacitated through supervision and mentoring on survivor-centered approach; basic protection skills, whose main tenet is ensuring a "do no harm" principle.

1 - Needs

The scale and magnitude of the disaster caused by the explosion at Beirut Port on the 4th of August left people in immediate need of aid and saw a drastic increase in psychological distress and trauma due to the loss of lives, the many missing and the thousands injured, in addition to the damages on properties and businesses.Many people reported that their homes were unsafe to live in (HelpAge/Dorcas Rapid Assessment, Beirut Blast, 14 August 2020). In addition to the vast needs for rehabilitation of the damages to housing units, the situation impacted significantly on the mental health and wellbeing of women and girls, due to uncertainty about their recovery.Sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) is among the greatest protection challenges individuals, families and communities face during humanitarian emergencies. SGBV risks were already on the rise before the Beirut explosion incident due in large part to the COVID-19 pandemic and its resultant restrictions on movement, the deteriorating economic situation, and volatile security environment. Socio-economic vulnerabilities and SGBV risks have been worst amongst particularly vulnerable groups, including women and girls, heads of household, migrant workers, LGBTQI+ communities, people with disabilities and older people. UNFPA through its IPs (static and mobile services) will ensure to target vulnerable people, especially women and girls without discrimination. Special consideration will be given to the most vulnerable groups including people with disabilities, migrant workers, the LGBTIQ communities and in order to ensure their specific needs are addressed and their access to the services. Mobile team/outreach team will reach out to the people with disabilities to facilitate the access to the required services. Information material will be translated in different languages to be accessible for migrant workers. Frontline workers deployed in the affected areas will always respect the non-discrimination principles and ensure empathic and compassionate assistance. The direct targeted beneficiaries of the project will be vulnerable at risk of violence and SGBV survivors with special attention to vulnerable population (adolescent girls, women heads of household, people with disabilities, migrant workers, older people, LGBTQI+ people)Men and boys will also benefit from the project activities, especially outreach/awareness-raising activities, to be sensitized on the challenges women and girls face in the current conflict/displacement, GBV risks and vulnerability, how men and boys can support women and girls.Service providers at the partner organizations will benefit from the mentoring and technical support related to specific interventions such as GBV case management, psychological first aid, Psychosocial support, community mobilization, self-care.

2 - Activities

Activity 1.1 Conduct GBV safety audits in the affected areas including in temporary shelters to ensure risk of GBV are identified and mitigatedActivity 1.2 Develop/update, translate and distribute Information, Education and Communication (IEC) materials on GBV risk mitigation and GBV available services, Protection from Sexual Exploration and Abuse (PSEA) as well as on COVID-19 mitigation measuresActivity 1.3 Conduct awareness raising sessions on GBV risk mitigation with other sectors, including shelter, health and protectionActivity 2.1: Provide psychosocial support services to women and girls at risks and GBV survivors, including GBV case management, basic counselling (individual or group sessions) through mobile, static and remote UNFPA e services. Through case management GBV survivors will be referred to specialized services including clinical management of rape.Activity 2.2 Support existing women and girls’ safe spaces that need to be refurbished Activity 2.3: In coordination with the SGBV Task Force, develop, disseminate and update service mapping and referral pathways and inform communities on the updated referral pathways Activity 2.4: Provide Cash and Voucher assistance to women and girls at risk survivors to mitigate GBV risk and facilitate access to GBV services Activity 2.5: Procure and distribute dignity kits to women and girls together with information on available services, menstrual hygiene, and risk mitigation relevant to COVID-19 pandemic
