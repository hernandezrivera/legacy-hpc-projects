Project Summary

This project will provide protection services for the most critically vulnerable Lebanese population, including women, men, girls, and boys, working both to prevent protection violations as well as respond to needs. It will address GBV needs through provision of services including psychosocial support and case management. It will also address child protection needs through protection case management, focused and non-focused psychosocial support, and recreational activities. The project will address legal needs for GBV and CP cases through provision referral to legal services, including legal counselling and payment of legal fees, for individuals both in and out of detention through partner organization, Migration Services and Development (MSD).
The project will operate through Tabitha-run community centers (CC) in Batroun, North Gov.; Aley, Mount Lebanon (3 centers in Aley city, Choueifat and Bayssour) in addition Chouf through the intervention in the SDC of Chehime; Achrafieh, Beirut, and in Ghazir and Ain el Remaneh where Tabitha implements inside the SDC protection activities. Moreover, Tabitha will target greater Beirut area (Metn and Baabda) through an already OCHA funded project. These centers and mobile unit contain Tabitha’s community outreach workers, a psychologist, and social workers who maintain a presence within targeted communities. Cases taken up by these social workers may come from multiple sources- referrals from other NGOs and CSOs, cases identified by community outreach workers, cases identified through individuals or family members already participating in Tabitha’s community center protection services, etc. Tabitha also partners with MSD, specialized in legal services and has access to individuals in detention facilities. MSD lawyers also maintain designated offices within Tabitha community centers.
Tabitha will also coordinate its work with the stakeholders of each area, for Achrafieh and Greater Beirut, Tabitha will coordinate with World Vision as well mainly on the GBV component.
Case Management will be implemented through the use of the social work methodology including individual vulnerability/needs assessments (through interviews and home visits when possible), a network of outreach workers (selected and trained among vulnerable refugees/host communities) to facilitate trust and access to isolated groups on a timely and safe manner. Tabitha follows the national protection working groups, all the team is trained on the usage of the national SOP for GBV and CP, using the recognized referrals pathways to ensure cases that require specialized follow-up are referred to be assessed, specialized service providers. MHPSS services will be made available for those attending programs at the CC in all the locations- this includes the elderly, children, and those who have experienced severe trauma and abuse. A licensed psychologist is present in all locations and animators/facilitators are trained to facilitate group PSS sessions, especially for children. The legal assistance program operates using a holistic approach, whereas both lawyers & social workers provide services to those in need of assistance.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

The project will target the following SO:GBV: Lebanese, migrants & Palestinians from Lebanon are able to live in a protective environment where their human rights are respected at household & community level and their protection risks are addressed in a comprehensive manner allowing a life in safety and dignity. National systems to prevent, mitigate & respond to GBV are strengthened & accessible to all. Community-level perceptions & attitudes on power, GBV, women’s rights are changed, & actors have the capacity to respond to immediate & acute life-saving GBV risks and shocks.CP: Child Protection services, including case management, Emergency Cash Assistance and psychosocial services, are available and accessible to children and caregivers in need. In addition to women, men, boys and girls enabled to improve their protection including through strengthened community and family support.All of the proposed activities below will take place in the different CC that Tabitha runs whether in Aley, Chouf, Beirut, Batroun or via mobile teams in addition to Metn, Baabda under OCHA project, & the 5 SDCs where Tabitha is working. Tabitha will be conducting prevention activities such as awareness-raising and education providing participants with information and knowledge on their rights, legal procedures, and available services can help prevent various forms of abuse, exploitation, and discrimination, in addition to community engagement & empowerment.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

This project will base the intervention on the national SOPs using vulnerability/needs assessments (through interviews and home visits), a network of outreach workers (selected among vulnerable refugees/host communities to facilitate trust building and access to isolated groups). Tabitha mainstreams protection in all its activities which allows maximization of the protective impact of the project through ensuring that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Activities create a space for discussion and peer mentoring which allows different groups to reflect on their similarities rather than differences. Project activities are designed to include any critically vulnerable individual within the population, regardless of their gender identity, disability, race, religion, or socioeconomic status.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Accountability towards the served population and stakeholders is key to Tabitha’s humanitarian approach. Tabitha has already well established feedback and complaint mechanisms, this mechanism will be established at each site so children, adults, and anyone else who wants to is able to share their opinion, feedback and/or complaints. Tabitha builds its programming based on feedback and the needs of the community; this is achieved utilizing Tabitha's community centers which are well-established, known, and trusted within each community. Tabitha has successfully participated in a series of coaching/mentoring sessions on PSEA & safeguarding integration done by ABAAD in partnership with OCHA, LHF and the Lebanese National PSEA Network.
