Project Summary

Needs

The aim of the needs assessment is by supporting the livelihoods of the most affected host communities by creating job opportunities, income generation activities, restoring productive assets, reinforcing agriculture production (crop production, livestock, fisheries) with a special focus on women from the poorest households, and in general the communities most affected by the Rohingya influx Challenges to Overcome: With the opportunity of different livelihoods vulnerable women of these areas are facing serious kind of challenges. Lack of Educational qualification, financial weakness, lack of skill, unemployment, no electricity and many more other things are common challenge of these communities and social environment here are still not so woman friendly so in addition to these women are facing serious types of constrains which includes eve teasing, social barrier, fanaticism, violence, early marriage. The community is still holding their women inside households. So overcoming the economic status of the ultra-poor women will not be enough, to achieve ultimate goal of a sustainable resilient community it is must to work on the social value and acceptance of women.Possible area to Help: By considering all the opportunity and constrains below actions can be taken to ensure the economic empowerment of the most vulnerable women through training and- Formation of women care groups- Understand on developing business plan through attending in technical sessions- Life skill development training for beneficiaries- Nutrition / BCC counselling- group introductory sessions on group savings- poultry and livestock vaccination campaign for reducing mortality of the live assets- Provide inputs support to targeted beneficiaries for livelihoods activities- Life skills training on basic literacy, financial literacy and leadership development- Training on Health and Nutrition GVB, Education, communication, social security and DRR- Day observation (Handwashing, NDPD, IDDR)- Participatory cooking demonstration- Support dietary diversification through home gardening and school gardeningMore needs to be done for Host Communities and the district of Cox’s Bazar. Development agencies and programs can help expand and scale up coverage for the most affected area of the district. Development Programmes that complement and expand the Joint Response Plan programmes for the benefit of both host communities and refugees can ensure alignment between humanitarian and development approaches.
