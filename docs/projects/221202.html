Project Summary

Following the rampant abuse/neglect of the rights of children as a result of the on-going crisis in this part of the country, the Project seeks to strengthen the community based Child Protection systems and practices that are not adequately responding to the violations, abuse, and neglect to children’s rights. This will be done by identifying, creating, strengthening and engaging community based child protection stakeholders from all the sub divisions of the Region and building their capacities on the immediate, mid and long term negative consequences on the neglects of child protection.

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project objectives are as follows:(a) To raise awareness of mainstreaming of the Child Protection in Emergencies.(b) To promote the development of a child Protection Information System(c) To build capacity of stakeholders working with children(d) Provide support to families to better protect their children (e) To support the Child Protection referral and case management systemThe project activities includes, training on child protection systems, PSEA and GBV mitigation measures, cover transport cost for children in the case of emergency referrals for better health care services, enhance to basic health care for children with disabilities There will also be the establishment of complain and feedback mechanism through whom feedback will be gotten for re strategizing so that child protection is reinforced in the North West Region.
