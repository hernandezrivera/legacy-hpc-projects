Project Summary

This project will address a variety of urgencies and dire health and nutrition needs in Idleb governorate by matching with the health and nutrition clusters strategic plan and approach. A lifesaving primary health care services will be supported by providing medical staff financial remuneration and gap-filling facilities operational support.
THIS PROJECT WILL SUPPORT tow primary health care centers including MHPSS along with nutrition services through establishing two rapid response teams with its OTP at PHC level. Both of primary health care centers and RRT are in underserved areas and heavily populated with IDPs and affected population.
This grant through health and nutrition intervention will serve 81,667 population based on the update of the IOM report.
The first PHC is Barisha Primary Healthcare Center [G0703043], located in (Idleb > Harim > Qourqeena > Barisha) which is heavily populated with IDPs especially women, elderly and boys, and girls under five years. It is serving 64,036 population, 33,381 out of them are IDPs. This PHC was providing the child health care services, reproductive health services and internal medicine services including NCD and CD giving extra attention for those elderly and people with disabilities.
The second is Alfardan Camp Primary Healthcare Center [E0502005], located in ((Idleb > Harim > Harim > Alfardan Camp)). This PHC is serving 17,631 population in 16 camps as primary health care center. It will provide the outpatient primary health care services with temporary residency. This crucial PHC is providing its health services daily except Friday for those in need covering boys, girls, men, and women. All cases need referral will be referred when needed to Bab Al-Hawa Hospital.

4 - Needs

There is crucial health and nutrition gap which in the mentioned areas, which will be bridged by supporting Barisha and Alfardan Camp PHCs and establishing one Rapid response team that will cove Barisha and Alfardan Camp with OTP in linked PHC.As far as SIMRO is concerned, it is very clear that there exist urgent primary health care gaps in two different areas. Barisha PHC is serving 64,036 population, 33,381 out of them are IDPs and Alfardan Camp PHC, is serving 17,631 population in 16 camps. It is worth mentioning that this critical center is out of support since December 2020. In returning to 4W health cluster coordination tool, which demonstrates that: there are no alternative health facilities neither in Barisha nor in Alfardan Camp PHC. SIMRO conducted rapid needs assessment in mentioned area to see which urgent health gap needs to be bridged. As such, we collected data by using using three types of tools: KII, FGDs, and direct observation, and we depended on Barisha PHC operational records as we were operating it for one year between Jan and Dec 2020 with SCHF fund, which make the needs assessment easier and more efficient.The rapid needs assessment confirms the needs to re-operate both proposed PHCs along with community level services, which is summarized in the following points:Both health facilities are not fully functional and needs supportThe needs of routine reproductive, maternal, neonatal and child health services was confirmed.The needs of the treatment for non-communicable diseases is confirmedThere is lack of mental health services in Alfardan Camps

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: if the hostilities either carry on or get closer to our working areas, we instantly coordinate with cluster and partners to relocate our project filling other crucial gap.Mitigation: we will coordinate with local health partner on the ground to relocate this health facility to more safe and secure place in areas withIDPs waves. Risk: operational risks comes in second as, there are many instances where the fuel prices increase insanely affecting the projectimplementation.Mitigation: As for our response to mitigate these huge obstacles, fuel will be procured from the very beginning at a fix price then it shall besafely stored in our warehouses. Risk: disbursements might be delayed due to uncontrollable conditions which may hugely affect the entire chain of the activitiesMitigation: Therefore, we have sought from the very beginning to secure alternative payments from SIMRO side to cover the potential shortages in the financing. Risk: use of cashMitigation: We developed out policies to reduce the use of cash to the lowest level and depend as much as possible on the PTT office by adding this term to all request for qutations and tenders.
