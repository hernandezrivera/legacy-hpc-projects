Project Summary

This project seeks to improve access to justice for IDPs and returnees in Nigeria through
- Improvement of access to legal assistance and legal remedies.
- Increase in the level of individual documentation.
1. Render legal service, representation and/or defending of individual identified cases of criminal, civil child protection and SGBV in State and Federal High Courts, Magistrates Court, Sharia Court, Mobile court, Sharia court of Appeal Federal Court of Appeal and ADR proceedings.
2. Legal counselling and advice to IDPs and Returnees on a face to face basis in cases that do not require litigation.
3. Provision of necessary legal documents to IDPs and returnees to enable them obtain National Identity Card in collaboration with the National ID Management Board for IDPs.
4. To register and issue birth certificates to IDPs and Returnees from age 0-17years
5. Visits to prisons and police stations by NBA project defence lawyers to identify persons of concern and to offer legal advice and assistance.
6. To institute individual action on behalf of vulnerable groups namely women and children who have been disinherited by the disappearance and or death of male heads of household; providing legal assistance on probate matters, including obtaining letters of administration.

Activities

This project seeks to improve access to justice for IDPs and returnees in Nigeria through - Improvement of access to legal assistance and legal remedies- Increase in the level of individual documentation.1. Render legal service, representation and/or defending of individual identified cases of criminal, civil child protection and SGBV in State and Federal High Courts, Magistrates Court, Sharia Court, Mobile court, Sharia court of Appeal Federal Court of Appeal and ADR proceedings.2. Legal counselling and advice to IDPs and Returnees on a face to face basis in cases that do not require litigation 3. Provision of necessary legal documents to IDPs and returnees to enable them obtain National Identity Card,and or Indigent Certificate in collaboration with the National ID Management Board and Local Government Council. 4. To register and issue birth certificates IDPs and Returnees from age 0-17years 5. Visits to prisons and police stations by NBA project defence lawyers to identify persons of concern and to offer legal advice and assistance. 6. To institute individual action on behalf of vulnerable groups namely women and children who have been disinherited by the disappearance and or death of male heads of household; providing legal assistance on probate matters, including obtaining letters of administration.The Nigerian Bar Association, Maiduguri will assign lawyers to all police stations and the prisons to work on cases involving IDPs and Returnees as complainants or accused/suspects to ensure that IDPs and Returnees Cases are prosecuted/defended to logical conclusion. Lawyers assigned to all police stations and prison will follow up all cases involving IDPs and Returnees in police stations and take necessary action. Cases referred by PSWG/UNHCR for legal action will be followed up accordingly.Each case will be recorded on the specific forms designated for this purpose. A database of attended cases will be established which will take into account respect of confidentiality. The NBA will also work with other national agencies to promote and establish best practices in case management and date collection.
