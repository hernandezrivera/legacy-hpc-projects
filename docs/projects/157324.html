Project Summary

WASH sector aims to provide critical water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) promotion services to internally displaced people IDPs ,Returnees and host communities in south Kordufan and Blue Nile. The primary purpose of this project is to provide relief and prevent the outbreak of diseases and loss of lives by delivering critical and sustainable WASH services to IDPs ,Returnees and host communities in targeted areas. Pan Care aim to deliver WASH services to women, children, the disabled and elderly and the most vulnerable and therefore recommends gender and disability mainstreaming in future actions.
In this project, appropriate WASH technologies and hygiene promotion material will be designed specifically to meet the gender needs of children, women and men whose situation has been worsened by the deteriorating socio-political situation amid escalating demand for sustainable and appropriate WASH services. This action will ensure significant reduction and prevention of WASH related diseases. Water facilities will be technically designed to allow easy access and use by women and children. They will be conveniently located to reduce the risk of child and women abuse. The project will create many access points to reduce time women spend collecting water. Prevailing disability situations will determine additional features on water points to improve access for the disabled and elderly. Latrines will be user friendly to women, children and men and equipped with handwashing facilities. Girls’ school latrines will be designed to meet their menstrual hygiene needs. Hygiene promotion material will be in local language to ensure they are effective. Mimics and animated hygiene material will be produced for children. As a result of this sustainable action.

Needs

Water supply infrastructure is inadequate in Blue Nile and South Kordofan states, people are forced to use unsafe sources, several water points have been identified as being malfunctioning and in need of rehabilitation /maintenance. Open defecation is practiced and other indicators of poor hygiene and sanitation knowledge were observed in the assessed areas. This is in addition to the severe shortage of health and nutrition services in all of the targeted villages. The proposed activities in this project directly correspond with the identified needs across the four sectors in the targeted localities and villages.

Activities

the rehabilitation .5 of water pumps the construction of 8 latrines and water systems with hand washing facilities the formation training of water management committeesthe formation of 4 schools health clubs CLTS activities targeting 5 villages training of 50 hygiene promoters distribution of IEC materials targeting the promotion and prevention of water born diseases (AWD)conducting 12 clean up campaigns in the 5 targeted villages procurer and distribute clean up tools and materials.
