Project Summary

Essential package of health services (EPHS) will be used in order to address the needs of the crises affect population, and reduce mortality and morbidity among women and children in the target area . Existing guidelines and policies (IMCNI, malaria treatment guideline, Cholera treatment guideline and other cluster guidelines) will be used the implementation of this program through fixed health facilities and outreach services. The proposed intervention aims that women of childbearing age have access and utilize quality IMNCH services including HIV testing, PMTCT and VDR testing and emergency obstetrics care, ANC, PNC, skilled delivery, and referral of high-risk pregnancies, through providing pregnant women four focused ante-natal care (ANC) in MCH/HC; Providing women PNC within 48 hours of childbirth, distributing Iron supplements to pregnant and lactating women, and testing pregnant women for HIV. The proposed project foresees children under five have access to essential package of health services to reduce morbidity and mortality from main childhood illnesses like Malaria, pneumonia, diarrhea, and measles. Health staff will be trained on COVID19 prevention in order to convey COVID19 awareness messages to the community. Following targets will be reached throughout the proposed project period. The project will response and address the needs of people with disabilities through involving them throughout the project life (planning, implementation and monitoring the activities and achievements of the project).
