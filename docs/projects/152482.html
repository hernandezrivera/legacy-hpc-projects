Project Summary

The Gender Based Violence project is targeting 36,415 vulnerable IDPs and host communities Lake state Yirol East county to reduce suffering of women, girls, men and boys.Which will contribute to the SO1: Save lives by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs among the most vulnerable women, men, girls and boys ,SO2: Protect vulnerable women, men, girls and boys through provision of specialized and integrated services and SO3: Support at-risk communities to promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks in line with the cluster objective C.O.1: Provide immediate lifesaving assistance to address priority protection needs for the most vulnerable populations in hard-to-reach areas,C.O.2: Prevent, mitigate and respond to protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience and C.O.3 Enable durable solution for IDPs and other affected populations the following activities will be strengthened in this project through Training of protection committees on Comprehensive case management and Psychosocial support (PSS) to strengthen Safe referrals, including information dissemination on available services,Women & girls friendly spaces programming will .also be supported including strengthened integration with child protection to support child survivors, supporting of the Legal awareness, assistance and counseling for survivors (including training police and justice authorities) , Community-based protection (activities may include: Establishment of women's safety committees; Strengthening participation of women’s groups and women-lead organizations in protection coordination structures) will be promoted and also Community awareness raising and sensitization on women’s rights, etc To strengthening Protective livelihood programming for women (example: safe alternative fuel projects) will be promoted and Programming targeting female and male youth as agents of change for gender equality (Activities targeting adolescent girls and young women (as priority): empowerment, self-esteem, rights awareness and collective power; Activities targeting male youth (transforming discriminatory attitudes and behaviors that contribute to inequality; negative masculinities, accountable practices, women's rights); Conducting gender analysis of needs and aspirations of female and male youth in local community will be strengthened, to support Peace building and peaceful co-existence (activities include: Capacity building local women's groups/women's organizations and women leaders on mediation and peaceful resolution of conflicts; Involving women in decision-making in Community Dispute Resolution, conflict mitigation, mediation & peace building; Conduct gender analysis of community relations and norms affecting peaceful co-existence)initiatives will be promoted and leverage on the already existing WAV Supporting and strengthening women Peace initiatives at the grassroots project in the area.

Needs

In recent years, Lakes State has experienced a great deal of violent conflict that has caused degenerated stability and rampant instability issues. Majority of the State’s inhabitants are displaced and in constant fear of violence and disease. Due to the constant upheaval, the capacity of the local social and economic infrastructure is one of the lowest in the country. Furthermore, Lakes State has been subjected to numerous displacements which are caused by conflicts of inter and intra-communal violence from armed non state actors and tensions between authorities and the youths such as revenge killing, land disputes, clan fighting, cattle raiding and many more making women, children, elderly and PSNs victims of circumstances. The May 2018 joint inter-agency needs assessment exercise updates continue to highlight the escalation insecurity and sexual violence and other forms GBV against women and adolescent girls are becoming wide spread due to continued conflict and displacements, in the past one month, an alarming number of Pakam people of Rumbek North County have had to abandon their homes and livelihoods as a result of communal fights with Gok of Gok State, Luany-jang and Rek of Warrap State, Rup of Rumbek centre of Western Lakes and Nuer of Unity State over cattle as their main source of livelihood. This situation has intensified with the recent ongoing disarmament in the greater lakes state where government forces went to their area and burnt down all the houses and destroyed their household properties which forced the Pakam community to leave their area and relocate for resettlement in Yirol East. The displaced settle in 3 sites of Wun-theep,Wun-Tit, Rual-meei payams deeply in the bush not accessible. The populations have moved to Pagarau and Nyang payams where most of them put up with host communities to share shelter key concerns including displacements of populations in Yirol East. This recent conflict- affected displacements worsens the existing displacements due to inter-communal conflicts which had displaced over 36,415 people October 2018 .Through GBV programming WAV has noticed increasing and targeted attack of with existing and growing levels of sexual violence perpetrated .This is linked to the escalating insecurity militarization of existing and mobilization groups and instability caused by violent cattle raiding. adolescent girls are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information and experience growing levels of sexual violence, abductions ,sexual harassment ,early and forced marriages sexual exploitation and abuse and restriction s paced on their movements by their families .Through this project GBV interventions atleast 30% targeted beneficiaries through PSS and lifeskills will be adolescent girls .According to lakes September updates ,conflict affected communities continue to have cattle confiscated or looted by state security actors. This depletion of assets is significant for the county falling into the IPC 4 categor.The cattle camp youth in 2 of panyijiar and Yirol East counties staged inter and intra-clan conflicts that have resulted into 50 deaths m and thousand wounded .The conflicts were then followed by revenge killings, arrests ,detentions and robbery along the main roads of Yirol East and West. The situation is compounded with the recent fighting and clashes. Responding to this new crisis requires targeted emergency response outside the current WAV 3 months RRF program in and Yirol East is currently responding to the needs with support from IOM however there is need to extend to extend this respond quickly and effectively to ,emerging crisis and will provide emergency GBV prevention and protection responses to the Yirol East over six month period.

Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Collection and supply of fuel, energy and natural resources is associated with a variety of health and protection risks, including SGBV. These activities and associated risks are highly gendered. In Yirol East , activities linked to the household – including water and firewood collection – are considered to be “women’s work”. As a result, women and children are usually the most affected by all aspects related to fuel, energy and natural resources. deforestation, which is common in many areas the IDPs, forces women and girls to walk even longer distances thereby increasing the risk even further. Women and girls are also be at risk of violence by members of the host community who are suffering from the strain on their natural resources, in order to discourage the displaced communities to collect water, wood, animal fodder, thatching When availability of firewood or other forms of fuel is limited, in the area of Nyang and Pagarau payams where firewood has been depleted, households must purchase fuel Women and girls in households that cannot afford to buy firewood or fuel and are compelled to engage in survival sex in order to ensure that their families have access to food this is based on the protection risk analysis done during the County consultative meeting in September 2018 however this will be addressed
