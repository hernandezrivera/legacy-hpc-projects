Project Summary

The project aims to improve the infrastructure in the city of Grapples by rehabilitating the old damaged sewage network with new network with a length of 5285 m. capable of absorbing and discharging sewage water for the people of Jarablus, 65250 between the displaced and host. thus alleviating the suffering of the population by reducing the resulting diseases from pollution drinking water and agricultural lands. SAED will be committed to the humanitarian standards and criteria of SPHERE and the Syrian code in the implementation in such engineering projects.

Needs

Due to displacement waves that came to North Syria and to Jarablus city in North-west Aleppo countryside, which turned into a safe area after the increased population there. This unplanned increment in addition to military clashes have affected the drainage system and large parts of it went inoperable (70% as per the study SCA team has conducted) SAED team has conducted a rapid needs assessment of the damage caused by weak drainage system, the result was as following:1 - The records of hospitals and medical points showed increased number of children infected with diarrhoea as a result of pollution, as only in Aug, 2018 number of cases examined in health centre was: 140 boys, 189 girls 60 men, and 47 females. Diseases come by drinking water which is contaminated while sewages water since sewage drainage is damaged. This results in contamination of crops, and because of soil pollution from sewage, that led to many families' loss of livelihoods in agricultural work. In addition to the spread of mosquitoes and epidemics, including Leishmania, cases of infection recorded in public hospitals in the city, 230 cases of boys, 197 cases of girls, 73 men and 54 women. Also, odors emitted from the sewage affects people who suffer from respiratory diseases. Cases recorded of suffocation in health centers, hospitals received more than 25 cases of respiratory congestion among the local population. As the results showed a decrease in population capacity in the movement and access especially "women and the elderly" because of sewage floats during rain. It was also noted that the number of students who go to schools decreased during rain.

Implementation of activities

Implementation Activities:- Coordination with main stakeholder in the project and taking opinions of beneficiaries by conducting FGDs for women and men separately taking into consideration the people with disabilities and elderly to take their experiences in account to get the best ways to implement the project and to measure the expected results. - Conducting field needs assessments to the drainage network to determine type and size of required intervention to make list of quantities. - Contracting with experienced contractors according to SCA policies and putting the technical specifications and ensure safety and security measures especially GBV ones. - Implementing the rehabilitation and maintenance operations on 5 levels: 1. Maintenance for main pipe-line in the city with diameter of 80 CM and length of 1000 M and depth of 200 CM and width of 160 CM. 2. Maintenance and replacement of the secondary line of the dimensions of: 60 cm diameter, length of 1100 m, depth of 160 Cm and width of 100 Cm. 3. Maintenance and replacement of the second secondary line of the dimensions of: 50 cm diameter, length of 1630 m, depth of 160 Cm and width of 100 Cm. 4. Maintenance and replacement of the third secondary line of the dimensions of: 50 cm diameter, length of 1020 m, depth of 140 Cm and width of 80 Cm. 5. Maintenance and replacement of the forth secondary line of the dimensions of: 30 cm diameter, length of 535 m, depth of 120 Cm and width of 60 Cm. - After finishing maintenance operations, ports of water drainage shall be monitored in sewage holes points to see the quantities of water flow. After that, sewage holes shall be closed with metal caps so project comes to be in use and real investment. - Monitoring and Evaluation: it includes the monitoring and evaluation of daily activities of maintenance and rehabilitation activities in addition to employees', contractors, and workers' behavior with protection principles agreed in the signed contracts with them especially (child protection and GBV).outcome 1Helping the people of Jarablus to get rid of the sewage and reduce the epidemics causing by it.Output 1Replace the old sewage network with new one with various diameters and length of 5285 m

Protection Risk Analysis

Category: Beneficiaries are at additional risks. Risk: - Children and elderly people are subject to risks of falling in the holes during implementing the activities. Mitigation- Putting marks with warning signs that can be understood by illiterate (sketches). - Coordination with local authorities to close roads while implementing. Risk: Beneficiaries are at risk of contamination during implementation and the draining systems stay open for long times. Mitigation: 1. Dividing work area to smaller sectors and finishing each sector during a small period. 2. Raising awareness for populations about risks of exposing to contaminated water or extra use of water during work. Category: Beneficiaries are at risk of physical and psychological harm: Risk: Beneficiaries, especially children and elderly, are at risk of being exploited or to GBV by workers, contractors, etc. Mitigation: Contractors, workers, and employees shall sign Code of Conduct of Saed Charity in addition to including a category in the contract that force the contractor to commit Child protection and GBV issues. SCA project employees, especially M&E worker shall monitor workers during implementing and respond to any complaints in this relation. Risk: Survivors from GBV violations are there. Mitigation: In case of discovering any, the case shall be dealt with secretly and referring it to service providers in the area (there are a centre for Ihsan Organization there). Category: Affected population are not encouraged to participate and express their opinion Risk:Affected population, especially women, to participate and express their opinion to complain. Mitigation: 1. There shall be a female employee in the project to ensure wider participation of women in the project and alleviate community social restraints that hinge women to participate. 2. There shall be a complaining box in the local council building, and SCA M&E employee only open it. 3. Brouchors shall be printed and distributed in public places that include information about the project and ways of complaining in addition to notes and phone number to receive complaints available with M&E officer.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks of the project: 1. Not giving the permission to implement the project from Local council and Turkish AFAD there. Solution: coordination with local council and AFAD from the first beginning of the project, also they are included in the project design phase. 2. Raise in prices of materials in local markets. Solution: Procurement shall be done for one time whether from Syria or Turkey. 3. Contractor's cannot fulfill his/her commitments. Solutions: there shall be a contract to implement activities with more than one contractor at the same time
