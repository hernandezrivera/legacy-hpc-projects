Project Summary

HACI seeks to respond to the protection needs of men,boys, girls and women in 3 counties of Baliet, Tonj, North and Tonj South within a period of one year with a budget of $ 200,000 as per the GBV sub cluster Objectives contributing to the 2020 HNO objectives . This project is targeting most vulnerable individuals 8000 (2500 girls,500 boys, 4500 women and 500 men). These 3 counties appear on the HNO 2020 severity map and among counties which are hard to reach and greatly under-served with no GBV services but with huge needs attributed to the 2016 conflict which caused massive displacements, death , loss of livelihoods and property .The project will focus on the needs of women, girls , men and boys who are returnees, IDPS, host community at risk of sexual and Gender Based violence and other protection threats like abduction, torture and detention without trial and who face elevated risks of GBV attributed to food insecurity and loss of livelihoods, floods, social status and economic decline. This project aims at addressing these shocks by providing comprehensive and integrated GBV services in coordination with other sectoral partners in WASH, CCM, Education, FSL, S/NFI and Health. HACI will work closely with all relevant stakeholders in the referral pathway to provide life-saving GBV prevention and response services by making sure that frontline services providers have the competence and skills to professionally and ethically serve the population in need . The case workers will undergo a refresher training on case management and PSS to quip hem with skills for provision of Comprehensive case management services and referrals for both adult and child survivors with critical protection concerns. Other frontline workers like thee CBPNs, GBV community mobilizers will be trained on protection risk assessments an early warning mechanisms PFA and PSS to better provide Individual and group Psychosocial Support (PSS) activities; awareness raisings campaigns using EMAP and Strengthening of community-based protection mechanisms to prevent , mitigate and respond to GBV /protection concerns . HACI will also establish 3 Women and Girl friendly spaces which will serve as entry points to providing quality and timely case management , information sharing and Income Generating activities/livelihoods and appropriate referrals targeting women and girls in the three counties .HACI will build upon existing efforts and target vulnerable populations, while coordinating closely with the GBV Sub-Cluster and other partners implementing this project and ensure that the standard IASC guidelines for prevention and response services are adhered to and integration of GBV services is guided by IASC standards for each cluster.
In addition, HACI, will adopt an integrated approach towards GBV risk mitigation and ensure that other multi sectoral partners, community leaders and line ministries coordinate activities in relation to GBV risk mitigation with a focus on behavior change to address negative social norms that undermine women and girls and increasing access to livelihood opportunities for durable solutions. HACI outreach team will ensure that communities participate equally in project activities in line with AAP guidelines, Centrality of protection principles and are enlightened on feedback and complaint mechanisms including modes for reporting SEA. The organization will work with CCM,WASH, Health, FSL and Education partners to provide integrated services in the 3 counties.

1 - Needs

UNFPA Study of 2018 indicate that 65 percent of women and girls have experienced physical and/or sexual violence in their lifetime.In addition harmful traditions and patriarchy undermines the social status of women and girls in warrap and Upper Nile counties these include, celebration of adolescent girls at the onset of first menses, forced child marriages and wife inheritance. Decision making at all levels of the society lies on men and women have little control over their freedom of movement ,access to health care , livelihoods and property. There is need to coordinate with other protection partners to engage men and boys through EMAP on the rights of women and girls and stopping the harmful traditional practices that condone GBV and reinstate livelihoods for women and girls .Seasonal flooding that started in June 2019 have displaced thousands of men, boys women and girls with 100 % destruction of livelihoods, roads, communication systems and shelters. A total of 364,600 flood affected people are in Warrap State and about 300,000 people are in Upper Nile state (OCHA situation report ,October 2019.) Flooding increases the risk of GBV including sexual violence and negative coping mechanisms like transactional sex as women and girls often, lose livelihoods, shelters and lack basic needs. Conflict, floods, economic decline are all stressful situations which increases vulnerabilities of women and girls and dependency on humanitarian aid which has lately seen women and girls as victims of SEA. There is need to orient frontline service providers on PFA/PSS , PSEA and train service providers to identify mental health cases and other needs for specialized services.HACI’s FGDs with adolescent boys and girls in June 2019 ,revealed that boys and girls do not have equal access to education as girls are viewed to be better fit as brides and boys are encouraged to accumulate enough cows in order to marry at a young age. This has led to increased drop outs of both boys and girls from schools with boys engaging more in cattle raiding to acquire cattle used as bride price. There is need to conduct comprehensive case management , GBV prevention messaging targeting adolescent boys and girlsREACH assessment report of March 2019 , says that targeted attacks against women and girls are linked to the escalating insecurity, militarization of political leadership, mobilization of armed groups and instability caused by violent cattle raiding especially in Baliet, Tonj East and North .; sexual violence acts are very common but also under reported . women and girls who are survivors of rape often harbor thoughts of suicide when rape results into pregnancy : There is need to coordinate with health partners to ensure GBV survivors access CMR services timely . HACI will also engage in PSS activities and engage community mobilizers to conduct anti-stigma campaigns on children born out of rape, rape surviors and adolescent girls . According to IOM DTM report of march 2019, returnees continue to over stretch local resources in areas where the host communities are food insecure. Tonj North population are food insecure with a total of 17,400 people while in Tonj East 15,500 people affected. (IPC figures September 2019). HACI seeks to respond to these needs through working together with other multi-sectoral partners to provide quality GBV services and GBV risk mitigation strategies in WASH, Health, CM, Education, S/NFI and FSL and ensure men, women, boys and girls are at the center of the response participating in all the stages of the project cycle in line with AAP guidelines and centrality of protection .

2 - Activities

Conduct comprehensive case management targeting both adult and child survivors using the IASC guidelines on case management. Engage community based protection network members to provide community based psychosocial support and Group PSS at payam level and at household level Conduct awareness raising and community outreach(dialogue) on available services and use of referral pathway including access to multi-sectoral services. Train Frontline workers on case management , PFA/PSS , GBV guiding principles and safe referrals  Conduct assessment for livelihood support and income generating activities including referrals to livelihood opportunities targeting vulnerable women and girls. Train women and girls on life skills training/IGA of their interest and provide direct support to their livelihood options through monitoring , referrals and technical input. Establish and strengthen community based structures like CBPN teams, Women support groups and state female paralegal associations. Strengthen referral mechanism in Baliet, Tonj North and Tonj East to facilitate timely access to services  Engage men and boys in dialogue about GBV prevention at community level in order to promote GBV prevention strategies and behaviour change to promote women and girls rights.

6.6 - Will beneficiaries' rights be legally recognised?

The project will implement activities using a human rights-based approach, considering gender balance and gender equality, and the rights of the child. Protection mainstreaming will be included throughout the project design and will be observed and monitored during implementation.

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

The affected population will not be forced to participate in GBV activities because this is their basic human right but will be given opportunity where possible to express opinions about desirable improvements, prioritizing goals and negotiating with different agencies involved in the GBV Sub-Cluster ,criticizing plans, raising awareness in the community about GBV, establishing or supporting organization structures within the community, participating in trainings to enhance communication and psychosocial skills and contributing directly to supporting survivors of GBV.

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

The project will work with various stakeholders including leaders of Payams, different key government ministries like the Ministry of Health, Finance and Economic Planning, Interior, Justice, and UN agencies like UNMISS, UNICEF,UNHCR,WHO, IOM among others focusing on interventions like meeting humanitarian needs, enhancing stability and building national ownership with specific focus on saving lives, providing dignified living conditions, and reducing vulnerability through comprehensive response that incorporates Camp Coordination and Camp Management,Shelter/Non-food items and Health

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

The project aligns with South Sudan’s National Development Strategy (2019-2021). It addresses one of the 6 National Development Strategy deliverables of strengthening social services and also aligns toward achieving Sustainable Development Goal 16- Peace, Justice and strong institutions and also the the United Nations Cooperation Framework(UNCF) Priority Area IV of Empowering Women and Youth.The South Sudan Regional Refugee Response Plan 2019-2020 is also a key document that seeks to mount a regionally coherent inter-agency response supported by host governments in five countries of asylum, including Ethiopia, Uganda, The Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC), Kenya, and Sudan, over the next two years.

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

The project will take into account women’s dignity and security during service provision to reduce possible risks of fear of not using the services . During the project cycle, the project team will conduct focus group discussions to make sure that views and special needs of females, children and persons with disabilities are incorporated in project activities, which will improve services effectiveness, timeliness, usage of services, items and beneficiary participation. The project will implement activities using a human rights-based approach, considering gender balance and gender equality, and the rights of the child. Gender mainstreaming will be included throughout the project design and will be observed and monitored during implementation.

6.4 - Does the project include sustainability/handover?

Partners on ground will be involved in monitoring for continuous review and gaps will be discussed jointly and addressed in order to meet goal of the project. Key community members will be involved during joint monitoring of the activities; and Focused Group Discussions will be held at project site before and during the project hand over to the community authority for sustainability.
