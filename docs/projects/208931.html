Project Summary

The escalation of conflict in Sudan has caused the destruction of civilian infrastructure and has forced people to flee their homes seeking safety, protection and assistance. Despite the recently declared ceasefires, hostilities between the SAF and the RSF have persisted since 15 April 2023.
Almanar aims to address life-saving and life-sustaining protection needs among crisis affected families, while also enhancing the capacity of stakeholders to strengthen the protective environment through provision of protection, child protection and GBV services in Khartoum, Al Jazeira, Gedarif, White Nile, South Kordofan, West Kordofan, East Darfur states. Almanar will ensure engaging men, women, girls and boys, older people, disable PSNs ensuring the full and equal respect for the rights of all individuals, IDPs, Refugees and host communities, regardless of age, gender, ethnic, social, religious or other background in the activities.
General protection activities will be presented through legal aid assistance, activate referral pathways to receive needed support, Establishment of gender balanced CBPCs and train them on protection essential functions, GBV and PSS.
Conduct protection monitoring will be conducted and advocacy interventions will be supported through organizing networking meetings and forums to discuss community issues related to protection and seek possible solutions with decision makers. Also, awareness sessions will be held in addition to capacity building trainings for community members and technical staff.
Under GBV SS, Almanar will rehabilitate and operationalize women centers to be utilized as a safe space. Community Based Committees will be established and trained on GBV principles and standards. Women & girls will be supported with startup capital for Income Generating Activities and vocational skills, Women empowerment/life skills training, case management, consultation and psychosocial support in addition to raise their knowledge through awareness raising campaigns on GBV related issues, psychosocial support and first aid, GBV survivors referral pathway, legal aid/awareness and gender. CMR Training will be provided targeting health providers in addition to PSEA training to project staff & suppliers on PSEA basic concepts.
For CP, Almanar plan to run Child Friendly spaces/ multi-service centers and equip them with needed furniture, stationaries and equipment. Girls & boys who have experienced Gender based violence, abuse, and neglect will be provided with needed services according to their needs, will be registered for Case Management and/or referral and will provide them with legal assistance in detention. Capacity building training will be done targeting men and women on child protection and the minimum standards.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

One of the most significant factors, which is increasingly recognized as an integral component of UN programming, is the gender equality and protection of girls and women. Inequalities between the sexes typically reflect, and can increase, vulnerabilities of young girls, teens, may face different protection risks, have different needs and choices, and possess different skills, knowledge and coping strategies. The project activities, will aim to ensure community participation through the project cycle, capacity and vulnerability analysis, reduce the level of exploitation to women and promote protection of girls in communities and families with the support of legal and traditional protection systems. The project will use and advocate for Minimum Standards of Protection package amongst the targeted communities throughout the project implementation process. This project through the referral pathway will ensure that women, girls and boys victims of S/GBV, elderly, disables and their families and guardians are fully aware of available services around them. Almanar will ensure that the service locations are safe and accessible to all without discrimination, and ensure that the beneficiaries receiving services in dignity and confidentiality.
