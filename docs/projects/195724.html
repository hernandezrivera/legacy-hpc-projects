3 - Needs

Heavy fighting in Raqqa countryside and before in Raqqa has led to the displacement of large numbers of civilians to camps and more stable areas, while some of them have returned to their main areas of residence despite the poor infrastructure, and many families have repeatedly been displaced to escape the fighting and its effects.This has affected the increased pressure on schools run by self-administration, which has had a negative impact on the overall educational situation due to crowded classes and neglect of the age, gender, and disability in learning spaces and schools which came out of service during the war. In addition to the poverty and the lack of awareness of the importance of education.All these circumstances led to a delay in the academic and cognitive levels of many students.School closure in NES as a part of the precautionary measures tocounter the Covid-19 pandemic several times has affected children cognitively. Dropping out might have caused an increase in child marriage and prevented girls from their right to education.

4 - Implementation of activities

SMART Center aims to respond to educational needs in accordance with the QBMs.Through this project, the SMART Center will provide non-formal education and psychosocial support services to children (about 600 students) in Karama sub-district.Therefore, it will rehabilitate two schools in the targeted area, build capacity for teachers, and train them on CSG and SIR topics. It will also carry out back to schools campaigns, as well as raise awareness about the importance of personal hygiene.Each student will receive a schoolbag with complete stationery plus a PSS kit and several hygiene Kits.

* Protection Risk: cholera / Covid-19. likelihood: High. Impact: High. Acceptable Level of Risk: acceptable. * Protection Risk: curfews and school closures. likelihood: Medium. Impact: High.Mitigation measures: Implementing activities online. Monitoring: Follow-up decisions issued by the competent authorities. Resources required: Training of staff on online teaching. Acceptable Level of Risk: acceptable. * Protection Risk: Outbreak of internal hostilities. likelihood: Low. Impact: High. Mitigation measures: Suspension of activities.
