Plan fields

1 - Needs

Prior to the explosion, “the number of surveyed households relying on less than 200USD per month in the Greater Beirut area increased from 44 to 86%”. The COVID-19 lockdown overlapped with a prolonged economic crisis leading the International Labour Organization (ILO) to estimate that 41% of small businesses surveyed as part of a rapid assessment on the impact of the COVID-19 lockdown are expecting to survive the current crisis. The damages sustained in the impacted areas has led to significant loss to individuals and business owners alike.

2 - Activities

Crisis affected individuals will receive a one-time distribution $2,000 to rehabilitate their damaged business. Crisis affected individuals will receive MPCA to address acute loss of income. Crisis affected individuals will participate in cash for work (CfW) activities to help with the recovery efforts in Beirut as well as to supplement a loss of income as a result of the explosion. MPCA will be given to vulnerable households affected by the Beirut explosion, who have lost their source of income.
