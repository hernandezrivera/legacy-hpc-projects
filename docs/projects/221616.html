Project Summary

CSI WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) integrated Project interventions, aim to improve access to clean water, adequate sanitation, and proper hygiene practices, particularly in vulnerable and underserved communities. These interventions address critical public health issues, prevent waterborne diseases, and enhance overall well-being.
Summary of the Intervention:
Purpose: The primary goal of WASH interventions is to ensure the availability of safe drinking water, promote hygiene education, and provide access to proper sanitation facilities. This helps reduce disease transmission, improve health outcomes, and support sustainable development. WASH programs often integrate with broader health, education, and emergency response creativities.
Key Objectives:
1.To Improve Access to Safe and Sustainable Water Sources; By Develop, rehabilitate, and maintain water supply systems to provide clean, reliable water for drinking, cooking, and other household needs, and ensure water quality through regular testing and treatment, reducing waterborne diseases.
2.To enhance Sanitation Infrastructure and Practices through Construction and rehabilitation of sanitation facilities such as latrines and waste management systems to eliminate open defecation that promote proper waste disposal and sustainable sanitation solutions to maintain a healthy environment.
3.To Promote Hygiene Awareness and Behavioral Change by Conducting hygiene education campaigns on handwashing, personal hygiene, and menstrual hygiene management, which Foster long-term behavioral change to reduce preventable diseases through community-led awareness initiatives.
4.To Build Resilience and Strengthen Community Capacity through training of local communities to manage, operate, and maintain WASH infrastructure, ensuring sustainability, and Empowering women and youth as leaders in WASH activities to promote inclusion and long-term resilience.
Target Beneficiaries are;
1. Vulnerable Populations of women, children, and individuals in rural and urban slum areas with limited access to clean water and sanitation.
2. Floods affected Communities: Host community, internally displaced persons (IDPs), and the returnees.
3. Schools and Health Facilities. This project will ensure that access to WASH infrastructure in learning and healthcare environments is prioritized.
4. CSI project will response to 14,217 Households in Aweil South County, equal to 85,302 individuals.

1. Needs

It was on 26th July,2024 the deputy RRC coordinator Aweil South shared a reported through IRC to UNOCHA reporting number of destructions by the above normal rainfall water flooding that affect all the eight Payams in Aweil South County with 124 villages affected in 21 Bomas with 1120 households affected and 383 households displaced to the safer sites (highland areas) and neighborhoods the reports show total individual 7,840 were affected according to the report. However, with response, from UNOCHA requesting inter-agencies repaid assessment in the flood affected areas, For Afrika coordinated the flood assessment planning meeting with all the partners operating in and outsides Aweil South County which was held on Friday 9th August at the county headquarter (Malek Allel). The initial Rapid need assessment carried out by partners were able to verified and estimate total number of affected households 14,217 (85,302 individuals) by flood in the whole county. Among this affected population there are returnees who returned from Sudan civil war and intergraded within communities. 6 Payams out of 8 are severely affected in term of crops Wathmuok, Panthou, Tarweng, Ayai, Gakrol and Nyoc Awany. The most vulnerable groups affected by the disaster are children, pregnant and lactating mothers, old aged persons and people with disabilities. In according to the IRNA report conducted in August,2024, here are the CSI project needs. Construct the boreholes in all need areas. Rehabilitation of the broken borehole in across the affected areas.Construction of temporal toilets in public places in both residential areas and displacement Site. Provision of water containers, chlorine tablets and filters to internally Displace Persons IDPs, Returnees and the Host Community.Procurement and distribution of Menstrual Hygiene management MHM dignity kits to adolescent girls and young women. Installation of handwashing facilities in schools, churches, health facilities, residential areas and displacement sites.Procurement and distribution of sanitary kits for women and girls.Provision of soap for basic hygiene maintenance.
