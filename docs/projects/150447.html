Project Summary

This project aims at providing medical and rehabilitation services to newly injured people, people with
new disabilities after Mass Demonstrations and PWDs who live in ARAs, the service is provided by 4
well-trained outreach home care teams in different Gaza Strip governates, each team includes; A
Physician, A physiotherapist, A nurse, An occupational therapist and a psychologist). Teams also will
provide in-house trainings for the families of patients and PWDs on home care basics and dressing. The
project also includes providing assistive devices, medical kits and hygiene kits to target group. The
project also contains a referral system to assist patients in need for further medical treatments.

Needs

According to the statistics of Health Cluster in their latest situational report on 22th September, a total of 6,702 limb injuries arrived to emergency departments in referral hospitals since 30th of March 2018, from them are 81% lower limbs causalities and 19% upper limb causalities according to the statistics of MOH in 8th September.According to Health Cluster statistics (22th September), about 1,200 injured people from the limb causalities will need limb reconstruction, and will require up to 7 surgeries and extensive rehabilitation and treatment for up to 2 years. The total number of amputations was 77, including 14 children and 1 female, out of this total, 69 were lower limb amputations and 8 upper limb amputations, the total number ofpatients with paralysis due to spinal cord injury was 12, and those types of disabilities need professional intervention by experienced rehabilitation teams.Patients are discharged early every week as the hospitals cannot receive them for a sufficient period because of the high pressure on hospitals and to make room for a new wave of expected casualties. According to Health Cluster statistics on 22th September approximately 700 patients have been discharged early since the 30th March. Most of these patients need continuous medical care to avoid any advancedeffects that may led to amputations. Moreover, hospitals and health centers do not provide patients with the rehabilitation services.Based on the problem and causes tree analyses in addition to the National Center experience in providing home care services for almost 20000 people with disability (PWD) on the past 18 years, people with physical disability already suffer from acute shortage on rehabilitation and care services, especially medical and psychological rehabilitation. It is important that comprehensive services are provided to PWDs (Right holders) as part of the humanitarian response. In addition to acute shortage on the availability of the assistive devices such as Wheelchairs, Crutches etc. All this caused them serious health complications and made them more introverted in the society, in addition to their inability to live a normal life.PWD who are living on border areas are more exposed to be in danger, besides, their families are very poor and have not enough awareness and knowledge on how to deal with their children and cannot afford buying assistive devices and paying transportations to them, so that their access to health services is very restricted.The families of PWDs and injured need also to be practically trained about the basics of home care; in order to be able to provide medical care needed to maintain their children psychological and health stability at the end of this project. In addition, the target group needs occupational therapy, which help them to adapt to the environment around them in a better way, they need to adapt home facilities, tools and furniture to facilitate their life.

Activities

-Implement 4 needs assessment focus groups with participation of beneficiaries, Households & Community Figures.-Carry out 2 meetings with Health Centers & hospitals near targeted areas to recognize the kind of services they provide, in addition to determining target group needs, also to agree on setting up a referral system.-Implement Home Care Evaluation Visits for 600 vulnerable injured people & persons with new disabilities (amputations and paralysis) because of Mass Demonstrations, in addition to PWDs living in ARAs. After evaluation teams will choose 400 cases according to NCCR’s pre-set criteria. Opening initial files for cases baseline assessment & setting up case management plans & their needs from assistive devices & medical supplies. Note: The NCCR home care team adopts a multidisciplinary approach in providing services. teams meet regularly to discuss the care plan & activities given to the patients & their families. This meeting is registered in an MDO file that stands for multidisciplinary organization, where every member writes down their input for follow up.-Provide Medical & rehabilitation Services for 400 Injured and PWD from both genders, also the project will provide Occupational Therapy services for almost 100 PWD from the targeted group. Those multidisciplinary services will be implemented by four highly experienced home care teams in different governates of Gaza strip where every team consists of (a physician, a physiotherapist, a psychologist, occupational therapist & a nurse), the work of these teams is technically monitored by an expert supervisor.Contribution: this activity will contribute to the needs of target group by providing primary health care for injured & secondary rehabilitation services for PWDs (amputation injuries & paralysis), especially those who were discharged early every week to make room for a new wave of expected casualties, outreach team will solve the problem of accessibility & lower the economic burden on their families.-Provide in house training & psychological support services for 800 care givers from the target group families, training will be about the basics of home care services, as part of preparedness for emergencies & also to sustain the services for the PWD who needs intensive care; such as (Basic physiotherapy home exercises & dressing of injuries and bedsores) training will be carried out by home care teams as part of home care activities.Contribution: this activity will contribute to the needs of target group by providing their families with trainings on basics of home care & dressings, those families are very vulnerable & poor so that those trainings will raise their awareness & speed the healing process of their children.-Provide Assistive Devices e.g. wheelchairs, crutches, etc., for almost 100 from the targeted group.Contribution: this activity will contribute to the needs of target group by continuing the rehabilitation process to promote their opportunities for independent living, achievement of their individual life goals, & inclusion in the community.-Provide 200 Medical kits and 200 hygiene kits for injured people and PWDs.Contribution: this activity will contribute to the needs by solving the problem of acute shortage in medical disposables & also lower the economic burden of buying it on families of patients and PWDs. Also, by providing patients with medical kits & hygiene kits will help speeding the healing process.-Refer 100 cases in need for immediate medical intervention by equipment that are not available with the outreach teams especially physiotherapy equipment & minor surgeries, project will cover the transportation cost for cases to assure their affordability & accessibility to health services.-Conduct 8 M&E meetings with the participation of a number of beneficiaries & care givers; to find out the level of satisfaction from the services they received, if they have any feedback, in addition to setting a reachable Complaints system.
