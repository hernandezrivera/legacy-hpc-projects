Project Statement
In August 2022 join humanitarian agencies team conducted assessment to people affected by flooding caused by torrential rain estimated around (1150 households) that on 4 to 5 August 2017, according to the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC), of whom 5,000 are internally displaced persons (IDPs)living in Kalma IDP camp and 2,406 are IDPs in AlsalamIDP camp. A team comprising of HAC, WES, AOSCD and NCA conducted a rapid needs assessment in Alsalam and Kalma camps, which indicates that an estimated 480 shelters were damaged. Alsalam camp, about 17 km, north Nyala Town, South Darfur and about 13,710 IDPs live in Alsalam camp, individual (2,406) and some of them in need to provision of shelter and basic NFIs to the most critical priority for families to settle in the community in a conducive environment, therefore, are vulnerable families were identified who desperately require emergency support of shelter materials, shelter repair and NFIs.

Activities proposed:
Activity 1.1.1: Identify IDPs in need of life saving ES and NFI by conducting assessments and verifications.
Activity 1.1.2: Procure shelters (locally available construction materials such as reed, bamboo sticks (karanik3 x2.5m).
Activity 1.1.3: Forming NFIs committee & conduct training 10 committees' members with training on distribution of NFIs managing, gender issues and community governance method includes all segments of community.
Activity 1.1.4: Conduct registrations and distributions of shelter and/or nonfood items.
Activity 1.1.5: Storage and handling of transport 450 emergency shelter kits & distribution of ES/NFIs for the IDPs in Alsalam camp(250HHs).
Activity 1.1.6: Conduct combine GBV awareness campaign activities with shelter project to the target beneficiaries on GBV response
Priority selection criteria
The project will target all people affected by flooding with particular emphasis on households who have the following:
• Unaccompanied children
• Malnourished and/or sick children
• Pregnant and lactating women
• IDPs
• Refugees
• People with disabilities
• People with chronic illnesses
• Head of household who have lost their livehoods
• Women head of affected households
• Young women and girls
• Youth
