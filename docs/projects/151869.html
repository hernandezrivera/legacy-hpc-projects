Project Summary

The Project is mainly targeted towards out of school children ages 3 - 18 in the North East of Nigeria, mainly in Adamawa, Borno and Yobe States specifically targeting 30,000 out of school children. The project seeks to provide access to inclusive and protective learning including safe classrooms, with WASH facilities, equipped teachers and learning materials for conflict-affected children aged 3-17 and to Improve the ability of conflict-affected children aged 5-18 to cope in an emergency through relevant learning, life-skills and psychosocial first-aid and also to restore and protect learning in conflict affected states through community advocacy, mobilization and sensitization.

Needs

From the Joint Education Needs Assessment it has shown that there are over 1 million out of school children in northeastern Nigeria. This project will work to reduce this number of out of school children.
