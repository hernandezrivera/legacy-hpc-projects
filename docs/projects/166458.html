Project Summary

WFP aims to support the COVID-19 response in Cox's Bazar district through life-saving food assistance to individuals in isolation, quarantine, and treatment centres, and providing food and cash support to vulnerable Bangladeshi host communities throughout the district.

Objectives

Expand support to improve food security and compensate for loss of livelihoods of the most vulnerable Bangladeshis in Cox’s Bazar whose livelihoods will be impacted by the pandemic, through food and cash support according to need. Support the District health response in coordination with the Health Sector – provide support to existing and planned isolation, treatment, and quarantine facilities in Cox’s Bazar District

Needs

As the COVID-19 virus continues to spread in Bangladesh, it has led to acute humanitarian needs and economic distress to vulnerable groups that WFP currently supports in Cox’s Bazar district. Rohingya refugees and vulnerable host communities who are mainly daily wage earners and poorest of the poor in rural and urban districts of Cox`s Bazar, are disproportionately affected by this current crisis. Support to health facilities such as food provision in Cox's Bazar district with prevention and mitigation measures in place is essential to ensure potential health risks are mitigated and treatment is possible. Some of the most vulnerable groups in Cox's Bazar district, particularly the urban population who are mainly daily wage earners, are already impacted by the crisis. Loss of income for these groups will lead to the use of negative coping mechanisms. This coupled with rise in food prices caused by shortages in labour and transportation is endangering host community population's access to critical food.

Activities or outputs

Distribution of high energy biscuits, in-kind food items to complement GoB's rice distribution, and multi-purpose cash grant of BDT 4,500 Delivery of hot meals to individuals in quarantine centers Distribution of dry food rations to individuals in isolation centers Door to door delivery of food to vulnerable groups in homecare
