1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project's central objective is to deliver swift and sustainable WASH solutions while empowering affected communities with essential skills, fostering resilience, and enhancing their overall well-being. Through a holistic approach, the initiative addresses the critical needs of those forced to flee conflict or natural disasters, offering not just WASH but pathways for early recovery. The key target groups encompass displaced populations, returnees and in some cases the host community (for social cohesion purposes) with particular attention to vulnerable households including ones with persons with special needs (PWSN), women and children.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

SI presents a comprehensive WASH project designed to address critical needs and bolster community resilience in Cabo Delgado. The multifaceted intervention encompasses various components to ensure the delivery of essential services as part of an emergency response. In Mueda, in Palma, in Nangade and Muidumbe the project focuses on improving water services through the implementation of water trucking and network reinforcement, alongside the construction and rehabilitation of Rainwater Harvesting Systems (RWHS). SI will support families in constructing latrines and conduct extensive hygiene promotion activities to foster sustainable behavioral change. Recognizing the potential threat of cholera outbreaks, the project includes a prepositioned response mechanism for rapid deployment. Additionally, flexible interventions are planned, such as the distribution of survival dignity kits in response to new shocks, ensuring a nimble and adaptive approach to emerging needs. Emergency sanitation measures are a key component, involving the construction and decommissioning of emergency latrines and showers. SI remains flexible in adapting sanitation measures to address emerging challenges as they arise. Incorporating a holistic approach, in Mocimboa Da Praia the project also integrates WASH in Schools programs and provides Cash for Work (CFW) support for families to construct latrines and promote best hygiene practices within communities. Furthermore, SI plans to distribute WASH survival kits and flexible family kits to respond effectively to new shocks and emergencies. Through this comprehensive WASH initiative, SI aims to address immediate needs and fostering sustainable practices in the face of emergencies.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project prioritizes a comprehensive and proactive approach to identify and mitigate potential risks, particularly in the realm of protection. Anchored in a thorough protection risk analysis, among other approaches (lessons learnt from previous actions, observations) the project engages local communities actively, ensuring their voices shape interventions and addressing specific protection concerns. Protection risk assessments as part of the RRM rapid needs assessment tool will be conducted in the beginning of the project in the areas that have not been covered by RRM assessments before and will be updated regularly throughout the project's lifecycle, enabling dynamic adjustments to emerging risks and maintaining a responsive stance. Continuous monitoring and adaptive management mechanisms will allow for real-time feedback from the community on protection risks or timely reporting of cases of misconduct aiming to foster an environment of safety and swift corrective action when needed. The project's commitment extends to close coordination with local authorities, humanitarian agencies, and protection-focused organizations, fostering a collaborative environment for early risk identification and collective response.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Acknowledging the nuanced vulnerabilities and distinct needs of different genders and age groups, SI places paramount emphasis on fostering inclusivity, particularly for women, girls, and elderly individuals. Practical examples include the establishment of Women's and Elderly Committees within the community, providing dedicated platforms for these individuals to actively participate in decision-making processes related to project activities. These committees serve as forums where women and elderly persons can express their needs, contribute insights, and directly influence the design and implementation of interventions. The findings drive the design of adequate interventions that meet the needs and the preferences of the various concerned groups aiming at a safe, meaningful and inclusive service provision.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project prioritizes inclusivity and endeavours to ensure equitable access to assistance for individuals with disabilities. The project will begin with an inclusive needs assessment, actively engaging persons with disabilities and disability organisations to comprehensively understand the unique challenges faced by the most vulnerable groups. Collaboration with disability/protection organizations will serve as a cornerstone, tapping into their expertise (receiving feedback and lessons learnt) and help identify and reach individuals with disabilities effectively so that they can participate and contribute actively to the SI assessments. Based on the findings, the project will proactively adapt infrastructure, ensuring shelters and distribution points are accessible. Individualized assistance plans will be developed, tailoring support to the specific needs and preferences of individuals with disabilities. Families with members with disabilities will be targeted by both 1st and 2nd line activities as part of the vulnerability criteria.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

SI is committed to minimizing its environmental impact through a proactive and comprehensive approach. Prior to initiation, the project will conduct an Environmental Impact Assessment to identify and address potential risks, ensuring compliance with environmental considerations. Sustainability is at the forefront, with an emphasis on utilizing locally sourced and eco-friendly materials for construction. This approach will not only reduce the environmental footprint associated with material transportation but will also promote responsible resource management. The project will also actively engage local communities in awareness campaigns, fostering a sense of environmental stewardship and responsible natural resource use. Appropriate waste management practices will be integral to the project, with a focus on proper disposal and recycling/upcycling (when possible) to minimize environmental harm. Climate-resilient construction techniques are incorporated, considering the local climate and potential climate-related risks. Continuous monitoring and adaptive management mechanisms will allow for real-time adjustments to mitigate unforeseen environmental impacts, ensuring that the project will not only facilitate the recovery of displaced populations but will do so in harmony with the local environment.
