Project Summary

This project is developed in cooperation with the Republican Center of Protection of Mothers and Children and the acting "de facto" Minister of Health of Donetsk*. The AICM Foundation has many of its humanitarian activities for maternity hospitals. 37 maternities located in the conflict zone received our help in medical equipment, medicines, neonatal packs and others. We are in permanent contact with dozens of Gynecologists and Pediatricians located on both sides of the conflict zone. Several trainings has been carried out since 2018 for the early detection of certain newborn pathologies and it is obvious that the situation is increasingly critical for both pregnant women and their newborns in the non controlled territories. AICM Ukraine has the project to provide a critical and sustainable assistance to a large number of pregnant women living in the uncontrolled area of ​​the Donetsk Oblast via the hospitals with diagnostic medical devices, specific drugs, and life-saving equipment but also with some training sessions, and specific COVID-19 protection and equipment. Pregnant women living in NGCA are not only vulnerable during their pregnancy but they also present pathologies directly connected with the conflict and specific COVID-19 needs. Despite a relatively constant 8500/8700 yearly birth rate in territories not controlled by the Ukrainian government, post-natal mortality and congenital malformations are becoming increasingly important. Although statistics are difficult to verify, according to the majority of local doctors, pathological pregnancies are on the rise. Many women are in poor health conditions (due to rare medical follow-up, poor diet, pollution, working conditions) and under permanent psychological trauma and stress (due to the financial situation, the family, the conflict, an uncertain future, domestic violence, GBV...). Most of the pregnancies are also pathological because the chart of the ages for pregnant women is inversed compared to 2013. Tha majority is now composed of very early pregnancies (15-18 years due to lack of prevention, bad education, rapes,...) or very late (from 35 to 44 years due to the couples who waited the end of the conflict, problems of fertility). These pathological pregnancies need very strict medical monitoring. The financial situation of the hospitals makes them completely dependent on humanitarian aid, equipment is no longer maintained, and gynecological consultations are less frequent because of the availability of equipment. Concerning the people living in rural areas, the Primary Healthcare Units (called FAPs) located in villages, have been destroyed or are mostly deserted. Before the conflict, the FAP's were in charge of a large part of deliveries in rural areas. These pregnant women have now a limited access to the medical follow-up and are obliged or to give birth at home (which is unusual in Ukraine), or have to travel by Ambulance or in private car for several hours to the nearest hospital. With a correct portable echographic equipment the nearest hospitals should be able to perform medical consultations at home, but it's not possible today. To this must be added a significant number of pregnancies of HIV-positive women (between 800 and 900 in 2019 according to the AntiAids Donetsk center), sometimes also everything is complicated by a bad hygienic condition due to the access to water during their pregnancy. In addition, the arrival of the COVID-19 epidemic has further limited access because of COVID patients and infected medical staff. Thus, 5 perinatal centers had to be temporarily closed recently due to the existence of a COVID-cluster. A single center is specialized for COVID-19 pregnant women in Makiievka but with only 5 beds and very light equipment. Thus, this project includes also assistance to COVID-19 positive pregnant women through the provision of PPE. *Official letters of need of assistance are available upon request + support letter from administration.

1 - Needs

In the NGCA of Donetsk, the biggest number of delivery is located in one main Neonatal Hospital in Donetsk, 4 Regional Centres in large cities + 13 local departments of Maternity in cities' hospitals. Needs are identified continuously by our local team for 5 years who ensured the monitoring of the 16 AICM projects installed in these hospitals in the non-governmental areas but, unfortunately, largely insufficient. During training and on-site investigations, our specialists have identified serious problems in antenatal surveillance: Needs 1: Diagnosis materials are mostly out of order and those that work are not sufficient or poorly distributed in uncontrolled areas. Ultrasound equipment, even basic, is needed in 5 maternity wards, 12 laboratories for maternities face to lack reagents or spare parts, materials for clinical/presurgical investigations are almost absent in the 5 main hospitals, Needs 2 Emergency equipment (neonatal kit) and monitors are not sufficiently present in ambulances and intensive care units and 50 are needed. Neonatal resuscitation equipment is also very rare in 10 of the healthcare facilities. Needs 3 From the point of view of medical treatments: The AICM Foundation distributed 33600 preventive treatments to pregnant women in non-controlled areas in 2018/2020 to fight against anemia and other deficits. The results are very positive, and this operation will have to continue in 2021/2022 because Anemia is still a large problem for 30/40% of pregnant women. This part of the project is already funded by a US donor. But not for the specific medicines for early and late pregnancies proposed in this project. They are useful before the delivery, and during the lactating period. Needs 4 Similarly, hygiene packs included in this proposal for women and newborns should be systematically distributed to pregnant and lactating women during their consultations of monitoring. Needs 5 Special attention should be paid to HIV-positive pregnant women and women with other infectious diseases, not only with access to preventive care (specific antiviral medicines) but also with specific protection during the birth, Needs 6: training of 40 gynecologists on the specific diagnosis for congenital malformation such as heart, brain, digestive and uro genital system. Needs 7: In view of the COVID-19 epidemic, regular check-ups during pregnancy and childbirth are taking place under difficult conditions. Cesarean section is now systematic when mothers are diagnosed COVID positive or are suspected of being. Some examples of intrauterine contamination exist but fortunately very rare. On the other hand, there are many cases of mother-to-child transmission during the breastfeeding period. Once again, COVID-19 has very little effect on the newborn unless it is premature. Only 3 cases of newborn death connected with the COVID-19 have been recorded in Ukraine. COVID-19 has negative effects during pregnancy, but these effects are not sufficiently documented for the moment. A pregnant woman should be protected more if she caught COVID-19 during her pregnancy to avoid any problem for her during the birth (oxygen complement) or for the baby (if premature).

2 - Activities & Outcomes

Restore and/or rehabilitate disrupted health services and infrastructure (due to lack of maintenance), including for people with specific needs Outcome: Provide emergency and time-critical assistance to vulnerable people and ensure access to basic essential services to conflict-affected people with humanitarian needs, Output: Restoration and specific professional AICM assistance to medical services to prevent infant mortality and malformations in NGCA. After a detailed inventory and professional advice by the AICM reproductive health medical team. The activities are tailored to reply to the most urgent needs with robust and adequate equipment with the best value of money. Activity 1 Restoration and/or rehabilitation of disrupted health services and infrastructure with installation and training Activity 2 Procurement, pre-positioning, and distribution of medical supplies, essential medications, and equipment through established healthcare services Activity 3 Direct support to laboratories to improve availability, quality and safety standards to detect blood defect at the birth. Activity 4 Health care and nutrition education to improve and update the knowledge and skills of healthcare providers, especially about the connection between the pregnant women diet and the consequence for the newborn; 15 sessions of training gynecologists will present the best methods to detect congenital malformations. Detail of the activities: Project opened with the AICM procedures appropriate to provide equipment at the 17 places used by the right specialists. 11 ultrasound equipment 3 stationary, 8 mobiles, 5 system of diagnostic/endoscopy/little surgery will be provided to the 5 main hospitals, 80 neonatal kits and 25 monitors 15 foetal monitors. 10 Neonatal resuscitation equipment. Provision of specific drugs to the maternity hospitals necessary during the pregnancy and the lactating period + preventive treatments against anemia. Refurnish reagents and spare parts to 12 laboratories in the neonatal departments Training for the monitoring and prevention of congenital malformation (training of gynecologists). Training of Doctors about the Mental Health Support specific for pregnant women in situation of stress. Monthly inventory, Monitoring of the use of equipment, medicines and consumables. Specific COVID-19 PPE, medical equipment and medicine for the medical personnel, the parturient and the visitors.
