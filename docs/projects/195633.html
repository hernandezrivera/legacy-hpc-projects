Project Summary

This is a community driven project aims to positively improve living conditions and life-sustaining basic services of vulnerable populations to impact in the socio-economic well-being of targeting total of 29000 IDPs, and Urban poor HH, 11,000 Mayo Jebel Awlia, 10,000 in Al Salam West and South Ombada, 9000 Sharq Elneel, and 5000 Haj Yousif localities. 60% of which are women/G, 20% Youth, 5% elderly and disables, 5% children without parental care, in Khartoum State. BPWVO will enlarge their awareness and sense during flood, and inflation crises through REFLECT Methodology which will help them to advocate to solve their problems, reduce vulnerabilities, increase the diversify of income to access to the local labor market and create an enabling environment for livelihoods in the target localities as well as prevent negative coping., It will work on strengthening the protection security and increase the economic capacity of households, facilitating skills and development training through certified training institutions based on Reflect Methodology(as one of fitting literacy tools), setting to help more people and make a bigger impact. These empowering women and girls as well as boy’s programs will help to end cycles of gender-based violence. The three populations IDP, host communities and refugees live in extreme poverty and illiteracy, children girls/boys are dropped out of schools are subjected to abuse, child labor, trafficking, beggary, homeless, wrong practices and other offenses, for education components BPWVO will initiate illiteracy classes for adolescents boys and girls who didn't attend or dropout early from schools ,and those who were out recently can be enrolled by initiating 3 classes for children to sit for checkpoints examination in the three localities, programs will also be created that focus on children with special needs, besides social, linguistic & cultural inclusion sessions, training teachers to provide a specific focus on equity and need which seeks to , characterized by gender equity and fairness for all. Protection for children and Women is needed by equipping skilled, education, counseling, medical care, small business loans, and other programs that empower women and girls as well as boys helping to end cycles of gender-based violence to meet the aspiration of childhood and fill the gabs, spread awareness and apply principles proclaimed in the charter of UN & international, regional and local covenants appeal to decision makers BPWVO will be engaged with key actors that could influence changes in the community, the multi-pronged strategy will keep the whole community active, and campaigns facilitation that sensitize Leaflet and prepare host communities to sustain the project

1 - Needs

Due to HNO assessment 2022 humanitarian needs continue to grow across the country, despite progress and gains on the road to a new Sudan.These needs are driven by an economic crisis, exacerbated by COVID-19, protracted internal displacement that is yet to find durable solutions, increased insecurity and localised violence in parts of the country, coupled with floods, disease outbreaks, and more than 1.16 million refugees and asylum seekers hosted by Sudan. Khartoum State (7 Localities) has one of the highest food-insecure urban populations in the country, with refugees and the urban poorest most affected population as they are part of the IPC 3 (food security severity classification (HNO assessment 2022) and exacerbated by the political situation, lack of livelihood opportunities, economic deterioration which is not expected to improve and the most vulnerable will not be able to meet their basic needs, water sources do not have sufficient water, and most of them are not functioning. The cost of fuel has gone up to 500% therefore fuel reliant water yards cannot be maintained and its beyond affordability of the affected people, the cost of barrel of water is tripled prices which worsen Sanitation coverage, Sudan is driven by several factors including displacement, climatic shocks, floods, and hazards, economic challenges such as economic decline, high inflation, resulted in elevated levels of food insecurity, political tensions escalated between the civilian and military components of Sudan’s transitional authorities, the increase of the number of food insecure people, limiting purchasing capacity and raising prices of basic goods adding to that shortage of protection, insecure situation, Violence and high range of GBV. The deteriorating situation in Sudan and the political instability has increased violence, especially both youth, who suffered of inequities in social, economic and political conditions, which lead to increasing hunger, Psychological distress includes mental health issues such as depression and anxiety, deterioration in living conditions and poverty among youth and to their marginalization as displaced persons and migrants, The three populations IDP, host communities and migrants live in extreme poverty and illiteracy, children are dropped out of schools are employed child laborer, beggary, homeless and wrong practices, which expose them to risks of sexual and gender−based violence, harassment, Sexual Abuse and Exploitation and discrimination unsustainable livelihoods, the chances of girls and boys returning to school are low especially adolescent girls who suffer from gender inequality. 6 - Protection mainstreaming
6.1 - Does the project incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity for the beneficiaries?

4=Fully in place

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this intervention, BPWO is committed safeguard Right holders from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. BPWO will implement the project in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance during assessment, design of project and delivery of services. The project team will ensure all elements of accountability to affected population to be captured through extensive engagement and consultation with the community. Project is designed to inform all displaced about the availability and location of services. The project ensures safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of services. The intervention will also carefully apply Do No Harm approach to avoid any negative impact that it might bring to the area or the targeted communities. Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. The assistance and protection measures will not undermine local capacities for self-protection as well as support the efforts of the affected population and local communities to find security and restore dignity. Project intends to respond to the root causes of the protection concerns in the area. To achieve this, BPWO embraces community based protection approach, for building capacity of local communities, service providers and government officials. Their capacity will be enhanced in analyzing & finding solutions to protection problems, inclusive of reducing threats& risks, reducing vulnerability of affected population and increasing their capacity to cope with protection problems following exposure to shock and disaster.
