1 - Needs

The influx of Ethiopian refugee into Eastern Sudan due to the ongoing conflict in Tigray region, with over 45,000 new arrivals in In Gadaref and Kassala have created new humanitarian situation that require urgent supports in all sectors. South Sudanese and COR refugees continue to be in urgent needs for assistance in diverse sectors. although large response is ongoing, there is a significant gap in WASH, Education protection, livelihood assistance and ESNFI services. In conflict contexts, women and girls bear the brunt of the violence and brutality as rape, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation and other gender-based violence are common. The forms of violence and abuse encountered by minors including child marriage, child labor, child recruitment by armed groups, unaccompanied and separated children and child trafficking. Women and youth are also the most dynamic in seeking day to day opportunities for income to cover partial need of their demand which has not been satisfactorily met throughout this period; this exposes them to risks, violence, and neglect. Provision of livelihood coping mechanisms is urgently needed to help the women, girls, boys to meet their demands and contribute to self-reliance. It was observed that, most of the family’s male and females are sharing houses with their extended relatives and accommodated in shelters built partially from local materials (bamboo, sticks and grass) but lacking preventive materials that protect them from rain. Insides the shelters, they managed to collect very few items that can assist them for cooking, fetching and keeping water, therefore there is an urgent need for ES and NFIs. Refugees living in Umrakuba and Village 8 are in desperate situation and lack of urgent supports not limited to water , food , education and health. Increased caseloads of CAR refugees in Umdafug ,Alradoum areas, with the majority settled in temporary sites are posing a health risk in each of these areas, . UPO will integrate GBV risk reduction strategies into the implementation of the activities through the consultation of women in locating resources and services, as well as including them as members of the decision-making committees that govern these resources and services.

2 - Activities/Outputs

1. Distribution of NFIs and improved emergency shelter materials to 4,000 SS Refugees (2,500 females and 1,500 male) most vulnerable refugees. 2. Conducting training for 200 members of government institutions and local authorities (100 females and 100 males) on protection and refugee and human rights. 3. Establish and train 12 community-based protection committees, composed of male and female members to enable them identify and monitor protection incidents (risks, violence, kidnapping, SGBV) 4. Provision of Agricultural inputs and seeds to 2000 most vulnerable refugees a (1100 women and 900 men) to enable them produce to meet their food demands and increase self-reliance. 5. Conducting vocational training for 500 most vulnerable women and young girls 3. Provision of IGAs to 1,000 vulnerable people (550 women and 450 men).6. provision of WASH services to the refugees in Darfur and Eastern Sudan.7. provision of education services including construction of classroom, provision of learning materials, teacher training and capacity building , incentive for the teachers etc.
