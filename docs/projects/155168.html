Project Summary

The aim of this project is to strengthen community-based protection through the availability of various community center where activities such as recreational activities are offered, discussion sessions on protection topics such as trafficking, child marriage, DRR, PSEA, SGBV etc. The centers also include case management and legal services. Outreach awareness activities are conducted by community volunteers.

Needs

"After one year of the influx many refugees face protection risks in camp situation. Risks stem from living in overcrowded conditions, the lack of meaningful access to information and services, and their absence of women and girls from decision-making process, these risks disproportionally affect the most vulnerable in particular women, children at risk, persons with disabilities and older persons without support, who may have no choice but to resort to negative coping mechanisms. There is an ongoing need in the Refugee community to ensure that the community is well informed about the services available and where and how to access these, as well as a need to continue to increase the level of social self-management mechanism and community-based protection system. CBP provides a vital role in both these areas with services including information points, community outreach and self-defined community groups giving the community the opportunity to lead in the provision of protection awareness and services. There are gaps in the provision of CBP within the camps. Due to gendered roles, men and male youth who have suffered/witnessed trauma have little space to express their struggles and obtain appropriate support (e.g., men/male youth groups/ men friendly spaces), as well as loss/absence of adult male family members in a situation of displacement have resulted in young boys especially having to work. CBP provides equal opportunity for all members of the community to access services including men and boys.

Activities or outputs

• 4 community centers established (1 in each of the 3 targeted refugee camps and 1 in the host community) with mechanisms in place to facilitate community involvement in protection service provision.• 12 community groups established (3 in each area where CBP services are provided) these groups will have facilities to make design and implement projects based on community perception of need.• Community outreach teams in place (1 in each camp or area where CBP services are provided), they will be properly supported to allow the community to be at the core of protection programming.• Information points will be established in camps where CBP services are provided, with trained staff in place to receive community feedback and complaints and to provide accurate information.Community Centres:CC will be hubs for the community man, woman, youth group providing a place for people to gather, to discuss and exchange ideas or to spend time engaging in activities. Community will design programming taking into account age, gender and diversity, and ensuring that these are based on choice and needs of them- Focus groups, need assessment, post services assessment with the support of the community outreach team to understand the needs of the community and review the activities according to the recommendation collected.- Community centers will provide space to support other protection programmes and will include private spaces in which casework, counseling and legal services can be provided taking into account confidentiality.Information Points:We will have information points within all camps in which we operate CBP services, inside CC and standalone. The role of information points will be:- Providing information, signposting and referrals to appropriate services. - Provide a complaints, feedback and suggestion mechanism.- Maintain Kobo system to monitor complaints.- Provided community support, emergency and life-saving messaging, and focal point during emergencies including the monsoon.Outreach (COM):- Act as a platform for community engagement and act as specific need identify.- Through door-to-door visits; complete community need assessments, deliver messaging, identify protection cases and make referrals. Witch will ensure community engagement and empowerment - also COM’s will train in various subject such as GBV, PSEA, Hygiene and Disaster massages which will help us aware for community specially woman, and adolescent ,girls who are not comfortable always come to in the facility Community Groups:We will facilitate and support at least 3 groups per camp in which we operate CBP services, these will be diverse and selected by the group members, however we anticipate at minimum of 1 women’s group, 1 men’s group and 1 youth group in each camps and host community. They act as a platform for the community to engage in activities and improvement their environment.They will meet regularly, discuss and select projects to meet needs within their community. Plan and implement projects with a specific budget provided for implementation.In the community group every aged group will be involved and trained up PSEA, life skills , DRR.PSEA reporting: All facility will have 1 complain box, and focal person number it would be help to ensure complain mechanism also from community center and information point awareness session will be conduct in regular basis for community awareness about PSEA .Co-ordination: Per camp 1 focal person will attend meeting in the camp co ordination meeting or other related meeting when held, also filed personnel will co ordinate with other service holder such as Wash , health, shelter, health ,MHPSS, SGBV and other service provider, BRAC also a regular member of GiHA, GBV-SS, PSEA, Protection and other working groups and SS.Space availability and use: We will ensure maximum uses of every spaces allocated for various Community based activities by multiple use of facilities,
