2 - Activities

This project aims to address GBV in conflict-affected zones & rural areas ofNortheast Nigeria through an innovative tech-based intervention. We will pilot acomprehensive & user-friendly mobile application & website that fosters communityownership & active participation in GBV prevention and response. The mobileapplication will enable women & girls to identify potential risks & access resources forrisk mitigation through interactive content & self-assessment tools such as digital riskassessment, peer support, group learning, etc. It will engage users in real-timereporting features, empowering them to play an active role in reducing GBVincidents. The platform will provide confidential virtual counseling & peer supportservices to survivors. Telemedicine features will ensure remote access to essential health services for adolescent survivors of GBV, especially in areas with limitedhealthcare facilities promoting replicability, effectiveness, and scalability. Thetechnology will facilitate community discussions, workshops, & sensitization sessionson GBV prevention and response. Local stakeholders will receive training on utilizingthe platform effectively, ensuring community ownership & commitment to addressingGBV. The technology will incorporate a robust data management system to track &analyze GBV incidents, interventions, & outcomes. The real-time data will informevidence-based programming & contribute to the GBV Information ManagementSystem. By involving multi-sectoral stakeholders, the project aspires to demonstratea holistic and adaptable model, contributing to lasting change in addressing GBV inconflict-affected zones & rural areas. This tech-based intervention aligns perfectlywith the Innovation Challenge's goal of leveraging technology for humanitarianaction. By engaging local communities & empowering survivors, the interventionensures a survivor-centered approach that fosters community-driven solutions toGBV.The project will develop and utilize tech-based mobile applications and virtualservices such as virtual counseling, and peer-to-peer, awareness-raising,engagements, which will provide innovative access to reach and support GBVsurvivors in remote and restricted areas including urban areas. It will prioritize techsavvy GBV specialists and average end users with the ability to use, adapt, andrecommend its usage for effective GBV mitigations. The mobile application/webplatform will be developed after due consultations and appropriate design stages toenhance the efficiency and effectiveness of service provision, ensuring that PSS andcase management services are accessible and available over an extended periodwhile providing GBV response in line with the GBV minimum standards and the interagency guidelines. It will be used to map out services on who does what, where, andwhen to make service provisions accessible. A key component of the project is theincorporation of telemedicine and remote healthcare consultations. The applicationwill enhance the capacity of humanitarian actors to implement innovative preventionstrategies, leveraging digital communications, data analysis, and evidence-basedapproaches. This helps address the identified gap of lacking specialized skills andresources in GBV prevention. The Tech-based solution will foster engagement withwomen and girls. This will harbor several close groups, intended to amplify the voicesof women and girls in the identification and mitigation of GBV issues, therebyenhancing empowerment and ownership in mitigating GBV risks. Other close groupswill involve the participation of men, boys, and community structures, demonstratingtheir commitments by engaging and participating in GBV mitigation efforts. Overall,the project will bring together a community of like minds, and beneficiaries throughcollaborations, partnerships, and close consultations with the GBV sector andgovernment agencies to combat GBV.

CPPLI will priorities the protection of vulnerable population and marginalized group through a community based approach. 9.1a - Specify (and give URL link if possible to) the relevant needs assessment document.

This project is highly relevant to humanitarian action. This project aims to provide acomprehensive & adaptive response to GBV in these crisis-affected populations byleveraging technology and innovation. It aligns with the principles of humanitarianaction by meeting essential needs, such as access to critical support, information, &resources through the tech-based intervention, it amplifies the protection & safety ofsurvivors through secure communication channels, confidential reportingmechanisms, & case management support. It enhances partnership and coordinationby actively engaging & collaborating with local organizations, community leaders, &stakeholders to foster community ownership & create sustainable solutions. Theproject recognizes the importance of empowering women & girls in crisis-affectedpopulations. The specific crisis-affected population that will benefit from this projectincludes women & girls living in rural areas & conflict-affected zones of NortheastNigeria. These populations face increased risks of GBV due to the breakdown ofsocial structures, displacement, & limited access to services. The project willprioritize reaching marginalized & vulnerable groups, including internally displacedpersons (IDPs), returnees & refugees, & women & girls living in remote & hard-to reach areas. We aim to ensure inclusivity, equity, & improved well-being for this crisis affected population by tailoring interventions to their specific needs

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

CPPLI CPPLI is committed to the core values of IASC framework on AAP by ensuring participation and inclusion, communication and transparency, feedback and response, organizational learning, and adoption. Diverse community members and leaders including women, men, girls, boys, and people with disabilities will be engaged from start-up to close-out through comprehensive sensitization activities that ensure their understanding and empowerment of project activities, and will be supported to propose mechanisms that reflect their own needs. CPPLI will recruit feedback interns to collect, record the feedback received using the sector CRM register, the feedback received will be analyzed and share with the relevant project staff for proper action and the feedback loop will be closed.The community members will be further informed in detail about their entitlements in the project and the criteria for accessing services under the project activities. CPPLI will also ensure that beneficiaries and the wider community members are involved in all decision-making processes during the implementation.
