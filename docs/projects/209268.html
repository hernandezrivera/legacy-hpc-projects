Project Summary

The project goal is to improve the well-being and self-reliance of refugees and host communities in three states of Sudan. The targeted states are West Kordofan, Gedaref and Kassala. This will be achieved through the provision of protection services including health and nutrition and protection (general protection, child protection and GBV).
The overall objective for health is to increase meaningful access to promotive, preventive, curative, rehabilitative and palliative care within a primary health care approach through the implementation of basic package of primary healthcare services including management of communicable & non communicable diseases, maternal and child health care, nutrition, mental health and psychosocial services, with access to referral to secondary and tertiary health care in reference to referral SoP in place. Alight will collaborate with other stakeholders to ensure epidemiological surveillance and will actively participate in prevention and response for any new or existing disease outbreak. All these will be achieved through maintaining skilled health care providers and reinforcing their capacities based on identified gaps.
Alight will also collaborate with SMOH in terms of capacity building such as training, supervision and coaching. Alight will operate a field hospital which will provide basic package of comprehensive emergency obstetrical and neonatal care (CEmONC)to refugees and host community including performing c-section for people in needs, provide post abortion care, obstetrical consultations by a Gynecologist, provision of family planning, clinical management of rape, blood banking and blood transfusion and manage other conditions that care beyond primary health care but within hospital capacity.
Under nutrition the project aims at supporting refugees to access nutrition services including early detection of cases, treatment in different programs and also other prevention interventions such as promotion of infant and young child feeding IYCN.
For protection, ALIGHT will engage with and leverage community-based preventative and protective interventions towards delivering protection services to the affected populations. This will be implemented through existing coordination and collaborative initiatives that prioritize the inclusion of population of concern with specific protection needs and girls and women, among others. The range of services will include prevention and response to Gender Based Violence, (GBV), community-based protection, Child Protection, support to PSNs and general protection for refugees. To appropriately respond to the needs of the targeted population, 2024 Alight’s strategy will be to continuously train CBPNs, CPN and other community structures, which will help in identify PSNs and other extremely vulnerable individuals in the settlements and camps and ensure that errors of omission, non-inclusion are minimized. The key activities to undertake will include; identification of PSNs, individual case management for PSNs, GBV survivors and UASC, and follow-ups, Psychosocial Support, cash or in-kind assistance, awareness raising activities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

As part of protection mainstreaming, ALIGHT will consider persons ages, gender, and other characteristics for better assessment of their risks, capacities, solutions and provide meaningful services to all without discrimination but rather inclusion for a stronger response. Effective accountability and protection programming will pay attention to diverse and intersecting forms of structural oppression, inequalities, and equitable provision of information, services, and assistance to men, women, boys and girls. ALIGHT will train the project staffs on protection mainstreaming and orient them on referral pathways where it exists. ALIGHT ensures policy on Prevention and reporting sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment (PRSEAH) are in place and ensure new staff, consultants and contractors are oriented while existing ones are refreshed and will sign their commitment to prevent and report such incidents.Beneficiaries will also be oriented on prevention and reporting mechanisms as part of their education. If any feedback on risk of PSEAH, Alight will work with other stakeholders including protection partners, refugee camps coordination mechanism to mitigate the risks and improve the environment that minimize protection risks.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

ALIGHT is focused on implementing systems and methods that reveals customer experience of organizational services so that it can align a response and re-establish trust between ALIGHT, refugees, IDPs and the host communities. Trust works in both directions and must be built on the foundation of honesty and mutual accountability. ALIGHT commitment to accountability to the affected population will focus on the following: - governance, transparency, feedback, participation, design, monitoring and evaluation. ALIGHT will work with community structures in increasing the role of the community participation in maintaining the services. In addition, through the established community feedback and response mechanism, ALIGHT will continue to facilitate community consultation meetings to ensure active community participation, transparency and handling community feedback. The community members and beneficiaries provide feedback and seek responses in relation to activities provided by ALIGHT and other development partners in their communities in a manner that is safe, non-threatening and accessible to improve accountability across ALIGHT operational areas. ALIGHT’s Feedback/Complaint Response Mechanism (F/CRM) provides a formal channel through which ALIGHT beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries can register voices and complaints. The F/CRM is centrally operated through feedback forms and a toll-free hotline number from all mobile phone networks companies (Sudani, Zain and MTN). ALIGHT has developed awareness materials including banners and flyers placed at ALIGHT project service delivery points such as health centers, water points etc. with the hotline number and explanations on how the FCRM works as well as information about our accountability standards and practices to which customers should hold ALIGHT accountable. In addition, through the MEAL department, ALIGHT conducts regular customer interviews and post service/distribution monitoring using standard survey feedback forms. As per ALIGHT procedures, the analysis of feedback information from communities is fed directly to senior management for action. These mechanisms have proven to be an effective tool in providing beneficiaries and communities with a voice and ultimately improving ALIGHT operations and identifying misconduct within the organization. A well-structured FCRM also enhances lessons learned and good practices across our network.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

The Project will ensure that participation, feedback and complaints mechanisms are integrated in all strategies and plans all throughout the project cycle. This is to ensure the project decisions and actions are responsive to the expressed priorities, needs, capacities and views of all persons of concern irrespective of Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. This is aimed at ensuring meaningful participation, enhanced communication and transparency, improved feedback and response. ALIGHT will ensure a balanced boys, girls, women and men in all project activities. Data for beneficiaries will be disaggregated by gender, age groups and location, as the project activities are tailored for different groups in line with developmental stages, needs and vulnerabilities and basing on relevant parameters appropriate in reverence to heightened protection risks of the persons of concern and issue affecting their access to essential services. The Project will ensure at all persons of concern have equal and non- discriminatory access to health and nutrition services. Alight will maintain effective feedback systems (including comments, suggestions and complaints) using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non- sensitive feedback e.g. use of complaints boxes, toll free hot lines. In regard to PRSEAH. All Alight staffs have committed to and signed PSEA policy, Child Protection policy, Code of Conduct as well as the engaged community-based networks Alight is a member of National and State levels PSEA task force and regularly coordinates with other partners in relevant PSEA forums. To ensure PRSEAH, Alight will ensure all new staff, contractors and consultants receive training on PRSEAH and sign the policy. Also, beneficiaries will be mobilized on prevention and reporting such incidents and channel of reporting in confidential way. The PRSEAH training, refresher trainings, community awareness have been considered in the budget.
