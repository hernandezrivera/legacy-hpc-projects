Project Summary

A1. Distribution of immediate life-saving NFi Shelter kits (HH level). A2. Distribution of NFi at HH level to to enable the vital household functions. A3. Provide technical assistance to vulnerable people in shelter reconstruction assuring at least minimum level of safety, security, privacy and dignity. A4. Increasing risk awareness and preparedness at the community and institutional levels to mitigate future disasters (removing desbris, cleaning canals, cutting trees, etc).
