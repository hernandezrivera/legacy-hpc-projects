Project Summary

The areas of intervention of this project are located in Borno (Monguno, Mafa, Jere, Bama) and Adamwa (Madagali). People living in these areas are in condition of extreme poverty and consequently they have no possibility to afford the costs of basic needs, such as shelter, education, healthcare and food and skills that can provide them with basic needs due to the insurgence that over thrown them of their communities for several year which has made life for this displaced persons hard coupled with face that they are not in their respect communities which make life hard for them.
The majority of the families have an average number of six children per household and they struggle to survive by doing odd jobs. Therefore, almost all of the family incomes depends on Humanitarian service providers and as such are basically oriented to first needs and in most cases they cannot afford the cost of living standard.
The said project is being initiated to address the living standards of IDPs and Host communities through establishments of vocational trainings or skills acquisition centers as fact findings have indicated that 75% of the youth (primary beneficiaries) in the communities affected by insurgency face issues and problems associated with “idealness”, thereby lacking basic way of livelihood.
The same study also shows that youths spend their time on substance abuse and exploitations of younger ones (especially women). The youths have indicated that the establishment of vocational training or skills acquisition center could alleviate the problems being faced as result of idealness and lack of source of livelihood, this was as obtained during a fact finding and assessment on the need to intervene on the current state of the IDPs and host communities.
If these youth are been mobilized in an organized manner, engaging them through vocational training or skills acquisition, they can really bring about great change in their lives and in the society. Most of the Youths presently are being scorched by HIV/AIDS and unwanted pregnancy, thereby making them one of the prime victims of the pandemic. Thus it is essential to intervene as soon as possible to reverse the deteriorating situation of the youth at least to maintain the sustenance of our generation. Their potentials could be Hanes for the socio-economic development of the nation only if attention is paid to them.
The said project, aim at targeting the youths, women and caregivers. The total targeted number of potential beneficiaries is 1500 in which 90% (1350) will constitute of IDPs and 10% (150) constitute of the Host community.

1 - Needs

Based on need assessment, the youths/adults (with both genders inclusive) are faced with much anxiety and depression because of uncertainty of their future with no skills and coupled with the fact that some of them are over aged for conventional education. The youths presently, find themselves in idleness and joblessness that lead them to aggression, frustrations, depression and anger, due to the insurgency that displaced them out of their communities, and place them under restriction in camps as they can’t earn a means of livelihood as before, such as farming, fishing, animal rearing and small scale businesses. And as a result of that, they find themselves involve in an illegal activities such as theft, gangsters, exploitation, substance abuse etc. However, due to lack of vocational training facilities and awareness, the youths have never been offered these techniques to create their micro enterprises in a profitable manner. They are mainly undertaking some local workload to generate daily meal, and as such they never get the opportunity to explore other skills and markets beyond the existing one due to lack of skill and training support. This program would include directly vocational and skills development and will be reaching the targeted people with appropriate capacity building to alleviate their suffering, emotional distress and mischief aimed at raising the standard of living.Thus, there is a high and urgent need to address the situation by establishing vocational training centers that will contribute towards alleviating the emotional distress and mischief.

2 - Activities

Relief Rescue Initiative will continue employing community friendly approaches to reach to the targeted states and LGAs areas through Vocational Training Skills and development. Also the approach will involve various levels of stakeholders. Each of the components of the project will be tackled technically by designing interventions that effectively and appropriately address vocational training skills and associated activities that can contribute to community development. It is anticipated that by the end of the project period, there will be realization of positive living and reduced levels of dependency among young people in project districts. Identification/Selection and registration/enrollment of 1,500 which shall include GBV survivors, UASC, CAAG, Drug abusers and foster parents who will be reached and the service will be managed jointly with community leaders, development workers, organizations and RRI staff. It will still focus on assessing the nature of the community and the status of beneficiaries. Identification/Mobilization will also be in preparation for identifying and forming Vocational Training skills centers Vocational/Skills training sessions:1070 participants will be trained in sustainable activities which include: Shoe Making, Cap knitting, bag making, Soap Making, Tailoring fashion design, painting and designing for both in and out of schools. The above mention activities will be segregated across genders as the beneficiaries will be engaged and trained extensively and promoting the best interest of the child. The various skills training sessions will last within the period of 12 months, through the above activities. At the end of the project the beneficiaries will be empowered with sustainable skills tools that will help them to be self-dependence. Vocational Training skills and parental training for 180 parents and 250 caregivers will be carried out across the implementing state and LGAs and shall include knowledge build up on the importance of child rights, Environmental protection, parenting strategies and child development and support them in their sustainable skills in their areas of interest.Counseling: There shall be Counseling and guiding sessions to assist the young people on various development aspects in such a way to recognize their potential skills relevant to their professional development. Advocacy: Extensive advocacy through Net-working services with other organizations especially child protection agencies, to better enhance the implementation of the project will be conductedFollow-up activities: Staff members will always go back to places where they have carried out the activities to meet young people to assess the changes that have occurred, guide beneficiaries in their respective activities, share with them experiences that they have met and get measures on them accordingly.Monitoring and Evaluation: Monitoring visits will be carried throughout the project and will be carried out on monthly basis to track and assess the progress of implementation. It will be carried out also as a means to supervise the facilitators of the various skills activities. This will be an ongoing activity throughout the project life. Deliverables shall include monitoring visit reports, check lists etc. Evaluation shall be conducted at the end of the project implementation to measure the impact of the project.
