Project Summary

The project aims at improving health service provision in underserved communities, communities with limited/ overstretched health services, prevention of disease outbreaks especially in high risk areas and improving health seeking behaviour of affected populations. The approach will be a needs based one, efforts will be towards strengthening locally available capacities and community structures but with gender responsiveness such that the health services are accessible by women, children, youths, elderly, men and people with disabilities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at improving access and utilisation of emergency health care services by vulnerable communities of Mocimboa, Quissanga, Metuge and Montepuez in the province of Cabo Delgado. This will involve: Support and provision of integrated mobile health services through collaborating with health facility management to facilitate health workers to conduct mobile clinics in underserved communities. The project will aim at providing basic sanitation facilities, equipment and medical supplies alongside logistics for the health teams to be able to offer services to the target communities. The health package will be basic primary health care services including EPI services, antenatal services, general outpatient consultations and treatment, HIV & TB services, nutrition services and adolescent services. Support health facilities will include provision of medical equipment, medical supplies, non-medical equipment, non-medical supplies, cleaning equipment, cleaning agents based on a needs assessment and recommendation by the provincial health team. The supplies will be replenished on a quarterly basis and basic monitoring efforts will be in place to avoid stock outs and skewed support. Provision of Sexual Reproductive Health integrated services will focus on boosting utilisation of antenatal services, promotion of health facility deliveries, improvement in utilisation of postnatal care services including family planning services. In addition to health facility level and mobile clinic SRH services, local midwives will be engaged to boost health education for such mothers at community level but to also encourage mothers to go to the health facilities for routine antenatal care, delivery and postnatal care not forgetting immunisation of their babies. Develop and distribute IEC material for health facilities, Community health workers and for mobile brigade sites. The Ministry of Health has several IEC material so effort will be on printing and dissemination based on identified gaps. Where need arises, IEC material will be developed together with the provincial teams and in consultation with the ministry such that they are well adopted to the Mozambique context. Johanniter will also support in translating of existing Portuguese IEC material to local languages such that community members better understand the information.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project utilizes a thorough risk assessment and safeguarding strategy to proactively identify and mitigate potential risks, ensuring no harm to beneficiaries, especially those most vulnerable. • The project initiates with a detailed assessment of security, environmental, cultural, and logistical factors. Regular updates allow the team to anticipate and respond to shifting conditions, especially those impacting displaced or conflict-affected populations.• Community members, particularly women and leaders, are actively involved in risk identification. Feedback mechanisms enable beneficiaries to report concerns, raise issues, and provide recommendations, ensuring risks are swiftly addressed and interventions remain safe and effective.• Adopting a “do-no-harm” approach, the project emphasizes gender sensitivity and the protection of vulnerable groups. Activities respect cultural contexts while encouraging gender equality and health improvements.• Regular monitoring and adaptive planning allow adjustments to strategies in response to emerging risks, supporting safe delivery of health services. This approach maintains a safe, respectful, and adaptable environment, ensuring positive, risk-sensitive impact across target communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project fosters meaningful access to assistance and actively empowers women and elderly persons through targeted, inclusive strategies: • From the onset; women, elderly persons, and local leaders participate in decision-making processes to ensure that interventions reflect their specific needs and perspectives. Inviting women’s groups and elder representatives into planning sessions promotes interventions sensitive to their unique challenges.• Health services will be segregated to cater for specific categories of people both in the health facilities and during mobile clinics. For instance, immunisation and pediatric consultation services for children, nutrition services for both children and women, maternity services for women but with spouses encouraged to come along with their wives, adolescent services, specialised services for diabetics, hypertension, HIV/ TB and general outpatient and inpatient care services to support all other categories of people.• Through skills-based training tailored for women and elderly community members, the project will support active, inclusive engagement in sustainable solutions. This training will equip women with knowledge in emergency health practices, fostering their capacity to champion healthy practices and contribute to long-term community resilience in humanitarian settings.• Regular community feedback sessions with men, women, adolescents and elderly groups will be conducted for timely feedback on quality of service delivery and gender responsiveness of the services such that timely improvement can be implemented or feedback can be considered for future programming. This approach enhances access to essential services, improves health seeking behavior and emphasizes the roles of women and elderly persons in community well-being and leadership.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Since health care is a basic need for all, the project will take an inclusive and proactive approach to identify individuals with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance: • The project will collaborate with community leaders, and health workers to identify individuals with disabilities within target areas. This will involve direct outreach, household assessments, and consultations to ensure these individuals are included in all project activities.• A thorough needs assessment will be conducted to identify the specific challenges faced by people with disabilities in accessing nutrition and health services. This will focus on physical, informational, and social barriers, addressing both environmental and attitudinal challenges.• During health service delivery, persons with disability will be exempted from lining up for services and in case of any infrastructural works at the health facilities or mobile clinics, contractors will be guided to adapt designs such that even people with disabilities are able to utilise the infrastructure. • Project staff and volunteers will undergo training on disability inclusion and rights, ensuring they are equipped to meet the needs of individuals with disabilities. Community members will also be sensitized to reduce stigma and foster an inclusive environment. These measures aim to ensure full participation and equitable access for people with disabilities, empowering them as active participants in emergency health activities. Regular monitoring and feedback will help address any emerging barriers.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed project in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique, is committed to minimizing environmental impacts while implementing emergency lifesaving Nutrition interventions. Several measures will be taken to ensure the integration of environmental considerations:• The project aims to reduce the environmental footprint wherever possible. As far as quality permits, project materials are locally purchased in order to reduce transport and the related emissions and plastic is avoided wherever possible.• Environmental education will be provided to promote sustainable health practices, such as clearing bushes and destroying any broken containers to avoid breeding grounds for mosquitoes, responsible waste disposal, efficient water use and proper personal hygiene practices among others. • Regular monitoring will assess the effectiveness of environmental mitigation measures, ensuring that project activities are adjusted as needed to reduce risk of harmful effects.
