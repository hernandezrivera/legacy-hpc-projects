Project Summary

The Project Purpose: -
The project is intended to provide safe, equitable, dignified and unhindered access to critical basic services and livelihoods opportunities for the most vulnerable war affected groups to prevent further erosion of their coping abilities and strategies to keep poor resources sustained.
Under this objective the project will provide humanitarian support to affected host communities, IDPs and returnees who will receive agricultural seed and tools, veterinary services including livestock vaccination, treatment, and goat's distribution. The project will also boost revival efforts to sustain normal livelihood practices. Protection intervention will support existing IGAs as well as introduction of suitable new ones through availing training to both male and female groups on appropriate skills for the production of marketable goods and services. Current war has displaced many economic activities from major urban centers due to insecurity, so it would be appropriate to transfer the know-how in its simpler forms to areas of new settlements for the affected population. Markets that used to produce traditional farming tools had been destroyed in places like Nyala and Kass, and thus encouragement of young elements involved to move out to places where demand is high like Edelfursan and Bilail towns.
Targeting and prioritization: -
The project for the target population will focus on the lifesaving and live sustenance, targeting the most severe food insecure population groups in South Darfur State, the target will cover about 21780 people in the five localities of South Darfur State: 1- In Nyala Shimal, the target is 3,000 people of host community and IDPs.
2- In Nyala Janoub, the target is 4,500 people from host community and returnees 3- In Kas locality 5,000 people from IDPs and host communities and none-displace groups 4-In ED Al Fursan, the target is 5,000 people from host community, IDPs and none displaced 5- In Beliel the target is 4,100 people of IDPs, host community and none displaced. Targeted females from the aforementioned figures will form no less than 40% since most of farmers are women. GFO will form community committees (with women representing minimum1/3 of members) to participate in the project interventions, monitoring, feedback and learning. GFO selected the previous sides because they are areas which faced erratic rainfall and war during period from April 2023 to October 2023. Government technical services directed to the agricultural sector, though very poor in the past, have totally collapsed due to war. The desperate situation in this sector has been manifested in low supply of cattle for meet in Nyala as an example. Improved Frisian cattle type have been slaughtered in Nyala during the last 6 months to compensate for supply shortage. Grain production was low as well, as reflected by high prices even during harvest time (Oct-Dec this year) for the first time in recent history. Project targeted area falls within IPC classification +3m hence humanitarian support to bring some hope to the people is direly needed. Implementation mechanism through engagement of GFO with its vast relevant field experience is ensured.
Key areas of project interventions will include below activities:
1- Support Agricultural seeds and tools
2- Livestock restocking (Goat distribution)
3-Animal vaccination and treatment
4- Support emergency livelihood (skills training, IGA, Fuel stove) etc….
GFO will conduct thorough assessment surveys in the area to identify eligible beneficiaries and appropriate interventions with strong participation of the grass root communities of the area before embarking upon implementation of the activities. This will ensure ownership of the intervention and thus sustainability of the activities by the communities after gaining good practical lessons.

3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project commits to address and mitigate protection risk in the targeted areas through focus and empowering on most vulnerable affected people through taking lead and planning in the project on, risk facing women, children and disables, be given priority during the project cycle. The project will conduct risk assessment, risk ranking and select most relevant protection issues that concern and affect vulnerable people, the project will develop mitigation measures to and take actions and report.
