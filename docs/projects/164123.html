Project Summary

Maram has been working in the areas of Harim / Al-Dana, Jisr Al-Shughour / Zarzour with 2 WGSS at aldana and one in Zarzour to provide prevention and mitigation services, also to provide specialized services to survivors. This has contributed to women’s access to life - saving services more easily through referrals by case managers.During the last 4 year, there has been a significant change in the disclosure of the most serious cases, following the targeting of males in the activities of the centers and the focus on the provision of awareness services and individual counseling for reproductive health, which contributed to women's access to life-saving services.Jasmine centers will continue to provide specialized services during case management and referral within centers and through outreach activities through trained case managers and psychologists.The area survey will be conducted for coordination to provide specialized services for referrals, especially legal, medical, and other services related to GBV.There will be a merger of reproductive health activities within centers through a midwife.The awareness-raising messages "Awareness Raising toolkit" played a role in the ability to raise different categories by sex and age, which takes into account the specificity of each category. The focus will be on supporting the services that were added last year with a better focus on the elderly, empowering them and benefiting from their experiences, and provide activities for girls using curricula designed for them. targeting widowed and divorced women living in special camps where Maram can reach and provide services to these groups, who were targeted throughout the past year: Maram can obtain official approvals to provide gender-based violence services to the 13 widows' camps, which are obtained every month. There are no barriers to providing services within these camps and they can bring women to receive services provided within the centers, but transportation must be provided for access. This has led Maram to target these centers as the most vulnerable to gender-based violence. A mobile team will be formed to specifically target these camps with a clear plan. we will give priority for People with special needs through using materials, supporting access and staff who can provide services to them" inside and by the mobile team to integrate them effectively into society. because last year we responded to many cases monthly. Provide specialized services to male survivors who can report cases of gender-based violence as a result of continuing activities dedicated to males, by male case managers who will be able to provide "counseling, case management, and referral. As a result of the displacement, the situation continued it became necessary to focus on the provision of services related to cash assistance which will be provided by the procedures and sub-bloc based violence gender guidelines

4 - Needs

According to VOICES From Syria 2020 the humanitarian situation in north-west Syria has experienced continuous deterioration during 2019.in December300,000 people were displaced. 80 % are estimated to be women and children. Displacement patterns over the course of 2019 saw people impacted by hostilities moving toward densely populated areas of northern Idlib Aldana, Further, people with disabilities are disproportionately impacted by the ongoing conflict, HNAP estimates that at least 27% of the population (aged 12 years and above) in Syria have a disability.In the Al Dana Region there are multiple informal camps dedicated only to widows and female-headed households. These camps are largely off the radar of humanitarian interventions and are in dire need for support.Aqrabat/Dar Hammoud: 42 families/Atmeh: AL Kuaity: 127 families /Al Reeh al Mursala: 119 families/Bayan and Mushref: 132 families/Abdul Hamid: 81 families/AL bir wa ALtaqwa: 51 families/Mshhed Ruhin: 173 families /AL Riaaya: 175 families, and Hamad Al Ammar: 150 familiesThis displacement has resulted in many needs for women and girls the most needs in these situations are: •Displaced girls and women need more privacy to maintain their security, dignity, health and personal cleanliness and their special needs must also be taken into consideration when designing and implementing programs in these centers and camps. •The insufficient humanitarian aid that addresses the needs of the displaced girls and women in the collective centers, camps that compared to their needs, this gap is pushing girls and women to seek alternatives to meet the needs, which increases their vulnerability, affecting their resilience and recovery. •There is a serious need to mainstream or incorporate the provision of personal safety and security plus psychological support and means to protect human dignity for girls and women living in the collective centers and camps during and after the war

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Security: Risk: attacking by aircraft to the center, likelihood: Medium/Impact: high, Mitigation: the relocating center site will be according to risk analysis regarding the nature of the building, away from sensitive facilities in the area, also will be de-conflicted through OCHA de-confliction mechanism. Access: Difficulty in the transfer and access of beneficiaries to activities due to the unstable security situation within the specific work areas, likelihood: Medium, Impact: High/Mitigation: A plan linked to security and safety department within the organization to protect workers and beneficiaries, Preventing women and girls from reaching centers to receive services within the center, likelihood: Medium/Impact: high/Mitigation: Ensuring the provision of services for males and family members of the families and couples during the implementation of the project activities and in particular, awareness activities and advocacy campaigns in order to mitigate risks and reduce the risk of preventing the access of beneficiaries to services/Category of BNFs: Risk: Disability of PWDs, older persons and areas away from centers, likelihood: High, Impact: High, Mitigation: Mobile teams will be allocated to reach beneficiaries who cannot access the centers to participate in activities for PWDs and elderly people with mobility difficulties in remote areas. Community Environment: Risk: Lack of acceptance of society and the forces controlling the content of the activities provided they contain highly sensitive topics, likelihood: High, Impact: High, Mitigation: The beneficiaries and local staff will be consulted about the possibility of implementing the sessions of the program.
