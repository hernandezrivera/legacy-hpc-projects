Project Summary

The project will contain series of activities such as construction and rehabilitation of the damaged WASH infrastructure, undertake environmental clean-up related activities in addition to building the capacities of the local communities and local government authorities. The project will support environmental debris removal and rehabilitation of such points and places. In addition, the project will strive to support the active participation of women in all aspects of the programme and will build the capacities of the women to ensure their active and equal participation in the whole programme. In order to address Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) related issues and gaps, the project will endeavour to build the capacities of the local communities on hazard resistant methods in micro infrastructures. The proposed project will further undertake the environmental impact assessment on the damaged infrastructure to determine the impact it had on the lives of the targeted communities. Using cash for work modality, the project will select beneficiaries to participate in rehabilitation of damaged infrastructures, debris removal in areas of return. In order to strengthen resilience among the beneficiaries, basic services such shelter repair/tool kits, utensils such as NFIs kits will be provided among the targeted population especially returnees and host community population.
In order to incorporate the DRR measures during the construction and rehabilitation of the damaged houses/shelters, disaster risk reduction trainings will be administrated in the project locations. Gender marker will be a guiding principle throughout the implementation phase where equal and active representation will be given to all the groups of the communities including men and women, boys and girls. To promote inclusiveness, special people with disabilities and vulnerabilities will be mainly focused and targeted in all project related activities. Equal representation and participation of the female community members will be ensured keeping in view culture sensitivity.

Needs

The severe damages to key public infrastructure and service provision, including LGA government facilities (i.e. LGA offices, schools, clinics, markets, WASH infrastructure in schools, health facilities and other government or private institutions) in Monguno, Ngala, Bama, Damsak and Banki, are adding to the long history of marginalization and chronic under-development in the north-east. There is an urgent need for early recovery interventions, including restoring livelihoods and access to essential services, including access to justice, to strengthen the self-reliance of affected populations, especially girls, youth, women, and the elderly, against future vulnerabilities and shocks. As INTERSOS exists in all the LGAs that fall into the high and acute humanitarian and early recovery needs, the project will focus on these areas as were explored to be assisted. During the assessment it has been revealed that, all the project focusing LGAs are severally affected by the conflict, all the infrastructure including WASH, housing, community gathering centres, government buildings, farm to market access roads, livelihood infrastructure are completely or partially devasted. All the project focusing areas are in dire needs of reconstruction and or rehabilitation. The destructed infrastructure affected the routine life and means of income of the affected communities, they do not have adequate resources for the restoration of the demolished infrastructure and resuming their businesses due to the unavailable resources. The selected LGAs are considered the potential zones for hosting new arrivals and returnees form its outskirts. The huge influx of the returnees and new arrivals overstretched the economic situation of the local communities. The increasing insurgency and military operations caused huge displacement and distortion in the lives of the affected communities. Due to improper water and sanitation and hygiene services, the people are severally affected with acute diseases Besides least availability of the livelihood opportunities and income resources to support their daily and normal lives. With the support of the proposed activities, the project will endeavour to address all the aforementioned shortcomings and ensure enabling environment by the providing of water and sanitation and hygiene services, rehabilitation and reconstruction of the demolished infrastructure through cash for work modality for the targeted communities

Activities

The proposed project will endeavour to restore the basic communal infrastructure in the proposed intervention areas. In order to achieve the objective, construction and rehabilitation of the damaged infrastructure including, houses/shelters, water supply schemes such as deep wells, tube wells, pressure pumps, sanitation infrastructures such as latrines and showers, street pavements, draining lines, sewerage lines, installation and construction of the solid waste points for the debris removal will be constructed under the proposed programme. In order to provide livelihood opportunities, the proposed programme will provide cash injection to the affected communities through participation in the restoration of the aforementioned infrastructure. During the identification process equal opportunities will be given to all genders including men, women and boys, girls above form 18. The cash for work component will also include conditional cash grants such as making local handicrafts using of local available materials. The cash for work component will help the IDPs to promote project ownership and sustainability as beneficiaries will take part in reconstruction, rehabilitation of their local community infrastructure. In the soft component of the project, capacity building training of the targeted beneficiaries on water and sanitation and hygiene, construction and rehabilitation of damaged infrastructures and trainings on disaster risk reduction during the construction practices. clean up campaigns and organization of special days or events for the awareness will be used as mechanisms for early warning systems in the communities.
