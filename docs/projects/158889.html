Project Summary

The old city of Hebron is considered one of the most struggling areas in the West Bank due to the settler’s crimes,arrogance of the Israeli occupation,and the large number of military checkpoints and repetitive closures.HWC Hebron Emergency Center became one of the main health facilities that provides Women Health Program’s services,Chronic Disease Program’s services among other primary health care services in HebronH2.The current project is an outcome of the continuous assessment of the general situation and particular health needs of the communities in the old city of Hebron(H2)through HWC’s Hebron Emergency Center with special focus on the health needs of the vulnerable communities.in addition to analyzing secondary data review published by others.The assessment revealed the presence of two pressing problems faced by the communities in the old city of Hebron(H2),which are the escalation of political violence imposed by the Israeli occupation and its settlers upon communities in the old city of Hebron(H2)and so these communities have been considered one of the most vulnerable communities in the West Bank.As the presence of the Israeli occupation and its systematic violence practices have been imposing threat on the livelihood of these communities.Moreover,the Israeli occupation has been imposing restriction movements upon these communities,which hinders the ability of these communities to reach out for health services outside the old city of Hebron(H2).Thus, the project “Access to Essential Health Services and Emergency Preparedness for Marginalized Communities in HebronH2”has the overall objective to contribute to the promotion of equitable access to essential and quality health services to communities in the old city of Hebron(H2)and have proper emergency preparedness.The overall objective will be accomplished through two specific objectives;(1)To increase the capacity of Hebron Emergency Center to provide essential health services to communities in the old city of Hebron(2)To increase the access to women’s health services,chronic disease services and access to medical supplies and lifesaving drugs in the targeted communities.This will be accomplished through two expected results(1)The capacity of the Center of HWC to provide essential health services is increased,this correspond to the first specific objective and(2)The communities in the old city of Hebron(H2)have received women’s health services,chronic disease services,emergency services and preparedness and medical supplies and lifesaving drugs are accessed through Hebron Emergency Center of HWC.The first expected result will be accomplished through the implementation of the following activity;providing the Hebron Emergency Center with basic medications and medical disposables.While the second expected result will be accomplished through the following activities;the Hebron Emergency Center provides healthcare services including primary health services,through the Women Health Program,the center provides services that focus primarily on early detection of diseases,especially breast and cervical cancer and on health education.In addition to the organization of open health days in which topics related to women and their health in all aspects of life,legal and human rights,are discussed.Moreover,GBV cases are to be detected and the women will receive legal, social and psychological services and referred when needed.Through the Chronic Disease Program,the center provides services which is essential for the early detection of complications of diabetes and therefore early treatment.It also provides educational sessions in areas of proper nutrition personal hygiene,and how to deal and cope with diabetes.The project will also involve emergency preparedness of 11 schools in H2and the schools are provided with essential first aid kits and fire extinguishers.2 teachers from each school will be trained for emergency preparedness and the students will receive training on evacuation

1 - Needs

The proposed project aims at addressing the problem of limited accessibility to primary healthcare services in the Old City of Hebron with special focus on women’s health and chronic disease. In 2018, the total population of Palestine has reached around 4.9 million Palestinian, 49.1% being female. With total fertility rates reaching 4.0 which has decreased from 6.0 in 1997, Women’s health services are of vital importance thus several indicators for SRH services have been reported at the national level; for antenatal care services, 95.5% of women aged 15-49 years received health care during pregnancy at least 4 times by any health care provider in 2014. And for postnatal care services, 89.7% of women 15-49 years in the West Bank received health care after birth, both during their stay in the health institutions or at home after birth or during a visit to post-natal care within two days of birth. This implies that there is high coverage for the antenatal care and postnatal care services. While in terms of family planning, 57.2% of married women aged 15-49 years in Palestine used a family planning method, 44.1% of currently married women aged 15-49 years used modern methods, and 13.1% used traditional methods. Despite the fact that there is high coverage for the family planning services, there is still unmet needs especially in H2 area. It is important to ensure the access to a full range of modern contraceptives, including emergency contraception, which should also be included in the essential medicines’ list to increase availability. In terms of chronic diseases, Palestine is facing challenges and constrains by diabetes mellitus on the Palestinian health system; there has been an increase on the prevalence of diabetes mellitus between 2000 and 2010; it was estimated that the prevalence of diabetes mellitus in 2000 was 9% for people aged 30 years and above. While in 2010, the prevalence of diabetes mellitus was estimated to be 15.3%, and expected to reach 20.8% by 2020. Moreover, it has been ranked as the 5th leading cause of death accounting for 6.4% of Palestinian deaths. Prevalence of chronic disease is increasing within women due to the increase in the elderly population of women. An increase in overweight and obese women has also been observed. This is related to the assigned reproductive role of women in most communities in Palestine. This limits the women’s physical activity to only house chores and taking care of the children, which even though consumes energy, is not considered a physical exercise to improve health. In many areas, it is not socially acceptable for women to exercise in public or even take walks. Moreover, constant increase in stress levels from the unhealthy lifestyle is proven to correlate with unstable blood sugar levels. All these factors, with the relatively high cost of gym subscriptions, prevents the women from leading a healthy and active lifestyle and eventually leads to increases in chronic disease. Females in the Palestinian society are still being exposed to GBV and the risks it carries. One third of women exposed to some sort of GBV especially from a spouse would rather stay silent about it, and that a huge percentage of 37% of Palestinian women previously married have been exposed to one or more forms of GBV. Of those, 58.6% have been exposed to emotional violence at least once, 55.1% to nonracial violence, 54.8% to social violence, 23.5% to physical violence and 11.8% were exposed to sexual violence from their spouses. HWC conducts needs assessments for areas with and within the community throughout our work in Hebron. This is done by meetings with community leaders, focus groups from women and men, feedback and assessments after services provided. The following document supports our justification National Strategy to promote gender equality, Violence Survey in the Palestinian Society, Country Assessment of the SRH and Right Situation in Palestine, PCBS.

2 - Activities

-Hebron Emergency Center is provided with basic medications and medical disposables. In order to ensure the quality, availability and accessibility to chronic and women’s health services, essential medications and medical supplies and disposables should be available. Thus purchasing of the listed required medications and supplies will be performed through this project. -Hebron Emergency Center provides primary health care and emergency services to the population in H2 area. Essential services for chronic diseases including the early detection and treatment of diabetes are provided. Hebron Emergency Center will be providing prevention and treatment of the most common diseases and emergency injuries. -Through the Women’s Health Program, SRH services including post and anti-natal, family planning and gynecological services are provided. Women’s Health Program’s services have been provided through Hebron Emergency Center in which Anti-natal & Post-natal services, family planning services, early detection of breast, cervical and gynecological cancer, clinical and gynecological services.-Identify women exposed to GBV and provide them with legal, psychological and social group and individual counseling and refer them to specialized organizations and institutions when necessary. -Organize and conduct open medical days to provide women’s health and chronic disease services to marginalized communities of the H2 area in Hebron The open days are planned to be conducted in enclosed areas where accessibility as well as availability is minimal, and residents in these areas have very difficult times trying to access health services. These areas include neighborhoods like Haret Jaber, Tel Irmeideh and the areas surrounding the Ibrahimi Mosque. -Conduct awareness sessions on topics of GBV, SRH and Chronic disease. This is supported by the community education program in which health awareness lectures are provided to raise awareness about healthy practices and healthy lifestyles. Moreover, the sessions will include group and individual sessions and counseling tackling GBV issues. -First Aid training and dealing with Emergency situations for 11 schools (6 boys and 5 girls’ schools) in H2. The schools are located in dangerous and exposed areas of H2 with high occurrences of emergency situations due to the increased Israeli military presence in the area. 2 teachers per school will be trained on specific emergency training and evacuation training will be conducted for the students for the entirety of the schools.-Provide the targeted schools with first aid kits and re extinguishers. The schools are located in marginalized H2 and area C locations so do not receive basic first aid kits and re extinguishers. 11 first aid kits and 11 fire extinguishers are to be provided for the schools.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

HWC proactively plan ahead to anticipate adverse effects of programs and to make adjustments as appropriate to prevent and curtail harm. Our organization tries to avoid negative effects on individuals and communities where they work, and to take steps for positive improvement of the security and dignity of these beneficiaries. We make sure that our activities do no harm, have no negative consequences or negatively impact the dignity of our beneficiaries. We as HWC priorities the safety, and dignity of all individuals (women, girls, boys and men) without discrimination, adopts a community-based approach to protection work that enables the active participation of communities in determining appropriate risk reduction measures particularly the poor and marginalized areas, and ensures all personal and protection sensitive information is managed responsibly. HWC is committed to assuring accountability to the beneficiaries provided the services and stakeholders in all aspects of the project. The design of the project took into consideration the different health and social needs of the different groups in the communities (sex and age). HWC has been working in H2 for more than 20 years, and through these years it has built strong relationship with the community and is trusted in providing services in the community. The community was part of the design of the project where the safe space (Ishraqa Center) in H2 area has a steering committee consists of different community members from the municipality, governorate, police department, and woman organizations.HWC staff are trained on identifying GBV victims and trained on providing confidential services. HWC is part of the organizations database when all cases of GBV are recorded in a confidential way.HWc was the main organization that developed the database with ALMuntada , (the Forum for combating violence against women )Additionally, the health awareness activity will respond to the needs of the communities and will be identified by the communities themselves. HWC staff has been working with different beighborhoods in H2 and a continuous assessment of their needs is performed.community feedback and complaints mechanism, which includes complain boxes, phone calls, face interviews,. These mechanisms are also expected to help HWC to identify and address potential issues and risks. Throughout the project HWC monitoring plan will include field visits, monthly reporting from the field staff to the director of the program who reports to the PHC director. Data will be disaggregated by sex and age and type of services.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The center has been established in Hebron since 2001; it was able through the past years to foster its relation with the local community. Several meetings took place during the last years with representatives of the different communities who stressed time and again the necessity to continue the services in the different selected villages. The team at the center does constant need assessments and evaluation of the rapid and changing community needs. These assessments are performed regularly with the Palestinian Civil Defense, community leaders and other parties. HWC mainstream disability in providing health services especially sexual and reproductive health (SRH) for women. Hebron emergency center in H2 area was rehabilitated to adapt and include persons with disabilities in programs and service, and the staff that consists of doctors, nurses, health educators and social workers was given several trainings on how to provide health services to people with disabilities and how to provide women with disabilities with SRH services. To further increase and build the capacity of our staff, HWC is in the process of preparing a guideline for service providers on how to provide services for people with disabilities specifically sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services for women with disabilities. Number of people provided services will be disaggregated by age and gender. Data gathered will be indicating the number of women with disabilities who were provided SRHS. HWC works in applying the humanitarian principles of humanity, impartiality, neutrality and independence in a relevant manner in concrete operational settings. The project will integrate humanitarian principles by providing the basic and much needed health services to individuals whose access to such services is limited. The services will be provided to any individual needing them impartially and without discrimination. The project will aim to establish and maintain access to basic and primary healthcare services to communities who lack such access and are affected by the conflict in the area and the discriminatory actions that the occupation takes against all Palestinians and especially in Hebron (H2) area. HWC staff will be reaching marginalized neighborhoods inside the old city of Hebron that suffer from moving restrictions and deprived from basic medical and health services. HWC has been mainstreaming disability in all its services. Health centers including H2 center has been adapted and rehabilitated to be accessible to PwD . Furthermore, the staff in H@ center were trained on how to provide services with , HWC provides the necessary medical services to the communities, and beneficiaries who needs referral will be advanced to specialized services. Additionally, the social worker, who will be part of the medical team, will work with them on the psychological aspect and provide counselling sessions. For the elderly the project will provide the medical treatment and the social worker will work with them on special needs, follow up to the elderly patient could prevent serious medical problems. Home visits will also be planned for those who can’t access the clinic. HWC Hebrom Emergency Center has Ishraqa Center that provides a safe space for women and girls victims of GBV. The center is staffed with experienced social worker, psychologist, legal counselor, in addition to the medical team in order to provide comprehensive services to women and girls . the medical center is the entry point to the women victims of violence as it is located inside the health center and can be accessed safely without the tabu of visiting a GBV service center .
