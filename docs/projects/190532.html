Project Summary

This project strives to contribute to improving the living condition of drought & conflict-affected beneficiaries by enabling IDPs to access dignified, healthy, and safe services. The proposed activities will improve protection against diseases that can arise due to lack of proper sheltering, bedding, cooking, and personal hygiene. Target beneficiaries will receive cluster-standard quantity and quality of emergency sheltering sets, climate-specific bedding sets, basic kitchen sets for cooking and eating, and hygiene sets to keep personal and family hygiene including menstrual hygiene, as well as for the collection and storage of water. The project will target 6,136HHs conflict affected IDPs and returnees in Oromia & Somali region(2360 HHs with NFI, 200HHs with shelter repair kits, 2960HHs with emergency sheltering and 1000HHs with ESNFIs) with target to contribute to cluster objectives CO2.1: Ensure contextualized access to lifesaving shelter and NFIs for displacement affected population with a focus on the most vulnerable and at-risk population group to safeguard their health security, privacy, and dignity & CO3.1 Contribute to improving protection outcomes by ensuring the conflict/displacement affected people have adequate safety, dignity, well-being, and equitable access to shelter solutions.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

SC will establish a FRM with multiple community-friendly channels, including both reactive (hotlines, suggestions box) and proactive (community satisfaction consultations, FGDs, Post Distribution Monitoring) channels and posters/advertisements to ensure that participants understand their entitlements and know how to raise concerns as needed. The FRM channels introduced in the project locations will allow targeted communities, including children, women, and marginalized people, to safely and confidentially report serious complaints (including child safeguarding concerns, sexual abuse and exploitation concerns and breaches of the code of conduct). SC will manage complaints in a timely, fair, and appropriate manner that prioritizes the safety of the complainant and those affected at all stages with confidentiality. SC’s MEAL Team will analyze and present the information from FRM to Senior Management to inform decision-making. SC’s MEAL team will work with the MEAL and Accountability officers at field offices to close the feedback loop, communicating with communities and children about the actions that have been taken based on their feedback.
