Project Summary

ATAA is planning to target children and youth including ECCD in locations as follows:Idleb governorate: Dana, Harim, Maaret Tamsrin, Teftnaz, Badama, Idleb sub-districts.Aleppo governorate: A'zaz, Al Bab, Jarablus, Aghtrin, Atareb, Daret Azze Jandairis, Afrin sub-districts. The 41 schools located in Aleppo & Idleb The proposed project will target children and youth from the age of 3 -24 who are in the ECCD, Basic, SecondaryVocational Education (Pre-school, 1st&12th grade) including 5-7% CwDs. These communities prioritized based on severity 5,4 &3. ATAA’s concern is towards the dropped-out children, those at the risk of being dropped out and newly displaced as a result of frequent displacement and difficult living conditions. The project will provide FE, NFE, ECCD, & TVET for 19897 (F 9746, M; 10151, CWD F; 127, CWD M; 189) in addition to 862 educational personnel (F339, M523), students (49% females and 51% males), staff (45% F & 55% M).
The project targets children with FE & NFE and provide incentives to education personnel and minor rehabilitation of schools, TLCs based on a rehabilitation need assessment and school furniture to be friendly environments to achieve safe, equitable and protective education considering COVID-19 measures. The project is cost-efficient as some of the targeted schools are equipped with furniture, fuel tanks, heaters, book corners, solar panels, textbooks,etc. FE (SIG curriculum) will be provided & NFE (ALP,Curriculum B, Remedial, Catch-up&SLP) for OOSC more than one academic year based on age & period of dropping out, TVET will be given to the dropped out and OOSC who are between 16 and 24 years who are at risk of being recruited to armed groups, early marriage & child labor. There will be an MoU signed with ED to recognize the students who got NFE in order to be referred to FE.
CWDs will be provided with tailored activities and facilities to come back to education (recreational, rehab of WASH facilities & classrooms). School safety plans include fire extinguishers, first aid kits, evacuation maps, drill and sharing information (banner, brochures, roll-up). Teacher recruitment and incentives payment will be strictly applied by ATAA, Regular teachers’ observation, PTA and TLC will be conducted on monthly basis, and training package to teachers (TICC, BLN, CSG, PSS, SLP, Distance Education, SEL, Safe learning). Monthly running cost to schools, COVID-19 schools’ disinfection and PPE provision to each school. Education process (physically, remotely, blended, Radio Education), apply distance education modality via a digital platform, WhatsApp groups. Summer program will include Catch-up-UP and remedial classes, SEL to 1st&4th grades, Base & end-line ASER, one BTL campaign per school to decrease the rate of dropping out. ATAA will invest in strengthening community by PTA, Provide ECCD, school in box materials, recreational, teachers & students kits in addition to coordination with UNICEF. Winterization (fuel & heaters) and individual support activities will be there also. Visual aids, textbooks printing, recreational, ECCD and school in box materials will be provided. Recreational and CP activities will be given and tailored in the targeted locations considering the INEE minimum standards along with teacher students’ ratio will be there. In the schools, maintain water tanks & taps to be gender-separated, provide drinkable water on a daily basis (ratio 10 liters/student/day). Rehabilitate latrine units and septic tanks, desludge them regularly for schools that not linked to sewer systems, connect some schools with the main sewer system. All activities mentioned will be reflected through both standard and custom indicators which in turn will be monitored and reflected using MEAL activities and tools classified in MEAL plan which also contains all of MoVs will be collected, target, timing, frequency and roles and responsibilities

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2022, an estimated 7.6 M people (83% children) need humanitarian education assistance and many children were OOSC or at risk of dropping out due to effect of the conflict, closure of learning facilities, and/or reduced learning hours due to COVID-19. Covid-19 appearance forced schools to move between physical, remote, and blended learning modalities. The project is in line with the three objectives: Increased safe and equitable access to FE & NFE education for crisis-affected children and youth, enhance the quality of FE & NFE education and strengthen the capacity of education & communities. ATAA has collected secondary data in sub-districts (A'zaz, Al Bab, Jarablus, Aghtrin, Atareb, Daret Azze Jandairis, Afrin sub-districts, Dana, Harim, Maaret Tamsrin, Teftnaz, Badama, Idleb sub-districts) to reflect how far the education status has been affected by the current conflict. The main reasons behind the non-enrolment of different school-aged children are low income, child labor and multi displacement. The proposed project will go in line with the prioritized activities by cluster to address the requirements of education in FE, NFE, ECCD & vocational education such as: monthly incentives, textbooks printing, stationery and recreational kits, school rehabilitation, WASH facilities, provision of winterization, safety school planning & equipment training, monthly PTA & TLC meetings & BTLs.

5 - Implementation of activities

ATAA will carry out direct implementation through coordinating with all stakeholders (Education Cluster & ED, local communities, &parents). Different curricula: FE & NFE (SIG, Curriculum B, catch up, ECCD & TVET). Rehabilitation will be very effective considering the schools including CWDs in which ramps, latrine will be equipped with special tools considering COVID-19 requirements & school disinfection & COVID-19 protective measures. Enrollment of students along with BTLs, recreational, ECCD & teacher kits, winterization, individual support, transportation will be provided. Baseline & end-line Placement test. Provide needed training as TICC, BLN, CSG, PSS, SLP, Distance Education, SEL, Safe learning. Hygiene promotion to increase the awareness of students &communities. CP activities & awareness raising sessions will be integrated such as PSS, SEL, referral mechanism in each school. TLCs & PTAs will be activated. CRM in schools using suitable methodologies will be activated, textbooks. MEAL: Checklist to monitor the construction of TECs, WASH facilities & rehabilitation of the existing ones & to verify their quality & suitability for CWDs, school safety will be measured by school safety checklist, trainings will be measured by both KAP survey & training evaluation & PDM will be conducted after each distribution, Physical & distance education modality will be monitored. Students will be paid month incentives (40$) to continue their vocational education.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ATAA PSEA policy was updated in December 2021 and shared with donors, and set a dedicated PSEA committee including (PSEA focal point, HR focal point, MEAL focal point, Grants and partnership focal point).ATAA adopted a core concept in all interventions to make PSEA and CSG mandatory in all projects and activities, in this regard, all ATAA staff, volunteers and contractors will got orientation sessions about PSEA and CSG policies and sign both PSEA and CSG code of conducts.All project locations will include banners and complaint boxes with delivering messages from accountability teams regarding the complaints and feedback channels in addition to ATAA Feedback and Complaints system regarding reporting and response of any PSEA case starting from receiving the report, to the investigation process, and provide aid and support to the survivor till the closure of the case after achieving the objectives of investigation.ATAA is PSEA network member participates in all regular network meetings, in addition to submit monthly reports to the network, in addition to conducting training for all staff about PSEA and targeting humanitarian workers in the intervention location to support the role of PSEA network.Gender balance will be a core point in the project within all teams (programs, media, MEAL) and locations will be accessed by women and girls safely.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

One of the biggest risks is the intervention of military factions or civilian entities in humanitarian work. To mitigate this, ATAA SSD depends on achieving community acceptance during all phases of projects and building a trust relationship with the local authorities by clarifying for the local authorities the impact of the project on the region. The SSD is committed to all humanitarian principles, especially neutrality and impartiality.•In a complex conflict environment, all changes in conflict are expected. Thus, the SSD follows any developments in the project area, conducts a daily risk assessment, and informs the crisis cell of possible scenarios and mitigations, including updating the evacuation and contingency plans (attached contingency plan)•In ATAA, the safety of field staff comes first, especially arrests, kidnappings, or even direct targeting. Therefore, the staff is trained on humanitarian principles, neutrality, movement clearance, as well as the provision of S&S equipment. ATAA is committed to the duty of care towards staff in accordance with the HR policy.•In terms of COVID-19, As a part of risk management, ATAA trains all staff on personal hygiene and continuously measuring staff temperatures in addition to providing medical masks and hand sanitizers. ATAA will distribute brochures electronically through WhatsApp groups to the local authorities and community on COVID-19 as possible.
