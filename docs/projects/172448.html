Project Summary

The COVID-19 pandemic has exposed the vulnerabilities in the health system with severe disruption of essential and lifesaving services. The Primary Health care delivery network including the vaccination program was unable to sustain continuity of vaccination service exposing children to vaccine preventable diseases. 1. PHC service 2. Vaccination services 3. Nutrition interventions 4. Treatment of childhood illnesses. Moreover, displaced people, refugees and migrants are an additional burden to the already overstretched health and nutrition system, unable to provide responsive services in a timely manner. The most vulnerable women and children are at risk of increased morbidity and mortality if the situation remains same or further deteriorate. Hence their is urgent need to ensure availability of essential and life-saving services in the given situation of the covid-19 pandemic., where needs have amplified due to poor human and institutional capacities. Through this project implemented in coordination with relevant departments of ministry of Health UNICEF will contribute in improvement and continuity of health and nutrition services with particular focus on vulnerable population and geographical areas.

1 - Needs

The COVID-19 pandemic has severely disrupted the already compromised health delivery network in the country enhancing the vulnerabilities of the population to preventable diseases and death. The Primary Health care delivery network including the vaccination program was unable to sustain continuity of vaccination service exposing children to vaccine preventable diseases. The ongoing conflict and political fragility resulting in huge gaps in Maternal, newborn and child health and nutrition services exposing population to increased levels of vulnerability. In the given situation the critical needs include mitigating the impact of COVID-19 pandemic through a) Continuity of PHC service-urgent provision of essential and lifesaving medicines and equipment to health facilities especially in West, south (most affected) and east b) Ensure availability of Maternal and Newborn Care to prevent morbidity & mortality c) Prevent major childhood diseases and establish and implement Integrated Management of Childhood Illnesses strategy for prevention and treatment of major childhood killer d) Establish the system for timely screening and appropriate treatment of children with all kinds of malnutrition including Severe Acute malnutrition and micro nutrient deficiencies e) regular updating of health and nutrition of the children and women for need based support f) strengthening quality and coverage of vaccination interventions and preparation and response to vaccine preventable diseases outbreaks g) regular monitoring of the situation h) reaching masses with key messages to prevent spread of diseases i) ensure infection prevention and control in health facilities.

2 - Activities

The activities are planned to be undertaken in line with COVID-19 guidelines and precautionary measures are 1. Provision of essential medicines and equipments 2. Contribute in Strengthening vaccination against childhood vaccine preventable diseases and timely response to outbreaks. 3. Strengthening and scaling up Infant and Young Child Feeding practices, identification, treatment and prevention of malnutrition. 4. Contribute in evidence generation through DHIS and SMART Nutrition Survey 5. Capacity building on Risk communication, Health and Nutrition in Emergencies, Maternal, Newborn and Child health and Disease Outbreaks-pandemic prevention and Response 6. Monitoring of activities and situation for appropriate response 7. Promote use of lifesaving vaccines and behaviours
