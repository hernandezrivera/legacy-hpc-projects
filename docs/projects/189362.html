Project Summary

The South Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview 2022 found an estimated 835,000 people were affected by severe flooding between May and December 2021, and many flood-displaced people, including those who were displaced by the 2020 floods were unable to return to their homes until early 2022, if at all.
The cumulative impact of recurrent flooding contributed to the destruction and damage to water facilities, leaving vulnerable people in urgent need of water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services, and intensified existing vulnerabilities of affected people, which include limited access to basic services, high prevalence of disease outbreaks, and widespread displacement. Covariate climatic, conflict and economic shocks, as well as other household-level stressors, for example, gender-based violence (GBV) and poor water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services, are some of the key drivers of humanitarian needs in the country(See: South Sudan: Humanitarian Needs Overview 2022).
No market assessment has been conducted in Cueibet county for a long time, however, HARECOD is prioritizing market assessment, and then integrate these local markets in our programming.
Protection concerns are present when accessing WASH facilities and services across all counties of South Sudan. 18 per cent of households reported feeling unsafe while collecting water (FSNMS, 2021). Of those, 12 per cent of women and girls reported avoiding water points due to GBV concerns (FSNMS, 2021).
HARECOD is proposing a project in line with the achievement of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals Number 6 which advocates for available and sustainable Water and sanitation for All worldwide. The project is also aligned to the implementation of the South Sudan WASH Cluster strategy 2022-2023 and aligned to the WASH HRP 2022-2023 guidance for Partners.
The target county: Cueibet is a HRP 2022 priority 1 county and is in WASH severity classification 4(critical phase) and is a flood affected county experiencing conflicts leading to massive displacement of populations.
The purpose of this project is to achieve WASH Cluster sectoral objective 2.1 and 2.2 that are respectively; Provision of basic WASH services in emergencies and WSC 4/5 and Mitigation of WASH related GBV.
To achieve the WASH cluster objectives, HARECOD will provide lifesaving WASH emergency interventions to improve access to safe water, secured and gender appropriate sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion, which will be integrated with Mitigation of Gender Based Violence.
This project aims to increase access to safe and dignified services leading to provision of portable safe water from improved water sources and improvement of sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion for households in Cueibet and for communities nearby to reduce the risk of water-borne diseases particularly flood-prone areas of Malou-pec, Abiriu, Mayath, Chitcok, and Pagor payams.
The capacity of hygiene promoters, community mobilisers, and county health staff shall be improved to promote WASH key messages within the community; with WASH related promotional materials provided at all community and facility hot spots such as schools, markets, cattle camps, community centers. HARECOD will ensure these messages are translated to the local languages and Arabic for easy of understanding.
HARECOD will recruit, train, and deploy community WASH focal points to strengthen and reinforce messages earlier provided to communities. These focal points will consist of 60% women and 40% men. This will also help to mitigate WASH-related GBV, and protection risks that particularly women and children face when spending long hours at the boreholes.
A WASH assessment integrated with GBV.

1 - Needs

Cueibet county is a WASH Cluster Severity Classification priority 1 county and is in WASH Severity Classification 4 which is critical and could likely lead to catastrophic phase 5 if priority is not given to this county which is a flood affected county in Lakes state experiencing repeated intra-communal conflicts coupled with the continued displacements due to Floods, food insecurity, and conflicts. This has negatively impacted access to basic WASH services resulting to more than 114, 426 vulnerable persons in dire need of basic WASH services under critical WASH Severity Classification (WSC) phase 4. The affected populations represent 49.7 percent of the WASH HRP target in Lake’s state and 3.4 percent of the overall WASH Cluster target of 3,347,598 persons in need of WASH humanitarian assistance in 2022. (WASH Cluster HRP Target PIN 2022). Over 8,561 households were reported to have been affected by Floods in Cueibet county according to a latest IRNA assessment conducted in September 2021 by United Nations agencies, including HARECOD leading the WASH Cluster with CEDS and Caritas(see: IRNA Report-Flood Assessment in Cueibet County Lakes State_September 2021 | Assessment & Analysis Knowledge Management Platform (hpc.tools)) HARECOD is targeting 38,142(6,357 Households, at 6persons per a Household) vulnerable persons representing 33.3 percent of WASH HRP target for Cueibet and 1.1 percent of the overall WASH target for 2022 countrywide. We are prioritizing 4,577 children under 5(8% Male and 4 % Females), 16,401 children within age range of 5-17 years (23% Male, 20% Female), we shall target 12,968 adults aged 18-60(18% Male, 17% female), and the elderly represent 11 percent of our HRP target translating to 4,196 adults aged 60 above. According to the Humanitarian Needs Overview, South Sudan 2022(see: South Sudan: Humanitarian Needs Overview 2022 | HumanitarianResponse), floods regularly damage existing sanitation infrastructure and given their annual occurrence and paired with frequent displacement, there is great hesitation by communities to invest in, reconstruct, or repair sanitation facilities. Cueibet county has experienced annual heavy flooding and intra-communal conflicts leading to mass displacement and destruction of sanitation facilities. The masisve flooding in Cueibet county led to the collapse of over 408 households as found during a Rapid Needs Assessment in 2021(IRNA Report-Flood Assessment in Cueibet County Lakes State_September 2021 | Assessment & Analysis Knowledge Management Platform (hpc.tools). The collapsed households were in Cueibet, Ngap, Duony, Malou-Pec, Mayath, Pagor, Abiriu, Chitcok, Tiap-tiap. The affected populations were displaced and left behind necessary WASH materials such as hygiene kits making them vulnerable to WASH related diseases such as diarrhoea, cholera, and skin diseases. An IRNA Multi-sectoral assessment in Cueibet county conducted in September 2021 where WASH Cluster was led by HARECOD, CEDS, and Caritas Diocese of Rumbek found that all four payams of Abiriu, Duony, Mayath, and Malou Pec had over 10 boreholes and hand dug wells broken and submerged in flood water limiting access to safe water to over 5000 vulnerable persons. Limited access to WASH infrastructure coverage alone, for instance, water points and latrines has carry-on impacts when utilizing a multi-sectoral view. Poor access to WASH services and goods combined with high levels of food insecurity has a detrimental impact on the health of the most vulnerable people, as seen through the high prevalence of malnutrition. Cueibet county has seen latrines, water points including boreholes, and crops destroyed due to heavy flooding leading to displacement of people which has made them more vulnerable to water-borne diseases such as diarrhoea in children under 5 raising prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM).

Activity 1: Carryout Market based assessment in all payams 2. Conduct WASH specific assessment and safety audit Intergrated with WASH related GBV risk questions 3. Conduct community inclusive project design discussions to improve on designs and implementation and Intergrated further areas of disability, design of sanitation facilities for high at-risk groups, localized hygiene promotion, etc.4. Procure WASH hygiene materials, prioritizing local market to improve on beneficiaries coping mechanisms5. Procure standard and special toolkits for sanitation infrastructure repairs and rehabilitation 6. Conduct training of project stakeholders(Local authorities, County officials, project staff, and women and leaders of at-risk groups) on WASH, Conflict sensitivity, AAP, Disability inclusion, GBV MITIGATION, Gender and Child protection, etc.7. Conduct Training of community hygiene promoters and Water User committees on hygiene promotion practices, water Revenue Management, household water treatment, etc.8. Conduct water quality testing, analysis and chlorination at all targeted water sources.9. Repair or rehabilitate non-functional water facilities especially boreholes, hand dug wells, etc.10. Conduct Intergrated hygiene and sanitation promotion to impact and improve on prevention and mitigation of WASH related diseases.11. Distribute WASH hygiene kits including water treatment tablets to all targeted households in a safe manner. 12. Printing and Distribution of promotional materials with messages in local languages and national spoken Arabic and English. 13. Conduct monthly activity Review meetings with all project stakeholders and act on areas highlighted for improvements.14. Conduct End-Line and behavioural change Surveys and submit reports to WASH Cluster and donors.
