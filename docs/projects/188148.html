Project Summary

The planned project is not secured and will will be implemented during 2022-2023, the planned project will increase the self-resilience in the targeted communities by supporting most ‎vulnerable households with sustainable livelihood through providing under-conflict doable income ‎generation sources, The project will assist internal displaced and host communities households with ‎‎400 milk cows for 400 HHs to be as an income generator, supported with practical vocational shorth-‎term training about cow breeding conducted by senior vet trainers, trained new bread winners will be ‎supported throughout 6 months with a close veterinarian backup by providing vet care and treatments ‎field by expert veterinaries, moreover the project will strengthen the created livelihood by
supporting cows with the required fodder for 6 months. In addition to the provided livestock the ‎project will support the wheat value chain in the communities with high severity by providing 400 most ‎vulnerable farmers with wheat cultivating inputs for 1 hectare, the following inputs will be provided: ‎‎250 Kg/he seeds and irrigation vouchers for 50 mm irrigation depth, 20% of another vulnerable farmers ‎yield
will be procured to supply a complement QRCS project with flour. QRCS also will support mid-scale ‎agricultural practices by supporting 800 most vulnerable farmers who planting 0.5 hectare with ‎legumes by providing seeds, fertilizing materials, and irrigation vouchers for 200 mm of irrigation ‎water, and supporting 200 most vulnerable farmers who have olives trees by providing services, ‎fertilizing materials, and irrigation vouchers for 200 mm of irrigation water.‎

4 - Needs

Today, 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance 21 per cent increase ‎compared to 2020 - with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline.‎IDP’s income and livelihoods situation, food insecurity status, settlement and shelter conditions, and ‎access to basic services are inter-related. Similar to overall trends, two-thirds of IDP households report ‎being unable to meet the basic needs of all household members, and the majority of IDPs prioritize ‎access to food and livelihood support..193 Of all population groups, IDPs have the largest relative ‎income deficit, with income insufficiency reported in all governorates The situation in Syria has ‎drastically deteriorated, with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and ‎agriculture assistance, representing 68.6 per cent of the total population. Based on a countrywide ‎assessment of 36,484 households and technical reviews and consultations, at least 12.4 million people ‎are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. ‎HNO, 2021 advanced copyQRCS implemented a need assessment in 2021, The findings showed that the food consumption costs ‎more than 80% of the families living expenses 54.5% of the communities, which confirm the low ‎income of the populations in the targeted communities. 5 - Implementation of activities

The project intervention will be throughout: - Providing 400 milk cows in addition to fodder and vet ‎materials for 6 months. - Providing in-kind 100MT of wheat seeds for 400 farmers, with fuel vouchers. - ‎Providing in-kind legumes seeds and fertilizing materials for another 400 farmers, with fuel vouchers. - ‎Providing agricultural services, and fertilizing materials for 400 olives farmers, with fuel vouchers - ‎Purchasing 320 MT of wheat yield from 400 wheat farmers. Project implementation cycle will start ‎from coordination with the communities representatives and local councils, performing livestock ‎marketassessments to evaluate cows, fodder, and seeds availability. Beneficiaries selection will be built ‎according to their vulnerability and need prioritizing displaced families affected by last escalation with ‎the focus on female/children headed HHs and disabled persons, the project has been designed to ‎mitigate the conflict consequences on adolescents as well by included them in the targeted ‎beneficiaries. Qatar Red crescent society will implement the project by Syrian expertise through an ‎infield team with wide knowledge of the Syrian context and belong to the targeted communities, the ‎field team will comprise: vet experts, field, logistics officers and workers, all members have high ‎awareness and long experience in the humanitarian service providing and had been trained on GBV, ‎PSEA, doing no harm, protection and security aspects.
