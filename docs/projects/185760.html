Project Summary

As a result of ongoing protracted conflict causing unpredictable and continuous large- scale displacement, recurrent large-scale cholera and acute watery diarrhea (AWD)AWD epidemics, the COVID-19 pandemic, and seasonal flooding, fierce storms and winds, north- east Nigeria continues to experience multifaceted humanitarian crisis, and severely impacting access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) . The main drivers of vulnerabilities related to WASH are closely linked to the conflict- induced and recurrent population displacement, and are also a result of pre-existing low level of WASH services in the Northeast. An estimated 2.8 million people across north-east Nigeria remain in critical need of sustained and equitable access to clean water and dignified hygiene and sanitation services (WASH Sector Gap analysis and MSNA 2020 data). 1.02 million people receive less than 15 liters of safe clean water per person per day while about 1.06 million people have intermittent water access with over 500 meters walking distance and over 30 minutes queuing time at water points
In response to the needs identified and in contribution to the Nigeria WASH Sector objectives 1,2, 3 and 4, IOM will scale-up its provision of WASH services targeting internally displaced people and returnees in camp, camp-like settings, as well as and hosting community in BAY (Borno, Adamawa and Yobe) States.
IOM will (A) Ensure safe access to water facilities and services through the drilling, installation, maintenance, rehabilitation and water quality monitoring of boreholes; (B) Ensure safe and equitable access to sanitation facilities (latrines, showers including laundry spaces), handwashing stations) through the operation and maintenance (O&M) of existing facilities and the construction of additional gender segregated facilities and waste water treatment system; (C) Promote improved public health outcomes in targeted settings through the systematic promotion of personal and environmental hygiene messages (following national guidelines on COVID-19 preventive measures, such as physical distancing) and including COVID-19 preventive measure; and the support for increased capacity of established gender-balanced WASH committees (D) Ensure environmental sanitation (solid waste management) in operation area through the establishment of WASH Committees including- male and female community members, procurement and distribution of tools and equipment; (E) Distribute appropriate WASH NFI following the national/ cluster standards; and (F) Conduct capacity building of WASH partners and stakeholders with an overall aim at also mitigating the risks linked to the COVID pandemic.
The project will be implemented in three states: Borno (13 LGAs), Adamawa (5 LGAs) and Yobe State (8 LGAs), targeting an estimated 757,620 individuals.
IOM ensures that design and implementation of all WASH activities take into consideration the age, gender as well as disability-specific needs of project beneficiaries, and specifically addresses women and girls’ needs, such as Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM). All activities related to this project will benefit women and men, girls and boys and will be delivered on a sustainable and equitable basis. In regard to assessments, monitoring and evaluation of gender, age and disability specific needs and the impact of activities, IOM will engage with community structures on the ground in these locations during missions and set-up communication channels where possible.
Using the IOM WASH Monitoring and Evaluation Framework, IOM will ensure that “Do No Harm” principles are adhered to throughout the WASH interventions, particularly in WASH activities pertaining to latrine and water point locations and construction, through the use of various mechanisms, such as consultations with vulnerable groups.

1 - Needs

WASH needs, mainly access to clean, sufficient and safe water for drinking, hygiene and sanitation have continued to increase in scale and severity across north-east Nigeria The spread of COVID-19 has increased needs for improved hygiene practices through improved hand-hygiene facility access and hygiene awareness Access to safe water has been constrained as a result of new and protracted displacement as well as returns (self- organized and government-led) resulting in host communities registering a reduced resilience due to high pressure on the existing and already overburdened water points and sanitation facilities. New displacements and challenging groundwater potential in some areas have resulted in need for emergency water provision through water trucking, and increased costs for in-depth hydrogeological studies and optimization of water points to ensure water resources are used effectively. Access to sanitation continues to be diminished due to wear and tear of emergency type construction, continuous displacements and seasonal flooding leading to high open defecation and reduced handwashing practices. Sanitation needs for persons with special needs, women and children to ensure safe and equitable access are a critical gap in the sector, which requires additional resources as other issues of proximity and design of the facilities are considered. The WASH and Health sectors continue to work together on disease early warning and cholera prevention campaigns coupled with environmental hygiene to prevent high AWD cases and mitigate cholera outbreaks. Recently, the WASH sector has further rationalized partners’ operations to ensure more accountability to beneficiaries and better coverage of needs with reduced funding, which has improved the quality of the response. IOM, as a WASH Sector partner, continues to respond to the WASH needs in north-east Nigeria. IOM WASH services will not be limited to the planned camps – but also to the new arrival sites, transit and reception centers, spontaneous camps and camp-line settings, and host communities with limited or no WASH services in BAY States.

2 - Activities

Provision of safe and equitable access to a sufficient quantity of water for affected population through drilling of new boreholes and installation of hand pumps; rehabilitation and upgrading of existing boreholes; water quality monitoring; capacity building of water system maintenance committees and WASH committees. Provision of safe and dignified access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of new sanitation facilities (latrines and showers); rehabilitation and upgrading of existing sanitation facilities; operation and maintenance of sanitation facilities; desludging of latrines; construction of waste water treatment and disposal systems; provision of hand washing stations inclusive of soap at public sanitation facilities and institutions; provision of solar lamps at blocks of latrines and showers; Provision of safe and dignified access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of household latrines as per sector’s standard. - Provision of safe and dignified access to improved sanitation facilities through desludging and cleaning of latrines as per sector’s standard. -Provision of safe and dignified environment through provision of sustained environmental sanitation services (solid waste management). -Provision of safe and dignified access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of sanitation facilities in institutions and relevant public places provided with adequate gender segregated sanitation facilities (latrines, showers, handwashing stations) and services as per sector’s standard and in consultation with the community. Provision of waste bins and waste disposal pits and sites; and transportation of garbage to disposal sites. Affected communities benefit from community tailored gender and age-sensitive personal and environmental hygiene messages aiming to promote hygienic behavior and practices through sensitization sessions with community members and stakeholders to minimize the spread of water borne diseases – including cholera and AWD, and COVID-19 pandemic; establishment and/or continuation of WASH Committees and provision of WASH facility maintenance tools; establishment of School Hygiene Clubs; training of Hygiene Promoters and conducting hygiene promotion activities; cholera preparedness and response activities; distribution of hygiene, WASH NFI and menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) kits, production and dissemination of contextualized Information, Education and agreed Communication (IEC) materials. Conducting Safety audits on WASH facilities and consultations with key informants. Provision of safe and dignified access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of new sanitation facilities (latrines and showers). Provision of safe and dignified access to improved sanitation facilities through establishment and/or continuation of WASH committee composed of 50% women. -Distribution of hygiene, cholera and MHM kits complemented by fund transfer modalities such as in-kind solutions or cash-based interventions depending on the context and where feasible Strengthen the resilience of conflict affected people and promote early recovery, durable solutions and social cohesion - Establishment and training of community-based water management committees for boreholes, to enable equitable access to water to all community members, conflict resolution and sustained operation and maintenance of the facility. Support and build capacity of local water bureaus and technical staffs; regular water quality monitoring in the municipal settings.
