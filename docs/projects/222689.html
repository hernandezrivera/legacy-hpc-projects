Project Summary

CIDOs’ 2025 project intends to deliver life saving static and mobile services to the most vulnerable children and their caregivers affected by shocks attributed to displacement by conflict and floods ,diseases and famine. This comprises of returnees, Host community and internally displaced population ( men women, boys , girls , young people and adolescents) living in Jonglei state ( Akobo and Pibor counties ) . The total target population is 9500 vulnerable individuals ( 5,000 girls, 5500 boys, 2500 women and 2000 men). In Akobo CIDO will target 5000 individuals ( 1500 boys,1500 girls, 1000 women, 1000 men) and in Pibor CIDO will target 4500 individuals ( 1500 boys ,1500girls,750 women,750 men).The primary purpose of the project is to address compounding effects of converging factors i.e displacement ,severe food insecurity /loss of livelihoods, economic decline, protection threats and lack of services which contributes and aggravates the prevalence of violence against children and adolescents. This project will focus on both preventive and responsive aspects of Child protection and work closely with GBV , FSL, Health ,S/NFI WASH, Education and Nutrition partners to ensure an integrated approach for a holistic service delivery that entrenches resilience and sustainability of the services rendered to the affected children, adolescents and their care givers affected by multiple shocks. The intervention will be guided by the IASC guidelines for child protection ,national SOPs and cluster strategy. The response is strategic to the attainment of Cluster objectives and aligned to activities, indicators, and outcomes of the HRP 2025.The project activities, focus on mitigating child protection risks, preventing and responding to child protection cases through; CFS programming, capacity building of frontline service providers on CPiE, Case management including FTR services , Structured/non-structured PSS, community Outreach and dialogues, and safe referrals. It is expected that by the end of the project period, lives of vulnerable women and girls, young people and their caregivers will be saved and that they will have increased access to multi sectoral services and better coping mechanisms. The project activities will be coordinated well with other protection partners and will draw learnings and good practices from CIDOs existing child protection project implemented in Akobo and Pibor with support from BHA/USAID.

1. Needs

Various protection assessments indicate that more children and adolescents, including those with disabilities, are at heightened risk of violence, abuse, and exploitation and need critical child protection services. The situation for children has deteriorated over 2024, with continued incidents of recruitment, abuse, exploitation and other grave violations. According to CIDOs’ Beneficiary assessment report of Pibor county ( October 2024) , 66 girls have been subjected to sexual violence since the beginning of the year and 48 have dropped out of school and forced into child marriages , 46 boys and 12 girls have escaped from armed groups while over 88 children abducted in 2023 remain unaccounted for. According to IRNA assessment of Akobo county conducted in October 2024, there are over 21,000 returnees from Sudan living in Akobo without services.There is a critical gap in regards to child protection services in Akobo where CIDO is covering only 2 payams. Akobo and Pibor are counties affected by seasonal flooding yearly and this further complicates accessibility of child protection services with increased child labour cases and transactional sex among young girls. In Akobo and Pibor, community displacement is very intensive due to cycles of revenge killings and cattle raiding . Notably forced early marriage of girls is common attributed to subnational violence and revenge attacks among Pibor and Akobo communities . Adolescent girls are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information. CIDO has an ongoing CAAFAG project in Pibor which targets 400 CAAFAG children in need Comprehensive Case Management and re-integration services. In 2025 CIDO will build on the existing project to provide capacity building to caregivers on appropriate and culturally acceptable child care practices. CIDO intends to coordinate with other sectoral partners like GBV, Health, Nutrition and Education to provide integrated basic and comprehensive services to children. By establishing community child protection and support groups for prevention and support of children with long-term psychosocial needs, about 120 community child protection committee members will need training on age appropriate MHPSS and how to report PSEA.
