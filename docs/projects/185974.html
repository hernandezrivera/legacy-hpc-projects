Project Summary

This project is aimed at improving sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water, reducing the risk for poor hygiene-related mobility and reinforcing the capacities of local actors in improving WASH services and resilience for vulnerable IDPs and host population in Donga Mantung, Menchum and Momo Divisions of the North West Region. The goal of the project is to reduce mortality and morbidity, reduce protection needs, reduce vulnerability and strengthen resilience of affected populations in these divisions within the ongoing Anglophone crisis. The main objective of the project is to provide safe drinking water, sanitation facilities like toilets with hand washing facilities attached, communal hand washing stations, distribution of WASH kits to vulnerable IDPs and affected host populations in the 3 Divisions.
The worsening security situation of the ongoing Anglophone crisis in the two English Speaking regions of the North West and South West Regions and the COVID-19 pandemic has a major impact on the WASH sector according to the WASH Cluster. In the report, WASH cluster said more than 600,000 people are in urgent need of WASH services in the North West and South West Regions. According to the MSNA of October 2021, 51.3% of HH in the NWSW do not have access to potable water and depend on unprotected and surface water sources for drinking water and domestic use. Up to 47% of HH do not have access to containers for water storage and purification, 47% of all villages access have very few water points, 22% with non-functional water points and 33% with water points too far to b access by the vulnerable populations. 59.9% in Donga Mantung, 74.2% in Menchum and 46.3% in Momo of IDP and returnee HH suffers from these challenges.
As far as access to basic sanitation is concerned, out of the 3656 villages covered in the MSNA of October 2021, 1146 villages use unprotected latrines, 1075 report insufficient latrines, 843 indicated their latrines had no privacy, 128 villages complained the latrines are dangerous and risky especially for children, the sick and the elderly, while 46 villages practice open defecation with villages from Donga Mantung, Menchum and Momo Divisions affected the most.
Regarding Hand washing facilities, 31% of villages covered reported that nearly no one has access to HWF and up to 51% reported that just a few people had access to HWF. In addition, the lack of financial means limits access to hygiene supplies like soap. More than 76% of girls and women in these communities do not also have access to menstrual hygiene management kits.
This project would intervene through; construction, rehabilitation and extension of 6 water schemes, construction of 9 VIP gender sensitive latrines with hand washing facilities, distribution of WASH and menstrual management kits to 600 HH for IDPs and vulnerable host populations to improve personal hygiene, and capacity building for WASH actors in the targeted communities. The creation of gender sensitive water management committees and complaint/feedback mechanisms would be carried out to ensure accountability to the populations at all levels of the project. The project would recruit a staff force of 14 to ensure the effective implementation of the project. The Specific objectives of the project would be to;
i. To improve sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water for 3600 IDPs and host population
ii. To reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity of 3600 IDPs and host population
iii. To reinforce capacities and coordination of WASH activities in the 3 targeted divisions for 18 WASH Actors.
The project would be targeting IDPs and vulnerable host community members specifically in hard to reach bush settlements in the divisions that are presently facing WASH challenges

1 - Besoins / Needs

Following the degeneration of the ongoing Anglophone crisis in the NWSW, WASH situations have become very critical especially for the vulnerable populations within the NWSW regions. There has been increasing lack of safe and sufficient quantity of water for drinking, cooking, bathing, washing and other domestic uses, inadequate functional and improved sanitation facilities in the form of toilets/hand washing facilities with increasing open defecation especially for IDP informal settlements, and a lack of WASH commodities for proper water collection and storage. Many women and girls especially in the hard to reach conflict hit areas like bushes and some peri-urban communities in the targeted divisions do not have access to any form of menstrual management kits. They depend on dry tree leaves and worn out dresses for the sanitary pad needs which contributed to significant health complications among these women. These needs includes 1. To improve sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water for 3600 IDPs and host population in Donga Mantung, Menchum and Momo Divisions in the North West region where the majority of the population depends on unprotected/surface water sources which are not treated. This would be by; - Recruiting and Building the capacity of project staff (Project manager, WASH Engineer, Divisional Field Coordinator, M & E officer) -Construction, Rehabilitation and extension of 9 water schemes 3 in each division - Training of the community on household on water treatment techniques - Site identification for the construction of gender sensitive VIP latrines- Construction of 9 VIP gender sensitive latrines in IDP sites and Host communities 2. To reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity of 3600 IDPs and host population - Identification of vulnerable IDPs and host populations to benefit from the WASH kits - Purchase and transportation of WASH kits (2 jerry cans, 2 buckets, 6 drinking cups and a mosquito net)- Purchase and transportation of menstrual hygiene management kits - Distribution of WASH kits to girls, boys, women and men- Distribution of Menstrual hygiene management kits to girls and women in the targeted communities - Provision of hand washing facilities in the communities - Provision of WASH supplies (6 bars of 250g of Soap, tooth paste, tooth brush and aqua tabs) to 600HH- Monthly Awareness raising campaigns or promotion of good wash practices in the targeted communities 3. To reinforce capacities and coordination of WASH activities in the 3 targeted divisions for 18 WASH Actors - Carry out 1 WASH survey in each of the Divisions targeted - Identify and train community mobilizers on WASH issues - form and train 9 water management committees for the 9 water projects in the 3 divisions - Training of Community feedback committees and establishing of feedback and reporting channels to ensure accountability to affected populations - Mainstream GBV/Protection in the project. The project would have the following expectations - 3600 IDPs and host populations have sustainable access to safe drinking water - 3600 IDPs and host population gain access to sustainable basic sanitation services - Capacities of 60 local actors reinforced through trainings to reach out to over 60% of the targeted communities through sensitization on WASH issues
