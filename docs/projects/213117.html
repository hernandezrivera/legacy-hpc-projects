Project Summary

Needs:
According to the HNO 2024, approximately estimated 6.8 M PIN are in need for shelter
assistance in Syria.
In different housing contexts, 38% of 21.3 million people in housing/apartments (finished
or unfinished), are estimated to live in partially safe conditions, or unsafe conditions.
Furthermore, among the top shelter adequacy issues identified are the lack of access to
electricity/lighting (24%), and leakage from roof and ceiling (20%).
Intervention
In addition to HNO, PIN and severity scale, GOPA-DERD has gathered its assessment from
the response plan through sector reports, information from GOPA-DERD centers and
offices team operating in all governorates, In addition to its own in-house registration
system and R&D Unit.
Accordingly, GOPA-DERD aims to intervene under Sector Objective 2 and adhere to the
Sustainable Development Goal 11: “Sustainable Cities and Communities” - making cities
and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable.
As such, the proposed activities are focused on Activity (2.2) Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing in Rural Damascus, Hama, Homs, Latakia, Tartous and Aleppo: For 850 HHs, 4250
individuals in Hama (100), Aleppo (150), Latakia (200), Homs (175), Rural Damascus (125)
and Tartous (50), in addition to Activity (2.2.b) Supply and installation of HH level solar energy systems, for the rehabilitated houses for 850 HHs to mitigate suffering from lack of electrical supply in Hama, Aleppo, Latakia, Homs, Rural Damascus and Tartous.
PRA:
Risk (R)/ Likelihood (L)/ Impact (I) / Mitigation measures (M)
(R)1 tension in community due to different assistance packages, unclear beneficiary
selection criteria leading to a feeling of discrimination.
(L): High, (I): Low (M): Standardize assistance package - Ensure beneficiary selection
criteria is uniform agreed among sector members and with the government- Raise
awareness and engage community leaders through a clear selection criterion- poster can
be hung in public areas- Applying GOPA-DERD complaint at community level- Training for
operational and MEAL staff.
(R)2: Physical safety of beneficiaries/communities are at risk due to structural integrity of the building and/or the presence of ERWs.
(L): High, (I): Low (M): Structural assessment of buildings by qualified personnel-targeting structurally safe buildings- Ensure the site safety with related local authorities, communities and agencies involved in ERW clearance- aware contractors, workers staff on risks of ERW and procedures-
(R)3: Lack of documents to prove property/occupancy rights and civil documentation.
(L): High, (I): Medium (M): Work with MoLA to ensure that alternative documents to ownership are respected by all governorates- Work with the local authorities and partners to establish a mechanism to deal with such issues at local level- Liaise with mayor/municipality to ensure owner has the correct title deed.
PSEA:
(R): Possibility of exposure to exploitation and sexual abuse of beneficiaries by restoration supervisors and technical workers- Sexual exploitation by contractors or from the local community base- There are unreported cases of victims.
(M): Awareness for all volunteers, contractor and beneficiaries on the principles of PSEA and GOPA-DERD’s reporting mechanism - awareness posters on PSEA within offices-Follow up on reported complaints, if any, and take the necessary procedures- Signing by volunteers and contractors a pledge to commitment to the policy of PSEA- Commitment to the IASC Core Principles on Sexual Exploitation and Abuse.
Risks, assumptions/ mitigation:
•no access to targeted area for security reasons/ continuous coordination with related authorities and stakeholders and development of alternative plans
•Lack of materials and workers /closely supervision for works, flexibility, market analysis
•Currency inflation may lead to deficiency in budget for covering needed works/ finishing work ASAP, monitoring of prices and economic situation.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

GOPA-DERD will conduct the following activities: • Activity 2.2: Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing for 800 HHs • Activity 2.2.b: Supply and installation of HH level solar energy systems for Rehabilitated houses for 800 HHs. Implementation of activities: The real and practical way of implementing the required work, which is compatible with all procedures documented by GOPA-DERD Engineering Unit, is the execution of the work by contractors who are experienced in the fi eld of architectural, civil projects and solar power project, including all damages in the buildings that will be repaired as follows: - The works will be carried out according to the tendering method followed by GOPA-DERD through a dedicated contractor, following GOPA-DERD’s Procurement policy. -Beneficiaries are being selected according to approved selection criteria taking into account targeting the most vulnerable HHs, taking into consideration the targeted houses to be structurally safe, and submitting the final list to MoLA for approvals by then. - BoQs of the necessary needs will be prepared according to the criteria for reusing the private apartment. - BoQs are being explained directly with the beneficiaries to develop the project with a view of the works that will be implemented to give them the right to choose or replace some of the works within the specific budget of the apartment. - GOPA-DERD and its partner will coordinate with regards to the implementation monitoring and follow-up during all phases to achieve the implementation according to the technical conditions adopted that achieve donors’ trust. - Some necessary modifications may be made during the implementation phase, as rehabilitation works require sometimes the necessities of cancellation, addition or modification. After all planned works are being completed, a special committee is formed to take over the executed works from the contractors and to ascertain the quality in the specified time. The work will then be handed over to the houseowner and make sure that the planned BOQ for each house has been fully implemented, as the owner will be asked to sign the BOQ before and after. A warranty period is given foreach project, the contractor will be responsible for any maintenance could be needed for any imbalance in execution may arise during the use of beneficiaries. Procedures: Theprocedures for all kind of rehabilitation buildings projects as per GOPA-DERD Engineering Unit guidelines are as follows: o Selection of beneficiaries according to approved criteria :o Submitting for MoLA approval o Preparing BoQ for each apartment and general BoQ Preparing (Tender Book) o Tendering process o Implementation (commitment letter- time schedule - implementation) o Technical follow-up for execution of works o Reception of the works and handing over it to their owners o Closing the project and final report
