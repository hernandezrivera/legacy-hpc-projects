Project Summary

The project will provide life-saving and life-sustaining assistance to ensure access to food security and livelihoods for the IDPs and host communities in Cabo Delgado province (districts of Chiure, Montepuez and Mueda) and Nampula (district of Meconta) particularly vulnerable women and girls. Interventions will contribute to the restoration of the normalcy of the life of the IDPs providing them livelihoods and life skills opportunities, addressing the food insecurity, women economic empowerment and wellbeing.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Conflict and fear of violence in Cabo Delgado continued to force people to flee. Nampula province is the province hosting the hghest number of IDPS outside Cabo Delgado. According to latest update of the National Institute for Disaster Management and Risk Reduction (INGD), there are 854,264 people displaced due to conflict in Cabo Delgado .Of these, 48.2 percent are children. IOM estimates that around 642,000 IDPs in Cabo Delgado are living with host families or resettlement areas with little to no access to basic livelihood activities. According to the Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWSNET) , conditions of Integrated Phase Classification (IPC) 3, or Crisis phase, continue to persist in conflict-affected areas of Cabo Delgado.Increasing food insecurity among the IDP’ families is exacerbated by COVID-19 pandemic. The situation is putting lot of strains on the families which are likely to exhaust the little savings they had and are turning to negative coping mechanisms, including child marriage and transactional sex. There is a particularly need for attention to women economic resilience and addressing the food insecurity, supporting with livelihoods restoration and income generating activities, especially for vulnerable women (young mothers, single/widow women), that are founding themselves in high vulnerability situation.The project will provide life-saving and life-sustaining assistance to IDPs, particularly to vulnerable women, to ensure access to livelihoods, by promoting food production and livelihood diversification and life skills development.The intervention is expected to benefit a total of 55,000 people being 17,600 women, 12,650 men, 12,870 Girls and 11,880 boys in the districts of Meconta, Chiure, Montepuez and Mueda.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Faciltate the set up of the beneficiary registration committees with members of good reputation elected in public gatherings and with equal representation of men and women;• Support the beneficiary registration community committees in collaboration to identify and select vulnerable families (particularly families lead by young mothers/single/widow women) for provision of agriculture inputs to improve their livelhoods; • Support the restoration of production capacity of affected households through provision of agricultural inputs and drought tolerant seeds (through vouchers or in-kind distributions) to promote climate change adaptable agriculture practices• Support context relevant Income Generating Activities (IGAs) and entrepreneurial pathways for youth with a focus on women and adolescent girl empowerment through business including bee keeping• Job Market opportunities survey to identify skills in high demand in Cabo Delgado and Nampula in effort to prepare youth to cease local job opportunities• Support life skill interventions to renew youth’s taste for learning particularly for out of school youth and adolescents• Life skills training to youth

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims to strengthen protective environment and promote access to supported services for children and adolescents (girls and boys) affected by the crisis, prevent SGBV and provide appropriate gender and age support to child survivors of SGBV, neglect and exploitation. Girls and boys become vulnerable particularly when in humanitarian crisis like the risk of GBV, SEA, violence and child labour is exacerbated. To prevent these, the project will include a series of child protection activities, ensuring that crisis affected girls and boys will access psycho-social support and referral responses, including gender-based violence and comprehensive CP case management in order to prevent and respond to protection risks in the affected population Furthermore, the project will conduct a child-centred baseline risk mapping and ensure child protection concerns are seeing from children’s perspectives and ensure meaningful child participation according with the CP Principles and CPMS. The mapping will also take into consideration existing CP structures and community resources that support and addres children’s issues. Results will feel into the project to improve implementation and performance.Capacity development of children’s groups and adolescents will help enhace their meaningful participation in their own protection and ensure they lead on protection activities in their respective communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

PLAN, informed by the IASC Commitments on Accountability to Affected People and Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, will ensure array of measures to ensure accountability. PLAN is committed to involvement of beneficiaries in the design and the implementation of the response, starting with the rapid assessments at the onset of the response, PLAN will initiate an AAP assessment to ensure accountability and feedback mechanisms are in place and relevant for the response. A key component of the accountability strategy will be timely, accessible and accurate information-sharing.This will be done face-to-face, through flyers and banners, at all project sites. Information will be shared about topics such as the planning of activities and distributions, the content of the support, but also the commitments of the organisation, staff behaviour and the rights of people affected. The selection and verification of beneficiaries will be conducted in consultation with men and women in target locationsPLAN will ensure the establishment of community level feedback mechanisms that are easily assessible, appropriate, child and community-friendly, through consultations and participation of cross-section of community members including children. All feedbacks will be analyzed and results felt back to the community to help to improve project implementation and performance.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

PLAN is committed to disability inclusion in all its programming. This project is also designed to include all children including child with disabilities. PLAN makes explicit the need to include children with disabilities and promotes children with disabilities to be afforded equal access to protection activities; upholding the principles of non-discrimination, participation and inclusion rights.PLAN will work to include children with disabilities in baseline mapping, project activities design, implementation and monitoring and will seek to identifiy and address the barriers children with disabilities facing to participate in project activities in order to increase access to activities and services. Project staff will be provided orientation and training on disability inclusion so that they consciously adopt inclusive principles at outset of project implementation, so that their personal attitudes and practices are more aligned with humanitarian principles, inclusion principles and child protection standards. Consultation with children with disabilities will be critical and the project team will adopt activities that tend to empower and increase children with disabilities inclusion in project activities, ie attention to access issues-CFS locations, training venues, sites for PSS support activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Food secure communities, especially those reliant on the environment for their livelihoods, require healthy and productive ecosystems. As such we will ensure that the interventions will take into consideration the environment and will be sensitive to climatic conditions as well to ensure current and future food security and access to water and energy. The livelihoods proposed interventions will be delivered in a way that preserve and restore the natural environment from further degradation and do not contribute to deforestation or soil erosion. For agriculture/small-scale farming opprotunities, attention will be given on the water consumption for irrigation, water pollution with pesticides, loss of biodiversity (crop selection), soil degradation, risk of fire, use of innapporiate farming machinery. And sustainable farming/agricultural production solutions will be promoted whenever possible. In coordination with relevant stakeholdrs we will ensure that the access to natural resoources and sue of the land for famring is governed. Similarly, we will not promote small-scale livelihoods intervention that could have environmental impact. Community rehabilitation schemes that are environmental friendly will be promoted.
