Project Summary

PRONANAC is a National NGO based in Pemba and committed to delivering comprehensive Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) services to communities in the rural area since 2009. With this project, PRONANAC aims to deliver WASH minimum package to those for internally displaced persons (IDPs) and returnees in Nangade District. Utilizing local know-how and multisectorial expertise, PRONANAC adopts an approach that encompasses ensuring safe water access, sustainable sanitation, and promoting hygiene. This involves various activities such as rehabilitating or improving water points in affected communities and support the construction of family latrines with the distribution of latrine slabs. The initiative also includes hygiene promotion efforts in affected communities, the distribution of hygiene kits, and the implementation of the Community-Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) process. The overall target for these initiatives is to reach 3500 individuals

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

PRONANAC is committed to providing secure and sustainable WASH solutions in Mozambique's Northern Province, focusing on Nangade. The initiative, aligned with cluster and response plans, includes manual water points, hygiene promotion and sanitation including local production of concrete slabs. Operations cover multiple locations, reaching IDPs, Hosting communities and returnees with solutions tailored to their specifc needs. emphasizing partnerships with local artisans and people for outreach and capacity building. The effort spans rehabilitation and construction of water points, emergency and family latrine construction, sanitation distribution, CLTS promotion, and hygiene training, targeting crisis-affected communities, returnees, and internally displaced people in Nangade.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Rehabilitation or improvement of water points in affected communities;• Support the construction of family latrines with the distribution of latrine slabs;• Distribution of hygiene kits;• Implementation of the Community-Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) process;• Training young volunteers in slab production and latrine construction.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

PRONANAC in coordination with Protection cluster, ensures the integration of protection aspects into all activities, emphasizing staff training and community worker education. The organization prioritizes safety and security to prevent GBV, delivering culturally sensitive WASH services for vulnerable populations. Accessibility concerns for individuals with disabilities, chronic diseases, the elderly, and marginalized individuals are addressed. PRONANAC's design strategy emphasizes protection mainstreaming, safety, and dignity, conducting a multi-sector risk analysis, promoting equitable access, participation, and transparency. PRONANAC commits to train employees on protection, standards, and humanitarian principles to prevent harm and uphold safety and ethical standards in the implementation of the activities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

PRONANAC ensures meaningful participation by engaging women and elderly persons since early stages, through focus group discussion. They will be involved in the design and coordination of the WASH program in the community, and actions like committing to a minimum of 50% representation in the water and sanitation committees and hygiene promotion teams, will empower women and the elderly within the community.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Throughout the project cycle, the organization ensures active participation and solicitation of ideas and opinions from people with disabilities. The team actively involve individuals with disabilities in designing WASH infrastructure, incorporating suggestions for improved access, such as handrails, seats, or ramps. PRONANAC disseminates disability-related information to the community, enhancing understanding of needs, and shares progress with the WASH cluster for ongoing contributions on WASH inclusion. To remove barriers, a community survey will determine the number of people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

PRONANAC ensures environmentally friendly solutions with locally sourced materials for WASH activities, promoting a reduce, recycle, and reuse system. Rapid environmental screening and impact assessments will be conducted in various sites, aligning with country regulations and maintaining coordination with relevant authorities.
