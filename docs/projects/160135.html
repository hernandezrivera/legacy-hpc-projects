Project Summary

Food security is an important element in the stability and health of the people in Garbaharey, Gedo Region. Erratic weather conditions especially the recent prolonged drought that affected the planting season has irreparably affected food security. As such the project seeks to increase food security for the at risk communities in Garbahare host and IDPs by integrated food-related multipronged interventions including screening, provision of food referrals, and awareness. The project aim is to reduce malnutrition, ameliorate severe effects, or fatalities associated with lack of adequate and nutritious food to the at-risk population especially Pregnant and Lactating Women and their children between 6 - 59 months.
A very important element of the project is building resilience through provision of relevant skills to increases self-reliance, enhance self-sustenance and provide cutionary livelihood activities against shocks. The targeted are the IDPs and those whose assets and sources of livelihood were destroyed affected by prolonged drought and demonstrate susceptibility to food deficiency diseases. Identification is done through standard screening processes and capacity building to PLW to undertake MUAC screening methodologies. Periodic screening, coordination with the local actors including the local administration.

Project Activities

The project will consist of the following activities;1. Undertake communication, mobilization and community awareness of the project.2. Do basic screening to determine the level of food insecurity needs among the targeted at-risk population.3. Provide food vouchers to 2300 HHs with over 50% going to women-headed households. 4. Asses and increase capacity to food production through provision of farm input to 4200 households. 5. Training and capacity building on food-safe storage for 150 households 6. Engage 1000 households through rehabilitation of assets and provision of food. 7. Creation of short-term job opportunities through food for work activities targeting 1,500 people.. 8. Enhance resilience by provision of marketable vocational skills to 50 youths

Mitigation Measures

GEWDO programming approach is underpinned by clear and elaborate communication about the project activities, expected beneficiaries, methods of beneficiaries selection and how the assistance is delivered to the beneficiaries. This is one of the strategies to avoid hostilities, and protection risks, associated with the feeling that a group of people, or clans, are excessively benefiting from the project at the expense of the other, a very potent and root cause of exposure of other communities to harm and a potential protection risks. This is a pertinent element, yet salient protection risks. As such the issues related to protection risks associated with hostilities are dealt with at the communications phase. To deal with issues of protection risks associated with exposure GEWDO's beneficiaries are always rerouted within the nearest FDPs. This reduces the time of travel and exposure significantly. Furthermore, the organization undertakes all food distributions, and project-related activities, in the morning hours to enable beneficiaries who may have travelled from far-reaching home in time to avoid protection risks and exposures. The project is implemented with full collaboration with the local community stakeholders and notables from across the breadth and width of the community. This cushions the project, and beneficiaries from any external protections harm from disgruntled parties. This approach has also perpetuated a sense of ownership.
