Project Summary

The project will target 63153 most vulnerble people in 6 district in Cabo Delegado ( Nangade, Quissanga, Mocimboa da Praia, Macomia, Muidumbe and Meluco) and 5 districts in Niassa province Marrupa, Maua, Nipepe,Metarica and Cuamba, among IDPs, host communities and returnees.
The vulnerable groups include pregnant and women breastfeeding, widows, Older People, child-headed families, adolescent girls-headed families, young women-headed families and people with disabilities. Beneficiaries will be confirmed and validated by community members during beneficiary list verification and validation sessions.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will target 63153 most vulnerble people in 6 district in Cabo Delegado ( Nangade, Quissanga, Mocimboa da Praia, Macomia, Muidumbe and Meluco) and 5 districts in Niassa province Marrupa, Maua, Nipepe,Metarica and Cuamba, among IDPs, host communities and returnees.The vulnerable groups include pregnant and women breastfeeding, widows, Older People, child-headed families, adolescent girls-headed families, young women-headed families and people with disabilities. Beneficiaries will be confirmed and validated by community members during beneficiary list verification and validation sessions.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Community mobilization/sensitization, identification, selection, beneficiaries registration and verification (dissemination of selection criteria, etc.) and validation of target beneficiaries Conduct market assessment in all targeted districts Establish agreements with food and agricultural inputs suppliers in all targeted districts Conduct distribution of food to 63153 people.Conduct distribution of seed kits and agricultural tools as well as training measures for beneficiaries and advisory services focusing on good agriculture practices to prevent negative coping mechanism.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The risk for all the project activities will be assessed and mitigation methods will be proposed and implemented. Oxfam staff have already been trained on safeguarding. Risk matrix have also been developed for the targeted district humanitarian work. A refresher training will be conducted on safeguarding during project inception. Staff will also sign code of conduct before commencing implementation; Consent will be sort with project beneficiary before data collection and sharing. Data will be handled with confidently and will not be shared with a third party unless authorized by the benefiary or other party such as funder accordingly.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

A gender and protection analysis will be carried out to support the design of response strategies that effectively address the needs of girls, boys, women and men affected by the crisis; ensure that our programming integrates, mitigates and responds to gender-based violence.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Oxfam will engage civil society organization of people with disabilities, during the development of beneficiaries selection criteria process, to ensure full their participation on identification key criteria of inclusion and good practice to remove barriers to access the assistance. At field level people with disabilities will engaged and integrated um selection committees and project communities working groups

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Oxfam will conduct environment risk assessment in all district targeted by project and identify mitigation measure to minimize negative environmental of project implementation activities
