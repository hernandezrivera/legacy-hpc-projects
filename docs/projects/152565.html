Project Summary

Capacity building will be provided for boys and girls to have genuine opportunities of getting involved in community gender based violence awareness creation - through effective participation in decision making processes, especially in the area of protection scaling up Gender based violence prevention and response in Juba – Gudele, Lemungaba , Munuki , Rejaf, Kator and Gondokoro payams on the other hand, in Old Fangak ,New Fangak , Pholm –zaraf Juaibor, Manajang Mereang , Old Fangak town, Nyadin –, Paguir , Bulita , Wangachat locations of Fangak County in Jonglei State. WAO shall trained 120 youth in peace building and conflict prevention and response to learn about conflict management, use their initiative, evaluate options, learn to make informed decisions and choices, develop and enhance leadership skills in Juba and in Old Fangak and New Fangak locations. In this project implementation process, 250 boys and girls ,women and men will have chances to develop innovative livelihood programs for youth-appropriate to provide protection solutions to address personal, group and community challenges in Juba , Old Fangak and New Fangak project locations . Handicraft , bakery Tailoring , business and marketing options will be offered in this category to prevent the occurrence of gender based violence instead boys and men being agents of change for gender equality. Conflict affected women and girls will have strengthened resilience to prevent sexual and gender based violence occurrence within their community and their grassroots structures in these project locations. One of its objectives will be to involve women in political governance at all levels, the construction of lasting peace and support initiatives aim to promote woman rights in the crosscutting dimension. WAO will build socio safety nets through organizing women groups at community level to assess the progress of implementation lasting peace and conflict analysis WAO will build capacity of 100 Influential people men, women boys and girls as – community based leaders to have strong community protection and support in women’s and girls’ lives to improve their attitudes, knowledge and skills to protect girls boys and women and men from protection risks and supported them to be safe from violence. WAO will Harmonize, operationalize and cover early preventive warning mechanism at local levels in the project locations. WAO will increase the protection service to men, women boys and girls preventing them from protection risks and threats by training 250 men ,women , boys and girls in community protection principles , human rights and provision of psychosocial support services in Juba , Old Fangak and New Fangak locations to enhance the counseling services to vulnerable and traumatized conflict affected men, women, boys and girls . WAO shall strengthen community resilience to prevention violence and protect Conflict against the affected women and vulnerable population in Juba and Fangak counties within their communities from the grassroots structures where School Pupils school pupils and students shall be reached with GBV prevention messages. 100 pupils and teachers shall be targeted and trained on protection principles and human rights in Juba in Central Equatoria , Old Fangak and New Fangak locations in Jonglei states. WAO will work as an integrated whole , by combining efforts with the local government authorities on ground, in Juba ,Old Fangak and New Fangak locations as SGBV preventing actors, civil societies , religious leaders , politicians and other individuals from within and without those areas where the project shall be implemented using the integrated community approach methodology .

Needs

For almost half a century, South Sudan has experienced protracted conflict, poverty, lack of infrastructure and disruption of social services has been so rampant. Due to conflict, More than half of the population of Women and girls have experienced Gender-based violence (GBV) being a human rights violation, a public health challenge, and a barrier to civic, social, political, and economic participation. Gender based violence in South Sudan has continued undermining not only the safety, dignity, overall health status, and human rights of the thousands of Women and girls who experience it, but also the , economic stability, and security of the nation . Conflict and insecurity have undermined the Governance and institutions of South Sudan and weakened the state society relations as well as the national identity of South Sudanese people. Women are not considered for peace negotiations and dialogues due to neglect by gender –male population and through this attitude peace in South Sudan is derailed. The Conflict caused lack of mobility for women and girls leading them to forced, and early marriage resulting into shame with sexual violation. There was high level of school dropout among school girls who became pregnant signalling end of education for them. Others felt stigma of being un married mothers which pushed many girls to illegal abortion, infanticide and child abandonment. The conflict caused huge social inequitable gender norms for the constantly moving population which later concentrated in IDP camps in the Country who felt immediate risks of physical harm and sexually transmitted infections including HIV preventing GBV in the community. This can be achieved through engaging men, women, girls and boys in reducing gender based violence while mitigating its harmful effects imposed on individuals and the communities to improve Governance , peace, security , protection of human rights and fostering stronger and resilient state-society relations . . Most of the affected IDPs were children and women. The entire host population of the six locations of Fangak County ie Paguir, Pulita, Juaibor, Nyadin , New Fangak and Wangichat where the IDPs are displaced is approximately 20 000 people. The IDPs are currently living with host families in rural areas. Host community is willing to assist the IDPs but they don’t have enough capacity, as the result tensions between them started to emerge due to limited food, water, shelter including; insecurity and protection risks. In Juba County Central Equatoria state the IDPs and host communities faces long standing underdevelopment and chronic poverty, leading to some of the lowest development indicators within South Sudan. Since July 2016 conflict flared in Juba town where, Kataor, Rock city and Munuki localities were politically volatile and cut off from normal trade routes to Juba town. With access improving, these areas became particularly vulnerable as returnees come back to areas with little or no services. Seasonal migration of cattle keepers risk exerted further pressure on an already precarious situation. The assessment found that only 1.6 percent of the population experienced in Juba County high rates of GBV cases such rape physical assault Since September 2017, the Overall, education level and school attendance in Juba and Fangak County remained very low due to the ongoing conflict and insecurity situation. The number of people affected by conflict disaggregated by gender in Old Fangak County is 17,000 displaced (10,200 females and 6,800 male). This means 60% female and 40% male. The most affected population are women and children. Generally, 74% of the affected population is children and Youth while the 26% represents adults with high populations being female whose health standard are very poor and hygiene condition was 0% at the time of the IRNA assessment

The recruitment of children into the armed forces and child labor engagement are some of the protection risks which this project will deal with . Since the eruption of the conflict and insurgence begun in south Sudan, about 20,000 children were recruited in the armed forces by both Government and opposition sides where by 3200 children were forcibly abducted in the army. • Insecurity and insurgence of conflict are the primary risks of protection in the society where by massive population displacement takes place. • School dropout children vary in magnitude and severity of suffering in the current political conditions in the country. Children and women during the conflict faced many protection risks • Lack of safety audits for women and men , girls and boys in the community latrines and other essential locations may pose protection threats to girls and women for example POC camps lack safety audits on the sanitation facilities. This caused many protection challenges which results into Gender based violence incidences. •In the pastoral communities cattle raiding is the main protection risk . Cattle rearing is central as community livelihood source but also to social and cultural systems. • During the assessment, the communities interviewed reported that they have lost their cattle, food, cooking utensils, clothes and shelter meaning a cattle raiding is a protection threat to them.
