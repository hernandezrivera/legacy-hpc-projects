Project Summary

Recent Torrential rains and floods havoc the southern districts of Sindh. Districts Mirpur Khas and Umarkot faced destruction and devastation. Heavy rains damages crops and livestock. Houses in low line areas came under water and inhabitants became IDPs. The Infrastructure of affected districts was badly damaged. Water supply and Sanitation systems totally destroyed while Drainage (Sim) Nalas LBOD over floated and water came into fields where crops ready to harvest. During the early days of emergency, a number of families were witnessed who suffered a lot and remained away from the basic facilities, however with the emergency response by Government of Sindh, Pak Army and local Humanitarian agencies now things are comparatively better but still a lot of humanitarian works is required throughout the district in general and particularly in the proposed union councils. The inhabitant of the area were compelled to defecate in the open. Especially women and children suffered more. Women and young girls go for defecation at dawn or wait to dark. This creates many complications in their health and lead to intestinal diseases among women and young girls. Due to Stagnant water and open defecation cholera, diarrhea and skin diseases are spreading. Although the emergency phase is almost over but affectees still in camps or could not live in their existing houses due to stagnant water. Other sanitation related problems like stagnant water and solid waste also contribute to water and sanitation related diseases and affect children under five. The overall living conditions of the flood survivors is not satisfactory, the government don’t have enough resources to address all their needs thus, they are looking towards the humanitarian community for help and support in the rehabilitation of at least WASH related issues, the nature of their needs very much basic and it do fall under BASIC NEEDs, because it also affect Human Dignity , Privacy, Pride and Respect. There is need of immediate steps to be resolved will in time, in case of failure, it may lead to another misfortune (water born and water related diseases. The poor sanitation and unhygienic environment will lead to outbreak of communicable diseases and the most marginalized population i.e. women and children are feared to be suffered more.
