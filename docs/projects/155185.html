Objectives

"WFP, as the lead agency for Logistics Sector, aims to oversee all the strategic objectives from the sector, namely: 1. Improve logistics operations based on joint operational efforts and shared use of key logistics resources. Related to SO 3.2. Collect, produce and share timely and accurate data and information in support of humanitarian logistics operations. Related to SO 3.3. Implement common logistics services to address identified gaps and bottleneck in the supply chain. Related to SO 3.4. Enhance the humanitarian response through supplementary logistics support and preparedness activities. Related to SO 3"

Needs

• A lack of available storage capacity;• Congestion along road infrastructure leading to refugee settlements and makeshift sites;• Poor and insufficient vehicle access within refugee settlements and makeshift sites; • Minimal availability of logistics facilities, assets and infrastructure capable of remaining fully functional throughout the up-coming storms and rainy season.

Activities or outputs

a. Lead the dedicated coordination cell from the sector. Among other actvitivies, WFP will organize coordination meetings and synchronize partners efforts as needed;b. Develop capacity building initiatives in relevant logistics gaps such as training (e.g. basic humanitarian logistics courses) and simulations (e.g. cyclone preparedness drill); c. Establish and maintain Information Management (IM) support for the provision of accurate information products such Access Constraint maps;d. Expand Logistics Capacity Assessments and Risk Assessment initiatives to contribute with a better undertanding of supply chain vulnerabilities in Cox's Bazare. Manage common services for the handling of humanitarian cargo in Cox’s Bazar District such as the common storage locations located in areas with low density of storage capacity. Moreover, WFP will continue to assess logistics gaps and address them whenever they are result of a general contraint to the humanitarian community;f. Enhance stand-by logistics capacity as part of natural disaster preparedness and supply chain continuity, including assessing risk vulnerability in key logistics infrastructure; g. Provide support to organizations - on bilateral or multilateral level - through advisory in technical aspects of the operation and consolidate advocacy efforts for logistic gaps. Advocacy efforts will prioritize, but not be limited to, domestic cargo checks, physical road access and traffic management within the area of operations.
