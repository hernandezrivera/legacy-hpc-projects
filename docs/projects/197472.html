Project Summary

In response to the most pressing needs in the most underserved areas in Syria, several cities and towns will be targeted in Idlib and Aleppo governorates with WASH assistance in light maintenance and rehabilitation of water supplies, sewage and solid waste collection and disposal.
The targeted governorates inherently suffer from severe weakness in the delivery of all basic services related to water, sanitation, and solid waste management. With the on-going crisis, depleted assets hindered local authorities to respond to the immediate and humanitarian high needs in this area.
The proposed project’s WASH interventions seek to improve the humanitarian situation for the war-affected population by restoring water and sanitation systems that will be able to respond to the increasing demand in the overburdened communities because of the continuous arrival of tens of thousands of forcefully displaced people because of warfare. The action will be combined with emergency response WASH activities to the affected population in Idlib and Aleppo.
Directly 71877 people will benefit. The coordination with local councils, camp management, and other actors is the first step for all outputs. Through the proposed project AHF will produce the following outputs:
- Water trucking and solid waste collections for IDPs in17 informal settlements in Idlib and Aleppo governorates. 105 m3 of safe drinking and chlorinated water will be delivered to 3000 BNFs for 6 months in the average of 25l/day/BNF will provide to most vulnerable IDPs by trucks, including daily water quality control in the water source, trucks, and household level.
• Undertake the rehabilitation for two water systems with operation and maintenance for three months with gradual activation for cost recovery system: the rehabilitation includes provision and installation of submersible and horizontal pump set pump sets, generator sets, chlorine dosing pumps, with all valves and other accessories. Also, the rehabilitation includes the Construction of the water station building that included (generator room, pumping room, worker room, and groundwater tank and repairing the water network.
- Daily removing 10 trips of 4m3 of solid waste for 6 months to serve 3000 BNFs.
- Hygiene kits to most vulnerable IDPs. 1500 hygiene kits will be distributed to most vulnerable IDPs in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. the selection criteria include women or child-headed households, disables, more than 5 individuals per family, who did not receive hygiene kit recently. verification will be done, and people who meet the criteria will be listed in the final distribution list. Within the distribution, two separate queues for men and women in distribution points and there will be chairs for elderly people. PDM report will be conducted after distribution to measure the level of satisfaction

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Low level of obligations related to sexual exploitation and abuse, women disproportionately exposure to misconduct by humanitarian workers at risk of sexual exploitation and abuse, and low level of safe reporting of complaints of SEA. Mitigation: 1 - Impose mandatory training on protection from PSEAon all employees, including management, field staff, day laborers, volunteers, contractors and vendors, in each area where there are active processes to ensure that employees are aware of their obligations related to protection from sexual exploitation and abuse and to equip employees with the knowledge and skills to prevent Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and Response to both. 2 - Create mechanisms for reporting protection from SEA at the community level, including establishing contact points, according to the location and settings for reporting and posting. 3 - Engage with communities and key stakeholders in strengthening community complaints mechanisms, including determining the provision of victim assistance services in all targeted locations. Conduct regular awareness-raising activities on protection from sexual exploitation and abuse, policies, standards, and codes of conduct related to protection from sexual exploitation. 4 - Provide specific awareness sessions to target groups such as women, girls, boys, and men in reception centers, camps, sites for displaced people, host communities, and society All project personnel sign the Code of Conduct for PSEA
