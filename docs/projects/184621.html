Project Summary

Approximately 15.7 million people are acutely food insecure in Somalia. According to FSNAU post-Gu 2021 assessment report indicated that 3.5 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse outcomes through December 2021 in the absence of humanitarian assistance across Somalia this was due to the combined effects of widespread and severe drought Desert Locust infestation, socioeconomic impacts caused by COVID-19 and the cumulative impacts of previous shocks, In addition, 986,400 children under the age of five are likely to be acutely malnourished through December
2022. The 2021 Gu season exhibited erratic performance leading to livestock loss by the pastoral livelihood zones.
Desert Locust continues to pose a serious risk of damage to both pasture and crops at least until the end of 2021. The 2021 Deyr (October-December) rainfall season is likely to be below average to average across the country, which could lead to drought and trigger a worsening of the humanitarian situation if the 2021 Gu (April-June) season rainfall is also delayed or performs poorly. However, Mudug and Galgadud are not exceptional to the infestation of locusts as well as the long dry spell which resulted in acute food insecurity in the region. Market functions across the country have been severely disrupted and the prices of staple foods have become exorbitantly high due to the limited availability of foreign currency and continuing depreciation of the Somalia shillings, making the import of staple food commodities increasingly challenging. As a result, poor households with limited or no sources of income are unable to access food through the markets and have adopted negative coping strategies Source HNO 2022
DEH intends to provide immediate access to conditional cash assistance and unrestricted to 330 most vulnerable women and men in the host communities. The 330 most vulnerable (150 men and 150 women) will receive 50 USD equivalent in Somali shillings for four months. The project will enhance farmers' capacity, procure and distribute farm tools and seeds to farmers in Dhusamareb, and Hobyo district in Galmudug state. The farmers will be expected sensitization training and commit establish their own farms. Afterwhich they receive the cash to enable them to restore their livelihoods as well as catalyze market functionality in the region. The conditional cash will provide immediate access to household income in a dignified way and allow freedom to purchase the most pressing household needs.

1. - Projects activities

DEH will conduct a rapid baseline Market assessment in the main local market and gauge current market functionality based on the available goods and services. Thereafter the community will be informed through local gatherings by the local authority about the project and beneficiary selection criteria. Through community leadership with guidance from DEH, the most vulnerable beneficiary will be inclusively selected members. The 330 selected beneficiaries will be bestowed the responsibility to identify leaders that will be involved in the cash working group. Participating in the cash working group will support identifying viable and mobile money transfers. The service provider will affect the payment according to DEH and the community agreement. Thereafter, through community participation, farmers will be supported to identify feasible crops that will survive short rains. training will be conducted and farm tools and seeds will be distributed. At the end of every month, the 330 beneficiaries will receive 50 USD for consecutive four months from mobile the supplier. The project will be monitored and evaluated

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Conditional and unrestricted cash or voucher-based intervention provides freedom to the beneficiary to purchase commodities they need most. It empowers the community and provides them with the opportunity to build resilience and reduce dependability to relief. the intervention activates market functionality and contributes to communal social-economic growth. Market engagements also support social cohesions among the conflict-affected population and foster peaceful coexistence. The cash transfer will enhance self-worthiness and dignity thus enabling them to recover from the shocks and start sustainable income-generating activities. The cash transfer interventions are cost-effective and yield desired results as opposed to some in-kind projects.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

The intervention factored in the importance of market functionality to enhance sustainability and resilience building. The cash transfer enhances interactions in the market thus supporting providing a lasting solution through the exchange of goods and services. market functionality supports economic growth and raises GDP thus contributing to the stabilization of Somali shillings as well as improving its devaluation. Through market assessments, essential goods and services are able to be identified to help support strike a balance between demand and supply to support livelihoods. DEH will conduct routine assessments and establish trends of the most essential commodity desired to support the needs of the most vulnerable. In collaboration with cash working groups, DEH will utilize guidance provided by the external market information and track functionality to support the economic recovery.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

DEH purposes to provide sustainable intervention and build resilience among the most vulnerable community in Galmudug state. the project will be inclusive and equal to all that meet admission requirements. to nurture ownership, DEH will involve community stakeholders and beneficiaries in the design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation phases of the project. Adopting the AAP model, DEH will establish a feedback mechanism platform where farmers will be able to confidentially provide suggestions and complaints to enable better outcomes. Minority and the most vulnerable will be at the center of the intervention despite their ability. Deh is upholding gender, safeguarding, and child protection, PSEA principles, and will ensure that the project does no harm to the vulnerable community. Routine market assessments will be done and market functionality assess to establish the effect of the project. To mitigate the further risk that may occur during the cash collection, DEH will engage mobile money transfer suppliers and deposit the cash in the beneficiaries' phones at the comfort of their homes irrespective of their gender.
