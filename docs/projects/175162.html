Project Summary

Strengthening the humanitarian community’s fight against SEA requires a shift in the way we collectively address sexual exploitation and abuse. Critically it requires ongoing efforts to have adequate systems with sufficient resources allocated to prevent and respond to cases. This project engages the Pakistan PSEA (P-PSEA) network to strengthen accountability in the enforcement of PSEA in areas where the HRP 2021 is implemented. Established in 2016, the P-PSEA network member organizations have worked in the areas of prevention, mitigation and response to SEA. However, results remain lukewarm. Particularly concerning remains the fact that zero SEA reporting happens and agency level work is little known of. This project aims to strengthen coordination among member agencies, building capacity on PSEA in communities and organizations and coordination with existing sexual and gender-based violence programs as a whole to ensure accessibility, confidentiality and quality provision of services to survivors. This project has four strategic directions: (1) Understanding PSEA in the context of Pakistan; (2) Coordination for better results; (3) Capacity building/strengthening; (4) Effective use of community-based complaint and referral mechanism. While the work of P-PSEA is national in nature, for the purposes of the HRP it will prioritize areas covered in the HRP.

1- Project Activities

(1) Understanding PSEA in the context of Pakistan - this group of activities focuses on review of common forms of SEA; Indicators of SEA; Current response mechanisms in place and the extent to which such mechanisms are addressing SEA. Specific attention is placed on the Community Based Complaint Referral Mechanism (CBCRM) endorsed by HCT and used by the Pakistan PSEA Network organizations. (2) Coordination for better results - this group of activities strengthens the engagement of the Pakistan PSEA (P-PSEA) Network, the creation of a task force within the P-PSEA Network to coordinate action during the tenure of the HRP, setting up a PSEA Focal Point in all participating agencies in HRP and network of these focal points for better coordination and exchange of ideas/information and hiring a PSEA Coordinator based on global guidance for this coordination post (please note that the position will be technically supported by PSEA surge capacity in the OCHA Regional Office).(3) Capacity building/strengthening - this set of activities anticipates training of PSEA Focal Points and relevant staff; Cascading content to individual agencies; Preparation and dissemination of PSEA related information in areas where HRP focuses. (4) Effective use of community-based complaint and referral mechanism - this group of activities anticipates a massive awareness campaign on PSEA reporting in the different communities where HRP is being implemented; monitoring how well the CBCRM and its internal system is working; and develop a fully independent investigation mechanism resourced with in-country and global capacity.
