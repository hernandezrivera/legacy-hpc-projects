Project Summary

There is an ongoing outbreak of COVID-19 in Pakistan. The first case was notified on the 26th February 2020 from Karachi. As of 23rd Dec 2020, the total number of cases notified since the beginning of the outbreak is 462,814 with 9,557 deaths (CFR: 2.06%). Presently Pakistan is passing through 2d wave of the outbreak, which is still increasing as shown by increase in the weekly incidence and the weekly attack rate. (WHO daily situation report attached)
The government of Pakistan with support from partners have responded to COVID-19 by establishing coordination structures for the response at all levels, strengthening case detection, contact tracing, isolation and quarantine; strengthening case management, crisis communication and community engagement. Major gaps regarding nutrition : specifically in SC context
- Case management facilities including nutrition stabilization centres lack trained staff, required equipment and supplies and IPC practices.
- The isolation and quarantine facilities in some of the districts are unsuitable for quarantine.
- Nutrition Stabilization Centres in COVID-19 hotspot districts of Pakistan are not well equipped and are inadequate to accommodate and manage malnourished children whose numbers will be exacerbated by COVID-19 control measures.
- Dearth of real time data on malnutrition which could be obtained through Health and Nutrition Sentinel Surveillance (HANSS) and integrated nutrition surveillance (INS).
- Poor maternal infant and young child feeding practices.
- Inadequate capacity of health facilities in the management of malnutrition and COVID-19 comorbidity.
