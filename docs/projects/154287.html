Needs

Besides having the recurrent food insecurity in its Sahel belt regions, Chad has been hosting many refugees and returnees from CAR and Nigeria due to ongoing conflicts in the two neighboring countries. Returnees in the west of Chad are settled in Lake region after fleeing attack of Boko Haram in Nigeria. Those in the South mainly come from the Central African Republic where they left following the on-going war between Seleka and Anti Balaka militias. They are settled in four regions namely Logone Orientale, Logone Occidentale, Mandoul and Moyen Chari.They face many challenges since areas they have settled lack basic social services and the little resources available have to be shared with local population making a strain on the already overstretched situation.

Activities or outputs

Procurement and distribution of nutrition supplies, materials and equipment to health facilitiesEnsure regular nutrition coordination in 5 regional clustersTraining and refresher training of 130 health workers and 325 community health workers on CIMAMProvide Vitamin A supplementation and deworming to children aged 6 to 59 monthsSetup 60 support groups to Exclusive breastfeeding and IYCFConduct mass screening and active case finding of acute malnutrition among children under fivesProvide treatment of severe cases of acute malnutritionCreate 8 mobiles Clinics to provide care for sites situated far and without access to health and nutrition care servicesConduct field monitoring visits and joint supervision to health facilities and mobile clinicsProvide technical and financial support for quality implementation of CIMAM both at health facility and community level. Ensure equity in access, and service delivery to all the population of concern.
