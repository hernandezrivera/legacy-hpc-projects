Project Summary

Save the Children will work to prevent an increase in the maternal mortality rate and the under 5 mortality rate through improvement of community capacity on identification and referrals of pregnancy complications, childhood illness, and acute malnutrition. Especially in drought-affected areas, Save the Children will work to prevent the occurrence of waterborne diseases and cholera. Health workers and CHWs will be trained on early warnings system IDSR to be able to detect, report, and make referrals at the earliest stage. Household hygiene kits will also be provided to improve hygiene practices at household level as disease preventive measure.
Save the Children will work to complement the MoHCC to prevent the spread of COVID-19. We will support community engagement, building on the existing work within the health system to address COVID-19. These interventions will focus on the promotion of health information and behaviours through Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) in alignment with the recommendations of government and the WHO. Where relevant, this will be supported by hygiene activities including the delivery of hand-washing facilities, hygiene kits, hygiene promotion training and other activities that will enhance COVID-19 management in alignment with the recommendations of the government, WHO and other key actors.

8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

SC will engage with the communities to gather and share evidence to inform programme design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. SC will support monitoring by the community to contribute to better accountability and to engage them for improved ownership. Feedback meetings will also be held with children as well as adults in the targeted areas to strengthen accountability and engage the targeted communities.
