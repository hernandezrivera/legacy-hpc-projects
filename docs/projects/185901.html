Project Summary

The protracted armed conflict between the State security forces and the non-state armed groups (NSAGs), growing insecurities, famine and massive displacements in the North West Region of Cameroon especially in the Boyo Division have placed women and girls in extreme vulnerabilities and at increased risk of gender-based violence (GBV). Boyo Division is one of the hardest hit areas in the Region with many villages affected. High levels of displacements, food insecurity and sexual exploitation and gender-based violence are rife. This is causing fluctuation levels of vulnerabilities in the region. Women and young girls are used as weapons of war and reports of raped are very common. Many girls have dropped out of school and the boys are massively joining the ranks of the NSAGs. Many lack civil documentation or have lost them. The main goal of the project is the promotion of IDP women empowerment through community engagement. This will be done by promoting women participation in capacity building and skills trainings to be independent. A total of 100 women and young girls are targeted during the implementation of this project.
The people of concern here are IDP women, young girls and boys who face various protection risks. This particular project will address their needs in a completely new way. Community-based organizations and mixed groups including, IDPs and the host community will work to mobilize the community for common causes and solutions that arise in their living environment. These associations will work within the project areas to enable the effective inclusion of people under UNHCR in all programmes. The women and young girls will be economically empowered through vocational training as attempts will be made to provide them with basic survival skills and civil documentation.
All these are in line with SDG 5, which focuses on advancing gender equality and empowering women and girls, a joint effort will be made with the government, humanitarian and development stakeholders, including the civil society in all sectors, as well as host communities and persons of concern. In BIHAPH’s Five-year Strategic Plan, strategic Priority 3 clearly articulates this as well.
The project is envisaged to be highly participatory with all the stakeholders like community mobilizers, traditional authorities, opinion leaders, Religious authorities and council officials. BIHAPH proposes to work with all relevant stakeholders to promote women’s empowerment initiatives, to disseminate information and skills, to advocate against gender-based violence and discrimination against women. We will prioritize vocational training to target the POC as a means of empowerment.
In close corporation with UNHCR, BIHAPH will regularly monitor the project in order to verify the proper implementation and the activities. Reports of activities will be regularly provided in accordance with the Partner’s guidelines and our Organization. The total cost of the project will be 159,009USD and the duration will be 12 months.
The empowerment of survivors of SGBV on their legal rights, leadership, sexual and gender -based violence as well as provision of basic survival skills and training of focal persons is an access way for women to campaign against gender-based violence and report cases of gender-based violence having known their rights. The increased knowledge on legal rights and service providers also opens long term benefits to women for responding to gender-based violence, thus ensuring sustainability,
