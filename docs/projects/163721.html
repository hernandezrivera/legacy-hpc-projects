Project Summary

The Overall objective of the intervention is to contribute to promoting more inclusive and more accessible health care services in Zawiya municipality. Its specific objectives are 1- Upgrade services and facilities of Zawiya Teaching Hospital (ZTH). 2- Strengthen the capacities of health personnel (medical and administrative) to provide good quality and inclusive health services. 3- Promote the collaboration between local actors in order to foster better public services and promote social cohesion and migrant inclusion. Target population.1.500 participants to the awareness campaigns; 20 CBOs/CSOs members, 2.400 people in need receiving health assistance.

1 - Needs

The health care system in Libya has always presented several weaknesses such as high dependence on foreign health workers, marginalized health services in some areas, weak Primary Health Care network, especially in the main cities, lack of effectiveness, transparency and accountability. Since the 2011 revolution and subsequent 2014 civil war, public services have never really resumed to an acceptable quality standard due to continuous instability, decline of national revenues, absence of effective public administration. The ongoing conflict has worsened the situation with mounting health needs. The availability and readiness of various health care programs are seriously limited in many sectors, especially in maternal and new-born health due to lack of resources, qualified staff, equipment and supplies. The hospitals are overwhelmed with trauma patients, which often limit the access to other patients in need of clean surgical facilities (i.e. pregnant women). Women are particularly affected by the current political context as well as by the cultural one. Critical specialized services such as sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services are weak across the country on multiple issues, for example, only 4% of all public health facilities are able to offer a diagnosis of cervical cancer. Furthermore, women and girls are required to ask permission from their husbands or male relatives to visit health clinics and therefore experience constraints. The availability of preventative and curative services for children under five is also extremely limited. Over one-third of municipalities cannot provide child healthcare, and even where PHC facilities do offer services, the package is very limited. There are major gaps in the provision of Integrated Management of Childhood Illnesses (IMCI) and essential New-born Care. As far as the Zawiya Mantika is concerned, it shows the highest rates of reported challenges accessing health care. As for Zawiya Teaching Hospital, the problems of the gynecological and pediatrics departments are: 1. Maintenance works have not performed since a long time (the last works have concerned the laboratory in 2016 and the cardiology ward in 2019): the A/C, electric and water systems have not been upgraded and do not function properly, replacement of toilets, windows and coverings of floors and walls has not been implemented. This creates problems in terms of thermic isolation and sterilization. Moreover, the facility is not suitable to ensure access for disabled people; 2. Essential medicines are running low in the hospitals’ pharmacies as not regularly refurbished by the government pharmaceutical store; 3. Personnel has not been updated and there is lack of knowledge and equipment for specific conditions on most recent treatments and technology.

2 - Activities

A1.1 Procurement, supply and set up of medical equipment: based on the assessment done between May and June 2020, the necessary medical equipment will be delivered and installed and staff trained on how to use and maintain it; A1.2 Rehabilitation works: based on the assessment, the necessary rehabilitation works will be carried out. A2.1 Set up of a Multi-Stakeholders Group: the group will be composed by local authorities and local CBOs and will have the task to define the local health strategies; A2.2 Awareness initiatives: the will focus on the importance of inclusive health services.
