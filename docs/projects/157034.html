Needs

The project will aim at ensuring that the basic life saving needs of the most vulnerable populations are met whilst ensuring greater preparedness and emergency response capacityAccording to the recent reports coming from NES areas, especially areas that have been recently liberated from ISIS, the health system and services are completely deteriorated. People are facing multiple challenges to get resisted to the current situation. The most important needs were those related to the availability of health services and access to health facilities, especially among vulnerable groups (Women, children, chronically ill patients, and elderly persons). Reproductive-aged women are in need for a crucial support of maternity and reproductive health services (BEmONC, and CEmONC). Pediatrics services are not so common nowadays in NES areas, especially in the eastern part of DEZ governorate.The referral system is collapsed, and the only one existing is being administered and supported by the humanitarian actors in NES, which need more support and tools. Trauma management is very important to those fleeing ISIS controlled areas. Women, girls, and children are in need to get their access improved to the health facilities. According to the recent M&E reports coming from Bahar health facilities in NES areas, the access, medicine, and availability of RH services are among the most demanded services for reproductive-aged women nowadays at those areas.• Activities will take place in NES areas, specifically: Aleppo governorate: Ain Al Arab and Menbij districts.- In Ain Al Arab, a total of 23 communities will be targeted by a primary health care center in Ain Al Arab city center, 77304 populations will be supported, out of them,1817 is planned to be women of reproductive-aged who will be benefit RH services.- In Menbij, a total of 12 communities will be targeted by a PHC in little Al Hayyeh, 13220 population will be supported, out of them, 311 women of reproductive aged is estimated to be target by RH services. • In Ar Raqqa governorate, a total of 5 PHCCs will be established and supported targeting 31 Communities, 63791 population, out of them, 1500 women of reproductive aged is estimated to be target by Reproductive health services. • For DEZ, a PHC in Basira community in Basira sub-district is planned to be supported which 33 communities will target, 70764 population, 1664 out of them is expected to be women of reproductive-aged who will be receive RH services.

Implementation of activities

Through this project, Bahar will deliver primary health services to the affected population in the target areas. These activities will be mainly related to medical consultation, medicine distribution, normal deliveries and Reproductive Health RH services, leishmaniasis, lab services, health promotion, and referral services. The services will be delivered through Bahar-supported health facilities in Ar Raqqa, DEZ, Aleppo governorates. Specifically in Ar Raqqa city and sub-districts, Basira, and Menbij, and Ain Al Arab areas. # Number and Modalities of our health activities:1- Ain Al Arab (Bahar) PHC Center: in Ain Al Arab district, Ain Al Arab sub-district, Ain Al Arab community, Aleppo governorate. 2- Little Hayyeh (Bahar) PHC Center: in Little Hayyeh community, Menbij subdistrict, Menbij district, Aleppo governorate. 3- Ar-Raqqa (Bahar) PHC Center: Ar Raqqa district, Ar Raqqa sub-district, in Ar-Raqqa city, Ar-Raqqa governorate. 4- Abbara (Bahar) PHC Center: in Abbara community, Ar-Raqqa subdistrict, Ar-Raqqa district, Ar-Raqqa governorate. 5- Kaltah (Bahar) PHC Center: in Kaltah community, Ar-Raqqa subdistrict, Ar-Raqqa district, Ar-Raqqa governorate. 6- Mashrafa (Ar-Raqqa) (Bahar) PHC Center: in Mashrafa (Ar-Raqqa) community, Ar-Raqqa subdistrict, Ar-Raqqa district, Ar-Raqqa governorate. 7- Ein Issa (Bahar) PHC Center: in Ein Issa community, Ein Issa subdistrict, in Tell Abiad district, Ar-Raqqa governorate. 8- Basira (Bahar) PHC Center: in Basira community, Basira subdistrict, DEZ district, DEZ governorate. 9- Referral System: consists of 3 Ambulances in Ar-Raqqa city, Ar-Raqqa governorate, Ar Raqqa district.

Protection Risk Analysis

- Arrest of the humanitarian workers, especially in Ar Raqqa and DEZ if any military operation take place in NES by Turkey against SDF, and if there is any advance of GoS forces to SDF-controlled areas. To mitigate this risk, Bahar has set an evacuation plan for its staff with early criteria of triggering this plan.- Sexual Exploitation: By any of the health staff against female beneficiaries to deliver the required service. In order to mitigate this risk, Bahar has developed HR rigid policy for protection and accountability, and delivered several training to its staff about PSEA. M&E department of Bahar is closely observing and monitoring the adherence of the staff to Bahar HR policies and SOPs. Additionally, Bahar has a policy of gender balance during the recruitment of its staff, including M&E staff, where the female employees will conduct meetings with female beneficiaries to make sure that none of them were exploited by any of Bahar staff.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- GoS advance towards northern and eastern DEZ governorate (SDF-controlled areas) - Medium - Preparation of evacuation plane when there is any risk to our team- Late delivery of medical and laboratory equipment, consumables - high - local procurement.- Late delivery of medications - high - local procurement or looking for donations from other INGOs & agencies.- Access to health services / facilities - Medium - IEC campaign to measure the access of vulnerable people to the health facilities and investigate if there are any challenges against the access of these groups to the health centers - High - (Shift to mobile services, special days for females, raise the issue with local authorities). - scanty of qualified staff - Medium - capacity building training and workshops.- Intervention of local authority in our project's activities in such a way that obstruct our work - High - negotiation with them and coordination our negotiation together with the NES TWGs.
