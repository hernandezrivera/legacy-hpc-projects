Project Summary

The project will focus on enhancing household food, and nutrition security of the poorest and vulnerable households of both the host community and refugee. The targeted households will get support for nutritious food production. The activity will cover vegetable and fruits produced in the homestead with low irrigation facilities. In addition, it will promote self-reliance of the Host Community and refugees with selected beneficiaries who will receive capacity building training on the targeted trade and start-up package support to establish their enterprise. 80% of the targeted beneficiaries will be female from both the host communities and refugees. The project will work for empowering women through exclusively working with the women in the host community and camps for increasing their self-reliance. The intervention will be market oriented and including with the support from social enterprise. Overall, the project will raise awareness of the local institutions and communities for improving social cohesion between the Host Community and refugees.

Needs

Cox’s Bazar, with a population of 2,290,000, is one of Bangladesh’s poorest and most vulnerable districts, with 33% live below the poverty line, and 17% below the extreme poverty line. The rapid and massive increase of the refugee population, concentrated in the south of Cox’s Bazar district, has had an enormous impact on Bangladeshi host communities’ food security, economic vulnerability, market access, labour opportunities and environment. In addition, the refugee influx of 2017 occupied about 6,000 acres of forested land including more forest lands are degraded due to fuel collection. These forest land degradations severely impacted upon the host communities as forest lands were their major livelihood means. Only half of the host community households and almost no refugee household can afford nutritious diet, even with assistance (REVA2). Under existing conditions, the refugee population remains 100% reliant on food assistance to sustain the minimum daily required kilocalories per individual and for diet diversification. The food and nutrition security of the poorest amongst the host community is a growing concern. Many have lost access to previously farmed lands (often government forest land) and have simultaneously lost work opportunities to cheaper refugee labour market. Forest products on which they previously depended are no longer available. The situation accumulates grievances among the host communities may trigger unrest situation that affect peaceful co-existence and may end up violent conflicts. Therefore, it has become increasingly clear that the response actors will need to increase their focus on host community livelihood security, particularly in hard-hit communities. In addition, natural hazards and deforestation may prolong and worsen food insecurity situation for both host communities and refugee and exacerbate the likelihood of natural hazards and its impact on both communities.

Activities or outputs

Activity-1: Enhance household food and nutrition security The poorest and vulnerable (bottom 30%) of the targeted host community households will get support for household food production. The activity will cover vegetable and fruits production in the homestead with low irrigation facilities. Also the beneficiaries will be supported in poultry and livestock rearing. It is expected that after self-consumption, a portion of the production will be sold in the specific market area. 80% of the beneficiaries will be selected from women folks of the communities/ households. Co-benefit of these interventions would contribute to household nutrition. To achieve similar objective, homestead vegetable production will be promoted in selected camps with inputs and capacity building support where 80% beneficiary will be women. Activity-2: Promote self-reliance of the Host Community and refugeesBased on market demand and needs assessment, cultural norms and practices- project will support the target beneficiary to establish different income generation activities like handicrafts production, cattle & goat rearing, poultry birds rearing, tailoring, shrimp farming, dry fish production, grocery, petty traders, tea stall, etc. Selected beneficiaries will receive capacity building training on the targeted trade and start-up package support to establish their enterprise. 80% of the targeted beneficiaries will be women from both the host communities and refugees with limited scope.The interventions will be market oriented and largely with the support from social enterprise. The targeted activity is designed for engaging women in above mentioned productive sectors and the products will be linked with the existing markets. If the target women are from the camps, then the production will go to the camp markets and for the host community, formal market linkage will be developed which would finally contribute in women empowerment. Activity-3: Increase awareness of the local institutions and communities for improving social cohesion between the Host Community and refugees Under this activity, the host community institutions which can play important role on promoting social cohesion will be developed for replenishing their services. This activity will forge the cohesion between the host community and refugee and also support the stakeholders who have an active role in the refugee operation. The intervention will also facilitate dialogues among key stakeholders to established a positive social cohesion and minimizing the gaps between the host community and refugees.
