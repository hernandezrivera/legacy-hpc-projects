Project Summary

This project will increase access to lifesaving primary and reproductive health care services through a two pronged approach. The IRC will support the delivery of a minimum package of integrated services at Libyan primary and secondary care facilities levels, in areas identified with the highest scale and severity of humanitarian needs targeting the vulnerable individuals including migrants, refugees, displaced and non displaced Libyans. Support of the PHCCs will include basic capacity enhancement, provision of essential medicines and basic equipment as well as minor repairs of the health facilities. The IRC will also employ mobile clinic approach to reach vulnerable individuals with limited access to health services in Tripoli, Misrata and Sirte. The two pronged access approach will strengthen comprehensiveness of communicable and non-communicable disease, MNCH, mental health and psycho-social support to vulnerable populations. This mixed approach of deploying both emergency medical teams and strengthening capacity of health facilities to provide integrated and lifesaving services to vulnerable individuals including children, women, girls, men, boys. Project will work with the protection actors to scale up management of rape survivors.

Needs

These needs have been identified and prioritized in the 2019 humanitarian needs overview following thorough review of various assessments reports, program reports and migration data from key agencies.• Weak and fragmented health system including weak capacity of the health workforce with mal-distribution of skill sets. This had been compounded by destruction of health infrastructures in most health facilities, particularly in Sirte. • Lack of essential medicines and medical supplies for management of common illnesses, laboratory reagents for diagnosis of infectious diseases. This has limited capacity of public health facilities to provide essential health care to local communities, migrants, refugees and the displaced• The plight of migrants in Tripoli, Misrata, Benghazi, IDPs in Misrata and Sirt, refugees, vulnerable groups and rural communities with limited access to life saving services• Weak disease surveillance systems and rapid response mechanism for early identification and response to disease outbreak.

Activities

• Provide PHC clinical consultations for maternal and reproductive health, child health and communicable and non-communicable diseases as well as referrals to secondary health facilities for the entire population through emergency medical teams and static facilities.• Support to common diagnostic services for improved diagnosis and patient management through static facilities and emergency medical teams.• Support to clinical care of sexual assault survivors through emergency medical teams and PHCCs• Basic mental health and psycho social services by supporting the PHCC clinician to conduct a routine mental health screening and consultation and referral of sever mental health cases to secondary health facilities. IRC will support the PHCCs with basic mental health commodities • Human resource capacity enhancement through refresher trainings (mhGAP, CCSAS), supportive supervision to PHCC staff• Procurement of drugs and medical supplies for emergency medical teams and for donation to PHCCs • Referral support to secondary health care• Basic repairs and infrastructure improvement for PHCCs• Health promotion and education through CHW network
