Project Summary

Conflicts and changing climatic conditions in Somalia have increased the IDPs in the country and vast majority are vulnerable.
KAAH Relief and Development Organization is submitting this proposal to enhance the living conditions of the internally displaced persons (IDPs). To achieve that KAAH organization will provide enhanced coordination, access to information. The proposed also includes provision of multi-services from site planning, levelling, site maintenance and durable solutions.
These proposed interventions targets IDPs in Lower-shabelle and Gedo displaced by floods and conflicts.
The organization also aims to improve sustain the life and livelihood of people including 2.2 million Non-IDPs and 1.8 million IDPs across 74 districts by ensuring safe, equitable and dignified access to food and essential basic services by the end of 2021.
The organization aims to achieve the cluster Objective -> To improve the living conditions and protection of IDPs in sites and settlements and ensure equitable access to services and assistance of all persons in need, with a clear focus on moving towards durable solutions with full participation of the displaced and host communities.

Project Activities

In a bid to achieve the cluster objectives, KAAH Relief and Development organization aims to undertake a number of interrelated and complementary activities including setting up and strengthening of camp governance structures where necessary to enhance information-sharing and foster system-wide coordination among service providers.KAAH organization will offer services to support community-led site maintenance activities through cash for work schemes to ensure upkeep of sites, enhance hygiene and sanitation levels and contribute to overall reduction in communicable diseases. Where necessary, the organization will implement emergency site improvement projects to minimize protection risks and ensure safety. Key program activities will also include service mapping and site monitoring processes that will be carried out in close coordination with the CCCM cluster and other partners to effectively address existing gaps and maximize efficiency of the humanitarian response. Compiling of a master list of sites and mapping of both accessible infrastructure and partners operating in sites will be prioritized to enhance service delivery and coordination. In the selection of beneficiaries to participate in program activities, KAAH will adapt a systematic approach with a clear criterion to prioritize most vulnerable households including the marginalized, single headed-households, large families and/or families with children under the age of 5, women at risk as identified by protection officers and those with special needs. Data collected to inform beneficiary selection will be segregated by gender to ensure that no group is left-out. The effectiveness of the aid package will be evaluated through monitoring of activities thus ensuring that beneficiaries access their services safely and in a dignified manner. Protection will be mainstreamed throughout project duration by closely coordinating with other partners operating Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV) crisis centers and facilitating principled referrals where necessary. An elaborate feedback and complain mechanism will be setup to ensure accountability to the affected population.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and services- KAAH will ensure that during registration and distribution access for the beneficiaries and reduce the distances of travel and close proximity to the settlement sites.- The organization will employ distribution security personnel to ensure no violence occurs during the distribution process. The organization will ensure that no beneficiary is subjected to violence by the staff or other affiliates of the organization and will minimize the turnout of beneficiaries to mitigate violence.- To reduce risk of exposure and spread of COVID-19, the organization will limit the number of people turnout during registration and distributions. - The organization upholds the ‘DO NO HARM’ policy and will practice to ensure beneficiaries are dignified and are treated humanely at any stage of the implementation. 2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance- To prevent any form of sexual exploitation and abuse, KAAH will enforce the organizational policies. - All personnel and affiliates of the organization are asked to sign the organizational PSEA policy and the code of conduct. KAAH will ensure due process for any allegation of SEA including investigation and consequences and referral of victims to support centers.- KAAH will implement feedback mechanisms including hotline numbers for complaints.- Regular awareness and campaigns are made to ensure beneficiaries know of their rights
