Project Summary

Increase the telecommunications capacity in the refugee response area of operations and improve the access of humanitarians to telecommunications tools for operations and security messaging.
Improve the emergency telecommunications preparedness of field actors to respond to natural hazards.
Collaborate with inter-sector initiatives to improve affected communities' access to vital information and communications services.

Needs

Telecommunications capacity in the camps needs to be improved - there is uncertainty regarding the availability of mobile networks in this hazard prone area.Data connectivity in the camps is a challenge to the desires of humanitarian actors to coordinate and work closer to the affected population.The emergency telecommunications disaster preparedness and response capacity should be increased so that system redundancy and skills of humanitarian actors to confidently respond to disasters is improved.

Activities or outputs

1. Connectivity to the common humanitarian hubs will be maintained, and ETS will implement a wireless point to point data network through the refugee camps to serve the needs of the humanitarian community and the CiC. The network will have centralised access control with a sign up system and back up redundancy to increase robustness.2. The ETS will work with the BTRC to find a path forward for NGOs to have access to VHF radio equipment, this will include the permissions for NGOs to import their own VHF transceivers, as well as additional frequency licenses to increase the channels available in the camps. ETS will provide the VHF backbone for NGO/UN operational use.3. Disaster preparedness and response capacity will be increased by prepositioned equipment, and training for humanitarian actors - including NGO once access to emergency telecommunications equipment is approved.4. In collaboration with the Communications with Communities working group, provide continued support to the information hubs in the camps through the provision of audio visual equipment, connectivity, training and technical assistance.5. The ETC Connect app will be maintained as a lightweight feedback and reporting platform with some development to be carried out to provide a case management and complaints feedback platform.6. Increase the telecommunications capacity of the Cyclone Preparedness Program through technical advisory and equipment support through partners.
