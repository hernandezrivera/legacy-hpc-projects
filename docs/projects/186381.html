Project Summary

REREHABET (Recover and Rehabilitate for Better Tomorrow) is a civil society organization with head office in Mile 16 – Buea. The ongoing crisis in the North West and South West coupled with the Covid-19 pandemic has led the entire regions to an increase in need for education assistance and preventive measures respectively. Distribution of school kits to vulnerable IDPs, returnees and home based children in the SWR of Cameroon will be one of the most important strategy since education cannot wait.
Many children with limited access to education equally experiencing little or no access to learning materials. The objective of this project is to see that children will have access to basic learning materials and additional class rooms in order for them to be able to assimilate what is been taught in school and good learning space for them. Our target beneficiaries are IDPs, returnees and homebased children/host. Both male and female with special note on disabled children shall be considered. The projected number of months to execute this project will be 5 starting from August 2022 to December 2022. The total cost of the project is 200,000USD being for direct cost, operational cost, overheads, staff, monitoring and evaluation not leaving out logistics.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The south west and the North West regions of Cameroon has been experiencing crisis for the past 5 to 6 years. Some daily activities derived from income generating, empowerment and host of others have been put on hold. The future of every child lies on knowledge which is the gateway to sustainable empowerment. Apart from the crisis, Covid-19 has caused more harm than good by slowing down economic activities preventing children from schooling due to little or no household income. All can wait but education which is the key to a successful nation can’t wait. The impact of the anglophone crisis alongside Covid-19 caused school closure and limited access to home education as well leading to a high social and economic cost for people across communities. Their impact however is particularly severe for the most vulnerable and marginalized boys and girls and their families. At this point, our foundation found it necessary to introduce a free learning space for most vulnerable children who could not afford some associated costs on education for young boys and girls of primary and secondary level with a special note to internally displaced persons whose parents have lost most of their income generating activities and are finding ways of re-establishing in current locations not leaving out disabled children. Based on Covid-19 rapid need assessment conducted in May, 2020, education assistance was at 3% from total needs of the population in both Meme and Fako division carried out by Miracle Charity Foundation. Our learning center established on the 25/09/2019 with limited access to basic school needs to these IDPS and home based children such as exercise books, text books and other important items needed to facilitate learning. Meanwhile other basic necessities such as Covid-19 and hygiene wash kits shall be considered during implementation. The current count of registered children as compared to last academic year has increased by 44%. Approximately 500 young/adult boys and girls of age 5 to 16 pupils and 16 to 25 years of age for students. Majority of these children in addition to those located in other communities have limited access to above mentioned needs. Very limited access to environmental resources such as latrines, waste bins and class rooms. As a means of preventing the spread of Covid-19 with the use of social distancing, the current learning space needs to be upgraded.We intend to reach out to at least 4100 vulnerable children within Fako, Meme, Ndian, Kupe Muanenguba and Lebialem Division in addition to the construction of 4 classrooms. These vulnerable children shall be both male and female with special notes on disabilities. Activities involve are distribution of exercise, text books and other learning materials. In addition, we intend to improve on the hygiene condition of the environment by constructing latrines, waste management and construction of additional class rooms. It will take a period of 9 months beginning April 2022 to December 2022. Total cost of the project is seen to be 200,000USD being for direct cost associated to the children, operational, overheads, monitoring and evaluation and logistics.
