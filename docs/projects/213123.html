Project Summary

According to the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2024, an estimated 16.7 million
Syrians in need of humanitarian assistance. An estimated 13.6 million are in need for
WASH services. Amid the lack of fuel and electricity, the level of public services to the
population has remarkably decreased, this is including the water supply and sanitation
services. Moreover, water and sanitation networks have been damaged due to the armed
conflict and in many areas became under serving the population especially in densely
populated areas. Access to WASH services is compromised by the prolonged
deterioration of existing systems caused by damage over years of conflict, wear from
long-term functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, continuous drain of
technical staff, weak system management and poor natural resource management. Lack
of or an inadequate quantity of safe water is leading people to revert to unsafe water
sources, threatening lives.
The proposed activities will be: Given the above challenges and needs, it is vital to
respond to and prioritize the provision of solutions for the insufficiency of the provision of
drinkable water and the insufficiency of available sewage systems in vulnerable target
areas in need.
The proposed activities, locations, and number of people served by each activity:
1-Water network expansion in Daraa-Ankhal, Jbab, Homs-Ar-Rastan.
2-Equipping water stations with solar power systems in Daraa – Kafr Nassij, As-Sweida –
Kherba, Thaala.
3-Sewage network expansion in Daraa-Al Mansheya, Sheikh Miskine, As -Swedia city,
Qarayya, Walgha, Homs-Bustan Al Diwan neighbourhood, Al Arman neighbourhood.
Protection Risk Analysis: The risk: During the implementation maybe beneficiaries fall
down in barriers, GOPA-DERD will ensure having safety barriers, warning taps holed by
cones.
During the excavation work maybe other infrastructure services such as electric cables,
other existing water network, to avoid this risk we will have infrastructure drawing to
follow depend on it.
The safety of the target population during the implementation of the projects will be
considered. For example, to assure the safety of pedestrians and traffic movement,
proper signs will be in place at the project site in addition to placing safety barriers and cones at the project site. Target communities are informed about the planned intervention and the complaint response mechanism (CRM) and available channels for providing feedback and complaints.

Moreover, projects are highly needed and top priority, so that more likely approval will be quicker.

The real and practical way of implementing the required work, which is compatible with all procedures documented by GOPA-DERD Engineering Unit, is the execution of the work by contractors who are experienced in the field of civil projects, including all damages in the sewage, water network also equipping the water station with solar power system that will be repaired as follows: - The works will be carried out according to the tendering method followed by GOPA-DERD through a dedicated contractor, following GOPA-DERD’s Procurement policy. - BoQ of the necessary needs will be prepared according to the criteria for reusing the private apartment. - GOPA-DERD and its partner will coordinate with regards to the implementation monitoring and follow-up during all phases to achieve the implementation according to the technical conditions adopted that achieve partners’ trust. - Some necessary modifications may be made during the implementation phase, as rehabilitation works require sometimes the necessities of cancellation, addition or modification. After all planned works are being completed, a special committee is formed to take over the executed works from the contractors and to ascertain the quality in the specified time. The work will then behanded over to the house owner and make sure that the planned BOQ for each house has been fully implemented, as the owner will be asked to sign the BOQ before and after. GOPA-DERD will handing over the project in coordination with Departments of water recourses in areas. The procedures for all kind of projects as per GOPA-DERD Engineering Unit guidelines are as follows: o Preparing BoQ for each project o Preparing the needed documents for the tender (Tender Book) o Tendering process (announcement - offers evaluation- selection - contracting) o Giving a letter of work commencement and handing over the site o Implementation (schedule - implementation): 1-Water network expansion: Laying water pipe including excavation, back filling, tests. 2-Equipping water stations: assembling solar power systems including site mobilisation and connections. 3-Sewagenetwork expansion: Laying sewage pipe including excavation, back filling, tests. Technical follow-up (Follow up – supervision) o Reception of the works and handing over it to their owners o Closing the project and final report
