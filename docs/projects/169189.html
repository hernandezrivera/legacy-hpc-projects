Project Summary

The project will improve access to essential gender-responsive primary health care to people affected by the Beirut Port explosions by rehabilitating the damaged health centers to enable and maintain functionality and service delivery. Medair is aiming to support 2+ damaged centres within the next 3 months, and is currently in preliminary discussions with MoSA about Achrafiye and Bourj Hammoud SDCs, together with the other 3 SDCS which are damaged, as Medair has a close working relationship with MoSA since 2014. This is likely to be through structural repair, provision of equipment and supplies including PPEs, capacity building of health care providers, and subsidizing essential services while strengthening the infection prevention and control measures related to COVID19. The project may also address community outreach through general health, mental health, sexual and reproductive health, COVID-19 awareness sessions, and promotion of available services and resources for health.

1 - Needs

Additional support is required to ensure remaining medical facilities can continue to deliver primary health care and manage the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic for thousands of patients. In consultation with WHO, Connecting Research to Development (CRD), a Lebanese thinktank, completed a rapid health assessment of 55 primary healthcare centres (PHCs) in Beirut. The main findings showed that 37% suffered moderate-to-serious-damage, 13% had health workers injured during the blasts and 47% are capable to provide full routine health services. Approximately 55% of facilities expressed the need for equipment. As the COVID-19 situation remains extremely alarming with an increased resurgence of positive cases including among healthcare workers, extensive efforts are undertaken to link the response of COVID-19 to the blast emergency response in order to promote awareness and preventive measures.

2 - Activities

Medair's activities are aligned with the priorities of the flash appeal, the health cluster and the National Mental Health Programme. Medair will contribute towards the 1st Response Priority in sections 1.7 (Contribute to the rehabilitation of the affected primary health care centres) and 1.8 (Procurement of required equipment for affected PHCs to enable them to restart service provision) by rehabilitating 2+ health centres (particularly SDCs) and providing medical equipment. Medair will contribute activities towards the 2nd Response Priority in sections 2.3 (Procurement of needed supplies of essential medications (acute, chronic and mental health medications), medical consumables, reproductive health supplies sufficient for at least 3 months at the PHC level) through equipping the SDC's with supplies and providing PPE. Medication is unlikely to be included due to procurement/import timeframes. And section 2.4 (Provision of outreach services to affected populations including for general health services, acute conditions, chronic diseases, mental health conditions, and sexual and reproductive health, and link them to operational PHCs in the area for further follow up; subsidisation of consultations and procurement of appropriate PPEs and capacity building for service providers.) through the provision of community health volunteers and PSS Support Groups.
