Project Summary

The Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon have been facing an armed conflict since 2016. As a result, this has led to many individuals being displaced from their homes and communities and face difficulties in settling in other communities or upon return have lost most of their source of livelihood. Some others are faced with hosting families from communities with very high insecurity and has led to a significant increase in the burden of providing the needed resources for daily survival and upkeep. Even though food assistance and cash transfers are being provided in some of these communities, a great proportion of these individuals that are very vulnerable are still not benefitting.
Food assistance and cash transfers are present in some areas of the Northwest, but the threat of insecurity and accessibility as well as limited resources has greatly affected implementation in the hard-to-reach communities in the region.
In these hard-to-reach communities, host families of internally displaced persons and returnees are usually the most faced with burdens of survival due to the difficulties in readjusting to the changes that has happened within their communities due to the impact of insecurity. Through food assistance with nutritious food items provided are most at times not culturally what the locals are used to eating.
To address the shortcomings that have been experienced during these past years in the supply of food stuffs directly to the beneficiaries as well as the fact that beneficiaries had no alternative than what was supplied to them, we want to provide them with conditional cash transfer to improve upon their dignity. This delivery method will also go along way to ensure the use of innovation in project delivery (ICT4D)
Over the past two decades, Information Communication Technologies (ICTs) have become integral part of every walk of our life. ICTs have enabled organizations, businesses, financial institutions, manufacturers, farmers and entrepreneurs to achieve desired goals. ICT is an effective tool for humanitarian delivery to manage timely actions and response to market demands. Key benefits for the usage of ICTs are but not limited to time saving, accuracy, donor acceptability, Monitoring Evaluation Accountability Learning (MEAL), inclusiveness, fast decisions making, quality, global outreach, connectivity, collaboration, effective planning and quick response to feedback from the key stakeholders.
This assistance will be provided through conditional food assistance using the Red Rose app called “ONE app”. This will be used to manage activities that include beneficiary registration, distribution of cash and NFI using paper based QR code coupon. Red Rose web platform called “ONE platform” was used for data management & data analysis. and ONEplatform are part of Red Rose platform called “ONE Solution”. For this programme, we chose to use Red Rose due to its ability to provide a clear and transparent cash payment system (One Card) particularly important in a remote management context like that of the NW/SW regions, as well as the real-time monitoring options.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The project shall be implemented using the RedRose One Platform. The food needs of the beneficiaries shall be calculated and a food basket arrived at with the support of the CVWG. Once this conditional assistance is arrived at, we will then select respective beneficiaries who will benefit from the conditional food assistance. A market assessment will then be done to determine the availability of vendors in the respective communities. All basic project implementation preparatory processes shall cover the first 3 months and beneficiaries shall be served for 9 months for conditional food assistance. Each household shall be expected to benefit from 450,000frs by the end of the project.Objective:To improve upon the food assistance of at least 1,000 households in food insecure needs in the North West Region by the end of 2023.Specific objectives:1. Ensure food diversity in the food basket of households in the NW region2. Ensure flexibility with the use of CVA and incorporate the need of beneficiaries3. Maximum use of local markets in the project implementation area4. To ensure that beneficiaries are able to afford for desired food needs. Activities:1. Carry out an assessment of the food needs of the beneficiary then determine the food basket. 2. Undertake a market study of the possible vendors to serve in the project. Once they are analyzed, in areas where these vendors are present but don’t have enough capital, we shall determine an advance amount to be provided to the vendors to equip their shops with these materials.3. Installation of the RedRose platform and training of all vendors and staff of the project on its functionality.4. Pilot test the process with one community in the project area and learn from the process before scaling it to cover the while project area.5. Systematic monitoring and evaluation of process6. Carry out a post distributionTarget:This project is intended to target 1,000 households in the Donga and Mantung division, Bui Division, Boyo Division and Momo Division, that is 4 divisions of the 7 divisions in the NW region. We shall use complaint and feedback boxes in the communities where we be implementing as well as a complaint and feedback phone number for beneficiaries to express their challenges. These complaints shall be managed by the M&E of the project
