Project Summary

The project will assist in increasing the functionality of the existing water and sanitation facilities among the Rohingya and host community under the Friendship WASH Project. Through the assistance of hygiene volunteers, it will also improve the hygiene practices among the target group.

Objectives

To ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for all refugees living in camps and affected host communities to sufficient and sage water for drinking and domestic needs.

Needs

According to the latest report by the WASH sector, there is still a 21% gap in water facilities, 26% gap in functional latrine facilities and 6% gap in hygiene kits. WASH facilities in settlements are still underdeveloped with most facilities lacking basic protection measures (including gender segregation, MHM facilities) and are in locations not easily accessible for women. The risk of GBV for women while accessing latrines at night have also been reported.

Activities or outputs

1.1) Repairment and maintenance of the new and existing WASH facilities1.2) Desludging 213 latrines1.3) Repairment and maintenance of household-level water supply facilities.1.4) Repairment and maintenance of Bio gas plant2.1) Ensuring latrine facilities and hygienic by the volunteer2.2) Provioding hygiene messages among Rohingya and host community2.3) Organizing capacity building trainig for staff and 142 Volunteers2.4) Hygiene kit and MHM (menstrual hygiene materials ) kit distribution among 10,000 families

Key project/performance indicators

# of targeted people in settlements and host communities benefitting from safe water of agreed standards and meeting demand for domestic purpose: 18,391, # of targeted women, men, children in settlements and host communities benefitting of functional gender-segregated latrines of agreed standards: 18,391, % of targeted women, men, boy and girls able to demonstrate at least 3 critical hygiene practices: 80%, % of targeted women, men, boy and girls including older people and those with disabilities satisfied with WASH response: 80%.
