Project Summary

UPO will focus primarily on supporting 12,600 families and ensuring that the most vulnerable women, men, boys, and girls are at the center of the UPO ESNFI response. The ESNFI Needs Assessment will continuously be conducted with support from other partners to understand the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programs addressing these needs. The communities in the target areas are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support in improving their lives to cope with managing recurrent shocks and building resilience. In order to have an effective and efficient response to the needs, UPO will continue with its ESNFI response in Darfur, Khartoum, Gedaref, Al Jazirah, Red Sea, the Northern State, and South Kordofan. Since the recent events erupted in Sudan on the 15th of April between the Sudan armed forces and the Rapid Support Forces, which caused mass displacement in all the above-mentioned locations and further exacerbated the needs and vulnerabilities. UPO will ensure close coordination with all partners throughout its interventions. UPO activities in target locations will include coordination of needs assessments, NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization of communities, cash for shelter, reception and communal centres, tent distribution, ESK distribution, gender and environment protection and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people.
The project will also focus on promoting peaceful coexistence and community-shared management of the provided services to ensure ownership and sustainability in these harsh times that Sudan is going through.
UPO focuses on gender-sensitive programming modes throughout the implementation and exit stages of all the projects, ensuring complete community ownership and representation.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project will contribute to protection mainstreaming by ensuring that the activities are designed in a protection-sensitive way. i.e., distribution of the materials at safe points and building protective shelters. The project will also ensure (avoiding causing harm), (ensuring access to impartial assistance), (protecting people from violence), and (assist with rights claims, access to remedies, and recovery from abuse—i.e., HLP issues et al./support to transition with regards to shelter). ensuring confidentiality in sensitive matters through safe complaint mechanisms and valuing life and safety above all else

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

UPO recognizes the critical role of community feedback in enhancing its responsiveness and effectiveness. The utilization of community feedback will be embedded in various aspects of the project through the following strategies:1/ Community Consultations and Participatory Approaches:UPO will conduct regular community consultations, meetings, and focus group discussions to actively engage with community members. In addition to utilizing participatory approaches that involve community members in decision-making processes related to the project activities. 2/Inclusive Representation:UPO will ensure representation from diverse community groups, including women, men, boys, and girls, in feedback sessions to capture a broad range of perspectives. UPO will also consider the inclusion of marginalized and vulnerable individuals who may face additional challenges in expressing their needs. 3/ Capacity Building for Community Engagement:UPO will build the capacity of local community leaders and representatives to effectively articulate community needs and feedback, by fostering a sense of ownership and empowerment among community members to actively participate in shaping and improving project activities.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

UPO regularly informs the inter-agency coordination mechanism through information sharing, including best practices and lessons learned. Quarterly reports that include activities conducted, concerns raised, issues identified, and mechanisms established to address the issues will be prepared and submitted to the sector coordinators both at the field and Khartoum. At the field level, the organization will establish a committee to meet on a monthly basis to review the complaints and feedback received from the communities. The reports will be shared widely with other agencies working in the same areas.
