Project Summary

The International Rescue Committee will implement the project in the districts of
Beitbridge, Chimanimani, Chipinge, Chiredzi and Nyanga where it is operational. 18040
households (90 200) people will be targeted. In Chimanimani district, 550 households
affected by Cyclone Idai will be supported with multi-purpose cash transfers. In
Beitbridge, Chiredzi and Chipinge districts, inorder to minimize the risk of further food
insecurity deterioration, the IRC will provide support to 14 990 households by improving
access to stockfeed and support fodder production, processing and utilization as well as
support livestock vaccination, disease surveillance and management which will provide
immediate benefits to drought affected communities, protecting livestock-related assets
and assisting the rebuilding of key assets among the drought and COVID 19 affected communities.
Households in very dry parts of the five districts will be supported with access to small
grain seeds.
