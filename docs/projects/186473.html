Project Summary

The project aims at providing safe access to education and child safeguarding for 5,000 children in Ngala, Biu and Jere LGAs of Borno State. The project has two results area:
Result 1: 5000 children from age 5 to 18 will benefit from the provision of basic education through enhancement of school enrolment and retention of out of school children;
Result 2: 100 women and children will benefit from the establishment of a protection centre and strengthening of child referral pathways in Borno State through enhanced partnership.

1 - Needs

The ongoing conflict, which is now in its twelfth year, and its associated displacement of people has continued to put pressure on communities in the north east. According to a secondary data review by the Education in Emergencies Working group, attendance and enrolment rates continue to be low across northeast Nigeria, with 2.2 million boys, girls and adolescents having no access to basic education. Attendance rates vary significantly by population group (e.g. displaced vs. non-displaced) as well as between and within states. Across most locations, girls are less likely to be attending schools than boys. Barriers to accessing education have been identified by previous assessments. Financial constraints and poverty are the most frequently cited barriers, followed by early marriage and cultural beliefs for girls and to a lesser extent, distance from schools. The inability of most households to carry out livelihood activities due to a combination of factors such as security constraints, lack of access to land and displacement has made it difficult for caregivers to support their children and keep them enrolled in school. Ngala, Biu and Jere LGAs with a large number of IDPs and out of school children continue to need support to ensure that children are enrolled into schools and retained. These needs require a multi sectoral approach to ensure that children acquire education in a safe and conducive environment where their protection is taken into full consideration. In addition to the needs for education and food, and livelihood assistance for families, girls, boys and women who are victims of abuse often do not have anywhere to turn to as there are limited safe homes for such victims and survivors of violence to stay and receive support.

2 - Activities

1.1 Provision of Education support to 9 Basic Schools; the project aims at providing education supplies to 3000 children in 9 basic schools. Each education supply pack will consist of a bag, stationery and age-appropriate study materials based on Borno State Universal Basic Education curriculum.1.2 Training and Engagement of 45 Basic Education in Emergencies Teachers 45 basic education in emergencies teachers will be supported to provide educational services in the target schools. They will receive training in child protection and safeguarding in accordance with LABDI Child Safeguarding protocols, using UNICEF guidelines and training slides and UNICEF Education training manual.1.3 Set Up of 6 Child Friendly Spaces across 3 LGAs; the project will set up 6 child friendly spaces to serve as avenues to attract and educate 1500 out of school children in 3 LGAs. Each child attending the CFS will be given education pack containing a bag, stationery and study materials using the Teaching at the right level (TaRL) strategy.Activity 2 Establishment of Protection Centre and Strengthening of Child Referral Pathways2.1 Set-up A Referral System for children and women in need of temporary shelter to ensure unhindered access of deserving survivors, LABDI will provide pick-up services for survivors and utilize UNHAS helicopter service for pick-up of cases outside Maiduguri. Activity 3 Family Strengthening3.1 Provide Quarterly Food Assistance to Vulnerable Families of Children at the CFS and Basic Schools to ensure that children are well taken care of and reduce school dropout rates, the project will provide quarterly food assistance to vulnerable caregivers of children at the CFS and Basic Education. 3.2 Provide Income-Generating Activities for Caregivers of Children in the CFS as a sustainability strategy, caregivers will be trained on income-generating activities and supported to establish businesses to enable them care for their children

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

LABDI consults widely with all community members during project design and provides a platform for all beneficiaries to give feedback freely. The project will target school children and utilize child representative platforms to seek their views on all decisions that concern them. LABDI also implements projects in consultation with caregivers of children, who are part of the Key Implementing Partners (KIPs) and are fully involved in decision-making. To ensure that everyone's voice is heard, LABDI utilizes community volunteers and team leads selected from the beneficiaries and serve as the first point of call for beneficiaries to be able to give feedback. In implementing this project, LABDI will use the approach of focus group discussion give feedback of the intervention and raise any concerns they may have. This focus group discussion will be done across all project locations, including schools and Child Friendly Spaces.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will collect feedback in two ways:1. Community volunteers and team leaders are selected from caregivers in the community and are accessible to the community members for easy access and assistance.2. Periodic feedback sessions with different categories of the communities and camps will be carried out during quarterly surveys. Attention will be given to feedback from all categories, including boys, girls, men, women, elderly and persons with special needs. The monitoring and evaluation team of LABDI will carry out quarterly post distribution monitoring of the food and ensure that beneficiaries receive their entitlement and give feedback on the support received.
