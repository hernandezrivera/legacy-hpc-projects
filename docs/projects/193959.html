Project Summary

The project aims to minimize the health impacts of humanitarian and public health emergencies through provision of immediate life-saving activities, strengthening of service delivery & improve access to quality health services to people in need specifically for those who are affected by armed conflict and vulnerable one like PWD, IDPs, and Host communities in Khartoum, North Kurdufan, South Kurdufan, North Darfur, White Nile and Kassala.
The project will be strengthening provision of quality primary Health Services across 6 states, in Kassala state (Reifi Nahar Atbara, Reifi Aroma, Reifi Hamashkureib, Reifi Shamal Ad Delta), North Darfur (Melit, A Serief, Dar As Salam), North Kurdufan (Ar Rahad, Sheikan, Soudari, Um Rawaba) South Kurdufan (Abu Jubayhah Kadugli, Talawdi), Khartoum (Jebel Awlia Karrari, Um Bada), White Nile (Ad Diwaim, Aj Jabalain, As Salam / Ar Rawat, Guli,Kosti,Rabak.,Tendalti,Um Rimta)
The project targets 31100 HH in Khartoum, White Nile Kassala & North Darfur North Kurdufan, South Kurdufan. Among them is 30000 above 18 years old female 25000 male above 18 years old, 35000Female age below 18 years old and 30000 males under 18 years old.
The targeted localities selected from PIS operation areas determined by the HNO severities of health needs of 4 and 5 where significant proportion of population lacking access to basic services due to unavailability and poor coverage of outreach services, and also from the area affected by armed conflict or received IDPs from conflict areas.
The project link to Humanitarian Response Plan 2023 1st objective: Provide timely multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to crisis affected people to reduce mortality and morbidity. The health response will focus on accessible areas prioritizing pre-identified facilities, including existing emergency obstetric and new-born care facilities, to support with a comprehensive package of services (including operational cost, fuel and WASH in health facilities). In addition, reactivating the disease surveillance system and reporting in both government and partner’s supported facilities and supplying the static vaccination centres with the needed supplies will be a priority.
By support provision of emergency life saving health services in the targeted areas in the 6 targeted states and reduce the impact of the conflict on the health system by strengthening the capacity to respond timely to different emergencies and health out breaks by provisions of quality health services, including treatment of communicable & non-communicable disease and trauma , reproductive health services, support of EPI coverage enhance the clinics infrastructure provision of essential drug and medical supplies, capacity building of health services providers to response timely during the emergency conduct mobile clinics
Plan International will address the urgent needs by support provision of free accessible quality lifesaving health services in the targeted localities we will also work with health authorities across our programme areas to ensure availability of medical supplies and qualified health cadre and healthcare workers and build their capacity to promptly respond to health need of the targeted population ,we will also ensure availabilities of life saving medications and supplies , improve the infrastructures of the health facilities ,and enhance the reporting mechanism by expand the reporting capacity of the early warning systems, Strengthen emergency preparedness.
The project identified and prioritized the most at-risk individuals in the project areas as key affected persons/project participants, the common thread however, was that they were selected from IDPs, returnee and the host communities in the project areas. the main focus is not on targeted assistance to IDPs, but on assistance that can benefit both IDPs and host communities alike.

1 - Needs

As identified by the 2022National Multi-Hazard Health Emergencies Preparedness and Response Plan for Sudan,6,5 million people are residing in 141 localities (75% of all localities) that were identified as having a risk of the 6 ‘vital few’ hazards – causing 80% of the encountered emergencies - and hence prioritized and targeted for operational readiness and risk reduction interventions. The decline in service provision by the public sector is forcing the population to seek health services at the private sector that is dominating the scene with69.3 per cent of current health expenditure (National health recovery and reform policy and strategic plan NHRRP 2020-2022). According to the 2021 multi-sectorial needs assessment MSNA 72.3 per cent of the population had to pay in full for their health services despite a reported coverage by the national health insurance fund of 67 per cent. During 2021 the availability of emergency medicines declined steadily to reach 43 percent compared to 57 per cent during 2020.Throughout 2022 the targeted localities population were struck by drought in summer time (North Kurdufan &Kassala, North Darfur), floods at rainy season (Kassala) during which they lost their live stocks and stored seeds, tribal conflict (Kassala, North Darfur) There have been incidents of localized conflicts and inter-communal violence in some parts of targeted localities (Kassala) which have resulted in civilian casualties. All this continuous crisis with lack and reduced fund to health sectors and increase the cost of health services make it very difficult for those who leave in poverty to seek the health services. Currently most of the selected localities had received people who esape from war, and those localities had already experience disease outbreaks of endemic, water-borne, and vector-borne diseases like haemorrhagic fevers (North Kurdufan), and malaria (North Darfur), and monkeypox outbreak (Kassala), Vaccine-preventable diseases can recur due to low immunity among children and shortages in immunization coverage in several areas. Since the beginning of the current conflict, health service provision has been heavily impacted across the country, but specifically in Khartoum. Several critical programs have been discontinued due to violence and attacks on health facilities, disruption of supply chain, and poor accessibility to still operating facilities. Disruptions affected the power grid, water piping systems, fuel availability, and mobility of health staff rendered around 70 per cent of the health facilities across Khartoum out of service. Hospital facilities are severely impacted with mobile clinics activated. Due to heavily centralized health system and reliance on the country’s capital for medical supplies distribution, performing laboratory tests, funding, and other aspects of the health provision across the country, all states were negatively affected by the violence that erupted in Khartoum.According to rapid assessment report released recently by save the children, Health facilities in Khartoum and in the greater Darfur are either not functioning or closed, which limits access to care, malnutrition treatment and vaccination for children under five. • In Khartoum, 61% of health facilities are closed and only 16% are operating as normal, leaving millions of people without access to health care. • Health system in West Darfur is on the verge of collapsing and no health services is available for the affected people with the exception of some mobile clinics established by the armed groups to provide first aid for the injured. • An estimated 50,000 acutely malnourished children have had their treatment disrupted due to the conflict. • Health facilities and warehouses in Khartoum and greater Darfur looted; there is lack of syringes, medicines, gloves and other medical supplies, further weakening the health system.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Plan International will address the urgent needs by support provision of free accessible quality lifesaving health services in the targeted localities we will also work with health authorities across our programme areas to ensure availability of medical supplies and qualified health cadre and healthcare workers and build their capacity to promptly respond to health need of the targeted population ,we will also ensure availabilities of life saving medications and supplies , improve the infrastructures of the health facilities ,and enhance the reporting mechanism by expand the reporting capacity of the early warning systems, Strengthen emergency preparedness.The project adheres to health cluster objective1: Support essential public health functions with focus on strong primary health care.by support Provision of essential primary and secondary health care services, including trauma care, EmONC and referrals to 150,574 PIN in the targeted locality as well as support the referral cost for secondary levels for those who need it.Also, the project links to the sector objective 2: Strengthen emergency preparedness, response, and all-hazards emergency risk management. By Addressing health emergencies and providing timely response in the targeted localities, the response will include but not limited to expand the reporting capacity of the early warning systems, strengthen capacity to investigate and detect disease outbreaks, increase access to health services during emergency time by supporting mobile medical units and referral pathways. Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services as well as Increase access to health services by rehabilitating and refurbishing health facilities with provision of medical equipment.The health services will be provided as the treatment of non- communicable diseases, ensuring the availability of essential medicines, provision and expansion of Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) for RH to support reproductive, neonatal, and child health; and strengthening the expanded program of immunization EPI will be guided by adopting Plan values as equality, the services will be gender responsiveness and ensure the safety of the children while they are receiving the health services. Services for GBV survivors will be provided across all the center, confidentiality, safety and privacy will be core of services provision, PIS will ensure that health providers are equipped to provide survivor-centered, right-based, non-discriminatory, high-quality health care.
