Project Summary

Cameroon has experienced a prolonged and escalating conflict, particularly between the Anglophone regions (Northwest and Southwest) and the central government. The conflict, which began in 2016 as protests over marginalization of Anglophone communities, has evolved into a full-scale armed conflict. The ongoing violence has led to displacement, loss of life, and a breakdown in social cohesion between different linguistic and ethnic groups in the country.
The education system has not been immune to the crisis. Schools have been disrupted by violence, and students and teachers have been caught in the crossfire. Beyond the physical disruptions, the social and psychological impact of the conflict has also deepened divisions among students, especially in areas where ethnic, linguistic, and political lines are drawn.
Hate speech and divisive rhetoric have become prevalent, both online and in the communities, exacerbating mistrust and hostility. In schools, these tensions manifest as bullying, discrimination, and exclusion, especially between students from Anglophone and Francophone backgrounds. Teachers, often caught between their professional duties and the polarized political climate, may struggle to create neutral, inclusive, and peaceful classrooms.
In light of this context, there is an urgent need to address these divisions in schools and promote values of respect, tolerance, and social cohesion to ensure that the next generation can build a peaceful and united Cameroon.
This project on Promoting Social Cohesion and mitigating hate-speech in schools in Mezam and Bui Division of the North West Region of Cameroon aims at addressing the impact of hate speech and violence within schools in Mezam and Bui Division of the North West Region of Cameroon, affected by the ongoing Anglophone crisis. The project will focus on mitigating hate speech, countering violence, promoting social cohesion, and fostering a culture of peace and understanding among students and teachers. Through trainings, awareness campaigns, and student-led initiatives, the project seeks to create safe and inclusive learning environments where students and teachers from diverse backgrounds can coexist peacefully, irrespective of their ethnic, linguistic, or political differences thereby bridging inter-generational gaps and building good parent and teacher relationship.
Targeted Audience
• Primary Target: Students and teachers in schools located in Mezam and Bui Divisions of the North West Regions of Cameroon
• Secondary Audience: School administrators, parents, local community leaders, and regional education authorities in Mezam and Bui Division of the North west Region of Cameroon.

Awareness Campaigns• Develop learning materials (posters, videos, radio messages) that highlight the destructive impact of hate speech and encourage the use of inclusive, non-violent language in schools.• Use local media, including community radio stations, to reach a wider audience and raise awareness about the importance of unity and reconciliation.B. Capacity Building of Teachers• Provide training for teachers on conflict-sensitive pedagogy, including strategies for managing classroom dynamics in politically and ethnically charged environments.• Offer practical tools to help teachers address issues of hate speech, bullying, and political discourse while fostering inclusive and neutral learning spaces.• Train teachers on addressing SGBV and Sexual harassment in schools. C. Student Workshops and Peacebuilding Activities• Organize workshops that teach students about the importance of social cohesion, empathy, and conflict resolution. Topics will include overcoming ethnic divisions, understanding different perspectives, and working toward a shared vision of peace.• Host role-playing activities, debates, and discussions that challenge stereotypes and foster dialogue between students from different backgrounds.D. Peer Mentoring and Student-Led Initiatives• Establish a peer mentoring program where students from different linguistic and ethnic groups mentor one another, focusing on building trust and promoting understanding.• Encourage the formation of student clubs focused on promoting peace, tolerance, and social justice within the school.E. Safe Spaces and Dialogue Sessions• Create regular opportunities for open dialogues between students and teachers to address sensitive issues such as language, ethnicity, and politics.• Facilitate "talk circles" or "community forums" where participants can express their experiences and emotions in a controlled, respectful environment.F. Community and Parental Engagement• Involve parents and community leaders in the project to build broad-based support for social cohesion efforts.• Organize community meetings to discuss the impact of the conflict on education and share ideas for fostering peace at the school and community levels
