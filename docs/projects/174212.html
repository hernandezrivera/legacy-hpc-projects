Project Summary

The aim of the proposed project is to provide sexual and reproductive health care services to IDPs and host communities in Michika and Madagali LGAs by Supporting the community to prevent and report Sexual GBV cases especially during the after the post-Covid-19 through awareness-raising campaigns in the communities.
Also, the project proposed will support the communities with Registered Midwives, Practical Skills Training of Traditional birth attendants (TBAs) and Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs) to provide safe delivery services and support for exclusive breastfeeding to women, to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality.
The project will improve Sexual Reproductive Health Services in the Local Government Areas (LGAs) strengthening health systems, build capacity, provide Medical Care to Survivors of Sexual Violence.

1 - Needs

The 2020 Humanitarian Needs Overview identified 3,349,094 women of reproductive age and 4,286,774 children in their needs of life-saving health activities. There are insufficient numbers of skilled and appropriately trained health care staff, health workers are also reluctant to work in remote communities. Poor referral services further limit access to life-saving emergency maternal and newborn healthcare services. The components of the project include prevention and treatment of HIV and other sexually transmitted infections, preventing maternal and newborn deaths and illness, and strengthening the health systems capacity for comprehensive sexual and reproductive health care.

2 - Activities

Project activities would include: 1. Procurement of essential maternal health medicines and equipment. 2. Refresher training of Midwives and Community Extention workers on the provision of quality family planning, antenatal care, safe delivery and emergency care to mothers and newborns. 3. Facilitating deployment of Midwives and Community extension workers to remote/hard-to-reach communities. 4. Strengthening referral networks for maternal and newborn emergencies within communities. 5. Provision of adolescent sexual reproductive health counseling and education. 6. Mobilization and sensitization of communities for the uptake of SRH services. 7. Recruitment of 30 community health volunteers (CHV) and training them on Antenatal, Post-natal referrals, and sensitization on danger signs in Pregnancy and reporting. 8. Working and interacting with community members through focus group discussions on preventive health care. 9. Interacting with clients who had accessed reproductive services through client exit interviews. 10. Physical observation of stores where commodities are kept for sustainability. 11. Strengthening referral networks for mental health concerns and emergencies within communities.
