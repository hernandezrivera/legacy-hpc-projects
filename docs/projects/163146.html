Project Summary

The intervention breaking on GBV in grass root communities of three division (Fako, Manyu, Ndian) in the south west region is the process of mainstreaming GBV into the ongoing performance base financing (PBF) health care project model that covers this three division. These ongoing (PBF) health project has already engage partnership with grass root operational CBO in this communities to monitor and evaluate the quality of health care delivery services patience received in hospital and health centre quarterly, meaning this CBO master this grass root communities and often come across GBV violation cases but lack the competence to carry out holistic intervention. With regard to humanitarian response in this grass root communities GBV have been silence as this grass root communities have not benefit from any intervention. As a result of this reason, IYEC a UN ECOSOC Accredited grass root NGO working in this PBF health care project in the three division came out with this project to break the silence and make GBV a history in this communities by creating a coalition of these grass root CBOs working in the PBF health project and build their capacities on the holistic response to GBV in this communities in order to mainstream GBV in their PBF health care project since they master this communities and have been working in this grass root communities even at the peak of the crises. The objective of this project is to build the capacity of frontline service providers (CBO, Community leaders) in these grass root communities on GBV Psychosocial First Aid and Case management, in these three divisions of the south west region of Cameroon. The target beneficiaries will be women and men, young girls and boys, internally displace persons (IDPs) and returnees. IYEC plan to intervene in Fako division ( west coast crick, Limbe III crick,Tiko crick), Manyu ( Akwaya, Tinto, Eyumudjock), Ndian (Toko, Mondemba,Ekondotiti, Bakassi Mbenmong, Akwa, Kombo itindi). In the project IYEC will encourage IDPs in these communities to return to their place of origin in order to increase the number of returnees’ in these communities

Besoins / Needs

The project will enable front line service providers to carry out psycho social first aid and Case management of GBV victims in the aspect of Rape, physical assault, sexual assault, emotional and psychological abuse, force marriage, denial of resources, opportunities and services. This is with regard to the assessment IYEC have carried out in this grass root communities on the GBV violations mention above.The project will trained front line service providers on psychosocial first aid and case managementThe project will also ensure CBO to mainstream GBV into their ongoing activitiesThere will also be mass sensitization on GBV in this communities to break the silence of GBVThe project will encourage IDPs to return to their original communities
