Project Summary

This project proposal is to improve the residency quality for 13000 IDP’s spread throughout 17 camps during the winter season of 2019-2020, and to reduce their suffering to the negative effect of rainy and snowy winter season especially to the most vulnerable group of women, children, and elderlies, through the specifically selected activities for each camp separately.
The followings are the suggested interferences that will be implemented in 17 selected camps at Idleb Governorate, Harem area, Al-Daana area, and Deir Hassan Cluster:
• To raise the flooring of the tent to prevent water leakage during the winter season.
• To insulate the outside of the tents and protect them from rain and snow to prevent water leakage.
• To insulate thermally the tent from inside to increase the efficiencies for retaining heat inside the tent during winter season, and reduce the cost of heating subsequently reducing the risk of fire.
• Improve the separation between women’ and men’ latrines, especially in camps that SPHERE standards are not implemented and the number of latrines is small.
• Gravelling some sidewalks and roads in camps to facilitate the arrival to latrines.
Jana Watan (JWA) designed this proposed project to insure the participation of local communities in two aspects:
- The active participation of women to their need assessment during winter season and determine areas of activities for interferences.
- To ensure the participation of beneficiaries in the execution of the activities of the project, especially in rising the tents from the ground to encourage the feeling of responsibility (this will be explained later).

Needs

JWA surveyed the need assessments of 21camps that is located at Deir Hassan area, and are registered in CCCM. We found that 17camps need immediate interference and attention to prevent further deterioration and affect IDP’s negatively during winter season. Nevertheless, camps do not need any interferences for any activities . The following are the suggested 17camps for intervention:Abu Obeida Ben Aljaraah- Ezza-Al Ghoraba Homs-Al Naief-Al Rahmah Lahl Al Gab (Al Qaqaa)-Ghetaa Al Rahma 1-Khan Toman-Nasaem Al Khayr-Nour Al Houda-Sons of Mehin-Ahl Al Balad-Ebtieen-Al Mashhad-Reef Aleppo-Tal Al Karama-Al Wdhehee-Al Foqaraa (The Poor)The information and need assessments were obtained from several focus group discussions of camps inhabitants of men and women separately, even children were surveyed to let them express their fears about the winter season. The following the summary of our findings: • The above mentioned camps, some were built in an organize manner and others are randomly spread. All of the camp they are administered by a respected group of IDP’s whom are residing on the premises. Some camps are general ownership and some are privately owned with a lease or have contract of sale premises.• All camps at Deir Hassan cluster cannot do any infrastructure related to building an underground drainage system network, due to the rocky nature of the soil and the objection of landowners of the camps and the surroundings. Most of tents in that area were flooded during last winter.Raising the level of tents by using stones A bricks barrier around the tent will be constructed with height of 15cm. The gap will be filled by stones and sand, and will be compacted manually• Most camp residents suffer from the lack of ability to secure fuel for heating with enough quantity during winter season. Therefore, some resort to burning organic residue and plastic material to warm up, which cause high danger on family members especially children, and pregnant or breast feeding women. To reduce the consumption of fuel, and raising the ability of families to purchase fuel in an affordable manner, we suggested to thermally insulate the tents internally. This type of insulators proofed to save fuel consumption by25%, and essential role to reduce the spread of fire. Nasaem Al Khayr camp has tents with “Boat Style”, therefore it is impossible to install the internal thermal insulator, so we will make an exception and not install insulators in this camp. Sons of Mehin camp’s tents were internally insulated by another NGO last year. Ahl Al Balad camp is a concrete building with a plastic ceiling, will not be insulated. Abu Obeida Ben Aljaraah camp most of the tents with “Boat Style”, therefore it is impossible to install the internal thermal insulator, so we will make an exception and not install insulators in this camp also. The rest of Deir Hassan cluster tents will be internally insulated, with total number of 1460 tents with different sizes (4x4m, 4x6m, and 4x8m). We will purchase the thermal insulator from Turkey with this specification (200 – 210 g/m2).• Most tents at the selected camps are old and subject to rain water leaking, therefore, the activity to install tents’ cover for 2645 tents in the 17selected camps is highly needed.• The degree of separation standards between the latrines for men and women varies from camp to camp. Some camps have separated premises for women and men, some are joined, and some do not have any at all.According to HRP2019 Jana Watan Organization it's will working in the needs for HRP2019 also to the janawatan assesment in Idleb city and the needs for camps in this city and the huge poplution in camp we will work to help this pepole in this assesment .we see about 40% children living in hard situation and about 35% women ,25% man living in formal camp and camp .if some of the assessed sites will be targeted by other cluster members Jana Watan will transfer the implementation of the project activities

Implementation of activities

Camps residents will be encouraged to form community’s committees to represent them, which should include at least one women. Targeted camps managers, and the above mentioned committees, will participate with the beneficiaries’ in the implementation of the activities after explaining the procedure to how they can share their opinion and/or complain about the project.- After doing the final survey for the beneficiaries, determine the number and sizes of tents that will be worked on for each activity, and consequently determine the size of the stones activity, biding will be announced and a contract will be signed with the best contractor after comparing prices and experience in stones work according to our technical specification. - The targeted camps will be divided into sections in coordination with beneficiaries, and community’s committees to facilitate the raising level of tents by using stones A bricks barrier around the tent will be constructed with height of 15cm. The gap will be filled by stones and sand, and will be compacted manually activity on the ground of the tents, before the it starts raining, and supervise the work for contractor through technical supervisor, community’s committees, and M&E team. - The procurement for thermal insulator and plastic sheets will be done in Turkey, and transported to SCA’s warehouses in Syria at early stages of the project. JWA will train 15 workers on the how to install the internal thermal insulator, and divide then into 5 groups with 3 workers in each team. This activity will start immediately after the finishing of the stones activity on the ground’s tent, followed by the tight installation of the plastic sheets after printing the statement: “Donation not for sale”. - JWA ‘s M&E team and community’s committees will follow the progress of all project activities, along with the continuous feedback from the beneficiaries. JWA will be in responsible for the following-The procurement of all material to be purchased in the project, including thermal insulator, plastic sheets, and installing tools.-Raising tents activities;-M & E activities for the project;also JWA will carry out the following:-Transporting and storing purchased material in their warehouses;-Installing the thermal insulator; - Installation of plastic sheet by especial team.Activity1.1.3 :Raising the level of tents by using stones will be conducted as follows:A bricks barrier around the tent will be constructed with height of 15cm. The gap will be filled by stones and sand, and will be compacted manuallyActivity 1.2.24*4 m tents size will require 40 m2 thermal insulators, 4*5 m tents size will require 55m2, 4*6 m tents size will require 70 m2, and 4*8 m will require 90 m2 thermal insulator.
