Project Summary

The proposed project entails three integral parts, all aimed at increasing the access to and quality of basic education in the North Eastern part of Nigeria.
1. JRS Nigeria will work with Local Government Education Authorities (LGEAs) and other relevant stakeholder to identify and build capacity of 1500 (750M, 750F), custom- made teacher trainings for educators in classroom management, trauma management, Gender Norm Initiatives, etc, who will in turn use their newly acquired skills in the classroom to improve the educational quality of the public-school system, reaching a minimum of 45,000 children.
2. After school programs for 5000 out-of-school children to improve numeracy and reading skills.
Provision of teaching materials to trained teachers to assist in providing quality service to the pupils. 12,000 identified students (4800M, 7200F) between the age 3 and 18 years will receive learning kits, Dignity/Hygiene kit would be provided to students and orientation on how to use these kits will be conducted.
3. Ensuring effective school management in Borno and Adamawa states, Nigeria by training 200 school-based management committee members

1 - Needs

School structures were among other government properties that were affected as a result of insurgency and this had led to reduction in access to education in the BAY states of Nigeria. UNICEF reported that at least 802 schools remain closed and 497 classrooms are listed as destroyed, with another 1,392 damaged but repairable . The affected schools and pupils lost their teaching and learning materials to the incident.The following needs for education arise as a result of insurgency:i. Provision of teaching and learning kits for teachers and pupilsii. Provision of WASH facilities and hygiene kitsiii. Teacher’s trainingiv. SBMC training

2 - Activities

To address the above mentioned needs, JRS Nigeria will engage with local education authorities and other relevant stakeholders to identify 5 most affected schools each per local government authority (LGA) across ten LGAs of Adamawa and Borno states. Each of the selected schools will be provided with teaching materials and pupils attending those schools will receive learning kit to enhance learning. 1500 teachers (900M, 600F) across the ten LGAs will receive training on pedagogical skills and psychosocial support. Hygiene kits will also be distributed among pupils especially girls to encourage them to stay in school. School based management committee of the selected schools across the ten LGAs will be trained as well.
