Project Summary

- Based on the studies and reports submitted by international organizations and the questionnaires and studies carried out by the work team at the Qaderoon Foundation, it was found that there are several cases of malnutrition in infants and children under the age of 3 years, in addition to cases of health weakness in pregnant and breastfeeding women, and many individuals suffering from psychological trauma resulting from Loss of a loved one or situations of fear, whether from war or natural disasters. Many cases of autism, mental development disorders, Down syndrome, and mental and motor disabilities have also been observed.
- Within its 2024 strategy for the response plan in the health sector, Qaderoon Foundation offers a set of specialized programs to meet the aforementioned needs, including diet programs prepared by a nutritionist that include the provision of food baskets and periodic monitoring. Special programs have also been prepared to monitor and educate pregnant and breastfeeding mothers through awareness workshops and courses. Training that includes both fathers and clarifies the role of the father in the care process. There are also psychological support programs for those exposed to psychological trauma under the supervision of counselors and psychologists. These programs include workshops and recreational activities, in addition to our special programs related to people with special needs, which include intelligence measures, mental and physical rehabilitation programs, and physical therapy sessions. These programs include all target groups, including women, girls, children, adolescents, and people with disabilities. Special needs
- In our strategy, we face several risks that vary in degree of severity and probability, including:
. The weakness of funding and the lack of supporting entities led to the weakness of providing the required services as a result of the lack of supportive economic activities within Idlib Governorate.
o Difficulty in reaching some areas due to the lack and cost of transportation, which is a high and certain risk. We seek to provide a means of transportation for the organization to facilitate movement and reduce transportation costs
o Another high-level and confirmed risk is the lack of electricity and communications in many of the targeted areas in Idlib Governorate. If the organization provides a means of transportation, we can provide a mobile generator or battery and a transformer to provide electricity during the implementation of the programs
- The strategy for 2024 within the health sector response plan may be exposed to the risk of exploitation or gender-based violence. All employees and volunteers at Qadroun Foundation have been trained on the risks of exploitation and gender-based violence, and a response plan has been developed that includes a direct hotline with the project management in addition to training a special team. He makes periodic visits to the targeted areas, communicates directly and confidentially with the targeted people, and identifies any problems. A code of professional conduct has also been developed for the organization’s employees and volunteers.
- Protection risks were analyzed during the implementation of the programs within the response plan, and the results were simple, and the degree of risk and probability was minimal. This is because most programs aim to provide services collectively or at the family level, and the work teams include a group of girls, women, and males, and each group is headed by a person from the public administration. 0 - Project Summary (extended)

Idlib Governorate is suffering from post-crisis problems, the most important of which is the difficulty of reaching some areas, which has led to a significant spread of many diseases, especially among children, infants, pregnant and breastfeeding women.Within the strategy of the Qaderoon Foundation for Development for the year 2024/2025 and within the response plan, the Foundation has developed an action plan to meet the needs of the targeted areas within Idlib Governorate through rehabilitation and support programs for the targeted people provided by a specialized work team of psychologists and nutritionists to address cases of malnutrition and psychological trauma to which children and women have been exposed.
