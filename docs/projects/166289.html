Project Summary

The North East of Nigeria has been suffering lost of lives and massive displacements of women and men and children to safe places. According to IOM DTM , January 2020, 70 % of women including children were displaced from H2R( hard to reach places) in search of food assistance, WASH and protection. This massive displacements and killings have negatively impacted education of girls and boys with significant dropout of schools. The protection situation has exacerbated the low enrollment, retention and continuity of education of learners in the North East. Furthermore, In December 2019, an acute respiratory disease, known as COVID-19, appeared in Wuhan, China. The disease has spread rapidly around the world and has been found to be highly contagious. While currently the death rate remains low, between 2-4%, it is higher for certain at-risk groups and in areas without proper medical facilities. In Nigeria 40 cases have already been identified, as of March 23, 2020, and the government is taking measures to slow the continued spread of COVID-19 throughout the country. Among others, the closure of schools, recurrent locks down. This health pandemic has affected learning outcomes, with the change of examinations schedule, disruption of learning habits, and use of schools as healing centers, and exposure of girls to sexual exploitation and abuse and more domestic burdens.
in order to prevent spread of corona virus, keep learning habits of learners and prepare safe return o schools. In order to enhancing learning behaviors among adolescent girls and boys and prepare safe school return, AHA shall implement age,gender and context specific alternatives learning programs, including children with special needs. In addition, AHA shall raise awareness among learners, teachers and SBMC on importance of school resumption as well as provision of safe WASH facilities for effective prevention and response of COVID-19 among learners/teachers in a safe learning environment.

1 - Needs

The closure of schools in the North East is characterized by significant drop out of schools of boys and girls thus given girls burden of household chores, exposing them to cases of Gender-based violence with an undeniable negative impact on their learning habits. The closure of schools with intermittent lock down has affected the school year calendar. In addition, the use of schools by states authorities as quarantine centers will significantly affect schools reopening coupled with the states of certain school building in need of rehabilitation and necessary equipment as well as provision WASH facilities to prevent and curb Covid -19 among learners. however, there is an urgent need to develop remote learning options for crisis affected adolescent girls and boys to keep their learning habits, including programs that meet educational needs of children with special needs.

2 - Activities

-Provision of alternative remote learning programs among IDPs( adolescent boys/girls) via radio;-Provision of age, gender and context specific learning materials for children special needs;-Provision of context specific learning materials for learners in the host communities via radio, per-recording messages, posters...-supply of Hygiene promotion kits as well as Covid-19 preventive kits.-provision of water and sanitation in schools for effective prevention and response to COVID-19.- Awareness raising among learners, teachers/ SBMcs on importance of schools reopening- Provision of disinfectant for schools disinfection;-rehabilitation of classes/TLS for school reopening.
