Project Summary

Vulnerable Farmers and other Food Insecure Households in Jabal Al Risan located on lands of Ras Karkar, Kharbatha and Kufr Ni’meh have been crippled after the Israel Defense Forces confiscated 650 dunum of their land. In response to the crisis, Global Communities along with its local partner NGO Shiam – Youth Make the Future, aim to assist this community by helping them rehabilitate their unconfiscated land by open land activities, water storage facilities and fencing.

Needs

The impact of the occupation has far been the main factor in the deteriorating conditions of the Palestinians. Since 1967 Palestinians have been at risk of conflict and violence, displacement, and denial of access to livelihood, among other threats. The most vulnerable Palestinians are currently the ones denied access to their land. The most recent events that took place at Jabal AlRisan where the IDF confiscated 650 dunums of land in Ras Karkar, Kharbatha and Kufur Ni’meh. The land owners were handed confiscation orders as per the Emergency Regulations (Cultivation of Waste [Uncultivated] Lands) Law, where this law authorizes the Israeli Ministry of Agriculture to declare lands as waste lands and take control over uncultivated lands. Ras Karkar is a village located 10.1km West of Ramallah City and its confiscated land is located South of the village. The unemployment rate is 30% where 40% of its employed population is in the Government/Private sector, and 60% working as day labourers or farmers (reference from ARIJ Village profile 2012, and confirmed by the Village council). Kufur Ni’meh is located South of Ras Karkar and is 10.3km West of Ramallah City and its confiscated land is located North of the Village (lands between Ras Karkar and Kufur Ni’meh). Unemployment rate is 16 % where 25% of its employed population is in the Government/Private Sector, 35% service sector, 20% agricultural sector, 15% daily labourers and 5% trade sector (reference from ARIJ Village profile 2012, and confirmed by the Village council). Kharbatha Bani Harith is located West of Ras Karkar and South West of Kufur Ni’meh and is 13.1km West of Ramallah City. Its confiscated land is located South and South East of the village. Unemployment rate is 12 % where 25% of its employed population is in the Government/Private Sector, 35% daily labourers, 20% agricultural sector, 15% trade sector (reference from ARIJ Village profile 2012, and confirmed by the Village council). The intervention aims to protect the farmers affected by the land confiscation and enabling them to cultivate their land surrounding the confiscated area. The project will target the most vulnerable – especially the female headed households. The intervention contributes to the country strategic objective.

Activities

After coordinating with the Ministry of Agriculture and it’s offices for the target area, Global Communities confirmed the needs and concluded that the following activities should take place under the condition that the beneficiaries need to contribute to the project through their labor. The rehabilitation of 40 water storage facilities in the target area that would serve 70 Households. Open land rehabilitation of 3 dunums per household. Fences and retaining walls would be built around the lands to prevent animals from entering it. The intervention activities will include work contribution from the beneficiaries. It is expected and mutually agreed that the beneficiaries will contribute in building retaining walls, installing fences and cleaning light rocks from land and water wells. All coordination in the field will be through the Local NGO SHIAM – Youth Make the Future and the procurement and provision of fencing materials, excavator/bulldozer services through Global Communities. The first activity will be the provision of excavator/bulldozer services for the open land rehabilitation and water well rehabilitation with some light labor contributed from the beneficiaries, each in their own land. The second activity building retaining walls and preparing for fencing of land. Each beneficiary in their own land. The third activity is provision of fencing materials to seal their land from animals and protect it against confiscation. The forth activity is provision of agricultural tools and supplies at discounted prices to the beneficiaries to enable them to start planting and cultivating their land. Gender analysis in the activities:As part of the land rehabilitation, some stones will have to be cleared of the rehabilitated agricultural land, therefore kids (boys and girls) are encouraged to participate in this activity. The heavy lifting will be done by the machinery, only for the building of retaining walls some women and kids (boys and girls) can assist but not solely responsible to contribute. On the other hand, women and kids (boys and girls) are encouraged to participate in the planting activity part of the provision of agricultural tools and supplies. It is also socially acceptable and highly relied on that the women and children participate in planting and harvesting of plants. The forth activity is provision of agricultural tools and supplies at discounted prices to the beneficiaries to enable them to start planting and cultivating their land.

Indicators

Global Communities in coordination with SHIAM – Youth Make the Future conducted field visits to the target area and collected data from the local village councils that consist of beneficiary lists and needs. The suggested households by the village councils will be visited and baseline survey will be filled. The beneficiary lists will be shared with the Ministry of Agriculture and Ministry of Social Development to illuminate assistance duplication and ensure that all the project activates are in line with the Ministry’s plans. Ongoing monitoring will help Global Communities and SHIAM – Youth Make the Future to evaluate the activities, methodologies, and impact and to recognize challenges and best practices in coordination with the Ministry of Agriculture. More focus will be on methods of working with the beneficiaries, gender approach, in addition to the relevance of the intervention. Global Communities will create a WhatsApp group for its partner SHIAM – Youth Make the Future and beneficiaries to encourage transparency and ensure constant communication and accountability. The same WhatsApp number will serve as a hotline for any complaints that the beneficiaries or others might have. If by any chance the head of the household is not able to communicate through Whatsapp, then we will insure that the other members of the family (younger generation or children) will contribute on their behalf, thus having the whole household participate. The project expects the following outputs:Output 1: Rehabilitation of 200 dunums of land for 70 HouseholdOutput 2: Rehabilitation of 40 water storage facilitiesOutput 3: 1000m³ of retaining walls and fences Output 4: Agricultural assets distributed to farmers The project expects the following indicators:350 direct beneficiaries (disaggregated by gender) will gain access to their agricultural land505 Dunums will be protected in Kharbatha Village*282 Dunums will be protected in Ras Karkar*312 Dunums will be protected in Kufr Ni’meh* *The protected area is larger than the rehabilitated area due to the fact that when an area closer to the confiscated area is rehabilitated its surrounding areas.
