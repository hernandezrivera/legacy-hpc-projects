Project Summary

Needs

* A significant number of children and youth are not accessing any education, including over 39% of children 3-14 years and 97% of adolescents and youth aged 15-24 years. * Over 2,000 learning facilities are needed to meet the existing needs of pre-primary and primary aged children 3-14. Over 26 camps (out of 34) have less than half of the learning facilities required to cover out-of-school children. * Learning facilities have suffered from a lack of standardized and relevant teaching and learning materials, due to the sensitivities associated with Rohingya refugees having access to the national formal and non-formal curriculum. * 40% of parents of adolescent girls and 33% of parents of adolescent boys reported that education is not appropriate for their children, revealing a gap in community sensitization on education and child and youth rights. * Cox’s Bazar net intake rate for the first grade of primary school is the lowest in the country at 72.6% for boys and 69.1% for girls, compared to the national average of 98%. Similarly, the dropout rate in primary education is the highest in the country—39.6% for boys and 22.8% for girls, compared to the national average of 22.3% and 16.1% respectively. * Host community school infrastructure is under prepared for environmental risks including monsoons and cyclones.

Activities or outputs

Objective 1.* Provision of basic education, including early learning to Rohingya and host community children (4-14 years boys and girls)* Provision of non-formal education, life-skills and livelihoods, including technical and vocational training to Rohingya and host community children (15-18 years boys and girls)* Establishment, equipment and/or maintenance of learning spaces for refugee setting* Rehabilitation, equipment and/or maintenance of classrooms in host community schools* Capacity development of local authority (district and Divisional levels) to support educational planning, supervision and monitoringObjective 2.* Provision of teaching material to host community teachers and Burmese language instructors from the refugee community* Provision of supplies and/or learning material to Rohingya and host community children, adolescents and youth (4-18 years boys and girls)* Train teachers to delivery of the Learning Competency Framework and Approach (LCFA) at respective levels and support in the use of materials.* Develop, print, and distribute LCFA levels 1-5 core teaching and learning materials to deliver skills and education in the camps.* Strengthen planning and information management systems in the local education system of host community. Objective 3.* Strengthen participatory mechanisms within schools and learning centres to increase enrolment and prevent drop-out and improve practices at community level including awareness on children with disabilities, child protection, girls' education, and social cohesion.* Provision of parental education to support parents and families to be effective caregivers.

Key project/performance indicators

276,696 of children and youth benefiting from learner-friendly environments, including life skills and resilience programs in refugee and host communities (dis-aggregated by age, gender).3,914 of safe and equipped learning facilities with gender segregated WASH facilities in refugee camps.6,823 of teachers/learning facilitators from the host and refugee community who participate in basic/foundational training.341,071 of learners receiving adequate education materials, supplies and equipment aligned with Education Sector standards (in refugee and host community).2,200 of School and Community Education Committees (CECs) trained on learning facility/school management, Disaster Risk Reduction, and the participatory engagement of community (women, men, girls, boys) in the learning, development and well-being of children and youth (with 50% female participation).
