Project Summary

**Mercy-USA for Aid and Development in this project will support internally displaced persons (IDPs)
in Maaret Tamsrin and Idleb camps in Establishing fire units (points) and fire warden committees and trainings for fire prevention and response in the targeted camps.
40 fire units/points will be established and 40 fire warden committees will be established and trained in the targeted camps to maintain safety of IDPs and feeling secure without fear of fire hazards might hits in any moment especially during winter season.
Also, Provision of required materials/equipment to all fire points:
The items and quantities will be aligned with Fire Taskforce:
1-Anti-electrostatic gloves 5
2-Powder extinguishers (6 kg) 15
3-Shovel 5
4-Metal stick 5
5-Scissors for Iron cutting 5
6-Water transport container (20 liter capacity) 10
7-Ax 2
8-Fire blankets (1.3*1.5 meter dimensions) 10
9-Light 6
10-First aid kit 2
Each committee will receive two days Fire warden Training.
Each training will have 15-20 participants to maintain social distancing, so total of 20 training will be conducted

These activities will be implemented before the winter seasons as proactive preparation for the winter season which caused increase fire incidents due to improper use of the stoves and fuel.
Also, Mercy-USA has plan for winterization project in the same camps, moreover, we have monthly food assistance project which gives Mercy-USA advantage to follow up the training impact on regular basis and provide advices whenever needed, so this will reflect complementarity and integration approach for strong sustainability.
Prioritization of activities in case of underfunding will be:
1-Conduct one training and cancel the refresher training
2-Provision of fire extinguishers and first aid kits is top priority
3- Reduce place for the fire points to two instead of three in each site
4-Reduce staff cost
So the prioritizing will be scale down activities rather than remove any activity

3 - Needs

According to Mobility and Needs Monitoring report in October 2022 which was launched by HNAP:Total population in NS Syria is 4,59 million people from them 2.8 million IDPs Based on IDP Sites Integrated Monitoring Matrix (ISIMM): As of October 2022, the CCCM database includes 1,430 IDP sites that host 1,859,651 IDPs or 354,655 families. Of these, 80% are women and children, and 74,983 IDPs are reported to be persons with disabilities. Last year, 30 per cent of IDP sites in north-west Syria flooded, affecting over 540,000 people . Self-settled sites are the most vulnerable to extreme weather conditions, due to lack of site planning, infrastructure, and camp management systems.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Sexual Exploitation and Abuse due to fire incidents and need to help Mitigation: -Training for all staff about PSEA -Have three places in each site for fire point to cover all site land and to avoid any crowds during fire incident Monitoring: 1-Mercy-USA will have complaints and feedback mechanism in place and the SEA complaints will be treated with high confidentiality and referral system will be in place for such cases to PSEA focal in the area. 2-esnure existence of trained people in all the fire points places3-All staff and vendors will be trained, acknowledge, and sign the Mercy-USA PSEA policy documents as relevant to ensure full adherence with the Do No Harm principle and to stress that SEA and GBV issues will be dealt with firmly and expeditiously.4-All recipients will be provided with information sessions about the scope of the project and services available including possibility of a live demo.5-Fire points will be established to be accessible for all IDPs in each respective camp


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: Individual residents may over-rely on any civil response/DRR committee for responding to a natural disaster and may shirk individual responsibility Likelihood: Medium Impact: Medium Mitigating Measure: 1.) Any dedicated DRR response (such as a training of first responders) incorporates general awareness raising focused on the individuals capacities of men, women, and children 2) Ensure that training indicatives include sessions on DNH and protection mainstreaming Risk 2: Big fire above the fire committee and equipment capacity which may lead to huge damage in tents. Mitigating Measure: 1-Mercy-USA will provide fire equipment to be aligned with fire taskforce based on the number of tents and IDPs in each site to mange any fire in any area inside the camp 2-Mercy-USA will try to disseminate the knowledge which received by the participants in training through some experiments inside the sites to ensure other IDPs get this knowledge. 3-Mercy-USA will have dedicated staff in each camp to support in case of big fire incidents 4- We will have communication and referral plan in place to request support for the nearest civil defense center as well as refer any inquires to the nearest heath facility Risk 3: Lockdown due to Spread of COVID-19 pandemic in NW Syria Likelihood: medium, Impact: highMitigation: Mercy-USA will apply precautionary measures which are in the CCCM guidance about COVID-19 for staff and contractors by use PPE, and maintain physical distance for safety
