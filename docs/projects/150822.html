Project Summary

This project is to enhance the protection and address life-saving needs of IDP families through Shelter/NFIs response in Banadir, Lower Shabelle (Afgoye Corridor) and Bay regions in South and Central Somalia targeting to the newly and protracted displaced IDP families of those affected by natural hazards. It is designed to mitigate the risk and its consequences on the affected IDP families in the targeting areas. The main objective is that to improve the living conditions and safe access to services of IDP families including refugee returns in the targeted locations. HINNA aims at providing 5000 NFI kits to 5000 (3000 female and 2000 male) beneficiaries through in kind distribution and cash to improve their living conditions of newly and protracted internally displaced persons those affected by natural hazards.
Direct beneficiaries of the project totaled 5000 including men, women, boys and girls who will automatically be receiving any benefits of the project through selection criterion taking into account gender mainstreaming. 3000 of them will receive 3000 NFI kits through in kind distribution, while 2000 remaining will receive 2000 NFIs through restricted cash voucher.

Needs

Over two decades of armed conflict and severe recurring droughts and floods have forced a remarkable part of the Somali population to leave their homes. On IOM monthly update reports March 2018, continues internal displacements become major challenges in Somalia as such the humanitarian needs remain at critical levels. As Somali people faced a fifth consecutive below average seasons this accelerated the needs for humanitarian assistance. Based on OCHA report of revised humanitarian response plan 2018 needs are high among IDPs and rural populations and the existence of overwhelming humanitarian impact in most regions of the country, especially south central Somalia including Banadir. Further, stated that an estimated 4.6 million Somalis are in need of urgent life-saving of humanitarian assistance including IDPs. This is exacerbating humanitarian needs and human rights violations across Somalia particularly in IDP settlements and new displacements on the outskirts of Mogadishu (SHF-2018). On the revised HRP report, number of evictions has been registered in Somalia in 2018. Around 171,000 people, the vast majority of them internally displaced persons (IDPs), were evicted in the first five months. Further, many of the IDPs have been displaced several times further degraded and vulnerability increased. As a result, HINNA will directly target 5000 of newly and protracted IDPs/returnees affected by natural hazards (3000 female and 2000 male) in Banadir, Lower Shabelle (Afgoye Corridor) and Baidoa. 3000 of them will receive 3000 NFI kits through in kind distribution, while 2000 remaining will receive 2000 NFIs through restricted cash voucher.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age
