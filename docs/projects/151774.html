Needs

Somalia’s health system has significantly underperformed for many years because of the longstanding conflict and instability. The health sector was further overburdened in 2017 due to the impacts of the drought and worsening humanitarian crisis. This has increased a burden to a population with an already poor health, leading to increased levels of malnutrition across the country, a major AWD/cholera outbreak with over 78,000 cases reported, and a measles epidemic affecting all regions with a total of nearly 19,000 cases reported. Drought and conflict can be identified as the major underlying factors causing major population displacement during 2017, and thus, increased health needs across the country The health risks remain from malnutrition, diseases such as measles and possible resurgence of AWD/cholera outbreak following the Deyr 2017 rains, poor hygiene and sanitation, through to the end of 2017 and early 2018. This is beyond excess avoidable morbidity and mortality occurring as a consequence of major gaps in health service availability, accessibility and utilization. The lack of comprehensive and reliable epidemiological and disease information from all regions has made it very difficult to ascertain the extent of gaps in service provision and burden of disease across the country. This has made prioritization and strategic planning a challenge for the medium-long term. 2016 reports on core indicators for Somalia’s health situation and health system performance43 show that even prior to the onset of the 2016/2017 drought, young children and women of child bearing age disproportionally bear the brunt of the protracted health crisis across Somalia. Somali Vulnerable Actors (SOVA) organization with limited financial Support from SCWW Somali Diaspora organization in USA and JMOH/UNICEF drug supplies and SOVA wants to address health needs of Bardera Kurman Community MCHN and 12 hard to villages where most need and vulnerable people IDPs and host communities lives.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Beneficiaries are excluded clans,status and culture,marginalized communities are some times looked as different from the rest of the populations,this is negative cultural attitude,therefore SOVA will avoid any exclusion act based on gender and age,community educating and law enforcement and enhancing organization policies and taking action against any staff involved exclusion will be officially accounted.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

SOVA collect and use data disaggregated by sex, age and diversity to effectively inform programming and will ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable and will dentify and prioritize the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalised groups Vulnerable persons are exposed to a combination of risks due to their vulnerability and typically have less capacity to withstand the impact of a crisis. Vulnerable persons therefore experience a cycle of vulnerability and poverty, which limits their access to assistance and services and exposes them to protection concerns, such as isolation, exploitation and abuse. Prioritising the most vulnerable groups in humanitarian programming reinforces the protection element of providing assistance based on need and without discrimination and ensures vulnerable persons are not neglected or deprived from humanitarian assistance SOVA will ensure lack of documentation does not exclude individuals from accessing services

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

SOVA will ensure the location and access routes to health facilities and services are safe and will identify central locations and avoid its health beneficiaries areas near military installations or threats such as attacks, stray bullets, crossfire or violence and have a proportionate number of female health staff, female doctors/nurses will be available, in this case, women will be adequately informed of which days a female doctor will be available in order to utilize.
