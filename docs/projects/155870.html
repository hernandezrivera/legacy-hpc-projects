Project Summary

Although return in Orkazai tribal district of Khyber Pakhtunkhuwa is still ongoing and remaining is planned after winter season. Overall situation in return areas is not good in terms of humanitarian needs including health, WASH, FSL and nutrition. Returned communities are facing acute problems of health, nutrition, WASH and livelihood. This is also triggering the secondary displacement in the area. The Food Security and Livelihood Assessment conducted in February/March 2017 which revealed that 64% of households had acceptable food consumption, 29% had borderline consumption and remaining 6% had poor consumption. The report showed that Orakazai had the worst food consumption situation, where 34% of the surveyed households had poor and 31% had borderline consumption. Returned families are using a variety of coping strategies when they have problems meeting their food needs including selling household goods or assets to buy food, selling productive assets or means of transport (such as a sewing machine or tractor), or consuming seed stock held for the next planting season.
The proposed project will support returned families in Orkazai tribal district regarding their restoration of their livelihood activities and initiating income generation activities. The project interventions will target acute vulnerable returned communities including women headed families, Orphans, widows, older women and men and persons with disabilities. The overall objective of the project intervention will provide fully support in reduction of food insecurity and negative coping mechanism among returnees of Orkazai tribal district. We'll provide livelihood enhanced skills training to 800 vulnerable families, provide them livelihood tool kits and on job support for running a successful livelihood. The project will target 800 families in Orkazai tribal district for FSL activities for a 9 month duration. The project activities will start from March 2019 and will completed in December 2019.

Needs

A protracted crisis since 2008 saw millions of individuals displaced. The situation worsened with the emergence of militancy on both of Afghan Pakistan border. By April 2015, 5.2 million people had been displaced from former FATA region, as reported by the FATA Disaster Management Authority. However, with increasing improvement in security in former FATA region in 2015, large scale returns of Temporary Displaced Persons to their areas of origin commenced. By the end of October 2017, total 438,657 families returned to their places of origins out of 471,126 families. (TDP Fact Sheet October 2017 UNHCR). 32,469 families are currently remained displaces and are yet to return excluding unregistered TDPs locating in Peshawar and Bannu. "PRELIMINARY FINDINGS FATA VULNERABILITY ASSESSMENT 2017" by Vulnerability Assessment Group UNOCHA with support of humanitarian organizations in former FATA region, Govt. of Pakistan and World Bank in Aug Sep 2017 revealed that 36% of the households faced severe or moderate food insecurity. Analysis by agency shows that Orakzai had the worst food consumption situation, where 34 per cent of the surveyed households had poor and 31 per cent had borderline consumption. The situation was better in Khyber, Kurram and South Waziristan, where no household were in the poor category and 13%, 17% and 19%, respectively, had borderline consumption. Overall, 54 %t of the households reportedly used at least one livelihood based coping strategy to meet their food needs. The proportion of households adopting stress, crisis and emergency coping strategies were 38 %, 5 % and 11 %, respectively. The highest proportion of families resorting to livelihood based coping strategies was found in Orakzai (87 per cent), while it was the lowest in Khyber (11 per cent). The severity of the coping reflected in the rate of crisis and emergency strategies was also highest in Orakzai, Kurram as well as Mohmand where 26 28 per cent of the households adopted some of these resource depleting strategies. Overall 36% households are estimated to have moderate and severe food insecurity in FATA agencies/FR regions, whereas 16 per cent have severe food insecurity. As per the vulnerability assessment report the identified need related to food security and livelihood in returned areas of Orkazai tribal district are: 1.Conditional food assistance, either through food or cash, for asset creation activities remain important. 2. Following types of assistance are recommended: • Immediate assistance for returnees in Orakzai and North Waziristan Agencies to reduce high food insecurity and protect livelihoods.• Medium-term livelihood recovery activities for returnees • Long-term development programmes to address chronic causes of food insecurity are more appropriate for returnees 3. Consider and design livelihood support programmes to engage more women that are culturally appropriate and accepted by the community.4. Trainings on importance of diet diversity and food safety.The project will address following needs of the communities in returned areas:1- Medium term livelihood restoration and recovery activities2- Training on enhanced livelihood skills and initiating income generation activities. The training will include importance of diet diversity and food safety3- Provision of livelihood tool kit to restore/initiate income generation activities. Women will be focused for project activities as per local context and cultural perspectives.
