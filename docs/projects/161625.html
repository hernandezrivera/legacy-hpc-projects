Project Summary

The proposed one (1) year project aims at provision of emergency CCCM assistance to conflict affected IDPs, living in formal camps and informal settlements in Borno State through dedicated static & mobile CCCM teams. Almost half of IDPs Population lives in camps or camp like settings. Despite this, the resources for CCCM are currently inadequate, leading to living standards in camps which are below
SPHERE standards, lack of coordination on camp level and lack of an existing referral system for beneficiaries to accommodate especially the most vulnerable protection cases.
The proposed project is part of SHI’s wider programming in the target area. SHI currently implements a CCCM project in MMC at Cheazcoan, Shuwari5, Doron Baga and Umara Bolori.
The proposed project will strengthen camp coordination mechanisms and site facilitation in Borno state through the activation of CCCM activities which will provide a clear referral pathway for beneficiaries in order to ensure that sensitive cases are being referred to the correct partner in a swift and respectable manner, it will improve the camp infrastructure through urgently needed site upgrades and the establishment of camp committees; and lastly it will build capacity of communities and local authorities on CCCM. SHI will deploy dedicated camp management teams to the proposed locations. SHI is proposing to implement this project in Maiduguri, Konduga, Kala Balge LGAs targeting together 30,000 beneficiaries. The locations have been identified in close consultation with other CCCM/shelter/NFI/DMS sector and complement SHI’s ongoing operations as described above.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Series of camps-camp like setting/host communities are housing displaced population as a result of increase in the trend of displacement as the long insurgent in the northeast continues to evolve. with DTM figures of above 50,000 individual in Rann (Kala/Balge) displaced/cut off, with little or no access to humanitarian assistance, and over 30,000 influx into MMC, Konduga and Jere as a result military operation to retake the affected LGAs affected and also chronic poor living condition which is rated below sphere standard. SHI will Establish and construct Reception Center in Rann, for proper screening and immediate response to need and gaps identified and create a good referral pathways for managing immediate protection cases and needs. Have a dedicated CCCM teams in all the LGAs for adequate site facilitation and site maintenance with effective coordination.

2 - Activities

Activity 1.0 Reception centere in Ran: SHI will ensure adequate reception Centere management is in place and comprehensive first contact response to new arrivals is done, in close collabration with IOM DTM SHI will assist in the registration of new arrivals during and after biometric registration/verification and also take vulnerability assesment and effective referrals for further humanitarian assistance as available ti needs.activity 1.1 SHI will directly and in close consultation Stakeholders (NEMA/SEMA, IOM) , IDPs leaders take gap analysis and need assesment to identify critical/urgent needs and share results with Sector.Activity 1.2 SHI will establish effective coordination and prompt information sharing mechamism: this is achiveable by setting up of coordination mechanism, and maximun usage of CCCM Sector formal camp monitoring tool and monthly reporting to the sector; also establish bi-monthly camp coordination meeting. With once in a month meeting of different camp committee established, this is to give the community a lead in all intervention.Activity 1.3 Camp improvement, SHI will ensure the improvement and upgrade of site after site assesment in consultation with NEMA/SEMA, IOM and Community leaders, priorities will be placed base on urgent/critical needs identified, the upgrade will basically to reduce enviromental risk such as flooding mitigation, fire mitigation and other associated environmental risks.Activity 1.4 Establishment of Complain Feedback Mechanism (CFM): SHI will maintain a good/confidential CFM to enable beneficiaries make inquiry about interventions, be a guide to planning and make necessary complain and get response from partners involve, this will includes the creation of Complain desk, suggestion box, resolves issues desk and unresolved issues desk for proper accountability to partners.
