Project Summary

To contribute to enhancing the life-saving livelihoods of affected households by increasing agricultural and livestock production, protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives, SCA is linking between the SO1 and SO2 referring the vulnerable cases who benefited from the activities under SO1 to be included in activities under SO2 in the targeted locations to improve the livelihoods of those families and support their resilience targeting 800 direct Ben. 4800 INDV (1650 F/1200 M, 1950 children <18)
The project will provide Vocational training and CFW to enable them to restoring their livelihood critically needed life-saving, including:
1. Creating income-generating activities.
2. Building capacities of youth and women through Vocational training and enabling them to start their own businesses.
Transparency of shortlisting of Ben's will be achieved by establishing a committee in each Camp consist of one member from camp management, one community leader and the team of SCA. This mechanism ensures accountability and promotes trust between SCA and beneficiaries, SCA, will pay particular attention to this role and to ensure a high standard of recruitment.
SCA's MEAL plan will be done by regularly monitoring the implemented activities and evaluating the objective achievements, to ensure that the project meets the required quality and time frame, to tackle challenges faced and summarize lessons learned.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021 The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 per cent in the last year.• The agriculture sector, which was once a cornerstone of the Syrian economy, has been severely impacted, with half (50 per cent) of national grain requirements met through imports.• According to Food and Agricultural Organization (FAO), high cost and limited availability of quality crop and livestock production inputs, damaged irrigation infrastructure across the country, degradation of livelihoods and income earning opportunities, coupled with the direct and knock-on effects of Covid-19 and climate-induced shocks continue to drive more people into food insecurity. To have a factual and tangible gap analysis for the proposed project areas, SCA has conducted a needs assessment covered all the project areas overstretched capacities, unemployment or displacement. Limited livelihood opportunities lead to food insecurity due to limited income, especially for women. Thus, vocational training can support women who are in need of sustaining their families such as opportunities/ knowledge/ skills to create income.Due to the crisis, the people in need in camps be face higher protection risks specially women and children as well as increased negative coping mechanisms of the communities such as crime, child labor or early marriage. therefore, there is a need to tailor activities to their needs and providing childcare to enable their participation. Further, PWDs are in need of opportunities that are accessible and create independence.
