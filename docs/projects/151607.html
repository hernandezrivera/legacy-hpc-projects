Project Summary

Due to the combined effects of widespread and severe flooding, Desert Locust infestation, socioeconomic impacts of COVID-19 and the cumulative impacts of previous shocks, up to 2.1 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse outcomes through December 2020 in the absence of humanitarian assistance. In addition, 849 900 children under the age of five are likely to be acutely malnourished through August 2021. Sustained and large-scale humanitarian assistance and government support are currently preventing more severe outcomes in many areas. Desert Locust continues to pose a serious risk of damage to both pasture and crops at least until the end of 2020, Marginal decrease in the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) of 13.8% up to 14% The FSNAU report indicated a critical GAM (14%) and serious SAM (2.8%) prevalence were recorded in Gedo region while health services in Elwak and Garbaharey districts are facing stock out of emergency medical supplies.The proposed project will be implemented in Gedo region particular Elwak and Garbaharey districts where ARD is implementing health and WASH. ARD implemented health project in Elwak district in partnership with UNFPA, SHF and MOH since 2016 . the heath facilities have been functional through community contribution and ARD in kind of drugs and staff incentives. This has saved many vulnerable children and women affected by drought/AWD at target district. ARD will target a total 30,000 drought-affected people of whom 60% are women/girls with equity access to girls, women, boys and men through one health center and two mobile clinics. ARD will provide free outpatient consultations to 30,000 drought/AWD affected communities including 9000 girls, 9000 boys, 7500 women and 4500 men. Provision of emergency obstetric care including ANC, skilled delivery and PNC to 3000 eligible women and improve referral system through provision of transportation. The organization will conduct routine and supplementary immunization services to 20% of target population approximately 6000 children under the age of ﬁve years. In addition, the capacity of 35 staff will be improved through training while 350 community members will also be increased their knowledge and skills on disaster risk reduction, AWD prevention, identiﬁcation of cases and early referral to health facilities. The organization is active health cluster member in Gedo and Mogadishu and will actively share information with health cluster members. The project will also be integrated into ongoing WASH project as integrated package of intervention. The target district is accessible, and the organization has active PCA with UNFPA in reducing maternal mortality rate through skilled deliveries and strengthening referral system in Somalia. There are reporting system that links to the EWAR system within the national surveillance system. ARD will report weekly/monthly IDSR /HMIS report for earlier detection of acute public health events, earlier and more effective response and reduce impact on health.
The project will target 15% of persons living with disabilities and ensuring that they are involved in the project design implementation and monitoring. people with disability will also part of project implementation team/committee. the project team also work with the organization working on disability such as Humanity inclusion to train the project staff on disability as well as providing technical relevant support.

Project Activities

1. Provide free outpatient consultations to 30,000 drought/AWD affected people both IDPs and host communities ( including 7500 women, 9000 girls, 9000 boys and 4500 men) through support of one ﬁxed health facility and two mobile clinics in Elwak district . The services to be offered include to treatment of pneumonia, malaria, diarrhea, measles, skin conditions, injury care in children and urinary tract infections, sexually transmitted infections. 2. Provision of emergency obstetric care services including ANC, skilled delivery and PNC to 3,000 pregnant and lactating women women in Elwak and Garbaharey districts in Gedo region. In addition, community health worker will Identify, refer complicated deliveries to the health facility. ARD will provide standby transportation from IDPs to health facilities/hospitals and vice-versa in the effort to reduce maternal and child mortality rate in Somalia. 3. Conduct routine and supplementary immunization to children under the age of ﬁve years in the target population. This is approximately 6000(3000boys and 3000girls) and estimated 3000 women of child bearing age in Elwak and Garabahrey districts. ARD will ensure coverage of 80-90%. There are reporting system that links to the EWAR system within the national surveillance system. ARD will report weekly/monthly IDSR /HMIS report for earlier detection of acute public health events, earlier and more effective response and reduce impact on health. 4. Identify women focal points in the IDP settlements to lead the sensitization campaigns on the safe delivery and safe motherhood issues. These women will organize 10 community awareness and education sessions on available SRH services including family planning targeting 200 adolescent girls, 100 women and 50 men at target location. this will be conducted by mobile clinic.. ARD will also develop advocacy materials on safe mother hood. 5. Provide training for 20 staff (10 male, 10 female) on Basic Emergency Obstetric Maternal and Neonatal Care for a period of 5 days 6. Conduct refresher training for 15 community health workers on prevention and response of communicable diseases ,disease surveillance with emphasis on AWD, malaria, maternal health, child and maternal malnutrition.The project will target 15% of persons living with disabilities and ensuring that they are involved in the project design implementation and monitoring. people with disability will also part of project implementation team/committee. the project team also work with the organization working on disability such as Humanity inclusion to train the project staff on disability as well as providing technical relevant support.We will include to disability specific indicators and disaggregated data in our monitoring framework to monitor service provision to persons with disability (PWDs) since 15 per cent our target beneficiaries are PWDs. We will use WASHINGTON Questionnaire to capture data disaggregated by disability and train our M&E focal points and staffs on how to collect, analyse and report on disability.
