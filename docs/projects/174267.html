Project Summary

PADO is proposing WASH assistance for the 35,000 flood-affected population of District Swat to cope up with urgent WASH needs in communities, schools, and health centers. Proposed sites are finalized based on identified needs through a rapid assessment conducted by PADO from 9 to 15 September 2020 and shared with the WASH working group KP. The technical survey conducted by PADO further guided the planning of culturally sensitive and locally relevant activities for the proposed areas while the proposed activities are in line with the WASH sector strategy for Pakistan 2021 Humanitarian Response Plan.
The proposed WASH interventions will contribute to the WASH sectoral objective 1 and will focus on life-saving and dignified WASH support through the provision of water supply and sanitation facilities along with hygiene promotion and COVID-19 awareness in communities, schools, and health facilities. The key activities will include Repair/Rehabilitation of gravity water supply systems. Installation of user-friendly latrines and promotion of critical WASH-related information in communities and institutions.
To ensure the longer-term sustainability of the proposed interventions, the key focus will be to follow the people-centered approach by the formation and training of WASH committees in communities and health centers, and SMCs in schools. The committees will be trained on the Operation and Maintenance (O&M) of the provided facilities. The proposed activities will also focus on hygiene education in communities and schools along with solid waste management awareness. Effective hygiene promotion campaigns within communities, schools, and health facilities will be key elements of the proposed project activities. For managing drinking-water quality from catchment to beneficiaries, PADO will develop and implement water safety plans and approaches. While supporting construction activities, environmental considerations will be given the highest priority to protect the external atmosphere from contamination and health hazards. PADO will ensure compliance with GAM - Gender Marker 4(M), CHS, and SPHERE standards.
