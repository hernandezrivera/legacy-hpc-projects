Project Summary

The Joyful Learning for Rohingya Refugee & Host Community Children (JLRRHCC) project is a comprehensive education and psychosocial support program designed to improve the learning outcomes and well-being of Rohingya refugee and host community children in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh. The project will be implemented by JSUS and supposed to funded by Embassy of Japan .
Target : Direct Beneficiaries : 848 (Boys-241 ,Girls-607) , Location : Camp 7 from Guest Community and Haldia Palong from Host community ,Ukhiya Upazilla.
Objectives :
The JLRRHCC project aims to achieve the following objectives:
• Increase access to quality education for Rohingya refugee and host community children
• Improve the learning outcomes of Rohingya refugee and host community children
• Provide psychosocial support to Rohingya refugee and host community children to help them cope with the trauma of displacement.
• Strengthen the capacity of teachers and parents to support the learning and well-being of Rohingya refugee and host community children
Activities :The JLRRHCC project will implement a range of activities to achieve its objectives, including:
• Expanding access to formal (Early Childcare and Development & Primary ) and non-formal education for Rohingya refugee and host community children
• Improving the quality of education through teacher training, curriculum development, and the provision of learning materials
• Providing psychosocial support to Rohingya refugee and host community children through individual and group counseling, play therapy, and life skills training.
• Strengthening the capacity of teachers and parents to support the learning and well-being of Rohingya refugee and host community children through training and workshops.
Expected Outcomes :The JLRRHCC project is expected to achieve the following outcomes:
• Increased enrollment and attendance rates for Rohingya refugee and host community children
• Improved learning outcomes for Rohingya refugee and host community children in numeracy, literacy, and other core subjects
• Reduced levels of stress, anxiety, and depression among Rohingya refugee and host community children
• Improved relationships between Rohingya refugee and host community children
Expected Impact : The JLRRHCC project is expected to have a significant positive impact on the lives of Rohingya refugee and host community children in Cox's Bazar. The project will help children to learn, grow, and develop in a safe and supportive environment, and it will help them to develop the skills and resilience they need to build a better future for themselves and their communities.
Sustainability :The JLRRHCC project is designed to be sustainable in the long term. The project will work closely with the Government of Bangladesh, Education Sector and other stakeholders to develop and implement sustainable education and psychosocial support programs for Rohingya refugee and host community children.
Conclusion : The JLRRHCC project is a critical intervention that will help to improve the lives of Rohingya refugee and host community children in Cox's Bazar. The project is a testament to the commitment of the international community to providing quality education and psychosocial support to children in need.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

"No .The Jouful Learning for Rohingya Refugee & Host Community Children (JLRRHCC) Project does not have any direct Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) or Disaster Risk Management (DRM) elements. However, the project does indirectly address some of the underlying factors that contribute to disaster risk, such as poverty, lack of education, and lack of access to basic services. By providing education and psychosocial support to Rohingya refugee and host community children, the project is helping to build resilience and reduce the vulnerability of these children to disasters.The project also has some indirect environmental benefits. By providing education and psychosocial support to children, the project is helping to promote sustainable development and reduce the pressure on natural resources. Additionally, the project is working with schools to implement environmentally friendly practices, such as waste reduction and recycling.Finally, the project is taking steps to mitigate the effects of climate change on children. For example, the project is working to build schools that are resilient to climate change, such as by using flood-resistant materials and designs. Additionally, the project is providing children with education on climate change and how to adapt to its effects.Overall, the JLRRHCC Project is making a positive contribution to DRR, DRM, and environmental sustainability in Cox's Bazar. The project is helping to build resilience, reduce vulnerability, and promote sustainable development."
