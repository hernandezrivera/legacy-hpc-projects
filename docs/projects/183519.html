Project Summary

In line with Sudan’s S/NFI Cluster Strategy 2021-2022, the Action aims to cover S/NFI gaps among vulnerable community members, IDPs and Returnees, with a particular focus on vulnerable groups and people with special needs (PSNs), in Bau, Geissan, Wad Al Mahi and Rusayris locality of Blue Nile.
The activities will include distribution of in-kind NFI and Emergency Shelter kits, cash/voucher assistance for shelter, and provision of shelter construction material.
The proposed intervention will be aligned to the S/NFI’s cluster objectives:
1. Provide lifesaving NFI assistance to crisis affected populations
2. Enable crisis affected populations to access adequate shelter solutions that provide protection, safety, security, and space to live in a dignified manner

1 - Needs

According to the latest S/NFI figures, over 68,000 individuals are in need of S/NFI assistance on a severity scale of 3 and 4 in the localities of Bau, Geissan, Wad Al Mahi and Rusayris. This figure includes returnees, IDPs and vulnerable households across the four localities. While the majority of IDPs is concentrated in Bau, Geissan and Rusayris; returnees in need of assistance are located mainly in Geissan and Bau. Further return movements from South Sudan could continue, boosted by the signed Peace Agreement as well as by the funding challenges affecting the service provision in South Sudan refugee camps. Sudan accounts for a high prevalence of emergency/ makeshift shelter typologies and overall poor shelter conditions, whereby for example 68% of affected households report living in damaged shelters.IDPs and returnees, among whom especially female/child/elderly headed households as well as households with disable members, are highly vulnerable without shelter to protect them from health and protection risks. This action aims to fill the gap for both immediate emergency S/NFI needs as well as more durable solutions; to ensure affected households’ minimum living standards, dignity, and to contribute to their physical and mental wellbeing.Beneficiaries will be selected by conducting household level vulnerability surveys based on their vulnerability and the damage level of their shelter. The criteria that will be used for selection include but are not limited to 1) Household status and households living in a spontaneous emergency site; 2) Shelter typologies and conditions (for shelter assistance) / access to essential household items (for NFI assistance); 3) Female, child and elderly headed households, households with chronically ill or disabled family members; 4) Source of income; 5) ratio of dependent to employable members. The technical assessment will take into account the structural integrity, environmental protection, and available living spaces.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The Program envisages the specific needs faced by different socio-demographic groups direct and encourages gender-balanced participation of target communities– with a zero-tolerance policy towards discrimination– throughout the Action, which is embedding the “Do No Harm Principle” and follows a conflict-sensitive approach. Preparation Phase:- Before each intervention, ACTED conducts an assessment of the security context and protection risks, followed by a regular monitoring of the situation throughout the project’s lifespan.- The whole community will be sensitised on the scope and the activities of the project and fully informed on the selection criteria, which have been designed to address the protection risks and cover the sectoral needs of the most vulnerable (single women, PLWS, children, elders, PwDs, HHs with no source of income, HHs with PwDs, HHs with more children under 5 years old). Program Implementation Phase:- ACTED mainstreams gender-adapted and key protection messages during its community sensitization and trainings. - First aid kits and protective wear are also provided to ACTED’s staff, beneficiaries and daily workers, in line with the type of activities. Accountability:- ACTED has an independent Appraisal, Monitoring and Evaluation Unit (AMEU) with experience in setting up Complaints and Response Mechanisms (CRMs). A dedicated staff will be responsible to manage the CRM at Area office and Field office levels, where beneficiaries can report complaints/feedback in a safe and confidential way. Beside its staff permanently deployed in the field, ACTED will also conduct regular spot checks. ACTED will evaluate if the complaint was triggered due to flaws, shortcomings or gaps in procedures or implementation strategy. In addition to case-by-case handling, anonymized complaints reports will be shared internally to possibly reshape the ongoing Action and inform the future projects’ design. A more detailed description of the CRM is provided under the AAP section of this proposalHuman Resources - ACTED has a Code of Conduct that is consistent with the U.N. Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Task Force on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse in Humanitarian Crises. ACTED also complies with The Code of Conduct for The International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement and the UNHCR Code of Conduct. - Finally, ACTED will set-up a gender-balanced Program team to ensure a safer environment and inclusion mechanisms.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

As part of its accountability strategy, ACTED will mainstream protection and PSEA elements during all activities, particularly trough the safety audit tools, the CRM and the inclusion of PSEA training modules into capacity development action plans for community governance structures. ACTED has appropriate and proportionate procedures and controls in place to prevent, report, detect, respond and report on safeguarding issues & allegations. Recruitment- ACTED prevents employment/deployment of unsuitable individuals through rigorous screening processes. All people employed by ACTED already have the obligation to read and sign the code of conduct and internal policies. - Administration and employee cycle:- Presentation and signature of the Code of Conduct for staff; - Monitoring of trial periods for cases detected,- Disciplinary measures follow-up on cases detected in conjunction with country coordination. Training program related to safeguarding issues. - ACTED has an in-house training program related to safeguarding issues: i) Annual training on the Code of Conduct and Policies in all countries and at headquarters for 7000 staff; ii) Pre-departure training for international employees. Iii) Specific ad-hoc training on SEA & Child Protection in countries of intervention. ACTED Complaints and response mechanisms. - As part of its on-going efforts to improve accountability to beneficiaries, ACTED will establish a beneficiary Complaints and Response Mechanism (CRM) for beneficiaries to raise concerns, make complaints and give feedback in a secure way, and receive a response in a timely manner. Complaint’s investigation & disciplinary measures- ACTED has the capacity to investigate safeguarding issues in an independent and confidential way, while protecting both the alleged victim and the alleged perpetrator (ACTED SEA Policy, Articles 19 to 21; ACTED Child protection policy, Articles 20 to 22). In any case, ACTED must always ensure that the author of a report does not suffer from any harm as a result. ACTED also endeavours to protect victims of prohibited acts (ACTED SEA policy, article 23). ACTED has psychologists available to support individuals who would have been impacted as a result of a failure to comply with ACTED's safeguarding policies. The HQ HR department will assess all allegations to design an investigation plan and select an investigation team. Proactive approach- ACTED aims at improving its gender, age and disability representativeness in its team, to the extent possible. Because sexual exploitation and abuse cases disproportionally affect women and girls, ACTED intends on implementing protection and gender mainstreaming throughout the project implementation.
