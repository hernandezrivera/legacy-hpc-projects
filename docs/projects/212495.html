Project Summary

The residents of all camps in northeastern Syria are suffering from a state of stress and loss of community and cultural identity, especially children, because they are the most affected group in all of this and in need of support and safety. They are affected psychologically and socially and are deprived of most of their rights. Therefore, we are seeking to secure a center in the Washokani camp due to the large percentage in it, and according to NES-PiN-2024, it was estimated PiN (3-5 Severity) - Children (92602) Sub-district Severity level - 2024 (4), where the project will provide support to 100 children. In the early childhood stage, the center will care about securing the needs of children from two and a half years old to six years old. The center will rely on the Montessori curriculum with arts to teach children arithmetic, geography, science and language through play and arts. The program will affect the child positively and will provide him with great psychological support that will enable him to rely on himself and develop his sensory development. Physically, intellectually and artistically through the tools of the Montessori curriculum
The aim of the project:
Contributing to raising the level of education and support for 100 children in Washokani camp in the sensory, intellectual, cultural, linguistic, physical and practical aspects and enhancing their full confidence in themselves and their surroundings through the Montessori school (an educational program through arts and play) over the course of a year.
Specific objectives:
1- Enabling 100 boys and girls from two and a half to 6 years old to rely on themselves and develop their abilities through Montessori tools, activities and arts within 8 months.
2- Enabling 12 teachers to master the practice of Montessori methods, activities and curriculum during 24 sessions over a period of two months, 2-3 hours a day in the city of Qamishli.
3- Enabling 10 teachers specialized in arts to carry out the teaching process using artistic tools (drawing - sculpture - stop motion - music).
4- Enabling 22 teachers, including 12 Montessori teachers and 10 art teachers, to acquire knowledge about child protection and childhood during 4 sessions for a week.
These objectives will be implemented through a set of activities specific to each objective
Target people:
Direct objectives:
100 boys/girls from 2 and a half to 6 years old
12 Montessori teachers
10 artists
Indirect beneficiaries:
200 family and friends around the child
Risks and challenges:
The resignation of one of the teachers - the risk to the project is 30% - as this risk will be overcome by allocating two teachers who have received training as a reserve in the event of any resignations - it will affect the project by 10%.
Internal and external security tensions - the risk to the project is 40% - as this risk will be overcome by suspending and removing activities until the situation stabilizes - will affect the project by 15%.
The departure of some families of children from the camp - the risk to the project is 5% - as this risk will be overcome by filling the place of the departing child with another child - it will affect the project by 10%.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

In addition to the project summary on the first page, the following activities will be carried out according to each specific objective.Children in a Montessori home will undergo a set of exercises:1- Practical life training within the Practical Life section (Practical life activities include real household tools that the child deals with with order and discipline. He brings the tool from the shelf, performs the activity, then returns it to its place. He learns personal hygiene, care for the environment, and self-reliance) - Practical life training.2- Training in linguistic activities within the Language Corner (the child acquires the written, read, and audible language and possesses a large amount of vocabulary. Here, education is sensory, then abstract, and gradual).3- Training in sensory life activities within the sensory corner (this corner includes sensory methods and tools that provide the child with several concepts such as long, short, thick, thin, sizes, geometric shapes, three-dimensional shapes, and precise gradation, all of which increases the child’s self-confidence and lays the foundations for organized education with a specific system and arrangement. The importance of this pillar is that it is preparation for language and mathematics.4- Training in mathematics activities within the Mathematics Corner (This corner is called the Magic Corner because the child is very attracted to it and loves to practice it. Here too, education moves from sensory to semi-sensual, then to abstraction, and he learns the concept of numbers and the four operations in a concrete and realistic way, then formal, then written.)5- Training in cultural activities within the Culture Corner (this corner is called the Speech Corner. Here, many questions are asked to the child, and the corner includes (Science - Geography - History). The child learns about nature, the universe, seasons, continents, oceans, some countries and seas, In it, the child learns the shape of a tree through the shape of a leaf, and gets to know animals closely, their food, production, and homes, all in order to gain knowledge of the outside world, increase his self-confidence, and increase his knowledge.6- Artistic training for 100 boys and girls in drawing, sculpture, and music (children are taught the arts in the three disciplines and are empowered with the tools of drawing, sculpture, and music at the hands of specialists in the artistic fields).7- Training children to participate in preparing food on a daily basis during the project period, at a rate of half an hour daily. 8- Enabling children to make their own films via the iPad.
