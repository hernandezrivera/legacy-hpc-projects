Project Summary

Kuwaiti patients Helping Fund (KPHF) proposes to assist vulnerable population affected by confict, displaced and malnutrition by supporting 50 OTPs , 50 TSFPs (with FBMAM& HF) and 6 SCs in South Darfur State (in Nyala Janoub , Nyala Shimal, Ed El Fursan , Beliel , Shattaya , Al Radoum , Kubum , Tulus Um Dafoug & As Suntalocalities), North Darfur State (in El Fasher , Dar As Salam , Tawila , Um Baru & Saraf Omra localities) and Kassala State (in Reifi Gharb Kassala , Reifi Hamashkureib , Reifi Kassla , Reifi Shamal Ad Delta and Reifi Telkok localities). The project proposed to support 322,690 beneficiaries including (202,690 children including boys & girls with SAM and MAM as well as 120 ,000 women for MAM ,FBPMAM & IYCF ) comprising of IDPs, returnees ,resident and host communities experiencing with displaced and malnutrition problem. Malnutrition remains a critical problem affecting the North Darfur, South Darfur and Kassala States. KPHF will provide health services through SCs and nutrition services through OTPs and TSFPs. Nutrition services to cover SAM and MAM cases for U5 and PLW which include screening at admission, medical treatment of severely acutely malnourished (SAM) children with and without medical complication, dietary management and individual child follow up, among others. It will also cover the administrative (support) cost of case management of moderate acute malnourished (MAM) girls and boys of 6-59months and pregnant and lactating women (PLW). Furthermore, nutrition education and counseling will be provided for mothers/caregivers in Infant and Young Child feeding (IYCF) practices and hygiene promotion as well as MSGs will built to facilitate of nutrition education and contribution in MUAC screening activities. Selected trainings will be provided on SC case management, CMAM, NiE, identification of medical complications at OTPs and TSFPs level, community screening and follow up. In addition to, women club, women cornea and community awareness session campaigns will be conducted under nutrition program.

1 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Need Overview HNO 2020, the nutrition situation in Sudan is characterized by persistently high levels of acute malnutrition and stunting. The nutrition situation of children is worsening, aggravated by deteriorating health care, poor water and sanitation, food insecurity, and the economic crisis. Young children, as well as pregnant and lactating women (PLW) are more susceptible to being malnourished as their nutritional requirements are higher. The prevalence of MAM among PLWs is 6.3 per cent, and the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in children under 5 is14.1 per cent About 123 out of the 186 localities in Sudan have GAM rates above 10 per cent. Approximately 522,000 children under 5 require treatment for SAM, and approximately 2.2 million children require treatment for moderate acute malnutrition (MAM). The nutrition needs continue to persist in North Darfur, South Darfur and Kassala States on the background of low coverage and access of nutrition services and inadequate response capacities. HNO 2020 shows that North and South Darfur States are the highest malnutrition rates with GAM rates ranging between 16 – 19 per cent and SAM between 3.7 and 4.9 per cent and Kallala has stunting rates above 30 per cent. S3M 2018 round two shows; SAM, MAM & GAM rate are highest in project targeted localities. In El Fasher locality (SAM is 3.18% , MAM is 12.27% , GAM is15.58%) , in Dar As Salam locality (SAM is 0.97% , MAM is 9.18% , GAM is10.30%) , in Tawila Locality (SAM is 1.75% , MAM is 8.71% & GAM is10.40%) , in Um Baru locality (SAM is 0.47% , MAM is 5.37% & GAM is 5.98%) & in Saraf Omra locality ( SAM is 0.47% , MAM is 5.37% & GAM is 5.98%) in North Darfur State. While in Nyala Janoub locality ( SAM is 0.76% , MAM is 5.01% & GAM is 6.32%) , Nyala Shimal ( SAM is 0.86% , MAM is 5.28% & GAM is 6.32%), in Ed El Fursan locality ( SAM is 0.76% , MAM is 5.70% & GAM is 6.32%) , in Beliel locality ( SAM is 1.75% , MAM is 8.86% & GAM is 10.30%), in Shattaya locality (SAM is 3.60% , MAM is 14.70% & GAM is 18.42%), , in Al Radoum locality ( SAM is 0.97% , MAM is 6.81% & GAM is 7.51%), in Kubum locality ( SAM is 1.07% , MAM is 6.% & GAM is 6.58%), in Tulus locality ( SAM is 1.14% , MAM is 6.35% & GAM is 7.59%), in Um Dafoug locality ( SAM is 0.46% , MAM is 4.60% & GAM is 5.07%) and in As Sunta locality ( SAM is 4.59% , MAM is 14.70% & GAM is 19.14%) in South Darfur State. In addition to , in Reifi Gharb Kassala locality ( SAM is 1.14% , MAM is 6.45% & GAM is 7.59%) , in Reifi Hamashkureib locality ( SAM is 6.46% , MAM is 17.17% & GAM is 23.76%) , in Reifi Kassla locality ( SAM is 1.93% , MAM is 9.17% & GAM is 11.52%) , in Reifi Shamal Ad Delta locality ( SAM is 0.97% , MAM is 6.82% & GAM is 7.59%) and in Reifi Telkok locality ( SAM is 4.96% , MAM is 13.46% & GAM is 18.42%). These above indicators there are higlly gap in nutritionand a need to strengthen the quality, access and coverage of community-based management of acute malnutrition (CMAM) programming to access nutrition through public health facilities, targeted outreach and management interventions are needed.
