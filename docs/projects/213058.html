Project Summary

As per HNO, HRP 2024: In 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023
The current humanitarian situation in Syria had resulted in growing health needs, poor hygiene, and overcrowded living conditions, inaccessible or limited health care servics
The multifaceted crisis in Syria creates continuous cycles of vulnerability, disproportionately impacting those most in need, including individuals with disabilities
People in Aleppo and latakia forced into temporary shelters with limited water and sanitation along with the severe damage to water and sewage infrastructure, leading to heightened risks of infectious diseases, including cholera.
Without urgent and sustained funding, essential services like water, sanitation, healthcare, electricity, will continue to deteriorate, pushing more people into desperate need. Investing in basic services now is essential to pave the way for a more durable response and prevent an even larger crisis from unfolding in the future.
Evolution of People in Need (wash sector) is expected to be 13.6 million people, 2,962,485 in Aleppo , 1,717,593 in Rural Damascus, 341,441 in Homs, and 619,996 in Lattakia.
During Altamayoz work in Aleppo, Rural Damascus and through provision of health services, there was a high increase in infectious diseases related to poor hygiene environment and poor water availability, in addition to spread of lice and scabies. The difficult economic situation made additional pressure on households who were not able to secure food for their children accompanied with poor hygiene practices n food preparation due to lack of clean water. All these documented factors made hygiene promotion and hygiene kits of high importance in all locations.
This project aims at supporting hygiene practices in Rural Damascus, Aleppo, Homs and Latakia through provision of awareness raising sessions along with hygiene kits and water tank distribution.
Tanks and kits will be supported to households who don’t have tanks or hygiene materials, this riteria will be clearly discussed with community to prevent tension in the community.
Project activities will be linked with other activities implemented by Altamayoz or any other NGOs working in the same area and under different sectors, for example, the health services provided by Altamayoz in partnership with UNICEF, and reproductive services provided by Altamayoz in partnership with UNFPA can be provided to people that are targeted under this project. Noting that Altamayoz will make good coordination with WASH sector and other actors in the targeted location to prevent duplication in services provision
Through this project, Altamyoz aims at delivering different wash services to 40350 individuals in all targeted locations during 2024.
Altamayoz has either centers or mobile teams in targeted locations through which awareness raising sessions can be provided

PRA:During project planning phase, Altamayoz took into consideration the followings:-the risks women and girls, older people, people with disabilities might be exposed to while attending awareness raising sessions- expected tension among communities (IDPs, Returnees, Host community) while delivering water tanks or hygiene kits-Expected negative consequences of activities.Altamayoz could identify the following risks:1-Tension in the community while water tank or hygiene kits distribution to selected families 2-Women and girls are at GBV risks in the road while coming to the attend the awareness raising sessions3-People with disability have difficulties in approaching the centersMitigating measures: 1-To consult with different segments of affected population on type of assistance, selection criteria, and to discuss with community the modalities of interventions most suitable for them 2- assure that women and girls have safe access to services through choosing day hours for conducting awareness raising sessions and distribution of tanks or kits, prepare a separate waiting area for women, assure a separate que for women, assure a separate bathrooms for male and female3-Support the center with suitable mechanism to help PwD (provide the center with wheelchair, easy access through flat and suitable path, train staff on how to deal with persons with disability or those of special needs) and conduct awareness raising sessions that provide information dedicated to PwDRisk: Women and adolescent girls are at risk of sexual exploitation and abuse Likelihood: medium. Impact: high MM:1.Promote awareness of GBV 2. A PSEA policy that describes standard of behavior for staff, that forbidding SEA 3.Ensure awareness-raising on SEA amongst personnel Altamayoz has PSEA strategy that is introduced to all staff membersAll staff has to sign a Code of Conduct related to PSEA.
