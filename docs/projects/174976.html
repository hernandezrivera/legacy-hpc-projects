Project Summary

The proposed project will create pathways for the 250 GBV survivors for employment and entrepreneurship. Its an initiative towards social inclusion, market inclusion and resilience to live with dignity and honor in society which is other side of coin of Khwendo Kor signatures programmes. We perceive that GBVs survivors and their dependents are resilient enough to support their families through income generating livelihood interventions. This also includes GBVs survivors doing business and are linked with project beneficiaries as well as private sectors and government Business Support schemes/initiative
4 skill development and market facilitation hubs will be established at community level as well as strengthening government led Vocational skills center to cater GBV survivors needs for employment and self employment. The Hubs will be strengthened by public private sector partnership for business start-ups of GBVs Survivors and mentorships.
250 GBV Survivors will trained (25 participants per training) on 10 market driven innovative vocational skills such as Hospitality Management, Artificial Jwellery, Industrial stitching, Embellished fabric, Livestock powered by retail business modeling framework as entrepreneurial skill and KK market facilitation services for market inclusion as mentors. Who will assess the products quality, timely delivery to markets assess by qualified NAVTTC assessors.
A competitive process will give an opportunity to selected GBVS Survivors’ to show case their 10 Business Model frameworks or canvases sessions to panel of 5 mentors members from Public and private sectors. It will be an effort to of GBV survivors and street children families to understand the contours of markets and facilitation for market inclusion and preparation for market competition.
A Virtual marketing and Job placement strategies will be developed to kick start their livelihood income generating income. Where virtual platform will render digital online shops and digital marketing services in collaborative support of National Incubation platforms as well as social media Bloggers to boast up their sales and marketing of Products & Services.
The proposed project Methodologies comprises
1. Capacity building of GBVs survivors and Street Children Families on innovative vocational skills
2. Capacity building of GBVs survivors and Street families on Business Management and Entrepreneurship and Job placements.
3. Developing Linkages with Markets for IRP Beneficiaries and small entrepreneurs
4. Virtual Market Place for self-employment and entrepreneurship
5. Exploring youth Innovative business ideas for business start-up through mentoring sessions
6. Private and public Partnership for market and financial inclusion of skilled youth and enterprising business ideas.
The project model will be replicated and cascaded by the GBV survivors and Government Department in collaboration with Private sectors for enhancing economic participation and market inclusion.
