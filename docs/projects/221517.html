Project Summary

This intervention will focus on protection, with the aim at increasing the resilience of women affected by the crisis through GBV case management and economic empowerment. This solution is the most important as it is going to tackle women and girl’s vulnerabilities and provide them with means to care for the other needs expressed such as health care, food, WASH, Shelter and education.
Objectives:
This project intends to improve the protection of women and girls affected by the crisis in the North West, South West and Far North regions, in order to better resilience for their autonomy.
Beneficiaries
The project targets 10 Councils in the Northwest, Southwest and Far-North regions. With 30000 direct beneficiaries and 60 000 indirect beneficiaries, among vulnerable women and girls, from IDPs and host communities.
The project will be implemented from January to December 2025, with the budget of $3,175,000.
Outcome 1: Women and girls are protected against gender based violence
Indicators:
• Number of women accessing information, goods, resources and/or services through UN-Women supported platforms and programmes in humanitarian and development settings
• Number of civil society organizations working on gender equality and women’s empowerment, especially women’s organizations, that have strengthened capacities to exercise their leadership role towards the achievement of gender equality and women’s empowerment
Output 1: Increased access to quality services for women and girls GBV survivors or survivors of crisis related sexual violence;
Indicators
• Number of Community GBV Desk functional at the end of the project
• Number of women and girls that benefited from GBV case Management over the project period
• Number of disabled women and girls that benefited from GBV case Management over the project period
Output 2: Strengthened community mechanisms for reporting and preventing gender-based violence;
Indicator: Number of women at decision making level in local GBV Prevention and Response mechanism
Outcome 2: Women and girls affected by the crisis have increased capacities and leadership to care for their needs
Output 1: Facilitation of livelihood and economic empowerment of vulnerable women and girls
Indicators

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Cameroon has been facing for several years now many security challenges with both endogenous and exogeneous causes including: (i) Management of Central African refugees arriving on its eastern border (in particular the East and Adamaoua regions) as a result of the various armed conflicts caused by the multiple socio-political crises in CAR, (ii) Abuses of the terrorists armed group Boko - Haram which rages in the Lake Chad area causing huge displacement of populations within the country, particularly in the Far North region (iii) and the socio-political crisis which degenerated into armed conflict in the North West and South West regions. Since 2017, clashes between the government defense forces and non-state armed groups (NSAGs) pursuing a separatist objective have resulted in a humanitarian crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions on one side and the neighboring West, Center and Littoral regions on the other side. According to UNHCR statistics in October 31, 2024, there are 2 120 560 individuals victims of forced displacement in Cameroon, among them 409 961 refugees, 15 280 asylum seekers, 1 036 775 IDPs and 658 544 returnees.From the need analysis conducted by the humanitarians in the crisis affected regions, it was noted that mainly women and girls were facing protection issues including discrimination (8%), child marriage (10%), family separation (14%), GBV (18%), drug abuse (22%), economic exploitation (24%), kidnapping (26%), early pregnancy (29%), arbitrary detention (37%), lack of civil documentation (40%). In the GBV, sexual attacks stand at 29%. Showers, latrines and water points are among the most insecure places in these regions. The security concern affecting women include risk of attack within the community (27%), sexual violence (29%), lack of safe spaces (30%), the risk of attack when travelling out of the community (53%).Women and girls are particularly affected, as they suffer the great responsibility of the household charges because the men having greater risk of abduction by armed forced have limited opportunity for conducting income generating activities. The women also face double jeopardy as in one hand they suffer consequences of GBV and in other hand are prevented from seeking for help to the state services by non-armed groups.
