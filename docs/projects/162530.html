Project Summary

Provide an emergency humanitarian mine action response for effective community resilience, mitigating the impact of the weapons used in the Malian conflict on civilians in the regions of Gao, Kidal, Menaka, Timbuktu and Mopti, by assisting the population affected by explosive ordnances and Small Arms and Light Weapons (SALW). MAG will deliver risk education on the threat posed by explosive ordnances (including landmines and explosive remnants of war) and SALWs. These risk education sessions will be gender and age-sensitive and tailored to be delivered to women, girls, boys and men by gender-balanced teams. MAG will set specific targets for risk education delivery to key individuals or groups, such as community and religious leaders as well as associations gathering women, youths or professional drivers. This will ensure to maximize the resonance of risk education messages within target communities. Additional specific targets will be set for groups judged as especially vulnerable to explosive ordnances and SALWs. For instance, MAG will focus some of its interventions on Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs), nomadic groups, NGO workers, professional drivers and children. MAG will organize RE sessions in formal and unformal schools to spread risk education messages among children belonging to different groups (sedentaries, nomads, IDPs) as well training teachers and children, building their capacity to spread independently risk education messages among the target communities. Training will be provided as well to key community actors, enabling them to spread education messages independently.
This year, MAG will as well conduct non-technical survey to identify areas contaminated by explosive ordnances and facilitate further clearance activities. Finally, MAG aims to reinforce the victim referral system, to facilitate the access to the required assistance for the victims of explosive ordnances and SALWs and their families, as many victims may live in remote location with limited access to relevant assistance services and health infrastructures.
