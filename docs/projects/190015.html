1. - Projects activities

IOM’s CCCM Direct Implementation response will focus on four pillars:1. Site/Area Coordination and Information Management, including meetings organized at displacement locations (i.e. Site & Woreda/Sub-City levels), and sharing of site and area-level information products such as Site Profiles, CFM Dashboards, Areas of Intervention maps, etc.2. Site Planning, Development, Maintenance and Upgrades, such as site feasibility assessments, design of site plans for new, converted or extended sites; partitioning/repair of communal living spaces for dignity and protection risk mitigation; installation of communal facilities including communal kitchens, multi-purpose shades, etc.; improved accessibility for PwDs through site levelling, installation of walkways, handrails, ramps, etc.; Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) measures, etc.3. Community Participation/Self-Governance, including through support/training of community self-governance structures and running of a Community Feedback Mechanism (CFM), creation and distribution of CwC/IEC materials on issues of importance to communities, including where to access information about humanitarian and protection assistance, fire safety, COVID-19 prevention, mine risk, etc.4. Capacity Building of Key stakeholders (staff, authorities, partners and other stakeholders) on key CCCM concepts and protection mainstreaming, using the Global CCCM Basic CCCM standard training package, delivered by certified trainers (male, female, local and international), in coordination with the CCCM Cluster and Cluster members.In view of the continuing risks posed by the COVID-19 pandemic, IOM will mainstream COVID-19 mitigation and prevention measures across the above pillars. IOM will support training for new CCCM Cluster partners to respond to urgent camp management needs across Ethiopia. IOM will provide financial support and continued supportive supervision for partners selected through proposal process. Implementing partners will be expected to focus on pillars 1, 2 and 4 at a minimum. IOM is the co-lead of the national Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) Cluster and sub-national CCCM Clusters in Shire and Mekelle. IOM will continue to facilitate CCCM Cluster Coordination meetings, enhance the capacities of the sub-national cluster, for example through providing CCCM training at national and regional levels to strengthen the overall humanitarian response in the country.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

In 2021, IOM Ethiopia Mission piloted Accountability for Affected People (AAP) Hotline as one of complaint and feedback mechanism (CFM) tool for Nekempte and Dire Dawa Sub Offices. Based on the learning from the pilot and with some change on the approaches, AAP Hotline scaled up to the country level to receive complaints and feedback on responses and needs from affected population. The complaints and feedback information will be periodically analyzed and reported to inform the management community needs and improve response quality. IOM, working with implementing partners will use a mix of methods to receive feedback on the project. IP Staff will be trained to receive or refer feedback to appropriate confidential channels (particularly for cases of SEA or GBV). IOM has a hotline for anonymous feedback and complaints. CFM complaint boxes, hotlines, referral desks and other methods recommended by beneficiaries will be made accessible for the most vulnerable community members based on their advice.
