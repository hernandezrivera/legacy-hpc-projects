Project Summary

This project is intending to improve the hygiene condition of IDPs in crisis affected communities in Kupe-Muanenguba. This will be achieved through promotion of good hygiene and sanitation practices, rehabilitation of water points, and establishment of hygiene and sanitation promotion agents as focal point for each target community, just to name a few. with the abundance of small streams and rivers flowing from the Kupe-Muanenguba mountains, most communities within Kupe-Muanenguba division still do not have access to good drinking water. Made up of hard to reach communities/ areas, humanitarian response is still needed in the WASH domain especially women and girls, elderly population and people living with disabilities who face difficulties in terms of moving to long distances to access water facilities. Compare to the needs, what has been done recently was really helpful but from our feedback mechanism, more is still needed to imrpove upon the hygiene conditions, facilities in many IDPs locations and public establishments like health centers, schools.
Within the course of the Anglophone crisis, the WASH situation has not been favurable. This is beacause most water points that were being used were not effectively managed and as such many were abandoned. IDPs and most Host communities now have little water for their hygiene, sanitation and most importantly to drink. Even some of the water points flowing are not closer to where most IDPs have afford to stay meaning that they have to go for some long distances or even to streams to fetch water. Therefore, this project will be targeting these target areas especially Nguti-subdivision heavily hit by the crisis to construct water points in strategic locations.
Also, sensitisation and awareness raising will be continuous, in target communities on household use of various hygiene facilities under the best hygiene and sanitation conditions and prevent spread of diseases.
The district hospitals in the division now needs extra WASH facilities as they are the main health centers that IDPs can afford considering their moderate cost for health related issues. Looking at the economic hardship, most IDPs cannot afford hygiene kits like sanitary pads, detergents. As such, this project will strengthen the hygiene facilities of public hospitals in the three subdivisions in several ways ; provission of hygiene facilities and sanitation necessities like buckets, jericans, detergents, soaps, tiolet tissues and a regular clean up campaign, talks, and sensitisation on WASH in these establishments by AFRINET staff, WASH volunteers and WASH social workers.
Going by the UN sustainable development goal No-6 communities should have access to safe drinking water. AFRINET have been enhancing this priority since 2008 as a local response to a national/ global support. Therefore, this project will making an increase in water availability in in the most needy villages, communities with IDPs. This will involve, establishing water points in these locations, clean up campaigns of already existing water points, rehabilitating water points and treating some with aquatabs as the case may be.
Also, this project will be strengthening the hygiene facilities, sensitisation and awareness raising campaigns on good hygiene and sanitation practices of some schools such as GTTC Bangem (public), catholic primary, nursury, secondary-Ave Maria schools (private), Regina Chelly Tombel (private), Oxford accademic complex Tombel (private), GBHS Bangem (public), Government Practicing School Bangem (Public), JOSSAM Bangem, GHS Muebah (public), etc.
The project will also specifically target IDPs women and girls of child bearing age to improve their mentrual hygiene condition. This will involve workshops, seminars, talks targeting these women and girls. They will be assisted by AFRINET medical staff team( nurses, nurses assistant, and our partnership doctors etc to teach the women and girls effective menstual hygiene.
