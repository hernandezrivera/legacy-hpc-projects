Project Summary

Prevention and response to Intimate Partners Violences (IPV) and sexual exploitation related to COVID-19 COVID-19 likely to increase domestic violence and intimate partner violence rates: Anecdotal evidence of the COVID-19 pandemic has already highlighted the increased vulnerability of women and girls to domestic violence. Recent research prior to the pandemic consistently found intimate partner violence (IPV) against women and girls in conflict-affected settings are higher than rates of non-partner sexual violence. Crisis affected, and women and girls are often vulnerable in compromised and temporary shelters without adequate security and normal social protection structures, living with complete strangers. They often face additional risks when they venture out of temporary shelters to look for fuelwood, water, food and other necessities needed to fulfil domestic duties. Women who are crisis affected and are already underserved by social services, and their access to information and strategies to prevent COVID 19 such as testing, handwashing, self- isolation and quarantine will be particularly difficult. Goal: Crisis affected, women and girls (IDP, host communities, refugees including person with disabilities) play their fullest role in response to and are protected from the impacts of COVID-19.
Outcomes:
 Reduced/slowed infection and transmission rates among vulnerable crisis affected and IDP women and host communities including person leaving with disabilities.
 Strengthened livelihood and resilience of crisis affected for women and girls including person leaving with disabilities.
 Strengthened prevention and better response to Gender Based Violence, including domestic and intimate partner violence against crisis affected women and girls including person with disabilities.
 Increased crisis affected and migrant women’s leadership and voice in the COVID 19 humanitarian response.
The project will be implemented through awareness-raising activities, interpersonal and small groups interventions , online and phone counselling, radio communication campaign, training and capacity-building, pre-positioning and distribution hygienic menstrual kits, revise GBV referral pathways ,including incorporation COVID-19 IEC materials, develop cash transfer or Cash and Voucher assistance, small livestock production, develop and disseminate “life saving” GBV message stimulating community engagement and strategic advocacy towards all stakeholders, integrate protection staff into COVID-19 health response, capacity building of humanitarian actors and another keys actors on gender lens including the impact of COVID-19 on differing rates of infection, differential economic impacts, differential care burden, and incidence of domestic violence and sexual abuse, psychosocial support, Develop cash transfer or Cash and Voucher assistance vulnerable women girls and COVID-19, initiate a research on the linkages between women’s leadership and access to GBV protection and livelihoods (including the challenges of unpaid care work), training of women organizations as first responders to support women and girls on GBV and PSEA prevention and response mechanisms to adapt to COVID 19 restrictions. In the intervention area, 137600 person will affected by activities ; Far Nord (40000) Nord-West(38000) Sud West (25600) et Littoral (34000).
