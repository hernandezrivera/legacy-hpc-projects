Project Summary

Muzun is planning to respond in the most vulnerable camps in Idleb Salqin sub-district based on the severity of WASH needs. The camps that will be targeted by this project have all been heavily affected by conflict and are in need of additional WASH support, based on a rapid muzun assessment in some of the selected camps, consultations with Syrian and international NGOs, and reliable secondary data, including the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2021. The project is designed to meet specific life-saving needs around water sanitation and hygiene (WASH) in Saqlin sub-district / Salqin Cluster, and is aligned to HRP 2021 for Syria. The project focuses on WASH response with gender mainstreamed, and with a well-designed response strategy suitable to access situation and security challenges implementation through reliable and experienced site engineers, carefully screened private contractors, and coordination with other actors. 4 - Needs

IDPs in formal sites and informal settlements are highly dependent on continuous humanitarian assistance. Disruptions of services in IDP sites and their quality will have direct negative impact on people physical and mental wellbeing as well as protection consequences, specially for women and girls. As per the HNO 2021 36% of the population is relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs. 29% of garbage is inappropriately disposed within communities. According to the needs assessment conducted by the Muzun organization, the increasing number of informal IDP sites in north west Syria has also complicated the response in which these sites access water through water tracking mostly. In light of the high population density and the dependence of civilians on alternative sources such as water trucking, which is absolutely unsafe due to the unreliability of water from wells transported by tanks, new concerns arise from the spread of disease WASH services in sites require significant and continuous investments. IDPs living in sites across Syria are amongst the most vulnerable groups often solely relying on humanitarian aid. Access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations, IDPs living in IDP sites , as well as other most vulnerable households with limited livelihood opportunities and access to basic WASH services. Consideration of people with disabilities is key, specific materials will be considered to ensure their access to services that Muzun will implement over WASH interventions.

5 - Implementation of activities

1.Adequate quality and quantity of water: Water supply will ensure through water trucking.Muzun will sign an agreement with a contractor to supply water to the targeted camps on daily basis (35 L per person will be provided on daily basis). This water will be sufficient to cover the drinking, bathing and household need of the individuals. If required by the targeted families, MHD will also distribute Jerry cans. This will be contributed by MHD. 2. Water testing and treatment (chlorination): MHD team will conduct water testing and chlorination before it is available for use by the beneficiaries. This will help to eliminatethe risk of using contaminated water. 3. Waste removal service (solid waste management): Through a service provider (contractor) MHD will ensure to collect the garbage from the camps and deport it to the agreed damp sites on daily basis. This will improve the overall environment and decrease the risk of diseases spread. MHD will also place garbage bins 4. Operation and maintenance of sanitation facilities: Sanitation facilities (latrines and bathing areas) will be gender segregated and will have protection measures this will be considered during the regular maintenance of the facilities (lockable doors, lighting of paths to facilities). 5. Hygiene awareness activities (such as awareness sessions) will be organized in order to raise awareness of the population of the personal hygiene as well as the importance of keeping environment clean.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Quality and/or quantity not adequate as per humanitarian standards ( MHD will ensure provision of safe water as per country Water Quality standards where possible and establish regular water quality monitoring including water source, water trucks and end user samples; Collect water from safe sources certified by water authorities or local councils. MHD will comply with minimum humanitarian standard (SPHERE) and provide at least 35 liter/person/day and establish water quantity tracking & monitoring system involving beneficiaries. 2. Not enough water distribution points available so people have to wait for extended periods to get water, lights and harassment could occur; inequality in distribution. (MHD will ensure context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consult separately girls, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases;) 3. Water distribution not safe or accessible, especially for women, girls (exposure to GBV risk) and children elderly and people with disabilities; inequality in distribution MHD will ensure context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consult separately girls, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases; Make sure that distance of water collection points remains at minimum distance as outlined in country policy document as to ensure that this does not overburden women and adolescent girls with extra work load in particular.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

The risk of sexual exploitation and abuse during the intervention: All MHD staff are receiving regular training on the code of conduct and PSEA policy and they signed on these policies, The contractors have the obligation to sign an ethical declaration with a specific clause on PSEA with an emphasis on mitigation measures and sanctions in case of abuse by an employee, Beneficiaries will receive information on the CRM and the expected behavior of MHD staff and its contractors with an emphasis on how to report any SEA incidents. A woman staff is present during all visits to women and child led-households. MHD has already existing SEA system in which MHD protection specialists at the HQ and field offices will apply the measures and keep monitoring the implementation accordingly.
