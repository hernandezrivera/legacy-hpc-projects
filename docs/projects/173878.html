Project Summary

Initiative for Development and Empowerment Axis (IDEA) proposes to implement the project for boys and girls in the targeted schools in District Mardan. The purpose of the project is to support the government’s efforts in increasing access and improving the quality of education through teachers capacity building, school improvement plan (SIP) through SMCs and safe school operation protocols (Infection Prevention and Control-IPC). Development and distribution of risk communication material in local language, implementing safe school guidelines; equipping schools with hygiene packages (including IPC kits) and circulating critical information on curtailing the transmission of COVID-19 pandemic. The target beneficiaries are children “girls and boys”, teachers and SMC members, beneficiaries for RCCE will be the communities living in the catchment areas of the schools. 210 members (!20 female and 90 male) from 70 SMCs (40 Girls and 30 Boys schools) will be capacitated on the development of School Improvement Plan, record keeping, Fund utilization, increasing enrollment and retention in the schools. Capacity of the 280 teachers (160 female and 120 male) will be enhanced on Child Friendly schools, life skills based education, psycho-social support and teaching methodologies including Activity Based Learning Skills, a total number 11250 students (6250 Girls and 5000 boys) shall be provided with safe school facilities and school kit and the targeted 3200 children (1280 male and 1920 female) will be provided home-based learning/alternate learning opportunities. IDEA Head office will ensure overall management, financial oversight, and conduct regular monitoring visits to the project site, and will hold regular update meetings with Field staff. The Project Manager will lead implementation of the project and as well as providing technical support to project staff. IDEA management will attend meeting of Education cluster at Provincial & National level to share their experience and to learn from good practices implemented by other cluster members. IDEA team will regularly report its progress on project specified template as deemed. IDEA will keep close coordination with the education cluster and will update the 4Ws matrix on regular basis. IDEA will conduct the following activities;
1. Training of PTC/SMC members on effective school management and children's access to education.
2. Training of male & female teachers on Child Friendly schools, life skills-based education, psycho-social support and teaching methodologies including teaching through different modalities.
3. Development and dissemination of the safe school operation protocols for boys and girls students.
4. Provision of school kits to the male and female students.
5. Provision of teaching materials (guide) to the teachers.
6. Creating opportunities for the student (boys and girls) to reach with home-based learning/alternate learning opportunities .
Currently IDEA is implementing various projects in education sector in 12 Districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in close collaboration and coordination with Education Department. Beside this IDEA is also working/ed with Education Department and Ministry of SAFRON for the safe re-opening of the schools/Infection, Prevention & Control and enhancing access to quality education.
IDEA has unique advantage to the project in terms of experience, expertise in variety of education initiatives with Government, International partners and donors. IDEA has successfully mainstreamed 70 informal schools in Public Sector system in KP, where 9650 (Girls 60%) children continued their education after primary level.
IDEA has valid MoU with EAD, Allow to Work from SAFRON Ministry and NOCs from Home & Tribal Affairs Department KP.

1- Project Activities

1. Development of implementation strategy and work plan: IDEA will develop the project implementation strategy and work plan which will be submitted and agreed with UNICEF.2. Training of PTC/SMC members on effective school management and children's access to education.IDEA team will provide facilitation role to endorse all formed/reactivation of SMCs from local Education Office. IDEA Education Specialist will develop a comprehensive capacity building session. Capacity of SMCs will be enhanced through regular two days training session and later on one day refresher. The training programs will be designed, the content of training will be on the role, responsibility, School Implementation Plan, Financial Management regarding SMCs and Record keeping. During the two days training School Improvement Plan will be developed and SMC swill be made responsible to advocate for prioritizing the school related issues. 3. Training of male & female teachers on Child Friendly schools, life skills-based education, psycho-social support and teaching methodologies including teaching through different modalities. IDEA team in consultation with education department will train the 280 teachers (160 Female and 120 male) on the concept of child friendly schools, life skills based education, psychosocial support and best teaching techniques.4. Development and dissemination of the safe school operation protocols for boys and girls students. IDEA team will develop the RCCE messages, which will be translated in the local language. The RCCE messages will be linked with the safe school guidelines. The messages will be displayed in the catchment areas of the schools and inside the schools. Beside this local mosque loud speakers will also be utilized for RCCE message dissemination. 5. Provision of school kits to the male and female students. IDEA team in consultation with education and the relevant school administration will identify the essential items to be included in the school kit (IPC Kit) for the male and female students. IDEA procurement section will procure the kits and will be distributed in the targeted schools. The kits will be provided to 11250 (6250 Girls and 5000 boys) students in the presence of SMC members. The teachers and students of the relevant schools will be oriented on the utilization of these kits. 6. Provision of teaching materials (guide) to the teachers. After the completion of the training, IDEA will provide the teaching materials to 280 teachers. The materials will also be shared with the rest of the teachers in the school. The trained teachers will further cascade the training approach in their respective schools. 7. Creating opportunities for the student (boys and girls) to reach with home-based learning/alternate learning opportunities. IDEA team in consultation with the relevant school administration and education department will develop a home based learning/alternate learning plan for the students. The students will be provided work sheets/book, in which the students will complete their home related assignments. IDEA team will also sensitize their parents to focus on the alternate learning programs.
