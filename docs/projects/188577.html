Project Summary

The project aims to continue and increase the accessibility to essential life-saving curative and preventative nutrition services for all boys, girls, pregnant and lactating women, and the caregivers (other women, and men) with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities in NW Syria.
The project will provide comprehensive quality CMAM services, disseminate and support optimal IYCF-E practices and promote health and nutrition and basic hygienic behaviors within the population in need in northwest Syria, in addition, fill the current service gap and provide emergency integrated health, nutrition, WASH, and child protection rapid services through 16 Rapid Response Teams (RRTs) which will be dispatched to the areas of high IDP flow.
This project will target Idleb and Aleppo governorates including 12 sub-districts: Al-Dana, Salqin, Harim, Marret Tamisrin, Kafartakharim, Qurqania, Idleb, Ariha, and Armanaz in Idleb governorate, in addition to Al-Bab, A'zaz, and Afrin in Aleppo governorate.
The scope of activities includes the following:
1st : Scaling up integrated nutrition response support through 16 Rapid Response teams which will be dispatched to the areas of high IDP flow in Idleb and Aleppo governorates.
2nd : Scaling up the OTP services in 6 fixed and mobile CMAM centers (2 in Al-bab, 2 in Dana, 1 in Ariha, and 1 in A'zaz sub-districts). The services in these centers will be integrated with SEMA health facilities.
3rd : Functioning 5 IPF/stabilization centers in four maternity and pediatric hospitals: Al-farabi and the Maternity hospitals in Al-bab sub-district, Muaz Abu Mahdi Hospital in A'zaz sub-district, Al-Ekha' hospital in Atmeh (Dana), and Ariha maternity hospital in Ariha sub-district, to provide treatment of SAM children under five with medical complications.
4th: SEMA will conduct training to build the capacity of the nutrition services providers on IYCF-E and CMAM programs.
All activities and nutrition intervention will be provided according to nutrition cluster recommendations and CMAM, IYCF- E protocols, including the adapted interventions in the context of COVID-19 such as simplified CMAM protocol, family MUAC approach, etc..
All the prevention measures from COVID-19 infections will be applied during providing nutrition services, the nutrition staff will encourage beneficiaries to get COVID-19 vaccination as the main prevention measure, and disseminate the communication key messages about COVID-19 preventions that are recommended by the RCCE.
The targeted population reached to 325,000 beneficiaries (120,000 host communities, 200,000 IDPs, and 5000 returnees) including the most vulnerable groups (children with disability, women, children under 5 from IDPs, and host community) (targeting 180,000 children under five, 100,000 pregnant and lactating women PLWs, and 45,000 other caregivers including 40,000 women and 5000 men)

5 - Implementation of activities

RRT intervention:Nutrition: Screen Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) and Children under 5 (CU5) to detect and refer the malnourished cases, provide IYCF-E counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children, distribute micronutrients to prevent anemia and malnutrition among children, pregnant and lactating women, disseminate messages on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, and monitor distribution and use of breast milk substitutes, support health/nutrition inclusive education and promotion, refer the needed cases and follow up the referred ones. All the RRT will be trained to take COVID19 precautions during the work and the project will cover the PPE for the staff and beneficiaries.Protection: identify and refer the cases exposed to abuse or violence via the visual signs on the children under five and PLW bodies, where the discovered cases will be referred to the child protection CP or GBV case management teams in coordination with SEMA's CP specialist according to CP and GVB service map.Health: Detect and refer CU5 and PLWs with danger signs and medical problems.CMAM OTPs:Scaling up the OTP services in 6 fixed and mobile CMAM centers (2 in Albab, 2 in Dana, 1 in Ariha, and 1 in A'zaz sub-districts) these centers will be integrated into SEMA's health facilities in the targeted locations. Nutrition status and anthropometric measurements of children under five and PLW are regularly screened, recorded, reported, and required program actions are taken

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

SEMA ensures the privacy of the beneficiaries and vulnerable groups through adjusting and keeping the data of the beneficiaries secured, being responsible for the principle“Do-no-Harm” to the beneficiaries, and keeping their dignity. There is effective feedbackand complaint mechanism and beneficiaries are made aware of how to access it. This mechanism includes developed practices and channels for reporting Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. The concept of PSEA is also considered in the awareness-raising topics offered for children and adults to raise their awareness on their rights and safety and the safety measure to report such misconduct. IEC materials, banners, and posters will be available in all facilities and distribution points. Staff will be oriented in the contracting stage on SEA issues and will sign the CoC. Refresher training for the staff will be conducted on a regular basis. All complaints will be taken into consideration and will be investigated following SEMA’s investigation policies, Disciplinary measures will be taken against any breach of CoC.
