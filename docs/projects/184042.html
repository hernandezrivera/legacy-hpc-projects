Project Summary

This one-year project NCA will provide protection include Prevision and response for 16,530 (6,305 women, 3,665 Men, 4,900 Girls, 3,500) Refugees and affected Host communities in Tunaydbah. To ensure sustainability, the project will integrate protection with other multi-sectorial intervention focused on supporting protection Concerns mainly for women, children and youth at risk or affected by conflict. in line with the CHS and SPHERE standards, will ensure that community members are empowered to actively participate in project planning, implementation, monitoring to continue protection and income generation activities after project end. The one-year project approach is necessary to have sufficient time to achieve self-sufficiency, resilience, and evidence-based advocacy.

1 - Needs

Protection environment remained highly precarious in heightened risk areas, characterized by potential intercommunal conflict and generalized violence, currently around 2.4M people include Women, Men, Girls and Boys in needs with Protection Services in Sudan. During a rapid GBV assessment conducted by NCA in early February, women, girls and boys from the refugee and host communities identified their priority needs as safety, along with access to healthcare, water, livelihoods, protection, WASH and education. Protection concerns were identified across the humanitarian intervention. Access to qualified legal aid services through the assistance of lawyers, legal counsellors and protection actors is critical for guaranteeing the effective enjoyment of rights by refugees, more and less for the host community, in line with the international, regional, and national legal frameworks. Access to legal services, particularly legal consultation and clear guidelines on their rights are among the main challenges, that Host community and refugees are facing. NCA project contributes to building a protection environment conducive to finding durable solutions (for example, by increasing the knowledge of the refugees and host community member on legal aid, women and girls right, and legal consultation. Moreover, the protection concerns have various aspect based on vulnerability groups in the community and harmful practices, according to the intersectional assessment conducted by UNICEF. Early marriage and FGM in a common issue among girls ages 14, prevalent in both refugees and host community. There is a lack of awareness on personal hygiene and how to properly use sanitary pads alternatives ways, among adolescent, especially those with not access to primary education. Women are more subjected to domestic violence due to their duty of carrying all of homework, as well as supported farming activities in the rain season. In addition, child labor is identified as a key issue for Boys and youth dropping out of school and searching for opportunity to work, causing of increasing the trafficking particularly among refugees. NCA has a long experience in working in refugees and IDP camps, acquired in more than 50 years in emergency and post emergency response in various localities in Sudan, NCA been working in Tundaydbah since December 2020 providing protection and SRH activity for women and girls across the camp, NCA manage to address the needs of women, girls, men and boys include GBV survivors through Women and girls safe space and community centre.

2 - Activities/Outputs

• Protection Monitoring/analysis and advocacy enhanced.NCA will work alongside with other protection service provision in identifying the key protection concern in addition to improve the organizational referral mechanism through updated and regular participation and protection cluster and working groups. NCA will train its staff in prevention and response, in addition of identification of people with vulnerability through conducting KAP survey focusing on key various protection concerns. • protection services/ assistant provision expanded.NCA will provide comprehensive case management, through team of case workers adhering the ethical CM guidelines. Moreover, will work with all community level and increase their knowledge on legal rights following the global and international guidelines. NCA will conduct awareness campaign using tools such as engaging men and boys in practice (EMAP) and SASA. Also conduct PSS/ recreational activities include (art therapy, face mask, net therapy, psychoeducation etc.), and mitigate the protection concerns through cash for protection. • Peaceful co-existence promoted. NCA will establish 2 community-based disputed resolution structures/committees with clear ToR among Refugees and host community, and build their capacity conflict analysis, stakeholders mapping, and conflict resolution skills. • Identification of beneficiaries for multi-purpose cash grant NCA will approach the community and raise awareness on a Cash grant programme, in addition will involve the community member designing the project modality, NCA will conduct pre-assessment on identification of severe vulnerable women, girls, men and boys include people in needs and link with the available income generation activities include (farming, Food drying. processing and preservation, preparation and marketing of dairy products for host community, handicraft etc.). NCA would build the capacity of beneficiaries through five days training on financial and project management and NCA would provide a serious post distribution monitoring the share the results attached with case studies with UNHCR. 6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The NCA programme will follow the ACT child protection (CP) and CAFOD Child Protection (2015) policies to ensure children are protected and treated equally irrespective of race, religion, gender, age or abilities. NCA also follows the Code of Conduct for the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement and NGOs in Disaster Relief (CoC) for staff and partners with guidance on appropriate and expected standards of behaviour, including with children. NCA & partners will during the first quarter of 2021 complete CP safety audits to assess gaps that might need to be addressed to ensure that children are protected and included in all aspects of decisionmaking affecting their well-being. NCA will strive to ensure safety, inclusiveness, and protection of children in camps and host communities. NCA will create awareness to staff, visitors, and other stakeholders about child protection protocols to make sure that the wellbeing of children is secure and their right to access basic services is understood. NCA additionally strives to create child friendly spaces where children (including any unaccompanied children) can come together, play and talk comfortably. The multi-sectoral programmes are designed to address the immediate and strategic needs of children. NCA has a strong GBV sector with an international GBV advisor leading the overall implementation as well as national officers who are based in Gedarif, Darfur and Khartoum
