Project Summary

Through this project African Christian Kush Aid Program aims to support 20,000 individuals, comprising of men, women, boys, girls, widow, disable persons and elderly with shelter and Non-Food items and GIK 20,000 in Maiwut and Longechuk County of Upper Nile State. The project will contribute to the Cluster objectives, 1). Support most vulnerable returnees, host communities/affected non-displaced, and IDPs with durable solutions, rebuilding lives through shelter and NFI solutions, and 2). Improve access to safe, appropriate emergency shelter and lifesaving NFIs to newly displace or populations with new vulnerabilities in the following payams(Malual, Jekow, Litkong, Guong, Lolnyanyang, Dhardhar, Pinythor and Bathelam)
In Maiwut and Longechuk County of Upper Nile State, communities will be assisted through the project with in-kind shelter and non-food Items, however, locations of Maiwut and Longechuk County. This component of intervention will contribute to the local economy hence, improving community resilience and coping mechanism of local people.
The 2020 South Sudan humanitarian overview anticipates high return of people to their native communities, this include internally displaced people and those who flee to refugee outside the country. The course of this movement of people is attributed to the 2018 Peace Agreement between the Government and Warring parties which has so far shown some level stability and normal return to life in some parts of the Country.
The return of people to their homes of origin will add to the already overwhelming humanitarian crisis across South Sudan communities. Therefore through this project, African Christian Kush Aid Program will use approaches that also contribute to long term community solutions by ensuring proper coordination within the Cluster and stakeholders at community level. Beneficiaries of the project will be involved in the project from assessment to distribution. With this community local structures like local chiefs and faith based institutions will be used to enhance community participation.
Also, local committees will be formed and trained to sensitize communities on coping mechanisms. They will also be enlightened on local resource mobilization for other community projects. And adding to holding Focus Group Discussions in consultative community meetings, a complaint Feedback Mechanism will formed from inception of the project to enable African Christian Kush Aid Program to gather feedback from communities.
Under this project African Christian Kush Aid Program will also use the mobile team to undertake multi-sectorial approach in serving most vulnerable people with shelter and Non-food Items. By using this approach, there is great Programme impact as sectors like FSL, WASH, Protection are engaged along the SNFI sector to jointly address community needs

1 - Needs

NFI will enable the displaced community to settle back home in 2020 after long process of peace dialogues between the President and the oppositions and the agreement reached to end conflict and form transitional government with commitment of both side agreeing to form the government, many people are expected to move back home. In overall there has been relative improvement of peace and stability in South Sudan with significant drop in escalations of violent fighting compared to the previous years of the conflict. This is attributed to the signing of the revitalized peace agreement in September 2018 between the Government of South Sudan and warring parties, paving way for the formation of a-three-year transitional Government that will officially begin in 2020. The regional mediating body for the Peace Agreement has also made efforts to persuade other opposing groups that are non-signatory to join the Agreement as they pose potential risks to the full stability of the country. The humanitarian context remains overwhelming, humanitarian actors are facing the wrath of matching limited resources with the increasingly enormous humanitarian needs, especially for IDPs and Returnees. Majority of communities are struggling to survive due to the accumulative effects of the half decade years of conflict which has hurt livelihoods and coping mechanism. As the year 2020 approaches, a more complex wave of humanitarian situation is expected to uncover, with unprecedented raise in humanitarian needs across the country as cited in the 2020 Humanitarian overview‘’ The majority of people were displaced in 2013 and 2016 from their state of origin; therefore, as a result of peace implementation the bulk of returns will happen within the boundaries of each state. A few returnees have been witnessed returning from neighbouring countries and its projected that returnees both inside and outside South Sudan may increase in 2020, based on 2019 trends as the situation at home normalizes’’ most of the returnees have been staying in camps, where they have no source of income to re-establish their homes thus they need shelter and non food items, like blankets, sources pan utensils, mattress and household items. The people returning to their homes of origin in South Sudan will face the challenge of starting to rebuild lives from the scratch. And this is likely going to increase cases related to House, Land and Property. On the other hand, an extreme scenario could happen, for example, when the Peace Agreement collapsed or the rest of opposing forces fail to join the agreement, full scale hostilities may resume, thus plaguing the country into deeper humanitarian crisis, with more loss of lives and properties, and thousands of people been displaced internally and outside the country. African Christian Kush Aid Program to support 20,000 individual vulnerable returnees, host communities/affected non-displaced, and IDPs with durable solutions, rebuilding lives through distribution of in-kind shelter and non-food items in Maiwut and Longechuk County of Upper Nile State. The project aims to contribute to durable solutions by ensuring all stakeholders are involved from beginning of assessment level to distribution level. The S/NFI cluster will continue to provide the pivotal role of guiding interventions technically, this include review and approval of intervention missions and reports. And the Sub - national Coordination will give location-specific directives during interventions which contributes to the overall effectiveness of the Cluster as overlap among partners is avoided from onset. IOM, which serves as the core Cluster pipeline will also be consulted closely on the supply of emergency shelter and non-food materials to communities; In event that African Christian Kush Aid Program is faced with difficulty in accessing a community that is in dire need of humanitarian support due to political reasons, the support UN OCHA will be requested.

2 - Activities

African Christian Kush Aid Program will take the lead of distribution in Maiwut and Longechuk County of Upper Nile State by ensuring that every beneficiary is verified, registered, and given the distribution. 1. We shall conduct Beneficiaries verification and registration with consultation with community leaders. African Christian Kush Aid Program will work with community structures to target the right vulnerable beneficiaries with aid and register them. 2. Conduct ES/NFI Distribution. During the NFI Distribution, African Christian Kush Aid Program will ensure that all aid distribution is based on the needs of the affected population and will ensure that structures are in place to ensure that On Site Distribution Monitoring are done3. Engage and deploy mobile teams to deep field locations. The mobile team will be able to engage and respond to the needs of the affected population. 4. Monitor the progress and achievement by conducting Rapid and Post Distribution Monitoring. This activity will inform the project and African Christian Kush Aid Program in particular on the level of satisfaction and impact on the affected population. 5. African Christian Kush Aid Program will Lead the ES/NFI activities at county level In Maiwut and Longechuk County of Upper Nile State African Christian Kush Aid Program will continue to coordinate the activities of the ES/NFI at county level ensuring that the activities on of the state are well coordinated through working with the various partners including the agencies are site focal points
