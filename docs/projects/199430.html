Project Summary

Objective 1: To improve nutrition, health, sanitation, and safety and wellbeing of learners, teachers and other staff at all school level- ECD, primary, secondary
Objective 2: To increase access to clean and safe water within the learning institutions at the ECD, Primary and Secondary levels
Objective 3: Improved physical, psychological protection, psychosocial, and social emotional wellbeing of vulnerable school age children and teacherss in schools towards resilience programming at all levels in ECD, primary, and secondary schools
Objective 4: Uninterrupted learning to safeguard progress, build back better and prevent school dropouts for boys and girls affected by drought at all levels (ECD, primary and secondary)
Objective 5: Strengthened coordination of education drought response at county and national levels
