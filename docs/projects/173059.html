Project Summary

The project designed to provide lifesaving Protection interventions in conflict and crises areas in Sudan.

The aim of the project is to contribute in improvement the general conditions and enhancing resilience through improvement of restoring access to basic protection through the sub-sectors of Child protection , gender based violence , and general protection, , as well as building the communities and improving their access to basic services for prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to their protection needs through quality and principled humanitarian action. Through implementation it is in this context that GAH proposed the use of an integrated protection intervention aimed at maximizing impact on vulnerable communities . In an effort to address these challenges, the protection interventions intend to targets venerable host communities , IDPs and returnees in the states and localities N. Abyei PCA , West Kordfan ( Al-Sunut , Al-Logawa , Al-Nohoud , AL-Idia),South Kordofan ( Al-Buram , Kadugli , Al-Leri , Talowdi), White Nile (Aj Jabalain , As Salam) , Kassala ( Raifi Hamashkureib , Reifi Shamal Ad Delta , Reifi Kassala , Reifi Telkok), Gadareif ( Basunda , Al-Botanah , Galaat Al-Nahal , Ar Rahad , Galaat Ash-Shargiah ) , Blue Nile ( Al-Tadamon, Baw , Al-Kurmuk , Wad Al-Mahi), EAst Darfur ( Abujabrah , Asssalaya Ad, Al-Fursan , Al-firdous , Yassin ), South Darfur ( Buram , Damso , AlRadoum , Kabum , Rihaid Al-birti), Red Sea ( Haya , Sinkat , Sawakin , Tawkar) , North Darfur ( Al-Fasher , AL-Koma , Al-Malha , As-Serif , At-Tawisr)
The project aims to address all protection need and gabs through enhancing monitoring and linkages and integration to service provision at individual, household and community levels.
The intervention in general protection will be through enhancing the essential welfare activities to provide people sufficient services to decrease the trauma of conflict and crises they have been through , and its effect on their psychological status with raising their dignity by delivering direct livelihood activities
with to PSN , WAR , PWD , EAR . Through diverse training and livelihood activities to increase they ability to have dignified services. through providing communities with small scale projects to give them the ability to enhance their areas with live saving activities
They will be activities for women empowerment to unable women to receive more trainings to support the families and children and decrease the number of WAR and CAR , and give them the ability to stand alone. Providing the knowledge to vulnerable people to know their right , assistance , and raising their awareness through out psychological , psychosocial , referrals pathways , and sessions to have an over all outcome in changing their behaviors to decrease the violence that have been or might be facing them. With enhancing their capacities for sustained resilience, involvement and community participation CBPN , and Women Committees . Strengthening referral and working with other community based structures. communities’ vulnerable including GBV survivors, PLWDs, the elderly and other PSN through community based gender sensitive and culturally appropriate interventions that targets building of community resilience, multiplier effect and sustained results and nd establishing women centers for development.
The project will improve access to CP emergency and prevention services to reduce the incidents and effect of violence, exploitation and abuse. Vulnerable children will be provided with adequate psycho-social support at constructed/rehabilitated CFSs at the community level. GAH will also enhance the protection capacity of the of communities to identify, report , respond and prevent different protection risks and concerns by sensitization and training of CBCPNs to act and respond better to CP issues.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

GAH being a protection based organization will ensure that protection mainstreaming is not only visible in all of the project activities but also implemented to the later. Consideration will be given to ensure that all services are offered with dignity. Given that there will be a clear strategy of CWC vulnerable targeted beneficiaries will easily be reached examples of such practical and easy to conduct activities include developing quick access cards for beneficiaries that needed specialized attention e.g. pregnant women, SGBV survivors, UASC and PLWD. In this regard the cards help identify the beneficiaries and they are taken to quick lines to avoid them staying longer in the service centers. The project then develops referral pathways for support to the identified PoC to enable them receive other services from other sectors ( Nutrition, WASH Health) this ensures an integrated approach. The clinics will have trained doctors in CMR and with support from UNFPA GAH will ensures centers are prepared for the survivors including medical and PSS support. All the constructions works done will be gender and disability sensitive to ensure all have access and can utilize the services. Men women boys and girls will receive services that are sensitive to their needs and considerate of their gender. All WASH facilities will be construction in consideration of Privacy and safety of users and separate for boys and girls
