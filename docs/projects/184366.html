Project Summary

MARDO calls for urgent provision of sustainable and safe access to water, hygiene and sanitation resources, facilities and along with increased awareness among IDPs, host communities, returnees, and persons with disabilities being the extremely vulnerable and affected by war, drought, floods, poverty and food insecurity.
In Bay and Bakool, MARDO proposes urgent measures through water vouchers and rehabilitation of strategic water sources such as boreholes, wells and earth-dams supported by distribution of hygiene kits ( include soap, aqua tab, bucket, Jerrycan, and sanitary pads along with COVID 19 mitigations ( masks, sanitisers and thermometers) aiming extremely high-risk families, i.e., families with malnutrition children, affected by AWD cholera.
Temporary and sustained access to water will be delivered by providing water vouchers and rehabilitation of strategic water points. Hygiene kits that include soap, aqua tab, bucket, Jerrycan, and sanitary pads will be distributed to targeted high-risk families, (Persons living with disability, families with malnutrition children, affected by AWD cholera, IDPs, Returnees, Host communities and minority clans). It is recommended that sensitisation be focused on good hygiene practices within strategic points such as schools, water points, and health/nutrition centers in disaster-affected areas. The promotion and communication is cognizant of different audience in terms of language, community ownership and leadership, literacy, disability, gender and age. IEC materials cater for the needs of the visually and hearing impaired people. Disability disaggregated data will be collected throughout the project. WASH management committees (WMC) will be set up comprising of Persons with Disabilities, women, the Minority Clans, IDPs, Returnees, Host Communities, the Village Elders and the experts in Water, Sanitation and Hygiene.
Additional services include:
• Emergency chlorination, dehydration , de-silting and fencing of wells and boreholes
• Construction of gender-sensitive latrines with hand-washing facilities raised to avoid flooding and fitted with locks from the inside to curb GBV and enhance dignity.
• Construction of accessible WASH facilities for People with disabilities and the Elderly.
• Initiation of CLTS approach to encourage good hygiene and sanitation behaviors aimed at achieving ODF free villages.
• Distance, safety and security, and time are critical factors determining accessibility of WASH services and facilities for all the targeted segments of the beneficiaries
• Direct consultation and engagement of women, men, girls, boys, people living with disabilities and the Elderly in the Project design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.
• Mainstreaming protection and GBV risk reduction through trainings, sensitization and setting up monitoring and reporting platforms as well as staff and beneficiary commitment to uphold the policy in the interest of Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse within the intervention cycle.
• Mobilisation, sensitisation and meetings with all project stakeholders, Actors, and targeted communities informing them on objectives, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, and beneficiary entitlement and roles.
• Incipiency of the Accountability framework to the affected population supported by feedback and complaints and awareness mechanisms and systems such as hotlines, mail-boxes, involvement with and beyond the Gatekeepers.
• Awareness and monitoring, reporting and performance evaluation of the project logframe in view of the inputs, out-puts, indicators, monetary probity, reviews, flexibility, lessons learnt and best practices touching on gender, protection, sustainability and adaptability.
• Handing over WASH structures to community elders, government officials, and religious leaders upon completion.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The water-supply procurement service is advertised and selected competitively based on capacity, ability, technical know-how, financial integrity and cross-cutting due-diligence process. MARDO adopts a water-voucher approach in water-trucking. Cash voucher is cost effective and risk-mitigating in management and implementation.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

MARDO's market research analysis and interaction with the local communities in Bay and Bakool allows us to continuously study the monetary status in view of per person purchasing power helps us to design the voucher-value and understand the changing nature of livelihoods and quality of life among IDPs, Refugees, Returnees, Persons with Disabilities, the Minorities, Women, Young Girls and the Most Poor affected by climate and environmental changes.
