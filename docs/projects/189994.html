1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Objectives: follow-up and follow-up on cases of GBV, sexual abuse and rape against women and girls.Target group - women and girls of fertil age from 14 years of age onwards.Main activities:Psychosocial support for GBV survivors women and girls.Follow-up of GBV cases through MULEIDE's legal sector in partnership with the provincial attorney general's office.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Chiure- Sensibilization, referral and follow-up of GBV and PSEA cases.Montepuez- Sensibilization, referral and follow-up of GBV and PSEA cases.Namuno- distribution of women dignity kits, Mecufi- Sensibilization, referral and follow-up of GBV and PSEA cases.Metuge- Sensibilization, referral and follow-up of GBV and PSEA cases.Palma- distribution of women dignity kits, Sensitization, referral and follow-up of GBV and PSEA cases.Cidade de Pemba- Sensibilization, referral and follow-up of GBV and PSEA cases.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

5.1.1. Insecurity in Cabo Delgado province due to violent extremism - to mitigate this risk, Muleide will effectively participate in protection cluster meetings to obtain information about security in the province. 5.1.2. The climatic changes that occur in the province - where there may be obstruction of access roads, Muleide accepts the possibility of this risk occurring, and will monitor in order to take measures to prevent it.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Cabo Delgado Province constitutes the risk factors for climate change (cyclical floods and droughts) and insurgent attacks during project implementation, causing the increase of natural disasters in the country with greater risk and vulnerability to floods and prolonged droughts. impact, recording mainly effects related to the unavailability of water, food and environmental sanitation. On the other hand, the military instability in Cabo Delgado, increased the vulnerability of the populations, with a greater incidence for women, girls and children were affected.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

All Muleide activities are inclusive, Muleide through partnerships with AIFO, Humanity and Inclusion received training on how to work and deal with people with disabilities and thus learn to identify and eliminate any barriers that may hinder access to assistance.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Despite the Government-led and coordinated humanitarian response system through the National Institute for Disaster Management (INGC) supported by emergency coordination mechanisms (Humanitarian Country Team, HCT), the level of foresight, information, communication, response and/or resilience it is worrying considering the economic vulnerability of most citizens and the inefficiency of the ecological and climate-resilient infrastructure concept in housing policy. In addition, specialized institutions still have difficulties in responding to the needs of Women and Girls in emergency situations,Against this backdrop, Muleide will propose partnerships to tailor interventions according to the priorities of the target group in emergency areas. There will also be mobilization of funds/resources for emergency response for women and girls between Muleide and partners.
