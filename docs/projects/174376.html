Project Summary

Pakistan is one of the signatories of the sustainable development goals, where the SDG#5 invites member countries to ensure gender mainstreaming in every arena of the life. However, the gender parity gap in widening in Pakistan with every passing day. According to last year's UNDP report, Pakistan was among bottom 10 countries and deteriorated its position by four points.
The increasing poverty, illiteracy, intolerance, and lack of enactment of laws poses great challenge to the situation of women in Pakistan.
In 2020, world has seen major disruptions due to the COVID - 19 pandemic. On one hand it created massive health emergency on the other hand it affected education, livelihood and social cohesion in the communities. Due to the side effects of poverty, diseases and immobility, the extreme rise in gender based violence cases have been witnessed.
In central part of Sindh province, the torrential rains and river fed flood added to the miseries of the people. Here again, adolescents, women and children were the most vulnerable segment of the society.
Due to flood related displacements in Dadu districts there are tens of thousands of women who lost their homes, livelihood and also dignity as they were compelled to either sleep hungry or beg or in the worst scenario, face sexual exploitation in exchange of food and shelter.
Though, inefficient system didn't facilitate majority of the women to register their concerns related to gender based violence, still an alarming rise can be seen in GBV related cases, especially toward adolescents, middle aged adult in Dadu district.
Development and Empowerment Society (DES) aims to engaged adolescents, young girls and women (either affected or at risk) and provide them awareness raising knowledge and advocacy skills so they can prevent, report, response, and redress the incidents related to violence, exploitation and abuse.
DES also intend to strengthened community level and district based protection system by providing the capacity building, technical assistance, coordination and institutional support so those institutes can response to such incidents under the system based continuous approach instead of issue based temporary approach.
