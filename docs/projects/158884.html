Project Summary

This is a 12-month responsive project, which aims to increase the accessibility, and quality of life of any 300 physically disabled people including the GMR 2018 injured, and older people (40% female, 60% male) in the north Gaza Governorate and Gaza Governorate, as well as their families. This project will respond mainly to the essential health needs of the target group through holistic rehabilitation activities, which include provision of assistive devices and diapers also provision of rehabilitation services including physiotherapy (PT), occupational therapy (OT), speech therapy (ST), psycho-social support (PSS), and home care nursing. The project also will provide nursing care for those who suffer from diabetic foot, Bedsores and the GMR 2018 injured people. Especial training will provided for the caregivers on how to deal with the consequences of pathological conditions of older people, and complications of injuries to reduce it. The project target two areas, the Gaza Governorate and the North Gaza governorate. the project target 300 people of physically disabled people including the GMR 2018 injured, and older people, and their families.

1 - Needs

The situation in Palestine in general, and in the Gaza Strip has been worsening dramatically than before two decades for external reasons including the repeatedly Israeli aggression, a 10-years strict blockade, backwardness of funding civic organizations, and USA’s decision to stop funding UNRWA, which supports 70% of refugee population in the Gaza Strip, and for internal factors related to the political split, high rate of poverty, unemployment which raised the rate of dependency on UNRWA to 80% as reported by AIDA on Feb, 8th. 2018.This has resulted in the extreme difficulties of accessing quality to essential health and nutrition services and serious shortages of multidisciplinary rehabilitation services for the physically disabled people, including children, youth, adults, and old people. It increased the negative conditions of the physical disability, undermined the activities, participation, and mobility of the physically disabled people, and On the other hand, the acute shortage of the availability of assistive devices caused them serious health complications and made them more Introverted in society, in addition to their inability to live a normal life. PCBS (2018) indicated that the total percentage of older people aged 60 and overreached 4.3.% in Gaza Strip (4.6% M against 5.4% F), about 45.7% of older people in Gaza have disability, 30.6% with mobility difficulties. Baitona Society conducted a needs assessment survey for 300 samples of older people and physically disabled people in 2018. The main findings were: (1) 100% of their caregivers needed for training. This is needed to change the negative attitudes of families against physical disability and the elderly stage and protection of human rights including independence and decent life. (2) 70% needed for assistive devices, and 30% of them needed diapers. This is required to give the beneficiaries more access, benefit from services facilities, and increase their social participation. (3) 76% needed for rehabilitation sessions (PT, OT, ST, and PSS). This is needed to respond to essential health problems, improving their mobility, alleviating the economic cost over their families, and improving their psychosocial condition. (4) 30% needed for nursing especially for those who suffer from diabetic foot and bedsores. This is very important to decrease the possibility of having an amputation, and the consequences of infections as well as negative psychological effects. Help Age International (HAI) 2016, reported 90% of older people had not taken part in any psychosocial intervention, while 20.7% suffer from depression and 19.8% suffer from anxiety. The same report added, neglect of the older generation is apparent in the absence of any effective national policies or programs tailored to meet the special needs of this sector, or even minimal protection of basic rights to safeguard health, well-being, and dignity.On other hand, WHO estimates that at least 1,200 of “GMR 2018 injured who sustained limb injuries will require 12 to 30 physiotherapy sessions. The Baitona staff, who worked in rehabilitation and healing the GMR injured people project with a partnership of HI. They indicated that many of the GMR injured need for long-term rehabilitation. This is required to give the injured people more access to rehabilitation services such as wound dressing, multi-disciplinary rehabilitation, and assistive devices to avoid any advanced effects that may lead to amputations. On the other hand, the “HI” with its partners reported that the people with disabilities in the Gaza strip still suffer from lack of rehabilitation services, especially in the most vulnerable areas. It was due to several causes such as the insufficient rehabilitation services of MOH, limited access to community-based rehabilitation services for people with physical disabilities and older people in NGOs sector, weakness of referral processing, and the poor experience of the caregivers.

2 - Activities

Activity 1: conducting one orientation session for the staff regarding the physically disabled people and older people's specific needs.Activity 2: conducting a needs assessment survey for 300 physical disabled people and older people (50% Female & 50% male) through 300 home visits during the second month of the project.Activity 3: Provision of assistive devices (wheelchairs, walkers, canes, hearing aids, and disposable and screening diapers) to targeted 235 physical disabled and older people (50% Female & 50% male), based on the need assessment survey.Activity 4: Provision outreach sessions of physiotherapy, speech therapy, and occupational therapy for 300physically disabled people and older people (50% Female & 50% male), based on the need assessment survey.Activity 5: provision nursing care for 100 of physically disabled people and older people (50% Female & 50% male), based on the need assessment survey.Activity 6: distributing nursing kits for 30% of physically disabled people and older people.Activity 7: Provide 600 caregivers (50% Female & 50% male) of physically disabled people and olderpeople and/or cares field (home) training on home care, and the importance of wellbeing and mobility. Activity 8: Provide 300 physical disabled people and older people (50% Female & 50% male) with individual psychosocial support sessions using evidence-based approach (psychoeducational approached) Activity 9: Provide 100 physical disabled people and older people (50% Female & 50% male) with group psychosocial support sessions using evidence-based approach (psychodrama approach)Activity 10: Provide 100 physical disabled people and older people (50% Female & 50% male) with four recreational tripsActivity 11: referring 20% of total Beneficiaries to other health services providers.
