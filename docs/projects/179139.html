Project Summary

DRC intervention includes both lives–saving WASH assistance and more durable and equitable solutions such as maintenance and light rehabilitation of existing WASH systems. Interventions will particularly be focused on preventing the potential spread of covid-19 in all areas.
The conflict-affected population is assisted in compliance with the Sector and MoLWR standards. DRC aims to address overburdened communities with a high ratio of returnees and IDPs in the rehabilitation of sewage and water systems. A total of 185,000 beneficiaries and 3700 PWDs will be reached out in different locations with a 3 and above severity scale.DRC will achieve this through the following outcome:
1. Newly displaced IDP s and returnees living in life-threatening emergency conditions and/or impacted by covid-19 have access to basic WASH assistance in Idleb, Aleppo, Dara'a, Rural Damascus, and Deir Ez Zor. DRC will prevent, mitigate, and respond to risks aggravated by the COVID- 19 pandemic, contributing to a strengthened protective environment for conflict and displacement affected individuals that are facing a sudden onset, a recent emergency that gives rise to an urgent WASH risk that they are unable to overcome.
DRC will respond to emergencies where sudden population movements cause conflict-affected populations to be living in conditions posing immediate life-threatening risks as well as address needs arising due to the covid-19 pandemic.
IDPs assistance follows WASH Sector Standards will be provided in collective shelters, and private unfinished/demolished buildings in both, urban and rural areas. Newly displaced populations get prioritized as an emergency response including COVID 19 response, according to the Sector’s directive. Prioritization is set on water supply, water storage, and provision of sanitary facilities also considering the additional needs of disabled populations. The intervention will concentrate on areas where DRC has a current presence.The Identified locations are in huge need of borehole rehab, pump stations, clean water networks, and sewage network rehab for the long term, and during emergencies, the response will be water tanks, water trucking HK distribution, and hygiene promotion.

4 - Needs

The scale, severity, and complexity of needs across Syria remain overwhelming. Entering its tenth year, the impact of present and past hostilities on civilians remains the principal driver of humanitarian needs in Syria compounded by the covid-19 pandemic and increasing financial hardships.According to HNO 2021. And comprehensive WASH assessments that were conducted in mid-2020 by the Sector, 12.2M people need WASH assistance in Syria, the identification of the actual needs is undertaken in the potential project location after funding is secured. Major wash identified needs for newly displaced populations and IDPs living in IDP sites are related to access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management, and/or hygiene supplies. IDPs informal sites and informal settlements are highly dependent on continuous humanitarian assistance, specifically women and girls. The WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria especially in rural areas require significant repair and operational support. WASH systems have suffered from damages owing to years of functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, destructions, displacement of technical staff, and effects of poor water resources management. 45 percent of the population is relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs. At least 85 percent of sewage is discharged untreated while at least half of the sewage systems are not functional. Such factors pose significant public health risks and have negative cascade effects on communities in terms of public health, inequality, and poverty.

5 - Implementation of activities

DRC will provide emergency WASH assistance activities to vulnerable communities which will include the distribution of water tanks and hygiene kits to beneficiaries who have access to or intermittent access to water, in need of water storage containers, etc. as well as basic rehabilitation of water networks inclusive of boreholes. Generators for the water networks that will be rehabilitated will be provided in coordination with local authorities and other actors in the area such as ICRC and UNICEF. Implementation will be done by DRC directly through selected suppliers in line with DRC’s global procurement rules. DRC field staff involved in the monitoring and implementation will be equipped with PPE, including masks, gloves, and hand sanitizer. DRC staff received guidance on WHO key messages related to what COVID-19 is, how people can protect themselves, etc., and will communicate these messages to beneficiaries to increase community-level awareness. The evaluation of the outcome of the final executed work is taken over by the DRC internal MEAL department in conducting a satisfactory survey and other monitoring processes involving different stakeholders.The Identified locations are in huge need of borehole rehab, pump stations, clean water networks, and sewage network rehab for the long term, and during emergencies, the response will be water tanks, water trucking HK distribution, and hygiene promotion. Participation with the WASH sector is made on 2 levels: subsector meeting on the level of Aleppo and Damascus, and coordination on the second level with SARC & MoWR.DRC is continuously reporting on a monthly basis to the sector lead on the 4 Ws.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1.Increased risk of inter or intra-community tension due to varied assistance packages, unclear beneficiary selection criteria, or poorly timed distributions leading to a feeling of discrimination. LH: HIGH, IMPACT: MIDUM, Mitigating Measure: Ensure beneficiary selection criteria are uniform/agreed upon among sector members and with the government and are communicated widely within a community and amongst different groups. Engage with community leaders, to raise awareness in the community. Establish complaint mechanisms at the community level.2.The population targeted by the intervention is put at risk as a result of the location of the facility; lack of consideration for the accessibility to services (in terms of freedom of movement, safety, and security for men, women, boys, girls, elderly persons with disabilities); Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigation: Consider mobility patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing a static facility or a mobile service provision or combine both. Engage with local authorities on safety and security in the community. Engage with diverse members of the community on safety and security in the community (to ensure diverse perspectives and perceptions are considered).3.Different vulnerable groups might not have the same access to services and assistance. Likelihood: High, Impact: High. Mitigation: Coordinate at the local level with other protection actors to ensure balanced coverage responding to needs; avoid overlapping or concentration of activities if possible and unless motivated by needs; consider focusing on underserved areas.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Non-availability of good performing contractors - Maintaining a good relationship to good performing contractors, contractor evaluation for procurement processes 2. Expanding sanctions of key construction material to Syria - Discussing the new development within the TWG of both Sectors 3. The long approval process for work execution - Considering the long approval process in the work plan, advocacy, and awareness-raising in the sector coordination mechanism, maintaining a good relationship with the ministries in the field 4. Increasing inflation and high depreciation of Syrian Pound with consequent increase of prices in the market. Contractors may be affected by the constant increase of price and withdraw from their contractual obligation, creating a delay in the implementation of the activities. 5. Non-acceptation of DRC delivered work by beneficiaries - Remaining in compliance with Sector’s guidelines, informing in advance local authorities and beneficiaries on the planned intervention, referring to MEAL findings of previous similar projects, feedback mechanism.
