Project Summary

We plan to implement Water, sanitation and hygiene activities for IDPs in Haj Ali camp in south of Mosul. the activities will include monitoring the provision of water, organizing the water distribution, water quality, operating and maintenance of Sanitation facilities and daily cleaning for it, desludging, conduct hygiene promotions sessions and distribute of hygiene kits there. the activities will be for twelve months with total amount about (500,000) USD.

Needs

December 2017 marked the end of major military operations in Iraq against the so-called Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant (ISIL). While more than 4 million returns have been recorded as of September 2018, almost 2 million internally displaced persons (IDPs) remain, of whom 71% reside outside of formal camps. Moreover, secondary displacement and new arrivals to formal camps signal the tenuous nature of some returns.Till the ending of October; there are about (1,866,594) IDPs; (30%) from them in formal camps living mainly in Nineveh, Salahaddin, Kirkuk and Anbar provinces. According to last Multi-Cluster Needs Assessment (MCNA) report that issued by REACH, there are 65% of household from IDPs in the camps in need for WASH assistance, only (37%) from IDPs in the camps are reach to at least 50 litre/day/person and the remain percentage access to less than this quantity per day. 42% of households reported communal access to the network as their primary source of drinking water and (35%) of households with access to a private latrine.

Activities

A) Water sector:1. Operating and Maintenance of Water treatment plant inside the camps and water pipeline network.2. Water test quality and organizing the water provision in the camps. B) Sanitation Sector:1. Operating and maintenance of Sanitation facilities (Latrines and showers) 2. Dislodging for the cesspools and maintenance of cesspools there.3. Garbage collecting and treatment in the camps. C) Hygiene Sector:1. Hygiene Promotion for IDPs.2. Distributing Hygiene consumable items for families in the camps according to the need.

Priority / Category

Critical

Protection Risk Analysis

The potential protection risks are exposure of women to harassing during use of latrines, especially at night, as well as being harassed while taking water or queuing for a register or receive of hygiene kit.

Mitigation Measures

Our organization will allocate a special line for women during the distribution of hygiene items and not mixing with men, also we will work with the camp management to ensure that there is enough light in the toilets at night and to ensure that there are latrines for only women inside the camps in order to prevent any attempt to harass them, especially at night. While they are harassed while accessing water, we can also be allocated water point inside the camps for women.Our organization is working to remove the armed people who are there to guard the camp against getting close to the beneficiaries while using the services we provide, as well as continuing coordination with the Protection Working Groups in Mosul and Erbil in order to receive any situation regarding the protection risks in the provision of our services.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Accountability to affected populations will be strengthened by coordination with the WASH Cluster through the following actions:• Use community-based resources as much as possible and in roles where they can affect decisions—such as WASH committees that will form by this project and community-based monitoring groups.• In parallel to the enhanced promotion of the IDP Information Center, local complementary complaint mechanisms based on the preferred community communication means and site-specific information will be piloted.
