Project Summary

WSA runs three WGSS in Hazano, KafrTakarim, and Kafrlucin in Idleb, since 2018, and planning to continue implementing activities through providing protection services for women and girls affected by the crisis, including GBV survivors, persons affected by war injuries and mild physical disabilities.
Hazano WGSS is funded through HF, and it started on1-7-2020 and counting until 30-6-2021.and contributes to the promotion of services and measures of MA and quality GBV providing services to prevent and reduce the risks of GBV, ERW, and COVID in the mentioned location, with the GBV SC & MA SC Guidelines. For this WGSS we are proposing to continue providing specialized GBV services, Case Management, and specialized individual PSS. In addition to providing direct support service and referrals to other services according to the referral paths that are shared by the sub-clusters and creating a service mapping through coordination with actors in the area, continuously updated. Preventive activities, such as life skills (My safety my wellbeing), recreational activities, GBV awareness-raising (GBV-SC toolkit), and Covid related awareness(WHO tools
For MA activities the associated mobile team will continue to support victims of ERW accidents, affected by war injuries and mild physical disabilities. Refer cases/victims in need of individual assistance (VA) Victims’ assistance.
The other two WGSSs in KafrTakarim and Kaferlucin funded by UNFPA, on 1-1-2021 and continue until 31-12-2021 provides life-saving response services in addition to prevention services for women and adolescent girls within the WGSSs in line with the current response to the COVID-19 pandemic. different types of GBV servicers including both response and prevention services are provided. MA services are funded through the same HF fund that covers Hazzano WGSS and it includes RE and Victim Data Collection (VDC)
The newly planned project will start from 1-7-2021and continue until 30-6-2022 new activities will be added in the three proposed WGSS with the aim to enhance the protection and empowerment of women and girls, namely:
1-conducting vocational trainings for W&G, such as hairdressing, sewing, Microsoft Office programs, and ..etc. based on needs,400 W&G will be targeted in the three sites
2-Providing Individual Protection Assistance (IPA) to vulnerable women and girls According to specific criteria total of 100$
3 - 5 widow camps will be targeted to provide a package of life-saving services to empower adolescent girls and their mothers including safety audit activity to assess and address risk factors related to protecting women and girls from GBV in camps.
Awareness sessions on GBV
Life skills programs
Connecting male children to services according to referral pathways to child protection.
Services will be provided to widows within the WGSSs or activities halls in camps, according to the situation of each camp and the type of the service
In case of an emergency situation, WSA will use the Protection Cluster Emergency Response Package to Provide emergency response for W&G: PFA, referrals, registration, and Victim Data collection VDC for ERW victims.
4-women and girls in WGSSs and camps linking them to small grants As per specific criteria, on average 850$.per case.
Capacity-building activities will include training and refresher sessions on PSEA to all WSA staff in the three locations. Training for non-specialized GBV service providers in Hazzano (training in the two other locations are covered under UNFPA fund), on GBV basic concepts in accordance with SC GBV standards.
WSA will implement GBV prevention activities, GBV awareness-raising activities (GBV-SC toolkit), prevention of SEA for Women, Men, Boys, and Girls, psychosocial first aid, life skills activities, and advocacy campaigns for women's rights, as well as COVID 19 information sessions in nine Communities in(Ariha and Jisr-Ash-Shugur districts) in Idleb integrated with agricultural activities in one project.

4 - Needs

According to the HNO draft 2020: W&G, in particular, are exposed to various forms of GBV, including rape, sexual harassment, and abuse in many areas." Voices from Syria 2020 report indicated that: "the consequences of GBV on W&G are manifold and are often interlinked. A woman who has experienced sexual violence will most likely have experienced physical, verbal, and emotional violence.Widows, divorced women, older women, and women with disabilities are the most vulnerable to the risks of GBV. There has also been an increase in cases of sexual assault, during the arrest and as a method of torture inside prisons and detention centers for women, girls, men, and boys, in addition to the emergence of new types of violence, such as incest, forced puberty, forced pregnancy, and armed recruitment of girls, as well as the use of technology for sexual harassment, which eventually leads to violence and sexual exploitation.According to the last WSA MA needs assessment, there is a high spread of ERW in the agricultural lands 46% and roads 34%, and private property and public buildings. One third of populated communities is estimated to be contaminated by explosive ordnance. 10.3 million people, approximately 1 in 2 Syrians, live in areas affected by explosive ordnance.Men and boys are considered to be the most exposed to the direct, immediate threat of explosive incidentsCOVID-19 Pandemic and the risks of its spread among displaced people in NW Syria have increased the concern about the potentially catastrophic impact on displaced women and girls. The fear and tension increase the risk of violence against women and girls, especially domestic violence and intimate partner violence, due to severe stress in the family.

The activities will implement with Women over 18 years old and adolescent girls in addition to children of women beneficiaries and as for young boys and older men WSA will target them outside the safe spaces in outreach activities. The outreach teams will work on the identification of case which needs case fund and the cases will be evaluated, verified and then receive the support.Specialized RE teams will deliver the planned activities through teams of well-trained staff, all activities will be accordance with the GBV SC SOPs, Awareness Toolkit, MEAL Staff in HQ are in the day by day contact and follow-up with the MEAL Assistants in Syria and will design and adapt the monitoring tools according to GBV M&E Toolkit, The MEAL Assistants will conduct regular site visits for verification and documentation of project implementation progress or remotely interview with beneficiaries due to COVID19During this project, WSA will be accountable to affected populations through open dialogue, training for local partners, and providing feedback mechanisms. At the beginning of the project, WSA will introduce the project to the target communities, including goals, outcomes, and any eligibility requirements to participate. WSA has instituted feedback/complaints mechanism, including ensuring that feedback-providing tools will be available in each of the centersRegarding PSEA, WSA is a focal point for the PSEA IA network in northwest Syria, and all our field staff have been oriented remotely and signed on PSEA CoCWSA will use GBV Ethical Closure Planning that shared with GBV SC previously in cases of temporary suspension and relocation, in addition to the permanent closure or reallocation of locations

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Assessments indicated that GBV continues to pervade the lives of women and girls in Syria inside and outside the home, resulting in very few spaces where women and girls feel safe. Women (15 – 49) and children under 5 make up more than 35% of the affected population. The socioeconomic situation, lack of livelihood opportunities, and increased poverty are ultimately exposing W&G to increased risks of vulnerability due to lack of access to basic services. Evidence suggests that elderly may be isolated, leading to concerns about their physical and mental well-being.Widows, divorced women, elderly, and women with disabilities are the most vulnerable to the risks of GBV.There has also been an increase in cases of sexual assault,during the arrest and as a method of torture inside prisons and detention centers for women, girls, men, and boys, in addition to the emergence of new types of violence, such as incest, forced puberty, forced pregnancy, and armed recruitment of girls, as well as the use of technology for sexual harassment, which eventually leads to violence and sexual exploitation.COVID-19 Pandemic and the risks of its spread among displaced people in NW Syria have increased the concern about the potentially catastrophic impact on displaced women and girls. The fear and tension increase the risk of violence against women and girls, especially domestic violence and intimate partner violence, due to severe stress in the family.One third of populated communities is estimated to be contaminated by explosive ordnance. 10.3 million people, approximately 1 in 2 Syrians, live in areas affected by explosive ordnance.Men and boys are considered to be the most exposed to the direct, immediate threat of explosive incidents

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption 1:Movement restriction to basic services due to the high increase in the number of COVID-19 cases and the high level of risk. Mitigation: Providing remote response services.Assumption 2: Safety and health of GBV service providers during COVID-19 pandemic.Mitigation: -Prioritize the safety and well-being of staff, specifically those who provide services to survivors of gender-based violence.- Ensure the continued regular supervision of employees and the allocation of personnel to shifts in order to take into account changes in responsibilities.Self-care strategies as part of programs and human resource policy in the organization.Assumption 3:Some beneficiaries were exposed to sexual exploitation and abuse by WSA staff. Mitigation -raise awareness among the beneficiaries about complaints mechanisms regularly and will verify that all employees fully understand and signed on PSEA CoC.Assumption 4:Persons with disabilities, the elderly, and widows have difficulty accessing to WGSSs. Mitigation Measure:- Provide transportation support for them.- Mobile teams will be allocated to reach beneficiaries who cannot access the WGSSs to participate in activities.Assumption 5:Lack of support from family members to allow women and girls to travel to WGSS and access services.Mitigation: Ensure the support of male family members by engaging them in awareness-raising activities.Assumption 6 :Preventing women, girls, and PWDs from accessing services within the widow's camps. Mitigation:Ensuring the provision of services for family members and camp management during the implementation of the project activities and in particular, awareness activities and advocacy campaigns.
