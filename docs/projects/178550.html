Project Summary

The project works to Installation 800 of transitional shelter (harder material/locally built shelter) for the 800 families already lives in tents ( 880 fimale, 760 male, 1120 girls, 1240 boys) displaced families in Bennash (Tom) area, Afrin, Azaz, Dabeq
130 of them were completed in Tom camp and provided with the basic needs of the
displaced families
The Cluster recommendations will be taken into consideration for Covid-19 through preventive measures, communication and implementation
Train stakeholders in flexibility and capacities in providing quality-oriented shelter/nonfood skills.
The project was partially secured, and the rest was secured through ongoing campaign

4 - Needs

According to HNO March 2021In 2020, the number of people requiring shelter assistanceremained substantial, with 5.88 million people in need.It is estimated that at least 31 per cent of the population live in inadequate shelter conditions, At least 1.95 million IDPs are residing in last-resortsites in the north-west, north-east and the south. 30 per cent either live in camps or tents, are hosted byothers or have informal occupancy arrangements. A sustainableshelter solution is yet to be found for those displaced, howevercontinuous support remains crucial to maintain the dignity,health and safety of those displaced. Infrastructure upgrades,provision of emergency tents, NFIs and winterization of existingshelters remain vital support And through the needs assessment conducted by our organization, and it was proven that a group of tents existed in the Dabiq area and that they were deplorable to him, so I decided to replace these tents with housing made of reinforced blocks. In the next stage, we will move to the Afrin region, knowing that we have nished building 130 shelters in Toum camp and currently we have nished 30 shelters in Dabiq
