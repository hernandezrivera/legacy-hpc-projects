1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to carry out family tracing and reunification and alternative care and Identification or establishment of referral pathways for age- and gender-sensitive child protection in specific places to refer children in need of specialised services;Integration of child protection and case management (child protection), mainly CAFAAG and its integration into the community;Providing structured psychosocial support programmes through group activities or recreational games, holding community awareness sessions to prevent child protection risks in the targeted locations in order to strengthen the child protection system to better cope with future shocks.Create and support a community child protection structureMonitoring child protection Strengthening national and local capacities to provide sustainable child protection services Parenting programmes.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

As mentioned above, the project will benefit 15,000 people in the districts of Ancuabe and Macomia, among whom 9,000 women are expected to be assisted, equivalent to 45 per cent of the entire project, 50 per cent corresponds to the total number of men and 5 per cent are children of both sexes and people with disabilities of both sexes.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Caritas is committed to integrating protection and gender into all programmes to promote safety, dignity and meaningful access, especially for those who may be most vulnerable and marginalised, including individuals with disabilities and the elderly. Caritas applies a framework to support the integration of protection principles into humanitarian activities. The project will be implemented based on the Global Protection Cluster approach and the principles of ensuring access to impartial assistance and services, especially for the most vulnerable and at-risk groups; accountability to beneficiaries; supporting the development of self-protection capacities; and ensuring "do not harm" by adapting activities to mitigate identified protection risks. Finally, during a monitoring visit, beneficiaries will be consulted on the degree of satisfaction and will be informed to donors and local government.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Concerning the PSEA, affected populations will be consulted on both the shelter design and the shelter repair package, including women and girls and people with disabilities, who constitute the most vulnerable group. During the selection process, Caritas will facilitate intergenerational discussions between female staff members and potential women and girls beneficiaries to solicit their input on shelter options that best guarantee safety, dignity and privacy. Prior to any activity, Caritas will disseminate key information about PSEA, complaints mechanisms, the hotline and information requests.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As it is considered an inclusive project, people in need will be the focus of Caritas, including the elderly to identify specific needs for infrastructure adaptation. The infrastructure will be adapted to these needs. Persons with disabilities will be one of the priorities during distributions. Distribution points will be serviced, ensuring ease of access for community members, especially people with disabilities and women. If there are any people with disabilities and if they have some leadership character and ability to do some work, such as registering, organizing beneficiaries, writing reports, they can be included in the field team.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is the construction and repair of shelters, which aims to deliver tarpaulins, construction material, in short, the entire shelter kit, depending on the situation of vulnerability. Field agents will be trained to explain to local target communities how to keep the environment clean, avoiding littering in inappropriate places, as well as promoting the use of latrines to avoid infection of the environment. Avoiding digging big holes, throwing garbage anywhere and promoting debates to ensure the well-being of society as a whole.
