Project Summary

In 2024, 16.7 million people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced.
After 12 years of hostilities and persistent economic crisis, people in
Syria their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit northern Syria. As Syrians grappled with these shocks, economic indicators
took a further downturn, increases in commodity prices continued to
drive humanitarian needs in Syria. This resulted in an increase
in poverty and reliance on humanitarian assistance (HNO, 2024)
This project aims to increase the food security and enhancing the livelihoods of 1500 HHs of IDPs and host communities in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb Governorate
through following activities:
1- wheat value chain activities:
ONSUR will provide agricultural inputs for 10 donum of wheat for 600 HHS of crop farmers in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb according to specific selection criteria (The eligibility and vulnerability criteria). Additionally, ONSUR will hire agronomists to follow the growth and life cycle of the wheat crop
ONSUR will support wheat marketing by purchasing 1 ton of wheat from each supported farmers
ONSUR will contract with mill to grind 450 ton of wheat the purchased wheat.
- ONSUR will distribute free bread bundles to IDPs inside camps.
- ONSUR will contract with sifter to sift and sterilize the rest of purchased wheat seed (150 ton ) to produce135 ton of clean-good seeds to Distributed for other 600 wheat farmers in same location in next season.
2-Support olives value chain activities:
- ONSUR will distribute agricultural olive agricultural inputs in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb for 600 of olives farmers according eligibility and vulnerability criteria
Additionally, ONSUR will contract with seasonal workers from IDPs by cash for work activities to harvest and prune olives for 600 HHs.
ONSUR will contract with presses in project area to extract the oil of olive of HHs.
ONSUR will purchase 1 cans (16 kg of olive oil) from each supported farmer
ONSUR will distribute olive oil for IDPs inside camps
3- support vegetables value chain farmers:
The project will target 300 vulnerable Vegetables farmers in Western rural of Aleppo and Idleb governorate according to specific selection criteria (The eligibility and vulnerability criteria).
ONSUR will provide agricultural inputs (seedling, fertilizer, and pesticide) vouchers at the right time for cultivation 1 dunum . Furthermore, ONSUR will hire two agronomist to follow-up the stages of the Vegetables production cycle in beneficiaries’ field
ONSUR will buy 500 Kg of Vegetables crops from targeted farmers to support their marketing.
Also, ONSUR will create small women vegetables processing group:
30 women beneficiaries will be selected based on the vulnerability criteria by beneficiaries scoring system.
- Each group will receive a set of necessary tools and 10 ton of Vegetables
-Each women group will be trained for Vegetables Processing, Packaging, branding and
marketing.
ONSUR will strongly committed to integrating a gender-sensitive approach through all stages of project management.
ONSUR has zero tolerance for any kind of abuse and exploitation against women, girls, and children/ by the project beginning, ONSUR will conducted PSEA training for field staff inside Syria.
All ONSUR Staff will attend the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA)
ONSUR will be committed to the FSL cluster and protection cluster recommendations will be taken into consideration to avoid any GBV/PSEA risk.
The S&S department will follows up on any development in the project area, conducts a daily risk assessment of the area, and informs the administration of any possible scenarios and how to deal with them, including evacuation and contingency plans and training the field staff on all expected scenarios.
