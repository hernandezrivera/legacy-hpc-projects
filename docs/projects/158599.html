Project Summary

• Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level.
• Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in long-term resettlement and returning areas (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households).
• Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media.
• Community and household level hygiene promotion activities in return communities, and related institutions.
• Procurement and distribution of WASH related non-food items, with focus in most vulnerable families / households.
