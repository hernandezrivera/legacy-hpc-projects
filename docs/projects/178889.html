Project Summary

This project is a multi-pronged approach, serving at both individual and community levels in both basic sectors, Livelihood Support for the target beneficiaries and Early Recovery by stimulating the economic growth of the target communities.
The project is going to be implemented within 12 months to achieve:
The Overall Objective is To reform and strengthen the performance of the local economic system and scaling-up the community resilience in NW Syria.
Takaful will work to achieve this objective through four main interventions:
1st: Providing a grant of 1,000$ to 700 beneficiaries to establish new business projects or repair or resume existing ones that are heavily affected by the war or the current Covid-19 pandemic.
Initial beneficiaries (M/F), women-headed households, PWDs, and other vulnerable segments as families having child-labour are a priority, to be targeted, where 2-day sessions, be conducted to developing the Business Plans "BPs" and applying to the grant, and then, Takaful team be evaluating the submitted proposals, select the best projects to be supported, and identify the final beneficiaries of the grants.
Afterwards, providing to the finally selected beneficiaries a 5-day Training Program on Entrepreneurship Skills to build their capacities in managing and sustaining their businesses.
The grant is to be provided in the form of CASH for assets and into several payments to ensure that the beneficiary is committing, achieving good progress, and be businesslike to receive the next payments. Additionally, TAS, to provide 200$ to the most successful 70 projects, (representing10% of BNFs), as additional monetary bonuses, be given based on hard work and noticeable progress.
2nd: Providing Vocational Training (VT) for 300 male and female and PWDs, paid for three months, and then, providing Internship Training within the private sector, also paid for 3 months, to link them to the market.
The appropriate professions and training are according to two groups:
1. The professions do not require any qualifications e.g. Hairdressing and Sewing.
2. The professions require relevant experience e.g. Computer Maintenance and Web Development.
Three months paid internship are to be provided by contracting with business owners in the private sector, who are willing to accommodate the trainees and provide them with the Internship, and TAS will provide allowances to the beneficiaries for three months of VT and three months of Internship, $120 a month (totalling $360), which is empowering the beneficiary to gain the trust of the employer and gain the practical experience.
3rd: Improving community access to markets for 63274 (M, F) through repairing and paving three main roads leading to markets (totalling 8km in Dana, Salqin, Jindires, and Azaz sub-districts), be contracting with specialized contractors according to detailed technical plans with the direct supervision of TAS engineering team.
4th: Supporting Community Initiatives through CFW modality, to adorn and improve 8 trading roads which facilitate people's access to the markets within the target four sub-districts (totalling 8 km, 2 in each) mindset supporting 300 (males and females) with short-term employment opportunities for 44 days, to working in cleaning, rubble removal, and painting sidewalks, where each beneficiary be paid 100$ a month (totalling 200$).
Within the continuous Covid-19 pandemic, TAS be supporting and ensure all precautionary measures through disinfecting meeting venues before and after each meeting, distributing masks to attendees, printing guiding instructions and caution signs everywhere, and also, selecting suitable venues that be primarily accessible by women and PWDs and having all comfort facilities for both genders.

5 - Implementation of activities

First, TAS to coordinate with ERL Cluster for the planned and actual interventions in the target areas.And mapping all stakeholders, their roles, influences, and potential impacts to set the necessary participatory mechanisms.During implementation, active community mobilization and participation. As to do so, Need assessment is essential.Then, conducting workshops aiming to share information, and signing MoUs outline the roles and responsibilities of all parties and how the LC can support smooth implementation.BNFs selection is based on community mobilization mechanisms and focusing to target vulnerable segments including women, elders, child vulnerability, and PWDs.The beneficiaries are delivered the grant in CASH for Assets modality and be in two phases, half number in each, and monitoring the local market regularly to ensure that Do No Harm if injecting cash in one go, and developing the second phase according to lessons learned to support the most appropriate projects or professions.The VT beneficiaries are delivered vocational training (theoretical and practical) for 3 months within equipped VT centres for the selected professions.Post VT, trainees be connected to the private sector and receive Internship training paid for 3 months to ensure employment and sustainability.Besides, providing Emergency Employment through CFW in rehabilitating market roads to enhance community dialogue and increase initiating with the community.MEAL, to monitor and evaluate this project based on the work plan including logical framework, M&E Plan, Baseline, Endline, PDM, and indicators performance tracking table (IPTT) with disaggregated data by gender, age, and PWDs, all are to evaluate the overall impact of the project.
