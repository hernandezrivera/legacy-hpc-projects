Project Summary

The project overall objective is to improve household livelihood and support livelihood diversification to vulnerable community in Dar Alsalam – block 13, 16 and 17 , Oumbada locality, Khartoum state. The population in these locations are urban poor who come from North Darfur and west Kordofan in 1980s due to desertification and famine.
The population generally faces challenges to cope with the urban poverty due to limited livelihood opportunities . Women in this community face most challenges since they are mainly the household livelihoods providers, serving on as casual workers, petty trading among other limited livelihoods income opportunities.
The current situation in Sudan, economic downturn, outbreak of disease and COVID-19 have had huge impact to livelihood of vulnerable community. The impact of lock-down of Khartoum state during COVID-19 first wave, most vulnerable families, with low incomes, whose income depends on daily work, were affected, most of them are experiencing food shortage.
The project will targets 1,600 families (8,000 individuals, men and women) in Dar Alsalam community block ,13,16 and 17. Selection of individual beneficiaries of the project will be based on the level the needs in terms of food security, socio-economic services, household vulnerability and women headed household. Community consultations including community forums, meetings and the leadership of the villages will be involved in leading the processes
Project interventions include–Training community on Saving and internal Lending community – SILC- modality. Establishment of saving and loan groups in block 13,16 and 17. Provision of small-scale business/start-up kits - income generation projects- Use MPCA-cash. Support in skills development for income generation through training of women on construction of Fuel Efficiency Stoves (FES) and provide them with start-up capital for FES.
The project will monitor through field visits, partners update, and progress report. An internal mid-term review will also form part of the monitoring system and this will also provide an opportunity for the team and communities to reflect and adapt the adaptive monitoring system.

1 - Needs

Sudan FSL HRP / HNO- 2021, stated the Food insecurity and humanitarian situation in Sudan has deteriorated rapidly in the first half of the year 2020. The devastating combination of recurrent climate shocks, conflict, economic downturn, disease outbreaks, exacerbated by the consequences of the COVID-19 pandemic and floods, has driven rising food insecurity levels across the country. Dar Alsalm areas is in west Omdurman is inhabited by vulnerable households who fled famine and destratification North Darfur and west Kordofan in the 1980s. The population generally faces challenges to cope with the urban poverty due to limited livelihood opportunities . Women in this community face most challenges since they are mainly the household livelihoods providers, serving on as casual workers, petty trading among other limited livelihoods income opportunities. According to FSL HNO 2021 total people analysed in Oumbada locality, Khartoum state are 1,614,434, PiN are 242, 201 and people targeted by sector are 88,794. The current lock-down of Khartoum state- which has the highest number of COVI-19 caseload- has had great impact on the vulnerable families in Dar Alsalm community. Most vulnerable families, with low incomes, whose income is depended on daily work, are mainly affected, most of them are experiencing food shortage. Project intervention will target Dar Alsalm communities in block 13,16 and 17 through set of project activities which includes; Training of community field agent on saving and loan to support in establishment of Saving and Internal Lending Community (SILC) groups, undertaking of SILC modality. Support SILC groups/individual with Income Generating Activities (IGA) kits. Support in skills development for income generation through training of women on construction of Fuel Efficiency Stoves (FES) and provide them with start-up capital for FES.
