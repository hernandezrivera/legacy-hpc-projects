Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing timely and appropriate Gender based violence services to 1500 survivors as wells protection services to 650 boys and 980 girls in Bay and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia: The targeted beneficiaries are the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees, refugees, asylum seekers and marginalized host communities that are worst affected by combined effects of drought and serious flooding that has further condemned them to mass displacement where live in deplorable conditions in informal and unplanned settlements under very poor living conditions. Their plight is worsened by increased exposure to violence, exploitation and other forms of abuse coupled by serious risks and threats to their safety and security as well as negative coping strategies that further expose them to rights violation. Of particular concern is the fact that children face multiple protection risks and violations of their rights including family separation, sexual and physical violence, killing and maiming, psychosocial distress, child labor, widespread recruitment and use of children, forced and protracted displacements, limited access to humanitarian assistance due to insecurity and/or discriminatory practices and exploitation hence urgent need for protection services. Further more , persons with disabilities are at heightened risks of exploitation, abuse and discrimination by other marginalized group such as IDPs, where there are numerous cases of stealing of their entitlements, in addition to cases where they are often denied access to water, food, sanitation and accommodation hence strong need for their inclusion in to the project right from needs the commencement of the project till its completion including regular monitoring exercises including putting in place effective feedback and complains platforms. The project activities are: Provision of protection services to all affcted persons including those with disabilities during eviction, conducting eviction monitoring services, provision of counseling as well as medical support for children and GBV survivors, building capacity of case workers, provision of material support and access to essential services for other protection victims and child survivors, Prevention and Response services for unaccompanied and Separated Children, Medical, Psycho-social and legal support to GBV survivors, livelihoods skills training for GBV survivors, Recreational activities for children.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites and ensuring centrality and distribution points of assistance to limit distance covered as well as walking in the cover of darkness by women and girls to limit associated risks.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring beneficiary feedback and complains mechanism in place in addition to all staff signing PSEA code of conduct3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls on importance of their inclusion and participation in decision making processes and the entire project to ensure their active involvement right from the project inception throughout during project implementation including monitoring exercises till completion.5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
