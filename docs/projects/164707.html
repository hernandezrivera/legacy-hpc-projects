Project Summary

In this submission, NGO X is submitting an integrated approach to Shelter and Winter MPCA. This integrated project will address the multiple vulnerabilities that households and communities are facing after eight years of conflict. At the household level, the program design creates links between its multi-donor funded emergency response program and its food security, winterization and shelter programming, and complements food assistance programming with basic needs assistance via Multi-Purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA).
NGO X will enable highly vulnerable IDP, returnee and host communities to meet their winter needs through a coordinated cash-based response. NGO X will select those areas most vulnerable to extreme winter conditions through analysis of the Humanitarian Needs Overview, Multi-Sector Needs Assessments and in close consultation with the Shelter/NFI Cluster. A fuel market assessment and household interviews will then be conducted in order to ascertain the availability of fuel, market access and functionality.
NGO X will aim to complete winter response by the end of December 2020. Cash will be the default response mechanism as long as minimum preconditions for the use of cash exist. The cash transfer value will be based on the fuel cost which will cover HHs needs for the 40 coldest days of the year with an average of 6 liters of fuel per-HH per-day.
NGO X plans to utilize e-cash to reach a selected number of particularly vulnerable beneficiaries under this activity, priority will be given to child headed households, single/widowed women, the elderly and people with disabilities.
NGO X will implement the proposed action in partnership with NGO Z. Please note that NGO Z will input into the OCHA HPC separately under this project. This decision was agreed between both parties and submissions have been coordinated to ensure there is no duplication with regards to either budget or beneficiary targets.
• Winter MPCA, total of 18,000 households living in particularly cold and mountainous areas, will be targeted under this project.
• 300 shelters (300 HHs) will be rehabilitated
• 10 collective centres (community/public infrastructure) (150 HHs) rehabilitated.
A total of 18,450 HHs will benefit from this project. Donor approval is still pending.

4 - Needs

NGO X has identified Shelter and winter needs as one of the top three priority needs in its area of operation (AoO). Due to the security escalation which let to IDP influxes into NGO X’s AoO, NGO X conducted two rapid needs assessments (RNAs) on the 22nd/23rd December, the first RNA was carried out in 6 subdistricts: Idleb, Salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Quorqeen, Armanaz and Mhambal and covered 21 villages. The second RNA was carried out in 8 subdistricts: Idleb, Darkosh, Janudiyeh, Kafr Takharim, Armanaz, Maaret Tamsrin, Qourqeena and Salqin and covered 20 villages. In addition, NGO X conducted an RNA in North Aleppo in December 2019. The assessments used affected people surveys in order to gather data. The RNAs found the following urgent needs:Cash, food and non-food items along with fuel for heating and cooking were the top three priority needs of the affected people. Interviewed beneficiaries indicated that cash is one of their top three priority needs. Based on NGO X’s previous winter response, 74% of HHs reported that they used the cash they received to purchase fuel for heating. In addition, NGO Xs PDM Report which covered July 2019 to September 2019 indicated that 41% of HHs reported spending cash on shelter and rent. Similarly, to previous responses we expect the majority of this cash will be spent on winter related needs and the rest will be spent on rental payment.Based on Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2019, the adequacy of shelter, the ability of people to repair or rehabilitate their residences and the affordability of shelter is deteriorating for both IDPs and host communities. According to the CCCM Cluster, more than 195,229 HHs / 1,021,275 individuals were displaced towards the northern zones of Idleb governorate between Dec 1st 2019 and Feb 2nd, 2020 alone. This project will be implemented in the last quarter of 2020; thus, NGO X will continue conducting needs assessments to inform the Shelter and winter response prior to implementing project

5 - Implementation of activities

Winter MPCA • Prepare and pre-position MPCA ready for distribution • Household selection and verification • Distribute Winter Multipurpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) NGO X will select areas most vulnerable to extreme winter conditions through analysis of the HNO, Multi-Sector Needs Assessments and in close consultation with the Shelter/NFI Cluster. A fuel market assessment and household interviews will then be conducted in order to ascertain the availability of fuel, market access and functionality. Through NGO X’s Vulnerability and Inclusion Criteria, those beneficiaries who are most in need of winterization assistance will be selected. MPCA is the default response modality under this action. (estimated at $ 125 per HH for 18,000 HHs). NGO X’s distribution monitoring teams will carry out distribution monitoring process in all distribution sites. NGO X will conduct PDM following the cash distributions, assessing how beneficiaries spent the cash and whether they had enough cash to cover their fuel and other winter NFI needs. NGO X has a standard PDM form for cash distributions which was adapted for winter. The PDM survey is conducted 4 weeks after the distribution and the sample is drawn using proportional two-stage cluster random sampling methodology. Shelter and Settlements NGO X will target shelters and collective centres (community/public infrastructure) classified as moderately damaged (damage level/grade 2) as per the Guidance on Repairs and Rehabilitation developed by the Shelter Cluster (Annex 40). NGO X will rehabilitate 300 shelters and 10 collective centres (hosting approx. 150 HHs). NGO X will also assess the household’s or collective centers’ access to water and sanitation and minor repairs to water and sanitation facilities will also be eligible. NGO X will adopt a rolling strategy, whereby shelters and settlements will be continuously assessed, the work tendered, contractors selected, and repairs conducted throughout the life of the project
