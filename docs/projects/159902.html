Project Summary

Protracted crisis in Somalia presents unique vulnerabilities to affected populations, and more so for children. The June 2019 Joint Multi Cluster Assessment indicated that in times of crisis children are likely to drop out of school. Chances of enrolling back to school after crisis for these children is unlikely, especially because education is not considered a priority during crisis. Protection concerns also increase in times of crisis as indicated in a gender analysis report conducted in Gedo by Trocaire in September 2019. There is need therefore, for actors like Trocaire to continue providing basic access to education while working with the community to shape long-term solutions to education dropouts and inclusive access constraints. This project will aim to address existing causes of school dropout such as lack of access to inclusive and protective learning environments, while increasing quality of education through equipment of education facilities and capacity building of key education delivery agents. Protection mainstreaming will be ensured by creating a safe learning environment that encourages positive dialogue on inclusivity in schools, tailored service provision especially for girls, and collective monitoring of protection outcomes in schools.
The two approaches to programming will include activities to ensure retention of enrolled children throughout the year, and increased access to education for vulnerable displaced children and children from the host communities. Hence the need for Trocaire to increase coverage of education services from the current 2 districts to 4 districts, while increasing coverage in the existing districts of intervention. To this end, Trocaire, in consultation with teachers, school-going children, local authorities and other education stakeholders will deliver tailored and inclusive education to 1,250 boys, 1,250 girls, and 125 children living with disabilities in a protective learning environment in Belet Hawo, Dollow, Grbaharey and Luqq districts in Gedo. This project is aligned to the 2020 Education Cluster objectives; ensuring that 2,500 emergency and crisis affected children have access to safe and protective learning environment; that these same children are engaged in life-saving learning that promotes well-being and social cohesion, and strengthening the capacity of teachers and Community Education Committees to deliver effective and coordinated education. The project will complement Trocaire and other partners’ ongoing efforts, more specifically addressing the growing gaps within Trocaire’s education programme in Gedo, and complementing the WFP feeding programme in schools in Belet Hawo and Luuq districts, while extending education services to underserved districts such as Garbaharey.

Project Activities

- Payment of monthly teacher incentives to 27 teachers.- Rehabilitation of 20 gender sensitive latrines and construction of 20 new gender sensitive latrines- Provision of teaching/learning materials to 7 schools. These include text books and other scholastic materials, chalks, dusters, pens, exercise books, rulers, school bags, teacher support equipment – tables, chairs, etc.- Water piping and connection to all the 7 schools across the 4 districts with an average distance of 4km for all the schools This will enhance more access to safe water for the targeted children. - Construction of 20 ceramic water tanks to aid the targeted schools for water storage- School fencing for all the 7 schools to ensure safety of children from trespassers (for girls), danger emanating from armed conflicts, dignity for girls as they visit the latrines, school land demarcation and for school trees/environmental conservation. - Guided monthly protection promotion sessions held in schools with the schools CEC, teachers and pupils’ representatives to enhance and inculcate the need to ensure gender inclusivity, girl-child rights, etc.- Capacity build of two local partners in effective education programming, child safeguarding, systems and beneficiary accountability, reporting and compliance- 168 hygiene promotion sessions (1 session per school per month for all the 7 schools for 12 months) held in schools, including distribution of dignity kits to 500 girls;- 60 community outreach sessions (Bi-monthly) held with the communities and led by CECs to strengthen parental and caregivers’ knowledge and skills to support ECD children in school enrollment, including teachers’ support.;- School meal programme for children in at least 2 schools to cushion the children from effects of malnutrition and promote attendance and retention of children in schools during situations of rampant food insecurity.- Five (5) Teachers’ training on pedagogy held in one year,with inclusion of key topics such as basic child protection, psycho-social and psycho-emotional teaching, and school management. - School management training for 14 (7 head teachers, 7 Deputy head-teachers) to equip them with the requisite skills of school management with focus on child and teacher management, stakeholder liaison, resource mobilization, gender equality in education, and financial prudence.- Six (6) CEC trainings for 49 CEC members in the 7 target schools. The trainings will be conducted to build their capacity on school infrastructural safety and maintenance, resource mobilization, and school development. - Rehabilitation of existing learning spaces, including construction of ramps to improve access for children with physical disabilities in all the 7 schools

Mitigation Measures

2a) Exclusion and Marginalization (mitigation measures):- All boys, girls, male and female education stakeholders will be involved and consulted in all stages including in the design, implementation and monitoring of the project;- Girls and boys will be involved in gender-specific focus group discussions separately, and when the issues touch on all genders, well-monitored inclusive focus groups will be adopted;- The capacity of teachers and CECs to protect children will be reinforced through regular trainings on child protection, safety in schools and protection risk management;- The community through the CEC and parent associations will be involved in discussions to identify barriers to inclusive education and to collectively come up with solutions to these barriers;- Trocaire, working with local education partners, will focus on the recruitment of female teachers who are under-represented in schools, train them as gender focal points and provide mentorship to them throughout the project duration. 3b) External interference by stakeholders in beneficiary selection:- Strengthening of school girls’ clubs and facilitating their in-house forums on weekly basis as a platform to discuss their common challenges and sharing the same to the school management (the headteacher/deputy headteacher) for mitigation;- Girls will be exclusively targeted for distribution of sanitary kits and will have their own segregated WASH facilities to minimize risks associated with abuse and promote access with dignity;- Active monitoring of protection concerns by the school community;- Strengthening of the existing GBV referral system in schools and linking this to the health facilities;- Consultation with project beneficiaries in the refining of the beneficiary selection criteria, and having in place a representative selection committee, especially for the cash grants activity. 3c) External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection- Consultation of girls and female teachers in determining the location and safety of assets to be rehabilitated and/or constructed;- Conducting KAP surveys with girls and female teachers to gauge their satisfaction, or lack thereof in the services provided;- Working with education stakeholders to determine safe learning hours, and to encourage children to walk in groups to mitigate protection risks;- Playgrounds will be situated in clearly visible and safe locations to ensure reduced protection risks;- Encourage teachers to supervise children during breaks, and for teachers to walk home with children to continue this supervision even on the road whenever possible.

Please provide further explanation

While the protection environment in the target districts is not completely safe to implement longterm durable solutions, opportunities to enshrine sustainability exist through the community structures. That is why Trocaire will be working closely with the CECs, the teachers and the community at large to increase ownership of the project and create a conducive environment for them to learn and eventually take over education service delivery. Trocaire will also be working with local education partners, who are present in Gedo, to further encourage collaboration and coordination in delivery of services. This will ensure Value for Money and collective efforts in empowering the community and the children in Gedo to advocate for education rights with the local and regional authorities.
