Project Summary

In the next 12 months of 2019 CIDO will provide emergency lifesaving WASH interventions covering IDPs, Returnees and host communities residing in Morobo, Yambio and Ikotos counties of Greater Equatoria region. The response is tailored in such a way that Key sectoral activities in Nutrition, GBV, Health and Education are integrated to form a comprehensive response and address needs of women, men, boys and girls. The WASH minimum package will be guided by minimum operating guidelines including, the sphere standards and WASH SOPs. The estimated cost for this project is $ 800,000

Needs

HNO 2019 estimates that about 5.7 million people are in need of WASH assistance. The compounding effects of widespread violence and sustained economic decline have further diminished the capacity of people to face threats to their health, safety and livelihoods. Sustain access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene improvements and awareness is critical to save thousands of lives of children and women. Excretal and poor disposal coupled with lack of proper good hygiene practices characterized open defecation poses high risk of preventable water related diseases such as cholera and AWD.According IRNA assessment for Yambio June 2018 ,there are over 12000 IDPS in need of critical humanitarian assistance. Ikotos and Morobo counties are both hard to reach area and has been under-served.The distribution of the burden for the collection of household water is 60 and 21 percent for adult women and men, respectively, and 8.3 and 6.8 per cent for girls and boys, respectively. In Equatoria state like other parts of south Sudan, women bear the great burden of water collection, and thereby carry great influence on maternal health and health of the family. Women need to be empowered to take control and responsibility of promoting hygiene and sanitation in the community through building their capacity to manage water points and through inclusion in the water management committees. Emergency situation may increase the vulnerability of people living with HIV and affected populations and expose them to risks of contracting diarrhea and malaria. This is mainly due to breeding of mosquitoes in flooding areas and disruption of essential service delivery including HIV services. Eastern Equatoria State has a HIV prevalence rate of 2.1% while its capital Torit Hospital has a HIV prevalence rate of 3 % among the vulnerable groups, i.e. women and orphans. These groups need access to safe water points and toilets that are made acceptable and safe for all users This project will target conflict affected displaced and vulnerable host communities of Ikotos Morobo and Yambio to reduce morbidity and mortality associated with poor hygiene including health hazards like Malaria, cholera, skin infections . It will ensure that women, men, girls and boys are consulted in the decision making affected have equal access to safe water supply, sanitation and hygiene services to prevent potential public health risk as a result of using unprotected water sources poor sanitation and poor hygiene practices. There is need to integrate WASH activities in other sectors like Nutrition in recognizing that lack of proper hygiene can worsen malnutrition symptoms like AWD. Apart from nutrition, CIDO will coordinate with sectoral partners to integrate WASH activities in health, education and GBV actors to maximize the impact and meet the needs of the target populations. CIDO has experience and capacity to deliver the project having integrated GBV and WASH activities in Budi county of Eastern Equatoria. CIDO will adhere to standard operating procedures including IASC and Sphere standards to execute this project successfully in the proposed project locations. CIDO will deliver an integrated basic package for response which is cholera, malnutrition and GBV risk mitigation among the affected vulnerable population

Activities

Provide timely, equitable access to safe water supply through construction and rehabilitation and household water treatment systems• Provide hygiene kits at functional OTPs/SC to mothers and care takers having boys and girls with SAM cases. • Provide sustainable access to safe water points to 8 OTPs/SC • Train health volunteers on minimum WASH package safe drinking water and waste management• Participate in joint WASH -epidemiological mitigation conducted on outbreak cholera and other WASH related diseases • Equip schools /Temporary learning Spaces in outbreak hotspot with minimum WASH package and safe drinking water, hygiene• Train male and female WASH staff on protection, SOP and GBV guidelines • Organize focus groups discussion targeting women and girls conducted by WASH female staff on WASH related GBV issues • Conduct protection risk analysis and integration in WASH construction facilities by WASH female staff equipped with MHM • Train OTPS/SC, health and Nutrition workers/volunteers on WASH minimum package of activities

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project activities has been tailored to fit the South Sudanese context in respect to various community cultural values. The project has therefore factored in gender and age appropriate activities to address needs of different sexes and age groups. Secondly CIDO is keen to ensure that the appropriate staff ( male and female ) are engaged to deliver the planned activities depending on sensitivities related to culture across the beneficiaries’ target locations. The project give allowance for separate and confidential discussions with different community groups (including gender and age disaggregated groups)

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO has a functional policy on PSEA . The policy has been signed by all signed by all staff in respect to the 6 core principles of PSEA.The respective project staff leading this project will be guided by the standard operating Procedures (SOP) of PSEA and where applicable will use the community based complaint Mechanism to address cases of SEA. CIDO will ensure that PSEA IEC materials are used and are visible in all designated areas of service delivery. In collaboration with other partners and I recognition that CIDO is an active protection partner, efforts will be consolidated to conduct capacity building of frontline workers on PSEA and extend applicability of SOP to other NGOs at grassroots level

Centrality of Protection in humanitarian action
Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO being an active Protection Cluster partner has conducted a protection Mapping and risk analysis exercise of the targeted beneficiaries. Both exercises provided essential information on groups and individuals who are at risk, the reasons why they are at risk and how, while taking into account the specific vulnerabilities that underlie these risks. The project therefore take into consideration specificities and unique needs for men, women, girls and boys, and groups such as internally displaced persons, older persons, persons with disabilities, and persons belonging to sexual and other minorities and most importantly how to mitigate the identified risks.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In reference to the risk analysis exercise, it is noted that some beneficiaries could be falling under the elderly, Physically handicapped or under Persons Special needs (PSNs) categories. Under such circumstances, the project activities are designed to give priority to safety and dignity of all men, women boys and girls and prevent negative coping strategies and mitigate risks including issues of discrimination in regards to accessing services. Through regular coordination meetings, partners on ground will be encouraged to train the frontline workers on disability mainstreaming and also report and share protection concerns for persons with specific needs.

Addresses inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In regards to addressing protection threats ,CIDO will be guided by the four principles of South Sudan HCT Protection Strategy.(2015) i.e • Prioritize safety and dignity, and avoid causing harm,• Equity and Meaningful access, • AAP • Participation and EmpowermentCIDO will be guided by the protection mainstreaming tool kit (PMT) to address protection inequalities. The goal is that potential protection risks are identified from the outset and mitigated.CIDO will conduct protection assessments and risk analysis, stakeholders mapping, and needs verification with clear AGD data to understand context dynamics, protection risks, vulnerability level and inform respective partners depending on needs, access, safety, and gap findings. This will be done through focus group discussions, key informant interviews, meetings and HH surveys with women, men, children, institution workers, community leaders, within IDPs, host community and returnee population. CIDO will also conduct awareness raising by use of IEC materials for protection specific messages.as well as advocate for other sectoral partners to conduct NFI distributions at HH level to address needs of people with disabilities, elderly and chronically ill. CIDO will be promoting equality through recruitment of men and women on various level of activities as deemed necessary.
