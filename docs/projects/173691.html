Project Summary

Centre of Excellence for Rural Development (CERD) capitalizing on its vast experience, proposes the following three key areas to preserve the aim of child protection.
1. Provision of Mental Health and Psychosocial support Services through Community-Based Child Protection Centers:
CERD will form/revitalize Child Protection Committees (either newly established or existing from previous work with communities affected by conflict/militancy and operations). The activity shall be implemented in collaboration /consultation with the government to determine vulnerabilities of children in conflict affected and unaware areas of the project districts. Work shall be done together with the Child Protection Committees to determine criteria to identify location of 30 Child protection Centers and the beneficiaries. Geographic/administrative formations of the districts and tehsils and further combination of several localities to make a central zone will be considered for addressing child protection issues on practical and demonstrational grounds. CERD shall limit its intervention under this project to most severely affected areas i.e. District North Waziristan, Kohat, FR Kohat, FR Peshawar, FR Lakki Marwat and FR Bannu. CERD shall set up 30 Child protection Centers in the 06 project districts and similarly 30 child protection committees (CPCs) i.e. one per center. Further, the CPCs in facilities provided by communities as part of their contribution to the child protection response.
2. Establish and support the operations of a case management and referral mechanism to ensure access of vulnerable children to basic services:
CERD intends to hold meetings with government at district and provincial level to seek support in favor of child protection through case management and referral mechanism. Alongside, government consultation and support, the project shall engage parents and local communities in awareness, linkages, prevention, pre-emption and response to cases of concern.
In this regard, CERD shall take hold of the district based service pathways and establish 06 case management and referral mechanism centers i.e. 01 per district. The centers shall be in link with SWD collaborating with rest of the service providers
3. Dissemination of information and promotion of dialogue among families and communities on stigma and Violence against Children (VAC) and GBV prevention.
• CERD with UNICEF's consent shall ensure availability of the IEC material for dissemination through child committees, parent groups, teachers, community meetings. Apart from this, the project shall make use of the government facilities like schools and maybe dispensaries or so to disseminate the information widely.
• The other concurrent activity with this would be, to hold dialogues and community meetings (virtual or physical) with parents, communities in the target areas in connection with: awareness on child protection, children vulnerability, early warnings of incidences, prevention, pre-emption and response. There is a dire need to “take care of receipt/collection, analysis and communication of relevant and credible information that alerts the parents/ community to potential problems pertaining to child protection and the need for action. The project aims to support and empower communities to identify issues and solutions and protect their children by addressing those issues. Only the verified information would be shared with the community at large and the service providers for necessary actions.
When, the early warning would be received, a set of interconnected steps/processes with a common purpose, i.e. help children and communities to act swiftly and appropriately to pre-empt any adverse consequences to the children and even their parents. “A total of 60 dialogues and 300 community meetings shall take place in this connection
