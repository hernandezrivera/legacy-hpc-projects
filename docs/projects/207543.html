Project Summary

The main objective of this project is to promote and support access to inclusive and adapted education offer and and provide potable water to children in the Fako division in southwest and in Wdikum subdivision in Northwest. The project aims to renovate and rehabilitated school buildings and support education facilities and improving safety, healthy and secure environment to all children in school age. To achieve this objective, BHODEV will lead the intervention on four objectives: 1- Rehabilitation and renovation of school buildings, provide chairs and benches to offer the needed classrooms for the Government Nursery, Primary and secondary school of Bomaka, Muea, Tiko and Bokwango so as to provide quality education and better learning space to benefit over 2000 school going children. 2- Students have access to courses given according to the national education’s programs and benefit from WASH services and a healthy and secure environment. 3- To raise the awareness and advocacy for change behavior in communities and BHODEV will settle WAHS Clubs in school to promote inclusive education issues. 4- Support to local and administrative educational structure and ensure adaptable program and provide school material support to needy school children.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The socio-political crisis rocking the English-speaking regions of Cameroon have consequences like: persistent ghost towns, shut down of schools and offices, insecurity, and various forms of violence are being suffered by the populations of the concerned regions. An estimated 1,033,000 school-aged children have been affected by the ongoing socio-political crisis as they were denied the opportunity to learn and develop themselves, failing in achieving Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) specifically, goal 4: ''ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all''. For the 2022-2023 only 3,013 schools are functioning (46%) out of 6,515 planned in the NWSW regions. Although, some schools have reopened, they are not completely functional and only 60% of their capacities are use, especially in Bomaka where learning institutions were closed for more than 2 years and were later forced to move out of their original accommodation to continue classes for the past six years. This greatly affected the population of the pupils and student of Government Nursery, Primary and Secondary School in Fako Division as it reduced drastically as many pupils and students flee to other towns in the country to seek refuge while continuing their educational career. Over the years, In Bomaka for example as the population increased so did the demand for learning space and other learning facilities increased making the campus insufficient to accommodate the learners and the learning environment became very uncomfortable for the pupils and students as there are lack of WASH facilities, 3 to 4 classes are combined in the same hall, no fence around the campus as such pupils and students are sharing toilets with passers-by, outsiders defecating in classrooms and threatening the lives of students and school staff during and after school hours. Visiting the school for a sensitization campaign on hygiene and sanitation, BHODEV seeks to help these children by advocating for them to return to their main campuses. This can only be successful by carrying out renovation in their main campuses as most of the buildings were destroyed and putting in place measures to mitigate the risks involved in returning to their main campuses. So, in order to achieve the goal of “Every child learns” and guaranteed a Sustainable Development by ensuring inclusive and equitable education for all BHODEV has engaged in the first stage of project with her own resources which entails mobilization and awareness campaign on importance of education to the community, school site cleaning, repairs and purchase of some school benches, the purchase of hygiene equipment, advocacy for the Government Nursery, Primary and Secondary School to return to their main campuses to all authorities responsible for educational activities in the Buea Subdivision, and authorization to the competent authorities to carry out the renovation activities on the school campuses.The crisis has also disrupted access to essential services, including clean water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH), exacerbating existing challenges and creating new needs. In fact, many factors contribute to increase WASH needs in Buea, Tiko and Widikum subdivisions include: the displacement of thousands of people, putting pressure on existing WASH infrastructure and services in host communities and increasing the need for clean water and sanitation facilities. The destruction of water and sanitation infrastructure, lack sufficient sanitation facilities like latrines and waste management systems, disruptions in water supply networks, contamination of water sources, and inadequate maintenance of infrastructure contribute to limited access to clean water for drinking, cooking, and hygiene purposes. After needs assessment by BHODEV localities with most necessity and urgent localities are in Buea (Bomaka, Wonya-Mavio and Peters Stream), Tiko (Sonne, Old and New Pongo) and Widikum (Atong).
