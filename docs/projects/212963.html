Project Summary

The project is strategically aligned with the 2024 HNO summary and the 2024 PiN for education cluster in NES, particularly in terms of targeted locations. Specifically, the project focuses on educations gaps in Deir Ezzor and Abo kamal districts. These areas have been identified as the first-priority underserved regions, characterized by high severity indices and significant numbers of dropped out children.
The project will be focusing on the sustainable support of non-formal education through many quality interventions including teachers professional development, non-formal education programs and school-based psychosocial support (PSS), monthly operational costs, distribution of teaching materials and learning supplies for teachers and students, Parent-Teacher Associations (PTAs) establishment as well as providing light furniture, rehabilitation, safety equipment and solar systems. This comprehensive approach seeks to enhance access to quality non-formal education for 12,000 children, ECE for 800 children and TVET for 2400 children and youth with a focus on gender equality mainstreaming, cash transfer for 600 children and the provision of schools transportation for 200 CwDs. This will also include the TPD and the payment of monthly incentives for 528 teachers.
