Project Summary

Under the leadership of the Education Cluster, UNICEF will create participatory opportunities for out of school adolescents aged 15, 16 and 17, years to acquire financial literacy. UNICEF, in collaboration with the Children's Radio Foundation will train 200 youth reporters at 5-10 radio stations to narrowcast and/or broadcast shows on financial literacy, agriculture, and other available business. 236 shows will be narrowcast and broadcast over 12 months. At least 25,000 listeners will be reached with information on (local) financial opportunities. In previous experiences in DRC and Tanzania, impact on the youth reporters and the listeners was assessed: 100% of the 180 youth reporters increased in their understanding and knowledge of financial systems and felt confident to share the information with listeners. 86% of listeners felt that their understanding of financial systems and services and knowledge of agriculture as a business had increased. 80% of listeners reported using financial literacy information presented in the shows after listening to the shows. 67% of listeners reported taking up financial services (opening bank accounts, taken out loans, or using mobile money). 27% of listeners reported having started their own business or accessed agriculture-related opportunities. Broadcasting experiences of youth entrepreneurship in DRC and Tanzania, allowed youth to understand the steps to take to pursue similar careers. Increased emphasis needs to be placed on the targeting female listeners.Increased focus needs to be placed on how to engage with listeners.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The target population are out of school adolescents of the North West and South West, aged 15 to 17 years. The NW and SW crisis has left more than 1.2 million school-age children (51% girls) in need of humanitarian assistance in NW and SW, Adamawa (Mayo Banyo), Littoral, West, and Centre (Yaounde). Approximately 900,000 of them need urgently access to education service. An estimated 59,000 adolescents are displaced within the 2 regions (NW and SW). The main needs for children of the NW and SW are linked to the risks associated with attending formal education, the unavailability of formal education offer in areas of insecurity, the general lack of protective learning environment. The project targets 200 youth reporters, and 25,000 listeners with basic financial education and entrepreneurship skills. Radio is an effective medium for adolescents to to connect youth to relevant information, training, and employment opportunities. Radio stations are important community convening spaces to advance dialogues on youth employment for a broad range of partners, including the private sector, NGOs, and youth.
