Project Summary

Sanjh Preet Organization (SPO) has proposed WASH assistance in rural areas of District Lahore and Faislabad (Punjab-Pakistan) for 10000 community members, 2,000 school children and 500 clients of health facility to cope up with urgent WASH needs & attending to COVID-19 cases of the targeted communities. Proposed areas are finalized based on identified needs through the involvement of local communities and coordination with districts authorities), education and health departments to ensure maximum transparency and accountability. Local partner’s assessment also guided the planning of locally relevant activities for the proposed communities and institutions.
The focus of the proposed interventions will be on life-saving WASH support for communities with fully and partially damaged water systems which includes repair/rehabilitation/installation of water supply systems including hand pumps, pressure pumps and dug wells in communities, while Installation of user-friendly latrines, defection trenches, bathing cubicles and washing places will be established in schools and in priority health facilities for treating COVID-19 cases of targeted areas of both districts. Moreover, hand washing stations will be installed at key public places and the distribution of soap. Capacity building of key front-line workers on measures related to COVID-19, training of committees/Schools councils Members on operation and maintenance. Special focus is given on female adult school children and solid waste management of schools. Information regarding preventive measures, against COVID-19, will be broadcasted through social and mass media, local FM radio jingles, displaying posters, banners, and billboards at prominent places of targeted districts. Hygiene promotion approaches will be used i.e., PHAST, CHAST and C2C during the intervention in schools.

1- Project Activities

1. Repair/Rehabilitation/installation of water supply systems including hand pumps, pressure pumps and dug wells: 100 schools (50 form each district) and 20 healthcare facilities will be targeted under the proposed project. Therefore 100 hands pumps will be installed in schools recommended by district education departments, while in close coordination with the TMA/Zila Council and Municipal Committees 50 Community based hands pumps will be installed where drinking water sources are fully or partially damaged. The latrines will comply; DRR, Sphere and WASH cluster standards. 2. Provision of appropriate WASH installations at schools and healthcare facilities: 20 need-based modern Pour flush latrines will be constructed (10 in each district) in selected schools, Basic Health Units (BHU’s) and rural health Centers (RHC’s) with missing or less than required WASH facilities. At the same time rehabilitation of existing latrines in health facilities will also be done to ensure the appropriate WASH facilities. SPO will use the already approved design and technical guidelines of the WASH cluster. Structures will also comply with DRR aspects of construction. The construction work will be done by the vendor while technical support will be provided by SPO engineers. WASH Committees will be involved to ensure transparency and quality of work. 3. Promotion of critical WASH-related information using interpersonal communication, social and mass media, radio jingles, leaflets, posters, billboards and radio & television shows: Communities will be educated on good personal, environmental, and domestic hygiene through hygiene promotion sessions using Participatory Hygiene and Sanitation Transformation (PHAST) and Child Hygiene and Sanitation Training (CHAST) approaches.500 sessions will be conducted by hygiene promoters reaching out to 25 individuals in each session (12,500 individuals including men, women and children in total).• 3,000 hygiene kits will be distributed to each family once during the project. One hygiene kit is sufficient for 3 months of use. Distribution of Hygiene kits is in line with the target beneficiaries reached through sanitation. Each hygiene kit caters to the need of women especially for menstrual cycle management. 4. Ensuring access to information on preventive measures, against COVID-19 through social and mass media: In order to create awareness regarding at mass level on preventive measures, against COVID-19. Sanjh Preet will engage local electronic and print media firms. In coordination with OCHA and District Health authorities, Sanjh Preet Will develops messages for the broadcasting of preventive COVID-19 messages through local media (FM Radio); this will help in increasing awareness. The activity will be done with the support of the WASH cluster, messages will be provided by Sanjh Preet while messages will broadcast through local radio channels. Moreover, COVID-19 preventive measures awareness messages will be disseminated through wall chalking/ posters/ leaflet/ banners in public places of targeted areas; Posters with key messages will be displayed at prominent places and leaflets will be distributed to increase awareness to help in saving human lives form COVID-195. Installation of handwashing stations at key public places and distribution of soap: 50 Handwashing stations will be installed at public places of both districts with messages on COVID-19 preventive measures.6. Capacity building of key front line workers on measures related to COVID-19”: 100 key front line workers from the health department will be trained on measures related to COVID-19. Four one day orientation session (2 in each district) will be conducted, 25 front line workers will participate in each session. Session contents will be finalized with the consultation of district health authorities and WHO standards guidelines.
