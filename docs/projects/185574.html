Project Summary

We are writing this proposal to improve on the level of awareness of Covid-19 and measures to prevent transmission of the virus. Due to data collected and analysis from our last Covid-19 rapid need assessment, we turned to realize the areas of slowdown of responsibilities regarding preventive measures are very low in rural communities unlike urban where there exists high level of awareness. But neither urban nor rural communities have been putting in to practice preventive measures. Covering face with mask is almost the only measure known by these communities. As a means of mitigating the effect of the pandemic, we intend to impact positively in 100 communities within 4 Divisions and 12 Sub-divisions in the South West Region with special note on hard to reach areas where there is less concern and increase in vulnerability of the population. While some of us have learned to live with mask and social distancing, as well as handwashing after handling packages and touching surfaces, we need some basic sensitization activities to improve level of awareness and preventive measures in order to live in a more stable and healthier environment. Covid-19 pandemic has caused more harm in some of our studied communities such as psychological trauma, stigmatization, loss of lives, and high level of urban-rural migration increasing the rate of internally displaced persons. Our target beneficiaries are 5000 students, 500 teachers, 1000 parents and 500 educative staffs. After impacting in the communities positively increasing awareness and preventive measures, we shall equally sensitize beneficiaries of the importance and available protection services that have been put in place such as hotlines or toll numbers, counselling services, case management services, child friendly spaces, institutional care not leaving out social worker groups and their inputs regarding overall management of affected or suspected cases.

1 - Besoins / Needs

We intend to reach out to at least 50000 persons through which 7000 for teachers, students, supervisors, parents while a general sensitization with use of megaphones in areas that can make out 43000 persons of all age groups, gender and disabilities. This project will reach out to 100 communities. Miracle Charity Foundation (MICHAFOUND) shall be engaging in various activities; Awareness: • Sensitization through physical seminars and also through the use of mega phones, local radio and television Preventive measures: • Placing of wash kits at entrances of at least 100 streets in some selected rural areas/villages with sensitization to the village counsels in order to make it continual as a means of sustainability. • Physical seminars with care givers, community leaders, teachers, students, parents and host of others with 2M social distance to implement. We shall work with the assistance of health personnel from district hospitals and other mission establishments to better spread measures put in place by the UN.• Distribution of sanitizers to at least 2000 vulnerable households as a means of educating them on the essence of using such measures to prevent the effect from the pandemic• Distribution of durable face masks to at least 5000 vulnerable persons with special notes on disabled children within sampled communities.  Special support: • Purchase of wash and hygiene kits to be given to 10 orphanages in rural communities.• Purchase of wash and hygiene kits to be given to 3 handicap centers in rural communities• Purchase of wash and hygiene kits to be given to 3 deaf centers• Putting in place hotlines for reported cases.• Psychological support to affected families. During project execution, we shall put in place all preventive measures needed in order not be infected by the virus. Below are methods that shall be considered for our proposed activities; Public sensitization which will include physical, media through radio and TV announcements and online conferences with various community leaders, facilitators, care givers, health workers, institutional centers, social groups, NGOs, child friendly centers, counseling centers, case management centers, teachers and parents to better create a channel for information flow for sustainability. For public sensitization with use of megaphone and seminars, we shall implement 2M social distance. Awareness can not be effective without putting in place preventive measures. MICHAFOUND shall distribute wash and hygiene kits relating to Covid-19.We shall equally introduce hotline services for reported cases to better assist the state. Seminars will be conducted to disclose all possible measures with the assistance from health personnel. Trainings will be done as well on how to manage case management services. Special support: Some supports shall be extended to orphanages, handicap centers, hospitals, deaf centers, hotlines and for affected families undergoing effect from stigmatization hence psychosocial support. Extraction of data from Google sheet (data collected during the project) and run analysis using Excel and Power BI (Microsoft Power Business Intelligence) in order to come out with reports that can be used to understand overall reached and predict future change/sustainability. After overall analysis, a project report will be communicated to donors. This report can be communicated or shared through PowerPoint, words formats, pictures, videos and any other form of available feedback not leaving out proposals from donor. At the end of the evaluation period MICHAFOUND will make an overall reports showing planned, reached and gap analysis.
