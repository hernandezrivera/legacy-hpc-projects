Project Summary

Districts of intervention: Metuge, Mecufi, Ancuabe, Chiure, Montepuez. The intervention aims to provide nutritional screening to children and women, especially during Brigadas Moveis. The outreaches will be deployed to high populated and rural areas and services will be provided from technicians of the Health Center.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This proposal is the result of CUAMM's collaboration with the Provincial Management Teams of Cabo Delgado and the District authorities of targeted districts (Ancuabe, Chiure, Mecufi, Metuge, Montepuez) to meet the deterioration of the nutritional situation particularly in the north of Cabo DelgadoAbout 228,000 IDPs and host families are in high levels of acute food insecurity, despite the mitigating effects of humanitarian food assistance. Around 74,700 children U5 and 22.000 pregnant and lactating women are likely suffering from acute malnutrition and in need of treatment through January 2022. The unstable and volatile situation also caused low PRN coverage with less than 20 percent of estimated AD cases admitted since the beginning of 2021. The following components will be implemented: - support the delivery of life saving health and nutrition services in remote communities and IDPs sites: mobile brigades will be put in place in the highest populated/hard to reach areas in the targeted districts;- The integration of the screening, referral and treatment of severe acute malnutrition and moderate acute malnutrition in children under 5 years of age and pregnant and lactating women PLW Beneficiary of the action will be 10.168 people: - 3.600 pregnant and lactating women- 6.568 children U5

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will be aligned to Nutrition Cluster specific objectives, namely 1. To increase the number of children U5 receiving nutritional supplementation; 2. To prevent the increase of acute malnutrition and lack of micronutrients for children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women; 3. To promote exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months of life and introduction of nutritionally-adequate and safe complementary foods at 6 months, and general information messages for best practices for children feeding The project will work at different levels: - Cuamm will support the realization of mobile brigades with integrated nutritional screening, Integrated Community Case Management, micronutrients supplementation- Cuamm will support the screening, referral and treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) in children under 5 years of age and PLW- Sensitization and delivery of key messages on Infant Young Child Feeding Counselling will be done to the participants of mobile brigades. - The quality of provided care will be strengthened through technical assistance and supportive supervision and monitored through an enhanced data collection and analysis, functional to informed decision making process to improve the current situation.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Community representatives’ involvement in the realization of Brigadas Moveis (planning, mobilitating the communities and implementation) improve services responsiveness/acceptability and people's awareness on their importance, reducing fear and cultural resistance towards institutional care. CUAMM will ensure gender balanced and inclusive composition and attendance of the health staff that will provide the services in the community. On the other side, health services enhanced accessibility and quality made women healthier and also partially released from the caretaker role they had to play towards the other family members, enabling them to engage in public functions and tasks.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project supports IDPs and host communities in the targeted Districts, prioritizing the most vulnerable groups, such us children U5 and lactating and pregnant women. Gender Empowerment and Social Inclusion is the mainstreamed approach and perspective. The aim is not just implementing activities addressing vulnerable groups but ensuring their effective access to and utilization of quality services, responsive to their problems. Free of charge frontline services, provided at the closest possible level to the communities, minimize economical and geographical obstacles. Health services integration (preventive/curative) reduce the missing opportunities and maximize the impact of a single access to service.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The attempt of bringing services where people live is functional to put them in conditions to access what they need, limiting the risks connected to the movements in such an unstable environment and overcoming access constraints related to women domestic cores and to family/social power dynamics, disability or mental issues. Mobile brigades clinics will be done in rural and remote communities as well IDPs sites in places easily reachable also from people with disabilities and as closest possible to beneficiaries. CUAMM is already training health staff on these topics and IEC materials. A protection map will be defined through the feedback collected at community level and making reference to other partners/institution assessment so that identifying vulnerable groups and situations that might endanger the goals of the interventions and maintaining a focused approach, avoiding wasting resources and addressing the priority sites and issues.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed programme will limit its impact on the environment by ensuring appropriate sensitization on healthcare workers as well as community health workers on safe disposal of consumables, especially PPEs.In addition the project will focus on sensitizing host and displaced communities on environmental and personal hygiene, sanitation, disease outbreaks prevention/control at the community level during BM.During the project implementation CUAMM will encourage: - integration of activities, implying movements by vehicles, to limit the number of cars and motorbikes going to the same destination and, consequently, the consumption of fuel and petrol; - careful monitoring of fuel storage and management, to avoid leakage and waste and hazard for the surrounding environment; - discussion with local health authorities on how safely handling the products resulting from the drugs store dejunking regular exercise to avoid all wastage to be left in the field, contaminating water sources or cattle feeding spaces.
