Project Summary

"• Screening of at least 90% of the children 6-59 months (64,005) carried out in all the wards in the proposed districts to guide emergency response; and referral of those identified with acute malnutrition for treatment in the nearest facility.
• Support the therapeutic feeding and care for 2,761 children 6-59 months old with acute malnutrition to protect their lives and bring them back to healthy growth and development
• Ensure that 90% of 78,942 targeted children under five and 14,383 pregnant women receive micronutrient supplementation
• Ensure at least 80% of mothers (78,942) of children under five receive community IYCF counselling care in next 9 months from August 2019 to April 2020

Activities

"• Orientation of community volunteers, Lead Mothers and/or Lead Fathers on active screening, tallying, and referral of identified cases of acute malnutrition to health facilities for PLWs and children aged 6-59 months• Monitoring and supervision of screening of children under five in targeted wards• Training of village health workers on MUAC screening • Monitor referral, admission and treatment of children identified with SAM in health facilities• Sex and age disaggregated data compilation and reporting• Monitoring and supervision to ensure quality of care for children with SAM• Monitor and support the identification of children eligible for vitamin A supplementation and their referral to health facilities• Monitor and support counselling and referral of pregnant women to health facilities to receive and take IFA• Monitor and support community IYCF activities in all wards with focus on IYCF in emergencies training• Provide refresher training for EHTs, nurses on monitoring of the Code of BMS"
