Project Summary

• Output 1. Assessment. Evaluation of damage caused to public infrastructure and identification of priorities and strategies to introduce labor intensive solutions aligned with the DW agenda
• Output 2. Creation of emergency employment opportunities through restoration of public infrastructure. Roof and electrical system restoration of public buildings with employment generation for 150 youngsters with technical qualifications
• Output 3. Skills development. Skills of young professionals upgraded to find employment executing resilient construction works
