Project Summary

The project is specifically aimed at providing appropriate support to ensure minority dignified living conditions for 32,000 displaced person parentage (14,000 women, 7,425men, 7,121 boys and 2,010 girl and disability 1,444) at the minority and marginalized informal settlements in Hiraan, Regions Somalia. The targeted minority/marginalizes IDPs including people with disabilities are living precariously in crowded informal settlements in Hiraan regions, Somalia. The recent climate shocks minority group’s needs protection and insecurity affected IDPs are minority living under very poor and deplorable conditions in unplanned informal settlements with limited access to basic needs owing to inconsistent and unreliable service provision coupled by cases of exclusion from accessing humanitarian assistance. Women, girls and people with disabilities are further affected by unplanned nature of the IDP sites that limit them from accessing essential services such as protection, education have been discriminated located within the sites which compromise their safety. The situation is exacerbated by poor coordination and management structures at the settlement owing to the fact that they are managed by gatekeepers who are not accountable and further keeps demanding for favors from them in the face of fears of rampant evictions. The highlighted plight calls for need for timely and appropriate site improvements to enhance safe and secure access to critical services available within the sites particularly for women, girls and people with disabilities in addition to very strong need for site-level coordination and management systems to ensure equitable access to services and increase protection of minority displaced persons. Furthermore there is need for information sharing with meaningful consultation and participation of affected IDP communities and People with disabilities to ensure their dignified living non-discrimination conditions sustainable impact.
MRDO will implement emergency sites improvement projects to minimize discrimination risks of minority exclusion and high incidence of protection minority/marginalized group’s violations in IDP sites, as well achieving non-discrimination to reach durable solutions. MRDO will also conduct settlement planning training for settlement leaders; create coordination structures and for emergency minority and marginalized displacements are needs to build the sustainable houses, conduct capacity building non-discrimination trainings for government’s actors and relevant stakeholders; establish/reinforce community mechanisms for communication channels among others. Gender and protection will be mainstreamed in all program activities. Finally MRDO will develop systems to strengthen community self-management to promote durable solutions for displaced minority people in sites. Activities. The project will ensure that all minority marginalized participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis-segregated data. Settlement mapping exercises will be conducted to get the information of all targeted settlements for necessary services in place and gap analysis, the settlement mapping exercise data will be shared with CCCM cluster lead, which will be useful in creating a master planning list of the area. This master list will then be posted online for the ease of access to all humanitarian actors, which will be used by different humanitarian partners for their future planning and response.
The majority of IDPs settle in informal and unplanned settlements in the target Regions have poor management which has been appropriated by the ‘gatekeepers’ (informal managers), with living conditions being poor, observed high rates of Gender Based violence.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure: Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff/ additional guards as well as ensuring that assistance is provided at centralized locations where women and girls don’t cover very long distance that compromise their safety on the way back home 2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as conducting awareness on SEA and the reporting procedures that doesn’t expose the victims to the perpetrators. 3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.) Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences 4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities Mitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls and ensuring their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as active participation on all project phases/stages right from initiation throughout implementation including monitoring, till completion 5. The risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms Mitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same activities for minority/marginalized communities in Hiraan regions
