Project Summary

The project intends to meet the food security requirements of particularly vulnerable internally displaced persons and host populations in the most affected areas of Northeast Nigeria and Northern Cameroon, especially in the annual lean season. In addition, the project aims to provide protection monitoring and support to affected population, with a particular focus on prevention of SGBV, child protection, and psychsocial support.
