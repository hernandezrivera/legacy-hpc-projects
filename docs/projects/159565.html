Project Summary

The project intends to improve protection skills and daily care of children, with specific focus on children with disabilities, by creating prerequisites to overcome the existing problem of orphanhood and reduce the impact of labor migration of parents on the psycho emotional state of children by promoting positive parenting to caregivers and improving day care kindergarten; As well, lessons adapted to children mine awareness will be conducted to acquire skills needed in conflict situations.

1 - Needs

Children are the most vulnerable social group: during conflict, they face many challenges that even an adult can rarely overcome on their own. Due to the disastrous socio-economic situation in NGCA, there is an increased rate of temporary labor migration. Able-bodied people are forced to go to work in the GCA, Russia or the EU, leaving their children in the care of a second parent, or grandparents. Because of this, children who remain in their homes on NGCA, so are already a vulnerable group, are deprived of attention from one or even two parents and experience negative emotions. Due to the lack of parental attention, children do not get the necessary social skills, have problems with socialization. They are not able to walk on the streets unaccompanied by adults because of the threat of unexploded ordnance, forced to be in their homes without communication with other children. There is a need to minimize this effect by improving the quality of kindergarten’s work. There are 524 pre-school educational institutions in the NGCA of Donetsk region, they are attended by about 55,500 children, 49,400 of them — in the zone of 20 km along the demarcation line. These institutions have been suffering from fighting and underfunding for five years. In many of them, repairs were not carried out, equipment was not purchased. Their staff are deprived of the opportunity to improve their skills and the opportunity to use modern methodological developments for the most effective preschool age children`s education of skills necessary in conflict. Another vulnerable social group is children with disabilities, including those, who were affected by conflict. They need medical and educational equipment to exercise their rights. According to the clinical center of neurorehabilitation in Donetsk, during 2015-2017, only 190 children with cerebral palsy aged from 2 to 15 years (87 boys, 103 girls) were observed, their number is higher in the whole NGCA. Specialized wheelchairs for children with cerebral palsy and other motor disorders have a high cost, which cannot be afforded by the inhabitants of the NGCA. During the five years of the conflict, such assistance for children with disabilities has never been provided by the state or charitable foundations. These children are deprived of the joy of a healthy childhood, experience constant deprivation and are in dire need of specialized medical equipment that would allow them to overcome the difficulties caused by their illness. The Donbass region is one of the most mined regions in the world and children are seriously endangered by mines. About 16,000 km² of land ought to be demined, what is yet to be done in the uncertain future. While this has not happened, there is an urgent need to conduct capacity building on mine and psychological safety, first aid, safe life to children. According to the Ministry of defense of Ukraine 833 civilians, including 125 children, suffered from mines for five years of the conflict. 269 people, including 27, children were killed by mines.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Within the project it is planned:Outcome 1: Improved daily care and protection of children, with specific focus on children with disabilitiesActivity 1: Conduct a campaign of promotion of appropriate and positive parenting. Parenting means providing the ongoing care and support a child needs in order to survive and thrive. Our extensive work with children made us assess that parents and guardians/caregivers, due to the recent impact of labor migration of parents, often do not have the skills, knowledge or resources to raise children to their full potential. In order to give families, the skills and knowledge to provide care, feeding and protection, DDC aims to further invest on parenting programs tailored to the varying needs of children and their caregivers/parents. All children have the need for and the right to appropriate and positive parenting. Positive parenting has been linked to resilience that is especially important in conflict settings. Such ongoing care and support include making sure the child has protection from physical danger, opportunities to engage in activities that support cognitive development, support in the development of self-worth, opportunity for socialization to become group member and to have an identity, i.e. a sense of belonging, positive role models among others. Activity 2: Rehabilitation and equipment of 20 preschool facilities in the 20 km zone along the demarcation line in NGCA. DDC will provide assistance in the rehabilitation and equipment of 20 preschool facilities in the 20 km zone along the demarcation line in NGCA in order to ensure that children affected by the conflict receive appropriate care and attention. School kits and other materials will be provided, classrooms rehabilitated and equipped, and community-based caregivers committees enforced to improve the school environment. If financial assistance is provided to these institutions, it will be possible to establish children's centers on their basis that meet all the challenges of the conflict.Activity 3: Provide humanitarian assistance to sedentary children with specialized wheelchairs. DDC will provide humanitarian assistance to children with disabilities in the form of strollers for sedentary people and electronic equipment for distance learning. Outcome 2: Mine risk education for children is adapted and implemented on a community basis with the participation of children themselvesActivity 1: Conduct course of adapted classes "Knowledge that saves lives". DDC will conduct a course of adapted lessons "Knowledge that saves lives" including information of mine and psychological safety, first aid, safe life so that children learn about the risks connected to mines and explosive items in contaminated areas. The knowledge will help to reduce the number of victims and the threat to the life of children by acquiring necessary skills for survival in a conflict.
