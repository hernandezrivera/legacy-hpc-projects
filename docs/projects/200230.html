Project Summary

The proposed project seeks to promote human development for vulnerable children and families, with particular reference to education, protection and psycho-social support in the school, family and community spheres in Beirut and Mount Lebanon area. In particular, the project aims to increase the resilience of children affected by trauma enhancing access to services, supporting the intervention capacities of relevant local institutional and civil society actors, and thus fostering the integration of the refugee community with the host community.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Based on the needs assessment conducted in 9 schools by INTERSOS Shelter Department, 3 schools will be identified as priorities for the implementation of structural works on school buildings through this project. The following work will be carried out and will benefit 2,270 minors (boys and girls): ● Provide access to ground floor classrooms and toilets for children with disabilities, through the installation of access ramps for disabled persons and other minor interventions where necessary ● Installation of sustainable energy systems (specifically solar panels) ● Roof water proofing ● Repair of doors and windows and/or supply and installation of new ones ● Rehabilitation and adaptation of sanitary facilities and water pipes.Due to the severe financial crisis occurring in Lebanon, Lebanese schools and the Ministry of Education, suffer from an exceptional shortage of public resources, which limits their ability to purchase teaching materials and stationery. Also, households in Lebanon are struggling to meet the basic needs and are being forced to change their main priorities. INTERSOS, as a complement to its other activities will provide the following: ● New and updated teaching materials inline with MEHE requirements in addition to stationery supplies to the identified schools ● Cash for Education for 300 children ● Awareness sessions with the communities surrounding the schools on the importance of education ● Teachers capacity building

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

INTERSOS will ensure safety for the staff and management of business continuity through its standard operational tool tailored to each area of implementation: the Comprehensive Planning Process (CPP). This dynamic analytical, contingency and risk mitigation tool is complemented in Lebanon by a set of tailored procedures for all the main risks identified (SOPs for cash safety management, movements, Covid-19, centers safety, demonstrations, etc.), while also relying on set-ups that are built on past years lessons learnt (e.g., energy, exchange rate and fuel crisis management) to ensure business continuity through an agile and flexible adaptation to the context.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

This intervention upholds all four Sphere Protection Principles and the 9 Core Humanitarian Standards. INTERSOS strictly adheres to the do no harm imperative and strengthens people’s safety and dignity by adopting a beneficiary-centred approach. AGD Mainstreaming approach will be ensured, taking into consideration communities’ specific needs and risks. Accountability to Affected Populations is ensured in all the project implementation phases, and INTERSOS is committed to the beneficiaries' participation at all stages. To ensure accountability to PoC, complaints and feedback mechanisms will be available through different modalities to receive and respond to beneficiaries’ needs and suggestions. MEAL team will conduct regular monitoring, including field inspections and follow up with the teams, to ensure the activities are conducted properly. Anti-fraud measures and policies in place, as well as CP Safeguarding Policy and PSEA Policy that are signed by all staff, partners and volunteers.
