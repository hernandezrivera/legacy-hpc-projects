Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2021

End Date

31/12/2021

Project Summary

This project aims to restore and expand capacity for life-saving nutrition interventions for children under five, pregnant and lactating woman of IDP’s suffering from Acute Malnutrition (AM) and promotion of improved behaviors, practices and attitude in relation to appropriate infant feeding, hygiene and nutrition pratices based on SBCC strategy. The target group of the project are about 7300 Beneficiaries, based in implementation of actions aimed at promoting a good diet, hygiene care, exclusive breastfeeding and adequate complementary feeding, screening and treatment of SAM and MAM cases.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Even before the start of armed attacks, Cabo Delgado and Nampula was the provinces with the highest rate of chronic malnutrition in the country as a result of a poor diet, inadequate nutrition, hygiene and sanitation practices, including poor coverage of primary health care. With the deterioration of security conditions in most northern districts of Cabo Delgado and an exponential increase in the entry of IDP's in the last months in districts such as Metuge (with around 7 provisional resettlement centers), Montepuez, Ancuabe and Chiure in Cabo Delgado and Nampula city, Meconta and Monapo in Nampula Province, most of the families do not have the minimum conditions to have adequate food, hygiene and health care, which is important for children under the age of 5 and pregnant and lactating women. For this reason, health systems are reaching their maximum capacity to provide assistance and the number of children with severe acute malnutrition is increasing significantly each day as a result of these immediate shocks and in the medium term it can become chronic malnutrition.In this context, SEPPA, an organization that works with food assistance to IDP’s in Cabo Delgado and SBCC in Nutrition and WASH in Nampula and with experience in community nutrition activities, intends to implement a project to promote good nutrition and hygiene practices based on SBCC and implementation PIN pakage. With this project SEPPA expect to directly benefit around 2500 CU5 and 2200 pregnant and lactating women in the districts of Metuge, Montepuez, Chiure and Ancuabe in Cabo Delgado and Nampula city, Meconta and Monapo in Nampula Province.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

PRIORITY ACTIVITIES OF THE PROJECT:1. Implement active case finding and referral of malnourished children and monitor the nutrition situation through MUAC screenings;2. Undertake nutrition assessments and SMART surveys for assessing the nutritional status of the populations at risk;3. Capacity strengthening of health staff on PRN protocol, outpatient and inpatient treatment, M&E and stock management;4. Conduct awareness-raising sessoes on good hygiene, sanitation and nutrition practices, including infant feeding (exclusive breastfeeding and adequate complementary feeding) based on SBCC strategy;5. Establishment and training of Model Mothers Groups for the dissemination of nutrition messages, cooking demonstrations and support for other mothers in adopting good nutritional practices including infant feeding mainly for women with children with MAM;6. Conduct nutritive cooking demonstration sessions based on locally available products;7. Promotion of community debates about nutrition and hygiene with the involvement of community commitees and IDP's;8. Training of APEs and community activists in SBC and implementation of the PIN to ensure that most of IDPs have access to nutrition messages and PIN interventions;9. Community radio to provide radio spots, live debates, and call in programmes related to IYCF and HIV10. Distribution of Cooking demonstration kits to groups of models mothers;11. Establishment of save space for adolescent to participate in sessions of nutrition and sexual health including prevention of early pregnancies.12. Sensitization in prevention of SEA and Establishment of community comitees, complain boxes and LINHA Verde/Linha Fala Crianca to adress all complains or feedbacks regarding SEA. GEOGRAFICAL TARGETED AREAS IN THE PROJECT:1. Cabo Delgado (Metuge, Ancuabe, Chiure and Montepuez);2. Nampula ((Nampula city, Meconta and Monapo).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project identifies the potential risks and avoids causing damage, as well as seeking to guarantee people's participation and empowerment. To this end, the following activities within the components will be developed, such as:1. Promotion of awareness and mobilization activities on the basic rights and dignity of women, men, girls and boys, without discrimination, for the general well-being, avoiding unnecessary anguish, fear and pain (eg, physical, emotional and spiritual );2. Construction of local and community strategies for community self-help groups, promoting community ownership and control over aspects related to security, protection and available resources, using participatory commitment and decision-making processes.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Promotion of local activities to return the optimized functioning of affected communities and individuals through their involvement in social activities and meetings in local structures such as schools, community centers, churches, mosques and local organizations.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In order to identify and maximize the inclusion of people with disabilities, our project will take a planning process that should include all levels and sectors, namely:1. Project definition (targets, objectives and the scope of an emergency plan) - here special attention must be paid to people with disabilities and the products and services they need must be created;2. Participation of planning groups and representatives of sectors, ethnic groups, gender, with a view to analyzing data, problems, examining the vulnerabilities and risks of people with disabilities;3. Identify resources to implement risk mitigation strategies, based on the best information and analysis available;4. Provide recommendations for strategies for formulating initiatives aimed at action and for new synergies at local, national, regional and international levels, with a focus on people with disabilities;5. Definition of roles and responsibilities in the program management process;6. Finally, documentation of positive experiences. IMPORTANT NOTE: To this end, community mobilization is a process in which our organization works together with the community to meet their needs, where the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance that not only contributes greatly for our understanding of disability, but we will also share good practices that have already changed the lives of people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

As the project will be implemented in resettlement areas, it is worth noting that we recognize that human beings constantly modify the environment, generating positive and negative environmental impacts on it. Eventually, the fact that we now have a higher population density in the districts where the project will be implemented, these impacts can lead to a vast ecological imbalance, decimating some species and devastating ecosystems, once the populations resort to local resources for building houses (bambo, stakes, etc.) and cook food (firewood).In order to try to avoid and minimize such impacts, we have to hold coordination meetings with the main bodies related to the preservation of the environment that supported us in the development and implementation of guidelines and mechanisms; among them, the Mitigating and Compensatory Measures of Environmental Impacts are considered very important. To this end, environmental programs and control measures must be identified in order to minimize, compensate and even eliminate the negative impacts of the installation of the project, as well as measures that can enhance the positive socio-environmental impacts arising from the project.
