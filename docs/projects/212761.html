Project Summary

International NGO will continue providing CCCM to ensure equitable access to assistance,
protection, and services to improve the quality of life and dignity for approximately 80681
IDPs living in Mahmoudly, Tuwahinah, Talsaman, Newroz, Al-Hol and Roj camps and 40
Collective Centers (CC) in Al-Hasakeh Governorate & 15 CCs in Tabqa. Under WASH we will provide domestic water in Al Hol camp, phase, SWM and dislodging in all camps and CCs, hygiene promotion activities in all sites, and few small WASH maintainance in all sites.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

After over a decade of conflict, civilians in Syria continue to suffer as nearly 14.6 million people need humanitarian assistance, an increase of 1.2 million from 2021. Across the country, food insecurity remains extremely high. Syria ranks among the 10 most food-insecure countries with an estimated 12 million severely food-insecure individuals. Within NES, almost 2.6 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance, including 290,000IDPs living in last-resort sites. In Al-Hol, Roj Camps Mahmoudly, Newroz, Talsaman and Tuwahinah in NES, International NGO is responsible for Camp Coordination and Camp Management services (CCCM) for the 80,681 internally displaced Syrian IDPs, Iraqi refugees, and third-country nationals (TCNs) in coordination with the U.N. and other Humanitarian Actors (HAs). Despite this coordinated assistance, camp residents continue to need ongoing support to meet their essential and basic services.
