Project Summary

According to JMCNA, approximately 8.9 million people are affected by the prevalent poor WASH
conditions in Somalia. The attendant effects of the flooding are already being felt in other sectors not least among them the provision of basic services. A cholera outbreak fueled by limited access to safe drinking water, poor sanitation and displacement has already been reported in some of the flood affected districts, underscoring the need for urgent repair and rehabilitation to damaged WASH infrastructure and the provision of clean water. More cases of AWD are being reported in children under five years old, who are particularly vulnerable to disease, especially if their immune systems are compromised by malnutrition. While response is already underway, the WASH Cluster has reported critical gaps in Balcad, Marka and Wanlaweyn where no WASH assistance has been received despite high risk levels for acute watery diarrhoea (AWD). 2020 post GU results show favorable outcomes yet for nutrition it is estimated that in 2021
around 2.3 million people (1.14 million for treatment services and 1.16 million for preventive services) in
Somalia will require both lifesaving curative and prevent. it is also a cause of relapse (ADD) when treated patients and children returning to their communities where safe water and sanitation coverages are limited, resulting in limited sustainability of health programs. According to JMCNA, 53% of the
IDPs face challenges in terms of access to WASH services in Somalia (53% as highest pertaining to access
to soap and handwashing facilities). As Somalia’s vulnerability to climate change is projected to increase
especially with the upcoming La-Nina where there is a likelihood of drought, the country might be further
affected by water scarcity, which calls for a stronger collaboration between the humanitarian and
development partners to work on collective outcomes. PLWs and children in IDP camps are more prone and therefore vulnerable to the consequences and impacts of the lack of access to safe drinking water and sanitation services.
JF in the proposed intervention will work towards improving access to water hygiene and sanitation to the affected HH within the selected districts . This key factors that will be adhered to is a do no harm to the beneficiaries and in adherence to protection of the targeted beneficiaries. The support provision will be to people living with disabilities.
The project will aim at increasing timely and equitable access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services to 12000 (women, men, girls and boys) IDPs and host communities. Water trucking will be an immediate response to emergencies. WASH needs were identified by Juba Foundation nutrition project staff while providing nutrition services to IDPs and Host community through focus group discussion with community members especially women and community leaders. Limited access to safe clean water has conditioned the IDPs and vulnerable/ crises affected host communities in the Project target locations in Lower Shabelle. The aim of the project is to ensure that the affected populations are ensured with safe, equitable, and sustainable access to sufficient quantity of water, affected populations have access to safe, sanitary, and hygienic living environment through provision of sanitation that are secure, sanitary, user¬ friendly and gender appropriate. Currently the community use river water that is exposed to pollution due to careless dumping of garbage into and along the banks of the river. There are common cases of IDP and Vulnerable host communities women, girls covering over 500m in search of safe water as well as those queuing for around 15-25 minutes at water points to acquire water far beyond WASH cluster sphere standards of not more than 3 minutes.
JF will coordinate with all the stakeholders to ensure that there is proper coordination of response and adequate early warnings in cases of calamities like flood and droughts.

Project Activities

1. Water trucking in locations that need urgent water supply using a phased response strategy (i.e. working on rehab of available infrastructure in parallel to minimize water trucking).2. Rehabilitation and/or extension of existing water infrastructures damaged/affected as a result of floods3. Construction of new water infrastructures equipped with appropriate pumping and power systems, tanks and distribution networks where rehabilitation of existing sources is not possible.4. Establishment of gender balanced water management committees to ensure continuity of services, appropriate operation and maintenance as well as strengthen women participation in WASH projects. 5. Installation of new and/or rehabilitation of sanitation facilities. The rehabilitation work should focus on latrines damaged by floods and those in need of desludging/emptying. 6. Hygiene promotion campaigns focusing on hand washing with soap and hygiene kits distribution, including female specific hygiene items (sanitary clothes, etc.).7. Market based approaches (CVA modalities) to be applied to the WASH activities where applicable.8. All WASH facilities must be set/designed to remain functional in case of flooding while also ensuring integration of protection elements and sustainable solutions in all response activities.9. AWD outbreak prevention; preventive treatment of unprotected water sources, provision of household water treatment and safe storage products (HWTSS) supported by regular water quality monitoring.

Please provide further explanation

The Project aims at promoting durable solution/resilience of displacement affected and host communities. This objective will be achieved by ensuring that the targeted communities have access to clean water and sanitation facilities. In order to achieve this result, the project will be integrated with other ongoing actions in the areas of intervention to promote access to basic services for the targeted communities such as schools and hospitals in order to build their resilience and to promote long term solutions to displacement. To address the risks associated with people living with disability, The project will ensure that the community wash volunteers who on regular basis will be interacting with the beneficiaries, identify HHs having people living with disabilities to be able to get adequate service delivery.
