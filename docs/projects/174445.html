Project Summary

FR Peshawar and FR Kohat are among most vulnerable districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhuwa in terms of protection and mainstreaming. Older People and Persons with disabilities are among the most vulnerable groups neglected in any emeregency response and humanitarian support activities. 43.80% older people are those who have one or more disability in their later age while 2-3% people are specialized people with some sort of disabilities in Pakistan. The number of persons with disabilities is even high in FR Peshawar and FR Kohat due to IED blasts and other terrorist activities in the region. The project will address the specialized needs of Older People and Persons with disabilities including Physiotherapy, age assitive technology, protection monitoring and assessment and their inclusion in emergency response activities. The project will specifically target age and disability needs for all genders in humanitarian response activities.
