Project Summary

Improving the quality of and access to health care and protection services for displaced and host populations and vulnerable groups in 10 health areas of Ndian and Manyu divisions, Southwest region.
In the selected health districts, DEMTOU will support 10 IHFs; for medical consultations for children under 5, adults and the provision of reproductive health care. Thus, in each IHF, the free care package offered by the project will include the provision of curative consultations for children under 5 years of age and adults for PEC of uncomplicated cases, support for routine EPI to improve vaccination coverage. The project will also ensure the provision of reproductive health care, in particular: ANC, assisted deliveries, PoNC, vaccination, family planning. DEMTOU will finally support the referral system for IHFs towards the referral health center and definitely, to secondary level.
Depending on needs, DEMTOU will support health structures through the supply of inputs, equipment, medical materials and additional human resources. Inputs for family planning will be provided through UNFPA and MINSANTE.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

In the sense of strengthening the national health system following an express request from the DMOs of Mamfe and Ekondo-Titi, and followed by an assessment which revealed much greater needs; as part of this action,DEMTOU will support 10 health facilities and 02 referral centers. Support for the management of common diseases, for reproductive health care in the 10 IHFs in Mamfe and Ekondo-Titi. The objective is to bring together primary health care and reproductive health care to beneficiaries. In the selected health districts, DEMTOU will support 10 IHFs; for medical consultations for children under 5, adults and the provision of reproductive health care. Thus, in each IHF, the free care package offered by the project will include the provision of curative consultations for children under 5 years of age and adults for PEC of uncomplicated cases, support for routine EPI to improve vaccination coverage. The project will also ensure the provision of reproductive health care, in particular: ANC, assisted deliveries, PoNC, vaccination, family planning. DEMTOU will finally support the referral system for IHFs towards the referral health centers and definitely, to secondary level. Depending on needs, DEMTOU Humanitarian will support health structures through the supply of inputs, equipment, medical materials and additional human resources. Inputs for family planning will be provided through UNFPA and MINSANTE. Community health promotion The strengthening of community activities makes it possible in the medium and long term to improve the attendance rate and the use of health structures. DEMTOU will work with a network of 40 community health workers in both health districts who will be responsible for disseminating messages within the communities and in accordance with the cultural values of the beneficiaries. The leaders will liaise with the population to raise awareness and get messages across for better community support. The topics covered during the sessions will depend on the needs identified, but will mainly focus on common pathologies (infection, prevention and control), reproductive health (sensitization of pregnant women to the importance of pregnancy monitoring, recognition of danger signs during pregnancy, the dangers of community childbirth etc.). The Health Promoters will also work on the search for dropouts and the supervision of community health workers. Awareness-raising activities will target 45,000 people. Nevertheless, DEMTOU will take care to promote the participation of men in awareness-raising activities because of their decision-making role on issues relating to the health of women and children.
