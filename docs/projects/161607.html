Project Summary

The Proposed GBV prevention and mitigation project will aim to sustain and scale up GBV prevention and mitigation interventions as well as build resilience through promoting social and behavioral change to address the root causes of GBV. The project will seek to improve safe access to quality survivor-centred GBV services for survivors of GBV in three (3) local government areas (Bama, Biu , and Haw)ul in Borno state.
This project will use a consultative, phased-in approach to allow SAHEI to work with individuals, groups and institutions over time using different strategies to inspire, encourage and support them in making positive changes in their lives which will eventually influence a change in social norms. For example SAHEI will use the “Engaging Men through Accountable Practices” (EMAP) framework to engage men and adolescent boys during the first six months of the project implementation to address the root causes and contributing factors of GBV, while preparing to roll-out EMAP approach in the next 12 months in communities that meet EMAP criteria.
SAHEI will also conduct advocacy activities to spread information on GBV. IEC materials will be produced and used to educate individuals and incite critical thinking around the consequences of GBV within the family and community, and how negative behaviors contribute to GBV
The activities outlined below are organized under two major areas of intervention: Community prevention, and risk mitigation for women and girls at risk of GBV, and empowerment services for women and girls.
Project activities will reach an estimated 155,411 persons, but the exact number cannot yet be targeted due to the very fluid situation.

1 - Needs

Gender-based violence is a pervasive public health, affecting the physical and psychological health of survivors, as well as the health and wellbeing of families and communities. Women and girls are particularly vulnerable. Therefore this project will address and assessed the affected persons of concern need in term of improving the physical & mental well being, living standards and recovery and resilience of the affected survivors. In relation to these mentioned needs, SAHEI will focus on prevention, training and creating awareness rising programmes for women, girls, men and boys at risk of sexual and gender based violence. Special consideration will be given to protection of children, persons with disabilities and older persons. Referral would be made where necessary and will done in a way that does not put the in format to danger. Data will be collected, store and analyze for Donors/partner need and will be done on safe and ethical standard. SAHEI will registered and profile the survivors and vulnerable at risk groups for skill acquisition and benefit core relief items. In addition, community resilience systems/institutions will be strengthen thatprevent and respond to gender based violence including harmful practices.
