1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

IOM will build this intervention by taking into account key results and best practices of the previous phases, as well as the region's humanitarian conditions and needs. IOM will implement a multi-sector emergency response programme in line with the 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP)[2] for Northern Mozambique, that operates across CCCM, Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFI), (Emergency) Health, Protection and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS), and DTM.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

DTM PRIORITY ACTIVITIESI. Detailed, locational-level DTM monitoring across conflict-affected communities in Macomia, Metuge and Mueda: Activity 4.1.1 Continued roll-out of DTM Emergency Tracking Tool and Baseline Assessments in Macomia, Metuge and Mueda ensuring data collection with age, sex, and vulnerability disaggregation. Activity 4.1.2. DTM location-level thematic assessments across host communities, displacement site settings and areas of return (in coordination with Shelter, Protection and CCCM partners). Activity 4.1.3. Timely development and dissemination of DTM products for humanitarian response planning (Emergency Tracking Movement Alerts, Flash Reports, Dashboards and Maps). GEOGRAPGIC AREASCabo Delgado

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Training on data collection techniques, methodologies, introducing IOM’s data protection, general protection, disability, and inclusion as well as PSEA standards in data collection efforts for local authorities, partners and IOM staff.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Direct data collection and data management that is gender responsive and is inclusive of sex and age disaggregated data, population needs vulnerabilities

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disaggregated data collection that includes identification of persons with disabilities in process of monitoring conditions and needs of affected populations
