Project Summary

The project aims to recover and expand the economic situation of the returnees, Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) and host community business owners in the Aleppo governorate of Northern Syria, specifically targeting Ar-Ra'ee and Azaz sub-districts as hot spots for high number of returnees and IDPs.
These early recovery efforts will be achieved by, firstly, conducting a needs assessment on understanding the factors of production (Labor, Capital, Input and Technology) for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) which will lead to development of customized business development plans for local SMEs; and secondly, by providing a combination of business development services in the form of consultancy and seed grants based on the needs of the local businesses to revive and enhance the production capacities of local businesses in the targeted locations.
The overall aim of this intervention is to improve the local population’s access to the labour market by stabilizing and/or expanding local businesses in order to generate further jobs. In order to enhance social cohesion and reduce competition for resources, the project will target 90 businesses, supporting 30% IDPs and 70% host community enterprises, including 15 female business owners.
The factors of production needs assessment will be conducted in the form of a base and end-line multi-sectorial assessments on the local economy in the targeted locations, defining the gaps in factors of production as well assessing the causes of these gaps, their effect on the production capacity of local SMEs in the selected locations.
Upon acceptance into the programme, WATAN will be responsible for providing business development services through a combination of consultancy and seed grants. The support package can contribute towards the purchase of assets including consumable raw materials, initial premise rental payments, water and fuel infrastructure, electricity generation, repair of equipment and productive assets, the rehabilitation of work premises, and staffing costs. Business consultancy will continue to be offered after delivery of the support package, with WATAN coordinating referrals among IDPs and the host community to job opportunities that have been generated as a result of the project.

4 - Needs

The proposed project will focus on supporting SMEs in Ar-Ra'ee and Azaz within the Aleppo Governorate, prioritizing business models that either require revitalization support or have the potential to create a conducive environment for further livelihood development. Aleppo is the most densely populated governorate in Syria with a population of 3.3 million and it is divided into different areas of ever-changing control. The protracted conflict has led to widespread evacuation and displacement of civilians within Syria, with newly arriving IDPs from Al-Bab, Idleb, A’rima, Tadaf and Ar Ra’ea itself continuing to exacerbate the capacity of host communities in Ar-Ra'ee and Azaz. The sub-districts of Menbij and Abu Qalqal in the governorate of Aleppo reported the biggest number of returnees. there is a new wave of displacement towards the northern part of Syria coming from Idlib and western side of Aleppo, this will lead to a shortage of services provided from the different sectors.A detailed assessment will be provided once the project starts.

5 - Implementation of activities

Activity 1 - Provide services for SMEs to enhance their production and business capacity1.1.1 Develop a framework and template for the NPM's local market production needs analysis1.1.2 Conduct local market production needs analysis through NPM which will identify priority sectors, analysis on factors of production, gaps, and potential business target groups1.1.3 Develop seed grants mechanism in line with the outcomes/recommendations of the needs analysis1.1.4 Conduct a prioritization assessment to select 90 SMEs to be integrated into the program/call for expression of interest. (This will include financial and management assessments, taking into consideration operational status, size, production rate, growth potential, finances and asset/technical needs)1.1.5 Deliver customized business development plans and consulting services to each SME, which will address the current operational/production issues, gaps and enhance overall productivity potential1.1.6 Procure essential services or materials/assets/equipment according to the business development plans and submitted response plan from the business owners through in-kind seeds grants Activity 2 – Provide support for jobs development1.2.1 Monitor and follow up job vacancies, referring job seekers through a combination of online and print media1.2.2 Conduct follow-up visits to SMEs to monitor the process1.2.3 Conduct final assessment of each beneficiary to measure the impact of business development support1.2.4 Conduct regular monitoring and reporting including end multi-sectorial evaluation to assess the impact of the project on the local economy of the targeted locations
