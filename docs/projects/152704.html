Project Summary

Malnutrition situation in Unity state in Mayendit, Leer and panyijar continues to be a major concern showing localized pockets of humanitarian needs which are being attributed to severe food insecurity , poor access to health nutrition services ,extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene. According to the September 2018 – March 2019 IPC project analysis its places Mayendit and leer at phase 5 with GAM (WHZ) 15.0- 29.9 while panyijar is at phase 4 with nutrition cluster fortnight monitoring showing panyijal among the counties with GAM 16.2,MAM 18.9 and prevalence of 35.2 to PLW. Thus in this project UNIDOR seek to continue in providing live saving support to the conflict affected and most vulnerable population,Targeting children age 6- 59 months, Pregnant and lactating mothers and people with disabilities through the provision of integrated nutrition services to the needy population.The project will provide treatment and rehabilitation to children suffering from severe acute malnutrition( SAM) 1198 in leer, 3496 in panyijar and 4496 in mayendit and moderate acute malnutrition(MAM) Leer 2862,5008 panyijar and 6440 mayendit and treatment of 230 girls and 220 boys severely malnourished children with medical complication in the 2 SC in mayendit and treatment of 8000 PLW in the respective counties and will also provide linkages and referral within the programs like Health,WASH ,Protection/GBV and FSL through referrals and provision of key interventions with the programs i.e OTP,TSFP and SC.UNIDOR will ensure that there is integration of Early childhood Development at every OTP/TSFP facilities so as to ensure there are friendly spaces for stimulation for the U5 children .
there will be a strong component of surveillance through continuous program monitoring that will also include SMART surveys and Mass MUAC screening. Routine community mobilization and sensitization, active case finding, referrals and follow ups at the household level will be done to strengthen all the components with key nutrition practices ,hygiene and promotion and the MIYCN practices.
The main objective of this project is to ensure: 1,Timely,quality and quantity lifesaving management of acute malnutrition for the most vulnerable and at risk children boys and girls under five years of age and pregnant and lactating mothers by reaching at least 80% of SAM, 62% of MAM and 53 % of PLW in Mayendit, Panyijar and Leer counties.2, Increased integration of Nutrition with Health, WASH, GBV and FSL activities in mayendit , panyijar and leer in line with the cluster objective 3, Increased access to Maternal Infant and Young Child Nutrition preventing under nutrition to the vulnerable and at risk in mayendit , panyijar and leer counties4, continuous nutrition situation monitoring, analysis and utilization of early warning information for timely coordination response and decision making and ensure we report to the cluster and taking part in assessments and surveys
UNIDOR will continue with nutrition activities for instance community nutrition assessment through community screening, treatment of MAM and SAM U5 children ,Reaching mothers with MIYCN messages , conducting CMAM training to health workers , screening and testing for malaria for U5 children ,RDT trainings to the health workers, de-worming of children 12-59 months twice a year, Vitamin A supplementation to children 6-59 years twice a year and Intergration of Early Child Development with Nutrition services

Needs

Mayendit September 2018 –March 2019 IPC analysis projects Mayendit to be in Phase 5 with GAM(WHZ) 15.0-29.9% attributed to the likely hood of to poor harvest; possible returns from within and outside of the country may cause further pressure on already scarce resources; insecurity, lack of freedom of movement and extreme depletion of livelihoods prevails. As a result, extreme levels of acute food insecurity are expected to persist in many areas Intermittent armed conflicts & cattle raiding in Southern Unity with recent one in April 2018 exacerbated humanitarian situation as the general populace live in fear due to the growing tensions in the area. Utilizing qualitative livelihood change data for the area & the dis-aggregation of acute malnutrition & mortality data Mayendit showed localized pockets of humanitarian need. SMART survey conducted by UNIDOR in November, 2017 showed a prevalence of GAM as 27.4%(21.4-34.4 95% C.I.) and SAM rate of 5% (3.1-7.9 95% C.I.). Poor nutrition situation was majorly attributed to insecurity, poor/non-existent access to safe water, sanitation & access to affordable PHC & food insecurity. The IPC-May-July 2018 put Mayendit among 11 IPC4 counties likely tipping into the famine and according to nutrition cluster`s fortnight monitoring information nutrition situation continued to be in critical with GAM levels above 27.4% and MAM at 18.2% with the prevalence of 35.2% in PLW. RRM conducted in collaboration with UNICEF in Dablual in July 2018 showed Proxy GAM (38.9% ) . UNIDOR in collaboration with other Humanitarian actors seek to ensure continued nutrition support to affected populations factoring in promotion of equity and access in participation of men, women, boys & girls in needs assessment. Integration with health has been on going in Mayendit because UNIDOR is the lead IP for health supporting 10PHCUs and 2PHCCs where the nutrition sites are as well located UNIDOR is WASH and child protection partner in Mayendit as well. Therefore, identification & referral of the malnourished children will be done by HWs & the management of SAM case with screening medical complications be done in Mayendit PHCC where SC is located. Nyal (Panyijiar county): The September 2018 – March 2019 IPC analysis place Panyijar at IPC phase 4 and the fortnight nutrition cluster monitoring information shows Panyijiar among 4counties with GAM levels above 16.2% and MAM of 18.9% with the prevalence of 35.2 in PLW. IPC-May-July 2018 put Panyijiar county as one of the 11counties classified as IPC4. High morbidity as result of diarrheal diseases and fever, poor health seeking behavior, and food insecurity were cited in November 2017 SMART surveyUNIDOR supportins 4PHCUs where OTP/TSFP sites are also located supporting Growth monitoring, MIYCN awareness, integration of nutrition and health management of severe acute malnutrition and Micro nutrient supplementation. UNIDOR is also a child protection and WASH partner in Panyijiar hence, hence an advantage for integration approach as in Mayendit. Leer county: Leer has also been the center of protracted conflict with continuous displacement of people to the islands & Leer TPA in Leer town. In 2017 a famine was declared in Leer but later on recovered due to intense humanitarian response however, Leer still remains with likelihood of another round of famine because there has been no significant improvement in the food insecurity, poor/non-existent access to safe water, sanitation & access to affordable care. According to IPC-May-July 2018, Leer is among 5counties classified as very critical (IPC5) with malnutrition rate increased from 28% in 2017 to 42% in 2018 same period, according to nutrition cluster`s fortnight monitoring information nutrition situation continued to be in critical with GAM levels above 20.1% and MAM at 18.9% with the of 35.2 % in PLW.September 2018 –March 2019 IPC analysis projects leer to be in Phase 5 with GAM(WHZ) 15.0-29.9%
