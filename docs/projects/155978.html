Needs

As result of the large number and growing population in the town, and the calm in the area and the return of the people to the town. Resulting in a significant increase in waste and accumulation of garbage on the roadsides. Because the local council does not have garbage trucks, as well as the lack of garbage containers. The purpose of the project in the first is to eliminate the causative agent of this epidemic through the disposal of waste and garbage in more effective ways, and aims to provide pesticides to spray in the village to eliminate insects

Implementation of activities

The project implementation plan takes several steps it is:Provide insecticides to eliminate the insects ,Disposal of garbage and waste accumulated in the city by providing trucks to transport garbage out of the city and disposal in ways that prevent harm to the peopleRecruitment of workers for cleaning in the city• spraying pesticides in the streets and in the houses (due to the possibility of having a mosquito in the cracks of the walls) Insurance of containers for rubbishConduct awareness campaigns on how can prevent from the garbage which bring the insects

Protection Risk Analysis

The risks to the project-based team, as well as some of the beneficiaries of the project, should be taken into account. These risks are: Workers who cleaning the city maybe incur verbal or physical abuse because of ignorance about the causes and consequences of this work, and maybe exposed to steal their equipmentThis risk can be mitigated by informing the beneficiaries of the work, the procedures and the crisis measures during the project implementation period , and allocation of equipment protection factorSuch as dividing the operation on days and evacuating the residential neighborhoods of the population until the air is purified of the smell, so as not to cause the odor to harass the people

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

. The most important risk that this project could cause is the spread of the garbage which causes spread the Leishmania insects , that we will could not be controlled in itThis project will significantly reduce the spread of the garbage with more caring for the keep the city clean
