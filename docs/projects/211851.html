Project Summary

This programme falls under a multi-annual framework (2022-2025) across DRC operations in the Middle East (Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Turkey), designed to address a critical gap of durable solutions. As a scale-up from the previous years, DRC in Syria will implement an integrated Economic Recovery and Protection programme in Homs with the aim of supporting individuals in achieving self-reliance and resilience through increased access to sustainable livelihoods, while ensuring vulnerable populations have improved access to rights and entitlements through protection services - thereby contributing to improved well-being. Activities will continue to target vulnerable individuals, particularly women, ensuring they have equitable access to income, services, and opportunities. DRC will provide structured psychosocial support (PSS) and case management, and link individuals receiving protection services with livelihoods interventions. Given the vulnerability status of participants, livelihoods activities will be initiated through consumption support to ensure individuals are able to meet their basic needs and can effectively engage in income generation opportunities. Following the suitability/feasibility assessment in parallel to career counselling, DRC will provide a combination of Vocational Trainings and Business Development Support (BDS trainings, BSGs).
