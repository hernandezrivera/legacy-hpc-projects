Project Summary

Bait Hasan supply and hygiene promotion project aims to improve the health situation of 1,599 people in Bait Hasan region. The community in Bait Hasan are currently suffering from chronic health problems due to lack of access to clean and adequate water and hygiene education that in turn resulted in poor economic status. The project will address these key needs which form the basis of all development through participatory, inclusive and effective means to bring about the following life changing project purposes:
- Fulfillment of fundamental of human right to water by Ensure equitable, sufficient and sustainable access to basic quality WASH services, available when needed in accordance with safety and dignity of the unserved, under-served and most vulnerable Palestinians under occupation in Bait Hasan by enhance access to water through installation of water networks and construction of water reservoir.
- Strengthen national WASH sector accountability, governance system, advocacy, evidence-based planning, and coordination mechanism including inter-sectorial capacity .
The project will have primary beneficiaries of 1599 people with access to safe and adequate water and hygiene education, of this figure around 500 are students that will benefit from the constructed water facilities in educational schools.
Union of Agriculture Work Committee will implement the project with the Council and the target beneficiaries and CBOs, the project will be implemented by integrating water supply, facilities and mass hygiene education as a package, community participation, and Capacity development of CBOs.

Needs

Water is an essential condition for life, which is indispensable for development, ensuring nutrition, providing health and environmental services, A needs assessment conducted in May 2018 reveals that Bait Hasan community in the Governorate in Nablus are in dire need of improved access to water services. The needs assessment reveals that in this village “there is no network water available, and the families take the water by water tanker from A number of unsafe and unreliable sources. The magnitude of the problem is illustrated by an increase in water demand in the summer, where families cannot access water more than five times per month. Social security of the community requires the accessibility to clean water sources and the necessity to secure water and sanitation. Due to low coverage of improved water supply and the utilization of polluted water sources, Bait Hasan inhabitants are at risk of increased mortality.Water sources at Bait Hasan are limited to rainwater collected in domestic wells as well as water tankers from unreliable water.The results of the survey carried out for a number of residents of the region and the council showed the following.1. Activities

A considerable part of Bait Hasan is located in Area C and consisting of 1150 individuals who are not connected to a water network similarly to those living within the other part of the community within Areas A & B. Activities are designed to enhance accessibility to water sources and increase water storage in significance of better water consumption and reduced expenditure allocated to purchase water. Furthermore, the activities are planned to improve the sphere of water and sanitation in a community that utilizes unreliable water sources and requires the availability of a water network. Therefore, the project outputs are assumed as follows: • The safe water supply coverage of the districts increased.• Elapsed time to fetch water for rural women and girls reduced after project implementation,• The project district population with access to and use of sanitation facilities increased.Therefore, the project includes the following activities:• Area1 : installation of water pipeline with capacity of 70-80 cups/hour with 4 "diameter conveyor line with 700 m length of 2 diameter distributor line with a length of 500m in addition to 40 house connections. Area 2: installation of water pump with capacity of 70-80 cups/hour with 4 "diameter conveyor line with 1320 m length of 2 diameter distributor line with a length of 660m and 1 diameters for 1 Km in addition to 120 house connections. •Area3: Construction of a 200 m3 ground tank, installation of water pump with capacity of 80 cups with 6 "diameter conveyor line with 700 m length of 2 diameter distributor line • Awareness raising sessions for both men and women will be organized separately to promote good hygiene practices (focusing on hand washing with soap, self-cleaning, etc), saving and recycling of water will be provided to women and men at household and community level.

Indicators

Monitoring and Evaluation: A baseline survey will be conducted at initiation of the project, which will be updated and compared to a final evaluation. Information collected through semi structured interviews, household visits and field observations. On-going monitoring will allow UAWC to evaluate methodologies and impact of the activities, to identify challenges and best practices; information will be gathered and analyzed on monthly basis. Particular attention will be paid to the methods of working with local communities and protections committees. UAWC will ensure proper mechanism of asset distribution, gender sensitivities, as well as the appropriateness of the intervention in the target area. Project activities will be monitored by a Project Management Team composed of key stakeholders responsible for monitoring and evaluation; the results communicated to humanitarian actors and project beneficiaries.Indicators: Indicator 1.
