Project Summary

Balad Organization is an active humanitarian NGO and has been working continuously in Idlib and Aleppo for the past 6 years.
Balad has offices and access both in northwest side of Syria as well as in Euphrates shield area. Balad has gained widespread acceptance where it operates and has established good relations with stakeholders such as local communities, council structures, and IDP sites during implementing several projects and responding to newly IDPs in different camps in targeted areas. This facilitates a proper access for all of our teams into all the location of implementing the project and monitoring them.
The organization seeks to bring more protection staff into its team and take advantage of trainings and capacity development opportunities provided by the protection sector.
The main objective of project is increasing awareness of people in targeted communities and camps on protection and improve their well-being, support humanitarian workers to better respond to protection-related issues.
Project will supports affected populations with providing protection services in rapidly changing circumstances to respond to existing gaps and anticipated growing needs for the IDPs in several camps in Deir Hassan, Sarmada, and Ma'arrat Tamasrin, with stress on prevention measures of COVID 19, targeting 18145 IDPs (4574 M, 4610 W, 4503 B, 4458 G) 388 of them with disabilities.
The project intends to provide integrated community-based and individually targeted protection interventions to conflict-affected populations across Northwest Syria, through enhancing the prevention and mitigation of protection risks, prioritizing critical gaps in life-saving protection services, and designed to be people-centered project supports a self-sustainable protective environment for the most vulnerable IDPs, host communities and returnees in targeted areas.
Balad will provide protection services in the camps through 4 outreach mobile teams and 3 centers.
The project targets 5000 boys, girls. Women, and men (including people with disability) through mobile outreach teams covering several sites in targeting areas in coordination with Protection Cluster and 7000 boys, girls. Women, and men (including people with disability) through tent centers in the targeted 18 camps. Community and camps in targeted locations will have access to community-based protection services, including awareness-raising over general protection, Child Protection, GBV protection with adherence to cluster guidance and SOPs such as the Awareness Raising Toolkit, and M&E Toolkit of GBV SC.
The project will contribute to helping to create a safer and more secure environment in targeted locations, and enhancing the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs through supporting the protective environment in Syria.
Main project activities are:
• Conducting rapid general protection assessments and GBV assessments in target locations.
• Raising the awareness about protection and GBV topics through campaigns.
• Conducting awareness-raising activities for children, parents, humanitarian workers and community members on child protection concerns, focusing on following topics (Child Labor, Family tracing and reunification, Basic CP, Child Rights), using IEC materials, home visits and advocacy with service providers
• Expanding outreach services to cover older people, and people with disability (with GBV prevention services).
• Providing referral services.
• Training humanitarian workers on PFA, Confidentiality and referral mechanisms; and Case identification of psychosocial distress.
• Distributing dignity kits.
• Implementing PSEA awareness activities.
Project will ensure of prevention measures of COVID-19 by providing sterilization, masks, instructions brochures, and orientation about COVID-19 prevention.

4 - Needs

Immense humanitarian needs remain for people in northwest Syria (NWS), such as access to food, shelter, and education, protection.The HNO 2021 identified that protection remains a great concern in Syria. These concerns are varied and inter-related and therefore must be met through inter-linked protection services. Women, girls and boys continue to face pervasive violence and a range of protection risks: death, injury, involvement in armed groups, torture, detention, abduction, sexual violence, attacks on schools and hospitals, and denial of humanitarian access. These risks affect those Vulnerable group's wellbeing – weakening their sense of security, purpose, control, and significance, or exercising social agency, and causing profound distress.According to HNO 2021that 13.1 M people (51.5% female, 48.5% male) in need to GP and GBV Services, and 5.9 M children (0-17) (48.4% female, 51.6% male) in need to Child Protection Services.As mentioned in GBV Dashboard that 2,219 GBV actors trained on GBV i.e. 32.6% of HRP target, and 913,731 beneficiaries reached with women and gids enmpowerment änd GBV prevention activities i.e. 54.2% of HRP target.As also mentioned in CP Dashboard that 81.84% of children in need of focused non-specialised services, 48.52% of children in need of specialised services, and 71.48% of parents (or other caregivers) in need of specialised services.Balad works in the targeted sites and implements WASH activities, and has conducted Multi-needs assessment in those camps to verify the needs of population and to check on the available services to ensure no overlapped activities are included in project, we found that large numbers of children, girls, and women suffer from a lack of protection services.

5 - Implementation of activities

The first month of project will be invested in preparations and recruitment, organization will recruit a Protection Specialist to provide a ToT for the field staff on PFA, Confidentiality, Referral Mechanisms, and Case identification of psychosocial distress, then the field staff will provide trainings on the mentioned topics for humanitarian workers.There will be 3 centres and 4 mobile teams for 11 months, field staff will conduct rapid protection assessments in target locations, distribute dignity kit, conduct awareness sessions focusing on (Child Labor, Family tracing and reunification, Basic CP, Child Rights) using IEC materials, home visits and advocacy with service providers, in addition to provide referral services, ensuring that PWD are included in all activities.PSEA Focal Point will provide training for staff, and PSEA activities will be implemented to increase awareness between beneficiaries with a focus on complaint mechanism.Project will ensure of prevention measures of COVID-19 by providing sterilization, masks, instructions brochures.At the beginning, field teams will meet with communities representatives to explain project and its goals, take any suggestions which help in success of project and ensure their support. M&E field officers will send daily reports to M&E officer at HQ to produce monthly reports including implemented activities, challenges, learned lessons, and planned activities for following month. Monitoring tools include surveys, interviews, pictures, recordings, and direct observations. M&E officer is responsible for for managing complaints mechanism so users can submit complaints and suggestions anonymously through complaint boxes , phone number, an email, or by direct communication.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although all Syrian population has been heavily affected by the current crisis, due to traditional local norms, women are exposed to additional protection related risks.as such as the domestic violence, sexual exploitation and child marriage. Also women and girls are exposed to structural and societal risks as the lack of safe spaces and privacy, limited freedom of movement, poverty, unemployment, displacement, financial desperation, and lawlessness, stress associated to war, gender role change, lack of documents. All these poses enormous pressure, Parental violence against children has also been reported as increasing. Protection Risks:1. Lack of quality services and referrals expose survivors to harm, enhancing the risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. Lack of quality services also leads to fewer disclosures and a lack of trust in services. 2. GBV service providers' safety and security because of their role and PTS for dealing with violence. 3. Privacy concerns along with confidentiality and safety might prevent women & girls to access GBV response services Mitigating Measures: 1. Balad will ensure to build the capacity of team members that will be directly engaged in the provision of specialized services. 2. All GBV cases that will be faced through this project will be referred to the protection department to make sure that these cases are dealt with.3. GBV case management service providers ensure confidentiality principles are in line with global standards and the GBV SOPs, ensure data protection protocols and private physical space are in place.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Balad is a member of the PSEA network and works according PSEA network guidance.To prevent any SEA issue from occurring during the implementation period of the project activities, especially the activities in which the project staff and service providers are in direct contact with the beneficiaries, such as the dignity kits distribution.All field activities have been reviewed and assessed for risk factors for PSEA with the use of the PSEA Network Field and Organizational Mitigation and Prevention Checklists and the following prevention and mitigation measures will be taken:1- Balad will make sure that All staff, contractor, and volunteers understand and sign the Code of Conduct.2- Field staff with direct contact with beneficiaries will receive PSEA training 3- Balad will review internal reporting mechanisms and ensure all staff know about reporting mechanisms4- female staff will always be present at the distribution site and involved in the distribution process5- Beneficiaries will be given clear information on timing and content of distribution6- Beneficiaries will be given information that aid is free7- Contractors and their workers will not have direct contact with beneficiaries8- PSEA messaging and awareness raising will be done though pasting PSEA posters and placing PSEA banners at the distribution site or other service place.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Lack of qualification in protection domain in the targeted area.- Lack of protection services in the targeted area especially the specialized one. - Lack of coordination with other sectors like NFI, Livelihood and other services regarding to referrals. - Dealing with huge needs of beneficiaries especially the new IDPs. - Difficulties in targeting Men with awareness raising sessions.- There still be no confirmed cases of COVID-19 Viruses in the project location- Risk Mitigation- Working on providing intensive capacity building plan for the new staff. - Setting a service map including the main services to have a strong and clear referral pathway. - Working on flexible timetable to be suitable for all beneficiaries especially men. - Select a qualified staff to have a good services.- Risk: COVID-19: Mitigation: Provide the project staff with PPE materials.
