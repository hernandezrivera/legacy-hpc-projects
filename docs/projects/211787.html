Project Summary

In this 12 month's education project, (SHO) is going to do light rehabilitation to the infrastructures of 114 schools to create a protective learning environment for 76,008 children (36,488 B & 39,520 G) including 750 CWDs to access quality inclusive formal and non-formal education in 15 of sub-districts in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. These targeted sub-districts have been classified with high severity (between 3 to 5). Based on the HNO 2024.
The protracted crisis in Syria, further compounded by the aftermath of the earthquake continues to negatively impact the education system. There has been a 4% increase in the People in Need (PiN), compared to the previous year. This emphasizes the urgent need for emergency education services to support up to 7.1 million school-aged children and teachers, including children with disabilities.
There are more than 2.45 million Out of School Children (OoSC) and over a million who are at risk of dropping out.
Financial hardship causes the family to pull their children out of school resorting to negative coping strategies, exposing children to gendered protection risks. Learning environments are unconducive due to overcrowding, lack of school furniture/school supplies, and operational WASH facilities. Lack of electricity is affecting education supply, as schools lack heating, cooling, and lighting to operate in multiple shifts. The education infrastructure was already insufficient before the earthquake. An estimated 3,700 schools need rehabilitation/repairs. Additionally, there are 1,054 schools damaged in northwest Syria. There is a decrease in the availability of qualified teachers and High transport costs coupled with irregularity of teacher payment in some areas effectively inhibit teachers from accessing schools.
Sadad HRP project will respond to the previous needs through:
Provide quality inclusive formal education services to 57805 students(27749B & 30056G )including 560 CWDs in 114 schools using SIG curriculum during the second semester year of 2023-2024, and the first semester academiy year 2024-2025 including contracting with 1829 (formal teachers & educational staff )and pay their incentives, provide running costs to114 schools(cleaning material, stationery for school personnel and drinkable water), transportation cost to 150 CWDs, procure and distribute stationery kits (school bags & stationery)to 57,805 children in 114 schools, print and distribute of textbooks to 57805 children, train the teachers and the children on safety and evacuation, provide professional development to the teachers and other supporting staff in 114 schools on(Integration of children with disabilities in the education process and TICC), procure and distribute 114 sets of teaching materials to 114 schools, conducting 114 back to learning campaigns in 114 different locations, winterization support(heating fuel, heaters ) to 114 schools during (01-01-2024 to 31-03-2024), and (15-11-2024 to 31-12-2024).
SHO is going to recruit 342 remedial teachers(3 per school in average) to provide one cycle of remedial education for three months in (October to Dec 2024using Adopted Syrian curriculum targeting 17,353(8,389 B,8,964 G) students enrolled in the formal education of low achievers(their final scores in end-year exams of( 2023-2024) academic year is less than 50% in English, Arabic, Math)who didn’t have the chance to attend the schools regularly due to the interruptions and displacement caused due earthquake in 114 schools, in addition to recruit 342 Catch up teachers(3 per school) to provide one cycle of catch-up classes for three months to 850( 350 B&G500) OOSC using the curriculum (B) during summer (June to August 2024), Print and distribute 850 copies of curriculum(B)to the children in catch-up classes.
Provision of child protection essential services targeting 76008 children in formal and non-formal education, these services (PSS using Ideal curriculum, awareness raising sessions,referrals)
