Project Summary

The social centre of Zahret Al Madan (ZAM), partner of the initiative, was created with the specific task of assisting families in which there are children and adolescents with physical and / or cognitive disabilities. The central activity of the association is the creation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for minors, to which have been added those on which the impact of the conflict was particularly dramatic, both from a psychological and physical point of view (e.g. minors with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder - PTSD). The strong reduction for families of the possibility of accessing social and health assistance services, both due to the lack of structures in the suburbs and the lack of economic resources, makes it necessary to strengthen the services offered - free of charge - by the ZAM Centre, through strengthening the technical skills of operators and volunteers and increasing the relationship between ZAM and other realities (local NGOs and specialized treatment centres, such as the Centre for Prosthetics and Rehabilitation of the Ibn Al Nafis Hospital).
The main protection programme goals of Armadilla are: a) implementation of therapeutic rehabilitation pathways for children with disabilities, and favouring their social inclusion through a wide range of activities; b) enhance the social and economic conditions of women, especially through delivering psychosocial services (including support for women and girls victims of violence), and vocational training for the economic empowerment of lonely women; c) promote awareness campaigns among the communities of Damascus and Rural Damascus for the social inclusion of children with disabilities; d) raising awareness about women and girls victims of violence; e) raising awareness about explosive ordnance risk.
Furthermore, the lack of resources of the families of coming from Hajar Al Aswad, Yarmouk and Midan (host community) does not allow them in access quality services for their relatives. The reinforcement of the technical skills of volunteers and workers of the ZAM association, as well as of the other associations involved in the activities, is the first answer to the increasing needs of the population of Damascus and Rural Damascus. The project foresees the strengthening of capacity of ZAM volunteers and workers in the field of assistance to minors with disabilities, as well as the training of other 4 associations of operating in Damascus and Rural Damascus in the field of assistance and care of children and youngsters with disability.
The project is integrated with activities designed under the Health and Food Security sectors’ umbrella.

4 - Needs

The following needs analysis is based on the permanent data collection and discussion with local authorities (MoSA and MoH), local associations and beneficiaries focus groups targeted by the intervention programme 2021. In the area of intervention, civilians are suffering the effects of over ten years of conflict: mainly disintegration of community structures, hindered social safety nets, high levels of trauma and psychological distress. Beneficiaries of the proposed intervention are extremely vulnerable. The initial conditions of poverty and scarce access to basic services have been further aggravated by the conflict, especially as a consequence of displacement from their home (more than 90% of targeted beneficiaries come from Hajar Al Aswad, Yarmouk, Sbeneh, Set Zeinab, Babila…). 91% the targeted families see the presence of one or more minors with disability, and their lack of resources aggravate the physical and cognitive status of the children, as well as the emotional and psychological burden of the families. It should be noted that as per the survey conducted by Armadilla and ZAM, 74% of the families interested by this proposal are led by breadwinner women. According to the analysis realized by the I-NGO in collaboration with the local partner and the Ministry of Social Affairs, there is a continuous need to increase the capacity of the workers, as well as the number of volunteers and workers in order to match the increased quantity of beneficiaries of social services in the area. Furthermore, the consequences of the COVID-19 mitigation measures have exacerbated many already difficult situations, claiming an urgent response in terms of psycho-social and economic support for women.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented in 2 locations: 1) Midan, at the Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) Centre. Since more than 10 years, the Centre work in the ﬁeld of assistance to minors with disability, in strict collaboration with Armadilla and the CPR of the Hospital Ibn-Al Naﬁs of Damascus (partner of Armadilla under the health sector). Project activities will be implemented by Armadilla and ZAM staff. ZAM staff will be coordinated by specialized health workers (physical therapists, paediatricians …). Recreational days involving both minors with disabilities and peers without disabilities will be coordinated by ZAM and other NGOs involved in the project. 2) Ghouta (Rural Damascus), in which ZAM staff on bi- weekly basis will deliver assistance to children with disabilities and, at the same time, training local associations volunteers and relatives of children with disabilities. List of activities to be implemented: Therapeutic pathways for children with disabilities; PSA addressing families of minors with disabilities and women; Training for volunteers and workers of ZAM and other local associations located in Ghouta, as per the learning by doing approach. Recreational days for children with disabilities and children (peers activities). Supply of equipment (assistive aids and educational toys for children with disabilities) to the associations. Awareness community sessions on inclusion for children with disabilities; awareness campaigns for women victims of violence (or at risk of); vocational training for women (and distribution of tools for the start-up of professional activities); awareness campaigns about explosive ordnance risk at community level and at the CPR of Ibn Al Nafis Complex.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Access to the Area of the activities: The access to the area of activities, both in Damascus and Rural Damascus, does not seem problematic nowadays. However, the Organisation will be in continuous communication with the relevant security actors in order to organize activities and movements only after consultation with them. Monitoring activities will be realized previously informing the Ministry of Social Affairs (MoSA), who has speciﬁcally approved the activities of the present project. Lack of permission to enter in Syria for expatriate staff: while there are no problems at all in obtaining visa for professionals experts, there is the possibility that some Armadilla’s management staff may be face diﬃculties in obtaining the visas to come in the Country (e.g. for internal monitoring work). Visas issue will be tackled by advocating with the MoSA and MoH in supporting the Organisation. This method worked just ﬁne in recent years. However, training sessions may be delivered by remote methodology, and activities on the ﬁeld will be guaranteed by the Local partner and local staff of the Organisation. Availability of goods (assistive aids and toys for children disabilities): Goods will be purchased on the local market, looking for the best vendor in terms of economy and capacity of guarantee the provision of the service. A back-up list of potential suppliers to contact will be drafted, in order to avoid any delay. In case there will be the need to purchase items abroad, the MoSA will facilitate the import of goods.
