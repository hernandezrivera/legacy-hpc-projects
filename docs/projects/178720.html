Project Summary

The project will secure the food need of part of newly displaced HHs in north of Syria who fled from the last escalation through providing RTE rations for 2 rounds as a lifesaving rapid response, in addition to provide food baskets for newly displaced HHs and most vulnerable HHs of the host communities for 8 months throughout two modalities in-Kind and vouchers, aids modality will be determined after a communities market assessment to check market access and availability of essential food items, QRCS also will support
bakeries managed by local councils with 960 MT of flour to produce bread to around 5,500 HHs with subsidized price for 8 months in daily basis, targeted beneficiaries by bread will not be the same targeted beneficiaries of GBF.
45,500 of the most vulnerable crisis affected people will benefit from the project including women, men, girls and boys in all age groups and disabled people in the targeted communities.
Beneficiaries will be involved in project designing and implementation through a close coordination with local council and community representatives, also beneficiaries will be engaged in evaluation through satisfaction survey and complaint mechanism.

4 - Needs

Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, includinghomes, schools, health facilities, water supply and irrigation systems. Today, 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance - a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020 - with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline.IDP’s income and livelihoods situation, food insecurity status, settlement and shelter conditions, and access to basic services are inter-related. Similar to overall trends, two-thirds of IDP households report being unable to meet the basic needs of all household members, and the majority of IDPs prioritize access to food and livelihood support. While conflict and insecurity remain the most important reason for displacement, the deteriorating economic situation and lack of access to services have become the second most important reason for people to leave and seek improved conditions elsewhere.193 Of all population groups, IDPs have the largest relative income deficit, with income insufficiency reported in all governoratesThe situationin Syria has drastically deteriorated, with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68.6 per cent of the total population.Based on a countrywide assessment of 36,484 households and technical reviews and consultations, at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. HNO, 2021 advanced copy

5 - Implementation of activities

The project intervention will be throughout: - Providing in-kind RTE rations for 4,000 newly displaced HHs for two rounds, - Providing in-kind food baskets for 2,000 internal displaced HHs and host communities for 8 months. in kind RTE rations and food baskets will be prepared as Food Assistance recommendation in Humanitarian Response Plan 2020- Providing food assistance via cash/voucher modality to 2,000 internal displaced HHs and host communities who lives in communities with full access to active markets,each HHs will receive 60 USD/month for 8 months, transportation reimbursement is included. - Providing 960 MT of flour (about 5 MT per day for 6 months) to bakeries managed by local councils to produce 5,500 bread bundles with subsidized price in daily basis, the produced bread will be distributed to ID and host communities HHs in communities with high severity.Project implementation cycle will start from coordination with the communities representatives and local councils, performing market assessments toevaluate market access, food availability, diversity, and price. Beneficiaries selection will be built according to their vulnerability and need prioritizing displaced families affected by last escalation with the focus on female/children headed HHs and disabled persons, the project has been designed to mitigate the conflict consequences on adolescents as well by included them in the targeted beneficiaries.Trained M&E team will be assigned/hired locally on this project who will be charged with collecting information regarding beneficiaries and data analysis and will also closely monitor and verify the services and renovations rendered to beneficiaries to ensure correctness, timeliness and efficiency.
