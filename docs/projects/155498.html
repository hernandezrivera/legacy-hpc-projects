Needs

The current emergency faced by Cameroon has disrupted the ability of the state to ensure the adequate provision of basic social services, including that of education in crisis affected regions. Influx of Central African refugees has increased pressure on host communities in the East and Adamawa regions, thus exacerbating pre existing structural issues within national education system. Furthermore, geopolitical instability in Central African Republic are maintain all refugees in Cameroon. This project aims to enhance the quality of response and build resilience of system by continuing providing access to quality education to all school-aged children from Central African refugee populations and host communities. For the out-of-school children from 10 to 14 years old, accelerated and adapted curricula will be organized for their insertion/reinsertion in school. All refugee and host community children will benefit from a second learning material distribution. The project need to support Cameroon government to increase the host school capacity and receive more children, mobilize communities for enrolment and retention of all children in school with focus on girls, strengthening pedagogical supervision and improve teaching in classroom. The advocacy to the government will focus on the provision of more teachers in the schools receiving refugees. This project will be the occasion to reinforce the promotion of preschool in host communities.The participation of host communities will be encourage to ensure enrollment and retention of children in school, also to improve the school management
