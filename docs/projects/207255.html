PrEduCaDo - Access to quality and inclusive education in a protective and climate-ready environment for children affected by conflict and climate change in Cabo Delgado

31/12/2024

Project Summary


The PreEduCaDo project aims to guarantee education that promotes the protection and prevention of risks to the well-being of children and young people in the districts of Nangade, Mueda, Chiure, Pemba in Cabo Delgado province.
The project will assure access to quality and inclusive education at school (school as center of opportunities and inclusion), by first mobilizing children and parents on the importance of education, as a human right, but also as a means of facilitating peace and ensuring improvements in the social and socio-economic structures of society. Children who have been out of school for long periods will need remedial classes and the project will provide catch-up accelerated learning for internally displaced and host community children who have experienced disrupted education. Older children will receive life skills training and vocational education. By project end, around 5,640 IDP and host community children will be educated to age-appropriate levels to engage fully in educational opportunities offered to them.
Schools are a tool of protection (ODI). They can provide children with physical, psychosocial, and cognitive protection. Teachers and schools play a key role in implementing education efforts and gathering and giving out protection-related information. Therefore, the project will offer teacher training in child protection, child rights and training in positive pedagogy – an approach based on positive and humanistic psychology which aims to promote peace and wellbeing. Schools will be linked to wider community-based support, through the creation of mental health and psychosocial support services and establishment of a child protection network/referral links (school-community-district services). This will include training on child protection and child rights for community agents and for agents of district protection services. Structures will be established in the community, involving the selection of profiles for the constitution of Community Child Protection Committees (CCPC); and the preparation of statutes and action plan for CCPC. The committees will support one another through the establishment and strengthening of a CCPC network. By project end, children and young people will be supported with child protection and psychosocial support services at school, in their community, and up to district level.
Many children and parents fear schools because they see them as places of danger. They also see their journey to school – transversing through sometimes hostile terrain unsupervised and vulnerable – as too high a risk to bear. Therefore, efforts must be made to make school and the wider community safe, and to change perceptions of them. School and the wider community At school, Disaster and Risk Management Committees will be formed. Participatory risk analyses will be done and schools and institutional authorities, involving students, will ensure schools have risk management plans in place and actively promote school safety. In addition, multidimensional risks will be researched and addressed. Socio-community animators and protection committee agents will monitor children at risk and a report will be produced “Children’s Situation and Responses Given”. Given the fact that many children are internally displaced and may be living within families suffering significant stress from conflict and/or climate change’s impacts on their livelihoods, or they may be boarding or fostered by extended family, their home safety also needs to be considered.
The project will be implemented in 6 communities in Chiure, 3 communities in Pemba, Nangade and Mueda.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will be implemented in 6 communities in Chiure, 3 communities in Pemba, 3 in Nangade and 3 in Mueda of Cabo Delgado province.In all geographic areas we will have the following activities:Assuring quality and inclusive education at school (school as center of opportunities and inclusion)• Remedial Classes and play-based activities• Catch-up and Accelerated Learning• Training in life skills and vocational orientation• Campaign to raise awareness on the right to educationStrengthening child-protection at school (school as center for child protection)• Teacher Training in Child Protection and Child Rights• Teacher Training in Positive Pedagogy• Creation of Mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS) services and establishment of a child protection network/referral links (school-community-district services)Making the school environment safer (school as a safe space)• Creation and Training of School Disaster and Risk Management Committees• Definition of the school risk profile based on risk analysis• Implementation of mitigation measures Revitalization and capacity-building of District and Community Child Protection Committees• Training on Child Protection and Child Rights for community agents and for agents of district protection services• Selection of Profiles for the constitution of Community Child Protection Committees (CCPC)• Preparation of statutes and action plan for CCPC• Establishment and strengthening of a CCPC networkMultidimensional Assessment and Monitoring of children at risk• Preparation and testing of a specific multi-dimensional tool to assess and monitor the children at risk• Training of socio-community animators and protection committee agents in applying and filling out the tool• Data collection and analysis• Participatory response to children's risk situations• Preparation of the Report “Children’s Situation and Responses Given”Strengthening the child protection role of families through research and capacity-building• Study of parental practices in target communities• Parental Education Training for families and for socio-community animators in target communities• "Child Fosterage" case study in the target communities• Awareness-raising on child protection through media (Broadcast of the radio spot "Cenas de Familias") and community sensitization campaigns .Strengthening the climate change resilience capacity of schools, youth and civil society in conflict-affected areas and at national level• Workshops at national level to exchange experience on environmental education involving MINEDH (Ministry of Education and Humanitarian Development), CSOs, students’ representatives to include the environmental education in school curriculum.• Trainings for youth (i.e. volunteers, young environment ambassadors) and CBOs in Cabo Delgado on techniques for campaigning and community sensitization on the impact of climate change and natural disasters in conflict affected areas• Radio and video campaign at national and provincial level to raise awareness on impact of climate change and natural disasters.• Provision of kits for the implementation of environmental protection and sensitization actions awarded in the form of prizes to youth groups, young activists, CSO/CBOs, schools and other environment protection change-makers in the target areas.• Organization of a National Forum for the Young Environment Ambassadors and exchange of experience with the Fridays for Future movement at regional and global level.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project is aligned with international guidelines for the education, protection and DRR sector, but also with national government entities and civil society authors. It also maintains a strong and close relationship with the target communities, involving them in the project design process but also in its implementation. There is also great coordination with working groups and sectoral clusters for a more articulated and well-founded response.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Regarding the empowerment of women, the project focuses on female children's access to education, with teacher training in gender equality and the development of life skills among adolescents, especially teenage girls. The project also aims to work with families, re-educating sexual roles, which are often stereotypically gender roles.The project does not have a specific answer for elderly people, but it does provide values of citizenship and respect for human rights.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will identify children with disabilities in the target communities, providing their right to education and training teachers in inclusive education. Likewise, training in positive and participatory pedagogy will help create a safer and more inclusive environment.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project provides for environmental training and sustainable construction, looking at respect for the environment.
