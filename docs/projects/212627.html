Project Summary

The HNO 2024 mentions that assessments and analyses carried out across the country continue to show a general deterioration in access to livelihoods, households unable to meet needs, Lack of jobs, services, and assistance driving needs, and amid depreciation,
inflation, and insufficient wages, households are facing significant income-expenditure
gaps In NWS, monthly household incomes reach approximately with 85 USD, and expenditures at 183 USD per month, a 53.5% shortfall, based on MSNA data.
The proposal aims to enhance livelihood access and promote social cohesion by working with and for communities, strengthen local governance, and empower women and youth for a more sustainable livelihood in Idleb and Aleppo Governorate as locations that have
a high need to support economic and livelihood opportunities. The project will be implemented according to several interventions as follows:
1- establishing and building the capacity of the Community Representative Committee in
each location to represent the local community in all activities.
2- rehabilitation of the infrastructure of 70 classrooms in four schools
3- rehabilitation of the infrastructure of 4 markets.
4- rehabilitation of the infrastructure of 4 roads with 4 KM.
5- rehabilitation of one irrigation water station and the associated canals.
6-All rehabilitation work will include preparing and updating technical studies and
contracting with contractors in conjunction with the announcement of registration within
the cash-for-work activity to engage 100 workers who are selected to carry out
rehabilitation work for a period of two months / 22 days per month / 5-8 hours per day
depending on the activity for a daily wage of $6 for each day
7- Provide vocational training through the Apprenticeship (on Job Training) Methodology
for 730 beneficiaries and deliver business management and soft skills training for them
then TAS will link trainees with local markets
8- provide scale-up grants for 200 existing business owners with 1200 $ for each to expand their production and services after 5 days of business development training to be more sustainable and capable
9-Providing small grants to 200 beneficiaries to support them in starting or restart their small projects, entering the labor market, and achieving self-sufficiency after training them on 2- day proposal training to help them develop their business and procurement plan to be
submitted to the evaluation committee in addition to 5- days of Business Development training
Launch of 10 initiatives promoting social cohesion in the targeted communities.
