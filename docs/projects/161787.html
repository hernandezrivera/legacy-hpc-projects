Project Summary

The crisis in north-east Nigeria has had a major impact on the health care system, about 5.3 million people are in need of life-saving and essential health care services. Two thirds of health facilities in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states have been damaged, creating a critical health care gap. This Project is targeted at bridging critical gaps in the life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian health services, disease surveillance, prevention and response, with an emphasis on providing support to enhance health protection and access to health care services for 6900 affected persons especially women and children Maiduguri, Monguno and Mafa LGAs.
GOALPrime Organization aims to provide support for out-patient consultations to access medical services for vulnerable women, men, Boys and girls as well as people with special needs in the health facilities of engaging locations throughout the period of the project for 2000 affected persons in the IDP camps and host communities in Maiduguri, Monguno and Mafa LGAs using the outreach team (GPON mobile medical health team). Also, support vulnerable 2000 women, men, boys and girls referred to access medications with medical bills and transportation to hospital. GOALPrime organization will conduct capacity building for community health extension workers (CHEWs) and Community Volunteers (CVs) on proper referral mechanism for secondary health care access and feedbacks from the referral points, in other for them to be able to effect these referral activities from their fields, desks and points of interventions.
GOALPrime Organization will through this project reach out to 3000 health-seeking Women and men boys and girls living in IDP camps in Maiduguri, Monguno and Mafa LGAs, through mobile medical health teams with medical supports that includes first aid treatments, referral of severe health cases as well as identification of survivors of rape and other GBV cases and refer to specialized medical services. The mobile health team and CHEWs will also be used to carry out communicable disease surveillance in the IDP camps of Maiduguri Monguno and MafaLGAs.
GOALPrime will through the mobile health team distribute the Kit 3 (Post-Rape Treatment) to Health care facilities in the areas of engagement, ensuring that rape survivors can be reached with treatment within 48 hours. Collaborate with the SMoH to conduct a 3-day training for 50 CHEWs on clinical management of rape (CMR) and the use of the post-rape kits. As holistic care for GBV survivors remains largely non-existent.
Through this project GOALPrime will support the SMoH for its national immunization and vaccination campaigns for Vaccine-Preventable Diseases (VPD) as well as supplementary and/or reactive vaccination activities (including measles, cholera, meningitis and hepatitis) targeting 1900 women, men, boys and girls living in IDP camps and high-risk epidemic prone areas of Maiduguri, Monguno and Mafa LGAs of Borno state.

1 - Needs

In a multi-sectoral and multi-agency joint assessment conducted by the humanitarian community and the government regarding the health status in the north-east, it was estimated that in north-east Nigeria, about 5.3 million people need life-saving and essential health care services. The crisis has had a major impact on the health care system; there are 375 (50%) non- functioning (of total 755 assessed health facilities), 292 (39%) fully damaged, 205 (27%) partially damaged, 253 (34%) not damaged. Two thirds of health facilities in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states have been damaged while those that are functioning are short of staff, lacks safe water, basic drugs and equipment. In inaccessible and newly accessible areas, communities face serious challenges in accessing health services due to various reasons, including lack of medical staff, unavailability of drugs and supplies, security barriers, transportation issues and damaged/destroyed health facilities, lack of money to access medications. Also the north-east remains highly endemic to diseases due to seasonal patterns, lack of access to potable water and sanitation infrastructure, and vulnerabilities as a result of continuous displacement and low access to essential health care. New influxes of IDPs, refugees and returnees into overcrowded and under-serviced camps and settlements also exacerbate the risk of disease outbreaks. All these and more in the health sector keeps creating critical gaps in the functional primary and secondary health services. Thus, there is an urgent need to ensure Life-saving and Life-sustaining Health Support as well as the Strengthening of the several Linkages in the Health Care Services to affected IDPs, returnees and Host Communities in Maiduguri, Mungono and Mafa LGAs of Borno state.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime shall establish functioning complaints and feedback mechanisms to receive feedback from the community members and the beneficiaries on the impact of this project on the beneficiaries, and the community. GPON shall set up free toil line for the beneficiaries to easily reach out to GOALPrime Organization Nigeria to complain or give feedback. GOALPrime shall also hold bi-weekly focus group discussions with target women, men, boys and girls, other community stakeholders to receive feedback on the project impact. GOALPrime shall provide feedback on complaints from the beneficiaries, and other relevant community stakeholders on a weekly basis. Effective and timely actions shall be taken to address concerns received through our established feedback mechanism. The identity of complainants shall remain confidential unless otherwise requested by the complainant or feedback provider.
