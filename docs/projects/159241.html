Project Summary

The upcoming rain season, (April to June 2020) rains are projected perform below average. The populations' groups classified as Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse require urgent humanitarian interventions, including and the most affect being the people in the rural areas, the hard to reach areas with water access crisis. Unless there is timely humanitarian intervention, there is risk of worst situation than already is. Timely upport is also required for people categorized as Stressed (IPC Phase 2 or higher) to prevent relapse of the 2017 drought. This proposed intervention by SRDA aims at improving access to sustained WASH services to prevent incidences of AWD through provision of safe water and sanitation facilities coupled with intensive hygiene promotion program. The project shall also target public institutions (schools and health facilities) through, multi -sectoral approach in coordination with other projects in Health, WASH, Food Security and protection. Sustainable WASH intervention will be accompanied by emergency lifesaving water supply through vouchers to vulnerable drought affected pastoralists and displaced populations, including; construction of gender sensitive ;latrines to schools and health/nutrition facilities and distribution of hygiene kits. The proposed project activities will include construction of strategic priority deep boreholes, elevated storage tanks and distribution systems, formation WASH management committees and training them on AO&M of the water supply infrastructures. The project will provide O&M toolkits to ensure sustainability for each committee. Project will make follow up for effective operations and where possible sensitize the WASH committees on where they can access spare parts for maintenance of the WASH infrastructure. Construction of gender sensitive sanitation facilities will target schools and health/nutrition facilities users especially women will be encouraged to form groups of 4-5HH per latrine and trained on basic maintenance of latrines to ensure they are clean always. Hygiene awareness promotion and distribution of hygiene kits will cover all the target population.
Implementation process of the project activities shall be participatory to ensure beneficiaries own the process and the outcomes. Selection of beneficiaries of emergency water supply will target the most vulnerable HH who will be identified using participatory process & criteria agreed with communities ensuring equal opportunities for men, women and minorities. Large forum sensitization meetings will be held and project introduced to the village members widely. Clear information will be provided on process & criteria of selection of beneficiaries, beneficiary entitlements and means for submitting complaints and feedback. The criteria will include Households having children under 5 years attending outpatient therapeutic feeding or malnourished, HH headed by orphaned children, Female headed HHs, HHs with members who have physical disabilities, HHs headed by elderly person, vulnerable minority groups, pastoralists having minimum number of animals to support their households basic needs. Project beneficiaries (men, women and where possible children) will participate in selecting sites for construction of WASH facilities with technical advises from the project staff. This will ensure all the diverse need of different beneficiaries are met to increase sense of ownership. Beneficiary capacity building will be mainstreamed in the implementation process to equip them with skills and knowledge to fully participate in the implementation processes including M&E
