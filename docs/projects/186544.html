Project Summary

This project aimed to provide improved access to safe, inclusive basic education and dignified learning environment targeting 7360 school-aged conflict-affected Adolescents and Children (age 3-17, 4000 Girls and 3000 Boys, 360 Women and Men IDPs, Returnees and Host community) in Bama, Monguno, Mobar, Dikwa and Kala/ Balge of Borno State.
To address the key gaps and critical emerging education needs, this intervention will Coordinate with community Base stakeholders, partners, and families of children missing out of school in the target communities for Proper identification and profiling, children identified as being out of school will be referred for education support, providing a more holistic response. Furthermore, the CP and Education capacity of community structures ( SBMC, PTA, CBCPCs, ) will be developed, ensuring that schools and communities are more protective environments in the long term. Leveraging on existing structures and materials also ensures a value-for-money approach. 1200 (700 Girls and 500 Boys) will benefit from supplies and distribution of teaching aids and learning materials including ECD and vocational training Kits, this project will invest in capacity building and recruitment of volunteer teachers, 200 teachers (120 Female and 80 Male) will receive training on an improved teaching and learning approach to meet the education needs of children, to ensure they receive quality and conflict sensitive education services to enhanced their wellbeing and learning resilience, existing TLS/class rooms will be renovated or constructed where critical and equipped including provision of appropriate WASH facilities and menstrual hygiene kits for girls to ensure learning environments are child-friendly, gender-responsive, equitable, disability-friendly and inclusive.

1 - Needs

A report by UN OCHA indicates that the humanitarian crisis in the BAY states remains one of the most severe in the world today. The crisis in Northeast Nigeria exacerbated by COVID-19 has further worsened an already fragile education system, resulting in increasing the number of out-of-school children especially girls, A further area of concern is the lack of education opportunities and school adaptation for children with disabilities.The JENA 2021 findings indicated that poor infrastructure such as classrooms, school furniture and WASH facilities was recorded as a critical need and gap. 33% of schools in Borno reported that they were not able to enroll some children into the 2020-2021 calendar year whilst the most common barrier to Children attending school or enrollment as stated by the report is the inability to afford education materials, pay school fees with 46% and 47 respectively for Boys and girls in Borno. The Prevalence is higher in Yobe with 54%, whilst Adamawa 45% and Borno 44%. Schools across northeast Nigeria are not typically adapted for children with disabilities, with just 19% reporting having additional provisions,despite a majority (65%) hosting at least one child with special needs.

2 - Activities

■ Advocacy visit to line ministries, local Education Authority, community, and religious leaders, and other relevant stakeholders ■ Conduct Back to learning enrolment campaign targeting 10000 Girls and Boys and caregivers with key education messaging through Community engagement, sensitization, awareness, and dialogue meetings with families of and girls not attending school on the importance of Education with focus on Girls ■ undertake Identification and enrolment of children missing out of school into formal and Non-formal programs In the communities. YIPDI will engage with community leaders to ensure enrolment and retention of learners ■ Rehabilitation of 3 in each location disability-inclusive Temporary Learning Space (TLS) outfitted with necessary furniture. YIPDI will collaborate with local communities in planning, designing, rehabilitation, and maintenance of TLS for sustainability ■ designs WASH training curricula and materials for schools and teachers ■ Establish teachers, adolescents and children School Based Hygiene Promotion clubs ■ undertake Profiling and identification of teachers and adolescents as hygiene promotion leaders ■ delivers School Based Hygiene promotion training to Teachers, volunteers and adolescents club leaders ■ develop School Based Hygiene promotion campaign materials and mechanisms ■ Delivery of School Based Hygiene Promotion events
