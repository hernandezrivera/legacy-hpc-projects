Project Summary

The project aim to provide food assistance and livelihoods support to internally displace persons (IDPs) and returnees in Jonglei State. The project will focus on two areas: emergency food distribution and livelihoods support. The food distribution component will ensure that IDPs and returnees have access to sufficient and nutritious food. The livelihoods support component will provide trainings.
