Project Summary

INSO in Cameroon through this project will continue to disseminate and aggregate detailed analysis of the contextual developments in its services to the partners.
INSO Cameroon will provide the following services:
INSO Alerts - Prior to, or on the occurrence, of an incident carrying an imminent threat to NGOs and requiring an immediate operational response (such as movement restriction) INSO will provide an immediate Alert via the quickest technical medium available (e-mail, SMS text, voice call). The Alert will include the time, place and nature of the incident and a recommended response plan.
INSO Reports - Upon the anticipation or occurrence of a significant incident - including all incidents which directly involve the humanitarian community- but which does not require an immediate operational response from NGOs, INSO will provide a written Report of the incident via email noting the time, place and nature of the event along with a succinct analysis that includes an assessment of the probable impact and - where necessary - an advisory notice.
Bi-Weekly/Monthly Reports - A written bi-weekly/monthly analytical report with individual analysis plus key data and trends for each province as well as a macro level summary for the period. The report is intended to complement the statistical data with observations on security, political, demographic, climactic and cross border issues etc for that period. The report is provided in electronic format only and is distributed regionally and nationally. The report is currently in a biweekly format but will move to a monthly format in 2024.
Quarterly Reports - A quarterly report, providing the highest level of analysis and statistics. The report has a focus on longer term trends and incorporates developments at the national, regional and international levels with summarized data on the humanitarian sector and the various conflict trends. The report is intended to orient NGO in the 'strategic' context, within which all local events are occurring, and so to try and look ahead at the implications of changes in the structure of the conflicts, politics and inter-regional dynamics.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The security context in Cameroon during this period remains dominated by the two crises in the Far North and in the North-West and South-West regions. In the Far North, it is the JAS and ISWAP groups that continue their acts of predation and internal clashes over control of territory in the Lake Chad Basin. Parallel to this crisis, tensions between herders and farmers for control of resources on the LCB shores are exacerbated by the scarcity of resources and the impact of climate change.In the North-West and South-West regions, the conflict remained intense with a 94% increase in security incidents for the first three quarters of 2023 compared to the same period in 2022. In both regions, OAG activities increased by 83%. These groups maintain their predatory activities, mainly targeting civilians to extort money from them through kidnapping, taxation, etc.The conflict in both parts of the country has had a significant impact on civilians, including NGOs and their staff. At the end of November 2023, INSO recorded 4,039 security incidents compared to 2,500 for the same period in 2022, an increase of 61.56%. Regarding IED utilization, INSO noted an increase of 69%. 51 NGO incidents were reported, compared to 66 in the same period of 2022. While this figure is down 23% from last year, it remains a concern for humanitarian actors who continue to face access limitations and administrative burdens. These incidents highlight the challenges faced by NGOs operating in the region and the need to strengthen security measures to protect their staff and operations.It is in this context that INSO Cameroon wishes to continue to provide credible, reliable, and high-quality security information, including analysis and advisory services to registered humanitarian partners. This will be done in a timely and appropriate manner, which will help them plan, operate and deliver aid more safely to affected communities in INSO-covered areas in Cameroon.In addition, INSO Cameroon plans to organize a series of thematic training sessions focused on capacity building for international and national NGO staff.
