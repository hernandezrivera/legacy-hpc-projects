Project Summary

In the line with the strategically objectives of the early recovery and livelihood (ERL) sector this proposal has been designed to support livelihoods recovery; rehabilitation of critical infrastructure and services; and promoting social cohesion and community security. In addition, in particularly, targeting the most vulnerable groups such as youth, and PLWDs as well as IDPs and host communities including supporting some of FHHs of productive families to establish small workshops convenient with them such as food industry, pastry industry, sewing. The main objectives of this proposal has been to minimize the impact of disrupted livelihoods, to support and secure the income generation and self-reliance and source of livelihood for beneficiaries. As well as, the intervention will contribute to achieve a durable solution for displaced families through encouraging them to return back to their origin areas of intervention, moreover, will contribute to achieve the area's recovery through the services provided by the workshops.
This proposal aims to support the rehabilitation activities and provision of the needed equipment and tools, which will contribute to the resumption of functioning of a number of small, medium, and big workshops in Deir Ez Zor province in the cities and rural countryside that exposed to great damages during the crisis and or vandalized or robbed, where, as of the latest assessment, there has been only 500 production workshops currently operating in Deir Ez-Zor province out of 15,000 pre-crisis workshops.
The proposal takes into account that the diversity of the targeted beneficiaries and the proportions of men, women, people with disabilities and young people in order to bring them back to the labor market, where 50% of the beneficiaries will be young people, who have suffered during the crisis and face problems to find sustainable job opportunities to secure their livelihoods.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be carried out under close supervision of an ad hoc committee that will be composed and will include representatives of: UNDP, artisans' Union, local community, beneficiaries and the BICA. The assigned committee exercises its functions and competences in accordance with the established rules of procedure, project workplan, and in particular adhering to the stages of the implementation of the project including advertising process, selection of the beneficiaries, follow up the project implementation steps, as well legal issues raised during the span of the project and suggest the durable solutions for the potential problems. The main activities of the project have been: 1. Identifying the beneficiaries as of selection criteria plan. 2. Carry out the assessment to identify the needed works and tools for each workshops.3. Conduct of awareness and work management training for the selected beneficiaries.4. Carry out open tender process for provision of the project tools and equipment as well needed services.5. Distribution of the tools and production equipment to beneficiaries.6. M & E7. Reporting.

Mitigation: BICA will intensify efforts to ensure that all beneficiaries have got the required training activities.Risk 3: some beneficiaries may trying to break the standards. Likelihood: low. Impact: medium. Mitigation: The project criteria will be well explained in the documents, the staff will be trained to explain the roles, standards to the beneficiaries random samples will be explained to the beneficiaries who will sing the relevant papers in order to ensure they will adhere to the applied roles and standards prior to launching the distribution activities of the tools.Risk 4: Some beneficiaries do not comply to the conditions of grants. Likelihood: low. Impact: medium. Mitigation: The status of applicants will be studied by the career guidance team and if the non-compliance is the result of reasons beyond the will of the beneficiaries, BICA will help in finding solutions to the difficulties they face and in the event of the beneficiary's inability, the entire assistance items will be delivered to an other applicant.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

BICA will only pay for items upon completion of delivery and once quality has been checked and guaranteed. Additionally BICA has a registered Syrian lawyer to help mitigate and deal with any issues present with contractors and suppliers, and has clear contracts, pertaining to the requirements of the works.Ethical risk 2: Related to any kind of abuse (Sexual, Power, etc. Likelihood: low.
