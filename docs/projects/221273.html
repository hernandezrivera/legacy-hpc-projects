Project Summary

This project seeks to respond to the WASH, Shelter, Food distribution and SRH needs of women and girls experiencing deprivation to various responses due to the inaccessibility of their communities, entrepreneurial economic development needs by reinforcing their capacities, build resilience and economic upscaling including food distribution in Momo , Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions facilitating the number of Households assisted with shelter that meet environmental criteria through Provision of rental assistance either free rent, cash-for-rent, or subsidy, agro-transformation and development of value chains to increase targeted community centered support interventions fostering an enabling environment conducive for protection of women and girls, and their participation in the economy and goes further to increase their technical capacities to enable aptitude to economic access to be able to regain normalcy and engage in various income generating activities to high value purchasing markets and high income value markets increasing income enabling a conducive economic power to improve shelter, SRH, WASH, and living conditions and a significant contribution to local development. This 12 months intervention will ensure that the target beneficiaries, woman and girls have access to adequate shelter through Provision of rental assistance (either free rent, cash-for-rent, or subsidy), WASH, SRH, mentorship, partnerships and access to funding that will enable them to contribute meaningfully to their self development and participate in local development, increase income, improve living conditions through various community based strategic Shlter, WASH, Livelihood and SRH interventions, protect themselves, Build resilience and transition to autonomy and be economically empowered benefitting the community at large by enabling them to contribute meaningfully, support, participate and promote local development activities and initiatives.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Women and Girls who are experiencing deprivation from various response due to the humanitarian context in the North West and South West regions of Cameroon especially in hard to reach communities continue to shoulder disproportionate opportunities to their economic potentials, sexual and reproductive health rights, nutrition, Livelihood, shelter and education seriously limiting their thrive to equality, social status, Access to shelter that meet environmental criteria and contributions to local development. This undermining and response push behind is worsened by the conflict especially in hard to reach communities and has exacerbated to allowing their shelter, WASH, education, Nutrition and livelihood initiatives tampered adversely. This is mainly caused by Limited access to resources including technical resources which impedes their ability to protect themselves and worsened in terms of human capital contribution to local community development, their dignities destroyed, and widens barriers to accessing basic human needs, worsening their efforts to self-promotion in supporting local development initiatives. In response, GLOCIR is proposing a 12 months comprehensive approach addressing systemic barriers, foster an enabling environment conducive for building resilience and economic upscaling including shelter, WASH, food distribution, nutrition and case management by reinforcing the technical capacities of target beneficiaries to access to essential knowledge, networks, and platforms they need to launch and scale successful personal development initiative, build social cohesion and economic empowerment for self reliance in these target areas driving women led local prosperity through various economic sectors in their respective communities and Households assisted with shelter that meet environmental criteria and provision of rental assistance (either free rent, cash-for-rent, or subsidy). The intervention will further restructure local women led Cooperatives and CIGs into promoting local women led collective protection response to various cluster channels including referrals, PSEA and reporting mechanisms, the development of economic value chains strengthening women and girls income generation to regain economic power and dignity and to access to high value purchasing markets.
