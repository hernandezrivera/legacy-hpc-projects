Project Summary

In line with the Government’s effort to improve emergency preparedness and response, WFP keeps a contingency food stock to respond rapidly in case of hurricane or other shocks. Most recently, the stock was essential to the swift and rapid response to initial needs during the hurricane Matthew emergency. The stocks are stored on WFP premises and, after a shock occurs, are distributed by its cooperating partners in close collaboration with the Government and in particular with Civil Protection Agency (DPC) agents in the affected communes.
WFP will seek to purchase its emergency food assistance locally or nationally in order to support agricultural production and promote sustainable food systems. Moreover, WFP will provide specialized nutritious products to prevent acute malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women and girls and children aged 6–59 months, as well as people living with HIV/AIDS.
During the second phase of the response, WFP will transition to unrestricted cash-based transfers when markets are functional and other conditions are met.

Needs

In line with the Government’s effort to improve emergency preparedness and response, WFP keeps a contingency food stock to respond rapidly in case of hurricane or other shocks. Most recently, the stock was essential to the swift and rapid response to initial needs during the hurricane Matthew emergency. The stocks are stored on WFP premises and, after a shock occurs, are distributed by its cooperating partners in close collaboration with the Government and in particular with Civil Protection Agency (DPC) agents in the affected communes. Given Haiti's exposure to natural hazards, WFP will continue to provide direct life-saving assistance to shock-affected populations in the event of a disaster or other emergency, ensuring their food security and nutrition by delivering unconditional food or cash-based assistance during the crisis and its immediate aftermath. This activity is aligned to the Government’s national contingency plan for the hurricane season to ensure rapid response to food needs in case of large disasters.

Activities or outputs

Key activitiesProvide unconditional food assistance (food and cash) to food insecure households affected by shocks: given Haiti's exposure to natural hazards, WFP will continue to provide direct life-saving assistance to shock-affected populations in the event of a disaster or other emergency, managing national contingency stocks for timely and rapid responses. The activity will be put in place in line with the Government priorities and plans during an emergency; cooperating partners will be pre-identified to provide food assistance in the zones hit by the disaster. Integrating a gender approach and based on the results of past vulnerability analysis, WFP will focus on women-headed households as well as households with children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women and girls where blanket assistance is not needed and when resources are a major constraint to implementing blanket assistance. Moreover, WFP will adapt its assistance to the particular needs of women, men, girls and boys, with consideration of for persons with disabilities, ensuring a minimum “do no harm” standard for all delivery mechanisms; in particular, the nutritional needs of PLWGs and children between 6 and 59 months of age will be taken into account and the distribution of specialized nutritious food for the prevention of acute malnutrition might be included in the response, if needed. In addition, WFP will integrate a strong protection component to the programme by systematically involving local communities and authorities. Assistance will aim to improve food consumption and food access. WFP will also aim at linking this assistance with the social safety nets, in particular for targeting after a shock occurs. Expected output:Affected populations receive sufficient food assistance to enable them to meet their basic food needs.
