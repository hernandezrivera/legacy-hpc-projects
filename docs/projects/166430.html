Needs

With limited access to construction materials and shelter support put on hold prior to the monsoon season, it is highly likely that damage caused to the shelters during the monsoon season will increase. This will be exacerbated with potential increase in the number of people that are unable to leave their shelter to collect the materials and repair their shelter because they are in home-care or in medical facilities. The need for emergency shelter response during the monsoon season, including the delivery of shelter materials, will increase. Furthermore, refugees in quarantine facilities and the ones in home-care will need support receiving NFIs. In the host communities, the amount of LPG refills is sometimes limited and linked to the livelihood programs. As the livelihoods are currently severely impacted, those households will not have the capacity to purchase their own refill after the amount of refills programmed for is over. The continuation of provision of the LPG refills to those households will remain a need.

Activities or outputs

In order to ensure families affected by COVID-19 are supported with specific NFIs and are also able to receive materials for shelter repair, this project will provide support to refugee volunteers and porters with additional risk incentives to ensure the delivery of the necessary items. In addition LPG refills will contribute to be provided to host community households. A total of 11,000 households in the host communty will be supplied with LPG through the end of 2020. As a cleaner alternative to firewood, LPG energy creates a healthier, smokeless environment, which is extremely important in light of the COVID-19 pandemic. The distribution of LPG also helps the economy, which has been severely impacted by COVID-19, as approxiately 30 jobs are created for every 10, 000 cylinders provided. Support for full shelter and NFIs delivery and implementation will be done under the regular program and funded through reprogramming of existing shelter projects and NFIs. Training on safety, code of conduct and PSEA will also be undertaken under the regular programme.
