Project Summary

The neighborhood of Borj Hammoud is one of the most affected area by the blast. Through minor rehabilitation, PU-AMI will provide decent and safe housing for 30 families who cannot afford a long term rehabilitation. PU-AMI will follow the Shelter WG SOPs regarding the MR in the context of the blast area. Keeping as a central point, the vulnerability of the beneficiary, the modality of implementation will be adapted case by case. Thus, the MR could be done by the owner, by the tenant, or by a contractor.
Moreover, the MR due to the blast will be the opportunity to tackle other minor adjustement for the persons with disability or elderly with mobility difficulties (e.g Handles near the toilets).
In the same area, PU-AMI will support (thanks to other secured funds) Boulghourjan PHCC with subsidized services and rehabilitation in addition to ECA. The shelter rehabilitation wil be integrated in this approach in order to support, in the most appropriate way the most vulnerable HHs.
