Project Summary

This intervention is targeting 50000 people (25000 children, 15000 young people and 10000 caregivers) The project will promote prevention and reduction of COVID 19 related deaths through prevention of new infections, by ensuring access and adherence to prevention measures.The COVID-19 outbreak and response has been accompanied by an over-abundance of information some of which is not accurate thereby making it difficult to find trustworthy sources. To prevent and slow down transmission FOST will ensure communities are well informed about the COVID-19 virus FOST will work with the national IEC Technical working groups (TWGs) to develop and disseminate correct and consistent information on COVID 19. Information will be shared through virtual platforms such as FOST WhatsApp groups, Bulk Text messaging, FOST website and Facebook platform. Hard Copies of posters, flyers and manuals on COVID 19 will be developed and shared with the communities. Radio and TV talks on COVID 19 will be facilitated
Community volunteers and frontline staff worry about personal safety and how to continue to do their work whilst protecting themselves and the ones they serve. FOST will support its staff and community volunteers with PPE so that when they interact with communities they are not at a higher risk of either spreading the disease or getting the disease.
The project will mitigate the effects of COVID 19 on communities through provision of Psycho-social support services. Measures taken by the Government to mitigate the outbreak, in particular a country-wide lockdown, the restriction of cross-border movements, have impacted negatively food security and child protection across Zimbabwe. Economic stress, further increased by the impact of COVID-19 lockdown on those dependent on the informal economy for their survival, anxiety surrounding the COVID-19 outbreak and living in lockdown without access to basic services, increases stress in households, psychosocial distress and depression, resulting in increased risk of sexual and other forms of violence and abuse. Children and families affected and infected by the virus are also bound to experience stigma and discrimination in the community. COVID has caused deaths globally, a fact which has spread panic within communities. FOST will provide counselling and Psychological First Aid to boys and girls, women and men affected through child friendly spaces and support groups.
The humanitarian crisis in Zimbabwe is expected huge implications for school readiness, attendance and participation. Pressure is coming from communities and other stakeholders for government to reopen schools. However without a well-resourced response, the combined impact of these crises risks having a lasting negative children’s’ education and increasing drop outs. Without a conducive and disease-free school environment, COVID-19 poses a risk to children’s health and wellbeing. FOST will provide schools in remote and rural areas with disinfectants. Schools will need to be disinfected before reopening their gates to children and disinfecting the schools will continue during the schooling. In return for these disinfectants, FOST will in return request the schools to allow a certain number of OVC to attend school without paying fees the rest of 2020 terms. This will promote access to education for OVC whilst ensuring that the schools are safe for the children.
FOST is a member of the national Education and Child Protection Clusters hence will work with the IEC TWGs to produce simple child friendly messages on COVID 19 that are consistent and factual. Messaging directed at boys and girls appears to be unavailable. The main focus of our messaging will be on who do we want to tell what, and why and how? FOST will include child protection issues in messaging as it appears that GBV and child abuse is exacerbated during lock downs and some CP safety nets have been compromised though lack of movement of community volunteers

1 - Activities

-Development of key messages that are child friendly and circulating them through print and electronic media-Production of 2000 IEC materials that are simplified and translated to vernacular languages-Facilitate discussion and dialogue on 12 Radio and 12 TV shows to disseminate ways of prevention, information on how COVID 19 affects children and how its impact can be mitigated, Advocate for issues affecting children and their families in communities that we serve such as access to education, clean water, protection, -Create and maintain 8 WhatsApp groups for community volunteers from 80 wards and 2 provinces to share information on COVID 19 for dissemination, -Use mass Text Messaging to disseminate information on Coronavirus, -Counselling on line and face to face with children affected by Covid 19-Establishment of 8 child friendly spaces and support groups in 8 districts to offer PSS in Emergencies and Psychological First Aid, -Provision of block grants to schools in form of disinfectants in return for free education for orphaned and vulnerable children affected by the corona virus -Provision of accelerated learning materials and training to catch up on time lost, -Capacity building of Child Safe spaces to provide PSS to children affected by COVID 19, -Stakeholder dialogues to reduce stigma and discrimination on children and families as a result of COVID 19 - Use of art, play therapy and drama to provide emotional support to children psychologically distressed due to the effects of the COVID 19-Mobilize and Support cluster sectors meetings through cluster leads and discuss progress on issues affecting children due to the COVID 19, -Provision of PPE for front-line staff who are working in communities in child protection cases
