Project Summary

the project aims to rehabilitate the sewer and water infrastructure in three different locations (Afrin, Jarablus and Azaz districts) based on needs assessment to secure safe access to sanitation and water services and to contribute to decrease the number of WBD cases in the area of implementation. Furthermore, to ensure integration between activities WATAN is planning through this intervention to rehabilitate the WASH facilities in schools, and health and community centers. Therefore, WATAN will target the girls’ schools and women health and community centers to support in this respect the most vulnerable groups from the area. The hygiene promotion sessions dedicated to women and children will be an integral part of the interventions in target localities. WATAN will ensure hygiene promotion as a key component of programming through its interventions in schools, community/protection centers, and health facilities.
In conclusion, through this intervention WATAN will bring back the safe sewer to the homes of 3 communities’ inhabitants whom are still suffering from the danger of contracting diarrheal disease as sanitation services contribute enormously to human health and well-being, especially for girls and women.

Needs

Recent months of the Syrian conflict have seen an escalation of violence in the north, particularly in and around southern rural of Idleb governorate. Accordingly, southern Idleb and northern Hama have witnessed mass-scale displacement were most of the people moved toward northern Aleppo, area considered safer. This led to an overpopulation of the villages and cities in Afrin, Jarablus and Azaz districts. The WASH cluster estimates that more than 346,000 people were displaced to northern rural of Aleppo. While, the number of WBD cases in 2018 in Azaz, Afrin and Jarablus reached 32,231 in October 2018 (WASH cluster dashboard). Afrin city is located in the northern rural of Aleppo hosting around 52,412 individuals (WASH cluster dashboard). However, a letter is received from the local council indicates that the number of total populations in Afrin is 120,000 individuals this includes 62,400 children under 18, 34,802 IDPs. The sewer systems in Afrin are 80 years old. With no water pumped in the network, the sewer network didn’t show any problems until the rainy season. However, after a detailed assessment by WATAN’s field staff, it discovered that in many locations across Afrin neighbourhoods, the streets are flooded with wastewater during the rainy season. In addition, to offer a more comprehensive intervention, WATAN has and is still coordinating with Bahar organization. The second one is planning to pump water from Midanki dam to Afrin, activity which will defiantly show the defects of the sewer lines in the city. To secure the right of people from Afrin to safe access to sanitation services, WATAN is planning to rehabilitate the sewer network in the city while employing the “Do no harm” approach. Furthermore, WATAN established a health center (Khayr Afrin), this health center will support achieving the objective from this intervention by monitoring and providing treatment to the number of WBD cases in the city of Afrin.Azaz is a district neighbor to Afrin hosting 74,865 individuals (WASH cluster dashboard). However, and after getting in touch with the local council of Azaz, the number of total populations in Azaz is 130,000, this includes 67,600 children under 18, and 65,274 IDPs. In terms of the sewer network, it has the same problems as Afrin. However, in terms of severity scale, the dashboard of the WASH cluster estimates at 5 the level of Azaz sub-district due to the high number of WBD. This was caused mainly by the malfunction of the sewers produced by the lack of water pumped through the network in the past 2 years in addition to the depreciation of the sewer network older than 70 years. . Jarablus hosts more than 23,650 individuals (WASH cluster dashboard). However, WATAN through its great presence in the city received a formal letter from the local council indicating the number of total populations is 80,000, this includes 41,600 children under 18 and 45,000 IDPs. while relying on poor sewer lines. WATAN is already working on the water systems in the city and it is expected to start pumping the water during the coming days. With around 500 cubic meter / hr to be pumped to the city, WATAN is planning to rehabilitate also the sewer networks as it started to show defects during October 2018 with the rainy season coming. Moreover, with the occasion of the present intervention for restoration of water stations, WATAN has noticed some gaps in the water network as new neighborhoods don’t have access to it. Therefore, WATAN is planning to connect the water network to these new neighborhoods as many households live above the threshold of the existing water tanks. In addition, WATAN will set up a water high tank of 300 m3 and 28 meters height. This activity will ensure access to safe water to 4,000 individual host and IDPs. Furthermore, as in the other 2 locations, the sewer lines will be rehabilitated.

Implementation of activities

WATAN field engineers and staff will carry out participatory planning, joint monitoring with the local councils and community leaders and those authorities responsible for WASH services, while the end users of these services will also be consulted to triangulate the information provided through FGDs and KIIs. The cost recovery mechanism will be part of the early discussion to ensure management of services are well taken care by the contribution of beneficiaries. Those consulted will include men, women, elderly, children, people with disabilities, IDP and host community members. Transparency will be a cross cutting element of the project with the purpose to ensure good understanding, avoid accusations and secure efficiency of the spent funds. Thus, WASH assessments will support in finalizing the selection of targeted infrastructure needing repair. Number of people benefiting from the repair, cost, time and resource availability will all be key considerations.WATAN will be a crucial player in the implementation of this project, capitalizing on its extensive relevant experience and established presence in Aleppo governorate. WATAN will be responsible for the implementation of the activities, coordinating with local authorities in Syria by building on their extensive network, and facilitating access.

Protection Risk Analysis

WATAN will promote gender-sensitive programming at all stages of the project cycle (i.e. in assessments, participation in activities and monitoring). Gender analysis will allow to gain insight from both men and women as well as those with special needs such as female and child-headed households, the elderly and those with disabilities that will shape the implementation of activities. Furthermore, the project will target 1,000 women and 4,500 children with rehabilitation of WASH facilities in schools and community/protection centers.Project data will be disaggregated by age and gender in order to support project management decisions and ensure that programme interventions are tailored to meet the needs of the most vulnerable segments of the target populations. Moreover, as other NGOs are planning to pump water to Afrin and Azaz communities, this project will ensure the pumped water is safely disposed by employing the "Do No Harm" approach.This intervention will bring back the safe sewer to the homes of 3 communities’ inhabitants whom are still suffering from the danger of contracting diarrheal disease as sanitation services contribute enormously to human health and well-being, especially for girls and women.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

WATAN has considered the security situation and the number of beneficiaries in designing the project activities. The project doesn’t depend too much on importing materials from out of Syria, thus WATAN eliminates the potential delays caused by the closing of crossing points with Turkey. When implementing the activities, WATAN will ensure quality of the materials and safeguarding the international standards for WASH facilities.
