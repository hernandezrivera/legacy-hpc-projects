Project Summary

winterisation assistance to meet the emergency NFI needs of the target population through the provision of core items (Activity 1.1.1.2: Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs) as defined by the S/NFI working group. The Project will target IDPs in informal settlements and use an in-kind delivery modality to reach ~ 2000 households/ 10,000 individuals with seasonal NFIs and children's clothing. Target locations will be decided closer to the time of implementation and based on assessment data collected by the implementing partners field team and gap analysis provided by the SNFI working group. As we have good access across NES it is unclear at this stage where will be targeted, previous project have been implemented in ArRaqqa and DeZ governorates.

3 - Needs

The overall NFI PiN for the WoS has increased by 5 per cent in 2022 to4.91 million. Unaffordability is the main driver of need, with 67 percent of the population reporting that they are unable to afford NFIs.According to the HNO 2022 there are over 200,000 Individuals in need of NFI assistance in Ar-Raqqa governorate alone. Given the cost of items needed for winterisation across NES have risen by 31% as compared to this time last year the unaffordability of these essential items puts further pressure on the affected population and their HHI needs.Across Syria as a whole Winterisation assistance is one of the top 3 needs as expressed by households in informal settlements Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO 2022).According to the 2022 HNO it is predicated that in 2023 the overall NFI PiN will increase to over 5 million individuals with 894,850 individuals in extreme need and over 2000 in catastrophic need.

4 - Implementation of activities

Assessments will be undertaken in Augusts 2023 to identify a case load and collect demographic information used as a foundation for procurement. Selected caseloads will be communicated to the SNFI working group to mitigate against duplication. Selected informal settlements will be assisted via an in-kind, full coverage (blanket distributions) modality. Exit survey data and distribution observation checklists will be analysed in real time to ensure the principles of AAP are being upheld. PDM will take place approximated 2 weeks after distributions.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Risk - inter community tension through varied assistance. Mitigation - adherence to SNFI guidance & full coverage modality.2. Risk - Tension between host communities, IDPs and returnees. Mitigation - Rigorous selection criteria, well communicated to communities, inclusion of key stakeholders in selection and communication 3. Risk - Abuse of Authority by distribution actors. Mitigation - Code of conduct signed and adhered to by implementing partner, CRM process in place, whistle blowing policy and process in place. 4. Risk - Risk of physical harm at distribution points (security, safety). Mitigation - Experience distribution team, good community acceptance through on going communication, distribution guidlines minimum standards, priority lines and assistance for vulnerable, timely distributions, checklist observations and exit surveys.5. The risk towards the humanitarian nature of the intervention for the presence and activities of military. Mitigation - Pre agree terms with authorities, upholding minimum standards of distribution guidelines6. Lack of accepted documentation may unfairly exclude certain population. Mitigation - communication to communities and authorities regarding rights to assistance, CRM in place 7. Access restrictions to area due to active fighting. Mitigation - planned assistance for early in the winter season, constant security analysis


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

1. Risk - Staff member engages in SEA against community member. Mitigation - Implementing partner receive internal safeguarding training and refresher training, sign code of conduct, IEC material (posters) displayed at distribution sites to increase community knowledge of PSEA, safeguarding focal points on hand to receive complaints and refer to PSEA services.2. Risk - Implementing partner receive PSEA related disclosure. Mitigation (action) - Implementing partner connected to Protection focal points as referral pathways, internal training on safeguarding principles and receiving disclosures.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Risk - access restraint due to security context. Assumption - implementing partner have access to alternative caseload. Mitigation - on going security and trend analysis and risk assessment.2. Risk - implementing team are killed or injured through remnants of or active conflict. Assumption - surge capacity within team is sufficient to ensure project completion. Mitigation - good security management SOPs are adhered.3. Risk - non acceptance from community following non renewal of X-Border resolution. Assumption - humanitarian actors are considered accountable for the lack of service provision. Mitigation - open communication channels with community leaders and local administrations.
