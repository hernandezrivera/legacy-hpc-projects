Project Summary

According to Somalia WASH Cluster , the protracted crises in Somalia, now compounded by the effects of Covid-19, Floods and droughts has resulted in massive challenges pertaining to Water, Sanitation and Hygiene. Approximately, 4.6 million Somalis are still in need due to insufficient access to portable water and inadequate sanitation services of the affected population in many districts, thus negatively affecting the country’s public health and the dignity of highly vulnerable and displaced communities. This spike in numbers compared to 2020 is mainly exacerbated by the situation developed as a result of Covid-19 and its rapid transmission. Lack of adequate WASH services to the population is a main cause of spread of Acute Diarrheal Diseases (ADD), leading to malnutrition in the country and contributing to increasing the existing stunting rate of 25.3% in children below 5 years of age. It is also a cause of relapse (ADD) when treated patients and children returning to their communities where safe water and sanitation coverages are limited, resulting in limited sustainability of health programs.
The proposed project will be implemented in Jubaland state specifically Gedo and lower Juba region. The objective of the project is Communities and women, men, boys and girls affected by crises in the target area receive life-saving WASH assistance appropriate and relevant to their immediate needs. To achieve this the project will ;rehabilitate two boreholes and water extension system that can cater for domestic and livestock needs of men, women, boys and girls of at least 750 HHs; Construct 10 communal berkads in strategic locations based on the WASH guidelines; Construct 100 ( gender and disability sensitive) pit latrines and in Gedo and Lower Juba regions to promote safe defecation and promote good hygiene practices; Establish and train 4 Water Resource Management committee who will be responsible for the management and maintenance of the rehabilitated shallow wells and the constructed of borehole. Women will be allocated 50% representation in all committees; Distribute 1000 ceramic water filters at household level to improve water treatment; Engage men, women, boys and girls in 20 hygiene promotion sessions in strategic locations such as the market place, health centers and schools. In coordination with heath centers, engage people visiting the center in hygiene promotion sessions and provision of IEC materials; Distribute hygiene/sanitary kits using CVA approach to 1500 vulnerable women and girls for protection against key public health risks. This will be done in coordination with existing health facilities. The project will deliberately target PWD to access WASH services. A project implementing committee (PIC) will be established comprising of community structures (women and youth), local authorities, community leaders and organizations representing PWDs. The PIC will be involved in selection of beneficiaries to ensure that the most vulnerable people(PWDs , women and youth ) receive immediate WASH assistance. The project will also address barriers that hinder women and PWD access WASH services

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash programming has contributed to greater level of accountability through electronic reporting, local ownership engagement, building local coping mechanism and support cohesion and transition to development. The project will target vulnerable households from both IDP and host communities who have an able-bodied member that can engage in CFW activities. In WASH, Cash for work will be adopted for construction and rehabilitation of water facilities, rehabilitation of latrines , construction of garbage pits and community clean up campaigns. E vouchers on the other hand will be used for distribution of hygiene kitsDo No Harm approach will be applied to ensure that the interventions do not cause or fuel conflict. The mobile money transfer, currently used to administer Cash transfers as well as E- vouchers will also be used for this project to administer payments to target beneficiaries.The approach will ensure beneficiaries restore their dignity since they will be working for their income instead of getting in-kind hand-outs. The cash transfer will also offer them freedom of choice on how to spend their income. The decision to use the approach was identified following discussion with the community.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

The project was designed in a way that will strengthen the local markets and boost the demand and supply chain in the target locations .all project supplies and equipment will be bought locally to boost the local economy , Cash based modality for work in construction of WASH facilities will strengthen livelihoods at household level, giving families an income through which they can be able to meet other basic needs including water access

Mitigation Measures

The project takes cognisant of the fact that the crises has different impacts men, women boys and girls differently which can exacerbate unequal power relations, discriminate affected populations on basis of disability, gender and age. recognition of this differences will enable the program to reducing existing inequalities by ensuring that services are available to all persons including people with disability. Just like men and boys are engaged, women, girls and people with disability will also be empowered to participate actively in program activities and take up leadership positions in project committees for enhanced development as they enjoy equal opportunities. Protecting human rights of people with disability and promoting gender equality in this regard will remain central to this response. Gender will be mainstreamed and integrated throughout the project cycle to ensure that adequate and efficient services are provided. Cash based interventions will accommodate both male and female beneficiaries and people with disability in affected locations. The will be need to strengthen and improve safe programming based on ‘Do No Harm’ principle ensuring that basic protection principles are weaved into the humanitarian response across thematic priorities. To enhance protection of target groups , the project will uphold standards and guidelines that enhance service delivery. The SPHERE project and the Somalia operational Framework ( SOF) by the WASH cluster Somalia will be referenced to ensure that WASH facilities constructed/ rehabilitated provide protection for women and girls and they are easily accessible and within acceptable distanced to reduce risk of abuse.
