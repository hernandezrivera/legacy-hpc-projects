Project Summary

The main objective of this project is to reduce food and nutrition insecurity, restore livelihood and build resilience of the affected population/communities in the protracted crisis areas in Blue Nile, South Kordofan, West Kordofan (including Abyei north), Kassala, and Red Sea states by safeguarding their livelihood’s assets and strengthening their productive capacity. The affected population includes IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host communities. The project is in line with the three FSL Sector Strategic Outcomes 1, 2 and 3; and in line with the Multi Year Humanitarian strategy (2017 2019) Outcome 1, 2 and 3. The multi-year strategy will allow FAO to provide immediate lifesaving assistance whenever the need arises and at the same time engage strategically to address the drivers of vulnerabilities and underlying structural issues across the targeted states. In particular, the project will focus on increasing the access to productive assets, inputs and services to enable the beneficiaries to improve food and nutrition security status and sustain their livelihoods. It will also enhance the income generation capacities of particularly disadvantaged population groups, such as women headed households and support them with diversified livelihood means and skills. The project will ensure a balanced dietary intake to avoid any type of malnutrition, including micronutrient deficiency. The project will also contribute to environmental protection and conservation through interventions on establishment/rehabilitation of tree’s nurseries for production and distribution of multipurpose trees’ seedlings as well as training of the targeted communities’ members (especially women) on production and use of Fuel Efficient Stoves (FES).

Needs

The ongoing economic situation, along with the recent lifting of fuel and wheat subsidies, contributes to the devaluation of the local currency and high rates of inflation leading to an increase in food prices and costs of agricultural production. Despite the good harvest in 2018; the high cost of production because of high agricultural input prices as well as shortages of fuel and cash have affected farming production. Subsequently, the national average price of crops and livestock on the market has increased significantly compared to historic averages.Increased prices, compounded by limited access to livelihoods opportunities for the most vulnerable population, have led to a severe decline in the purchasing power of the most vulnerable communities including returnees, refugees, IDPs, as well as vulnerable host communities and residents.According to the latest IPC results, 5.67 million people in Sudan are classified as food insecure. Among the food insecure population, the sector partners plan to target 4.39 million of the most vulnerable people that fall under IPC Phase 3 and above; from this FSL sector target, FAO will target 532,960 persons from the IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in Blue Nile, White Nile, South Kordofan, West Kordofan, Kassala, Gedaref and Red Sea states. The targeted population lack economic access to food due to limited livelihoods and income sources; this contributed to limited availability of household food stocks, hence suffers from reduced dietary diversity and high rates of malnutrition. As the whole Country of Sudan continues to face economic challenges, the food insecurity of IDPs, vulnerable residents host communities members, refugees and returnees is expected to worsen. With the upcoming lean season in May, the food insecurity of those most vulnerable population is likely to continue to deteriorate. Timely and adequate agriculture and livestock based livelihood interventions for small-scale farming households, pastoralists and agro-pastoralists, IGA and environmental protection and conservation interventions are necessary to ensure self-reliance and resilience to seasonal food insecurity.The targeted geographical areas under this project include Blue Nile, South Kordofan, West Kordofan (including north part of Abyei box), Kassala and Red Sea States. These targeted states usually suffer from protracted insecurity situation attributed either to the prolonged conflict between GoS and SPLM-North in Blue Nile and South Kordofan states; the continuous influx of SSRs to West and South Kordofan, Blue Nile, in addition to the negative impact of climate change factors.The project will introduce veterinary voucher system to improve access to veterinary services by vulnerable HHs so as to safeguard private service delivery. Cash intervention for community asset creation before the main crop season will be considered after intensive market assessments.

Activities

Output/Activity 1: Through this agricultural support intervention, FAO will provide good quality Declared seeds of the main crops, which are usually cultivated in the targeted areas based on the priorities of the beneficiaries. It will promote diversification of crop production including cultivation of nutritious crops such as vegetables, legumes and fruits as well as cultivation of the traditional staple food crops (sorghum and millet) and the major cash/oil crops (ground nut and sesame). This intervention will enable the targeted households to produce their own food and obtain income from marketing the surplus production. Output/Activity 2: Through this intervention, veterinary services such as livestock vaccination for the endemic and TADS will be provided mostly on cost recovery basis; only for the most vulnerable households under emergency to be provided free of charge. Training of CAHWs (basic and refresher training) will be provided and the trained CAHWs to be supported with kits to deliver the veterinary services to the beneficiaries at their home villages on cost recovery basis. Provide access to animal feed concentrate on cost recovery to promote and enhance traditional livestock production and food consumption;Output/Activity 3: Capacity of some categories of the population developed and supported with assets for income generationSuch as restocking with small ruminants to most vulnerable households with special focus on women headed households, to improve their accessibility to milk special children to improve the nutritional status of the targeted beneficiaries. Support with donkey carts for income generation, as it is most effective mean of creating job opportunities for the youth. Training of women on food processing is one of the important activity for women empowerment.Output/Activity 4: Assistance for environmental protection and conservation provided at households and communities levels through establishment/ rehabilitation of nurseries.
