Project Summary

Gedaref state as one of the eastern states went through a period of violence and turmoil, which was partly caused by fragile ecosystems, deeply entrenched poverty and under investment lack of basic services most critical water and sanitation across all localities. After the signing of the Eastern Sudan Peace Agreement in 2005, the situation has been calm, but the three states still have high rates of poverty , malnutrition and basic services most critical water and sanitation. The project is targeting to improve water, sanitation and hygiene promotion in twolocalities ( Gorisha and East Galabat).
Access to improved sanitation and access to improved water facilities were both independently associated with stunting rates in the eastern states are high Gadaref (45.5%, Sanitation situation is also a problem with 61% in Gadaref. The target localities had over 60% open defecation area. Hygienic practices including hand washing is very low. The objective of the project is to improve water, sanitation and hygiene promotion, water treatment and safe storage and to certify ODF communities in the target locality by respective state Ministry of Health. Strong local presence and prior experience of working with the target communities are essential factor for successful of the project.
The project target two localities Gorisha and East Galabat, the beneficiaries will be more than 25,000 population in the two localities, where in the targeted areas, hygiene standards and sanitation coverage are extremely low, despite the fact that in some community’s hygiene and sanitation promotion is been done at regular intervals . Hand washing, even after defecating or after cleaning a child after it has defecated, is not a commonly practiced. In most villages, less than 10% of households have a latrine.
