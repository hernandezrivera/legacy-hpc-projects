Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing comprehensive protection services to conflict displaced children and women affected by the conflict between the government forces and non-state armed actors in Guriceel and Hobyo districts. It targets families, children and women displaced from Guriceel and Baadweyne who have settle in IDP settlements and host communities in Hobyo, Guriceel and outside villages within the two districts in Galgadud regions.
Following the conflict, a joint interagency assessment found that at least 100,000 people (16,740 households) have left and sought shelter in 28 nearby villages from the Guriceel conflict while other 7,656 people from the Baadweyne. The displaced include about 1,000 unaccompanied minors and 2,000 PLWDs and health related complications. About 18,440 of the affected people were IDPs living in Guri Ceel before the situation escalated. Three new IDP settlements are accommodating about 44,000 people (7,300 families) with the rest living with relatives in overcrowded shelters or have remained within Guri Ceel area due to lack of capacity to move or no clan ties in rural villages. In Hobyo IDPs who have been forced out of their homes due to armed conflict, drought and other causes are predominantly residing within the host communities and the host communities have been burdened with hosting the IDPs, food, water basic services availability is strained. In addition, families displaced by the recent Baadweyne conflict have settled in Hobyo where they lack protection services.
TUOS proposes to provide protection services to the displaced children and women in Guriceel and Hobyo. The services will not only be provided within the two towns but also accessible villages in the outskirts where the displaced have settled. Of the displaced more than 1000 were unaccompanied and separated children, women, children and the elderly. In provision of the services TUOS targets to train a core group of women and men as community based child protection committee who will assist in referral of survivors to TUOS and other service providers. The organisation will train 120 community members as CBCP. The project targets to provide GBV services to affected women and girls from the community and the displaced families. Apart from the services trainings will be conducted to capacitate the community in developing home base solutions in the prevention of GBV/CP, provision of solar lamps and dignity kits. The project will work with all members in the community to enhance protection mechanisms. TUOS proposes to establish 4 child friendly spaces, establishment of 6 mobile protection services which will include CFS providing services to the nearby villages. 10 CFS facilitators will be engaged to coordinate and provide basic PSS services, numerical and literacy skills at the 4 CFS, providing a safe environment for the children among other services. 6 child help desks will be established which will be vital in the collection of information on IDTR, UASC and protection incidences. The proposed project plans to conduct training to 10 social workers who will be providing protection services, referral and overseeing the provision of comprehensive medical services to survivors, and working with 300 foster families for UASC children before reunification. Furthermore 2,009 PLWDs and health related conditions to provide a gender sensitive structured mental health and PSS as well as case management. Capacity building activities will also encompass issues of PLWD, minority groups, women, men, boys and girls Human rights issues, children’s rights, risk mitigation, service provision among other topics to improve on the safety environment of all vulnerable groups in the community. In addition, TUOS will also conduct trainings to health workers on clinical case management to enhance and provide timely medical services to survivors in the 2 target areas.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Working with the Ministry of Women and Human Rights Affairs and representatives from the local authorities and community stakeholders TUOS will buildon the already good working relation in seeing that all groups in the communities have access to the services being provided under the project activities. The organization will conduct activities at community centers where all members get the opportunity to participate in the activities and information being provided. TUOS will engage all community members and groups who will be presented equally in all the meetings and all the engagementsThe organisation plans to work with the community especially women and girls in designing the implementation of the activities and committees to work with TUOS and the community in the provision of GBV/CP services hence building and bringing about gender inclusion in the project implementation period. TUOS will conduct trainings to enhance the community capacity in reduction of risks and development of community mechanism for protection. As mitigation measure the community will be trained on community prevention mechanism on protection which will be vital in the reduction of risks. It will highlight on the various vulnerable groups :PLWDs, minority groups, women, men, girls and boys and how as community is vital in working together towards a common goal in the upholding human rights, children's rights, protection and how to get services Safety issues are of most top priority at TUOS where we have been conducting protection, nutrition and health projects in Galgadud and Mudug regions hence the same criteria and procedures will be followed in the implementation of the proposed project.
