Project Summary

National Nutrition Survey (NNS) 2018 revealed that the prevalence of wasting among young children in Pakistan, at 17.7 per cent, remains above the internationally agreed emergency threshold (15.0 per cent), and all provinces are seriously affected (9.4–23.3 per cent). The COVID-19 crisis has increased vulnerabilities and there is a strong likelihood that Pakistan will not be able to achieve its nutrition-related national and global commitments, including World Health Assembly Targets and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 2 (zero hunger), if bold, sustained, coordinated and performance-based measures are not taken. The proposed project is consistent with the UNICEF Conceptual Framework of Malnutrition. Consultations with stakeholders, Government departments, and secondary data analysis and field studies provided the base for the designing of the project. The proposed project in target districts (Kambar Shahdadkot and Tando Allahyar in Sindh Province and Battagram District in KP province) is continuation of Action Against Hunger (ACF) current Nutrition and Health projects - Programme for Improved Nutrition in Sindh (PINS) and Provision of lifesaving undernutrition treatment and prevention support to vulnerable communities affected by critical nutrition emergency in District Battagram, KP. Continuation of lifesaving care and malnutrition prevention activities are proposed in this project to overcome challenges of malnutrition and WASH in proposed target districts. Children from 6-59 months and mothers at risk and infants 0 to 6 months will be direct beneficiaries. The project will provide nutrition-specific and sensitive support to the population living in target districts through the continuation of Outpatient Therapeutic Programme (OTP) sites, Nutrition Stabilization Centers (NSCs), Infant Young Child Feeding (IYCF) corners, outreach component and WASH at healthcare facilities including rehabilitation / safe water points, latrines, handwashing stations with supplies.
