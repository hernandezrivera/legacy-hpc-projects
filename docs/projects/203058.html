Project Summary

The project is going to tackle the food security needs of the most vulnerable households through unconditional-based transfer to support the identified households to cover their basic needs. Linked with this, a small component of cash for work is foreseen for a group of identified beneficiaries in order to improve their dignity and ownership on the project and enhance the social cohesion, as the activity will see the involvement of different group of people working together to improve the condition of the neighborhood they live in.
Starting from this, the intervention will address some other needs encountered at household level in terms of protection and education: this approach will help to deal with the different needs of all members in the households: men, boy, women and girls. Based on the needs emerged, households will be supported by Cash for Education, in order to retain children in school and prevent the risk of drop out, child labor and child marriage; case management and focused PSS for children at risk, protection support through Cash for protection, awareness sessions as positive parenting skills, and finally referrals for GBV cases and caregivers.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

- Deterioration of economic conditions and social tensions linked to the selection of direct beneficiaries: COOPI has planned to hold public meetings to disseminate the initiative and the method of beneficiaries’ selection. The project will be well coordinated with partners, and local authorities and other actors within the cluster “Child Protection and Food Security” both at central and local level, with UNHCR and OCHA. - Worsening of security situation in the country: COOPI works in constant coordination with UNHCR, and UNDSS and with other humanitarian actors regarding security in the country and required emergency measures. Additionally, COOPI owns a punctual security plan, which establishes the measures to use carried out on security levels.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The geographical area of the project as well as the target groups have been identified based on local urgent needs, without discrimination related to religion, social or political background and gender. The identified households will be properly informed about the activities proposed and their opportunities through preliminary outreaching activities. All COOPI interventions respect the do no harm and impartialassistance principles.The project is adopting at a participatory approach at all levels, involving all the actors concerned in the decision makings, from planning to implementation through focus groups and regular meetings. COOPI will guarantee proper communication and transparency with beneficiaries and stakeholders at all levels by using different culturally appropriate tools and languages to make them accessible to different groups. It will provide accessible and timely information to affected populations on programmes, organizational rocedures, structures, and processes.
