Project Summary

Zimbabwe has been affected by the Covid 19 outbreak despite the low numbers of infection and death. Currently 146 people have been infected and 4 deaths have been recorded as of 29 May 2020 (MoHCC, May 2020). Zimbabwe instituted lockdown measures to contain the spread of the disease allowing the Government of Zimbabwe and its partners to put measures to reduce morbidity and deaths from the disease. Rural areas of Zimbabwe are high risk areas as there are limited WASH facilities and knowledge on the disease in limited. It is from this background that Help will mainstream COVID 19 measures in Goromonzi, Kwekwe and Marondera districts and wards of operation to contributes to government efforts to reduce the spread of COVID 19. The project will target the setting up of handwashing stations at public places especially business sentinels and health facilities in the wards of operation. The project will also distribute hygiene kits containing soap and alcohol based sanitisers to beneficiary households. Further the project will ensure frontline field staff are equipped with adequate PPE in the fight against COVID 19. Help will work with MoHCC, other government departments and other NGOs to broadcast risk communication messages with beneficiaries to ensure improved WASH knowledge, attitude and practices.
