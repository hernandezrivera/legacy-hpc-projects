Project Summary

This initiative expands on Tabitha's continuing work in response to the food insecurity in Lebanon especially after the 2020 Beirut bombing and the ensuing political and economic crises, which has left almost half of Lebanon's people in poverty. Food insecurity is becoming increasingly severe for the most disadvantaged, and resorting to negative coping techniques, such as reduced food consumption, remains a concerning trend. Tabitha is already providing healthy meals to school aged children, through public schools and community centers. Through its community centers, Tabitha is targeting children in school aged attending education or PSS activities. Tabitha will provide parents of the students attending education and or PSS activities at the community centers with the awareness raising related to healthy eating habits, this is becoming an urgent need especially with the currently economic crisis, where people are unable to purchase food. The sessions will include awareness on healthy food to be served for their children, including stress on avoiding fast foods, mothers would be instructed to pack only healthy homemade low fat, low sugar foods for the child’s snack and lunch time, list the food items that are not advisable to be brought in the school, encourage parents to model healthy eating.
Tabitha also works in Bourj El Barajneh Palestinian camp through one partner PWHO (Palestinian Women Humanitarian Organization). The feeding program in the community centers and the public school has already its funding secured for 2023. This project consists of delivering healthy sandwiches in addition to one piece of fruits or vegetables on daily basis for students attending education or PSS activities.
The healthy meals distribution is also accompanied with awareness on healthy nutrition. The awareness is done for children but also with their parents as done at Tabitha's community centers (CC). The activities will be done at the 3 CCs of Tabitha in Aley, Batroun and Achrafieh and at the the partner PWHO at Bourj El Barajneh and Shatila camps.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

This project falls under the following sector objective of the nutrition sector: "school age children and adolescents have access to and demand for services aiming at prevention of all forms of malnutrition particularly among girls" and the targeted activity is school age children and adolescents have promoted knowledge and skills on healthy nutrition and benefiting from anemia prevention. This project is already being implemented in Aley, Batroun, Achrafieh and through the partner PWHO in Bourj El Barajneh and Shatila camps.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Tabitha has immense experience working on inclusion and gender mainstreaming, particularly under its protection program. Tabitha utilizes social work methodology to ensure that beneficiaries receive individual vulnerability/needs assessments through a network of outreach workers (selected among vulnerable host communities) to facilitate trust building and access to isolated groups. Tabitha mainstreams protection in all its activities which allows maximization of the protective impact of the project through ensuring that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing/ perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Tabitha also engages in social cohesion activities that are designed to bring together individuals of different backgrounds. Tabitha staff have been sensitized to gender & diversity and should recognize that vulnerabilities differ from one to another.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Community engagement is at the core of Tabitha's humanitarian work across Lebanon. Tabitha has created a robust two-way feedback mechanism, operational through various channels including calls, emails, written boxes, and verbal feedback. Tabitha builds many of its projects based on the feedback received at its community centers which are trusted, longstanding entities. Tabitha also maintains high monitoring and evaluation standards, and ensures that staff are well-trained on the code of conduct and humanitarian standards. Tabitha has successfully participated in a series of coaching/mentoring sessions on PSEA & safeguarding integration done by ABAAD in partnership with OCHA, LHF and the Lebanese National PSEA Network. A training on integrity is followed by all staff of Tabitha and it is mandatory. Moreover, in the feeding program in schools and community centers, women parents of the students are the one preparing the sandwiches and their feedback is always taken into consideration.
