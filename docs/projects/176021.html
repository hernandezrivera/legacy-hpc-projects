Project Summary

Living conditions of IDPs in Metuge and other places in CD significantly improved through enhanced Camp Management and access to basic needs and services and participation.
In conjunction with local authorities and relevant humanitarian partners operating in Metuge and Cabo Delgado, AeA will ensure effective leadership and coordination of service delivery in the designated displacement sites (Metuge, Ancuabe, Namuno, Chiure). Additionally, AeA will ensure that those residing in IDP sites are able to actively participate in service provision and have the ability to voice their opinion regarding service delivery.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential risks to protection are identified through continuous assessments, including the creation of mechanisms that allow for community participation. Some of these do pre-exists and need to be fostered (committees of vulnerable groups), others will need to be implemented from scratch (information desks). Protection risks are identified conjointly with the community and followe up on by the team. Trainings and the fostering of community structures allow for improved and sustainable agency of the displaced community, with special focus on those who are more vulnerable, in all community relevant aspects. Information derived from FGDs, community gatherings and committees will be processed and forwarded to the relevant clusters.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

All our projects have a methodology based on listening, co-creation, execution. Through continuous evaluation and monitoring, these stages are flexible and adapted to the needs.Fostering the community structures will aid this process, as well as the divulgation of feedback and complaint mechanisms alongside any other activity, such as linha verde. The constant presence of AeA staff on the ground additionally allows for close community engagement. AeAs long term presence in the area fosters stronger bounds and relationships of trust with host families and local government structures.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Specific committees of persons with disabilities and their relatives will be created, in order to voice out specific needs and gaps in the response. This will take into consideration the different types of disability as relevant to the implementation accessible service structures. This refers to enhances participation, as well as to sufficient physical assess to all necessary services.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Before acting in development projects, AeA carries out a diagnosis of the territory in which it includes the environmental risk depending on its actions.Risk management, adaptation and mitigation are the transversal axis of our actions.Therefore, we try to undertake certain actions such as the planning of the territory, analysis of ownership or use in this case, analysis of environmental risks by sector (agriculture, fishing, services, etc.), are actions that define our work.
