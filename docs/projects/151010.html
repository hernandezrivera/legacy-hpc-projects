Project Summary

This project is an integrated response around Nutrition. It is intended to mitigate the physical and protection consequences of drought on populations and communities directly affected in Elwak, and Baardhere of Gedo region, The main objective of this project is to provide integrated life-saving emergency Nutrition services to 19,700 drought affected direct beneficiaries and intends to achieve the following, capacity building of nutrition staff on IMAM/IYCF services, infant young child feeding best practices (IYCF), as well as conduct caregivers infant and young child feeding practices, IYCF promotion session for better information dissemination to the caregivers in the project area. Referral of malnourished children with complicated cases will be done to the Outpatient Therapeutic Programme (OTP) centers managed by HARD and other actors in the program areas thus providing more holistic opportunity to the target beneficiaries HARD will closely coordinate with Local MOH as well as Federal level MOH and social mobilizers, elders all involved in activities on mobilizing communities. To support increase access to basic nutrition services in the area

Needs

According to FSNAU Nutrition Update: July 2018,Gu (June) 2018 season nutrition assessment among Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) in main settlements shows Critical prevalence of acute malnutrition (Global Acute Malnutrition-GAM ≥ 15%) in 7 out of 15 population groups surveyed in June 2018: Bossaso, Qardho, Galkacyo Garowe, Mogadishu, Dolow and Baidoa IDPs. From nutrition surveys conducted by FSNAU and partners between June-July 2018 indicate that the overall nutrition situation in Somalia has continued to improve due to a combination of improved food security conditions, reduced outbreak of diseases and sustained humanitarian interventions. However, high level of acute malnutrition tends to persist in several population groups due to underlying/structural causes. There are no improvement in the overall current acute malnutrition prevalence compared to 2017/18 Deyr (median GAM of 13.8%). GAM prevalence is Critical (15-29.9%) in 12 out of 33 populations surveyed. Accordingly, an estimated 294 200 children will likely face acute malnutrition, including 55200 who are likely to be severely malnourished (total acute malnutrition burden). North Gedo Riverine, South Gedo Agro-pastoral, South Gedo Riverine, according to FSNAU-FEWS NET 2018 Post Gu Technical Release, 01 Sep 2018, are considered populations hotspots in need of urgent nutrition and health support interventions havjng Critical prevalence of acute malnutrition (≥15% GAM) or10.7% or more of children have Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) below the 125(mm) threshold As per the nutrition cluster case load estimation across Gedo is 45,040 which is quite critical as ugent intervention is needed to prevent deterioration and possible loss of life. pregnant women need micronutrient supplementation to prevent fetal growth retardation and consequent occurrence of malnutrition after birth HARD will reach at least 3267 Children (Boys1445 and Girls1822) 0-59 months for nutrition treatment, deworming vit A supplementation

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Due to the looming food insecurity and possible flooding in the event of more than average rains, the targeted population face among other risks starvation, displacement and upsurge of diseases

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

HARD has gender policy that ensure that when hiring staff that women are given 50% consideration in different positions. In addition, the project target populations are segregated into men, women, boys and girls hence giving emphasis to all gender groups in the community. During the screening and admission of children into nutrition programs, boys and girls will be given equal chances. Gender considerations will be taken into account throughout the assessment, planning and design of the interventions and significant consideration will be given to pregnant women, mothers, and women of child-bearing age. In the provision of health and nutrition promotion campaigns, messaging will be targeted for specific gender and age groups and as appropriate the events will be organized separately. Equitable and gender-sensitive participation and decision-making approaches will be adopted throughout the project, including women’s participation in the community nutrition committees and access to gender-specific health and nutrition information. Community consultations through focus-group discussions will take place during the project implementation, and stakeholders will be involved in decision-making processes to tailor the project to the needs of different gender and age groups. Furthermore, data will be dis-aggregated by age and gender in all recording and reporting

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Currently, the situation in Gedo is stable with no major attacks in the town and surrounding villages however HARD will undertake necessary assessment of the security situation and work with relevant authorities.
